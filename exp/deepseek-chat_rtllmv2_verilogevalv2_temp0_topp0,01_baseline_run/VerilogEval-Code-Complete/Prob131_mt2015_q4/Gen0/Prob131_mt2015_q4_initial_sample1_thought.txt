To implement the top-level module, we need to:
1. Instantiate two instances of Module A (A1 and A2) and two instances of Module B (B1 and B2)
2. Connect all their first inputs to 'x' and second inputs to 'y'
3. Connect outputs of A1 and B1 to an OR gate
4. Connect outputs of A2 and B2 to an AND gate
5. Connect the outputs of the OR and AND gates to an XOR gate to produce final output 'z'

Module A implements z = (x^y) & x
Module B's behavior is defined by the given truth table (we'll implement it using a lookup table based on inputs)
