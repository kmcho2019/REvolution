To design a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we need three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter should be in BCD format (two digits each). The seconds and minutes counters should roll over from 59 to 00, while the hours counter should roll over from 12 to 01. The PM indicator should toggle when the hours counter rolls over from 11 to 12 (AM to PM) or from 12 to 01 (PM to AM). The reset signal should initialize the clock to 12:00:00 AM with highest priority.

1. Seconds counter (ss): increments on ena, rolls over at 59
2. Minutes counter (mm): increments when ss rolls over, rolls over at 59
3. Hours counter (hh): increments when mm rolls over, special handling for 12-hour format
4. PM indicator: toggles when hours go from 11->12 or 12->1
5. Reset handling: sets time to 12:00:00 AM when active
