To design a 100-bit left/right rotator with synchronous load and enable, we need to:
1. Implement a synchronous load that updates the register with input data when load is high.
2. For rotation:
   - Right rotation (ena=2'b01): The LSB is shifted out and becomes the new MSB, all other bits shift right by 1.
   - Left rotation (ena=2'b10): The MSB is shifted out and becomes the new LSB, all other bits shift left by 1.
   - No rotation (ena=2'b00 or 2'b11): The register remains unchanged.
3. The operations should be synchronous to the clock edge.
