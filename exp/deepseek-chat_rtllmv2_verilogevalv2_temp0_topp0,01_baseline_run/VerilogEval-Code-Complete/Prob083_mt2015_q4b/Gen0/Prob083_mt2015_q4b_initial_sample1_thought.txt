Analyzing the waveform, we can see that z is 1 in three cases:
1. When both x and y are 0 (first 5 time units)
2. When both x and y are 1 (at 45ns and 50ns, and again at 70ns)
3. At 55ns when both transition to 0

This suggests that z is the XNOR of x and y, which is equivalent to (x ~^ y) or (x == y). The XNOR operation outputs 1 when both inputs are equal, and 0 otherwise.
