To implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1:
1. The LFSR shifts right on each clock cycle.
2. The MSB (q[31]) is always the feedback bit (XOR of tapped bits with q[0]).
3. Tapped bits (positions 31, 21, 1, and 0 after right shift) are XORed with q[0] to get their next value.
4. Other bits simply shift right.
5. Synchronous reset sets q to 32'h1.
