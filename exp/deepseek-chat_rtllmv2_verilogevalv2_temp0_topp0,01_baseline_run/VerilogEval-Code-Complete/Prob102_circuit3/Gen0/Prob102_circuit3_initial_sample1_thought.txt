Analyzing the waveform, we can see that the output q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and d is 1 (regardless of a and c), or
2. a is 1 and d is 1 (regardless of b and c), or
3. b is 1 and c is 1 (regardless of a and d), or
4. a is 1 and c is 1 (regardless of b and d)

This can be simplified to: q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d)
