To build a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. The LFSR will shift right on each clock cycle
2. The MSB (q[4]) will be the feedback bit that gets XORed with tap positions
3. Tap positions are 5 and 3 (note: for a 5-bit register, position 5 is the feedback input, so we XOR q[3] with q[0])
4. On reset, initialize to 1 (00001)
5. The feedback is calculated as q[0] XOR q[2] (since q[3] is at position 4-3=1 in 0-based indexing)
