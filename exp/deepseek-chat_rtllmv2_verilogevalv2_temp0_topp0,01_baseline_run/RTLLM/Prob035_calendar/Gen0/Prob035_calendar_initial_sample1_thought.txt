The perpetual calendar module needs to track time in hours, minutes, and seconds with proper rollover behavior. We'll use three separate always blocks to handle each time component (seconds, minutes, hours) with appropriate reset and increment logic. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and rolls over at 59. The minutes block increments when seconds reach 59 and rolls over at 59. The hours block increments when both minutes and seconds reach 59, rolling over at 23. All outputs are 6-bit to accommodate values up to 59 (for minutes/seconds) and 23 (for hours).
