The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine with three main states: red, yellow, and green. The system will:
1. Use an 8-bit counter to track timing for each state
2. Implement normal timing cycles (60 for green, 5 for yellow, 10 for red)
3. Handle pedestrian button press by shortening green time to 10 if remaining time > 10
4. Maintain proper light pairing (lane and sidewalk lights are complementary)
5. Reset to red state with counter at 10 when reset is active

The design will use three always blocks:
1. State transition logic
2. Counter management
3. Output signal generation
