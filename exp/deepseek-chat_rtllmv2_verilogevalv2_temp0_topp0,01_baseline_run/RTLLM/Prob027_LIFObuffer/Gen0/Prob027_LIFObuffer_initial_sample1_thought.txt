The LIFO buffer will be implemented as a stack with a stack pointer (SP) tracking the top of the stack. The stack pointer starts at 4 (empty) and decrements on push operations and increments on pop operations. The buffer is considered full when SP is 0 and empty when SP is 4. The design uses synchronous operations triggered by the rising clock edge, with reset functionality to initialize the stack. The EMPTY and FULL flags are combinational outputs based on the current SP value.
