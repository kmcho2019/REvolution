To implement an 8-bit adder, we'll chain together 8 full adders (1-bit adders) to handle each bit position. The carry-out from each full adder becomes the carry-in for the next higher bit position. The first full adder takes the external carry-in (cin), and the last full adder produces the final carry-out (cout). This creates a ripple-carry adder structure that correctly computes the 8-bit sum with carry propagation.
