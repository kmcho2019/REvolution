ANALYSIS:
The simulation log shows that while the hour (hh), minute (mm), and second (ss) counters are functioning correctly (no mismatches), there are 177,947 mismatches in the PM indicator output. This indicates a fundamental flaw in the AM/PM toggling logic. 

The issue stems from the PM indicator (pm_reg) being toggled every time the hour counter transitions from 12 to 1 (when hh_low == 2 && hh_high == 1). However, this logic would cause the PM indicator to toggle twice per 12-hour cycle (once at 12:00:00 PM->1:00:00 PM and again at 12:00:00 AM->1:00:00 AM), when it should only toggle once (at 12:00:00 PM->1:00:00 PM). This explains why there are so many mismatches in the PM output while the time counters remain correct.

The reset logic correctly initializes the clock to 12:00:00 AM, and the cascading counter logic for seconds, minutes, and hours appears to function as intended based on the lack of mismatches in those outputs.
