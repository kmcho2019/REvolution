To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we'll need to chain together 4 full adder modules. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and carry-out. The carry-out from each stage becomes the carry-in for the next stage. The final carry-out becomes the overflow bit (MSB) of the 5-bit sum output.

1. Instantiate 4 full adders in series
2. Connect the carry-out of each adder to the carry-in of the next
3. The first carry-in is 0
4. The final carry-out becomes sum[4]
5. The individual sums form sum[3:0]
