The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating and cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should activate when it's too cold, while in cooling mode, the aircon should activate when it's too hot. The fan should turn on whenever either the heater or aircon is active, or when explicitly requested by the 'fan_on' input. The key is to use the mode input to select between heating and cooling behaviors, and to combine the conditions for fan activation.
