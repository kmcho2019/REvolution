ANALYSIS:
The simulation errors indicate two key issues in the Verilog code:

1. For out_always_comb: The error shows that out_always_comb is declared as a wire but is being assigned within an always_comb block. In Verilog, outputs assigned in procedural blocks (always_comb/always_ff) must be declared as reg, not wire.

2. For out_always_ff: Similarly, out_always_ff is declared as a wire but is being assigned within an always_ff block. Sequential logic outputs must also be declared as reg since they hold state between clock cycles.

The design intent was correctly implemented in terms of the XOR functionality (continuous assignment, combinational logic, and sequential logic), but the output port declarations don't match the assignment methods being used.
