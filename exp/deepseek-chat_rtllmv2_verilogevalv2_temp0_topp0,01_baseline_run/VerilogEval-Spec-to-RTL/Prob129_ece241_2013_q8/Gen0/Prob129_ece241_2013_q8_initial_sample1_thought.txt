To implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "101" with overlapping sequences, we need 3 states:
1. IDLE: Initial state, waiting for the first '1'
2. GOT1: Received '1', waiting for '0'
3. GOT10: Received '10', waiting for '1'

The output z is asserted when we're in GOT10 state and receive '1'. The FSM transitions:
- From IDLE to GOT1 on '1', stays on '0'
- From GOT1 to GOT10 on '0', back to IDLE on '0', stays on '1'
- From GOT10 to GOT1 on '1' (with z=1), to IDLE on '0'
