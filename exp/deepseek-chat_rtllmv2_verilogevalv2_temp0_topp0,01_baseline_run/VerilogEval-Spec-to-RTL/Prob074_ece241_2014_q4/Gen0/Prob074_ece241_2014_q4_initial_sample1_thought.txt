The problem requires implementing a finite state machine with three parallel paths, each consisting of a two-input gate (XOR, AND, OR) followed by a D flip-flop. The outputs of these flip-flops are then NOR'ed together to produce the final output z. Each gate's second input is derived from its corresponding flip-flop's output (with complementation for AND and OR gates). The design involves:
1. Three D flip-flops to store the outputs of the gates
2. The three gates (XOR, AND, OR) with their specific input configurations
3. A three-input NOR gate for the final output
4. All sequential elements triggered on the positive clock edge
