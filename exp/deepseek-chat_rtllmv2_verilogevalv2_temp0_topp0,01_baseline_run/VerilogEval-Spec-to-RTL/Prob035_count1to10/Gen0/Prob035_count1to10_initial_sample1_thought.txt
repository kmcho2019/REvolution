To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10, we'll need a 4-bit register to store the current count value. The counter should increment on each positive clock edge, wrapping back to 1 after reaching 10. A synchronous reset will force the counter to 1 when active. The key points are:
1. Use a 4-bit register for the counter
2. Increment the counter on each clock edge
3. Reset to 1 when reset is high
4. Wrap around to 1 after reaching 10
