To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and another on the negative edge of the clock. Then, we'll use a multiplexer to select the appropriate output based on the current clock state. The multiplexer will choose the positive edge flip-flop's output when the clock is high and the negative edge flip-flop's output when the clock is low. This approach effectively captures data on both clock edges while using standard single-edge triggered flip-flops.
