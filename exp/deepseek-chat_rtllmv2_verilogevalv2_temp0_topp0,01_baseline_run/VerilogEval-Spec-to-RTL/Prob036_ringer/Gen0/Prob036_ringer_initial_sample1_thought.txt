The design needs to control two outputs (ringer and motor) based on two inputs (ring and vibrate_mode). The ringer should be activated when ring is high and vibrate_mode is low. The motor should be activated when both ring and vibrate_mode are high. The outputs should never be active simultaneously. This can be implemented with simple combinational logic where:
- ringer = ring AND NOT vibrate_mode
- motor = ring AND vibrate_mode
