Optimize the 2-to-1 multiplexer by replacing the always block with a continuous assignment statement. This reduces the complexity by removing the procedural block and the reg data type, leading to simpler combinational logic that synthesizes directly into a mux gate. This approach improves area and power efficiency as it avoids unnecessary inferred registers or latches, and the logic is clearer for synthesis tools. The module interface remains unchanged except 'out' is declared as wire. This is the most area- and power-efficient implementation for such a simple mux.
