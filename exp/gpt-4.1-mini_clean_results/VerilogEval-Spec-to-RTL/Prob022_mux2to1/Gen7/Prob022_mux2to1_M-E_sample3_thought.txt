Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a conditional (ternary) operator in an always_comb block. This approach uses behavioral Verilog with a procedural assignment inside an always block triggered by any input change. It explicitly models the mux behaviorally rather than purely with continuous assignment, providing clarity in sequential designs or when more complex conditional logic might be introduced. This method is syntactically different yet functionally equivalent to the previous approach.
