Given the existing implementation is already optimal for a simple 1-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer, further optimization at the RTL level is minimal. The design is clear, minimal, and uses a continuous assignment with a ternary operator which synthesizes efficiently.

However, to slightly enhance power by reducing potential glitching and unnecessary switching, I will implement the multiplexer using a behavioral always_comb block with case statement, which can help some synthesis tools infer a cleaner mux structure or better glitch reduction through inferred mux cells.

Additionally, I will keep explicit wire declarations and maintain good style and documentation.

This small change may lead to similar or marginally improved power characteristics depending on the synthesis tool and library, without impacting area or timing. It also demonstrates an alternative RTL coding style for muxes.
