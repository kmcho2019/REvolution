Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using explicit bitwise logic operators to create an equivalent expression: out = (a AND NOT sel) OR (b AND sel). This decomposes the mux function into basic gates (AND, NOT, OR) and may be more intuitive from a gate-level design perspective. It provides the same functionality with clear logic gate behavior, useful for illustrating fundamental mux construction without relying on the ternary operator.
