To achieve the best of both examples, we combine clarity, synthesis efficiency, and modern Verilog style by implementing the 2-to-1 mux as a continuous assignment to a wire output. This approach removes unnecessary procedural blocks and reg declarations, leading to minimal logic and better PPA metrics.
