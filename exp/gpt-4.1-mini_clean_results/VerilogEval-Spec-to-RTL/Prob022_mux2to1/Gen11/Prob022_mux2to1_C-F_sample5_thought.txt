To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I aim to keep the design minimal and optimal for power, area, and performance by using a continuous assignment with a ternary operator, which synthesizes efficiently into a single mux gate.

From Example 1, the insight to maintain clear documentation and explicit port directions is valuable.

From Example 2, the clarity of an always block is noted but does not improve PPA over the continuous assignment.

Therefore, the fusion strategy is:
- Use continuous assignment for minimal, clean, and optimal synthesis.
- Provide clear comments explaining the functionality.
- Use explicit wire inputs and output for best style and tool compatibility.
- Avoid unnecessary default or procedural constructs to reduce switching and area.

This approach produces a minimal, readable, and optimal RTL for a 1-bit 2-to-1 mux, combining clarity and power/area efficiency.
