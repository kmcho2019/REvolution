To optimize the existing implementation for better power and area without affecting functionality, I will replace the procedural always block and reg output with a continuous assignment and wire output. This reduces unnecessary inferred flip-flops or latches and allows synthesis tools to map the 2-to-1 mux directly to a single mux gate, minimizing logic and switching activity.

The continuous assignment is simple, clear, and synthesizes efficiently, thus improving power and area metrics slightly without any performance degradation since the original logic is purely combinational.

This is a standard best practice for simple combinational logic like multiplexers.
