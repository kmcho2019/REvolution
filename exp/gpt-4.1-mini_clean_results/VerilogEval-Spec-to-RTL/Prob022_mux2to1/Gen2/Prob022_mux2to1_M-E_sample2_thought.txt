Instead of using a ternary operator, implement the 2-to-1 mux using basic gate-level primitives (AND, OR, NOT). This approach explicitly shows the internal structure of the multiplexer and can be educational in hardware understanding. We use the logic: out = (a AND ~sel) OR (b AND sel). This is logically equivalent but expressed as gate-level logic. While it might not reduce area or power, it offers clarity on underlying hardware and can be easier for certain synthesis tools to optimize or for teaching purposes.
