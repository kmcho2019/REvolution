The original code is already minimal and optimal for a simple 2-to-1 mux in terms of area and power. To potentially improve power slightly, I will use the built-in 'assign' statement as before but write it using the more explicit bitwise operations: a common technique is to use the formula out = (a & ~sel) | (b & sel). This could lead synthesis tools to infer a better mux structure in some cases. This change is mostly stylistic and unlikely to affect PPA significantly but might offer marginal improvements in some tools. Also, explicitly declaring 'out' as wire improves clarity.
