The original design is already minimal and optimal for a 1-bit 2-to-1 mux. However, a minor stylistic improvement can be to use an explicit if-else inside an always_comb block, which some synthesis tools might map more directly to mux primitives, potentially helping in technology mapping. This is more a stylistic change than a PPA improvement, but it does not impact performance or area negatively. Since the reported power and area are already minimal, no code-level optimization is feasible. Thus, the improvement here is code clarity and synthesis friendliness by using an always_comb block.
