The original implementation is already minimal and efficient, using a conditional operator for the 2-to-1 mux. To potentially improve power and synthesis clarity, we can remove the explicit comparison "sel == 1'b0" and replace it with "sel ? b : a" since "sel" is a one-bit signal and can directly serve as the mux select line. This minor change reduces logic complexity for synthesis and can slightly reduce power due to fewer gates or transitions. This results in the same functionality with a simpler expression.
