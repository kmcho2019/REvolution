The original design is already minimal and optimal in terms of logic for a 2-to-1 mux. However, to potentially improve power slightly, one can implement the mux using gate-level primitives (AND, OR, NOT) which can sometimes lead to better control of the synthesized netlist and more opportunities for optimizations by synthesis tools. This may also clarify intent to the synthesis tool, potentially resulting in lower power or area due to simpler gates.

Additionally, using continuous assignments with gate-level operators can slightly reduce logic levels and ensure better control of inferred gates.

Therefore, implement the mux as out = (~sel & a) | (sel & b).
