Since the original design is already a minimal and optimal 2-to-1 mux implemented using a ternary operator, further RTL simplification is not possible. However, I can re-implement the mux using the bitwise operators explicitly (out = (~sel & a) | (sel & b)) which is structurally equivalent, and often synthesis tools map this directly to mux logic as well. This may marginally help synthesis tools infer mux primitives better for certain flows, potentially improving power slightly due to clear logic structure. No other improvements are applicable at this scale without changing input or output interface or adding gating logic, which is not beneficial here.
