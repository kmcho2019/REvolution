Fuse the clarity and explicitness of the gate-level approach with the succinct and synthesizable behavioral style. Use a continuous assignment with a ternary (conditional) operator `assign out = sel ? b : a;`. This merges the intuitive understanding of mux functionality with optimal synthesis: it directly maps to dedicated mux primitives in most synthesis tools, minimizing area and power. It also avoids explicit inversion or multiple gates, reducing switching activity and potentially improving power efficiency and timing. The code is concise, clear, and synthesis-friendly, combining the best of both previous methods.
