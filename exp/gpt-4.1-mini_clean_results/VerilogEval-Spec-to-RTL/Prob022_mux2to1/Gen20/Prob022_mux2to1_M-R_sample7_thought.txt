I will refactor the 2-to-1 multiplexer by implementing it inside an always_comb procedural block, which is a more explicit way to define combinational logic in SystemVerilog and supported by Verilog-2001 tools using always @(*) for Verilog. This approach uses a procedural block with if-else statements to assign the output based on the select signal. This style is often preferred when scaling to more complex multiplexers or when the designer wants clearer procedural semantics, while maintaining combinational behavior.
