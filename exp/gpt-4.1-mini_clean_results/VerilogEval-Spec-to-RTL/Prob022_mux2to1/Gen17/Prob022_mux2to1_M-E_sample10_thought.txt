Instead of using a straightforward ternary operator, this design employs a structural gate-level approach by explicitly instantiating basic logic gates to build the 2-to-1 mux. The architecture uses AND gates for each input gated by the select signals (sel and its inverse) and an OR gate to combine the results.

This approach illustrates the traditional mux construction and can be beneficial for educational purposes or low-level gate control. Although it might have slightly higher delay and area compared to continuous assignment, it provides explicit control over gate-level implementation. Additionally, it avoids using the conditional operator, offering a completely distinct style from typical behavioral descriptions.
