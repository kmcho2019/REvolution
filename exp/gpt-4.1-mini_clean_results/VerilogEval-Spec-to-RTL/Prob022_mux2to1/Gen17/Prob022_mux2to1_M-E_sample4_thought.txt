I will implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using behavioral Verilog with a continuous assignment and the ternary conditional operator. This approach directly expresses the multiplexer logic at a high level, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the implementation efficiently. Using a single assign statement reduces the gate-level complexity and can improve power and area by leveraging built-in mux primitives in the target technology. It also keeps the design compact and easy to read, which benefits maintainability.
