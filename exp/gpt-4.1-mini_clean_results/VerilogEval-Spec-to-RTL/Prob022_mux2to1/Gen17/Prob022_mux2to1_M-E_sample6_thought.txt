Instead of using a direct ternary operator, implement the 2-to-1 mux using a structural approach by explicitly constructing it from basic gates: two AND gates, one NOT gate, and one OR gate.

This approach uses the classic multiplexing equation:
  out = (~sel & a) | (sel & b)

While slightly more verbose than the ternary operator, this approach clearly shows the underlying logic and can be beneficial for educational purposes or for synthesis tools that infer muxes from gate-level netlists.

Additionally, this can help ensure glitch behavior is explicit and controllable by the engineer. It also provides an alternative design style that might be preferred in certain design flows or for transparent debugging.

This design demonstrates how a mux can be built using primitive gates rather than a conditional operator, offering an architectural variation.
