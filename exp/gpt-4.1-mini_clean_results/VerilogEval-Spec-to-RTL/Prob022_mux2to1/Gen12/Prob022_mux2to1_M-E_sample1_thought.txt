Instead of using a ternary operator for the 2-to-1 mux, I will implement the multiplexer using basic logic gates (AND, OR, NOT). This gate-level approach explicitly shows how the mux can be built from primitive gates, which might be useful for teaching, debugging, or custom synthesis flows targeting gate-level netlists. The logic is:

out = (~sel & a) | (sel & b)

This is functionally equivalent to a mux but expressed purely as a combination of AND, OR, and NOT gates rather than a conditional operator or always block.
