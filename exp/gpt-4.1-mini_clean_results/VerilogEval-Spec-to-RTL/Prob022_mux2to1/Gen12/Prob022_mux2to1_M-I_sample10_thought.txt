- Performance is at a single mux gate level, minimal delay.
- Area is minimal (one mux).
- Power is inherently low due to minimal switching logic.

Alternatively, implementing the mux as a primitive gate (if allowed by synthesis tool) may guide the tool more explicitly, but most modern synthesis tools already infer the mux optimally from the ternary operator.

Hence, the best improvement strategy is maintaining the same structure and style, focusing on clear and concise documentation. This is already done well.
