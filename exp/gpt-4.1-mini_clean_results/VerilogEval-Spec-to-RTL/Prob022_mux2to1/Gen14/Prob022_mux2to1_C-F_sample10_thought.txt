- Keeping all port declarations explicit and consistent with best synthesis practices.
- Providing clear, detailed comments on functionality and implementation choices to aid understanding and maintainability.
- Avoiding unnecessary procedural blocks or regs that could add complexity or infer latches.
- The result is a clean, minimal, synthesis-friendly, and clearly documented module.
