I will implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a behavioral 'always' block with a combinational sensitivity list. This approach uses a procedural block with an if-else statement to select the output based on the select signal. This is different from both the gate-level and continuous assignment styles. It is often intuitive and easily synthesizable, allowing clear control flow representation while still being purely combinational logic. This technique is practical for larger multiplexers or when the multiplexer is part of a sequential or more complex logic.
