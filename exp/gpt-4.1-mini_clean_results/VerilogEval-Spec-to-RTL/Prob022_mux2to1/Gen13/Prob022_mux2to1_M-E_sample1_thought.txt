Instead of using a continuous assignment with a ternary operator, we can implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer structurally using basic gates. By using AND, OR, and NOT gates explicitly, we create a gate-level netlist representation of the mux. This approach can help demonstrate how the mux is composed from fundamental logic gates and is a different architectural perspective from the original approach. It is also fully synthesizable and functionally equivalent, though slightly larger in area and possibly power.

Specifically, the mux output is constructed as: out = (a AND ~sel) OR (b AND sel).
We explicitly instantiate these gates to clearly show the underlying hardware.
