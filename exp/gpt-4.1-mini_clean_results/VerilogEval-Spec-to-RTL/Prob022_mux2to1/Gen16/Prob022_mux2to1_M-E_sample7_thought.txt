To create a novel architectural solution for a 2-to-1 multiplexer, I will implement it using basic logic gates rather than the direct ternary operator or a procedural block. Specifically, I will express the mux functionality with AND, OR, and NOT gates:

out = (a AND NOT sel) OR (b AND sel)

This gate-level approach explicitly shows the underlying logic of the mux and is educational for understanding how multiplexers can be constructed from fundamental gates. It may not be as minimal as the ternary operator for synthesis but serves a different conceptual purpose and can be useful if gates are individually optimized or if integrating into gate-level netlists.

This alternative logic is functionally equivalent and can be straightforwardly synthesized into mux hardware. It also highlights the relationship between the select signal and the data inputs without relying on conditional assignments.
