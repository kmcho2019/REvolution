Instead of using a continuous assignment with a ternary operator, I will implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using basic logic gates: AND, OR, and NOT. This gate-level approach can make the multiplexer logic explicit and may be beneficial for educational purposes or certain synthesis tools that optimize based on gate-level descriptions. 

Specifically, when sel=0, out = a, which can be implemented as (NOT sel) AND a, and when sel=1, out = b, which is sel AND b. Then the final output is the OR of these two terms. This approach explicitly represents the multiplexer functionality with gate primitives.
