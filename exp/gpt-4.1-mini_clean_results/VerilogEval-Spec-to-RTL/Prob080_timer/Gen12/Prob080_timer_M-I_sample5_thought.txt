To optimize for power and potentially performance, we can add a clock enable signal that disables updating the counter when the counter is zero and load is not asserted. This avoids unnecessary register toggling when the counter has reached zero, reducing dynamic power consumption and switching activity.

The single D flip-flop usage is maintained by gating the register update with a clock enable inside the always block (instead of using an actual gated clock to avoid clock gating complexity). The combinational next_counter logic remains the same.

This approach is a simple yet effective enhancement that does not affect functionality but can reduce power and improve effective clock period by reducing toggling in the steady zero state.
