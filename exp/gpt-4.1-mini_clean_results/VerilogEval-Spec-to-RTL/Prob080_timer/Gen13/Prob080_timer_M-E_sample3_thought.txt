Implement the timer using a shift-register approach within a 10-bit shift register acting as the state-holding element (a single 10-bit D flip-flop). On load, the input data (number of clock cycles) is loaded into the shift register. On each clock cycle when not loading, shift the register right by one bit, effectively counting down. The terminal count (tc) is asserted when all bits in the register are zero. This approach models countdown as a shift operation rather than subtracting by 1, providing a different architectural solution with minimal combinational logic.
