To optimize for better power and timing, I will introduce a clock enable (cen) signal that activates the counter only when it needs to change (on load or when counter is non-zero). This reduces unnecessary toggling of the register when the counter is zero and no load occurs, lowering dynamic power.  
The logic for next_counter is simplified since the counter only updates when enabled, and the counter stays stable otherwise. This also slightly improves timing by reducing the active logic on each clock edge.  
The terminal count output remains combinational and asserted when the counter is zero. Overall, this small change reduces switching activity while preserving functional correctness and the single D flip-flop requirement.
