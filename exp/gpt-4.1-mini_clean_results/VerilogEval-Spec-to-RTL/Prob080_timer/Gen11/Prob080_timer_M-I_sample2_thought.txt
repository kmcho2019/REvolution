To improve power efficiency without sacrificing performance or area, I will add an enable signal that disables counting when the counter is zero, effectively gating the decrement operation. This prevents unnecessary toggling of the counter register when the timer is stopped at zero. The counter will only decrement when enable is asserted, i.e., when the counter is not zero and load is low. This small optimization reduces dynamic switching, lowering power consumption. The logic remains within a single always block and uses a single 10-bit register, complying with the one flip-flop storage requirement, keeping the design simple and efficient.
