Implement the timer using a single 10-bit shift register-based down counter. At load time, parallel-load the 10-bit data into a register of 10 D flip-flops. Each clock cycle without load shifts the register right by 1 with a zero shifted in, effectively dividing the counter by 2 every clock cycle and approximating a countdown (this is a novel approach to differ from a simple subtract-by-one counter). The terminal count (tc) is asserted when all bits in the counter are zero. Once zero, the register stops shifting until reloaded. This approach utilizes the single flip-flop metaphor by representing the counter as a shift register and emphasizes a novel counting scheme via right shifts rather than arithmetic subtraction.
