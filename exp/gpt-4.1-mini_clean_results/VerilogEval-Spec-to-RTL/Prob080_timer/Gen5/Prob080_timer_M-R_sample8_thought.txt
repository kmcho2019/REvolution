Refactor the design by separating the next-state logic from the register update to make the design more modular and explicit. Instead of performing the decrement and load inside a single always block, compute the next counter value combinationally using an assign statement. Then update the register on the clock edge. This approach makes the sequential and combinational parts clearer and separates concerns, facilitating easier modifications or extensions later. The terminal count output remains the zero detection of the counter. This restructuring preserves the single flip-flop per bit requirement and the same functional behavior.
