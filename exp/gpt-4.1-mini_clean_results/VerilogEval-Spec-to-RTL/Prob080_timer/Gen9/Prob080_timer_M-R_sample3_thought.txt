Refactor the timer to separate combinational logic for next-state calculation and the sequential register update. Implement the internal counter as a 10-bit register updated on the positive clock edge, and compute the next counter value combinationally based on load and current counter. This uses a next-state variable to hold the computed next counter value, assigned with a combinational always block, while the sequential always block updates the register. The terminal count is asserted when the counter is zero. This approach makes the logic structure clearer, distinguishing sequential and combinational parts, but the underlying behavior remains the same.
