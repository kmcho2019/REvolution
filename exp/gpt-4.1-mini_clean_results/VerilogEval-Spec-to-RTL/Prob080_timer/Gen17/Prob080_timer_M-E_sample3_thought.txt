Implement the countdown timer using a shift register approach rather than a binary counter. When load is asserted, the internal shift register is loaded with a one-hot representation having a '1' in the position corresponding to the timer duration (encoded as a 1 shifted left by (data-1)) if data is not zero, or zero if data is zero. On each clock when load=0, the '1' in the shift register shifts right by one, effectively counting down. The terminal count (tc) is asserted when the shift register reaches zero, indicating the countdown is complete. This approach uses the shift register as a one-hot counter and the single D flip-flop holds the shift register state. This is a novel alternative to a binary down-counter.
