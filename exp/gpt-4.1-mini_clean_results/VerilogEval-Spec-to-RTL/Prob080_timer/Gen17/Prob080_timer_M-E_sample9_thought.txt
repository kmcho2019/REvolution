I will implement the timer as a single 10-bit register that holds the current countdown value. To adhere strictly to the single D flip-flop constraint, I will represent the entire 10-bit counter as a concatenation of a single register with a next-state value derived combinationally. The main twist is that I will implement the decrement operation using a conditional addition of -1, implemented via bitwise logic to avoid explicit subtractors, and load operation will overwrite the counter. The terminal count (tc) will be driven from a combinational zero detect of the current counter value. This approach reimagines the decrement with basic bitwise operations, eliminating the explicit subtractor to potentially optimize area and power, while respecting timing and functional correctness.
