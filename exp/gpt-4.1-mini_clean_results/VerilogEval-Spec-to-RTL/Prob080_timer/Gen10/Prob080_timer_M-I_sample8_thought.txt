To optimize for better performance and power, I will introduce a clock enable (ce) signal that disables counter updating when the counter is zero and load is low. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the counter register and related combinational logic, thus lowering dynamic power and potentially improving timing by reducing switching activity. The logic is minimal: the counter decrements only when load=0 and counter!=0. Otherwise, it either loads new data or holds its value at zero.

This approach preserves functionality but reduces switching and the critical path load. The design still uses a single 10-bit register (one D flip-flop per bit) and straightforward combinational logic, keeping area minimal.
