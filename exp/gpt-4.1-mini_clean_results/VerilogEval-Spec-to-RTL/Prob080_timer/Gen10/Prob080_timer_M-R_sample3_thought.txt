Refactor the design by explicitly describing the counter logic inside an always block with a single non-blocking assignment and performing combinational logic for the terminal count output separately. Instead of computing the entire next_counter value in a continuous assignment, embed the load and decrement conditions inside the always block, maintaining a single register for the counter. This structure preserves the single flip-flop usage, clearly separates combinational and sequential logic, and is a straightforward style familiar in many Verilog designs. The output tc can be assigned directly from the register to reflect the terminal count state. This approach may be more intuitive to some designers while functionally identical.
