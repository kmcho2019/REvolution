To improve the PPA metrics, especially power and timing, I will introduce an enable signal that activates the counter only when it needs to decrement, thereby reducing switching activity when the counter is zero or loading. Also, instead of direct subtraction, the decrement will be controlled through the enable signal, which allows synthesis tools to optimize better. For the terminal count logic, I'll use a reduction OR to detect non-zero state, which might help reduce combinational delay for tc output. The single always block remains with a clock enable style inside, maintaining a single D flip-flop per bit. This approach keeps the logic minimal, reduces unnecessary toggling, and can improve timing by simplifying the logic the synthesizer sees.
