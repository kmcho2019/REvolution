To optimize the design for better PPA (timing, power, and area), I aim to reduce switching activity and critical path delay. Currently, the counter decrements by 1 whenever load=0 and counter!=0, causing constant toggling and some delay from subtracting 1.

Improvement strategy:
- Implement a gated clock enable style: only update (decrement) the counter when load=1 or counter!=0, avoiding unnecessary register toggling when counter is 0 and load=0, which reduces dynamic power.
- Since only a single D flip-flop register is allowed, I cannot add a separate clock enable flip-flop. Instead, I will condition the counter update with an enable signal in the sequential block, performing the decrement only when necessary.
- Break down the next state logic to minimize combinational delay by avoiding the subtract operation unless needed.
- Keep the logic simple with minimal arithmetic.
- This results in the counter only toggling when required, improving power, and the smaller mux logic reduces timing critical path.

This approach preserves the single 10-bit register, triggers only required updates, and removes toggling of the counter at zero count.
