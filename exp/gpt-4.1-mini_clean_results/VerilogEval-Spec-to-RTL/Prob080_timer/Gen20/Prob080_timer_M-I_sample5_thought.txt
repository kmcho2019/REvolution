To improve PPA, especially power and performance, I will add an enable signal to the counter register update to avoid unnecessary toggling when the counter is zero and load is not asserted. This prevents the counter from decrementing or reloading when not needed, thus reducing switching activity and dynamic power. The combinational logic for next_counter remains the same, but the register only updates when enabled. This gating approach keeps the design minimal and still uses only one D flip-flop register bank, meeting the original constraints. It will likely reduce power and slightly improve timing by shortening the effective critical path through fewer register toggles.
