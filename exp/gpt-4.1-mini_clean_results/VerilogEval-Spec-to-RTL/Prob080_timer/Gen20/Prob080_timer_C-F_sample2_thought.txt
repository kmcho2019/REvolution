Combine the clean separation of combinational next-state logic with an additional clock-enable signal to minimize toggling and reduce dynamic power. Specifically, define `next_counter` to choose between loading and decrementing logic, and generate a single-bit `en` signal that is asserted only when the counter is active and load is low. Use this enable to gate the counter update, so the register only toggles when needed (load asserted or counter decrements), preventing unnecessary register toggling when the counter is zero and not loading. This preserves the single D flip-flop register for the counter and retains the simple, easy-to-understand logic, while reducing switching activity and potentially improving timing by reducing unnecessary transitions. The terminal count output `tc` remains combinationally derived from `counter == 0`. This fusion brings improved power efficiency without added area or complexity.
