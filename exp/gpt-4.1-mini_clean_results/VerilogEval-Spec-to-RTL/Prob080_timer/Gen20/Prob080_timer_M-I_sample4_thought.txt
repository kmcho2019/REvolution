To improve PPA, introduce a counter enable signal so that the counter only toggles and decrements when necessary (counter nonzero and load is low). This reduces switching activity, lowering dynamic power without changing functional behavior. We keep the single always block and assign next_counter combinationally for clarity and synthesis friendliness. This optimization minimizes toggling on the register and combinational logic when the counter is zero, improving power and potentially timing without impacting area. The terminal count signal remains a simple comparator.
