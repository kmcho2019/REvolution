Use a two-stage counting scheme splitting the 10-bit count into two cascaded counters: a low 6-bit counter counting from 0 to 39, and a high 4-bit counter counting from 0 to 15. The low counter increments every cycle; when it reaches 39, it resets to 0 and increments the high counter. When the high counter reaches 15 and the low counter reaches 39, reset both counters to 0 synchronously. This approach breaks the large 10-bit counting logic into smaller counters with simpler terminal count detection and carry logic. This can improve timing by reducing long carry chains and simplify comparison logic, while maintaining a synchronous reset. The output `q` is formed by concatenating the high and low counters.
