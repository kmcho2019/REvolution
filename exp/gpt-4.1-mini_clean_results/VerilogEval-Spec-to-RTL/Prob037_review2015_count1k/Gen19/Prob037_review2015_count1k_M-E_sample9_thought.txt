Implement the 10-bit counter using a modulo-1000 counter with a synchronous reset and a ternary (conditional) operator for next-state logic to minimize combinational logic complexity. Use a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset to update the output `q`. 

When `q` reaches 999, reset it to zero on the next cycle. This avoids explicit comparator splitting or extra wires by using direct numeric comparison inside sequential logic, simplifying timing and resource usage.

This design leverages behavioral Verilog succinctness for clarity and minimal resource usage while meeting all requirements.
