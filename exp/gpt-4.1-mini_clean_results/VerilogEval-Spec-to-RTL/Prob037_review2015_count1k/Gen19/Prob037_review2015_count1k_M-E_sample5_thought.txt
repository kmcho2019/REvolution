Implement the 0-to-999 counter using a binary-coded decimal (BCD) style approach with two separate counters: one for the hundreds digit (0-9) and one for the units digit (0-99). This splits counting into two smaller counters chained together, reducing wide arithmetic and comparator complexity.

Specifically:
- Use a 4-bit units counter that counts from 0 to 9 and resets.
- On units counter wrap, increment a 3-bit hundreds counter (0 to 9).
- When hundreds counter reaches 9 and units counter reaches 9, reset both counters to 0.

The output q concatenates hundreds and units counters to form the final 10-bit count value (hundreds * 100 + units). For simplicity in output, multiply digits by their weights using shift and add operations internally.

This approach reduces wide arithmetic to small digit increments, and the reset and counting logic is clear. It also provides modularity and can facilitate future expansion or BCD output use.

The synchronous reset remains active-high on clock edge.
