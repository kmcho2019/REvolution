Simplify the counter by directly comparing the entire 10-bit counter value to 999 (10'd999) to detect the terminal count. Use a straightforward synchronous reset and count logic without intermediate next-state registers. On each clock edge, if reset is high, reset to 0; else if the counter reached 999, wrap back to 0; otherwise increment by 1. This reduces comparator complexity and logic levels, improving readability and potentially timing and area.
