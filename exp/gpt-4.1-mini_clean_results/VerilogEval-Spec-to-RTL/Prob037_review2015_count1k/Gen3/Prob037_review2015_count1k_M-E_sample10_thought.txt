Implement the 10-bit counter using a 4-bit low digit counter (0-9) and a 6-bit high digit counter (0-99) to count from 0 to 999. The low counter increments every clock cycle; upon reaching 9, it resets to 0 and triggers the high counter to increment. When the high counter reaches 99 and the low counter reaches 9, the count resets to 0. This approach mimics decimal counting, avoiding a large single comparator for 999 and potentially simplifying timing by splitting counting stages. The output q is assembled by combining high and low counters: q = high*10 + low.
