Implement a two-stage counting scheme to reduce the comparator complexity: use a 4-bit low counter counting from 0 to 9 and a 6-bit high counter counting from 0 to 99. The low counter increments every cycle and resets to 0 after 9, causing the high counter to increment. The combined count is (high_counter * 10 + low_counter), which counts from 0 to 999. On reset, both counters are set to 0. This approach removes the need for a large 10-bit comparator and instead uses simpler comparisons on smaller counters, potentially optimizing timing and area by exploiting modular counting stages.
