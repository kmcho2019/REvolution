Implement the counter using two cascaded smaller counters to count to 1000: a lower 4-bit counter counts from 0 to 9, and an upper 7-bit counter counts from 0 to 99. The lower counter increments every clock cycle; when it rolls over from 9 to 0, the upper counter increments by one. When both upper reaches 99 and lower reaches 9, the entire count resets to zero. The output q is formed by concatenating the upper and lower counts appropriately (q = upper * 10 + lower). This modular counting reduces the size of the comparator and uses simple small counters cascaded together, which can reduce combinational path delay and improve clarity.
