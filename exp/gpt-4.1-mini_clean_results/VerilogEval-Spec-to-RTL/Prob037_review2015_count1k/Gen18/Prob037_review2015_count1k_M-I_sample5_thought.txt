To optimize the design for better PPA while maintaining correctness, I will simplify the max count detection logic by replacing the partial bit comparison with a single equality check (q == MAX_COUNT). This reduces the combinational logic complexity and likely shortens the critical path, improving timing and area slightly. The synchronous reset and separation of combinational and sequential logic will be kept as-is for clarity and synthesis friendliness. This small change improves timing, area, and power by reducing unnecessary logic and switching activity.
