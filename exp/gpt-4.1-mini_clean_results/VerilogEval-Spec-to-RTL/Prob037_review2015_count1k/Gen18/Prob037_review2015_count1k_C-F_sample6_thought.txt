Fuse the clarity and modularity of explicit next-state combinational logic from Example 2 with a direct single equality comparator for terminal count detection (q == 10'd999) instead of splitting bits, to optimize both timing and area.

Specifically:
- Use a combinational block to compute next_q from q and reset.
- Detect terminal count with a single 10-bit equality check, which is simpler and yields better performance and area than partial bit matching.
- Synchronously reset q to 0 on reset or when terminal count is reached.
- Keep the registered output q updated on the positive clock edge.

This fusion simplifies comparator logic, reduces switching activity in the comparator (improving power), and maintains readability and maintainability with separate combinational and sequential blocks.
