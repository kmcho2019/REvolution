Implement the 0-999 counter using two cascaded decimal digit counters (BCD counters) for the hundreds and the tens/units digits. The lower 10 bits output the combined decimal count by encoding units and tens in a single 7-bit field for tens and units combined as a binary number (0-99) and the hundreds digit (0-9) in 3 bits. At each clock cycle, increment the units/tens counter; when it wraps from 99 to 0, increment the hundreds counter. Reset both counters synchronously on reset. Then combine the hundreds and tens/units counts to form the final 10-bit output representing 0-999 binary count. This approach modularizes counting, avoids a large single comparator for 999, and uses simple BCD digit counters cascading.
