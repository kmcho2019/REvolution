Implement the counter using two cascaded smaller counters: a 4-bit counter counting from 0 to 9 (least significant digit) and a 6-bit counter counting from 0 to 99 (most significant digits). On each clock cycle, increment the 4-bit counter; if it rolls over (reaches 9 then resets), increment the 6-bit counter. When both counters reach their max (6-bit counter is 99 and 4-bit counter is 9), reset both to zero. Combine the two counters to form the 10-bit output q representing values from 0 to 999. This segmented approach mimics decimal counting in hardware and divides the counting logic.
