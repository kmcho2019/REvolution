Implement the 0 to 999 counter by splitting it into two smaller counters: a lower decimal digit counter (0-9) and an upper decimal digit counter (0-99). The lower digit counter increments every clock cycle and rolls over from 9 back to 0, triggering the upper digit counter to increment. The upper digit counter counts from 0 to 99 and then rolls over to 0. The combined count is output as a 10-bit value calculated as (upper_counter * 10 + lower_counter). Reset synchronously sets both counters to zero.
