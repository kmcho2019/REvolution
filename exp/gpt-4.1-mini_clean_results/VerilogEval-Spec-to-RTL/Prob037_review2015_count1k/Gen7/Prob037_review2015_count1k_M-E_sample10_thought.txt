Implement a synchronous 10-bit counter with an arithmetic modulo operation using a binary adder and a conditional subtraction to avoid explicit equality comparison. Instead of checking if the counter equals 999, increment the counter by 1 every cycle and conditionally subtract 1000 when the count reaches or exceeds 1000, effectively wrapping around. This method uses simple arithmetic and can be more efficient because it replaces the equality comparator with a greater-or-equal check and arithmetic operations, potentially reducing critical path delay by leveraging the natural carry chain of the adder. The synchronous active-high reset clears the counter to zero.
