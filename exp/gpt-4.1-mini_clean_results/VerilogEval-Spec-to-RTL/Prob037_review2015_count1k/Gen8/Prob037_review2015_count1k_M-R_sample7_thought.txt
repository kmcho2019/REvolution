I will refactor the synchronous counter to separate the combinational next state logic from the sequential state update. This involves:

- Defining a wire `next_q` to hold the next counter value computed combinationally.
- Using an always block triggered on the clock to update `q` with `next_q` synchronously.
- Keeping the synchronous active-high reset inside the sequential always block.
- This structure makes the counter logic clearer by decoupling next-state calculation and register update, which can aid readability and support formal methods or further optimization.
- The counting condition remains `q < MAX_COUNT` for simplicity and efficient comparator synthesis.
