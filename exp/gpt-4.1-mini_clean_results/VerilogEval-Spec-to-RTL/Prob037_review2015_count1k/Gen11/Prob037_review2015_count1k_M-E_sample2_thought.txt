Implement the 0-to-999 counter using a binary-coded decimal (BCD) style approach with two separate counters: one 4-bit counter for the least significant decimal digit (0-9) and one 6-bit counter for the most significant digits (0-99). Increment the least significant digit every clock cycle; when it rolls over from 9 to 0, increment the most significant digit. When the most significant digit reaches 99 and the least significant digit reaches 9, reset both counters to zero synchronously. This approach breaks down the decimal count into decimal digits, simplifying rollover detection and potentially easing timing by distributing comparison logic. Then combine these two counters into the 10-bit output.

This modular approach also avoids a wide 10-bit comparator for 999; instead it uses smaller, simpler comparisons and addition logic, potentially improving synthesis optimization and readability. The reset remains synchronous active-high. Non-blocking assignments maintain clean sequential logic.
