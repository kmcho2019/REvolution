To optimize for better PPA (Timing, Power, and Area), I will simplify the terminal count detection by using a single 10-bit comparator instead of splitting into two parts. Modern synthesis tools are efficient with a single comparator and this reduces the combinational logic and net fanout, potentially improving timing and power. 

Additionally, since MAX_COUNT is less than 1024, the counter can naturally wrap with a single comparator. This also enhances readability and slightly reduces logic complexity.

The synchronous reset and counting logic remains unchanged for clarity and correctness. This balanced approach likely improves timing slack and reduces power by simplifying the comparator logic, while keeping area minimal.
