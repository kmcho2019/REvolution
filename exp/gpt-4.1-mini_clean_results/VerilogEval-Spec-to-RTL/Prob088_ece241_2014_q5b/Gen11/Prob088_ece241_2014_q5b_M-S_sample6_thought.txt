I will simplify the design by merging the two one-hot encoded state bits into a single bit since there are only two states (A and B). This binary encoding reduces flip-flop usage from two to one, simplifying the next state and output logic. The asynchronous reset still forces the state to A (0). The Mealy outputs and transitions are preserved, with state=0 representing A and state=1 representing B. This maintains full functionality but with a simpler and more area- and power-efficient design.
