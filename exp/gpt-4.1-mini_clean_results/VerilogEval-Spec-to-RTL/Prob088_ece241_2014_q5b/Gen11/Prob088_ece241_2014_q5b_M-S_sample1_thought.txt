Since this FSM has only two states and the one-hot encoding requires two flip-flops, explicitly encoding both states with two flip-flops is straightforward and minimal. Use two registers state_A and state_B for states A and B respectively, ensuring only one is asserted at a time (one-hot). The asynchronous active-high reset initializes state_A=1 and state_B=0. Transition logic updates states according to the FSM transitions. Output z is the Mealy output: z=1 when in state A and x=1, or in state B and x=0; else z=0. This explicit two-flip-flop one-hot encoding matches the requirement, simplifies reasoning, and avoids implicit complement logic.
