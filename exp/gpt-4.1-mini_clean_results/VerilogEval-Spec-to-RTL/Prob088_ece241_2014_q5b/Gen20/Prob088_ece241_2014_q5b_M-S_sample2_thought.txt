Use a single-bit one-hot state encoding with a single flip-flop representing state_B (state_A is implied as the complement). Initialize asynchronously to state_A (state_B=0). This reduces registers and logic. The next state and output follow:

- next state_B = (state_B & 1) | (state_A & x) = state_B | (~state_B & x) = state_B | x when in A.

Since state_A = ~state_B, next state_B = state_B | x.

Output z is Mealy: z=1 when (in A and x=1) or (in B and x=0), which simplifies to z = ( ~state_B & x ) | ( state_B & ~x ).

This minimal implementation meets the spec with fewer registers and simpler logic.
