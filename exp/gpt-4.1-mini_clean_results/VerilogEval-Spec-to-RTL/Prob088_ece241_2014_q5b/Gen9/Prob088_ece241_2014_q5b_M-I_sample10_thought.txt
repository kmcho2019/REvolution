The original design is already minimal, using one flip-flop to encode the two states with one-hot style semantics and asynchronous active-high reset. The output is a Mealy output computed combinationally, which is appropriate.

To optimize PPA slightly, especially power and potential glitches on output z, we can register the output z with a flip-flop. This change converts the output from a Mealy to a Moore-style signal (latched output), which removes glitches and helps with timing closure and power due to fewer glitches on the output net.

We'll add a flip-flop for output z, clocked on the same clock and asynchronously reset on areset (to zero). The output z register will take the Mealy combinational output as next input.

This improvement slightly increases area and flip-flops by one but can reduce glitches and dynamic power, and may improve timing due to registered output.

Thus, the strategy is to add an output register with asynchronous reset. The FSM state encoding and logic remain unchanged, preserving the simplicity and clarity of the original solution.
