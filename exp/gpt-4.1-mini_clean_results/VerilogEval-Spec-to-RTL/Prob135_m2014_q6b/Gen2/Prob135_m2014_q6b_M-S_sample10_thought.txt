We can simplify the design by directly expressing the next y[1] logic without separately decoding each state. From the previous solution, next_y1 = B + (C & w) + (E & w) + F, where B = ~y2 & ~y1 & y0, C = ~y2 & y1 & ~y0, E = y2 & ~y1 & ~y0, F = y2 & ~y1 & y0.

Note B and C differ only in y1 and y0 bits, and similarly E and F only differ in y0. We can factor these terms:

- B + (C & w) = ~y2 & ((~y1 & y0) + (y1 & ~y0 & w))
- (E & w) + F = y2 & (~y1 & ((~y0 & w) + y0))

This factoring reduces the number of gates and avoids redundant signals. So, the simplified combinational logic directly implements this factored expression without explicit intermediate wires.
