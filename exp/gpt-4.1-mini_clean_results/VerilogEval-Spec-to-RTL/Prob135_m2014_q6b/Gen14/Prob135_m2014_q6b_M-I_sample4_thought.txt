The existing solution is already simple and minimal, but a slight optimization can be done by simplifying the Boolean expression further to reduce logic depth and the number of signals.

The expression:
next_y1 = (y0 & ~y1) | (~y0 & w & (y1 | y2))

can be factored as:
next_y1 = (y0 & ~y1) | (~y0 & w & (y1 | y2))

Instead of creating separate signals for not_y0 and not_y1, we use bitwise negation directly inline to reduce net count and synthesis overhead.

Also, we can reduce intermediate signals to a minimum to possibly reduce area and switching power, making the combinational logic more compact.

This marginal simplification could reduce gate count and switching, which helps power and area without hurting performance.

Output Y1 remains assigned as next_y1, consistent with the spec.
