Instead of decoding each state explicitly, use the encoded values of y directly to implement the next y[1] logic through a case statement that enumerates transitions that set next_y1 to 1. For states B and F (001 and 101), next_y1 is always 1. For states C (010) and E (100), next_y1 depends on w being 1. For other states, next_y1 is 0. This approach simplifies the combinational logic by directly mapping encoded states with w to next_y1, avoiding separate decoding signals and making the design more readable and synthesizable.
