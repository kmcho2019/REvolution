The issue with the original design is that the FSM toggles state whenever bump_left or bump_right is high during a clock edge, which can cause multiple toggles if bumps are sustained over multiple clock cycles. To fix this, implement edge detection for bump signals to trigger a single toggle per bump event. This can be done by registering previous bump inputs and detecting rising edges (0->1 transitions) on either bump_left or bump_right. The FSM will toggle state only once on the detection of a rising bump signal. The asynchronous reset remains the same. Outputs remain Moore style, directly driven from the state register.
