To avoid multiple toggles caused by bump signals held high over several clock cycles, implement edge detection for bump inputs. Capture the previous state of bump_left and bump_right signals synchronously, and detect rising edges (0->1 transitions) for each bump signal. The state toggles only once per bump event on these detected edges. This ensures the FSM changes direction only once per bump event, preventing repeated toggles while the bump signal remains asserted. The rest of the design stays the same with asynchronous positive edge reset and Moore outputs.
