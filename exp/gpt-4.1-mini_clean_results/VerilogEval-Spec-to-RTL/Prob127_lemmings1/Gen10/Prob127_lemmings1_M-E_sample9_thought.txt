Instead of encoding states as a single bit representing direction, implement the FSM with two distinct one-hot encoded state flip-flops: one for walking left and one for walking right. This approach simplifies output logic as outputs directly reflect the state bits, eliminating the need for state decoding logic. The state transitions are managed by setting the appropriate one-hot bit based on bump inputs. On reset, initialize the FSM to walk left by setting the left state bit high and the right state bit low. The asynchronous reset remains active-high and edge-triggered. This one-hot FSM style also naturally maps well to FPGA flip-flops, potentially improving synthesis results in some toolchains.
