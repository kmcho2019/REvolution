To improve the PPA (performance, power, area), I will simplify the next_state logic by separating bump conditions and using straightforward boolean expressions without nested ternary operators. This approach reduces combinational logic depth and potential glitches, improving performance and power. I'll keep the asynchronous reset and Moore FSM style, as required. Also, I'll unify the bump conditions into one boolean expression for switching state, which makes the logic cleaner and more synthesis-friendly. The FSM state remains a one-bit register to minimize area. The outputs will remain as simple assignments from state. This should reduce the critical path delay and switching activity, thus improving timing and power, while keeping minimal area.
