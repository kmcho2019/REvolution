Use a single-bit register to represent the direction (0 = walk left, 1 = walk right). On async reset, set direction to 0 (walk left). At each positive clock edge, if bumped on either side (or both), invert the direction. This way, a single line of logic handles all bump cases uniformly, simplifying the next-state logic and outputs. Outputs walk_left and walk_right are generated directly from the direction register and its complement, ensuring outputs remain mutually exclusive and synchronized to the current state (Moore FSM).
