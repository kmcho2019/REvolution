Optimize the original Lemmings FSM by simplifying the next-state logic to a single combinational expression rather than a case statement, reducing logic complexity and potentially improving timing. Since the two states are complementary, represent the state as a single bit where 0 = WALK_LEFT and 1 = WALK_RIGHT. On any bump (left or right), the state toggles direction. Thus, next_state = (state ^ bump_any) where bump_any is the OR of bump_left and bump_right. This also handles the case when both bump_left and bump_right are 1 (still toggling). Outputs can be directly derived from the state bit without a case statement: walk_left = ~state and walk_right = state. Maintain the asynchronous positive-edge reset to WALK_LEFT. This approach minimizes combinational logic, reduces switching, and leads to more straightforward synthesis optimizations, enhancing both power and performance.
