Use a single flip-flop to hold the direction state: 0 means walk left, 1 means walk right. On asynchronous reset, set to 0 (walk left). On each clock edge, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted (including both), toggle the direction state. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are generated by decoding this state with walk_left = ~state and walk_right = state, ensuring complementary outputs derived from one register bit.
