Implement a simple two-state Moore FSM with asynchronous positive edge reset, where the state encodes walking direction: 0 for left, 1 for right. On any bump (left or right), the state toggles direction, covering the case of both bumps simultaneously as well. This toggling logic can be compactly expressed as next_state = state ^ (bump_left | bump_right). Outputs directly map from the state for clarity and minimal logic.
