The FSM is already minimal with one bit of state and simple next-state logic. To optimize for power and slightly reduce toggling, I will implement a clock enable that only updates the state when a bump occurs or reset is asserted. This prevents unnecessary state register toggling when no bump inputs change state.

Additionally, I will simplify next-state logic by noting that any bump (left or right) causes a state flip unless only one bump is active, in which case the state switches deterministically. By combining conditions logically, the next-state logic can be streamlined.

Outputs remain Moore and directly driven from state. This small clock gating approach reduces flip-flop toggling, potentially improving power with negligible impact on area or timing.
