To improve PPA, I will simplify the next_state logic into a minimal, clear boolean expression that covers all bump cases and holds state otherwise, eliminating unnecessary terms. Specifically, next_state should be:

- If bumped both sides: toggle state
- Else if bumped left only: walk right (1)
- Else if bumped right only: walk left (0)
- Else hold current state

This can be expressed as:
next_state = (bump_left ^ bump_right) ? bump_left : (bump_left & bump_right) ? ~state : state;

This concise conditional reduces gate levels and eases synthesis optimization. I will implement this with a ternary operator for clarity and efficiency. The outputs remain the same. Also, I keep the asynchronous positive edge reset and positive clock edge update.

This optimization reduces combinational logic depth and potential glitches, slightly improving performance and power, while area remains minimal.
