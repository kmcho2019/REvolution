Implement a two-state Moore FSM with states WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. On asynchronous positive edge reset, set state to WALK_LEFT. On each clock rising edge, check bump inputs: if bumped on left or right, switch direction (i.e., if walking left and bumped left or right, switch to walk right; if walking right and bumped left or right, switch to walk left). Outputs walk_left and walk_right are derived directly from the state.
