Implement a Moore FSM with two states: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT. On each clock rising edge, if bump_left or bump_right is high, switch states accordingly: if bumped on left, switch to WALK_RIGHT; if bumped on right, switch to WALK_LEFT; if both bumped, switch state (the problem states it switches directions regardless). Outputs walk_left and walk_right are driven by the current state.
