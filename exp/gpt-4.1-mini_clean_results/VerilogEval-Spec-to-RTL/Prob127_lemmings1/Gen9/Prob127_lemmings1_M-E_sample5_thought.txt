Implement the Lemming's walking direction as a 1-bit register that directly stores the output walk_right signal (1 means walking right, 0 means walking left). On each clock edge with asynchronous reset, update walk_right based on bump inputs: if bumped on left (bump_left=1), walk_right becomes 1; if bumped on right (bump_right=1), walk_right becomes 0; if bumped on both sides (both 1), toggle walk_right. The walk_left output is simply the complement of walk_right. This approach integrates state and output into the same register, preserving Moore FSM characteristics while minimizing internal signals.
