To simplify the design while preserving correct functionality, I will remove the two-stage synchronizers on bump inputs and directly use bump_left and bump_right signals in the FSM next-state logic. This assumes bump inputs are synchronous or stable at the clock edge; if they are asynchronous, external synchronization should be handled outside this module or by the environment.

The FSM has two states: walking left (0) and walking right (1). On areset (asynchronous, positive edge), state resets to walk left. On each positive clock edge, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted, the state toggles. If both bumps occur simultaneously, toggling still happens, as required.

This approach reduces complexity by not adding synchronizers, making the design minimal and easy to understand while meeting the problem requirements exactly.
