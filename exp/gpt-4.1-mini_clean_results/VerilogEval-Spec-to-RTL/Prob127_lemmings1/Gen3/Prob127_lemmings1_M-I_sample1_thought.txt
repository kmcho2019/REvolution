The original design incorrectly uses edge detection on bump inputs, causing the state to toggle only on rising edges of bump signals. According to the problem, the Lemming should switch direction anytime it is bumped (bump_left=1 or bump_right=1), regardless of whether the bump signal just rose or is continuously asserted. To fix this, the FSM transition should be level-sensitive: on every clock cycle, if bump_left or bump_right is high, toggle the walking direction. The asynchronous active-high reset remains the same, initializing the state to walk_left. This results in a simple Moore FSM with state toggling whenever either bump input is asserted.
