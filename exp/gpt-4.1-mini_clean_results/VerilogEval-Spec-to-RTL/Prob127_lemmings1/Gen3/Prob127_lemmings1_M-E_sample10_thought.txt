Use a single flip-flop to hold the direction: 0 for walking left, 1 for walking right. On asynchronous reset, set direction to 0 (walk left). On each clock edge, if either bump_left or bump_right is asserted, toggle the direction (walking direction flips whenever there's a bump on any side). This approach unifies the bump conditions and simplifies the next-state logic into a single toggle operation. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are generated combinationally from the direction bit (walk_left = ~direction, walk_right = direction), reducing redundant output logic.
