The FSM only needs to switch direction whenever either bump_left or bump_right is asserted (logic high) at the clock edge, regardless of whether the bump signals are edges or held high. Edge detection logic is unnecessary and incorrect per the specification.

Therefore, the state should update on every clock edge (except during asynchronous reset) by checking if bump_left or bump_right is 1. If yes, toggle the walking direction state; if not, keep the current state.

The reset is asynchronous and positive edge triggered, setting the state to walk_left.

The outputs are Moore outputs, derived solely from the state. This approach ensures correct toggling behavior regardless of bump signal duration, aligning perfectly with the problem statement and feedback.
