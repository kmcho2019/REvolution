The original FSM implementation is already minimal and efficient for the given problem, with a simple one-bit state encoding and clear next state logic. To further optimize power, area, and timing marginally, we can:

- Simplify the next state logic by directly computing the next state as XOR between current state and the OR of bump inputs (since bump on left or right or both switches state).
- This reduces the combinational logic complexity and potentially improves timing.
- Maintain asynchronous reset to ensure fast reset response.
- Keep outputs Moore style and directly derive from state.

This approach reduces the next state logic to a single XOR gate and OR gate, minimizing gate count and toggling in logic, which can slightly reduce power and area while potentially improving timing.
