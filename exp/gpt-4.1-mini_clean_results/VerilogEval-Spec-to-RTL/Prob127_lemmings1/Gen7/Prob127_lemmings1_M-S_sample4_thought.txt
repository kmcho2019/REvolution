Since the FSM has only two states and the outputs are complementary, we can represent the state by a single bit indicating walking right or left. The asynchronous reset sets the state to walk left (0). On each clock, if bumped on either side, we flip the state; otherwise, we keep it. The outputs are assigned directly from the state bit and its complement, removing the need for next_state and a case statement, simplifying the logic while maintaining functionality.
