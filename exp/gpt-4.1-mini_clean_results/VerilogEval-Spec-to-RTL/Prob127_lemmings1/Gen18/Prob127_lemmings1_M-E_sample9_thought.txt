Implement the Lemmings FSM by representing the state implicitly as a single bit register that directly encodes the walking direction: 0 for walk left, 1 for walk right. On each clock edge or asynchronous reset, update this state bit by XOR-ing it with the logical OR of bump_left and bump_right inputs. This captures the toggle behavior of switching direction on bump(s). The outputs walk_left and walk_right are generated combinationally as mutually exclusive signals driven directly by the state bit. This approach eliminates explicit state encoding and case statements, reducing logic to a single bit toggle based on bump signals, simplifying the FSM while retaining all specified behavior.
