Implement the Lemmings FSM with a two-bit state encoding representing direction explicitly as separate bits for walk_left and walk_right outputs. Use a synchronous active-high asynchronous reset to initialize to walk_left. The FSM uses a combinational block to decide direction flips based on bump inputs, but rather than encoding direction as a single bit, store the direction as a 2-bit vector with one hot encoding: 2'b01 for left and 2'b10 for right. This encoding makes output assignments direct and mutually exclusive without extra decoding logic. If either bump input is high, toggle the direction state accordingly. This approach simplifies output logic and avoids inverting state bits for output generation, which may help in gate-level optimization and clarity.
