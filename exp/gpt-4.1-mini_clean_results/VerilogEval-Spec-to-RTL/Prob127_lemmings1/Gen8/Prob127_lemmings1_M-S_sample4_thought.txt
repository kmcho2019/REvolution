Represent the walking direction as a single bit state with 0 for walk_left and 1 for walk_right. On each clock edge with async reset, update the state by XORing it with the OR of bump_left and bump_right. This captures the requirement: if bumped on either or both sides, toggle direction; otherwise, hold state. This simplifies the next-state logic to a single boolean expression without a case statement, maintaining the Moore FSM behavior.
