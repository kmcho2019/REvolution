Use a two-bit one-hot encoded state register to represent the walking direction: 2'b01 for walking left and 2'b10 for walking right. On asynchronous reset, initialize the state to walking left. On each clock edge, if bump_left is asserted, move to walking right; if bump_right is asserted, move to walking left; if both are asserted simultaneously, toggle between the two states by swapping bits. Outputs are generated by decoding the one-hot state bits, ensuring a Moore FSM with outputs dependent only on current state.
