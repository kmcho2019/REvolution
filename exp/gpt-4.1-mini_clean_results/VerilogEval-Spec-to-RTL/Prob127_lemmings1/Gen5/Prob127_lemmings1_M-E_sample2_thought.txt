Implement a two-state Moore FSM with explicit state encoding for WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. Use an asynchronous posedge reset to initialize to WALK_LEFT. On each clock edge, if bumped on either side (bump_left or bump_right are high), transition to the opposite walking direction state. Outputs walk_left and walk_right are derived solely from the state (Moore outputs). This design uses a 2-bit state encoding with clear state names for readability and correctness, ensuring direction flips on any bump input.
