To optimize PPA while maintaining clarity and functionality, I will simplify the FSM and outputs by using a single-bit state and deriving walk_right as the inverse of walk_left. This reduces output logic and signal toggling. The next state logic is expressed directly with combinational logic without a case statement for fewer gates and simpler synthesis. The asynchronous reset remains for compliance with the spec. This approach maintains mutually exclusive outputs while slightly improving area and power due to fewer logic elements and removing redundant output logic.
