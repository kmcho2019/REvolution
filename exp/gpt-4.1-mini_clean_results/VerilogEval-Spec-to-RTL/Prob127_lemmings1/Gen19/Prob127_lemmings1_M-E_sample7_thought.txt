Implement the Moore FSM as a single-bit state indicating walking direction, where 0 means walking left and 1 means walking right. Use asynchronous reset to set the initial state to 0 (walk left). On each clock edge, if bump_left or bump_right is asserted, flip the current state to switch directions (walking direction toggles on any bump input). This simplifies next-state logic to a single XOR operation of current state and the OR of bumps. Generate outputs walk_left and walk_right directly from the state bit and its inverse, minimizing logic.
