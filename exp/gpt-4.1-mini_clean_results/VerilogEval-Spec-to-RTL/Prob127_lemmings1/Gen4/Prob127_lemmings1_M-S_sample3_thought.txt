I will simplify the FSM by using a one-bit state register instead of an enum type. The state bit will be 0 for walking left and 1 for walking right. The next state is determined directly from the bump inputs. The outputs walk_left and walk_right will be assigned based on the registered state synchronously. This eliminates the need for typedefs or enumerations and keeps the design fully compatible with plain Verilog, while maintaining Moore FSM behavior with asynchronous reset.
