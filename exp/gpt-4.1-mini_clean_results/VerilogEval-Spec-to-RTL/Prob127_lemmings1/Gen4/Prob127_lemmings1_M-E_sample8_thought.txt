To robustly detect bumps and ensure the Lemming changes direction appropriately, regardless of how short or asynchronous the bump pulses are, I will use input synchronizers combined with edge detection. This will reliably detect the rising edge of bump_left and bump_right synchronized to clk, even if the original bump inputs are asynchronous and/or pulses shorter than a clock cycle.

On every detected rising edge of bump_left or bump_right (meaning a bump just occurred), the FSM will switch states. The FSM itself is a Moore machine with two states (walking left or right). The asynchronous active-high reset immediately initializes the FSM to walking left.

This approach avoids toggling based only on the raw bump inputs sampled at clk and prevents missed bumps due to sampling timing. The direction switches precisely once per bump event.

Internally, the design includes:
- Two 2-stage synchronizers for bump_left and bump_right.
- Rising edge detectors for each synchronized bump signal.
- FSM with two states switching on detected bump edges.
- Asynchronous reset as required.
