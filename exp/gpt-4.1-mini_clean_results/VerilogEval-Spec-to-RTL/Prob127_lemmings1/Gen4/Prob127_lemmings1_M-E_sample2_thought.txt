Implement the FSM with two explicit states (walk_left and walk_right). Use a toggle logic on any bump input: if either bump_left or bump_right (or both) are asserted, the state flips to the opposite direction. The asynchronous reset sets the FSM to walk_left state. For clarity and robustness, separate the state register update and next-state combinational logic. The next state logic XORs the current state with the OR of bump inputs to toggle direction on bumps.
