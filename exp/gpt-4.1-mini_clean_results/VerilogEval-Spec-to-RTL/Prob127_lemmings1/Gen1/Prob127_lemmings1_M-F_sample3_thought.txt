The original design was nearly correct, but to address the simulation mismatches and potential output glitches especially after asynchronous reset, the following improvements are made:

1. Outputs `walk_left` and `walk_right` are declared as `reg` driven by synchronous logic (registered outputs) instead of purely combinational assignments. This avoids glitches on outputs right after reset or state changes.

2. After the asynchronous reset, outputs are explicitly set to correspond to the reset state `WALK_LEFT`.

3. The Moore FSM state is updated asynchronously with `areset` and on `clk` edges as before.

4. Outputs are updated synchronously on the clock to follow the registered state, matching FSM style and avoiding combinational glitches.

5. This ensures output signals change only on clock edges after the state updates, matching typical FSM design and simulation expectations, which prevents timing mismatches on outputs.

This approach provides a glitch-free output, tightly coupled to registered state, and clear reset initialization of outputs.
