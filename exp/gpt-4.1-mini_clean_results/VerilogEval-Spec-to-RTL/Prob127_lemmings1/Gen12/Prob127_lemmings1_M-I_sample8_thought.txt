To optimize for better performance and area, rewrite the next_state logic as a single boolean expression instead of an if-else chain, reducing logic levels and improving synthesis efficiency. Also, simplify the logic to avoid redundant conditions by factoring expressions.

The next state should be:  
- walk right (state=1) if bumped left only OR bumped on both sides and currently walking left (state=0)  
- walk left (state=0) if bumped right only OR bumped on both sides and currently walking right (state=1)  
- Otherwise hold the current state

This can be expressed as:  
next_state = (bump_left & ~bump_right) | (bump_left & bump_right & ~state) | (~bump_left & bump_right & 1'b0 /* no walk right here */)  
More simply:  
next_state = (bump_left & ~bump_right) | (bump_left & bump_right & ~state) | (~bump_left & bump_right & 1'b0)  
Wait, since bump_right only sets walk left (state=0), it is covered if not setting state=1, so better to write the expression for state=1 and invert for state=0.

Hence,  
next_state = (bump_left & ~bump_right) | (bump_left & bump_right & ~state);

When bumped both sides, toggle state, which is implemented by (bump_left & bump_right & ~state) for next_state=1 when currently 0, and if currently 1, the next_state=0 is implicitly hold; so to get toggle, we need to consider that when bumped both sides: next_state = ~state.

So final expression for next_state=1 is:  
next_state = (bump_left & ~bump_right) | (bump_left & bump_right & ~state);

Similarly, next_state=0 in other cases.

To cover the toggling when bump_left & bump_right are both 1, include toggling:  
next_state = (bump_left & ~bump_right) | ((bump_left & bump_right) & ~state);

This covers walking right; else walk left.

Outputs remain complement of state.

Use a single always block for next_state combinational logic and a single always_ff block for state register with async reset.

This approach reduces logic levels, improves timing, and simplifies the code, which should enhance the PPA metrics.
