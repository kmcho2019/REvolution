The existing implementation is clean and minimal, using continuous assignments for purely combinational outputs. To further optimize power and area, we can simplify the keep_driving expression by using a single NOR gate instead of separate NOT and AND gates: (~arrived) & (~gas_tank_empty) is equivalent to the NOR of (arrived | gas_tank_empty). This reduces the number of inverters and gates, slightly improving power and area without changing functionality. The shut_off_computer output is a direct wire, already optimal. Overall, this minor logic simplification can reduce gate count and switching activity.
