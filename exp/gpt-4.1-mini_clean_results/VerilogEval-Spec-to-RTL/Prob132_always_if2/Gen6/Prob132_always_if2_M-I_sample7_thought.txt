The current solution uses a single always block with default assignments to avoid latches, which is clean and correct. To optimize power slightly, I will separate the logic into two independent always blocks—each controlling one output signal. This potentially reduces unnecessary toggling by localizing updates only when relevant inputs change. Additionally, using blocking assignments consistently and maintaining default assignments in each block ensures latch-free combinational logic. This modular approach can assist synthesis tools in optimizing independently and may slightly reduce power without impacting area or timing.
