To reduce unnecessary toggling and thus dynamic power, avoid assigning outputs to zero by default every cycle. Instead, assign outputs only when their enabling conditions are met, and assign zeros otherwise to prevent inferred latches. This can be done using separate always blocks per output, each with default zero assignment and conditional setting. This reduces output switching when inputs remain steady by separating logic and avoiding recomputation of unrelated signals, potentially lowering power while maintaining minimal area and zero timing slack. The logic remains straightforward and combinational.
