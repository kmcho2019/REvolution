To optimize the design for better power and area while maintaining correctness and no latch inference, remove the always block and use continuous assignments with wire outputs instead of reg. This reduces logic complexity, simplifies the synthesis, and lowers power by avoiding unnecessary registers or inferred latches. Since the outputs are purely combinational, using wires and assign statements directly expresses the logic and avoids the overhead of the always block and reg declarations, benefiting PPA. This approach preserves all original logic while improving clarity and synthesis quality.
