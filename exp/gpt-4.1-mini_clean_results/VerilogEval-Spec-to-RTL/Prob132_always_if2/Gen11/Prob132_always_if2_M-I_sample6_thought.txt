The current solution is already functionally correct and avoids latches by default assignments. To optimize for PPA further, especially power and area, I will replace the always block with continuous assignments since outputs can be reg or wire in modern synthesis tools as long as outputs are driven combinationally. Continuous assignments reduce inferred hardware and enable better optimization by synthesis tools. Also, continuous assignments avoid the overhead of always block logic, potentially reducing area and power. Finally, I will explicitly declare outputs as wires and drive them with continuous assignments, simplifying the design while preserving combinational behavior and clean logic.
