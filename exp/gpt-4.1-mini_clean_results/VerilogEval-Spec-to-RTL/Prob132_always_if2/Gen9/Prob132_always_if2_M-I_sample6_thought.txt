The original solution uses a combinational always block with default assignments to avoid latches, which is correct. To optimize for area and power, we can remove the unnecessary use of an always block altogether, since both outputs are simple combinational functions of inputs. Instead, declare them as continuous assignments (using assign statements) driving wires. This reduces logic overhead from the always block infrastructure, avoids reg usage, and results in simpler logic cones. This approach slightly improves area and power by removing register logic and simplifies the code while preserving functionality and timing.
