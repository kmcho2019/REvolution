Refactor the design to use continuous assignments (`assign`) instead of combinational always blocks. This eliminates the need for `reg` types and makes the outputs purely combinational wires derived directly from inputs. This approach simplifies the logic, potentially reduces area and power, and clarifies the intent. We declare outputs as `wire` and use bitwise operations directly. The logic remains the same: `shut_off_computer` is asserted when `cpu_overheated` is high; `keep_driving` is asserted when not `arrived` and the gas tank is not empty.
