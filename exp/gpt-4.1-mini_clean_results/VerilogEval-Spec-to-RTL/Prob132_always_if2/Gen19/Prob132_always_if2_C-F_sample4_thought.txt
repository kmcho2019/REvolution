Fuse the clarity and explicitness of declaring outputs as wire types from Example 2 with the minimalism and straightforward combinational logic of Example 1. This fusion leverages explicit wire output declarations to prevent accidental latch inference and uses simple, direct continuous assignments for clear combinational logic expressions. The outputs depend only on the given inputs, eliminating any unused condition or partial assignments that caused latches originally. This approach ensures the design is clean, optimal for synthesis, minimal in area and power, and easily understandable and maintainable.
