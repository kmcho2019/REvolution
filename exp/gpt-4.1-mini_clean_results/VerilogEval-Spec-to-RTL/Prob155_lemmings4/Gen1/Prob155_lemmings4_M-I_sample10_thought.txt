The original FSM logic and structure correctly model the described Lemming behavior, but the bump logic in walking states can be simplified and clarified for correctness and potential simulation mismatches.

Improvement strategy:

1. Clarify bump direction handling: 
   - On bump_left=1, Lemming should walk right.
   - On bump_right=1, Lemming should walk left.
   - If both bumps occur simultaneously, direction reverses regardless.

The original code merged these into `if (bump_left || bump_right)` which leads to unconditional flip direction but does not distinguish exact bump side.  
Better to explicitly check bump_left and bump_right inputs to determine new direction precisely, matching spec wording.

2. Slight optimization: 
   - Use a single bit `dir` to indicate direction (0 = left, 1 = right) internally, to simplify walking direction changes.
   - This bit is used in walking, falling, and digging states to keep track of original direction.
   - This reduces the number of states by encoding direction as data rather than separate states, resulting in fewer explicit states (this is a classic FSM optimization: convert some states to data bits).
   
3. Adjust fall counter logic for clarity and safety by saturating counter at max (31).

4. Retain asynchronous positive edge reset for `state` and `fall_count`.

5. Use Moore outputs derived from `state` and direction bit.

6. Preserve priority: fall > dig > bump in walking.

7. Ensure bump and dig ignored during falling and digging.

8. Use enumerated states for walking, falling, digging, splat (direction tracked by `dir`).

Resulting states (4 states):

- WALK
- FALL
- DIG
- SPLAT

With internal `dir` bit for direction.

This reduces FSM size, simplifies logic, and clarifies bump handling, improving correctness and power/area since fewer states and simpler logic are needed.

This approach keeps FSM Moore style, and meets all specs.
