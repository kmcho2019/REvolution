To improve the PPA of the original design while preserving functionality, the following strategies are applied:

- Use one-hot encoding for the FSM states (walk, dig, fall, splat) to simplify next-state logic and reduce critical path delay.
- Implement clock gating for the fall_timer register to avoid unnecessary toggling and dynamic power when not falling.
- Reduce combinational logic complexity by explicitly handling bump conditions with simple logic instead of nested if-else.
- Avoid redundant assignments in next-state logic by grouping common cases.
- Retain direction register update only when changed to minimize switching.
- Simplify fall_timer increment saturation logic with minimal conditions.
- Maintain a clean separation between combinational and sequential logic for easier synthesis and optimization.
- Maintain Moore machine style by generating outputs solely from registered state and direction.

These improvements aim to reduce the worst slack (timing) by decreasing logic levels, reduce power by gating the fall timer, and maintain or slightly reduce area by simplifying logic expressions.
