To improve PPA (timing, power, and area), we simplify and optimize the FSM by merging fall_timer increment and saturation logic with fewer comparisons. We use a one-hot style flag (fall_too_long) instead of continuously comparing fall_timer > 20, saving comparator resources. Also, we remove repeated assignments and use smaller counter width since we only care if fall duration exceeds 20 (5 bits to count to 31 is minimal already, but we can avoid saturating at 31 explicitly). For power, we minimize direction toggling by updating direction only once per bump event and ignoring bumps while in fall or dig mode. We also simplify the next state logic by assigning defaults up-front, reducing switching. The reset logic remains asynchronous on posedge areset.

These changes reduce logic complexity in next-state combinational block, keep registers minimal, and simplify fall_timer saturation logic, thus improving timing and power without changing FSM behavior.
