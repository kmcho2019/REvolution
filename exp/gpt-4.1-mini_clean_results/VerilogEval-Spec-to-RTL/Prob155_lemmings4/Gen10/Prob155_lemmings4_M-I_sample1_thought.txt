To improve PPA (timing, power, and area) from the previous design, I will:

- Simplify bump logic to a single toggle signal that directly determines if direction flips, removing redundant bump condition wires.
- Introduce an explicit enable signal for the fall_timer counter to increment only when in FALL mode and the timer is below max, reducing toggling and power.
- Replace the saturating counter logic with a simple enable gated increment, limiting the counter at 31 to reduce glitching and logic depth.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently.
- Combine related next-state assignments to minimize redundant assignments and improve synthesis optimization.
- Keep outputs Moore type with combinational assignments for glitch-free outputs.
- Maintain asynchronous reset and state encoding as-is for simplicity and minimal area impact.

These changes will slightly reduce the critical path length in next-state logic, reduce switching activity on the fall_timer, and simplify direction logic, improving timing slack and power without increasing area.
