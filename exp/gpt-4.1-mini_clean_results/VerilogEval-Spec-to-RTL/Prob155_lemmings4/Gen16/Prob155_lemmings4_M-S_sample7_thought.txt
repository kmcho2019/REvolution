We simplify the FSM by combining mode and direction state into a single reg with clear encodings, reducing separate registers and making next-state logic more compact. We keep the fall timer and splatter flag, but we remove the separate next_fall_too_long signal and directly saturate the timer and check its overflow during landing. We unify direction update logic by using simple conditions without nested ifs. The asynchronous reset initializes the FSM in walking left mode. This simplification reduces state registers and logic while preserving exact FSM behavior and output correctness.
