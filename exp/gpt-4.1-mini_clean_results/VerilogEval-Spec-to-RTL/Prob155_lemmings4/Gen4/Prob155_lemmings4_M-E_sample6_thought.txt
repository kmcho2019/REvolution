Use a combined state encoding that merges both walking direction and mode into one state variable, representing all distinct behaviors explicitly: walking left, walking right, digging left, digging right, falling left, falling right, and splattered. Implement the fall timer as a separate 6-bit saturating counter to track fall duration; increment only when in a falling state. When ground is regained, check fall duration to transition either to splatter or walking in the last falling direction.

Handle asynchronous reset by resetting the FSM state to walking left and clearing the fall timer.

Prioritize transitions according to the problem: falling > digging > bump for walking states. While digging or falling, bump inputs are ignored.

Outputs are decoded directly from the current state (Moore style).
