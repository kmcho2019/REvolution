To improve PPA (Timing, Power, Area) without changing the functional behaviour, I will:

1. Flatten and simplify the next state logic by removing redundant assignments and explicitly encoding priorities with if-else rather than case statements. This can improve combinational logic synthesis and timing.

2. Use one-hot encoding for the FSM modes. One-hot FSMs often synthesize faster and smaller in FPGA or ASIC by reducing decoding logic.

3. Replace the saturating fall_timer increment with a conditional increment and clamp using min(fall_timer+1, 21). Since splat threshold is >20, counting only up to 21 is enough, reducing logic.

4. Register inputs (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) once on clk to reduce glitches and power from asynchronous inputs causing spurious transitions.

5. Remove any redundant resetting of fall_timer to zero in states where it's unused by properly controlling its updates.

These combined changes simplify logic, reduce glitches, and can improve timing and power while maintaining the exact original functionality and asynchronous reset behavior.

The asynchronous reset is kept for the main registers (mode, direction, fall_timer). Outputs remain Moore style.

This improved design should lead to faster clock period, lower power, and possibly smaller area due to simpler logic and one-hot encoding.
