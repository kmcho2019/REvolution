Specifically, I will freeze the fall_timer when not falling by default keeping next_fall_timer equal to the current fall_timer, only incrementing and saturating it during falling. This prevents unnecessary counter resets and switching when in walk or dig modes. This also preserves the fall duration count correctly.

Initializing next_state variables at the start reduces logic duplication. The asynchronous reset remains as required.
