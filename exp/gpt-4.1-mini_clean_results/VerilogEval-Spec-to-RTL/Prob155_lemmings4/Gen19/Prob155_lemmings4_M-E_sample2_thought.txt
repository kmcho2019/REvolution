Implement the Lemmings FSM using separate one-hot state registers for each major mode (walking, digging, falling, splatted) and a dedicated direction flip-flop. Use combinational logic for next-state decoding based on priority: falling has highest priority when ground goes low, then digging when commanded and on ground, else handle bump-induced direction changes during walking. Maintain a 5-bit fall counter incrementing only in fall state, triggering splatter if it exceeds 20 cycles upon landing. The outputs are generated purely from the current state registers, ensuring a Moore machine. The asynchronous reset initializes the FSM to walking left with zero fall counter and no splatter. This modular one-hot approach clearly separates states and simplifies combinational next-state logic, easing debugging and verification.
