I will refactor the FSM by splitting the next-state and next-direction logic into separate always_comb blocks to improve readability and modularity. Instead of packing all logic in a single always_comb with a large case, I will use individual combinational assignments for next_state, next_direction, and next_fall_timer. The fall_timer counter will explicitly use a clock enable derived from the current state, making it more power-aware by avoiding unnecessary increments. I will define the state machine states as localparams instead of typedef enums for simpler synthesis compatibility. The outputs remain Moore-style, but their logic will be expressed in a single assign block for conciseness. Asynchronous reset remains, and the main logic is triggered on posedge clk. This structure clarifies the separation of concerns (state transitions, direction update, timer update), facilitating easier verification and potential optimizations while preserving original behavior.
