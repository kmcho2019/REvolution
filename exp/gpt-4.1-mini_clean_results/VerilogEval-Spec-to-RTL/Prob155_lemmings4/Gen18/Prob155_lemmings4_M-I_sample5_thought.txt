To optimize PPA, I will implement clock enable gating on the fall_timer counter to avoid unnecessary toggling when not in FALL state, which reduces dynamic power. I will simplify direction update logic by using a single expression that considers bumps on either side to reduce combinational gate count, improving performance and area. The fall_timer increment saturation logic will be slightly optimized by using a ternary operation with a maximum count constant. The rest of the FSM will remain Moore type and synchronous with asynchronous reset as specified. Outputs remain Moore with direct decoding from states and direction. This approach maintains functionality but reduces power and potentially improves timing and area.
