To optimize the existing FSM for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will focus on reducing switching activity and simplifying combinational logic without altering functionality.

Key improvements:
- Gate the fall_timer increment by enabling counting only when fall_timer < 31 to reduce unnecessary toggling when saturated.
- Simplify bump logic by combining bump signals into a single 2-bit code representing bump states, allowing a clearer and smaller direction selection logic.
- Use consistent non-blocking assignments and remove redundant assignments to reduce synthesis complexity.
- Retain Moore output logic but express outputs with minimal logic by sharing expressions.
- Keep asynchronous reset intact as required.
- This balances power savings by reducing toggles in fall_timer, reduces combinational logic complexity, and does not increase area.

These tweaks improve timing by reducing the critical path in next-state logic, lower power from fewer transitions, and maintain a compact design.
