For the fall_timer, I will gate its increment only during the falling state, and avoid resetting it unnecessarily during walking or digging states to reduce unnecessary toggling and power consumption. The fall_timer increments only when falling, and saturates at 21 using a simple comparator to avoid complex logic.

All other logic and prioritization remain the same, preserving correctness and functionality, while aiming to improve timing, reduce toggling power, and slightly reduce area by clearer separation of mode and direction.
