To improve the existing design's PPA, I will focus on reducing unnecessary toggling, simplifying combinational logic, and gating the fall_timer increment for power savings, while keeping the same FSM behavior.

Key improvements:
- Introduce an enable signal for the fall_timer to only increment when in FALL state and the timer is less than maximum (31), reducing switching activity.
- Simplify bump direction toggling logic by consolidating bump conditions into fewer signals and conditions, reducing combinational complexity.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently in sequential blocks.
- Keep outputs as pure Moore outputs to avoid glitches.
- Avoid resetting fall_timer every cycle; reset only on mode changes, minimizing transitions.
- Retain asynchronous reset for fast recovery.

These changes keep the design behavior unchanged but reduce switching and logic complexity, thereby improving power and timing margins without area increase.
