Use a single FSM with explicit states encoding both mode and direction, combining walking direction into distinct states. This allows simpler and more explicit state transitions and output decoding.

Define states:
- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- DIG_LEFT
- DIG_RIGHT
- FALL_LEFT
- FALL_RIGHT
- SPLAT (terminal)

Direction is implicit in state, so no separate dir signal needed.

FSM transitions are on clock posedge with asynchronous posedge reset.

Transition logic:
- If ground=0 and currently walking or digging, transition to falling state with same direction.
- If falling and ground=1, check fall_counter: if >20 transition to SPLAT else transition back to walking with same direction.
- While falling, increment fall_counter.
- While not falling, reset fall_counter.
- While walking on ground and dig=1, transition to digging state (same direction).
- While digging on ground, remain digging.
- While digging and ground=0, transition to falling state.
- While walking on ground and bumped:
    - bump_left -> walk_right
    - bump_right -> walk_left
    - both -> switch direction (same as bump_left and bump_right both 1)
- While falling or digging, bumps have no effect.

Outputs are simple:
- walk_left: state==WALK_LEFT
- walk_right: state==WALK_RIGHT
- digging: state is DIG_LEFT or DIG_RIGHT
- aaah: state is FALL_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT
- SPLAT state outputs zero.

This approach makes direction encoding explicit in states and eliminates separate direction signals and complicated direction toggling logic, simplifying correctness and clarity.

Fall counter saturates at max count, updated only in FALL states.

The asynchronous reset sets the FSM to WALK_LEFT and fall counter to zero.
