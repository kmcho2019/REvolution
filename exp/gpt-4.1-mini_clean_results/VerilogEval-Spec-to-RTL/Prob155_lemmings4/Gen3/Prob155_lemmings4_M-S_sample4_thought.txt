Use a simple 3-state Moore FSM with states: WALK, FALL, DIG, plus SPLAT as terminal. Keep a direction bit (0=left,1=right). On reset, walk left.

- On WALK and ground lost → FALL (start fall_counter at 1).
- On WALK and dig=1 and ground=1 → DIG.
- On WALK and bumped:
  - If bump_left=1 → direction = right (1).
  - Else if bump_right=1 → direction = left (0).
  - Else no change.
- On FALL:
  - If ground=0 → fall_counter++
  - If ground=1 and fall_counter > 20 → SPLAT.
  - Else ground=1 and fall_counter ≤ 20 → WALK (same direction).
- On DIG and ground=0 → FALL.
- On DIG and ground=1 → stay DIG.
- In SPLAT, outputs all zero forever.
- Outputs depend only on state and direction (Moore outputs).
- Asynchronous posedge reset to WALK left.
- Simplify by only counting in FALL mode and zeroing fall_counter otherwise.
