Implement the Lemming FSM using a separate walking direction register and a state machine that explicitly encodes the 4 states (walk, dig, fall, splat) with a simple counter for the fall duration. Use a synchronous reset to initialize the state and direction. The direction changes only on bump events when walking. The priority of transitions is fall > dig > bump switches in walking state, and bumps are ignored during falling or digging. Fall timer increments only when falling; upon hitting ground after falling, if the timer exceeded 20, transition to splat. Outputs are determined purely from the current state and direction. This approach uses explicit state encoding with separate direction logic and ensures clear separation of concerns. Asynchronous reset is handled on posedge, consistent with the problem statement.
