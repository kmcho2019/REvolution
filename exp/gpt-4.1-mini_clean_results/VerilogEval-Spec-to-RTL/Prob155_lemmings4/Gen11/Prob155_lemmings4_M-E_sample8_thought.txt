Implement the Lemmings FSM with separate one-hot encoded states for walking left, walking right, falling, digging, and splatted. Use explicit direction states to avoid a separate direction bit. Transition between walk_left and walk_right states on bumps. Falling and digging states are distinct and handle ground and dig inputs as specified. Fall duration is counted with a saturating 5-bit counter to detect splatter condition. The outputs directly map from the one-hot states, preserving the Moore FSM style. Asynchronous reset initializes to walk_left. This approach removes the separate direction register by encoding direction in the walking states, simplifying direction updates on bump events and making state transitions more explicit and easier to verify. It also cleanly separates logic for walking left/right and transitions on bumps without extra logic for direction bit toggling. Digging and falling are independent states that transition on ground/dig signals. Splatted is a terminal state. This design retains the priority order fall > dig > bump in walking states by explicit state transitions, with a saturating fall timer incremented only in falling states. The code uses non-blocking assignments consistently and separates combinational next-state logic from registered state updates for clarity.
