Design a Moore FSM with states representing walking direction (left or right), falling, and digging. Use a direction bit to track walking direction. When bumped, switch direction if walking (not falling or digging). When ground=0, transition to falling, and count fall cycles. If fall count exceeds 20 and ground returns, transition to splattered state (all outputs zero). When ground returns from falling or digging, resume walking in the previous direction. Start digging only when walking on ground and dig=1; digging continues until ground=0, then fall. Outputs depend solely on current state: walk_left or walk_right in walking states, aaah when falling, digging when digging, all zero if splattered. Implement asynchronous positive-edge areset to initialize to walking left state.
