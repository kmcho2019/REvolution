Implement a Moore FSM with these states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING_LEFT, FALLING_RIGHT, DIGGING_LEFT, DIGGING_RIGHT, SPLATTERED.

- The Lemming starts in WALK_LEFT on areset.
- Walking states output walk_left or walk_right = 1.
- Falling states output aaah=1.
- Digging states output digging=1.
- Splattered outputs all zero.
- Walking states can switch direction if bumped (bump_left or bump_right).
- Falling states track a fall counter; if it reaches >20 and ground reappears, transition to SPLATTERED.
- Fall overrides dig and bump.
- Digging starts only from walking on ground when dig=1.
- Digging continues until ground=0, then fall.
- Falling and digging ignore bump and dig inputs.
- On ground falling transitions back to walking in prior direction.
- All state transitions and outputs follow a Moore FSM style: state registers on posedge clk with asynchronous posedge areset.
