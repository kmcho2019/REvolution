To optimize PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we simplify the FSM's combinational logic by splitting next_direction calculation into its own small always block. This reduces logic depth in the main next_state combinational block, potentially improving timing.

We also optimize fall_timer increment logic by gating it with the FALL state and saturating at 31 using a simpler expression, minimizing switching and combinational complexity.

Using explicit reset values and only updating registers when needed reduces unnecessary toggling, helping power.

Outputs remain simple Moore signals.

Overall, this structural refinement reduces critical path complexity and switching activity with minimal code changes, aiming for better timing slack, lower power, and slight area improvement.
