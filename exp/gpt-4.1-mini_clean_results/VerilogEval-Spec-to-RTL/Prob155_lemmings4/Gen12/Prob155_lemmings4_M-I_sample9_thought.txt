To optimize the design for better power and timing (PPA), I will implement explicit gating of the fall_timer counter: incrementing it only when the mode is FALL, otherwise hold it at zero. This prevents unnecessary toggling of fall_timer bits outside falling mode, reducing power. Also, I will inline the direction change logic to remove the function call, replacing it with combinational assignments to simplify synthesis and potentially improve timing. Additionally, I will reduce redundant bump signals by keeping only the minimal required signals for direction logic, thus decreasing switching activity and area slightly. These changes maintain the same functional correctness and Moore FSM style but reduce unnecessary activity and simplify logic for better overall PPA.
