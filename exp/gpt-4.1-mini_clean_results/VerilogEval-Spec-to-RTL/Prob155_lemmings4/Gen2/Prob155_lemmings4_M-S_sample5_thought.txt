I will simplify the FSM by encoding the walking direction directly into the state instead of separately tracking direction_before_fall. The FSM states will be:

- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- DIG_LEFT (digging while originally walking left)
- DIG_RIGHT
- FALL (with direction saved as a separate signal)
- SPLATTERED

To simplify direction tracking during fall, I'll store direction in a 1-bit register whenever the Lemming enters falling, allowing simpler transitions back after falling.

Also, the fall counter increments only in FALL state and resets otherwise.

State transitions and output logic follow the problem specification and precedence rules (fall > dig > bump).

This approach removes the need for complex logic handling direction_before_fall in various states, reduces conditional branches, and keeps the FSM and outputs Moore-type with minimal logic.

Asynchronous reset places the Lemming in WALK_LEFT state, with fall counter reset and direction set accordingly.

All outputs derive directly from the current state.

This simplified encoding will maintain full functionality while being easier to follow and verify.
