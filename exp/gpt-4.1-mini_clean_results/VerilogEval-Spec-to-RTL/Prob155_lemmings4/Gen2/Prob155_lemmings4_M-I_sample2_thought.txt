The main functional fix is to prevent direction switching on bumps occurring in the same cycle as ground transitions (ground going from 1→0 or 0→1). According to the problem, bumps during these transitions or while falling or digging do not affect direction.

To achieve this, we can:

1. Register the previous ground signal (ground_d) to detect edges (fall start or ground reappearance).
2. On falling start (ground_d=1 and ground=0) or ground reappearance (ground_d=0 and ground=1), ignore bumps for direction switching in that cycle.
3. Also ignore bumps during falling or digging modes.
4. Only allow direction switching on bumps in walk mode and stable ground (no transitions in this cycle).

This prevents direction toggles caused by bump signals coincident with ground changes.
