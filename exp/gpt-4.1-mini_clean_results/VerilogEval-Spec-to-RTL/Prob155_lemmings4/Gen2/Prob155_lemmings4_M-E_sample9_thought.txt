To provide a fresh architectural approach, I will separate concerns more explicitly and store direction as its own state variable, distinct from the behavior state (walking left/right, falling, digging, splattered).

I will use two separate state variables:
- A "mode" state indicating what the Lemming is doing: WALK, FALL, DIG, SPLATTERED.
- A direction bit indicating left (0) or right (1).

This clean separation clarifies logic:
- Direction changes only when walking and bumped.
- Direction is preserved during fall and dig.
- On fall landing, direction is preserved.
- Dig is only entered if on ground and walking.
- Fall has highest precedence, then dig, then bump.
- Splattered occurs on landing if fall duration exceeded 20 cycles.

I will implement the FSM with synchronous reset (reset asserted asynchronously, synchronous clear). The mode and direction are updated each cycle based on inputs, ground, bumps, dig, and fall count.

The outputs are Moore style from mode and direction.

Fall counter increments only in FALL mode, resets otherwise.

This approach improves clarity, maintainability, and correctness by distinctly tracking direction separately and focusing mode on current activity.
