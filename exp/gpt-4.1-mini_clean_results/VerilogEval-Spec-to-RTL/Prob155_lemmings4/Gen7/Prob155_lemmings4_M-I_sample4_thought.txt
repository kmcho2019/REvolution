To improve the design for better PPA (Timing, Power, Area), focus on reducing combinational logic complexity and switching activity without changing functionality.

Key improvements:
- Simplify bump handling by computing a single 'bump' signal and direction toggle condition once, reducing duplicated logic.
- Replace the saturating increment of fall_timer with a conditional increment without saturation logic delay by limiting counter maximum logically in next_fall_timer assignment.
- Since fall_timer only increments in FALL state, gate its increment explicitly and reset to zero elsewhere to reduce switching.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
- Add a single always_comb block for bump logic to simplify next_state logic.
- Keep asynchronous reset as is, since it's required.
- Keep outputs as Moore outputs derived from mode and direction to keep glitch-free outputs.
- Overall, simplify next-state combinational logic by factoring out common assignments and intermediate signals to reduce critical path.

This should slightly improve timing slack and reduce power by less toggling and simpler logic, while keeping area minimal.
