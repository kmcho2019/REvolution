To improve PPA, the following optimizations are implemented: 

- Direction register updates only when the direction actually changes, preventing unnecessary toggles and saving dynamic power.
- Fall timer increment gated explicitly by mode==FALL to reduce switching activity.
- Use a combinational flag to indicate splat condition (fall_timer > 20) instead of a direct > comparison each cycle; this subtle change can help synthesis tools optimize better.
- Simplify fall_timer saturation logic by limiting maximum count with a conditional increment, reducing comparator complexity.
- Reorder bump handling logic so that when both bump_left and bump_right are 1, the direction toggles; else bump_left sets direction right; else bump_right sets direction left; else direction unchanged. This explicit priority may help synthesis.
- Maintain clear separation of state and outputs for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
These changes target small improvements in timing (by simplifying combinational logic) and power (by reducing toggling) while keeping area minimal and not changing FSM behavior.
