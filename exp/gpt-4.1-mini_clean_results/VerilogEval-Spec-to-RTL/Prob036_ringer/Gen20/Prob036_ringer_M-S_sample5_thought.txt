The original design is already minimal and straightforward, implementing the logic directly with AND and NOT gates. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive and depend only on ring and vibrate_mode, any further simplification risks losing clarity or correctness. However, I can express the outputs using a single always_comb block with conditional assignments, which might be considered simpler in terms of control flow, although functionally equivalent. This removes explicit AND/NOT expressions and uses conditional logic to assign outputs, which might be preferred in some coding styles.
