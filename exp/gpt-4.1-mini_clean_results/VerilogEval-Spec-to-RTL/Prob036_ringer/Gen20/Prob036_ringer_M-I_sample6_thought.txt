The original design is already minimal and efficient, consisting of two simple AND gates and an inverter. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive and depend solely on combinational logic, there is no simpler or smaller implementation possible in terms of logic gates. To further improve power, one could consider disabling the outputs when ring is low, which is already implicitly handled by ANDing with ring. Also, timing and area are optimal for such a straightforward logic function. Therefore, no structural change is necessary. However, to make the design more robust and potentially aid synthesis tools, the outputs can be combined into a single vector and then assigned separately, which sometimes helps with optimization in synthesis flows. This is a minor stylistic change rather than a PPA gain, but it may improve readability and clarity without impacting functionality or resource usage.
