To provide a novel architectural approach, I will use an explicit priority encoder style logic with intermediate signals. I will define two intermediate signals: call_active = ring (to indicate an incoming call) and vibrate_active = vibrate_mode & call_active (to indicate the motor should vibrate when ringing). The ringer will be active when there is a call but vibrate mode is not active, and the motor will be active only if vibrate mode is active during ringing. This clearly separates the control signals and makes the logic more explicit, aiding readability and maintainability without changing behavior.
