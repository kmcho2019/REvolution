Since the outputs ringer and motor are mutually exclusive and depend on ring and vibrate_mode inputs, we can simplify the design by using one output to directly indicate the selected mode and derive the other by complementing it when ring is asserted. This eliminates the need for two separate conditional assignments. Specifically, ringer can be assigned as ring AND NOT vibrate_mode, and motor as ring AND vibrate_mode, but since these are complementary when ring is high, we can directly assign motor as ring AND vibrate_mode and ringer as ring AND (NOT vibrate_mode), keeping logic minimal and clear.
