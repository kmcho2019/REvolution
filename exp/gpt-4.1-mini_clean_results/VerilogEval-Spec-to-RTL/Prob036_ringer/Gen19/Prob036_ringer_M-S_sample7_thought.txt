Since only one of the outputs (ringer or motor) should be active at a time based on the vibrate_mode when ring is active, and both are zero when ring is zero, I can implement ringer as simply ring AND NOT vibrate_mode, and motor as the complement case ring AND vibrate_mode. This is already minimal and simple. To further simplify, I can output motor directly as ring & vibrate_mode and ringer as ring & ~vibrate_mode, which is as simple as possible without intermediate signals or extra logic.
