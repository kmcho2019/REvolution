I will implement the module using a small finite state machine (FSM) to control the outputs, providing a distinct architectural approach. The FSM will have an idle state where both outputs are off, and an active state triggered by the ring input. In the active state, the FSM will check the vibrate_mode input and selectively enable either the motor or the ringer. This stateful design allows potential future extensions such as timed ringing or motor activation, and differentiates from purely combinational approaches. Outputs will be registered for glitch-free transitions, improving reliability in synchronous designs at the cost of a one-clock-cycle latency.
