I will implement the module using a small finite state machine (FSM) style approach even though the problem is combinational. By encoding the output logic inside a combinational always block triggered by the inputs, I demonstrate a different style using procedural assignments rather than continuous assignments. This approach could facilitate future extension, such as adding ringing patterns or motor control delays. The outputs will be assigned exclusively in a combinational always block, ensuring mutual exclusion between ringer and motor and meeting the original requirements.
