An alternative approach is to use a simple priority encoder style logic that first checks if ring is active. If ring is low, both outputs are zero. If ring is active, then vibrate_mode determines which output is set. Instead of explicitly using AND gates with inverted signals, I will use conditional assignments with an always_comb block to clearly express the behavior and ensure mutual exclusivity. This approach may facilitate easier extension if the control logic becomes more complex, and provides a clear readable behavior description, albeit with slightly more synthesis overhead. The mutually exclusive outputs are guaranteed by a simple if-else condition.
