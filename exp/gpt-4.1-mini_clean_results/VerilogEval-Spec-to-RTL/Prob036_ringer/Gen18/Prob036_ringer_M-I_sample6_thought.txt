Since the original design is already minimal and optimized with simple combinational logic, we can further optimize the power and area by using a single output with conditional assignment and then decoding it to outputs. This reduces redundant gating logic. Also, we can use a single signal for 'active' (ring) and then output ringer or motor depending on vibrate_mode, ensuring only one output is driven and reducing switching. This slight logic sharing and reduction in total gates can improve area and power. The implementation remains purely combinational with zero timing overhead.
