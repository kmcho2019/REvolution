I will use a single 2-to-1 multiplexer (mux) controlled by vibrate_mode to select which output to activate when ring is high. Specifically, when ring is asserted, the module outputs a 2-bit vector where one bit represents the ringer and the other the motor; the mux picks the motor when vibrate_mode=1 and the ringer when vibrate_mode=0, ensuring mutual exclusivity.

This approach encodes the outputs as a vector and then extracts them, allowing a compact and parameterizable selection structure that cleanly separates output logic from control logic. It emphasizes modularity and showcases the use of vector and mux assignment in Verilog.

If ring is low, both outputs are zero. If ring is high, the mux selects one of the two active outputs according to vibrate_mode.
