Fuse the clarity and explicit mutual exclusivity of the always @(*) procedural style with the minimal combinational logic expressions from continuous assignments to create a clean, efficient design. 

The fusion strategy is to use a combinational always block with blocking assignments for clear, explicit control flow and future extensibility, while implementing the logic internally using simple Boolean expressions (ring & ~vibrate_mode for ringer, ring & vibrate_mode for motor). This avoids nested if-else chains, making the code more compact and readable, and it ensures mutual exclusivity and zero outputs when ring is low.

This approach combines the clarity and easy extendability of the always block style with the simplicity and minimal logic of Boolean expressions, improving readability and maintainability without sacrificing synthesis efficiency or power/area metrics.
