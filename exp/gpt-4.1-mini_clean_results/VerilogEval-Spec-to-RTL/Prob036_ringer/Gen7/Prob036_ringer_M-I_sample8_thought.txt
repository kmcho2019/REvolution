The initial design is already minimal and clean, implementing the logic with two simple AND gates and one inverter, which is optimal for this straightforward function. To slightly improve power and area, I will restructure the logic as a 2-to-1 multiplexer controlled by vibrate_mode, which chooses between ringer and motor. Since only one output can be active at a time when ring is asserted, encoding it as a mux might reduce the total gates by sharing logic and inverter implicitly, potentially lowering gate count and power usage. The ringer and motor outputs can then be driven from the mux output and its complement gated by ring, maintaining mutual exclusivity and proper function. This approach keeps logic minimal and may give a modest PPA improvement.
