Refactor the design to use continuous assignment statements (`assign`) for both outputs instead of an always block with reg outputs. Since the logic is purely combinational, we can declare outputs as wires and assign them directly with bitwise operations:

- `ringer` is on when `ring` is active and `vibrate_mode` is inactive.
- `motor` is on when both `ring` and `vibrate_mode` are active.

This removes the always block and reg declarations, simplifying the code, potentially reducing synthesis overhead and improving readability and area/power efficiency.
