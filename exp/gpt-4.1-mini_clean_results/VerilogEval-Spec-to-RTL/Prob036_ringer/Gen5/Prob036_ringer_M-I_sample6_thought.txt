To optimize for performance, power, and area, we will replace the combinational always block with continuous assignments using simple logic expressions. This approach removes the need for reg-type outputs, instead using wire outputs that represent pure combinational logic, minimizing resource usage.

The logic is straightforward:
- ringer = ring AND NOT vibrate_mode
- motor = ring AND vibrate_mode

This simplification reduces gate complexity, eliminates branching, and should reduce switching activity due to direct and minimal logic, improving power. Area is reduced by removing flip-flop inference and nested control structures, and performance benefits from reduced gate delays.

Thus, outputs will be declared as wire and driven by assign statements directly, achieving minimal, clean, and synthesizable design.
