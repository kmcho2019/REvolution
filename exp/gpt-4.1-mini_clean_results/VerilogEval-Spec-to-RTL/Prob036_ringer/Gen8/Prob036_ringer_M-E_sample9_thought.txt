An alternative approach is to treat the ringer and motor outputs as one-hot encoded states activated by a 2-to-1 multiplexer controlled by vibrate_mode. When ring is asserted, a 2-bit vector is selected: if vibrate_mode=0, output "10" (ringer active, motor inactive); if vibrate_mode=1, output "01" (ringer inactive, motor active). If ring is low, output "00" (both inactive). This uses vector-based logic and conditional assignment to implement mutually exclusive outputs with a simple multiplexer.
