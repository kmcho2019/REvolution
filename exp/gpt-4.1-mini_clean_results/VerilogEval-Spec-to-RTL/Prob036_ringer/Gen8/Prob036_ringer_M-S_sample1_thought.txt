Since the outputs are purely combinational functions of the inputs, we can simplify the design by using continuous assign statements with wire outputs instead of an always block and reg outputs. This reduces code complexity and makes the functionality explicit and minimal.

Specifically:
- Declare ringer and motor as wire outputs.
- Assign ringer to ring AND NOT vibrate_mode.
- Assign motor to ring AND vibrate_mode.
- This inherently ensures that only one of ringer or motor is active when ring is high, and both are zero when ring is low.

This simplification maintains full functionality with fewer lines and simpler structure.
