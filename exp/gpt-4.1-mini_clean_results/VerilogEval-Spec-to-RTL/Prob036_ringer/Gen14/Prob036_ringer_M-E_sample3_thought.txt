To provide a novel architectural idea, I will implement the logic using continuous assignments without any procedural blocks, relying purely on combinational logic expressions.

The key points are:
- Use continuous assignment (`assign`) for outputs directly from inputs for clarity and minimalism.
- Make outputs wires instead of regs since no procedural assignments are needed.
- Ensure mutual exclusivity by using the vibrate_mode signal directly for motor, and its complement (ANDed with ring) for the ringer.
- When ring=0, both outputs are zero.
- This approach is fully synthesizable and succinct, minimizing the amount of code and avoiding the use of reg or always blocks.

This style leverages the direct combinational nature of the problem and is well-suited for synthesis, with very low area and power footprints, and is easy to understand and verify.
