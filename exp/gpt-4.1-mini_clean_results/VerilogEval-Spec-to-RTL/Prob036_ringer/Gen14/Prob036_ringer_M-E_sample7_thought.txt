Implement a one-hot encoding approach using a 2-bit wire to represent the outputs ringer and motor. Use a combinational always block that sets this one-hot signal based on the ring and vibrate_mode inputs. This approach explicitly encodes the mutually exclusive outputs and decouples the logic from direct AND gates, potentially making the design more scalable if more modes or outputs are added later. The output bits are then assigned from this one-hot vector, ensuring exactly one output is active when ring is high, and none when ring is low.
