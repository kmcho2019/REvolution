Implement the logic purely with continuous assignments using bitwise operators to drive the outputs directly from inputs without any procedural blocks. This approach uses minimal hardware: 

- ringer is active only when ring is high and vibrate_mode is low, so ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode
- motor is active only when ring is high and vibrate_mode is high, so motor = ring & vibrate_mode

This guarantees mutual exclusivity and keeps the combinational logic concise and efficient. Continuous assignments simplify the design by avoiding registers or always blocks.
