Refactor the design to use direct continuous assignments for `ringer` and `motor` outputs without any intermediate registers or always blocks. This approach simplifies the logic by expressing each output as a combinational expression directly dependent on the inputs. It ensures mutual exclusivity by leveraging the input conditions and inherently prevents both outputs being active simultaneously, as `ringer` is active when `ring` is high and `vibrate_mode` is low, while `motor` is active when both `ring` and `vibrate_mode` are high. This removes the intermediate 2-bit register, reducing switching activity and area while maintaining clear, combinational logic.
