Implement the module using a single always_comb block and a case statement that covers all possible input combinations of ring and vibrate_mode. The outputs ringer and motor are declared as reg and assigned inside the always block. This approach emphasizes procedural style logic modeling rather than continuous assignment, suitable for combinational logic with mutually exclusive outputs. It also prepares the design for potential future extensions such as additional modes or prioritized outputs. The case statement sets both outputs to zero when ring is low, and activates either ringer or motor when ring is high depending on vibrate_mode.
