The original solution uses minimal and straightforward logic, but we can slightly optimize power by gating outputs with the ring signal first. This reduces unnecessary toggling on the vibrate_mode input when ring=0, as both ringer and motor will be zero directly.

Since the outputs are only meaningful when ring=1, this gating prevents the combinational logic related to vibrate_mode from switching unnecessarily.

This small adjustment can reduce dynamic power without adding area overhead or increasing delay, maintaining zero latency and minimal gate count.

The optimized design is functionally identical, with motor and ringer outputs driven only when ring is asserted.
