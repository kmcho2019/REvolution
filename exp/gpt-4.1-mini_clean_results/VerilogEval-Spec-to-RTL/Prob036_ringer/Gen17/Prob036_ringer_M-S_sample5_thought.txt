The design can be simplified by combining the logic for ringer and motor into one expression for motor and deriving ringer as the complement (when ring is active). Since only one output can be active during ring, and none if no ring, we can assign motor = ring & vibrate_mode, and ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode directly as before, but since these are minimal already, the best simplification is to inline everything in a single assign statement using conditional operator for clarity while maintaining minimal logic.
