To optimize the module for better PPA, I will implement the mutual exclusivity using a 2-to-1 multiplexer pattern, which can reduce logic gates by sharing logic rather than duplicating AND gates with inversions. Specifically, the output can be selected based on vibrate_mode: when ring is asserted, ringer is enabled if vibrate_mode=0; otherwise motor is enabled. Using this, the outputs ringer and motor become mutually exclusive signals from a single ring input, controlled by vibrate_mode as a selector. This should slightly reduce gate count, marginally improving area and power, while keeping zero timing overhead as the logic remains combinational.
