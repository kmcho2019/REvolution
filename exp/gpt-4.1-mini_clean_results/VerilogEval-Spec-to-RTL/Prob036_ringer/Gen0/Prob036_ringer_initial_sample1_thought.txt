The outputs depend on the two inputs, ring and vibrate_mode. If ring is 0 (no incoming call), both outputs are 0. When ring is 1, if vibrate_mode is 1, the motor is on and the ringer is off; if vibrate_mode is 0, the ringer is on and the motor is off. Thus, ringer = ring AND NOT vibrate_mode; motor = ring AND vibrate_mode.
