To optimize the PPA of the TopModule controlling the ringer and motor, the existing minimal combinational logic is already highly efficient. However, a potential improvement involves consolidating the outputs into a single encoded signal internally, then decoding to outputs. This can help synthesis tools identify the logic as a 2-to-1 mux, potentially improving area and power by sharing logic.

Thus, define an internal single-bit signal `active` to indicate if ringing is needed. Then, based on vibrate_mode, select which output is active.

This approach uses a simple combinational always block or continuous assignment with a mux-like structure, improving clarity and potentially enabling better tool-level mux recognition and resource sharing.

The logic remains fully combinational and glitch-free with minimal gate count.

This slight restructuring can marginally improve power by reducing redundant logic and potentially minimize area by sharing decoding logic, though gains will be small due to the problem simplicity.
