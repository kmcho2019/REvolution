The existing implementation is already minimal and optimal combinational logic. However, we can slightly reduce switching activity by outputting ringer and motor as a single 1-bit output signal that selects either the ringer or motor function, followed by decoding outside if this were a larger design. Since the problem constraints require two separate outputs, the only minor optimization is to reduce redundant logic by assigning motor as vibrate_mode & ring, and ringer as ring & ~vibrate_mode, which is what is done already. So the design is already optimal. No further PPA improvements are practical here without changing the interface or external system-level behavior. Hence, the best approach is to keep the simple assign statements as is.
