I will implement the TopModule using continuous assign statements with a priority encoded output to drive a 2-bit internal signal representing the ringer and motor states. This 2-bit signal uses one-hot encoding where bit0 corresponds to ringer and bit1 corresponds to motor. The final outputs ringer and motor are driven from this signal. This method ensures mutual exclusivity naturally and uses simple combinational logic without procedural blocks. Additionally, this approach keeps outputs as wires, simplifying the design and potentially reducing synthesis resources.
