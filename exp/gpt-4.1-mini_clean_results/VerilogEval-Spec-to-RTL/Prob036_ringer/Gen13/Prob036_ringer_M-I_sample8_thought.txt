The initial solution already uses minimal combinational logic with two AND gates and one inverter, which is close to optimal for area and power. To further optimize power and area, I will replace the two AND gates and the inverter with a single 2-to-1 multiplexer structure driven by vibrate_mode, gating the ringer and motor outputs directly using ring as an enable. This approach eliminates the explicit inverter and reduces the gate count slightly by sharing logic. It also maintains mutual exclusivity and the correct output behavior. Since ring enables either ringer or motor, the logic can be implemented efficiently using continuous assignments that act as multiplexers, minimizing transistor count and switching activity.
