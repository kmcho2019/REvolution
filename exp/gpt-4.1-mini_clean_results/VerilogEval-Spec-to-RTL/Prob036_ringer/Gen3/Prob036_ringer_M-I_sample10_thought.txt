The original implementation uses an always block with explicit if-else statements and reg outputs. Since the logic is purely combinational and simple, we can optimize for area and power by using continuous assign statements and wire outputs. This reduces flip-flops, latch risk, and unnecessary logic complexity. The direct boolean expressions ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode and motor = ring & vibrate_mode are minimal and produce non-overlapping outputs. This eliminates conditional statements and improves synthesis optimization potential, leading to lower power and smaller area. Hence, the improvement is a cleaner, fully combinational, assign-based design with wire outputs.
