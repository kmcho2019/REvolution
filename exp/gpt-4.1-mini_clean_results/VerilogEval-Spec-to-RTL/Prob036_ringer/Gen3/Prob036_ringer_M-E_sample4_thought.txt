Implement the design using a single 2-bit encoded output that represents the current state: 00 for no ring, 01 for ringer on, and 10 for motor on. Then decode this state into the two one-bit outputs. This approach abstracts the output selection into a state machine with only combinational logic. It emphasizes modularity and clarity by separating the decision from the output signals, and can easily be extended if more modes are added.
