However, a slight improvement can be made by reducing the number of logic gates from two AND gates plus one inverter (used for ~vibrate_mode) to just one XOR gate and one AND gate.

- ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode
- motor = ring & vibrate_mode

This can be expressed using XOR and AND as:
- ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode = ring & (vibrate_mode ^ 1)
- motor = ring & vibrate_mode

Using XOR instead of NOT gate might reduce gate count since XOR is a common primitive and can be more area/power efficient than a separate inverter and AND when implemented in certain technologies.

Hence, 
- ringer = ring & (vibrate_mode ^ 1)
- motor = ring & vibrate_mode

- Replace NOT gate with XOR for vibrate_mode inversion
