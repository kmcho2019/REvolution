Fuse the best ideas by maintaining combinational clarity and explicit mutual exclusivity using a concise always @(*) block, but optimize further by implementing one-hot encoding on a 2-bit output vector internally. Then, drive individual output ports (ringer and motor) via continuous assigns from this encoded vector. This hybrid approach combines the clarity and glitch-free updates of the always block, the resource efficiency of one-hot encoding to reduce wiring and gate count, and the ease of pin assignments through continuous assigns. It also explicitly ensures that only one output can be active at a time or both off if no ring. Declaring the internal signal as reg supports procedural assignments, while the top-level ports remain wires driven by assign, optimizing synthesis and clarity.
