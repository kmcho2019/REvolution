Since ringer and motor are mutually exclusive and both depend on ring, we can simplify by making one output a direct function and the other its complement when ring is high. Specifically, assign ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode and motor = ring & vibrate_mode is already minimal. Alternatively, assign ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode and motor = ring & vibrate_mode is the simplest form. Hence, no simpler or more minimal solution exists; but using a single always_comb block with a simple if-else can slightly reduce the number of assign statements and be stylistically simpler.
