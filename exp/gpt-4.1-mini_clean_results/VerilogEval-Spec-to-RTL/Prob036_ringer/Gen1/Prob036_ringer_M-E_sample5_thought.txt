This implementation uses a one-hot multiplexer style logic controlled by the inputs. First, it masks the 'ring' signal to determine whether the phone should respond at all. Then it generates a 2-bit select signal from vibrate_mode to choose between turning on the motor or the ringer using simple logical conditions. This approach uses OR and AND gates combined with inversion but arranged to emphasize priority encoding: when ringing, motor is enabled if vibrate_mode=1, else ringer is enabled, and when not ringing both outputs are off. This modularizes control signals separately from the outputs.
