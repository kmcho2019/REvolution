The original design is already minimal and optimal for the given logic, with only two simple AND gates and an inverter implied by the negation of vibrate_mode. To optimize further for power, we can reduce the number of inverters by using an XOR gate, which also ensures mutual exclusivity. By using ring & (vibrate_mode XOR 1) for ringer and ring & vibrate_mode for motor, we avoid an explicit NOT gate. This may slightly reduce area and power by eliminating the inverter gate and potential glitching on ~vibrate_mode. The functional behavior remains identical, ensuring that exactly one output is active when ring is asserted.
