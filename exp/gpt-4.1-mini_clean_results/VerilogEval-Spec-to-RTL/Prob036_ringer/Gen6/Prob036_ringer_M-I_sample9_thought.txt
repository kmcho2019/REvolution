The original implementation is already minimal, using a concise conditional assignment with a concatenation to produce mutually exclusive outputs. Since the PPA metrics are very good with zero timing slack and low power/area, there is limited room for improvement in logic complexity.

However, an alternative style uses explicit boolean expressions for ringer and motor outputs, which may make synthesis tools generate even simpler gates (AND, NOT) instead of a conditional operator. This might marginally improve area and power by avoiding multiplexers in the hardware implementation of the ternary operator.

The new approach:
- ringer is high only when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=0.
- motor is high only when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=1.
- Both outputs default to 0 when ring=0.

This yields straightforward AND/NOT logic:
  ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode;
  motor = ring & vibrate_mode;

This is logically equivalent, simpler, and can yield minimal gate count in synthesis.

Hence, the improvement strategy is to replace the conditional operator with boolean AND/NOT expressions to potentially reduce gate count and improve area and power while maintaining the same functionality and timing.
