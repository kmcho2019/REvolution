Implement the design using simple combinational logic with individual assign statements for ringer and motor. Use the fact that ringer should be on only when ring is asserted and vibrate_mode is not set, and motor should be on only when ring and vibrate_mode are both asserted. This approach uses straightforward AND and NOT gates to produce outputs. It explicitly separates the logic for ringer and motor, improving readability for beginners while ensuring mutual exclusivity.

Logic:
- ringer = ring AND (NOT vibrate_mode)
- motor = ring AND vibrate_mode

This guarantees that if ring is 0, both outputs are 0. If ring is 1, exactly one of ringer or motor will be 1, depending on vibrate_mode.
