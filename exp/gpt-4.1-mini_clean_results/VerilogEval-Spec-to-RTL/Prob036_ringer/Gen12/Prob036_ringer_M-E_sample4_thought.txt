Instead of using simple combinational logic directly on the inputs, I will implement a small synchronous design using a clock and registers to demonstrate state-based control. The ring and vibrate_mode inputs will be sampled into registers on the rising edge of a clock. Then, based on the registered inputs, outputs will be driven by a combinational block ensuring only one output is active. This approach mimics a simple control FSM in hardware and can be extended for future features, making it architecturally different from purely combinational logic.
