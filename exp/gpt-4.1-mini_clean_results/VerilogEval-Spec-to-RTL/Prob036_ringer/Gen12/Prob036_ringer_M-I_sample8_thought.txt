To marginally improve area and power, replace two separate AND gates with an inverter by a 2-to-1 multiplexer controlled by vibrate_mode that selects which output (ringer or motor) is activated by ring. This reduces the logic to one inverter and one 2:1 mux instead of two AND gates plus an inverter. This can slightly reduce gate count and power by avoiding redundant logic. The functionality remains the same: when ring=1, only ringer or motor is 1 depending on vibrate_mode; otherwise both outputs are 0.
