To fuse the best of both examples, I will maintain clear modularity with explicit NotGate and AndGate primitives for clarity, reusability, and structural intent. However, I will simplify their implementation to continuous assign statements without optional delays or additional parameters for synthesis friendliness and minimal overhead. This approach avoids procedural blocks, making it easy for synthesis tools to optimize while preserving hierarchy and readability. The TopModule will instantiate these gates structurally, making the bubble on in2 explicit, but the gates themselves will be minimal and synthesis-efficient. This balances clarity, modular design, and efficient hardware mapping, and also allows easy verification and potential reuse of the gate modules in other designs.
