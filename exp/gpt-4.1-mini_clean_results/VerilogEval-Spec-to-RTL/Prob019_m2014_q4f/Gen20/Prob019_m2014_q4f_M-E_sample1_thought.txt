Implement the TopModule using a single always_comb block with internal signals to explicitly compute the inverted in2 and then combine it with in1 via an AND operation. This approach uses behavioral RTL style with clear intermediate signals, aiding readability and ease of debugging while allowing synthesis tools to optimize away the intermediate inversion if desired. This removes the need for separate gate modules, resulting in a simpler hierarchy and potentially lower area and power, while preserving explicit logic structure and facilitating testbench-driven verification.
