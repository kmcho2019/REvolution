The original design is already minimal and straightforward: out = in1 & ~in2. However, it defines NotGate and AndGate modules without using them in TopModule, which adds unnecessary code size and possible synthesis overhead.

To optimize for area and clarity, I will remove the unused gate modules and implement TopModule purely as a single continuous assignment. This eliminates extra hierarchy and potential overhead.

This approach maintains optimal performance and power, while minimizing area by relying on the synthesis tool to implement the AND gate with bubble (input inversion) efficiently.

Hence, the improvement is to remove unused modules and keep the TopModule minimal and clean.
