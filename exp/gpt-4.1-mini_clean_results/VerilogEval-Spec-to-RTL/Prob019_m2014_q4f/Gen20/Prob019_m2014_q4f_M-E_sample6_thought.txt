Implement the TopModule using behavioral Verilog with an always_comb block to compute the output. The always_comb block provides a clear sequential logic style that is synthesizable and offers easy extensibility. Inside the block, assign 'out' to the logical AND of 'in1' and the negation of 'in2', clearly showing the bubble on 'in2'. This approach avoids structural gate instantiations and uses behavioral style, improving readability while maintaining synthesis friendliness.
