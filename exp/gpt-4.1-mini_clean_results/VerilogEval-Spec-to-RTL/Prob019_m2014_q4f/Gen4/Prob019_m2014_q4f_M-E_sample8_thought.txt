Implement the circuit using gate-level primitives to explicitly instantiate a NAND gate followed by an inverter. Since the AND gate with a bubble on the in2 input is logically equivalent to in1 AND (NOT in2), we can realize it as NAND of in1 and in2, then invert the NAND output to get the AND with inverted in2 input. This structural approach demonstrates using basic gates to achieve the function.
