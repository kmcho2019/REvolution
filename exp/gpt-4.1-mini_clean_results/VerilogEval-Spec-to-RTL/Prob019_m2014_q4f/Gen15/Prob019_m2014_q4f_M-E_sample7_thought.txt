Instead of using a simple continuous assignment, implement the TopModule in a purely structural style by instantiating basic gate modules to model the logic explicitly. Create separate modules for the inverter and the AND gate and connect them structurally. This approach highlights modularity and a clear gate-level view, which is useful for understanding and verifying gate-level timing and power. It also aligns well with gate-level simulation and synthesis tools expecting explicit gate primitives. The inverter module will invert in2, and its output feeds one input of the AND gate along with in1 to produce the output. This style enhances reusability of gate modules and enables easy parameterization or replacement with technology-specific gates.
