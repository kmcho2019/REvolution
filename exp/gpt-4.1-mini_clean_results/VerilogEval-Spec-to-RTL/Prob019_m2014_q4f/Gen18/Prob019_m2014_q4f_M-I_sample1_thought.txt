Since the original design is functionally correct but uses explicit modules for inverter and AND gate, this can introduce extra overhead and possibly increase delay, power, and area. To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, and area), the logic should be consolidated into a single continuous assignment expression using built-in operators. This reduces module overhead, eliminates unnecessary internal nets, and lowers switching activity by minimizing intermediate nodes. The new design directly implements out = in1 & ~in2; which is equivalent to the AND gate with a bubble on in2.
