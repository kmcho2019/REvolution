The initial solution uses explicit NotGate and AndGate modules to represent the inversion (bubble) and AND operation, which is clear and modular. However, from a PPA perspective (power, performance, area), this can be optimized by consolidating the logic into a single continuous assignment. This allows synthesis tools to optimize the logic further by merging the inverter and AND gate, potentially using simpler transistor-level implementations (like a NAND gate with bubble on one input and inverter inline), minimizing area and power.

Therefore, I will replace the two-module hierarchy with a single continuous assignment implementing 'out = in1 & ~in2'. This keeps the design combinational and synthesizable while reducing gate count and wiring overhead, improving PPA metrics without sacrificing readability significantly for such a simple circuit.
