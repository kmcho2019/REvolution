Instead of explicitly instantiating separate NOT and AND gate modules, implement the bubble (inversion) on the in2 input directly by using a wire to hold the inverted value, and then use a single assign statement for the AND operation in the top module. This creates a simple two-stage combinational logic with explicit inversion as a separate signal, which improves readability while avoiding additional module overhead. It keeps the design structural but flattens it within one module, leveraging continuous assignments for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
