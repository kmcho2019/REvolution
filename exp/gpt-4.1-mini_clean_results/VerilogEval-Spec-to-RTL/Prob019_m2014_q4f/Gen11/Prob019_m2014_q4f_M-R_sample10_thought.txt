The function to implement is out = in1 & ~in2, i.e., an AND gate with a bubble (inversion) on in2. The previous solution used gate instantiations of NOT and NOR, but the requirement is to refactor with the same logic yet with a different structure.

A straightforward and clear approach is to explicitly invert in2 first, then AND it with in1, using continuous assignments with assign statements. This simplifies the gate-level view into RTL-level assign statements and better matches the bubble on in2 concept.

This approach enhances readability and clearly separates the inversion of in2 from the AND operation. It also avoids any confusion with gate-level primitives and their bubble interpretation.

The module will declare a wire for the inverted in2 and then assign out as the AND of in1 and the inverted in2.
