To optimize the original design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will remove the explicit NotGate and AndGate module instantiations and replace them with a single continuous assignment using Verilog's built-in operators. This reduces hierarchy, enabling synthesis tools to perform better logic optimization and possibly reduce the gate count, leading to improved area and power efficiency without affecting performance. The functionality remains identical: output is the AND of in1 and the inverted in2.
