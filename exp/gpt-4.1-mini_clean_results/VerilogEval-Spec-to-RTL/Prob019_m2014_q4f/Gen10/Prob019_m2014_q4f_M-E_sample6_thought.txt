Instead of using an explicit inverter and an AND gate, use a single NAND gate followed by an inverter to implement the logic out = in1 & ~in2.

The NAND gate inputs will be in1 and in2, producing ~(in1 & in2), and then the inverter will invert this output to obtain out = ~~(in1 & in2) = in1 & in2.

However, since we want out = in1 & ~in2, we can rearrange the logic as: out = ~(~in1 + in2) = in1 & ~in2.

So if we use a NOR gate on inputs ~in1 and in2: out = ~(~in1 + in2) = in1 & ~in2.
