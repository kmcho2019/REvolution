To optimize the design for better PPA, I will remove the separate NotGate module and instead inline the inversion directly within the AND gate expression in TopModule. This reduces area by eliminating an extra gate, reduces switching nodes thereby lowering power, and simplifies the netlist for faster synthesis. The continuous assignment expression will be synthesis-friendly and allow tools to map the logic optimally, e.g., into a single NAND gate with proper input polarity. This approach preserves correctness, maintains clarity, and produces minimal, efficient hardware.
