Implement the TopModule using behavioral Verilog with a single continuous assignment that directly expresses the logic of an AND gate with one inverted input. Instead of instantiating separate modules for NOT and AND gates, apply inversion inline by using the bitwise NOT operator on in2 within the AND operation. This results in a compact, efficient, and synthesis-friendly implementation that clearly communicates the bubble on in2 through the expression (~in2 & in1). It also minimizes module hierarchy and gate count, which can improve area and power by enabling synthesis tools to optimize directly to minimal standard cells.
