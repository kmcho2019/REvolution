Implement the TopModule by describing the logic functionally with a single continuous assignment using bitwise operators. Since the AND gate has a bubble on the in2 input, this translates to ANDing in1 with the inverted in2 signal (out = in1 & ~in2). This approach is minimal and avoids explicit module instantiations, reducing overhead and improving readability. It also yields straightforward synthesis results with optimized performance, power, and area.
