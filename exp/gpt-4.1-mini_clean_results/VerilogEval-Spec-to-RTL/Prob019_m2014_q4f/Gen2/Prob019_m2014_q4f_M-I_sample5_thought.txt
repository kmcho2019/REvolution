The original implementation is already minimal and optimal for this simple logic: an AND gate with one input inverted. Given the extremely small logic, no further optimization in terms of PPA (performance, power, area) is realistically possible at this scale. The logic is a single AND and NOT, which is the minimal representation. For improved clarity and synthesis friendliness, I will explicitly instantiate a NOT gate followed by an AND gate using continuous assignments, which could aid in logic recognition in some synthesis tools. This does not change functionality or area but improves readability and may assist some tools in mapping the design more explicitly. However, overall PPA will remain essentially the same.
