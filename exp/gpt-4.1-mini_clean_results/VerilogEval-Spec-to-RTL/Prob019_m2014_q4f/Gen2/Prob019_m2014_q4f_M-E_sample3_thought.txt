Instead of directly implementing the AND gate with a negated input, we can explicitly instantiate primitive gates to show the inversion bubble and AND gate distinctly. This can clarify the bubble's origin and make the design more modular and educational. 

Specifically:
- Use a NOT gate to invert in2.
- Feed in1 and the inverted in2 into a 2-input AND gate.
- Connect the output of the AND gate to out.

This approach visually separates the inversion and the AND operation, reflecting the bubble on the input clearly in gate-level structure.
