An alternative approach to realize an AND gate with a bubble on in2 is to first implement a NAND gate on inputs in1 and in2 and then invert the output. The bubble on in2 effectively inverts that input before the AND operation, so in terms of logic, out = in1 & (~in2). By double negation, out can be also expressed as ~(~in1 | in2), which can be implemented by first NANDing in1 and in2, and then NANDing that output with itself (a NOT gate). This structural approach uses only NAND gates and a NOT realized as a NAND with tied inputs.
