To optimize PPA while maintaining clarity, remove the explicit NotGate and AndGate module instantiations and implement the logic directly in a single assign statement: out = in1 & ~in2. This reduces gate count (area), wiring complexity, and switching power by eliminating unnecessary module overhead, while synthesis tools can still recognize and optimize the bubble (inversion) on in2. This direct coding approach improves area and power slightly without impacting timing, given the simple combinational nature.
