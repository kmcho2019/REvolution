Since the desired behavior is a simple one-bit wire, the most optimal hardware mapping is achieved by direct continuous assignment without instantiating any primitive or additional module. This approach yields minimal area and power consumption, as it maps directly to a wire in hardware with zero delay and no gate overhead.

The previous solution, while modular and scalable, introduces unnecessary buffer primitives and module hierarchy, increasing power and area slightly.

Therefore, the improved solution is to implement TopModule with a continuous assignment: assign out = in; This guarantees the best PPA metrics for a 1-bit wire.
