To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will implement TopModule as a simple, direct continuous assignment connecting the input to the output. This minimalistic approach removes hierarchy and extra logic levels from Example 1 while avoiding the overhead of interfaces from Example 2. It ensures optimal PPA—minimal delay, area, and power—by letting synthesis tools infer a simple wire connection. This solution captures the intent of a "wire" function in the cleanest and most efficient manner, preserving modularity at the top level without unnecessary abstractions.
