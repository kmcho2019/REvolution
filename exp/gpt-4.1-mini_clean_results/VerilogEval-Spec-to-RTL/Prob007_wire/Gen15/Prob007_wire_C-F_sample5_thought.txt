Fuse the modularity and scalability of using a SystemVerilog interface from Example 2 with the minimal area and power efficiency of a direct continuous assignment from Example 1 by defining a lightweight interface purely as a net alias. Instead of creating intermediate logic signals or using modports that may incur overhead, implement the interface with a single continuous assignment to directly connect the input and output ports through the interface signal. This approach preserves the structured, reusable nature of an interface for future scalability while synthesizing down to a simple wire with no extra gates or power penalty. It also avoids instantiating extra modules or buffer primitives, providing clean semantics and optimal PPA.
