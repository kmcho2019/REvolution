To fuse the best ideas, I will create a minimal, reusable primitive buffer module (BufGate) using the built-in Verilog buf primitive for optimal synthesis and minimal area/power. However, to reduce unnecessary hierarchy and improve PPA, I will integrate the buf primitive instance directly inside TopModule without a separate module. This combines the modular clarity and synthesis friendliness of example 2 with the minimal hierarchy and direct connection efficiency suggested in the feedback. The design is thus a single-level module with a direct buf primitive instance connecting input to output, which synthesis tools map to an ideal wire or buffer with minimal overhead, ensuring optimal power, area, and zero delay while maintaining clean structure for potential extension.
