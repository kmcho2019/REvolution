Since the original design is already the simplest possible wire connection and uses a continuous assignment which synthesis tools optimize as a direct net connection, there is no room for meaningful improvements in timing, power, or area. Any alternative coding style, such as using a continuous assignment or a structural primitive, results in equivalent hardware.

To confirm, I will implement the same functionality using a structural primitive (buf gate), which some tools may interpret more explicitly as a direct buffer. This may potentially give a clearer semantic to synthesis tools, although practically the PPA impact will be negligible.

Thus, the improvement strategy is to provide an alternate representation (using a buf gate) to reinforce the direct wire nature of the design without changing functionality or complexity.
