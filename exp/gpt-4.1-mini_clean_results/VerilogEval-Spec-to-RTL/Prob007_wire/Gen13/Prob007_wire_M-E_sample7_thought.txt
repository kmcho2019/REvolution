Implement the TopModule using an always_comb block that continuously assigns the output to the input. This approach models the wire behavior behaviorally without using the buf primitive or continuous assign statement. It leverages combinational logic semantics with an always block, maintaining simplicity and synthesizability, while providing a clear structural alternative.

Though this adds a minimal combinational logic level internally, synthesis tools will optimize it as a direct wire since the assignment is purely combinational and has no state or delay.

This approach offers a different architectural style compared to continuous assignments or buf primitives and removes explicit primitive gates or module hierarchies.
