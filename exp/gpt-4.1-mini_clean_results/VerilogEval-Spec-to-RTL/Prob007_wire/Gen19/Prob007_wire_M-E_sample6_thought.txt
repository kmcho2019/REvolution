Implement TopModule by instantiating a bidirectional tri-state buffer concept using two complementary internal signals and continuous assignments, mimicking a pass-through wire with enable control implicitly fixed to active. This approach uses internal signals and gate-level style continuous assignments instead of direct assignment or interfaces. It demonstrates how one can architect a wire connection by explicitly splitting driving and sensing paths, which may be extended for bus enable/disable functionality in future versions.

Specifically, create two internal signals, one driven by the input and another feeding the output. Connect them with continuous assignments such that the output follows the input through the internal signals without introducing registers or logic delays. This structure could be used to demonstrate explicit signal direction and drive management in a small design, serving as a tutorial example for tri-state logic synthesis in FPGA/ASIC flows.
