To achieve an optimal design that fuses the modular clarity of a dedicated buffer primitive from Example 1 and the minimal overhead of a direct continuous assignment from Example 2, I will use a generate block with a simple continuous assignment inside TopModule. This approach avoids extra module instantiation (thus reducing power and area), preserves straightforward semantics, and can be extended easily in the future (e.g., adding buffering or modifying behavior) without hierarchical overhead now. It also keeps the code clean and synthesizes to a direct wire connection, ensuring zero delay and minimal resource usage. This strikes a balance between modularity, clarity, and PPA efficiency.
