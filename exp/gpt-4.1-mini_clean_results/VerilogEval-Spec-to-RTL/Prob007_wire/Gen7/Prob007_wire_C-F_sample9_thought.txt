To achieve the best balance of modularity, clarity, and optimal PPA, I will implement TopModule as a direct continuous assignment from input to output without any extra module instantiation or buffer primitives. This approach retains the wire-like behavior with minimal overhead, reduces unnecessary hierarchical layers and logic gates, and thus optimizes power and area while maintaining zero delay. The fusion strategy here is to keep the design structurally simple like Example 1 but avoid any extra buffer modules, unlike Example 2, thereby combining the clarity of direct wiring with the PPA advantages of minimal logic and hierarchy.
