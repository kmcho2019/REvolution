Since the module is to behave exactly like a wire (passing input to output without modification), the most optimal approach in terms of performance, power, and area is to remove any additional module hierarchy or buffer primitives. Instead, I will implement a single TopModule that directly connects the output port to the input port using a continuous assignment. This eliminates extra instantiations, reduces routing and logic overhead, and enables the synthesis tool to map it to a direct wire with minimal resource usage.
