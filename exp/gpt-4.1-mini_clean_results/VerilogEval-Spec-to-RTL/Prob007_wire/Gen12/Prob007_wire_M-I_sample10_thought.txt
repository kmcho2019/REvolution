Since the original solution uses an explicit buf primitive through a parameterized module, it incurs slight overhead in area and power, especially unnecessary for a single-bit wire. The best optimization for a one-bit wire is to use a continuous assignment `assign out = in;` directly in TopModule without any instantiation or module overhead. This ensures minimal area and power while keeping zero delay and zero critical path. Continuous assignments synthesize to a direct wire connection, which is optimal for this use case.
