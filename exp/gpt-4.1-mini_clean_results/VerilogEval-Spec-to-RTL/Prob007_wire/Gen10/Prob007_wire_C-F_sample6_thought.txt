To combine the clarity and modularity of the BufGate approach with the power, area, and simplicity benefits of direct wiring, I will implement the TopModule as a single module using a continuous assignment statement without any additional module instantiation. This fuses the best of both examples by eliminating unnecessary hierarchy and logic overhead, directly connecting input to output as a wire. This design is minimal, optimal for synthesis and PPA metrics, and fully meets the specification with the simplest and clearest implementation.
