Implement the wire behavior using a continuous assignment directly inside the TopModule without any intermediate modules or primitives. This approach uses the simplest and most direct form of Verilog connectivity, ensuring zero logic overhead and optimal synthesis for a wire connection.

By declaring both input and output as wires and using an assign statement, synthesis tools will infer a direct connection, minimizing delay, area, and power consumption.

This method is the most straightforward and avoids the instantiation of any additional components, primitives, or buffers.
