Fusion strategy:
- Define a minimal, reusable module (WirePassThrough) with a parameterized bit-width, encapsulating the simple continuous assignment.
- In the TopModule, instantiate WirePassThrough to maintain clear hierarchy and modularity.
- Introduce a parameter in TopModule (USE_BUF) to optionally instantiate a BufGate module as a buffer, enhancing flexibility for future PPA tradeoffs.
- Default to no buffering (direct pass-through via WirePassThrough) for optimal area, power, and minimal logic.
- This approach ensures:
  - Modular reuse and clear hierarchy from WirePassThrough.
  - Optional buffering for flexibility inspired by BufGate and parameterized generate.
  - Minimal logic and direct continuous assignment to allow synthesis tools to optimize to a wire.
- This design merges clarity, configurability, and synthesis optimization, balancing future-proofing and minimal PPA overhead.
