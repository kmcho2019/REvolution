The original solution uses an intermediate BufGate module which introduces an extra hierarchical level and buffer instantiation, leading to slightly higher power and area overhead. To optimize for PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will remove the BufGate module and directly assign the output to the input using a continuous assignment within TopModule. This achieves the same behavior as a wire with minimal logic, lowest delay, and minimal power and area usage by eliminating unnecessary instantiations.
