We are given a 6-bit one-hot encoded state vector y representing states A to F in order from y[0] to y[5]. The FSM transitions depend on an input 'w'. We need to derive the next-state logic signals Y1 and Y3 corresponding to the next-state bits y[1] and y[3] respectively.

We analyze the transitions to determine when the next state will be B (y[1]) or D (y[3]):

- Next state B (y[1]) is active when:
  From A(0) on 0 -> B
  So if current state is A (y[0]) and w=0 then Y1=1

- Next state D (y[3]) is active when:
  From B(1) on 1 -> D
  From C(2) on 1 -> D
  From E(4) on 1 -> D
  From F(5) on 1 -> D

So Y3 is 1 if (y[1] & w) or (y[2] & w) or (y[4] & w) or (y[5] & w)

This can be simplified to Y3 = w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

We'll implement Y1 and Y3 outputs as per this logic.
