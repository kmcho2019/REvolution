Since y is a one-hot encoded current state vector, Y1 and Y3 represent next-state signals corresponding to y[1] (B) and y[3] (D). By inspection of the transitions, Y1 is active only when state A (y[0]) and w=0, and Y3 is active when w=1 and the current state is any of B, C, D, E, or F (y[1] to y[5]). This logic can be expressed concisely with simple bitwise operations.
