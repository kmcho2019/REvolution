Implement the TopModule by deriving the next-state bits Y1 and Y3 (corresponding to y[1] and y[3]) directly using combinational logic expressions derived by inspection for one-hot encoding.

Since y is one-hot coded over 6 bits (A to F), and each state transitions depend on current state and input w, we can write Boolean equations for next_B (Y1) and next_D (Y3) in terms of y and w.

By analyzing the FSM:

- For next_B (Y1):
  Next state B (y[1]) is active only after:
    - From A with w=0 (A= y[0], w=0)
  So,
    Y1 = y[0] & ~w

- For next_D (Y3):
  Next state D (y[3]) is active after:
    - From B with w=1 (y[1] & w)
    - From C with w=1 (y[2] & w)
    - From E with w=1 (y[4] & w)
    - From F with w=1 (y[5] & w)
  So,
    Y3 = w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

This approach implements concise, directly derived combinational logic for Y1 and Y3, avoiding case statements and facilitating easy understanding and efficient implementation.

The module ports are:
- input [5:0] y
- input w
- output Y1 (for y[1])
- output Y3 (for y[3])
