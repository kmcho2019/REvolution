Since only outputs Y1 and Y3 are requested and these correspond to y[1] and y[3] of the *next* state, we can directly derive Y1 and Y3 from current state bits and input w by inspection of the given FSM transitions using the one-hot encoding. Instead of constructing the full next-state vector, we derive Boolean expressions for Y1 and Y3 by summing the contributions from each state's next transitions that lead to those bits.

This removes unnecessary intermediate signals and simplifies the logic to concise combinational assignments.
