Instead of directly deriving Y2 and Y4 from the one-hot state vector by inspection, implement a small combinational next-state logic that explicitly encodes the transitions into a 6-bit next-state vector using the one-hot encoding. Then, assign Y2 and Y4 as the bits corresponding to states B (y[1]) and D (y[3]) from this next-state vector. This modular approach allows for a clear and scalable design where the full next-state vector is computed, and the required outputs are extracted. This also facilitates potential extension to other outputs or a full next-state register in the future.
