Instead of directly encoding next-state logic for single next-state bits, implement a simple combinational next-state decoder for all six states in one-hot encoding. Use the FSM transitions to determine which state bits (y[5:0]) will be set on the next cycle based on the current state (y) and input w. Then assign outputs Y2 and Y4 as the next-state bits for states B (y[1]) and D (y[3]) respectively from that next-state vector. This modular approach can easily be extended and makes the derivation of Y2 and Y4 signals straightforward, while providing a clear separation between next-state decoding and output assignments.
