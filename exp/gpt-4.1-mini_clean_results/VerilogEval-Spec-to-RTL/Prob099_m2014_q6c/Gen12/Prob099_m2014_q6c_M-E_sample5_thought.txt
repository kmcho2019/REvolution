Instead of directly deriving outputs for next states B and D from inputs, implement a combinational next-state decoder for the entire one-hot state vector given current state y and input w. Then, output Y2 and Y4 as the next-state signals corresponding to y[1] (B) and y[3] (D). This approach explicitly computes all next states as one-hot signals, from which Y2 and Y4 are extracted.
