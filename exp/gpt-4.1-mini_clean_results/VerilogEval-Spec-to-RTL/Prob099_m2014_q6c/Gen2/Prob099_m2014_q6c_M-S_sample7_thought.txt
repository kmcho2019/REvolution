Since the FSM uses one-hot encoding, each state corresponds to one bit in y. The next-state bits Y1 and Y3 correspond to the bits of the next state vector for states B (bit 1) and D (bit 3), respectively.

I can directly encode the logic for Y1 and Y3 based on the current state bits and input w by inspecting the transitions for those particular next states, without constructing the entire next state vector.

Specifically:

- Y1 (next state B) occurs from A on w=0.
- Y3 (next state D) occurs from B on w=1, C on w=1, D on w=1, E on w=1, and F on w=1.

Expressing Y1 and Y3 as boolean expressions directly from y and w reduces complexity.

This approach avoids a case statement and simplifies the design.
