Since the problem is to output the next-state signals Y1 and Y3 corresponding to y[1] and y[3], and assuming one-hot encoding for states A to F as y[0] to y[5], we simply express Y1 and Y3 as combinational logic based on the given state transitions and input w. From the state diagram, next y[1] (state B) is active only from state A with input w=0, so Y1 = y[0] & ~w. Next y[3] (state D) is active when w=1 and current state is B, C, E, or F, so Y3 = w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]). Thus, the module assigns these directly to outputs Y1 and Y3.
