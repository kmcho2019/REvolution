Since the state encoding is one-hot and we only need the next-state signals Y2 and Y4 corresponding to y[1] and y[3], we can directly write the combinational logic expressions by inspecting the transitions:

- Y2 (next B) is active when current state is A (y[0]) and w=0.
- Y4 (next D) is active when w=1 and current state is any of B, C, D, E, or F (y[1] to y[5]).

We just assign these signals directly without extra functions for simplicity.
