Implement a purely combinational logic module that derives the next-state bits Y1 and Y3 directly from the current one-hot state vector y and input w. Since the state encoding is one-hot and the state transitions depend on w, build logic expressions for the next states corresponding to y[1] (B) and y[3] (D) by examining all transitions leading into those states, combining the relevant conditions logically. This approach avoids intermediate wires and implements the outputs directly using Boolean expressions reflecting the next-state conditions for states B and D.
