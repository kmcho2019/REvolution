The problem requires outputs Y2 and Y4 representing next-state signals for states y[1] (B) and y[3] (D) respectively. Using one-hot encoding and given transitions, we see:

- Y2 (next B) is active only from A with input w=0: Y2 = y[0] & ~w
- Y4 (next D) is active from B, C, E, and F with w=1: Y4 = w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])
