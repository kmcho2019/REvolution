Implement a synchronous FSM with the following states:
- IDLE: searching for pattern 1101 by shifting in bits of data.
- SHIFT: once pattern 1101 is detected, assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles to shift in delay bits.
- COUNT: after shifting delay bits, assert counting until done_counting is high.
- DONE: assert done to notify user, wait for ack to return to IDLE.

Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101 on incoming data serially.
Use a counter to count 4 cycles of shift_ena assertion.
All state transitions and outputs are synchronous and active high reset initializes the FSM to IDLE.
