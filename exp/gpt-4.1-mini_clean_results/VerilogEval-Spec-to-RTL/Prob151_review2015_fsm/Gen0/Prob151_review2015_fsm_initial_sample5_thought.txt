Implement an FSM with states: SEARCH (search for pattern 1101), SHIFT (shift in 4 bits with shift_ena asserted), COUNT (assert counting until done_counting is high), DONE (assert done until ack is high). Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101. On reset, go to SEARCH state. On each clock, update state and outputs accordingly. All signals are synchronous active high reset.
