Implement a synchronous FSM with states: IDLE (search for pattern 1101), SHIFT (assert shift_ena for 4 cycles to shift in delay bits), COUNT (assert counting and wait for done_counting), DONE (assert done and wait for ack). Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the 1101 pattern by shifting in the data bit each clock cycle. On reset, go to IDLE. In IDLE, shift data in shift_reg and check for 1101. On detection, move to SHIFT. In SHIFT, assert shift_ena for 4 cycles using a counter, then move to COUNT. In COUNT, assert counting until done_counting is high, then move to DONE. In DONE, assert done until ack is high, then go back to IDLE.
