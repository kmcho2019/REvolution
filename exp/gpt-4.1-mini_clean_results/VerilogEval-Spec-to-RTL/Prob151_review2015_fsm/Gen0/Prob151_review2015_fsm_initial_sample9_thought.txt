The FSM will have the following states:
- IDLE: waiting for pattern 1101; on each clock, shift in the new data bit to a 4-bit shift register and check if it matches 1101.
- SHIFT: after detecting 1101, assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles to shift in the duration bits.
- COUNTING: assert counting, wait for done_counting to go high.
- DONE: assert done, wait for ack to go high.
On reset, return to IDLE.

We implement a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101. Then, after pattern detection, count 4 clock cycles with shift_ena asserted, then go to COUNTING, then DONE states as per specification.
