To improve PPA (performance, power, and area) while keeping correctness and clarity, I propose these enhancements:

- Use a one-hot state encoding instead of binary. One-hot encoding often yields faster FSMs with simpler next-state logic, improving timing at a small area cost that is negligible for this small FSM.
- Replace the 2-bit shift counter with a 3-bit shift register acting as a countdown, driven by a shift enable signal, which can be gated with the state to reduce toggling power.
- Implement state register and counters in a single always_ff block with synchronous reset.
- Drive outputs combinationally from state signals in an always_comb block.
- Maintain pattern detection states clearly and support overlapping detection of "1101".
- Minimize switching in counters by only updating when needed (in SHIFT).
- This approach improves timing by simplifying next-state logic (due to one-hot encoding) and reduces toggling by gating counters, also slightly improving power.
- Area impact is minimal as FSM is small, and the clearer, glitch-free outputs are retained.

This balanced method should produce a better PPA profile without complicating the design.
