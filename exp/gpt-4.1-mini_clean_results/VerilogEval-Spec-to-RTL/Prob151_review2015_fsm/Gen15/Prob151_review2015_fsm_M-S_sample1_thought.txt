Simplify the FSM by combining the pattern detection states into a single 4-bit shift register that captures the last 4 bits of serial data, checking for the pattern 1101 directly on every clock cycle. This removes multiple states for pattern detection and avoids complex state transitions for that purpose.

Use a simple 3-state FSM: IDLE (searching for pattern), SHIFT (shift_ena asserted for 4 cycles), and WAIT (waiting for done_counting and then ack).

Maintain a 2-bit shift_count to count the 4 cycles of shifting.

Outputs are purely Moore outputs from the current state.

This approach reduces the number of states and logic complexity while keeping clear, synchronous logic and meeting all functional requirements.
