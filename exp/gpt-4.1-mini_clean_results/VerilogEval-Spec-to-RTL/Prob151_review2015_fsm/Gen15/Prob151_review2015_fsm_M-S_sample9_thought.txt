Simplify the FSM by merging the pattern detection states into a single shift-register style pattern matcher. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern "1101" in serial data input, advancing each cycle without multiple states. Once detected, move directly to a SHIFT state that counts 4 cycles with a 2-bit counter to assert shift_ena, then to COUNT and DONE states as before.

This approach reduces the number of explicit states, simplifying next-state logic, while maintaining the same behavior. Outputs remain Moore style and state register plus a small counter implement the FSM with synchronous reset. The simpler FSM structure is easier to maintain and synthesize with fewer states and transitions.
