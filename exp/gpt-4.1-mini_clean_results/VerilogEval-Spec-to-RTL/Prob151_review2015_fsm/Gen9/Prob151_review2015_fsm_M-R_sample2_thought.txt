Refactor the FSM to use classic Verilog constructs without SystemVerilog-specific features such as typedef enums, logic types, or always_ff/always_comb blocks.

- Use simple `reg` and `wire` types to maximize compatibility.
- Represent states as localparams with binary encoding.
- Use a single always block triggered on the rising edge of clk for synchronous state and shift_count updates with synchronous active-high reset.
- Use a combinational always block for next_state logic.
- Drive outputs with continuous assignments based on current state, avoiding comparisons of enum types.
- Keep shift_count increment within the synchronous block only when in SHIFT state.
- This approach maintains the original FSM behavior but uses a more traditional Verilog style that is broadly compatible with most tools.
