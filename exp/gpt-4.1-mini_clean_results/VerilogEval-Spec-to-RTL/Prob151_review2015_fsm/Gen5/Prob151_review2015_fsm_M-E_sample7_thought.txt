Implement a Mealy FSM with a 4-bit pattern shift register always shifting in incoming data, independent of state.

- The FSM has five states: IDLE, SHIFT (with a 2-bit counter inside to count 4 shifts), COUNTING, DONE.

- IDLE state searches for the pattern 1101 by continuously updating the 4-bit shift register each clock.

- On detecting pattern 1101, the FSM transitions to SHIFT state, asserting shift_ena immediately and starting the internal 2-bit shift counter at 1.

- While in SHIFT state, shift_ena remains high and the shift counter increments each cycle. After 4 shifts (counter == 4), transition to COUNTING state.

- In COUNTING, shift_ena is deasserted and counting asserted. Wait here until done_counting is asserted.

- Once done_counting is high, transition to DONE, asserting done until ack is high.

- Once ack is high, return to IDLE.

Outputs shift_ena, counting, and done are generated combinationally (Mealy) based on current state and internal shift count to assert signals at the earliest cycles to avoid delays of Moore FSM outputs.

All state and counters update synchronously at posedge clk with synchronous reset.

This approach ensures:

- Continuous pattern detection without stalling pattern shifting.

- Precise 4-cycle shift_ena assertion managed by an internal counter within SHIFT state.

- Immediate assertion of outputs upon state and counter conditions (Mealy), reducing output latency.

- Simple, minimal state encoding and counters.

- Clear separation of pattern detection and shift counting.
