Implement a simple FSM with three main states: IDLE (searching for pattern), SHIFTING (shifting in 4 bits), COUNTING (waiting for done_counting), and DONE (assert done and wait for ack). Use a 4-bit shift register to continuously shift in data every clock cycle. Detect the pattern 1101 by checking when the shift register equals that pattern, then start the shifting state for exactly 4 cycles. Use a simple counter to track shift cycles. After shifting, enter COUNTING and wait for done_counting to go high, then move to DONE state waiting for ack. Outputs are asserted directly from the FSM state. The FSM resets synchronously on reset.
