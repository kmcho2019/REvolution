Implement a Mealy FSM that updates the 4-bit pattern register every clock cycle regardless of state, enabling immediate detection of the 1101 pattern as soon as it appears.

Use five states:

- IDLE: looking for pattern 1101;
- SHIFT_0 to SHIFT_3: four consecutive shift cycles asserting shift_ena one cycle at a time;
- WAIT_COUNT: assert counting until done_counting;
- WAIT_ACK: assert done until ack.

By breaking SHIFT into four separate states, we can assert shift_ena precisely once per shift cycle.

Because pattern detection depends on current pattern register updated every cycle, transition to SHIFT_0 is immediate upon detection.

Outputs (shift_ena, counting, done) are driven combinationally based on the current state to ensure correct timing (Moore outputs).

Reset synchronously sets FSM to IDLE and clears pattern register.

This approach ensures precise control over shift cycles, immediate pattern recognition, and clear output timing aligned with state transitions.
