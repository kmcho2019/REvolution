Use a simple Moore FSM with four states: SEARCH (looking for 1101), SHIFT (assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles), WAIT_COUNT (assert counting until done_counting), and WAIT_ACK (assert done until ack).

Detect pattern 1101 by shifting data into a 4-bit shift register every clock. When detected, transition to SHIFT and assert shift_ena for 4 cycles controlled by a 2-bit counter.

After 4 cycles, move to WAIT_COUNT, assert counting until done_counting is high, then move to WAIT_ACK, assert done until ack is high, then return to SEARCH.

All outputs are driven purely by state to keep the design clear. Reset synchronously clears state, shift register, and counters.

This minimal FSM cleanly separates each phase, keeping logic straightforward and easy to verify.
