Use a Moore FSM with five states: SEARCH, SHIFT_0, SHIFT_1, SHIFT_2, SHIFT_3, COUNTING, DONE.

- In the SEARCH state, a 4-bit pattern register shifts in the serial data. When pattern 1101 is detected, transition to SHIFT_0.

- Instead of using a shift counter, we sequence through SHIFT_0 to SHIFT_3 states, each representing one shift cycle with shift_ena asserted. This ensures exactly 4 clock cycles with shift_ena high.

- After SHIFT_3, transition to COUNTING and assert counting until done_counting is asserted.

- Then transition to DONE state asserting done until ack is high.

- From DONE state, transition back to SEARCH to wait for next pattern.

This approach avoids counters and relies purely on states to control outputs and timing precisely. Outputs are driven solely by the current state (Moore outputs).

The pattern register updates on every clock cycle in the SEARCH state only, avoiding unwanted shifts later.

All registers update synchronously on the positive edge of clk with active-high reset.

This design guarantees correct detection timing, precise 4-cycle shift_ena assertion, and synchronous, glitch-free outputs.
