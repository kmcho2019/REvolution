Implement a simple Moore FSM with four states: IDLE, SHIFT, COUNTING, and DONE. 

- Use a 4-bit shift register updated synchronously every clock to detect the pattern 1101 in the IDLE state.
- When pattern detected, transition to SHIFT and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles counted by a 2-bit counter.
- After shifting, transition to COUNTING asserting counting output until done_counting is high.
- Then transition to DONE asserting done output until ack is high.
- All outputs depend solely on the current state, updated synchronously on clock.
- Synchronous reset initializes all registers and state.

This keeps the logic clear, avoids output timing issues, and guarantees correct output durations.
