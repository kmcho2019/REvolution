To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will:

- Use one-hot state encoding instead of binary to simplify next-state combinational logic, reducing the logic depth and improving timing (better WNS and TNS).
- Remove the shift_count register and replace it with a 2-bit shift_counter encoded as two flip-flops, clock-enabled only during shifting, reducing switching activity and power.
- Only update the shift_counter when shifting, holding its value otherwise, thus saving toggling power.
- Simplify pattern detection logic by moving pattern progress from state encoding to the FSM states directly without a separate data shift register.
- Keep all outputs combinational from one-hot states, keeping outputs glitch-free and minimal logic.
- Retain synchronous active-high reset.
- Overall, this should reduce combinational complexity and power and improve timing by reducing critical paths and unnecessary toggling in counters.
