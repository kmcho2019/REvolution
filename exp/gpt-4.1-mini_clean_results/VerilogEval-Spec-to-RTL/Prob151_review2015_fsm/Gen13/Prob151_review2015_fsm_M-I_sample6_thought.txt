To optimize the FSM for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will apply one-hot state encoding instead of binary encoding. One-hot encoding simplifies state decoding logic, often resulting in faster and smaller FSMs in FPGA and some ASIC flows.

Additionally, I will:
- Use an enable signal for the shift_count counter to avoid unnecessary toggling.
- Keep synchronous reset as required.
- Use Moore outputs driven by the state signals.
- Retain clear, classic Verilog style for compatibility.

This should reduce the combinational logic complexity, lower switching in counters, and marginally improve timing and power without impacting functionality.
