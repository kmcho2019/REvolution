Use a Moore FSM with five distinct states:
- SEARCH0 to SEARCH3: represent progressive detection of the pattern "1101" on the serial data input.
  Each SEARCH state corresponds to matching a certain prefix of the pattern, allowing continuous serial matching without missing overlapping bits.
- SHIFT: after full pattern detected, assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles to shift delay bits.
- COUNT: assert counting until done_counting goes high.
- DONE: assert done until ack is received, then return to SEARCH0.

This approach eliminates a separate pattern shift register by encoding pattern matching directly in states.  
The FSM updates on each clock edge and synchronously resets to SEARCH0 state.

A 2-bit counter tracks 4 cycles of SHIFT state (0 to 3).  
Outputs are purely combinational based on current state for clear timing.  
Pattern detection states update according to input data without separate shift register, enabling immediate recognition of overlapping patterns.
