Implement a simple FSM with four states: IDLE (search for 1101 pattern by shifting data in each clock), SHIFT (assert shift_ena exactly 4 cycles to shift in duration bits), COUNT (assert counting until done_counting is high), and DONE (assert done until ack is high). Use a 4-bit shift register continuously shifted in IDLE to detect pattern 1101. In SHIFT state, keep shifting in the duration bits by asserting shift_ena and count 4 cycles with a counter. Then proceed to COUNT and DONE states as specified. Reset brings the FSM to IDLE and clears counters and registers. This simplification keeps the functionality clear and directly tied to the problem requirements.
