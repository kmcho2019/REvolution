To optimize the FSM for better performance, power, and area while preserving correctness, I propose the following improvements:

1. Use one-hot encoding for the FSM states: This reduces combinational next-state decoding complexity and can improve maximum clock frequency at the cost of slight area increase, which is minimal for 7 states.

2. Refine the pattern detection logic to reduce transitions and minimize toggling by integrating the pattern matching and overlapping seamlessly in one-hot style, which typically leads to simpler next state logic.

3. Replace the 2-bit shift_count counter with a 3-bit shift_count that counts from 0 to 3 but implement it as a small binary counter with synchronous clear, optimized in sequential logic. The one-hot FSM encoding makes controlling shift_count only in the SHIFT state straightforward.

4. Use non-blocking assignments carefully and separate sequential and combinational blocks clearly.

5. Generate outputs directly from one-hot states with minimal combinational logic.

6. The synchronous reset is retained as active high.

This approach should reduce critical path delay by simplifying next state logic, minimize glitches on outputs, and keep power and area low while allowing a higher effective clock frequency.
