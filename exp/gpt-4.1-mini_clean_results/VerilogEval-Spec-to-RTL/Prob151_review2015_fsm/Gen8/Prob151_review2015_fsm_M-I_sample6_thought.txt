To optimize the FSM for better power and area, reduce the shift counter width from 3 bits to 2 bits since counting 0 to 3 requires only 2 bits. This reduces flip-flop usage and switching activity.

Also, instead of resetting the sequence detector state to IDLE outside the ST_IDLE state every cycle—which causes unnecessary toggling—gate the sequence detector enable signal so that the seq_state register only updates in ST_IDLE and holds its value otherwise. This prevents repeated resetting and unnecessary transitions, reducing dynamic power.

Keep the same state encoding and overall FSM functionality to maintain correct operation.

These two changes minimize sequential logic toggling and slightly reduce area without affecting timing or functionality.
