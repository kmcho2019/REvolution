To improve PPA, I will use one-hot encoding for the states to potentially simplify next-state logic and speed up state decoding at the cost of modestly increased area. This often improves timing (WNS) because only one flip-flop toggles per state change.

Additionally, I will rewrite the shift_count counter as a simple 2-bit binary up-counter with reset, avoiding addition by just incrementing it on SHIFT state. The counter will reset outside SHIFT state to avoid glitches.

Outputs will be driven by combinational assignments from one-hot encoded state signals.

This approach trades slight area increase for improved timing (lower combinational logic depth) and potentially lower power by simplified next-state logic, as only a small number of bits toggle per clock.

The FSM functionality remains identical.
