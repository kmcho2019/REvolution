Implement a Moore FSM with five states: IDLE (searching for pattern), SHIFT_0 through SHIFT_3 (each asserting shift_ena for exactly one cycle to shift in 4 bits), COUNTING (assert counting until done_counting), and DONE (assert done until ack). Use a 4-bit shift register that updates on every clock cycle regardless of state to detect the 1101 pattern. Transition from IDLE to SHIFT_0 only when the pattern is detected, then advance through SHIFT states with shift_ena asserted exactly one cycle per SHIFT_n state. This explicit state sequencing guarantees exact timing of shift_ena for 4 cycles. Counting and done states handle their inputs and outputs simply, all state and outputs updated synchronously on the positive edge of clk, with synchronous reset initializing all to IDLE and outputs deasserted. Outputs are registered and driven solely from the current state for clean timing. This approach avoids mixing next_state and current state for outputs and gives precise control over shift_ena assertion timing.
