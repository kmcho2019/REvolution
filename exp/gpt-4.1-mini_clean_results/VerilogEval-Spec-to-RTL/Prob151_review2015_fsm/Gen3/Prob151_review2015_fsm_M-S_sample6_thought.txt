Implement a simple FSM with four states: IDLE, SHIFT, COUNTING, DONE. Use a 4-bit shift register only in the IDLE state to detect the pattern 1101. Once detected, move to SHIFT state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles by counting them. During SHIFT, stop updating the pattern detector since pattern detection is only needed in IDLE. Then transition to COUNTING state and assert counting until done_counting is high. Next, assert done in DONE state until ack is received. All outputs are registered and driven solely by the current state. This approach simplifies pattern detection to happen only in IDLE, preventing interference during SHIFT and later states.
