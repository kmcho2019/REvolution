Simplify the FSM by merging the partial pattern matching states into a single 4-bit shift register that continuously captures the last 4 bits of serial data. On each clock, shift in the new data bit and check if the pattern "1101" is detected. This eliminates multiple SEARCH states and related complex transitions.

After detecting "1101", transition to the SHIFT state and count exactly 4 cycles (using a 2-bit counter) to enable shift_ena.

Then move to COUNT state, wait for done_counting to go high.

Finally, assert done in DONE state until ack is received, then return to pattern detection.

All outputs are purely combinational based on the current state.

This reduces states from 7 to 4 and simplifies next-state logic, while preserving all functionality.
