To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will reduce the state register size by encoding states using a 2-bit Gray code for the pattern detection states to minimize glitches and logic transitions, while keeping the rest as separate states to simplify control signals. This will slightly reduce area and dynamic power.

Also, I will merge the pattern detection states more efficiently by implementing the pattern detector as a small shift register with combinational logic to detect 1101 pattern, instead of multiple sequential states, thus reducing the number of FSM states and logic complexity.

The FSM states will be reduced to four main states:
- IDLE (waiting for start pattern)
- SHIFT (shift in 4 bits)
- COUNT (waiting for done_counting)
- DONE (wait for ack)

A 4-bit shift register will detect the 1101 pattern in parallel from the serial input, so FSM enters SHIFT when the pattern is detected.

This approach greatly reduces the number of states and the complexity of next-state logic and registers. The shift_count remains 2 bits for the 4 shift cycles.

Outputs remain Moore-type to avoid glitches, and synchronous reset is preserved.

Overall, this reduces area and power by simplifying FSM and combinational logic, while possibly improving timing due to reduced logic depth and fewer states.
