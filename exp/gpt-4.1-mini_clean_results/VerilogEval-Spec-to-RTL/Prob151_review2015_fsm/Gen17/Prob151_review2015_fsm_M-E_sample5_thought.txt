Use a Mealy FSM to detect the pattern 1101 with overlapping inputs by tracking the history of data bits in a 4-bit shift register updated every clock. When the 1101 pattern is detected, transition immediately to a state that enables shifting for 4 cycles using a 2-bit shift counter. After shifting, move to a counting state where counting signal is asserted until done_counting is high, then signal done and wait for ack before resetting. Outputs are generated combinationally from state and counter. This architecture centralizes pattern detection in a shift register, simplifying state complexity and allowing for a minimal 3-state FSM plus a shift counter. This reduces state encoding and transition complexity compared to multi-state pattern search FSM, while maintaining full functionality.
