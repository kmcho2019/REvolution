To improve PPA (timing, power, and area), I will:

- Use one-hot encoding for states to simplify next-state logic and minimize decoding delay, likely improving timing.
- Replace the 2-bit shift_count counter with a 3-bit shift register that shifts in a '1' each cycle during SHIFT state until the highest bit is set. This approach reduces toggling on counter bits and can be clock-gated implicitly by enabling shifts only in the SHIFT state, reducing switching power.
- Use synchronous reset and separate always_ff blocks for sequential logic to maintain clarity.
- Drive outputs combinationally from the current state (Moore style) for glitch-free outputs.
- Keep pattern detection logic as is, since it is already efficient.
- Since one-hot encoding increases flip-flop count, the timing benefits often compensate, and the small increase in area is acceptable.
- Overall, these changes should reduce critical path delay (better timing), reduce toggle rate (better power), and have modest area increase but simpler logic for state decoding.

This approach balances better timing and power with a slight area tradeoff.
