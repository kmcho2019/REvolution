I will implement the FSM using one-hot encoding to reduce combinational logic complexity and potentially improve timing. The FSM will have separate states for each step in detecting the 1101 sequence, shifting 4 bits, counting, and signaling done.

To detect the 1101 pattern, I will use a simple 4-bit shift register of serial input data that updates every cycle. Once the pattern matches, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT state.

In the SHIFT state, a 3-bit shift counter counts exactly 4 cycles, enabling shift_ena output.

After shifting, the FSM moves to COUNT state and asserts counting output while waiting for done_counting input.

Then, it moves to DONE state asserting done output and waits for ack before returning to the IDLE (search) state.

The synchronous reset initializes all registers and sets the FSM to the IDLE state.

This approach separates pattern detection via a small shift register, which simplifies the FSM states and removes multi-step pattern matching inside the FSM states. One-hot encoding simplifies output decoding, and the 3-bit shift counter manages the 4-bit shift enable cycles cleanly.
