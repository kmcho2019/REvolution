To fix the issues:

- The 4-bit shift register used for pattern detection should only shift in new serial bits during SEARCH state to avoid corrupting the detected pattern when in SHIFT state. During SHIFT, the FSM is shifting in delay bits separately (outside this module), so we freeze the pattern detector shift register to prevent false detection or overwriting.

- The shift counter for SHIFT state should start counting exactly when entering SHIFT, and count 4 full cycles with shift_ena asserted for all these cycles. We increment shift_cnt when in SHIFT state. To properly detect the end of the SHIFT period, compare shift_cnt to 3 (0 to 3 counts 4 cycles) but handle state transition on the next clock cycle to guarantee 4 full shift_ena pulses.

- Outputs (shift_ena, counting, done) are registered signals, updated synchronously with state. This ensures outputs change aligned with state transitions, avoiding combinational glitches or timing mismatches.

- On reset, initialize state to SEARCH, clear shift register and shift counter.

This approach ensures pattern detection is stable and isolated, timing of shift_ena and counting are precise, and output signals align perfectly with FSM states.
