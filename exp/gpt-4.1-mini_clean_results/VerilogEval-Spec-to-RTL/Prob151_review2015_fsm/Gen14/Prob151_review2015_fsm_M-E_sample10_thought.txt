Implement the FSM using one-hot encoding for states to reduce next-state logic complexity and improve timing. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101 on the serial data input; shifting in each bit every clock cycle when not resetting. When the pattern is detected, transition to a SHIFT state where a simple 2-bit shift counter increments each cycle to enable shifting for exactly 4 cycles. Then move to COUNT, waiting for done_counting input, followed by DONE state, waiting for ack. Outputs are generated as Moore signals directly from the states and shift counter. This approach blends one-hot FSM encoding with a small counter for shifting, improving timing and power by limiting combinational logic, and reducing area by separating pattern detection from FSM states.
