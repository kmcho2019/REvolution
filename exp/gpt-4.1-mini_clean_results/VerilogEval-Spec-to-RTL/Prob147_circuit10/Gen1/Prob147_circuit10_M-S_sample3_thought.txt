The circuit has one flip-flop storing a bit `state`. At each positive clock edge, `state` updates according to inputs `a`, `b`, and its previous value. The output `q` is purely combinational based on current inputs and stored `state`. 

From the waveform, `state` transitions to the previous `q` value on every clock edge, and `q` equals `(a & ~state) | (b & ~a & state)`.

This simpler logic matches the waveform transitions well and clearly defines how `state` evolves:

- q = (a & ~state) | (b & ~a & state)
- state <= q at each rising clock edge

This approach reduces complexity from XOR logic to simple AND/OR expressions with clear dependency on `a`, `b`, and `state`.
