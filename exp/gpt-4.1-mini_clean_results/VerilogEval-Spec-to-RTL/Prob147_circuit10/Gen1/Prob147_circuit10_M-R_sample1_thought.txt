Refactor the design by separating combinational next-state and output logic from the sequential state update, and using continuous assignments for outputs. Use a single flip-flop to store state, updated on the clock's positive edge. Derive the next state and output q as combinational functions of current state and inputs `a` and `b`. This separation clarifies the design, avoids blocking vs non-blocking assignment confusion, and uses a more idiomatic Verilog style by employing `assign` for combinational signals and `always @(posedge clk)` for sequential state update.
