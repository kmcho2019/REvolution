The circuit has one bit of state updated on the rising edge of clk. From the waveform:

- At a=0, b=0: state holds (no change).
- At a=0, b=1: state becomes 1.
- At a=1, b=1: state becomes 0.
- At a=1, b=0: state remains 0 (no toggling observed).

The earlier assumption of toggling for (a=1,b=0) is incorrect based on the waveform.

Output q matches the current state only when b=1; otherwise q=0.

Thus:
- next_state = state if (a=1 and b=0) or (a=0,b=0)
- next_state = 1 if (a=0,b=1)
- next_state = 0 if (a=1,b=1)

This simplifies to:
next_state = (a==0 & b==1) ? 1 :
             (a==1 & b==1) ? 0 :
             state;

And q = state & b.

This logic matches the waveform transitions and outputs, and is simpler and clearer.
