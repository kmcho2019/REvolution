Analyzing the waveform, the sequential circuit has one state bit 'state' updated on the clock edge and one output 'q' combinationally dependent on inputs and the state.

From the given waveform observations:

- At clock edges, the 'state' is updated.
- 'state' resets to 0 when both a=0 and b=0 (e.g., at 15ns state=0).
- 'state' sets to 1 when a=0 and b=1 (e.g., at 45ns state=1).
- When a=1 and b=1, 'state' is cleared to 0 (e.g., 65ns).
- When a=1 and b=0, 'state' holds its previous value (see from 55ns to 65ns).
- When a=0 and b=0, 'state' clears to 0.
- When a=1 and b=1, 'state' clears to 0.
- When a=0 and b=1, 'state' sets to 1.

So, next_state logic based on inputs and current state can be summarized as:

| a | b | next_state        |
|---|---|-------------------|
| 0 | 0 | 0                 |
| 0 | 1 | 1                 |
| 1 | 0 | state (no change) |
| 1 | 1 | 0                 |
