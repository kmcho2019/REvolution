The circuit is a single-bit state machine updated on the clock's positive edge. From the waveform:

- The output 'state' is the flip-flop value.
- The output 'q' equals 'state'.
- The state only changes (toggles) when both a and b are 1 on the clock edge.
- Otherwise, the state holds its previous value.

This behavior can be implemented simply by toggling the state flip-flop when (a & b) is high at the positive clock edge. Output 'q' just mirrors the state.

This matches the waveform while simplifying the logic.
