This approach is more idiomatic for sequential circuits and avoids potential issues with partial logic or sensitivity lists.

The one-bit `state` flip-flop is updated on the positive edge of `clk`. The next_state is computed from current `state`, `a`, and `b` following the same logical conditions inferred before, but laid out in a clearer case-like structure.

Output `q` is computed combinationally as a function of current `state`, `a`, and `b` using if-else conditions reflecting the waveform behavior more explicitly.

This refactoring will keep the original logic idea but improve readability and maintainability, allowing easier debugging or later adjustments to correct logic if needed.

The core logic remains:

- next_state = (state & ~(a & b)) | (~state & b & ~a)
