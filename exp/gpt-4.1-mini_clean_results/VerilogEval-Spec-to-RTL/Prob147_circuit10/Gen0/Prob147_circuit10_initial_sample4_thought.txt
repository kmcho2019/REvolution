The circuit has one state bit (flip-flop). The output `q` is combinational, depending on current inputs and state.

From the waveform:
- At each rising edge of clk, the state updates with the current `q` value.
- The output `q` depends on inputs `a`, `b` and current state.
- Observing the waveform, the next `state` equals current `q`.
- `q` is high when either:
  * Current state is high and `b` is low, or
  * Current state is low and `a` and `b` are both 1.
- So, `q = (state & ~b) | (~state & a & b)`
- On clk posedge, `state <= q`.

Implement this logic with one flip-flop holding `state`, output `state` is state bit, output `q` is combinational from state and inputs.
