From these, infer:

- q depends on state, a, b.
- q=1 in these cases:
  - state=0 and ((a=0,b=1) or (a=1,b=0))
  - state=1 and ((a=0,b=0) or (a=1,b=1))
- q=0 otherwise.

This matches q = (~state & (a ^ b)) | (state & ~(a ^ b))

which is q = (state & ~(a ^ b)) | (~state & (a ^ b))
