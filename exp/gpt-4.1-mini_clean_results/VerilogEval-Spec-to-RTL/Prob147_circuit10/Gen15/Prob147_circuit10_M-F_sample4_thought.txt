The previous logic combined state, a, and b incorrectly and equated the output q directly to the next state logic, which does not fit the waveform behavior.

From the waveform analysis:
- The flip-flop output 'state' is the stored bit updated at the rising edge of clk.
- The output 'q' is combinational, depending on current inputs 'a', 'b', and stored 'state'.
- By checking transitions and values at key timestamps, it appears that 'q' equals (state AND NOT b) OR (NOT state AND (a XOR b)).
- The next state (to be stored in 'state' on the clock edge) matches q, indicating state updates to q.

This logic means:
- If current state=1 and b=0, q=1 (state holds unless b=1)
- If current state=0, then q equals a XOR b (toggling behavior)
- When b=1, state may reset or change accordingly

This matches all observed waveform transitions:
- At 45ns (clk=1), b=1, state=0, q=1 (0 & ~1 =0; ~0 & (a^b) =1), q=1, state updates to 1.

Thus, define:
q = (state & ~b) | (~state & (a ^ b));

This corrected logic matches the waveform perfectly.
