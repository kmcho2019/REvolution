Analyze the waveform to identify the behavior of the state flip-flop and output q:

- 'state' holds the previous cycle's 'q' value (memory element).
- 'q' depends on current inputs 'a', 'b' and previous 'state' in a specific way.

From the waveform:

- When 'state' is 0 and b=1, q=1 (regardless of a).
- When 'state' is 1 and a=1 and b=1, q=1.
- When 'state' is 1 and a=0 and b=0, q=1.
- Otherwise, q=0.

This suggests:

q = ( (~state) & b ) | ( state & a & b ) | ( state & ~a & ~b )

Rewrite for clarity:

q = (~state & b) | (state & ((a & b) | (~a & ~b)))

The state updates on posedge clk with q. Output 'state' reflects current flip-flop value.

This logic matches waveform trends: 'q' is set when transitioning from state 0 to 1 on b=1, and when in state 1, it either stays 1 if inputs match (a=1,b=1 or a=0,b=0), else resets.
