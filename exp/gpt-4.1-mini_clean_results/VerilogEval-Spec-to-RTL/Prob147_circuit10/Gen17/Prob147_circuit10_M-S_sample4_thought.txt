- next_state = (state & ~(a & b)) | b;

The output `q` directly reflects the current state (i.e., `q = state`).

This simplification matches the waveform behavior: `q` tracks the flip-flop output, and state updates depend mainly on `b` and the condition `(a & b)` when state is 1.
