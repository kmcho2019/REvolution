Implement the TopModule using a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge to store the state flip-flop, and use combinational always @* block to compute next_state and output q based on current inputs and state. This separates the combinational next-state and output logic from the sequential storage clearly.  
From the waveform analysis, next_state should be computed as (state OR b) AND NOT (a AND b), and output q should be the XOR of a, b, and state to match the observed behavior.  
This approach retains the functionality but restructures the logic to combinational always block instead of assign statements for combinational logic, improving clarity and potential extensibility.
