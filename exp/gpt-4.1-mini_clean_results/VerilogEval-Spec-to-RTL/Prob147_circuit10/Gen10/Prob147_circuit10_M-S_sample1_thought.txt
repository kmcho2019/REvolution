From the waveform, the state updates only on clock edges when b=1; otherwise it holds its value. When b=1, state becomes equal to the inverted a (state <= ~a). The output q equals the previous state when b=0, and equals a when b=1.  
This yields simple next_state and q logic:  
- next_state = b ? ~a : state;  
- q = b ? a : state;  
This matches the waveform behavior and reduces complexity.
