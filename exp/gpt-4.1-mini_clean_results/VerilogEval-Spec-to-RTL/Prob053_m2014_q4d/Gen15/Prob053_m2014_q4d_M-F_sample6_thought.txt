To strictly follow the problem, the D flip-flop's D input is the XOR of 'in' and the flip-flop's current output 'out'. The key is to use a separate wire to compute the XOR of 'in' and 'out' (the previous state), and then assign that to 'out' on the positive clock edge with a non-blocking assignment. This clarifies the combinational path and avoids any ambiguity of using 'out' on the RHS of its own assignment within the sequential block. Although the initial value of 'out' is undefined due to no reset, this approach clearly separates combinational and sequential logic, improving simulation and synthesis clarity.
