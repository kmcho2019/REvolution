The simplest and correct approach is to define 'out' as a register updated on the positive edge of 'clk'. Its next value is the XOR of the current input 'in' and the previous value of 'out'. Since nonblocking assignments in Verilog update registers at the end of the always block, the right-hand side 'out' is the old value as desired. This forms the required feedback loop inherently and uses only one always block.
