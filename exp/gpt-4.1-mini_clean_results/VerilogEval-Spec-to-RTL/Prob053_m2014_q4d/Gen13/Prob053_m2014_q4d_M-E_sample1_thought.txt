I will implement the TopModule by explicitly declaring an internal wire for the XOR output, connecting 'in' and the current flip-flop output 'out'. The D flip-flop will store this XOR result on the positive clock edge. Instead of using a behavioral always block, I will implement the flip-flop using continuous assignments and a register with a clocked process. I will use a nonblocking assignment in an always block sensitive to posedge clk for the register update. 

This approach keeps a clean separation between combinational logic (the XOR wire) and sequential logic (the register holding 'out'), making the design straightforward and clearly showing the data flow. This also avoids any functions and directly uses wire/reg signals as per classical RTL style.
