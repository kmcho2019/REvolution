Instead of directly feeding the XOR of the current output and input into the flip-flop, I will introduce an intermediate combinational register (a one-bit wire) that holds the XOR result. Then, I instantiate a positive-edge triggered D flip-flop as a separate module, and connect the XOR output as its D input and the flip-flop output as the final output. This modular approach separates combinational and sequential logic, improving clarity and reusability. Also, the flip-flop module is simple and generic, which can be used elsewhere.
