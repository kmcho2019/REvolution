The output 'out' is itself a register holding the stored state. The D input to the flip-flop is simply the XOR of 'in' and the current stored 'out' value. This feedback can be directly implemented by computing the XOR between 'in' and 'out' before the clock edge, then updating 'out' with that XOR result on the positive edge of 'clk'. No intermediate registers are needed, which simplifies the design.
