The original design calls for a D flip-flop that captures the XOR of 'in' and the current output 'out' on every positive clock edge without any enable conditions. The previously submitted code introduces an unnecessary enable condition that alters the functional behavior, leading to simulation mismatches.

To fix this, I will remove the enable logic and update the flip-flop unconditionally on every positive clock edge. This adheres strictly to the specification, ensuring the flip-flop captures the XOR output on every clock rising edge.

This approach guarantees correct functionality, minimal complexity, and good synthesis results without extra logic for enabling the flip-flop. This is the cleanest and most straightforward solution.
