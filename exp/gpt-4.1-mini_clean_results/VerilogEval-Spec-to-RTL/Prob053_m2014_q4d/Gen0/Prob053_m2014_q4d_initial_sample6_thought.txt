Implement a TopModule with inputs clk and in, and output out. Inside the module, instantiate a D flip-flop that on the positive edge of clk updates its output. The D input to the flip-flop is the XOR of the current input 'in' and the current output 'out'. Since out is also the output of the flip-flop, this creates a feedback loop where the next state depends on the XOR of in and the current stored value.
