Implement a D flip-flop triggered on the positive edge of clk. The input to the D flip-flop is the XOR of 'in' and the current output 'out'. Since the output depends on its previous state and the input, we model this feedback by using a reg to hold the state of the flip-flop. The XOR is computed combinationally with the input 'in' and the current stored output, and the result is latched into the flip-flop on the rising edge of clk.
