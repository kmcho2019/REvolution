Implement the same functionality by separating the combinational XOR logic and the flip-flop register explicitly: create a wire for the next state which is the XOR of 'in' and the current flip-flop output; then capture this wire into a register at the positive edge of clk. The output port is a wire driven by the current register value, thus cleanly separating combinational and sequential logic. This clarifies the data flow and keeps the design easy to analyze while producing identical behavior without reset.
