The original design uses a D flip-flop implemented with an always block and a register output. To refactor, I will implement the D flip-flop as a separate module instantiated inside the TopModule, separating the combinational XOR logic from the sequential flip-flop logic. This modularization clarifies the design by isolating the state-holding element. The XOR output feeds directly into the D input of the flip-flop. The flip-flop module will be a positive edge-triggered DFF without reset, matching the original behavior but structured as a reusable component.
