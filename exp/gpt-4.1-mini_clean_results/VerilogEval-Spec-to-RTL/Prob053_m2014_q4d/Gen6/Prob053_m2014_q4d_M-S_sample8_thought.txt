Since the output 'out' is driven by a D flip-flop clocked on 'clk' with input as XOR of 'in' and 'out', we can implement this directly without an initial block. Removing the initial block simplifies the code, while the behavior is functionally equivalent. The lack of explicit initialization means the initial value of 'out' is undefined, but this matches the original specification of no reset and avoids simulation-synthesis mismatch caused by initial blocks.
