To fix the initial unknown state of the flip-flop output 'out' and thus ensure correct initial behavior, explicitly initialize 'out' to a known value (0) at start-up. This avoids the undefined initial value that causes simulation mismatches on the first clock edge. Since the problem states no reset, initialization can be done with an initial block. This also does not affect synthesis on real FPGAs/ASICs that support initialization or will synthesize a reset-less flip-flop with known power-up state. This approach preserves simplicity, correctness, and does not add extra logic or critical path delay, maintaining optimal performance and area.
