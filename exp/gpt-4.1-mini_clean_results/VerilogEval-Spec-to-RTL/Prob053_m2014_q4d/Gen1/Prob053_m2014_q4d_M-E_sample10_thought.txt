Implement the TopModule by internally instantiating a separate D flip-flop module that receives the XOR of 'in' and the flip-flop's current output as its D input. To break direct combinational feedback on the output signal, use an intermediate wire 'ff_out' to hold the flip-flop's output. Then assign 'out' to 'ff_out'. This approach clearly separates the combinational XOR logic, the flip-flop state storage, and the output assignment, offering a modular and clean architecture different from embedding the flip-flop logic directly in TopModule.
