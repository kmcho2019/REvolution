To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will simplify the implementation by removing the separate combinational register `next_out` and directly assigning the XOR expression to the flip-flop's D input inside the sequential block. This reduces the number of signals and simplifies synthesis.

Directly coding `out <= in ^ out;` inside the sequential always block combines combinational and sequential logic in a clean, minimal way, potentially reducing gate count and propagation delay.

The behavior remains exactly the same and adheres fully to the specification (positive-edge-triggered D flip-flop with no reset, D input as XOR of `in` and current `out`).

This change also slightly improves readability and resource utilization without sacrificing clarity or correctness.
