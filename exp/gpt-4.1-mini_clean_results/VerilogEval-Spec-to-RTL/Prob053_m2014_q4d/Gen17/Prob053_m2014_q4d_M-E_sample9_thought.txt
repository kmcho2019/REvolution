To separate combinational and sequential logic clearly, I will define an intermediate wire to hold the XOR result of the input 'in' and the current flip-flop output 'out'. This wire represents the D input to the flip-flop. The flip-flop will capture this wire's value at every positive clock edge. This modularizes the design, clarifies the data flow, and avoids mixing read/write of 'out' in the same always block.
