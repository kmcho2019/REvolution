To fix the initial mismatch caused by the undefined initial state of the flip-flop, explicitly initialize the internal register `prev_state` to a known value (e.g., 0) at declaration. Although there is no reset signal, synthesis tools allow registers to be initialized this way for simulation purposes. This will ensure consistent startup behavior without violating the specification of no reset. The rest of the design remains unchanged, maintaining minimal logic with the XOR feeding the D input of the flip-flop.
