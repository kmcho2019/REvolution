Since the problem specifies no reset, the output of the flip-flop is initially undefined. To make simulation results deterministic and avoid unknown states, we can initialize 'out' to 0 at declaration. This initial value does not affect synthesis in most FPGA/ASIC tools (it is interpreted as power-up default if supported). This ensures correct simulation behavior without contradicting the "no reset" requirement and keeps the design minimal with the required feedback XOR logic.
