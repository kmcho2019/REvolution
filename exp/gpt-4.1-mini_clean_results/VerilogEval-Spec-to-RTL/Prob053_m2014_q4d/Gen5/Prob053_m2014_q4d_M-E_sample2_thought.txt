Instead of directly storing 'out' and updating it by XOR'ing 'in' with 'out', we can implement the same behavior by using a two-stage pipeline of flip-flops to delay and generate the output. The first flip-flop captures the input 'in' at each clock edge, and the second flip-flop captures the XOR of the delayed 'in' and the current 'out'. This adds latency but is functionally equivalent and demonstrates an alternative approach by decomposing the XOR and flip-flop into sequential stages, emphasizing signal flow and pipelining.
