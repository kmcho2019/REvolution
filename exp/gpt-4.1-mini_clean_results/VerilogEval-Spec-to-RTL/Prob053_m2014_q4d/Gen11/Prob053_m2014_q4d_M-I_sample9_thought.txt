To optimize the original modular solution for better performance, power, and area, I will inline the XOR logic directly inside the always block that implements the D flip-flop behavior. This removes separate module instantiations, reducing gate overhead and potential propagation delay from the XOR gate output wire. The feedback loop is implemented by storing the flip-flop output in a reg and computing the XOR with the input within the same sequential block. This approach provides a clean, compact design with minimal logic levels, improving timing and reducing area and power from unnecessary module interfaces.
