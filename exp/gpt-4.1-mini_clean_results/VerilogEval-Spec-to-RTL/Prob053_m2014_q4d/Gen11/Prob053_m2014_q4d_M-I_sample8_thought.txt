To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will remove the intermediate `xor_in` register and directly compute the XOR inline at the flip-flop input. This reduces the logic depth, removes an unnecessary register, and lowers switching activity. The output `out` remains a `reg` to store the flip-flop state. The logic stays functionally identical, with the D flip-flop input being `in ^ out`, and no reset to match the problem specification.
