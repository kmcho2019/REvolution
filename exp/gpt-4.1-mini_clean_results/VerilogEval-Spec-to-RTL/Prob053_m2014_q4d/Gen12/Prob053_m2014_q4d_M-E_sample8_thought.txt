Instead of directly feeding back the output of the flip-flop into the XOR gate, this design uses a two-stage shift register internally to store the history of the previous output. The XOR now operates on 'in' and the value from one cycle ago, held in an intermediate register. On each clock edge, the shift register advances, and the new output is driven by the latest XOR result. This architecture provides the same functional behavior with a slight pipeline of the feedback signal, offering a different structure and potentially better timing characteristics by breaking the combinational loop inside one clock cycle.
