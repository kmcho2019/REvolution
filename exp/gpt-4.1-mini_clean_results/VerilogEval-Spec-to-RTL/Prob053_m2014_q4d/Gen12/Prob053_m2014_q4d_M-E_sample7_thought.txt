Instead of directly using a wire for the XOR and feeding it to the flip-flop, we can implement the XOR functionally inside the always block using a temporary register. This approach encapsulates all logic inside the sequential block, avoiding a separate combinational wire. This might slightly simplify synthesis tools' inference and clarifies the feedback loop internally. The output 'out' remains a reg updated on the positive clock edge. Functionally, the flip-flop still captures the XOR of 'in' and the previous 'out' value. This method uses only one always block and no explicit combinational assign statements.
