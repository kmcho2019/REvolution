Implement the D flip-flop behavior using a single always block with a non-blocking assignment, but instead of using a separate reg for the state, declare the output 'out' as a reg directly, and update it on the positive edge of clk. The XOR input is computed inside the sequential always block based on the current 'out' and 'in'.
