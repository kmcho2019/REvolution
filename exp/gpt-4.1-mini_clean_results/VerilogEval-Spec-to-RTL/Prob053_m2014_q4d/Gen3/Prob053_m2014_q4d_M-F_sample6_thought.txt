Since no reset is allowed and synthesis tools typically ignore 'initial' blocks for flip-flops, the best approach is to remove the initial block entirely and accept that 'out' has an undefined initial state in hardware. This preserves the intended logic while avoiding simulation vs. synthesis mismatches caused by forced initialization. The design remains minimal and straightforward: on each positive clock edge, 'out' updates to 'in' XOR 'out', inherently implementing a toggle behavior if 'in' is high. The output is declared as reg and updated synchronously.
