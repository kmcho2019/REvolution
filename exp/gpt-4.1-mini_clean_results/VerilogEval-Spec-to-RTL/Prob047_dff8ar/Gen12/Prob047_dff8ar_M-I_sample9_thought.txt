To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we keep the asynchronous reset but simplify the design by removing the extra submodule instantiation, which saves some overhead. Instead, we define the 8-bit asynchronous reset DFFs directly inside TopModule in one always block. This reduces module overhead and might improve synthesis results. Also, using a single vector register reduces redundant clock gating logic. The asynchronous reset remains as specified for immediate reset response. This solution is minimal and synthesis-friendly, potentially reducing area and slight power savings due to less hierarchy and simpler structure.
