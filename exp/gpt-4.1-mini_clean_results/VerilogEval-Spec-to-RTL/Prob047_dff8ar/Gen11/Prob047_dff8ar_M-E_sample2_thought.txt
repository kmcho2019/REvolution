Implement the TopModule using eight individual 1-bit D flip-flop modules with asynchronous reset, each instantiated separately. This architectural choice emphasizes modularity and explicitness, making each flip-flop a standalone component.

Each DFF module has its own asynchronous reset and clock input, triggering on the positive edge of clk and resetting its output q to 0 asynchronously.

In the TopModule, instantiate eight of these 1-bit DFFs, connecting each bit of the 8-bit input vector d to the corresponding flip-flop input, and concatenating the outputs to form the 8-bit output q.

This approach gives clear hierarchical structure, making debugging and verification easier, and facilitates potential per-bit modifications or extensions without altering a large vector module.
