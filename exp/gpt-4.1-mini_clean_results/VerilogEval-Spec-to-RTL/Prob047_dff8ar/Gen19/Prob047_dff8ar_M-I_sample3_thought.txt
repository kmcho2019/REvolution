To optimize for power, area, and maintain performance with this simple 8-bit asynchronous reset DFF array, I will:

- Integrate an enable signal that only updates outputs when inputs change, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power.
- Implement input data comparison within the always block to determine if the flip-flops need updating.
- This approach keeps the asynchronous reset intact, ensuring immediate reset response.
- This technique reduces switching activity, lowering power without adding clock gating complexity or increasing area significantly.
- Maintain the modular and clean structure with the parameterized vector DFF module.

This balances power efficiency improvements while maintaining simplicity, performance, and area efficiency.
