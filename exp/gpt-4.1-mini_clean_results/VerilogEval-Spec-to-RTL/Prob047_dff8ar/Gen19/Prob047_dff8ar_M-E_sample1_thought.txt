Implement eight individual D flip-flop submodules with asynchronous reset, each handling one bit of the input independently. Instantiate these submodules within TopModule in a generate loop for modular clarity and easy bit-wise control. This fine-grain approach allows potential future extensions (like per-bit enable signals or scan chains), provides explicit one-to-one mapping from inputs to outputs, and maintains all problem requirements with asynchronous active-high reset and positive clock edge triggering.
