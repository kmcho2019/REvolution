Implement the 8-bit register with asynchronous reset using a single always block, treating the entire 8-bit vector as a single register. This approach consolidates the flip-flops into one vector, improving code compactness and potentially reducing area and power by enabling synthesis tools to optimize the register as a single unit instead of eight separate modules. The asynchronous reset will reset the entire 8-bit output vector to zero on the positive edge of areset, and the data will be latched on the positive edge of clk.
