To optimize for area and power, replace the array of eight individual single-bit DFF modules with a single vector register with asynchronous reset. This reduces module instantiation overhead and enables synthesis tools to use optimized register primitives. The asynchronous reset remains active-high and resets the entire 8-bit output vector at once, preserving functionality. This approach simplifies the design, lowers area, and potentially reduces switching power by minimizing interconnect and logic replication.
