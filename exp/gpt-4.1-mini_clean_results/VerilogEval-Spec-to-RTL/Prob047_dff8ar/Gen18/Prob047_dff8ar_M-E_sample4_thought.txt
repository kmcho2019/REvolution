Implement eight individual single-bit asynchronous reset D flip-flop modules and instantiate them separately in TopModule, each handling one bit of the 8-bit input/output vector. This granular approach explicitly shows each flip-flop and its connection, improving readability for each bit and potentially enabling fine-grained control or modification per bit if needed in future enhancements.

The asynchronous reset and clock edge sensitivity is managed inside each single-bit DFF module. The TopModule simply wires together these eight DFF instances to form an 8-bit register.

This approach sacrifices some compactness but improves modularity and clarity at the bit level, facilitating easier debugging and potential per-bit customization. It also better demonstrates the fundamental structural hierarchy in digital design.
