To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will break down the 8-bit vectorized DFF into 8 individual single-bit D flip-flops with asynchronous reset. This can potentially enable better clock gating or power gating at a finer granularity since each flip-flop is instantiated separately. It may also allow synthesis tools to optimize each flip-flop independently for area and power.

I will define a simple single-bit DFF module with async active-high reset. Then instantiate it 8 times inside TopModule, connecting each bit of `d` to the corresponding flip-flop and collecting outputs on `q`.

This approach:
- Improves power by enabling potential fine-grained gating.
- May improve area by allowing synthesis optimizations per bit.
- Maintains clean and understandable design.
- Maintains correct asynchronous reset functionality.

The single-bit DFF module's code is minimal, and instantiations in TopModule are explicit, improving modularity and aiding power/area optimizations during synthesis.
