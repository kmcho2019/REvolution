Implement eight separate single-bit D flip-flop modules each with asynchronous reset, then instantiate them individually inside TopModule. This approach emphasizes component granularity and explicit instantiation, allowing potential fine-grained control or future per-bit customization. Although this might increase module instantiation overhead slightly compared to a vector approach, it simplifies debugging at the bit level and clarifies signal flow for each bit, which can be beneficial during verification or modification. All DFFs are triggered on the clock's positive edge and asynchronously reset high to zero, as requested.
