Implement the TopModule using a single always block that handles all eight bits of the input and output simultaneously. This approach uses a vector-based asynchronous reset D flip-flop design, grouping all flip-flops into one register.

The always block is sensitive to the positive edge of clk and the positive edge of the asynchronous reset signal (areset). When areset is asserted, the entire 8-bit output vector is reset to zero asynchronously. Otherwise, on each positive edge of clk, the register captures the 8-bit input vector.

This design is compact, efficient in area and power due to consolidated logic, and easy to read and maintain. It also leverages synthesis tools' ability to optimize vector registers effectively, ensuring all flip-flops share the same reset and clock signals without duplication.
