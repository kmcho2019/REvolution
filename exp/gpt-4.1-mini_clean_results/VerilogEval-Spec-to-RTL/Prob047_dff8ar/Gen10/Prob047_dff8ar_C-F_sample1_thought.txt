To create a superior solution, fuse the modularity and clarity of single-bit asynchronous reset DFFs from Example 1 with the area and power efficiency of vectorized DFFs from Example 2 by instantiating eight single-bit DFF_AR modules inside TopModule. Each DFF_AR implements a single-bit flip-flop with asynchronous active-high reset triggered on posedge clk or posedge areset.

This approach achieves:
- High modularity and ease of verification/debugging by using a well-defined single-bit DFF module,
- Explicit asynchronous reset in each DFF_AR module ensuring correct and clear reset behavior,
- Vectorization done structurally via 8 instances instead of a single large vector register, which can improve power gating granularity and synthesis tool optimization,
- Explicit connections for each bit simplify timing analysis per flip-flop and allow for potential selective power optimizations,
- No extra combinational logic or redundant wires, maintaining clean synthesis results.

Thus, the design balances modularity, clarity, reuse, area, and power optimization opportunities while fully meeting the specification.
