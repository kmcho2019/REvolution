Implement the entire 8-bit D flip-flop register with asynchronous active-high reset using a single always block. The reset and data update logic will be handled together, which reduces module instantiations, simplifies the design, and potentially lowers area and power by enabling the synthesis tool to optimize a single 8-bit register with shared asynchronous reset. This approach avoids multiple one-bit flip-flops, instead creating a vector register that asynchronously resets all bits simultaneously on areset and captures input data at the rising clock edge. This design is straightforward, concise, and efficient.
