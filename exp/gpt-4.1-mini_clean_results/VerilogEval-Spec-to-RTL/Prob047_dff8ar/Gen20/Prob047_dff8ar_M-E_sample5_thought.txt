Implement the TopModule by using an array of 8 individual single-bit D flip-flop modules, each with its own asynchronous reset and clock input. This explicit per-bit instantiation avoids vector registers and allows independent control and clear visibility into each bit's flip-flop behavior. Each DFF module has a simple always block sensitive to posedge clk or posedge areset, asynchronously clearing its output when areset is asserted, otherwise capturing d on clock edge. This bitwise approach enhances clarity of asynchronous reset on a per-bit basis and enables fine-grained scalability. The TopModule wires outputs by concatenating all 8 bits from each DFF instance. This approach is straightforward, easily testable, and synthesizable while differing architecturally from a vectorized implementation.
