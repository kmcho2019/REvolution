Implement the TopModule by explicitly instantiating eight separate single-bit D flip-flop modules with asynchronous active-high reset. Each single-bit DFF module has its own always block sensitive to posedge clk or posedge areset, allowing fine-grained control and easy extension. This modular bitwise instantiation contrasts with vectorized register approach, enhancing testability and facilitating potential bitwise customizations or clock gating in future extensions.

By instantiating eight single-bit DFFs, the design offers:
- Clear mapping between each input bit and its corresponding flip-flop output.
- Independent flip-flop behavior, which may aid debugging or detailed timing analysis.
- Explicit asynchronous reset handling in each DFF.
- Modular hierarchy, easing integration into larger designs where individual bits might be controlled differently.

Though this approach might incur slightly more area and power than a fully vectorized register, it improves readability, modular reuse of the DFF module, and potential for per-bit customizations or specialized synthesis optimizations.
