To optimize the original design for better PPA metrics, consolidate the eight individual D flip-flops into a single vectorized always block with asynchronous reset. This reduces module overhead and potentially allows synthesis tools to better optimize register packing, thereby decreasing area. Additionally, handling the asynchronous reset vector-wise reduces the number of reset signals toggling independently, which can reduce power consumption. This approach still meets all requirements: positive-edge triggered flip-flops with asynchronous active-high reset, resetting outputs to zero. It maintains clean, synthesizable RTL and improves area and power without impacting performance.
