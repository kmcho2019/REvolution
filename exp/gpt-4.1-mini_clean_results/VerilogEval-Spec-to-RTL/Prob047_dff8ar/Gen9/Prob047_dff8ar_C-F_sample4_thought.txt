Combine the clarity and modularity of single-bit D flip-flop modules with the efficiency of a vectorized register. Define a parameterized single-bit D flip-flop with asynchronous active-high reset for clarity and potential reuse. Then, in TopModule, implement a single always block handling the entire 8-bit vector asynchronously resetting q to zero and capturing d on clk's rising edge. 

This fusion maintains clean, reusable DFF logic at the module level but benefits from the area and power advantages of vectorized register implementation in TopModule. The asynchronous reset is preserved in the sensitivity list, meeting the specification fully.

By doing so, the design achieves:
- Modular clarity and ease of verification via a reusable single-bit DFF module.
- Compact and power/area-efficient vectorized always block for the actual register storage.
- Correct asynchronous reset semantics.
This approach balances readability, modularity, synthesis efficiency, and PPA for the 8-bit asynchronous reset D flip-flop register.
