To optimize for area and possibly improve power, I will remove the hierarchy of single-bit DFF modules and implement the 8-bit asynchronous reset DFF array directly in a single always block with a vector register. This flattens the design hierarchy, potentially reducing synthesis overhead and area.

The asynchronous reset will be implemented as a single asynchronous reset event triggering the entire register vector update. This approach also avoids multiple module instantiations, helping synthesis tools to optimize the flip-flops collectively. It preserves the functional requirements: positive edge clock triggering and active high asynchronous reset to zero.

Overall, this reduces area by minimizing module overhead and should maintain or slightly improve power and timing.
