Fuse the modular clarity and reusability of a single-bit DFF with async reset from Example 1, and the synthesis- and power-friendly approach of grouping them into a single 8-bit register from Example 2. 

To achieve this, define a parameterizable single-bit asynchronous reset DFF module for reusability and clear semantics. Internally, in TopModule, instantiate an 8-bit vector register with an asynchronous reset for the 8 flip-flops, using a single always block. 

However, to maintain modularity, instantiate eight of the single-bit DFFs internally in a generate loop *inside* the parameterized 8-bit vector DFF module, rather than writing separate always blocks or multiple distinct modules. This leverages the modular single-bit DFF unit, avoids repetitive code, preserves clarity, and guides synthesis to optimize area and power well, thanks to vectorizing the reset and clock control in one place.

This fusion benefits from:
- Modular single-bit DFF definition (clear, reusable).
- Generate loops for scalability and clarity.
- Vectorized storage and asynchronous reset to aid synthesis.
- Clear separation of concerns: single-bit behavior defined once, and vector behavior instantiated logically.

This approach offers the best balance of clarity, maintainability, modularity, and synthesis-friendly power and area characteristics.
