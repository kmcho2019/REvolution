Fuse the clarity and modularity of individual single-bit asynchronous reset DFF modules with the area and synthesis efficiency of a vector-based implementation by creating a parameterized vector DFF module that internally instantiates single-bit DFFs using a generate loop.

This approach maintains clear abstraction at the bit-level with reusable single-bit DFF modules, while enabling vector-wide instantiation for clean and scalable code. It also leverages the clear timing and reset semantics of single-bit DFF modules and allows easy extension of width by parameter.

The TopModule simply instantiates this parameterized vector DFF with width 8, connecting the 8-bit input and output buses directly. This fusion optimizes modularity, clarity, reusability, and area efficiency while meeting asynchronous reset and positive edge clock triggering specs.
