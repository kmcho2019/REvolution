Implement each of the 8 D flip-flops as individual one-bit modules with asynchronous active-high reset, then instantiate them in the top-level module. This approach modularizes the design clearly, allowing each flip-flop to be tested and optimized independently, and facilitates scalability to other bit-widths. The top module acts as an array of these 1-bit DFFs, collecting their outputs into an 8-bit vector.

The asynchronous reset is included in each flip-flop's sensitivity list to ensure immediate clearing upon reset. This fine-grained modularization enhances code readability, debug ease, and potential for parameterization or future enhancements without sacrificing clarity or correctness.
