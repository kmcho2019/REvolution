Implement the 8-bit register with asynchronous reset as a single always block that handles the entire 8-bit vector in one piece of sequential logic. This approach simplifies the code by using a vector register instead of multiple single-bit instances. The always block is triggered on the positive edge of clk and asynchronously on the positive edge of areset. When areset is high, the whole output register is reset to zero simultaneously. This approach reduces module instantiations and can potentially improve area and power by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the vector register as a whole.
