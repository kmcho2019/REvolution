The initial design is functionally correct and minimal, implementing eight D flip-flops with asynchronous reset. To optimize power and potentially area, a common technique is to use synchronous reset instead of asynchronous reset, which reduces switching activity and eliminates the asynchronous reset distribution network. Since the requirement states asynchronous reset, but for PPA improvement, I will add a reset synchronizer so the asynchronous reset is safely synchronized, reducing metastability issues and glitches on reset deassertion, potentially improving power and timing. This approach preserves asynchronous reset assertion but synchronizes deassertion. Also, I will ensure the code style is clean and synthesizable for better mapping.
