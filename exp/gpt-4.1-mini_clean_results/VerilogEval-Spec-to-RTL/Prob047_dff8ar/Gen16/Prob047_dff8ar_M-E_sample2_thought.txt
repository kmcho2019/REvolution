Implement the TopModule by instantiating eight individual 1-bit D flip-flops with asynchronous reset, each handling one bit of the input and output. This fine-grained approach clearly maps each bit to a separate flip-flop instance, improving debug visibility and potential bit-wise customizations. The asynchronous reset is active high and resets each flip-flop output to zero asynchronously. The always block in each flip-flop is triggered on the posedge of clk or areset. Non-blocking assignments ensure proper synthesis behavior. This approach emphasizes modularity by defining a small single-bit DFF module and instantiating it eight times in a generate loop within the TopModule.
