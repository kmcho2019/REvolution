To optimize the existing design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the vectorized DFF_AR module with eight individually instantiated single-bit asynchronous reset D flip-flops.

This approach enables:
- Potential finer-grained power gating or clock gating by allowing selective enable or power management per flip-flop bit, which can reduce power.
- Slight improvement in area as synthesis tools can optimize individual bits separately, possibly mapping to smaller hardware primitives.
- Maintains the asynchronous active-high reset on each flip-flop as per specification.

Additionally, I will implement a single-bit DFF_AR module with asynchronous reset to ensure clarity and reuse, but instantiate it bitwise in TopModule rather than as a vector.

This small modular refinement often helps synthesis tools optimize flip-flop packing, lowering power and area while maintaining equivalent timing.

The functionality remains unchanged: positive-edge triggered flip-flops, asynchronous active-high reset resetting outputs to zero, and capturing input data at clock edge.
