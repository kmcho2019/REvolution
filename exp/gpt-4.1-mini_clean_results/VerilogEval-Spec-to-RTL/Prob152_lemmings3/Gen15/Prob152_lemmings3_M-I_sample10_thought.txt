To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) while preserving correctness, I will:

- Simplify direction switching logic by merging conditions to reduce redundant checks and logic.
- Use non-blocking assignments only in the combinational always block for clearer synthesis interpretation.
- Minimize input gating by removing unnecessary multiple condition checks.
- Use localparams instead of typedef to reduce area slightly in some synthesis tools.
- Ensure bump inputs are sampled only when ground is present and Lemming is walking (since bumps have no effect otherwise), reducing combinational dependencies and switching.
- Overall, maintain the modular style, but with trimmed and simplified logic to reduce critical path and dynamic power.

These minor refinements can lead to shorter critical paths and less toggling, improving PPA.
