To improve PPA, I will focus on simplifying combinational logic and removing redundant state assignments to reduce logic depth and switching activity. Specifically:
- Remove unnecessary `state <= state;` assignments which can generate redundant logic.
- Combine bump condition checks into a single expression to avoid repeated conditionals.
- Encode the bump handling logic as a single expression for next direction, reducing logic gates.
- Use a localparam for direction to clarify assignments.
- Ensure outputs remain purely combinational from state.
- This optimization reduces gate count and critical path by minimizing complex nested conditionals, potentially improving timing and power with equal or smaller area.
