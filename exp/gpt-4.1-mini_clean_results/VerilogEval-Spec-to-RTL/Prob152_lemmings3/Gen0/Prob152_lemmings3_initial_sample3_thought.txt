Implement a Moore FSM with these states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, DIGGING, FALLING.
- On async posedge areset, state resets to WALK_LEFT.
- Outputs are assigned based on current state.
- State transitions on clk posedge according to inputs and precedence:
  1. If ground=0, enter FALLING (unless already falling).
  2. Else if dig=1 and currently walking on ground, enter DIGGING.
  3. Else if bumped (bump_left or bump_right), switch walking direction.
- When falling and ground=1, return to previous walking direction.
- When digging and ground=0, enter FALLING.
- While falling or digging, bumps and dig inputs are ignored.
- Keep track of walking direction to resume after fall.
