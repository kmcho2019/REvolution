Design a Moore FSM with states encoding both direction and mode: walking left, walking right, falling (aaah!), digging left, and digging right. On async posedge reset, state initializes to walking left.

Inputs: bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig.

Priority of events on each clock:
1. If ground=0 and not already falling, transition to falling state (aaah! output).
2. Else if dig=1, ground=1, and walking (not falling or digging), transition to digging state in the current direction.
3. Else if bumped (bump_left or bump_right) and walking (and not falling/digging), switch direction.
4. Otherwise stay in current state.

While falling, bump or dig inputs have no effect. On ground rising from 0 to 1, falling state transitions back to walking in previous direction.

While digging, ground=0 causes transition to falling state. Bumps and dig inputs do not affect state.

Outputs depend only on state (Moore machine):

- walk_left: 1 when walking or digging left
- walk_right: 1 when walking or digging right
- aaah: 1 when falling
- digging: 1 when digging (either direction)

Use an FSM with these states (encoded as enum):

- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- FALLING_LEFT
- FALLING_RIGHT
- DIGGING_LEFT
- DIGGING_RIGHT

When falling or digging states are entered, store the previous walking direction to know where to return.

This FSM is implemented with synchronous state update on clk posedge and async positive reset.
