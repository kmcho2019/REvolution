Model the Lemming behavior as a Moore finite state machine with three main states:

- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- FALLING
- DIGGING_LEFT
- DIGGING_RIGHT

On reset, the Lemming walks left.

Transitions:
- If ground == 0 (fall condition), transition to FALLING from any walking or digging state.
- If dig == 1 and currently walking on ground, transition to DIGGING in the current walking direction.
- If bumped on left or right and walking on ground (not falling or digging), switch walking direction.
- While falling or digging, bumps are ignored.
- Digging continues until ground == 0, then falls.
- Falling continues until ground == 1, then resumes walking in previous direction.

Outputs:
- walk_left = 1 when in WALK_LEFT or DIGGING_LEFT state
- walk_right = 1 when in WALK_RIGHT or DIGGING_RIGHT state
- aaah = 1 when in FALLING state
- digging = 1 when in DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT state

Implement asynchronous reset to WALK_LEFT.

All state transitions occur on the positive clock edge.

Use a registered state and next_state.

Note: The order of precedence for conditions is fall > dig > bump.
