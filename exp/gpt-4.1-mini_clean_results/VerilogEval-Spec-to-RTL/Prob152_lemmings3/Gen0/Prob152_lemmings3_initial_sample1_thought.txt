Implement a Moore FSM with four states representing the Lemming's behavior: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Outputs depend only on the current state. The state transitions respect priority: falling has the highest precedence, then digging, then bump direction changes. On reset, state is walking left. While walking, if ground=0, transition to falling; else if dig=1 and ground=1, transition to digging; else if bumped, switch walking direction. While falling, remain falling until ground=1, then return to prior walking direction. While digging, stay digging while ground=1; when ground=0, transition to falling; after falling ends, resume walking in the same direction as before digging and falling. Bump inputs during falling or digging do not affect direction. The walking direction is stored as part of the state or with a separate variable to keep track through falling/digging. Outputs encode current state.
