Implement a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling (aaah), and digging. The outputs are determined solely by the state. The state transitions depend on inputs bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. Priority of transitions is fall > dig > switch direction. On reset, go to walking left. When falling, remember the walking direction before the fall so we can resume it when ground returns. While digging, continue until ground disappears, then fall, then resume walking in the original direction. Bumps only affect walking states and only when not falling or digging.
