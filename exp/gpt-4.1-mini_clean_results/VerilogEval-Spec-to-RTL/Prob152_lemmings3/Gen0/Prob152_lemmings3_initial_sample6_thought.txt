Design a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling left, falling right, digging left, and digging right. The outputs are derived from the state. The asynchronous reset initializes to walk left. 

On each clock edge:
- If areset, reset to walk left.
- Else, update state:
  - If falling (ground=0), stay falling.
  - If falling and ground=1, resume walking in previous direction.
  - If not falling and ground=0, transition to falling in current walking direction.
  - If walking on ground and dig=1, start digging in current walking direction.
  - If digging and ground=0, transition to falling in the same direction.
  - If walking and bump on either side, switch walking direction.
  
Priority:
1. Falling has highest priority.
2. Then digging.
3. Then bump/direction change.

Outputs:
- walk_left is high only in walking left state.
- walk_right is high only in walking right state.
- aaah is high in falling states.
- digging is high in digging states.

The bump signal affects direction only when walking on ground and not digging or falling.

Implement state encoding with parameters. Separate sequential logic and output logic.
