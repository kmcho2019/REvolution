I will implement the FSM with separate state bits for direction (dir) and mode (mode: WALK, FALL, DIG), but will explicitly encode the modes as separate one-hot signals to simplify state transitions and outputs. This way, the walking direction and mode are more clearly separated. The logic will use a combinational process that considers all input signals and current state, applying the priority rules fall > dig > bump to decide next mode and direction. The asynchronous reset will initialize the Lemming to WALK left. Outputs are assigned combinationally from the one-hot encoded states and direction bit. This architecture improves readability and modularity by separating direction and mode state encoding and leveraging one-hot states to simplify output logic.
