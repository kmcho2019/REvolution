Refactor the design into a classic Moore FSM with a single state register encoding all states (walking_left, walking_right, falling_left, falling_right, digging_left, digging_right) instead of separate bits for mode, direction, and digging flags. This way, the state fully determines outputs and transitions, and direction and mode are implicit in the state encoding. The FSM transitions are captured in a case statement, with priority fall > dig > bump direction switching implemented in the state transition logic. This classic explicit FSM style enhances clarity of states and transitions, suitable for formal verification and easier debugging.
