Implement the FSM using separate mode and direction state registers to clearly separate the Lemming's mode (walking, falling, digging) from its direction (left or right). Use two 1-bit registers: one for mode (2 bits encoded for walking=00, falling=01, digging=10) and one for direction (0=left, 1=right).

This split architecture allows for straightforward handling of precedence: falling overrides dig and bump; digging overrides bump; walking responds to bumps. Direction changes only occur in walking mode when bumped. Falling and digging modes keep the direction stable regardless of bumps or dig signals.

The FSM transitions are coded in a clear priority order in combinational logic, simplifying debug and verification. Outputs are decoded combinationally from mode and direction registers. Asynchronous reset sets mode to walking and direction to left.

This approach increases code clarity by explicit separation of behavioral aspects and reduces complex state encodings. It makes adding extra behaviors or debugging easier.
