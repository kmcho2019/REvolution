Use a single 3-bit state encoding to represent both mode and direction in one vector, simplifying state transitions. Define states for walking left/right, digging left/right, and falling (direction preserved in a separate reg). The direction updates occur only on bump signals while walking, stored in direction reg separately. When falling or digging, direction stays unchanged. On ground loss, transition to falling. On dig input and grounded while walking, transition to digging. On ground return from falling or digging, revert to walking with preserved direction. Outputs are derived directly from the current state. This approach encapsulates direction and mode in combined states, reducing conditional checks and making transitions explicit and symmetrical for each direction.
