Implement the FSM with two separate registers: one bit for direction (0=left,1=right), and two bits for mode (00=walk,01=fall,10=dig). Use a priority-encoded combinational logic block to determine next mode and direction separately. The direction only changes on walking mode when bumped. Falling mode ignores bumps and dig inputs. Digging mode ignores bumps but transitions to falling if ground is lost. On areset, reset to walking left. Outputs are derived from mode and direction signals. This separation of direction and mode registers simplifies logic and makes direction updates explicit, potentially improving synthesis and clarity.
