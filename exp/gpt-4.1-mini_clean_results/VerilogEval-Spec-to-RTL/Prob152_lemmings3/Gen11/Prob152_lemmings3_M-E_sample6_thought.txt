Implement the Lemming behavior using two separate registers: one for direction (1 bit) and one for mode (2 bits). Use an explicit enumerated type for modes to clarify FSM states. Direction only updates on bumps and when not falling or digging. The mode transitions are prioritized by checking falling (ground=0) first, then digging initiation, then direction changes. When falling, mode changes back to walking once ground reappears. When digging, it continues until ground disappears, then switch to falling. Outputs are driven by the mode and direction registers. This separation simplifies direction logic and emphasizes FSM clarity. Asynchronous reset forces walking left state, and the clocked process updates mode and direction sequentially.
