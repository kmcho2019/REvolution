To optimize PPA, I will further reduce logic depth by collapsing the bump direction switching logic into a single XOR operation, since being bumped on both sides or just one side results in flipping or setting direction accordingly. This simplifies and speeds the bump condition evaluation. Also, I will eliminate redundant `state <= state` assignments by only updating state when needed, thus reducing toggling. The FSM will remain packed in 3 bits for compactness and simplicity, but the next state logic will be expressed with fewer nested conditions. Outputs will remain purely combinational derived from state bits. This results in simpler and smaller combinational logic, less power from glitching, and potentially better timing due to reduced logic depth.
