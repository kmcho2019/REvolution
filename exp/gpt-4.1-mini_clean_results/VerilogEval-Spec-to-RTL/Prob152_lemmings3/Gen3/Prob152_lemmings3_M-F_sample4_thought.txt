- States:
  0: WALK_LEFT
  1: WALK_RIGHT
  2: FALLING_LEFT
  3: FALLING_RIGHT
  4: DIGGING_LEFT
  5: DIGGING_RIGHT

This encoding captures direction and activity in one state variable, simplifying transitions and output logic.

- If no ground (ground=0), transition to FALLING state of current direction.

- walk_left = in WALK_LEFT state
