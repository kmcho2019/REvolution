- If bumped on the left (bump_left=1), walk right (direction=1).

Also, bumps only affect direction when walking and ground is stable (ground=1 and previous ground=1).

- Both bumps: flip direction.
- Only bump_left: direction = right (1).
- Only bump_right: direction = left (0).
- No bump: direction unchanged.

This ensures the FSM behavior matches the specification exactly and prevents direction glitches or mismatches.
