Refine the state machine by encoding only the behavior (WALK, DIG, FALL) and keeping the walking direction in a separate register. This allows clear separation of walking direction and state. The state transitions prioritize falling > digging > bump direction switch. Bumps only cause direction flips when the Lemming is walking on ground. While digging or falling, bumps are ignored. Direction is preserved during falling and digging, and restored when ground returns or digging ends. The output signals are derived from the combination of current state and direction. Async reset initializes state to WALK and direction to LEFT. This fix eliminates direction toggling during digging or falling and properly prioritizes falling and digging over bump-induced direction changes.
