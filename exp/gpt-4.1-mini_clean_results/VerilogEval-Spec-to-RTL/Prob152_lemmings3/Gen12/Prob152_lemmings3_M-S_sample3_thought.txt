Simplify the FSM by explicitly separating the states for walking left/right, falling left/right, and digging left/right, making digging part of the FSM states rather than a separate flag. This eliminates the extra digging register and simplifies the logic. The new FSM has six states: walk-left, walk-right, fall-left, fall-right, dig-left, dig-right. On ground loss, switch to fall states; on ground regain, resume walking; from walking, bump switches direction; from walking on ground with dig=1, switch to dig state; digging continues until ground=0, then fall. This removes complicated if-else priority handling by encoding behavior directly in FSM states, simplifying next-state logic and outputs.
