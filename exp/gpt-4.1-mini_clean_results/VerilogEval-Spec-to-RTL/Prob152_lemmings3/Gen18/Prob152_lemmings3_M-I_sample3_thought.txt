To improve timing and potentially reduce power and area slightly, separate next-state and output logic into two always blocks: one combinational for next state/direction computation and one sequential for state and direction registers update. This also avoids mixing state updates and direction changes within nested conditionals, reducing combinational logic depth. Furthermore, encode the direction toggling in a simpler way and minimize condition checks by computing next direction only when walking on ground and no fall/dig conditions. This preserves the Moore FSM behavior and output logic but improves synthesis results by cleaner separation and simpler conditions.
