Use a 3-bit state encoding with distinct states for each behavior and direction: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, DIG_LEFT, DIG_RIGHT, and FALL. Keep the current walking direction encoded in the state itself, removing the need for a separate direction register. On async reset, go to WALK_LEFT. Next-state logic prioritizes falling (if ground=0), then digging (if dig=1 while walking on ground), then switching directions on bumps (if walking). When falling, ignore bumps and dig; when ground returns, return to walking in the same direction as the falling direction. When digging, continue until ground disappears, then fall. Outputs are based solely on current state (Moore). This approach removes separate direction tracking and cleans up transitions, simplifying design and avoiding output glitches.
