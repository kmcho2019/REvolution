Simplify the FSM by tracking a single walking direction register and three states: WALKING, DIGGING, and FALLING. The states are mutually exclusive, making logic clearer:

- States: WALKING, DIGGING, FALLING.
- On reset, start WALKING left.
- While FALLING: remain falling until ground returns, then resume walking in the saved direction.
- While DIGGING: continue digging while ground=1, else fall.
- While WALKING: 
    - If ground=0, fall (save current direction).
    - Else if dig=1, start DIGGING.
    - Else if bumped (left or right), switch direction.
    - Else continue walking.

Save walking direction upon FALLING to restore after falling ends. 
Outputs are assigned based on current state and walking direction.

This approach removes extra digging flags and saved_dir registers by embedding digging as a state, making the FSM a 3-state machine with clear transition priorities: FALLING > DIGGING > WALKING. It simplifies next state logic and output assignment.
