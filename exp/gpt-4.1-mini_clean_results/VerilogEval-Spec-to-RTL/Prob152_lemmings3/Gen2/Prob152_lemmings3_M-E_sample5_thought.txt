Implement a Moore FSM with separate state variables to explicitly encode the four main states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, and DIGGING. Instead of encoding digging direction as separate states, keep digging as a single state with a stored direction bit (dig_dir) that tracks walking direction at dig start. Use another direction bit (walk_dir) for walking direction. Store direction bits only on state transitions where walking direction actually changes (on bumps or after falling). Prioritize states explicitly in combinational next state logic as fall > dig > bump. Handle bumps only in walking states, ignore during fall or dig. When falling, store walking direction before falling and resume it after landing. When digging, continue until ground disappears and then transition to fall. Outputs are purely combinational and based on the current state and direction bits. Reset initializes to walking left with appropriate direction bits. This decouples state encoding from direction bits, reduces number of states, and clarifies direction updates.
