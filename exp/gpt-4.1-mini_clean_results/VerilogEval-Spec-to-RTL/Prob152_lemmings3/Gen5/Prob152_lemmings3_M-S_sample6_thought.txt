Simplify the FSM by combining the walking direction and state into a single state variable representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging states. This removes the separate direction register and makes the logic clearer.

Key points:
- Encode four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING.
- On bumps while walking, explicitly set direction based on bump side: bump_left means walk right, bump_right means walk left. If both bumps occur, switch direction (toggle).
- On ground falling edge, enter FALLING state, preserving direction (switch to falling state).
- On ground rising edge, from FALLING return to walking state with the previous direction.
- Digging can start only when walking on ground and dig=1, and continues until ground disappears, then transition to falling.
- Ignore bumps while falling or digging.
- Asynchronous reset sets state to WALK_LEFT.
- Use one register for ground delayed sample to detect edges.
- Outputs directly map from states, making Moore outputs simple.
This reduces registers and logic while preserving behavior and priority of fall > dig > bump.
