Implement the FSM using one-hot state encoding with separate states for walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Use two directional walking states (WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT) so direction is implicit in the state, eliminating the need for a separate direction register. The transitions check inputs in priority order: falling if no ground, then digging if dig=1 and ground present, else switch walking direction on bumps. Falling and digging states ignore bumps. The outputs are directly derived from the current one-hot states. Asynchronous reset initializes to WALK_LEFT. This approach simplifies direction tracking and distinctly separates each mode and direction in unique states, facilitating clear transitions and outputs.
