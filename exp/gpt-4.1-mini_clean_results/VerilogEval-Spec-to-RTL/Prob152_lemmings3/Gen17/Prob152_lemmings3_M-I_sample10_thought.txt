To improve PPA, I will add input signal synchronization registers for bump_left, bump_right, dig, and ground signals to reduce metastability and glitches, thus improving timing and power. This avoids asynchronous glitches feeding combinational logic, which can reduce switching and dynamic power. The FSM state encoding is already minimal and the logic is simple, so no change to encoding is needed. The separated combinational next-state logic remains the same for clarity and robustness. This small additional pipelining of inputs improves timing margins (positive slack) and reduces glitch power with minimal area overhead.
