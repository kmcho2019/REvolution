Use a single 3-bit state register to encode both direction and mode (walking left, walking right, falling, digging). Define four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALL_LEFT, FALL_RIGHT, DIG_LEFT, DIG_RIGHT. This approach removes separate direction and mode variables by encoding direction into the state itself. Prioritize fall over dig over bump switching in transitions. This leads to simpler combinational logic and cleaner state transitions while preserving the Moore FSM outputs.
