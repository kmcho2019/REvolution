Use a one-hot encoded FSM with four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALL, and DIG. Store the walking direction implicitly in the state encoding rather than a separate direction bit. Transition rules are implemented prioritizing fall over dig over bump. This eliminates the need for a separate direction register and simplifies direction updates. Asynchronous reset initializes the FSM to WALK_LEFT state. Outputs are asserted based purely on the current state for clean Moore outputs. This approach simplifies control logic and may improve timing by reducing the number of registers and condition checks per cycle.
