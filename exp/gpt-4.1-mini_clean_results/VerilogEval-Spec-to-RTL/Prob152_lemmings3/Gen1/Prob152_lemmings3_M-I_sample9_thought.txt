The original design implements the required Moore FSM with states WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING, plus a digging flag. The outputs are derived from state and digging_reg. The main correctness concern is subtle handling of digging_reg and saved_dir during transitions, especially on bumps and digging start, which can affect walk_left and walk_right outputs.

To improve correctness and PPA:

1. Separate the notion of "current walking direction" from "state" to avoid confusion. The walking direction is naturally represented by WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states, so saved_dir is only needed to remember the direction during FALLING.

2. Track digging explicitly as a separate state, merging digging into WALK_LEFT_DIG and WALK_RIGHT_DIG. This clarifies outputs and transitions because digging is a distinct state.

3. This gives us five states:
   - WALK_LEFT
   - WALK_RIGHT
   - DIG_LEFT
   - DIG_RIGHT
   - FALLING

4. This simplifies output logic as outputs map clearly to states.

5. State transitions respect precedence: falling > digging > direction switching.

6. On falling, remember the last walking direction (left or right) in saved_dir (1-bit reg) for recovery.

7. Asynchronous reset sets state to WALK_LEFT and saved_dir=0.

8. On each cycle, based on inputs and current state:
   - If falling and ground=1, recover to saved_dir walking state.
   - If walking or digging and ground=0, fall.
   - If walking and dig=1, start digging.
   - If walking and bumped, switch walking direction.
   - If digging and ground=0, fall.
   - Else stay in current state.

9. By encoding digging into states, no separate digging flag is needed, simplifying design and outputs.

10. This reduces logic complexity, helps prevent glitches in digging flag, and improves clarity and power since only state is updated.

11. saved_dir only changes when entering falling and remains stable to restore direction after fall.

This design is clearer, more maintainable, and outputs are purely a function of state, making a true Moore FSM.

We implement the FSM accordingly with five states and a saved_dir register.
