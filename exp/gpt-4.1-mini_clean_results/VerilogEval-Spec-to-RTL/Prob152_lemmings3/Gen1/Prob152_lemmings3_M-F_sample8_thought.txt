The mismatch on walk_left and walk_right likely comes from the separate `dir` register and the fact that outputs depend only on `state`. This can cause inconsistencies or glitches during transitions since `dir` updates asynchronously to `state`.

To fix this, fully integrate the walking direction into the FSM states, encoding walking left and right explicitly, and eliminate the separate `dir` register. The FSM states become:

- WL: walking left
- WR: walking right
- FA_L: falling, remembered direction left
- FA_R: falling, remembered direction right
- DG_L: digging, remembered direction left
- DG_R: digging, remembered direction right

By encoding direction in the state itself, outputs can be assigned purely based on state, removing mismatch risks and improving clarity.

This redesign keeps a Moore FSM (outputs depend only on current state), simplifies the logic, and ensures consistent direction during falling and digging.

Transitions respect priority: falling > digging > bump/switch direction.

Reset initializes state to WL.

Outputs are asserted based on state encoding (walk_left if in any _L walking state, walk_right if in any _R walking state, etc).

This approach avoids maintaining a separate direction register and makes the design more robust and glitch-free.
