To fix the functional issues, we must ensure a single, unified combinational block drives the `next_state` logic without conflicting assignments, especially for the FALLING state. Currently, two separate always blocks update `next_state`, causing race conditions and incorrect next states.

Also, the `dir_is_left` register must update consistently with the next state transitions, capturing the correct walking direction to resume after falling or digging.

We will combine the entire next_state logic into one always @(*) block that:

- Implements the priority of transitions: fall > dig > bump.

- Uses `dir_is_left` to decide next state when transitioning out of FALLING.

- Prevents invalid dig commands when ground=0 or when falling/digging.

- Ignores bumps while falling or digging.

The sequential block will update `state` and `dir_is_left` consistently on clock edges.

Finally, outputs remain Moore outputs, driven only by current state.

This will ensure correct FSM behavior, no conflicting combinational assignments, and proper storage of walking direction for resuming walking after falling or digging.

This also simplifies debugging and synthesis due to clean state logic and single next_state combinational logic.
