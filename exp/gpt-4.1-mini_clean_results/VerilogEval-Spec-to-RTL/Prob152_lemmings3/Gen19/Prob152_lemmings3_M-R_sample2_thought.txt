I will refactor the FSM by splitting state and direction updates into separate always blocks to clarify their distinct roles. Instead of computing both mode_next and dir_next together, I will compute mode_next combinationally first, then update dir_next only when in WALK state and bumped, otherwise keep previous direction. I will use explicit localparams for directions (LEFT=0, RIGHT=1) for clarity. This separation improves readability and maintainability, while maintaining the same behavior. Outputs remain Moore style, based solely on registered state. The asynchronous reset is retained.
