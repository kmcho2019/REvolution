To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will restructure the next-state logic to minimize combinational depth by using a prioritized if-else ladder directly in the combinational block, removing the extra bump_detect wire and combining bump conditions into a single expression. This reduces logic levels and signal fanout. I will also pack the mode and direction into a single 3-bit register internally, which can potentially reduce area by sharing register resources and simplifying control logic. The asynchronous reset will still initialize to walking left with mode WALK and dir=0. Output assignments remain straightforward Moore outputs, but with simplified expressions. This optimization targets slightly faster timing and marginally reduced area and power due to simpler and fewer signals and gates without changing the FSM behavior.
