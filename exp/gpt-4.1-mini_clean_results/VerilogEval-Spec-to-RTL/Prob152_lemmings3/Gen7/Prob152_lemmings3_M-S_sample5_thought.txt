Simplify the FSM by merging the walking direction into the main state encoding, representing four states: walking left, walking right, falling left, and falling right. Add two digging states as well: digging left and digging right. This reduces complexity by removing separate direction registers and edge detection of ground.

Use only the current ground input to control transitions, relying on the fact that bumps only affect walking states on stable ground (ground=1). Since bumps have no effect when ground=0 (falling/digging), no need for previous ground tracking.

Direction switching is done directly by transitioning between walking states on bumps. Fall has highest priority, dig second, bumps last.

Outputs are simple and directly derived from the current state encoding.

This approach reduces logic and registers, eliminating prev_ground register and separate direction register, yet preserves all behavior and priority.
