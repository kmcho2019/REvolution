To improve the existing FSM for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), we focus on these aspects:

- Replace the 2-bit encoded state with one-hot encoding to simplify combinational logic and potentially reduce critical path delays by eliminating decoding logic.
- Use synchronous reset instead of asynchronous reset to improve timing (reducing reset-related timing bottlenecks).
- Reduce toggling of the `prev_ground` register by gating its update: only update it when ground=1, otherwise hold its value. This reduces unnecessary switching and dynamic power.
- Simplify stable ground detection to rely on stable assertion of ground over two cycles (prev_ground=1 and ground=1).
- Keep Moore outputs driven directly from one-hot state bits for minimal logic and reduced glitching.
- Keep direction register and bump logic the same, as it is simple and efficient.
- Maintain the prioritization of transitions (fall > dig > bump).
- Keep coding style clear and synthesis-friendly (non-blocking in sequential, blocking in combinational).

These changes collectively should reduce toggle activity, simplify logic for faster timing, and improve power efficiency with only a modest area increase from one-hot state encoding, which is acceptable given the PPA trade-offs.
