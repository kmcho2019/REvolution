I will simplify the FSM by using a single always_comb block with a case statement for next-state logic, which is clearer and easier for synthesis tools to optimize. The state encoding remains 2-bit binary with parameters. The synchronous reset resets to state A. The output is combinational and simply assigned as 1 when in state D, otherwise 0. This approach reduces boolean complexity and makes the design more readable and compact while preserving functionality.
