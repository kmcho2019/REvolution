To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will simplify the next-state and output logic by using a single `always_comb` block with a `case` statement for the next state and output signals, avoiding complex boolean expressions and equality comparisons. This reduces combinational logic depth and improves timing. Also, encoding the FSM states as a 2-bit binary remains area efficient for a 4-state FSM. The Moore output will be a simple continuous assignment based on current state == D, minimizing logic and power by eliminating unnecessary procedural logic. The synchronous reset and state register remain unchanged. This approach enhances readability, timing, and power efficiency without increasing area.
