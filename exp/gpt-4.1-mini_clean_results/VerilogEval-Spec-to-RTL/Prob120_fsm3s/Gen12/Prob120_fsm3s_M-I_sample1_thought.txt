To optimize the FSM for better performance, power, and area, I will simplify the next-state logic and output generation by using case statements for both next state and output. This approach tends to map to simpler and faster hardware than complex boolean expressions and equality checks. I'll keep the 2-bit encoding for area efficiency. The synchronous reset and state register remain the same. The output will be generated by a continuous assignment comparing the state with D, which is simpler and reduces logic. This reduces the combinational logic complexity and critical path length, improving timing, and also reduces toggling and gate count, enhancing power and area metrics.
