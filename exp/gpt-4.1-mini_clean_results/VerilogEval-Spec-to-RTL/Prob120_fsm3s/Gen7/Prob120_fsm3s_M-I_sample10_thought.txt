To optimize the existing FSM for better PPA, I will replace the next-state function with a combinational always block. This can give synthesis tools better visibility for optimizing the next state logic, potentially reducing combinational delay (improving TNS/WNS). Additionally, I will register the output signal to reduce output glitches and potentially improve timing and power due to glitch suppression, especially if the output is connected to downstream logic. The registered output still follows Moore machine semantics, as it depends solely on the current state. This trade-off can slightly increase area (one more flip-flop) but is beneficial for power and timing. The synchronous reset remains unchanged. The state encoding remains binary for area efficiency.
