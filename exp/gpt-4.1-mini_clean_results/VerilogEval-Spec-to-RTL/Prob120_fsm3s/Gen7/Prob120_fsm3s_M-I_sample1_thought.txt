To optimize the FSM for better timing (TNS/ WNS), power, and area while maintaining clarity, I will replace the next_state function with a dedicated combinational always block. This approach provides the synthesis tool with more visibility for optimization and may reduce combinational delays. Additionally, registering the output can reduce glitches and switching activity, potentially improving power and timing, especially if the output drives significant load. State encoding remains binary for area efficiency. This slightly increases logic for output register but benefits glitch-free output and timing. The synchronous reset remains unchanged for reliability. Overall, these changes aim for a balanced improvement in PPA metrics while keeping the design readable.
