To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will use one-hot state encoding. One-hot encoding can improve timing due to simpler decoding logic and faster state transitions at the expense of area (more flip-flops). However, since the FSM has only four states, the area overhead is minimal, and the timing can benefit from simpler next-state logic implemented with AND/OR gates directly on one-hot signals. Power may slightly increase due to more flip-flops, but overall toggling can be reduced by clear combinational logic. The output logic is trivial with one-hot and remains combinational based solely on the state register. The synchronous reset is preserved. The implementation will keep non-blocking assignments and clean, synthesizable style.
