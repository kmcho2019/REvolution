To simplify the FSM implementation, I will encode the four states with a 2-bit register as before but use a clean synchronous always block for both next state logic and output logic, leveraging a case statement for next state and Moore output. This approach improves readability and maintainability by clearly showing transitions and outputs in one place, removing complicated combinational assignments. The output is simply assigned based on current state directly, avoiding a separate always block or complex logic. The synchronous reset will reset the state to A on clk rising edge. This keeps logic straightforward, easier to verify, and synthesis-friendly.
