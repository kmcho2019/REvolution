To improve PPA, restructure the combinational next state and output logic into an always_comb block with a case statement for better synthesis and timing optimization. This reduces the critical path and potential glitches compared to nested ternary operators. Also, register the output signal on the clock edge to minimize glitches and reduce dynamic power by avoiding combinational output glitches. The FSM remains Moore style, but with registered outputs, which often helps timing and power. State encoding is retained as 2-bit binary for area efficiency and sufficient speed.
