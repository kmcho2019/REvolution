To improve the original FSM design for better performance and potentially power, I will switch from binary encoding to one-hot encoding. This will increase the number of flip-flops from 2 to 4 but reduces combinational logic complexity in the next_state logic, which can improve timing and reduce glitches in some technologies. The output logic becomes a simple direct check of the D state bit. Also, I'll keep synchronous reset and maintain non-blocking assignments for sequential logic and blocking assignments for combinational logic for simulation correctness. This trades a slight area increase for potentially better performance and cleaner logic for synthesis tools.
