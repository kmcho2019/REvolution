To improve PPA, I will simplify the FSM design by using a single combinational always block with a case statement for next-state logic, which usually synthesizes into cleaner logic than multiple complex boolean assignments. Also, the output will be assigned directly as a continuous assignment (wire) based on the current state to reduce combinational logic and gate count. This reduces logic depth and power consumption by minimizing unnecessary comparisons and logic gates. The state encoding will remain binary for area efficiency. Overall, this approach balances clarity and improved performance and power without increasing area.
