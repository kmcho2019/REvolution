To improve the PPA metrics, I will apply one-hot encoding to the FSM states instead of binary encoding. This reduces combinational logic complexity in next-state and output logic, potentially increasing the maximum clock frequency and reducing dynamic power due to simpler decoding logic. One-hot encoding uses more flip-flops (4 vs 2), but for a small FSM this tradeoff typically improves timing and power without significantly increasing area. I will keep the synchronous reset and positive-edge clock triggering. The next-state and output logic will be simplified as assignments instead of case statements to leverage one-hot state encoding clarity.
