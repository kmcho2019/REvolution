The original design is clear and efficient with a 2-bit encoded Moore FSM and synchronous reset. To improve PPA, I will apply one-hot encoding to the FSM states. One-hot encoding often reduces the complexity of next state logic and can improve timing (higher clock frequency) by simplifying combinational logic at the expense of slightly higher area due to wider state registers. Here, area overhead is minimal for 4 states. The simpler decoding can also reduce dynamic power due to fewer toggling bits in logic. The synchronous reset and output logic will be updated accordingly for one-hot state registers. This approach targets a better clock period and potentially reduced power due to less combinational complexity and glitching, trading a little area increase for improved frequency and power.
