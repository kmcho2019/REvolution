To optimize PPA, reduce power by minimizing toggle activity and improve timing slightly by using one-hot state encoding since the FSM has only 4 states. One-hot encoding uses 4 flip-flops, potentially enabling faster next-state logic due to simpler decoding and fewer logic levels at the cost of slightly increased area, but with a very small area increase for four states. Also, combine output logic with next-state logic to simplify and remove separate output decode. Keep synchronous active-high reset. Use non-blocking assignments everywhere. This approach balances performance and power well for a small FSM.
