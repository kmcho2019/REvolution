Refactor the FSM to use an explicit always_comb block for the next-state and output logic with a case statement instead of nested ternaries and continuous assignments. This approach improves readability and may help synthesis tools better optimize the logic for power and timing. The state register update remains in a synchronous always block triggered on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset. The output is registered as part of the Moore machine output by assigning it inside the combinational block based on the current state.
