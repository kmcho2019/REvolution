To simplify the FSM implementation, I will replace the multiple boolean expressions with a combinational always block using a case statement, which is more straightforward and readable. This also makes it easier to verify and maintain the next state logic. The synchronous reset and state register remain the same. The output is a simple combinational assignment based on the current state.

This approach reduces the number of continuous assignments and logical operations, improving clarity while maintaining the same functionality and synthesis friendliness.
