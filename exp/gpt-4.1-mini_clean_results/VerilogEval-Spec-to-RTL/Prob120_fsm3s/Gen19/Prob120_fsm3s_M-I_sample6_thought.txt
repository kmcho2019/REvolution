To improve the FSM for better performance and power, I will use one-hot state encoding. One-hot encoding uses one flip-flop per state, simplifying next-state and output logic by reducing combinational complexity and potentially improving timing. This may slightly increase area (4 FFs vs 2), but can reduce the critical path delay, lowering setup time and increasing max clock frequency. 

Additionally, I will implement output as a simple combinational OR of the state D flip-flop, and keep synchronous reset logic for simplicity and correctness.

This approach should reduce logic depth in next_state and output functions, improving timing (positive WNS) and reducing power by simpler combinational logic.
