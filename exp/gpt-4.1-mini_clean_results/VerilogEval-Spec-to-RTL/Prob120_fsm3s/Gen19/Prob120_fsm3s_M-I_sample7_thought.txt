To improve the PPA, I will simplify the next_state combinational logic by using a combinational always block with a case statement instead of multiple OR conditions. This approach will enable synthesis tools to optimize logic more effectively, potentially reducing gate levels and thus improving timing and power.

I will keep the synchronous reset and state register as before for clarity and reliability.

For the output, I keep a simple combinational continuous assignment since the output depends only on the current state.

This solution balances readability, synthesis friendliness, and optimization potential for area, timing, and power.
