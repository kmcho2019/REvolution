To improve the PPA, especially power, I will implement clock gating to avoid unnecessary toggling of the flip-flop when neither loading nor shifting is enabled (L=0 and E=0). This reduces dynamic power by preventing clock toggling of the register when its value will not change. The combinational logic remains the same, ensuring functional correctness and performance, but the gated clock reduces power. The overhead of clock gating logic is minimal and benefits the overall power consumption in larger shift registers.
