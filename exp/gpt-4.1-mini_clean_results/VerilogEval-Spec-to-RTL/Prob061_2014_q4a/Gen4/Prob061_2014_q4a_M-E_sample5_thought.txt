Implement the shift register stage with priority on load (L), by first deciding the next data to be loaded based on load or shift inputs, and using an if-else structure inside the clocked process without explicit clock enable. This approach always updates the register at every clock edge but chooses whether to load R, shift in w, or retain its value based on control signals. It simplifies control logic by making load have highest priority, then shift, otherwise hold state, and relies on the synthesizer to optimize the register enable internally.
