Refactor the design by separating the combinational next-state logic and the sequential state update. Implement a combinational always block to compute next_Q based on priority: load (L), then shift enable (E), else hold Q. Then, in a sequential always block on the clock edge, update Q with next_Q. This approach makes the next state explicit and distinct from the register update, providing clearer modularity between combinational and sequential logic compared to the single always block style.
