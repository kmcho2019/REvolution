The existing design is already minimal and clean, but a slight optimization can reduce combinational logic by avoiding an explicit next state variable and instead implementing the synchronous logic with conditional non-blocking assignments directly in the clocked always block. This removes one combinational always block, potentially reducing logic area and power by simplifying logic synthesis. The priority (L > E) and retention behavior is preserved. This approach reduces registers and logic stages and may marginally improve power and area without impacting timing.
