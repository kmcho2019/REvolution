Implement the shift register stage using a tri-state-like behavior controlled by a multiplexed next state logic. Use a combinational always block to determine the next value of Q based on L and E signals with priority on load (L), then shift (E), and finally hold the current value. Then update Q on the clock edge. This separates the combinational next-state logic from the sequential state update, which can improve readability and modularity. It also explicitly shows the retention case without relying on implicit latching behavior.
