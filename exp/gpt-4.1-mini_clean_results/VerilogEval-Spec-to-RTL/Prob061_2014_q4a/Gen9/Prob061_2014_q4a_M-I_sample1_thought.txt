To further optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA), I will implement a clock enable (CE) signal derived from L or E that controls when the flip-flop updates. This avoids toggling the flip-flop on every clock edge unnecessarily, which can reduce dynamic power by allowing synthesis tools to infer clock gating or gated clock cells.

Additionally, the mux selection can be rewritten to a single conditional expression for better synthesis friendliness. Holding Q explicitly with `Q <= Q;` is unnecessary in an enabled flip-flop; using a clock enable properly replaces that pattern and prevents redundant toggling.

Overall, the design will be functionally identical, but more synthesis-friendly to help optimize power by minimizing state changes, while preserving performance and area. This leverages common FPGA/ASIC design practice of adding clock enable signals to flip-flops.
