Implement the functionality using a combinational multiplexer feeding a D flip-flop. The multiplexer selects between loading R, shifting in w, or holding the current state based on L and E signals, but without using nested if statements inside the sequential block. By separating the combinational selection logic from the flip-flop update, the design increases clarity and could facilitate easier pipelining or modification in larger designs.
