To improve PPA, remove the explicit clock enable signal and instead implement the enable logic inside the data path mux. This avoids potential issues with gated clocks and hold-time violations, improving timing (lower WNS and TNS). It also reduces power by eliminating clock gating logic, and may slightly reduce area by removing the clock enable gating logic, since synthesis tools often better optimize data-driven muxing compared to gated clocks. The code remains functionally equivalent, simple, and synthesis-friendly.
