To improve power and timing, I will eliminate the separate clock enable wire by integrating the load/shift control into the always block condition directly, which can help synthesis tools generate more optimized enable logic inside the FF. Additionally, I will explicitly reset Q to 0 on power-up by adding a synchronous reset behavior with a simple default value when neither load nor shift is requested to avoid unknown states that may cause unnecessary toggling at startup. This also prevents glitches on Q at initialization which can lower power. The logic remains simple, but the slight restructuring can help PPA, especially power and timing, by giving clearer intent to synthesis and avoiding separate mux and enable signals driving combinational paths.
