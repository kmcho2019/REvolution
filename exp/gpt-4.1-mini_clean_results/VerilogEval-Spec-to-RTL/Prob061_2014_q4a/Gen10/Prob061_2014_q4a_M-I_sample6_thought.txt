To optimize the design for power and timing, I will add an explicit clock enable signal to the register. The clock enable will be asserted only when a state change is required, i.e., when L=1 (load new data) or E=1 (shift in w). When neither is asserted, the register holds its value and the clock enable is deasserted, allowing synthesis tools to implement clock gating or clock enables to reduce dynamic power and possibly improve timing. This optimization reduces unnecessary toggling of Q and potentially shortens timing paths due to gating logic. The behavioral functionality remains unchanged.
