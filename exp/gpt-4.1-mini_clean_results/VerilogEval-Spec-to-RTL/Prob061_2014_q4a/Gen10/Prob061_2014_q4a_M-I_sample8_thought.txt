To improve power and performance, apply a clock enable scheme by using the E (enable) and L (load) signals as a combined clock enable. This will prevent unnecessary clock toggling and register updates when neither shifting nor loading is required, reducing dynamic power. The behavior remains the same, but now Q only updates when enabled or load is asserted. This avoids redundant assignments and switching inside the register. This small change can also improve timing by reducing toggling and switching activity on the clock path to the register. The logic for next_Q is preserved, but the register update is gated by the combined enable signal.
