This change may slightly increase switching activity (power) due to the register updating every clock cycle, but the improved timing and simpler clocking scheme often outweigh this. The combinational logic remains the same and minimal, so area impact is negligible or slightly reduced by simpler control logic.

Overall, this approach trades a small potential power increase for better timing and simpler design, which is often preferable in high-speed designs.
