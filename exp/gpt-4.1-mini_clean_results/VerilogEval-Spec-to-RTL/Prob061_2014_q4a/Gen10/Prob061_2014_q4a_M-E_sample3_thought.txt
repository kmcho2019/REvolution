Implement the module using a single synchronous always block with a priority decision on L and E signals to update Q. This removes the combinational next-state logic and conditions the update directly inside the clocked process. The design explicitly updates Q to R if load (L) is asserted; else if shift enable (E) is asserted, Q is loaded with w; otherwise, Q retains its old value. This straightforward approach relies on a default retention of the register state without separate combinational logic, simplifying the architecture and potentially improving synthesis results by removing extra intermediate signals and combinational processes.
