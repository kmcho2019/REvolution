Fuse the clarity of separating next-state logic from state update (Example 1) with the efficient clock-enable gating approach (Example 2) to optimize timing and power. Compute the next state (Q_next) combinationally by selecting R when loading (L), w when enabled (E) but not loading, and hold current Q otherwise. Then use a clock enable (ce) signal (L | E) to update Q only when needed. This keeps the register update conditional for power savings while always updating Q at clock edges with a proper clock enable signal, avoiding gated clocks or conditional registers that might complicate timing. This fusion preserves explicit next-state calculation for readability and allows synthesis tools to infer clock gating or enable signals cleanly, balancing performance, power, and area.
