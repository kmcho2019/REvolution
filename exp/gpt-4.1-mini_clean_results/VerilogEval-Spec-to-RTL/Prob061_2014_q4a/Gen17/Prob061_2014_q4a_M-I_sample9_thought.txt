To optimize for better power and area (PPA), further simplify the logic by removing the explicit next_Q register variable and combine the load and shift control signals into a single multiplexer that feeds directly into the register input. Since the register naturally holds its value on clock edges without enable, removing the enable gating reduces control signal toggling and potentially simplifies the circuit. This trades a slight increase in toggling (since the register updates every clock) for simpler logic and removes the enable gating overhead. The load has priority over shift via a single mux controlled by L and E signals. This approach reduces area (less combinational logic) and critical path complexity, while maintaining correctness and potentially improving timing. Power may increase slightly if toggling increases, but this can be mitigated by the simpler mux and reduced control signals. Overall, it is a good PPA trade-off for this small module.
