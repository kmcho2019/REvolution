To improve the PPA (performance, power, area), I will slightly optimize the mux logic by encoding the priority explicitly and reducing logic levels. Since L has the highest priority, followed by E, and then retaining Q, I can implement this with a nested conditional operator that ensures stable mux output and potentially easier synthesis. Additionally, I'll declare nextQ as reg and compute it inside a combinational always block to encourage inferred muxing by synthesis tools, which might improve timing and power by avoiding extra wires and multiple drivers. This also makes the code style consistent and often preferred in ASIC flows. The flip-flop remains the same.

This change is mostly stylistic and minor, but can help synthesis and place-and-route tools produce a better netlist. Overall, the design is already minimal and efficient.
