To improve the design for better performance and power efficiency, I will implement clock gating to disable the clock to the flip-flop when neither load (L) nor enable (E) is asserted. This reduces unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. The clock gating logic uses a simple AND gate between the original clock and (L | E) signal. The flip-flop only updates Q on gated clock edges when shifting or loading is required. This also potentially improves timing by reducing switching activity on the clock input. The functional behavior remains identical.
