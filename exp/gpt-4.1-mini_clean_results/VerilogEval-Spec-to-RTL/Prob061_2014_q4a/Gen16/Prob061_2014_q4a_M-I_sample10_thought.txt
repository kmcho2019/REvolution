To optimize the design for power without sacrificing timing, introduce clock gating to disable the clock to the flip-flop when no update is needed (i.e., when both L=0 and E=0). This reduces unnecessary flip-flop toggling and dynamic power consumption. The combinational next state logic remains the same, preserving minimal logic delay. Clock gating can be implemented with an enable signal gating the clock input to the flip-flop. This method can yield power savings while maintaining area and timing efficiency.
