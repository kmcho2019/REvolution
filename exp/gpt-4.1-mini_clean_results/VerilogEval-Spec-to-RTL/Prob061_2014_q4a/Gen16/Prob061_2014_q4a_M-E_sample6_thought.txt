Implement the shift register stage with separate combinational logic to calculate the next state of Q based on the inputs, then update Q in a clocked always block. Use a wire 'next_Q' to hold the combinational output, where priority is given to load (L) to load R, then shift (E) to load w, otherwise keep Q unchanged. This approach separates combinational and sequential logic explicitly and makes the design modular and easier to extend or modify.
