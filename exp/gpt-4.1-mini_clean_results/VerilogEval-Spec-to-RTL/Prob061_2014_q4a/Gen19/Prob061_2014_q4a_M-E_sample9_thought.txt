Implement the shift register stage using a combinational multiplexer feeding into the flip-flop. Use the inputs L and E as select lines of a 2-bit control signal to decide next Q value: when L=1, load R; else if E=1, shift in w; otherwise retain current Q by feeding Q back. This makes the next state logic explicitly combinational with a simple 3-to-1 mux. The flip-flop clocked on posedge clk stores this result. This approach separates combinational decision-making from the sequential element, potentially improving clarity and synthesis tools' ability to optimize the mux separately from the flip-flop.
