To optimize the design for better power and possibly area, remove the explicit retention assignment (Q <= Q) inside the always block. This allows the synthesizer to infer that the register holds its value when neither load (L) nor shift enable (E) is asserted, reducing unnecessary toggle and combinational logic. Additionally, using a clock enable signal based on (L | E) will allow synthesis tools to infer a flip-flop with enable, further reducing switching activity and dynamic power. This approach keeps the load priority over shift by controlling the mux inputs, while enabling the clock only when state update is needed.
