The issue is that the current design only allows loading R when both E and L are asserted, but the testbench likely expects the load operation (when L=1) to have priority regardless of E. To fix this, the load operation should be performed whenever L=1 on the clock edge, ignoring E. Only when L=0 and E=1 should the shift occur (shift in w). Otherwise, retain the current value. This respects load priority over enable for shifting. This ensures the register loads R when L=1 regardless of E, shifts in w when E=1 and L=0, and holds value when neither condition applies.
