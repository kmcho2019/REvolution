To implement the pairwise 25-bit output of equality comparisons between inputs a, b, c, d, e, I will adopt a fully structural approach without generate loops or indexing arithmetic. Instead, I will explicitly instantiate all 25 XNOR gates connecting every pair of inputs in a fixed order. This completely unrolled approach removes all intermediate arrays or calculations, providing a direct, simple-to-read connection map from inputs to outputs.

The outputs will be indexed so that out[24] corresponds to comparing a with a, out[23] to a with b, out[22] to a with c, and so forth, following the original ordering but using manual assignment. This approach is explicit and illustrative, perfect for teaching or debugging small fixed-size problems. It also guarantees no tool-dependent interpretation of generate loops or indexing.

This design trades code brevity for absolute clarity and straightforwardness, with minimal logic and no indexing overhead, making it ideal for understanding the mapping between inputs and outputs.
