Instead of nested loops and manual indexing, pack the inputs into a vector and use a straightforward combinational logic with a 5x5 generate loop to compute each pairwise comparison. To simplify mapping of output bits, flatten the 2D (i,j) indices into a 1D vector in a natural order: row-wise from top-left (a,a) to bottom-right (e,e). Assign out[24] = eq(a,a), out[23] = eq(a,b), ..., out[0] = eq(e,e) by indexing out as out[24 - (5*i + j)]. The XNOR operation for equality is implemented by ~(in_vec[i] ^ in_vec[j]). This approach provides a clean, maintainable design without complex indexing or manual assignments.
