The original design computes all pairwise bit equalities using generate loops and XOR/XNOR logic, which is already simple and efficient. To optimize for better PPA:

- Area: Instead of indexing the inputs vector with [4 - i], reverse the inputs ordering once into a local wire to avoid repeated indexing with variable indices, simplifying mapping and potentially improving synthesis.
- Power: Share the inversion (~) by using XNOR operator directly, which is synthesizer-friendly and avoids unnecessary gates.
- Performance: Avoid nested generate loops by flattening the logic into a single loop for better clarity and possibly improved synthesis results.
- Also, directly list inputs in an array for clarity and explicitness.

Overall, these changes marginally improve readability and may slightly help the synthesis tool optimize gate count and delay due to simpler indexing and less complex generated loops.
