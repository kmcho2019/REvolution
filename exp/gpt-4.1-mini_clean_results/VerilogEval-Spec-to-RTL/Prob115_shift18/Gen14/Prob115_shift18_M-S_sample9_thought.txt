To simplify the design and reduce combinational complexity, implement the shift operations using simple concatenations and replication instead of arithmetic shift operators. Since the shifts are fixed amounts (1 or 8) and directions, they can be directly wired. For left shifts, fill the vacated bits with zeros; for arithmetic right shifts, replicate the sign bit. Use a case statement inside the sequential block to select the shifted value, reducing combinational multiplexing complexity. This approach eliminates multiple combinational wires and nested ternary operators, making the logic clearer and potentially improving synthesis results.
