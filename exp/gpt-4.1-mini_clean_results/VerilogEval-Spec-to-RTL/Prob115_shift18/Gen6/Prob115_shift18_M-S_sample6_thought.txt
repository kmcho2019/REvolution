Simplify the design by removing the staged shift approach and implementing the shifts directly with a single always block and combinational assignments. Use continuous assignments for the shifted results instead of an always @(*) block, and directly select the shifted output based on the amount input.

This reduces complexity by:
- Eliminating intermediate variables/stages.
- Using combinational wire assignments for shifts.
- Keeping synchronous load and enable logic in one always block.
- Making the shift logic straightforward with minimal conditional logic.

The arithmetic right shift replicates the sign bit on shifts. The four shift modes are handled explicitly with a case statement for clarity and efficiency.
