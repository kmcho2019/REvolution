Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register as a single 64-bit register with a combinational barrel shifter stage before the register update. On each clock, if load is high, load data directly; else if ena is high, shift the current register content according to amount: 
- For left shifts (amount[1]==0), shift logically by 1 or 8 bits.
- For right shifts (amount[1]==1), shift arithmetically by 1 or 8 bits by replicating the MSB for sign extension.

Use a unified barrel shifter approach with multiplexed shift amounts and directions, employing a case statement for clarity. This avoids splitting the data into segments and performing multiple partial shifts, reducing combinational complexity and improving performance. Sign extension for arithmetic right shifts is done by extending the MSB to fill the vacated bits. This single barrel shifter approach also only computes the selected shift, saving power and area compared to parallel computations of all shifts. The design is synchronous and simple to verify.
