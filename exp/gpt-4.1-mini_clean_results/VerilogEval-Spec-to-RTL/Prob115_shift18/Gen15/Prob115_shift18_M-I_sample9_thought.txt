To optimize for performance, power, and area (PPA), I will simplify the shift operations by decomposing the shift amount selection into two steps: first shifting by 1 bit or 8 bits, then selecting direction. This allows the use of fewer multiplexers by reusing intermediate partial shifts. Also, for right arithmetic shifts, the sign extension can be done once per shift size rather than duplicating logic for 1 and 8 bits. Additionally, enable gating is implicitly achieved by selecting q when ena=0, so no redundant register toggling occurs. This approach reduces redundant muxing and concatenations, lowering logic depth and switching activity. The synchronous load logic remains unchanged for functional correctness.
