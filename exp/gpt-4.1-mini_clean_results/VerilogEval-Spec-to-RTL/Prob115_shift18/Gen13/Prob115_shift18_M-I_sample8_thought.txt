To optimize for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will reduce redundant logic by factoring the shift operations into a hierarchical approach rather than parallel duplication. Since shifts are always by either 1 or 8 bits and directions are left or right, I can first select shift direction, then apply a variable shift amount (1 or 8) with minimal multiplexing and simpler wiring.

For left shifts, the zero-filling approach is maintained; for arithmetic right shifts, sign extension is preserved. This hierarchical approach reduces the number of multiplexers and concatenations, thus cutting area and power and potentially improving timing.

Additionally, I will minimize the use of nested ternary expressions by splitting control signals into decoded wires for clarity and efficiency.

The synchronous load and enable logic remains unchanged for clean operation and power gating on enable.

This refined shift logic should offer a better tradeoff between complexity and speed/power/area.
