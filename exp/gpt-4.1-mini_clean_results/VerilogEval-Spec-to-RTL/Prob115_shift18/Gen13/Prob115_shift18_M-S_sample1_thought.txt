We simplify by implementing a single combinational always block to generate the shifted output based on 'amount' using a case statement, instead of preparing all four shifted variants with separate wires. This removes redundant combinational logic and muxing. The shift left and right operations use concatenations for shifts by 1 or 8 bits, and the arithmetic right shift replicates the MSB sign bit. The synchronous load and enable logic remain unchanged.

This approach reduces code complexity, mux layers, and logic duplication while maintaining the same functionality and clear, easily synthesizable style.
