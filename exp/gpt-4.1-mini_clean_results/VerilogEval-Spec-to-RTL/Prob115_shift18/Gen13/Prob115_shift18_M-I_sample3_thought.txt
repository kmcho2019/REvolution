To optimize the given design for better PPA without changing functionality, reduce combinational complexity by decomposing the shifts into smaller steps and replacing large multi-bit shift operators with concatenation and slicing, which synthesis tools can handle more efficiently. Instead of performing separate 64-bit shift operations and selecting among them, perform one 8-bit shift first (which can be no shift if amount[0]=0), then a 1-bit shift, and select direction separately. This hierarchical approach reduces the size of multiplexers and logic depth. Also, use a single arithmetic right shift logic for sign extension only once at the end. The improved approach reduces critical path delay and area by minimizing duplicated large shifts. The synchronous load and enable logic is maintained as is.
