Simplify the design by directly implementing the shift logic with simple conditional operators instead of precomputing all shifts and using multiplexers. Since there are only four fixed shift operations (shift left/right by 1 or 8), use a single combinational block that performs the appropriate shift based on the 'amount' input. The arithmetic right shift can be done by replicating the sign bit for the shifted-in bits. This removes extra wires and multiplexers and results in cleaner code without redundant signals, while retaining the required synchronous load and enable functionality.
