To improve PPA, I will combine the two-stage shift into a single-level multiplexed shift operation. This reduces combinational logic depth, lowering delay and area. The design will precompute left shift by 1 and 8 bits and right arithmetic shift by 1 and 8 bits as separate candidates, then select the final shifted output using a single multiplexer indexed by amount. This avoids intermediate registers and two always@(*) blocks, reducing logic and power. The synchronous load and enable logic remain unchanged. This approach implements an efficient barrel shifter tailored for the four shift amounts required, improving timing and area compared to the prior two-step shift.
