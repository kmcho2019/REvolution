Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a single always block with a case statement on the amount input, handling load and shift operations together. For shifting, use a register to hold the current value and perform shifts using variable bit slicing and concatenation inside the case. To avoid evaluating all shifts in parallel, only compute the shift corresponding to the selected amount. For arithmetic right shifts, sign-extend by replicating the MSB for the shifted-in bits. The shifting and loading happen synchronously on the rising clock edge, conditioned by the load and ena signals. This architecture simplifies logic by avoiding parallel shift computations and uses explicit case branches for clarity and potential optimization by synthesis tools.
