Implement a compact 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a simple two-stage multiplexing structure to handle direction and amount shifts efficiently.

The approach:
- Use a single synchronous always block triggered by clk.
- On load, directly load the data input.
- On enable (and no load), shift by either 1 or 8 bits as specified.
- For left shifts:
  - Shift left by concatenating shifted bits with zeros.
- For arithmetic right shifts:
  - Extend sign bit replicated either 1 or 8 times to fill the shifted-in bits.
- Use a single multiplexer tree for choosing shift amount (1 or 8) based on amount[0].
- Use a separate mux for direction selection (left or right arithmetic).
- This modular approach avoids multiple redundant shift computations.
- Sign extension logic is centralized and reused.
- Minimizes hardware by sharing sign extension and shift multiplexers.
- Keeps combinational logic depth minimal for timing, with clear synchronous load/enable control.
