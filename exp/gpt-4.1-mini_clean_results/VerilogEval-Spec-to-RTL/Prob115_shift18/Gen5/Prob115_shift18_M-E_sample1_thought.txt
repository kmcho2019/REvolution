Implement a single combinational barrel shifter using a 3-level multiplexer tree that shifts the input by the exact amount (1 or 8 bits) in the specified direction in one step. Decode the direction and shift amount separately, then use a single always_comb block to perform the shift combinationally.

For left shifts, perform zero-filling on the right.

For arithmetic right shifts, replicate the MSB as sign extension on the left.

This approach minimizes combinational logic stages by avoiding staged shifts and intermediate registers, reducing combinational delay and potentially improving timing.

On the positive clock edge, if load is asserted, load data synchronously; else if ena is asserted, update the register with shifted data; otherwise, hold the value.

This design simplifies the data path by using a straightforward barrel shifter with direct multiplexing controlled by direction and amount signals.
