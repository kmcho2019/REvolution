To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with selectable shift amounts (1 or 8) and direction (left or right), I will leverage explicit concatenation wiring for shifts instead of the generic shift operators. This reduces logic complexity and helps synthesis tools optimize better.

For left shifts by 1 and 8 bits, I can use direct concatenation: 
- Left shift by 1 bit: {q[62:0], 1'b0}
- Left shift by 8 bits: {q[55:0], 8'b0}

For arithmetic right shifts by 1 and 8 bits, I replicate the MSB sign bit for the vacated positions:
- Right shift by 1 bit: {q[63], q[63:1]}
- Right shift by 8 bits: {{8{q[63]}}, q[63:8]}

I will use a case statement inside the sequential always block to select the shifted value according to amount, which could improve clarity and synthesize mux trees more optimally.

- On clk rising edge:
