To simplify the design while maintaining full functionality, I will remove the intermediate wires for each shift variant and directly compute the next value inside the always block using a case statement keyed on 'amount'. This eliminates multiple parallel shift computations and multiplexers, reducing the combinational logic and improving clarity. The synchronous load and enable logic will remain the same. The arithmetic right shift sign extension will be done inline by replicating the MSB for the shifted-in bits. This approach keeps the design straightforward and easy to understand while being functionally correct.
