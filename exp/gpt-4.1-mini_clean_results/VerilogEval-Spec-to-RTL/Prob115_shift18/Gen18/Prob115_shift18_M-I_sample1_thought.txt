To optimize the existing design for better PPA, we reduce redundant computations by computing only the required shift based on the direction bit first, then selecting the shift amount. This halves the number of shift operations computed in parallel, saving area and power. Also, implementing shift-left and arithmetic shift-right by concatenation and replication rather than shift operators may guide synthesis tools towards simpler logic. This also simplifies the sign extension for arithmetic right shift. The control mux hierarchy is simplified from two levels (direction then amount) to one level (combined amount), minimizing logic levels and improving timing. These changes maintain the exact functionality while reducing combinational complexity and potentially improving timing, power, and area.
