To optimize the original two-stage shift logic for better PPA (performance, power, and area), combine the shifting into a single combinational stage using a 64-bit barrel shifter approach. This single-stage barrel shifter uses multiplexers controlled by the 'amount' input to perform shifts by either 1 or 8 bits in left or right directions, including arithmetic right shifts with proper sign extension. By merging the shifts into one stage, the critical path is reduced and the logic becomes more compact and power-efficient. This eliminates the intermediate registers and separate combinational stages, reducing mux and logic depth. Load and enable signals remain synchronous for clean timing and gating of register updates, thus minimizing unnecessary switching activity. Overall, this results in a simpler and more efficient hardware implementation without changing functionality.
