Implement a single combinational barrel shifter that handles all four shift operations (left/right by 1 or 8 bits) in one step using multiplexers controlled by 'amount'. For the right arithmetic shifts, replicate the sign bit for the shifted-in positions. The sequential block loads data when load is high; otherwise, if ena is high, it updates q with the shifted value. This design reduces sequential combinational stages into one, simplifies control logic, and potentially improves timing and area by avoiding staged shifts.
