Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a two-step shifting approach that separates shift amount and direction. First, use a small multiplexer stage to select between no shift, shift by 1, or shift by 8 bits for left and right directions independently. This can be done by first shifting by 1 bit if needed, then by 8 bits if needed, selected by amount signals, effectively decomposing the shift into smaller multiplexing steps. For the arithmetic right shift, maintain the sign bit extension for all shifted-in bits. Also, use a single synchronous process with load priority, and only shift when enabled and load is low. This approach may reduce combinational complexity in synthesis and improve clarity by modularizing shift operations, differing from the original single barrel shifter style.
