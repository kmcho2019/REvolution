Use the Verilog shift operators (<< and >>>) directly to perform shifts, which are simpler and cleaner. On a clock edge, if load is asserted, load the data; else if ena is asserted, shift q according to amount: left by 1 or 8 bits by shifting and zero-filling the right; right by 1 or 8 bits by arithmetic shift right using >>> operator, which automatically sign-extends. Otherwise, keep q unchanged.
