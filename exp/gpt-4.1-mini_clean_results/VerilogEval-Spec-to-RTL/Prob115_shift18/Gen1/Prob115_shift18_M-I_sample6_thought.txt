To improve the PPA of the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, I will implement the shift operation using a two-stage hierarchical barrel shifter: first shifting by the 8-bit amount if needed, then by the 1-bit amount. This decomposition reduces the combinational logic fan-in and shortens the critical path compared to doing large concatenations in one step.

For both left and arithmetic right shifts:
- Perform an 8-bit shift when amount[0] = 1 (i.e., for amounts 01 or 11).
- Then perform a 1-bit shift when amount[1] = 0 or 1, corresponding to left or right direction.
  
Additionally:
- Use Verilog's shift operators (<<, >>>) for clarity and synthesis efficiency.
- Sign-extend arithmetic right shifts by replicating the MSB before shifting.
- Avoid large concatenations to reduce gate count and fan-in.
- Retain synchronous load and enable as before.

This staged approach balances the shift amount and direction with smaller multiplexers and shifters, reducing the critical path, area, and potentially power, improving overall PPA.
