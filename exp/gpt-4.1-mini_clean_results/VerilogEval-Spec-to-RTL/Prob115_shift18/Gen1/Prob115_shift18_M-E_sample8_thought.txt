Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register by decomposing the shift operation into two steps: first perform an 8-bit shift if needed, then a 1-bit shift if needed, using multiplexers to select intermediate results. This avoids large barrel shifters shifting by 1 or 8 bits in a single step and may reduce combinational delay and area. On each clock edge, if load is high, load the data directly; otherwise, if ena is high, apply the two-step shift determined by the amount input. For right shifts, replicate the MSB during both steps for arithmetic shift right behavior. Output q holds the current register value.
