Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a barrel shifter approach with hierarchical multiplexing. The barrel shifter performs the shift in two stages: first a byte-wise shift by 8 bits, then a bit-wise shift by 1 bit, based on the amount signal. This allows shifting by either 1 or 8 bits in either direction by selecting the appropriate multiplexers. The design uses combinational logic to generate the shifted value from the current state q, then on clock edge, loads the shifted value or the input data depending on load and enable signals. For the arithmetic right shift, sign extension bits are inserted appropriately. This modular approach avoids complex concatenations with large replicated bits and promotes a clean and efficient hardware structure, likely to result in a balanced tree of multiplexers and improved synthesis results.
