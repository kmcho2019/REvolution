Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register by splitting the shifting operation into two steps: first, select whether to shift by 1 or by 8 bits using a barrel shifter-like approach, and second, decide the direction (left or right) and perform the appropriate shift with correct sign or zero extension. This two-stage decomposition reduces combinational complexity by isolating the shift magnitude and shift direction operations. Also, use a procedural combinational always block to compute the next value, improving readability. The synchronous load and enable controls are handled by the clocked always block that updates the register.
