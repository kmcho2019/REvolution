Simplify the design by explicitly implementing only two fixed shifters for left and right shifts: one shifting by 1 and another by 8 bits. Then select the appropriate output with a small multiplexer controlled by 'amount'. This eliminates multiple large shifts and complex selection logic. The arithmetic right shift sign extension can be done efficiently by replicating the sign bit only when shifting right. By separating the direction and amount decisions cleanly, the logic is more transparent and likely simpler for synthesis tools. The rest of the logic remains synchronous with load and enable signals controlling the register update.
