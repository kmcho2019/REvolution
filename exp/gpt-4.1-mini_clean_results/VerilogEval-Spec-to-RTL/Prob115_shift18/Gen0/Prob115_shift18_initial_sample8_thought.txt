Implement a synchronous process triggered on the rising edge of clk. On each clock:
- If load is high, load the 64-bit register with input data.
- Else if ena is high, shift the register based on amount:
  - 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit (shift in zeros)
  - 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits (shift in zeros)
  - 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit (shift in sign bit)
  - 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits (shift in sign bit)
  
For arithmetic right shift, the leftmost bits should be filled with the sign bit q[63]. For left shift, fill shifted-in bits with zeros. Store the current register in q.

Provide all signals as specified.
