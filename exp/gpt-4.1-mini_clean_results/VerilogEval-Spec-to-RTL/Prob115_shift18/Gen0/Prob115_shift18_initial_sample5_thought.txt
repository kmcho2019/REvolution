Implement a synchronous 64-bit register that either loads the input data when 'load' is high or shifts the current content by a specified amount and direction when 'ena' is high. The shift amount and direction are determined by the 2-bit 'amount' input as described: 00 - shift left by 1, 01 - shift left by 8, 10 - arithmetic shift right by 1, 11 - arithmetic shift right by 8. When shifting right, use arithmetic right shift which preserves the sign bit (MSB). If neither load nor ena is high, hold the current value.
