Implement a 64-bit register that synchronously loads data when load is high.
When ena is high and load is low, shift the register contents according to the amount signal.
Shifts:
 - 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit (fill right with 0)
 - 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits (fill right with 0)
 - 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit (fill left with sign bit)
 - 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits (fill left with sign bit)
On each clock rising edge, update q accordingly.
