Implement a 64-bit register with synchronous load and shift functionality. On each clock edge, if load is high, load the input data into the register. Otherwise, if ena is high, perform a shift according to the amount signal: shift left by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b00 and 2'b01 respectively, or arithmetic shift right by 1 or 8 bits for 2'b10 and 2'b11 respectively. For the arithmetic right shift, replicate the sign bit (MSB) on the left while shifting right. Output the current register value on q.
