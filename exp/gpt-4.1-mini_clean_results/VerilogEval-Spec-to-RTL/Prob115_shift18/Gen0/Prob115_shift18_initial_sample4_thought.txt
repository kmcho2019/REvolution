Implement a 64-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk. On load high, load data into the register. Otherwise, if ena is high, shift the register according to amount:
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit (fill with zeros)
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits (fill with zeros)
- 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit (fill with sign bit)
- 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits (fill with sign bits)
Output the current register value on q.
