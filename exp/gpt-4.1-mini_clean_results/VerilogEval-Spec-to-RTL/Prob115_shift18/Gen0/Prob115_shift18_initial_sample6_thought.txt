Implement a synchronous always block triggered on the rising edge of clk. If load is high, q is loaded with data directly. Otherwise, if ena is high, perform the shift operation based on the amount input: 
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit, inserting zeros at LSB.
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits, inserting zeros at LSB.
- 2'b10: arithmetic shift right by 1 bit, MSB replicated.
- 2'b11: arithmetic shift right by 8 bits, MSB replicated.
If ena is low and load is low, q holds its value.
