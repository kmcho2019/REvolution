Implement a 64-bit register that on the rising edge of clk, if load is asserted, loads the input data directly. Otherwise if ena is asserted, shift the register contents according to amount: shift left by 1 or 8 bits, or shift right arithmetically by 1 or 8 bits. For left shift, fill with zeros on the right. For arithmetic right shift, fill the leftmost bits with the sign bit (bit 63). If neither load nor ena is asserted, retain the current value. Output the register contents as q.
