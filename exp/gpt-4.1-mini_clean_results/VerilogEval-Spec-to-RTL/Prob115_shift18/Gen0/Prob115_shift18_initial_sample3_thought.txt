Implement a 64-bit shift register with synchronous load on clk's rising edge. When load is high, load input data directly into the register. When load is low and ena is high, perform shift based on the amount value:
- For left shift by 1 bit: shift left by 1, fill LSBs with zeros.
- For left shift by 8 bits: shift left by 8, fill lower 8 bits with zeros.
- For arithmetic right shift by 1 bit: shift right by 1, fill MSB with original MSB (sign extension).
- For arithmetic right shift by 8 bits: shift right by 8, fill upper 8 bits with original MSB (sign extension).
When ena is low and load is low, maintain current value.
Output q is the current value of the shift register.
