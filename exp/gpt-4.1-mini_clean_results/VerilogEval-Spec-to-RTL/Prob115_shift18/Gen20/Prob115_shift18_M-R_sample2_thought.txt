Refactor the design to use a combinational always block that calculates the next shift register value based on load, enable, and shift amount signals, and then register the result on the clock edge. This approach centralizes the next-state logic, making it easier to extend or modify. Also, compute the shifted values upfront with clear intermediate signals for each shift operation (left1, left8, right1, right8) and then select among them with a case statement inside the combinational block. This improves readability and maintains the same functionality without nested ternary operators or multiple assign statements. The synchronous load and enable signals control whether the next state comes from data or shifted values, and finally q is updated on clock edge.
