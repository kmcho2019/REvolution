I will implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a hierarchical multiplexing scheme based on splitting the shift operation into two stages: first deciding direction (left or right), then applying the shift amount (1 or 8 bits) accordingly. 

However, instead of precomputing all shifts in parallel, I will:

- Use two intermediate registers updated combinationally for left and right shifts separately, to avoid redundant computations.
- For left shifts, use concatenation with zero fill.
- For arithmetic right shifts, replicate the sign bit for sign extension.
- Use a case statement inside a combinational always block to select the next value based on load, ena, direction, and amount signals.
- This approach reduces combinational logic duplication by computing only the required shift.
- Synchronous load overrides shifts.
- Overall, this design emphasizes clarity and minimal redundant computation by sequential conditional selection rather than multiple parallel wires.
