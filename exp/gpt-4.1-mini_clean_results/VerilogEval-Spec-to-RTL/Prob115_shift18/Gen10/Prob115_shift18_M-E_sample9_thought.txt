Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register using a two-stage combinational barrel shifter approach for left and right shifts separately, selected by the MSB of "amount". Internally generate two shifted candidates: one for left shifts (either by 1 or 8 bits) and one for arithmetic right shifts (by 1 or 8 bits), and then select between them based on the direction bit of "amount". Use the LSB of "amount" to choose between shifting by 1 or 8 bits within each direction. On each clock, if load is asserted, synchronously load the input data; else if enable is asserted, load the selected shifted value; else hold previous value. This approach breaks down the shift direction and magnitude distinctly, simplifies the combinational logic, and allows separate optimized shift wiring for left and arithmetic right shifts. It also avoids a function call by using explicit combinational logic and minimizes the multiplexer tree complexity.
