To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will directly implement the shift operations inside a single always block using a case statement on 'amount' when shifting is enabled. This avoids precomputing all possible shifts and reduces combinational complexity and multiplexer logic. The arithmetic right shift will be done by replicating the sign bit as needed. When 'load' is active, synchronous loading of 'data' occurs. When 'ena' is low, the register retains its value. This straightforward approach minimizes combinational logic outside the register and makes the logic easier to understand and verify.
