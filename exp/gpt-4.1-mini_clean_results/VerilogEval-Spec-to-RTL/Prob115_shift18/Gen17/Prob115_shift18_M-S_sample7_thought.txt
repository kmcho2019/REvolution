Implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load and enable using a single combinational shift operation based on the direction and amount, using the Verilog shift operators with explicit sign extension for arithmetic right shifts.

Simplify the logic by directly encoding the shift amount as a signed integer: positive values for left shifts and negative for right shifts, then perform either a left or arithmetic right shift accordingly.

This approach reduces the number of muxes and separate computations, leveraging Verilog's built-in shift operators for cleaner, more compact code while maintaining full functionality.

Synchronous load and enable are handled straightforwardly in an always block.

This yields concise and readable RTL code, simplifying the previous design without altering behavior.
