Refactor the design to separate the next state logic from the register update and use a combinational always block with a case statement for shift selection. This structure replaces the multiple nested conditional operators with a clean and explicit case-based approach for selecting the shifted value, improving readability and potentially helping synthesis tools optimize multiplexers.

The shift computation remains the same, but the final selection of the shifted value is performed inside a combinational always block assigned to a `next_q` signal. The register `q` is then updated synchronously on the clock with `next_q` or `data` if load is asserted.

This refactor preserves functionality while providing a clear state update pipeline and makes the shifting logic explicit and structured, which can ease further optimizations or modifications.
