To improve PPA (Power, Performance, Area) over the given well-structured design, focus on reducing unnecessary combinational switching by gating shift computations with the enable signal and selecting shift direction earlier to avoid computing both left and right shifts every cycle.

Specifically:

- Compute only the shifts required by the current direction and amount, enabled only when shifting is enabled (ena=1).
- Use local signals for the selected shift amount and direction to minimize mux complexity.
- Implement shift computations inside a combinational always block triggered by the relevant inputs (q, ena, amount) to reduce power when not shifting.
- Retain synchronous load and hold behavior.
- This reduces dynamic power by avoiding parallel shift computations and muxing unnecessary signals.
- The overall latency and area should be similar or better since redundant logic toggling is minimized.

This approach balances power by gating shift computations, potentially improving timing by reducing mux fanout, and keeps the design clean and easy to understand.
