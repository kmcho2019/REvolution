Implement the 64-bit shift register using explicit concatenation and partial slicing, performing the shift by manually wiring segments shifted by either 1 or 8 bits in either direction. This avoids general-purpose shifters and leverages fixed shift amounts for clean and area-efficient implementation.

On each clock edge, if load is asserted, synchronously load the data. Else, if ena is asserted, update the register by shifting according to amount.

For left shifts:
- For shift by 1, shift left by 1 bit and fill the LSB with zero.
- For shift by 8, shift left by 8 bits and fill the lowest 8 bits with zero.

For arithmetic right shifts:
- For shift by 1, shift right by 1 bit and replicate the MSB in the newly introduced bit.
- For shift by 8, shift right by 8 bits and replicate the MSB in the newly introduced upper 8 bits.

This method uses explicit assignments without variable shifts, improving synthesis quality and potentially saving area and power compared to variable barrel shifters. The design prioritizes load > shift > hold logic straightforwardly.
