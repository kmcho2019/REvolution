Implement the shift register using a hierarchical two-stage shift approach. First, shift by 8 bits if needed, then shift by 1 bit if needed. The amount input encodes both the direction and the magnitude in a way that can be split into two shift enables (shift by 8 and shift by 1). For left shifts, shifting is logical (zeros shifted in). For right shifts, the shift-in bits for the arithmetic right shifts replicate the sign bit. This approach uses two separate stages of shifting combined with multiplexers, minimizing the number of complex multiplexers for all four cases at once and clearly structuring the logic. Synchronous load and enable operate as before. This design can reduce redundant logic by reusing intermediate signals and split shifts instead of a full 4-way mux.
