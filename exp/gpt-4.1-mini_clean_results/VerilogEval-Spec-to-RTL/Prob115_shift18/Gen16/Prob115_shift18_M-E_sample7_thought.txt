Implement the shift register using a single always block with a case statement selecting the shifted output based on the "amount" input. Compute the shifted values explicitly for each mode within the synchronous block, thus avoiding complex combinational logic outside the register and potentially improving synthesis optimization.

- When load is high, load data directly.
- When ena is high and load is low, perform the shift specified by amount:
  * 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit (shift in zeros).
  * 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits (shift in zeros).
  * 2'b10: arithmetic right shift by 1 bit (shift in MSB sign bit).
  * 2'b11: arithmetic right shift by 8 bits (shift in MSB sign bit).
- Hold the previous value when ena is low.
- This approach centralizes shifting logic, making the logic simpler to follow and reducing intermediate wiring.
- Sign extension for right shift is done using replication of MSB as required.
