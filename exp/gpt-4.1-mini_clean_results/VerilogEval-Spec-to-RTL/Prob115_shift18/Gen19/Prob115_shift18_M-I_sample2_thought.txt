To optimize for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) based on the existing metrics and feedback, I will apply the following improvements:

- Reduce unnecessary combinational toggling by gating the shift computations with the enable and load signals. This prevents the shift wires from switching when not needed, saving dynamic power.
- Use hierarchical muxing to reduce the fanout of large 64-bit multiplexers, slightly improving timing and area.
- Retain the sign extension and zero fill shift implementation for area efficiency.
- Ensure the synchronous load and enable logic remains clean.
- Avoid complex barrel shifters and wide shifters since shift amounts are fixed (1 or 8 bits) and direction controlled by only two bits.
- This gating can be done using an intermediate enable signal to control the shift computations, turning off unnecessary logic when no shift is performed.

This should reduce dynamic power, possibly improve timing slightly by gating switching, and marginally reduce area by limiting toggling and mux fanout.
