To optimize the initial design for better PPA, I will reduce unnecessary computation and switching activity by gating the shift calculations with the enable signal. When ena=0, shift outputs will not be computed, and the register will hold its value, reducing dynamic power. I will merge the shift amount and direction selection into a single combinational block using a case statement to simplify mux trees, improving timing and area. Also, by performing the sign extension only when shifting right and based on amount, we avoid redundant logic. This design still uses parallel computations but disables them when not needed, which reduces power without adding extra pipeline stages or complex control. This keeps the implementation clean and synthesizable for both FPGA and ASIC targets.
