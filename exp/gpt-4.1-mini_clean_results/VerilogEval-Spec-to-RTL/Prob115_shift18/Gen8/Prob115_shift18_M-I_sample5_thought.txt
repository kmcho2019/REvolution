To improve the PPA metrics (timing, power, and area), I will optimize the combinational shift logic to reduce the critical path and switching activity:

1. Use hierarchical shifting: perform shift by 1 bit and shift by 8 bits in separate steps rather than separate full 64-bit multiplexers for each case. This reduces logic duplication and mux sizes.

2. Implement a two-step shift approach:
   - First, shift either by 8 bits or keep original.
   - Then, shift either by 1 bit or keep result of previous stage.
   - The direction is applied once after composing these shifts.
   
   Since shift amount is only 1 or 8 bits, this approach can be done via multiplexing with smaller muxes, reducing logic depth.

3. Since the directions and amounts are limited, split left and right shifts and use smaller intermediate wires.

4. Also, introduce clock enable gating by only updating the register when (load | ena) is high, reducing unnecessary toggling in q.

5. By chaining shifts, we reduce the size of multiplexers and combinational fan-in, improving timing and power.

Overall, this modular approach reduces the critical path mux complexity and switching power.

Note: The output remains registered on clk's rising edge, and synchronous load has highest priority.
