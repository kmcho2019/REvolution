To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we focus on reducing the critical path and switching activity:

1. Break the 64-bit shift operations into two stages:
   - First, perform either an 8-bit shift or a 1-bit shift.
   - This reduces large multiplexers and concatenation logic.
   
2. Use a two-level shift approach:
   - Shift by 8 bits if amount[0] is set, else shift by 1 bit.
   - Then, select direction (left or arithmetic right) afterwards.

3. By splitting shift amount and direction, we reduce the fan-in of multiplexers and complexity of combinational logic.

4. Implement the arithmetic right shift using sign extension explicitly to limit the number of replicated bits.

5. Keep the synchronous load and enable logic as is for clarity.

This restructuring reduces the complexity of multiplexers, resulting in a shorter critical path and less switching, improving overall PPA.
