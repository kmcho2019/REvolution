Since the original design is a minimal combinational AND gate already, there is no room to reduce logic or critical path. However, to optimize power slightly, we can use an always_comb block to describe the AND operation instead of a continuous assign statement. This can help synthesis tools with optimization and could reduce glitching due to direct combinational logic on the output net. Also, using 'logic' type for output (SystemVerilog style) can improve clarity and synthesis friendliness. This is a very minor stylistic and potential power optimization, but given the triviality of the logic, the impact is minimal.
