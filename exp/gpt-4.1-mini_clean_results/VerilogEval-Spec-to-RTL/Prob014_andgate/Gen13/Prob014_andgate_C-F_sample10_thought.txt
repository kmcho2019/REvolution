To combine the strengths of both examples, I will implement the 2-input AND gate directly and concisely in the TopModule by using a built-in Verilog primitive AND gate. This provides the minimal area and power benefits seen in the first example due to direct gate instantiation, while maintaining the simplicity and clarity of a single-module design from the second example by avoiding hierarchy overhead. This fusion avoids the slight overhead of a separate module instantiation and benefits from explicit gate-level primitive mapping to hardware, resulting in optimal PPA metrics with clean and easy-to-understand code.
