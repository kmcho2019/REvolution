Fuse the modularity and clarity from the hierarchical design with the directness and minimalism of the flattened design by defining a parameterized reusable 2-input AND gate module that can be easily instantiated in TopModule. This allows scalability for future reuse while keeping the TopModule code clean and minimizing hierarchy depth. The parameterization doesn't impact this 2-input case but prepares for extensibility. Internally, the AndGate uses a continuous assignment ensuring optimal synthesis. This approach balances modularity, scalability, and minimal overhead for area and power.
