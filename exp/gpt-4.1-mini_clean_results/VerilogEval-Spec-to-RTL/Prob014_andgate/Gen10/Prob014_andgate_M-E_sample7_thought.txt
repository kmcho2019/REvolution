To provide a novel architectural idea distinct from the direct continuous assignment, I will implement the 2-input AND gate using a behavioral always block with a reg output. This approach explicitly describes the combinational logic within an always @(*) block, which some designers prefer for clarity or to integrate with more complex logic later. The output will be declared as reg, and updated combinationally based on inputs a and b. Although functionally equivalent, this style uses a procedural block instead of continuous assignment, showcasing a different Verilog modeling style.
