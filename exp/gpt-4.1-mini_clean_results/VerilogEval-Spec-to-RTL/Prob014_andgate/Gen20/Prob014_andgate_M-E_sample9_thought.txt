Implement TopModule as a single structural Verilog module that explicitly instantiates two NOT gates and one NOR gate to create an AND gate using De Morgan's law: A AND B = NOT (NOT A OR NOT B). This design demonstrates the ability to build basic logic from universal gates, providing educational insight and alternative architectural style. Though less optimal in power and performance than a direct AND gate, this approach showcases hierarchical composition of basic gates and can be extended to custom gate libraries or FPGA LUT mapping scenarios.
