Implement the 2-input AND gate using only NOR gates and inverters, exploiting De Morgan's theorem: A AND B = NOT (NOT A OR NOT B). First, invert both inputs using NOT gates (inverters), then feed these inverted signals into a 2-input NOR gate, whose output is the AND of the original inputs. This approach uses a different gate-level decomposition than NAND-inverter and demonstrates the use of NOR gates for implementing AND logic.
