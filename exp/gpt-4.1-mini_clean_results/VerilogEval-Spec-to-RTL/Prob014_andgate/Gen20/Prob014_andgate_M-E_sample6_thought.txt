Implement the 2-input AND function by first using two inverters on inputs a and b to produce their complements, then feed these complemented inputs to a NAND gate, and finally invert the NAND output to get the AND function. This leverages De Morgan's law: AND(a,b) = NOT( NAND( NOT a, NOT b ) ). This design demonstrates hierarchical gate-level decomposition emphasizing the use of inverters and NAND gates, but in a different structural configuration from the original approach.
