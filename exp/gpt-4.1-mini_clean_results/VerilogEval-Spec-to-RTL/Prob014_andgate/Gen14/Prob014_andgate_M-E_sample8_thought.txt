I will implement the 2-input AND gate using behavioral Verilog with a continuous assignment statement. Instead of using a built-in gate primitive, the AND functionality will be described with a bitwise AND operator in an assign statement. This approach is very simple and leverages high-level RTL style, which is commonly used in synthesis tools. It also avoids explicit gate primitives, which may make the design more portable and easier to integrate or modify in a larger system.
