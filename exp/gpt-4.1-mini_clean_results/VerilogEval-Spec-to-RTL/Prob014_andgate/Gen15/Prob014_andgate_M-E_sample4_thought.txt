Implement the 2-input AND functionality inside TopModule directly using structural Verilog by instantiating two 1-input buffers feeding into a 2-input primitive and, instead of using a single AND gate directly, construct the AND gate using two NAND gates and one inverter for demonstration of hierarchical primitive decomposition. This shows how complex gates can be built from simpler gates, which can be useful in some design or educational contexts. The TopModule will internally instantiate two 1-input buffers (which simply forward inputs), then a NAND gate and an inverter to produce the AND output. This approach emphasizes fundamental gate decomposition and hierarchical design without parameterization.
