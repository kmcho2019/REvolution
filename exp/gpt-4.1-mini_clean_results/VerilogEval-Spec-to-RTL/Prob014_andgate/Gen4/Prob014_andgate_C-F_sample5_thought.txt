To fuse the strengths of both examples, I will implement the 2-input AND gate as a parameterized module with a direct continuous assignment. This maintains the minimal area and power benefits from continuous assignment (Example 2) but adds flexibility and reuse potential by allowing the width of the inputs and output to be set through a parameter. This approach introduces no procedural complexity while enabling scalable designs without code modification. The output remains a wire, and no always blocks are used, preserving optimal synthesis and PPA characteristics. Thus, the design is both minimal and scalable.
