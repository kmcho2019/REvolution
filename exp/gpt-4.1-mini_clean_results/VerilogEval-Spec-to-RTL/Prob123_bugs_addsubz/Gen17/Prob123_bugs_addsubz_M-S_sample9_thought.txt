The original solution implements a full adder chain explicitly, which is more complex than necessary for this problem.

Since the addition and subtraction are straightforward 8-bit operations and the zero flag just needs to indicate if the result is zero, we can simplify by:

- Using the built-in Verilog addition/subtraction operators directly with the conditional operator.
- Using a reduction NOR (~|) on the output to generate the zero flag.
- Keeping outputs as reg to match the original module interface and making the always block purely combinational.

This reduces complexity, improves readability, and maintains correctness without manually implementing adder logic.
