To improve the original ripple-carry adder-based solution for better PPA while maintaining correctness:

- Replace the ripple carry adder with the built-in Verilog addition operator using a 9-bit wide sum to internally handle carry-out cleanly and use two's complement subtraction by conditional inversion and adding do_sub.
- Using the built-in '+' operator allows synthesis tools to infer optimized carry-lookahead or other fast adder architectures, improving the delay (better WNS and TNS).
- Keep the logic purely combinational with assign statements for clarity and best synthesis results.
- The zero flag is computed by a simple reduction NOR on the 8-bit output sum.
- This approach reduces the explicit carry propagation logic in the RTL, enabling better synthesis optimizations for performance and power.
- Area should stay comparable or better due to simpler RTL and possible adder optimizations by synthesis tools.

Overall, this change improves performance by enabling better adder inference, simplifies the design, and reduces logic levels without changing functionality.
