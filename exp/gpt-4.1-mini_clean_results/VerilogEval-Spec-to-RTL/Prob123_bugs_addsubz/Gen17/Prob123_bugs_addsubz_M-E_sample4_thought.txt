Implement the adder-subtractor using a purely arithmetic approach that leverages the Verilog built-in addition operator with a single conditional operand, avoiding explicit bitwise manipulation or carry chain logic. Specifically, compute the result as `a + (do_sub ? -b : b)` using two's complement negation for subtraction. This approach simplifies the design, uses synthesis tools' optimized arithmetic units, and eliminates manual carry handling.

For the zero flag, perform a reduction NOR on the output to accurately detect zero.

The outputs are assigned in an always_comb block to maintain combinational semantics, and declared as reg to drive them procedurally for compatibility.

This method is concise, leverages synthesis optimizations, and improves clarity and maintainability without explicit carry chains.
