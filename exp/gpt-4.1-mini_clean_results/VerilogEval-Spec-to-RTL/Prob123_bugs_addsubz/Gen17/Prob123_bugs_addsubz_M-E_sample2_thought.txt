I will implement a unified adder-subtractor using a single 8-bit adder with the subtraction operation performed by conditionally inverting the second operand (b) and adding a carry-in of 1 for two's complement subtraction. The carry-in will be controlled by the do_sub signal. This approach saves area and power by avoiding duplicate adders. The zero flag will be generated using an equality comparison to zero, using a continuous assignment for correctness and simplicity. The entire operation and zero flag update will be combinational and cleanly separated from the output register, ensuring no latch inference or undriven signals.
