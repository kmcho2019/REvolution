To optimize the design for better PPA, I will simplify the zero flag generation by using a bitwise NOR reduction instead of an equality comparison. This reduces logic levels for zero detection, potentially lowering delay and power. I will also implement the zero flag as a combinational wire instead of a reg to avoid unnecessary register inference or latch risks. Since out is driven combinationally, changing it to a wire also removes the need for reg declaration, simplifying logic inference. Overall, this slightly improves timing and power by minimizing logic depth and removes unnecessary registers for outputs. The main adder/subtractor logic using two's complement subtraction with conditional inversion and carry-in remains unchanged for optimal area and performance.
