Instead of using a bitwise conditional inversion and carry-in, I will implement the adder-subtractor by explicitly performing both addition and subtraction operations using two separate adders, then multiplex the results based on the do_sub signal. The zero flag will be computed after the output is assigned. This approach ensures a straightforward and bug-free implementation by separating concerns clearly, and also simplifies debugging and verification, though it may use slightly more area. This architectural change is novel compared to the original and previous example.
