I will implement a simple combinational adder-subtractor using the built-in '+' operator, leveraging Verilog's ability to perform two's complement subtraction by negating the second operand when do_sub is asserted. This approach reduces complexity by avoiding explicit carry-chain logic and uses a straightforward conditional operand for subtraction:

- Use a wire for an operand modified by subtraction control: when do_sub=1, use the two's complement of b by negating b; when do_sub=0, use b directly.
- The out is assigned as a + modified operand.
- Generate the zero flag by reduction NOR of the out bits to check for zero.
- Use only continuous assignments and wires, no procedural blocks, ensuring purely combinational logic.
- This method is clean, efficient, and synthesizable, relying on Verilog's arithmetic operators to handle addition and subtraction correctly.
- It also improves synthesis friendliness by avoiding explicit carry logic while preserving functionality and zero detection.
