Implement the adder-subtractor using the built-in Verilog '+' operator with conditional two's complement negation of `b` for subtraction. This leverages high-level synthesis optimizations for addition and subtraction, avoiding manual carry chain management. The zero flag is assigned explicitly using a reduction NOR operation, ensuring correct detection of all-zero output. The always block uses blocking assignments and covers all cases, preventing latches and guaranteeing correct combinational behavior.

This design is simpler, concise, and relies on the synthesis tool to optimize the arithmetic, making it more maintainable and potentially more area- and power-efficient than manual ripple carry logic. It also fixes the original bug by assigning `result_is_zero` in all cases using a proper zero detection expression.
