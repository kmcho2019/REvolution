To simplify the adder-subtractor module, I will use the classic approach of conditionally inverting B with XOR using the do_sub signal and adding do_sub as the carry-in for subtraction. This allows a single addition operation for both addition and subtraction. The zero flag will be generated with a reduction NOR (~|out), which is concise and efficient. This approach removes the explicit ripple-carry implementation and uses Verilog's built-in addition operator to handle carry internally, greatly simplifying the design while maintaining correct functionality.
