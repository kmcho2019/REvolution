To optimize the original ripple-carry adder-subtractor for better performance (timing) without increasing area or power significantly, replace the ripple-carry logic with a built-in 8-bit adder operation and use Verilog's built-in addition and subtraction operators. This leverages synthesis tool optimizations, as modern tools map '+' and '-' to efficient adders internally, often using carry-lookahead or carry-select adders automatically.

Also, directly compute the zero flag with a reduction NOR on the output, maintaining combinational logic clarity.

Keep all signals as wires and outputs as wires, ensuring purely combinational logic.

This approach simplifies the code drastically, reduces manual carry logic and thus critical path delay, and is often better for synthesis and timing due to tool vendor optimizations.

Power and area will be similar or slightly improved because the design is more straightforward and better optimized by the tool.

No unnecessary intermediate carry wires or generate blocks, so less structural overhead.

This method relies on synthesis tools' efficient adder inference instead of manual ripple carry chains, resulting in better PPA.
