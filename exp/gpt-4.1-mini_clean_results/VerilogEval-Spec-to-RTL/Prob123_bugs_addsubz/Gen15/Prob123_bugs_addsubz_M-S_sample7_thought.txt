I will simplify the design by implementing the adder-subtractor as a single always block that conditionally performs addition or subtraction using the do_sub signal to invert and add one to b when subtracting (two's complement). This removes the need for separate modules and duplicated logic. The zero flag will be assigned explicitly by checking if the output is zero using a proper if-else statement to avoid inferred latches. This approach reduces complexity and maintains correct functionality.
