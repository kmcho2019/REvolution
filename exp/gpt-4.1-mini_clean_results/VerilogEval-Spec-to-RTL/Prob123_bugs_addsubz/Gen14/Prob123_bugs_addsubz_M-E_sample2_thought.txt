I will implement a single unified adder-subtractor using a conditional xor operation on the second operand to produce its two's complement when do_sub is asserted. This approach uses one 8-bit adder that adds 'a' to either 'b' or its bitwise inverted form plus 1, depending on do_sub. The carry-in for the adder will be the do_sub bit to effectively add 1 when subtracting. This eliminates the need for two separate modules and multiplexing. The zero flag is then assigned by checking if the output is zero explicitly with a comparison operator. This method is compact, efficient in area and power, and can achieve better performance by minimizing logic and routing.
