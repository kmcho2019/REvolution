To optimize the existing solution for better power and area while maintaining the same functional correctness, I will consolidate the always blocks into a single combinational block to reduce duplicated logic and improve synthesis efficiency. Also, I will use a continuous assign for the zero flag rather than a separate always block since `result_is_zero` is combinational and directly depends on `out`. This reduces unnecessary flip-flops and combinational logic replication. The addition and subtraction remain implemented with two's complement logic, which is optimal. The combined and simplified logic reduces area and potential glitching, thereby slightly improving power and performance as well.
