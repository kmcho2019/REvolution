I will implement the adder-subtractor by explicitly using a generate block to instantiate either an adder or a subtractor module, selected by do_sub, making the architecture modular. The subtractor will be implemented as an adder with two's complement negation internally. This clear separation allows for modular verification and reusability. The zero flag will be assigned using an if-else construct based on the combinational result. This approach differs from the prior single-adder approach by leveraging modular hierarchy and conditional instantiation.
