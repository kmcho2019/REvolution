I will refactor the module to use continuous assignments (`assign`) instead of an always block for combinational logic. This approach simplifies the design by directly assigning the outputs from intermediate wires, improving clarity and potentially allowing better synthesis optimizations.

Specifically:
- Compute the addition or subtraction result using a wire for the full sum.
- Assign `out` and `result_is_zero` with continuous assignments based on the computed sum.
- Use the reduction NOR operator (~|) on the output to generate the zero flag.

This structure reduces the number of registers and procedural code, providing a cleaner and more direct description of combinational behavior.
