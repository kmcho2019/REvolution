Refactor the design by making all outputs purely combinational with continuous assignments instead of using an always block and registered outputs. This simplifies the structure, reduces register usage, and clearly separates logic from storage. The two's complement subtraction logic remains the same, with conditional inversion and carry-in addition. The zero flag is computed using a reduction NOR on the combinational output.
