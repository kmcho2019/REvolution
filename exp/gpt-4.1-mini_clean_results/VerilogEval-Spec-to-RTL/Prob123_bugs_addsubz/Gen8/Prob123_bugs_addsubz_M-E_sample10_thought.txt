Instead of using conditional inversion of B and a carry-in, I will implement the adder-subtractor by using a multiplexed adder and subtractor. The subtractor will be implemented explicitly with an adder and two's complement logic (b negation with increment). The selection between adder and subtractor will be done by the multiplexer after computation.

Furthermore, to detect zero, I will use a simple equality comparison to zero instead of reduction NOR for variety.

This approach explicitly separates addition and subtraction computations, computes both in parallel, and selects the result and zero flag based on do_sub. Although potentially less area-efficient, this architecture is functionally distinct and maintains combinational logic only.
