To improve PPA while preserving correctness and synthesis friendliness, replace the explicit ripple-carry implementation with direct use of Verilog’s built-in addition and subtraction operators combined with a conditional operand for subtraction.

Key improvements:
- Use `{do_sub ? -b : b}` to let the synthesizer infer a more optimal adder-subtractor implementation (e.g., carry-lookahead or carry-save).
- Retain single combinational always block with blocking assignments for clarity.
- Compute zero flag via reduction NOR on the output to correctly detect zero.
- Avoid explicit loops and carry signals, which reduce area and power and improve timing due to better mapping to dedicated hardware.
- Maintain all output registers as reg and update in always @(*) block to keep original interface semantics.

This approach leverages synthesis tool capabilities for optimal adder-subtractor generation, leading to smaller area, lower power, and better timing, especially for small 8-bit widths.
