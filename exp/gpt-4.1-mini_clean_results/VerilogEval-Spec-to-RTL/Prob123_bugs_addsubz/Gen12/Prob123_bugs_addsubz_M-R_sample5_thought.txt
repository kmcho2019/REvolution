I will refactor the design by eliminating the separate adder and subtractor modules and instead implement a single combinational adder-subtractor directly inside the TopModule. The subtraction is done by conditionally inverting the second operand and adding the carry-in accordingly to form a two's complement subtraction. The output and zero flag will be assigned using continuous assignment statements (`assign`) rather than inside an always block. This approach simplifies the code, reduces module instantiations, and improves readability and synthesis efficiency while maintaining the correct functionality.
