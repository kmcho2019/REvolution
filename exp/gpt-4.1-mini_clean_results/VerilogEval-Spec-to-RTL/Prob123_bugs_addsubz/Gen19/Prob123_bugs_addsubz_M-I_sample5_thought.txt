To improve the PPA of the previous modular solution, I will implement a single unified 8-bit adder-subtractor module that uses the control input do_sub to decide whether to add or subtract by conditionally inverting b and adding the carry-in. This approach saves area and power by sharing logic rather than duplicating adders and subtractors. The zero flag will be computed combinationally by checking if the output is zero. The zero flag and output are registered to avoid glitches. This design reduces area and power by reusing the adder logic and reduces delay by removing muxing of two parallel results.
