I will implement the adder-subtractor using a single 8-bit adder with a conditional operand modification based on the subtraction control. Specifically, when do_sub is high, I will invert the bits of input b and add 1 (to form two's complement), enabling subtraction using the adder. I will use a carry-in signal as do_sub to add the +1 automatically. The result and zero flag will be assigned in a straightforward combinational always block. This approach saves logic by sharing the same adder for addition and subtraction, simplifies the design, and avoids duplicating adders or multiplexers, improving efficiency.
