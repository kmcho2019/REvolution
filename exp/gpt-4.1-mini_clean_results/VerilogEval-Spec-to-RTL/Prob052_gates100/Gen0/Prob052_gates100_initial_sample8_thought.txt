Implement a module TopModule with a 100-bit input vector 'in'. The outputs are:
- out_and: the AND of all bits in 'in', computed using a reduction AND operator (&).
- out_or: the OR of all bits in 'in', computed using a reduction OR operator (|).
- out_xor: the XOR of all bits in 'in', computed using a reduction XOR operator (^).
Use continuous assignments for combinational logic.
