Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR reductions using a hierarchical tree structure of pairwise reductions. This approach recursively reduces the input vector in stages, combining pairs of bits at each level until only one bit remains for each output. This tree-based reduction mimics how large fan-in gates are often implemented in hardware, potentially improving synthesis results by balancing the logic depth and fan-in, which may enhance timing and power characteristics over a flat reduction. The implementation uses internal wires for each reduction level and combines pairs of signals in parallel.
