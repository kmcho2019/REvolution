Fuse the clarity and performance advantages of Verilog's built-in reduction operators with the balanced hierarchical reduction tree approach to optimize timing and maintain clear, modular code.

Specifically:
- Use the balanced tree structure from Example 1 to minimize gate fan-in and depth, improving timing.
- For partial group reductions, use Verilog reduction operators (&, |, ^) to succinctly implement 5-bit reductions, leveraging synthesis optimization.
- Instead of an overly complex multi-level tree, reduce the levels by combining pairs iteratively until a small number of intermediate signals remain, then finalize outputs.
- Use wire outputs (combinational) instead of reg outputs and avoid procedural blocks to ensure clean combinational logic and enable synthesis optimizations.
- This fusion yields good timing due to balanced tree depth and low fan-in at each stage, while also reducing area and power due to synthesis-friendly constructs.

This design is concise, readable, efficiently synthesizable, and exhibits improved PPA by combining tree structuring with Verilog reduction operator efficiency.
