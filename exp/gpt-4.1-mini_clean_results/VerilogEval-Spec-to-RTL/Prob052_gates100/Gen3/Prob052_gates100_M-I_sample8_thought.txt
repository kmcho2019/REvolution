To improve the original hierarchical reduction for better PPA, I propose restructuring the reduction tree into a balanced binary tree with groups of 2 inputs at each stage rather than groups of 10. This approach significantly reduces gate fan-in, lowers logic depth, and improves timing by minimizing the critical path delay. It also helps power by reducing switching activity in large fan-in gates and potentially reduces area as smaller gates are generally more efficient.

Specifically, I will implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR as balanced trees with stages of 2-input gates. Since 100 is not a power of two, the extra inputs will be handled by propagating them unchanged at the last stages. This balanced reduction is better for synthesis tools to optimize timing and area.

This approach results in roughly log2(100) ≈ 7 stages for each output, improving delay and area over the original two-stage wide fan-in implementation.
