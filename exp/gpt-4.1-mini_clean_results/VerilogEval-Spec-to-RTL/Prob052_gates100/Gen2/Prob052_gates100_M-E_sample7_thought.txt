Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR outputs by hierarchically grouping the inputs into 10 groups of 10 bits each. First compute partial reductions (AND, OR, XOR) for each 10-bit group, then combine these intermediate results to get the final output. This breaks down the large reductions into smaller chunks, which could be beneficial for clarity and might help synthesis tools optimize the logic tree better or allow easier pipelining or logic sharing if extended. Although functionally equivalent to a flat reduction, this structure explicitly exposes the reduction stages, potentially improving timing and synthesis quality for very wide input vectors.
