To improve the given solution for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the explicit for-loop inside the always block with Verilog’s built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These operators synthesize efficiently to balanced gate trees and reduce the logic depth, thus improving timing and lowering area. Additionally, this reduces switching activity, which helps power consumption. Since the outputs depend purely on the input vector and require no internal state, continuous assignment statements are the cleanest and most optimal way to implement this combinational logic. Hence, I will change the outputs to wire and use continuous assignments with reduction operators, removing the always block and reg declarations altogether.
