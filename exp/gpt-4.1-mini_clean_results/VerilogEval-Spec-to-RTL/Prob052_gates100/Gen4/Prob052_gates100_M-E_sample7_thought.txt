Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR outputs by splitting the inputs into smaller groups, performing reduction operations on these groups in parallel, then combining the intermediate results. This hierarchical reduction approach manually creates balanced trees for the large fan-in gates, potentially improving timing by reducing logic depth.

Specifically:
- Partition the 100 inputs into 10 groups of 10 bits each.
- Compute a 10-bit AND, OR, XOR reduction for each group.
- Then, combine the 10 intermediate results with another layer of reduction to get final outputs.
- This approach keeps the design purely combinational but uses intermediate wires to explicitly describe the two-level reduction tree.
- This can help synthesis tools infer more balanced logic trees and improve timing, while also making the structure explicit for clarity.

This solution differs from the simple direct reduction approach and can be a starting point for performance tuning in large fan-in gates.
