Implement the reduction logic using hierarchical pairwise reduction modules to mimic a balanced binary tree structure for each of the AND, OR, and XOR operations. This approach breaks down the 100-bit inputs into pairs, reduces each pair's outputs, and iteratively reduces these intermediate results until a single output bit remains for each operation. This design is modular and scalable and emphasizes a structural, modular approach rather than a single procedural block or direct reduction operators. It clarifies the data flow and can facilitate gate-level synthesis with clear hierarchical design and potentially improved timing by emphasizing balanced logic depth.

No procedural blocks or loops are used inside the TopModule; instead, the module instantiates smaller reduction stages to do partial reductions, combining them step-by-step. This promotes clarity and explicit hierarchy.
