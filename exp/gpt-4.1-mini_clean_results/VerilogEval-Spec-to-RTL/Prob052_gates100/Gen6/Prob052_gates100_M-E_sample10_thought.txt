Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR outputs by hierarchically grouping inputs into smaller subgroups of 10 bits each. Compute intermediate results for each subgroup using reduction operators, then combine those intermediate results again with reduction operators. This two-level hierarchical reduction reduces the fan-in at each logic stage, potentially improving synthesis quality by enabling better balanced logic trees and easier optimizations. The hierarchical structure can also offer improved readability for large input widths and might map better to physical multi-input gate structures. All operations remain purely combinational with continuous assignments.
