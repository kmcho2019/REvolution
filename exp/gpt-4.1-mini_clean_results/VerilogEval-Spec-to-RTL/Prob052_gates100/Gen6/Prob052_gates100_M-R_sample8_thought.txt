Refactor the design to use continuous assignment statements with Verilog's built-in reduction operators (`&`, `|`, `^`) directly on the 100-bit input vector. This removes the procedural always block and loop, resulting in more concise and idiomatic Verilog code that is easier to read and maintain.

Using reduction operators enables synthesis tools to better optimize the combinational logic for AND, OR, and XOR reductions over many inputs, potentially improving performance, power, and area. This approach also ensures outputs remain purely combinational without requiring `reg` declarations or procedural blocks.
