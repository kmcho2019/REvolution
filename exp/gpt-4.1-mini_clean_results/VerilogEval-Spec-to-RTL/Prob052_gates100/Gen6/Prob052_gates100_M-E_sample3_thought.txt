Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR functions using a hierarchical tree structure of smaller logic blocks (e.g., 4-input gates) to perform the reduction in stages. This approach breaks the large reduction into multiple layers, balancing the logic and potentially improving synthesis optimization and timing by reducing the longest combinational path from 100 to about log4(100) ≈ 4 stages. This design uses generate blocks to instantiate multiple small modules to reduce the input vector step-by-step until a single output remains for each function.
