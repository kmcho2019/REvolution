To optimize the original solution for power, performance, and area, I will replace the explicit for-loop and always block with direct continuous assignments using Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These operators synthesize to balanced, optimized logic trees, improving timing and reducing area compared to sequential bitwise accumulation. This approach also reduces switching activity by minimizing intermediate signals, thus lowering power consumption. The outputs can be declared as wire since they are driven combinationally by these continuous assignments. This is the most concise and efficient implementation without changing functionality.
