Combine the clarity and synthesis efficiency of built-in Verilog reduction operators with the explicitness of continuous assignment for purely combinational logic. Declare outputs as wires and use continuous assignments with built-in reductions (&, |, ^) to enable synthesis tools to infer balanced reduction trees, minimizing area and power. This approach avoids manual looping which can be less efficient and makes the intent clear, improving readability and optimization potential. This fusion maintains the simplicity and correctness of the design while optimizing for PPA metrics.
