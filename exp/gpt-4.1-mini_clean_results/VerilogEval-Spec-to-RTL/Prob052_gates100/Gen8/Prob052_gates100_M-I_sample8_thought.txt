To optimize the existing design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the explicit for-loop-based accumulation in the always block with Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These operators are inherently synthesizable into balanced logic trees, which are generally faster, smaller, and more power efficient compared to manually implemented loops that could generate cascaded logic.

The outputs can be declared as simple wires assigned via continuous assignments since all logic is purely combinational. This simplification removes the need for registers and always blocks, reducing complexity and potential glitches.

This approach:
- Improves timing by creating balanced gate trees.
- Reduces area by leveraging synthesis-optimized reduction gates.
- Lowers power by minimizing switching activity and glitches.
- Maintains correctness and clarity with concise code.

Hence, the improved module uses simple continuous assignments with reduction operators for the 100-bit input vector.
