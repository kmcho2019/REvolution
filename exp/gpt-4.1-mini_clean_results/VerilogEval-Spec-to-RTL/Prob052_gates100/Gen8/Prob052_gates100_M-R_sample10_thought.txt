Refactor the module to use continuous assignments with Verilog built-in reduction operators for AND, OR, and XOR. This removes the always block and the explicit for loop, resulting in more concise and idiomatic Verilog. Using reduction operators directly allows synthesis tools to more easily recognize and optimize these large reductions as balanced trees of gates, potentially improving performance, power, and area.

This structure ensures combinational outputs are directly tied to input reductions without intermediate procedural variables, enhancing clarity and synthesis efficiency.
