Implement a hierarchical tree-structured reduction for the AND, OR, and XOR outputs by recursively combining pairs of inputs until one final result remains for each output. This approach structures the logic explicitly as a binary tree of two-input gates, which can help the synthesizer recognize and optimize balanced gate trees more easily. It also avoids procedural loops or built-in reduction operators, giving a clear hardware-like structural view of the reductions.

The design will use generate blocks and intermediate wires to build the tree levels for each logical operation. At each stage, pairs of signals are combined until the tree reduces to a single output bit. This method helps visualize the tree depth and can facilitate manual pipelining or timing optimization if extended. The final outputs are assigned from the last stage signals.

This approach results in a purely combinational and synthesizable design with clear modularity and may give synthesis tools better hints for optimization.
