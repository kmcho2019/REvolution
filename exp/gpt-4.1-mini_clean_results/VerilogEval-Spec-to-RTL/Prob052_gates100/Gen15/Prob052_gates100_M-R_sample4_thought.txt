Refactor the design by replacing the always block with continuous assignments using Verilog's built-in reduction operators. This approach directly uses `&in`, `|in`, and `^in` to produce the AND, OR, and XOR outputs, respectively. This refactoring maintains purely combinational behavior, simplifies the code by removing the procedural loop, and allows synthesis tools to more easily optimize the reduction operations for better performance, power, and area. The outputs are declared as wires to reflect the combinational nature of the assignments.
