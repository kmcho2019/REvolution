Implement a hierarchical reduction approach by splitting the 100-bit input into 10 groups of 10 bits each. For each group, compute the local AND, OR, and XOR reductions first, then combine these 10 intermediate results into the final outputs. This staged reduction allows more structured synthesis, potentially enabling better balanced logic trees, and making it easier for synthesis tools to optimize timing and area by handling smaller gates in stages. It also allows potential reuse of group results if needed for future enhancements. The implementation uses combinational always blocks with intermediate wires, keeping outputs purely combinational.
