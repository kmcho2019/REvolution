Optimize the design by replacing the explicit for-loop and always block with continuous assignments using Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). This approach enables the synthesis tool to infer balanced reduction trees for better timing, area, and power. Using continuous assignments also clearly indicates combinational logic, simplifying synthesis and improving readability. Since the inputs and outputs are one bit (except the 100-bit input vector), we can directly assign the reduction operations to the outputs.
