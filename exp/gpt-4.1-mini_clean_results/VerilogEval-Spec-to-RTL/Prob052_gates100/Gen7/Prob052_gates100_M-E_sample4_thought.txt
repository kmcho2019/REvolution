Implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR reductions using a balanced binary tree structure in a hierarchical manner. This approach breaks down the large reduction into smaller stages, each combining pairs of inputs, thereby reducing the logic depth to about log2(100) (~7). This should improve timing by shortening the critical path compared to a linear chain. The logic will be purely combinational, using wires, and will explicitly instantiate intermediate wires for each stage of reduction for clarity and potential synthesis optimization.
