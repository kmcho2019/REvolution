Implement a balanced binary tree reduction architecture for the three logic functions (AND, OR, XOR). Instead of grouping inputs into fixed-size blocks, recursively combine pairs of inputs at each stage until a single output remains for each operation. This minimizes logic depth by producing a logarithmic reduction tree (depth ~ log2(100)), improving timing performance compared to flat or single-stage grouping. The entire reduction is done using generate loops and intermediate wire arrays to hold partial results for each stage. This approach is fully structural and purely combinational, enabling easier mapping to efficient tree-style logic by synthesis tools.
