Implement a hierarchical tree-based reduction approach for the AND, OR, and XOR outputs. Instead of a flat loop or built-in reduction operators, the 100 inputs will be grouped into smaller fixed-size chunks (e.g., groups of 10 bits), each reduced in parallel to intermediate signals. Then these intermediate results are recursively combined in a multi-level tree structure until the final outputs are computed. This approach models how large reductions are commonly implemented in hardware, allowing better control over logic fan-in and potentially enabling pipelining or balanced timing paths in future extensions. It also explicitly demonstrates hierarchical decomposition of combinational logic without relying on synthesizer-inferred reduction operators.
