To optimize the original code for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the manual for-loop accumulation of AND, OR, and XOR with the Verilog built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These operators enable synthesis tools to implement the logic as optimized balanced trees, reducing logic depth, switching activity, and area. Additionally, I will declare the outputs as wires instead of reg since the outputs represent purely combinational logic, simplifying the synthesis and removing unnecessary inferred registers or latches. This approach maintains identical functionality while improving timing, power, and area metrics.
