To optimize the solution for power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the explicit for-loop in the always block with Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These operators synthesize to balanced logic trees, reducing gate levels and complexity, which lowers delay and area. This also reduces switching activity by avoiding unnecessary intermediate operations, improving power efficiency. 

Using continuous assignments with reduction operators removes the need for output registers and the always block, simplifying the design and making it cleaner and more synthesis-friendly. The functionality remains identical, but this style is preferred for multi-input reductions in combinational logic.
