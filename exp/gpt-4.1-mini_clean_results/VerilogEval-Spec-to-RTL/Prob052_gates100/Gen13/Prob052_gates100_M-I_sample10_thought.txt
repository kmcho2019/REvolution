This reduces the fan-in of each gate to 10 instead of 100, creating a more balanced and structured reduction tree.

Using generate blocks for chunk processing keeps the code clean and scalable. The final outputs remain purely combinational and are implemented with continuous assignments.

This explicit hierarchical reduction can improve synthesis quality by guiding it towards smaller balanced logic trees, reducing the maximum fan-in and thus improving timing and possibly lowering power and area.
