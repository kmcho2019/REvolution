Refactor the design to use continuous assignment with built-in Verilog reduction operators for the outputs. This approach leverages the concise syntax and allows the synthesis tool to implement efficient balanced logic trees for AND, OR, and XOR reductions. The outputs can be declared as wires rather than regs, eliminating the need for an always block. This structure is more succinct, has no explicit loop, and is ideal for combinational logic synthesis, improving timing, area, and power compared to iterative loop-based assignments.
