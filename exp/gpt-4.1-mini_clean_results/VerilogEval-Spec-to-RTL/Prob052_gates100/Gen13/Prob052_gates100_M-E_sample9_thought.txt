Implement the 100-input AND, OR, XOR by constructing balanced binary reduction trees for each operation. This approach hierarchically combines pairs of inputs step-by-step, halving the number of signals at each stage until only one result remains. This tree-based structure minimizes the logic depth (log2(100) ≈ 7 stages), improving performance compared to flat or grouped loops. The module will use internal wires to represent each stage's intermediate results for AND, OR, and XOR separately, then assign final outputs. The design is fully combinational with continuous assignments and no procedural blocks.
