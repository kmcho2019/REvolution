Refactor the design to use continuous assignment statements with built-in Verilog reduction operators (&, |, ^) instead of a procedural always block. This approach directly expresses the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR logic as simple reduction operations on the input vector. It simplifies the code, improves readability, and allows synthesis tools to optimize the combinational logic more effectively, potentially enhancing performance, reducing area, and lowering power consumption.

The outputs are declared as wire (implicit from continuous assignments) instead of reg, and no procedural blocks or loops are used. This clean structural coding style is standard for purely combinational logic in Verilog.
