To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the explicit for-loop in the always_comb block with Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^). These reduction operators synthesize into balanced trees of gates, improving timing and reducing logic area compared to a sequential bitwise operation loop. This change eliminates the need for intermediate registers and combinational loops, thereby reducing switching activity and power consumption.

The outputs can be declared as wire and assigned directly via continuous assignment for cleaner and more efficient code. This also simplifies the code and makes it more synthesizable for optimized logic.

Thus, the improved design:
- Uses continuous assignments with reduction operators.
- Declares outputs as wire instead of reg.
- Maintains purely combinational logic with zero latency.
- Is concise, clear, and optimized for synthesis.

This results in a more compact, faster, and power-efficient implementation without any change in functionality.
