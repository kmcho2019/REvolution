The original design introduced a buffer instance for signal 'b' driving outputs x and y, which adds area and power overhead without providing a functional or timing benefit for this simple wiring problem. Since the problem only requires direct wire connections, the optimal solution is to eliminate the buffer module entirely and directly assign the inputs to their corresponding outputs. This reduces area by removing an extra module, reduces power by minimizing toggling and switching on unnecessary buffers, and keeps the design fully combinational with minimal critical path delay. The simplicity also aids clarity and synthesis tool optimization.
