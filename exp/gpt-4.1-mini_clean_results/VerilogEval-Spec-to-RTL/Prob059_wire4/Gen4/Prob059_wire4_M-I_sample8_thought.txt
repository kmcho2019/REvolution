The original solution uses continuous assignments for simple wire connections, which is already optimal for performance, power, and area in a typical synthesis scenario. However, since the outputs are purely driven by input signals with no logic or state, we can further reduce code verbosity and potential synthesis overhead by declaring the outputs as wires and connecting them directly to inputs using port connections, avoiding the assign statements. This can help synthesis tools recognize direct wiring more straightforwardly, potentially improving area and power slightly due to simpler netlist structures. The improvement also enhances readability and clarity by leveraging implicit wire declarations and port connection semantics.
