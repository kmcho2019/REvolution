The original design is a direct wiring module with continuous assignments, which is already optimal for performance, power, and area given its simple function. No logical operations or registers are involved. Therefore, no improvement in PPA metrics is feasible at the RTL level. However, for code simplicity and potentially even smaller area due to direct port-to-port connections, the outputs can be declared as wires and assigned directly using port connections in the port list (Verilog-2001 style). This avoids the explicit assign statements, leading to a minimal and concise design with identical functionality and PPA.
