The original design is already minimal, consisting solely of continuous assignments that directly connect inputs to outputs, which is optimal for PPA. However, one minor refinement for area and power might be to explicitly declare outputs as wires and avoid redundant declarations, since outputs default to wire type in Verilog. Additionally, grouping related assignments inside a single assign statement using concatenation can potentially reduce synthesis complexity. This subtle change can marginally improve the synthesis tool's ability to optimize wiring, possibly reducing area and power slightly without affecting performance.
