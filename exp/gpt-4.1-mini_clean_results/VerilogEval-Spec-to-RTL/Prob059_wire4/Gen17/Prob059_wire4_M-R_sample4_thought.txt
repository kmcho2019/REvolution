Since the module implements simple direct connections with no combinational logic, the most straightforward and power/area efficient approach is to use direct continuous assignments for all outputs. This removes the need for an intermediate Buffer module and reduces the instance count and additional logic, thus saving area and power. The outputs 'x' and 'y' that share the same input 'b' are driven directly by two separate continuous assignments. This maintains clarity, simplicity, and minimal hardware while preserving the original functional behavior.
