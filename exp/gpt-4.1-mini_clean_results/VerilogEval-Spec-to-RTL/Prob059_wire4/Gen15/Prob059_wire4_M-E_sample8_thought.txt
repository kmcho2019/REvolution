Instead of using direct continuous assignments or buffer instantiations, I will implement TopModule using combinational always blocks with a single always @(*) block that assigns all outputs simultaneously. This approach models the outputs as registered in combinational logic style, enhancing readability and grouping related assignments logically. It also allows easy extension to more complex combinational logic if needed. Though functionally equivalent to direct wiring, this structural style shows clear intent of combinational behavior while keeping hardware minimal.
