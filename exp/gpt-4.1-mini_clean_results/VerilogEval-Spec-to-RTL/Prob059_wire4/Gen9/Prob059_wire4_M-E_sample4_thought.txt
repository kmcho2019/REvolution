A novel approach is to implement the TopModule using a single multiplexer-based unified output driver controlled by fixed select signals that statically route inputs to outputs. Instead of direct wire assignments or buffer instantiations, we use combinational multiplexers inside the module to forward inputs to outputs based on constant select lines.

This approach demonstrates a different structural style: the outputs are driven by multiplexers selecting among inputs, where the select lines are fixed parameters or localparams. Since the select lines are constant, synthesis tools will optimize the multiplexers into simple wire connections.

This design showcases how multiplexers can be used to route signals even when routing is fixed, enabling more flexible future extension where select signals might become dynamic.

Hence, this modular approach models wiring as constant multiplexing logic, combining clarity and future extensibility with slightly more structural complexity than direct assignments.
