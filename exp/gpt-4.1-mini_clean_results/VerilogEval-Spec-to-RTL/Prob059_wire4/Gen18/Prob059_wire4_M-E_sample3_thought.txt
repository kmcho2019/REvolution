Use a single 4-to-4 one-hot demultiplexer module that routes each input to its corresponding output wire by selectively enabling the output based on input selectors. The module will take three inputs concatenated into a 3-bit bus and output four bits corresponding to w, x, y, z with internal logic to route 'a' to 'w', 'b' to both 'x' and 'y', and 'c' to 'z'.
