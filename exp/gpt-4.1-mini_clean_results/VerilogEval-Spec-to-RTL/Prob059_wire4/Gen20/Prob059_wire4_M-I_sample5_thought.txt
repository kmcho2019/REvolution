The original solution uses instantiated Buffer modules for simple wire connections, which adds unnecessary structural overhead and slightly increases area and power usage without any timing benefit. Since the design is purely combinational wiring, the most efficient implementation is direct continuous assignments, which map inputs to outputs directly without module overhead. This reduces area and power consumption by avoiding additional logic and routing resources. Hence, I will optimize the design by removing the Buffer module and directly assigning outputs from inputs, preserving the specified connectivity exactly.
