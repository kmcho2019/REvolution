Instead of using straightforward continuous assignments, I will implement the wiring behavior by using combinational always blocks with intermediate reg signals. This approach stores the input signals into internal registers and then drives the outputs from these registers. Though functionally equivalent and less common for simple wiring, this style could be used in scenarios where signal buffering or registered outputs are desired. This design illustrates a different architectural pattern by employing an always_comb block and reg types to provide the same passthrough behavior.
