The original design is already optimal in terms of logic and structure since it simply connects inputs to outputs without any combinational logic. However, to potentially reduce power and area slightly, we can use a single assign statement with a concatenation for outputs driven by the same input (x and y). This will marginally reduce the amount of assignment statements and may allow the synthesis tool to optimize the netlist better. This minor code restructuring might lead to small PPA improvements by reducing driver count and logic nets.
