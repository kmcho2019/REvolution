Fuse the clarity and synthesis-friendliness of continuous assignments with the structural expressiveness of an always_comb block by defining outputs as wires and explicitly instantiating an intermediate combinational block. This can be achieved by using an interface wire assignment combined with a separate always_comb block that drives internal signals, which are then assigned to outputs. This makes the module both clean and explicitly combinational while keeping outputs as wires for minimal area and delay. The intermediate signals clarify the functional mapping without unnecessary reg outputs or inferred latches, combining the best of both approaches.
