To improve the original ripple-carry adder for better performance (timing), I will implement a 4-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA). This reduces the carry propagation delay by computing carry signals in parallel using generate and propagate signals, rather than waiting for carry ripple. The sum bits and final carry out are computed from these intermediate carry signals. This maintains correctness and minimizes critical path delay without increasing area significantly, thus optimizing the design for better timing and power.
