To optimize the 4-bit adder for better PPA, I will use a generate-for block to instantiate the full adders instead of explicitly instantiating four modules. This reduces code repetition and aids synthesis tools in optimization. Also, I will declare the carry wires as an array for clearer carry chaining. The architecture remains ripple-carry as it is area-efficient for 4 bits. This style can improve readability and possibly synthesis efficiency without changing the logic or timing characteristics.
