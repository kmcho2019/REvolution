To optimize the given 4-bit ripple carry adder for better PPA, I will replace the ripple carry architecture with a small carry-lookahead adder (CLA) module. The CLA reduces carry propagation delay by computing carries in parallel using generate and propagate signals, improving timing (WNS and TNS). This also tends to reduce dynamic power due to shorter critical path, although area may slightly increase. Given the very small bit-width (4 bits), the CLA area overhead is minimal and timing gains are significant.

I will implement a 4-bit CLA inside TopModule without separate FullAdder instances, directly calculating propagate and generate signals for each bit, then computing carry signals quickly. The final sum and overflow bit will be produced combinationally. This modular yet efficient design maintains clarity and improves PPA metrics significantly.
