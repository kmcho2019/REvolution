Implement a 4-bit adder using a hierarchical structure: first create a 2-bit adder module using two full adders, then instantiate two of these 2-bit adders inside the TopModule. Connect the carry-out of the lower 2-bit adder to the carry-in of the upper 2-bit adder. This modular, hierarchical design reduces the size of each building block, makes the design more scalable, and illustrates how smaller blocks can be composed to form larger adders. Finally, concatenate the 4-bit sum outputs and the final carry-out to form the 5-bit sum output.
