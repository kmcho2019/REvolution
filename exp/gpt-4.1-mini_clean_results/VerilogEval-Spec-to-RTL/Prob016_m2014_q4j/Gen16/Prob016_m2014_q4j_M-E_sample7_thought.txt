Instead of using carry-lookahead logic or ripple-carry full adders, I will implement the 4-bit adder using a hierarchical two-level approach by splitting the inputs into two 2-bit halves. Each half will be summed by a 2-bit ripple carry adder using full adders. The carry-out from the lower half is then used as the carry-in for the upper half.

This approach groups the addition into smaller blocks, simplifying the carry propagation. It trades off some speed compared to carry-lookahead but is easy to understand and modular. Also, the upper 2-bit adder can be designed independently, improving design reuse.

The final 5-bit sum includes the carry-out from the upper 2-bit adder as the overflow bit.

Summary:
- Implement a 2-bit ripple carry adder module using two full adders
- Instantiate two 2-bit adders: lower and upper halves of the 4-bit inputs
- Connect carry-out of lower half as carry-in of upper half
- Concatenate the sum and the final carry-out as 5-bit output
