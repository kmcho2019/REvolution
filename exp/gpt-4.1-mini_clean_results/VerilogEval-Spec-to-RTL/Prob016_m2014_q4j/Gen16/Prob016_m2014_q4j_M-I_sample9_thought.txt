To improve the PPA of the 4-bit ripple carry adder, I will replace the ripple carry chain with a carry look-ahead logic for faster carry computation and reduced propagation delay, thereby improving performance (lower TNS and WNS). This approach computes all carry signals in parallel, significantly speeding up the addition.

For power and area, while carry look-ahead is slightly larger than ripple carry, it is still small at 4 bits and the speed gain is worth it. The design remains fully combinational and simple.

I will maintain modularity by embedding the carry look-ahead logic inside the TopModule and remove the full adder instantiations, directly computing sum bits and carry signals in parallel using generate-propagate-kill signals. The final sum[4] bit will be the carry-out from the most significant bit.

This approach optimizes the critical path and improves timing without much area or power penalty for such a small adder.
