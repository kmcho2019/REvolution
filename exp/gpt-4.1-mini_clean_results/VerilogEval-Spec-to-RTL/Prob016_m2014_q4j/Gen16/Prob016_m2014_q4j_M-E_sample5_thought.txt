Implement the 4-bit adder by building a single 5-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) to generate the sum and carry-out efficiently. This eliminates the ripple carry delay by computing propagate and generate signals for each bit and using a hierarchical carry calculation to quickly resolve all carries in parallel. The sum output includes the final carry-out as the most significant bit. This approach improves performance over ripple-carry by reducing carry propagation delay while keeping the design fully combinational and modular.
