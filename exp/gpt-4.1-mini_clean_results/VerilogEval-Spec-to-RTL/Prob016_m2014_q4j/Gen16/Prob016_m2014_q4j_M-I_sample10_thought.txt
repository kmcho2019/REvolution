To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) of the given ripple carry 4-bit adder, we can replace the ripple carry architecture with a carry lookahead adder (CLA) approach. This reduces the carry propagation delay significantly by computing carry signals in parallel rather than sequentially. It improves performance (critical path delay) at a slight area cost, which is acceptable for a 4-bit adder.

The CLA will generate propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit, then compute carries with these signals combinationally, and finally compute the sum bits. This will reduce the worst-case carry chain delay from O(n) to O(log n), significantly speeding up the addition.

Additionally, using this approach simplifies the design by not requiring multiple full adder instantiations or generate loops, reducing code complexity.

The 5-bit sum output will have the least significant 4 bits from the computed sums and the MSB from the final carry out.

This approach optimizes performance and power due to shorter carry logic delay and less sequential toggling of carry signals, with only a minor area increase acceptable for small bit-width adders.
