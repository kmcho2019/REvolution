Implement a 4-bit adder using a single behavioral always_comb block with an intermediate 5-bit wide sum register to perform the addition in one step, leveraging Verilog's built-in addition operator for clarity and efficiency.

This approach:
- Treats x and y as 4-bit unsigned numbers.
- Adds them together directly, producing a 5-bit result that includes the overflow bit.
- Avoids instantiating multiple full adders or using generate loops.
- Is concise, easy to understand, and suitable for synthesis.
- Outputs the sum on the 'sum' output port.

This method leverages high-level synthesis optimizations and is ideal for small-width addition where performance bottleneck of ripple carry chain is minimal.
