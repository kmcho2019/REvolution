Implement the 4-bit adder by explicitly writing out the carry logic using propagate and generate signals (P and G) for each bit. Use the carry-lookahead method to compute carry bits in parallel rather than ripple-carry to reduce carry propagation delay. This approach improves speed by calculating all carry signals with minimal gate delay. Sum bits are computed as XOR of the inputs and their carry-ins. The final carry-out is the overflow bit. The design remains modular and clear but uses a different architecture emphasizing carry lookahead logic instead of chained full adders.
