To optimize the 4-bit ripple-carry adder for better PPA, I will simplify the FullAdder module by directly expressing the sum and carry-out logic with bitwise operators instead of using the addition operator `{cout, sum} = a + b + cin;`. This can reduce combinational logic complexity slightly and may improve synthesis results for area and power.

Additionally, since the design is small and purely combinational, I will inline the full adder logic inside the generate loop instead of instantiating separate FullAdder modules. This removes module instantiation overhead, potentially reducing area and improving power by enabling more efficient logic sharing by synthesis tools.

This approach:
- Removes a separate FullAdder module.
- Implements full adder logic with explicit XOR and AND/OR gates.
- Keeps ripple-carry chaining for clarity.
- Maintains combinational structure and includes the overflow bit.

Overall, this maintains functionality while simplifying logic and module hierarchy to improve area and power without increasing delay.
