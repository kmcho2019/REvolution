Instead of chaining individual full adders with ripple carry, implement the 4-bit adder using a carry lookahead logic approach to compute carry signals in parallel. This reduces the carry propagation delay by calculating carry generate and propagate signals for each bit and then using these to produce carries quickly. The sum bits are then computed by XORing inputs and the respective carry-in bits. This approach offers better performance for small bit-width adders without extra module instantiations and results in a fully combinational, efficient design.
