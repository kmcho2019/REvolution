To simplify the design, I will implement a straightforward 4-bit ripple-carry adder using a chain of full adders. This approach is simpler and more intuitive than carry-lookahead logic. Each full adder adds corresponding bits of x and y along with carry-in from the previous stage. The final carry-out becomes the overflow bit.

This design is easy to understand, small in code size, and fully functional for a 4-bit adder with overflow output. Although ripple carry is slower than carry-lookahead, for a 4-bit width the delay is minimal and the design complexity is much reduced.

Summary:
- Instantiate four full adders in series.
- Propagate carry from LSB to MSB.
- Concatenate the four sum bits with the final carry-out to form the 5-bit sum.
