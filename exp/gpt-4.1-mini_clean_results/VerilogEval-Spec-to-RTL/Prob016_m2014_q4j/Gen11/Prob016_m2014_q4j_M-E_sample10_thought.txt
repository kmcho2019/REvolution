Implement the 4-bit adder using a purely behavioral approach by leveraging Verilog's built-in addition operator on the 4-bit inputs plus an explicit zero initial carry-in, producing a 5-bit sum directly.

This approach:
- Eschews explicit full adder instantiations and carry chaining.
- Uses Verilog's built-in arithmetic operator, which synthesis tools efficiently map to optimized hardware.
- Results in concise and readable code with minimal structural overhead.
- The 5-bit sum naturally includes the overflow bit as the MSB without extra wiring.

This design offers simplicity and leverages the synthesizer's capability to infer an optimal adder circuit directly from the high-level code.
