To optimize the existing 4-bit ripple carry adder for better PPA without increasing complexity, we can merge the four full adders into a single structural module using simple combinational logic. This approach replaces explicit full adder instantiations and the generate loop with direct bitwise addition using the Verilog '+' operator, which typically gets synthesized into an optimized adder circuit by synthesis tools. This reduces area slightly by removing intermediate carry wires and module overhead. The performance remains the same for 4-bit addition, and power is likely improved due to fewer nets and simpler routing.
