Refactor the 4-bit adder to use a single continuous assignment that performs the addition of two 4-bit vectors plus an initial carry-in of zero. This approach removes the need for explicit FullAdder module instantiations and carry chain wiring, while still producing a 5-bit sum that includes the overflow bit. The core functionality remains the same but the design becomes more concise and purely combinational using Verilog's built-in addition operator.
