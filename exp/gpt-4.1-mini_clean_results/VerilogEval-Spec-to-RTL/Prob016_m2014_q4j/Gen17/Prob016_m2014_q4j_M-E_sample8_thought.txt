Implement a 4-bit adder using a hierarchical two-level approach: first add the lower two bits and upper two bits separately with 2-bit ripple-carry adders, then add their carries with an additional 1-bit full adder. This reduces the maximum carry propagation length from 4 to 3 stages, providing a slight performance improvement over a flat 4-bit ripple-carry adder. The final 5-bit sum concatenates the combined results and the overflow bit. This modular hierarchical adder design remains simple yet explores architectural variation for improved timing.
