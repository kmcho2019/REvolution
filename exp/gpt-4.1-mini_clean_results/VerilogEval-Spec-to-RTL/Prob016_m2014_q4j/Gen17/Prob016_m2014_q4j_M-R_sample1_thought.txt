Refactor the TopModule to remove the generate loop and explicitly instantiate each FullAdder separately for clarity. Instead of relying on carry-lookahead computed carries alone, implement a ripple carry chain of full adders, where each FullAdder's carry-out feeds the next FullAdder's carry-in. This structure preserves the modular full adder approach but clarifies the carry propagation path. The 5-bit sum will have the 4 sum bits from the full adders plus the final carry-out as overflow. This approach trades off some speed compared to carry-lookahead but simplifies the carry path and code structure.
