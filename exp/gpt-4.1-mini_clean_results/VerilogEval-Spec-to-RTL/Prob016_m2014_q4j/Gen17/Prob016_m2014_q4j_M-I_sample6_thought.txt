To improve performance and power while maintaining area, implement the 4-bit adder using a carry lookahead structure. This reduces the carry propagation delay from linear to logarithmic scale, improving timing (WNS and TNS). The carry lookahead logic also reduces unnecessary toggling of carry signals, lowering dynamic power. The area increase is minimal due to the small adder size. We still use the FullAdder only for summation bits, but the carries are computed by carry lookahead logic, which also helps reduce critical path delay significantly.
