Implement the 4-bit adder purely structurally by cascading four FullAdder modules in a ripple-carry configuration without carry-lookahead logic. This classical approach chains each adder's carry-out to the next adder's carry-in, which is straightforward and easy to verify. The initial carry-in is zero. The sum output will be constructed by concatenating the four sum bits from each FullAdder and the final carry-out as the 5th bit. This solution prioritizes simplicity and clarity over speed but remains fully modular and testable.
