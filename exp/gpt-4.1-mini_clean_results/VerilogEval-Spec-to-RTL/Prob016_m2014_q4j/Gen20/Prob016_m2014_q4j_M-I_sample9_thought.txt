The original design correctly implements a 4-bit adder with full adders and carry-lookahead logic, but it has some redundant components (fa_cout wires) and separate full adder modules that duplicate carry logic, leading to slightly increased area and power.

To optimize for better PPA while maintaining correctness and clarity, I will:
- Remove the full adder module instantiations since carry signals are already computed using carry-lookahead logic.
- Compute sum bits directly using the propagate signals and the computed carry-in bits, eliminating the need for explicit full adder instances.
- This removes redundant logic and power from the instantiated modules.
- The carry-lookahead logic remains to compute carries efficiently.
- The result is a smaller, simpler design with less power and area, while still fast due to carry-lookahead.
- This approach retains a clean modular structure and is easier to scale or modify.

This optimization should reduce area and power, and keep or improve timing since sum bits and carries are computed combinationally in parallel.
