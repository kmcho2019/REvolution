Refactor the 4-bit carry-lookahead adder by eliminating the full adder module and directly computing the carry and sum bits using continuous assignments with intermediate wires. This avoids defining separate modules and makes the design more compact and readable. The carry signals are computed directly from generate and propagate signals via the standard carry-lookahead logic formulas, explicitly expressing each carry as a function of generate and propagate signals including the previous carries. The sum bits are then assigned as XOR of propagate bits and corresponding carry-in bits. This maintains the same fast carry-lookahead principle while streamlining the code structure and improving clarity.
