To optimize for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), replace the ripple carry adder with a small carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. For 4 bits, a CLA reduces carry propagation delay by generating carry signals in parallel using propagate and generate signals, improving performance without increasing area significantly. This also reduces dynamic power as the critical path delay is shortened, allowing potential higher clock frequency and less glitching on intermediate carries. The CLA logic uses simple combinational gates and maintains clear modularity. The sum still includes the overflow carry-out as the MSB.
