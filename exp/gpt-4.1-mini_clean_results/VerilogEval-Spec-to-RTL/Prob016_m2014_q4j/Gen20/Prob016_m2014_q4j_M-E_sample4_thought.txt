Implement the 4-bit adder using a single behavioral always block that performs addition directly on the two 4-bit inputs extended to 5 bits to capture the carry-out. This approach avoids instantiating multiple full adders and intermediate carry wires by using Verilog's built-in addition operation. The sum output will directly reflect the 5-bit addition result. This provides a simple and clean RTL description suitable for synthesis, leveraging the synthesis tool's ability to map the addition efficiently.
