To optimize the existing 4-bit ripple carry adder for better performance (timing) and area while maintaining clarity, I will replace the chain of individual full adder instances with a simple combinational addition using the '+' operator. This leverages the synthesis tool's built-in optimizations (such as carry look-ahead or carry select implementations) to generate an efficient adder circuit, which can reduce critical path delay and potentially minimize area as well. This reduces the manual carry handling overhead and keeps the code concise and maintainable. The single-line '+' operator also simplifies the RTL without losing the explicit overflow bit, which is just the 5th bit of the sum.
