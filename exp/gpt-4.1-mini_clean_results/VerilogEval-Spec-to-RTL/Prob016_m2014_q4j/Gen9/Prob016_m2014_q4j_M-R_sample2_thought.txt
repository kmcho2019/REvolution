Refactor the design by eliminating the explicit FullAdder module and instead implement the full adders combinationally inside the TopModule using continuous assign statements and internal wires. Use an internal carry vector of size 4 to hold carry outs between bits, initialized to zero for the first carry-in. Calculate each sum bit and carry-out directly with bitwise and logical expressions for sum and carry. The overflow bit is assigned from the final carry-out. This approach keeps the ripple carry structure but simplifies the code by flattening the hierarchy, making it compact and easy to read without module instantiation or generate blocks.
