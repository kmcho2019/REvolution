Refactor the 4-bit ripple carry adder by eliminating the explicit FullAdder module and instead using direct assign statements for sum and carry bits using the same logic expressions. Define the internal carry signals as wires. This approach flattens the hierarchy, reduces the number of module instantiations, and keeps the logic purely combinational and easy to analyze. The carry chain is still maintained explicitly as a wire array with carry[0] initialized to zero. The final carry-out is appended as the MSB of the 5-bit sum output. This refactoring provides a more compact single-module design while preserving clarity and modular logic.
