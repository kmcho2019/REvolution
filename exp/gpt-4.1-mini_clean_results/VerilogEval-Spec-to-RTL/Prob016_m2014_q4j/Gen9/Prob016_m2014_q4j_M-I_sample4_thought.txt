The original design uses four separate FullAdder modules connected in a ripple carry chain. While this is clean and modular, it introduces multiple instantiations and extra wires. Given the small 4-bit width, we can simplify the design by implementing the 4-bit adder directly using a single 5-bit addition operation (`{carry_out, sum_bits} = x + y`). This behavioral style lets the synthesis tool optimize carry chains and gate-level implementation more efficiently, potentially reducing area and power slightly. It also removes the overhead of multiple modules and wire routing. The output sum remains 5 bits wide to include the overflow bit.

This approach keeps the design fully combinational and simple, and should achieve similar or better PPA without explicit full adder instantiations.
