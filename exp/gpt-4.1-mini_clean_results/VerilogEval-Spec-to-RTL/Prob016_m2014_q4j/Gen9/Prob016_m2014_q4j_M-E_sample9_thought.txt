Implement the 4-bit adder using a behavioral approach with continuous assignments to compute sum and carry bits without instantiating full adders explicitly. Use a chain of wires to represent carry bits calculated via bitwise addition formulas. This approach models the carry propagation using combinational logic equations derived from the full adder truth table but fully unrolled for clarity and efficiency. The final carry-out is appended as the MSB of the 5-bit sum. This method provides a succinct, purely combinational, gate-level inspired arithmetic without explicit module instantiation.
