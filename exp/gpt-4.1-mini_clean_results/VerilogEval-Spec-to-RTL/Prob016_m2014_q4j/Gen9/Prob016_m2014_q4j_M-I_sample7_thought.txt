To improve performance and power while maintaining clarity, I will implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for 4-bit inputs. A CLA reduces the carry propagation delay significantly compared to a ripple-carry adder by computing carry signals in parallel using generate and propagate signals. This reduces critical path delay and switching activity on intermediate carry wires, improving timing and dynamic power consumption. The CLA logic adds minor area overhead but is justified for better PPA, especially with a 5-bit sum output including the carry-out as the overflow bit. The design will remain modular and clean without excessive gate-level complexity.
