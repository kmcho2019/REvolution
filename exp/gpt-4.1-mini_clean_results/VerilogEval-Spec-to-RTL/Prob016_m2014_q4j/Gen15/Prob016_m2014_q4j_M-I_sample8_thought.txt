To improve the PPA of the existing 4-bit carry-lookahead adder, I will implement a minimal hierarchical carry-lookahead logic with parallel generation of carries to further reduce carry delay and logic depth. Instead of computing carries linearly (c1 depends on c0, c2 on c1, etc.), I will explicitly compute the carries in parallel using the standard carry-lookahead carry equations:

- c1 = g0 + p0*c0
- c2 = g1 + p1*g0 + p1*p0*c0
- c3 = g2 + p2*g1 + p2*p1*g0 + p2*p1*p0*c0
- c4 = g3 + p3*g2 + p3*p2*g1 + p3*p2*p1*g0 + p3*p2*p1*p0*c0

Since c0=0, terms involving c0 vanish. This full parallel carry lookahead reduces carry propagation chain from 4 to 1 level of logic, improving timing.

I will also remove the FullAdder module since it is not used in the original improved design, simplifying the code.

This will slightly increase area and power due to more gates in carry logic but significantly reduce delay, beneficial for high-speed designs.

- Use full carry-lookahead expressions for c1 to c4 in parallel.
- Sum bits as before: sum[i] = p[i] XOR c[i]
