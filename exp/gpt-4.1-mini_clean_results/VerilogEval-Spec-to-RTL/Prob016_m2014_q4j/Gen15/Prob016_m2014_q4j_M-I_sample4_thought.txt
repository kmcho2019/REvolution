To optimize for better performance and power-delay product, replace the ripple carry chain with a carry lookahead logic to speed up carry computation and reduce critical path delay. This decreases the carry propagation time significantly compared to the ripple carry approach, enhancing timing (lower WNS and higher max frequency).

Also, by calculating carries in parallel through generate signals, we reduce the switching activity and short-circuit currents associated with ripple carry chains, potentially reducing power.

The FullAdder module can be replaced with pure combinational logic inline to avoid overhead of multiple instances, slightly reducing area and simplifying routing.

The overall area impact is minimal but the timing and power improvements justify the carry lookahead approach in a 4-bit adder.

The sum output is 5-bit including the overflow bit, as before.
