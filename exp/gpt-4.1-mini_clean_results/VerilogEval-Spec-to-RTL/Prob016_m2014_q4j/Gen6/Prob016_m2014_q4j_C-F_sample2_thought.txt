Combine the modular clarity and scalability of the generate loop approach (Example 1) with the straightforward simplicity of the explicitly declared full adders (Example 2) to produce a clean, scalable, and easily maintainable 4-bit ripple carry adder.

Specifically:
- Use a generate block with a carry wire array to avoid repetitive code and improve scalability.
- Keep the FullAdder module simple and combinational.
