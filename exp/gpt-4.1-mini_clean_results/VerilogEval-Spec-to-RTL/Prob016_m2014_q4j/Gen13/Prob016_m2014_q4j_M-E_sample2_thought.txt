Implement the 4-bit adder using a single behavioral always block with the '+' operator, which infers a built-in adder in synthesis. This approach eliminates explicit full adder modules and carry chaining, leading to simpler and more concise code. The overflow bit naturally emerges as the 5th bit of the sum result. This design uses RTL-level abstraction for clarity and potential synthesis optimization, suitable for small widths like 4 bits.
