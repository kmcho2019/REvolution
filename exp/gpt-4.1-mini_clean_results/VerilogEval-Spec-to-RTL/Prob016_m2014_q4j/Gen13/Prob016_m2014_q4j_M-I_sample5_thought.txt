For a small 4-bit adder, structural full adder instantiations add overhead in area and power due to gate-level granularity and extra wiring. To optimize area and power, and likely improve timing, I will replace the structural full adder implementation with a simple behavioral addition using Verilog's built-in '+' operator. This lets the synthesis tool generate an efficient adder optimized for the target technology.

This reduces code complexity, wiring, and instantiations, while improving potential performance by leveraging synthesis optimizations such as carry lookahead or fast adders, which are usually better than manual ripple carry.

The output sum width will be 5 bits to accommodate overflow. This approach also lowers switching activity since only the necessary bits toggle.

Hence, the improvement strategy is: replace explicit full adder structure with a simple '+' operator and remove the generate loop and full adder module entirely for a cleaner, smaller, and potentially faster design.
