Implement the 4-bit adder using a parallel prefix (Kogge-Stone) structure to quickly compute carry signals in logarithmic time, enabling faster addition compared to ripple carry. First, generate propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit. Then, perform prefix operations to compute carry signals for all bit positions. Finally, sum bits are computed by XORing propagate signals with their corresponding carry-in. The overflow bit is taken from the final carry-out. This architecture minimizes carry propagation delay at the expense of more wiring and logic, demonstrating a different and high-performance approach than ripple carry.
