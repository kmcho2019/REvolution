To create a superior 4-bit adder with overflow output, I will fuse the modular full adder design from Example 1 with the carry-lookahead approach from Example 2. This provides a clear, modular structure for readability and reuse (FullAdder modules), while the carry-lookahead logic improves performance by minimizing carry propagation delay.

Specifically:
- Precompute generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for each bit.
- Use carry-lookahead logic to calculate all carry bits in parallel, reducing critical path delay.
- Instantiate four full adders, feeding them the inputs along with the precomputed carry-in signals from the carry-lookahead logic.
- The sum bits are taken directly from the full adders, ensuring clean modular logic and good synthesis results.
- Output the carry-out (overflow) as the MSB of the sum.

This fusion balances modularity, clarity, performance, and potentially power and area efficiency:
- Carry-lookahead reduces critical path.
- FullAdder modules ensure clear, reusable structure.
- All assignments remain combinational, suitable for synthesis.

This approach leverages the best of both examples:
- Performance from carry-lookahead logic.
- Readability and modularity from structural full adders.
- Clean separation of concerns (carry logic vs. sum computation).

This design is suitable for easy extension or replacement of FullAdder with optimized cells, and should synthesize efficiently with improved PPA metrics.
