Implement a 4-bit adder using a single always_comb block with structural-style bitwise operations and explicit carry propagation. Instead of instantiating separate FullAdder modules, we directly model the ripple carry in a compact behavioral block using bitwise XOR and AND operations to generate sum bits and carry bits stepwise. This reduces module overhead, improves synthesis friendliness by avoiding multiple instances, and keeps the design simple and readable. The overflow (final carry-out) is assigned as the MSB of the 5-bit sum output.
