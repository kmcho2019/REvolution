To improve the PPA while keeping the same functionality, I will replace the ripple-carry structure with a carry-lookahead logic for faster addition. Since the adder is only 4 bits wide, a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) is a good trade-off to reduce carry propagation delay and improve timing without significant area increase. The carry-lookahead logic computes the carry signals in parallel using generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for each bit.

This reduces the critical path delay compared to the ripple carry approach, improving performance. Power may remain comparable, but the faster timing is a significant improvement. The area may slightly increase due to more complex carry logic, but for 4 bits it remains minimal. The design remains clean and fully combinational.

We implement a single TopModule without separate FullAdder modules to reduce module overhead and clearly integrate the carry-lookahead logic. The sum and final carry-out are computed accordingly.
