Refactor the 4-bit ripple carry adder by implementing the full adder logic as combinational assign statements directly within the TopModule instead of instantiating separate FullAdder modules. This flattens the design, reduces hierarchy, and makes the carry chain and sum logic explicit in one module using wires and assign statements. The carry-in is propagated step-by-step from the least significant bit to the most significant bit, and the 5-bit sum includes the overflow bit as the final carry-out. This approach retains the ripple carry functionality but improves code conciseness and readability without separate module instantiation.
