To improve the power, performance, and area (PPA) of the 4-bit adder, the ripple-carry chain can be replaced with a carry-lookahead structure to reduce the carry propagation delay, thus improving performance. A carry-lookahead adder uses generate and propagate signals to compute carry bits in parallel, significantly speeding up the addition.

This approach can also reduce power slightly by limiting the carry propagation logic depth and removing the need for multiple full adder instances and their associated interconnects. The area might increase slightly due to the extra generate/propagate logic, but for 4 bits it will be minimal, and the speed gain is generally worth it.

The implementation will:
- Compute propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit.
- Use these signals to compute the carry bits in parallel.
- Compute sum bits using p and carry bits.
- The 5th bit of sum is the final carry out.

This is a common improvement over ripple-carry adders for small bit-widths.
