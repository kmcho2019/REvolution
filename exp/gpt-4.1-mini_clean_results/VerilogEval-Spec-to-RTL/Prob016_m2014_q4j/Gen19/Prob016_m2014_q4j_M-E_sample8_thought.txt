Implement the 4-bit adder by explicitly computing all possible carry bits using carry-lookahead logic to speed up the addition. This approach uses generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for each bit to calculate carries without waiting for ripple propagation. The sum bits are computed with XOR of inputs and carries, and the final carry-out is the overflow bit. This design improves performance by reducing carry propagation delay compared to ripple carry adders.
