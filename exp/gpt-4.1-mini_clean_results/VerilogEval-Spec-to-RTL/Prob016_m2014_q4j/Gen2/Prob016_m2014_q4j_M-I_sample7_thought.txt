To improve the design for better performance and power without increasing area, replace the ripple-carry adder with a carry-lookahead adder. A carry-lookahead adder computes carry signals in parallel using generate and propagate signals, significantly reducing the carry propagation delay, which improves maximum operating frequency (performance). Also, fewer carry signal toggles in a structured way can reduce dynamic power. Area will slightly increase due to additional logic but remains small for 4-bit width. This is a good tradeoff for better PPA overall.
