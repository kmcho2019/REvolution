To optimize the 4-bit adder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we can replace the ripple-carry structure with a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) architecture. The CLA reduces carry propagation delay by computing carry signals in parallel using generate and propagate signals, thus improving performance significantly while keeping area and power overhead small for 4 bits. This also keeps the design modular and scalable.

The CLA logic will:
- Calculate generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for each bit.
- Compute carry signals (c1 to c4) using these in parallel.
- Use carry-in=0 for the least significant bit.
- Compute sum bits by XORing input bits and their carry-in.
- The final carry-out forms the MSB of the 5-bit sum.

This approach improves timing by removing carry chain delay, slightly increases area/power due to extra logic but overall yields better PPA for small bit-width adders.
