Instead of implementing a ripple carry adder with full adders, I will design a carry lookahead adder (CLA) to improve performance by calculating carry bits in parallel, reducing the carry propagation delay. 

The CLA adder generates propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit and then computes the carry signals using these, avoiding serial carry dependencies. This approach results in faster addition suitable even for a small 4-bit adder.

The sum outputs are generated by XOR'ing the input bits and their respective carry-in bits. The final carry-out is included as the MSB of the 5-bit sum output.

This method reduces delay compared to ripple carry, though it uses slightly more logic for carry calculation. It demonstrates an alternative architecture to the original full adder chain.
