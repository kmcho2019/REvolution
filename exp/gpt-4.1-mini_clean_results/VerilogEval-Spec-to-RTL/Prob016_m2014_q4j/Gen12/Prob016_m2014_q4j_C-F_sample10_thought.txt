To create a superior 4-bit adder with overflow, I will fuse the modular clarity and minimal gate-level full adder logic from Example 1 with the improved timing of carry-lookahead logic from Example 2.

Specifically:
- Implement a compact FullAdder module using minimal XOR and AND/OR gates for sum and carry out.
- In TopModule, instantiate four FullAdders for modularity and clarity.
- Compute all carry inputs for each full adder using the carry-lookahead approach to break the ripple chain and reduce carry propagation delay.
- Pass precomputed carries as carry-ins to each full adder instance.
- Connect outputs sum bits from each FullAdder, and assign the final carry out as the overflow bit sum[4].

This fusion leverages:
- The clean, easy-to-understand modular full adder blocks.
- The fast carry-lookahead to improve timing and performance by parallel carry computation.
- Avoids writing sum bits purely by logic expressions, maintaining full adder modularity for potential future scaling or substitution.
- The area/power will remain reasonable since the full adders are simple, and carry-lookahead logic is minimal for 4 bits.

This design balances modularity, speed, clarity, and minimal logic complexity, yielding a better PPA profile than either example alone.
