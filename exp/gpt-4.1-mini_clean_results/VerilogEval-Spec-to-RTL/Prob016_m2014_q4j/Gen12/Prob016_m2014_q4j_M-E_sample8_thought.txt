Implement a 4-bit adder using a parallel prefix adder approach, specifically a simple carry look-ahead logic without modular full adder instantiation. The idea is to compute generate (g) and propagate (p) signals for each bit, then calculate carry signals in parallel using these signals. This approach reduces carry propagation delay compared to ripple-carry. The final sum bits are computed by XOR-ing input bits and carry-in signals. The carry-out from the MSB is concatenated as the fifth bit of the sum, providing overflow information. This solution is fully combinational and does not rely on hierarchical module instantiation, yielding a flat and fast design.
