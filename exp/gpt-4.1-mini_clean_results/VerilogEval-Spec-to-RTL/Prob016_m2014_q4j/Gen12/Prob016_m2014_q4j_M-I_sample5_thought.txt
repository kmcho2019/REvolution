To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) of the 4-bit adder, I will replace the ripple-carry structure with a simple carry-lookahead logic, reducing the critical path delay significantly. Instead of chaining full adders with carry-out feeding into the next full adder's carry-in, I will compute the carries directly using generate and propagate signals. This removes the sequential carry propagation and improves timing. 

Power is likely improved due to reduced glitches and toggling in the carry chain. Area may slightly increase due to carry logic, but for a 4-bit adder, the carry-lookahead logic is compact and efficient.

The sum bits are still calculated via XOR of inputs and carry-in. Carry bits are computed in parallel using the generate (g) and propagate (p) signals.

I will implement a single TopModule without instantiating full adders for minimal overhead and clear logic.

This approach balances improved performance and power with minimal area impact and straightforward code.
