To optimize the 4-bit ripple carry adder for better timing (reduce carry propagation delay), I will implement a carry look-ahead adder (CLA) for 4 bits. CLA reduces the carry propagation to logarithmic depth by generating carry propagate and generate signals, computing carries in parallel, and thus improving effective clock period compared to a ripple carry adder. This improves timing without significant area or power increase for just 4 bits. The module will compute sum bits and an overflow carry-out bit accordingly.
