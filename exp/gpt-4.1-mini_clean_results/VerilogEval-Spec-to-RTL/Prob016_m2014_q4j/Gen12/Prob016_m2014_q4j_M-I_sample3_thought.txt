To improve the PPA of the 4-bit ripple carry adder, I will implement the full adders using a generate loop to reduce repetitive code and enable easy scalability. Also, I will optimize the carry logic by leveraging a carry-lookahead approach for 4 bits to reduce propagation delay, thus improving timing (lowering the critical path). This slightly increases area and power due to extra logic but is beneficial for performance.

The carry-lookahead logic computes carry signals in parallel using propagate and generate signals, which accelerates carry determination.

The sum output remains the same as before, but carry signals are generated more efficiently.

This design balances performance improvements while keeping area and power reasonable for a 4-bit adder.
