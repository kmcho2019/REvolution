Refactor the 4-bit ripple carry adder by implementing the full adders as continuous assign statements using simple boolean expressions directly inside the top module, removing the separate FullAdder module. This flattens the hierarchy, reduces module instantiation overhead, and uses straightforward wire assignments for sum and carry generation. The carries are chained explicitly with intermediate wires, and the final carry-out is assigned to sum[4] as the overflow bit. This approach keeps the design fully combinational and structurally clear while achieving the same functionality without generate or module instantiation.
