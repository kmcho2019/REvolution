Use a simple ripple-carry adder built from four FullAdder modules connected sequentially, where each adder's carry-out feeds the next adder's carry-in. This eliminates the explicit generate/propagate and carry-lookahead signals, simplifying the design. The final carry-out from the last FullAdder becomes the most significant bit of the 5-bit sum output, representing the overflow. This approach is straightforward, easy to understand, and meets the requirement of outputting the 5-bit sum including overflow.
