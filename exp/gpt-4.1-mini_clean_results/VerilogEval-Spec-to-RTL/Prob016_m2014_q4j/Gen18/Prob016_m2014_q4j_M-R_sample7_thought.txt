Refactor the 4-bit adder design to use explicit assign statements to compute sum bits directly from propagate, generate, and carry signals, removing the FullAdder module and generate loop for a more compact and fully combinational implementation. This approach maintains the carry-lookahead carry generation but calculates sums with bitwise expressions using propagate and carry-in bits, simplifying the design and removing module instantiation overhead. The final carry-out (overflow) is still the MSB of the sum.

This refactor:
- Eliminates the FullAdder module and generate loop.
- Directly computes sum bits as sum[i] = p[i] ^ c[i].
- Keeps carry signals computed by carry-lookahead.
- Maintains clean, minimal logic with fewer lines and simpler structure.
- Is fully combinational with no registers.
- Keeps the same functional behavior and interface.
