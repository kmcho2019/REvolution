Implement a 4-bit adder using a hierarchical carry-select approach to achieve faster carry resolution compared to ripple-carry and simpler carry-lookahead logic. The main idea is:

- Divide the 4-bit addition into two parts: lower 2 bits and upper 2 bits.
- Compute the sum and carry-out for the lower 2 bits using a ripple-carry adder.
- For the upper 2 bits, precompute two possible sums and carry-outs assuming carry-in = 0 and carry-in = 1.
- Select the correct upper sum and carry-out based on the actual carry-out from the lower 2 bits.
- Combine lower sum, selected upper sum, and the final carry-out as the 5-bit sum output.

Advantages:
- Carry-select reduces the carry propagation delay compared to pure ripple-carry over 4 bits.
- The modular approach uses small ripple-carry blocks to simplify logic.
- No complex generate/propagate signals, leading to straightforward combinational logic.
- The design is easily scalable by adjusting the size of the carry-select blocks.
- Only necessary logic is used; no redundant carry calculations or unused outputs.

This approach balances between area, power, and speed in a neat modular style.
