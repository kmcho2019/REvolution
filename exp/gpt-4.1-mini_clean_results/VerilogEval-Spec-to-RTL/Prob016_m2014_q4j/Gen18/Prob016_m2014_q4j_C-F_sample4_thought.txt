To create a superior 4-bit adder design that balances clarity, modularity, timing, area, and power efficiency, I will combine the best aspects of both examples:

- From Example 1: The simple carry-lookahead logic that computes carries in parallel without relying on ripple carry, minimizing area and power.
- From Example 2: The instantiation of modular FullAdder blocks to maintain clear, testable structure and facilitate debugging, but removing redundant fa_cout wires to save area and power.

Fusion Strategy:
- Keep the FullAdder module to provide a modular sum and carry-out calculation.
- Use carry-lookahead logic to compute all carries in parallel, reducing critical path delay.
- Pass the precomputed carry signals as carry-in to each FullAdder instance using a generate loop, so the sum bits are computed in parallel with minimal delay.
- Remove the unused fa_cout outputs to save area and power.
- Assign the final carry-out (overflow) from the carry-lookahead logic.
- This approach delivers improved timing from carry-lookahead, modular clarity from FullAdder blocks, and efficient area/power usage by avoiding redundant signals.

The resulting design efficiently computes a 5-bit sum (including overflow) from two 4-bit inputs with good PPA metrics and clean structure.
