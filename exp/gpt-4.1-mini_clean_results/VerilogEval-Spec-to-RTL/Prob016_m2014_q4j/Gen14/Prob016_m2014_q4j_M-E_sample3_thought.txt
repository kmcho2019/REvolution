Implement a 4-bit adder using a single always_comb block that performs bitwise addition with explicit carry propagation coded procedurally rather than using separate full adder modules. This approach uses a simple procedural loop inside an always block with a reg array to accumulate carry, producing the sum and overflow bit. It avoids instantiating multiple modules and uses a behavioral style that might be clearer and more flexible for synthesis tools. The final carry after the most significant bit addition is assigned to sum[4] as the overflow bit.
