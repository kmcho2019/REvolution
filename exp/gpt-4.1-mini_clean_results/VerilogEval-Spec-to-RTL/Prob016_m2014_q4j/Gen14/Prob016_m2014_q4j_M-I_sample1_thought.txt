- Remove the unused wire vector `carry_out` to reduce logic resources.
- Simplify carry-lookahead logic by reducing the number of intermediate wires to only those necessary for carry propagation.
- Inline carry calculations without redundant wire assignments to minimize logic overhead.
- Keep modular FullAdder for readability and reuse, but avoid extra signals not used externally.
- Use only necessary wires (carries c0 to c4) directly for the full adders and output sum.
- This reduces wiring and gate count slightly, potentially lowering area and dynamic power.
- The performance remains unchanged because the carry-lookahead logic is still there, but logic fanout and routing complexity decrease.
- The simpler wiring can also marginally reduce switching power.

This balances retention of clarity and modularity with minor PPA improvements for a small design.
