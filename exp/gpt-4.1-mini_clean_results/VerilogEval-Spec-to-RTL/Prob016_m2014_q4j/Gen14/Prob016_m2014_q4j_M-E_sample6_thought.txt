Implement the 4-bit adder using a single 5-bit adder with behavioral Verilog addition instead of chaining individual full adders. This approach uses Verilog's built-in arithmetic operator on zero-extended inputs to directly compute the sum including the overflow bit, resulting in simpler and more compact code. 

Specifically:
- Extend both 4-bit inputs to 5 bits by prefixing zero to handle carry out.
- Perform a single addition operation to produce a 5-bit result.
- This avoids explicit carry propagation logic and structural components.
- The approach is highly synthesizable and efficient for small bitwidths, trading explicit modularity for simplicity and potentially better synthesis optimization.

This design emphasizes conciseness and clarity without losing functional correctness and keeps the output sum width at 5 bits to capture overflow.
