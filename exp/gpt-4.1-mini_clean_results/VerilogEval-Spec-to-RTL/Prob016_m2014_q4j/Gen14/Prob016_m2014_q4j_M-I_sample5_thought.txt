To optimize for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will implement a carry-lookahead logic within the 4-bit adder instead of the ripple carry adder. This will significantly reduce the carry propagation delay (improving performance). 

The carry-lookahead adder (CLA) uses propagate (p) and generate (g) signals to compute carry signals in parallel, reducing the critical path from linear to logarithmic delay. This directly reduces the effective clock period (improving timing).

Power may also benefit as fewer gates toggle repeatedly across the carry chain, though CLA adders sometimes use slightly more area due to extra logic. However, the 4-bit CLA logic is compact and area-efficient compared to ripple carry with multiple full adders chained.

By designing the module with explicit generate and propagate signals and using continuous assignments without intermediate instances, the code remains clean and synthesizable.

Finally, the output sum[4] is assigned the carry-out of the most significant bit to reflect overflow as required.

This approach improves PPA, especially timing, with modest area and power tradeoffs for a small 4-bit adder.
