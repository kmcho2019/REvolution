Implement the 4-bit adder using a single behavioral always block with a bitwise addition and an explicit carry chain simulated via sequential statements. This approach uses a simple procedural description rather than instantiating multiple full adders, effectively creating a ripple-carry adder in behavioral style. The carry is generated and propagated step-by-step within the always block, and the final carry out is assigned to the 5th bit of the sum. This method is concise, easy to understand, and avoids the explicit use of submodules, while still giving a clear ripple-carry carry chain. It is synthesizable and suitable for basic synthesis tools. Also, it demonstrates an alternative style leveraging procedural constructs rather than structural instantiations.
