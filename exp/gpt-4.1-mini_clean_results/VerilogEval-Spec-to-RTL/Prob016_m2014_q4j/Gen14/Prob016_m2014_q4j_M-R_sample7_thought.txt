Refactor the 4-bit ripple carry adder by explicitly instantiating four separate FullAdder modules inside the TopModule, each taking a carry-in and producing a carry-out. This restores modular hierarchy and code reuse, improves readability, and separates concerns between the FullAdder and TopModule. The carry chain is formed by connecting carry outputs to carry inputs of subsequent FullAdders. The sum and carry-out signals are collected accordingly to produce the final 5-bit sum output including the overflow bit.
