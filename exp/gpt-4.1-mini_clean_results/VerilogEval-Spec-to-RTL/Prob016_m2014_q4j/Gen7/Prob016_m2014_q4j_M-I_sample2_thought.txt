To improve the PPA of the original 4-bit ripple carry adder, the main bottleneck is the ripple carry delay which impacts timing (WNS and TNS), and potentially power due to toggling in every stage.

Improvement strategy:
- Replace the ripple carry structure with a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for faster carry generation, significantly reducing critical path delay.
- The CLA generates group propagate and generate signals for the 4 bits, computes carry signals in parallel without waiting for sequential carry propagation.
- This reduces carry propagation delay, improving timing (WNS, TNS).
- The CLA logic slightly increases combinational complexity (area), but for 4 bits, the overhead is minimal.
- Power can improve since logic toggling is less dependent on serial carry propagation, and the circuit may have shorter switching activity chains.
- The code keeps modular clarity by implementing the TopModule with internal CLA logic without instantiating separate full adders, reducing unnecessary module overhead.

This approach strikes a good balance between timing and area for a small 4-bit adder while retaining clear design and easy synthesis mapping to fast carry chains.
