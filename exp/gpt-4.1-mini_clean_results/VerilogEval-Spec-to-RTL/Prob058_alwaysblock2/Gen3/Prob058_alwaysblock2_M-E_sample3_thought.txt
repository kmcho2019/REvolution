To create a novel architectural approach, I will compute the XOR of inputs 'a' and 'b' into an intermediate wire first, which then feeds the three output styles. This allows separation of the combinational XOR logic from the outputs. For out_assign, I'll drive it directly from the intermediate wire with an assign statement. For out_always_comb, I'll use an always_comb block that samples the intermediate wire to allow easy insertion of more logic if desired. For out_always_ff, I'll register the intermediate wire on the positive clock edge. This modular approach clarifies the design flow, helps with reuse, and enables easier debugging and potential extension.
