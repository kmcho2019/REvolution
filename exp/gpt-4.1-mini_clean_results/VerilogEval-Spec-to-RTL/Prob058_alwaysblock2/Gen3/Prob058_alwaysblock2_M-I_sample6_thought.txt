To optimize the existing design for better PPA, I will remove the intermediate wire and directly use the XOR expression in both the assign and always blocks. This eliminates one net and potentially reduces routing complexity. Additionally, the combinational always block is unnecessary since the assign already drives a combinational output; replacing the combinational always block with a continuous assign to the output as well will reduce logic and improve power and area. For the sequential output, we keep the registered XOR but use the direct expression to avoid extra nets. This reduces logic duplication and simplifies the netlist without changing functionality. Overall, this should reduce area and power marginally and simplify timing.
