Instead of directly implementing XOR in all three outputs separately, I will first create an internal combinational wire that computes the XOR result. The out_assign will be driven directly by this wire through an assign statement. The out_always_comb will be assigned inside a combinational always block from this wire, showing that the always block uses internal combinational logic. For out_always_ff, I will use a clocked always block to register the internal XOR result, highlighting the separation between combinational and sequential logic. This modular approach separates the XOR logic from outputs and emphasizes clean coding style and reusability.
