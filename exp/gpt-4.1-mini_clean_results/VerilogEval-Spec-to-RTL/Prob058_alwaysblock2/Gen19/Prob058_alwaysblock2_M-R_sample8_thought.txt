I will refactor the module by removing the structural XOR gate instance and instead using the built-in bitwise XOR operator (^) directly in the assign and always blocks. This reduces the module hierarchy and can potentially reduce netlist overhead. 

Specifically:
- For out_assign, directly assign a ^ b.
- For out_always_comb, compute XOR inside the combinational always block.
- For out_always_ff, register the XOR computed on the fly at clock's positive edge.
