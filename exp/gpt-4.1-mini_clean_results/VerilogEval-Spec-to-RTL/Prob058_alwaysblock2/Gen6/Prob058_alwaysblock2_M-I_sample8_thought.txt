The existing design is already minimal and functionally correct, implementing XOR in three styles efficiently. For minor PPA optimization, I will:

- Maintain the simple and direct XOR expression without intermediate wires to keep logic depth minimal.
- Use nonblocking assignments only in the sequential always_ff block (already done).
- For the combinational always block, use blocking assignment (already done), which is optimal for combinational logic.
- To slightly reduce unnecessary toggling in the flip-flop, I will add a clock enable signal that activates only when inputs differ from the previous values (a simple input change detector), thus gating the flip-flop toggling and reducing dynamic power in cases of stable inputs.
- This will save dynamic power by avoiding flip-flop toggling when inputs do not change, with negligible area overhead.
- Functionality remains correct: out_assign and out_always_comb are immediate XOR results, out_always_ff is delayed XOR output updated only on input changes.

This small clock-enable strategy improves power without impacting timing significantly or increasing area much.
