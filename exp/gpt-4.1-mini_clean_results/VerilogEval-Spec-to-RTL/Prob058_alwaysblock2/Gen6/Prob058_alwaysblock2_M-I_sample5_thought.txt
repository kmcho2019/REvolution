To optimize for better power and area, I will remove the intermediate wire `xor_val` and directly instantiate the XOR operation in all three places. This eliminates the potential overhead of an extra net, reducing routing capacitance and net switching. Additionally, I will convert the combinational always block to a continuous assignment because combinational logic that simply drives an output based on inputs is more power-efficient and simpler as a continuous assignment, minimizing glitches and unnecessary simulation delta cycles. This approach maintains clarity and correctness but reduces area and switching activity marginally, improving overall PPA.
