The original implementation is already minimal and efficient in terms of logic and synthesis. The only area for slight power and area improvement could be to avoid redundant XOR computations by calculating it once and then assigning to outputs. This also slightly reduces toggling because the XOR is computed once per event. We'll introduce an intermediate wire `xor_ab` that computes `a ^ b` once, then use it in the assign and the always blocks. This keeps the separate implementations intact but avoids repeated XOR operations, which might help synthesis tools optimize the netlist better. We'll keep the clocked always_ff block with nonblocking assignment to maintain correct sequential behavior.
