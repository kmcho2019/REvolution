I will refactor the module by introducing an intermediate wire to hold the XOR of inputs a and b. This wire will be driven by an assign statement. Then, I will use this intermediate wire as the source for all outputs: directly for out_assign, inside a combinational always block for out_always_comb, and inside a clocked always block for out_always_ff.
