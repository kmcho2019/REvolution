I will implement the XOR operation by explicitly instantiating a simple gate-level XOR primitive module for the assign output to demonstrate the use of structural modeling. For the combinational output, I will use a case statement inside an always_comb block to compute XOR logically by enumerating input combinations, highlighting the behavioral approach without direct operator use. For the sequential output, I will register the output of a two-stage pipeline where the first stage computes XOR using a blocking assignment and the second stage captures the intermediate result on the clock edge using a non-blocking assignment. This pipelined approach introduces an additional stage delay, differentiating the sequential output behavior further.
