To optimize the design for better power and area, I will reduce the number of XOR computations by driving both combinational outputs from a single wire declared inside the module. This avoids synthesizing multiple XOR gates for the same expression. The sequential always_ff output remains registered on the clock edge with a non-blocking assignment. By sharing the XOR wire for combinational outputs, we simplify logic duplication, potentially reducing power and area slightly, while maintaining the same functionality and timing behavior.
