To optimize the design for better power, area, and timing (PPA), I will simplify each implementation of XOR:

1. For out_assign: replace the structural XOR module with a direct continuous assignment using the bitwise XOR operator to reduce module instantiation overhead.

2. For out_always_comb: replace the case statement with a simple direct XOR assignment inside the always_comb block to reduce combinational logic complexity and improve synthesis quality.

3. For out_always_ff: remove the unnecessary two-stage pipeline. Use a single nonblocking assignment on the clock's positive edge to register the XOR result directly, reducing latency, area, and power.

These changes preserve the original functionality while improving simplicity, area, power, and timing.
