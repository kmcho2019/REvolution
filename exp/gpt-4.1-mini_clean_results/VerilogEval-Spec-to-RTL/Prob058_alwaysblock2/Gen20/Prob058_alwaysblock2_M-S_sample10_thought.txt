I will simplify the design by removing the intermediate combinational register xor_comb_wire. Instead, the clocked always block will compute the XOR directly from inputs a and b at each clock edge, eliminating the extra combinational reg and logic. 

- out_assign will remain a continuous assign of a ^ b.
- out_always_comb will remain assigned in an always_comb block as a ^ b.
- out_always_ff will be assigned synchronously in an always_ff block, computing a ^ b directly without any intermediate signals.

This reduces logic and net count, while preserving all three distinct coding styles and functionality. The sequential output remains delayed by one clock cycle as required.
