To implement the three XOR outputs differently, I will introduce an intermediate wire for the XOR result and reuse it for two outputs, which reduces duplication. For the clocked output, I will register the wire instead of recomputing XOR inside the clocked block, ensuring modularity and a single source of logic. Also, for the combinational output, I will use a combinational always block assigning from the intermediate wire. This approach separates combinational logic computation from output assignments and can ease debugging or future extension.
