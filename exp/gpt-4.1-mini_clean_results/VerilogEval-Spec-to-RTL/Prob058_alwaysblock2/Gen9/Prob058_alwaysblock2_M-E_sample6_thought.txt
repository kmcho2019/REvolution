1. Use three separate logic paths explicitly to emphasize structural differences rather than sharing intermediate wires.
2. For the assign statement (out_assign), directly assign the XOR of inputs a and b, as a simple continuous assignment.
3. For the combinational always block (out_always_comb), use a procedural block with blocking assignments (=) to clearly demonstrate standard combinational style, triggered on changes of inputs a or b.
4. For the sequential always block (out_always_ff), create a small register stage with an intermediate register (reg xor_reg) to hold the XOR value, updating on the positive edge of clk. This register stores the XOR value of a and b at the clock edge, then outputs it via out_always_ff, showing the explicit pipeline step.
5. Make all outputs declared as reg except for out_assign which is a wire, matching the assignment methods.
