To implement three XOR outputs differently while ensuring functional equivalence, I will:
1. Use a wire that holds the XOR of inputs a and b, then assign it to out_assign directly.
2. In a combinational always block, use this intermediate wire to assign to out_always_comb.
3. In the sequential always block triggered on posedge clk, register the intermediate wire to out_always_ff.
This approach promotes code reuse by computing the XOR once in an internal wire and referencing that in all outputs, reducing redundant XOR gate instantiations.
