To implement the XOR operation three ways while providing a distinct architecture, I will introduce an intermediate wire driven by the assign statement, and use that wire in the always blocks to demonstrate data reuse. Specifically, the combinational always block will register the intermediate XOR result into a temporary reg, and then output it combinationally, while the clocked always block will sample this temporary reg on the clock's positive edge. This creates a small data path with an intermediate combinational register variable, highlighting a subtle difference in dataflow and ensuring a clear separation between the data generation, combinational logic, and sequential logic. This approach also illustrates clean coding style for reusing combinational logic results.
