The design requires three XOR outputs: one from an assign statement, one from a combinational always block, and one from a clocked always block (flip-flop). To simplify, we can avoid the intermediate wire and directly compute a^b in each construct since the XOR is minimal logic and duplication is not costly here.

- The assign output can be directly assigned a^b.
- The combinational always block assigns a^b to its output.
- The sequential always block registers a^b on the clock's positive edge.

This avoids the internal wire and reduces lines of code, keeping the design straightforward and clear while meeting all functional requirements.
