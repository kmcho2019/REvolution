The original design is simple and already optimal for functionality and area, but slight improvement in power and area can be achieved by sharing the XOR computation among all three outputs via a single intermediate wire. This lets synthesis tools better optimize the combinational logic since the XOR gate is instantiated once, potentially reducing logic duplication. The outputs then derive from this wire: out_assign uses the wire directly, out_always_comb is combinationally assigned from the wire, and out_always_ff registers the wire at the clock edge. This approach maintains correct behavior and timing, reduces redundant logic, and may slightly improve synthesis area and power.
