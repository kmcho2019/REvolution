The original design efficiently shares the XOR logic by computing it once and distributing the result to three outputs via different methods. To optimize further for power, we can gate the clock to the flip-flop so that it only toggles when the XOR output changes, preventing unnecessary flip-flop toggling and dynamic power consumption. This optimization slightly increases the logic area due to clock gating circuitry but reduces power by lowering flip-flop switching activity. The combinational and assign outputs remain unchanged to preserve immediate response. This maintains functionality, improves power efficiency, and preserves timing.
