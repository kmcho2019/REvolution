Implement the XOR in three different ways with a slight architectural twist:
1. Use a parameterized function for the combinational assign statement output.
2. Use an always_comb block with a local wire to compute intermediate XOR, then assign to the output reg.
3. Use an always_ff block that stores the XOR of registered inputs (a_reg and b_reg), demonstrating registering inputs before XOR operation and outputting delayed XOR.
This showcases use of functions, local wires, and input registering before combinational logic inside the clocked always_ff.
