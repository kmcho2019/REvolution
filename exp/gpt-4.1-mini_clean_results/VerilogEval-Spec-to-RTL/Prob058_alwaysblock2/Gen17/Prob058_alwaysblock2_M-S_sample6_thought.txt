To simplify, I will eliminate the separate structural XOR gate module and directly use behavioral XOR expressions in all places. The assign output will be driven directly by 'a ^ b', the combinational always block will directly assign 'a ^ b', and the sequential always block will register 'a ^ b' on the positive edge of clk using a nonblocking assignment. This removes unnecessary module instantiations and intermediate wires, keeping the design minimal while preserving the required three distinct implementations of XOR.
