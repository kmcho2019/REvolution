To optimize the design for better PPA while maintaining clarity and distinct implementation styles, I will remove the structural XOR gate instantiation and replace it with a direct assign statement for out_assign. This eliminates the extra module boundary and wiring overhead, reducing area and potentially improving timing.

For out_always_comb, I will keep the combinational always block using direct XOR as it is clean and concise, ensuring synthesis of minimal logic.

For out_always_ff, I will add a clock enable signal that disables the register update when inputs `a` and `b` have not changed, reducing unnecessary toggling and lowering dynamic power without affecting functionality.

These changes keep three distinct implementations (assign, combinational always, and sequential always with register), improve area by removing one module, and reduce power by clock gating the flip-flop implicitly. Performance remains optimal due to simple logic and controlled register toggling.
