I will implement the XOR operation using an intermediate wire for the combinational logic, which is then used by all three outputs. For the assign output, I will assign directly from this wire. For the combinational always block, I will register the value of the wire inside the block. For the sequential always block, I will use a two-stage pipeline: the first register captures the XOR output, and the second register outputs it. This creates a different sequential behavior with a one-cycle delay compared to the original design. This approach illustrates wire reuse and introduces a two-stage pipeline to differentiate the sequential logic more distinctly.
