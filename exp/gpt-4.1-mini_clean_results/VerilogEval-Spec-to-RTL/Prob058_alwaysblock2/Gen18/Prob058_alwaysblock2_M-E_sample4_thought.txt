I will implement the XOR function in three distinct ways without using a structural primitive gate instance. For out_assign, I will use a continuous assign statement directly with the XOR operator. For out_always_comb, I will create a combinational always block that computes XOR using bitwise operators on a 2-bit concatenation of inputs to show an alternative style. For out_always_ff, I will use a clocked always block with a registered XOR result, but this time I will implement the sequential output as a one-bit shift register that delays the XOR output by one clock cycle, emphasizing a slightly more complex sequential behavior.
