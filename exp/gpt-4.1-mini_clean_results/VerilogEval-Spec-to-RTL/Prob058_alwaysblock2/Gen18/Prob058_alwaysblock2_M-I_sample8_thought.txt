To optimize for better PPA while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will remove the structural XOR gate instantiation and replace the out_assign and out_always_comb outputs with direct behavioral XOR expressions. This eliminates the overhead of module instantiation and wiring delays. Both combinational outputs will use the same behavioral expression, reducing logic duplication by sharing a common wire internally.

The sequential output remains the same, registering the XOR directly on the clock edge with a nonblocking assignment, ensuring minimal latency.

- Use a shared internal wire for the XOR expression: assign xor_behavioral = a ^ b;
