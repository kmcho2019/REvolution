To optimize the design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will remove the structural XOR gate module and replace it with a direct assign statement for out_assign to reduce module overhead and wiring complexity. I will also convert the combinational always block to a continuous assign statement to reduce redundant logic and improve synthesis efficiency. For the sequential always block, I will keep it as is but add a clock enable signal that enables the register update only when inputs a or b change, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. This will overall simplify the design, reduce area by eliminating a module and combinational always block, and save dynamic power by gating the clock effectively.
