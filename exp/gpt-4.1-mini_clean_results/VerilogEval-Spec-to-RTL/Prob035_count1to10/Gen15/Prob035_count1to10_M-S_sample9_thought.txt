To simplify the decade counter implementation while preserving its functionality, I will:

- Merge the combinational and sequential logic into a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge.
- Implement synchronous reset directly inside the sequential always block.
- Use a straightforward condition to check if q has reached 10 (`4'd10`) and reset to 1; otherwise increment by 1.
- Eliminate the separate next_q variable, reducing code and register usage.
- Maintain q as a 4-bit register to hold count values.
- This approach reduces code complexity and logic without sacrificing clarity or performance, making it easier to understand and maintain.
