Implement the decade counter using a simple binary counter from 0 to 9 internally, and then output q as the binary counter plus one. This way, counting from 0 to 9 internally corresponds to output counts from 1 to 10. The synchronous reset sets the internal counter to zero. This method simplifies the terminal count detection to checking if the count reached 9, and uses arithmetic for output generation, which might optimize the logic synthesis and improve clarity.
