Implement the decade counter with an internal 4-bit register that holds the count value directly from 1 to 10 inclusive. On each clock, increment the count by 1 until it reaches 10, then wrap back to 1. On synchronous reset, set the count to 1. This approach eliminates any arithmetic manipulation at output time since q is directly the count value, simplifying the output logic and potentially improving timing. The output q is driven directly by the registered count.
