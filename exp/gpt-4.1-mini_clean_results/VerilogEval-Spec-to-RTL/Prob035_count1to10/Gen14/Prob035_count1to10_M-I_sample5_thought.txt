To improve the PPA of the existing decade counter, reduce the critical path delay caused by the multi-gate combinational logic detecting the count of 10. Instead of detecting decimal 10 (1010) with bitwise AND/NOT gates, perform a direct equality check using a comparator operation (q == 4'd10). This synthesis-friendly approach typically infers optimized hardware comparators, often leading to reduced combinational delay and simpler logic.

Additionally, remove the intermediate wire and directly use the comparison in the combinational block, simplifying logic and possibly aiding synthesis tools in optimization.

This change preserves the synchronous reset and proper separation of combinational and sequential logic, maintaining clarity and correct functionality while targeting better timing and slightly reduced area.

No clock gating or one-hot encoding is added to keep design minimal and power/performance trade-offs balanced. This approach primarily focuses on logic simplification and timing improvement.
