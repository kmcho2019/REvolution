Implement the decade counter by directly storing and incrementing the count value in the output register q itself, which ranges from 1 to 10. On each clock cycle, if q is 10, it rolls over to 1; otherwise, it increments by 1. This eliminates the need for a separate internal register and combinational logic to compute q. The synchronous reset sets q to 1 directly. This approach simplifies the design by using a single register to hold the output value and reduces combinational logic after the clock edge.
