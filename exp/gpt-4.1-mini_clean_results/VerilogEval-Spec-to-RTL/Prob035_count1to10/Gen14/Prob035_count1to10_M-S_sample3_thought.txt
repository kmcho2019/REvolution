To simplify the decade counter, remove the explicit combinational next_q register and compute the next count directly within the sequential always block. Use a straightforward numeric comparison (q == 10) to detect wrap-around, which is clearer and only slightly more logic than the bitwise pattern. On reset, synchronously set q to 1. On each clock edge, if q is 10, reset to 1; otherwise increment by 1. This eliminates an always_comb block, making the design simpler and easier to understand while maintaining functionality.
