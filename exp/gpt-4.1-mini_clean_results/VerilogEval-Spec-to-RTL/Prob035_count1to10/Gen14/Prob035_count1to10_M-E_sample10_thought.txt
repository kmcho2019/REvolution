Implement the decade counter using a 4-bit register initialized to 1 on synchronous reset, and then increment it each clock cycle until it reaches 10. When the count reaches 10, reset it back to 1. This way, the count directly represents the output q, eliminating the need for adding 1 to an internal count. This approach simplifies the output logic and makes q a registered output reflecting the current count, potentially improving timing on the output path by avoiding combinational addition.
