Implement the decade counter as a modulo-10 counter using a simple 4-bit binary counter from 0 to 9 internally, but output the count offset by 1 so it counts 1 through 10 externally. On synchronous reset, set the internal count to 0, which corresponds to output 1. On each clock cycle, increment the internal count modulo 10 by wrapping from 9 back to 0. This approach simplifies the counting logic by counting zero-based internally and only adjusting output mapping, removing the need for an explicit comparator for 10. The output q is assigned as internal count + 1 combinationally, keeping sequential logic minimal and clean.
