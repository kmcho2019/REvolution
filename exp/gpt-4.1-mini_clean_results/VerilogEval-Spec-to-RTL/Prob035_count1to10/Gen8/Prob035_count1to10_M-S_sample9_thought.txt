To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will combine the next-state logic and the counter update within a single always block, removing separate combinational assignments. Instead of a separate is_ten wire and next_q, the code will directly increment q or reset to 1 inside the sequential block. This reduces the number of wires and combinational logic, making the code more concise and easier to understand while preserving synchronous reset and counting from 1 to 10.
