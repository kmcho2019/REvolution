I will implement the decade counter from 1 to 10 using a simple state machine approach encoded as a 4-bit register, where the next state logic explicitly checks if the counter is at 10 to reset to 1, otherwise increment by 1. 

To reduce combinational logic complexity, instead of a bitwise condition or numeric comparison, I will implement the counter increment and reset with a ternary operator using simple equality check (q == 4'd10) synthesized efficiently. This keeps the design readable and straightforward.

The synchronous reset will asynchronously set q to 1 on the rising edge of clk when reset is high.

I will also use non-blocking assignments to maintain sequential logic correctness.

This solution emphasizes code clarity and leverages synthesis optimizations for equality comparison, maintaining a minimal footprint without additional enable logic.
