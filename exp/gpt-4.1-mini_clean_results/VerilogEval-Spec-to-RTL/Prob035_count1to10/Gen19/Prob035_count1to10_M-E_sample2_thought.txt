Implement the decade counter using a simple up-counter with a conditional wrap-around at 10. Instead of detecting '10' by a specific bit pattern, use a numeric comparison (q == 4'd10) to determine when to reset to 1. The counter will increment by 1 on every positive clock edge if reset is not asserted. The synchronous reset sets q to 1. This design avoids a separate next-state signal by updating q directly in the sequential block, making the code concise and easy to understand. The counter output q is a 4-bit register as requested, and counts from 1 to 10 inclusive, wrapping around to 1 after 10.
