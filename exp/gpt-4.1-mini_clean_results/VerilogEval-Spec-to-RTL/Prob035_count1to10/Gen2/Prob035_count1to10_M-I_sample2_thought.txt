To optimize the decade counter for better PPA, I will remove the equality comparator and replace it with a smaller combinational condition that detects when the count reaches 10 to reset it to 1. Instead of a direct equality check, I'll implement the modulo-10 counting using a smaller logic expression to reduce area and potentially improve timing. Also, I'll introduce an enable signal to avoid unnecessary toggling when the counter resets. This will reduce power by preventing redundant count increments. The counter will still count synchronously on the rising edge of clk, with a synchronous active-high reset resetting to 1.
