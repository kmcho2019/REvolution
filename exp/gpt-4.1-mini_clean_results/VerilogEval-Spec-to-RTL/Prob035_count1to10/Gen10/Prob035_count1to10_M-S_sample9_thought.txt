To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will implement the counter with straightforward synchronous logic: increment q each clock cycle, and reset to 1 when reset is asserted or q reaches 10.  
Rather than creating an explicit wrap detection wire or separate combinational next-state logic, I'll use an if-else structure inside the always block to directly update q synchronously.  
This reduces code complexity and leverages the fact that synthesis tools handle simple comparisons and additions efficiently.  
The reset remains synchronous active-high, resetting q to 1.  
This approach removes extra wires and combinational assignments while preserving clear and correct counting from 1 to 10.
