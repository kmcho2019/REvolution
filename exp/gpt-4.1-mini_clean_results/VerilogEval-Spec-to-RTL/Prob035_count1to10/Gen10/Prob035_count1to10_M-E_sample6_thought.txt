Implement the decade counter using a simple 4-bit binary counter that counts from 0 to 9, and then add 1 to the output to obtain a count from 1 to 10. The internal state counts from 0 to 9, resetting back to 0 on reaching 9. The synchronous reset initializes the counter to 0 (which corresponds to output 1). This approach reduces the logic needed to detect the terminal count and simplifies the combinational logic, since counting and detection are done with numeric comparison. The output q is always the internal count plus 1.
