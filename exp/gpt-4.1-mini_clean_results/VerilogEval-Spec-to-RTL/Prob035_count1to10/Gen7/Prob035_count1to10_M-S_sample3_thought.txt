Implement the decade counter with synchronous reset to 1 by using a straightforward numeric comparison (q == 10) to detect when to wrap back to 1. This removes the need for bitwise logic and makes the code simpler and easier to read. Use a single always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock. The counter increments by 1 each cycle if not at 10; otherwise, it resets to 1. This meets the requirement of counting from 1 through 10 synchronously.
