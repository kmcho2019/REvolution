Implement a simple synchronous reset decade counter counting 1 to 10 using a 4-bit register. Reset sets the counter to 1 synchronously. On each clock edge, increment the counter if less than 10; otherwise wrap back to 1. Detect count of 10 by comparing the 4-bit value with decimal 10 using a straightforward equality check (`q == 4'd10`). This reduces comparator logic complexity compared to bitwise detection, making the code simpler and easier to understand while preserving function.
