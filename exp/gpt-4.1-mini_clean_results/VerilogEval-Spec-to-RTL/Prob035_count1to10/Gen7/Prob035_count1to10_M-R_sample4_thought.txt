Refactor the decade counter by moving the next-state logic inside the synchronous always block, thereby eliminating the continuous assignment. This consolidates the counter increment and wrap-around logic into the sequential process, simplifying the module by reducing the number of intermediate wires. The synchronous reset is maintained, and the counter counts from 1 to 10 inclusive, resetting back to 1. This approach is more traditional and keeps the entire state update logic localized, which some designers find clearer and easier to maintain.
