Refactor the decade counter by implementing a combinational next-state logic with a registered state update. The synchronous reset sets the counter to 1. The combinational logic computes the next count value: if the counter is at 10, next value wraps to 1; otherwise, increment by 1. This separates the sequential and combinational logic clearly, enhancing readability and ease of modification. The detection of 10 is done similarly by checking the bit pattern. Using a registered next-state approach maintains proper synchronous design while changing the structure from the prior always block. No explicit count_enable is needed; counting proceeds unless reset is asserted.
