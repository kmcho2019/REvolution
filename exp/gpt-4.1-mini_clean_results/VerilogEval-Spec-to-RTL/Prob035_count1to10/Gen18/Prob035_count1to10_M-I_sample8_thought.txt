To optimize the existing synchronous decade counter for better PPA, I will apply these improvements:

1. Simplify the detection of decimal 10 by replacing the bitwise expression with a simple equality check (q == 4'd10). This can lead to easier synthesis and potentially smaller area.
2. Use a synchronous reset inside the sequential always block to reduce combinational logic, improving timing by making the next-state logic purely arithmetic and the reset explicit.
3. Replace the combinational next_q logic with direct arithmetic increment and reset handling inside the clocked process, minimizing combinational logic depth.
4. Add a small enable signal so that q only toggles when counting is active; however, since counting is continuous here, enable would just be implicit in reset and wrapping, so no clock gating is applied.
5. Since this counter counts from 1 to 10 and wraps back to 1, we'll implement the wrap logic with a simple if statement in the clocked process.

Overall, this reduces the combinational critical path, simplifies logic expressions, and can marginally improve power and area, while keeping the design clean and easy to synthesize.
