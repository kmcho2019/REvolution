Refactor the decade counter by implementing the counter using a synchronous always block with explicit if-else conditions without separate next state logic wires. The counter will hold its value unless reset or incremented. When the count reaches decimal 10, it wraps back to 1.

Specifically:
1. Use a 4-bit register q to hold the count.
2. On every positive clock edge, if reset is active, set q to 1.
3. Else if q equals 10, set q back to 1.
4. Else increment q by 1.
5. This approach embeds next-state logic and state update in a single always block, making the logic straightforward and intuitive.

This structure avoids combinational next-state logic outside the always block and directly models the synchronous counting behavior. The behavior remains identical to the original, counting from 1 to 10 inclusively with synchronous reset to 1.
