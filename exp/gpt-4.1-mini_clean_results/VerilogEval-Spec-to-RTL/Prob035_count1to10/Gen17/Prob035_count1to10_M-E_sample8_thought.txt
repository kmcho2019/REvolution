Implement the decade counter as a 4-bit register holding values 1 through 10 directly. On each clock edge, the counter increments by 1. When it reaches 10, it wraps back to 1. The synchronous reset sets the output directly to 1. This eliminates the need for addition in the combinational logic for output q since the register value is the actual count. This approach simplifies the output logic to a direct register assignment and might improve timing on q by avoiding combinational arithmetic.
