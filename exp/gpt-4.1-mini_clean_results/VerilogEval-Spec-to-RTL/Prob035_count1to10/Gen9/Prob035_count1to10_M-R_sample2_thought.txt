I will refactor the design by separating the combinational next-state logic from the sequential state update. Instead of embedding the logic directly inside the always block, I will use a combinational always block (or continuous assignment) to generate the next value of the counter and then update the state on the clock edge. This clarifies the design by making it a classic two-process FSM style, which may improve readability and help synthesis tools optimize the design. The counter will still count from 1 to 10 and reset synchronously on reset.
