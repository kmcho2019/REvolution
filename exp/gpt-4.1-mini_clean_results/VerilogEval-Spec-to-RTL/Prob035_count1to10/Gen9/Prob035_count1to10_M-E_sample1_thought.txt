I will implement the decade counter as a simple 4-bit binary counter starting from 1 and counting up to 10, but rather than checking for the exact decimal 10, I will detect when the count reaches 11 (4'b1011) and reset the counter back to 1. This approach uses a slightly simpler reset condition by checking only q == 11, which can be detected as (q[3] & ~q[2] & q[1] & q[0]), and resets synchronously. The counter increments each clock cycle unless reset is asserted. This will reduce combinational logic slightly compared to comparing with 10 and provides clear behavior. The reset will remain synchronous active high. The output q will always be in the range 1 to 10, inclusive.
