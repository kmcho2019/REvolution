To improve PPA, I will merge the output q logic into the main sequential always block. By registering q alongside count in the clocked block, I eliminate the combinational addition at output time, reducing combinational logic depth and fanout on the output path. This should improve timing (lower critical path delay) and reduce dynamic power caused by combinational toggling of q. The synchronous reset will also reset q to 1, maintaining correct initial state. This approach maintains the counting scheme (count from 0 to 9, q = count+1) but with a registered output q, optimizing performance and power while slightly increasing register count but without significant area penalty.
