I will implement the decade counter by using a 4-bit register to hold the count, counting from 1 to 10. To minimize combinational logic for the reset condition, I will increment the counter normally until it reaches decimal 10, and then wrap it back to 1. Instead of checking the exact value 10 by a direct bitwise condition, I will use a "greater than or equal to 10" comparison implemented by analyzing the bits that define 10 or higher (for 4 bits, values greater than 10 are 11,12,...15). To do this efficiently, I will leverage the fact that any value greater than or equal to 10 must have bit 3 set and either bit 2 set or bit 1 set (e.g., 1010 or more). This check will reset the counter to 1. The counter will be synchronous and reset synchronously on a high reset input. This approach avoids a strict equality check and uses a simple, small combinational logic condition for rollover.
