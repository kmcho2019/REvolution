I will implement the decade counter using a 4-bit binary counter with a custom saturation and wrap-around logic to cycle from 1 to 10 inclusively. 

Specifically, the counter will increment on each clock cycle unless it reaches 10. Upon reaching 10, the next count will roll over to 1 on the following clock. This approach uses minimal flip-flops (only 4), reducing area and power.

The synchronous reset sets the count directly to 1.

The logic will use a simple comparison to determine when to wrap, and no one-hot encoding is used. The 4-bit output 'q' directly represents the binary counter value, simplifying output assignment and reducing combinational decoding logic.

This binary counter design is efficient for timing, area, and power while meeting the design requirements precisely.
