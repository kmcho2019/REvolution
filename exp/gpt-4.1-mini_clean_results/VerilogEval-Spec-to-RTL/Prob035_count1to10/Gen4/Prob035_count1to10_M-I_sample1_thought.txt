To optimize the decade counter for better PPA, I will implement the counter with a Binary Coded Decimal (BCD) style approach that counts from 1 to 10 and then wraps back to 1. Instead of directly incrementing and comparing for 10, I will add a small combinational logic that checks if the counter reaches 10, then resets it to 1; otherwise, it increments. This approach reduces the logic depth compared to the previous explicit bitwise comparison. Also, I will use a ternary operator for succinctness and possibly better synthesis optimization. The synchronous reset remains active high and sets the counter to 1. This design potentially improves timing by reducing combinational logic depth and slightly reduces switching by avoiding redundant comparisons.
