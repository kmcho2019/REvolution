I will implement the decade counter as a synchronous up-counter with a ternary condition in the sequential block directly, eliminating the need for a separate combinational logic block. The counter will increment on every clock cycle unless it reaches 10, in which case it wraps back to 1. The reset is synchronous and sets the counter to 1. The detection of 10 will be done using an equality comparison rather than bitwise logic, trading a bit of complexity in combinational logic for simpler readability and potentially easier formal verification. This approach reduces the logic to just one always block, simplifying the design.
