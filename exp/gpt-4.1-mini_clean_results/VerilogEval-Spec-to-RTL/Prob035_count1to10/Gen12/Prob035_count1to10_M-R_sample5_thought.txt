I will refactor the decade counter by using a fully synchronous process without a separate combinational next-state block. Instead, I will implement the counting and reset logic directly in the clocked always block for simplicity. The comparator for the terminal count (10) will be replaced with a direct equality check (q == 4'd10) to simplify logic and potentially improve timing and area slightly. The reset will remain synchronous active-high, setting the counter to 1, and on reaching 10, the counter wraps back to 1. This approach eliminates the need for a separate count_enable signal and consolidates logic, maintaining clarity while providing an alternative clean structure.
