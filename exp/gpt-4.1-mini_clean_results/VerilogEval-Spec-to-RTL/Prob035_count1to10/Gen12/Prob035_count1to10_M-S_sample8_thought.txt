I will implement the decade counter as a simple 4-bit binary counter that counts from 1 to 10. On each positive clock edge, the counter increments by 1, and when it reaches 10, it rolls over to 1. The synchronous active-high reset sets the counter to 1. This approach reduces area and power compared to one-hot encoding, simplifies the design by removing extra decoding logic, and improves timing by minimizing combinational logic in the state transitions. The output `q` is directly assigned from the counter value.
