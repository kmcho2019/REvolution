Implement the decade counter using a modulo-10 counter internally counting from 0 to 9 and then output q as the internal count plus 1. This approach leverages simple arithmetic to handle counting and reset logic, simplifying the comparison for terminal count. The synchronous reset sets the internal counter to 0 (representing decimal 1 output). The increment logic is straightforward: increment internal count on each clock cycle, wrap to 0 after reaching 9. This clean separation avoids explicit bitwise terminal detection and keeps the output in the specified range 1 to 10.
