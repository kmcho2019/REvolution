Refactor the decade counter to use a synchronous process with a case statement instead of combinational next-state logic. This approach uses a single always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock to handle both reset and counting logic, simplifying the design by avoiding a separate combinational next-state block. The counter resets to 1 on a synchronous active-high reset and increments until 10, then wraps back to 1. Using a case statement explicitly enumerates each count value, potentially improving clarity and allowing synthesis tools to optimize the counting sequence effectively.
