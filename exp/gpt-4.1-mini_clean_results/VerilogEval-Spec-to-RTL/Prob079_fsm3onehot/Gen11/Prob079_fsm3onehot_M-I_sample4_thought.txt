The original design is clear and efficiently groups signals to minimize fan-in and gate count. To improve power and area further while maintaining performance, we can:

- Reduce the number of intermediate signals and OR gates by expressing next state logic directly with minimal expressions derived from the truth table.
- Use direct sum-of-products style equations for each next_state bit, factoring the input and state bits to reduce logic.
- Since only one-hot encoding is used, each next_state bit depends on few inputs. Minimizing intermediate wires reduces net switching and area.
- Keep the output logic direct and simple as it is already optimal.

This approach slightly reduces gate count and net switching activity, hence improving power and area without impacting performance.
