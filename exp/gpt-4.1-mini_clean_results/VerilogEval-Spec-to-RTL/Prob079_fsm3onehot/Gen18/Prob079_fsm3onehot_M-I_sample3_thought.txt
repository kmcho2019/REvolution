To optimize the design for better PPA, I will focus on reducing logic redundancy and improving signal reuse to lower area and power while maintaining clarity and performance. The original design factored common terms well, but some overlap remains, like A and B signals appearing multiple times in different next_state bits. 

I will introduce intermediate wires for common terms such as A_or_B and B_or_D to enable logic sharing, which can reduce gate count after synthesis and hence area and power. The inverter for in_n will be retained but also used in multiple places to minimize duplicated inversion logic.

Furthermore, I will re-express the next_state logic using these shared intermediate signals, enabling potentially simpler and smaller gate-level implementations with less signal switching.

Output logic is minimal and direct, so I'll keep it as is.

This approach targets slight improvements in area and power with no timing impact by enhancing logic reuse and reducing duplicated gate structures.
