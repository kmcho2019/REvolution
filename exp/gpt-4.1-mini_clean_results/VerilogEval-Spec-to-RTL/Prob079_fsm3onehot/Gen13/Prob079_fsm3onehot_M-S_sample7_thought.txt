Exploit the one-hot encoding where only one state bit is set at a time, so next_state bits can be computed by combining conditions without repeated terms. For example, since B and D are mutually exclusive, we can simplify next_state[1] to in & (A | B | D). Similarly, group terms for other next_state bits. The output is simply the D bit. This approach reduces complexity and redundant conditions while preserving the exact transition and output logic.
