To optimize the PPA, I aim to reduce logic complexity and switching activity by factoring next_state equations to minimize the number of terms and gate inputs. Since one-hot encoding is fixed, I'll simplify the OR expressions by extracting common sub-expressions, and reduce the number of input signals combined per gate. This reduces gate count and potentially lowers power due to less switching. Specifically:

- For next_state[1] = in & (A | B | D), factor as in & ((A | B) | D) and reuse intermediate signals.
- For next_state[0] = ~in & (A | C), next_state[2] = ~in & (B | D), next_state[3] = in & C, share common signals where possible.
- This also improves readability and may help synthesis tools optimize better.

The output remains simply assigned as D, which is minimal.

Thus, the main improvement is factoring expressions with intermediate wires for shared terms to reduce gate input fan-ins and logic duplication.
