Factor common terms in next_state assignments to minimize logic gates and reduce switching activity. For example, next_state[0] can be (A|C) & ~in, next_state[1] can be (A|B|D) & in, and next_state[2] can be (B|D) & ~in, which reduces gate count and critical path length.
