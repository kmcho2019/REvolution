The existing solution is already clean and efficient, but a minor optimization can be applied to slightly reduce the number of slice operations and possibly improve synthesis efficiency:

- For out_both, instead of slicing in twice for `in[98:0] & in[99:1]`, we can create a shifted version of `in` once and AND it with `in[98:0]`, reducing redundant slicing.
- For out_any, similarly, perform a right shift once for `in` to get neighbors to the right.
- This reduces redundant slicing and might help synthesis tools optimize better.
- The out_different logic using concatenation is already optimal for wrap-around XOR.
- The assignments remain continuous and combinational.
- Maintain setting the boundary bits explicitly as zero.

This approach maintains the same functionality but streamlines the expression of neighbor bits, potentially marginally improving area and power by fewer intermediate nets.
