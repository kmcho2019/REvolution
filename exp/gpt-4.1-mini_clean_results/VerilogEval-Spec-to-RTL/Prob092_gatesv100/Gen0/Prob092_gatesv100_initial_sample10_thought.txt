Implement bitwise operations on the input vector according to the neighbor definitions:
- out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1] for i=0 to 98, and out_both[99] = 0 since in[99] has no left neighbor.
- out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1] for i=1 to 99, and out_any[0] = 0 since in[0] has no right neighbor.
- out_different[i] = in[i] ^ in[(i+1) mod 100] to wrap around, so out_different[99] = in[99] ^ in[0].
