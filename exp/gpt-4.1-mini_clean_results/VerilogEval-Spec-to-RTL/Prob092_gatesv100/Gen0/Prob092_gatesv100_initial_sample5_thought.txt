To implement the TopModule, I will create a module with a 100-bit input vector and three 100-bit output vectors. For out_both, each bit i (except 99) is the AND of in[i] and in[i+1]. For out_both[99], set to zero as there is no left neighbor. For out_any, each bit i (except 0) is the OR of in[i] and in[i-1]. For out_any[0], set to zero since no right neighbor. For out_different, wrap around the neighbor to the left, so for bit i, compare in[i] and in[(i+1) mod 100]. The XOR operation will be used for difference.
