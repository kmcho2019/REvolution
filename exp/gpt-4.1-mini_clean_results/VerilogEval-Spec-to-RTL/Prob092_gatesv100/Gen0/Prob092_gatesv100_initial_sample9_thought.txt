Implement the TopModule with three output vectors of 100 bits each. For out_both, each bit (except the last) is the AND of in[i] and in[i+1], with the last bit forced to zero. For out_any, each bit (except the first) is the OR of in[i] and in[i-1], with the first bit forced to zero. For out_different, treat the vector as circular and compare each bit with its left neighbor using XOR; the left neighbor of in[0] is in[99].
