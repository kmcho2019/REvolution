For out_both: For bits 0 to 98, out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1]; out_both[99] = 0 as specified.

For out_any: For bits 1 to 99, out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1]; out_any[0] = 0 as specified.

For out_different: Since wrapping is needed, neighbor to the left of in[0] is in[99].
For all bits i, out_different[i] = (in[i] != in[(i+1) mod 100]), which is the XOR of in[i] and in[(i+1) mod 100].
