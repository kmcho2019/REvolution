We can simplify by expressing all outputs with uniform vector operations using shifts to represent neighbors. For out_both, shift in left by 1 bit and AND with in (except for the MSB, which is zero). For out_any, shift in right by 1 bit and OR with in (except for the LSB, which is zero). For out_different, shift in right by 1 bit with wrap-around and XOR with in. This removes explicit slicing for indices and makes the code more uniform and concise.
