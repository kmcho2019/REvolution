Combine the clarity of explicit per-bit handling with the efficiency of vector operations by using generate-for loops with local wires to assign outputs bitwise. Use continuous assignments (wire outputs) rather than reg to keep combinational logic concise and enable parallel synthesis. Handle boundary conditions explicitly within the generate block for clear and maintainable code. This approach fuses the explicitness and modularity of the loop-based method with the performance and area benefits of vector operations, yielding a design that is both readable and optimized.
