To optimize for power and area, we reduce shift operations by directly wiring neighboring bits using concatenations and bitwise operations. For out_both, instead of shifting right, we concatenate in[98:0] with a zero bit at LSB to align neighbors. For out_any, similarly concatenate zero at MSB and in[99:1] for neighbors. For out_different, rotate by concatenating in[0] with in[99:1] is already efficient. This approach reduces logic depth and switching power since shifts are replaced by direct wiring. Masking bits is naturally handled by zero padding in concatenations.
