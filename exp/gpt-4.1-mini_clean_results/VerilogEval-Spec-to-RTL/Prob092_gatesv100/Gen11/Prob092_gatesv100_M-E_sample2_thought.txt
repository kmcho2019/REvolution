Instead of directly using vector slicing, we can perform the neighbor checks by generating shifted versions of the input vector and combining them accordingly. For out_both, shift `in` left by one to align each bit with its left neighbor, then AND them; set the MSB to zero. For out_any, shift `in` right by one for the neighbor to the right, OR with the original, and set LSB to zero. For out_different, utilize XOR with a rotated left shift of `in` (to represent the left neighbor with wrap-around). This approach uses shifts and bitwise operations explicitly for clarity and differs structurally from the slice-based implementation.
