The original design is already efficient and clean with vectorized bitwise operations and explicit boundary handling. To optimize PPA further, especially power, I will insert local wire signals to help the synthesizer potentially optimize logic sharing and reduce switching activity by reusing intermediate signals (neighbors), though modern tools often handle that automatically. This explicit intermediate assignment can sometimes help synthesis in recognizing shared subexpressions. Also, to minimize glitching and balance logic depth, I split the expressions cleanly and group neighbors once. Area is unlikely to improve significantly, but power might benefit from less repeated fanout of inputs. Performance remains similar since only combinational bitwise logic is involved. Comments are added for clarity.

Overall, the optimization is subtle and targets synthesis tools' ability to optimize, given that the original code was already near-optimal at the RTL level.
