Use vector-level operations and bit-shifting to compute neighbor relations directly without per-bit generate loops or conditional assignments. For out_both, shift the input left by one to align neighbors and AND with the original vector, setting the MSB to zero since it has no left neighbor. For out_any, shift the input right by one to align neighbors and OR with the original vector, setting the LSB to zero as it has no right neighbor. For out_different, perform a circular left shift by one (wrap-around) and XOR with the original vector. This eliminates all conditionals and generate loops, leading to simpler, cleaner, and more efficient code.
