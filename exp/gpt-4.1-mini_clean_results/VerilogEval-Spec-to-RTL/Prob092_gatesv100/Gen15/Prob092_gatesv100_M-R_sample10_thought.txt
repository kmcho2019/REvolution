Refactor the design to use continuous assign statements and vector slicing instead of an always block with loops.  
By leveraging vector operations, we eliminate explicit loops and use direct bitwise operations with shifted vectors to compute outputs efficiently.  
This approach reduces logic complexity, improves synthesis results (PPA), and produces cleaner, more concise code.  
Outputs can be declared as wires (instead of reg) since the logic is purely combinational and directly assigned.  
Special cases at vector boundaries are handled explicitly by concatenating zeros or wrapping with modular indexing.  
The wrap-around difference output is implemented by XORing the entire input vector with itself rotated left by 1 bit, creating a neat and efficient solution.
