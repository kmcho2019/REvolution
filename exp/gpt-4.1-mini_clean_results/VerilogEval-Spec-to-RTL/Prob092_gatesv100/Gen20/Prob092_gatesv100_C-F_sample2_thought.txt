Fuse the clarity of explicit per-bit logic with the efficiency of vectorized continuous assignments by using a generate-for loop combined with wire outputs and assign statements for most bits, while handling boundary conditions separately. This approach makes boundary conditions explicit and easy to verify, while letting synthesis tools optimize parallel bitwise logic for the bulk of the bits, improving readability, synthesizability, and maintainability. Using generate and assign within a generate block avoids reg and procedural blocks, enabling parallelism and optimization like Example 1, but retains the clarity of looping and conditionals from Example 2.
