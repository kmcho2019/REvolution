We can simplify by uniformly handling the boundary bits using concatenation and bitwise operations without partial slicing. For out_both, shift 'in' left by 1 bit and AND with 'in', then set MSB zero. For out_any, shift 'in' right by 1 bit and OR with 'in', then set LSB zero. For out_different, XOR 'in' with a rotated version formed by shifting right by 1 bit and placing MSB at LSB.
