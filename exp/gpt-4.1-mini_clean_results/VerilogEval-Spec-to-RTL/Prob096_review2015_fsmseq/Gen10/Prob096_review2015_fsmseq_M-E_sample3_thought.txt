Implement a Moore FSM using binary-encoded states that track the progress of matching the sequence '1101'. Use a simple 3-bit state variable representing the number of matched bits. Transition to next states based on the current state and input bit, and assert start_shifting output once the full sequence is detected. Once detected, the FSM locks into a sticky detected state to hold start_shifting high indefinitely until reset. All transitions and output assertions occur synchronously on the clock's positive edge with a synchronous active-high reset.

This binary encoding reduces flip-flop count compared to one-hot encoding. The output start_shifting is registered (Moore output) for timing stability and synchrony, and this design avoids clock gating or clock enable logic, keeping the FSM simple and easy to synthesize.
