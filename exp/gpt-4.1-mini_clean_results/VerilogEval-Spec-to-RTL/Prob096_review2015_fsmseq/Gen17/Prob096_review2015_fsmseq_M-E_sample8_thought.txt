Implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "1101" using a one-hot encoding scheme for improved timing at the expense of area. The FSM outputs start_shifting as soon as the last bit of the sequence is detected (Mealy output) and then latches the output high forever by transitioning into a final locked state that holds start_shifting. The FSM uses synchronous active-high reset, and all state transitions occur on the rising edge of clk. Using one-hot encoding simplifies next-state logic and may improve performance. The output is registered along with the state to avoid glitches.
