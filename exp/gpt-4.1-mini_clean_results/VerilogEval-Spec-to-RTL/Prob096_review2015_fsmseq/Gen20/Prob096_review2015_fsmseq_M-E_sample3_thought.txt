Implement the sequence detector using a Mealy FSM with binary state encoding for compactness. The FSM will have four states representing how much of the sequence "1101" has been matched so far. The output start_shifting is generated immediately upon detecting the last '1' in the sequence within the next-state logic (Mealy output). After detection, the FSM transitions into a special "detected" state where start_shifting stays asserted indefinitely until reset. This approach reduces the number of flip-flops compared to one-hot encoding, potentially lowering area and power, and generates the output faster due to Mealy output style. The synchronous reset initializes the FSM. Blocking assignments are used in combinational logic for clarity.
