Implement a Mealy FSM to detect the sequence "1101" with fewer states by leveraging output assertion on transitions, enabling immediate start_shifting assertion as soon as the last bit is seen. Use a 2-bit state encoding to reduce flip-flops. The start_shifting output is latched once asserted by a register so it remains high forever until synchronous reset. This approach detects the sequence on the clock edge following the last bit and captures start_shifting in a set-reset register, minimizing logic and area while keeping the output stable as a registered signal. The synchronous reset ensures proper initialization.
