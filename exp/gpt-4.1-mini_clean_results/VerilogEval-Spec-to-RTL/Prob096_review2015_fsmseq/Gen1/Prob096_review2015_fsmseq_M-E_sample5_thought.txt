Implement a Moore FSM with five one-hot encoded states representing the progress of matching the sequence "1101":
- IDLE: no bits matched yet
- ONE: matched '1'
- ONE_ONE: matched "11"
- ONE_ONE_ZERO: matched "110"
- MATCHED: sequence "1101" detected, start_shifting asserted forever

On each clock edge, transition between states based on the input bit. When the FSM reaches the MATCHED state, start_shifting is set to 1 and held until reset. Reset synchronously returns the FSM to IDLE and clears start_shifting.

Using one-hot encoding simplifies the next-state logic and avoids enum or parameter casting issues. This approach ensures easy-to-understand state transitions and clean synchronous reset behavior.
