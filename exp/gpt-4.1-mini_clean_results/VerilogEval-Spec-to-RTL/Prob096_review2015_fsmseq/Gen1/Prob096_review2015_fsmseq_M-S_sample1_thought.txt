Implement the FSM using simple localparam state encodings instead of enums for better compatibility.
Use four states representing partial matches of the '1101' sequence:
S0 (no match), S1 (matched '1'), S11 (matched '11'), S110 (matched '110').
On detecting the full sequence (when current state is S110 and input data is 1), assert start_shifting permanently until reset.
Reset is synchronous active high and clears the FSM and start_shifting.
All sequential logic triggers on posedge clk.
