The root cause of the errors is the strict typing of SystemVerilog enums requiring explicit casts during assignment, which some tools may not support or require verbose syntax. To ensure maximum compatibility and simplicity, I will replace the typedef enum with simple `reg [2:0]` state encoding using localparams for each state. This approach avoids type casting issues. The FSM logic remains unchanged, and `start_shifting` is latched on sequence detection and held until reset. Reset remains synchronous and active high, and all sequential logic is on the positive edge of `clk`.
