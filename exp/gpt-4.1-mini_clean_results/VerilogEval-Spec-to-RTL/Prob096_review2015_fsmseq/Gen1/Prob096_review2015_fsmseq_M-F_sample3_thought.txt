To fix the compilation issues caused by SystemVerilog enum typedef usage, rewrite the FSM using plain Verilog with localparam state encoding. This avoids compatibility issues with synthesis tools or simulators that do not fully support enums. 

The FSM will have four states encoded as 2-bit values:
- S0 (00): No partial match
- S1 (01): matched '1'
- S11 (10): matched '11'
- S110 (11): matched '110'

Transitions are based on the input bit 'data'. When the sequence 1101 is detected (i.e., when current state is S110 and data=1), assert start_shifting to 1 forever until synchronous reset. Reset clears state and start_shifting.

Use synchronous reset and update state and output on the positive edge of clk. This implementation is fully synthesizable and compatible with plain Verilog tools.
