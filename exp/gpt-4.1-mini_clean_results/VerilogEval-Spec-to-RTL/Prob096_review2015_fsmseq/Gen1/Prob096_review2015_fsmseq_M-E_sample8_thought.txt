Implement the sequence detector using one-hot encoding for states to simplify combinational logic and potentially improve timing. Represent each matching state by a single bit register to reduce decoding complexity. Use a separate output register that is set when the sequence is detected and held until synchronous reset. The FSM will have five one-hot states: IDLE (no match), S1 (matched '1'), S11 (matched '11'), S110 (matched '110'), and FOUND (sequence detected). On detecting the sequence, move to FOUND and latch the output start_shifting to 1 until reset.
