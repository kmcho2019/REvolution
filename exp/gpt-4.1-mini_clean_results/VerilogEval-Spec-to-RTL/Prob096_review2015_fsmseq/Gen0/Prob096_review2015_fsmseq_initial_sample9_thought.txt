Implement a Moore state machine that detects the sequence 1101 on the serial input 'data'. The FSM moves through states corresponding to how much of the sequence has been matched. When the sequence is detected, the FSM moves to a final "found" state where the output start_shifting is asserted high permanently (until reset). The reset is synchronous and active high, so all registers and states are reset on the clock edge if reset is high. The output start_shifting is derived from the FSM state.
