I will design a finite-state machine (FSM) that detects the sequence "1101" in the input stream. The FSM will have states representing how many bits of the sequence have been matched so far. When the sequence "1101" is detected, start_shifting will be set to 1 and latched until reset is asserted. Reset is synchronous and active high. The FSM transitions on the positive edge of clk. The output start_shifting is set asynchronously in the FSM but latched synchronously.
