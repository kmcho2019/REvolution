To improve PPA over the given solution, I will implement a one-hot state encoding FSM which often results in faster combinational logic and simpler next-state decoding at the cost of more flip-flops. Given only 5 states, the area increase is minimal but the critical path for next-state logic usually becomes shorter, improving timing.

Additionally, instead of a combinational clock enable (ce), I'll rely on the synchronous reset and straightforward state register updates without gating. This may slightly increase dynamic power but simplifies timing paths and improves worst negative slack (WNS).

The output will be asserted directly from the sticky final state flip-flop (state 4), maintaining the same sticky output behavior.

This tradeoff targets improving timing (reducing WNS and TNS), with a slight area increase, accepting minor power overhead, aiming for better overall PPA metrics.
