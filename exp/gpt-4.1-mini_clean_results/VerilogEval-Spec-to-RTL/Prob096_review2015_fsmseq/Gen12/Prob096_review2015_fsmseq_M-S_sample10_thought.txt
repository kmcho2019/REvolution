Since the FSM has only 5 states, we can use a smaller 3-bit binary encoding as before, but simplify the next-state logic by directly encoding transitions with fewer intermediate states and removing the final state latching behavior. Instead, keep start_shifting as a registered output that is set high immediately upon detection of the sequence and stays high until reset. This eliminates the need to latch the FSM in a final state, simplifying next state logic to only the sequence detection, while start_shifting is held separately. This reduces complexity and makes the design easier to understand without losing functionality.
