To optimize the existing FSM for better PPA while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will merge the combinational logic for next_state and sequence_found into a single always_comb block. This reduces redundant logic evaluations and can minimize glitches and area by sharing comparisons. Also, I will encode states in one-hot since there are only four states, which often reduces combinational logic complexity and can improve timing in FPGAs or certain ASIC flows. One-hot encoding may increase area slightly but with four states this is minimal. The start_shifting signal will be latched synchronously as before. This approach balances clarity, timing, and area well for this small FSM.
