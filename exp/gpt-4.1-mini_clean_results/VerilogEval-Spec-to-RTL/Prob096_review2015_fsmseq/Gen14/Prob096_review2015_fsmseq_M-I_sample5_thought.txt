To optimize the FSM for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will focus on simplifying the state encoding and next-state logic while maintaining synchronous reset and a glitch-free output.

- Convert the FSM to one-hot encoding: This can improve performance by reducing the complexity of next-state logic and speeding up state decoding at the expense of slightly increased area. Given the original area is small (33), a small increase is acceptable for performance gain.

- Simplify the next-state transitions using one-hot logic with minimal combinational logic, reducing logic depth for better WNS and TNS.

- Keep `start_shifting` output as a registered signal from the detected state to avoid glitches.

- Maintain synchronous active-high reset.

- The one-hot FSM states are S0 (no match), S1 (matched '1'), S11 (matched "11"), S110 (matched "110"), and S1101 (detected sequence 1101).

This approach optimizes for performance and power by reducing LUT depth and switching, with minor area increase due to one-hot encoding. It also simplifies synthesis mapping in FPGA flows, potentially improving timing and power usage.
