To improve PPA while keeping correctness, I will optimize the FSM encoding to one-hot. This reduces the combinational logic depth for next state logic, improving timing (smaller logic paths). The power may slightly increase due to more flip-flops toggling, but timing gains are often worth it in high-frequency designs. The area cost is minimal for 5 states (only 5 FFs needed).

Also, I will keep synchronous reset as requested for design robustness. Outputs remain Moore-type for glitch-free assertion.

The FSM states:
- S0: no match
- S1: matched '1'
- S11: matched "11"
- S110: matched "110"
- S1101: final detected state, latched indefinitely.

One-hot encoding makes next state logic a simple set of AND/OR gates, reducing path delay.

Overall, the one-hot FSM provides better timing at minor area and power overhead, improving the effective clock period and slack reported previously.
