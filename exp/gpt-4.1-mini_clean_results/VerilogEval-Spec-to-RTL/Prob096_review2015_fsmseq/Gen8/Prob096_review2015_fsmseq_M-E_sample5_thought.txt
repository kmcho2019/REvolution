Implement the FSM as a Mealy machine with a single-bit output register that captures whether the sequence 1101 has been detected yet. The FSM uses a compact 3-bit binary state encoding tracking the partial matches of the sequence. Instead of a sticky final state, use an output register that sets start_shifting to 1 the first time the sequence is found and holds it until reset. The output register is updated synchronously along with state on the rising clock edge with synchronous reset. This method simplifies output logic and allows start_shifting to be updated immediately upon sequence detection, while still holding it indefinitely until reset.
