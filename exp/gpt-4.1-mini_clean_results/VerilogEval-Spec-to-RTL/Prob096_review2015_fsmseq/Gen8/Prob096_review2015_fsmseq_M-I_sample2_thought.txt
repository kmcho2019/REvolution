To improve PPA, I will switch from one-hot encoding to binary encoding to reduce flip-flop count and area, which also tends to reduce dynamic power. The FSM remains a Moore machine with registered synchronous reset. This will reduce the number of registers from 5 to 3 (enough to encode 5 states). The next-state logic is adjusted accordingly. The output remains combinational and driven only by the final detected state. Binary encoding slightly increases combinational logic complexity but saves area and power. The synchronous reset and output behavior remain the same. This strikes a good balance between area/power reduction and acceptable timing for the sequence detection.
