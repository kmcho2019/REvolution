Implement the sequence detector as a Mealy FSM to allow immediate detection of the sequence 1101 on the input bit stream without waiting for the next clock cycle. Use binary-encoded states with localparam for clarity. The output `start_shifting` is a registered output (Moore-like), which once set, remains asserted until synchronous reset, making it sticky. To achieve this, add a separate flip-flop to hold the output latch state. The FSM transitions based on current input and state, and sets the latch when the sequence is detected. This approach blends the responsiveness of a Mealy machine with the stable output of a Moore machine by decoupling detection from output, allowing fast sequence recognition and stable sticky output with synchronous reset.
