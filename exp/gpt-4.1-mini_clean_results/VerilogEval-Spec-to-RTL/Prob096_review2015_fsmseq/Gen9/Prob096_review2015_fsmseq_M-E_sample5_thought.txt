Implement the FSM using binary-encoded states and a fully registered output signal `start_shifting`. The FSM searches for the sequence "1101" by transitioning through states representing partial matches. Once the sequence is detected, the output `start_shifting` is set synchronously high and held until a synchronous reset. This approach uses fewer flip-flops than one-hot encoding, improving area and power efficiency. The output is registered separately to decouple output logic from the state register, which can aid timing closure. The FSM transitions and output update all occur on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset.
