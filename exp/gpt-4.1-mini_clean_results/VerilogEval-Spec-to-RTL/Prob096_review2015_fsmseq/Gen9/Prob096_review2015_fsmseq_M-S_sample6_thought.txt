Use a simpler FSM encoding with just 3 states to track the sequence progress: IDLE (no match), S1 (matched first '1'), and S2 (matched "11"), plus a FOUND state to indicate detection. This reduces the state count and the complexity of the transition logic. The FSM transitions on the input bits accordingly and stays in FOUND state indefinitely once the sequence "1101" is detected, asserting start_shifting. The synchronous reset returns the FSM to IDLE. This approach simplifies the design by minimizing states and next-state logic without affecting the detection functionality.
