Implement the sequence detector using a Moore FSM with binary-encoded states that represent the longest prefix of the sequence "1101" matched so far. Use four states (S0 to S3) corresponding to 0 to 3 matched bits and a special "FOUND" state once the full sequence is detected. The output start_shifting is asserted only in the FOUND state and held high until a synchronous reset. Use a single always block for both state transitions and synchronous reset, and assign output combinationally. This approach keeps the FSM minimal, simple, and easily extendable. Unlike previous designs, reuse the state bits to encode the partial matches compactly, ensuring minimal logic and registers.
