Simplify the FSM by reducing the number of states from 5 to 4 by merging the final detected state into a latched output flag. Keep only the states needed to track the partial matches of the sequence 1101. Once the sequence is detected, set a register output `start_shifting` which stays asserted until reset. This removes the need for a dedicated "detected" state and output comparison, and avoids a separate output combinational logic. The synchronous reset and positive-edge clock triggering remain the same.
