To improve the PPA metrics, especially timing (tns and wns), we can convert the FSM from binary encoding to one-hot encoding. One-hot encoding usually improves the speed of FSMs by simplifying next-state decoding logic at the expense of a few more flip-flops, which is acceptable here since the FSM has only 5 states. This reduces logic complexity in the critical path and can help the timing slack. The synchronous reset remains unchanged for correct behavior. The output start_shifting is driven directly from the final state's flip-flop, which simplifies combinational logic. This approach may slightly increase area but can enhance timing and reduce power by simplifying logic cones. The FSM transitions are explicitly handled for clarity and performance.
