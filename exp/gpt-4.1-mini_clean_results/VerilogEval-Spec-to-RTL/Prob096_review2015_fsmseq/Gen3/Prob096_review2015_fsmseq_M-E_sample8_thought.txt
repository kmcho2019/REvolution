Use a one-hot encoded FSM with five states to detect the sequence '1101' bit-by-bit. 
States represent how much of the sequence has been matched so far:
- S0: no match yet,
- S1: matched '1',
- S11: matched '11',
- S110: matched '110',
- S_FOUND: sequence '1101' found.

When the sequence is found, set start_shifting = 1 and latch it until synchronous reset.
The FSM transitions on positive clock edge with synchronous reset, using one-hot encoding for simpler combinational logic.
