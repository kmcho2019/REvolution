Implement the sequence detector using a Mealy-type FSM encoded with one-hot states. The FSM will have five states: IDLE (no match), S1 (matched first '1'), S11 (matched '11'), S110 (matched '110'), and DETECT (sequence found). The output start_shifting will be a registered output that is asynchronously set when sequence "1101" is detected and latched high until synchronous reset. By making output depend directly on inputs and states (Mealy), the output can assert earlier in the clock cycle. One-hot encoding makes state transitions and decoding straightforward, which can improve timing and synthesis results. The synchronous reset will clear both state and output. The FSM sequential logic is all synchronous with non-blocking assignments, and output is latched after detection.
