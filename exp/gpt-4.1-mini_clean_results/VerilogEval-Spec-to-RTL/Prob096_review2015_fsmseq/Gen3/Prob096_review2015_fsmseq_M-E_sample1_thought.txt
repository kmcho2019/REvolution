Implement the sequence detector using a Mealy FSM instead of Moore, to immediately assert start_shifting at the clock edge when the final sequence bit is detected, potentially reducing latency by one cycle. Use a 3-bit binary encoded state machine with five states representing the matching progress. The output start_shifting is generated combinationally as part of the next-state logic (Mealy output).
