To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will merge the combinational next_state and output logic into a single always_comb block to reduce logic depth and glitch potential, which can improve timing and power slightly. I will also encode the state with 3 bits using one-hot encoding to enable faster state transitions at the expense of minimal area increase, which is negligible for four states. This can reduce the critical path and thus improve timing (TNS and WNS). The output start_shifting remains a registered output that latches once the sequence is detected. The reset remains synchronous and active high. Overall, merging logic and adopting one-hot encoding typically yields better performance in small FSMs.
