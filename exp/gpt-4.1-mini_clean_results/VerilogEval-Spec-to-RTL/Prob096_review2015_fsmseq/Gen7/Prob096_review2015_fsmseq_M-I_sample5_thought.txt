To improve PPA, I will use one-hot encoding for the 4 FSM states to reduce combinational logic depth, improving performance and potentially reducing power. Also, I will register the output `start_shifting` in the FSM sequential block to avoid glitches and set it immediately when the sequence is detected, then hold it until reset. One-hot encoding will increase area slightly due to 4 flip-flops instead of 2, but the tradeoff usually benefits timing in FPGA. I will also simplify the next state logic using if-else to make it clearer. Power could be further optimized by gating the clock or enable, but that adds complexity and is optional here.
