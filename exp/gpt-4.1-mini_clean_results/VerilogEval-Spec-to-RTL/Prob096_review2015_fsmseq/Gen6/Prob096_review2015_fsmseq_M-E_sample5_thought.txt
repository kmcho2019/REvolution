Implement a sequence detector FSM using one-hot encoding for clarity and simplicity. The FSM will have five states representing the progress of matching each bit in "1101". Transition between states based on input data, and when the full sequence is matched, assert a registered flag `start_shifting` permanently until reset. Use synchronous active-high reset and update outputs only on clock edges. One-hot encoding reduces combinational logic for next-state decoding and simplifies debugging.
