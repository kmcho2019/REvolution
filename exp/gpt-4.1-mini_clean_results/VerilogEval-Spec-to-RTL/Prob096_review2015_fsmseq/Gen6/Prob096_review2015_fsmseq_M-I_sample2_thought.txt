To improve PPA, I will optimize the FSM by using one-hot encoding for states, which can reduce combinational logic depth and improve timing (TNS and WNS) at the cost of a few more registers—acceptable here given only 4 states. Also, I will minimize toggling of start_shifting by only setting it when the sequence is found and then holding it. The synchronous reset remains. The one-hot FSM allows simpler next state logic via direct transitions and reduces logic complexity, often beneficial for FPGAs and some ASIC flows.
