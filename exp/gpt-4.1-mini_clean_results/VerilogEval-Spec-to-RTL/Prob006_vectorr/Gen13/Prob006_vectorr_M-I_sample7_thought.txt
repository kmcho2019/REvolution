The existing design is already clean, modular, and synthesizes efficiently. However, for a slight potential improvement in area and power (especially switching activity), the bit reversal can be implemented using a single continuous assignment with explicit concatenation of bits in reverse order. This removes the generate loop and intermediate wire, slightly reducing resource usage and possibly simplifying netlist connectivity. Since the input is fixed at 8 bits in TopModule, writing out the reversed bits explicitly enhances clarity and may marginally improve synthesis results without losing parameterization modularity in BitReverser. The BitReverser module is maintained for modularity but simplified internally.

This approach:
- Uses a single assign with concatenation rather than generate-for loop
- Avoids intermediate wire and multiple assignments
- Keeps design fully combinational and synthesizable
- Retains clear, reusable BitReverser module interface
- Slightly reduces switching nodes and netlist complexity, which might reduce dynamic power and area minimally
