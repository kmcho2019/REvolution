Fuse the modularity and parameterization of the functional approach with the synthesis-friendly and explicit clarity of structural assignments by using a generate-for loop for bit-wise continuous assignments. This approach avoids procedural blocks and functions, directly connecting each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit via explicit assignments inside a generate block.

This ensures:
- Maximum clarity and simplicity for synthesis tools, often resulting in minimal delay and area.
- Purely combinational continuous assignments, aiding low power consumption.
- Easy parameterization and scalability to other widths.
- No intermediate procedural variables or function overhead, while maintaining readable, maintainable code.

The TopModule instantiates this parameterized BitReverser with WIDTH=8, meeting the requirements succinctly and efficiently.
