Since the original solution is already clean, synthesizable, and modular, and the PPA metrics are very good (zero timing slacks, very low power, and minimal area), further improvements for 8-bit reversal are minimal.

To optimize for area and power, removing the generate loop and using a direct continuous assignment with a single concatenation operation can reduce logic and wiring overhead. This approach is simpler for a fixed 8-bit width and can potentially result in slightly smaller area and marginally lower power since it avoids multiple assign statements.

Thus, the improved design will remove the BitReverser module and implement the bit reversal directly in TopModule by assigning `out` as the concatenation of `in` bits in reverse order in one statement.

This simplification should improve area slightly and maintain the same performance and power.
