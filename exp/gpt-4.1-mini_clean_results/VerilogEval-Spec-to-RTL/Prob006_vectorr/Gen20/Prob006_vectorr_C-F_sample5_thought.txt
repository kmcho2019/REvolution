To create a superior solution combining the strengths of both examples, I will implement a parameterized BitReverser module that uses a generate-for loop with continuous assignments for each output bit, ensuring purely combinational logic for optimal power and performance. Additionally, I will provide an optional always_comb block with a simple for-loop (commented out) to show an alternative procedural approach that is easy to read and modify if needed. The TopModule instantiates this parameterized module for 8 bits, keeping the design modular and scalable.

This fusion ensures:
- Clarity and maintainability via the generate loop and continuous assignments (best for synthesis and minimal toggling power)
- Availability of a procedural version (commented) for easier understanding or future modifications
- Parameterization for easy width changes and reusability
- Clean separation of combinational logic, avoiding registers that are unnecessarily driven combinationally

This approach preserves the benefits of low power, minimal area, and zero timing bottlenecks, while providing a clear and flexible code structure.
