Implement TopModule using a for-generate loop directly within the module to reverse bits without an intermediate module. This avoids the need for parameters and extra modules, making the design straightforward and tightly scoped. The loop iterates over each bit of the output vector and assigns it to the corresponding reversed input bit index, providing a clean, scalable, and synthesizable combinational logic block. This approach is easy to read and maintain, and synthesizers handle such generate loops efficiently.
