Implement the bit reversal by using a bitwise reduction approach with concatenation of individual bits extracted in reverse order directly in the output assignment. This approach uses a single continuous assignment with explicit bit indexing without generate loops or instantiations. It is simple and concise for fixed-width vectors like 8 bits. This method clearly shows how each input bit maps to the output bit by direct concatenation and manual ordering, making the intent explicit and easy to read for a fixed-size signal.
