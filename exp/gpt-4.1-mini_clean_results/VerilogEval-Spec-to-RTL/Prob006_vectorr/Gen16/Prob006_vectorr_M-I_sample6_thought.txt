Since the original design is already clean, fully combinational, and parameterized, the main room for improvement for an 8-bit fixed-width module is minimal. To optimize area slightly, we can eliminate the parameterized generate loop and directly assign reversed bits in a single continuous assignment with bit slicing. This reduces the number of individual assign statements and may help synthesis tools optimize wiring. It also simplifies the code by removing the generate loop and parameterization which is unnecessary for a fixed 8-bit width.

Performance will remain combinational and timing unchanged. Power might improve slightly due to simpler wiring. Area may improve marginally due to fewer assign statements and simpler structure.

This small tradeoff loses generality but gains slight PPA improvement for the fixed 8-bit case.
