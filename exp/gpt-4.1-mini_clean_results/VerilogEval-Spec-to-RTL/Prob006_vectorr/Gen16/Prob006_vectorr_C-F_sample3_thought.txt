Fuse the modular, parameterized design of the BitReverser with the clarity and simplicity of a single continuous assignment using Verilog's bit-slice concatenation. Instead of a generate-for loop, use the bit-slice operator and concatenation to reverse the bits in one expression, which results in a more concise and elegant design without sacrificing modularity or parameterization.

This approach keeps the design purely combinational and scalable, minimizes code complexity, and improves readability by explicitly showing the reversal in a single statement. The TopModule simply instantiates this compact BitReverser for 8 bits.

This fusion leverages the modularity and scalability from the examples, and combines it with a more succinct and direct bit manipulation style that synthesis tools can optimize efficiently.
