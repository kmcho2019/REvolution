The existing design is clean, modular, and efficient with zero timing slack, minimal area, and low power. The generate-for loop assigns each bit with reversed indexing, which is optimal for clarity and synthesis.

To improve power and area marginally, I can replace the generate-for loop with a single continuous assignment using a concatenation of input bits in reversed order. This eliminates generate constructs and potentially reduces the amount of intermediate logic inferred by synthesis tools, possibly lowering area and dynamic power slightly.

The TopModule will use this simplified combinational assignment directly, avoiding the overhead of an instantiated module. This reduces hierarchy and might benefit synthesis and PPA metrics.

Overall, the change trades some parameterization flexibility for compactness and potentially lower area/power, which is beneficial here as WIDTH is fixed at 8.
