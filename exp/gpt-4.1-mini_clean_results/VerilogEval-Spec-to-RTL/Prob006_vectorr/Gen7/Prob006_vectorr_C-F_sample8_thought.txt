To create a superior solution, we combine the clarity and procedural style of the always block with the area and power efficiency of a continuous assignment. Specifically, we use a generate-for loop with continuous assignments to each output bit. This approach removes the need for reg and procedural blocks, allowing synthesis tools to infer simple wiring connections with no registers or logic overhead, minimizing area and power while maintaining clarity and scalability. The generate block explicitly shows the bit reversal mapping at compile time, making the code readable and easily parameterizable for wider vectors. This also avoids the overhead of loop variables and procedural assignments in always blocks, improving synthesis optimization potential while staying fully combinational and clear.
