The original design is already clean and efficient by using a generate-for loop for bit reversal. However, since the bit width is fixed at 8 bits, we can simplify the design and potentially improve area and power by eliminating the generate loop and parameterization overhead entirely. Instead, we use a simple continuous assignment with a concatenation that directly reverses the bits. This should result in minimal wiring and simpler synthesized logic, reducing area and possibly power due to simpler netlist, while maintaining zero timing overhead. The TopModule becomes self-contained with no module instantiation, making it easier to integrate and slightly reducing hierarchy overhead.
