Implement the bit reversal by using a simple continuous assignment with a single concatenation expression, explicitly enumerating bits in reverse order. This approach avoids the generate loop and multiple assign statements by utilizing Verilog's powerful vector concatenation operator to map all bits in one statement. It is concise, easy to read, and synthesizes to the same hardware as the loop-based version, but may provide slightly cleaner synthesis results with potentially lower power due to reduced intermediate signal toggling. This design is fully combinational and straightforward for synthesis tools.
