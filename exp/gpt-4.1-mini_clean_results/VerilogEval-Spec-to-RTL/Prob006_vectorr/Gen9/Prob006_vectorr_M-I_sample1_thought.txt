Given the original design is already clean, fully combinational, and optimal for an 8-bit width with negligible impact on PPA, improvements for such a small width are minimal. However, to reduce any slight overhead caused by the generate loop and multiple assign statements, we can replace the generate loop with a single continuous assignment using bit slicing and concatenation. This approach eliminates the generate construct and multiple assignments, potentially reducing area and power slightly by having a simpler netlist.

This solution still synthesizes to the same functionality with zero delay and minimal power, but is simpler and more concise, which may marginally improve PPA metrics for small fixed widths like 8 bits.

No pipeline or registered outputs are necessary due to purely combinational requirement. This also preserves the fully combinational nature with no clock dependency.
