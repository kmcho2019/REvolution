Implement the bit reversal purely using continuous assignments by directly wiring each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit. This approach avoids procedural blocks entirely, relying on structural wiring which is simple, synthesizes optimally, and consumes minimal resources. The module is non-parameterized (fixed at 8 bits) as specified. The TopModule does this bit reversal internally without instantiating a submodule. This clean, direct connection method reduces complexity and latency by eliminating loops and always blocks. It also improves readability for fixed width vectors, making the reversal logic instantly visible in the port assignments.
