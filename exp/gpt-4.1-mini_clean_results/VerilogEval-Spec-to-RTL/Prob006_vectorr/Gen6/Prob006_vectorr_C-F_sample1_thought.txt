Fuse the clarity and conciseness of explicit concatenation with the scalability and maintainability of a generate-for loop. Use a parameterized generate block to reverse the bits structurally, ensuring clear intent and easy vector width adaptation. This approach combines the explicitness of bit-level control seen in the generate loop with the clean structural style of concatenation, improving readability and easing future width changes without hardcoding each bit. It avoids potential confusion from manual concatenation order while producing a purely combinational and synthesizable bit-reversal circuit.
