Refactor the Moore FSM to use a one-hot style state encoding and separate output logic with continuous assignments (`assign`) rather than registering outputs inside a clocked always block. 

The FSM still tracks the count of consecutive ones in states S0 to S6, with transient DISC and FLAG states for the one-cycle output pulses, and an ERROR state. 

The state is updated synchronously with the clock and reset. The outputs disc, flag, and err are purely combinational and depend only on the current state (Moore), via continuous assignments.

This clearly separates the sequential logic (state register and next-state logic) from output logic, which is combinational and simplifies timing analysis.

Also use `localparam` for state definitions and avoid SystemVerilog typedefs to maximize compatibility.

This approach retains the exact FSM behavior and output timing, but with a cleaner separation of logic and no clocked output registers.
