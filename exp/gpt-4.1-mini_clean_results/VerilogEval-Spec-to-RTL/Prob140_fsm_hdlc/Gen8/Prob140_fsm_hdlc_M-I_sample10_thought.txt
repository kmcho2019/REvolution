To optimize PPA (performance, power, area), reduce the number of states and simplify next-state logic by representing the consecutive count of 1s as a 3-bit counter (0 to 6) plus special states for discarding, flag, and error. This allows arithmetic-based next-state logic instead of a large case statement. Use a compact FSM with fewer states and simple conditions.

Outputs (disc, flag, err) are asserted only in dedicated states representing the detection conditions, keeping Moore FSM style. Separate output signals as simple combinational logic based on state.

This reduces combinational logic complexity and state register size, improving area and potentially timing and power without changing functionality.
