To optimize the FSM for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will:

1. Use one-hot encoding for the FSM states. Since there are only 10 states, one-hot encoding simplifies next-state logic and potentially reduces logic depth, which can improve timing.

2. Replace the large combinational case statement with more straightforward logic that counts consecutive ones up to 6 and detects conditions for disc, flag, and err states.

3. Implement outputs as registered signals inside the synchronous always block to reduce glitches and switching activity, improving power.

4. Combine disc/flag/err output flags with the state transitions to use fewer combinational signals.

5. Keep synchronous reset and positive edge clock triggering.

This approach reduces combinational complexity by exploiting one-hot encoding and simple counters, reducing critical path delay and switching power while maintaining a clean Moore FSM design.
