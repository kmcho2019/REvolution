Implement a classic Moore FSM with explicit named states representing the count of consecutive ones seen, ranging from S0 (zero ones) to S7 (seven or more ones/error state). The FSM transitions on each input bit, tracking consecutive ones. When a zero is detected after five or six ones, it outputs disc or flag respectively on the next cycle. Upon entering the error state (7+ ones), err is asserted. The outputs are registered for a single cycle, synchronous and starting one cycle after the detected condition, matching Moore machine behavior. The reset synchronously returns FSM to S0. This approach emphasizes clarity of states, direct state encoding, and straightforward combinational output and transition logic.
