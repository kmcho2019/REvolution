To optimize PPA while preserving correctness and clarity:

1. Use a simpler binary-encoded FSM with a 3-bit counter representing the count of consecutive ones (0 to 7).
2. Detect disc, flag, and err conditions by outputs combinationally derived from state and current input.
3. Outputs (disc, flag, err) will remain Moore-type, asserted for exactly one cycle after detecting the respective conditions.
4. The next-state logic will count consecutive ones, reset on zero input, and enter error state if count exceeds 6.
5. This reduces the state register width from 4 to 3 bits and simplifies next-state logic and output logic.
6. Eliminate separate disc/flag/error states, instead generate outputs based on the combination of state and input, ensuring outputs pulse one cycle later.
7. This approach lowers logic complexity and area, reduces switching for power, and improves timing by simplifying combinational paths.

The synchronous reset initializes the counter to 0 (no previous ones), consistent with previous design.

This will yield a smaller FSM with simpler logic, maintaining required output pulse timing and meeting functional spec.
