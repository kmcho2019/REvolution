Implement a Moore FSM with explicit states representing the count of consecutive ones (from zero up to 6 or more), plus outputs encoded per state. The FSM transitions on each input bit, updating the state according to the input (0 resets count; 1 increments count up to 7+). The outputs (disc, flag, err) are asserted based on the previous state and current input, meaning outputs are registered and delayed by one cycle after the detection condition, as required. This explicit state approach improves clarity and extendability, cleanly separating state transitions and output logic, and it naturally implements the synchronous reset by initializing to the "zero ones" state.
