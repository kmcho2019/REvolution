- S0 (0 ones), S1 (1 one), ..., S5 (5 ones), S5_disc (discard zero after 5 ones), S6 (6 ones), S6_flag (flag after 6 ones), S7 (error).

This structure cleanly separates outputs as Moore outputs depending only on the current state, and ensures output signals `disc` and `flag` are asserted exactly one cycle after detecting the condition.
