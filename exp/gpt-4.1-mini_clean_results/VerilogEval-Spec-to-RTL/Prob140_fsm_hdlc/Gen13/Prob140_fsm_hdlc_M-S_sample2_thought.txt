Simplify the FSM by using a 3-bit counter to track consecutive ones, but eliminate the prev_in register by basing output conditions only on current state and input. Since the FSM state directly encodes the count of consecutive ones, and the transition on input zero resets the count, we can detect the disc and flag conditions when transitioning from specific states to zero input.

Outputs (disc, flag, err) will be registered and asserted one cycle after detection to comply with the specification.

This reduces complexity by removing the additional prev_in register and simplifying output logic while preserving the synchronous one-cycle-late output assertion and overall functionality.
