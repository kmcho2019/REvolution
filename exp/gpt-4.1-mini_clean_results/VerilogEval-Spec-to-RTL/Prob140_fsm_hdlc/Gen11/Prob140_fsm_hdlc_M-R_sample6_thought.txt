Refactor the design into an explicit Moore FSM with named states representing the count of consecutive ones (from 0 to 7+). Each clock cycle, the FSM transitions depending on the input bit, incrementing the count on '1' and resetting on '0'. The outputs (disc, flag, err) are determined solely by the current FSM state, ensuring Moore behavior with outputs stable during the cycle. This structure makes the FSM behavior clearer and separates next state combinational logic from state and output registers. It also enables easier modification or expansion of the state machine.
