To optimize the existing FSM for better PPA while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will encode the states more compactly and simplify the next-state logic.

Improvement strategy:

1. Use a 3-bit state encoding representing the count of consecutive ones (0 to 6) plus three extra states for disc (SD), flag (SF), and error (SE) states encoded as 7, 4, and 5 respectively. This overlapping state numbering allows simpler next-state logic.

2. Consolidate the "consecutive ones count" states to a straightforward count (0 to 6), and when hitting disc/flag/error conditions, jump to dedicated states with outputs asserted.

3. Implement next-state logic with fewer conditions using arithmetic where possible (increment count on input=1, reset to zero on input=0, and special transitions to output states).

4. Maintain Moore outputs as combinational assignments from the current state.

This reduces the next_state logic complexity and state width from 4 bits to 3 bits, decreasing area and potentially improving timing and power.

The FSM synchronous reset still sets the state to zero (count=0) as before.

Overall, this makes the logic more concise and efficient without sacrificing functionality or clarity.
