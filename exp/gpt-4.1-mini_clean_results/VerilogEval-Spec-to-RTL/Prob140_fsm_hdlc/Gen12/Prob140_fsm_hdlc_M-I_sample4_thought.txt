To optimize the existing FSM design for better power and timing without changing its functionality:

- Add a clock enable signal that activates counting logic only when input `in` is high or when the counter is non-zero. This avoids unnecessary toggling when the input is zero and the counter is already zero, reducing switching activity and power.
- Keep the counter increment and saturation logic, but gate it with the clock enable to save power.
- Keep outputs as Moore signals derived from the registered counter and input, registered on clock edge as before.
- This approach reduces unnecessary state transitions and output toggling, improving power, and can also slightly reduce logic depth by not updating counter when no change needed.

This optimization maintains synchronous reset and single clock domain. It balances power reduction and timing without adding complex encoding or restructuring.
