Simplify the FSM by using a single 3-bit counter for consecutive ones (0 to 7+). On each clock, update the count based on input; reset count to zero on input=0. Then directly generate outputs in the sequential block by checking previous count and current input to produce one-cycle pulses for disc, flag, and err signals. This merges combinational and sequential logic, removing the separate next-state outputs and simplifying the design while preserving all functionality.
