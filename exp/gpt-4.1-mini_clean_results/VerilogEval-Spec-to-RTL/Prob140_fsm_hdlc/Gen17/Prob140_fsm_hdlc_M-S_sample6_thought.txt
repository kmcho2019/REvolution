Use a simple 3-bit counter to track consecutive ones and update it synchronously on every clock with reset. Combine next-state and output logic into one always block to minimize complexity, removing separate next-state variables. Outputs are asserted the cycle after detection as Moore FSM outputs. Saturate the count at 7 to detect error. The design keeps functionality intact but simplifies code by avoiding separate combinational next-state logic and using straightforward synchronous logic with direct assignments.
