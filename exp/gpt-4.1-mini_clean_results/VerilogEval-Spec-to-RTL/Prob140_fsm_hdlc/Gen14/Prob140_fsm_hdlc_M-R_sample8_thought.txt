Refactor the design by implementing an explicit Moore FSM with named states representing the number of consecutive 1s seen. This replaces the saturating counter with a state-encoded FSM. The FSM transitions on each clock edge based on the input bit and current state.

Outputs are generated purely as functions of the current state, preserving Moore output timing (one cycle after condition detection). The design uses synchronous state updates and combinational next-state logic, separating combinational and sequential logic clearly. This improves readability and aligns with classic FSM design principles.

The Moore FSM states explicitly track from zero up to seven or more consecutive ones, and output signals are asserted based on these states and input transitions, matching the original behavior but with clearer FSM semantics.
