We simplify the FSM by using a single counter to track consecutive ones, saturating at 7 (error). On input=0, we reset the counter to zero. Outputs disc and flag are asserted one cycle after detecting a zero following exactly 5 or 6 consecutive ones, respectively. err is asserted continuously while the counter is 7 or more (error state). We keep a register to remember the previous count to determine disc and flag outputs. This removes explicit state encodings and directly uses the count of consecutive 1s for clarity and simplicity.
