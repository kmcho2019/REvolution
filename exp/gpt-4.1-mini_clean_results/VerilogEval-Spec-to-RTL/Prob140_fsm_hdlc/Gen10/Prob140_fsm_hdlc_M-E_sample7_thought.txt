The FSM will have states: ZERO (no preceding ones), ONE through SIX (count of consecutive ones from 1 to 6), DISC (to output disc signal for one cycle), FLAG (to output flag signal for one cycle), and ERR (to output err signal for one cycle and stay until reset). Transitions advance the count of consecutive ones on input=1, reset count on input=0, and generate the output states synchronously in the next cycle after detecting a specific pattern. The FSM will return to ZERO after outputting disc or flag, and stay in ERR until reset. This approach makes the FSM explicitly Moore by having output states separated from counting states, yielding clear state-dependent outputs and transitions.
