Fuse the direct bit-slicing and continuous assignment approach from Example 1 with a minimal generic bit extraction method inspired by Example 2, but implemented using a localparam array and generate-for loops inside the TopModule. This allows flexible indexing and scalability without separate module overhead. The output vector outv is assigned directly from vec for optimal area and power, while the outputs o0, o1, and o2 are assigned individually using a generate block that iterates over the bit indices, maintaining clarity and compactness. This approach blends direct wiring efficiency with a structured, scalable, and self-contained design, eliminating overhead of multiple instantiated submodules while keeping a clear, parameterized style for possible future extension.
