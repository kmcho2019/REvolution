To optimize the design for better power and area without impacting performance, I will remove the BitSplitter submodule and flatten the hierarchy by directly assigning each output bit from the input vector inside TopModule. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and additional wiring. The 3-bit output outv will still be assigned directly from the input vector to avoid any reconstruction logic. This approach simplifies the design, slightly reduces area and power, and keeps the functionality and timing unaffected.
