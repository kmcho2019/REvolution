The original design is already minimal and optimal for PPA since it involves only direct wire assignments without any logic. However, one small improvement for power and area could be merging the individual bit assignments into a single continuous assignment of outv, then slicing outv to drive o0, o1, and o2. This might allow the synthesis tool to share buffers or routing resources for the output vector, potentially reducing power and area slightly. It also improves code clarity by defining outputs from a single source (outv) rather than from two separate sources (vec and individually assigned bits). Functionally and performance-wise, this remains equivalent and optimal.
