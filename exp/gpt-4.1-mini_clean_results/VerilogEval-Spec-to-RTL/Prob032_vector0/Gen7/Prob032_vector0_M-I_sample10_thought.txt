To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will eliminate the extra BitExtractor module instantiations. Instead, I will directly assign the output bits o0, o1, o2 to the respective bits of the input vector vec. Also, I will assign outv directly to vec, avoiding the redundant recombination of bits. This reduces module hierarchy, internal nets, and buffering, yielding a leaner, simpler design with equivalent functionality.
