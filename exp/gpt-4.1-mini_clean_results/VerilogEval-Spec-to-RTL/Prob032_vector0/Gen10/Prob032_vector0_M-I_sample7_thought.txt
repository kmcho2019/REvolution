The original design uses parameterized BitExtractor modules to extract individual bits, adding unnecessary hierarchy and overhead. To optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA), directly assign the output bits from input vector slices without intermediate modules or signals. Also, assign the output vector outv directly from the input vec since it's the same vector, eliminating redundant concatenations. This reduces logic complexity, routing overhead, and synthesis complexity, leading to better area and power efficiency while maintaining correctness.
