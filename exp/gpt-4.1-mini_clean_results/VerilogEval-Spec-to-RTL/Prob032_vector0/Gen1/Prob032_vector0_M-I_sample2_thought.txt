The original design is already minimal and purely combinational, which is optimal for PPA given its simplicity. However, to potentially improve area and power slightly, we can use a continuous assignment to directly connect the individual outputs to the vector bits, avoiding separate assigns for each bit. This also keeps the design concise. Additionally, we can explicitly declare the outputs as wires for clarity, though synthesis tools infer this by default. No registers or logic are added, maintaining zero critical path delay and minimal power. This slight refactor may marginally improve area by reducing redundant net declarations, while keeping all functionality intact.
