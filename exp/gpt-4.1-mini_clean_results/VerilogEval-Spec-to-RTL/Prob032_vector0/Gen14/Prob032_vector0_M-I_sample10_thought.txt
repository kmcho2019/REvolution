Since the original design is functionally correct but includes unnecessary hierarchy and intermediate wiring through the BitSplitter module, we can optimize for power and area by eliminating that module and intermediate signals. The bit splitting can be done directly by assigning each output bit to the corresponding bit of the input vector. This reduces net toggling, routing complexity, and module overhead, leading to better power and area with no performance penalty. The outv output remains a direct assignment from the input vector. This approach maintains full correctness and simplicity.
