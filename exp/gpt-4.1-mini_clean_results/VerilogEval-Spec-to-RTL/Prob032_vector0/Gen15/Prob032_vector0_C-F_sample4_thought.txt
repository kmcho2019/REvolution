The optimal fusion eliminates the BitSplitter submodule entirely to flatten the design, reducing area and power overhead from module instantiation and extra wiring while maintaining clarity. The input vector `vec` is directly assigned to the 3-bit output vector `outv`, and each output bit `o0`, `o1`, and `o2` is directly assigned from the corresponding bit positions of `vec`. This approach preserves the exact functional requirements, ensures minimal logic levels, reduces routing complexity, and thus optimizes power and area without compromising readability or performance.
