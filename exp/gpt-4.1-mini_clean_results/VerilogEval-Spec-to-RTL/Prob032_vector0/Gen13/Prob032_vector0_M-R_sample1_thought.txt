To refactor the original code and maintain the core functionality but change the structure, I will remove the separate BitSplitter module and directly assign the individual bit outputs within TopModule using continuous assignments. This simplifies the hierarchy, reducing area and power by avoiding an extra module instantiation while keeping the design fully combinational. Also, instead of grouping the single-bit outputs in an instance, I assign them in a compact manner using vector slicing directly in TopModule.

This approach improves power and area slightly due to reduced net fanout and simpler wiring, and it maintains clarity by having all related logic in one module.
