To optimize the design for power and area without sacrificing readability or functionality, I will remove the BitSplitter submodule and directly assign the individual output bits (o0, o1, o2) from the input vector inside TopModule. This flattening of the design hierarchy reduces net toggling and eliminates the minor overhead introduced by the submodule instantiation. Additionally, directly assigning outv to vec remains to avoid redundant concatenation logic. This approach keeps the code simple, ensures zero critical path delay, and reduces switching power and area by minimizing wiring and module overhead.
