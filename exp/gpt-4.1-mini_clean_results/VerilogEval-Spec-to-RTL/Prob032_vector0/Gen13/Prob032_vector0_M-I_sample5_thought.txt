To optimize the design for better power and area, I will remove the BitSplitter submodule to flatten the module hierarchy. Since the splitting is a simple direct bit extraction, using continuous assignments directly inside TopModule will eliminate the minor overhead of module instantiation and port wiring. This results in slightly reduced area and power with no effect on timing or functionality. The code remains clean and straightforward, with direct connections from the input vector to both the output vector and the separate output bits.
