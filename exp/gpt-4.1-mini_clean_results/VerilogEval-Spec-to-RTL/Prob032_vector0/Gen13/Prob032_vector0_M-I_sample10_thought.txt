To optimize for power and area while preserving modularity, integrate the bit-splitting logic directly within TopModule instead of instantiating a separate BitSplitter submodule. This removes the overhead of a module instance, reducing resource usage and potential power consumption slightly. The direct assignments to o0, o1, o2 from the input vector bits maintain clarity. The outv is assigned directly from vec to avoid redundant logic. This approach keeps the design clean, simple, and minimal in logic and wiring, suitable for very small combinational logic blocks.
