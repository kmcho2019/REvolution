The TopModule has a 3-bit input vector 'vec'. The output 'outv' is connected directly to 'vec' to output the same 3-bit vector. The individual bits of 'vec' are assigned to outputs o0, o1, and o2, where o0 is vec[0], o1 is vec[1], and o2 is vec[2]. This direct bit-wise assignment achieves the desired output splitting.
