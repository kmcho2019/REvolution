Achieve the best of both modularity and minimal overhead by removing the BitSplitter submodule entirely and implementing all required functionality inside TopModule using straightforward continuous assignments. This fusion eliminates extra module instantiations and intermediate wiring, reducing area and power, while maintaining clear, concise code. The 3-bit output vector outv is assigned directly from vec, and the single-bit outputs o0, o1, o2 are each assigned from the corresponding bits of vec using simple bit-select. This approach balances simplicity, readability, and optimal PPA without sacrificing modular clarity because the functionality is trivial and self-contained.
