The original design is already optimal in terms of simplicity and direct wire assignments. To improve power slightly and potentially area, we can reorganize the output assignments by leveraging vector slicing and concatenation. This reduces the number of explicit assignments and might help synthesis tools optimize wire routing and buffer insertion better. By defining o0, o1, o2 as part of a single vector internally and assigning that vector directly, we provide a clearer structure. This approach may also marginally improve readability and maintainability without adding logic or delay. Since outputs are just wires, no registers or logic are introduced, preserving zero timing penalty.
