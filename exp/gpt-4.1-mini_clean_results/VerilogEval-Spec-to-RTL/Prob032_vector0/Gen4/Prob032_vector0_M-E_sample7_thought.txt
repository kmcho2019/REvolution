Implement the TopModule by using a single continuous assignment where the 3-bit output vector `outv` is directly connected to the input `vec`. The three individual outputs `o0`, `o1`, and `o2` are obtained by direct bit slicing from the input vector, but in a single continuous assignment block that simultaneously assigns all outputs. This flat approach avoids extra modules or intermediate signals, and uses Verilog's bit-select operations clearly. It keeps the design simple and minimal while meeting the interface requirements.
