Fuse the modular clarity of using a BitSplitter submodule with the power and area benefits of direct assignments by integrating the bit-splitting logic directly inside TopModule, avoiding an extra module instantiation and intermediate wires. Specifically:

- Eliminate the BitSplitter module to reduce hierarchy overhead, saving area and power.
- Inside TopModule, directly assign o0, o1, o2 from the corresponding bits of the input vector vec.
- Directly assign outv from vec, preserving zero-delay, minimal logic.
- This approach maintains clear, clean, and readable code while optimizing PPA by flattening the logic and minimizing signals.
- The design is fully combinational and simple, ensuring the best possible performance and minimal area/power footprint.

This fusion keeps the clarity of explicit bit indexing without redundant intermediate signals or modules, balancing modularity, simplicity, and efficiency.
