Fuse the best ideas by eliminating the unnecessary BitSplitter module entirely, directly assigning the input vector to the output vector `outv` and splitting the individual bits from `vec` to outputs `o0`, `o1`, and `o2` in TopModule itself. This approach flattens hierarchy to minimize area and power by reducing net counts and toggling nodes, while maintaining clarity through straightforward, clean continuous assignments.

This direct assignment strategy improves power and area by avoiding intermediate vectors and submodules, retains zero-delay combinational logic for maximum performance, and keeps the code simple, readable, and maintainable. It combines the modular clarity of splitting bits explicitly with the simplest possible implementation.

Overall, this design:
- Assigns outv = vec directly, avoiding redundant reconstruction.
- Assigns o0, o1, and o2 from vec bits directly, avoiding intermediate wires or submodules.
- Reduces logic depth and net count, improving power and area metrics.
- Maintains very clear and understandable code without hierarchical overhead.
