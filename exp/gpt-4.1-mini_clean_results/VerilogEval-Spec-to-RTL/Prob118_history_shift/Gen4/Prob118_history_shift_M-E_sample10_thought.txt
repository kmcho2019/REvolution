Implement the global history register using a clock-enable signal to update the register only when necessary, thereby reducing switching activity and power consumption. Use two always blocks: one asynchronous reset block to clear the history, and a synchronous block for state updates gated by the clock enable. When a misprediction occurs, load the rollback history by shifting the input train_history left by one bit and inserting train_taken as the LSB, which preserves the 32-bit width correctly. When a prediction occurs (and no misprediction), shift in predict_taken. This approach cleanly separates reset logic and uses an enable signal to minimize unnecessary updates, improving power without affecting functionality.
