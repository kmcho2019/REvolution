To optimize the existing design for better power and performance while maintaining correctness, I will introduce a clock enable signal so the register only updates when necessary (either misprediction or prediction valid). This reduces unnecessary toggling of the 32-bit register, lowering dynamic power. Additionally, I will explicitly handle the rollback assignment width to ensure the 32-bit history is correctly formed by shifting train_history left by 1 bit and inserting train_taken at LSB, matching the spec "train_history concatenated with train_taken" (which means older bits shifted left, youngest branch as LSB). This avoids any ambiguity or truncation issues.

The always block remains simple with asynchronous reset, priority for misprediction, and conditional update on predict_valid, but the register update only occurs when enabled, improving timing and power due to reduced switching activity.

Finally, outputs are registered and directly reflect the internal history state.
