Implement the history register using two separate always blocks: one asynchronous reset block to clear the register and one synchronous block for updates. Use a clock enable signal that activates only when an update is needed (misprediction or prediction valid) to reduce switching activity. When misprediction occurs, load the rollback history by shifting train_history left by 1 bit and inserting train_taken at LSB (matching the 32-bit width). When prediction is valid (and no misprediction), shift the current history left by 1 bit inserting predict_taken at LSB. Otherwise, hold the history constant. This separation improves modularity and reduces unnecessary updates, and ensures proper width and precedence handling.
