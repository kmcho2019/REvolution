To optimize the initial design for better power and area without sacrificing performance, introduce a clock enable signal that activates the shift register update only when needed (when either a misprediction or a valid prediction occurs). This prevents unnecessary toggling of all 32 flip-flops when no update is required, reducing dynamic power. The logic for clock enable is simple: enable when train_mispredicted or predict_valid is asserted. The rest of the logic remains the same, with misprediction taking precedence. This change slightly reduces switching activity and thus dynamic power, with negligible area impact and no impact on timing.
