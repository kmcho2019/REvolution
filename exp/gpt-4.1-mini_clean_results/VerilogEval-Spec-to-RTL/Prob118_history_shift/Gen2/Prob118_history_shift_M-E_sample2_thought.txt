Implement the history register as a 33-bit register internally to directly store the concatenation of train_history (32 bits) and train_taken (1 bit) during a misprediction rollback. Then output only the lower 32 bits to predict_history. On predict_valid, shift left by 1 bit and insert predict_taken at bit 0. On misprediction, load the full 33-bit concatenation. On reset, clear the full register. This explicitly handles the 33-bit width internally to avoid truncation or ambiguity, cleanly separates the width concerns, and simplifies correct rollback behavior. The output is always the lower 32 bits, matching the spec for predict_history. This approach improves clarity and correctness while maintaining a single always block with async reset.
