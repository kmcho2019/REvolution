To optimize the original design for better timing, power, and area (PPA), the main strategy is to minimize switching activity and reduce combinational logic depth inside the sequential block. 

Improvements:
1. Add a clock enable signal so the 32-bit history register updates only when necessary—i.e., when either a misprediction or a prediction occurs. This reduces switching when idle, lowering dynamic power.
2. Separate the logic for the next state calculation outside the always block, to reduce combinational logic within the sequential block. Use a simple mux on next_history.
3. Use a wire for next_history that directly encodes the multiplexed update between misprediction load, prediction shift-in, or holding the current state.
4. Keep asynchronous reset as requested.
5. This approach reduces the amount of combinational shifting and concatenation inside the clocked always block, improving timing.
6. Power savings come from reduced toggling when no update happens.
7. Area is not increased and may even reduce slightly due to simplified control logic.

Overall, the design preserves the exact functional behavior but adds an enable to update only when necessary and simplifies combinational logic, which will improve PPA.
