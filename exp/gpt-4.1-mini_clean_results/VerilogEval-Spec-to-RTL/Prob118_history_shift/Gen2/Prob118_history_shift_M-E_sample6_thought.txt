Use a dual-register scheme to implement the history rollback: keep two 32-bit registers, one for the current speculative history that updates on predictions, and one for the committed history reflecting the last confirmed branch state. On a correct prediction, update the speculative history by shifting in the predicted bit; on a misprediction, revert the speculative register to the committed history concatenated with the actual taken bit. After a misprediction flush, update the committed history with the corrected history. This separation allows simpler control logic and more explicit handling of state rollback, improving clarity and potentially easing timing closure by reducing combinational logic depth. The asynchronous reset clears both registers. The output predict_history reflects the speculative history, representing the pipeline's current view of branch outcomes.
