Implement the 32-bit global history register as a shift register composed of individual flip-flops that shift in bits sequentially at each clock cycle when a prediction is valid. To handle the misprediction rollback, asynchronously load the entire 32-bit history concatenated with the actual branch outcome using a parallel load. Prioritize misprediction load over prediction shift. Use a clock enable signal to only update when necessary, reducing toggling when no update is needed. The asynchronous reset clears the entire shift register to zero.
