Refactor the design by implementing the 32-bit history register as a module-internal register updated using a combinational next-history signal decided by priority logic. The always block updates the register on clk or areset edges, but the next state is computed outside the sequential block via a combinational block. This clearly separates the combinational logic deciding the next history state from the sequential storage, improving readability and making the update logic explicit. The misprediction update has priority over prediction, and asynchronous reset is still respected.
