To improve PPA, we will implement clock gating to enable updates to the history register only when necessary, reducing unnecessary switching and dynamic power consumption. Instead of separate combinational signals for update enable and data input, we combine the logic for selecting the next history state inside the clock-gated always block. We will also remove the extra combinational signals that do not need to be stored or separately declared, simplifying the design and potentially improving timing. The asynchronous reset remains unchanged to maintain the required reset behavior. This approach keeps misprediction priority logic straightforward and leverages clock gating to reduce power, with minimal impact on area and improved timing margin by reducing combinational path complexity.
