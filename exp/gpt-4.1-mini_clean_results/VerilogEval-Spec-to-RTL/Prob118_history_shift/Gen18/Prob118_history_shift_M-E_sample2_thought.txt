Use a two-stage update scheme for the history register to separate the pipeline flush correction from the predicted update. Maintain an internal 32-bit register for current history. On misprediction, asynchronously load the corrected history (train_history concatenated with train_taken). When no misprediction occurs but a prediction is valid, store the predicted next history in a temporary register, then update the main history register at the clock edge, ensuring that misprediction update has priority by gating the prediction update. This decouples prediction updates from misprediction flushes and avoids combinational multiplexing on the main register input. Also implement a synchronous reset on areset's positive edge (as it is positive-edge async reset) to clear both registers. Output predict_history is driven by the internal history register.
