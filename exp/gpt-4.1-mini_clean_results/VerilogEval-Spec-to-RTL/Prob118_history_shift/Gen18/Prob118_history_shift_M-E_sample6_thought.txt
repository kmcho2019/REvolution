I will implement the global history register as a shift register updated only when necessary by using an explicit enable signal in the always block. To cleanly prioritize rollback on misprediction, I will use an if-else structure that first checks for rollback, then prediction, else hold. To optimize power, the register only updates when predict_valid or train_mispredicted is asserted, reducing unnecessary toggling. For clarity, I will explicitly declare the enable condition and then update the register only when enabled. Asynchronous reset remains active high and clears the register. This design separates the enable logic from the data path, facilitating easy insertion of clock gating later and improves readability by explicitly showing when updates occur.
