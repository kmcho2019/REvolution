Remove the separate combinational signals for next history and update enable by embedding the priority and update logic directly into the sequential always block.  
This eliminates unnecessary intermediate registers and simplifies the design, while preserving the asynchronous reset and priority of misprediction rollback over prediction update.  
On each clock edge, if areset is asserted, reset history to zero; else if train_mispredicted is asserted, load rollback history; else if predict_valid is asserted, shift in predict_taken; otherwise hold the current history.  
This approach keeps the logic simple and synthesizable, minimizing code and registers without changing behavior.
