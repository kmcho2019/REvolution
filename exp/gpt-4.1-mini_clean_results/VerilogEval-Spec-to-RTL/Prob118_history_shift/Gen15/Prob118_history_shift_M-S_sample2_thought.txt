Implement the 32-bit global history register with asynchronous reset and update it on the rising clock edge. Prioritize misprediction load over prediction shift by using a single always block with straightforward if-else statements. When a misprediction occurs, load the corrected history; else if a valid prediction occurs, shift in the predicted bit. Otherwise, hold the current state. This avoids explicit enable signals and extra wires, simplifying the design while maintaining correct functionality and priority.
