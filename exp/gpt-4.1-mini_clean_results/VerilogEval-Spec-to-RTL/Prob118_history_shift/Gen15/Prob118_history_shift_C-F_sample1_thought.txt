I will combine the strengths of both examples by using a single 32-bit shift register to minimize routing complexity and area, but also incorporate a clock enable signal to update the register only when a prediction or misprediction occurs, thereby reducing switching power. The update logic will prioritize misprediction over prediction as specified. The asynchronous reset remains positive-edge triggered. The shift direction is preserved with the youngest branch at LSB. This fusion keeps the design simple and efficient while adding power savings from conditional updates and avoiding the overhead of splitting the register.
