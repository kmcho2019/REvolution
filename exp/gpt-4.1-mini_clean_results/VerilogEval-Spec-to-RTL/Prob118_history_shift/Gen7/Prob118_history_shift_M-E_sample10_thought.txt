Implement two separate 32-bit shift registers: one to hold the current predicted history and one as a buffer to store the history before the most recent prediction. On each predict_valid, shift the current history and simultaneously save the old history in the buffer. On a misprediction, restore the buffered history combined with train_taken as the new current history. This double-register approach naturally supports rollback without directly concatenating train_history and train_taken every cycle, separating prediction updates from rollback updates. Use asynchronous reset to clear both registers. This architecture simplifies rollback by isolating the pre-prediction state and makes the pipeline flush operation a simple restore, improving clarity and modularity.
