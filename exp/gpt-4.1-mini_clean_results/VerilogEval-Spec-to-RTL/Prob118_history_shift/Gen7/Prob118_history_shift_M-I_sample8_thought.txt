To optimize PPA without changing functionality, I will replace explicit concatenations with a shift operator that is typically better optimized by synthesis tools for shift register behavior. This can reduce area and improve timing by leveraging shift register primitives. I will implement the shift using a single left-shift plus insertion of the LSB bit, avoiding slicing and concatenation. Also, I will add an enable signal to update the register only when needed (i.e., either misprediction or prediction valid), potentially allowing synthesis tools to optimize clock gating or reduce switching activity. The logic priority remains the same: on reset clear, on misprediction load rollback history, else on prediction update, otherwise hold.
