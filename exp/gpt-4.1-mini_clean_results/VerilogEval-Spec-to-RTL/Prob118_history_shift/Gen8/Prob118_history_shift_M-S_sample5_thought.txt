Since the current solution is already minimal, simplification can be achieved by removing explicit bit slicing from `train_history` when concatenating, because `train_history[30:0]` corresponds to the lower 31 bits, and we just need 31 bits plus one bit to form 32 bits. Using a single concatenation with `train_history` shifted right by one bit and appending `train_taken` from the LSB side simplifies indexing and keeps the same logic. Also, directly assign `predict_history` from the `history` register without intermediate signals to keep code minimal.
