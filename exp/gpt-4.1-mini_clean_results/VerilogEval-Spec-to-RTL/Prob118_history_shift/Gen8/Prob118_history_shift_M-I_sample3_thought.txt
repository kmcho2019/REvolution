To further optimize PPA, I will implement a one-hot encoded shift register style update to minimize switching activity by updating only the least significant bit and shifting via logic instead of a full 32-bit left shift. However, since the requirement is a 32-bit global history, a full shift is necessary. Instead, I will leverage a generate block with a shift register implemented as a parallel load and a shift register structure for better synthesis and timing optimization by mapping to shift register primitives in FPGAs or efficient SRLs in ASICs. Also, I will introduce an explicit clock enable signal to gate updates only when needed, which can reduce power. This is similar to the original but makes the enable explicit and rearranges the priority clearly. The asynchronous reset remains. This approach could improve timing by reducing the logic complexity and improve power by preventing unnecessary toggles.
