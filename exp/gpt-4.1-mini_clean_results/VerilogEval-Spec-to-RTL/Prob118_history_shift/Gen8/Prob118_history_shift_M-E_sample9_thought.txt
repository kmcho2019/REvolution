Implement two separate 32-bit registers: one to hold the current history (current_history) and one to hold the next predicted history (next_history). On every clock, update current_history from next_history. When a prediction is valid, update next_history by shifting in predict_taken. When a misprediction occurs, next_history is loaded directly with the corrected history formed by concatenating train_history[30:0] and train_taken, overriding any prediction update. The output predict_history reflects current_history, which represents the committed global history. This decouples state updates and makes clear distinction between current state and next state, enabling easier debugging and potentially better timing by isolating combinational update logic.
