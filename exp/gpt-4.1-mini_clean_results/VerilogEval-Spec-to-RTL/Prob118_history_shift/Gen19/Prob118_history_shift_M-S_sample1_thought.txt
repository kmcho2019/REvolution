The design can be further simplified by using a single always block with clear priority handling and without slicing the train_history bits, as we just need to concatenate train_history[31:1] with train_taken to form the new history. However, since train_history is the 32-bit history before the mispredicted branch, concatenating train_taken to its LSB is sufficient. We keep the asynchronous reset and update history on posedge clk, prioritizing misprediction rollback over prediction shift and otherwise maintaining the current state. This approach uses minimal code and keeps logic clear and concise.
