Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the global branch history. On the positive edge of clk, asynchronously reset the history to zero if areset is asserted. If a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted=1), load the history with {train_history, train_taken} (train_taken is the newest branch, so it should be shifted in at the LSB side). Else if predict_valid=1 and no misprediction, shift in predict_taken at LSB. Output the current history as predict_history. Misprediction has priority over prediction when both happen simultaneously.
