Implement a 32-bit shift register to hold the global branch history. On each clock cycle, if asynchronous reset is asserted, clear the history to zero. Otherwise, if train_mispredicted is asserted, load predict_history with {train_history[30:0], train_taken} (the train_history shifted left by one with train_taken as the LSB) as this represents rolling back the history due to pipeline flush. If no misprediction but predict_valid is asserted, shift in predict_taken at LSB side, shifting history left by one. Otherwise hold the current history value. Since misprediction has precedence over prediction when both are asserted simultaneously, check train_mispredicted first.
