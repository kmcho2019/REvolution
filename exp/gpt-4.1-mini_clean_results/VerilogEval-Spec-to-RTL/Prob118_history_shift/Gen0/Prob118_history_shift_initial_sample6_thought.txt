Implement a 32-bit shift register (global history register) that updates on the positive clock edge.
- On asynchronous positive edge of areset, reset the history to zero.
- On a clock edge:
  - If train_mispredicted is asserted, load the register with {train_history[30:0], train_taken}.
  - Else if predict_valid is asserted, shift the current history left by 1 and input predict_taken at LSB.
  - Otherwise, hold the current value.
Output predict_history is the current register value.
