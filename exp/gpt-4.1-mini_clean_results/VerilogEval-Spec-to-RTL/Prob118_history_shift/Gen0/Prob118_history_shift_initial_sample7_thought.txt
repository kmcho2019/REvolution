Implement a 32-bit shift register that updates on the positive edge of clk or asynchronously resets on areset. On reset, set the history to zero. On a misprediction (train_mispredicted=1), load the history register with {train_history[30:0], train_taken}, which represents the history before the mispredicted branch concatenated with the actual result. On a valid prediction without misprediction (predict_valid=1 and train_mispredicted=0), shift left by 1 and insert predict_taken at LSB. If neither event, keep the current history. Misprediction has precedence over prediction.
