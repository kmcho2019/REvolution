Instead of directly shifting the history register on every prediction or misprediction, I will implement a 32-bit shift register with an explicit enable signal and use a priority mux inside the sequential logic. To minimize power, the register updates only when necessary: during misprediction rollback or valid prediction. The rollback loads the history with the corrected value, and otherwise, prediction shifts in the new bit. To achieve this, I will implement the register update logic inside the clocked always block, including a single level priority mux to select the next state. This avoids using combinational assign for next state and centralizes state update, potentially helping synthesis tools better optimize clock gating and reduce toggling. The asynchronous reset clears the entire 32-bit register on its positive edge.
