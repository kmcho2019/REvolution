To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will unify the global history into a single 32-bit register instead of splitting it into two 16-bit registers. This reduces routing overhead and area, and simplifies the data path.

I will implement the history as one 32-bit register that shifts left by 1 on prediction (inserting predict_taken at LSB), or loads the corrected history (train_history concatenated with train_taken as the new LSB) on misprediction. The priority of misprediction update over prediction update will be preserved by ordering the if-else blocks.

Additionally, I will add a clock enable signal derived from predict_valid or train_mispredicted, gating register updates to minimize switching activity when no updates are needed, improving power efficiency.

The asynchronous reset remains positive edge triggered as specified.

This approach simplifies the design, reduces interconnect complexity, reduces switching activity when idle, and should improve overall PPA.
