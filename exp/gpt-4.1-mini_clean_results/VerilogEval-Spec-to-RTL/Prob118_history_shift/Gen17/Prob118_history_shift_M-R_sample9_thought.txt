I will refactor the design by splitting the next history computation into a separate combinational always block, which computes the next value based on the priority and control signals. The sequential always block will then only update the history register on the clock edge or asynchronous reset. This separation improves clarity by isolating combinational logic from sequential logic and avoids using a combined conditional inside the sequential always block. The asynchronous reset remains on the clocked always block. The final output is assigned directly from the history register.
