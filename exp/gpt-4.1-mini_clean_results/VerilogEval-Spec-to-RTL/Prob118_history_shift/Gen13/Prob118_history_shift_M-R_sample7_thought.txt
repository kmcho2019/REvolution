Refactor the design by removing the combinational always block and instead using a continuous assignment with a conditional operator to determine the next history value. Then, use a single sequential always block to update the history register on the clock edge with asynchronous reset. This approach simplifies the logic by eliminating an extra reg for update_enable and data_in, directly computing the next state combinationally and updating conditionally.
