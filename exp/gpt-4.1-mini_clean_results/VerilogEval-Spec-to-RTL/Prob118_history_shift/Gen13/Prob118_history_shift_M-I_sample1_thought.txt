To optimize for better PPA (timing, power, and area), I will simplify the design by using a single 32-bit shift register instead of splitting it into two 16-bit registers. This reduces routing complexity and area. Also, I will add a clock enable signal to update the register only when a valid update is needed (predict_valid or train_mispredicted), reducing switching power. The asynchronous reset remains as specified. The misprediction update has priority over prediction update. The shift direction is preserved (youngest bit at LSB). This approach simplifies logic, minimizes flip-flop count, reduces interconnect switching, and improves timing and power efficiency.
