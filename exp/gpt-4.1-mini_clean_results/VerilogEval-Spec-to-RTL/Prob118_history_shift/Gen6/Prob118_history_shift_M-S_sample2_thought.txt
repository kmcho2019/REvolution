Use a single always block triggered on clk and areset to update the 32-bit history register. On reset, clear the history. On a misprediction, load the new history formed by shifting train_history left by 1 and inserting train_taken at LSB. Else, if predict_valid and no misprediction, shift in predict_taken at LSB. Otherwise, keep the history unchanged. This removes the combinational next_state logic and extra wires for clarity and reduces complexity while keeping all functionality intact.
