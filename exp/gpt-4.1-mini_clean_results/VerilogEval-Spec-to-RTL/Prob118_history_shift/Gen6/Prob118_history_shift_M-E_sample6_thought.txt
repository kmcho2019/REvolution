Implement the 32-bit global history register as a shift register with explicit control signals: an asynchronous reset clears the register, and a synchronous update always occurs on the clock edge. Use a single always block triggered by clock and asynchronous reset. Prioritize misprediction rollback by loading the combined rollback history (train_history shifted left by 1, with train_taken at LSB). On a valid prediction without misprediction, shift in predict_taken at LSB. When neither condition applies, maintain the current history state. The output directly reflects the current history register. This approach uses straightforward sequential logic with a priority encoded in nested if-else statements, ensuring clear and efficient state updates.
