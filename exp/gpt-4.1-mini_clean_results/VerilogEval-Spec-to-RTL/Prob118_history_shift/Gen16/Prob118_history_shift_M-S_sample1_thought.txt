Implement a single 32-bit shift register to store the global branch history. On asynchronous reset, clear the history. On misprediction, load the history with train_history concatenated with train_taken (train_history[31:1] plus train_taken as LSB), giving the corrected history after the mispredicted branch. On prediction (if no misprediction), shift left by 1 and insert predict_taken at LSB to update the predicted history. Misprediction takes priority over prediction. This approach is simpler, reduces routing complexity, and maintains the same functionality.
