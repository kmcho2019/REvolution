To optimize the design for better power without sacrificing correctness or timing, I will add a clock enable signal to avoid unnecessary updates to the 32-bit history register. The history only needs to update when either train_mispredicted or predict_valid is asserted. This reduces switching activity and dynamic power consumption. Since the register updates only on relevant events, timing can improve by reducing the data switching load on the register input. The asynchronous reset and priority logic remain the same. This simple enhancement preserves functionality while improving power and potentially timing.
