To optimize for power and area while maintaining the same functionality and timing, introduce a clock enable signal to update the history register only when necessary (i.e., on misprediction or prediction). This reduces unnecessary toggling of the 32-bit register and lowers dynamic power consumption. The logic for next_history remains unchanged, but the register update is conditioned on this enable. This avoids extra complexity and keeps the reset asynchronous with simple logic. This approach should improve power without affecting performance or area significantly.
