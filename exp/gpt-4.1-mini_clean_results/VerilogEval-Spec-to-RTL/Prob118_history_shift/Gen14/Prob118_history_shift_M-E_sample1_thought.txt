I will implement the 32-bit global history as a single 32-bit register and use a clock enable to update it only when predict_valid or train_mispredicted is asserted to reduce switching. On misprediction (train_mispredicted=1), the register is synchronously loaded with {train_taken, train_history}, with the newest bit at LSB per spec. On prediction (predict_valid=1 and no misprediction), the history is shifted left by one and predict_taken is shifted in at LSB. Asynchronous reset clears the register to zero. This approach prioritizes misprediction updates cleanly, reduces unnecessary shifting by gating the update, and simplifies logic with a single 32-bit register.
