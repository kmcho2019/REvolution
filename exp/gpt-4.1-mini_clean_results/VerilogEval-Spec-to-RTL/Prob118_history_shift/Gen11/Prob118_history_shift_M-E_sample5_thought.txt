I will implement the global history register as a 32-bit register with an asynchronous reset. To avoid large 32-bit concatenation logic during the misprediction update, I will update predict_history using a priority mux that selects between three states: reset, misprediction update, and prediction update. Instead of shifting the entire 32-bit history each time, I will update only the relevant bits using bitwise operations: on prediction, shift left by one and insert predict_taken; on misprediction, replicate train_history shifted left by one bit with train_taken at LSB. This uses a simple combinational mux selecting the next state, optimizing logic complexity. The prioritization of misprediction over prediction and asynchronous reset is clearly encoded. This design simplifies combinational logic by avoiding ORing 33-bit wide values, which could reduce critical path and improve timing.
