To improve the original design for better PPA, introduce an enable signal to clock-gate updates to the history register, thereby reducing unnecessary toggling and saving power. This avoids shifting the register when neither predict_valid nor train_mispredicted is asserted. The logic priority remains the same: misprediction rollback takes precedence. Additionally, using a local enable signal simplifies conditional updates and can help synthesis tools optimize the logic better. The asynchronous reset and shift-with-load logic remain unchanged to preserve functionality and timing.
