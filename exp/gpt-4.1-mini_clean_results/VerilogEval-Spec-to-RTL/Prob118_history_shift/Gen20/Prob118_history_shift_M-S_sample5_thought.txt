To simplify the design, I will merge the combinational next-state calculation directly inside the sequential always block. This removes the separate next_history signal and update_enable wire, reducing code size and complexity. The priority of misprediction over prediction will be maintained with straightforward if-else logic inside the always block.

The asynchronous reset will remain as is, and the register updates only occur on valid events. This results in a more compact and easy-to-read design without sacrificing any functionality.
