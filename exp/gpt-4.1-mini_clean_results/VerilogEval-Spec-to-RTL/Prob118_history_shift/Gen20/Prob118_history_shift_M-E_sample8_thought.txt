Implement two separate 32-bit registers: one to hold the current history and one to hold the last valid history before a branch is predicted. On each predict_valid, shift in the new predict_taken into the current history and simultaneously save the previous current history to the "previous history" register. On misprediction, restore the history by concatenating train_history and train_taken into the current history. This approach enables a rollback without directly overwriting the history in-place and keeps the rollback mechanism explicit. The asynchronous reset clears both registers.
