3. Use a single mux for next state selection controlled by misprediction and prediction signals, but arrange logic so that the shift operation and load are simple and not both performed combinationally.
4. Keep update_enable gating the state update to avoid toggling when no meaningful update is happening.
5. Preserve asynchronous reset as requested.
6. These refinements reduce glitching inside combinational logic and may improve critical path and power consumption.
