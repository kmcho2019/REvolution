To optimize for better power, area, and timing, the main lever is to reduce unnecessary updates to the history register when the inputs don't require a change. Adding a clock enable (update_enable) gating the register write to only when a misprediction or a prediction occurs will reduce switching activity and dynamic power. This avoids updating the history every cycle if inputs are inactive.

Also, separating the combinational next_state logic and using the clock enable cleanly simplifies synthesis and may improve timing by reducing the data path load on the sequential register.

No complex FSM or additional buffering is needed as the logic is straightforward.

This optimization maintains the same functional correctness and priority of misprediction over prediction.

The asynchronous reset remains, and non-blocking assignments are used appropriately.
