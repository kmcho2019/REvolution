I will implement the 32-bit global history shift register with two separate registers: a current history register and a backup register. On every valid prediction, the current history shifts in the predicted bit, while the backup holds the previous state. On misprediction, the backup register is loaded with the corrected history, which is concatenation of train_history and train_taken, and the current history is replaced by this backup. This separation cleanly handles rollback without complex combinational logic or multiplexing on the same register. The asynchronous reset clears both registers. This two-register approach isolates prediction updates from rollback updates, minimizing glitches and improving timing by avoiding large multiplexers on a single register update.
