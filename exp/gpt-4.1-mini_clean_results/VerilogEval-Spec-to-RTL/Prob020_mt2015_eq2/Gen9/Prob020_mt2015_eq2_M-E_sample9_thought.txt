Implement the equality comparison by first XOR-ing corresponding bits of A and B, then OR-ing the XOR results. If the OR result is 0, it means all bits match, so output z = 1; otherwise, z = 0. This approach uses XOR and OR gates and an inverter to determine equality, offering an alternative logic structure compared to the XNOR-AND method. The design highlights that equality is the negation of any bit difference.
