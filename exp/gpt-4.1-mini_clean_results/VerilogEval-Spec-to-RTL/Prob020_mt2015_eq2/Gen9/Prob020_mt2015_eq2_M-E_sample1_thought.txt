Instead of directly using the equality operator, the circuit can perform a bitwise XOR of the inputs A and B and then NOR the results. Since XOR outputs 0 when bits are equal and 1 when bits differ, if both bits are equal, the XOR result will be 2'b00, and NOR of these bits will be 1. Thus, output z = 1 only when A equals B. This approach explicitly breaks down the comparison into basic gate-level operations.
