The original implementation uses a direct equality operator which is concise but might synthesize to slightly more complex logic than necessary. Since A and B are only 2 bits each, the equality can be implemented explicitly as the bitwise equivalence of corresponding bits: (A[1] == B[1]) AND (A[0] == B[0]). This potentially simplifies the synthesized logic to two XNOR gates followed by an AND gate, which may slightly reduce area and power while keeping zero delay on critical path.
