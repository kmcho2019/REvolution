This replaces the direct use of the equality operator (==) with explicit bitwise logic that is functionally identical but structurally different.

This approach:
- Still produces a purely combinational circuit.
- Makes the equality check more explicit at the bit level.
- Is equally synthesizable and typically results in the same minimal hardware, but expressed as primitive bitwise operations.
- Maintains clarity and minimal logic without introducing intermediate registers or always blocks.
