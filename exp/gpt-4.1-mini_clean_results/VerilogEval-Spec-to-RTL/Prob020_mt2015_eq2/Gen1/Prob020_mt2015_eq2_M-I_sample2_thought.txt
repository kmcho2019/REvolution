The original design uses the equality operator, which is efficient and clear. To further optimize for power and area, we can replace the equality operator with an explicit bitwise XOR and NOR operation. This reduces the logic to simple gates (XOR for bit comparison and NOR for aggregation), which may have a smaller gate footprint and potentially lower power due to simpler gate structures. This change might give a slight improvement in area and power with equivalent timing since it explicitly states the logic.
