Fuse the semantic clarity and synthesis-friendliness of the equality operator (A == B) with the explicit bitwise XOR and reduction OR approach for robustness and minimal gate-level inference. 

The output z is assigned by negating the reduction OR of the bitwise XOR of inputs A and B, ensuring a precise, compact comparator structure. Alongside, the design includes a comment highlighting the equivalence to (A == B) to improve maintainability and synthesis tool guidance.

This fusion leverages:
- The succinctness and explicit equality intent of (A == B).
- The minimal and efficient gate-level logic of ~( | (A ^ B) ).
- Clear documentation for synthesis and human readers, improving clarity and maintainability.

This balanced approach achieves minimal critical path, small area footprint, low power, and clear semantic expression, maximizing PPA and clarity without any added complexity.
