Since the original solution already uses the minimal and optimal vector equality operator which synthesizes to a minimal comparator logic, further significant PPA optimizations are unlikely at the module level. However, a micro-optimization can be applied by manually implementing bitwise XNOR and AND operations instead of relying on the equality operator. This explicitly shows the exact gate-level operations and may sometimes yield slightly smaller or faster hardware depending on synthesis tool optimizations. This approach could reduce power by limiting logic depth to just XNOR and AND gates and clarifies the comparison logic. While improvements are minor, this explicit logic is a common technique in low-power and area-sensitive designs.
