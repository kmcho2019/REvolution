To improve the design's area slightly, we can replace the explicit bitwise XNOR and AND with a vector reduction XNOR operation. Using (A ~^ B) performs bitwise XNOR across all bits and results in a 2-bit vector; applying a reduction AND (&) on this result yields 1 if all bits are equal. This is more succinct and can synthesize to equally minimal or smaller logic. This approach maintains the same functionality and potentially reduces area without impacting power or timing.
