Refactor the original design by performing the equality check using a single combined XOR of the 2-bit vectors A and B, then inverting the result. The idea is that A == B if and only if A ^ B == 0. So the output z can be assigned as the NOR of all bits of (A ^ B). This avoids intermediate wires and uses a single expression with bitwise reduction, simplifying the logic and maintaining the same hardware efficiency. It also explicitly shows how vector-level XOR and reduction operations can be used for equality checking in a concise way.
