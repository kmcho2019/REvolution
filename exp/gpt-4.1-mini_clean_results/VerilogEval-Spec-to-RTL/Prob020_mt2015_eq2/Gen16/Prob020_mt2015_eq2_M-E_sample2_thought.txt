Instead of comparing bits directly with XNOR gates, we can use XOR gates followed by an OR gate to detect inequality and then invert the result to get equality. Specifically, if any bit differs (XOR outputs 1), then inputs are not equal. So we XOR corresponding bits and OR the results; if the OR output is 0, the inputs are equal, so output z should be 1. This approach leverages XOR and OR gates with a final inversion and may map efficiently to certain logic cells, offering a different logic structure that is functionally equivalent but architecturally distinct from the first approach.
