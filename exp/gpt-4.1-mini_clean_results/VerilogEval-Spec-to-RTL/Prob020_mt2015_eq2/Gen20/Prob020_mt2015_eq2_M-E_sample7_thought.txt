Implement the equality check using bitwise XNOR gates for each bit followed by an AND gate on their results. 

- This explicitly shows the bitwise comparison at the gate level without relying on the `==` operator.
- Each bit of A is XNORed with the corresponding bit of B to produce intermediate signals.
- The output z is the logical AND of these intermediate signals, indicating A equals B only if all bits match.
- This approach is fully combinational, minimal, and readily synthesizable.
- It provides a structural viewpoint of equality useful for teaching or synthesis tools favoring explicit gate-level constructs.
