To improve the design's power and area, I will use the simple vector equality operator (==) instead of explicit bitwise XNOR and AND gates. Modern synthesis tools efficiently optimize the (A == B) comparator, often resulting in fewer gates and lower power consumption compared to manual bitwise operations. This approach reduces code verbosity and potentially minimizes the gate count and switching activity, thereby improving overall Power, Performance, and Area (PPA). Given the negligible timing constraints here, this simplification is optimal.
