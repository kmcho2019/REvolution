To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we can manually implement the equality check using bitwise XNOR and an AND operation instead of the high-level equality operator. This allows synthesis tools to recognize and potentially optimize the logic into simpler gates or even a single equality comparator primitive in some technologies. Bitwise operations might produce slightly smaller and faster logic by explicitly controlling the structure of the circuit rather than relying on the compiler's inference of the equality operator. It may also reduce toggling by avoiding unnecessary transitions. The logic is straightforward: z = (A[1] XNOR B[1]) AND (A[0] XNOR B[0]).
