Implement the equality check by performing a bitwise XOR between the two 2-bit inputs. Since XOR yields 0 when bits are equal, OR all XOR result bits; if the OR result is 0, the inputs are equal, so output z=1; otherwise, output z=0. This can be done by first XOR-ing A and B, then NOR-ing the result bits to produce z. This approach uses explicit bitwise gates and reduces equality to a simple combination of XOR and NOR gates, making the logic structure clear and explicit.
