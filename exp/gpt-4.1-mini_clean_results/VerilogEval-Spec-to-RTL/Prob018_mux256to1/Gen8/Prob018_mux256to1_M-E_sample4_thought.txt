Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a fully combinational approach with a case statement inside a procedural block. The case statement will directly select the bit from the 256-bit input based on the 8-bit select signal. This approach is straightforward and synthesizes into an efficient mux tree by the synthesis tool without the need for hierarchical modules. It offers a flat design that can yield low latency and smaller area by letting the synthesis tool optimize the large multiplexer directly.
