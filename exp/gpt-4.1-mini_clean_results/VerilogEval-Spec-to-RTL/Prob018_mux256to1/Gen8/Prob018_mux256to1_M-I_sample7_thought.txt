To improve PPA, I replace the hierarchical 4-to-1 mux tree with a direct combinational indexing approach using an array-like selection of the input vector by the selector signal. This flat implementation reduces the total number of mux instances and avoids cascading logic levels, minimizing the critical path and area overhead from multiple mux stages and wiring. It also likely reduces dynamic power by eliminating intermediate signals and multiple stages of gating. Modern synthesis tools handle wide indexed selects efficiently and optimize mux trees internally. This approach simplifies code and should yield better area and power while maintaining zero delay on timing metrics. Given no explicit constraints on inferred mux style, this indexing method is the cleanest and most efficient way to implement a 256-to-1 mux in Verilog.
