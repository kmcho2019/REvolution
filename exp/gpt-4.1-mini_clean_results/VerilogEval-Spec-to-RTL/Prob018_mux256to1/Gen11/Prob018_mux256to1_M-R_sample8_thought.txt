Refactor the 256-to-1 multiplexer to fully utilize continuous assignments without procedural blocks. Implement the 16-to-1 mux as a purely combinational module using indexed part-select and assign statements rather than case statements and always blocks. For the TopModule, perform a two-level mux hierarchy where the first level produces 16 intermediate wires, each assigned by indexing its 16-bit slice with sel[3:0]. The second level output is assigned by indexing these intermediate wires with sel[7:4]. This approach simplifies the code, reduces register usage, and helps synthesis tools infer efficient mux trees from straightforward assignments.
