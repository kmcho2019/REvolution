Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer as a hierarchical structure of smaller 16-to-1 multiplexers arranged in a 4-level tree. At each level, select among 16 inputs using 4 bits of the select signal, progressively narrowing down to the final selected bit. This modular approach breaks down the large mux into smaller blocks, enhancing clarity and potentially allowing better synthesis optimization for area and timing by reusing 16-to-1 mux modules.
