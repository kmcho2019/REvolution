Refactor the 256-to-1 multiplexer by implementing it in a fully combinational style without instantiating separate 16-to-1 modules. Instead, use a single continuous assignment with a shifted indexing expression on the input vector. The 8-bit selector directly indexes the 256-bit input vector, producing the output bit.
