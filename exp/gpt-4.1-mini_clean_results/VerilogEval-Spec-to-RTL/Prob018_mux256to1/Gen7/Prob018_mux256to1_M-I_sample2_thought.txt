To optimize the original hierarchical 256-to-1 mux for better performance and area, we reduce the number of multiplexer levels by implementing a balanced 4-level mux tree, each using 4-to-1 multiplexers. This approach breaks the 8-bit selector into four 2-bit groups to index progressively smaller subsets of the input vector, reducing the mux fan-in at each stage and thereby the critical path delay and area. The 4-to-1 muxes are smaller than 16-to-1 muxes, so the logic is simpler and should have lower delay and area. The tree structure has a balanced depth of 4, improving timing over the original two-level 16-to-1 mux tree with large fan-in. This hierarchical approach also reduces glitching and switching activity because each stage only selects among four inputs. Overall, this design trades more stages with simpler multiplexers to optimize timing and area effectively.
