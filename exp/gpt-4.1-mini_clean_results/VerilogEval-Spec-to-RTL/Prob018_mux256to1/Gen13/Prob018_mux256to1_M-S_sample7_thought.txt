The simplest and most straightforward way to implement a 256-to-1 mux in Verilog is to use the indexing operator directly on the input vector with the selection input. This approach results in very concise code, is easy to understand, and lets the synthesis tool handle optimized mux implementation internally. Although this may synthesize to a large mux, it avoids the complexity and overhead of multiple levels of smaller multiplexers. This direct approach preserves correct functionality and reduces code complexity significantly.
