Implement the 256-to-1 mux using a flat case statement for direct indexing. This approach leverages the built-in Verilog vector indexing and a single combinational always block with a case statement to select the output bit based on sel.

- Avoid hierarchical mux modules and generate loops.
- Use a single always_comb block and case statement for sel [7:0].
