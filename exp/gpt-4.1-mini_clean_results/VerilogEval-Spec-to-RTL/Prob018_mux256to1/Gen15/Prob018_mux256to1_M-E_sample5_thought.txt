Implement the 256-to-1 mux using a single continuous assignment with indexed bit selection, relying on Verilog's ability to directly index into a vector by a variable. This approach eliminates the need for any hierarchy or procedural logic, resulting in minimal and clean RTL code. The synthesis tool will infer a wide mux optimized for area and power. This is the simplest and most straightforward approach for a purely combinational mux with a wide input vector and a wide select signal.
