Optimize the 256-to-1 multiplexer by replacing the hierarchical instantiation of multiple 16-to-1 mux modules and procedural case statements with a single continuous assignment using indexed bit selection. This leverages Verilog's ability to directly index a vector with a variable, allowing the synthesis tool to infer an efficient multiplexer tree without extra logic overhead. This approach reduces area and power, simplifies the code, and improves synthesis optimization while maintaining zero delay and correct functionality.
