Instead of building a hierarchical mux tree with 4-to-1 multiplexers, we can implement a simple flattened 256-to-1 multiplexer by directly indexing into the input vector using the sel input as an index. This approach uses Verilog's vector bit-select capability (in[sel]) to pick the correct bit directly. It leads to very simple and concise code, and synthesis tools can optimize it well, potentially generating a minimal-area and low-power multiplexer. The critical path may be slightly longer due to the wide indexing, but modern tools handle wide multiplexers efficiently. This design avoids multiple mux instances, reduces wiring complexity, and is ideal for clarity and area optimization at moderate frequencies.
