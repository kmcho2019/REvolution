To improve the power, area, and potentially timing of the original hierarchical mux, I will simplify the design by leveraging the synthesis tool's ability to infer large multiplexers directly from indexed continuous assignments. Instead of instantiating multiple 16-to-1 mux modules with case statements, I will implement the entire 256-to-1 mux using a single continuous assignment with direct bit indexing: `out = in[sel];`. This approach removes all intermediate signals and procedural blocks, resulting in minimal logic, less switching activity, and smaller area. It also simplifies the code, making it easier to understand and maintain. This style is well supported by synthesis tools and typically leads to better PPA for large muxes.
