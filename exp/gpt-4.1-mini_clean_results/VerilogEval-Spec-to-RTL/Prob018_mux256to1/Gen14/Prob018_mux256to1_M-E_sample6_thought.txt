Implement the 256-to-1 mux as a balanced 4-level tree of 4-to-1 multiplexers to minimize the critical path depth and fan-in.

Architecture:
- Level 0: 64 instances of Mux4to1, each selecting one bit out of 4 bits from the input vector based on sel[1:0].
- Level 1: 16 instances of Mux4to1, each selecting one bit from 4 outputs of level 0, based on sel[3:2].
- Level 2: 4 instances of Mux4to1, each selecting one bit from 4 outputs of level 1, based on sel[5:4].
- Level 3: 1 instance of Mux4to1, selecting one bit from 4 outputs of level 2, based on sel[7:6].

This hierarchical approach results in a balanced mux tree with only 4 layers of 4-to-1 muxes (mux depth 4), improving timing over 8-to-1 mux hierarchies.

Key advantages:
- Uniform use of 4-to-1 muxes simplifies design and reuse.
- Smaller fan-in muxes (4 inputs each) allow better synthesis optimization.
- Balanced tree reduces the maximum mux level, improving propagation delay.
- Straightforward use of sel slices without overlapping bits simplifies wiring and control.

The final output is the output of the top-level Mux4to1.

The Mux4to1 module is parameterized for generality and implemented with a case statement for clarity.

All muxes are purely combinational.

This design favors critical path delay improvement and design clarity with a novel mux tree structure different from the previous solution.
