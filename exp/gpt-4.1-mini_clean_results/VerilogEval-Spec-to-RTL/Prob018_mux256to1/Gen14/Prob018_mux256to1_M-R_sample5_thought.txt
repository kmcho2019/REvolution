Refactor the TopModule to a fully combinational and flattened implementation using a direct index selection from the input vector without hierarchical internal mux modules. Use a single assign statement for the output: out = in[sel]. This eliminates multiple levels of mux instances, reduces area and power, and improves performance by minimizing combinational delay. The internal Mux4to1 and Mux8to1 modules become unnecessary since the selection is directly done by indexing. This approach leverages Verilog's indexed bit selection, which synthesis tools efficiently implement as a large multiplexer.

Advantages:
- Simpler and more concise code.
- Reduced critical path due to removal of mux hierarchy.
- Lower area due to no replicated mux modules.
- Likely reduced dynamic power because fewer gates toggle.

This keeps the exact same functionality: selecting one bit from 256 based on the 8-bit selector sel.
