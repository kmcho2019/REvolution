To optimize the existing hierarchical 256-to-1 mux for better power, area, and potentially timing, I will remove the procedural always blocks with case statements and replace them with direct continuous assignments. Specifically, I will implement the entire 256-to-1 mux in TopModule as a single continuous assignment using Verilog's indexed bit selection (out = in[sel]) which is the most compact and efficient way for synthesis tools to infer a large mux tree.

This approach eliminates the overhead of multiple intermediate signals and hierarchies, reducing logic replication and register inference risk, resulting in simpler, smaller, and lower-power hardware. The single continuous assignment style is widely supported and generally results in optimal mux implementations by synthesis tools.

The Mux16to1 module and the hierarchical structure are removed to minimize logic and code complexity, directly implementing the functionality as requested with a single line continuous assignment.

This is expected to produce the best PPA while preserving the exact functional behavior.
