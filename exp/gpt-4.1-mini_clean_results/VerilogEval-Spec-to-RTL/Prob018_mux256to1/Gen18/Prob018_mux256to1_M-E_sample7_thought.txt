Implement the 256-to-1 mux as a single-level priority encoder style mux by using a variable part-select with the 'sel' index directly indexing into the 256-bit input vector. This approach leverages Verilog's indexed bit-select for a clean, combinational, and fully parallel mux without hierarchical instantiation. It minimizes logic depth to essentially one level of decoding and multiplexing. This solution is concise and synthesizable, with potentially better delay and area compared to a multi-level mux tree. However, large fan-in muxes might be generated by synthesis tools, which might need consideration. Overall, this is the simplest and most direct approach to implement a wide mux in RTL.
