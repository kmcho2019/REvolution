Refactor the hierarchical 256-to-1 multiplexer by flattening the hierarchy into a single-level generate block that instantiates 256 1-bit 2-to-1 muxes in a tree structure, connecting them stepwise from the 256 inputs down to the single output. This approach replaces the multiple levels of 4-to-1 muxes with a balanced binary tree of 2-to-1 muxes, organizing wires for each stage with explicit indexing and generate loops. The sel bits will be consumed one by one at each tree level, resulting in 8 levels (since 2^8=256). This maintains modularity with a small 2-to-1 mux module, clarifies the mux tree structure, and uses `assign` statements for direct combinational connections instead of nested mux modules with wide selects.
