Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a fully combinational dataflow style with a single continuous assignment. Since the input is a 256-bit vector and the selection is 8-bit, the output can be assigned directly by indexing the input vector with the select signal. This approach requires minimal hardware and leverages the synthesis tool's ability to infer an efficient mux tree for indexed bit selection. It is the most compact and likely the lowest area and power solution, while being fully combinational and simple.
