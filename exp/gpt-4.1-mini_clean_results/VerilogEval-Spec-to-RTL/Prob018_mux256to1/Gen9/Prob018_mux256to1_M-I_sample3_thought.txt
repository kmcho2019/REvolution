To optimize for better power and area while maintaining zero timing slack, I will simplify the design by removing the hierarchical instantiation of 16-to-1 mux modules. Instead, I will use a single continuous assignment that directly indexes the 256-bit input vector with the 8-bit select signal. This style leverages the synthesis tool's ability to infer an efficient mux tree, typically resulting in better area and power due to less redundant logic and simpler routing. The code becomes more concise, easier to maintain, and has no combinational always blocks or case statements, which might reduce logic replication. Since the output is a single bit selected from a wide vector, direct indexed selection is the cleanest and most efficient approach.
