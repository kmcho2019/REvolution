To improve PPA while preserving correctness, replace the hierarchical multiplexer tree with a simple direct indexing of the input vector by the select signal. This flattens the mux to a single combinational assignment, eliminating intermediate mux modules and reducing logic depth. This approach leverages synthesis tool optimizations for vector indexing to reduce area, power, and delay while maintaining the same functionality.

This reduces:
- Logic depth from 3 levels of muxes to 1.
- Number of instantiated modules and intermediate wires.
- Switching power due to fewer active logic elements.

The resulting design is simpler, more efficient for synthesis, and likely yields better timing, power, and area metrics.
