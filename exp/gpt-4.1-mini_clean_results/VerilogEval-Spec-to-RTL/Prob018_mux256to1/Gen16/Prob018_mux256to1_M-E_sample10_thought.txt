Implement the 256-to-1 1-bit mux as a fully behavioral case statement driven by the 8-bit sel input. This approach leverages the synthesis tool's ability to infer an optimized large multiplexer directly from a simple, flat, readable case structure without manual hierarchical mux instantiations. It minimizes structural complexity and possibly area by allowing the tool to choose the best mux implementation (e.g., LUT-based or tree-based) without our explicit hierarchy constraints.

Advantages:
- Single combinational process, very clean and concise.
- Synthesis tool has full freedom to optimize.
- Minimal intermediate wires and modules.
- Easier to maintain and verify.

This approach contrasts with hierarchical mux trees and trades explicit manual control of mux granularity for synthesis tool's optimization capabilities.
