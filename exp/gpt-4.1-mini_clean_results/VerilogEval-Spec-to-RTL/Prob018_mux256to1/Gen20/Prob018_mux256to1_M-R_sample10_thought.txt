Refactor the 256-to-1 multiplexer to a fully flat, combinational implementation using a single large continuous assignment with bit slicing and a one-level multiplexer expression. This approach uses a single `assign` statement with a bit-select indexed by the `sel` input, which is naturally synthesizable in modern tools and typically inferred as an optimized multiplexer.

This removes the hierarchical sub-module instantiations and generate loops, simplifying the structure and potentially reducing overall area and power by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the mux directly.

The behavioral style remains purely combinational and concise, improving readability and maintainability, while still meeting the design specification exactly.
