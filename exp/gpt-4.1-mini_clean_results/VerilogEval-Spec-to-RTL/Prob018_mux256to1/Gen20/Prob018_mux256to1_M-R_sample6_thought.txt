Refactor the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a flattened approach by leveraging Verilog's indexed part-select and a single continuous assignment statement without hierarchical mux modules. 

- Instead of building a hierarchy of smaller mux modules, directly select the bit from the 256-bit input vector using the 8-bit selector as the index.
