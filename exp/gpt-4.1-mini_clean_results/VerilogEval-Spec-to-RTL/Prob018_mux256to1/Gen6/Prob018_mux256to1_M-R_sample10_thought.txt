Refactor the 256-to-1 multiplexer by leveraging Verilog's built-in indexed vector selection with a simple continuous assignment `assign out = in[sel];`. This behavioral description eliminates the need for explicit two-to-one mux instantiations and generate loops. Synthesis tools will infer an optimized multiplexer tree internally. This approach greatly simplifies the code, improves readability, and can help synthesis tools optimize area and power more effectively while maintaining the same functional behavior and timing.
