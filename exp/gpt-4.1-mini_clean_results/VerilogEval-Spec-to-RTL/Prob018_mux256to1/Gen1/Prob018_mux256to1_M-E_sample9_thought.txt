Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer as a hierarchy of smaller multiplexers organized as an 8-stage tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers. Each stage uses one bit of the 'sel' input to select between pairs of inputs from the previous stage. This creates a binary tree structure of 2-to-1 muxes, reducing gate fan-in and potentially improving timing compared to a flat 256-to-1 mux. The final output is the result after all 8 stages of selection. This approach leverages generate blocks and simple 2-to-1 mux modules to build the overall mux.
