Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer by hierarchically breaking it down into a two-stage approach: first, partition the 256-bit input vector into 16 groups of 16 bits each; then use the upper 4 bits of the selector to choose which group to select from, and the lower 4 bits to select the bit within that 16-bit group. This reduces the complexity in each stage, potentially improving synthesis optimization. The first stage outputs a 16-bit vector selected by the upper 4 bits, and the second stage picks the bit indexed by the lower 4 bits within that 16-bit vector.
