Refactor the design to implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a hierarchical structure composed of smaller 16-to-1 multiplexers. The 256 inputs are divided into 16 groups of 16 bits each. Each group is first multiplexed down to a single bit using a 16-to-1 mux selected by the lower 4 bits of sel. Then, the 16 outputs of these smaller muxes are themselves multiplexed using another 16-to-1 mux selected by the upper 4 bits of sel. This hierarchical approach makes the mux logic more explicit and structured, which can help synthesis tools optimize timing and area by balancing the mux tree, compared to a single flat indexing operation.
