To improve the PPA (power, performance, area) for this 256-to-1 multiplexer, implement the mux hierarchically as an 8-stage tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers. Each stage reduces the input width by half, thus distributing the selection logic across multiple smaller muxes. This approach shortens the combinational path, improves timing by reducing logic depth, and can reduce area by allowing better optimization. Power can also be improved because fewer signals toggle simultaneously in one flat large mux. The hierarchical structure uses simple 2:1 muxes instantiated in a generate loop, controlled by each bit of the select signal in sequence, making the design clean and scalable.
