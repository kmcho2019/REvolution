To improve timing and area efficiency, implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer hierarchically by breaking it into two stages: first, 32 multiplexers each selecting 1 of 8 bits (8-to-1 mux), then a final 8-to-1 mux selecting among those 32 outputs. This reduces the maximum fan-in per multiplexer, thus reducing critical path delay and potentially area. The selector 'sel' can be split into two parts: lower 3 bits select within each 8-bit group, and upper 5 bits select which group output to forward. This hierarchical approach helps optimize logic depth and switching activity.
