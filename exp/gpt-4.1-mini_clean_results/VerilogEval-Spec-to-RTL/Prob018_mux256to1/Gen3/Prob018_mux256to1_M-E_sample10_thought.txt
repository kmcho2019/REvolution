Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a balanced 8-level binary tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers. Each level halves the number of inputs by selecting between pairs based on one bit of `sel`, starting from the least significant bit. This approach uses a cascade of 2:1 muxes, minimizing fan-in at each stage, improving timing by reducing large multiplexers, and potentially optimizing area and power by better balancing the logic tree.
