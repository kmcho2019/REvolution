Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer by directly indexing the 256-bit input vector `in` with the 8-bit select signal `sel` using a continuous assignment. This approach leverages Verilog's ability to perform variable indexing on vectors, which synthesizes efficiently into a multiplexer tree internally. It is the simplest and most area- and power-efficient way to realize this functionality, minimizing logic, potential glitches, and improving synthesis optimization. The output is updated combinationally based on the input and selection without any explicit case or always blocks.
