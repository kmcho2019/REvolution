Combine the clarity and procedural style of an always block with the efficiency of direct indexed selection. Implement the 256-to-1 mux as a combinational always block that directly assigns `out = in[sel]`. This retains explicit RTL procedural style (which some tools and designers prefer for clarity and debugging) while avoiding the large case statement overhead, resulting in a simpler, smaller, and more power-efficient design. This fusion merges readability with optimal PPA characteristics.
