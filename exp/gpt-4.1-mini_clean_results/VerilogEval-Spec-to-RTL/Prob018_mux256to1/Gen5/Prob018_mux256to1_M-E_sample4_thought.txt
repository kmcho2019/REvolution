Use a purely behavioral approach to implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer by directly indexing the input vector 'in' with the select input 'sel'. This leverages the synthesis tool's ability to efficiently implement large multiplexers through optimized hardware primitives or logic trees internally, removing the need for hierarchical explicit mux instantiations.

The one-line indexing `out = in[sel];` is concise and produces minimal RTL, which can result in optimal area, power, and timing after synthesis. This flat approach minimizes gate count, reduces routing complexity, and removes redundant intermediate signals.

This solution is very clean, highly readable, and synthesizes efficiently on modern tools. It is a completely different architecture compared to hierarchical mux trees and is well-suited for combinational multiplexing with wide inputs.
