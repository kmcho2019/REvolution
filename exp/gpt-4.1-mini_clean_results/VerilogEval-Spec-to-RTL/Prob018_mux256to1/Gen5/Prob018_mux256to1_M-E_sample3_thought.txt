Implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer by leveraging Verilog's ability to index vectors directly with the selection input. Since the input is a packed vector and sel is an 8-bit index, the output can be assigned directly using a continuous assignment: `out = in[sel];`. This approach is the most straightforward, minimal, and synthesizable, resulting in an optimal design in terms of area, power, and performance without any hierarchical mux structures or case statements.
