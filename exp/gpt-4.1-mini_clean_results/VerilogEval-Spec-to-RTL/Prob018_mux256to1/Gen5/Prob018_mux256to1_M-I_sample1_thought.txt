To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), replace the hierarchical tree of 2-to-1 mux modules with a direct behavioral indexing approach: assign out = in[sel]. This lets the synthesis tool infer a large multiplexer optimized for the target technology, potentially using dedicated mux primitives, resulting in reduced area and power compared to manual mux trees. It also eliminates the multiple levels of mux stages, improving delay. The code is simplified, fully combinational, and easy to verify.
