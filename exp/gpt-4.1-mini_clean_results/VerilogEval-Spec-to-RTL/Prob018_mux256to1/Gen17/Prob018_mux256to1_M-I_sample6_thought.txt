To improve power, area, and timing (PPA), we can eliminate the multi-level hierarchical mux tree and replace it with a direct indexed assign statement for the 256-to-1 mux. Since the selection input is 8 bits and the input is a 256-bit vector, a direct assignment `out = in[sel]` is both logically equivalent and far more optimal.

This approach yields:
- Zero mux hierarchy depth and associated delays.
- No intermediate signals, reducing switching power.
- Minimal logic and wiring, reducing area.
- Simpler and clearer code.

Most modern synthesis tools can efficiently implement wide multiplexers directly when given indexed access, so this approach is superior unless specific constraints forbid it.

Therefore, the improved implementation is a simple module with a direct continuous assignment for `out` using `sel` as an index.

No submodules or generate loops needed.
