The existing solution is functionally correct, using a balanced 4-level 4-to-1 mux tree for the 256-to-1 mux. While this approach is modular and clear, it introduces many intermediate signals and mux instances, leading to higher area and power consumption due to switching on multiple levels.

To optimize for better PPA, we can leverage Verilog's direct vector indexing capability to implement the 256-to-1 mux as a single continuous assignment: `assign out = in[sel];`. Modern synthesis tools efficiently handle such indexed selections, producing minimal logic with the best possible timing and area.

This approach:
- Eliminates intermediate signals and multiple mux instances, reducing area and power.
- Minimizes logic depth and routing complexity, improving timing.
- Maintains functional correctness.
- Provides the simplest, most concise code.

Therefore, the improved design replaces the hierarchical tree with a single direct index assignment in TopModule and removes the Mux4to1 module entirely.
