Fuse the strengths of both examples by leveraging the simple, synthesis-friendly direct vector indexing for the top-level 256-to-1 mux selection while maintaining the clear, modular 4-to-1 and 16-to-1 mux hierarchy internally. 

Fusion Strategy:
- Use the direct indexing feature of Verilog at the top-level module to select the output bit from the 256-bit input vector, eliminating unnecessary mux hierarchy and thus reducing logic depth, area, and power.
- Keep the well-structured Mux4to1 and Mux16to1 modules built as balanced trees of 4-to-1 muxes available as reusable building blocks (good modularity and clarity).
- Provide these mux modules for potential reuse elsewhere, but do not instantiate them inside TopModule for the 256-to-1 mux to maximize PPA.
- This hybrid approach benefits from:
  - Example 1 and 2’s modular 4-to-1 mux structure for clarity, testability, and smaller mux fan-in.
  - Example 1’s hierarchical concept, but avoids unnecessary muxing by leveraging synthesis tool support for direct vector indexing in TopModule.
  - Resulting in a zero logic depth from TopModule muxing, minimal area, and lowest power, while preserving module clarity and reusability.

In effect, the final TopModule becomes a clean, one-line multiplexer using vector select, and the auxiliary mux modules remain available for other design parts, verification, or future expansion.
