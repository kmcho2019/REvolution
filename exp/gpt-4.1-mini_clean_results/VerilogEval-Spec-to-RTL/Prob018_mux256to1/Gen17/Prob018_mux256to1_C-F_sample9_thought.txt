Fuse the hierarchical 4-to-1 mux tree of Example 1 with the efficient two-level 16-to-1 mux tree of Example 2 by:

- Implementing a parameterized Mux4to1 module as a simple indexed 4-to-1 mux.
- Constructing a parameterized Mux16to1 module from four Mux4to1 modules and one final Mux4to1, as in Example 2, for optimal balanced delay.
- Building the top 256-to-1 mux as a two-level hierarchy:
  - Level 1: 16 Mux16to1 instances, each selecting 1 bit out of 16 bits of the 256-bit input, using sel[3:0].
  - Level 2: One final Mux16to1 selecting among the 16 outputs from level 1 using sel[7:4].
- This reduces mux tree depth (only 2 levels at the top), limits fan-in at each mux, keeps hierarchical clarity, and optimizes performance and area balance.
- The explicit generate blocks and wire arrays make the structure clear for synthesis and easy to read.
- Keeps modularity and scalable hierarchy, avoids a large flat mux or very deep trees, achieving superior PPA by merging both examples’ best architectural traits.
