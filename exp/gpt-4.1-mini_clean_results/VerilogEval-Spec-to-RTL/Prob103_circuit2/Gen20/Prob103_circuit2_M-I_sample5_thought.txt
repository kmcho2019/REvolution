The current implementation uses a straightforward XOR of all inputs followed by a NOT to generate even parity, which is already minimal in terms of logic depth and gate count for a 4-input parity function. To improve PPA (performance, power, area), we can factor the XOR chain into two 2-input XORs combined with a final 2-input XOR and then invert. This slight structural change can help synthesis tools optimize for lower power by balancing the logic tree and potentially reduce switching activity through better gate sharing or sizing. We maintain the same logical function (even parity) with no increase in delay or area.
