The existing implementation uses a reduction XOR followed by a negation to implement even parity, which is already minimal and efficient. To improve PPA, we can remove the inversion operator by expressing even parity as XNOR of all inputs, which synthesizes to balanced XOR/XNOR trees potentially with less delay and slightly lower power. Using the reduction XNOR operator (^~) or chaining XNORs can avoid the explicit inversion step and may optimize logic synthesis further. This simplifies the logic expression and can give better PPA with equivalent functionality.
