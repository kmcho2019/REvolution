The function is the even parity of four inputs, which can be computed using the XOR operator combined with a NOT at the output. Since even parity means an even number of '1's, the XOR of all inputs is 1 for odd parity; thus, the output q is the negation of the XOR of inputs. This can be directly written as q = !(a ^ b ^ c ^ d), which is simpler and more intuitive.
