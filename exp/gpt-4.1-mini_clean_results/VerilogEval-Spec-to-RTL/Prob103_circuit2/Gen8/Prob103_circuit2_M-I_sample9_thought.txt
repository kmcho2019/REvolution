The current solution is already minimal in logic depth and area since parity functions inherently require XOR/XNOR operations. However, we can slightly improve power by restructuring the logic to balance the gate tree, avoiding a long chain of XNORs which could create a longer critical path and potentially more switching.

  q = ~((a ^ b) ^ (c ^ d))
This breaks the XNOR chain into balanced XOR pairs, potentially improving timing and reducing dynamic power.
