Implement the even parity function by using a hierarchical approach of XOR gates grouped pairwise, instead of summation or a single XOR chain. Specifically:

- First compute intermediate XORs of pairs (a XOR b) and (c XOR d).
- Then XOR these two results together to get the final parity output.

This architecture resembles a balanced XOR tree which potentially reduces the logic depth compared to a linear XOR chain, and differs from the adder or single XOR line approaches.

Additionally, instead of directly assigning q as the parity bit, invert the final XOR output so that q=1 when parity is even, matching the waveform.

This design is purely combinational, uses minimal gates, and emphasizes structural clarity with explicit intermediate wires, enabling easy modifications or extensions. It demonstrates a simple balanced tree structure for parity calculation.
