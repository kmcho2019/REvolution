The original design correctly implements the logic by producing the inverted parity of inputs a, b, c, and d, which matches the waveform. To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the always block with a continuous assign statement. This will simplify the design, reducing synthesis overhead and potentially enabling better optimization by synthesis tools. Using a continuous assign removes the need for a reg type and procedural block, which reduces code complexity and can lead to slightly smaller area and power. The logic remains combinational and straightforward as an XOR followed by inversion, which is optimal for 4-input parity. This change improves succinctness and synthesis friendliness without altering functionality.
