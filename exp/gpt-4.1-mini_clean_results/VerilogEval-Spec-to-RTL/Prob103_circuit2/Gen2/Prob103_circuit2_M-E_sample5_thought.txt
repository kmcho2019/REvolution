Instead of using XOR gates, implement the logic by expressing q in terms of the parity of the inputs using AND-OR-NOT logic. From the truth table, q is high when the number of ones in the inputs is even. We can encode parity as a sum of product terms representing even parity patterns. Then, q is 1 for these specific input combinations. We can write the even parity minterms explicitly and combine them using OR and AND gates, then assign q directly without XORs. This shows an alternative to XOR-based parity generation.
