A different way to implement the same even parity function is to use intermediate signals representing pairs of inputs XNORed together, then combine these intermediate results with another XNOR gate.

Specifically, compute two intermediate signals:
- w1 = XNOR of inputs a and b
- w2 = XNOR of inputs c and d

Then, the final output q is the XNOR of w1 and w2.

This approach uses a hierarchical structure of XNOR gates, which can be beneficial in certain design flows by balancing logic depth and improving readability. It also explicitly shows the pairing and recombination, offering an architectural alternative to the direct XOR inversion method.

This design still produces the even parity output but structures the logic in two stages instead of a single expression.
