The original implementation uses a 4-input XOR followed by an inversion to compute even parity, which is already concise and minimal.

For improved PPA:
- To reduce glitching and potentially lower dynamic power, implement the XOR in a balanced two-level tree instead of a flat XOR4.
- Use two 2-input XORs in the first stage, then XOR their outputs in the second stage, and finally invert the result.
- This reduces the fan-in at each gate, potentially reducing delay and power by lowering glitch susceptibility.
- Also, explicitly instantiate the intermediate wires for clarity and optimization friendliness by synthesis tools.

This balanced XOR tree is a standard practice for multi-input parity circuits for better timing and power characteristics.
