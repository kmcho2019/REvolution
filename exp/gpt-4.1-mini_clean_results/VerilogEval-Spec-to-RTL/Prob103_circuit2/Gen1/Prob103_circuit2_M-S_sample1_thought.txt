The original solution implements q as the even parity function of four inputs, using an XNOR of all inputs. Since XNOR is associative and commutative, we can implement q simply by applying the reduction XNOR operator over all inputs at once using Verilog's reduction operator (~^), which reduces verbosity and complexity.

Thus, q = ~^(a,b,c,d) is a minimal and simple one-line expression that maintains the same functionality.
