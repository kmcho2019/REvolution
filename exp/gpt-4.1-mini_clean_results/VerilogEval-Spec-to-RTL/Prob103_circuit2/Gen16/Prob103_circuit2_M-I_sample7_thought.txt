The original design uses XNOR gates for partial parity calculations and combines them with another XNOR, resulting in multiple inversions that could increase switching activity and gate count. 

Since XNOR is XOR followed by NOT, the entire expression can be simplified by directly using XOR on all inputs and then inverting the final output once. This reduces the number of inversions and intermediate signals, which may lower power consumption and area while maintaining the same functionality. 

Thus, the improved design computes the output as the negation of the XOR of all four inputs, implemented in a single expression without intermediate wires.
