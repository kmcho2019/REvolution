- Then compute p2 = c XNOR d = ~(c ^ d)
- Finally, q = p1 XNOR p2 = ~(p1 ^ p2)

This breaks the logic into balanced two-level XNORs, potentially enabling better gate sharing and reduced glitching compared to a four-input XOR+negation.
