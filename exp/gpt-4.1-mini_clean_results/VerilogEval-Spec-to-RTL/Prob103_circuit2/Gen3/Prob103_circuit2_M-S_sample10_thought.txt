The function q outputs 1 when the parity of inputs a, b, c, d is even (XNOR of all inputs). Since an XOR gate outputs 1 for odd parity, negating XOR directly implements even parity. This is already the minimal and simplest Boolean expression for this function. Any attempt to rewrite the expression (e.g., with AND/OR combinations or sum-of-products) would increase complexity. Hence, the original assignment using one XOR chain followed by negation is already minimal in terms of gate count and complexity.
