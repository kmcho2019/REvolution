The current implementation uses a XOR followed by a NOT to produce even parity detection. Since the output q is the XNOR of all inputs, this is already the simplest logical form for even parity output. Any further simplification would just be a cosmetic change, such as using the Verilog reduction operator explicitly. Using the XOR reduction operator (^) and negating it (~) is both compact and clear, so the implementation cannot be simplified further without losing clarity or functionality.
