Another way to implement the 4-input XNOR is to pairwise XNOR the inputs and then XNOR the results. Specifically, compute x1 = a XNOR b, x2 = c XNOR d, then q = x1 XNOR x2. Since XNOR is associative and commutative, this produces the same result as the 4-input XNOR.

This approach modularizes the design using intermediate signals, which may aid readability and potential optimization in some synthesis tools.
