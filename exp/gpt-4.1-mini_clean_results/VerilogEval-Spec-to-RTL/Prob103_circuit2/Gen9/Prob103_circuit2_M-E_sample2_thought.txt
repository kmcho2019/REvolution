The waveform corresponds to the even parity function of inputs a, b, c, and d (output q is 1 if an even number of inputs are 1). To implement this with a different architectural style, I will use a binary tree of XOR gates computing the parity step-by-step, then invert the final result to produce even parity.

Specifically:
- Compute intermediate xor1 = a ^ b
- Compute intermediate xor2 = c ^ d
- Compute xor3 = xor1 ^ xor2 (overall parity)
- Invert xor3 to get even parity output q = ~xor3

This approach uses XOR gates grouped as a balanced binary tree and a final inverter, making the structure clear and modular, differing from chaining XNOR gates directly.
