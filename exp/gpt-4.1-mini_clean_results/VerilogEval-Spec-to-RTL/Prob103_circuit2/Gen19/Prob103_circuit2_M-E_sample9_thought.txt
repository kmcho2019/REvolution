Analyzing the waveform, q outputs '1' when the number of inputs set to '1' is even (including zero), which defines an even parity function. Instead of directly computing XOR or XNOR of all inputs, I will implement the even parity by dividing inputs into pairs, XNOR each pair (detecting even parity per pair), and then AND the results to get the overall even parity. This modular approach uses XNOR and AND gates to realize the same function with a different internal structure.
