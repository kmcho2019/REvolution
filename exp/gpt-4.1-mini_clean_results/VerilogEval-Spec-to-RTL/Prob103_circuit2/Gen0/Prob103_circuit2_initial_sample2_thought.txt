We can notice that q=1 when the number of 1's in abcd is even:

Check the number of ones for each input where q=1:

Indeed, q=1 if the parity of inputs is even.

Therefore, q = ~(a ^ b ^ c ^ d), i.e. the even parity of the inputs.
