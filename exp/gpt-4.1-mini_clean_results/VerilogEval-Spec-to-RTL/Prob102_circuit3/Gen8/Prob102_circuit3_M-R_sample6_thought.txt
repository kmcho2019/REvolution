The original implementation uses NAND gates to build NOT, OR, and AND functions explicitly. To refactor with the same core logic q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d), I will directly use assign statements with the logical operators `|` and `&`. This simplifies the structure, reduces intermediate wires, and improves readability while preserving the exact combinational behavior. It also avoids unnecessary double negations and redundant NAND constructs, leading to potentially better synthesis results.
