The logic function from the waveforms is q = (a | b) & (c | d). The existing solution uses De Morgan’s law to express it as q = ~((~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d)) which introduces extra inverters and wires, increasing power and area slightly. To optimize for power and area without affecting performance, implement the direct logic expression q = (a | b) & (c | d). This reduces the number of gates, removes unnecessary inversions, and simplifies wiring, which lowers power consumption and area while maintaining zero delay.
