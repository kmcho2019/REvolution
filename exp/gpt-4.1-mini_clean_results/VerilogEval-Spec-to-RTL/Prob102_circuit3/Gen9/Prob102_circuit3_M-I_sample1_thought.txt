The original implementation uses multiple levels of NAND gates to build NOT, OR, and AND operations, resulting in extra logic depth and area due to repeated inversions and intermediate signals. The function based on the truth table is q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d). To improve performance, power, and area, I will implement this function directly using Verilog's built-in logical operators without redundant inversions or gate decompositions. This reduces logic depth, signal toggling, and gate count while preserving correctness.
