The original design implements q = (a | b) & (c | d) directly and efficiently, which matches the waveform exactly and uses minimal gates.

To optimize further for power and area while maintaining timing:

- Combine the two OR gates into a single OR gate by using continuous assignment for the OR result without intermediate wires, slightly reducing wiring complexity.
- Use an expression that synthesizers easily map to minimal logic.
- Removing intermediate wires reduces net count and small area.
- The combinational logic remains two-level, minimal delay.
- This direct expression has minimal glitch potential and is straightforward for synthesis tools to optimize.

Overall, I maintain the same logical function with a more concise style to reduce minor wiring overhead and improve clarity.
