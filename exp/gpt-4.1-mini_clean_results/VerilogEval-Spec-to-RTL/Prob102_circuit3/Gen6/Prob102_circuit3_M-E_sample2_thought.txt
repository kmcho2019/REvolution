An alternative implementation is to express the logic using De Morgan's laws and NAND gates only. The logic q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d) can be rewritten as q = NOT ( (NOT (a OR b)) OR (NOT (c OR d)) ). This means q = NAND of (NOT (a OR b)) and (NOT (c OR d)).

Implementing this, first compute na_b = ~(a | b), nc_d = ~(c | d), then q = ~(na_b | nc_d).

This uses only NAND and NOR style gates, which can be beneficial for certain standard cell libraries and might reduce delay or power depending on the target technology.

This design maintains purely combinational logic with minimal depth (3 levels) and uses inverters implicitly via NAND/NOR, trading off slightly more gates for potential benefits in optimization.
