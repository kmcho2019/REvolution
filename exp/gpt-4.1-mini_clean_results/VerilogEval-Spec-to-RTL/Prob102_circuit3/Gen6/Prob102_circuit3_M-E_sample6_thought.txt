To implement the same logic q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d) with a different structural approach, I will use NAND and NOR gates instead of just AND and OR gates. Recall De Morgan's laws: (a OR b) = NAND(NAND(a, a), NAND(b, b)) and (c OR d) = NAND(NAND(c, c), NAND(d, d)). Then q = AND(x,y) can be written as NAND(NAND(x,y), NAND(x,y)). By building the OR gates from NANDs and AND from NANDs, I will realize the function purely using NAND gates, which is a universal gate and often preferred in CMOS for uniformity and possible optimizations.
