To optimize for performance, power, and area (PPA), the expression q = (a|b) & (c|d) is more efficient than the sum-of-products form. This reduces the gate count from 5 to 3 and minimizes logic depth, improving timing. Also, fewer gates reduce switching activity, saving power, and smaller gate count reduces area. Therefore, the implementation will assign q as (a | b) & (c | d).
