Recall that q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d) can also be expressed using De Morgan's laws as:

q = NOT( (NOT (a OR b)) OR (NOT (c OR d)) )
  = NOT( (NOT a AND NOT b) OR (NOT c AND NOT d) )

This suggests the logic can be implemented as q = NAND( NAND(a,b), NAND(c,d) ).

Thus, I will implement intermediate signals as NANDs of inputs and then NAND these intermediate signals together to produce q. This structure uses only NAND gates and no explicit OR gates. This structure may be beneficial in technologies where NAND gates are more optimal.
