The output function q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d) has already been correctly implemented as q = (a&c) | (a&d) | (b&c) | (b&d). To optimize PPA (performance, power, area), we should reduce the gate count and logic depth by factoring the expression explicitly as q = (a | b) & (c | d). This reduces the number of gates from five to three, lowers switching activity, and shortens the critical path from two levels of AND-OR to just OR-AND, improving all PPA metrics.
