The output q is 1 when (a or b) and (c or d) is true. This is equivalent to q = (a | b) & (c | d). To simplify further, note that q can also be expressed as the OR of four product terms: a&c, a&d, b&c, b&d. Since this has four terms, it’s more complex than the original. The original expression is already minimal in terms of literals and gates. No simpler form exists. Thus, the original implementation is already the simplest minimal logic for the given function.
