Instead of using OR and AND gates directly, implement the logic by using De Morgan’s laws: q = (a | b) & (c | d) is equivalent to q = ~((~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d)). This approach uses only NAND and NOR style logic by negating inputs and outputs, which can be advantageous in certain gate libraries or to encourage NAND/NOR dominance for technology mapping. This gives a different structural perspective on the same combinational function.
