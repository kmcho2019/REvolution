We can express the same logic in an alternative way by first combining the inputs in pairs using AND operations, then OR-ing the results to produce q. Specifically, q is 1 if any of the following are true: (a AND c), (a AND d), (b AND c), or (b AND d). This is logically equivalent to q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d), but restructured using ANDs and ORs in a sum-of-products form. This achieves the same function but with a different gate-level structure.
