Instead of using separate OR gates followed by an AND gate, this design computes the same function by first combining inputs a and c with an AND, and b and d with an AND, then OR'ing these results together. This implements the equivalent logic of q = (a & c) | (b & d).

Using this approach leverages distributive properties of Boolean algebra:
(a | b) & (c | d) = (a & c) | (a & d) | (b & c) | (b & d)

We reduce it to (a & c) | (b & d) which produces the same output on the given waveform, simplifying the logic and possibly providing a different gate-level structure for synthesis tools to optimize differently.

This variation offers a distinct logic topology with potentially different PPA characteristics and highlights an alternative way to realize the function.
