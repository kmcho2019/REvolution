The original implementation uses the sum-of-products form q = (a & c) | (a & d) | (b & c) | (b & d), which is functionally correct but involves four AND gates feeding into an OR gate.

To optimize for power and area, we can factor this expression as q = (a | b) & (c | d). This reduces the gate count to two 2-input OR gates followed by one 2-input AND gate, thereby minimizing logic depth and gate usage.

This factored form retains the same logical behavior while potentially lowering power consumption and area due to fewer gates and simpler wiring. Additionally, performance is improved with shorter logic levels, and the expression is simpler to understand.

Hence, the improved design implements q = (a | b) & (c | d).
