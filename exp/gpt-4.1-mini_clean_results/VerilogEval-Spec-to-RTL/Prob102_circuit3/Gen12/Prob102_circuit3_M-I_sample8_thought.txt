The original logic q = (a & c) | (a & d) | (b & c) | (b & d) can be factored to q = (a | b) & (c | d). Factoring reduces the number of logic gates from five (four ANDs plus one OR) to three (two ORs plus one AND), thereby reducing area and power consumption while maintaining the same functionality and performance.

This factorization simplifies the circuit, reducing gate count and switching activity, beneficial for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area).

Therefore, the improved design implements q = (a | b) & (c | d).
