Observe that q is 1 whenever either c or d is 1 and at least one of a or b is 1, as before. An alternative expression is to use De Morgan's laws and write q as the negation of the case when q is 0. q=1 except when ((a=0) & (b=0)) or ((c=0) & (d=0)), which can be compactly expressed as q = ~((~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d)). This implements the same logic but with NOR and NAND style expressions.
