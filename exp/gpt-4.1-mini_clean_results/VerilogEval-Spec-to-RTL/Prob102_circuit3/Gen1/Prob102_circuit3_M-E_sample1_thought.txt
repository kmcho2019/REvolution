From the waveform, q=1 if:
- b=1 and at least one of c or d is 1, OR
- a=1 and at least one of c or d is 1, OR
- b=1 and c=1 and d=0 (seen at 45ns), also q=1

Looking closer, q=1 when ( (a OR b) AND (c OR d) ) is true.
When (a=0, b=0) q=0 even if d=1 (at 20ns), so d alone does not suffice.

Thus, q = (a | b) & (c | d) implements the observed pattern:
- q=1 only when either a or b is 1 AND either c or d is 1.
- Otherwise q=0.

This logic fits all the waveform values exactly.
