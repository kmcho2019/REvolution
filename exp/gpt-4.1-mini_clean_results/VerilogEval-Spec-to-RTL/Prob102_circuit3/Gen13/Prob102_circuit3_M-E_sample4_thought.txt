Observing the waveform, q is high only when either (a or b) is 1 and simultaneously (c or d) is 1. The logic function can be written as q = (a|b) & (c|d).

To provide a novel architectural idea, instead of directly implementing the logic as a single assign statement, I will decompose the logic into intermediate signals and use nested multiplexers to construct the same functionality combinationally. This approach can be beneficial for certain FPGA architectures or for illustrating hierarchical combinational design.

Specifically, I will:
1. Use a 2:1 mux to select between b and a based on c.
2. Use another 2:1 mux to select between d and the previous mux output based on a.
3. Finally, use the output of these muxes to generate q.

This gives a different structural perspective to the same function and demonstrates the use of conditional operators for combinational logic.
