Analyzing the waveform, q outputs '1' if and only if c=1 or d=1 and at least one of a or b is 1. Another way to implement this logic is by expressing q as the OR of two AND terms: (a AND (c OR d)) OR (b AND (c OR d)). This structurally differs from the previous approach that factorized (a OR b) AND (c OR d).

To create a new architectural idea, I will implement this expression using two separate AND gates feeding into a final OR gate:

- First, generate c_or_d = c | d

- Then, compute a_and_cd = a & c_or_d, and b_and_cd = b & c_or_d

- Finally, q = a_and_cd | b_and_cd

This uses three two-input gates instead of two ORs and one AND. The logic depth remains two levels, but the explicit structure differs. This implementation may be better for designs that prefer separate AND gates and OR gate for certain synthesis or testing strategies.

The module will be fully combinational with no sequential elements.
