Analyzing the waveform, q is 1 if and only if at least one of a or b is 1, and at least one of c or d is 1. This is equivalent to q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d). To provide a different implementation, I will use intermediate wires to separately compute the OR results of a and b, and of c and d, then combine them with an AND gate. This structural style provides a modular and easy-to-understand design alternative.
