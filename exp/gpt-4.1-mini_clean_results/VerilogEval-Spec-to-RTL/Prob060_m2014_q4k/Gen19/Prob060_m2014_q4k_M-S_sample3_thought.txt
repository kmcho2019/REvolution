Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single 4-bit register vector updated within one always block triggered on the posedge of clk. Use synchronous active-low reset to clear the register. Shift data in by moving bits left and inserting the new input at bit 0 each clock. Assign output as the MSB bit of the shift register. This approach reduces module instantiations and wiring, simplifying code and potentially improving synthesis results while keeping exact functionality.
