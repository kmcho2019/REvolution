To improve PPA while maintaining functionality, I will consolidate the four separate DFF instances into a single 4-bit register implemented inside one always block. This reduces area by allowing the synthesis tool to optimize register packing and lowers power by minimizing logic overhead. The synchronous active-low reset will be handled cleanly inside this block using non-blocking assignments. Output will be taken from the most significant bit of the shift register vector. This approach reduces flip-flop instantiation overhead and critical path complexity, potentially improving timing and area without changing functional behavior.
