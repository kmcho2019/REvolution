To optimize the 4-stage shift register for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we can integrate the four flip-flops into a single always block using a 4-bit register. This approach infers a shift register with a single register variable, minimizing module instantiations and reset logic duplication, which reduces area. It also simplifies clock and reset routing potentially improving power due to less wiring and logic. The synchronous active-low reset can be handled with a single check. This integrated approach is cleaner, likely to synthesize more efficiently, and maintains the same functionality and timing behavior.
