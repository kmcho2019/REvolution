Refactor the 4-stage shift register to use a single 4-bit register (`reg [3:0] shift_reg`) instead of chaining four separate DFF modules. The shift operation and synchronous active-low reset will be handled inside one always block triggered on the positive clock edge. This approach simplifies the design by consolidating all flip-flops into one vector register, reducing module instantiations and enhancing clarity.

Key points:
- Use a 4-bit shift register vector.
- On each clock, if reset is active (resetn=0), clear the entire register synchronously.
- Else, shift in the input bit.
- Output is the MSB of the shift register.
- Maintain synchronous active-low reset behavior.
- This style is more compact and typical for shift register designs.
