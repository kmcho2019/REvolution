Refactor the shift register by implementing it as a single vector register updated in an always block on the positive clock edge. The synchronous active-low reset will clear the entire register. Instead of instantiating individual DFF modules, maintain a 4-bit register shifting all bits in one statement, assigning the input to the least significant bit. The output is the most significant bit. This approach simplifies the code, reduces module hierarchy, and makes the design concise while preserving the synchronous reset and shifting functionality.
