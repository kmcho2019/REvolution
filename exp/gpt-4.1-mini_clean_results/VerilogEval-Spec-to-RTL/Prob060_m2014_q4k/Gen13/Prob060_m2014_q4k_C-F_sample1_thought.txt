Fuse the modularity and clarity of a reusable DFF module with the succinctness, maintainability, and area benefits of a vectorized shift register implemented in a single always block. Use one 4-bit reg vector for the shift register state to reduce area and improve timing, while keeping synchronous active-low reset handled cleanly inside the always block with non-blocking assignments for correct simulation and synthesis semantics. This approach:

- Keeps the reset logic centralized and synchronous for clarity and power efficiency.
- Avoids multiple module instantiations to reduce overhead and enable better synthesis optimization.
- Provides straightforward scalable code that is easy to extend or modify.
- Extracts the output from the last flip-flop in the vector to fulfill the interface.
- Maintains explicit synchronous reset behavior and positive clock edge triggering as required.

This fused design combines the best of modular clarity and explicit reset from the DFF approach with the area and timing efficiency of vectorized shift register coding.
