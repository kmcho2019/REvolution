Implement a single always block containing a 4-bit shift register vector for compactness, with explicit synchronous active-low reset logic. The 4-stage shift register will be represented as a 4-bit register where each clock cycle shifts left by one bit, and the input bit enters the LSB. The output is taken as the MSB of the shift register. This approach avoids separate D flip-flop modules and generate loops, simplifying the design to a straightforward vector shift register. Synchronous reset is applied by clearing the entire vector to zero on the clock's rising edge when resetn is low. This design emphasizes minimalistic and efficient RTL with clear and explicit reset and shift functionality.
