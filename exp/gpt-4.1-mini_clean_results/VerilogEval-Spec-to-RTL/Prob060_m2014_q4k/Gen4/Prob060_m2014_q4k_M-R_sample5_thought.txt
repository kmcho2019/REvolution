Refactor the design by implementing the shift register as a single 4-bit register updated synchronously in one always block, instead of chaining individual DFF modules. The synchronous reset is handled by clearing the entire register when resetn is low on the clock edge. This reduces hierarchy and improves compactness while preserving the same synchronous, positive-edge triggered behavior. The output is simply assigned to the most significant bit of the shift register vector. This approach simplifies the code by leveraging vector registers and eliminates the need for separate DFF module instantiations, yet functionally it remains equivalent to the original.
