Refactor the 4-stage shift register by implementing it as a single always block with a 4-bit register vector instead of instantiating separate D flip-flop modules. The vector will shift in the input bit at each clock edge with synchronous active-low reset. The output 'out' is taken from the most significant bit of the register. This reduces module instantiations, consolidates reset and shift logic in one place, and is a common idiomatic style in Verilog for shift registers.
