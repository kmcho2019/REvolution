Fuse the best ideas from both examples by combining the modularity and clarity of a parameterized DFF module with synchronous active-low reset, a generate loop to instantiate exactly four flip-flops in a chain, and vectorized wiring for the shift register.

Key points in the fusion:
- Maintain a single, reusable DFF module with synchronous active-low reset.
- Use a generate loop to create a scalable chain of DFFs, but keep parameterization for WIDTH in ShiftReg for potential reuse.
- Use vector wires to simplify data wiring between stages.
- Keep reset synchronous and active-low for controlled reset behavior.
- The shift register logic connects the input to the first DFF, and each subsequent DFF takes data from the previous DFF.
- Output is the last stage of the shift register.
- This approach combines modularity, scalability, concise code, clear clock/reset semantics, and clean wiring to enable good PPA.
