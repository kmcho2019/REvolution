Implement the 4-stage shift register as a single always block with a 4-bit register vector, shifting in the input bit on each positive clock edge. Use synchronous active-low reset to clear the entire register in one assignment. This approach simplifies the design by avoiding multiple instantiated DFF modules and uses vector operations for compactness and clarity. The output is the most significant bit of the register, representing the last stage of the shift. This method is straightforward, easy to maintain, and suitable for synthesis, while being structurally different from the prior module-based implementation.
