Implement the 4-bit shift register by using a single 4-bit register instead of discrete flip-flops, updating the register on each clock edge. Use synchronous active-low reset to clear the register to zero. On each positive clock edge (when not in reset), shift the register left by one bit, and load the new input bit into the least significant bit. The output is the most significant bit of the shift register. This design minimizes module complexity and uses vector operations directly, which synthesizes efficiently and is easy to read and maintain.
