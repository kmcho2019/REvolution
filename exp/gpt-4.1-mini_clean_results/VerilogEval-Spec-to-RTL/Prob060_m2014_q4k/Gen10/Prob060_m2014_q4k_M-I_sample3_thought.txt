To improve PPA, I will replace the explicit chain of four DFFs with a single SRL (shift register LUT) primitive where supported, which reduces area and improves power and timing by implementing the shift register with LUT resources instead of discrete flip-flops. 

Since reset is synchronous active low, we can implement reset logic as a synchronous clear by initializing the SRL contents to zero on reset. However, standard SRLs do not support direct synchronous reset inputs, so to maintain correct reset functionality, we'll gate the input with reset so the SRL shifts in zeros when resetn is low (effectively clearing the register synchronously).

This approach:
- Reduces area by replacing 4 FFs with one LUT-based shift register.
- Reduces power by fewer clocked elements toggling.
- Improves timing by having shorter data paths and fewer registers in series.
- Preserves synchronous active-low reset functionality.
- Keeps a simple single-bit output interface.

The TopModule will directly instantiate the optimized ShiftReg using the SRL-based approach, removing the DFF module.

If SRL primitives are not available on the target FPGA or ASIC library, this approach may need fallback to FF chains, but for typical Xilinx 7-series or similar FPGA flows, SRLs are available and inferred by synthesis when coding accordingly.
