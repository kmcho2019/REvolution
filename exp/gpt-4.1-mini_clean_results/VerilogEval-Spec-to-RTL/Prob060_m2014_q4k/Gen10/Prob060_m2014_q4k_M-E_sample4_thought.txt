Implement the 4-stage shift register as a single 4-bit register updated in one always block triggered on the positive clock edge. Use synchronous active-low reset inside the always block to clear all bits simultaneously. At every clock tick (when not resetting), shift the register left by one bit and insert the input 'in' at the least significant bit. Output the most significant bit as 'out'. 

This vectorized approach:
- Minimizes code by avoiding multiple module instantiations or generate loops.
- Is area and power efficient by allowing synthesis tools to infer a compact shift register.
- Simplifies timing analysis as the entire shift register is updated in one clock cycle in a single process.
- Retains all requested features: synchronous active-low reset, positive edge clocking, and a 4-stage shift register.
