Fuse the modular clarity and parameterization of the DFF and ShiftReg modules with the explicit, per-flip-flop synchronous active-low reset and sequential connection from both examples, but enhance robustness and maintainability by:

- Defining a reusable DFF module with synchronous active-low reset, ensuring clear reset semantics and localizing reset logic.
- Implementing the ShiftReg as a vector of regs updated in one always @(posedge clk) block for compactness and potential better optimization by synthesis tools, rather than chaining individual DFF instances. This reduces module hierarchy overhead and may improve area and power.
- Incorporating the synchronous reset inside the always block of ShiftReg to handle all bits uniformly, ensuring correct and clean synchronous reset behavior.
- Using a parameter for WIDTH (fixed to 4) for scalability and easy modifications.
- Assigning the final output as the last bit of the shift register vector.
- The TopModule simply instantiates this ShiftReg module.

This fusion blends modular code reuse (through DFF module availability if needed in future) and concise vectorized logic (which often leads to better synthesis results) with clear reset semantics, minimal hierarchy overhead, and maintainability. It respects the problem requirements of synchronous active-low reset and positive edge clock triggering while potentially improving PPA by reducing module instantiations and using a compact always block.
