Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single always block using a shift register vector instead of individual DFF instances. Use a synchronous active-low reset to zero the entire register on the clock's positive edge. The input bit shifts into the least significant bit, pushing existing bits up by one. The output is the most significant bit of the shift register. This architecture eliminates the need for a separate DFF module and generate loops, reducing hierarchy and making the design straightforward while still meeting timing and synthesis expectations.
