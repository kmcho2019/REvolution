Implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register. On each positive clock edge, the register shifts left by one bit, inserting the input 'in' at the least significant bit. The output 'out' is the most significant bit of this register. Use a single always block with synchronous active-low reset to clear the entire register to zero. This approach consolidates the shift register into one vector, improving area and potentially power by reducing overhead from multiple module instantiations.
