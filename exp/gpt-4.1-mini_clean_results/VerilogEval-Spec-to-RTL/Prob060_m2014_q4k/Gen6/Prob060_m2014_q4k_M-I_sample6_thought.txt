To optimize the existing design for better PPA while retaining synchronous active-low reset and modular clarity, I will integrate the reset directly into the ShiftReg module's always block using a single vector register instead of instantiating four separate DFF modules. This reduces structural overhead and can enable better synthesis optimizations for area and power by merging reset logic and flip-flops in a single always block.

This approach:
- Keeps a synchronous active-low reset.
- Avoids separate DFF module overhead.
- Uses a single always block for the entire shift register state for better control and minimal logic.
- Maintains clear and clean code.
- Keeps TopModule interface unchanged for easy integration.
  
By removing modular DFF instances, the design becomes smaller and synthesis tools can optimize the shift register as a simple register vector with integrated reset, improving area and power with no timing penalty.
