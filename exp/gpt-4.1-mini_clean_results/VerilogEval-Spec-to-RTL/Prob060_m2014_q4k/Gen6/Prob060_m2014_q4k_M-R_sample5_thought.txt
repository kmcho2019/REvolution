Refactor the shift register to use a single always block with a 4-bit register vector instead of chaining individual DFF modules. The synchronous active-low reset clears the entire register at once. The input bit shifts in at the LSB on each clock edge, and the MSB is assigned to the output. This reduces hierarchy by eliminating the separate DFF module and generate loop, making the design more compact and easier to analyze while preserving functionality and synchronous reset behavior.
