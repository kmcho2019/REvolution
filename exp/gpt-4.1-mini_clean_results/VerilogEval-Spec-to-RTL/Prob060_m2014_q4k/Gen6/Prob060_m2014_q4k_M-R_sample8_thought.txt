Refactor the 4-stage shift register using a single always block with a 4-bit register vector instead of instantiating separate DFF modules. Implement the synchronous active-low reset inside this always block. The serial input 'in' is shifted into the least significant bit on each clock edge, and the output 'out' is the most significant bit of the shift register. This approach simplifies the design by avoiding multiple module instantiations and makes it more compact while maintaining the same behavior.
