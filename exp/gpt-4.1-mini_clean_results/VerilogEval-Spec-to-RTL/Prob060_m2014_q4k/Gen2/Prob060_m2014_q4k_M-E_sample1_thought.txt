Instead of a simple serial shift register, implement a ring shift register using four flip-flops connected in a circular shift manner. The input bit will be injected into the first flip-flop on each clock cycle, but the data will circulate among the flip-flops rather than shifting linearly. The output is taken from the last flip-flop.

This ring architecture can be beneficial in designs that require recirculation of data and can potentially reduce glitching on the output by maintaining data within a closed loop. Although functionally similar to a linear shift register for this simple example, this approach gives a fresh perspective on data movement within registers and could be adapted for pattern generation or error detection schemes.

The synchronous active-low reset clears all registers to zero. Sequential logic triggers on the positive clock edge.
