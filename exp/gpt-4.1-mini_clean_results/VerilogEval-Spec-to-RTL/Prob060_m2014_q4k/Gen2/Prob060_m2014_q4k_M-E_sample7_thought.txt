Implement the 4-stage shift register using four individual D flip-flops instantiated separately, connected in a chain. Each flip-flop captures its input on the positive edge of clk with synchronous active-low reset. The first flip-flop takes 'in' as input, and each subsequent flip-flop takes the output of the previous flip-flop. The output 'out' is taken from the last flip-flop. This modular approach breaks down the shift register into smaller components for clarity and reusability.
