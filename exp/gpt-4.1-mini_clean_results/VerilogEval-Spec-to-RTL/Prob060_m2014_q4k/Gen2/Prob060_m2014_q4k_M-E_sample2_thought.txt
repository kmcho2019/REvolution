Instead of a single 4-bit register, implement the shift register using four individual D flip-flops connected in series with explicit individual registers. On each positive clock edge, if resetn is low, synchronously reset all flip-flops to zero. Otherwise, each flip-flop captures the value from its preceding stage, and the first flip-flop captures the input 'in'. The output 'out' is the output of the last flip-flop. This approach clearly separates stages and explicitly shows data propagation between flip-flops.
