Instead of using a simple shift register built from flip-flops connected in series, implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register and a combinational left-shift operation at each clock cycle. This reduces the number of explicit registers and combines shifting logic into a single assignment, simplifying the design. The synchronous active-low reset will clear the whole register to zero. The output is taken from the most significant bit of the shift register. This approach can make synthesis tools generate efficient shift register logic leveraging vector slicing and shifting operators, potentially reducing area and improving synthesis clarity.
