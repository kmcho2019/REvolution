Refactor the design by using a single 4-bit register vector to implement the 4-stage shift register instead of instantiating separate DFF modules. The synchronous active-low reset will be handled in one always block triggered on the positive clock edge. This reduces module hierarchy, potentially improving synthesis optimization, area, and timing, while preserving functionality. The output will be the last bit of the shift register vector. This approach is simpler, more compact, and maintains clear synchronous reset semantics.
