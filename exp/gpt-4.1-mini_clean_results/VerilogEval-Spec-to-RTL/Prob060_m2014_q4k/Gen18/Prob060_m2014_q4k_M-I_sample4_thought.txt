To optimize the original design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will transition from the structural approach with four separate DFF instances to a fully behavioral, compact implementation using a single 4-bit shift register register variable. This reduces module instantiation overhead, potentially improving synthesis efficiency and area.

Key improvements:
- Use a 4-bit register instead of chaining four separate flip-flops.
- Implement synchronous active-low reset in a single always block.
- Capture shift operation as a single register assignment, minimizing interconnect and logic.
- Output is taken as the LSB (oldest bit) of the shift register.
- This approach is simpler, more compact, and likely more power and area efficient.
- Performance should remain the same or improve slightly due to reduced logic layers.
