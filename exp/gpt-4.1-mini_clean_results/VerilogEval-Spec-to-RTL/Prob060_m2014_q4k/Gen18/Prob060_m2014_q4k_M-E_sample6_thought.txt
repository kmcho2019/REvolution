Implement the 4-bit shift register using a single always block with a 4-bit register vector. On every positive clock edge, if resetn is low, synchronously reset the entire shift register to zero; otherwise, shift in the input bit at LSB and shift existing bits upwards. Output is the MSB of this register. This approach uses fewer module instantiations and less code, providing a compact and efficient RTL description with minimal hardware and straightforward synchronous reset behavior.
