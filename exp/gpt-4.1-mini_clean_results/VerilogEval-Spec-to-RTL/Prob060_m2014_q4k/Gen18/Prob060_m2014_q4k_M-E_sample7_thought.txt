Implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register with synchronous active-low reset, shifting the entire register on every clock cycle. The 'in' bit shifts into the LSB, and the MSB becomes the output. This approach leverages vector operations for concise RTL and reduces hierarchy and module instantiations compared to per-bit flip-flop modules. Synchronous reset clears the entire register to zero when resetn is low on a clock edge. The TopModule contains all logic internally, making it a clean and compact solution for a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset.
