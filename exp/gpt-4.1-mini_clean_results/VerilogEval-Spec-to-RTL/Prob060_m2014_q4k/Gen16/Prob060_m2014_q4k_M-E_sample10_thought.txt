Implement the 4-stage shift register directly inside TopModule using a single 4-bit register and synchronous active-low reset. On each rising clock edge, shift the register left by one bit, inserting the input bit at the least significant position. This approach eliminates the need for a separate DFF module or generate loops, resulting in a compact, easy-to-read design with minimal overhead.

This design benefits include:
- Single always block for all flip-flops, simplifying RTL.
- Simple synchronous active-low reset handled once.
- Efficient and minimal logic by leveraging vector register shifting.
- Straightforward maintenance and verification.
