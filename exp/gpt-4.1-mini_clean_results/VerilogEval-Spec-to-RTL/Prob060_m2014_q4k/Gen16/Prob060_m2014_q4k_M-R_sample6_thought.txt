Refactor the 4-stage shift register by using a single always block with a 4-bit register vector instead of instantiating four separate DFF modules. This vector register shifts on every positive clock edge and resets synchronously when resetn is low. The input 'in' shifts into the least significant bit, and the output 'out' is taken from the most significant bit. This approach simplifies the design by reducing module instantiations, improves area, and centralizes the sequential logic.
