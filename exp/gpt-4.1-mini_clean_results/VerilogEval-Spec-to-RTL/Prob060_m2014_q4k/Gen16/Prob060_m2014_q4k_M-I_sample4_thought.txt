To improve power and area efficiency, we can simplify the design by inferring the shift register directly inside TopModule using a 4-bit register, rather than instantiating separate DFF modules. This reduces module overhead, potentially allowing the synthesis tool to optimize the register chain better. We will implement synchronous active-low reset as specified. The single always block will update all four stages of the shift register on the rising clock edge. This approach maintains the same interface and functionality but results in a more compact, efficient design in both power and area, without impacting timing.
