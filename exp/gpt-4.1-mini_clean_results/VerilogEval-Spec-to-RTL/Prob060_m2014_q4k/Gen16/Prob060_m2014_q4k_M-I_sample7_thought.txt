To optimize for better power and area while preserving the required functionality and synchronous active-low reset semantics, the shift register can be implemented with a single 4-bit register variable instead of instantiating four separate DFF_SR modules. This reduces hierarchical overhead and can lead to more compact and efficient synthesis results.

Specifically:
- Replace the array of DFF_SR modules with a single always block updating a 4-bit reg.
- Implement synchronous active-low reset inside this always block.
- Shift input serially into the register on each clock.
- Assign the output as the MSB of the shift register.
- Keep interface unchanged for compatibility.

This approach reduces module instantiation overhead, simplifies the design, and likely reduces area and power due to fewer module boundaries and simpler combinational routing. It keeps the synchronous reset logic explicit and clearly defined in one place, improving synthesis efficiency. This direct RTL style often leads to better optimized results for such small shift registers.

Overall, the design trades modularity for improved PPA in a small, straightforward shift register use case.
