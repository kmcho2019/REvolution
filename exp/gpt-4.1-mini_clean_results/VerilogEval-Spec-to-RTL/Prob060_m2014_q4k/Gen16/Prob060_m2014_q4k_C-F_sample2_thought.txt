Fuse the strengths of both examples by implementing a single always block inside TopModule that holds a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset, avoiding multiple module instantiations and generate loops. This approach combines the clarity and modularity of explicit reset handling with the simplicity and area/power efficiency of inferred flip-flops.

Key advantages:
- Clean, compact code with only one always block, easier to maintain and verify.
- Synchronous active-low reset explicitly handled in one place.
- The 4-bit register is inferred by synthesis tools as four D flip-flops, ensuring minimal area.
- No generate or multiple submodule instantiations, reducing overhead.
- Output is simply assigned as the last bit in the shift register.
- Readable, professional RTL suitable for easy extensions and potential future clock gating or enable signals.

This fusion leads to an optimal balance of modularity, code simplicity, area, and power efficiency while maintaining the required synchronous reset behavior.
