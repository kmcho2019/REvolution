Implement the 4-bit shift register using a single always block with individual flip-flop registers declared separately instead of as a vector. Each flop is updated synchronously on the clock's rising edge with active-low synchronous reset. The shift operation is performed by explicitly assigning each flop to the previous flop's output, with the first flop taking the 'in' input. This avoids arrays or generate blocks, making the dataflow clear by naming each stage individually.
