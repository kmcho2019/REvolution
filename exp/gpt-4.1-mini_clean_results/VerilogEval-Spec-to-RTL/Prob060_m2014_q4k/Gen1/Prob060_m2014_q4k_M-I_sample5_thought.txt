The original design is already clean and efficient; however, a slight optimization can be made by removing the 4-bit vector and instead using individual D flip-flop registers for each stage. This can help the synthesis tool better map registers and possibly reduce the switching activity by isolating bits.

Additionally, by using individual registers, we can explicitly model each D flip-flop and potentially enable fine-grained clock gating later (if the design is extended). For now, this approach may slightly improve power and area due to better register inference and reduced combinational fanout.
