To improve power efficiency without sacrificing timing or area, add an enable signal to the shift register that only shifts when the input changes or when reset is active. This avoids unnecessary shifting (and toggling) of the register on cycles when input is stable, reducing dynamic power. Keep the synchronous active-low reset and positive edge-triggered clock. This small conditional update avoids gating the clock (which can be complex) while still minimizing switching activity. The timing and area overhead of a simple enable is negligible for a 4-bit register, but power can improve.
