To optimize for better PPA while maintaining the synchronous active-low reset and shift register functionality, I will refactor the design to use a single always block with a 4-bit register instead of four separate DFF instances. This approach reduces area by eliminating multiple module instantiations and allows the synthesis tool to infer an efficient shift register. It also centralizes reset logic, potentially improving performance and reducing power due to simplified logic and fewer routing resources. The output 'out' will be the MSB of the 4-bit register.
