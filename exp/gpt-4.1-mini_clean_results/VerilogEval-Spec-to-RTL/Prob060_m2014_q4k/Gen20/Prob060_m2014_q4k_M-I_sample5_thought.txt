To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will replace the four separately instantiated D flip-flops with a single 4-bit vector register. This will allow synthesis tools to infer an optimized multi-bit register instead of separate flip-flops. It reduces routing and cell overhead, potentially improving area and power.

The synchronous active-low reset will be implemented inside one always block for the entire 4-bit shift register. The shift register operation shifts the input bit into the LSB each clock cycle. The output port will be connected to the MSB of the shift register.

This approach:
- Reduces area by using a vector register.
- Likely reduces power by lowering net toggling on reset lines and register chain routing.
- Keeps synchronous reset active low as requested.
- Maintains the same interface and functionality.
- Potentially improves timing due to simpler register structure.
