Refactor the original design by removing the modular DFF instantiations and generate loop, instead implementing the 4-bit shift register as a single vector register updated inside one always block. Keep synchronous active-low reset and positive edge clock triggering.

This approach simplifies the code, reduces module overhead, and is more area- and power-efficient since the synthesis tool can optimize the register vector as a single entity. It maintains the same functionality: on each clock edge, if reset is asserted, clear the register; otherwise shift in the input bit. The output is the last bit of the shift register.

This refactoring retains the synchronous reset and positive edge clocking requirements, and makes the design more concise and potentially faster due to simpler routing and logic.
