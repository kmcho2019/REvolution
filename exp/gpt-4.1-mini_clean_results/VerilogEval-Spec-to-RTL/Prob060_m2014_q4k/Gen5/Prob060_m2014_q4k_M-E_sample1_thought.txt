Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single always block using a 4-bit register. On each positive clock edge, shift the register left by one bit and input the new bit at LSB. Use a synchronous active-low reset to clear the entire register to zero. The output is taken from the MSB bit of the register. This approach reduces module hierarchy by combining all flip-flops into a single register variable, minimizing instantiation overhead and potentially enabling better synthesis optimization for timing and area while preserving the synchronous active-low reset behavior.
