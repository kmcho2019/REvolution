Instead of instantiating four separate D flip-flop modules, implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register. On each positive clock edge, shift in the input bit into the register if resetn is asserted; otherwise, synchronously reset the entire register to zero. This approach reduces module overhead, potentially lowering area and power, and simplifies reset logic by centralizing it. The output is taken from the most significant bit of the shift register.
