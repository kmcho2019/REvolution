To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, area) while maintaining functionality, I will simplify the design by implementing the 4-bit shift register as a single 4-bit register instead of chaining four separate DFF modules. This approach:

- Reduces module instantiations and wiring overhead, thus potentially improving area.
- Simplifies the logic, potentially improving synthesis and timing.
- Still uses synchronous active-low reset.
- Keeps the output as the last bit of the shift register.

This integrated approach can lead to better area and power efficiency, while preserving the same positive edge clocking and synchronous reset behavior.
