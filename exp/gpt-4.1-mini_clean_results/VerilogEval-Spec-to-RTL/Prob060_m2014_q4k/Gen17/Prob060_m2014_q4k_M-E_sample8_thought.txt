Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single always block with a vector register instead of individual flip-flop modules. Use a 4-bit reg to represent the shift register stages and on each clock edge, shift the bits left by one position, inserting the input bit at the least significant bit. Implement synchronous active-low reset by setting the entire register to zero on reset. Assign the most significant bit of the register to the output.

This approach simplifies the design by leveraging vector registers and reduces module hierarchy and instantiation overhead. It is concise, easy to understand, and is easily inferred by synthesis tools into four D-flip-flops connected in a chain.
