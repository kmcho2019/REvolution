I will implement the 4-bit shift register directly inside TopModule using a single always block with a 4-bit register. This removes the need for separate DFF modules and wiring between them. The synchronous active-low reset will be handled inside the always block on posedge clk. The input 'in' will be shifted in, and the output will be the MSB of the shift register.

This approach is more concise, reduces code complexity, and still meets all requirements.
