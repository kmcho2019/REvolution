I will implement the 4-stage shift register by explicitly instantiating four separate D flip-flops with synchronous active-low reset. Each DFF will capture one stage of the shift register, connecting the output of one flip-flop to the input of the next. This approach emphasizes structural clarity and modularity by defining a reusable DFF module with synchronous reset and then instantiating it four times in TopModule.

This approach:
- Uses four distinct flip-flop instances for explicit stage control.
- Implements synchronous active-low reset inside each flip-flop.
- Keeps clock edge sensitivity clear and isolated in the DFF module.
- Provides easily testable and debuggable flip-flop units.
- Allows flexibility for extension or modification at each stage.
- Maintains the same TopModule interface.
