Implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register. On each positive clock edge, if resetn is low, the register resets to zero synchronously. Otherwise, the register shifts left by one bit and loads the new 'in' bit into the LSB. The output 'out' is the MSB of the 4-bit register. This approach uses a compact, single always block for synchronous logic and avoids multiple module instantiations, minimizing area and simplifying the design.
