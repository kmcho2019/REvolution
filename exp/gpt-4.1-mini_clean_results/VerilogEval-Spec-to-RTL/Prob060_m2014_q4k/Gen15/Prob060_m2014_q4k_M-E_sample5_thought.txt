Instead of instantiating four separate D flip-flops, I will implement the 4-bit shift register entirely within a single always block using a 4-bit register vector. This behavioral approach is more compact and synthesizes efficiently into a 4-bit shift register, with synchronous active-low reset logic included.

Key points:
- Use a 4-bit register to hold the shift register state.
- At each positive clock edge, shift left by one and insert the new input bit at LSB.
- If resetn is low, synchronously reset the entire shift register to zero.
- Output is the MSB of the shift register.
- This approach offers simplicity and clarity, and can be easily extended or modified as a vector.
