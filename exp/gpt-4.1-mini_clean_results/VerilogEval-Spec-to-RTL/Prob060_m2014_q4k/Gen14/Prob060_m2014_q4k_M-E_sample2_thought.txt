Implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register with synchronous active-low reset, avoiding explicit D flip-flop instantiations. On each clock edge, if resetn is low, clear the entire register to zero. Otherwise, shift in the input bit into the least significant bit and shift previous bits to the left, effectively implementing a left-shift shift register. Output the most significant bit as the out signal. This approach results in compact, concise RTL with simple synchronous reset and straightforward sequential logic, suitable for efficient synthesis and minimal area.
