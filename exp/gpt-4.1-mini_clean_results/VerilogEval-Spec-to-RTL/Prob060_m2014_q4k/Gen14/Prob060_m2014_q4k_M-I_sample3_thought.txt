To optimize the original design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will simplify the design by removing the explicit DFF module and instead use an inferred 4-bit shift register implemented as a register vector. This reduces area by avoiding module overhead and helps synthesis tools better optimize the flops. The synchronous active-low reset will be handled inside a single always block, making the design more compact and potentially improving timing due to fewer module boundaries.

Power can be slightly improved by reducing unnecessary instantiations and simplifying logic, though no clock gating is introduced as no system-level conditions were provided. Performance is maintained because the critical path remains a single flop delay. The code is simpler and more readable while maintaining the same functionality.

This approach leverages the synthesis tool's ability to optimize inferred registers and yields a clean, minimal RTL description.
