Implement the 4-stage shift register using a single 4-bit register instead of individual flip-flop instances. On each positive clock edge, shift the register left by one bit, inserting the input at the least significant bit. Use a synchronous active-low reset to clear the entire register. Output the most significant bit of the register as the shift register output. This approach simplifies the design by aggregating flip-flops into a vector register, improving area efficiency while maintaining functionality.
