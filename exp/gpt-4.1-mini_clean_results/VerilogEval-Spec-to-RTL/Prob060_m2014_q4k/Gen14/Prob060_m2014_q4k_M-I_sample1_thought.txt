Optimize the shift register by replacing the four individual D flip-flop instances with a single 4-bit register, implementing the shift register behavior as a single sequential always block with synchronous active-low reset.

This change:
- Reduces hierarchical overhead and flip-flop instantiation logic, improving area efficiency.
- Simplifies clocking and reset logic, potentially improving timing by reducing inter-flop net delays.
- Keeps the synchronous active-low reset logic explicit and clear.
- Reduces power by minimizing the number of sequential elements and simplified logic.
- Maintains the same functional behavior with a clean and concise RTL description.

Overall, this is a common and effective design style for small shift registers when no special flop-level control is needed, balancing clarity and PPA improvements.
