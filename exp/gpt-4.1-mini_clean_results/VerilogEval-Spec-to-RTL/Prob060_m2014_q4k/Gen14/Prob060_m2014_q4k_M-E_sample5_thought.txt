Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single 4-bit register with synchronous active-low reset using one always block. This design uses a vector to hold all four flip-flops, shifting bits in a single clock cycle on the positive edge. This approach reduces code redundancy, minimizes hierarchy, and can improve synthesis optimizations for area and timing compared to separate DFF instantiations. The output is taken from the MSB of the shift register vector. Synchronous reset zeroes the entire vector when active low.
