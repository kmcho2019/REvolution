Implement the 4-stage shift register using four individual D flip-flops instantiated as registers. Each flip-flop holds one bit of the shift register and updates synchronously on the clock's positive edge. On the synchronous active-low reset, all flip-flops are cleared. The input feeds into the first flip-flop, and each subsequent flip-flop takes the previous flip-flop's output. The final flip-flop output drives the module output. This approach explicitly models each stage, facilitating potential future modifications or debugging per stage.
