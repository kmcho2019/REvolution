Simplify the design by removing the separate DFF module and implementing the entire 4-stage shift register directly within one always block in the TopModule. Use a 4-bit register to hold the shift register state, updating synchronously on the positive clock edge with active-low synchronous reset. Shift the register left on each clock by loading the input bit at LSB, and output the MSB as 'out'. This reduces module hierarchy and wiring, making the design straightforward and minimal while preserving correct functionality.
