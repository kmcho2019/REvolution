Implement the 4-bit shift register as a single 4-bit register updated synchronously on the positive clock edge with a synchronous active-low reset. Use a single always block with a vector register to hold the shift register state. At each clock edge, if reset is active, clear the register; else shift left by one bit and insert the new input bit at the LSB. Output the MSB of the register. This approach minimizes flip-flop instantiations by using a vector register and avoids generate loops, resulting in a compact and straightforward RTL implementation.
