Refactor the 4-stage shift register by using a single always block with a 4-bit register to hold the entire shift register state, shifting the bits on every positive clock edge. This reduces the number of always blocks and module instantiations, centralizing the synchronous reset and shift logic. The output is taken from the most significant bit of the shift register. This approach is simpler, more compact, and leverages Verilog's vector operations for clarity.
