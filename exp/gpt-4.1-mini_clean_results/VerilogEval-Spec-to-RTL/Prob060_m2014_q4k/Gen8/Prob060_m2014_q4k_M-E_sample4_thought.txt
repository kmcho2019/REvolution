Implement the 4-stage shift register using four individual D flip-flop (DFF) modules instantiated explicitly and connected in series. Each DFF handles one bit of the shift register. The DFF modules implement synchronous active-low reset internally. This modular approach makes the design more structural and clear, with explicit flop instantiations.

Advantages:
- Clear one-to-one mapping between flops and DFF instances, good for educational clarity.
- Each DFF is a reusable module with consistent synchronous active-low reset semantics.
- The TopModule composes the shift register explicitly from DFFs, which may be easier to inspect or adapt for architectures where individual flop control is needed.

Though it may increase gate count slightly compared to a vector register approach, it enhances modularity and allows per-flop control or instrumentation if needed.
