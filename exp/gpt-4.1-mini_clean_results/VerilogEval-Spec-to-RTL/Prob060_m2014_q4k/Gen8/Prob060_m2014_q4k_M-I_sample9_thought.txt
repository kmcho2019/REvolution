To optimize the shift register for better PPA, I will replace the four separate DFF module instances with a single always block that implements a 4-bit register array. This reduces module instantiations and duplicated reset logic, leading to reduced area. By inferring registers within one always block, synthesis tools can optimize the shift register better, potentially improving performance and power. The synchronous active-low reset will be handled centrally. The output 'out' is taken from the last bit of the shift register.
