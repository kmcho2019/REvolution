Refactor the 4-bit shift register by implementing it fully within a single always block using a shift register vector instead of individual DFF instantiations. Use a synchronous active-low reset within this always block and non-blocking assignments to update the entire shift register in one statement. This removes the modular DFF instantiation and generate block, simplifying the hierarchy and potentially improving synthesis by making the register chain explicit as a vector. The output is assigned from the MSB of the shift register vector. This approach keeps the synchronous reset and positive-edge clock triggering intact while demonstrating an alternate common idiom for small shift registers.
