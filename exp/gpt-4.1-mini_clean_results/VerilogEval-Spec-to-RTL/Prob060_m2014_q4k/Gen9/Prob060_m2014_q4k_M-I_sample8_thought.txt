Optimize the 4-bit shift register by consolidating the four scalar DFFs into a single 4-bit register with synchronous active-low reset. This reduces code complexity, area, and potentially power due to fewer instantiated modules and simpler routing. Using a single always block with a vector register reduces duplication and enhances synthesis efficiency. The shift register shifts the 4-bit register on every rising clock edge when reset is not active, shifting in 'in' at the LSB and outputting the MSB as 'out'. This approach yields better area and power efficiency with identical functional behavior.
