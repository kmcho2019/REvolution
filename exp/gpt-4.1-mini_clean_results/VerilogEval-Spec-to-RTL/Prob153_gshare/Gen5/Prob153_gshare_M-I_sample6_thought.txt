The main cause of the mismatch errors is the subtle timing of when the prediction outputs (`predict_taken`, `predict_history`) are latched and when the GHR is updated on prediction.

To fix this, the key idea is:

- The prediction outputs should reflect the PHT state and GHR used *at the moment of prediction*, i.e., before the GHR is updated for that prediction.
- The update of the GHR on prediction must shift in the predicted bit output at *that* cycle, not a prediction taken bit latched from the same cycle (which would cause a one-cycle delay).
- Therefore, we compute the predicted taken bit combinationally and output it immediately (or latch it on predict_valid), and use that combinational predicted bit to update the GHR in the *same* clock cycle.
- Since both prediction output and GHR update depend on the same combinational predicted bit, we introduce a combinational intermediate signal `predict_taken_next`.
- On clock edge, if `predict_valid` is asserted and training mispredict is not asserted, we update GHR by shifting in `predict_taken_next` (the combinational predicted bit).
- On the other hand, if `train_valid` and `train_mispredicted` are asserted, the GHR is restored to `train_history` with priority.
- Prediction outputs (`predict_taken_reg` and `predict_history_reg`) are latched from the current PHT and current GHR *before* the GHR update, ensuring outputs correspond to the prediction made at this cycle.
- This approach aligns with the problem statement: prediction sees old PHT and old GHR state; then GHR is updated by the predicted outcome; training updates PHT and if mispredicted, restores GHR.
- We use non-blocking assignments in the clocked always block for all sequential updates and combinational logic outside.
- The PHT remains unchanged from the original design.
- This fix removes timing mismatch between the latched predicted bit and the updated GHR, correcting the predict_taken and predict_history mismatches.

Additionally, for optimization:

- We keep the PHT as a reg array (128 x 2 bits).
- Use a localparam for weakly not taken state for clarity.
- Function to saturate counters remains.
- The indexing remains 7-bit XOR of PC and GHR.
- No extra pipelining or complexity added, favoring clear timing and correctness.

This corrected timing and priority logic will fix the mismatch problem, ensuring outputs are stable and consistent with the prediction used to update the GHR.
