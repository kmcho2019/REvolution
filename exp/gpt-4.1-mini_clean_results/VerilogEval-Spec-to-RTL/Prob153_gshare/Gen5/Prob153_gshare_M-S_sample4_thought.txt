Use registered outputs for prediction signals to hold stable values and reflect the predictor state at prediction time. Compute prediction index and read PHT combinationally but register the predicted taken and history outputs on predict_valid cycles. At clock edge, on reset initialize GHR and PHT; else update PHT if training valid, and update GHR prioritizing recovery on misprediction, otherwise update with predicted bit if predict_valid. This simplification cleanly separates combinational prediction read and synchronous state updates, ensuring stable outputs and correct priority between training and prediction updates.
