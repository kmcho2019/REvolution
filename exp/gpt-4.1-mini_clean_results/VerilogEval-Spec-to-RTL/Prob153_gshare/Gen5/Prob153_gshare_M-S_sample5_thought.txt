Implement a 7-bit gshare branch predictor with a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter PHT and a 7-bit global history register (GHR).

- Maintain two copies of the GHR:
  - `ghr_spec`: speculative history used for prediction, updated after prediction (unless a misprediction training happens)
  - `ghr_commit`: committed history updated on training if no misprediction

- On prediction (predict_valid):
  - Compute index = predict_pc ^ ghr_spec
  - Read PHT entry combinationally
  - Output predicted taken bit (MSB of counter) and the current ghr_spec *before* updating
  - After clock edge, update `ghr_spec` by shifting in the predicted taken bit only if no misprediction training this cycle

- On training (train_valid):
  - Update PHT entry indexed by train_pc ^ train_history with taken outcome
  - If mispredicted, restore `ghr_spec` to train_history to recover speculative history
  - Else update committed GHR by shifting in train_taken

- Prediction outputs are registered to keep stable outputs aligned with cycle timing.

- Prioritize training misprediction recovery over speculative GHR updates on the same cycle.

This simplified approach removes redundant variables and complex functions, performs PHT update directly, and carefully sequences updates to ensure outputs correspond to predictor states at prediction request time.
