Also, the PHT state read for prediction must reflect the old PHT contents before any training updates on the same cycle.

4. Retain the training precedence over prediction for global history update at clock edge.

5. Update the PHT only on training valid, after reading for prediction is done, ensuring predictions see the old PHT state.
