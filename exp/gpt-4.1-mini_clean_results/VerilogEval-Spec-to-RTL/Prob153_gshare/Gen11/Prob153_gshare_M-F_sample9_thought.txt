The main issue is the incorrect combinational assignment of `predict_taken`, which causes output mismatches because the prediction outputs must come from the pipeline registers (`predict_taken_r` and `predict_history_r`) updated on the clock edge at `predict_valid`. 

To fix this:

- Assign `predict_taken` directly from the registered `predict_taken_r`, not the combinational `predict_bit`. This ensures stable output reflecting the PHT state and GHR at the prediction cycle.

- Keep `predict_history` output assigned from the registered `predict_history_r` as before.

- Maintain the combinational logic that reads the PHT indexed by `predict_pc ^ ghr` for the prediction bit, which is used to update the GHR speculatively at the next clock edge.

- Update the GHR with correct priority on each clock edge:
  1. Recover GHR from `train_history` if training mispredicted.
  2. Else update GHR with `train_taken` if training valid.
  3. Else update GHR speculatively with prediction if prediction valid.
  4. Else hold GHR.

- Keep the PHT updated on the clock edge when `train_valid` is asserted.

- On asynchronous reset, initialize the PHT to weakly not taken (2'b01) and clear GHR and prediction pipeline registers.

This fix resolves output timing mismatches and ensures outputs strictly reflect the pipeline timing expected by the spec.
