Implement the gshare predictor using separate committed and speculative global history registers, but organize the speculative history update distinctly from the prediction interface timing:

- Maintain:
  - `ghr_commit`: The committed global history, stable and outputted with prediction.
  - `ghr_spec`: The speculative global history updated at clock edge, used internally to update PHT indices during prediction.

- On a prediction request (`predict_valid`):
  - Compute the index as XOR of PC and *committed* history (`ghr_commit`).
  - Read the saturating counter at that index (before any PHT update).
  - Output prediction and the *committed* history.
  - Update the *speculative* history by shifting in the predicted outcome *using the committed history*.
  - Delay updating the committed history to the next clock edge.

- On a training request (`train_valid`):
  - Compute the index from `train_pc` XOR `train_history`.
  - Update the PHT entry saturating counter *at the next clock edge*.
  - If mispredicted (`train_mispredicted`), recover the speculative history to the `train_history` provided (which reflects the post-commit history after the mispredicted branch).
  
- Priority to training recovery over prediction speculative update.

- At every clock edge:
  - Commit the speculative global history to `ghr_commit`.

This approach cleanly separates stable history (used for prediction output and indexing) and speculative history (used for updating PHT and future predictions).

Additionally, to avoid subtle races and input timing glitches, register `predict_valid` and `predict_pc` inputs in the pipeline stage before using for prediction logic.

This architecture guarantees:
- Predict outputs and history used for prediction align perfectly with stable committed history.
- Speculative history evolves based on predicted outcomes but can be corrected (rolled back) upon misprediction training.
- PHT updates apply cleanly at clock edge after prediction outputs.
