Implement the gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT indexed by XOR of PC and history. On each clock, update PHT entries and global history. Prediction outputs reflect PHT state before any update. Training update takes precedence over prediction update for both PHT and global history. On misprediction during training, restore global history to the provided history, overriding prediction update. On normal training, update the PHT entry accordingly. On prediction without training, update global history by shifting in the predicted taken bit. Initialize PHT to weakly taken (2'b10) and global history to zero on async reset. Use a single always block with clear priority for updates to simplify logic and ensure correct timing and precedence.
