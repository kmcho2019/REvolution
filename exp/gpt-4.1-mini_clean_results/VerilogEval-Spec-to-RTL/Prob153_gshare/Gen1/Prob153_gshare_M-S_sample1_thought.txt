Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of 7-bit PC and 7-bit GHR.

- Maintain a 7-bit global history register (GHR).
- On predict_valid, output prediction and current GHR as predict_history.
- Update GHR with predicted bit on next clock edge only if no training misprediction occurs simultaneously.
- On train_valid, update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history according to train_taken.
- If train_mispredicted, restore GHR to train_history (the corrected GHR after the mispredicted branch).
- If train_mispredicted and predict_valid occur together, training's GHR update has priority.
- PHT updates happen synchronously at clock edge.
- Prediction reads current PHT and GHR before any update.
- Initialize PHT to weakly not taken (01) and GHR to zero on asynchronous reset.

This simplified approach uses straightforward priority for GHR update and ensures prediction outputs use stable registers before update.
