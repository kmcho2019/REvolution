Implement the gshare predictor with a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter table and a 7-bit global history register (GHR). On reset, initialize GHR and all counters to weakly not taken (01). On prediction, output the prediction and current GHR, and update GHR next cycle by shifting in the predicted bit. On training, update the PHT entry according to the actual outcome. Ensure prediction outputs see PHT state before training updates by reading PHT combinationally and updating registers only at clock edge. Use consistent reg and wire types and simplify logic to avoid illegal assignments or complex combinational reads of memories.
