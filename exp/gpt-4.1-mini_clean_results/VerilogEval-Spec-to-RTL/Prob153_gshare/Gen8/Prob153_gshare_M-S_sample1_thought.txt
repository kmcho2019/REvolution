Implement the gshare predictor with a single global history register (GHR) updated speculatively on prediction and corrected on misprediction during training. Store the PHT as a 128-entry array of 2-bit saturating counters.

On a prediction request:
- Compute the index as predict_pc XOR current speculative GHR.
- Read the PHT entry to get the prediction.
- Output the prediction and the current GHR (before update) as predict_taken and predict_history.
- On the next clock, shift the predicted bit into the GHR.

On a training request:
- Update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history using train_taken.
- If misprediction, restore the GHR to train_history (recover state).
- If no misprediction, update GHR by shifting in train_taken.
- Training takes priority over prediction updates on GHR.

All updates happen synchronously on clock edge; asynchronous reset clears PHT and GHR.

This simpler approach uses one GHR register updated speculatively or recovered on misprediction and cleanly separates combinational prediction logic from sequential updates.
