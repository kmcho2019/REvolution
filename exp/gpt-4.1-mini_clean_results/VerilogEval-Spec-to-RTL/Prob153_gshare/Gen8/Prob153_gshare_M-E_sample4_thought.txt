Implement the gshare branch predictor with two global history registers: one "committed" history that tracks the branch history after correct branches, and one "speculative" history used for prediction. On reset, initialize the pattern history table (PHT) and both history registers.

For prediction, use the speculative history register (ghr_spec) XORed with predict_pc to index into the PHT. Output the prediction and the speculative history used. The speculative history is updated on the clock edge only if predict_valid is asserted and no training misprediction takes priority.

For training, when train_valid is asserted, update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history according to train_taken. If the training branch is mispredicted, recover the speculative history register to train_history, which corresponds to the committed state after the mispredicted branch was resolved. Also update the committed history register when training a correct branch to maintain the architectural history.

If both training (especially with misprediction) and prediction occur in the same cycle, training takes priority, discarding the speculative prediction update because the pipeline will flush. This ensures correctness.

This dual-history approach cleanly separates architectural (committed) and speculative histories, enabling correct recovery on misprediction and accurate speculative updates for prediction. The PHT is updated only on training to reflect actual outcomes, not speculation.

The saturating counter is a standard 2-bit saturating counter incremented on taken and decremented on not taken.

This design supports concurrent training and prediction requests with well-defined priority and updates.
