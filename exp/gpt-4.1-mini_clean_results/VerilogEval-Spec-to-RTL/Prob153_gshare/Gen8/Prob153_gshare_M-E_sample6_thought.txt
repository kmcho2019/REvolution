Implement two separate 7-bit global history registers (GHRs): a "speculative GHR" that is updated on prediction cycles with predicted outcomes, and an "architectural GHR" that is updated only on training cycles, representing the committed branch history. Predictions use the speculative GHR for indexing the PHT combinationally, allowing speculative tracking without corrupting architectural state. Training updates the PHT and the architectural GHR. On misprediction training, the architectural GHR is rolled back to the provided train_history to recover state, and the speculative GHR is also reset to the same state to resynchronize. When prediction and training happen simultaneously, training updates take precedence: the speculative GHR is reset or updated based on training (rollback or commit), ignoring speculative update for that cycle. Prediction outputs reflect the current speculative GHR and PHT state at that cycle, before updating the speculative GHR. This separation cleanly manages speculative and architectural states, supports rollback on misprediction, and ensures outputs and history updates happen correctly without conflicts or ambiguity.
