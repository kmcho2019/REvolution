Implement the gshare predictor with a clear separation between the speculative and committed global history states.

Maintain two 7-bit global history registers:

- ghr_spec: speculative global history that reflects the history including predicted branches (used for indexing during prediction).
- ghr_comm: committed global history reflecting only actual committed branch outcomes (used for training and recovery).

Prediction path:

- On predict_valid, index the PHT with (predict_pc XOR ghr_spec) and output prediction and ghr_spec as predict_history.
- Speculatively update ghr_spec by shifting in the predicted taken bit at the next clock edge.

Training path:

- On train_valid, update the PHT indexed by (train_pc XOR train_history).
- If train_mispredicted, restore ghr_spec to train_history (the known committed history after the mispredicted branch).
- Always update ghr_comm on train_valid by shifting in train_taken.

Conflict priority at clock edge:

- If train_valid && train_mispredicted, ghr_spec <= train_history (recovery).
- Else if train_valid, ghr_comm <= shift in train_taken and ghr_spec <= ghr_comm (commit the history after training).
- Else if predict_valid, ghr_spec <= shift in predicted taken (speculative update).

This approach cleanly separates speculation (prediction updates) and commit (training updates) in the history register, allowing precise recovery after misprediction.

Prediction outputs are generated combinationally from ghr_spec before any update, ensuring outputs correspond to the state used for prediction.

The PHT updates occur synchronously on training cycles only.

This design avoids ambiguous timing and guarantees correct behavior in the presence of simultaneous prediction and training, as well as correct recovery after mispredictions.
