Refactor to separate combinational and sequential logic more clearly and explicitly pipeline prediction outputs:

- Keep the GHR as a registered speculative history updated on clock edge.
- Compute prediction index combinationally from (predict_pc XOR current ghr).
- Read the PHT entry combinationally at that index.
- Register prediction outputs (predict_taken and predict_history) in a pipeline register when predict_valid is asserted. This aligns prediction outputs timing explicitly, preventing timing mismatches between combinational PHT reads and registered history outputs.
- On the clock edge, update PHT and GHR following priority rules:
  1) If training valid and mispredicted, restore GHR from train_history and update PHT entry.
  2) Else if training valid and not mispredicted, update PHT entry.
  3) Else if prediction valid and no mispredicted training, update GHR by shifting in predicted bit.
- Initialize PHT entries to weakly not taken (01) on asynchronous reset.
- This structure improves timing alignment and makes prediction outputs fully registered, eliminating combinational-to-sequential mismatches.
