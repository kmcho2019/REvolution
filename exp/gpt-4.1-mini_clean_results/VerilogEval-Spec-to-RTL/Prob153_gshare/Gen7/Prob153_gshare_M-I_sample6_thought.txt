Refine the global history register (GHR) update logic to strictly follow the problem specification:

- The committed GHR reflects the architectural (correct) branch history and is updated only on training events, by shifting in the actual branch outcome (train_taken). The input train_history is the GHR *before* the branch is trained (i.e., before shifting in the current branch outcome), so committed_GHR must be updated by shifting train_history with train_taken.

- On a misprediction training event, committed_GHR is set to the recovered history train_history shifted with train_taken, ensuring the correct architectural history after the misprediction. The speculative GHR is reset to this recovered committed GHR to rollback speculative updates after the mispredicted branch.

- On training without misprediction, committed_GHR is updated normally by shifting train_history with train_taken, and speculative_GHR remains unchanged.

- On prediction valid (and no training misprediction that cycle), speculative_GHR is updated by shifting in the predicted branch direction from the PHT.

- When training and prediction occur simultaneously with misprediction, training takes precedence: speculative_GHR recovers to the committed GHR state before being shifted by prediction.

- The prediction outputs (taken and history) reflect the PHT entry and speculative GHR *before* update (combinationally read and registered at clock edge).

- The PHT is updated at clock edge during training using train_pc XOR train_history index and updated using saturating counter with train_taken.

- Asynchronous reset initializes both GHRs to zero and PHT entries to weakly not taken.

This approach strictly differentiates architectural vs speculative histories, maintains correct timing for prediction and training updates, and ensures the global history state used for indexing is always consistent and correct at each pipeline stage. This will fix mismatches in predictions and histories.

Additionally, minor code cleanup improves readability, but the primary correction is in the GHR update logic.
