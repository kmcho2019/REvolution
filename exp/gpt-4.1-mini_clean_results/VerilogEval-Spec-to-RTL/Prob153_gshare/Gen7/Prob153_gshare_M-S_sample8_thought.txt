Implement a single 7-bit global history register (GHR) updated speculatively with predictions on predict_valid cycles and updated with actual outcomes on train_valid cycles. On misprediction training, recover the GHR to the given train_history (architectural state) to roll back speculative updates. The pattern history table (PHT) is a 128-entry array of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by pc XOR GHR. Prediction reads PHT combinationally with the current GHR; training updates PHT at the clock edge with the train_pc XOR train_history index. Prediction outputs (taken and history) are registered when predict_valid is high. Training and misprediction take precedence over prediction updates: if train_mispredicted, update GHR from train_history, ignoring speculative update from prediction that cycle. This approach keeps only one GHR, minimizing complexity while respecting update ordering and pipeline rollback.
