Simplify the gshare predictor by using a single speculative global history register updated only on the clock edge. For each prediction, use the current speculative history for indexing and output this history as `predict_history` (i.e., the history used to make the prediction before updating). 

Drive prediction outputs combinationally from the current PHT entry indexed by `predict_pc ^ ghr_spec`. On the clock edge:

- If reset, initialize PHT entries and history.

- If training valid and mispredicted, restore `ghr_spec` to `train_history` (branch history after recovery).

- Else if prediction valid, update `ghr_spec` by shifting in the predicted bit.

- Update PHT entries on training valid with saturating counter updates.

This simplified approach avoids separate committed/global history registers, removes unnecessary complexity, and ensures that `predict_history` corresponds to the global history used in prediction (i.e., before the speculative update), matching timing and output expectations.

Training has priority to update history and PHT.

The saturating counter update logic is straightforward.

This clean separation and single global history register better aligns outputs with the timing and reduces potential mismatches.
