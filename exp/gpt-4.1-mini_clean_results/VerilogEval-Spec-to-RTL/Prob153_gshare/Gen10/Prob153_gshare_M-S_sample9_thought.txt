Implement a straightforward dual-GHR gshare predictor with:

- A single 7-bit global history register `ghr` updated only on the clock edge.
- A 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter table indexed by XOR of PC and `ghr`.
- On prediction (predict_valid), output the prediction based on current `ghr`, and output current `ghr` as predict_history (before update). On the next clock edge, update `ghr` by shifting in the predicted bit.
- On training (train_valid), update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history with the actual outcome.
- If the branch was mispredicted, restore `ghr` to the given train_history to recover from speculative history.
- If training and prediction happen in the same cycle, prioritize training update and recovery of `ghr`.
- This simple design handles recovery, updates, and prediction in a clean way and meets all specs without complex dual speculative/committed GHRs.
