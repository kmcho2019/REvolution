I will simplify the design by focusing on clean separation between combinational prediction outputs and synchronous updates of GHR and PHT. The key simplifications:

- Declare all signals and variables clearly outside procedural blocks.
- Compute prediction index and outputs combinationally from current GHR and PHT.
- Update PHT synchronously on the clock edge only for valid training.
- Update GHR synchronously on clock edge, prioritizing training misprediction recovery; otherwise update from prediction if valid; else keep unchanged.
- Initialize PHT entries to Weakly Not Taken on asynchronous reset.
- Avoid declaring variables inside always blocks to maintain compatibility and clarity.
- Use simple saturating counter increment/decrement functions.
- Assign prediction outputs as zero when prediction is not valid.

This maintains functionality while reducing risks of simulation or synthesis issues and keeping the code straightforward.
