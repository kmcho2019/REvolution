To simplify the design while preserving functionality:

- Maintain a single global history register (GHR) which holds the speculative branch history.

- The PHT is a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter array, indexed by XOR of PC and GHR.

- For prediction:
  - Compute the index from the speculative GHR and predict_pc.
  - Output the prediction and the GHR used (before update).
  - At next clock edge, update the speculative GHR by shifting in the predicted bit if no training misprediction occurs.

- For training:
  - On train_valid, update the corresponding PHT counter based on actual outcome.
  - If train_mispredicted, restore GHR to the train_history to recover from misprediction.
  - Otherwise, update GHR by shifting in actual outcome to keep GHR consistent with committed history.

- When both predict_valid and train_mispredicted occur in the same cycle, training has priority to update GHR.

- Outputs reflect the state before any update in the current clock cycle to ensure correctness.

- This removes the committed/speculative GHR distinction, reducing complexity while still correctly handling misprediction recovery and predictions.

- Registers and arrays are updated on clock edge with non-blocking assignments.

This approach is simpler, respects priority and timing, and meets the specification.
