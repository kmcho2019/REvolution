Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT indexed by XORing the 7-bit PC and the 7-bit global history. The PHT entries are 2-bit saturating counters. On prediction (predict_valid=1), output the prediction (MSB of the PHT entry), output the current global history used, and update the global history at the next clock cycle with the predicted outcome (0 or 1). On training (train_valid=1), update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history according to train_taken and train_mispredicted. If train_mispredicted=1, restore the global history to the train_history value, overriding any prediction update for that cycle. If both training and prediction occur in the same cycle, training update and history override take precedence over prediction update. Asynchronous active-high reset initializes PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10) and global history to 0. The prediction outputs reflect the current PHT state before any training update at the same cycle.
