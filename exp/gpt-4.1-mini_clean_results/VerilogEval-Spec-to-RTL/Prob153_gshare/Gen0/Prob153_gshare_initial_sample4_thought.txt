Implement a gshare branch predictor with 128 2-bit saturating counters (pattern history table, PHT) indexed by XOR of the 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history register. The global history register is updated on predictions and can be restored on mispredicted training. Prediction output uses the PHT counter MSB as prediction. Training updates the PHT counters based on actual outcomes and handles misprediction by restoring the global history register to the trained history value. If training misprediction and prediction happen concurrently, training takes precedence on history update. Use asynchronous active-high reset to initialize history and counters to weakly taken (2'b10) and zero history. Prediction outputs current global history before update. PHT updates and history updates happen on clock edge. Implement one-hot muxing for prediction vs training history update, giving training precedence.
