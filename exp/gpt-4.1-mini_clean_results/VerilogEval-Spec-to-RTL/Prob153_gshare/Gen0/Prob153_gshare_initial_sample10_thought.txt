Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry pattern history table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of PC and global history. 

Two interfaces:
- Prediction interface: on predict_valid, read PHT at (predict_pc ^ history), output prediction and current history. At next clock, update global history with the predicted branch direction unless a training misprediction takes precedence.
- Training interface: on train_valid, update PHT at (train_pc ^ train_history) based on train_taken. If mispredicted, restore global history to train_history to recover state before mispredicting branch. 

Priority: if train_valid and mispredicted and predict_valid occur in the same cycle, global history update due to training takes precedence over predicted update.

The PHT updates and global history update happen on the positive clock edge. Asynchronous active-high reset clears history and PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10).

The outputs predict_taken and predict_history reflect combinational PHT read and global history on predict_valid.

Edge cases:
- When predict_valid is low, outputs should hold or be defaulted (we'll output 0).
- When no predict_valid, no history update from prediction.
- Training always updates PHT and may update history if mispredicted.

The global history is updated after prediction with predicted outcome or after training misprediction with train_history recovery.

Implement this logic with synchronous processes and combinational outputs.
