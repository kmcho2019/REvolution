Implement a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter PHT indexed by the XOR of the 7-bit PC and the 7-bit global history. On each predict_valid, output the prediction and the history before updating history at the next clock edge based on the prediction. On train_valid, update the corresponding PHT entry based on the actual outcome and if mispredicted, restore the global history to the supplied train_history. If both train_valid with misprediction and predict_valid happen simultaneously, training takes precedence and the global history update after the clock edge will follow the train's history recovery. PHT updates and history updates occur at clock edges accordingly, with asynchronous reset to clear global history and set PHT counters to weakly not taken (01). Prediction sees the PHT state before training updates at the same cycle.
