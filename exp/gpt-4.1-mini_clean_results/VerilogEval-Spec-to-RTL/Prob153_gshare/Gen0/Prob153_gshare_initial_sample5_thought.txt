Implement a gshare predictor with 128-entry 2-bit saturating counters and a 7-bit global history register (GHR). The predictor index is the XOR of the 7-bit PC and the 7-bit history. On reset, initialize GHR and all counters to weakly not taken (01). On predict_valid, output the prediction and the current history; also queue an update to GHR at next clock based on the prediction. On train_valid, update the corresponding PHT entry according to train_taken, saturating counter rules, and if train_mispredicted is asserted, restore GHR to train_history instead of updating by prediction. If predict_valid and train_valid occur in the same cycle and train_mispredicted is set, training takes precedence on GHR update. Prediction output sees PHT before training updates. This means prediction combinationally reads from PHT and GHR, training updates PHT and GHR synchronously at clock edge.
