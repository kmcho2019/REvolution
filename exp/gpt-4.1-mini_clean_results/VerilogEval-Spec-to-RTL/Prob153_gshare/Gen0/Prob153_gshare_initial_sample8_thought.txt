Implement a 7-bit global history register (GHR) and a 128-entry Pattern History Table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of PC and GHR (gshare). On prediction (predict_valid), output prediction based on PHT state indexed by (pc ^ ghr). Also output the GHR used for indexing (predict_history). Update GHR on the next clock edge to reflect the predicted branch direction. On training (train_valid), update the PHT at index (train_pc ^ train_history) based on train_taken and train_mispredicted. If train_mispredicted is high, recover the GHR to train_history on the next clock edge. When both prediction and misprediction training occur in the same cycle, training update to GHR takes precedence. Asynchronous active-high reset clears GHR and initializes PHT entries to weakly taken (2). The predict_taken is derived from the MSB of the 2-bit saturating counter (values 2 or 3 indicate taken).
