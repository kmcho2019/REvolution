Implement a gshare branch predictor with 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history register. The predictor uses XOR of PC and global history as index to a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters. On predict_valid, output prediction and current global history; update the global history next cycle based on prediction. On train_valid, update the PHT entry corresponding to train_pc and train_history according to the actual taken result and saturating counter rules; if mispredicted, also restore global history register to train_history (recover after misprediction). If both train and predict occur in the same cycle and train is a misprediction, train takes precedence on global history update. The PHT update and global history update occur at the clock edge. Use asynchronous active-high reset to initialize global history and PHT entries to default state (weakly not taken, e.g., '01'). Prediction output is based on MSB of 2-bit counter (>= 2 means predict taken). Provide all signals as specified.
