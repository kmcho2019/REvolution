Implement a 128-entry PHT indexed by a 7-bit value from XOR of global_history and pc. The 2-bit counters saturate between 0 and 3, with states 0,1 predicting not taken, 2,3 predicting taken. Maintain a 7-bit global history register that updates on prediction or on training of mispredicted branch. Training updates the PHT entry based on actual outcome, and if mispredicted, global history is recovered to train_history. Prediction interface outputs prediction and history. On reset, initialize PHT to weakly not taken (1) and clear global history. Priority: if train_valid & mispredicted, global history update on training takes precedence over prediction update. All sequential updates happen on posedge clk; asynchronous reset areset clears all registers.
