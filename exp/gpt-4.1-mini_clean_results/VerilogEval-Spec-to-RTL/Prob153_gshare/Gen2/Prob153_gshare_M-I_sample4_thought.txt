The main correctness concern is the update and usage of the global history register (GHR) when prediction and training occur simultaneously, especially if training is a misprediction and requires GHR recovery. The updated approach explicitly latches the predicted bit for the GHR update next cycle, ensuring the predict_taken used to update GHR is stable and not directly combinational from current PHT read which could cause glitches. Also, explicitly separate the GHR update logic into registering a predicted taken bit for use in the next cycle, reflecting the intended one-cycle delay before GHR update on prediction.

This approach:

- Introduces a registered predicted_taken signal (`predicted_taken_reg`) to be used for GHR update on prediction.
- Ensures GHR update has a clear priority: on training misprediction restore `train_history`, else on prediction update GHR with `predicted_taken_reg`.
- Separates the reading of PHT entries for prediction (combinational) from updates for training (synchronous) cleanly.
- Keeps asynchronous reset initializing PHT entries and GHR.
- Maintains saturating counter updates only when training valid.
- Keeps predict_history as the GHR value *before* updating for the prediction, matching the specification.
- Avoids combinational dependency loops by using registered signals for GHR update.

This resolves glitches or mismatches caused by updating GHR directly from a combinational prediction signal and ensures that prediction and training updates do not race improperly.

This refinement ensures functional correctness while preserving the clean architecture from the original design.
