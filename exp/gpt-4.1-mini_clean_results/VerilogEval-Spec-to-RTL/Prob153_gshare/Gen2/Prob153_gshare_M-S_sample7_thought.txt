Implement a straightforward gshare predictor by storing the PHT as a 128-entry array of 2-bit saturating counters and a 7-bit global history register. Perform combinational read of the PHT on the XOR of PC and GHR for prediction outputs, ensuring prediction outputs are immediate (no clock delay). Update the PHT and GHR on clock edge: if training is valid, update PHT entry and on misprediction restore GHR to training history; else if prediction was valid (and no mispredicted training), update GHR with predicted taken bit. Reset asynchronously initializes GHR and PHT to weakly not taken. This design keeps combinational prediction simple, updates on clock edge, and cleanly prioritizes training updates and GHR restoration over prediction updates.
