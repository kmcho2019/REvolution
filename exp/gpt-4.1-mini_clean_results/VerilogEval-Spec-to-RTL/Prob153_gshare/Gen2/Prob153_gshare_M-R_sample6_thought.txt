To fix the issues, implement the PHT as a synchronous RAM with combinational read port: the prediction output is generated combinationally by reading the PHT entry indexed by (predict_pc XOR current GHR). This ensures prediction outputs are valid immediately in the same cycle as predict_valid. The global history register (GHR) updates on the clock edge with predicted outcome if no training misprediction, or recovers to train_history on misprediction, with training update having priority over prediction for GHR updates. The PHT updates occur synchronously on the clock edge during training, and training updates take effect after the prediction in the same cycle, ensuring the prediction always sees pre-training PHT state. The GHR update logic uses next_GHR variable resolved in the clocked block based on priority to avoid conflicts. Initialize PHT and GHR on async reset. This approach cleanly separates combinational prediction and synchronous training and GHR update, respects update priority, and matches the problem timing requirements.
