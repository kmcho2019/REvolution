Refactor the design to explicitly separate combinational and sequential logic, improving clarity and correctness:

- Implement the PHT as a synchronous RAM array updated only on training cycles.
- Use combinational logic for prediction: compute index by XORing the current GHR and predict_pc, read the PHT entry combinationally, and determine predict_taken.
- Register the prediction outcome and the GHR used for the prediction in dedicated pipeline registers to ensure stable outputs aligned with clock edges.
- Update the GHR only on the positive clock edge, giving training (mispredicted) updates priority over prediction updates, recovering the GHR from train_history if mispredicted, otherwise shifting in the predicted taken bit from the previous cycle.
- By pipelining the prediction outputs (taken and history), we ensure outputs correspond exactly to the PHT state and GHR used for prediction, removing ambiguity about history timing.
- On asynchronous reset, initialize the PHT entries to weakly not taken and clear the GHR.
- This approach cleanly distinguishes between combinational indexing and sequential update, improving readability and potentially fixing subtle timing mismatches from the original design.
