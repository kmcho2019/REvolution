To correct the timing and output mismatches, the predictor must explicitly latch the prediction outputs and the history register value used for prediction at the time predict_valid is asserted. This way, the outputs reflect the GHR and PHT state before any updates, consistent with the spec.

I will separate the combinational read of the PHT and GHR from the registered prediction outputs. When predict_valid is high, the current GHR and PHT counter (indexed by predict_pc XOR GHR) are sampled into registers. The outputs predict_taken and predict_history are driven from these registers, ensuring stable outputs corresponding to the prediction request time.

Meanwhile, the GHR is updated synchronously on the clock edge. The update prioritizes restoring GHR from train_history on mispredicted training, else updates GHR by shifting in the predicted bit from the just sampled prediction (not the current PHT state). This makes the update consistent with the prediction registered earlier in the cycle.

The PHT is updated on train_valid, at the entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history, using saturating counters as before.

This approach aligns the outputs' timing with prediction requests, maintains correct update precedence and timing, and cleanly separates combinational prediction logic from sequential updates.

Overall, the design uses a small FSM implicit in the sequential logic for stateful updates and a combinational block for prediction index calculation. The outputs latch the prediction results for stable downstream usage, preventing glitches and mismatches.
