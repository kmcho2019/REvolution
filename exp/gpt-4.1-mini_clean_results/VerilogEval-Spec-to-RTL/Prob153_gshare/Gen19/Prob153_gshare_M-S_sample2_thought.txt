Implement the gshare predictor using a single global history register that is updated every cycle based on either training or prediction, prioritizing training updates on misprediction flushes as required. Use the XOR of the PC and global history to index a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter table. Predictions are made combinationally from the current PHT and history, outputs latched at the clock edge when predict_valid is high. On training, update the PHT and global history with actual outcome; if a misprediction flush occurs, recover the global history to the provided flushed history. This single-history approach simplifies logic and ensures correct update and recovery order, maintaining required functionality.
