Implement a straightforward synchronous gshare predictor with a 7-bit GHR and 128-entry 2-bit PHT. On each clock edge, update PHT on training and update GHR either by recovery on misprediction training or by predicted outcome on prediction (if no concurrent misprediction). To keep outputs simple and aligned, produce the prediction outputs combinationally based on current GHR and PHT contents without extra pipelining. Update GHR and PHT only at the clock edge. Prioritize training updates over prediction updates for GHR recovery. Reset asynchronously. This simpler approach keeps prediction outputs always consistent with the current state before update and meets the specification timing.
