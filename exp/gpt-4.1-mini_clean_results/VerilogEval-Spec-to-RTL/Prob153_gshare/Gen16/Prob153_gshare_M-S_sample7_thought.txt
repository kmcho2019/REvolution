Maintain a single 7-bit global history register (GHR) that always reflects the committed branch outcomes after training updates.

At each clock edge:

- If a misprediction training occurs, restore the GHR to the provided train_history to recover from the misprediction.
- Else if a training (non-mispredicted) occurs, update the GHR by shifting in the actual train_taken.
- Else if a prediction is valid and no training misprediction occurs, update the GHR by shifting in the predicted taken bit.

For prediction, use the current GHR to index into the PHT with predict_pc XOR GHR, and output the prediction and the GHR *before* any update occurs.

Updates to the PHT occur only on training cycles, indexed by train_pc XOR train_history.

Since prediction output depends on GHR before update, latch outputs on the clock edge when prediction occurs.

This priority ensures training misprediction updates take precedence and the outputs correspond to the exact predictor state used for prediction.

The code uses simple if-else priority to manage GHR updates, and straightforward latching of outputs on predict_valid cycles.
