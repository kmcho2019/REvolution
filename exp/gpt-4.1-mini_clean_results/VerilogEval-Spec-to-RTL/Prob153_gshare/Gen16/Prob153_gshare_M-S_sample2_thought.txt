Maintain a single 7-bit global history register (GHR) updated every cycle based on a priority:

- Highest priority: training with misprediction (restore GHR to train_history)
- Next: training without misprediction (shift in train_taken)
- Last: prediction valid (shift in predicted taken)

Use a 128-entry pattern history table (PHT) of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of PC and GHR.

For predictions:
- Combinationally read the PHT at index = predict_pc XOR current GHR.
- Output the predicted taken bit and the GHR before update.
- Latch these outputs when predict_valid is asserted for stable output.

On training:
- Update the PHT at index = train_pc XOR train_history with saturating update towards train_taken.

By updating the PHT only on training and using the GHR update priority, we avoid conflicting updates.

This design keeps the logic simple and timing consistent with spec.
