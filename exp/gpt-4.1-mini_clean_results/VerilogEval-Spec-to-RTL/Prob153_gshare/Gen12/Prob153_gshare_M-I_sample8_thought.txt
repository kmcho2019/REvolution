To fix the timing mismatch issues and strictly comply with the spec, the prediction outputs (`predict_taken` and `predict_history`) must reflect the PHT and GHR *before* any updates caused by training or prediction occur on the clock edge.

Key improvements:
- Make `predict_taken` and `predict_history` purely combinational outputs based on the current GHR and PHT state (no registered outputs). This ensures the prediction outputs correspond exactly to the predictor state *before* update.
- Store the prediction request data (`predict_valid` and `predict_index`) in registers on the clock edge for use in GHR update only, ensuring the GHR update uses the predicted value from the *last* prediction request.
- Perform PHT and GHR updates synchronously on the clock edge with priority given to training misprediction recovery.
- Update GHR either by recovering on training misprediction or by shifting in the predicted bit from the *previous* cycle's prediction request.
- This approach cleanly separates combinational prediction outputs from sequential updates and matches the spec’s timing: prediction outputs are based on current state and only update after the clock edge, while GHR and PHT update after the clock edge.
- Also initialize PHT entries to weakly not taken (`2'b01`) on asynchronous reset.

This clean separation prevents off-by-one errors in prediction outputs and history, and respects the priority and timing described.
