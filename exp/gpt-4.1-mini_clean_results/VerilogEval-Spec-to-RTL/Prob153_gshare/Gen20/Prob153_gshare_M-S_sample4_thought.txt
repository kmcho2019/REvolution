Implement a simple synchronous gshare predictor with a 7-bit global history register (GHR) and a 128-entry 2-bit saturating counter table (PHT). On each clock edge, update the PHT on training and update the GHR either by recovery on a misprediction or by shifting in the predicted bit from a prediction request, with training taking precedence. Outputs (predicted taken and history) are generated combinationally from the current GHR and PHT before any update. This straightforward approach matches spec timing and prioritizes training updates over prediction updates, ensuring the predictor outputs the history state used for prediction, and simplifies the design to avoid timing misalignments.
