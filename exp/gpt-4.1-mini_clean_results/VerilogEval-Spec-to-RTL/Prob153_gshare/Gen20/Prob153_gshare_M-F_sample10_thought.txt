1. Use combinational logic to generate prediction outputs (`predict_taken` and `predict_history`) from the current committed `ghr` and PHT before any updates occur on the clock edge. This ensures outputs reflect the state *used* for prediction.

- Correct output timing consistent with spec.

- Training updates have priority and always overwrite speculative history.
