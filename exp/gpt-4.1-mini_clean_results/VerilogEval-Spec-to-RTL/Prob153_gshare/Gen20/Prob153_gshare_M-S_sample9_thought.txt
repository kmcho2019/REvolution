Simplify the design by using a single global history register (ghr) for both prediction and training, updating it only on the clock edge. On prediction, output the current ghr as predict_history and the prediction result based on the PHT entry indexed by PC XOR ghr. On training, update the PHT entry and update or recover the ghr depending on whether the branch was mispredicted or not. On misprediction training, recover ghr directly from train_history. Prioritize training update and flush over prediction update when both happen simultaneously. This removes separate fetch_ghr and train_ghr registers and reduces complexity while preserving functionality and timing correctness.
