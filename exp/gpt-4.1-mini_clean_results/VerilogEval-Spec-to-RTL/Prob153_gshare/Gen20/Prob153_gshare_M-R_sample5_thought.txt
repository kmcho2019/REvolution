Refactor the gshare predictor by separating combinational prediction logic and synchronous state updates more explicitly. Use combinational logic to produce prediction outputs based on the current GHR and PHT read, and register these outputs on the clock edge only when predict_valid is asserted. For the PHT, keep it as a synchronous RAM with one read port and one write port. To avoid read-write race conditions, implement a read-first behavior by registering the read address and output. The global history register (GHR) update prioritizes training (including flush recovery) over prediction update each clock. By clearly separating combinational and sequential logic and adding registered intermediate signals for PHT reads, we reduce timing ambiguities and better follow the required update precedence and timings.
