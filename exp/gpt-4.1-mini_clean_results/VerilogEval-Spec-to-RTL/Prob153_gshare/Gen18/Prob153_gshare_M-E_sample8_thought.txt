I will implement the gshare predictor by separating the speculative and committed histories to cleanly handle speculation and misprediction recovery:

1. Maintain two 7-bit global history registers:
   - `ghr_spec`: speculative history updated on prediction.
   - `ghr_comm`: committed history reflecting the state after the last resolved branch.

2. The PHT is a 128-entry array of 2-bit saturating counters, indexed by XOR of PC and the history value.

3. On `predict_valid`, use `ghr_spec ^ predict_pc` as index into PHT to predict direction and output the current speculative history as `predict_history`.

4. On the next clock edge after prediction, update `ghr_spec` by shifting in the predicted taken bit to reflect speculative execution.

5. On `train_valid`, update the PHT indexed by `train_pc ^ train_history` using the actual branch outcome.

6. If `train_mispredicted` is asserted, recover `ghr_spec` to `train_history` (the committed history state after the mispredicted branch), and also update `ghr_comm` to `train_history`. This flushes speculative history beyond the mispredicted branch.

7. If training is not a misprediction, update `ghr_comm` by shifting in the actual outcome, committing the speculative history for this resolved branch.

8. If prediction and training happen simultaneously:
   - The PHT update happens with the training data.
   - The `ghr_spec` update on prediction is overridden by the recovery or commit from training to maintain correct ordering (training has priority).
   - Outputs reflect the PHT and history prior to any updates in the current cycle.

9. This dual-history approach cleanly separates speculative and committed states, simplifies reasoning about recovery, and naturally matches the timing requirements.

10. Asynchronous active-high reset clears PHT entries and both histories to zero.

11. The outputs `predict_taken` and `predict_history` hold the predicted direction and speculative history *before* the update in the current cycle and remain stable when no prediction is made.

This novel architecture emphasizes explicit speculation tracking and rollback, ensuring correct prioritization and timing of updates in complex pipeline scenarios.
