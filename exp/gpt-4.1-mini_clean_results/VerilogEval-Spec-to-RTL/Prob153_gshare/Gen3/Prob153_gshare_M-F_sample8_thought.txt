The root cause of the mismatches is that the GHR update on prediction shifts in the current combinational PHT prediction bit (`predict_counter[1]`) rather than the latched predicted bit (`predict_taken_reg`) output from the prior cycle. This can cause divergence between the predictor output and the actual internal history, leading to incorrect predictions and history outputs.

To fix this, I will modify the GHR update logic to shift in the latched predicted bit (`predict_taken_reg`), which is stable and corresponds exactly to the prediction output at that cycle. This ensures the GHR updates align precisely with the prediction outputs, maintaining consistency.

All other aspects remain the same: 
- Prediction outputs (`predict_taken` and `predict_history`) are latched on `predict_valid`.
- Training updates PHT using `train_pc` XOR `train_history` on `train_valid`.
- If a mispredicted training occurs, GHR is restored to `train_history`, taking precedence over prediction updates.

This fix ensures the predictor outputs and internal state evolve consistently and satisfies the timing and functional requirements described.
