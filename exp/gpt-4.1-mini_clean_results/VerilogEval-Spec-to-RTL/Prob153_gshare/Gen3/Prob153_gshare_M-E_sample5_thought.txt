Implement the gshare predictor using a two-stage pipeline to separate prediction and update logic clearly. In stage 1 (combinational), compute the prediction outputs based on the current GHR and PHT state. The predictor outputs the predict_taken and predict_history before the clock edge. In stage 2 (on clock edge), handle updates: the PHT updates on training requests, and the GHR updates either by training recovery (if mispredicted) or by shifting in the predicted bit (if no misprediction and prediction valid). 

To avoid conflicts, use separate registers for the PHT and GHR, with the PHT as a reg array and asynchronous reset initializing entries to weakly not taken (01). The prediction reads are fully combinational from the PHT, indexed by xor of pc and current GHR. The GHR update prioritizes recovery on misprediction over prediction update, exactly as required. 

By explicitly pipelining prediction output before updates and carefully ordering updates inside the clocked block, we eliminate race conditions and ensure outputs reflect pre-update state. This structure cleanly implements priority and timing rules, with stable combinational prediction outputs and synchronous updates to PHT and GHR on the clock edge.
