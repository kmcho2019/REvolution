Implement the gshare predictor with a two-stage pipeline approach inside the module to cleanly separate prediction logic and update logic. Use separate combinational logic for predicting based on the current GHR, but latch the prediction outputs and prediction history in registers to provide stable outputs across the clock cycle. Maintain two GHR registers: one representing the current GHR state (used for indexing the PHT), and one "next" GHR that updates each cycle based on training or prediction. On clock edge, update the PHT if training is valid, and update the current GHR to the next GHR. The priority for GHR update is: if training valid & mispredicted, recover GHR to train_history; else if prediction valid, shift in predicted bit from latched prediction; else hold GHR. By latching prediction outputs, any race conditions or combinational glitches are avoided. This also guarantees stable outputs matching cycle expectations and cleaner debug visibility. Initialize PHT and GHR on async reset. This approach differs by adding registered outputs and using a two-GHR scheme to resolve timing ambiguities.
