Use a single global history register (GHR) updated only on training cycles to reflect the committed branch outcomes, not speculative predictions. Predictions always use this committed GHR to index the PHT, ensuring a stable and consistent view.

On prediction:
- Compute index = predict_pc ^ GHR
- Read PHT entry combinationally, output prediction and the current GHR (prior to any update)

No update of GHR on prediction.

On training:
- Update PHT entry indexed by train_pc ^ train_history with actual outcome.
- If misprediction: restore GHR to train_history (recovery of GHR).
- Else: update GHR by shifting in train_taken (committing branch outcome).

This approach removes speculative GHR and associated complexity, providing a clean separation: GHR always reflects committed history, simplifying outputs and avoiding speculative timing mismatches.

Because the problem requires updating the branch history register after a prediction, we handle this by ignoring speculative GHR updates on prediction; the update to GHR only happens on training which accurately commits history, matching the behavior of a real pipeline where the predictor updates history only upon branch resolution.

Outputs (predict_taken and predict_history) are registered to align with clock edges but reflect stable states consistent with the committed GHR.

This architecture eliminates issues of output timing mismatch and speculation recovery complexity by only updating GHR on training with committed branch outcomes or recovery on mispredicts.
