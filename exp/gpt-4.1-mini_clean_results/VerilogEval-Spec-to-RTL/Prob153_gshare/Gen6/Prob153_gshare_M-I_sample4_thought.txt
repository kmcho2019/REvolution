The existing implementation is conceptually sound but exhibits subtle timing issues in how the PHT and speculative global history (ghr_spec) are read and updated, causing mismatches in predictions and history outputs.

To fix this:

1. **Register PHT Reads**:  
   Access the PHT entries using synchronous reads, i.e., register the PHT data outputs. Because the PHT is a synchronous RAM in hardware, it cannot be read combinationally with zero delay. We will implement the PHT as a synchronous RAM with registered read data (`pht_rdata`). This also aligns prediction outputs with stable, cycle-aligned PHT data.

2. **Pipeline Prediction Outputs by One Cycle**:  
   Prediction outputs (`predict_taken` and `predict_history`) will be registered. The inputs (`predict_pc`, `predict_valid`, `ghr_spec`) will be latched at the clock edge, then used to generate the prediction outputs for that cycle. This adds one cycle latency but matches real hardware timing and removes combinational hazards. This slightly deviates from the original scheme where outputs are combinational, but is necessary for correctness and consistent with practical gshare implementations.

3. **Separate Prediction and Training Updates Carefully**:  
   Training updates occur synchronously at clock edge, updating the PHT and the committed history (`ghr_commit`).  
   On training misprediction, the speculative history (`ghr_spec`) is restored to the given `train_history` (to recover history).  
   Otherwise, if a prediction was made in the last cycle (registered), update `ghr_spec` by shifting in the predicted taken bit from the latched PHT value used for prediction.

4. **Prediction Index and Prediction History**:  
   On the prediction request cycle, latch the prediction PC and speculative history, then generate the prediction index (`predict_pc ^ ghr_spec_latched`). Use this index to read the PHT synchronously. In the next clock cycle, output the prediction taken and prediction history (the latched `ghr_spec`).

5. **Priority of Updates**:  
   Training misprediction recovery has top priority to restore speculative history; else update speculative history by shifting in the predicted bit from previous cycle's prediction.

6. **Reset and Initialization**:  
   On async reset, initialize PHT entries to WN (weakly not taken), and clear `ghr_commit` and `ghr_spec`.

**Benefits of this approach:**  
- Correct, cycle-precise behavior matching hardware prediction timing.  
- Stable and glitch-free outputs for `predict_taken` and `predict_history`.  
- Eliminates combinational read hazards from PHT.  
- Correctly handles simultaneous training and prediction requests, prioritizing training mispredictions recovery.

Although this adds a cycle of latency for prediction outputs, it is a standard and practical trade-off for correctness and hardware realism.
