To fix this, the improved design:

1. Separates combinational and sequential logic clearly.
2. Uses intermediate combinational signals outside the always block to read PHT entries needed for GHR update.
3. Stores the predicted bit for GHR update in a reg updated combinationally before the clock edge.
4. Retains the priority of GHR update: training misprediction recovery has highest priority, else prediction updates GHR.
5. Keeps the predict_history_reg updated only when predict_valid is high.
6. Uses nonblocking assignments inside the always block for sequential updates.
7. Initializes PHT entries, ghr, and predict_history_reg on asynchronous reset.

This resolves the syntax issues and improves clarity, ensuring synthesis-friendly, synthesizable and correct Verilog.
