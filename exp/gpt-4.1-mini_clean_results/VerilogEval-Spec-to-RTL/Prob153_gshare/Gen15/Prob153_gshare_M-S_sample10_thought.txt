Use a single GHR register reflecting the committed (actual) branch outcomes. On each cycle:

- When training with misprediction occurs, restore GHR to the supplied train_history to recover from misprediction, ignoring the prediction update on that cycle.
- Otherwise, if training (non-mispredicted) occurs, update GHR by shifting in train_taken.
- Else if prediction valid (and no misprediction training), update GHR by shifting in predicted taken bit.

To avoid conflicting non-blocking assignments, perform GHR updates in a priority-encoded manner.

For prediction outputs, capture the PHT prediction and GHR used *before* GHR update (i.e., current GHR) in registers on predict_valid.

The PHT is updated on training valid cycles using the index = train_pc XOR train_history and saturating counters.

This design simplifies timing by updating outputs on the clock after prediction request, reflecting the predictor state used at request time, and maintains correct priority and GHR state.
