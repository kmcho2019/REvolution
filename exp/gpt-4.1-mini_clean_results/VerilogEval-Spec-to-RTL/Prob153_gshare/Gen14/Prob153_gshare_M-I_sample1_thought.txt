The main issues causing incorrect predictions and history outputs stem from how the committed global history register (committed_ghr) and training indexing are handled.

To fix these:

- The `train_history` input is the history state to be used for indexing and recovery during training, as given by the interface. We trust it represents the global history register state after the branch executed.

- Update `committed_ghr` on every training (both mispredicted and correctly predicted branches), so the global history correctly reflects the last committed branch outcome.

- When `train_mispredicted` is asserted, restore (recover) the committed_ghr and prediction_ghr to `train_history` (flush recovery).

- When training a correct branch, update the committed_ghr by shifting in the actual outcome (`train_taken`).

- For prediction, use the stable `prediction_ghr` for outputs and index, and on a new prediction, update `prediction_ghr` by shifting in the predicted outcome (`predict_taken`).

- When training and prediction occur in the same cycle, training takes priority in updating committed_ghr and prediction_ghr.

- Keep the PHT read combinational, with prediction seeing PHT before training update in the same cycle.

- Initialize PHT counters on async reset.

This approach ensures the committed_ghr always reflects the last committed branch, the prediction_ghr tracks speculative predictions and is updated properly or recovered on mispredictions, and the PHT is indexed consistently. This resolves mismatches in prediction outputs and history values and follows the specification precisely.
