Refactor the module by explicitly separating the prediction cycle's history snapshot and the GHR update. Introduce a register `pred_history_reg` that captures the global history (GHR) at the time of prediction request to output stable `predict_history`. The GHR is updated on the clock edge according to the priority:

1. If training with misprediction, recover GHR to `train_history`.

2. Else if training without misprediction, update PHT entry but do NOT update GHR.

3. Else if prediction only, update GHR by shifting in predicted bit from PHT.

This separates the output history (which must reflect the history used in prediction) from the updated GHR (speculative global history). The PHT updates occur synchronously, reading the state combinationally before update, so prediction always sees pre-update state.

We implement the following:

- A combinational wire for prediction index and train index.

- Combinational reads from PHT for prediction and training.

- `predict_taken` is assigned combinationally from the pre-update PHT entry at `predict_pc ^ ghr`.

- `predict_history` is registered on predict_valid, capturing GHR before any update.

- On posedge clk, apply training PHT updates and GHR updates according to priority above.

This explicit staging of `predict_history` matches the timing diagram, guarantees stable outputs during prediction, and properly prioritizes GHR updates.

Initialization on async reset is done for PHT and GHR.

This approach cleanly separates combinational prediction outputs from synchronous state updates and maintains the required timing semantics.
