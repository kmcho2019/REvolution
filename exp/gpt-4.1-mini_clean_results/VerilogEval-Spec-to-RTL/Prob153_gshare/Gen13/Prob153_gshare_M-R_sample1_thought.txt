Refactor the module to separate combinational and sequential logic clearly and improve the timing clarity of outputs. Instead of capturing `predict_taken` and `predict_history` in a clocked process directly, output these signals combinationally based on the current state of GHR and PHT, while registering the GHR update and PHT training updates on the clock edge.

Key changes:
- Use combinational logic for the prediction outputs (`predict_taken` and `predict_history`) directly derived from the current GHR and PHT state when `predict_valid` is high. When `predict_valid` is low, outputs hold their previous registered values.
- Use a small registered pipeline stage for the outputs to stabilize the outputs across clock cycles.
- Update GHR and PHT on the rising clock edge with training and prediction updates, maintaining the correct priority: training with misprediction, then training, then prediction.
- Separate combinational calculation of prediction index and predicted bit from the registered updates.
- Use a dedicated register to hold last outputs (`predict_taken_r` and `predict_history_r`) updated combinationally when `predict_valid` is asserted, else hold previous outputs.
- This decouples the prediction outputs from the timing of the updates and cleanly reflects the spec requirement that outputs reflect the state used for prediction before updates.
- Initialize PHT and GHR properly on asynchronous reset.
- Maintain the saturating counter update function.

This approach enhances clarity on when outputs are stable and visible, and cleanly distinguishes prediction output generation from state updates, avoiding subtle timing conflicts.
