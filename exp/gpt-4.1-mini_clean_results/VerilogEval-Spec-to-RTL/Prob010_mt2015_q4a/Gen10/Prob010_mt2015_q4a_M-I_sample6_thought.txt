To optimize the original design for better power and area without affecting performance, I will remove the separate XOR2 and AND2 submodules and express the logic directly using a single continuous assignment statement inside TopModule. This reduces module instantiation overhead, intermediate nets, and simplifies netlist complexity, thus improving area and power metrics. The logic expression z = (x ^ y) & x will be directly implemented in RTL, maintaining the same functionality with minimal gates inferred by synthesis tools.
