To implement z = (x ^ y) & x, I will create a single module TopModule without any submodule instantiation. I will use an always_comb block to describe the combinational logic with intermediate signals and explicit bitwise operators. This approach highlights the use of behavioral Verilog for combinational logic, allowing clearer control-flow-like semantics while maintaining synthesizability. It is a different architectural style compared to gate instantiations or continuous assignments, giving a clear procedural interpretation of the boolean expression.
