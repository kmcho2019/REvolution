To optimize for better power and area while maintaining the same functional correctness, I will replace the explicit XOR decomposition into basic gates with the direct use of the built-in XOR operator (^). This eliminates intermediate wires and gates, reducing switching nodes and complexity. The final output will be assigned directly as `z = (x ^ y) & x;`. This approach reduces gate count, switching activity, and area without impacting timing since the original design was purely combinational with minimal delay.
