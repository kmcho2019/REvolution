The original implementation perfectly matches the specified boolean function z = (x ^ y) & x and is already minimal and optimal from the perspective of performance, power, and area. The boolean expression can be algebraically simplified to z = x & ~y, which uses just an AND and a NOT gate, potentially reducing one logic level compared to XOR + AND.

Using the simplified form (x & ~y) could marginally reduce area and power by eliminating the XOR gate, as a NOT gate and an AND gate are simpler and potentially lower power than XOR + AND.

Therefore, I will implement z = x & ~y for a slight optimization without affecting correctness. This keeps the design combinational and minimal.
