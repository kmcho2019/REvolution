To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will maintain the clarity and succinctness of a single TopModule with continuous assignment (as in Example 1) but also include the option for modularity and reuse by defining parameterized gate modules internally using built-in gate primitives. This approach provides a clean, minimal, and directly synthesizable design while keeping the gate modules reusable for potential extension without sacrificing PPA. The single continuous assign in TopModule ensures minimal module overhead, optimal power, and area, while the internal gate modules provide a basis for gate-level hierarchy if desired. The design documents how to inline the logic or instantiate the basic gates, combining direct RTL simplicity and modular gate-level clarity within the same source file. This hybrid strategy supports both synthesis tool optimization and modular readability/flexibility.
