I will implement the boolean function z = (x ^ y) & x using a purely behavioral approach with a single always block. This will leverage a procedural assignment with bitwise operations inside a combinational always block triggered by any change in inputs. Using an always block instead of continuous assign emphasizes procedural style modeling and enables easy extension if sequential logic or more complex behavior were needed in the future. This approach eliminates the overhead of gate instantiations while still describing the desired combinational logic clearly.
