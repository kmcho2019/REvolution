The boolean function z = (x ^ y) & x can be simplified by applying boolean algebra. Noting that (x ^ y) & x = x & (~x & y | x & ~y) = x & ~y, since x & ~x = 0 and x & x = x, so the expression simplifies to z = x & ~y. Implementing this simpler function reduces gate count by replacing XOR and AND with a single AND and NOT gate, minimizing complexity while preserving functionality.
