The function z = (x ^ y) & x can be algebraically simplified to z = x & ~y. This removes the XOR gate completely, reducing the gate count from two to one, which will lower area and power. Additionally, by eliminating parameterized modules and intermediate hierarchy, we get a minimal and direct implementation that synthesis tools will optimize efficiently. This simplification maintains zero delay (no flip-flops), zero slack issues, and minimal switching activity, improving power and area without impacting timing.

Hence, the improved design uses a straightforward continuous assignment implementing z = x & ~y, with a single module TopModule.
