The original function z = (x ^ y) & x can be simplified logically. Given the XOR operation followed by an AND with x, this reduces to z = x & ~y. This is because XOR with y is 1 only when y is 0 and x is 1, so ANDing with x restricts output to when x is 1 and y is 0. Therefore, implementing z = x & ~y reduces gate complexity while maintaining the exact same functionality.
