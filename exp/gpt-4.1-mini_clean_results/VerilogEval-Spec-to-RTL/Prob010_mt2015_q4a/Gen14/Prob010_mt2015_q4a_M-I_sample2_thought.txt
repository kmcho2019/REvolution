The original logic z = (x ^ y) & x can be algebraically simplified using Boolean identities. Since (x ^ y) & x = x & ~y, we can replace the XOR and AND gates with a single AND gate and one inverter on y. This reduces the gate count from two to two simpler gates with less switching activity. Removing the parameterized modules and intermediate signals also minimizes hierarchy and area. This direct implementation improves power and area while maintaining zero delay combinational logic. Hence, the improved solution uses only basic operators and a simple continuous assignment for minimal overhead.
