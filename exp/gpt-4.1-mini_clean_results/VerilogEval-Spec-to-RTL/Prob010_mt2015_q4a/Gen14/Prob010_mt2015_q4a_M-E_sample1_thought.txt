I will implement the boolean function z = (x ^ y) & x using a behavioral approach with a single always_comb block to combine the operations in one procedural block. This approach uses procedural assignment and local intermediate signals internally, which is different from gate-level module instantiations. This style enables easy future extension with complex logic, simulation friendliness, and clear expression of the boolean logic without explicitly instantiating gates. The output will be driven by a reg assigned in the always block, with inputs in the sensitivity list to maintain combinational behavior. This is a clean, synthesizable, and straightforward implementation different from the gate-level modular approach.
