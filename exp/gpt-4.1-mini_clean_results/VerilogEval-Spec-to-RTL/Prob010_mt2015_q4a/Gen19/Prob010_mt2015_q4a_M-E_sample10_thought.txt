I will implement the TopModule using a behavioral style with a single always_comb block to compute the output. Instead of gate-level primitives, this approach uses procedural assignment which can be easily mapped to efficient hardware by synthesis tools. The always block clearly defines the combinational relationship without intermediate wires or submodules, simplifying the design hierarchy and potentially reducing area and power due to less routing. This method also facilitates easy extension for more complex logic later if needed. The output z is assigned as (x XOR y) AND x in a single statement inside the always block.
