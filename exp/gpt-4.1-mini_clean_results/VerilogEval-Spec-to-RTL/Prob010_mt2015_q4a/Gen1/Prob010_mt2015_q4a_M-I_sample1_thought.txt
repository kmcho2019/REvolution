The original implementation is already minimal and directly maps to the boolean function (x ^ y) & x. To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will try to simplify the logic expression.

Starting with z = (x ^ y) & x:

- (x ^ y) & x = (x & ~y) & x + (~x & y) & x = (x & ~y) + 0 = x & ~y

So the expression simplifies to z = x & ~y.

This simplification reduces one XOR gate, lowering area and power. The AND and NOT gates are generally simpler and faster.

Implementing z = x & ~y instead will improve PPA metrics slightly while preserving functional correctness.
