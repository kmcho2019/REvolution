Analyze the given function z = (x ^ y) & x for possible simplification. Expanding the expression:

(x ^ y) & x
= ((x & ~y) | (~x & y)) & x
= (x & ~y & x) | (~x & y & x)
= (x & ~y) | (0)
= x & ~y

The simplified function z = x & ~y uses only an AND and a NOT gate, potentially reducing gate count and switching activity compared to XOR followed by AND. This should reduce power and area marginally without affecting performance negatively.
