The original design uses a NOT gate and an AND gate instantiated as separate modules, which adds some module overhead and wiring complexity, slightly impacting area and power. To optimize for better PPA, I will:

- Eliminate the NOT1 and AND2 module instantiations.
- Use a direct assign statement with the simplified Boolean expression z = x & ~y.
- This reduces net count and gate overhead, improves area and power.
- The performance remains optimal since this is purely combinational logic with minimum delay.

This approach provides the simplest, cleanest, and most synthesis-friendly implementation of the required function.
