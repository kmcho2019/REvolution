Instead of instantiating separate XOR and AND gate modules or using a continuous assignment, I will implement the logic in a procedural always block triggered by any change in inputs x or y. Inside the always block, I will explicitly compute the XOR first, then AND with x, and assign the result to z using a reg type output.  
This approach demonstrates a simple behavioral style suitable for synthesis, avoids intermediate wire signals, and clearly separates the computation steps. It also allows easy extension for more complex logic if needed.  
Thus, the design is functionally equivalent but uses a sequential procedural approach rather than purely structural or continuous assignments, offering a fresh architectural perspective.
