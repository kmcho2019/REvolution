The original design combines XOR and AND gates to implement z = (x ^ y) & x, but this can be logically simplified to z = x & ~y, which reduces gate count and switching activity.

I will refactor by removing the XOR2 and AND2 modules and implementing the entire logic as a single continuous assignment in TopModule. This approach reduces hierarchy, area, and power while maintaining the same functional behavior. Using direct assign statements is straightforward and typically results in cleaner synthesis results for simple combinational logic.

This implementation also improves readability and reduces unnecessary module instantiation overhead.
