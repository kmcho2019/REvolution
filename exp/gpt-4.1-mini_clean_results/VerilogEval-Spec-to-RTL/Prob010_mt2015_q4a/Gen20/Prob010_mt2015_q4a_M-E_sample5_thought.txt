To create a novel architectural solution, I will implement the function z = (x^y) & x by decomposing the XOR operation into its fundamental gate-level expression using only NAND gates, which are universal gates.  
Specifically, XOR can be built from NANDs as follows: XOR = (A NAND (B NAND B)) NAND ((A NAND A) NAND B). Then, the final AND with x will be implemented also via a NAND followed by inversion (double NAND) to maintain a uniform gate style.  
This approach provides a gate-level structure suitable for NAND-based standard cell libraries and shows how to implement boolean functions from universal gates only, which is important for uniform cell generation and optimization in ASIC design.  
By explicitly instantiating NAND gates and wiring them, this design demonstrates the principle of universal gate implementation, helping to illustrate low-level digital design concepts.  
It also has potential benefits in ASIC flows that optimize uniform gates for power and area, although it introduces more gates logically.  
No intermediate wires are left unconnected and naming is carefully chosen for clarity.
