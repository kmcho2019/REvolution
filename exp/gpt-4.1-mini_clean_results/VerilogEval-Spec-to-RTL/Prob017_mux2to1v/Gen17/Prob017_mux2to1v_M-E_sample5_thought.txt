A novel architectural idea is to implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using bitwise logic gates instead of the ternary operator or direct continuous assignment. This method explicitly uses bitwise AND, OR, and NOT gates to perform the mux operation: out = (a & ~sel) | (b & sel). This approach represents the mux in terms of gate-level logic which might be beneficial for certain synthesis tools or to guide custom technology mapping.

Additionally, I will create a single flat module without submodule instantiations, directly implementing this logic for all 100 bits at once with vector operations. This will reduce hierarchy and module overhead, potentially improving area and simplifying analysis.

Overall, the design uses the basic gate-level mux equation and a flat implementation for all 100 bits in one step, providing a clean alternative to a ternary operator or chunked mux hierarchy.
