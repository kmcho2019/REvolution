To improve PPA (power, performance, area), I will flatten the design and implement the entire 100-bit 2-to-1 mux with a single continuous assignment using a ternary operator on the 100-bit vectors. This removes the overhead of instantiating multiple smaller mux modules, reducing area and power by simplifying routing and control signal distribution. 

Since this is a purely combinational mux, the single-wide assignment will also minimize logic duplication and switching activity compared to multiple smaller muxes. The code becomes simpler and easier for synthesis tools to optimize.

Performance remains optimal as the mux is just a direct data selector with no additional logic. This approach streamlines the design without sacrificing modularity or clarity.
