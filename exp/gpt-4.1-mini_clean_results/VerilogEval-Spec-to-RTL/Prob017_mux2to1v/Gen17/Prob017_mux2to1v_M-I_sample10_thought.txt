The original design uses 10 instances of 10-bit mux modules, which adds module instantiation overhead and potentially increases switching power due to duplicated control signals. To optimize power and area while maintaining performance, I will flatten the design into a single 100-bit-wide 2-to-1 mux using a simple combinational ternary operator. This eliminates unnecessary hierarchy and control signal duplication, reducing routing complexity and switching activity. The single mux expression is synthesis-friendly, preserves readability, and likely improves overall PPA without loss of clarity.
