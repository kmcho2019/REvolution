Since the top-level multiplexer is a simple 2-to-1 mux selecting between two 100-bit inputs based on a single-bit select signal, the simplest and most efficient implementation is to use a single ternary operator on the entire 100-bit vector without any modular chunking or submodules. This reduces module overhead, simplifies the code, and maintains the same functionality and performance. The synthesis tool will efficiently infer a 100-bit wide 2-to-1 mux.
