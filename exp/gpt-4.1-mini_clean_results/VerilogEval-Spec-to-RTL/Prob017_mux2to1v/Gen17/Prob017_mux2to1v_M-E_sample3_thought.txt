Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer for 100-bit vectors using a bitwise approach without any submodule instantiations or generate loops. Instead, directly use a vectorized conditional operator on the entire 100-bit input vectors. This eliminates hierarchy, reduces instance overhead, and provides a simple and flat logic structure.

This approach leverages built-in synthesis support for vector conditional assignments, producing minimal mux logic for the entire bus at once. It is straightforward, compact, and can yield better synthesis and place-and-route results by allowing tools to optimize the large vector mux holistically.

In summary:
- Single flat module with direct vectorized conditional assignment of 100-bit inputs.
- No submodules, no generate blocks, minimal and clear code.
- Simple and easy to maintain; synthesis tools will handle mux logic efficiently.
