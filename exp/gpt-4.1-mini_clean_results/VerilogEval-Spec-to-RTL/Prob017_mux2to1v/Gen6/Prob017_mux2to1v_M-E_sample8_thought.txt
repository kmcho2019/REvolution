Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer for 100-bit vectors using a generate-for loop that instantiates 100 separate 1-bit multiplexers. Each 1-bit mux is a simple conditional assign based on sel. This approach explicitly elaborates each bit's mux individually, enhancing clarity on the per-bit operation and allowing potential bit-level optimizations or modifications in the future. Although this might incur slightly more instantiation overhead compared to a vectorized assignment, it provides very fine-grained control and modularity for each bit's mux, which could be beneficial in designs requiring bitwise selective behaviors or constraints.
