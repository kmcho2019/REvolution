Since the original design uses 10 separate 10-bit mux modules which may add overhead in area and power due to extra routing and hierarchy, a flatter approach can improve PPA. 

The simplest and most efficient way for a large vector 2-to-1 mux is to use a single continuous assignment with a ternary operator on the entire 100-bit vector. This allows synthesis tools to optimize resource sharing, reduce area, and minimize power by avoiding unnecessary logic replication. 

This flattened implementation also reduces interconnect complexity and simplifies the netlist. It maintains the same combinational behavior and identical functionality.

Therefore, the improvement is to replace the hierarchical mux instances with a direct assignment on the entire 100-bit bus, improving area and power without sacrificing timing.
