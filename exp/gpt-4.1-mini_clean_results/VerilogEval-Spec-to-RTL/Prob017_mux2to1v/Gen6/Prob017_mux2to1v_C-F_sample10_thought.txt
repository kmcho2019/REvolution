Fuse the best ideas by leveraging a fully vectorized continuous assignment for the TopModule to maintain synthesis-friendly flat logic and minimal area/power overhead, while retaining a simple reusable parameterized N-bit mux module for modularity and scalability. This parameterized mux can serve as a clear building block for other widths, offering reusability beyond this problem. The TopModule then instantiates a single ParamMux2to1 #(WIDTH=100) instance, combining the modular clarity of Example 1’s 1-bit mux approach (generalized) and the hierarchical chunking modularity of Example 2, without incurring multiple instantiations or generate loops. This design provides:  
- A single clean instantiation for the whole 100-bit mux, minimizing routing and area.  
- Parameterization for easy scalability to other widths, improving reusability.  
- A simple continuous assign inside ParamMux2to1 for efficient synthesis and optimal PPA.  
- A modular structure that supports verification and potential formal proofs.  
Thus, it merges modular clarity, concise synthesis-friendly RTL, and scalability into a unified, superior design.
