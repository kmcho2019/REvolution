Fuse the modular clarity of the reusable 1-bit BitMux2to1 submodule with the flat, fully vectorized assignment approach of TopModule to achieve the best of both worlds: maintain a clear, testable, reusable 1-bit mux abstraction for clarity and potential formal verification reuse, yet implement the entire 100-bit mux in TopModule using a single vectorized assign statement without hierarchical submodule instantiations or generate loops. This ensures minimal area and power overhead and optimal timing since the synthesis tool sees a flat vector mux. The reusable 1-bit module remains available for other uses without incurring instantiation overhead in TopModule. This strategy leverages hierarchical clarity with zero runtime/area penalty at the top level, producing an elegant, efficient, and scalable design that surpasses previous examples in synthesis-friendly structure while preserving modular abstraction.
