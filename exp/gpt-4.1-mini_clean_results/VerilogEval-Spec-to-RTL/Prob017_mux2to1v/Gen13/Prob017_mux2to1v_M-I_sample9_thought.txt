To optimize for better power and area, I will revert the bitwise masked logic to a simple conditional (ternary) operator, as synthesis tools typically map `assign out = sel ? b : a;` directly to efficient multiplexers with minimal area and power. This reduces the number of gates (no separate AND/OR trees) and switching activity, improving dynamic power. The timing remains optimal due to single-level mux logic. I will keep the module parameterized for reusability but instantiate it directly inside TopModule with WIDTH=100.
