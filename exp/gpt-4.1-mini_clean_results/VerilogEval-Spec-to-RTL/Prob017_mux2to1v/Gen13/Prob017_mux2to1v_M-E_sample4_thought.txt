Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer by using a generate loop to instantiate 100 independent 1-bit mux cells. Each mux cell takes corresponding bits from inputs `a` and `b` and the common select signal `sel`. This approach modularizes the design, potentially enabling synthesis tools to optimize each bit individually, which may improve power due to localized switching. It also provides clear hierarchy and easier debugging. The 1-bit mux module is a simple ternary operator for conciseness and readability.
