Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a bitwise gate-level approach that explicitly uses bitwise AND and OR operations combined with the sel signal and its complement. This approach effectively builds the mux logic at the bit level without using the conditional operator or hierarchical chunk modules. The output is computed as (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) for each bit, which directly reflects the fundamental mux equation and is easily scalable to 100 bits.
