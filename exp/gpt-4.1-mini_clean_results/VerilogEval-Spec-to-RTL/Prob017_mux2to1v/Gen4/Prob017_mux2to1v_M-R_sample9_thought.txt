Refactor the hierarchical 10-bit mux chunked design into a fully flat structure that uses a single continuous assignment with a vector-wide ternary operator. This removes the submodule instantiations and generate loops, simplifying the design hierarchy and potentially improving timing and power due to reduced wiring and control logic. It keeps the same function (2-to-1 mux on 100-bit vectors controlled by sel), but expresses it directly and compactly in one line, which synthesis tools can easily optimize.
