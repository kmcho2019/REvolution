Implement the 2-1 multiplexer using bitwise logic operations rather than per-bit instantiated modules or vectorized ternary operators. Specifically, use bitwise AND, OR, and NOT operations to combine the inputs with the select signal, thereby constructing the output as `out = (a & ~sel) | (b & sel)`. This approach avoids module instantiations, uses only primitive bitwise operations synthesized efficiently, and clearly expresses the mux function at a vector level without relying on the conditional operator, which some synthesis tools may transform into similar logic anyway. It can improve area by avoiding the overhead of multiple module instances and can have similar or better power and performance characteristics due to simple combinational gates. The design is concise, fully combinational, and intuitive.
