Fuse the modular clarity of a reusable 1-bit mux module with the synthesis and PPA benefits of vectorized logic by parameterizing the BitMux2to1 module to operate on a generic width, here 100 bits. This maintains modularity with a single reusable component while enabling synthesis to treat the entire bus as one unit, facilitating efficient optimization.

TopModule then instantiates this parameterized mux with width=100, enabling both clear modular design and the advantages of vectorized assignment internally. This approach unifies the benefits: explicit module reuse without the overhead of 100 separate instances and with better area/power/timing than separate per-bit instances.

The BitMux2to1 module uses a single vector conditional assignment internally, preserving simplicity and performance, while TopModule only needs one instantiation, improving maintainability and synthesis friendliness.
