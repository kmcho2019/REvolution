Implement the TopModule as a fully flattened vectorized 100-bit 2-to-1 mux without any submodule instantiation. Use a single continuous assignment with a vectorized ternary operator for maximum simplicity, minimal instantiation overhead, and potentially better synthesis optimization. This approach leverages the synthesis tool's ability to handle wide vector muxes efficiently, avoids generate blocks and hierarchical structure, which reduces area and power by minimizing control and wiring overhead while keeping the design clear and concise.
