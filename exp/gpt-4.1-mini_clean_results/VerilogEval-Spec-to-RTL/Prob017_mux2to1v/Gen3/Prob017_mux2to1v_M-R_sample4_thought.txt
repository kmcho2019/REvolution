The core functionality remains a 2-to-1 mux for 100-bit vectors selected by 'sel'. Instead of instantiating multiple smaller mux submodules, this refactoring implements the entire 100-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer directly in the top module with a single continuous assignment statement. This flattens the hierarchy, potentially reducing routing overhead and allowing the synthesis tool to optimize the large vector mux more effectively. It also simplifies the design by removing the generate block and separate module, keeping the code concise and easier to maintain.
