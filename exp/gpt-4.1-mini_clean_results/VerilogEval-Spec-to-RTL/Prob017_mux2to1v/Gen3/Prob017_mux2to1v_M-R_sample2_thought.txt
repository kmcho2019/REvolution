Refactor the 2-to-1 multiplexer to implement the selection with a single continuous vector assignment rather than instantiating multiple 1-bit mux modules. This approach directly assigns `out` using a ternary operator on the entire 100-bit vectors `a` and `b` controlled by `sel`. It simplifies the design hierarchy, reduces area and power by minimizing the number of logic instances, and allows the synthesis tool to optimize the entire bus-wide mux efficiently.
