Fuse the clarity and modularity of a dedicated 1-bit BitMux2to1 submodule with the synthesis benefits of vectorized logic by leveraging a generate loop to instantiate 100 instances of BitMux2to1, each handling a single bit. This approach combines explicit, modular 1-bit mux logic facilitating easier verification and potential reuse, with clear per-bit structural decomposition aiding synthesis tools in logic optimization and routing. It also allows hierarchical debugging and potential submodule reuse in other contexts, unlike a monolithic vector assign. Compared to fully vectorized or purely bitwise assign solutions, this balanced approach enables clearer hierarchy, easier testbench targeting, and potentially better mapping to standard cell muxes for ASIC, while maintaining good PPA metrics.
