Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer on 100 bits using a generate loop that instantiates 100 single-bit 2:1 mux modules in parallel. Each single-bit mux selects the corresponding bit from 'a' or 'b' based on 'sel'. This structural approach provides fine-grained modularity and explicitly shows how each bit is muxed separately, supporting clarity and easy scalability. It contrasts with vector-wide conditional assignments or 2-bit chunk muxes by leveraging uniform bit-level mux cells instantiated repeatedly.
