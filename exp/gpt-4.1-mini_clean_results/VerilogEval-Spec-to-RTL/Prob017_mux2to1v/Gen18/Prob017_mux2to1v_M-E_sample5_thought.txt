Instead of implementing the 2-to-1 multiplexer as 100 independent 1-bit muxes or using a single vector select, this design divides the 100-bit inputs into 10 chunks of 10 bits each. Each chunk is then multiplexed using a 10-bit wide 2-to-1 mux. This hierarchical chunking approach balances between flat and fully bitwise implementations, potentially aiding synthesis by promoting moderate logic chunking which can enhance routability and pipeline insertion if needed later.

The TopModule instantiates 10 small 10-bit multiplexers and concatenates their outputs to form the final 100-bit output. This architectural idea can simplify layout and potentially improve modularity, testability, and synthesis tool guidance by limiting the mux size.

This approach is still purely combinational and maintains zero latency while providing a neat modular structure.
