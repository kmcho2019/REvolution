The original hierarchical 2-bit mux approach is functionally correct but unnecessarily complex for a simple 2-to-1 multiplexer on wide buses with a common select signal. Since all bits share the same `sel`, a single vectorized conditional assignment (`assign out = sel ? b : a;`) achieves the same function with less logic, lower power, and simpler routing.

This approach reduces logic duplication, net switching, and area by removing intermediate nets and modules. It also improves timing by flattening the mux logic into a single level, minimizing fanout and critical path length.

Hence, the improvement is to remove the 2-bit mux modules and hierarchical instantiation and replace them with a straightforward, single-line vector mux assignment. This results in better PPA while maintaining full correctness.
