Refactor the hierarchical 2-bit mux tree into a single vectorized 2-to-1 mux assignment. Since `sel` controls the entire 100-bit bus selection, a direct vector mux (`assign out = sel ? b : a;`) is simpler, more efficient, and reduces the number of intermediate signals and module instantiations. This approach improves performance, reduces power by minimizing switching on intermediate nets, and decreases area by letting the synthesis tool infer a wide mux directly. It maintains the same functionality but with cleaner and more concise code.
