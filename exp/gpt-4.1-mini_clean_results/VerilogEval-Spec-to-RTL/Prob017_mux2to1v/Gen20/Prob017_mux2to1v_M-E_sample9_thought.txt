To provide a completely different approach, I will implement the 2-to-1 100-bit multiplexer using an explicit bitwise generate loop of 1-bit 2-to-1 mux instances. This approach creates a structural design where each bit is handled by a dedicated 1-bit mux module. While less compact than a vectorized assignment, this method clearly shows the bit-level multiplexing architecture and enables potential bitwise modifications or instrumentation if needed. The top module will instantiate 100 individual 1-bit muxes using a generate-for loop, wiring each bit of inputs and outputs accordingly. This hierarchical design is fully synthesizable and provides clear, modular structure contrasting with the single vectorized mux approach.
