The simplest, most area- and power-efficient solution is a flat, single continuous assignment that directly selects between the entire 100-bit input vectors based on sel. This eliminates all module instantiations and wiring overhead associated with chunking inputs into smaller mux modules. 

This flat approach maintains zero latency and minimal logic depth since it is a simple vector mux. Although the hierarchical chunking approach provides modularity and could aid in some specific synthesis flows, the flat mux leverages synthesis tool optimizations better, resulting in potentially lower area, power, and wiring complexity without impacting performance.

Thus, the fusion strategy is to adopt the clean modularity from the examples but simplify to a single assign statement:
- No submodules or chunking needed.
- Use continuous assignment for the entire 100-bit vector mux.

This solution improves area and power, maintains simplicity, and keeps timing minimal.
