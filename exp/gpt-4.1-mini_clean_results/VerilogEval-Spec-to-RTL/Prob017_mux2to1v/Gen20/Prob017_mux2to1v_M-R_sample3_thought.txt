Refactor the hierarchical chunk-based mux design by replacing the submodule instantiations with a single continuous assignment using a ternary operator for the entire 100-bit bus at once. This approach simplifies the code, reduces module hierarchy and instantiation overhead, and achieves the same 2-to-1 mux functionality with a direct conditional assignment.
