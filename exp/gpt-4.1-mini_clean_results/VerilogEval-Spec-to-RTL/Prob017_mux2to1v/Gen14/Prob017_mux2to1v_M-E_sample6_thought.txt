Implement the 2-1 multiplexer by splitting the 100-bit inputs into ten 10-bit segments, then use an array of 10 small 10-bit multiplexers operating in parallel. This hierarchical approach balances routing complexity by localizing muxing to smaller segments, which can reduce fanout and improve timing on large wide buses. The top-level module instantiates a generate block that creates 10 instances of a 10-bit mux module. The selection signal is shared for all segments, maintaining synchronous behavior. This modular design enhances scalability and makes debugging easier while potentially improving synthesis and layout results in large datapaths.
