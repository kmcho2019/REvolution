Fuse the best ideas from both examples by designing a parameterized 2:1 multiplexer module that accepts a generic WIDTH parameter, and then instantiate it once in the TopModule for the full 100-bit width to keep the design flat and minimal.

However, to improve PPA beyond the flat large mux, implement a hierarchical generate-for structure *inside* the parameterized mux module itself, dividing the input vectors into smaller fixed-size chunks (e.g., 10 bits) internally. Each chunk is muxed individually using simple conditional assignments and then concatenated to form the output.

This internal chunking approach keeps the TopModule clean and flat with a single instance, preserves modularity and scalability in the mux module, and allows synthesis tools to optimize smaller units hierarchically, potentially improving timing, power, and area by balancing between flat vector muxes and many submodule instances.

Key advantages:
- TopModule simplicity: single instance with full width parameter.
- Internal generate-for modularization for better optimization and possible PPA gain.
- Parameterized WIDTH and CHUNK_WIDTH for flexible reusability.
- Minimal hierarchy outside the mux block reduces routing overhead.
- Avoids overhead of multiple module instantiations while preserving some chunk-based modularity.

In summary, a single instantiation of a parameterized mux with internal generate logic chunking yields a superior balance of modularity, maintainability, and efficient PPA.
