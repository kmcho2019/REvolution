Implement the 100-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer using bitwise logic operators without any generate loops or separate module instances. The module directly assigns the output by combining the inputs with bitwise AND and OR operations controlled by the single-bit select signal. Specifically, the output is computed as (sel AND b) OR (NOT sel AND a).

This approach leverages the vector operation capabilities of Verilog, resulting in a clean and concise design that synthesis tools can easily optimize into a single wide mux. It avoids hierarchical module overhead, reducing area and power, while potentially improving timing by eliminating additional logic levels or instantiation overhead.

This flat, combinational expression is also easy to understand and maintain. The design is fully combinational, with no clock or registers, making it a pure data-path element ideal for high-performance multiplexing of wide buses.
