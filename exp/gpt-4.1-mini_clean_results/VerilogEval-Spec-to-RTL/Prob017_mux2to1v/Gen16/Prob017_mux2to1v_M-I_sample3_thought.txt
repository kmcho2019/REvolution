To optimize for better PPA, especially area and possibly power, I will remove the hierarchical chunking and implement the 2-to-1 mux as a single vector-wide assign statement. This reduces the number of module instances and routing overhead, leading to a simpler and more compact design. Since the mux is purely combinational, a single assign statement is efficient and easy for synthesis tools to optimize. Performance should not degrade and may improve slightly due to reduced hierarchy and routing complexity.
