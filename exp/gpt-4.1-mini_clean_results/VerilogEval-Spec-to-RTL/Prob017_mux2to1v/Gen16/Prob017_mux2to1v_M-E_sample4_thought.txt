Implement the 2-1 multiplexer as a single vector-level conditional assignment instead of using multiple smaller mux instances. This approach leverages the Verilog conditional operator directly on the entire 100-bit vectors, simplifying the design to just one line of combinational logic. This reduces module overhead and instantiation complexity, likely lowering area and power by avoiding replication of mux logic. It's a clean and efficient way to express the wide mux behavior in RTL, which synthesis tools can easily optimize.
