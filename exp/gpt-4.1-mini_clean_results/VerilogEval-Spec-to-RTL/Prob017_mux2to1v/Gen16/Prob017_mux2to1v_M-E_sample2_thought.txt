Implement the 2-1 multiplexer as a single continuous 100-bit wide conditional assignment without hierarchy or generate blocks. Using a single assign statement with a vector ternary operator simplifies the code and synthesis results, minimizing area and power by letting the synthesis tool optimize the mux as a wide data selector. This approach reduces fanout of the selection signal to just one place and avoids the overhead of multiple module instances, which benefits overall design compactness and performance for a wide bus mux.
