Instead of using the replication operator with complement (~sel), which may cause unnecessary inversion and replication logic, implementing the mux using a generate-for loop with conditional assignments can guide synthesis tools toward efficient implementation. 

Also, splitting the vector into smaller chunks or bit-by-bit muxes inside a generate loop helps synthesis tools map logic to smaller cells and may reduce glitches.

This approach keeps the output purely combinational and does not add registers, preserving timing. Additionally, this can improve area and power slightly by avoiding wide replication and complement operations.
