Refactor the 2-to-1 multiplexer by removing the hierarchical 10-bit mux modules and implementing the entire 100-bit mux directly with a single continuous assign statement using the ternary operator. This flattening eliminates module instantiation overhead and can improve area and power by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the entire 100-bit mux as one unit. It also simplifies the code while preserving functionality and combinational behavior.
