Fuse the clean hierarchical chunking approach with the masking technique to implement a power-efficient and area-conscious 2-to-1 mux. Specifically, instantiate 100 single-bit muxes in a generate loop, each selecting between bits of `a` and `b` based on `sel`. This granular approach allows synthesis tools to optimize switching activity at the bit level, potentially reducing power compared to flat vector operations. Unlike the first example, we remove redundant modules and avoid wide vector masking, allowing better gating of switching internally in the tool. Compared to the second example, using 1-bit muxes instead of 10-bit chunks maximizes synthesis optimization granularity, possibly reducing power while incurring minimal area overhead. The overall design is straightforward, fully combinational, and modular without unnecessary intermediate modules.
