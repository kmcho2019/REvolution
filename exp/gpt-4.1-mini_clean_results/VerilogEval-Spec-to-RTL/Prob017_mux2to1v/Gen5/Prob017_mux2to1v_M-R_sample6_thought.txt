Refactor the TopModule by removing the submodule hierarchy and implementing the entire 100-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer with a single continuous assignment using a ternary operator directly on the full input vectors. This flattens the design, potentially improving synthesis optimization, timing, and power by eliminating the overhead of multiple smaller mux instances and intermediate wiring. The functionality and interface remain identical. This simpler structure is easier for synthesis tools to optimize and reduces complexity.
