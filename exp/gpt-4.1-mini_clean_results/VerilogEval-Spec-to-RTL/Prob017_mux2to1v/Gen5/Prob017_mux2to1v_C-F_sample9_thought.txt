Fuse the modular clarity of a reusable 1-bit BitMux2to1 module with a generate loop instantiation for improved structural clarity and easy per-bit control, but optimize area and power by instantiating only a small number of wider multiplexers rather than one per bit. Specifically, partition the 100-bit inputs into segments (e.g., 10 segments of 10 bits each) and instantiate a small-width mux submodule that handles each segment via vector-level assignment internally. This maintains modular hierarchy and verification convenience while minimizing overhead from instantiating 100 separate 1-bit mux modules. The TopModule uses a generate loop to instantiate these segment muxes, promoting manageable hierarchy and synthesis friendliness. Internally, each segment mux uses a vectorized continuous assignment for efficiency and minimal logic overhead, preserving the compactness and performance benefits of vector-level operations. This approach balances clarity, modularity, synthesis efficiency, and PPA benefits, improving on the prior examples by reducing module instance count drastically while maintaining a clear structural decomposition.
