Since the initial modular design is functionally correct but incurs overhead due to multiple submodule instantiations, I will flatten the design into a single 100-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer using a vector-level ternary operator. This approach simplifies the netlist, reduces routing complexity, and provides the synthesis tool maximum freedom to optimize mux implementation, leading to potential area and power improvements without sacrificing performance. The select line remains a single bit controlling the entire vector, minimizing switching nodes.
