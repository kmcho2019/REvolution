Refactor the TopModule by eliminating the per-bit instantiated submodules and replacing them with a single vectorized continuous assignment. This vector-level mux directly selects between the two 100-bit inputs `a` and `b` based on the single-bit selector `sel`. This approach simplifies the hierarchy, reduces module instantiation overhead, lowers power by minimizing toggling on control lines, and shrinks area by synthesizing to a single wide mux rather than many smaller muxes. Functionally it is equivalent and maintains combinational behavior while improving PPA.
