Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a generate-for loop to explicitly instantiate 100 individual 1-bit 2-to-1 mux cells, each selecting between the corresponding bits of inputs a and b based on sel. This structural approach clarifies bit-level selection, provides fine-grained control and can be useful if later per-bit customization or instrumentation is needed.

TopModule defines an internal 1-bit mux module and then uses generate to replicate it 100 times, connecting each bit pair of inputs to its respective mux. While more verbose than vectorized assignment, this method models the design explicitly at bit granularity and emphasizes structural regularity. This may ease formal verification and debugging, albeit at the cost of some verbosity.
