Refactor the original hierarchical multiplexer by removing the explicit instantiation of 10 submodules and instead implement the entire 100-bit 2-to-1 mux using a single continuous assign statement with a vector conditional operator. This flattening simplifies the design by directly assigning the output vector based on the select signal without the overhead of multiple generate blocks and submodules. This approach keeps the design purely combinational, clean, and may further improve synthesis optimizations by letting tools handle the large vector mux natively. It reduces code complexity and improves readability while maintaining identical functionality.
