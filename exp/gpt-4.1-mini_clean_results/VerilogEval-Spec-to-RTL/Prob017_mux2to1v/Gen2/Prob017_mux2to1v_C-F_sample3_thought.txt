Combine the clarity and modularity of the bitwise 2-to-1 mux submodule from the examples with the synthesis and PPA advantages of vectorized logic by implementing the TopModule using a single vector-level conditional assignment. This eliminates the instantiation overhead of 100 separate mux instances, improving area and power efficiency while maintaining clear, readable code. To preserve modularity and explicitness, retain the 1-bit BitMux2to1 module but only as a separate reusable component if needed; however, in TopModule, directly use a vector assignment for best PPA. This fusion takes advantage of synthesis optimization on wide vectors and keeps the design simple and efficient.
