To combine the clarity and flatness of Example 2 with the modular, chunked approach of Example 1, I propose a hierarchical design that instantiates 10 smaller 10-bit multiplexers inside the top module but implements each 10-bit mux using a concise ternary operator assignment within a dedicated submodule. This preserves the modular structure that can aid synthesis tools in optimization and power reduction by localizing switching activity, yet each submodule remains simple and clean, enabling good maintainability and clear code. By using generate loops and clear slicing, the design remains scalable and easy to extend. This fusion aims to gain the potential PPA benefits of chunking with the simplicity and directness of using a ternary mux operator in the submodules.
