Fuse the flat vectorized mux approach from Example 2 with hierarchical chunking from Example 1 to optimize PPA and maintain modularity. Implement a single parameterized 2-to-1 mux module that supports any width. Instantiate it hierarchically in TopModule by splitting the 100-bit inputs into 4 chunks of 32 bits and one chunk of 4 bits, rather than 10 chunks of 10 bits. This reduces the number of instances (from 10 to 5), lessens routing overhead, and aligns better with common datapath widths (32-bit chunks). Each chunk mux uses the single flat assignment for optimal synthesis. This approach preserves modularity, improves potential timing by reducing mux tree depth and fanout, and enables scalable reuse. Also, chunk sizes chosen for synthesis-friendly widths support better technology mapping in ASIC/FPGA. This fusion leverages hierarchical design benefits without the high instance count overhead, balancing performance, power, and area.
