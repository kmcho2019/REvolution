Implement the 100-bit 2-1 multiplexer using a single wide bitwise conditional operator without hierarchical submodules. This approach uses a simple assign statement applying the sel signal as a mask, performing bitwise selection between a and b in one expression. It reduces hierarchy and instantiation overhead, potentially simplifying synthesis and improving readability. It also leverages Verilog's support for vector conditional operators, making it highly concise and efficient for wide data buses. This flat architecture may provide slightly better area and power results since no module instantiation overhead exists and synthesis tools can optimize the mux as a whole.
