The original hierarchical design with ten 10-bit mux instances is modular but can introduce extra routing overhead and area without significant benefits for a simple combinational mux. Since the mux operation is straightforward (a direct selection of two 100-bit inputs based on sel), the most area-, power-, and performance-efficient approach is a flat, single-level continuous assignment using a ternary operator over the entire 100-bit vector. This reduces logic complexity, improves routing, and leverages synthesis tool optimizations for large multiplexers.

Hence, I will remove the chunked submodule instantiations and implement the entire 100-bit mux in one continuous assign statement.
