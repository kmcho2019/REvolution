To improve PPA (performance, power, and area) over the given 4-instance 25-bit chunked mux design, the simplest and most effective step is to remove the modular chunking altogether and implement the mux as a single flat 100-bit vector mux.

This approach benefits from:

- Eliminating module instantiation and generate block overhead.
- Allowing synthesis tools to better optimize mux logic for the full vector width.
- Simplifying wiring and potentially reducing net capacitance and power.
- Minimizing redundant sel signal buffering since only one mux instance uses it.

Although modularity is reduced, the simplicity and direct vector assignment maintain excellent clarity and synthesis efficiency.

Thus, the improved design implements the entire 100-bit 2-to-1 mux in a single module with a simple vectorized assign statement.

This should yield improvements in power (due to fewer transitions and simpler wiring), area (less module overhead), and maintain zero timing slack as the logic remains combinational and flat.
