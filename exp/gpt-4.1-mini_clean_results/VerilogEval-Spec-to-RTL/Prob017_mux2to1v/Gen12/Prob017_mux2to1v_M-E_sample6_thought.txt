An alternative architectural idea is to implement the 2-to-1 mux using bitwise logic operations instead of using conditional operators or chunked submodules. Specifically, we use the property that `out = (a & ~sel_mask) | (b & sel_mask)`, where `sel_mask` is the sel signal replicated across all bits.

By broadcasting the 1-bit `sel` signal to a 100-bit mask, we can efficiently select bits from `a` or `b` with simple bitwise AND and OR operations. This approach eliminates the need for generate loops or submodule instantiations, resulting in a fully flat and minimal combinational logic.

Advantages:
- Minimal hierarchy and simpler logic for synthesis tools to optimize.
- Potentially reduced routing complexity since no separate chunk instances.
- No parameterization, making the module straightforward and compact.
- Easy to understand and maintain as it leverages straightforward bitwise logic.

This design trades modularity for a very compact, efficient, and flat solution that should synthesize well and potentially reduce area and improve performance by avoiding additional mux instances.
