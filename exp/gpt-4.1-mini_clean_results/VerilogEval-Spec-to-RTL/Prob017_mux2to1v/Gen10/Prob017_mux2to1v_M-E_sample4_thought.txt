An alternative design uses a fully flattened, single-vector continuous assignment for the entire 100-bit bus without any submodule instantiation. This eliminates module overhead and generate complexity, potentially improving area and power by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the entire vector mux as one entity.

Implementation details:
- Implement the mux directly using a conditional (ternary) operator on the entire 100-bit vectors.
- This approach is extremely simple, fully synthesizable, and concise.
- It avoids hierarchical boundaries, minimizing routing and logic duplication.
- The design is easier to maintain due to its simplicity.
- This method leverages Verilog's ability to handle wide vector operations efficiently.

Overall, this flattened vector mux approach favors minimalism and synthesis tool optimizations for area and power, suitable when the entire vector mux is controlled by a single select line.
