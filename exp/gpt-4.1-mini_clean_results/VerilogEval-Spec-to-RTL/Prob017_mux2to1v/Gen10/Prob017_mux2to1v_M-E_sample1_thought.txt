Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a bitwise mask approach. Create a 100-bit mask where all bits are either 0 or 1 based on sel (all 0s when sel=0, all 1s when sel=1). Then select the output bits by combining inputs using bitwise AND and OR operations: out = (a & ~mask) | (b & mask). This flat, mask-based method eliminates multiple submodule instantiations, keeps the design fully combinational, and can be efficiently optimized by synthesis tools.
