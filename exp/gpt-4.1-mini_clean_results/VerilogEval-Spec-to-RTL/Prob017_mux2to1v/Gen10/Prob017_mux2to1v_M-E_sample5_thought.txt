Instead of chunking the input vectors, implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer as a bitwise mux using a generate-for loop that selects each output bit individually with a simple assign statement. This approach maintains modularity at the bit level without creating separate submodules and keeps the design fully combinational. Using a generate loop with a ternary operator for each bit balances readability and explicit bit-level muxing, which might facilitate bitwise optimizations during synthesis.
