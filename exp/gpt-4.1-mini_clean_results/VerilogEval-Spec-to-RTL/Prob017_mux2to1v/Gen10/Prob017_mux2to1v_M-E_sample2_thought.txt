Implement the 100-bit 2-to-1 multiplexer by instantiating an array of 1-bit mux modules, one for each bit position, effectively creating a structural design. This design uses a generate loop to instantiate 100 instances of a reusable 1-bit mux module (BitMux2to1). This explicit bitwise modular structure clearly illustrates the bit-level multiplexing logic, which is good for educational clarity and testbench debugging. Although this approach may have slightly more area and power overhead compared to a vectorized assign, it enforces modularity and allows for potential future enhancements on individual bits.
