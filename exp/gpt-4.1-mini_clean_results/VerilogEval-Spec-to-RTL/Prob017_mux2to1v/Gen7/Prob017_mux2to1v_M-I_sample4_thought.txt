The original design is already minimal and efficient for a 2-to-1 100-bit mux: a single parameterized module with vectorized conditional assignment maximizes synthesis quality.

To optimize for better power, area, and timing (PPA), a common RTL-level technique is to pipeline the output register, reducing glitches and output switching, which decreases dynamic power and may improve timing by breaking combinational paths.

Thus, I propose to add an optional output register stage clocked by an input clock. This will be a common improvement in practical designs for PPA:

- Add an input clk to TopModule for synchronization.
- Register the output of the combinational mux.
- This pipelining reduces glitches at out, potentially lowering power.
- Timing will improve by reducing combinational delay on critical paths.
- Area slightly increases due to the register but is often beneficial overall.

If clk is not desired, an enable input or bypass could be added, but to keep it simple, I will just add a clk input.

This design balances PPA by adding a simple register stage after the mux, a standard industry practice for such wide data muxing logic.
