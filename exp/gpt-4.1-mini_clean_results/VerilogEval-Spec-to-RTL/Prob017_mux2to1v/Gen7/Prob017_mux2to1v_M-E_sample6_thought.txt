Implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer by decomposing the 100-bit inputs into ten 10-bit chunks, each muxed independently using a simple dedicated 10-bit mux module. This chunk-based hierarchical design improves modular testing and can provide synthesis tools with clearer boundaries for optimization or partial reconfiguration. Each small mux uses a vectorized conditional operator, promoting efficient synthesis. The TopModule then instantiates ten such 10-bit mux blocks, connecting slices of the input and output vectors accordingly. This approach improves maintainability by handling fixed-size chunks and demonstrates an alternative structural style compared to a single wide mux or a fully parameterized one. It is suitable if partial or incremental resource usage control is desired, and can ease debugging or future extension to selective segment muxing.
