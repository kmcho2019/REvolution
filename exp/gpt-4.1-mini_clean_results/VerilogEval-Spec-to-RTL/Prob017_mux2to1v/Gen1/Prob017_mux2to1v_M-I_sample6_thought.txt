To optimize the 100-bit 2-to-1 mux for better power and possibly area, we can partition the large mux into smaller segments (e.g., 10-bit chunks) and instantiate 10 smaller 2-to-1 mux submodules. This hierarchical approach can improve synthesis results by enabling better optimization on smaller muxes. It might also facilitate better mapping to hardware mux structures and potentially reduce switching power by localizing mux logic. The output is reconstructed by concatenating these smaller mux outputs. The top-level remains combinational and straightforward.
