Implement the 2-to-1 100-bit multiplexer using a hierarchical tree of smaller multiplexers, mimicking a balanced binary mux tree. Divide the 100-bit inputs into groups of 4 bits and first mux each 4-bit group with a 2-to-1 4-bit wide mux module controlled by sel. Then recursively combine outputs in pairs using 2-to-1 wider mux modules until a single 100-bit output vector is formed. Since 100 is not a power of two, pad inputs internally to the nearest power of two (128 bits) and truncate output to 100 bits. This approach structurally represents the mux as a reduction tree of muxes, which could enable balanced routing and potential pipelining or partial evaluation optimizations, improving scalability and easing timing closure on large multiplexers. It also exposes a modular structure that can be instrumented or adapted easily for more complex selection logic, and reduces fanout per mux stage.
