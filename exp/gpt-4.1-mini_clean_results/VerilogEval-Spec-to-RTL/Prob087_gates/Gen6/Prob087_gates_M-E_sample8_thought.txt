Implement the TopModule by using a single always_comb block with a combinational logic sensitivity list to compute all outputs simultaneously. Instead of multiple intermediate wires, store the intermediate results in local variables inside the always block to minimize net count and keep combinational logic grouped. This approach simulates how an FPGA LUT or ASIC logic block might evaluate all outputs from inputs in a single process, making it straightforward for synthesis tools to optimize internal sharing of gates automatically. It is a different architectural style from purely continuous assignments and highlights use of procedural combinational logic.
