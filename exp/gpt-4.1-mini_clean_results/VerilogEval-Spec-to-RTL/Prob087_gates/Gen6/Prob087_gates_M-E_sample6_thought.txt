Instead of using direct continuous assignments for each gate, implement the logic using an intermediate two-bit vector that encodes the inputs in one-hot form. Then decode the vector to produce the outputs by combining bits in different ways. This approach uses a small intermediate representation to derive all outputs, demonstrating a structural decomposition rather than direct gate assignments. This method highlights how the two inputs can be encoded and re-used to compute various logic functions without directly operating on the raw inputs multiple times.
