To optimize for power and area while maintaining clarity, I will flatten the design by merging all logic directly into the TopModule without separate submodules. This eliminates intermediate wires and reduces module overhead, thus minimizing net switching and area. The combinational logic remains simple, and sharing intermediate signals is less beneficial here due to the overhead of additional wires. Using inline assignments reduces routing resources and simplifies the netlist, potentially improving power and area metrics while preserving correctness and readability.
