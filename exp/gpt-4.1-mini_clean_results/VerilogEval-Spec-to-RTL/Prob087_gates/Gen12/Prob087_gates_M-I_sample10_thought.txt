To optimize PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will flatten the basic gate modules into direct continuous assignments inside TopModule. This removes module overhead and reduces gate delay by removing inter-module boundaries. For the inverted outputs (NAND, NOR, XNOR), instead of creating separate inverter modules that invert intermediate signals, I will compute them directly using built-in Verilog operators with inputs a and b. This eliminates extra wires and reduces switching nets. The out_anotb can also be assigned directly without a separate module. This approach minimizes internal signals and connections, reducing area and switching power while simplifying the design and improving timing.
