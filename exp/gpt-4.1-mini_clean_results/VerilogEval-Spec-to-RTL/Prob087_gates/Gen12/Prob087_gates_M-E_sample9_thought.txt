Implement the TopModule using a single always_comb block that computes all outputs simultaneously with straightforward bitwise operations. This architectural choice avoids multiple module instantiations and intermediate wires, thus minimizing area and potentially improving power due to fewer nets. The always block is sensitive to inputs a and b, ensuring combinational behavior. This design favors clarity with a centralized logic definition and eliminates extra gate modules or inversion steps by coding each output explicitly with direct Verilog operators.
