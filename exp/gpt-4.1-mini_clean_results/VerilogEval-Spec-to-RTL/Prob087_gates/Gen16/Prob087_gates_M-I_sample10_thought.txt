To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we will simplify the design by removing separate gate modules and intermediate wires. Instead, we will directly assign all outputs using continuous assignment expressions within the top module. This reduces module instantiations and net overhead, leading to simpler, smaller, and more power-efficient logic. 

Using direct assignments leverages the synthesis tool's ability to optimize the logic more effectively, and removes the need for extra wires and module ports. The design remains fully combinational, clear, and easy to verify. 

This approach improves area and power by reducing netlist complexity and gate count while preserving timing (which is already minimal).
