To optimize for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will share intermediate signals for expressions used multiple times, such as a & b and a | b. This reduces redundant logic when synthesized, potentially saving gates and power. Outputs dependent on the same sub-expressions will use these intermediates. The combinational structure remains the same, so performance impact is minimal or improved due to reduced complexity. Area is likely reduced due to gate sharing, and power reduces by lowering switching activity on redundant signals.
