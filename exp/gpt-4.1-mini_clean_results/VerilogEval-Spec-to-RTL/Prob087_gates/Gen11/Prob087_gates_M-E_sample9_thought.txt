Implement the TopModule as a single module without submodule instantiations, using internal wires to compute shared intermediate signals once. For example, compute `and_ab = a & b` once, and reuse it for `out_and` and `out_nand` outputs, similarly for `or_ab = a | b`. Use assign statements for all outputs derived from these intermediates or directly from inputs. This reduces redundant logic calculations, minimizes gate count and wiring overhead, and improves area and power efficiency while keeping the design straightforward and fully combinational.
