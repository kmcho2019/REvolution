Create a purely structural design by instantiating primitive gate modules for each output. Define small dedicated modules for each logic gate (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, AND-NOT) and instantiate them inside TopModule to drive the outputs. This approach demonstrates modular reuse and hierarchical design, and emphasizes explicit gate-level instantiations rather than procedural or continuous assignments. It also serves as a clear example of structural Verilog design.
