Fuse the clarity and modularity of individual simple gate modules (from Example 1) with the shared intermediate signals approach (from Example 2) to minimize redundant logic computations and reduce switching and area. Specifically, define simple modules for each basic gate that take direct inputs (a,b) or intermediate signals as inputs, but do not complicate module interfaces. Then, in TopModule, compute shared signals (and_ab, or_ab, xor_ab) once, use them to drive corresponding inverted gates (nand, nor, xnor), and directly assign out_and, out_or, and out_xor from these shared signals. For out_anotb, compute directly in its simple module from a and b. This fusion keeps modular reuse and clarity, minimizes redundant logic and switching, and avoids complexity in gate module interfaces. The design remains fully combinational, easy to understand, and synthesis friendly with minimal area and power overhead.
