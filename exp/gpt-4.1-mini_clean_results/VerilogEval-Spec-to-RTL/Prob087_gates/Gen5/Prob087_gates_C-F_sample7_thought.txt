Fuse modular clarity with area and power efficiency by implementing the TopModule as a single module that uses intermediate wires to share common subexpressions (a&b, a|b, a^b). This eliminates redundant computations seen in the concatenated assign example and avoids extra module overhead of the first example. The intermediate signals drive the outputs directly or through inversion, maintaining readability and minimizing gate count and switching activity. This design balances modular style clarity (clear named signals) with optimized combinational logic sharing, reducing power and area while preserving straightforward code.
