Fuse the modular clarity of dedicated gate modules for all outputs with the efficiency of minimizing intermediate signals and simplifying the top module instantiations. Define individual gate modules for each gate type (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, ANDNOT) using direct assign statements to ensure clear, reusable, and explicitly defined logic per gate. Instantiate all gate modules directly in the top module without intermediate shared wires to avoid unnecessary net declarations and fanout, reducing wiring complexity and potential power consumption. This approach maintains a fully structural, hierarchical, and easy-to-verify design while eliminating overhead from intermediate signals, achieving optimal clarity, reusability, and minimal area/power usage in a concise form.
