Implement the entire combinational logic inside a single TopModule using continuous assignments with intermediate signals to share common subexpressions. For example, compute "and_ab = a & b" once and reuse it for out_and and out_nand to save logic. Similarly, compute "or_ab = a | b" to use for out_or and out_nor. Use bitwise negation and XOR to get other outputs. This reduces redundant computation and eliminates the need for submodules, minimizing area and power while keeping timing optimal.
