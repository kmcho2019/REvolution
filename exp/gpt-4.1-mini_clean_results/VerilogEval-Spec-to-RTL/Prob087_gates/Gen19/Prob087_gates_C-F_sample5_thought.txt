To create a superior solution, fuse the clarity and modularity of defined basic gate modules (AND, OR, XOR, AND_NOT) with optimal area and power efficiency by directly implementing NAND, NOR, and XNOR as inverted expressions within the top module without extra modules or intermediate wires.

Key points of the fusion strategy:
- Keep basic gates as simple, reusable modules for clarity and potential reuse.
- Instantiate these basic gate modules only for fundamental operations to maintain modularity.
- Avoid separate modules or intermediate wires for complemented outputs (NAND, NOR, XNOR). Instead, directly invert the basic gate outputs inside the top module.
- Implement all logic combinationally using continuous assignments, minimizing hierarchy depth, wiring overhead, and gate count.
- This balances hierarchical clarity, ease of verification, and synthesis-friendly flat logic, leading to minimal area and power with zero performance penalty.

This approach preserves clear modular boundaries for primary gates and derives complementary outputs efficiently in the top module, thus improving PPA metrics while maintaining maintainability and readability.
