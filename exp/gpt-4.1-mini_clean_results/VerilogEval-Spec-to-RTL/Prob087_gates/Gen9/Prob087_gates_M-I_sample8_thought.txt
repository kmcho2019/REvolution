To improve the original modular design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will flatten the design into a single TopModule that uses direct continuous assignments with combined logic expressions. This removes the overhead of multiple small module instantiations and intermediate wiring, reducing logic depth and interconnect area. I will implement NAND, NOR, and XNOR directly rather than inverting outputs of AND, OR, and XOR gates, avoiding unnecessary inverters and reducing switching activity. This approach keeps the design functional and combinational but yields a more compact and efficient implementation suited for synthesis with minimal delay, power, and area.
