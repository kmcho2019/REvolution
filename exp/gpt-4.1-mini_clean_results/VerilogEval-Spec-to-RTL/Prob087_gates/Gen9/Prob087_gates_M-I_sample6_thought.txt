To optimize the design for better power and area, I will consolidate all gate computations into a single combinational module without instantiating separate gate modules. By computing shared intermediate signals like `and_ab = a & b` once and reusing them for outputs like `out_and` and `out_nand`, we reduce redundant logic and switching activity. This approach reduces area and power while maintaining zero timing slack. The code remains simple and clear, improving overall PPA.
