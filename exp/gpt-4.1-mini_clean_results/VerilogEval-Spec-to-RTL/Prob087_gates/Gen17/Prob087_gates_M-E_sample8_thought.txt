Instead of defining separate gate modules, implement the entire logic directly inside TopModule using continuous assign statements. To introduce some architectural novelty, group related outputs into intermediate wires using generate blocks or localparams to emphasize logic sharing and minimize redundant evaluations. For example, compute the basic operations (and, or, xor) once and reuse their results for derived outputs (nand, nor, xnor). Also, demonstrate use of a simple combinational always block with a packed struct for outputs for clarity and future scalability. This approach flattens the design, reduces overhead from module instantiations, and showcases a mixed style combining continuous assignments and procedural blocks within one module.
