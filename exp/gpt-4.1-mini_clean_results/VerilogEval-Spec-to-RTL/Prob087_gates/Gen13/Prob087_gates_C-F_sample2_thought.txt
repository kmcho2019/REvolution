Fuse the modular clarity of individual gate modules with the efficiency and area savings of shared intermediate signals within the top module. Instead of instantiating separate gate modules, define concise parameterized local functions within TopModule or use localwire signals to compute shared basic logic operations once (e.g., and_ab, or_ab, xor_ab). Then directly assign all outputs by combining these intermediate signals or their inverses, including the out_anotb term. This approach retains clear structure and readability but eliminates overhead of multiple module instantiations and redundant calculations, reducing area and power while maintaining the easily understandable modular design style. It also enables easy future expansions or modifications by centralizing logic in TopModule, with all combinational logic visible and shared.
