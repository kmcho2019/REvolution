To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will flatten the hierarchy by eliminating the individual gate modules and directly implement all combinational logic in the TopModule using continuous assignments. This removes module instantiation overhead and intermediate wires, thereby reducing area and potentially lowering net switching for power. The direct inline assignments also improve synthesis optimization opportunities without sacrificing clarity since each output clearly maps to its logic expression. This approach retains all functionality but with a more compact, efficient implementation suitable for minimal combinational logic.
