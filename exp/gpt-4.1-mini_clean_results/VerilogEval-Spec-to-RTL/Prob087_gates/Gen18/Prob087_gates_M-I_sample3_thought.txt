To optimize for better power and area while preserving clarity and functional correctness, replace the small module instantiations of basic gates with direct continuous assign statements using built-in Verilog operators. Implement all outputs directly (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, AND_NOT) without intermediate signals or complements, thus eliminating unnecessary nets and reducing switching activity. This minimizes gate count, interconnects, and net toggling, improving power and area. The design remains fully combinational and readable, with each output expressed cleanly in a single assign. This approach optimizes PPA while maintaining explicit logic expressions.
