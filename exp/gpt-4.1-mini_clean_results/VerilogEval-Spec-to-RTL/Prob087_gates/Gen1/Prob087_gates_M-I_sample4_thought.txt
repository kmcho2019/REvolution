The existing design is already minimal and optimal for a small combinational logic block. However, we can slightly improve area and power by sharing common sub-expressions where possible (e.g., computing a & b once and reusing it for both out_and and out_nand). Similarly, (a | b) can be reused for out_or and out_nor, and (a ^ b) for out_xor and out_xnor. This reduces gate duplication. Although modern synthesis tools usually handle such optimizations, explicitly coding it can assist tools and slightly reduce the critical path and area.

Therefore, the improvement strategy is to use intermediate wires to hold shared sub-expressions, reducing redundant computation and potentially improving PPA metrics without changing functionality.
