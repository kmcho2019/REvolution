The original design is already minimal, clear, and uses direct gate-level operations which are optimal for area and performance in a simple combinational logic module. Each output corresponds to a unique logic function with minimal gate delay and resource usage. No redundant logic or unnecessary complexity is present.

To slightly improve power by potentially reducing switching activity without impacting performance or area significantly, I will use localwire signals to share common terms where applicable. Specifically, since `a & b` is used in both out_and and out_nand, and `a | b` in both out_or and out_nor, these intermediate signals can be computed once and reused. Similarly, `a ^ b` is used in out_xor and out_xnor.

This approach introduces local signals and reduces repeated computation, which can help synthesis tools optimize transistor sharing and reduce power slightly.

The combinational nature is preserved using `assign` statements, and no registers are introduced. The code remains clear and straightforward.

Overall, this minor refactor may provide a small power saving without increasing area or harming timing.
