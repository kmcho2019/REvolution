Flatten the design by implementing all logic directly in a single TopModule without instantiating separate modules for basic gates or inverters. Compute each output using direct continuous assign statements that correspond to their respective logic expressions (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, and AND_NOT). This reduces module overhead, wiring complexity, and extra logic depth from inversion modules. It simplifies synthesis, reduces area and power, and improves overall PPA while preserving the correct functionality and clarity.
