Implement the TopModule purely using continuous assign statements with bitwise operators, without any sub-modules, for simplicity and minimal hierarchy. This approach reduces module instantiation overhead and can help synthesis tools optimize logic more aggressively.

All outputs are expressed directly in terms of inputs 'a' and 'b' using bitwise operators:
- out_and = a & b
- out_or = a | b
- out_xor = a ^ b
- out_nand = ~(a & b)
- out_nor = ~(a | b)
- out_xnor = ~(a ^ b)
- out_anotb = a & (~b)

This flat combinational design is fully synthesizable, easy to maintain, and can potentially minimize delay and area by allowing the synthesis tool to merge and share logic gates internally.
