Specifically, I'll implement AND using a classic NAND-based approach: AND(a,b) = NAND(NAND(a,b), NAND(a,b)).

This approach uses just two NAND gates and keeps the design purely structural without behavioral statements or submodules. It demonstrates how to build AND logic from only NAND gates at the gate level, providing a clean, technology-mappable netlist style that differs from a direct assign or modular behavioral approach.

This solution offers:
- Gate-level clarity using basic universal gates (NAND).
- No hierarchy or intermediate modules, resulting in a flat design.
- Clear demonstration of how AND can be built from NAND gates.
