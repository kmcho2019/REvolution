A novel architectural idea is to implement the AND function using only NOR gates based on De Morgan's law: A AND B = NOT(NOT A OR NOT B). By creating a small internal hierarchy with three NOR gates, the design is structurally different from the straightforward AND or NAND implementations. This approach showcases how basic gates can realize the AND function and may be used for educational or specific technology mapping purposes. The TopModule directly instantiates three NOR gates to achieve the AND behavior.
