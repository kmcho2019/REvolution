The original solution uses a modular gate-level design that implements AND with two NAND gates, ensuring clarity and modularity but incurring extra gate delay, power, and area overhead.

To optimize for power and area without sacrificing correctness, I will simplify the design by directly implementing the AND function using a continuous assign statement in the TopModule, eliminating the intermediate AndGate module and reducing gate count from two to one. This reduces switching activity and wiring, improving power and area metrics.

This approach keeps the design purely combinational and straightforward, making it synthesis-friendly and minimal in resources. Performance remains optimal since a single gate-level delay is less than or equal to the two NAND gates previously used. This refinement is preferred for trivial combinational logic like a single AND.

Hence, I trade off modular hierarchy for better PPA metrics, which is justified by the simplicity of the function.
