The original design implements the AND function using three NOR gates, which is functionally correct but suboptimal in area, power, and delay. To optimize PPA, the design should directly implement a two-input AND gate since the behavior matches A AND B exactly. This reduces gate count to one primitive gate, eliminating intermediate signals and minimizing switching activity, thus improving power and area. The simplified combinational implementation with a direct AND gate also reduces logic depth, enhancing timing.
