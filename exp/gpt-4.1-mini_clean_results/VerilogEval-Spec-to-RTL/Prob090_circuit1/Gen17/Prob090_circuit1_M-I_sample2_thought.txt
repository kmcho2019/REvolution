The circuit implements an AND gate between inputs a and b, producing output q. The previous solution uses a modular approach with a separate AndGate module instantiated in TopModule. While modular and clear, this adds a small hierarchy overhead that slightly impacts power and possibly timing.

To optimize for PPA, we can flatten the hierarchy by removing the AndGate submodule and directly assign q = a & b in the TopModule. This reduces routing and instance overhead, potentially lowering power and area slightly while maintaining optimal performance. The logic remains combinational and synthesizable.

This results in simpler code, minimal area, and minimal power without sacrificing clarity or functionality.
