The observed behavior of the output `q` matches the logical AND of inputs `a` and `b`: output is 1 only when both inputs are 1, otherwise 0. Instead of using a separate module or structural gates, I will implement the circuit using a continuous assignment with a conditional operator. This approach offers a concise and readable solution, highlighting the boolean logic directly in the assignment. Additionally, this style allows easy extension or modification of the logic, providing a flexible template.

Hence, the TopModule will have a single continuous assignment statement implementing `q = (a && b) ? 1'b1 : 1'b0;`. This is logically equivalent to `q = a & b` but explicitly shows the condition, making the behavior very clear.

This design approach:
- Implements the AND functionality in a simple, single-line conditional assignment.
- Avoids additional gates or modules.
- Clearly expresses the intended logic behavior.
- Is easily synthesizable and efficient.
