The original solution implements the AND function using a hierarchical module which is clean and modular but adds slight overhead due to module instantiation. Since the combinational logic is very simple (a single AND gate), we can optimize for power and area by removing the intermediate AndGate module and directly assigning the output in TopModule. This will reduce the logic levels, routing complexity, and potential buffering, improving power and area with no impact on performance because the function is trivial.

Hence, the improved strategy is to implement TopModule with a direct assign statement `q = a & b`, removing all hierarchy for minimal overhead while preserving the intended AND logic from the waveform.
