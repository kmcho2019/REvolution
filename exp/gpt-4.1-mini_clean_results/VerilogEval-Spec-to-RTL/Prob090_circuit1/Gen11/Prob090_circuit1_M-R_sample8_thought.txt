Refactor the AND gate implementation from a structural, module-instantiated version using NAND gates to a direct continuous assignment in the TopModule. This approach retains the same logical functionality (AND of inputs a and b) but simplifies the design by removing the extra module and gate-level instantiation. Using a direct assign statement improves clarity, reduces code complexity, and can lead to better synthesis results in area and power. The combinational nature is preserved without using an always block, maintaining clear, simple RTL style.
