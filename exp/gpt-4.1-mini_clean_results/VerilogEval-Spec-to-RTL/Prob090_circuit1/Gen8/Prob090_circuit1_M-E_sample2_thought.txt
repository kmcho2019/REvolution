Since the output q is 1 only when both inputs a and b are 1 (i.e., it behaves as an AND gate), a new architectural approach is to use a simple always_comb block with a ternary operator for the combinational logic. This approach is behaviorally descriptive, straightforward, and synthesizable, providing clarity and compactness without explicit gate instantiations. This also facilitates easier modification or extension in the future, for example adding more complex logic.

This method contrasts with the previous gate-level structural approach by directly describing the combinational relation in a behavioral manner, reducing code complexity and enhancing readability for synthesis tools and designers, while maintaining the exact functionality observed from the waveform.

No explicit assign statements are used; instead, an always_comb block with a nonblocking assignment is employed to emphasize combinational logic, which can be useful in certain synthesis and simulation environments.
