The original solution implements AND using a two-level NAND gate structure for modularity and a gate-level style. While functionally correct and structurally clear, it introduces unnecessary gate overhead, resulting in higher power and area usage.

To optimize for better power and area without compromising correctness or performance, the design can be simplified by directly implementing the AND function as a continuous assignment in the TopModule. This eliminates extra gate instantiations, reduces netlist complexity, and minimizes switching activity, thereby improving power and area metrics.

Since the design is combinational and trivial, removing the separate AndGate module and directly coding the AND logic in the TopModule is the most effective improvement. The simplicity will also aid synthesis and optimization tools.

Hence, the improved design uses a direct assign statement for q = a & b in the top module, achieving better PPA while maintaining exact functional equivalence.
