The original design implements the AND function using three NOR gates, which is structurally interesting but not optimal for PPA metrics. Since the specification only requires the AND functionality and there is no constraint on gate types, a direct AND gate implementation is the simplest and most efficient approach. This reduces logic depth, gate count, switching activity, and routing complexity, leading to improved performance, power, and area. Thus, the improvement strategy is to replace the three-NOR-gate structure with a single AND operator assignment in a purely combinational manner.
