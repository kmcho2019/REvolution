Another novel way to implement an AND gate is to use the conditional (ternary) operator in a continuous assignment, which is often less obvious. The output q is assigned 1 only when both inputs a and b are high; otherwise, it is 0. This approach highlights the flexibility of Verilog operators and makes the combinational logic very explicit and readable without instantiating any gates or modules. It also avoids direct use of bitwise operators and uses a simple expression that synthesizes efficiently.
