Implement the two-input AND function by expressing it in terms of NAND and NOT gates instead of directly using an AND gate. This alternative approach decomposes AND into NAND followed by NOT, which can be advantageous in some technologies where NAND and NOT gates are more efficient or standardized cells.

The architecture consists of two simple modules: a NandGate module and a NotGate module. The TopModule instantiates these in series: inputs go into the NandGate, whose output feeds the NotGate, whose output is the final AND output. 

This structural decomposition keeps modularity for clarity and reusability, but uses only NAND and NOT gate primitives, showcasing an alternate gate-level approach. It also enables easy replacement of NAND and NOT implementations if needed for technology mapping or optimization.

This design differs fundamentally from using a single AND primitive and emphasizes building up the AND function from universal gates.
