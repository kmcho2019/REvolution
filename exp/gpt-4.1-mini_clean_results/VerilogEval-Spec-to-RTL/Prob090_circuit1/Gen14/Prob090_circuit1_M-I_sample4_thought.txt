To optimize power and area while keeping performance optimal, the best approach is to remove the hierarchical AndGate module and directly implement the AND logic in TopModule using a simple continuous assignment.

This reduces routing complexity, eliminates module instantiation overhead, and minimizes switching capacitance, improving power and area metrics without affecting performance or functionality.

- Remove AndGate module.
- Implement AND function directly in TopModule with a single assign statement.
