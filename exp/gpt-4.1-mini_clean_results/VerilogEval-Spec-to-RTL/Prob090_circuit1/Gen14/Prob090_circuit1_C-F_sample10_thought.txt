I will fuse the best ideas by implementing the TopModule combinationally with a direct behavioral AND assignment for maximum simplicity, minimal gate count, and optimal PPA metrics. This approach combines:
- The direct AND operator's efficiency (lowest area, power, and delay) from behavioral modeling.
- The modular structure for clean design and future extensibility.
- Simple, synthesizable, and widely portable code.

It avoids unnecessary NAND gate overhead and complexity but retains a clear hierarchy and easy-to-understand modular design, providing an excellent balance of clarity, synthesis friendliness, and PPA optimization.
