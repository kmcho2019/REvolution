To fuse the strengths of both examples, I will implement the TopModule as a purely combinational module using a direct assign statement for the AND function, which reduces gate count, power, and area. This is the simplest, most efficient solution since the logic is trivial.

At the same time, to preserve modularity and reusability from the examples, I will include a separate AndGate module that also uses a direct assign for clarity and minimal delay, allowing easy reuse and clear hierarchy when needed.

The TopModule will instantiate the AndGate module, keeping modularity and clarity, but internally the AndGate uses the simplest behavioral assign expression (assign y = a & b;) to optimize power and area.

This approach:
- Combines modular hierarchy with minimal logic complexity
- Uses behavioral assign for minimal gate usage and power
- Avoids unnecessary structural NAND gate chaining
- Keeps the design clean, readable, and synthesis-friendly
- Ensures zero timing overhead and minimal power/area

Thus, the fusion strategy is a clear, modular, and minimal gate count design by directly using the AND operator in the submodule, and top-level clean instantiation for hierarchy.
