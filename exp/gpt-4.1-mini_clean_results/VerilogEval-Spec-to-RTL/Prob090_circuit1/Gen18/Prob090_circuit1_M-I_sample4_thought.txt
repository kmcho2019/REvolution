The original solution implements the AND function using two NAND gates, which is functionally correct but slightly suboptimal in terms of power and area. Since the problem is a simple combinational AND gate with single-bit inputs, a direct behavioral implementation using the built-in '&' operator will reduce gate count, simplify the circuit, and likely improve power and area by eliminating the extra NAND gate stage. This direct approach is also more efficient in synthesis and leads to better PPA metrics without losing clarity.
