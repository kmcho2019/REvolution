I will fuse the clarity and modularity of using a dedicated AndGate module from Example 2 with the efficiency of the built-in AND primitive operator for maximum simplicity, minimum gate count, and minimal power and area. 

This approach keeps the modular structure, which aids readability, testability, and reusability, but replaces the structural NAND-based AND implementation with a direct assign statement (y = a & b). This produces a pure combinational logic AND gate in the simplest form, enabling the synthesis tool to map directly to an optimized AND gate primitive in the target library. 

Advantages:
- Modular design improves maintainability.
- Built-in AND operator reduces gate count, power, and area compared to NAND-based structural implementations.
- Simplifies logic, improving synthesis friendliness and potentially enhancing timing.
- The design remains fully combinational and straightforward.

This fusion combines best design practices for clarity, reuse, and PPA optimization.
