To fuse the strengths of both examples, the best approach is to implement the TopModule using a direct behavioral continuous assignment to minimize gate count, power, and area, while preserving clarity and simplicity.

From Example 1, the hierarchical approach clarifies intent and reuse, but adds gate overhead by using NAND-based AND implementation. Example 2 shows the universal gate decomposition with NOR gates but also adds gate levels and power due to multiple gates.

The fusion strategy is to implement the AND function directly with a continuous assign statement in TopModule, which results in the simplest and most efficient solution. This approach removes unnecessary intermediate gates and hierarchy, achieving minimal power and area while preserving functionality and performance.

This design is clear, synthesizable, and optimal for PPA. It can be easily extended if hierarchy or gate-level detail is later required, but for now, the direct assign implements the function cleanly and efficiently.

No separate module instantiations or gate primitives are used; the module interface is kept minimal and clear.
