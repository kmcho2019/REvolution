Since the original design implements AND using two NAND gates, we can improve power and area by directly using the built-in Verilog AND operator to describe the combinational logic. This removes one gate level, reducing delay and power consumption, and reduces the synthesized area by enabling the synthesis tool to map directly to a single AND gate primitive if available in the target technology. This approach keeps the design simple, clear, and fully combinational without any overhead from extra modules or gates.
