The original design uses a hierarchical structure by instantiating an AndGate module with a structural NAND-based implementation. To refactor while preserving the core idea (an AND gate for q = a & b), I will simplify the design by removing the submodule and implementing the AND operation directly in the TopModule using a continuous assign statement. This maintains combinational behavior, makes the code more concise, and leverages Verilog's built-in bitwise AND operator for clarity and synthesis efficiency, achieving the same logic function without extra wires or instances.
