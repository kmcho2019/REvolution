To refactor the existing design implementing the AND function using NAND gates, I will simplify the structure by removing the separate NANDGate module and implementing the NAND logic directly with assign statements inside TopModule. This keeps the core idea of computing AND via two NAND operations but avoids separate module instantiation, making the design more compact and easier to understand at a glance. The NAND logic will be directly coded as continuous assignments using bitwise operators, preserving combinational behavior and the same output functionality.
