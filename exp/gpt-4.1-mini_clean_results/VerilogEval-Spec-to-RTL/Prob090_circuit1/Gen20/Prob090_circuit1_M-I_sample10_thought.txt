The previous implementation correctly uses NAND gates to realize an AND function, but it employs two NAND gates to do so, which increases power and area. To optimize power and area while maintaining a purely combinational and synthesizable design, I will simplify the logic by directly assigning the AND of inputs a and b to output q using the built-in AND operator. This reduces gate count to one, lowers switching activity, and results in more efficient synthesis. It also maintains the same functional behavior as seen in the waveform.
