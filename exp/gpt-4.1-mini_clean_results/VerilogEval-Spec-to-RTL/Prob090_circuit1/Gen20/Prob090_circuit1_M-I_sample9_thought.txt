The original solution uses two NAND gates to implement the AND function. While functionally correct, this adds unnecessary area and power overhead compared to directly using a single AND gate primitive.

To optimize for better power and area without sacrificing performance, I will implement the TopModule using a simple, direct AND operator in a continuous assignment. This eliminates the extra inverter NAND gate stage, reducing gate count and complexity.

This approach:
- Reduces the total gate count from two to one.
- Lowers area and power consumption by removing the second gate.
- Keeps the design purely combinational and simple.
- Improves readability and synthesis friendliness.

Since the critical path was already minimal (only two gates in series), the timing will be as good or better due to fewer logic levels.

This straightforward behavioral coding style is preferred in most practical digital design flows and will lead to a better PPA profile.
