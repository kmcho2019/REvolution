The original design is already optimal for the required function (AND gate). To slightly optimize power and area further at RTL level, we can use the bitwise AND operator directly and explicitly declare the output as a wire. Additionally, if the synthesis tool supports it, adding a `wire` declaration and using continuous assignment remains standard practice. For performance, area, and power, the minimal AND gate can't be simplified further. Thus, the improvement is mainly stylistic and ensuring clarity, without changing functionality.
