The original design uses a hierarchical module (AndGate) to implement the AND function, which is modular but adds slight overhead in area and power due to module instantiation.

To optimize PPA, I will inline the AND operation directly in TopModule using a single assign statement. This removes unnecessary hierarchy, reducing area and power by eliminating extra nets and buffers introduced by module instantiation. The combinational logic remains minimal with zero delay, ensuring no performance degradation.

This direct assign approach is the simplest and most efficient implementation for the given function and waveform, making the design leaner and more suitable for ultra-low area and power constraints without losing clarity.
