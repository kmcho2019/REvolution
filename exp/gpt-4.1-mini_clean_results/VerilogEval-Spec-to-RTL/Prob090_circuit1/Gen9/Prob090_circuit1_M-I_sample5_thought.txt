The original design uses a hierarchical structure with a custom AndGate module internally implemented by two NAND gates. While this approach is modular and structurally clear, it leads to extra gate usage and power consumption.

To optimize for power and area without sacrificing clarity or correctness, the improved design directly implements the AND function in the TopModule with a continuous assignment. This reduces the gate count to a single logical AND operation, minimizing switching activity and area.

The combinational nature of the circuit is preserved. The code becomes simpler and more efficient. Removing the AndGate module eliminates unnecessary hierarchy and gates, improving PPA metrics notably for power and area.

Overall, this solution balances code clarity and simplicity with optimal resource usage for this simple AND function.
