The function implemented is a simple AND gate. The previous design used two NAND gates to implement AND via De Morgan's law, which is functionally correct but uses two gates. To optimize power and area while keeping timing optimal, we can directly use an AND gate primitive in Verilog, which is more efficient in area and power since it uses only one gate. This also reduces gate count, lowering switching activity and dynamic power consumption. Timing remains minimal as the critical path is just one gate delay.
