The original solution implements the AND function with a hierarchical module that instantiates AndGate. While this is modular and clear, it adds some module instantiation overhead that can marginally increase area and power due to routing and buffering.

Since the function is simple (AND of two inputs) and no hierarchical reuse or complexity is required, the optimal solution for power and area is to implement the AND function directly in the TopModule using a continuous assignment. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and reduces logic depth and routing complexity, thereby optimizing power and area with zero timing penalty.

Therefore, I will simplify the design by removing the AndGate module and directly assigning q = a & b in TopModule.
