Analyzing the waveform, the output q is high only when both inputs a and b are high simultaneously. This is the behavior of a logical AND operation.

This approach:

- Uses a direct conditional expression, making the intent clear without instantiating submodules or gate-level primitives.
- Avoids structural gates and purely behavioral assign with bitwise operators, showing an alternative style.
- Ensures minimal logic and no additional wires.

Thus, this solution demonstrates how to implement AND behavior functionally via conditional expressions.
