The original design uses an extra module (AndGate) to implement a simple AND operation, which adds unnecessary module hierarchy and wiring overhead. Since the functionality is straightforward — output 'q' is high only when both inputs 'a' and 'b' are high — we can inline the AND operation directly in TopModule. This eliminates module instantiation overhead, reducing area and power slightly while maintaining the same zero-delay combinational behavior and maximizing synthesis friendliness. 

This flattening improves PPA by:
- Minimizing the number of nets and module boundaries to reduce routing and switching.
- Simplifying the design for better synthesis optimization.
- Keeping the design straightforward and clean for verification.

No functional change is introduced, only structural simplification for better Power, Performance, and Area metrics.
