I will implement TopModule as a purely structural design using only NAND gates to realize the AND function. Using De Morgan's theorem, an AND can be formed by NAND followed by a NAND inverter stage. This approach contrasts with the direct behavioral 'and' operator by explicitly instantiating basic NAND gate modules.

This structural design breaks down the AND operation into two NAND gates:

1. NAND of inputs a and b.
2. NAND inverter of the first NAND output to generate AND.

This approach provides insight into gate-level implementation, mimics real physical gate structures, and may be useful for educational purposes or certain synthesis flows where explicit gate structures are desired.

The design remains fully combinational and synthesizable while showcasing hierarchical gate-level module composition.
