Instead of using a behavioral AND or a simple submodule, I will implement the AND function using only NAND gates structurally. NAND gates are universal, and many standard-cell libraries optimize NANDs more than ANDs. By using two NAND gates — the first NAND of inputs a and b, and the second NAND inverting the output of the first — we realize an AND gate. This approach emphasizes structural design and gate-level modeling, which might be useful for low-level synthesis or custom cell mapping scenarios. It also demonstrates how to build the function from universal gates without any behavioral operators.
