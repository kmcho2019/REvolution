The current implementation uses two NAND gates to create an AND function. While functionally correct, it is possible to optimize for power and area by using a direct AND operator instead of two NAND gates. This reduces gate count from two to one and minimizes switching activity, improving power and area with potentially better timing as well. The combinational logic is simple, so using the built-in '&' operator is clean, efficient, and synthesizes to a single AND gate in typical libraries. This change maintains correctness and simplifies the design.
