The original solution uses three NOR gates to implement an AND function, which is structurally interesting but introduces extra gate delays, area, and power. Given that the functionality is simply q = a & b, a direct AND gate implementation will optimize power and area by reducing gate count and switching activity, and improve timing by minimizing logic depth. Since the circuit is purely combinational with one-bit inputs, using the Verilog built-in AND operator directly for the output is the best approach for optimization without losing clarity.
