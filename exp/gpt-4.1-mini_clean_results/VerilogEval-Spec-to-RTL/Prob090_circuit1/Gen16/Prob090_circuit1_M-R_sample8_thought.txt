The original design uses a structural instantiation of an AND gate built from NAND gates. To refactor while preserving the same AND functionality and a combinational interface, I will implement the AND function directly with a continuous assignment statement. This removes the hierarchical module and structural gates, simplifying the code and improving clarity, area, and power without changing the logic behavior. The output q will be assigned as the logical AND of inputs a and b, maintaining the same truth table and waveform behavior.
