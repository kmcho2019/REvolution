To improve the PPA of the FSM, I will:

1. Use a `case` statement for next-state and output combinational logic inside an always_comb block. This reduces the long chain of conditional operators and helps synthesis tools optimize logic better for timing.
2. Retain binary encoding for area efficiency but simplify the decoding by direct comparisons.
3. Keep output `z` combinationally assigned using the same case block for clarity and efficiency.
4. Use a synchronous reset with non-blocking assignment.
   
This approach should reduce critical path delay by removing nested ternaries, improve power by cleaner logic, and potentially reduce area by clearer structure for synthesis.
