I will implement the FSM using one-hot encoding to simplify next-state logic and reduce decoding complexity. Each state will be represented by a separate flip-flop bit, and next-state logic will be expressed as combinational assignments based on current state bits and input w. The output z will be a combinational OR of the flip-flops corresponding to states E and F. This approach reduces the combinational decoding overhead, potentially improving timing, and provides a different architectural style compared to the binary encoded state machine.
