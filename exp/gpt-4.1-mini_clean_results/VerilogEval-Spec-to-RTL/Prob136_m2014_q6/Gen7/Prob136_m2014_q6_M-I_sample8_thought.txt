To optimize for better PPA, especially timing and power, we use one-hot state encoding to simplify the next-state logic and reduce gate levels in decoding, potentially improving timing. This may slightly increase area but can reduce critical path delay. Also, we separate output logic into a dedicated always block with non-blocking assignments to enhance readability and timing. We keep synchronous reset and positive-edge triggering. The next-state logic uses straightforward combinational logic with one-hot encoded states for minimal combinational depth. This should reduce power by minimizing glitching and switching in the state register, while improving timing due to simpler decoding.
