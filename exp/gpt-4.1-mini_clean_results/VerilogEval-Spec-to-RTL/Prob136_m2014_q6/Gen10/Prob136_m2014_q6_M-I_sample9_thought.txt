To improve PPA, we simplify the next_state logic by using a case statement instead of nested ternary operators for better synthesis optimization and timing. We also refine the output logic by encoding output z as a simple combinational check of the two highest bits of the state, since states E (4) and F (5) have the MSB set. This can slightly reduce area and combinational logic depth. The synchronous state register remains unchanged. These changes aim to reduce critical path delay, logic depth, and power by cleaner control flow and simpler output decoding.
