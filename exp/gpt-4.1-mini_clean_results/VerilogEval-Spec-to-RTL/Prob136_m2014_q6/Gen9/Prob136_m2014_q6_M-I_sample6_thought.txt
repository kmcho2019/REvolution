To optimize for better PPA, I will switch the state encoding from binary (3-bit) to one-hot encoding. One-hot encoding typically reduces the complexity of next-state and output logic by turning state decoding into simple AND/OR gate combinations, which can reduce combinational delay and improve timing (WNS), at the cost of increased flip-flop count (area). Given the current area is small (43 units), this tradeoff is acceptable to achieve lower WNS and better clock period. Moreover, with one-hot encoding, the output logic becomes a simple OR of the two active states (E and F), which can be faster. Also, I'll keep the combinational logic in a single always block and maintain synchronous reset. This should improve timing and potentially reduce power by simplifying next-state logic, even if area increases slightly.
