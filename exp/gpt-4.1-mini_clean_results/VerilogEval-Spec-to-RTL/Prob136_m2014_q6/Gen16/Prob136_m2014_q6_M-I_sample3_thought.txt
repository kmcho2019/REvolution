To optimize PPA, I will switch the state encoding from a 3-bit binary to one-hot encoding with 6 flip-flops. One-hot encoding often improves maximum clock frequency (performance) by simplifying next-state logic and reducing glitches, at the cost of slightly increased area, which is acceptable here given the modest state count.

Additionally, I will implement the output z using a simple OR of the corresponding state bits (E or F) to minimize output logic depth and switching.

I will keep synchronous reset and positive clock edge triggering. The next-state logic will be implemented with clear combinational conditions that map directly to each state flip-flop, ensuring fast and clean state transitions.

This should improve timing (reduce negative slack) and power due to fewer glitches, with moderate area increase but better performance overall.
