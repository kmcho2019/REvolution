To improve PPA, I will switch the state encoding from binary to one-hot. This typically reduces the complexity of next-state combinational logic and can improve timing at the expense of more flip-flops. Since the number of states is small (6), one-hot encoding overhead is manageable. Also, I will move the output logic to be registered on the clock edge to reduce glitches and improve timing. These changes usually help improve worst negative slack and power by reducing glitching and combinational logic depth, which may also slightly reduce area by simplifying combinational decoding.
