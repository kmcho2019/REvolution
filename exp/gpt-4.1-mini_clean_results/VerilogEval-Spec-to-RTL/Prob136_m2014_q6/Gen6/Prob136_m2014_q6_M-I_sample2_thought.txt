To improve PPA, convert the state encoding from binary to one-hot. One-hot encoding can simplify next-state logic by using fewer logic levels, potentially improving timing (better TNS/WNS) at the expense of slightly more area. Given the small FSM (6 states), area increase is minimal and the power impact is small. Also separate output logic into its own combinational block to improve timing clarity and reduce logic fanout on the next state logic. This will maintain the FSM functionality while potentially improving timing and power efficiency.
