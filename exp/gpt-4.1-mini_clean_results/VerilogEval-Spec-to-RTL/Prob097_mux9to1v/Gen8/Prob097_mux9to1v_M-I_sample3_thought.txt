To optimize for better PPA, I will replace the array-based indexing with a combinational always block using a case statement. This often helps synthesis tools generate more efficient multiplexing logic, potentially reducing area and power by avoiding large multiplexers inferred from indirect array indexing. Also, an explicit case provides clear control over default outputs (all ones) when sel is out of range. This approach retains the same functionality but can produce improved timing and area in many tools.
