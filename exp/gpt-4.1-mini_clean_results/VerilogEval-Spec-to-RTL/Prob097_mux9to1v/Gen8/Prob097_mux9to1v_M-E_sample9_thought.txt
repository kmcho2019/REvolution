Implement the 9-to-1 mux by cascading two smaller multiplexers: first a 3-to-1 mux stage on the inputs grouped as (a,b,c), (d,e,f), (g,h,i), producing three intermediate 16-bit signals; then a 3-to-1 mux stage selects one of these three outputs based on the higher bits of sel. For sel values 9 to 15, output 16'hFFFF. This hierarchical mux approach showcases modular design and can balance logic depth for potentially improved timing.
