Fuse the clarity and compactness of array-based input indexing with a streamlined and fully combinational multiplexer using a case statement to enable explicit, fine-grained control over each sel value from 0 to 8, and a default output of all ones (16'hFFFF) for sel values 9 to 15.

This combines the strengths of indexed inputs (clean, maintainable) and the explicit case-based mux logic (potentially better synthesis and power optimization by allowing synthesis tools to recognize and optimize each case distinctly, reducing switching on unselected inputs). 

Using a combinational always_comb block ensures clear intent and avoids any inferred registers while allowing concise case dispatch. This method balances readability, synthesis friendliness, and potential improvements in power and area by avoiding wide conditional comparisons and facilitating synthesis optimizations for the multiplexer tree.

The output is registered as a wire driven by the always_comb block (or equivalently a combinational always block) for proper synthesis and ease of simulation.

Overall, this approach fuses best practices: indexed inputs for compactness, explicit case statements for optimized mux implementation, and a default catch-all for unused sel values.
