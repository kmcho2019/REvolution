Implement the 9-to-1 multiplexer by concatenating all 9 inputs into a single wide bus and then using a shift operation combined with a mask to select the correct 16-bit segment. Specifically, place inputs a to i in consecutive 16-bit chunks within a 144-bit vector. Shift this vector right by sel*16 bits and assign the least significant 16 bits to output. For sel values beyond 8, output all ones by checking if sel is out of range. This approach leverages simple bit slicing and shifting instead of an explicit case or array indexing, offering a novel, arithmetic-based selection method.
