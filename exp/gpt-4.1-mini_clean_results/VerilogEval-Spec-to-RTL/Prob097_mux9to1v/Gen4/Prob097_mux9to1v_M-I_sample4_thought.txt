To optimize PPA, especially power and area, implement the multiplexer as a hierarchical tree of smaller 2-to-1 multiplexers. This reduces the multiplexer input fan-in and can decrease mux logic depth, improving timing and potentially lowering area and power. The 9-input mux can be built by first multiplexing pairs of inputs and then multiplexing their outputs progressively, and finally handling the case of unused sel values by OR'ing the higher bits and using that to select a constant 16'hFFFF output. This explicit structure is often easier for synthesis tools to optimize and map efficiently.
