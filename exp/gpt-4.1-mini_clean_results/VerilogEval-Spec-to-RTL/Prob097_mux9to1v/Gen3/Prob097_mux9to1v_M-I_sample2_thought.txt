To optimize for power and area, implement the mux with a generate loop that selects each bit using a 9-to-1 one-hot encoded multiplexer instead of a large 16-bit wide array indexed by sel. Use a decoded one-hot signal from sel for enabling each input vector bitwise. This approach enables finer synthesis optimizations, reduces switching on the select lines, and can reduce glitching, improving power. Also, for sel values 9 to 15, force output bits to '1' by OR-ing with a signal derived from sel comparison, avoiding a full mux for these cases and potentially improving area and power.
