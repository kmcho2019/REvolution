Implement the 9-to-1 16-bit wide multiplexer using a hierarchical mux approach: first create three 3-to-1 mux modules that each select among three inputs based on the two least significant bits of sel, then use a final 3-to-1 mux to select among their outputs based on the next bit of sel. For sel values 9-15, detect invalid selection via a comparison and output all ones. This approach modularizes the design and can promote reuse and clarity.
