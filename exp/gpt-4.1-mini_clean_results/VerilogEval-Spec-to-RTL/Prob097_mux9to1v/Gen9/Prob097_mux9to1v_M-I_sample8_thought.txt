To improve power, area, and possibly timing of the current 9-to-1 16-bit mux implementation, simplify the design by replacing the hierarchical mux tree with a single combinational case statement. This approach:

- Minimizes the number of intermediate signals and logic levels, reducing area and power due to less toggling on intermediate wires.
- Synthesizes efficiently on FPGAs and ASICs by allowing direct mapping to multiplexers or LUTs.
- Clearly handles the out-of-range sel cases by assigning 16'hFFFF.
- Maintains clean and readable code without the complexity of hierarchical muxing.
- Keeps the output fully combinational and glitch-free.

This streamlined design should reduce LUT count and switching activity, improving PPA metrics compared to the prior multi-level mux tree.
