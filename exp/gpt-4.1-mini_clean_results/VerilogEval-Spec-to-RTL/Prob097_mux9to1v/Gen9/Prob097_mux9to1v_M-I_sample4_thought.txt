To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will implement the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer using a single flat combinational case statement instead of a hierarchical tree of muxes. This eliminates the multiple levels of muxing, reducing combinational delay (improving performance). Also, the case statement ensures only the selected input is chosen, potentially reducing switching activity (power). Area can also be improved by avoiding multiple intermediate wires. The default case covers sel = 9 to 15 by outputting all ones (0xFFFF). This simpler approach typically synthesizes into efficient logic, especially since inputs are 16 bits wide and only one input is selected at a time.
