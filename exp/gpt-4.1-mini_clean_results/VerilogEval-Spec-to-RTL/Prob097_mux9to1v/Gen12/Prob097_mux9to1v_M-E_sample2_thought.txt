Implement the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer using a single always_comb block with a case statement on sel. This straightforward behavioral approach directly assigns the output based on the value of sel, covering all valid inputs (0 to 8) and defaults to all ones for invalid sel values (9 to 15). This minimizes combinational logic depth by avoiding multiple AND-OR gates per bit and leverages synthesis tools' ability to efficiently map case statements to multiplexers, reducing area and possibly improving timing and power compared to one-hot or bitwise logic.
