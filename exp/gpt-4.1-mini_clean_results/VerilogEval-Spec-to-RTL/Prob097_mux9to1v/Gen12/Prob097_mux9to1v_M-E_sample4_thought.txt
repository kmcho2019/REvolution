Implement the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer using a hierarchical two-level muxing approach. First, group the 9 inputs into three groups of three inputs each, each group multiplexed by a 2-bit select signal (lower bits of sel). Then use a second-level 3-to-1 mux controlled by the higher bits of sel to select among the three groups. For sel values beyond 8, output 16'hFFFF. This approach uses smaller 3-input mux blocks and a 3-input mux at the top, demonstrating hierarchical decomposition.
