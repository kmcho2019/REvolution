Implement the 9-to-1 mux by breaking it down into a hierarchical structure of smaller 3-to-1 multiplexers. First, group the nine inputs into three groups of three inputs each, select within each group using the lower two bits of sel, then use the higher bits of sel to select among the three group outputs. For sel values 9-15, explicitly assign 16'hFFFF. This approach demonstrates modular composition of small multiplexers and can be extended easily.
