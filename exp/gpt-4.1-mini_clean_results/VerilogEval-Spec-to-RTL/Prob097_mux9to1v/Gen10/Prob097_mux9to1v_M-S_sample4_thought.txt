Implement the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer using a straightforward combinational always block with a case statement on sel. This eliminates the hierarchical mux structure and intermediate wires, reducing code complexity, logic depth, and potential power and area overhead. For sel values 0-8, output the corresponding input; for sel 9-15, output all ones (16'hFFFF). This approach is clean, synthesizable, and likely to map efficiently to hardware LUTs.
