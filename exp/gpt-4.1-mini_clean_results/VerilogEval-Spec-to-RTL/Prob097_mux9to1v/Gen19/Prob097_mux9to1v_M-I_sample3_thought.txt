To further optimize the 9-to-1 multiplexer for better performance, area, and power, I will replace the conditional operator with a binary-encoded case statement inside a combinational always block. This often allows synthesis tools to better infer efficient mux trees and can reduce logic depth, improving timing.

Additionally, I will use a localparam array for inputs to ensure no unintended latches or glitches and explicitly handle all sel values (0-8 selecting inputs, 9-15 all ones).

The case statement approach also ensures explicitness, which can lead to better area and power optimizations by the synthesis tool by minimizing switching on unused inputs.

This change maintains the design’s simplicity but can improve synthesis friendliness and clarity for the tool to generate optimal mux logic.
