To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the combinational always block and reg output with a continuous assignment using a ternary operator on a packed input array. This approach removes the inferred register and may reduce mux tree overhead and switching activity by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the mux directly as a combinational function. 

I will pack inputs into an array to enable indexed access with sel but implement the mux purely with a continuous assignment. For sel values 0-8, select corresponding input; otherwise output all ones. This is a more concise and synthesis-friendly style, potentially improving area and power slightly by reducing mux logic and eliminating reg usage.

This also simplifies the design for tools without changing functional behavior or timing. Overall, this style should maintain zero delay (tns=wns=0) while marginally reducing power and area compared to the previous case statement + always block implementation.
