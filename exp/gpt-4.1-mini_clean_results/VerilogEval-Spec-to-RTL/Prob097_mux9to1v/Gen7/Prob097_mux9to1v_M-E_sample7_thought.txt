Implement the 9-to-1, 16-bit wide multiplexer using a hierarchical approach: first use a 3-to-1 multiplexer stage to reduce the nine inputs into three intermediate 16-bit values, then a final 3-to-1 multiplexer stage to select the final output based on the upper bits of sel. This two-level mux reduces the complexity at each stage and avoids indexing arrays directly by sel. For invalid sel values (9 to 15), an explicit condition sets output to all ones. The selection signals are derived by splitting sel into two parts: lower 2 bits to select among groups, and upper bits for overall selection. This approach reduces the mux tree complexity, can improve synthesis optimization opportunities, and shows a clear hierarchical structure for easier debugging and understanding.
