I will implement the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer using a hierarchical two-level multiplexing approach to showcase a structurally different method. First, I will divide the nine inputs into three groups of three inputs each. For each group, a 3-to-1 16-bit mux will be instantiated. Then, a final 3-to-1 mux will select among the three intermediate outputs from the first stage.

The 4-bit selector will be split into two parts: the lower two bits will select within each group (0-2), and the upper bits will select the group. For sel values from 9 to 15, the output is forced to all ones.

This approach uses smaller 3-to-1 mux blocks hierarchically, which might better map to basic multiplexer cells in some technologies and allows modular reuse of smaller muxes. It also provides an alternative structural style compared to a flat 9-to-1 mux or a giant case statement.

Finally, the top module will instantiate two levels of mux modules accordingly and manage the output conditions properly.
