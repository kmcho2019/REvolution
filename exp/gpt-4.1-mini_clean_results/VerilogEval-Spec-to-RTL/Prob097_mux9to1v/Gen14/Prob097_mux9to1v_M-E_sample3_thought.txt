Implement the 9-to-1, 16-bit-wide multiplexer using a hierarchical approach by first grouping inputs into three groups of three inputs each. Each group outputs a 16-bit intermediate signal selected by a 2-bit part of the sel signal. Then use the remaining most significant bit of sel to select among the three intermediate outputs or output all ones if sel is outside 0 to 8. This approach decomposes the large mux into smaller 3-to-1 mux blocks, which may improve synthesis optimization, timing, and area by enabling more localized logic. The hierarchy is expressed via nested conditional operators, ensuring combinational logic without always blocks. Sel values from 9 to 15 directly produce 16'hFFFF, maintaining the original specification.
