To optimize power, area, and performance (PPA), I will replace the conditional operator with a case statement inside a combinational always block. This approach allows synthesis tools to better optimize the multiplexer tree structure, potentially reducing the depth of multiplexers, thereby improving timing and area.

Also, rather than packing the inputs into an array, I will directly use a case statement on 'sel' to assign 'out'. This can avoid the creation of an internal array structure, which might reduce area slightly.

For sel values 9 to 15, I will explicitly assign 16'hFFFF as required.

This explicit case-based mux implementation can help synthesis tools generate more optimized mux trees by clearly defining each input mapping, potentially lowering switching activity on unselected inputs and thus power.

The design remains purely combinational, maintaining minimal logic depth and ensuring zero latency. This approach balances clarity, synthesis friendliness, and PPA improvements.
