Refactor the 9-to-1 16-bit multiplexer by replacing the conditional (ternary) operator with a combinational always block and a case statement. This change explicitly uses a case-based selection for clarity and ease of modification, while maintaining a purely combinational design by declaring the output as reg and assigning inside always @(*) block. The default case sets the output to all ones for select values 9 to 15. This approach is functionally equivalent, easy to understand, and commonly used in RTL coding styles for multiplexers.
