Implement the 9-to-1 multiplexer by using a two-level hierarchical mux approach. First, group inputs into three sets of three inputs each. Use a 3-to-1 mux for each bit across each group (a,b,c), (d,e,f), (g,h,i). Then, use a final 3-to-1 mux to select between the three grouped outputs.
