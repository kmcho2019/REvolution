Implement the 9-to-1 multiplexer by first combining the nine 16-bit inputs into one large concatenated vector and then extract the selected 16-bit segment based on sel using bit slicing in a combinational always block. For invalid sel values (9-15), assign 16'hFFFF. This method leverages arithmetic to calculate the slice position, creating a compact and easy-to-understand mux implementation without arrays or multiple case entries.
