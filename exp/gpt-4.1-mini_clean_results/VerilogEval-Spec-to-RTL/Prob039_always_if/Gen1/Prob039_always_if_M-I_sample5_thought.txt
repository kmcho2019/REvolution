The original design is already minimal and efficient for such a simple combinational mux. However, we can slightly optimize by computing the selection signal once and reusing it for both outputs, reducing redundant logic and potentially saving area and power. Instead of evaluating (sel_b1 & sel_b2) twice separately, introduce a wire sel_b to represent this condition, then use it in both assignments. This maintains the required dual implementation style (assign and always) while sharing common logic.
