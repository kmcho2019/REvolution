The original design is already minimal and optimal in logic complexity; however, we can slightly optimize power and area by eliminating redundant logic duplication for the select condition. Since both outputs depend on the same select signal (sel_b1 & sel_b2), compute this once in a wire and reuse it. This avoids repeated AND operations in both the assign and always block, potentially reducing toggle activity and logic usage. This small refactor can marginally improve power and area while maintaining clarity and performance. No functionality changes are made.
