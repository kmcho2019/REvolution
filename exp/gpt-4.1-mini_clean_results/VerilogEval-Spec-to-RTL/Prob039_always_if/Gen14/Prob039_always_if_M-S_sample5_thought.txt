To simplify the design, I will remove the separate Mux2to1 module and directly implement the two muxes inside TopModule. The combined select signal (sel_b1 & sel_b2) will be computed once. Then, out_assign will be assigned using a continuous assignment with a ternary operator, and out_always will be assigned in a single procedural always block using if-else. This reduces hierarchy, lines of code, and potential synthesis overhead while keeping functionality identical.
