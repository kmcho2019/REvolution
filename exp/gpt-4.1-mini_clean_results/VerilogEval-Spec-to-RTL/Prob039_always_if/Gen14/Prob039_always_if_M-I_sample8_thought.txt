To optimize the original design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will remove the separate Mux2to1 module instantiation for the continuous assignment output and implement that mux directly inside TopModule with a single assign statement. This avoids unnecessary module hierarchy and extra nets, thus reducing area and power slightly. The always block implementation remains unchanged because it's already minimal and clear. This direct approach keeps the design simple, efficient, and easy to understand, achieving the same functionality with fewer resources.
