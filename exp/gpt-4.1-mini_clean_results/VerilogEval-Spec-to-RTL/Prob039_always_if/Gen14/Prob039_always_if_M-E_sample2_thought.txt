Instead of using a direct mux with an assign or always block separately for each output, this design will compute the select signal (sel) once using an intermediate wire generated by combining sel_b1 and sel_b2 through an AND gate. Then, for out_assign, a simple assign statement will mux between a and b based on sel. For out_always, the design will use a procedural case statement inside an always @(*) block to implement the mux behavior based on the sel signal, making the approach structurally different and demonstrating alternative combinational logic coding styles. The output out_always is declared as reg to accommodate procedural assignment. This separation emphasizes pre-computation of control signals and use of a case statement, which can be useful for scaling or clarity in more complex mux logic.
