To optimize the existing solution for better power and area efficiency, I will remove the unnecessary MuxCell module and dummy outputs. The mux logic is simple enough to be implemented directly within TopModule. For the assign-based output, I will use a direct continuous assignment expression. For the procedural always-based output, I will implement the 2-to-1 mux logic inside an always block without any extra module instantiation.

This approach eliminates redundant modules and dummy wires, reducing area and switching power while maintaining the required functionality and readability.
