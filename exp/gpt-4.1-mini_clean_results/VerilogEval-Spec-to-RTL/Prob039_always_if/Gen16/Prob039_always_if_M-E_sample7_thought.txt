I will implement TopModule without any submodules. The module will internally compute the combined select signal (sel_b1 & sel_b2). For the assign-based output, I will use a single continuous assignment with the ternary operator directly on the inputs and combined select. For the procedural output, I will use an always_comb block (SystemVerilog style) or always @(*) block (Verilog-2001 compatible) that uses an if statement to assign out_always, thus fulfilling the requirement to implement the mux twice differently without extra module instantiations or redundant logic. This reduces area, power, and complexity while maintaining the clear separation between assign-based and procedural mux implementations.
