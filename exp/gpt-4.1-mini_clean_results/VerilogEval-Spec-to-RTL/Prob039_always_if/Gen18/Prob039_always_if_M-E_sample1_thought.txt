Instead of using a separate Mux2to1 module, I will implement the mux functionality directly inside TopModule but split the logic paths differently: for the continuous assignment output, I will generate the output by using a bitwise AND and OR combination that achieves the mux functionality without a ternary operator. For the procedural output, I will use a case statement with the combined select bits encoded as a 2-bit signal to demonstrate another structural style of combinational logic inside the always block. This showcases the flexibility of Verilog in expressing mux behavior in distinct ways without hierarchy and with varied control structures.
