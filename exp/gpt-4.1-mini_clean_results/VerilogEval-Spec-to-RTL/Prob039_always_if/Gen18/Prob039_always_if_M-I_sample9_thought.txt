The original design is correct and clear but uses a separate Mux2to1 module for the assign-based output, which adds a small overhead in hierarchy and area. To improve PPA, I will flatten the design by removing the module instance and implementing both mux outputs directly inside TopModule using simple continuous assignment and procedural always block respectively. This reduces the area and power by minimizing logic levels and hierarchy while preserving clarity. The select signals will still be combined into one wire for readability and reusability. This approach keeps the code succinct and efficient for synthesis tools, achieving better area and power with identical functional behavior and timing.
