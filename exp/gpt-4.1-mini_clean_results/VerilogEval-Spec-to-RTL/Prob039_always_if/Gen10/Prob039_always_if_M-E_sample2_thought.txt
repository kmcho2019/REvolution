Instead of coding two separate versions (one with assign and one inside an always block), use a single always block to implement the mux logic and then drive both outputs from the same internal registered signal. The continuous output will be driven by a wire connected to the internal signal, while the procedural output is driven by the registered signal. This avoids duplicating the mux logic and provides the same functionality. The internal signal is updated combinationally to ensure both outputs reflect the same logic, demonstrating reuse of logic and architectural neatness.
