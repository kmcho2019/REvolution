Fuse the clarity and modularity of the function abstraction (Example 2) with the area and power efficiency of sharing intermediate logic (Example 1). Implement the mux selection as a localparam function but compute the selection condition `(sel_b1 & sel_b2)` once as a wire `use_b`. Use this wire as the select signal, feeding it into the mux function to get a single shared combinational signal, then drive both outputs from it—one by assign and one by a procedural always block. This avoids duplicating mux logic, keeps the function abstraction for readability, and achieves optimal area and power through logic sharing.
