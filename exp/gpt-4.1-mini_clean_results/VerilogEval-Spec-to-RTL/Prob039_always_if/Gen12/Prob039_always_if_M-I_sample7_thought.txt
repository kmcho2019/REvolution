To optimize for better power and area while preserving functionality, avoid duplicating logic by directly using the same select expression in both assign and always blocks without introducing an intermediate mux_out wire. This reduces minor overhead in logic and wiring. Use the direct conditional expression (sel_b1 & sel_b2) ? b : a in both places. The always block uses blocking assignments since it's combinational and does not involve clocked logic, simplifying the code. This removes the extra wire and slightly reduces the logic, marginally improving area and power, while keeping performance identical.
