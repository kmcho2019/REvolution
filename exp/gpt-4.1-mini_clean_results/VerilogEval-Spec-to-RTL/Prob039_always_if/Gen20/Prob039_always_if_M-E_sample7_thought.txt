To implement the required 2-to-1 mux functionality in a novel way, I will use a single always_comb block inside TopModule to calculate both outputs simultaneously but distinctly. The combined select (sel_b1 & sel_b2) will be evaluated once and stored in an internal signal. Then, out_assign will be driven via a continuous assign statement with a bitwise expression derived from the internal signal and inputs, implementing the mux functionally without instantiating submodules. Meanwhile, out_always will be assigned in an always_comb block using an explicit if-else statement based on the internal select signal. This approach merges procedural and continuous assignment styles while sharing computation internally to reduce duplicated logic and improve clarity. It demonstrates a neat hybrid approach that leverages both RTL styles elegantly.
