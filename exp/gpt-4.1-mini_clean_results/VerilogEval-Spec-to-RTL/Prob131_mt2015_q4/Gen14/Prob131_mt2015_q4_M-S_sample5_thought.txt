Since all four submodules receive the same inputs x and y and produce the same outputs (A computes (x^y)&x and B computes ~(x^y)), instantiating two identical A and two identical B modules is redundant. We can instantiate a single A and a single B module, and use their outputs twice as needed for the OR and AND gates. Then, combine these results with the XOR gate as before. This reduces the module count and wiring complexity, while maintaining the exact same functional behavior.
