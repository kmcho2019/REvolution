To optimize for better PPA while preserving the exact functionality, I will remove redundant instantiations of modules A and B since they are used twice with the same inputs in the top-level module. Instead, instantiate one A and one B module and reuse their outputs for both the OR and AND gates. This reduces area and power by avoiding duplicated logic and lowers switching activity.

The logic remains:
- OR gate combines the single A and B outputs (used as the "first pair" output)
- AND gate combines the same A and B outputs (used as the "second pair" output)
- XOR of the OR and AND outputs produces final z

This design meets the functional requirements, decreases module count from four to two, and cuts gate duplication while keeping clear combinational structure.

No procedural blocks are introduced, keeping synthesis and timing clean. This approach should improve area and power without impacting delay negatively.
