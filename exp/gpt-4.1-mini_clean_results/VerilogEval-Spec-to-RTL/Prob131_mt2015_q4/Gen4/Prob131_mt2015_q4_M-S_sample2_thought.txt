Since all four submodules A and B receive the same inputs (x and y), we can instantiate each module only once and reuse their outputs for both the OR and AND gates. This eliminates duplicated logic, reduces area and power, and simplifies the design without changing functionality.

Specifically:
- Instantiate one A module and one B module.
- Use the outputs from A and B twice in the OR and AND gates.
- Compute z = (A_out | B_out) ^ (A_out & B_out).

This also corresponds to the XOR of OR and AND of the same two signals, which simplifies to z = A_out ^ B_out, but we keep the structure as per the problem for clarity.
