To optimize the given solution for better power and area while maintaining performance, we eliminate redundant module instantiations by sharing outputs for identical inputs. Since all four submodules receive the same inputs (x,y), their outputs are deterministic and can be computed once per module type and reused. We implement modules A and B with continuous assign statements rather than always blocks to reduce logic complexity and improve synthesis efficiency. This reduces unnecessary registers and switching. The top-level module then uses these shared outputs to compute the OR, AND, and XOR as described. This reduces gate count, switching activity, and area while preserving correct functionality and timing.
