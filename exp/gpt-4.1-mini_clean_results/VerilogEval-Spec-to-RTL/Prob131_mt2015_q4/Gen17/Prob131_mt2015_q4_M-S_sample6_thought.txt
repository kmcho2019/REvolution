The top-level logic involves only combinational gates operating on outputs from two identical functions of the same inputs. Since all four submodules A and B receive identical inputs and compute the same functions, we can combine the logic of A and B directly inside the top module without separately instantiating modules. This simplifies the design by removing submodule instantiations and replacing them with direct assign statements for A and B outputs, then applying OR, AND, and XOR gates accordingly. This preserves the original behavior with minimal code and lower complexity.
