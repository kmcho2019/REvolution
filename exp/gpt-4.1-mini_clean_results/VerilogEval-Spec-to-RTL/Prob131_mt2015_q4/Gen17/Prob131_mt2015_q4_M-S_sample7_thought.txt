Since both pairs of A and B modules have identical inputs and identical logic, their outputs will be the same. Thus, we can instantiate just one A and one B module. Then the OR and AND gates will operate on the same two outputs, simplifying to OR(a,b) and AND(a,b). Finally, XOR of these two values is equivalent to XOR(OR(a,b), AND(a,b)). We can implement this directly without duplicating module instances. This simplification reduces area and power while preserving the exact functionality.
