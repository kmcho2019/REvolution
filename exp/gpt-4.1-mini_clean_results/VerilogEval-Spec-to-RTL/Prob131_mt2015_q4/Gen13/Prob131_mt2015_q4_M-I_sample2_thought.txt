The original solution duplicates the logic of modules A and B twice each with identical inputs, causing redundant computations, area overhead, and increased switching power. To optimize for power and area without changing functionality, we can instantiate one A and one B module, and share their outputs. Then, to create the two required outputs for the OR and AND gates, use the shared outputs directly, reusing signals instead of duplicating logic. This eliminates redundant module instantiations and reduces area and power while maintaining the exact logic. The final XOR combines the OR and AND as before. This maintains timing since fewer gates reduce logic depth and fanout, improving overall PPA.
