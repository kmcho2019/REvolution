Since the inputs to all four submodules are identical, and modules A and B produce the same outputs for the same inputs, we can instantiate just one A and one B module each and reuse their outputs for both pairs. Then, the first pair’s output is OR of A and B, the second pair’s output is AND of the same A and B outputs, and finally XOR these two results to produce z. This preserves the original logic and functionality while significantly reducing redundancy and complexity.
