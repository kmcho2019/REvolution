To optimize the design for better power and area while preserving functionality, we can share the outputs of the two identical A and two identical B instances since both pairs have identical inputs and produce identical outputs. Instead of instantiating A and B twice each, instantiate them once each, and reuse their outputs for both the OR and AND combinations. This reduces redundancy, lowering area and dynamic power due to fewer gates switching. The logic structure remains the same: OR and AND outputs combined with XOR for final output.
