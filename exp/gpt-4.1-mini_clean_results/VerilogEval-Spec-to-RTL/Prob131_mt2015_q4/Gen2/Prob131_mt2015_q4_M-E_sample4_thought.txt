Instead of modeling module B with a complex timed waveform inside the module itself, implement B as a pure combinational lookup table based on the inputs x and y and the current simulation time divided into fixed intervals. The top-level module will instantiate two A modules and two B modules, with all their inputs connected to x and y. Their outputs will be combined using OR, AND, and XOR gates as specified. To supply the simulation time index to B modules, create a free-running counter (time_index) in the testbench that steps every 5 ns, and connect this as an additional input to B. This approach cleanly separates the combinational logic and timing control, making the design modular and easy to test.
