Since both pairs of A and B modules receive the same inputs and are combined by OR and AND gates before XOR, and the two pairs are identical, we can instantiate just one A and one B module and directly compute the final output z by expressing the logic in a simplified Boolean expression. Therefore, the top-level output z is simply the XOR of A's and B's outputs, allowing us to remove redundant gates and reduce the design to one A and one B module and a single XOR gate.
