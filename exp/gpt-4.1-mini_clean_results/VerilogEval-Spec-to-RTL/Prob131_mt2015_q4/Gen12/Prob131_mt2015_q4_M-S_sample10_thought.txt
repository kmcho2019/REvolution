Since all four submodules (two A and two B) have identical inputs and identical behavior, their outputs will be the same. Therefore, we can instantiate just one A and one B module each and reuse their outputs to build the OR and AND gates as needed. This eliminates redundant module instantiations while preserving the same logical functionality. The top-level logic is thus simplified, with only one A and one B instance, and the rest done with simple combinational gates.
