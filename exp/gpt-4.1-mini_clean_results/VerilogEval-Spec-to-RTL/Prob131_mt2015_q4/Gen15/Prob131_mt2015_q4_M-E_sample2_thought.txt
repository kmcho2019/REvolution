Instead of precomputing and sharing xor_xy, this design instantiates all four submodules (two A and two B) exactly as specified, each receiving the original inputs x and y.

To optimize and simplify wiring, we create two intermediate signals:

- OR_out: the OR of the outputs from the first A and first B submodules,
- AND_out: the AND of the outputs from the second A and second B submodules.

The final output z is the XOR of OR_out and AND_out.

This approach fully respects the problem's instantiation and connection requirements without introducing additional shared intermediate signals beyond what is necessary for combining submodule outputs.

It is straightforward and clean, with no changes to A and B internals, making it easy to verify and debug. This method emphasizes modularity and clarity by separately instantiating each submodule, preserving the original boolean logic function of each submodule.
