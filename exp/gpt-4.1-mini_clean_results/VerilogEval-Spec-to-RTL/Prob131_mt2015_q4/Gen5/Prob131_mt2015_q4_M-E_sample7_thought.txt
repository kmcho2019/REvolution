Instead of instantiating two separate copies of modules A and B with identical inputs, we can optimize by instantiating a single module A and a single module B. We then replicate their outputs internally to feed into the OR and AND gates as specified, reducing redundant logic and improving area and power efficiency.

Additionally, we encapsulate the logic for the OR-AND-XOR combination inside a dedicated intermediate combinational module, improving modularity and testability.

For modules A and B, implement them with always_comb blocks using behavioral operators, which can facilitate easier modification later and clear semantics.

This structure achieves the same functional behavior while reducing module instantiations from four to two and logically organizing the top-level operation inside a combinational "CombBlock" submodule.
