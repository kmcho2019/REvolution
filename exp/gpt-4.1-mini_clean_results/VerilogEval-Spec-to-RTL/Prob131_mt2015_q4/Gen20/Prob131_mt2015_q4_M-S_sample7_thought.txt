Since both pairs of A and B modules have identical inputs, their outputs are the same. The first pair's output is (A_out | B_out) and the second pair's is (A_out & B_out). The final output is XOR of these two results, which simplifies to A_out ^ B_out by Boolean algebra:

(A | B) ^ (A & B) = A ^ B

Therefore, the entire top-level logic can be simplified to just XOR of A_out and B_out, which reduces gate count and complexity without changing functionality.
