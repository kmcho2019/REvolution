To optimize the design for better area and power without affecting performance, I will eliminate the redundant duplicate submodules that compute identical outputs with identical inputs. Instead of instantiating two identical A modules and two identical B modules, I will instantiate one A and one B module each, then use their outputs multiple times in the logic. This reduces logic duplication and switching activity, thereby decreasing area and power.

I will also remove the generate loops since only one instance of each submodule is needed, simplifying the design. The intermediate logic (OR, AND, XOR) will use the single outputs appropriately to recreate the behavior of the original design. This preserves the functional correctness but reduces gate count and toggling.

The shared intermediate signals xor_xy and inv_xor_xy remain computed once and shared, maintaining the design's efficiency.

This approach maintains the exact same combinational functionality, but with fewer instantiated submodules and less duplicated logic, improving power and area metrics with negligible impact on timing.
