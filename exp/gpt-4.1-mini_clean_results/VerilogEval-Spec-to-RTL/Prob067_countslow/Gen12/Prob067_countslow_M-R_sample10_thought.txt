Refactor the decade counter to use a single 4-bit register `q` updated synchronously on the positive clock edge. On each clock cycle, if `slowena` is high, increment `q` by one with wrap-around (reset to 0 when it reaches 9). Use synchronous active-high reset to clear the counter to 0. This approach simplifies the design by removing explicit state enumeration and combinational next state logic, reducing area, power, and potentially improving timing. The output is directly driven by the register `q`.
