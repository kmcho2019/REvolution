To optimize for performance and power, replace the addition and comparison with a simple terminal count check: if q is 9 and slowena is high, reset to 0; otherwise, increment q only when slowena is high. This reduces combinational logic delay by avoiding addition and comparison in one expression. For power, use clock enable style logic by gating the counter update only when slowena is high or reset is asserted. This reduces switching activity in the flip-flops. This approach keeps the design simple and efficient for area as well.
