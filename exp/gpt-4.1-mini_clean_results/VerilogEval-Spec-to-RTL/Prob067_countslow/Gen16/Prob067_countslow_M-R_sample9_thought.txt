Refactor the decade counter to use a single 4-bit register `q` that holds the count and directly update it on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset. Instead of explicitly enumerating states, use simple arithmetic to increment `q` when `slowena` is high, resetting to 0 when it reaches 9. This removes the separate next_state combinational logic and redundant output register, simplifying the design. This approach improves timing and area by reducing combinational complexity and registers, while keeping the same functional behavior.
