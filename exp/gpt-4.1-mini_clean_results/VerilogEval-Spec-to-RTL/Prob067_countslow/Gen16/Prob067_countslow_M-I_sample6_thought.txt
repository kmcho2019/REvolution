Replace the explicit state machine and separate next_state logic with a simple synchronous 4-bit binary counter that increments only when slowena is high. On each positive clock edge, if reset is asserted, the counter resets to zero; else if slowena is high, increment the counter with a wrap-around back to zero after 9. This approach simplifies the design, reducing combinational logic delay, flip-flop count, and power by eliminating the extra state encoding and decoding. The output q is the same register holding the count, avoiding duplicated storage and simplifying routing.
