Implement the decade counter using a binary-coded decimal (BCD) counter with a carry ripple approach. Instead of directly comparing the count to 9, increment the counter on each slowena pulse, but when the count reaches 10 (4'b1010), reset it to zero. Use an intermediate signal for the next state calculation to clearly separate combinational logic from sequential. The reset is synchronous and active high, and the counter only increments when slowena is high. This approach cleanly separates combinational next-state logic from the sequential register update, improving readability and potentially aiding synthesis and timing analysis.
