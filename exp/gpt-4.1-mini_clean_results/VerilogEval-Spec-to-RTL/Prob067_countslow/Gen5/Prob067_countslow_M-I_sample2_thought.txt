To improve PPA, I will optimize the design by removing the combinational always block and incorporating the enable logic directly into the sequential block. This reduces the combinational logic delay and critical path by eliminating the extra comparator and mux. Instead, the counter will increment only if slowena is high and q is less than 9, otherwise reset to zero or hold value accordingly. This simplifies logic, reduces area, and should improve timing and power by minimizing toggling and logic depth. The synchronous reset remains unchanged for robustness.
