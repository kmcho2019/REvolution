To improve PPA (performance, power, area) from the given solution, I will reduce switching activity and critical path delay by simplifying the next-state logic. Instead of a 4-bit comparator and addition for the next state, I will implement the counter as a ring-like sequence with direct state transitions from 0 to 9 and back to 0 using a simple equality check on 4'b1001 (decimal 9). This reduces the logic for the comparator and adder.

Additionally, to further reduce power, I will gate the register update solely with slowena, avoiding unnecessary toggles. This approach effectively keeps the existing function but simplifies the combinational logic, potentially lowering delay and area slightly. This simpler logic also makes it easier for synthesis tools to optimize the design for timing and area.
