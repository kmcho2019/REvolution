To improve PPA, I will add clock gating to disable clock toggling of the flip-flops when the counter is not enabled (slowena low) and reset inactive, reducing dynamic power consumption. This will slightly improve power while maintaining performance since gating the clock reduces unnecessary switching inside the flip-flops. The combinational logic remains minimal, so performance impact will be negligible or slightly improved. Area increase due to a clock gating cell is minimal and justified by power savings.

Specifically, I will generate an internal gated clock using an enable signal that is high only when slowena is high and reset is low. This gated clock will be used for the counter register. Synchronous reset remains on the flip-flop inputs. This approach avoids toggling flip-flops unnecessarily, reducing power while preserving timing and functionality.
