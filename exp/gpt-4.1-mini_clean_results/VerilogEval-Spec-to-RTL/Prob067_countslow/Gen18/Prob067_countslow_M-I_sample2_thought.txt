To further improve PPA, separate the synchronous reset path from the counting enable to avoid unnecessary toggling caused by combining reset with slowena. Only gate the increment operation with slowena while always allowing reset to asynchronously set the counter to zero on the clock edge. This reduces switching activity during reset assertion, which typically is short but can cause unnecessary toggling when ORed with slowena. Additionally, keep the counting logic simple and maintain synchronous reset for predictable timing. This approach slightly lowers power by reducing unnecessary toggling and preserves area and timing simplicity.
