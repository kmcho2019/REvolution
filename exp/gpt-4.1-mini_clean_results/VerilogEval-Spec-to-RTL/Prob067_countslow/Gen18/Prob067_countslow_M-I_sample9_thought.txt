To optimize the existing decade counter design for better power, we can avoid unnecessary toggling of the q register when slowena=0 by updating q only on cycles when slowena is asserted or on reset. This reduces switching activity on q, potentially lowering power consumption. The wrap-around logic is unchanged for correctness and simplicity. Performance impact is minimal since the combinational path is unchanged. Area remains minimal. Thus, we introduce a conditional update inside the always block so q is only assigned a new value when necessary.
