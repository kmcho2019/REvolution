To optimize the decade counter for better PPA, introduce clock gating to prevent unnecessary toggling of the flip-flops when slowena is low and reset is inactive. This reduces dynamic power by disabling the clock to the counter when it isn't incrementing. The combinational next state logic remains unchanged, but the sequential logic only toggles q on the gated clock derived from clk AND (slowena OR reset). Since reset is synchronous, we keep the clock gating simple: clock enables the flip-flops only when counting or resetting. This can improve power with minimal impact on timing or area. Also, keep the simple comparator logic for clarity and maintainability.
