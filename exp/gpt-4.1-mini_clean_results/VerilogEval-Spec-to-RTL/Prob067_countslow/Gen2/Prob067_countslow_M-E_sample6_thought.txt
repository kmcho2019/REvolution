Implement the decade counter using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock, where the counter is updated only when slowena is high. On reset, the counter synchronously resets to zero. The counter increments by 1 when slowena is asserted and wraps from 9 back to 0 using modular arithmetic. This approach avoids separate combinational next-state logic by embedding all behavior into the synchronous block, resulting in a compact and easy-to-understand design.
