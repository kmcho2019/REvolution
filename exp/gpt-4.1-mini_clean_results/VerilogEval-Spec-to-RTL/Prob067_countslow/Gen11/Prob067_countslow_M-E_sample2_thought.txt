Implement the decade counter using a 4-bit register and add a separate enable signal inside the always block. Instead of combining reset and slowena in a single enable signal, handle reset synchronously first. Then, only increment the counter when slowena is asserted. When slowena is low, the counter holds its current value. This avoids unnecessary toggling during reset, as the counter resets immediately and increments only when enabled by slowena. The counter wraps from 9 back to 0 to maintain the decade counting sequence.
