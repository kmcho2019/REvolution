Implement the decade counter as a single 4-bit register `q` that increments by 1 on each clock cycle when `slowena` is high. When `q` reaches 9, the next increment wraps it back to 0. Synchronous active-high reset sets `q` to 0. This removes the explicit state machine and case statement, using simple arithmetic and conditional assignment for next count, reducing combinational logic and registers, and thus improving timing, power, and area.
