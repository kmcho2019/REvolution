To optimize the decade counter for better PPA (performance, power, and area), I will implement clock gating using the slowena signal to reduce unnecessary toggling when the counter should not increment. This disables the clock input to the flip-flops when slowena is low, lowering dynamic power consumption. Additionally, I'll rewrite the increment logic using arithmetic addition and modular arithmetic (`q + 1` mod 10) which can be efficiently synthesized by modern tools. Clock gating improves power without impacting functionality or frequency, and the increment logic remains simple and compact.
