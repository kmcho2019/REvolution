To improve PPA while maintaining functionality, I will implement clock gating using slowena to reduce power by disabling the clock to the counter flip-flops when not counting. This saves dynamic power by preventing unnecessary toggling inside the counter. The decade counting and synchronous reset logic remain unchanged. Clock gating will be implemented via an enable signal controlling the flip-flops clock enables (using a gated clock or enable signal within the sequential block). Since the original design uses synchronous logic on posedge clk, we can introduce an enable (slowena) inside the always block to reduce unnecessary state updates. This approach avoids complex gated clock synthesis issues and is typically supported by synthesis tools for clock gating inference, improving power without affecting area or performance notably.
