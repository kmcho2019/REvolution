Implement the decade counter using a simple 4-bit register 'q' that increments by 1 modulo 10 whenever 'slowena' is high on the positive edge of 'clk'. On synchronous reset, set 'q' to zero. This approach uses straightforward arithmetic to handle the wrap-around (if q == 9, reset to 0). It avoids an explicit state machine, reducing combinational complexity and potentially improving timing, power, and area.
