This aligns well with synthesis tool optimizations for clock enables, potentially improving power efficiency by reducing unnecessary toggling inside the register. This approach combines the simplicity and efficiency of the binary counter with a clear enable-driven update, making it suitable for high-performance and low-power implementations.
