To improve PPA, especially power, add an explicit clock enable for the counter so that the register only updates when slowena is high or during reset. This prevents toggling of the q register when slowena is low, thus reducing dynamic power due to unnecessary switching. The synchronous reset remains unchanged. The counter increments by 1 and rolls over to 0 at 9 as before, but now the register load is gated by (reset || slowena). This explicit clock enable simplifies logic and can help synthesis tools optimize flip-flop enable signals, improving power and possibly timing slightly without increasing area.
