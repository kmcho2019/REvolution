Refactor the design to use a single 4-bit register `q` to hold the counter value and update it synchronously on the clock's positive edge. When `reset` is asserted synchronously, reset `q` to 0. When `slowena` is high, increment `q` with wrap-around from 9 back to 0. This approach removes the explicit state machine and separate combinational logic for next state, simplifying logic and potentially improving timing and area. The output `q` directly reflects the counter value stored in the register.
