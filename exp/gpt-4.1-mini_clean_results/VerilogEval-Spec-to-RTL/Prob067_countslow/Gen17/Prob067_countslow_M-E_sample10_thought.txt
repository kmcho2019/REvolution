Implement the decade counter as a single 4-bit register `q` that increments by 1 only when `slowena` is high. When `q` reaches 9, the next increment wraps it back to 0. The synchronous reset sets `q` to 0. This arithmetic-based approach uses minimal combinational logic and state storage, optimizing area and timing. The counter updates on the positive clock edge, and the output `q` is directly this register.
