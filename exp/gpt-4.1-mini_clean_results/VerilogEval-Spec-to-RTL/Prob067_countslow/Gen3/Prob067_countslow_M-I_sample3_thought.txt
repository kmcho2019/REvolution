To optimize the decade counter for better power and timing performance, implement clock gating to disable the clock to the counter flip-flops when slowena is low and reset is inactive. This reduces unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. The gating condition will be an enable signal active only when counting is needed (slowena high) or when reset is active (to allow synchronous reset). This approach reduces switching without changing the functional behavior or increasing area significantly, and can improve timing by reducing switching noise.
