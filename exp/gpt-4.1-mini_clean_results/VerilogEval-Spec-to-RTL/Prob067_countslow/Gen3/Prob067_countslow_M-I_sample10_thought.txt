To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will implement the decade counter as a synchronous up-counter that uses a single 4-bit binary counter with a modulus of 10. Instead of using a comparator to reset at 9, I will implement the counter using a ripple carry adder style by checking the carry-out of addition by 1 modulo 10. This reduces comparator logic and simplifies the next-state logic.

I will also use an explicit enable signal for counting (slowena) and synchronous reset, minimizing unnecessary toggling.

This approach potentially improves timing by simplifying combinational logic, lowers dynamic power by reducing switching in the comparator, and maintains minimal area usage.

The counter increments by 1 when slowena is high, wraps at 9, resets to zero on synchronous reset.
