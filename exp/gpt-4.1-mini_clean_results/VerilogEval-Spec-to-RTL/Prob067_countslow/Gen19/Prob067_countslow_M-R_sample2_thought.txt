Refactor the decade counter to use a single 4-bit register `q` that holds the count directly. On each rising clock edge, if reset is asserted, synchronously reset `q` to 0. Otherwise, if `slowena` is high, increment `q` by 1 and wrap around to 0 when it reaches 10. This approach eliminates the explicit state machine and separate next state logic, simplifying the design and potentially improving timing, area, and power. The output `q` is driven directly by the register.
