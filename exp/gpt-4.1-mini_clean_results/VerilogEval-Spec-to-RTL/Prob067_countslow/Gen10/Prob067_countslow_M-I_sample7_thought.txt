To optimize the decade counter for better power and timing, introduce a synchronous enable signal to gate the counting logic more explicitly, which helps synthesis tools optimize register enable. Additionally, use a simple arithmetic modulo by comparing q to 9 and resetting to 0. This is already minimal in area, but adding a dedicated enable signal may improve timing slightly. Clock gating can be risky, so we rely on the enable signal approach to reduce toggling. The code remains succinct and easy for synthesis optimizations.
