To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will focus on reducing switching activity by implementing a one-hot encoded counter instead of a binary counter. One-hot encoding typically reduces glitching and dynamic power since only one bit is high at a time, and combinational logic is simpler. To keep area small, I'll use a 10-bit one-hot register but output the equivalent 4-bit binary count. The reset logic and enable remain synchronous. The counting sequence is implemented by rotating the one-hot bit. This reduces logic complexity and can improve timing by simplifying carry logic. The output binary count is decoded via a simple combinational block from the one-hot state.
