Fuse the modular clarity of per-bit DFFs with the power and area efficiency of a single vector register by implementing the synchronous reset and negedge clock in one always block operating on an 8-bit register.

This design:
- Implements all 8 bits as a vector register updated together on negedge clk.
- Applies the synchronous reset of the entire vector to 8'h34 in one operation, reducing control logic and switching activity.
- Avoids passing parameters or instantiating multiple modules, reducing area and power.
- Maintains clean and simple code, easy to verify and maintain.
- Retains the exact functional behavior: 8-bit DFF with synchronous reset active high on negedge clk, reset value 0x34.

By consolidating the flip-flops into a vector register, this design enhances PPA (performance, power, area) without sacrificing clarity or functionality.
