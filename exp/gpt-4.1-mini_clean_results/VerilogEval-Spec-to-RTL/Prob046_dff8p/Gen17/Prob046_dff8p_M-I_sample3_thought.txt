To improve power and area (PPA), reduce toggling by implementing clock gating to disable clocking when input d doesn't change or reset is not asserted. This avoids unnecessary flip-flop toggles on negative edge clk. Also, keep the synchronous reset to 0x34 on negedge clk as required.

Strategy:
- Add a clock enable (ce) signal, active when reset is asserted or d input differs from stored q.
- Use this ce to gate the negedge clock internally in the register.
- This prevents flip-flops from updating q with the same data repeatedly, lowering switching power.
- Maintain a single always block for the synchronous reset and data load for minimal area.
- The clock gating uses a simple enable internally, avoiding complex clock gating cells which may complicate timing.

This approach trades a small extra compare logic and enable gating for potentially significant power savings by reducing unnecessary flip toggles. The design remains modular and clear.
