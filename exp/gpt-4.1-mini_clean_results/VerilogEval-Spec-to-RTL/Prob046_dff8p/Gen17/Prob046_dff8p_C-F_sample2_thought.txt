Fusion strategy:
- Use the simple, clear, and synthesis-friendly approach from Example 2 of a single always block triggered on the negative clock edge with synchronous reset.
- Reset q to 8'h34 as required.
- Avoid per-bit conditional updates to minimize logic complexity, area, and improve timing.
- To gain power savings benefits of selective updating from Example 1, add a simple vector-level clock enable that only updates q when d != q (instead of per-bit checks). This reduces unnecessary toggle in q without adding complex bitwise logic and loop.
- This provides a good balance: 
  * Simplicity and clarity of a single always block on negedge clk.
  * Power savings by only updating q when input changes, but with minimal combinational overhead.
  * Keeps synchronous active-high reset with fixed 0x34 reset value.
- The module is parameterized, modular, and meets the functional spec.
