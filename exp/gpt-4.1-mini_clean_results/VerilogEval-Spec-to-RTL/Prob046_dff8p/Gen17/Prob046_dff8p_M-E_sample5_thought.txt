Implement a single always block driven on the negative edge of clk with synchronous active-high reset. Instead of storing the entire 8-bit value in a single register, break the register into two 4-bit registers, each handling half of the data. On reset, both halves are synchronously loaded with the corresponding 4-bit parts of 0x34. On normal operation, data d is loaded into these two registers. This partitioning demonstrates an alternative architectural approach that still meets timing and reset requirements and could allow more granular control or pipelining in extended designs.
