Implement the TopModule by instantiating eight individual D flip-flops, each with negative edge clock triggering and synchronous active-high reset. Each flip-flop outputs one bit, and the 8 outputs collectively form the 8-bit output q.

Key points:
- Each DFF is a separate module to explicitly model each bit flip-flop.
- On synchronous reset at negedge clk, each flip-flop is loaded with the corresponding bit of 0x34.
- This bitwise approach improves flexibility for potential bitwise modifications or extensions.
- The TopModule connects each bit of d and q to the corresponding DFF instance.
- This approach highlights bit-level register implementation rather than a monolithic 8-bit register.
