To optimize the design for better area and potentially power, consolidate the eight individual D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register with a negative edge-triggered always block implementing synchronous reset. This reduces module instantiations and routing overhead compared to eight separate instances.

Use a localparam for the reset constant 8'h34 to enable better synthesis optimization and constant propagation instead of a wire. This also simplifies the code.

The always block will trigger on negedge clk, and synchronously reset q to 0x34 if reset is asserted; otherwise, load d. This vectorized approach reduces area and interconnect complexity while maintaining correct functionality and timing.

This design keeps the synchronous reset active high and negative edge clock triggering per specification but improves PPA by leveraging synthesis tools' ability to handle multi-bit registers more efficiently than multiple scalar flip-flops.
