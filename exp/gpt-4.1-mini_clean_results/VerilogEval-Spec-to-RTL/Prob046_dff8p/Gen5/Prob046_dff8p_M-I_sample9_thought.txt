To improve the PPA of the design, consolidate all eight D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register within one always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. Implement the active high synchronous reset inside this block by loading the fixed value 8'h34, otherwise load the input d. This reduces module overhead, area, and routing complexity by avoiding separate module instantiations for each bit, and generally improves timing and power due to simplified clock and reset logic.
