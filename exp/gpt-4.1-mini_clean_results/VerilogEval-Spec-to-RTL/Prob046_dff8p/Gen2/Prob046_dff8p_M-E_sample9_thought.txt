Implement a single 8-bit register with an always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. Use a synchronous active high reset inside this block to load the fixed value 8'h34 into the register. This approach uses a single always block and avoids instantiating multiple flip-flops, making the design simpler and easier to maintain. The synchronous reset directly sets q to the desired reset value on negedge clk when reset is high.
