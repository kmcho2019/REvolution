Instead of implementing 8 individual D flip-flops directly, I'll use a generate loop to instantiate 8 single-bit D flip-flop modules with synchronous active-high reset and negative-edge clock triggering. Each flip-flop will reset to the bit of 0x34 corresponding to its index.

This modular approach increases code clarity and reusability, allowing each flip-flop to be managed independently while maintaining synchronous reset and negative-edge clock triggering. It also separates the concerns, making the design more scalable and testable.

The TopModule will instantiate eight DFF modules (DFF_SyncReset_NegEdge) with the provided clk, reset, and respective data bits.
