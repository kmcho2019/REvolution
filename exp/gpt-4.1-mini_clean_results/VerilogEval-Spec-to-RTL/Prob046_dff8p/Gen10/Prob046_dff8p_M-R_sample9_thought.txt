Refactor the design by combining the 8 D flip-flops into a single always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. Use a single 8-bit register to hold q, with synchronous active-high reset loading 0x34, otherwise loading the input d. This reduces module overhead and eliminates the load_enable signal. It simplifies the design, reduces area slightly by not instantiating 8 separate modules, and keeps synchronous reset and negedge clk behavior intact. The single always block also improves readability and makes timing analysis straightforward.
