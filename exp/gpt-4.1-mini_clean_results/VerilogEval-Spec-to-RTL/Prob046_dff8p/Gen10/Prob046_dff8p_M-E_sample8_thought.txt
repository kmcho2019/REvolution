Implement the 8-bit register as eight independent negative edge-triggered single-bit D flip-flops with synchronous reset. 
Each flip-flop will be a separate module instance inside TopModule. 

This approach emphasizes clear bitwise separation and explicitness, aiding debugging and potential bit-level modifications. 
The reset value 0x34 (00110100) will be hardcoded into each flip-flop as its synchronous reset value, allowing different bits to reset to 0 or 1 individually. 

This modular per-bit design facilitates selective bit customization and better testability at the bit level, unlike vectorized designs that treat bits collectively. 
While slightly less area-efficient, the design is straightforward, easily extendable to different reset patterns, and shows clear correspondence between each flip-flop and its reset bit.

All flip-flops are negative edge triggered with synchronous active-high reset, fulfilling timing and reset requirements.
