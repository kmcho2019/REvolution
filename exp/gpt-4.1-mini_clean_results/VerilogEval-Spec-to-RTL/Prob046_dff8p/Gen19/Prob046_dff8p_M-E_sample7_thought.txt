Use a single 8-bit shift register styled approach with a synchronous load on the negative clock edge and synchronous reset to 0x34.
- On negative edge of clk, if reset is high, load 8'h34.
- Else load the parallel input d.
- The state is stored in an 8-bit register q.
- This avoids per-bit logic and conditional assignments, simplifying the design.
- No bitwise gating; entire vector q updates together for simple synthesis and clean timing.
- Negative edge clocking is preserved as specified.
