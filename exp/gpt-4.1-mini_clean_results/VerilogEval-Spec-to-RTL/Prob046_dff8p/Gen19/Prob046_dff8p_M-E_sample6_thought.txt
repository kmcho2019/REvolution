Implement a vector register with 8 D flip-flops triggered on the negative edge of clk using a single always block. On synchronous active-high reset, set q to 8'h34. On normal operation, load q with d entirely (no per-bit gating). To add a twist, use a parameterizable reset value and instantiate 8 individual DFF modules inside a generate loop. Each DFF is a separate module with synchronous reset and negative-edge clocking, simplifying the design by encapsulating each flip-flop individually. This modular approach aids clarity, testability, and potential bit-level reuse. The TopModule will instantiate these 8 DFFs, wiring each bit of d and q separately.
