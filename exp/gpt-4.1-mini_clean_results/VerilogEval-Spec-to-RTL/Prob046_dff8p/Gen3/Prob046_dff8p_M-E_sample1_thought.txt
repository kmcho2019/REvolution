Implement each of the 8 D flip-flops as separate always blocks triggered on the negative edge of clk, with a shared synchronous active-high reset. Instead of a single 8-bit register, instantiate 8 individual 1-bit registers (flip-flops), each responsible for one bit of the output q. On reset, each flip-flop sets its output bit to the corresponding bit of 8'h34. This approach modularizes the design, mimicking the discrete flip-flops implementation and allows bit-level customizations or modifications more easily in the future.
