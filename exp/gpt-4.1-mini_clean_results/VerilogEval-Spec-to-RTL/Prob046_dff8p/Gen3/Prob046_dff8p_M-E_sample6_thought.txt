Instead of using a single always block to implement an 8-bit register, instantiate eight individual D flip-flop modules, each with synchronous active-high reset triggered on the negative edge of clk. Each flip-flop will hold one bit, reset to 1 bit of the constant 0x34 value, and take the corresponding bit from d as input. This modular approach explicitly shows the composition of the 8-bit register from 8 distinct flip-flops, which can be useful for structural clarity and potentially allow per-bit customization or reuse of the DFF module.
