To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will consolidate the 8 individual flip-flops into a single 8-bit register with a synchronous active-high reset triggered on the negative clock edge. This reduces the overhead of instantiating multiple modules and simplifies the wiring of the reset value.

This approach keeps the functionality intact (negative-edge triggered, synchronous reset to 0x34) but minimizes area by using a single register. It also reduces switching activity by updating the entire register in one clock cycle rather than multiple separate flip-flops.

Using a single always block for all bits enables more efficient synthesis and potentially better timing optimization. It simplifies the code and avoids per-bit reset value wiring overhead.

The reset value 0x34 is directly assigned on reset, and on non-reset cycles, the input d bus loads into the q register on the negative edge of clk.

This maintains the original design intent with improved PPA characteristics.
