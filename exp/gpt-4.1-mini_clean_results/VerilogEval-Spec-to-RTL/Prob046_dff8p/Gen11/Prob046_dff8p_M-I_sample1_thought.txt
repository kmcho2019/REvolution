To optimize the original design for better PPA while keeping the same functionality, I will combine all 8 D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register instead of instantiating 8 separate 1-bit modules. This approach reduces module overhead and wiring complexity, potentially improving area and power.

Specifically, I will:
- Use a single always block triggered on the negative edge of clk.
- Use a single 8-bit reset constant (0x34) assigned synchronously when reset is asserted.
- Avoid per-bit parameter passing and generate constructs.
- This also reduces the number of control signals toggling during reset, which can lower switching power.

The synchronous reset behavior and negative edge clock triggering remain as specified, ensuring functional equivalence but with a simpler, more compact RTL structure that typically leads to improved synthesis results.
