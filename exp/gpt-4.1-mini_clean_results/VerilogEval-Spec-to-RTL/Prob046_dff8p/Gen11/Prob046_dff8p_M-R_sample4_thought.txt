Refactor the design by implementing the 8-bit D flip-flops as a single vector register instead of eight separate single-bit modules. This register will be triggered on the negative edge of clk with synchronous active-high reset, setting the entire 8-bit register to 8'h34 at reset.

This approach:
- Consolidates the flip-flops into a single always block.
- Simplifies the reset logic by using a fixed 8-bit reset value.
- Reduces module instantiations and potentially area.
- Maintains the negative edge triggering and synchronous reset requirements.
- Provides clear, concise, and standard RTL coding style.

No submodules are needed, making the module self-contained and easier to maintain.
