Implement the 8-bit register using individual negative edge triggered D flip-flops instantiated separately rather than as a vector assignment. Each flip-flop has a synchronous reset that sets its output to the corresponding bit of the reset value (0x34) on the negedge clk when reset is high. 

This approach uses 8 instances of a parameterized DFF module with synchronous active-high reset and negedge clock trigger. Each DFF handles one bit independently, providing clearer granularity and potential for bit-wise customization or extension later. The TopModule connects these 8 DFFs in parallel, assembling the 8-bit register.

This architecture increases modularity and reusability of single-bit DFF components, making it easy to modify behavior per bit if needed. Although it results in more verbose code, it explicitly shows the structural composition of the register from individual flip-flops.
