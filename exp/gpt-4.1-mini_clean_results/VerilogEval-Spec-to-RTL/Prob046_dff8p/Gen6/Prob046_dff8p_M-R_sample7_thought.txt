Refactor by instantiating eight individual D flip-flop modules, each with synchronous reset and triggered on the negative clock edge. This modular approach separates each bit's storage into a dedicated DFF module that handles the synchronous reset to 1'b0 or 1'b1 depending on bit position of 0x34. The TopModule then aggregates these DFF instances to form the 8-bit register. This method emphasizes structural design and bit-level clarity while preserving the original behavior.
