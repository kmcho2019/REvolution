Refactor the design by integrating all 8 flip-flops into a single always block instead of individual DFF modules. Use a single 8-bit register 'q' updated on the negative edge of clk. The synchronous reset condition will be checked inside this always block, and if active, q will be synchronously loaded with the constant reset value 0x34; otherwise, it will load the input d. This removes the generate loop and separate DFF modules, potentially improving area and power by reducing module instantiations and signal routing complexity while maintaining the synchronous negative-edge triggered behavior and synchronous reset.
