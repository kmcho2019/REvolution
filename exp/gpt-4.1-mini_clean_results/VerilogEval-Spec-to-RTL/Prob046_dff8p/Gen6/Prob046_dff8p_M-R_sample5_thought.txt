To refactor the design while keeping the core idea intact, I will implement a single 8-bit register inside the TopModule with an always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. This register will have an active-high synchronous reset that loads the constant 0x34.

This eliminates the need for instantiating multiple DFF modules and the generate loop, simplifying the design into a single always block that handles all 8 bits collectively.

This approach improves readability and reduces the complexity of the hierarchy while maintaining exact functionality: negative-edge triggered synchronous reset loading 0x34 and data loading of d when reset is inactive.
