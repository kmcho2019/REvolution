Instead of instantiating eight individual D flip-flop modules, implement a single 8-bit register with synchronous reset inside one always block triggered on the negative edge of the clock. On reset, the register loads the constant 8'h34. This approach reduces module overhead, simplifies clock tree complexity, and improves area and power by consolidating flip-flops into a vector register with a synchronous reset value. It is a behavioral style solution that still meets all requirements, including negative edge triggering and active-high synchronous reset.
