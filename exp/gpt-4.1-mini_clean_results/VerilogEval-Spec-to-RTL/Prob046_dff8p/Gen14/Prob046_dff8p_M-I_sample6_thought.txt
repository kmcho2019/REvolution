To improve PPA while maintaining functionality, I will remove the vector comparator-based clock enable (ce) since it adds area and dynamic power overhead, especially for vector width 8 or larger. Instead, I rely on the synchronous reset and the nature of DFFs to avoid toggling when d equals q naturally. Removing ce simplifies logic and may improve timing and reduce area without impacting correctness. The clocking on negedge clk is kept as per requirement. The design remains parameterized for reuse and clarity.
