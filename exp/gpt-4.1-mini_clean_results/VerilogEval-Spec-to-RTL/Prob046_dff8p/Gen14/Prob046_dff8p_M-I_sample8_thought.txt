To optimize for better power and area without sacrificing clarity or functionality:

- Add a clock enable (ce) internally, derived by comparing d and q: flip-flops update only when input d differs from output q. This suppresses unnecessary toggling and data loading, reducing dynamic power.
- Keep synchronous active-high reset loading 0x34.
- Maintain negative edge clocking and a single always block for timing clarity.
- This approach avoids complex gating cells, uses simple comparison logic, and still meets all specs.
- Area overhead of the comparator is negligible compared to savings from reduced flip-flop toggling.
- Performance impact is minimal because the clock enable is purely internal logic.

This optimization primarily targets power reduction by minimizing data transitions and thus dynamic power, while preserving all other design aspects.
