To optimize for better PPA, I will consolidate the eight single-bit DFF instances into a single vectorized register within one always block. This reduces module overhead, routing complexity, and area. The synchronous reset will be handled internally by loading the reset constant 8'h34 on negedge clk when reset is active, otherwise loading input d. This structural simplification should reduce area and power by minimizing toggle activity and improve timing by lowering clock loading and mux overhead.
