To optimize PPA, I will hardcode the reset constant 0x34 inside the DFF module rather than passing reset_val as an input per bit. This removes the extra reset_val input port and associated wiring, reducing area and power from toggling signals. The DFF module will synchronously load the hardcoded reset value bits individually, improving power and area by eliminating redundant interconnect. Also, the generate loop will instantiate the DFFs with only clk, reset, d, and q signals, keeping the interface clean. This should preserve the original functionality and timing but reduce the complexity and switching in reset signal routing, enhancing overall efficiency.
