The original solution correctly implements the negative edge triggered 8-bit D flip-flops with synchronous reset to 0x34. However, it adds extra complexity and area by using an internal register and comparator for clock enable logic, which may affect timing and increase power and area slightly.

To optimize for better PPA while keeping the required functionality:

1. Remove the clock enable logic and the internal d_reg comparator. This reduces area and simplifies the logic.
2. Use a single always block triggered on the negative edge of clk directly to match the specification and avoid clock inversion that can cause skew or timing issues.
3. Keep the synchronous reset straightforward, directly loading 8'h34 when reset is asserted.
4. This simple approach reduces combinational logic and area, improves timing robustness (no clk inversion or gating), and achieves minimal dynamic power by avoiding extra comparators and registers.

Overall, we simplify the design by implementing a direct negative edge triggered register with synchronous reset to the required value, trading off some power saved by conditional updates for better timing, smaller area, and simpler logic.
