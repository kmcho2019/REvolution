Simplify the design by removing the clock enable logic to eliminate the 8-bit comparator and associated combinational delay. The flip-flops will always load the input `d` on every negative clock edge when not in reset, which still meets the functionality requirement. The synchronous active-high reset loads 0x34 at the negative clock edge as required. This simplification reduces area and power overhead from the comparator and clock enable logic, and improves timing by removing the critical path through the comparator, while maintaining the same behavior.
