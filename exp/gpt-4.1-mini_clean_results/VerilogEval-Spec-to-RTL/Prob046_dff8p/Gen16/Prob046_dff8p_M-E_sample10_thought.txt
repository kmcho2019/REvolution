To implement the 8-bit register with negative edge-triggered flip-flops and a synchronous active-high reset that loads 0x34, I will use an array of 8 individual D flip-flops, each instantiated separately. Each DFF will have the synchronous reset inside its always block and triggers on the negative edge of clk. This modular approach improves clarity and traceability of individual bits and matches exactly the requirement of using 8 DFFs.

No clock enable or additional logic will be added, ensuring simplicity and minimal combinational path on the clock. The reset logic will synchronously load 0x34 into the register, by assigning the appropriate bit of 0x34 to each flip-flop during reset.

This approach avoids vector comparison logic, has no gating or enable signals, and strictly follows the requirement of synchronous reset and negative-edge clock triggering. It is straightforward, easy to understand, and synthesizes cleanly.
