Refactor the original design to instantiate eight individual 1-bit D flip-flops with synchronous active-high reset triggered on the negative edge of clk. Each flip-flop is a separate module with the specified behavior, and the TopModule assembles them into an 8-bit register by connecting each bit separately. This modular per-bit approach clearly isolates flip-flop behavior and enables easier pinpointing in debugging or simulation, while preserving the synchronous reset to 0x34.

This approach avoids vector non-blocking assignments and instead relies on separate always blocks per flip-flop instance internally, still synchronous reset on negedge clk. The parallel instantiation aligns with structural design styles and emphasizes per-bit explicitness, differing structurally from the vectorized always block approach, yet functionally equivalent.
