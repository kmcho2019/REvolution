To provide a novel solution, I will implement the entire 8-bit register as a single always block with negative edge triggered clock and synchronous reset. Instead of separate flip-flop modules, a single vector register will hold all 8 bits, and on the negative edge of the clock, it will either be synchronously reset to 8'h34 or loaded with the input d.

This approach simplifies the design by removing the need for generating individual flip-flops and passing reset values per bit, thereby reducing the module hierarchy and potentially improving synthesis optimization. It keeps the exact functionality of synchronous reset and negative edge triggering.

Using a single always block is a common and efficient way to implement vector registers with synchronous resets and negative edge triggering.
