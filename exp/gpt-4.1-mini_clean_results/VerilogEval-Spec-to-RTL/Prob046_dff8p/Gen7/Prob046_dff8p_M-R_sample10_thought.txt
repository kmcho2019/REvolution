Refactor by consolidating the eight individual D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register implemented in one always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. Use a synchronous reset that sets the entire 8-bit register to the constant 0x34. This approach simplifies the design, reduces module instantiations, and decreases clock load by using a single multi-bit register rather than eight separate DFF modules. This structural change maintains the exact same functionality but improves area and power efficiency.
