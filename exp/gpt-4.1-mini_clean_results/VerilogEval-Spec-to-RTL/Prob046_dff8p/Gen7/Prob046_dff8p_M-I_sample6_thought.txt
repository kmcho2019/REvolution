To optimize the design for better PPA, consolidate the eight single-bit D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register implemented in one always block. This reduces module overhead and logic duplication, thus lowering area and potentially improving timing by minimizing clock loading. Implement the synchronous active-high reset to load 0x34 on the negative edge of clk within this single always block. This approach is both clean and efficient for power and area without sacrificing functionality.
