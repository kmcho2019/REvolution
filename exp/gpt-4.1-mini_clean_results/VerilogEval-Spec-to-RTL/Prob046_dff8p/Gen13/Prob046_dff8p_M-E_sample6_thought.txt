Implement eight independent D flip-flops, each with its own always block triggered on the negative edge of clk. Each flip-flop has a synchronous active-high reset that sets it to a specific bit of the constant 8'h34 (0011_0100). Instead of a vector register, use scalar 1-bit registers for q[7:0]. This approach improves modularity and testability at the bit level and allows for straightforward individual bit manipulation if needed later. The synchronous reset condition applies simultaneously to all flip-flops on the negative edge, setting their outputs to corresponding bits of 0x34. This design avoids any vector inequality or clock enable logic, simplifying timing and logic gates.
