Implement the 8-bit register using eight individual negative-edge triggered D flip-flops with synchronous reset, each explicitly instantiated as a separate module instance. Each flip-flop will synchronously reset to 0x34's corresponding bit value. This explicit instantiation approach promotes modularity and clear mapping to hardware flip-flops.

Instead of clock gating or clock enable logic, keep the design simple: on every negative edge of clk, if reset is asserted, each flip-flop synchronously resets to its bit of 0x34; otherwise it loads the respective bit of input d. 

This approach provides clarity in hardware mapping, easier testability of individual bits, and clean separation of each bit's flip-flop behavior. The synchronous reset pattern remains intact and follows the negative edge clk triggering.

The TopModule will instantiate eight instances of the DFF module parameterized by the reset bit. This is a straightforward and structurally clear design differing from the previous clock enable approach.
