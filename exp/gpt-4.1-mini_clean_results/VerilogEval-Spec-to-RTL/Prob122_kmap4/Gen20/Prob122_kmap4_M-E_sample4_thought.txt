Instead of implementing the XOR of all four inputs directly, the design can be structured hierarchically by first XORing pairs of inputs separately and then XORing their results. Specifically, compute x1 = a ^ b and x2 = c ^ d, then assign out = x1 ^ x2. This modular approach separates the logic into smaller blocks, which could be beneficial for understanding or certain synthesis tools, while still exactly matching the Karnaugh map.
