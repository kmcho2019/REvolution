The given solution implements the function as a 4-input XOR by decomposing it into two 2-input XORs followed by another XOR, which is logically minimal and already optimal in terms of logic depth and gate count.

To optimize further for PPA:

- From a performance standpoint, the critical path is two XOR gates deep; this is minimal for a 4-input XOR.
- For area and power, the current solution is minimal since XOR gates are necessary to implement parity functions.
- One possible minor improvement is to merge the XOR operations into a single continuous assignment expression (out = a ^ b ^ c ^ d) to reduce the number of intermediate nets and slightly reduce the synthesis overhead. This may help synthesis tools optimize better by enabling direct mapping to dedicated XOR4 gates in some technologies.
- Also, eliminating intermediate wires reduces routing complexity and capacitance, which can marginally improve power.
- Maintaining combinational style with continuous assignment preserves clarity.

Thus, the improvement is to replace the two intermediate XOR wires with a single continuous XOR expression for the output.

This retains all functionality, slightly reduces area and power due to simpler netlist, and keeps the minimal logic depth of two XOR levels as synthesis tools optimize the XOR tree internally.
