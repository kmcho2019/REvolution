Instead of directly computing the XOR of all inputs, I will implement the logic by grouping the minterms that produce a '1' output in the Karnaugh map. The map shows alternating 1s that form a checkerboard pattern, which corresponds to the parity of bits. To create a novel architecture, I will use intermediate signals representing partial XORs of pairs (a and b, c and d), then XOR these partial results to get the final output. This effectively partitions the logic into two 2-input XORs feeding into another XOR, creating a tree structure different from a single 4-input XOR gate.
