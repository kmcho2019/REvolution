The existing solution implements the output as the XOR of all four inputs, which exactly matches the Karnaugh map pattern. Since XOR is the minimal form and already the most area- and performance-efficient implementation for this checkerboard pattern, no further logic simplification is possible.

However, for slight power optimization, we can consider restructuring the XOR operation as two 2-input XOR gates arranged hierarchically to potentially enable better synthesis and technology mapping. This sometimes helps synthesis tools to optimize XOR depth or share resources.

Hence, rewrite the output as out = (a ^ b) ^ (c ^ d), which is logically equivalent but may be better for synthesis. This doesn't change performance or area meaningfully but may help power due to reduced simultaneous toggling in some flows.

No other PPA gains are realistically achievable given the minimal logic.

So, the improvement is to structure the XOR chain hierarchically for a potential slight power and synthesis benefit.
