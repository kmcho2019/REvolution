Fuse the best practices from both examples: keep the minimal and optimal 4-input XOR logic but use a continuous assignment for output instead of an always block. This merges the functional simplicity and minimal logic depth of the XOR parity function with the synthesis-friendly and concise coding style of a continuous assignment.
