The Karnaugh map pattern repeats in a checkerboard pattern indicating an alternating output depending on the parity of the sum of inputs. Instead of XORing all four inputs directly, we can implement this by first XORing pairs (a XOR d) and (b XOR c), and then XOR the results. This grouped XOR approach is logically equivalent but highlights a different structural grouping, which could be advantageous for certain synthesis tools or pipeline structures.
