Analyze the Karnaugh map pattern to find an alternative implementation. The map corresponds to cases where the number of 1s in inputs a, b, c, d is odd. Instead of directly XORing all inputs, group the inputs into pairs and XOR each pair, then XNOR the pairwise XOR results. This yields the same output but uses an alternate XOR-XNOR combination. Implement this using assign statements to keep the design purely combinational and simple.
