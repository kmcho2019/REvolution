The Karnaugh map pattern repeats every two bits and can be seen as a checkerboard pattern based on the parity of the concatenation of inputs. Another way to express the pattern is that the output is high if and only if exactly two of the four inputs are high, or equivalently, if the number of high inputs is even but not zero or four. To implement a novel architecture, I will use a small 16-entry ROM lookup table indexed by {a,b,c,d}. This approach is different from pure combinational logic and uses a combinational ROM to implement the logic function.
