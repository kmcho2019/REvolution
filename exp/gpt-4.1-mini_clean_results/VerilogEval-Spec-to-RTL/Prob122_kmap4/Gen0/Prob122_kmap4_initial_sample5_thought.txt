The Karnaugh map is a 4-variable map with variables a,b and c,d, where the rows correspond to cd and columns to ab in Gray code order 00,01,11,10. The output is 1 in cells where cd and ab differ in parity (i.e. the output is 1 when the number of ones in the four inputs is odd). Observing the pattern, the output can be expressed as the XOR of all four inputs a, b, c, and d.
