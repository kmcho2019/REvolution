The Karnaugh map specifies the output for each combination of inputs a,b,c,d, with a and b as the two MSBs and c and d as the two LSBs. Observing the pattern, the output is 1 when exactly one of the inputs is 1, i.e., when the number of ones in a,b,c,d is exactly one. This is equivalent to checking if the 4-bit input vector has a Hamming weight of 1. So, the output can be expressed as (a & ~b & ~c & ~d) | (~a & b & ~c & ~d) | (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & ~b & ~c & d).

Alternatively, since the pattern corresponds to output 1 when exactly one input is 1, we can implement this by counting the number of 1's and outputting 1 if count is 1. The first approach with explicit terms is simpler and efficient for a 4-input scenario.
