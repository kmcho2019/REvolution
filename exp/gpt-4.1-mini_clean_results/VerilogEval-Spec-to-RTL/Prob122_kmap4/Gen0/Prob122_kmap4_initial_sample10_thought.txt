The Karnaugh map can be interpreted as a 4-variable boolean function f(a,b,c,d). The map is indexed by cd (rows) and ab (columns). The pattern shows that the output is 1 when the number of 1's in the input is odd. Checking the map carefully, the output equals 1 when the parity of the four inputs is odd, which is effectively the XOR of all inputs. So, out = a ^ b ^ c ^ d.
