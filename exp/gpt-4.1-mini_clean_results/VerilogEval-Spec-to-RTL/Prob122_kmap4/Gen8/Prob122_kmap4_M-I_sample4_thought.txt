The original design is already functionally minimal and optimal by implementing parity using XOR gates, which aligns perfectly with the Karnaugh map. Given that XOR gates have inherently balanced delay and low gate count for this function, there is minimal room for improvement in logic depth or area.

To potentially improve power and area slightly, I will remove the intermediate wires and combine the XOR operations into a single continuous assign statement. This reduces the number of named nets, simplifying the netlist and possibly aiding synthesis tools in optimizing away redundant signals.

Additionally, I'll add explicit input port types and keep the module clean and commented for maintainability, but I won’t change the fundamental XOR logic because it's minimal.

This approach provides a marginal improvement in power and area (slightly less net overhead) while keeping the design clean, clear, and equally performant.
