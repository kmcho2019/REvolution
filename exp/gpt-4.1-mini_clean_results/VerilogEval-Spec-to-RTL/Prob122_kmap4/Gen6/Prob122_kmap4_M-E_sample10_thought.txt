The Karnaugh map shows a checkerboard pattern with output 1 for minterms where the sum of the number of 1s in inputs a,b,c,d is odd. This is the definition of odd parity. To implement this differently from a single XOR chain, we can build a hierarchical XOR structure using intermediate XOR gates. Specifically, XOR inputs a and b, XOR inputs c and d, then XOR these two intermediate results. This approach is structurally distinct but functionally equivalent.
