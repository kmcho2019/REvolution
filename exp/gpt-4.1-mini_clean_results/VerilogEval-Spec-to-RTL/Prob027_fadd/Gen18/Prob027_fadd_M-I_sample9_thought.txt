To optimize for better power, area, and timing (PPA), replace the two half adders and OR gate with direct combinational logic expressions for sum and cout. This eliminates intermediate signals and module instantiations, reducing gate count and wiring complexity. The sum is the XOR of all three inputs, and cout is the majority function implemented with three AND-OR terms. This approach minimizes gate levels and switching, improving power and area without compromising timing or clarity. The single module is simple, efficient, and suitable for synthesis.
