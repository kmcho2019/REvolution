Fuse the full modular clarity and reusability of the HalfAdder module with the direct, fully flattened combinational logic that yields the most compact, lowest-latency and lowest-area full adder implementation. 

The fusion approach is:
- Define the HalfAdder module as a reusable building block, preserving clarity.
- In TopModule, avoid any extra intermediate wires or multiple half adder instantiations.
- Instead, implement the full adder logic directly with continuous assignments using minimal gate-level operators, fully flattening the sum and cout expressions.
- This approach keeps the HalfAdder module available for reuse or extension, while the TopModule implements the full adder with a simple one-line assignment to sum and cout.
- This reduces module overhead, minimizes the number of signals and logic levels, and thus improves performance, power, and area.
  
The direct logic is:
- sum = a ^ b ^ cin
- cout = (a & b) | (b & cin) | (a & cin)

This is the canonical minimal full adder logic, achieving the lowest possible gate count and logic depth.

This fusion yields:
- Minimal gate levels (XOR tree for sum, 3-input OR of ANDs for cout)
- Minimal interconnect (no internal wires)
- Maximum clarity (standard full adder equations)
- Maximum reusability (HalfAdder module remains for other uses)
- Best PPA for a one-bit full adder

Overall, this solution combines the modular clarity and reusability of defining HalfAdder with the simplicity and efficiency of direct combinational assignments in the top-level full adder.
