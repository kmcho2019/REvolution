Implement the full adder using direct combinational boolean expressions without any submodule instantiation. The sum is computed as the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin). The carry-out is computed as the majority function of the three inputs: it is '1' if at least two of the inputs are '1'. This approach minimizes gate count, reduces logic levels, and avoids overhead from hierarchical instantiation, potentially improving area and power while maintaining clear and simple combinational logic.
