To create a superior full adder implementation, fuse the modular clarity of using half adders with the efficiency of direct combinational logic by integrating the half adder functionality inside the TopModule as internal wire assignments rather than separate module instantiations. This approach maintains hierarchical clarity at the code level while eliminating overhead from module boundaries and wiring, reducing area and power. The sum and carry-out outputs are generated by combining XOR and AND gates directly, preserving minimal gate depth for performance. This design offers a clean, readable structure while improving PPA by reducing redundant modules and wiring.
