To improve the original modular full adder design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), replace the hierarchical two half-adder instantiation with a direct gate-level implementation inside TopModule. This eliminates the overhead of submodules and reduces the number of logic gates by directly expressing sum as the XOR of all three inputs and cout as the majority function (carry-out) using minimal gates. This reduces net count, gate count, and switching activity slightly, improving area and power without affecting timing, which is already optimal.
