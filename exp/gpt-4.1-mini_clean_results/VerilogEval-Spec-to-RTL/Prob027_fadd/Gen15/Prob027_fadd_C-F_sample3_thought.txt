Fuse the modular clarity of using half adders with the efficiency of direct combinational logic to optimize performance, power, and area. Define the HalfAdder module separately for reuse and readability. In the TopModule, instantiate two half adders to compute intermediate sums and carries as in previous examples, but then directly assign the final sum and carry-out using minimal logic expressions without extra intermediate wires. This reduces the number of wires and logic levels. For carry-out, instead of ORing two carries explicitly through wires, directly use the OR operation on half adder carry outputs combined with minimal routing. This approach keeps modularity while trimming redundant nets, lowering gate count and switching, thereby improving power and area without sacrificing timing or clarity.
