Implement the full adder logic directly within TopModule using well-known boolean expressions:

- sum = a XOR b XOR cin
- cout = majority(a,b,cin) = (a & b) | (b & cin) | (a & cin)

This approach retains the minimal logic depth and provides optimal performance, power, and area by avoiding intermediate wires and redundant modules.
