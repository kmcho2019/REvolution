Fuse the modular clarity of the two half adder approach with the efficiency of direct logic implementation by incorporating the half adder logic as local parameters or inline logic rather than separate instantiated modules. This retains the clear conceptual modularity but eliminates overhead from module instantiation, reducing area and power. The sum is computed as a three-input XOR and the carry-out as the majority function of the inputs directly, thus minimizing gate count and critical path depth for improved PPA while keeping code readability and structural clarity.
