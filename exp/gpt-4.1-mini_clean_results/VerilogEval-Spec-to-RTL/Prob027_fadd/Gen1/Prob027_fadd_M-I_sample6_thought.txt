To optimize the full adder for better PPA, the main focus is on reducing gate delay (for performance) and possibly minimizing switching activity (for power). While the original implementation is minimal, one possible improvement is to reduce the gate depth on the carry-out signal. Instead of using the sum-of-products form for carry-out, using a carry-lookahead expression can reduce delay. For instance, computing an intermediate generate and propagate signals:

- generate g = a & b
- propagate p = a ^ b

Then:
- carry-out = g | (p & cin)

This reduces the carry-out logic to two gate levels with less fan-in per gate, which may improve timing.

Power and area remain minimal due to simplicity.

This optimized implementation keeps the same interface and functionality but may provide better timing on carry-out, which is usually on the critical path in adders.
