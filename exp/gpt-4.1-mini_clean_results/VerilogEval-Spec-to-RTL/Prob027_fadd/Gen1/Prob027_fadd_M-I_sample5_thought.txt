The original design is already optimal in terms of logic simplicity, latency, and area since it uses the minimal logic expressions for sum and carry-out in a full adder. Given the zero timing slack and minimal power and area numbers, any RTL-level optimization is unlikely to improve PPA metrics. However, to potentially reduce gate count slightly and thus area/power, we can express carry-out using a conditional (ternary) operator instead of multiple OR-ed AND terms. This can sometimes help synthesis tools optimize logic better. Alternatively, we can factor the carry equation as cout = (a & b) | ((a ^ b) & cin), which has fewer gates. This reduces the number of AND gates from 3 to 2, potentially improving area and power slightly without affecting performance. So the improvement is to rewrite carry-out as cout = (a & b) | ((a ^ b) & cin).
