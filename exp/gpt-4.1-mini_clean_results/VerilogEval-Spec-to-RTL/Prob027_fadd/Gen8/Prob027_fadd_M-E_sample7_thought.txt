Implement the full adder using direct logic expressions for sum and carry-out, relying on the Boolean algebra definition of a full adder. The sum is the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, and cin), and the carry-out is true if at least two inputs are high, expressed as the majority function: (a & b) | (b & cin) | (cin & a). This approach uses minimal logic gates and levels, resulting in a compact, fast, and low-power full adder.
