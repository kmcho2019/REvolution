Fuse the clarity and modular reuse of the half adder approach with the succinctness and optimal PPA of direct logic expressions by implementing the full adder using explicit bitwise logic (XOR and majority carry logic) without intermediate modules or wires. This achieves minimal gate count and shortest logic depth, improving performance and area compared to the half adder hierarchy, while being more explicit and possibly more power-efficient than using '+' operator. The code remains clear and concise, directly expressing sum as XOR of inputs and cout as the majority function, maximizing the best of both previous solutions.
