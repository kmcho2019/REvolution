Optimize the full adder by directly assigning the sum and carry outputs with minimal logic expressions instead of decomposing into half adders. This flattens the logic, reducing gate levels and intermediate signals, thereby improving performance, power, and area. The sum is computed as the XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out is generated by the majority function of the inputs, which can be expressed as (a & b) | (b & cin) | (a & cin). This direct combinational implementation removes unnecessary module instantiations and wiring overhead.
