Implement the full adder using direct combinational logic expressions based on the majority function for carry-out and XOR for sum. Specifically, the sum is the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out is set if at least two of the inputs are high, which can be expressed as the majority logic: (a & b) | (b & cin) | (a & cin). This approach minimizes logic levels and gate count, leading to potentially better performance and area compared to decomposing into half-adders.
