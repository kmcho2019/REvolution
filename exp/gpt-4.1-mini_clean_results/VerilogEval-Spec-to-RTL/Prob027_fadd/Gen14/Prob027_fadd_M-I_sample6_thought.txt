To optimize the full adder for better area and power without sacrificing timing, I will eliminate the two half adder submodules and implement the full adder logic directly inside TopModule using the minimal boolean expressions: sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = (a & b) | (b & cin) | (cin & a). This reduces gate duplication, removes the overhead of intermediate signals and module instantiation, and simplifies the logic. It keeps the critical path minimal while reducing total gate count and interconnect, improving power and area metrics.
