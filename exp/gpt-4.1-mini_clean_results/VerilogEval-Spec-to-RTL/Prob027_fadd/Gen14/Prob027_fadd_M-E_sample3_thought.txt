Implement the full adder using a gate-level approach with only bitwise operators and without hierarchical modules. Compute the sum output as XOR of all three inputs directly, and compute the carry-out as the majority function of the three inputs, which can be expressed as (a&b) | (b&cin) | (a&cin). This approach minimizes module instances and explicitly shows the fundamental gate logic, potentially reducing overhead and improving area and power by eliminating module boundary wiring.
