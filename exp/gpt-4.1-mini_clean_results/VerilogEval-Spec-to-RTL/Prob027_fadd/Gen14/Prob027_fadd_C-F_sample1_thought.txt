- Implement the half adder as an inline parameterizable function (local function) to retain modularity without instantiating modules and extra nets.

- Compute the sum and carry by combining outputs of two half adders implemented as functions.
- This approach reduces wiring overhead and inter-module net delays seen in the hierarchical solution, potentially lowering power.
- At the same time, it avoids the flat logic of the second example by explicitly showing the half adder steps, aiding understandability and testability.

This strategy keeps the clean hierarchical logic structure but compiles to flat combinational logic, gaining performance and power advantages while maintaining clarity.
