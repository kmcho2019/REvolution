To optimize the full adder for better PPA, I will remove the hierarchical decomposition into half adders and instead implement the full adder with direct combinational logic expressions for sum and carry-out. This approach minimizes gate levels, reduces intermediate signals, and decreases the logic cell count. It directly expresses sum as the XOR of all three inputs and carry-out as the majority function of the inputs (carry generated if at least two inputs are high). This will reduce delay, power consumption, and area compared to the original modular design while preserving correctness.
