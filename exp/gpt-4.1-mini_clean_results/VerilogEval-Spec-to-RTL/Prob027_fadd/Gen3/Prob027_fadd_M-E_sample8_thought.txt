Implement the full adder by breaking down the carry-out into an intermediate carry generate and propagate signals. First, compute the propagate (p) as the XOR of a and b, and generate (g) as the AND of a and b. Then, the sum is the XOR of propagate and cin, and carry-out is generated if either the generate is high or both propagate and cin are high. This method leverages carry look-ahead logic concepts in a simple full adder.
