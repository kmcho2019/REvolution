To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), remove the always block and reg outputs, and implement the full adder using continuous assignments with wire outputs. This eliminates unnecessary registers and inferred flip-flops, resulting in minimal combinational logic. The design uses direct Boolean expressions for sum and carry-out, which is optimal for synthesis tools to generate minimal gate-level implementations, reducing area and power consumption. The simple XOR and AND/OR logic ensures minimal delay.
