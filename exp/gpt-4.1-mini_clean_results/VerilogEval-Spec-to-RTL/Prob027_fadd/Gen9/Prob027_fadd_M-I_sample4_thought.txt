The current design uses minimal and optimal logic expressions for a full adder with XOR for sum and majority function for cout, which is already optimal in terms of logic depth, gate count, and power for a standalone full adder. Further PPA improvement in such a small combinational block is difficult without changing the interface or technology.

However, a common alternative implementation that can sometimes optimize gate-level synthesis and power is to compute sum and cout using generate and propagate signals:

- Generate (g) = a & b
- Propagate (p) = a ^ b
- sum = p ^ cin
- cout = g | (p & cin)

This can reduce gate input capacitance in some technologies by reusing p signal for both sum and cout, potentially leading to slight power and area improvements at the gate level. Also, the re-use of p signal may help the synthesis tool optimize better.

This approach preserves correctness and can be better for practical PPA even though logically equivalent.

Hence, the improvement strategy is to express the sum and cout via generate and propagate signals, enhancing signal reuse and potentially aiding synthesis for better PPA.
