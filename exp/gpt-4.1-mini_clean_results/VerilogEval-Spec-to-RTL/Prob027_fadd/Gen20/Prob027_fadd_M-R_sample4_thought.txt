Refactor the full adder by removing the HalfAdder submodule and implementing the full adder logic directly in the TopModule using continuous assign statements. This avoids module instantiation overhead and extra intermediate signals for sum and carry between half adders. The sum is computed as a ^ b ^ cin, and carry-out as the majority function (a&b) | (b&cin) | (cin&a). This approach simplifies the design, reduces wiring, and can slightly improve area and power without changing the functionality.
