To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will maintain the clarity and modularity of using half adders while avoiding redundancy and minimizing intermediate nets and module overhead. I will implement the half adder as an inline local module within the TopModule for encapsulation without extra instantiation overhead. The full adder logic will directly use two half adders connected with minimal wires, and carry-out computed by OR of half adder carries. This approach preserves modularity and readability, reduces area and power by avoiding external module instantiations, and maintains a minimal critical path with two XOR gates and an OR gate. By embedding the half adder as a localmodule, synthesis tools can optimize effectively, merging benefits of modular design and compact logic.
