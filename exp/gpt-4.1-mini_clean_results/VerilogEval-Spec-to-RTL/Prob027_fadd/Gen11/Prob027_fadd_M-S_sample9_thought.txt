Since a full adder requires both sum and carry-out, the implementation must use at least two logic expressions. The current solution is already minimal using continuous assignments of XOR for sum and a majority logic for carry. Any further simplification risks losing clarity or introducing intermediate signals, which increases complexity. The shortest expression for sum is XOR of the three inputs, and the simplest carry-out is the standard majority logic. Thus, the original solution is already optimal in terms of simplicity, logic depth, and gate count. To slightly reduce code lines, combine assignments into a single assign with multiple outputs, but this is mainly cosmetic. Therefore, the best simplification is to keep the original concise form without comments and whitespace.
