Instead of using the standard XOR and majority carry logic, implement the full adder using a simple multiplexing approach. Use the carry-in as a selector to choose between two intermediate sums and carries computed from inputs a and b. When cin=0, sum and cout are directly a XOR b and a AND b; when cin=1, sum is the inverse of a XOR b and cout is a OR b. This approach reuses intermediate signals and exploits the properties of addition to compute sum and carry-out with fewer explicit XOR gates, offering an alternative structural style.
