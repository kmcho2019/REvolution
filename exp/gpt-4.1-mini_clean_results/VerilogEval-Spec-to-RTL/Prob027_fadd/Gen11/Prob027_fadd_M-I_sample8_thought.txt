To optimize the full adder for better power and area while maintaining performance and correctness, I will integrate the half adder logic directly into the full adder without instantiating separate modules. This inlining eliminates module boundaries and extra nets, reducing wire capacitance and dynamic power. The sum is computed as the XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out is computed as the majority function of the three inputs (carry generated when at least two inputs are high). This approach uses minimal logic gates and thus optimizes area and power without affecting timing.
