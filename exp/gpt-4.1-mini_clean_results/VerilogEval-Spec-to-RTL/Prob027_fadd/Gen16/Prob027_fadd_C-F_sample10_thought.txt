To create a superior full adder, directly implement the sum and carry-out logic using their minimal Boolean expressions rather than instantiating half adders. This approach eliminates intermediate nets and submodule overhead, reducing gate count and logic depth, which leads to improvements in area and power without sacrificing timing performance. The sum is the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out is the majority function (carry if at least two inputs are 1). This flattening of logic leads to the most efficient implementation for a single-bit full adder while preserving clarity and correctness.
