To optimize power and area while preserving functionality, I will inline the full adder logic directly inside TopModule rather than instantiating two half adders. This removes extra module boundaries, nets, and interconnect overhead, reducing capacitive loading and thus dynamic power. The critical path delay remains minimal because the logic is equivalent (two XORs and OR for carry). The design stays combinational and simple, with fewer wires and no module instantiations, leading to a more compact and potentially lower power implementation.
