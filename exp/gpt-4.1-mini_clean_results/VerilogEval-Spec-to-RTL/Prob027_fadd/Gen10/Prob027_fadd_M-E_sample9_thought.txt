Implement the full adder using a two-stage approach with two half-adders and one OR gate for the carry-out. The first half-adder adds inputs a and b, producing an intermediate sum and carry. The second half-adder adds the intermediate sum and the carry-in (cin), producing the final sum and a second carry. The carry-out is the OR of the two half-adder carry outputs. This modular structure reflects a common hierarchical approach in digital design, promoting clarity and reuse of half-adder logic.
