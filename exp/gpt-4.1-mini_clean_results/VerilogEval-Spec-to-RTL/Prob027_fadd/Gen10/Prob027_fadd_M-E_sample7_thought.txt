Implement the full adder as a single module using direct Boolean expressions for sum and carry-out. Use the minimal and canonical sum-of-products logic: sum = a XOR b XOR cin, and cout = majority(a,b,cin) implemented as (a&b) | (b&cin) | (cin&a). This approach uses no submodules, reducing overhead and potentially optimizing area and power by eliminating inter-module wiring. It provides a compact, straightforward, and fully combinational design.
