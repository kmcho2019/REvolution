Implement the full adder by structurally instantiating two half adders and one OR gate. Each half adder takes two inputs and produces a sum and carry. The first half adder adds inputs a and b, the second half adder adds the first half adder's sum and cin. Finally, the carry-out is obtained by OR-ing the carries from both half adders. This hierarchical approach uses modular reuse and clearly reflects the adders' architecture.
