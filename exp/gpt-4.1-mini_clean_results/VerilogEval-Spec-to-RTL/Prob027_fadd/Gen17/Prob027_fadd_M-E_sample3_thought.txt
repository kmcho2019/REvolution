Implement the full adder using a single always_comb block with behavioral Verilog for clarity and to centralize logic in one place. This approach simplifies debugging and can facilitate direct control of the logic flow. The carry-out is generated using the majority logic of the three inputs, and the sum is the XOR of all three inputs. This approach avoids intermediate modules and wires, and uses the inherent simplicity of combinational logic within an always block to express the full adder succinctly and clearly.
