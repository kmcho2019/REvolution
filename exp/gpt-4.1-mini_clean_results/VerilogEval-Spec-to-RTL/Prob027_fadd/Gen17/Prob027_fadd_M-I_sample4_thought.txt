To optimize for better power and area while maintaining performance, flatten the logic into a single combinational assignment instead of using two half adder modules. This reduces the total gate count by sharing XOR and AND logic directly, eliminating inter-module wiring overhead. The full adder sum is computed as XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out as majority function (carry if any two or more inputs are 1). This flattening reduces switching activity and area slightly by removing duplicated gates from the half adders.
