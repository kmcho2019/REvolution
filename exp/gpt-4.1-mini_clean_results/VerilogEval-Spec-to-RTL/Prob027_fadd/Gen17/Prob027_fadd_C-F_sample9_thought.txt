To create a superior full adder design, fuse the clarity and modularity of using a HalfAdder module with the efficiency and minimal logic overhead of direct combinational expressions in TopModule. Keep the HalfAdder module for reuse and readability, but inside TopModule implement the sum and carry-out directly using the full adder logic expressions without instantiating two HalfAdders and extra intermediate wires. This reduces gate count, critical path length, switching activity, and area, while maintaining modularity through the reusable HalfAdder. The sum can be implemented as XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out as the majority function (at least two inputs are high), which simplifies the logic and improves timing and power.
