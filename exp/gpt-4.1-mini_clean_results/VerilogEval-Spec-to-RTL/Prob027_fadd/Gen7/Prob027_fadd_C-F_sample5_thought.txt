Fuse the modular clarity of the hierarchical half adder approach with the area and power efficiency of the direct boolean implementation by integrating the half adder logic as inline code (without separate module instantiations) and sharing common intermediate signals to minimize redundant operations. Specifically, compute the intermediate XOR of a and b once, reuse it for sum and carry calculation (carry-out = (a & b) | (cin & (a ^ b))), and sum = (a ^ b) ^ cin. This approach preserves modular style clarity by using meaningful intermediate signals, reduces gate count and interconnect capacitances compared to separate module instantiations, and achieves optimal power, performance, and area.
