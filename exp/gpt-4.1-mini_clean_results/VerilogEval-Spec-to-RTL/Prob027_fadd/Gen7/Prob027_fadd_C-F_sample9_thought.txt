To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will implement the full adder with direct combinational logic expressions for sum and carry-out instead of hierarchical instantiation of half adders. This eliminates the overhead of extra modules and intermediate signals, reducing gate count, switching activity, and area, thus improving power and performance. The sum is computed as the XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out as the majority function (carry generated if any two or more inputs are high). This approach preserves correctness, minimizes logic levels, and is optimal for a single-bit full adder. It provides a more compact, clear, and efficient implementation.
