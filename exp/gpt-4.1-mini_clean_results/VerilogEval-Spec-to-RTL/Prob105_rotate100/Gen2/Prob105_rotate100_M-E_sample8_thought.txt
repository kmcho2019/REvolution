Implement the 100-bit rotator as a shift register with two intermediate 1-bit registers representing the bits shifted out from each side to be wrapped around in rotation. On each clock edge, if load is high, load data directly. Otherwise, based on ena: if rotate right, shift right by one, and feed the LSB back to MSB through the intermediate register; if rotate left, shift left by one, and feed the MSB back to LSB similarly. This method breaks the rotation into explicit single-bit shift and feedback registers, allowing more modularity and potentially easier timing optimization. The q output is driven by the main 100-bit register.
