To improve PPA, reduce unnecessary toggling and switching activity by updating the register only when load is high or a single-bit rotation is requested. Specifically, when ena=2'b00 or 2'b11 (no rotation), do not re-assign q, thus avoiding unnecessary toggles. This can be done by gating the register update condition. Also, combine the rotation logic with load to minimize logic depth and simplify the clock domain logic.

This approach reduces dynamic power by preventing redundant writes, may slightly improve timing by reducing combinational load during hold states, and keeps area minimal without complex barrel shifters. The functionality remains the same.
