Implement the 100-bit rotator using a shift register with feedback for rotation, controlled by a state machine-like logic inside the always block. Instead of computing rotate_left and rotate_right vectors explicitly, store the register content and perform the rotation directly in the always block by shifting bits and reinserting the rotated-out bit. On load, synchronously load the input data. On enable, shift either left or right with rotation by reassigning bits using a for-loop to reduce combinational logic external to the sequential block. If ena is 0 or 3, hold the register state. This approach minimizes the combinational logic outside the clocked process and uses iterative assignment inside the always block to simplify synthesis and potentially improve PPA.
