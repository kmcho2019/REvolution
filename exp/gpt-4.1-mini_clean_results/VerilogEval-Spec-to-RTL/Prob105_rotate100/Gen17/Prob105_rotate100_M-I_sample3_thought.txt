To optimize the existing design for better power and timing without changing functionality, apply clock gating to the 100-bit register to avoid unnecessary toggling when no load or rotation occurs (ena == 2'b00 or 2'b11 and load == 0).

Implement a simple clock enable (clk_en) signal that is asserted only when load is high or ena requests a rotation (2'b01 or 2'b10). This reduces switching activity in the flip-flops during idle cycles.

Since the rotation is only by one bit, the existing concatenation approach is efficient and concise. Adding clock enable doesn't add latency and improves power by gating register updates.

This approach provides a practical PPA improvement by lowering dynamic power and reducing toggle activity on the 100-bit register without increasing complexity or area noticeably.

Note: Some synthesis tools recognize synchronous enable and convert it to clock gating internally, but explicitly coding the enable improves clarity and encourages better tool optimization.
