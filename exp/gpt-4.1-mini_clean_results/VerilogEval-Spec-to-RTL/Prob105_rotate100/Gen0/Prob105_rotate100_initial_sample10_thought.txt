Implement a 100-bit register with synchronous load and rotate functionality. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load data into the register. Otherwise, check ena: if ena==2'b01, rotate right by one bit (the LSB moves to MSB position, and the other bits shift right); if ena==2'b10, rotate left by one bit (the MSB moves to LSB position, and other bits shift left). If ena is 2'b00 or 2'b11, hold the current value. Output the register contents on q.
