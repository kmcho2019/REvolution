Implement a 100-bit register with synchronous load and rotation controlled by a 2-bit enable signal. On the rising edge of clk, if load is high, load the 100-bit data input into the register. Otherwise, depending on ena, rotate left by one bit if ena==2'b10, rotate right by one bit if ena==2'b01, and hold the register unchanged for ena==2'b00 or ena==2'b11. Output q is the current contents of the register.
