Implement a 100-bit register that on the rising edge of clk either:
- loads the input data when load is high,
- or rotates left by one bit if ena==2'b10,
- or rotates right by one bit if ena==2'b01,
- or retains the current value if ena==2'b00 or ena==2'b11.

Rotation means taking the bit shifted out from one end and inserting it at the other end.
