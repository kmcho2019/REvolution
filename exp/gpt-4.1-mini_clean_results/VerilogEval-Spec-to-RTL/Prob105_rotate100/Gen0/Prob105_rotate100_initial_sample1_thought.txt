Implement a 100-bit register that on each rising clock edge either:
- Loads the input data when load is high,
- Rotates left by one bit when ena == 2'b10,
- Rotates right by one bit when ena == 2'b01,
- Holds its value otherwise (ena == 2'b00 or 2'b11).
The rotation means taking the bit shifted out on one side and putting it at the other side.
