To optimize the 100-bit rotator for better performance, power, and area, I will avoid using large concatenations inside the always block which can create a deep critical path. Instead, I will implement the rotation by wiring outputs to shifted register bits and use a simple enable gating on the clock to reduce unnecessary toggling when neither loading nor rotation occurs. This avoids recomputing q when no changes happen, thus saving dynamic power. Also, performing rotation with direct wiring logic (assignments) rather than concatenations can reduce LUT usage. I will keep a single clocked always block for clarity and synchronous operation, but add an internal enable signal derived from load or ena to gate the update. This improves power and possibly frequency without area penalty.
