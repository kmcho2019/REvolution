Implement the 100-bit rotate register using two 50-bit registers to split the wide register into halves. On each clock, depending on load or enable signals, update the halves accordingly. For rotate left, shift both halves left by one and move the MSB of the lower half into the LSB of the upper half, while the MSB of the upper half wraps around to the LSB of the lower half. Similarly, for rotate right, shift both halves right and wrap around the bits accordingly. This approach illustrates modularity and may aid synthesis tools to optimize large wide registers better. The two halves are concatenated to form the output q.
