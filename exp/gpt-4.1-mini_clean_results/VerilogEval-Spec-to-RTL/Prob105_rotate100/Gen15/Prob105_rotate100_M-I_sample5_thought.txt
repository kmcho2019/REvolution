To improve the design's performance, power, and area (PPA), I'll implement clock gating to disable the register update when neither load nor rotation is active, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power. This gating will prevent the register from updating if load=0 and ena is 00 or 11 (no rotation). Also, the rotation is by only one bit, so I'll keep the simple concatenation but introduce a clock enable signal to limit switching. This reduces power without adding significant complexity or latency. The overall structure remains a single always block for clarity and simplicity.
