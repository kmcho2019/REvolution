To optimize PPA, especially timing and power, I will reduce the rotation logic complexity by implementing the 100-bit rotation as two 50-bit rotations concatenated, thus splitting the critical path. Also, introducing a conditional clock enable with the ena and load signals combined to gate updates on q can reduce unnecessary toggles and save power.

By structuring the rotation logic into smaller parts and controlling updates only when load or ena indicates rotation, flip-flop switching activity and the critical path delay due to wide concatenation are reduced.

This approach maintains the same functionality but should yield better timing and power results while keeping area roughly the same.
