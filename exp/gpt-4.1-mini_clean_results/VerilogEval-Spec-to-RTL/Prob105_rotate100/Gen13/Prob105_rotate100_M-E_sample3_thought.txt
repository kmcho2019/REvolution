Implement the 100-bit rotator as a synchronous shift register with explicit left and right shift operations controlled by ena, and a synchronous load. Instead of calculating next_q combinationally using concatenations, perform rotation inside the clocked always block by explicitly assigning bits shifted with wrap-around. Prioritize load over rotation, and do nothing when no rotate is selected. This approach avoids a separate combinational next-state logic and uses direct bit manipulations inside the sequential logic to clearly show the state updates, and may be more intuitive for synthesis tools to map into shift register structures.
