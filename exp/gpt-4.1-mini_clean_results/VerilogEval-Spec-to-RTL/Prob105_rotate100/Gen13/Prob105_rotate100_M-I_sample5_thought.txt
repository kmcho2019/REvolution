To optimize PPA while maintaining clarity and correctness, replace the loop-based rotation with vector slicing and concatenation. Rotations by one bit can be implemented efficiently as:

- Rotate left: q <= {q[98:0], q[99]};
- Rotate right: q <= {q[0], q[99:1]};

This reduces combinational logic complexity and improves timing and area.

Add an enable signal to gate clocking and minimize toggling for no-operation cycles.

Thus:

- On load, q <= data.
- Else if ena == 2'b01, rotate right by one bit.
- Else if ena == 2'b10, rotate left by one bit.
- Else, hold q unchanged (no toggling).

This approach simplifies logic, reduces critical path, and likely lowers area and power.
