To optimize for power and performance, reduce unnecessary register toggling when no rotation or load occurs by gating the clock enable signal. Implement a clock enable (ce) signal that is high only when load=1 or ena=01 or ena=10, so the register updates only in those cases. This avoids redundant writes and switching when holding the value. This simple clock gating reduces dynamic power without changing the logic.

The rotation logic itself is already minimal and the one-bit rotate ensures manageable routing and delay. The gating improves power and may slightly improve timing by reducing toggle rates on registers.

Maintain the synchronous semantics with the gated update inside the always block using the enable signal.
