Refactor the design to use a sequential always block with a case statement on ena to choose rotation direction or hold, and with a synchronous load condition prior to rotation logic. This centralizes the next state logic inside the always block, improving readability and potentially helping synthesis tools optimize the mux selection. The rotate-left and rotate-right values will be computed inside the sequential block to avoid unnecessary combinational wires when not used. This structure replaces the parallel combinational mux logic with a clearer sequential control flow.
