- Remove the redundant assignment "q <= q;" to prevent unnecessary toggling and reduce power.
- Simplify logic by computing next_q combinationally only when load or rotation occurs; otherwise, keep q unchanged implicitly.
- Use a combinational always block to compute next_q and assign q <= next_q at clock edge, reducing register toggling and improving synthesis results.
- Implement rotation via vector concatenation which is efficient for synthesis on FPGA and ASIC, avoiding explicit per-bit loops.
- Prioritize load over rotation.
- Since ena=00 or 11 means no rotation, just hold state without assignment to avoid glitches and reduce power.
