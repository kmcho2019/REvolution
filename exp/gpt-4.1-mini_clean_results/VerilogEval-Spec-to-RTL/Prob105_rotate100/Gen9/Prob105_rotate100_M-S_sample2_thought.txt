Use vector concatenation to implement rotations instead of bit-by-bit indexing, simplifying the code and potentially improving synthesis results.

- On load, assign q <= data.
- For rotation right (ena = 2'b01), rotate by concatenating q[0] to the left and q[99:1] to the right.
- For rotation left (ena = 2'b10), rotate by concatenating q[98:0] to the left and q[99] to the right.
- For other ena values, hold q.

This reduces combinational complexity, improves readability, and synthesizes efficiently.
