To optimize the original design for power and potentially for timing, I will:

1. Implement clock gating internally by using an enable signal that is only active when load or rotation is required, to reduce unnecessary toggling.
2. Avoid redundant register writes by updating the register q only when load is high or a valid rotation (ena == 2'b01 or 2'b10) is requested.
3. Remove the explicit 'q <= q;' assignment in the no-operation case, allowing synthesis tools to infer that q remains unchanged.
4. Retain the simple concatenation-based rotation to keep the logic minimal and clean.
5. Add comments and use a local wire for gated clock enable for clarity.

This strategy reduces dynamic power consumption by clock gating the register when no action is needed, while preserving functional correctness and maintaining a clean and synthesizable design.
