To optimize the original 100-bit rotate register for better power and timing, reduce unnecessary register toggling by only updating the register when load or rotation is enabled. This avoids redundant assignments and switching activity when the register should hold its value. Instead of always assigning q in every clock cycle, gate the register update with a clock enable signal derived from load or ena indicating rotation. This reduces dynamic power and may improve timing by reducing mux complexity. The rotate logic remains the same using concatenations for minimal area. This optimization keeps the synchronous behavior and functionality identical but improves power and timing by avoiding unnecessary toggling.
