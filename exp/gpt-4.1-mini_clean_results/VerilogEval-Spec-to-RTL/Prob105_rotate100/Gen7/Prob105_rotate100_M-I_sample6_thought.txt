This avoids redundant toggling of the register bits when no operation is needed (ena=00 or 11). I will use a local wire 'rotate_enable' to hold this condition.

This reduces unnecessary switching activity inside the register, improving power.

The rotation logic remains implemented with simple concatenations to maintain clarity and avoid increasing area or latency with more complex barrel shifter stages.

The always block updates q only when load or rotate_enable is asserted; otherwise, q retains its previous value without assignment.

This simple conditional update improves power without affecting timing or area significantly, aligning with the reported PPA guidance.
