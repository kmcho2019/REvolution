To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will implement clock enable gating to update the register only when load or rotation is requested, thus reducing switching activity and power consumption. Instead of always computing both rotate_left and rotate_right, I will conditionally select the rotation based on ena, and only compute the relevant rotate vector to reduce unnecessary logic toggling. To further aid synthesis in optimizing multiplexers, I will use an explicit case statement for the 2-bit ena control. This reduces redundant logic and improves timing. Overall, this reduces toggling and area, improves timing by eliminating unnecessary mux inputs, and lowers power with clock gating.
