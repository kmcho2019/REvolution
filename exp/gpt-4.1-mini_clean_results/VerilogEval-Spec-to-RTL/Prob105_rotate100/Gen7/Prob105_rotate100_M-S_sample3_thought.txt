Implement the 100-bit rotator using vector concatenations for rotation instead of bitwise loops. On load, assign input data to the register. For rotate right by 1, assign q <= {q[0], q[99:1]}; for rotate left by 1, assign q <= {q[98:0], q[99]}; and for other ena values, hold q constant. This concise approach reduces combinational logic depth and simplifies synthesis without changing functionality. The entire logic remains synchronous and respects the enable and load signals.
