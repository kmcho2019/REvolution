To improve the PPA of the existing 100-bit rotator, I will optimize combinational logic and reduce muxing complexity without sacrificing clarity. Key improvements:

- Avoid generating full 100-bit rotated vectors every cycle to reduce switching and mux complexity.
- Use a one-hot style enable decoding to avoid nested conditional operators, simplifying the mux structure.
- Combine rotate-left and rotate-right wiring into a single mux by leveraging bit-selective wiring rather than explicit wide vectors.
- Implement clock gating on the register with update_en to reduce toggling in flip-flops.
- Maintain straightforward always block for clarity but simplify combinational logic for next_q by using direct bit-select assignments.

These changes reduce the number of wide intermediate signals, minimize overall muxing complexity, and allow synthesis tools to better optimize routing and logic levels. This should improve timing (lower critical path), reduce switching power from combinational muxes, and modestly reduce area by avoiding duplicated rotate vectors.

The logic maintains the same functionality:
- On load, data is loaded.
- On ena=2'b10 rotate left by 1 (MSB <- LSB).
- On ena=2'b01 rotate right by 1 (LSB <- MSB).
- Otherwise hold current state.

This approach balances readability and improved PPA.
