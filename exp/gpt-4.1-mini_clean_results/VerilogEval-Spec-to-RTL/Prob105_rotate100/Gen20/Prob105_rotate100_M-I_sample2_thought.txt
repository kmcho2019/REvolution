To improve the initial design's power and area efficiency without sacrificing timing, we can introduce a clock enable signal to update the register only when loading or rotating occurs. This gating prevents unnecessary toggling in the flip-flops when the data remains unchanged, thus reducing dynamic power. Also, to avoid calculating both rotate_left and rotate_right unconditionally (which generates redundant switching), compute these only when their respective ena bit is active. This conditional calculation is done combinationally with multiplexers selecting zero vectors otherwise, effectively gating the rotate logic and reducing switching. The mux for next_value then selects load, rotate_left, rotate_right, or hold accordingly. This approach maintains the simple synchronous design, keeps minimal logic complexity, and reduces power and area by avoiding unnecessary updates and rotations.
