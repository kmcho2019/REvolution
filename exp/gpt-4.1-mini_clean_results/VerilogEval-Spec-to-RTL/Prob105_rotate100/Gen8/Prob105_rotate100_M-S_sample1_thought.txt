Use vector concatenations to perform rotations, which is simpler and maps to efficient hardware. On load, synchronously load the data input. On rotate right (ena=01), assign q to {q[0], q[99:1]} which rotates bits right by one. On rotate left (ena=10), assign q to {q[98:0], q[99]} rotating bits left by one. For ena=00 or 11, hold q. This approach avoids loops and conditional indexing, resulting in simpler, more efficient RTL that synthesizes to fast, low-power logic.
