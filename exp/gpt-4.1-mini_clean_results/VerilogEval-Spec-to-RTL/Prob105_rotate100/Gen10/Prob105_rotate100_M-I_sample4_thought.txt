To optimize the existing design for better power and performance without altering functionality, insert a conditional check to update the register q only if load is asserted or if ena selects rotation (01 or 10). This approach keeps the synchronous load and rotation logic in one always block with a clean case statement, maintaining clarity and correctness. The rotation logic uses simple concatenations, which synthesis tools efficiently handle for a single-bit rotation in a wide bus.
