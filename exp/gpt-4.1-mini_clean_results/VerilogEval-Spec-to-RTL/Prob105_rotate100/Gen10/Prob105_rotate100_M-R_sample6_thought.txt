Refactor the design by separating the next-state combinational logic from the state register update. Use an intermediate wire to compute the next value of q based on load and ena, then assign q synchronously on clk's rising edge. This approach enhances code readability by clearly distinguishing combinational and sequential parts, and is a common best practice in synchronous design.
