Implement a synchronous FSM with a reset state A. On reset, FSM is in A. On release of reset, next clock sets f=1 for one cycle, then FSM waits for x sequence 1,0,1 over three successive cycles. Use a small state machine or counter to detect the x pattern. Once pattern detected, set g=1 next cycle and move to monitoring y for up to two cycles. If y=1 occurs within two cycles, keep g=1 permanently, else clear g permanently until reset. All outputs are registered, outputs f and g are zero by default except when asserted by FSM logic.
