Design an FSM with states representing the sequence of detecting reset, asserting f=1 for one cycle, monitoring the x input for the 1-0-1 pattern, then setting g=1 on the cycle after the pattern. After g=1 is set, enter a state to monitor y for up to two cycles. If y=1 within two cycles, keep g=1 permanently; otherwise, clear g=0 permanently. The FSM resets to state A on synchronous active-low resetn asserted. Outputs f and g depend on states. All state changes occur on posedge clk with synchronous reset.
