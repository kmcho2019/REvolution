Implement a synchronous FSM with the described behavior and synchronous active low reset. The states are:

- A: initial/reset state, outputs f=0, g=0
- B: after reset deasserted and one clock edge, f=1 for one cycle, then move to monitor x sequence
- C0, C1, C2, C3: states to detect the 1-0-1 pattern on x
- D: g=1, monitor y for two clock cycles
- E: g=1 permanently (y=1 seen within two cycles)
- F: g=0 permanently (y=1 not seen within two cycles)

We will implement state transitions based on input and counters for monitoring cycles.

Outputs f and g depend on the current state.

Use an enumerated type or localparam states for clarity.
