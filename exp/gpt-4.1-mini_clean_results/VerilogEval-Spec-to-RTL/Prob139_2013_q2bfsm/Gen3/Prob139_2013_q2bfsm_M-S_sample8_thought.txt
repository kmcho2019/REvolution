Implement a straightforward FSM with clearly defined states representing each step, including a three-step sequence detector for x and a two-cycle y monitor, while keeping outputs registered for stable timing. Use a small state machine with states: A (reset state), F_PULSE (output f=1 one cycle after reset), X1, X10, X101 (tracking the x=1,0,1 sequence progressively), MONITOR_Y (g=1 and monitoring y for up to 2 cycles), G_ON (permanent g=1), and G_OFF (permanent g=0). On reset (active low, synchronous), go to A. On release, pulse f=1 one cycle (F_PULSE), then start sequence detection. Update outputs only on clock edges (registered outputs). This simple FSM avoids shift registers and counters by encoding sequence progression in states and uses a 2-bit counter for y-monitoring duration. The outputs depend only on current state. This reduces complexity, improves timing, and ensures correct sequence detection and output timing.
