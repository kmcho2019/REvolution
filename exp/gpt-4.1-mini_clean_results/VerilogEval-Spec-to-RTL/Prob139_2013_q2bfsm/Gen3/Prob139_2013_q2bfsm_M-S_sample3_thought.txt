Simplify the FSM by encoding states minimally and reducing the number of states used for sequence detection and y monitoring. Use clear, sequential states as follows:

- S_A: reset state, hold until resetn=1  
- S_B: output f=1 for one cycle immediately after reset release  
- S_SEQ0, S_SEQ1, S_SEQ2: detect the sequence x=1,0,1 in three consecutive cycles  
- S_MON: output g=1 and monitor y input for at most 2 cycles  
- S_G1: g=1 permanently  
- S_G0: g=0 permanently

Use a 2-bit counter only in S_MON to track the two cycles of y monitoring. Increment counter only in S_MON and reset in other states. Outputs depend solely on the current state (no outputs driven by next_state). This reduces complexity and state duplication. The simplified states and logic make the FSM easier to verify and maintain while fulfilling all functional requirements.

All sequential logic triggered on posedge clk with synchronous active-low reset. Counter and outputs reset appropriately on reset.
