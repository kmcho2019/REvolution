Use a Mealy-type FSM approach with a shift register to detect the x=1,0,1 pattern and with an explicit two-cycle timer for y monitoring. After reset release, pulse f for one cycle immediately. Then shift in x values on each clock and check if the last three bits equal 3'b101 to trigger g pulse for one cycle. After g pulse, start a two-cycle counter to monitor y input: if y=1 within two cycles, keep g asserted permanently; otherwise, clear g permanently. Outputs f and g are registered and updated directly in the clocked process for precise timing. Synchronous active-low reset controls FSM state and outputs. This approach simplifies pattern detection with a shift register and uses counters for timing, making the FSM states simpler and logic clearer.
