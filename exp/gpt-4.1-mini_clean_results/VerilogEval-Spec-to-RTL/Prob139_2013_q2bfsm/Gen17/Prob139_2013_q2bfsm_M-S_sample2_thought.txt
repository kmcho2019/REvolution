Simplify the FSM by combining pattern detection and state transitions directly in the sequential logic, removing the combinational next_state block. Use a simple one-hot style or binary encoded state machine with minimal registers: a 2-bit shift register to track the x input sequence for detecting pattern 101, and a 2-bit counter for monitoring y. Outputs are purely registered and assigned in the sequential block. Synchronous reset sets FSM to state A. After reset release, pulse f for one cycle, then start monitoring x pattern. When detected, pulse g for one cycle, then monitor y for up to two cycles. Depending on y during that time, hold g high or low permanently. This approach eliminates next state combinational logic and potential timing mismatches, making the FSM easier to understand and verify.
