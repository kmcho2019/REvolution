Implement a simple FSM with clearly separated states and synchronous active-low reset. Use a straightforward encoding of states to track progress:  
- STATE_A: wait for reset release, keep outputs low  
- STATE_B: pulse f=1 for one cycle  
- STATE_C: monitor x for pattern "101" using a 3-bit shift register updated every cycle  
- STATE_D: pulse g=1 for one cycle after pattern detected  
- STATE_E: hold g=1, monitor y for up to 2 cycles with a small counter; if y=1 within 2 cycles, go to STATE_F (g=1 permanently), else after 2 cycles to STATE_G (g=0 permanently)  
- STATE_F: g=1 permanently  
- STATE_G: g=0 permanently  

By using a 3-bit shift register for x pattern detection, and a 2-bit counter for y monitoring, the logic becomes clear and easy to verify. All outputs are registered synchronously. Transition between states depends on inputs and counters. Reset synchronously clears all registers and sets FSM in STATE_A.

This simple, direct FSM with explicit state and output assignments should meet the requirements and avoid timing or sequencing errors.
