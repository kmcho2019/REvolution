Implement a simple FSM with these states:

- A: reset asserted, outputs f=0, g=0.
- B: one cycle after reset release, output f=1.
- C0, C1, C2: sequentially track x pattern 1,0,1 respectively.
- D: g=1 pulse one cycle after detecting pattern.
- E0, E1: two cycles monitoring y while g=1.
- F: hold g=1 permanently after detecting y=1 in time.
- G: hold g=0 permanently if y=1 not detected in time.

Use clear states to track pattern detection step-by-step rather than shifting bits. This removes the shift register and simplifies transitions.

Synchronously reset with active low resetn.

Outputs f and g are Moore outputs, depend only on current state.

This approach reduces complexity and state bits while preserving the required behavior and timing.
