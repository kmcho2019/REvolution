- Use a Moore FSM style: outputs depend only on the current state (registered state), not next_state.
- Make `f` and `g` outputs registered, driven directly from the current state.
- Update auxiliary counters (`pattern_step`, `y_count`) synchronously, based only on current state and inputs, and reset them appropriately.
