To simplify the FSM while preserving all functionality:

- Use a small set of states reflecting the key phases: RESET (state A), F_PULSE, MONITOR_X, MONITOR_Y, G_ON_PERM, G_OFF_PERM.
- Merge pattern_step and y_count as simple counters tracked only when needed, updated synchronously.
- Use one always_ff (sequential) block for state and counters with synchronous reset.
- Use one combinational always_comb block for next_state logic, relying only on registered signals.
- Use Moore outputs derived purely from current state.
- This clear separation and minimal state encoding reduces complexity, making the logic easier to verify and understand.
- This approach avoids multiple nested case statements and complicated pattern-step logic by simplifying pattern detection to a straightforward progression through expected x inputs.
- Counters (pattern_step and y_count) are reset outside their monitoring states to keep logic clean.
- Maintain the synchronous, active-low reset behavior.

This simplification keeps the same behavior but with fewer states and simpler logic, easing debugging and reducing risk of mismatches.
