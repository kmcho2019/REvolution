Implement the FSM using an explicit sequence detector submodule for the pattern 1-0-1 on input x, combined with a main controller FSM.

Key differences from the original approach:
- Use a Mealy-style sequence detector for detecting the pattern 101 on x, which outputs a detection pulse one cycle after full pattern matched.
- Main FSM states:
  - S_RESET: stays here while resetn=0.
  - S_F_PULSE: after resetn=1, output f=1 for one cycle.
  - S_WAIT_G: wait until the sequence detector signals detection of pattern 101 on x; then output g=1 for one cycle.
  - S_MONITOR_Y: keep g=1; monitor y for at most two cycles.
    - If y=1 detected within two cycles, stay forever with g=1.
    - Else after two cycles, go to S_G0_PERM.
  - S_G0_PERM: output g=0 forever until reset.

Advantages:
- Clean separation of pattern detection and output control.
- Sequence detector produces a pulse for pattern detection, easing main FSM logic.
- Timing of f and g signals controlled clearly on dedicated states.
- y monitoring uses a counter and clear transitions.

All logic synchronous with positive clock edge; resetn synchronous active low.

Outputs f and g are registered.

This modular approach clarifies design and ensures correct timing and functional behavior.
