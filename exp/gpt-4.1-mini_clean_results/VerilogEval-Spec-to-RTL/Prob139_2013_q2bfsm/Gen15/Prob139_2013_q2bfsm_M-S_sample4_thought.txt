- On synchronous active-low reset asserted, stay in RESET with f=0,g=0.

- After reset deasserted, go to PULSE_F state and assert f=1 for exactly one cycle.

- Then start monitoring x input every cycle, shifting in new x bits into a 3-bit shift register.

- When the shift register matches pattern 101, go to PULSE_G state and assert g=1 for one cycle.

- Then move to MONITOR_Y state, where g=1 and count up to 2 cycles, waiting for y=1.

- If y=1 detected within two cycles, move to G_ON (g=1 permanently), else to G_OFF (g=0 permanently).

- All outputs are Moore type, driven only by state.

- Update shift register and counters only in relevant states; reset them properly.

- Keep logic sequential, simple and clear, ensuring proper one-cycle pulses and permanent outputs until reset.
