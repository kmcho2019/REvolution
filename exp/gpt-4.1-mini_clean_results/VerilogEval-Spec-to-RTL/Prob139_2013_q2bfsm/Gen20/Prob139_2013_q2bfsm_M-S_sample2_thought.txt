Simplify the FSM by focusing on clear states reflecting each functional phase and explicitly tracking progress:

- Use a 4-state FSM: 
  - A: Reset (f=0, g=0)
  - B: Output f=1 for exactly one clock cycle right after reset is released
  - C: Monitor x for the 101 pattern by tracking pattern progress with a 2-bit counter (0 to 3)
  - D: Once pattern detected, set g=1 for one clock cycle, then move to E
  - E: Monitor y for at most two cycles while g=1. If y=1 within two cycles, stay in a permanent "g=1" state F; else after two cycles, move to permanent "g=0" state G
  - F: permanent g=1
  - G: permanent g=0

- Use a small pattern progress counter to track matching the 101 sequence step-by-step instead of shifting bits.

- Use a separate y_timer counter limited to two cycles.

- Outputs f and g are Moore style (purely combinational from states).

- Reset is synchronous active low: when resetn=0, state is forced to A, counters cleared.

This reduces complexity, avoids shift registers, and makes pattern detection and output timing explicit and simpler.
