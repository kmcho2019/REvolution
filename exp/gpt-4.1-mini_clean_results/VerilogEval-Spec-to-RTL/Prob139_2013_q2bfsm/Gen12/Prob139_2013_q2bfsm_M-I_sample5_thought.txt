To fix the elaboration errors and improve compatibility with all Verilog tools and simulators, remove the SystemVerilog-specific enum and typedef constructs and replace them with classic Verilog parameter definitions for states. Declare the state registers as simple reg with enough bit width (at least 4 bits to cover 10 states). This ensures standard Verilog compliance and tool compatibility.

The functional FSM design remains unchanged, so the behavior stays identical and correct. The outputs remain purely Moore style, preserving glitch-free timing.

This also slightly reduces complexity in some tools and eases synthesis, potentially improving area and timing marginally by avoiding enum overhead or tool-specific translation steps.

Thus, the main improvement is portability and compatibility by switching from SV enum to classic parameter state encoding, while retaining the functional FSM design exactly as before.
