Fuse the clarity and succinctness of the Verilog built-in bitwise XNOR operator (~^) with the explicitness of the continuous assign statement driving a wire output. This combined approach uses the minimal and clear operator form (`a ~^ b`) for XNOR, ensuring the design is easy to understand and directly synthesizable with minimal logic. Using continuous assignment avoids unnecessary reg declarations or always blocks, keeping the module purely combinational and optimal for PPA.
