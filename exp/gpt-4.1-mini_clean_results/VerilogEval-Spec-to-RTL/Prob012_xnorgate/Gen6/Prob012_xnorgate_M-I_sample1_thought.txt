The original design is already optimal at the RTL level for an XNOR gate using a direct combinational expression. The expression `~(a ^ b)` is minimal and maps directly to a single XNOR gate in hardware. Any further RTL-level optimization is not possible since XNOR is a primitive operation. 

One possible very minor stylistic improvement is to use the Verilog built-in XNOR operator `^~` or `~^` to make the intention clearer. This might help synthesis tools directly infer an XNOR gate without the inversion of XOR. It doesn't reduce area or power significantly, but it can make the RTL more idiomatic.

Hence, the improvement strategy is to use the Verilog XNOR operator `~^` for clarity and possibly slightly better synthesis recognition.
