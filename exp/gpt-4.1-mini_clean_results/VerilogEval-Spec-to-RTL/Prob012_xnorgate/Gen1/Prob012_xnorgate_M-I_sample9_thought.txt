The existing solution is already minimal and optimal for a simple XNOR gate at the RTL level. However, for marginal power and area improvement, we can use the Verilog built-in XNOR operator (a ~^ b) instead of inverting the XOR. This can allow synthesis tools to better recognize the operation and optimize gate-level mapping. The logical equivalence is maintained, but the use of a single XNOR operator may yield slightly improved synthesis results.
