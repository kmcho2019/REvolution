Implement the XNOR function in TopModule by using only basic gates: two inverters, two AND gates, and one OR gate, based on the Boolean expression for XNOR: (A AND B) OR (NOT A AND NOT B). This gate-level approach demonstrates a fundamental structural composition of XNOR, avoids the direct use of Verilog's ~^ operator, and promotes understanding of the underlying logic. It also flattens hierarchy by implementing everything within TopModule without submodules.
