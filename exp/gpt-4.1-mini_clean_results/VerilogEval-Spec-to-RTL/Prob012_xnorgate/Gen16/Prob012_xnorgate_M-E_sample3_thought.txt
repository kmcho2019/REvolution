Implement the XNOR gate using only basic gates and structural Verilog without using the Verilog built-in ~^ operator. Specifically, use two XOR gates and one inverter to realize XNOR: out = ~(a ^ b). This illustrates gate-level design and helps understand how XNOR can be decomposed structurally, which might be useful for FPGA mapping or educational purposes. The design uses an intermediate wire for the XOR result and then inverts it.
