Fuse the clarity and simplicity of using a direct continuous assignment with the structured top-level module interface. Instead of multiple gate modules (Example 1) or an extra instantiated XNOR module (Example 2), directly implement the XNOR logic in the TopModule using a simple assign statement: `assign out = ~(a ^ b);`. This approach leverages the built-in operator for minimal logic, optimal PPA, zero extra hierarchy, and maintains clarity by putting the core logic right in the top module without external module dependencies. This fusion reduces area and power by eliminating unnecessary modules and wires, improves performance by flattening the design, and keeps the design straightforward and easily understandable.
