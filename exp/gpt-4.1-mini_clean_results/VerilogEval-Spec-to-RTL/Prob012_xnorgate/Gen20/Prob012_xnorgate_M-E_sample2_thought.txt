Implement the XNOR gate by first XOR-ing inputs a and b using basic Boolean expressions and then inverting the XOR result. The XOR can be expressed as (a & ~b) | (~a & b), and the XNOR is simply the negation of XOR. This approach uses only basic operators (&, |, ~) without instantiating individual gate modules, and directly implements the logic in a single assignment for minimal logic levels and area.
