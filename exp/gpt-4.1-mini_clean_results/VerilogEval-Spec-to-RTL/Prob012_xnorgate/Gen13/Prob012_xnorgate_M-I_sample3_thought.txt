To optimize for even better power and area, we eliminate the submodule instantiation to reduce routing overhead and hierarchy, thereby simplifying the synthesized netlist. The XNOR logic is directly implemented in TopModule using the built-in ~^ operator for minimal logic depth and gate count. This reduces net capacitance and slightly improves timing by having one less module boundary. The resulting design remains fully combinational, clean, and minimal, achieving the same functionality with marginally better PPA.
