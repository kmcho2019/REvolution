Fuse the clarity and simplicity of directly using the built-in XNOR operator in a single, non-parameterized module for minimal area and power, with the modularity and readability of a small dedicated XNOR submodule (without parameterization) that focuses solely on 1-bit operation. This eliminates overhead from parameterization and multiple gate instantiations, reduces logic depth to one level (direct XNOR), and maintains modular structure for clean code and potential reusability without sacrificing PPA. The TopModule then instantiates this 1-bit XNOR submodule, balancing code reuse, optimal PPA, and maintainability.
