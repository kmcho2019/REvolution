Implement the XNOR function by explicitly constructing it from basic gates (AND, OR, NOT) rather than using the built-in XNOR operator. This design uses the identity that X XNOR Y = (A AND B) OR (NOT A AND NOT B). This approach illustrates how to build XNOR functionality purely from basic primitives, which might be useful for environments where XNOR operator is unsupported or for educational purposes to show internal logic decomposition.
