The original solution already implements the XNOR gate in the most efficient and straightforward manner using a direct continuous assignment with the Verilog ~^ operator. This yields minimal area, minimal delay, and minimal power consumption. Any module instantiation would add unnecessary overhead.

To improve power even further, given that this is a simple combinational gate, there are no internal modifications possible. However, adding synthesis directives or attributes to encourage the synthesis tool to map this directly to a primitive XNOR gate can help ensure optimal hardware mapping, especially in complex flows.

Thus, the improvement here is to add a synthesis attribute (`(* keep = "true" *)`) to the output to hint the tool to preserve the logic as-is without optimization or restructuring, which can ensure that the gate remains a single XNOR primitive, potentially aiding tools in timing and power optimization.

No structural changes are made, preserving minimal area and delay. This is a minor but practical enhancement for quality of results in typical ASIC/FPGA flows.

No added complexity or modularity is introduced since it does not provide PPA benefits here.
