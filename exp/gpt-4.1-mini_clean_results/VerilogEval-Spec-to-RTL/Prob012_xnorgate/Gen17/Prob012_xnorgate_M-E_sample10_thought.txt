Implement the TopModule without any sub-module instantiation by expressing the XNOR functionality through structural composition of basic logic gates. Specifically, use an XOR gate constructed from NAND gates and then invert its output to obtain the XNOR result. This leverages fundamental gates, demonstrating how complex logic can be built from primitives. This approach is purely structural, avoids built-in operators, and serves as a clear educational example of constructing an XNOR from NAND gates while keeping the design minimal and fully self-contained within TopModule.
