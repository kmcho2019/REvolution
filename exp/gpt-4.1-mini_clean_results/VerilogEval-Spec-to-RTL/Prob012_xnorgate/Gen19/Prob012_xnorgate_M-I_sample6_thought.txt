The current design is already optimal in terms of simplicity, area, and performance since it uses the built-in XNOR operator directly. The PPA metrics indicate no timing slack issues, minimal power, and minimal area usage. Given the trivial nature of the circuit, further optimization within this module is neither practical nor meaningful.

However, a minor code clarity improvement is to declare ports with explicit directions in the module header (using Verilog-2001 style). This does not affect PPA but aligns with modern coding practices, improving readability and maintainability.

Thus, the improvement strategy is to:
- Keep the direct continuous assignment of the XNOR operation for minimal logic and power.
- Use ANSI-C style port declarations for clarity.
- Keep the reusable xnor_gate module commented for modular reuse.
No changes to logic or structure, as it’s already minimal and efficient.
