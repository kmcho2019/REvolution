Instead of using the built-in XNOR operator, implement the XNOR function using basic logic gates: AND, OR, and NOT. The XNOR of two inputs a and b can be expressed as (a AND b) OR (NOT a AND NOT b). This approach decomposes the XNOR gate into fundamental gates, providing a clear structural RTL description that can be beneficial for educational purposes or when targeting synthesis flows favoring explicit gate-level constructs.
