The initial solution implements the XNOR function using XOR followed by inversion, which is minimal and clear at RTL level. The PPA metrics show near-zero delay and minimal power and area, indicating optimal logic at this abstraction. There is no meaningful further RTL-level optimization possible without changing the logic representation. Using Verilog's built-in XNOR operator (^~) is a stylistic improvement that directly expresses the intended operation, potentially aiding readability and synthesis tools, but functionally equivalent.
