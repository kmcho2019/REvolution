Fuse the best ideas by directly using the built-in XNOR operator in a simple, concise continuous assignment without intermediate wires or modules. This combines the clarity and simplicity of a direct behavioral approach with the minimal logic depth and gate count of the primitive XNOR operation, improving Power, Performance, and Area over both prior examples. The design is succinct, synthesizable, and maps efficiently to optimized XNOR gates in silicon, thus offering the best PPA trade-off while retaining clear and maintainable code.
