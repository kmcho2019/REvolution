Implement the XNOR gate using a purely structural approach by decomposing the XNOR function into basic gates AND, OR, and NOT without using XOR or direct XNOR operators. Recall that XNOR can be expressed as (a AND b) OR (NOT a AND NOT b). By instantiating AND, OR, and NOT gates, this approach illustrates building XNOR with fundamental logic primitives in a different configuration than the XOR-then-NOT structure.
