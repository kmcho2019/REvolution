To implement an XNOR gate differently, I will create it using basic gates without directly using the Verilog XNOR operator. Specifically, I will use two AND gates, two NOT gates, and one OR gate to construct the XNOR function based on the expression: out = (a AND b) OR (NOT a AND NOT b). This provides a structural approach to the XNOR functionality that is clear and demonstrates gate-level composition.
