To optimize for better timing (tns/wns) and slightly simplify the clock path, remove the clock enable (ce) signal from the state register update so that the state updates unconditionally on every clock edge (unless asynchronously reset). This reduces clock enable logic and related clock gating delays, simplifying the clock path and helping timing closure. Although this may slightly increase power due to toggling on some cycles, the FSM is simple and toggles infrequently, so power impact is minimal. Maintaining the same minimal 1-bit state encoding and clearly separated combinational next-state logic preserves clarity and area optimality. Keep asynchronous active-high reset as specified. Output remains purely combinational from current state (Moore). This trade-off favors better performance and timing while maintaining low area and reasonable power.
