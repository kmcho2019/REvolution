Implement the two-state Moore FSM using a single flip-flop to hold the state bit, but integrate the output directly as the state signal itself (since output=1 in ON and 0 in OFF). Use synchronous reset instead of asynchronous to simplify timing and improve robustness. Combine next state logic inside the sequential always block to reduce combinational logic outside and keep output as a continuous assignment driven by the state flip-flop. This design leverages direct mapping of output to state and synchronous reset to offer an alternative clean and concise FSM implementation.
