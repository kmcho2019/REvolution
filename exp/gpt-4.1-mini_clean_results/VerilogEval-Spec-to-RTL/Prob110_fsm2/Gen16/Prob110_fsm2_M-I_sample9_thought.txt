To optimize the existing design for better PPA, I will separate the output logic from the clocked process and make 'out' purely combinational and directly driven by the registered state. This avoids extra registers and reduces toggling and glitches on 'out' by directly reflecting the Moore output from the stable state register. The state encoding remains minimal with one bit, and next_state logic stays simple. I will keep asynchronous reset as required. This reduces output logic complexity and slight power usage while maintaining correctness and timing.
