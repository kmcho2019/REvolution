To improve performance and clarity, I will separate the next-state combinational logic from the state register update. This allows synthesis tools to better optimize timing by isolating combinational and sequential parts. For power efficiency, I will add a clock enable signal that activates only when the next state differs from the current state to avoid unnecessary state toggling and reduce switching activity. Area impact is minimal because adding a clock enable involves a simple combinational comparison and does not add registers or complex logic. This approach maintains the asynchronous reset and the Moore FSM semantics while enhancing PPA metrics.
