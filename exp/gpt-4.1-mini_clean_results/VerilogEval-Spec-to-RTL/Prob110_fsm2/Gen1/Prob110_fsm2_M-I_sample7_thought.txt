To optimize PPA, I will simplify the design by replacing the typedef enum with a single-bit reg for state, since we only have two states, reducing synthesis complexity slightly. Also, I will combine next state and output logic into one combinational block using nonblocking assignments to avoid any combinational loops or mismatches, and minimize combinational logic stages. This can slightly reduce area and improve timing. Clock gating is not applied here due to simplicity. The asynchronous reset is kept as required.
