The original design is already minimal with a single flip-flop for state and direct output assignment, which is near optimal. However, to slightly improve timing and power, I will remove the asynchronous reset and replace it with a synchronous reset. This reduces asynchronous reset-related timing complexities and improves timing closure in most flows.

I will keep the state encoded as a single bit and keep the combinational logic for next state calculation separate for clarity and easier timing optimization by synthesis tools.

Additionally, removing the default case in the state machine will reduce any potential extra logic synthesis infers, slightly improving area.

This synchronous reset approach reduces potential metastability and can reduce power by avoiding asynchronous reset glitches, thus improving overall PPA.

The output remains a direct reflection of the state as before.
