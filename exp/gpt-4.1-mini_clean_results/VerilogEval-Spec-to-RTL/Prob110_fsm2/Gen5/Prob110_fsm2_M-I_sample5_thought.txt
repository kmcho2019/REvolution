To optimize the design for better PPA while maintaining the same functionality, I will convert the asynchronous reset into a synchronous reset. Synchronous reset generally improves timing robustness (better TNS/WNS) and can reduce glitches and power caused by asynchronous resets. Also, the state encoding is already minimal with a single bit; hence no change is needed there. I will keep non-blocking assignments and maintain the Moore output logic as it is. This slight change should enhance timing and power with minimal or no impact on area.
