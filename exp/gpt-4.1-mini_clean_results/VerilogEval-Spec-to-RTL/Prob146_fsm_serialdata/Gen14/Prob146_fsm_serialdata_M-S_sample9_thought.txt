Use a simpler FSM with four states encoded as 2 bits, but reduce the control complexity by merging bit count and shifting into one always block with an enable controlled by the state. Shift the incoming bits LSB first by shifting the shift register right and putting the new bit in MSB. Reset synchronously clears all registers. Done pulses high for one cycle after a valid stop bit is detected. On invalid stop bit, simply stay in a recovery state waiting for the line to return to idle (high). The design avoids multiple always blocks where possible, minimizing logic and keeping all sequential logic clean and straightforward.
