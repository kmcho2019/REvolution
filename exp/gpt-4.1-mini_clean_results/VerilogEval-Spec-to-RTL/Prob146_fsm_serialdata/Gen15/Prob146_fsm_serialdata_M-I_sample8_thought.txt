To improve power, performance, and area (PPA), I will switch from one-hot to binary state encoding, which reduces flip-flop count and routing complexity. I'll simplify the shift register to shift right, directly capturing the LSB first without bit reordering, which reduces combinational logic complexity. The bit counter increment is gated only during READ_BITS to minimize toggling outside active reception. The done pulse is generated cleanly and synchronized. These changes reduce area and dynamic power and potentially improve timing by shortening combinational paths, while maintaining the required FSM behavior and interface.
