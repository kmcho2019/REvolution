To optimize the given design for better PPA, the key improvements are:

1. Add clock enables to registers so that data_shift and bit_count only update when needed (during RECEIVE state). This reduces switching activity and power consumption.

2. Avoid unnecessary assignments in IDLE and WAIT_STOP states to minimize toggling in data_shift and bit_count registers.

3. Use non-blocking assignments consistently and keep the done signal as a one-clock-cycle pulse.

4. Keep state encoding the same (2-bit binary) since the FSM is small and simple, minimizing area.

5. Minor simplification in always blocks to reduce logic and improve synthesis efficiency.

These changes will reduce power by gating register updates, slightly reduce logic area, and potentially improve timing by simplifying transitions without changing functionality.

The FSM logic and outputs remain the same. The design still correctly detects start, receives 8 LSB-first bits, checks stop bit, asserts done, and handles bad stop bit with WAIT_STOP.

This approach retains correctness while optimizing dynamic power and area.
