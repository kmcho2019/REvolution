To optimize the existing FSM design for better power and potentially timing, I will add clock enables to registers so that they update only when needed. This will reduce unnecessary switching in IDLE and WAIT_STOP states, thereby lowering dynamic power consumption. 

Specifically:

- Update `bit_count` and `data_shift` only in RECEIVE state.
- Update `out_byte` only in STOP state on correct stop bit.
- Generate `done` synchronously for exactly one cycle when a byte is done.
- Keep the FSM and next-state logic unchanged since the original was simple and efficient.
- This approach reduces register toggling during idle or waiting states without changing functional behavior or FSM structure.

These incremental changes keep the clean design and improve power with minimal logic overhead.
