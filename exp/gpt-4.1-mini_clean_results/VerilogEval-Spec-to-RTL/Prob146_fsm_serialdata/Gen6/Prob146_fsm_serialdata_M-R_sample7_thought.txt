Refactor the design by splitting the FSM into separate combinational next-state logic and sequential state registers, improving timing and clarity. Implement a Moore-style FSM: the state register updates on the clock's positive edge with synchronous reset, and the next state is computed combinationally. Similarly, outputs `done` and `out_byte` are registered and updated only when valid, reducing unnecessary toggling of `done`. This separation of concerns can ease synthesis optimization and potentially improve timing and power, while keeping the core functionality identical.
