To improve PPA, I will adopt one-hot FSM encoding to simplify next-state decoding and improve timing, trading off some area for faster transitions. I will refactor the done signal to assert only for one cycle exactly on the stop bit detection and hold it until the next start bit, reducing unnecessary toggling. The shift register will shift LSB first explicitly by shifting right and inserting the new bit at MSB, matching the protocol and making the shift logic clearer and possibly more efficient. I will separate the bit counter and shift register update to update only during READ_BITS state, minimizing switching. Also, I'll keep reset synchronous and active-high. This approach aims to reduce critical path length and toggle rates to improve power and timing while maintaining the functionality and clarity of the design.
