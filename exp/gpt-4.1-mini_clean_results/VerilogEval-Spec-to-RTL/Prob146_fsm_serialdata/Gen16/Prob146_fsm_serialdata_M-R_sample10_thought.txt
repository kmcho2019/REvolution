I will refactor the design to use an explicit Moore FSM with enumerated states and a separate always block for outputs, implementing state and output logic distinctly. Instead of a combined next-state logic block, I will use synchronous state transitions with registered next_state and synchronous output updates for cleaner timing. The shift register will be implemented with a load-enable signal, and the bit counter will be incremented only when reading bits. Also, I will use a synchronous done flag reset in the same clock cycle to avoid glitches. This restructuring improves clarity and separation of combinational and sequential logic without changing functional behavior.
