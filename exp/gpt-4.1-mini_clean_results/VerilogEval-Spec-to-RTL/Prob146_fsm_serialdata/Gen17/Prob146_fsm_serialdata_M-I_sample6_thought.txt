To improve PPA, I will implement a one-hot FSM encoding to reduce combinational logic and critical path delay in next-state decoding, which can improve timing. Also, I'll split the data-shifting and bit counting logic into separate always blocks for better timing and clarity. Done will be asserted and held only for one clock cycle immediately after a valid stop bit is detected, and cleared only then to reduce toggling. The shift register will shift LSB-first by shifting left and inserting the new bit at LSB, which matches the serial protocol more directly and might help synthesis. Additionally, the FSM transitions will be fully synchronous and stable. This redesign aims to reduce critical path length, lower power from toggling, and keep area overhead minimal with one-hot states (4 flip-flops vs. 2 bits).
