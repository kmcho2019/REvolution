Simplify the FSM by using binary-encoded states instead of one-hot to reduce state register count and combinational logic. Integrate done signal generation directly in the sequential block without extra gating, outputting done only for one cycle after a valid stop bit. Use a straightforward right shift of the received bit into the data register to collect LSB-first bits. Keep a 3-bit bit counter to track received bits. Avoid explicit stable holding of shift register in error recovery; just reset counters and wait for the line to return idle. The simpler FSM states are IDLE, RECEIVE, CHECK_STOP, and RECOVER encoded as 2-bit states. This reduces complexity while preserving all required functionality and synchronous reset.
