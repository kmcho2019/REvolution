Implement data shifting by shifting in the LSB first via shift-left operation `{in, data_shift[7:1]}` changed to `data_shift >> 1` and inserting `in` at MSB if needed; but since LSB first is required, better to shift right and put new bit at MSB. However, the original approach `{in, data_shift[7:1]}` shifts right inserting MSB; to clearly shift LSB first, use `data_shift <= {in, data_shift[7:1]}` which places new bit at MSB, but bits are received LSB first, so shifting right is correct.
