To improve the original design’s power and timing, introduce enable signals for the bit counter and shift register to update only during READ_BITS state, reducing unnecessary toggling. Also, separate the done pulse generation into a dedicated register set at CHECK_STOP state to simplify output logic. This avoids repeated conditional assignments and might shorten critical paths. Keep the FSM binary encoding for area efficiency. In RECOVER state, do not update any registers, minimizing switching activity. This targeted gating will reduce dynamic power and may slightly improve timing by lessening combinational path complexity and register toggling outside relevant states.
