To optimize the design for power and timing, I will implement the following improvements:

1. Use a clock enable (ce_receive) to update bit_count and data_shift registers only during the RECEIVE state. This reduces unnecessary toggling in IDLE, STOP, and WAIT_STOP states, lowering dynamic power.

2. Assign the done signal combinationally based on state and input, instead of updating it every clock cycle in sequential logic. This reduces toggling and improves timing on done.

3. Reset bit_count and data_shift explicitly in the IDLE state upon detecting the start bit, but avoid updating these registers otherwise in IDLE to reduce toggling.

4. Keep state register updating every cycle (no clock gating) for correct operation.

5. Use simple 2-bit binary encoding for states to minimize area.

This approach maintains functional correctness while reducing switching activity, improving power and timing metrics without area penalty.
