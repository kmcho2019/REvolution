To improve PPA, I will convert the FSM encoding from one-hot (4 flip-flops) to binary encoding (2 flip-flops), reducing area and dynamic power. The state machine logic will be adjusted accordingly. I will add clock enables for the bit counter and data_shift registers, activating them only during data reception, which reduces switching activity and power. The done signal will be asserted only when needed and otherwise held stable to reduce unnecessary toggling. The shift register will shift LSB first with clearer bit assignments by shifting left and inserting the new bit at LSB, which aligns directly with the protocol and eases timing. Overall, this binary-encoded FSM with gated registers preserves the original behavior while reducing area, power, and possibly improving timing.
