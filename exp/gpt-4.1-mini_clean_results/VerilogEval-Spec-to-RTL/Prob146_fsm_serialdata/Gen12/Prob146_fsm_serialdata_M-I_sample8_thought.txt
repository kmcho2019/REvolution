To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, the strategy is:

1. Introduce synchronous enable signals for bit counter and data shift register so they only update during READ_BITS, minimizing unnecessary toggling.
2. Separate the done pulse generation into a dedicated register driven by a simple condition at CHECK_STOP, simplifying output logic and potentially improving timing.
3. Use one-hot encoding for FSM states to improve timing by reducing combinational next-state decoding complexity.
4. Simplify combinational logic for next-state and outputs, explicitly gating register updates.
5. Keep the FSM states and transitions explicit for clarity, with minimal extra logic.
6. Retain the synchronous active-high reset.
7. Ensure done pulses only one cycle when stop bit is valid, and hold output byte stable until next valid done.

These improvements reduce switching activity outside READ_BITS, simplify logic paths, and should improve the critical path slack and power consumption without changing functionality.
