To improve the PPA while preserving functionality, I will change the FSM from one-hot encoding to binary encoding, reducing flip-flop count and combinational complexity, thereby saving area and power. I'll add clock enables to the bit_count and data_shift registers to avoid unnecessary toggling when not receiving bits, reducing power further. The done signal will be registered only when needed (in the STOP state), avoiding redundant toggling. The data_shift will explicitly shift LSB first by shifting left and inserting the serial input at LSB. The next state logic remains combinational with defaults to prevent latches. This preserves the FSM's robustness in handling invalid stop bits. Overall, these changes maintain correctness while improving area and power, and potentially timing.
