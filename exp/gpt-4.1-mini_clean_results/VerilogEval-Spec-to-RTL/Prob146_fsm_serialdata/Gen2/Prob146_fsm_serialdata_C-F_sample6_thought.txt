To fuse the best ideas, I will keep the clear FSM structure from Example 1 and the clock enable style register updates from Example 2 to reduce unnecessary toggling and power consumption.

- Maintain the four states: IDLE, RECEIVE, STOP, WAIT_STOP.
- Update bit_count and data_shift only during RECEIVE state using clock enables.
- Reset bit_count and data_shift only when the start bit is detected (transition from IDLE to RECEIVE).
- The done signal is asserted for one clock cycle when a valid stop bit is detected.
- To further reduce toggling, done is cleared immediately after assertion.
- State always updates on clk to keep FSM responsive.
- Keep the shift register shifting LSB first.
- This design optimizes power by gating updates to data and count registers, keeps timing simple with minimal combinational logic, and maintains small area with 2-bit state encoding.

This fusion yields a clean, efficient FSM with minimal toggling, good timing, low power, and a compact design.
