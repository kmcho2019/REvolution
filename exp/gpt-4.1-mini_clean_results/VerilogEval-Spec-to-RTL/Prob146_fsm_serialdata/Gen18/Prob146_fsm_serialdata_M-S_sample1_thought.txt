Use a simple FSM with three states: IDLE (wait for start bit), RECEIVE (shift in 8 bits), and STOP (check stop bit). On reset, clear all registers. In RECEIVE, shift bits LSB first by placing the new bit at the MSB and shifting right. When 8 bits are received, move to STOP to verify the stop bit. If valid (1), assert done and output the byte; otherwise, go to a RECOVER state that waits until line returns to idle (1). This minimal FSM and combining shift and bit count in one always block reduce complexity while ensuring correct functionality and synchronous reset.
