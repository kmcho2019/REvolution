The original design is already minimal and optimal for a simple combinational wire connection between input and output. No registers or logic gates are added, so propagation delay, power, and area are minimal and cannot be improved further in isolation. However, to achieve the absolute minimal resource usage and potentially reduce power slightly, the output can be declared as a wire and connected directly using a continuous assignment inside the port list itself. This is an equivalent style but sometimes preferred for its concise syntax and to highlight the direct wire nature of the output. This change is largely stylistic and does not impact functionality or PPA significantly but aligns with the most minimal combinational connection idioms.
