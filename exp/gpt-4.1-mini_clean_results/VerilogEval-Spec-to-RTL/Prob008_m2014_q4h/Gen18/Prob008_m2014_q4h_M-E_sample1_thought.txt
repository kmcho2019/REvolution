To implement a different architectural idea while still achieving the same combinational behavior, I will use an intermediate wire to hold the input value before driving the output. This introduces a simple internal net assignment, maintaining combinational logic and no clock or state elements. This approach illustrates modular signal routing inside the module, useful in designs where signals are often registered or buffered but here remain purely combinational. It differs structurally from the direct continuous assignment by explicitly naming an internal signal to reflect the input before connecting to output.
