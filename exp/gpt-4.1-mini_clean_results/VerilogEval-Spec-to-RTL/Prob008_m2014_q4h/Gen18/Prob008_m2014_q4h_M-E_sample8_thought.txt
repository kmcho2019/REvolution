I will implement the combinational connection using an always_comb block that continuously assigns the output from the input. This procedural continuous assignment style is functionally equivalent to a continuous assign statement but uses a procedural block with an output declared as reg. This approach explicitly models the combinational logic in a procedural manner, which is often more familiar and flexible in RTL design, especially when multiple conditions or intermediate processing may be added later. It also demonstrates a method suitable for cases where the output port is of reg type.
