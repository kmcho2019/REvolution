The original design is already minimal and optimal for this simple combinational connection. There are no logic gates or registers added, and the output is driven directly by the input using a continuous assignment. This is the best approach for minimal delay, power, and area in isolation. 

Minor stylistic improvements can be made by using the implicit wire declaration for output, or by directly connecting the output in the port declaration (Verilog-2001 style), but these do not impact PPA meaningfully.

Since the reported TNS, WNS, and power are already negligible, no changes can meaningfully reduce timing or power further. The area is minimal since no logic is instantiated.

Therefore, to keep the design clean and concise, I will implement the output port as an implicit wire and connect it combinationally with a continuous assign statement, which is the standard idiomatic approach.

No further PPA improvements are feasible given the trivial nature of the module.
