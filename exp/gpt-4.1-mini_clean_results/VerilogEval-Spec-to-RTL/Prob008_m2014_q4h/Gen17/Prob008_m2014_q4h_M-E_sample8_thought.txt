To provide a novel architectural idea while preserving the same functional behavior, I will implement the combinational connection from input to output using a continuous assign through a wire intermediate signal. This approach explicitly declares an internal wire connecting the input and output, reflecting the internal datapath rather than a direct assign. Although functionally equivalent, this structure mimics datapath style design and can be advantageous in larger designs where the wire might be reused or observed. It also separates the interface from internal connections for clarity.
