The original solution is already the most minimal and direct way to implement combinational pass-through from input to output. There is no logic or register insertion that could improve performance or area. Since power is primarily due to toggling and capacitance, this single wire cannot be further optimized at the RTL level.

One potential improvement in style for better synthesis compatibility and clarity is to explicitly declare the output as a wire (which is default, but explicit is clearer), and to use the ANSI-style port declaration with data types. This does not affect PPA metrics but improves readability and synthesis tool compatibility without changing functionality.

No structural or logical changes are possible to reduce power, area, or timing due to the triviality of the design.

Therefore, the best "improvement" is stylistic clarity without changing functionality or increasing area/power.
