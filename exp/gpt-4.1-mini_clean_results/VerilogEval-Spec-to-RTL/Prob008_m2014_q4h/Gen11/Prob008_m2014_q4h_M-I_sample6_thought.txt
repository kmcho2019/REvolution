The existing design is already minimal and optimal, performing a direct combinational assignment from input to output. There are no sequential elements, no additional logic gates, and the output simply follows the input with no delay. Therefore, no further optimization can be made in terms of performance, power, or area at the Verilog RTL level. The module effectively maps to a single wire connection, which is the most efficient implementation possible.

Hence, the strategy is to maintain the current implementation as it is already optimal.
