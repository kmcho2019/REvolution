The existing solution is already the most minimal and efficient way to implement a constant zero output in Verilog. Assigning a constant '0' directly to the output ensures minimal logic, zero delay, and minimal power. Any other method (such as always blocks or additional wires) would increase complexity and resource usage.

Since the current implementation yields optimal PPA metrics (no timing, minimal area, negligible power), no further RTL-level optimization is possible.

If we want to marginally reduce area or power further, that would be a physical implementation or synthesis constraint matter, not a Verilog change.

Therefore, the best strategy is to keep the solution as is.
