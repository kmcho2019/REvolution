The current solution already implements a minimal, optimal constant zero output using a continuous assignment. Further PPA improvement beyond this is limited since the output is a constant signal. However, to potentially help synthesis tools better recognize the constant nature and optimize area and power, we can declare the output as a wire explicitly (although it's implicitly a wire by default) and keep the constant assignment. Additionally, adding synthesis directives (like `/* synthesis keep=0 */` is not needed here and might inhibit optimization). Overall, the current implementation is effectively optimal, so we will keep it as is with clean formatting to ensure best clarity and synthesis friendliness.
