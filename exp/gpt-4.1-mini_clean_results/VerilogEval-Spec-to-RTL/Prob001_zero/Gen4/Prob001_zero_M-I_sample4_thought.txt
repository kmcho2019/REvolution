The output is a constant zero, so the simplest and most area- and power-efficient implementation is to directly assign zero to the output using a continuous assignment without any localparam. Removing the localparam simplifies the code slightly, making the intent immediately clear to synthesis tools and humans. This can lead to minimal area savings and marginally improved synthesis clarity, while maintaining zero power switching activity and no timing complexity. This approach is optimal for PPA in this trivial design.
