The original design is already optimal for outputting a constant zero signal as it uses a simple continuous assignment with no clock or combinational logic. To optimize power further at the RTL level is not practical since the output is tied directly to a constant 0 and will synthesize to a ground connection in hardware. Similarly, area and timing are minimal and cannot be improved further in RTL.

However, a very minor style improvement could be to declare `zero` as a wire explicitly for clarity, although synthesis tools infer this by default. Since the original code is minimal and clear, no meaningful PPA improvement is achievable through RTL changes alone.

Therefore, I will keep the code essentially the same but explicitly declare `zero` as wire output to emphasize the minimal nature of the design.
