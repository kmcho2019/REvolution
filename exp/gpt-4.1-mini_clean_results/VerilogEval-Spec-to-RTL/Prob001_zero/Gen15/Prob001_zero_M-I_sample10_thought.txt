This approach results in minimal area and zero dynamic power. There is no clock or combinational logic. Hence, the design is minimal and optimal in terms of PPA. No further improvements are possible without changing the problem specification. For utmost clarity and synthesis friendliness, I'll keep the implementation as simple as possible, explicitly declaring zero as an output wire with the constant value 1'b0.
