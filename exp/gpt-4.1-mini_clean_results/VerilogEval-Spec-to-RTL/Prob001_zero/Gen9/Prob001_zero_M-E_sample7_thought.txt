To provide a novel architectural approach distinct from a direct continuous assignment, the output zero can be driven from a registered value initialized to zero. Although this introduces a flip-flop and a clock, it still guarantees the output is always low after reset, and synthesizes to a constant low if the clock and reset are handled properly. This approach demonstrates a state element maintaining a constant zero, differing structurally from a simple combinational tie-off. The use of a clocked process can be useful in scenarios where outputs are driven by registered signals, so this alternative might fit well in a design context where synchronous logic is preferred.
