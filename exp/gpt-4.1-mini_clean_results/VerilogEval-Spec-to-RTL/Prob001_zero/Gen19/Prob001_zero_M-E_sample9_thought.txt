Instead of using a continuous assignment, implement the output as a reg and drive it low using an always block triggered by any change (sensitivity to all signals using always @(*)). This approach models the output as combinational logic inferred by the synthesis tool, but structurally uses an always block and a reg output. This differs architecturally from a continuous assignment by explicitly modeling the output with a procedural block. It still meets the requirement of always outputting zero with zero delay and minimal logic.
