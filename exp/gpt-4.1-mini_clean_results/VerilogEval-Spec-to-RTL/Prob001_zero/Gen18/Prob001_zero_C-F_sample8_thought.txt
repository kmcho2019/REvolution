The best approach combines the minimality and efficiency of directly assigning a constant zero output (as in Example 2) with the modular and clear design style seen in Example 1, but without adding unnecessary hierarchy. 

We will define a parameterized local constant or macro for zero inside TopModule to improve readability and maintainability, yet still directly assign zero to the output without any submodule instantiation. This keeps the design straightforward and minimal while providing semantic clarity similar to having a ConstZero module. 

This fusion avoids the overhead of instantiation (improving area and power) and retains explicitness about the constant value being driven, improving future code clarity and ease of modification.

Hence:
- No submodules (avoid overhead).
- Use a local parameter to represent zero explicitly.
- Directly assign the parameter to the output.

This is a balanced, explicit, and minimal implementation that blends both designs' strengths.
