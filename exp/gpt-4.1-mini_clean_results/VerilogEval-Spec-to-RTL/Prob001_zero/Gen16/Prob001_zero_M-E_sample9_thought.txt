To create a different architectural approach while still outputting a constant LOW, I will instantiate a simple D flip-flop module initialized to zero and continuously reset it asynchronously to ensure the output stays LOW. This approach introduces a sequential element and a reset signal internally tied to zero, ensuring the flip-flop output remains zero at all times. This provides a clocked, state-retentive form of a constant zero output, which is functionally equivalent but architecturally different from a direct assignment.
