The initial solution already outputs a constant low using a continuous assignment, which is optimal in terms of power, performance, and area. There is no register or logic to optimize further since the output is tied directly to a constant zero. Physically, synthesis tools will likely implement this as a tie-low cell or just a hardwired connection. Any attempt to declare the output as a reg with an initial block or to use a procedural assignment would only increase complexity and resource usage.

Therefore, no improvements to PPA or functionality are possible beyond the current minimal implementation.
