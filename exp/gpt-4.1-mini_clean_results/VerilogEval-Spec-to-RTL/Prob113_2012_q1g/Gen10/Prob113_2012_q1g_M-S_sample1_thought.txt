- The rows corresponding to x[3]x[2] = 11 and 10 have many 1s, suggesting the term x[3] OR x[2].
- The columns corresponding to x[1]x[0] = 00 have some 1s in different rows.

  x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 0000, 0010, 1100, 1101, 1110, 1010, 1011, 1000, 1001

This expression covers all 1s in the map with minimal terms and uses no redundant signals.
