To improve the existing solution for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) without altering functionality, I focus on simplifying logic expressions, reducing gate counts, and sharing complemented signals efficiently.

Key improvements:
- Notice that the term (~x3 & ~x1) and (~x3 & x2 & x1 & ~x0) both contain ~x3, so factor ~x3 out to reduce the number of gates and internal nets.
- The term (~x0 | x1) appears only in term3, so no factorization there.
- Merge some intermediate signals to reduce net count and potential routing overhead.
- Keep complemented signals shared to minimize inverter count.
- The resulting expression can be rewritten as:

  f = ~x3 & ( ~x1 | (x2 & x1 & ~x0) ) | (x3 & x2 & (~x0 | x1))

- The term (~x1 | (x2 & x1 & ~x0)) can be simplified:
  Since (~x1) OR (x2 & x1 & ~x0)
  = (~x1) OR (x1 & x2 & ~x0)
  = (~x1) OR (x1 & ... ) 
  = (~x1) OR (x1 & something)
  = (~x1) OR x1 & something  (distributive)
  = (~x1) OR (something) when x1 is 1, else (~x1) is 1 anyway
  So (~x1) OR (x2 & ~x0) because if x1=1, then x1 & x2 & ~x0 reduces to x2 & ~x0.

  But careful: Let's rewrite:

  (~x1) + (x2 & x1 & ~x0) = (~x1) + (x1 & x2 & ~x0)
  = (~x1) + x1 & x2 & ~x0
  = (~x1) + x1 & (x2 & ~x0)

  Using consensus theorem: A + A'B = A + B
  So, (~x1) + x1 & C = (~x1) + C

  Therefore:

  (~x1) + (x2 & ~x0)

Thus, f = ~x3 & ( ~x1 | (x2 & ~x0) ) | (x3 & x2 & (~x0 | x1))

This reduces logic complexity.

Implementing this logic directly reduces the number of terms and gates, improving area and power. Also, intermediate wires can be simplified.

This approach keeps fan-in low and leverages signal sharing.
