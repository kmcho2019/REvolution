Since (~x0 | x1) and (x1 & ~x0 & ~x3) are related, expand or use consensus theorem to simplify:

(x1 & ~x0 & ~x3) | (x3 & ~x0) | (x3 & x1)

= (x1 & ~x0 & ~x3) | (x3 & ~x0) | (x3 & x1)

This is x3 + x1 & ~x3 (OR form), meaning it's true if x3=1 or (x1=1 and x3=0).

(~x0 & (x3 | x1)) | (x3 & x1)

Therefore,

This is a much simpler expression.

Final simplified expression:

This expression is minimal in terms of logic terms and gates.

This expression has two terms:

1. (~x3 & ~x1): true when both x3 and x1 are 0.

2. (x2 & (x3 | x1)): true when x2=1 and either x3=1 or x1=1.
