The existing design is already efficient with a minimized logic expression derived from the Karnaugh map. However, to further optimize for PPA, I will:

- Remove redundant intermediate signals to reduce net count and slight wiring overhead.
- Avoid declaring redundant complemented signals if they are used only once.
- Inline simple expressions directly where used to potentially allow synthesis tools better optimization.
- Rearrange the logic slightly to minimize gate count by leveraging De Morgan’s laws where appropriate.
- Still keep the expression readable.

This approach marginally reduces area and power by reducing wiring complexity and possibly gate usage while maintaining performance. The critical path and delay will stay effectively the same, but overall area and power may see a small improvement.
