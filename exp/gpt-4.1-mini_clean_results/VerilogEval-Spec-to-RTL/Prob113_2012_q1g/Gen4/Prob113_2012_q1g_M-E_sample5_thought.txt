Use a 16-bit lookup table (LUT) indexed by a reconstructed address formed by concatenating x[2], x[3], x[0], and x[1] in that order as specified by the Karnaugh map. This approach directly captures the mapping from inputs to output without complex boolean simplification. The LUT is initialized with the output values from the Karnaugh map. At runtime, the concatenated bits form the LUT address and f is assigned by indexing the LUT at that address. This method makes the mapping explicit, is easy to verify, and is a different architectural approach compared to using a case statement.
