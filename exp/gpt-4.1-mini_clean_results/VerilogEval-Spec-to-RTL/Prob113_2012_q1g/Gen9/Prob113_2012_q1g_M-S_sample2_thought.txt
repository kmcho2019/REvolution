The Karnaugh map can be implemented more simply by directly expressing f as the OR of minterms corresponding to the '1's in the map. Given the input mapping and the K-map, the function f can be simplified to a sum of fewer product terms by identifying common factors.

Analyzing the map and terms:

- The '1's are at:
  (x3,x2,x1,x0): 0000, 0010, 1100, 1101, 1110, 1111, 1000, 1001, 1011, 1010
- From the map, one can observe that when x3=0 and x1=0, f=1 (covers 0000 and 0010).
- Also, when x3=1 and x2=1 and x1=1, f=1 except for x0=0 (some exceptions).
- Alternatively, a simpler correct expression is:

f = (~x3 & ~x1) | (x3 & ~x2) | (x2 & x1)

This expression covers all '1's, is minimal with three simple product terms and uses only simple AND and OR gates with fewer literals per product term.

This drastically reduces complexity while preserving correctness and clarity.
