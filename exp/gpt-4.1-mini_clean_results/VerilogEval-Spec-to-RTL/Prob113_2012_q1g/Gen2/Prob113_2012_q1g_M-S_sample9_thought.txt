Simplify by directly implementing the function from the Karnaugh map using boolean logic expressions. Since the K-map groups are small, derive the logic terms manually.

From the Karnaugh map:

- Rows are indexed by x[2]x[3]
- Columns by x[0]x[1]

Map cells with value 1:

Positions (row,col):
00 00 (x=0000)
00 10 (x=0010)
11 00 (x=1100)
11 01 (x=1101)
11 11 (x=1111)
10 00 (x=1000)
10 01 (x=1001)
10 10 (x=1010)

Writing out minterms (x[3:0]) as (x3 x2 x1 x0):

- 0000 → x3=0,x2=0,x1=0,x0=0
- 0010 → x3=0,x2=0,x1=1,x0=0
- 1000 → x3=1,x2=0,x1=0,x0=0
- 1001 → x3=1,x2=0,x1=0,x0=1
- 1010 → x3=1,x2=0,x1=1,x0=0
- 1100 → x3=1,x2=1,x1=0,x0=0
- 1101 → x3=1,x2=1,x1=0,x0=1
- 1111 → x3=1,x2=1,x1=1,x0=1

We can derive a simplified expression by inspecting patterns:

- f=1 if x3=1 and (x2=0 or x1=0 or (x2=1 and x0=1 and x1=0)) 
- or if x3=0 and x2=0 and (x1,x0) in {00,10}
