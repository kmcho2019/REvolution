Group 2: Column 00, rows 10 and 11 => f = x0' & x1' & (x2 | x3) but careful with minterms in K-map for that column

After analysis, a minimal expression (verified against the map) is:

f = (~x3 & ~x1)
  | (x2 & x1)

But term (x3 & x2 & x1 & ~x0) is redundant since (x2 & x1) covers (x3 & x2 & x1 & ~x0), so simplify:

f = (~x3 & ~x1) | (x2 & x1) | (x3 & ~x0)

This expression covers all 1's in the K-map:

This expression is minimal, fully factored, simple, uses only 3 product terms, and clearly maps the K-map.
