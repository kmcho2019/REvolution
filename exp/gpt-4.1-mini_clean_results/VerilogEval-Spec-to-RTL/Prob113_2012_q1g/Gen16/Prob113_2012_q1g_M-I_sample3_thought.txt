To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will:

- Use the built-in XNOR operator (^) directly instead of manually constructing it from AND, OR, and NOT gates. This reduces logic depth and gate count, improving delay and area, and likely decreases switching activity.
- Keep the original factored expression as it is minimal and clear.
- Maintain clear signal naming for readability but reduce intermediate wires only to necessary ones for concise code.
- The final expression will be:
  f = (~x2 & ~x3 & ~x1) | (x2 & (~x0 | (x1 ~^ x3)))

This should reduce gate count and depth, improve timing, reduce power by leveraging optimized XOR/XNOR primitives, and maintain clarity and correctness.
