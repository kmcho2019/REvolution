To fuse the best ideas from both examples, we aim for a design that balances clarity, minimal logic depth, and minimal redundant logic.

- Generate complemented signals once and reuse them (from Example 1).
- Express f as a single assign statement combining the two main terms, factoring common subexpressions as in Example 1, but inline them to reduce intermediate wires.
