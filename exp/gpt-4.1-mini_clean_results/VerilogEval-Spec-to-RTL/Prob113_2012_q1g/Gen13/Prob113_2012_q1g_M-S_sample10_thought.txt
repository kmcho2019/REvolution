Examining the Karnaugh map reveals some clear patterns that allow simplification. Notably, f=1 when:

- x3=0 and x1=0 (covers multiple cases with no dependency on x2 or x0)
- x3=1 and x2=1 and x1=1 (when x3 and x2 and x1 are all set)
- x3=1 and x2=1 and x0=0 (when x3 and x2 are set but x0 is zero)

These conditions can be combined to simplify the expression to:

f = (~x3 & ~x1) | (x3 & x2 & (~x0 | x1))

This eliminates one AND term from the previous solution and simplifies the logic while maintaining the same function.

The expression has fewer product terms and literals, which reduces gate count and complexity, improving area and potentially power.

This approach retains clarity by naming inputs explicitly and using a straightforward assign statement.
