To fuse the best ideas from both examples, we combine the clarity and area/power optimality of a continuous assignment from Example 2 with the explicitness and potential synthesis guidance of Example 1’s byte-level assignments.

The fusion strategy is:
- Use a continuous assignment (for minimal area and power, as it infers simple wiring).
- Write the assignment clearly in a byte-wise concatenation style to maintain readability and synthesis friendliness.
- Add comments explicitly showing the byte mapping to aid human understanding and assist synthesis tools that use comments.
- Avoid the always block to prevent unnecessary inference of registers or combinational logic complexity.
  
This approach keeps the implementation minimal, clear, and optimal for performance, power, and area, merging the best practices from both examples.
