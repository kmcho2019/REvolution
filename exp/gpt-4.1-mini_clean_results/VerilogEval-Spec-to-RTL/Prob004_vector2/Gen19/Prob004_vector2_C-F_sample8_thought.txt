Use a parameterized localparam for byte count and a generate-for loop to build the output concatenation in reverse byte order. This keeps the output as a wire for minimal logic, enables easy scalability to different widths, maintains straightforward combinational logic, and improves readability by explicitly showing the reversed byte mapping. The generate block unrolls at compile time, thus synthesizing to clean wire connections without registers or loops in hardware, achieving the best of both worlds: clarity, scalability, and optimal PPA.
