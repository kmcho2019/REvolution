To improve PPA (power, performance, area), the main optimization is to replace the shifts and masks with direct byte slicing of the input vector, which is simpler for synthesis tools and reduces logic depth and toggle activity. Directly wiring byte slices is clearer, likely reduces switching power, and can minimize area by eliminating extra intermediate logic for shifts and masks. This also simplifies the RTL and should maintain or improve timing. The module remains fully combinational with no registers.
