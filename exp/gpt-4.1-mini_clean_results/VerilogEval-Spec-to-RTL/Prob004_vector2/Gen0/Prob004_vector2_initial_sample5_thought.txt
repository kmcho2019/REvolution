To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we need to reorder the bytes such that the least significant byte becomes the most significant byte, the second least significant byte becomes the second most significant byte, and so forth. Specifically, if the input is divided into 4 bytes: [byte3, byte2, byte1, byte0], the output should be [byte0, byte1, byte2, byte3]. We can achieve this by slicing the input 32-bit vector into four 8-bit segments and concatenating them in reversed order.
