The original solution is already optimal in performance, power, and area since it is purely combinational wiring to reorder bytes. However, to slightly improve power by reducing unnecessary switching, we can register the output so the byte-reversed output is only updated synchronously with a clock. This registers the output which may reduce glitches and switching power at the cost of introducing a clock and one cycle latency. This tradeoff is often acceptable in synchronous designs and can improve power at the system level without impacting area significantly.
