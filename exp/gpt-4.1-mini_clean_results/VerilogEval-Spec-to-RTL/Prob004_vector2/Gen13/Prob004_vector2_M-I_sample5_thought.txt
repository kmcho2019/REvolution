The current design is already optimal in terms of logic simplicity and area, as it is a purely combinational assignment directly reversing the byte order. To optimize further for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), we can consider adding output registers to pipeline the output, which can improve timing by reducing combinational path delay at the cost of one clock cycle latency. This also helps reduce glitches on outputs, lowering dynamic power consumption. Moreover, registering outputs can help meet timing in high-frequency designs and reduce switching activity. The area overhead of registers is minimal for a 32-bit register and is a worthwhile tradeoff for improved timing and power.

Therefore, the improvement strategy is to add a clock input and register the reversed output on the rising edge, producing a synchronous output. This makes the module ready for high-speed synchronous designs and reduces glitching power, while maintaining the functional behavior of byte reversal.
