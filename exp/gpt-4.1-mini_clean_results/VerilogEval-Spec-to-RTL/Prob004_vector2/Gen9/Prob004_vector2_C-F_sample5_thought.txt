Combine the clarity and minimalism of the continuous assignment approach with parameterization and generate blocks to enhance scalability and maintainability. Use a parameter for byte width and number of bytes to make the design easily extendable to different data widths, while retaining a purely combinational, non-registered continuous assignment for optimal performance, power, and area. The generate-for approach improves readability and reduces error-prone manual indexing, blending the best of both worlds: concise hardware and scalable, clean RTL.
