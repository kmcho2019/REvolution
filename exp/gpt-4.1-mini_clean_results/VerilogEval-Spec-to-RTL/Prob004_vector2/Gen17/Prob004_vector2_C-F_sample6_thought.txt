Fuse the concise and clear continuous concatenation style from Example 1 with the explicitness and clarity of byte-wise assignments from Example 2 by using a generate loop to reverse bytes. This approach maintains the simplicity and zero-delay wiring characteristics of direct concatenation while increasing scalability and readability for vectors with multiple bytes. The generate loop provides a parametrizable, clean, and maintainable style, useful for larger widths, without adding procedural complexity or registers. This combines the minimal power and area advantage of continuous assignment with the modular clarity of explicit byte handling.
