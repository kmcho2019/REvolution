Combine the explicit clarity and minimalism of direct concatenation with the scalability and reusability of a parameterized approach by defining a parameterized constant for byte width and number of bytes, then using a generate-for loop with an intermediate wire to build the reversed output. This keeps the implementation fully combinational and synthesizable, ensures minimal logic delay by leveraging simple wiring rearrangement, maintains minimal area and power, and also improves maintainability and adaptability for other vector sizes. This approach removes the function overhead while keeping systematic scalability and explicit byte ordering.
