The original implementation is already optimal in terms of logic simplicity and power, since it uses pure wiring to reverse the byte order. However, a slight improvement in readability and maintainability can be achieved by explicitly defining the byte slices and reusing a generate statement for clarity and scalability. This also prevents potential errors when extending the design to wider buses in the future. The PPA metrics will remain essentially the same as no new logic or flip-flops are added, but the code will be cleaner.
