Simplify the byte slicing using the Verilog indexed part-select operator [base +: width], which is clearer and potentially better for synthesis tools. This removes the need for arithmetic in the bit-range expressions, enhancing readability and maintainability. Also, as the size is fixed at 32 bits (4 bytes), unrolling the generate loop into four explicit assignments could slightly improve clarity and reduce the synthesis tool's effort in loop unrolling, potentially improving timing and area. The design remains fully combinational and simple wiring.
