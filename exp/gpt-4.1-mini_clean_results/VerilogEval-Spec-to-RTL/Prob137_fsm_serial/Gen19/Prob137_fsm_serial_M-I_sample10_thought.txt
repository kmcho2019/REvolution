To improve the existing design's power and area without sacrificing correctness, I will add clock enable signals to the registers (shift_reg and bit_count) so they only toggle when needed—in the RECEIVE state—avoiding unnecessary transitions in IDLE and WAIT_STOP states. This avoids resetting shift_reg and bit_count in WAIT_STOP and IDLE, thus reducing toggling and power. Also, I will simplify next_state logic by encoding states as one-hot, which might improve synthesis optimization potential and speed up state decoding, beneficial for timing. The done pulse remains a synchronous one-cycle pulse on correct stop bit detection. This approach maintains the original FSM robustness while reducing unnecessary toggling to optimize power and potentially improve timing slightly.
