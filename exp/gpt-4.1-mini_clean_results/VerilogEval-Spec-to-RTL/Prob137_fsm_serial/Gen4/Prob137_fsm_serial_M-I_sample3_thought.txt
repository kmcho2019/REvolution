To optimize the existing FSM for better PPA metrics, we focus on improving timing and power without significantly increasing area.

Improvements:
1. One-hot FSM encoding: Using one-hot encoding for 4 states reduces combinational logic depth in next_state decoding, potentially improving maximum clock frequency (timing).

2. Clock enable for data shift register: Enable shifting only during RECEIVE state to reduce unnecessary toggling of data_reg flip-flops and reduce power.

3. Simplify bit_count increment and resetting only when needed.

4. Keep the done signal assertion logic the same to ensure correctness.

These changes keep the FSM behavior unchanged but aim to improve timing and reduce power consumption with minimal area impact.
