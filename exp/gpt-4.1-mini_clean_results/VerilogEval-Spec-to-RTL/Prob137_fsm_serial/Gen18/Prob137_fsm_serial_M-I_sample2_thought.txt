To improve the original design for better PPA while preserving functionality, I will apply the following optimizations:
- Use one-hot state encoding (4 bits) to potentially improve timing on FPGA architectures by simplifying the next-state logic.
- Reduce switching activity by updating the shift register only during the DATA state.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently for better synthesis and timing.
- Reset done to zero outside the STOP state to ensure it pulses exactly one cycle.
- Optimize state transitions by reducing redundant assignments.
- Keep the bit_count counter as small as possible and only increment in DATA state.
