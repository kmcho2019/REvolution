To improve the PPA of the existing FSM design, I will reduce unnecessary switching by updating the shift register and bit counter only during the DATA state. This prevents toggling of these registers in other states, thus saving power. I'll also simplify the done signal generation by using a pulse generated only in the STOP state, ensuring minimal toggling. The state machine remains binary encoded for area efficiency. Additionally, I will keep all logic in one always block with synchronous reset for synthesis friendliness. This slight optimization reduces dynamic power without affecting performance or area significantly.
