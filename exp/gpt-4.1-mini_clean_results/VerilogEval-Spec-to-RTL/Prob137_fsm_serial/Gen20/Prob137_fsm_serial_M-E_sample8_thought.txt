Use a Moore FSM with separate combinational next-state logic and synchronous output registers. Introduce explicit states: IDLE (waiting for start bit), RECEIVE (shifting in 8 bits), STOP_BIT (checking stop bit), and RECOVER (waiting for stop bit after error). Shift bits into the LSB of a register using right shift to emphasize LSB-first serial reception. Generate the done pulse only in the STOP_BIT state on a successful stop bit, held high for one cycle. Synchronize all logic on the positive clock edge with an active-high synchronous reset. This modular approach makes state transitions and outputs clearer and more maintainable.
