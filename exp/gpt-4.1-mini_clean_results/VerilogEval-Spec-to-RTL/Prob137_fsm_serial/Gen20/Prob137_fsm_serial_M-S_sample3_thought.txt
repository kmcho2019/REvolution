Use a simple three-state FSM encoded with 2 bits, combining the STOP and ERROR states since both wait for a stop bit (1) to return to IDLE. This reduces state complexity from four to three states. Manage the bit count and shift register only in the DATA state. Shift in bits LSB first by shifting left and placing the new bit in LSB. The done signal pulses high for one cycle when a valid stop bit is received. Reset and default outputs are synchronous and straightforward.
