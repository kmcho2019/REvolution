To improve the PPA of the design, I will:

1. **Use binary encoding for the FSM states** instead of one-hot encoding to reduce flip-flop count and area. This lowers area and switching power at the expense of slightly more complex next-state logic.

2. **Add an enable signal to the shift register and bit counter** to update them only when needed (RECEIVE state). This reduces unnecessary toggling in other states like WAIT_STOP and IDLE, saving dynamic power.

3. Keep `done` generation synchronous and asserted for exactly one clock cycle after detecting a valid stop bit.

4. Maintain synchronous reset and clear the shift register and counter in IDLE and WAIT_STOP states.

These changes will reduce area, decrease toggling for power saving, and potentially improve timing by simplifying state encoding and reducing register toggling without changing the functional behavior.
