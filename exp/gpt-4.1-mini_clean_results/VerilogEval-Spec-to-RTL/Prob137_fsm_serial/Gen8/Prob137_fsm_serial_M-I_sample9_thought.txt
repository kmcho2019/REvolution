To optimize for power and timing, I will:
- Use one-hot encoding for FSM states to simplify next_state combinational logic and potentially improve timing.
- Add a clock enable (clk_en) to the shift register, so it only shifts when in the RECEIVE state, reducing unnecessary toggling in other states (power optimization).
- Keep the done pulse logic the same, asserting it only one cycle after correct stop bit.
- Keep synchronous reset and all sequential logic triggered on posedge clk.
This approach keeps the FSM behavior identical but reduces toggling on the data shift register and improves timing by simpler next state decoding with one-hot states.
