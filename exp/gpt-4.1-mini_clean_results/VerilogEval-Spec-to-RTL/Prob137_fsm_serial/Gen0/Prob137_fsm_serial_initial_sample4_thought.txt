Implement an FSM with states: IDLE, START, DATA_BITS, STOP, WAIT_STOP. The FSM starts in IDLE where the line is expected to be high. When a start bit (0) is detected, move to DATA_BITS and shift in 8 data bits (LSB first). After 8 bits are received, move to STOP to check the stop bit (should be 1). If stop bit is correct, output 'done' for one cycle and return to IDLE. If stop bit is incorrect, move to WAIT_STOP and remain until line goes high (stop bit detected), then go to IDLE. All operations and state transitions happen on the rising clock edge and respect synchronous reset.
