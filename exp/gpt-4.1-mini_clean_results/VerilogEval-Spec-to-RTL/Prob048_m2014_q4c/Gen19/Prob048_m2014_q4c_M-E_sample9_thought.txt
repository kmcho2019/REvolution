Implement the synchronous reset D flip-flop within a single always block inside TopModule without any submodule. Use a blocking assignment approach inside the always block triggered on the rising edge of clk. The synchronous reset is checked first, then the data input is loaded into q. This minimizes the number of signals and hierarchy levels, making the design compact and direct. It still maintains the required functionality: on a clock rising edge, if reset is active high, q is cleared to 0; otherwise, q captures d.
