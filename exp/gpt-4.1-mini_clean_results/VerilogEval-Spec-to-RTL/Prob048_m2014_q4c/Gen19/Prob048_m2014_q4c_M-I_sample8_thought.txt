To optimize the given synchronous-reset D flip-flop for better power, area, and performance, I will inline the DFF_SR logic into the TopModule to reduce module overhead and eliminate the intermediate wire. This simplification can slightly reduce routing and area costs. 

Additionally, to reduce unnecessary switching when reset is active for multiple cycles, I will add a clock enable signal internally that disables the flip-flop update when reset is asserted, so that the flip-flop doesn't keep toggling unnecessarily during reset assertions, which can lower dynamic power. 

These optimizations preserve the original synchronous reset functionality while aiming to reduce power consumption and area with a minor potential improvement on timing by reducing logic levels and module boundaries.
