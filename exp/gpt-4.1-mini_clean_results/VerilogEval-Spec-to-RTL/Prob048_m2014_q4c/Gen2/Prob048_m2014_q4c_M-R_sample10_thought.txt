Refactor the D flip-flop with synchronous reset by implementing the logic within a single clocked always block. This merges the next-state logic and state update into one block, eliminating the intermediate next_q signal. When reset 'r' is high, reset the output 'q' to 0; otherwise, on the rising clock edge, update 'q' to 'd'. This approach reduces combinational logic depth, improves timing, and decreases area and power by simplifying the design.
