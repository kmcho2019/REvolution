To combine the best aspects of both examples, I will eliminate the intermediate next_q signal and embed the synchronous reset logic directly inside a single sequential always block triggered on the rising clock edge. This approach reduces combinational logic depth, eliminates unnecessary registers, and simplifies the design, improving area, power, and timing. The synchronous reset is active high and sets q to zero when asserted; otherwise, q follows d on the clock edge. This fusion delivers the simplest, cleanest, and most efficient implementation of a D flip flop with synchronous reset.
