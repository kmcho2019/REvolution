The original design is already minimal and synthesizes to an efficient D flip-flop with synchronous reset. To slightly optimize power, I will add a check to update `q` only when its next value differs from the current value, potentially reducing unnecessary toggling. This approach can slightly lower dynamic power consumption at the expense of a minimal area increase (an additional comparator). Since the reset sets `q` to zero, the condition covers both reset and data transitions. This small optimization is common in power-sensitive designs.
