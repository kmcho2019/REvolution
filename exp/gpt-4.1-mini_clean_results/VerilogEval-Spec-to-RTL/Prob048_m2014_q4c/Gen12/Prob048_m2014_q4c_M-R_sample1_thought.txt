I will refactor the synchronous reset D flip-flop by removing the separate combinational block and integrating the reset logic directly inside the sequential always block. This approach simplifies the design by eliminating the intermediate next_q signal, reducing logic levels and potential delay. The reset condition is checked first within the sequential process, setting q to zero when asserted; otherwise, q captures d on the rising edge of clk. This preserves the functionality while potentially improving timing, power, and area metrics.
