The original implementation is already optimal for a synchronous reset D flip-flop in terms of simplicity, timing, area, and power. However, one minor improvement could be made by using non-blocking assignments consistently within the always block for better synthesis clarity and simulation correctness, although it may not impact PPA noticeably. Additionally, adding an explicit reset condition in an if-else structure instead of using the ternary operator can improve readability without affecting performance or area.
