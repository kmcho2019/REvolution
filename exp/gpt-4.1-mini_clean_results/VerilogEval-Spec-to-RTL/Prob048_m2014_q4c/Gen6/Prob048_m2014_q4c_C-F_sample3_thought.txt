Fuse the minimalistic, single always block synchronous reset D flip-flop design from Example 1 with the conditional data update approach from Example 2. This fusion maintains the synchronous reset and simplicity (good for performance and area) while adding a conditional update to prevent unnecessary toggling of q when d has not changed and reset is not asserted (good for power). The always block prioritizes synchronous reset to zero, then only updates q if q differs from d, avoiding redundant writes and reducing switching activity without adding extra enable signals or complexity.
