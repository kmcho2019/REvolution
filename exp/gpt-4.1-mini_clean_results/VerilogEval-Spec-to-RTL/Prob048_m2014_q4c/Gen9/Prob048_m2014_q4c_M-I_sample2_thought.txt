The original design is already minimal and optimal for a synchronous reset D flip-flop. To optimize further for power and area without impacting timing, we can add synthesis attributes or pragmas to encourage the synthesis tool to map the logic to an optimized flip-flop cell with synchronous reset support, which may reduce area and power marginally at the technology level. Additionally, explicitly declaring the output as reg with initial value 0 (if allowed in the synthesis environment) could help during simulation and might guide initialization for FPGA implementations. However, since initialization isn't always supported in ASIC flows, it's optional.

We should keep the single always block structure with non-blocking assignments and explicit if-else for clarity and synthesis friendliness. Since power gating or clock gating are not recommended for such a simple register in RTL code, no changes there.

Hence, the main improvement is the addition of synthesis attribute for synchronous reset and an optional initial block for simulation convenience.
