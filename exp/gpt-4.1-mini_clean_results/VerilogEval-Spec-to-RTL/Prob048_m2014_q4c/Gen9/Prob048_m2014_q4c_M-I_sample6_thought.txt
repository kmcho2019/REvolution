The existing design is already minimal and optimal for a simple D flip-flop with synchronous reset in terms of performance, power, and area. The synchronous reset is handled inside a single always block with non-blocking assignments, which is best practice. No combinational logic or unnecessary signals are present to optimize away. Since the reset and data signals are single-bit inputs feeding directly into the flip-flop, and clock gating is outside this module's scope, no further improvements on PPA are feasible here. Therefore, I will keep the implementation as is, ensuring clarity and optimality.
