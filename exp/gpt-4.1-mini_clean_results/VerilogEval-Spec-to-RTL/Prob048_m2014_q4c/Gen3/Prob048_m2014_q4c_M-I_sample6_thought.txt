To optimize the design further for power and area while maintaining functionality, I will introduce an enable signal that only updates the flip-flop when either reset or input data differ from the current output. This can reduce unnecessary toggling of the output and thereby reduce dynamic power. This optimization is minor but effective in low-power designs. The synchronous reset remains active high and within the clocked always block to keep the timing and reset semantics intact. The additional logic is minimal and should not negatively impact the timing or area significantly, while potentially improving power by reducing output toggling.
