The original design is already optimal in terms of simplicity, timing, area, and power for a single synchronous reset D flip-flop. No combinational logic or unnecessary assignments are added. To achieve any meaningful PPA improvement, the design could implement clock gating to reduce unnecessary toggling of the flip-flop when the output is already at the reset state or the input data does not cause a change, thus saving dynamic power. This technique can reduce switching activity by only enabling the clock when q needs to update. However, since this is a minimal module, the complexity added by clock gating may not be justified unless explicitly required. Alternatively, adding an asynchronous reset might improve performance under certain conditions, but that was not part of the specification. Given the requirement, the simplest and most efficient approach remains the original. As a minor refinement, explicitly define q as reg in the port list for clarity. Thus, the best optimization for PPA is to keep the design as is but add synthesis attributes or comments for downstream tools to recognize the flip-flop as a standard element, potentially improving synthesis optimizations without changing RTL logic.
