To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, I will merge the synchronous reset logic directly into the clocked always block and remove the intermediate combinational signal 'next_q'. This reduces combinational logic levels before the flip-flop, potentially improving timing. It also reduces switching activity on internal nets, lowering power. Eliminating the extra register and the combinational always block reduces area slightly. The design remains clean, functional, and easier for synthesis tools to optimize.
