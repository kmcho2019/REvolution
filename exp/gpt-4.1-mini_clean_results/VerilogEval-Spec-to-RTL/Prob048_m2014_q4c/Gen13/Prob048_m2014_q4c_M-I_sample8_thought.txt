The existing synchronous reset D flip-flop implementation is already minimal and clean. However, for slight improvements in power, I will add a clock enable signal that only enables updating q when reset is active or when d differs from q. This avoids unnecessary toggling of the flip-flop output and saves dynamic power by preventing redundant writes. The reset remains synchronous and has priority. This clock enable logic is simple and will not significantly increase area or critical path delay, while potentially reducing switching activity and power consumption.
