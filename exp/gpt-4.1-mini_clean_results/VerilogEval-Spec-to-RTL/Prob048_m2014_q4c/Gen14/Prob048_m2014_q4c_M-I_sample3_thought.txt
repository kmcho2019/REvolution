To optimize for better performance, power, and area, I will merge the reset logic into the sequential always block. This removes the intermediate combinational mux signal and reduces the combinational path before the flip flop input, thereby improving timing. It also decreases switching activity by confining all input-dependent logic to the clock edge, which benefits power consumption. Area is reduced slightly by eliminating extra logic. The design remains functionally identical—a D flip-flop with synchronous active-high reset.
