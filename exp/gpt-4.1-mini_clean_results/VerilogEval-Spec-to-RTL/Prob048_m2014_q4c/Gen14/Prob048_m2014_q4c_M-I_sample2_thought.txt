The original design is already minimal and optimal for this simple functionality: a D flip-flop with synchronous active-high reset. The only possibility for marginal power improvement is to use a conditional clock enable (load enable) to avoid unnecessary toggling of the flip-flop when reset is inactive and d equals q (no change in output). However, in a simple flip-flop with synchronous reset, this rarely justifies the extra logic.

Another approach is to ensure that the reset input is synchronized and doesn't glitch, but since the reset is synchronous, glitches are naturally filtered by the clock.

Given these facts and the already optimal code, no significant PPA improvement can be made without changing the interface or functionality.

Therefore, I will keep the code as is, confirming it is the cleanest and most efficient solution.
