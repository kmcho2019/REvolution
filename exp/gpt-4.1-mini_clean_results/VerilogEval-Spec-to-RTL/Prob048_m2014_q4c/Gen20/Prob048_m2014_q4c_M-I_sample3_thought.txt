To optimize the existing design for better power and area efficiency without sacrificing performance, we can inline the DFF_SR module directly into TopModule. This eliminates the need for an intermediate wire and module instantiation overhead, slightly reducing area and routing complexity. Since the synchronous reset may cause toggling on every clock edge while active, adding a clock enable signal driven by reset and data comparison can reduce unnecessary toggling, thus lowering dynamic power consumption. The clock enable logic only updates the flip-flop when reset is asserted or when the input d differs from the stored q, avoiding redundant updates and hence saves switching activity.

The resulting design remains functionally identical but is more power-efficient due to clock enable gating and slightly smaller in area due to module inlining.
