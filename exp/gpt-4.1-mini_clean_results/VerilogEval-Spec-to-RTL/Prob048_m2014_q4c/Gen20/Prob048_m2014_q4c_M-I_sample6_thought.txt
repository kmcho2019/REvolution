To optimize the design for better PPA, I will inline the synchronous reset D flip-flop logic directly inside the TopModule, removing the extra module instantiation and intermediate wire. This reduces area slightly by eliminating module overhead and routing. Furthermore, I will implement a clock enable signal derived from the reset and data to reduce unnecessary toggling when the stored value is already zero during reset or when d equals q, thereby potentially reducing dynamic power consumption. This clock enable gating ensures the flip-flop only updates when needed. The synchronous reset remains intact for correct behavior.
