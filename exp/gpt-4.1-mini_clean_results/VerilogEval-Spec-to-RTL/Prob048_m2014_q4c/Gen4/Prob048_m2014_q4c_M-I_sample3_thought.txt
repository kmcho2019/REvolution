The existing implementation is already optimal in terms of simplicity, area, power, and timing for a single synchronous reset D flip-flop. To potentially improve power slightly in a real silicon environment, we can gate the reset input internally to avoid unnecessary switching inside the register when reset is not active, by splitting the assignment into if-else for clarity and possibly better synthesis optimizations. However, this is a minor coding style change rather than a major PPA improvement. The overall design remains the same since the original single-line ternary is concise and synthesis-friendly. Therefore, keep the design functionally equivalent and clean.
