I will implement the D flip-flop with synchronous reset by using a clocked always block, but instead of directly assigning the output q, I will use an intermediate signal 'q_int' declared as a reg to hold the state, and assign q from q_int continuously. This separates the storage element from the output driver, which may aid certain synthesis tools or allow for easier debugging or modifications later. The synchronous reset will clear q_int on the clock edge if r is asserted; otherwise, it will load d. This is functionally equivalent but architecturally different from the typical direct assignment approach.
