Implement the synchronous reset D flip-flop by first storing the next state in a combinational always block that depends on the reset and data inputs, and then updating the flip-flop output on the rising clock edge. This approach separates the next state logic from the state update logic, resembling a Mealy-style structure within a simple flip-flop. It explicitly computes q_next based on r and d, making the reset and data behavior more modular and easier to extend.
