Implement the D flip-flop with synchronous reset using two always blocks: one that samples the input d on the rising edge of clk and another that updates the output q on the next clock edge or resets q synchronously. This architecture mimics a pipeline register style, where an intermediate register stage holds the data, and the output register incorporates the reset logic. Although functionally equivalent, this style clearly separates data sampling and output updating stages and can aid in certain testbench or debug scenarios.
