The FSM uses one-hot encoding with states A to F represented by y[0] to y[5]. The transitions depend on w and the current state. We need to find expressions for Y1 and Y3, which are the next-state inputs for flip-flops y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D).

From the transitions:
- To get to state B (y[1]), look at transitions ending at B:
  - From A on input 1: A(0) --1--> B, so if y[0] & w is true, go to B.
- To get to state D (y[3]), look at transitions ending at D:
  - From B on 0: B(0) --0--> D, so y[1] & ~w
  - From C on 0: C(0) --0--> D, so y[2] & ~w
  - From E on 0: E(1) --0--> D, so y[4] & ~w
  - From F on 0: F(1) --0--> D, so y[5] & ~w

Thus:
Y1 = y[0] & w;
Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w;
