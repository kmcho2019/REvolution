We have a one-hot encoded state vector y[5:0], representing states A through F. The FSM transitions are defined for input w. The output signals Y1 and Y3 are to be the inputs of the flip-flops for y[1] (state B) and y[3] (state D), respectively.

To derive Y1 and Y3, we look at the transitions that lead to states B and D:

- State B (y[1]) transitions:
  From A (y[0]) with input w=1: A->B if w=1
  So, Y1 = y[0] & w

- State D (y[3]) transitions:
  From B (y[1]) with w=0: B->D if w=0
  From C (y[2]) with w=0: C->D if w=0
  From E (y[4]) with w=0: E->D if w=0
  From F (y[5]) with w=0: F->D if w=0
  So, Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & (~w)

This gives us the combinational logic for Y1 and Y3.
