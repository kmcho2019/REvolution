The FSM is one-hot encoded with states A to F assigned to y[0] through y[5].

The transitions specify which next state bit should be set (Y1 for state B and Y3 for state D).

We derive Y1 and Y3 as follows:

Y1 = next y[1] = 1 when moving to state B.

From the FSM:
- A (y[0]) --1--> B: when y[0] and w=1
- No other transitions to B

Therefore:
Y1 = y[0] & w

Y3 = next y[3] = 1 when moving to state D.

From the FSM:
- B (y[1]) --0--> D: y[1] & ~w
- C (y[2]) --0--> D: y[2] & ~w
- E (y[4]) --0--> D: y[4] & ~w
- F (y[5]) --0--> D: y[5] & ~w

So,
Y3 = (~w) & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

The module outputs these signals based on current state y and input w.
