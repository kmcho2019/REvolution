Since Y1 and Y3 only depend on a few specific bits of the one-hot state y and input w, we can assign their logic directly in the TopModule without extra module instantiation. The logic is simple:

- Y1 = y[0] & w
- Y3 = ~w & ~(y[0] | y[3])

This removes unnecessary hierarchy and variables, making the code simpler and more concise while maintaining exact functionality.
