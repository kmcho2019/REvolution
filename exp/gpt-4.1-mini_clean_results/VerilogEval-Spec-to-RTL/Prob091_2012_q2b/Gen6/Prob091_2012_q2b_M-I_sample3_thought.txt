To improve the existing design's Power and Area, the focus is on further simplifying the output logic and reducing gate count. The original Y3 logic was ( ~w ) & ~( y[0] | y[3] ), which implements a NOR gate on y[0] and y[3] followed by AND with ~w.

By applying De Morgan's laws, we can explore alternative expressions:

Y3 = (~w) & ~(y[0] | y[3]) = (~w) & (~y[0] & ~y[3]) = (~w) & ~y[0] & ~y[3]

This can be implemented as a three-input AND of (~w), ~y[0], and ~y[3], which is straightforward and uses simple inverters and a 3-input AND gate.

Similarly, Y1 = y[0] & w is already minimal.

The simplification reduces the need for a NOR gate by replacing it with separate inverters, which can be beneficial if inverters are cheaper or already buffered elsewhere, improving power by lowering dynamic switching and possibly area by using simple inverters rather than a NOR gate.

We also integrate all logic directly into TopModule to remove module overhead and simplify synthesis.

Overall, this yields minimal, clean, and efficient combinational logic with reduced gate complexity and switching activity.
