The original design uses simple combinational logic that directly reflects the FSM transitions, which is already minimal and efficient for this small one-hot FSM. To optimize for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), a key improvement is to reduce unnecessary logic toggling and possibly share common terms.

Looking at Y3 logic: Y3 = ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

The OR term (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) can be computed once and reused. Additionally, since Y1 depends on y[0] & w, no shared terms with Y3 exist, but we can introduce a signal for the OR term in Y3 to reduce gate fan-in and possibly achieve better optimization by synthesis.

Furthermore, to minimize switching, we can AND w or ~w with the corresponding state terms as early as possible.

Thus, modularizing Y3 logic further with intermediate signals and writing the logic with minimal depth gates can slightly improve power and area.

We keep the same modular structure but implement the improved combinational logic with better term factoring.
