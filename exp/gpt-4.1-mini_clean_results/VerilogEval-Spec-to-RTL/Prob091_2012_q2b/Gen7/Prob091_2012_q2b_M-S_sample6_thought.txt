Since the FSM uses one-hot encoding and only a single state bit is active at a time, we can directly express Y3 as the AND of ~w and the OR of the relevant state bits: y[1], y[2], y[4], y[5]. This form is straightforward and minimal, avoiding complement of OR expressions which might add unnecessary complexity.

For Y1, the logic is already minimal: it is simply y[0] & w.

This simpler direct expression for Y3 improves clarity and potentially reduces logic levels without changing functionality.
