Since only Y1 and Y3 outputs are needed, and they correspond directly to specific next-state inputs of the one-hot FSM, the logic can be expressed directly without a separate module. By inspection of the FSM transitions and one-hot encoding:

- Y1 (input for flip-flop y[1]) is asserted only when the current state is A (y[0]) and w=1.
- Y3 (input for flip-flop y[3]) is asserted when w=0 and the current state is one of B (y[1]), C (y[2]), E (y[4]), or F (y[5]).

The simplified implementation directly assigns these two signals in the top module without instantiating another module.
