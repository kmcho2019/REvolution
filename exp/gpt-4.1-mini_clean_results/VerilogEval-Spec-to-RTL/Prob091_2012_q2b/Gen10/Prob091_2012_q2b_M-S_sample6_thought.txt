Since the problem involves simple combinational logic derived directly from the one-hot state encoding, we can simplify by using direct continuous assignments without separate gate modules. This reduces module overhead and intermediate wires, improving readability and potentially area and power. The expressions for Y1 and Y3 are straightforward:

- Y1 = y[0] & w
- Y3 = (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w

We can implement these directly with assign statements inside the TopModule.
