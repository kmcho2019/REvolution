The original implementation is already minimal and correct, with simple logic for Y1 and Y3. To optimize power and area further, especially to reduce toggling and gate complexity on Y3, we can slightly refactor the logic:

- Current Y3 is (~w) & (~y[0]) & (~y[3]) which uses three negations and a 3-input AND.
- Instead, notice that Y3 is high only when w=0 and neither y[0] nor y[3] are active.
- Implement Y3 as an AND of (~w) and a NOR of (y[0], y[3]): Y3 = ~w & ~(y[0] | y[3])
- This uses one inversion on w and one NOR gate on y[0] and y[3], potentially reducing gate levels and transitions.
- Sharing the NOR gate may save area and power.
- For Y1, it’s already minimal: Y1 = y[0] & w.

This refactoring keeps the logic equivalent but may yield slightly better PPA by reducing inverted inputs per gate and leveraging multi-input NOR gates that are common and efficient in standard cell libraries.
