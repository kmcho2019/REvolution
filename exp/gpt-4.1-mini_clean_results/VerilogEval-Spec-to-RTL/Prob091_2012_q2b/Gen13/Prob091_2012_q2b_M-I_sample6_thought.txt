The current design is functionally correct and relatively minimal. To improve PPA, particularly power and area, we can slightly simplify the logic expressions to minimize gate usage and switching activity:

- For Y1, the current logic is y[0] & w, which is already minimal.

- For Y3, the current expression is (~w) & ~(y[0] | y[3]), which uses a NOR gate on y[0] and y[3].

To reduce gate count and possibly switching:

- Replace ~(y[0] | y[3]) with ~y[0] & ~y[3] (De Morgan's law), so Y3 = (~w) & ~y[0] & ~y[3].

This expands the NOR into three ANDs and NOTs but removes the NOR gate, possibly simplifying gate types and improving static power due to fewer transitions on the NOR output.

Additionally, we can avoid creating an intermediate wire for w_n and directly apply negations in assigns to reduce signal toggling.

This approach slightly increases the number of AND gates but uses only simple gates (AND/NOT) instead of NOR, potentially reducing glitching and power in practice.

We keep the modular structure for clarity and future expandability.
