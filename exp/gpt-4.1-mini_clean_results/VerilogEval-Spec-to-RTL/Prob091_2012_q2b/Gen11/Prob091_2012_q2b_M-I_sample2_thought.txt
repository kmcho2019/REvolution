To improve the original design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), the focus is on reducing logic switching and gate count slightly.

Observing Y3:
Y3 = ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

The OR of these four signals can be implemented using a balanced tree or by grouping to reduce gate fan-in. However, since y bits are one-hot (only one is high), the OR is effectively detecting if the current state is any of {B, C, E, F}.

To optimize:
1. Replace (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) by a single wire named "active_states".
2. Implement the OR using a balanced structure to minimize delay and switching.
3. Also, Y1 = y[0] & w is minimal and should remain as is.
4. Since these two outputs do not share common sub-expressions, no further sharing is done.

This modular approach keeps logic shallow, reduces unnecessary switching by buffering with the named intermediate signal, and provides clear, maintainable code.

The code is rewritten to inline the logic in TopModule for better synthesis and removes the separate module to reduce overhead.

Overall, improvements are subtle but targeted at reducing the switching of large fan-in OR gates and simplifying the hierarchy for synthesis tools.
