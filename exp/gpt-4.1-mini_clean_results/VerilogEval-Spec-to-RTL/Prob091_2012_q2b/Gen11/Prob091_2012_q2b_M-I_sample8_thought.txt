The initial design cleanly implements the FSM next-state inputs Y1 and Y3 from one-hot encoded state and input w with a modular combinational logic approach. To optimize for better Power and Area while maintaining zero timing slack, I will:

- Reduce the OR gate fan-in in Y3 by factoring common terms and splitting logic to reduce large multi-input OR gates, which can lower switching and gate count.
- Reuse inverted signals to avoid repeated inversions.
- Simplify logic expressions with minimal gates.

Specifically, Y3 = ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) can be factored by grouping:

    y[1] | y[2] = y12
    y[4] | y[5] = y45

Then:

    Y3 = ~w & (y12 | y45)

Using intermediate signals reduces gate fan-in and may allow better synthesis optimization.

This restructuring should reduce gate complexity and dynamic power slightly without increasing delay or area, improving overall PPA.
