To simplify, observe that Y3 depends on (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]) & ~w. Since y is one-hot, at most one bit is set, so the OR can be replaced by a check if any of these bits is set. We can keep the original logic since it's already minimal. But we can reduce parentheses by distributing & ~w.

Hence, the simplified expressions are:
Y1 = y[0] & w;
Y3 = ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5]);
No unnecessary parentheses, minimal logic.
