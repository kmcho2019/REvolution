Refactor the logic to use direct continuous assignment expressions instead of instantiating separate gate modules. This simplifies the design, reduces the number of intermediate signals, and can help synthesis tools optimize better for power and area. The one-hot encoded state bits will be referenced directly by their indices, but we keep localparams for clarity. The inversion of `w` is done inline within the assignment expressions without intermediate wires. This approach keeps the design concise and readable while maintaining the original FSM output logic.
