Fuse the clarity and direct ROM-style lookup from Example 1 with the synthesis friendliness of a case statement instead of individual wire assignments. Use a combinational always block with a case statement to map the 3-bit input to the 16-bit output constants. This approach benefits from clear, concise code that is easily inferred as a ROM or multiplexer by synthesis tools, likely yielding better performance and power efficiency than chained ternary operators, and cleaner than multiple wire assignments. It also avoids indexing arrays of wires, which some tools might infer less optimally. This fusion produces a design that is both straightforward and synthesis-friendly.
