To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will replace the chained ternary operator with a case statement inside a combinational always block. This typically yields better synthesis results with clearer hardware, especially for a small input space like 3 bits. The case statement enables synthesis tools to implement an efficient multiplexer tree or ROM lookup with minimal switching and area. This approach also improves readability and can help reduce power consumption by minimizing unnecessary comparison logic toggling.
