The original solution uses a clean LUT-based approach with explicit constants and direct indexing, which is clear and synthesizable. However, to improve power and area slightly, we can replace the LUT array with a simple combinational 'case' statement.

A 'case' statement typically synthesizes into a multiplexer tree, which synthesis tools optimize well, potentially reducing gate count and switching activity compared to an array of constants. It avoids the potential overhead of wires and assignments related to the LUT array and constant declarations.

This approach maintains readability and functional clarity, while potentially yielding a slightly smaller and lower-power implementation, especially for such a small, fixed combinational mapping. It also remains purely combinational with zero delay.

Thus, I will implement the same constant mapping using a combinational 'case' statement in an always_comb block.
