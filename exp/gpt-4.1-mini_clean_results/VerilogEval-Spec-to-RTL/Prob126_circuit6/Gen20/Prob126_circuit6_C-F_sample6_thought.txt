Combine the clarity and synthesis-friendliness of a case statement with the succinctness of a direct combinational assignment by using a localparam array (ROM-style lookup table) indexed by input 'a'. This approach provides the cleanest and most scalable design: it allows constant-time decoding, minimal logic overhead, clear intent, and good power and area efficiency due to direct indexing rather than chained comparisons or multiple if-elses. Using a localparam array also enhances readability and maintainability, making any extension easy. The output is assigned combinationally by indexing this read-only table.
