The original design uses two separate 8-bit arrays for high and low bytes, which is unnecessary and slightly more complex. Since the output is just a fixed combinational lookup based on the 3-bit input, we can simplify by using a single 16-bit wide array to represent all output values and index into it directly.

This reduces the number of wires, simplifies code, and still synthesizes efficiently as a ROM or LUT without changing functionality.

The combinational circuit directly assigns q = LUT[a], where LUT is the 16-bit output array indexed by a.
