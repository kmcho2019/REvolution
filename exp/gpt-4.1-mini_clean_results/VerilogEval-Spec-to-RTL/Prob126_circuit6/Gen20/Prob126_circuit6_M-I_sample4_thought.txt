To improve PPA (performance, power, and area) while keeping the logic combinational and simple, I will replace the chained ternary operators with a case statement inside a combinational always block. This will typically synthesize into a more optimized multiplexer structure, enabling the synthesis tool to generate a balanced mux tree. It can reduce the critical path, minimize power due to less switching, and potentially reduce area. Using a reg type for output inside an always_comb block is standard practice for combinational lookup tables.
