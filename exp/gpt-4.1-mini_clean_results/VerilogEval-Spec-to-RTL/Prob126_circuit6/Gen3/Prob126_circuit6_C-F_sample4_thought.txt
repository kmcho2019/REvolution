Use a packed array (memory) of 16-bit constants indexed by the 3-bit input 'a', but implement it as a localparam array instead of wires to ensure the constants are embedded in logic optimally with no unnecessary shift operations. This approach avoids a large shift on a concatenated vector (which can add routing complexity and area), while keeping the interface clean and the logic directly inferred as a small ROM or multiplexers. It also maintains purely combinational behavior with direct indexing for minimal delay and power consumption. The use of localparam improves readability and synthesis guidance compared to separate wire assignments.
