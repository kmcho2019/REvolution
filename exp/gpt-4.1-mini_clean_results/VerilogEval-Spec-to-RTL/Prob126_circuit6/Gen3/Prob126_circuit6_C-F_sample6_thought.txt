Combine the clarity and compactness of the case statement approach with the efficiency of using a local parameter constant lookup table (array). This array-based lookup enables clean, scalable code while allowing the synthesis tool to optimize the mapping as a ROM or multiplexer internally, typically producing efficient area and power results. Using a localparam array indexed by 'a' also improves readability and maintainability compared to lengthy if-else or case blocks, and guarantees purely combinational logic without the need for reg outputs. This fusion leverages a parameterized combinational constant vector to deliver a concise, readable, and synthesis-friendly implementation.
