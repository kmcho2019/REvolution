To improve power and area efficiency slightly, replace the wire array with a localparam array and use a combinational always block with a case statement. This reduces switching activity by avoiding multiple continuous assignments and enables synthesis tools to optimize the logic more effectively. Using a case statement is often more power- and area-friendly for small ROM-like lookup tables, while retaining zero combinational delay. This style is clear and synthesis-friendly, potentially producing a smaller and lower-power netlist.
