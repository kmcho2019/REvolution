It also ensures that all input combinations are explicitly covered, which helps synthesis tools optimize the multiplexer structure and potentially generate a more efficient logic or ROM-based solution.

This also helps reduce redundant comparisons inherent in chained ternary operators, lowering power and area slightly. The case statement encourages the synthesis tool to infer a multiplexer or ROM lookup cleanly without priority encoding, which can improve timing (no long mux chains) and reduce switching activity.
