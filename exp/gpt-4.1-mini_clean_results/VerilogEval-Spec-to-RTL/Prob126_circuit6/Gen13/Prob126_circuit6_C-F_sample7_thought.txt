Fuse the clarity and concise indexing of the ROM array method with the structural clarity of a localparam array for synthesis friendliness and potential optimization. By using a localparam array for the constants and assigning q directly from it, we enable efficient synthesis as a constant ROM, improve readability, and reduce potential power and area by avoiding multiple separate assignments. This method combines the clean indexing of Example 2 with the synthesis-friendly constant declaration of Example 1's function-based approach, yielding a clean, maintainable, and optimal combinational decoder.
