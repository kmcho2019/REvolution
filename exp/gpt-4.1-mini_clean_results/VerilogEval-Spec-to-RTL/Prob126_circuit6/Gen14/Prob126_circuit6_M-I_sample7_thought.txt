To optimize for performance, power, and area while preserving combinational behavior, replace the long ternary chain with a combinational case statement inside an always_comb block. This approach can map better to LUTs or ROM structures in synthesis tools, improving both clarity and potential optimization. The case statement avoids priority encoding overhead inherent in chained ternary operators, which can reduce delay and area. This also improves readability and maintainability without changing the functionality.
