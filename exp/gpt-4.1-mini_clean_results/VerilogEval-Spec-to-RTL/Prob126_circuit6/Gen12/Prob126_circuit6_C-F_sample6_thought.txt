This combines the clarity and synthesis friendliness of the case statement with the concise, direct combinational mapping of the chained ternary operator or LUT indexing.

Specifically:
- Use a combinational case statement for improved clarity and explicit mapping, which synthesis tools optimize into minimal multiplexers.
- Avoid defining a separate LUT array to reduce overhead and potential synthesis of larger ROM structures.
- Keep the design purely combinational, simple, and directly reflecting the waveform's mapping.
- This provides clean, readable code that synthesis tools can efficiently map to hardware, with potentially better PPA than a naive LUT array or ternary chain.

This fusion yields a maintainable, efficient design that is easy to verify and optimize.
