Fuse the clarity and data-driven approach of using a wire array (ROM) indexed by 'a' with the maintainability and synthesis-friendliness of a single packed constant vector to avoid multiple continuous assignments. Use a localparam array to store the 16-bit constants, then assign 'q' combinationally by indexing this array.
