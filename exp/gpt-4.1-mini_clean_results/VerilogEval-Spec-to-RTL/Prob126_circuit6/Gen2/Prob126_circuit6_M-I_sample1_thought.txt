To optimize the design for power and area without impacting performance, we can encode the 16-bit output words as differences relative to a base constant (e.g., the value for a=0). Then, reconstruct the output by XORing the base with the encoded differences using a smaller LUT. This approach can reduce switching activity on the output bits and enable synthesis tools to optimize the logic better by exploiting shared sub-expressions. It also reduces the explicit storage of 8 full 16-bit constants. The combinational logic remains simple and fully synthesizable.
