To improve PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will replace the chained ternary operators with a "case" statement inside a combinational always block. This typically synthesizes into a more efficient and balanced multiplexer tree or ROM-like structure. The case statement improves synthesis friendliness and can reduce gate count and switching activity. Also, defining 'q' as reg within the always_comb block is idiomatic and makes intent clearer for synthesis tools.
