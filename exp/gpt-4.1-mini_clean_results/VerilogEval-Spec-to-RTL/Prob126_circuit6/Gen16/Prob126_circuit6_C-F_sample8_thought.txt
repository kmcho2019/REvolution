The best solution combines the clarity and synthesis friendliness of defining a constant ROM via a packed array with the simplicity and explicitness of a direct continuous assignment without reg or always blocks. To further improve, the ROM content will be declared as a localparam packed array, ensuring the constants are truly read-only and potentially better optimized by synthesis tools as a ROM or LUT. This approach avoids multiple wire assignments, making the code cleaner and more synthesis-friendly. Using a localparam array also helps to prevent accidental modification and may allow for easier parameterization in future. The module remains fully combinational with zero runtime overhead, minimal area, and straightforward timing.
