The original code uses two separate 8-bit arrays for the high and low bytes of the output LUT. To refactor, I will combine these two arrays into a single 16-bit LUT array indexed by the input `a`. This simplifies the structure, reduces signal count, and potentially improves power and area due to fewer wires and simpler routing. The functionality remains the same: the output `q` is assigned combinationally from the LUT indexed by `a`. I will implement this using a single packed array and a direct assign statement for combinational output.
