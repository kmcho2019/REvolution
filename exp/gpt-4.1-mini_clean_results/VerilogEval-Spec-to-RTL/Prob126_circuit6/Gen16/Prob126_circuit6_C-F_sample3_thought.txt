Fuse the clarity and synthesis friendliness of the case statement approach with the direct combinational style of continuous assignment. Implement the mapping inside a function that returns the 16-bit output for given 3-bit input, called within a continuous assignment. This approach improves readability, is easily scalable, and synthesis tools handle functions efficiently, often translating to minimal mux logic or ROM/LUTs. It reduces the chained ternary complexity, keeps logic purely combinational, and provides a clean abstraction of the lookup table.
