Implement the combinational logic as a binary tree of multiplexers, progressively selecting between pairs of the known constants. This approach encodes the lookup table through nested conditional expressions (ternary operators), avoiding an explicit array or case statement. The nested mux style could help synthesis tools optimize the logic into balanced trees, potentially improving timing and area by leveraging shared logic in hardware.
