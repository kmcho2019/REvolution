To optimize for power and potentially area without sacrificing performance:

- Add input change detection to gate the updating of prev_in and pedge registers. By comparing current and previous inputs, we detect if any bit changed.
- If no bits changed, avoid updating registers, thus reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption.
- This clock enable gating is implemented as a simple enable signal derived from XOR of in and prev_in.
- The combinational edge detection remains the same to keep minimal logic depth.
- This approach reduces switching activity on registers and output, improving power while maintaining performance and minimal area impact.
