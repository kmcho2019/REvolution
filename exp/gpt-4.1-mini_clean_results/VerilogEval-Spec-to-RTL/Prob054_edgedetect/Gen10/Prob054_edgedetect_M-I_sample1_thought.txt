To improve the design for better power and potentially area while keeping performance stable, the strategy is:

- Add an enable signal that activates the registers update only when there is any change in input `in`. This reduces unnecessary toggling of `prev_in` and `pedge` registers, lowering dynamic power consumption.

- The enable can be computed as a simple comparison between current input and previous input (`in != prev_in`), which is cheap logic-wise.

- Use a clock enable style update: registers update only when `enable` is high; otherwise, hold their value.

- This gating reduces switching activity in registers and output, reducing power.

- Since the detection logic is simple, performance should be unaffected or improved due to reduced toggling.

- Area impact is minimal since only one equality comparator and some gating logic are added.

- This approach keeps the design synchronous and stable, maintaining correctness (pedge asserted one cycle after 0->1 transition) and minimal latency.

- Overall, the design becomes more power efficient with little to no impact on timing or area.
