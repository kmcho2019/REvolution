To optimize the design for better power and potentially area, introduce an enable signal for updating the `prev_in` register only when the input changes. This reduces unnecessary toggling of `prev_in` bits, which lowers dynamic power. The positive edge detection logic remains the same, but `prev_in` updates only when `in` differs from `prev_in`. This change avoids redundant register updates when inputs are stable, minimizing switching activity without adding clock gating complexity. The output `pedge` still reflects the previous cycle's 0-to-1 transitions.
