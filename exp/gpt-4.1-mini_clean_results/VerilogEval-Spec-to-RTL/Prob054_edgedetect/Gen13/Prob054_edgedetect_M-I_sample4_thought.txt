To optimize the design for better power and area without sacrificing performance, implement clock gating on the `prev_in` register updates to avoid unnecessary toggling when the input vector is stable (no changes from previous cycle). This reduces dynamic power due to switching.

Specifically:  
- Compare `in` with `prev_in` each clock cycle; update `prev_in` only if `in` has changed (using a clock-enable signal).  
- Use this clock-enable to gate the register update, reducing unnecessary flip-flop toggling.  
- Keep `pedge` registering as is for timing stability and glitch avoidance.

This approach reduces power by minimizing unnecessary switching in the state register while keeping the positive edge detection logic intact and maintaining high frequency performance with minimal added logic.
