A better simplification is to keep a single always block with two registers and update pedge and prev_in as in the original code but with minimal code and no unnecessary comments or spacing. This keeps the logic concise and clear.

Hence, the simplest and clearest implementation is essentially the same as before, using just one always block and two registers, with pedge assigned as (~prev_in) & in.
