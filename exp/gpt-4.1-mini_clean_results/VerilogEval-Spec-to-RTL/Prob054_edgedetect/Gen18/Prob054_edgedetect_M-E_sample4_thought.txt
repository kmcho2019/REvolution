Use two registers to pipeline the input: one to store the current input and one for the previous cycle's input. Then, generate the pedge output combinationally by detecting bits that were 0 two cycles ago and 1 in the previous cycle, and then registering the result. This creates a two-stage pipelined positive edge detector that asserts pedge one cycle after the input transitions from 0 to 1. This approach explicitly pipelines the input signals for potentially improved timing and separates the edge detection combinational logic from the registering of the output.
