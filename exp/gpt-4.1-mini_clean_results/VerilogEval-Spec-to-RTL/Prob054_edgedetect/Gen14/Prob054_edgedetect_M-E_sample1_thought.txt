Implement the positive edge detection by explicitly storing the previous input state in a non-blocking always block triggered by the clock. Then, create the output pedge as a registered signal updated at the next clock cycle after detecting a 0-to-1 transition. Instead of combining the logic into one block, separate the state storage and edge detection into two always blocks to clarify the data flow and timing: one block to store previous input, and another block to compute and store the output edge detection result based on previous and current inputs. This pipelined approach adds a cycle of latency to pedge but clearly stages the logic and can be beneficial for timing closure and modularity.
