To optimize for better power and area while maintaining performance, I will combine the prev_in and pedge registers into one 8-bit register, storing the positive edge detection bits directly and updating prev_in only when needed. This avoids duplicating registers by replacing prev_in with a flip-flop that only updates on input change, reducing unnecessary toggling and saving area. Also, by gating the update of pedge so it only asserts bits when transitions happen, we reduce switching activity for power saving. This approach keeps the logic simple and synchronous but reduces register count and switching, which improves area and power without impacting timing significantly.
