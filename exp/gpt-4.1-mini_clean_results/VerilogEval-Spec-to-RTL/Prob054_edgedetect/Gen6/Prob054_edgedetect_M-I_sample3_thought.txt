The original design is already clean and minimal for positive edge detection on each bit of the vector. One small optimization opportunity is to avoid storing pedge as a register and instead make it a combinational output based on the stored previous input and current input. This way, we save one clock cycle of latency and remove one register, reducing area and power. 

Because the requirement states that pedge should be "set the cycle after a 0 to 1 transition occurs," this means pedge is registered output reflecting the previous cycle's edge. To meet this while reducing registers, we can register only prev_in and generate pedge combinationally from (prev_in, in).

Removing the pedge register reduces area and toggling power. Also, since prev_in is registered and pedge output is combinational, timing may improve due to one less flip-flop stage.

Hence, the improved solution registers only prev_in; pedge is computed as (~prev_in) & in combinationally.

This meets the functional specification and improves PPA: lower area, less power, and potentially improved timing.
