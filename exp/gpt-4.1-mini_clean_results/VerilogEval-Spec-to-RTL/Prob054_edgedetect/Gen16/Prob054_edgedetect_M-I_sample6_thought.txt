Optimize the design by eliminating the redundant 'current_in' register and directly capturing 'prev_in' from the input 'in' each clock cycle. The positive edge detection logic will then simply compare the previous input (prev_in) with the current input (in) combinationally. This reduces register count from two 8-bit registers to one 8-bit register, lowering area and power, while maintaining the same one-cycle delay for the output. The logic remains simple and timing-critical paths are minimal, enhancing performance slightly.
