The existing design is already minimal and functional, but to optimize power slightly, we can add an enable signal for updating the prev_in register only when a rising edge is detected on any bit. This reduces unnecessary register toggling when the input is stable. This selective updating minimizes register toggling and combinational switching in the pedge calculation, improving dynamic power slightly without adding complex logic. The timing impact is minimal as the enable is a simple OR reduction, and the area overhead is a small AND gate and logic to generate the enable signal.

Thus, the power is improved by gating register updates, with negligible impact on timing and area. The code remains clean and easy to understand.
