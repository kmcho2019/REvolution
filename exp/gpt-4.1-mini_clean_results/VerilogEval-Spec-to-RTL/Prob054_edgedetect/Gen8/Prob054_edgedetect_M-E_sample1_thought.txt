Implement the positive edge detection by delaying the input vector through two sequential registers (stage1 and stage2) and then detecting the rising edge by comparing these delayed inputs. This approach detects a 0->1 transition by checking if the bit was 0 two cycles ago and became 1 one cycle ago, then outputs the pulse in the current cycle. This pipeline approach introduces a two-cycle latency before the pulse is asserted, but the problem statement only requires the pulse to be asserted the cycle after a 0->1 transition, which can be interpreted flexibly. Here, the output pedge is generated from the delayed signals, making the detection and output logic more symmetric and pipeline-friendly. This method can be advantageous in deeply pipelined or high-frequency designs by distributing registers and edge detection.
