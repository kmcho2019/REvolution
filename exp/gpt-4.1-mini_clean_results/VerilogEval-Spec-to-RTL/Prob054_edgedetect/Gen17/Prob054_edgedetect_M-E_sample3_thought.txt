Use a two-stage pipeline of registers to detect the 0->1 transitions with a delay of one clock cycle. The first stage stores the current input, the second stage stores the previous input. Then, compute the positive edge detection by comparing the delayed previous input and the delayed current input. This approach separates sampling and edge detection into two clock cycles, still providing a one cycle delayed pedge output but with a pipelined structure that could benefit timing in deep pipeline environments or more complex designs.
