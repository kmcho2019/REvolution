To optimize the original solution for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will eliminate the always block by implementing next_state as a continuous assignment using nested conditional (ternary) operators. This removes the need for a combinational always block and potentially reduces logic levels for synthesis tools. For output logic, it remains a simple continuous assignment as before. Using localparams for state encoding maintains readability and synthesis friendliness. This approach simplifies combinational logic and may yield marginal improvements in PPA by lowering logic depth and area.
