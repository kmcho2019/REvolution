To improve the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) of the FSM, I will:

- Implement next_state using continuous assignments with ternary operators to enable easier logic optimization and sharing of common terms by synthesis tools. This often helps reduce gate count and power by allowing expression factoring.
- Keep output as a single comparison expression, which is already minimal and optimal.
- Use localparams for readability.
- Avoid always blocks to reduce inferred logic overhead and improve combinational logic mapping.
  
This approach can reduce logic duplication in next_state decoding and improve area and power slightly, while maintaining clarity and correctness.
