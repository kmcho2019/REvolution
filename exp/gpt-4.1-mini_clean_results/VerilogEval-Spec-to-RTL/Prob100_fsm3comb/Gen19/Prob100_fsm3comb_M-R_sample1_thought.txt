Refactor the implementation to use a combinational always block with a case statement for both next_state and output logic. This style provides explicit and clear expression of state transitions and output assignments, which synthesis tools often optimize well. The case statement improves readability, reduces possible redundant condition checks compared to nested ternaries, and makes extending the FSM easier. Outputs and next_state signals are declared as reg and assigned inside the always block. This structure still maintains purely combinational logic without registers or clocks.
