Implement the combinational next state and output logic using a lookup table approach with an array of packed 3-bit entries: two bits for next_state and one bit for output. The LUT is indexed by the concatenation of current state and input (3 bits). This approach encodes all transitions and outputs in a single combinational ROM-like structure, enabling compact and direct indexing without conditionals or case statements. The next_state and out outputs are extracted from the LUT entry bits. This style is different from conditional logic, providing a clean, data-driven FSM combinational logic representation.
