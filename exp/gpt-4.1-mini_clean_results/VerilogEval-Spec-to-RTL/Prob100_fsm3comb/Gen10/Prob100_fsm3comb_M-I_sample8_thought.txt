To optimize the design for better PPA, we focus on reducing area and slightly improving timing by using continuous assignment with nested ternary operators instead of a case statement, eliminating the always block. This reduces the inferred logic by allowing synthesis tools to directly optimize the multiplexers in combinational logic. Also, combining output logic into a simple equality check as before maintains minimal logic. This approach makes the combinational next_state and output signals purely combinational expressions, potentially reducing logic levels and area without sacrificing readability or correctness.
