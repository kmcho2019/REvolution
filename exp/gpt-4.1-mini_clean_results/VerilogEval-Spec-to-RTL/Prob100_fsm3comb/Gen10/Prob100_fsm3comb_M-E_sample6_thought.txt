Use a lookup table (LUT) approach with a single combinational always block to implement next_state and output. Encode all transitions and outputs into a small array indexed by {state, in}, enabling direct indexing for the next state and output values. This approach separates logic clearly, makes the mapping explicit, and could simplify synthesis by enabling ROM-like structures or multiplexers. It reduces conditional logic and leverages parallel case decoding, improving clarity and potentially improving timing by eliminating multiple nested condition checks.
