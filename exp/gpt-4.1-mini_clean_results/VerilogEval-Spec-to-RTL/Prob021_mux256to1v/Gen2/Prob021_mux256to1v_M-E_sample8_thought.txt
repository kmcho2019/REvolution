Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit wide multiplexer hierarchically using a tree of smaller multiplexers. Specifically, break the 8-bit selector into 4 groups of 2 bits each and perform multiplexing in four stages. Each stage selects among 4 inputs from the previous stage, effectively constructing a 4-level 4-to-1 mux tree. This modular approach creates balanced logic stages, potentially improving timing, and is synthesizable. The tree is implemented combinationally using continuous assignments.
