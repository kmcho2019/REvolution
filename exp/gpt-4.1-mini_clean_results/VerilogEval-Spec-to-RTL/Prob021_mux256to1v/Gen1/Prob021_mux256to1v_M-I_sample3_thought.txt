To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, area), break down the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer into a two-level hierarchical mux: first select one of 16 groups of 16 4-bit words, then select one within that group. This reduces the size and fan-in of any single multiplexer, potentially improving timing and power by partitioning the large mux. Also, registering the intermediate stage can improve timing if pipelining is acceptable, but here we keep it purely combinational for minimal latency. Use explicit case statements for small 16:1 muxes, which synthesis tools can efficiently implement with small, fast mux trees. This approach partitions logic hierarchically and potentially reduces area and power compared to a flat mux using a wide indexed part-select.
