Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer using a hierarchical structure of smaller multiplexers. Specifically, first break down the input into 16 groups of 16 4-bit slices. Each group of 16 slices is multiplexed by a 4-bit selector (lower 4 bits of sel) into a 4-bit intermediate result. Then a final 16-to-1 4-bit multiplexer selects among these 16 intermediate results using the upper 4 bits of sel. This approach reduces the overall mux fan-in at each stage, potentially improving synthesis quality and timing by creating a balanced mux tree.
