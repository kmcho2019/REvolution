Use a fully combinational, direct indexing approach that exploits Verilog's variable part-selects to implement the 256-to-1 mux in a single expression. Since inputs are packed as 256 4-bit words in a 1024-bit vector, and `sel` selects which word to output, simply compute the start bit of the selected 4-bit word as `sel * 4` and slice directly out of `in`. This approach avoids hierarchical mux trees, reduces logic depth and area, and yields the most efficient design possible for this problem.
