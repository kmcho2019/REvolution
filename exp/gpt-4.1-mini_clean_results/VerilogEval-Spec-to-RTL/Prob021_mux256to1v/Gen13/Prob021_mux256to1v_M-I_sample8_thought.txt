To optimize power, performance, and area (PPA), we replace the multi-level mux hierarchy with a direct indexed bit-slice from the packed input vector. Since the inputs are packed as 256 4-bit words consecutively, the output is simply the 4-bit slice starting at sel*4. This approach eliminates multiple mux instances and intermediate wiring, reducing logic complexity and delay.

This flat mux implementation requires no submodules or case statements, enabling synthesis tools to implement an efficient direct multiplexer with minimal area and delay.

In summary:
- Use a single continuous assignment to select the 4-bit slice indexed by the 8-bit sel.
- Remove all hierarchical mux modules and intermediate signals.
- Maintain full combinational behavior with no registers or always blocks.

This yields a minimal, clean, and highly efficient 256-to-1, 4-bit wide multiplexer.
