Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer using a fully combinational one-level approach by directly indexing into the packed input vector with the 8-bit select signal. This can be done by calculating the starting bit position as sel * 4 and slicing 4 bits from `in`.

- This approach avoids any hierarchical or staged multiplexing.
- It greatly simplifies the design, improving area, power, and performance by eliminating intermediate mux trees.
- It leverages Verilog's bit-select indexing with variable part-select `[start +: width]`.
- Since `sel` is 8 bits selecting among 256 4-bit groups, `sel*4` generates the bit offset directly.
- The output `out` can be assigned continuously without any registers or case statements.
- This single-level indexing approach leads to a very compact and efficient RTL.
