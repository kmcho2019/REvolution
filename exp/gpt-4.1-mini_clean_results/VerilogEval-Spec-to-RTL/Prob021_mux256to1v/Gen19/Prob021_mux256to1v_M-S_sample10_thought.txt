Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit mux directly as a simple combinational expression by leveraging indexed part-selects. Since each 4-bit input block corresponds to 4 bits in the input vector and the selector `sel` chooses which block, directly select `out = in[sel*4 +: 4]`. This removes the need for hierarchical mux modules, intermediate signals, and generate blocks, drastically reducing complexity, area, and power while maintaining functionality.
