The original hierarchical multiplexer implementation is functionally correct but introduces extra logic levels and intermediate signals that increase area and power and may degrade timing.

To optimize for better PPA:
- Replace hierarchical mux stages and unpacked arrays with a direct indexed part-select.
- This will reduce combinational logic levels to a single indexing operation.
- Eliminates intermediate registers and wires, reducing area and switching power.
- Direct indexing is synthesizable and maps efficiently to hardware multiplexers.
- The output is simply assigned from the input vector with offset sel*4 and width 4.

This approach produces minimal, efficient logic that implements the required 256-to-1, 4-bit mux with a single continuous assignment.
