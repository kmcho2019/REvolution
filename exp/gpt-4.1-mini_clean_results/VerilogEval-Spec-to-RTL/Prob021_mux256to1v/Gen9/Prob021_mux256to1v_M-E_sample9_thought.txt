Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer using a hierarchical two-stage multiplexing approach to balance complexity and depth. First, break the 8-bit select input into two parts: the higher 4 bits select one of 16 blocks, each block containing 16 consecutive 4-bit inputs. Each of these 16 blocks is implemented as a 16-to-1, 4-bit mux selecting among its 16 inputs using the lower 4 bits of sel. Finally, a second 16-to-1 mux selects among the outputs of these 16 blocks using the higher 4 bits of sel. This approach uses smaller multiplexers and exploits structural hierarchy to reduce the maximum fan-in at each mux, improving timing and power compared to a flat large multiplexer or one-hot decode approach.
