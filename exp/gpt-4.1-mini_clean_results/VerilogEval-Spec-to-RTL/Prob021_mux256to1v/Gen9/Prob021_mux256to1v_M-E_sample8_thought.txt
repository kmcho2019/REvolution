Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit multiplexer by building it hierarchically as two stages of 16-to-1 multiplexers followed by a final 16-to-1 multiplexer. The 1024-bit input is first split into 16 groups of 64 bits (each group representing 16 4-bit words). The lower 4 bits of sel select one 4-bit word from each group in parallel (using 16 instances of a 16-to-1 mux). The outputs of these 16 multiplexers (each 4-bit) are then fed into a final 16-to-1 mux selected by the upper 4 bits of sel. This reduces the mux fan-in at each stage from 256 to 16, potentially improving timing by breaking the large mux into smaller, faster stages and potentially saving area due to smaller muxes synthesized per stage.
