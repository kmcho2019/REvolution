To optimize for better performance, power, and area, replace the hierarchical multiple-module instantiation and large case statements with a single-level direct arithmetic indexing approach. 

By computing the output as a direct slice of the input vector using `sel` as an index, we eliminate intermediate wires, generate loops, and large case constructs. This reduces logic levels, fanout, and switching activity, thereby improving timing and power consumption. It also minimizes area by allowing synthesis to infer a straightforward mux tree using indexed part selects instead of multiple nested mux modules.

This approach uses only a single combinational assignment without auxiliary modules, making the design compact and efficient while maintaining full functionality.
