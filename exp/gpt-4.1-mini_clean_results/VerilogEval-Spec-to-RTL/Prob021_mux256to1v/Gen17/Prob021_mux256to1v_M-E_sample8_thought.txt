Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit-wide multiplexer using a single continuous assignment with variable part-select indexing. Since the inputs are packed as 256 contiguous 4-bit words in a 1024-bit vector, and sel chooses the word index, the output can be directly assigned as the 4-bit slice starting at bit 4*sel. This approach minimizes logic hierarchy, avoids instantiating multiple small muxes, and results in compact, efficient code. The synthesis tool can implement this as a large multiplexer or optimized logic. This method yields minimal area and power, and simpler timing analysis, trading off explicit hierarchy for simplicity and potentially better performance.
