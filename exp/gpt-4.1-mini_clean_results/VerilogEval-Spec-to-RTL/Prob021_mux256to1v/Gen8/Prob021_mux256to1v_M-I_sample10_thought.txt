Optimize the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer by directly indexing into the input vector using Verilog's variable part-select (bit slicing) to extract the selected 4-bit slice, eliminating the need for one-hot decoding, masking, and large arrays. This approach drastically reduces combinational logic depth and area, lowers switching activity by activating only the selected slice, and leads to simpler and more efficient synthesis. The `out` is assigned combinationally from `in[sel*4 +: 4]`.
