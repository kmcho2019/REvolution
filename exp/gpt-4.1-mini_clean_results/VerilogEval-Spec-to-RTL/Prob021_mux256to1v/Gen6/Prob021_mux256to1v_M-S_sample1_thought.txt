Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer using a direct indexed part-select on the packed input vector. The selected output bits can be obtained by slicing 'in' at position sel*4 with a width of 4 bits. This eliminates the need to unpack the input into an array and avoids hierarchical mux stages and case statements, thus simplifying the design and reducing logic, area, and power while maintaining the same functionality.
