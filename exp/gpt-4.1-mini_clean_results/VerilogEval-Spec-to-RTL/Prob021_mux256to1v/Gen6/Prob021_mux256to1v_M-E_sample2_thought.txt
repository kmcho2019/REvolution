Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit wide multiplexer by using direct arithmetic indexing of the input vector to avoid explicit unpacking and hierarchical multiplexing. Since the input vector 'in' is 1024 bits wide (256 groups of 4 bits), we can calculate the starting bit position of the selected 4-bit chunk as sel * 4, and select those 4 bits directly with a single indexed part-select.

This approach minimizes logic, reduces power and area by avoiding intermediate mux stages and unpacking arrays. It also improves timing by removing hierarchical mux delays and enabling the synthesis tool to map a single, simple multiplexer.

No generate loops or case statements are needed; just a single continuous assignment.

This idea uses Verilog's indexed part-select with arithmetic on the selection signal.
