Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit wide multiplexer using a direct combinational bit slicing approach without any intermediate mux modules or hierarchies. Since each of the 256 inputs is 4 bits and packed consecutively in a 1024-bit vector, the output can be directly selected by indexing into the input vector using the 8-bit selector multiplied by 4. This single-level direct indexing mux reduces logic levels, improves timing by eliminating intermediate mux trees, reduces area by removing submodules and extra wires, and lowers power by minimizing switching through fewer logic gates. This approach leverages Verilog's variable part-select syntax for succinctness and clarity.
