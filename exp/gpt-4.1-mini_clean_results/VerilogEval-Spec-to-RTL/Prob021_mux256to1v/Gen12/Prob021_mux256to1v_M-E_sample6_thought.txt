Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer by leveraging a single arithmetic shift operation to compute the exact bit slice directly instead of building multiple hierarchical mux stages. Use the `sel` index multiplied by 4 to generate the base bit offset, then assign the 4-bit output by slicing the input vector at that offset. This approach removes any internal multiplexer hierarchy, minimizing delay, area, and power by using a simple vector slice indexed by the product of `sel` and 4. The implementation uses a combinational `always @(*)` block and a variable offset to select the required bits, making the design straightforward, highly efficient, and easy to maintain.
