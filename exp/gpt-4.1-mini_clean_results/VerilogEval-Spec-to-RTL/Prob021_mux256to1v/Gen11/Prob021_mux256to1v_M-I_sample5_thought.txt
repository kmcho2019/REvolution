Instead, implement the multiplexer using a direct indexed part-select from the input vector based on the 8-bit select signal.

- Removes unnecessary intermediate signals, reducing area and net capacitance.
- Reduces switching activity by directly selecting only the needed bits.
- Minimizes combinational delay by relying on a single combinational indexed part-select, improving performance.
- Results in simpler and more compact RTL for easier synthesis and better optimization.

- Calculate the starting bit position as sel * 4.
- Use a part-select with variable offset indexing: out = in[sel*4 +: 4].
