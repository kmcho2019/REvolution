Optimize the 256-to-1, 4-bit multiplexer by removing the hierarchical two-level mux and replacing it with a single-level direct bit slice using arithmetic indexing. Since `sel` selects which 4-bit chunk from the packed 1024-bit input, the output can be directly assigned by slicing `in` at `sel * 4`. This eliminates the need for any intermediate registers or case statements, improving timing by reducing mux depth, reducing area by eliminating the large case mux logic, and reducing power by minimizing switching logic.
