To optimize the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer for better area and power, replace the large barrel shifter with explicit hierarchical multiplexing. We can decompose the 8-bit select input into two 4-bit indices: the upper 4 bits (sel[7:4]) to select one of 16 groups, each consisting of 16 4-bit segments, and the lower 4 bits (sel[3:0]) to select one of 16 within that group. First, use 16 16-to-1 muxes to reduce the 256 inputs into 16 outputs. Then, use one 16-to-1 mux to select the final 4-bit output based on the upper 4 bits. This tree reduces the number of inputs each mux handles, limits wide multiplexing at once, and avoids a large barrel shifter, improving area and power while maintaining combinational performance.
