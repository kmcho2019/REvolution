To improve the given 3-level hierarchical 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer for better PPA (performance, power, area), I aim to:

1. Reduce the number of mux stages from three to two to lower logic depth and critical path delay:
   - Use sixteen 16-to-1 4-bit muxes in stage 1 indexed by sel[3:0].
   - Use one final 16-to-1 4-bit mux in stage 2 indexed by sel[7:4].

2. Flatten the top-level mux structure compared to the previous three-level tree, reducing pipeline latency and power.

3. Keep the design fully combinational and modular with parameterizable 16-to-1 mux modules.

4. Improve the synthesis friendliness by using indexed part selects directly and avoiding unnecessary flattening/unflattening arrays between stages.

5. This approach reduces the number of intermediate nets and mux stages, which should lower area and dynamic power while improving maximum clock frequency due to reduced logic levels.

6. Additionally, provide clear comments and maintain modularity for maintainability.

This two-stage structure balances logic complexity and mux input size effectively, likely improving timing and power over the prior 3-stage design.
