Use a packed 2D array representation for the inputs as 256 entries of 4-bit words directly by unpacking the 1024-bit vector. This allows simple and direct indexing without any intermediate muxing layers or large case statements. The selection of the 4-bit word is then just a direct array index using sel as the index. This greatly simplifies the design and should synthesize efficiently.

To implement this, define a localparam word count and word width. Use a generate loop to assign each 4-bit chunk from the input vector into an array of [255:0] 4-bit words. The output is assigned as the selected word indexed by sel.

This approach leverages Verilog's packed arrays and indexing capabilities, avoids any manual mux decoding or large case statements, and is compact and easy to maintain.
