The previous hierarchical mux solution is functionally correct but can be optimized for better PPA by using a direct indexed part-select. Since the inputs are packed as 256 groups of 4 bits (1024 bits total), and sel is 8 bits selecting which group, a direct part-select using in[sel*4 +: 4] achieves the same 256-to-1 4-bit mux in a single combinational assignment without unpacking or intermediate mux stages.

This direct indexing approach:
- Reduces combinational path complexity to a single mux stage, improving timing.
- Eliminates intermediate unpacked arrays and registers, reducing area and power by cutting net capacitance and switching activity.
- Simplifies the code, making it easier for synthesis tools to infer efficient mux structures.

Thus, replacing the entire hierarchical structure with a direct continuous assignment `assign out = in[sel*4 +: 4];` is the best improvement here.
