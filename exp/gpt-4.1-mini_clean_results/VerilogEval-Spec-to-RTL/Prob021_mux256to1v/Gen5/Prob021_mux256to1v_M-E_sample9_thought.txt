Implement the 256-to-1, 4-bit multiplexer by directly indexing into the packed input vector using the 8-bit selector `sel`. Since each 4-bit slice is contiguous and uniformly packed, we can use the Verilog bit-select `in[sel*4 +: 4]` syntax to select the 4-bit chunk dynamically. This avoids generating one-hot encodings, masks, or large arrays, significantly reducing combinational logic, area, and power. This direct indexing approach is concise, synthesizes efficiently, and leverages the synthesis tool's natural mux inference capabilities.
