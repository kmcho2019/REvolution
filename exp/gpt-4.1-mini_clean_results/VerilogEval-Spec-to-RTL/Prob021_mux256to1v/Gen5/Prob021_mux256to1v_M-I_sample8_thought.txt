Optimize the 256-to-1, 4-bit multiplexer by replacing the large case statement with a continuous assignment using a variable part-select indexed by 'sel'. This approach directly selects 4 bits at an offset of sel*4, greatly simplifying the logic. It reduces area and power by removing explicit enumeration and improves performance by enabling the synthesizer to infer a simple wiring offset or efficient mux tree. The code becomes more concise and efficient while maintaining correct functionality.
