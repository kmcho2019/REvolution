To optimize the original hierarchical 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer implementation for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we will eliminate intermediate unpacking arrays and hierarchical mux stages. Instead, we will use a direct indexed part-select expression `out = in[sel*4 +: 4]`. This approach:

1. Reduces combinational delay by implementing a flat, single-level mux, minimizing logic depth.
2. Lowers switching activity and net capacitance by avoiding unpacking into arrays and intermediate signals.
3. Decreases area by synthesizing fewer logic elements, as most synthesis tools efficiently handle indexed part-selects.
4. Simplifies code, improving readability and maintainability.

Given that the input vector is fully packed and the select controls a contiguous 4-bit slice, this direct indexing provides an equivalent and more efficient implementation.
