To improve PPA from the original hierarchical design, I will simplify the implementation by leveraging a direct indexed part-select using the input selection vector `sel` as a single arithmetic index. This eliminates all intermediate hierarchical mux stages and arrays, reducing logic, wiring, and switching activity.

Specifically:

- Use a single combinational always block that selects 4 bits directly from `in` via indexed part-select `in[sel*4 +: 4]`.
- This avoids large case statements and multiple generate loops.
- The synthesis tool can implement this as a simple and efficient mux tree.
- This approach reduces area and power by minimizing intermediate signals and logic levels.
- Timing should improve due to less logic depth and fanout.

This design is functionally equivalent, much simpler, and optimized for PPA while maintaining clear, synthesizable code.
