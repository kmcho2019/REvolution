To optimize the 256-to-1, 4-bit wide multiplexer in terms of power, performance, and area (PPA), replace the hierarchical multi-level mux structure with a single-level direct bit slicing approach.

Since the input `in` is a packed vector of 256 4-bit values, indexed consecutively, and `sel` is 8 bits selecting one of the 256 entries, we can directly extract the desired 4-bit slice by indexing as `in[sel*4 +: 4]`. This eliminates intermediate mux levels, reducing logic depth and area, and decreasing switching activity by avoiding redundant muxes.

This approach improves timing by removing mux chains, saves area by using minimal logic, and reduces power by minimizing switching.

The module becomes a simple combinational assignment, making it straightforward, efficient, and synthesizable across all tools.

No additional submodules or case statements are necessary.

The direct bit-slice solution will yield better PPA metrics than the hierarchical mux tree.
