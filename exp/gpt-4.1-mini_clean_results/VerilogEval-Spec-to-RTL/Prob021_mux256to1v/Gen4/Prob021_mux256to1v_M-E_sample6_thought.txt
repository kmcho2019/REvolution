Implement the 256-to-1 4-bit multiplexer by first splitting the 1024-bit input into 256 4-bit slices, then use a 256-to-1 one-hot decoder driven by `sel` to generate one-hot signals for each slice. Perform a bitwise AND between each 4-bit slice and its corresponding one-hot bit replicated to 4 bits, and OR-reduce all these masked slices to produce the final output. This approach uses a one-hot selection mechanism rather than hierarchical muxing or direct indexing, providing explicit structural clarity and potentially better parallelism or ease of mapping to LUTs in some architectures.
