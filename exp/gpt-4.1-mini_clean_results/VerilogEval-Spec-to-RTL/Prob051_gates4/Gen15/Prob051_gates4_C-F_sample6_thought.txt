Fuse the succinctness and clarity of parameterized, reusable 4-input gate modules built as balanced two-input gate trees with the direct and simple use of Verilog built-in reduction operators, by creating a parameterized module that can either instantiate explicit gate trees or directly assign reduction operators based on a parameter. Then, instantiate this module three times inside TopModule for AND, OR, and XOR outputs, minimizing intermediate signals and maximizing synthesis-friendly explicitness. This hybrid approach leverages the readability and scalability of modular design, the clarity and potential optimal synthesis mapping of explicit gate primitives, and the compact expressiveness of reduction operators, thus achieving the best trade-offs in maintainability, performance, power, and area for a small combinational design.
