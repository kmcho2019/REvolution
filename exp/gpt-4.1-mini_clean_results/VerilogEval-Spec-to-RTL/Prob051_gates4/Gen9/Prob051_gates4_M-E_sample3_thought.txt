Implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR outputs using Verilog reduction operators applied directly on the 4-bit input vector. This approach leverages concise behavioral expressions to represent multi-input logic gates efficiently. The reduction operators (&, |, ^) internally perform the required operations across all bits of the input, simplifying the code and eliminating intermediate wires or gate instantiations. This method is succinct and synthesizes to optimized hardware with minimal delay and area. It also improves readability and maintainability by abstracting away the gate-level detail.
