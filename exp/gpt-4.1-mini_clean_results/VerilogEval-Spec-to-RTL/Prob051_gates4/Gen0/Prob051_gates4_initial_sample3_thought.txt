Implement a combinational module TopModule with a 4-bit input 'in' and three outputs 'out_and', 'out_or', and 'out_xor'. Compute out_and as the AND of all 4 bits of 'in', out_or as the OR of all 4 bits, and out_xor as the XOR of all 4 bits. Use bitwise reduction operators to simplify the expressions.
