Since the original solution already uses Verilog reduction operators, which are the most concise and efficient way to compute the AND, OR, and XOR of all bits in a vector, there is minimal room for RTL-level optimization. However, for slightly better readability and possibly more explicit synthesis results, one can unroll the logic using bitwise operations explicitly, though this likely won't improve PPA.

An alternate approach is to pipeline or register outputs to improve timing, but that adds latency and area.

Given the purely combinational and minimal nature of the design, the best improvement for PPA is to keep the current approach.

Therefore, the improvement is to maintain the original design as it is already optimal in terms of area, power, and timing for a simple 4-input AND/OR/XOR combinational logic.

For demonstration, we can also add comments clarifying each output's purpose to improve code readability without affecting PPA.
