Instead of using Verilog reduction operators directly on the input vector, this design explicitly instantiates four 1-bit AND, OR, and XOR gates for each bit and then combines their outputs hierarchically in two stages to produce the final results. This approach simulates a small tree of gates, which can help better illustrate gate-level structuring and may provide more explicit control during synthesis for gate placement or timing optimizations.

Specifically:
- For the AND output, first AND pairs of bits, then AND the results.
- Similarly for OR and XOR outputs.

This modular style can also make it easier to scale to larger input widths or to add pipelining stages if needed.

The trade-off is slightly more verbose code, but it provides an architectural pattern that can be used in other contexts.
