The original implementation is already highly optimized in terms of performance, power, and area since it uses native Verilog reduction operators that map directly to minimal hardware gates. Further PPA improvements at the RTL level are unlikely for such simple combinational logic. 

However, to make the design slightly more explicit and potentially help some synthesis tools with better mapping and optimization, I will:
- Declare outputs as wires explicitly (already done).
- Use continuous assignments directly within the port declaration for conciseness and clarity.
- Add comments explaining each output function for readability and maintainability.
- Use a compact style that synthesizers recognize for efficient mapping.

These changes do not alter the logic or increase area/power and may help the synthesis tool's interpretation of the intended logic, possibly providing marginal improvements in optimization and readability.
