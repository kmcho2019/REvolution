Refactor the design by implementing the outputs inside a combinational always block using blocking assignments. This approach explicitly groups all output assignments in one place, which can enhance readability and maintenance when scaling up or modifying logic. While functionally equivalent to assign statements for combinational logic, this style provides a more familiar template for designers transitioning from procedural logic or preparing for potential future expansions involving more complex logic. Outputs remain wires connected via reg signals driven inside the always block.
