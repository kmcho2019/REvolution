- In TopModule, instantiate the parameterized module twice (once for AND, once for OR) and the dedicated XOR module, maintaining clean separation.
- Use explicit intermediate wires inside modules to achieve balanced gate trees and minimal logic depth (two levels).
- This design keeps modularity, synthesis friendliness, balanced timing, and clear separation while allowing synthesis tools to optimize further.
- It avoids the complexity of generate-if for selecting gate types inside the module by having a clean parameter for gate type with simple conditional instantiation.
