Fuse the modular clarity and synthesis efficiency of parameterized gate modules using balanced two-level trees with the succinctness and resource sharing advantages of computing all three functions (AND, OR, XOR) together in one module. 

This fusion uses a single internal module that simultaneously produces all three outputs by sharing intermediate wires (the level-1 partial results) to reduce redundant logic and switching power. The top-level TopModule then directly instantiates this multi-function module, cleanly separating concerns and improving maintainability.

This approach leverages:
- Balanced 2-input trees for clarity and minimal delay,
- Shared intermediate signals for area and power savings,
- Parameterized design principles for clarity and extensibility,
- Efficient reduction operators for synthesis friendliness.

Overall, it offers improved area and power due to logic sharing, maintains excellent performance due to balanced structure, and keeps clean code organization through clear modularization.
