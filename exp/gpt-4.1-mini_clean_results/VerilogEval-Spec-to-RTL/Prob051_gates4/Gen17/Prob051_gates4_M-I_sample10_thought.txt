To optimize for power and area while maintaining performance, I will simplify the design by removing the parameterized module abstraction and directly instantiate the balanced 2-input gates in TopModule. This avoids the overhead of parameterization and module instantiations. The balanced tree of 2-input gates ensures minimal logic depth, hence optimal delay. Direct instantiation gives synthesis tools more explicit gate-level info and may allow them to map gates more efficiently. The logic remains purely combinational, and the explicit structure helps maintain clarity and ensures the tool can easily optimize. This reduces area and power slightly, as fewer nets and module boundaries are introduced.
