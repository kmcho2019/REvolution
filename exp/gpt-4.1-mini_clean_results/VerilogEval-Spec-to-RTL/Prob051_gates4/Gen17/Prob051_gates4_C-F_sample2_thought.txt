Fuse the comprehensive modularity and flexible parameterization of Example 1 with the optimized clarity and synthesis efficiency of Example 2 by designing a single parameterized module that outputs all three functions (AND, OR, XOR) concurrently using balanced 2-input gate trees for AND and OR, and the built-in reduction operator for XOR. 

This fusion yields:
- One parameterized module computing all outputs together, minimizing redundant partial computations and wiring.
- Balanced 2-level gate trees for AND and OR for minimal delay and clear gate structure.
- Direct XOR reduction operator for minimal logic levels and efficient synthesis.
- Reduced intermediate signals and optimized sharing within the module boundary.
- Simplified TopModule instantiating only one module, enhancing area and power by local sharing and centralized logic.

The design trades off a single, well-structured combinational logic block over three separate modules, improving PPA by consolidating logic, enhancing synthesis friendliness, and preserving clarity and maintainability.
