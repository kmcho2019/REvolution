Fuse the modularity and clarity of Example 2's parameterized balanced two-input gate trees with the efficiency and succinctness of Example 1's use of built-in reduction operators. 

Specifically:

- Create a parameterized FourInputGate module that defaults to efficient built-in reduction operators (`&`, `|`, `^`) for AND, OR, XOR. This ensures minimal logic depth, area, and power, leveraging synthesis tools' optimized reduction gates.

- For pedagogical clarity and potential future extension, internally instantiate a combinational submodule CombinedGates that uses reduction operators to produce all three outputs at once, sharing inputs and avoiding redundant logic. The FourInputGate module will then connect these outputs selectively based on the GATETYPE parameter.

- The TopModule instantiates three FourInputGate instances with different GATETYPE parameters, producing out_and, out_or, and out_xor outputs, respectively.

This fusion benefits from:
- Performance: One-level reduction operators ensure minimal critical path delay.
- Power & Area: Shared input vector feeding all outputs once; no redundant logic or intermediate wires.
- Scalability & Readability: Parameterized, modular design with clear separation of concerns.
- Maintainability: Easy to add more gate types or expand input width later by modifying CombinedGates or FourInputGate.

This design blends the minimal overhead and optimal logic from Example 1's reductions with the reusable, parameterized module structure from Example 2, achieving the best of both worlds.
