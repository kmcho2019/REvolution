The existing design is already optimal in terms of logic depth, area, and power by using Verilog's built-in reduction operators and a modular structure. However, to slightly reduce area and possibly improve synthesis overhead, we can eliminate the CombinedGates submodule and integrate the logic directly into the TopModule. This removes module instantiation overhead, simplifying hierarchy without sacrificing clarity or functionality.

Additionally, using continuous assignments directly inside TopModule avoids extra nets or ports, minimizing netlist complexity and potentially reducing routing overhead. Since the original is purely combinational with minimal logic levels, this structural flattening is the best practical PPA improvement for such a small design.

Thus, the improved design:
- Removes the CombinedGates module.
- Implements reduction operators directly inside TopModule.
- Keeps a clean, fully combinational design.
- Retains readability and synthesis friendliness.

This will marginally optimize area and power by reducing the module hierarchy and potential duplicated interface logic.
