To optimize the design for better power, area, and potentially timing, I will replace explicit gate instantiations with behavioral reduction operators. Reduction operators (&, |, ^) are typically synthesized into minimal, optimized gate structures by synthesis tools, reducing gate count and interconnect complexity. This approach minimizes intermediate wires, thus reducing switching activity and routing area. It also keeps the code concise and readable without sacrificing clarity. Since the original timing metrics are already very good, this change primarily targets area and power improvements while maintaining or slightly improving timing.
