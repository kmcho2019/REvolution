Implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR functions using a hierarchical approach with intermediate two-input gates. First, pairwise combine the inputs with 2-input AND, OR, and XOR gates, then combine the intermediate results with another 2-input gate to produce the final output. This approach uses explicit instantiations of basic gates rather than reduction operators, which clarifies the gate-level structure and can facilitate gate-level optimizations or testing.
