Implement the TopModule without any intermediate wires or additional modules by utilizing Verilog's built-in reduction operators directly on the 4-bit input vector. This approach maximizes simplicity and compactness by relying on native language features that synthesize efficiently to minimal logic. The outputs for AND, OR, and XOR correspond to reduction AND (&), OR (|), and XOR (^) operations on the input vector. This results in clean, succinct, and clear code that is highly maintainable and directly expresses the combinational logic intent.
