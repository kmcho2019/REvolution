Refactor the TopModule by removing explicit gate instantiations and intermediate wires, and instead use continuous assignment statements with built-in Verilog bitwise operators (&, |, ^) directly on the 4-bit input vector. This simplifies the code, reduces lines, and improves readability while maintaining the same balanced logic function, since these operators are defined as multi-input bitwise operations in Verilog.
