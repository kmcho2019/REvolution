To optimize for power and area, remove the intermediate partial wires and directly use the Verilog reduction operators on the full 4-bit input vector. This reduces the gate count and net load by avoiding extra signals and gates, which can reduce switching activity and simplify the logic. Since the original solution is already combinational and minimal in delay, this change will maintain performance while improving power and area. The direct reduction operators (&, |, ^) inherently perform the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR operations concisely and efficiently.
