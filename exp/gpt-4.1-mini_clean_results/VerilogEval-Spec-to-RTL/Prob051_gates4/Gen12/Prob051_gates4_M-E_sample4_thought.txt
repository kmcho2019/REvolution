Instead of using Verilog reduction operators, implement the 4-input logic gates using explicit gate-level primitives and intermediate wires. This approach demonstrates how to manually build multi-input gates by cascading 2-input gates, which can be educational and useful when targeting specific gate structures or when custom gate libraries are involved.

For the 4-input AND and OR gates, cascade three 2-input gates sequentially. For the 4-input XOR gate, cascade three 2-input XOR gates similarly.

This architecture explicitly reveals the gate-level structure, showing the intermediate signals and connections, which is helpful for detailed gate-level analysis or in teaching scenarios.
