Implement the 4-input logic gates using a two-stage hierarchical approach: first compute intermediate signals for pairs of bits, then combine those results to generate the final outputs. This uses explicit wire declarations and gate-level instantiations of AND, OR, and XOR primitives to make the structure clear. This approach shows a different architectural style compared to the built-in reduction operators, potentially aiding synthesis tools in gate-level mapping and giving explicit control over intermediate signals. The design remains purely combinational with no registers.
