Since the original design already uses minimal and highly optimized reduction operators for the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates, further RTL-level PPA improvements are unlikely. However, we can explicitly declare the output ports as wires to clarify intent and slightly improve synthesis tool interpretation. Also, separating input/output declarations line-by-line can enhance code readability and maintainability without sacrificing compactness or performance. As there are no sequential elements or complex logic, the critical path, power, and area are already optimal. Therefore, the improvement focuses on clean coding style to aid downstream tools and engineers.
