Implement a fully combinational TopModule that directly assigns the outputs using Verilog's built-in reduction operators for AND, OR, and XOR. This approach is extremely compact, low-overhead, and synthesis-friendly, enabling optimal mapping to underlying technology without intermediate nets or gate instantiations. By avoiding any explicit gate instantiations or parameterized modules, the design is minimal, making it ideal for simple combinational logic with fixed inputs. This approach leverages the inherent capabilities of Verilog reduction operators to achieve a direct and clean implementation, minimizing both area and power, while ensuring zero logic delay other than intrinsic reductions.
