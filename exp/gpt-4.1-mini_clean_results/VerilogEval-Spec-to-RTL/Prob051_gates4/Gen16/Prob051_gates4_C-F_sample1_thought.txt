Fuse the modular clarity and potential reusability of parameterized gate modules using balanced two-input gate trees (from Example 1) with the synthesis efficiency and minimal wiring of reduction operators (from Example 2) by implementing the TopModule to compute all three outputs in a single unified module using built-in reduction operators directly. This approach eliminates redundant module instantiations and intermediate signals, reducing area and power. Meanwhile, defining the TopModule explicitly with direct continuous assignments of reduction operators preserves clarity, ensures optimal synthesis, and minimizes gate delay. This fusion thus provides an efficient, readable, and easily maintainable design with superior PPA compared to both previous examples.
