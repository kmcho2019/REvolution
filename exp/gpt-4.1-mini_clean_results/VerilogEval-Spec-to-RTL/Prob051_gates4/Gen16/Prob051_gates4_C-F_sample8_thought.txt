Fuse the modular clarity and reusability of the parameterized balanced 2-input gate tree module for AND and OR outputs from Example 1 with the succinctness and synthesis efficiency of the Verilog reduction operator for XOR output from Example 2.

This approach leverages:
- Explicit balanced two-level 2-input AND and OR gate trees implemented as parameterized modules for clear structure and optimal gate depth.
- Direct use of the built-in XOR reduction operator in the top module to minimize code, intermediate signals, and allow synthesis tools to implement the most efficient XOR tree.
- Keeping the XOR separate from the parameterized module avoids extra wiring and overhead, improving area and power efficiency.

This fusion results in a design that is clean, modular, minimal in wiring and gates, and optimized for speed, power, and area.
