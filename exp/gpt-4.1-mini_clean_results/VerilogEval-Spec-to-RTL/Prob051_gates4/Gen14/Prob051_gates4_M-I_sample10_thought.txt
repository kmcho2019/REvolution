To optimize for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will simplify the design by directly using Verilog's built-in reduction operators (&, |, ^) instead of instantiating multiple gate primitives. Reduction operators synthesize to efficient, minimal logic with minimal delay and area, and reduce netlist complexity. This approach reduces wiring overhead and module hierarchy depth, yielding potentially lower power and area without compromising performance. It also improves readability and maintainability by eliminating the intermediate parameterized module and associated wires.
