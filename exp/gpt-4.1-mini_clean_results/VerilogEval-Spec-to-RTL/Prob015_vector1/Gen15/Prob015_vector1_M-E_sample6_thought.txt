Implement the 16-bit input byte-splitter using a structural approach that explicitly wires individual bits to two separate 8-bit output registers, leveraging concatenation and bit-level assignments. The design will be purely combinational but uses an always_comb block with continuous bit assignments instead of direct slicing in assign statements.

This approach provides an alternative style, emphasizing explicit bit extraction and concatenation inside procedural logic rather than continuous assignments, demonstrating a different coding style that may improve readability for designers familiar with always blocks, while maintaining zero logic overhead.

The TopModule contains no hierarchy; it directly extracts bytes into output regs assigned combinationally, thus removing hierarchy and showing an alternate method. This method is equally synthesizable and straightforward.

The output ports are declared as reg since they are driven inside an always_comb block to illustrate the procedural style.
