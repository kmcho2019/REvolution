The original design correctly splits the 16-bit input into two 8-bit outputs but uses redundant 8-bit multiplexers with fixed select inputs and duplicate data inputs. This adds unnecessary combinational logic and increases power and area without benefit. 

The improved strategy is to directly assign the output vectors to the appropriate slices of the input vector. This removes redundant logic, minimizes area and power consumption, and maintains the same combinational behavior and timing (0 delay). Direct slicing is more efficient for synthesis tools and results in cleaner code.
