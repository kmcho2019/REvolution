Since the original design is already functionally correct and extremely simple, the best way to improve power and area is to remove the unnecessary Splitter8 submodule, flattening the hierarchy. This will eliminate the overhead of the extra module instantiation and allow the synthesis tool to optimize the simple bit slicing directly. The outputs can be assigned in TopModule with simple continuous assignments, reducing wire and gate overhead. This change retains a purely combinational design with zero timing penalty and minimal power and area usage.
