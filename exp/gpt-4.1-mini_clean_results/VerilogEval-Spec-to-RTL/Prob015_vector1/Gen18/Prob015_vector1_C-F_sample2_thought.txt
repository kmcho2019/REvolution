Combine the clarity and modularity of defining a dedicated Splitter8 module with the minimalism of directly assigning input slices to outputs, but flatten the hierarchy by integrating the splitting logic directly inside TopModule as continuous assignments. This fusion eliminates the submodule instantiation overhead (saving area and power marginally), retains clean, explicit bit slicing, and produces the simplest, most efficient combinational circuit. The design remains highly readable, maintainable, and optimal for PPA, as it leverages direct wire assignments and straightforward port definitions without extra hierarchy.
