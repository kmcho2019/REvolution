Instead of directly slicing the input bits or using a helper module to split the 16-bit input, I will implement a solution using bitwise shifts and masks to extract the high and low bytes. This approach uses simple combinational logic operations (shift right and bitwise AND) to demonstrate an alternative arithmetic/logical method to achieve the same result without slicing.

This method leverages the fact that the upper byte can be obtained by right-shifting the 16-bit input by 8 bits and masking with 0xFF, and the lower byte is obtained by masking the input directly with 0xFF.

The solution maintains a fully combinational logic, is efficient in hardware, and differs architecturally from direct slicing by explicitly applying arithmetic/logical operations to extract the bytes.

TopModule will perform these operations internally without instantiating additional modules, emphasizing simplicity and clarity within a single module.
