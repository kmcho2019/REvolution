A novel approach is to avoid direct slicing by using bitwise operations to extract the high and low bytes from the 16-bit input. This can be done by applying bit masks and shifts. Although less direct than slicing, this method illustrates an alternative way to achieve the same goal, which might be useful in contexts where slicing syntax is limited or to emphasize bit manipulation logic.

Specifically:
- For the low byte, mask the lower 8 bits with 16'h00FF.
- For the high byte, shift the input right by 8 bits and then mask with 8'hFF.

This design is still fully combinational and simple but showcases a different conceptual perspective on splitting bytes, demonstrating how bit-level masking and shifting can replace slicing operations.

The TopModule directly contains this logic without additional module instantiations, making the design flatter and eliminating hierarchy-related overheads.
