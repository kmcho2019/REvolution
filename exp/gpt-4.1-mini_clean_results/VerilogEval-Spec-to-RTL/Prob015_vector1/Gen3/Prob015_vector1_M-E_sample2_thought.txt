To provide a novel architectural idea, instead of directly slicing the input bus, we can implement two parallel 8-bit multiplexers that select between the lower and upper bytes based on a fixed select signal. This approach mimics a demux-based byte splitter and introduces a level of logic, making it conceptually different. The select lines for these multiplexers will be tied to constants: one mux always selects the upper byte, and the other always selects the lower byte. This design is still purely combinational but uses an explicit mux structure rather than direct slicing.
