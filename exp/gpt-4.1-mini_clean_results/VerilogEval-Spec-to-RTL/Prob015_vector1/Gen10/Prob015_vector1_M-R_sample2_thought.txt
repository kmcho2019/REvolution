The original design uses a separate Splitter8 module instantiated inside TopModule, which adds modularity but introduces additional hierarchy. To refactor, I will remove the Splitter8 module and implement the splitting logic directly inside TopModule using continuous assignment statements. This approach reduces the module hierarchy, potentially simplifying synthesis and minimizing area and power slightly, while keeping the same direct slicing approach. The design remains purely combinational and straightforward, emphasizing simplicity and efficiency by assigning outputs directly from input slices in TopModule.
