The existing design is already optimal as it uses direct slicing without any extra logic, yielding minimal delay, power, and area. Modularizing the code adds clarity and reusability without synthesis overhead. Since the PPA metrics are already excellent (zero timing violations, minimal power, and minimal area), further RTL-level optimizations would be marginal or nonexistent. The only potential minor improvement is to inline the assignments in TopModule to slightly simplify the hierarchy, which may help synthesis tools optimize nets better and reduce any trivial overhead from the module instantiation. This trade-off reduces modularity but can marginally improve area and power in some tools without impacting timing.
