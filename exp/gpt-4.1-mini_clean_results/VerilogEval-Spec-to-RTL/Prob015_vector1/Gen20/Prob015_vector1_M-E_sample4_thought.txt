Implement the byte splitting by using two 8-bit wide tri-state buffers controlled by complementary enable signals. The upper byte passes to out_hi when enabled, while out_lo is driven by the lower byte under opposite enable. This architecture emphasizes data gating and isolation, showcasing a different hardware structure from direct slicing. Though not necessarily more efficient, it highlights bus-driving techniques for separating halves of a wider bus combinationally without direct slicing.
