The original design cleanly separates concerns by using a Splitter8 submodule. However, this modular approach adds minimal overhead due to hierarchy and interconnect. To optimize for area and power, the Splitter8 module can be eliminated and its logic moved directly into TopModule using continuous assignments. This reduces hierarchy overhead and can slightly lower area and power by minimizing routing and logic instantiation. Since this is a purely combinational split, the timing cannot be improved further, but flattening hierarchy removes synthesis overhead. The new implementation is equally clear and simple while being optimal for PPA.
