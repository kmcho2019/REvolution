To achieve the best possible area and power for such a simple combinational splitting, flatten the hierarchy by removing the intermediate Splitter8 module and perform direct continuous assignments inside the TopModule. This avoids the minor overhead of module instantiation and improves synthesis results slightly by enabling better optimization. The design remains purely combinational with zero logic delay. This flattened approach reduces area marginally and can simplify power analysis since there is no inter-module interface. No functional changes are made, and readability remains clear.
