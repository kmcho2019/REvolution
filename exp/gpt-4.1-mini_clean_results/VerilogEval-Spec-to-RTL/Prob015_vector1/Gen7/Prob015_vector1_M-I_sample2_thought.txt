To optimize area and power slightly, flatten the hierarchy by removing the Splitter8 module and directly assign the output bytes in TopModule using continuous assignments. This reduces module instantiation overhead, yields simpler netlist with fewer gates (or none beyond wires), and maintains the zero delay and minimal power switching. Performance cannot be improved because the logic is purely wiring. This slight flattening enhances area and power marginally, suiting extreme area- or power-constrained designs.
