The original solution uses two 8-bit 2-to-1 multiplexers with fixed select lines and identical inputs, which is logically redundant. To optimize power, area, and performance, I will remove these unnecessary mux modules and directly assign the 8-bit slices from the input to the outputs. This direct slicing is a combinational wiring operation that synthesis tools will implement with minimal logic and no overhead. This simplification reduces complexity, power consumption, and area without affecting functionality.
