Instead of using direct continuous assignments, this design extracts each bit of the upper and lower bytes individually using a generate-for loop and concatenates them into the outputs. This approach explicitly shows the bit-by-bit assignment in a systematic manner, which can help when wanting to insert additional logic per bit in future modifications. The design remains purely combinational but leverages generate constructs and wire arrays to create a modular and scalable splitting circuit. This method also demonstrates a structural style rather than simple slicing, providing an alternative architectural viewpoint.
