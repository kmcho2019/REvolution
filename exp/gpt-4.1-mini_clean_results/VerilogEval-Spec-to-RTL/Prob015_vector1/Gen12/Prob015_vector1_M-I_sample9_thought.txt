The original design is already clean, modular, and minimal in logic, with direct slicing for the output bytes. However, to optimize power and area slightly, I will remove the separate Splitter8 module and implement the slicing directly inside TopModule. This flattens the hierarchy and eliminates a module boundary, which can help synthesis tools reduce any overhead from module instantiation.

Since the slicing is simple and purely combinational, this change does not impact timing or correctness but can provide a slight improvement in power and area. The design remains clear by meaningful signal names and direct assignments.

Thus, the improved solution is a fully flat TopModule with direct slicing, which is optimal for power, area, and timing.
