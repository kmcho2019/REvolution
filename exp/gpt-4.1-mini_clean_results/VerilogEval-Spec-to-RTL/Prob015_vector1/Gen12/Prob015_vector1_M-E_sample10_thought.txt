Implement the byte splitting functionality by explicitly assigning each bit of the upper and lower byte outputs using a generate-for loop. This bitwise approach demonstrates a different architectural style from direct slicing. It emphasizes fine-grained control over bit assignments and can be easily extended for inputs of arbitrary width or customized bit manipulation in the future. The module remains purely combinational, with no additional logic beyond wiring, and the TopModule simply instantiates this bitwise splitter module.
