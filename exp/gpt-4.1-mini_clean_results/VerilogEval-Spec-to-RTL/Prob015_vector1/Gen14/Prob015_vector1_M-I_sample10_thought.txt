Since the original design is already optimal in functionality and clearly modular, to improve power, performance, and area (PPA) slightly, I will remove the intermediate Splitter8 module and directly implement the bit slicing in TopModule. This flattening of hierarchy reduces potential overhead from module boundaries, helping synthesis tools optimize the logic more aggressively. The direct slice assignment remains the most area- and power-efficient method to split the input bits. This change keeps the code clean and simple while potentially minimizing area and power marginally, as indicated in the original feedback.
