The existing solution is clean, modular, and highly efficient, but the instantiation of a separate Splitter8 module may introduce negligible overhead in area and power if the synthesis tool does not flatten hierarchies. To optimize power and area further, I will remove the Splitter8 module and directly implement the byte slicing within TopModule. This reduces module overhead while preserving the same combinational logic and clarity. The design remains fully combinational, simple, and efficient, with a potentially smaller area and power footprint due to reduced hierarchy.
