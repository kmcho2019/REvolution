Instead of directly slicing the input bus, I will implement a bit-wise shifting and masking approach to extract the high and low bytes. This method highlights the use of arithmetic and bitwise operations for data extraction, offering an alternative to simple slicing.

Specifically, for the upper byte, I will right-shift the 16-bit input by 8 bits and then mask the lower 8 bits. For the lower byte, I will mask the least significant 8 bits of the input directly. This approach emphasizes the arithmetic perspective and can be easily extended or modified for other bit-width manipulations.

The TopModule will perform these operations in combinational logic without any additional hierarchy, demonstrating a minimalist design with arithmetic operators.
