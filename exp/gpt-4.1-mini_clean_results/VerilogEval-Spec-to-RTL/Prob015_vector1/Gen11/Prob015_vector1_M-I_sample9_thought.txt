To optimize PPA while preserving functionality and clarity, remove the parameterized ByteSplitter module since the widths are fixed and no future extensibility is required. This flattens the design hierarchy, enabling synthesis tools to optimize better by directly connecting the output slices from input. Direct slicing in TopModule avoids any slight overhead from parameterization and module instantiation. This reduces tool complexity, potentially lowering power consumption and improving timing slightly due to simpler netlist and fewer buffers. The area should remain minimal as no additional logic is introduced. This approach strikes a balance between zero overhead, simplicity, and maintainability for the fixed-width problem.
