Instead of using a dedicated Splitter8 module or direct slicing, I will implement the splitting functionality using a concatenation and part-select approach within a single always_comb block inside TopModule. This will illustrate an alternative structural style that uses temporary registers and procedural assignments for combinational logic.

The idea is to declare two 8-bit regs for the high and low bytes and assign them inside an always_comb block, with out_hi and out_lo driven by these regs. This demonstrates a different approach using procedural assignments rather than continuous assignments or module instantiations. 

This design maintains combinational behavior, but makes the data flow explicit via procedural blocks, which can be useful for certain synthesis or tooling requirements, or for future extensibility where more logic might be added during splitting.
