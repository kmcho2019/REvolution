Fuse the clarity and modularity of a dedicated ByteSplitter-like module from Example 1 with the minimal overhead of direct slicing seen in Example 2 by defining an internal function inside TopModule for splitting, thereby removing module instantiation overhead while maintaining a clean, reusable splitting logic. This leverages Verilog's functional abstraction to encapsulate the split operation without hierarchy cost, reducing power and area slightly compared to module instantiations.
