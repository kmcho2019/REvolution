The initial design is already optimal in terms of performance, power, and area since it uses direct slicing for combinational logic without unnecessary modules or logic layers. However, the code can be simplified by eliminating the ByteSplitter16 submodule and performing direct slicing inside TopModule itself, reducing hierarchy and slightly improving synthesis optimization opportunities. This inlining removes an extra module instantiation, which can marginally improve power and area while maintaining clarity and directness. The combinational logic remains minimal and direct, preserving zero combinational delay.
