To optimize the design for better power and area, flatten the hierarchy by removing the intermediate ByteSplitter module and directly assign the upper and lower bytes in TopModule. This eliminates the marginal overhead from module instantiation and extra wiring, reducing switching activity and resource use. The logic remains purely combinational with simple bit-slicing assignments, preserving minimal critical path delay and zero timing impact. This direct, flat structure improves PPA by reducing area and power slightly without sacrificing readability or modularity since the logic is trivial.
