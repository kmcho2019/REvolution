Since the original design is already optimal in modularity and clarity but introduces slight overhead by parameterizing and instantiating a separate module, an improvement is to flatten the design by removing the parameterized ByteSplitter and directly assign the output slices in TopModule. This eliminates the instantiation overhead and might reduce power and area due to simpler netlist and fewer hierarchy levels. The direct slicing maintains zero combinational delay and preserves functional correctness, improving PPA slightly especially power and area without sacrificing readability.
