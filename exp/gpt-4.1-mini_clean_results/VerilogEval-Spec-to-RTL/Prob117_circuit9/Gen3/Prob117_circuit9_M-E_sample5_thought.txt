Instead of a direct conditional assignment on each clock, implement a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: HOLD (when a=1) and COUNT (when a=0). In HOLD state, q is fixed at 4. When a transitions to 0, the FSM enters COUNT state, where q increments from 4 up to 6 and then wraps to 0, continuing to count on each rising clock. This separates output assignment and counting logic clearly, and allows explicit state control rather than inline conditional muxing.
