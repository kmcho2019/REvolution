Implement the behavior as a small finite state machine (FSM) with three states representing q values: state_q4, and a 3-bit counter for q when a=0. On each rising clock edge, if a=1, force q=4 (state_q4). When a transitions to 0, q increments modulo 7 starting from 4. We use a register for q and combinational logic to decide next q based on a and current q. This approach encapsulates the logic in a state-like structure without using explicit multiplexers on q_next, and relies on the observation that q is fixed at 4 while a=1 and counts up modulo 7 starting at 4 when a=0.
