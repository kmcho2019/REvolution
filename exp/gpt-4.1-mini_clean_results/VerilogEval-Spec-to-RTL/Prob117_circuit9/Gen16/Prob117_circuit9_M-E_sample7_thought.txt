Implement the state machine using a simple 3-bit counter with special load and hold conditions, without using a case statement.

- When input `a` is high, load q with 4.
- When input `a` is low, increment q modulo 7 (0 to 6 cycling).
- To implement modulo-7 counting easily in 3 bits, count from 0 to 6, and after 6 wrap back to 0.
- When `a` goes low from high, the counter resumes counting up from the last state.
- Use synchronous logic with a single always block, updating q on the rising edge of clk.

This approach avoids explicit case statements and functions, relying on simple arithmetic and conditional load to achieve the desired behavior. It is minimalistic and friendly for synthesis. Also, the modulo-7 counting with reset at 6 to 0 matches the observed sequence after the `a=0` phase starts.

Since the sequence with `a=1` forces q=4 constantly, the counter loads 4 when a=1 and counts up when a=0 from that 4 state forward, matching the waveform.
