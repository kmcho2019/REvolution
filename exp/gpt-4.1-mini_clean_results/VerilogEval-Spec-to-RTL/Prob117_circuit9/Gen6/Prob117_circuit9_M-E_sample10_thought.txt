Implement the sequential logic with a state machine approach that explicitly models the two behaviors as states: when a=1, the output q is fixed at 4; when a=0, q increments modulo 7 from its previous value. 

This can be done by defining two states: HOLD_4 and COUNT. When a=1, state transitions to HOLD_4 and output q=4. When a=0, state transitions to COUNT, and q increments modulo 7. This makes the design conceptually clearer and separates the logic for the two modes.

No asynchronous reset will be used, so q starts as unknown (X) as per original waveform. The counter wraps from 6 back to 0.

This approach differs from the prior design by modeling the behavior as a simple FSM instead of combinational conditions inside the always block, which might improve readability and modularity.
