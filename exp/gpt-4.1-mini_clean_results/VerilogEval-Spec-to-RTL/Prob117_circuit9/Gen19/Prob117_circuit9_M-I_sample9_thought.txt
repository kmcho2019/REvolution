To optimize for PPA, I will simplify the state update logic using arithmetic rather than a case statement. Since the sequence counts from 4 up to 6, then wraps from 0 through 3 and back to 4, it can be implemented by a modulo-7 counter with offset. Specifically, when a=0, increment q modulo 7, and map values 4-6 and 0-3 accordingly, resetting q to 4 when a=1. This reduces the combinational logic complexity and potentially improves timing and area. Power is improved by only updating q when the next state differs from the current state.
