Refactor the design by implementing the next state logic with a continuous assign statement instead of a combinational always block. This separates combinational and sequential logic more clearly: next_state is purely combinational and driven by assign; the state register q is updated synchronously on the rising edge of clk.

By removing the conditional update in the always block (no checking if q differs), the sequential logic is straightforward: q updates to next_state every clock cycle.

This design is functionally equivalent but leverages continuous assignment for combinational logic, improving clarity and potentially synthesis quality.
