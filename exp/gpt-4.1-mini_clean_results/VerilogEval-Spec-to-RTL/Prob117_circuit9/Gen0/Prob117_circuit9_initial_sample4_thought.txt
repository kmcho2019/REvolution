From the waveform, q updates only on the rising edge of clk. When 'a' is 1, q stays fixed at 4 (binary 100). When 'a' is 0, q increments by 1 mod 8 on each rising clk. Initially q is unknown (x), then when 'a' is 1, q is set to 4 and held there. When 'a' changes to 0, q counts up by 1 on each rising edge of clk, wrapping after 7 to 0.
