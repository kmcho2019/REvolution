From the waveform data, the output q is a 3-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk.

- When 'a' is high (1), q remains constant at 4 (3'b100).
- When 'a' is low (0), q increments by 1 on each rising clock edge, wrapping after 6 to 0.

Thus, on each rising clk edge:
- If a == 1, q <= 4
- Else q <= q + 1 mod 7 (0 to 6)

Implement a synchronous always block triggered on the posedge of clk that updates q accordingly.
