The output q is a 3-bit register updated on the rising edge of clk. When input a is high (1), q stays constant at the value 4. When a is low (0), q increments by 1 modulo 7 on each rising clock edge. This matches the waveform where q is 4 while a=1 and counts up from 4 to 6, then wraps to 0, 1, etc. when a=0.
