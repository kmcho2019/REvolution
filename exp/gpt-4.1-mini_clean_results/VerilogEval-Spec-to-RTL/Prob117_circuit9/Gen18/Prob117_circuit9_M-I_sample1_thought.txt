Since states 0-6 form a cycle when a=0, the next state can be implemented as a modulo-7 counter increment, simplifying the combinational logic.

- Represent the state q as a modulo-7 counter when a=0.
- When a=1, q is forced to 4.
- Use simple arithmetic (addition and comparison) instead of case statements.
- This reduces logic complexity (no case or function), potentially improving area and power.
- Maintain clean separation of combinational and sequential logic.
- Initialize q to 4 on reset (if needed) or let it start at unknown as original waveform.
