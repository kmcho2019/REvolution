Since when a=1, q is always 4 regardless of previous state, and when a=0 q cycles through values 4,5,6,0,1,2,3 in sequence, we can simplify by treating q as a 3-bit counter with a fixed reset to 4 when a=1. For the count sequence, we can use modular arithmetic with a small conditional: when q=3, next is 4; else increment q modulo 7 with offset from 4. This avoids the explicit case statement, reduces combinational logic, and maintains correct state transitions.
