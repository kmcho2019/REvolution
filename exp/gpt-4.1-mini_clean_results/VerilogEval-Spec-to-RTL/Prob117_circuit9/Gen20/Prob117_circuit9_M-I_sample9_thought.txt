- Optimize the modulo 7 counter by replacing the compare-to-6 logic with a more compact ternary operator, minimizing combinational logic depth and area.
- Avoid re-assigning q to the same value repeatedly when a=1 by gating the assignment; assign q=4 only if q!=4. This reduces toggling and power consumption.
- Use non-blocking assignments and keep synchronous logic only on posedge clk, maximizing timing predictability.
- Keep localparams for readability and easy modifications.
