Implement the sequential circuit as a simple synchronous counter with conditional reset and load functionality, eliminating the explicit case statement. On each rising edge of clk:
- If input `a` is high, the state `q` is synchronously loaded with the constant 4 (3'd4).
- If `a` is low, `q` increments by 1 modulo 7, cycling through values 4→5→6→0→1→2→3→4...
We utilize an arithmetic modulo-7 increment operation combined with a multiplexed input to the register, simplifying the combinational logic and potentially improving synthesis results in terms of area and timing by leveraging adder-based state updating instead of case-based mapping.
