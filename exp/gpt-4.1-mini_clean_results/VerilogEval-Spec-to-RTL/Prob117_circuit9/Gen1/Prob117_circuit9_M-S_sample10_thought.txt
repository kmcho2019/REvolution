Since q is always set to 4 when a=1 at the rising clock edge, and increments modulo 7 starting from 4 when a=0, we can simplify the logic by directly assigning 4 when a=1; else increment q wrapping around at 7 (0 after 6). Using modulo 7 arithmetic is straightforward and the comparator for 6 can be simplified with a ternary operator. This minimal logic keeps functionality and reduces gates by removing redundant conditions.
