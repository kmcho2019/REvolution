Instead of directly setting q to 4 when a=1, implement q as a 3-bit up-counter with a synchronous load feature. When a=1, load q with 4 on the rising clock edge; when a=0, increment q modulo 7. This is achieved by generating next_q as either 4 or incremented q, then loading it synchronously into q. This approach uses a mux before the register and separates combinational logic from sequential storage.
