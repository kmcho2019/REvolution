Implement the module as a register-based counter with a multiplexer selecting the initial load or counting behavior based on input `a`. When `a` is high, the register loads the value 4 at the next clock edge and holds it; when `a` is low, the register increments modulo 7 (0 to 6) on each clock cycle, cycling through the sequence 4,5,6,0,1,2,3,4... This captures the waveform pattern: with `a=1`, output stays at 4; with `a=0`, output counts up mod 7 starting from 4. This design avoids explicit state machines, relying on arithmetic and conditional loading to model the sequential behavior.
