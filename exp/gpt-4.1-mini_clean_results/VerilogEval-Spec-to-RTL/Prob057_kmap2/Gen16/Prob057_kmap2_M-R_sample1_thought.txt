I will refactor the design by eliminating the hierarchical ProductTerm module and instead implementing the logic directly inside TopModule using explicit boolean expressions derived from the Karnaugh map.

This approach will:
- Use direct assign statements with combinational logic (AND, OR, NOT) operators.
- Explicitly encode each product term as a boolean expression with minimal gate level operations.
- Combine the product terms using OR.
- This flattening removes the mask/value comparison abstraction, reducing gate overhead and complexity.
- The refactoring keeps the same functional behavior but improves clarity and likely reduces area and power due to fewer intermediate signals and simpler logic.

This direct-style is common in RTL coding, concise, and synthesis-friendly.
