The Karnaugh map is given for inputs a, b, c, d with rows indexed by cd and columns by ab.
The 16 minterms can be directly mapped to the inputs (a,b,c,d).

Map coordinates to input bits:  
Row (cd): 00, 01, 11, 10  
Column (ab): 00, 01, 11, 10

Therefore, the minterm at cd=0 (c=0,d=0) and ab=0 (a=0,b=0) corresponds to inputs a=0, b=0, c=0, d=0.
