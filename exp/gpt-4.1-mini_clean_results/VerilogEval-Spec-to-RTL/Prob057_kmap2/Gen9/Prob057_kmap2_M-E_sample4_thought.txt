This time I will implement the TopModule using a purely combinational, direct logic expression derived from the K-map, but expressed in a structured way by first computing intermediate signals representing groups of minterms, then combining these groups.

Specifically:

- I will interpret the K-map to find the minimized Sum of Products form.
- Then, I will encode these products as intermediate wires named by the significant variables.
- The final output will be the OR of these intermediate product terms.
- This approach is straightforward, clear, and directly maps to the logic expression from the K-map without extra modules.
- It offers low latency and minimal area since it avoids unnecessary indirections.
- Using intermediate named wires improves readability and maintainability.
