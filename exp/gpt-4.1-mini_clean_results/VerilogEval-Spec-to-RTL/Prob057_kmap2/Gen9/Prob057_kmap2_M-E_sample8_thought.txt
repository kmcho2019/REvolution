I will implement the Karnaugh map function by directly using a hierarchical approach with intermediate minterms generated for each 2x2 quadrant of the map, then combining these intermediate results. 

This approach breaks the 4-variable map into four smaller blocks (each corresponding to a fixed c,d combination), computes local OR terms for each block, and then ORs these block outputs.

This modular style creates a clean structural design where each block represents a specific (cd) value and sums the relevant ab terms based on the map, improving clarity and traceability to the Karnaugh map.

The steps are:
1. Compute the outputs for cd=00, cd=01, cd=11, cd=10 separately as 4 wires.
2. Each output is an OR of the corresponding ab terms according to the Karnaugh map.
3. Finally, OR all four outputs to produce 'out'.

This also allows easy debugging and extension if inputs or logic change, and demonstrates a structural decomposition style distinct from a fully minimized expression or direct equation.
