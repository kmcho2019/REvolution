1. Decode inputs a,b,c,d into their binary combination to identify each cell.
2. Use intermediate wires representing each minterm (combination of inputs) that outputs a '1' in the K-map.
3. OR all those minterms to produce the final output.
4. This approach clearly shows how each cell corresponds to a product term.
5. It serves as a reference implementation to verify minimized expressions and can help with debugging or educational purposes.
