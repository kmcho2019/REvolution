To simplify the implementation while maintaining functionality, I will directly encode the minimized sum-of-products expression derived from the Karnaugh map without introducing intermediate signals for inverted inputs or reused terms. This reduces wire declarations and logic overhead. Using direct product terms with basic AND and OR operations simplifies the logic.

From the K-map, the minimized SOP expression can be written as:
out = (~a & ~d) | (a & ~b & c) | (~a & b & ~c) | (b & c & d) | (~b & ~c & ~d)

This expression covers all minterms marked as 1 in the K-map and is minimal in literals.

Implementing this directly will reduce complexity and area while keeping the logic correct.
