The Karnaugh map can be directly translated into a minimized Boolean expression by analyzing the groups of ones. From the K-map, the simplified expression is:

out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & (a | b)) | (~d & ~c & a) | (~d & b & ~a)

However, the last two terms (~d & ~c & a) and (~d & b & ~a) are covered by the bigger groups (~d & ~a) and (~c & ~b), so the essential minimal expression is:

out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & (a | b))

This expression can be implemented directly using bitwise logic gates, resulting in a simpler and more efficient design without extra modules or complicated masking. This approach reduces gates, wires, and logic levels, improving area and power while maintaining the original function.

The implementation will:
- Use direct logical operations for each product term.
- OR the product terms to produce the final output.
