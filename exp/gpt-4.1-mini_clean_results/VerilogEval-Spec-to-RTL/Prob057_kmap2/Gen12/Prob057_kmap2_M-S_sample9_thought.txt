I will simplify the implementation by expressing the logic function directly as a Boolean equation derived from the Karnaugh map, avoiding modular decomposition and equality checks. 

From the K-map, the function can be derived as:
out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & (a | b)) | (d & ~c & ~a & b)

But by carefully examining the map, the minterms can be covered by these simpler groups:

- Group 1: ~d & ~a
- Group 2: ~c & ~b
- Group 3: c & d & (a | b)
- Group 4: d & ~c & ~a & b (covers missing ones not included in above groups)

Combining these terms into a single assign statement implements the function efficiently with minimal logic and no extra modules.
