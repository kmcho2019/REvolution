By analyzing the Karnaugh map, we can identify larger groups of ones to simplify the boolean expression.

Given the K-map:

        ab
   cd  00 01 11 10
00 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
01 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |

We try to find groups:

1) Group of four 1s in column ab=00 (a=0,b=0) covering rows cd=00,01,10 and a 1 at cd=00, ab=01 (m1) can be partially grouped.

2) Group the four 1s at ab=00 and 10 (a=0,b=0 and a=1,b=0) with cd=00 and 10: minterms m0,m2,m8,m9; this group covers c=0 or c=1, d=0, b=0, so simplifies to b' d' (b=0, d=0).

3) Group three 1s in row cd=00 and 01 at ab=00 and 10: m0,m2,m4,m6 with c=0 can be grouped as c' and (a' b' + a b') + d'

4) Another group covers minterms m13,m14,m15 (c=1,d=1 and b=1 or a=1), simplifying to c d (a + b)

5) Additional groups are possible, but main terms found are:

   - b' d' (covers m0,m2,m8,m9)
   - c' a' (covers m0,m4)
   - c d (a + b) (covers m13,m14,m15)
   - b d' c' (covers m9, m1) - overlaps with above but acceptable.

Final simplified expression:

out = b' d' + c' a' + c d (a + b)

This uses fewer logic gates and reduces the number of minterms explicitly implemented.

Implementing these terms yields a smaller, more efficient circuit.
