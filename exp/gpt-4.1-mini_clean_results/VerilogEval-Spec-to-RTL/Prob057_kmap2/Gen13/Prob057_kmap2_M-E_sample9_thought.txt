An alternative approach is to express the Karnaugh map function using exclusive-OR (XOR) operations combined with AND and OR gates, aiming for a different structural decomposition.

From the K-map, consider grouping terms and looking for expressions involving XOR of inputs to capture the bit differences that produce output=1.

Observing the map, a notable pattern is that the output is 1 for several combinations where parity or certain conditions between inputs hold.

One can rewrite the function as:

out = ( (a XOR b) AND (c OR d) ) OR ( (~a AND ~b AND ~c) ) OR ( (~d AND ~a) )

This structure uses an XOR gate and a couple of AND gates, enabling a different logic style compared to pure SOP.

This decomposition potentially reduces the number of product terms but uses XOR logic, which might map well in some technologies or FPGA LUTs.

This is a novel architectural alternative to the original SOP solution and provides a different perspective on the implementation.
