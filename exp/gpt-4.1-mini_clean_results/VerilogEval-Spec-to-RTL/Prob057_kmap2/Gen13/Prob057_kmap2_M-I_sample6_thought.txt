After close inspection, minterms with output 1 correspond to the union of:

1. (~c & ~d) & (~a | ~b) - matches 4 minterms
2. (~c & d) & ~b        - matches 2 minterms
3. (c & ~d) & ~a        - matches 2 minterms
4. (c & d)  & (a | b)   - matches 2 minterms

This matches the original solution's four terms.

To optimize, note that the shared complements are already used efficiently. Trying to reduce number of literals further seems challenging.

Alternate factorization: Group terms differently.

Notice that term2 and term3 both have ~a or ~b and involve c or d complements.

Try factoring terms:

(out) = (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b))  +  (~c & d & ~b)  +  (c & ~d & ~a)  +  (c & d & (a | b))

= (~c & ~d & na_or_nb) + (~c & d & nb) + (c & ~d & na) + (c & d & a_or_b)

No obvious simplification in sum-of-products form.

Alternatively, express the function as:

out = (~c & (~d & na_or_nb | d & nb)) | (c & (~d & na | d & a_or_b))

The inner expressions:

(~d & na_or_nb) | (d & nb) = ?

na_or_nb = ~a | ~b

Check if:

(~d & (~a | ~b)) | (d & ~b) = ?

= (~d & ~a) | (~d & ~b) | (d & ~b) = (~d & ~a) | (~b & (~d | d)) = (~d & ~a) | ~b

Similarly:

(~d & na) | (d & a_or_b) = (~d & ~a) | (d & (a|b)) 

No further factoring reduces literals.

Hence, implementing two 2-to-1 multiplexers controlled by c and d could help.

But that adds multiplexers and no clear logic reduction.

Conclusion: The original solution is already minimal and well-factored.

For power, we can reduce transitions by merging the two separate OR terms na_or_nb and a_or_b into single LUTs or use De Morgan's laws carefully.

However, that won't impact gate count here.

For area, we can share complemented inputs as done.

Hence, I will keep the same logic but reorder expressions for possible better optimization by synthesis tools:

- Group the terms in a hierarchical way:

out = (~c & (~d & (~a | ~b) | d & ~b)) | (c & (~d & ~a | d & (a | b)))

This could reduce the total gate inputs since some ORs are shared inside parentheses.

This re-structure helps logic synthesis tools to create smaller logic.

Therefore, I will implement this hierarchical expression.
