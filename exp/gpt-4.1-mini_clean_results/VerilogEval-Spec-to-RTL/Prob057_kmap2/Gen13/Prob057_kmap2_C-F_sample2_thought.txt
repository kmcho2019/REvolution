This expression combines the modularity of identifying groups and the compact, flattened combinational logic style, avoiding extra module overhead, nested ternary operators, or equality checks. The explicit use of complemented inputs and OR/AND operators preserves readability and synthesis friendliness. This approach balances performance (low logic depth), power (minimized switching via common terms), and area (fewer gates and no module overhead).
