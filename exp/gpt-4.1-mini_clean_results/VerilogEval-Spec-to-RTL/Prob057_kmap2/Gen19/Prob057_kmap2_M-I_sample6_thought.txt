The original modular design with the ProductTerm module is clear but adds overhead from conditional equality checks, which may increase area and power slightly. To optimize for better PPA:

- Flatten the logic by deriving a minimal sum-of-products or using the K-map simplification directly.
- Avoid the general-purpose ProductTerm module and write explicit boolean expressions using simple AND/OR/NOT operators.
- Combine product terms where possible to reduce the number of gates and logic depth.
- Implement the minimal expression directly in the TopModule for simpler, lower-area and lower-power hardware.
