I will implement the TopModule by directly encoding the Karnaugh map using a 16-entry truth table lookup inside a 4-bit input vector formed by concatenating inputs d, c, b, and a (in that order). This approach uses a simple combinational ROM (case statement) with the input vector as the address to produce the output. This method is straightforward, highly readable, and has a fixed logic depth, which can be beneficial for synthesis and timing.

Advantages:
- Minimal logic levels since it is a flat combinational lookup.
- Clear and direct mapping of inputs to outputs, easy to verify.
- No additional submodules or complex partial-matching logic.
- Simple to modify or extend for different maps or more inputs.

This approach is different architecturally from product term decomposition or mask/value matching by treating the function as a combinational lookup table.
