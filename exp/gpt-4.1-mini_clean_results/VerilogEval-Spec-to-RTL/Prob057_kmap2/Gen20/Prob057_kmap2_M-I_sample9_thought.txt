The original solution uses a modular ProductTerm block for the complex product terms involving (c & d & (a | b)). This adds unnecessary logic and depth since those two product terms can be combined into a single, simpler product term: c & d & (a | b).

To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, and area), I will:
- Remove the ProductTerm module and implement all product terms with direct AND/OR/NOT logic.
- Combine the two product terms p4a and p4b into a single product term p4 = c & d & (a | b).
- Continue sharing inverted inputs and common signals like or_ab and or_nab.
- Keep the logic flat with minimal gates and minimized combinational depth to reduce timing delay and power.

This approach reduces gate count and switching activity, simplifying the logic without losing clarity or correctness, thereby improving power and area while preserving or improving performance.
