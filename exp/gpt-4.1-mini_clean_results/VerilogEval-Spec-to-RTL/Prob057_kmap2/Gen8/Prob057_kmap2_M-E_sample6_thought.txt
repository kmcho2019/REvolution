To offer a fresh approach, I will implement the K-map function using a hierarchical module structure rather than a flat one. 

The new design will:
- Decompose the function into four smaller product terms corresponding directly to each '1' group in the K-map.
- Each product term will be implemented in a separate submodule named ProductTerm.
- These submodules will each take inputs a,b,c,d and a 4-bit mask representing the minterm to match.
- The ProductTerm outputs 1 if the inputs match the minterm mask with don't-care bits, else 0.
- The TopModule instantiates four ProductTerm modules, each implementing one of the identified product terms from the K-map groups.
- The outputs of the ProductTerm instances are ORed together to produce the final output.
- This approach exemplifies modular design, making the logic reusable and testable at smaller granularity.
- It also improves clarity by explicitly reflecting the K-map groupings as independent product terms.

This architectural pattern, while a bit heavier on gates, demonstrates an alternative structural style emphasizing modularity and explicit minterm checking, which could be useful in larger or parameterized designs.
