Reducing the number of intermediate inverted signals and assigning them inline can slightly reduce resource usage and switching activity. Also, since (a|b) is used only once, it can be combined inline to remove one wire.

Another slight improvement is to reorder terms for potential better optimization by synthesis tools.

Overall, the change is subtle but aims to reduce the total number of wires and gates instantiated, thus saving area and power while maintaining zero logic depth beyond minimal and preserving timing.
