I will refactor the design by replacing the ProductTerm module with direct combinational logic expressions derived from the Karnaugh map groups. Instead of using a mask/value matching module, I will manually encode the simplified Boolean expressions for each group as direct assign statements. This approach eliminates the overhead of the ProductTerm module, reduces wire count, and potentially improves PPA by flattening logic.

Specifically:
- Derive minimal Boolean expressions for each group from the K-map.
- Implement these product terms directly using assign statements with bitwise AND, OR, and NOT.
- Combine the product terms with OR to produce the output.
- The TopModule is thus simpler, purely combinational, and structurally flat.

This preserves the original logic and functionality but achieves better readability and likely improved PPA by avoiding extra modules and conditional checks.
