Implement the logic by explicitly decoding each minterm corresponding to the '1' cells in the Karnaugh map and then OR them together. This approach enumerates all minterms (combinations of inputs that produce output 1) as individual product terms and sums them to produce the final output. Using a generate-for loop is not ideal here since inputs are scalar, so manually write out each minterm. This direct minterm enumeration is straightforward, clear, and distinct from previous factoring approaches.
