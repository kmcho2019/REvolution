The existing solution is already quite optimized in terms of logic minimization and sharing common sub-expressions. To further optimize for PPA (power, performance, area), I will:

1. Re-express the logic to share more terms and reduce overall gate count and depth.
2. Use the Boolean consensus theorem and factor common terms to reduce the number of AND and OR operations.
3. Combine terms with similar factors to reduce the number of logic gates.

Starting from the original expression:

out = 
    (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b)) |
    (~c &  d & ~b)        |
    ( c & ~d & ~a)        |
    ( c &  d & (a | b))

I notice that:

- (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b)) and (~c & d & ~b) both share ~c.
- (c & ~d & ~a) and (c & d & (a | b)) both share c.

Try to factor these:

out = (~c & ((~d & (~a | ~b)) | (d & ~b))) | (c & ((~d & ~a) | (d & (a | b))))

Focus on the sub-expressions inside the parentheses:

- For (~d & (~a | ~b)) | (d & ~b):

  This can be rewritten as (~d & ~a) | (~d & ~b) | (d & ~b)

  Note that (~d & ~b) | (d & ~b) = ~b

  So the expression becomes (~d & ~a) | ~b

- For (~d & ~a) | (d & (a | b)):

  This can be rewritten as (~d & ~a) | (d & a) | (d & b)

There might be no simpler factorization in the second half but in the first half it reduces nicely.

Thus, out = (~c & ((~d & ~a) | ~b)) | (c & ((~d & ~a) | (d & a) | (d & b)))

Now note that (~d & ~a) appears in both sides:

out = (~c & ((~d & ~a) | ~b)) | (c & ((~d & ~a) | (d & a) | (d & b)))

= ((~d & ~a) & (~c | c)) | (~c & ~b) | (c & (d & a)) | (c & (d & b))

Since (~c | c) = 1, the first big term becomes (~d & ~a)

So the expression reduces to:

out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & a) | (c & d & b)

We can also write (c & d & a) | (c & d & b) = c & d & (a | b)

So the final minimized expression is:

out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & (a | b))

This is a simpler expression with only three main product terms.

Implementing this will reduce gate count and logic depth, thus improving PPA.
