To further optimize the existing design for better PPA, we can:

1. Reduce the number of explicit inverted signals by inlining the necessary negations where used to reduce gate count and capacitance, potentially lowering power and area.
2. Use shared intermediate signals effectively to avoid recomputing the same expressions multiple times.
3. Retain the minimized expression derived previously:
   out = (~d & ~a) | (~c & ~b) | (c & d & (a | b))

Given that only three inversions are actually needed in the logic (~d, ~a, and ~b) and ~c is only used with ~b, which can be combined directly in the expression, we will only invert inputs as needed in a minimal manner.

This avoids creating separate wires for all inverted inputs upfront and only creates inversions when necessary, which can reduce dynamic power and area.

Thus, the expression implementation is:

- ~d & ~a  → invert d and a in-place
- ~c & ~b  → invert c and b in-place
- c & d & (a | b) → no inversion needed here

We will implement it with minimal inversions, directly in the assignment, and use intermediate wires for shared terms (a_or_b) to avoid recomputing them.

This should reduce the gate count for inversions from 4 to 3 and potentially reduce area and power further.
