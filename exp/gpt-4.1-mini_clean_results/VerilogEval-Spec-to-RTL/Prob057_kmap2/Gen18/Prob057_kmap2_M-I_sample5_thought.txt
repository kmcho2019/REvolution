To improve the solution for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will eliminate the ProductTerm module abstraction, which adds unnecessary logic complexity and conditional operators that increase area and power.

Instead, I will:

- Directly implement the Karnaugh map logic using simplified boolean expressions derived from the K-map groups.
- Share inverted inputs to reduce logic duplication.
- Use straightforward AND/OR expressions without extra submodules.
- Combine the complex terms (c & d & (a | b)) directly as (c & d & (a | b)) without breaking them into two ProductTerm instances.
- This results in fewer logic levels, less gating, and simpler wiring, improving performance, power consumption, and area.

The final logic expression is:

out = (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b))    // Group 1
    | (~c & d & ~b)            // Group 2
    | (c & ~d & ~a)            // Group 3
    | (c & d & (a | b));       // Group 4

This directly represents the K-map solution with minimal complexity.
