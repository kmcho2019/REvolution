I will refactor the design by directly implementing the simplified boolean function from the K-map using straightforward combinational logic with assign statements. 

This avoids the ProductTerm module and the mask/value checking, leading to a flat and compact expression. The Karnaugh map groups produce the following simplified sum-of-products:

- Grouping the ones in the K-map, the function simplifies to:

  out = (~d & ~a)            // group of cells with d=0,a=0
       | (~c & ~b)           // group of cells with c=0,b=0
       | (c & d & (a | b))   // group of cells with c=1, d=1, and either a=1 or b=1

This is a minimal and clean expression implemented with basic AND, OR, NOT gates via assign.

This refactoring:
- Removes the submodule and parameterized mask/value complexity.
- Reduces gate count and logic depth.
- Makes the logic immediately clear and synthesizable.
