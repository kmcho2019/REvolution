- Avoids deep wide addition chains,
- Maintains full combinational parallelism for performance,
- Uses clear 2D to 1D indexing and clean structure.

This fusion improves clarity, might reduce critical path by simplifying neighbor sum, and maintains correctness and easy synthesis.
