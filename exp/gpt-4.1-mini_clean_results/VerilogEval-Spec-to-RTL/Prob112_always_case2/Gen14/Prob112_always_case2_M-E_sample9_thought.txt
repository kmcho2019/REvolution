Use a casez statement inside a combinational always block to decode the input vector by matching the pattern where the highest priority bit is '1', treating the 'z' as a wildcard to ignore lower bits. This approach simplifies the priority logic by leveraging the pattern matching capabilities of casez, which is concise and easy to read.
