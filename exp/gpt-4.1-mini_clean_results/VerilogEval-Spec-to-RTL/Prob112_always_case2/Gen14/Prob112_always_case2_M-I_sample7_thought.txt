To optimize the existing 4-bit priority encoder for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will reduce the number of intermediate signals by using a simple priority cascade with conditional assignments inside a single always_comb block. This approach avoids generating multiple one-hot signals and redundant negations, reducing combinational logic depth and switching activity.

By encoding directly from input bits with if-else priority checks (from LSB to MSB), the combinational logic will be more straightforward and balanced, minimizing critical path delay and power consumption. This method also reduces area by eliminating extra intermediate wires.

Thus, the improved design uses a single always block with a clear priority sequence and no extra intermediate signals, providing a minimal and clean implementation with better synthesis friendliness.
