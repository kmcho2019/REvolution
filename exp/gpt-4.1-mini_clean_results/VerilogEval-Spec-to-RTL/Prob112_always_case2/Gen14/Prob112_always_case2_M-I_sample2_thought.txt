To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, and area), remove the intermediate bit_sel signals and instead use a single combinational always block with a casez statement directly on the input. By leveraging Verilog's casez with "don't care" bits, the synthesizer can generate minimal priority encoding logic. This reduces combinational gate count and switching activity by avoiding the explicit AND/NOT logic chains. It also simplifies the design, improving clarity and potentially reducing critical path delays. Additionally, explicitly default to zero if no bits are set to maintain correctness. This approach balances succinctness and synthesis-friendliness, improving overall PPA while maintaining the exact priority encoder functionality.
