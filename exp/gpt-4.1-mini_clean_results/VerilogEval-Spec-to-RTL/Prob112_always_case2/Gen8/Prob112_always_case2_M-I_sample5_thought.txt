To optimize the priority encoder for better power and area without sacrificing clarity or correctness, remove intermediate one-hot selection signals and instead directly encode the output position using a single always_comb block with if-else priority checking. This approach eliminates multiple AND and NOT gate combinations, reducing combinational logic depth and switching activity. The sequential if-else checks implement the priority inherently, leading to simpler synthesized logic. Default output is zero when no bits are set. This simplification can lead to a small reduction in area and power by lowering gate count and switching while keeping the code highly readable and synthesis-friendly.
