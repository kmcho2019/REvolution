Refactor the priority encoder to use continuous assignments instead of an always block. Specifically, compute the output position directly from the priority signals using a simple multiplexer-style conditional expression. This eliminates the always block and casez statement, simplifying the logic and potentially improving synthesis friendliness. The priority signals remain explicitly defined to preserve clarity, but the output is now directly assigned with a nested ternary operator that selects the position based on priority order. This approach maintains the exact functionality and priority order, while making the design more concise and purely combinational with assign statements.
