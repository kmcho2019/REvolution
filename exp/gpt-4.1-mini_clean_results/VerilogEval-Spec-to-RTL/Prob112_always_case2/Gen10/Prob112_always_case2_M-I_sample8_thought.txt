To optimize the original design for better power and area while keeping performance similar, I will replace the explicit combinational logic with a straightforward if-else priority encoding approach. This method uses a single combinational always block and sets the output based on the first set bit detected, which reduces redundant negations and logic replication. This approach minimizes toggling by only updating pos once per input change, reducing switching activity, and typically leads to simpler synthesized logic and less area compared to multiple overlapping AND terms with negations. The always_comb block also improves clarity and maintainability without sacrificing performance.
