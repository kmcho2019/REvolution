Use a combinational if-else ladder directly on the input vector from the most significant bit to the least significant bit to assign the position of the first high bit. This approach inverts the priority by starting from MSB, but to keep the original priority from LSB to MSB, we traverse from bit 0 to bit 3, using if-else cascading. The first condition met sets the output pos accordingly. If no bits are set, output zero. This simple approach requires minimal intermediate signals and is easy for synthesis tools to optimize into efficient priority encoder hardware.
