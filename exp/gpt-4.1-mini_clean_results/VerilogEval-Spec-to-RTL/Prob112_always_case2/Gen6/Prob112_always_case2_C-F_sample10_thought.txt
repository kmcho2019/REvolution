The casez will handle "one-hot priority" by encoding the input with wildcard 'z' in lower priority bits masked out, thereby optimizing the priority encoding logic. This approach explicitly prioritizes bit 0 through bit 3, simplifies debugging, and can synthesize to minimal mux logic similarly to ternary assignments, while allowing for straightforward extensions or modifications.
