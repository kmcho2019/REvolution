To optimize the original priority encoder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will replace the explicit one-hot enable signals and cascading if-else with a compact combinational `casez` statement that directly matches the input pattern with wildcards, reflecting the priority naturally. This reduces the number of gates and inversions, decreases switching activity (power), and improves synthesis efficiency (area and performance). The `casez` with masked patterns elegantly captures priority in fewer logic levels and typically results in simpler mux-based hardware. The output defaults to zero when no input bits are set, as required.
