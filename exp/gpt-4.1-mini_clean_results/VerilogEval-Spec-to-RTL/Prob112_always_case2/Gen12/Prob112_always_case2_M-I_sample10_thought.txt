To improve power and area while maintaining performance, I will replace the explicit AND gates with a compact, case-like priority encoder implemented using a single combinational always block. This approach reduces redundant evaluations and logic gates by directly encoding priority using a priority-style if-else structure. The logic is clearer and more efficient for synthesis to a minimal priority mux. This reduces switching activity as only one condition will be true at a time, lowering dynamic power and gate count, thus improving area and power metrics without impacting timing. Output defaults to zero when no bits are set as required.
