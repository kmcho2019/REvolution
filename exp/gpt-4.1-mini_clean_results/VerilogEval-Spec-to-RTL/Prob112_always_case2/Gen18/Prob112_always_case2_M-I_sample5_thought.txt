To optimize the priority encoder for better PPA, a more compact RTL style using nested ternary operators is used. This eliminates the 'priority case' statement and potentially reduces logic levels and area by synthesizing a simple mux chain. The nested conditional approach explicitly encodes priority from the lowest bit to the highest and defaults to zero when no input bit is set. This can lead to slightly improved timing due to reduced decoding overhead and minimal area usage, which can also slightly reduce power.
