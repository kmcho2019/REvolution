Optimize the priority encoder by using a simple if-else ladder in a combinational always block. This avoids multiple negations and redundant gate usage in the original parallel logic, reducing power and area through simpler and fewer logic gates. The if-else structure naturally implements priority encoding by checking bits from LSB to MSB and outputting the position of the first high bit. If no bits are set, output zero. This approach leads to efficient hardware mapping with minimal logic depth, enhancing overall PPA without sacrificing clarity or correctness.
