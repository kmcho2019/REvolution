To improve PPA, simplify the combinational logic by eliminating the intermediate bit_sel signals and instead encode the priority directly using a casez statement on the input bits. The casez pattern matching inherently implements priority from the highest to lowest pattern listed, enabling a minimal combinational depth and fewer gates. Starting from the most significant bit (bit 3) to the least significant bit (bit 0) ensures the first set bit is detected in order. This removes multiple AND and NOT gates, reducing switching activity and gate count, thus improving power and area without affecting timing. Defaulting to zero when no bits are set is naturally handled in the casez default clause. This approach yields a more compact, efficient, and synthesis-friendly priority encoder.
