Implement the 4-bit priority encoder using a single combinational always block with an if-else ladder. This approach leverages behavioral synthesis tools' ability to optimize priority encoders automatically. The priority is checked from the least significant bit (bit0) to the most significant bit (bit3). If no bits are set, output zero. This structure keeps the code concise, readable, and easily synthesizable, often leading to optimized area, power, and timing compared to explicit gate-level logic.
