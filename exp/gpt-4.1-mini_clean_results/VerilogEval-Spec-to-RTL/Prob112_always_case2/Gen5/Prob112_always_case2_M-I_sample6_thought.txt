To improve the existing 4-bit priority encoder for better PPA, we can reduce the logic depth and switching by using a simple if-else chain inside a combinational always block instead of multiple AND gates with inverted inputs. This approach yields fewer intermediate signals, less combinational logic, and a straightforward priority decision from MSB to LSB or LSB to MSB. Here, since the example counts from LSB(0) up to MSB(3), we will check inputs in increasing bit order and assign pos accordingly. This reduces the number of inversions and AND gates, thus improving power and potentially area slightly. It also can improve timing by using a straight conditional check rather than multiple wire assignments with inverted signals. The output defaults to 0 if no inputs are high.
