Fuse the clarity and explicit priority logic of Example 1 with the succinctness and minimal logic levels of Example 2 by implementing a combinational always block that uses if-else conditions to encode priority from LSB to MSB. This approach maintains explicit priority, is easy to extend, and reduces unnecessary logic compared to masked signals. It also avoids the verbosity of the masked one-hot and the possible glitching from multiple inverted signals. The always block provides a clear and maintainable structure, and the if-else priority checks short-circuit as soon as a set bit is found, improving synthesis efficiency.
