To improve the 4-bit priority encoder, I will simplify the combinational logic by replacing the masked one-hot encoding and case statement with a straightforward if-else ladder using ternary operators. This approach reduces the logic depth and the number of gates, lowering power and area by avoiding multiple AND/inversion chains and minimizing glitches. It is functionally equivalent but results in more compact, efficient logic with a clear priority flow. Also, this method is more concise and synthesizes efficiently in modern tools.
