Implement the 4-bit priority encoder using a 'case' statement inside a combinational always block. This approach scans the input bits starting from the least significant bit, outputting the index of the first high bit encountered. If no bits are set, output zero. Using an always_comb block with a casez statement is intuitive, synthesizes efficiently, and easily extensible. This method avoids multiple AND gates and makes the priority explicit through the case structure, possibly leading to clear and compact hardware.
