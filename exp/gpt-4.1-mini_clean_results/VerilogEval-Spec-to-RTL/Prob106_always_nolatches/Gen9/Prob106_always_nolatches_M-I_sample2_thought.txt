To optimize for better power, area, and slightly improve performance, I will consolidate the output signals into a 4-bit bus internally with a case statement, which encodes which arrow is active, then assign the individual outputs from that encoded signal. This approach can help synthesis tools recognize the mutually exclusive nature of outputs and potentially reduce redundant logic. Also, using a case statement on the full 16-bit scancode (rather than splitting bytes and using nested ifs) may help the synthesizer optimize the decoding logic better. Finally, outputs are driven from combinational logic without registers, and no latches will be inferred.
