To refactor, I will replace the procedural always block with continuous assign statements that directly decode the scancode. Each output will be assigned a combinational expression that checks for the corresponding scancode. This removes the need for reg outputs and an always block, simplifying the design and making it purely combinational with concurrent assignments. This approach enhances clarity by explicitly showing the decoding logic for each output individually, and it reduces the verbosity since no default assignments or nested if-else are needed.
