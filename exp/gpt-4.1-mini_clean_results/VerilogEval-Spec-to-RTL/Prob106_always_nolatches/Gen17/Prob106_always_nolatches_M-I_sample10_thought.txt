To optimize the PPA metrics, I will replace the if-else ladder with a case statement that directly matches the entire 16-bit scancode input. This approach synthesizes into a small, efficient lookup decoder with minimal logic and no priority encoding, improving timing and potentially reducing power by eliminating redundant checks. Also, I will encode the four output signals as a single 4-bit one-hot vector internally to reduce toggling, then assign them to outputs. This reduces the number of separate reg variables and logic gates. Since the inputs are combinational, the design remains purely combinational and simple. This should yield better area and power without sacrificing clarity or functionality.
