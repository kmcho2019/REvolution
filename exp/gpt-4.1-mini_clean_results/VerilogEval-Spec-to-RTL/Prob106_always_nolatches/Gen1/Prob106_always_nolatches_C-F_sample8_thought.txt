Combine the clarity of a simple case statement with the efficiency of direct continuous assignments to outputs.  
Use continuous assignment statements that compare the 16-bit scancode input to each known value. This removes the always block and intermediate registers, reducing logic levels and minimizing toggling.  
Since outputs are mutually exclusive, each output is directly driven by a simple equality comparison, which synthesizes to minimal logic and likely results in better timing and lower power.  
This approach preserves readability, optimizes performance, and keeps area minimal.
