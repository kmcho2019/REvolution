Fuse the best ideas by first decoding the upper byte of the scancode to identify if it matches 0xE0 (the keyboard prefix byte), then decode the lower byte to identify the arrow key. This hierarchical approach reduces the number of full 16-bit equality comparisons to just a few 8-bit comparisons, potentially lowering power and area by gating the lower byte comparisons only when the upper byte matches 0xE0. Outputs are asserted based on this efficient two-step decode, maintaining combinational logic with minimal complexity.
