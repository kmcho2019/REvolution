I will refactor the TopModule by implementing the output signals as continuous assignments using combinational logic with conditional (ternary) operators instead of an always block. This approach is purely combinational and concise, relying on direct comparison of the input scancode to the known values. It eliminates the need for reg outputs and always blocks, improving readability and potentially simplifying synthesis results. Each output signal will be assigned an expression that evaluates to 1 when the scancode matches its respective arrow key code, and 0 otherwise.
