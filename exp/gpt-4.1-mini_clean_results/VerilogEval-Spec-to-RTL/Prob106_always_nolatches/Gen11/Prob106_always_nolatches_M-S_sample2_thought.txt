To simplify the solution, I will use a continuous assignment with a case statement on the scancode input, directly driving the outputs without the need for reg types or always blocks. This approach eliminates the always block and reg declarations, making the code more concise and purely combinational. Each output will be driven by a simple equality comparison embedded in the case structure. This reduces verbosity while maintaining the same functionality and ensuring all outputs are mutually exclusive and only asserted when the scancode matches.
