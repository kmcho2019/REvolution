Implement the scancode decoding using direct continuous assignments for each output signal by comparing the entire 16-bit input to each known scancode constant. This approach eliminates the need for any internal registers or always blocks, making the logic purely combinational and explicit. Each output is asserted high only when the scancode exactly matches the corresponding arrow key code, resulting in minimal and straightforward logic gates. This also makes the design extremely clear and potentially more optimized for synthesis since the output signals are simple comparators to constants.
