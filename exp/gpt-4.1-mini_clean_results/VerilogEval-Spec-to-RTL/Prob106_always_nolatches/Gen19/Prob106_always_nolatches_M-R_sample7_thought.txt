I will refactor the TopModule to use continuous assign statements with combinational logic expressions instead of an always block. This approach uses simple boolean expressions to compare the scancode input directly to each known arrow key scancode, generating one-hot outputs. It removes the need for reg outputs and procedural blocks, making the module purely combinational with wires as outputs. This style can sometimes improve clarity and synthesis results for simple decoders by enabling direct mapping from inputs to outputs without procedural overhead.
