To simplify the design and reduce gate count and switching activity, I will replace the always block with a purely combinational assignment using continuous assignments. Each output will be directly driven by a simple equality check of the input scancode to the respective arrow key scancode. This removes the intermediate 'direction' variable and avoids unnecessary logic overhead.

This approach:
- Eliminates the reg variables and always block.
- Uses bitwise equality checks on scancode for each output.
- Directly drives outputs with assign statements, ensuring minimal logic and power.
- Keeps the logic straightforward and fully combinational.

Such restructuring makes the design simpler, likely reduces area and power, and is easy to understand and maintain.
