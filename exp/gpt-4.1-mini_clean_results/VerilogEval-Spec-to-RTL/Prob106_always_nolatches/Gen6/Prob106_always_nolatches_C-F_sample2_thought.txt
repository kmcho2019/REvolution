Fuse the clarity and explicitness of the prefix check from Example 1 with the simplicity and directness of the if-else ladder from Example 2, but implement outputs as continuous combinational assignments instead of reg signals inside an always block. This removes intermediate registers and procedural code, reducing area and power by minimizing toggling and logic depth. By extracting the prefix check as a separate wire and decoding the lower byte with combinational logic expressions, the design is easier for synthesis tools to optimize. The outputs are mutually exclusive and one-hot, so directly encoding each output as a boolean expression from prefix and keycode is concise, readable, and minimal. This fusion gives a minimal, clean, efficient, and synthesizable RTL module.
