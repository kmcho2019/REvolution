To optimize for power and area while maintaining the same functionality, I will use a case statement on the entire 16-bit scancode, removing the nested if and redundant comparisons. This can lead to slightly simpler logic since synthesis tools often generate efficient decoding from case statements. Also, since only one output can be asserted at a time, the outputs will be assigned via a single vector that encodes which direction is active, reducing logic duplication. This approach improves readability and may help synthesis reduce gate count and switching activity by having a single source of combinational logic driving mutually exclusive outputs.
