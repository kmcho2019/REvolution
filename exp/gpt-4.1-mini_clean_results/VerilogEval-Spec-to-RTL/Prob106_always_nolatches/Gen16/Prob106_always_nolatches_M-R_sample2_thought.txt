Refactor the design to use continuous assignments instead of an always block and reg outputs. By directly assigning the output signals as combinational expressions based on the scancode input, we can simplify the logic and reduce register usage. Each output is asserted when the high byte equals 8'hE0 and the low byte matches the corresponding scancode for each arrow key. This approach is purely combinational, clean, and slightly reduces logic by avoiding intermediate registers and explicit default assignments. It is easy to maintain and guarantees mutual exclusivity as only one output can be asserted at a time for valid scancodes.
