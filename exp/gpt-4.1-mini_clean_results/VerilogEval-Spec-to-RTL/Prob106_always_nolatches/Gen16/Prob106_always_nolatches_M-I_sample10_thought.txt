The original solution is correct and simple, but it uses four separate reg outputs and an if-else ladder, which may cause unnecessary toggling and marginally larger logic. To optimize power and area, I will encode the outputs as a single 4-bit one-hot vector internally, then assign each bit to an output wire. This reduces internal logic and can help downstream usage. Also, I will replace the if-else ladder with a case statement on the lower byte when the high byte is 0xE0, which synthesizes efficiently as a decoder. Outputs will be combinational wires driven by the one-hot vector, removing the need for reg outputs and a procedural block, reducing area and power further.
