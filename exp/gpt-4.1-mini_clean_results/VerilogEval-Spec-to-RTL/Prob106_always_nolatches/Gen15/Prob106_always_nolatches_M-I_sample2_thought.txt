To optimize for power and area, I will reduce the logic by encoding the four outputs into a 2-bit value internally, which simplifies the decoding logic and ensures only one output is asserted at a time. The case statement will be used for clarity and synthesis friendliness. Also, by checking the whole 16-bit scancode at once in a single case, the synthesis tool can efficiently generate minimal logic. This approach decreases toggling on individual output signals and can reduce power. The outputs are then driven by simple combinational assignments derived from this internal code. This improves readability, potentially reduces area, and lowers power due to fewer output drivers switching.
