I will implement the TopModule using continuous assignment statements and bitwise equality comparisons to generate the output signals directly from the scancode input. This approach avoids any always block and registers, yielding purely combinational logic described in a concise and declarative style. Each output will be a one-bit wire assigned as the result of a boolean expression comparing the 16-bit scancode input to its corresponding arrow key value. This method improves readability, reduces the likelihood of latch inference, and could lead to efficient combinational logic by synthesis tools.
