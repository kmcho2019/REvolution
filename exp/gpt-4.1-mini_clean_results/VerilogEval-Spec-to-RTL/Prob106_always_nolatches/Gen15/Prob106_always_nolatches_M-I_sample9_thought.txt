To optimize for better power and area, I will replace the always block and reg outputs with continuous assignments and wire outputs. This removes unnecessary flip-flops or inferred latches and creates purely combinational, minimal logic. Using a case statement on the 16-bit input is also more compact and synthesis-friendly than nested if-else. Additionally, by encoding the outputs as one-hot bits derived directly from matching the scancode constants, we avoid redundant logic. This reduces switching power and gate count without changing functional behavior.
