To optimize for area and power while keeping functionality and readability, I will remove the intermediate 4-bit register and the always block, replacing the logic with direct continuous assignments for each output. This eliminates unnecessary registers and the case statement, allowing synthesis tools to produce minimal combinational logic with simple boolean expressions. Using blocking assignment in always blocks is good style, but completely removing the always block for pure combinational logic driven by simple comparisons will further reduce logic and switching activity. The direct comparisons also minimize area and power by avoiding decoding and multiplexing logic inherent in the case statement approach.

This approach maintains correctness, is fully combinational, and yields the smallest area and power footprint for this simple decoding task.
