To refactor the design, I will replace the procedural always block and reg outputs with purely combinational continuous assignments and wire outputs. Each output will be asserted by directly comparing the 16-bit scancode input to the full 16-bit scancode constants for the arrow keys. This removes the need to split the byte comparisons and makes the design concise and straightforward with parallel logic. It is functionally equivalent but uses a different Verilog style (dataflow modeling) favored for simple combinational decoders.
