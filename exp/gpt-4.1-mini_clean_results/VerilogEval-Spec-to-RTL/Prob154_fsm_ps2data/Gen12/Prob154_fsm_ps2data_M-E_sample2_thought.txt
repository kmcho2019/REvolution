Implement the FSM with three explicit states: WAIT_FOR_START, BYTE2, BYTE3.  
- In WAIT_FOR_START state, discard all bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is received; capture it as byte 1 of the message.  
- Transition to BYTE2 on next clock cycle, store the incoming byte as byte 2.  
- Then transition to BYTE3, store next byte as byte 3.  
- On the cycle after receiving byte 3, assert done and output the concatenated 3-byte message (byte1:out_bytes[23:16], byte2:out_bytes[15:8], byte3:out_bytes[7:0]).  
- Then return to WAIT_FOR_START to search for the next message.  
- Use three separate 8-bit registers to hold bytes, avoiding shift operations to reduce switching and area.  
- done is asserted for exactly one clock cycle immediately after the third byte arrives, out_bytes valid at the same cycle.  
- Synchronous active-high reset clears all registers and FSM to WAIT_FOR_START state.  
This approach is clear, easy to verify, reduces shifting logic, and aligns with the specified output format.
