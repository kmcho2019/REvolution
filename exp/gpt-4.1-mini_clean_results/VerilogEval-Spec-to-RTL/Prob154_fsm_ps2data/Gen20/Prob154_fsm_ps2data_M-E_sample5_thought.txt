Implement the FSM with four explicit states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. Start in IDLE and discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 arrives, marking it as BYTE1 and storing it in the most significant byte of out_bytes. On subsequent clocks, shift the previously stored bytes left by 8 bits and insert the new byte in the least significant position. After receiving the third byte (BYTE3), assert done for one cycle and transition back to IDLE to search for the next message. This approach naturally builds the 24-bit message by concatenation and cleanly separates FSM states for clarity and easy timing analysis. The reset synchronously clears state, done, and out_bytes. This method uses a 2-bit state register and a simple shift operation to assemble the message.
