To improve timing and power, I'll use one-hot encoding for the FSM states to reduce the decoding complexity and possibly improve the critical path. I'll separate the three bytes into individual 8-bit registers and concatenate them only when assigning out_bytes. This avoids unnecessary partial updates to a single 24-bit register, potentially reducing toggling and power. The done signal will be asserted for exactly one clock cycle after the third byte reception, cleared at reset or next cycle. I will also tightly control done to be assigned only in one state to reduce glitches. This structure tends to improve timing and power without increasing area much.
