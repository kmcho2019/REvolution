To optimize the original solution for better power and area without sacrificing timing or clarity, I will:

- Add clock enables to the byte registers so that they update only when needed, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power.
- Avoid updating the done and out_bytes registers except when done is asserted to reduce switching activity.
- Keep the FSM and state encoding unchanged for minimal logic and good timing.
- Keep synchronous reset as before.
- This selective updating reduces overall register toggling, which should improve power and potentially area due to smaller inferred clock gating or mux logic.
- The combinational next_state logic remains simple and minimal.
- The done signal will be registered and asserted only in the cycle after the third byte.
- out_bytes will be updated only on done cycle, minimizing data path toggling.

Overall, the design remains simple and robust with incremental improvements in power and area by gating register updates.
