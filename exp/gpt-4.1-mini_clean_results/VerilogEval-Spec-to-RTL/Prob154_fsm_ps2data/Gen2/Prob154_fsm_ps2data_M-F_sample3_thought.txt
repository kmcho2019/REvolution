The problem stems from misalignment of bytes in the shift register and complex reconstruction of out_bytes at the done assertion. To fix this, store the three message bytes in three separate 8-bit registers for clarity and proper alignment. 

- When a byte with in[3]=1 is found (start of message), save it as byte1.
- Capture the next byte as byte2.
- Capture the next byte as byte3.
- At the cycle immediately after byte3 is captured, assert done and output the concatenated bytes as out_bytes = {byte1, byte2, byte3}.
- Use a synchronous reset to initialize all registers and FSM state.
- Keep FSM states as IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3 for clarity and easy counting of bytes.
- 'done' is asserted for exactly one clock cycle after receiving the third byte.
- This approach avoids shifting complexity and ensures correct byte ordering.
