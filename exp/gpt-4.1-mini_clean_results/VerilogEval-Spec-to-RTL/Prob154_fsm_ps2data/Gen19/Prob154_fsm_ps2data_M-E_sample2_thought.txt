Implement the FSM using four states: WAIT_START, BYTE1, BYTE2, DONE_SIGNAL. In WAIT_START, keep discarding bytes until one has in[3]=1, marking it as the start byte (stored in the highest byte of out_bytes). Then move to BYTE1 to store the second byte, then BYTE2 to store the third byte. After storing the third byte, transition to DONE_SIGNAL state where 'done' is asserted for exactly one cycle, with out_bytes fully valid. Then return to WAIT_START for next message. Use one-hot encoding for states to simplify output logic and reduce decoding delay. Use clock-enable logic to only latch bytes on relevant states to reduce unnecessary toggling and power. Reset sets FSM to WAIT_START and clears outputs synchronously.
