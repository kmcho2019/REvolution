To improve timing, power, and area while maintaining functionality, use one-hot encoding for the FSM states to simplify combinational decoding logic and improve timing. Add clock enables to the out_bytes byte registers so they only latch new data in the states that require it, reducing unnecessary toggling and saving dynamic power and area. Keep the done signal a pulse only for one cycle after the third byte. Use synchronous reset and all logic triggered on posedge clk. This approach improves timing by reducing logic depth, lowers power by limiting register toggling, and reduces area with simpler state decoding.
