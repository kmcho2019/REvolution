To improve PPA (timing, power, and area) from the existing design, we'll simplify the datapath by using a shift register approach for out_bytes instead of conditionally updating individual byte segments. This will reduce muxing and switching activity inside the register, improving timing and power.

Specifically:

- Implement a 2-bit FSM with states WAIT_SYNC, BYTE2, BYTE3 as before.
- When waiting for sync, discard bytes until in[3]=1. When detected, load the first byte as the MSB of a 24-bit shift register.
- For the next two cycles (BYTE2 and BYTE3), shift in the new byte into out_bytes by shifting left 8 bits and ORing the new byte at the LSB.
- Assert done high for exactly one cycle immediately after the third byte is received.
- Use synchronous reset.
- This shift register method simplifies datapath logic, reduces conditional load signals, and minimizes glitching.
- done is gated low each cycle except when the third byte is latched.
- The entire out_bytes register is loaded once per cycle, avoiding multiple partial loads and reducing switching.
- This should improve timing slack and reduce power by minimizing toggling and mux complexity.
- FSM remains straightforward for area efficiency.
