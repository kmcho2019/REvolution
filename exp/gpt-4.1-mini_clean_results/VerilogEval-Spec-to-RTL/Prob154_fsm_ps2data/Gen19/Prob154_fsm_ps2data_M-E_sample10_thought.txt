Implement the FSM with explicit states IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. When reset is asserted, return to IDLE and clear outputs. In IDLE state, continuously check if the incoming byte has in[3] set, ignoring others. Upon detecting a start byte (with in[3]=1), store it as the highest byte and proceed to BYTE2. In BYTE2, capture the second byte into the middle byte register and move to BYTE3. In BYTE3, capture the last byte, signal done for exactly one cycle, then return to IDLE. The done signal is driven combinationally as a registered pulse immediately after the third byte is latched. Maintain separate 8-bit registers for each byte, concatenated to form out_bytes, minimizing glitches and making the design clear and modular.
