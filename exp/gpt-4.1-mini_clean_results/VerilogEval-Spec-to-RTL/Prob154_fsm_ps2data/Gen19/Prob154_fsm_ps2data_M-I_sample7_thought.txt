To improve PPA over the initial one-hot FSM design, I will:

- Use a 2-bit binary encoded FSM instead of one-hot to reduce area.
- Implement a simple shift register for the 3-byte message to reduce complex selective assignments and routing in out_bytes.
- Generate done as a one-cycle pulse right after the third byte is received.
- Use synchronous reset as specified.
- Ensure out_bytes is updated only when bytes are valid, minimizing toggling.
- Keep the FSM and datapath logic simple and sequential, improving timing.
- This reduces flip-flop count, mux complexity, and switching activity while maintaining correctness.
