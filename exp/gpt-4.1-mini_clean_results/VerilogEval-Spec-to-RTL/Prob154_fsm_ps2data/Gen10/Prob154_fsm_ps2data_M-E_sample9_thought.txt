I will implement the TopModule using a shift-register approach combined with a simple two-state FSM. The FSM has two states: SEARCH (looking for the start byte where in[3]=1) and CAPTURE (capturing the next two bytes). Instead of individual byte registers, I will use a 24-bit shift register to serially shift in each byte when valid. 

In SEARCH state, when a byte with in[3]=1 is found, I load it into the most significant byte of the shift register and move to CAPTURE. In CAPTURE, I shift in the next two bytes on each clock cycle. After receiving the third byte, I assert done for one cycle and output out_bytes.

This architecture simplifies the datapath and control, reduces register count, and allows straightforward byte shifting. The done signal is generated by counting the number of bytes captured after synchronization. The synchronous reset clears all registers and returns to SEARCH state.

This approach leads to a compact, easy-to-extend FSM and shift register that efficiently constructs the 3-byte message aligned as specified.
