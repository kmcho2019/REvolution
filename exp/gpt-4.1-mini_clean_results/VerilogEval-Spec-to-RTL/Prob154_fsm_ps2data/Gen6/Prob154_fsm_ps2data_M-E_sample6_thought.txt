Implement a Mealy-style FSM with four states: WAIT_FOR_START, READ_BYTE2, READ_BYTE3, DONE_SIGNAL.
- WAIT_FOR_START discards bytes until finding a byte with in[3]=1 (start byte), capturing it as byte1.
- Then transitions to READ_BYTE2, capturing byte2.
- Then to READ_BYTE3, capturing byte3.
- DONE_SIGNAL asserts done and outputs the assembled 3-byte message on out_bytes.
- In DONE_SIGNAL, done is asserted for one cycle, then FSM returns to WAIT_FOR_START.
- Use a separate registered "done" output asserted exactly one cycle in DONE_SIGNAL state.
- Use a single 24-bit shift register to collect bytes by shifting left 8 bits and inserting new byte on the right.
- Synchronous active-high reset clears FSM and output.
- All state and outputs updated on rising clock edge.
