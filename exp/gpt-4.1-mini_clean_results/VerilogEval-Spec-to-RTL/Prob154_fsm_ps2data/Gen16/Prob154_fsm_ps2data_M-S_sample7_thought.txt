Implement a simple 3-state FSM with states WAIT_SYNC, BYTE2, BYTE3. On receiving a byte where in[3]=1 in WAIT_SYNC, store it as the first byte and move to BYTE2. Then capture the next two bytes sequentially. After the third byte, assert done for one cycle and output the concatenated 3-byte message. Use a single 2-bit state register and update the entire out_bytes register each cycle when capturing bytes to simplify logic. Reset synchronously to WAIT_SYNC and clear outputs. This reduces complexity by avoiding partial byte enables and multiple conditional register updates while maintaining functionality.
