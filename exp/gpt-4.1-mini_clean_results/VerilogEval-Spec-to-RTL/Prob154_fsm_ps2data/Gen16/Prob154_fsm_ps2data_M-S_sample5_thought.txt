Use a simple 2-bit FSM with three states to track byte reception, sequentially capturing each byte into out_bytes. On reset, clear state, out_bytes, and done. Wait in the initial state until a byte with in[3]=1 is detected, then collect three bytes in order, asserting done for one cycle immediately after the third byte is captured. Update the entire out_bytes register each cycle for simplicity, keeping the design minimal and functional with synchronous reset and positive-edge clock.
