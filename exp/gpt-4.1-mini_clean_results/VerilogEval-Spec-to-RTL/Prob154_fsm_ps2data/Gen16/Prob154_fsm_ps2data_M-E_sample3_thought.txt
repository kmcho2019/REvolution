Implement the FSM with four explicit states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. In IDLE, continuously scan the input stream until a byte with in[3] = 1 is found, marking the start of a message. Upon this detection, latch the first byte into the highest byte of out_bytes and move to BYTE1. Then capture the next two bytes sequentially in BYTE2 and BYTE3 states. After the third byte is captured, assert done for one cycle while holding the complete 24-bit message valid on out_bytes. Use synchronous reset that clears the FSM and outputs. Outputs are only updated upon byte capture to avoid unnecessary toggling. done is a one-cycle pulse immediately following the reception of the third byte. This clear, stepwise FSM improves clarity, eases debugging, and explicitly sequences message assembly.
