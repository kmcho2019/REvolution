To optimize power and timing further, the improved design will:

- Use one-hot encoding for FSM states to reduce combinational logic delay on next_state decoding.
- Introduce a clock enable signal for the out_bytes register, updating it only when a valid byte is loaded, thereby reducing toggling and power.
- Drive 'done' as a combinational signal derived directly from the state transition condition instead of registering it, avoiding unnecessary flip-flop toggling.
- Store each byte in a dedicated 8-bit register instead of shifting a 24-bit register. Concatenate them at output to reduce wide shift delay and routing complexity.
- Implement synchronous active-high reset as required.
- Keep the overall FSM and datapath simple and clean for better synthesis results.

This approach reduces switching activity, simplifies sequential logic, and improves timing by minimizing wide shift operations and registered outputs that toggle unnecessarily.
