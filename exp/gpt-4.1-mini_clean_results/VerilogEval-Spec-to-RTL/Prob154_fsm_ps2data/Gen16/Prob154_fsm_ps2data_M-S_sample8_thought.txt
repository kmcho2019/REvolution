Use a simple 2-bit counter to track the byte position after detecting the sync byte (in[3]=1). When not synchronized, wait for a byte with in[3]=1. On detecting it, capture it as the first byte and start counting. On subsequent bytes, capture them in sequence until three bytes are stored. Assert done right after the third byte is stored and then reset to waiting state. This eliminates explicit FSM encoding with states, instead relying on a counter and a synchronization flag. The outputs are assigned directly to byte positions, matching the specification. The synchronous reset initializes all registers. This simplification reduces FSM logic while keeping clear data flow and correct done timing.
