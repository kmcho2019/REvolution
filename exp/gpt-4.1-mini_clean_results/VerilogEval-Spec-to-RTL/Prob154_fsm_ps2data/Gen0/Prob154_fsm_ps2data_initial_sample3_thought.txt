Implement an FSM with three states: IDLE (looking for start byte), BYTE1 (collected first byte), BYTE2 (collected second byte). Initially in IDLE, discard bytes until in[3]==1. When found, store byte as first byte and go to BYTE1. Then accept next two bytes, shifting stored bytes accordingly. After receiving third byte, output concatenated message on out_bytes, assert done for one cycle, then return to IDLE to look for next message. done and out_bytes asserted only for one cycle immediately after third byte. Use synchronous reset.
