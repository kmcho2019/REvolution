Implement an FSM with three states: WAIT_FOR_START, BYTE_2, and BYTE_3. 
In WAIT_FOR_START, discard bytes until a byte with bit 3 (in[3]) set to 1 is found; this byte is stored as byte 1.
Then move to BYTE_2 to capture the second byte.
Then move to BYTE_3 to capture the third byte.
After receiving the third byte, output the 24-bit message with the three bytes concatenated in order (byte1 as the most significant byte, byte2 middle, byte3 least significant), assert 'done' for one cycle immediately after the third byte is captured.
Reset synchronously sets the FSM to WAIT_FOR_START and clears outputs.
