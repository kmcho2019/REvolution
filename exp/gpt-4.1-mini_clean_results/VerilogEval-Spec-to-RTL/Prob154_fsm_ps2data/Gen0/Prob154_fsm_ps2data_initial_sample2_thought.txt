We implement a simple FSM with three states: IDLE (waiting for a byte with bit 3 = 1 to start a message), BYTE1 (have received first byte), BYTE2 (have received second byte), and then output done with all 3 bytes captured. We shift in the bytes as they arrive to form out_bytes as {byte1, byte2, byte3} (each 8 bits). The done signal asserts for one clock cycle immediately after receiving the third byte of a message. The reset is synchronous active high and clears all state and outputs. The FSM transitions and registers update on the positive edge of clk.
