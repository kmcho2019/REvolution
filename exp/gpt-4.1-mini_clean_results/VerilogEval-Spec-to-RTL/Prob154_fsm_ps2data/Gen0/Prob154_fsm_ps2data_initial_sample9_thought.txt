Design a FSM with three states: WAIT_FOR_START, BYTE_2, BYTE_3.
- In WAIT_FOR_START, wait for a byte with in[3]=1, then store it as byte 1 and go to BYTE_2.
- In BYTE_2, store the incoming byte as byte 2 and go to BYTE_3.
- In BYTE_3, store the incoming byte as byte 3, assert done, and output the concatenated 3-byte message. Then return to WAIT_FOR_START.
Reset synchronously clears state and outputs.
done is asserted one cycle after the third byte is received, with out_bytes valid that same cycle.
