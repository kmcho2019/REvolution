Implement a synchronous FSM with three states: WAIT_SYNC, BYTE1, BYTE2. Initially, in WAIT_SYNC state, discard bytes until a byte with bit 3 set is received. This byte marks the first byte of the message. Then move to BYTE1 state and store that first byte at out_bytes[23:16]. Next cycle store the next byte at out_bytes[15:8] and move to BYTE2. Next cycle store the next byte at out_bytes[7:0] and assert done for one cycle, then go back to WAIT_SYNC. Reset synchronously sets FSM to WAIT_SYNC and clears outputs.
