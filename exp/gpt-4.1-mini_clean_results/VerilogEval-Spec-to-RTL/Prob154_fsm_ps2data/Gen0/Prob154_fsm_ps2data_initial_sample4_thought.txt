Implement a FSM with three states: IDLE (discard bytes until in[3]=1), BYTE1 (store first byte), BYTE2 (store second byte), BYTE3 (store third byte and assert done). After done, go back to IDLE to wait for next message. Use synchronous reset to initialize state and registers. Store bytes in 24-bit register with first byte in [23:16], second in [15:8], and third in [7:0]. Assert done for one cycle immediately after third byte is stored. out_bytes is valid only when done is asserted.
