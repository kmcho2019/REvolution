Implement an explicit finite state machine with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3.  
- In IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is received, then transition to BYTE1 and store that byte as the first byte.  
- In BYTE1 and BYTE2, store incoming bytes as the second and third bytes respectively.  
- After receiving the third byte (BYTE3), assert done and output the combined 24-bit message in the same cycle.  
- Transition back to IDLE after asserting done to wait for the next message.  
This approach separates control and datapath cleanly with clear states, making the design easy to understand and extend.  
Synchronous reset clears state and outputs.  
The done signal is asserted for one cycle immediately after receiving the third byte, with out_bytes valid concurrently.
