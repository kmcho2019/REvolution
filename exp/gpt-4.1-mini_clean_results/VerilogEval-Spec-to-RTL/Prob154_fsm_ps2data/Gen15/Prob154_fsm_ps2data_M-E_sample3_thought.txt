Implement a three-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, to explicitly represent waiting for the start byte, collecting second byte, and collecting third byte. When in IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 arrives, then store it as the first byte and move to BYTE1. On BYTE1, store the second byte and move to BYTE2. On BYTE2, store the third byte and assert done for one cycle, then return to IDLE. Store the three bytes in three separate 8-bit registers for clarity and reduce shifting; at done, concatenate them into out_bytes. This modular approach eases timing and power optimization by minimizing shifting and simplifying FSM transitions.
