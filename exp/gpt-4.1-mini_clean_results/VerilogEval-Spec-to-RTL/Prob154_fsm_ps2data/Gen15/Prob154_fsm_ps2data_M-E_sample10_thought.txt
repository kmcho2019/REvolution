Use a 2-bit FSM with states IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2. Initially stay in IDLE until a byte with in[3] = 1 is detected, then shift in the three bytes into a 24-bit shift register. Each received byte shifts the register left by 8 bits, inserting the new byte at the least significant position. After the third byte is received (on BYTE2 state), assert done for one cycle and output the full 24-bit message. Use synchronous reset and nonblocking assignments. This approach simplifies the datapath by treating the message as a shift register, avoiding separate byte lanes, and keeps the done signal a one-cycle pulse right after the message completion.
