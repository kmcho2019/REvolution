To improve PPA while maintaining the one-cycle done pulse and valid output timing, I will optimize data storage by using a single 24-bit shift register instead of separate registers for each byte. On detecting the start byte (in[3]==1), shift it in as the most significant byte, then shift in the next two bytes on subsequent cycles. This reduces register usage and logic complexity. Also, I will add a clock enable signal internally to avoid unnecessary toggling of registers when idle, improving power. The FSM state is maintained by a 2-bit counter (0 to 3) indicating how many bytes have been received. Reset and output signals remain synchronous and aligned. This approach should reduce area and toggling, and simplify combinational logic for better timing.
