Use a simple 2-bit FSM with states IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. On reset, go to IDLE. In IDLE, wait for a byte with in[3]=1 to start capturing. Once started, shift bytes into a 24-bit register each clock. After receiving the third byte, output the assembled message and assert done for one cycle, then return to IDLE. Combine sequential logic in one always block to keep it simple and synchronous. No separate combinational next_state logic needed; transition states directly in sequential block.
