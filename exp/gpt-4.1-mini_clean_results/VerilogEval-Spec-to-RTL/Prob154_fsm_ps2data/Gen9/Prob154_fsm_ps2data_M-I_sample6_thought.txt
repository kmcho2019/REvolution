To optimize the existing design for better performance and power, minimize unnecessary updates to the 24-bit shift register by enabling shift only when receiving bytes of a valid message. Also, separate the output register to hold out_bytes only when done is asserted to reduce glitching and reduce toggling. Keep the FSM simple but add an explicit byte counter instead of states to simplify transitions. By registering the input byte and gating the shifts, the critical path can be shortened and switching activity reduced. The done signal is asserted only one cycle after collecting 3 bytes and then reset. This approach reduces dynamic power, simplifies timing, and keeps area similar.
