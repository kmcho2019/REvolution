To optimize the existing design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will apply the following improvements:

- Implement clock gating on the byte registers by using explicit load enable signals to prevent unnecessary toggling, reducing dynamic power.
- Register the done signal separately, updating it only one cycle when the third byte arrives, and hold it low otherwise, minimizing glitch and toggle.
- Keep the state machine binary encoded for minimal flip-flops but separate the outputs done and out_bytes update from state transitions to reduce logic on the critical path.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently and synchronous reset only.
- Simplify and clarify the next-state logic and datapath control signals to aid synthesis tools in optimization.
- Overall, focus on minimizing toggling, separating control signals, and clear FSM structure to enhance timing and power efficiency.

These changes maintain full compliance with the specification and the waveform example while improving PPA.
