Use a simple 2-bit FSM with states IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2 to track byte reception. In IDLE, ignore bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 arrives, then capture it as the first byte and move to BYTE1. In BYTE1 and BYTE2, capture subsequent bytes until the third byte is received. Assert done and output concatenated bytes in the cycle the third byte arrives, then return to IDLE.
