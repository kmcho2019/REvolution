To optimize the design for better power and timing, restrict evaluation of in[3] only when in the IDLE state to reduce unnecessary switching and combinational logic complexity in other states. Also, add enable signals to the byte registers so they update only when needed (on valid byte captures) to reduce toggling power. This prevents continuous loading of the byte registers when not capturing messages. Maintain synchronous reset and correct FSM behavior. The done and out_bytes signals remain asserted for one cycle immediately after the third byte. This approach reduces dynamic power and critical path length with minimal area overhead.
