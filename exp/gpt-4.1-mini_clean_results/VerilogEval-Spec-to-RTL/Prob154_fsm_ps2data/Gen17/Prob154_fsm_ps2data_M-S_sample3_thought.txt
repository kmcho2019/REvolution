Use a simple 2-bit FSM with states IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3 to track byte count after sync detection.

On reset, go to IDLE. When in IDLE, wait for a byte with in[3]=1 to mark first byte; store it in out_bytes[23:16].

Then on subsequent bytes, shift the previous bytes down by 8 bits and store the new byte in out_bytes[23:16].

After receiving the third byte, assert done for one cycle.

This approach uses a shift-register style to store the 3-byte message, simplifying the logic and removing explicit byte lane indexing while preserving functionality and synchronous reset.

Output done is asserted only for one cycle after receiving the third byte.

All registers update on posedge clk with synchronous active-high reset.
