Use a simple binary encoded FSM with 4 states: IDLE (discarding), BYTE1 (found sync byte), BYTE2, BYTE3. 
Implement a small shift register for the three message bytes, shifting in each new byte only when in BYTE1, BYTE2, or BYTE3 states. 
Detect the sync byte (in[3]==1) only in IDLE state to start capturing. 
Assert done signal for one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is captured.
Use synchronous reset to initialize the FSM and output registers.
This approach simplifies the datapath by shifting bytes through a 24-bit register and reduces explicit byte enables.
