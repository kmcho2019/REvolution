To optimize the design for better PPA, reduce unnecessary toggling on the done signal and on out_bytes bytes that are not updated in a given cycle by adding byte load enables and gating done as a single-cycle pulse only when the third byte arrives. Also, keep the FSM simple and binary-encoded for minimal state registers. Use synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments properly. This will reduce dynamic power and possibly improve timing by minimizing switching activity. The done pulse is generated combinationally and then registered, to keep done asserted for only one cycle exactly. Byte loading is gated by enables to avoid unnecessary register toggling.
