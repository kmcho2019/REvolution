Implement a simple 2-bit FSM with three states: WAIT_SYNC, BYTE2, BYTE3. On reset, start in WAIT_SYNC. While in WAIT_SYNC, discard bytes until seeing a byte with in[3] = 1, then save it as the first byte and move to BYTE2. In BYTE2 and BYTE3, store the bytes as second and third bytes respectively. Assert done only in the cycle after the third byte is captured, and present the concatenated 3-byte message on out_bytes. Use a straightforward sequential process with minimal logic to reduce complexity.
