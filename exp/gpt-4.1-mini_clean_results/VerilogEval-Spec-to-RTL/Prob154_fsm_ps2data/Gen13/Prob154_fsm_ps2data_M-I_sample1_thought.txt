To improve PPA, avoid shifting the entire 24-bit register each cycle by storing the bytes in three separate 8-bit registers indexed by a byte counter. This reduces combinational logic on the critical path, improving performance and power by limiting switching activity. The FSM remains simple with a 2-bit byte counter to track message bytes. When the third byte is received, assert done and output the concatenated 24-bit message formed by combining the three registers. This reduces area by eliminating the 24-bit wide shift operation and reduces power by limiting bit toggling to only updated bytes. The done signal remains asserted for one cycle immediately after the third byte reception. The reset is synchronous active high.
