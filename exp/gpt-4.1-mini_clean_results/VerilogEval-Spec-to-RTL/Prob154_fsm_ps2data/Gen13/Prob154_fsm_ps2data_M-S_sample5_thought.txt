Use a simple byte counter from 0 to 2 to track how many message bytes have been received. When the counter is 0, wait for a byte with in[3]=1 to start a new message. On each valid byte, shift the new byte into out_bytes as {previous_bytes, new_byte}. Assert done immediately after the third byte is received (counter reaches 3). This approach removes explicit FSM states and uses a simple counter, making the design more straightforward and compact while preserving functionality.
