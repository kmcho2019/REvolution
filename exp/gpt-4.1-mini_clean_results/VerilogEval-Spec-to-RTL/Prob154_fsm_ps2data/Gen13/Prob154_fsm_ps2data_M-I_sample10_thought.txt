Optimize PPA by storing the three bytes in separate 8-bit registers instead of shifting the entire 24-bit out_bytes register each cycle. Use a simple 2-bit state counter to track the message byte index (0: searching, 1: second byte, 2: third byte). On receiving the first byte with in[3]=1, store it in byte0 and transition to state 1. Then sequentially store subsequent bytes in byte1 and byte2. When the third byte is stored, output the concatenated bytes and assert done for one cycle. This reduces switching activity, shortens the critical path by avoiding 24-bit shifts, and reduces area by using small registers rather than a large shift register.
