Use a simple 2-bit counter to track how many bytes have been collected in the current message. When in idle (count=0), wait for a byte with in[3]=1 to start the message. Once started, shift bytes into a 24-bit shift register by left-shifting the stored bytes by 8 bits and appending the new byte at the least significant byte position. After receiving the third byte (count=3), assert done and output the collected bytes. Then reset count to 0 to wait for the next message. This removes explicit FSM states and separate byte registers, simplifying the design.
