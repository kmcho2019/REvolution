Implement a four-state FSM: WAIT_START, GOT_BYTE1, GOT_BYTE2, DONE_SIGNAL. The FSM waits in WAIT_START until it sees a byte with in[3]=1, then moves to GOT_BYTE1 and stores it. Next two bytes are stored in GOT_BYTE2 and GOT_BYTE3 (implicit) states. After the third byte is stored, the FSM transitions to DONE_SIGNAL state where it asserts done for exactly one cycle and outputs the 3-byte message. On next clock, it returns to WAIT_START, scanning for the next message. The done and out_bytes signals are registered and updated synchronously to ensure done is asserted in the cycle immediately after receiving the third byte. This approach cleanly separates byte reception states and done signaling, avoiding glitches or timing mismatches.
