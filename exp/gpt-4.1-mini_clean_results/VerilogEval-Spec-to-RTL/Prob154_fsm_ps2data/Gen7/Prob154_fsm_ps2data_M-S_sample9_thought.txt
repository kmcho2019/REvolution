Use a simple two-bit FSM with three states: WAIT (searching for start byte), BYTE2, BYTE3. On reset, go to WAIT. While in WAIT, ignore bytes until a byte with in[3]==1 arrives; store it as byte1 and move to BYTE2. Then store the next two bytes in order (byte2, byte3). After receiving the third byte, output the concatenated 24-bit message, assert done for one cycle, and return to WAIT. Use synchronous reset, done and out_bytes valid only on done cycle. Keep logic minimal and clear.
