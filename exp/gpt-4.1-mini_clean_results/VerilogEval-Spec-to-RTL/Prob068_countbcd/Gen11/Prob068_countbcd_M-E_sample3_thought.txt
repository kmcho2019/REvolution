Instead of managing each BCD digit separately with explicit carry logic, I will implement the entire 4-digit BCD counter as a single 16-bit register representing the full BCD number. On each clock, I will increment the counter by 1 using a dedicated combinational BCD incrementer that operates on all 4 digits simultaneously with carry propagation.

This approach encapsulates carry handling into a compact combinational block, reducing nested conditions and function calls, potentially improving timing and readability.

Enable signals (ena) will be generated by detecting when the lower digits roll over from 9 to 0, which can be derived directly from the counter value before increment. This way, the ena signals indicate precisely when each upper digit should increment.

A synchronous reset will clear the entire counter to zero. The counter increments by 1 each clock cycle on positive edge.

This design modularizes the BCD increment logic in a single combinational process and simplifies enable generation, avoiding multiple intermediate registers or deep nesting. It may also minimize switching and improve power efficiency since the increment operation is centralized.
