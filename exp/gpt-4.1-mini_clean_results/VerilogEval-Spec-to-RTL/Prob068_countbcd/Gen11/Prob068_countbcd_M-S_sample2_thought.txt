I will simplify the design by representing the 4-digit BCD counter as four separate 4-bit registers, each corresponding to one digit. This allows a straightforward and clear increment logic: increment the ones digit on every clock; if it rolls over from 9 to 0, enable and increment the tens digit; repeat similarly for hundreds and thousands digits.

The enables (ena) signals are generated combinationally based on the current digit values at the clock edge to indicate when each upper digit should increment (i.e., when the lower digit is 9).

The synchronous reset sets all digits to zero.

This approach avoids nested function calls and complex carry propagation in combinational logic, resulting in simpler and more readable code with minimal logic depth while maintaining the required functionality.
