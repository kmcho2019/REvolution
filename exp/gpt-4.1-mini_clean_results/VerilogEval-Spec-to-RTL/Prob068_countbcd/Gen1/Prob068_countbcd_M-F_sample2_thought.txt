To fix the issue of enable signals and digit increments occurring in the same clock cycle, we must separate the rollover detection and increment enable signals from the actual increments. The enable signals generated by a digit rolling over on the current clock cycle should be used in the *next* clock cycle to increment the higher digit. 

This can be done by:
- Using intermediate registers to hold enable signals that propagate from the ones digit up to thousands digit, but delayed by one clock cycle.
- Increment the ones digit every clock cycle.
- On each clock cycle, increment the tens digit only if the previous cycle's enable for tens is asserted, similarly for hundreds and thousands digits.
- The enable outputs will indicate when each upper digit is incremented (i.e., when the corresponding enable register is asserted).
- This approach ensures sequential logic updates cleanly, with no combinational cycles or race conditions.
- On reset, clear digits and enable signals synchronously.

This design meets the requirement of outputting enable signals for digits 1 to 3 and implements a 4-digit BCD counter correctly.
