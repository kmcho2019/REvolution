The main issue in the original design is that the `ena` signals are used both as output signals and as internal carry flags within the same clocked block, causing incorrect enable propagation due to non-blocking assignment semantics. To fix this, separate internal carry signals should be computed combinationally to control digit increments within the clocked process. Then, after the clock edge, `ena` outputs are updated to reflect which digit increments occurred. Also, instead of a separate combinational block for `q`, assign `q` synchronously inside the clocked block to ensure stable outputs. This approach properly handles carry ripple through the BCD digits and aligns the `ena` outputs with digit increments on that clock cycle.
