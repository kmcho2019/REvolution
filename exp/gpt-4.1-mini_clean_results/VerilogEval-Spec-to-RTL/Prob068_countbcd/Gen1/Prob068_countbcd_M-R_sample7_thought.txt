Refactor the 4-digit BCD counter to separate combinational carry and enable logic from sequential counting logic. Compute carry-out and enable signals combinationally based on the current count values and increment conditions. Use a single always block to update all digits in parallel on the clock edge with non-blocking assignments, avoiding overlapping partial updates. The ena signals are generated combinationally to indicate when the upper digits should increment (when the lower digit wraps from 9 to 0). This approach ensures correct carry propagation and timing of enable signals, solving the issues with registered carry and overlapping assignments.
