Implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a ripple-carry approach with separate enable flags for each digit. The ones digit increments every clock cycle unless reset. Each digit, when reaching 9 and rolling over to 0, generates a carry enable signal for the next higher digit on the *next* clock cycle. This carry enable is registered to ensure stable, one-cycle pulse enables on upper digits. This approach cleanly separates digit increment logic and enable signal generation, using registered carry signals to synchronize increments and the ena output.

The ena output bits correspond directly to these carry enables for tens, hundreds, and thousands digits. On synchronous reset, clear digits and disables. This structure avoids nested if-else and potential glitches in ena signals, producing clean, one-clock-cycle enables for upper digits aligned to their increments.
