To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter, I will use a single 16-bit register representing all four BCD digits together instead of separate digit registers. The counter will increment the entire 16-bit BCD number as a whole, performing BCD correction after adding 1 to the least significant nibble. This approach removes nested if-else blocks for each digit increment and carry propagation by leveraging a simple iterative correction loop inside a combinational block.

Enable signals will be generated combinationally by checking if each lower digit is at 9, indicating a carry to the next digit, similar to the original design. The synchronous reset and positive edge clock behavior remain unchanged.

This approach reduces sequential logic complexity and uses simpler BCD addition logic, making the design more concise and easier to maintain while meeting the interface and functionality requirements.
