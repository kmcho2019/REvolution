I will simplify the 4-digit BCD counter by using separate 4-bit registers for each digit, making the logic more modular and readable. Each digit increments on the clock edge if its enable signal is high. The enable signals are generated by simple combinational comparisons: the ones digit always increments, and higher digits increment only when all lower digits roll over from 9 to 0. This removes nested if-else structures and the use of a function, resulting in clearer, easier-to-maintain code with straightforward carry propagation. The synchronous reset clears all digits. This approach maintains functionality but reduces complexity and improves readability.
