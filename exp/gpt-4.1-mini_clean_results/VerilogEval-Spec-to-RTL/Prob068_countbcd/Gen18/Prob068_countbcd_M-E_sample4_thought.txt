I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a ripple carry approach where each digit is a separate 4-bit register and increments only when the lower digit reaches 9 and resets to 0. To reduce nesting and improve clarity, I will implement individual always blocks for each digit with enable signals driving when each digit should increment. The enables are registered to synchronize them with the clock, avoiding combinational loops or glitches.

The ones digit always increments on every clock cycle unless reset; the tens digit increments only when ones digit rolls over from 9 to 0, etc. The enable signals output will be the synchronized increment enables for hundreds, tens, and thousands digits respectively. This modular approach improves readability and may help timing by breaking combinational carry logic into separate registers.
