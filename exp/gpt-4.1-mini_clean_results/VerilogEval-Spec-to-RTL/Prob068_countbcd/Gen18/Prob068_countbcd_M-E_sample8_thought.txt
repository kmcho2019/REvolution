I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a 16-bit register to hold the entire BCD count, but instead of nested ripple increments, I will use a combinational addition of 1 with decimal correction after addition. On each clock, the register increments by 1. After adding 1 in binary, the result is corrected per digit: if any digit is greater than 9, add 6 to that nibble to adjust to BCD. Enable signals for the upper digits are generated by detecting when their lower digit(s) wrap from 9 to 0 in the previous state. This method uses a carry look-ahead style correction for BCD and avoids nested conditionals, improving timing. The synchronous reset clears the entire count.
