To improve the original design's timing and potentially reduce power consumption, I will restructure the carry logic to flatten the nested if-else chain by precomputing carry signals for each digit increment. This approach reduces the critical path delay by limiting the longest combinational chain in the always block. Additionally, I will register the enable (ena) outputs rather than driving them combinationally, which can reduce switching activity and glitches downstream. The synchronous reset remains unchanged, and positive edge clock triggering is preserved. The functional behavior is identical, but timing and power efficiency should improve due to shorter combinational paths and registered outputs.
