To improve the PPA metrics of the original 4-digit BCD counter implementation, I will optimize timing and power by removing nested sequential carry propagation within the clocked block and instead use a ripple-carry style that directly computes the next values for each digit combinationally before updating registers.

The key changes:

- Compute carry-out and next digit values combinationally for each digit based on the current digits and the increment of the ones digit.
- Use registered carry flags and next digit values so that all digits update in one clock cycle without nested if-else.
- This flattens carry propagation and reduces combinational logic depth, improving timing.
- Use clock enables on upper digit registers, driven by their corresponding enable signals, to avoid unnecessary toggles and save dynamic power.
- The enable outputs remain combinational from digit equals 9 checks.
- Keep synchronous reset and all logic triggered on posedge clk.
- This approach balances minimal area increase due to additional combinational carry wires with improved timing and power benefits.

This method reduces the critical path from sequential nested bcd_increment calls to a combinational carry chain of simple comparisons and sums, minimizing delay and switching activity.
