I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter as four separate 4-bit BCD digit registers, each incrementing conditionally based on enable signals generated in a sequential fashion.

Instead of ripple carry combinational logic in one clock cycle, each digit will only increment on the clock edge if its enable input is high. The lowest digit (ones) increments unconditionally every clock (unless reset).

The enable signals for the upper digits are generated sequentially from the synchronous register outputs: the enable to the tens digit is set when the ones digit rolls over from 9 to 0, the enable to the hundreds digit when tens roll over, and similarly for thousands.

This approach decomposes the problem into four small counters chained by enables in registers, resulting in a sequential carry propagation across clock cycles rather than combinational carry in one clock. This may increase latency by one clock for digit increment propagation but simplifies combinational logic and can reduce critical path delay.

All registers update on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset. The ena outputs correspond directly to the enable signals for digits [3:1].

This approach provides a modular design, easier to pipeline or extend, and may improve timing due to reduced combinational complexity per clock cycle.
