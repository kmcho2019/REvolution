To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter, I will split the 16-bit register into four separate 4-bit BCD digit registers and increment them sequentially using a simple ripple carry approach. On each clock cycle, the ones digit increments; if it rolls over from 9 to 0, it enables incrementing the tens digit, and so on.

This approach keeps each digit register simple with straightforward increment and rollover logic and avoids combinational ripple carry chains inside one large register. The enable signals are directly generated from each digit's rollover condition, making the design clearer and easier to understand.

The synchronous reset clears all digit registers. The clock edge triggers sequential logic, and the ena outputs correspond to the enable signals for incrementing the upper three digits.

Overall, this solution reduces complexity by explicitly managing each digit separately with simple ripple carry increment logic.
