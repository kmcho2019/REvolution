I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a single 16-bit register representing the entire BCD value. Incrementing will be done by first adding 1 to the lower 4 bits (ones digit) and then correcting any digit that exceeds 9 by resetting it to 0 and carrying 1 to the next digit. This approach uses combinational logic to handle carry propagation within the clocked always block, avoiding deeply nested if-else statements.

For the enable signals, I'll generate them synchronously based on whether the lower digits are at 9 before incrementing — these indicate when the next higher digit should be enabled for incrementing.

This design packs the BCD counter into a single register with concise carry correction, improving timing by avoiding nested conditions while meeting the synchronous reset and enable output requirements.
