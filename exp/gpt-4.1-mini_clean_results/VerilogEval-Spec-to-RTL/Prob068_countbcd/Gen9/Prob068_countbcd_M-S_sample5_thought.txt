To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter while maintaining functionality, I will:

- Use four separate 4-bit registers for each BCD digit instead of a single 16-bit register. This simplifies the logic by handling each digit independently.
- Perform digit increments and carry propagation sequentially within a single always block triggered by the clock and reset.
- Generate the enable signals combinationally based on when each lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0 (the carry condition).
- Keep the logic straightforward without nested function calls or extra intermediate signals.
- Use non-blocking assignments for proper synchronous updates.
- This approach reduces combinational complexity and makes the design easy to understand and maintain while meeting all requirements.
