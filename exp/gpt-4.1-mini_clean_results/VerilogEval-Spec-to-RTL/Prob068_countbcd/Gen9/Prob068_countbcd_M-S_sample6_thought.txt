To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter while preserving its functionality, I will use four separate 4-bit registers for each BCD digit instead of a packed 16-bit register. This modular approach avoids nested if-else blocks for carry propagation by handling each digit increment independently with enable signals, making the logic straightforward and easier to follow.

The increment logic:
- Always increment the ones digit.
- Enable the tens digit to increment when ones roll over from 9 to 0.
- Similarly, enable hundreds when tens roll over from 9 to 0, and thousands when hundreds roll over.

The enable signals (ena) will directly reflect the carry-out conditions for the upper three digits.

This design trades off a bit of area for simplicity and improved readability, and potentially reduces critical path complexity by removing deeply nested logic, improving timing and ease of synthesis.
