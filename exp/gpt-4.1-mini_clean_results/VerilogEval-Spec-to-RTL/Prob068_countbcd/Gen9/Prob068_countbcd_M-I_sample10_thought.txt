To improve PPA, especially timing (tns and wns), I will optimize the carry (increment enable) generation by using a binary ripple approach with minimal combinational depth rather than ANDing multiple rollover signals at once. This reduces the AND gate levels from 3 down to a ripple carry style with only one AND gate per stage.

I will also replace the inc_bcd function with explicit combinational logic for incrementing each BCD digit, which can help synthesis tools optimize increment and wrap-around better. This explicit logic uses ternary operators to assign the next digit value with minimal logic.

Additionally, I will register the enable signals ena to reduce glitches and hold stable enables for each clock cycle. This can improve timing and reduce dynamic power due to glitching on the enable signals.

Overall, this reduces combinational path depth for carry generation, simplifies increment logic, and stabilizes enables to optimize power and timing without increasing area noticeably.
