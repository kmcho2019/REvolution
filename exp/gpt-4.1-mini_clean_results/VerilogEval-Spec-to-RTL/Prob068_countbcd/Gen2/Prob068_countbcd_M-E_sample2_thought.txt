Use four separate 4-bit registers for each BCD digit to clearly separate the logic of each digit. On each clock edge, increment the ones digit and propagate carry flags through each upper digit sequentially within the same clock cycle. The enable signals for the upper three digits (ena) indicate when those digits are incremented during this cycle, i.e., when carry from a lower digit causes their increment. This requires explicit carry signals computed from comparisons after incrementing lower digits. Reset synchronously clears all digits and disables enables. Finally, combine the four digit registers into the 16-bit output vector. This modular approach simplifies increment and enable calculation and aligns enables exactly with when the digits increment.
