The previous implementation computed the enable signals based on the current digit values before incrementing, causing a timing mismatch with when the digits actually increment. To fix this, the enable signals should reflect whether each digit increments in the current clock cycle, which occurs exactly when the increment condition is met (e.g., lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0).

A cleaner approach is to first calculate whether each digit will roll over (carry out) during the increment and use these signals to update digits and generate the ena signals simultaneously in the sequential block. By computing carry enables based on current digits and then updating digits and ena outputs synchronously, ena will be correctly aligned with the increments.

This means:

- Determine carry_out signals before update.
- Increment digits based on carry_in (carry from lower digit).
- ena output signals assert when the corresponding digit increments (i.e., when the carry_in to that digit is high).
- Synchronize ena and q updates in the same clock cycle.

This removes any race or timing mismatch and matches the functional intent for ena signals precisely when digits increment.
