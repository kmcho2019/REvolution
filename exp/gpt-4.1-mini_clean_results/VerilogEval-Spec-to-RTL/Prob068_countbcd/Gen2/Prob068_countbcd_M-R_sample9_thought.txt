To fix the enable signal timing issue while keeping the same counting logic, I will make the 'ena' output purely combinational signals derived directly from the carry conditions that occur in the current clock cycle. This means 'ena' will immediately indicate when a digit should increment based on the rollover of the previous digit without registering the enable signals.

The approach:
- Use registers only for the BCD digit values (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands).
- Compute the next digit values and carry conditions combinationally.
- Generate the enable signals combinationally based on these carry signals for tens, hundreds, and thousands digits.
- Update the digit registers synchronously on the clock edge.
- Output 'ena' directly from the combinational carries, avoiding any clock-edge delay.
- Implement synchronous active-high reset clearing all digits.

This preserves synchronous counting but gives 'ena' signals that directly correspond to enables for the current incrementing cycle, aligning with expected simulation results and design intent.
