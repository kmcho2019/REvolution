Implement the 4-digit BCD counter by maintaining each digit as a separate 4-bit register. On each clock, always increment the ones digit. Whenever a digit rolls over from 9 to 0, assert its corresponding enable signal to increment the next digit in the next cycle. To avoid nested logic, use separate carry signals that propagate ripple carries through digits and directly assign ena signals based on these carry events. This way, ena signals indicate precisely which upper digits will be incremented on the current cycle. The synchronous reset clears all digits and disables all enable signals. This modular approach cleanly separates digit increments, carry detection, and enable generation.
