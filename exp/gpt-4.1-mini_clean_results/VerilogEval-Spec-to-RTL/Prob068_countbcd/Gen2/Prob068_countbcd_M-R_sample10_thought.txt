Refactor the design by separating the carry detection logic into combinational logic before updating the digits in a synchronous always block. Compute the carry-out signals for each digit as combinational signals based on the current digit values and the increment enables. Use these carries to generate ena outputs and to enable increment of upper digits on the next clock cycle. This avoids the problem of using carry signals updated by non-blocking assignments in the same clock cycle for subsequent logic. The digits are updated synchronously on the clock edge according to the computed enable signals. This approach ensures correct ripple carry and synchronous ena outputs indicating when each upper digit increments.
