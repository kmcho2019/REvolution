To fix the enable signal timing and avoid mixing combinational and sequential logic for ena, I will refactor the design so that:
- All digits are stored in registers and updated synchronously on the clock.
- The carry and enable signals are computed combinationally based on the current digit values before the clock edge.
- The 3-bit output ena directly reflects the combinational carry signals indicating when the upper three digits should increment, i.e., when the previous digit rolls over.
- No internal register is used for ena signals; instead, ena is a combinational output updated continuously.
- The synchronous reset clears all digits to zero.
This approach ensures ena outputs are stable, valid during the clock cycle, and aligned with the counting logic. It avoids enable registers that cause delays and timing mismatches.

This refactor maintains the core carry-chain BCD counting logic but simplifies the enable signal generation and output timing.
