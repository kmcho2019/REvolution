I will simplify the BCD counter by treating the entire 16-bit value as four independent BCD digits and incrementing the ones digit first. Carry propagation will be done via simple if statements sequentially, without intermediate 5-bit function returns. The enable signals will directly reflect when the corresponding lower digit reaches 9, indicating the next digit increments on the next clock.

This avoids nested function calls and reduces combinational complexity. The implementation will have a single always block with straightforward carry checks and updates. The synchronous reset clears the counter to zero.

This approach keeps the design easy to understand and maintain, while preserving correct carry behavior and enable signaling.
