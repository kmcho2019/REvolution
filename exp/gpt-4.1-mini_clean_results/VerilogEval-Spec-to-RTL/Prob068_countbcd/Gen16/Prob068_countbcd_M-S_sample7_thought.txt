I will simplify the BCD counter by using a single 16-bit register representing all four digits together, and incrementing the ones digit first. When a digit reaches 9, it rolls over to 0 and enables the next digit increment. I will use a single always block with straightforward conditional updates and directly generate the enable signals based on digit values. This reduces the number of variables and nested conditions while maintaining correct BCD counting and enable signal generation with synchronous reset.
