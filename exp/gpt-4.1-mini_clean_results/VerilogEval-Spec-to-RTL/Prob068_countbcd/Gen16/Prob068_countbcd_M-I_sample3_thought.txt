To optimize the 4-digit BCD counter design for better PPA (power, performance, and area), I will:

1. Simplify carry propagation logic to reduce critical path length by decoupling combinational carry calculation from the function calls, explicitly managing carries in a flatter structure without nested ternary operations.

2. Replace function calls with direct combinational logic for increment and carry detection to reduce combinational logic depth and allow easier optimization by synthesis tools.

3. Remove clock-enable gating on register updates, as the comparison logic adds combinational delay and power; modern synthesis tools often implement gating automatically or can better optimize straightforward logic.

4. Optimize enable signals to correspond only to the three upper digits (tens, hundreds, thousands) as requested, so ena[2] for thousands, ena[1] for hundreds, and ena[0] for tens digit increments; omit ones digit enable to align exactly with spec.

5. Make synchronous reset logic straightforward.

6. Keep the design modular and clean but reduce unnecessary combinational logic layers to improve timing and power while maintaining area efficiency.

This approach trades a slight increase in switching power due to removing clock gating for improved timing and simplified combinational paths, aiming to improve WNS and TNS while keeping area minimal.
