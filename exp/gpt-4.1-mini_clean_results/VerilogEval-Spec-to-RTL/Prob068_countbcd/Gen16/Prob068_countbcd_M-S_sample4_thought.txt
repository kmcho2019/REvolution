I will simplify the BCD counter by using a single 16-bit register to hold all four digits and perform the increment by separating the digits combinationally. On each clock, I increment the lowest digit; if it reaches 10, reset to 0 and carry to the next digit, and so on. The enable signals will be combinational outputs indicating when each lower digit is 9, showing when the next digit should increment. This approach removes nested if-else structures by using a straightforward ripple carry method with a simple procedural block, improving readability while keeping synchronous reset and the original interface.
