To improve the PPA metrics, I will focus on reducing the critical path delay and power consumption by simplifying and partially parallelizing the carry logic.

Instead of deeply nested if-elses which create a sequential carry chain, I will use a ripple carry approach with intermediate signals that generate carry enables between digits. This allows synthesis tools to optimize carry chains better.

I will separate the carry generation and digit increments in a way that reduces logic depth. Also, I will use non-blocking assignments carefully for all sequential logic.

To reduce power, the digits only update when incremented, so I will only update digits that change. Enable signals will be updated combinationally based on carry propagation signals.

This approach keeps synchronous reset and meets the interface requirements but reduces logic depth and switching activity, improving timing and potentially reducing power and area.
