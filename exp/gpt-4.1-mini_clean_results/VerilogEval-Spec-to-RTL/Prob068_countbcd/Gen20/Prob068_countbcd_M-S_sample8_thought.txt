I will simplify the design by breaking the BCD counter into four separate 4-bit digit registers instead of using a single 16-bit vector. This allows straightforward carry propagation by generating enables on the fly and updating each digit individually in a single clock cycle, keeping the logic clean and easier to read. The ena signals will be the carry-out signals from each digit increment, indicating when the next digit should increment. This approach reduces nested conditions and makes code more modular while preserving synchronous reset and correct BCD counting.
