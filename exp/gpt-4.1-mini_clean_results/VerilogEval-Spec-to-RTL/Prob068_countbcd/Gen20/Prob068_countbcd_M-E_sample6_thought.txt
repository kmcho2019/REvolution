I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter by treating each digit as a separate 4-bit register, each with its own increment logic. Instead of nested if-else, I will generate carry signals between digits that indicate when the next digit should increment. The enable outputs will be directly driven by these carry signals occurring after incrementing a digit. On each clock, the ones digit increments unconditionally unless reset. If it overflows from 9 to 0, it produces a carry that enables increment of tens, and so forth up to the thousands digit. This carry-chaining approach simplifies the logic, improves clarity, and naturally produces the enable signals from carry propagation, maintaining synchronous reset and positive edge clocking.
