To improve the PPA of the original 4-digit BCD counter design, I will optimize the carry propagation and reduce switching activity by introducing clock enable gating for the upper three digits.

Key improvements:
- Use explicit clock enables (derived from the carry signals) for each digit register to update only when that digit needs incrementing. This reduces unnecessary toggling of upper digits and thus power.
- Flatten the carry propagation logic by computing the carries combinationally before the clock edge, enabling a cleaner and shorter critical path.
- Maintain synchronous active-high reset and correct enable outputs.
- Keep the same interface and digit layout for compatibility.
- The enable signals (ena) remain combinational and indicate when each upper digit should increment on the next clock.

This approach trades a bit of combinational logic for carry generation before the clock, simplifying the sequential logic and improving timing and power by gating updates on upper digits.

Overall, this design reduces the critical path by limiting sequential nested carries and reduces power by avoiding unnecessary toggling of digits and output enables.
