I will simplify the BCD counter implementation by using four separate 4-bit registers for each digit and increment them sequentially on the positive clock edge. The carry logic will be straightforward: increment the ones digit; if it rolls over from 9 to 0, increment tens; if tens roll over, increment hundreds; and so forth.

The enable signals (ena) will be combinational outputs directly reflecting when the lower digit(s) are 9, indicating the next digit should increment on the next clock.

This avoids a function and complicated nested increments, improving clarity and maintainability while maintaining functional correctness and synchronous reset.

This approach uses simpler always blocks and combinational logic for enables, reducing complexity but still meets all requirements.
