To optimize the original design for better power and area without sacrificing correctness or timing:

- Add per-digit enable signals to update each digit register only when necessary, preventing unnecessary toggling and saving dynamic power.
- Keep the carry and next digit logic combinational as before for clean, glitch-free logic and immediate enable signals.
- Use the existing 'ena' signals as increment enables for digits [1:3], and define 'ena[0]' as always 1 because the ones digit increments every cycle.
- Use these enables in the sequential always block to conditionally update each digit register.
- This reduces switching activity in upper digits when they are not incremented, improving power.
- Minor logic simplification is kept minimal to maintain clarity and timing.
