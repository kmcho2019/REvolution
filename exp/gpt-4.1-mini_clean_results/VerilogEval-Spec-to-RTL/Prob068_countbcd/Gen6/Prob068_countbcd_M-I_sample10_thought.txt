To improve PPA, I will reduce switching activity and critical path delay by implementing clock enables per digit rather than unconditional updates. The ones digit always increments, so no change there.

For upper digits:
- Use the carry-out signals as clock enables to update digits only when necessary, thus gating their registers and saving power.
- This also simplifies combinational carry logic by avoiding nested conditional assignments inside the sequential block.
- Implement a synchronous reset with non-blocking assignments for proper timing.
- The carry signals remain combinational but the updates are enabled, reducing logic toggling.
- This reduces switching and shortens critical path by breaking long if-else chains into simpler enable-controlled increments.

This approach improves timing by minimizing logic depth in sequential updates and saves power by gating register toggling. Area is also reduced since simpler sequential logic per digit is used without nested conditions.
