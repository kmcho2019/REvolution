To optimize the 4-digit BCD counter for better timing and power:

- Separate the carry generation from the counting logic to reduce combinational depth in the clocked always block.
- Calculate carry signals combinationally and in parallel rather than nested if-else inside the clocked block, reducing the critical path.
- Use enable signals to trigger increments for the upper digits only when needed, minimizing toggling.
- Keep the synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments.
- This approach improves the critical path delay (better WNS/TNS) by parallelizing carry detection and reduces unnecessary switching for power savings.
