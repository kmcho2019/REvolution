I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a single 16-bit register `q` to hold all four BCD digits together. Each digit will occupy 4 bits as specified. Instead of nested if-else blocks, I will use a sequential approach that increments the least significant digit and then cascades carry flags for each digit increment in combinational logic inside the always block. This will reduce the nested structure and enable straightforward carry propagation.

Enable signals (ena) will be generated based on the carry flags to indicate when the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits should be incremented on the next cycle.

The counter logic will perform BCD addition modulo 10 for each digit, and synchronous reset will initialize the entire count to zero.

This approach keeps the digits packed in one register and uses explicit carry propagation with flags to increment each digit, providing a different structural design from the nested if-else approach.
