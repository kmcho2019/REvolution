To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will break the carry propagation chain by updating digits sequentially inside the clocked always block, using the enables to gate increments of higher digits. This will reduce the worst-case combinational delay because upper digits only update when the corresponding lower digit rolls over (i.e., enable is active), preventing unnecessary carry logic.

For power, I will ensure that upper digits only toggle when enabled by gating their increments with the enable signals. This reduces switching activity.

For area, the logic becomes simpler by removing nested function calls and instead using simple combinational logic for carry and digit update in a ripple-carry manner. This also makes the design more hardware friendly.

I will compute the enable signals combinationally, as in the original, based on digits before increment, reflecting when each upper digit should increment on the next clock cycle.

The synchronous active-high reset will reset the entire 16-bit register to zero.

This approach preserves functional correctness, cuts down carry propagation delay within one clock cycle, and reduces power by gating increments.
