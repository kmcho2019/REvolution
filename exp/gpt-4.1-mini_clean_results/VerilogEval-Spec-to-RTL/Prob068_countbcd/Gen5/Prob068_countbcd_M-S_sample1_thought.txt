Implement the 4-digit BCD counter by incrementing the ones digit every clock cycle, rolling it over at 9 to 0. When the ones digit rolls over, enable and increment the tens digit; similarly, when the tens digit rolls over, enable and increment the hundreds digit; and likewise for the thousands digit.

Generate the enable signals directly from the rollover conditions of the lower digit, ensuring each enable indicates when its corresponding digit should be incremented.

Use simple if-else logic within the clocked process to handle increments and rollovers sequentially, avoiding nested conditions and minimizing complexity.

On reset, clear all digits to zero.

This approach cleanly separates increment enables and digit updates, ensures correct carry propagation, and matches the output enable signals with the increment conditions.
