I will simplify the BCD counter by implementing it with four separate 4-bit digit registers for ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands. Each digit increments only when enabled, with enable signals generated combinationally based on the lower digit's value being 9 (indicating a carry to the next digit).

This approach avoids nested function calls and intermediate variables, resulting in simpler, cleaner sequential logic and easier-to-follow carry propagation. It also makes the enable signals naturally represent when the next digit should increment.

A synchronous active-high reset will initialize all digits to zero. The output q will be formed by concatenating the four digits, and ena will be the enable signals for tens, hundreds, and thousands digits.

This structure is straightforward, easier to maintain, and reduces combinational complexity inside the always block, while preserving the required behavior.
