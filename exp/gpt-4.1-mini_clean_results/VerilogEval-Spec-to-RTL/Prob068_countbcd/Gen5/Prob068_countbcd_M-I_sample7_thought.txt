To optimize the original design for better timing, power, and area (PPA), I will focus on:

- Timing: Minimize the combinational carry chain by restructuring the carry and next-value logic for the digits using simpler expressions to reduce logic depth.
- Power: Introduce register enables for the upper digits so they only update when needed (i.e., when their lower digit rolls over), reducing switching activity.
- Area: Simplify carry logic and use more straightforward if-else conditions inside sequential blocks to avoid large nested ternaries that can inflate multiplexers.

Implementation details:
- Keep the ones digit always enabled since it increments every cycle.
- For tens, hundreds, thousands, use enable signals derived from carry-out of the lower digit to clock-enable their updates.
- Calculate the carry signals combinationally for ena outputs and to gate register updates.
- Use synchronous reset as before.

This will reduce unnecessary toggling of upper digit registers (power saving), flatten combinational logic (timing improvement), and simplify muxing (area saving).
