To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter implementation while maintaining correct functionality and enable signals:

- Use a single 16-bit register for the entire BCD count to reduce explicit digit registers.
- On each clock cycle, increment the count by 1, then fix invalid BCD digits by adding 6 where needed, using a standard BCD correction approach.
- Generate enable signals for digits 1-3 based on whether the lower digits rolled over from 9 to 0 by detecting carries.
- Keep synchronous active-high reset.
- This approach removes nested if-else structures and relies on well-known BCD correction logic, thus simplifying the design with a clean, flat structure and fewer explicit comparisons.
