Refactor the 4-digit BCD counter by splitting the increment and carry propagation into parallel combinational steps before the clocked update, thus reducing combinational path length inside the always block. 

Specifically:
- Compute the carry and next digit values for each digit in combinational logic outside the sequential block using intermediate wires.
- Update the bcd_count register synchronously based on these precomputed values.
- Maintain ena signals as combinational outputs derived from current digit values.
- This approach improves timing by reducing nested conditional logic inside the clocked process and allows the synthesizer to better optimize the critical path.
- Retain the synchronous active-high reset and output assignments.
