Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q[3:0], q[7:4], q[11:8], and q[15:12]. Increment the ones digit each clock cycle. When a digit overflows from 9 to 0, generate an enable signal to increment the next digit. The ena output will be a 3-bit signal where each bit corresponds to enabling the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits respectively. Synchronous active-high reset clears the counter to zero.
