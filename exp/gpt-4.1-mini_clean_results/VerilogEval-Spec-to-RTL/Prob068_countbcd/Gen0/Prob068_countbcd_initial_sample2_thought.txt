Implement a 4-digit BCD counter using 4 separate 4-bit registers, each representing one decimal digit of the counter (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands). Increment the ones digit on every clock cycle. When a digit reaches 9, it resets to 0 and enables the increment of the next higher digit. The ena output is a 3-bit signal indicating when to increment the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits, respectively. The counter resets synchronously when reset is high.
