Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with digits stored in q[3:0], q[7:4], q[11:8], q[15:12]. On each clock cycle, increment the ones digit. When a digit rolls over from 9 to 0, set the corresponding enable signal (ena) for the next digit to increment it. The ena output is 3 bits for the tens, hundreds, and thousands digit increments. Reset clears all digits to zero synchronously. All operations happen on the positive clock edge.
