Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with synchronous reset. The counter is represented as four 4-bit digits within a 16-bit output q. The least significant digit (ones) increments every clock cycle (if enabled by reset). Each digit rolls over from 9 to 0, and on rollover, the enable output for the next digit is asserted for one clock cycle to increment it. The ena output is 3 bits corresponding to the enable signals for digits tens, hundreds, and thousands (q[7:4], q[11:8], q[15:12]). On reset, the counter is cleared to zero.
