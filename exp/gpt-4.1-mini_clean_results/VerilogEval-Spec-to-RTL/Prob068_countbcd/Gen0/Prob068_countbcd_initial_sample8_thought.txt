Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with each digit represented by 4 bits. The counter increments on each clock cycle. When the ones digit reaches 9 and increments, it rolls over to 0 and enables the tens digit (ena[0]). Similarly, when the tens digit reaches 9 and increments, it rolls over and enables the hundreds digit (ena[1]), and when the hundreds digit rolls over after 9, it enables the thousands digit (ena[2]). The reset synchronously resets all digits to zero. The ena outputs are generated based on carry conditions during increment.
