Implement a 4-digit BCD counter with each digit stored in 4 bits of q. On each clock cycle, increment the ones digit. If it overflows from 9 to 0, enable increment of the tens digit (ena[0]). Similarly, if tens digit overflows from 9 to 0, enable the hundreds digit (ena[1]), and if hundreds digit overflows from 9 to 0, enable the thousands digit (ena[2]). Synchronous active-high reset clears the counter to zero.
