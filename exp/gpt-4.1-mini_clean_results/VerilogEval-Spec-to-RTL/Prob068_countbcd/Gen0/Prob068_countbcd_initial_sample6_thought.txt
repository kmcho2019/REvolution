Implement a 4-digit BCD counter using a 16-bit register divided into four 4-bit BCD digits. On each clock cycle, increment the ones digit. If the ones digit rolls over from 9 to 0, enable and increment the tens digit, and similarly for hundreds and thousands. The ena output is a 3-bit signal indicating when to increment each of the upper three digits (tens, hundreds, thousands). Implement synchronous active-high reset to clear the counter to zero.
