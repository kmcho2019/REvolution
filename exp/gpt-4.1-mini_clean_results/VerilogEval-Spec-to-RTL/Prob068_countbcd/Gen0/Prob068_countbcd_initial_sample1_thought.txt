Implement a 4-digit BCD counter where each digit is 4 bits (BCD). The counter increments the ones digit (q[3:0]) every clock cycle. When a digit rolls over from 9 to 0, it enables the increment of the next higher digit, which is indicated by the corresponding ena bit. The ena signals correspond to digits 1, 2, and 3 (tens, hundreds, thousands), indicating when those digits should increment. The counter resets synchronously on an active-high reset. All counting and enable signals are updated on the positive edge of clk.
