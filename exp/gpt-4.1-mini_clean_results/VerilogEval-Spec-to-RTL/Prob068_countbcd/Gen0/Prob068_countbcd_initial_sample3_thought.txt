Implement a 4-digit BCD counter, with each digit represented by 4 bits in q. The least significant digit (ones) counts from 0 to 9 and rolls over to zero when it reaches 10, generating an enable pulse to increment the next digit (tens). Similarly, the tens digit counts and enables the hundreds digit, and the hundreds digit enables the thousands digit. ena[2:0] correspond to the enable signals for digits 1 (tens), 2 (hundreds), and 3 (thousands). On reset, q and ena are cleared to zero. All logic is synchronous with the clock's positive edge.
