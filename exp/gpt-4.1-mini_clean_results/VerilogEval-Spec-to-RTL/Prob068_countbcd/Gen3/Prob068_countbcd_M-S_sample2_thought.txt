Use a single 16-bit register to represent all four BCD digits, incrementing the ones digit every clock. Detect when each digit wraps from 9 to 0 to generate carry signals for the next digit. Apply these carry signals sequentially within the same clock cycle to increment upper digits. The ena signals correspond to carry occurrences at digits 1 to 3 (tens to thousands). On reset, clear the entire counter and disable enables. This approach avoids multiple enable registers by computing carry enables combinationally from the updated digit values, simplifying the logic and ensuring correct carry propagation in one clock cycle.
