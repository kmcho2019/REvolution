To improve the original design's performance and power without losing correctness, I will:

- Simplify carry and enable logic by avoiding nested ternary operators, replacing them with straightforward comparisons and addition with carry.
- Introduce enable signals internally to gate digit updates, so digits only update when needed, reducing unnecessary toggling and thus dynamic power.
- Keep ena output combinational for stable timing and correct enables for incrementing upper digits.
- Minimize combinational path length by calculating carry signals in a more direct manner.
- Maintain synchronous reset and positive-edge clocking.
- This approach balances improved timing by shorter carry logic, reduces power by gating digit updates, and keeps the design clear and synthesizable.
