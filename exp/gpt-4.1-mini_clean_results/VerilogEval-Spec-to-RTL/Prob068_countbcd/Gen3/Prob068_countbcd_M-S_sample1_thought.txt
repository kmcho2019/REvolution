Use a single 16-bit register to hold all four BCD digits packed together. On each clock edge, if not in reset, increment the counter as a whole by adding 1 to the ones digit and handle carry propagation by checking each digit for overflow (reaching 9). Generate enable signals for the upper three digits based on whether their preceding lower digit wraps around (i.e., causes a carry). This approach centralizes counting logic, reduces the number of registers, and naturally sequences carries and enables in a simple and clear manner.
