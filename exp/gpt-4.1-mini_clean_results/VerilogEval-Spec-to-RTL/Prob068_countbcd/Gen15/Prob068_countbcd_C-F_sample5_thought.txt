- Use separate 4-bit registers for each digit (like Example 2) to simplify enable signal generation, reduce complexity, and enable selective updating of digits, aiding power reduction.
- Incorporate a combinational carry-propagation circuit outside the clocked process to break the long combinational chain inside the clock edge logic (addressing performance from Example 1).
