I will refactor the BCD counter by replacing the nested always block increment logic with a single always block that uses a ripple carry approach with explicit carry signals, reducing nested conditionals. Each digit increment and carry-out is computed combinationally inside the sequential block to avoid deep function calls and nested ifs. Enable signals will be driven directly from carry propagation signals rather than comparing digit values, reducing switching. This structure will improve timing by flattening the logic and simplify the increment path, while preserving synchronous reset and output packing as before.
