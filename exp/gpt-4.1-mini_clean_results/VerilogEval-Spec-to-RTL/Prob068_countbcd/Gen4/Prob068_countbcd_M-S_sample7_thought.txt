To simplify the BCD counter, I will leverage a single 16-bit binary counter and convert its value to BCD digits on the fly using simple logic rather than cascading carry logic. Since it's a 4-digit BCD counter incrementing every clock, the ones digit increments every cycle and rolls over after 9, triggering the next digit increment, etc.

I will implement each digit as a simple 4-bit register incremented only when enabled by the lower digit rolling over from 9 to 0. The enable signals for digits 1-3 will be derived from these rollover conditions and output directly.

This avoids complex combinational carry chains by encoding increment enables and digit resets plainly and incrementally. The synchronous reset clears all digits to zero.

The design will use simple conditional increments, minimize combinational complexity, and keep the ena signals aligned with digit increment enables.
