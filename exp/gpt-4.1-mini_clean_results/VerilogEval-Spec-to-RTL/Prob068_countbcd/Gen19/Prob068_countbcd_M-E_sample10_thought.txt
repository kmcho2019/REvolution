I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter as four separate 4-bit BCD digit registers chained together with carry signals connecting each digit increment. On each clock edge, the ones digit increments; if it reaches 9, it rolls over and triggers an enable signal that increments the tens digit, and so forth for hundreds and thousands digits. The enables for digits 3:1 correspond directly to the carry-out signals generated from the lower digit reaching 9. This approach uses simple increment and rollover logic for each digit separately and makes enable signals explicit from carry propagation without complex nested functions. The reset synchronously clears all digits to zero. This modular digit-by-digit approach improves clarity and can facilitate better timing by avoiding deep combinational chains.
