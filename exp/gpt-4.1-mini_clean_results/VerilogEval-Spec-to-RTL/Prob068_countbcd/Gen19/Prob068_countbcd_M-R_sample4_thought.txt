I will refactor the design by breaking down the BCD counter into four separate 4-bit registers, each representing one decimal digit. Each digit will be incremented based on the enable signals derived from the lower digits reaching 9. This approach avoids a function call and nested if-else statements, using synchronous logic that updates digits only when enabled.

The enables will be generated by combinational logic that checks if the lower digits are at 9, causing a carry enable for the next higher digit.

This modular approach clarifies the increment logic, making the enable signals explicitly control each digit's increment, and provides a cleaner structure with four individual registers instead of one large register. It also potentially shortens the combinational paths for increment and carry detection by distributing logic.
