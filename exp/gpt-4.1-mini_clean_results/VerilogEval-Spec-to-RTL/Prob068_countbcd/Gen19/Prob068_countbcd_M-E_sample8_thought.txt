I will implement the BCD counter using four separate 4-bit registers, each representing one digit. Each digit increments only when enabled by the carry from the lower digit. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on when the previous digit rolls over from 9 to 0. This modular approach reduces logic dependency within a single register and makes the carry propagation explicit and localized. The synchronous reset will clear all digits to zero. This design can help reduce critical path delay by isolating digit increments and makes enable signals straightforward to produce.
