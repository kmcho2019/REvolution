I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter by using a single 16-bit binary counter that counts from 0 to 9999, and then convert its value into BCD digits using a double dabble (shift-add-3) algorithm. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated by detecting when each digit is about to roll over (i.e., when the lower digits are 9). This approach uses a simple binary counter for counting and a combinational BCD conversion, simplifying carry logic and improving timing by separating counting and digit extraction. The synchronous reset and clock edge behavior will be preserved.
