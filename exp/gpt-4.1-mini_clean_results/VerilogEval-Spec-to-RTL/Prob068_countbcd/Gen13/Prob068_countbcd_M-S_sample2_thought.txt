To simplify the design, I will implement a single 16-bit BCD counter that increments as a whole, but still treat each digit separately by unpacking and repacking the BCD digits. Instead of nested if-else statements, I will use a straightforward digit-by-digit increment approach within a single always block.

Enable signals will be derived combinationally by checking if the lower digit is 9, indicating that the next digit increments.

This reduces nested conditions and improves code clarity, while keeping synchronous reset and meeting all interface requirements.
