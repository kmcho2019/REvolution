Implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a single 16-bit register representing all digits concatenated, and leverage combinational logic to calculate the next counter value on every clock cycle. The counter increments as a 16-bit number, but after each increment, each 4-bit digit is individually checked for invalid BCD values (>9). If a digit exceeds 9, it is corrected by adding 6 (BCD correction). This method is known as "Double Dabble" or BCD correction via addition of 6.

Enable signals for the upper digits are generated based on whether a digit overflow (carry-out) from the previous digit occurred during the increment. This approach centralizes the digit incrementation and correction logic and decouples carry chain from nested if-elses, potentially improving timing and making the code more compact.

Synchronous active-high reset clears the entire 16-bit register to zero. The counter increments on every positive clock edge, and the ena outputs reflect when the corresponding digit is incremented due to carry from the previous digit.

This design benefits from a more arithmetic-style counting, which may be more amenable to synthesis optimizations and can simplify timing closure.
