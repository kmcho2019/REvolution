To improve the original 4-digit BCD counter design for better PPA (performance, power, area):

- Replace the deeply nested if-else carry propagation with a more parallel carry and increment approach to reduce critical path delay.
- Use intermediate "carry" signals computed combinationally before the clock edge to minimize sequential logic complexity.
- Generate the enable signals directly from the carry signals rather than repeated equality checks.
- Keep non-blocking assignments to maintain synchronous behavior.
- The cleaner carry chain reduces combinational logic depth, improving max frequency (WNS).
- Reduced toggling in enable signals by tying them directly to carry signals, potentially reducing power.
- This approach keeps area low by avoiding complex arithmetic while improving timing and power due to simpler carry logic.

This design calculates the carry signals upfront, increments the digits based on those, and finally outputs enable signals based on the carry chain, thereby flattening combinational logic and improving timing without significant area cost.
