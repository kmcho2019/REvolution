I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter using a single 16-bit register representing the entire counter and a combinational BCD incrementer module to calculate the next counter value. On each clock edge, if not reset, the counter updates synchronously to the incremented value. The enables for the upper digits (ena) will be generated by detecting when the lower digit(s) roll over from 9 to 0 in the incremented value (using simple combinational logic comparing current and next values). This approach separates the increment logic from the register storage, reducing deeply nested if-else in sequential logic, potentially improving timing by pushing increment computation to combinational logic and register update to sequential logic. It also eases scaling to more digits and keeps digit increments and enables consistent.
