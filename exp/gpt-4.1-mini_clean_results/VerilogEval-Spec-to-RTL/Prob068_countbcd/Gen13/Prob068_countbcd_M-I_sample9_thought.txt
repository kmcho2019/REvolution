To optimize the existing BCD counter for better performance and power:

1. Flatten the increment and carry logic to minimize combinational path depth within the clock cycle by computing all carries upfront in combinational logic.
2. Use a single always block for synchronous updates, but precompute the next state of each digit with simple combinational logic outside the always block.
3. This reduces nested if-else complexity and the critical path associated with cascading increments.
4. The enable signals are updated consistently based on carry-outs from each digit rollover.
5. The synchronous reset remains intact.
6. Although clock gating could further reduce power, it is not introduced here due to interface simplicity and synthesis friendliness.

This approach balances clarity and improved timing by minimizing sequential carry propagation delay within one clock edge.
