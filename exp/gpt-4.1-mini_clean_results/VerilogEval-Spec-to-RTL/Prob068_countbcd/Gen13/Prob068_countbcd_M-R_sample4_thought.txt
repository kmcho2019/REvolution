I will refactor the BCD counter by implementing a ripple carry approach using a carry chain computed combinationally before the sequential logic, separating the increment conditions and updates. 

Specifically, I'll create carry signals that indicate if a digit should roll over on the next increment. These carry signals are computed combinationally from the current digits and the current increment conditions.

In the sequential block, all digits are updated in parallel based on the carry inputs, avoiding nested if-else statements and potentially improving timing by reducing combinational path depth.

Enable signals are also generated combinationally from the carry chain, clearly indicating when each digit is incremented.

This refactor maintains the synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments, but restructures the logic into clearer, parallel operations that can improve readability, synthesis, and timing.
