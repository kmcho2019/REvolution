I will simplify the 4-digit BCD counter by storing all digits in a single 16-bit register and performing the increment using a ripple carry approach in a sequential always block with non-blocking assignments. Each digit increments only when the lower digit reaches 9, triggering a carry. The enable signals are assigned combinationally based on whether the lower digits have rolled over to 9, indicating the increment enable for the next digit. This approach keeps the code concise and straightforward while maintaining synchronous reset, proper digit assignment, and required enable signals.
