I will implement the 4-digit BCD counter by using a single 16-bit register representing all digits concatenated, and increment it as a packed BCD number using a combinational BCD incrementer logic. The counter will increment by adding 1 to the least significant BCD digit, propagating carries with conditional adjustments (adding 6 when a digit exceeds 9). This removes nested ifs and uses parallel BCD addition logic, which can be more hardware-friendly for timing. The ena signals will be generated synchronously by detecting when each digit should be incremented, i.e., when the previous digit overflows from 9 to 0. This approach simplifies carry logic and leverages packed BCD arithmetic for clean and fast counting with synchronous reset and clock edge triggering.
