To optimize the FSM for better performance and power, I will switch the state encoding from binary to one-hot encoding. One-hot encoding generally reduces the logic depth of next-state decoding and output logic, which improves timing (reducing critical path delay) and can slightly reduce dynamic power by lowering combinational switching activity. Since the FSM has only five states, one-hot encoding is area-viable. I will implement next-state logic using simple combinational assignments per state flip-flop, maintaining synchronous reset. Output logic will be a simple OR of the corresponding one-hot states producing output z. This refactor should improve timing and power with minimal area overhead.
