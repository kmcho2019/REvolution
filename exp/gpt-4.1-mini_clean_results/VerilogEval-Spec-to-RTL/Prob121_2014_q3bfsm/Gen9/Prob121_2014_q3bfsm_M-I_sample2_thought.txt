To optimize PPA, trade off a slight increase in area for potentially improved timing and power by using one-hot state encoding instead of binary encoding. One-hot encoding simplifies next-state logic to mostly simple muxes and can reduce logic depth, improving timing (potentially improving negative slack) and reducing switching activity per bit (potentially lowering power). Although one-hot uses more flip-flops (5 FFs for 5 states), the next state and output logic become simpler and more direct, often leading to better overall PPA in modern synthesis flows.
