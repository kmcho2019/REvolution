To optimize the FSM for better timing, power, and area (PPA), I will implement the next-state logic using a combinational always block with a case statement. Case statements are often better optimized by synthesis tools, potentially improving timing. Additionally, I will encode the FSM states using one-hot encoding instead of binary encoding to reduce logic depth and switching in next-state decoding logic, which can improve performance and power consumption. Since there are five states, one-hot encoding fits naturally with 5 bits. Output logic will be a combinational assignment directly from state bits, reducing logic levels. The synchronous reset remains. This approach balances readability, efficient synthesis mapping, and better PPA metrics.
