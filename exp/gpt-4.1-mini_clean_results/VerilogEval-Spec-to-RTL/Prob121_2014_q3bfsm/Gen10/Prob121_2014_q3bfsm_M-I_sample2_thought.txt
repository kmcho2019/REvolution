To optimize for better performance and power while maintaining a clean design, I will change the FSM state encoding from binary to one-hot encoding. One-hot encoding reduces the complexity of the next-state logic by using simpler AND/OR logic, improving timing due to fewer logic levels. Although one-hot encoding uses more flip-flops, the area increase is negligible for 5 states, and it can reduce switching activity by localizing state changes to a single bit. Additionally, I will maintain the separation of next state and output logic for clarity and keep synchronous reset. The output z will be generated by simple OR of specific one-hot state bits, minimizing combinational logic depth and potentially improving timing. This should improve the timing metrics (tns, wns) and reduce power by simplifying combinational logic.
