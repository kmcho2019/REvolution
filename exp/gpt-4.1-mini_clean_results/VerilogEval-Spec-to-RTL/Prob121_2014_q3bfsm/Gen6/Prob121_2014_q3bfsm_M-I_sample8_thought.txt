To optimize for PPA (performance, power, and area), we will merge the output logic into the synchronous state register block, registering output z to reduce combinational paths and glitches. This also reduces switching activity by ensuring z only changes on clock edges, which can reduce power. The next state logic remains combinational for clarity and speed. The state encoding is kept binary to minimize area since FSM is small. This registered output approach reduces critical path delay and glitches, improving timing and power without area penalty.
