To optimize the FSM for better performance, power, and area, I will adopt a one-hot state encoding. One-hot encoding often reduces combinational logic complexity and can improve timing by minimizing decoding logic depth, especially beneficial in small FSMs like this one. Additionally, I will separate the output logic into a simple continuous assignment based solely on the state register to reduce unnecessary toggling and potential glitches, improving power consumption. The synchronous reset and state update logic remain unchanged for proper timing and simulation behavior. This approach simplifies the next-state logic, potentially reducing area and improving clock frequency, while making output transitions glitch-free and power-efficient.
