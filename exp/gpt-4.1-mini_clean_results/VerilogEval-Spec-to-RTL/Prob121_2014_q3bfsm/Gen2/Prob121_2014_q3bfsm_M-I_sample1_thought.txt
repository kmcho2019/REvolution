To improve power and timing (PPA), I will register the output signal z to reduce glitches and switching activity, as z depends only on the current state. Separating the output logic into a simple combinational block that derives z solely from the present state and then registering z on clock reduces glitches and optimizes timing. Additionally, I will keep next_state logic clean and separate. The synchronous reset remains unchanged for predictable timing and synthesis friendliness. This registered output approach generally improves power and timing with minimal area impact.
