To optimize the FSM for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will use one-hot state encoding since it can simplify next-state logic and reduce combinational logic depth, potentially improving maximum clock frequency and lowering switching power by minimizing logic transitions.

The FSM has 5 states, so one-hot requires 5 flip-flops. The reset synchronously sets state 000 to the one-hot equivalent (state 000 = s0). Next-state logic will be a combinational assignment using simple boolean expressions derived directly from the transition table.

Output z will be registered to reduce glitches and switching power on output, improving power and timing stability.

Overall, this one-hot approach trades a small area increase (5 bits vs 3 bits) for simplified combinational logic, registered output, and potentially faster and lower-power operation.
