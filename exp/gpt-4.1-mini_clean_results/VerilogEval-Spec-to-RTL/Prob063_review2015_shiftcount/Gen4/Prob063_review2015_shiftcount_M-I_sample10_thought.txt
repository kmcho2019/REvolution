To optimize for better performance, power, and area, combine the shift and decrement logic into a single mux-based data input to the register, avoiding explicit if-else priority coding. This allows synthesis tools to better optimize the combinational logic. Specifically, create a next value signal that selects between shifted and decremented values based on the enables. Since shift_ena and count_ena are never asserted together by design, this mux is safe. Also, avoid concatenation inside the always block by defining shift result combinationally. This structure reduces critical path muxing and helps synthesis produce efficient logic, slightly improving delay and power.
