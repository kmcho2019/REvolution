To simplify, I will implement the 4-bit register update using one always block that prioritizes shifting over counting since the problem says the two enables won't be active together and priority doesn't matter if they are. The shift will be done by shifting left inserting the data bit at LSB, decrement by subtracting 1, and otherwise hold the value. This removes the separate combinational next_val signal, reducing logic complexity and improving readability, while maintaining the same functionality.
