I will implement the 4-bit shift register and down-counter by splitting the logic into two separate always blocks to emphasize clarity and modularity:

- One always block triggered on the positive clock edge will handle the shifting operation if shift_ena is high.
- Another always block, also on the positive clock edge, will handle the counting down operation if count_ena is high.
- Since the problem states that shift_ena and count_ena are never high simultaneously, this separation avoids multiplexing logic inside a single always block.
- To resolve potential contention, each always block will only update the register when its enable is asserted, and otherwise hold the previous value.
- The register q will be declared as reg and updated exclusively by one of these two always blocks depending on the enable signals. For simulation clarity, a combined non-blocking assignment approach will ensure no race conditions.
- On reset (implemented as synchronous reset using the first positive clock edge), q will be initialized to zero.
- Shifting inserts new data at the MSB side (left), shifting existing bits right towards LSB side.
- Counting down subtracts 1 mod 16 (4-bit wraparound).
- This approach emphasizes separation of concerns and is easy to read and verify.
