To refactor the design, I will separate the combinational logic from the sequential update by creating next-state logic signals. This approach improves code clarity and synthesis friendliness by clearly defining the combinational next value and then registering it on the clock edge. I will use a combinational always block (or continuous assignments) to compute the next value of the shift register based on shift_ena and count_ena, and then update the register q synchronously on clk's positive edge. Initialization will be done by synchronous reset logic emulated with an initial condition flag. This modularizes the design and helps synthesis tools optimize the combinational and sequential parts separately.
