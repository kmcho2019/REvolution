Implement the shift register and counter as two separate always blocks sharing the same register 'q'. On each positive clock edge, if shift_ena is high, perform a left shift with the incoming data in the LSB position (most-significant-bit first means data goes into MSB, so shift left). If count_ena is high, decrement q by 1. If neither enable is active, hold the current value. Prioritize shift_ena over count_ena to avoid conflicts. This separation makes the operations conceptually distinct and may help clarify timing paths.
