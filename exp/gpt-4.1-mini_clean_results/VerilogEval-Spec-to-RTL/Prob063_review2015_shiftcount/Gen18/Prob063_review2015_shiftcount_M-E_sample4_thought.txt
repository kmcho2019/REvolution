Implement the shift register and counter using two separate registers: one for shifting in the data and another for holding the count value. On shift_ena, update the shift register by shifting left and loading the data bit into LSB; on count_ena, decrement the count register. Then combine both registers via a multiplexer controlled by shift_ena and count_ena to drive the output q. Since shift_ena and count_ena never assert simultaneously, this MUX cleanly selects which value appears on q. This separation clarifies the design and could facilitate easier debugging or modification.
