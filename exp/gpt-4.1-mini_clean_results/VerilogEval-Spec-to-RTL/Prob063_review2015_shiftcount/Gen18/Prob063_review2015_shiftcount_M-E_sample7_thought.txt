Instead of updating the register in a single always block with conditional logic, I will separate the logic into two always blocks: one for shifting and one for counting, both clocked on the positive edge. A multiplexing stage will select between the shifted value, the decremented value, or hold, to update the register. This structure clarifies data flow, allowing more straightforward optimization by synthesis tools and enabling potential pipeline or gating improvements. For decrementing, I will use the subtraction operator with a constant one to clearly express the down counter. To implement "most-significant-bit first" shifting, I will shift right and load the new data into the MSB, effectively shifting bits towards the LSB. This approach differs from a left shift and corresponds directly to the problem statement. When neither enable is asserted, the register retains its value. This modular approach is clean and synthesizable.
