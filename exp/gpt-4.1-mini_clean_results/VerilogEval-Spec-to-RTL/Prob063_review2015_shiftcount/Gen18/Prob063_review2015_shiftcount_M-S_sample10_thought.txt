To simplify the design, I will directly update the 4-bit register inside a single always block without separate combinational signals. On each clock edge, if shift_ena is high, the shift operation occurs; else if count_ena is high, the decrement happens; otherwise, q holds its value. This removes the need for intermediate wires and a multiplexer outside the sequential block, reducing combinational complexity. The logic prioritizes shift_ena over count_ena, which is acceptable per the problem statement. This simplification improves readability and slightly reduces logic levels while preserving functionality.
