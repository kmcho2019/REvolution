Implement the shift register and down counter functionality using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock. Instead of separate next-state logic, embed the shift and count operations directly within the sequential block. When shift_ena is high, shift the register left and input the new data bit into the MSB (most-significant bit first). When count_ena is high (and shift_ena is low), decrement the register value. If neither enable is high, hold the register state. This approach merges control and state update for simplicity. To align with "MSB-first" shifting, shift left with the new bit entering the LSB is incorrect; instead, shift right with data entering the MSB.
