The original solution shifts data into the LSB side, which contradicts the requirement to shift data in most-significant-bit (MSB) first. To correctly implement MSB-first shifting, the new data bit must enter at the MSB (bit 3), and existing bits should shift right.

To optimize PPA:

- Shift right by one bit, inserting new data at MSB, reflecting MSB-first shifting.
- Implement the decrement operation as (q - 1), since it is straightforward and efficient for 4-bit width.
- Prioritize shift_ena over count_ena as before, since both are never asserted simultaneously.
- Use a clock enable on the register update to minimize unnecessary toggling when neither enable is asserted.
- Maintain a single always block triggered on clk's rising edge for simplicity and synthesis friendliness.

This approach corrects the direction of shifting to meet the specification and keeps the logic minimal for good performance and power characteristics.
