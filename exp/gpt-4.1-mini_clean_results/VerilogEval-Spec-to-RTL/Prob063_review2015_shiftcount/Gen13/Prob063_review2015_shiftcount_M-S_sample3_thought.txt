Since the problem does not specify a reset, initialization can be omitted for simplicity. We can combine the shift and decrement logic into a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge. When shift_ena is high, shift in the new bit at the MSB by shifting right; when count_ena is high, decrement the 4-bit register by 1. Since the problem states that both enables are never high simultaneously, priority can be given to shift_ena. This straightforward approach simplifies the design while maintaining correct functionality.
