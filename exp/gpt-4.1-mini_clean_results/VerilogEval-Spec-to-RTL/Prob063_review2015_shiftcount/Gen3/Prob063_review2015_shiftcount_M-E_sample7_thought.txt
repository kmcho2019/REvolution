Implement the four-bit shift register as a register `q` that updates on the clock's positive edge. When `shift_ena` is active, shift the current bits toward the LSB (right shift) and insert the new `data` at the MSB (bit 3). When `count_ena` is active (and `shift_ena` is inactive), decrement the 4-bit number stored in `q`. To ensure clear initialization and predictable behavior, include an asynchronous reset input that initializes `q` to zero. This reset also guarantees proper initial conditions, preventing simulation mismatches due to unknown states. By explicitly handling reset and respecting the priority where shifting dominates counting if both enables are asserted simultaneously, the module cleanly performs both functions in one register.
