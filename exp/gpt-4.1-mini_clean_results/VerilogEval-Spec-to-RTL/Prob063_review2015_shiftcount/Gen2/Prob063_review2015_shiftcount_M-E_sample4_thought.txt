Use a 4-bit register with separate logic for shifting and counting. When shift_ena is asserted, shift the register left by one bit (towards MSB) inserting data at LSB, then reverse the entire register bits so that the new data appears at the MSB, effectively achieving MSB-first shifting. When count_ena is asserted, simply decrement the current register value. This approach distinctly separates the shift operation from counting, ensuring clarity and correctness in both functionalities.
