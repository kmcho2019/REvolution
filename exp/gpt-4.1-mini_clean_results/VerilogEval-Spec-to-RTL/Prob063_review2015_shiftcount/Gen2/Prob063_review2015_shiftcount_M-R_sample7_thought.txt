Refactor the design by using a single sequential always block triggered on the positive clock edge without any combinational next-state logic. Use an if-else statement to update the 4-bit register `q` directly: if `shift_ena` is asserted, shift `data` into the MSB and shift the other bits right; else if `count_ena` is asserted, decrement the value of `q` safely as an unsigned 4-bit number (wrapping around on underflow). If neither is asserted, hold the current value. This approach avoids any bit-width mismatch in decrement and simplifies the design by removing intermediate signals and combinational blocks.
