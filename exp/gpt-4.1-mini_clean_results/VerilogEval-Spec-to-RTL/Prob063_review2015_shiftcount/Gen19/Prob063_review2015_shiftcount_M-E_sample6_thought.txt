Implement the shift register and down counter using a combinational next-state calculation. When shift_ena is asserted, the register shifts left by one bit, shifting in the input bit as the most significant bit, effectively inserting the new data at q[3]. When count_ena is asserted, the register decrements by one. If neither enable is asserted, the register retains its value. Use a single always block triggered by the clock edge, with a combinational next state variable updated based on the enables. This approach cleanly separates the next state calculation from the register update, providing a clear architectural distinction and facilitating easier modifications or extensions later.
