To improve PPA, I will simplify the decrement logic by using addition of a 4-bit constant -1 instead of a subtract operation, which may lead to simpler carry logic in some synthesis tools. Also, I'll reorder the shift direction to be MSB-first by shifting right, inserting new data at MSB, which better matches the specification "Data is shifted in most-significant-bit first." This avoids confusion and aligns with the naming and comments. This can also reduce toggling in LSB bits during shift. Finally, I'll gate the register update by the enables (shift_ena or count_ena) to reduce unnecessary toggling and save dynamic power. These modest changes can slightly reduce switching and area while keeping the design functionally correct.
