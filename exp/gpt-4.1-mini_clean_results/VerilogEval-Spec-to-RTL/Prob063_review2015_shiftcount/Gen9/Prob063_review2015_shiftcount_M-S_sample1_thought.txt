Combine the shift and decrement operations into one always block with a simple if-else structure, since both never occur simultaneously.

Shift the register left by one bit and insert the new data bit at the most significant bit position (q[3]) when shift_ena is high, reflecting "most-significant-bit first" shifting.

Otherwise, decrement the value when count_ena is high.

No reset is added to keep design minimal, and q holds its value when neither enable is asserted.

This results in clear, minimal logic meeting the spec.
