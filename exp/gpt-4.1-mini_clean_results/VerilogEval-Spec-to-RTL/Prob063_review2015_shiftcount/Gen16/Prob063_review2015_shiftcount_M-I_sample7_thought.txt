To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, I will replace the manual ripple borrow decrement logic with a straightforward arithmetic subtraction (`q - 1`). This allows synthesis tools to implement efficient carry logic, reducing critical path delay and area. For the shift operation, I will use a single vector concatenation assignment (`q <= {q[2:0], data};`) instead of multiple bitwise assignments, enabling better resource sharing and simpler hardware. Since the design does not need to handle both enables asserted simultaneously, I will give priority to shift_ena as before. These changes will simplify the code, enhance readability, and improve timing, power, and area metrics.
