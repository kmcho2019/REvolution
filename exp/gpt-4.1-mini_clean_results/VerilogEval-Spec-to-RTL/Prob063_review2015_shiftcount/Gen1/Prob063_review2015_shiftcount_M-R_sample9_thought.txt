Refactor by separating the combinational logic for the next state of the register from the sequential update. Compute the next value of the 4-bit register `q_next` in a combinational always block depending on `shift_ena` and `count_ena`, then update `q` on the positive clock edge. This approach keeps the state update clean and clear. We will also omit the redundant hold case as the register will maintain value by default. No reset is added since it was not in the original requirements. The priority remains shift over count if both are asserted.
