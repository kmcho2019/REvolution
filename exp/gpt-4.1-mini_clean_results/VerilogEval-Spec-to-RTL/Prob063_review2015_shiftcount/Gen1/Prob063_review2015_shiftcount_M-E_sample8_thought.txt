Implement the 4-bit shift register and counter as two separate registers internally, then multiplex between them on the output based on which operation was last performed. On each clock edge, if shift_ena is high, perform a left shift inserting `data` at MSB and update an internal shift register; if count_ena is high, decrement an internal counter. Then drive the output `q` from whichever register was last updated. This decouples shifting and counting operations, clarifies state, and avoids mixing shift and count logic. Initialize both registers to zero at the start using an asynchronous reset input for proper known start state.
