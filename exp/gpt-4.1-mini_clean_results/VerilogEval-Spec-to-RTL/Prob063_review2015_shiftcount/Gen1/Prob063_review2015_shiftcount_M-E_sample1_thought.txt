Use two separate always blocks to clearly separate shift and count operations to avoid unintended interactions. Maintain an internal 4-bit register. On a rising clock edge, if shift_ena is asserted, shift left by one position (toward LSB) inserting the input data at MSB (thus "most-significant-bit first"). If count_ena is asserted instead, decrement the register value treating it as a binary number. If neither is asserted, hold the value. Since the problem states shift_ena and count_ena will never be high simultaneously in real use, no explicit priority handling is needed. This architecture simplifies understanding and debugging by isolating the two behaviors, ensures that the register behaves consistently as both a shift register and a down counter, and cleanly models the functionality.
