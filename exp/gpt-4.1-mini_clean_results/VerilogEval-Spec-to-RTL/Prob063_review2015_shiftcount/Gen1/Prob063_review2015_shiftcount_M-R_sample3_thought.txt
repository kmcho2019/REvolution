Refactor the design by separating the next state logic from the register update. Use a combinational always block to compute the next value of the 4-bit register based on shift_ena and count_ena inputs, then update the register q on the clock's positive edge. When shift_ena is asserted, shift the register contents right by one bit (toward LSB) and load the input data into the MSB (q[3]). When count_ena is asserted and shift_ena is low, decrement the register by 1. This structure clearly separates combinational logic from sequential logic and avoids any confusion about the shifting operation.
