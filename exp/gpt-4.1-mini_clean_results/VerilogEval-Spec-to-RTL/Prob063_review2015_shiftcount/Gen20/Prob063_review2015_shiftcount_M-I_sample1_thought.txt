To optimize the design for better PPA, I will separate the shift and decrement logic into two always blocks with gated clocks enabled only when needed, reducing unnecessary toggling and logic depth. This explicitly isolates the shift and count paths, which can improve timing by reducing mux complexity and fanout. Instead of adding 4'b1111 for decrement, I will use a simple decrementer (q - 1) using a subtractor to potentially leverage synthesis tools' optimizations. Initialization will be done via synchronous reset for safer and cleaner startup without additional state variables. Overall, this approach reduces switching activity by enabling updates only when needed and slightly improves timing by reducing conditional logic in a single block.
