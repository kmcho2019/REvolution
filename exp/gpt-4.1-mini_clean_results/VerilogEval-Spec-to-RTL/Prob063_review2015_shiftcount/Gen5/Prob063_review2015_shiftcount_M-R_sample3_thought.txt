Refactor the design by separating the shift register and the counter functionality into two combinational signals and then selecting the next state in a synchronous always block based on enables. This clarifies behavior and uses an intermediate wire for the shifted value and the decremented value. The shift operation remains a right shift with data entering MSB. The decrement operation subtracts one with wrap-around on 4 bits. No reset is added since the problem does not specify one, but the structure improves readability and modularity. This approach uses explicit intermediate wires instead of embedding logic directly in a single assignment.
