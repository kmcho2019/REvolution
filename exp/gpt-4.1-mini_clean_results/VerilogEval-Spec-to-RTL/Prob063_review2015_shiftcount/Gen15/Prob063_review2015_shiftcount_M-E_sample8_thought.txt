I will implement the module by explicitly separating the shift register and counter operations using two always blocks: one for shifting and one for decrementing. Internally, the shift register holds the data; when shift_ena is asserted, the data shifts left with the new bit entering the MSB (most-significant bit first). When count_ena is asserted (and shift_ena is not), the stored value decrements by 1. To avoid conflicts, I will use a 4-bit internal register and update it conditionally based on the enables in separate blocks synchronized on the same clock. This clean separation of concerns improves readability and modularity without adding extra control logic, leveraging Verilog's non-blocking assignments and clocked processes.
