I will implement the 4-bit shift register and down counter as two separate registers and multiplex their update inputs based on the enables. Instead of updating the same register differently for shift and count, I compute both potential next values combinationally: one for shift operation and one for decrement operation. On clock edge, q loads either the shifted value or the decremented value depending on which enable is active. This avoids priority encoding inside the always block and simplifies timing. The decrement will be implemented as a combinational subtractor (q - 1), and the shift will shift left inserting the data bit at LSB to achieve MSB-first shifting. When both enables are 0, q holds its value. This approach cleanly separates combinational next state logic and register update, which can help synthesis tools optimize muxing and arithmetic separately, potentially improving timing and area.
