I will implement the 4-bit shift register and down counter as two separate 4-bit registers internally, but only one will hold the valid state at a time. When shift_ena is asserted, the shift register shifts in data MSB-first, and its value updates the main register; when count_ena is asserted, the down-counter decrements the main register value and updates it. This partitioning allows cleaner combinational logic, separating the shift operation and the decrement operation, simplifying timing and possibly improving area and power. The output q directly reflects the main register. Since the problem states that shift_ena and count_ena are never asserted together, we do not need to arbitrate. The clock always drives the update to the main register, so no clock gating is needed, and we keep non-blocking assignments. This approach cleanly separates shifting and counting logic, likely improving synthesis optimization and making the design more modular and clear.
