To improve PPA based on the given metrics and feedback, I will remove the internal init_done register and instead assume an asynchronous active-low reset input (resetn) to initialize the shift register. This avoids startup logic toggling, improving power and area slightly, and simplifies initialization. The resetn input will be added as a required design improvement since real designs typically include reset.

Other improvements:
- Prioritize shift_ena over count_ena as before.
- Use a single always @(posedge clk or negedge resetn) block with synchronous reset for better synthesis results.
- Avoid redundant assignments, letting register hold value if neither enable is active.
- Keep shifting MSB-first by shifting left and inputting data into LSB.

This will improve initialization clarity, reduce area by removing init_done register, and potentially reduce switching power. The asynchronous reset also can improve timing and avoid startup uncertainty.

If the environment disallows adding reset, this approach can be adapted to a synchronous reset as well. But adding reset is a typical and recommended best practice.
