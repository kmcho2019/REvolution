To simplify the design, I will combine the shift and decrement operations into a single always block without an initialization flag or synchronous reset. The register 'q' will be updated directly based on shift_ena or count_ena, assuming the initial state is undefined or set externally. The shift will be left-shift with new data shifted into the LSB, matching "most-significant-bit first" shifting. The decrement will be a simple subtraction by 1 using the '-' operator. Since both enables are never active simultaneously, priority can be arbitrary. This simplification reduces control signals and logic, making the code shorter and easier to understand without affecting functionality.
