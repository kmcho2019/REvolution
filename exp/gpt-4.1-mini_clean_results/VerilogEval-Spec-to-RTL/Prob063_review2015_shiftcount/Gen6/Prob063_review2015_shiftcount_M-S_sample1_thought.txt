Implement a simple 4-bit shift register with decrement functionality using one clocked always block. When `shift_ena` is asserted, shift bits towards the LSB by moving bits [3:1] down to [2:0] and insert the input `data` at the MSB. When `count_ena` is asserted, decrement the 4-bit value by one. Since both signals are never high together, the order of if-else checking does not matter. No reset or extra logic is needed.
