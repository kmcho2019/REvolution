Refactor the shift register and decrement logic using a combinational next-state calculation and a sequential register update. The next value of `q` is first computed based on `shift_ena` and `count_ena`. Then, on the positive clock edge, `q` updates to this next value.

This separates combinational logic (next-state calculation) from sequential logic (register update), making the design clearer and potentially easier for synthesis tools to optimize. 

Since both enables are never asserted together, priority is given to shift_ena in the next-state logic, matching the original behavior.

No asynchronous reset is added, preserving the problem assumptions.
