Implement the shift register and down counter operations using separate always blocks with enable signals to avoid priority conflicts. Use a shared 4-bit register updated either by shift or count, controlled by two separate flip-flop enable signals generated combinationally from shift_ena and count_ena. On a clock edge, if shift_ena is active, shift in 'data' at MSB; else if count_ena is active, decrement q. This approach cleanly separates logic for shifting and counting and avoids assigning q redundantly when no operation occurs, potentially reducing switching activity.
