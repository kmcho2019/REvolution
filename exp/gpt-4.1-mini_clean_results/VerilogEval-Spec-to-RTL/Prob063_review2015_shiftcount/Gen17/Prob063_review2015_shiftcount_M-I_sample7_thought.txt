To further optimize PPA, I will separate the shift and decrement operations into two always blocks with gated clocks using enable signals internally, effectively reducing switching and logic depth on non-updated clocks. Instead of using an initialization flag, I will add a synchronous reset input (active low) for cleaner, synthesis-friendly reset and reduced startup glitches. The shift operation will explicitly shift in data into MSB (since problem states "most-significant-bit first"), so the input data should be shifted in at the MSB position (not LSB as in the example). The decrement operation will use a simple ripple borrow subtractor implemented combinationally with minimal hardware or use addition of q + 4'b1111 (q - 1 mod 16), which is efficient, but with clock gating it will not toggle unnecessarily. This gated update reduces dynamic power. Overall, this change reduces toggle rate, shortens critical path by separating logic, and includes a proper reset for better reliability and PPA improvement.
