To optimize the PPA while preserving the original functionality, combine the shift and decrement logic into a single multiplexer-based update. This removes sequential priority and enables synthesis tools to optimize the combinational logic better, potentially reducing delay. Also, reset the register to a known value (like 4'b0000) on initialization for safer simulation and synthesis. The clock gating for power is not introduced due to design simplicity and marginal gains. This approach slightly improves timing by allowing the synthesizer to optimize logic merging, reduces minor mux overhead, and maintains minimal area and power.
