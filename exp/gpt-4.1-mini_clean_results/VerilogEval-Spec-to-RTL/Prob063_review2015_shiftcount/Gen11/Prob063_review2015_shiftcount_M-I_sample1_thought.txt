To improve PPA (timing, power, and area), we can add clock gating logic to minimize unnecessary switching, as only one of shift or count operations happens per cycle. We generate an internal enable signal that is the OR of shift_ena and count_ena and gate the clock to the register only when needed, reducing dynamic power. The shift and count operations remain mutually exclusive since the inputs are never both 1 in actual use. This reduces toggling of the register flip-flops and associated logic, improving power and potentially timing. The design remains simple and area overhead from clock gating is minimal compared to power savings.
