Since shift_ena and count_ena are never asserted together, we can simplify the always block to directly prioritize one over the other without extra logic. Shift operation shifts the register left by one bit and inserts 'data' into the MSB (bit 3), as required for "most-significant-bit first" shifting. Decrement operation simply subtracts 1 from the current 4-bit value. No reset or additional logic is needed. This keeps the implementation minimal and clear.
