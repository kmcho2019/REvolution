To simplify the design, I will combine the shift and count operations into a single always block without the initialization logic, assuming the register powers up in an undefined state which is acceptable here. The shift will be a left shift inserting 'data' at LSB when shift_ena is high; the count will decrement the register when count_ena is high; if both enables are zero, the register holds its value. Since the problem states behavior when both enables are high doesn't matter, I will give priority to shift_ena for clarity. This reduces code size and complexity while preserving required functionality.
