Refactor the design by consolidating the next-state logic for the 4-bit register into a single combinational always block. This block will select between shifting and counting based on the enable signals, with priority given to shift_ena. On each clock edge, q is updated with the computed next value. This reduces redundancy, eliminates unnecessary intermediate registers, and clarifies the shift direction to MSB-first: shift right, inserting the data bit into the MSB. The counter decrements q by 1 when count_ena is asserted without shift_ena. Holding occurs when neither enable is asserted.
