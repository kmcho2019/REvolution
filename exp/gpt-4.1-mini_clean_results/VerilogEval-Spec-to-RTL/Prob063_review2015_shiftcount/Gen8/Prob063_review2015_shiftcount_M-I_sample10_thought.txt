To optimize the original design for better PPA (timing, power, and area), I will simplify the decrement logic by replacing the subtract operation with a simple combinational decrement using a ripple borrow approach, which for a 4-bit value is minimal and potentially faster. Also, since both enables are never high simultaneously, I can prioritize count_ena over shift_ena, which enables the simpler decrement logic to take precedence and may reduce critical path delay. Additionally, I will use a single always @(posedge clk) block with if-else conditions to minimize register complexity and simplify synthesis. This also reduces combinational logic levels by integrating next-state logic directly into the sequential block. This approach can improve timing by reducing combinational delay before the register, and reduce switching by gating updates to only when enables are asserted. Overall, these changes aim to reduce logic depth, slightly lower area, and reduce dynamic power due to fewer intermediate signals and simpler combinational logic.
