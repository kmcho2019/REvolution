Implement a 4-bit shift register using a reg [3:0]. On the positive clock edge, if shift_ena is high, shift the register left by 1, inputting the new data bit at the MSB (bit 3). If count_ena is high instead, decrement the 4-bit register value by 1. When both shift_ena and count_ena are high, either operation can take priority, so we'll just check shift_ena first. The output q is assigned to the current shift register value.
