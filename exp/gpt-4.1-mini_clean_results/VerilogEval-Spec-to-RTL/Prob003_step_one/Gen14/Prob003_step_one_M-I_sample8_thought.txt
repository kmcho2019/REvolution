The original solution is already minimal and optimal for generating a constant logic high output. However, to possibly improve power and area by guiding synthesis tools more explicitly, we can use a localparam constant and assign it to the output. This might help the synthesis tool better recognize the constant and map it to a tie-high cell, potentially reducing power further. While the improvement impact may be marginal or synthesis-tool-dependent, this is a common practice to ensure constant propagation and optimization.
