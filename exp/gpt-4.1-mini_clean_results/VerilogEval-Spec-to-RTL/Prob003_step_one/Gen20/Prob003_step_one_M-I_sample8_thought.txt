The original implementation is already minimal and optimal for the given specification. Since the output is a constant logic high, the continuous assignment is the simplest and most power/area-efficient method. There are no clocks or complex logic, so performance is optimal by design. For further minor power improvements, one could declare the output as a wire connected directly to a constant 1'b1 without involving registers or additional logic. This remains effectively the same but slightly emphasizes the direct connection to a constant net. No structural changes are possible or beneficial at the RTL level for this trivial constant output.
