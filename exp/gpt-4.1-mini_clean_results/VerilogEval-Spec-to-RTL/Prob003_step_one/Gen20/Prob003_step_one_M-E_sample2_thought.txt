Instead of a simple continuous assignment, we implement the output as a registered signal driven by a flip-flop that is asynchronously preset to logic high. This guarantees the output is always '1' after reset or power-up without any combinational logic driving it. Although this approach uses sequential logic, it can be useful in designs where a clocked constant output is needed or to better integrate with synchronous design styles. The asynchronous preset ensures the output is driven to '1' immediately, preserving the functional requirement while providing a different architectural approach.
