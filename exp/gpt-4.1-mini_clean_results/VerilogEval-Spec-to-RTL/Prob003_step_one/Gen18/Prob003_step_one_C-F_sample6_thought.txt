Fuse the minimalism and directness of Example 1 with the configurability concept of Example 2 by declaring a localparam inside TopModule to represent the constant logic high value, then drive the output with a continuous assignment to that localparam. This keeps the design purely combinational and minimal, while enabling easy future modification of the constant value within the module itself, without extra module instantiation. This balances simplicity, area/power efficiency, and potential extensibility.
