The original solution is already optimal: assigning the constant 1 directly to the output is minimal in logic, area, and power. No clock or combinational logic exists, so performance cannot be improved. To potentially reduce power even further and avoid any glitches, we can declare the output as a wire and directly tie it to the constant '1'b1' in the port declaration using Verilog-2001 shorthand. This slight syntax refinement may allow the synthesizer to recognize the constant tie more explicitly, although practical PPA improvements would be negligible.
