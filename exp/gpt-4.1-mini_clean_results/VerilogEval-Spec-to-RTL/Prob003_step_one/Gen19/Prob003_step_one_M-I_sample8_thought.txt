The original design is already optimal in terms of logic simplicity and power because it drives a constant logic high on the output without any unnecessary logic or flip-flops. One small improvement to potentially reduce power and area is to explicitly declare the output as a wire driven by a localparam constant, which might help synthesis tools recognize it as a tie-high constant net and optimize it efficiently. Using a localparam constant for the logic '1' can sometimes improve tool inference for constant nets. Other than this minor style change, no meaningful PPA improvement is possible in RTL.
