The original solution is already minimal and optimal for this simple functionality, as it continuously drives the output to a constant logic high without any registers or unnecessary logic. The synthesis tool will most likely optimize it into a direct tie to VDD or a hardwired logic '1'. Any further "optimization" in Verilog code is unnecessary and won't improve power, performance, or area. Hence, we retain the same implementation, which is the best practice for driving a constant '1'.
