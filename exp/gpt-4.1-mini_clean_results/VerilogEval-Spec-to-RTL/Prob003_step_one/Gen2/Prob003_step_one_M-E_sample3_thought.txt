A different approach is to use a parameterized constant and an internal wire driven by a parameterized constant value. The module declares a parameter DEFAULT_VALUE set to 1 by default and then drives the output through a wire assigned to this parameter. This adds configurability and shows a different coding style by involving parameters and internal nets instead of a direct constant assignment on the output port.

This design is still minimal and synthesizes to a constant logic high, but illustrates how parameters and internal wiring can be used even for simple constant outputs.
