The original solution is already optimal in terms of design simplicity and clarity. However, to hint synthesis tools more explicitly that the output is a constant tied to logic high, we can use the Verilog keyword `wire` and a local parameter with a constant 1 value for clarity. This can sometimes help synthesis tools map the constant to dedicated tie-high cells or power rails. Alternatively, directly assigning a constant literal is the most straightforward and widely accepted practice. Hence, the original solution remains the best practice with minimal area and power impact.
