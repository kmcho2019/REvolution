Since the original solution already drives a constant logic '1' using a continuous assignment, it is optimal for performance, area, and power. However, to better assist synthesis tools in inferring a constant tie-high, explicitly declaring the output as a wire tied to a parameter constant or using a localparam constant may help. Alternatively, marking the output as a constant net with a 'localparam' inside may assist some tools. But practically, the original simple assignment is ideal and requires no code change.

Hence, no code-level optimization will meaningfully improve PPA metrics beyond what synthesis tools can achieve internally. The design is minimal and optimal.
