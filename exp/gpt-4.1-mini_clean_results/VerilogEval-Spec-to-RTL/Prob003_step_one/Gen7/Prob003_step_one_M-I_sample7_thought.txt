The original solution is already minimal and optimal in terms of logic, area, and power, as it directly ties the output to a constant '1'. One slight improvement for power and area is to use a parameter constant or a localparam instead of a hardwired literal, which can help synthesis tools recognize the constant tie more explicitly and potentially map it to an optimal tie-high cell. This approach may also improve readability and allow easier modifications. Performance metrics are not relevant here since there is no timing path. Thus, the improvement strategy is to define a localparam for the constant high and assign the output from that localparam.
