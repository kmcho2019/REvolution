The original solution is already optimal for this trivial requirement: a constant logic high output. The output is directly tied to a constant 1, so synthesis tools will implement it as a hardwired logic high or tie. No clock or logic is involved, so no timing or power improvements are achievable. To emphasize constant driving, an explicit wire can be declared and assigned, but this is purely stylistic and not a true optimization. Hence, maintain the original approach for minimal area, power, and zero timing.
