The initial solution is already minimal and optimal, driving a constant logic 1 directly. Since the output is a constant tied to logic high, the synthesis tool will infer this as a tie to VDD with minimal or no logic. There are no clocks or combinational logic to optimize further.

One minor improvement is to declare the output as a 'wire' explicitly to emphasize it is continuously driven combinationally. This is more stylistically clear and can help synthesis understand the intent, but functionally it is the same.

Also, adding a parameterized constant could improve reusability, but since the spec demands a constant '1', it is not required.

Thus, the best improvement here is to explicitly declare the output as wire and keep the assign statement as is.

This keeps the design as simple and PPA-friendly as possible.
