The original solution is already minimal and optimal for driving a constant logic high output. Since the output is a constant tied to 1, synthesis tools typically implement it as a direct connection to the power rail, resulting in minimal area and power. To potentially improve power and area slightly, we can declare the output as a wire and use a continuous assignment as before. Additionally, we can add a synthesis directive to hint to the tool that this is a constant tie, though such directives are tool-specific and optional. Overall, the current design is ideal, and no significant PPA improvement is feasible without changing the design intent.
