Fuse the simplicity and minimal resource usage of direct constant assignment with the extensibility of a parameterized module by defining a small internal parameter constant within TopModule itself and directly assigning it to the output. This approach keeps the design minimal—no extra module instantiation—while preserving flexibility for future parameterization inside the module without external dependencies. It also provides a clear, maintainable constant definition and ensures the output is driven by a parameterized constant, combining the clarity of parametrization with optimal power, area, and performance.
