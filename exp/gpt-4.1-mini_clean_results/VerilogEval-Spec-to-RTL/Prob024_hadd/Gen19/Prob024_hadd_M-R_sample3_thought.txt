Refactor the half adder to use continuous assignments instead of an always block. This approach uses wires for the outputs and direct assign statements for sum and carry-out, which better represents pure combinational logic. This style avoids the use of reg types and procedural blocks, leading to cleaner RTL, potentially better synthesis results, and more straightforward hardware mapping with minimal area and power overhead.
