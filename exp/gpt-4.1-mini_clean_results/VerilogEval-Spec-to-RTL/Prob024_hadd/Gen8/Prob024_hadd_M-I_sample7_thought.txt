The half adder implementation with XOR and AND gates is already minimal and optimal in terms of delay, power, and area for this functionality. The original design uses continuous assignments with simple gates, which is best practice for synthesizable and efficient Verilog RTL. Given the reported metrics (zero delay, minimal power, minimal area), there is no logic simplification or restructuring to improve PPA at the RTL level.

However, for code clarity and synthesis friendliness, I will explicitly declare output ports as wires, keep continuous assignments, and add brief comments to improve readability and maintainability without affecting PPA. This slight improvement in code style ensures clarity and matches synthesis expectations without altering functionality or performance.
