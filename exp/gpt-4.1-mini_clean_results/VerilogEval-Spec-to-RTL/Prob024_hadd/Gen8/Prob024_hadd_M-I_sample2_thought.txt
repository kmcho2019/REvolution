The original design is already minimal and optimal at the gate level for a half adder. However, the use of internal wires and explicit primitive gate instantiations adds some slight overhead in terms of code length and possibly synthesis complexity. To potentially improve synthesis power and area, I will simplify the implementation by directly using continuous assignment with built-in operators (xor and and) rather than gate primitives and intermediate wires. This structural simplification reduces the number of nets and instantiations, possibly aiding synthesis optimization tools in optimizing the logic better and slightly improving power and area. The functional behavior remains exactly the same.
