Refactor the half adder implementation by removing the explicit instantiation of XORGate and ANDGate modules and instead using continuous assign statements directly inside the TopModule. This simplifies the design by reducing module hierarchy and instance overhead, improves readability, and likely leads to better synthesis results in terms of area and power. The functionality remains exactly the same, producing the sum as XOR of inputs and carry-out as AND of inputs, but the design is more compact and idiomatic.
