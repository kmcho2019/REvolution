Fuse the clarity and explicitness of continuous assignment with the minimalism and straightforward gate-level style by using continuous assignments with explicitly declared wire outputs. This retains the minimal logic footprint of the half adder (XOR and AND), provides clear, readable code with good synthesis friendliness, and remains optimal in performance, power, and area. Explicitly declaring output ports as wires and using assign statements is stylistically clearer for larger designs while maintaining minimal logic from the gate-level approach.
