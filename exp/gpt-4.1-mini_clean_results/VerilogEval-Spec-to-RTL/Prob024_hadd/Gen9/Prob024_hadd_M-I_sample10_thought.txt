The original design is already optimal for a half adder with minimal logic and no unnecessary signals. The XOR and AND gates are the minimal required gates for sum and carry outputs. Since it's purely combinational, further RTL optimizations cannot reduce gate count or power significantly. However, to potentially improve power consumption slightly, we can replace the continuous assignment with explicit instantiation of primitive gates, which some synthesis tools might optimize better. Also, adding `wire` declarations explicitly can make code style clearer, though it doesn't affect synthesis results. Overall, the original design is minimal, and any improvements would be marginal and mostly stylistic.
