The original half adder implementation is already minimal and optimal in terms of logic and power for a simple combinational circuit. There is no redundant or complex logic to simplify. Since sum and cout are single-bit combinational outputs derived from a and b, using continuous assignments to XOR and AND is the best practice. 

To slightly improve code clarity and synthesis friendliness, I will explicitly declare sum and cout as wires, and structure the module cleanly. However, there is no logical or PPA optimization possible at the RTL level for such a minimal design. 

Hence, the improvement is stylistic and for good coding practice rather than performance or area.
