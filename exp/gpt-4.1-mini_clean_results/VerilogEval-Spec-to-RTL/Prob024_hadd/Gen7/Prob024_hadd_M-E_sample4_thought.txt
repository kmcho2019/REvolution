Implement the half adder by instantiating primitive logic gates explicitly rather than using continuous assignment. This approach uses gate-level instantiations (xor and and gates) which illustrates the hardware structure more directly. It is equally optimal in performance and area but provides a structural view, which can aid understanding in educational or low-level design contexts. The ports remain one bit and the functionality remains the same, but the sum and cout signals are driven from instantiated gates rather than assign statements.
