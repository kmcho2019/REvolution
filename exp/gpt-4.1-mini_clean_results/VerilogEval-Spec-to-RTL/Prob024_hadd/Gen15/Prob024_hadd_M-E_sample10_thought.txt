Instead of using direct XOR and AND gates, implement the half adder using only NAND gates to demonstrate an alternative gate-level construction. NAND gates are universal, and this approach highlights how to build sum and carry out from NAND primitives. This also shows how the half adder can be expressed purely with NAND gates, which can be useful in some standard cell libraries or teaching contexts.

Specifically:
- sum = a XOR b = NAND(NAND(a, NAND(a,b)), NAND(b, NAND(a,b)))
- cout = a AND b = NAND(NAND(a,b), NAND(a,b))

This design will be purely combinational but based on NAND decomposition rather than direct operators.
