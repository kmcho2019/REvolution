To optimize power and area slightly, eliminate the intermediate 32-bit wire by directly assigning each 8-bit output from the concatenation expression. This removes the explicit wire declaration and associated routing resources, reducing area minimally. It also reduces signal fanout and switching since each output directly receives only the needed slice, possibly lowering power slightly. The performance remains unaffected as the assignments are still purely combinational. This approach simplifies the logic and can aid synthesis tools in optimizing the routing and placement further.
