Instead of concatenating all inputs first, assign each output by directly concatenating specific input portions and the appended bits in a staggered manner to form the 8-bit outputs. For example, form output w by taking inputs a and upper bits of b; output x by taking the remaining bits of b plus c; output y by c and d; output z by e, f, and finally append the two constant bits in the least significant bits of z. This distributes the appended bits uniquely and demonstrates an alternative way to map inputs to outputs without using an intermediate combined wire. It keeps the outputs fully combinational and the functionality equivalent.
