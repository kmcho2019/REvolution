The original design correctly uses a helper module to concatenate groups of three inputs, but this adds unnecessary module instantiations and intermediate wiring. Since the operation is purely combinational concatenation, flattening the logic by directly concatenating all six inputs and the two appended bits into one 32-bit vector will reduce area and power slightly with no impact on performance. This also simplifies the design by removing the helper module and extra wires, improving readability and maintainability.
