Instead of concatenating inputs directly or using a helper module, this design uses an 8-bit shift register approach implemented combinationally to load all 30 input bits sequentially into a 32-bit register with the two LSBs forced to 1. The inputs a to f are concatenated into a 30-bit vector, which is then shifted left by 2 bits and OR-ed with 2'b11 to produce the final 32-bit output vector. This 32-bit vector is then sliced into four 8-bit outputs. This approach conceptually treats the inputs as serial data loaded into a shift register, simulating a serial-to-parallel conversion, but implemented entirely combinationally. It highlights a different way to manipulate and align input bits and appends the 2'b11 at the least significant bits through bit-shifting and bitwise OR operations.
