Fuse the clarity and explicitness of using an internal 32-bit wire for the concatenated inputs and constants with the succinctness and maintainability of a named localparam for the appended constant bits. This allows clean documentation of the constant, easy future modification, and explicit slicing of the concatenated vector to each 8-bit output port. Such modularity improves readability, debugging, and maintainability while retaining synthesis-friendly minimalism. The approach keeps logic purely combinational and straightforward, optimizing for PPA by leveraging native Verilog operations without introducing unnecessary logic or registers.
