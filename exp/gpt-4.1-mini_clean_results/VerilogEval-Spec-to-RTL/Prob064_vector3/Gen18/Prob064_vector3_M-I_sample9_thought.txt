The original design uses an extra helper module (MultiConcat) that simply passes through the concatenated inputs, adding unnecessary module overhead and routing complexity without functional benefit. To optimize for area and power, we can remove this module and perform all concatenations directly inside TopModule. This reduces hierarchy, simplifies the design, and may improve routing and synthesis quality. The logic remains purely combinational, so timing will not degrade. The constant bits are still appended cleanly. Overall, this approach yields a smaller, simpler, and more efficient design without sacrificing readability.
