To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will inline the concatenation directly inside TopModule and remove the separate TripleConcat module. This reduces module instantiation overhead, slightly lowers wiring complexity, and eliminates a hierarchical boundary, which may marginally improve timing and area metrics. The two appended '1' bits will remain as a localparam constant for clarity and maintainability. Outputs will still be assigned as slices of the 32-bit concatenated vector. This approach simplifies the design while preserving readability and functionality.
