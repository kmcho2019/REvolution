Instead of concatenating all input vectors first and then splitting, form each 8-bit output independently by carefully selecting and combining appropriate bits from the inputs and the trailing two '1' bits. The last two bits '11' will be appended specifically in the lowest bits of the last output vector to complete the 32 bits. This approach eliminates the need for an intermediate 32-bit vector wire and distributes the concatenation logic directly into the outputs. This can also simplify routing and possibly improve timing by creating direct expressions for outputs rather than slicing a wide wire.
