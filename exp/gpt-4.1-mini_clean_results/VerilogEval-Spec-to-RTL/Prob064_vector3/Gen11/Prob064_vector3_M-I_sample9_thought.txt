The current solution is already minimal, purely combinational, and uses direct concatenation and slicing, which is optimal for PPA in such a straightforward task. However, to further reduce power and area marginally, we can remove the intermediate wire and directly assign the concatenation expression to the outputs. This eliminates one wire declaration, simplifying the netlist slightly and may allow synthesis tools to optimize routing better. The logic remains the same with two appended LSB bits set to '1'. This tweak keeps the design compact and clean.
