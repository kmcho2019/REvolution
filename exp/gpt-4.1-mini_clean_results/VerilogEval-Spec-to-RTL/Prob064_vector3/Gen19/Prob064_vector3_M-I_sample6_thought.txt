The current design is functionally correct and very clean but can be further optimized for power and area by removing the intermediate MultiConcat module and the intermediate 30-bit wire. Instead, I will directly concatenate the six 5-bit inputs and the two constant '1' bits in a single step inside TopModule. This flattening reduces hierarchy, eliminates an intermediate wire, and reduces net capacitance and routing complexity. This should marginally improve power and area without impacting performance, as all operations are simple concatenations and assignments. The code also becomes simpler and easier to maintain.
