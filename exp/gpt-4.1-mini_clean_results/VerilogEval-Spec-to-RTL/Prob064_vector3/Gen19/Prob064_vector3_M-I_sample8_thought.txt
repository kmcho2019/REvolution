The current solution is functionally correct and well-structured, but it introduces an intermediate module and wire (MultiConcat and concatenated_inputs) which add hierarchy and nets that slightly increase area and power. To optimize for better PPA, I will flatten the design by removing the MultiConcat module and the intermediate wire, directly concatenating the six 5-bit inputs and the two '1' bits into a single 32-bit vector within TopModule. This reduces hierarchy and intermediate nets, thereby improving area and power slightly. The outputs will be sliced from this directly concatenated vector. The logic remains purely combinational, so performance is not adversely affected.
