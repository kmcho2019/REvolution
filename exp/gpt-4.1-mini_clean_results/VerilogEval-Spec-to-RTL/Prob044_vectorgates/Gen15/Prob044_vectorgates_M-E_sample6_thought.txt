Instead of performing the bitwise NOT operations directly in a single continuous assignment, this design uses separate always_comb blocks to explicitly compute the inverted vectors a and b, storing them in intermediate registers. This approach highlights a sequential-style description of combinational logic, which can aid in certain verification flows or synthesis tools favoring procedural assignments. The logical OR output is computed directly using a reduction OR of the concatenated inputs, avoiding intermediate signals. The final output vector out_not is formed by concatenating the registered inverted b and a. This style demonstrates a different structural approach using always blocks and registers while achieving the same functionality.
