To optimize the original solution for better power and area while maintaining clean and readable code, I will remove the BitwiseNOT module and directly assign the bitwise inverses of inputs a and b within the TopModule. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and slightly reduces logic and wiring complexity.

Additionally, I will retain the approach of computing a single wire for the bitwise OR result (a | b) and use it for both the bitwise-OR output and the logical OR reduction. This avoids redundant logic and keeps the design efficient.

The concatenation for out_not remains the same, placing the inverted b bits in the upper half [5:3] and inverted a bits in the lower half [2:0].

This strategy balances simplicity, readability, and optimized PPA by reducing instance count and gate count while preserving all original functionality.
