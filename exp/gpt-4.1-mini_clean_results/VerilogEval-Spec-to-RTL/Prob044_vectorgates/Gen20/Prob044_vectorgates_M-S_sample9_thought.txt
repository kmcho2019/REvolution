I will simplify the design by removing the separate BitwiseNOT module and performing all bitwise inversions inline with continuous assignments. This eliminates unnecessary module hierarchy and intermediate wires for the inverted inputs, reducing area and power. I will keep a single wire for the bitwise OR result to reuse for both outputs, maintaining clarity. The concatenation of the inverted vectors for out_not will be done inline as well, directly using ~b and ~a in the concatenation. This keeps the implementation minimal, straightforward, and fully functional.
