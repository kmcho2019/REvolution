The original design is functionally correct and modular but uses separate BitwiseNOT modules to invert inputs a and b. This adds hierarchy overhead, increases area, and can lead to extra toggling and power consumption. To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, area), I will inline the bitwise NOT operations directly into continuous assignments, removing the module instantiations. This reduces gate count, logic duplication, and synthesis overhead while keeping code clear.

I will:
- Remove the BitwiseNOT module instantiations.
- Directly assign ~a and ~b to wires.
- Compute out_or_bitwise as a|b.
- Compute out_or_logical as the reduction OR of out_or_bitwise.
- Concatenate ~b and ~a directly for out_not as specified.

This approach keeps the code simple and efficient with minimal logic and maximal synthesizer optimization potential.
