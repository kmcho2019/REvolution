To optimize the design for better power and area without affecting functionality, I will remove the instantiations of the simple BitwiseOR and BitwiseNOT modules and replace them with direct continuous assignments inside TopModule. This eliminates module overhead and interconnect logic, reducing area and power consumption. The inversion operations on both input vectors will be done with a single concatenated expression rather than two separate module instances. Logical OR will be implemented with a reduction OR directly on the bitwise OR result. This approach maintains clarity and correctness while improving PPA.
