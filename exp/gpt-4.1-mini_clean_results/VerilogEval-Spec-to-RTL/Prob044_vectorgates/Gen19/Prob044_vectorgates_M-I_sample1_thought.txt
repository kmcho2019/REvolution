To optimize for power, area, and simplicity, inline the bitwise NOT operations directly using assign statements instead of instantiating a separate module for inversion. Since the inputs have fixed 3-bit width, parameterization and modularization for bitwise NOT are unnecessary overhead. Also, computing the bitwise OR and logical OR directly in assign statements simplifies the design and reduces hierarchy levels, which improves synthesis results and can reduce gate count and switching activity without affecting performance. This approach keeps the design clean, straightforward, and efficient in terms of power, area, and timing.
