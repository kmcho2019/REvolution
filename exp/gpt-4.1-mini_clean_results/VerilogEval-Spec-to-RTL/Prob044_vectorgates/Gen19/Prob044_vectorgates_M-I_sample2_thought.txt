To optimize the design for better power, area, and simplicity, remove the submodule instantiations for the simple bitwise NOT and OR operations. Instead, implement them directly inside TopModule using continuous assignments. This avoids module overhead and redundant logic, reducing gate count and interconnect. The logical OR is computed using a reduction OR on the bitwise OR result. Concatenating the inverted b and a inputs inline minimizes duplication. This retains functional correctness while improving PPA.
