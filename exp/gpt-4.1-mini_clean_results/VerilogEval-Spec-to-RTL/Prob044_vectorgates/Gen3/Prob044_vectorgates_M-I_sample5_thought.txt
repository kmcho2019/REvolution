To optimize the existing design for better performance and power without changing functionality, we can reduce logic redundancy by reusing the bitwise OR result for both out_or_bitwise and out_or_logical. Instead of performing the OR reduction on the combined signals separately, we use the already assigned out_or_bitwise vector for the logical OR reduction. This reuses existing logic results, potentially reducing power and area slightly. Also, continuous assignments are kept as they are the most efficient way for combinational logic.

No registers or intermediate wires are added to keep area and timing optimal. The improvement mainly lies in clarity and avoiding unnecessary recomputation.
