To improve the original modular design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will inline all simple bitwise operations directly inside TopModule rather than instantiating separate small modules for bitwise OR and bitwise NOT. This will reduce module overhead, eliminate interconnect logic between submodules, and reduce total gate count. Since the logic is purely combinational and straightforward (bitwise OR, bitwise NOT, and logical OR reduction), direct assignments are more efficient. Concatenating the inverted vectors is maintained as specified. This approach preserves functionality while minimizing area and dynamic power.
