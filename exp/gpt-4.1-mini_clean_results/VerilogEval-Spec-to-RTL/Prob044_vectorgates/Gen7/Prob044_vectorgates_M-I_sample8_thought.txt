To optimize the design for power and area while maintaining performance, we can compute the bitwise OR of inputs `a` and `b` once, store it in an internal wire, and reuse this for both `out_or_bitwise` and `out_or_logical`. This avoids redundant computations of `a|b`, reducing logic duplication and switching activity. Other signals are simple inversions and concatenations which are already minimal. This small refactor improves power slightly and reduces area with no effect on timing.
