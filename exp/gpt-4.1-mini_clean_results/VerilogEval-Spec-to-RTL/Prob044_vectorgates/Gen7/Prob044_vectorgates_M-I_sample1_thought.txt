To optimize for power and area without sacrificing readability or functionality, replace the instantiated simple modules with direct continuous assignments inside TopModule. This removes module overhead and duplication of bitwise NOT logic by directly computing the outputs in a single step. The bitwise OR and NOT operations are trivial and efficient as continuous assignments, and the logical OR can be derived from the bitwise OR vector using a reduction operator. This reduces gate count and interconnect, improving the overall PPA while preserving the original design intent and clarity.
