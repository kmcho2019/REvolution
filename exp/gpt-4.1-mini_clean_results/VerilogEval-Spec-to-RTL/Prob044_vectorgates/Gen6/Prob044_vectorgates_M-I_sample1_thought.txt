The original design is already minimal and efficient for a simple combinational module performing bitwise OR, logical OR, and NOT operations on small 3-bit vectors. 

To potentially optimize for PPA (performance, power, area) further, I will:
- Eliminate the intermediate wire `or_result` to reduce net count and potentially minimize routing resources and area.
- Compute `out_or_bitwise` and `out_or_logical` directly from inputs `a` and `b`, trusting the synthesis tool to efficiently share logic.
- Use continuous assignments only, no registers, preserving zero logic depth beyond the inherent gates.
- This minor code simplification reduces netlist size and may slightly reduce area and power without affecting timing.

Given the very small scale of the logic, these changes are subtle but can slightly improve area and power metrics.
