Leverage the built-in bitwise and reduction operators in Verilog directly within a purely combinational TopModule, without any submodules or intermediate wires. Compute the bitwise OR and logical OR straightforwardly from inputs a and b. For the inversion, concatenate the inversions of both inputs directly in the assignment of out_not by exploiting bit-slicing and concatenation, placing the NOT of b in the upper half and a in the lower half in one combined expression. This approach minimizes hierarchy and wiring overhead, improves readability, and likely reduces area and power while maintaining clarity and simplicity.
