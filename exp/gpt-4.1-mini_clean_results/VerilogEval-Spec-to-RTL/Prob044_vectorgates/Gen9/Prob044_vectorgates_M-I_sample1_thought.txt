The original solution correctly implements the required functionality using separate submodules for bitwise OR and bitwise NOT operations. To improve power and area (PPA) while maintaining correctness and readability, I will inline all bitwise operations directly inside TopModule, eliminating the overhead of module instantiations. This removes redundant module logic for simple bitwise operations, reduces interconnect complexity, and should marginally lower power and area without impacting performance. The logic remains purely combinational and simple.
