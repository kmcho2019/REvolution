To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area, I will:

- Remove the intermediate wire `or_result` and directly use `a | b` in both `out_or_bitwise` and `out_or_logical`. This shares the OR computation and prevents duplicating OR gates.
- Compute `out_or_logical` directly as the reduction OR of `(a | b)` to avoid an extra wire.
- Keep the concatenation for `out_not` as `{~b, ~a}`, which is already optimal.
- This approach reduces gate count by sharing combinational logic, decreases switching activity marginally, and minimizes net count, thus improving overall PPA without changing functionality.
