To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will use vector-level operations instead of explicit per-bit assignments for the bitwise OR and bitwise NOT computations. This approach is more concise, allows better optimization by synthesis tools, and reduces code verbosity, which benefits power and area. For the logical OR output, I will use a single reduction OR over the concatenated input vectors `{a, b}`, simplifying the logic and improving performance and power efficiency. I will implement this using continuous assignments instead of an always block, since all outputs are purely combinational, avoiding unnecessary registers and further optimizing area and timing. The inversion of inputs will be done by bitwise NOT and concatenation, placing `~b` in the upper half and `~a` in the lower half as specified.
