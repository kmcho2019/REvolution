The original design is already minimal and efficient for a small combinational block with bitwise and logical operations. However, we can slightly optimize for power and area by:
- Using a single reduction OR on the concatenation of a and b for out_or_logical to simplify the logic.
- Explicitly declare outputs as wires (not necessary but good practice).
- Use continuous assignments as in the original.

This reduces the logical OR operation to a single reduction instead of two reductions and an OR, potentially marginally reducing logic. Overall, improvements will be subtle due to the simple nature of the design.
