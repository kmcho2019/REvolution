Instead of directly performing bitwise OR and logical OR on inputs a and b, this design separates the tasks into smaller blocks. First, it computes the bitwise OR for each bit position individually using three separate OR gates. For the logical OR output, it first reduces each input vector with an OR-reduction gate, then ORs these two results with an additional OR gate. Finally, it constructs the out_not output by individually inverting each bit of both inputs and placing the inverted bits of b in the upper half and a in the lower half explicitly with bit indexing. This approach emphasizes gate-level clarity and explicit bit operations.
