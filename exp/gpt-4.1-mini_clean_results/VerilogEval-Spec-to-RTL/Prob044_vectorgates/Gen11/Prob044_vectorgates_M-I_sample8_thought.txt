The original design is already minimal and efficient for such simple operations. However, to optimize power slightly, I will reduce redundant operations by sharing the bitwise OR result between the outputs instead of computing `a|b` twice. This saves logic and dynamic switching for the logical OR output. For area and timing, this reduces gate count and logic depth, since the logical OR becomes a reduction of the previously computed bitwise OR instead of recomputing the OR operation again. The bitwise NOT concatenation is already optimal. Overall, this minor change improves power and area by removing a redundant OR gate while maintaining the same functionality.
