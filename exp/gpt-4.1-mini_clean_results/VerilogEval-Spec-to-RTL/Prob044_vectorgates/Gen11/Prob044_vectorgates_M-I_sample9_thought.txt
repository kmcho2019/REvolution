The original design is already very efficient in terms of area and power, with minimal combinational logic. However, a small optimization can be made by calculating the bitwise OR only once and reusing it for the logical OR reduction, avoiding a redundant bitwise OR. Additionally, using the reduction OR operator directly on inputs 'a' and 'b' for the logical OR output yields the same result more directly and reduces a small amount of logic. This reduces logic gates used for the logical OR output. The bitwise NOT concatenation remains unchanged as it is already minimal and clear. This small change slightly improves area and power with negligible impact on performance.
