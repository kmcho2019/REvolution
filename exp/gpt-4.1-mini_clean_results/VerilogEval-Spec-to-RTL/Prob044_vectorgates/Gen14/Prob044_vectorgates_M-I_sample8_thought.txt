To optimize power, area, and synthesis efficiency (PPA), I will simplify the original design by removing the separate BitwiseNOT module and directly using inline bitwise negations. This reduces hierarchy, allows the synthesizer to optimize better, and cuts down on extra module instantiations and wiring. I will also eliminate the intermediate wires for not_a and not_b, performing the inversions directly in the final concatenation. The bitwise OR output and logical OR reduction will remain separate for clarity and to avoid recomputation. This approach maintains full functionality and readability while improving PPA.
