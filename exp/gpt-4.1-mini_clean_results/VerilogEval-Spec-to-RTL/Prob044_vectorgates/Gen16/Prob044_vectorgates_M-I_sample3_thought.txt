To optimize the design for better power and area (PPA) without affecting functionality or timing, I will remove the BitwiseNOT module instances and replace them with direct bitwise negation assignments inside the TopModule. This eliminates the overhead of module instantiation and potentially reduces gate count and switching activity. I will keep the code simple and fully combinational, using continuous assignments for all outputs. The logical OR output remains a reduction operator on the precomputed bitwise OR vector, ensuring minimal logic duplication.
