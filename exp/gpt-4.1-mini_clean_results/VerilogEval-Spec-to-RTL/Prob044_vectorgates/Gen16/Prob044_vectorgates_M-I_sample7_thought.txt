To optimize the original design for better power and area (PPA), I will inline the bitwise NOT operations directly in the top module instead of instantiating the BitwiseNOT module twice. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and redundant logic, reducing gate count and switching activity slightly. 

Also, I will keep the clear assignment style for readability but use direct negations, which synthesis tools will handle efficiently. The bitwise OR will still be computed once and reused for both outputs to avoid redundancy. This balances clarity and optimization well.

This approach maintains the same functionality and timing (all combinational) while reducing area and power, as the metrics suggested that overhead from multiple instances can be trimmed without complexity loss.
