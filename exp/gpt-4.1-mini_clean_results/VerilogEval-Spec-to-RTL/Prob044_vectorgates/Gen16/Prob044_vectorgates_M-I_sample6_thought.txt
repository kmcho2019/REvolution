To optimize for power, area, and simplicity while preserving functionality, I will eliminate the parameterized BitwiseNOT module and replace it with direct inline bitwise negations. This reduces module hierarchy overhead and allows the synthesis tool to optimize better.

I will also remove unnecessary intermediate wires for the inverted vectors and assign directly to the concatenation output. The bitwise OR intermediate wire is small and used twice (for bitwise and logical OR), so I will keep it for clarity and to avoid duplicating OR logic.

Overall, the improvements:
- Inline bitwise negation instead of separate modules to reduce area and power.
- Maintain the single OR wire for clarity and reuse.
- Keep code minimal and clean.

This should reduce logic duplication, module instantiations, and switching activity, thereby improving power and area metrics without affecting timing.
