Analyzing the waveform, q is zero only when b=0 and c=0; in all other cases, q=1. Equivalently, q can be expressed as NOT(~b & ~c), which is a NOR gate inverted: q = ~(~b & ~c) = b | c.

To implement this differently from the direct OR gate, I will use De Morgan's law and NAND gates instead, building q as a NAND of inverted b and c, i.e. q = ~(~b & ~c). This uses basic gates differently and illustrates logic reuse without relying on OR directly.

Also, I will implement it with an always_comb block and intermediate signals to show a different style of combinational design.
