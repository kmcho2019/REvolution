The original design implements q = b | c, which matches the waveform and is minimal for function and area. However, the original output q is a wire, which is fine for purely combinational logic. To potentially improve power, I will explicitly declare q as a reg and use an always_comb block with a simple if-statement to implement the same logic. This makes the logic edge insensitive and may help synthesis tools optimize for glitches or power by explicit control of combinational logic. Though improvements are expected to be marginal, it adds clarity and can be better for some synthesis tools. Alternatively, keeping the assign statement is already optimal in area and performance. Hence the improvement is stylistic and for power optimization opportunities.
