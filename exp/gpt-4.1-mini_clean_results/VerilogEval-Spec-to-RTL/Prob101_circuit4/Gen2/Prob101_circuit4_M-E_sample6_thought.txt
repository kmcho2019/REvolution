Analyzing the waveform, the output q is 1 whenever either b or c is 1, regardless of a or d. To implement the same behavior differently, I will express q as the negation of the NAND of b and c inputs: q = ~(~(b & c)) = b | c (De Morgan's law). This uses a NAND gate and an inverter structure, providing a different architectural implementation from a direct OR gate.

This architectural choice demonstrates functional equivalence but uses different primitive gates, which might help in certain technologies or optimizations, and provides design diversity.

The module will remain purely combinational, with clear comments explaining the logic transformation.
