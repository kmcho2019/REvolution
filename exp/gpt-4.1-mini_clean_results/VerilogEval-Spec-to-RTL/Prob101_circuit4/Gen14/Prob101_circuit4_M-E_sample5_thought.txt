The waveform indicates q = 1 when either b=1 or c=1, irrespective of a and d, except for cases when both b=0 and c=0, where q=0. To implement this combinational logic differently, use a multiplexer (MUX) controlled by a OR gate of b and c. The OR gate's output acts as the selector for the MUX. The MUX outputs 1 if the selector is 1, else 0. This approach explicitly uses a MUX module with the OR gate output controlling it, introducing a different structural architecture that still achieves the same function q = b | c.
