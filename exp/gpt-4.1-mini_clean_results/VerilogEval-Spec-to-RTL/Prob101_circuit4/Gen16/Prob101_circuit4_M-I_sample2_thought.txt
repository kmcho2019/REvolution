The original design correctly implements q = b | c using a 4-to-1 MUX, which adds unnecessary logic depth, area, and power. To optimize for performance, power, and area, I will remove the MUX and implement q directly as a combinational OR of b and c. Inputs a and d remain unused as per the waveform and spec. This simplification reduces propagation delay, power consumption, and area while preserving the exact functional behavior.
