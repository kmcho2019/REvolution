Observing the waveform, q is high if either b=1 or c=1, regardless of a and d. We can implement this combinational logic using De Morgan’s law by first computing the NAND of b and c, then NAND the result with itself (double NAND to get OR), thus q = b | c implemented purely with NAND gates. This uses only NAND gates structurally and no OR gates or multiplexers, offering an alternative gate-level architecture.
