The previous solution uses an instantiated OrGate2 module to realize the logic q = b | c, which is functionally correct but adds unnecessary hierarchy and area overhead. To optimize power and area while maintaining zero delay, I will simplify the design by implementing q as a direct continuous assignment of the OR operation on b and c. This reduces module instantiations, wiring overhead, and improves power by minimizing unnecessary gate levels. Inputs a and d remain unused as per waveform analysis.
