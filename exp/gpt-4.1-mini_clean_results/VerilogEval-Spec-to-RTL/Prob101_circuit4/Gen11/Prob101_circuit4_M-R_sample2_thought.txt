The core functionality is that output q should be the OR of inputs b and c, as derived from the waveform. To refactor the design while preserving the same combinational behavior, I will remove the submodule instantiation and implement the logic directly using an inline continuous assignment in TopModule.

This approach simplifies the hierarchy, reduces gate and wiring overhead, and keeps the code concise and clear. The inputs a and d remain unused as observed from the waveform and the OR function of b and c defines the output.

No use of always blocks is needed since this is pure combinational logic, and direct assign statements are the most straightforward and synthesis-friendly approach for such simple logic.

This refactoring enhances area and power efficiency slightly by eliminating an additional module and improving code simplicity without changing functional behavior.
