From the waveform, q is 1 whenever c=1 or b=1, except when a=0 and b=0 and c=0 (q=0). When a=1, q is 1 if either b=1 or c=1. When a=0, q is 1 if c=1 or b=1. The only time q=0 is when a=0 and b=0 and c=0, regardless of d. So q = (b | c). That matches all cases.

d does not affect output.

Therefore, the output q is the OR of inputs b and c.
