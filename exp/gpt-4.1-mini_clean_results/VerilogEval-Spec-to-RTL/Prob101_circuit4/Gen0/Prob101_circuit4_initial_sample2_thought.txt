From the waveform, q = 1 whenever c=1 or b=1, except when a=0 and b=0 and c=0 (q=0 regardless of d). The output q is 1 if either b or c is 1, or if a=1 and c=1 or b=1. Actually, the simplest is q = b OR c. Checking the waveforms confirms q=1 when b=1 or c=1, regardless of a and d. When both b and c are zero, q=0, regardless of a and d. Hence, q = b | c.
