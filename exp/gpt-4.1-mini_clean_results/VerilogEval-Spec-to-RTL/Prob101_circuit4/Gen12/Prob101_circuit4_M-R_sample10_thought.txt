The original design uses structural modeling with explicit Buffer and OrGate2 modules. To refactor, I will implement the same combinational logic functionally with a single continuous assignment statement inside the TopModule. This removes intermediate wires and modules, simplifying the design.

This approach uses a direct assign statement for q = b | c, improving readability and reducing area and power by eliminating redundant buffers and extra gates.

Thus, the functionality remains the same, but the implementation is purely behavioral and concise.
