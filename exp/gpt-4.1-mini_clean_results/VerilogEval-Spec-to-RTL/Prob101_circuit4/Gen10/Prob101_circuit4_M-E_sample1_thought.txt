From the waveform, q is 1 whenever either b or c is high, independent of a and d. To implement the logic differently from a simple OR gate, I will use De Morgan's theorem and implement q as the negation of the AND of the negations of b and c. In other words, q = ~(~b & ~c). This approach uses basic gates (inverters and an AND gate) to realize the OR function structurally but differently from the previous OR instantiation. This introduces an extra level of gates, but is a common alternative realization of OR gates using NAND/AND+NOT structures.
