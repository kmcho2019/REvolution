To refactor the original modular gate-level design into a more concise and efficient combinational logic style, I will replace the structural instantiation of Buffer and OrGate2 modules with a single continuous assignment using the logical OR operator directly on inputs b and c. This approach maintains the exact functionality (q = b OR c) but simplifies the design by removing unnecessary intermediate signals and modules, thereby reducing area and power while keeping the combinational behavior straightforward. This is a typical behavioral style suitable for synthesis and improves readability and maintainability without changing the logic.
