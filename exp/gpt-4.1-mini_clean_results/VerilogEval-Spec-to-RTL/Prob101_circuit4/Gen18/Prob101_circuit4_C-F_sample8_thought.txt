Both examples correctly identify the core logic: q = b OR c, independent of a and d. Example 1 provides a dedicated OrGate2 module for modularity, and Example 2 improves clarity with clean structural instantiation but also uses the same OrGate2 module. The redundant buffering in Example 1 and the hierarchical overhead can be avoided for better PPA by flattening the hierarchy. The superior fusion is to implement q directly as a continuous assignment (assign q = b | c;) inside TopModule, removing the OrGate2 module entirely. This yields minimal hierarchy, optimal area, power, and performance, while keeping the design clear and easily understood. This direct logic approach is the simplest, most efficient solution for this trivial combinational function.
