The waveform data indicates that output q = 1 if either b=1 or c=1, regardless of a and d. This corresponds to q = b | c. The original solution uses two gate-level modules (OR2 and AND2) with the AND2 gate effectively redundant (AND with constant 1). To optimize power, area, and performance (PPA), remove the unnecessary AND2 gate and implement the logic with a single continuous assignment. This minimizes gate count and wiring, reduces toggling, and lowers delay. Inputs a and d are unused and can be left as inputs for the interface but not used in logic.
