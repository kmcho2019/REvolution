Analyzing the waveform, the output q is 1 whenever either b or c is high, regardless of a and d. To provide a different architectural approach from the earlier direct OR gate, I will implement the logic using a combinational always block with an intermediate wire for the OR of b and c, then assign q from that intermediate signal. This approach introduces a clear separation of logic computation (b or c) and output assignment, helping clarity and scalability if the design were to be extended.

Additionally, I'll use continuous assignment for the intermediate wire and an always_comb block for the output, which is somewhat unconventional for such a simple logic but demonstrates clear separation of signal flow and can be beneficial in more complex combinational designs.

This also allows explicit use of a reg type for the output q, showing a distinct method from purely structural or continuous assign approaches.

The design remains combinational and synthesizable.
