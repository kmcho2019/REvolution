The existing design uses a separate OrGate2 module instantiation for an OR operation between b and c, which is functionally correct but introduces unnecessary hierarchical overhead and wiring, increasing area and power slightly. To optimize for power and area without impacting performance, I will remove the OrGate2 module and directly implement the OR operation in TopModule using a continuous assignment statement. This simplification reduces hierarchy, decreases routing and net switching, and maintains the same functional correctness and performance.
