Fuse the simplicity and minimal area/power benefits of continuous assignment (assign) with the clarity of using a wire output type, since the logic is purely combinational. This approach avoids using reg and always blocks, reducing synthesis overhead and improving power/area slightly. It keeps the logic explicit and concise: q = b | c. Using continuous assignment on a wire output is optimal for synthesis and maintains the exact required behavior from the waveform. This fusion takes the best of both examples — minimal, clear, and synthesis-friendly coding style that synthesizes to a single OR gate.
