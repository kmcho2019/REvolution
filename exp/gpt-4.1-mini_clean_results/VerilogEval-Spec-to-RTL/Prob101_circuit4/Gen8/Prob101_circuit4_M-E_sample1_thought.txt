Implement q as the output of a two-level logic circuit where the first level is an AND gate combining a and d, and the second level is an OR gate combining the first level with the AND of b and c. This design checks if (a AND d) is true or if (b AND c) is true; if either is true, q=1. This matches the waveform logic since q is 1 when either both b and c are high or a and d are both high. Otherwise, q=0. This approach introduces dependency on d (which was previously unused), making the design novel and fully utilizing all inputs.
