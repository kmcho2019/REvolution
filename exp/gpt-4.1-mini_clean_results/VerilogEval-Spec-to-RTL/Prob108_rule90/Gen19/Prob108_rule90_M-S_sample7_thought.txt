Implement the entire 512-bit Rule 90 cellular automaton as a single always block without hierarchical blocks, using vector operations for clarity and simplicity. Treat the boundaries explicitly as zeros. Calculate the next state by XORing the shifted vectors to the left and right, inserting zeros at boundaries. Use nonblocking assignments with synchronous load and update on the positive clock edge.

This reduces complexity by removing module hierarchies and per-bit generate loops, relying on Verilog vector operations to implicitly compute neighbor XORs, which are synthesizable and compact. The solution is straightforward, easy to read, and maintains the required functionality.
