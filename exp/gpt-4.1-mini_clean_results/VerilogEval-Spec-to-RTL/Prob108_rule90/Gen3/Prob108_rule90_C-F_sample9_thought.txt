Fuse the two approaches by removing per-bit conditional logic inside the generate loop to reduce critical path delay. Precompute left and right neighbor vectors by shifting the state vector with explicit zero boundary padding, then XOR them to produce next_state. This avoids per-bit multiplexers for boundaries, yielding simpler combinational logic. The next state is registered synchronously with a load-enable. This approach merges the clarity and modularity of the generate loops with the efficiency and timing improvements from vectorized operations and simpler boundary handling.
