To simplify the design, avoid the conditional operator inside the generate loop by first extending the current state vector `q` with zero padding at both ends. This creates a 514-bit vector where the first and last bits are always zero (the boundary conditions). Then, the next state of each cell is simply the XOR of bits i and i+2 in this extended vector, corresponding to the left and right neighbors of q[i]. This approach removes the need for per-bit conditional checks and muxes, reducing complexity and improving clarity. The sequential logic remains the same, loading `data` on `load` and otherwise updating `q` with the computed next state.
