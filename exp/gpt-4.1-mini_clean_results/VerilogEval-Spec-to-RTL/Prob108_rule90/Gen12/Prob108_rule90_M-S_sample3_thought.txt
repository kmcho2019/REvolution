Simplify the design by implementing the entire 512-bit Rule 90 logic in a single always block without partitioning into blocks. Compute next state for all 512 bits at once using bitwise operations and simple boundary conditions (zero for out-of-range neighbors). Update the state on clock edge, loading data when load is high or progressing to next state otherwise. This reduces complexity, eliminates inter-block wiring, and simplifies the design at the cost of a larger combinational XOR operation on all bits.
