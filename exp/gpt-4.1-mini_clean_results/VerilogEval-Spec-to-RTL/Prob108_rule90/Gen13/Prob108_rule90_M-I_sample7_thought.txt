To improve PPA, I will implement a simple clock enable (ce) signal to prevent updating the state when the state does not change, reducing unnecessary toggles and dynamic power. This can be done by comparing the next_state with the current q_out and enabling the register update only when load is asserted or when next_state differs from q_out.

While this adds a comparison, it significantly reduces switching activity during steady states, lowering power consumption.

To reduce area and combinational complexity from multiple comparisons (64-bit per block), I will:

- Use a single OR-reduction of bitwise XOR of q_out and next_state to generate ce.
- Implement the design with 8 blocks of 64 bits each, as before.
- Maintain clean boundary handling with zero padding on the ends.
- Keep non-blocking assignments and synchronous design style.

This approach balances power savings with moderate area increase due to comparator logic and slight performance impact from ce gating.

Overall, this design should reduce power significantly, with minimal area overhead and no impact on functional correctness.
