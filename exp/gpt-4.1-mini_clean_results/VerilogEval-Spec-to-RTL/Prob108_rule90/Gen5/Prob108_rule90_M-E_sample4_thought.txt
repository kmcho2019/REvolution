Implement the entire 512-cell Rule 90 automaton as a single 512-bit shift register with combinational logic that calculates the next state in one clock cycle. To efficiently handle boundary conditions (the outside neighbors are always zero), the design concatenates a zero bit at both ends of the current state and computes the next state as the bitwise XOR of the left and right neighbors. This approach treats the entire 512-bit vector as a single large vector and leverages vectorized operations for concise logic. On load, the entire state is synchronously loaded. Otherwise, at each clock edge, the next state is computed based on XOR of neighbors with zero-padded boundaries. This avoids block partitioning and reduces inter-block wiring complexity, at the expense of one large combinational XOR operation.
