To optimize the design for better performance and power without changing functionality, I will separate the boundary bits from the main loop to remove conditional logic inside the generate block. The first and last bits will be computed explicitly with constant-zero neighbors, while the inner bits (1 to 510) use a simpler XOR of neighbors without conditions. This reduces muxing delay in the critical path. Also, I will add an enable signal that activates updates only when load is low, preventing unnecessary toggling of the flip-flops and combinational logic when the state doesn't change (load asserted or no clock). This reduces dynamic power. The q_next logic remains combinational but is only clocked into q when enabled or loaded, using a clock enable. This simple gating improves power and possibly timing. The approach maintains a fully parallel update but slightly reduces complexity at the boundaries and avoids unnecessary toggling inside flip-flops.
