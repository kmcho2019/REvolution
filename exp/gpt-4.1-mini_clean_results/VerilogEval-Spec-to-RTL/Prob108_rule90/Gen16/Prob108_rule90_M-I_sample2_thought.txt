To improve PPA while preserving correctness and clarity, I will simplify the design by removing the clock enable logic based on partial comparison. The original ce logic adds significant combinational overhead and area, and Rule 90 naturally updates every cycle, so gating the clock register updates is less beneficial.

This revision will:
- Always update state on the positive clock edge; if load is asserted, load the new data; otherwise, update to the next Rule 90 state.
- Retain the modular 64-bit block structure to keep the design clean and manageable.
- Keep the zero boundary conditions as specified.
- Remove the partial equality check and associated combinational logic, reducing area and timing complexity.
- This simplification trades a slight increase in toggle activity (compared to gating) for simpler timing paths and smaller area.
- The overall design remains fully synchronous, easy to verify, and optimized for better synthesis and timing closure.

This should yield improved timing and area metrics while maintaining the exact functional behavior.
