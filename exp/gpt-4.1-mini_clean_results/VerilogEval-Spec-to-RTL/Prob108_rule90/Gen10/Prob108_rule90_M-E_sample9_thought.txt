Implement the entire 512-bit Rule 90 automaton using a fully combinational next-state logic followed by a single 512-bit register update each clock cycle. Use vectorized bitwise operations to compute next states efficiently without bit-by-bit loops.

Specifically, since the next state of each cell is the XOR of its left and right neighbors (center cell is irrelevant), the entire next state vector can be computed as:
  
  next_state = (q << 1) ^ (q >> 1)

where the out-of-range bits (beyond 511 or below 0) are zero as per boundary conditions.

The shift operators naturally fill with zeros at boundaries in Verilog.

The load signal is synchronous and loads the input data into the register on the clock edge, else updates with computed next_state.

This approach leverages the full-width vector operation for minimal combinational logic and easy synthesis. It reduces module overhead and signal routing between blocks.

It is simpler, cleaner, and potentially faster due to one simple XOR of two shifted registers.
