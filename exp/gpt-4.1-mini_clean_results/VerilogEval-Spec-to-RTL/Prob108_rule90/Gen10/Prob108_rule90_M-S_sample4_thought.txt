Simplify the design by removing the modular 64-bit block approach and implementing the entire 512-bit Rule 90 logic in a single always block. Compute the next state for all 512 bits by directly applying the XOR of the left and right neighbors, treating boundaries as zero. The entire next state computation can be done in a combinational block or directly inside the sequential block since the XOR is simple. This reduces hierarchy and wiring complexity while preserving functionality.
