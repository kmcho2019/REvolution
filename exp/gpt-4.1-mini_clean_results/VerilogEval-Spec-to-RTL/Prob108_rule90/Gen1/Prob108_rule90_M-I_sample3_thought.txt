To improve synthesizability and timing for the 512-bit wide combinational XOR and neighbor calculation, replace the vector-wide concatenation and XOR with a generate loop that computes each next bit individually. This approach clarifies boundary handling, allows the synthesis tool to optimize local bit-wise operations more effectively, and reduces the complexity of handling large concatenated vectors. Keeping the same semantics (neighbors beyond boundary are zero), each bit's next state is computed as q[i-1] ^ q[i+1], with missing neighbors treated as zero. The sequential load and register update remain unchanged.
