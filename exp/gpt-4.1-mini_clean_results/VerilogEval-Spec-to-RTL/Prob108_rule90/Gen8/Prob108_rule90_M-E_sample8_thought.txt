Implement the entire 512-cell Rule 90 cellular automaton as a single 512-bit register updated each clock cycle. The next state of each cell is computed as the XOR of its immediate left and right neighbors, with boundary conditions fixed to zero (cells beyond the ends are always zero). This can be done by shifting the current state one bit left and one bit right, padding zeros at the boundaries, and XORing the two shifted vectors. On a load signal, the full 512-bit state is loaded directly. This approach uses simple vector shifts and XORs, allowing a fully combinational next-state logic with one big register, resulting in a compact and clean implementation without subdivision into blocks.
