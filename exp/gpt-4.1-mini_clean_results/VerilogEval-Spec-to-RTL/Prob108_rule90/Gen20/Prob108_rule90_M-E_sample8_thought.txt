Implement the entire 512-bit Rule 90 automaton as a single shift-register-like state vector updated synchronously each clock cycle. To avoid complex boundary management between sub-blocks, treat the entire 512-bit vector as one state. For each bit's next state, calculate XOR of its immediate left and right neighbors, with boundaries fixed at zero.

To efficiently compute next state simultaneously for all bits, perform bitwise left and right shifts of the entire 512-bit state padded with zero at boundaries, then XOR the shifted versions. This creates a fully combinational next state vector computed in parallel from current state.

On the positive clock edge, load new data if `load` is asserted, else update the state to the newly computed next state.

This single-register approach avoids complicated inter-block wiring, simplifies boundary conditions, and uses native Verilog vector shifts and concatenation for concise logic. It should yield clear and compact RTL with parallel next-state calculation for all cells in one step, suitable for synthesis with straightforward timing and area characteristics.
