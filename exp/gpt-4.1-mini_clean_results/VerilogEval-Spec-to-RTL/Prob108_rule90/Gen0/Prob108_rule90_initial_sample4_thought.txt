Implement a 512-bit register to hold the current state q. On each clock's positive edge, if load is high, load q with data; otherwise update q according to Rule 90: each bit in the next state is the XOR of the immediate left and right neighbors of the current q. For boundary bits, neighbors outside the 0-511 range are treated as 0. This can be done efficiently by shifting q left and right by one bit, padding with zeros, and XORing the two shifted values to get the next state.
