Implement a 512-bit register q to hold the current state of the 1D cellular automaton. On the positive clock edge, if load is high, load q with input data. Otherwise, update each bit of q according to Rule 90: the next state of each cell is the XOR of its left and right neighbors. The left neighbor of q[0] is zero, and the right neighbor of q[511] is zero. Update all bits simultaneously. Provide q as output.
