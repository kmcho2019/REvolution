To optimize the design for better power and area while maintaining performance, I will merge the two logic gates into a single assign statement by expressing the output directly as a boolean expression combining the XNOR and XOR operations. Since XNOR is equivalent to equality check, and XOR with in3, the output simplifies to (~(in1 ^ in2)) ^ in3. This is already in the original form, but we can rewrite using only XOR and basic operators. However, synthesizers usually handle this well. For minimal net switching and gate count, I will avoid intermediate wires and implement the output as a single continuous assignment with combined logic. This eliminates the intermediate net and may reduce power and area marginally by enabling better gate merging during synthesis.
