To create a superior solution, I will fuse the modular clarity and explicit gate-level instantiation from both examples while optimizing area and power by removing unnecessary hierarchy overhead. Instead of separately defining reusable gate modules (XNOR2 and XOR2), I will implement their logic directly inside TopModule using continuous assignments and intermediate wire signals. This keeps the design simple, easy to understand, and synthesis-friendly with minimal module overhead.

This fusion strategy:
- Preserves explicit two-level logic by clearly naming intermediate signals (xnor_out),
- Uses direct continuous assignments for XNOR and XOR to reduce hierarchy and net overhead,
- Retains readability and modular-style naming without module instantiation overhead,
- Is optimal for PPA since fewer modules and less wiring reduce area and power while maintaining performance,
- Avoids the slight delay and overhead of gate instantiations but expresses exactly the desired functionality.

Thus, the design benefits from the clarity and modular style of intermediate signals and descriptive naming, combined with the minimal overhead and efficient logic of direct continuous assignments, resulting in a concise and synthesis-friendly implementation.
