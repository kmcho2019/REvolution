To optimize the design for power and area while maintaining the same functionality and timing, I will replace the two instantiated gate modules with a single continuous assignment expression. This removes module overhead, reduces wiring complexity, and minimizes toggling nodes, which can reduce switching power and area. The combined expression (in1 XNOR in2) XOR in3 can be directly written using Verilog operators as assign out = (in1 ~^ in2) ^ in3;. This keeps timing ideal and simplifies the netlist.
