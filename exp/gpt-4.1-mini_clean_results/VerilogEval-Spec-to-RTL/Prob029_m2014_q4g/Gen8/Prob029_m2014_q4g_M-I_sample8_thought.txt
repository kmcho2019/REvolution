The current implementation is already optimal for PPA due to the simplicity of the logic: a single two-level combinational expression using built-in XOR and XNOR operators. However, for a marginal potential improvement in power and area, we can explicitly instantiate standard primitive gates to enable the synthesis tool to better map to library cells, which may lead to slightly better area or power optimizations on specific flows. This explicit gate-level style can sometimes help synthesis tools pick better cells or reduce net wiring overhead, although the impact is generally minimal for such simple logic.

Thus, the improvement strategy is to replace the continuous assignment with explicit gate instantiations: one XNOR gate followed by one XOR gate, connected as described. This matches the functional description exactly and can provide slight PPA gains depending on synthesis technology. Timing and logical correctness remain unaffected.
