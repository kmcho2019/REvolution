I will implement the logic in a fully combinational style without instantiating any submodules. This approach will directly express the logic equation out = ((in1 XNOR in2) XOR in3) as a single assign statement, avoiding intermediate nets and hierarchical modules. 

To emphasize clarity and compactness, I will use a single assign statement utilizing the bitwise operators in Verilog. Specifically, since XNOR is equivalent to equality in bits, I can write the XNOR as ~(in1 ^ in2). Then XOR the result with in3.

This approach minimizes hierarchy and signal declarations, reducing potential area and power, and simplifies the design for synthesis tools while being very readable. It is functionally equivalent but architecturally simpler than gate instantiations.
