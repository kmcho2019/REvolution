To optimize the given solution for power and area while maintaining correctness, I will eliminate the separate module instantiations for XNOR2 and XOR2 and reduce internal wiring by using a single continuous assign statement directly in TopModule. This approach:
- Removes unnecessary module overhead,
- Removes intermediate signals,
- Minimizes net switching and routing,
- Reduces area and power consumption,
- Keeps the design fully combinational and functionally equivalent.

Such code is concise, synthesizable, and ideal for high-quality synthesis results for a simple combinational circuit like this one.
