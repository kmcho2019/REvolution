Instead of instantiating separate gate modules, I will implement the entire logic combinationally inside TopModule using continuous assignments with an intermediate wire. This approach directly reflects the logic expression without extra hierarchy: first compute the XNOR of in1 and in2 using an assign statement, then XOR that result with in3 to produce out. This simple, flat design minimizes module overhead and improves readability, while allowing synthesis tools to optimize the logic efficiently. The use of an intermediate wire keeps the logic clear and traceable, unlike a single combined expression, which can sometimes obscure individual operation boundaries.
