To simplify the design while maintaining the exact functionality, I will remove the separate gate modules and implement the logic directly using a continuous assignment in TopModule. The output is the XOR of in3 and the XNOR of in1 and in2, which can be expressed concisely in one line as out = (~(in1 ^ in2)) ^ in3.
