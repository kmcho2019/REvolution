I will refactor the design by removing the explicit gate module instantiations and directly implementing the logic within the TopModule using continuous assignment. This reduces hierarchy and net count, potentially improving area and power without changing functionality. The logic expression is simply: out = (in1 XNOR in2) XOR in3.
