Since the output is a two-level combination of XNOR and XOR gates with single-bit inputs, we can simplify the expression by directly using XOR and XNOR operators in one line without explicitly using the negation of XOR. Using the equivalence XNOR = ~(a ^ b), the output can be directly assigned as out = (in1 ~^ in2) ^ in3, where ~^ is the XNOR operator in Verilog. This single assign statement is both simple and clear.
