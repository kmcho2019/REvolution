The original assigns an intermediate wire for the XNOR result. However, the entire function can be expressed as a single continuous expression: out = (~(in1 ^ in2)) ^ in3.

We can simplify by directly assigning out = (in1 ~^ in2) ^ in3 using Verilog's built-in bitwise XNOR (~^) operator, eliminating the intermediate wire. This reduces the code lines and might help synthesis tools optimize gate-level mapping, potentially reducing area and power marginally by removing a net.

No structural changes are necessary as the logic depth remains the same (two gates in sequence). Using built-in operators improves readability and may yield identical or slightly improved PPA metrics.
