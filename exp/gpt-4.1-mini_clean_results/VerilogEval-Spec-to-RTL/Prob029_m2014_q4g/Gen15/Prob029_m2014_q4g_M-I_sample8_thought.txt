To improve power and area metrics without sacrificing clarity, I will replace the instantiations of separate XNOR2 and XOR2 modules with a single continuous assignment using a combined logical expression. This eliminates the intermediate net and module overhead, reduces net toggling nodes, and simplifies the netlist. The combined expression `out = ~(in1 ^ in2) ^ in3` precisely implements the described two-stage logic in one step, providing a smaller footprint and potentially lower dynamic power, while maintaining the exact functional behavior. This change favors PPA improvements, especially power and area, with minimal impact on readability.
