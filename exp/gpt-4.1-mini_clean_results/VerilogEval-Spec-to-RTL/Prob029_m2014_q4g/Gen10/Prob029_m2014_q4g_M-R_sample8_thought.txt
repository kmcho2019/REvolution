I will refactor the TopModule to use continuous assignment statements with wire signals instead of an always block and reg variables. This purely structural approach directly describes the XNOR and XOR operations using Verilog operators, which allows the synthesis tool to infer simple combinational gates without any registers or latches. This reduces inferred hardware resources, potentially lowers switching power, and simplifies the design for better synthesis results. The functionality remains the same, but the style is more concise and optimal for combinational logic.
