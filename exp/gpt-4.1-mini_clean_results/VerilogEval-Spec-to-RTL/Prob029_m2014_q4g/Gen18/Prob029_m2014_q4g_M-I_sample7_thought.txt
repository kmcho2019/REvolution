The original design uses explicit module instantiations for XNOR2 and XOR2 gates which improves modularity but adds small overhead in terms of area and power. Since the circuit is simple combinational logic, replacing the instantiation with a single continuous assignment that directly computes the output expression out = ((in1 XNOR in2) XOR in3) will reduce module overhead, minimize net usage, and lower switching power slightly. This change should not affect performance since the critical path remains the same. This optimization keeps the logic concise, synthesis-friendly, and slightly more area and power efficient.
