Instead of explicitly instantiating separate XNOR and XOR gate modules, I will implement the logic purely using structural gate primitives available in Verilog. By using built-in gate primitives (xnor and xor), the design will be succinct while still maintaining clear structural hierarchy. This approach directly leverages Verilog's built-in gates, which often map efficiently to underlying technology, reducing both code complexity and synthesis overhead. The output of the xnor primitive feeds directly into the xor primitive, producing the final output. This solution remains modular at the gate level but avoids creating additional modules, simplifying the hierarchy and potentially improving synthesis and area results.
