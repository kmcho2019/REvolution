To further optimize the design for PPA, I will replace the direct continuous assignment with explicit instantiations of small gate-level modules (XNOR2 and XOR2). This approach enables better control for synthesis tools to optimize gate-level mapping and potentially share logic, which could reduce area and power in some technologies. 

Although the direct assign is compact, explicitly instantiating the gates allows the synthesis tool to identify the logic boundaries and map to standard cells more efficiently, potentially improving delay and area. This can also enhance timing control in complex flows.

I will implement the XNOR2 and XOR2 as simple modules and instantiate them in TopModule with internal wire for the intermediate net. This slightly increases hierarchy but can yield better PPA in practical ASIC flows, especially when combined with appropriate synthesis constraints.

This tradeoff optimizes for better timing and area by giving synthesis explicit structure to the logic.
