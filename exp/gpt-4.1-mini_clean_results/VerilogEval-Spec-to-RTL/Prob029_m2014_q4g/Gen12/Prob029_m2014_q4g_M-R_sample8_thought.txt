To refactor the design while keeping the same functionality, I will remove the explicit instantiation of gate modules and implement the entire combinational logic directly in the TopModule using continuous assignments. This approach simplifies the design hierarchy, reduces overhead from extra module boundaries and wiring, and improves clarity by expressing the logic in one line. Specifically, the output will be computed as the XOR of in3 with the XNOR of in1 and in2, using Verilog operators.
