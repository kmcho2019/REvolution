Simplify the design by removing the separate XNOR2 and XOR2 modules and the intermediate wire. Use a single continuous assignment in TopModule that directly implements the logic: out = (in1 XNOR in2) XOR in3. Verilog's built-in operators ~^ (XNOR) and ^ (XOR) can be used for clarity and compactness. This reduces module overhead, wiring complexity, and improves area and power while maintaining functionality.
