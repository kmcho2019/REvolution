To fix the missed detections right after reset, initialize `prev_in` to all ones (32'hFFFFFFFF) on reset instead of zeros. This way, if the input bits start at zero, the first transition from 1 to 0 can be captured correctly, as the initial state simulates that all bits were previously high. The main logic and structure remain the same: store the previous input, detect falling edges by `(prev_in & ~in)`, and OR them into the latched `out` register. This subtle change ensures no falling edge at the start is missed.
