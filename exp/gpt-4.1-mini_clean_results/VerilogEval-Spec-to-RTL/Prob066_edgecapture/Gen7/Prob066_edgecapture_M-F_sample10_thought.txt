To fix the early mismatches and improve initial detection of falling edges, initialize `prev_in` to all ones on reset. This ensures that any bit transitioning from 1 to 0 right after reset is properly detected as a falling edge, since the initial previous input reflects a high state. Also keep `out` reset to zero as before. This change aligns the design intent to capture all falling edges after reset release, preventing missed detections at startup. The rest of the design remains the same: combinational detection of falling edges and synchronous update of `prev_in` and latched `out`.
