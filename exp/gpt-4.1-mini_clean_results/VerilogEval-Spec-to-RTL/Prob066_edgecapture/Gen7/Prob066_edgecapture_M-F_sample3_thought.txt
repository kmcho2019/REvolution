The original logic for detecting falling edges (prev_in & ~in) and accumulating captures in `out` is fundamentally correct. The primary issue causing mismatches is the initialization of `prev_in` to zero on reset. This means at the first clock cycle after reset, if an input bit was already low, a falling edge detection could be missed. To fix this, initialize `prev_in` to all ones (32'hFFFFFFFF) on reset so that a transition from 1 to 0 immediately after reset is correctly detected and captured. This preserves detection of falling edges right from the start while keeping all logic synchronous and simple.
