To fix the initial transient mismatches caused by synchronous resetting of `prev_in` to `in`, initialize `prev_in` to all ones (`32'hFFFFFFFF`) on reset instead of sampling `in`. This avoids missing falling edges occurring immediately after reset deassertion and ensures a known stable starting state. By assuming all bits were previously high on reset, the first falling edge to zero will be reliably detected. Keep the synchronous reset as requested, but avoid using input-dependent reset assignments to `prev_in`. This change improves consistency without altering the core logic.
