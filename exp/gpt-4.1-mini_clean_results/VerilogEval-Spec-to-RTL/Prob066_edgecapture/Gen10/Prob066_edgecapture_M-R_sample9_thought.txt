Refactor the design by separating combinational detection of falling edges from the sequential logic that captures and holds them. Use a combinational wire to detect falling edges (`falling_edge = prev_in & ~in`) and then update the sticky output register on the clock edge accordingly. On synchronous reset, initialize both `prev_in` and `out` to zero to avoid spurious detection at startup (assuming input is stable low at reset). This design explicitly shows the detection step and output latch step separately, improving clarity and maintaining the same functionality. Using a separate wire for falling edge detection and then an always block for sequential logic is a clean structural style that differs from the original all-in-one always block.
