Use a two-stage pipeline approach to detect falling edges: in the first clock cycle, capture the current input into a register `reg_in`. In the second clock cycle, compare the previous registered input `reg_in` with the new input `in` to detect a falling edge per bit. Maintain a sticky register `out` to latch any detected falling edges until reset clears it. The synchronous reset initializes both registers to zero to avoid any spurious detections. This separation of sampling and edge detection into pipeline stages may simplify synthesis by isolating registers and combinational logic, improving tool compatibility while meeting all functional requirements.
