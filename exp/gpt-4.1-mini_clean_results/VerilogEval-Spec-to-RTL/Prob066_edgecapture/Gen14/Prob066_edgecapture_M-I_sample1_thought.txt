To ensure functional correctness and avoid simulation mismatches, the "out" register must be assigned in every clock cycle within the synchronous always block, not conditionally. This prevents inferred latches and maintains consistent simulation behavior.

Therefore, instead of conditionally assigning `out` only when a falling edge occurs, always assign `out` as the OR of its current value and the detected falling edges every clock cycle.

This preserves previously captured falling edges indefinitely until reset.

Additionally, initializing `prev_in` and `out` to zero synchronously on reset ensures a well-defined start state.

This solution ensures robust, glitch-free detection of falling edges and stable output.

Power consumption impact is minimal as unnecessary toggling is avoided by only setting bits in "out" on detected falling edges.

Overall, this improves simulation correctness and synthesis friendliness while preserving the original functionality.
