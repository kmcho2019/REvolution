To avoid spurious detection of 1->0 transitions immediately after reset, we should not assume any initial state of prev_in that causes false transitions. Instead, on reset, prev_in should be initialized to the current input value 'in'. This way, the first comparison after reset finds no transition since prev_in == in, preventing false triggering of the falling-edge capture. The out register still clears on reset. This simple change ensures only actual 1->0 transitions after reset are captured.

No other changes are needed since the update of out and prev_in on each clock edge already correctly preserves previous captures and detects new falling edges.

This approach preserves correctness without adding extra logic, also beneficial for PPA as no unnecessary initialization to all-ones is done.
