To avoid false detection of falling edges immediately after reset, initialize `prev_in` to the current input value on reset rather than zero. This ensures no spurious 1→0 transitions are detected on the first cycle. By setting `prev_in <= in` at reset, the comparison `prev_in & ~in` will yield zero on the first cycle, preventing false latches of the output. The rest of the design remains unchanged: the falling edges are detected combinationally, and `out` accumulates these events until a synchronous reset clears it.
