Instead of storing the entire previous input vector, use a 32-bit edge detection register that directly captures the falling edges (1 to 0 transitions) by comparing the current input with its delayed version through a dedicated flip-flop stage. Upon reset, clear both the edge detection register and the delayed input. Then latch detected falling edges into the output register by OR-ing with previous detected edges, maintaining them until reset. This approach separates the edge detection stage from the output latch, making the design modular and potentially easier for synthesis tools to optimize.
