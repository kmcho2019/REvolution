Instead of storing the entire previous input vector, track detected falling edges in a dedicated register that sets bits only when a 1->0 transition occurs on that bit. To avoid any ambiguity with simultaneous updates, separate the edge detection logic into combinational logic that generates a "falling_edge" signal on each clock cycle, then update the "out" register by OR-ing it with this falling edge mask. On reset, clear the output register. This approach cleanly isolates detection from latching, simplifying timing and potentially improving synthesis results by decoupling combinational and sequential logic.
