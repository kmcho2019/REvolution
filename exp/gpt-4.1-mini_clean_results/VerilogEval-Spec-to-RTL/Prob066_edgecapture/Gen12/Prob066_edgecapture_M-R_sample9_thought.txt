Refactor the design by separating combinational detection of falling edges from the sequential logic that latches the falling edges. Use a combinational wire to detect falling edges (`fall_edge = prev_in & ~in`), and an always block to update `prev_in` and `out` synchronously with reset. This clear separation improves readability and debugging. Also, initialize `prev_in` on reset to all ones to prevent spurious detections at startup. The output `out` captures falling edges and remains sticky until reset clears it. This modular structure preserves the same logic but clarifies data flow by isolating combinational detection from sequential storage.
