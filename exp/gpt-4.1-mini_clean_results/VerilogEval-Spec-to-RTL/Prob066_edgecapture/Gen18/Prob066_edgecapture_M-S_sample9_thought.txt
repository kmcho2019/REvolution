To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will keep only one sequential always block triggered on the positive clock edge. Within this block, I'll store the previous input value and detect falling edges directly by comparing the current input with the previous input. When a bit transitions from 1 to 0, the corresponding output bit will be latched high and held until reset. The synchronous reset clears both the output and the previous input register. This approach avoids extra combinational wires and consolidates logic, making it clearer and straightforward.
