Refactor the design by separating the previous input register update and the falling edge detection logic into different always blocks and use an intermediate wire for the detected falling edges. This approach improves clarity by distinctly isolating the combinational detection of falling edges from the sequential registers updating logic. The falling edges are detected combinationally by `(prev_in & ~in)` and then latched into the output register on the clock edge. The previous input is also registered in a separate always block. This separation can sometimes help synthesis tools and simulators more clearly understand the design intent. The reset still initializes `prev_in` to all ones and clears the output, avoiding false detection right after reset.
