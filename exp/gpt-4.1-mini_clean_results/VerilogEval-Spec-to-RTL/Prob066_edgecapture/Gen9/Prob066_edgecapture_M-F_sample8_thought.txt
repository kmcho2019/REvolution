To address the subtle functional mismatches after synthesis, the key improvement is to better synchronize the input `in` before sampling its transitions. This prevents metastability and timing-related glitches from affecting the detection of falling edges. I will implement a two-stage synchronizer for `in` to ensure stable sampling. Additionally, to avoid the reset-related race condition, I will initialize `prev_in` to all ones (32'hFFFFFFFF) on reset. This guarantees that no falling edges are falsely detected at the first clock after reset since all bits are considered previously high. The synchronous reset still clears the output register `out` to zero. The detection logic remains `prev_in & ~in_sync`, and output bits are latched with OR to hold detected falling edges until reset. This approach improves robustness against timing issues, removing race conditions between reset and input transitions.
