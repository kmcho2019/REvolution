Maintain a register `prev_in` that stores the input from the previous clock cycle. On each clock edge (except during reset), detect falling edges by comparing `prev_in` and `in` bits where `prev_in` was 1 and `in` is 0. Set the corresponding `out` bits to 1 when a falling edge is detected, and keep them latched until reset. On synchronous reset, clear `out` and set `prev_in` to the current `in` to avoid false detections immediately after reset. This approach is simple and ensures accurate detection of 1-to-0 transitions.
