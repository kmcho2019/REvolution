The problem was caused by declaring a wire inside the always block, which is illegal in Verilog. To fix it, I will move the falling edge detection expression outside the always block by defining it as a wire at the module level. Then, inside the always block, I will use this wire to update the output register. This preserves the original design logic (capturing falling edges per bit and latching them until reset) while fixing the syntax error. This approach cleanly separates combinational edge detection from sequential output updates and complies with Verilog syntax rules.
