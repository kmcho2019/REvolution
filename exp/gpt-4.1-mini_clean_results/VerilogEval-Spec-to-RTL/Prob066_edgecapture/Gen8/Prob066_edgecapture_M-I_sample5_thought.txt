To ensure correct detection of falling edges from 1 to 0 on each bit, the main improvement is to initialize the `prev_in` register with the input `in` value upon reset rather than zero. This approach avoids missing falling edges immediately after reset when bits might be high. Synchronous reset remains active-high. We retain the logic: detect falling edges as `prev_in & ~in`, and latch them in `out`. The `out` register is only reset when `reset` is asserted. This fix addresses missed detections due to incorrect `prev_in` initialization, thereby eliminating the mismatch issue. No other optimizations are needed given the problem constraints.
