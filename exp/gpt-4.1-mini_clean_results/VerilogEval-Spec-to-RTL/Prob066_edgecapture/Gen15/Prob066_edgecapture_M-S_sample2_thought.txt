We can simplify by combining the previous input storage and edge detection in a straightforward sequential block. On reset, clear both `prev_in` and `out` to zero to establish a known initial state, which avoids ambiguity in detecting transitions just after reset. Then, on each clock cycle, detect falling edges by checking where the previous bit was 1 and current bit is 0, and latch those bits in `out` until reset clears them. This approach maintains the required functionality with minimal logic and straightforward initialization.
