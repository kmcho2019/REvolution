Use a single sequential always block triggered on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset. Keep a register `prev_in` to store the previous input for edge detection. On each clock cycle, detect falling edges by comparing `prev_in` and `in` (bits that transition from 1 to 0). Update the output register `out` by OR'ing the newly detected falling edges with the previous `out` value to accumulate all captured falling edges until reset. This approach is straightforward, uses only one always block, and meets the requirement to latch detected falling edges until reset.
