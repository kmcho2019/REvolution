We can simplify the design by combining the prev_in and out update logic into one always block without an intermediate wire. Detect the falling edge directly inside the sequential block and update the output accordingly. This keeps the logic straightforward and reduces combinational signals without losing any functionality.

On each clock edge, if reset is active, clear prev_in and out. Otherwise, detect bits that have fallen from 1 to 0 by comparing prev_in and current input, then OR the detected bits into out and update prev_in.

This removes the extra wire and combinational assign, simplifying the code while preserving the original behavior.
