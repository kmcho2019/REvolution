To address the subtle initialization and detection issues, initialize prev_in to all ones (32'hFFFFFFFF) on reset. This ensures that any transition from 1 to 0 on the input at the first cycle after reset will be correctly detected, as previously all bits are considered '1'. The rest of the logic remains the same, capturing falling edges by OR-ing with out to latch the detected transitions until reset. This approach avoids missing any first-cycle transitions after reset and removes ambiguity from prev_in initialization.
