Instead of storing the entire previous input vector to detect 1-to-0 transitions, use a two-stage pipeline register to hold the input delayed by one and two clock cycles. Then, detect falling edges by comparing the two delayed signals. Once a falling edge bit is detected (when the bit was 1 two cycles ago and is now 0), latch that bit into the output register until reset. This approach uses an extra pipeline stage but can help avoid combinational loops or race conditions by explicitly pipelining the input and detecting transitions with a stable delay.
