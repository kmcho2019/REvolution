To fix this, I will:

- Initialize `prev_in` to all ones (`32'hFFFFFFFF`) on reset instead of zero, so that any transition from 1 to 0 in `in` immediately after reset is correctly detected as a falling edge.
- Keep the existing logic for detecting falling edges and latching them until reset.
- This fix ensures that the detection is correct from the very first clock cycle after reset.

This approach will fix the missed edge detection immediately after reset without affecting the rest of the logic, as the initial state of `prev_in` logically means all bits were '1' before the first input sample.
