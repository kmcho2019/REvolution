Implement the 2's complement serial converter FSM as a Mealy machine instead of a Moore machine to produce the output bit directly from the current state and input, reducing the need for extra registers for output generation. Use two states: "NO_CARRY" and "CARRY", where "CARRY" indicates that the LSB 1 has been seen and subsequent bits are inverted.

Since output z depends on current state and input x, define it combinationally in the always_comb block. Use asynchronous reset on the state register to ensure immediate reset. This approach reduces the latency of output generation by avoiding registering the input separately and producing output in the next-state logic combinationally.

This Mealy FSM approach simplifies the logic by directly computing z and next state together and may reduce area and power by eliminating the input register and explicit inversion_bit register.
