To improve the PPA of the given design, I will remove the unnecessary input register x_reg and directly use the input `x` in the next state and output logic. This reduces one flip-flop, lowering area and power. 

I will also convert the asynchronous reset to a synchronous active-high reset to reduce complexity and improve timing closure, as asynchronous resets can complicate timing and power.

The FSM state register will update on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset, simplifying reset handling.

Next state logic and output remain combinational, preserving the Moore FSM style.

This approach keeps functionality intact but reduces area and power and improves timing metrics by eliminating asynchronous reset and one register.
