Implement the two's complement serial converter as a three-state Moore FSM to explicitly track the conversion process: IDLE while reset is asserted, PASS_THROUGH before the first '1' bit, and INVERT after the first '1' bit has been encountered. This FSM outputs a registered bit based solely on its current state, ensuring Moore machine behavior. The output z in IDLE is 0, in PASS_THROUGH it matches the input bit x, and in INVERT it outputs the inverted input bit. This approach clearly separates the reset state and conversion states, enhancing clarity and robustness while maintaining asynchronous reset and positive edge clocking.
