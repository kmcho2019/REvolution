Implement the circuit using a combinational always block with a case statement on input c to select the output q. This approach clearly expresses the selection logic, improves readability, and enables easier modifications or expansions in the future. Instead of a chained ternary operator, the case statement can synthesize into a mux efficiently and may allow synthesis tools to optimize timing and area better.
