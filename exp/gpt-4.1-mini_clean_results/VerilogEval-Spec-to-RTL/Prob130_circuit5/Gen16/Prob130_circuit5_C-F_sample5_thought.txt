This fusion yields:
- Clean, direct, and efficient data selection using an indexed array (less wiring/shifting).

- Simplified logic with predictable synthesis for multiplexers and gating.

This approach balances area, power, and timing effectively, improving over wide concatenation shifts and ungated multiplexers.
