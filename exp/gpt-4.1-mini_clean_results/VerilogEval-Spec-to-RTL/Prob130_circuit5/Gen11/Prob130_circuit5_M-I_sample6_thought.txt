To optimize PPA (performance, power, area), I will replace the shift-based selection with an explicit 4-to-1 multiplexer controlled by c[1:0]. This avoids a variable shift operation on a 16-bit vector, reducing logic depth and switching activity. The mux will directly select among b, e, a, and d depending on c values 0,1,2,3 respectively, and output 4'hF for c >= 4. This will improve timing by reducing critical path and decrease power by limiting switching to only the selected 4-bit vector. It also reduces area by eliminating the barrel shifter logic.
