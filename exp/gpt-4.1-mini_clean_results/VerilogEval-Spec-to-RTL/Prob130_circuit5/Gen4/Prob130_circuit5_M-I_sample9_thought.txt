To optimize the module for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will replace the shift-based selection of the 4-bit slice with an explicit 4-to-1 multiplexer using a case statement. This eliminates the costly variable shift operation, reducing logic complexity and switching activity. 

The logic is straightforward:
- If c < 4, select q from one of {b, e, a, d} based on c[1:0].
- Else output 4'hF.

This approach produces simpler multiplexers, smaller area, and faster timing while preserving exact functionality.
