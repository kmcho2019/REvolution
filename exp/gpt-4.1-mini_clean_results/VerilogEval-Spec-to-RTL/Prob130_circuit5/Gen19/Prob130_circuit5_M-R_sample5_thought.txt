Refactor the design by replacing the bit-shift based slice extraction with an explicit case statement for output q. The case statement selects q based on the value of c for cases 0 to 4, mapping to b, e, a, d, and 4'hF respectively; for other values of c, output q will default to 4'hF. This approach avoids the multiplication and shift operation and simplifies the logic, enabling synthesis tools to infer a small multiplexer. The functional behavior remains unchanged, preserving the original indexing scheme, but the implementation is clearer and more efficient in terms of logic depth and potential power consumption.
