The outputs q appear to select among inputs b, e, a, and d for c=0 to 3, and output 4'hF for c≥4. Instead of concatenating and shifting, this design uses a combinational case statement on the lower nibble of c to directly map c to the output without any shifts or multiplications. This approach uses a simple multiplexer implemented with a case statement, which allows clear, constant-time selection, and may synthesize into a small multiplexer network minimizing logic area and power, with very predictable timing.
