Simplify the design by implementing a straightforward combinational multiplexer using a case statement on input c. Map c=0 to output b, c=1 to e, c=2 to a, c=3 to d, and for all other c values output 4'hF. This approach eliminates the need for one-hot encoding and gating, reducing logic complexity, area, and power while maintaining the exact functionality demonstrated by the waveform.
