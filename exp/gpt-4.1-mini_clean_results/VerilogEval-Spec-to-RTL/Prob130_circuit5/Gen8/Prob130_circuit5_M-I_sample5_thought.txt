The original solution uses a 16-bit vector and shifts it by c[1:0]*4 to select the output, which can lead to less optimal hardware due to a variable barrel shifter. To optimize for performance, power, and area, I will replace the shift operation with an explicit 4-to-1 multiplexer that selects among b, e, a, and d directly based on the lower two bits of c. 

This direct mux implementation reduces logic depth (improving timing), limits switching activity (power), and decreases area by avoiding the complex shift logic. The functionality remains identical: for c < 4, q = one of {b, e, a, d} selected by c, else q = 4'hF.
