This design interprets the input 'c' as a selector for a 5-way 4-bit multiplexer with inputs {b, e, a, d, 4'hF} mapped respectively to c=0,1,2,3 and any c≥4 selecting 4'hF. Instead of concatenation and shifting, we use a simple combinational 'case' statement to explicitly assign 'q' based on the value of 'c'. This approach is straightforward and readable, enabling the synthesis tool to infer an optimized multiplexer. It also naturally handles values of 'c' outside the 0-4 range by defaulting to 4'hF.
