To optimize the original design for better PPA, the multiplication in the bit-shift operation for index selection will be replaced by an explicit shift operation, eliminating multiplication hardware. Instead of shifting a wide 20-bit concatenation, I will use a small combinational case statement that explicitly maps each valid c value (0 to 4) to the corresponding output nibble. For c ≥ 5, the output will default to 4'hF. This approach results in a simple 5-to-1 multiplexer with constant inputs, reducing logic complexity and switching activity, which benefits both power and area. The performance will also improve due to a straightforward combinational path without variable shifts.
