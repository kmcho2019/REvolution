The original design uses a nested ternary operator with equality checks on the lower two bits of c, along with an explicit check on c[3:2]==0 to determine if c<4, else output 4'hF.

To optimize PPA further:
- Replace the equality checks on c[1:0] with a direct 4-to-1 mux indexed by c[1:0], which synthesizes to a more efficient multiplexer.
- The check for c<4 can be done more naturally using a less-than operator (c < 4) to potentially simplify the comparison logic.
- Implement the mux and selection using a case statement inside a combinational always block for better synthesis friendliness and clearer intent.
- This style tends to produce a cleaner LUT-based mux in synthesis, improving timing and area.
- Keeping the output registered as purely combinational (no clock) to maintain functional equivalence.

These changes aim to reduce logic depth, improve synthesis efficiency, and possibly lower power and area slightly without changing functionality.
