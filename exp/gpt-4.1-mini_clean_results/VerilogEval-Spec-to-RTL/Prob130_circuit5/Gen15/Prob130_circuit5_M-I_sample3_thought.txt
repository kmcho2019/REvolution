Optimize the design by replacing the one-hot decoding and multiple gated inputs combined with OR gates with a simple 5-input multiplexer using a case statement. This reduces logic depth and fan-in, improving timing and power by eliminating wide OR gates and redundant gating.

The select input c drives the multiplexer directly:
- c=0: output b
- c=1: output e
- c=2: output a
- c=3: output d
- c>3: output 4'hF

This single case-based approach minimizes combinational logic area and switching activity, yielding better PPA metrics without losing functionality or clarity.
