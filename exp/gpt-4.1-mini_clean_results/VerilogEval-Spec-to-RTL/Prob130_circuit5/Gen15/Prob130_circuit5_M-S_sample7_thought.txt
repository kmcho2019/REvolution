The original solution uses a 16-bit vector and shift operation to select among four inputs based on c[1:0]. However, since only four known inputs (b, e, a, d) are selected when c<4, and otherwise output is 4'hF, this can be directly implemented as a simple combinational multiplexer using a case statement or conditional operator without shifts.

This approach reduces complexity by avoiding the shift operation and makes the circuit easier to understand and potentially more efficient for synthesis tools. It preserves the same functionality exactly.
