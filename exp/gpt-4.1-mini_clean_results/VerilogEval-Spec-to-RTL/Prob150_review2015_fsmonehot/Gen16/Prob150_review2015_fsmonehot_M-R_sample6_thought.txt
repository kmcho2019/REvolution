I will refactor the design by explicitly listing the next-state signals for all states, not just the requested output next states, using assign statements. This clarifies the entire next state decoding and makes the next-state logic fully explicit for every state bit, which is often helpful for verification and debugging. The requested outputs (B3_next, S_next, etc.) will still be extracted as assigned signals from the next state bits. The outputs done, counting, and shift_ena are assigned directly from current states. This approach does not change the functional behavior but reorganizes the combinational logic for easier traceability and possible extensions.
