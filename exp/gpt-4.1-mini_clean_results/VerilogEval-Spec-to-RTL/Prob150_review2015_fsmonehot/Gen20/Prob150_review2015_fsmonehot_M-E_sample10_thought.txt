To provide a distinct architectural style, I will implement the next-state and output logic by explicitly enumerating all next states as separate assign statements without intermediate terms, using the one-hot encoded current state bits and inputs directly in the expressions.

The approach here is to write each next-state signal as a sum of product terms clearly reflecting each transition from the FSM diagram. This maximizes readability in a direct mapping way rather than factorization.

Similarly, the Moore outputs will be assigned from the current state bits straightforwardly, matching their definition in the FSM.

This method results in explicit, direct expressions for each next state, clearly showing how inputs and current states determine the next state activation, which contrasts with previous more factored or intermediate-wire based implementations.
