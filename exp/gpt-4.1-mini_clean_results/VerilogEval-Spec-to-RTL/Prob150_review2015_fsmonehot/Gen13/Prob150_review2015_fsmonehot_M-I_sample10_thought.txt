To improve the PPA (power, performance, area) of the one-hot next-state combinational logic, we focus on simplifying and factoring the Boolean expressions further to reduce gate count and logic depth, which can help timing and power.

Key improvements:
- Combine terms with common factors more aggressively, e.g., group (S | S1 | S110) & ~d for S_next, reducing multiple OR gates.
- Factor the Wait_next and Count_next expressions to avoid redundant terms.
- For Count_next, rewrite as Count & ~done_counting | B3.
- For Wait_next, rewrite as Wait & ~ack | Count & done_counting.
- Keep output logic minimal and derived directly from the current state bits.
- Use localparams to clarify the bit indices.
- Maintain a consistent style that is friendly to synthesis tools for optimized implementation.

This yields slightly smaller logic and potentially faster critical paths due to reduced gate fan-in and number of logic stages while preserving the one-hot structure and clarity.
