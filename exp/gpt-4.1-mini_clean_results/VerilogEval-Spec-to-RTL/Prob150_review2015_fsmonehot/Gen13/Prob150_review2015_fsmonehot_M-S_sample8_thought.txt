Simplify the combinational next-state logic by directly expressing each next state as a single assignment combining all conditions that lead to it, removing intermediate single-use wires. This reduces the number of intermediate signals and the overall verbosity, while retaining clarity by decoding current states at the start. The outputs remain simple ORs of current state bits. This approach keeps the design purely combinational, concise, and fully compliant with the FSM and one-hot encoding requirements.
