To optimize the existing one-hot FSM next-state and output logic for better PPA, we can reduce the number of intermediate wires and redundant logic by directly combining conditions in the continuous assignments for next state outputs, minimizing the number of OR and AND gates needed.

Also, the intermediate next-state signals like S11_next, S110_next, B0_next, B1_next, and B2_next are not output ports and not required explicitly. Removing these intermediate wires reduces complexity and saves area and power due to fewer nets and logic.

We can integrate their logic directly into relevant next-state signals where needed or omit them if not required as outputs.

Furthermore, for shift_ena, which is asserted for states B0 to B3 inclusive, we can encode it as a comparison of the one-hot vector range or directly OR the bits as done originally; given that is minimal logic, keeping it OR is fine.

Thus, the improved approach:

- Remove intermediate next-state wires that are not output ports
- Express next state signals only for requested outputs (B3_next, S_next, S1_next, Count_next, Wait_next)
- Write minimal combinational logic expressions
- Keep output logic as simple direct assignments

This will reduce the combinational complexity, thus area and power, and likely improve timing slightly by simplifying the logic path.

Since the original implementation is already clean, these refinements represent marginal optimization without functional change.
