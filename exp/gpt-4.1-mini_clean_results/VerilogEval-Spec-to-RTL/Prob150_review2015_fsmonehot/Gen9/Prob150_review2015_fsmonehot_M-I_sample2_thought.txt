The existing solution is functionally correct and clearly expresses the next-state and output logic using straightforward OR and AND combinations of one-hot states and inputs. However, the next-state equations contain some overlapping terms that can be factored to slightly reduce gate count and potentially lower dynamic power and area.

For example, S_next depends on (S & ~d), (S1 & ~d), and (S110 & ~d). This can be factored as (~d) & (S | S1 | S110) to reduce repeated AND gates.

Similarly, Count_next has B3 | (Count & ~done_counting) which is already minimal, but Wait_next also includes (Count & done_counting) | (Wait & ~ack).

Shift_ena is asserted for four consecutive states (B0, B1, B2, B3). Instead of OR'ing four bits, we can OR the group as a contiguous vector slice: state[4] through state[7].

Also, since only the S, S1, B3, Count, and Wait next states are requested as outputs, no other next state outputs are assigned, which is efficient.

Therefore, the improvement focuses on factoring and grouping terms for a cleaner logic implementation, which can lead to marginal area and power improvements.

The combinational style is preserved, avoiding any always blocks.

No functional changes are made; only logic simplification and code clarity improvements.
