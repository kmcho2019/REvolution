Implement the Moore FSM next-state and output logic directly by decoding the one-hot encoded state bits using Boolean expressions derived by inspection. The main novelty is to explicitly derive and write next-state equations for each next state output (B3_next, S_next, S1_next, Count_next, Wait_next) using the explicit conditions from the transition table, with no intermediate signals or factoring, providing a straightforward, literal mapping. The output signals (done, counting, shift_ena) are directly driven by current state bits according to their definitions. This approach ensures clarity, directness, and minimal logic levels, relying on explicit Boolean conditions rather than factoring or combining terms.
