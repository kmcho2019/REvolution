We simplify the next-state logic by combining terms where possible and removing intermediate wires to reduce verbosity. The key observations are:

- The initial decoding of state bits can be done with direct indexing without separate wires.
- The next-state expressions can be written directly and compactly without repeated intermediate signals.
- Outputs remain directly derived from current state bits as per Moore logic.
- This reduces code lines and complexity while maintaining the same logic behavior and readability.

By avoiding unnecessary intermediate signals and grouping terms succinctly, the design is cleaner and more concise without losing clarity or correctness.
