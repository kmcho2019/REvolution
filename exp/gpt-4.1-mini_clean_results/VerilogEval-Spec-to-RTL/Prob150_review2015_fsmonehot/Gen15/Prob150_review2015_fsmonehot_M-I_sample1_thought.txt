To optimize for better PPA, I will simplify the next-state logic by factoring shared conditions and removing redundant intermediate wires. The S_next next-state conditions can be combined into one expression using OR of the involved states gated by their respective input conditions, reducing logic depth and area. Similarly, the Wait_next and Count_next signals can be consolidated without separate intermediate signals. The shift_ena output can be generated by OR-ing the continuous block B0 to B3 using a single range check of state bits. This also reduces switching and improves synthesis quality. Outputs counting and done remain direct mappings from current states.

This maintains functional correctness but reduces the total number of signals and logic gates, improving power and area while keeping timing intact.
