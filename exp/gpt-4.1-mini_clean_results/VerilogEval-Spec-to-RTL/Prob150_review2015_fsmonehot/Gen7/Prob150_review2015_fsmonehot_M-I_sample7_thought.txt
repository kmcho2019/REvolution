The original code is functionally correct and well-structured. For PPA improvement, we focus on simplifying the next-state logic to reduce gate count and critical path delay.

- The next-state expressions for S_next and S11_next can be factored more aggressively to reduce the number of terms, exploiting mutual exclusivity.
- The expression for S_next currently duplicates (S & ~d) and (S1 & ~d) separately; these can be merged into (S | S1) & ~d.
- The S_next also includes (S110 & ~d) which can merge with the previous into a single OR with three states: (S | S1 | S110) & ~d.
- Wait_next is the OR of two mutually exclusive states' conditions, so it remains as is.

These simplifications reduce logic gates and levels, improving area and delay. The one-hot encoding is fixed by specification and is suitable here.
