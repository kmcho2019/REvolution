Refactor the Moore FSM next-state and output logic to express the next states as explicit combinational functions for each state bit, using separate intermediate wires for each next state bit. Then directly assign the requested output signals (like B3_next) from these intermediate next-state bits. This approach modularizes next-state decoding by explicitly defining the full next-state vector for all 10 bits, making the logic clearer and easier to extend or debug.
