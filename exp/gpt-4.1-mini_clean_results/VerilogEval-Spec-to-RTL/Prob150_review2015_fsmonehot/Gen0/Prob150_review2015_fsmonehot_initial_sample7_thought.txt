Implement combinational logic for next-state and output signals of a Moore FSM with one-hot encoded states. Given the current one-hot encoded 10-bit state input and three inputs d, done_counting, ack, derive next state signals for states S, S1, B3, Count, and Wait based on the state transition diagram. Also derive output signals done, counting, and shift_ena based on the current state. The outputs are combinational functions of current state and inputs as specified, following the next state logic by inspection from the one-hot encoding.
