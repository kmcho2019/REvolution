This reduces three separate AND gates with ~d into one AND gate.

Similarly, for Count_next and Wait_next, the logic is already minimal.

Additionally, since B3_next is just B2, no optimization needed there.

This factoring reduces gate count and fanout, potentially improving power and area slightly without affecting performance or clarity.
