The module only needs combinational logic for next-state signals and outputs based on the current one-hot encoded state and inputs. The next-state signals to output are B3_next, S_next, S1_next, Count_next, Wait_next. 

We can directly derive each next-state output from the current state bits and inputs, combining conditions with OR, and we only need to output those specific next states.

Intermediate states like S11, S110, B0, B1, B2 do not need dedicated outputs; they can be handled internally in the expressions.

Outputs done, counting, shift_ena are asserted when in Wait, Count, and B0-B3 states respectively.

This approach reduces intermediate signals and keeps the code clean and concise.
