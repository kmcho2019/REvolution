The original solution correctly implements the combinational next-state and output logic for the one-hot FSM. To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, and area), the improvements focus on simplifying the next-state logic by factoring and merging terms, reducing the number of intermediate signals, and combining logic where possible without sacrificing readability.

Specifically:
- Merge all transitions leading to S_next into a single expression with minimal ORs.
- Remove intermediate wires that are used only once, integrating their logic into direct assigns.
- Combine next-state logic for Count and Wait states similarly.
- Since shift_ena is active on states B0-B3, which are four consecutive bits, use a simple OR reduction of those bits instead of separate ORs.
- Outputs done and counting are simply assigned from the current state bits, kept as direct assignments for clarity.
- The simplified and combined expressions reduce gate count and logic depth, potentially improving frequency and lowering power and area.
- Maintain clear naming and comments for readability and verification.

These changes do not alter functional correctness and keep the code purely combinational for next-state and output logic.
