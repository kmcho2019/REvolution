Use explicit next-state signals for each one-hot encoded state bit, derived by directly implementing the transition conditions for each state. For states with input-dependent transitions (S, S1, S11, S110), compute next states based on input d. For the chain of states B0 through B3, use fixed next-state moves since they always advance sequentially. For Count and Wait states, use inputs done_counting and ack respectively. The outputs done, counting, and shift_ena are derived only from the current state (Moore outputs). The next-state outputs (B3_next, S_next, etc.) correspond exactly to the one-hot bit asserted in the next cycle. This approach clearly separates combinational next-state logic from outputs and uses concise combinational assignments for clarity and easy maintainability.
