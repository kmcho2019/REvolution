To improve PPA, especially power and area, simplify and compress the next-state logic by eliminating intermediate wires and combining expressions where possible without losing readability. Also, rearrange the next-state assignments to use fewer terms by merging logically equivalent conditions, which reduces logic gate count. While one-hot encoding inherently uses more FFs, this approach focuses on minimizing combinational logic complexity. This maintains clarity and correctness but slightly reduces the number of logic operators and fan-in, potentially improving timing and power. Moore outputs remain purely combinational on current state bits for clarity and to avoid glitching.
