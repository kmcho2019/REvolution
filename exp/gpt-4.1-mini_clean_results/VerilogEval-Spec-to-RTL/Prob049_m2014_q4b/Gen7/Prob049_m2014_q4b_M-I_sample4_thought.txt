The original design is minimal and functionally correct for a positive edge triggered D flip-flop with asynchronous reset. However, to potentially improve power by reducing unnecessary toggling, a clock enable signal can be added to update q only when d differs from q, effectively avoiding redundant toggling. This saves switching power without changing the external behavior. This is a common low-impact power optimization for flip-flops. The asynchronous reset behavior remains unchanged. Area and timing remain essentially the same, but power could improve slightly.
