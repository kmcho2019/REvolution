To refactor the design while keeping the same functionality, I will use a continuous assignment with a register updated inside an always block that triggers on the clock and asynchronous reset. However, instead of using a synchronous assignment inside the always block, I will implement a two-process style: one always block for asynchronous reset and another always block for capturing the data on the clock edge when reset is not active. This separation clarifies the asynchronous reset behavior from the synchronous data capture. The output `q` will be driven from the register as before.
