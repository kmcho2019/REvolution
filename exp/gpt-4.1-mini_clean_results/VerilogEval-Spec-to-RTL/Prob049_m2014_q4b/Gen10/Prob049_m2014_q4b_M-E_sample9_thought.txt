Implement the asynchronous reset using a combination of a latch to hold the reset state and a positive edge-triggered D flip-flop. The latch samples the asynchronous reset signal and generates a synchronous reset signal for the D flip-flop. This way, the flip-flop's reset is synchronized internally, but the reset input is still asynchronous externally. This reduces metastability risks in the flip-flop and provides a novel architecture compared to the traditional direct asynchronous reset in the always block.
