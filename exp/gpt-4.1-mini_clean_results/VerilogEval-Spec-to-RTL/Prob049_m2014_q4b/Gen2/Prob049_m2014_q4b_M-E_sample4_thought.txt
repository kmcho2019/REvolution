Implement the positive edge-triggered D flip-flop with asynchronous reset by splitting the reset logic from the data latch using a gated latch approach internally. Use an always block sensitive only to clk, and another always block sensitive only to ar to asynchronously force q to zero when ar is asserted. This approach separates the asynchronous reset handling from the clocked data capture, providing a clear architectural partition and illustrating an alternative method to implement asynchronous resets by combinational forcing rather than sensitivity list inclusion.
