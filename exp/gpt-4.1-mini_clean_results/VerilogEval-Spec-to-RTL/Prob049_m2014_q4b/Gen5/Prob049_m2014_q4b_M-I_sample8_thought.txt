The existing design is already minimal and optimal for a single D flip-flop with asynchronous reset. To marginally improve power, we can ensure the asynchronous reset signal is sampled only when necessary. However, adding clock gating or enable signals is out of scope and can add complexity and area. Since the asynchronous reset is active high and directly affects the flip-flop, no further simplification is possible. The current structure with sensitivity on posedge clk and posedge ar is the standard and optimal approach for asynchronous reset. Thus, no RTL changes are made because the design is minimal and meets all criteria for PPA optimization at this level.
