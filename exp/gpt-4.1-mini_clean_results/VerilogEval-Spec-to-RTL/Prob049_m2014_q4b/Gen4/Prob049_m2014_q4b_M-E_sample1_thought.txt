Implement the asynchronous reset and data latch in a single always block sensitive to both the clock's positive edge and the asynchronous reset signal. This approach directly uses the asynchronous reset input in the sensitivity list, eliminating any intermediate signals. Upon reset asserted, the output q is immediately cleared; otherwise, on each clock's positive edge, q captures the value of d. This is the standard and most concise form to implement a positive-edge triggered D flip-flop with asynchronous reset, ensuring minimal hardware and straightforward synthesis.
