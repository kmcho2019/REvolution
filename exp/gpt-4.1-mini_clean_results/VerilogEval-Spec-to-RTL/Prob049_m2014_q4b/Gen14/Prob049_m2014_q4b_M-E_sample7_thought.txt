I will implement the asynchronous reset by separating the reset logic from the main D flip-flop logic. The asynchronous reset will be handled in a combinational always block to drive an internal reset signal, which then synchronously resets the flip-flop on the next clock edge. This approach results in a synchronous reset triggered by an asynchronous input, effectively turning the asynchronous reset into a synchronous one but initiated immediately when ar is asserted. This can help reduce metastability issues and improve timing at the cost of a one-cycle delay in reset release.
