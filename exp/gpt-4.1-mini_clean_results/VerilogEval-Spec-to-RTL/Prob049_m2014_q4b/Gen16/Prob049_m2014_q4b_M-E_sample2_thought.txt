Implement the asynchronous reset by splitting it into two stages inside the TopModule: first, asynchronously clear an internal register 'q_int' on reset, and second, synchronously transfer the internal register to the output 'q' on the clock edge. This approach separates asynchronous reset logic from synchronous output logic, which may help in certain timing scenarios or for clarity. The internal register handles the reset asynchronously, while the output register updates synchronously, effectively creating a two-stage pipeline for the flip-flop state. This modular approach avoids submodule instantiation but still clearly distinguishes asynchronous reset and synchronous data capture.
