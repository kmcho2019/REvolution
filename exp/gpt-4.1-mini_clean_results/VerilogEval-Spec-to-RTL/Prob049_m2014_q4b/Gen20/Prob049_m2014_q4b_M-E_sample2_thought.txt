Implement the asynchronous reset by using a gated latch followed by a synchronous D flip-flop, creating an effective asynchronous reset behavior with a two-stage approach. The latch is transparent when reset is inactive, allowing the reset signal to asynchronously force q low when asserted, and the flip-flop then synchronously captures the latch output on the clock's positive edge. This decomposes the asynchronous reset into latch and flip-flop combination, offering a different structural perspective. The top module will directly include this two-stage design without hierarchy to keep it concise.
