Instead of using a traditional asynchronous reset in the always block, implement the D flip-flop as a latch-based master-slave flip-flop with an asynchronous reset that forces both master and slave latches to zero independently of the clock. This involves two levels of latches: a master latch transparent on clk=0 and a slave latch transparent on clk=1. The asynchronous reset overrides the outputs of both latches, forcing q to zero immediately when asserted. This approach explicitly models a master-slave structure, providing a new architectural perspective while ensuring asynchronous reset behavior and correct positive edge-triggered D flip-flop functionality.
