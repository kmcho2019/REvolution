The original solution is already minimal and optimal for the requirement: driving a constant zero signal. The continuous assignment of 1'b0 is the most concise and synthesis-friendly way to express a constant low output in Verilog.

Since the output is a constant tied to logic low, synthesis tools will recognize this and optimize it to either no logic or a tie-low cell, which leads to minimal area and power consumption. There is no clock or combinational logic to optimize for performance or timing.

Therefore, no changes are necessary to improve power, performance, or area. Any alternative implementations, such as using a reg or always block, would not improve PPA and would make the code unnecessarily complex.

Hence, the best approach is to keep the original implementation as is.
