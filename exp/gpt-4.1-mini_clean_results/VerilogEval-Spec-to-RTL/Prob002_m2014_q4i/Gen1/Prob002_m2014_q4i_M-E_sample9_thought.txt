Implement the TopModule as a clocked register always loaded with zero. By using a flip-flop initialized or reset to zero, the output remains stable at zero. This approach introduces a sequential element rather than a continuous assign and demonstrates a different architectural style. The clock and reset inputs are added to drive the register; however, these ports are internal to the module and not exposed externally, keeping the external interface with only the single output 'out'. This will be a different structural style that still maintains a constant zero output.
