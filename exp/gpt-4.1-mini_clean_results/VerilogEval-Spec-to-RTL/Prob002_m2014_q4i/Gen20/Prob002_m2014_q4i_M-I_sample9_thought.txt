The original design uses an additional module instantiation (ConstZero) to drive a constant 0 output. While functionally correct, this adds unnecessary module hierarchy and slightly increases area and power due to extra wiring and module overhead. To optimize power, performance, and area (PPA), the simplest and most direct approach is to drive the output directly with a continuous assignment of 1'b0 in TopModule. This removes the extra module, reduces hierarchy, and leads to a minimal logic footprint. Since the output is always low, there's no clock or combinational logic delay, resulting in optimal timing and power characteristics.
