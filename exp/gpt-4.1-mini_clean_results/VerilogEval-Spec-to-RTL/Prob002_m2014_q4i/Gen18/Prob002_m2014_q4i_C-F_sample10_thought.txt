To fuse the strengths of both examples, I will retain the simplicity and minimalism of directly assigning a constant zero to the output, ensuring minimal area and power. However, to leverage modularity from the first example (which aids clarity and potential reuse), I will use a `localparam` inside the TopModule to define the constant zero. This approach:
- Avoids external module overhead (better area/power).
- Provides clear intent and easy modification by isolating the constant in a parameter.
- Is optimal for synthesis tools that will inline constants and minimize logic.
