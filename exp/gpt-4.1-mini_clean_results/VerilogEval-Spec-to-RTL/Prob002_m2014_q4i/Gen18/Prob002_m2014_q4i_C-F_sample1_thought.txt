However, to minimize any overhead and keep the design synthesis-friendly, I will mark the parameter as localparam, indicating it is a fixed constant internal to the module. This ensures clarity that the output is a constant zero tied to ground with no chance of external override, avoiding any unnecessary parameter propagation overhead.

The output remains a wire driven by a continuous assignment of this localparam, combining the reusability and clarity of the parameter constant with the absolute minimalism of direct constant assignment.

This approach improves readability, synthesis optimization guidance, and maintains absolute minimal logic, resulting in an optimal blend of configurability (if needed in the future by converting to parameter), minimal area, zero timing, and minimal power.
