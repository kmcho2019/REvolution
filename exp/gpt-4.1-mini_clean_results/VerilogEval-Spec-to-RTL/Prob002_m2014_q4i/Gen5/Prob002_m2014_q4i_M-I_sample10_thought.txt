The original design is already minimal by continuously assigning a constant 0 to the output wire. Since there is no clock or combinational logic, synthesis tools will directly tie the output to ground, yielding minimal power and area usage. To further enhance clarity and potential synthesis optimizations, we can declare the output as a wire and explicitly mark it as a constant net with a parameter, though practically this will not change PPA much. Another approach is to use a localparam to define the constant 0 and assign it, potentially improving tool readability. However, these are mostly stylistic and have negligible impact. Overall, the original solution is optimal from a PPA perspective.
