To optimize for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), the current solution is already minimal and optimal in RTL since it's a direct constant assignment. However, an alternative method is to declare the output as a `wire` driven by a Verilog constant 0 through an implicit net declaration in the port list itself. This sometimes allows synthesis tools to optimize the tie-off even more aggressively by recognizing the constant at the port level. Since no additional logic or registers exist, this will not impact timing and power but may help synthesis infer a ground tie cell directly with minimal area usage.
