The current design uses a separate module instantiation to drive a constant zero, which introduces a minor overhead in area due to the additional module hierarchy. To optimize area slightly without affecting power or performance, I will remove the ConstantZero module and drive the output directly with a continuous assignment inside TopModule. This reduces the module hierarchy and the synthesis tool can optimize the constant assignment efficiently. The power remains minimal because the output is statically driven to zero with no toggling, and timing remains trivial without clocks or paths.
