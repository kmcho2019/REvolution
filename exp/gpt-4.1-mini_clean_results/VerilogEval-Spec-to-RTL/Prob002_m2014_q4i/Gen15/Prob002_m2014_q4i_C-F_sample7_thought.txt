To combine the best ideas from both examples, I will keep the output as a wire and drive it with a continuous assignment of a named constant declared as a parameter (rather than localparam) at the module level. Using a parameter instead of localparam increases reusability and configurability, allowing the constant to be overridden if needed without changing the code. This still results in minimal logic, no registers, and a clean constant assignment that synthesis tools will easily optimize to a tied ground net. This solution blends modular clarity and flexibility (from Example 1) with direct simplicity and minimal overhead (from Example 2), achieving optimal clarity, reusability, area, and power efficiency.
