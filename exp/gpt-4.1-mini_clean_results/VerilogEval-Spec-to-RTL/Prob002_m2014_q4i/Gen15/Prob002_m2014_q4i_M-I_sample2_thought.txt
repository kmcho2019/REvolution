The existing solution is already optimal in RTL for driving a constant zero output using a continuous assignment. Any further PPA improvements largely depend on synthesis and technology mapping rather than RTL changes. However, to potentially aid synthesis tools in recognizing this as a constant tie-low net and reduce power and area, we can mark the output as a wire tied to a localparam constant zero. Using a localparam can sometimes help synthesis tools understand the constant nature more explicitly. This will not change functionality but might marginally improve downstream optimizations.
