The current implementation is already optimal: it drives the output with a constant logic low, which incurs minimal area, zero power due to no switching, and zero delay. Since the output port defaults to wire and is directly assigned, no additional improvement can reduce PPA further. To reinforce clarity and synthesis intent, we can explicitly declare the output as wire and add a synthesis attribute to hint the constant output to tools, but this is mostly stylistic and rarely affects PPA. Thus, no significant PPA improvement is possible beyond the existing minimal implementation.
