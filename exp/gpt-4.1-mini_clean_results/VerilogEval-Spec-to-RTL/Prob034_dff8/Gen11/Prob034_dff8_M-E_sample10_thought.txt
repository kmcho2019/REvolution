Instead of directly using a single always block to capture all 8 D flip-flops, I will instantiate eight separate D flip-flop modules and connect them in parallel. Each DFF module will be a positive edge-triggered flip-flop capturing one bit of input d to the corresponding bit of q. This modular approach improves code clarity and reusability, making each flip-flop explicitly defined and isolated. It also allows potential reuse of the DFF module elsewhere or easier modifications such as adding asynchronous reset later. The top module will simply instantiate and connect eight DFFs for each bit.
