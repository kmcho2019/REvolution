To optimize the design for better PPA (performance, power, and area), we can avoid the overhead of instantiating eight separate DFF modules and instead use a single always block with an 8-bit register that captures all bits in parallel on the clock's positive edge. This reduces module instantiation overhead (area), simplifies the code, and maintains parallelism for performance. Additionally, initializing 'q' to zero on simulation start improves simulation consistency. No reset signal is specified, so initialization is done with an initial block for simulation only.
