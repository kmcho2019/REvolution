I will refactor the code to use an explicit instantiation of eight single-bit D flip-flop submodules within TopModule instead of a single always block with a vector register. Each D flip-flop will be a separate module instance triggered by the positive clock edge, capturing one bit of the input vector `d` to the corresponding bit of output `q`. This structural approach explicitly models eight parallel DFFs and clearly shows the internal hardware composition. The behavioral functionality remains the same, but the hierarchy is clearer and the design becomes more modular. This also makes it easy to reuse or customize individual flip-flops if needed.
