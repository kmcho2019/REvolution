To optimize for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) without altering functionality, I will add a clock enable signal that only updates `q` when the input `d` changes. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the flip-flops, lowering dynamic power consumption. The clock enable logic will be implemented as a simple combinational comparator between `q` and `d`, generating an enable signal used inside the clocked always block. This optimization is lightweight and synthesis-friendly, and can help reduce switching activity, improving power. The implementation remains a single always block for better timing and area.
