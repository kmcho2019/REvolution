To optimize for power and potentially reduce unnecessary toggling, I will add a clock enable signal internally that only updates the output register `q` if the input `d` changes from its current stored value. This conditional update reduces switching activity when the input is stable, lowering dynamic power consumption. The timing and functionality remain the same, capturing `d` on the rising edge of `clk` but only when the input changes.

The change does not add complexity in terms of clock domains or asynchronous logic, maintaining good timing and area efficiency while enhancing power characteristics.

This approach assumes that the toolchain can infer clock enable from the conditional inside the clocked always block, which most modern synthesis tools support.
