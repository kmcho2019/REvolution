To address synthesis and simulation mismatches, I will remove the initialization from the port declaration and add an explicit synchronous active-high reset input. This reset will initialize the output register `q` to zero on the clock's positive edge when reset is asserted. This approach improves synthesis tool compatibility, ensures deterministic initial conditions, and aligns with common FPGA design practices. The rest of the design remains minimal, simply capturing `d` into `q` on the positive clock edge when reset is not asserted.
