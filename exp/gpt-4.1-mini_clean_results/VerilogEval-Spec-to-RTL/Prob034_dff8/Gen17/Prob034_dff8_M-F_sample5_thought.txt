To address the feedback, I will simplify the design by implementing all eight D flip-flops as a single vector register updated synchronously at the positive edge of the clock within one always block. This avoids instantiating separate modules, reducing routing complexity and potentially improving power, performance, and area (PPA). The output `q` will be declared as reg and assigned inside the always block. Since the original problem does not specify a reset, I will not include one, but this style ensures the initial state is well defined by the simulator or synthesis tool's reset behavior if desired.
