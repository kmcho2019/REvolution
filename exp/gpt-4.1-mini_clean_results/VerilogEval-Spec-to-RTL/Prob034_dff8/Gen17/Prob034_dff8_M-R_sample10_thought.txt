To implement the same functionality differently while preserving the behavior of 8 D flip-flops triggered on the positive clock edge, I will use an explicit instantiation of eight single-bit D flip-flop modules within TopModule. Each flip-flop will take one bit of input `d[i]` and produce one bit of output `q[i]`. This approach modularizes the design and explicitly shows the hardware structure, rather than using a single always block with a vector assignment. 

Each D flip-flop module will use an always block triggered on `posedge clk` to store its input bit. Then, the TopModule simply instantiates eight of these and connects them to corresponding bits of `d` and `q`. This structural style clarifies the hardware mapping and is functionally equivalent to the original solution.
