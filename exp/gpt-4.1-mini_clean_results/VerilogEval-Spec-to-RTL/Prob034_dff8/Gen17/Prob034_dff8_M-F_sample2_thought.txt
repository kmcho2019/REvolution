To address the feedback and optimize the design for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the eight individual DFF module instantiations with a single always block that implements an 8-bit register capturing the input vector `d` on the positive edge of `clk`. This approach uses one vector register, reducing module instantiation overhead and routing complexity, which helps synthesis tools optimize timing and area better. It also simplifies the code and avoids potential initialization or synchronization issues with multiple submodules.
