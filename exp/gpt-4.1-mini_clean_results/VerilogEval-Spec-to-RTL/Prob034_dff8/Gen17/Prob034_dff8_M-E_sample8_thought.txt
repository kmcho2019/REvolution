To provide a clear and synthesis-friendly design, I will implement the TopModule with 8 individual D flip-flop instances, each storing one bit of the input `d`. This structural approach explicitly models each flip-flop, making the design modular and clear. To handle initialization, I will include an active-high synchronous reset input named `rst` that clears all flip-flops when asserted. This reset ensures known starting conditions and compatibility with synthesis tools, avoiding reliance on register initialization at declaration. The flip-flops capture their respective bits of `d` on the positive clock edge when reset is inactive.
