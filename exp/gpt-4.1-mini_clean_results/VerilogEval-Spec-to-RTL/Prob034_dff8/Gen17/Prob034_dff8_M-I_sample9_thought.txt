To optimize for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the eight separate DFF module instantiations with a single always block that registers the entire 8-bit input `d` into an 8-bit register `q` on the positive clock edge. This reduces module overhead, simplifies routing, and allows synthesis tools to better optimize the design internally by inferring an 8-bit vector of flip-flops instead of multiple separate instances. The behavior remains identical, with `q` capturing `d` at every rising clock edge. This approach also avoids any initial simulation mismatches due to implicit initialization of `q`.
