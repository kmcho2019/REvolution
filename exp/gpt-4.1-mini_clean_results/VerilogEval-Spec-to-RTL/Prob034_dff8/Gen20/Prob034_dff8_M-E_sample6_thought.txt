Implement the 8-bit register by instantiating eight individual 1-bit D flip-flop modules. Each DFF is clocked by the same clock signal, and each stores one bit of the input vector `d`. This modular approach explicitly models each flip-flop as a separate entity, demonstrating structural design and potentially allowing per-bit customization or extension. The outputs of the individual flip-flops are concatenated to form the 8-bit output vector `q`.
