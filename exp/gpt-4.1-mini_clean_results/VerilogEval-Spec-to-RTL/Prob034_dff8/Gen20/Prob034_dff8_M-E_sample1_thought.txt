Instead of instantiating eight separate single-bit D flip-flops, I will implement the 8-bit register as a single always block using an 8-bit reg to hold the output `q`. On each positive edge of the clock, the entire 8-bit input `d` will be latched into `q`. This approach uses a single register variable for all bits, making the design more concise and efficient in terms of synthesis and resource utilization while satisfying all interface and functionality requirements.
