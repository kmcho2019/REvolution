Implement the TopModule using a single always block with an 8-bit register to store the captured data from the input vector `d`. This approach uses a vector register rather than multiple instances of a DFF module, leading to simpler and more compact code. All bits are latched simultaneously on the positive edge of the clock. This method is efficient for synthesis and typical in real designs, improving maintainability and potentially reducing area and power compared to separate flip-flop instantiations.
