Since the design requires 8 D flip-flops triggered on the positive edge of the clock, the core behavior is inherently sequential and cannot be implemented purely with continuous assignments. To provide a different structural style while preserving the functional behavior, I will implement the 8 D flip-flops as individual modules instantiated inside the TopModule. Each DFF module will take one bit of input `d`, clock `clk`, and output one bit of `q`. This breaks the vector logic into discrete components and instantiates 8 of them, preserving the synchronous positive edge triggering and register functionality without using a single always block on the vector.
