I will implement the 8 D flip-flops as individual single-bit always blocks, each triggered on the positive edge of clk. This explicitly models each flip-flop separately and ensures a clear and direct correspondence between each bit of the input `d` and the output `q`. This approach also clearly shows how each bit is latched independently on the clock edge, making the design modular and easy to understand.

No reset or clock enable logic will be added, strictly following the problem statement. The output register `q` will be declared as a reg type and updated synchronously with the clock.

This architectural style differs from a vectorized assignment inside a single always block, and helps to illustrate the parallelism of flip-flops in hardware.
