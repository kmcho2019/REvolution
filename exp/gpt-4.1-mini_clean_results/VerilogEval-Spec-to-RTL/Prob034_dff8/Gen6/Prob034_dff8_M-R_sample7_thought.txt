To refactor the design while keeping the same core functionality of capturing 8 bits on the positive clock edge, we can use a generate-for loop to instantiate eight individual 1-bit D flip-flops. Each flip-flop is a simple always block triggered by posedge clk, capturing one bit of input d into the corresponding bit of output q. This modular approach makes it explicit that there are eight separate flip-flops and avoids a single multi-bit register, providing clearer structural clarity while preserving synchronous behavior. The outputs q are declared as reg here since they are driven in always blocks.
