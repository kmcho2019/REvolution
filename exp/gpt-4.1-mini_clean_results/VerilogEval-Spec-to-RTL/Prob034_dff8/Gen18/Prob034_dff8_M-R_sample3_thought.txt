This removes direct combinational comparison inside the clocked process, avoiding potential synthesis mismatches.

1. On each clock edge, compare d and q and register the result as a clock enable signal (en).
2. In the next clock cycle, if en is asserted, load q with d.
This two-stage approach breaks the combinational feedback path and separates enable logic from data loading, improving synthesis clarity.
