To address the initial mismatch in simulation, I will add an asynchronous active-high reset input `rst` to initialize the output register `q` to a known state (all zeros) on reset. This ensures that `q` starts at a defined value rather than undefined, eliminating simulation mismatches caused by initial unknown states. The reset will asynchronously clear `q`, but data capture will still occur on the positive edge of `clk` when `rst` is deasserted. This approach is standard in RTL design and is often required by testbenches to avoid unknown initial states. All other design aspects remain the same for optimal PPA.
