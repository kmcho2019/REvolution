To strictly implement 8 D flip-flops capturing input 'd' on every positive edge of 'clk' without gating or enable logic, I will use a straightforward always block triggered on `posedge clk`. To provide a different structure from the previous code, instead of directly using a procedural assignment inside the always block, I will create a register that is continuously assigned to output `q`. The new design will declare an intermediate register `q_reg` updated on `posedge clk`, and the output `q` will be driven by a continuous assignment from `q_reg`. This cleanly separates storage and output logic while maintaining the same fundamental functionality as required.
