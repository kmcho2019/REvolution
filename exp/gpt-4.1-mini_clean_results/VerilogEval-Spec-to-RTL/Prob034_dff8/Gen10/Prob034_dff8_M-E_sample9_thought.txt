I will implement the TopModule by instantiating eight separate single-bit D flip-flop modules, each triggered on the positive edge of clk. Each DFF module will have a synchronous reset input to initialize the outputs to zero. The TopModule will include a synchronous reset input, which will allow controlled initialization of all 8 flip-flops together.

This modular approach explicitly models the 8 D flip-flops as distinct units, making the design clear and easy to verify, as well as fully synthesizable. The synchronous reset ensures defined initial states without relying on initial blocks.
