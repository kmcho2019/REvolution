The root cause of the functional issue is the conditional update inside the clocked always block. For proper D flip-flop behavior, the output register `q` must unconditionally capture the input `d` at every rising edge of `clk`. Introducing a condition to update only when `q` differs from `d` breaks synchronous behavior and causes incorrect simulation results. Therefore, to fix the design, simply remove the conditional check and assign `q <= d` on every positive clock edge. This ensures that all 8 D flip-flops trigger correctly and synchronously latch the input.
