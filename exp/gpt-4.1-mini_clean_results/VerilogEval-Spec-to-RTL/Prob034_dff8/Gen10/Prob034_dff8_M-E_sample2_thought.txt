I will implement the TopModule by instantiating eight individual 1-bit D flip-flop submodules, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock. This explicit instantiation approach clearly models each flip-flop separately and satisfies the requirement of having eight distinct DFFs.

Each flip-flop submodule will have a simple interface: input clk, input d, output q. Inside the TopModule, a generate block will create eight instances of this DFF module, connecting each bit of the 8-bit input and output vectors appropriately.

This method increases modularity and readability for designs that require per-bit DFF instances, and it also explicitly demonstrates the presence of eight flip-flops rather than relying on synthesis inference.

No reset or initialization is added, keeping the design simple and synthesizable. The DFFs use non-blocking assignments and positive-edge clock triggering to ensure proper synchronous behavior.

This design style is useful for educational purposes and can also facilitate bitwise control or modification if expanded later.
