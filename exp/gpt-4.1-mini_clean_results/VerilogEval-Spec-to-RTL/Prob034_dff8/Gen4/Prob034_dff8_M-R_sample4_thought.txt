Since D flip-flops require edge-triggered behavior, we cannot use continuous assignments (`assign`) to replace the clocked process. However, we can instantiate 8 separate D flip-flop modules, each capturing one bit of the input `d` on the rising clock edge and outputting to `q`. This structural approach explicitly shows the eight DFFs and matches the same functionality but implemented differently. It helps illustrate the one-bit flip-flop behavior in a modular way while preserving the overall functionality of an 8-bit register.
