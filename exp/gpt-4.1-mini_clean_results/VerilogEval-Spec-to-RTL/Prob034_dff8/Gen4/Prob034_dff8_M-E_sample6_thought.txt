I will implement the TopModule by explicitly instantiating eight single-bit D flip-flop submodules, each handling one bit of the input vector 'd'. This hierarchical approach models each flip-flop separately, which is more modular and makes the design scalable and easy to extend for additional features per flip-flop if needed.

Each DFF submodule will have inputs clk and d, and output q, and will update q on the rising edge of clk. The top-level module will instantiate eight such DFF modules, connecting each bit of 'd' and 'q' individually.

This approach shows clear hierarchical design and component reuse, which can be beneficial for learning and debugging, even though it is less concise than a vectorized approach.
