To optimize for power and performance, add an enable signal that updates the register only when the input `d` changes. This avoids unnecessary toggling of flip-flops when `d` remains constant, reducing dynamic power. The enable is generated by comparing `d` and the current output `q` within a combinational always block. The clocking and synchronous behavior remain intact, preserving performance. This small gating is area-efficient and improves power without adding complex gating or clock domain crossing.
