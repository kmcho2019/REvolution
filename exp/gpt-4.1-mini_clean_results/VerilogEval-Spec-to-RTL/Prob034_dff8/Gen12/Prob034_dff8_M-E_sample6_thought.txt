Implement the 8-bit register by instantiating eight separate D flip-flop submodules, each handling one bit of the input and output. This modular approach ensures clarity in design and makes the structure explicit, as each flip-flop independently captures its bit of `d` on the positive edge of `clk`. It meets the specification to have 8 DFFs clocked on the rising edge of `clk`. This method also facilitates easier scalability and testing of individual flip-flops.
