To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we can implement clock gating to enable the flip-flops only when the input data 'd' changes. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the flip-flops and minimizes dynamic power consumption. Performance remains the same since the flip-flops still trigger on the clock's positive edge, and area overhead is minimal due to simple clock gating logic. Since no load enable or reset signals are specified, clock gating is the best approach to reduce power without impacting functionality or complexity.
