To improve the original solution's mismatch issue at the beginning of simulation, I will add an asynchronous active-low reset input that initializes the 8-bit output register 'q' to zero. This ensures a known initial state and eliminates unknown ('X') values that may cause simulation mismatches. This reset also improves simulation correctness without affecting the core functionality or increasing area significantly.

From a PPA perspective:

- Performance: No critical path increase since reset is asynchronous and synchronous logic remains unchanged.
- Power: Having a reset does not increase switching during normal operation.
- Area: Minimal area overhead for the reset logic.

This change enhances correctness and simulation consistency without compromising the original requirements.
