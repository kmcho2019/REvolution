Instead of instantiating eight individual single-bit DFF modules, I will implement the TopModule using a single always block with an 8-bit register that captures the entire 8-bit input vector 'd' on the rising edge of 'clk'. This vectorized approach directly models the 8 flip-flops as bits of one register, which is a common and efficient practice in RTL coding.

This approach reduces module instantiations, simplifies the design, and is easier for synthesis tools to optimize for timing, power, and area, while still clearly modeling eight flip-flops triggered on the positive edge of the clock.

Additionally, I will make the output port 'q' a reg and drive it directly from the internal register to keep the code clean and concise.
