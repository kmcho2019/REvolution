Instead of using a single 8-bit register to store all flip-flops collectively, instantiate eight individual D flip-flop submodules, each handling one bit of the input. This hierarchical approach models each flip-flop explicitly, making the design modular and scalable. The TopModule simply connects the clock and corresponding data bits to each DFF instance and collects the outputs into the 8-bit vector `q`.

This method clearly demonstrates the use of multiple instances to form a wider register and ensures that each bit is captured on the positive edge of `clk`. Such a structural design makes the flip-flops' behavior transparent and separate, which may aid formal verification and hardware understanding.
