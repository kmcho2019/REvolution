Since the original design requires edge-triggered D flip-flops capturing the input `d` at every positive clock edge, it cannot be done purely with continuous `assign` statements. However, to refactor with a different structure while keeping the same functionality, I will instantiate eight separate single-bit D flip-flop modules, each triggered by the positive clock edge and capturing one bit of `d` into the corresponding bit of `q`. This modular approach explicitly shows each flip-flop, emphasizing parallelism and clarity. It avoids conditional logic and ensures `q` updates on every clock edge unconditionally, fulfilling the original specification differently.
