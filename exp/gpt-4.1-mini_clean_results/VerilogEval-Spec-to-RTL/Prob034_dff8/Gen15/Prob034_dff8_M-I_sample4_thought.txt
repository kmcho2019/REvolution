To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will consolidate the eight individual D flip-flops into a single 8-bit register inside one always block. This reduces module overhead and can help the synthesis tool generate more optimal hardware by treating the 8-bit register as a single entity rather than 8 separate flip-flops. This approach will simplify routing, minimize control signal redundancy, and improve area and potentially power consumption by reducing clock load.

The functional behavior remains identical: on each positive edge of clk, the 8-bit input d is captured into the 8-bit output q.

This solution maintains clarity and correctness while improving PPA metrics compared to instantiating eight separate DFF modules.
