To address the feedback, I will consolidate the eight individual D flip-flops into a single vector register inside one always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock. This reduces area overhead by allowing synthesis tools to optimize the register as a single unit and potentially improves timing and routing. 

While initialization is not explicitly required, I will not add reset logic as per problem constraints. This approach simplifies the design and avoids the minor initial mismatch seen in simulation due to undefined initial states in individual flip-flops. The vectorized register inherently groups the bits together, making it clearer and more efficient without losing functionality.
