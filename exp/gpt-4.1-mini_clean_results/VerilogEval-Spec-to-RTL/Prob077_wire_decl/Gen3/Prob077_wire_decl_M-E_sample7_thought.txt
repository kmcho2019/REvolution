Instead of using explicit wires to connect the AND gates to the OR gate, I will use continuous assignments to intermediate wires declared with the 'wire' keyword. The intermediate wires will be assigned the output of the AND operations via assign statements, demonstrating a dataflow style within the structural approach. Then the OR gate is instantiated using the intermediate wires as inputs to produce 'out'. The 'out_n' output will be generated by inverting 'out' using a continuous assign statement rather than a gate instantiation.
