To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, and area), I will reduce the gate count and wiring overhead by expressing the logic using continuous assignments with built-in Verilog operators instead of instantiating separate gate modules. This avoids the overhead of module instances and intermediate wires, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the logic into minimal gate-level implementations. I will still declare the intermediate wires explicitly for clarity and adhere to the requirement of having two intermediate wires (for the two AND gate outputs) feeding into the OR gate output wire. The inverted output will be generated directly by a NOT gate primitive to keep the design purely structural and minimize power compared to using assign statements. This hybrid style balances clarity with efficiency.
