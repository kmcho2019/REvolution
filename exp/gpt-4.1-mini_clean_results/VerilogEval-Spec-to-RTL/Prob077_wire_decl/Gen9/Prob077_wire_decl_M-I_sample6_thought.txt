To optimize for area and power while preserving the exact functionality, I will replace the explicit gate module instantiations with a single continuous assignment implementing the logic expression directly. This eliminates intermediate wires and module instantiations, enabling synthesis tools to produce more optimized gate-level implementations. The inverted output will remain a continuous assignment for simplicity and minimal delay. This approach reduces code complexity and potential overhead from gate module instantiations, leading to better PPA without altering the logical behavior.
