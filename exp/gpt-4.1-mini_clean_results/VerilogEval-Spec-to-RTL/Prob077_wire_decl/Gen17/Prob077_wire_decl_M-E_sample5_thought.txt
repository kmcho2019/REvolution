Instead of using separate gate modules or purely structural gate primitives, I will implement the logic using continuous assignment statements with intermediate wires. I will declare two intermediate wires for the AND results explicitly, then assign them using continuous assignments representing the AND of inputs. The final OR output will be assigned by OR-ing these intermediate wires. The inverted output will be generated by an explicit continuous assignment using the negation of the OR output wire. This approach keeps the design flat and concise, making use of the structural nature of continuous assignments to model combinational logic clearly, without instantiating submodules or primitives. It also respects the one-driver-per-wire rule and explicitly uses named intermediate wires for clarity.
