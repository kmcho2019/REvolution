To optimize the existing solution for better PPA metrics, remove explicit gate module instantiations and replace them with built-in gate primitives for OR and NOT gates. Keep the AND gates as continuous assignments since they are simple bitwise operations and the continuous assignment style reduces overhead and aids synthesis optimization.

This approach maintains the required intermediate wires, ensuring clarity and single-source driven nets, but reduces module instantiation overhead which can improve synthesis efficiency, reduce area slightly, and potentially lower power due to simpler netlist.

Using built-in primitives also preserves structural clarity and allows synthesis tools to better optimize delays and resource sharing, improving overall PPA without changing functionality.
