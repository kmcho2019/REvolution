To optimize for better power and area without sacrificing clarity or correctness, I will eliminate the explicit NotGate module instantiation and instead implement the inverted output as a continuous assignment using a simple bitwise NOT operation. This reduces module hierarchy, removes a module boundary, and can improve synthesis optimization and area utilization. I will keep the two intermediate wires for the AND gate outputs as per the original requirement to maintain clear signal separation and single-source driving of wires. The design remains purely combinational and is straightforward for synthesis tools to optimize.
