Refactor the design to a purely behavioral style using continuous assignments without separate gate modules. Declare intermediate wires explicitly for the AND gate outputs to maintain clarity and one source driving each wire. The OR and NOT operations are also done with continuous assignments. This eliminates the overhead of separate gate modules while keeping the same structure: two AND outputs combined by OR, and an inverted output from the OR result.
