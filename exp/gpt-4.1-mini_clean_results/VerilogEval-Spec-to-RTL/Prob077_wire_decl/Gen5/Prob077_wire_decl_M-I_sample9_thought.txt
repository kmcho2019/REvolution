To optimize for PPA, I will reduce the number of module instantiations by using built-in gate primitives directly in TopModule instead of separate module wrappers. This eliminates the overhead of extra modules and potential additional routing. Also, I'll keep the intermediate wires to maintain clarity and ensure each wire has a single driver, which aids synthesis. The inverted output will be generated using a built-in not gate primitive instead of a continuous assign, which can be more efficiently mapped to hardware. This approach reduces area and power while keeping the same logical structure and clarity.
