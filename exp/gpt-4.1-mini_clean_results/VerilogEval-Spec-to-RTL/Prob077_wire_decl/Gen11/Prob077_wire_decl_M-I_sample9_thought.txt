To optimize the original modular design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will flatten the hierarchy by removing the separate AndGate and OrGate modules and instantiate primitive gates directly within TopModule. This reduces instance overhead and enables synthesis tools to better optimize the netlist by treating the logic as a single combinational block. I will keep the two intermediate wires as required and maintain clear naming and a fully structural style using gate primitives. The inverted output will be derived directly from the OR output with a primitive NOT gate. This approach preserves functionality and clarity while improving PPA.
