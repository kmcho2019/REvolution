To optimize the design for better power, area, and possibly slightly better timing, I will simplify the hierarchy by removing the separate gate modules and instantiate the gate primitives directly inside TopModule. This reduces module overhead and can help synthesis tools optimize better. The two intermediate wires will still be declared explicitly as required, maintaining clarity and meeting the problem constraints. The inverted output will remain assigned via a continuous assignment for simplicity and clean separation. This flattening reduces structural complexity without compromising readability much and should slightly reduce area and power.
