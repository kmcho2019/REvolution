Combine the modular clarity and reusability of explicit AND and OR gate modules with the synthesis-friendly use of Verilog built-in primitives directly instantiated for the gates (without extra overhead of user-defined modules). Declare two clearly named intermediate wires for the AND outputs as required. Use a continuous assignment for the inverted output for minimal overhead, cleaner code, and better synthesis optimization. This fusion keeps the design hierarchy simple by avoiding extra module definitions, satisfies all wiring requirements, and produces clean, readable, and synthesis-efficient RTL.
