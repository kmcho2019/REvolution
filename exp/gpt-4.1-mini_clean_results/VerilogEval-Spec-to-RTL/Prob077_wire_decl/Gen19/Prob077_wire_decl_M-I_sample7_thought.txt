To optimize for power and area while maintaining the same logical function and clear wire usage, replace the instantiated AndGate and OrGate modules with continuous assignments using built-in operators. This reduces the number of module instances and wiring overhead, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the logic more aggressively.

We keep the explicit intermediate wires to maintain signal clarity and ensure each wire is driven by exactly one source, as requested. The inverted output `out_n` remains a continuous assignment of the inversion of `out`. This design reduces gate instantiation overhead, potentially lowering area and power while keeping zero timing slack.

Overall, this approach simplifies the design without sacrificing readability or correctness, and should yield better PPA metrics by leveraging built-in operators and straightforward continuous assignments.
