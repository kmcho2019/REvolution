To optimize for better power and area, I will flatten the hierarchy by removing the separate AndGate and OrGate modules and directly instantiate the primitive gates inside TopModule. This reduces module instance overhead, minimizes routing complexity, and enables synthesis tools to optimize the logic more aggressively. I will still declare exactly two intermediate wires for the AND outputs as required, maintaining clarity and correctness. The inverted output is directly derived from the OR output using a NOT gate primitive. This approach preserves full functionality, ensures each wire has a single driver, and results in a simpler and more area- and power-efficient netlist.
