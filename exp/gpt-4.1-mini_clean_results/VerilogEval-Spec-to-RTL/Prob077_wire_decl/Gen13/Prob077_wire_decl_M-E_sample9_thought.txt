Implement the design entirely using continuous assignment statements with explicitly declared intermediate wires. This avoids using gate-level module instantiations and instead leverages the built-in Verilog primitives directly with continuous assignments for AND, OR, and NOT operations. The intermediate wires clearly separate the AND operations from the OR, preserving modularity while keeping the design concise. The inverted output is directly assigned as the negation of the OR output wire. This approach is simpler and flattens hierarchy, potentially reducing synthesis overhead while still meeting all interface and naming requirements.
