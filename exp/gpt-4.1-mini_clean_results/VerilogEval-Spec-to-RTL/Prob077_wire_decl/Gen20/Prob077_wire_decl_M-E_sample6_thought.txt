I will implement the TopModule with a fully flat design, using only Verilog built-in gate primitives directly inside the module. To meet the requirement of two intermediate wires connecting the AND and OR gates, I will name these wires distinctly. Instead of instantiating submodules, the AND gates and the OR gate will be instantiated as gate primitives with continuous connections. The output 'out_n' will be derived by a NOT primitive directly driven from 'out'. This approach eliminates hierarchy for better synthesis optimization and reduces module overhead, while maintaining clear naming for easy debugging. The design remains fully structural and directly reflects the gate-level logic.
