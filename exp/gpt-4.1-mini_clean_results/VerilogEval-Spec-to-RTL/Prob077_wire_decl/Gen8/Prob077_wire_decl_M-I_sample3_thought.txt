The original design is clear and modular but instantiates separate gate modules which adds hierarchy and may slightly increase area and power. To optimize for PPA, I will remove these extra modules and implement the logic using gate primitives directly inside TopModule. This eliminates module boundary overhead and allows the synthesis tool to optimize better. I will still use two intermediate wires for the AND gate outputs, meeting the problem requirement, and then OR them with an or-gate primitive. The inverted output will remain a continuous assignment for clarity and optimal synthesis. This approach reduces area and power while keeping the design fully combinational and readable.
