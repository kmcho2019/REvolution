To improve PPA, remove the separate gate module instantiations and instead use built-in Verilog primitives directly with continuous assignments, preserving the intermediate wires for clarity and the single-source driving rule. This eliminates module instantiation overhead, reduces netlist complexity, and allows synthesis tools to better optimize the logic, which should improve area and power while keeping performance at its minimum since the logic depth is unchanged. The inversion of out is still done with a primitive 'not' gate for clarity, but as a gate primitive rather than a module instantiation.
