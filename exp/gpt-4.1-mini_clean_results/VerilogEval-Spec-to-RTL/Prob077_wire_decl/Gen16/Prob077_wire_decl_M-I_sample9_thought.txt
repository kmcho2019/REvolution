To optimize PPA without sacrificing structural clarity, we will remove the explicit gate modules and use built-in Verilog gate primitives directly. This approach reduces module instantiation overhead, allowing synthesis tools to better optimize the design for area and power. We will keep the intermediate wires to maintain readability and structural clarity and drive all wires from exactly one source. The inverted output will be created with a simple 'not' primitive directly on 'out', keeping the design fully structural and easily synthesizable. This simplifies the design hierarchy, potentially reducing area and power while maintaining minimal propagation delay.
