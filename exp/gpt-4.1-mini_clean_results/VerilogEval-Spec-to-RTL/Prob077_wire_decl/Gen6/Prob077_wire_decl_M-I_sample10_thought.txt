To optimize for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will simplify the design by removing the separate module instantiations for AND and OR gates and instead use built-in gate primitives directly. This reduces the hierarchy and potential overhead. Also, I'll keep two intermediate wires for the AND outputs as required and one wire for the OR output connected directly to the output port. The NOT gate for out_n will be implemented using a built-in primitive as well for uniformity. This approach simplifies the netlist and minimizes cell count, improving area and potentially power with minimal effect on timing.
