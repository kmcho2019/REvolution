Implement the TopModule purely with built-in Verilog logic operators to reduce module hierarchy and area. Declare two intermediate wires to hold the outputs of the two AND operations explicitly, then assign the OR of these wires to 'out' and the inverted 'out' to 'out_n' using continuous assignments. This keeps wiring explicit and simple, satisfies the exact requirement for two intermediate wires, and avoids instantiating separate gate modules or primitives. The design remains fully combinational, clean, and synthesis-friendly.
