The existing solution uses simple addition of three 1-bit inputs to produce a 2-bit population count, which is optimal for such a small input width. Since the synthesis results show minimal delay, power, and area, there is little room for improvement in PPA by changing the logic structure. Therefore, the best improvement is to keep the simple addition but implement it inside an always_comb block with a reg output to clarify the combinational intent, which might improve the design's readability and synthesis predictability.
