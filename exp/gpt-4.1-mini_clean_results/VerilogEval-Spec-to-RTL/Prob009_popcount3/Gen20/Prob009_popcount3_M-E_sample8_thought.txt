Implement the population count using a combination of partial sums: first sum two bits and then add the third bit using a small two-level hierarchy of 1-bit adders. This avoids using a direct '+' operator on the 3-bit vector and instead uses explicit bitwise logic by implementing two half-adders. The first half-adder sums the first two bits producing a 2-bit sum, and the second half-adder adds the third bit to the least significant bit of the first sum, with carry handled accordingly. This approach demonstrates hierarchical and modular design while remaining combinational and efficient for a 3-bit population count.
