Implement the population count using a hierarchical approach with intermediate partial sums computed by a small combinational tree of adders. First, add the two least significant bits to get a partial sum, then add the most significant bit to this partial sum. This modular approach can improve clarity and potentially enable better synthesis optimization in some toolflows by explicitly showing addition steps rather than a direct sum expression. The output remains 2 bits as the maximum count of ones in a 3-bit input is 3.
