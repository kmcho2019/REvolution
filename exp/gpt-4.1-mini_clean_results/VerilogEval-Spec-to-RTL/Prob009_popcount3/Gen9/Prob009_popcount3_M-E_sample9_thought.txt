Implement the population count by explicitly using bitwise logic gates to count the number of ones. For three bits, the count can be computed by combinational logic using simple boolean expressions. Specifically, the least significant output bit (out[0]) can be computed as the parity (XOR) of the three inputs, and the most significant output bit (out[1]) can be determined by majority logic (at least two inputs set). This gate-level approach avoids addition operations and demonstrates a structural style, which is different from arithmetic summation. It is also fully combinational and easily synthesized.
