To improve PPA, I will replace the addition-based population count with explicit combinational boolean logic that directly encodes the count of set bits from the 3 input bits. This avoids the use of adders and intermediate vectors, which can save area and reduce power by minimizing gate usage and switching activity.

The 3-bit population count can be implemented as:

- out[0] = in[0] XOR in[1] XOR in[2]
- out[1] = majority(in[0], in[1], in[2]) = (in[0]&in[1]) | (in[1]&in[2]) | (in[0]&in[2])

This implementation is minimal, combinational, and maps naturally to small LUTs in synthesis, improving power and area compared to the previous adder approach while maintaining zero timing slack and no functionality changes.
