The original solution is optimal for this simple 3-bit population count in terms of performance and area, as it only sums three 1-bit signals directly. To slightly improve power and area, I will implement the population count using explicit bitwise operations and a minimal combinational logic approach, avoiding implicit addition operators. This may help synthesis tools map to simpler gates like OR/AND gates instead of adders, which can reduce power and area slightly. The logic is: out[0] = parity (odd count of ones) of the 3 bits, and out[1] = at least two bits set. This approach is more structural and explicit.
