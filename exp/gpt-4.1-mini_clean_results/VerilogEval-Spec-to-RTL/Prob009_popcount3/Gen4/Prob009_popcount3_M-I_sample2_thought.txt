To improve the PPA metrics, especially power and area, I will simplify the logic for out[1] (which detects if at least two bits are set). The original logic uses three 2-input AND gates followed by a 3-input OR gate. Instead, I will sum the bits to a 2-bit value and compare if the sum is greater than or equal to 2, which can be implemented with fewer gates since the sum for 3 bits ranges from 0 to 3. Using a simple bitwise addition and direct comparison can reduce gate count and switching activity. This approach also retains the out[0] parity output as is, which is already minimal. Overall, this method leverages basic arithmetic relations rather than enumerating pairwise products, potentially lowering switching and gate usage.
