The original solution uses explicit combinational logic with XOR and a three-term majority function, which is already minimal and efficient for a 3-bit population count. To optimize further for PPA (performance, power, area):

- We can use a simple addition of the bits instead of explicitly coding parity and majority. Synthesizers are very good at optimizing adders and can implement a 3-bit population count with potentially fewer gates by leveraging carry logic efficiently.
- This approach may reduce power by using fewer logic transitions and gates since it uses a single 3-bit addition instead of separate logic gates for parity and majority.
- Area may also be reduced due to the straightforward summation rather than multiple gate-level logic operations.

Overall, summing the bits directly and assigning the output bits from the sum is a compact and scalable approach with similar or better PPA compared to the original explicit logic version.
