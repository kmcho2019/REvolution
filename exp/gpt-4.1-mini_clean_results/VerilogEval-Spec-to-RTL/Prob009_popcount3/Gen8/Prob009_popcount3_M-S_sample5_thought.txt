For a 3-bit input, the population count result fits in 2 bits. Since the population count only ranges from 0 to 3, we can simplify the 2-bit output logic to straightforward combinational logic rather than performing addition. Specifically, out[0] is the parity (XOR) of the three bits, and out[1] indicates if at least two bits are set (majority logic). Implementing these directly reduces logic complexity without needing an adder.
