I will implement the TopModule by directly using continuous assign statements for the outputs without intermediate wires or parameterized modules. Each output will be expressed as a Boolean expression composed of basic AND and OR operations using bitwise operators and parentheses. This keeps the design minimal, simple, and highly readable, while avoiding any extra wiring or module instantiations. It is a straightforward combinational logic expression that exactly reflects the 7458 chip's functionality: p1y is OR of two 3-input ANDs, p2y is OR of two 2-input ANDs. This approach minimizes area and power and is easy for synthesis tools to optimize directly.
