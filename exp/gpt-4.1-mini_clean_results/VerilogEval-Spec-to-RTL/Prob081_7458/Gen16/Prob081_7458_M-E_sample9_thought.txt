Implement the TopModule by directly using Verilog continuous assignment statements that leverage built-in multi-input bitwise operators to express the entire logic concisely and clearly. Instead of decomposing the 3-input AND gates into smaller 2-input AND gate modules, directly express them as three-input AND expressions (p1a & p1b & p1c, etc.). Similarly, use two-input AND expressions for p2 outputs.

Then compute the OR outputs by OR-ing those intermediate expressions inline, without introducing intermediate wires or submodules. This approach maximizes code simplicity, reduces module overhead, and is synthesis-friendly. The logic mapping matches the 7458 chip exactly, and the direct assign style makes it easy to read and efficient in hardware.

This design is flat, fully combinational, with no internal wires or submodules, making it minimal, elegant, and optimal for synthesis tools.
