Implement the TopModule by directly using assign statements for each individual AND gate output without any intermediate module, and then OR the corresponding AND gate outputs to form the final outputs. This flat combinational approach uses no separate modules, keeping the design concise and straightforward. Each AND gate is represented explicitly as a simple bitwise AND expression, and OR outputs are direct bitwise ORs of these results. This minimizes hierarchy, simplifies the design, and may enhance synthesis optimization due to the absence of parameterization or module overhead.
