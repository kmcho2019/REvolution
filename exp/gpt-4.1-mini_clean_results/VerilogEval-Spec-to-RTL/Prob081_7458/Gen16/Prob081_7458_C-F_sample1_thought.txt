Fuse the modularity and clarity of separate submodules for the triple- and double-input AND-OR logic (from Example 1) with the efficiency and scalability of parameterized reduction AND gates (from Example 2) to produce a clean, reusable, and maintainable design. Define two submodules—TripleAndOr and DoubleAndOr—each using parameterized AndGateN modules internally for the AND gates. The TopModule then instantiates these submodules and connects inputs and outputs.

This approach combines:
- Clear separation of functionality into logical units (enhances readability, reusability, and testing).
- Parameterized AND gate implementations to avoid code duplication and allow scalability.
- Direct, simple assign statements in submodules to combine AND outputs into the OR output.
- Minimal intermediate wiring to keep the design straightforward and synthesis-friendly.

Such fusion leads to a design that is modular, concise, and efficient, with hierarchical clarity and parametric flexibility, offering an excellent balance between code maintainability and logic optimization.
