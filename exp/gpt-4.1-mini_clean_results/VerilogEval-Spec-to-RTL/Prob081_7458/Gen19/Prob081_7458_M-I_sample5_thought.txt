Optimize the design for better power and area by eliminating the hierarchical And2 and And3 modules and using direct continuous assignments with built-in bitwise operators.

This reduces the number of instantiated modules and intermediate wires, minimizing gate count and switching activity, which improves power and area metrics. It also slightly improves timing by reducing logic depth.

The outputs p1y and p2y will be directly assigned from the bitwise ANDs and ORs of their respective inputs in a clear, concise, and fully synthesizable manner.
