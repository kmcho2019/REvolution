To fuse the best ideas from both examples, the approach is to keep the clarity and modular decomposition of the design, but avoid unnecessary module instantiations that add overhead. Instead of separate modules for each AND gate, declare intermediate wires explicitly inside TopModule for each AND gate output. Then, assign these intermediate wires using simple continuous assignments. Finally, assign the outputs as ORs of those intermediate AND results. This retains the explicit gate-level clarity seen in Example 2, aiding understanding and testing, while using direct assign statements to minimize overhead and power as in Example 1. This gives a good balance of readability, maintainability, low power, and minimal area without extra hierarchy or interconnect.
