Fuse modularity and clarity of the parameterized AndGateN module with a flat, minimal-complexity approach by:
- Instantiating parameterized AND gates for each required group of inputs for clear, reusable code.
- Avoiding unnecessary intermediate wires by directly connecting AND gate outputs to the OR expressions.
- Combining OR operations into single assign statements per output to minimize logic depth.
- Keeping explicit intermediate wires only for AND gate outputs to maintain readability and synthesis friendliness.
- Leveraging simple combinational logic (reduction AND and OR) for clean, concise, and efficient implementation.

This fusion results in a design that is modular, easy to maintain, synthesizable with minimal logic levels, and slightly optimized for area and power by avoiding extra nets or hierarchies beyond the parameterized AND gate instances.
