To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), flatten the design hierarchy by removing submodules and parameterized reduction gates. Implement all AND and OR gates directly with simple assign statements in TopModule. This reduces module instantiation overhead, simplifies logic for synthesis, and helps the synthesizer generate minimal and efficient gate-level implementations. Using explicit bitwise AND/OR directly also minimizes switching activity and gate count, especially since input widths are fixed and small.
