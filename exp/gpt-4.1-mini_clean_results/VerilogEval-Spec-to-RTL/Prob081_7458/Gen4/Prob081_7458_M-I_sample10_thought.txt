To improve power and area while maintaining minimal delay, I will introduce intermediate wires for the outputs of the four AND gates. This lets synthesis tools better share and optimize the logic. Though more lines are added, explicit intermediate signals may enable better gate sharing and reduce redundant computations, potentially lowering switching activity and area slightly. The outputs then become simple OR gates combining these intermediate signals. This explicit structure is also more readable and aligns with the internal structure of the original 7458 chip, possibly aiding technology mapping and optimization.
