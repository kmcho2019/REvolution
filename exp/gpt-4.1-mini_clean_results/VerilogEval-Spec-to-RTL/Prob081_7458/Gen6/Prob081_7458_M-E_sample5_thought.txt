Implement the 7458 functionality using a hierarchical approach by defining two separate submodules: one for the triple-input AND-OR logic for p1y and another for the double-input AND-OR logic for p2y. Each submodule internally computes the AND gates and the OR output. The TopModule then instantiates these two submodules and connects the inputs and outputs accordingly. This modular style enhances reuse and clarity by separating the logic corresponding to the two output blocks, while also allowing each submodule to be independently tested or replaced.
