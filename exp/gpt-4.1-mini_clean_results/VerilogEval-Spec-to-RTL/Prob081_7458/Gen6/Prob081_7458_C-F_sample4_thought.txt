To combine the modularity and reusability of the parameterized AND gate module from Example 1 with the straightforward, concise wiring style from Example 2, I will implement the TopModule using explicit internal wires for the four AND gate outputs as in Example 2, but drive those wires using instances of a parameterized AndGateN module from Example 1.

This fusion captures the clarity and directness of explicit intermediate signals while leveraging modular, reusable AND gate instances for better code structure and potential scalability. The OR logic is implemented with simple continuous assignments for minimal overhead.

This approach keeps the design clean, modular, and readable, with clear correspondence to the 7458 chip functionality, and potentially aids synthesis tools in optimization due to explicit modular boundaries.
