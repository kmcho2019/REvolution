To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will eliminate the parameterized module instantiations and intermediate wires, and replace them with straightforward continuous assign statements using direct bitwise AND and OR operations.

This reduces the number of module boundaries and net toggling, which can improve timing by reducing logic depth and improve power by lessening switching on intermediate signals.

Furthermore, direct bitwise logic will reduce area since synthesis tools can better optimize simple expressions than multiple instantiated modules.

Thus, the design remains functionally identical but is cleaner and more efficient for synthesis.
