Implement the TopModule using purely combinational logic with direct assign statements for each AND gate output and OR them to generate final outputs. 

Instead of using parameterized modules or intermediate wires for all AND gates, I will declare only four intermediate wires corresponding to the four AND gates of the 7458 chip:

- Two 3-input AND gates for p1y
- Two 2-input AND gates for p2y

Then, p1y and p2y will be driven by OR gates of these intermediate signals.

This approach clearly reflects the hardware structure of the 7458 chip with four AND gates feeding two OR gates, using intermediate nets to improve code readability and structural clarity while avoiding hierarchical modules.

It balances readability, modularity, and minimal net overhead and is easy to synthesize efficiently.
