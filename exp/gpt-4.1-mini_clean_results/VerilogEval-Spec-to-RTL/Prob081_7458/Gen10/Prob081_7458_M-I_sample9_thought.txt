The original design uses modular submodules for each AND gate, which aids readability but introduces additional wiring and module overhead that can slightly increase power and area. To optimize for PPA, the design can be simplified by removing the separate And3 and And2 modules, and instead directly implementing the logic with continuous assignments. This eliminates intermediate wires and modules, reducing net count and improving area and power slightly, while maintaining the same timing since the logic levels remain minimal.
