To optimize the existing solution for better PPA (power, performance, area), I will remove intermediate wires and combine the logic expressions directly in a single assign statement per output. This reduces the number of signals and simplifies synthesis mapping, potentially lowering area and power slightly. This direct combinational expression style also reduces gate delay levels by allowing synthesis to optimize the logic fully. The code remains functionally equivalent but more compact and efficient, trading off some readability for marginal PPA gains, which is acceptable for such small logic.
