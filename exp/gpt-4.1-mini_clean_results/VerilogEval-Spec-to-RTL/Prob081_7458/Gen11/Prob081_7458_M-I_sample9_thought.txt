The original solution structurally models the 7458 chip with instantiated 2-input AND and OR gates, which is clear but adds hierarchy, more intermediate signals, and slightly more overhead for synthesis and power.

To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will simplify the design by eliminating the small gate modules and intermediate wires, replacing them with direct assign statements using bitwise operators. This flattens the logic hierarchy, reducing gate-level instances and net capacitances, which typically leads to lower power, reduced area, and potentially better timing.

The functionality remains identical: 
- p1y = (p1a & p1b & p1c) | (p1d & p1e & p1f)
- p2y = (p2a & p2b) | (p2c & p2d)

This approach keeps the code concise, synthesizable, and easier to read while optimizing for PPA.
