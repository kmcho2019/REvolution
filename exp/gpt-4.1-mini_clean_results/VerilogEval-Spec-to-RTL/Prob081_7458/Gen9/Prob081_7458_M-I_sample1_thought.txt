To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), remove the parameterized AndGateN module and directly implement the AND and OR logic using simple assign statements. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and potential synthesis artifacts caused by generic reduction operators. By explicitly writing the AND operations with bitwise operators and then ORing them, we reduce logic levels and possibly minimize gate count. This leads to slightly improved delay and area while maintaining clarity. Also, using direct expressions minimizes intermediate signals, reducing switching activity and power.
