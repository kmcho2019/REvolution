Since the FSM is very simple with only two states, we can remove the conditional state update logic and always update the state register on each clock cycle. This eliminates the comparator and extra gating logic, simplifying the design and potentially improving timing and area slightly. 

The synchronous reset remains to initialize to OFF state. The next state is calculated combinationally from the current state and inputs, and the output remains directly driven by the state (Moore machine).

This simplification keeps the design functional and clean while reducing unnecessary logic.
