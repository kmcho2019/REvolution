Implement the Moore FSM using a one-hot style approach with two one-bit flip-flops representing the OFF and ON states separately. At any time, only one flip-flop is set. State transitions will be done by enabling the appropriate flip-flop based on inputs j and k. The synchronous reset clears both flip-flops to represent the OFF state. The output is driven combinationally from the ON state flip-flop. This architecture is structurally different from encoding states as a single bit and may facilitate easy expansion or debugging.
