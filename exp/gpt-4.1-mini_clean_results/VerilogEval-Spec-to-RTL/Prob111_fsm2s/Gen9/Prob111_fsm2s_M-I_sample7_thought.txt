To improve PPA, especially power and timing, remove the separate next_state register by directly computing the next state in the sequential always block. This eliminates one unnecessary register and simplifies the logic, reducing area and potentially improving timing by shortening the data path. Output is derived directly from the registered state (Moore output), assigned combinationally for glitch-free operation. Also, keep synchronous reset but only on the state register; output derives from state, further reducing flip-flops. Use blocking assignments in combinational logic and non-blocking in sequential for clarity and synthesis friendliness. This leads to a simpler and more efficient FSM implementation with the same functionality.
