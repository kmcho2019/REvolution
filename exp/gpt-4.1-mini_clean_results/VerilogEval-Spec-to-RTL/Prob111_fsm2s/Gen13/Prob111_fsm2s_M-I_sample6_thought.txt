The original design is already minimal with a single-bit state register and synchronous reset. To optimize for PPA further, I will combine the next-state logic into a single combinational assignment outside the sequential block to reduce conditional evaluation inside the clocked process, which can help synthesis tools optimize better. I will implement a case statement for clarity and better synthesis mapping. Also, explicitly defining the next state logic in a combinational block can enhance timing (WN/S), and keep the output as a direct state assignment to minimize logic and switching. This approach maintains the Moore FSM semantics and synchronous reset while potentially improving synthesis optimization opportunities.
