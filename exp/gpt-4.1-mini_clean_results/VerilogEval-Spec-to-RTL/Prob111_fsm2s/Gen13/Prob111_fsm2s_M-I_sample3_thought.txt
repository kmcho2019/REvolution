To further optimize PPA (timing, power, and area), I will reduce the state register to a single bit as before, but now implement the FSM using a single always block with synchronous reset and inline next-state logic. This reduces logic levels by avoiding separate next_state register and combinational block. I will also use a ternary operator for next-state decision to keep the combinational logic minimal. The output remains purely combinational from the state register, maintaining the Moore FSM behavior. This approach can slightly reduce the area and power by cutting one register and simplify the logic, which may marginally improve timing while keeping functionality identical.
