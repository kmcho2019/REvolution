Implement the FSM using a two-bit shift register that encodes state and history of inputs, enabling state determination by simple combinational logic. Specifically:

- Represent OFF as 2'b00 and ON as 2'b11, so the output depends on both bits being high.
- On each clock, shift in input j or k depending on the current state (j when OFF, k when ON) into the two-bit register.
- The two-bit register transitions as follows:
  - OFF (2'b00): on j=0, stay 00; on j=1, shift in '1' -> 01 or 11; we'll define 11 as ON
  - ON (2'b11): on k=0, stay 11; on k=1, shift in '0' -> 10 or 00; 00 is OFF
- To simplify, on OFF state, next state is {state[0], j}, and on ON state, next state is {state[0], ~k}.
- Output is high only if both bits are 1 (state == 2'b11).
- Reset synchronously clears the register to 2'b00 (OFF).
This approach leverages bit-shifting for state update and reduces explicit next-state conditional checks, presenting a novel state encoding and transition method.
