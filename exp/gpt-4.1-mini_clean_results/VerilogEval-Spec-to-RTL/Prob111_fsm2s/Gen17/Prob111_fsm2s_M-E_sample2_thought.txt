Implement the Moore FSM using a one-hot encoding style with two separate flip-flops for the states OFF and ON, eliminating the need for combinational next_state logic. The state transitions are expressed directly as next inputs for each flip-flop using synchronous logic with the inputs j and k. The reset will synchronously clear the OFF and ON flip-flops setting OFF active and ON inactive. The output is directly assigned from the ON flip-flop. This approach leverages one-hot encoding to simplify combinational logic to straightforward AND/OR gating, potentially improving timing at the cost of slightly more flip-flops, which is negligible in a two-state FSM.
