The existing solution is already minimal and efficient with a single-bit state and simple combinational logic. To slightly optimize for better power and timing (PPA), I will apply clock gating to prevent the state register from toggling unnecessarily when the next state equals the current state. This can reduce switching activity and dynamic power.

The clock gating is implemented by generating an enable signal that is high only when the state is about to change, gating the clock input to the flip-flop accordingly. This improves power efficiency without affecting performance or area significantly.

The synchronous reset and output logic remain unchanged. This approach keeps the design compact and low power.
