Implement the Moore FSM using a one-hot encoded state register with two flip-flops (one per state). Use combinational logic to determine next state and output, driven by current state flip-flops and inputs. The synchronous reset clears both flip-flops. Outputs are directly generated from the state flip-flops, leveraging one-hot encoding. This approach can simplify state decoding and can make the next state logic straightforward, especially for small FSMs, providing a clear and explicit mapping between states and flip-flops.
