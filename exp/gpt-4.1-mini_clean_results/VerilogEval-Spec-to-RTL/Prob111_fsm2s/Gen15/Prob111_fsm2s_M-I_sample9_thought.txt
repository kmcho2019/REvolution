To improve PPA (performance, power, area), replace the function call for next_state with a simple combinational always block. This eliminates potential synthesis overhead from function inlining and helps synthesis tools optimize next_state logic more effectively. Using an always_comb block with a case statement keeps clarity and may reduce logic depth, improving timing and power slightly. The synchronous reset and Moore output are retained as is. The state encoding remains simple as one bit, which is already minimal area-wise.
