- Separate the next-state combinational logic into an always @(*) block for clarity and better synthesis optimization (like Example 1).

- Keep synchronous active-high reset that sets state to OFF.

- Clear and readable state transition logic separated from sequential logic.
- Output directly mapped from the state register (Moore output).

- Power benefits from fewer registers and glitch-reduced logic due to explicit combinational logic.

This approach captures area and power efficiency of Example 2 while adding synthesis friendliness and clarity from Example 1.
