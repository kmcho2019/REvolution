To optimize the existing FSM for better PPA, I will remove the conditional state update inside the clocked always block. This avoids extra combinational logic from the 'if (state != next_state)' condition, simplifying the register's input path. It slightly reduces power by eliminating unnecessary gating logic and reduces the critical path.

Also, I will preserve the simple enum encoding and continuous assignments for next state and output logic, which are already efficient.

This change preserves synchronous reset and the Moore output FSM behavior while potentially improving timing and power with minimal area impact.
