Since the FSM only has two states that correspond directly to the output (OFF = 0 and ON = 1), the state can be represented directly by the output signal `out` without using a separate state register. On each clock cycle, `out` is updated based on the inputs and its current value. This eliminates the need for an explicit `next_state` signal and a separate state register, simplifying the design while maintaining synchronous reset and correct FSM functionality.
