To improve the existing minimal FSM for better PPA, we focus on reducing power by implementing clock gating for the state register. Since the state changes only when inputs j or k cause a state transition and reset is not asserted, gating the clock when the next state equals the current state and reset is inactive prevents unnecessary flip-flop toggling and dynamic power consumption. This optimization keeps the design functionally equivalent but reduces switching activity and dynamic power. The area overhead is minimal due to simple gating logic, and timing is unlikely to degrade thanks to the simple clock gate condition.
