To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without illegal sensitivity lists, we sample the input 'd' on both rising and falling edges of 'clk' into two separate registers (q_pos and q_neg). Then, instead of using a purely combinational mux to select the output, we register the mux output on the rising edge of clk, reducing glitches and improving timing and power. This approach fuses the concept of dual-edge sampling from both examples with an additional registered output stage to minimize glitching on 'q' and provide cleaner timing, thus improving performance and power without increasing area significantly.
