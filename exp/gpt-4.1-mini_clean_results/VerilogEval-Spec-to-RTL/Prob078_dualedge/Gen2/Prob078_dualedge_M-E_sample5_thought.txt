Implement the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by generating a 2x frequency clock internally using a toggle flip-flop triggered on the positive edge of the input clock, and then using a positive edge-triggered flip-flop clocked by this 2x frequency clock. This way, the output 'q' updates at both edges of the original clock without using an illegal sensitivity list. The toggle FF effectively converts the clock edges into a higher frequency clock, so the data 'd' is sampled on both edges indirectly.
