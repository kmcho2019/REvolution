Simplify the design by merging the two edge-triggered flip-flops into a single clock domain with a toggling internal clock signal derived from the original clock's edges. Since we cannot trigger on both edges directly, we create an internal clock that toggles on every clock edge using a simple flip-flop, and then use this internal clock to clock a single flip-flop capturing input d. This effectively emulates a dual-edge triggered flip-flop with only one sequential block and no combinational multiplexing, thus reducing complexity while preserving functionality.
