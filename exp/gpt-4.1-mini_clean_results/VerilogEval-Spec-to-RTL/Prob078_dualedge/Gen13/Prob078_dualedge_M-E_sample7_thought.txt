Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by using a single positive-edge triggered flip-flop clocked at double frequency, generated internally by XORing the original clock with its delayed version.

- Generate an internal clock signal (clk_xor) that toggles on every edge (both posedge and negedge) of clk by XORing clk with a 1-clock-cycle delayed version of clk.
- Use a simple positive-edge triggered flip-flop clocked by clk_xor to sample input `d`.
- Because clk_xor toggles twice as fast and aligns with both edges of clk, the positive-edge triggered FF clocked by clk_xor effectively captures data on both edges of the original clock.
- This approach avoids illegal sensitivity lists or negative-edge FFs, and uses only one flip-flop.
- The clock doubling logic (XOR + delay) can be implemented using a small synchronizer register or combinational XOR with a delayed clk (using a flip-flop).
- The design is simple, uses less flip-flops, and is synthesizable in typical FPGAs.
