Use a single clock domain (posedge clk) and internally generate a delayed version of clk (inv_clk) to sample data on both edges. Implement two flip-flops clocked on the positive edge: one capturing d on posedge clk, the other capturing d on the delayed inverted clock. Then combine their outputs based on the actual clock level. This way, we avoid using negedge in sensitivity list while still sampling on both edges, simplifying the original approach to only use posedge-triggered flip-flops.
