Refactor the design to use a single always block clocked on the positive edge of the input clock and a delayed version of the clock (clk_d) to capture data on both edges. We generate clk_d by delaying clk by one half clock cycle through a register clocked at the same clk. Then we use two positive edge-triggered flip-flops: one clocked by clk and one clocked by clk_d (a phase-shifted clock). The output is multiplexed between these two registers using clk as the select signal. This eliminates the negative-edge sensitivity and avoids separate modules for posedge and negedge FFs while retaining dual-edge functionality.
