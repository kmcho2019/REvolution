To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without illegal sensitivity lists, we can use two single-edge flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge and one on the falling edge. Both sample the input 'd' at their respective clock edges. Then, we use a multiplexer to select between the two outputs depending on the current clock level. This approach effectively captures data on both edges. To avoid glitches, we register the outputs properly and combine them.

A common method is:
- Use two flip-flops: one triggered on posedge clk, another on negedge clk.
- Use the clk as a select signal to mux the outputs.
- This creates a dual-edge behavior.
