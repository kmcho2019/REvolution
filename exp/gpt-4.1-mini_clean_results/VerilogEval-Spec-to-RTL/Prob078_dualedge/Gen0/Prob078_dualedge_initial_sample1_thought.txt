A dual-edge triggered flip-flop can be emulated using two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of the clock. Then, we use a multiplexer to select which flip-flop output to present on q, based on the current clock level. Specifically, on the rising edge, sample d into q_pos; on the falling edge, sample d into q_neg; output q is chosen as q_pos when clk=1 and q_neg when clk=0. This way, q updates on both edges.
