To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in an FPGA (which typically only supports edge-triggered flip-flops on one clock edge), we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of the clock and one on the falling edge. Both flip-flops capture the input data 'd'. Then, we combine their outputs with a multiplexer controlled by the clock to select which data to output based on the clock level. This technique effectively creates a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by sampling on both edges.
