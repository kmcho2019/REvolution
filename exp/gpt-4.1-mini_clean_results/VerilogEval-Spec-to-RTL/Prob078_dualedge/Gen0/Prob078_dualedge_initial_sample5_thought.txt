To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in an FPGA where always blocks cannot have both posedge and negedge in the sensitivity list, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other triggered on the negative edge. Then, use a multiplexer controlled by the clock to select which flip-flop output to pass to the output. This way, on the rising edge of clk, the first flip-flop samples d, and on the falling edge, the second flip-flop samples d. The output q is chosen based on the current clock level, producing behavior equivalent to a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
