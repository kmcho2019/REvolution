Since we cannot use dual-edge triggering directly, we can emulate it by sampling the input 'd' on both the rising and falling edges of 'clk' using two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge and one on the falling edge. Then we combine the outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the current clock level to produce an output 'q' that updates on both edges. This approach effectively creates a dual-edge triggered flip-flop behavior using only single-edge triggered flip-flops.
