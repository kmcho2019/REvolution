To optimize the dual-edge triggered flip-flop for better power and area, I reduce redundant toggling by gating the negative-edge flip-flop clock using an inverted clock gated by the original clock. This avoids unnecessary toggling of the negedge FF when clk is high, since it won't trigger until clk falls. I also replace the transparent latch with a simple combinational mux since synthesis tools efficiently infer a mux from a ternary operator, thus reducing latch-related hold time risks and possibly improving timing. This approach maintains functional correctness and synthesis legality, with potentially reduced switching power and area overhead.
