Refactor the design by replacing the separate PosEdgeFF and NegEdgeFF modules with a single module containing two internal registers updated on posedge and negedge respectively using always blocks inside one module. Then output is assigned combinationally via a mux based on the clk level.

This reduces module instantiations, groups related logic, and keeps the dual-edge sampling concept intact. The top module contains the two flip-flops internally, making the design more compact while functionally equivalent.
