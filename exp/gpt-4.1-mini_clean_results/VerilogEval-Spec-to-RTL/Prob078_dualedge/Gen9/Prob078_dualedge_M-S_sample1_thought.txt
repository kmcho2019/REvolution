To simplify the design, I will merge the two separate flip-flops into one module that internally stores both positive and negative edge captures using a single always block with separate posedge and negedge sensitivity. However, since FPGA synthesis tools do not accept dual-edge sensitivity lists, I will still instantiate two separate single-edge flip-flops inside the TopModule.

To reduce complexity, instead of separate modules for PosEdgeFF and NegEdgeFF, I will instantiate two generic D flip-flops implemented as always @(posedge clk) and always @(posedge clk_n) where clk_n is generated by inverting clk. Then a simple ternary mux selects the output based on clk level, avoiding the case statement and the separate modules.

This approach keeps the dual-edge behavior and FPGA legality but uses fewer module definitions and simpler mux logic. It is easy to understand and concise.
