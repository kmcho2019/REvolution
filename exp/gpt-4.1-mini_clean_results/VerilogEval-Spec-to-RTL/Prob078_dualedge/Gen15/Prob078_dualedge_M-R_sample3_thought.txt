To refactor the dual-edge triggered flip-flop design while keeping the core idea of two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a selector of their outputs, I will:

- Keep the two flip-flops: one positive edge-triggered and one negative edge-triggered.
- Replace the always block that assigns 'q' with a continuous assignment using a ternary operator on clk.
- Instead of a transparent latch implied by always @(*) with clk controlling output, use a wire output assigned by a mux between the two flip-flop outputs controlled by clk level.
- Make 'q' a wire output, driven by the mux of q_pos and q_neg.
- This removes the inferred latch and replaces it with a pure combinational mux.
- Although this re-introduces a combinational mux (compared to latch in original), it is functionally equivalent and legal.
- This will slightly change timing characteristics (multiplexer vs latch) but is simpler structurally and uses explicit combinational logic.

Overall, the design retains dual-edge sampling by two flip-flops but outputs data using a mux continuously selecting based on clock level.
