To improve the given dual-edge triggered flip-flop implementation, I will reduce power and area by removing the explicit transparent latch and instead implement the output selection with a clock-gated mux formed by a simple combinational multiplexer followed by a register clocked on the positive edge. This approach eliminates latch transparency, simplifying timing closure while potentially reducing glitches by registering the mux output before driving `q`. Although the positive and negative edge flip-flops still toggle, this registered mux can reduce glitches on output transitions, hence lowering dynamic power.

Additionally, I will merge the PosEdgeFF and NegEdgeFF modules into the TopModule for simplicity and clarity. The registered mux output approach is synthesizable on FPGAs and avoids illegal sensitivity lists.

This balances power and area improvements without compromising functionality or timing robustness compared to the transparent latch approach.
