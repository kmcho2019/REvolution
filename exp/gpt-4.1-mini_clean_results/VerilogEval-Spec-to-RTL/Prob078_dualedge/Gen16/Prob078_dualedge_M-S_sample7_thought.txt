To simplify the dual-edge triggered flip-flop design, I will eliminate the parameterized EdgeFF module and directly instantiate two simple always blocks inside TopModule: one triggered on the posedge of clk and the other on the negedge of clk, each sampling the input d. Then the output q will be continuously assigned from q_posedge or q_negedge depending on the current clock level. This reduces module hierarchy and improves code conciseness while preserving the original behavior and synthesis legality.
