We create an internal clock signal at double the input clock frequency by XOR-ing the clock with a delayed version of itself (implemented by registering clk once). This toggling signal changes state on both edges of the original clock. Then, using a single flip-flop clocked by this doubled-frequency clock, we sample the input 'd' on both edges of the original clock.

This approach internally generates a double-rate clock, avoiding the need for negative-edge flip-flops or complex muxes, simplifying timing paths and potentially improving area and power since only one flip-flop is used.
