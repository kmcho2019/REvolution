To optimize the dual-edge triggered flip-flop for better power and area efficiency while maintaining correctness, I will:

- Replace the transparent latch with a simple combinational 2:1 mux using the clock signal. This reduces the latch transparency duration, thus minimizing switching activity and power.
- Use blocking assignments in the mux to avoid unnecessary register inference and reduce complexity.
- Keep the dual FF instantiation for posedge and negedge to maintain functional behavior.
- This approach slightly improves power by reducing the latch transparency window and area by removing the latch element.
- It may slightly impact timing since the mux output is purely combinational, but for typical FPGA designs, this is acceptable.

This balances simplicity, power, and area better than a transparent latch and remains synthesizable and FPGA friendly.
