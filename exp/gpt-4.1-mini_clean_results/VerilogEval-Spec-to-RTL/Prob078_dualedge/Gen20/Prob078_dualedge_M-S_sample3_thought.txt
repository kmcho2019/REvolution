Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by using a single flip-flop clocked at the positive edge of the clock and a combinational XOR gate with a delayed clock signal to generate the negative edge sampling internally. This can be done by creating a divided clock with an inverter and sampling 'd' at the positive edge, then toggling the output on both edges by XORing the flip-flop output with the clock's delayed signal. This removes the need for two flip-flops and a mux, simplifying the design while preserving dual-edge behavior.
