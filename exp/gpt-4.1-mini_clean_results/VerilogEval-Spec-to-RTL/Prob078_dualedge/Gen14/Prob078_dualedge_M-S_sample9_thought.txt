Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by using a single flip-flop clocked at the positive edge of the clock doubled in frequency. Since the clock cannot be doubled externally, instead, sample data on the positive edge and then sample again at the negative edge by sampling the data with a flip-flop on the positive edge and then a second flip-flop clocked by the inverted clock.

To simplify and avoid two separate flip-flop modules, instantiate two flip-flops inside the TopModule: one clocked on posedge clk, the other clocked on posedge of inverted clk (which effectively is negedge clk of original clk). Then, use a simple 2-to-1 mux controlled by the clock to select between these two outputs as the final output.

This reduces complexity by integrating everything into one module and using a simple mux rather than a latch, preserving functionality while simplifying the design.
