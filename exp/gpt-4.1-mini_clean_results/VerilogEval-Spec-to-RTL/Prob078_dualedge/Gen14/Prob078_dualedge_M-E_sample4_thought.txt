Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by sampling the input `d` on the positive edge of the clock and also sampling the clock signal on the negative edge. Then use a transparent latch that is enabled during the low phase of the clock to combine these signals. Specifically, use a positive-edge triggered flip-flop to capture `d` on the rising edge, and a negative-edge triggered flip-flop to capture `d` on the falling edge. Then, use an output register clocked by the positive edge that updates its value based on a mux selecting between the outputs of the two flip-flops. The mux select is driven by the current clock level. This effectively captures input changes on both edges, while only using FPGA-friendly positive or negative edge triggered flip-flops and a simple mux in synchronous logic.
