- Instantiate two EdgeFF modules parameterized for posedge and negedge sampling of the input `d`.

- Registering the mux output on the positive clock edge breaks combinational paths, reduces glitches, and aids timing closure by synchronizing output changes to the clock.
- This approach avoids transparent latches that can cause hold time issues, uses only supported sensitivity lists, and reduces glitch-induced power usage compared to a purely combinational mux or latch.
- The result is a modular, clean, glitch-reduced, and FPGA-synthesis-friendly dual-edge FF emulation with minimal timing and power risks.

This fusion balances clean modular code, glitch minimization, synthesis legality, and ease of timing closure better than either purely muxed or latched output selection in previous examples.
