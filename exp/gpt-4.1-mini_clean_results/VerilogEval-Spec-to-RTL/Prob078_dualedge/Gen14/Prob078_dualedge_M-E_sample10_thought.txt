Implement the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by sampling the input `d` on the positive edge into a register, then sampling that register on the negative edge of the clock into another register. Finally, drive the output `q` with an XOR between these two sampled registers. This approach creates a toggle of `q` at every clock edge where `d` is high, effectively simulating dual-edge triggering. It avoids using illegal sensitivity lists or latches, uses only standard edge-triggered flip-flops, and outputs a toggling signal based on input changes sampled at both edges. This architecture also exploits simple logic (XOR) for output generation, keeping synthesis straightforward and FPGA-friendly.
