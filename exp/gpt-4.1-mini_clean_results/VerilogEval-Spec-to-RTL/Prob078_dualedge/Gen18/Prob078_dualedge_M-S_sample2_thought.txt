Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by using two single-edge triggered flip-flops clocked on opposite edges of clk, and use a simple combinational mux (assign) to select the output based on clk level. To simplify further, merge the positive and negative edge flip-flops into a single module parameterized by edge type to avoid separate module definitions. The top module then instantiates two such flip-flops and muxes their outputs to produce the dual-edge behavior with minimal code and clear structure.
