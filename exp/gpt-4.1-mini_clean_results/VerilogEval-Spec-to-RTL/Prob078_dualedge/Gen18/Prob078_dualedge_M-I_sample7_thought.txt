To improve the dual-edge triggered flip-flop design for better PPA while preserving its function, I will:

- Replace the transparent latch with a clocked MUX implemented using a small always block triggered on posedge and negedge of clk. This approach removes level-sensitive latches, which can complicate timing and cause unnecessary switching, improving timing and power.
- Use two flip-flops as before: one capturing data on the posedge of clk, one on the negedge.
- Use a small register to select which flip-flop output to propagate to q on each clock edge, so q changes synchronously only on clock edges, minimizing glitches.
- This reduces dynamic switching and output glitches caused by the transparent latch.
- It also removes latch-based timing complexities and reduces the critical path.
- The area overhead is minimal and power is improved due to less level-sensitive toggling.
- This approach retains the dual-edge triggered functionality faithfully.

Hence, the output q updates at each clock edge with the appropriate flip-flop output, providing dual-edge capture behavior without illegal sensitivity lists or level-sensitive logic.
