Refactor the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by replacing the level-sensitive latch with a combinational multiplexer implemented using a continuous assignment. This mux chooses between the two flip-flop outputs (one triggered on the posedge and one on the negedge) based on the current clock level. This approach removes the always block controlling the latch and uses purely combinational logic for output selection, simplifying the design and potentially reducing latch-related timing issues while maintaining functional equivalence.
