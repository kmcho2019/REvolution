Fuse the two approaches by:

- Keeping modular, parameterized single-edge flip-flops for reusability and clarity.
- Using a latch-based output selector like Example 2 to reduce glitches and improve power/stability compared to a purely combinational mux.
- Implementing the latch as an explicit level-sensitive always block controlled by clk, passing the posedge or negedge FF output accordingly.
- Avoiding inverted clocks or illegal sensitivity lists to ensure FPGA-friendly synthesis.
- Using non-blocking assignments in flip-flops and blocking assignment in latch for correct behavioral modeling.
- Structuring the TopModule cleanly with minimal hierarchy.
- This fusion maintains dual-edge functional behavior, glitch reduction at output, simple structure, and is fully synthesizable and FPGA compatible.

This design balances clarity, power, and timing better than pure mux or latch approaches alone.
