Simplify the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by merging the two edge-triggered flip-flops into a single module that instantiates two internal registers triggered on opposite clock edges. Use the clock itself and its inverted form to drive the flip-flops, reducing the number of separate modules.

Output is selected by the clock level, muxing the two registers' outputs. This reduces hierarchy and eliminates separate modules, making the design more compact without changing behavior.
