Simplify the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by using two single-edge triggered flip-flops, one positive-edge and one negative-edge triggered, and combine their outputs with a simple combinational mux controlled by the clock level. This eliminates the latch and makes the design straightforward and easy to understand, while preserving correct dual-edge sampling functionality. Although this may cause minor glitches on output q, it is acceptable for many applications and reduces complexity.
