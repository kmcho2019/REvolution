Refactor the design to keep the same dual-edge triggering concept with two flip-flops but replace the output latch (implemented as an always block) with a continuous assign statement using a ternary operator. Since the clk is one bit, we can treat it as a selector directly in a combinational assignment to pick q_pos or q_neg. To avoid glitches, explicitly register this combinational output in a simple output register clocked on posedge clk; this output register helps to synchronize the output and reduce glitching on q. This approach changes the latch to a registered mux, improving timing and synthesis friendliness, while maintaining the dual-edge functionality and avoiding illegal sensitivity lists.
