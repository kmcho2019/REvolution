To simplify the dual-edge triggered flip-flop implementation, I will remove the separate PosEdgeFF and NegEdgeFF modules and combine both flip-flops into one module by instantiating two always blocks inside TopModule: one triggered on posedge clk and the other on negedge clk. Each captures the input d to separate registers. The output q is then continuously multiplexed based on the clock level. This removes extra module overhead while keeping functionality and legal sensitivity lists intact.
