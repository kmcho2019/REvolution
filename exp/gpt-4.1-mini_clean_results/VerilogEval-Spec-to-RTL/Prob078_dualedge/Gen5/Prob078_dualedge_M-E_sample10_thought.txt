Use a toggle flip-flop to generate an enable pulse on every clock edge (both rising and falling) by detecting clock transitions, and then latch the input data with this enable pulse synchronized to the positive edge of the clock. This approach uses a single positive edge-triggered flip-flop and a simple edge detector circuit to create a dual-edge triggered effect without the need for negative edge-triggered FFs or output muxes. The clock edge detector toggles a state on both edges, and this toggling generates an enable pulse for a positive-edge flip-flop that captures data on both edges indirectly.
