Instead of using two flip-flops clocked on opposite edges, we can sample the input 'd' twice per full clock cycle using a single-edge flip-flop running at double frequency. Since we can't increase the clock frequency, we simulate this by sampling 'd' on the rising edge and then on the falling edge internally by using a flip-flop triggered on the positive edge and a transparent latch enabled by clk. The latch captures 'd' on the falling clock phase (when clk is low), and the flip-flop captures the latch output on the rising clock edge, effectively storing both phases' data. Then, we multiplex between the flip-flop and latch outputs based on clk to produce a dual-edge triggered flip-flop behavior without negative edge sensitivity or multiple flip-flops clocked on opposite edges.
