Use a single positive-edge triggered flip-flop clocked by a clock doubled signal (clk_x2) derived by XORing the original clock with a delayed version of itself, thus generating pulses at both edges of the original clock. By carefully generating clk_x2 as a short pulse at every edge of clk, a single positive-edge FF samples the input d on both edges effectively, emulating a dual-edge triggered FF without needing two flip-flops or an output mux. To maintain correct output timing and avoid metastability, a latch captures the FF output synchronized back to the original clock domain. This method reduces FF count and eliminates output mux delays.
