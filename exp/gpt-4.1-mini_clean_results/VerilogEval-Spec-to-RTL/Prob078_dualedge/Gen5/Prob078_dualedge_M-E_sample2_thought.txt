To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using negative-edge sensitive constructs, we can use a single positive edge-triggered flip-flop clocked by the main clock, and a second positive edge-triggered flip-flop clocked by the inverted clock. By doing this, one flip-flop captures data on the rising edge of clk, and the other on what corresponds to the falling edge of clk. We then combine their outputs with a toggle latch that switches its output on every clock edge, thus emulating a dual-edge triggered FF. This approach requires generating the inverted clock internally and avoids any illegal sensitivity lists. The output toggles between the two registered values based on a sampled version of the clock to reconstruct the dual-edge output.
