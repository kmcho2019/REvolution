Refactor the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by implementing the positive and negative edge-triggered storage using separate always blocks within a single module rather than separate submodules. Store the two data samples in registers updated on the posedge and negedge respectively. Use a continuous assignment to output the value selected by the current clock level. This removes the hierarchy of separate modules, making the design more compact and easier to integrate while preserving the original functional behavior and synthesis friendliness.
