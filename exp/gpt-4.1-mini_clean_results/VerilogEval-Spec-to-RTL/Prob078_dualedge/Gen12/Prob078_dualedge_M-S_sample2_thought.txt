Use two single-edge triggered flip-flops, one triggered on the posedge and one on the negedge of clk, to capture the input d. Then, combine their outputs with a simple combinational mux controlled directly by clk, selecting q_posedge when clk=1 and q_negedge when clk=0. This avoids latches and complex sensitivity lists, simplifying the design while retaining dual-edge flip-flop behavior in a synthesizable and FPGA-friendly way.
