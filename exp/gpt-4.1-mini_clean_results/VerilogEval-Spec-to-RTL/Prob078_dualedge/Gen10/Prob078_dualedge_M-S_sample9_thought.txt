Implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by sampling the input `d` on the positive edge and then updating the output `q` on the negative edge of the clock. This uses a single positive-edge triggered flip-flop to capture `d`, and a negative edge triggered flip-flop to update `q` with the sampled value. This approach avoids using illegal sensitivity lists and eliminates the latch-based mux, simplifying the design while preserving the dual-edge behavior.
