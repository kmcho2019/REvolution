Refactor the dual-edge triggered flip-flop by replacing the separate instantiated DFF modules with two always blocks inside TopModule itself: one triggered on the positive edge of clk, and one triggered on the positive edge of clk_n (inverted clk). This removes the explicit DFF submodule and encapsulates the storage registers directly. Then use a continuous assignment mux on clk to select between the two registers. This keeps the same dual-edge behavior but simplifies the module hierarchy and may allow the synthesis tool to optimize the registers more efficiently.
