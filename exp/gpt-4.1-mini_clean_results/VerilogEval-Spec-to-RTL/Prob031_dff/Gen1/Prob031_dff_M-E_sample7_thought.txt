Instead of a straightforward D flip-flop, implement a transparent latch triggered by the clock, followed by a register stage. On the positive edge of clk, the latch becomes transparent, storing the value of 'd' into an intermediate signal. On the falling edge, the register captures the latched value. The output 'q' is updated by this register, effectively introducing a two-phase capture of 'd' with the clock. This will still behave like a flip-flop overall but uses an architectural variation leveraging a latch and a register.
