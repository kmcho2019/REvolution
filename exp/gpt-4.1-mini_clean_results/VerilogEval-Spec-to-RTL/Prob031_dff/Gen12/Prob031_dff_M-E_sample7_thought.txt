Instead of using a separate DFF module, I will implement the D flip-flop directly within TopModule using an always block triggered on the positive clock edge. To add some variation, I will include an asynchronous reset input internally driven to zero to show how internal signals could be used (though not exposed externally). The 'q' output will be a reg driven by the flip-flop, and the asynchronous reset will initialize 'q' to 0 asynchronously (for demonstration). Since reset is not specified as an input, it will be tied internally low to disable reset behavior. This approach keeps the design self-contained and minimal, avoiding module instantiation while illustrating an alternative flip-flop coding style.
