To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will flatten the hierarchy by merging the D flip-flop directly into TopModule, eliminating the DFF instantiation overhead. This removes extra module boundaries, allowing synthesis tools to better optimize the register and minimize net toggling or buffering overhead. The single DFF functionality will still use a non-blocking assignment on the positive clock edge, which is optimal for sequential logic. The interface will remain the same for compatibility. This flattening reduces potential power overhead from hierarchical instantiations, slightly reduces area by removing module overhead, and preserves timing performance by minimizing logical levels and nets.
