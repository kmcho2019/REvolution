The initial design is already minimal, clean, and synthesis-friendly, correctly implementing a D flip-flop with proper module encapsulation and signal direction. Given the simplicity, the key to improving PPA lies in potentially reducing module instantiation overhead by inlining the flip-flop logic directly in TopModule, removing the separate DFF module.

Inlining removes extra module boundaries, possibly allowing the synthesis tool to optimize better for area and power, though performance is already optimal. Also, keeping non-blocking assignments and synchronous logic on posedge clk is essential for correct behavior.

Hence, I will inline the flip-flop directly into TopModule, keeping the interface unchanged, and use a reg for q driven by a single always block. This reduces area slightly and may help power marginally by avoiding module overhead, while preserving clarity and functionality.
