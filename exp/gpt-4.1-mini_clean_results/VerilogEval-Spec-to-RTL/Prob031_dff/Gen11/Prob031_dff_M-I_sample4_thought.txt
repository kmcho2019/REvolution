The existing design is minimal and functionally correct. Given the single flip-flop, there is very limited room for area or power optimization at the RTL level. However, we can slightly improve synthesis friendliness and potential PPA by:

- Removing the separate DFF module and integrating the flip-flop directly into the TopModule. This reduces hierarchy overhead and may help synthesis tools optimize register inference.
- Using the nonblocking assignment correctly.
- Keeping the interface as required.
- Adding a minimal vendor-neutral synthesis attribute hint as a comment for register inference, which some synthesis tools recognize and use for better optimization.
  
This approach maintains clarity, reduces hierarchy, and makes the design potentially more optimal in synthesis without changing functionality.
