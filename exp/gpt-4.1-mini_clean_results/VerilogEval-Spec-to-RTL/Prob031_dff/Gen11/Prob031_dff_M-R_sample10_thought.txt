Since a D flip-flop inherently requires sequential logic triggered on a clock edge, it cannot be implemented purely with continuous assignments like 'assign'. To refactor with a different structure while preserving the functionality, I will embed the flip-flop logic directly inside TopModule without a separate DFF submodule. This reduces hierarchy and slightly improves area by avoiding module instantiation overhead. The always block will be written with a begin-end block to contrast the previous single-line style, showing an alternative style of writing sequential logic. The output q will remain a reg driven by the always block on posedge clk, and input q will be corrected to output q as specified.
