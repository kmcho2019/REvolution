To improve the given design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will fold the DFF logic directly inside TopModule, removing the separate DFF instantiation. This eliminates one module level, potentially reducing area slightly and improving synthesis efficiency. Also, using a non-blocking assignment in an always block triggered by posedge clk is a standard and minimal way to implement a DFF.

Since the design is already minimal, additional clock gating or power optimizations require extra control signals not specified, so are beyond scope. Hence, direct behavioral coding is preferred for better area and simplicity.

This inline approach keeps the design clean and synthesizable, with no overhead from module instantiations.
