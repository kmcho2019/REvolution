I will implement the single D flip-flop functionality entirely within the TopModule without instantiating a separate module, using a behavioral always block triggered on the posedge of clk. This approach eliminates hierarchy and results in a fully self-contained module. It also simplifies the interface as no internal wires or signals are needed. The design is minimal and straightforward yet fully conforms to the requirements, demonstrating an alternative modularity tradeoff by embedding the flip-flop logic directly.
