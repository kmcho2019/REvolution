Given the original design is already minimal and clean, the only practical PPA improvement here is to infer the flip-flop directly in the TopModule, removing the extra module instantiation to potentially reduce netlist complexity and slight area overhead from hierarchy. This inlining avoids any unnecessary module boundaries, and since it's a single DFF, it won't affect code clarity significantly.

Additionally, I will maintain the use of nonblocking assignment on the positive clock edge to ensure correct sequential behavior. This approach also simplifies the design for easier downstream synthesis optimizations.

No clock gating or power gating is added since it's a single flip-flop, and such techniques are more beneficial in larger designs.

This results in a slightly more efficient design in terms of area and possibly power, while preserving functionality and readability.
