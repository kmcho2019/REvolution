I will implement the D flip-flop functionality directly inside the TopModule without creating a separate submodule. To add a unique architectural twist, I will use a register to hold the state and an assign statement for the output, emphasizing a clear separation between storage and output logic.

This approach uses an internal reg to store the data on the rising edge of the clock, and the output q is continuously assigned from this register. This style differs from a direct output reg and shows a clean, combinational-style drive from the internal state register, which may improve readability or certain synthesis tool interpretations.

No additional signals or complexity will be added beyond the required interface. The design is fully synchronous with non-blocking assignment to model correct sequential behavior.
