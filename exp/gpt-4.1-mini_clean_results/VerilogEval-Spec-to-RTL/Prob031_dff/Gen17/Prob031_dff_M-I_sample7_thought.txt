To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will consolidate the D flip-flop directly inside the TopModule. This eliminates the extra module instantiation, which slightly reduces area and might marginally reduce routing complexity. The behavior remains the same, using a simple non-blocking assignment on the posedge of clk. Although this does not change the fundamental sequential logic, removing the submodule wrapper simplifies the design hierarchy, which can help with minor area and power optimizations. Given the simplicity, clock gating or power gating is not applied here, but integrating the flip-flop directly is the most straightforward minimal improvement.
