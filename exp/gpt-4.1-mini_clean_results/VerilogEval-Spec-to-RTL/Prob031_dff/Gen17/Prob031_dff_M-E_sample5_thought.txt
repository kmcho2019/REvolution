I will implement the D flip-flop inside the TopModule directly, without submodule instantiation, to minimize hierarchy and reduce any inter-module wiring overhead. The output q will be declared as a reg since it stores state. The design will use a synchronous reset signal internally set to zero (unused here) to illustrate possible future extensibility. I will also use an explicit initial block to initialize q to 0 to prevent undefined startup states in simulation, though synthesis tools may ignore this or map it to hardware reset behavior depending on the target device. This approach gives a self-contained, straightforward, single-module design with clear sequential semantics and a template for adding synchronous resets or enable signals later if needed.
