Implement the single D flip-flop directly inside TopModule without instantiating a separate module. Use a reg-type output declared inside the module and drive it as the registered output updated on the positive edge of clk. Avoid synthesis attributes and extra hierarchy to keep the design minimal and straightforward. The port q is declared as output reg to directly hold the flip-flop state, which is a common and accepted style for simple sequential elements. This approach simplifies the design to just one module and keeps the interface as specified.
