I will implement the TopModule as a single module without separate submodules. To demonstrate an alternative architectural style, I will use an explicit register for q within the module and use continuous assignment to connect the internal register to the output port. This approach keeps the sequential logic clearly separated in an always block and the output assignment purely combinational.

Additionally, I will use a blocking assignment in the always block to illustrate a less common style for a flip-flop (though nonblocking is preferred in practice). This showcases a different coding technique that still synthesizes correctly for a simple DFF when the always block is only triggered by a clock edge.

This design reduces hierarchy and module count, simplifying instantiation at the cost of slightly less modularity, which can be suitable for small designs or quick prototyping.
