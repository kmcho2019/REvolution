To further optimize for power and area given the already minimal and clean design, I will inline the flip-flop directly inside TopModule without instantiating a separate DFF module. This removes the module instance boundary, potentially allowing synthesis tools better optimization for area and power. I will use a non-blocking assignment in an always block triggered on the positive edge of clk to implement the flip-flop. The interface remains exactly as specified, with clk and d as inputs and q as output. This approach slightly reduces structural overhead and keeps the design as simple and minimal as possible.
