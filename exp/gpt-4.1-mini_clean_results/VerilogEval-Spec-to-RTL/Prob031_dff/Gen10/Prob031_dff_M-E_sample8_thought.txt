I will implement the TopModule as a self-contained single D flip-flop without instantiating any submodules. Instead of using a reg output, I will use an intermediate register internally and continuously assign the output q from it. This approach emphasizes the separation of the registered storage and the output port, which may improve readability in certain design styles. Additionally, I will use an explicit nonblocking assignment in a positive edge clocked always block for proper synchronous behavior.

This approach provides a clean, hierarchical flattening-friendly design, minimizing module hierarchy while maintaining clear register behavior. This approach can be beneficial in designs where modularity is less important, and direct control of internal storage is desirable. It ensures the output is always driven by the internal register and clearly defines the clocked behavior without extraneous logic.
