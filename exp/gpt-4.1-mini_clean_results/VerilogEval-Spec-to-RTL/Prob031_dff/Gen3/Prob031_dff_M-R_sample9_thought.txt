Refactor the D flip-flop by explicitly declaring an internal register 'q_reg' to hold the state, and then continuously assign the output 'q' to this register. This separates the storage element and output logic, clarifying data flow. The sequential logic remains in an always block triggered by the positive clock edge, updating 'q_reg' with the input 'd'. This approach contrasts with the previous version where 'q' itself was a reg updated inside the always block.
