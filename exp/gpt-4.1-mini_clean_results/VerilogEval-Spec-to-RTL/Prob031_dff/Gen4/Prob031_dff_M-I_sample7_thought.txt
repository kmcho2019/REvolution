To optimize the design for better PPA, I will inline the DFF behavior directly inside TopModule using an always block instead of instantiating a separate submodule. This removes the overhead of module instantiation, slightly reducing area and potentially improving power by minimizing net routing. Since the design is trivial, this change also improves code simplicity without affecting timing or functionality. No clock gating or enable signals are added as they weren't specified, and such additions would increase complexity and area.
