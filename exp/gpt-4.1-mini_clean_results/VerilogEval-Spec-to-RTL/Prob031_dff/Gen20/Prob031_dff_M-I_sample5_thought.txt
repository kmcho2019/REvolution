The original design is clean and modular but includes a mild synthesis attribute (* syn_preserve = "true" *) that can prevent certain power and area optimizations. Since the DFF is a very simple element and typical synthesis tools will infer the flip-flop directly from the always block, removing this attribute will allow more aggressive synthesis optimizations such as clock gating, register merging, or power gating. 

Also, since the top module only instantiates a single DFF, flattening the design by merging the DFF into TopModule reduces hierarchy and may help synthesis tools optimize better without affecting clarity significantly.

Hence, my improvements are:
- Remove the (* syn_preserve = "true" *) attribute.
- Merge the DFF logic directly into TopModule using a simple nonblocking assignment on posedge clk.
- Keep all ports one-bit, consistent with the spec.
- Retain clear coding style with explicit input/output declarations.

This change reduces hierarchy and gives the synthesis tool maximum freedom to optimize for power and area while preserving correct functionality.
