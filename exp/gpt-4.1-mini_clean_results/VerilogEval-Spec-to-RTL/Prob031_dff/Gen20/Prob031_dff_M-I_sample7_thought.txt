The existing design is functionally correct and minimal, instantiating a dedicated DFF module within TopModule. To optimize for PPA (Power, Performance, Area) further given the metrics:

- Performance and timing slack are already ideal (zero critical path delay).
- Power can be slightly improved by reducing unnecessary module overhead and removing optional synthesis attribute comments.
- Area can be minimized by inlining the flip-flop directly within TopModule, eliminating one level of hierarchy, which synthesis tools can better optimize.

Thus, I will integrate the DFF logic directly into TopModule as a single always block. This reduces module overhead and yields a smaller netlist and potentially less power due to fewer nets and no extra module instantiation.

This retains the same interface and behavior, ensuring the output `q` is registered on the positive edge of `clk` capturing input `d`.

In summary:
- Remove the separate DFF module.
- Implement the flip-flop directly inside TopModule.
- Use a nonblocking assignment in an always block triggered on posedge clk.
- Declare `q` as reg because it is driven inside an always block.

This leads to simpler RTL, smaller area, and marginal power improvement while maintaining perfect timing and correctness.
