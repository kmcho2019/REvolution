The original design is already clean and minimal, correctly implementing a D flip-flop with modularity. To optimize further for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), there is limited scope due to the simplicity of the task. However, one area for slight improvement is removing the separate DFF module and integrating the logic directly inside TopModule. This reduces the module instantiation overhead and can marginally reduce area and power due to fewer hierarchical boundaries and wiring.

Additionally, we can declare 'q' as a reg output in TopModule to avoid unnecessary internal nets, simplifying the synthesis mapping.

This single-module implementation keeps the code minimal and efficient without losing clarity. It also follows best practices by using non-blocking assignment inside an always block triggered on the positive edge of clk.

This subtle consolidation should provide a small but measurable PPA improvement while meeting all requirements.
