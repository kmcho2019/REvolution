I will implement the D flip-flop functionality within the TopModule itself, avoiding a separate instantiated module to reduce hierarchy depth. The output q will be declared as a reg to allow direct assignment in the always block triggered on the positive clock edge. To add some uniqueness, I will explicitly initialize q to 0 for simulation and synthesis friendliness (some tools support reset via initialization) and include an asynchronous reset input internally tied low to illustrate how one could extend the module later. The design remains minimal but demonstrates a different architectural style that is common in smaller designs or when hierarchy is not necessary.

This approach leverages direct in-module sequential logic, improving simplicity and enabling straightforward extensions (like reset) without external submodules.
