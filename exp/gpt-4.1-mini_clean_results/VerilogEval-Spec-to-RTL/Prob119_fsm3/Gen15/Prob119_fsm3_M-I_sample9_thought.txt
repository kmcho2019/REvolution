To improve the existing FSM design for better performance and power, I will apply one-hot state encoding, which typically reduces combinational logic levels and can improve timing in FPGA implementations. Although it increases area, the FSM is small (4 states), so area impact is minimal. I will also register the output signal to avoid combinational glitches and reduce toggling power, converting it to a registered Moore output. Additionally, I will simplify the next-state logic conditions by directly using 'in' as a condition without redundant comparisons, which reduces toggling in nets. The asynchronous reset remains as specified. This clear one-hot FSM with registered output should improve timing (tns, wns) and reduce power slightly with minimal area increase.
