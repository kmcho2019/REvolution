To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will implement one-hot state encoding instead of binary. This often reduces combinational logic complexity and switching activity in the next state decoding, improving timing and potentially lowering dynamic power. Also, I'll replace the asynchronous reset with a synchronous reset since the clock is available and synchronous resets simplify the reset network and may reduce power and improve timing consistency. The output logic can be implemented with a simple combinational assignment without a case statement. These changes maintain functionality while improving PPA.
