To optimize for better PPA, I will apply one-hot encoding for the states instead of binary encoding. Although one-hot increases area by using more flip-flops, it reduces the combinational complexity of next-state logic, potentially improving timing and power. Given only four states, the overhead is minimal. Also, using one-hot encoding simplifies the next state logic to mostly simple gated signals instead of multi-bit comparisons, which can reduce critical path delay and power consumption. The asynchronous reset logic remains unchanged. Output logic becomes a simple wire tap from the corresponding state flip-flop. This approach targets performance and power improvements while keeping area increase modest.
