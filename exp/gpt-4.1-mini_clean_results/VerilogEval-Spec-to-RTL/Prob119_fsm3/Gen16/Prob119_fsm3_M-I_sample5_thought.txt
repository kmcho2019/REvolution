To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will apply one-hot state encoding to simplify next-state and output decoding logic, potentially improving performance by reducing logic depth at the expense of a small increase in area due to more flip-flops. I will register the output separately from next-state logic to avoid glitches and use non-blocking assignments throughout for consistency and synthesis friendliness. The asynchronous reset remains the same to ensure fast reset response. One-hot encoding typically improves timing and power due to simpler decoding, beneficial especially when the number of states is small (four here). Overall, this balances a slight area increase for improvements in timing and power.
