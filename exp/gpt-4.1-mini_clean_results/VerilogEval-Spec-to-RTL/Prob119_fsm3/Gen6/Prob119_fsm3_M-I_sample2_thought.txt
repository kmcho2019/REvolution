To improve PPA, restructure the next state logic as a combinational always block with a case statement instead of chained ternary operators. This typically synthesizes into simpler mux trees, reducing the critical path delay and potentially improving timing (tns/wns). The asynchronous reset remains for fast reset response. The output remains combinational and derived from the current state, preserving Moore FSM semantics. This change maintains low area and power by avoiding unnecessary complexity and keeps the design clean and readable.
