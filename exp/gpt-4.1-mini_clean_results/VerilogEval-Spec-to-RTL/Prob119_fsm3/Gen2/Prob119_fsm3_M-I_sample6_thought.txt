To optimize for better PPA, use one-hot encoding instead of binary encoding since it often simplifies state decoding logic and can reduce critical path delays in FPGA or ASIC implementations, improving performance (timing). One-hot state encoding also reduces glitches and can reduce power from fewer signal transitions in logic. The area overhead of one-hot with 4 states is acceptable (4 flip-flops instead of 2), especially since the area is already minimal.

Also, consolidate the next state logic and output logic into a single combinational always block to reduce redundant case statements and simplify logic, potentially improving synthesis efficiency.

Keep the asynchronous active-high reset and use non-blocking assignments consistently for sequential logic.

This approach balances improving timing and power at minor area cost and maintains the clear Moore FSM structure.
