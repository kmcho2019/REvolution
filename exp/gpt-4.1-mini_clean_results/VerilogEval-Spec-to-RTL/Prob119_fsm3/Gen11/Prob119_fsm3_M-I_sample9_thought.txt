To optimize the FSM for better PPA, convert the state encoding from binary to one-hot. One-hot encoding often improves timing by simplifying next state decoding logic and can reduce the logic depth, especially in FPGA implementations where flip-flops are abundant. It may slightly increase area but can improve the timing slack (tns/wns) and reduce power due to simpler combinational logic. Also, merge the next state and output logic into a single combinational always block for minimal logic duplication. Keep the asynchronous reset and positive-edge clocked state register unchanged. This should yield better timing and potentially lower power while maintaining the correctness and simplicity of the design.
