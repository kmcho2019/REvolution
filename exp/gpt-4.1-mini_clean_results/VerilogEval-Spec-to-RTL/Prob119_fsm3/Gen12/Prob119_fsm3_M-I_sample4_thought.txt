To improve PPA, I will convert the FSM from binary encoding to one-hot encoding. One-hot encoding typically simplifies next state logic by reducing combinational complexity and can improve timing (TNS/WNS) at the cost of a small increase in area. Given the FSM has only 4 states, the area overhead is minimal and power might slightly improve due to simpler decoding logic. Additionally, I will keep the asynchronous reset as required and maintain separate combinational next state logic and output logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness. The output remains a simple combinational function of state. This trade-off is expected to improve timing (lower negative slack and smaller effective clock period) with modest area increase and likely minor power impact, thus improving overall PPA.
