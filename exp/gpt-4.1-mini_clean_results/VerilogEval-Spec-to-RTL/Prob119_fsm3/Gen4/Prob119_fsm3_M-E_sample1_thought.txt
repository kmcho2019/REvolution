Use a one-hot encoded FSM to simplify combinational logic and improve timing at the cost of increased flip-flops. Implement asynchronous reset using a separate reset process assigning the one-hot encoded reset state. Separate combinational logic for next state decoding from state register and output logic. Since output is Moore, encode output as combinational logic directly from one-hot encoded state bits. This approach yields a fast and conceptually straightforward FSM with easily modifiable states, and clear state-to-output mapping, differing from binary encoding.
