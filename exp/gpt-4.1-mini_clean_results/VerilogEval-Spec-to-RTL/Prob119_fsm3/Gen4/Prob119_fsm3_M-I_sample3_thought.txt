To optimize the FSM for better power and area without compromising timing, I will convert the state encoding from binary to one-hot. One-hot encoding uses one flip-flop per state, simplifying next state logic and potentially reducing glitches in combinational logic. This can reduce dynamic power due to simpler decoding. Also, I'll retain the asynchronous reset for immediate reset response as specified, but the one-hot encoding inherently results in simpler logic. For output logic, I'll implement it as a simple combinational assignment from the one-hot state signals instead of a case statement to minimize combinational logic. This approach should reduce the overall area and power while maintaining or improving timing.
