To optimize for power and area without affecting performance, reduce unnecessary toggling in the anyedge register by only updating it when an edge is detected. Introduce an enable signal to register anyedge only on edge detection, preventing needless flip-flop toggling and saving dynamic power. This minor gating will slightly reduce power and switching activity with negligible impact on timing, as the enable is simple combinational logic. The area impact is minimal, potentially reducing toggling-related routing congestion.
