To optimize for better PPA, we focus on reducing power and slightly improving timing while keeping area minimal. Since the original design is already minimal, the primary opportunity is to gate the clock to the anyedge register updates, preventing unnecessary toggling when no edge is detected. We can add an enable signal that is high only when an edge is detected on any bit. This clock gating reduces dynamic power when inputs are stable. We keep the logic simple by using the edge_detect signal to generate this enable. This optimization balances power reduction with minimal area overhead and can slightly improve effective clock timing by reducing switching activity.
