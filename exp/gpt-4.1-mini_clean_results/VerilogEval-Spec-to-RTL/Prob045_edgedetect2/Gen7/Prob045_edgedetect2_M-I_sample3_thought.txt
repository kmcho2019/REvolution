The original design is already efficient and minimal in registers and logic. To optimize power and area slightly, we can add an enable signal so that the anyedge output register only updates when an input change has occurred, reducing unnecessary toggling of anyedge bits. This gating reduces switching activity and dynamic power with minimal added logic (one OR gate across XOR bits for enable). This may slightly improve power without impacting timing or area significantly, as the logic is simple. It maintains the one-cycle delayed edge detection requirement.
