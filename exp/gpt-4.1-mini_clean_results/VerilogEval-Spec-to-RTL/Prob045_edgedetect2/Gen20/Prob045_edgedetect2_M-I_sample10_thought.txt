To improve PPA, we consolidate the per-bit previous state registers into a single 8-bit register, and perform the edge detection using bitwise XOR on the entire vector at once inside a single always block. This reduces the number of always blocks, the flip-flop count (one vector register vs. 8 scalar registers), and enables synthesis tools to optimize the design more effectively. This approach maintains correct functionality while likely reducing power and area, and potentially improving timing due to better register packing and less clock tree load.
