To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will consolidate the per-bit edge detection logic into a single vectorized always block instead of using eight separate submodules. This approach uses one 8-bit register to store the previous input vector and performs an 8-bit bitwise XOR with the current input vector to detect edges. This reduces the number of registers, simplifies the logic, and reduces dynamic power by minimizing toggling and unnecessary clocked elements. It also enhances synthesis optimization opportunities for area and timing, as the logic is more compact and shared. The output is registered and delayed by one clock cycle, fulfilling the requirement.
