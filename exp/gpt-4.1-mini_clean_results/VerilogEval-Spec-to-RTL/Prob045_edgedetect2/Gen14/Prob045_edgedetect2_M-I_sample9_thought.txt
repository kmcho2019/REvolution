To optimize power and area further, reduce unnecessary toggling of the anyedge output register. Currently, anyedge toggles every clock cycle based on the XOR of current and previous input, which can cause frequent register switching even when no edges occurred. Introducing an enable signal to update anyedge only when an edge is detected minimizes switching in the output register. This can be achieved by calculating an edge detection vector and only updating anyedge when this vector is non-zero, otherwise hold the previous output. This reduces dynamic power and potentially simplifies the synthesized logic, improving overall PPA without impacting functional correctness or latency.
