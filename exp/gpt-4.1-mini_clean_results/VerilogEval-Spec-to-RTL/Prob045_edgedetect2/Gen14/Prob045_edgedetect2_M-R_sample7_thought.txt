Refactor the design by storing the previous 8-bit input vector in a single 8-bit register and computing the edge detection output by bitwise XOR of the current input and the registered previous input. This vectorized approach eliminates the need for multiple submodules and separate registers per bit, leading to simpler RTL and better synthesis optimization potential. The edge detection output is registered one clock cycle after the change to meet the timing requirement, by first computing the XOR combinationally and then registering the result. This differs from the original per-bit always block by using a single always block with vector operations and separating combinational and sequential logic.
