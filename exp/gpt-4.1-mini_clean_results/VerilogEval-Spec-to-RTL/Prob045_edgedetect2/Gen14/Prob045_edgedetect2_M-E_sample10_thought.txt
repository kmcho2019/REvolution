Implement the edge detection by maintaining a pipeline of two consecutive input samples and using a combinational comparison of these two registers. Specifically, use two registers prev_in and curr_in clocked on positive edges, where curr_in holds the previous input and prev_in holds the input before that. Then, anyedge is produced by XORing prev_in and curr_in combinationally, giving a one-cycle delayed detection compared to the input change. This pipeline structure explicitly separates the sampling of inputs and the edge detection logic, allowing better timing isolation and potentially easier timing optimization in large designs.
