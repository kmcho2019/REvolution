To optimize the design for better power and area without hurting performance, I will add clock enable logic to update registers only when an edge is detected in any bit. This avoids unnecessary toggling of registers and combinational XOR logic when inputs are stable, reducing dynamic power. The output anyedge will be zero when no changes occur and will still assert one cycle after input edges. This change introduces a small control logic but likely reduces switching activity and overall dynamic power, while maintaining minimal area and timing impact.
