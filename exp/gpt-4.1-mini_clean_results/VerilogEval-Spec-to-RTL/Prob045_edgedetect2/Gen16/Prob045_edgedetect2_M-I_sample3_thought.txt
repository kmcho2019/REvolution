To improve PPA (power, performance, and area), I will consolidate the edge detection logic into a single always block operating on the entire 8-bit input vector simultaneously. Instead of eight separate one-bit registers, I will use one 8-bit register to store the previous input vector. This reduces area by eliminating duplicate register logic and improves power by reducing clock toggling and control overhead. The XOR can be done in parallel across the vector to detect edges per bit, and the output anyedge is registered to ensure it is asserted one cycle after the edge occurs. This vectorized approach reduces logic replication and allows synthesis tools to optimize XOR and registers efficiently, likely improving timing and reducing power consumption.
