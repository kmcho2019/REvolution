To optimize the existing design for power and possibly area without impacting performance, I will add a clock enable signal that only enables capturing prev_in and updating anyedge when at least one bit of the input changes. This reduces unnecessary toggling of flip-flops and logic when the input is stable, lowering dynamic power consumption. The XOR operation and edge detection remain unchanged, so performance and correctness are maintained. The clock enable gating is simple and synthesizable, with minimal overhead.
