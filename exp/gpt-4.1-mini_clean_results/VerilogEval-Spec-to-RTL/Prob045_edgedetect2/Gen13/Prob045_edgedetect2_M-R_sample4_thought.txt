Refactor the design by storing the entire previous 8-bit input vector in a single register and then computing the bitwise XOR with the current input vector to detect edges for all bits simultaneously. The result of the XOR is registered to ensure that the anyedge output is asserted one cycle after the input transition. This approach replaces multiple single-bit edge detector modules with one vectorized implementation, improving compactness and potentially enhancing area and power efficiency.
