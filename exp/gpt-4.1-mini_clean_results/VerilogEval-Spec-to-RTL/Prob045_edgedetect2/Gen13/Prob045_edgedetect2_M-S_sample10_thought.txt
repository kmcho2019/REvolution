The given solution is already minimal in logic and straightforward in implementation. One small simplification is to initialize the delayed input register to zero by default and combine the two assignments in a single always block without changing functionality. However, this is mostly stylistic and does not reduce complexity. Since the requirement is to detect edges and output 1 the cycle after, we must keep the delayed input register. Thus, the minimal solution remains an always block on posedge clk where anyedge is assigned the XOR of current and delayed input, then delayed input is updated. No further simplification is possible without changing the behavior.
