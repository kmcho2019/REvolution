Refactor the design by separating the storage of the previous input and the computation of the anyedge output into distinct steps. Instead of directly computing anyedge inside the sequential block, store the edge detection result in a wire by XORing current input with the registered previous input, and then register this edge detection result on the clock's positive edge. This way, the combinational part (XOR) is explicitly separated from the sequential logic (registers), improving readability and making the design clearer about data flow stages.
