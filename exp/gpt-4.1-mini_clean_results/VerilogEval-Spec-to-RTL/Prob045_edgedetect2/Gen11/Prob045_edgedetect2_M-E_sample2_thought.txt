Instead of storing the entire previous input vector and XORing with the current input, maintain two registers per bit: one capturing the input at the previous clock cycle, and the other capturing the XOR of the current and previous input (i.e., edge detection). The edge output is then delayed by one clock cycle by storing this XOR result in a register. This introduces an explicit pipeline stage for the edge detection signal, making the output anyedge a registered, one-cycle-delayed edge pulse for each bit. This approach is more explicit about the pipelining and can be easily extended or modified for further timing optimization or integration with multi-stage pipelines.
