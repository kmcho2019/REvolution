Instead of directly XORing the current input with its delayed version, use a two-stage register pipeline to capture the input at the last two clock cycles. Then, compare these two registers to detect edges with a one-cycle delay, setting the output accordingly. This double-register approach may improve timing by evenly pipelining the input data and making edge detection logic structurally clearer. It also separates the delay stages, which can help with timing closure and debugging in complex designs.
