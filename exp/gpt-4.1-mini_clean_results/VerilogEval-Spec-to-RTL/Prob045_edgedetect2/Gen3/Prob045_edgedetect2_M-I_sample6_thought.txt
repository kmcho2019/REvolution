Optimize the design by reducing storage from a 2-bit shift register per bit to a single 8-bit register holding the previous input state. This cuts area and power by halving registers and simplifies logic. Compute edges by XORing the current input with the stored previous input, and register the edge detection to meet the requirement that `anyedge` is high one cycle after the input changes. This approach is simpler, saves registers, and maintains the required output timing without added logic complexity.
