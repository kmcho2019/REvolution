Refactor the design by storing the previous input in a register, then using a registered delayed version of the XOR of the current and previous inputs to generate the output. This approach replaces the separate wire for edge detection with a registered intermediate signal. By first registering the current input, then XORing the delayed signals, and registering the result again, we explicitly pipeline the detection process into two clock stages, maintaining the original functionality but structuring the logic as a two-stage pipeline.
