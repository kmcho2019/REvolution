To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) of the 8-to-3 priority encoder, I will implement it using a one-hot to binary encoder approach via a casez statement on the input. This approach leverages the priority encoding feature of casez to directly encode the position of the least significant set bit. This can reduce logic depth compared to cascaded if-elses and may enable synthesis tools to optimize for speed and power better. Additionally, it is more concise and clear. The output remains zero if no bits are set.
