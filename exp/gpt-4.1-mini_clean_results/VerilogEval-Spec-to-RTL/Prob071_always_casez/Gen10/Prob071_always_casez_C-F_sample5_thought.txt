Fuse the clarity and simplicity of the chained conditional approach with the robustness of a casez-based priority encoder. Use a combinational always block with a casez statement that explicitly matches the input pattern starting from the least significant bit. This approach avoids long ternary chains and enables synthesis tools to generate optimized priority encoder logic, potentially improving performance, power, and area by enabling better logic sharing and minimizing evaluated conditions. Default output zero is set when no bits are high, per the spec. The always block ensures clean, structured priority encoding without relying on complex nested ternaries, while maintaining readability and ease of extension.
