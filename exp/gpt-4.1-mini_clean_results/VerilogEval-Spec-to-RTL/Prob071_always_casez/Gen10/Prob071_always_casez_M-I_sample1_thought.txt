To optimize the priority encoder for better performance and possibly reduce the critical path, use a balanced binary-tree approach by splitting the input into two halves and encoding each half recursively. This approach reduces the number of logic levels compared to a linear chain of priority checks. The design will first check if any bit is set in the lower 4 bits; if yes, encode the position there; otherwise, encode the position in the upper 4 bits offset by 4. This reduces the priority chain depth from 8 to about 2-3 logic levels and can improve timing with minimal increase in area or power.
