To improve the priority encoder's PPA, replace the linear chain of ternary operators with a binary-tree structure using intermediate signals that encode whether a higher or lower group of bits contain any '1's. This reduces the longest combinational path, improving timing (WNS, TNS) by balancing the logic depth. The area and power remain similar or slightly better due to reduced logic fan-in and better structure. Specifically, we split the input into two 4-bit groups, detect which group has a set bit, and then prioritize inside the selected group using smaller priority encoders recursively. This reduces logic depth from 8 to about log2(8)=3 levels, optimizing critical path.
