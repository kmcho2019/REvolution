To optimize the priority encoder for better timing and potentially reduced power, implement the encoding using a casez statement inside a combinational always block. This approach enables synthesis tools to map the logic into optimized priority encoder primitives or parallel structures, reducing logic depth compared to a chained ternary operator. The casez pattern matches from least to most significant bit, reporting the first set bit's position, and outputs zero if none are set. This also helps reduce switching activity since the logic evaluates in parallel rather than sequentially in a chain.
