To improve timing and power of the priority encoder while maintaining a compact area, implement the priority encoder using a parallel approach based on bitwise AND with the inverted lower bits. This approach uses a mask to isolate the least significant set bit and then encodes that bit position using a simple priority encoding logic. Using a one-hot mask and a binary encoder reduces the number of sequential mux stages, improving timing by enabling the synthesis tool to optimize the logic better and reducing switching activity since only one bit is selected. This also minimizes power consumption and keeps the area low.
