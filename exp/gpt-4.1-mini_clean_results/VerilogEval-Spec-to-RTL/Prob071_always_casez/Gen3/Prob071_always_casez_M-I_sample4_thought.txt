To optimize the priority encoder for better timing and power, I will implement a binary-search style priority encoder rather than a linear chain of ternary operators. This method breaks the input vector into halves recursively to find the lowest set bit more quickly, reducing the longest combinational path and thus improving the maximum frequency. It also allows the synthesis tool to generate a balanced tree of multiplexers rather than a long chain, reducing switching activity and power. The output is zero when no bits are set, consistent with the specification. This approach provides a good tradeoff for an 8-bit input, improving timing and power with minimal area impact.
