To optimize the priority encoder for better performance, power, and area, use a casez statement inside a combinational always block. This enables synthesis tools to infer an efficient parallel priority encoder circuit rather than a sequential if-else ladder. The casez with bit pattern matching allows matching the input with priority starting from LSB to MSB succinctly and encourages synthesis tools to produce optimized hardware with reduced critical path delay and possibly smaller area. The output remains zero if no bits are set.
