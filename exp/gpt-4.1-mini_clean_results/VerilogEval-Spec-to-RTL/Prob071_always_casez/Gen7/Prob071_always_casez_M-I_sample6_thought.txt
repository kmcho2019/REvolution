To optimize the priority encoder for better performance (lower delay) while maintaining minimal area and power, replace the long priority-encoded ternary chain with a parallel approach that uses a one-hot to binary encoder method. Specifically, compute a mask of all the input bits up to each position to identify the least significant set bit, then encode the one-hot vector into binary using a compact combinational logic. This reduces the critical path delay compared to the chained conditional operators by avoiding long cascades. The approach is still purely combinational and synthesizable, suitable for 8 bits, and reduces switching activity along long mux chains, likely improving power slightly.
