Fuse the compactness and clarity of the combinational conditional operator chain (Example 1) with the clarity and synthesis friendliness of structured combinational logic (Example 2). Use a combinational always block with a casez statement to encode the first set bit from LSB to MSB. This combines the easy readability, better synthesis optimization potential, and clean priority logic. Default output is zero if no bits set. This approach balances performance (shorter combinational chain due to casez), power (less switching from serial checking), and area (structured logic allows better optimization) for an 8-bit priority encoder.
