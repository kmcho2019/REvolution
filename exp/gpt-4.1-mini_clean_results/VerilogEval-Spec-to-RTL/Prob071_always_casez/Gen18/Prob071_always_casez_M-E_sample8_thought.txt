Implement the priority encoder using a parallel one-hot encoding approach with a combinational casez statement. We first create a one-hot mask corresponding to the first set bit in the input by checking bits from least significant to most significant, leveraging the "casez" pattern matching with a 'z' wildcard to simplify priority encoding logic. Then, the position output is derived from the one-hot mask using a simple combinational case statement. If no bits are set, output zero. This approach is clean, synthesis-friendly, and avoids nested if-else conditions, potentially yielding improved area and power due to simpler and flatter logic.
