We observe from the waveform that z = 1 only when both inputs are equal (both 0 or both 1), except for some moments where z=1 even if x=y=0, indicating an OR condition with both inputs being low. Another way to express this is that z is high either when both x and y are 0 or both are 1. We can implement this with two conditions combined: one AND gate that detects when both inputs are 1, and one NOR gate detecting when both inputs are 0. Then OR the outputs of these gates to produce z. This approach uses basic gates explicitly rather than relying on the xnor operator.
