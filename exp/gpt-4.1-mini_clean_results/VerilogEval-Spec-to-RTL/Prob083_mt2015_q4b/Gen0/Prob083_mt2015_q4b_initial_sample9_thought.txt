From the waveform, we can deduce the logic for output z based on inputs x and y:

- When both x and y are 0, z = 1
- When x=1 and y=0, z = 0
- When x=0 and y=1, z = 0
- When x=1 and y=1, z = 1

This matches the logical operation: z = ~(x ^ y), which is equivalently the XNOR of x and y.

Therefore, we implement z as the XNOR of x and y.
