Analyze the waveform and derive the logic function for output z based on inputs x and y.

Observing the waveform:

- z is 1 when both x and y are 0 (x=0,y=0 => z=1)
- z is 0 when x=1,y=0 or x=0,y=1 (exclusive OR is 1)
- z is 1 when both x and y are 1 (x=1,y=1 => z=1)

This behavior matches the XNOR of x and y, i.e., z = ~(x ^ y).

Implement z as the XNOR of inputs x and y.
