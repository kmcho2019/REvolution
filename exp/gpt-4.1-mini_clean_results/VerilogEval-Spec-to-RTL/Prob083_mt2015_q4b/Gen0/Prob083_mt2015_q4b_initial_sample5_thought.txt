From the waveform, we see the output z depends on the inputs x and y as follows:

- When both x and y are 0, z is 1.
- When either x or y is 1 but not both, z is 0.
- When both x and y are 1, z is 1.

This matches the XNOR operation (output is 1 if inputs are equal, 0 otherwise).

Therefore, we can implement z = x XNOR y.
