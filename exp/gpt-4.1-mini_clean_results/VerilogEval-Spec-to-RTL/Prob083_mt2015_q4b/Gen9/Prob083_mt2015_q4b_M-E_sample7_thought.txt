Implement the module using a small synchronous FSM with a register to hold the output state. The output z is stored in a flip-flop and updated on any change in inputs x or y. The FSM sets z=1 when both inputs are equal (00 or 11), and z=0 otherwise. This explicit sequential implementation highlights the input-to-output relationship using a simple clocked process, differing from combinational logic, and allows a design with timing control and potential extension for more complex behaviors.
