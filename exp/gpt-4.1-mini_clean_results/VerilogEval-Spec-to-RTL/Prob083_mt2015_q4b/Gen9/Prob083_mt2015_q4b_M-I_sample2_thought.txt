The original implementation uses the sum-of-products form (x & y) | (~x & ~y) which is logically correct but can be slightly less optimal than a direct XNOR operator. Modern synthesis tools usually optimize the built-in XNOR operator (~^) more effectively in terms of power and area because it maps directly to XOR/XNOR gates in the standard cell library, resulting in potentially fewer transitions and less logic complexity.

Therefore, to optimize power and area without affecting performance, I will replace the sum-of-products expression with the concise XNOR operator: assign z = ~(x ^ y);

This change maintains readability, slightly reduces gate count, and may yield better PPA after synthesis.

The code remains purely combinational with no additional logic, ensuring zero delay overhead.
