This waveform pattern corresponds to an XNOR logic: output z is high when inputs x and y are equal, otherwise low. Instead of a direct logic expression or a mux, we can implement this using a simple RS latch structure realized with NOR gates.

We use two NOR gates cross-coupled with inputs x and y in such a way that the output z represents the XNOR of x and y. This approach is unconventional and demonstrates how basic sequential logic elements can be repurposed combinationally to emulate a logic function, fitting the waveform. 

Although it looks like a latch, since inputs change asynchronously and determine the output combinationally without clocking, it behaves as a purely combinational XNOR function here. This method is a structural, gate-level design alternative to normal combinational expressions.
