The output z is high only when both inputs are equal and not both zero, or when both inputs are zero except for some intervals. Analyzing the waveform, z = 1 exactly when (x AND y) OR (NOT x AND NOT y AND NOT (x AND NOT y OR not x AND y)) simplifies just to z = (x & y) | (~x & ~y). This is the XNOR operation. So the simplest solution is to assign z as x XNOR y using the ^~ operator for readability and minimal logic.
