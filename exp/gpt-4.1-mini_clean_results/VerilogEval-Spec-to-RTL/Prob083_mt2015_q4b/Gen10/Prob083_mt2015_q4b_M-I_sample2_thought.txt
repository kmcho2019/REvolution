The original implementation uses a single XNOR gate, which is already minimal and optimal in terms of area and power for combinational logic performing equality check on two bits. The output z matches the waveform perfectly, being 1 only when x and y are equal.

An alternative implementation could be a behavioral style or using a ternary operator, but this won't improve PPA and might even increase area and delay due to synthesis heuristics.

Hence, the best approach is to keep the original XNOR-based design, which is minimal and direct.

If an enhancement is required, adding a few synthesis directives or attributes could potentially help synthesis tools optimize better, but these are tool and technology-specific and not guaranteed to improve PPA universally.
