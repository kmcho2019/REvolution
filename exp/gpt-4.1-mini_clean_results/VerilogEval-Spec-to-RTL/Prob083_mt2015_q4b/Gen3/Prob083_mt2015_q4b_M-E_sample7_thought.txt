The output z can be derived by checking if the inputs x and y are equal. Instead of directly using an XNOR gate, we can implement equality by comparing the inputs using a small combinational circuit: output z is 1 if (x and y) or (not x and not y). This logic can be expressed as (x & y) | (~x & ~y). This is equivalent to XNOR but realized through AND, OR, and NOT gates.
