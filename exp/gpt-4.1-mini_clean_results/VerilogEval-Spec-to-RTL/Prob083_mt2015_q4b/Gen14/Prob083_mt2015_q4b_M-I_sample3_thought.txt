The existing implementation uses a direct combinational XNOR gate, which perfectly matches the waveform and is minimal in area and power. Since the waveform behavior is stable and purely combinational, any further improvement to PPA is negligible or impractical within the module boundary. However, to potentially further reduce power consumption in a real silicon environment, especially if inputs are expected to be stable for intervals, we could consider registering inputs or outputs to reduce glitching or use a latch-based approach with a clock. But this requires additional clocking which the specification doesn't provide.

Thus, no changes are necessary or beneficial to the design as given.
