The original solution implements z as the XNOR of x and y, which perfectly matches the waveform. Since the waveform corresponds exactly to z = 1 when x equals y and 0 otherwise, this is already the minimal Boolean function.

From a PPA perspective, using a single XNOR gate is optimal for area and power. Performance cannot be improved because the logic is combinational and minimal.

Hence, no improvements can be made in logic or coding style that would reduce area, power, or delay beyond the current solution. Any further optimizations must be technology-specific or synthesis-tool-dependent.
