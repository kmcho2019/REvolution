The output z should be high when inputs x and y are equal, matching the waveform. Both the XNOR operator (~^) and the equality operator (==) achieve this with minimal logic. To combine the best ideas, we use the explicit XNOR operator for direct hardware correspondence and clarity, along with a concise comment explaining the logic equivalence, improving readability without losing hardware efficiency. This fusion keeps the code minimal, idiomatic, and clearly documents the intended function, ensuring optimal PPA and maintainability.
