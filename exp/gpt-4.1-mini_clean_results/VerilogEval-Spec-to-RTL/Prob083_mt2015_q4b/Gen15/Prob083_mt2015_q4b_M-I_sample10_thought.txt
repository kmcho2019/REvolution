The original solution uses an always_comb block with reg output, which, although correct, is slightly heavier than necessary. Since z is purely combinational and depends only on whether x equals y, the logic corresponds to an XNOR gate. Replacing the procedural always_comb block with a continuous assignment using the XNOR operator will reduce area and power by synthesizing a simple combinational gate without any register inference. This yields minimal and clean logic, improving PPA (power, performance, area) slightly and simplifying the design.
