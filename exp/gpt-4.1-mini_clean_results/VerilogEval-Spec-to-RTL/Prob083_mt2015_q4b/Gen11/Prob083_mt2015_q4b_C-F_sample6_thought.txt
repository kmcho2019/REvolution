To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will combine the clarity and synthesis friendliness of the equality comparison `(x == y)` with the explicitness of an XNOR operator form. Since `(x == y)` is functionally equivalent to `~(x ^ y)` and is clearly understood by synthesis tools as an XNOR, I will use the bitwise XNOR operator (`^~`), which is concise, explicit, and guaranteed to synthesize efficiently as a single XNOR gate in most tools. This approach maximizes readability, synthesis optimization, and clarity by explicitly indicating the XNOR relationship while avoiding any ambiguity of equality operators in certain synthesis scenarios.

Fusion strategy:
- Use the bitwise XNOR operator (`x ^~ y`) for clarity and synthesis efficiency.
- Provide a clear comment explaining the logic and relation to the waveform.
- Maintain a continuous assignment for simple combinational logic.
- Keep the interface exactly as specified.

This design achieves the minimal logic expression, excellent PPA metrics, and maximal clarity.
