Implement the output z as the output of a small synchronous finite state machine (FSM) that samples inputs x and y at fixed time intervals and sets z accordingly. This FSM approach will store the previous inputs and update z based on conditions reflecting the waveform behavior: z is 1 when both inputs are 0, both are 1, or after certain transitions. The FSM state encoding and combinational logic will reflect the pattern in the waveform, providing a novel solution different from direct combinational logic.
