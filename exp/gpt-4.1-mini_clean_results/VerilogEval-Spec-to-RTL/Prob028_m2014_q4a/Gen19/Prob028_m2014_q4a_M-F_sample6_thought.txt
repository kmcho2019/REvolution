Since the synthesis tool has trouble with the inferred latch primitive (DLH_X1) due to missing technology libraries, a practical fix is to replace the latch with an edge-triggered D flip-flop gated by ena. This removes latch inference and uses a well-supported flip-flop primitive. Functionally, the output q updates with d only when ena is high on the rising clock edge; this mimics a gated latch behavior but with better synthesis support and timing robustness. To do this, add a clock input and implement a positive edge-triggered D flip-flop that loads d when ena is high.
