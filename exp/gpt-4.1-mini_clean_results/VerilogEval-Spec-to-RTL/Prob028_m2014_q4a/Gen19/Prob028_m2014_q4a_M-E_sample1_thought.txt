To implement a D latch differently, use a positive level-sensitive enable latch approach by defining the latch behavior inside an always block sensitive only to ena and d, but instead of relying on the absence of assignment to hold state, explicitly assign q in both conditions: when ena is high, q follows d; when ena is low, q retains its previous value by assigning q <= q. This explicit self-assignment ensures latch inference with better synthesis compatibility in many tools. Using non-blocking assignments in this latch model is acceptable and sometimes preferred for synthesis clarity.
