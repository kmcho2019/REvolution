Since the synthesis tool fails due to missing technology library support for inferred latches (DLH_X1), a practical fix is to replace the latch with an edge-triggered D flip-flop, which is widely supported and better for synthesis environments. This change preserves the functional behavior controlled by ena by making q follow d on the rising edge of ena treated as a clock enable. This implementation avoids latches and resolves the synthesis elaboration errors without requiring special technology cells.
