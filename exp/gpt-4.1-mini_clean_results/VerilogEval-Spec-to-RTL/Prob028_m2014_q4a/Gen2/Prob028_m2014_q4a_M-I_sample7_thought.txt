To ensure better synthesis compatibility and to avoid issues with latch inference, I will switch from non-blocking (`<=`) to blocking (`=`) assignments inside the level-sensitive always block. Blocking assignments are generally preferred in combinational and latch logic as they simplify synthesis tool interpretation. Additionally, I will keep the sensitivity list explicit and limited to `ena` and `d`. This should help the synthesis tool infer a latch correctly without requiring explicit latch cell instantiation. This approach maintains functional correctness while improving synthesis portability and potentially area and power due to clearer intent for latch inference.
