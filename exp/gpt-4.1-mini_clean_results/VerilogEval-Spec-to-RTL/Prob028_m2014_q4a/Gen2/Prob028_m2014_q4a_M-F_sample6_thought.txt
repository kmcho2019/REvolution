To help synthesis tools properly infer the latch and avoid issues with non-blocking assignments in level-sensitive latch code, replace the non-blocking assignment (`<=`) with a blocking assignment (`=`) inside the always block. Using blocking assignments in latch descriptions is a common best practice for better synthesis results and tool compatibility.
