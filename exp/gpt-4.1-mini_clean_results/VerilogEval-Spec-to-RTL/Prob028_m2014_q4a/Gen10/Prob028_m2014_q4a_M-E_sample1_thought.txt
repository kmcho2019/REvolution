Implement the D latch using a continuous assignment with a conditional (ternary) operator. Declare an internal reg to hold the latch value and use an always block triggered only on ena or d. When ena is high, the latch is transparent, so the output follows d; when ena is low, the output holds its previous value by maintaining the internal reg. Use a blocking assignment (=) in the always block to aid synthesis tools in latch inference. This approach separates the data holding element and simplifies sensitivity and assignments, improving synthesis compatibility and avoiding the tool-specific latch primitive errors.
