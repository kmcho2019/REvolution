To ensure the latch is correctly inferred by synthesis tools like Yosys, the always block should be written so that the output q is assigned only when ena is high, and left unassigned otherwise. Leaving q unassigned in some branches of a combinational always block is the standard method to infer level-sensitive latches, as it signals to the tool that the value should be held (latched). Therefore, removing the explicit "q = q;" assignment and making the else branch do nothing will allow proper latch inference. This approach relies on q being declared as reg and the always block being sensitive to d and ena.
