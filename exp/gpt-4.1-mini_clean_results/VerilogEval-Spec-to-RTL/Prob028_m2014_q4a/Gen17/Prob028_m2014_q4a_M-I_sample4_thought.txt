To improve synthesis compatibility and clarity, especially with tools like Yosys, I will implement the D latch using a style known to reliably infer latches: an always block sensitive only to ena and d, assigning q non-blockingly (`<=`) when ena is high and leaving q unassigned otherwise to infer the latch. Nonblocking assignments in level-sensitive latches improve synthesis tool recognition and simulation correctness. The sensitivity list will include both ena and d so changes to inputs update q appropriately when enabled. This style avoids relying on specific latch primitives, enhancing portability. Power and area remain minimal as the design is a simple latch. Timing behavior remains a level-sensitive latch.
