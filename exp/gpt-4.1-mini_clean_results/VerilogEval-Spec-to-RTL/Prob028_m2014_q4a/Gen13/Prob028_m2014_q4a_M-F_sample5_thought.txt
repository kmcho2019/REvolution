To improve compatibility with synthesis tools and possibly avoid errors related to missing latch cells like DLH_X1, rewrite the always block sensitivity list using the more modern and standard Verilog style: `always @*` or `always @(ena or d)`. Also, use non-blocking assignments (`<=`) to better match synthesizer expectations, even though for latches blocking assignment is common.
