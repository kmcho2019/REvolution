To ensure proper latch inference and synthesis compatibility, use a level-sensitive always block with blocking assignments. The always block should be sensitive only to signals that control latch transparency (ena and d), and the latch behavior can be modeled by assigning q to d when ena is high, and by not assigning q otherwise (to hold state). Avoid redundant self-assignments and non-blocking assignments in a latch model. This style is widely accepted by synthesis tools like Yosys and avoids unknown primitive errors.
