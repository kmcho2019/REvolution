To ensure synthesis tools correctly infer a transparent D latch, use an always block sensitive to both the enable signal and data input explicitly (i.e., `always @(ena or d)`), and provide a complete assignment to `q` only when `ena` is high. When `ena` is low, do not assign `q`, allowing it to retain its previous value. Also, use blocking assignment (`=`) inside the always block for latch inference. This style clarifies the latch inference for synthesis, avoids implicit incomplete assignments inside an `always @(*)` block, and improves compatibility with synthesis tools.
