Implement the D latch by using a transparent latch style with an always block sensitive to changes in ena or d, but instead of directly assigning q inside the always block, use an internal register 'latch_q' to hold the value and assign it continuously to the output q. This keeps the output a wire but still models latch behavior with a reg inside. By separating the internal state from the output, the design can be clearer in intent. The always block updates 'latch_q' when ena is high, otherwise retains its value, thereby modeling the latch.
