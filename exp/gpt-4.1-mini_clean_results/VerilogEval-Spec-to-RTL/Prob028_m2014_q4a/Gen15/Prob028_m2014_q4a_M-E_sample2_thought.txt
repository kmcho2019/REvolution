Implement the D latch using a clocked always block with the enable signal as the clock enable inside an asynchronous latch style. Instead of listing both d and ena in the sensitivity list, use "always @(*)" for combinational logic combined with an internal register to hold the value. Use a conditional operator to update the output q based on ena: when ena is high, q follows d; otherwise, it holds its previous value. This style clearly describes latch behavior and is often better supported by synthesis tools.
