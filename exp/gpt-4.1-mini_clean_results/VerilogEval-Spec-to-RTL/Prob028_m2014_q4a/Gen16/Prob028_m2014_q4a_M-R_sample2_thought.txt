Refactor the D latch by using a continuous assignment with a ternary operator to implement the latch behavior combinationally. The output q follows the input d when ena is high; otherwise, q retains its previous state. To achieve retention, model q as a reg updated inside an always block triggered by changes in ena or d. However, instead of keeping an internal register separate from q, make q itself a reg and update it conditionally in the always block. This avoids a separate internal register and removes the continuous assign, making the latch inference explicit and clearer for synthesis tools.
