To improve synthesis compatibility and avoid latch inference issues, replace the transparent D latch with an edge-triggered D flip-flop, which is better supported by synthesis tools and standard cell libraries. This change improves timing predictability and eliminates hold-time violations associated with latches, leading to more reliable and portable designs. Since the original interface has an enable input, implement the enable as a synchronous load: when `ena` is high at the clock edge, `q` takes the value of `d`; otherwise, `q` retains its previous state. This is a common and efficient way to implement a load-enabled D flip-flop. This approach improves PPA by enhancing performance and timing robustness at the cost of slightly increased area and power compared to a latch, but it is preferable in practical designs.
