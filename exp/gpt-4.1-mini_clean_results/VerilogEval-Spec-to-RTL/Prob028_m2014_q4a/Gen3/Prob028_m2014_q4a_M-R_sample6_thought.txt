Refactor the D latch by implementing it as a continuous assignment with a ternary operator to directly model the level-sensitive latch behavior. When enable (ena) is high, q follows d; otherwise, q retains its previous value. Since Verilog does not support direct retention of previous values in continuous assignments, model q as a reg driven by an always block that triggers on changes of ena or d, but with a nonblocking assignment for better synthesis compatibility. This approach preserves the latch behavior but uses a nonblocking assignment and a slightly different sensitivity list to avoid the synthesis issue related to technology-specific latch primitives.
