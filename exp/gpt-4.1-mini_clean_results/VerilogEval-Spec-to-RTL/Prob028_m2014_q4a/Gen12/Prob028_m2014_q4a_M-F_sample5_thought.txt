To correctly infer a D latch, use an always block sensitive to both d and ena signals. Use a blocking assignment (=) within the always block when ena is high to update q with d. When ena is low, q retains its previous value, implying latch behavior. Avoid non-blocking assignments and always @(*) to prevent synthesis issues. This coding style aligns with best practices for latch synthesis and prevents synthesis tool confusion or missing latch primitives.
