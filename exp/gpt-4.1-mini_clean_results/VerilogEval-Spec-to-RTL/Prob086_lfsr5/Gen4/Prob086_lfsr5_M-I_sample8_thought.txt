To optimize the existing 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation for better PPA, I will streamline the next state logic by avoiding a function call and instead compute the next state combinationally inside the always block. This removes function call overhead and slightly simplifies logic synthesis. I will implement the tap feedback by directly XORing the feedback bit with the tapped bits inline during the shift operation. Also, by expressing the next state in a more compact manner (using concatenation and explicit XOR), the synthesis tool can better optimize LUT sharing and area. The synchronous reset will remain the same to guarantee correct initial state. Overall, this will slightly reduce area and power, and may improve timing due to simpler combinational logic.
