I will implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a combinational feedback calculation outside the sequential block and a single non-blocking update of the whole register on the clock edge. The feedback bit is computed by XORing the LSB (q[0]) with the tapped bits q[4] and q[2] (positions 5 and 3, zero-based). Then the shift register shifts right by one bit with the feedback inserted at the MSB (q[4]). The synchronous reset initializes q to 5'b00001. This approach centralizes the feedback logic and simplifies sequential logic to a single assignment, potentially improving clarity and performance.
