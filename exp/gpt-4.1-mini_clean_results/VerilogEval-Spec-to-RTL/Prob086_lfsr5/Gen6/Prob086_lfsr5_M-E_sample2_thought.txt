I will implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a single shift register and calculate the feedback bit combinationally before the synchronous update. Specifically, I will compute the feedback as the XOR of the output bit q[0] and the tapped bits q[4] and q[2], then shift the register right and insert the feedback bit at the MSB (q[4]). This approach uses a temporary wire for feedback, keeping combinational logic separate from sequential logic, making the code clear and modular. The synchronous reset sets q to 1 on the positive edge of clk.
