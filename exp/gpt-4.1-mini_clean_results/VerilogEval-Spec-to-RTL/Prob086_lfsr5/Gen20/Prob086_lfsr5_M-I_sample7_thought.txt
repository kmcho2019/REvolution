To improve PPA, I will optimize the LFSR implementation by computing the next state combinationally as a single vector before the clock edge, then updating the register q at the positive clock edge. This reduces the number of separate non-blocking assignments and simplifies the synthesis tool's optimization by presenting a clean vector assignment. Additionally, computing the feedback once and applying XOR operations together in a vectorized manner minimizes combinational logic depth. This approach helps reduce critical path delay (improving timing), and potentially reduces power by minimizing glitches and logic toggling. The synchronous reset remains active-high and sets q to 1.
