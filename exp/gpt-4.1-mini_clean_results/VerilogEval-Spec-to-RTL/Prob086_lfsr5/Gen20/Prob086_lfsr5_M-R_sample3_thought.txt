I will refactor the LFSR by separating the next-state combinational logic from the sequential logic. The feedback bit will be computed combinationally as the XOR of q[0] and q[2] (the taps at bits 5 and 3 in zero-based indexing). Then, the next state vector is formed by shifting right and injecting the feedback at the leftmost bit. The sequential always block just updates q on the clock edge and reset. This separation improves code clarity and makes the feedback logic explicit and easy to verify.
