Instead of the conventional approach where each bit is updated in sequence using intermediate values, this design calculates the next 5-bit LFSR state as a whole in a single combinational expression inside the always block. Using the Galois LFSR taps at positions 5 and 3, the feedback is the XOR of q[0] with the taps. Then the shift is performed by concatenating the feedback bit at the MSB and the shifted lower bits. This reduces intermediate assignments, improves clarity, and centralizes the next-state logic. The synchronous reset sets the register to 1, ensuring the all-zero state is never reached.
