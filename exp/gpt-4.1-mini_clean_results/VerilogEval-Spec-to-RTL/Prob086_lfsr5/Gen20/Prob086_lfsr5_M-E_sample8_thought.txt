I will implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 by representing the state as a shift register that shifts right on every clock. The feedback is calculated from the XOR of the bits at tap positions 5 and 3 before the shift, then injected into the leftmost bit q[4]. This approach uses a combinational feedback wire calculated before the clock edge. The synchronous reset will initialize the LFSR to the non-zero state 5'b00001. The always block triggered on the positive clock edge updates the state with non-blocking assignments. Using a combinational feedback wire clarifies the feedback logic and makes the code straightforward. This design keeps the same behavior but structurally differs from the initial example by computing feedback explicitly before the register update.
