To optimize the original design for better PPA, I will compute the next state of the LFSR in combinational logic before updating the register in the sequential always block. This approach reduces the critical path inside the sequential logic by performing XOR operations outside, and simplifies the non-blocking assignments to a single register update. Also, I will use the feedback bit derived from q[0], XORed with tapped bits q[4] and q[2], to generate the new MSB and intermediate bits accordingly, following the Galois LFSR structure. This should improve timing (lower critical path delay), reduce dynamic power slightly by fewer intermediate signal toggles, and possibly reduce area by sharing the feedback calculation. The synchronous reset remains active-high and sets the register to 1.
