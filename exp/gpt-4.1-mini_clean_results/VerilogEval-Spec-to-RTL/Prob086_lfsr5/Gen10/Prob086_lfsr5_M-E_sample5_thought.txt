I will implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR by calculating the next state combinationally in a separate always_comb block and then updating the register synchronously. The feedback polynomial with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 corresponds to feedback applied to bits q[4] and q[2]. Since this is a Galois LFSR shifting right, q[0] shifts in q[1], q[1] shifts in q[2], etc., except for tapped positions where the input bit is XORed with the feedback bit (q[0]). To clarify and improve readability, I will compute the next state bits in a wire vector and then update the register q at the clock edge, with synchronous reset setting q to 5'b00001.
