Refactor the 5-bit Galois LFSR by computing the next state combinationally using an intermediate wire vector, then registering the entire next state at the clock edge. This replaces individual bit assignments with a single vector assignment, which can improve readability and potentially aid synthesis optimization by reducing bit-wise dependencies. The synchronous reset remains, setting the register to 5'b00001 on reset. The Galois LFSR feedback taps at bits 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]) are handled in the combinational next state logic, with a right shift incorporating the XOR feedback accordingly.
