Instead of directly shifting and XORing bits in a straightforward manner, we can implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR using a single feedback XOR calculation on q[0] combined with a right shift, but updating all bits together in one assignment for clarity and succinctness. We generate the feedback bit by XORing q[0] with q[2] (since taps are at bit 5 and 3, corresponding to q[4] and q[2] zero-indexed). Then the new state is formed by shifting q right and inserting the feedback bit at the MSB position. The synchronous reset sets q to 1, avoiding the zero state. This approach provides a clean and compact implementation of the maximal-length Galois LFSR.
