Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR using a single 5-bit shift register that shifts right on each clock. Instead of updating individual bits manually, compute the feedback bit as XOR of the taps (bit 4 and bit 2) and the output bit (bit 0). Then concatenate this feedback bit at the MSB while shifting right all other bits. On synchronous reset, initialize to 1 to avoid all-zero state. This approach uses a compact shifting operation with dynamic feedback injected as the new MSB.
