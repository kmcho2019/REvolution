Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 by calculating the feedback bit first as the XOR of the LSB (q[0]) and the tapped bits. Then, shift the entire register right by one position in a single vector assignment, inserting the feedback bit at the MSB position (q[4]). This approach uses a single 5-bit register assignment per clock cycle, improving clarity and potentially performance by reducing multiple partial bit assignments. Reset initializes q to 5'b00001 synchronously on the clock's positive edge.
