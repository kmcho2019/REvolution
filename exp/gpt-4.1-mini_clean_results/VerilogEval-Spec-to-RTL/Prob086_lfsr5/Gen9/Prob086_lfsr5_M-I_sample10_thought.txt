To optimize the original 5-bit Galois LFSR for better power and timing, I will explicitly compute the feedback bit first, then selectively XOR it only to the tapped bit (q[2], representing tap at position 3) during the shift. This avoids unnecessary XOR operations and makes the feedback logic clear for synthesis tools to optimize better. Also, rearranging the shift to make the right shift explicit and using a separate feedback wire can improve readability and possibly allow the synthesis tool to infer simpler logic, reducing power and improving timing slightly. The synchronous reset remains the same, initializing to 1.
