Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR by computing the entire next state vector combinationally outside the clocked always block. Use a wire to hold the next state calculated from the current state, performing the XOR feedback for tapped bits (bit 5 and bit 3). On each positive clock edge, load this next state into the register. This separates combinational and sequential logic, improving clarity and potentially synthesis optimizations. The synchronous reset sets the LFSR state to 1. This approach allows a single non-blocking assignment updating the entire vector, which is efficient and clean.
