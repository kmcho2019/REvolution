I will simplify the LFSR implementation by using a single right-shift and incorporating the feedback XOR for the tap bits in a more compact way. The taps at positions 5 and 3 correspond to bits q[4] and q[2], so the feedback bit is computed as q[0] ^ q[4] ^ q[2]. On each clock edge (if not reset), I shift q right by one bit and insert the feedback bit into the MSB, effectively implementing the Galois LFSR in a concise and clear manner while preserving maximum-length behavior and synchronous reset.
