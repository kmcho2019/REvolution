Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR by computing the next state in a purely combinational expression based on the current state and then registering that value at each clock edge. The feedback bit is the current LSB, and taps at positions 5 and 3 produce XOR feedback on corresponding bits. Using a single non-blocking assignment with the precomputed next state vector simplifies the code, improves clarity, and may yield better synthesis optimization. On reset, synchronously initialize the register to 1.
