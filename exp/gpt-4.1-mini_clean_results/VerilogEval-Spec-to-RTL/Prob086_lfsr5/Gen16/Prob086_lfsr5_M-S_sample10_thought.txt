Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3 by computing the feedback bit first, then shift right all bits and update the tapped bits by XORing with feedback. Use a single always @(posedge clk) block with synchronous reset to 1. This reduces the assignments to a single vector assignment with next state calculated beforehand, improving readability and avoiding bit-by-bit assignments.
