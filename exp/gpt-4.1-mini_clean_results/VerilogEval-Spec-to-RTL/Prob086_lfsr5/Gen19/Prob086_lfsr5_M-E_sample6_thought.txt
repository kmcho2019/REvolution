I will implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using an explicit feedback wire and a single shift operation inside the sequential always block. The feedback will be computed as the XOR of the tapped bits (q[4] and q[2]) XORed with the LSB (q[0]). This feedback bit will then be shifted into the MSB (q[4]). All other bits shift right by one position. Using a temporary wire for feedback improves clarity and avoids multiple XOR operations scattered in the always block. The synchronous active-high reset initializes q to 1.
