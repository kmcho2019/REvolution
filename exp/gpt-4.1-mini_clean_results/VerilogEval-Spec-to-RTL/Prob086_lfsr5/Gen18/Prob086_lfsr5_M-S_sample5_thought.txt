To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation, I will compute the feedback bit first as the XOR of q[0] with the tapped bit q[2]. Then, I will perform a single vector shift-right assignment in one non-blocking statement within the clocked always block. This reduces multiple bitwise assignments to a single concise update without changing functionality. The synchronous reset will set q to 1 as required. This approach keeps the logic clear, minimal, and synthesizable.
