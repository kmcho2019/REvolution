Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR as a right-shifting register where the next state is computed in a combinational always block, then assigned to q on clock rising edge. Use a wire for feedback calculated as q[0] XOR q[2] (corresponding to taps at bits 5 and 3 in 1-based indexing). Instead of updating individual bits separately, calculate the whole next-state vector at once using concatenation. This approach improves clarity and reduces scattered assignments, making the design more modular and maintainable while preserving the synchronous reset behavior.
