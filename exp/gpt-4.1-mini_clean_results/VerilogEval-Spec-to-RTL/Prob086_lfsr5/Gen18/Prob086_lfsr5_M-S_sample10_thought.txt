To simplify the design, I will compute the feedback bit once and then shift the register right in a single non-blocking assignment. The feedback bit is the XOR of q[4] and q[2], XORed with q[0] before shifting since taps are at bits 5 and 3 (zero-based 4 and 2). This approach reduces line count and avoids multiple individual bit assignments, making the code more concise while maintaining correct maximal-length Galois LFSR behavior with synchronous reset.
