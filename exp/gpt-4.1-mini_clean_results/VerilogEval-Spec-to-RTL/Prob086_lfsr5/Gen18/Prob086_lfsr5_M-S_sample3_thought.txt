To simplify the design, I will use a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge to update the entire 5-bit register vector at once. The Galois LFSR shifts right by one bit; for the tap bits at positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]), if q[0] is 1, they are XORed with q[0] to produce the next state bit; otherwise, they shift right unchanged. This can be compactly expressed by first storing the feedback bit q[0], then shifting the register right by one, and XORing the tapped bits conditionally. The reset sets the register to 1 synchronously.
