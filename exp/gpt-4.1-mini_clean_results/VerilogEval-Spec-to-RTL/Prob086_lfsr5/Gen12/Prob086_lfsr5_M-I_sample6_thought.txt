To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area, I will rewrite the LFSR using a single vector shift with feedback calculated once, minimizing the number of XOR gates in the critical path and simplifying the assignments. The feedback bit will be computed by XORing the taps q[4] and q[2] with the LSB q[0]. This approach reduces combinational logic depth and area by leveraging vector operations instead of per-bit assignments. The synchronous reset remains the same, setting the LFSR to 1. Using a compact expression for the next state also aids synthesis tools in inferring efficient shift-register implementations.
