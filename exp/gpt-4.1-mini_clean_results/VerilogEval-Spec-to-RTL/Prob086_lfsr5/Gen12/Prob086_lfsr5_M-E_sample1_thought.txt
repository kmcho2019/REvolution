I will implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using an explicit next-state combinational logic block to compute the new LFSR value before updating the register on the clock edge. This separates combinational feedback calculation from the sequential register update, improving clarity and potentially aiding synthesis optimization. The taps at bit positions 5 and 3 correspond to q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing; their XOR with the outgoing LSB (q[0]) forms the feedback. The LFSR will shift right, updating bits accordingly with feedback applied where taps exist. The synchronous reset sets q to 1 as required.
