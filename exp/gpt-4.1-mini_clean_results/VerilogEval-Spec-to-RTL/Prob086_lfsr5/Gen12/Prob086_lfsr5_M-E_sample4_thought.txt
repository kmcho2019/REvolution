Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR by representing the register as a single 5-bit vector and use a left-shift with feedback inserted at the LSB. Calculate the feedback bit as the XOR of the MSB and bit 2 taps with the current LSB. Update the register on every positive clock edge synchronously resetting it to 5'b00001. This approach differs by shifting left and combining the XOR feedback calculation into a single vector assignment, enhancing compactness and ensuring clarity, while maintaining the maximal-length property.
