Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR by directly updating the register q on each clock edge. Calculate the feedback bit as q[0]. Then shift right by one bit, and XOR the tapped bits (q[4] and q[2]) with feedback for their new values. Use nonblocking assignments inside the always block. Reset initializes q to 1. This avoids intermediate variables and is straightforward and concise.
