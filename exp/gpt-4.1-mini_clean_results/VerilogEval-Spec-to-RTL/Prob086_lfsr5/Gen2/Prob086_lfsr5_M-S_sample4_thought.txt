Use a single always block with nonblocking assignments to update the 5-bit register q on each positive clock edge. On reset, initialize q to 5'b00001. Otherwise, compute the feedback bit as the current LSB q[0], then shift q right by one. Update the tapped bits q[4] and q[2] by XORing their previous values with the feedback. The other bits just shift down from the next higher bit. This approach uses only one always block, no intermediate variables declared inside it, and straightforward assignments.
