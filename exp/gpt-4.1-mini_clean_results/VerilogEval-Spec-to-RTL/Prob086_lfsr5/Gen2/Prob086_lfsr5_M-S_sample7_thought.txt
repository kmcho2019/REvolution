Use a simple shift-right register that updates synchronously on the clock edge. Compute the feedback bit as the XOR of q[0] with the taps q[4] and q[2] (positions 5 and 3 respectively). Shift q right by one bit and insert the feedback bit at q[4]. Reset synchronously sets q to 1. This concise approach avoids complex bit-wise assignments and clarifies the LFSR operation in minimal lines.
