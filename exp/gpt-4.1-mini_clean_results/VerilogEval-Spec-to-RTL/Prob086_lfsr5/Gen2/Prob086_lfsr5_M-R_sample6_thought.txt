Refactor the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a combinational next-state logic block and a sequential register update. Compute the feedback bit as q[0] (LSB before shifting). For the next state, shift the register right; tapped bits (positions 3 and 5, i.e. q[2] and q[4]) are updated by XORing the corresponding shifted-in bit with the feedback. The MSB (q[4]) loads the feedback bit directly. Use a synchronous reset inside the clocked always block to initialize the LFSR to 1. This separation clarifies the logic by explicitly defining the next state as a combinational signal, then updating the register on clock edge.
