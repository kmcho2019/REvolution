Implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR by first storing the feedback bit as the current q[0]. Then shift all bits right by one position. For tapped bits (positions 5 and 3 → q[4] and q[2]), XOR the feedback bit with the bit shifted in. Non-tapped bits simply take the shifted bit. Use one always block triggered on posedge clk and synchronous reset to 5'b00001. This keeps logic clear and minimal.
