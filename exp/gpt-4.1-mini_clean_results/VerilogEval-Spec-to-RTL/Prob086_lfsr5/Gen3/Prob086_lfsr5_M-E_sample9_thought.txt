Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3 (1-based) by calculating the feedback bit as the XOR of the current LSB q[0] with zero (i.e., q[0] itself) and then shifting the entire register right by one bit while conditionally XORing the feedback bit into the tapped positions. Instead of bit-by-bit assignments, compute the next state vector combinationally and then register it on the positive clock edge. This approach isolates the next state calculation, making it more readable and potentially improving synthesis optimization.
