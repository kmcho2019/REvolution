To fix the simulation error, declare the feedback bit as a reg or wire outside the always block or compute it with a blocking assignment inside. Also, avoid partial bit assignments to q in the always block. Instead, create a next-state vector (next_q) that calculates all bits simultaneously based on the current q and feedback, then update q in a single nonblocking assignment. This approach ensures clean combinational logic for next state and synchronous state update. The logic remains the same: feedback = q[0], bits with taps (q[4] and q[2]) are XORed with feedback, others shift right by one.
