Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a single always block that directly updates the register on the clock's positive edge, combining synchronous reset and next-state logic in one place. The feedback bit is computed inside the sequential block as the XOR of q[0] (LSB) with q[2] (tap at bit 3) and q[4] (tap at bit 5). Then, the register shifts right, with the MSB loaded by this computed feedback. This approach avoids a separate combinational block and expresses the entire update logic sequentially, simplifying the design and emphasizing the sequential nature of the LFSR.
