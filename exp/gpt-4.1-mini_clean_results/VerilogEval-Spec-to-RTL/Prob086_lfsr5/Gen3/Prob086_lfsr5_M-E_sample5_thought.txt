Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a combinational feedback signal computed before the shift operation within a single clocked always block. Instead of declaring a temporary reg inside the always block, compute the feedback bit directly as the XOR of the tap bits and the current LSB. Then, perform a right shift of the register, injecting the feedback into the MSB. The synchronous reset sets the register to 5'b00001. This design treats the LFSR as a vector shifted in one step with a simple feedback calculation, keeping the logic concise and clear.
