To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can adopt a combinational next-state calculation approach. Instead of updating each bit individually, we compute the entire next state vector combinationally by applying the feedback XOR logic across the tapped bits and then load this next state into the register at the clock's positive edge. This method simplifies timing paths by isolating combinational logic before register updates and enables easier extension or modification of taps. The synchronous reset sets the state to 1 as required.
