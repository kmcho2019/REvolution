To optimize the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can reduce combinational delay and simplify the logic by reorganizing the assignments. Instead of directly shifting bits with multiple XORs involving q[0], we compute the feedback bit once and use it to update tapped bits only. Specifically, calculate feedback = q[0] XOR q[2] (taps at bits 5 and 3), then shift the register to the right with q[4] assigned feedback. This avoids repeated XOR uses on q[0] and decreases critical path complexity, potentially improving timing. Reset behavior remains synchronous and sets q to 1 to avoid all-zero state.
