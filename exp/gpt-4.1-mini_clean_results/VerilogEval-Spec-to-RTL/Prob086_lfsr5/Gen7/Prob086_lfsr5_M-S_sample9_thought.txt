The Galois LFSR shifts right every clock cycle, updating tapped bits by XORing them with the feedback bit (original q[0]). We can simplify the logic by directly assigning the new register state in a single synchronous always block, computing feedback once per cycle. Since the taps are at positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]), only these bits get XORed with feedback on update. The reset synchronously initializes the register to 1 (00001). By removing unnecessary comments and minimizing variable usage, the code becomes concise yet fully functional and clear.
