I will refactor the design by separating the combinational logic for the next state calculation from the sequential logic. The feedback bit is computed combinationally based on the LSB of the current state. Then, using an always block triggered on the positive clock edge, the shift register updates synchronously with reset logic. This separation improves code readability and modularity while maintaining the same functionality of a maximal-length 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 5 and 3.
