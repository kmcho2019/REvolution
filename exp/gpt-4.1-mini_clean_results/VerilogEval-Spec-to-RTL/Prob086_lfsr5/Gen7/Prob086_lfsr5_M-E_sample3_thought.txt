Instead of directly coding the shift with individual bit assignments, I will use a single concatenated shift operation combined with conditional XOR feedback logic to implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR. The feedback bit will be computed as the XOR of q[0] with the tapped bits q[4] and q[2]. After shifting right by one bit, the feedback will be conditionally XORed into the tapped positions as per Galois LFSR behavior, but to simplify, we can handle this by constructing the next state from shifted bits and updated tap bits using combinational logic. This approach keeps code clean, avoids multiple assignments, and emphasizes the shift register nature. The synchronous reset sets the register to 5'b00001 as required.
