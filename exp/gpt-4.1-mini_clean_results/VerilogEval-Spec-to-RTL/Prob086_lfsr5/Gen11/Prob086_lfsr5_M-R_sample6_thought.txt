I will refactor the design by separating the next state logic into a combinational block and then updating the register q synchronously in a sequential block. This approach uses an intermediate wire to hold the next state of the LFSR, which is computed by shifting and XORing tapped bits, preserving the maximal-length polynomial behavior. The synchronous reset resets q to 1 as before. This separation clarifies combinational and sequential parts, which can improve readability and is a common RTL style.
