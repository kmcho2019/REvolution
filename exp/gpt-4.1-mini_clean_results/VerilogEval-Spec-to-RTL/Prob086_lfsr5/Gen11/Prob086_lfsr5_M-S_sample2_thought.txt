I will simplify the implementation by expressing the next state of the LFSR in a single assignment using a shift and conditional XOR. Specifically, the feedback bit is computed by XORing the output bit (q[0]) with the tapped bits at positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]). The entire 5-bit register is then shifted right by one, with the feedback bit fed into the MSB. This avoids multiple bit-by-bit assignments while maintaining the same behavior and a synchronous reset to 1.
