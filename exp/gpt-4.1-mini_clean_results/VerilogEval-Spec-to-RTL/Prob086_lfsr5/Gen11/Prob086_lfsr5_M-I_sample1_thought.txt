To improve the PPA of the existing maximal-length 5-bit Galois LFSR implementation, I will focus on the following enhancements:

1. Use consistent non-blocking assignments (<=) inside the always block for all sequential assignments, including the feedback calculation. This removes possible timing ambiguity and helps synthesis tools better schedule the logic.
2. Remove the intermediate 'feedback' register and compute the feedback signal combinationally within the always block before updating q. This avoids an extra register and reduces logic depth.
3. Use a localwire (or reg) inside the always block declared as reg for combinational feedback calculation for clarity and avoiding synthesis inference issues.
4. Simplify the feedback calculation by directly using q[0], since only the feedback bit (q[0]) is XORed with taps.
5. Maintain the synchronous reset to 5'b00001.
6. Keep the bit-wise update style for clear logic and easy mapping.
7. Since the LFSR runs every clock cycle, consider clock gating outside this module if power optimization is needed; internally the logic is minimal.
8. This approach reduces register count and cleans assignment semantics, potentially improving timing and area slightly without affecting functionality.

The overall design remains a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (bits q[4] and q[2]) and synchronous reset to 1.
