Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, counting from 1 at the MSB. This means taps at bits q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing. The LFSR shifts right on each clock edge. The feedback bit is the XOR of the tapped bit and the LSB. For each bit position, if it is tapped, its next value is current bit XOR LSB; if not tapped, next value is the bit to the left (higher index). On synchronous reset, output is set to 5'b00001.
