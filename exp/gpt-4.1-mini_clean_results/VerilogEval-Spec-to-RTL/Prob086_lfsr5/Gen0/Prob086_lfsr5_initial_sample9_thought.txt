Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. The bits are indexed from 1 to 5 in the problem statement, but in Verilog indexing is zero-based. So bit 5 corresponds to q[4], and bit 3 corresponds to q[2]. According to the Galois LFSR principle, on each clock edge the register shifts right by 1 bit, and bits at tap positions are XORed with the output bit (q[0]) to produce their next value. The LSB q[0] shifts in a new bit that comes from the taps. With taps at positions 5 and 3, the feedback logic will XOR q[0] with the tap bits to update those bits. The synchronous reset sets the LFSR output to 1 (meaning 5'b00001). The LFSR is maximum length, so it cycles through all states except 0.
