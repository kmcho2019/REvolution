Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR shifting right on each positive clock edge.
The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3, meaning bits 4 and 2 in zero-based indexing.
The feedback bit is the current q[0] (LSB) and it XORs with bits 4 and 2 to generate their next states.
Other bits shift right without modification except when tapped.
On synchronous active-high reset, set q to 1 (5'b00001).
