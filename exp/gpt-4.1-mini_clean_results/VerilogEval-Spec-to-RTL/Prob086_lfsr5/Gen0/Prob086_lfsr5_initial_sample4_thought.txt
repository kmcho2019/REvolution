Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, where bit indexing is 1-based. This means taps correspond to bits q[4] (bit 5) and q[2] (bit 3) in 0-based indexing. On each clock rising edge, if reset is high, set output q to 5'b00001. Otherwise, shift the register right by one bit, and update the bits with feedback from XOR of q[0] and the taps. The LSB (q[0]) is replaced with q[1], q[1] with q[2], and so forth, but tap positions get XORed with q[0]. Use the Galois LFSR update logic accordingly.
