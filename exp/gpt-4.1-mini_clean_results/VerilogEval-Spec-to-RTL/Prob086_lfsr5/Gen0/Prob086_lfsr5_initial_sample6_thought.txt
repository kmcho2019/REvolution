Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based). The LFSR shifts right on each clock edge, and the next state bits depend on the current LSB and taps. On synchronous reset, set the LFSR output to 1. The shift register is 5 bits, indexed from q[4:0] with q[4] as the MSB and q[0] as the LSB. For the Galois LFSR, taps at bit 5 and 3 mean bits q[4] and q[2] are XORed with the LSB (q[0]) when updating. The LSB shifts right in from the feedback bit. The maximal-length polynomial is x^5 + x^3 + 1, so the feedback bit is the XOR of q[0] with tap bits q[4] and q[2]. Implement sequential logic triggered on the positive edge of clk, with synchronous active-high reset setting q=1.
