Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. In Galois configuration, the LSB bit shifts out, and the bits at tapped positions are XORed with this output bit to update their next states. The LFSR shifts right on each clock, and the feedback is applied to tapped bits. The reset is synchronous and active-high, and sets the register to 1 (5'b00001). The output q is the 5-bit LFSR state.
