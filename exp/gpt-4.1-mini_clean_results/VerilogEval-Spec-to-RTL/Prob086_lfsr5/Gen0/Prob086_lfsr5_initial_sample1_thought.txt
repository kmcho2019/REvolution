Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock cycle. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3, which correspond to q[4] and q[2] respectively (0-indexed). On each clock edge, compute the feedback as the XOR of q[0] (LSB) and the tap bit, then shift right and update tapped bits by XORing them with q[0]. On synchronous reset, set output to 1 (5'b00001). Use a register to hold the state.
