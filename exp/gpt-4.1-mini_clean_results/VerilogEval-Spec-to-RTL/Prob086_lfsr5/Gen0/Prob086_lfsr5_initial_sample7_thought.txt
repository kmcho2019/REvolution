Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3. Since the shift is right, we will interpret q[4] as bit 5 and q[2] as bit 3 (0-based indexing). At every positive clock edge, if reset is high, set q to 5'b00001. Otherwise, shift the register right and XOR the tapped bits with the outgoing bit to produce new bits. The LSB is updated based on XOR of previous tapped bits and the outgoing bit.
