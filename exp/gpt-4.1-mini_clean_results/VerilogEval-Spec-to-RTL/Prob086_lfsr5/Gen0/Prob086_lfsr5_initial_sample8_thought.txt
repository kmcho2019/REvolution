Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each clock edge. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing), which correspond to q[4] and q[2] in zero-based indexing. In a Galois LFSR, bits shift right, and bits with taps are XORed with the output bit q[0]. We'll update q on the positive clock edge and synchronously reset to 1 on active-high reset.
