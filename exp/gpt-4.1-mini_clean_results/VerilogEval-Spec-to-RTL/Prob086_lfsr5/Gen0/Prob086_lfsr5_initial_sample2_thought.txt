Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right every clock cycle. The taps are at bit positions 5 and 3 (counting from 1 as MSB). The next input to these tapped bits is the XOR of the current LSB output bit (q[0]) and the tapped bit value before shifting. On synchronous active-high reset, set the LFSR output to 1. Use non-blocking assignments in a clocked always block triggered on the positive edge of clk.
