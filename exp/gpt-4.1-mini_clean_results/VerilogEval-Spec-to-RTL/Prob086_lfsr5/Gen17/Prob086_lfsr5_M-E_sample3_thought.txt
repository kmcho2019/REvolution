Instead of assigning each bit separately, implement the 5-bit Galois LFSR update using a single combinational "next" state vector calculated from the current state and feedback. The feedback bit is q[0], which is XORed into the tapped positions (bit 4 and bit 2). For untapped bits, just shift right by one. This approach reduces scattered bit assignments, uses bitwise operations to form the next state, and then registers the next state on the clock edge. This method improves clarity and possibly synthesis optimization.
