Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR using a combinational next-state logic separated from the sequential register update. On each clock edge, if reset is active, synchronously reset q to 5'b00001. Otherwise, update q to next_state computed as follows:

- Shift q right by one bit.
- For taps at bit positions 5 and 3, XOR q[0] with q[4] and q[2], respectively.
- Use q[0] as the feedback bit.
- The bit positions with taps get new values as q[i] ^ q[0].
- Other bits simply shift right.

This approach separates combinational logic (next state calculation) and sequential logic (state register), improving timing by avoiding read-after-write hazards. It also clarifies the design by making next_state explicit.
