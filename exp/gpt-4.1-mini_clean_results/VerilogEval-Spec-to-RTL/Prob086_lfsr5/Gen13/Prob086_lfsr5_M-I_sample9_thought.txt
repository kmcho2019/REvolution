To optimize the 5-bit Galois LFSR for better PPA, I will implement the feedback computation combinationally in a wire to minimize logic depth inside the sequential always block. This approach reduces the critical path by calculating the feedback bit once and then performing a simple right shift with XOR on the tapped bits. The synchronous reset is retained as required. The taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]) are XORed with the LSB q[0] to generate the feedback signal. This method reduces the number of sequential assignments, simplifies timing, and minimizes switching within the clocked block, potentially improving power and timing without increasing area.
