Implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (bits 4 and 2 in zero-based indexing) by performing the shift and selectively XORing the tap bits with the feedback bit (which is the previous LSB q[0]). Instead of building the next state vector in one concatenation, update the register bits sequentially using a procedural approach. On each clock, shift all bits right by one position. For the tap positions (q[4] and q[2]), XOR their previous values with the feedback bit (the old q[0]). Other bits simply take the value of their immediate higher bit. This style clearly separates which bits get XORed and which are just shifted. On synchronous reset, set q to 5'b00001. This method ensures clean, clear, and correct Galois LFSR behavior.
