To optimize the existing 5-bit Galois LFSR implementation for better PPA, I will refactor the sequential logic to use a single next-state vector computation before updating the registered output. This approach reduces parallel non-blocking assignments on individual bits, potentially improving timing by simplifying the combinational path and fanout. Additionally, it improves code clarity and ease of maintenance without changing functionality.

The feedback bit is q[0], and taps are at bits 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]). The next state is computed in combinational logic and registered on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset.

This vectorized approach can marginally reduce critical path delay and simplify synthesis optimizations.
