The main issue is the improper declaration of a wire inside an always block, which is illegal in Verilog. To fix this, we remove the wire declaration inside the always block and instead use a temporary reg variable declared outside the always block or simply use the current q[0] value directly in the logic. Since q is a reg and updated on the clock edge, we can assign feedback as a reg inside the always block before updating q or directly use q[0]. To keep it clear, we declare a temporary reg feedback before shifting, then update q accordingly. This eliminates the syntax error and maintains the LFSR logic for the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2]). The synchronous reset sets q to 5'b00001.
