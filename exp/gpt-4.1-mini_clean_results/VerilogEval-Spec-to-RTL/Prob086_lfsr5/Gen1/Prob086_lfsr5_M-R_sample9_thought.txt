To fix the syntax error and still keep the same logic, we will declare a temporary reg variable inside the always block to hold the feedback bit (q[0]) at the start of the clock edge. Then we will update each bit of the LFSR in a single non-blocking assignment step after calculating the feedback. This avoids declaring a wire inside the always block and keeps the design clean and synthesizable. The synchronous reset will initialize the register to 5'b00001 as required.
