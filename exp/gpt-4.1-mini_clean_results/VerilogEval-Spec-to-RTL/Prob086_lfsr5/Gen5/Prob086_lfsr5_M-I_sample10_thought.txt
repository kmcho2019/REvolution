To optimize the 5-bit Galois LFSR for better performance, power, and area, I will compute the feedback bit combinationally before the clock edge, then update the entire register with a single non-blocking assignment. This reduces the number of sequential assignments and associated overhead. Also, using a single concatenation for shifting with feedback simplifies hardware mapping and may improve synthesis results. The reset remains synchronous and active-high, initializing to 1 as required. This approach should reduce critical path delay and logic usage while maintaining functionality.
