To optimize the existing design for better power and area while maintaining performance, I will eliminate the procedural always block and replace it with purely combinational continuous assignments. This reduces flip-flop and mux logic inferred during synthesis. 

I will implement next_state as a set of combinational logic expressions that OR together all possible ways each next state can be reached, based on active current states and input `in`. This approach removes the repeated if-conditions and incremental ORing inside the always block, allowing synthesis tools to optimize better by flattening and sharing logic.

For outputs, the direct OR of corresponding state bits remains optimal and clear.

This style generally leads to lower logic depth, less area due to logic sharing, and reduced dynamic power from more straightforward signal dependencies.

In summary:
- Replace procedural block with continuous assign for next_state.
- Use logic expressions combining current states and input to determine each next state bit.
- Keep outputs as direct OR of relevant states.
