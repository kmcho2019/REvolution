- Combine repeated zero_in AND terms into a single gate to avoid redundant logic.
- For other next_state bits, group states leading to the same next state on input 1 to reduce redundant AND gates.
- For outputs, keep the bitmasking, but replace the OR reduction with a single wire OR for clarity and possible optimization.
- Minimize intermediate signals and reuse existing wires for area saving.
