Simplify the design by eliminating the for-loop and using direct combinational assignments for each next_state bit based on the current state vector and input. Since the FSM is one-hot encoded and only 10 states, explicit logic expressions can be derived straightforwardly by OR-ing next states from all active current states. This approach improves clarity, reduces control structures, and makes the logic more explicit and easy to follow, while maintaining correct functionality for multiple active states. The output logic remains the same with simple bitmasks to detect states that drive out1 and out2.

This yields a simpler, more readable combinational block without loops or cases, easily synthesizable and efficient for such a small FSM.
