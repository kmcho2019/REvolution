To improve PPA, I aim to reduce logic complexity and power by minimizing the number of active terms in next_state calculations, exploiting mutual exclusivity of input signals, and merging common transition conditions where possible. Since the FSM is one-hot encoded and inputs often mutually exclusive (in is 1 bit), I will use conditional (ternary) operators to split next_state assignments per input value, thus reducing redundant AND gates with in.

Also, since outputs are driven by known state bits, I will explicitly assign them without ANDing, reducing logic.

By splitting next_state computations based on input 'in', synthesis tools can optimize logic by enabling multiplexing rather than large OR-AND gate arrays.

This approach reduces the number of AND gates and the switching activity in logic, slightly improving power and potentially timing.

Summary of improvements:
- Use conditional expressions per input value to simplify next_state logic.
- Remove unnecessary AND gates for output logic by directly wiring outputs to the current states.
- Keep all logic fully combinational.
