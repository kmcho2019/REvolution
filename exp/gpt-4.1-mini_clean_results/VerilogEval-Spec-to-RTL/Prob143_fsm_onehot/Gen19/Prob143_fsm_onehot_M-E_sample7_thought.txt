Instead of direct combinational logic for next_state, use a "decoded" style approach where each state is individually decoded with a bit mask and input, and contributes to next_state via a priority-encoded scheme. We compute partial next_state vectors per state and then OR them all together. This modularizes the logic for easier comprehension and extension, at the cost of some duplication.

For outputs, implement them as multiplexers selecting outputs based on the currently active states. Since multiple states may be active, outputs become OR of the outputs associated with active states.

This approach provides:
- A systematic way to add or change transitions by editing per-state logic.
- Clear modular separation between per-state transitions and final output logic.
- Explicit handling of multiple active states with an OR reduction.
- Good readability and maintainability, at a small cost of duplicated terms, which synthesis can optimize.

This is distinct from direct bitwise AND/OR expressions by introducing an intermediate layer per state and aggregating them.
