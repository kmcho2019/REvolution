Implement a combinational logic that considers all active current states in the one-hot encoded input vector. For each active state, according to the input, determine the next state(s) and the outputs. Because multiple states can be active simultaneously, next_state is the OR of next states from all active states. Outputs out1 and out2 are also OR of all outputs from active states. Use the state transition table to encode the logic for each state and input combination.
