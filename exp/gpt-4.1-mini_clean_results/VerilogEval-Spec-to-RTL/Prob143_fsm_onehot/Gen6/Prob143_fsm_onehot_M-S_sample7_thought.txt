Simplify the design by directly expressing the next_state logic as combinational assignments without a loop or case statement. Since the FSM is one-hot encoded and each current state leads to only one next state per input, define each next_state bit as the OR of all possible source states that transition into it for the given input. This approach removes the loop, registers, and case statements, producing a clear and compact expression.

For outputs, keep the simple masked OR reduction from the current state vector.

This simplification enhances readability and synthesis friendliness by turning the next state logic into straightforward combinational equations.
