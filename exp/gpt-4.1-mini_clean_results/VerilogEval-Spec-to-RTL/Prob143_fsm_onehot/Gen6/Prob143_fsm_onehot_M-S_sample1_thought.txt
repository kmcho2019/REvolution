Since the machine is one-hot encoded and inputs are one bit, we can simplify by computing each next_state bit directly as a combinational OR of the conditions that lead to that next state. This eliminates the loop and case statements.

For each next_state[i], find all (state[j] & input_condition) combinations that lead to next_state[i] = 1 and OR them together.

Outputs can remain as simple bitwise OR masks on the current state vector.

This approach is simpler, more direct, and easy to read while preserving the functionality and supporting multiple active states simultaneously.
