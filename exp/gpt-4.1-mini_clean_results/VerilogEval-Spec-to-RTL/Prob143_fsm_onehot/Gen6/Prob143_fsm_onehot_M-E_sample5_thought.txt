Use an approach where the next_state vector is computed by accumulating contributions from each possible current state independently. For each current state bit set, the corresponding next state bit(s) is asserted based on input. The next_state is formed by bitwise ORing all these partial next state vectors. Similarly, out1 and out2 are computed as ORs over current states weighted by their output values. This method leverages vectorized operations and intermediate one-hot "next state" partial vectors to simplify and modularize logic, enabling efficient parallel synthesis and clear mapping from states to next states.
