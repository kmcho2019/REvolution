Since the input state vector can have multiple bits set, next_state outputs are ORs of selected states masked by the input. The original solution used intermediate wires to group ORs for next_state[0], but this is unnecessary; simply OR all relevant states directly. Similarly, next_state[7] is already a simple OR of two terms, so keep it direct. Outputs are simple ORs of states, so keep them straightforward. Removing intermediate wires reduces code size and complexity while maintaining clarity and correctness.
