Refactor the design by using a combinational always block to compute next_state and outputs. This approach encapsulates the combinational logic in one block with blocking assignments, improving readability and grouping the logic together, while still using one-hot encoded inputs and outputs. Instead of multiple assign statements, this also facilitates easy modification or extension of the logic. We'll initialize outputs and next_state to zero and then update according to active states and input, OR-ing contributions for multiple active states.
