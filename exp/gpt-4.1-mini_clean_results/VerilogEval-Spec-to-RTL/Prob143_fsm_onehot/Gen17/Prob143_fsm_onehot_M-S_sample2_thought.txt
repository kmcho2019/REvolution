Simplify the design by removing the for-loop and writing the next_state logic explicitly for each current state. Since there are only 10 states, a direct combinational assignment using bitwise OR for all transitions is straightforward and more readable. This reduces complexity, eases verification, and synthesis tools will optimize it well.

The outputs can remain as simple bitwise OR reductions over the relevant state bits.

This approach maintains the functionality, handles multiple active current states, and improves clarity.
