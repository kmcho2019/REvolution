Implement the TopModule by creating a combinational logic block that iterates through all states (S0 to S9). For each state bit set in the input, compute its next state contribution based on the input signal 'in' and the state machine transitions. Then, aggregate all contributions with bitwise OR operations to form the final next_state vector. Outputs out1 and out2 are generated by checking if any relevant states are active (state 7, 8, or 9) and combining their output values accordingly. This approach avoids intermediate wires, uses a generate-for loop and localparams to encode the transitions as arrays for compactness, and cleanly handles multiple concurrent active states.
