Instead of directly encoding the next_state bits with large OR expressions, compute intermediate one-hot next states for each active current state, then combine them using bitwise OR. This approach modularizes the transition logic per state, making the logic clearer and potentially easier to optimize by synthesis tools. Outputs are computed by checking if any of the states that generate nonzero outputs are active, as a bitwise OR of masked current states. This modular per-state next-state computation also naturally supports multiple active states, preserving the combinational behavior required.
