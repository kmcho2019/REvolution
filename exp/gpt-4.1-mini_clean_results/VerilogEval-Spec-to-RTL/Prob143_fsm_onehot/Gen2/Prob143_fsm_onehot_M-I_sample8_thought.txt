To optimize power and area while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will refactor the design to separate the combinational next_state logic into per-state partial next state vectors, then OR them together at the end. This avoids repeated assignments to next_state bits and allows synthesis tools to optimize shared logic better. For outputs, since only three states have nonzero outputs, I'll OR their contributions directly from the active states, eliminating register overwrites. This reduces switching by minimizing the number of assignments and logic duplication. I will keep a single always_comb block using blocking assignments for clarity and maintain one-hot encoding handling multiple active states. This approach better supports multi-hot inputs, reduces unnecessary switching, and simplifies logic, thus improving overall PPA.
