To simplify the 4-bit counter, I will use straightforward increment logic with the '+' operator, which is clear and concise. The synchronous active-high reset remains the same. This approach is easy to understand and maintain, and modern synthesis tools efficiently infer the increment logic. While this might have slightly higher gate-level complexity than the ripple carry XOR-AND approach, it significantly reduces code complexity and is perfectly acceptable for a small 4-bit counter.
