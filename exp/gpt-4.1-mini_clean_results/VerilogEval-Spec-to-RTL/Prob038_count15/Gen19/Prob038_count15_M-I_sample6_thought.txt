To optimize the design for better PPA, I will simplify the combinational logic by replacing the explicit bitwise increment logic with a straightforward arithmetic increment operation (q + 1). This tends to synthesize into efficient carry chain hardware automatically, reducing area and improving timing. The synchronous reset and clocked process remain the same for correct behavior. This change should improve critical path delay and area with minimal impact on power, as arithmetic incrementers are highly optimized in modern synthesis tools.
