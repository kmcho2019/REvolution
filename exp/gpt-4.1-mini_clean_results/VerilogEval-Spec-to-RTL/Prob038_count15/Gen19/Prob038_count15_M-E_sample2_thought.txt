Implement the 4-bit counter using a classic synchronous binary counter approach with a single always block triggered on the clock's positive edge. Use a synchronous active-high reset to initialize the counter to zero. For incrementing, use simple arithmetic addition of 1 to the current value. This method leverages the built-in addition operator, keeping the logic straightforward, compact, and easy to optimize by synthesis tools. This approach contrasts with enable-driven toggling by relying on arithmetic increment, which can simplify the combinational logic and potentially reduce the critical path by avoiding cascaded AND gates.
