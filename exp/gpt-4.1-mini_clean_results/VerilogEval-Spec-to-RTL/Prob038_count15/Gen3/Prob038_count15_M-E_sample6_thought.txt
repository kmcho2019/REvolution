Implement the 4-bit counter as a ring counter using a 4-bit one-hot encoded register combined with a binary to one-hot decoder and a one-hot to binary encoder. On each clock, the one-hot register rotates its '1' bit to the left, cycling through 4 states, but to achieve counting 0 to 15, we cascade a 2-bit binary counter that increments after each full rotation of the 4-bit ring counter. Thus, the ring counter cycles four times while the 2-bit counter increments once, together forming a 4-bit binary counter with period 16. A synchronous reset sets both registers to zero.
