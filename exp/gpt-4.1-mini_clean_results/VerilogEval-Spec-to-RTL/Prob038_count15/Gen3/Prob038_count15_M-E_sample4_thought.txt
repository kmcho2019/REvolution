Implement the 4-bit binary counter using a cascade of flip-flops with explicit next-state logic without relying on the '+' operator. This approach calculates each bit of the counter based on the current bits, mimicking ripple carry addition logic in combinational form.

Each bit toggles based on the carry from lower bits:
- q[0] toggles every clock cycle.
- q[1] toggles when q[0] is high.
- q[2] toggles when q[0] and q[1] are both high.
- q[3] toggles when q[0], q[1], and q[2] are all high.

This explicit logic could be synthesized into a ripple counter style, providing an alternative structure. The synchronous reset clears all bits to zero.

This design avoids using the addition operator and directly implements the counting logic with bitwise operations, showcasing a different structural approach.
