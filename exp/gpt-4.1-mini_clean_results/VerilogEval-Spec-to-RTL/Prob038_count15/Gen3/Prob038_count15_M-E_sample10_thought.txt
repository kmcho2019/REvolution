Implement the 4-bit counter using a chain of four T flip-flops, where each flip-flop toggles when its preceding flip-flop outputs a high, mimicking a ripple carry counter. The reset synchronously clears all flip-flops. This architecture uses toggle flip-flops rather than an adder, which can reduce the combinational increment logic, and may offer power or area benefits by localizing toggling. The output q is the concatenation of all flip-flop states.
