To improve the PPA metrics, I will simplify the design by replacing the manual ripple carry increment logic with the more straightforward and synthesis-friendly increment operator `q <= q + 1;`. This reduces combinational logic complexity and critical path delay, improving timing (WNS and TNS) and potentially lowering area and power due to simpler logic synthesis.
