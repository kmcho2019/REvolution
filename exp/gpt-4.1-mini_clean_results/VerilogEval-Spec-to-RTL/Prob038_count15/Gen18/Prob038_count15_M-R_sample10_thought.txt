I will refactor the design by replacing the combinational always block used for next_q calculation with continuous assignments using assign statements. This keeps the same ripple carry increment logic using bitwise XOR and AND operations, but expresses it more concisely in a purely combinational fashion. The sequential update of q on the clock edge remains unchanged, with synchronous active-high reset. This approach separates combinational and sequential logic clearly, making the design more idiomatic for simple counters and potentially helping synthesis tools optimize the logic better.
