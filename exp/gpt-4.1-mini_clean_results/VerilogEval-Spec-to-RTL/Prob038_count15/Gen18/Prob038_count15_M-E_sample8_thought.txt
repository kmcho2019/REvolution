I will implement the 4-bit counter using a simple synchronous binary up-counter approach with the direct arithmetic increment operator inside the sequential always block. This simplifies the combinational logic by avoiding explicit carry chain logic and lets the synthesis tool generate an optimal incrementer circuit internally. The synchronous reset will reset q to zero on the rising clock edge. This approach is clear, concise, and usually results in efficient hardware due to the synthesizer's ability to optimize addition, potentially improving timing and power by reducing explicit combinational path complexity.
