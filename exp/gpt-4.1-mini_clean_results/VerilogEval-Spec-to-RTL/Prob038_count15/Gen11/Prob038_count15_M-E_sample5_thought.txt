Implement the 4-bit counter using a synchronous binary incrementer style where the next count value is computed as q + 1 in a combinational manner inside the clocked process. This approach models the counter as a register updated on each clock cycle by adding 1 to its current value, with synchronous reset clearing it to zero. This leverages the synthesis tool's capability to optimize addition operations, potentially resulting in a carry-lookahead or fast incrementer implementation, improving critical path timing. The design remains clean, minimal, and easily understandable.
