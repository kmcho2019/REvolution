Refactor the 4-bit counter by implementing it as a simple ripple counter using a chain of T-flip-flops. Each flip-flop toggles on the positive edge of the clock when its enable is asserted. The enable for each bit is generated by AND-ing all the lower bits, causing the counter to increment naturally through ripple carry. The synchronous reset is handled inside each flip-flop triggered on the clock's positive edge. This approach replaces the combinational addition with toggling logic and uses sequential logic to generate the count bits, demonstrating an alternative structural design that achieves the same counting functionality.
