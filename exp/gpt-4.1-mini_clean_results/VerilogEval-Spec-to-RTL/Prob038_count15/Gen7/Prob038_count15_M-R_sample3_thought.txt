I will refactor the 4-bit counter by separating the combinational next-state logic from the sequential state update logic. Specifically, I'll declare a wire representing the next counter value computed combinationally from the current value, and then update the register q synchronously at the clock edge using that next value. This two-step approach improves code readability and modularizes combinational and sequential logic. The reset remains synchronous active-high and sets the counter to zero on the rising clock edge. The functionality remains identical, but the structural separation clarifies the data flow.
