To improve the design for better performance (timing), power, and area, I will switch from the hierarchical enable-based toggling to a straightforward arithmetic increment by 1 using the '+' operator. This simplifies the combinational logic significantly, reducing the critical path and logic levels. The synthesis tool can map this to efficient incrementer hardware, improving timing and potentially area. Although this may cause all bits to toggle together, modern synthesis and power optimization tools handle such incrementers efficiently, often better than complex enable logic.

We keep synchronous reset as required.

This approach reduces combinational complexity, improves timing by minimizing logic depth, and often yields better area and power when synthesized on typical FPGA/ASIC flows.
