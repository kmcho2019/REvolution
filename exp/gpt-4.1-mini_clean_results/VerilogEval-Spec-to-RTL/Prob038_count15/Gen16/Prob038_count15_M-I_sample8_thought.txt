To improve timing and simplify the design, I will replace the ripple carry style combinational logic with a simple binary increment using the '+' operator. This eliminates the carry ripple chain and reduces combinational logic depth, likely improving maximum clock frequency (lower WNS and TNS). The synchronous reset remains unchanged. This approach also simplifies the combinational always block, improving readability while not increasing area significantly due to synthesis optimizations. The reset remains synchronous active-high as required.
