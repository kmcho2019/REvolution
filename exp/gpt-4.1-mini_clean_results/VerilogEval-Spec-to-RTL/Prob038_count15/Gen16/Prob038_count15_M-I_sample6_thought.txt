To optimize for better performance (timing) and slightly reduce dynamic power while maintaining area efficiency, I will simplify the combinational logic by replacing the ripple carry increment logic with a straightforward binary increment expression using the addition operator (`q + 1`). This allows synthesis tools to map the increment to efficient carry chain structures or fast adders, minimizing critical path delay. This simplification also reduces glitching and unnecessary switching compared to manual ripple carry XOR-AND logic, lowering dynamic power slightly. The synchronous reset remains active-high and synchronous on the positive clock edge as before. This approach improves timing and power metrics while keeping the area minimal and code clearer.
