To improve the PPA (timing, power, area) for this 4-bit synchronous counter, I will implement the counter using a single always block combining synchronous reset and increment logic directly, avoiding the intermediate combinational next_q signal. This reduces logic and net count, potentially improving area and power due to simpler control flow and fewer signals.

Additionally, rather than using combinational ripple carry increment logic, I will use a binary counter increment by simple addition (q + 1). Modern synthesis tools optimize small counters efficiently into compact, fast logic. The simpler code also improves readability and clarity.

Since the critical path for a 4-bit counter is minimal either way, the direct addition approach should not degrade timing and may even improve it through better synthesis mapping.

Therefore, the improvement is:
- Remove next_q combinational register, use direct increment in synchronous logic
- Keep synchronous active-high reset
- Use non-blocking assignment for register update

This should yield a minimal, clean, and synthesizable 4-bit counter with improved PPA.
