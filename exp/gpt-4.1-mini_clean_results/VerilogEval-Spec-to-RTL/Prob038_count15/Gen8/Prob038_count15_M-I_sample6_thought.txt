To optimize the 4-bit counter for better performance (timing) and power without changing its behavior, I will replace the adder-based increment with a simple combinational increment logic using carry ripple implemented as a chain of bitwise XOR and AND gates, which can potentially reduce the carry propagation delay compared to a general adder inferred by "q + 1". This is especially beneficial in small counters by providing a faster, more straightforward carry chain. Also, I will avoid declaring the next count as a wire, and instead compute it combinationally within always @(*) block to clearly separate combinational next state logic from sequential update, improving clarity. The reset remains synchronous and active high. This structural optimization should modestly improve timing and power due to simpler logic and clearer separation.
