To optimize the 4-bit counter for better timing (WN S, TNS) and slightly reduce power and area, I will implement a straightforward binary incrementer instead of the ripple carry logic. Using the built-in '+' operator to increment q by 1 creates an adder optimized by synthesis tools, potentially yielding better carry propagation and improved clock frequency. This approach eliminates the custom XOR/AND gate chain, reducing combinational logic depth and area. The synchronous reset remains the same for correctness. This design is more idiomatic and efficient for synthesis, which should help improve timing and reduce area and power marginally.
