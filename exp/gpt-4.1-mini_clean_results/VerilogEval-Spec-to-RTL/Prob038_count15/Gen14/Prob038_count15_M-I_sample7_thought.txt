To improve timing (reduce critical path) and potentially power, I will replace the ripple carry increment logic with a simple binary addition using the '+' operator. This uses the FPGA or ASIC native adder resources, typically producing better synthesis results than manually implemented ripple carry logic.

Using a simple binary adder also reduces combinational gate levels on the critical path, improving the worst negative slack (WNS) and effective clock period metrics.

The synchronous reset remains, and the counter wraps naturally from 15 to 0 due to 4-bit overflow.

This approach should keep the area minimal while improving timing and possibly power due to more optimized synthesis of the '+' operator.
