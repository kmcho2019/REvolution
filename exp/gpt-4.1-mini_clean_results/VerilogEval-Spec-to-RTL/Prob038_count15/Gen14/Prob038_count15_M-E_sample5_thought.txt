Implement the 4-bit counter using a shift register with feedback. The counter value is stored in q, and on each clock cycle, the bits shift left by one, while the least significant bit is computed as the XOR of all bits before shifting. This approach uses linear-feedback shift register (LFSR) principles to count through all 16 states in sequence. Reset synchronously clears the register to zero. This design explores an alternative counting architecture to the standard binary increment approach.
