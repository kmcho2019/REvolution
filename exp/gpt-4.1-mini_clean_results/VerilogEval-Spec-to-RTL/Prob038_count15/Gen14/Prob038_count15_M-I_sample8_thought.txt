To improve the timing (TNS and WNS) and possibly power without increasing area, I will replace the ripple carry combinational logic with a straightforward binary increment using the addition operator. Modern synthesis tools efficiently implement such increments with fast carry logic internally, likely yielding a better critical path and enabling higher clock frequencies. This approach also reduces glitching since it uses a single arithmetic operator rather than cascaded gates. The synchronous reset remains unchanged for clarity and correctness. This change simplifies the combinational logic and enhances timing with minimal area impact.
