I will implement the 4-bit binary counter using a synchronous increment approach with a simple 4-bit register and a single 4-bit adder that adds 1 to the current count. This design will replace the ripple-carry logic with an explicit increment by 1 at each clock cycle. The reset will synchronously reset the counter to zero. This approach separates the counting operation from the bitwise toggle logic, making it conceptually straightforward and synthesizable efficiently.
