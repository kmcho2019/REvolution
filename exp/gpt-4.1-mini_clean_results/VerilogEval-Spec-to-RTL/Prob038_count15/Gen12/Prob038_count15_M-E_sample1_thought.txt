Implement the 4-bit counter using a one-hot encoded ring counter with 16 states represented by a 16-bit shift register. On each clock cycle, shift the one-hot bit left by one, and decode the current one-hot state back to a 4-bit binary count output. Use synchronous reset to initialize the one-hot state to the first position. This approach separates counting (one-hot position) from binary output (decoded), offering a unique architectural perspective distinct from direct binary increment or combinational increment logic.
