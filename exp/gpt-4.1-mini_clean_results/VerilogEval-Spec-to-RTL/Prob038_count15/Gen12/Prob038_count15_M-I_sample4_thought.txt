To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will implement a simple synchronous increment-by-one counter using addition rather than bitwise toggle logic. Although the bitwise toggle exposes carry logic explicitly, using a direct addition (q + 1) is often more optimizable by synthesis tools with fast carry chains, resulting in lower logic depth and improved timing.

Additionally, using the addition approach reduces the number of logic levels for the critical path, potentially improving timing (lowering critical path delay) and can also slightly reduce power by minimizing intermediate toggling logic.

The synchronous active-high reset remains as specified.

This approach balances clarity, optimal synthesis support, and minimal logic depth while maintaining a clean, compact implementation.
