To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, area), reduce the logic depth of the counter increment and minimize switching activity. Instead of manually toggling each bit via XOR and AND chains (which leads to a ripple of toggling and thus logic depth), implement a direct synchronous increment using the built-in Verilog addition operator. This reduces combinational logic depth, potentially improving timing and lowering power by producing a cleaner carry propagation path optimized by synthesis tools.

Additionally, introduce a clock enable (enable counter) signal that activates counting only when reset is deasserted. This reduces unnecessary toggling during reset and can help power if downstream logic uses clock gating or enable signals.

This approach leverages the synthesizer's ability to efficiently map a binary addition, possibly resulting in better timing and smaller area due to optimized carry logic. The design remains simple, synchronous, and clean.

The counter resets synchronously to zero on active-high reset as specified.
