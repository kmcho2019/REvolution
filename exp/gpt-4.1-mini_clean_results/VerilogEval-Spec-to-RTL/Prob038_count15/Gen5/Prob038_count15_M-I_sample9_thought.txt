To improve the PPA (performance, power, and area) of the 4-bit counter, I will implement a simple synchronous binary counter with a carry chain by using a ripple of flip-flops with toggling logic rather than a direct addition. This approach potentially reduces logic complexity and can yield faster carry propagation in hardware by directly controlling bit toggles. For example, bit 0 toggles every clock cycle, bit 1 toggles when bit 0 is high, bit 2 toggles when bits 0 and 1 are high, etc. This approach minimizes combinational logic on each clock cycle.

The synchronous active-high reset remains the same to ensure proper reset behavior.

This approach can reduce the critical path delay compared to a generic adder-based increment, potentially improving maximum frequency and reducing power since the toggling logic is straightforward and localized.

The 4-bit output q is still a register updated at every positive edge.
