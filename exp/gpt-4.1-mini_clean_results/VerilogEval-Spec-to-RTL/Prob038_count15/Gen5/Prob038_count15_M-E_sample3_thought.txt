Implement the 4-bit binary counter entirely within a single synchronous always block triggered on the clock's positive edge. The synchronous reset condition will be checked first: if active, the counter resets to zero; otherwise, the counter increments by one on every clock cycle. This inlined approach reduces combinational logic outside the clocked process and creates a straightforward, minimal path from clock to output. It also prevents any potential glitching on the output since updates happen atomically at the clock edge.
