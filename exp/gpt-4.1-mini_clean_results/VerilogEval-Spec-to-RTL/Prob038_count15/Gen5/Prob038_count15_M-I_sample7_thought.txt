To optimize the design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will replace the ripple enable counter style with a single always block implementing a parallel binary increment (q <= q + 1). This reduces the critical path delay since it removes the chain of AND gates generating enable signals, potentially allowing faster clock frequency and lower dynamic power due to less combinational switching. The synchronous reset remains unchanged. This approach also simplifies the code, reducing area slightly by relying on the synthesis tool's optimized adder implementation.
