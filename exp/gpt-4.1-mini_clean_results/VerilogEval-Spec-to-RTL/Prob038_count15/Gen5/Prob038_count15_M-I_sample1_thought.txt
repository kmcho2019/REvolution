To optimize power and performance, incorporate a synchronous count enable signal that allows counting only when enabled. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the counter register, lowering dynamic power. Also, use a toggle flip-flop approach for the least significant bit and ripple up with toggles for higher bits to potentially reduce switching activity and critical path delay, as toggle-based counters can be more power-efficient than incrementers. The reset remains synchronous active high, clearing all bits. This approach balances simplicity with improved PPA by reducing switching and leveraging toggle logic for counting.
