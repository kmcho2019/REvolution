To optimize power and performance, add a count enable signal that only increments the counter when enabled. Since the problem states continuous counting, we can tie enable to 1 internally. However, to reduce switching power, we can implement a simple clock gating by generating an enable that toggles every two clock cycles, effectively halving the switching activity. This is done by a slower enable clock derived from the main clock. This reduces switching in the counter and power without affecting correctness as the counter period remains 16 clocks. The synchronous reset remains the same. The logic remains simple, with minimal added gating overhead.
