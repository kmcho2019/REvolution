To improve the performance (timing) of the 4-bit counter, I will implement the counting logic using explicit carry chain signals rather than relying on a generic adder inferred by the synthesizer. This approach allows better synthesis tools to map the logic into fast carry-lookahead or carry-select structures, potentially reducing critical path delay. The synchronous reset remains the same.

Functionality remains unchanged, but timing might improve due to the explicit ripple carry style addition which synthesis tools can optimize well. Area and power remain roughly the same.
