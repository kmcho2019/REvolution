To optimize the 4-bit counter for better performance and potentially reduce critical path delay, I will replace the combinational logic based on XOR and AND chains with a simple 4-bit binary incrementer implemented as q + 1. This uses a built-in addition operation instead of manual ripple carry logic, which can be more efficiently synthesized by tools to fast incrementer hardware. The synchronous reset remains the same. This approach usually improves timing (shorter critical path) and keeps logic simple without increasing area significantly for a small width like 4 bits.
