To optimize the 4-bit synchronous counter for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will replace the explicit combinational logic for next_q with a simple 4-bit increment operation (q + 1). This reduces the critical path by using a ripple carry adder (which synthesis tools can optimize well) rather than multiple levels of AND and XOR gates. It also simplifies the combinational logic and lowers switching activity by leveraging standard increment logic. The synchronous reset is preserved. This approach balances simplicity and synthesis friendliness to improve overall timing and possibly reduce power and area.
