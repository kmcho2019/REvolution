I will implement the 4-bit binary counter as a synchronous register incrementer using a simple binary addition by 1 on each clock cycle. Instead of using toggle flip-flop logic, I'll use the standard arithmetic approach with the '+' operator. This approach is straightforward, easy to understand and synthesize, and will rely on the FPGA or synthesis tool's adder resources. The reset is synchronous active high and sets the counter to zero. This method avoids the combinational toggling cascade and potentially leads to better timing by leveraging dedicated adder hardware in the target device.
