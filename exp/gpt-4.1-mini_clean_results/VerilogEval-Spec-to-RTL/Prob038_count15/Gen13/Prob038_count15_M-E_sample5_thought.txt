I will implement the 4-bit binary counter using a shift register-based ring counter approach combined with combinational logic to decode the count. Instead of incrementing the count directly, I will represent the count in a one-hot style and rotate the '1' bit through a 16-step shift register. Then, I will use a combinational decoder to translate the position of the '1' in the 16-bit shift register into a 4-bit binary count output. The synchronous reset will initialize the shift register to the first position. This approach differs architecturally by replacing the typical binary counter increment logic with a rotating one-hot pattern, which can reduce carry propagation and potentially optimize timing for larger counters, while here it serves as a novel idea to meet the requirements.
