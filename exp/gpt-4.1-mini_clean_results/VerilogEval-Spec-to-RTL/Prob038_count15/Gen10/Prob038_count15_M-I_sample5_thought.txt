To optimize the PPA of the 4-bit counter, replace the ripple enable logic with a single always block that increments the counter by 1 on each clock cycle. This reduces the critical path delay significantly by eliminating chained enable signals and combinational AND gates. The synchronous reset is maintained. This approach also simplifies the design, reduces logic area, and minimizes switching power by toggling all bits simultaneously with a simpler increment operation. The resulting code is more concise and synthesizes efficiently for fast and low-power operation.
