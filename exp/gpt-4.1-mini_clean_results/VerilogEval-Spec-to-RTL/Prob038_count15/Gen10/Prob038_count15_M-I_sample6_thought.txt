To improve PPA, I will replace the ripple toggle combinational logic with a straightforward increment-by-one approach. This reduces the longest combinational path, lowering timing delay (better WNS/TNS). Increment logic is simpler and typically produces less switching activity on upper bits, improving power. It also minimizes gate count by using a single adder rather than multiple XOR/AND gates, reducing area slightly.

The synchronous reset will be handled inside the sequential always block directly, simplifying the combinational logic and minimizing logic duplication. This makes the critical path only the incrementer logic plus flip-flops, a standard and well-optimized pattern.

Overall, these changes improve timing, reduce switching power, and slightly reduce area, addressing the main concerns from the previous design.
