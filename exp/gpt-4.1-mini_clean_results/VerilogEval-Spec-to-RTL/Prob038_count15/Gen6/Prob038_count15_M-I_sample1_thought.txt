To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) from the given ripple-toggle implementation, I will implement a synchronous 4-bit binary counter using a simple binary addition by 1, which synthesis tools will typically map efficiently onto carry chain hardware. This approach reduces the critical path delay by using a parallel increment operation instead of a ripple toggling logic, improving maximum frequency and potentially lowering area. The synchronous active-high reset remains unchanged. This will keep the design minimal and clear while achieving better timing performance and potentially less power due to reduced switching complexity in the combinational increment logic.
