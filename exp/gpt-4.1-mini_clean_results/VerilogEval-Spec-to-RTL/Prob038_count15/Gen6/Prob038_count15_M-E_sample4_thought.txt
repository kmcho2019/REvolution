Implement the 4-bit counter using a synchronous loadable register with feedback that increments the count by adding 1 combinationally to the registered value. Use an always block triggered on the clock's positive edge to perform synchronous reset or load the incremented value. This approach makes the increment explicit and separates combinational and sequential logic. The reset clears the count to zero synchronously. The increment logic is implemented outside the sequential block, highlighting the design's separation of concerns.
