To improve timing and simplify the design, I will replace the ripple carry logic with a direct binary increment using the addition operator (q + 1). This will reduce the critical path because synthesis tools efficiently optimize adders internally, often using parallel carry-lookahead or carry-save structures, improving maximum frequency. The synchronous active-high reset remains unchanged. This simplification also enhances code clarity and maintains minimal area and power, as modern synthesis optimizes the addition well.
