I will implement the 4-bit counter using a synchronous counter architecture with a simple register and a binary addition by 1 using the '+' operator. This approach leverages the synthesis tool's ability to optimize the addition operation internally (e.g., carry lookahead), potentially improving timing compared to ripple carry logic. The synchronous reset will set the counter to zero on the rising clock edge. This method is straightforward, clean, and allows for easy scaling if needed.
