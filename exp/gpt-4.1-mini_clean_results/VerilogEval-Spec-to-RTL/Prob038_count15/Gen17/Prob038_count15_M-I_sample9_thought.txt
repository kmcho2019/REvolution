To optimize for better performance and potentially power, replace the bitwise logic for next_q with a simple increment operator (q + 1). This allows synthesis tools to infer fast carry-chain adders, improving timing (tns and wns). The synchronous reset and non-blocking assignment style are already good for power and timing, so they are kept. Area impact is negligible for this small counter, but the simpler combinational logic reduces gate count slightly. This approach retains functional correctness and further clarifies intent.
