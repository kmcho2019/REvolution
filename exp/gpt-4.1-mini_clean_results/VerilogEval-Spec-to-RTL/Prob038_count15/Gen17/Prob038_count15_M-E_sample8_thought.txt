I will implement the 4-bit counter using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk to simplify the design. The counter will increment using a simple addition operation q + 1, which synthesizes efficiently. The synchronous active-high reset will clear q to zero. Instead of separating combinational and sequential logic or manually implementing the ripple carry, this single-block approach improves readability and is functionally equivalent, relying on synthesis tools to optimize the increment operation internally. This yields a clean, minimal, and efficient counter design.
