To optimize the 4-bit counter for better timing and potentially reduce power by minimizing combinational carry propagation delay, I will replace the explicit ripple carry combinational logic with a direct binary increment expression using the '+' operator. This allows synthesis tools to infer a fast, parallel adder for incrementing. The synchronous active-high reset remains inside the sequential always block. This approach simplifies the combinational logic, reduces critical path delay, and may slightly reduce dynamic power due to minimized carry chain toggling, all while maintaining the original functional behavior and minimal area.
