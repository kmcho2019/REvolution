Implement the TopModule as a purely structural Verilog design using only basic gate primitives to explicitly describe the 2-input NOR function. This approach will use an OR gate followed by an inverter in series. By doing so, we avoid behavioral constructs like assign statements or parameterized modules, making the design fully explicit and easily understandable at the gate level.

This method also facilitates a direct mapping onto standard cells in ASIC flows or FPGA LUTs, with clear signal flow: inputs go into OR, and then OR output is inverted to get NOR. This design can serve well for educational purposes or situations requiring low-level gate control.

Advantages:
- No abstraction or behavioral code, purely structural.
- Clear step-by-step signal transformation.
- Easily extended to wider buses if needed by replicating the structure.
