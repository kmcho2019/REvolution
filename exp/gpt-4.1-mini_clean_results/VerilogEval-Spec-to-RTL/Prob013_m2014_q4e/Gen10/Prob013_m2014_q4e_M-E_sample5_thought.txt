Implement the TopModule as a fully structural design using only two simple primitive gates: a 2-input OR gate followed by an inverter. This approach explicitly shows the decomposition of the NOR operation into basic logic gates, providing clear gate-level insight without behavioral assignments.

Specifically:
- Instantiate a 2-input OR gate to produce an intermediate signal.
- Invert the output of the OR gate using a NOT gate to yield the NOR result.
  
This structural approach:
- Makes the logic gate composition explicit and easily modifiable.
- Uses Verilog's gate primitives directly for minimal overhead.
- Demonstrates fundamental gate-level design style, useful for educational and low-level synthesis scenarios.
- Can be easily extended for larger bit widths or more complex gates by instantiating more primitives.
