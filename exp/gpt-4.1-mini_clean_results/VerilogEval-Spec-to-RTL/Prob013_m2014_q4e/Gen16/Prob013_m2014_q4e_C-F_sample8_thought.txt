To fuse the strengths of both examples, I will implement the 2-input NOR gate directly using a single assign statement inside TopModule for optimal PPA (power, performance, area). This direct expression reduces gate levels, switching activity, and area compared to the two-module approach. Additionally, to maintain modularity and reusability, I will include simple parameterizable OrGate and NotGate modules that can be optionally instantiated elsewhere if needed, but not used inside TopModule itself. This keeps TopModule clean and minimal while preserving a modular hierarchy for potential future expansions or testing of primitive gates. This fusion offers the best of both worlds: minimal critical path and area for TopModule, plus reusable building blocks for modular design.
