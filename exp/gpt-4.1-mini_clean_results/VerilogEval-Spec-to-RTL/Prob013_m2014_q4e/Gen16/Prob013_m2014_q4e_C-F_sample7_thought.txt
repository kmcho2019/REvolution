To create a superior solution, I will fuse the modular clarity and testability from the examples with the efficiency and simplicity of direct combinational logic for optimal PPA metrics. Instead of instantiating separate OrGate and NotGate modules (which add area, power, and delay), I will directly implement the NOR function inside TopModule using a single assign statement: `assign out = ~(in1 | in2);`. This approach reduces logic depth to one gate, eliminates intermediate wires and toggling, and minimizes routing complexity.

However, to preserve modularity and educational clarity, I will keep the OrGate and NotGate modules defined but unused in TopModule, so they remain available for reuse or hierarchical design if needed. This fusion strategy balances modularity and reuse with synthesis-friendly, efficient design, yielding the best of both worlds.
