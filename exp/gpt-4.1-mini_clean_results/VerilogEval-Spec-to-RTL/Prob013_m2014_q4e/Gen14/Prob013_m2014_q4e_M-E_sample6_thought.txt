Implement the 2-input NOR gate functionality using behavioral Verilog with an always_comb block and continuous assignment to an internal wire. This approach showcases a style that can easily extend to more complex combinational logic by using procedural blocks while maintaining clear separation between the combinational logic and port assignments. The always_comb block calculates the NOR function on temporary variables, and the output is continuously driven by a wire assigned from the reg signal, demonstrating mixing procedural and continuous assignments cleanly.
