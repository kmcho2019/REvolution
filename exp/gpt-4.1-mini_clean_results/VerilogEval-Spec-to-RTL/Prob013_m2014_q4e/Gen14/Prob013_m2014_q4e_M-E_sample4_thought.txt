To provide a novel architectural idea, I will implement the 2-input NOR gate using only NAND gates and inverters, which are commonly used primitive gates in CMOS logic. This approach showcases how to realize a NOR function from universal gates, demonstrating fundamental logic synthesis techniques. 

Specifically, I'll define two modules: a NAND gate and an inverter. The TopModule will instantiate these gates accordingly to realize the NOR operation as NOR(a,b) = NAND(inverter(a), inverter(b)). This structure illustrates gate-level decomposition and modular reuse, differing structurally from a direct NOR gate or an OR+inverter composition. It also keeps a clear hierarchical design while using basic primitives.
