The original design uses a direct assign statement with the NOR operator, which is already minimal and optimal for area, power, and delay. Since NOR gates are basic primitives mapped efficiently to silicon, no logic restructuring can improve PPA metrics. However, to potentially enable better optimization by synthesis tools, I can explicitly instantiate a 2-input NOR gate primitive instead of using a behavioral assign expression. This small change can sometimes help synthesis with gate-level mapping and timing optimizations. Overall, the design remains functionally identical and equally minimal.
