Implement the 2-input NOR gate purely structurally by explicitly instantiating an OR gate and then a NOT gate. Define reusable OrGate and NotGate modules and instantiate them in TopModule. This modular, gate-level structural design reflects how NOR can be built from simpler primitives and allows clear tracing of logic gates, beneficial for hierarchical design and formal verification. It avoids direct continuous assignment with complex operators and provides a clear hardware mapping that may assist synthesis tools in certain flows or for educational clarity.
