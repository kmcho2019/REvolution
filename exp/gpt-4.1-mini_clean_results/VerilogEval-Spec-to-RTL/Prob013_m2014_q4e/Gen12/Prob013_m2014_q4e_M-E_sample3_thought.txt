Instead of using a dedicated NorGate module or direct assign, implement the NOR gate using primitive gate instantiations at the transistor logic level by combining an OR gate and a NOT gate in a structural style inside TopModule. This approach illustrates fundamental logic construction, explicitly showing the internal logic composition and giving a clearer view of signal flow. It also demonstrates how to build complex gates from simpler gates, which is educational and beneficial for understanding hierarchical design and for potential custom gate optimization later.
