The original TopModule implementation is already minimal and optimal, implementing a 2-input NOR gate with a direct assign statement. The only inefficiency is the presence of unused OrGate and NotGate modules, which unnecessarily increase code size and could affect synthesis. To optimize PPA, I will remove these unused modules, leaving a concise module containing just the needed logic. This will reduce area and potentially lower power slightly by minimizing netlist complexity and removing redundant declarations.
