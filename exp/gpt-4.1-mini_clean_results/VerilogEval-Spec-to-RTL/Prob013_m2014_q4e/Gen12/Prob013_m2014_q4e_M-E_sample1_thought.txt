To implement the 2-input NOR gate in a novel architectural way, I will leverage a behavioral Verilog style using continuous assignment but with De Morgan's law expressed differently: NOR(a,b) = (~a) & (~b). Instead of OR followed by NOT, I will implement the NOR gate using two NOT gates on inputs followed by an AND gate. This avoids direct OR computation and uses AND and NOT primitives instead, giving a structurally different yet functionally equivalent solution. I will instantiate small primitive modules for the NOT and AND gates, and wire them accordingly. This approach explores an alternative structural composition, emphasizing reusability and different logic decomposition that could be beneficial in some gate-library or synthesis scenarios.
