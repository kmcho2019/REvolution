To optimize the original solution for better power and area while maintaining performance, I will flatten the hierarchy by removing the separate NorGate module. Instead, the NOR logic will be directly implemented inside TopModule using a single continuous assignment. This eliminates the overhead of module instantiation and extra nets, reducing switching activity, interconnect complexity, and silicon area.

This direct approach is also the simplest and most efficient for synthesis tools to map the logic, providing minimal delay and lowest power consumption for a 2-input NOR gate. It sacrifices some modularity but maximizes PPA metrics for this trivial function.

In summary, the improved design:
- Removes the separate NorGate module.
- Implements the NOR operation directly inside TopModule with a continuous assign.
- Minimizes area, power, and wiring without impacting timing.
- Remains clean, minimal, and easy to understand.

This is the best practical balance given the trivial combinational logic and the provided metrics.
