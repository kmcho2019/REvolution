Fuse the best ideas by implementing the 2-input NOR gate as a direct continuous assignment for minimal logic levels and optimal PPA metrics, while retaining modularity through internal definition of basic gates for potential reusability. The TopModule uses a single assign statement for the NOR output, providing the simplest and most efficient implementation. Meanwhile, the OrGate and NotGate modules remain defined but unused here, preserving modular code structure for future scalability without introducing extra delay or area overhead. This approach balances clarity, modularity, and PPA optimization.
