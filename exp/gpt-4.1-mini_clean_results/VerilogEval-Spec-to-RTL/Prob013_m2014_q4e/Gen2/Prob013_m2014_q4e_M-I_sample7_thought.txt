The original design is already minimal and optimal for implementing a 2-input NOR gate. However, to potentially enhance power efficiency and slightly improve timing, we can use the Verilog primitive `nor` gate directly instead of using a bitwise OR and negation. This maps directly to a NOR gate in synthesis, which might produce better-optimized results in backend tools. Also, the primitive instantiation clearly expresses the intended gate, potentially aiding synthesis tools in technology mapping and optimization. The area and performance should remain the same or slightly better, and power might slightly improve due to direct gate inference.
