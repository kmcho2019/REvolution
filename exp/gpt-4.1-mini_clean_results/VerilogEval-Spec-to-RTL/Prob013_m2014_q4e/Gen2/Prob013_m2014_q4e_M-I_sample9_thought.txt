The initial implementation uses a direct continuous assignment with a bitwise NOR, which is already minimal and optimal for a simple 2-input NOR gate in terms of logic, timing, and area. However, to potentially improve power consumption by reducing switching activity, we can use a structural approach with a built-in nor primitive gate rather than using the bitwise negation of an OR.

Using the dedicated 'nor' gate primitive might provide better mapping during synthesis and technology mapping stages, potentially resulting in more efficient gate-level implementation and reduced power.

Therefore, the improvement strategy is to replace the continuous assignment with a structural instantiation of the NOR gate primitive.
