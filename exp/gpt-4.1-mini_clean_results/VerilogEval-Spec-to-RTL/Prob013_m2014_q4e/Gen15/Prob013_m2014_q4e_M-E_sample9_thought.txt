I will implement the NOR functionality by building it from two simple modules: an OR gate module and a NOT gate module, explicitly demonstrating the construction of a NOR gate through modular composition. The TopModule will instantiate the OR gate and then feed its output into the NOT gate, producing the NOR result. This approach highlights modular design principles and makes each logic function reusable and testable in isolation, which is beneficial for larger designs where gate-level building blocks are common.
