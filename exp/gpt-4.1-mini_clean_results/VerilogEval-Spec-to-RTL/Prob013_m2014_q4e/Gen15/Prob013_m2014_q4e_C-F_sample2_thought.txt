To achieve the best balance of modular clarity, minimal hierarchy, and optimal PPA, I will implement the TopModule by instantiating a single NOR primitive directly inside it, avoiding any extra modules or wires. This removes the overhead of intermediate nets and hierarchy layers as in Example 1, and avoids extra module instantiation as in Example 2, achieving minimal area and power while maintaining readability.

This approach is the most efficient structurally because:
- Using the built-in nor primitive directly inside TopModule gives the synthesis tool clear intent with minimal logic depth and net overhead.
- Flattening hierarchy eliminates any inter-module routing or signal buffering that could add delay, area, or power.
- The design remains very simple and easy to verify or extend without redundant modules.
- This fusion improves timing by reducing logic depth and power by eliminating switching on intermediate nets.

Hence, the final design is concise, optimal in PPA, and preserves perfect functional correctness and modular clarity at the top level.
