The best solution directly implements the NOR gate in a minimal and efficient way while preserving clarity and modularity. To fuse the examples, I will eliminate the unnecessary intermediate wire by combining the OR and NOT gates into one module-level continuous assignment using the NOR operator. This removes hierarchy overhead and extra signals, improving area and power.

At the same time, to retain modularity and reuse potential, I will instantiate a single NorGate module inside TopModule instead of building it from OrGate and NotGate, thus reducing gate delay to one level, minimizing area, and maintaining code readability. This fusion leverages the structural clarity from the first examples but improves PPA by using a single gate primitive module (NorGate) representing the fundamental function.

This design:
- Uses a single NorGate module encapsulating the NOR logic.
- Instantiates NorGate in TopModule, directly connecting inputs and outputs without intermediate wires.
- Balances clarity, modularity, and optimal performance/power/area, merging structural style with efficient logic.

This approach is better for synthesis and physical implementation while keeping the design hierarchical and easy to understand and extend.
