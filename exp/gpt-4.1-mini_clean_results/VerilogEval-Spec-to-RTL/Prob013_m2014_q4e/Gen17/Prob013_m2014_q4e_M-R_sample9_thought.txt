I will simplify the TopModule by removing the explicit instantiation of the OrGate and NotGate modules and instead implement the NOR function directly using a single continuous assignment statement: `assign out = ~(in1 | in2);`. This preserves the core logic (NOR gate) but uses a more concise structure. This refactoring reduces module hierarchy, lowers the area and power slightly by eliminating the intermediate net, and keeps the design purely combinational and easy to read.
