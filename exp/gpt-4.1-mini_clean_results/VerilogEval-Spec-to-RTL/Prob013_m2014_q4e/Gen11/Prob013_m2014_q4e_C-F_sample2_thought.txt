To fuse the strengths of both examples, I will implement the 2-input NOR gate directly with a single assign statement for maximal performance, minimal area, and low power, as in Example 1. At the same time, I will retain modularity and scalability by internally defining minimalistic OrGate and NotGate primitives and instantiate them within TopModule using a `generate` block. This approach provides a clean structural hierarchy and code clarity like Example 2 but avoids the overhead of additional wires and module instances in the critical path by making the direct assign the functional output. The generate block structure allows easy scalability and potential reuse of the basic gates for future extension without compromising the current minimal gate delay, power, and area. In summary, I combine direct optimal logic in TopModule’s final output with structural primitives available internally and prepared for extension, balancing modularity with efficiency.
