Combine the clarity and modularity of structural gate-level design with the efficiency of direct assign logic for optimal PPA. Define minimal OrGate and NotGate modules for reuse and clarity, but implement the TopModule's NOR function directly using a continuous assignment with the NOR operator (~|). This fusion provides a clean hierarchy for scalability and testing, while leveraging the simplest, most performant implementation in TopModule itself. This approach reduces delay and power compared to chaining gates and limits area by avoiding unnecessary wiring and module instantiations inside TopModule, yet retains reusable gate definitions for potential expansion or alternate module use.
