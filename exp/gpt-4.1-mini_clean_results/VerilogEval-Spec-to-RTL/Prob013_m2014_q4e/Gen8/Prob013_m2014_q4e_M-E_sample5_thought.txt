Implement the 2-input NOR gate using behavioral Verilog inside an always_comb block with non-blocking assignments. This approach demonstrates usage of procedural style for combinational logic, allowing easy expansion for multi-bit inputs in the future. The design uses a single always block that computes the NOR result by first OR-ing inputs and then inverting the result. This differs from a pure assign-based implementation by explicitly modeling combinational logic in procedural style, useful for teaching or testbench-like scenarios.
