Combine the clarity and modularity of structural gate-level design with the simplicity and optimal PPA of direct gate primitives by defining a single NOR gate module (NorGate). This specialized module internally uses a direct assign statement for minimal delay and area. The TopModule then instantiates this NorGate. This approach keeps the design hierarchical and easily scalable with reusable basic gate modules, but avoids extra wiring and delays caused by separate OrGate and NotGate instantiations. It yields the best PPA by merging modularity with minimal logic.
