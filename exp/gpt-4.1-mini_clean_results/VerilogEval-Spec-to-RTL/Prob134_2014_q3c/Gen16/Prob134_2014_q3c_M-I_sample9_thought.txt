To improve the previous solution's PPA, the key idea is to remove the conditional operator (ternary) in ns0 to avoid the MUX logic and to share repeated subexpressions for both ns2 and z, thus reducing area and power by minimizing duplicated logic. 

Specifically:
- Extract common conditions like state_001 and state_100 to wires.
- Express ns0 without a conditional by using logic expressions derived from the truth table or directly combining minterms.
- This reduces gate count, critical path complexity, and switching activity.

All next state bits and output z are purely combinational based on present state y and input x, so the output Y0 can be directly assigned from ns0 without registers.

This approach retains clarity and correctness while optimizing logic for better PPA.
