To improve the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) of the FSM logic:

1. Replace explicit 3-bit equality comparisons (which synthesize to comparators) with encoded one-hot-style signals for states present in the FSM (since only 5 states are defined). This reduces the number of comparators.
2. Compute next_state and output z signals from these simpler one-hot encoded signals.
3. Use minimal boolean expressions for next state and output.
4. For undefined states (101, 110, 111), output next state 000 to avoid glitches.
5. This approach reduces complex comparators and results in smaller, faster, and lower power logic.
6. The output Y0 remains next_state[0] as specified.
7. The code stays combinational and synthesizable with no sequential elements as per spec.

This method reduces area by eliminating multiple 3-bit comparators and can improve performance by reducing logic depth. Power is improved due to fewer toggling signals and simpler combinational logic.
