To improve PPA, especially power and area, I will eliminate explicit equality comparators used for decoding the present state. Instead, I will encode the states as a 5-bit one-hot signal directly using minimal combinational logic derived from y[2:0]. This reduces comparator complexity and switching.

Then, I will derive next state bits and output z from these one-hot signals and input x through minimized boolean expressions using simple gates.

Additionally, I will flatten the ns0 logic into a pure combinational expression instead of a ternary operator.

This method reduces the number of equality comparators, saves logic gates, and lowers switching activity, thus improving power and area, while preserving timing due to shallow combinational depth.
