Since y is given as the present state, and x as input, we can implement the entire logic combinationally without registers by directly mapping present state and input to next state and output. The output z depends only on present state, and Y0 is simply the LSB of the next state. Thus, we can use a single combinational always block to compute next_state based on y and x, assign z from y, and assign Y0 from next_state[0]. This removes unnecessary registers and simplifies the design while maintaining full functionality.
