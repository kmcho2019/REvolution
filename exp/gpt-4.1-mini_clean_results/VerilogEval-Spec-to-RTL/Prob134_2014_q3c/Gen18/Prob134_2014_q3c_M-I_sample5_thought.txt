2. Simplify the next_state[1] logic by expressing it directly in terms of present state bits and input x, minimizing the number of separate decoded signals.
3. Reduce redundant signals by reusing intermediate terms and minimizing logical operations.
4. Use continuous assignments as in the original since synchronous logic isn’t specified.
