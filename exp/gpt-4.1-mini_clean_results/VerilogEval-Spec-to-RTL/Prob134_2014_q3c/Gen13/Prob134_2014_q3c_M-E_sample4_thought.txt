Implement the FSM logic using lookup tables (LUTs) encoded as combinational ROM arrays for next state and output z. Index these ROMs with the concatenation of present state and input x. This approach uses constant arrays to directly map (present state, input) pairs to next states and outputs. It improves clarity and enables easy extension or modification of state transitions by changing ROM contents. Outputs are assigned from ROM lookups, and Y0 is taken from the least significant bit of the next state. This architectural style is different from functions or case statements and emphasizes data-driven design.
