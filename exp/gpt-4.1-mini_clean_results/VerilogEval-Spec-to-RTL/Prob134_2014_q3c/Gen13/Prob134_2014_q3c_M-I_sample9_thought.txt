To optimize the design for better PPA, I will simplify the next state logic by expressing it as combinational assignments with minimal logic expressions instead of a case statement inside a function. This will reduce the depth and complexity of multiplexers generated by the case statement. For the output z, I'll implement its logic as a single expression without equality comparisons, by recognizing that z=1 only when y=3'b011 or y=3'b100, so I will decode this with a direct combinational expression. By doing so, the synthesizer can produce simpler gates, reducing area and power. Also, since the clock input is unused, it will be left as is.
