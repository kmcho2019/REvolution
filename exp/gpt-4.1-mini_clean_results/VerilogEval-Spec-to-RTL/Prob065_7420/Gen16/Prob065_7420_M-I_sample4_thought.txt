To optimize the original modular design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will inline the 4-input NAND gate logic directly inside the TopModule instead of instantiating a separate nand4 submodule. This reduces hierarchy and potentially lowers area and delay due to direct gate-level logic, helping synthesis tools better optimize it. Also, explicitly implementing the 4-input NAND using two 2-input NAND stages can help in some target libraries by reducing large fan-in gate delay and power, trading off some area for better timing and power.

Thus, the improvement strategy is:
- Remove the nand4 submodule to reduce hierarchy overhead.
- Implement each 4-input NAND gate as two stages of 2-input NAND gates to balance delay, power, and area better.
- Use local wires for intermediate signals.
- Keep code combinational and easy to synthesize.
This approach should reduce power and delay slightly, and area may remain similar or reduce due to smaller fan-in gates. The improvement aligns with typical CMOS logic design practice where multiple 2-input gates outperform a single high fan-in gate.

The TopModule will then consist of two blocks of cascaded 2-input NAND gates, one for each 4-input NAND gate function of the 7420.
