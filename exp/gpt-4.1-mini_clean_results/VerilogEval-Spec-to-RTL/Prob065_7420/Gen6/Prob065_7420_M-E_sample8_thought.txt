To provide a novel architectural approach different from direct gate instantiations, I will implement the 7420 functionality by decomposing each 4-input NAND gate into a hierarchy of smaller gates. Specifically, each 4-input NAND can be constructed by two 2-input NAND gates feeding into a 2-input NAND gate. This approach mimics a typical gate-level design using only 2-input NAND gates (since NAND is functionally complete) and reflects how a 7420 might be internally realized from more basic cells. This hierarchical structure also clarifies how the large NAND gate is built from simpler units, which may help in understanding and mapping to various technology libraries without a native 4-input NAND gate. It also introduces gate instantiations as modular 2-input NANDs, a common cell in many standard libraries.
