The original design is already clean, modular, and perfectly matches the 7420 chip's function using two 4-input NAND gates. Its performance and area are optimal for this simple combinational logic. The only opportunity for marginal improvement is reducing power consumption by minimizing internal wiring and switching activity.

To achieve this, I will inline the 4-input NAND functionality directly inside the TopModule with continuous assignments, removing the nand4 module. This eliminates the slight overhead of instantiations and internal nets, potentially reducing net switching and simplifying the netlist for synthesis. While this slightly reduces modularity, the straightforward continuous assignments can lead to marginally lower dynamic power due to less interconnect.

The inlined direct assign style maintains the exact functionality and gate count (two 4-input NAND gates), so area and timing remain optimal. Power may improve slightly due to simplified netlist and reduced switching from fewer nets.

This approach strikes a good balance of minimalism, clarity, and potential minor power optimization, keeping performance and area unchanged.
