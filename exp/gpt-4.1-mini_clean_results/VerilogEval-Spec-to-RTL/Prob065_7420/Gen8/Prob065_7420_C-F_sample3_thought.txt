I will fuse the clarity and modularity of having a dedicated 4-input NAND gate module with the directness of continuous assignments and clear port naming for straightforward mapping to the 7420 chip. To improve maintainability and synthesis friendliness, I keep the nand4 module simple with a single continuous assign using bitwise AND and negation. For TopModule, I instantiate two nand4 gates to represent the two independent 4-input NAND gates in the 7420 chip. Additionally, I add concise comments describing each part’s purpose and ensure consistent naming. This combination offers code reuse, clean hierarchy, clear functionality, and synthesis efficiency, while preserving minimal area and power by avoiding unnecessary logic.
