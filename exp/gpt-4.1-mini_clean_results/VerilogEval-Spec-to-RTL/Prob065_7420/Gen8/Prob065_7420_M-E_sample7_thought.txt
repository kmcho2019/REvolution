Instead of instantiating a separate 4-input NAND gate module twice, I will implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly inside the TopModule using continuous assignments. This approach removes the need for a submodule, resulting in a flatter module hierarchy. It still closely represents the 7420 chip functionality by computing NAND of each group of four inputs separately, but now the logic is explicitly laid out at the top level. This may simplify debugging and enhance synthesis tools' ability to optimize the logic since there are no module boundaries. This approach is still fully combinational, minimal, and clear.
