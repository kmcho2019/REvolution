Instead of creating a separate nand4 module, I will implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly inside the TopModule using intermediate wires to explicitly AND all four inputs and then invert the result. This decomposes the NAND operation into AND followed by NOT operations, which can enhance visibility into each step of the logic and allow synthesis tools to optimize the gates independently if desired. This approach maintains functional correctness and closely models the 7420 chip while offering a different architectural style that emphasizes clear logic stages.
