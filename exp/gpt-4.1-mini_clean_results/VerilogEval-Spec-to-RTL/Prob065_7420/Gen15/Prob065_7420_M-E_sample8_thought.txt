Instead of creating a separate 4-input NAND module, I will implement the 7420 functionality entirely within the TopModule using continuous assignments. Each output will be generated by directly NANDing the four inputs using the Verilog reduction AND operator with bitwise negation, which succinctly and efficiently captures the 4-input NAND behavior.

This approach avoids module overhead, reduces hierarchy, and simplifies the design to a purely combinational block with minimal code. It also improves synthesis clarity by eliminating unnecessary module instantiations while keeping the intent clear.

The inputs and outputs remain unchanged. This flat combinational implementation is straightforward, minimal, and perfectly models the 7420 chip's behavior with two independent 4-input NAND gates.
