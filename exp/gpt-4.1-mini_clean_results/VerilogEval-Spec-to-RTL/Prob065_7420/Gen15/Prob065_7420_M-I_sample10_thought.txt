To optimize the design for better power and potentially area while preserving correctness and clarity, I will remove the synthesis `(* keep = "true" *)` attributes. These attributes prevent gate merging and optimization by synthesis tools, which may slightly increase power and area unnecessarily in such a simple design. 

Removing these constraints allows the synthesis tool to merge or optimize the NAND gates internally, potentially reducing switching activity and cell usage. Since the original design is already minimal and simple, this change maintains clean modularity and readability without impacting functionality.

The code will remain modular with a reusable `nand4` module, but with no synthesis preservation hints, allowing efficient synthesis. This change trades strict physical gate mapping for improved PPA in a practical design context.

Overall, this results in slightly better power and area metrics, with unchanged performance given the negligible delay in 4-input NAND logic.
