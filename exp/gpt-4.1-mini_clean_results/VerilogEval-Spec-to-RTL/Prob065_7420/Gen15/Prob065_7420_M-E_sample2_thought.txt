I will implement the 7420 chip functionality without using a dedicated 4-input NAND gate module. Instead, I will construct each 4-input NAND gate directly within the TopModule by composing two levels of 2-input NAND gates. This approach mirrors gate-level structural design and showcases how a 4-input NAND gate can be synthesized from multiple 2-input NAND gates, which are more fundamental building blocks.

This design offers a different architectural perspective and could be useful when only 2-input NAND gates are available. It maintains explicit structural hierarchy but avoids additional modules for a compact solution. The logic depth will be two NAND gate delays, still suitable for synthesis. Comments will clarify the construction for maintainability and readability.

Thus, the TopModule will:
- Build each 4-input NAND gate from three 2-input NAND gates.
- Use internal wires to connect intermediate signals.
- Provide the same functionality as the 7420 but from a different gate-level construction.

This solution highlights a foundational gate composition technique while fully meeting the problem requirements.
