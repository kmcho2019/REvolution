Instead of defining a separate nand4 module, I will implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly inside TopModule using structural gate-level primitives. This means instantiating 2-input NAND gates and building the 4-input NAND gate from smaller 2-input NANDs, replicating the actual transistor-level structure of a 4-input NAND. This approach showcases how the internal structure of a 7420 chip can be modeled using basic gates, offering a more granular and educational architectural perspective. It also allows easier tracing or modification of each NAND stage.
