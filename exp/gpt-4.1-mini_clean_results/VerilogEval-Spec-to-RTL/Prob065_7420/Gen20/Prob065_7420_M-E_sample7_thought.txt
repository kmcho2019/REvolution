To implement the 7420's functionality with a fresh architectural approach, I will design a single, parameterized 4-input NAND gate module that can be instantiated twice inside TopModule. However, instead of straightforward continuous assignment, I will implement the NAND logic using a structural approach with built-in Verilog gates, breaking down the NAND into smaller components.

Specifically, I will:

- Create a nand4_struct module that internally uses three 2-input AND gates and a single 2-input NAND gate to compose a 4-input NAND gate structurally.
- This approach highlights gate-level construction resembling how the hardware might be built internally, offering clarity on the composition of the 4-input NAND.
- Instantiate two instances of nand4_struct in TopModule, connecting inputs accordingly.
- This structural style might slightly increase area but better illustrates the gate interconnections, which is beneficial for educational and debugging purposes.
- Also, the module remains purely combinational with no sequential elements.
- Keep the design modular and maintainable by clear hierarchy and comments.

This method provides an alternative viewpoint on the 7420's logic, moving away from simple assign statements toward gate-level module composition.
