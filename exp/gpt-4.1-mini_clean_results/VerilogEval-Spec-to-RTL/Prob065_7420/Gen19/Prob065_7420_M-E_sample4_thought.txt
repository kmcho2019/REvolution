I will implement the TopModule using direct continuous assignments without creating a separate nand4 module. Each output will be computed by applying a bitwise AND to the four inputs followed by a negation to mimic a 4-input NAND gate. This approach uses fewer lines of code and avoids extra module instantiations, potentially simplifying synthesis and reducing overhead. While this sacrifices some modularity, it is a straightforward solution that faithfully replicates the 7420 chip's behavior with minimal logic.
