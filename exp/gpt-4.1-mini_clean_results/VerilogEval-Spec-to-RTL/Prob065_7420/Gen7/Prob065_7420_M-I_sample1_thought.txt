The original design already implements two 4-input NAND gates using a clean modular approach, which aligns well with the 7420 chip's functionality and is minimal in logic depth and gate count.

To further optimize for power and area without changing the logical behavior, I will eliminate the separate nand4 module and implement the 4-input NAND gates directly as continuous assignments inside TopModule. This removes module overhead and simplifies hierarchy, which may aid synthesis tools in optimization (e.g., gate merging or direct mapping). 

This approach reduces the module instantiation overhead and removes the explicit nand4 instance signals, thus potentially lowering the area marginally and possibly reducing power by preventing additional nets and related switching.

Since the logic remains a single-level NAND operation for each output, performance will remain optimal.

In summary, the improved design:
- Removes the nand4 sub-module
- Implements two direct 4-input NAND gates in TopModule via continuous assignments

This should provide slight improvements in power and area while maintaining perfect correctness and timing.
