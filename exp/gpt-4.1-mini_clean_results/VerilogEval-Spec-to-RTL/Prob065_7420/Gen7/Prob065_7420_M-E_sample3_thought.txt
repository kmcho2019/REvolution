Implement the 7420 functionality by directly using continuous assignments for both 4-input NAND gates within the TopModule, without defining a separate nand4 module. This flattening of hierarchy minimizes module instantiations and simplifies the code, which can sometimes assist synthesis tools in optimizing logic further.

Each output is assigned the NAND of its corresponding four inputs in a single line, making the design concise and clear.

This approach maintains the functional equivalence to the 7420 chip but achieves it with a minimalist, flat module style.
