The original design is functionally correct and clean, but we can further optimize area and power by eliminating the explicit nand4 module instantiations and directly implementing the 4-input NAND gates using continuous assignments inside TopModule. This removes the module hierarchy overhead and may lead to simpler logic during synthesis.

Additionally, combining the NAND expressions directly in TopModule reduces the number of nets and instances, potentially lowering area and power slightly.

This approach trades off modularity for minimal structural complexity, which is acceptable here since the nand4 module is simple and only used twice.

No timing degradation is expected, as the logic depth remains the same (one NAND stage).

Hence, the improved design directly assigns p1y and p2y outputs from the NAND of the four inputs each, with no additional module instantiation or wires.
