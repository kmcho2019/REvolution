The original design is already functionally correct and clean, instantiating two separate 4-input NAND gates as the 7420 chip specification requires. However, the small overhead of separate module instantiations can be avoided by merging the logic directly into the top module, which can reduce area and potentially improve power slightly by eliminating module boundary overhead. Using a single-level assign with direct bitwise operators is the most efficient for synthesis. This design is minimal in gate count and keeps critical path short. Thus, I'll simplify by removing the nand4 module and directly assigning outputs in TopModule using built-in operators. This reduces area and power marginally without sacrificing clarity or correctness.
