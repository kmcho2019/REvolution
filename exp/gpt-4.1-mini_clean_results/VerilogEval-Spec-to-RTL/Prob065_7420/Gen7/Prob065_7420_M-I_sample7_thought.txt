The previous design is functionally correct and clean, implementing the 7420 chip behavior by instantiating two 4-input NAND gates as separate modules. To optimize the power, area, and possibly timing further, I will inline the 4-input NAND functionality directly into the TopModule instead of using separate submodule instantiations. This removes the small overhead of module boundaries and allows synthesis tools to better optimize the two NAND functions together. Using direct assign statements with combined bitwise operations typically results in slightly reduced area and power, as the synthesis tool can potentially share logic or optimize the gates better. This approach sacrifices some modular clarity for a slight PPA gain, which is beneficial for such a simple structure.

Additionally, the direct assign statements are concise and synthesis friendly, ensuring minimal logic depth and optimal critical path delay. Overall, this change should yield improved or comparable timing and power with reduced area.

This improvement targets RTL-level optimization without changing the logic functionality.
