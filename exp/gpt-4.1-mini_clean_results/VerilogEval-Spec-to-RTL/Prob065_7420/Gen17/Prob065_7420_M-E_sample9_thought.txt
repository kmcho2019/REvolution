Instead of creating a separate nand4 module, I will implement the TopModule using behavioral continuous assignments directly in the top-level module. To do this, I will first compute the AND of each group of four inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) using intermediate wires, then invert those results to produce the NAND outputs. 

This approach removes the need for a separate nand4 module and consolidates the logic within one module. It clearly shows the logical steps of 4-input NAND as an inverted 4-input AND, preserving functionality but demonstrating a different coding style. This method is easy to synthesize and clearly expresses the 7420 functionality with simple combinational logic.

Additionally, using intermediate wires improves readability and helps with debugging or further modifications without creating multiple modules.
