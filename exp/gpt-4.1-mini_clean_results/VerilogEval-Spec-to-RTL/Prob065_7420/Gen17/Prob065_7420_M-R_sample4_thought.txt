I will refactor the design by removing the separate nand4 module and instead implementing the 4-input NAND gates directly inside the TopModule using continuous assign statements. This eliminates an extra module and makes the design more compact while still keeping it purely combinational and easy to understand.

The combinational logic will be expressed directly with a single assign for each output, computing the NAND of the four respective inputs without procedural blocks or intermediate wires. This approach provides a concise, flat RTL structure that synthesizes equivalently to the original design but is simpler by having a single module.

This refactoring maintains:
- The exact 7420 logic functionality (two independent 4-input NAND gates)
- Clear, minimal RTL using only assigns in the top-level module
- Purely combinational logic with no unnecessary hierarchy or complexity
- Easy maintainability and direct mapping to NAND gate primitives during synthesis
