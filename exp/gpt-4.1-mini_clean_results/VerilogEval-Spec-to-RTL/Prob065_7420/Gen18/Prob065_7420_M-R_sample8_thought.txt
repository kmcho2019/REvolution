Refactor the original design by removing the separate nand4 module and directly implementing the two 4-input NAND gates within the TopModule using continuous assign statements. This approach eliminates module instantiations, simplifying the hierarchy and making the design flat. Using direct assignments improves readability for simple combinational logic and slightly reduces overhead associated with instantiations. It retains the exact functionality of the 7420 chip while providing a straightforward and concise RTL implementation.
