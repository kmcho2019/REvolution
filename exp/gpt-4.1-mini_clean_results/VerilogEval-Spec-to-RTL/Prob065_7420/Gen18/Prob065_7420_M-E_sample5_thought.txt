I will implement the 7420 functionality using behavioral Verilog with direct NAND expressions for each output without instantiating any submodules. This approach provides a fully self-contained TopModule with explicit logic expressions, improving readability by avoiding hierarchy for such a simple function. The two 4-input NAND gates will be implemented by combining bitwise AND and negation operations directly in continuous assignments.

This method removes module instantiation overhead and may lead to simpler netlists after synthesis. It also demonstrates a straightforward architectural style: each output is driven by a single continuous assignment implementing the NAND of the corresponding four inputs.

Thus, the design is a concise, flat module representing the 7420 chip’s two 4-input NAND gates purely with behavioral expressions.
