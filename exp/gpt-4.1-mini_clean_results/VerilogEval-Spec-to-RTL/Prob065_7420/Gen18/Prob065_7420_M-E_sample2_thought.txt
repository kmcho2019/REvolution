I will implement the TopModule using direct continuous assignments without instantiating a separate nand4 module, thereby flattening the design hierarchy and reducing module overhead. Each 4-input NAND gate will be implemented inline using a combination of bitwise AND and negation operators.

To add a unique architectural touch, I will create intermediate wires for the AND results of the four inputs of each gate, followed by negation for the NAND output. This gives a clear signal flow, improves readability by naming intermediate signals, and could help synthesis tools optimize logic sharing or mapping.

This approach still results in the same logical behavior as the 7420 chip (two independent 4-input NAND gates), but the intermediate signals provide a clean structure without module instantiation, potentially aiding debugging and simulation visibility.

The design remains fully combinational, zero latency, and simple for synthesis tools.
