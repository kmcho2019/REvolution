To create a superior solution, we combine the clarity and synthesis-friendliness of explicit NAND primitives from Example 1 with the minimalistic, direct expression style of Example 2. Using the built-in 4-input NAND primitives explicitly signals to synthesis tools that these are fundamental NAND gates, improving mapping and possibly optimizing area and power.

At the same time, the design remains concise and simple with just two instantiations of 4-input NAND gates, ensuring minimal logic depth and no redundant logic.

This fusion:
- Uses the nand primitives for gate-level clarity and possible synthesis optimization.
- Keeps the code minimal and direct without unnecessary intermediate signals.
- Matches exactly the 7420 chip functionality with two independent 4-input NAND gates.

This approach provides the best balance of readability, optimal synthesis results, and minimal logic implementation.
