To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will maintain the modular clarity and reusability of a dedicated 4-input NAND gate module from Example 1, which aligns with good design practices and improves maintainability. At the same time, I will keep the simplicity and compactness of direct assignments as in Example 2 by implementing the nand4 module internally using a single continuous assignment, ensuring minimal and clear logic.

This approach combines modular abstraction for clean code organization and synthesis friendliness with straightforward, minimal combinational logic for optimal power and area.

By defining a small nand4 module, the design remains scalable and readable, while the internal implementation stays minimal and efficient.

The TopModule instantiates two nand4 gates, preserving both clarity and efficiency.
