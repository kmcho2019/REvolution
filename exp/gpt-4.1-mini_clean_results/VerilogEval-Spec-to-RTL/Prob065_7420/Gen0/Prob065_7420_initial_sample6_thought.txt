The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each gate takes four inputs and outputs the NAND of those inputs. For the TopModule, we'll create two 4-input NAND operations:

- p1y = NAND of p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d
- p2y = NAND of p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d

This can be implemented using the Verilog NAND operator (~&) or by combining the inputs with an AND and then inverting the result.
