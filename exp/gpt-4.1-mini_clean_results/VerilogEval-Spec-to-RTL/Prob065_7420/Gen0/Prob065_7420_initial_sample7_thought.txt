The 7420 chip contains two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate takes 4 inputs and outputs the NAND (NOT AND) of these inputs. For the TopModule, inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d form the inputs to the first NAND gate whose output is p1y; inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d form the inputs to the second NAND gate whose output is p2y. The output is low only if all inputs to the gate are high; otherwise, output is high.
