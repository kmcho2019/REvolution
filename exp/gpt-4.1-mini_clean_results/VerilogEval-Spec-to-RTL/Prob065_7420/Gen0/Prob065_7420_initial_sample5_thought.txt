The 7420 chip consists of two 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate outputs the negation of the logical AND of its four inputs. 
We have two sets of inputs: p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d for the first NAND gate, and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d for the second NAND gate.
Outputs p1y and p2y are the NAND results of the respective inputs.
