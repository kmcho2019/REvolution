The 7420 chip has two independent 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate outputs a low signal only when all its four inputs are high; otherwise, it outputs high. The module will implement this behavior by connecting inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d to one NAND gate producing output p1y, and inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d to another NAND gate producing output p2y.
