The original design is already minimal and structurally clear by using two instantiations of a 4-input NAND gate module, matching the 7420 functionality. Since PPA (Performance, Power, Area) metrics indicate the design is optimal at the RTL level (pure combinational 4-input NAND gates), the only potential improvement could be to merge the two nand4 gates into a single parameterized module that can handle multiple instances internally. 

This may slightly reduce area by allowing synthesis tools to share gate-level resources or optimize placement better, especially if the two 4-input NAND gates are part of the same module scope. However, such optimization depends heavily on synthesis and physical tools.

Thus, I will refactor the code to use a single `Nand7420` module that contains two 4-input NAND outputs internally without external module instantiations. This reduces hierarchy and may help synthesis optimize better. The logic remains the same.

This approach keeps the design fully combinational, minimal, and very clear while possibly helping synthesis tools with logic sharing and optimization opportunities to improve area or power marginally.
