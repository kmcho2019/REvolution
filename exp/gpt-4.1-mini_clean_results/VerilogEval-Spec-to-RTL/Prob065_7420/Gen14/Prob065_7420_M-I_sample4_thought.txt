The initial solution is already clean and modular, but we can optimize it slightly to improve power and area without sacrificing clarity. Instead of defining a separate nand4 module, we can directly use the built-in 4-input nand primitive gate available in many synthesis tools, which can lead to more optimized gate-level mapping and possibly reduced power/area due to vendor-specific library optimizations.

Moreover, eliminating the extra module layer can slightly reduce area and simplify the design hierarchy while maintaining the same functionality and performance.

Thus, the improvement strategy is:
- Replace the custom nand4 module with direct use of the Verilog built-in 4-input nand gate primitives.
- Keep code concise and clear.
- Maintain identical input/output interface and behavior.
- This should yield slight gains in power and area due to direct primitive mapping and reduced hierarchy overhead, while keeping timing essentially the same.

This approach prioritizes synthesis tool friendliness and minor physical improvements without losing readability or verification simplicity.
