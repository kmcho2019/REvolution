The existing design cleanly and correctly implements the two 4-input NAND gates of the 7420 chip with a modular nand4 submodule. Given the already minimal combinational logic, zero timing slack, and very low area and power, structural or logical optimization opportunities are very limited.

To further optimize:
- I can inline the nand4 logic directly in the TopModule to eliminate module instantiation overhead, which may slightly reduce area and possibly power due to fewer nets and slightly simpler netlist.
- This removes hierarchical boundary for synthesis, potentially allowing the synthesis tool to optimize the NAND logic even more effectively.
- The top-level will still remain clear and straightforward by using assign statements for the NAND outputs.
- This should not affect timing since 4-input NAND is inherently minimal logic depth.
- Minimalistic coding and fewer instantiated modules slightly favor smaller area and possibly lower power.

Overall, inlining the NAND gate logic will give a marginal PPA improvement without loss of readability or correctness.
