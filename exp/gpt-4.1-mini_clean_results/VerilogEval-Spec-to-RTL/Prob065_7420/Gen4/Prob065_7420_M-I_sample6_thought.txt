The existing implementation is already clean and modular, but we can optimize for better area and power by removing the extra module instantiation and writing the dual 4-input NAND gates directly using bitwise reduction operators. This approach eliminates the overhead of module instantiation and makes the logic compact and easy for synthesis tools to optimize into standard NAND4 cells or equivalent. It also reduces net count and wiring, which can improve area and power slightly. Using assign statements with bitwise operations is synthesis-friendly and maintains clarity.
