To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, and area), I will eliminate the explicit nand4 module instantiations and implement the two 4-input NAND functions directly using built-in Verilog operators. This reduces hierarchy and can encourage synthesis tools to map directly to the best available 4-input NAND primitives in the technology library, potentially reducing area and power. Additionally, I will implement the 4-input NAND function as two levels of 2-input NAND gates because some standard cell libraries do not have optimized 4-input NAND gates, and 2-input NANDs are usually the most optimized cells. This can help minimize area and power consumption while maintaining equivalent timing. This structural approach provides a clear mapping to efficient hardware and reduces overhead from extra module instantiations.
