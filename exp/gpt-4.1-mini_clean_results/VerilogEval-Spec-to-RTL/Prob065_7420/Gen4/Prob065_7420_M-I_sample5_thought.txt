The original design is functionally correct and clear, using a reusable 4-input NAND module. However, to improve power and area metrics further, I will replace the 4-input NAND gate with a hierarchical structure of 2-input NAND gates, which can better exploit library-optimized 2-input NAND cells and reduce internal node fanout capacitances. This structure is also more synthesis-friendly and might lead to better optimization by synthesis tools.

Specifically, implement each 4-input NAND as two levels of 2-input NANDs:
- First level: nand_ab = ~(a & b), nand_cd = ~(c & d)
- Second level: y = ~(~(nand_ab) & ~(nand_cd)) = nand(nand_ab_bar, nand_cd_bar)
Because the output is NAND of all four inputs, this two-level nand implementation is equivalent.

This reduces the gate size at each stage, potentially improving power and area while maintaining the original logical functionality. The interface remains unchanged.

I will implement this hierarchy inside a module named nand4_2level and instantiate two of them in TopModule.
