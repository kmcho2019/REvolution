The initial design uses straightforward logic expressions for two 4-input NAND gates. To optimize for better power and area, a good approach is to explicitly instantiate two 4-input NAND gate primitives if the synthesis library supports them. This removes the need to implement NAND gates via AND + NOT internally, allowing the synthesis tool to map directly to efficient NAND4 cells, which typically consume less power and area and offer better timing. Since the standard Verilog does not define a NAND4 primitive, we can create a simple nand4 gate module and instantiate it twice for clarity and synthesis friendliness. This modular approach can also improve readability and reusability.
