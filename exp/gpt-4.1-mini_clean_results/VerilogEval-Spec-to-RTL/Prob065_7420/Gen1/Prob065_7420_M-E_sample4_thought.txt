Instead of using a direct four-input NAND gate, we can implement each 4-input NAND as a two-stage NAND tree. First, NAND pairs of inputs to generate two intermediate signals, then NAND these intermediate signals to get the final output. This structure mimics how a 7420 might be internally constructed from smaller NAND gates, and can be more easily mapped to standard cell libraries that predominantly offer 2-input NAND gates. It also explicitly shows the hierarchical structure of the logic.
