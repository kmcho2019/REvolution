Instead of directly using the four-input NAND operation, implement each 4-input NAND gate using two 2-input NAND gates chained together with an inverter at the end to form the 4-input NAND functionality. This approach models how the 7420 might be constructed internally from smaller NAND gates. We first NAND pairs of inputs, then NAND their outputs, and finally invert to get the overall NAND of four inputs. This makes the design structurally different from the previous straightforward single-level implementation.
