The initial design is already functionally correct and minimal. However, to optimize for power and area, we can explicitly implement each 4-input NAND gate as a tree of 2-input NAND gates. This can help synthesis tools better map the design to standard cells (since many libraries have 2-input NAND gates as the base cell) and can reduce overall area and possibly power by creating balanced trees, improving performance as well.

We will instantiate internal wires for intermediate signals and build each NAND gate from 2-input NAND gates step-by-step:
- First, NAND the first two inputs
- NAND the last two inputs
- Then NAND the outputs of the above two NAND gates to get the 4-input NAND output

This structure explicitly shows the decomposition and guides synthesis to an optimized structure.

The critical path is still minimal, and the modular structure may also improve place-and-route outcomes.
