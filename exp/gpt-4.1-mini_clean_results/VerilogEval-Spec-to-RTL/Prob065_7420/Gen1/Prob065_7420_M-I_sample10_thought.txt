The current design already directly implements two independent 4-input NAND gates using simple combinational logic, which matches the 7420 chip behavior perfectly. To optimize for area and power slightly, we can use the built-in Verilog nand primitive for four inputs rather than using AND followed by a NOT. This allows synthesis tools to potentially map the design to dedicated NAND4 gates with better efficiency.

Using nand primitives clarifies intent to synthesis tools and might enable minor optimization in gate-level implementation, possibly reducing area and power a bit without changing logic depth or performance.
