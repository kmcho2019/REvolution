To create a new architectural approach, I will implement the TopModule without creating a separate nand4 module. Instead, I will directly implement both 4-input NAND gates using continuous assignments in the top module with straightforward boolean expressions. This reduces hierarchy and keeps all logic in one place. To add a slight twist, I will use the Verilog reduction AND operator (&) combined with bitwise negation to produce the NAND output, demonstrating a compact expression style. This approach simplifies the design, reduces module instantiations, and still models the exact behavior of the 7420 chip.
