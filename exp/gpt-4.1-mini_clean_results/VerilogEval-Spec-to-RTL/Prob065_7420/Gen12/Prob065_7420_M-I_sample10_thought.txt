The original design is already structurally optimal in terms of logic depth and clarity by using a dedicated nand4 module. However, to improve PPA (power, performance, area) slightly:

- Power and Area: We can implement the 4-input NAND gate directly in one assign statement without an extra module to reduce module overhead and possibly synthesis tool mapping overhead. This may slightly reduce area and improve power by simplifying hierarchy.
- Performance: The 4-input NAND is a single logical expression, so no performance gain is expected, but flattening hierarchy can sometimes aid synthesis in further optimization.
- Clarity: Though modularity is reduced, the TopModule remains straightforward with clear signal names.

Thus, I will remove the separate nand4 module and implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly in TopModule with simple assign statements. This should yield slightly better area and power due to hierarchy flattening and less net routing.

This approach balances minimal hierarchy for optimization and maintains clarity by clear port naming and no procedural code.
