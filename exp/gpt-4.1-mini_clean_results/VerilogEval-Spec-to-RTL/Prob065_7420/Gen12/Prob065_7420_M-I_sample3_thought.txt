The current design is functionally correct and modularly clean, with two separate 4-input NAND gates implemented in a single module. Given the simplicity of the function, opportunities for improving PPA at the RTL level are limited. However, we can slightly improve the area and power by removing the intermediate module and implementing the two 4-input NAND gates directly in TopModule using simple continuous assignments. This reduces one level of hierarchy, potentially lowering area and delay slightly. Also, using bitwise reduction NAND can improve synthesis efficiency if the synthesis tool supports it, by using built-in primitives or optimized gates. Overall, this minimal flattening reduces wiring and instantiation overhead and leads to a marginal PPA improvement without sacrificing clarity.
