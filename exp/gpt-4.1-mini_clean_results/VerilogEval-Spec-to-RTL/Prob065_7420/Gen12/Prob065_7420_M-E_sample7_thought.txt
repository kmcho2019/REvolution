Instead of creating a separate module for the 4-input NAND gate, I will implement the functionality directly inside the TopModule using continuous assignments. I will perform the NAND operation by first ANDing the four inputs and then inverting the result, explicitly showing the two-step logic. This eliminates module hierarchy, resulting in a flat, straightforward design. While this trades modularity for simplicity, it reduces instance overhead and clearly shows the internal logic of the 7420 chip's two NAND gates, making the design minimal and easy to understand at the gate level.
