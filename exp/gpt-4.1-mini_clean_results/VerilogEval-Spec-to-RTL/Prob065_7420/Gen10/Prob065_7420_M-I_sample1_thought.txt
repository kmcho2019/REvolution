To optimize for better power and area, I will remove the parameterized `NandChain` module and implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly using simple continuous assignments. This reduces module instantiation overhead, eliminates intermediate signals, and synthesizes into minimal combinational logic. The direct expression `~(a & b & c & d)` maps closely to the actual 7420 NAND gate hardware. This flattening approach also simplifies the code and can slightly improve timing (though timing is not critical here).
