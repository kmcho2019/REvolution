Instead of directly instantiating built-in NAND primitives, this approach explicitly creates a 4-input NAND gate using a combination of simpler 2-input NAND gates. This structural style breaks down each 4-input NAND into three 2-input NANDs: first, NAND pairs of inputs two-by-two, then NAND the outputs together, and finally NAND the result with itself to replicate the 4-input NAND behavior.

This demonstrates how the 7420 chip functionality can be built from fundamental 2-input NAND gates, which might reflect a more realistic gate-level implementation in cases where 4-input NAND primitives aren't available.

Advantages include:
- Illustrates structural decomposition of complex gates into smaller NAND gates.
- Keeps the module purely combinational.
- Still maps correctly to the specified inputs and outputs.
