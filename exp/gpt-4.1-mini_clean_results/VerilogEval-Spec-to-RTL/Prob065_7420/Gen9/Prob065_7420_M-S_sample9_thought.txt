To simplify the design, I will eliminate the separate nand4 module and implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly within TopModule using simple continuous assignments. This reduces module hierarchy and source code size while maintaining the exact same functionality of two independent 4-input NAND gates, just as in the 7420 chip. The direct use of bitwise operators in assignments is clear, minimal, and synthesis-friendly for this straightforward combinational logic.
