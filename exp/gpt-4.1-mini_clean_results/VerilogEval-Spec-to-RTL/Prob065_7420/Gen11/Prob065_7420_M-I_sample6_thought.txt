To improve the PPA (power, performance, area) of the original modular implementation, I will remove the separate nand4 module and instead directly implement the two 4-input NAND gates inline in the top module. This eliminates one module instantiation level, potentially reducing area and slight power overhead. Using built-in bitwise and negation operators ensures minimal logic with no added delay. This simplification should keep timing excellent, reduce area by removing module overhead, and marginally decrease power without affecting readability too much since the design is still straightforward.
