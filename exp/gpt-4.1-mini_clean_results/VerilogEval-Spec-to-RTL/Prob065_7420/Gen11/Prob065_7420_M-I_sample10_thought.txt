The existing solution is already clean, modular, and optimal from a timing and area perspective given the simplicity of the 7420 chip (two independent 4-input NAND gates). The reported metrics show zero timing slack (no timing issues), minimal area, and low power, indicating no major structural inefficiencies.

To optimize power slightly, I will:
- Reduce any possible glitching by ensuring the output is only driven by the NAND of the inputs directly, avoiding any unnecessary signals.
- Remove the dedicated nand4 module to reduce module instantiation overhead, folding the logic into continuous assignments in the top module. This can slightly reduce area and power by avoiding extra module boundaries and enable better synthesis optimization.

Though the nand4 module is good for clarity and reuse, the chip 7420 is simple enough that inlining the NAND expressions directly could yield a tiny improvement in power and area by avoiding extra module overhead.

Hence, my improvement strategy is:
- Inline the two 4-input NAND gates directly inside TopModule as continuous assignments.
- Keep clear naming and comments.
- This reduces hierarchy, slightly decreasing area and power, with no timing impact.

This approach preserves the exact functionality and timing, with a marginal PPA gain due to removing module overhead.
