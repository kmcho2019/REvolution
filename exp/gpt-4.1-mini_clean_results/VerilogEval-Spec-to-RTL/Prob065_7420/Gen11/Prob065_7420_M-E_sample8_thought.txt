I will implement the 7420 functionality using gate-level primitives without creating a separate nand4 module. Instead, I'll chain two 2-input NAND gates to create an effective 4-input NAND gate for each output, demonstrating a structural approach at gate level. Specifically, each 4-input NAND will be implemented as NAND of two NANDs: first, NAND the first two inputs, second, NAND the last two inputs, and then NAND the two results together. This method shows how the 4-input NAND can be decomposed from 2-input NAND gates, common in standard cell libraries. This approach is novel compared to direct 4-input NAND instantiation and reflects real hardware gate composition.
