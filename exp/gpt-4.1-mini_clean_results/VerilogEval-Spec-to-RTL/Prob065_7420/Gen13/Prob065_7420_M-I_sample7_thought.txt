The existing design cleanly and correctly models the 7420 chip with two independent 4-input NAND gates, using a dedicated nand4 module for clarity and maintainability. The design is already optimal in terms of performance and area because it is purely combinational and minimal in logic depth.

To improve power and area slightly, I can remove the separate nand4 module and instead implement the two 4-input NAND gates directly in TopModule with explicit gate-level expressions. This avoids the overhead of module instantiations, potentially reducing synthesis overhead and area. Also, explicitly coding the NAND logic with bitwise operators ensures synthesis tools generate optimized logic.

This approach sacrifices modularity for a small gain in area and power, which is acceptable here since the module is simple and the problem scope is limited to exactly two 4-input NAND gates.

In summary, the improvement strategy is:
- Inline the two 4-input NAND gates directly in TopModule.
- Use bitwise NAND expressions instead of module instantiation.
- This reduces hierarchy and may help synthesis tools optimize power and area slightly.
- Maintain concise comments and clean style for readability.

Functionality remains identical, with potential minor gains in PPA due to reduced module overhead.
