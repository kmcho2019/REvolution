Instead of directly implementing two separate 4-input NAND gates, I will build a hierarchical design that uses reusable 2-input NAND gate modules to construct each 4-input NAND gate. This approach mirrors how large gates can be composed from smaller universal gates, emphasizing modularity and structural clarity. Each 4-input NAND gate will be constructed using three 2-input NAND gates connected in stages, leveraging the NAND gate's functional completeness to achieve the desired 4-input NAND behavior. This also explicitly models how complex gates can be built from smaller primitives, which could aid educational or low-level synthesis processes.
