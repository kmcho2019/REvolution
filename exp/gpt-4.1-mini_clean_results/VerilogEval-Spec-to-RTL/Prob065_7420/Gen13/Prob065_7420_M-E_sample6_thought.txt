I will implement the TopModule by directly instantiating two separate 4-input NAND gates as structural gate-level primitives using Verilog's built-in nand gate primitive. Each NAND gate will be wired to the corresponding four inputs. This approach reflects the actual hardware structure of the 7420 IC more explicitly, avoiding any extra intermediate modules or logic, and represents a minimalistic, gate-level design which clearly models two independent 4-input NAND gates.
