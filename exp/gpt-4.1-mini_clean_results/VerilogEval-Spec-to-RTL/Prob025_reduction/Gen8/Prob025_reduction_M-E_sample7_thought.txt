Instead of using a direct XOR reduction operator, the parity can be computed by hierarchically XORing pairs of bits, thereby explicitly implementing a small XOR tree. This approach visually demonstrates the step-by-step aggregation of parity bits and might be instructive for educational purposes or specific synthesis flows that benefit from explicit intermediate signals.
