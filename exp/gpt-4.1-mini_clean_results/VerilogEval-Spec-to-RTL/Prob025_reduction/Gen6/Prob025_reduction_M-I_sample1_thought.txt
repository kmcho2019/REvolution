To optimize the given parity calculation for better performance, power, and area (PPA), we simplify the logic by directly applying the XOR reduction operator on the entire 8-bit input vector. This reduces the logic depth to a single gate level, eliminates intermediate wires, and minimizes switching activity. Consequently, the design will have lower combinational delay, reduced power consumption due to fewer toggling nets, and decreased area because of fewer gates and routing resources.

This approach retains the functional correctness (even parity), is concise and highly synthesizable, and improves critical path timing by flattening the XOR tree depth from two levels to one.
