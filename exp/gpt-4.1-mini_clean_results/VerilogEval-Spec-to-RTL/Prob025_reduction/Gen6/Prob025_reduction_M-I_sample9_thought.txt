The existing implementation uses a reduction XOR operator which is already the minimal and fastest way to compute even parity in RTL. Since the logic is a simple combinational XOR of all bits, further logical simplification is not possible. However, for slight PPA improvements, the parity can be computed hierarchically as a balanced XOR tree instead of a flat reduction, which can sometimes lead to better logic depth and area after synthesis. This might reduce the critical path delay and balance switching activity slightly, potentially improving power and timing in some synthesis flows. Therefore, the improvement is to explicitly structure the XOR as a balanced tree of 2-input XORs rather than rely on the flat reduction operator.
