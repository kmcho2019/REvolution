Refactor the design by removing the recursive XOR tree module and the HalfXor submodule, and instead compute the even parity directly using a single Verilog reduction XOR operator. This operator synthesizes efficiently to a balanced XOR tree by synthesis tools. The approach maintains the same functionality but simplifies the code structure, reduces module instantiations, and enhances readability and synthesis efficiency. The TopModule directly assigns the parity bit as the XOR reduction of the input bits, implementing even parity in a concise and synthesis-friendly manner.
