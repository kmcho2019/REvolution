The previous design used a hierarchical XOR tree built from multiple HalfXor modules to explicitly show the parity calculation structure. While clear, this approach introduces extra module overhead and wiring that may impact power and area negatively.

To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the hierarchical HalfXor module instantiations with a single built-in Verilog XOR reduction operator (^). This operator synthesizes into an optimized XOR tree internally by synthesis tools, minimizing logic depth and net count. It reduces module instantiation overhead and wiring complexity, improving area and power metrics without sacrificing functionality.

This change yields:
- Simplified and more concise code.
- Potentially faster timing due to optimized logic synthesis of XOR reduction.
- Lower power through reduced switching activity and shorter net routes.
- Smaller area by removing multiple module instantiations.

The interface remains the same, so this is a drop-in replacement maintaining the original behavior (even parity calculation on 8-bit input).
