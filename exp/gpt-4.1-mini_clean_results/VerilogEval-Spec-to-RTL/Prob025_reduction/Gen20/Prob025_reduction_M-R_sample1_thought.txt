To refactor the parity computation while keeping the core idea of hierarchical XOR combination, I will replace the explicit HalfXor module instances and balanced XOR tree with a straightforward assign statement that performs a reduction XOR on all input bits. This keeps the parity calculation logic simple, removes the need for intermediate wires and generate blocks, and eliminates the HalfXor module. 

This refactoring uses Verilog's built-in reduction XOR operator (^) to directly compute even parity as the XOR of all bits in 'in'. The design stays combinational and concise, improving readability and synthesis efficiency without changing the functionality.

This approach preserves correctness (even parity), is succinct, easier to maintain, and is likely better optimized by synthesis tools for performance, power, and area, as the hierarchical XOR tree is now implicit in the hardware implementation synthesized for the reduction operator.
