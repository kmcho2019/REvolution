To optimize for better PPA while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will simplify the design by removing the extra module instantiation of XorTree and directly using the built-in reduction XOR operator in the TopModule. This removes the slight overhead of an additional module, potentially reducing area and power consumption with no timing penalty. The direct assign statement is synthesizable and efficient, and synthesis tools will still implement a balanced XOR tree. The solution remains fully modular for this fixed 8-bit input width and is easier to read and maintain.
