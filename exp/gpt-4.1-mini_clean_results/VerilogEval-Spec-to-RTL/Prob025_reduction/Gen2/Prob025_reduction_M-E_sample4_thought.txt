Instead of using a reduction XOR operator, I will compute even parity by summing all input bits and checking the least significant bit (LSB) of the sum. Since the sum counts how many '1's are in the input, the parity bit can be derived as the inverted LSB of the sum: if the count of '1's is odd (LSB=1), the parity bit is 1 to make the total even; if even (LSB=0), parity bit is 0. This approach shows an alternate arithmetic method to determine parity.
