Instead of using a straightforward XOR reduction, I will implement the parity computation using a balanced binary tree of XOR gates explicitly defined in structural style.

This approach will:
- Show a clear hierarchical XOR computation path from the 8 input bits up to a single parity bit.
- Reflect a real hardware structure with multiple levels of XORs rather than relying on the reduction operator.
- Help illustrate how a balanced XOR tree reduces the logic depth and evenly distributes the XOR operations.
- Avoid procedural or generate loops for clarity by explicitly wiring intermediate signals.
- This architectural style also makes the gate-level structure more visible and can assist in manual optimization or mapping.

The module TopModule will contain the explicit XOR gates connecting pairs of bits step by step until the final parity is computed.
