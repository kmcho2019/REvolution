The simplest and most efficient way to calculate even parity over an 8-bit input is to use Verilog's built-in reduction XOR operator `^`. This operator XORs all bits of a vector automatically and synthesizes to an optimized XOR tree internally in hardware.

By using this operator directly in the TopModule, we eliminate the need for extra modules and recursive structures, greatly simplifying the code, reducing area and power consumption, and improving readability without affecting functionality.

This approach keeps the design fully synthesizable and easy to understand, ideal for practical parity computation.
