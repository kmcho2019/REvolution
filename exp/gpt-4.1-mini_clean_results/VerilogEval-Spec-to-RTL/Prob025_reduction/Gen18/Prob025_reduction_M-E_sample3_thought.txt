Implement the TopModule to calculate even parity by using a single continuous XOR reduction operator on the 8-bit input vector. This approach directly computes parity as the XOR of all bits in a single expression, avoiding intermediate wires or additional modules. 

This method is succinct, highly readable, and synthesizes to minimal hardware, optimizing area and power. It leverages built-in Verilog operators for clarity and efficiency, with zero logic levels beyond the minimum necessary XOR tree that the synthesis tool decides.

Thus, the design is purely combinational, fully synthesizable, and represents a clean, flat approach to parity generation.
