Implement the parity calculation using a balanced XOR tree made of explicit 2-input XOR gates, manually instantiated to form a hierarchy. This approach explicitly shows how to build an XOR reduction using basic gates, emphasizing structural design and clarity on how parity is constructed in hardware without relying on the reduction operator. This can be helpful for educational purposes, illustrating the hierarchical XOR structure and showing that parity can be computed by successive pairwise XORs.

The TopModule wires the input bits into this XOR tree step by step:

- First, pairwise XOR the 8 input bits to get 4 intermediate signals.
- Then XOR those 4 signals into 2 signals.
- Finally XOR those 2 signals to get the parity bit.

This approach uses no parameterization, no reduction operators, and clearly shows the signal flow in a simple hierarchy of XOR gates.
