Implement even parity by explicitly cascading XOR gates in a balanced tree structure without using the reduction XOR operator. First, pair adjacent bits and XOR them to produce four intermediate signals; then XOR pairs of these intermediates to get two signals, and finally XOR those two to produce the parity bit. This structural approach makes the XOR tree depth explicit, potentially improving clarity for synthesis tools that prefer explicit gate instantiations, while also serving educational purposes by illustrating parity calculation as a binary tree of XOR operations.
