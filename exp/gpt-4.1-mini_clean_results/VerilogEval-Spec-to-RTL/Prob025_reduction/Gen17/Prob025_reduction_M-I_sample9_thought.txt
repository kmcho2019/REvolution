The existing design is already efficient, modular, and clear by using a parameterized XOR tree module with a reduction XOR operator. However, for an 8-bit fixed-width input, the additional module instantiation of XorTree can be removed to reduce area and power slightly by eliminating the module overhead. Using a direct assign with the reduction XOR operator in the TopModule leads to minimal combinational logic and the fewest logic levels. This will improve area and power slightly without sacrificing clarity or performance. This simplification is justified because the fixed input width (8 bits) does not require the scalability of a parameterized module.

Improvement strategy:  
- Remove XorTree module instantiation.  
- Implement parity directly in TopModule via a single reduction XOR assign.  
- Keep the design combinational and succinct.  

This will reduce area and power marginally while maintaining zero delay and perfect timing.
