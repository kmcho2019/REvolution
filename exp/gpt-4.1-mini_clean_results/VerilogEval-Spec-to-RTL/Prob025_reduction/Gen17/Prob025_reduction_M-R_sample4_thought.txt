Refactor the parity calculation by replacing the hierarchical XOR tree of HalfXor module instances with a straightforward continuous assignment using Verilog's built-in reduction XOR operator (^). This eliminates all intermediate wires and module instantiations, leading to a simpler, more concise, and synthesizable implementation that still produces the same even parity output. This flat structure reduces netlist complexity and switching activity, improving area and power without changing timing or functionality.
