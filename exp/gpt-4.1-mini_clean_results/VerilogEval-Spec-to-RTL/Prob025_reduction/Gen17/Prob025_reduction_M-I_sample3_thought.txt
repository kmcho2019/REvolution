The original solution uses a hierarchical tree of HalfXor modules to compute parity, which is clear but introduces extra wires and modules, leading to slightly higher power and area. To optimize for power and area without sacrificing correctness or timing, we can replace the entire XOR tree with a single Verilog reduction XOR operator (^) that computes parity of all bits in one step. This removes intermediate nets and module instantiations, reducing netlist complexity and switching activity, resulting in better PPA metrics. Functionally, this is identical and synthesis tools optimize this efficiently.
