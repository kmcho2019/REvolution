I will implement the parity calculation using a simple combinational logic approach by explicitly writing out the XOR tree in a flat, wire-based manner inside the TopModule. This method manually creates pairs of XOR gates step-by-step to reduce the 8 bits to one parity bit, without any hierarchy or separate modules. This will provide a concise, straightforward circuit that is easy to understand and synthesize, and it exposes the XOR structure clearly in the RTL. This approach differs from recursive or built-in reduction XOR by explicitly showing each XOR operation stage in combinational logic with intermediate wires. It strikes a balance between clarity and minimalism, avoids recursive module instantiations, and does not rely on built-in operators.

Advantages:
- Explicit and educational XOR tree showing each step.
- No extra module overhead for recursion or hierarchy.
- Clear, flat logic suitable for straightforward synthesis.
- Slightly better visibility into gate-level structure than a one-liner reduction.
