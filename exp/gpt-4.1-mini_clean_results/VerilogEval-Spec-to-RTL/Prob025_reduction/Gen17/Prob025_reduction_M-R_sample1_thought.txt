Refactor the parity generation by replacing the hierarchical HalfXor module instantiations with a direct combinational assignment using the Verilog reduction XOR operator (^). This operator naturally computes the XOR of all bits in a vector, producing the even parity bit directly. This simplification removes the need for intermediate wires and module instances, reducing area and potentially power, while maintaining the same functional behavior in a single-line combinational expression. The design remains purely combinational and clear, leveraging built-in language features for succinctness and synthesis efficiency.
