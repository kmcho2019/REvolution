To optimize the design for better power and area without sacrificing timing or functionality, I will replace the hierarchical HalfXor module structure with a simple built-in Verilog reduction XOR operator. This reduces the gate count, wiring overhead, and switching activity by flattening the logic into a single-level expression. The even parity bit is then computed simply as the XOR of all 8 bits in the input vector. This change improves power and area metrics while keeping the code clean and synthesizable.
