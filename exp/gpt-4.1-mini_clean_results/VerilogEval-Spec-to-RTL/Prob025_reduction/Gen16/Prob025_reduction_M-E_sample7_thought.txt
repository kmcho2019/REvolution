I will implement the TopModule to compute even parity by cascading simple 2-input XOR gates in a balanced binary tree structure. This explicit gate-level approach replaces the built-in reduction XOR operator with clear hierarchy and structural clarity, suitable for hardware mapping and educational purposes.

The parity is computed as the XOR of all eight input bits, grouped as pairs and combined step-by-step:
- Level 1: XOR pairs of adjacent bits (4 outputs)
- Level 2: XOR pairs of Level 1 outputs (2 outputs)
- Level 3: XOR the two Level 2 outputs to get the final parity

This approach:
- Avoids using built-in reduction operators
- Is easy to scale and modify for different input widths
- Makes the XOR tree structure explicitly visible in the RTL code
- Provides deterministic gate-level netlist for synthesis tools

I'll also provide a concise testbench that verifies correctness for a few representative inputs.
