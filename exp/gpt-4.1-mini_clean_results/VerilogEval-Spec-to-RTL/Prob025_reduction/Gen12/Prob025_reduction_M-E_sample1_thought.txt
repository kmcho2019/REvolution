Compute the even parity bit by using a combinational priority-based conditional XOR accumulation with a generate-for loop. Instead of building a balanced XOR tree, iterate through each bit in a procedural block and sequentially XOR it into a parity register. This method uses a single always_comb block to accumulate parity in a simple and clear way. It demonstrates the use of behavioral constructs in Verilog for combinational logic, which might aid readability and synthesis tools that support such coding style. The final parity output is assigned from the accumulated parity value.
