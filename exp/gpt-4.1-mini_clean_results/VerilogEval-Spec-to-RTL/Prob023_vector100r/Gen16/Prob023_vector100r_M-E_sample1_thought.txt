Implement the bit reversal by using a simple combinational for-generate loop that directly maps each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit index. This flat approach avoids recursion and hierarchical instantiations. It improves synthesis efficiency by creating a straightforward bitwise assignment from output[i] = input[width-1 - i]. This method is fully combinational, concise, and scales easily for any vector width. TopModule simply instantiates this logic without additional hierarchy.
