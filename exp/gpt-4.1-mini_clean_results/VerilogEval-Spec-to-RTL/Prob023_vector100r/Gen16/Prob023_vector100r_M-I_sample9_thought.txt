The previous hierarchical approach with 10 BitRev10 instances introduced extra nets and module boundaries, which can increase area and power due to routing overhead.

To optimize for better PPA, I will flatten the design by directly reversing all 100 bits in a single generate loop. This eliminates intermediate wiring, array indexing, and sub-module instantiations, reducing logic duplication and routing complexity.

The solution is straightforward: assign each output bit out[i] to input bit in[99 - i] in a single combinational generate loop. This maintains full functionality, reduces area and power, and preserves zero-delay combinational behavior.

This flattening simplifies the RTL, reduces nets and modules, and yields better synthesis results for PPA without sacrificing readability or correctness.
