The existing hierarchical solution correctly reverses the 100-bit input vector using multiple small modules, but this introduces additional logic depth, routing complexity, and potential power overhead.

To optimize for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will flatten the design by implementing the full 100-bit reversal with a single continuous assign statement. This approach eliminates intermediate wiring and module instantiations, reduces logic duplication, and allows synthesis tools to optimize the reversal efficiently.

The single assignment will directly map each output bit to its corresponding reversed input bit, i.e., out[i] = in[99 - i], for i in 0 to 99.

This simple, combinational assignment reduces area and switching activity, likely improves timing by reducing logic depth, and simplifies the design for better power efficiency.
