To optimize the original recursive hierarchical approach for better PPA, replace the recursive module instantiations with a simple flat, combinational assignment using a generate-for loop. This approach directly assigns each output bit to the corresponding input bit at the reversed index, eliminating multiple hierarchical module instances and intermediate signals.

Benefits include:
- Lower area by removing recursive module overhead.
- Reduced power by minimizing switching activity on intermediate signals.
- Improved timing due to shorter combinational paths and less logic depth.

This solution is fully combinational, synthesis-friendly, and easily scalable to any vector width without creating deep module hierarchies.
