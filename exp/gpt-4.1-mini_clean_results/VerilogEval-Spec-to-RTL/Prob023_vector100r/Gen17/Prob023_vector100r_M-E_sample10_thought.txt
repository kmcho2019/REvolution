Implement a single flat combinational module that directly assigns each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit using a generate loop.

This straightforward approach:
- Avoids submodules and hierarchical complexity.
- Uses a single generate loop for bit reversal over all 100 bits.
- Minimizes wiring by one-to-one bit assignments without chunking.
- Easier to understand and maintain with minimal code.
- May synthesize efficiently as a simple wire reordering without overhead.
