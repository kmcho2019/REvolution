Implement a fully combinational bit reversal for the entire 100-bit vector in one step without hierarchical modules or functions.

Specifically:
- Use a generate-for loop inside the TopModule to assign each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit directly.
- This approach produces purely wiring-based reversal, eliminating any combinational logic and minimizing area and power.
- The single-module design improves clarity and synthesis friendliness by removing unnecessary module instantiations.
- The generate loop provides scalable and concise code, easy to maintain and extend.
- This design is optimal for PPA as it achieves bit reversal with zero delay and minimal resource usage.
