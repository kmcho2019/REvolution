Implement the 100-bit reversal using a fully flat, combinational, bitwise assignment without any intermediate modules or wires.

Key points:
- Since the input and output are fixed width 100 bits, directly assign each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit using a single generate loop.
- This approach minimizes hierarchy, reduces wire and module overhead, and can be synthesized into a simple wire swap network.
- The design is highly readable and efficient in terms of area and power due to no instantiation overhead.
- The generate block ensures scalability and clarity in the code, even if the width changes in future.
