To optimize the original modular design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we can flatten the hierarchy by eliminating the parameterized BitRevN module and avoiding multiple module instantiations. Instead, implement a single generate loop inside TopModule that directly reverses the 100-bit input by connecting each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit. This removes intermediate wiring and module overhead, reducing area and potential routing complexity, and slightly improving power and performance due to simpler logic and fewer module boundaries. The fully combinational reversal is straightforward, efficient, and easy for synthesis tools to optimize.
