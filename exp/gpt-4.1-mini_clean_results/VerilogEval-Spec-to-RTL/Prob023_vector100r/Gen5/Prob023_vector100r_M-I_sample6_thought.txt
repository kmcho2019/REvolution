To improve PPA, especially performance and clarity for synthesis, replace the function-based bit reversal with an explicit generate-for loop that directly connects each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit. This structural wiring approach makes the intent of bit reversal explicit and allows synthesis tools to optimize routing better, avoiding potential long combinational chains or inferred logic from function calls. 

The module BitRevN remains parameterized and reusable. The TopModule instantiates BitRevN with N=100 as required. This modification should yield better or equivalent timing and area by explicitly describing bitwise connections and reduce any ambiguity for synthesis, while maintaining zero latency and minimal power.
