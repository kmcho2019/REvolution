Implement a fully combinational hierarchical approach that divides the 100-bit input into ten 10-bit chunks, reverses each chunk independently using a small parameterized BitRev module, then concatenates these reversed chunks in reverse order. 

This "divide and conquer" strategy:
- Breaks the problem into smaller fixed-size reversals (10 bits each).
- Reuses a compact, parameterized BitRev10 module optimized with a simple generate loop to reverse 10 bits.
- Assembles the final output by concatenating reversed chunks in reverse order to achieve full 100-bit reversal.
- This modular hierarchy improves clarity, reuse, scalability, and potentially enables better synthesis optimization than flat concatenation or one monolithic reversal.
- It also avoids a large generate loop of 100 bits, instead using smaller loops and straightforward concatenation for less wiring congestion and potentially improved power and area.
