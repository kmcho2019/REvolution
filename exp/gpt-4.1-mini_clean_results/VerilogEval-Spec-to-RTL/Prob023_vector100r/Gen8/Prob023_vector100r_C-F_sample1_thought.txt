Fuse the modular hierarchical chunking approach (from Example 1) with the flexible parameterized BitRevN module (from Example 2) by implementing a parameterized BitRevN module and using it to reverse fixed-size chunks. This fusion combines the strengths:
- Using a parameterized BitRevN (with chunk size 10) allows reusability and clean chunk reversal.
- Chunk reversal reduces wiring congestion and enables localized synthesis optimizations.
- Concatenating the reversed chunks in reverse order ensures a correct full 100-bit reversal.
- The modular hierarchical structure improves clarity, scalability, and potentially power by limiting the fanout and switching to smaller chunks.
- Using a parameterized BitRevN makes the chunk reversal submodule reusable for other chunk sizes or different projects.
- The top-level concatenation order is corrected (concatenation of chunks from highest to lowest index) to avoid confusion and precisely implement the bit reversal.
- This approach balances flat wiring simplicity with modular hierarchy and maintains clean and readable code.

Hence, it improves maintainability and potentially power/area through hierarchical chunking, while keeping parameterized, reusable submodules and clean, clear concatenation logic.
