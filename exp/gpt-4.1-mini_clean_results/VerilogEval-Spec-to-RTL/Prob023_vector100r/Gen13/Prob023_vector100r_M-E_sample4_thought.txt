Implement the bit reversal using a generate loop with a for-generate block that explicitly assigns each bit of the output to the corresponding reversed bit of the input. This systematic approach creates clean, indexed assignments for each bit and enables the synthesis tool to optimize wiring and resource usage more effectively. It also improves readability and maintainability since the reversal is clearly expressed as out[i] = in[N-1 - i]. The design remains fully combinational and simple.
