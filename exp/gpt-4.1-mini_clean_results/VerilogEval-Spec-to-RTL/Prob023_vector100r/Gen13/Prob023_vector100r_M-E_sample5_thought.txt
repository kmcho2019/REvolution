Implement a flat, fully combinational approach without hierarchical modules by directly assigning each output bit as the input bit from the reversed index. 

This straightforward method:
- Uses a single generate loop to wire each output bit to the corresponding reversed input bit.
- Eliminates submodules and chunking for minimal structural overhead.
- Enhances clarity and reduces module instantiation overhead, which may improve synthesis results.
- Enables straightforward scalability and direct bitwise reversal without intermediate data structures.
- Is very efficient in area and power as it involves simple wire connections with no logic gates beyond rewiring.

This approach trades modularity for simplicity and directness, making it easy to verify and maintain.
