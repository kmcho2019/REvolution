Implement a fully combinational flat approach without hierarchical modules, directly assigning each output bit as the corresponding reversed input bit.

Specifically:
- Use a single generate loop from 0 to 99.
- For each output bit i, assign out[i] = in[99 - i].
  
This approach is the simplest and most direct solution:
- No submodule instantiations needed.
- Minimal wiring complexity with straightforward indexing.
- Likely the most area- and performance-efficient as there is no hierarchy or intermediate signals.
- Easy to understand and verify.
- Suitable for synthesis tools to optimize as a simple wire permutation.

This also allows better control and reduces code size, making the bit reversal logic explicit and concise.
