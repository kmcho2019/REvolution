Implement a hierarchical bit-reversal by dividing the 100-bit input into 10 chunks of 10 bits each, reversing each chunk internally using a dedicated BitRev10 module, then reversing the order of these chunks at the TopModule level to produce the final 100-bit reversed output. 

This approach leverages hierarchical composition:
- BitRev10 reverses the bits inside each 10-bit chunk using direct wiring.
- TopModule reverses the order of chunks, effectively flipping chunks in the higher dimension.
- Combining chunk-level bit reversal with chunk order reversal achieves a full 100-bit reversal.
  
Advantages:
- Smaller, reusable building block BitRev10 is simpler and can be easily tested and optimized.
- The top module rearranges chunks with simple wiring, reducing long-distance connections.
- This hierarchical design might improve routability and timing since bit reversal is localized.
- The code remains clear and parameterizable at the chunk level.
- This approach is novel compared to direct flat reversal, emphasizing modularity at two hierarchy levels.
