Implement a fully combinational one-level design using a generate-for loop that directly reverses the 100-bit input without intermediate chunking or hierarchy.

This approach uses a single generate loop within TopModule to assign each bit of the output as the reversed bit from input, i.e., out[i] = in[99 - i]. 

This design is minimal, flat, and straightforward. It avoids any intermediate wires or module instantiations, reducing hierarchy, potentially improving synthesis runtime and power due to less routing complexity and no intermediate signals.

Because the input and output widths are fixed, no parameterization is necessary, simplifying the design further.

This approach optimizes for area and power by minimizing logic and wiring overhead, while maintaining clarity and ease of verification due to its straightforward mapping.

The purely combinational continuous assignment ensures the output is always the reversed input, with no latency or state elements.
