Implement a fully combinational bit reversal by leveraging a single generate loop that directly assigns each output bit from the corresponding reversed input bit index. This approach avoids any intermediate segmentation or module instantiations, resulting in minimal logic, reduced area, and power.

The design simply maps out[bit_index] = in[total_width - 1 - bit_index] for all bits from 0 to 99.

This flat, direct wiring method is the simplest and most efficient way to reverse bit ordering for a fixed-width vector and is highly synthesis-friendly, producing purely wire routing without additional combinational logic.
