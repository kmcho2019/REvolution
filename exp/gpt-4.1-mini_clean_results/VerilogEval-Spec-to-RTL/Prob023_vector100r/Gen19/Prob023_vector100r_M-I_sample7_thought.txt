The original design is clean and modular but can be optimized for area and power by removing hierarchical module instantiations and intermediate signals. Since bit reversal is purely a fixed wiring rearrangement, directly assigning the output bits as the input bits in reverse order with a single continuous assignment will minimize logic, routing, and switching.

This flat approach:
- Eliminates the BitRevN module and function call overhead.
- Avoids segmentation and intermediate arrays, reducing net switching and routing congestion.
- Reduces critical path to a single wire assignment layer, improving timing.
- Minimizes area by relying solely on wiring rearrangement, which synthesis tools optimize efficiently.

Thus, a straightforward continuous assignment with a generate loop reversing the bits directly is the most optimal solution for PPA while maintaining full correctness.
