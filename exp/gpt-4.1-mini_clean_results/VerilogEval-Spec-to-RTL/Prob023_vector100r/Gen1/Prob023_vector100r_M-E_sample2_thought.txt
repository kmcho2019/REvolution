Implement TopModule by splitting the 100-bit input into 10 chunks of 10 bits each, reversing the order of these chunks, and then individually reversing the bits inside each chunk using a small submodule. This hierarchical reversal leverages modular design and keeps the code organized. The submodule BitRev10 reverses 10-bit vectors. TopModule instantiates 10 instances of BitRev10 and arranges their outputs in reverse order to produce the full 100-bit reversed output.
