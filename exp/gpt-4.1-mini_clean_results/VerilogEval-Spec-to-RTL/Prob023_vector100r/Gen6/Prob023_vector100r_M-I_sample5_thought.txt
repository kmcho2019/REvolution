To improve PPA, replace the function-based reversal with an explicit generate-for loop to create a clear one-to-one bit mapping. This structural approach makes the reversal operation explicit and easily recognized by synthesis tools, potentially optimizing routing and timing paths better than an implicit function. It also avoids the possibility of the function being interpreted as a sequential loop, thus reducing any unintended long combinational paths.

The generate loop instantiates bit assignments in parallel, which can improve performance and possibly reduce power by minimizing logic depth and switching activity spread. The area will remain similar, as the operation is purely wiring.

This approach is a good balance between succinctness, clarity, and synthesis friendliness, likely yielding better PPA.
