- Keep a register `state` and also a `prev_state` to detect state transitions.
- On the rising edge of the clock, detect when state changes to COUNT.
- On entering COUNT, initialize `block_cnt` to `delay + 1` and `cycle_cnt` to 0.
- Then implement counting logic properly: increment cycle_cnt each clock, and when it reaches 999, reset to 0 and decrement block_cnt.
- Update `count` output accordingly.
- Keep the other logic mostly unchanged.

This correction ensures proper initialization and counting behavior.
