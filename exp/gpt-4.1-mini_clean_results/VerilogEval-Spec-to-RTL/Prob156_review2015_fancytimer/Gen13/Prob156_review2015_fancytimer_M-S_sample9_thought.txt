Use a simple FSM with four states: SEARCH, LOAD_DELAY, COUNTING, DONE.

- SEARCH: shift in data bits each clock into a 4-bit shift register; when pattern 1101 is detected, transition to LOAD_DELAY.
- LOAD_DELAY: shift in 4 delay bits MSB first into delay register over 4 cycles, then transition to COUNTING.
- COUNTING: count a fixed number of cycles calculated from delay: total cycles = (delay + 1) * 1000.
  Use a single cycle counter counting down from total cycles - 1 to 0.
  Output `count` shows the current remaining block (delay blocks of 1000 cycles). E.g., delay=5 means 6 blocks; when in block 6, output 5, when in block 5, output 4, ..., block 1 output 0.
- DONE: assert done=1, wait for ack=1 then go back to SEARCH.

Only shift and sample data in SEARCH and LOAD_DELAY states. Synchronous reset initializes everything. This approach reduces complexity by using a single cycle counter and avoids complicated multiple counters and race conditions.
