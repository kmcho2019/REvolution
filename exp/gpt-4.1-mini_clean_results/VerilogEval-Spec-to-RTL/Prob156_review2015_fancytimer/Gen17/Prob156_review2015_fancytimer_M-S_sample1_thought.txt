Implement a straightforward FSM with four states: SEARCH for pattern 1101, LOAD_DELAY to serially load 4 delay bits MSB first, COUNT to count (delay+1)*1000 cycles, and WAIT_ACK to signal done and wait for acknowledgment.

Use a 4-bit shift register shifting left, inserting new data at LSB, to detect 1101 pattern in SEARCH. After detecting pattern, in LOAD_DELAY shift in 4 delay bits MSB first similarly.

In COUNT, use a 10-bit cycle counter counting down from 999 to 0, and a 4-bit segment counter counting down from (delay+1) to 0. The output count reflects the current segment number counting down every 1000 cycles.

Upon completion, assert done and wait in WAIT_ACK for ack signal to return to SEARCH.

All synchronous logic in one always block triggered by clk and synchronous reset.

This approach removes unnecessary complexity, explicitly separates state behaviors, and cleanly initializes counters upon entering COUNT, ensuring correct timing and outputs.
