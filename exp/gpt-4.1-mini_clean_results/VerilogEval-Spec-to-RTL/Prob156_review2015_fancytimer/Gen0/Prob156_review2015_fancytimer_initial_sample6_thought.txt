Implement a synchronous FSM with these main states: SEARCH for the start pattern (1101), SHIFT_DELAY to capture 4 delay bits MSB first, COUNT to count down the timer, and DONE to indicate completion and wait for ack. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern during SEARCH. In SHIFT_DELAY, shift in 4 bits to form delay[3:0]. In COUNT, maintain a 1000-cycle sub-counter and a delay counter to determine remaining time. When done, assert done and wait for ack before returning to SEARCH. Use synchronous reset.
