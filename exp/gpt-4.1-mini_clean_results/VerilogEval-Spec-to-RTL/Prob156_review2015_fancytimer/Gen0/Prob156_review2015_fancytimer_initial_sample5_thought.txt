The design implements a finite state machine with these main states: SEARCH (looking for the pattern 1101), LOAD_DELAY (shifting in 4 delay bits MSB first), COUNTING (counting down the timer), DONE (asserting done and waiting for ack). 

A 4-bit shift register keeps the last 4 bits to detect the pattern 1101. Once detected, transition to LOAD_DELAY to shift in 4 delay bits MSB first. Then transition to COUNTING, where a cycle counter counts down total clock cycles ((delay+1)*1000), and a 4-bit "count" outputs the current remaining delay value. When done, assert done signal and wait for ack to restart the sequence.

The synchronous reset forces the FSM into SEARCH state and clears relevant registers.

All sequential logic triggers on posedge clk and synchronous reset.

The design will output:
- count[3:0]: remaining time steps (delay countdown)
- counting: high during counting state
- done: high when timer completes and ack not yet asserted
