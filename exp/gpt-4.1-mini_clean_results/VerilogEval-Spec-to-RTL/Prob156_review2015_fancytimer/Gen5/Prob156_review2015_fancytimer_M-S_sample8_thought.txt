Implement a single synchronous FSM with four states: IDLE (search for pattern 1101), DELAY_LOAD (shift in 4 delay bits MSB first), COUNTING (count (delay+1)*1000 cycles), and DONE (assert done, wait for ack).

Use a 4-bit shift register for pattern detection in IDLE. Once detected, move to DELAY_LOAD and shift in 4 bits to delay_reg.

In COUNTING, use a 10-bit micro_counter counting 0..999 and a 4-bit step_counter counting down delay steps from delay_reg to 0. Each step represents 1000 cycles. The count output shows the current step remaining.

When counting finishes, assert done and wait for ack to return to IDLE.

All registers update synchronously in one always block. No multi-driver conflicts.

Outputs: counting asserted during COUNTING; done asserted during DONE; count shows current step remaining only in COUNTING, else don't-care (zero).

This approach is clean, minimal, and meets all requirements.
