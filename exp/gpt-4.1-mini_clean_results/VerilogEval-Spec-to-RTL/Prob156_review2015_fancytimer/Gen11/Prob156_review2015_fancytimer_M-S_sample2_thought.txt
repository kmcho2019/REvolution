I will implement a clean FSM with these states: SEARCH (scan input for pattern 1101), LOAD_DELAY (shift in 4 delay bits MSB first), COUNTING (count cycles), and DONE (assert done and wait for ack).

- In SEARCH, maintain a 4-bit shift register updated each clock cycle. When it matches 1101, transition to LOAD_DELAY.
- In LOAD_DELAY, shift in 4 bits of delay MSB first into delay_reg, counting bits loaded with a 3-bit counter.
- On completing LOAD_DELAY, initialize counting registers:
  - block_count = delay + 1 (number of 1000-cycle blocks)
  - cycle_count = 0 (cycles within current block)
- In COUNTING:
  - Each clock cycle increment cycle_count.
  - When cycle_count reaches 999, decrement block_count and reset cycle_count.
  - Output `count` = block_count - 1 to represent remaining blocks.
- When block_count reaches zero and cycle_count hits 999, transition to DONE.
- In DONE, assert done, keep counting low, and wait for ack to return to SEARCH.
- Outputs:
  - When counting, counting=1 and count shows remaining blocks minus one.
  - Otherwise, counting=0 and count=0 (don't-care).
- All registers update synchronously on posedge clk.
- Reset synchronously resets FSM and all registers.

This single always block FSM with local counters and controlled updates will simplify the design and avoid races.
