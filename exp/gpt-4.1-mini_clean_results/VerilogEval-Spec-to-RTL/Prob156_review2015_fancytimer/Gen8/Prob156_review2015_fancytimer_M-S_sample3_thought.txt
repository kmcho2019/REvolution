Use a simple FSM with four states: SEARCH for the pattern 1101, LOAD to shift in 4 delay bits MSB first, COUNT to count delay+1 cycles of 1000 clocks each, and WAIT_ACK to wait for acknowledgment.

In SEARCH, shift in data bits to detect 1101. Upon detection, move to LOAD.

In LOAD, shift in 4 bits MSB first into delay register. After loading 4 bits, initialize cycle counters and move to COUNT.

In COUNT, use a 12-bit cycle counter to count to 999. Once cycle_counter reaches 999, decrement a tick counter initialized to delay+1. The output count shows the remaining ticks minus one (stable for 1000 clocks). When ticks reach zero, assert done and move to WAIT_ACK.

In WAIT_ACK, wait for ack to return to SEARCH.

Keep outputs stable by updating them only on clock edges. Reset is synchronous active high.

This approach is straightforward, clearly separates phases, and meets all requirements with minimal state and counters.
