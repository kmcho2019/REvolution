Use a straightforward FSM with four states encoded as simple parameters. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101 in SEARCH state. Once detected, shift in 4 delay bits MSB first by shifting delay_reg right and inserting new data at MSB during LOAD_DELAY. During COUNTING, use a 10-bit cycle counter to count 1000 cycles, decrement a 5-bit tick counter initialized to delay+1 at each 1000 cycles. Output count as the current tick value (remaining delay) during counting; otherwise, count is don't-care. Assert counting signal during counting and done signal after counting finishes, waiting for ack before returning to SEARCH. Reset synchronously initializes all registers and outputs.
