Use a simple 5-state FSM: SEARCH for pattern 1101, LOAD delay bits MSB-first, INIT_COUNT (initialize counters), COUNT (count down), WAIT_ACK.

In SEARCH, shift in bits to detect 1101 pattern on 'data'.

In LOAD, shift in next 4 bits into delay_reg MSB-first.

On entering INIT_COUNT, set tick_counter = delay_reg + 1, cycle_counter = 0, and count = delay_reg.

In COUNT, increment cycle_counter each clock; when it reaches 999, reset it and decrement tick_counter and count until tick_counter reaches 0.

Then assert done, deassert counting, and go to WAIT_ACK.

In WAIT_ACK, wait for ack=1, then return to SEARCH.

Keep outputs stable as per the requirements; count is valid only during COUNT.

Reset synchronous, active high, clears state and all regs.

This approach cleanly separates initialization from counting phase, avoids tricky if(state!=COUNT) conditions, and is easy to verify and maintain.
