Implement a clear synchronous FSM with four states: SEARCH, LOAD_DELAY, COUNTING, DONE.

- SEARCH: Shift in serial data into a 4-bit shift register each clock, look for pattern 1101. When found, transition to LOAD_DELAY.
- LOAD_DELAY: Shift in 4 bits MSB first into delay_reg. After 4 bits loaded, initialize delay_counter = delay_reg + 1 (to account for counting (delay+1)*1000 cycles) and reset cycle_counter, then transition to COUNTING.
- COUNTING: Count clock cycles in cycle_counter (0 to 999). On every 1000 cycles, decrement delay_counter. Output current delay_counter - 1 as count to match spec (since delay_counter counts from delay+1 down to 0, output counts down from delay to 0). When delay_counter reaches 0 and cycle_counter hits 999 (last 1000-cycle interval done), transition to DONE.
- DONE: Assert done, wait for ack==1 to transition back to SEARCH.

Data input is only sampled in SEARCH (pattern detection) and LOAD_DELAY (delay loading), ignored otherwise.

All registers and outputs updated synchronously on positive clock edge with synchronous reset.

This simple, single always block FSM with registers for state, pattern shift, delay bits, counters, and outputs ensures minimal complexity and clear timing without overlapping logic or race conditions.
