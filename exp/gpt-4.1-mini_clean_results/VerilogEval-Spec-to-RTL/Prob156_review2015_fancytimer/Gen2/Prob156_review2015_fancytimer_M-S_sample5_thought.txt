Implement a simple synchronous FSM with four states: SEARCH, READ_DELAY, COUNT, and DONE. Use a 4-bit shift register to detect the pattern 1101 by shifting in data MSB first (shift left and add new bit at LSB) each clock. Once the pattern is detected, transition to READ_DELAY, and shift in 4 delay bits MSB first into a 4-bit register. After reading the delay, move to COUNT, where a 10-bit cycle counter counts from 0 to 999 for each decrement of a 4-bit `remaining` register initialized to `delay`. The count output is the current `remaining` value during counting. When `remaining` reaches 0 and the 1000 cycles finish, transition to DONE asserting `done`. Wait for ack to return to SEARCH. Synchronous reset initializes all registers to zero and state to SEARCH. Use a single always block for state and registers. Assign outputs combinationally to avoid glitches and simplify logic.
