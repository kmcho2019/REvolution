Use a simple FSM with four states: SEARCH for pattern 1101, SHIFT_DELAY to shift in 4 delay bits MSB first, COUNTING to count (delay+1)*1000 cycles, and DONE_WAIT_ACK to assert done and wait for ack.  
Shift pattern and delay bits synchronously each clock in the respective states.  
Initialize the delay_counter to delay+1 on entering COUNTING. Use a cycle_counter (0 to 999) counting clock cycles per block.  
Decrement delay_counter at the end of each 1000-cycle block.  
Output count as the current remaining delay_counter-1 during counting, or zero otherwise.  
counting asserted only in COUNTING, done only in DONE_WAIT_ACK.  
Reset synchronous and outputs cleared or don't care in other states.  
This single always block with clear state transitions and counters is simpler and meets the spec without complex timing flags.
