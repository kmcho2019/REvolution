Refactor the saturating counter to use a simple 2-bit binary counter with asynchronous reset. The counter increments or decrements directly with saturating behavior implemented via conditional logic. Use a single always block with non-blocking assignments for the sequential logic to improve clarity and synthesis friendliness. This removes the one-hot encoding and decoding, reducing flip-flops and combinational logic. The asynchronous reset sets the counter state to 2'b01. The output 'state' is directly assigned from the counter register, eliminating extra decoding logic.
