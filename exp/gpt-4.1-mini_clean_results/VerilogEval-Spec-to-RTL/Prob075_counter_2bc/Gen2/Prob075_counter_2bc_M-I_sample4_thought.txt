To optimize for PPA, restructure the design by computing the next state combinationally before the clocked process. This eliminates redundant state assignments and simplifies the sequential block to a single register update, which may improve timing (TNS, WNS) by reducing logic depth in the clocked path. It also enhances readability and may marginally reduce power by avoiding unnecessary state re-assignments. The asynchronous reset remains positive-edge triggered. The saturating logic is compactly implemented using min/max operations.
