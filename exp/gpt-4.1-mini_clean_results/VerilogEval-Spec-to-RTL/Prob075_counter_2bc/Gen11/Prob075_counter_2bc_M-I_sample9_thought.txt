To optimize for better PPA, simplify the register update by removing the conditional update_enable signal and always assign next_state to the state register at each clock edge when not reset. This reduces combinational logic before the register, improving timing (WNS and TNS), and slightly decreases area due to simpler logic. Power may slightly increase since the register toggles even when next_state equals current state, but for a small 2-bit counter this is negligible and the timing benefits outweigh the minor power increase. The asynchronous reset remains as specified. The saturating logic stays unchanged. This approach results in a cleaner and more straightforward implementation that should improve clock period and timing slack.
