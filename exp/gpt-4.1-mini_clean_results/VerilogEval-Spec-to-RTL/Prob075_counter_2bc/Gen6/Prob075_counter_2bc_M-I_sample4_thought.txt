To improve PPA, we can simplify the update logic by removing the update_enable gating and always updating the state register with the next_state on each clock edge, which reduces combinational logic before the flip-flop and thus improves timing. Since the register will only change when next_state differs, unnecessary toggling is minimal, and synthesis tools will optimize the register accordingly. We keep the asynchronous positive-edge reset to meet requirements. This approach reduces logic depth, improves timing (lower TNS and WNS), slightly reduces area, and simplifies power management, trading off the minor power benefit of gating the update. Overall, a cleaner design with better timing and slight area and power improvement.
