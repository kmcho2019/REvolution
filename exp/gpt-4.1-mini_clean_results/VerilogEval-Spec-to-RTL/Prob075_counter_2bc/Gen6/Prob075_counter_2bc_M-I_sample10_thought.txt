To optimize the design for better PPA, I will replace the asynchronous reset with a synchronous reset, which can simplify timing closure and reduce potential metastability and reset-related timing issues. This change usually improves performance by simplifying the clock domain and allows better power gating during reset. 

Additionally, instead of an explicit FSM with states encoded as localparams and case statements, I will implement the saturating counter using simple arithmetic and saturation logic: increment the 2-bit state if train_valid and train_taken are asserted (up to max 3), decrement if train_valid and not train_taken (down to min 0), else hold. This reduces combinational logic and should improve timing and power slightly, as arithmetic operations are simpler than case statements.

The initial value after reset remains 2'b01 (weakly not taken).

This approach preserves functionality while aiming for slightly improved timing and power with minimal area impact.
