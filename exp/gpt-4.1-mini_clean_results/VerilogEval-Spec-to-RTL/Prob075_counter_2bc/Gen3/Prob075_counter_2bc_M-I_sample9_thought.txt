To optimize the existing saturating counter for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the case statement with arithmetic increment/decrement operations clamped at the saturation limits (0 and 3). This reduces combinational logic depth compared to multiple case comparisons. Additionally, I will update the state register only if the next state differs from the current state, minimizing unnecessary toggles and dynamic power consumption. The asynchronous reset remains as specified. This approach results in simpler, more efficient logic with fewer transitions, improving timing and power.
