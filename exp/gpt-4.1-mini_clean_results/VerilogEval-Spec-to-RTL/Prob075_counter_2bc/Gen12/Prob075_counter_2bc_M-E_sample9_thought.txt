Implement the saturating counter using a separate combinational next_state signal calculated by simple arithmetic with saturation logic, and register it on the clock edge with asynchronous reset. Instead of a case statement, use if-else to saturate increment and decrement operations. Additionally, add an enable signal that only updates the state register when the next state differs from the current state, preventing unnecessary toggling and reducing power consumption. This clear separation of combinational and sequential logic improves readability and potentially synthesis optimization while maintaining the required asynchronous reset.
