To optimize the original design for better timing and slightly reduced area while maintaining power efficiency, I will simplify the state update logic by removing the update_enable signal. Instead, the state will always update on the clock edge to the computed next_state, which directly reflects the saturating counter logic. This simplification reduces the combinational logic before the register input, improving the critical path and potentially the timing (tns/wns). It also reduces gate count slightly by removing the comparator and AND gate for update_enable. The power impact is minimal since the register only toggles when the next_state differs, and synthesis tools typically optimize unnecessary toggling internally. The asynchronous reset remains as requested, setting the counter to 2'b01 on areset. Overall, this approach balances simplicity, PPA benefits, and correctness.
