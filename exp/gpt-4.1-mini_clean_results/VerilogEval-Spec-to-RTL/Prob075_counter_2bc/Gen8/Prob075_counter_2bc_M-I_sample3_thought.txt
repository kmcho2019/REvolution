To improve PPA, I will simplify the update logic by removing the update_enable signal and always updating the state register on the clock edge. This removes the extra comparator logic that adds to the critical path, potentially improving timing (lowering WNS and TNS) and reducing area slightly. Because the counter is small (2 bits), the power overhead of updating state with the same value is minimal, and modern synthesis tools handle register retention efficiently. The asynchronous reset remains as per the specification. This simplification improves performance and area at a negligible cost in power.
