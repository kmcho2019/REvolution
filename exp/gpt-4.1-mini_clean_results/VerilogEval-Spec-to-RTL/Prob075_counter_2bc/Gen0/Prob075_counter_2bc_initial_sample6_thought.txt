Implement a 2-bit saturating counter that increments or decrements based on the train_valid and train_taken signals on the positive clock edge. The counter is asynchronously reset to 2'b01 on a positive edge of areset. The value saturates between 0 and 3, incrementing if train_valid and train_taken are both 1, decrementing if train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 0, and holds value if train_valid is 0.
