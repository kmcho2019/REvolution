Implement a 2-bit saturating counter with asynchronous positive-edge reset. On the positive clock edge, if train_valid is high, increment the counter if train_taken is high (up to 3), or decrement if train_taken is low (down to 0). If train_valid is low, keep the counter unchanged. On asynchronous positive edge of areset, reset the counter to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken).
