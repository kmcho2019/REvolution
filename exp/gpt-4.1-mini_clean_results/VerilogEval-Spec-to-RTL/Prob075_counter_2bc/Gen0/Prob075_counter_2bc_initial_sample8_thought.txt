Implement a 2-bit saturating counter with asynchronous positive-edge reset. On the rising edge of areset, the counter resets to 2'b01. On the rising edge of clk, if train_valid is asserted, increment the counter if train_taken is 1 (saturate at 3), or decrement it if train_taken is 0 (saturate at 0). If train_valid is 0, hold the current counter value. The output state is assigned the current counter value.
