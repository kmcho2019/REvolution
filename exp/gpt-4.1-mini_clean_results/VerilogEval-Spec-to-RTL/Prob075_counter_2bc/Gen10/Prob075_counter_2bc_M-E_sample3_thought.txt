We implement the saturating two-bit counter as a one-hot encoded state machine with four flip-flops representing the states 0 to 3. This approach leverages one-hot encoding to simplify the saturating increment and decrement logic to simple shift operations, avoiding explicit addition or subtraction. On increment, the '1' bit shifts left unless already at the leftmost position (state 3). On decrement, the '1' bit shifts right unless already at the rightmost position (state 0). The output state is decoded from the one-hot vector back to a 2-bit binary value. The asynchronous reset initializes the state to 2'b01 by activating the one-hot flip-flop corresponding to state 1. This method can potentially reduce combinational logic complexity and improve timing by replacing arithmetic operations with simple shifts and one-hot encoding.
