Implement the two-bit saturating counter as a one-hot encoded register array of four flip-flops representing states 0 to 3, with only one bit high at a time. On each training pulse (train_valid=1), move the one-hot bit left to increment or right to decrement, saturating at the edges (state 0 and state 3). When train_valid=0, keep the state unchanged. Asynchronous positive-edge reset sets the state to one-hot encoding of '1' (weakly not-taken, state 1). The output state is derived by decoding the one-hot vector into binary. This encoding approach avoids arithmetic operations, instead relying on simple shift and clamp operations, which could be beneficial for glitch reduction and straightforward synthesis with minimal logic.
