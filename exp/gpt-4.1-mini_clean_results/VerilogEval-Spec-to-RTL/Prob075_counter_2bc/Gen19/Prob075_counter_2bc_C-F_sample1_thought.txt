Fuse the clarity and explicit state transitions from the FSM approach with a more direct and concise saturating counter implementation using arithmetic increment/decrement and saturation logic. Use combinational logic that directly computes next state by saturating increments and decrements within the 2-bit range, avoiding the verbose case statement while keeping clean semantics. Maintain the asynchronous positive edge reset to ensure fast and reliable reset behavior to the weakly not-taken state (2'b01). This approach reduces combinational complexity, making the next_state logic minimal and easier to optimize, while preserving clarity by using arithmetic operators and saturation rather than enumerated transitions. The output state is directly the counter value. This fusion is optimal for PPA: timing is improved due to simpler next-state logic, power is reduced due to minimized logic transitions and easy clock gating opportunities, and area remains minimal due to arithmetic and simple saturation without large case statements.
