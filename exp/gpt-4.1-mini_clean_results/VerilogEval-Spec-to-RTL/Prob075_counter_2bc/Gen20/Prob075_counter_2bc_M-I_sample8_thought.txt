To improve PPA, introduce an enable signal for the state register that activates only when the counter value should change (i.e., train_valid is high and increment or decrement is required). This prevents unnecessary toggling of the state register, reducing dynamic power. Also, combine increment and decrement logic into a signed arithmetic operation with saturation to simplify combinational logic and reduce area. The asynchronous reset remains as specified. This approach balances clarity with better power efficiency and slightly simplified logic.
