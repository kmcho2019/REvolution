To optimize the design for better power efficiency without impacting timing, introduce clock gating to the state register so it only toggles when train_valid=1, i.e., when the counter can change. This reduces unnecessary clock toggling and dynamic power. We'll implement this by gating the clock input with train_valid using an enable signal, which is common for power savings in synchronous designs. The logic for next_state remains combinational and unchanged. The asynchronous reset remains as required. This approach minimizes additional logic, avoids increasing critical path, and preserves functional correctness while improving power consumption.
