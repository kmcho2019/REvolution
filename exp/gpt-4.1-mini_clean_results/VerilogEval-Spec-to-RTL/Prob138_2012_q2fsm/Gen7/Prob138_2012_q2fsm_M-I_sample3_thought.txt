To optimize the FSM for better power and performance without increasing area significantly, I will switch to one-hot encoding for states. One-hot encoding often improves timing due to simpler decoding logic in FPGA architectures and can reduce glitches by minimizing combinational logic levels. This also allows the output z to be generated by a simple OR of one-hot state bits, improving output timing and potentially reducing power by limiting switching activity. The synchronous reset and two always blocks will be maintained as requested. The output z will be a continuous assignment to reduce logic glitches further.
