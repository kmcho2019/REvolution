To improve area and power, switch the FSM encoding from one-hot (6 flip-flops) to binary encoding using 3 bits (since 6 states fit in 3 bits). This reduces flip-flop count and combinational decoding logic, leading to lower dynamic power and smaller area. The state transition and output logic are updated accordingly. The synchronous reset remains active-high, with state register and next-state logic separated in different always blocks. Output z is asserted only in states E and F. This binary encoding typically increases combinational complexity slightly but reduces flip-flop count and switching activity significantly, improving overall PPA.
