I will refactor the FSM by separating the output logic into a continuous assignment (assign statement) instead of assigning it inside a combinational always block. Also, I will keep the next state logic in one combinational always block and the state register in another sequential always block with synchronous reset. This approach emphasizes the separation of concerns and reduces the combinational always block's responsibility to only determining next_state, which can improve clarity and might help synthesis tools optimize output logic better.
