To improve the PPA metrics, I will use one-hot state encoding instead of binary encoding. One-hot encoding simplifies the next state logic to simple AND/OR combinations with less logic depth, improving timing (reducing negative slack) and potentially lowering power by minimizing complex decoding logic. The flip-flops will now be 6 one-bit registers, but this is acceptable given the state count and often yields faster FSMs. I will keep synchronous reset and separate always blocks for state register update and next state logic. The output z will be generated by OR-ing the one-hot signals for states E and F, which will be a simple logic expression. This approach should improve clock period and reduce logic complexity with negligible area increase.
