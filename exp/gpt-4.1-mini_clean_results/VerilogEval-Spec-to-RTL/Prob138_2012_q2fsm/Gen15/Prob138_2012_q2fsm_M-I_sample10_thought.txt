To optimize PPA, I will switch from one-hot encoding to a 3-bit binary encoding for the 6 states, reducing flip-flops and combinational logic area. The next-state logic will be implemented as a combinational always block using a case statement, which typically synthesizes more efficiently and leads to simpler, smaller logic.

This approach reduces area and power by minimizing the number of FFs and avoiding large OR-AND logic networks. The output `z` will be a simple combinational assignment based on the current state code.

Synchronous active-high reset and positive-edge clocking remain as before, with state flip-flops in a separate always block.

This balances performance and area/power while keeping the design clean and maintainable.
