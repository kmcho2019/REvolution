Implement the FSM using a one-hot state encoding to simplify next-state logic and potentially improve timing by reducing the complexity of decoding state bits. Use separate always blocks: one for state flip-flops with synchronous active-high reset, and one combinational block for next-state logic. The output z is asserted only in state E, realized through a continuous assignment from the state register bits. This approach leads to simpler next-state logic, easier debugging, and is often preferred in FPGA designs due to its speed and simplicity, despite increased flip-flop count.
