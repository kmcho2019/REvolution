To improve performance and reduce power, change the state encoding from binary (3 bits) to one-hot (6 bits), which simplifies next-state logic and reduces combinational path delay. This often leads to faster state transitions and lower dynamic power due to fewer logic levels. The one-hot encoding results in larger area due to more flip-flops but is acceptable given the FSM size. Use synchronous active-high reset and separate always blocks as before. For output z, implement with continuous assignment as a simple OR of the two active one-hot states. Keep coding styles consistent, use nonblocking assignments for sequential logic and blocking for combinational logic. This change should improve timing (lower TNS and WNS) and power with a slight area tradeoff.
