To improve the PPA, I will apply one-hot state encoding instead of binary encoding. One-hot encoding often reduces combinational logic depth, leading to improved timing (better WNS and TNS) at the cost of slightly increased flip-flop count. Since there are only 6 states, using 6 flip-flops is reasonable. Also, one-hot encoding typically reduces glitches, which can reduce dynamic power. The next state logic will use simple if-else statements with one-hot encoded states, making the logic straightforward and faster. The output z is assigned based on the E and F states as before but now easier as a direct wire from those flip-flops. This should improve clock period (eff_clk_period) and timing slack while keeping area moderate and reducing power due to simpler combinational logic.
