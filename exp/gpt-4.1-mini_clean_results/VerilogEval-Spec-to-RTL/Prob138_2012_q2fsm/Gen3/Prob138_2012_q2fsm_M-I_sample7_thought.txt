To optimize the original one-hot FSM implementation for better PPA (power, performance, area), I will change the state encoding from one-hot (6 flip-flops) to binary encoding using 3 bits for 6 states. This reduces flip-flop count by half, lowering area and dynamic power.

- Use a 3-bit encoded state variable.
- Use an enumerated localparam for better readability of states.
- Separate always blocks for sequential logic and combinational next-state logic as required.
- Output z depends on states E and F; output logic remains combinational.
- This reduces area and power due to fewer flip-flops and simpler next-state logic.
- Using a case statement over the encoded state instead of one-hot state vector improves synthesis results and can enhance timing.
- Synchronous reset is retained.

This approach balances reduced area and power with potentially better or similar timing due to simpler and narrower next-state combinational logic.
