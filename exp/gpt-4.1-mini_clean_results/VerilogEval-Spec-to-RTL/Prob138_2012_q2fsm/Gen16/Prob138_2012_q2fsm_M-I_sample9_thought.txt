The original design balances power, area, and performance well, but can be optimized further for performance and simplicity. To reduce the critical path and potential gate delays, I will remove the conditional state register enable, making the state register update unconditionally every clock cycle (except for reset). This can improve timing by eliminating the comparator and gate delay on the clock path.

For output logic, I will keep the continuous assignment for minimal area and power. I will also adopt a 3-bit binary encoding but reorder the states to a more sequential binary encoding from 3'd0 to 3'd5 for clarity. Since the number of states is less than 8, a 3-bit binary encoding is still efficient.

In summary:
- Remove state_change logic to reduce combinational logic in the clock path and improve timing.
- Keep synchronous active-high reset as required.
- Use separate always blocks for state register and next-state logic.
- Maintain continuous assignment for output z.
This should improve the effective clock period and worst negative slack while having negligible impact on power and area.
