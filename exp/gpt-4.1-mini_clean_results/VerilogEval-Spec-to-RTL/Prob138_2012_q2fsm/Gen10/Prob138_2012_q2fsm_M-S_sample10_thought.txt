To simplify the FSM design, I will remove the conditional state update enabling and always update the state register on each clock edge. This eliminates the extra gating signal and potential timing complexity. I will keep the same 3-bit binary encoding and separate always blocks for sequential state register and combinational next-state logic, as required. The output `z` will be assigned combinationally based on the current state being E or F. This approach keeps the FSM straightforward and fully functional with minimal logic.
