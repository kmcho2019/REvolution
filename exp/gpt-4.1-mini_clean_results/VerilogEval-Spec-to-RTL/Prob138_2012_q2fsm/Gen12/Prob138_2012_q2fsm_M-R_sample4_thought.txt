I will keep the same synchronous active-high reset and positive edge clocking, but instead of gating the state update with a conditional inside the sequential block, I will always update the state register on the clock edge to simplify timing and synthesis. The next-state logic will be implemented as a combinational function returning the next state, separating concerns nicely. This approach maintains the original FSM behavior but improves code structure, readability, and potentially synthesis timing robustness.
