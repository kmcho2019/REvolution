I will implement the TopModule by creating intermediate signals for pairwise minimums, effectively forming a balanced comparison tree. This approach compares 'a' and 'b' in parallel with 'c' and 'd' to get two minimum values, then compares those two results to find the overall minimum. Using continuous assignment statements without an always block keeps the design purely combinational and straightforward. This balanced tree reduces logic depth compared to a linear chain of comparisons, improving timing and possibly lowering power consumption.
