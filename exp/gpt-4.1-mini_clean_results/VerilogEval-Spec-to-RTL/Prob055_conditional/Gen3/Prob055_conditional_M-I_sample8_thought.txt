To optimize the original design for better area and potentially power, I will eliminate the separate Comparator8 module and implement the minimum computation directly in the TopModule using nested conditional (?:) operators. This will reduce module instantiations and wiring overhead, enabling the synthesis tool to better optimize the logic. This approach also reduces the combinational path by performing pairwise comparisons inline, minimizing logic and area usage. The logic remains purely combinational and straightforward, maintaining zero timing violations and low power while reducing area.
