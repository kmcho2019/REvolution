To optimize the PPA metrics, particularly area and power, I will eliminate the instantiation of separate Comparator8 modules and implement the minimum computation purely using nested conditional (ternary) operators in a single always_comb block. This reduces the number of module instances and associated wiring overhead, potentially decreasing area and power.

Moreover, this combinational logic is simple and has only two levels of comparison, so timing will remain optimal or improve slightly due to reduced interconnect.

The strategy is:
1. Compare inputs pairwise using conditional operators.
2. Select the smaller between a and b, and between c and d.
3. Compare the two selected minimums to get the overall minimum.

This approach reduces area by removing multiple comparator module instantiations and simplifies the design, while maintaining zero timing violations and low power consumption.
