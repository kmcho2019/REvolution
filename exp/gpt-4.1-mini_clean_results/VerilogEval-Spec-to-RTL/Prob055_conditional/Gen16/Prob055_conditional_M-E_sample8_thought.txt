Instead of using a modular min2 unit or balanced binary comparisons, I will implement a priority-encoded comparison in a single always block. The approach will sequentially check conditions to find the minimum without relying on intermediate modules. The priority sequence compares all inputs explicitly by pairwise conditions, ensuring one final minimum output. This method avoids instantiations, reduces wiring, and uses a compact combinational logic approach. It also simplifies the hierarchy and keeps the logic easily optimizable by synthesis tools, as all comparisons are local and explicit. The resulting code will be a single always @(*) block performing conditional logic to assign the minimum of the four 8-bit inputs directly to the output.
