To simplify, I will avoid the hierarchical min2 module and generate constructs, and instead directly implement the minimum logic inside TopModule using simple conditional operators. Since there are only four inputs, directly writing comparisons with the conditional operator is straightforward, less code, and perfectly readable.

This approach yields minimal code with no module instantiations and is purely combinational. The clarity remains high due to explicit comparison steps without intermediate signals or arrays.

The logic is:
- Find the smaller of a and b,
- Find the smaller of c and d,
- Then find the smaller of those two results for the final minimum.

This keeps the design minimal while fully meeting the specification.
