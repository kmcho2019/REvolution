The current design is already modular and clean, using min2 modules for clarity and balanced tree comparison, which is optimal for timing and power.

To improve PPA further:

- **Area reduction:** Instead of instantiating two min2 modules, I can inline the minimum comparisons using continuous assignments with ternary operators. This removes the overhead of separate module instantiations, potentially allowing synthesis to optimize better and reduce area.
- **Power and performance:** The logic depth remains two-level and purely combinational, so no timing or power penalty.
- **Code simplicity:** The design becomes more concise and easier for synthesis tools to optimize.

Thus, I will implement the balanced minimum tree purely with wires and continuous assignments in a single module, removing the min2 submodule. This saves area and possibly power, without sacrificing readability or timing.
