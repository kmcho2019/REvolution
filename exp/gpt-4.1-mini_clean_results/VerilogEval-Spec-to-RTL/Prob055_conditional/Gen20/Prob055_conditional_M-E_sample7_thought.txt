I will implement the minimum finder by using a purely combinational approach that avoids multiple module instantiations. Instead, the TopModule will perform a straightforward chain of comparisons using nested conditional operators to select the minimum value among the four inputs.

This design is simple and compact, with minimal wiring and no intermediate signals or generate blocks. It is a flat, direct implementation that calculates the minimum by first finding the minimum between `a` and `b`, the minimum between `c` and `d`, and then finally the minimum of those two results.

This approach keeps the design easy to understand and synthesize, minimizes hierarchy overhead, and avoids additional modules. Although it may result in a slightly longer combinational path than a balanced tree, it is fully combinational and well suited for small input sets. The logic is concise and self-contained inside the TopModule.
