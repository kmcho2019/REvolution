To optimize the existing modular design for better PPA (power, performance, area) while maintaining clarity, I will:
- Flatten the hierarchical min2 instances into a single combinational block in TopModule to eliminate extra module overhead and potential routing delays.
- Implement the four-way minimum using direct conditional operators without intermediate wires or submodules, reducing area and slightly improving timing by removing intermediate nets and module boundaries.
- This flattening should reduce area (fewer net drivers, no module boundaries) and power (less logic overhead) while preserving minimal combinational delay since the logic depth remains two comparisons.
- The code remains clean and simple, easy to understand and synthesize.
This approach trades modularity for slightly better PPA, which is justified by the given metrics and the small, simple problem size.
