Instead of building a modular min2 comparator tree, I will implement a fully combinational approach using simple combinational logic and conditional operators to directly compute the minimum of all four inputs in a flat manner.

The idea is:
- First compute the minimum of a and b as min_ab.
- Then compute the minimum of c and d as min_cd.
- Finally, compute the minimum of min_ab and min_cd as the output min.

This approach does not use any module hierarchy or arrays, resulting in straightforward and easy-to-read code.
It uses only simple nested ternary operators and generates minimal logic with the smallest possible number of gates.

This flat style is optimal for the fixed case of four inputs, minimizing intermediate nets and simplifying synthesis while still being purely combinational.
