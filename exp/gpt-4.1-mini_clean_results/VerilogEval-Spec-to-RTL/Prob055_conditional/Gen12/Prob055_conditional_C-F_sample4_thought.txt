To optimize performance and potentially reduce power and area, I will implement a balanced comparator tree that finds the minimum by first comparing pairs of inputs in parallel and then comparing the intermediate minima. This reduces the critical path length compared to sequential comparisons. I will use combinational logic and intermediate wires for clarity and synthesis efficiency, avoiding procedural blocks. This structural approach exploits parallelism inherent in hardware and supports cleaner mapping to balanced logic, thereby improving timing and potentially lowering switching activity and area without sacrificing readability or correctness.
