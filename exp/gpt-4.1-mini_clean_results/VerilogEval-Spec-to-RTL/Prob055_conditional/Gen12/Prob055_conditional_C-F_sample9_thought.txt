I will implement the minimum finder by combining the clarity and minimal overhead of the sequential if-else comparisons with the better performance characteristics of a balanced comparator tree. Specifically, I will perform parallel pairwise minimums (a vs b and c vs d) and then select the minimum of those two results. This reduces the longest combinational path from three comparisons to two levels, improving timing. The logic remains straightforward and easy to read without using arrays or loops, thus avoiding potential area and power overhead.
