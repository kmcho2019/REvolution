To optimize the original design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will remove the separate comparator module instantiations and replace them with purely combinational conditional operators directly in TopModule. This eliminates module overhead and hierarchy, potentially reducing area and improving timing by simplifying logic. Using inline ternary expressions to compare and select the minimum of the four inputs directly is efficient for a small fixed input size like 4. This approach also reduces routing complexity and power due to less wiring and fewer buffers. The resulting single-module design will be more compact and simpler without losing clarity.
