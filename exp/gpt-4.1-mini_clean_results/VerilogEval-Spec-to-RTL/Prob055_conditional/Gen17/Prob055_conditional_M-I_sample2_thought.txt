The original design uses a balanced comparator tree with three min2 modules, providing minimal combinational depth and modular clarity.

To optimize further for power and area (since timing is already minimal), we can:

- Remove the min2 module abstraction and write the comparisons directly using continuous assignments. This removes module instantiation overhead, potentially reducing area slightly.
- Use intermediate wires to hold the partial minimums as before.
- Because the design is purely combinational, direct assignment may help synthesis tools optimize better.
- Since inputs are 8 bits, using a direct conditional operator for min reduces logic from extra multiplexers instantiated by the min2 module.
- Overall, the logic remains two-level and combinational, so delay won't increase.

This "flattened" combinational approach should slightly reduce area and power without affecting timing or clarity.
