I will implement the minimum finder using a balanced comparator tree structure. First, compare pairs (a, b) and (c, d) in parallel to generate two intermediate minimum values. Then compare these two intermediate minimums to produce the final minimum output. This parallel, tree-based approach can reduce the critical path delay compared to sequential comparisons. It also makes the design more modular and scalable for more inputs. The entire comparison will be done combinationally using continuous assignment with nested conditional operators for simplicity.
