To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will reduce module instantiation overhead by removing the separate min2 submodule and implementing the minimum logic directly inside TopModule. This flattening of hierarchy may help synthesis tools optimize the combinational logic better.

Furthermore, I will use a single combinational always block with nested conditional operators or a chain of conditional minimum comparisons, avoiding redundant wire declarations and intermediate signals. This can slightly reduce the combinational logic and area.

The direct comparison of all four inputs can be done with a priority-encoded conditional selection minimizing the number of comparisons:

- Compare a and b, get min_ab
- Compare c and d, get min_cd
- Compare min_ab and min_cd, get min

This remains the same logic, but all done within a single always_comb block, avoiding module overhead and potentially improving synthesis results.

This approach keeps the clarity of logic but allows synthesis tools to optimize better for area and power by leveraging the flattened structure.
