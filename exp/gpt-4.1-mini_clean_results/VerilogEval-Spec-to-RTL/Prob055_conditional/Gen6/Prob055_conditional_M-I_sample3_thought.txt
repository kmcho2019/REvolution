The original solution uses a balanced pairwise comparison tree which is already efficient for delay. To optimize area and power further, I will eliminate the always block and implement the entire minimum logic purely with continuous assignments. This reduces unnecessary registers and non-blocking assignments that are not needed in purely combinational logic, leading to simpler synthesized logic and potentially lower power and area. Additionally, I will implement the comparisons in a nested ternary expression, enabling synthesis tools to optimize comparison operations more tightly and reduce logic duplication. This may slightly improve area and power by allowing the synthesis tool more freedom in resource sharing.
