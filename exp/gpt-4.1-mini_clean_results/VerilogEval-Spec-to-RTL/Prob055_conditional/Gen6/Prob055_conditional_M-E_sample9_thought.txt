I will implement the minimum finder using a balanced binary-tree approach with pairwise minimum modules. First, I create a reusable min2 module that compares two 8-bit inputs and outputs the smaller one. Then, in TopModule, I instantiate min2 twice to compare (a,b) and (c,d) in parallel, followed by one more min2 to compare the two intermediate minima. This structure reduces the comparator chain depth and can improve timing by enabling more parallelism.
