To find the minimum of four 8-bit unsigned inputs, I will implement a balanced comparison tree using intermediate wires. First, find the minimum between pairs (a,b) and (c,d) with two parallel comparisons, then compare those two results to get the final minimum. This approach reduces the maximum comparator delay by balancing the comparison stages and can be easily pipelined if needed, though here it's purely combinational. This method also enables reusing the same comparator logic multiple times and clearly shows the hierarchy of comparisons.
