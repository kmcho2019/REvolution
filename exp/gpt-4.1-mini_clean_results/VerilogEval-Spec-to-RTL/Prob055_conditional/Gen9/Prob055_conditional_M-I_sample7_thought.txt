To optimize PPA (Power, Performance, Area) beyond the modular multi-instance comparator approach, I will replace the multiple Comparator8 module instantiations with a single combinational block using a straightforward conditional operator tree. This will reduce area by avoiding module overhead and eliminate intermediate wires and instantiations. The logic is combinational and the depth is minimal (two stages), preserving timing performance. The single always_comb block with if-else statements is synthesizable and can optimize power by reducing unnecessary toggling as synthesis tools can better optimize this style. This approach also simplifies the code and may slightly reduce power due to fewer nets switching.
