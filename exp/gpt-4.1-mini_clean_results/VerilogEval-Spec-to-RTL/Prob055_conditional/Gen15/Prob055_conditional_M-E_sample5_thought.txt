I will implement the minimum finder using a purely combinational always block with a chain of if-else statements, directly comparing the four inputs step-by-step without separate min modules. This approach uses simple logic and avoids module instantiation overhead, potentially reducing logic duplication. The always block will sequentially update an internal register holding the minimum value found so far, first initializing it to input 'a', then comparing with 'b', 'c', and 'd' in turn. This yields a minimal combinational chain that is straightforward and easy for synthesis tools to optimize.
