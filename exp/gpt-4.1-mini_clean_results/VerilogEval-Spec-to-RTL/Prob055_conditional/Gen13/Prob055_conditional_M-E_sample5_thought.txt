I will implement the TopModule by building a hierarchical comparator structure using a reusable 2-input minimum (min2) submodule. The TopModule instantiates three min2 modules: first to compute the minimum of (a, b), second for (c, d), and finally a third to find the minimum of these two intermediate minima. This approach reflects a balanced tree comparator architecture that can scale efficiently for more inputs, potentially improving timing by reducing logic depth compared to a flat linear chain. Using modular subcomponents can also enhance clarity, code reuse, and facilitate incremental optimization or formal verification.
