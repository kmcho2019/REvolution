I will implement the minimum finder using a balanced binary tree structure of two-input minimum modules. Specifically, I will create a reusable min2 submodule that compares two 8-bit inputs and outputs the smaller one. Then, TopModule instantiates three min2 modules: the first two find the minimum of (a,b) and (c,d) respectively, and the last finds the minimum between those two results. This approach modularizes the design, facilitates scalability, and potentially reduces combinational path delay by balancing the comparison stages.
