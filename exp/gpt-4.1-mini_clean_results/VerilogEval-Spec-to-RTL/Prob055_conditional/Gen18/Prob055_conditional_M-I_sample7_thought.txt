To optimize the design for better performance, I will implement a balanced comparator tree to reduce the critical path delay. Instead of comparing the four inputs sequentially, I will:

- First compare 'a' and 'b' to get min_ab.
- Then compare 'c' and 'd' to get min_cd.
- Finally, compare min_ab and min_cd to get the overall min.

This approach reduces the comparator depth from 3 to 2, potentially improving timing (WNS) and power by reducing the maximum logic depth.

Additionally, I will use continuous assignments with wires instead of an always block and internal reg, resulting in cleaner combinational logic and potentially smaller area and better power optimization by synthesis tools.

This combinational logic with a balanced tree should provide better PPA with the same functionality.
