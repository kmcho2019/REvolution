To optimize PPA (power, performance, area), I will restructure the minimum comparison as a balanced tree of pairwise minimums. This reduces the comparator logic depth from three sequential comparisons to two levels of parallel comparisons, lowering the critical path delay (improving timing) and potentially reducing dynamic power by limiting sequential dependency.

Specifically:
1. Compute min_ab = minimum(a, b) and min_cd = minimum(c, d) in parallel.
2. Then compute min = minimum(min_ab, min_cd).

This balanced approach can reduce the overall combinational delay, potentially improving clock frequency or reducing power. Also, the code remains straightforward and synthesizable.

No additional registers are introduced, maintaining area efficiency.
