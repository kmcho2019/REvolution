To optimize the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) of the original design, I will eliminate the Comparator8 module instances and instead use a single combinational always block with conditional operators to directly compute the minimum of the four inputs. This approach reduces area by avoiding multiple module instantiations and minimizes logic levels for potentially better timing, as the synthesis tool can optimize the conditional operators efficiently. It also reduces power by less switching in multiple modules and avoids extra wiring overhead. The code will remain purely combinational, clean, and easy to maintain.
