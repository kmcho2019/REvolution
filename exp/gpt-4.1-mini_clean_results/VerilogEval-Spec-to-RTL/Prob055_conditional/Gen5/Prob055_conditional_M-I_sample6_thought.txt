The original design is clear and modular but instantiates three Comparator8 modules, which adds some area and power overhead. To optimize for better PPA, I will replace the comparator module hierarchy with a single always_comb block that uses nested ternary operators to find the minimum among the four inputs. This reduces the number of instantiated modules, thus decreasing area and power, and it also slightly reduces logic depth by doing all comparisons in parallel with combinational logic.

The trade-off is losing modularity and testability of the separate comparator module but for this simple task, the gain in area and power is worthwhile. Timing should remain equal or better since all comparisons happen simultaneously in combinational logic.

This approach provides a more compact and efficient minimum finder for four unsigned 8-bit inputs.
