To improve timing (reduce critical path length) and potentially reduce power, I will restructure the minimum value computation as a balanced comparison tree. This approach compares pairs of inputs in parallel (a vs b, c vs d) and then compares the two intermediate minimums, effectively reducing the number of comparison stages from three to two. This reduces combinational logic depth and can improve maximum clock frequency and power efficiency. The implementation will use combinational logic without loops and will maintain readability by using intermediate wires for partial minimums.
