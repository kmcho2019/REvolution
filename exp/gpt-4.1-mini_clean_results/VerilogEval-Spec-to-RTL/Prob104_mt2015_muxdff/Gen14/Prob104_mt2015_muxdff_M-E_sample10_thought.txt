Implement the TopModule by integrating the 2:1 multiplexer functionality inside an always block that triggers on the rising edge of clk. On each clock edge, use a blocking assignment to a temporary variable to select between r_in and q_in based on L, then non-blocking assign this temporary value to Q. This combines the mux and flip-flop behavior inside a single sequential always block without separate combinational logic. This approach provides a clear synchronous load operation and reduces the number of signals by avoiding an intermediate wire.
