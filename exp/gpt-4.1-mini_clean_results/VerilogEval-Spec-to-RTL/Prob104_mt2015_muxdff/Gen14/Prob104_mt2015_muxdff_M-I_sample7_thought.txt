The original TopModule implementation assumes that the combinational logic feeding q_in is done outside the module. However, from the original full_module code, the combinational logic for the next state (when L=0) depends on the other flip-flops' outputs and must be implemented outside of each TopModule instance, feeding into q_in.

Our TopModule should therefore remain a simple 2:1 mux + D flip-flop, selecting between loading r_in (when L=1) or q_in (when L=0). To optimize for PPA:

- Remove the function call and replace it with a direct combinational 2:1 mux using a ternary operator. This reduces logic levels and potential critical path.
- Implement Q as a reg updated on the rising edge of clk.
- Keep the design minimal and synthesizable.

This approach keeps the TopModule simple and efficient while the complex combinational logic (like q[1]^q[2]) will be computed outside and connected to q_in, as intended by the original hierarchical design.

Thus, the improved TopModule is a straightforward mux + flip-flop with no extra function overhead, improving performance and area slightly while maintaining correct behavior.
