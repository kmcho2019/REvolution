Implement the TopModule by explicitly instantiating separate submodules for the 2:1 multiplexer and the D flip-flop. The TopModule will include a small internal mux module that selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and a D flip-flop module that captures the mux output on clk rising edge. This hierarchical approach clearly separates combinational and sequential logic into reusable modules, improving readability and modularity. The mux and DFF will be defined inside the same file for simplicity.
