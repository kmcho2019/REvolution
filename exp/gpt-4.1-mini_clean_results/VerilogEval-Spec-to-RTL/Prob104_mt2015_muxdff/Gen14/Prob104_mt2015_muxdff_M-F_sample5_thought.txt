The previous implementation of TopModule is functionally correct for a flip-flop with a 2:1 mux on its input selected by L, as required. The only potential improvement is to use a reg type for the mux output inside the always block instead of a wire with a continuous assignment. This style can sometimes avoid subtle simulation or synthesis mismatches caused by separating combinational and sequential logic. Thus, move the mux logic inside the always block, assigning a reg variable d based on L, and then assign Q <= d. This keeps all sequential logic inside the always block and makes the design behavior explicit and clear, potentially eliminating early simulation mismatches. No interface change is necessary.
