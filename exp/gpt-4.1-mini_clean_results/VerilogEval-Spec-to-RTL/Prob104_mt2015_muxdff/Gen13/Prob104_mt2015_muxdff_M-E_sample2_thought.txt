A novel approach is to explicitly separate the 2:1 multiplexer from the flip-flop within the TopModule, with the mux output feeding the flip-flop input synchronously. Instead of combining mux and flip-flop behavior inside a single always block, this design will implement the mux as a combinational block and the flip-flop as a synchronous block, each clearly distinguished. The mux selects between r_in and q_in based on L, then the selected input is clocked into the flip-flop on the rising edge of clk. This modular separation improves clarity and could ease formal verification and debugging. It also follows good RTL design style by separating combinational logic (mux) and sequential logic (flip-flop), avoiding subtle timing or simulation mismatches. The output Q is registered, reflecting the stored bit. This design complies fully with the problem requirements and presents a structurally different architecture than the original solution.
