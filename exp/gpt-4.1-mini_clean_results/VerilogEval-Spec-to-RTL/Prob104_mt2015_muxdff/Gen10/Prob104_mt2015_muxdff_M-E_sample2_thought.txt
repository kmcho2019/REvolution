Implement the TopModule by embedding the 2:1 multiplexer directly inside the sequential always block rather than separating the mux as a separate wire. This means the flip-flop's next state is assigned using a conditional operator inside the clocked process, combining mux and flip-flop behavior in one block. This simplifies the module by removing the combinational mux signal and reduces the number of intermediate wires. The functionality remains the same: on the rising edge of clk, if L=1 then load r_in, else load q_in.
