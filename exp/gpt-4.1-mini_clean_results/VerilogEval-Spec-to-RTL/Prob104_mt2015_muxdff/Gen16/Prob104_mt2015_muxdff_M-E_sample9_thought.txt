Instead of combining the multiplexer and flip-flop into a single always block, we can explicitly separate the combinational mux logic from the sequential flip-flop. In this design, the TopModule contains an internal wire for the mux output that selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and then this mux output is registered at the clock edge to update Q. This clear separation improves readability and makes each functional block explicit. It also matches well the idea of factoring the full_module into smaller components and will simplify debugging and instantiation.
