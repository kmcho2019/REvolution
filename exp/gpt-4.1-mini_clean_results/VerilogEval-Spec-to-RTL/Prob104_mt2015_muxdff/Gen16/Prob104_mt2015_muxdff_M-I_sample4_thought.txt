To improve synthesis compatibility and avoid potential synthesis warnings, remove the initial block since synthesis tools often do not support flip-flop initialization through initial blocks. Instead, ensure functional equivalence by implementing the multiplexer within the always block, avoiding a separate wire assignment. This also can aid synthesis tools in recognizing the mux and flip-flop pattern clearly.

Thus, in the always @(posedge clk) block:
- If L is high, Q is loaded with r_in.
- Else Q is loaded with q_in.

This preserves functionality and provides a clear synchronous multiplexer-flip-flop structure with minimal logic and no asynchronous or initial states, improving synthesis friendliness while maintaining minimal area, power, and good performance.
