Implement TopModule as a single always block triggered on posedge clk that directly computes the next Q value using a mux operation inside the sequential block. Instead of using a separate combinational block or intermediate register, we use a ternary operator inside the sequential block. This reduces the design to one always block with a simple mux selecting between r_in and q_in before capturing Q. This compact design style is common in FPGA RTL and allows the full_module to feed q_in with the appropriately computed feedback bit externally.
