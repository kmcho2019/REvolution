Implement TopModule as a single-bit D flip-flop with synchronous load enable using a fully combinational 2:1 multiplexer feeding the D input. The mux output is selected by L, choosing between r_in (load data) and q_in (previous stored data), and fed into the flip-flop on the rising edge of clk. This approach separates combinational mux and sequential flip-flop clearly and uses non-blocking assignment inside the clocked always block for proper sequential semantics.
