Based on the feedback, the main improvement is to simplify the design by removing the separate mux2to1 submodule and instead implementing the 2:1 mux functionality directly inside TopModule using a ternary operator. This inline mux logic reduces instance overhead and potentially improves timing and area by letting synthesis tools optimize better. The flip-flop functionality remains the same, capturing the mux output on the rising edge of clk. This modification preserves the required behavior and hierarchical factoring (flip-flop plus mux within TopModule), while eliminating an unnecessary submodule layer.
