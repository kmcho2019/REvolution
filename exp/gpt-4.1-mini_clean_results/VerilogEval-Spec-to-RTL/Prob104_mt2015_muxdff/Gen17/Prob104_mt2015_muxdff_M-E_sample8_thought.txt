Implement TopModule as a simple synchronous D flip-flop with a 2:1 mux selecting between r_in (load input) and q_in (shift input), controlled by load signal L. The module itself does not compute any feedback or XOR logic; it just outputs Q = L ? r_in : q_in on the rising edge of clk. This keeps TopModule very generic and reusable. The original full_module will instantiate three such TopModules and supply the appropriate q_in signals externally, including the XOR logic for bit0 done outside. This clean separation allows simple, correct, and modular design.
