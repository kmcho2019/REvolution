Refactor the TopModule to explicitly separate the 2:1 multiplexer as a combinational assignment and the flip-flop as a sequential register assignment. Use a wire for the mux output selected by L, and assign that to the flip-flop input on the clock edge. This keeps the same functionality (loading r_in when L is high, otherwise holding q_in), but breaks down the logic more clearly between combinational mux and sequential FF, improving readability and modularity.
