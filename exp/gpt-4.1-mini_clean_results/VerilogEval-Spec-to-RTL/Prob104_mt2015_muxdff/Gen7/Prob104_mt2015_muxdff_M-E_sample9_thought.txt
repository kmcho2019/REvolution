To offer a distinct architectural approach, I will implement TopModule by explicitly instantiating a 2:1 multiplexer module and a D flip-flop module inside TopModule. This factorizes the internal logic further, separating the mux and flip-flop into their own submodules, which emphasizes hierarchy and reusability. The mux will select between r_in and q_in based on L, and the flip-flop will latch the mux output at the rising edge of clk. This modular structure is different from the initial single-module approach and enhances clarity, testability, and potential for reuse.
