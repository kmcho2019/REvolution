Implement the TopModule by explicitly separating the 2:1 multiplexer and the D flip-flop. The multiplexer will select between r_in and q_in based on L, producing an internal wire 'mux_out'. This mux_out will feed the D input of a positive-edge triggered flip-flop, which will output Q. This modular approach clearly reflects the hierarchy and separates the logic cleanly, allowing the full_module to handle the combinational logic for q_in.
