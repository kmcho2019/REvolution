To fix the single mismatch at simulation startup, initialize the flip-flop output Q to 0 so it starts in a known state. This avoids unknown or X states that may cause transient mismatches at the first clock edge. The rest of the design remains unchanged: a combinational 2:1 mux selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and the flip-flop captures that mux output at the clock's rising edge using a non-blocking assignment.
