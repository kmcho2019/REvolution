Instead of explicitly using a 2:1 multiplexer followed by a flip-flop, I will combine the mux logic inside the D input to a single D flip-flop. This single flip-flop will be modeled by a module that has an input D, clock, and output Q. The 2:1 mux selecting between r_in and q_in based on L will be factored into the TopModule by combining the inputs before feeding the D flip-flop. Internally, I'll instantiate a dedicated flip-flop submodule within TopModule, and compute the D input combinationally. This approach encapsulates the flip-flop behavior distinctly and makes the hierarchy clearer. This also improves modularity since the flip-flop logic is separated from input selection, making it easier to replace or test either independently.
