The previous solution incorrectly introduced a synchronous reset input that is not part of the original specification, causing port mismatches and simulation issues. To correct this, we will remove the reset input entirely and implement the TopModule strictly as a single-bit D flip-flop with a 2:1 mux input controlled by L. The mux will select between r_in (load value) and q_in (feedback) at each positive clock edge. The output Q will be a reg driven by the flip-flop. This minimal design exactly matches the original requirements, ensuring no extra inputs or ports that cause mismatches. It also maintains clarity and efficiency suitable for instantiating three times within full_module.
