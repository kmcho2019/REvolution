Implement TopModule as a positive edge-triggered D flip-flop with an explicit 2:1 multiplexer at the input. Instead of using if-else inside the always block, separate the mux output as a wire assigned combinationally. The mux selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and the selected value is loaded into Q on clk's rising edge. This clean separation of mux and flip-flop models each bit of the 3-bit register from full_module exactly and supports hierarchical instantiation. This structure also makes the module reusable and clear.
