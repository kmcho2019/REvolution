To correct the previous design, separate the mux logic from the clocked flip-flop logic by implementing the mux as purely combinational logic outside the sequential block. Use a wire for the mux output, computed by a continuous assignment or an always @(*) block, selecting between r_in and q_in based on L. Then, on the rising edge of clk, latch the mux output into Q. This ensures proper functional behavior, clear timing, and better synthesis mapping. Additionally, declare Q as reg since it is assigned in a clocked always block.
