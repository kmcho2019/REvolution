Instead of instantiating a separate flip-flop and a separate 2:1 mux, I will create a single TopModule that internally implements the multiplexer logic using a conditional (if-else) inside an always block triggered on the rising clock edge. This integrates the mux and flip-flop behavior without separate modules or wires, producing a registered output Q that updates according to L. When L is high, Q takes r_in; otherwise, Q takes q_in. This approach reduces wiring and explicitly models sequential behavior, making the design very straightforward and directly reflective of the desired logic.
