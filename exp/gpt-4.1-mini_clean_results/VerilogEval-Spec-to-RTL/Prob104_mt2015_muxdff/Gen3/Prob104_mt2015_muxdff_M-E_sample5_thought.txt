Implement the TopModule as a flip-flop with an explicit 2:1 multiplexer instantiated as a separate submodule within TopModule. The mux takes L as select input and chooses between r_in and q_in, feeding the D input of the flip-flop. The flip-flop itself is also a separate submodule instantiated inside TopModule. This hierarchical decomposition inside TopModule provides clear modularity: the mux and the flip-flop are independent reusable components. The output Q is the registered output of the flip-flop. This differs architecturally from the original direct mux in always block approach.
