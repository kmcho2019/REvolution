Refactor the TopModule by separating the 2:1 multiplexer from the flip-flop explicitly: first compute the mux output using a continuous assign statement, then register that mux output on the rising edge of clk in an always block. This structure clarifies the combinational and sequential parts, improving readability and possibly synthesis optimization. The functionality remains the same: on clk's rising edge, if L=1 load r_in; else load q_in. By using an assign statement for the mux, the design explicitly models the mux combinationally outside the flip-flop.
