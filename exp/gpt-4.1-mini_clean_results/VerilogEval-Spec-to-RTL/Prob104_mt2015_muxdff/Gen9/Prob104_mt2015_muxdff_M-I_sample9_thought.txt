To improve the initial design, merge the mux and flip-flop logic into a single always @(posedge clk) block. This removes the intermediate combinational reg and potential glitches at the mux output, reducing logic depth and minimizing timing risks at the clock edge. Using a single clocked process also avoids any possible hazards due to asynchronous combinational logic feeding the flip-flop input. This approach enhances performance by shortening the critical path, reduces power by minimizing unnecessary toggling, and keeps area minimal with only one flip-flop and a simple 2:1 mux in the same always block. It also matches synthesis-friendly coding style.
