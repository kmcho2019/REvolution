Implement the TopModule as two distinct components inside: a 2:1 multiplexer for selecting between r_in and q_in based on L, and a D flip-flop that captures the mux output on the rising edge of clk. This explicit separation clearly mirrors the hierarchical design intent. The mux output is a wire feeding the flip-flop's D input, and the flip-flop updates Q synchronously at the clock edge.
