Instead of having the XOR and next-state logic external to TopModule, embed the next-state combinational logic directly inside TopModule. This means TopModule not only contains the flip-flop and mux but also computes the next bit based on two other bits from the three-bit state vector. To enable this, add two additional inputs to TopModule for the bits it needs to XOR for its next state calculation. Inside TopModule, when L=0, the Q output updates with the XOR of these two inputs; when L=1, it loads r_in. This design centralizes the bitwise next-state logic and state storage in each instance, making TopModule self-contained and capturing the logic fully. The top-level full_module then just instantiates three of these, wiring the XOR inputs appropriately for each bit. This approach simplifies external logic and cleanly separates concerns.
