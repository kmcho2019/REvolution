To improve the original TopModule, we focus on enhancing synthesizability and robustness without changing the interface. The initial block for Q initialization is generally not synthesizable in hardware and may cause mismatch issues in some tools or FPGA implementations. Instead, we introduce a synchronous reset via the existing load signal L, which acts like a synchronous load enable: when L=1, Q loads r_in synchronously; otherwise, Q shifts in q_in.

This means Q is always initialized synchronously by the first assertion of L, avoiding unknown initial states and mismatches. We remove the initial block, improving synthesis compatibility.

Additionally, the mux logic is kept simple and minimal to maintain a short critical path, which is optimal for performance and power. Since L functions as a synchronous load signal, we do not add any asynchronous resets or enables that could increase power or complexity.

This preserves the intended design functionality, enhances robustness, and is suitable for synthesis in ASIC or FPGA flows.
