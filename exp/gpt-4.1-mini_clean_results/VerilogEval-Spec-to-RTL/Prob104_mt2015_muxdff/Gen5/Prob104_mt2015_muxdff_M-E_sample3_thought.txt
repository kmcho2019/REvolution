Implement the TopModule by explicitly instantiating two submodules: a 2:1 multiplexer and a D flip-flop. The mux selects between r_in and q_in based on L and outputs the selected data to d. The D flip-flop samples d at the rising edge of clk, producing Q. This approach explicitly models the hierarchy and clearly separates the mux and flip-flop functions as independent components, reflecting modular design principles and enhancing readability and testability.
