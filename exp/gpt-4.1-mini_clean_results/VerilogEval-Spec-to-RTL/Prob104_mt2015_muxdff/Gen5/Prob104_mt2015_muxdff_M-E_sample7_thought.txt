Implement TopModule as a combined module that internally instantiates a 2:1 multiplexer followed by a D flip-flop. The multiplexer selects between r_in (the load input) and a combinational input q_in (the feedback input) based on the load signal L. The D flip-flop stores the selected input on the rising edge of clk and outputs Q. This clear separation inside TopModule matches the intended hierarchy and provides flexibility to incorporate the external combinational logic (like XOR and bit rearrangement) outside each instance of TopModule in full_module. This approach preserves modularity and hierarchical clarity without mixing combinational logic inside the flip-flop module.
