Create a TopModule that implements one bit of the 3-bit register. It includes:
- A 2:1 multiplexer to select between the loaded input (r_in) or the feedback/input from the next stage (q_in),
- A flip-flop triggered on the positive edge of clk,
- The load control signal L to choose whether to load the r_in or shift the previous q_in,
- Output Q which is the stored flip-flop bit.

This TopModule will be instantiated three times by full_module, each instance handling one bit of q and r.
