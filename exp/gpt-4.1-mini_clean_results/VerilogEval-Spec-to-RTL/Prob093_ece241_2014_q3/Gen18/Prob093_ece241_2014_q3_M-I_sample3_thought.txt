However, the feedback suggests simulation mismatches. The likely cause is the interpretation of cd order or the mapping between c and d to cd.

Assuming:

- c is more significant bit of cd, d less significant, i.e. cd = {c,d} in that order:

Then cd=00 means c=0,d=0; cd=01 c=0,d=1; cd=11 c=1,d=1; cd=10 c=1,d=0.

The assignment above is consistent with that assumption.

Possibility is that the inputs c and d are swapped or interpreted in reverse order.

If we swap c and d interpretation (cd = {d,c}), then cd order:

11 → d=1,c=1

10 → d=1,c=0
