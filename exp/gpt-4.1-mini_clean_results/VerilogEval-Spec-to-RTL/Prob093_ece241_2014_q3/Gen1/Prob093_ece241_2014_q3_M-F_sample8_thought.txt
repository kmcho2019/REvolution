The main issue was a misunderstanding of how the K-map rows and columns relate to the inputs and the mux inputs. The selectors `a` and `b` of the 4-to-1 mux are external; `TopModule` only implements the inputs `mux_in[3:0]` (4 bits), each a function of `c` and `d`.

Each mux_in[i] corresponds to the K-map column for the corresponding `ab` selector value:

- ab = 00 → mux_in[0]
- ab = 01 → mux_in[1]
- ab = 11 → mux_in[2]
- ab = 10 → mux_in[3]
