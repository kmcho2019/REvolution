Let's reinterpret the problem and propose a different approach.

We want a function F(a,b,c,d) defined by the given K-map, where (a,b) select one of four mux_in inputs to a 4-to-1 mux, and each mux_in[i] is a function of (c,d) only, implemented using only 2-to-1 muxes and constants (no other gates).

The key is that the 4-to-1 mux is outside our module and uses (a,b) as selector inputs. We must provide mux_in[3:0] = {mux_in[3], mux_in[2], mux_in[1], mux_in[0]} that correspond to ab = 00,01,11,10 respectively.

The K-map is:

      ab
  cd  00  01  11  10
  00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
  01 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
  11 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
  10 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

Where rows are cd in order: 00,01,11,10 (top to bottom).

So each mux_in[i] is a function of (c,d) with values given by column i of the K-map.
