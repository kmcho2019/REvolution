We need to generate 4 signals `mux_in[3:0]` that depend only on inputs `c` and `d`, corresponding to the K-map columns selected by `{a,b}` for an external 4-to-1 mux with selector `{a,b}`.

Given the K-map:

      ab
  cd  00  01  11  10
  00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
  01 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
  11 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
  10 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

We must produce `mux_in[0]` (ab=00), `mux_in[1]` (ab=01), `mux_in[2]` (ab=11), and `mux_in[3]` (ab=10) functions of `c` and `d`.

We only can use 2-to-1 multiplexers inside TopModule and constants.

We'll implement each `mux_in[i]` as a small mux tree with inputs constants and selectors `c` and `d`. For compactness, use `d` as the top selector and `c` as the bottom selector. This matches the 2-bit `cd` input order: `d` is MSB, `c` is LSB.

For each `mux_in[i]`, we use two 2-to-1 muxes to select between `cd=00 & 01` and `cd=10 & 11` pairs on `c` selector, then a top mux on `d`.

This keeps the implementation minimal and clear, strictly with 2-to-1 muxes and constants.

Note: Some columns have constant zero outputs, so can assign `0` directly.

This yields a simple and correct implementation matching the K-map.
