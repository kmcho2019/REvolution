The original design is already optimal with simple combinational logic, minimal area, and low power for this functionality. However, a slight improvement in power can be achieved by sharing the common fan-on condition logic to avoid duplicate evaluation of heater and aircon signals when computing fan output. By introducing intermediate signals for heater and aircon, fan logic only ORs these intermediate signals plus fan_on, preventing re-evaluation of complex conditions multiple times if synthesized gates are not perfectly optimized. This can marginally reduce power and area by allowing the synthesis tool to better optimize shared expressions. Functionally and timing-wise, the change is neutral, but it improves readability and encourages optimal gate sharing.
