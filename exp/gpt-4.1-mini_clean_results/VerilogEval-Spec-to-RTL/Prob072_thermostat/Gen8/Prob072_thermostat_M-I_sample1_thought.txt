The original design is already minimal and efficient, with direct combinational logic and no unnecessary signals or state elements. The only minor enhancement possible is to remove the intermediate wires and directly assign the outputs, slightly reducing the number of nets and simplifying the code. This can reduce routing complexity and thus marginally improve area and power. We keep the fan logic as a single OR of heater, aircon, and fan_on signals to avoid redundant logic or fan toggling. This tiny refinement optimizes the code for PPA without sacrificing clarity.
