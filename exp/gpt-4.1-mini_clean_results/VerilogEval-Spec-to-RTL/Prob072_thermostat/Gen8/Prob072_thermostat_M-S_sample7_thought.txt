The original solution is already minimal and efficient, but the logic can be expressed more succinctly by directly combining the conditions within the fan assignment, thereby eliminating the need for separate heater and aircon signals for the fan logic. Since the heater and aircon are simple AND operations gated by mode, the fan can be assigned by directly OR'ing those conditions with fan_on. This reduces the number of intermediate signals used while preserving clarity, functionality, and simplicity.
