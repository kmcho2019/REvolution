The original combinational design is already minimal, but the fan output logic can be optimized to reduce gate fan-in and potentially lower switching activity. Instead of OR'ing heater, aircon, and fan_on signals directly, fan can be driven by the fan_on signal ORed with a combined signal indicating whether heating or cooling is active. This slight restructuring could reduce logic depth and switching activity, improving power and timing marginally. However, since the original design is already optimal in area and performance for such a simple logic, changes are minor. I will implement this change by creating an intermediate signal "active" indicating if heater or aircon is on, then fan = active | fan_on.
