The existing solution uses minimal combinational logic directly mapping inputs to outputs with straightforward AND, OR, and NOT operations. Given the already minimal combinational nature, there is limited scope for reducing power or area further purely by logic minimization. 

However, a small optimization can be applied by factoring out the fan control logic. Instead of ORing three signals, we can precompute heater or aircon active and OR that with fan_on, reducing the maximum fan input fan-in by one. This might slightly reduce gate complexity and power.

Additionally, marking outputs explicitly as wires and inputs/outputs as `logic` (if allowed by the tooling) can improve readability and synthesis clarity, but this is stylistic.

Overall, improvements are minimal due to the simplicity of the original design. The primary gain is in slightly reduced gate fan-in on the fan signal logic, which may improve area and power at a micro level.
