The original design is already minimal and efficient; however, to slightly optimize power and area, I will combine the heater and aircon logic into a single control wire using a 2-to-1 multiplexer approach controlled by mode. This removes the explicit NOT gate on mode (~mode) by using a ternary operator and may reduce logic depth and gate count slightly. This compact expression might synthesize to fewer gates as the inverter on mode is replaced by a mux control. The fan logic remains unchanged for clarity and minimal switching. Overall, this change is subtle but can improve area and possibly power by simplifying the mode inversion handling.
