The original design is already minimal and efficient for its simple logic. To potentially improve power, we can avoid redundant fan logic by gating the fan output only once after determining the heater and aircon states. Since heater and aircon already involve mode and temperature signals, the fan logic can be simplified by generating heater and aircon signals first, then OR them with fan_on to produce fan output. This avoids any recomputation or logic duplication. While the impact is minor, this minor rearrangement can slightly reduce switching by limiting changes on the fan signal to essential conditions only.
