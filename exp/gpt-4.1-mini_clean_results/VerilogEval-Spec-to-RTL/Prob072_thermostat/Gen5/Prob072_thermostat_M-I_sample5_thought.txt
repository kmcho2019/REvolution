The original design is already minimal and optimal in terms of logic complexity and area, given the simplicity of the required functionality. To further improve power and area slightly, we can avoid intermediate wire declarations by directly assigning outputs, reducing netlist overhead. We also can combine heater and aircon logic in the fan control to a single expression without introducing extra wires, relying on synthesis tools to optimize.

Additionally, since the fan must be on when heater or aircon is on or fan_on is asserted, and heater and aircon are mutually exclusive due to the mode input, we can simplify the fan logic slightly by recognizing that only one of heater or aircon can be on at a time. However, this optimization is minor.

In summary, the changes are:
- Remove explicit intermediate signals.
- Use direct continuous assignments.
- Keep logic combinational and minimal.

This results in slightly reduced area and possibly power due to fewer nets, while maintaining clarity.
