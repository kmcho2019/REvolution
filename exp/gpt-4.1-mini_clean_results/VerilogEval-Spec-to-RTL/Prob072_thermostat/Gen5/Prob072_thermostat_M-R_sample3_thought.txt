To refactor the design while maintaining the same logic, I will replace the combinational continuous assignments with a combinational always block. This approach uses a single always block sensitive to all inputs to compute outputs with procedural assignments. It demonstrates an alternative style preferred by some designers for clarity or tool compatibility.

The behavior remains combinational and simple, but the outputs are assigned inside an always block instead of using multiple assign statements. This also avoids intermediate wire declarations by directly assigning outputs in the block. This refactoring shows a different style with equivalent functionality and minimal logic.

Specifically:
- Use `always @(*)` block.
- Assign outputs (`heater`, `aircon`, `fan`) procedurally.
- Compute heater and aircon signals directly in the block.
- Derive fan signal from heater, aircon, and fan_on.
