To optimize the original solution for better power and area, the intermediate wire `heating_or_cooling` can be removed by directly combining the heater and aircon conditions into the fan output expression. This reduces logic levels and nets, simplifying the design slightly and potentially lowering power and area. The logic remains straightforward and fully combinational, preserving performance.

The heater and aircon remain mutually exclusive controlled by mode, so their signals can be directly OR'ed with `fan_on` for the fan output without using an extra signal. This small change reduces the gate count and net load, providing minor but meaningful PPA gains while maintaining the original functionality.
