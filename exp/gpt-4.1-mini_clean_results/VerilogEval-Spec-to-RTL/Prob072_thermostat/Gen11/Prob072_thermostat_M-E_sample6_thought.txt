To create a structurally different design, I will use a small combinational always block with if-else statements instead of purely continuous assignments. This approach encapsulates the control logic in procedural code, improving readability for some users and allowing easy future extension. The outputs will be driven by reg variables updated combinationally whenever inputs change, ensuring synthesizable logic. The fan output will be asserted if the heater or aircon are on, or if the user requests it, consistent with requirements. This stylistic change provides a distinct architectural style without changing the functionality.
