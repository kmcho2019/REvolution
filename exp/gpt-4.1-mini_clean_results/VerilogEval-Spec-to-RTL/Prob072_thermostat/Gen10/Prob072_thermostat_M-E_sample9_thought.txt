Instead of directly assigning heater and aircon outputs based on inputs, implement the controller as a simple state machine with three states: IDLE, HEATING, and COOLING. The state machine transitions to HEATING when mode=1 and too_cold=1, to COOLING when mode=0 and too_hot=1, and otherwise stays IDLE. Heater is on only in HEATING state, aircon only in COOLING state, and fan is on when in HEATING or COOLING state or when fan_on is asserted. This approach introduces explicit state management, which could be beneficial if we later extend functionality, while still adhering to original spec.
