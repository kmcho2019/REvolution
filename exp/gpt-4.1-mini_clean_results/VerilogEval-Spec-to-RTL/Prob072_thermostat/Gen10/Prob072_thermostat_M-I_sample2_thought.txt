The original design is already minimal and clean with simple combinational logic. However, a small potential optimization to reduce switching power and area is to avoid redundant recalculation of the fan signal by combining the heater and aircon signals into an intermediate wire. This does not change functionality or timing but may help synthesis tools optimize shared logic for the fan signal more effectively. The small addition of intermediate signals aids readability and can assist some synthesis tools in resource sharing and gate-level optimization, slightly improving power and area. Performance impact remains negligible since all logic is simple combinational.
