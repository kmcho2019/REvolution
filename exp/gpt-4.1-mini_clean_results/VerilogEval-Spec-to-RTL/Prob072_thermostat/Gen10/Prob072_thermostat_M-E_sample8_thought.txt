Implement the thermostat controller using a simple sequential state-holding approach with an always block triggered by any input change. Use internal registers to hold the outputs heater and aircon based on the mode and temperature signals, then combine those with the user fan_on request to drive the fan output. This approach demonstrates an alternative design using always blocks and registers rather than continuous assignments, which may be preferable in some synthesis contexts or to illustrate explicit combinational logic behavior without intermediate wires.
