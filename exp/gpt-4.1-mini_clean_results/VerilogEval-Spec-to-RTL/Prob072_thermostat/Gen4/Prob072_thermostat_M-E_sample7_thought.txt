Instead of generating separate signals for heater and air conditioner and then combining them for the fan, I will create a single state machine with three states: OFF, HEATING, and COOLING. The state is determined combinationally based on the mode and temperature inputs. Then, based on the state, outputs heater, aircon, and fan are assigned accordingly. The fan is also turned on if fan_on is asserted regardless of the state. This architectural approach uses an enumerated state variable internally for clarity and explicit control of outputs, which can be helpful if future extension or debugging is needed.
