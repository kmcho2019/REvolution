The original design is already minimal and efficient, using straightforward combinational logic with minimal gates, yielding optimal area and power. However, a small optimization to potentially reduce switching activity and power consumption is to avoid redundant signal generation. For instance, the fan output can be assigned as the OR of a single signal representing "heat or cool active" and the user fan_on input. This avoids recomputing heater and aircon multiple times and may help some synthesis tools optimize better. Also, combining heater and aircon into one internal wire may enable optimizations. The performance and area impact will be minimal but this is a cleaner representation while maintaining the same logic and functionality.
