The original design is correct but the fan output logic can be simplified. The fan should be on if heater or aircon is on, or if fan_on is on regardless. The current logic uses a more complex expression for fan, but simplifying it to `fan = heater | aircon | fan_on` reduces gate count and switching activity, thereby improving power and area slightly. Sharing the common sub-expression `(heater | aircon)` helps readability but is optional since the logic is minimal. This simplification keeps performance the same and is optimal for PPA.
