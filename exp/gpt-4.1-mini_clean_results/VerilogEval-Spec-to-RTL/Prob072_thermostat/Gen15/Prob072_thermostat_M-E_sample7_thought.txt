Implement the thermostat control using a sequential always block with a simple state encoding to explicitly represent whether the heater or aircon is ON or OFF. This approach stores the control signals in registers that update based on the inputs, allowing for potential extension to add hysteresis or state-based features later. The fan output is driven combinationally from the heater, aircon, and fan_on signals. Using sequential logic can aid in debugging or integration into clocked systems while maintaining the correct functional behavior.
