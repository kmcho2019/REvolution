Implement the thermostat controller using a case statement in an always_comb block that explicitly enumerates the two modes: heating and cooling. Inside each mode, assign heater and aircon accordingly, and then drive the fan output based on whether either heater or aircon is active, or the user requested the fan. This approach uses procedural combinational logic with registers (wire/reg) for outputs, providing a different structural style than purely continuous assignments. It still realizes all functions with minimal logic but demonstrates a behavioral approach suitable for easy extension and readability.
