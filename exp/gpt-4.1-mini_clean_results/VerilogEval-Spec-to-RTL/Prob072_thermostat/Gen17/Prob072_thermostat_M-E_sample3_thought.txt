I will implement the thermostat controller using a small combinational priority encoder style approach with case statements inside an always_comb block to explicitly handle the different modes. This structure makes the logic more explicit and easily extendable for future modes. In heating mode, the heater turns on when too_cold is asserted, ignoring aircon. In cooling mode, aircon turns on when too_hot is asserted, ignoring heater. The fan turns on whenever heater or aircon is active or when fan_on is asserted. Using always_comb ensures clean combinational logic and grouping assignments inside a single block improves readability and maintainability compared to multiple continuous assignments.
