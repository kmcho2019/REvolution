To improve PPA (power, performance, area) slightly, reduce switching activity in the fan output by gating the fan_on input with the heater and aircon signals. Instead of OR-ing fan_on directly with heater and aircon, enable fan_on only if no heating or cooling is active. This avoids unnecessary toggling of the fan signal when heater or aircon is already on, potentially saving power with minimal added logic. The heater and aircon assignments remain unchanged for simplicity and minimal area. This optimization trades a small increase in logic for reduced toggling on the fan output.
