I will refactor the module using continuous assign statements instead of an always block. The outputs will be declared as wire (default for outputs without reg). This approach simplifies the logic into combinational expressions directly, improving succinctness and potentially synthesis friendliness. The heater and aircon are driven by the mode and temperature signals using logical AND operations. The fan output is driven by the OR of heater, aircon, and fan_on signals. This eliminates the need for procedural blocks and reg declarations, making the design purely declarative.
