I will implement the module by explicitly defining the fan enable signal as a wire that depends on the mode and temperature conditions, and separately combining the user fan request. To reduce combinational logic depth, I will use multiplexers (ternary operators) to select the active device (heater or aircon) based on the mode and temperature, assigning the outputs accordingly. The fan is driven by the OR of the active device and user fan request. This approach leverages multiplexing to simplify logic expression, making it easy to extend later (e.g., adding more modes) while maintaining clarity and minimizing gate count.
