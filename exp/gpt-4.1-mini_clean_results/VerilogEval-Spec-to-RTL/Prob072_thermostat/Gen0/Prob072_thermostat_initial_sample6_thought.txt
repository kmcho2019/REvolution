The module will use combinational logic based on the mode and temperature inputs to control heater, aircon, and fan outputs. In heating mode (mode=1), heater is on if too_cold=1, and aircon is off. In cooling mode (mode=0), aircon is on if too_hot=1, and heater is off. The fan is on if either heater or aircon is on, or if fan_on is requested by the user.
