Implement the thermostat controller as a small FSM with states representing the fan status: OFF, FAN_ON. Based on inputs mode, too_cold, and too_hot, the FSM transitions between states and controls heater, aircon, and fan outputs accordingly. In the FAN_ON state, fan remains on if heater or aircon is active or fan_on input remains asserted; otherwise it returns to OFF. The FSM approach explicitly manages the fan operation as a state rather than purely combinational, which could be extended in future for timing or hysteresis. This separates control logic and allows a clocked design with registers, which might help in certain synchronous designs requiring stable outputs synchronized to a clock.
