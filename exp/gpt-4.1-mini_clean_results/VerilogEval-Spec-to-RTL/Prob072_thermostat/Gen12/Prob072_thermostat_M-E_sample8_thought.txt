Implement the thermostat controller using a priority encoder style logic: first, determine the active mode (heating or cooling) and based on that, activate only the corresponding device (heater or aircon) if the temperature conditions are met. Use conditional (ternary) operators to explicitly define mutually exclusive control signals. The fan control is then driven by the presence of any active HVAC device or the user-requested fan_on. This approach emphasizes explicit mutual exclusivity and clear separation of mode-dependent logic, making the design's intent very transparent and suitable for easy future expansion.
