To slightly optimize power and area, avoid redundant repeated logic by computing common conditions once and reusing them. The heater and aircon signals are mutually exclusive due to mode, so compute those conditions first. Also, for the fan, OR the precomputed heater or aircon signals with fan_on to avoid repeating the same logic. This reduces gate fanout and might reduce area and dynamic power due to less redundant logic evaluation, while keeping the design purely combinational and simple.
