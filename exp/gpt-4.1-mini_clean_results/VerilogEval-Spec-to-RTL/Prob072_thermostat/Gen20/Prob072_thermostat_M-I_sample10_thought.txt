The original design is already minimal and functionally correct. Since the logic is simple and purely combinational with only three outputs dependent on straightforward AND/OR operations, the critical path and area are minimal. However, I can marginally reduce the switching activity on the fan output by gating the fan_on input only when heater and aircon are both off, since if either heater or aircon is active, the fan must be on anyway. This could help slightly reduce unnecessary toggling of the fan when only fan_on changes while heater or aircon is off, potentially lowering dynamic power. This comes with slightly increased logic but still keeps the design combinational and simple.
