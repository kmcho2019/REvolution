The current combinational design is already minimal and meets all functional requirements perfectly. The logical expressions are straightforward, and since there is no clocking or state, performance, power, and area are already optimal for such simple logic.

To seek a potential slight improvement in power and area, we can merge the heater and aircon signals to a single intermediate signal indicating "active heating or cooling" and use it for the fan. This can reduce redundant signal recomputation and may help synthesis tools optimize the logic better. Also, using a single wire for this combined signal can avoid duplicate fan logic that separately ORs heater and aircon.

This does not change functional behavior but slightly cleans up the logic structure, potentially leading to minimal area and power savings by reducing duplicated logic and signal toggling.

Hence, the improvement strategy:
- Introduce a single intermediate wire (active) that combines heater and aircon conditions.
- Assign heater and aircon from mode and temperature conditions.
- Assign fan as active OR fan_on.
This simple restructuring might help synthesis optimize further without changing function.
