Fuse the best ideas by combining the clarity and structural advantages of the always_comb style with the optimized logic reuse from the first example. Compute heater and aircon enable signals once, then assign outputs inside a single always_comb block to improve readability and future extensibility. This approach keeps the design purely combinational, groups related logic together, avoids redundant expressions, and uses nonblocking assignments for clarity. The fan output is derived from the precomputed heater and aircon signals ORed with fan_on, minimizing redundant logic and enhancing maintainability.
