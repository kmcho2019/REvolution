Refactor the module to use an always_comb block for combinational logic instead of continuous assign statements. This approach groups the related output assignments in one procedural block, improving readability and maintainability when scaling or modifying logic. The outputs heater, aircon, and fan are declared as reg to be assigned inside the always block, preserving the same logical behavior: heater on when in heating mode and too cold; aircon on when in cooling mode and too hot; fan on if heater or aircon is on or user requests fan_on.
