The original design is already minimal and straightforward, implementing purely combinational logic. However, to optimize power and area slightly, we can avoid redundant evaluation of heater and aircon signals when mode changes by generating an enable signal for the active mode and gating the outputs accordingly. This could reduce switching activity and thus power, and may slightly reduce area if synthesis tools capitalize on the gated conditions. Also, reusing the intermediate mode-based control signal reduces duplicated logic for fan output. The overall logic remains purely combinational and simple.
