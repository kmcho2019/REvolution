To maintain the corrected 8-bit output mux functionality but implement it with a different structure, I will use an always_comb block with a procedural assignment to the output. This approach offers clear separation of combinational logic and is synthesizable. The logic simply assigns 'out' to 'a' or 'b' depending on 'sel', thus preserving the behavior while providing an alternative structural style to the continuous assignment with a ternary operator.
