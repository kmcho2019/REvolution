Implement Rule 110 using a shift-register style approach that updates cells serially over multiple clock cycles rather than all in parallel. Use a small 3-cell sliding window that moves through the 512-cell state sequentially, calculating next states and storing them in a dual-port RAM or two registers acting as ping-pong buffers. On each clock cycle, update one cell based on its neighbors and once a full pass is done (512 cycles), swap buffers. The load signal instantly loads data into the current buffer. This time-multiplexed approach reduces combinational logic complexity and area at the cost of throughput, trading parallelism for resource saving.
