Implement the 512-cell Rule 110 automaton as a single 512-bit shift register with combinational next-state logic computed using bitwise vector operations and carefully shifted versions of the current state vector. To handle boundary conditions, concatenate zeros at both ends. 

This approach uses vectorized operations to derive left, center, and right neighbor bit vectors by left and right shifting the current state with zero padding. The next state is computed simultaneously for all bits with simple bitwise expressions matching Rule 110 logic:

Next[i] = (~Left[i] & Center[i]) | (Center[i] ^ Right[i])
