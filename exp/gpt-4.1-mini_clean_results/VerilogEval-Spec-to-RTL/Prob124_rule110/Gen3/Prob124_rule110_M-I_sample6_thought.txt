To optimize for better PPA, I will focus on simplifying the combinational logic expression for the Rule 110 next state to reduce gate count and critical path delay. The original formula is:

  next = (~left & center) | (center ^ right)

We can rewrite this as:

  next = center & (~left | right) | (~center & right)

However, after Karnaugh map analysis, the minimal sum-of-products form of Rule 110 is:

  next = (center & ~left) | (center & ~right) | (~center & right)

Implementing this simplified form reduces gate count and potentially critical path, helping timing and area. Also, I will use a generate-for loop with a single always_comb block to compute all next_state bits together, reducing logic duplication overhead. 

The padding will remain as before for boundary conditions. The synchronous logic stays unchanged.

This should reduce logic complexity and delay, slightly improving PPA without changing functionality.
