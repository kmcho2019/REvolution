To improve PPA (Power, Performance, and Area), I will focus on reducing switching activity and simplifying combinational logic. Instead of padding and indexing large vectors for each bit, I will implement the combinational logic using bitwise operations across the entire 512-bit vector to compute neighbors in parallel. This will reduce gate count and wiring complexity by avoiding per-bit generate loops. I will also implement registered outputs for left, center, and right neighbors to improve timing through pipelining, trading one cycle of latency for better clock frequency and reduced critical path. Since load is infrequent, I will implement a clock-enable gating of the register so the main state only updates when load is high or when the system advances, reducing unnecessary toggling. This approach balances complexity and optimizes timing and power by minimizing combinational depth and switching. The neighbor bits are obtained by shifting q left and right, inserting zero for boundaries, enabling fully vectorized logic.
