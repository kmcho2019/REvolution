Refactoring Strategy:
- The original design uses a parameterized DFF module with internal inversion of the input.
- Instead, this refactoring will separate the inversion of 'a' explicitly in the top module using a simple combinational assign.
- The DFF module will be a clean, conventional positive-edge triggered flip-flop without parameterization or internal logic changes.
- This makes the design clearer and modular by keeping combinational logic outside sequential logic.
- The explicit inversion outside the DFF reduces module complexity and improves reusability of the DFF as a pure storage element.
- This approach also helps slightly with power by isolating inversion toggling outside the sequential logic.
- Functionally, the output 'q' is the registered inversion of input 'a' sampled on the rising edge of clk, matching the waveform.
