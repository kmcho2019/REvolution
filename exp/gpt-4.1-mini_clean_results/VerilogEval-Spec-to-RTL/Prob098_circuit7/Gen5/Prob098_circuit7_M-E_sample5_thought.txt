From the waveform, output q changes only on the rising edge of clk, and it follows the previous value of a (sampled at the prior rising edge), but inverted. More specifically, q at time t (rising edge) = ~a at previous rising edge. Therefore, we can implement q as a flip-flop output that samples the inverted value of input a synchronously on clk's rising edge, using a behavioral style directly within the TopModule without separate modules or intermediate wires for simplicity and compactness.
