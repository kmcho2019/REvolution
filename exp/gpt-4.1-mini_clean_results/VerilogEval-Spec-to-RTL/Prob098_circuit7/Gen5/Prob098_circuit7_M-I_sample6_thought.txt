Integrate the inversion of 'a' into the sequential logic by directly assigning 'q' as the inverted value of 'a' inside the clocked always block. This eliminates the separate combinational assign and the separate registered variable, reducing logic and wiring. This approach slightly improves timing and area by removing the inverter gate and shortens the critical path.
