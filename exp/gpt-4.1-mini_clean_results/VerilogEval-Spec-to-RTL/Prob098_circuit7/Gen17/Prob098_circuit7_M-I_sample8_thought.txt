Improvement Strategy:
- The original solution uses a parameterized DFF with internal inversion, which adds extra combinational logic inside the flip-flop module.
- To improve PPA (power, performance, area), remove the parameterization and internal inversion from the DFF module.
- Perform the inversion explicitly outside the DFF on the 'a' input before feeding it to a simple DFF.
- This eliminates unnecessary combinational gates inside the DFF, reducing area and power.
- The code remains simple, modular, and functionally equivalent but with fewer gates and simpler logic, which can improve timing slightly.
