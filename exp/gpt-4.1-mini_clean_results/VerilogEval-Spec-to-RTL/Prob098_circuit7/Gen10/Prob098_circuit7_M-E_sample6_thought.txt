Implement the sequential logic as a T flip-flop that toggles its state only when input 'a' is low at the positive clock edge; when 'a' is high, the output 'q' resets to 0. This behavior matches the waveform where 'q' sets to 1 when a=0 at clock edge and resets to 0 when a=1 at clock edge. Using a T flip-flop style enables direct encoding of the state transition without needing explicit data or inversion, making the design compact and elegant.
