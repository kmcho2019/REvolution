Fusion Strategy:
- Use a simple, clear, and reusable DFF module as in both examples to maintain modularity.
- Incorporate the inversion directly inside the DFF module via a parameterized input inversion flag to eliminate extra combinational logic and signals outside the flip-flop.
- This reduces routing and toggling caused by separate inverter gates, improving power and timing slightly.
- It keeps the top-level module interface simple and clean without explicit inversion logic, while allowing reuse of the DFF with or without inversion.
- The DFF module remains a single always block with a blocking assignment style that matches best practice, using non-blocking for sequential logic.
- Overall, the DFF module abstracts inversion internally for cleaner RTL, reduces area by avoiding separate inverter gates, and improves timing/power by minimizing switching and routing overhead.
