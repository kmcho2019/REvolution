The original design implements q as the inverted value of input a captured on the positive clock edge using a flip-flop with internal inversion. To refactor this, I will keep the behavior identical but change the structure by first registering 'a' normally in a flip-flop, then assigning 'q' as the complement of that registered value using a continuous assignment. This separates the sequential and combinational logic clearly: the flip-flop captures 'a' directly, and the inversion happens combinationally after the register. This might aid readability or synthesis in some flows while preserving the same functional behavior and timing.
