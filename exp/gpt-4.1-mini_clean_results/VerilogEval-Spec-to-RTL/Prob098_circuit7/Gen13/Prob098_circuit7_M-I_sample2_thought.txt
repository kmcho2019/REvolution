The current solution correctly implements q as the delayed inversion of input a on the positive edge of clk using a separate DFF module. To optimize PPA, we can simplify by integrating the flip-flop logic directly inside TopModule to remove the module boundary overhead. This inline style also reduces the number of module instantiations, easing place-and-route and timing closure.
