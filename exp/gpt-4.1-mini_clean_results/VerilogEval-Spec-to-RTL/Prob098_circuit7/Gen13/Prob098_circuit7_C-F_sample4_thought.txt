Advantages of this fusion:
- Explicit combinational inversion before the DFF improves transparency and potential logic sharing.
- The DFF remains a simple standard positive-edge triggered register without embedded inversion, easing reuse.
- Avoids parameterization complexity, enabling straightforward synthesis and optimization.
- Maintains a very short critical path (one inverter and one flip-flop).
- Helps tools optimize the inverter separately for power and area.
- Clean, minimal, and easily maintainable RTL code.
