The current design implements q as the negation of input 'a' registered on the rising edge of clk. While functionally correct and minimal, it instantiates a separate DFF module and an explicit inverter wire. To optimize for better PPA, I will combine the inverter inside the always block and use a non-blocking assignment directly, removing the intermediate wire and extra module. This reduces module overhead, interconnect delay, and area. The simplification still maps to the same functionality: q <= ~a at every positive clock edge.
