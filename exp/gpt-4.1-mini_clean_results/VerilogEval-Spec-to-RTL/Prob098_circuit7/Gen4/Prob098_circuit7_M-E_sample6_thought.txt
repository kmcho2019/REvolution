Analyze the waveform reveals that q follows the inverted value of 'a' sampled on the rising edge of clk. Instead of directly inverting 'a' and feeding to a DFF, this solution uses a register to store 'a' on each clock edge, then outputs the inverse of that stored value as 'q'. This separates the storage and inversion steps explicitly, demonstrating the use of an internal register and a continuous assignment for output, while maintaining identical functionality.
