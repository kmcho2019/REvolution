Analyze the waveform shows that q follows the inverted value of a sampled at the positive clock edge, i.e., q <= ~a on posedge clk. Instead of directly implementing q as the negation of a at the clock edge, I will implement this behavior using a toggle flip-flop (TFF) architecture. Specifically, I will design a TFF internally that toggles q only when a is 1. When a=0, q remains stable. This is equivalent to q <= ~a, since toggling on a=1 flips q, and not toggling on a=0 keeps q the same. To achieve this, I will compute T = a XOR q; at the clock edge, if T=1, q toggles; else, q stays. This demonstrates an alternative approach using T flip-flop behavior rather than a direct D flip-flop, achieving the same functionality in a different architecture.
