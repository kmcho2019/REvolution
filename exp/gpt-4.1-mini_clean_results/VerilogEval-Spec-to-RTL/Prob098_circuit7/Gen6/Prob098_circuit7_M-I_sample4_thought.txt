To optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA), we can eliminate the explicit inverter wire by directly assigning the negation of 'a' inside the always block of a single DFF flip-flop. This removes the combinational inverter logic and simplifies the design to a single always block, potentially reducing power and area. The functionality remains the same since q captures ~a on the positive clock edge. This reduces gate count and critical path complexity by combining inversion and flip-flop input.
