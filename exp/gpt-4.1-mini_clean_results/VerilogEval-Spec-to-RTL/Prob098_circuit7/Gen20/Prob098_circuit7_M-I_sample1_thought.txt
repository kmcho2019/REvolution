Improvement Strategy:
The previous design encapsulated input inversion inside the DFF module using a parameter, which adds extra logic and parameterization overhead. Since the problem requires a DFF that stores the inverted value of 'a' on the rising edge of clk, the simplest and most power/area-efficient approach is to directly invert 'a' outside the flip-flop input and instantiate a plain DFF.

This removes extra combinational inversion inside the DFF, reduces gate count, switching activity, and routing complexity, thereby improving power and area. Also, removing the parameterization simplifies synthesis and verification.

Hence, implement a simple DFF storing inverted 'a' by assigning d = ~a, and use a standard DFF with non-blocking assignments on clk's positive edge.

This clean and minimalistic RTL matches the waveform and improves PPA metrics while preserving the exact functionality.
