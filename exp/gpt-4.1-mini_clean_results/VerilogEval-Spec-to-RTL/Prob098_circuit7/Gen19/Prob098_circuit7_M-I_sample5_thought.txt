The given solution implements q as the DFF output of the inverted input a (~a) sampled at the positive clock edge, which perfectly matches the waveform behavior.

To improve PPA:

- Since the function is q <= ~a at posedge clk, the logic is minimal. We cannot remove the inverter, as q toggles opposite to a.
- The separate DFF module is modular but adds slight overhead.
- We can merge the inversion inside the always block, directly assigning q <= ~a; avoiding the intermediate wire and separate DFF module, which may reduce area and power slightly by eliminating instantiation overhead and nets.
- This also reduces logic levels, potentially improving timing slightly.
- Using a synchronous reset is not needed as initial q is unknown, matching waveform unknown at time 0.
- The logic remains straightforward, synthesizable, and clear.

Therefore, the improvement is to implement the logic inside TopModule only, directly updating q <= ~a; at posedge clk.

This reduces the module hierarchy and the netlist complexity, which can improve area and power slightly without changing behavior.
