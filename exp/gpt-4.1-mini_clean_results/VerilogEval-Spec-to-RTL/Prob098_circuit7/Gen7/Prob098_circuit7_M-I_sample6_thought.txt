The original design registers input 'a' and outputs the inverted registered value 'q' via a combinational assign. To improve PPA (performance, power, area), integrate the inversion into the sequential block by directly assigning q as a reg inside the always block. This eliminates the separate combinational inverter gate and associated wiring, reducing area and potentially improving timing. Additionally, this approach reduces switching power by ensuring q only changes synchronously with the clock edge. This keeps the design minimal and clean without extra combinational paths.
