To improve the existing design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will eliminate the explicit inverter by integrating the inversion inside the sequential logic. This reduces combinational logic and potentially lowers switching activity and area.

Specifically:
- Replace the separate inverter wire with a DFF that directly captures the inverse of 'a' on the rising clock edge using combinational inversion inside the always block.
- This avoids a separate inverter gate and saves area and dynamic power.
- Maintain non-blocking assignments and modular clarity by implementing the flip-flop with inversion internally rather than relying on an external wire.
