The original design correctly implements a D flip-flop capturing the inverted input `~a` at every positive clock edge, matching the waveform. To improve PPA, we can integrate the inverter inline without a separate gate, as done, but we can simplify further by implementing the entire behavior in a single always block without extra modules. This reduces hierarchy overhead (area), slightly improves synthesis quality, and potentially improves power by reducing net toggle and logic. Also, since the design only requires `q = ~a` sampled on the rising clock edge, a single always block with a non-blocking assignment directly to `q` is the most area- and power-efficient implementation.
