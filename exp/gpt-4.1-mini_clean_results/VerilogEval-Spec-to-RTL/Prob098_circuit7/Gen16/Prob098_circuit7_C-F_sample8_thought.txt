Fuse the modular DFF design with an explicit combinational inversion stage for maximum clarity, reusability, and synthesis friendliness. Instead of inlining the inverter in the DFF instantiation port, create a clearly named wire for the inverted input 'a'. This makes the design easier to read and debug, separates combinational from sequential logic explicitly, and still keeps area and power minimal due to the single-bit width.

This approach keeps the advantages of the modular DFF, facilitates reuse and unit testing, and also provides clear visibility of combinational operations for static timing analysis or power gating.

Overall, this fusion yields a highly readable, modular, and easily maintainable design without sacrificing optimal PPA characteristics.
