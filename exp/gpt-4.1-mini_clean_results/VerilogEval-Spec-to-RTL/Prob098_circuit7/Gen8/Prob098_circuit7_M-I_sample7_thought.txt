The original design correctly implements q as the delayed inverted value of a. To improve performance, power, and area (PPA), we can integrate the inversion inside the flip-flop data path by using an inverted D input to the flip-flop directly, thus eliminating the separate combinational inverter. This merges combinational and sequential logic, reducing delay and area by removing a standalone inverter gate. The single flip-flop will now capture the inverted input inline, reducing complexity and potentially power due to fewer transitions and smaller logic footprint. This approach keeps the design simple and efficient without changing the functional behavior.
