The original design is already minimal, using a single D flip-flop with the input inverted. To optimize for power, we can avoid the explicit inverter by leveraging the flip-flop's data input directly with a negated signal inside the always block, removing the intermediate wire and the explicit inverter gate. This saves area and slightly reduces dynamic power by eliminating a separate inverter net. The behavior remains the same, as q is updated to the negation of 'a' on each rising edge of clk.
