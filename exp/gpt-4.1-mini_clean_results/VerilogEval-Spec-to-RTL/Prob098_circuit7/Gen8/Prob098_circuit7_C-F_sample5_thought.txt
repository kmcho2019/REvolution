Combine the modularity and clarity of a dedicated DFF module with the minimal area and power of directly connecting the inverted input to the DFF input without creating an extra net. This yields a clean hierarchical design with explicit separation of sequential logic, facilitates reuse, and avoids redundant wires, thereby optimizing both readability and resource usage. The inverter is applied inline in the module instantiation port, maintaining minimal combinational overhead and reducing unnecessary signal toggling or routing.
