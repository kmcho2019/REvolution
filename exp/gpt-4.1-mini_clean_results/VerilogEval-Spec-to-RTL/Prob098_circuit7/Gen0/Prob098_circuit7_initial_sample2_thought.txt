From the waveform, q is updated only on the positive clock edge. Initially q is unknown. At the first positive edge with a=0, q becomes 1 and stays 1 while a=0. When a becomes 1, at the next positive edge q becomes 0 and remains 0 as long as a=1. So q is a register updated at the positive clock edge with q = ~a. That is, q holds the inverted value of a on each rising clock edge.
