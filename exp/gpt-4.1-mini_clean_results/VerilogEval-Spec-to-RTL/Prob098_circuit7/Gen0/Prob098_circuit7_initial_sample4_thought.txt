The output q is a state that updates on the positive edge of clk. From the waveform:
- At time 15ns (clk rising edge, a=0), q becomes 1.
- q remains 1 on subsequent clk edges when a=0 or a=1, until at time 45ns (clk rising edge, a=1), q resets to 0.
- From 45ns onward, q remains 0 on every clk rising edge with a=1.

So the behavior appears to be: 
- On positive clock edge, if a is 0, q is set to 1.
- On positive clock edge, if a is 1, q is set to 0.

Hence, q is a flip-flop that captures the inverted value of a on every positive clock edge.
