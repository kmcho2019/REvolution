The output q is a state that updates on each positive clock edge based on input a. Observing the waveform:

- At time 15ns (pos edge clk, a=0), q becomes 1.
- At time 35ns (pos edge clk, a=1), q remains 1.
- At time 45ns (pos edge clk, a=1), q transitions to 0.
- Then q stays 0 for subsequent clock edges with a=1.

From the transitions, it appears q is set to 1 when a is 0 at the rising edge of clk, and cleared to 0 when a is 1 at the rising edge of clk.

Hence, the logic is: on posedge clk, q <= ~a.

This matches the waveform values. So the module stores q as the negation of a sampled at the clock's rising edge.
