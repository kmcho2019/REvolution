The output q changes only on the positive edge of the clock. From the waveform:

- At 15ns (posedge clk, a=0), q=1
- At 45ns (posedge clk, a=1), q=0

Between these edges q remains stable. So, q seems to latch the inverse of input a on each positive clock edge.

In other words, q <= ~a on posedge clk.
