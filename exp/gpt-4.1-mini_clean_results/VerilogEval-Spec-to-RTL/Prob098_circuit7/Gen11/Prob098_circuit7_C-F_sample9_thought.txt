Fuse the modularity and clarity of a dedicated DFF module with the optimal power and area efficiency by embedding the inversion of input 'a' inside the DFF module itself. This eliminates the explicit inverter outside, reducing switching and net count. The TopModule remains clean and simple by passing 'a' directly into the DFF, which internally inverts it before registering, thus maintaining clear separation of concerns and simplifying the interface. This structure also facilitates reuse and testability of the DFF module while achieving minimal combinational logic and power consumption, meeting all waveform requirements precisely.
