The original design uses a simple inverter feeding a D flip-flop, which implements q <= ~a on every clock edge. This is functionally correct and minimal in logic. To optimize for power and area further, we can remove the explicit inverter and integrate the inversion into the sequential logic itself by directly inverting 'a' inside the always block. This eliminates the extra inverter gate, potentially reducing area and power with minimal impact on performance. This keeps the code simple and clear without module instantiation overhead.
