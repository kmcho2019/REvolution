Implement a 3-state Mealy FSM to detect the sequence "101" on input x. Use three states representing how much of the sequence has been matched so far: S0 (no match), S1 (matched '1'), S2 (matched '10'). On each clock's positive edge, transition between states based on the input x. The output z is asserted when the sequence "101" is detected, which occurs when in S2 and x=1.
