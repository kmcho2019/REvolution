Implement the Mealy FSM with three one-hot encoded states to simplify combinational logic and clearly identify each state's role in detecting the "101" sequence. Use a negative-edge asynchronous reset that initializes the FSM to the initial state. The FSM transitions on the positive clock edge, with next-state logic computed combinationally based on current state and input. Output z asserts when the sequence "101" is detected at the current input, i.e., when the FSM is in the state representing "10" and input x is '1'. The one-hot encoding allows straightforward next state and output logic, making the design easy to understand and verify. This also enables minimal combinational complexity and inherently supports overlapping sequences by returning to the correct states after detection.
