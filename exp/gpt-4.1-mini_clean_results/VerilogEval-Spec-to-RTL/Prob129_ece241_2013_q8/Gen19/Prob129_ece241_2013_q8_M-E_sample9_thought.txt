Implement the Mealy FSM using one-hot encoding for the three states to achieve simple and fast state decoding. This uses 3 flip-flops where only one is high for each state, which often reduces combinational logic depth at the expense of a few more registers. The FSM detects "101" with overlapping sequences by transitioning among states representing the partial sequence progress: S0 (no match), S1 (matched '1'), S2 (matched "10"). The output z is asserted combinationally on the condition when the FSM transitions from S2 to S1 with input x=1 (i.e., the sequence "101" just completed). The state register is updated on the positive clock edge, and asynchronous active-low reset is triggered on the negative edge of aresetn to immediately initialize the FSM to S0. The output logic and next-state logic are coded with clear case statements for readability and explicitness. This one-hot encoding can improve timing by minimizing logic levels in state decoding and make the FSM more robust and simple to optimize by synthesis tools.
