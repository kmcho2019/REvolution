To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will streamline the FSM next-state and output logic by combining them into a single always block that computes both next_state and output z, thereby reducing combinational logic duplication. This eliminates redundant always blocks and may improve timing by reducing logic depth. I will keep the asynchronous negative-edge reset as required, but implement nonblocking assignments consistently for all sequential logic to ensure correct synthesis. Additionally, I will use a one-hot style decoding internally to simplify the output condition (though still encoded as 2-bit states) to reduce logic complexity for z. This should marginally reduce power and area while maintaining or improving clock period margins. Overall, this design balances clarity, compactness, and minimal logic for improved PPA.
