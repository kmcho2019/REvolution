To optimize the FSM for better PPA, the following improvements are made:

1. Separate output logic from next-state logic by registering the output 'z' in the sequential block. This reduces combinational path delay from inputs/state to output and improves timing, at the cost of one cycle output latency, acceptable for many designs.

2. Use one-hot encoding for the three states to simplify combinational logic and potentially reduce power and area in FPGA/ASIC since one-hot FSMs often map to simpler logic and faster clock paths.

3. The asynchronous negative-edge reset remains as per spec.

4. Keep the FSM transitions and sequence detection logic equivalent, preserving overlapping sequence detection.

This approach balances minimal area overhead from one-hot encoding against likely better timing and power results due to reduced combinational logic depth and registered output.

The output 'z' is asserted for one cycle after the clock edge when the sequence "101" is detected.
