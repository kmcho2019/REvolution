To improve timing slack and slightly reduce power and area, I will encode the states with one-hot encoding since it might simplify the next state logic to AND/OR terms, potentially reducing gate depth and switching activity, improving slack. Although one-hot encoding usually uses more flip-flops, here with only 3 states this will be only 3 flip-flops, which is acceptable. I will also combine the next-state and output logic into a single always_comb block for clarity and reduced logic duplication. I keep the asynchronous negative edge reset as specified. This approach should improve timing by flattening combinational paths, potentially reduce glitches, and keep power/area at reasonable levels due to the very small FSM size.
