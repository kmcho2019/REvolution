We can simplify these minterms:

Group (8,9): x3 & ~x2 & ~x1 & (x0 + ~x0) = x3 & ~x2 & ~x1

Group (12,13): x3 & x2 & ~x1 & (x0 + ~x0) = x3 & x2 & ~x1

f = (~x3 & x2 & x1 & x0) + (x3 & ~x2 & ~x1) + (x3 & x2 & ~x1)

We can factor the last two terms:

x3 & ~x1 & (~x2 + x2) = x3 & ~x1

So final simplified expression:

f = (~x3 & x2 & x1 & x0) + (x3 & ~x1)
