The main issue is a confusion between the Karnaugh map variable naming (x[1]x[2] and x[3]x[4]) and the Verilog input bits x[3:0]. The problem states x[1] and x[2] as the column bits, and x[3] and x[4] as the row bits, but our input vector only has bits x[3:0]. This probably means a 1-based indexing, so likely x[1] = x[0], x[2] = x[1], x[3] = x[2], and x[4] = x[3] (assuming the problem indexing is 1-based). 

To correctly map the Karnaugh map to the input vector `x[3:0]` where `x[3]` is the MSB, and `x[0]` is the LSB, we assign:
- Row bits = x[3]x[2]
- Column bits = x[1]x[0]

Therefore, for indexing the Karnaugh map:
- Row = x[3:2]
- Column = x[1:0]

Now, the Karnaugh map given:

            x[1]x[2]
   x[3]x[4]  00  01  11  10
     00     | d | 0 | d | d |
     01     | 0 | d | 1 | 0 |
     11     | 1 | 1 | d | d |
     10     | 1 | 1 | 0 | d |

Assuming (row,col) maps to bits as (x[3:2], x[1:0]):

Construct the 16-bit truth table indexed by `x` (x[3:0]) from 0 to 15 (binary input), where the address is:
- addr = {x[3:2], x[1:0]} (row concat column).

Assign all don't cares as 0.
