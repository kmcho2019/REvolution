Don't-cares are treated as zeros, but to keep the logic simple and efficient, we directly compare against the specified cells with output 1, returning 1 if the input matches any of those, else 0. This avoids Gray-to-binary conversion and case statements, yielding a minimal, clear, and fast combinational logic module.
