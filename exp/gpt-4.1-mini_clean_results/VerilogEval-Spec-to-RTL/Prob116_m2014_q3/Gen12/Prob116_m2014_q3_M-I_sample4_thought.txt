The previous solution attempted to group the ones based on the Karnaugh map but missed some coverage and potential simplifications. To fix this, I will re-express the Karnaugh map carefully and find the minimal sum-of-products with don't-cares optimally used.

The Karnaugh map with variables labeled:

- Inputs: x = {x4, x3, x2, x1} = {x[3], x[2], x[1], x[0]}
- Rows: x4 x3 (in order: 00, 01, 11, 10)
- Columns: x1 x2 (in order: 00, 01, 11, 10)

K-map cells (row,col) with output=1 or 0 or d:

|x4x3\x1x2| 00 | 01 | 11 | 10 |
|---------|----|----|----|----|
| 00      | d  | 0  | d  | d  |
| 01      | 0  | d  | 1  | 0  |
| 11      | 1  | 1  | d  | d  |
| 10      | 1  | 1  | 0  | d  |

Marking 1's and don't-cares:

- Ones at:
  (11,00), (11,01), (01,11), (10,00), (10,01)
- Don't-cares help us group further

Groups:

1. GroupA: (11,00) and (11,01) → x4=1, x3=1, x1=0 (x2 varies)
2. GroupB: (10,00) and (10,01) → x4=1, x3=0, x1=0 (x2 varies)
3. GroupC: (11,00), (10,00) → x1=0, x2=0, x4=1 (x3 varies) → This overlaps with GroupA and GroupB
4. GroupD: (11,01), (10,01) → x1=0, x2=1, x4=1 (x3 varies)
5. GroupE: (01,11) alone → x4=0, x3=1, x1=1, x2=1

Among these, to minimize:

- We can combine groups A and B into one group for x4=1 and x1=0 (x3 and x2 vary) since their columns are adjacent don't-cares or zeros? The columns are 00 and 01, so x1=0 fixed, x2 varies (0 or 1).
- Group for x4=1, x1=0 covers cells (10,00), (10,01), (11,00), (11,01) all 1's or don't-cares used as 1.
- So one prime implicant: x4 & ~x1

- The cell (01,11) = 0 1 1 1 (x4=0, x3=1, x1=1, x2=1) stands alone, so term: ~x4 & x3 & x1 & x2

Thus the simplified function is:

f = (x4 & ~x1) | (~x4 & x3 & x1 & x2)

This covers all 1's and leverages the don't-cares for simplification.

This approach reduces the logic to two main terms:

- Term1: x4 AND NOT x1
- Term2: NOT x4 AND x3 AND x1 AND x2
