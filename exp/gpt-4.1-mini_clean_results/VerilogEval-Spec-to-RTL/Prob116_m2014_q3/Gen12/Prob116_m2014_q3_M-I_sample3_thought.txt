The original solution misunderstood the Karnaugh map indexing due to the problem’s unusual labeling: it mentions x[1]x[2] for columns and x[3]x[4] for rows, but the input only has bits x[3:0]. We must interpret the problem so that the 4-bit input x = {x[3], x[2], x[1], x[0]} maps as:

- Row index = x[3]x[2] (the two MSBs)
- Column index = x[1]x[0] (the two LSBs)

This aligns with a standard 4x4 K-map where the MSBs form rows and LSBs form columns.

Then, we implement the function directly as a combinational logic expression derived from the K-map or by case statements matching each minterm. Don't-care ('d') entries can be assigned to optimize the logic.

To optimize for PPA (power, performance, area), instead of nested case statements, I will derive the minimal sum-of-products expression from the K-map with don't-cares optimally assigned to reduce logic complexity.

From the Karnaugh map:

| x[3]x[2] \ x[1]x[0] | 00 | 01 | 11 | 10 |
|---------------------|----|----|----|----|
| 00                  | d  | 0  | d  | d  |
| 01                  | 0  | d  | 1  | 0  |
| 11                  | 1  | 1  | d  | d  |
| 10                  | 1  | 1  | 0  | d  |

Assign don't-cares to 0 or 1 to maximize grouping:

- Assign d at (00,00),(00,11),(00,10) to 0
- Assign d at (01,01) to 1 to help cover more 1's
- Assign d at (11,11),(11,10),(10,11) to 0

After assignments, the minterms with f=1 are:

- (01,11) => x= {0,1,1,1}
- (11,00) => {1,1,0,0}
- (11,01) => {1,1,0,1}
- (01,01) => {0,1,0,1} (assigned 1)
- (10,00) => {1,0,0,0}
- (10,01) => {1,0,0,1}

From this, minimal expression (by K-map or Boolean algebra) is:

f = (x[3] & x[2]) | (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1]) | (~x[3] & x[2] & x[0]) | (x[3] & x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0] is in d so ignored)

Which simplifies to:

f = (x[3] & x[2])                       // covers rows 11
  | (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1])             // covers row 10, col 00 and 01
  | (~x[3] & x[2] & x[0]);             // covers row 01, col 01

This expression is simple, uses few gates, and implements the function correctly.

Thus, the improved design will implement this minimal boolean expression, ensuring correct mapping and an optimized PPA.
