The original design uses a structural approach with separate AND2 and OR2 modules, which adds modular overhead and wiring complexity. Given the simplicity of the function (f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)), we can optimize for power and area by implementing the logic directly with a single continuous assignment statement without additional modules.

This approach reduces the number of module boundaries and wiring overhead, leading to lower area and power consumption while preserving zero combinational delay logic (the critical path remains minimal with just two logic levels inherently).

No performance is lost because the logic depth is unchanged, but overall PPA should improve due to simpler netlist and gate-level synthesis optimization.

Hence, we remove the AND2 and OR2 module instantiations and implement the function directly using continuous assignment in TopModule.
