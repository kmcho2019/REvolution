Implement the combinational logic by using a 3-to-1 multiplexor where the select lines are {x3, x2} and the data inputs are chosen to produce the correct output. Since the output depends on the combination of x3 and x2 with an additional input x1 in some cases, this MUX-based approach offers a structured alternative. For inputs 00 and 10, output depends on x1 only for 10; for inputs 01 and 11, output depends differently, so carefully define mux inputs accordingly.
