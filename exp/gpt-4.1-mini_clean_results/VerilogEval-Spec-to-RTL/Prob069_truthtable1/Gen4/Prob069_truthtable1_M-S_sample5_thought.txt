Observe the truth table and express f directly as a minimized Boolean expression. From the table, f is 1 for inputs: (0,1,0), (0,1,1), (1,0,1), (1,1,1).

Writing minterms for f=1:
- x3'x2x1' (0 1 0)
- x3'x2x1  (0 1 1)
- x3x2'x1  (1 0 1)
- x3x2x1   (1 1 1)

Group minterms:

f = x3'x2 + x3x1

This matches the original condition in different form.

Alternatively, since from example f = x3? x1 : x2 is simple and minimal, it is already optimal.

To simplify further, express f as:

f = (x3 & x1) | (~x3 & x2)

This is a simple OR of two AND terms, which is equivalent to the conditional operator.

This approach explicitly uses basic gates (AND, OR, NOT), which might be clearer and minimal.

Hence, the simplified implementation is:

f = (x3 & x1) | (~x3 & x2)

This is functionally identical and minimal.
