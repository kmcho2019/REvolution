The original design uses separate AND2 and OR2 modules to implement the logic f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1). While clear and modular, this adds module instantiation overhead and more wiring which slightly increases area and power.

To optimize for PPA (power, performance, area), I will simplify the design by removing the AND2 and OR2 modules and directly implementing the logic expression using a single assign statement inside TopModule.

This reduces gate-level hierarchy, wiring capacitance, and module overhead, improving area and power slightly without impacting performance since the logic depth remains the same.

The design remains combinational and straightforward, but now has fewer instantiated modules and less wiring overhead, which should yield better overall PPA metrics.

Hence, the improved design is a purely structural implementation with a direct assign statement implementing the minimized Boolean expression.
