Analyze the function f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1). Notice this matches the logic of a 2-to-1 multiplexer with x3 as select:
- if x3=0, f = x2
- if x3=1, f = x1

This is equivalent to the mux operation: f = x3 ? x1 : x2

Use the conditional (ternary) operator for a simpler and more intuitive expression.
