The original implementation uses separate AND2 and OR2 gate modules and an explicit inverter wire to implement the function f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1). While modular and clear, this adds unnecessary module instantiations and wiring that slightly increase area and power.

To optimize PPA, I will:
- Replace all module instantiations with a single continuous assignment statement directly expressing the function f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1).
- This reduces the gate count to a minimum and removes explicit intermediate signals.
- The synthesis tool will infer minimal combinational logic gates, optimizing area and power.
- The performance remains optimal since logic depth is unchanged.

This improves area and power without affecting timing or readability, resulting in a compact and efficient combinational design.
