The current implementation is minimal and already optimal at the Boolean expression level. To optimize further for PPA (power, performance, area), I can rewrite the logic in a different equivalent form, such as using a single expression with XOR and AND gates, which might reduce gate transitions and power slightly. Specifically, f can be expressed as (x3 & x1) | (~x3 & x2), but also as x2 ^ (x3 & (x1 ^ x2)), potentially reducing logic transitions and complexity in certain synthesis technologies.

Alternatively, keeping the original expression is minimal in gate count, but adding an explicit `wire` can enable synthesis tools to better optimize. However, for such a small design, gains are negligible.

Hence, I propose to implement f = (x3 & x1) | (~x3 & x2) but factor it to reduce gate inputs: f = x2 ^ (x3 & (x1 ^ x2)). This may slightly reduce switching activity and possibly power without increasing area.

This is a minor improvement targeting power and possibly timing due to reduced logic depth for some inputs.
