The original solution uses a hierarchical structural gate-level design with a custom mux, which, while clear and modular, incurs extra logic depth and area.

To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will:

- Eliminate the custom gate modules and the structural mux to reduce hierarchy and intermediate signals.
- Directly implement the minimal Boolean expression for f derived from the truth table:

  f = (~x3 & x2 & ~x1) | (x1 & (x2 | x3))

  This expression corresponds to:
  - For x1=0, f = ~x3 & x2
  - For x1=1, f = x2 | x3

- Implement this logic as a single combinational assign statement in TopModule.

This approach reduces logic depth, gate count, wiring complexity, and switching nodes, improving power and area without sacrificing clarity or correctness.
