Example 2 uses clear structural AND and OR gate modules, which improve readability and reuse but introduce more modules and wiring.

Our fusion strategy is:
- Implement the function using a single Boolean expression (same as in Example 1 and 2).

- Inside TopModule, instantiate one MUX2 for the core selection (x3 selecting between x2 and x1).
- Use a small NOT module for the complement of x3, preserving modularity.
- This keeps design modular but reduces number of gates by not instantiating two ANDs and one OR.
- The NOT module emphasizes clear gate-level abstraction like in Example 2 but does not bloat logic.
- Overall, this is a neat structural design showing clarity, modularity, and efficiency by fusing gate instantiation style with MUX-based minimal logic.
