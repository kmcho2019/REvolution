The function can be directly implemented using a simple assign statement derived from the truth table. Observing the table, we see that f=1 under these conditions:

- When x2 = 1 and x3 = 0 (regardless of x1)
- When x3 = 1, x2 = 0, and x1 = 1
- When x3 = 1, x2 = 1, and x1 = 1

Combining these:

f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)

This expression is minimal and requires only two AND gates and one OR gate, implemented directly as assign statement for simple combinational logic without a multiplexer module.
