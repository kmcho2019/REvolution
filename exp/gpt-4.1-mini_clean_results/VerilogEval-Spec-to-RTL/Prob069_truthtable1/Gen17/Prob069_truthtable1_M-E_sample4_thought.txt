Implement the combinational logic directly using a behavioral assign statement that encodes the logic expression derived from the truth table.

From the table:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)

Instead of instantiating gates, use a single continuous assignment in the top module to compute f combinationally.

This approach provides:

- Minimalistic, concise code,
- No extra modules or intermediate wires,
- Direct, readable logic expression,
- Efficient synthesis with minimal overhead,
- Easy to maintain and extend if logic changes.

It leverages behavioral modeling of combinational logic using built-in operators.
