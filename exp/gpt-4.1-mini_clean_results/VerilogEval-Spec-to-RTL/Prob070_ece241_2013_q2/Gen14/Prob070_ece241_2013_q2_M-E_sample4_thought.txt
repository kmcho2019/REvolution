A new approach is to directly decode the three specific numbers 2, 7, and 15 using explicit comparisons in combinational logic, then combine these checks for the outputs.

For minimal sum-of-products (out_sop), express the function as the OR of minterms that match each number explicitly:
- 2 is 0b0010 → ~a & ~b & c & ~d
- 7 is 0b0111 → ~a & b & c & d
- 15 is 0b1111 → a & b & c & d

Thus, out_sop = m2 + m7 + m15.

For minimal product-of-sums (out_pos), express as the product of sums that block all zero conditions:
- Identify maxterms covering the zero outputs and don't cares, and write the product of sums explicitly that accept only 2,7,15.

This leads to the product of sums expression:
out_pos = (~a + b + ~c + ~d) & (~a + ~b + c + ~d) & (a + ~b + ~c + ~d)

This is the minimal POS matching the function.

This approach is explicit, clear, and structurally different from the initial design, using direct minterm and maxterm logic checks instead of factored expressions.
