out_sop = m2 + m7 + m15 = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d).

For the POS output (out_pos), instead of trying to minimize complex expressions, use the direct product of maxterms of zeros.

Explicitly write maxterms for zeros: 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14. This will cover all zero cases exactly.

This direct POS will be larger but correct and simple.
