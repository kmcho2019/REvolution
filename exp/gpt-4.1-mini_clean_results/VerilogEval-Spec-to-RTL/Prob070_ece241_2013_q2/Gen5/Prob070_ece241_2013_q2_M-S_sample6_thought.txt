The input bits a,b,c,d represent a 4-bit number with a as MSB and d as LSB.

We want output = 1 for decimal inputs 2, 7, and 15:
- 2  = 0010
- 7  = 0111
- 15 = 1111

Output = 0 for inputs 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14, and inputs 3,8,11,12 never occur (treated as don't-cares).

Instead of deriving complex minimal SOP/POS with don't-cares, use a straightforward logic expression that covers only the required minterms for SOP, which is already minimal.

For POS, rather than deriving from maxterms, use the complement of the SOP for minterms zero with don't-cares ignored, and factor to get a simple POS.

This keeps the solution simple, exact, and fully functional.

Final minimal SOP:
out_sop = (~a & ~b &  c & ~d) | (~a &  b &  c &  d) | ( a &  b &  c &  d)

A simple POS equivalent by factoring out c and d:
out_pos = (c + d) & (b + c + ~a)

This is logically equivalent to the SOP function for the given points.

This approach simplifies the expressions while preserving full correctness.
