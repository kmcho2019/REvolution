The inputs a,b,c,d represent a 4-bit number (with a as MSB and d as LSB). We want outputs that are logic 1 when the inputs correspond to decimal 2, 7, or 15, and logic 0 when inputs correspond to decimal 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, or 14. Inputs 3, 8, 11, 12 do not occur (don't care). 

First, list the minterms where output=1: 
- 2  = 0010
- 7  = 0111
- 15 = 1111

List maxterms where output=0:
- 0  = 0000
- 1  = 0001
- 4  = 0100
- 5  = 0101
- 6  = 0110
- 9  = 1001
- 10 = 1010
- 13 = 1101
- 14 = 1110

We can minimize the SOP and POS expressions using Karnaugh maps or algebraic simplification. For SOP, the minterms are for 2, 7, and 15. For POS, the maxterms are zeros listed above.

After simplification:

SOP (out_sop) = a b d + a c d + a' b' c d' 

POS (out_pos) = (a + b + d) (a + c + d) (a' + b' + c + d')

These forms are minimal expressions for the desired function.

We implement these in Verilog for outputs out_sop and out_pos.
