We want out=0 for 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14.

Inputs 3,8,11,12 never occur, so they can be treated as don't-cares.

out_sop = (~a & c & ~d) | (b & c & d)

We can verify these two clauses cover all zero minterms and exclude the ones.
