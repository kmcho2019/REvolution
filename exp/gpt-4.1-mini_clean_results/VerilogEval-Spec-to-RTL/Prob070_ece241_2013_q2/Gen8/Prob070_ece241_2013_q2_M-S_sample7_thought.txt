Map inputs a,b,c,d as a 4-bit number with a as MSB and d as LSB. 
The output is 1 for decimal inputs 2, 7, and 15, and 0 for given zeros.
Group the minterms for the SOP:
- 2: 0 0 1 0 (a'b' c d')
- 7 and 15 can be grouped as b c d since a is don't care for these two.
Thus, out_sop = b & c & d | ~a & ~b & c & ~d.

For the POS:
The zeros are at 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14.
Form maxterms for each zero and then find minimal groups:
By Karnaugh map simplification, minimal POS is:

  out_pos = (a + b + c + d) & (a + ~b + d) & (~a + b + c) & (~a + ~b + c + ~d);

This covers all zero minterms and handles don't-care conditions implicitly.

This approach simplifies the POS expression significantly while keeping the exact functionality.
