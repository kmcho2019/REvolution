The original implementation uses direct assign statements for SOP and POS expressions. To refactor with the same logic but a different structural style, I will use an always_comb block with an intermediate 4-bit input vector formed from the inputs (a,b,c,d). 

Inside the always_comb block, I will use a case statement keyed on the 4-bit number to explicitly set outputs for all relevant inputs, including zeros, ones, and don't cares, ensuring clarity and correctness. This approach is functionally equivalent but structurally different from direct combinational assigns and often improves readability and maintainability.

This also helps confirm the SOP and POS expressions logically by enumerating outputs directly.

Finally, I will use continuous assignments to drive the outputs from internal registers assigned in the always_comb block.

This approach addresses the POS expression concerns by defining outputs explicitly rather than relying on possibly misinterpreted POS literals.
