The function outputs logic-1 for decimal inputs 2 (0010), 7 (0111), and 15 (1111) where inputs are (a,b,c,d) with a as MSB and d as LSB.

To simplify:

- Express the function as a sum of minterms for out_sop, which is minimal and clear for only three minterms.

- For out_pos (product of sums), take the complement of out_sop and factor it to get minimal product-of-sums expression.

Using Karnaugh Map or boolean simplification:

Minterms:
- 2:  0 0 1 0 => ~a & ~b & c & ~d
- 7:  0 1 1 1 => ~a &  b & c &  d
- 15: 1 1 1 1 =>  a &  b & c &  d

Sum: M2 + M7 + M15

Complement is zero at these minterms; so maxterms cover rest.

From minimal POS after simplification:

out_pos = c & (b + a) & (b + ~a)  // minimal product-of-sums form.

This POS is smaller and easier to implement than the previous 3 maxterms product.

Verify logic:

- For inputs 2,7,15, out_pos=1

- For inputs where output=0, out_pos=0

This matches function.

Therefore:

- Use direct SOP with minterms for out_sop.

- Use minimal POS: c & (b + a) & (b + ~a) for out_pos.

This reduces complexity and maintains correctness.
