The core issue is the bit ordering and the corresponding minterms representing the numbers. The problem states the inputs a,b,c,d form a 4-bit number with 'a' as MSB and 'd' as LSB, so the number is: value = a*8 + b*4 + c*2 + d*1.

Given the example for 7 = 0,1,1,1, this confirms 'a' is the MSB.

We need to identify the decimal numbers for which out_sop = 1 (2,7,15) and out_sop = 0 (0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14). The numbers 3,8,11,12 never occur, so can be treated as don't cares.

We first express out_sop as 1 for decimal values 2,7,15:
- 2 = 0 0 1 0  (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0)
- 7 = 0 1 1 1
- 15= 1 1 1 1

Using a,b,c,d in order, we get minterms:

- For 2: (~a & ~b & c & ~d)
- For 7: (~a & b & c & d)
- For 15: (a & b & c & d)

So minimal SOP is out_sop = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d)

Next, for the minimal POS expression, out_pos = 0 for numbers 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14 and 1 otherwise.

Let's write the zeros (0s) minterms for out_sop:

Numbers for zero output:
- 0  = 0000: (~a & ~b & ~c & ~d)
- 1  = 0001: (~a & ~b & ~c & d)
- 4  = 0100: (~a & b & ~c & ~d)
- 5  = 0101: (~a & b & ~c & d)
- 6  = 0110: (~a & b & c & ~d)
- 9  = 1001: (a & ~b & ~c & d)
- 10 = 1010: (a & ~b & c & ~d)
- 13 = 1101: (a & b & ~c & d)
- 14 = 1110: (a & b & c & ~d)

From this, the minimal POS expression can be derived (using K-map or Boolean algebra). The minimal POS expression is:

out_pos = (a + b + ~c + d) & (a + ~b + ~c + ~d) & (~a + ~b + ~c + d)
