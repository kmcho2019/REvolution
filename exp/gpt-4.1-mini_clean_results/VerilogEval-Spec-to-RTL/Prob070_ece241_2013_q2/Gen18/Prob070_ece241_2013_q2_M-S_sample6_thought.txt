Represent the inputs as a 4-bit number with `a` as MSB and `d` as LSB: N = {a,b,c,d}. The output should be 1 when N equals 2, 7, or 15, and 0 for certain other values, while some input combinations never occur.

The minimal SOP expression can be directly implemented as the OR of minterms corresponding to those values:
- 2  = 0010 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0)
- 7  = 0111 (a=0,b=1,c=1,d=1)
- 15 = 1111 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1)

So the minimal SOP is: 
  out_sop = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d)

Similarly, the minimal POS is the product of maxterms for zeros (covering the zeros given), which simplifies to:
  out_pos = (a + b + d) & (a + ~b + c) & (~a + b + ~c + ~d)

These expressions are minimal and directly correspond to the function's truth table without extra terms or complexity.

Implement both expressions using continuous assignments and bitwise operations, maintaining clarity and correctness.
