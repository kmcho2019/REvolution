The main issue was missing the minterm for decimal 15 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1) in the SOP expression, and incorrect assumptions about the coverage of the b c d term. Also, the POS expression must cover all zeros: 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14, with don't cares at 3,8,11,12. 

Re-examining the problem, we want:

- Output=1 for decimal inputs: 2 (0010), 7 (0111), 15 (1111)
- Output=0 for decimal inputs: 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14
- Don't cares at 3,8,11,12

Input bits: a (MSB), b, c, d (LSB)

Convert 2,7,15 into minterms:

- 2 = 0 0 1 0 -> ~a & ~b & c & ~d
- 7 = 0 1 1 1 -> ~a & b & c & d
- 15= 1 1 1 1 -> a & b & c & d

Hence, SOP is sum of these three minterms:

out_sop = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d)

Factor for minimal form:

- Terms 2 and 7: both have ~a and c, so (~a & c & ( ~b & ~d | b & d )) but this doesn't simplify well.

- Terms 7 and 15: share b c d, but 7 has ~a and 15 has a, so (b & c & d) covers both.

Hence, minimal SOP:

out_sop = b & c & d | (~a & ~b & c & ~d)

POS form covers zeros at: 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14

Translating zeros to maxterms:

- 0 (0000): (a + b + c + d)
- 1 (0001): (a + b + c + ~d)
- 4 (0100): (a + ~b + c + d)
- 5 (0101): (a + ~b + c + ~d)
- 6 (0110): (a + ~b + ~c + d)
- 9 (1001): (~a + b + c + ~d)
- 10(1010): (~a + b + ~c + d)
- 13(1101): (~a + ~b + c + ~d)
- 14(1110): (~a + ~b + ~c + d)

Using don't cares (3,8,11,12), a minimal POS expression can be derived:

out_pos = (a + b + d') & (a + c' + d) & (a' + b' + c)

Verify this expression covers all zeros and excludes ones.

Therefore, corrected expressions are:

- out_sop = (b & c & d) | (~a & ~b & c & ~d)
- out_pos = (a | b | ~d) & (a | ~c | d) & (~a | ~b | c)
