The problem is to detect when the 4-bit input {a,b,c,d} equals decimal 2, 7, or 15, outputting 1, and output 0 for specified other values.

Decimal inputs in binary (a=MSB, d=LSB):
- 2 = 0 0 1 0  -> ~a & ~b & c & ~d
- 7 = 0 1 1 1  -> ~a & b & c & d
- 15= 1 1 1 1  -> a & b & c & d

These minterms have no common factors except c. Including only common factor c is not enough to separate out unwanted values.

Minimal SOP is simply the sum of these three minterms:
out_sop = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) + (~a & b & c & d) + (a & b & c & d)

Minimal POS can be derived from zeros at 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14 and don't-cares at 3,8,11,12.

From K-map analysis, minimal POS is:
out_pos = (a + b + ~d) & (a + ~c + d) & (~a + ~b + c)

This matches the original minimal POS.

To simplify further:
- Keep SOP as sum of the three minterms (no further reduction without including undesired outputs).
- Implement POS as given.

This is the simplest correct implementation with clear terms and minimal complexity.
