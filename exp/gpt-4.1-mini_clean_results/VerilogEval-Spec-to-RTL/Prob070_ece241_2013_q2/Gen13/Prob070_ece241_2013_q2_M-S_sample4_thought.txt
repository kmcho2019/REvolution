Represent inputs as a 4-bit number with 'a' as MSB and 'd' as LSB. The function outputs 1 for decimal inputs 2 (0010), 7 (0111), and 15 (1111); outputs 0 for certain others; and treats 3,8,11,12 as don't-cares.

From the minterms and don't-cares, the minimal sum-of-products (SOP) is: 
f = (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (c & d)

The minimal product-of-sums (POS) is:
f = (a + b + ~c + d) & (~a + ~b + c + ~d)

This matches the previous minimal forms and is already simple.

We keep the continuous assign statements with only basic gates, no always blocks. This is the simplest fully minimal solution meeting all specs.
