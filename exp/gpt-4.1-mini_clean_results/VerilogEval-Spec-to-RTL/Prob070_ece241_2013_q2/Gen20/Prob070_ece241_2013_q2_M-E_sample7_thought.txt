Instead of directly minimizing from the truth table, I will use a structural approach to identify the required outputs by comparing the 4-bit input (a,b,c,d) against the target decimal values explicitly.

Given the decimal values to output logic-1: 2 (0010), 7 (0111), 15 (1111)

- Compare the 4-bit inputs to these constants:
  - For 2: a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0
  - For 7: a=0,b=1,c=1,d=1
  - For 15:a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1

I'll implement three equality detectors (each comparing inputs to 2, 7, and 15) and OR their outputs for out_sop (Sum of Products).

For out_pos (Product of Sums), I'll implement the complement of zeros explicitly. The zeros are 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14. We can create an OR of equality for zeros and invert that for out_pos.

This approach avoids error-prone manual boolean simplification by leveraging direct equality checks. It's also synthesizable and clear.

This method guarantees correct function for both SOP and POS by structural construction rather than algebraic minimization.

This is a novel architectural idea different from purely boolean expression minimization.
