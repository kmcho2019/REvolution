The current solution directly implements the NOR gate with a simple continuous assignment, which is already minimal and optimal for area, power, and delay. The NOTGate and ANDGate modules are unused and only increase area and possibly power slightly. Removing these unused modules eliminates unnecessary code, reducing area and power. No further optimization in the NOR implementation is needed since the direct bitwise NOR operation is ideal. Therefore, the improvement is to simplify the design by keeping only the TopModule with the direct NOR implementation.
