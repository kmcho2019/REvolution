Implement the NOR gate using only NAND gates and inverters (NOT gates), reflecting a classic universal gate design approach. This uses De Morgan's law: NOR(a,b) = NAND(NOT a, NOT b). The design explicitly instantiates small submodules for NAND and NOT, promoting modularity and clarity, and demonstrating gate-level composition. This approach may be beneficial in technologies or teaching contexts where NAND and NOT gates are the primary available primitives. The TopModule instantiates two NOT gates on inputs and then feeds their outputs to a NAND gate, yielding the NOR output.
