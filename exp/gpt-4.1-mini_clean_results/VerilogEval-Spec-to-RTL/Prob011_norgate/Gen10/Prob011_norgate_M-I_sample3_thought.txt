The original solution uses the reduction NOR operator `~|{a,b}`, which is already concise and maps efficiently to hardware. However, an even clearer and potentially slightly more optimized approach is to use the explicit expression `~(a | b)`, which directly reflects the canonical two-input NOR gate. This expression is widely recognized and understood by synthesis tools and maps one-to-one to a 2-input NOR gate with minimal delay, area, and power. Thus, the improvement strategy is to replace the reduction operator with the explicit bitwise OR and negation. This can yield marginally better PPA by avoiding concatenation overhead (though typically negligible) and makes the design’s intent crystal clear.
