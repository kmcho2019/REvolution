The original solution is already minimal and optimal by directly instantiating the Verilog built-in NOR primitive, which maps efficiently in synthesis. However, we can improve readability and maintainability slightly by using a continuous assignment statement with the bitwise NOR operator (~|) instead of gate instantiation. This style is more idiomatic in modern Verilog, easier to read and maintain, and equally optimal during synthesis. Since the NOR operator is a primitive operation, the synthesis tool will still produce a minimal NOR gate with no additional logic. This could marginally improve synthesis clarity while maintaining the exact same PPA metrics.
