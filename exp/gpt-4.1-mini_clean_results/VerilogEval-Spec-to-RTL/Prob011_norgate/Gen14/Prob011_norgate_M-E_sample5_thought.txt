Implement the NOR gate using a structural approach by instantiating two NOT gates and one AND gate. First, invert each input separately, then feed both inverted signals into an AND gate. This structure leverages De Morgan's theorem: NOR(a,b) = NOT(a OR b) = (NOT a) AND (NOT b). This approach explicitly shows the internal structure of the NOR gate with basic building blocks, making the design modular and demonstrating hierarchical gate composition rather than using a single compound operator.
