Implement the NOR gate using a structural approach by instantiating a built-in primitive `or` gate followed by a `not` gate. This design decomposes the NOR function into an OR stage and an inverter stage, demonstrating a modular, gate-level structural style rather than continuous assignment. This style can aid in understanding the gate-level netlist and is suitable for detailed gate-level simulation or synthesis where explicit gate instantiations are preferred. The output `out` is declared as wire by default and the interconnection net `or_out` is declared explicitly for clarity.
