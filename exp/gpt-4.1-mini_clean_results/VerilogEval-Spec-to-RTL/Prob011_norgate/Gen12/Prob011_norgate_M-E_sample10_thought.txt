Implement the NOR gate functionality using behavioral Verilog with a continuous assign statement that explicitly applies bitwise negation to the OR of inputs. This approach avoids using the built-in NOR primitive and instead uses basic operators, demonstrating how to derive NOR logic from fundamental gates. This is useful for synthesis tools that support arithmetic and logical operators and offers a clear and simple alternative architectural style without instantiating primitives. The design remains purely combinational and efficient but relies on expression-level coding rather than gate-level instantiation.
