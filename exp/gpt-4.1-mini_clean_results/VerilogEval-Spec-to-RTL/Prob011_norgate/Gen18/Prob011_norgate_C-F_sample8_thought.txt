Combine the clarity and modularity of a dedicated NORGate module with the simplicity and efficiency of directly implementing the NOR logic inside that module. This keeps the TopModule clean and minimal by instantiating the NORGate without intermediate wiring in TopModule, preserving the hierarchical structure for reuse and clarity. At the same time, avoid extra OR and NOT modules to reduce area and power overhead. This fusion balances readability, modularity, and PPA optimizations by limiting hierarchy to one well-defined abstraction while directly implementing the NOR operation within it.
