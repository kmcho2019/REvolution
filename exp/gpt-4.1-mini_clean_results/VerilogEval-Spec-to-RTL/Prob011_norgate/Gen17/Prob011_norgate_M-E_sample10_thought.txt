Implement the NOR gate by structurally composing an OR gate and a NOT gate as separate submodules instantiated within TopModule. This hierarchical design promotes modularity and clarity, allowing reuse of the basic gates individually in other designs. The OR gate is defined with an assign statement combining inputs, and the NOT gate inverts the OR output to produce the final NOR result. This approach illustrates gate-level structural composition rather than direct expression, aiding in educational understanding or further expansion.
