The original solution correctly implements the NOR gate using a direct continuous assignment, which is already optimal for area and timing. However, it includes two unused modules (NOTGate and ANDGate), which increase the area and power consumption unnecessarily. Removing these unused modules will reduce both area and power without affecting functionality or performance. Hence, the improvement strategy is to eliminate the NOTGate and ANDGate modules, leaving a clean, minimal, and efficient TopModule that directly implements the NOR gate.
