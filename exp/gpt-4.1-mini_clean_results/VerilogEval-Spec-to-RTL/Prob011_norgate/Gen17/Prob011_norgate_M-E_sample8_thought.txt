Implement the NOR gate structurally using basic gates by first OR-ing the inputs and then inverting the result. This approach explicitly instantiates a separate ORGate and NOTGate, demonstrating a hierarchical and reusable design style. Although slightly less optimal than a direct NOR operator in terms of delay and area, this structural method enhances modularity and clarity, allowing each basic gate to be verified and reused independently. It also provides clear separation of functions, which can be useful in educational contexts or when targeting specific gate-level synthesis optimizations.
