Combine the clarity and directness of a continuous assignment using the simplest NOR expression ~(a | b) with Verilog's native bitwise NOR reduction operator (~|) for utmost conciseness and readability. Using assign out = ~(a | b) is clear and portable, and similarly assign out = ~|{a,b} explicitly shows the NOR operation on multiple bits. However, for only two inputs, the expression ~(a | b) is straightforward and universally understood. The fusion strategy is to keep the direct combinational assign with the NOR expression but to use the bitwise NOR reduction operator (~|) on a concatenated input vector, emphasizing the NOR function clearly and idiomatically. This reduces potential confusion, keeps RTL minimal, and leverages the language's concise operator for NOR without intermediate wires or additional complexity. This also aligns well with synthesis tools and optimizes PPA metrics inherently by describing the exact logic intended.
