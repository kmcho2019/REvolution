Since the original solution already implements the NOR gate with the minimal built-in primitive, it is optimal in terms of performance, power, and area. No additional logic or registers are needed. To maintain clarity and possibly assist synthesis tools, an equivalent continuous assignment using bitwise NOR (~|) can be provided as a stylistic alternative, but it won't improve PPA metrics. Therefore, the best approach is to keep the primitive nor gate as is for the cleanest and most efficient implementation.
