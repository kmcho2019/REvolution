Combine the modularity and reusability of having a dedicated NORGate module with the minimal gate level and direct logic implementation by defining the NORGate module as a primitive using a Verilog built-in primitive (nor gate primitive). This merges clarity, hierarchy, and optimal gate usage with minimal overhead. Then instantiate this primitive NORGate inside TopModule for clean structure and easy reuse, preserving the directness of the gate-level implementation without intermediate OR + NOT gates or extra assign statements. This approach benefits PPA by avoiding extra wires or logic levels, maintains modularity for future extensibility, and clearly shows the intended NOR function.
