The original implementation correctly uses a direct continuous assignment to implement the NOR gate, which is already optimal in terms of logic depth and area. However, the presence of unused NOTGate and ANDGate modules is unnecessary and increases the design footprint without benefit. Removing these unused modules will reduce area and power slightly by eliminating extra module overhead and net capacitance. Since timing and power are already minimal, the main improvement is to simplify the code for better clarity and leaner synthesis results.
