Fuse the clarity and minimalism of the built-in NOR primitive with the explicitness of expression from the De Morgan form by using a continuous assignment with the bitwise NOR operator in Verilog. Specifically, use `assign out = ~(a | b);` which is logically equivalent to the NOR primitive but written as a concise, synthesizable, and clear expression without intermediate wires. This approach combines the readability and expression clarity from the second example while retaining the minimal area, power, and performance benefits of the first by avoiding intermediate signals and using a single logical operation.
