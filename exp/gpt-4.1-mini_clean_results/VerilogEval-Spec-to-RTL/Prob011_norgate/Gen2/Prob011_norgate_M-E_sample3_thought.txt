Implement the NOR gate behavior by instantiating two NOT gates and one AND gate, chaining them to produce the output. Specifically, invert each input separately and then perform an AND on these inverted signals. This is logically equivalent to a NOR operation based on De Morgan's laws. While this approach uses more explicit intermediate signals and gates, it demonstrates a structural decomposition of the NOR function that can be useful for educational or mapping purposes in certain synthesis tools.
