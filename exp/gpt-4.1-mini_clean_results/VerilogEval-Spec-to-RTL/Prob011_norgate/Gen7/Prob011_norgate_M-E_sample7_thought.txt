Implement the NOR gate function by explicitly using a continuous assignment with the bitwise OR operator followed by a negation. This approach expresses the logic at the RTL level instead of instantiating a primitive gate. This method provides synthesis tools the flexibility to optimize or map the logic according to the target technology, potentially beneficial when integrated into larger systems or specific flow constraints require RTL coding style. It also serves as a good demonstration of behavioral Verilog for basic combinational logic.
