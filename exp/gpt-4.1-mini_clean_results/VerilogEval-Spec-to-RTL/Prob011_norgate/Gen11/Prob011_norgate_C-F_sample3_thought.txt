Fuse the two examples by directly using the built-in NOR operator `nor` primitive gate instead of continuous assignment expressions. This leverages the best of both styles: it clearly expresses the NOR gate as a dedicated primitive, which can be better optimized by synthesis tools for performance, power, and area. The primitive gate ensures minimal logic depth and is explicit about the intent, often leading to more efficient synthesis than reduction or bitwise expressions. This approach combines the clarity and synthesizability of behavioral Verilog with the efficiency of using the primitive gate directly.
