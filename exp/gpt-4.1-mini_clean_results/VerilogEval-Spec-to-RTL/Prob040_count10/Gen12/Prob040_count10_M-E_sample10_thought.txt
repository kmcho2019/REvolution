Implement the decade counter as a synchronous modulo-10 counter by using a 4-bit register that increments every clock cycle. Instead of resetting the counter when it reaches 9, implement the next count logic by detecting when the counter is at 9 and in that cycle setting the next value to 0. This next value calculation will be combinational, but the register update happens synchronously on the clock edge. The synchronous reset still asynchronously clears the counter to 0. This approach separates the combinational next count logic from the sequential register update logic, which provides clear separation of concerns and allows easy modifications for future expansions.
