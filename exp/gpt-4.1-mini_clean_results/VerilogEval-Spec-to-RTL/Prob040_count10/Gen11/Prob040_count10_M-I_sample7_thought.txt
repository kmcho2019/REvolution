To optimize the decade counter for better performance and potentially reduce the critical path, merge the combinational next-state logic directly into the sequential always block. This eliminates the intermediate next_q register and the separate combinational block, simplifying the logic and potentially reducing register-to-register delay. Also, replacing the if statement with a case or ternary operator avoids unnecessary extra logic. Since the counter counts 0 to 9, resetting to zero after 9, a conditional increment with a simple ternary operator can provide the same function with slightly simpler logic. Non-blocking assignment is still used, and synchronous active-high reset is preserved. This reduces overall logic levels and can improve synthesis optimizations.
