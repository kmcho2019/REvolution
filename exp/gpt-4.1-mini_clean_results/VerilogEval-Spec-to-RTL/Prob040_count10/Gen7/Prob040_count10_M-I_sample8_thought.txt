To improve PPA while maintaining simplicity, I will implement a small optimization in the counting logic by replacing the comparator and increment with a single arithmetic operation followed by a modulo operation (using subtraction) to reduce logic levels. This can slightly reduce critical path delay (thus improve timing) and may simplify hardware, as modulo-10 counters can be efficiently implemented using carry chains. The design will remain a single always block with synchronous reset, using a ternary operator for compactness. This approach should maintain minimal area and power while potentially enhancing timing margin.
