Fuse the straightforward synchronous reset and counting logic from Example 2 with the clear and readable conditional approach from Example 1, but optimize power by adding an enable signal that is high only when the count is less than 9. This enable reduces unnecessary toggling by preventing counting beyond 9 until reset, effectively gating counting activity without extra clock gating logic. This fusion keeps the design simple, with minimal logic and a synchronous reset, improves power by reducing switching activity when the counter is at terminal count, and maintains low area and good timing by not adding extra combinational logic or separate next-state registers.
