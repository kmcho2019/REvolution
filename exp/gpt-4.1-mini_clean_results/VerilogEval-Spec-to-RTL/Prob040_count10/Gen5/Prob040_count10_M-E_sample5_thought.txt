Implement the decade counter using an up-counter that counts from 0 to 15 and use combinational logic to wrap the output from 9 back to 0 by resetting the internal count to 0 on the next clock. This approach avoids an explicit comparator for 'q == 9' in the sequential block and instead uses a conditional assignment after incrementing. The counter increments every clock cycle synchronously with reset, and the output is taken directly from the internal counter. This design uses a simple 4-bit register and an internal next-state signal for clarity.
