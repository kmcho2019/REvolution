Implement a simple 4-bit binary up-counter that counts from 0 to 9, wrapping back to 0 after 9. Use synchronous active-high reset to set the counter to 0. Increment the counter by 1 on every positive clock edge when not resetting. When the counter reaches 9, on the next clock it wraps back to 0. Output q directly reflects the 4-bit counter value. This reduces area and power by using only 4 flip-flops and minimal logic, improving performance by avoiding large case statements and one-hot encoding.
