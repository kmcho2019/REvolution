To optimize the decade counter for better PPA, I will simplify the counting logic by using a single expression that wraps the count from 9 back to 0 with a conditional operator, eliminating the separate clock enable and multiple if-else branches. This reduces combinational logic depth and logic resource usage, improving timing (tns/wns) and area.

Additionally, the simplified logic inherently reduces switching activity because the counter only increments until 9, then resets, with no extra conditional checks or clock enable signals toggling. This can slightly improve power as well.

The synchronous reset remains active-high and inside the clocked block for correct operation.

Overall, the design remains clean, minimal, and functionally correct while improving the critical path, power, and area metrics through logic simplification.
