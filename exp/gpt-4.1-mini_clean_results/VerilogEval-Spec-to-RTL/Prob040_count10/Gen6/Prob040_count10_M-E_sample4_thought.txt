Implement the decade counter using a binary-coded decimal (BCD) style increment with explicit carry logic. Instead of a simple wrap check, increment the counter bit by bit with carry generated from the least significant bit to the most significant bit, resetting the count back to zero when the carry would propagate beyond the decimal digit 9 (1001 binary). This approach mimics a ripple-carry incrementer customized for decade counting, using bitwise operations and conditional resets while maintaining synchronous active-high reset.
