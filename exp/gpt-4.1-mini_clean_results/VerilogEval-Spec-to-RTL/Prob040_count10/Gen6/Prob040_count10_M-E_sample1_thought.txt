Implement the decade counter by using a 4-bit register that increments on every positive clock edge, but instead of checking for equality with 9, detect when the count is about to overflow beyond 9 by checking if the count is 9 and then reset to zero. The reset is synchronous and active high. The difference here is that the counter uses a case statement to explicitly enumerate count values, making it easy to extend or modify the counting sequence if needed. This structure provides clarity and explicit control of state transitions while remaining simple and synthesizable.
