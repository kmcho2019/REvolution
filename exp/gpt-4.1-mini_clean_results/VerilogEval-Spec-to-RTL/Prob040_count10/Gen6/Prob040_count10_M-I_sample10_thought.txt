To optimize the decade counter for better PPA, eliminate the separate combinational next state variable and instead compute the next count directly within the sequential block using a simple arithmetic expression. This reduces logic levels and area by avoiding an extra combinational block and intermediate register. Using a single always block with a straightforward conditional increment keeps the logic minimal and potentially improves timing by flattening the critical path. The reset remains synchronous active-high. This results in a smaller, faster, and lower power design due to simpler logic and fewer signals toggling.
