To improve power and potentially timing, I will implement a clock enable (ce) signal that disables counting when reset is active or when the counter reaches 9. This avoids unnecessary incrementing and logic toggling on wrap-around cycles or when reset is asserted, slightly reducing power consumption. 

I will keep the synchronous reset intact and use a single always block triggered on the clock's positive edge. The clock enable will gate the counter update, preventing toggling when the counter is at terminal count or reset is active.

This approach optimizes power by minimizing switching activity and might reduce the critical path length because the increment logic only triggers when the counter is enabled.

The area impact is minimal since only a small combinational enable signal is added.

Summary:
- Introduce a clock enable (ce) signal internally to suppress toggling on reset or at terminal count.
- Keep synchronous reset and single always block.
- Maintain simple, readable, and synthesizable code.
