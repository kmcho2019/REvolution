The original design is clean and minimal but counts every clock cycle, causing unnecessary toggling even during reset or stable states, slightly impacting power. To improve power efficiency without adding clock gating complexity, add a load-enable signal that is high only when counting is required, which reduces switching activity on the q register when the count is not advancing or during reset. The load-enable will be generated inside the sequential logic by checking the reset and terminal count. This will help reduce dynamic power by disabling toggling when the counter holds its value, improving power without compromising simplicity or timing. The area impact is negligible as no additional registers or complex logic are added, and timing improves slightly due to reduced toggling.
