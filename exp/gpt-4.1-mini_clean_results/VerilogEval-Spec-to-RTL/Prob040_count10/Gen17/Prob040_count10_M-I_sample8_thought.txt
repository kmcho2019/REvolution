To optimize the decade counter for better power and performance, I will implement a clock enable signal that disables counting when the counter reaches 9, avoiding unnecessary toggling on the carry-over cycle. This reduces dynamic power by preventing the counter from toggling once it hits 9 before wrapping to 0. Also, I will use a comparison with a constant for the enable condition, which is a simple combinational check that synthesis tools can optimize efficiently. This technique should slightly reduce switching activity, hence dynamic power, and can potentially improve timing by reducing toggle rate on higher bits. The reset remains synchronous active-high.
