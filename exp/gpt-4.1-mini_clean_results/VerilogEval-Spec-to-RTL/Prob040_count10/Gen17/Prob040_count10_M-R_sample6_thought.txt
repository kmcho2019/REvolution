I will refactor the decade counter by separating the next-state logic into a combinational always block, and then using a registered state update in a sequential always block. This structure splits combinational logic and sequential state update, which can clarify timing paths and make the design modular. The synchronous reset will still clear the counter to zero, and the counter will increment modulo 10 on each positive clock edge. The output `q` is directly driven by the state register.
