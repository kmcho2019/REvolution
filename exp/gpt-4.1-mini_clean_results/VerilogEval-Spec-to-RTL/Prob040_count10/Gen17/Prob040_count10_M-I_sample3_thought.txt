To optimize the decade counter for better power and performance, I will introduce an enable signal to prevent unnecessary toggling when the count reaches 9, by stopping the increment and wrapping synchronously in one cycle. This reduces switching activity slightly. Also, I will avoid the explicit comparison by using arithmetic modulo logic: if the counter value plus one equals 10, reset to zero; otherwise increment. This should have a similar hardware cost but can be efficiently inferred by synthesis tools. Additionally, I'll replace the `if-else` with a conditional operator inside the sequential block for concise logic, enabling the synthesis tool to optimize increment and reset logic effectively.
