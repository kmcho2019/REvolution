Both examples implement a straightforward decade counter with synchronous reset and wrap-around at 9, using simple arithmetic increment and a comparator. Their strengths lie in clarity, minimal combinational logic, and directness, ensuring good timing and low area and power.

To fuse these approaches optimally:
- Use a single always block triggered on posedge clk for synchronous reset and counting.
- Employ a conditional (ternary) operator inside the always block to succinctly select between zero and incremented count.
- This avoids the extra branching in the second example and keeps the logic minimal and clear.
- Retain the 4-bit register for output and non-blocking assignments.
- This fusion balances minimal logic (arithmetic plus comparator) with very clean and compact RTL code.

Hence, the design remains an up-counter from 0 to 9 with wrap-around, synchronous active-high reset, in a concise yet explicit style that synthesis tools handle efficiently.
