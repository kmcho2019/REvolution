I will implement the decade counter using a simple synchronous up-counter with arithmetic logic and a comparator for the wrap-around condition. The counter will increment by 1 on each clock cycle. When the count reaches 9, the next value will be reset to 0. The synchronous active-high reset will clear the counter to 0.

To introduce novelty, I will implement the wrap-around logic using a ternary (?:) operator instead of a case statement or if-else. This creates compact next-state logic by calculating the next count value combinationally and then assigning it in the sequential always block.

This approach differs from the previous state-machine-like case approach by relying on arithmetic and conditional expressions, making the design concise, efficient, and potentially easier to optimize for synthesis tools.
