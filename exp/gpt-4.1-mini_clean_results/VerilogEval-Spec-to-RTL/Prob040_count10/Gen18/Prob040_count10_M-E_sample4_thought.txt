Implement a modulo-10 counter directly using a 4-bit binary register that increments on each clock cycle, resetting to zero when it reaches 9 (counting 0 to 9). Use synchronous active-high reset to initialize the counter to 0. This approach eliminates one-hot encoding and decoding logic, reducing area and power while maintaining simplicity and clarity. The counting logic uses a simple comparison in the sequential block to wrap around at 9.
