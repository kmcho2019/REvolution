Implement the decade counter using a 4-bit register and an arithmetic increment operation combined with a conditional wrap-around. On every positive clock edge, if reset is asserted, the counter resets to zero synchronously. Otherwise, the counter increments by one. If the incremented value exceeds 9 (decimal), the counter wraps back to 0. This approach uses simple arithmetic and conditional logic without explicit state enumeration, leading to a compact and efficient implementation. This method minimizes logic levels and can improve timing and area compared to explicit case statements.
