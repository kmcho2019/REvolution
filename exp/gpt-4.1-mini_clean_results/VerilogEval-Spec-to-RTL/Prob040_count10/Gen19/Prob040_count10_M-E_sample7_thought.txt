Implement the decade counter as a 4-bit synchronous counter that increments by 1 on each clock cycle. Use an arithmetic approach where the counter increments normally but wraps to zero when the count reaches 10. This is done by a conditional expression inside the clocked process: if the counter equals 9, reset it to 0; otherwise, increment by 1. The reset is synchronous and active high, setting the count to zero. This approach avoids explicit state enumeration, reduces combinational logic depth, and yields an efficient and compact design.
