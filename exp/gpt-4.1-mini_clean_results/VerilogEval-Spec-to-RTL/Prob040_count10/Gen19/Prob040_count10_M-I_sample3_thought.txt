To improve the PPA of the decade counter, I will merge the combinational next-state logic directly into the sequential always block, eliminating the separate next_q register. This reduces area slightly by removing one extra register and simplifying the logic path. 

Additionally, I will explicitly use a synchronous reset within the sequential block and perform the count rollover logic concisely. This integrated style reduces logic levels and simplifies synthesis optimization. 

Since the design is simple and switching activity cannot be much reduced, the main gain will be from a slightly smaller area and possibly slightly improved timing due to fewer logic levels. Power improvements will be minimal but may improve because the logic path is simplified.
