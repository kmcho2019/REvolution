I will simplify the decade counter by using a single always block with a synchronous reset. On each clock edge, if reset is high, the counter resets to 0. Otherwise, the counter increments by 1 modulo 10, wrapping back to 0 when reaching 9.

This approach removes the separate clock enable and multiple conditions, reducing logic complexity. The wraparound is handled by a simple conditional: if q is 9, reset to 0, else increment by 1.

This simplification reduces combinational logic and mux complexity, leading to smaller area and lower power, while preserving the exact counting sequence and synchronous reset behavior.
