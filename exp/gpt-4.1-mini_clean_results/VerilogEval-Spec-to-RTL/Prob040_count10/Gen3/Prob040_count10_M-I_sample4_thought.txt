To optimize the decade counter for better power and area while maintaining performance, I will add an enable signal internally that disables counting when the counter reaches 9, avoiding unnecessary toggling. This reduces switching activity especially on higher bits when the counter saturates before resetting. Also, the combinational next state logic remains clean and minimal, but by gating the increment only when needed, toggling of bits is reduced. This simple clock enable technique trades off a minimal added logic gate for potentially reduced power and area from less switching.
