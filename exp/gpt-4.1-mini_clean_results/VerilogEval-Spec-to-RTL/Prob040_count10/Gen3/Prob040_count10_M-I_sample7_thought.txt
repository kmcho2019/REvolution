To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will optimize the decade counter by simplifying the wrap-around condition check using bitwise logic instead of an equality comparator. The value 9 in binary is 4'b1001; checking q==9 requires a four-bit comparator. Instead, I can detect when q == 9 by the condition that bit3 and bit0 are 1 and bits1 and 2 are 0 (i.e., q[3] & q[0] & ~q[2] & ~q[1]), which is simpler logic.

Also, to reduce switching power, the counter will only increment when not at 9, avoiding unnecessary toggling. The synchronous reset remains the same.

This should slightly improve timing by reducing comparator complexity and may reduce power by minimizing toggling.

The rest of the design remains unchanged and maintains correct functionality.
