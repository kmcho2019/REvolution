Refactor the JK flip-flop by separating the combinational logic that computes the next state from the clocked register update. Implement a combinational always block to compute the next value of Q (next_Q) based on the inputs j, k, and the current Q. Then, in a separate clocked always block, update Q with next_Q on the positive edge of clk. This approach explicitly shows the next state logic and makes the design clearer, while maintaining correct sequential behavior.
