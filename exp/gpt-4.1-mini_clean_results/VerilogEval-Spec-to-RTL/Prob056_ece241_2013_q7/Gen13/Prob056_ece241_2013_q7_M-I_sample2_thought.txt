To optimize the JK flip-flop for better PPA, we can use the characteristic equation of the JK flip-flop: Q(next) = J·Q' + K'·Q. This can be rewritten as: next_Q = (J & ~Q) | (~K & Q). This is a minimal and well-known implementation that reduces logic depth and gate count, improving timing and area.
