The best approach combines the clarity and minimalism of embedding the JK flip-flop logic expression directly inside the clocked always block (like Example 2) with the explicit initialization of Q to a known state for better simulation clarity and synthesis stability. 

This fusion leverages the compact, minimal combinational logic `(j & ~Q) | (~k & Q)` that directly implements the JK truth table without extra intermediate wires, minimizing logic depth and area. Adding an initial block to reset Q at simulation start ensures well-defined startup behavior, which is beneficial for simulation and formal verification without affecting synthesis or PPA metrics.

No further logic complexity or structural changes are needed as the current logic is near optimal in timing, power, and area. This solution is clean, easy to understand, and synthesis-friendly.

Hence, the final design uses a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge with a non-blocking assignment implementing the JK logic and includes an initial block to reset Q at startup.
