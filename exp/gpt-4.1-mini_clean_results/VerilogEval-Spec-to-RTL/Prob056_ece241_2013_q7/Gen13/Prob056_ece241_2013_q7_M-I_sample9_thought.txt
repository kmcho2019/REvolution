To improve the PPA (timing, power, and area) while keeping the design clear and maintainable, I will integrate the combinational logic directly into the always block without the intermediate wire. This reduces signal routing overhead and allows synthesis tools to better optimize logic and registers as a single unit.

The characteristic equation remains the same for minimal logic and correct behavior: Q_next = (J & ~Q) | (~K & Q).

This approach keeps minimal combinational logic, reduces net count, and maintains synchronous, non-blocking assignment for proper flip-flop inference. It also avoids unnecessary combinational nets that could add routing delay or power.

Overall, this small simplification can yield slightly improved timing and area, while power benefits from reduced signal toggling through minimal intermediate signals.

No functional changes are made, only stylistic and structural for PPA gains.
