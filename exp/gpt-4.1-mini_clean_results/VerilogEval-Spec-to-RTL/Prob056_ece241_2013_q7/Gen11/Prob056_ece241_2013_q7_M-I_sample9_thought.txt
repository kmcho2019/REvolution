The original implementation is already minimal and optimal for a JK flip-flop in terms of logic and timing. However, to further optimize power and potentially timing, especially in ASIC or FPGA implementation, I can add an enable signal derived from J and K that disables clocking the flip-flop when the output is not going to change (i.e., when J=K=0). This reduces unnecessary toggling inside the flip-flop and hence dynamic power without affecting functionality. The logic is: only update Q on clock edge if (J,K) != (0,0).

This can be done by gating the clock enable (implemented as an enable signal for the always block). This subtle change reduces toggling and dynamic power at no area cost. The characteristic nextQ logic remains the same.

This is a power-aware enhancement while maintaining simplicity and minimal logic.
