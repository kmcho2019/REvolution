The original implementation is already minimal and optimal in terms of logic complexity for a JK flip-flop. However, a small improvement can be made to potentially help synthesis tools by removing the explicit wire and writing the next state logic directly inside the always block. This avoids an extra combinational signal, possibly helping in area and slightly reducing power since the intermediate wire nextQ is removed.

Additionally, explicitly resetting Q at initialization can improve simulation clarity and ensure defined startup behavior, although it doesn't affect synthesis PPA.

Overall, this results in the most straightforward and clean code for the JK flip-flop with no overhead.
