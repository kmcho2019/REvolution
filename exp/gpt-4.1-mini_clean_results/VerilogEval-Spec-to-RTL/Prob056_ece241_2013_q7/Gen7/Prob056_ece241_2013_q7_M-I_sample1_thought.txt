To optimize the JK flip-flop logic for better performance and area, replace the conditional (ternary) operator chain with a minimized Boolean expression for the next state. The JK flip-flop's next state Q can be expressed as: next_Q = (j & ~k) | (j & k & ~Q) | (~j & ~k & Q). This eliminates priority encoding in the ternary operator and simplifies the combinational logic, potentially reducing delay and gate count. The sequential update remains the same with a nonblocking assignment at the positive clock edge.
