To optimize the JK flip-flop for better power and potentially area, implement clock gating to avoid unnecessary toggling of Q when J=K=0 (no change state). The clock gating logic will gate the clock only when an update is needed, reducing unnecessary toggles in Q and power consumption. This approach maintains the same functional behavior but improves power by lowering switching activity. The clock gating logic uses a simple enable signal derived from (J|K), enabling the clock only when the state can change. This is a common low-overhead power optimization in synchronous logic.
