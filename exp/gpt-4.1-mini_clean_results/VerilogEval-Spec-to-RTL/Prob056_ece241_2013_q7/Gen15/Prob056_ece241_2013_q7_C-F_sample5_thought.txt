- This fusion benefits from minimal logic, clear state transitions, and good synthesis friendliness.

Thus, this approach:
- Retains minimal logic complexity and optimal timing/power/area.
- Improves clarity by using an always @(*) block for next state instead of a combinational assign.

- Is easily extendable or modifiable.

This design delivers a clean, minimal, and well-documented JK flip-flop ideal for synthesis and verification.
