Implement the JK flip-flop using a behavioral approach with an explicit case statement inside the always block. On each positive clock edge, evaluate the inputs J and K, and update Q accordingly: hold when both zero, reset when K=1,J=0, set when J=1,K=0, and toggle when both are 1. This method clearly maps the JK truth table directly to sequential logic, aiding readability and straightforward verification without relying on combinational intermediate signals.
