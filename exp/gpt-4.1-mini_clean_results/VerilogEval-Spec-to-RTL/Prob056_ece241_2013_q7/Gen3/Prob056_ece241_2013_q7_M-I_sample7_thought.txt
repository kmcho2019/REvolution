To improve the PPA metrics, specifically to reduce power and possibly area, I will optimize the combinational logic expression for nextQ to reduce the number of logic transitions and simplify gate count. 

From the truth table, the JK flip-flop behavior is well-known and can be expressed using:
nextQ = j & ~Q | ~k & Q

This expression combines the set and toggle conditions implicitly:
- If J=1 and Q=0 => nextQ=1 (set)
- If K=0 and Q=1 => nextQ=1 (hold or set)
- The reset (J=0, K=1) and toggle (J=1, K=1) are handled accordingly by logic changes

This reduces logic to two AND gates and one OR gate without negating inputs multiple times. The simplified equation is easier for synthesis tools to optimize, potentially reducing glitches and switching.

Also, maintaining the always block triggered on posedge clk with non-blocking assignments is essential for proper register behavior.

This approach should reduce logic complexity, decrease power due to fewer transitions, and possibly area by using fewer gates.
