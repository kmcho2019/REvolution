Implement the JK flip-flop using a case statement inside the clocked process to explicitly handle all four input combinations of J and K. This approach enhances readability by clearly matching the flip-flop's truth table and makes it straightforward to extend or modify behavior if needed. Although slightly less compact than a Boolean expression, it makes the logic explicit, easing debugging and verification. The state is updated synchronously on the positive clock edge using a non-blocking assignment.
