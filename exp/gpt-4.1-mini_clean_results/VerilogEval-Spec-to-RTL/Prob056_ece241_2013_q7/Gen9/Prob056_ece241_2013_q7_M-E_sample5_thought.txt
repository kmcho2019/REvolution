Instead of directly using a case statement on the concatenated inputs {j, k}, I will implement the JK flip-flop logic using combinational logic equations to derive the next state of Q before registering it on the clock edge. Specifically, the next state Qnext is derived from the JK truth table as: Qnext = j & ~Qold | ~k & Qold. This can be simplified by noting that when J=K=1, Q toggles, which matches the equation Qnext = j & ~Qold + ~k & Qold. After calculating Qnext combinationally, it is then registered on the rising edge of clk. This approach explicitly separates combinational next state logic from the register, enhancing code clarity and making the behavior easier to analyze and modify.
