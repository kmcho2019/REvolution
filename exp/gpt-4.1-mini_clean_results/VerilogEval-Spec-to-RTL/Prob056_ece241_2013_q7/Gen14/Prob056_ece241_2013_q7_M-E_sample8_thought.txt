I will implement the JK flip-flop by explicitly modeling the flip-flop state update as a case statement inside the posedge clocked always block. The case statement will match each combination of J and K inputs to the corresponding Q next state, based on the truth table provided. This approach makes the logic very explicit and readable, showing the direct mapping from inputs to output transitions without relying on algebraic expressions.

This design uses a reg for Q, updates it synchronously at clock edges, and uses a default case to hold the current state, which inherently implements the "no change" condition for J=0 and K=0.

This method may be slightly less compact than a Boolean expression but improves clarity and can be helpful for educational or verification purposes.
