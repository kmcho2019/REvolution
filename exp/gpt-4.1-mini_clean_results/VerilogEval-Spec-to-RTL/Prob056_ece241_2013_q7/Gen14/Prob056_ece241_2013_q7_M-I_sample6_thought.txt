To optimize the JK flip-flop further for better power and area, I will minimize toggling by enabling the flip-flop only when the output changes. I can add an enable condition that checks if the next state differs from the current state. This clock enable approach prevents unnecessary register toggling and reduces dynamic power.

I will compute the next state in a combinational block and only update Q on the clock edge if the new state differs from Q. This approach does not add extra hardware registers and may help the synthesis tool insert clock gating or reduce toggling.

Also, the next state logic remains the same minimal form: Q_next = (J & ~Q) | (~K & Q).

This small change aims to improve power by preventing unnecessary toggling without increasing area or latency, keeping the design synchronous and simple.
