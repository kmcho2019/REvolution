The JK flip-flop can be implemented more simply by directly toggling Q when both J and K are high, setting Q to 1 when J=1 and K=0, resetting Q to 0 when J=0 and K=1, and holding Q otherwise. A straightforward approach is to check these conditions in order inside a clocked always block and assign Q accordingly. This eliminates the use of a combinational expression and makes the logic very clear and minimal.
