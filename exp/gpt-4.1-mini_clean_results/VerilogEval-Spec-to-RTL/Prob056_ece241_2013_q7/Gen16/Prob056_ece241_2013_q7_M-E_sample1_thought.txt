I will implement the JK flip-flop using an explicit sequential always block with a conditional if-else ladder that directly encodes the JK truth table. This approach uses a single always @(posedge clk) block and performs the logic decisions sequentially, making the behavior very clear and easy to verify.

Instead of using the characteristic equation or combinational signals, the output Q will be updated immediately inside the clocked always block by examining the J and K inputs and previous Q state (Qold) explicitly. This is a classical behavioral description of the JK flip-flop.

This method facilitates straightforward debugging and makes the control flow of state transitions very clear, at the slight cost of using sequential conditions rather than combinational logic expressions.

Advantages:
- Very readable and directly reflects the truth table.
- Avoids any intermediate combinational wires.
- Easy to modify or extend for additional features.
- Maintains synchronous behavior on the positive clock edge.

This approach suits educational or verification-focused designs where clarity is preferred over minimal logic expressions.
