To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will integrate the combinational next-state logic directly into the synchronous always block, eliminating the intermediate wire Q_next. This removes the continuous assignment and may allow synthesis tools to optimize the logic more efficiently.

Moreover, to reduce unnecessary toggling and save power, I will add a check inside the always block so that Q only updates when the next state differs from the current state. This conditional update approach can reduce switching activity on Q and downstream logic.

Finally, the logic implementation remains minimal using the characteristic equation, ensuring low combinational complexity and small area. Overall, these changes enhance synthesis friendliness, potentially reduce power through less toggling, and maintain clear, maintainable code.
