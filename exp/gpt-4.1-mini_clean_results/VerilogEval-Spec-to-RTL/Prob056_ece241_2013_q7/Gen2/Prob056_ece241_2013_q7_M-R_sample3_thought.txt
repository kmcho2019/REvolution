Refactor the JK flip-flop implementation by separating the combinational next-state logic from the sequential clocked register update. Compute the next state of Q (nextQ) in a combinational always block based on j, k, and the current Q. Then update Q synchronously on the positive clock edge. This structure improves clarity and synthesis friendliness by avoiding incomplete assignments in a single always block, and clearly specifying next state logic and state updates separately.
