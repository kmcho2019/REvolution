Implement the JK flip-flop using explicit if-else conditions on the inputs inside the posedge clock block. This approach directly expresses the JK behavior with minimal logic and explicitly updates Q only when needed. Using if-else may yield simpler combinational logic during synthesis and is equally clear. The hold condition (J=0, K=0) requires no update to Q to prevent unnecessary toggling. This implementation uses nonblocking assignments for synchronous behavior and minimal logic transitions.
