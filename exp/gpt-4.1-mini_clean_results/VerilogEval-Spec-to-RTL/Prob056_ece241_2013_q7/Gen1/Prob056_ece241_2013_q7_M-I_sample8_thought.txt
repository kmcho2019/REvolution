To avoid synthesis issues caused by the explicit self-assignment `Q <= Q;` in the "no change" case, remove that assignment and use an if-else structure instead of a case statement. In an edge-triggered always block, if no assignment is made to `Q`, it naturally holds its previous value, so no explicit assignment is needed for the "00" case. This simplification improves synthesis clarity and avoids redundant logic, benefiting power and area without changing functionality.
