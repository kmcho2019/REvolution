To fix the synthesis issue, remove the explicit self-assignment `Q <= Q;` in the "00" case. In an edge-triggered always block, if a register is not assigned a new value, it naturally retains its previous value, so no explicit assignment is needed for the "no change" condition. This prevents synthesis tools from misinterpreting the behavior or inferring latches. Retain the other assignments as they correctly implement the JK flip-flop truth table.
