Refactor the JK flip-flop by using a temporary register to hold the next state logic combinationally, then update Q synchronously on the clock edge. This approach separates combinational logic (calculating the next state based on J, K, and current Q) from the sequential update (register capturing next state at posedge clk). This avoids the self-assignment `Q <= Q` in the clocked block and is a canonical way to describe flip-flop behavior, improving synthesis compatibility and clarity.
