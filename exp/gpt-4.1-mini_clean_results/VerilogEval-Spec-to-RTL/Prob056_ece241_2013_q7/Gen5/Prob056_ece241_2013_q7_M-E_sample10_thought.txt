Implement the JK flip-flop using a behavioral approach with a single always @(posedge clk) block and a case statement on the concatenation of {j,k}. This simplifies the design and directly maps the JK truth table without intermediate signals. The output Q is updated based on the four cases:

- 00: Hold previous Q
- 01: Reset Q to 0
- 10: Set Q to 1
- 11: Toggle Q

This approach is minimalistic, clear, and leverages synthesis tools' ability to infer a JK flip-flop from behavioral code. It avoids extra combinational logic, potentially reducing delay and area. It also updates Q on every clock edge, which can be acceptable in designs where power gating is handled elsewhere.
