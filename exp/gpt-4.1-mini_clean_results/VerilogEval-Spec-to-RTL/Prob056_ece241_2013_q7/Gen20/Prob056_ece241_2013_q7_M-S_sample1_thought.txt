The JK flip-flop next state function can be simplified to next_Q = j | (~k & Q). This expression directly implements the JK characteristic equation in a concise manner: when J=1, output is set; when K=1, output is reset; otherwise, hold. This reduces logic complexity and improves readability by using a minimal Boolean expression. The always block captures the state on the positive clock edge. This approach maintains full functionality while simplifying the combinational logic.
