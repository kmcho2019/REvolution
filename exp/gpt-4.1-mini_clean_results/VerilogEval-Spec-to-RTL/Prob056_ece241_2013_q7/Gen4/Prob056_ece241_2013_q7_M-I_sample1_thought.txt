To improve synthesis friendliness and PPA, compute the JK flip-flop next state as a combinational signal before the clocked process using the well-known characteristic equation: Q_next = (J & ~Q) | (~K & Q). This avoids redundant assignments like Q <= Q and simplifies conditional logic, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the design better. Only one non-blocking assignment inside the clocked process updates Q, improving clarity, synthesis results, and reducing power and area by minimizing unnecessary toggling and logic complexity.
