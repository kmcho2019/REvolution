To create a superior JK flip-flop, I will fuse the strengths of both examples:

- From Example 1, I use the explicit Boolean expression that exactly matches the JK truth table, ensuring clarity, correctness, and minimal logic.

- From Example 2, I maintain the simple synchronous always block with non-blocking assignments for proper sequential behavior.

- Additionally, I incorporate a clock enable signal to suppress unnecessary toggling or updates when J=K=0 (hold state). This clock enable avoids redundant register updates, which can reduce power consumption by minimizing switching activity inside the flip-flop and in downstream logic.

- The clock enable signal will be combinationally derived as: en = (j | k). This enables the register only when a state change (set, reset, or toggle) is required.

- The nextQ logic remains as in Example 1 since it cleanly encodes all four JK cases.

- By gating the register update with an enable condition, the design reduces power without adding complex gating logic or latency.

- This approach combines the clarity and correctness of the first example’s next-state logic with the minimalism and simplicity of the second example’s always block, enhanced by power-aware clock gating at RTL level.

The final design thus delivers improved power efficiency, clarity, and reliable JK functionality.
