Implement the JK flip-flop behavior using a case statement inside a synchronous always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock. This approach explicitly handles each J-K combination as a separate case, making the design clear and readable. The case statement uses the concatenation of J and K as the selector, and updates Q accordingly based on the JK flip-flop truth table. This method is straightforward and suitable for easily modifying or extending the logic in the future.
