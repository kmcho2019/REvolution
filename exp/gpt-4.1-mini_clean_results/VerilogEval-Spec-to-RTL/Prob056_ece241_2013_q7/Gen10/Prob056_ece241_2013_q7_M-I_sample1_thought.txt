The existing solution is already concise and synthesizes well with minimal logic and a single flip-flop register. However, to optimize the design for better PPA (timing, power, area), I will:

- Simplify the nextQ logic using a direct Boolean expression derived from the JK flip-flop characteristic equation: Q(next) = JQ' + K'Q

  Specifically:
  nextQ = (j & ~Q) | (~k & Q)

- This expression uses only two product terms and is simpler than the previous three-term expression. This simplification reduces the combinational logic complexity, potentially improving timing (lower delay) and reducing power and area due to fewer logic gates.

- Keep the non-blocking assignment on the positive clock edge for proper sequential logic behavior.

This approach is functionally equivalent to the original design and implements the JK flip-flop truth table accurately with fewer logic operations.
