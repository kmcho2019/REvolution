I will implement the JK flip-flop using an explicit next-state logic calculation in a combinational always block, storing the next state in a temporary reg. This next state will then be registered on the positive edge of clk. This architecture separates combinational logic (determining next Q) and sequential storage (updating Q), making the design clear and easily extensible. The toggling condition (J=1 and K=1) will be handled by inverting the old Q.
