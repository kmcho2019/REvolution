To implement a population count for a 255-bit input, I will sum all the bits of the input vector. Since each bit is 1 or 0, the sum equals the number of '1's. I will use a reduction technique by breaking the input into smaller groups, summing counts of groups and then accumulating to get the final count. The output will be 8 bits wide to accommodate up to 255 (which fits in 8 bits).
