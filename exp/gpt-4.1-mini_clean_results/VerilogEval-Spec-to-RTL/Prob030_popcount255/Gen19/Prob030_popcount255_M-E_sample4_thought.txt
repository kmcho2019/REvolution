This approach benefits from:

- A balanced, fixed-radix tree that efficiently sums counts in log4(N) stages.
- Padding one bit to align the input to 256 bits simplifies grouping and recursion.
- Each tree stage sums fixed-width integers with proper bit-width extension to avoid overflow.
