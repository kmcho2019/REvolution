Implement the TopModule by directly summing the bits of the 255-bit input using Verilog's reduction operator. Since the reduction operator '+' is supported and efficient, this significantly simplifies the design. The result can be assigned to an 8-bit output directly because the maximum count of '1's in 255 bits fits in 8 bits. This approach avoids recursion, additional modules, and complicated adder trees, providing a straightforward and concise solution.
