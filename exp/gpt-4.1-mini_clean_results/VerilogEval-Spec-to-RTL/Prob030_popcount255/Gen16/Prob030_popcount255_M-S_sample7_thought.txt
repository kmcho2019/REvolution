Simplify the popcount by directly summing the input bits using a parallel addition approach without recursion or generate constructs. For 255 bits, perform a tree of fixed-width popcount stages: first sum the input bits in groups of 8 to form 8-bit partial sums, then sum those partial sums hierarchically until a final 8-bit count is obtained. This avoids recursion and multiple module instantiations, reducing code complexity and potentially improving synthesis results while keeping the output width at 8 bits (max 255). The design uses straightforward addition of vectors, making it more readable and efficient.
