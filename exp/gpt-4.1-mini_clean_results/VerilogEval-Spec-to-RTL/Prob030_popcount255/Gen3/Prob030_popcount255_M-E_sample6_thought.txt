Implement a population count by splitting the 255-bit input into 85 blocks of 3 bits each. Each 3-bit block is counted using a small 2-bit popcount logic (max count 3, needing 2 bits). Then the 85 partial 2-bit counts are summed using a wide parallel adder tree with multiple stages. This fine-grained 3-bit chunking reduces local logic in the small popcount units, potentially improving timing and area. The summation tree is organized in stages summing pairs of counts progressively until the final 8-bit count is produced. This approach avoids a large 17-bit summation per block and may allow more efficient mapping onto hardware primitives such as small adders or LUTs.
