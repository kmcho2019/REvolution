To improve the original hierarchical popcount design's PPA, I will reduce area and power by eliminating the zero-padding in the summation tree, replacing the "sum three at a time" approach with a binary adder tree for summations after the popcount5 stage. Summing in pairs reduces the number of adders and fan-in per adder, which can improve timing and power by reducing combinational logic depth and switching activity.

The design will still use popcount5 modules for bottom-level counts. Then, instead of summing three counts at once, I will sum pairs in successive levels until only one sum remains. This is a conventional binary reduction tree, which synthesizes efficiently and reduces unnecessary adders.

Also, I'll size the arrays exactly to the needed size per level and avoid statically sizing to a maximum of 17, eliminating unused hardware.

This approach trades a slightly higher tree depth (log2(51) ~ 6 levels) for simpler adders and less padding, reducing area and power while maintaining clarity.

Final output remains 8 bits, since maximum sum is 255 (8 bits).

This optimized binary tree summation is simpler, less padded, and has fewer large fan-in adders than the original ternary tree.
