Fuse the strengths of both examples by using a structurally instantiated popcount8 module for efficient, small 8-bit chunk counts (as in Example 2) combined with a hierarchical, balanced adder tree summing three operands at a time (radix-3 tree) for the intermediate sums (as in Example 1). This reduces logic depth more than binary trees and balances fan-in.

Specifically:
- Use 32 groups of 8 bits (pad the last one with zero), each fed to a popcount8 module (structural, not function) for a clean synthesis-friendly implementation.
- Stage 1: 32 popcount8 modules output 4-bit counts.
- Stage 2+: Use a radix-3 balanced adder tree summing three inputs at a time for all stages until a single 8-bit output remains.
- Radix-3 tree reduces overall adder stages (log3(32) ≈ 4-5 stages), each with 8-bit wide adders to hold the sums.
- The tree approach balances area, power, and timing better than a binary tree.
- Using structural popcount8 modules avoids the synthesis issues of behavioral functions.
- Fully combinational design with arrays and generate constructs for scalability and clarity.

This fusion yields a scalable, modular, and synthesis-optimized design combining the modular popcount8 efficiency and the balanced radix-3 adder tree benefits.
