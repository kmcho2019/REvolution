Implement the population count by first grouping the 255 input bits into 85 groups of 3 bits each. Use a small, optimized 3-bit popcount module based on a simple combinational logic expression (which outputs 2 bits per group). Then sum these 85 partial 2-bit counts using a balanced multi-level adder tree that reduces them to the final 8-bit count. This approach leverages many small 3-bit popcount units with low complexity, enabling efficient and fast parallel counting of small bit-groups, followed by a well-balanced tree of adders to combine these counts. It minimizes wide adders at the lowest level and uses simpler logic in the first stage, improving overall timing and area.
