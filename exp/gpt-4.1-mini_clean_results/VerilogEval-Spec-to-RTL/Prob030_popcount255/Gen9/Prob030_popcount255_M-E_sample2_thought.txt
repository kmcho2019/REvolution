Implement a multi-stage population count using a balanced adder tree but based on fixed-size small chunks instead of recursive modules. First, partition the 255-bit input into 16 groups of 16 bits each, except the last group which has only 15 bits. Compute partial popcounts of these small groups using a dedicated 16-bit popcount module. Then sum these 16 partial counts using a balanced tree of adders in stages until an 8-bit output is produced. This approach avoids recursion and recursion overhead by leveraging small, optimized popcount units and a well-structured adder tree for partial sums. The module hierarchy improves modularity and timing by having small popcount units and staged addition with known bit-widths, simplifying timing closure and reducing area compared to a fully recursive implementation.
