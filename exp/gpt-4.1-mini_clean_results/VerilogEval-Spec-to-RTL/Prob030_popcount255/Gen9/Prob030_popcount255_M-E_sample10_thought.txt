Implement the 255-bit population count using a hierarchical tree of fixed-width parallel counters that directly sum groups of bits with different granularities, eliminating the intermediate step of uniform 8-bit popcount modules. First, count small 3-bit groups with a 2-bit output since max count is 3. Then sum these 2-bit counts in groups of 4 (sum of four 3-bit groups) using a 4-bit adder, and so on, doubling the group size each stage until the full input is reduced to a single count. This approach uses smaller, simpler counters at the lowest level, reducing the width of intermediate adders early and balancing the tree so that no adder becomes too wide. It also eliminates padding since 255 is divisible by 3 into 85 groups, plus a leftover 0 bits (255=3*85 exactly), avoiding zero padding. The sums cascade through stages of 2-bit, 4-bit, 6-bit, 7-bit, and finally 8-bit adders to yield the final 8-bit population count. All modules are explicitly instantiated and combinational without loops or generate, favoring modular and flat structure for synthesis.
