To optimize the popcount255 implementation for better performance, power, and area (PPA), we retain the general partition and hierarchical summation approach but improve the summation stages to reduce critical path delay and area.

Key improvements:
1. Use carry-save adder (CSA) style 3-input partial sum stages to reduce the number of sequential binary adders and the overall critical path. CSA allows summing multiple operands with only a single carry propagate addition at the end.
2. Partition the 255-bit input into 15 groups of 17 bits each as before, each handled by the optimized popcount17 module.
3. The popcount17 module is kept combinational and similar but uses minimal width adders.
4. Instead of pairwise binary additions, use CSA trees to sum the fifteen 6-bit partial counts with fewer sequential carry propagate additions.
5. Finally, a single carry propagate adder combines the CSA results.
6. This reduces the total number of multi-bit adders and depth of carry propagation, improving frequency and reducing power due to fewer transitions on wide adders.
7. Maintain explicit wiring for clarity but optimize the summation tree accordingly.

This approach balances area and delay better than the original purely pairwise addition tree.
