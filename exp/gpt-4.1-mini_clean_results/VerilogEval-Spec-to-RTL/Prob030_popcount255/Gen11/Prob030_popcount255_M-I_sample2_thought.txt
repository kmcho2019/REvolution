To optimize the provided population count circuit for better PPA, we keep the modular and balanced structure but reduce area and power by:

1. Using a more compact popcount8 module based on a single-level parallel count using addition of bits through a 3-stage adder tree with fewer wires and simpler summations.
2. Implementing the summation tree with carry-save adder (CSA) stages to reduce the critical path delay and total adder area by accumulating partial sums in carry-save form, only performing the final addition at the end.
3. Avoiding the use of the last popcount8 padded with zero by instead processing the last 7 bits as 7 separate bits summed directly.
4. Minimizing wire width where possible and removing unused unused bits to save area.
5. Using generate loops and structural constructs for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
6. Keeping fully combinational logic for easy insertion into synchronous design and low latency.

This approach reduces the number of full adders and wide adders in the adder tree, reducing power and area, and improves timing via the carry-save adder tree method, which minimizes carry propagation delays.

The final output remains 8-bit wide, correctly counting all 255 bits.

Overall, the design gains from reduced complexity in partial popcount and efficient carry-save accumulation in the summation tree, leading to improved power and area with similar or better timing.
