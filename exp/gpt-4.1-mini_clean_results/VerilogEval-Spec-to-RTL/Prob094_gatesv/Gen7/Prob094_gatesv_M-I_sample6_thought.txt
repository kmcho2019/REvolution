To optimize for better power and area while keeping the logic simple and combinational, I will replace the generate loops with vector operations and explicit bit slicing for constant bits, reducing the number of individual assignments and net declarations. This consolidates the logic expressions into compact vector operations that synthesis tools can often optimize better. Specifically:

- out_both[0:2] = in[0:2] & in[1:3]; out_both[3] = 0
- out_any[1:3] = in[1:3] | in[0:2]; out_any[0] = 0
- out_different = in ^ {in[2:0], in[3]}; // rotation for wrap-around

This approach reduces the generate block overhead, minimizes net count, and lets the synthesizer infer optimal gates and wiring, potentially saving area and power.

The logical behavior remains identical.
