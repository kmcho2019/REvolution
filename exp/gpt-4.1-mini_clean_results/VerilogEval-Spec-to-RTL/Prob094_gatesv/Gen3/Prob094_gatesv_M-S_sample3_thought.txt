We can simplify by using vector rotations and basic bitwise operations to generate all outputs in a compact way without individual bit indexing:

- out_both: Perform bitwise AND of 'in' with itself rotated left by 1 bit, then mask out the MSB bit (since in[3] has no left neighbor).
- out_any: Perform bitwise OR of 'in' with itself rotated right by 1 bit, then mask out the LSB bit (since in[0] has no right neighbor).
- out_different: Perform bitwise XOR of 'in' with itself rotated left by 1 bit (wrap-around).

This removes explicit indexing and zero assignments while preserving functionality.
