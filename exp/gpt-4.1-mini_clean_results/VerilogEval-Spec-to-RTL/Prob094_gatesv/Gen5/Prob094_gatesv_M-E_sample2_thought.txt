Use combinational continuous assignments with vector slicing and concatenation to compute the outputs in a fully parallel manner without loops or always blocks. This approach leverages vector operations and Verilog's bitwise operators to express neighbor relationships directly and compactly.

- For out_any, shift the input right by one bit, pad the MSB with 0, and OR with the original input.
- For out_different, perform a bitwise XOR of the input with itself rotated left by one bit (achieved via concatenation).

This method provides a concise, high-performance, and area-efficient solution ideal for a fixed-width 4-bit input, with no procedural blocks, making synthesis straightforward and fast.
