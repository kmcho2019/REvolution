To optimize the original code for better PPA (power, performance, area), we eliminate the for-loop and modulo operations by explicitly coding the logic for each bit. This removes the synthesis overhead of the modulo operator and loop constructs, reducing combinational complexity and area. We also hardwire bits without neighbors (out_both[3], out_any[0]) to zero outside the logic to avoid unnecessary toggling and logic generation, helping power. The explicit logic improves clarity and eases synthesis optimizations. Since the bus is small (4 bits), this approach is practical and beneficial.
