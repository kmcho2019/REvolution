To optimize for better PPA while preserving the original behavior, I will manually unroll all assignments to avoid generate loops and modulo indexing, which can simplify synthesis and potentially improve timing and area. Since the vector size is fixed (4 bits), direct explicit assignments reduce indexing overhead and clarify constant bits. Also, to reduce power, I will keep unused bits tied to zero explicitly with constant assignments, so no unnecessary logic toggles these bits. The XOR wrap-around is preserved as is because it's required. This approach results in cleaner, flatter logic and may help synthesis tools optimize better.
