To optimize for better area and power while maintaining clarity and correctness, we can leverage vector operations and signal shifts instead of multiple generate loops and bitwise operations. This reduces the number of separate gates synthesized by the tool by merging logic across bits. Specifically:

- Use shifted versions of the input vector to create neighbor comparisons compactly.
- For out_both: AND 'in[3:1]' with 'in[2:0]' shifted left by 1, outputting zeros where neighbors do not exist.
- For out_any: OR 'in[3:1]' with 'in[2:0]' shifted right by 1, outputting zeros where neighbors do not exist.
- For out_different: XOR 'in' with a 1-bit left circular shift of 'in' to include wrap-around in a single vector operation.

This reduces explicit zero assignments and replaces multiple single-bit assign statements with fewer vector operations, improving area and power by enabling the synthesis tool to optimize the combined logic better. This approach also maintains full combinational logic with no loops or generate blocks, making the code simpler and more concise.
