All outputs depend only on bitwise operations between adjacent bits. We can use circular shifts to simplify neighbor accesses uniformly. For out_both, shifting left once gives the neighbor to the left; for out_any, shifting right once gives the neighbor to the right; for out_different, XOR with the left neighbor with wrap-around is needed. Using vector rotations uniformly removes explicit zero assignments by masking out invalid bits (positions with no neighbor) directly with a zero mask. This reduces complexity and keeps the functionality intact.
