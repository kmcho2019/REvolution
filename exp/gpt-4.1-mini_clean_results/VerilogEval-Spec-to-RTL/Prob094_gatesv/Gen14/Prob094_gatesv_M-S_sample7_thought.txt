Use direct vectorized bitwise operations for all outputs without any generate loops or intermediate wires. For out_both, shift the input left by one and AND with the original, placing 0 in the MSB. For out_any, shift the input right by one and OR with the original, placing 0 in the LSB. For out_different, perform a vector XOR between the input and a rotated version (left rotate by one), directly producing the output. This simplifies the code, reduces lines, and maintains the exact required functionality.
