Implement bitwise logic based on given neighbor relationships. For out_both, set bits 0 to 2 to the AND of the current bit and its left neighbor; out_both[3] is zero since no left neighbor. For out_any, set bits 1 to 3 to the OR of the current bit and its right neighbor; out_any[0] is zero since no right neighbor. For out_different, compare each bit with its left neighbor, wrapping around at the edges, and assign 1 if different else 0.
