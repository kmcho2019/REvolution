Implement a TopModule that calculates three 4-bit outputs based on relationships between each bit and its neighbors in the 4-bit input vector:
1. out_both[i] = in[i] & in[i+1], for i=0..2; out_both[3]=0 (since no left neighbor for in[3]).
2. out_any[i] = in[i] | in[i-1], for i=1..3; out_any[0]=0 (since no right neighbor for in[0]).
3. out_different[i] = (in[i] != in[(i+1) mod 4]) for all i, wrapping around so neighbor of in[3] is in[0].
