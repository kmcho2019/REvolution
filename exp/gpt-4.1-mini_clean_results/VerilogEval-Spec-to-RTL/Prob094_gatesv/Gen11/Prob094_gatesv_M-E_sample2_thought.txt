Utilize bitwise vector operations combined with bit shifts to compute the neighbor-based outputs compactly and efficiently. Specifically, shift the input vector left or right by one bit to align neighbors, then perform bitwise AND, OR, and XOR operations to generate out_both, out_any, and out_different outputs respectively. For edge bits where neighbors do not exist, explicitly mask those bits to zero. For out_different, implement wrap-around neighbor by shifting and using bitwise rotation achieved with concatenation. This vectorized approach reduces code verbosity, logic duplication, and is easily scalable to wider inputs while maintaining explicit handling of boundary conditions.
