To optimize for power, performance, and area, switch from reg outputs driven in an always block to wire outputs driven by continuous assign statements. This removes the overhead of always blocks and reg types, letting synthesis tools optimize better. Replace the modulus operator (%) with explicit index expressions since the vector size is fixed and small. Unroll assignments without loops to eliminate synthesis ambiguity and simplify generated logic. Initialize bits that don't have neighbors to zero explicitly. This approach yields clearer logic, potentially smaller area, and reduced combinational delay without altering functionality.
