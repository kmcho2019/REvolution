To improve PPA, I will eliminate vector concatenations with mixed widths by directly assigning each bit individually or via simpler bitwise operations and shifts. For out_both and out_any, I'll perform bitwise AND/OR between adjacent bits by shifting the input vector, which synthesis tools can optimize well. For out_different, I'll implement wrap-around XOR with a simple shift and XOR without concatenations. This approach enhances clarity, potentially reduces logic levels, and enables better synthesis optimizations. Also, explicitly zero out bits with no valid neighbors by direct assignment rather than concatenation to avoid potential overhead. The overall logic remains fully combinational and minimal.
