To optimize for power and area, avoid driving output bits that are always zero, by using 3-bit wide outputs for out_both and out_any corresponding only to meaningful bits. For out_different, keep it 4-bit as all bits are meaningful due to wrap-around. This reduces toggling and logic synthesis complexity on unused bits. Also, group output bits as vectors where possible for cleaner and slightly more efficient synthesis. This change reduces output port widths and logic gates, improving power and area without impacting functionality. The interface ports are changed accordingly.
