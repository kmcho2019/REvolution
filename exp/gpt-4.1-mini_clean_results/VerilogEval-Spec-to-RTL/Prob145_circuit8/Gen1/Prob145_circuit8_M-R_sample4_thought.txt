The original design updates p on the rising edge and q on the falling edge of the clock, causing potential timing issues and complexity. To refactor but keep the core idea, use a synchronous single-edge clocking approach with a state machine.

We will:

- Use only the positive edge of clock for all sequential updates.
- Implement internal state registers for p and q.
- Use combinational logic to determine the next value of p based on current q and input a.
- On each clock rising edge, update both p and q (q takes the previous p's value).
- This aligns all updates and avoids race conditions.
- Initialize p and q to 0 at start to match stable initial behavior.

This approach simplifies timing, preserves the behavior that q follows p from the previous cycle, and that p depends on a and q, but all synchronized on posedge clock.
