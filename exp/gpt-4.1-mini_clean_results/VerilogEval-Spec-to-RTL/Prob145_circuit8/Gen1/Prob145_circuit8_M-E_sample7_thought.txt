Observations:

- On falling clock edges, q updates based on p.
- From the pattern at 90ns, 100ns, 110ns, p is set to 1 when 'a' is 1 and q is 0, and q latches p at falling edge.
- At some points when q=1, p holds 1 or resets to 0 depending on a.
- The outputs p and q essentially behave as a two-bit state machine.

  - If q==0, p <= a.

- On negedge:
  - q <= p.
