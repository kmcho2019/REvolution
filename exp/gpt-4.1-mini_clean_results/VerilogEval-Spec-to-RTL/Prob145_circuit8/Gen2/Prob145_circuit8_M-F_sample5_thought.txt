- Use a state-holding register internally to hold previous 'p' or 'q' to produce the correct output behavior.
- Remove dependency on detecting unknown states 'x', and instead initialize outputs to 0 at start (matching waveform from first valid outputs at 25ns).
- Analyze waveform: 'p' goes high on posedge when 'a' is 1 and previous 'q' or 'p' is at a certain state.

- Implement a one-clock cycle delayed version of 'p' into 'q', both updated on posedge clock.
