The waveform behavior suggests p updates to the input 'a' only on rising clock edges, and q updates to the previous p value on falling clock edges, holding their states otherwise. Initial values of p and q are unknown (x), and thereafter they retain their values until updated on clock edges.

We can simplify by:
- Using two always blocks triggered on posedge and negedge clock.
- On posedge clock, assign p <= a.
- On negedge clock, assign q <= p.
- Initialize both outputs to 'x' to match initial unknown state.

This straightforward approach matches the waveform's timing and state holding while reducing complexity.
