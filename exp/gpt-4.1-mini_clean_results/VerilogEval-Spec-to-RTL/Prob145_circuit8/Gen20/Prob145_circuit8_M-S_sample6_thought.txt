The waveform suggests that p is updated on the rising edge of the clock depending on the current input a and the previous state q, while q holds its value until updated on the next clock cycle. To simplify, implement both p and q as registers updated on the positive clock edge: p gets the value of a & q, and q gets the previous p value.

This single-edge approach preserves the sequential dependency between p and q seen in the waveform while avoiding dual-edge triggering and complexity. Initial values of p and q can start at zero to remove unknown states.

This simplified design captures the essential sequential behavior indicated by the waveform while being synthesizable and less error-prone.
