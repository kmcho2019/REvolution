The waveform suggests a simple 2-bit state register formed by outputs p and q updated on the rising edge of clock. The state transition logic can be simplified to:

- On each clock rising edge:
  - p <= a OR p
  - q <= p OR q

This means:
- p sets to 1 if input a is 1 or p was already 1.
- q sets to 1 if p was previously 1 or q was already 1.

This simple latching behavior matches the observed waveform where p turns on when 'a' is high and remains high until cleared, and q latches p's previous state. This approach is much simpler and captures the sequential behavior without complex FSM cases.

Initialize p and q to 0 at start.
