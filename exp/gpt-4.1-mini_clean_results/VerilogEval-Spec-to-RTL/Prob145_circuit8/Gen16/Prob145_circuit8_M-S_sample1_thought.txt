The waveform shows a sequential circuit with outputs p and q changing synchronously on clock edges. The outputs initially start undefined and then become defined at the first rising edge of the clock. Observing the changes:

- p updates on rising clock edges, seemingly latching input a when q is low.
- q updates one clock cycle later, tracking previous p.
- Both p and q hold their states on clock low.
- At the negative clock edge, there is no update; all changes happen synchronously on rising edges.

Therefore, a simplified approach is to implement a two-stage shift register triggered on the positive clock edge:

- On each rising edge, q takes the old p value.
- On each rising edge, p takes the new input a only if q is zero; otherwise, p keeps its previous value.

This eliminates dual-edge triggered logic and avoids complexity, matching the observed waveform timing and behavior.

Initialize p and q as zero on reset to avoid unknown initial outputs in simulation.
