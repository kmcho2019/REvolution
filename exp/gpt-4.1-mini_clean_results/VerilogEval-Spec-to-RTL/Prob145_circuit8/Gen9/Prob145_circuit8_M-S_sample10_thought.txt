The outputs p and q represent a 2-bit state updated synchronously on the clock's rising edge. From the waveform, initially p and q are unknown, then at the first rising clock edge they start from 0. The state updates on each rising edge based only on current p, q, and input a.

The state transitions are straightforward:

- On reset (initial condition), p=0, q=0.
- On each posedge clock, p and q update according to these rules:
  - p <= a & ~q | p & (a | q)
  - q <= p | q & ~a

This logic can be derived to match the waveform behavior and keeps the design simple.

We implement a synchronous reset by initializing p and q to 0 at the first posedge clock (when they are unknown).

This approach uses only one always block, minimal logic, and no complex case statements.
