The waveform shows that outputs p and q are sequentially updated on the rising edge of clock only. At time 25ns (first rising edge), p is 0 and q is undefined (x). Later on, p changes based on input a and q retains or updates more slowly, appearing as a delayed version of p.

This suggests a simple two-bit state machine with two flip-flops (p and q), both updated on the posedge of clock.

A simple and intuitive approach is:
- On every posedge clock, update p with input a.
- On the same posedge clock, update q with previous p (q is a delayed version of p by one clock cycle).

This matches the pattern where q lags p by one clock cycle and p directly follows input a at each clock.

This approach removes negedge triggers, simplifying design and maintaining the sequential nature. It also removes combinational feedback between p and q inside the clock cycle, which matches the waveform's stepwise transitions.

Initial undefined states are acceptable since no reset is specified.

Hence:
- At posedge clock: p <= a; q <= p;
