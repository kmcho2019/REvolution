The waveform shows that p updates only on the rising clock edge when a=1; otherwise p remains 0. q updates on the falling clock edge to hold the previous p value and maintains that value until the next falling edge.

To simplify: Use two flip-flops, one clocked on the rising edge for p which captures a when high, zero otherwise; the other clocked on falling edge for q which captures p. Initialize both to 0.

This matches the original behavior while keeping the design minimal.
