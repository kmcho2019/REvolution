Simplify the design by using a single clock edge (rising edge) to sequentially update both p and q. Observing the waveform, p updates first based on a and q, and q then holds the previous p value. This behavior corresponds to a two-stage shift-like register where on each clock rising edge p becomes a & q, and q becomes the old p. Implementing both updates in one always block with nonblocking assignments ensures proper sequencing without dual-edge sensitivity, simplifying the circuit while maintaining the functional behavior.
