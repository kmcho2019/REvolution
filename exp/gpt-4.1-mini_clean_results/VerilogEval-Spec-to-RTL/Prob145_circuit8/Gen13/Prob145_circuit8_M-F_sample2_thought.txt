Analyzing the waveform:
- Initially, p and q are undefined (x).
- At the first clock posedge with a=0 (time 25ns), p=0 and q=x.
- On later posedges (with a toggling), p sometimes becomes 1 only when a=1 and q=0, suggesting p depends on both a and q.
- q updates only at negedge clock, taking the previous p value.
- At negedge clock (e.g., 115ns), q takes the value of p from the previous posedge, suggesting q is a delayed version of p by half a clock cycle.
- p updates only at posedge clock, based on a and q.

This indicates a dual-clock edge triggered circuit:
- At posedge clock: p <= a & ~q (p is set when a=1 and q=0)
- At negedge clock: q <= p

This matches the waveform where p is driven by a and q, and q latches p at negedge.

Hence, the corrected design uses two always blocks:
- posedge clock: p <= a & ~q;
- negedge clock: q <= p;

This matches the observed sequential behavior and waveform, properly implementing the circuit with p updated at posedge clock depending on current a and q, and q updated at negedge clock with previous p.
