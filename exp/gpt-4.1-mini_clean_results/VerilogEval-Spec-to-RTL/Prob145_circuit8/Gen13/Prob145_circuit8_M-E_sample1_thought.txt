Implement a two-bit state register that captures the sequential behavior of outputs p and q together. Use a single always block triggered on the rising edge of the clock where the two-bit state {p, q} is updated synchronously. The next state logic is derived from the input a and the current state, specifically: p_next = a & q_current, and q_next = p_current. This approach cleanly captures the mutual dependency and timing of p and q as a single state vector updated atomically on the clock's rising edge, avoiding separate updates on both edges and providing a stable, deterministic sequential circuit consistent with the waveform.
