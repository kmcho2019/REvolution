The waveform suggests that output `p` updates synchronously on the rising edge of `clock` based on input `a`, and output `q` updates synchronously on the falling edge of `clock` reflecting the previous value of `p`. This can be modeled as a simple two-flip-flop chain clocked on opposite edges of the clock.

To simplify, we keep two always blocks: one triggered on the rising edge that updates `p` with `a`, and one triggered on the falling edge that updates `q` with the current `p`. We initialize both outputs to zero to avoid unknown states.

This approach keeps the functionality but avoids any unnecessary complexity, precisely matching the waveform behavior.
