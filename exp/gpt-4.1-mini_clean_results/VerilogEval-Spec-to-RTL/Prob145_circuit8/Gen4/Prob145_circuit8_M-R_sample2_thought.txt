Refactor the design by explicitly separating the state storage for p and q into internal registers and defining their next-state logic combinationally. 

- Use two internal registers: p_reg and q_reg to hold the state.
- Define combinational signals p_next and q_next for their next values.
- On the rising edge, update p_reg <= p_next.
- On the falling edge, update q_reg <= q_next.
- According to the original behavior:
  - p updates on rising clock edge based on input a and current q_reg.
  - q updates on falling clock edge by latching the current p_reg.
- Outputs p and q directly reflect p_reg and q_reg respectively.
- This structure improves clarity and aligns with typical FSM coding style.
- Initialize p_reg and q_reg to 0 to match initial waveform.
This approach maintains the same behavior but is clearer, easier to modify, and separates combinational next-state logic from sequential updates.
