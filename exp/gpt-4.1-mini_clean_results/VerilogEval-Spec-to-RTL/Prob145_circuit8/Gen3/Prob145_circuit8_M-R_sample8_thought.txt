To detect the rising edge of 'a', sample and store 'a' from the previous clock cycle and compare it with the current 'a' at each rising clock edge; 'p' is asserted when a rises (previous 'a' low, current 'a' high).

For 'q', treat it as a set-reset flip-flop updated synchronously on clock rising edge:
- Set 'q' when 'p' is asserted (indicating detection of rising 'a').
- Reset 'q' when clock is low and 'a' is high between clock edges, so we store a flag for this condition and reset 'q' at the next clock edge.
  
Since we cannot sample clock at negedge in always blocks easily, we add a small combinational circuit to detect the reset condition asynchronously, then apply reset synchronously at the next clock edge.
