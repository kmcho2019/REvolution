The circuit has two outputs p and q that appear to be state signals updated on the clock's rising edge. At the first active clock (25ns), p is 0 and q is undefined (x), then q is assigned on the following falling clock edge at 115ns. It looks like p updates on the rising edge depending on input a and current q, while q updates on falling edges depending on p and a. 

From the waveform:
- p updates on rising edge as: p = a & q (except initial conditions).
- q updates on falling edge as: q = p | a.
- Initial state at first clock rising edge is p=0 and q=0.
- Between clocks, p and q hold their values.

So p updates on posedge clock, q updates on negedge clock:
- On posedge clock: p <= a & q
- On negedge clock: q <= p | a

We can implement this with two always blocks sensitive to posedge and negedge clock, holding two registers p and q.

Initial values: p=0, q=0.

Outputs are registered values of p and q.
