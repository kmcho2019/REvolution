The outputs p and q are sequentially updated on the rising edge of clock. The output p is set to 1 when a is 1 at the clock edge (with some latching behavior). The output q seems to latch to 1 when p transitions to 1 and holds it until some conditions. Analyzing the waveform: 
- Initially, p and q are unknown (x). 
- At the first rising edge of clock (25ns), p=0, q=x, and q is still undefined until the next falling edge where q initializes to 0. 
- p changes to 1 at clock edges when input a=1.
- q goes to 1 after p has been 1 and clock is low.
- q remains 1 once set, until a reset condition seems to happen when clock is 0 and a is 1, q goes to 0 at 175ns and 180ns.

Summary:
- p updates on rising clock edge: p <= a.
- q updates on falling clock edge: if p=1 then q=1 else q=0.
- q seems to store if p was 1 at the previous rising edge.
- q is cleared when clock is 0 and a=1 at falling clock edges.

This suggests q follows p delayed by a falling clock edge latch and also has reset conditions. We will implement p as a register updated at posedge clock with p <= a. q will be updated at negedge clock based on p and input a. The exact logic for q at negedge clock is q <= (p) | (q & ~ (clock & a)) but since clock is 0 at negedge, clock & a=0 so q holds if previously set, unless explicitly cleared when a=1 and clock=0 at falling edge. From waveforms, at 175ns (clock=0,a=1), q goes 1->0. So at negedge clock, if a=1, q <= 0 else q <= q or p.

Implement accordingly.
