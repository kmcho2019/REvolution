Analyze the given waveforms and signals:

- At clock=1, p updates based on input a and previous state:
  At 90ns (clock=1, a=1), p=1.
  At 100ns (clock=1, a=1), p=1.
  At 95ns and 105ns (clock=1, a=0), p=0.
  So p seems to latch the value of a on the rising clock edge.

- q changes at falling clock edge and depends on previous p and q:
  At 115ns (clock=0), q goes from x to 1 when p was 1 before.
  Then q remains 1 during subsequent clocks.

- At clock=1, when p=1 and q=1, at 165ns, p stays 1 and q stays 1.

Summary:
- On rising edge of clock, p <= a.
- On falling edge of clock, q <= p.

Therefore, p samples a on rising clock edge, q samples p on falling clock edge.

Implement this with two always blocks sensitive to posedge and negedge of clock.
Initialize p and q to 0.
