From the waveform, the circuit outputs p and q based on the clock and input a with some memory. On the rising edge of clock:

- When q=0, p follows the input a (p <= a).
- When q=1, p stays at 1 if a=1; otherwise, it resets to 0.
- q updates only on the falling edge of clock and latches p's value.

Analyzing transitions:

- At rising clock edge, p updates:
  - If q=0: p = a.
  - If q=1: p stays at 1 if a=1 else 0.

- At falling clock edge, q <= p.

This behavior suggests q latches the p value on falling clock edge, while p is controlled by a and q on rising edge.

Implement two sequential always blocks triggered by clock edges. Initialize p and q to 0 at start.
