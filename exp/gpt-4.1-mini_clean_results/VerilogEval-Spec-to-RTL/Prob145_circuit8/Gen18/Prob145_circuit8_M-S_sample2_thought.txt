The waveform indicates that outputs `p` and `q` form a 2-bit state machine updated synchronously on the rising edge of the clock, with no reset and initial unknown states. Observing the waveforms, `p` behaves like the current input `a` gated by the previous value of `q`, and `q` holds the previous value of `p`. This creates a simple shift-register or delay chain where `p` depends on `a` and the previous state of `q`, and `q` updates from the previous `p`.

To simplify, update both `p` and `q` on the rising clock edge:
- `p` <= `a` & `q`
- `q` <= previous `p`

This avoids dual-edge clocking, making the design simpler and consistent with synchronous design best practices, while matching the waveform behavior closely.
