The waveform suggests a two-stage sequential circuit with outputs p and q updated synchronously on clock edges. However, p does not simply follow input a, nor is q always the previous p value. Instead, q seems to latch p's value only when the clock is low, and p updates only on the positive edge of the clock with some dependency on a and q.

Observing the pattern:
- p updates only on posedge clock.
- q updates only on negedge clock.
- p appears to be set to 1 if a=1 or q=1 at posedge clock, otherwise 0.
- q holds p's value at the previous posedge clock, but updates on negedge clock.

This can be modeled as two clock-edge triggered flip-flops:
- p updates on posedge clock based on a and current q.
- q updates on negedge clock to hold p's value.

This approach explains the delayed and conditional updates of p and q, matches the waveform's initial unknown state, and the observed transitions.

Hence, implement two always blocks:
- one for p triggered on posedge clock,
- one for q triggered on negedge clock.

Initialize p and q as unknown initially to reflect x states.
