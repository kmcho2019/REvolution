Analyze the waveform to deduce the logic of outputs p and q relative to clock and input a. Observing the given data, q updates only on the falling edge of clock (clock=0), reflecting the registered value of p just before falling edge. Meanwhile, p updates synchronously on the rising edge of clock (clock=1) based on input a and previous q. In other words, p changes at posedge clock using current a and previous q, and q changes at negedge clock to the last value of p at the preceding posedge clock. This makes p a synchronous function of (a, q), and q a delayed version of p updated at negedge clock. Implement this dual-edge process: a posedge always block to update p from a and q, and a negedge always block to latch p into q. This novel architecture directly models the sequential timing observed in waveform and cleanly separates updates of p and q.
