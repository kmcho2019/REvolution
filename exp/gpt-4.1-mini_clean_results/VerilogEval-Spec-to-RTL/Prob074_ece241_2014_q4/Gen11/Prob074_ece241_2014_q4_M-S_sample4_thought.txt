The FSM can be implemented straightforwardly by storing the outputs of the three gates in registers updated on the positive clock edge. Since the problem states flip-flops are initially zero but does not provide a reset, we'll use an initial block to initialize registers for simulation convenience.

To simplify:
- Declare three registers q_xor, q_and, q_or to hold flip-flop states.
- Compute their D inputs combinationally from input x and the current q_* values.
- Update q_* on clk rising edge.
- Output z is the NOR of these three registers.
- Remove any unnecessary comments or complexity.

This implementation directly matches the problem and is easy to understand, simulate, and synthesize (assuming the synthesis tool supports init values or that the initial state is handled externally).
