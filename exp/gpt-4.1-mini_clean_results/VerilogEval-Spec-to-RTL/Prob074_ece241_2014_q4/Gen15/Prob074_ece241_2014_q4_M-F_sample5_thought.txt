The problem requires the flip-flops to be initially reset to zero before the machine begins but does not specify an explicit reset input. To comply strictly with the problem statement and avoid adding a new port, I will remove the synchronous reset input and instead use Verilog's initial block to initialize the flip-flops to zero, which is acceptable for simulation and common for FPGA synthesis tools to interpret as initial state.

All combinational logic for computing the D inputs will remain separated for clarity, and the flip-flops will be updated on the positive clock edge without any reset condition.

The output `z` will be computed continuously as the NOR of the three flip-flops' outputs.

This approach satisfies the problem's specification and eliminates issues caused by a floating reset input.
