I will simplify the design by implementing the three flip-flops directly inside the TopModule without separate modules or reset signals. Since the problem states the flip-flops start at zero before the machine begins, I will initialize the flip-flops' registers with zero using the Verilog register initialization syntax. This approach is widely supported in simulation and many synthesis tools and effectively models the initial zero state without additional reset logic.

On every positive clock edge, the flip-flops will capture the outputs of the specified gates:
- XOR gate input: x XOR q_xor
- AND gate input: x AND (complement of q_and)
- OR gate input: x OR (complement of q_or)

The output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.

This reduces complexity by avoiding separate modules and reset logic while meeting the specification of starting from zero.
