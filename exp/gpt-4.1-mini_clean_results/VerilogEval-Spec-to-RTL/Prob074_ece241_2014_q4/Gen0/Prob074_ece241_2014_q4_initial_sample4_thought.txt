Implement three D flip-flops initialized to zero. For each flip-flop, compute the D input as specified:
- D0 = x XOR Q0 (Q0 is output of flip-flop 0)
- D1 = x AND (~Q1)
- D2 = x OR (~Q2)

On every positive edge of clk, update Q0, Q1, Q2 with their D inputs. Finally, compute output z as the NOR of Q0, Q1, and Q2.
