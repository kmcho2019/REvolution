Implement three D flip-flops initialized to zero. For each flip-flop, the D input is the result of a two-input gate with the input x and a specific feedback from the flip-flop's output or complemented output: XOR with Q, AND with ~Q, OR with ~Q. On each rising clock edge, update flip-flops with computed D values. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
