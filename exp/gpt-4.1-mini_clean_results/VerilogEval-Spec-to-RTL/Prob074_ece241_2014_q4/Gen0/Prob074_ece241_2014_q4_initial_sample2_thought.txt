Implement three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or) each clocked by clk and initially reset to zero. For each flip-flop, compute the D input based on the logic: for ff_xor, D = x XOR current ff_xor output; for ff_and, D = x AND complement of ff_and output; for ff_or, D = x OR complement of ff_or output. Update the flip-flops on the positive edge of clk. Output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
