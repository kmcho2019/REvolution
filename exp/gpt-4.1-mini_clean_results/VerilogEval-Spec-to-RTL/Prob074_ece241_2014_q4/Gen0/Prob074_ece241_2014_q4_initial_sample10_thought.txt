Implement three D flip-flops initialized to zero. Each flip-flop takes as input the output of a two-input gate with x and the flip-flop output or its complement as the second input. The first flip-flop input is XOR of x and its Q output, the second is AND of x and complemented Q, the third is OR of x and complemented Q. On each clock edge, update the flip-flops. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
