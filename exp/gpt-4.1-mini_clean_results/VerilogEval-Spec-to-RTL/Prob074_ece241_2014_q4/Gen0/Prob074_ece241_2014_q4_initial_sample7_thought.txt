Implement three D flip-flops, each clocked by clk and initially reset to zero. For each flip-flop:
- The first flip-flop's D input is XOR of x and its own current output.
- The second flip-flop's D input is AND of x and complement of its own current output.
- The third flip-flop's D input is OR of x and complement of its own current output.
The output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.
The flip-flops are synchronous with positive-edge clock and synchronous reset to zero.
