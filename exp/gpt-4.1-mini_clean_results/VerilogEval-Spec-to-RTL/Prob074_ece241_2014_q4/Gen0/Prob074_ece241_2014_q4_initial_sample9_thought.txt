The design consists of three D flip-flops, each clocked by clk and initialized to 0. For each flip-flop, the D input is derived from x combined with the current output of that flip-flop (or its complement) through a two-input gate: XOR, AND, or OR.

Specifically:
- For the XOR path: D input = x XOR Q
- For the AND path: D input = x AND (~Q)
- For the OR path: D input = x OR (~Q)

At every positive clock edge, the flip-flops sample their respective D inputs. The outputs Q of the flip-flops then feed into a 3-input NOR gate to produce output z.

This structure is implemented sequentially with initial reset to 0.
