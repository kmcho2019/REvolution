Implement three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or) each driven by a 2-input gate whose inputs are x and either the current state or its complement. Specifically, for ff_xor D input is XOR of x and ff_xor output; for ff_and D input is AND of x and complement of ff_and output; for ff_or D input is OR of x and complement of ff_or output. On each clock positive edge, the flip-flops capture their respective D inputs. The output z is the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs. Initialize all flip-flops to zero.
