Implement three D flip-flops with initial reset to zero. For each flip-flop:
- Compute the D input based on the input x and the flip-flop's current output or its complement:
  - XOR gate: D = x ^ Q
  - AND gate: D = x & ~Q
  - OR gate: D = x | ~Q
On the rising edge of the clock, update each flip-flop with its D input. The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.
