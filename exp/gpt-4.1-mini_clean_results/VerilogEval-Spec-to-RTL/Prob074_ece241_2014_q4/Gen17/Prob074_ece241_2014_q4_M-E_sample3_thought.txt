Instead of directly storing the outputs of the gates in flip-flops, store the previous states in registers and create separate wires for the combinational inputs of the flip-flops. This approach clearly separates the combinational gate logic from the state registers, enhancing clarity. The flip-flops update on the clock's positive edge, and are initialized to zero using a synchronous reset mechanism embedded in the design (by introducing an internal reset that is asserted once at start via a simple reset release FSM). The output z is computed by a NOR gate from the three registered states. This method avoids using initial blocks and models a reset in a way synthesizers typically support, guaranteeing deterministic startup state without requiring an explicit reset input port.
