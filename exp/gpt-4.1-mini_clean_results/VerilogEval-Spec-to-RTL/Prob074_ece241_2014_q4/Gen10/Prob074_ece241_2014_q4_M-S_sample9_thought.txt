We can streamline the design by directly defining three flip-flops that store the respective outputs of the logic feeding them. Instead of using the flip-flop output as input to the combinational gates inside the always block, we explicitly compute the next state values with intermediate signals for clarity. This approach preserves the required logic and initial zero state without relying on explicit reset signals or complex feedback in the D input. The output is simply the NOR of the three flip-flop outputs.
