Instead of directly using registers with combinational expressions inside the clocked block, this design explicitly separates combinational logic and sequential state storage. The combinational logic computes the D inputs for the three flip-flops based on the input x and the current flip-flop outputs, respecting the required gate connections and complements. The flip-flops update synchronously on the clock edge. The output z is the NOR of the current flip-flop outputs.

This approach clarifies the data flow and ensures all logic is registered cleanly, improving readability and synthesis robustness. Also, flip-flops are initialized to zero with an initial block, consistent with the assumption of being reset before operation.
