Since the problem specifies that all flip-flops are initially reset to zero before operation but no reset input is provided, use Verilog's standard power-on initialization by setting the registers to zero in an initial block. This is simple, clear, and generally supported by simulators and many synthesis tools. The combinational logic feeding each D flip-flop uses the current outputs and input x as specified, and the output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs. This approach avoids internal reset logic, making the design simpler and easier to understand while meeting the requirements.
