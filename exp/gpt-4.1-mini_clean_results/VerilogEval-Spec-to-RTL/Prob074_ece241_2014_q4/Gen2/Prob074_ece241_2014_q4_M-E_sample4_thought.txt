Implement the three flip-flops without a reset port by initializing their registers to zero using Verilog's initial block, which works for simulation and some synthesis tools. The module interface matches the problem exactly: inputs clk and x, output z.

Each flip-flop input is driven by the described two-input gate: 
- XOR gate input: x and current XOR flip-flop output
- AND gate input: x and complement of AND flip-flop output
- OR gate input: x and complement of OR flip-flop output

On each positive clock edge, flip-flops update with their respective gate outputs.

The output z is the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs.

This approach avoids any additional reset signal, directly satisfies the initial condition of zero state, and matches the given FSM logic and port interface.
