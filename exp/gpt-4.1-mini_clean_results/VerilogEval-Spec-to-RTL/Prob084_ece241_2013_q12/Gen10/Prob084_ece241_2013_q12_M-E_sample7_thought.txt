Implement the 8-bit shift register as eight individual D flip-flops with explicit outputs Q[0] to Q[7]. On each positive clock edge, when enable is high, shift data from Q[i-1] to Q[i], and shift in S into Q[0]. For the output Z, use a hierarchical mux structure built from 2-to-1 multiplexers, selecting first between pairs of Q outputs according to the bits of C, then combining outputs with bits B and A to finally produce Z. This approach clearly shows the explicit flip-flops and muxes, emphasizing the building block nature of the design and meeting the problem constraint of using only the 8 flip-flops and multiplexers. It is more verbose but explicit and easy to follow, which can be valuable for educational and verification purposes.
