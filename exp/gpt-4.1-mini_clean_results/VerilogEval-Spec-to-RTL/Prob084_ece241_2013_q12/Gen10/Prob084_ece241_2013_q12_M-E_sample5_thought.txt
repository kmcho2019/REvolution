Instead of using a single 8-bit register and vector indexing, implement the shift register as 8 individual D flip-flops explicitly connected in a shift chain. This makes the shift operation and register stages very clear and the outputs Q[0] to Q[7] individually accessible. Then implement the 8-to-1 multiplexer for selecting output Z by cascading smaller 2-to-1 muxes in a hierarchical manner controlled by inputs A, B, and C. This explicit mux tree structure illustrates the random access mux clearly and could be helpful for educational or debugging purposes.
