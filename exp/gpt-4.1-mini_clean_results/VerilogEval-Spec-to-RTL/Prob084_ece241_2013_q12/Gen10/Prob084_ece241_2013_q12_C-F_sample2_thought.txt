For the output multiplexer, instead of using a case statement or direct vector indexing alone, we fuse the clarity and synthesis friendliness of the case statement from Example 2 with the succinctness of direct vector indexing from Example 1 by using a combinational assignment with an explicit indexing expression, but declared as a wire instead of reg to eliminate unnecessary procedural blocks. This simplifies logic, improves synthesis results, and ensures clean combinational mux implementation. Also, we ensure the output port is declared as wire for direct assignment, which reduces registers and logic. This fusion ensures minimum area and power, and good timing by simplifying mux logic, while maintaining clear, maintainable code.
