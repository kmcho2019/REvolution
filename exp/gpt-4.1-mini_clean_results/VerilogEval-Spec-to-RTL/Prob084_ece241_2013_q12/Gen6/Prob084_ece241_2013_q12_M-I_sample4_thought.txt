To optimize PPA, I will consolidate the eight flip-flops into a single 8-bit vector register, which simplifies code and allows synthesis tools to optimize area and timing better. The shift operation will be implemented using vector concatenation for clarity and to minimize critical path delay. The multiplexer logic remains as a case statement for clarity and efficiency. This reduces area by fewer reg declarations, improves timing by eliminating chained assignments, and keeps power low by avoiding unnecessary logic.
