Implement the 8-bit shift register as eight separate D flip-flops connected in series with synchronous enable, shifting in S to Q[0] each clock. For the multiplexer, instead of a case statement, use a combinational 8-to-1 mux realized by concatenating the bits of Q and indexing directly by {A,B,C}. This approach leverages vector indexing and reduces verbosity, while maintaining the same functional behavior.
