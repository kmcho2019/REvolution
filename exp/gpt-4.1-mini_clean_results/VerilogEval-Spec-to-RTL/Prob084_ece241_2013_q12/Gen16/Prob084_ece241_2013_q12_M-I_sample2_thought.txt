To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), refactor the design by using a single 8-bit vector register `Q` instead of eight separate flip-flops. This reduces the register control overhead and simplifies the shift operation. On each clock edge with enable high, shift `Q` right by one and insert `S` at `Q[7]` (MSB), maintaining the specification that `S` is shifted into the MSB first.

For output selection, use direct vector indexing with the concatenated 3-bit select input `{A,B,C}` as an integer index into `Q`. This enables the synthesizer to generate an optimized multiplexer (LUT or ROM-based) rather than a mux tree. Using vector indexing generally results in smaller area and improved timing.

Keep synchronous shifting and combinational output logic. This design keeps functional correctness but should yield improved area and timing metrics.
