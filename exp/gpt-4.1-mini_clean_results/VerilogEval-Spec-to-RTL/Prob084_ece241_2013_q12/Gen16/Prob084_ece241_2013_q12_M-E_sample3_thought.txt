Instead of a single 8-bit shift register with individual flip-flops, I will build the shift register as a chain of eight D flip-flops explicitly instantiated as separate registers. Each flip-flop's input is connected to the previous flip-flop's output, with the first flip-flop input coming from S. The enable signal will gate the clock internally using a clock enable signal approach, avoiding combinational logic in the shifting path. For the multiplexer, I will implement a parallel 8-to-1 multiplexer using a single assign statement with a one-hot decoding of {A,B,C} that selects the corresponding Q[i] flip-flop output. This design emphasizes structural clarity, explicit flip-flop instances, and a simple one-hot mux approach to minimize logic depth.
