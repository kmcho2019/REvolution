Implement the 8-bit shift register as eight individual D flip-flops connected serially, with the input S feeding the first flip-flop Q[0], and each Q[i] feeding Q[i+1]. Use separate always blocks or a single always block to register each flip-flop on the clock edge with enable. For the read logic, implement the 8-to-1 multiplexer explicitly using a case statement based on the 3-bit address {A,B,C}, producing Z. This explicit mapping keeps the design clear and makes the multiplexer structure more evident for synthesis and debugging, differing from vector indexing. The serial connection of flip-flops emphasizes the shift operation explicitly and modularly.
