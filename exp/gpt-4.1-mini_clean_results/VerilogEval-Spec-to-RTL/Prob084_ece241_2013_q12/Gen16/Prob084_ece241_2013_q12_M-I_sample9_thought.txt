To optimize for better performance, power, and area, I will remove the combinational next_shift_reg register and perform the shift register update directly inside the clocked process gated by enable. This will reduce combinational logic depth and glitching. I will also implement the multiplexer output Z using direct indexing (Z = shift_reg[{A,B,C}]) for a flat, efficient mux structure, which typically synthesizes better than a case statement. The code will remain functionally identical but be simpler and more efficient.
