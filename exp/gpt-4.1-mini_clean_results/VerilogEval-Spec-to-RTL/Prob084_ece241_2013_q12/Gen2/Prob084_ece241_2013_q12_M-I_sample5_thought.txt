To optimize the original solution for better PPA, I will replace the individual flip-flop declarations with a single 8-bit shift register vector `Q[7:0]`. This allows better synthesis optimization, reduces code size, and groups sequential logic naturally. For the 8-to-1 multiplexer, I will directly index the vector `Q` with the 3-bit input `ABC` interpreted as a binary number `{A,B,C}`, removing the explicit one-hot decoding and AND-OR logic. This simplifies combinational logic, reduces switching activity on decoded signals, and shortens the critical path for the mux. The synchronous enable and positive edge clock shifting will remain the same. This approach maintains the exact functionality but improves power, area, and timing performance.
