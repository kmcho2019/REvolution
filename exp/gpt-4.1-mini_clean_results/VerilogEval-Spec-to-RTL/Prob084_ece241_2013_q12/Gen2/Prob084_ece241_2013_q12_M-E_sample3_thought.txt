Implement the 8-bit shift register using a generate loop with individual flip-flop registers and a wire vector to represent their outputs. This modularizes the shift register flip-flops distinctly. For the multiplexer, implement a hierarchical mux tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers instead of a single 8-to-1 mux. The ABC inputs will select among the flip-flop outputs through this mux tree to produce Z. This approach breaks down the 8-input mux into smaller building blocks, demonstrating a different structural method while still relying only on flip-flops and muxes.
