To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will implement clock gating for the shift register clock using the enable signal, thus reducing unnecessary clock toggling and dynamic power when enable is low. This avoids shifting when not required and reduces switching activity in the flip-flops. The logic remains functionally identical but achieves better power efficiency. The 8-to-1 multiplexer output selection remains the same for minimal area and delay. The design is still clean, synthesizable, and efficient with added clock gating for power reduction.
