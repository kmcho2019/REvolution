Implement the 8-bit shift register as a chain of 8 D flip-flops explicitly, each updating on the positive edge of clk with synchronous enable, shifting data from the previous flip-flop into the next, with S feeding Q[0]. Use a hierarchy of 2-to-1 multiplexers arranged in a binary tree structure to create the 8-to-1 multiplexer for output Z selected by the 3-bit input {A,B,C}. This approach decomposes both the storage and the mux logic more explicitly, showcasing structural RTL style.
