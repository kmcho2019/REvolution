Since Q[0] corresponds to the MSB of the shift register and the 3-bit select {A,B,C} chooses which bit to output, directly indexing shift_reg with (7 - {A,B,C}) requires subtracting in hardware. To simplify, reverse the shift register bit assignment: store the shifted-in bit S into the LSB (Q[0]) and shift left on enable. Then, the select {A,B,C} can directly index the shift register without inversion, eliminating the subtraction logic and reducing complexity. This keeps the functionality unchanged but simplifies combinational logic for Z. The shift register shifts left, inserting S at LSB. Q[0] is shift_reg[0], Q[7] is shift_reg[7].
