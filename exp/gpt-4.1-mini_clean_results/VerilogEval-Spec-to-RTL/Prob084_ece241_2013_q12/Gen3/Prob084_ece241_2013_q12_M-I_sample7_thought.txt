To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will implement the following improvements:

1. Use a single 8-bit register vector for the shift register (reg [7:0] Q). This simplifies shift operations into a single statement, reduces register instantiation, and allows more efficient synthesis.

2. Perform the shift with one assignment: on the rising clock edge with enable asserted, shift left by one bit and assign S to Q[0]. This reduces logic and flip-flop load compared to separate registers.

3. Simplify the multiplexer by directly indexing Q with the concatenated ABC signals treated as a 3-bit unsigned number, i.e., `Z = Q[{A,B,C}]`. This eliminates intermediate signals and mux trees, reducing logic and routing complexity and critical path delays.

4. Avoid the use of functions for multiplexing; use direct vector indexing for clarity and efficient synthesis.

This approach should yield a more compact, faster, and lower power design while maintaining exact functionality.
