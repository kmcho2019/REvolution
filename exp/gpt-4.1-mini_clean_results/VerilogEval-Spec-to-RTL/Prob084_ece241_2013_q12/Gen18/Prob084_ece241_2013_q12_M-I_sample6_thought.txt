To optimize power and area, eliminate the subtraction operation by changing the shift direction to left shift with MSB as Q[0], so that Q[0] corresponds directly to shift_reg[7]. Then the 3-bit ABC can be used directly to index the shift register without inversion, avoiding additional combinational logic. This reduces the multiplexer complexity and power consumption.

To optimize timing, explicitly implement the 8-to-1 multiplexer with a case statement or logic instead of a variable part-select, which allows synthesis tools to generate optimized multiplexing logic and potentially reduces delay.

Also, to potentially reduce power, keep the shift register flip-flops clock enabled only when enable is high, by gating the clock internally (or at least logically gating the shift operation), though actual clock gating is tool-dependent.

This approach improves clarity, reduces combinational logic from subtraction, and helps the synthesis tool better optimize the multiplexer for PPA benefits.
