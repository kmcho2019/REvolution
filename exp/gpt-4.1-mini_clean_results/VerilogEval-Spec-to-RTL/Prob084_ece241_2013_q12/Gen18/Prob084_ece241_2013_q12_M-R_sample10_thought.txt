Refactor the design by explicitly implementing the shift register using individual flip-flops rather than a vector register, connected in a chain. This more structural style clearly illustrates the shifting of bits from Q[0] to Q[7]. The output multiplexer is implemented with an 8-to-1 mux instantiated using a case statement in a combinational always block. This approach keeps the functional behavior the same, but demonstrates a different style: structural for shift register and procedural for mux. The enable controls the shifting synchronously on the clock edge.
