To optimize for better PPA, reduce the critical path and switching activity. Instead of shifting all bits every clock, implement the shift register so that only the relevant flip-flops update (though this is limited by the nature of shifting). A small improvement is to reverse the shift direction to reduce fanout of Q[0], and minimize wire fanout by using a vector assignment for the shift operation in one line. Also, implement the multiplexer as a combinational always block using a case statement to explicitly infer a mux tree, which may synthesize better than vector indexing. These changes can help the synthesis tool better optimize delay and power while keeping area low.
