To improve power and performance, implement clock gating to disable the clock to the flip-flops when enable is low, thus reducing unnecessary switching and improving timing by removing the enable gating inside flip-flops. This approach uses a gated clock derived from clk and enable instead of the enable input inside the flip-flops. The flip-flops will be simple positive-edge-triggered with no enable input, improving critical path and power. The shift register logic remains unchanged otherwise. The 8-to-1 mux remains as a case statement for clarity and small size.
