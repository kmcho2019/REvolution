The current design is functionally correct and straightforward. To improve power, we can gate the clock of the flip-flops with the enable signal, thus avoiding unnecessary toggling when enable is low. This reduces dynamic power at the cost of slightly more complex clocking logic, which modern FPGAs and ASICs can efficiently handle. For timing, gating the clock can also reduce switching noise and improve slack slightly. The rest of the design is minimal and optimal. Hence, the improvement focuses on implementing clock gating for the shift register flip-flops using the enable signal.
