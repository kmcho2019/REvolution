To improve PPA, I will refactor the shift register as an 8-bit vector register and use vector concatenation for shifting to reduce code complexity and potentially enable better resource sharing. For the random access read, I will index the register vector using the concatenated ABC inputs as a 3-bit selector, producing a single-bit output. This approach reduces verbosity, likely reduces area due to simpler RTL, and may improve power by enabling the synthesis tool to optimize multiplexing logic more efficiently. The clock enable remains synchronous active high, and all sequential logic triggers on the positive clock edge. The combinational multiplexer is implemented with a simple indexed read from the vector.
