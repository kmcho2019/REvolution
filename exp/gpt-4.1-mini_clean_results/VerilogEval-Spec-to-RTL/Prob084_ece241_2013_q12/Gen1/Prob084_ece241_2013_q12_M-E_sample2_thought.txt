Implement the 8-bit shift register as individual D flip-flops connected in series, where each flip-flop is described separately to explicitly show the data shifting from Q[0] through Q[7]. Use a synchronous enable to control shifting on clock's rising edge. For random access output Z, use a combinational 8-to-1 multiplexer built from a hierarchy of 2-to-1 muxes selecting one bit of the shift register based on inputs A, B, C treated as a 3-bit binary address.
