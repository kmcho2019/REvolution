Implement the 8-bit shift register as eight individual D flip-flops with explicit wiring for each stage, using synchronous enable and positive edge clock. Instead of a case statement, implement the 8-to-1 multiplexer for output Z using combinational logic gates (AND-OR) driven by the decoded inputs A, B, C. This showcases explicit combinational logic design instead of case/select statements. The shift register inputs propagate from S into Q[0], then Q[0] to Q[1], etc.
