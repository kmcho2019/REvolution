To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, I will use an 8-bit vector register instead of eight separate registers to hold the shift register state. This allows concise shifting with a simple vector concatenation on the positive clock edge. I will keep the synchronous enable signal, but by using vector assignments, the synthesis tool can better optimize the shift operation. For the output multiplexing, I will use an indexed vector selection (Q[{C,B,A}]) rather than nested conditional operators or multiple comparisons. This yields a single 8-to-1 mux inference, reducing logic complexity, power, and area. Overall, this will lead to simpler, cleaner code and better synthesis results, since the mux and register operations are clearly expressed as vector operations.
