This will produce a cleaner, smaller mux logic that directly selects the addressed bit from the vector, reducing combinational delay and area. Also note that ABC addressing order is "when ABC=000 output Q[0]", so indexing by {C,B,A} (with C as MSB, A as LSB) matches little-endian interpretation correctly for natural vector indexing. This change will reduce the complexity of the multiplexer and improve timing. Overall, using vector registers and indexed selection will reduce code size, area, and switching, while maintaining exact functional correctness.
