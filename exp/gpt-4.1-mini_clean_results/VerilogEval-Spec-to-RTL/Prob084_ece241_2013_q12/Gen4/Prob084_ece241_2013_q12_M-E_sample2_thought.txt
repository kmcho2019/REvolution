Implement the shift register explicitly using 8 individual D flip-flops with named wires Q0 to Q7. Each flip-flop shifts in the previous flip-flop's output, with Q0 taking input S. Then build an 8-to-1 multiplexer for output Z using a hierarchical structure of 2-to-1 muxes controlled by inputs A, B, and C. This approach makes the design structure very clear and modular by explicitly showing flip-flops and the mux tree.
