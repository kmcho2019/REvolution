Implement an 8-bit shift register with synchronous enable and serial input S. Each bit Q[i] is a D flip-flop triggered on the positive clock edge. When enable is high, the register shifts in the bit S at Q[0], shifting the previous bits towards Q[7]. To read out, use a 3-to-8 decoder with inputs A,B,C to select one of Q[0] through Q[7] and output that as Z. The entire circuit consists only of the shift register and multiplexers for selecting the output bit.
