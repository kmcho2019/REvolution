Implement an 8-bit shift register using 8 D flip-flops triggered on the positive edge of clk and enabled by the enable signal. The input S feeds the first flip-flop Q[0], and the rest shift the previous Q[i-1]. For output Z, use a 3-to-8 decoder style multiplexing to select one of Q[0] to Q[7] based on inputs A, B, C, representing the index.
