To improve PPA (power, performance, area), introduce clock gating to reduce unnecessary toggling in the shift register when enable is low, thus saving dynamic power by disabling clock to the flip-flops when no shifting is needed. This gating can be implemented with a simple enable-gated clock signal internally generated. Keeping the clean shift logic and direct indexed multiplexer preserves performance and area efficiency. This small modification reduces switching activity without changing functionality or adding combinational delay to critical paths.
