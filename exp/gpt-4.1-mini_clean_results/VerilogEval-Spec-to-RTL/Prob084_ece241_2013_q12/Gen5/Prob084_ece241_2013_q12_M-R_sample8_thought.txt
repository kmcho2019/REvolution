Refactor the shift register by implementing it with a chain of eight D flip-flops instantiated separately rather than a vector register. Each flip-flop updates on the rising edge of the clock and uses enable to control whether it captures new data or holds the old data. This structural approach explicitly shows the shifting by wiring each flip-flop's D input from the previous flip-flop's Q output, with the first flip-flop taking S as input. The output Z is generated by an 8-to-1 multiplexer explicitly coded with a case statement selecting among Q0-Q7 based on the concatenated address {A,B,C}.
