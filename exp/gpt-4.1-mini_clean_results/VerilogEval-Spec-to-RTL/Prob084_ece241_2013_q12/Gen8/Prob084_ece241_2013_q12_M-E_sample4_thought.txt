Implement the shift register using eight individual D flip-flops explicitly named Q0 to Q7, updating them serially on clock edge when enable is high with S shifting into Q0 and the rest shifting from the previous flip-flop. For the output Z, use a hierarchical 2-level mux: first combine pairs of Q outputs into 2-to-1 muxes controlled by C, then combine these outputs with another level controlled by B, and finally select between the two resulting signals with A, thus building an 8-to-1 mux from basic 2-to-1 mux primitives. This approach uses explicit flip-flops and manual muxing instead of indexing, providing a clear structural view of the circuit.
