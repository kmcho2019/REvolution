To optimize power by reducing unnecessary toggling in the shift register, I will implement clock gating based on the enable signal. This prevents flip-flops from toggling when enable is low, saving dynamic power. I will create a gated clock signal inside the module and use it to clock the register. For area, the gated clock logic is minimal and usually beneficial for power without significant area penalty. Timing might improve slightly due to reduced switching noise and load. The read logic using direct indexing remains the most efficient. This approach improves power while maintaining simplicity and timing.
