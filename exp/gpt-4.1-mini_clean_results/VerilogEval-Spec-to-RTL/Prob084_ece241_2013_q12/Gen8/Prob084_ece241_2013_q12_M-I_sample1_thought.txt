To improve the PPA (Timing, Power, Area) beyond the original solution, I'll implement clock gating on the flip-flops to reduce dynamic power consumption when enable is low, thereby preventing unnecessary flip-flop toggling. This is done by creating a gated clock using the enable signal and the input clock, effectively reducing switching activity in the shift register when not shifting.

Also, to maintain timing integrity and prevent glitches, I'll instantiate a simple clock gating cell (using an enable latch and an AND gate) inside the module.

The combinational logic for output selection remains the same (direct vector indexing), as it is already minimal and optimal.

This approach should reduce power while maintaining or slightly improving timing and area, with the minimal overhead of clock gating logic.
