To improve PPA, especially power and area, I will switch from one-hot encoding to a binary encoding with 2 bits since only four states exist. This reduces flip-flop count from 3 to 2 and lowers dynamic power from less switching on multiple flip-flops.

State encoding:
- 2'b00: A (idle)
- 2'b01: B (grant device 0)
- 2'b10: C (grant device 1)
- 2'b11: D (grant device 2)

The next state logic uses 2-bit state and priority decoding for requests. The synchronous active-low reset remains. Outputs g[2:0] are generated combinationally from the state: only one bit of g is asserted corresponding to the current grant state; all zero for idle.

This encoding saves flip-flops and gate count for state transitions and reduces switching power by activating only 2 flip-flops instead of 3.

I will keep separate always blocks for sequential and combinational logic, use nonblocking for sequential, and blocking for combinational logic to follow best practices.

This redesign balances simplicity with improved power and area while maintaining the FSM behavior.
