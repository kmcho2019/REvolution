To improve PPA, I will optimize the next-state logic by replacing the priority if-else cascade with a combinational logic expression to minimize switching and logic depth. I will encode the FSM states with one-hot encoding for simpler state decoding, which often benefits timing by reducing critical path and logic complexity at the cost of minimal area overhead. Outputs will be implemented as continuous assignments using the one-hot state bits directly for zero-delay decoding and less switching. The reset remains synchronous active-low. This approach reduces logic depth, switches, and improves performance, while the slight area increase from one-hot encoding is offset by simplified combinational logic.
