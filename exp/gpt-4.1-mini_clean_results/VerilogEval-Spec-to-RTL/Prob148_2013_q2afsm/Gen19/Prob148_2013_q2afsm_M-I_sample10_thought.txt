To optimize the FSM for better area and potentially lower power, I will switch from one-hot encoding (4 flip-flops) to binary encoding using 2 bits for the states since there are only 4 states. This reduces flip-flop count by half and simplifies state register area.

I will implement the next state and output logic using a combinational always block with a case statement for clarity and potentially lower gate count compared to complex logic equations.

Grant outputs will be decoded from the current state in a separate combinational block.

The synchronous reset remains active-low and resets to state A.

This approach aims to reduce area, and possibly power due to fewer flip-flops and simpler combinational logic, while maintaining correct functionality and similar performance.
