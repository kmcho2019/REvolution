Define states A, B, C, D with B granting g0=1, C granting g1=1, and D granting g2=1. On synchronous active-low reset, go to A. In state A, prioritize requests r0 > r1 > r2 and transition to B, C, or D accordingly; otherwise stay in A. In states B and C, stay if the granted request remains asserted; otherwise return to A. The output grant signals g correspond to the current state. Use separate always blocks for state register and next-state logic, and use continuous assignment for outputs.
