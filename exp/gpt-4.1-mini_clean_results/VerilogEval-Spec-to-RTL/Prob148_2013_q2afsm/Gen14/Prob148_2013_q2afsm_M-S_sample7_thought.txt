We can simplify the FSM by encoding states as 2-bit values as before, but combine the next-state logic and output logic into one combinational always block with fewer branches. Also, as the FSM only depends on r[0] and r[1] for states B and C, and r[2] for D, we can directly assign outputs from state bits, avoiding the explicit case for output. This reduces code lines and complexity while maintaining full functionality and clarity. Reset remains synchronous active low.
