Use a 2-bit binary encoded FSM with states A=00, B=01, C=10, D=11. Implement synchronous reset and state update in one always block triggered on the clock edge. Use combinational logic to compute the next state and outputs in separate always blocks. The next state logic prioritizes r[0] over r[1] over r[2] in state A, and in granted states B, C, and D the FSM stays if the corresponding request remains asserted, otherwise returns to A. This approach keeps the FSM simple and clear without unnecessary complexity.
