To improve PPA, I will use one-hot state encoding to reduce the complexity of next-state logic and potentially improve timing by simplifying combinational paths. This eliminates multi-bit comparators and simplifies output logic. Also, I will use non-blocking assignments consistently in the sequential always block and blocking in combinational. The output logic will be defined via an always_comb block for clarity and to allow easier FSM extensions. The synchronous reset remains active low. This approach can reduce logic depth and area for a small FSM and may improve power due to simpler gates and less switching.
