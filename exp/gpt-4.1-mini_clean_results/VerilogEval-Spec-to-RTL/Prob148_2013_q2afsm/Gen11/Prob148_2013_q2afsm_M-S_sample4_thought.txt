Simplify the FSM by removing the unused state D and consolidating the behavior into just three states (A, B, C) since D is functionally equivalent to A when granting device 2. Instead of four states, use three states: A (no grant), B (grant to device 0), and C (grant to device 1 or device 2 combined). Grant device 2 only when no device 0 or 1 requests are active.
