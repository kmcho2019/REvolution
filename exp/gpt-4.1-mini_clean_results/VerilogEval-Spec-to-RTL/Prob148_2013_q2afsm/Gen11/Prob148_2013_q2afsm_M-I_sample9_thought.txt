2. **Simplify synchronous reset logic in the state flip-flops:**  
   Use the active-low reset signal directly in the state flip-flops with a synchronous reset, as before, keeping the reset handling consistent.

3. **Next state logic:**  
   Minor code clean-up for readability by explicitly assigning default next_state to state to reduce inferred latches and potential glitching.

4. **State encoding and outputs:**  
   Retain the 2-bit encoding as it's optimal for 4 states. The continuous combinational assignment for `g` avoids unnecessary clocked registers, further reducing switching.
