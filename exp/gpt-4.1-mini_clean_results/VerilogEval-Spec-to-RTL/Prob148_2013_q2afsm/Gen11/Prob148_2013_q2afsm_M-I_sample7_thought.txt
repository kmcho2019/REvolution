To improve PPA, the main opportunity is to reduce output glitches and switching power by registering the outputs, so that output changes occur synchronously at clock edges, not combinationally. This also can improve timing by breaking combinational chains.

We'll keep the 2-bit encoding since it's already minimal and suitable for simple state decoding.

We'll modify the FSM outputs to be registered on the clock, controlled by the state, and still implement synchronous active-low reset to clear grants at reset.

This will slightly increase area due to output registers but improve timing and reduce power by glitch-free outputs.

All other logic remains unchanged for simplicity and priority correctness.
