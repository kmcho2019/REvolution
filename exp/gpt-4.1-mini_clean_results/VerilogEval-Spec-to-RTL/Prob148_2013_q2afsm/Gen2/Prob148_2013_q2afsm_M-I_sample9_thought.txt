To optimize the FSM for better performance and power efficiency, I will adopt one-hot state encoding which typically yields faster next-state decoding and simpler combinational logic in FPGAs. This can reduce critical path delays and help timing closure. I will register the grant outputs to reduce glitches and switching power. The synchronous reset remains active low, and the next state logic is kept combinational. This approach slightly increases area (more flip-flops for one-hot encoding) but improves timing and power due to simpler logic and registered outputs.
