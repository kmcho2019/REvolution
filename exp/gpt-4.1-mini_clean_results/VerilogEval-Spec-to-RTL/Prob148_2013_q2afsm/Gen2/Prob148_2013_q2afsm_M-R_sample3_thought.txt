I will refactor the FSM by separating the state register, next state logic, and output logic into three distinct always blocks for clarity and modularity. The next state logic and output logic will be purely combinational and split: one always block for next_state, and one always block for outputs (`g`). The outputs will be registered for glitch-free behavior, meaning `g` will be updated synchronously with the clock in the state register block, ensuring stable outputs. The state encoding remains with localparam, and synchronous active low reset is kept. This separation improves readability and output stability.
