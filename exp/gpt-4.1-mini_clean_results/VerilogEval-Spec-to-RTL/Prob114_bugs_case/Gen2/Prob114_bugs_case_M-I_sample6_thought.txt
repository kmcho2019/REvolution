The current design uses a 256-entry ROM with mostly zero entries and a separate large OR for the valid signal, which is inefficient in area and power. To optimize for PPA, I will replace the large ROM with a simple case statement that directly maps scancodes to their digit outputs and sets the valid flag simultaneously. This reduces area by eliminating a large ROM and extra logic for valid. It also reduces power by avoiding unnecessary switching in unused ROM entries. The case statement is efficient in modern synthesis tools, often optimized into a small decoder circuit. This also improves timing by reducing logic depth compared to the multiple equality comparisons in valid. Overall, this approach is cleaner, smaller, and lower power without impacting functionality.
