To optimize for power and area while maintaining clarity, replace the parallel equality checks with a combinational case statement inside an always_comb block. This approach reduces the number of parallel comparators, lowering switching activity and logic gate count. The case statement directly maps scancodes to outputs, setting valid accordingly. Since this is purely combinational logic without clock, it avoids any timing penalties and improves synthesis efficiency. This reduces the complexity of output logic and eliminates the multiple AND-OR chains, thereby improving power and area without impacting performance.
