To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will replace the multiple parallel equality checks and the OR tree with a single combinational case statement. This reduces duplicated comparators and logic depth by mapping the input scancode directly to output digit and valid signals. A case statement is more synthesis-friendly and typically results in a smaller and faster multiplexer-based logic implementation. This reduces area and switching power due to fewer active comparators and simpler logic. Valid is set high only when the input matches one of the recognized scancodes, otherwise low. This approach preserves full combinational logic and functionality while improving PPA.
