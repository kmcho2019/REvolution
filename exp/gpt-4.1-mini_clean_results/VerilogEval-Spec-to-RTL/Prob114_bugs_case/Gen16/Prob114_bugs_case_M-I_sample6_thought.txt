To improve the existing design for better power and area efficiency while maintaining its correctness and combinational nature, I will:

- Replace multiple individual equality comparators with a single case statement inside a combinational always block. This approach synthesizes into a ROM-like structure or a compact decoder, reducing redundant comparison logic.
- Compute 'valid' and 'out' together based on the matched case.
- Default outputs to zero when no match is found, avoiding separate OR and masking operations that replicate signals multiple times.
- This simplification reduces logic duplication and switching activity, potentially improving power and area metrics without impacting timing.
