To optimize for power and area while maintaining timing, I will refactor the decoding using a single combinational always block with a case statement. This reduces redundant comparators by doing one lookup, shares logic for both out and valid signals, and is generally more efficient than multiple parallel equality comparisons. The case statement synthesizes efficiently into a multiplexer, minimizing area and power. This also reduces the number of comparators and shared logic for valid, improving power by limiting switching. Overall, this approach balances clarity, performance, area, and power.
