Fuse the clarity and minimal logic depth of the case statement approach with the efficiency of single combinational decoding that simultaneously produces both 'out' and 'valid'. Instead of performing separate equality checks for valid, embed the validity directly in the case statement by outputting a valid bit along with the decoded output. This avoids redundant parallel comparisons, reduces duplicated logic, and minimizes power by limiting switching to a single combinational block. The always_comb block outputs both the decoded digit and a valid flag, ensuring glitch-free logic and efficient synthesis. This fusion yields a concise, area- and power-efficient design with clear functional intent.
