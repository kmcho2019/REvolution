Implement the TopModule using a combinational case statement that directly decodes the 8-bit input scancode into the 4-bit digit and valid output signals. This approach leverages a single case statement to map all recognized codes to their corresponding digits, producing valid=1, while all other codes default to zero outputs and valid=0.
