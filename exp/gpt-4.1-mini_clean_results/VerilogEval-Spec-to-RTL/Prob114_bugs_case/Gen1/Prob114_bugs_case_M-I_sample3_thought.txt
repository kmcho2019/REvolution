To optimize the design for better power and performance, I will eliminate the redundant default output assignments by using a parallel logic implementation instead of a case statement. The logic will directly assign `valid` and `out` signals from combinational comparisons using bitwise OR and conditional expressions, which can lead to fewer logic levels and reduced switching. This approach removes the need for default assignments and can improve timing slightly by allowing synthesis tools to optimize parallel comparisons. Area will remain similar, but power can improve slightly due to fewer transitions on invalid inputs. The logic remains purely combinational and clear.
