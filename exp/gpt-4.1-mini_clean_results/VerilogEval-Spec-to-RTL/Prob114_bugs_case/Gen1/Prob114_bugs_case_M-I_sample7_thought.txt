To improve the existing design, I will optimize power by reducing unnecessary output toggling when the input is invalid. Instead of always assigning `out` and `valid` to zero on unmatched codes, I will keep `out` stable and only clear `valid`. This prevents toggling the 4-bit output unnecessarily and thus can reduce dynamic power without changing functional behavior. The combinational logic remains the same, keeping performance and area nearly unchanged, as the logic is simple and compact.
