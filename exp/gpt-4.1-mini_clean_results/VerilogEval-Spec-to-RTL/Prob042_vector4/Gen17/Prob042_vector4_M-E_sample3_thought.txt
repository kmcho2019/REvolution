An alternative way to achieve sign extension without using the replication operator or shifts is to explicitly generate each bit of the 32-bit output through bitwise multiplexing based on the sign bit. For each of the upper 24 bits, we select either 1 or 0 depending on whether the sign bit is 1 or 0, replicating the sign bit manually using bitwise conditional selection. The lower 8 bits are directly assigned from the input. This approach uses bitwise logic rather than shifts or replication, showing how conditional operators can implement sign extension.
