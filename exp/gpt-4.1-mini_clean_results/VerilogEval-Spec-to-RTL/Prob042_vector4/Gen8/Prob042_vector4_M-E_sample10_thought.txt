Instead of directly using concatenation and replication operators to sign-extend, we can implement the sign-extension through an arithmetic shift left followed by an arithmetic shift right. By first shifting the input left to position the sign bit at the highest bit of a 32-bit register, then performing an arithmetic right shift, the sign bit will be extended automatically. This approach mimics a common hardware technique using shift operations to propagate the sign. Although this is more complex than simple replication, it serves as an alternative architectural method showing how sign-extension can be achieved via shift operations in hardware.
