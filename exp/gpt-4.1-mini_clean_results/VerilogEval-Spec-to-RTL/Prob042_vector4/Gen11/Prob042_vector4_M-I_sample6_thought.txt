The given solution is already minimal and optimal for sign-extension in terms of area, power, and performance. Any attempt to manually replicate the bits with a generate loop or intermediate wires would add unnecessary complexity and possibly increase area and power. Since the 24-bit replication is inherent to the function of sign-extension, it cannot be reduced.

This matches the original replication but clarifies intent and could enable some synthesis tools to optimize power by gating the replicated bits when the sign bit is zero.

Overall, the code will remain combinational, succinct, and efficient, while possibly offering a slight advantage in power due to clearer conditions for replication.
