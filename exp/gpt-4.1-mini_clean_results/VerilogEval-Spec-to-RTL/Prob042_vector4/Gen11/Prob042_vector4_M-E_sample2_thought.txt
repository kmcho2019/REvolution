Instead of using a direct replication operator, I will implement the sign extension using a conditional (ternary) operator to select either all ones or all zeros for the upper 24 bits based on the sign bit. This approach avoids explicit replication and demonstrates an alternative way to sign-extend by using a bitwise fill. It can provide clear intent to synthesis tools that the upper bits are either all zeros or all ones depending on the sign, possibly enabling optimizations in some tool flows. The output is still purely combinational with no registers, maintaining minimal logic and zero latency.
