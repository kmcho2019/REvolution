The original design is minimal and efficient, performing a straightforward sign extension with a single concatenation and replication operator. Given its simplicity, there are no complex timing or power issues to optimize in isolation.

However, a slight potential improvement is to declare the output as a reg and perform the sign extension inside an always_comb block to better guide synthesis tools and potentially allow better PPA (power/performance/area) optimizations by making the operation explicit as combinational logic.

This also allows adding a pipeline register if needed in a larger system or easier modifications for future extensions.

For this task, I will implement the sign extension inside an always_comb block, assigning the output reg. This style can be preferred for clarity and may help synthesis tools optimize power or area by explicitly recognizing the logic as combinational.

No functional changes will occur, but synthesis tools sometimes better optimize this style compared to a continuous assignment on wider vectors.

This approach retains the same logic, ensuring the sign bit is replicated 24 times, concatenated with the original 8-bit input.
