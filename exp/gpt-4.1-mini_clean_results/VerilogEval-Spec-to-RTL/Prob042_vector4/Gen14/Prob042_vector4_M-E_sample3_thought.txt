Implement sign-extension using a behavioral always_comb block instead of a continuous assignment. Extract the sign bit into a separate wire, then use a for-loop inside an always_comb block to assign the replicated sign bit to the upper 24 bits of the output, and directly assign the input bits to the lower 8 bits. This approach demonstrates an alternate architectural style employing procedural assignment, making the design suitable for synthesis while being clear and educational for understanding bit-wise replication via procedural logic.
