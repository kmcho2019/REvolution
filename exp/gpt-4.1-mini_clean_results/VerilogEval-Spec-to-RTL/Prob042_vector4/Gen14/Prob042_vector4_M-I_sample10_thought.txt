The original solution is already optimal in terms of logic simplicity and area, as sign-extension via replication and concatenation is the minimal approach. To improve power and area further, we can leverage Verilog's signed typecasting, which can lead to better synthesis optimizations by clearly indicating that the input is signed and the extension should preserve the sign. This may allow synthesis tools to infer a more efficient sign-extension without explicit replication, potentially reducing switching activity and gates for the replicated bits. Keeping the `(* no_retiming *)` directive helps maintain timing and power characteristics. Thus, the improvement strategy is to use signed typecasting on the input and assign directly to the signed output, which is then cast back to unsigned if needed.
