The existing solution uses a direct replication and concatenation which is already optimal for sign extension in terms of simplicity, minimal logic, and minimal delay. Since this is purely combinational logic with no registers or additional complexity, further RTL-level optimization is essentially not possible. 

However, to potentially improve power slightly, I can reduce the possibility of glitches and unnecessary toggling on the replicated sign bit vector by using a registered version of the input sign bit if timing and power are critical, or by explicitly specifying that the output is a wire to avoid any confusion. But adding registers contradicts pure combinational logic and may increase latency.

Since the synthesis tools generally optimize replications efficiently, the best approach is to keep the implementation as is for minimal area, minimal delay, and low power. Any PPA improvements would come from tool-level optimizations, not code changes.

Therefore, I will maintain the current design, adding explicit wire declaration for 'out' for clarity, ensuring no unnecessary signals are introduced. This is the best balance between clarity, simplicity, and optimal PPA for sign extension.
