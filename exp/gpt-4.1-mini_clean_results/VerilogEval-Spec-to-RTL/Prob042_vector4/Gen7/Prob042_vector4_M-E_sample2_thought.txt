Instead of using the replication operator directly in a continuous assignment, we can implement the sign extension using a procedural block inside an always_comb (or always @(*)). This uses explicit conditional logic to assign the upper 24 bits either all 1's or all 0's based on the sign bit, and then concatenates with the original 8-bit input. Although functionally equivalent, this approach makes the sign-extension logic explicit in RTL and may facilitate easier debugging or modification in a larger design flow.

The upper 24 bits are assigned to either 24'b111...1 or 24'b000...0 depending on the sign bit. The output is declared as reg since it is assigned inside an always block. This method avoids the direct use of the replication operator in the assignment, presenting an alternative architectural approach to sign extension.
