Instead of using a single replication operator for the sign bit, generate the upper 24 bits of the output by performing a signed arithmetic right shift on the 8-bit input extended to 32 bits. By shifting right by zero positions with an arithmetic shift, the value gets sign-extended naturally by Verilog's arithmetic right shift operator. Then concatenate the lower 8 bits as is. This approach leverages the behavior of arithmetic right shifts for sign extension and demonstrates an alternative to direct replication.
