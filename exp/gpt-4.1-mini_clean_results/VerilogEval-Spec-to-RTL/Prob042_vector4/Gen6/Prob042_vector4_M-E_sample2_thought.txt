Implement the sign-extension using an intermediate wire to explicitly store the replicated sign bit field before concatenation. This separation clarifies the construction of the sign-extended output by splitting the replication step from the final output assignment. The approach trades a small additional intermediate wire for improved code readability and modularity. The intermediate wire uses the replication operator to create 24 bits of the sign bit, which are then concatenated with the 8-bit input to produce the final 32-bit output.
