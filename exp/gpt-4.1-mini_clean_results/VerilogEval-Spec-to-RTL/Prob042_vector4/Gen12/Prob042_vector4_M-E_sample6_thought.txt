Instead of using a direct replication operator in a continuous assignment, this design uses a procedural block with a register to hold the output. On every change of input, the most significant bit (sign bit) is extracted and used to explicitly construct the sign-extended output by concatenating a replicated sign bit vector with the input. This approach demonstrates a behavioral style suitable for synthesis, and can be easily extended to include pipelining or registered outputs in larger designs. It also illustrates an alternative method to achieve sign extension by explicitly creating the replicated sign bit vector in a procedural context.
