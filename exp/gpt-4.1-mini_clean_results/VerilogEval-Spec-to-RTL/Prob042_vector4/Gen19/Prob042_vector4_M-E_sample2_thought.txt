Instead of using the replication operator directly for sign extension, we can implement sign extension by using a conditional operator (ternary operator). The module will check if the sign bit (MSB of the 8-bit input) is 1; if yes, then we prepend 24 bits of '1's, otherwise prepend 24 bits of '0's. This approach replaces the replication operator with conditional logic, which can be synthesized equivalently, but stylistically differs and illustrates an alternate architectural style without changing functionality.
