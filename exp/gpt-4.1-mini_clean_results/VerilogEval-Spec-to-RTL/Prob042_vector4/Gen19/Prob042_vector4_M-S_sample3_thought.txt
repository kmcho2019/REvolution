The current implementation is already minimal and idiomatic for sign-extension in Verilog by using replication and concatenation. Since the input is an 8-bit vector and the output is 32-bit, replicating the sign bit 24 times and concatenating the input is the simplest and most straightforward approach. No further simplification is possible without changing the design or losing clarity. Thus, the simplest and most readable form is to keep the direct assignment using replication and concatenation.
