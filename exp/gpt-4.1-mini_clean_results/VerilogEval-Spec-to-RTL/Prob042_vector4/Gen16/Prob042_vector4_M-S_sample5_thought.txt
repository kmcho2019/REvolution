Simplify the sign-extension by directly replicating the sign bit without a conditional operator inside the replication. Since the sign bit is either 0 or 1, replicating it 24 times will produce the correct extended bits without extra logic. Then concatenate these 24 replicated bits with the original 8-bit input to form the 32-bit sign-extended output. This approach reduces complexity, power, and area while preserving functionality.
