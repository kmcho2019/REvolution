The original solution works correctly but uses a mask and conditional OR operation to implement sign extension, which adds unnecessary logic and switching activity. A more efficient way is to directly concatenate 24 copies of the sign bit (in[7]) with the original 8-bit input using the replication operator. This approach simplifies the combinational logic, reduces area by avoiding mask generation, decreases power by limiting switching, and can improve timing due to lower logic depth. The implementation will be a single concatenation without conditional logic.
