Fuse the best ideas by combining the succinctness and efficiency of the replication operator used in continuous assignment (Example 2) with the clarity and simplicity of purely combinational logic driving a wire output (to avoid unnecessary registers and procedural blocks). This yields the most power-, performance-, and area-efficient design. The output should be declared as a wire, and the replication operator should be used directly in a continuous assignment statement. This leverages Verilog's built-in features for replication, produces minimal logic, and is easy to maintain and synthesize.
