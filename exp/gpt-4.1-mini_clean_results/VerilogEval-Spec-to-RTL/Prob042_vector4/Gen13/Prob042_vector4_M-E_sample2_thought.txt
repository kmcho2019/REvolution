Implement the sign-extension by first isolating the sign bit into a single wire, then use a generate loop to replicate the sign bit 24 times into a separate 24-bit wire vector. Finally, concatenate this replicated vector with the 8-bit input in a continuous assignment to produce the 32-bit output. This modularizes the replication step and demonstrates using a generate block for bit replication instead of the direct replication operator, providing a different architectural perspective on sign extension.
