Instead of directly replicating the sign bit in a single concatenation, this design uses a two-step approach: first, it sign-extends the input from 8 bits to 16 bits by replicating the sign bit 8 times, then extends from 16 bits to 32 bits by replicating the sign bit of the intermediate 16-bit value another 16 times. This staged sign extension can be useful in a pipeline or multi-stage processing context and demonstrates modular replication steps.
