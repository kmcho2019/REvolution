To optimize PPA, I will separate direction and falling state updates into two always blocks to enable better synthesis optimization and potential pipelining. I will create explicit next-state logic for direction and falling signals before the clocked blocks to simplify combinational logic and reduce critical path. Direction will update only when walking (not falling) and grounded with a simple conditional expression instead of nested ifs. This also creates a clock enable for direction register, reducing unnecessary toggling and lowering power. Falling updates purely based on ground changes with simple assignment, minimizing glitches. This separation simplifies timing, potentially improves slack, and reduces power by avoiding direction toggling during falling or no bumps. Overall, the code is cleaner and more synthesis-friendly while preserving exact behavior.
