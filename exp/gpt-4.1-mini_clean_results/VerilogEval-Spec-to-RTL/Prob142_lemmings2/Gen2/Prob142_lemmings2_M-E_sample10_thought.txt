Instead of tracking direction and falling separately, encode all states explicitly in a 3-state Moore FSM: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the FSM goes to WALK_LEFT. When in WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT states (i.e., on ground), bump signals control state transitions: bump_left triggers transition to WALK_RIGHT; bump_right triggers WALK_LEFT; if both bump signals occur together, flip walking direction. When ground disappears (ground=0), transition to FALLING state and remember the previous walking direction in a separate register. While falling, remain in FALLING state with aaah output asserted and no walking. Upon ground return (ground=1), return to the stored walking direction state. Bump signals and ground changes during the transition cycle do not cause additional direction changes or state changes, ensuring stable transitions. Outputs are purely from state encoding, matching Moore FSM style.
