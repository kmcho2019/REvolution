To fix the issue, introduce a registered ground signal (prev_ground) to detect ground transitions on clock edges. We only allow bump-induced direction flips if the Lemming is in WALKING state *and* the ground did not just transition from 1 to 0 (start falling). Specifically:
- On the cycle ground transitions from 1 to 0 (falling begins), ignore bumps (do not flip direction).
- On the cycle ground transitions from 0 to 1 (landing), ignore bumps as well.
- Only when ground is stable (no transition) and Lemming is walking do bumps flip direction.

This means:
- In sequential logic, register ground each cycle.
- In next-state logic, compare prev_ground and current ground.
- Only flip direction on bumps if (state == WALKING) && (ground stable, i.e. prev_ground == ground == 1).

This satisfies the requirement that bumps coincident with ground disappearing or ground reappearing do not affect walking direction.

Output logic remains Moore and unchanged.

This approach cleanly enforces the masking of bump inputs during ground transitions.
