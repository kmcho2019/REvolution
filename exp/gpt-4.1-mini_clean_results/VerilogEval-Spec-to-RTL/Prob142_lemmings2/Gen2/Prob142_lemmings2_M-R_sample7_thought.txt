Refactor the FSM to separate direction state and falling state into two independent registers: a walking_direction (1 bit, 0 for left, 1 for right) and a falling flag. This decouples direction from falling, avoiding the need for a "last_walk_dir" state register and reducing complexity.

- walking_direction is updated only in walking mode, changed on bump signals.
- falling flag is set/reset based on ground input.
- When falling is asserted, the walk outputs are suppressed and aaah is asserted.
- When ground returns, falling clears and walking resumes in the stored walking_direction.
- Reset asynchronously initializes walking_direction to left and falling to zero.

This approach simplifies state transitions, avoids a three-state FSM, and eliminates issues with last_walk_dir timing.

The outputs are directly generated from walking_direction and falling signals. The direction flips only when walking and bumped.

Bumps during falling or ground transition do not change direction.

This structure implements the same behavior as the original problem with simpler, more robust state handling.
