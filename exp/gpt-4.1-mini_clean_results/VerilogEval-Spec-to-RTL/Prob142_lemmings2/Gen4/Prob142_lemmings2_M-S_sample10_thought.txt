Simplify the FSM by using two registers to track walking direction (walk_left/walk_right) and falling status separately instead of an enumerated state encoding. On reset, initialize to walking left and not falling. During walking, bump signals flip direction. When ground is lost, enter falling state, retaining direction. While falling, ignore bumps and stay falling until ground returns, then resume walking in the same direction. Outputs are directly driven by these two registers plus the falling condition. This approach reduces state logic complexity while preserving all required behavior.
