Implement the FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING, and store the walking direction explicitly in a separate register. 

On reset, initialize walking direction to left and state to walking. 

At every clock edge (with async reset), update the state and walking direction according to:

- When ground is present, Lemming walks in the stored direction.
- If bumped left or right when walking and ground present, flip walking direction immediately.
- When ground disappears (ground=0) while walking, transition to FALLING state but keep walking direction unchanged.
- While falling, ignore bumps.
- When ground reappears (ground=1) after falling, resume walking state in stored walking direction.
  
Edge detection on ground is not strictly needed if we base falling/walking transition only on the current ground signal and current state.

Moore outputs are produced directly from the current state and walking direction.

This approach keeps direction and state separate, avoiding confusion over direction in the FALLING state, simplifies logic by removing explicit edge detection, and avoids next_state combinational logic complexity by updating state and direction only synchronously. It cleanly separates walking direction updates and falling transitions.
