To optimize the FSM for better PPA without changing its behavior, merge the falling and direction bits into a single 2-bit state register to potentially enable better synthesis optimizations.

- Encode states as:
   00: walking left
   01: walking right
   10: falling left
   11: falling right
- On reset, state = walking left (00).
- When falling, ignore bumps; only ground returning causes transition back to walking (preserving direction encoded in LSB).
- When walking, bumps cause direction updates according to specs:
   - Both bumps: flip direction bit (LSB).
   - bump_left: walk right (01)
   - bump_right: walk left (00)
- On ground loss, transition from walking to falling states by setting MSB=1 but keeping direction LSB.
- Outputs are directly decoded from state bits:
   - aaah = MSB (falling)
   - walk_left = ~MSB & ~LSB
   - walk_right = ~MSB & LSB

This single-register FSM reduces registers from two to one, simplifies logic for direction updates (bitwise toggles and sets), and may allow synthesis tools to better optimize logic and register usage, leading to improved timing, power, and area.

The asynchronous reset still initializes to walking left (00). The logic is fully Moore.

This approach preserves full correctness and the problem's semantics while likely reducing flip-flop count and combinational logic complexity.
