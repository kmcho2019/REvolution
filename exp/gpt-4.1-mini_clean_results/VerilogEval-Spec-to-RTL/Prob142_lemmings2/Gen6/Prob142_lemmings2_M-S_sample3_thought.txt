Simplify the FSM by using two registers: one to hold the walking direction and one to indicate falling or not. On reset, walk left and not falling. When ground is lost, set falling; when ground returns, clear falling. While walking, bump signals flip direction if either bump_left or bump_right is high. While falling, bumps do not affect direction. Outputs are directly derived from these two registers. This reduces the FSM from four states to two simple bits and simplifies next-state logic without using enumerated types.
