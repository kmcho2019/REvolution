To optimize the existing clean design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), focus on the following improvements:

1. Change asynchronous reset to synchronous reset:
   - This reduces complexity in timing closure and avoids hold time problems, potentially improving performance.
   - It can reduce area because asynchronous reset logic often adds extra circuitry.

2. Simplify the combinational next_state logic by removing some redundant assignments:
   - Directly compute next_state without default assignments to reduce logic.

3. Register bump signals synchronously to reduce glitching effects and potential unnecessary toggling in the FSM.
   - This can reduce power by eliminating glitches on input signals used in combinational logic.

4. Keep the minimal 2-bit state encoding unchanged because it is already optimal.

5. Maintain Moore outputs from the state bits directly for stable timing and minimal logic.

These changes help streamline the design for better timing and less power while maintaining full correctness and simplicity.
