Optimize the FSM by encoding the state with a single bit for direction (1 = right, 0 = left) and a separate falling bit, thus splitting the state into two independent registers. This enables more parallel, simpler logic, potentially improving timing by removing dependencies on 2-bit state decoding. 

- Use separate reg for direction and falling.
- On bump events, update direction only if not falling and ground present.
- Transition falling bit based on ground signals only.
- Maintain asynchronous reset initializing direction=0 (left) and falling=0 (walking).
- Moore outputs derived straightforwardly from these two registers.
- Simplify next-state logic as independent direction and falling updates.
- This yields simpler logic, better synthesis optimization opportunities, and potentially reduced critical path.
