Fuse the clarity and simplicity of Example 1's separate `falling` and `direction` registers with the clean, enumerated state approach of Example 2. 

Strategy:
- Maintain `falling` as a separate 1-bit register indicating whether the Lemming is falling.
- Maintain `direction` as a separate 1-bit register (0=left, 1=right) independent of the falling state, so direction is always remembered.
- Use asynchronous reset (posedge areset) to initialize to walking left and not falling.
- On each clock:
  - If falling, remain falling until ground returns; `aaah`=1; bumps ignored.
  - If walking and ground disappears, enter falling state but retain direction.
  - If walking and ground present, bumps update direction: bump_left means go right, bump_right means go left; if both bumps present, flip direction.
- Outputs are combinational based on `falling` and `direction`.
- This approach isolates state and direction management, making bump logic explicit, avoids complex enum states, and reduces next_state combinational complexity, improving PPA metrics.
- Keeps Moore FSM style and asynchronous reset as requested.

This fusion combines Example 1's power and area efficiency with Example 2's clear state separation, yielding a clean, minimal, easy-to-understand FSM with good timing and power characteristics.
