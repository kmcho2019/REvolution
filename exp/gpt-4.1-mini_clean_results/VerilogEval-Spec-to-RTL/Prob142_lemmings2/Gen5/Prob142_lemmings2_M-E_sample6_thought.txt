Implement the FSM using three states as before, but encode the walking direction separately from the FSM state to simplify transitions and clarify direction retention during falling. Use two bits: one to encode if the Lemming is falling or walking, and one for the walking direction (left=0, right=1).

The state is thus split into a "falling" flag and a "direction" flag. On bumps while walking, direction toggles if bumped on either side. On falling, direction remains constant. Transitions:

- If ground=0, falling flag set to 1 (falling), direction held.
- If ground=1 and falling=1, falling reset to 0 (walking), direction resumes.

Use asynchronous reset to set direction to left and falling to zero (walking left). Outputs derive from these two flags directly.

This decomposition simplifies logic by separating direction and falling status, enabling simple toggling on bumps and straightforward falling behavior, improving clarity and potentially reducing area and power.
