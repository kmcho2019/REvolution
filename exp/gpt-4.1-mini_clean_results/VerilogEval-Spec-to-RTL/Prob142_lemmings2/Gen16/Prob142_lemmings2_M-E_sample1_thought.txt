Implement the FSM with three explicit one-hot state bits representing: walking_left, walking_right, and falling. Use a direction flip-flop to remember the walking direction before falling. On bump events when walking, flip the direction accordingly. Ignore bumps while falling. When ground disappears, transition to falling state but retain direction in the flip-flop. When ground reappears, transition back to walking state corresponding to the saved direction. Use asynchronous reset to initialize to walking left state and direction. Outputs are directly derived from the one-hot states, ensuring clear Moore outputs and simplified decoding logic. This approach separates direction storage from the main state encoding, clarifying state transitions and making direction retention explicit during falling.
