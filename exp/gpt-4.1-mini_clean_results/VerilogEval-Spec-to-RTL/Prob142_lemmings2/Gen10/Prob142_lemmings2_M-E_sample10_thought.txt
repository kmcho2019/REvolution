Model the FSM using three explicit states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. Use a two-bit state register to represent these states. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. When ground is lost, transition to FALLING. In FALLING, ignore bumps and wait until ground returns, then resume walking in the same direction prior to falling. Direction is encoded as part of the walking states, so no separate direction register is needed. Outputs are derived purely from the current state, fulfilling Moore FSM requirements with clear state transitions.
