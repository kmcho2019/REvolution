Use a one-bit direction state and a separate falling flag to simplify state representation. On reset, walk left (direction=0, falling=0). When ground is lost, set falling=1 and keep direction. When ground returns, clear falling but keep direction. Bumps only affect direction when not falling and ground is present, flipping direction if bump_left or bump_right is asserted. Outputs are directly decoded from direction and falling. This approach uses two single-bit registers instead of a 2-bit vector, making logic simpler and more readable while preserving behavior.
