Simplify the state machine by splitting the FSM into one-bit state for direction (left or right) and a separate falling flag. Use two separate registers (direction and falling) rather than encoding both in one 2-bit state vector. This separation makes the logic more straightforward and reduces combinational logic complexity.

- direction: 0 = left, 1 = right
- falling: 0 = walking, 1 = falling

On bumps (only when walking), flip direction accordingly.

On ground lost, set falling = 1; on ground restored, falling = 0.

Outputs are directly driven from these two registers.

Asynchronous reset initializes direction to left and falling to 0 (walking left).

This approach reduces the bit width of the state register, simplifies conditions, and makes the FSM easier to understand and maintain while preserving exact original behavior.
