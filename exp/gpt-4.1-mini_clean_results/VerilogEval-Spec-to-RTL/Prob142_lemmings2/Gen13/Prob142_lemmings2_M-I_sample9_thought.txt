To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will simplify the next state logic by combining bump conditions into a single check and minimize logic levels by using bitwise OR for bump detection instead of separate if-else chains. This flattens the combinational logic and slightly reduces LUT usage and critical path delay. Additionally, I will avoid re-checking bump conditions redundantly and remove the explicit separate checks for both bumps simultaneously since bump_left or bump_right being set is enough to flip direction. The asynchronous reset is kept as is for quick reset response. The encoding remains 2-bit (falling + direction) for minimal state bits. This approach simplifies the logic, which can reduce power and area due to fewer logic gates and transitions while maintaining the exact same functionality.
