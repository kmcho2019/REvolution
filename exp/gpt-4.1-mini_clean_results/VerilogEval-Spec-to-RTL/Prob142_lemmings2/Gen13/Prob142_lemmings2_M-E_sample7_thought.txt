Implement the Lemming FSM as a one-hot encoded 4-state Moore machine to improve clarity of each distinct state and simplify output logic.

- Define four states explicitly:
  1. WALK_LEFT
  2. WALK_RIGHT
  3. FALL_LEFT (falling while previously walking left)
  4. FALL_RIGHT (falling while previously walking right)

- Use a 4-bit one-hot register to hold the state, allowing easy decoding of direction and falling status.

- Next state logic:
  - When walking and ground lost (ground=0), transition to the corresponding FALL_* state, preserving direction.
  - When falling and ground returns (ground=1), transition back to the corresponding WALK_* state, preserving direction.
  - When walking and bumped (bump_left or bump_right), flip direction according to rules:
    - bump_left -> walk right
    - bump_right -> walk left
    - bump on both sides -> flip direction regardless
  - While falling, ignore bump inputs completely.

- Asynchronous reset sets the initial state to WALK_LEFT.

- Outputs:
  - walk_left is high when in WALK_LEFT state
  - walk_right is high when in WALK_RIGHT state
  - aaah is high when in either FALL_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT state

This approach explicitly separates states, makes output logic trivial, and helps isolate direction and falling logic clearly in combinational next-state logic.
