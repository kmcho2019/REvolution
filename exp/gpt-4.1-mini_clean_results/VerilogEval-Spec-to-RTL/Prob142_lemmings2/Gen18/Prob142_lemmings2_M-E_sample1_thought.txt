Implement the Lemming FSM using one-hot state encoding with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. Use a separate direction register to track the walking direction independently from the falling state. When falling, only the FALLING state is active, and the direction register holds the last direction before falling started. 

The FSM transitions:
- On reset, start in WALK_LEFT with direction = left.
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT states, if ground goes low, transition to FALLING, but preserve the walking direction in the direction register.
- From FALLING, if ground returns high, transition back to the walking state defined by the direction register.
- Bumps only affect direction when walking and grounded: bump_left sets direction right, bump_right sets direction left, bump_both flips direction.
- Bumps and ground changes in falling state do not affect direction.
- Outputs are generated purely from the state and direction signals (Moore outputs).

This separation of direction and falling as independent entities simplifies next-state logic, reduces combinational dependencies, and explicitly represents the FSM states for clarity and possible improved synthesis results on FPGA.

Use asynchronous reset to initialize to WALK_LEFT state and direction left.

This approach leverages separate registers for direction and state rather than packing both into one variable.
