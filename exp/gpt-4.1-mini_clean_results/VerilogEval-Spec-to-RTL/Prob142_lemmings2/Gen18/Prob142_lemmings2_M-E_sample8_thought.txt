Implement the FSM with three explicit states encoded in two bits: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. Transitions: from WALK states, if ground disappears, go to FALLING; if bumped, switch to the opposite walking direction. From FALLING, when ground reappears, return to the previous walking direction stored in a separate register. The outputs are derived from the current state only, making it a clean Moore FSM. This separates direction and falling clearly, uses state encoding for clarity, and preserves the previous walking direction while falling.
