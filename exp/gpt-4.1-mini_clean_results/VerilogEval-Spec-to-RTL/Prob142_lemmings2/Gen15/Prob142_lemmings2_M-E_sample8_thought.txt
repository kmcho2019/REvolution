Implement the Lemming FSM by explicitly encoding separate direction and falling state bits as individual registers, rather than a combined state vector. Use a two-bit state consisting of a direction bit and a falling bit stored in separate registers. Use combinational logic to compute the next direction only when walking and bumped, ignoring bumps while falling. Transition into falling when ground is lost, and resume walking preserving direction when ground is regained. Use synchronous reset logic with asynchronous reset only for the registers. Outputs are derived purely from the separate bits, making the FSM easier to understand and verify by splitting the responsibilities of direction and falling state. This modular approach increases readability and easier extensibility for future state additions.
