To improve PPA, I will refactor the state update by introducing separate next-state signals for direction and falling to simplify combinational logic and help synthesis tools optimize timing and area. I will also add enable signals for direction and falling registers to prevent unnecessary toggling, reducing power. The direction update will be computed as a simple expression avoiding nested conditionals. This clearer separation of combinational and sequential logic improves clock path timing and allows synthesis to optimize register updates better, while keeping functionality unchanged.
