Implement the FSM with three explicit states using an enumerated-type style: LEFT_WALK, RIGHT_WALK, and FALLING. Store direction separately as a single bit register, updated only on bumps while walking. Use a one-hot approach for the walking states and a separate falling flag. When ground is lost, transition to FALLING while saving the current direction. When ground returns, resume walking in the saved direction. This separation clarifies state transitions, isolates direction updates, and simplifies output logic. Use a synchronous process with asynchronous reset.
