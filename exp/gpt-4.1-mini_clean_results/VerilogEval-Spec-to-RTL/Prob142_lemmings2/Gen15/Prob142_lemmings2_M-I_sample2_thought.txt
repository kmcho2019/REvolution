To optimize PPA, flatten and simplify the next state logic by combining bump conditions and using fewer nested branches. Also, remove redundant state bit indexing by introducing descriptive signals for readability and reduced LUT usage. Since falling state prevents direction changes, factor that out upfront to minimize logic depth. This will improve timing (lower critical path), slightly reduce area by decreasing mux complexity, and reduce power by simplifying switching logic. The FSM remains Moore, with async reset and single clock edge-triggered state updates. Outputs remain combinational from the state bits.
