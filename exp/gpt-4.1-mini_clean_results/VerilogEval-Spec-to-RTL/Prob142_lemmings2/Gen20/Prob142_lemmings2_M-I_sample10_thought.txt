To optimize the FSM for better PPA (performance, power, area) while maintaining clarity and correctness, I will:

- Use one-hot state encoding instead of 2-bit encoding to potentially simplify logic and enable faster state decoding, since only one bit is active per state.
- Explicitly define four one-hot states representing walking left, walking right, falling left, and falling right.
- In one-hot encoding, output decoding and next state logic can be more straightforward and potentially faster due to simpler combinational logic.
- Retain the asynchronous active-high reset and Moore output style.
- Keep next_state logic simple and clear with default next_state assignment.
- Remove redundant condition checks to minimize combinational logic depth.
- One-hot encoding can slightly increase area due to extra flip-flops but can improve timing and sometimes power due to simpler combinational paths.
- This tradeoff is acceptable given the very small FSM size.

Overall, this modification aims to improve timing (critical path delay) and possibly power by simplifying combinational logic, at minimal area cost.
