Implement the FSM using an enumerated type for clarity and a separate register to hold direction during falling. Use a three-state FSM: WALK, FALL, and FALL_TO_WALK (the last to handle ground reappearing synchronization cleanly). Store direction in a separate reg to avoid encoding direction in the state. On bump inputs when walking, flip the direction if bump detected on either side. While falling, ignore bumps. On ground loss, transition from WALK to FALL, saving the direction. When ground returns, transition from FALL to FALL_TO_WALK for one cycle, then to WALK to avoid glitches and better separate state transitions. Outputs are Moore, derived from current state and direction. Use asynchronous active-high reset to WALK with direction left. This architecture separates concerns cleanly, uses explicit states for falling and transitioning back, and direction held separately, improving readability and extensibility.
