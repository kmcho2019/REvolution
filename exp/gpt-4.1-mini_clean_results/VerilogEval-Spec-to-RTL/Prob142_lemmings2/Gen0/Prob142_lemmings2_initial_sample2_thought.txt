Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, transition to FALLING state and output aaah=1. While FALLING, ignore bumps and keep direction stored internally. When ground=1 again, resume walking in the previously stored direction. On bumps (either bump_left or bump_right) while walking and ground=1, switch walking direction accordingly. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are determined by the current state; only one is asserted at a time. The falling state outputs aaah=1 and both walk_left and walk_right are 0.
