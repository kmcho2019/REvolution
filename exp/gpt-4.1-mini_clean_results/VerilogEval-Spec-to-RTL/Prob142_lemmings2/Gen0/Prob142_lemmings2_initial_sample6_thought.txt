Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, start in WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, transition to FALLING and assert aaah=1; walking outputs are 0. On ground=1 and currently FALLING, return to the previous walking direction state before falling, restoring that direction. While walking, if bumped on either side, switch directions. Bumps during falling or at transitions to/from falling are ignored regarding direction. Use asynchronous posedge reset. Outputs depend only on state: WALK_LEFT => walk_left=1, walk_right=0, aaah=0; WALK_RIGHT => walk_left=0, walk_right=1, aaah=0; FALLING => walk_left=0, walk_right=0, aaah=1.
