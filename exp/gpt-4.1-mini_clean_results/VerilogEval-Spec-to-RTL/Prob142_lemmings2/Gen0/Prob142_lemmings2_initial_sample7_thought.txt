Model the Lemming's behavior as a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING. The outputs depend only on the state. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT. While walking left or right, if bumped on left or right (or both), switch direction accordingly. If ground=0, transition to FALLING state, saving the current walking direction for resuming after fall. While falling, output aaah=1 and keep walking outputs 0. When ground returns to 1, go back to the saved walking direction state. Ignore bumps while falling or during transitions related to ground changes. Implement asynchronous reset triggered on posedge of areset, and synchronous state updates on posedge clk.
