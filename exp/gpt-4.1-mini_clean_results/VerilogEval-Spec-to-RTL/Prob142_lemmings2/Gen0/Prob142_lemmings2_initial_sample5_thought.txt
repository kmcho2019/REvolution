Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right indicate walking direction, and aaah indicates falling. On areset (asynchronous), reset to WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, transition to FALLING and output aaah=1. While falling, bump signals are ignored and walking direction is remembered. When ground returns to 1, transition back to the previous walking direction state. When walking and bumped on left, switch to WALK_RIGHT; if bumped on right, switch to WALK_LEFT; if both, also switch direction. All transitions happen at clock posedge, with asynchronous reset.
