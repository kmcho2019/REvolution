Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. 
- On areset, reset to WALK_LEFT.
- While in WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT, if ground=0, transition to FALLING and set aaah=1.
- While in FALLING, stay in FALLING while ground=0 (aaah=1).
- When ground=1 in FALLING state, return to the previous walking direction state (WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT), aaah=0.
- While walking, if bumped on left or right (or both), switch direction.
- Bumps are ignored in FALLING state.
- Bumps in the same cycle as ground disappearance or ground reappearance do not affect direction (because in FALLING state bumps are ignored and transitions occur only on clock edge).
Outputs depend only on the current state: walk_left=1 only in WALK_LEFT, walk_right=1 only in WALK_RIGHT, aaah=1 only in FALLING.
