Implement a Moore FSM with three states:
- WALK_LEFT: walk_left=1, walk_right=0, aaah=0
- WALK_RIGHT: walk_left=0, walk_right=1, aaah=0
- FALLING: walk_left and walk_right remain as before falling, aaah=1

Transitions:
- On reset, go to WALK_LEFT.
- From WALK states, if ground=0, transition to FALLING, preserving direction.
- While FALLING, if ground=1, return to previous walking direction.
- From WALK states, if bumped on left or right (including both), switch directions.
- Bumps have no effect in FALLING state.

The FSM outputs depend only on the current state (Moore). Store walking direction in the state or a register. Use a register to hold direction during FALLING.

Implement asynchronous reset triggered on posedge of areset.
