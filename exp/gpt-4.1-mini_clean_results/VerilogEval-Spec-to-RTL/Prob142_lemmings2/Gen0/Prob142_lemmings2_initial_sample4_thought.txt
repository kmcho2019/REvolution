Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right depend on the walking states, and aaah is asserted only in the FALLING state. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT. When ground is 0, transition to FALLING state and hold that state until ground becomes 1 again. While falling, ignore bump inputs and keep track of the walking direction before the fall to resume after falling. When bumped on left, go to WALK_RIGHT; when bumped on right, go to WALK_LEFT; if both bumped, switch direction regardless. Bump inputs do not affect state when falling or during the transition to falling (the cycle when ground falls to 0). Use registers to hold state and the direction before falling.
