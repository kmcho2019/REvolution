Design a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are asserted in their respective walking states, and aaah is asserted in FALLING. On async reset (areset), the FSM goes to WALK_LEFT. On each clock edge, if ground=0, transition to FALLING and remember the previous walking direction so it can be restored when ground=1 again. While in FALLING, bumps do not affect direction. On ground=1 while in FALLING, transition back to the remembered walking direction. When ground=1 and not falling, bumps cause direction change as specified (bump_left causes walk_right, bump_right causes walk_left, bump on both sides also toggles direction).
