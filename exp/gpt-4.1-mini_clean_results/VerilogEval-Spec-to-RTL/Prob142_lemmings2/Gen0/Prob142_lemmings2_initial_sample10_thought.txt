Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING. On reset, go to WALK_LEFT. When ground is 0, transition to FALLING regardless of bumps. When ground returns to 1 from FALLING, return to the previous walking direction. When walking, if bumped on either side (or both), switch direction. Bumps during FALLING or at ground transitions do not affect direction. Outputs depend only on the current state.
