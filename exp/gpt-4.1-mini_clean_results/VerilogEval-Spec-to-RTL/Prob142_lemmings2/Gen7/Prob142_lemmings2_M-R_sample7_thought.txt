Refactor the FSM by using a single synchronous always block triggered only on clk (no async reset), and implement the asynchronous reset with a separate always block that directly sets the registers asynchronously. Also, split next-state logic computation into combinational logic for clearer separation of concerns.

Specifically:
- Use two registers: falling and direction.
- Introduce combinational signals next_falling and next_direction.
- The asynchronous reset is done with an always block on posedge areset, setting falling and direction immediately.
- The main synchronous always block updates falling and direction on clk posedge.
- The outputs remain combinational from the registered state.
- This separation makes the design more modular, easier to modify, and can help synthesis tools optimize better.

This approach is functionally equivalent but uses separate logic for async reset and next-state computation, increasing clarity and potentially improving timing by reducing the logic in the clocked block.
