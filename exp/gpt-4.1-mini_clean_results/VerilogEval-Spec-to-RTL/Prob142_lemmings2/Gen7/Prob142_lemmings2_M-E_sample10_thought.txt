Implement the FSM with a three-state encoding instead of separate bits for falling and direction. Define explicit states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING_LEFT, and FALLING_RIGHT. This way, the FSM keeps direction information even when falling. On bumps while walking, switch walking direction; when ground disappears, transition to corresponding FALLING state; when ground returns, transition back to walking in the preserved direction. Use a Moore machine with synchronous reset. This approach fully encodes direction and falling in one state variable, simplifying direction update logic and making state transitions explicit.
