Use a single 2-bit register encoding the FSM states to combine walking direction and falling status. Define four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALL_LEFT, FALL_RIGHT. On bumps (when walking), switch walking direction states; on loss of ground, transition to falling states preserving direction; on ground return, transition back to walking states. Outputs are directly decoded from current state. This reduces registers and simplifies logic by using an enumerated FSM instead of separate flags, keeping it a Moore machine with asynchronous reset.
