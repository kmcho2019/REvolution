Simplify the FSM by using two 1-bit registers for direction and falling status instead of a 2-bit vector. On reset, direction is left and not falling. On each clock, update falling based on ground. When walking and ground=1, bumps update direction. While falling, ignore bumps. This breaks the state into two clear signals, making combinational next-state logic more straightforward, improving readability and potentially synthesis results, while preserving the Moore FSM behavior and asynchronous reset.
