Use a simple 2-bit state encoding for the FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, state is WALK_LEFT. If ground is lost, go to FALLING and remember the last walk direction. If ground returns, go back to the saved walk direction. While walking, bumps change direction accordingly. This keeps the design straightforward and easy to read with minimal registers, maintaining Moore FSM behavior and asynchronous reset.
