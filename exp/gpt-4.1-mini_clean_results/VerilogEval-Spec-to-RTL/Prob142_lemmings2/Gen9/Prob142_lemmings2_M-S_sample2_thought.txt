Use a two-bit state encoding for the FSM: one bit for direction (0=left, 1=right) and one bit for falling status (0=walking, 1=falling). On asynchronous reset, initialize to walking left. At each clock, update falling based on ground; if walking and bumped, update direction accordingly. Bump signals are combined simply by OR. This keeps the design minimal with straightforward next-state logic, no nested if-else, and a clean Moore FSM output based on state bits.
