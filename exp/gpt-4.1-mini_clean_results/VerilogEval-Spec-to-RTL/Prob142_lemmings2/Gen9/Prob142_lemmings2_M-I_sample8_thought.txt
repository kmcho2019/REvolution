To improve PPA while preserving the FSM functionality, I will convert the asynchronous reset to a synchronous reset. This often improves timing closure and reduces area and power consumption associated with asynchronous reset logic in the FPGA or ASIC implementation. Since asynchronous reset is not explicitly mandatory, synchronous reset simplifies the state register design and can reduce hold time constraints.

Additionally, I will simplify the next_state combinational logic by merging the bump conditions that flip direction into a single condition, making the code slightly more compact and potentially easier for synthesis tools to optimize. I will also make sure to remove redundant assignments and keep the logic clean.

The encoding remains the same, and outputs remain Moore style. This approach should preserve functionality but yield better timing, lower power, and slightly smaller area.
