To optimize the bump logic for direction switching, replace the nested ifs with a simpler XOR-based expression that captures the "flip direction if bumped (left or right), and also flip if both are bumped" condition. Specifically, when any bump occurs, direction toggles. This removes the extra conditional and yields a single, compact expression for direction update on bump events, minimizing logic depth and switching.

Also, since bumps only affect direction on ground and when not falling, the logic is kept minimal and synchronous.

This reduces logic gates, switching activity on bumps, and possibly improves timing by reducing combinational depth in the update logic. The reset and falling logic remain as before.

Outputs stay Moore-style derived from the current state registers.
