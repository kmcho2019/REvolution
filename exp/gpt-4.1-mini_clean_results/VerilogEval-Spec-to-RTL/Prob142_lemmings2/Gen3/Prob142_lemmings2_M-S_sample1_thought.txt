Use a single synchronous always block to update state and walking direction together at the clock edge, with asynchronous reset.

1. Keep a state register encoding WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING.
2. On reset, set state to WALK_LEFT.
3. Sample ground synchronously to detect rising/falling edges.
4. Only update walking direction when ground is stable (no edge) and state is walking.
5. When bumped while walking and ground stable, immediately flip direction.
6. Transition to FALLING state when ground lost (falling edge or ground=0).
7. Transition back to walking state matching stored direction on ground rising edge or stable ground=1.
8. While falling, ignore bump inputs and direction changes.
9. Output signals are purely Moore outputs from current state.

This approach avoids separate walking_dir register by encoding direction in states, uses ground edge detection within the same clock domain, and combines logic for clarity and fewer registers. It ensures immediate direction change on bumps, stable transition to and from falling, and clear, glitch-free outputs.
