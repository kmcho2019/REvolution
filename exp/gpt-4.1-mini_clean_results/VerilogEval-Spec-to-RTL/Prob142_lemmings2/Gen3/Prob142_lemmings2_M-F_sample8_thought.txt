To fix the walking direction mismatch, we must handle bumps precisely as per specification:

- If the Lemming is walking left and bump_left=1 (regardless of bump_right), it switches to walk right.
- If walking right and bump_right=1 (regardless of bump_left), it switches to walk left.
- If bumped on both sides simultaneously, the Lemming switches direction as well (bump_left and bump_right both 1).
  This effectively means if any bump on the side the Lemming is facing occurs, it switches direction.

Since "bumped on both sides" still causes direction switch, the logic becomes:

- Walking Left: if bump_left==1 (no matter bump_right) → switch to walk right
- Walking Right: if bump_right==1 (no matter bump_left) → switch to walk left

We will implement that logic explicitly.

For ground edge detection, sampling ground synchronously on clk to get ground_d and comparing current ground with ground_d in sequential logic allows us to detect falling and rising edges reliably.

We define ground_falling_edge and ground_rising_edge synchronously (one clock cycle delayed) in sequential logic.

We modify FSM next state logic to:

- On falling edge of ground or ground=0 → go to FALLING
- When falling and rising edge of ground or ground=1 → resume stored walking_dir
- During walking and stable ground, apply bump logic strictly as above.
- Ignore bumps during falling or ground edges.
  
We also update walking_dir only when entering a walking state, based on the direction actually selected from bump inputs.

Finally, initializing ground_d on reset to ground avoids initial edge detection glitches.

This corrected approach ensures direction switches exactly as the problem demands, with no spurious direction changes or mismatches between walking_dir and outputs.

Outputs remain Moore: determined solely by current state.
