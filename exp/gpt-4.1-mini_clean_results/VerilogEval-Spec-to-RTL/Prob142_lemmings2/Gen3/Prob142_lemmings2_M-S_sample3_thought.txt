Simplify the FSM to two states representing direction only: WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT. Use a separate flag "falling" to indicate the FALLING condition. This way, direction and falling status are tracked independently.

- On reset, start walking left (WALK_LEFT), falling=0.
- If ground==0, set falling=1 but keep direction unchanged.
- When falling=1 and ground==1, set falling=0 to resume walking.
- If not falling, bumps on either side flip direction.
- If falling, bumps are ignored.
- Outputs depend on direction and falling flag:
  - walk_left = (direction == left) && !falling
  - walk_right = (direction == right) && !falling
  - aaah = falling

This reduces the FSM states from 3 to 2 and separates falling status, making transitions and outputs simpler and clearer.
