Use a three-state Moore FSM with explicit state encoding for walking left, walking right, and falling. The walking states keep track of the current walking direction. On bump signals (from either side or both), the FSM switches between walking left and walking right states only when on ground (not falling). When ground goes low, transition to the falling state, saving the current walking direction in a dedicated register. While falling, bumps are ignored and outputs aaah=1; upon ground returning high, FSM returns to the previously saved walking direction state. Asynchronous reset brings the FSM to walk left state. This approach cleanly separates walking and falling states and makes direction changes explicit and easy to track.
