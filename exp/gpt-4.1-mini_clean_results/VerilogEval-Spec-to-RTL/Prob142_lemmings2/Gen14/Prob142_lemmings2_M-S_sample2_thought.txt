Use a single-bit state to represent direction (0=left, 1=right) and a separate falling bit. On reset, initialize to walking left and not falling. The next state updates falling first: if ground is lost, enter falling state; if ground returns, exit falling but keep direction. Only when not falling, bumps flip direction appropriately, otherwise direction remains unchanged. Outputs directly follow the current state bits. This approach minimizes logic and registers while preserving full behavior.
