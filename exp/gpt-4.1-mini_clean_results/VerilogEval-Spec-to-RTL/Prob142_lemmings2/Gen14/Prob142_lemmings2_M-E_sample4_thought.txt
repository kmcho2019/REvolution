Implement the Lemming FSM as a one-hot encoded Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The FALLING state remembers the last walking direction in a separate flip-flop. This approach separates falling from walking states explicitly and stores direction independently during fall. 

- Use three flip-flops for one-hot states: walk_left_state, walk_right_state, and falling_state.
- Use a direction register to remember direction during falling.
- On ground loss, transition from walking states to FALLING state, saving direction.
- While falling, ignore bumps; upon ground return, transition back to stored walking direction state.
- On bump_left, transition to walk_right; on bump_right, transition to walk_left; on bump_left & bump_right, also flip direction.
- Async reset sets to WALK_LEFT state and direction left.
- Outputs are derived directly from current state and stored direction for falling.
- This architecture makes direction explicit and separation of concerns clear, improving clarity at some cost of area.
