Implement the Lemming FSM using three one-hot encoded flip-flops representing three distinct states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. This makes state decoding and output logic very intuitive and eliminates the need for bit-field encoding. The FSM transitions as follows:

- On reset, start in WALK_LEFT.
- When in WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT and ground is lost (ground=0), transition to FALLING while storing the current walking direction in a direction flip-flop for resuming after fall.
- While WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT on ground, bumps cause immediate transition to opposite walking state.
- While FALLING, bumps and changes in ground do not affect direction; when ground returns (ground=1), transition back to the stored walking direction.
- Outputs are directly asserted from the one-hot states: walk_left, walk_right, and aaah for falling.
- Using separate direction storage avoids mixing direction bits inside falling state and simplifies logic.
- This design is clear, modular, and explicit with all states one-hot encoded, which can be beneficial for certain synthesis and debug flows.

This approach uses four flip-flops total (3 state + 1 direction) instead of 2 bits in a packed register, trading minimal area for clarity and explicitness.
