Implement the FSM with three explicit states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. Use a two-bit state encoding to clearly separate walking directions from falling. On bumps during walking, switch direction regardless if bump_left, bump_right, or both. When ground=0, transition from either walking state to FALLING, preserving the last walking direction in a separate register. When ground=1, return to the saved walking direction state. Outputs are derived directly from the current state. This explicit state approach enhances clarity and modularity.
