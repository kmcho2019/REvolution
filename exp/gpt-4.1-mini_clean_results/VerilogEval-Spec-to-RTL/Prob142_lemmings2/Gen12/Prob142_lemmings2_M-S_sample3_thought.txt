Simplify the FSM by encoding direction and falling as two separate 1-bit registers rather than a combined 2-bit state. This can improve readability and slightly reduce next-state logic complexity. Keep the asynchronous reset, but use two separate registers: one for direction and one for falling.

- direction = 0 for left, 1 for right
- falling = 0 walking, 1 falling

On reset, direction=0 (left), falling=0 (walking).

On each clock:
- If falling, ignore bumps; when ground returns, falling=0, keep direction.
- If walking and ground goes low, falling=1 (falling), keep direction.
- If walking and ground is high, bump_left or bump_right (or both) cause direction flip:
   * bump_left=1 sets direction=1 (right)
   * bump_right=1 sets direction=0 (left)
   * both bumps flip direction

Outputs are simple assignments:
- aaah = falling
- walk_left = ~falling & ~direction
- walk_right = ~falling & direction

This separates concerns, simplifies next-state logic, and still fully implements the required FSM behavior.
