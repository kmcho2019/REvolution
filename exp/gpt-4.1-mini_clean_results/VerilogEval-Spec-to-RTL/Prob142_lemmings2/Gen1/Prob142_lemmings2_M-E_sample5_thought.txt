Implement a three-state Moore FSM (LEFT, RIGHT, FALL) where the direction is stored in a separate register "dir" updated only when walking. On reset, dir=LEFT. The FSM state tracks whether the lemming is currently walking left, walking right, or falling. The outputs depend on the FSM state and dir. To handle bumps and ground transitions cleanly, split direction and falling state: direction changes only when walking and bumped (ignoring bumps during falling or in the exact cycle of ground loss). Use an edge detector for ground (register ground_d) to detect falling start (ground changes from 1 to 0). Only after the FSM has entered FALL state (one cycle after ground drops) bumps are ignored. This prevents direction change at the exact cycle ground disappears. On ground rising (0->1), return to walking with preserved dir. This approach cleanly separates direction and falling state and delays falling effects by one cycle, preventing glitches at ground edges.
