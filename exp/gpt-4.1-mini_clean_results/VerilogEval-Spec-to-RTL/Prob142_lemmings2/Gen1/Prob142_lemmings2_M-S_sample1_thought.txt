Simplify the FSM to only two states: WALKING and FALLING. Use a 1-bit direction bit (dir) to track walking direction (0 = left, 1 = right). On reset, start walking left (dir=0) and in WALKING state. While walking:
- If ground is lost, transition to FALLING, keep direction unchanged.
- If bumped on either side, flip dir.
While falling:
- If ground is regained, return to WALKING, keep previous direction.
- Ignore bumps during falling.
Outputs depend on state and direction:
- walk_left = WALKING & (dir==0)
- walk_right = WALKING & (dir==1)
- aaah = FALLING
This approach uses fewer states and simpler variables, avoiding a separate last_walk_dir register.
