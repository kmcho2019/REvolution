Refactor the FSM by separating the walking direction and falling state into two variables: a walking direction bit (walk_dir, 0 for left, 1 for right) and a falling bit (falling). On reset, walk_dir=0 (left), falling=0. The walking direction updates only on bumps and when not falling. The falling bit updates based on ground signal: falling=1 when ground=0, falling=0 when ground=1. Keep a register prev_walk_dir to remember walking direction before falling, updated only when not falling. The outputs derive from walk_dir and falling flags. This clean separation prevents timing mismatches between walking direction and previous direction, eliminating the lag in prev_walk_state update and avoids functional mismatches. Use synchronous logic with asynchronous reset, and combinational assigns for outputs.
