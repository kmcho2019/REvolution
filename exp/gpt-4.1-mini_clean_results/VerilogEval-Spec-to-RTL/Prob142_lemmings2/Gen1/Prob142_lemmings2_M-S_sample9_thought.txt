Use a two-bit state encoding to represent direction and falling: one bit for direction (0=left,1=right), one bit for falling status. On reset, start walking left and not falling. When ground=0, set falling bit and assert aaah. When ground=1 and falling, clear falling bit and restore previous direction stored in the direction bit (which does not change during falling). When walking and bumped on any side, flip direction bit. While falling, bumps do not affect direction. Outputs derive directly from the current state bits. This simplification removes the separate prev_walk_state register by embedding direction in the state and only changing it while walking.
