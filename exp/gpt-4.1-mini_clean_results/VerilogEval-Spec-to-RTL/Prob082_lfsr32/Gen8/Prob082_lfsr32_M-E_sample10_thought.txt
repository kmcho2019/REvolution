Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by updating each bit individually inside a for-loop, computing the next state bit-by-bit in a procedural block to clearly express the tap logic. On reset, initialize q to 1 synchronously. Use a wire to hold the feedback bit (the current LSB) for clarity. This approach avoids ternary operators and shifts, instead performing explicit bitwise assignments, improving code readability and giving a fine-grained view of the feedback propagation for verification and potential customization.
