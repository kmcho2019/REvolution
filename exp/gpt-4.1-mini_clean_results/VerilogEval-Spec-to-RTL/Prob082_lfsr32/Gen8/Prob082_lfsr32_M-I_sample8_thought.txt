To optimize PPA, I will keep the logic essentially the same since it is already minimal, but unroll the XOR operations explicitly instead of using a TAP_MASK constant. This can sometimes help synthesis tools optimize XOR trees more efficiently, potentially reducing delay. Also, replacing the shift operation by explicit bit assignments may improve timing and area by enabling better resource sharing. The reset remains synchronous and active high. Overall, this could yield a slightly better timing and area profile without changing functionality.
