Instead of using a single 32-bit XOR with a tap mask, implement the Galois LFSR by explicitly calculating the next value of each bit using conditional XOR with q[0] only on tapped bits. This means for each bit with a tap, the next bit is computed as q[i] XOR q[0], otherwise it is just q[i]. Shift bits right by one position to form the new state, inserting the feedback bit at the highest position. This bitwise approach is more explicit and could provide easier analysis and optimization opportunities during synthesis. Also, synchronous reset sets q to 32'h1, triggered on posedge clk.
