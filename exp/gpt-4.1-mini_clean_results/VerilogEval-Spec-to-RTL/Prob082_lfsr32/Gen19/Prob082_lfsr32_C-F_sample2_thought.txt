Use a single always block with synchronous active-high reset. Compute the feedback bit as q[0]. For non-tap bits, shift right normally; for tap bits (31, 21, 1, 0), XOR the shifted bit with the feedback bit. This keeps combinational logic local to tap bits only, minimizing switching and critical path complexity compared to applying the XOR across all bits. Writing the assignments explicitly preserves clarity and eases synthesis optimizations, avoiding complex bit masks or wide broadcasted XORs. This approach achieves optimal power, performance, and area balance by leveraging the best trade-offs from both examples.
