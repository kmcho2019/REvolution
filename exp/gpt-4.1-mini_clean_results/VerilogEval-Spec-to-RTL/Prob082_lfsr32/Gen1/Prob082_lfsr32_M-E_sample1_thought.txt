Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by directly computing the next state bits one by one using combinational logic inside the clocked always block. The LFSR shifts right each cycle. The feedback bit is the current LSB q[0]. For tapped bits (31, 21, 1, 0-based), the new bit is XOR of the previous bit shifted in and the feedback bit. For other bits, the new bit is just the previous bit shifted in from the left neighbor. Initialize synchronously to 32'h1 on reset. This approach avoids slicing and complicated range assignments, making the logic clearer and less error-prone.
