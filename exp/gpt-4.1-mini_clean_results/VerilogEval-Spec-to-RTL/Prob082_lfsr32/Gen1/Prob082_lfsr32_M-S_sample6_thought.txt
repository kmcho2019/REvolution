Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by shifting right each clock cycle. The feedback bit is q[0]. For each tap bit position (32, 22, 2, and 1), the bit to be shifted in is XORed with the feedback; other bits just shift right from the next higher bit. On synchronous reset, set q to 32'h1. This can be done compactly by computing next_q in a combinational way inside the sequential block.
