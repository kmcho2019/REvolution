Use a compact and elegant approach: at each clock edge (when not resetting), capture the feedback bit as q[0]. Then update q in one statement: shift right by one bit, inserting the feedback at q[31]. For the tapped bits, if the feedback is 1, XOR the corresponding bit with 1 (flip the bit); if feedback is 0, bits remain unchanged from the shifted value. This corresponds exactly to the Galois LFSR operation, where feedback acts as a control signal to XOR tap bits. This method avoids cumbersome per-bit assignments and aligns with the classic Galois LFSR implementation style.
