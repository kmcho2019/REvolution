Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using an explicit bitwise feedback calculation within the sequential block. Instead of using a mask and a single XOR operation on the entire vector, compute the feedback bit (new bit shifted in at the MSB) by XORing the tapped bits (q[0], q[1], q[21], q[31]). Then shift the entire register right by one and insert the feedback bit at the MSB. Use synchronous active-high reset to initialize q to 32'h1 on the clock edge. This approach makes the feedback taps explicit and may simplify logic for synthesis by clearly indicating which bits affect feedback.
