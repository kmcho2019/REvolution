Refactor the design by separating the next state combinational logic from the state register update. Compute the next LFSR value using an assign statement that implements the bit-level feedback logic explicitly for each bit, rather than relying on a vector shift and XOR with a mask. This approach improves clarity of feedback taps, breaks down the logic into clear bitwise operations, and keeps the synchronous reset and register update in the always block. The LFSR bits shift right each clock cycle, with XOR feedback applied to tapped bits only when the LSB is 1.
