Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by explicitly computing the feedback bit from q[0], then individually updating each bit of the shift register on the positive edge of the clock. This approach uses a generate loop to create combinational logic for each bit's next value based on whether its position is tapped. This explicit bit-by-bit update clarifies the tap XOR logic and maintains a synchronous active-high reset initializing q to 32'h1.
