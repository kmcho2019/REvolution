Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using a purely sequential approach with an explicit combinational feedback bit calculation. On every positive clock edge, if reset is asserted, set q to 32'h1. Otherwise, shift q right by one bit. For the tapped bits (positions 32, 22, 2, and 1), XOR the feedback bit into the bit just shifted into that tap position. The feedback bit is the outgoing bit shifted out (the LSB before the shift). We can implement this efficiently by treating q as a 32-bit register and updating it all at once in an always block with combinational logic producing the next state. To avoid indexing confusion, taps are at q[31], q[21], q[1], and q[0], corresponding to bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based). This method makes it clear and synthesizable, avoids loops inside the sequential block, and maintains proper synchronous reset and clocking.
