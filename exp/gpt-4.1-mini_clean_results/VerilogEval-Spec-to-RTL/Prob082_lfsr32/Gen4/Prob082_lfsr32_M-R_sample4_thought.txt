Refactor the design by moving the LFSR state into a separate internal register and driving the output q as a continuous assignment from that register. The state register updates on the clock's positive edge with synchronous reset, while next_state is computed combinationally in an always_comb block. This approach separates the sequential and combinational parts distinctly, avoids using a reg output, and makes q a wire output driven by the registered state, which can improve clarity and port usage.
