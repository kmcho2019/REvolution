To improve power and performance without sacrificing clarity, I will restructure the LFSR update logic to explicitly compute the feedback bit first. Then shift the register right and conditionally XOR only the tapped bits with the feedback, done via a mask and bitwise operations. This reduces XOR fanout by avoiding wide conditional expressions and isolates combinational logic to a smaller part. 

Additionally, I will declare the TAP_MASK with a shifted 1-based indexing consistent with the problem statement, and handle taps as (bit positions - 1) due to zero-based indexing. This makes the design clearer and less error prone.

This approach also facilitates better synthesis optimization, potentially improving timing and reducing switching activity by better localizing the XOR gates. The synchronous reset and clock edge triggering remain the same.
