To improve the PPA metrics, I will reduce the combinational logic fan-in and toggle rate by minimizing the use of a wide bitwise AND with a replicated bit ({32{q[0]}}). Instead, I will implement the feedback logic step-by-step to only conditionally XOR the tapped bits with the feedback bit, which is q[0]. This avoids unnecessary toggling on all bits and breaks down the logic into smaller XOR operations. This can reduce dynamic power and improve timing by spreading the XOR load. Also, I will use an intermediate variable for clarity and possible better synthesis optimization. The synchronous reset remains the same. This tradeoff keeps the design clean and potentially improves timing and power without increasing area significantly.
