Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using a generate-for loop to process each bit individually in combinational logic. Compute the next state of the shift register as a combinational signal based on the current q and then register it on the positive edge of the clock with synchronous active-high reset. This approach models the bit-wise XOR feedback more explicitly and can make tap positions clearer and scalable by handling bit updates uniformly inside a loop. The taps will be handled by checking if q[0] is high, then XORing tap bits accordingly. The output q is updated as a registered signal.
