Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by explicitly updating each bit individually within the always block. Instead of using a mask and a wide XOR, compute the feedback bit (from q[0]) and update each bit based on whether it is tapped or not by XORing it with the feedback. This approach breaks down the shift and feedback logic per bit position, making the structure more explicit and easily modifiable for other tap patterns. Use a synchronous reset to set q to 32'h1.
