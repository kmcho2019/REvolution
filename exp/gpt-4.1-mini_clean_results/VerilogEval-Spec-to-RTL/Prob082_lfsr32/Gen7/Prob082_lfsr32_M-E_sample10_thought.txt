Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using explicit conditional XOR feedback for each tapped bit during the shift, avoiding wide vector masking operations. Compute the next state by shifting right and selectively XORing feedback bit with tapped positions directly. This approach reduces combinational logic complexity and power by handling only the tapped bits instead of masking all bits. Use a synchronous reset to initialize q to 1, and update q at the positive clock edge.
