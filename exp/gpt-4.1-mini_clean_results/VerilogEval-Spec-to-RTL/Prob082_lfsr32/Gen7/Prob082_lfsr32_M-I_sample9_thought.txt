To further optimize the 32-bit Galois LFSR for improved PPA, the following strategy is applied:

1. Feedback Computation Simplification:
   Instead of using a fixed TAP_MASK and XOR with shifted register, compute the feedback bit explicitly from tapped bits XORed with q[0]. Since taps are at bits 31, 21, 1, 0 (0-based indexing), the feedback bit can be calculated by XORing these specific bits of q.

2. Use explicit feedback XOR:
   This reduces unnecessary XOR gates toggling bits unnecessarily. It also potentially shortens the critical path by reducing the number of bits involved in the XOR tree.

3. Update the register by shifting right, then inject the feedback bit at the MSB:
   The Galois LFSR shifts right by one bit, with the new MSB driven by the XOR of taps and the shifted out bit.

4. Maintain synchronous reset to 1.

This approach yields a minimal XOR tree (only 4 bits) and a simple shift plus feedback assignment, improving power due to less switching in the XOR network and possibly improving timing due to a shorter XOR critical path. Area remains minimal with a single 32-bit register and small combinational logic.

Also, using generate or parameterized approach is not necessary as taps are fixed. The combinational feedback calculation is explicitly described for clarity and synthesis efficiency.
