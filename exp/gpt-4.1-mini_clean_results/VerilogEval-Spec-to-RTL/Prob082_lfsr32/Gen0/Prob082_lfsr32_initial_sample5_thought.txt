Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. Since bit positions are 1-indexed, convert these to 0-indexed bits for indexing in Verilog as 31, 21, 1, and 0 respectively. On each clock positive edge, if reset is high, synchronously reset q to 32'h1. Otherwise, calculate the feedback bit as q[0], and for the tapped bits, XOR q[bit-1] with feedback to get their next value; all other bits shift right normally (q[i] = q[i+1]). Implement the shift with feedback logic accordingly.
