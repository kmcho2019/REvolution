Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. On synchronous reset (active high), set q to 32'h1. The taps are at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based indexing), corresponding to q[31], q[21], q[1], and q[0]. The feedback bit is the XOR of q[0] with the tap bits. Each bit shifts right, but tapped positions get XORed with q[0] to produce their new value.
