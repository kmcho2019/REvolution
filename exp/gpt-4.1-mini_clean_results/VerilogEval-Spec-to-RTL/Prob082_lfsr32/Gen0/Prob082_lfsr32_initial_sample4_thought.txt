Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based indexing). The LFSR shifts right on each positive clock edge. For each tapped bit, its next value is the XOR of the current LSB (q[0]) and the bit's current value; other bits shift right unchanged. On synchronous reset (active high), reset q to 32'h1.
