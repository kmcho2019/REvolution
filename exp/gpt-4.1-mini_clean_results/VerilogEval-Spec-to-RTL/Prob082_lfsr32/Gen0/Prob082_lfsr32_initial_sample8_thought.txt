Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. The LFSR shifts right each clock cycle. The input bit for the MSB is calculated by XORing the taps with the current LSB (q[0]). On synchronous reset, q is set to 32'h1. Use posedge clock triggering and synchronous active-high reset.
