Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR that shifts right on each positive clock edge. The taps are at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based indexing). In Verilog (0-based), these correspond to bits [31], [21], [1], and [0]. On each clock cycle, the rightmost bit q[0] is shifted out and fed back through XOR taps to certain bits. The feedback bit is q[0]. Each tapped bit is XORed with the feedback bit to produce its next value; other bits just shift right unchanged. Synchronous active-high reset sets q to 32'h1.
