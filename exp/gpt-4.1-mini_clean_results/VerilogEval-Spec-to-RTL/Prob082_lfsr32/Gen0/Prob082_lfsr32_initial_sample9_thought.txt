Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR shifting right. The feedback bit is the XOR of the tapped bits with the LSB before shifting. Specifically, taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1 correspond to indices 31, 21, 1, and 0 in zero-based indexing. On each clock rising edge, if reset is high, load 32'h1, else update q as per Galois LFSR logic: shift right, and XOR the feedback bit into the tap positions.
