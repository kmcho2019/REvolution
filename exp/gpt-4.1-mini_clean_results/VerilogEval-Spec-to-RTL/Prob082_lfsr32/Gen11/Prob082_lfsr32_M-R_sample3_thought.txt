Refactor the Galois LFSR to use a combinational next-state function and a registered output with a synchronous reset. Compute the next state of the 32-bit register as a wire by applying the right shift and XOR feedback on the tapped bits. Then on the positive clock edge, update the register q with either the reset value or the computed next state.
