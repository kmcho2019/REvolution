To fuse the best of both examples, we combine the clarity and modularity of separating combinational next-state logic with the minimalistic conditional approach from the first example. We update q synchronously on the clock's positive edge with an active-high synchronous reset. This keeps the logic concise and easy for synthesis tools to optimize. By avoiding explicit if-else in sequential logic and expressing the next state as a combinational signal, we aid timing and power optimization. The design is minimal in area and straightforward to understand, ensuring an optimal balance of performance, power, and area.
