Instead of using a direct shift-and-mask approach for the Galois LFSR, this design explicitly computes the feedback bit by XORing the tapped bits of the current state, then shifts the register right by one and inserts the feedback bit at the MSB. This approach makes the feedback calculation more explicit and potentially eases formal verification. The taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 correspond to bits q[31], q[21], q[1], and q[0]. The feedback is the XOR of these bits and the current LSB q[0] (since in Galois LFSR the feedback influences some bits during shifting). After calculating the feedback, the register shifts right by one, and the feedback bit is inserted into the MSB position. Reset is synchronous and sets q to 32'h1 on the positive clock edge.
