Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by explicitly shifting right bit-by-bit inside a for-loop and selectively XORing the feedback bit with tap positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. Instead of using a constant XOR mask, compute feedback at each tapped position individually by XORing the output bit q[0].
