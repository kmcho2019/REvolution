To improve the PPA of the 32-bit Galois LFSR, replace the vector XOR and right shift with explicit bit-level next-state logic. Each bit shifts right by one, and bits at tap positions (22, 2, 1) XOR the feedback bit (q[0]). This reduces combinational XOR gate fanout and toggling, improving timing and power. The code is fully synchronous with reset. Using bitwise operations at individual bits also reduces area compared to a full 32-bit XOR mask.
