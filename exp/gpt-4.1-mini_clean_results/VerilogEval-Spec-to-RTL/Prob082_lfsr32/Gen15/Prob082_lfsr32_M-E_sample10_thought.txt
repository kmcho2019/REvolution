Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 by explicitly calculating each bit's next state using combinational logic inside the always block. Instead of using a mask and a conditional XOR on the entire vector, compute the next bit values one by one: The MSB (bit 31) is the XOR of the current LSB (q[0]) and current MSB (q[31]) because of the tap at bit 32; similarly for bits 21, 1, and 0 (due to taps at 22, 2, and 1). All other bits shift right by one without change. This fully unrolls the LFSR update logic, making the feedback taps explicit and potentially improving clarity and synthesis optimization by avoiding a vector XOR operation.

Also, use a synchronous active-high reset to initialize q to 32'h1 on reset, and update q on the positive clock edge.
