I will implement the Galois LFSR by explicitly calculating the feedback bit as q[0] and then update each bit of the shift register individually based on whether it is a tap position or not. This means bit 31 (top bit) will be assigned q[0] XOR q[31], taps at bit 21 and 1 will XOR the feedback bit, while the rest of the bits simply shift right without XOR. I will define the next state in a combinational manner within the always block for clarity, and use synchronous active high reset to initialize q to 1. This approach explicitly demonstrates the LFSR feedback on tap bits and may be easier to modify or extend for other tap configurations.
