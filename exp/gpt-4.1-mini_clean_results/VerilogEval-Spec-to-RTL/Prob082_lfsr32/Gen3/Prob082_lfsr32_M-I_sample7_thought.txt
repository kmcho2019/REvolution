To optimize the existing 32-bit Galois LFSR, the focus will be on simplifying the combinational logic for improved timing and power. Instead of using a conditional inside the sequential block that selects between (q>>1) and (q>>1)^TAP_MASK, we can incorporate the feedback bit (q[0]) directly into the XOR operation as a mask multiplier. This removes the multiplexer, reduces switching activity by not toggling the entire XOR when feedback is zero, and can enable better synthesis optimizations. Also, by defining the mask as a wire that depends on the feedback bit, combinational logic can be flattened. This approach yields a single assignment inside the clocked process with fewer gates and potentially faster critical path. Reset behavior remains synchronous active high with q initialized to 32'h1.
