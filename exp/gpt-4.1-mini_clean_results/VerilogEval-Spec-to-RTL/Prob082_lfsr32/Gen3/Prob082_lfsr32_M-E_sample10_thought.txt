Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using a combinational next-state calculation where for each bit, the next value depends on the current bit and the feedback bit q[0]. This approach explicitly computes each bit of the next state: the LSB is always the feedback bit, and each higher bit is either the bit to its left or XORed with the feedback bit if that position is tapped. This avoids shifting the entire register at once and directly expresses the Galois LFSR structure with combinational logic before registering the result on the clock edge. The synchronous reset sets q to 32'h1.
