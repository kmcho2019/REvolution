Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by explicitly computing the feedback bit and performing the shift with the taps applied as XOR feedback on selected bit positions inside the sequential logic. Use synchronous reset to initialize the state to 1. This approach calculates the feedback once per clock cycle from the output bit and the tap bits by XORing them, then shifts right inserting the feedback at the MSB, achieving the required LFSR behavior without a large constant mask.
