Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR by explicitly updating each bit based on the Galois configuration with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. Instead of using a mask and vector operations, directly express the new value of each bit: bit 31 (MSB) is XOR of q[0] and q[31] because of the tap at bit 32, and other tapped positions are XORed with q[0], while non-tapped bits just shift in the previous bit. This explicit bitwise assignment improves clarity and reduces combinational logic complexity for synthesis. The synchronous reset initializes q to 32'h1. The design triggers on the rising edge of clk.
