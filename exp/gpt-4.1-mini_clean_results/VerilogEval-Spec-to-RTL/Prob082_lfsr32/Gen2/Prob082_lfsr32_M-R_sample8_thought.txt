Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR as a right-shifting register with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1 (indices 31, 21, 1, 0). Compute the feedback bit as q[0]. On each clock rising edge with synchronous reset, update the register by shifting right: each bit takes the value of the bit to its left; tapped bits are XORed with the feedback bit before shifting in. The least significant bit (q[0]) receives the feedback bit itself. Use a simple for-loop inside an always block to cleanly express the logic, ensuring clear and correct tap XORs and correct synchronous reset to 32'h1.
