Implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR using a for-loop inside the always block to explicitly update each bit of the shift register on every clock edge. Compute the feedback bit as the XOR of the LSB of q with a tap indicator bit. Since it's a Galois LFSR, bits without taps simply shift right by one; bits with taps XOR their shifted bit with the feedback. This approach explicitly models the feedback effect on each bit, providing clear visibility into the internal bitwise operations and ensuring correctness without relying on clever vector operations.
