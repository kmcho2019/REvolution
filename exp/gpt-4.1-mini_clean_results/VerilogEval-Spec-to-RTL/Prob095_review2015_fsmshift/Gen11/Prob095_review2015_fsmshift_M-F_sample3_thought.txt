The original code asserts shift_ena for 5 cycles instead of the required 4 because it holds shift_ena high immediately on reset with counter=4, then continues asserting shift_ena while counting down from 4 to 1, deasserting only when counter hits 0. This results in one extra cycle of enable.

To fix this, initialize the counter to 3 on reset instead of 4 and assert shift_ena whenever the counter is non-zero or on the first cycle after reset.

Concretely:

- On synchronous reset, load counter with 3 and assert shift_ena.
- On each clock cycle, if counter != 0, decrement it.
- shift_ena is asserted while counter != 0 or if we are in the first cycle after reset (counter just loaded).
  
By defining counter to count down the remaining *remaining* shift cycles after the first assert cycle, we ensure shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 cycles total.

This approach maintains a synchronous reset, simple counter logic, and ensures exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena asserted after reset, fixing the off-by-one error.
