To meet the specification, the module must assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles immediately after synchronous reset is deasserted (goes from 1 to 0). During reset, shift_ena must be 0. After those 4 cycles, shift_ena should remain 0 indefinitely until the next reset.

Key points and fix:
- Use a counter to track the 4 cycles post-reset.
- On synchronous reset (reset==1), initialize counter to 0 and shift_ena to 0.
- Detect the deassertion edge of reset by sampling reset in a register.
- When reset transitions from 1 to 0 (i.e., after reset is released), load counter to 4.
- While counter > 0, assert shift_ena and decrement counter each clock cycle.
- Once counter reaches 0, keep shift_ena deasserted.
- This logic cleanly separates the reset cycle from the 4 enable cycles post-reset.
- Non-blocking assignments ensure proper synchronous behavior.
- This version does not implement pattern detection as it is outside the current scope.
