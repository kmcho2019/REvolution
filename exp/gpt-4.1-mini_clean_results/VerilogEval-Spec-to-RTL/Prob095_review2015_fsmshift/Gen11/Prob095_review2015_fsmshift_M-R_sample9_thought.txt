To avoid SystemVerilog enum types and possible tool compatibility issues, I will replace the FSM with simple registers and counters using only standard Verilog constructs. The design keeps the same functionality:

- On synchronous reset, the module asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles
- Afterwards, shift_ena remains low indefinitely until next reset

I will implement a 3-bit counter that loads 4 on reset and counts down each clock cycle until zero. While the counter is not zero, shift_ena is asserted. When the counter reaches zero, shift_ena is deasserted and held low.

This approach eliminates the FSM typedef, uses only standard registers and combinational logic, making it more compatible with pure Verilog tools.

The counter and shift_ena logic are straightforward and synchronous with the clock and reset.
