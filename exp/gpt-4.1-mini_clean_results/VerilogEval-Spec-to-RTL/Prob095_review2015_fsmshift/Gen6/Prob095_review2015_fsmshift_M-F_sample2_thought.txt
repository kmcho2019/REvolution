Add an input `pattern_detected` to signal the proper bit pattern detection (since the problem states "whenever the proper bit pattern is detected"). Both reset and pattern_detected will trigger a reload of a 3-bit counter to 4, which counts down each cycle. While the counter is nonzero, assert shift_ena. When the counter reaches zero, shift_ena stays low indefinitely until next reset or pattern detection pulse. This handles both initial reset and pattern detection events properly with a clean FSM-like behavior. The reset remains synchronous active-high, and the counter is loaded on the rising edge of clk only once per event, avoiding multiple reloads during a prolonged reset.
