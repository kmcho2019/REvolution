To simplify, I will use a 2-bit counter counting from 0 to 3, representing the four cycles of assertion. On synchronous reset, the counter will be reset to 0, and shift_ena asserted. For the next three cycles, the counter increments on each clock edge while shift_ena remains asserted. Once the counter reaches 3, shift_ena deasserts and stays low until the next reset.

This approach uses a simple up-counter counting from 0 to 3, asserting shift_ena while counter <= 3 (i.e., for 4 cycles), then stops.

This eliminates the need for a decrement and reduces the counter size and complexity.
