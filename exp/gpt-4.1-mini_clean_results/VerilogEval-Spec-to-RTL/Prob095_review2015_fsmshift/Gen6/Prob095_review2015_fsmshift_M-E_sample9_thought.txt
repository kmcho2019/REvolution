Use a simple shift enable counter that starts at 4 on synchronous reset and counts down each clock cycle while shift_ena is asserted. When the counter reaches zero, shift_ena is deasserted and held low indefinitely until the next reset. Instead of a separate FSM state, use the counter's value itself as the control: when counter > 0, shift_ena is high; when counter == 0, shift_ena is low. This eliminates explicit state management, reduces logic, and provides clear, precise timing for exactly 4 enable cycles upon reset.
