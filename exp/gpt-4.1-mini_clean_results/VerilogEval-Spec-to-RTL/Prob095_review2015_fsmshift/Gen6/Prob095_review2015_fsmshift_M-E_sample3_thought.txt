Implement a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. On synchronous reset, the FSM transitions to SHIFT_ENABLE state and asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles by counting down a 2-bit counter. After 4 cycles, it transitions to IDLE state where shift_ena remains low indefinitely until the next reset. This design cleanly separates state and counter logic, uses synchronous reset, and ensures shift_ena is asserted precisely 4 cycles starting immediately at reset deassertion (i.e., first active cycle after reset). The counter counts the remaining active cycles in SHIFT_ENABLE state, making timing exact and the FSM extensible for future pattern detection triggers.
