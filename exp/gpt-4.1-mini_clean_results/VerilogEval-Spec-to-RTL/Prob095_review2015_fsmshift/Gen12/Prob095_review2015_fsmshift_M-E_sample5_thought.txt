I will implement a simple shift enable pulse generator driven by a 3-bit down counter that counts exactly 4 cycles when triggered. Since the problem only requires shift_ena to be high for 4 cycles after reset (and then remain low), I can avoid a multi-state FSM and instead use a single counter with synchronous reset.

- On synchronous reset (active high), load the counter with the value 4.
- On each clock cycle when the counter is non-zero, decrement it.
- shift_ena is asserted when the counter is non-zero.
- After the counter reaches zero, shift_ena stays low indefinitely.
  
This approach guarantees shift_ena is high for exactly 4 cycles after reset, is simple and clean, and ensures no off-by-one errors because the counter directly controls the output. Using a counter also makes it easy to extend the design for future bit-pattern detection triggers if needed.

All logic is synchronous on the positive edge of clk, and reset is synchronous active-high.
