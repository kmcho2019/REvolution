Implement a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and ENABLE. On synchronous reset, the FSM enters ENABLE state with a 4-cycle counter, asserting shift_ena during ENABLE. After 4 cycles, it returns to IDLE with shift_ena deasserted. When the proper bit pattern is detected (modeled here as a one-bit input "pattern_detected"), the FSM re-enters ENABLE state and counts 4 cycles again. This approach cleanly separates state transitions and counters, ensuring shift_ena is asserted exactly 4 cycles after reset or pattern detection. Using a dedicated enable counter and FSM states simplifies control and extensibility.
