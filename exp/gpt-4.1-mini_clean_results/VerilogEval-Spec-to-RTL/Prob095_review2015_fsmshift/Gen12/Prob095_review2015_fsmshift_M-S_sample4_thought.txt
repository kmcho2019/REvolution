Simplify the FSM by removing explicit states and using a single down-counter that starts at 4 on synchronous reset. While the counter is non-zero, assert shift_ena; when it reaches zero, deassert shift_ena and keep it low forever until the next reset. The counter decrements every clock cycle when non-zero. This ensures exactly 4 clock cycles of shift_ena asserted after reset, then stays low indefinitely. This approach avoids extra next-state logic and makes the code minimal and clear.
