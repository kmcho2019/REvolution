To simplify, I'll implement a 3-bit down-counter initialized to 4 on synchronous reset. The output shift_ena is asserted whenever this counter is not zero. Each clock cycle, if the counter is non-zero, it decrements by 1. Once the counter reaches zero, shift_ena stays low forever until the next reset. This straightforward counting approach ensures shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles after reset without using an explicit FSM.
