Implement a single 3-bit down-counter initialized to 4 on synchronous reset. Assert shift_ena whenever the counter is non-zero, decrementing the counter on each clock cycle until it reaches zero. After reaching zero, shift_ena remains deasserted indefinitely until the next reset. This eliminates the FSM state register, reducing logic, as the counter alone manages the enable period. shift_ena directly depends on the counter value, ensuring exactly 4 cycles of assertion immediately following reset, synchronous to the clock.
