To fix the off-by-one error and meet the specification of asserting `shift_ena` for exactly 4 clock cycles after a synchronous reset, I will use a 3-bit counter that counts from 0 to 3 inclusive while `shift_ena` is asserted. On reset, `count` will be set to 0 and `shift_ena` asserted. On each clock cycle while `shift_ena` is high, `count` will increment. Once `count` reaches 3, `shift_ena` will be deasserted on the next cycle. This ensures `shift_ena` is high exactly for 4 cycles (count = 0,1,2,3). After deassertion, the module holds `shift_ena` low indefinitely until the next reset, as required. This synchronous counting approach accurately controls the enable duration without extra complexity.
