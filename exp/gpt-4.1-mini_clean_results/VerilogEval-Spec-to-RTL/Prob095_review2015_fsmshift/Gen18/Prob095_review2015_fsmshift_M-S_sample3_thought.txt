Since the module only has clk, reset, and shift_ena ports, we cannot rely on any external pattern_detect input. The requirement is to assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles synchronously after reset, and then keep it low indefinitely (since there's no other input to trigger it again). To simplify, use a 3-bit counter loaded to 4 on synchronous reset; decrement the counter on each clock while non-zero, assert shift_ena whenever the counter is non-zero. This fulfills the requirement with minimal logic.
