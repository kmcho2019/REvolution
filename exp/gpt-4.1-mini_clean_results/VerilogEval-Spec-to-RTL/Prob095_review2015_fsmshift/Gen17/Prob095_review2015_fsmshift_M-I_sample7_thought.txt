To correct the off-by-one error and optimize for PPA, use a 2-bit counter counting from 3 down to 0 for exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena assertion. On reset, initialize the counter to 3 and enable the shift register. Decrement the counter each clock cycle while shift_ena remains asserted. When the counter reaches 0, disable shift_ena permanently until next reset. This removes the explicit FSM state register, simplifying logic and reducing area. The counter only toggles during the enable phase, reducing switching activity (power). Synchronous reset remains active high. Positive-edge clocking is maintained.
