Use a simple 3-bit counter that counts down from 4 whenever the synchronous reset is asserted. On reset, load the counter with 4, then count down by 1 each clock cycle until it reaches zero. Assert shift_ena whenever the counter is non-zero, which lasts exactly 4 cycles after reset. After that, shift_ena stays low until next reset. No pattern detection or extra logic needed since the problem only requires triggering on reset.
