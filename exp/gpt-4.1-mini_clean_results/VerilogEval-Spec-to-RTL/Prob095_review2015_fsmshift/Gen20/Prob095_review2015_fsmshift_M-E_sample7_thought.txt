Implement a shift_ena pulse generator using a one-hot 4-bit shift register that is loaded with '1111' on reset and shifted right every clock cycle. shift_ena is asserted as long as any bit in the shift register is high, guaranteeing exactly 4 cycles of assertion after reset. Once all bits are shifted out (all zeros), shift_ena remains low indefinitely. This approach cleanly separates state encoding and output logic, avoids counters, and provides precise control over shift_ena timing.
