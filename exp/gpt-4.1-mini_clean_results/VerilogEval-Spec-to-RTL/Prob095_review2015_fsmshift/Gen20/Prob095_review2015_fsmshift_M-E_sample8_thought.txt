Implement a simple state machine with two states: ACTIVE and IDLE. 

- On synchronous reset, transition to the ACTIVE state, assert shift_ena, and initialize a 2-bit counter to 0.

- While in ACTIVE state, keep shift_ena asserted and increment the counter each clock cycle.

- When the counter reaches 3 (meaning 4 cycles from 0 to 3 inclusive), transition to the IDLE state.

- In the IDLE state, shift_ena is deasserted and remains low indefinitely until the next reset.

This architecture cleanly separates the enable duration and the idle period, and the 2-bit counter ensures exactly 4 cycles with shift_ena asserted after reset.
