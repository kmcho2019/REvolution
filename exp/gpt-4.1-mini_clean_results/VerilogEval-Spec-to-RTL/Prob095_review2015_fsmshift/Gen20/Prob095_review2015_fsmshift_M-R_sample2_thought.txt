Refactor the design by separating the counter and output logic. Keep the counter as a register counting down from 4 on reset. Instead of assigning shift_ena inside the sequential block, make shift_ena a combinational output directly driven by whether the counter is nonzero. This avoids potential off-by-one errors by cleanly decoupling the enable signal from the register update. The synchronous reset still initializes the counter to 4, and the counter decrements each cycle if nonzero, otherwise holds zero. This structure clarifies timing and ensures shift_ena is asserted exactly 4 cycles after reset and then zero forever.
