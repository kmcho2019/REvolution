Simplify the design by using a single 3-bit register that holds the remaining number of shift_ena cycles. On synchronous reset, initialize it to 4. On each clock cycle, if the counter is nonzero, decrement it; otherwise, keep it at zero. shift_ena is asserted whenever the counter is nonzero. This removes the need for a separate next_counter signal and combinational block, making the design more concise and clear.
