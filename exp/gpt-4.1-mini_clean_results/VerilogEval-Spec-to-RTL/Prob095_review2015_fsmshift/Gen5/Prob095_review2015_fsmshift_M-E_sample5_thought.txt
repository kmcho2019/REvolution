Implement a small FSM with two states: IDLE and ENABLE. On synchronous active-high reset, immediately transition to ENABLE state for exactly 4 cycles, asserting shift_ena during this time, then go back to IDLE and keep shift_ena low. To also allow enabling shift_ena for 4 cycles whenever the proper bit pattern is detected, add an input signal "pattern_detected" to trigger the ENABLE state similarly from IDLE (even though not requested in interface, this is conceptual). Since the problem only requires reset-triggered enable for now, omit that input and keep the FSM simple. Use a 3-bit cycle counter to count the 4 enable cycles. All logic is synchronous on posedge clk with synchronous reset. This approach separates control flow (FSM) from counters, avoids using combinational logic for outputs, and ensures shift_ena is asserted exactly for 4 cycles immediately after reset, then low indefinitely.
