Since shift_ena must be asserted for exactly 4 cycles after each synchronous reset and then remain low forever, we can simplify by using a 2-bit counter to track those 4 cycles.

- On synchronous reset, set the counter to 0.
- After reset is deasserted, increment the counter each clock cycle until it reaches 4.
- shift_ena is asserted when the counter is less than 4 (i.e., for the first 4 cycles after reset).
- Once the counter reaches 4, shift_ena stays low indefinitely.

This approach uses an up-counter instead of a down-counter and avoids explicit countdown logic, simplifying the implementation while preserving precise timing and synchronous reset behavior.
