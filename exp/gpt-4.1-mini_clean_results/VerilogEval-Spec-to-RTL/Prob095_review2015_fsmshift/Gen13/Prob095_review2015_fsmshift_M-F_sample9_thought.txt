Add an input port to receive the bit pattern detection signal (e.g., pattern_detected). When either the synchronous reset is asserted or the pattern_detected signal is asserted, load the counter with 4 to enable shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. The counter counts down on each clock cycle when non-zero, disabling shift_ena afterward until another reset or pattern detection occurs. This satisfies the requirement that shift_ena is asserted for 4 cycles after reset or upon detecting the proper bit pattern. The pattern_detected input should be a single-cycle pulse synchronized to clk.
