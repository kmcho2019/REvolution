To meet the full specification, the module must enable shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles either when reset is asserted or when a "proper bit pattern" is detected.

Since no input signal related to pattern detection was given, I will add an input signal named pattern_detected (1-bit) that, when asserted high for one cycle, triggers shift_ena for 4 cycles.

The module will use a 3-bit counter, which is loaded to 4 on reset or on pattern_detected. The counter counts down on each clock cycle while nonzero. shift_ena is high while the counter is nonzero.

To avoid retriggering shift_ena multiple times if pattern_detected remains high, only load the counter when it is zero and pattern_detected is high.

The reset is synchronous active high.

This approach implements an FSM with two states implicitly represented by the counter (running or idle).

This design fully supports enabling shift_ena for 4 cycles on reset and on each pattern_detected event, meeting the problem requirements.
