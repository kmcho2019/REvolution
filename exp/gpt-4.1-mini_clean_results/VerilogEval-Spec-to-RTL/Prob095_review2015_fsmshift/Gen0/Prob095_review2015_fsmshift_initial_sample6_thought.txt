The module will implement a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. Upon reset (synchronous active high), the FSM enters the SHIFT state and enables shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles using a counter. After 4 cycles, shift_ena is deasserted, and the FSM moves to the IDLE state, where shift_ena remains 0 forever until the next reset. The counter increments on each clock cycle while in SHIFT state and resets when transitioning back to IDLE or on reset.
