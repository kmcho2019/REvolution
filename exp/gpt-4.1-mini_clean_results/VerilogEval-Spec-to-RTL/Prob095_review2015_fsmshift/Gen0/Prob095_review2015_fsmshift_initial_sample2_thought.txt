Implement a simple FSM with a 3-bit shift register to detect a specific pattern (assumed as all ones "111" here for demonstration). On reset, assert shift_ena for 4 cycles using a counter, then de-assert it. When the pattern is detected, assert shift_ena for 4 cycles using the same counter mechanism. The shift_ena signal is asserted exactly 4 clock cycles and remains low otherwise. Reset is synchronous and active high.
