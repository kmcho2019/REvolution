The original problem specifies a module with only clk, reset, and shift_ena ports and requires shift_ena to be asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles after a synchronous reset and then remain low forever (until another reset).

The previously suggested solution introduced an additional input (pattern_detected) which is not part of the interface, causing simulation issues and floating inputs.

The requirement simplifies to a one-shot pulse of length 4 cycles of shift_ena after each synchronous reset.

To fix the design:

- Remove any inputs not specified (no pattern_detected).
- On synchronous reset, assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles.
- Use a 3-bit counter initialized to 4 on reset to count down each clock.
- Assert shift_ena while the counter is not zero.
- After counting down 4 cycles, shift_ena remains low indefinitely until next reset.
- Implement all logic synchronous to clk with synchronous active-high reset.

This results in a simple counter-based design without an FSM or extra inputs, fully matching the specified interface and behavior.
