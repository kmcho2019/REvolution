I will implement a simple 3-bit down-counter driven by a synchronous reset. The counter will be loaded with the value 4 when reset is asserted (synchronously). When the counter is non-zero, shift_ena will be asserted. The counter decrements on every clock cycle while enabled, effectively enabling shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles after reset.

No FSM is necessary since the functionality is straightforward. After the 4 cycles, shift_ena is deasserted and remains low until the next reset.

This design strictly follows the problem interface with no additional inputs and only synchronous reset and clock. It ensures deterministic behavior and clean timing of the 4-cycle enable period on reset.
