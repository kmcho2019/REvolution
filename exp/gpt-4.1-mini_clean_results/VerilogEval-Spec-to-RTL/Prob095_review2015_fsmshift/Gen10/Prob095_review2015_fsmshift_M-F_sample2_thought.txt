The main issue was that shift_ena was deasserted when the counter reached 0, causing only 3 asserted cycles instead of 4. To fix this, we keep the counter initialization at 3, but change the output condition to assert shift_ena whenever the state is ENABLE regardless of counter value. The counter will count down from 3 to 0 inclusive, and shift_ena will be asserted for all four of these cycles. After the counter reaches 0, the FSM transitions to IDLE, and shift_ena deasserts. This matches the requirement of enabling shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles immediately after reset, with synchronous reset handling intact.
