Since the problem only defines inputs clk and reset (active-high synchronous) and output shift_ena, and does not specify how the bit pattern is detected, the module can only implement the reset behavior: on reset assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles, then de-assert forever until the next reset. 

No additional inputs or pattern detection logic are added. The design uses a simple 3-bit counter loaded to 4 on reset, counts down every clock cycle while nonzero, and shift_ena is asserted while counter is nonzero.

This satisfies the problem requirements with minimal complexity and no extra inputs.
