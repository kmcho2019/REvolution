Since reset is synchronous and active high, and shift_ena must be asserted for exactly 4 cycles starting immediately when reset is asserted, we can use a simple 3-bit counter that counts down from 4 whenever reset is active or just deasserted. On reset, we initialize the counter to 4, and shift_ena is high as long as the counter is non-zero. The counter decrements each clock cycle after reset goes low until it reaches zero, after which shift_ena stays low indefinitely until the next reset. This approach avoids extra flags and directly implements the required timing with minimal logic.
