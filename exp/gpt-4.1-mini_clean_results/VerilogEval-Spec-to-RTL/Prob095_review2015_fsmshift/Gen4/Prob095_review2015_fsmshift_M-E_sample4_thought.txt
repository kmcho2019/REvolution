Implement a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and ENABLE. On synchronous reset, transition to ENABLE state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles using a 3-bit cycle counter. The counter increments each clock cycle while in ENABLE state. After 4 cycles, transition to IDLE state, deassert shift_ena, and remain there indefinitely until next reset. This approach cleanly separates control logic and timing without relying on countdown counters and matches the exact interface with no extra inputs.
