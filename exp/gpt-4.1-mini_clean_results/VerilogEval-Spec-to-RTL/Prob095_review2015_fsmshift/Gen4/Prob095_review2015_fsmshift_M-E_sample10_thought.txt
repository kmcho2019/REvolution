Use a simple enable counter without explicit FSM states. On synchronous active-high reset, start a 4-cycle enable period by setting a 3-bit counter to 4 and asserting shift_ena. On every clock cycle, if the counter is nonzero, keep shift_ena asserted and decrement the counter by 1 until it reaches zero, then deassert shift_ena permanently until another reset occurs. This approach avoids extra inputs or states and meets the requirement: after reset, enable shift_ena exactly 4 cycles, then stay low forever. The entire logic is synchronous with active-high reset, and the output depends solely on the counter value.
