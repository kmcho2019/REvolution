To ensure shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 cycles after synchronous reset and then stays low indefinitely, we should use a 3-bit counter that increments every clock cycle after reset. On reset, the counter is initialized to 0 and shift_ena is asserted. As long as the counter is less than 4, shift_ena remains high. After the counter reaches 4, shift_ena goes low and the counter stops incrementing to avoid overflow. This avoids any off-by-one errors or stuck states and matches the exact 4-cycle enable window. This design uses minimal state and has clear, stable timing behavior.
