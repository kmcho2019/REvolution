To fix the timing and counting issues, we should count from 0 up to 4 exclusively, incrementing the count every cycle after reset until it reaches 4, then stop counting and keep shift_ena low permanently. We can assert shift_ena whenever count is less than 4. On synchronous reset, reset the counter to 0 to start the 4-cycle enable period again. This way, count will saturate at 4 and shift_ena will be asserted exactly for 4 cycles, then deasserted forever until next reset. This avoids freezing the count early or ambiguous timing.
