Since the module only needs to assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles after reset (and then remain low forever), we can simplify the FSM by using just a 3-bit counter without an explicit state variable. On synchronous reset, we start counting from 0 and assert shift_ena while the counter is less than 4. After counting 4 cycles, shift_ena is deasserted and stays low until next reset. This removes the need for state encoding and simplifies the logic.
