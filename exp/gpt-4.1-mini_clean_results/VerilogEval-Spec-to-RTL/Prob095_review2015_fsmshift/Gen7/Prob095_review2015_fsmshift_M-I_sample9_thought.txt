To meet the requirements precisely, I will implement a 3-bit counter to count the 4 cycles of shift_ena assertion, starting synchronously with the active-high reset signal. Upon synchronous reset, the counter is initialized to 4, representing the number of cycles shift_ena should stay asserted (including the reset cycle). At each clock cycle after reset, the counter decrements until zero, during which shift_ena remains asserted. Once the counter reaches zero, shift_ena deasserts and stays low until the next reset.

This approach ensures:
- shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 cycles starting immediately on the reset clock cycle.
- shift_ena remains low indefinitely until the next reset.
- Clear and simple counter logic with synchronous reset.
