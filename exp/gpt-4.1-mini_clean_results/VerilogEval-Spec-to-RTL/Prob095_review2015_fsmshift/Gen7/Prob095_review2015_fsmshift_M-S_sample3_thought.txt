Use a 2-bit counter to count from 0 to 3, enabling shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles after a synchronous active-high reset. On reset, initialize the counter to 0. While the counter is less than 4, assert shift_ena and increment the counter each clock cycle. When the counter reaches 4, deassert shift_ena and hold it low forever until the next reset. This simplifies the design by counting up instead of down and uses minimal bits.
