The problem interface has only clk, reset, and shift_ena signals—no pattern input is provided. The specification requires shift_ena to be asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles immediately after a synchronous active-high reset, then stay low indefinitely until the next reset. No other trigger restarts the enable.

To implement this, use a 3-bit counter that loads 4 at the positive clock edge when reset is asserted. On subsequent clock cycles, if the counter is nonzero, decrement it by 1; otherwise, keep it at zero. The shift_ena output is asserted whenever the counter is nonzero.

This approach meets the specification exactly and avoids extraneous inputs or complexity. It also ensures minimal toggling by counting down only when needed. The synchronous reset is respected per spec.
