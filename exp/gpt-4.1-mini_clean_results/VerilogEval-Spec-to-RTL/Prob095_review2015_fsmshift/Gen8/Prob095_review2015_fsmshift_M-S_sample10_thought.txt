Since the problem only provides reset, clk, and shift_ena signals with no input for pattern detection, I will focus on the reset-triggered 4-cycle assertion of shift_ena as specified. On synchronous active-high reset, shift_ena will assert for exactly 4 clock cycles starting immediately, then remain low indefinitely. Using a simple 3-bit counter initialized to 4 on reset and counting down on each clock cycle ensures shift_ena is high for those 4 cycles, then low afterward. This approach minimizes complexity while fulfilling the stated requirements.
