Refactor the design into a simple two-state FSM with states IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. On synchronous reset, transition to SHIFT_ENABLE state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 cycles using a 3-bit counter. When counter expires, move to IDLE and deassert shift_ena indefinitely until next reset. Use a state register and a counter register updated on each clock edge. The output shift_ena is driven combinationally from the state. This approach cleanly separates control logic (FSM) and counting, improving clarity and easier future extensions.
