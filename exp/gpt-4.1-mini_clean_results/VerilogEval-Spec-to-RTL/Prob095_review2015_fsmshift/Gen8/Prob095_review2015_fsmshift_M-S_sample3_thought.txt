Use a simple 3-bit counter that starts at 4 when reset is asserted and counts down each clock cycle while shift_ena is high. When the counter reaches zero, deassert shift_ena permanently until next reset. Since reset is synchronous active-high, on the clock edge when reset is high, initialize the counter to 4 and assert shift_ena. On subsequent clock edges with reset low, decrement the counter if non-zero and keep shift_ena asserted; when counter is zero, deassert shift_ena. This approach avoids an explicit FSM and uses only a counter, simplifying the logic.
