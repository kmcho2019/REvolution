Refactor the design into a small finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and ENABLE. On synchronous reset, transition to ENABLE state and start a 4-cycle counter to keep shift_ena high. After counting 4 cycles, return to IDLE and keep shift_ena low indefinitely until the next reset. This FSM-based approach cleanly separates states, makes the control explicit, and uses a counter for the enable duration.
