I will implement a shift_ena pulse generator using a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and ENABLE.

- On synchronous reset, the FSM transitions to ENABLE state with a 2-bit counter loaded with 4.
- In ENABLE state, shift_ena is asserted, and the counter counts down each clock cycle.
- When the counter reaches zero, the FSM transitions back to IDLE and shift_ena deasserts.
- In IDLE, shift_ena remains 0 until next reset.
- This FSM approach cleanly separates control logic, ensuring shift_ena is asserted immediately after synchronous reset for exactly 4 cycles.
- All outputs and state transitions happen synchronously on the clock rising edge.

This design provides clear FSM semantics and straightforward timing for the 4-cycle enable pulse.
