To ensure shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles immediately after a synchronous active-high reset, we should start counting from 3 down to 0 instead of from 4. This way, shift_ena will be high during counts 3, 2, 1, and 0, totaling four cycles. After that, shift_ena stays low indefinitely until the next reset. The count register should be 2 bits (since maximum count is 3). This also aligns shift_ena assertion timing clearly with the clock edges, avoiding any off-by-one issues. The synchronous reset logic remains the same.
