Implement a two-state FSM: IDLE and SHIFT. On synchronous reset, go to SHIFT state with a 4-cycle counter. In IDLE, monitor for the "proper bit pattern" input to trigger a transition to SHIFT and re-enable shift_ena for 4 cycles. To incorporate the pattern detection, add an input bit pattern_detected. While in SHIFT state, keep shift_ena asserted and count down from 4 to 0; when counter reaches 0, return to IDLE. This approach cleanly separates the enabling logic into a clear FSM with the synchronous reset starting the enable cycle immediately.
