Implement a state machine with two states: ENABLE and DISABLE. On synchronous reset, transition to ENABLE and assert shift_ena. In ENABLE state, count four clock cycles using a 2-bit counter. After completing four cycles, transition to DISABLE and deassert shift_ena indefinitely until the next reset. This makes the shift_ena timing explicit and clear, avoiding off-by-one errors by controlling the enable duration within a dedicated state and counter.
