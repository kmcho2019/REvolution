To fix the off-by-one timing issue and simplify the design, I will implement a single always block FSM with a 3-bit counter that counts from 0 up to 3 (4 cycles total). On synchronous reset, the FSM enters an ENABLE state and asserts shift_ena. The counter increments every clock cycle while in ENABLE state. When the counter reaches 3, after that cycle shift_ena deasserts and the FSM transitions to IDLE where shift_ena stays low indefinitely until reset. The counter resets to zero upon entering ENABLE state. This counting up method aligns better with natural cycle counting and makes the timing precise. The output shift_ena is assigned combinationally from the FSM state. This approach removes the need for two always blocks and leverages a straightforward counter increment logic ensuring exactly 4 cycles of enable on reset.
