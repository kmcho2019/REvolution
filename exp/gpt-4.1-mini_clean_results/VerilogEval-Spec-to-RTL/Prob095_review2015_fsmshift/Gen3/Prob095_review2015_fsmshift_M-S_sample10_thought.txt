Simplify the FSM by removing unnecessary states and counters, using a single 3-bit shift counter that counts down from 4 to 0. On synchronous reset, initialize this counter to 4, which asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles as long as the counter is non-zero. Once the counter reaches zero, shift_ena remains deasserted until the next reset. This approach eliminates the need for multiple states and complex next state logic while maintaining the exact enable duration and synchronous reset behavior.
