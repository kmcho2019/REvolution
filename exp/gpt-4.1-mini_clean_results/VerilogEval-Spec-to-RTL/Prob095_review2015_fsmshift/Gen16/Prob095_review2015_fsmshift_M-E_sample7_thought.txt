Use a 3-bit up-counter that counts from 0 to 3 to track the number of asserted cycles for shift_ena after reset. On synchronous reset, load the counter with zero and set shift_ena high. On each clock cycle, if the counter is less than 3, increment it and keep shift_ena asserted. Once the counter reaches 3, deassert shift_ena and freeze the counter until next reset. This approach clearly counts 4 cycles (0,1,2,3) with shift_ena high, ensuring exactly 4 asserted cycles after reset. This differs from the prior countdown approach by counting up, improving clarity and correctness.
