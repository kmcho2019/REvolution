To fix the off-by-one error, initialize the counter to 3 (not 4) so counting down to zero enables exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena. The counter counts the remaining enable cycles, decrementing each clock while in ENABLE state. Shift_ena is asserted whenever the counter is non-zero, ensuring it is high for precisely 4 clock cycles after reset. The FSM stays in ENABLE as long as counter is non-zero, then moves to IDLE and keeps shift_ena low indefinitely until the next reset. This corrects the duration and timing of shift_ena assertion without changing the FSM structure.
