Use a single 3-bit counter that loads 4 on reset and counts down every clock cycle when nonzero. Assert shift_ena whenever the counter is nonzero, ensuring exactly 4 cycles of enable after reset. Implement synchronous reset only in the sequential logic without duplicated combinational reset checks. Remove explicit FSM states, simplifying to a counter-based enable generator. This design cleanly guarantees 4 cycles of shift_ena asserted immediately after reset and 0 thereafter, aligning perfectly with the requirements.
