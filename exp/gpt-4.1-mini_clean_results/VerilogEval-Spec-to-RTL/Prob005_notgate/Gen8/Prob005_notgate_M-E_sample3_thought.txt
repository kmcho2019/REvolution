Implement the NOT gate by using an assign statement directly in TopModule without submodule instantiation for minimal area and power. To make the design novel, instead of a direct inversion, use a small lookup table implemented as a combinational always block with a case statement. This showcases a different architectural style—functional modeling with a small combinational LUT—which is easily extensible to multi-bit inputs or more complex logic. The output is declared as reg to support the procedural assignment, and the input is decoded to produce the inversion. This method is less common for a simple inverter, demonstrating a novel approach.
