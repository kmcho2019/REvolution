The original design is already the most optimal for a single NOT gate in terms of area, power, and timing since it's purely combinational and uses a direct bitwise negation. No additional logic or structural changes can reduce delay, power, or area meaningfully at the RTL level. However, for clarity and to align explicitly with gate-level representation, we can implement the NOT gate using a built-in Verilog primitive 'not' gate, which directly represents an inverter. This does not affect PPA but may improve readability or synthesis clarity on some tools. Otherwise, the design is already optimal.
