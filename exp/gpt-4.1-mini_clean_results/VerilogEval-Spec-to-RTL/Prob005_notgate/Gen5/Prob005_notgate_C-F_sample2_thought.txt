Fuse the explicit declaration of ports as wire types from Example 1 with the succinct continuous assignment style of Example 2. This fusion ensures clarity and explicitness for both human readers and synthesis tools, while maintaining minimal and optimal RTL for a simple NOT gate. By declaring input and output as wire, we reinforce the combinational nature and avoid any unintended inference of registers, even though Verilog defaults output to wire. The continuous assignment remains the best method for such simple combinational logic, providing the minimal area, power, and delay. This approach yields a clean, stylistically explicit, and synthesis-friendly implementation.
