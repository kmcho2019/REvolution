Fuse the minimal power and area overhead of the direct continuous assignment approach in TopModule with the clarity and modularity of a small, lightweight inverter submodule implemented purely with continuous assignments (no procedural blocks or reg outputs). This keeps the design modular and extensible while avoiding unnecessary procedural overhead, function abstraction, and extra logic layers. The inverter module is simple, synthesizes efficiently, and the top-level cleanly instantiates it, preserving a clear separation of concerns. This fusion balances modularity, clarity, and optimal PPA metrics by using simple wire-driven logic without extra overhead, producing a clear and reusable design that can be easily expanded if needed.
