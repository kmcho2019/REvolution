To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA) while maintaining functionality, the inverter logic can be integrated directly into the TopModule with a continuous assignment. This removes the module instantiation overhead of the separate Inverter module, reducing area and slightly lowering power by eliminating one level of hierarchy and associated wiring capacitance. The continuous assignment ensures minimal delay and clean synthesis into a single NOT gate. This approach maximizes simplicity and synthesis efficiency without sacrificing clarity or extensibility, given the trivial nature of the operation.
