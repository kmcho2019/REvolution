Fuse the clarity and modularity of having a dedicated Inverter module with the efficiency of continuous assignment, but further optimize by flattening the design—remove the separate Inverter module and implement the inverter directly inside TopModule using a continuous assignment. This fusion keeps modular code very simple, eliminates overhead from extra instantiation and wiring, and ensures minimal power, area, and delay. The approach maintains readability and straightforward synthesis flow, while yielding the best PPA by avoiding unnecessary hierarchy for such a trivial combinational function.
