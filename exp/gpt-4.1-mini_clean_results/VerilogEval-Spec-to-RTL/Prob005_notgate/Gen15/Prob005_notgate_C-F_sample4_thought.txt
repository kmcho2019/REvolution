Fuse the clarity and modularity of the first example's submodule approach with the simplicity and power/area efficiency of continuous assignment from the second example by defining the Inverter submodule with a continuous assign statement instead of a procedural block. This preserves modular design and clear separation of concerns, while eliminating the overhead of procedural logic inside the inverter. The TopModule instantiates the Inverter submodule, keeping hierarchy and reuse potential, but the inverter itself is implemented as a minimal continuous combinational assignment for optimal power, performance, and area. This fusion strikes a balance between modularity and PPA efficiency without compromising readability or future extensibility.
