Implement the NOT gate by using a behavioral approach with a ternary conditional operator inside a combinational always block in the TopModule itself, eliminating the need for any submodules. This integrates the inversion directly into TopModule, reducing module overhead and simplifying the design. The output is declared as reg to allow procedural assignment inside always @(*) block, maintaining clear and simple behavioral style. This approach offers a clean balance between synthesizability, readability, and minimal overhead, while providing a fresh perspective differing from continuous assignment or separate submodules.
