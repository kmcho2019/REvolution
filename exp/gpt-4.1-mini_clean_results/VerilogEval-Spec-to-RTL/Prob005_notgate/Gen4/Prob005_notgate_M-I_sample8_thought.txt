The original implementation is already minimal and uses a continuous assignment which is optimal for synthesis. The output is a wire by default and the assign statement is clear and portable. Since it is a simple NOT gate, further optimization in RTL is unlikely to reduce area or power. However, for explicit clarity and potentially allowing synthesis tools better inference for simple combinational logic, we can explicitly declare the output as a wire type (although by default it is wire). This might help with clarity for other developers and tools. We keep the assign statement and avoid any unnecessary constructs. The improvement is mainly stylistic with no RTL impact on PPA.
