Fuse the modular clarity and future extensibility of having a dedicated Inverter submodule with the efficiency of direct continuous assignment, while minimizing hierarchy overhead by using a generate block inside TopModule to instantiate the Inverter only when needed. This approach preserves modularity and readability, ensures minimal logic with no procedural blocks, and allows easy scalability. It slightly optimizes synthesis by conditionally instantiating the inverter, reducing unnecessary hierarchy when not needed, and keeps power and area minimal through purely combinational logic.
