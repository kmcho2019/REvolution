Refactor the shift register by separating the next-state logic from the register update. Use a combinational always block to calculate the next value of q based on load, ena, and current q, and then update q in the sequential always block triggered by clk and areset. This makes the data flow more explicit and modular, and can be useful for clearer state derivation or extension. The asynchronous reset remains in the sequential block, preserving the original behavior.
