To optimize for better power and performance, I will introduce an internal enable signal for the register update. Instead of always assigning q on every clock when ena is asserted, the register will update only if load or ena is active. This reduces unnecessary toggling of q when neither load nor ena is asserted, saving power. The asynchronous reset remains as is to preserve immediate reset behavior. This approach does not change functionality but helps synthesis tools map enables directly to flip-flop enable pins, improving power and potentially performance without increasing area.
