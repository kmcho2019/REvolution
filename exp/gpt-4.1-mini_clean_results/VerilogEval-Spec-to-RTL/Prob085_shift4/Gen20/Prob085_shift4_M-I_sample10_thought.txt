To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will separate the asynchronous reset from the synchronous logic by using two always blocks: one asynchronous reset block that resets q immediately on areset, and a separate clocked always block for load and shift operations. This separation can help synthesis tools better optimize reset logic and potentially reduce critical path delay. Also, to slightly improve power, the synchronous block will only update q when load or ena is asserted, preventing unnecessary switching. This conditional update reduces dynamic power by avoiding redundant assignments to q when neither load nor ena are active. This approach maintains the required behavior, preserves load priority, and may yield better timing and power characteristics without increasing area.
