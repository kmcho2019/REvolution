Implement the 4-bit shift register using separate asynchronous reset and synchronous clock enable signals with a combinational priority multiplexer before the register update. The asynchronous reset clears the register immediately. On the clock edge, a synchronous multiplexer selects between loading data, shifting right, or holding the current value, according to the priority: load > ena > hold. This decouples the shift and load logic from the register assignment, potentially improving clarity by separating data path selection from the register update.
