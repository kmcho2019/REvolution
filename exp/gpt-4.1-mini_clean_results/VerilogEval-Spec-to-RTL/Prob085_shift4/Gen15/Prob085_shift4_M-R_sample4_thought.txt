Refactor the design by using a continuous assignment to compute the next state of the shift register combinationally, and then update the register in a single always block triggered by clock and asynchronous reset. The continuous assignment cleanly expresses the priority logic (load > ena > hold) without an always block. This also makes the design more concise while maintaining functional correctness and the asynchronous reset. The asynchronous reset keeps highest priority, and the load has precedence over enable, which shifts the register right with zero fill.
