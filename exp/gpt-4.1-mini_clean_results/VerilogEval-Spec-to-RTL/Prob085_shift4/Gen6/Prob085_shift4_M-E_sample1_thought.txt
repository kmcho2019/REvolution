Implement the shift register with an intermediate next-state register that computes the updated value combinationally based on load and ena signals, then registers it on the clock edge with asynchronous reset. Use a single always block with asynchronous reset and synchronous load/shift priority handled by if-else statements. The shift operation is done by concatenating zero with the upper three bits of the current register value, ensuring a right shift with zero fill at MSB. This approach cleanly separates combinational next value generation from sequential update, improving clarity and potentially aiding synthesis tools in optimization.
