Implement the 4-bit right shift register using a temporary next-state signal to clearly separate combinational logic for next state generation from the sequential register update. The asynchronous reset will clear the register immediately on the reset signal's positive edge. The synchronous logic on the clock's positive edge will load data if 'load' is high; else if 'ena' is high, perform the right shift by moving q[3:1] down to q[2:0] and zero-filling q[3]. This approach uses an explicit combinational assignment for next state, improving readability and testability.
