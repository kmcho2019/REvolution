Use an internal wire to determine the next state of the shift register before the clock edge. Implement an asynchronous reset triggered on the positive edge of areset to clear the register. On the positive clock edge, synchronously update the shift register with either the load data (if load is high) or the shifted version of the current state (if ena is high and load is low). If neither load nor ena is asserted, hold the current value. This approach uses combinational logic outside the always block to clearly separate next-state logic from state updates.
