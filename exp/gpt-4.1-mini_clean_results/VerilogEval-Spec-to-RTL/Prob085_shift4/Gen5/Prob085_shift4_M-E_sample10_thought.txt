Implement the 4-bit shift register with an asynchronous reset using two always blocks: one sensitive to the asynchronous reset and the other to the clock edge. On the asynchronous reset, clear the register to zero immediately. On the positive clock edge, if load is asserted, synchronously load data; else if ena is asserted, shift right by inserting zero at MSB and shifting the register right. Load has higher priority over ena by using nested if-else conditions.
