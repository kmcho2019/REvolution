The original design is already clean and efficient, with asynchronous reset and priority load over enable shifting. To improve PPA:

1. **Performance:** Replace the conditional branches with a single priority-encoded mux to reduce mux tree complexity and potentially improve timing.
2. **Power:** Introduce a clock enable signal derived from load or ena to gate register updates, preventing unnecessary toggling on clock edges where no change occurs.
3. **Area:** The area is minimal; no major change needed.

This approach keeps behavior identical but may reduce critical path and switching activity, especially when both load and ena are low, improving timing and dynamic power without extra area overhead.
