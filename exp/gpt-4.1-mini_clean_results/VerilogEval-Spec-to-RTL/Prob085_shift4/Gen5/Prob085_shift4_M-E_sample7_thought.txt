Implement the 4-bit shift register with asynchronous reset using a two-process approach: one always block for asynchronous reset and clocked operations, and another always block for the combinational next state logic. Introduce a separate register to hold the next state (next_q). The next state logic prioritizes load over enable, otherwise shifts right with zero fill. This separation improves readability and modularity by clearly defining next state computation and state update distinctly.
