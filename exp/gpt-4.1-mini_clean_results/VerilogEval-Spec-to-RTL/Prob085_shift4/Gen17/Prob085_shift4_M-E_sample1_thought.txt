Implement the 4-bit shift register as four individual flip-flops each with explicit next-state logic. Use an asynchronous reset on all flip-flops. On the synchronous clock edge, if load is asserted, load the corresponding data bit; else if enable is asserted, shift right by connecting each flip-flop's input to the previous flip-flop's output, with the MSB shifted in as zero. This one-flip-flop-per-bit architecture clarifies the shift operation and prioritization of load over enable, and makes the design modular for easier potential extensions.
