Implement the 4-bit shift register using a flip-flop array with a small combinational multiplexer for next state selection. Use a single synchronous process triggered on the positive edge of the clock with asynchronous reset handled separately in an always block. To enforce priority, use a combinational always block to select the next input based on load and ena signals. The asynchronous reset clears the flip-flops immediately on areset. This separation of reset logic and next state logic improves modularity and clarity while preserving functionality.
