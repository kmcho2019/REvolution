I will implement the 4-bit right shift register using two separate always blocks to demonstrate a modular approach: one asynchronous reset block that clears the register, and another synchronous clocked block that handles loading and shifting based on control signals.

The asynchronous reset block will be a separate always block triggered on the positive edge of areset, clearing the register instantly.

The synchronous clocked always block will trigger on the positive edge of clk, and only update q if not in reset. Within this block, priority is given first to load (loading the input data), else if ena is asserted, perform a right shift (shift right with MSB zero), else maintain the current value.

This architectural separation enhances clarity by isolating asynchronous reset behavior from synchronous operation. It also allows better simulation and synthesis tool interpretations of asynchronous and synchronous domains.
