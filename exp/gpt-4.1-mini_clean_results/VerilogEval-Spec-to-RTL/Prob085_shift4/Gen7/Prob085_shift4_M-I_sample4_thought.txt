To optimize the design for better power and timing (PPA), I will add an explicit clock enable to the register to avoid unnecessary toggling of q when neither load nor ena is asserted, reducing dynamic power. This is done by gating the register update logic with (load | ena). Also, I will keep the asynchronous reset as per spec. This selective updating should not affect functionality but helps reduce switching activity, potentially improving power. The priority encoding is preserved with load having higher priority over ena. The shift operation remains the same for area and performance efficiency.
