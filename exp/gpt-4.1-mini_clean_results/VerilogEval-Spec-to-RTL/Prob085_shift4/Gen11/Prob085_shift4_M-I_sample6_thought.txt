The original design is already clean and functionally correct with a straightforward structure. To optimize for better PPA (timing, power, area), the main strategy is to simplify the combinational logic and reduce signal fan-in slightly. We can remove the intermediate next_q register and directly update the output register inside the sequential always block using if-else priority. This eliminates the combinational always block and may reduce logic depth and power slightly by avoiding an extra mux stage, while maintaining asynchronous reset and synchronous load/enable semantics. This direct style is common for simple small state machines or registers and often results in better synthesis optimization for timing and power. The logic priority load > enable > hold is preserved exactly. Overall, this reduces logic levels and registers, marginally improving timing and power without increasing area.
