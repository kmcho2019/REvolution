Implement the shift register with a single always block sensitive to clk and asynchronous positive-edge areset, where the priority is directly encoded using nested if-else statements. Use a temporary register to hold the shifted value, and then select the next value based on load and ena signals. This straightforward priority encoding avoids a combinational case statement, potentially simplifying synthesis. The shift is performed by concatenating a leading zero with the upper three bits of the register, shifting right logically. This approach keeps the logic simple and clear with minimal control signals.
