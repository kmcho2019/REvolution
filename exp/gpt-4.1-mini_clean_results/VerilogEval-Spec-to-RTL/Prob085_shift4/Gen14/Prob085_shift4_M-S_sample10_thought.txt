I will simplify the design by directly computing the next state inside the sequential always block without a separate combinational next_q variable. The asynchronous reset remains as specified, and the synchronous load and enable logic is implemented with if-else priority (load > ena > hold). This removes the combinational always block and the intermediate register for next_q, reducing complexity and resource usage while preserving all functionality and correct priority.
