To optimize the original design for better power and area while maintaining functionality, I will add a clock enable gating by only updating the register when load or ena is high (or during reset). This avoids unnecessary register toggling when both load and ena are low, reducing dynamic power. The asynchronous reset remains unchanged. The logic priority (areset > load > ena) is preserved, and the shift operation is unchanged. This gating can improve power without affecting timing significantly for this simple design.
