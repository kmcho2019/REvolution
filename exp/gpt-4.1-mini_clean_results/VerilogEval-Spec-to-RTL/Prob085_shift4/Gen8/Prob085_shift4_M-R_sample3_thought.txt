I will refactor the design by separating the next state logic from the state register update, making the design more modular and clearer. Instead of embedding all logic inside a single always block sensitive to clk and areset, I will create a combinational always block that computes the next state of the register based on the inputs (load, ena, data, and current q), and then update the register with this next state on the positive edge of clk or asynchronous areset. This separation clarifies the control flow and can ease formal verification or modifications. The priority of load over ena is preserved. The asynchronous reset remains the same.
