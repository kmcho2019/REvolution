Refactor the design by directly assigning the next state logic via a continuous assign statement to a wire instead of a combinational always block. The register update remains in the sequential always block with asynchronous reset. This approach separates combinational logic using assign statements, which can be simpler and more concise for small logic, while maintaining the load priority over enable. This also clarifies the distinction between combinational and sequential parts and may slightly reduce inferred mux complexity in some synthesis tools. Comments are added for clarity.
