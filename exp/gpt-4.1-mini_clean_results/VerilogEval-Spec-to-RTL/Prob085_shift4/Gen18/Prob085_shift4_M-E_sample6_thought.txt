Implement the shift register using a single always block that handles all control signals inside the clocked process, including asynchronous reset. The key difference is to perform all operations sequentially in the clocked process rather than using separate combinational logic. On the asynchronous reset, clear the register to zero immediately. Otherwise, at each rising clock edge, if load is high, load the data; else if ena is high, shift right by one inserting zero at MSB; otherwise hold the current value. This approach reduces combinational logic and potential glitches by directly updating the register state within the clocked process, making the design straightforward and compact.
