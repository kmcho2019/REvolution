To optimize the shift register for better performance and power while maintaining area, I will merge the combinational control logic into a single if-else structure inside the sequential block to minimize mux complexity. Using the right shift operator `>>` can simplify the hardware. Also, I will re-express the priority logic clearly with a case-like structure to help synthesis tools optimize the multiplexers.

To reduce unnecessary switching, I will ensure that when neither load nor enable is asserted, the register holds its value without toggling, which is inherently done by not updating `q`.

No clock gating is added as it can complicate design and is often handled by synthesis or higher-level blocks.

This approach should slightly improve timing by reducing mux chain complexity and lower dynamic power by avoiding redundant toggling. The area will remain minimal due to the simple logic.

The asynchronous reset remains positive-edge triggered as requested.
