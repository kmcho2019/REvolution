To optimize the original design for power without sacrificing performance or area, we can add a clock enable (write enable) signal to the register. The register only updates when load or ena is high; otherwise, it holds its value. This reduces unnecessary toggling of flip-flops, saving dynamic power. The asynchronous reset remains unchanged for fast reset response. The logic remains simple and area overhead is minimal. Thus, we insert a write enable signal derived from load or ena, and condition updates on it inside the sequential block.
