Use a two-process approach: implement the asynchronous reset and synchronous load/shift logic separately to make the design more modular. On the asynchronous reset (posedge areset), clear an internal register immediately. On the clock's positive edge, if load is asserted, load the data into the register; else if enable is asserted, shift right with 0 in MSB. Otherwise, maintain the value. Use an internal register to hold the state and assign q as output. This separate reset process with explicit sensitivity to areset makes the asynchronous behavior clear and the synchronous logic concise.
