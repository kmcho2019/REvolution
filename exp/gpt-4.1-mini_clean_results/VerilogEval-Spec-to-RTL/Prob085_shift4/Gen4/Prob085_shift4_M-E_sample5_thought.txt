Implement the shift register using a single always block triggered on the posedge of clk and the posedge of the asynchronous reset, eliminating the need for an explicit next state signal. Within the block, first handle the asynchronous reset to clear the register. Then apply synchronous logic with a priority: if load is high, directly load the input data; else if enable is high, perform the right shift by concatenating zero at MSB and shifting the lower bits; else retain the current value. This approach is minimalistic and avoids the combinational next-state logic, potentially simplifying the synthesis process and reducing the signal toggling caused by separate next state calculation.
