Implement the 4-bit right shift register using a 4-stage shift register constructed from four flip-flops arranged in a chain. Use an asynchronous reset on the first flip-flop and propagate reset through the chain. On each positive clock edge (and no reset), if load is asserted, load all flip-flops in parallel from the data input. Otherwise, if ena is asserted (and load is not), shift the register right by shifting each flip-flop's input from its left neighbor, inserting zero into the MSB flip-flop. This distributed flip-flop approach models the shift explicitly and ensures load has higher priority than shift. The output q collects the state of all flip-flops.
