Implement the 4-bit shift register as four flip-flops with individual assignments. Use asynchronous reset on each flip-flop triggered by the positive edge of areset. At each positive clock edge, check load and enable signals. If load is high, load corresponding bits of data to each flip-flop. Else if enable is high, shift right by passing the higher bit to the lower bit and assign zero to MSB. This approach explicitly models each bit and shift operation, providing clear individual bit control while meeting all requirements.
