Use two always blocks to separate asynchronous reset and synchronous logic. The first always block handles asynchronous reset by asynchronously clearing an internal register. The second always block, triggered only on the clock's positive edge, implements load and enable logic with priority: if load is asserted, load data; else if enable is asserted, shift right with zero fill in MSB; else retain value. This separation allows clear modular design and could aid in better synthesis optimizations or testability, while still respecting all functional requirements and priority rules.
