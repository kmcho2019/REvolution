Refactor the design by eliminating the explicit combinational always block and instead use a continuous assignment with a ternary operator for next-state logic. This removes the intermediate reg next_q and directly computes the next state in an assign statement. The sequential always block remains for the flip-flops and asynchronous reset. This structure simplifies the logic, reduces the number of registers, and may ease synthesis by using purely combinational assign statements for next-state computation.
