The original design is already minimal and functionally correct. However, the use of both an assign statement and an always block implementing the same logic duplicates hardware, increasing area and power slightly. To optimize, we can implement the AND operation once and share it for both outputs. Since one output must be driven by an assign and the other by an always block, we can compute the AND in a wire and connect the assign output directly, while the always block output is updated from the wire. This prevents synthesis from inferring two separate AND gates. This reduces area and power by sharing logic without affecting performance.
