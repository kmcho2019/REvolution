Implement the AND logic by first computing the AND into an internal wire, then use that wire to drive `out_assign` through an assign statement and `out_alwaysblock` via a combinational always block. This separates the computation and output driving stages, making the design modular and easy to modify if the logic grows more complex later. It also ensures both outputs come from a common source, simplifying verification. Although this approach introduces an intermediate signal, it clearly distinguishes combinational logic generation from output assignment, which can be beneficial for debugging and potential expansion.
