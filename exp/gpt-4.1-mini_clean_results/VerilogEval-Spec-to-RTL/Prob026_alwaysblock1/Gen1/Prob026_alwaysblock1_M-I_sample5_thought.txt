The original design correctly implements the AND gate with both assign and always blocks, but it duplicates the AND operation logic for the two outputs separately. To optimize for area and power, use a single internal wire to compute the AND once, then drive both outputs from this wire. This reduces redundant logic and potentially lowers switching activity slightly. This approach maintains the exact same functionality and timing but is more resource-efficient. The always block remains combinational and uses the internal wire to assign the output.
