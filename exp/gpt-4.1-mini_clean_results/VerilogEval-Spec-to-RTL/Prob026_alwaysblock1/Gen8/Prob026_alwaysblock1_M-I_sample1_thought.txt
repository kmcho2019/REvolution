Since the initial design is already minimal and fully correct, the only way to marginally improve power and area is by sharing the AND operation result between both outputs to avoid duplicated logic. Using a single internal wire that computes a & b and then assigning it to both outputs reduces redundant logic instantiation. This does not change the functionality but can slightly reduce the synthesis area and power due to logic sharing. It keeps the assign statement for out_assign and assigns the same internal wire to out_alwaysblock inside a combinational always block. This approach satisfies the problem requirements while improving area and power efficiency.
