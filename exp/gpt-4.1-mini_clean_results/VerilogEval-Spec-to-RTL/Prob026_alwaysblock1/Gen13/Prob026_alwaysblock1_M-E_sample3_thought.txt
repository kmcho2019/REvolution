To implement the AND gate for both outputs while avoiding direct duplication of logic, I will use a single 2-to-1 multiplexer internally that selects between inputs a and b based on a constant select signal tied to 'b'. This is a creative approach that effectively computes the AND function as out = a & b by selecting 'a' when b=1 and 0 otherwise. 

Then, out_assign will be driven by an assign statement connected to this internal logic. For the always block output, I will replicate the mux logic inside a combinational always block.

This architectural approach is different from the straightforward AND gate and shows how simple functions can be realized with multiplexers and control logic, meeting the requirement of implementing AND in both assign and always block styles.
