To optimize for better synthesis results, I will use blocking assignment inside the combinational always block instead of non-blocking assignment, as blocking assignments are recommended for purely combinational logic. This change reduces simulation vs synthesis mismatches and can help synthesis tools infer purely combinational logic more clearly, potentially improving area and power. Also, I will rename the outputs so their names match their implementation style to improve readability and maintainability: 'out_assign' will be driven by an assign statement and 'out_alwaysblock' will be driven by the always block. This is stylistic and does not affect PPA but improves code clarity.
