To optimize the design for better power and area without changing functionality, drive `out_assign` directly from the continuous assignment `a & b` instead of from `out_alwaysblock`. This removes the unnecessary routing from `out_alwaysblock` to `out_assign`, reducing toggling on nets and potentially saving area. This approach avoids redundant logic and improves overall PPA slightly.
