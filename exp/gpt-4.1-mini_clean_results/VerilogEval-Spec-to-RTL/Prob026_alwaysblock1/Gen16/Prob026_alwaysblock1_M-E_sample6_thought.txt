Implement the AND gate output through an intermediate wire that is assigned by an assign statement, then use this wire in the combinational always block to drive the out_alwaysblock output. This way, the combinational logic is factored once in an assign, and the always block simply buffers this intermediate result. It preserves the requirement that both outputs reflect the AND gate output, but uses a different architectural pattern where the always block output depends on a wire assigned combinationally.
