To optimize for power and area, avoid redundant logic by computing the AND only once and driving both outputs from a single signal. Instead of using a reg for out_alwaysblock and an assign for out_assign, assign both outputs directly from the same continuous expression or through a single intermediate wire. This reduces duplicated gates and fanout. Here, I use an internal wire 'and_out' assigned with the AND of a and b, and then both outputs are driven from it: out_assign is assigned directly, and out_alwaysblock is declared as wire and assigned by continuous assignment (to avoid reg usage and always block). This approach minimizes logic replication, saves area, and reduces power, with no timing or functional penalty.
