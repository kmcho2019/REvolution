Implement the AND gate twice independently without sharing intermediate signals to emphasize the difference between continuous assignment and always block approaches.

- Compute out_assign directly with an assign statement as a simple AND of inputs a and b.
- Use a combinational always block that directly computes out_alwaysblock without any intermediate wire, assigning the bitwise AND of a and b inside the always block.
- This design explicitly duplicates the logic to clearly demonstrate the two distinct methods for combinational logic implementation, without sharing any internal signals or wires.
- This provides clarity in teaching or demonstration contexts where showing two separate ways to implement the same logic is desired.
