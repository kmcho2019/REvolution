Instead of sharing a wire for the AND logic, this design implements the AND gate operation separately in two distinct ways to emphasize the difference in coding styles:

- `out_assign` is driven directly by an assign statement performing the AND operation inline.
- `out_alwaysblock` is implemented inside a combinational always block using explicit conditional logic (if-else), making it clear that this block models combinational behavior without relying on continuous assignments or intermediate wires.
  
This approach clearly differentiates the two methods of implementing combinational logic within one module and highlights behavioral and structural modeling styles without sharing signals.
