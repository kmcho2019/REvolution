To optimize for better power and area while maintaining functional correctness, share the AND computation between the two outputs instead of duplicating it. Compute the AND once internally and assign one output via a continuous assign and the other via a combinational always block using a blocking assignment. This reduces redundant logic, lowers switching activity and gate count, and improves coding style by using blocking assignment in the always block. This approach keeps the dual-style output requirement but achieves better PPA.
