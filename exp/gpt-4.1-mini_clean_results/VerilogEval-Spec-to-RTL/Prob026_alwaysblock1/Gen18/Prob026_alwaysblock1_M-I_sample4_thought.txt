The original design implements the AND gate twice, producing minimal logic with already optimal PPA for such a trivial circuit. Both outputs are needed independently, so logic sharing is limited. However, slight optimization can be done by using a single internal wire for the AND result, then assigning one output continuously and the other from a combinational always block driven by that internal wire. This removes redundant AND computations, slightly reducing area and power while keeping equivalent performance. The approach clarifies the relationship between the outputs and may enable better synthesis optimizations.
