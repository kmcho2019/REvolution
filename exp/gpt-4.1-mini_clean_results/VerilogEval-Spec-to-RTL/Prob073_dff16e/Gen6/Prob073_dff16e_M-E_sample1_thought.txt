Partition the 16-bit register into two separate 8-bit registers, each with its own always block and synchronous active-low reset. Use the individual byte enable signals to gate updates only to the corresponding 8-bit register. This modularizes the design, simplifies masking logic, and potentially enables more fine-grained optimization by synthesis tools. Each 8-bit block updates only when its byteena bit is high, and otherwise retains its old value. The reset clears both registers synchronously on active low reset. This approach avoids bitwise masking of the entire 16 bits and uses straightforward conditional loading per byte, making the logic clearer and potentially more power-efficient by limiting toggling to updated bytes only.
