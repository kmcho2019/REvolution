Implement the 16 D flip-flops as two separate 8-bit registers each with synchronous active-low reset. Instead of gating data assignments conditionally inside one always block, split the design into two always blocks—one for the lower byte and one for the upper byte. Each always block updates its respective byte of q only when the corresponding byte enable is asserted; otherwise, it retains its previous value. This explicit separation clarifies control paths and can help synthesis tools optimize enable logic and timing. The reset clears both bytes simultaneously. This modular approach also supports clearer clock domain partitioning or power optimization if extended.
