Implement the 16-bit register as two separate 8-bit registers, each controlled by one bit of byteena. Each byte has its own always block triggered by the positive clock edge with synchronous active-low reset. When resetn is low, both registers are cleared. When resetn is high, each byte register updates only if its corresponding byteena bit is high; otherwise, it retains its previous value. Finally, concatenate the two 8-bit registers to form the 16-bit output q. This approach partitions the design, making it modular and potentially easier to optimize or scale.
