Implement 16 D flip-flops with synchronous active-low reset triggered on the positive edge of clk. Use the two-bit byteena signal to selectively update either the upper byte (bits 15:8) or the lower byte (bits 7:0) of the output register q. If the corresponding byteena bit is low, the corresponding byte in q holds its previous value; if high, it loads from input d. On synchronous reset (resetn == 0), reset q to zero.
