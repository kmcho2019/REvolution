Refactor the design by splitting the 16-bit register into two separate 8-bit registers for upper and lower bytes. Each register has its own always block triggered by clk, with synchronous active-low reset. The byte enables control whether each 8-bit register updates on the clock edge. This separation clarifies logic, potentially aids synthesis tools in optimizing timing and power by isolating enable paths, and provides a modular structure. It keeps the same functionality but improves readability and possibly PPA.
