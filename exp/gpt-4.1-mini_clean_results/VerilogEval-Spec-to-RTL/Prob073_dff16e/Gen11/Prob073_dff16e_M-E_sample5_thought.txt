Implement the 16 D flip-flops as two separate 8-bit registers, each with its own byte enable and synchronous active-low reset. Use a combined mask that zeroes out the bits that should not be updated and preserves the current q bits otherwise. This is done by using bitwise operations on the entire 16-bit vector at once, which simplifies the assignment to a single statement and potentially improves timing and area by avoiding conditional branching and multiple separate assignments. The synchronous active-low reset clears all bits on the positive clock edge.
