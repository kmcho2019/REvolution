Implement a single 16-bit register 'q' updated synchronously on the rising edge of clk with synchronous active-low reset. On reset, clear q to zero. Otherwise, use a mask derived from the two-bit byteena to selectively update the upper byte and lower byte of q from the input d. This approach combines all flip-flops into one always block, reduces logic duplication, and registers the output q directly, minimizing combinational logic and simplifying timing and area.
