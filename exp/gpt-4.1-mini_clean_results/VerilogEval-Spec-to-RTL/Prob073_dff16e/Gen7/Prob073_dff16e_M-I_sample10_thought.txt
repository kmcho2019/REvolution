To optimize for better power and area, I will implement explicit clock gating per byte enable to prevent the flip-flops from toggling when the corresponding byte is not enabled. By gating the clock, the flip-flops only receive clock pulses when their byte enable is asserted, reducing unnecessary switching and dynamic power consumption. This approach also helps synthesis tools optimize flip-flop clock enables more effectively. The synchronous active-low reset will be preserved within each gated clock domain to ensure correct reset behavior. This method can improve power metrics without increasing area significantly, and can potentially improve timing by reducing toggle rates on non-enabled bytes.
