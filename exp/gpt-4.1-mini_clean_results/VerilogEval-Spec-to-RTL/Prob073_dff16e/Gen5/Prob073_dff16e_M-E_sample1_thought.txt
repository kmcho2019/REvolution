Implement the 16-bit register as two 8-bit registers (upper and lower byte) each with its own enable signal derived from byteena. On each positive edge of clk, synchronously reset both bytes to zero if resetn is low. If resetn is high, update each byte independently only when its corresponding byteena bit is asserted; otherwise retain its previous value. This modular approach separates byte operations for cleaner logic and can facilitate byte-wise clock gating or further optimizations later.
