To improve power and area while maintaining the same functionality, I will implement clock enables on the two bytes separately rather than using a byte-wide mux inside the always block. This approach reduces unnecessary toggling inside flip-flops when the byteena bits are zero, as the registers only update when enabled. This also helps synthesis tools to better optimize and gate the clocks or use clock enables internally, reducing dynamic power and potentially improving timing. The synchronous active-low reset will be retained. The flip-flops are split logically by bytes, allowing each byte to have its own enable signal derived from byteena bits.
