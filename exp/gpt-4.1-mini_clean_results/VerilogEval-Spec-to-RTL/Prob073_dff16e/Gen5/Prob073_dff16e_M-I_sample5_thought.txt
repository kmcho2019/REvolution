To optimize the design for power and potentially performance, introduce an enable signal to gate the update of the entire 16-bit register only when any byte enable bit is set. This avoids unnecessary register toggling when no byte is enabled for writing, reducing dynamic power consumption. The synchronous active-low reset remains prioritized. Using a single assignment with a mux based on byte enables consolidates the logic and might improve synthesis optimization. This maintains the same functional behavior with improved power efficiency and potentially better timing by reducing switching activity in the register.
