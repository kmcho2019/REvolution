To improve PPA (timing, power, area), partition the 16-bit register into two separate 8-bit registers, each controlled by one bit of the byte enable signal. This eliminates the need for a wide 16-bit masking logic, thereby reducing combinational logic depth and critical path delay. Each byte is updated independently only when its byte enable is asserted, reducing unnecessary toggling and switching power. Additionally, synchronous reset logic remains simple and separate for each byte, maintaining clarity and ease of timing closure. This approach can achieve better timing (higher clock frequency), lower dynamic power by reducing toggling, and potentially a slight area reduction due to smaller multiplexers per byte.
