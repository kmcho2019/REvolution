Use a single 16-bit register to hold all flip-flops and update the register on each clock cycle. Generate a mask from byteena to selectively update bytes: replicate each byteena bit 8 times to form two byte masks, then combine them to create a 16-bit mask. On synchronous active-low reset, clear the entire register. On normal operation, update only the bits enabled by the mask, keeping others unchanged. This approach reduces complexity, area, and avoids extra concatenation logic while maintaining functional correctness.
