Implement the 16-bit register as two separate 8-bit registers, each with its own clock-enable derived from the corresponding byteena bit. On the positive clock edge, if resetn is low, both registers reset synchronously to zero. Otherwise, each register updates only when its byteena bit is high, loading the corresponding 8 bits from d. This allows direct use of clock-enable signals to control byte-wise updates, potentially simplifying synthesis by mapping to native register enables, improving power and area. The resetn remains synchronous and active-low.
