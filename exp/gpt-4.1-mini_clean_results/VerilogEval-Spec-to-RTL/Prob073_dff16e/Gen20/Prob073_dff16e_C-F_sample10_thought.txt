Implement two separate always blocks—one for each byte slice (upper and lower bytes)—each with synchronous active-low reset and byte-enable controlled updating. This fusion yields:  
- Performance: Reduced combinational masking logic per byte, potentially shorter critical paths.  
- Power: Updating only relevant bytes independently prevents unnecessary toggling.  
- Area: Slightly more registers (two 8-bit regs) but simpler logic; overall area is comparable or better due to simpler combinational logic.  
- Readability: Clear byte-wise update semantics with explicit always blocks.
