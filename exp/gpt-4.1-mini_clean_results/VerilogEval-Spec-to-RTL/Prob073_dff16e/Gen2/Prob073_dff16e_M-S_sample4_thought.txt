Use a single masked update for the entire 16-bit register on each clock edge. Compute a mask from the byteena input: upper byte mask is 8'hFF if byteena[1] is set, lower byte mask is 8'hFF if byteena[0] is set. Then update q by combining the old q and new d with this mask, avoiding redundant self-assignments. Synchronous active-low reset sets q to 0.
