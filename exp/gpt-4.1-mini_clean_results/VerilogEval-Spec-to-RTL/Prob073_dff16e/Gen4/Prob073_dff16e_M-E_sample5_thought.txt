Implement the 16-bit register as two independent 8-bit registers (upper and lower bytes) each with its own write enable driven directly by the respective byteena bit. On each clock, if resetn is low, both registers reset synchronously to zero. Otherwise, each register updates only if its corresponding byteena bit is high; otherwise it holds its previous value. This byte-wise modular structure simplifies control logic, reduces unnecessary bit masking and switching, and provides clearer synthesis mapping to byte-granular registers. The final output q concatenates the two 8-bit registers.
