To optimize the design for better performance, power, and area, the strategy is to avoid complex wide bitwise masks and redundant OR operations. Instead, update each byte independently using a simple conditional operator inside a single register update. This approach creates minimal logic: for each byte, select either the new input byte `d` or the old register byte `q` based on the corresponding byte enable bit. This mux-based selection is straightforward, reduces switching by only changing bytes when enabled, and leads to simpler and faster synthesized logic. The synchronous active-low reset remains unchanged.
