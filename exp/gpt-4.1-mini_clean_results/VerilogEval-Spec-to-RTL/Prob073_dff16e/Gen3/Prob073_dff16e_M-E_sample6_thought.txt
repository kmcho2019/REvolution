Implement the 16-bit register as two separate 8-bit registers, each with its own always block triggered on the clock's positive edge. Each 8-bit register independently handles its synchronous active-low reset and byte-enable write enable. This modularizes the design, isolating the upper and lower bytes physically and logically, potentially simplifying timing analysis and enabling byte-wise clock gating in future enhancements. The two always blocks update their corresponding byte slices of the output q separately, reflecting the respective byte-enable bits.
