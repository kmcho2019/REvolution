Combine the clarity of separating combinational next-state logic from sequential update (from Example 2) with the direct conditional update style for each byte (from Example 1) to produce a concise and clear design. Use a single combinational block to compute the next_q by merging the unchanged bytes with the updated bytes selected by byteena bits. This fusion maintains readable structure, ensures minimal switching by selectively updating bytes, and helps synthesis tools optimize mux insertion and register enable logic, achieving balanced PPA.
