To improve power and slightly reduce area, implement the byte-enable controlled updates using a single clocked process with a combinational mux for the next state, reducing unnecessary register writes. This avoids conditional nonblocking assignments separately for each byte and helps synthesis tools optimize clock gating and data paths better. Keep synchronous active-low reset and posedge clock triggering. This approach helps minimize switching inside the register by preparing the next q value combinationally, thus reducing dynamic power without adding complexity.
