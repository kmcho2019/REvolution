To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, replace the separate if-else conditional updates with a single combined assignment using bitwise masking. This removes redundant self-assignments and reduces logic complexity by creating a single next-data vector that selectively chooses between new input bytes and held old values. Such mux-based data selection enables more efficient synthesis optimizations, reducing logic depth and switching activity. This approach also slightly lowers area by reducing unnecessary logic and improves power by minimizing toggling of unchanged bits. The synchronous, active-low reset and positive edge clock remain unchanged.
