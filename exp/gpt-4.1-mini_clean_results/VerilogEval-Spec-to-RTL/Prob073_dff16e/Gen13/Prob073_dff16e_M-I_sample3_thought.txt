To optimize for better PPA, implement the selective write as a single masked assignment using bitwise operations instead of conditional byte-wise assignments. This reduces multiplexer logic by masking the input data bits with replicated byteena signals and combining them with the retained q bits. This creates one assignment that synthesizes to simpler logic and possibly better timing and area. The synchronous active-low reset remains unchanged. This approach also slightly reduces switching by avoiding redundant mux selects, and may improve power by cleaner logic.
