I will implement the 16 D flip-flops as two separate 8-bit registers—one for the upper byte and one for the lower byte—each with its own always block triggered on the positive edge of clk. This modular approach cleanly separates the byte enables, making the design highly readable and maintainable. Both registers will have synchronous active-low resets. Each always block updates its respective byte only when the corresponding byteena bit is set. Splitting the bytes into two independent registers could simplify synthesis muxing and potentially improve timing by localizing enable logic. The top-level output q concatenates the two registers to produce the 16-bit output.
