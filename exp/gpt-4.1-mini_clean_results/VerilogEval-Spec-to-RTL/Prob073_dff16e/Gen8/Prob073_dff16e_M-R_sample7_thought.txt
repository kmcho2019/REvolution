Refactor the design into a single always block handling the entire 16-bit register with byte-enable masking applied during updates. This reduces register duplication and control logic, potentially improving area and power. Instead of separately storing upper and lower bytes in distinct registers, a unified 16-bit register is maintained. On each positive clock edge, if resetn is low, reset the entire register. Otherwise, update only the bytes enabled by the byteena bits by masking the input data with byteena and preserving the non-enabled bytes from the previous register content. The output q is registered directly inside this clocked always block, removing the combinational concatenation stage and minimizing glitches on outputs.
