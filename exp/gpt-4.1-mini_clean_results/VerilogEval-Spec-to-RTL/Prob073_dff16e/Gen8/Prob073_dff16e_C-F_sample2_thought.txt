Specifically, I will precompute the byte-enable mask once as a local wire with simple conditional concatenation, improving readability and synthesis friendliness. This approach maintains a single 16-bit register for area efficiency and a minimal logic depth update path, reduces toggling power by only updating enabled bytes, and improves performance by avoiding recomputing the mask multiple times in the always block. This fusion preserves all functionality and design goals, optimizing power, area, and performance simultaneously.
