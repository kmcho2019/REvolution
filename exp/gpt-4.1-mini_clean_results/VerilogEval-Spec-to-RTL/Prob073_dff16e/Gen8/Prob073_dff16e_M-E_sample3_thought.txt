I will implement the 16 D flip-flops as two separate 8-bit registers, each controlled by its own always block, synchronized on the rising edge of clk. The byte enables will directly gate each 8-bit register's update logic: the upper 8 bits updated only when byteena[1] is asserted, and the lower 8 bits updated only when byteena[0] is asserted. On synchronous active-low reset, both registers will clear to zero. This architectural separation simplifies the control for each byte, enabling independent clock gating or power optimizations if extended, and makes the code modular and explicit, improving readability and debugging. The output q is formed by concatenating the two 8-bit registers. This clear byte-wise modular design is a clean alternative to monolithic masking.
