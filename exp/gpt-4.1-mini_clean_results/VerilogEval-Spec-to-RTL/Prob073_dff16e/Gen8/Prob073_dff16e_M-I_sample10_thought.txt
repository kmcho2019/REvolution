To optimize the design for better PPA, I will group the flip-flops by bytes instead of bit-wise conditional assignments inside the for-loop. Each byte (8 bits) will be updated using a byte-wide 2-to-1 multiplexer controlled by its respective byte enable. This reduces the number of multiplexers and simplifies logic, improving timing and potentially saving area. The synchronous, active-low reset remains and sets all bits to zero. Non-blocking assignments ensure proper sequential behavior. This approach avoids per-bit conditionals, leading to more efficient synthesis and better power due to fewer toggling gates.
