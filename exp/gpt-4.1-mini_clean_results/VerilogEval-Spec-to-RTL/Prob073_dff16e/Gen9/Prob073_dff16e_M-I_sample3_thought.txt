To improve PPA, replace combinational masking with per-byte clock enables to reduce unnecessary toggling and logic depth, improving performance and power. Implement two 8-bit registers (upper and lower bytes) with separate write enables derived from byteena, and synchronous active-low reset. This reduces combinational mask generation and dynamic power by only updating enabled bytes. Also, splitting into two smaller registers helps timing closure and may reduce area by simpler muxes and clock gating support. The 16-bit output q is the concatenation of these two byte registers.
