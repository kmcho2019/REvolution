Implement the 16 D flip-flops as two separate 8-bit registers, one for each byte, each updated only when its corresponding byteena bit is asserted. Use two always blocks, one per byte, triggered on the positive edge of clk. For synchronous active-low reset, reset both registers to zero when resetn is low. This approach cleanly separates byte update logic, potentially improving modularity and allowing synthesis tools to optimize each byte independently. The output q is formed by concatenating the two 8-bit registers.
