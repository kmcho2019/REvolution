Refactor the FSM by using one always block for state and counters updates (sequential logic) and separate continuous assignments (`assign`) for the combinational outputs and next state/counter values. This leverages combinational logic outside always blocks to determine the next state, counters, and output, reducing the combinational always block complexity and making the design more explicit. The Mealy-style output z is computed combinationally based on the current cycle count and w_count plus current w at the 3rd cycle. This approach clearly separates sequential and combinational logic and uses assign statements to improve readability and possibly synthesis friendliness while preserving the exact FSM behavior and minimal states.
