We can simplify the FSM by using three states: A (waiting for s=1), and in B state, cycle through three sub-states counting cycles 0,1,2. Instead of packing counters, we use a 2-bit cycle counter and a 2-bit w accumulator directly as registers. The output z is asserted synchronously in the cycle after counting three w inputs with exactly two ones.

This approach avoids combinational arithmetic on multi-bit registers in the next-state logic by incrementing counters in sequential logic directly, which simplifies the combinational logic. The FSM uses one-hot or binary encoding with three states but minimal logic, and registers are updated only in B state.

This reduces complexity, improves clarity, and maintains functionality without complicating combinational assignments or packing bits.
