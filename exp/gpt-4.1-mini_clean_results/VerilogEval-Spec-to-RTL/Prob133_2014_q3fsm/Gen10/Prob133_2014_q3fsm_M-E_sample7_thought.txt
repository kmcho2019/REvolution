I will implement the FSM with only two main states: A (waiting for s=1) and B (processing groups of three w inputs). To minimize states, the group-of-three cycles will be tracked by a 2-bit cycle counter inside state B rather than explicit separate states.

In state B, a small counter increments each cycle (0 to 2) and accumulates how many times w=1 over those three cycles. After the third cycle (count=2), the FSM outputs z=1 if the count of w=1 is exactly 2, else 0, then resets the counters and continues processing the next group of three.

This approach reduces the FSM states to only two (A and B), using internal counters and combinational logic to handle the group counting and output logic. Output z is registered and updated after each group of three cycles.

This design simplifies state encoding (1 bit), uses internal cycle and count registers, and ensures synchronous reset and outputs on clock edges, meeting timing and simplicity requirements.
