Use three states to explicitly represent the counting phases within the 3-cycle window in state B:

- State A: waiting for s=1 to transition to B; output z=0.
- States B0, B1, B2: represent the three cycles of sampling w.
  
The FSM transitions: 
- From A to B0 when s=1.
- From B0 to B1 to B2, then back to B0.

During B0, B1, B2, accumulate the count of w=1 samples in a 2-bit register.

After B2 (on the next clock), output z=1 if w_count == 2, else 0, then reset w_count for the next 3-cycle window.

This approach encodes the counting position into the state, eliminating explicit counters, simplifying control logic. Output z is synchronous and aligned to the next cycle after sampling completes.
