We can simplify the FSM by using just two states and separate registers for the 2-bit cycle counter and 2-bit w accumulator, removing the packed register. The output z can be directly computed and registered when the 3-cycle window finishes, making the design straightforward.

In state A, we wait for s=1 to go to B; in B, we count 3 cycles of w inputs and then check if exactly two w's were 1. The output z is asserted one cycle after the counting completes. This approach avoids complex packed registers and multiple next-state variables, using clear sequential logic with minimal combinational complexity.

Synchronous reset and positive-edge clocking are maintained, and inputs s and w are used exactly as specified.
