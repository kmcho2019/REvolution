To improve PPA, especially to reduce unnecessary toggling and optimize the critical path, I will add clock enables for the cycle counter and accumulator so they only update in state B. This reduces switching activity in registers during state A. Also, I'll merge the combinational addition of w_accum+w into the sequential block to avoid the extra combinational adder and separate always @(*) block, simplifying critical path and logic. By computing the updated accumulator value inside the sequential block, we can eliminate one combinational logic stage for z_next, producing z directly at the end of the counting cycle. This maintains the synchronous reset and positive-edge-triggered sequential logic, keeps minimal states, and respects the original FSM behavior.
