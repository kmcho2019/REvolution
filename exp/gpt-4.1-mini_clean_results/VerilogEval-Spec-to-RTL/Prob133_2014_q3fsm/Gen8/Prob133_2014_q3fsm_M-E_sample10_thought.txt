Use three explicit states to represent the three cycles of w sampling in state B: B0, B1, and B2. Upon s=1 in state A, transition to B0. In each B state, sample w and increment an accumulator counting how many times w=1. At B2, after sampling the third w, set an output register signal that will drive z=1 in the following cycle if the accumulator equals 2, else z=0. Then reset the accumulator and return to B0 for the next 3-cycle window. This explicit per-cycle state encoding eliminates counters and simplifies logic by directly tracking the cycle position with FSM states. The output z is registered and delayed by one cycle after the window, as required. This approach trades a few more states for simpler control logic and direct state transitions.
