Implement the FSM with three states: A (idle), B0, B1, B2, representing each cycle in the 3-cycle window.

On reset, go to A. In A, stay until s=1, then move to B0.

In states B0, B1, B2, shift in the current w input as a bit in a 3-bit shift register.

At B2 (after sampling the third w), count how many bits in the shift register are 1. If exactly two, assert z=1 for one cycle, else 0.

After outputting z, move back to B0 to start the next 3-cycle window.

This approach explicitly models each cycle as a state, avoids counters and accumulators, using shift registers to hold sampled w.

Synchronous reset active high. Positive edge clocking.

This design keeps FSM states minimal and encodes cycle position directly, making next-state logic simple and output logic clear. Also, since z depends only on the shift register, combinational count and compare is straightforward.
