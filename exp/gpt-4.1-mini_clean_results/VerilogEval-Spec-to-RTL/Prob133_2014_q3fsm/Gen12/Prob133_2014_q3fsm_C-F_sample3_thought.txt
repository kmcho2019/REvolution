Enhance power efficiency by updating the packed register only in state B and adding an explicit clock enable controlled by state, preventing unnecessary toggling in state A.

Improve performance by replacing the addition of w_accum + w with a simple increment since w is one bit, using conditional increment logic to simplify combinational additions and reduce delay.

Keep next-state and output logic fully combinational with blocking assignments, providing clear default assignments to avoid latches and glitches.

Retain synchronous reset and non-blocking sequential updates for all registers, ensuring synthesis friendliness.

Output z is asserted exactly one cycle after the 3-cycle window when exactly two 'w's were high, maintaining a single-cycle latency.

This design balances clarity, minimal states, power reduction, simple arithmetic, and good timing, improving over previous examples without increasing area or complexity.
