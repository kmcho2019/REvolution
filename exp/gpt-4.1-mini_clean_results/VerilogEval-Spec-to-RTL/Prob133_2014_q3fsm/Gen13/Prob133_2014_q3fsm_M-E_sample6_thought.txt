Implement the FSM with three states: A (waiting for s=1), B0 (first cycle checking w), B1 (second cycle), B2 (third cycle), then output Z after evaluating the count. Use a simple 2-bit counter to count how many times w=1 in the three cycles, and a 2-bit cycle index from 0 to 2. Transition sequentially through B0, B1, B2, accumulating w counts. After B2, transition back to B0 to start a new 3-cycle window, outputting z=1 if the w count equals 2 in the next cycle. This approach uses explicit states and counters instead of packing counters into one register, improving readability and potentially easing timing analysis by separating state logic from counting logic. The output z is registered and delayed by one cycle after the 3-cycle window as required. Synchronous active-high reset initializes FSM to A and resets counters and outputs.
