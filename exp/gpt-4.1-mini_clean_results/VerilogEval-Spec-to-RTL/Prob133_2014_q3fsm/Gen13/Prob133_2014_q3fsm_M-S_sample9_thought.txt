Use only a 3-bit shift register to track the last three w inputs in state B, and count the number of 1s directly from this shift register at the end of each 3-cycle window. This eliminates the separate cycle and accumulator registers. Keep just the two states (A and B) and output logic straightforward by asserting z one cycle after the 3rd input is sampled if exactly two of the three inputs were 1. The shift register naturally stores the three samples, and a 2-bit counter keeps track of cycles. This reduces state and counters to a minimum.
