To optimize the original design for better PPA:

- Replace the combinational addition (w_count + w) with a registered addition inside sequential logic, removing the combinational dependency for output z calculation. This reduces critical path delay in combinational logic.

- Use a small saturating counter incremented by w inside the sequential block only in B_0 and B_1 states.

- Calculate z output solely in the state after counting (B_0), based on the registered final count, eliminating combinational logic for z_next.

- Gate the w_count increment to update only when w=1 to reduce unnecessary toggling, lowering dynamic power.

- Keep state encoding minimal and maintain synchronous reset.

This strategy simplifies combinational logic, reduces toggle rate in w_count, and registers output z cleanly one cycle after counting three w inputs, improving timing and power without increasing area or latency.
