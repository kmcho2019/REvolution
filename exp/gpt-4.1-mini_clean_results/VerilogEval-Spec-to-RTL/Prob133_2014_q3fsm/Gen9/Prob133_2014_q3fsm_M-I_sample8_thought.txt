The existing design is functionally correct and minimal in state encoding and counter width. To improve power and timing, I will optimize the combinational logic and reduce switching as follows:

- Pre-register input w once in state B to reduce glitches and simplify logic.
- Use a small FSM with 3 cycles tracked by a 2-bit counter (0 to 2).
- Instead of adding w_count + w every cycle combinationally, update w_count sequentially by accumulating registered w to avoid combinational adder.
- Generate output z in the same cycle when cycle_cnt == 2 based on stored w_count (which then already includes the 3rd w input).
- Hold z at 0 in other cycles to reduce toggling.
- Ensure synchronous reset and minimal combinational logic for next state.
- This reduces combinational path on w_count + w addition and output logic, improving timing and power.
- The output z update is registered (Mealy-style output from previous code replaced with Moore-style registered output) to further reduce glitches.
- Overall, this trades a 1-cycle latency in z assertion for better PPA metrics without increasing area.

This approach remains minimal in states and counter size but improves timing by simplifying combinational logic and power by reducing toggling on z and counters.
