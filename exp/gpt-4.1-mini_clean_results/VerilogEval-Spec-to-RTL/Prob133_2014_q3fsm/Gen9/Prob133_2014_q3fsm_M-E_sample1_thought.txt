Use three states: A (initial wait for s=1), B0, B1, B2 representing counting of the three cycles where w is observed. In states B0, B1, B2, accumulate the number of w=1's in a 2-bit register. After B2, output z=1 in the next clock cycle if exactly two of the three w values were 1, else 0, then restart counting cycles from B0.

This explicitly sequences through the three cycles by states rather than using counters, reducing arithmetic and packing logic. The output z is updated in state A and each time after completing B2, ensuring z is aligned with the "next cycle" after three samples. 

The FSM resets to state A, waits for s=1 to go to B0. In B states, s is ignored. The w accumulation uses a 2-bit register updated by incrementing by w each cycle, simple addition of one bit and a 2-bit counter.

This design simplifies cycle tracking by explicit states instead of cycle counters, and has straightforward sequential logic with minimal arithmetic, which might improve timing and simplify area.
