Use only two states (A and B) without separate counters by encoding the three-cycle counting in the state transitions. In state B, keep a 2-bit counter and a 2-bit accumulator for w inside registers updated synchronously. Output z is registered and asserted one cycle after collecting three w inputs when exactly two are 1. By combining next state logic with output and counters inside the synchronous block and removing separate next-state signals, complexity is reduced while maintaining functionality.
