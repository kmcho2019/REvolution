Implement the FSM with three states: A (initial/reset), B (counting cycles 1 and 2), and C (counting cycle 3 and deciding output). Use a 2-bit counter for counting how many times w=1 in the current 3-cycle window. On entering state B from A, initialize counters to zero. In states B and C, update the w count and cycle count every clock cycle. After cycle 3 (state C), assert z=1 if exactly two w=1 were detected, else z=0. Return to state B to process the next 3-cycle window. This state machine explicitly separates counting phases, potentially improving clarity and ease of timing optimization while maintaining minimal states and registers.
