Implement the FSM with three states: A (reset and waiting for s=1), C0, C1, C2 representing the three cycles of counting w values. Use a 2-bit counter to count the number of w=1 within these three cycles. After the third cycle (C2), transition back to C0 for the next counting window. Output z is registered and set to 1 only in the cycle following the completion of three cycles if the w count is exactly two, otherwise 0. This approach explicitly encodes each counting cycle as a state, removing the need for separate counters for cycle count and w count registers, thus simplifying state and output logic and providing clear cycle boundaries. Use synchronous reset active high and positive edge clocking.
