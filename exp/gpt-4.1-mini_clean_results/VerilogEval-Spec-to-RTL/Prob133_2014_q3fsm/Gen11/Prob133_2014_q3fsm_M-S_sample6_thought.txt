Simplify the design by merging the counters cycle_cnt and w_count into a single 3-bit shift register that captures the last three w inputs in state B. At each clock, shift in the current w value. When three samples are collected (after three clocks in state B), count the number of ones by summing the 3 bits. If exactly two are ones, output z=1 in the next cycle; otherwise 0. This removes separate counters and accumulators, reduces addition complexity, and clarifies logic while maintaining the same functionality and minimal states.
