Use a single register to hold the count of w=1 inputs within the three-cycle sampling window, and a 2-bit counter for the sampling cycle. Keep only two states: A (wait for s=1) and B (sample w and count). After three cycles, assert z based on the final count in the same cycle. Reset clears all registers synchronously. This eliminates the separate w_count register update and combines counting and cycle advancing simply, reducing complexity and registers.
