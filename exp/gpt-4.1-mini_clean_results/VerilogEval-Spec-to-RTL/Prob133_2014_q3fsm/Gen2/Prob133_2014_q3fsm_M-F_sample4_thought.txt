Simplify the FSM by eliminating the extra output state (B3) and instead use three sampling states (B0, B1, B2) corresponding to the three cycles of sampling w. Then, immediately in the next cycle after these three samples (which is the next B0 cycle), assert z based on the count from the previous three-cycle group.

This means:
- Use only states A, B0, B1, B2.
- Accumulate the count of w=1 during B0, B1, B2.
- Introduce a separate register to hold the output z value that corresponds to the previous group.
- When entering B0 state, output the stored z value (for the previous group's result) and reset the count to start counting the new group.
- This shifts the output z timing correctly: output z in the cycle immediately following the three sampling cycles.

This approach ensures:
- Output z is asserted one cycle after sampling three w inputs.
- Count resets only at the start of sampling a new group.
- The number of FSM states is minimal.
- Timing and counting logic are clearly separated, avoiding off-by-one or premature resets.

All synchronous logic triggered on the clock's positive edge with synchronous reset, and output z updated based on the stored result of the last group's count.
