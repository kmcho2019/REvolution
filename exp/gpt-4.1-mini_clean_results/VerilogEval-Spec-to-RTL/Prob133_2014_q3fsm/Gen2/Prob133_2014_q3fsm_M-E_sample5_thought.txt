Use three bits of a shift register in state B to capture the last three w inputs serially. After every three clocks in B, count how many of the three bits are 1 (popcount) and set z accordingly for one cycle. This avoids separate counters for cycle index and w count, and reuses a simple shift register plus combinational logic for counting. The FSM has only two states: A and B. In state A, wait for s=1 to enter B and start capturing w. In B, shift in w each cycle and every three cycles evaluate the count. Use a modulo-3 counter for cycle tracking. Output z is asserted one clock after the third w sample if exactly two w's were 1. This is efficient and minimal state logic.
