Implement the FSM with just two main states: A (waiting for s=1) and B (processing windows of 3 cycles to count w=1). Use a 2-bit cycle counter within state B to track which cycle of the 3-cycle window we are on, and a 2-bit accumulator to count w=1 occurrences. The cycle counter increments each clock in B; when it reaches 2 (the third cycle), we evaluate if the w count plus current w is exactly two to set z=1 for the next cycle; otherwise z=0. Then reset counters and continue next window. The output z is registered and asserted one cycle after the 3rd w input, as required. This uses minimal states (only A and B) and leverages counters for window and accumulation, reducing FSM complexity. Synchronous reset brings the FSM to A and clears counters and output.
