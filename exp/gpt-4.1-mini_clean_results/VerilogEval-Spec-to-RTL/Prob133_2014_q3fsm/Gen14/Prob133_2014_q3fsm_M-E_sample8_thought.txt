Implement the FSM using a three-bit shift register to sample the last three w inputs once the FSM enters state B. This approach uses a single state variable and a simple counter to know when three samples have been collected. Instead of accumulating counts, the FSM shifts in each new w value on every clock cycle and checks, at the end of the three-cycle window, whether exactly two bits in the shift register are set. Then z is asserted for one cycle accordingly. This design keeps the FSM simple with two states (A and B), uses a shift register for w sampling, and computes the output combinationally based on the shift register contents, reducing counters and arithmetic to just a bit count. Reset initializes everything. This approach reduces complexity by leveraging a shift register and population count logic instead of explicit counters for w=1 occurrences.
