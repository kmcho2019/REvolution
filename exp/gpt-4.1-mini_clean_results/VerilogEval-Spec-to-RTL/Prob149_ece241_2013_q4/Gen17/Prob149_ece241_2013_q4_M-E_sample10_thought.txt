Implement a small Moore state machine tracking the discrete water level states with explicit numeric encoding (0=LOW,...3=HIGH). On every clock, sample the sensors and determine the current level number. Maintain two registers: current_level and prev_level to remember the previous water level. Compare the new sampled level with current_level; if changed, update prev_level with old current_level, then update current_level. The dfr output is asserted synchronously for one cycle when current_level > prev_level (indicating rising water). The nominal flow outputs fr0, fr1, fr2 are purely combinational from current_level as per the table. On reset, initialize current_level and prev_level to LOW (0) and assert all flow outputs including dfr, matching the requirement. This approach avoids complex one-hot states and uses simple numeric encoding, making comparison straightforward and cleanly separating state memory from combinational output logic.
