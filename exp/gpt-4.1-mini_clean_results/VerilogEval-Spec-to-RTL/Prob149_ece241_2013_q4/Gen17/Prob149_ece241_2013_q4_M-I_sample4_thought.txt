The key functional issue is that `dfr` should be asserted only when the water level has risen compared to the *previous* water level before the *last* sensor change, meaning we need to retain a one-step history of water level state.

To fix this, we separate updating `current_state` and the comparison for `dfr`. We:
1. Update `prev_state` with the old `current_state` *before* updating `current_state` to the new decoded state.
2. Compute `dfr` by comparing `current_state` (new) against `prev_state` (old).
3. Assert `dfr=1` for one cycle when the level rises (new level > old level).
4. When no state change occurs, dfr is 0.
5. On reset, initialize to the LOW state with all nominal flows and dfr asserted per spec.

This ensures proper history is maintained and the rise condition is detected correctly.

The nominal flow outputs remain combinational based on `current_state`. This approach is straightforward and synthesizable.

No optimization for PPA is explicitly requested beyond correctness, so clarity and correctness are prioritized.
