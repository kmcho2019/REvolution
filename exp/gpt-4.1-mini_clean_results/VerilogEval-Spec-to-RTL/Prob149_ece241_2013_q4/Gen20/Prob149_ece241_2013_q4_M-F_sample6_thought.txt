The key correction is to assert dfr based on comparing the current water level to the immediately previous water level (prev_level), not the one before last (prev_prev_level). This aligns with the requirement that "If the sensor change indicates that the previous level was lower than the current level," then dfr should be asserted. We keep tracking three levels for stability, but only use current_level and prev_level for this comparison. On a level change, shift prev_level to current_level, current_level to sampled_level, and then assert dfr if sampled_level > prev_level. Reset logic and nominal flow output logic remain the same.
