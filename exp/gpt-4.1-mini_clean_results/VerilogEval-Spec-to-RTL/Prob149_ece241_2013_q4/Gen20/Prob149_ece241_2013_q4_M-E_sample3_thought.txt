Implement a small state machine with four states representing the water levels from 0 (lowest) to 3 (highest), updating synchronously with the sensor inputs. On each clock cycle, the FSM moves to the state matching the decoded sensor pattern. The supplemental flow valve (dfr) is asserted when the FSM detects a rising water level transition (new state > previous state). Nominal flow outputs (fr0, fr1, fr2) are asserted combinationally based on the current state according to the specification. The synchronous reset forces the FSM to the lowest water level state with all flow outputs asserted. This explicit FSM approach clearly separates water level states and simplifies tracking the level changes for dfr generation.
