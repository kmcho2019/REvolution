Instead of tracking only the previous and current discrete level states and comparing them on a clock edge, use a small state machine that encodes the water level states and transitions explicitly. The module stores the last stable water level state. On each clock, decode the new sensor input into a level. If the level changes, update the stored level and check if it is rising compared to the previous stored level. The supplemental flow valve (`dfr`) is asserted for one cycle whenever a rising transition is detected, indicating an increase in water level. Nominal flow valves are directly mapped from the stored level. On reset, the system initializes at the lowest water level state (below all sensors), opening all valves including `dfr`. This method explicitly captures and detects level transitions, ensuring stable outputs and clear control signals without glitches or timing hazards.
