Implement a state machine with explicitly defined water levels (4 levels) based on sensor patterns, with a registered state representing the previous water level rather than just the raw sensors. On each clock, sample sensor inputs and decode the current water level based on the pattern of sensors asserted:

- Level 3: Above s[2] (s=111)
- Level 2: Between s[2] and s[1] (s=011)
- Level 1: Between s[1] and s[0] (s=001)
- Level 0: Below s[0] (s=000)

If the sensor pattern does not match any of these valid states, treat it as the closest lower level (choose based on the highest matching sensor pattern below the current). This ensures stability even if sensor inputs glitch.

The previous water level is stored synchronously and reset sets it to Level 0 (lowest). Based on current water level and previous water level, determine if water level rose (current > previous) and accordingly assert dfr.

Nominal flow outputs are purely combinational from the current water level:

- Level 3 (above s[2]): fr0=0, fr1=0, fr2=0 (no flow)
- Level 2 (between s[2] and s[1]): fr0=1, fr1=0, fr2=0
- Level 1 (between s[1] and s[0]): fr0=1, fr1=1, fr2=0
- Level 0 (below s[0]): fr0=1, fr1=1, fr2=1

Reset synchronously initializes previous water level to 0 and forces all flow outputs high, consistent with spec. This cleanly models the water level states and transitions, ensuring correct dfr detection and flow outputs even if sensors temporarily glitch or have undefined patterns.
