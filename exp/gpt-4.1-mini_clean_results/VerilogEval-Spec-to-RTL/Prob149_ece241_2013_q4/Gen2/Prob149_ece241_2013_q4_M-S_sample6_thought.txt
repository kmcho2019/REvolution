On each clock:
- If reset, initialize outputs and previous level to represent the lowest water level (all valves open).
- Else if sensor input changed, update previous level to old current level, then update current level.
- Else keep previous level unchanged.

Supplemental flow valve (dfr) asserted only if the current water level is higher than the previous level (water rising).
