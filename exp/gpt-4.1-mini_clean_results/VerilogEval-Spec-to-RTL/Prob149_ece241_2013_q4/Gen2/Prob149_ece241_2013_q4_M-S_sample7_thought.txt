Simplify by tracking only the previous stable water level (from the last sensor change) in a register and the current decoded water level. Update the previous level when the sensor input changes, otherwise hold it. Decode the water level directly from the sensors combinationally. Outputs fr0, fr1, fr2 follow the table and depend only on the current level. The supplemental flow valve (dfr) is asserted if the current level is higher than the previous level, indicating rising water. On synchronous reset, set current and previous levels to the lowest level (no sensors asserted), asserting all outputs. This approach avoids timing ambiguities by updating previous_level on sensor changes only and simplifies output logic with straightforward combinational assignments.
