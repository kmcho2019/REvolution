Model the system as a simple finite state machine with four water level states (0 to 3), representing the sensor-detected water level ranges. On each clock, detect if the water level has changed by comparing the new level decoded from sensors with the stored current level. If changed, update a "previous_level" register with the old current level before moving to the new level. Use this to determine if the water level is rising (new level > previous_level). Generate nominal flow signals from the current level and assert the supplemental valve (dfr) only on rising transitions. Use a synchronous reset to initialize the FSM to the lowest water level (state 0) and all valves fully open.
