Refactor the design by explicitly separating the current and previous water levels as 2-bit numeric values rather than one-hot encoded states. Decode the sensor input combinationally to a numeric water level (0 to 3). Use a registered pair of these levels: `current_level` and `prev_level`. On each clock edge, if reset is active, initialize both levels to LOW (0). Otherwise, update `prev_level` to the old `current_level`, and `current_level` to the newly decoded level. The `dfr` output is asserted if `current_level > prev_level` (rising water level compared to previous). The nominal flow rate outputs `fr0`, `fr1`, and `fr2` are combinationally derived from `current_level` as per the specification. This approach avoids dealing with one-hot encoding state variables and leverages simple integer comparisons for clarity and correctness.
