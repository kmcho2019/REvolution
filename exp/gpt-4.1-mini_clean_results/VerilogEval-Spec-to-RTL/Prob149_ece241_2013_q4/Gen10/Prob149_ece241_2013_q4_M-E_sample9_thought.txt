Implement a two-stage pipeline of water level registers (prev_level and curr_level) updated synchronously on clk, with reset initializing to BELOW_S0 and all flow outputs high. Use a combinational function to decode the 3-bit sensor input into one of four discrete water levels. Generate nominal flow rate signals purely combinationally from curr_level, according to the given level-to-flow mapping. Determine dfr by a combinational comparator checking if the current water level is strictly higher than the previous water level, indicating rising water level, and latch this comparator output into a register on clk for stable synchronous output. This approach cleanly separates input decoding, nominal flow rate decoding, level comparison, and output generation, ensuring correctness and clear timing.
