Use a single registered water level state updated synchronously from sensor inputs. Keep the previous level in a register to detect rising water level transitions. Outputs fr2, fr1, fr0 are combinationally derived from the current level using direct sensor pattern matching, avoiding extra enums. The dfr output is registered and asserted only if the current water level is higher than the previous water level, indicating an increase. On reset, set the water level to below s0 (no sensors asserted) and assert all outputs. This approach minimizes state encoding complexity and relies on simple comparisons to detect rises, keeping the logic clear and concise.
