Implement a two-clock-cycle pipeline: first, sample and classify the sensor inputs into one of four water levels using a priority encoder approach, ensuring all valid sensor patterns are handled explicitly. Then, in the next cycle, compare the new level to the stored previous level to decide if the water level is rising, and assert the supplemental flow valve (dfr) accordingly. The nominal flow outputs (fr0, fr1, fr2) are combinational outputs directly driven by the current water level. On synchronous reset, initialize the previous level register to the lowest level (below s0) so that the outputs default to maximum flow (all valves open) and supplemental valve asserted. This design separates sensor decoding and level-change detection cleanly, improves robustness by explicitly handling all sensor patterns consistent with the spec, and makes the flow control logic clear and maintainable.
