Design a simple two-register approach to track the current water level and the previous water level using a compact 2-bit encoding of the sensor inputs, but encode the levels as a four-level numeric scale: 0 (below s[0]), 1 (between s[1] and s[0]), 2 (between s[2] and s[1]), 3 (above s[2]). Instead of using the previous water level state directly, latch the previous level on every clock cycle and compare to the current level to determine if the water level has risen.

The nominal flow outputs (fr0, fr1, fr2) map directly and combinationally from the current level according to the table. The supplemental flow valve (dfr) is synchronously asserted for exactly one cycle whenever a rise is detected in the water level compared to the last cycle, i.e., when current level > previous level. On reset, initialize the previous and current level to the lowest (below s[0]) and assert all flow valves including dfr.

This design uses only registered state for current and previous level and combinational decoding of nominal valves, which simplifies timing and reduces glitches. It also cleanly defines dfr as a one-cycle pulse on level rise, eliminating potential glitches or instability.
