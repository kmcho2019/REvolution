Use a simple 2-bit encoding of water levels in ascending order: 0 = Below s0, 1 = Between s1 and s0, 2 = Between s2 and s1, 3 = Above s2. On each clock, synchronously sample the sensor inputs to decode the current water level. Store the previous water level in a register to detect if the water level has risen (current > previous), in which case assert the supplemental flow valve (dfr). The nominal flow outputs (fr0, fr1, fr2) are purely combinational based on current water level per the specification. On reset, initialize the state to lowest water level with all outputs asserted, matching the requirement for a "long time low level" condition. This approach uses minimal registers and clean level encoding, ensuring correct and simple comparison for detecting rising water level.
