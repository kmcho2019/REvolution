Simplify the design by directly using a single registered water level state that updates synchronously on clk and reset. Keep the previous state in a register to detect if water level has risen (current state > previous state). Decode sensor inputs into one of four discrete water level states with a straightforward priority order. Outputs fr2, fr1, fr0 follow the nominal flow table based on current state. The supplemental flow valve dfr is asserted only if the water level rose compared to the previous state. The synchronous reset sets the state to BELOW (lowest level) with all outputs asserted. This simple FSM approach avoids combinational dependencies on registers, minimizing timing risks and glitches.
