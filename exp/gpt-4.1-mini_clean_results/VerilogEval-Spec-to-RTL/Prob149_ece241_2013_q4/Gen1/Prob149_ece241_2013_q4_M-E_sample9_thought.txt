Use a 2-stage register pipeline to remember the water level from two cycles ago and from the last cycle. By comparing the current water level to the level from two cycles ago, we can detect the direction of water level change across sensor boundaries reliably. This approach uses two registers: prev_level and prev_prev_level. At each clock, we shift in the current decoded level, so prev_level holds last cycle's level, and prev_prev_level holds the one before that. We compute dfr = 1 if current_level > prev_prev_level, indicating the water level has risen past at least one sensor threshold since two cycles ago.
