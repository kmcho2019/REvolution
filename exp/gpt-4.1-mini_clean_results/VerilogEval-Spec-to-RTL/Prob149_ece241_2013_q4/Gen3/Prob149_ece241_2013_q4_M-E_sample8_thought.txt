Use a state machine that tracks water level states directly and detects level transitions on each clock edge. Instead of storing sensor patterns, decode the current sensor input into a water level state each cycle. Then:

- Keep a register holding the previous water level state.
- On each clock edge, compare the current decoded level with the previous level.
- If current level > previous level, assert dfr to open the supplemental valve.
- Nominal flow valves (fr0, fr1, fr2) are combinational outputs from the current level.
- On synchronous reset, initialize the water level to BELOW_S0 (lowest level) and assert all flow valves.
- This approach avoids storing sensor patterns and prior sensor patterns, focusing on level transitions only.
- dfr is asserted for exactly one cycle when a rising water level is detected.
- This design is simple, clean, and strictly follows the specification by comparing only water levels.
