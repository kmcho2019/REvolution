Simplify by tracking only the previous and current decoded water levels and comparing them to decide dfr.

- On reset, set the water level to BELOW_S0 (no sensors asserted), and all flow valves and dfr asserted.
- On each clock edge, decode current sensor inputs to a water level.
- If the water level differs from the previous level, update previous level to old current level and current level to the new decoded level.
- dfr is asserted if the water level increased compared to previous level; else 0.
- Nominal flow valves are combinationally assigned from current level.
- This approach avoids complex pattern history and focuses only on level changes to decide dfr.
