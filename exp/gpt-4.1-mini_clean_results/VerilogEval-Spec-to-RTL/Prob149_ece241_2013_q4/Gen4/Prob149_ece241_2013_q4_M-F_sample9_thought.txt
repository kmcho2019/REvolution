To correctly track the previous water level before the last sensor change, we must register the current level every clock cycle and update the previous level only when a sensor change is detected. This means we keep both `prev_level` and `curr_level` as registered signals updated in sequence: on a sensor change, `prev_level` is set to the old `curr_level` (the one before the change), and `curr_level` is updated to the new level decoded from `s`. We initialize them on reset to represent the low water level with all valves open.
