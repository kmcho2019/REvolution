Use a simple register to hold the previous water level at each sensor pattern change. On every clock edge when the sensor pattern changes, update the previous level to the current level, then decode the new level from sensors. The supplemental flow valve dfr is set if the new level is strictly greater than the previous level, indicating a rising water level. Otherwise, dfr is cleared. On reset, initialize to the lowest water level (below s[0]) with all flows and dfr asserted, representing a long-term low level.

Nominal flow outputs (fr0, fr1, fr2) are combinationally generated based on the current water level per the problem table.

This simple sequential comparison and clean level decoding maintain correct behavior while reducing complexity.
