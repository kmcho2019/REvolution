Implement a simple finite state machine (FSM) representing the four discrete water level states defined by the sensors: BELOW_S0, BETWEEN_S1_S0, BETWEEN_S2_S1, and ABOVE_S2. On each clock edge, sample the sensor inputs and decode the current water level state.

Maintain a register holding the previous water level state. Detect transitions by comparing the new decoded state to the previous one.

- If the water level rose (current state > previous state), assert dfr (supplemental valve) for one cycle.
- Nominal flow valves fr0, fr1, fr2 are combinationally driven from the current state as per the problem’s flow table.
- Synchronous active-high reset initializes the FSM to the lowest water level (BELOW_S0) and asserts all nominal and supplemental valves.

This FSM approach clearly separates level detection, state transitions, and output logic, ensuring correct dfr assertion on rising water level and consistent nominal valve outputs.
