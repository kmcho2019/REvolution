To correctly generate dfr, track the two previous water levels along with the current one, since dfr depends on the transition relative to the level before the last sensor change. Use three registers: prev_prev_level, prev_level, and current_level. On each sensor level change, shift levels: prev_prev_level <= prev_level, prev_level <= current_level, current_level <= new_level. Then dfr is asserted if current_level > prev_prev_level (indicating rising compared to two levels ago). This aligns with "the level previous to the last sensor change." Nominal flows (fr0, fr1, fr2) remain combinational from current_level. Reset sets all outputs asserted and levels to LOW. This removes ambiguity in dfr timing and history, and ensures correct one-cycle synchronous dfr pulse on rising water level relative to the previous-to-last level.
