Simplify by directly decoding water level from sensors into a single encoded level signal. On each clock, store the previous level and current level registers. The supplemental flow valve (dfr) is asserted if the current level is higher than the previous level, indicating a rising water level. Reset initializes both current and previous levels to the lowest level (0) with all outputs asserted. Nominal flow outputs are combinationally generated from the current level. This approach avoids extra FSM complexity and keeps logic straightforward and clear.
