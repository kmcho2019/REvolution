Simplify the design by encoding water levels as an enumerated type based on sensor inputs, mapping only valid combinations to defined levels, defaulting invalid patterns to the lowest level. Maintain two registers: the previous stable level and the current stable level. On each clock cycle, if the water level changes, update these registers and set dfr to 1 if the new level is higher than the previous stable level, else 0. On reset, initialize to the lowest water level with all valves open and dfr asserted. The nominal flow outputs fr0, fr1, fr2 are purely combinationally derived from the current stable water level, per the specification, eliminating any complex conditional logic. This approach keeps the logic clean and straightforward while fully meeting the requirements.
