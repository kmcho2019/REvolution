Simplify the design by directly decoding the sensor inputs into the four defined water levels. Store the previous level in a register to compare with the current level on every clock cycle. If the level changes, update the previous level and assert `dfr` if the current level is higher (water level rising). This eliminates extra complexity and handles all valid sensor states explicitly. The nominal flow outputs (`fr0`, `fr1`, `fr2`) are directly combinational from the current water level. Reset initializes to the lowest water level with all valves open and `dfr` asserted as required.
