Implement a level decoder that assigns an explicit numeric water level from 0 (below s[0]) to 3 (above s[2]) based on sensor inputs. Use a small FSM with registers to remember the previous level, and on every clock, compare the current decoded level with the previous level to detect whether the level rose or fell. The dfr output is asserted only if the current level is higher than the previous level (an upward transition). Outputs fr0, fr1, fr2 are assigned combinationally based on the current level and follow the flow table given. On synchronous reset, initialize previous and current levels to 0 (lowest water level) and assert all outputs as per the requirements (fr0, fr1, fr2, and dfr all high). This approach cleanly separates level decoding from flow control, with explicit numeric levels for clarity, and maintains dfr state based on transitions, avoiding edge cases in previous approaches.
