In the given code, `prev_level` is updated at the same time as `stable_level`, but the order of assignment in a sequential block means `prev_level` is updated after `stable_level`, thus both might end up equal during the comparison, causing incorrect `dfr` behavior.

To fix this, we must carefully update `dfr` by comparing `new_level` against `prev_level` before updating `prev_level` and `stable_level`. Moreover, on reset, we initialize `prev_level` and `stable_level` to the lowest level (BELOW_S0) and assert all flow valves including `dfr`.

Finally, `dfr` is updated only when the level changes, otherwise it holds its previous value.
