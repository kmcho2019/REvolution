To fix the issues and improve the design, the main goal is to properly sequence the updates of current and previous water levels and derive `dfr` from stored state only, avoiding combinational timing hazards.

Improvements:
1. Update `prev_level` before updating `curr_level` inside the clocked always block, whenever the level changes, so the previous level reflects the last stable level.
2. Derive `dfr` purely from comparing stored `curr_level` and `prev_level` registers inside the synchronous block, not from combinational signals that change with inputs.
3. Register the nominal flow outputs (`fr0`, `fr1`, `fr2`) synchronously instead of combinationally to avoid glitches and improve timing.
4. On reset, initialize states and outputs to the "below lowest sensor" condition (all flows asserted).
5. Add a safe default state for unexpected sensor inputs as BEFORE (lowest level).

This ensures the `dfr` is asserted only on clock cycles when the level rises from a lower to a higher discrete water level, reflecting stable transitions without glitches.

This approach improves simulation correctness and timing robustness without increasing logic complexity.
