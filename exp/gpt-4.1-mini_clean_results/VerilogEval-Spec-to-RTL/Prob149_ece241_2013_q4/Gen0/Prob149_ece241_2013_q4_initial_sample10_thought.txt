The module monitors a 3-bit sensor input `s` that indicates water level at three vertical sensors: s[2] (highest), s[1] (middle), and s[0] (lowest). The outputs `fr0`, `fr1`, `fr2` represent nominal flow valves, and `dfr` is the supplemental flow valve.

Outputs are determined by the sensor pattern as follows:

- Above s[2] (all sensors asserted): no nominal flow valves on, dfr off.
- Between s[2] and s[1] (s[0], s[1] asserted, s[2] not asserted): fr0 on, dfr depends on previous level direction.
- Between s[1] and s[0] (only s[0] asserted): fr0 and fr1 on, dfr depends on previous level direction.
- Below s[0] (no sensors asserted): fr0, fr1, fr2 on, dfr depends on previous level direction.

We track the previous water level region to determine if the water level is rising or falling compared to the last level change. When the water level rises (previous level lower than current), we open the supplemental flow valve (`dfr` = 1). Otherwise, `dfr` = 0.

To implement this:

- Define an enumerated state for water level regions: 0=Below s[0], 1=Between s[1] and s[0], 2=Between s[2] and s[1], 3=Above s[2].
- On every clock, determine the current level from `s`.
- If the level changes from previous, determine if it is rising (new level > old level).
- Update previous level state.
- Drive outputs according to the table and dfr based on rising or not.
- On reset, initialize to below s[0] (lowest level) and outputs all asserted.

This approach uses synchronous reset and a single register to hold the previous level state.
