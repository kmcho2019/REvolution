Refactor the design by separating state registers (prev_level and curr_level) from combinational output logic. Use a synchronous always block for updating state registers only, and drive outputs through continuous assignments based on the stored curr_level and prev_level. This eliminates potential glitches and timing issues caused by mixing sequential state updates and output assignments in the same always block.

On every clock edge, if reset is asserted, initialize prev_level and curr_level to BELOW (lowest level). Otherwise, if the decoded_level changes, update prev_level to old curr_level and curr_level to new decoded_level.

Outputs fr0, fr1, fr2, and dfr are combinational signals derived from curr_level and prev_level:

- fr0, fr1, fr2 assigned per the nominal flow rate table based on curr_level.
- dfr asserted if curr_level > prev_level (water level rising).

This approach guarantees synchronous state updates and glitch-free outputs, matching the specification more robustly.
