To improve power and performance (PPA) further, the output can be registered instead of purely combinational. This reduces glitching on the output by making it synchronous to the clock, which generally reduces dynamic power caused by glitches and can improve timing on the output path.

Additionally, encoding the states as a one-hot style is not beneficial here since we only have two states; keeping a single bit state is minimal already.

We can keep the single-bit state encoding but register the output, thus making the output timing more predictable and potentially reducing switching noise.

This does not increase area significantly, as it adds one flip-flop for output but removes combinational glitch power. It's a common FSM optimization for better power and performance in synchronous designs.

Hence, implement a registered output driven by the current state.
