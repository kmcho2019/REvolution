To optimize PPA further while keeping the design minimal and clear, I will merge the next_state logic into the sequential always block to save on logic and avoid the extra combinational block, reducing area and possibly improving timing. Also, the output will be registered to create a full Moore FSM with all outputs synchronous, potentially improving timing by avoiding combinational output logic. This can reduce glitching and may help in power by reducing glitches on output. The state encoding remains binary with a single bit. The reset remains synchronous active-high. These changes slightly increase logic in the sequential block but reduce combinational logic and improve overall balance.
