The FSM is already minimal with just two states and simple logic. To improve PPA slightly, I will use one-hot encoding which, for two states, means using a single bit for each state, enabling simple logic and potentially better timing and power. This approach can simplify next-state logic and possibly reduce glitches since outputs are registered. Additionally, I'll register the output (instead of a continuous assign) to reduce glitches and minimize power by preventing output glitches on state transitions. This synchronous output update aligns well with the Moore machine concept. The state register and output register share the clock domain and reset. Overall, this approach provides a slight timing and power benefit for such a small FSM.
