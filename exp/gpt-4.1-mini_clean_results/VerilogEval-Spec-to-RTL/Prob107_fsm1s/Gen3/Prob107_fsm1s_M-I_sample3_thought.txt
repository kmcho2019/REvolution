To improve PPA, focus on reducing logic complexity and power. This can be done by using one-hot encoding for the FSM states, which simplifies next-state logic to simple AND/OR gates and often yields better timing and lower power in FPGAs or certain ASIC flows. Also, register the output to avoid glitches and reduce dynamic power. Using one-hot encoding removes the need for case statements and eases combinational logic, improving timing. Keep synchronous active-high reset. Overall, this reduces the critical path, switching power, and area by simplifying combinational logic and eliminating encoders/decoders.
