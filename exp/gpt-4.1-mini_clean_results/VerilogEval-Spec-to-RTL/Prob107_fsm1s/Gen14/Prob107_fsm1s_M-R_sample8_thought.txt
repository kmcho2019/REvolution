I will refactor the FSM by explicitly separating the next state combinational logic from the registered state update logic. This involves introducing a next_state signal computed using a combinational always block (or continuous assignment), then registered into the state on the clock edge. This separation improves readability and helps synthesis tools optimize timing by clearly delineating combinational and sequential logic. I will keep the output as a continuous assignment to reflect the Moore FSM output dependence on state.

The synchronous reset remains active-high and synchronous to keep the same behavior.

This structure is a canonical FSM style used widely in design for clarity and potential optimization.
