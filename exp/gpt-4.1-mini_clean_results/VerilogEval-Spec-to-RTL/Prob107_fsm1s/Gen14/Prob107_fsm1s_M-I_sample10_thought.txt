To optimize for better performance and clarity, I will separate the next-state combinational logic from the state register update, introducing a next_state signal. This separation often helps synthesis tools optimize the FSM timing by isolating the combinational path.

For power optimization, I will add a clock enable signal so that the state register only updates when the next state differs from the current state, reducing unnecessary toggling.

The synchronous active-high reset remains as required.

The output remains a pure Moore output assigned directly from the state, ensuring glitch-free output.

This approach will slightly increase area due to the next_state register and clock enable logic but can improve timing and reduce dynamic power by minimizing clocked transitions.
