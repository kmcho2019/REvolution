Use a one-hot encoding for the two states with two state flip-flops: state_B and state_A. On synchronous reset, state_B is set high and state_A low. Transitions set the corresponding state flip-flop high and the other low. Output logic directly maps from the state_B flip-flop (since out=1 only in B). This approach uses explicit one-hot style registers and parallel logic, differing from the single-bit encoding.
