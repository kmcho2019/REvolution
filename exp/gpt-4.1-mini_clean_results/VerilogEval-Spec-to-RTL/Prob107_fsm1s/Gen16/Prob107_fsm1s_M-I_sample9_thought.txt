To optimize the given FSM for better power and potentially area, apply clock gating to the state register so that it only toggles when a state change is needed. This reduces unnecessary flip-flop toggling and dynamic power consumption without affecting functionality. The one-bit state encoding and synchronous reset are retained. Clock gating is implemented with an enable signal derived from next-state comparison to the current state, and a gated clock generated by an enable clock gating cell (simple AND-based gate here for illustration).
