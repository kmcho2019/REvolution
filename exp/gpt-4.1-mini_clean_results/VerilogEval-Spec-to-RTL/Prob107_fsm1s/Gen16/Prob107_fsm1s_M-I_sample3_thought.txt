To improve the PPA metrics based on the given feedback, I will remove the conditional check `if (state != next_state)` inside the clocked always block. This simplification will enable the synthesis tool to generate simpler clock enable logic or avoid a gated clock, potentially improving timing (tns and wns) and simplifying the register implementation. Although this might slightly increase switching activity (power), the overall minimal FSM logic keeps power very low. The state encoding and output logic remain the same to keep area minimal. This change balances timing improvement with negligible area and power impact.
