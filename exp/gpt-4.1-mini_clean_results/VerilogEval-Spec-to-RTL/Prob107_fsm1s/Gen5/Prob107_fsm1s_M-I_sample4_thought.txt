To optimize the design for better power and performance without increasing area, I will separate the output logic from the next-state logic. Since output is purely a function of the current state in a Moore machine, it can be assigned continuously as a simple combinational assignment from the state register, reducing unnecessary toggling in the combinational next-state logic block.

Additionally, I will use nonblocking assignments only in the sequential block to ensure clear timing semantics and blocking assignments in combinational logic for clarity.

This separation can help the synthesis tool optimize output gating and reduce switching, potentially improving power and timing while keeping area minimal.

State encoding and synchronous reset remain unchanged as they are already optimal.

No case statements will be used for simplicity, and states remain 1-bit encoded for minimal flip-flops.
