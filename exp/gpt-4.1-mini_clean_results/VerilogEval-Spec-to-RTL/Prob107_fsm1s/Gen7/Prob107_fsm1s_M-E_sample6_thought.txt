Implement the FSM by explicitly defining state encoding with localparams for clarity and extensibility. Use two always blocks: one synchronous block for state updates with synchronous active-high reset, and one combinational block to compute the next state based on current state and input. The output is assigned directly from the state in a separate assign statement (Moore output). This modular approach separates combinational and sequential logic cleanly, enhancing readability and ease of modification for larger FSMs.
