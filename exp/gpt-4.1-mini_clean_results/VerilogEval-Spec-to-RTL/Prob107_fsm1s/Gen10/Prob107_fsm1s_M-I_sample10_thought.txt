This trades off one additional flip-flop but can improve timing by simplifying the next-state logic and potentially improving LUT mapping in FPGA or standard cells in ASIC. Maintain synchronous active-high reset on both state flip-flops. This approach slightly increases area (one more flip-flop), but can significantly reduce logic depth and critical path delay, improving timing and possibly dynamic power due to reduced logic switching complexity.
