To optimize the FSM for better PPA, I will convert the next_state logic from an always @(*) block with a reg variable to a continuous assignment with a wire. This reduces unnecessary register inference and can help synthesis tools optimize combinational logic more effectively. The state register remains a single bit with non-blocking assignment in a synchronous always block, and output logic remains a simple continuous assignment. This approach keeps the code clear and minimal while potentially improving area and power slightly by avoiding a temporary register for next_state.
