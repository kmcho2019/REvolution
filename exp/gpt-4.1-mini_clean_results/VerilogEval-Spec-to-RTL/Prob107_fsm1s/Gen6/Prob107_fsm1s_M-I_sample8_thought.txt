To optimize the original design further for power and performance while maintaining minimal area:

- Assign the output as a continuous assignment directly from the registered state (since output depends only on state). This avoids recomputing output in every combinational cycle and reduces switching on the output signal.
- Separate next_state combinational logic from output assignment clearly.
- Keep the state update logic synchronous with nonblocking assignment.
- Use blocking assignments in the next_state combinational always block.
- This separation reduces logic toggling on output, potentially lowering power.
- The single-bit state encoding and simple logic remain unchanged to keep area minimal.

Overall, output signal toggling is minimized and timing is simplified for potentially better PPA.
