The original design is already minimal and clean with optimal state encoding, clear separation of next-state logic, and synchronous reset. To improve PPA, especially aiming for better power and timing, I propose to optimize the combinational logic by avoiding the case statement and using straightforward conditional expressions. This reduces logic depth slightly by leveraging simple multiplexers instead of case decoding.

Also, I will directly assign the next_state based on the current state and input in a single expression, which can help synthesis tools generate minimal mux logic, potentially improving timing and power.

Finally, I'll keep the synchronous reset and Moore output as before, ensuring glitch-free output.

This approach maintains functionality while possibly yielding slightly better synthesis results by simplifying combinational logic and reducing gate count and delay.
