The current design is already minimal with a one-bit state and simple logic. To slightly improve power and area, I will remove the conditional state update inside the always block and instead use a combinational next_state signal. This approach allows better synthesis optimization and might improve timing by isolating combinational logic from the sequential block. Also, I will replace the ternary operator with explicit boolean expressions to make the logic clearer for synthesis tools. Finally, I will directly assign out as the inverse of the state signal, as before, to keep output logic minimal.
