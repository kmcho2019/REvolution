I will implement the TopModule using a behavioral approach leveraging Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators to perform the addition in one step. Then, to detect overflow in two's complement addition, I will use the standard overflow condition that checks the signs of operands and result: overflow occurs when the inputs have the same sign but the result has a different sign. This approach is concise and synthesizable, and it avoids manual carry propagation logic. 

Specifically:
1. Declare inputs and outputs as specified.
2. Use an intermediate 9-bit wire to hold the sum of inputs extended to 9 bits to detect carry-out.
3. Assign the lower 8 bits of the sum to the output s.
4. Detect overflow by checking the sign bits of the inputs and output as (a[7] == b[7]) and (s[7] != a[7]).
5. This method results in a small and clean design without hierarchical modules, potentially improving area and ease of verification while maintaining correct functionality.
