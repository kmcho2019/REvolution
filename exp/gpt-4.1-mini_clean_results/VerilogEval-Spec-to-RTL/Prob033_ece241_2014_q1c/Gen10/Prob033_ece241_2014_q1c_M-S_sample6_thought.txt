To simplify the design while maintaining correct 8-bit signed addition and overflow detection, I will implement a straightforward ripple-carry adder without hierarchical carry-lookahead.

This approach uses:
- Direct bitwise sum computation with carry propagation from LSB to MSB.
- A simple carry chain, calculated sequentially.
- Overflow detection using XOR of the carry into and out of the MSB.

Though this results in a longer carry chain delay than carry-lookahead, it drastically reduces logic complexity and code size, ideal for simpler or smaller designs where performance is less critical.

This method still correctly handles 2's complement addition and overflow detection without any intermediate propagate/generate signals or grouping logic.
