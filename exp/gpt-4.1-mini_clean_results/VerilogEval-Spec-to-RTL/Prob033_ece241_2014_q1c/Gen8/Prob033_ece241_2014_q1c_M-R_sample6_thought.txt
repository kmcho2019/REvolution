Refactor the 8-bit signed adder to use the built-in Verilog addition operator for clarity and compactness, replacing the chain of FullAdder modules with a single addition operation. The overflow detection remains based on the sign bits of inputs and the result: overflow occurs if the sign of the sum differs from the inputs when the inputs have the same sign. This method avoids explicit carry chains and is more concise while maintaining the same functional correctness.
