To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will implement the 8-bit adder and overflow detection using straightforward ripple-carry addition. I will use Verilog's built-in addition operator to sum the two 8-bit inputs into a 9-bit intermediate wire that captures the carry out. The lower 8 bits of this sum will be assigned to the output s. Overflow for signed addition in two's complement is detected by comparing the carry into and out of the MSB, which can be done by checking if the sign of inputs and result violate the rules: overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the sign bit of the sum. This approach reduces complexity by avoiding explicit propagate/generate signals and carry chains, relying instead on Verilog's arithmetic and simple boolean overflow logic.
