To improve PPA, I will optimize the carry-lookahead logic by implementing a hierarchical carry computation that reduces gate levels and fan-out compared to the fully parallel carry-lookahead. This approach computes group propagate and generate signals for the lower nibble (bits 0-3) and upper nibble (bits 4-7), thus reducing the number of logic levels in carry generation. It offers a good tradeoff between speed and complexity for an 8-bit adder.

Specifically:
1. Compute bitwise propagate and generate signals.
2. Compute group propagate and generate signals for two 4-bit groups.
3. Calculate carry for bit 4 using the group signals, then ripple carry within the groups.
4. This reduces the carry chain length from 8 to 4 bits per group, improving delay and reducing power due to lower fan-out.
5. Overflow detection remains the XOR of carry into and out of MSB.
6. Maintain clear modular code using assign statements, avoiding complex nested generate loops.
   
This approach slightly reduces power and area by minimizing the number of signals switching and gate counts while maintaining fast carry resolution and clear, scalable code.
