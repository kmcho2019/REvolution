To optimize the original ripple carry adder implementation for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will replace the ripple carry structure with a simple carry lookahead adder (CLA) for 8 bits. The CLA reduces the carry propagation delay by computing carry generate and propagate signals for groups of bits, enabling faster carry calculation. This significantly improves timing (lower delay and higher maximum frequency).

From a power perspective, the CLA reduces the number of sequential carry logic toggling, potentially lowering dynamic power. Area-wise, a CLA can use more gates for carry generation but eliminates deep carry chains, which can yield area benefits when targeting modern standard cell libraries or FPGAs that optimize carry logic.

The design remains fully combinational and modular, and overflow detection is still performed by XORing carry-in and carry-out of the most significant bit carry. The implementation uses explicit generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for each bit and carry signals calculated in parallel.

This approach balances complexity and PPA improvements over the ripple carry adder.
