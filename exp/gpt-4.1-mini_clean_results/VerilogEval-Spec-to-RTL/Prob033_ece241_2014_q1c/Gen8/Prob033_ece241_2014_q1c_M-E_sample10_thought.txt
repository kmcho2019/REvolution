Instead of implementing a carry-lookahead adder, I will use Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic to perform the addition and overflow detection directly. This approach leverages the synthesis tool's ability to optimize the signed addition internally, possibly generating efficient hardware without manually specifying carry logic. For overflow detection, I will compare the sign bits of inputs and result: overflow occurs if both inputs have the same sign but the result has a different sign. This method is a classic overflow detection technique for 2's complement addition and is straightforward to implement. This approach is simple, concise, and relies on the synthesis tool's optimization, potentially reducing design complexity and improving maintainability.
