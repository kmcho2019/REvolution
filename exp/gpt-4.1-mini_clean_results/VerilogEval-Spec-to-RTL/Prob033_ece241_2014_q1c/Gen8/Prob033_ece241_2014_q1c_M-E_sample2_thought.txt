I will implement the 8-bit adder using a simple signed addition with the built-in Verilog '+' operator to leverage the synthesis tool's built-in adder optimizations. Then, I will detect overflow by comparing the signs of the operands and the result: overflow occurs if the sign bits of inputs are the same but differ from the sign bit of the sum. This approach simplifies the design and lets the synthesis tool decide the optimal adder implementation. It ensures correctness with minimal manual logic. The overflow detection is done purely combinationally based on sign bit comparisons.
