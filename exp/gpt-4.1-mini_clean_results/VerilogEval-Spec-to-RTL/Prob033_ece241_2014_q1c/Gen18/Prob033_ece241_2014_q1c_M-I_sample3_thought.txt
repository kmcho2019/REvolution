To improve the given design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), the primary opportunity is to reduce carry propagation delay and logic duplication by implementing a full 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) instead of two 4-bit CLAs chained by carry-out/carry-in signals.

The main improvements are:
1. Use a single 8-bit CLA block that calculates propagate and generate signals for all bits.
2. Compute carries for all 8 bits in parallel using carry-lookahead logic, which reduces carry chain delay and increases maximum operating frequency.
3. Compute the sum bits directly from the propagate signals and carries.
4. Compute overflow as the XOR of carry into MSB (bit 7) and carry out of MSB (bit 7), consistent with signed overflow detection.
5. This will slightly increase combinational logic complexity, but significantly reduce critical path delay and potentially area by removing duplicated logic and inter-block carry chaining.
6. Power should improve as well due to shorter logic paths and reduced glitching from the ripple carry chain.

Overall, this 8-bit CLA module combines all 8 bits in one block, producing the sum and overflow in a more efficient way while maintaining code clarity and modularity.
