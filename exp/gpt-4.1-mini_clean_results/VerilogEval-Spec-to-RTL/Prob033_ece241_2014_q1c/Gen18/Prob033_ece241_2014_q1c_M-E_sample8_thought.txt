Implement the 8-bit adder using a simple ripple carry architecture for clarity and minimal resource usage. Each bit sum and carry-out is computed sequentially from least significant bit to most significant bit using full adders instantiated as a chain. Overflow detection is done by XOR of the carry into and out of the most significant bit, which naturally comes from the ripple carry chain. This approach is straightforward and easy to understand, ideal for small bit-width adders, trading off some speed for simplicity and compactness.
