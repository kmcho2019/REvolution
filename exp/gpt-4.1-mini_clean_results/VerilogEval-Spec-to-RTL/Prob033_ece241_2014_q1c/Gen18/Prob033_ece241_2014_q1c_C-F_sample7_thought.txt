The fusion strategy combines the clarity and explicitness of the first example's modular fixed 8-bit CLA adder with the performance insight from the second example, but further improves timing by implementing true parallel carry lookahead logic.

Specifically, instead of a simple ripple carry chain through c[1] to c[8], I will compute group propagate (PG) and group generate (GG) signals for the entire 8-bit block, which enables parallel calculation of all carry bits with minimal delay.

This uses the recursive carry lookahead formula:

c[i+1] = g[i] | (p[i] & c[i])

but instead of cascading sequentially, I will expand carries in terms of the initial carry-in and the propagate/generate signals to produce all carries in a few logic levels.

This parallel carry computation reduces the critical path compared to sequential assigns, improving performance.

The sum bits remain the XOR of propagate and carry-in bits.

Overflow detection remains the XOR of the carry into and carry out of the MSB (bit 7).

The modular structure is retained: a dedicated EightBitCLAAdder module feeding the TopModule.

This fusion thus optimizes for performance (parallel carry generation), clarity (explicit propagate/generate signals), and correctness (accurate overflow detection).

Area impact is modest since only 8 bits and the carry logic is explicit; power benefits from reduced switching in carry chain logic.

Overall, this approach yields an efficient and clean fixed 8-bit CLA with optimized parallel carry computation for superior PPA.
