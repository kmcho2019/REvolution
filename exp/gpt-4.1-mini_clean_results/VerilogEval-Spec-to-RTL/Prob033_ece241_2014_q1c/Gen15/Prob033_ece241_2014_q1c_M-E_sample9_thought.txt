Implement the 8-bit signed adder using a straightforward ripple carry adder at the bit level without hierarchical blocks. Calculate the sum bit-by-bit with full adders chained through carry signals.

For overflow detection, use the classic signed overflow formula based on the sign bits of inputs and result:
overflow = (a[7] & b[7] & ~s[7]) | (~a[7] & ~b[7] & s[7])

This expression directly detects when adding two positive numbers yields a negative result or two negative numbers yields a positive result, signaling overflow.

This approach sacrifices the speed advantage of carry-lookahead for simplicity and clear logic, avoiding modular CLAs and explicit carry propagate/generate signals. It is easy to understand and implement, suitable for educational and simple design contexts.
