To improve the PPA metrics over the original two 4-bit CLA blocks chained approach, I will implement a single 8-bit Carry Lookahead Adder (CLA). This eliminates the inter-block carry chaining delay and reduces area by sharing generate and propagate computations across all 8 bits. The single 8-bit CLA calculates all carries in parallel, decreasing critical path delay and potentially lowering power and area.

The overflow detection logic remains the same: overflow occurs if the carry into the most significant bit differs from the carry out of the most significant bit.

This unified 8-bit CLA design should improve timing (lower delay, better WNS/TNS), reduce area (less duplicated logic), and modestly reduce power by removing redundant logic and signals.
