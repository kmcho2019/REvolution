Implement a single TopModule that performs 8-bit 2's complement addition using carry-lookahead logic internally without instantiating a separate adder module. Compute propagate, generate, and carry signals once, derive the sum and overflow from these signals directly, thus removing duplicated carry chain calculations and redundant propagate/generate signals. Overflow is detected by XOR of carry into and carry out of the MSB. This simplification reduces code duplication, power, and area, while maintaining performance and correctness.
