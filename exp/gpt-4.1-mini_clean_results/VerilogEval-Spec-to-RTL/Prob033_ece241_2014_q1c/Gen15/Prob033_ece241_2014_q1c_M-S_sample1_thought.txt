To simplify the design while maintaining correct 8-bit signed addition and overflow detection, I will implement a straightforward ripple carry adder using Verilog's built-in addition operator. This avoids manual carry logic and the need for a separate parameterizable CLA module, thus reducing code complexity.

Overflow can still be accurately detected by XORing the carry into and out of the MSB. Since Verilog's built-in addition returns a sum wider than the inputs, we can extract these carry bits by looking at the 9th bit of the addition result for the carry out, and the carry into MSB can be derived from the bits of the sum.

This approach keeps the design concise and fully functional with minimal logic, suitable for many typical applications where simplicity is preferred over maximum speed.
