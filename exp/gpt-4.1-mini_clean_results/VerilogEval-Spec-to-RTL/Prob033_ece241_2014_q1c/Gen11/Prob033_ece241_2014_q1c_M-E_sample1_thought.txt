Use the built-in Verilog addition operator to perform the 8-bit signed addition in a single step and then detect overflow by examining the sign bits of the inputs and the result. Overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the result's sign bit. This approach simplifies the design, reduces code complexity, and leverages the synthesis tool's optimized adder implementation. It avoids the ripple carry adder's carry chain delay by delegating addition to the tool, potentially yielding better PPA. Overflow is detected by checking (a[7] == b[7]) & (s[7] != a[7]).
