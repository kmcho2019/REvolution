Implement a single 8-bit adder using straightforward bitwise addition with carry propagation via a ripple-carry style approach. Since the design is for 8 bits only, a simple ripple carry adder is efficient and clear enough without the complexity of carry-lookahead blocks. Compute the sum by adding a and b with an initial carry-in of zero. For overflow detection of signed 2’s complement addition, use the condition that overflow occurs if the carry into the most significant bit (bit 7) differs from the carry out of the most significant bit. This can be done by computing carries for all bits, extracting the carry into the MSB (bit 7) and the carry out of the MSB (bit 8). This design is simpler, purely combinational, and easy to understand and maintain.
