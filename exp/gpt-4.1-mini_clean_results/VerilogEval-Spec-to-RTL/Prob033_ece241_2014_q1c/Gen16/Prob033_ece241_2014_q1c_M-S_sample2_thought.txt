To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will use Verilog's built-in addition operator to add the two 8-bit signed inputs directly, which inherently produces the 8-bit sum. For overflow detection in signed addition, overflow occurs when the sign bits of the inputs are the same but different from the sign bit of the result. This can be easily detected by XORing the sign bit of the sum with the sign bits of the inputs. This approach significantly reduces complexity by removing manual carry lookahead logic and intermediate wires, while preserving correct signed overflow detection.
