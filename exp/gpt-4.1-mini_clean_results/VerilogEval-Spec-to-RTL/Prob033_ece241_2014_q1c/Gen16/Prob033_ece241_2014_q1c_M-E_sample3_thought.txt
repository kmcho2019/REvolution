Instead of using a carry-lookahead adder, I will implement an 8-bit ripple carry adder (RCA) with a dedicated overflow detection logic. This design uses a chain of full adders, each computing sum and carry out from its inputs. Although ripple carry adders have longer delay than CLAs, they are simpler and compact.

For overflow detection in two's complement addition:
- Overflow occurs if the carry into the most significant bit (MSB) differs from the carry out of the MSB.
- Another common method is to check if the sign bits of inputs are the same, but the sign bit of the result differs.

I will implement a ripple carry adder from eight full adder modules connected serially. The full adder module uses the classic carry and sum logic.

This approach highlights a clear modular design with explicit full adder stages and straightforward overflow detection. It is conceptually simple and easy to verify.

Summary:
- Implement 1-bit full adder module.
- Chain 8 full adders for 8-bit addition.
- Compute overflow as XOR of carry into and carry out of MSB, which are the carry out from bit 6 and carry out from bit 7 respectively.
