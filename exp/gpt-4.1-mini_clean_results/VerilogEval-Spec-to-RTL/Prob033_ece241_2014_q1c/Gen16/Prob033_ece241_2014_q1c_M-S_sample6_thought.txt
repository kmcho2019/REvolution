To simplify the design while maintaining correct 8-bit addition and overflow detection for 2's complement numbers, I will implement the adder and overflow logic combinationally in one module without hierarchical submodules.

- Use simple bitwise addition with the Verilog '+' operator, which synthesizes efficiently in most tools.

- Detect overflow by checking if the sign of the result is inconsistent with the inputs' sign: overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same, but the sign bit of sum is different.

This approach avoids generating and managing carry vectors explicitly, reducing code complexity and improving readability while producing correct outputs.
