Instead of implementing a carry lookahead adder, I will use a simple ripple carry adder approach with explicit carry propagation. This implementation is simpler and more modular, chaining eight 1-bit full adders in series. While this approach typically has a longer carry propagation delay, it is conceptually clear and straightforward for a small 8-bit adder.

Overflow detection in 2's complement addition occurs when the carry into the most significant bit differs from the carry out of the most significant bit. Using this fact, the overflow signal will be computed as the XOR of these two carry signals.

This modular approach also aids in ease of verification and could be a basis for pedagogical explanation or implementation in resource-constrained environments.

The design steps:
- Implement a 1-bit full adder module with inputs a, b, cin; outputs sum and cout.
- Chain eight 1-bit full adders to form the 8-bit ripple carry adder in TopModule.
- Capture the carry into MSB and carry out of MSB to determine overflow.
