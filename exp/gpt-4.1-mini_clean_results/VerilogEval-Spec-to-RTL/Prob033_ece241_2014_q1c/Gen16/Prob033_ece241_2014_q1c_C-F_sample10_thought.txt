To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will implement an 8-bit carry lookahead adder with a generate loop for scalable carry computation (from Example 1) while maintaining explicit internal propagate/generate signals and carry signals for clarity (from Example 2). 

Specifically, I will use a generate-for loop to compute carries based on propagate and generate signals, which keeps the code concise and scalable while still clear. This improves readability and modularity, and eases future scaling beyond 8 bits. 

For overflow detection, I keep the standard XOR of carry into MSB and carry out of MSB signals to correctly detect signed overflow in two's complement addition.

The TopModule instantiates this internal adder module, keeping clear separation of concerns. Unused outputs like the final carry out are kept internal and not exposed externally, saving area and simplifying the interface.

This fusion provides a clean, modular, synthesizable design that is scalable and clear, with efficient overflow detection and minimized interface signals.
