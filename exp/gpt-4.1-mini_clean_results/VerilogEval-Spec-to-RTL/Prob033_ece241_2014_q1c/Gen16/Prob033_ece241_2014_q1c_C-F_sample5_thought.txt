Fuse the hierarchical modularity and clarity of the 4-bit CLA blocks from Example 1 with the fully parallel carry lookahead approach of Example 2 to create a balanced 8-bit CLA with group propagate/generate signals for faster carry computation and cleaner logic.

Strategy:
1. Implement a 4-bit CLA block module that outputs sum, carry out, and group propagate/generate signals.
2. At the top-level, instantiate two 4-bit CLA blocks for the lower and upper 4-bit halves.
3. Compute the top-level carries between the two blocks using group propagate/generate signals, applying carry-lookahead logic at the 8-bit level with only two groups.
4. This hierarchy reduces carry logic complexity, limits fanout, and reduces carry propagation delay by computing inter-block carry in parallel.
5. Sum bits are concatenated from the two blocks.
6. Overflow detection uses the XOR of carry into and carry out of MSB (from upper block).
7. This fusion provides good timing by parallel carry lookahead at block and group level, modularity, and area efficiency by reusing the 4-bit block logic.
