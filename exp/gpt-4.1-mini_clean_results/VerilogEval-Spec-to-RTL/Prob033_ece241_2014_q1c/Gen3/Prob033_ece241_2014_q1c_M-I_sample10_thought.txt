The original design is already efficient in terms of area and power as it uses direct addition and a minimal overflow check. To further optimize for area and possibly power, we can eliminate the signed wire declarations since synthesis tools infer signed arithmetic from the addition operator when the operands are vectors. Additionally, we can compute the overflow using only the inputs and the sum sign bits without intermediate signals, making the design slightly simpler and potentially allowing the synthesis tool to optimize the logic better. These small changes reduce the number of assigned signals and may marginally reduce area and power by simplifying the logic cone, while maintaining the exact same functional behavior.
