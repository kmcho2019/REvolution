Instead of using the built-in '+' operator for addition, we can explicitly implement an 8-bit ripple carry adder using full adder logic. This approach decomposes the addition into individual bit-wise sums and carries, making the addition structure explicit. We then detect overflow by examining the carry into and out of the most significant bit, since in 2's complement addition, overflow occurs if these two carries differ. This manual full adder implementation exposes the addition logic hierarchy for possible further optimization or instrumentation, and demonstrates a fundamental hardware design technique.
