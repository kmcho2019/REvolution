Instead of sign-extending and performing a 9-bit addition, add the 8-bit inputs as unsigned values producing a 9-bit sum to capture any carry-out. Then, compute overflow by analyzing the carry into and carry out of the sign bit (bit 7). Overflow in 2's complement addition occurs when these two carries differ.

Specifically:
- Perform unsigned addition: sum = a + b, with a 9-bit result to include carry-out.
- The output s is the lower 8 bits of sum.
- Detect overflow by XORing the carry into the sign bit and carry out of the sign bit.
  The carry into the sign bit is the carry generated by bit 6 addition.
  The carry out is the MSB of the 9-bit sum.

This method avoids sign-extension and uses carry signals to detect overflow, leveraging hardware addition properties.
