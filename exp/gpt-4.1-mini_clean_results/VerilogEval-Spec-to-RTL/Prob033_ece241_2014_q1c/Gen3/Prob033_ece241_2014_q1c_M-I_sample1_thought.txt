To optimize for area and power, replace the manual ripple-carry adder logic with a direct use of Verilog's built-in addition operator. This lets synthesis tools generate efficient adders and remove explicit carry wires, reducing net switching and wiring overhead. The overflow detection remains the same, using the sign bits of inputs and output, which is a simpler and more common overflow condition check for 2's complement addition: overflow occurs if the sign of the sum differs from the sign of inputs when inputs had the same sign. This approach simplifies logic and typically yields better synthesis optimization in area and power.
