To improve the existing carry-lookahead adder, I'll replace the sequential carry computation loop with a parallel prefix structure (Kogge-Stone style) for carry generation. This reduces the carry propagation delay significantly by computing carries in a tree-like fashion, leading to better performance (lower worst negative slack and faster timing). The logic will still be fully combinational and maintain the same interface and functionality.

This approach:
- Uses generate and propagate signals as before.
- Constructs higher-level group generate and propagate signals in stages.
- Calculates the carry bits in log2(N) stages instead of N sequential steps.
- Improves the critical path by enabling parallel carry computation.
- Slightly increases wiring and logic complexity but gains timing.

Overflow detection remains the XOR of the MSB carry-in and carry-out.

This design aims at improving performance while keeping power and area reasonable.
