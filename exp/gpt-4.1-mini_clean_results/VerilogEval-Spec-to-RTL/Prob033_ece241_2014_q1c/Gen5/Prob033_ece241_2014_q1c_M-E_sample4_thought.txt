Implement the 8-bit addition using Verilog's built-in '+' operator to leverage the synthesizer's optimized adder inference, simplifying the design. For overflow detection, compute overflow by comparing the carry into and out of the most significant bit: overflow occurs if these carries differ. This method uses carry signals explicitly derived from the addition result and the inputs, giving a structural insight without manually building the ripple carry adder. This approach balances simplicity and structural transparency, relying on the well-known carry-out and carry-in overflow condition for two's complement addition.
