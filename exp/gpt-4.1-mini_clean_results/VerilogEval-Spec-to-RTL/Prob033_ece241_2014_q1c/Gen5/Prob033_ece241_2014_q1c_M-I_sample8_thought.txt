To improve performance and reduce critical path delay, I will replace the simple ripple-style carry computation loop with a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure. This approach computes group generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for the lower and upper 4-bit halves, allowing faster carry resolution for the most significant bits and reducing carry propagation delay.

This results in shorter carry paths compared to a fully serial chain in the generate-for loop. It also reduces power due to fewer intermediate signals switching on the critical path and potentially smaller area from more compact carry logic.

The sum and overflow detection remain the same: sum bits computed from propagate and carry, and overflow detected as the XOR of the carry-in and carry-out to the MSB.

This compromise keeps the code scalable and efficient for 8-bit width, balancing readability and improved PPA metrics.
