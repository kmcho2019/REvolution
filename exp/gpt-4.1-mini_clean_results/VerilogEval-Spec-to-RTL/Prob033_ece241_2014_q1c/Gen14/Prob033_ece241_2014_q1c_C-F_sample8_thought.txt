Fuse the simplicity and modularity of the ripple carry full adder chain (Example 2) with the efficient carry-lookahead logic (Example 1) by implementing a single 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) that performs addition in one block.

This approach gains the speed advantage of carry-lookahead logic over ripple carry and removes the overhead of chaining multiple CLA blocks. By using generate and propagate signals for all 8 bits and computing carries via carry-lookahead equations, we minimize carry propagation delay.

The overflow detection remains the same standard signed overflow check: overflow = carry into MSB ^ carry out of MSB.

This design:
1. Improves performance by eliminating carry ripple across blocks.
2. Maintains modular and clear generate/propagate logic.
3. Uses a single module for clarity and efficiency.
4. Keeps power and area reasonably low by balanced logic depth and no redundant carry chaining.

The TopModule instantiates this 8-bit CLA adder, which outputs sum, final carry-out, and carry into MSB for overflow detection.

This fusion leverages the clean, modular CLA from Example 1 but extends it to 8 bits for full carry-lookahead speed, avoiding the ripple carry delay of Example 2 and the extra inter-block chaining of Example 1.
