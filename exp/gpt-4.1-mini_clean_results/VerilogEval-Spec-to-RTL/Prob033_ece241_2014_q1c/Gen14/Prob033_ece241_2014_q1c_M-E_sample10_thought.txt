Implement the 8-bit signed adder directly with a single Verilog expression for summation and detect overflow by comparing the sign bits of inputs and the sum. Overflow occurs if the inputs have the same sign but the sum has a different sign. This approach avoids explicit carry chains or intermediate carry signals, simplifying the design.

Specifically:
- Sum is computed as s = a + b using the '+' operator, which performs 8-bit two's complement addition.
- Overflow is detected by checking if the sign bits of a and b are equal and differ from the sign bit of the sum, i.e., overflow = (~(a[7] ^ b[7])) & (a[7] ^ s[7]).

This solution:
- Is succinct and synthesizes efficiently as a combinational adder.
- Eliminates the need for explicit carry logic or multi-level hierarchical adders.
- Uses a standard and widely accepted overflow detection method for two's complement addition.
- Minimizes area and power by leveraging built-in arithmetic operators and simple combinational logic.
