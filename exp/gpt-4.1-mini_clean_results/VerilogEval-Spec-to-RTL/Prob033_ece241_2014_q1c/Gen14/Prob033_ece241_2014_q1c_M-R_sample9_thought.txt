I will refactor the 8-bit signed adder with overflow detection by explicitly calculating carry bits using a recursive assign structure without a generate loop. Instead of a loop, I will unroll the carry computation step by step using a vector of carry signals assigned via continuous assignments. This approach avoids the generate block, making the carry calculations more explicit and visible.

The sum will still be computed as the XOR of the propagate signals with the carry-in bits. Overflow is detected by XORing the carry into and carry out of the most significant bit.

This refactoring maintains the same logic and correctness but emphasizes direct continuous assignments and explicit intermediate signals for educational clarity and different style. It also avoids parameterization since width is fixed at 8 bits, simplifying the design for this fixed-size adder.
