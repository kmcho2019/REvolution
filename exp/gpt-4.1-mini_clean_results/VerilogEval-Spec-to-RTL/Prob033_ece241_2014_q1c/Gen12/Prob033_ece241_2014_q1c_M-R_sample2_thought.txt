Refactor the design by replacing the explicit FullAdder module and ripple-carry chain with a behavioral addition using Verilog's '+' operator. Then, extract the sum and carry out from the 9-bit intermediate result. Calculate overflow by XORing the carry into and out of the MSB bits using the intermediate sum bits.

This approach:
- Simplifies the design by avoiding instantiation of multiple full adders.
- Allows synthesis tools to optimize the adder structure for better performance and area.
- Makes the code more concise and readable.
- Still provides correct overflow detection by looking at the carries from the addition result.

Overall, the core idea of signed addition with overflow detection remains, but the structure is changed to a behavioral style.
