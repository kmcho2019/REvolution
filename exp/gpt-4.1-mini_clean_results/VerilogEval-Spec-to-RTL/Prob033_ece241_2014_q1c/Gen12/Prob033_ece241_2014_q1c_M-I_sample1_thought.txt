To optimize the original solution for better power and area efficiency while maintaining performance and correctness, I will remove the unnecessary FullAdder module since it only computes propagate and generate signals and produces a dummy sum output.

Instead, I will compute the propagate (p = a ^ b) and generate (g = a & b) signals directly in the TopModule, reducing hierarchy and allowing synthesis tools to better optimize logic.

The carry-lookahead logic remains the same, using simple carry generation equations.

Sum bits are computed directly as s = p ^ carry_in, and overflow detection remains the XOR of the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB.

This simplification reduces module instantiation overhead, lowers area and power, and slightly improves performance by letting the synthesis tool optimize combinational logic holistically.

The design remains fully combinational and structurally clear.
