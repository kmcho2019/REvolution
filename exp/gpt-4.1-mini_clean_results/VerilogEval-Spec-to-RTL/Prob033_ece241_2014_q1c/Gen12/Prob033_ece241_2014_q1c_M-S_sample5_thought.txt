Since the FullAdder module only produces propagate and generate signals and does not calculate sum internally, we can eliminate it entirely to simplify the design. We can directly compute the propagate (p) and generate (g) signals in the TopModule from inputs a and b using simple bitwise operations.

Next, we implement the carry-lookahead logic directly with a simple carry chain:
c[0] = 0 (initial carry-in)
c[i+1] = g[i] | (p[i] & c[i]) for i=0..7

Sum bits are computed as s[i] = p[i] ^ c[i].

Finally, overflow is detected by XORing the carry-in and carry-out of the most significant bit.

This approach is functionally equivalent to the original but simpler, with fewer modules and less hierarchy, improving readability and potentially saving area and power.
