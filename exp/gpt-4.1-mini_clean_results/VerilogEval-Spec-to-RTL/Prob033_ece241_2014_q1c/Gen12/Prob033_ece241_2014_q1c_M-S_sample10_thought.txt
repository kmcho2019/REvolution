Simplify the design by removing the unnecessary FullAdder module and directly computing propagate (p) and generate (g) signals inside TopModule using simple assign statements. Compute the carries using the standard carry-lookahead formula c[i+1] = g[i] | (p[i] & c[i]), starting from c[0] = 0. Compute sum bits as s = p ^ c[7:0]. Compute overflow by XOR of the carry into and out of the MSB (c[7] ^ c[8]). This reduces module hierarchy and code complexity, improving clarity and likely area and power efficiency while maintaining the same functionality and performance for an 8-bit 2's complement adder with overflow detection.
