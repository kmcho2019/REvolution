Refactor the design by using Verilog's built-in addition operator for 8-bit signed addition and then extract the sum and overflow signals directly. This approach is more concise and leverages synthesis tools' optimizations. The overflow detection remains the same conceptually but is done by comparing the sign bits of inputs and the result using an expression rather than carry bits. This still detects signed overflow correctly but simplifies the design by avoiding manual carry chains and full adder instances.
