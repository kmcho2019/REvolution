To improve the original modular 4-bit CLA adder approach, I will implement a single 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA). This reduces carry-chaining delay and should improve timing (lower critical path delay) by generating all carries at once in a fully parallel manner. The 8-bit CLA computes propagate and generate signals for all bits and then calculates carries for each bit using carry-lookahead equations in a single combinational block.

This will increase the combinational logic complexity slightly but reduce carry propagation stages compared to two chained 4-bit CLAs, improving performance.

Furthermore, the 8-bit CLA maintains the same overflow detection logic based on carry into and carry out of the most significant bit.

This approach also potentially reduces area by eliminating duplicated propagate/generate and carry logic and reduces power by lowering glitches due to simpler carry calculation and fewer logic levels.

Thus, the strategy is:
- Create a single 8-bit CLA module computing all propagate/generate and carries in one block.
- Compute sum bits by XOR of propagate and carry-in.
- Compute overflow as XOR of carry into MSB and carry out of MSB.
- Use this 8-bit CLA directly in TopModule.
