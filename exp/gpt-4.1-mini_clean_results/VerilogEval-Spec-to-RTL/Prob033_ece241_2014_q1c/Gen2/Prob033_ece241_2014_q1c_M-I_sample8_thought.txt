To optimize the design for power and area while maintaining correctness, replace the manual ripple-carry adder with Verilog's built-in '+' operator. This allows synthesis tools to infer an optimized adder and efficiently handle carry propagation. For overflow detection, use the standard sign-based condition: overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the result's sign bit. This method avoids explicit carry signals and reduces wire count, improving area and power. The design remains combinational and easy to understand.
