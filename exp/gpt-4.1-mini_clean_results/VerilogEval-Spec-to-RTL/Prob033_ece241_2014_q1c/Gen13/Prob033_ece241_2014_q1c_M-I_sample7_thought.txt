To optimize the existing ripple-carry adder implementation, I will replace it with a behavioral 8-bit signed adder using the '+' operator. This approach allows synthesis tools to generate a more optimal adder circuit, often resulting in better performance, lower power, and reduced area compared to manually instantiated ripple-carry adders.

Specifically:
- Use a signed addition with '+' operator to get the sum.
- Compute overflow by checking if the signs of a and b are the same but differ from the sign of the sum, which is the standard signed overflow condition.
- This reduces code complexity and lets synthesis tools choose an efficient adder architecture.
- This approach avoids explicit carry chains and thus improves timing.
