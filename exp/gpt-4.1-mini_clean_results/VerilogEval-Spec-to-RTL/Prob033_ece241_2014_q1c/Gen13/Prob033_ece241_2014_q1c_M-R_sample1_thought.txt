Refactor the original design by eliminating the parameterized RippleCarryAdder module and implementing the full 8-bit ripple carry adder directly inside TopModule using a generate loop with combinational assign statements.

The design approach is:

1. Use an internal wire vector to hold carry signals between each bit.

2. Compute sum bits and carry bits bitwise using assign statements without separate full adder modules.

3. Initialize carry-in at bit 0 to 0.

4. After completing the 8-bit addition, detect overflow by XORing the carry into the MSB and the carry out of the MSB (i.e., carry[6] and carry[7]).

This refactoring keeps the same functionality but improves compactness and clarity by integrating the adder logic directly in TopModule with a simple, flat structure, avoiding the extra module and carry bus outputs.

This also reduces module overhead and makes the carry signals purely internal, potentially reducing unnecessary I/O signal toggling.
