Implement a straightforward 8-bit adder with carry chain using simple ripple-carry addition, since 8 bits is small and this reduces complexity. Compute the sum as a+b using Verilog '+' operator. For overflow detection, use the standard signed overflow formula: overflow = (a[7] & b[7] & ~s[7]) | (~a[7] & ~b[7] & s[7]), which directly checks sign bit cases rather than relying on carry bits.
