The current design uses a two-level carry-lookahead that still computes carries serially within each 4-bit group, leaving some carry chain delay and extra logic depth. To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will:

1. Replace the 2-group carry-lookahead with a full 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) logic that computes all carry signals in parallel using propagate and generate signals for every bit. This eliminates serial carry dependencies within groups and reduces critical path.

2. This involves computing carry signals with a classical CLA formula using intermediate signals to directly compute carry[i+1] from carry[0], propagate, and generate, avoiding ripple chains.

3. Implement the sum bits from the carry signals as before.

4. Keep the modular FullAdder module, but adjust it to output just propagate and generate; carry calculation moves outside to optimized parallel CLA logic.

5. Maintain the overflow detection as XOR of carry-in and carry-out of MSB.

This approach should reduce the carry chain length to one logic level and improve timing while slightly reducing area and power by removing serial carry logic and AND chains for groups.

The design remains parameterizable and clear, while providing better PPA than the two-group CLA.
