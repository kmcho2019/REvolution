Implement an 8-bit adder by adding a and b as signed 2's complement numbers. Use a 9-bit wire to hold the sum (to capture carry out). The overflow for signed addition occurs if the carry into the sign bit and carry out of the sign bit differ. Alternatively, overflow can be detected by checking if the sign of the sum is incorrect given the signs of the inputs: overflow happens if the sign bits of a and b are the same but the sign bit of s is different.
