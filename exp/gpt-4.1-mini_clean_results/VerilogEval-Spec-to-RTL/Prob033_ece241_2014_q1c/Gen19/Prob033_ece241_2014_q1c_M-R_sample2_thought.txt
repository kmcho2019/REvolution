Refactor the design by consolidating the carry chain computation into a single shared function used only within the TopModule, thus avoiding duplicated carry computation in both TopModule and the adder module. Implement the adder logic directly inside TopModule using continuous assignments and the shared carry function, eliminating the need for a separate adder module instantiation. This reduces area and power by computing propagate, generate, and carry signals only once, while still producing the 8-bit sum and overflow flag correctly. The carry chain function remains a simple combinational for-loop inside a function, but is called only once. The sum bits are assigned as XOR of propagate and carries. Overflow is determined by XOR of carry into and carry out of the MSB from the shared carry chain vector.
