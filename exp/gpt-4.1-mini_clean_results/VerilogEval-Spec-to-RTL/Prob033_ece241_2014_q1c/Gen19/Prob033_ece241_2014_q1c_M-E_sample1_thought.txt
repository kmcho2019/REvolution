I will implement the 8-bit adder and overflow detection using a behavioral style always_comb block. Instead of explicitly calculating carry bits, I will perform the addition on signed 8-bit numbers using Verilog's signed arithmetic, which naturally models two's complement behavior. The overflow will then be detected by checking the sign bits of the inputs and the sum: overflow occurs when the inputs have the same sign but the result has a different sign. This approach simplifies the logic, avoids explicit carry computation, and leverages built-in signed arithmetic, which is both concise and clear.
