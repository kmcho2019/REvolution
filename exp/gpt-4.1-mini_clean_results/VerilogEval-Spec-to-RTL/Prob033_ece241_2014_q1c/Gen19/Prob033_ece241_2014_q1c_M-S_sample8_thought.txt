I will simplify the design by implementing a straightforward 8-bit ripple carry adder without using separate 4-bit CLA blocks. This reduces hierarchy and complexity, making the design more compact and easier to understand.

For overflow detection, I will use the standard signed overflow condition for 2's complement addition: overflow occurs if the sign bits of inputs are the same but the sign bit of the result is different. This eliminates the need to track carry signals explicitly, simplifying overflow logic.

Although ripple carry adders have longer delay compared to CLA, for an 8-bit adder this is acceptable and simplifies the code significantly.
