Since this is a small 8-bit adder, instead of explicitly implementing the full ripple carry logic, I will use the Verilog built-in '+' operator to add the two 8-bit inputs. This operator will infer an adder automatically in synthesis. To detect overflow for signed addition, I will check if the sign of the sum does not match the expected sign from the operands. Specifically, overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the sum's sign bit. This approach simplifies the design significantly, reduces code size, and leaves optimization to the synthesis tool without losing correctness.
