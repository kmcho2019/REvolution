To improve the given 8-bit signed adder design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the two-stage 4-bit CLA adder cascade with a single, flat 8-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA). This reduces the inter-stage carry delay, potentially improving performance by parallelizing carry computations across all 8 bits simultaneously.

The single 8-bit CLA will compute generate and propagate signals for each bit, then produce all carries with minimal logic levels, thus decreasing worst negative slack (WNS) and improving timing. It will also reduce area slightly by eliminating one CLA instance and associated wiring.

For overflow detection, the same principle applies: XOR the carry into the most significant bit (bit 7) and the carry out of bit 7.

Overall, this redesign will yield a compact, fast 8-bit signed adder with minimal carry propagation delay and a clean overflow signal, improving performance while potentially reducing area and power due to fewer logic blocks and simplified wiring.
