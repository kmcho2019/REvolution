Implement an 8-bit adder by dividing the operands into two 4-bit halves and using two 4-bit ripple carry adders (RCAs) chained together. The lower 4-bit RCA adds the lower halves and produces a carry-out used as the carry-in of the upper 4-bit RCA for the upper halves addition.

This hierarchical RCA approach is simple, area- and power-efficient, and easier to understand and synthesize compared to a full CLA for small bit-widths.

For overflow detection, use the standard signed overflow condition: overflow = carry_into_MSB XOR carry_out_MSB. Here, the carry_into_MSB is the carry-out of the 6th bit, which is the carry-in of the MSB adder bit (bit 7).

Specifically, the carry-out of the 7th bit adder (MSB) and the carry-in of the 7th bit adder (which is the carry-out from the 6th bit adder) are used to detect overflow.

This design:
1. Uses modular 4-bit RCAs for simplicity.
2. Chains two 4-bit RCAs to build 8-bit addition.
3. Computes the overflow signal from MSB carry signals.
4. Offers simplicity and moderate performance, trading speed for simplicity and potentially lower power/area than a full CLA.
