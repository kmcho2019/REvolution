Implement the addition and overflow detection using behavioral Verilog with a signed 9-bit intermediate value. First, extend both inputs 'a' and 'b' to signed 9-bit numbers by sign-extending their MSBs. Then perform the addition, producing a signed 9-bit sum. The lower 8 bits of this sum become the output 's'. Overflow occurs if the 9-bit result is out of the range representable by 8-bit signed numbers, which can be detected by checking if the 9th bit (sign extension) differs from the 8th bit (MSB of sum). This approach uses Verilog's signed arithmetic directly, avoiding manual carry computation and making the design concise and intuitive.
