Use Verilog's built-in addition operator to simplify the adder logic. The addition of two 8-bit signed numbers can be done directly, and the overflow can be detected by checking if the sign of the sum differs from the expected result based on the operands' signs. Specifically, overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but the sign bit of the sum is different. This eliminates the need for manual carry computations and reduces complexity while maintaining correct signed overflow detection.
