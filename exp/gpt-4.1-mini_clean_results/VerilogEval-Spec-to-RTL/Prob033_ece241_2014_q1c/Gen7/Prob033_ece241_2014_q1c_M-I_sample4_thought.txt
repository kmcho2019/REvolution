To improve performance and reduce critical path delay compared to the ripple carry adder, I will implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for 8 bits. The CLA computes carry generate and propagate signals for each bit and then quickly derives carries for all bits in parallel, significantly reducing the addition delay. This approach maintains the same logic functionality and produces the sum and overflow signals accurately, but with improved speed. Although the CLA may slightly increase area and power due to extra logic for propagate/generate signals, for an 8-bit adder this overhead is minimal and acceptable given the latency gain. The overflow detection logic remains the same, relying on the carry-in and carry-out of the most significant bit.
