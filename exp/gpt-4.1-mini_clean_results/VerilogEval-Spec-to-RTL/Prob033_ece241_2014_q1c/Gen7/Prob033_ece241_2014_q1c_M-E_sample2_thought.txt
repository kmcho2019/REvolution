Implement the 8-bit signed adder using a ripple carry adder architecture with a chain of full adders. Each full adder takes a pair of input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The final carry-out of the MSB is used along with the carry-in to the MSB to compute the overflow (based on the sign bits). This approach is simple and modular, demonstrating a fundamental design method different from carry-lookahead logic. The overflow is detected by XORing the carry into and out of the sign bit position.
