Use Verilog's built-in '+' operator to perform the 8-bit addition of a and b directly and obtain a 9-bit result to include the carry out. Then extract the sum bits and compute the overflow using an alternative overflow detection formula: overflow occurs if the carry into the most significant bit (bit 7) differs from the carry out of the MSB. This can be determined by XORing the carry into and out of the MSB, which is equivalent to the XOR of the carry from bit 6 addition and the carry out bit. This avoids manual bitwise full-adder implementation, reduces code complexity, and provides a clear hardware interpretation of overflow as the discrepancy between the internal carries at the MSB stage.
