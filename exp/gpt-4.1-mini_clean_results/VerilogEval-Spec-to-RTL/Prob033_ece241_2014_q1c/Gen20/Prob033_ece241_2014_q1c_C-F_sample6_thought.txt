Fuse the hierarchical modularity and simplicity of the two 4-bit ripple carry adders (RCAs) from Example 1 with the efficient carry-lookahead carry generation logic from Example 2.

Specifically:
- Implement two 4-bit CLA blocks instead of RCAs, chaining them to form the 8-bit adder. 
- Each 4-bit CLA block internally generates its carry signals using carry-lookahead logic to reduce carry propagation delay within the block, improving speed over pure RCAs.
- Chain the two 4-bit CLA blocks by feeding the carry-out of the lower block as the carry-in to the upper block. This gives a moderate improvement in delay compared to a full 8-bit RCA but reduces complexity and area compared to a full 8-bit CLA.
- For overflow detection, use the classic signed overflow detection logic: XOR of carry into MSB (bit 7) and carry out of MSB.
- This approach balances modularity (ease of understanding and testing), performance (reduced carry delay inside 4-bit blocks), and area (less complex than full 8-bit CLA).
- The structure avoids explicit full 8-bit carry-lookahead complexity but improves on pure ripple carry delay by faster carry generation inside each 4-bit block.
- The fused design is hierarchical, easier to extend or reuse parts, and optimized for PPA considering the tradeoffs.

This fusion leverages the clarity and modularity of Example 1 with the carry-lookahead performance gains of Example 2 at a block-level granularity.
