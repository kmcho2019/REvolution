To optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA), I will remove the duplicated carry chain calculation present both inside the instantiated NBitCLAAdder and separately inside TopModule for overflow detection. Instead, I will refactor the design so the carry chain is calculated only once within the NBitCLAAdder module, and the internal carry signals (specifically the carry into and carry out of the MSB) will be exposed as outputs. This allows TopModule to directly use these carry signals for overflow detection without recomputing anything.

This reduces redundant logic, lowers switching activity and area, and simplifies the interface by sharing intermediate carry results.

The approach is:
- Modify NBitCLAAdder to output the full carry vector (or at least c[WIDTH-1] and c[WIDTH]) alongside sum and cout.
- TopModule instantiates NBitCLAAdder, receives carry signals, and computes overflow as carry into MSB XOR carry out MSB.
- Remove all duplicate propagate/generate/carry chain logic from TopModule.
- Keep the existing clean carry lookahead function inside NBitCLAAdder for good readability and synthesis.

This directly addresses the main PPA inefficiency pointed out in the feedback by consolidating carry computation logic into a single module with proper outputs shared upwards.
