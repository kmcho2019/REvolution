The sum of two 8-bit numbers and the overflow detection can be done directly using Verilog's built-in arithmetic operator. Since Verilog arithmetic on vectors performs addition with carry, we can simply add a and b as 9-bit vectors (by extending their sign) to capture the carry out in the 9th bit. Then, assign the lower 8 bits as the sum and detect overflow by checking if the sign bits of inputs and result indicate overflow (which is equivalent to XOR of carry into and out of the MSB). This removes the need for a separate ripple carry adder module, reducing code complexity while maintaining functionality.
