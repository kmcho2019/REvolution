Implement a ripple carry adder (RCA) from scratch as a chain of full adders for the 8-bit addition, explicitly computing the sum bit-by-bit along with the carry out of each bit. Then, calculate overflow using the standard signed overflow condition for two's complement addition: overflow occurs if the carry into the MSB and carry out of the MSB differ. This approach is simpler than CLA, but modular with separate full adder blocks to increase code clarity. The ripple carry design offers smaller combinational logic at the cost of carry propagation delay, but for 8 bits this is acceptable. This modular approach also cleanly exposes carry signals to directly compute overflow without duplicated carry logic or extra propagate/generate signals. It makes the design intuitive for educational or small-scale use cases.
