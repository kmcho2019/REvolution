Perform the addition using the built-in Verilog '+' operator on the 8-bit inputs directly, then detect overflow by analyzing the carry into and out of the sign bit. Overflow occurs if these two carries differ. To find these carries, perform addition on 7 bits (excluding MSB) to get carry-in to the MSB and then add the MSB bits with the carry-in. This approach uses explicit carry bits rather than sign bit comparisons for overflow detection.
