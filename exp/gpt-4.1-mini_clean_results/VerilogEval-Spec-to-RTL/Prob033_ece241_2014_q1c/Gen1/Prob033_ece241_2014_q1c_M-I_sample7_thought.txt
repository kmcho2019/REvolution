To optimize the original design for better power and area while maintaining the same functionality, we can avoid declaring signed wires and the 9-bit intermediate sum. Instead, we perform the addition using the unsigned inputs and detect overflow using a well-known simpler signed overflow formula: overflow occurs if the carry into the sign bit differs from the carry out of the sign bit.

Calculating overflow using carry bits can be more efficient in hardware than evaluating multiple sign bit comparisons. We extract the carry into and carry out of the MSB by adding with carry, which can be done by concatenating a zero bit and performing addition on 9-bit operands.

This approach eliminates explicit signed types, uses simpler combinational logic for overflow detection, and potentially reduces switching activity and area due to simpler overflow logic.

Summary of improvements:
- Use unsigned 9-bit addition of {1'b0, a} + {1'b0, b} to get the 9-bit sum.
- Assign the lower 8 bits to s.
- Overflow is detected by XOR of carry into MSB and carry out of MSB bits.
- Avoid signed wires to minimize conversion overhead.
