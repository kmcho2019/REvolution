The initial design correctly computes the sum and overflow but uses a 9-bit adder by concatenating the sign bits for overflow detection. This slightly increases area and power. We can optimize by performing an 8-bit addition and detecting overflow solely with XOR and AND gates on the sign bits of inputs and result without extending bit widths. This reduces the adder size back to 8 bits and simplifies the logic for overflow detection, improving both power and area with no timing penalty.
