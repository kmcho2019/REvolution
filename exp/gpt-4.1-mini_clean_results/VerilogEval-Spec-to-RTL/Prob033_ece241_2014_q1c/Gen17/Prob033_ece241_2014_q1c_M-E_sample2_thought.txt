I will implement the addition using a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder for clarity and simplicity, which is fully synthesizable and intuitive. Each bit sum is computed as the XOR of inputs and carry-in, while the carry-out propagates to the next bit.

For overflow detection, I will rely on the fact that overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same, but the sign bit of the sum differs. Thus, I will explicitly check if the MSB of a and b match, and then XOR this with the MSB of the sum to detect overflow.

This approach avoids explicit carry lookahead logic and still efficiently detects signed overflow with minimal logic. It is easy to understand, debug, and scale, and suitable when simplicity and area are more important than high speed.

The TopModule will implement the entire functionality without submodules, making it compact and self-contained.
