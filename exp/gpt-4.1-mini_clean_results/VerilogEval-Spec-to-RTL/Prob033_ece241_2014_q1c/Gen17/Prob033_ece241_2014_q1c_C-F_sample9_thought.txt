To create a superior solution combining the best aspects of both examples, I will implement a hierarchical and more parallel carry-lookahead adder (CLA) that reduces the carry propagation delay compared to the simple ripple-style carry chain. Specifically:

- Implement a two-level carry lookahead structure: first compute bit-level propagate and generate, then group-level propagate and generate for 8 bits divided into two 4-bit blocks, allowing carries to be computed with fewer gate delays.

- Maintain the same overflow detection as before (XOR of carry into and out of MSB).
- Keep the module hierarchy clean by defining a 4-bit CLA block and then a top-level 8-bit CLA that uses two 4-bit CLAs and combines their carry outputs with group generate/propagate logic.
- This hierarchical CLA is more scalable and efficient for an 8-bit adder than a flat ripple or flat CLA with sequential carry logic.
- The TopModule instantiates this 8-bit CLA and exposes the sum and overflow outputs.

This fusion approach blends the clean, explicit carry logic from the second example with the hierarchical parallelism hinted at in the first example, improving performance (lower critical path), area (shared group logic), and power (reduced glitches due to fewer logic levels) compared to either original solution.
