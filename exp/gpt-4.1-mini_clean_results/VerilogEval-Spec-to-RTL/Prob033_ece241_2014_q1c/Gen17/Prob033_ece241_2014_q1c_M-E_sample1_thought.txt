I will implement the 8-bit signed adder by utilizing the built-in Verilog addition operator to directly compute the sum and then determine overflow by checking the sign bits of inputs and result. Specifically, overflow occurs if the sign bits of a and b are the same but differ from the sign bit of the sum. This method leverages the simplicity and synthesis efficiency of the '+' operator, avoiding manual carry or generate/propagate calculations while correctly detecting overflow for 2's complement addition.
