The original design uses a basic ripple-carry form of CLA logic, which has a linear carry propagation delay. To optimize for better PPA (power, performance, area), I will implement a true carry lookahead adder (CLA) by computing group generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for halves of the adder, allowing parallel carry computation and reducing carry delay significantly.

Key improvements:
- Use hierarchical carry lookahead: divide the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit blocks, each with their own group generate and propagate signals.
- Compute carry signals at block level to reduce carry chain length.
- This reduces the critical path delay of the carry chain.
- Maintain explicit propagate, generate, and carry signals for transparency.
- Keep combinational-only logic to minimize latency.
- Use minimal wires and avoid unnecessary signals to reduce area and power.

The overflow detection remains the XOR of carry into and carry out of the MSB as it is correct for 2's complement addition.

This optimized design will improve timing (reduce WNS and TNS) while also potentially reducing power and area due to less fanout and shorter carry paths.
