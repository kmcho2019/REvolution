Implement the 8-bit adder and overflow detection using ripple-carry addition with explicit full adder instances for each bit. Each full adder adds corresponding bits of a and b along with carry-in and produces sum and carry-out. The overflow is detected by XOR of the carry into and carry out of the most significant bit (i.e., the carry into bit 7 and carry out from bit 7). This modular approach explicitly structures the addition in terms of full adders and makes the design clear and scalable while avoiding the direct use of '+' operator or carry-lookahead signals.
