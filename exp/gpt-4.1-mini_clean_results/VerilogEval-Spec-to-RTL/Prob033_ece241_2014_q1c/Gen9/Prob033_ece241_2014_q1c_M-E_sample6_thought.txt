I will implement the 8-bit signed adder using a ripple-carry adder architecture with an explicit full adder module instantiated for each bit. This approach is simple and clear, making it easy to understand and verify.

Specifically:
- Create a separate FullAdder module that calculates sum, carry-out for given a, b, and carry-in bits.
- Instantiate 8 FullAdder instances, chaining the carry out of each to carry in of the next.
- Calculate overflow by XORing the carry into the MSB and carry out from the MSB.
- This architecture prioritizes structural clarity and modularity, allowing easy extension or modification.
- Ripple-carry might have slightly worse timing than carry-lookahead but is area- and power-efficient for 8 bits.
