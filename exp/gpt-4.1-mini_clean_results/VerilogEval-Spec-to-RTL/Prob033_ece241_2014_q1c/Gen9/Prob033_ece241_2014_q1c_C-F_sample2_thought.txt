To create a superior 8-bit 2's complement adder with overflow detection, I will fuse the clarity and modularity of the explicit full adder logic from Example 1 with the efficient, clean propagate/generate assignments directly from Example 2, but eliminate unnecessary module instantiations for area and power efficiency.

Specifically, I will:
1. Compute propagate (p) and generate (g) signals directly in TopModule as p = a ^ b and g = a & b.
2. Implement carry-lookahead logic with generate loops for carries c[i+1] = g[i] | (p[i] & c[i]).
3. Calculate sums as s[i] = p[i] ^ c[i].
4. Detect overflow as XOR of carry-in and carry-out of MSB.
5. Avoid extra modules or dummy signals to reduce area and improve power.
6. Use generate loops for scalability and code clarity.

This fusion keeps the clean, structural generate-propagate-carry methodology from both examples but removes redundant hierarchical overhead (like a FullAdder module with unused outputs). It also maintains efficient carry-lookahead logic, which minimizes delay compared to a ripple carry chain, and makes the design clean, concise, and easily synthesizable.

Overall, this balances functional correctness, performance, area, power, and readability better than previous examples.
