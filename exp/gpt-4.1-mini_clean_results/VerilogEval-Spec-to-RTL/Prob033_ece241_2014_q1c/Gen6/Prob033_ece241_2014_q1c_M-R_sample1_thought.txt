Refactor the ripple carry adder by using a single vector carry wire shifted by one bit to represent the carries between bits, instead of individual carry bits in an array. The carry-in to the least significant bit is zero. For each bit, the sum and carry out are computed from the operands and carry in using continuous assignments in a generate loop. Overflow detection logic remains the same, comparing carry into and out of the most significant bit. This approach consolidates carry management and uses a slightly different style of assign statements for clarity and compactness, while preserving the original ripple carry functionality.
