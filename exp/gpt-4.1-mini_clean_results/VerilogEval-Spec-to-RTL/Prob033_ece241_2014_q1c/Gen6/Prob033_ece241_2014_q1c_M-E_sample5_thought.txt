Implement the 8-bit adder using the built-in Verilog '+' operator for clean, concise code. Derive the overflow flag from the carry into and carry out of the most significant bit to directly detect overflow from the internal carry signals. Specifically, perform the addition using 9 bits to capture the carry out explicitly, then determine overflow by XOR of the carry into and carry out of the MSB. This method uses the carry signals directly instead of sign bit comparison and avoids the explicit ripple-carry logic, simplifying the design while still clearly and correctly detecting signed overflow.
