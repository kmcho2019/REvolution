Combine the modular clarity and potential reuse of an individual single-bit DFF with the efficiency of a single always block for the whole 8-bit register. Instantiate a parameterized single-bit DFF module that performs synchronous reset and data capture on the positive clock edge without an enable signal to minimize logic complexity.

Inside TopModule, use a generate block to instantiate eight DFFs, each handling one bit of the 8-bit vector. This approach maintains modularity and testability while avoiding additional enable logic that can increase power and area.

This fusion offers:
- Clear modular design with reusable single-bit DFFs.
- Minimal combinational overhead, since no enable or bitwise comparisons are added.
- Scalability and easy verification due to per-bit modular instantiations.
- Maintaining synchronous reset semantics on all bits.
- The advantage over vectorized always block is modular testability and easier per-bit logic replacement if needed.

This is a good compromise between the clean simplicity of example 2 and modular clarity of example 1, while eliminating enable signals to improve power and area efficiency.
