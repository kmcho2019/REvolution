To optimize power and potentially improve performance without increasing area, introduce a clock enable (en) signal inside the DFF module that only updates the register when data or reset changes are necessary. Since the original interface doesn't have an enable input, generate an internal enable signal that goes high only when reset is active or input data differs from the current output. This gating reduces unnecessary toggling of flip-flops, lowering dynamic power while preserving synchronous reset behavior. The clock enable approach avoids adding complexity or area overhead by leveraging existing signals and simple combinational logic. The modification keeps the design clean and modular with a single parameterized vector DFF module.
