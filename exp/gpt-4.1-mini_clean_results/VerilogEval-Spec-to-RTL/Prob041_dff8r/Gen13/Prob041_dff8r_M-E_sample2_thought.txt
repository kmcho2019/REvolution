Implement the 8-bit register directly using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk. Use a synchronous active-high reset to clear the entire 8-bit output vector at once. This approach consolidates all flip-flops into one vectorized register, minimizing overhead from multiple module instantiations, improving area and potentially power by enabling synthesis tools to optimize a single vector register. The design is simpler and cleaner without sacrificing readability.
