Implement the 8-bit register using a single always block with vectorized assignments for simplicity and efficiency. Instead of a generate loop or multiple always blocks, this approach consolidates all flip-flops into one sequential process triggered on the positive clock edge. The synchronous active-high reset clears all bits to zero in one assignment, minimizing logic and potentially improving power and area due to shared reset logic. This design is clear, concise, and easy to maintain, offering a straightforward synthesis-friendly solution.
