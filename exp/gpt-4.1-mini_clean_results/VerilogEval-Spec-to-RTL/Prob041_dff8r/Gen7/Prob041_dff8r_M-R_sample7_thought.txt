Refactor the design to use a single always block with an 8-bit register for the output `q`. This approach eliminates the instantiation of individual D flip-flop submodules and the generate loop, simplifying the hierarchy. The synchronous active-high reset and positive edge clock triggering are preserved. The single always block uses non-blocking assignments to update the entire 8-bit register at once, improving area and potentially power efficiency while maintaining the same functional behavior.
