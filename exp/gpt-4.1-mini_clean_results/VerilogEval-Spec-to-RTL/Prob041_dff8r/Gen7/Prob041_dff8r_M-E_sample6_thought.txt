Implement the 8-bit register with synchronous reset using a single always block that operates on the positive edge of clk. Use a vector register to hold the 8-bit output, and on reset synchronously clear all bits to zero; otherwise, load the input vector d. This single-block approach minimizes module overhead, reduces wiring complexity, and is easily synthesized efficiently. The module interface remains unchanged. This design leverages behavioral modeling for compactness and improved synthesis optimization.
