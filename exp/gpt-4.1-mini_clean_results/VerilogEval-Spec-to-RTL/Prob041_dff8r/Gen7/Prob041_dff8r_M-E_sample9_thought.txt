Implement the TopModule using eight independent one-bit D flip-flops with synchronous reset. Instead of a vectorized or parameterized register, instantiate eight separate DFF modules, each handling one bit of input and output. This approach emphasizes clear bit-level control and modularity, making it easy to modify individual flip-flops independently if needed. Each flip-flop triggers on the positive edge of clk, and the synchronous reset sets its output to zero. The top module then concatenates the outputs to form the 8-bit output vector.
