Implement the 8-bit register as an array of eight single-bit D flip-flops by instantiating the DFF module eight times inside the TopModule. 

Each DFF is a separate module that handles its own clock and synchronous reset, and stores one bit of data. This approach increases modularity and clarity at the bit level, allowing easy reuse and potential bit-wise control in the future. 

TopModule simply connects each bit of the input vector to each DFF and collects the outputs into the 8-bit output vector. All flip-flops share the same clock and synchronous reset signals.

This design exemplifies hierarchical and structural Verilog design principles by building complex registers from simple building blocks.
