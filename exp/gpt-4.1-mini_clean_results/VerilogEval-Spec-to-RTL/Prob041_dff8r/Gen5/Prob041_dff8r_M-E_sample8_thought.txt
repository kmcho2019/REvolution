Implement the 8-bit register as a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk with synchronous active-high reset. Instead of using a vector register, use a packed array of 8 single-bit registers declared as reg q [7:0]. Each bit is updated simultaneously inside the same always block. This representation emphasizes the individual flip-flops concept while maintaining simplicity and clarity. Synchronous reset sets all bits to zero simultaneously. This approach also allows easy expansion to larger widths by changing the array size and keeps clear bit-wise semantics.
