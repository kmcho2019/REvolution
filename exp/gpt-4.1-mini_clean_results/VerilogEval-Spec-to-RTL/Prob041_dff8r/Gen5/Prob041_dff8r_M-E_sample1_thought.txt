Implement the TopModule using a single always block that models the entire 8-bit register with synchronous reset. Instead of separate DFF modules or a generate loop, use a vector register with nonblocking assignments, capturing all flip-flops in one block. This approach minimizes module instantiations and wiring overhead, enabling synthesis tools to infer an 8-bit register with synchronous reset directly. The design is concise, easy to maintain, and optimized for area and power, while still meeting the timing and functional requirements.
