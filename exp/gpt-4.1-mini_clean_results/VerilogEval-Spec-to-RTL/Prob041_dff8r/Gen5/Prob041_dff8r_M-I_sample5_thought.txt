To improve PPA (performance, power, area), replace the array of individual D flip-flop modules with a single always block managing an 8-bit register. This reduces module instantiation overhead and logic duplication, potentially improving area and timing. The synchronous reset remains inside the always block for clean logic synthesis, while clock gating is not introduced here because the reset is synchronous and simple. This approach simplifies the design and leverages the synthesis tool’s ability to optimize vector registers efficiently.
