Implement the TopModule by instantiating eight individual single-bit D flip-flops, each with synchronous reset, connected in parallel to build the 8-bit register. This approach emphasizes modularity and allows potential reuse or replacement of individual flip-flop components, helpful for debugging or specific flip-flop level optimizations.

Each DFF instance receives the global clk and reset signals, and is connected to one bit of the input d and output q. The output q is constructed by concatenating outputs from all eight DFF instances.

This design favors clear hierarchical structure and explicit bit-level control, trading off some area and complexity compared to vectorized always block implementation.
