Refactor the design by implementing the 8-bit register using an array of eight single-bit D flip-flop modules instantiated inside TopModule. This approach modularizes each bit’s storage element explicitly while maintaining synchronous reset and clocking behavior. Each DFF module handles its own bit independently, and the TopModule aggregates their outputs into the 8-bit output vector. This demonstrates a hierarchical, structural design style as opposed to the vectorized procedural approach.
