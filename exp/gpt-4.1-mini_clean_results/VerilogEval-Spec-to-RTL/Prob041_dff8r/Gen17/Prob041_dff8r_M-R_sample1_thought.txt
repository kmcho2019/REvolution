Refactor the 8-bit register with synchronous reset by replacing the multiple instantiations of single-bit DFFs with one single always block that operates on the entire 8-bit vector at once. This removes the hierarchy of small modules, simplifies the design, and may reduce area and power by sharing clock gating and reset logic. The functionality remains the same: on the positive edge of clk, if reset is high, q is cleared to zero; otherwise, q captures d. This approach keeps the synchronous reset and positive edge clocking, but implements the entire register as a single vector reg instead of an array of individual flip-flops.
