To optimize the original design for better power while maintaining the same functionality, I will add a clock enable (ce) signal internally that only updates the register when the input data changes or when reset is asserted. This reduces unnecessary toggling and switching activity in the flip-flops, lowering dynamic power consumption. The synchronous active-high reset remains unchanged for design correctness. Adding clock enable logic is a low-area overhead and does not affect timing significantly. The design keeps the vectorized 8-bit register and synchronous reset for clarity and efficiency.
