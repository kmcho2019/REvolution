Fuse the modular clarity of separate DFF modules with the area and power efficiency of a single always block by implementing the entire 8-bit register inside TopModule using one always block and an 8-bit reg. This approach preserves synchronous active-high reset, positive edge clock triggering, and minimizes module instantiation overhead. By not instantiating separate DFF modules, we reduce area and power while maintaining straightforward synchronous reset logic. This is a common and efficient register design style in RTL.
