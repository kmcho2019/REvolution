To optimize area and power, consolidate the 8 individual single-bit DFF instances into a single always block implementing an 8-bit register with synchronous reset. This reduces module instantiation overhead and allows synthesis tools to better optimize register packing and routing. Maintaining synchronous active-high reset and positive-edge clk triggering preserves the required behavior. This approach should improve area and possibly power without sacrificing timing, as it eliminates the generate loop and multiple module boundaries.
