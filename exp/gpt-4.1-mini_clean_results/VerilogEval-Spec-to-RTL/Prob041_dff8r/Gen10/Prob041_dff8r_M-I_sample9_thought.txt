To improve the existing modular implementation for better power and area efficiency, consolidate the eight D flip-flops into a single always block using an 8-bit register vector. This vectorized approach allows synthesis tools to optimize the register implementation, potentially reducing area by sharing resources and reducing control logic. It also may reduce dynamic power by minimizing redundant control signals and logic gates. Maintaining synchronous active-high reset and positive-edge clock triggering as specified ensures functional correctness. This solution simplifies the design by removing per-bit module instantiations and generates a compact and efficient RTL for the 8-bit register with synchronous reset.
