To optimize for better power and area, I will consolidate the 8 individual single-bit DFF instantiations into a single always block handling an 8-bit register vector with synchronous reset. This reduces module instantiation overhead and allows synthesis tools to perform better resource sharing and packing. It also minimizes wiring complexity and can improve timing by shortening critical paths. The design remains fully synchronous and functionally equivalent, with no added complexity. This approach typically yields better area and power metrics than generating multiple single-bit modules.
