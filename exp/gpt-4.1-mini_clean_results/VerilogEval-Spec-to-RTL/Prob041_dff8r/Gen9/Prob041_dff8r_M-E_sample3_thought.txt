Implement the TopModule as a single always block handling the entire 8-bit vector simultaneously. This reduces hierarchy and simplifies the logic to a single vectorized D flip-flop array with synchronous active-high reset.

By combining all bits into one register and using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk, we minimize instantiation overhead and can improve timing and area efficiency. The synchronous reset clears all bits to zero in one cycle.

This approach is compact, easy to read, synthesizes efficiently, and reduces module instantiations compared to the generated single-bit DFF array. It trades off some modularity for area and performance improvements.
