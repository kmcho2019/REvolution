Combine the modularity and bit-level clarity of the first example with the efficiency and simplicity of the vectorized synchronous reset register from the second. Create a parameterized vector DFF module with synchronous active-high reset, but internally implement it using a generate loop that instantiates single-bit DFFs. This approach provides both clear structural bit-level DFF instantiation and compact vector interface, enhancing readability, reusability, and synthesis efficiency. The TopModule then simply instantiates this parameterized vector DFF with WIDTH=8, cleanly separating concerns and maximizing modularity while maintaining low area and power.
