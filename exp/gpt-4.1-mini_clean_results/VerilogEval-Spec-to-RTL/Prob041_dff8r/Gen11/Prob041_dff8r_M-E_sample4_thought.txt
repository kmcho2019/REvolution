Implement a fully vectorized 8-bit register with synchronous reset in a single always block without instantiating separate DFF modules. This approach uses a single reg vector `q` and assigns the input `d` or zero on the positive clock edge depending on the reset signal. This design reduces module hierarchy, potentially decreases area and power by enabling synthesis tools to optimize the vector register as a single unit, and simplifies the code. It maintains the same functionality of synchronous reset and positive edge clock triggering.
