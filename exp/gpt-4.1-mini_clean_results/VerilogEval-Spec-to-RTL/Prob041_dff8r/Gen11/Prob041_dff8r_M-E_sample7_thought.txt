Implement the 8-bit register with synchronous reset as a single always block operating on an 8-bit reg vector. This approach uses a compact vector register to reduce area and improve synthesis efficiency. Instead of instantiating multiple single-bit flip-flops, the entire register is updated in parallel on the clock's rising edge. The synchronous reset clears the entire register at once. This solution simplifies the design, reduces module instantiations, and is easy to maintain.
