Implement the eight 1-bit D flip-flops individually within a generate loop inside TopModule, each with synchronous active-high reset. This approach explicitly instantiates separate flip-flops for each bit, allowing fine-grained control or future extension on a per-bit basis, such as adding bit-wise enables or individual resets if needed. The synchronous reset sets each flip-flop's output bit to zero at the clock's rising edge when reset is asserted, and otherwise captures the corresponding bit of the input vector. This design provides modular clarity and explicit hardware mapping, facilitating bit-level instrumentation or modification.
