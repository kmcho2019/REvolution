The previous design added a clock enable signal derived from (d != q) to save power by preventing unnecessary updates, but this introduced an 8-bit comparator, increasing combinational logic and potentially harming timing and area. To optimize for better performance and area without sacrificing functionality or power significantly, I will remove the clk_en signal and update the flip-flops unconditionally on the positive clock edge, still with synchronous active-high reset. This reduces logic complexity, removes the comparator, and simplifies timing while keeping the design clean and fully synchronous as specified.
