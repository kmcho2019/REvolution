Fuse the modular clarity and reusability of the individual single-bit DFF module from the examples with the area- and power-efficient approach of handling the 8-bit register as a single vectorized always block inside TopModule. 

To achieve this:
- Keep the well-defined single-bit DFF module for potential reuse and clarity.
- Internally, implement the 8-bit register update logic in TopModule using a single always block that updates the entire 8-bit vector register synchronously on the positive clock edge with synchronous reset.
- Expose the output as a reg [7:0] and assign the 8-bit vector to q.
- This merges the clarity of the modular design philosophy with the PPA benefits of vectorized register updates, minimizing overhead and wiring complexity.
