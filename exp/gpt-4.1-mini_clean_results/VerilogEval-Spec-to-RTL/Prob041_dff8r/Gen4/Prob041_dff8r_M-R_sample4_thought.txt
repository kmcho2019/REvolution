Refactor by splitting the design into 8 individual D flip-flop cells with synchronous reset and enable, rather than using one vector register. Each DFF module has its own always block triggered on the posedge of clk, with synchronous reset and enable. The TopModule instantiates 8 of these DFFs in a generate loop, connecting each bit separately. The enable logic remains the same to reduce toggling but is now used per bit. This modular approach clarifies the structure, makes the design more scalable and testable, and maintains the original functionality and power optimization.
