To optimize the design for better power and area, I will merge the 8 individual DFF instances into a single 8-bit register implemented inside one always @(posedge clk) block with synchronous active-high reset. This reduces module instantiations, lowers switching activity, and minimizes area by enabling synthesis tools to infer an efficient register file. The functional behavior remains identical, preserving timing and synchronous reset semantics while enhancing PPA metrics.
