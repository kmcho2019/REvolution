Implement the 8-bit register using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk, with synchronous reset applied to the entire 8-bit register at once. Instead of instantiating individual D flip-flops, use a single 8-bit reg variable to store the state. This simplifies the design, reduces module overhead, and improves synthesis efficiency.

Specifically:
- Declare an 8-bit reg 'q_reg' internally to hold the register state.
- On each positive clock edge, if reset is high, clear q_reg to zero synchronously.
- Otherwise, update q_reg with the input d.
- Drive the output q by assigning q_reg.
This approach keeps the design minimal and compact while maintaining the required synchronous reset and positive edge triggering.
