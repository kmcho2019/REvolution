To improve power and area while maintaining timing and functionality, replace the eight individual single-bit DFF module instances with one vectorized 8-bit register implemented inside a single always block.

This approach shares the reset and clock input internally, reducing routing and loading on control signals and saving area by avoiding multiple module instantiations and associated overhead.

Also, synchronous reset is handled simply, avoiding extra enable signals or gating. The single 8-bit always block reduces logic duplication, improving power due to fewer transitions and simplified clock and reset routing.

Thus, the design becomes more compact and efficient, with the same synchronous reset semantics and positive clock edge triggering.
