Implement the 8-bit register as an array of individual single-bit D flip-flop modules with synchronous reset. Each bit has its own instantiated DFF module, which encapsulates the synchronous reset and clocked behavior. In the TopModule, instantiate 8 such DFFs in a generate loop, connecting each bit of 'd' and 'q' separately. This approach maximizes modularity and clarity at the bit level, facilitating bit-level testing or replacement, and explicitly shows each flip-flop's behavior. This structure can be advantageous for designs where individual bit control or modification is needed, and makes the reset and clocking very explicit per bit.
