Implement a single always block that handles the entire 8-bit register with synchronous active-high reset. Instead of using a generate loop or multiple always blocks, store the flip-flop state in an 8-bit reg and update it as a vector on the positive clock edge. When reset is asserted, clear the entire output vector to zero synchronously. This simple and clean approach reduces complexity and overhead, making it efficient in terms of area and power while ensuring clarity and easy maintenance. It also facilitates easier synthesis optimizations by explicitly showing the vector register behavior.
