Refactor the design by implementing the 8-bit register using an array of single-bit D flip-flop modules instantiated in a generate loop. Each single-bit DFF handles one bit with synchronous reset and positive edge clock triggering. This approach preserves modularity and clarity, demonstrating how to build a vector register from smaller reusable components.

Advantages of this structure:
- Explicit structural mapping of each bit, improving testability and debug granularity.
- Clear visibility of each flip-flop instantiation for hierarchical verification.
- Retains synchronous reset and positive edge-triggered operation as required.
- Alternative to vector always block; useful when fine-grained control or bitwise customizations are needed.

Though slightly larger in area and power due to replicated control logic per bit, this approach makes the architecture explicit and is easy to extend or modify per bit. The TopModule instantiates 8 single-bit DFFs, wiring them together to form the 8-bit output vector.
