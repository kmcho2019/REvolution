Implement the TopModule using an array of individual 1-bit D flip-flops instantiated within a generate loop. Each flip-flop has synchronous active-high reset and updates on the rising edge of clk. This approach explicitly instantiates each bit as a separate module instance, providing clear modularity and easy extensibility. By separating each flip-flop as a module, it also allows targeted optimizations or modifications per bit in future, such as adding asynchronous resets or enables individually if needed. The overall structure results in clean, well-structured code reflecting the hardware more transparently compared to vectorized or loop-based assignments.
