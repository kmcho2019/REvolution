To optimize the design for power and area while maintaining the correct synchronous reset behavior and timing, I will merge the eight separate single-bit D flip-flops into one single always block handling an 8-bit register. This eliminates multiple module instantiations, reducing overhead and switching activity. Using one procedural block for the vector register with synchronous reset lowers area and power consumption as compared to instantiating eight separate single-bit DFF modules. The design remains functionally identical and keeps a clear, simple interface.
