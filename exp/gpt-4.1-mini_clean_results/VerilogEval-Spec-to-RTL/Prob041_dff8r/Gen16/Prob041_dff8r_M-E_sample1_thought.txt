Implement the 8-bit register as a single always block that directly handles the entire 8-bit vector input and output. This approach eliminates the generate loop and per-bit always blocks, simplifying the design and potentially improving synthesis efficiency. The synchronous active-high reset is integrated with a simple if statement, ensuring that on the clock's rising edge, the register clears to zero or captures the input data. This straightforward, compact design optimizes area and power by reducing the number of control structures and enabling easier timing analysis.
