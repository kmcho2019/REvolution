Implement the 8-bit register by instantiating eight individual single-bit D flip-flop modules, each with synchronous reset, connected in parallel. This approach emphasizes modularity and explicit mapping of each bit to a dedicated flip-flop instance. Although this might consume slightly more area compared to a vectorized approach, it provides clearer structural insight, easier per-bit debugging, and potential for bit-wise customization in the future.

The TopModule will instantiate eight DFF modules, each taking one bit of input d and producing one bit of output q. All DFFs share the same clock and synchronous reset signals, triggered on the positive edge of clk.

This fully structural and modular design contrasts the vectorized style and highlights how individual modules compose the 8-bit register.
