To optimize for power and area, consolidate the 8 single-bit DFF instances into a single vectorized always block. This reduces the overhead of multiple module instantiations and allows synthesis tools to optimize register packing more efficiently, lowering area and potentially power consumption. The synchronous reset remains active high and affects all bits simultaneously as before, ensuring functional equivalence. This vectorized approach minimizes control logic duplication and clock tree complexity, thereby improving PPA metrics without sacrificing readability or modularity significantly.
