Refactor the design by consolidating all 8 D flip-flops into a single always block with a synchronous reset, instead of instantiating 8 separate single-bit DFF modules. This approach uses a vectorized register update inside one block, reducing module overhead, improving area and possibly power efficiency. It also maintains the same behavior—on the rising clock edge, if reset is asserted, output is cleared; otherwise, output is assigned the input vector. This vectorized style improves readability and synthesis optimization opportunities by making the register bank explicit as a single 8-bit register.
