Implement the TopModule as a single always block that models eight parallel D flip-flops with synchronous reset without using a separate DFF module. Use a register variable q to store the 8-bit state, and on the rising edge of clk, if reset is asserted, clear q to zero; otherwise, assign q the input d. This straightforward, flat register approach avoids parameterized modules or generate loops for simplicity, yet meets all requirements. This approach reduces module hierarchy and can be clearer for synthesis and simulation while preserving synchronous reset behavior.
