Implement the 8-bit register as an array of 8 single-bit D flip-flop instances, each with its own synchronous reset. This approach emphasizes modularity and reusability by explicitly instantiating 8 individual DFF modules within the top-level module.

Each DFF instance captures one bit of the input vector `d` on the rising edge of `clk` and resets synchronously when `reset` is high. Outputs from all DFF instances are combined to form the 8-bit output vector `q`.

This design improves clarity of signal flow on a per-bit basis and facilitates bitwise testability or modifications, while explicitly demonstrating how multi-bit registers can be constructed from single-bit elements.
