Refactor the design by implementing the entire 8-bit register in a single always block instead of instantiating multiple single-bit D flip-flops. This merges the synchronous reset and clocking behavior into one vector register with non-blocking assignments.

Advantages:
- Reduced module instantiation overhead.
- More compact and synthesizer-friendly code.
- Potentially better area and power due to consolidated register logic.
- Same positive-edge clock and synchronous active-high reset behavior preserved.

This approach directly models an 8-bit register with synchronous reset rather than using multiple single-bit DFF modules, leading to simpler, cleaner, and more efficient RTL code.
