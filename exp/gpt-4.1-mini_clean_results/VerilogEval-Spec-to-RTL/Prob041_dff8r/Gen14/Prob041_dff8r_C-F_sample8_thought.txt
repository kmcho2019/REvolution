Fuse the best of both examples by implementing a single vectorized 8-bit register with synchronous active-high reset inside one always block, avoiding individual single-bit module instantiations. This approach minimizes module instantiation overhead and allows synthesis tools to optimize clock gating, reset distribution, and packing for better power and area.

To retain modular clarity and facilitate potential per-bit control or testability, define a single parameterized vectorized DFF module that implements the entire 8-bit register with synchronous reset and positive-edge clocking. This keeps a clean hierarchy but flattens the design complexity compared to 8 separate single-bit DFF instances.

This fusion strategy offers:
- Area savings by reducing overhead of 8 module instantiations into one.
- Power savings through more efficient reset and clock signal management by synthesis tools.
- Performance retention by leveraging a single always block triggered on posedge clk.
- Modularity and parameterization to allow easy scalability beyond 8 bits.
- Clear, readable, and maintainable code.

Thus, the design merges the modularity and synchronous reset behavior of the example DFF modules with the efficiency and synthesis friendliness of a vectorized register in one always block.
