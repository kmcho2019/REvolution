Fuse the strengths of both examples by implementing a parameterized vector DFF module with synchronous reset in a single always block for area and power efficiency, while internally instantiating 8 single-bit DFF submodules purely for hierarchical clarity and potential testability. The top-level module interfaces with an 8-bit vector input and output, driven by the vector DFF module.

Key points:
- Maintain a single always block at vector level to update all bits simultaneously on posedge clk with synchronous active-high reset, minimizing muxing and conditional complexity.
- Instantiate single-bit DFFs internally for modular clarity, hierarchical debugging, and easy per-bit instrumentation, but wire their outputs to the vector register controlled by the single always block.
- This hybrid approach preserves modularity and scalability, avoids redundant enable logic, and ensures efficient synthesis for timing, power, and area.
- The TopModule simply instantiates the vector DFF, connecting clk, reset, d, and q.

Thus, the design is:
- Modular with reusable single-bit DFF,
- Efficient with consolidated synchronous reset vector update,
- Scalable and clean for synthesis and future extension,
- Balanced for best power-performance-area metrics without redundant logic.
