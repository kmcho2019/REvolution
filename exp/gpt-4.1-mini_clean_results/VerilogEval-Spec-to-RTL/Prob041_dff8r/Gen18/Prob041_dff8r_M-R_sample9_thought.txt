Refactor the original DFF_Vector by removing the per-bit enable signals and using a straightforward always block to implement a synchronous reset and data storage for the entire 8-bit vector. This simplifies the logic, improves power and area by avoiding unnecessary conditional checks and muxing inside the always block, and enhances timing by reducing complexity. The TopModule instantiates this simplified vector DFF with a direct connection. This structure keeps the same functionality but improves clarity and efficiency.
