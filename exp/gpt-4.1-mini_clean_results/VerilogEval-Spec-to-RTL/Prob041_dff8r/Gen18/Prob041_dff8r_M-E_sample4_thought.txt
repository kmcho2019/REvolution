Implement the 8-bit register directly inside TopModule using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk. Handle the synchronous reset by assigning zero to the entire 8-bit output q when reset is asserted. This approach avoids any separate module instantiation, reducing hierarchy and potentially improving synthesis results by giving the synthesizer a single clear register block. It is straightforward, easy to read, and fully meets the spec for synchronous active-high reset on the positive clock edge.
