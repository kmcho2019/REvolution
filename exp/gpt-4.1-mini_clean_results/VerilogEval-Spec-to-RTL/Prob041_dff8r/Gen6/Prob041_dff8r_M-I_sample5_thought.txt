To optimize area and power, replace the array of eight single-bit DFF modules with one single 8-bit synchronous reset register inside TopModule. This reduces module instantiation overhead and allows synthesis tools to implement the register more compactly. The synchronous reset and clocking semantics remain unchanged, preserving correct functionality and timing. Using a single always block with an 8-bit register reduces redundant control logic and likely lowers dynamic power due to fewer flip-flop enable signals toggling separately.
