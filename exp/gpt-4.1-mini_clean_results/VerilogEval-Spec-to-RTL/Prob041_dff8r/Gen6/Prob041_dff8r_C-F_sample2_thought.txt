Fuse the modularity and clarity of having a dedicated single-bit synchronous reset DFF module (from Example 1) with the area and power efficiency of a vectorized register (from Example 2) by implementing a parameterized single-bit DFF with synchronous reset and instantiate it in a generate loop. 

However, add a clock enable signal internally that only updates the flip-flop when the input data differs from the stored output or reset is asserted. This approach minimizes unnecessary toggling and power usage without complicating interface or timing.

The design keeps the positive edge clock triggering with synchronous active-high reset setting outputs to zero. Using a generate loop keeps modularity and scalability. The selective update with a simple data comparison helps power reduction compared to always clocking all DFFs.

This hybrid approach combines the readability and reuse of a single-bit DFF module with power optimization ideas, preserving performance and area benefits.
