Refactor the design by implementing the 8 D flip-flops with synchronous reset using a single always block and a vector register for outputs. This removes the per-bit enable logic and individual flip-flop modules, simplifying the design to a concise and efficient register update triggered on the positive edge of the clock. On reset, the entire 8-bit output vector is synchronously set to zero; otherwise, it captures the input vector directly. This approach reduces combinational logic, area, and switching activity, improves timing by eliminating enable signal logic, and preserves the specified functionality.
