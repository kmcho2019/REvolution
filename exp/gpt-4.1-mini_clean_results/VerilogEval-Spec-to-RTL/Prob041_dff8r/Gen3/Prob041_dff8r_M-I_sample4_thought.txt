To reduce power consumption while maintaining the synchronous reset and the positive-edge triggered behavior, I will add a clock enable (en) signal internally that only updates the registers when the input data 'd' differs from the current output 'q'. This avoids unnecessary toggling and switching inside the flip-flops when the input doesn't change, thus reducing dynamic power without increasing area significantly. The synchronous reset logic remains unchanged. This approach also keeps the design clean and easily synthesizable.
