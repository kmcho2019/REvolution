To optimize for better power without impacting functionality, I will add a clock enable signal that updates the register only when the input data differs from the stored output or when reset is asserted. This reduces unnecessary toggling and dynamic power. The synchronous reset remains active high. The design still uses a single vector DFF module, but with a built-in enable to clock-gate updates at the flip-flop level. This approach improves power efficiency while maintaining area and performance, as the enable logic is minimal and the clock remains clean.
