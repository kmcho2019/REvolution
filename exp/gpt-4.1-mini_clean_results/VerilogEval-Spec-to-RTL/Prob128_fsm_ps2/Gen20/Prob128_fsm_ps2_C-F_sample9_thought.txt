Fusion strategy:
Combine the low toggling and power efficiency from Example 2's gated counter enable with the clarity and minimal toggling of the done signal from Example 1. Use a 2-bit count register with a dedicated enable signal that is high only when inside a message or at the detection of a start byte, preventing unnecessary counter toggling outside messages. Generate the done pulse only in the cycle immediately after the third byte is received, and keep done low otherwise.

Additionally, reset is synchronous and active high. The done signal is asserted for exactly one cycle after the third byte, ensuring no glitches. The count and done registers use non-blocking assignments for safe timing.

This fusion leverages gated count increments for power saving and clean done pulse generation for clear handshake signaling, balancing performance, power, and area optimally for the simple FSM task.
