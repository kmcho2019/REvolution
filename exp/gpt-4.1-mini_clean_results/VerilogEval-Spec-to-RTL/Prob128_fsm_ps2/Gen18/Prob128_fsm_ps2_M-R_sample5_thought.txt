Refactor the FSM by explicitly defining three states to represent the byte positions: IDLE (waiting for the start byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2, and BYTE3. Transition between states synchronously on each clock cycle when a new byte arrives, resetting to IDLE on reset or after the third byte. Generate the done pulse combinationally by detecting the transition out of BYTE3 state. This approach removes the 2-bit counter and gating logic, replacing it with a clearly defined one-hot FSM, improving readability and making state transitions and output logic more explicit.
