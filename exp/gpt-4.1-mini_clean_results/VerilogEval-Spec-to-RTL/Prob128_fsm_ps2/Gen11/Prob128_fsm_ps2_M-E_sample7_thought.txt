Instead of using a simple counter, implement an explicit 4-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3. Start in IDLE discarding bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 arrives, then move to BYTE1. Upon next clock cycles, transition through BYTE2 and BYTE3 states, accepting bytes regardless of in[3]. After BYTE3, assert done for one cycle and return to IDLE. This state-machine approach makes the design's intentions explicit and easier to extend or modify, and done is asserted only in the BYTE3->IDLE transition cycle.
