Refactor the FSM to use explicit states instead of a byte counter. Define three states: IDLE (waiting for start byte), BYTE2 (second byte), and BYTE3 (third byte). Transition from IDLE to BYTE2 upon detecting in[3]=1, then proceed through BYTE2 and BYTE3 states sequentially. Assert done in the cycle after receiving the third byte (BYTE3).
