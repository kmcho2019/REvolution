Implement a three-state FSM explicitly naming states: IDLE (waiting for a byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2 (second byte received), BYTE3 (third byte received). Transition through states as bytes arrive; assert done one cycle after receiving the third byte (in BYTE3). If at any point a byte with in[3]=1 is seen unexpectedly in BYTE2 or BYTE3, treat it as a new message start and reset FSM accordingly. Use synchronous reset and positive clock edge.
