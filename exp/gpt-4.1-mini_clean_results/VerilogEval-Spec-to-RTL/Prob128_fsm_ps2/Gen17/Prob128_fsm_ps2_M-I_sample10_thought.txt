To optimize the design for better PPA metrics, we will slightly refactor the FSM to use a two-state one-hot encoding with a counter for the byte count. This can reduce gate complexity and critical path length. The "done" signal will be generated using a separate registered pulse output to avoid multiple assignments in the same always block, minimizing toggling and improving timing. Also, we'll use a clock enable signal to only increment the counter when inside a message, reducing switching activity. The synchronous reset and positive edge clock triggering will be preserved. This should maintain functionality while marginally improving timing and power consumption.
