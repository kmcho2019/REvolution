Use a simple 2-bit binary encoded FSM to track the byte count within a message. The FSM stays in state 0 (waiting for start byte) until it receives a byte with in[3] = 1, which is considered the first byte of a message. Then it progresses through states 1 and 2 for the second and third bytes. The done signal is asserted for one clock cycle immediately after receiving the third byte (state 2). The done signal is registered and reset synchronously. The FSM transitions and done signal are updated together inside a single always block on posedge clk. This approach is compact and efficient, with minimal flip-flops and simple logic.
