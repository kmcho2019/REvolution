To optimize for better PPA, reduce the toggling of the count register by only updating it when necessary, and make done a simple combinational output derived from the count state to avoid extra register toggling. This removes the done register, reducing register count and toggling, which can lower power and area. Keeping count updates conditional reduces unnecessary switching. Also, use a localparam for the count width and the maximum count for clarity and easier tuning. The FSM remains functionally identical but more power and area efficient with slightly improved timing due to simplified logic.
