Use a 3-state FSM with explicit states: IDLE (waiting for first byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2 (second byte), BYTE3 (third byte). Transition from IDLE to BYTE2 only on a byte with in[3]=1. Then move sequentially through BYTE2 and BYTE3 states on every byte received. Assert done on the cycle after BYTE3 is received, then return to IDLE. Synchronous reset puts FSM in IDLE and clears done. This separates control flow clearly, making it easy to understand and maintain.
