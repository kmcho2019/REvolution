Instead of counting bytes, implement a simple three-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Initially, the FSM waits for a byte with in[3]=1 (start byte). Upon detecting it, transition to BYTE2 state on next clock to receive second byte, then to BYTE3 for third byte. After receiving the third byte, assert done and return to IDLE. This explicit state encoding makes the design clearer and separates detection logic from counting logic.
