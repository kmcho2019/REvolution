To optimize PPA (Timing, Power, Area), we can simplify the FSM by using a one-hot state encoding instead of a counter, which is often faster and can reduce combinational logic depth in FPGAs. We'll implement three states representing waiting for byte 1, receiving byte 2, and receiving byte 3. This approach typically results in faster state decoding (better timing) and simpler combinational logic. We'll assert 'done' in the cycle after receiving the third byte as required. Also, reset will synchronously initialize the state to WAIT. This may slightly increase flip-flop count but often improves timing and reduces glitches, thus improving power. The done signal is generated combinationally from the next state transition, so no extra registers are needed for done, reducing toggling and area slightly.
