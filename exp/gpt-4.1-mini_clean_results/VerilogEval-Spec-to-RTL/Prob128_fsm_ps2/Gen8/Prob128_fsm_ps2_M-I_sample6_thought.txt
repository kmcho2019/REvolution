To optimize PPA, reduce toggling on the 'done' output by updating it only when needed (pulse after third byte) instead of clearing every clock cycle, thus lowering dynamic power. Separate combinational detection of the third byte from the counter update to minimize critical path delay. Use a small FSM with explicit states instead of counting, which might simplify control and reduce logic transitions. The FSM will have three states: WAIT_START (waiting for in[3]=1), BYTE2 (after first byte), BYTE3 (after second byte). On BYTE3, assert done and return to WAIT_START. This approach keeps done asserted only one cycle and avoids unnecessary toggling, improving power and timing slightly while keeping area minimal.
