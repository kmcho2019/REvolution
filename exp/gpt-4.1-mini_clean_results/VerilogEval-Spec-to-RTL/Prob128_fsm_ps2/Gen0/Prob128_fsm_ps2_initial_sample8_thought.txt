Implement a synchronous FSM with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3.
- Start in IDLE and discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is received.
- Transition to BYTE1 on seeing in[3]=1, counting that as the first byte.
- Then go to BYTE2 on next byte, then BYTE3 on the next.
- After receiving BYTE3, assert done for one cycle and return to IDLE, ready for next message.
- done is asserted in the cycle immediately after receiving the third byte.
- reset synchronously resets FSM to IDLE.
