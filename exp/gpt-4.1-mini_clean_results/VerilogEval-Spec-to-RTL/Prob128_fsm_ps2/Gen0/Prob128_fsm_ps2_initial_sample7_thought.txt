Implement a three-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3.
- In IDLE, wait until a byte with in[3]=1 is received; then move to BYTE2.
- In BYTE2, accept next byte and move to BYTE3.
- In BYTE3, accept next byte, signal done (done=1), then return to IDLE to find next message start.
- Signal done only for one clock cycle immediately after receiving the third byte.
- Synchronous reset sets FSM to IDLE and clears done.
