Implement a 3-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. 
In IDLE, ignore all bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 arrives, transition to BYTE2.
In BYTE2, accept the second byte unconditionally and go to BYTE3.
In BYTE3, accept the third byte and then signal done=1 for one cycle, then return to IDLE.
Reset synchronously sets the FSM to IDLE and done=0.
