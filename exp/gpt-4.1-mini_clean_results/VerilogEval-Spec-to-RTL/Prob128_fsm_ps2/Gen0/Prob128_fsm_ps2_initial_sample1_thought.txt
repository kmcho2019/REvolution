Implement an FSM with states: IDLE (searching for first byte of message), BYTE2 (waiting for second byte), and BYTE3 (waiting for third byte). In IDLE, discard bytes until in[3] = 1, then move to BYTE2. After BYTE2, move to BYTE3. After BYTE3, assert done for one cycle and return to IDLE. Reset synchronously sets state to IDLE and done to 0.
