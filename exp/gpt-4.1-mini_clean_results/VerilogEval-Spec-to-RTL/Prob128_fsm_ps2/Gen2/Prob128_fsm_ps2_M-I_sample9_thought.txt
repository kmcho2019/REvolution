Optimize the done signal by making it combinational instead of registered to reduce toggle rates and possibly improve timing. The done signal should be asserted for one cycle immediately after receiving the third byte, i.e., when the FSM is in BYTE3 state. Keep the FSM state register updated on the clock edge with synchronous reset, but drive done directly from the current state combinationally. This reduces flip-flop usage, toggling, and may lower power and improve critical path.
