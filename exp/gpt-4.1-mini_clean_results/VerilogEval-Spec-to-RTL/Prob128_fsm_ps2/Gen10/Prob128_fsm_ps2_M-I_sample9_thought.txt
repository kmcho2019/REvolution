To optimize for better power, area, and timing (PPA), I will encode the FSM with binary states instead of one-hot, reducing flip-flop count from 3 to 2 bits. This saves area and dynamic power due to fewer registers toggling. The done signal will be generated combinationally from the current state and input, avoiding an extra register bit and thus slightly reducing flip-flop usage and toggling. The synchronous reset remains intact to ensure clean startup. This binary encoding simplification will reduce the logic complexity and likely improve timing slack (WNS and TNS), while lowering power by fewer FFs toggling and less done signal activity. The FSM behavior and done timing remain identical, asserting done for exactly one clock cycle immediately after the third byte.
