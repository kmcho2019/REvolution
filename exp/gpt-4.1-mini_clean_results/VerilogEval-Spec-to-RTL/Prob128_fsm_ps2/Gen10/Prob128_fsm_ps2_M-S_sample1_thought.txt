Since the FSM only needs to identify the start of a message by checking in[3] and count three bytes, we can simplify by using a single 2-bit counter that increments only when inside a message. Resetting it to zero when not in a message or after done. The done signal is asserted immediately after the third byte and cleared otherwise. This reduces branching by always incrementing the counter when >0 and resetting it when starting a new message or after done.
