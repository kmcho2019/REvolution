Implement the FSM with three distinct states to explicitly represent the byte position within the 3-byte message: IDLE (looking for a byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2 (second byte), and BYTE3 (third byte). On reset, transition to IDLE. When in IDLE and a byte with in[3]=1 is received, transition to BYTE2. Then move to BYTE3 on the next clock cycle, and after receiving the third byte, assert done for one cycle and return to IDLE. This state-based design clearly separates the message reception stages and directly encodes the FSM states, improving readability and maintainability.
