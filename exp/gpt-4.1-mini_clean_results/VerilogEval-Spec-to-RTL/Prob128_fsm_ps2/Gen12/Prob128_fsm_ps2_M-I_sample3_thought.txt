Optimize the FSM by introducing an enable signal to gate the counter increments, thus reducing switching activity and power consumption when not inside a message. Also, separate the done signal generation from the count update by using a combinational signal derived from the counter value. This reduces the critical path by simplifying done logic and ensures done is asserted only for one cycle immediately after the third byte is received. The synchronous reset remains the same. This approach improves timing and power while keeping area minimal.
