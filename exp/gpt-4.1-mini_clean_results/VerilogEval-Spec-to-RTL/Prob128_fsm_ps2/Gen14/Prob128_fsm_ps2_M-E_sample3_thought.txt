Use a three-state FSM: IDLE (waiting for first byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2 (waiting for second byte), and BYTE3 (waiting for third byte). Transition from IDLE to BYTE2 only when in[3]=1; then advance to BYTE3 on next clock cycle; after receiving BYTE3, assert done and return to IDLE. This explicit state-based FSM separates concerns clearly and provides easy extensibility. Reset synchronously returns FSM to IDLE and clears done.
