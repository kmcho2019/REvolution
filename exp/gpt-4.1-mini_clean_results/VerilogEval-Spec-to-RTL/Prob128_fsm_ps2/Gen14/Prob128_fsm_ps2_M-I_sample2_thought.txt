To improve the PPA, I will reduce switching activity by enabling the counter and done logic only when necessary. Specifically, the count register will only toggle when inside a message, and the done signal will be asserted in the cycle after the third byte is received (i.e., registered done), ensuring timing clarity and reducing glitches. The design keeps a minimal 2-bit counter and uses an enable signal to gate counter increments, thus lowering power consumption. This does not add extra registers but improves timing and power efficiency by preventing unnecessary toggles when waiting for the start byte.
