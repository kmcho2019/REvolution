Use an explicit three-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Start in IDLE, waiting for a byte with in[3]=1 to detect the start of a message. Once seen, move to BYTE2 to accept the second byte. Regardless of in[3] during the second and third bytes, transition to BYTE3 for the third byte. After the third byte, output done=1 for one cycle and return to IDLE to detect the next message. This explicit FSM makes the states and transitions very clear and simple, and done is asserted only in the transition after the third byte. Reset synchronously returns FSM to IDLE. All logic updates on posedge clk.
