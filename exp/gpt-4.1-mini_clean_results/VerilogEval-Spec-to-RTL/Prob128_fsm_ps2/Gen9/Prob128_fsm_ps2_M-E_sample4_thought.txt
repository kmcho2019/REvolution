Implement a three-state FSM with explicit states: IDLE (waiting for start byte with in[3]=1), BYTE2 (received first byte), BYTE3 (received second byte). Transition from IDLE to BYTE2 upon detecting a start byte, then BYTE3 after second byte, and back to IDLE after the third byte while asserting done for one cycle. Use an enumerated type (localparam) for clarity, and ensure done is asserted only in the cycle immediately after receiving the third byte. Reset synchronously brings the FSM to IDLE and clears done. This approach clearly separates states and improves readability and maintainability.
