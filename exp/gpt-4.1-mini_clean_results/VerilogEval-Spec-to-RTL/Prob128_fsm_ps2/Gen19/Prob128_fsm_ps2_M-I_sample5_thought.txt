To optimize for better power and area without compromising performance, we can eliminate the continuous assignment for `start_byte` and directly check `in[3]` inside the always block, reducing combinational logic and potentially inferred gates. We can also optimize the done assertion logic by making done combinationally depend on the counter reaching 2, registered in the next cycle, instead of registering done explicitly. This reduces toggling of the done register, as done will only pulse for one cycle immediately after the third byte without extra register. Additionally, we will use a simple 2-bit counter as before but implement done as a combinational output, improving power by reducing output register toggling. The FSM counting logic remains synchronous and simple, and reset remains synchronous active-high. This approach reduces output toggling and potential area from removing the done register while maintaining the same performance.
