To optimize the 6-to-1 multiplexer for better area and power, I will use a simple combinational continuous assignment with a nested conditional (ternary) operator instead of a procedural always block. This approach can help synthesis tools generate a more compact and potentially faster multiplexer implementation, as it directly maps to a mux-tree structure without the overhead of a case statement. It also avoids the need for a reg output, reducing register inference. The logic remains fully combinational and covers all select cases 0 to 5, outputting zero otherwise.
