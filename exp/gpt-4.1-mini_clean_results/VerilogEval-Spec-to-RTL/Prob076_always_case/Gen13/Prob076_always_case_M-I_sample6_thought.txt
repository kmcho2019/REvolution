To optimize the PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will replace the concatenation and variable part-select with a straightforward case statement multiplexer. This removes the need for multiplication and slicing logic, potentially reducing critical path delay and switching activity, thus lowering power consumption. Using a case statement explicitly enumerates the outputs for each select value, helping synthesis tools infer minimal multiplexers without extra arithmetic. I will also keep the default case to output zero when sel is outside 0-5.
