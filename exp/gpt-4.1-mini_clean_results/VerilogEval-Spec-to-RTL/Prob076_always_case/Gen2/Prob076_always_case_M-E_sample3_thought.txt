Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a continuous assignment with a nested ternary (conditional) operator. This eliminates the need for an always block and reg output, making the design purely combinational with a wire output. It keeps the logic compact and straightforward. The output is assigned 0 if the selector is outside the range 0 to 5. This approach is concise, synthesizable, and easy to read, providing a different style from the previous case-based always block solution.
