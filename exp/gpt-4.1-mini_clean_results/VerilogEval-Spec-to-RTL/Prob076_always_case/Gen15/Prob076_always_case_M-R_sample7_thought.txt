I will refactor the 6-to-1 multiplexer by using a combinational always block with a case statement instead of a function. This approach explicitly models the multiplexer behavior with a procedural block, which some designers find more intuitive and straightforward for debugging. The always block is sensitive to all inputs (`*`), ensuring combinational logic without missing any signals. The output `out` is assigned inside the case based on `sel` and defaults to zero if `sel` is out of range. This structure avoids a function call and keeps logic clear and easily synthesizable.
