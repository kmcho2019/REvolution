I will implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a combinational always block with a case statement driven by sel. This approach uses a reg for the output and explicitly assigns the output based on sel. The advantage is clear procedural semantics, easy debugging, and intuitive mapping to hardware multiplexers. The default case outputs zero when sel is out of range (greater than 5). This approach also enables easier insertion of extra logic if needed later and is a classic style commonly used in RTL coding.
