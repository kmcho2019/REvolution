Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a parallel combination of 6 4-bit AND gates controlled by one-hot decoded sel signals, followed by a bitwise OR of all these intermediate results. First decode sel into a one-hot 6-bit signal using a combinational decoder; then AND each data input with the corresponding one-hot bit extended to 4 bits, finally OR all results to produce the output. This approach uses explicit decoder and bitwise logic instead of a case statement, showcasing a structural style and can potentially enable better parallel optimization in hardware.
