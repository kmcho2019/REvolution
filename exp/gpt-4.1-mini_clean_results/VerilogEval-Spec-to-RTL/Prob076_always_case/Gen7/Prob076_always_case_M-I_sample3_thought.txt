To optimize the multiplexer for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we can replace the case statement with a combinational continuous assignment using a compact ternary expression or a parallel mux using a concatenation and an indexed selection. This style often maps efficiently to multiplexers in hardware, potentially reducing area and improving performance by avoiding the explicit case construct. Also, by using a wire output instead of a reg and avoiding an always block, the design is simpler and might incur fewer registers and less switching power.
