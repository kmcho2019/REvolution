To fuse the best ideas from both examples, I will implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a continuous assignment with a packed array of data inputs indexed by sel. This approach removes the need for a reg output and always block, potentially improving synthesis optimization and reducing area. Also, it clearly handles the default case by outputting zero when sel is out of range (6 or 7). This dataflow style mux is clean, efficient, and succinct, leveraging synthesis tools' abilities to optimize indexed arrays. It yields better performance and area while keeping the design fully combinational and straightforward.
