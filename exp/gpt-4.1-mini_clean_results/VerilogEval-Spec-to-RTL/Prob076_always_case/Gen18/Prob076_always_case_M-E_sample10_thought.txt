Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a continuous assignment with a nested ternary conditional operator. This approach directly expresses the selection logic without an always block or case statement. It is concise and synthesizes to a priority encoder of conditions that select the appropriate 4-bit input. For sel values outside 0 to 5, output zero. This method yields a purely combinational logic with a single assignment, which can be preferred for its succinctness and readability in certain coding styles.
