To optimize for better PPA, I will replace the function-based mux with a combinational always block using a case statement. This removes the function call overhead and can help synthesis tools generate more optimized mux logic, potentially reducing area and power. Additionally, by assigning the default output zero at the start of the always block, we avoid unintended latches and ensure clarity. This approach results in a straightforward, flat mux implementation that synthesizes efficiently and minimizes redundant logic, helping with both power and area without affecting timing.
