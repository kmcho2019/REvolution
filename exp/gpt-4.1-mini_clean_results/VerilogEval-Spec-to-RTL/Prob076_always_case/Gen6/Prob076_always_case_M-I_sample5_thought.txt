To optimize the 6-to-1 multiplexer for better power and area while maintaining clarity and synthesis friendliness, use a continuous assign statement with a vector of concatenated inputs indexed by 'sel'. This removes the always block and case statement, enabling the synthesis tool to infer a clean multiplexer with potentially reduced glitches and simpler mux tree, lowering power and area. The 'sel' values 6 and 7, which are out of range, are handled by outputting zero by extending the vector and using a conditional expression. This approach provides a compact, elegant, and efficient mux implementation.
