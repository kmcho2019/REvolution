Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer by using a 64-bit wide concatenation of all six 4-bit inputs, placed in order, and use a shifted right logic to select the appropriate 4-bit chunk by shifting the concatenated vector by 4*sel bits. When sel is out of range (>5), output zero. This approach uses bit slicing and shifting operations instead of a case statement or function, which can be optimized by synthesis tools into a barrel shifter or multiplexer network. It avoids explicit enumeration and can be compact and elegant.
