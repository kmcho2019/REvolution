The original design is already clean and efficient for this small 6-to-1 mux. One small optimization to further reduce power and area is to replace the always block with a continuous assignment using a combinational conditional (ternary) operator or a small ROM-style vector indexed by sel. Using a vector indexed by sel is both concise and synthesizes into an efficient multiplexer with minimal logic. This removes the always block and reg type declaration, which might improve synthesis inference. It also reduces code complexity slightly, possibly leading to minimal area and power benefits while maintaining zero output for out-of-range sel values.
