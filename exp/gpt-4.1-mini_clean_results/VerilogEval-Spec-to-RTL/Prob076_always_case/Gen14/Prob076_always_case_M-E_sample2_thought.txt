Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer by concatenating all six 4-bit inputs into a single 24-bit vector and using a variable part-select indexed by the 3-bit select input. This approach treats the inputs as segments of a wide bus and extracts the selected 4-bit chunk directly. This method is concise, synthesizes to a straightforward mux tree, and uses a single combinational assignment instead of a case statement, offering an elegant alternative for mux implementation.
