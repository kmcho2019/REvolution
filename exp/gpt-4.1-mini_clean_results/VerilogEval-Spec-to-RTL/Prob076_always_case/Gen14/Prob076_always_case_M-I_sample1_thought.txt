To optimize the existing combinational multiplexer for better power and area, we can implement the mux using a continuous assignment with a nested ternary operator instead of an always block and case statement. This often synthesizes into a more compact and potentially faster structure with less logic overhead. It also eliminates the need for a reg output and an always block, which can marginally improve power and area by reducing inferred logic and routing. The functional behavior remains the same, outputting zero when sel is outside 0-5.
