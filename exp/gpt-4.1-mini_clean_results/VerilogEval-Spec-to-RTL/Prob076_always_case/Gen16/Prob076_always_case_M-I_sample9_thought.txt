To optimize the 6-to-1 multiplexer for better power and potentially slightly improved timing, I will replace the case statement with a continuous assignment using a nested ternary (?:) operator. This approach allows synthesis tools to optimize the multiplexer tree more efficiently, potentially reducing logic levels and toggling power. Additionally, using a wire output rather than reg with an always block removes unnecessary procedural logic and registers, saving area and improving clarity. The output defaults to zero when sel is outside 0-5, matching original functionality.
