To optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA), I will implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a continuous assignment with a chained conditional (ternary) operator instead of an always block with a case statement. This approach often maps directly to efficient hardware multiplexers, minimizing logic levels and potentially reducing both area and delay. The combinational logic is clearer and may enable synthesis tools to produce optimized mux trees. Also, it avoids the overhead of a procedural block and the register type output. The default output for out when sel is outside 0 to 5 will be zero as specified.
