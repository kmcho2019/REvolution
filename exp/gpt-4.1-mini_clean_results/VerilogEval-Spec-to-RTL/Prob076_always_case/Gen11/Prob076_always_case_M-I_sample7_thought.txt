To optimize for area and power while maintaining performance, replace the function-based multiplexer with a simple continuous assignment using a ternary operator chain indexed by 'sel'. This approach directly synthesizes into a mux tree, often yielding simpler logic and fewer gate levels than a function with a case statement. It also avoids potential overhead from function calls and enables synthesis tools to better optimize the mux. Additionally, zero output when sel is out of range is easily enforced by comparing sel against the maximum valid index (5). This keeps code concise, clear, and potentially reduces power and area slightly with no impact on timing.
