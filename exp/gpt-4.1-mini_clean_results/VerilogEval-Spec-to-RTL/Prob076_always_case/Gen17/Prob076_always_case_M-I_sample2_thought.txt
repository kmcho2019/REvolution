To optimize for power and area while maintaining the same functionality, restructure the 6-to-1 mux as a two-level mux tree: first use two 3-to-1 multiplexers selecting among pairs of inputs, then one 2-to-1 mux to select between the outputs of the 3-to-1 muxes. This hierarchical approach often maps better to hardware mux primitives, reducing the logic depth, and may help reduce power consumption due to reduced switching activity and simpler gates. Also use continuous assignments and avoid an always block to give synthesis more freedom to optimize. Keep output as reg only if necessary; here we can use wire outputs driven by assign statements to enable simpler netlist and possibly reduced area.
