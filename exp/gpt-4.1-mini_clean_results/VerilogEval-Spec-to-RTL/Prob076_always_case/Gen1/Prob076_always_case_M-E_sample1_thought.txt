Implement the 6-to-1 multiplexer using a combinational continuous assignment statement with a large concatenated vector of all data inputs, and use the selector as an index into this vector. This avoids explicit case or if-else statements and leverages Verilog's indexing to select the appropriate 4-bit segment from the concatenation of data inputs. When the selector is out of range (i.e., > 5), output zero by comparing sel to 6 and using a conditional operator.
