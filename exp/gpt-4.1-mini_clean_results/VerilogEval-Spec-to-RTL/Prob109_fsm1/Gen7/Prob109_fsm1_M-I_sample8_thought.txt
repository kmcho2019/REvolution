The FSM is already simple and optimal in structure for only two states, but we can slightly improve power and area by implementing one-hot encoding for the two states to avoid the multiplexing logic on state transition. Using one-hot encoding can reduce toggling on internal signals and ease synthesis optimizations. Also, we can make output a registered signal to align it with the Moore machine property (output depends only on state) explicitly. The asynchronous reset logic will remain intact.

This improvement trades off one more flip-flop (total 2 flip-flops instead of 1) but potentially reduces combinational logic and glitching, improving power and timing metrics marginally.
