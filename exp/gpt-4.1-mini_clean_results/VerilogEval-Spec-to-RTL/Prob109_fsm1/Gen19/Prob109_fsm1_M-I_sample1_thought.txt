To improve the PPA, I will separate the next state logic into a combinational always block and keep the sequential always block purely for state updates with nonblocking assignments. This separation enhances synthesis optimizations by clarifying combinational vs sequential logic boundaries. Using nonblocking assignments consistently avoids potential simulation/synthesis mismatches and timing issues. The output remains assigned directly from the state register. This structure typically results in better timing closure (improved TNS/WNS) and clean logic synthesis, while power and area remain minimal due to the FSM simplicity.
