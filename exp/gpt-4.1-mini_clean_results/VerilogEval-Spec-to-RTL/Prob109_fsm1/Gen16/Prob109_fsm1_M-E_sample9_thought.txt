Implement the FSM using one-hot encoding with two flip-flops representing states A and B explicitly. This approach uses two flip-flops: state_A and state_B, where only one is asserted at a time. Next state logic is derived directly from input and current states without a case statement. The asynchronous reset sets state_B high and state_A low. The output is taken from the state_B flip-flop to match the requirement that output=1 in state B and 0 in state A. This architectural style makes state decoding simple and can ease timing and enhance readability for larger FSMs.
