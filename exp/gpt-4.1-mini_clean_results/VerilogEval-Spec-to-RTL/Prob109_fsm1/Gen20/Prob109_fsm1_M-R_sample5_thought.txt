I will refactor the FSM to use a single always block triggered on both the asynchronous reset and the clock edge, but instead of separate next-state combinational logic, I will encode the next state directly inside the sequential logic using a case statement with nonblocking assignments. The output will be registered explicitly to reinforce the Moore machine nature, instead of continuously assigned from the state. This structure simplifies the design by avoiding a separate next-state signal and clearly shows output assignment in the sequential domain.
