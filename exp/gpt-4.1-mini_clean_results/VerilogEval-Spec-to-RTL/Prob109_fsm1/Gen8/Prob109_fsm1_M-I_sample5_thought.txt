To optimize the design for better PPA without changing functionality, encode the states explicitly as one-hot, which can reduce combinational logic complexity and potentially improve timing by simplifying next-state logic. This also simplifies the next-state logic to straightforward multiplexers. Additionally, output the Moore output from the state flip-flops directly, which remains the same as the previous state encoding (B=1, output=1; A=0, output=0). The asynchronous reset logic remains the same, resetting the flip-flop corresponding to state B. This approach maintains simplicity, potentially reduces combinational delay, and can improve power by reducing toggling in combinational logic. Area might increase slightly due to an extra flip-flop, but timing and power are prioritized for better overall PPA.
