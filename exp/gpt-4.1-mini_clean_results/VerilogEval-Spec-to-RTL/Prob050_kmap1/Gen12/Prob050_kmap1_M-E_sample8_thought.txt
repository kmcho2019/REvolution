The function can be directly derived from the Karnaugh map as out = a OR b OR c, since the output is only 0 when all inputs are 0. To offer a novel architectural approach distinct from direct OR or mux-based designs, I will implement the function using a priority encoder concept. 

I will create a small hierarchy of three 1-bit comparators checking if each input is 1, then combine their outputs with a simple OR gate. Instead of just OR-ing directly, this architecture models each input as a 'valid' signal and aggregates these signals to generate the output. This structure is modular and can be extended easily.

The modules will be:
- Comparator1: outputs 1 if input is 1, else 0
- Or3Combiner: 3-input OR gate for the comparator outputs
- TopModule: instantiates three comparators and one OR gate to produce out

This approach adds conceptual clarity by viewing inputs as valid flags and demonstrates hierarchical modular design with a distinct structure from previous examples.
