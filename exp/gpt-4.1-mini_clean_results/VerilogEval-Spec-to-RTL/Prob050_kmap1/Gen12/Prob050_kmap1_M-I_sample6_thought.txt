Optimize the implementation by expressing the logic directly with a Boolean equation instead of using a multiplexer module. From the Karnaugh map, the output is 1 when (b or c) is 1, or when b=0, c=0 and a=1. This can be written as out = (b | c) | (~b & ~c & a). Implementing this expression uses fewer gates and reduces logic levels, improving performance and area, while also lowering switching activity to reduce power.
