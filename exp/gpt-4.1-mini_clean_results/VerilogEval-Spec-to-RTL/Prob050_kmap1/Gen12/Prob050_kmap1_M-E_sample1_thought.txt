Implement the logic using a simple 2-to-1 multiplexer controlled by the variable 'a'. When 'a' is 0, the output depends on b OR c; when 'a' is 1, output is 1 regardless of b and c (based on K-map values). This approach uses a small mux structure that clearly maps the K-map logic and can be synthesized efficiently, showing a structural style alternative to direct OR.

Specifically:
- If a=0, out = b | c
- If a=1, out = 1

This matches the K-map truth table and offers a clean, modular design leveraging a 2:1 mux primitive.
