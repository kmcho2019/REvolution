The original solution used a separate 3-input OR gate module instantiation, which adds hierarchy overhead. Since the logic is simple, implementing the OR function directly with a continuous assignment inside TopModule will reduce area and potentially power by eliminating module instantiation overhead. This also reduces routing complexity and delay due to fewer nets and hierarchy boundaries.

The improved solution thus replaces the Or3 module instantiation with a single-line continuous assign statement for out = a | b | c.

This keeps the design minimal and clear, improves area and power slightly, and maintains optimal performance since the logic depth is minimal.
