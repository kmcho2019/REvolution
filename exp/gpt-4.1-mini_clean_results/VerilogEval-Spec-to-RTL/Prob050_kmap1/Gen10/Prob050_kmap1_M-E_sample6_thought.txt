Instead of implementing the logic function as a single OR gate of three inputs, I will create a 2-level hierarchical design that mimics the K-map coverage using multiplexers. The idea is to use input 'a' as the select signal of a 2-to-1 mux, and inputs 'b' and 'c' to generate the mux inputs, effectively dividing the input space into two parts: a=0 and a=1.

From the K-map, when a=0, the output depends on b and c, and when a=1, output is always 1. We can implement the output as:
out = a + f(b,c)

Here f(b,c) = 1 except when b=0 and c=0 (out=0).

So f(b,c) = b OR c.

The implementation uses a 2-input OR gate for b and c, then a 2-to-1 mux controlled by a, which outputs 1 when a=1 and outputs b|c when a=0.

This structural approach models the decision tree implied by the K-map and demonstrates hierarchical design with a MUX and OR gates, providing a clear, modular, and scalable structure different from the direct OR gate implementation.
