The Karnaugh map outputs 0 only when all inputs are zero (a=0, b=0, c=0) and 1 otherwise.

This function can be directly implemented as a 3-input OR gate: out = a | b | c.

Using a single OR gate minimizes the logic depth, gate count, power consumption, and area compared to the previous solution that used inverters and a 3-input AND gate.

This approach is simpler, more efficient, and functionally equivalent.
