The original design is functionally correct but uses two 2-input OR gate modules to implement a simple 3-input OR. This adds unnecessary module overhead, slightly increasing area and power and potentially affecting timing.

To optimize PPA, I will simplify the design by using a single continuous assignment statement implementing the OR of a, b, and c directly. This eliminates the intermediate wire and module instantiations, reducing area and power with no impact on functionality or timing since it remains a purely combinational OR logic.

This single-line implementation is more efficient and idiomatic in Verilog for simple combinational logic.
