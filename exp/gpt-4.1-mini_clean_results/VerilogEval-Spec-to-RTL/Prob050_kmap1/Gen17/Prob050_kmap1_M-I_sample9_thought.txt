The original design used one Or2 module for (a | b) and a direct OR for combining with c, incurring an extra gate instance and a wire. Since the function is simply out = a | b | c, we can optimize for better PPA by:

- Eliminating the intermediate Or2 module instantiation and wire.
- Directly implementing out = a | b | c using a single assign statement.
- This reduces area (fewer gates and wiring), reduces power (fewer transitions and loads), and maintains minimal logic depth, so performance remains excellent.

This approach is more optimal in area and power while preserving the same functionality and simplicity.
