To create a novel architectural approach distinct from the direct OR or explicit gate instantiations, I will implement the function by first generating partial negations and intermediate signals, then reconstructing the OR logic using NAND and NOT gates in a mixed structural and behavioral style.

Specifically, the implementation will:
- Use De Morgan's law to realize out = a + b + c as out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c).
- Implement this by NAND gates and NOT gates only, showing a different structural style than a direct OR.
- The design will contain explicit intermediate signals and modular subcomponents (NAND2, INV) instantiated structurally.

This approach highlights an alternative gate-level synthesis that might be favorable for technologies where NAND and NOT are primitive and have better PPA characteristics compared to OR gates. It is a novel architecture illustrating how the OR function can be built from NAND and inverter primitives.
