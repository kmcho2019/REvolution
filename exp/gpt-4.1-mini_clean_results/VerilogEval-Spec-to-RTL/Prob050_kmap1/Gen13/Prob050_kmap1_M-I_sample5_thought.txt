The function implemented by the Karnaugh map simplifies to out = a | b | c. The previous implementation used a separate Or3 module to implement this, which adds unnecessary module overhead and wiring complexity. To optimize for power, area, and synthesis efficiency, I will remove the extra Or3 module and directly assign out = a | b | c inside the TopModule. This eliminates hierarchical overhead and allows synthesis tools to infer a single 3-input OR gate, improving area and power slightly without impacting timing. This direct assignment is simpler and yields minimal and clean hardware.
