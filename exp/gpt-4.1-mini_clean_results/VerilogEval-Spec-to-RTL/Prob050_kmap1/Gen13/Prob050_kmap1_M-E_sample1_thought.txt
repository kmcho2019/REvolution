Implement the Karnaugh map logic using a hierarchical approach by decomposing the function into two smaller OR gates feeding into a final OR gate. Specifically:

- First OR gate combines inputs b and c.
- Second OR gate passes input a.
- Final output is OR of the two intermediate results.

This breaks the direct a|b|c into a two-level tree structure, which might be beneficial for certain architectures that prefer balanced logic depth, and it explicitly shows modularity for potential reuse or testability.

Additionally, the design uses a structural modeling style with assign statements, providing clear modularity without extra wires or complex conditions.
