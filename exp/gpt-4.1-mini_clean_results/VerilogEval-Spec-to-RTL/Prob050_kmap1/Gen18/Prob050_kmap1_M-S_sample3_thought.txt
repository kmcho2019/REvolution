The given function outputs 0 only when all inputs (a, b, c) are 0, and outputs 1 otherwise. This corresponds exactly to the logical OR of the inputs: out = a | b | c. 

Therefore, the simplest and most efficient implementation is a single OR gate of the three inputs.

This approach:

- Minimizes gate count (only one OR gate).
- Minimizes propagation delay (one gate delay).
- Reduces power consumption and area.

Hence, the TopModule simply assigns out = a | b | c.
