The original implementation uses three inverters and a 3-input AND gate followed by an inverter, which results in more gate levels and higher area and power.

The Karnaugh map logic corresponds to the function out = a | b | c, which can be implemented with a single OR gate, significantly reducing gate count, delay, and power consumption.

Therefore, to optimize PPA, I will implement the function directly as a 3-input OR gate with no intermediate inversions. This will reduce gate delay and area and simplify the design.

This straightforward implementation matches the logic from the Karnaugh map and optimizes timing, power, and area.
