The Karnaugh map specifies the output logic as 0 only when all inputs are zero; otherwise, the output is 1. Another way to express this function is as the complement of the AND of the complements of inputs: out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c). This is equivalent to an OR function but realized structurally as a NOR of three inverted inputs.

To implement this idea structurally:
- First, invert each input.
- Then AND the inverted inputs.
- Finally, invert the AND output to get the final output.

This approach illustrates a simple structural design involving gates (INV, AND, INV) rather than a single-level OR gate. It uses basic logic gates, useful for educational purposes and showing an alternative way to derive the logic from the Karnaugh map.
