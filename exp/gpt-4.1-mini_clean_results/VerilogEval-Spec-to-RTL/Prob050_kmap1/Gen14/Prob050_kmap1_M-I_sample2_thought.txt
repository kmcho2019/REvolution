The given design implements out = a | b | c using a parameterized 3-input OR gate module. While modular and clear, this abstraction adds minimal overhead without benefits in such a simple circuit. The function is just a simple OR of three inputs.

To improve power, performance, and area (PPA), I will:
- Remove the Or3 module and directly implement the output as a continuous assignment using the bitwise OR operator.
- This eliminates one level of module hierarchy and intermediate wires.
- Continuous assignments synthesize optimally to a single OR gate or an equivalent minimal gate.
- This also simplifies code and reduces code size without impacting functionality.
- The simplification will reduce area slightly, marginally improve timing (one less instantiation), and slightly reduce power due to less fanout/load and no intermediate nets.

This approach is the most straightforward, minimal, and optimal implementation for the given logic function.
