To optimize the design for better power and area, I will eliminate the separate NOT gate modules and the explicit three-input NAND gate instantiation. Instead, I will directly implement the function using a single assign statement with bitwise operators, which synthesizes efficiently into minimal logic. The logic is out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c), equivalent to out = (a | b | c). This reduces gate count, interconnects, and module overhead, thus improving power and area while keeping zero delay in timing.
