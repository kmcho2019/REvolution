The original design uses a separate 3-input OR module instantiation to implement out = a | b | c. This adds module overhead without reducing gate count or delay. 

To optimize power and area further while keeping the design functionally equivalent and synthesis-friendly, I will:

- Remove the Or3 module instantiation and directly use a continuous assignment inside TopModule.
- This eliminates hierarchy overhead, potentially reduces area and routing complexity.
- The logic remains minimal and efficient, as it's a simple OR of three inputs.
- This approach also reduces switching activity by minimizing logic depth.

Overall, this reduces area and power slightly without affecting performance or clarity.
