The original solution implements the function using a 4-to-1 multiplexer with select lines b and c. While functionally correct, it uses more gates than necessary, causing extra area and power usage. The Karnaugh map implies the function is:

out = 1 except when a=0 and b=c=0, where out=0.

Expressing this directly:

out = (b | c) | (~b & ~c & a)

This expression uses fewer gates and reduces logic depth, improving timing, area, and power by minimizing unnecessary logic toggling and gate count. Implementing this expression directly in combinational logic is optimal for the given function.
