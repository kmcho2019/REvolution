The output function from the K-map is out = a OR b OR c. Instead of using modular Or2 and Or3 gates which add extra gate delays, area, and power overhead due to additional instantiations and wiring, we can directly implement the output with a continuous assignment using a single-level OR expression. This approach minimizes logic depth, reduces gate count, and lowers power consumption by eliminating intermediate signals and modules. This is the most optimal implementation for this simple combinational logic.
