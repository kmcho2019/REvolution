The Karnaugh map has inputs b and c on one axis, a on the other, and defines output values. From the K-map:

          a
   bc   0   1
   00 | 0 | 1 |
   01 | 1 | 1 |
   11 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 1 | 1 |

Reading this, output is zero only when (a=0, b=0, c=0). In all other cases, output is 1.

Therefore, the output can be expressed as:
out = a + b + c

This is because when all inputs are 0, output=0; else output=1.
