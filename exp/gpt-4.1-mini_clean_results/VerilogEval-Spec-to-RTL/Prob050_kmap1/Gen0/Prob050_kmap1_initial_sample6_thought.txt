From the Karnaugh map, we analyze the output for combinations of inputs a, b, and c.

The K-map is:

      a
 bc  0  1
 00 |0| 1|
 01 |1| 1|
 11 |1| 1|
 10 |1| 1|

Rows correspond to bc in order: 00, 01, 11, 10
Columns correspond to a: 0, 1

Output is 0 only when (bc=00 and a=0), all others 1.

So the output is zero only for a=0, b=0, c=0.

Hence, output is 1 except when a=0 & b=0 & c=0.

Therefore, output = ~(~a & ~b & ~c) = a + b + c.

So, the module will assign out = a | b | c.
