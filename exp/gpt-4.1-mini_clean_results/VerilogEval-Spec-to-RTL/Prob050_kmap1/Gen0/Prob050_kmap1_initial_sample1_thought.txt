The Karnaugh map covers inputs b and c as rows and input a as columns.
From the map:
- When bc=00 and a=0, out=0; otherwise, out=1.
This means the output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, and c=0; otherwise, it's 1.
So the function can be expressed as:
out = !(~a & ~b & ~c)
which simplifies to:
out = a | b | c
