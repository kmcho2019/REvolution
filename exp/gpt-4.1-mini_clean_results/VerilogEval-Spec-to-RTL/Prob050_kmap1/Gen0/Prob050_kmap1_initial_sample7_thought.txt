From the Karnaugh map, we list all minterms with output 1:

When a=0:
- bc=01, output=1
- bc=10, output=1
- bc=11, output=1

When a=1:
- bc=00, output=1
- bc=01, output=1
- bc=10, output=1
- bc=11, output=1

Simplifying:
- For a=1, output=1 regardless of b,c (so output=1)
- For a=0, output=1 except for bc=00 (b=0,c=0)

Therefore, output = a + (b + c)

In boolean expression: out = a + b + c

We can implement this as a simple OR of a,b,c.
