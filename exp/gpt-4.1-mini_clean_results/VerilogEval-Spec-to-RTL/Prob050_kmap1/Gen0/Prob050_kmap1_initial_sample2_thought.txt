From the Karnaugh map:

       a
 bc   0   1
00 | 0 | 1
01 | 1 | 1
11 | 1 | 1
10 | 1 | 1

We see that the output is 0 only when b=0, c=0, a=0.
In all other cases, output = 1.

This can be implemented as:
out = (b OR c) OR a

which simplifies to:
out = a + b + c

So the output is 0 only when all three inputs are zero, but since in the K-map it is zero only at a=0, b=0, c=0 (top left corner), and one in all other entries.

Therefore, the output is the logical OR of inputs a, b, and c.
