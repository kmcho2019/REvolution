The original solution uses a separate 3-input OR gate module, which is clean and structurally clear but adds slight area and power overhead due to module instantiation and additional hierarchy.

To optimize for area and power while preserving clarity and correctness:

- Replace the separate Or3 module and its instantiation with a single continuous assignment in TopModule.
- The logic is very simple: out = a | b | c.
- This reduces module overhead and potential routing complexity, thus improving power and area.
- The continuous assignment is synthesis-friendly and typically maps to a single OR gate in hardware.
- This minimal implementation maintains zero delay logic depth and the same function.

This approach streamlines the design to its simplest form, improving power and area metrics while keeping functional correctness intact.
