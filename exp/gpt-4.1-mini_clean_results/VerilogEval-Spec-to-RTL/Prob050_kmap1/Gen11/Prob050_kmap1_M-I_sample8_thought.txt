The original design uses two 2-input OR gate modules to implement out = a | b | c, which is functionally correct but incurs slight overhead in area and power due to multiple instantiated modules. 

To optimize for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA):
- Replace the two 2-input OR gate instances with a single continuous assignment using the built-in OR operator for three inputs.
- This allows the synthesis tool to implement the logic as a minimal gate, potentially a single 3-input OR gate or a more optimized structure.
- The design becomes more concise, reduces gate count, and can improve both power and area without impacting performance.
- The module remains readable and fully synthesizable.

This approach improves PPA by enabling better synthesis optimization and removing unnecessary module overhead.
