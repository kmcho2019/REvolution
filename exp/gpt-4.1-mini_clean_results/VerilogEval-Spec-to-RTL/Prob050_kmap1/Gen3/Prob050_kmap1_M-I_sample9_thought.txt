The original function simplifies to out = a + b + c, implemented as out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c). This is already minimal in area and performance since it is a simple OR function. To further optimize power and area, we can implement the function as a single 3-input OR gate directly: out = a | b | c. This avoids the inverted inputs and an extra NOT gate, potentially reducing power by minimizing gate transitions and saves area by reducing the number of gates.

Since the logic is trivial, implementing a direct OR is the simplest and most efficient approach. This is a standard optimization to reduce inverters and gate count.

The output will match the Karnaugh map exactly.

We therefore replace the NAND3 + inverter style with a direct OR of inputs.
