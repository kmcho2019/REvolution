The Karnaugh map shows that the output is zero only when all inputs are zero; otherwise, it is one. Instead of directly ORing the inputs, we can implement this logic using a NOR gate followed by an inverter: the output is the negation of the NOR of the inputs. This approach uses a NOR gate feeding into a NOT gate, representing the function as out = ~(~(a|b|c)) which is logically equivalent to out = a|b|c.
