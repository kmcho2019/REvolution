Refactor the module by expressing the output as a direct combinational Boolean expression instead of using a multiplexer module. From the K-map, the output is 1 for all cases except when b=0, c=0, and a=0; otherwise, output is 1. This can be written as out = (b OR c) OR (NOT b AND NOT c AND a). Using this expression removes the need for the MUX module and simplifies the logic to straightforward assign statements, improving readability and possibly synthesis results.
