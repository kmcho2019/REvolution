The original solution correctly implements the function using a 4-to-1 multiplexer, but this adds unnecessary logic levels and gates. The Karnaugh map's minimized Boolean expression is:

out = (b | c) | (~b & ~c & a)

This means output is 1 if either b or c is 1, or if both b and c are 0 and a is 1. Implementing this expression directly with simple gates reduces logic depth, area, and switching activity, improving performance, power, and area (PPA). We'll share inverted signals for ~b and ~c to minimize gate count and use simple OR and AND gates to realize the function efficiently.
