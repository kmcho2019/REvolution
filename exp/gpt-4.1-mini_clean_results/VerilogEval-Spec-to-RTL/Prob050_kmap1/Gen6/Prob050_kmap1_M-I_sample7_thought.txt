The original design uses an explicit 3-input OR gate module instantiated inside TopModule, which is clear but slightly increases area and power due to hierarchy and possible routing overhead.

To optimize power and area while preserving correctness and minimal delay, I will inline the OR logic directly in TopModule using a continuous assignment statement. This eliminates module instantiation overhead, reduces routing complexity, and slightly lowers power by minimizing the logic hierarchy.

This direct RTL assignment preserves the minimal gate count (one OR3), ensures no extra gate delays are introduced, and maintains the function exactly as required by the Karnaugh map.

Thus, the improvement strategy is to replace the structural Or3 module with a simple continuous assignment in TopModule for better PPA.
