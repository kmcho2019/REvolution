The previous design correctly implements the function out = a + b + c using a structural 3-input OR gate module. To optimize for power and area, we can eliminate the extra module instantiation by using a direct continuous assignment inside TopModule. This reduces hierarchy and simplifies synthesis, thus lowering area and power with no performance penalty, since the logic is minimal and purely combinational.

This approach retains clarity and functional correctness while improving overall PPA by reducing gate count and interconnect complexity.
