The original solution uses three separate NOT gate modules and one 3-input NAND gate module, which incurs extra area and power overhead due to multiple instantiated modules and interconnects. We can optimize the design by replacing these with a single continuous assignment that uses Verilog's built-in logical operators, eliminating the need for explicit NOT and NAND gate modules. This reduces gate count, module overhead, and routing complexity, improving power and area metrics without affecting functionality or timing. The function out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c) can be directly expressed as out = a | b | c.
