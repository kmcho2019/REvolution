The given design correctly implements the function using a 4-to-1 multiplexer, but this adds extra logic levels and some unnecessary switching since three inputs are tied to constant 1. To improve PPA (performance, power, area), a direct Boolean expression implementation is better.

From the K-map, the function can be expressed as:
out = (b | c) | (~b & ~c & a)

This means:
- If either b or c is 1, output is 1.
- Else if b=0 and c=0, output equals a.

This requires fewer gates and less logic depth than the mux approach.

By sharing the inverted signals (~b and ~c), gate count and power are minimized. Also, the direct gate-level implementation improves timing by reducing the logic levels to two.

This approach reduces static and dynamic power (less switching on unused mux inputs), reduces gate count (area), and improves critical path delay (performance).
