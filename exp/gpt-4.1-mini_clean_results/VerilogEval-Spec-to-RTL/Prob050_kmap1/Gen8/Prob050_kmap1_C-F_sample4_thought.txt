The Karnaugh map output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0, and 1 otherwise, which corresponds to the function out = a OR b OR c. The first example structurally implements this with inverters and a NAND gate, leading to more gates and area usage. The second uses hierarchical OR gates, but with extra modules and intermediate wires adding overhead.

The superior fusion approach is to directly assign out = a | b | c in a single continuous assignment statement inside the TopModule. This combines the clarity and minimal logic depth of Example 2 with the simplicity of a direct behavioral implementation, avoiding extra modules and interconnects from Example 1. This reduces power and area, eliminates unnecessary gate delays, and simplifies the code while maintaining perfect functional correctness. 

Thus, no separate modules for OR or NOT gates are instantiated. The design is minimal, readable, and optimal for synthesis.
