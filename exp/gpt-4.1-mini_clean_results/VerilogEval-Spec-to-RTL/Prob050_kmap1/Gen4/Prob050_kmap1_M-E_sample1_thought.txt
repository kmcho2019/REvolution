From the K-map, out is 0 only for input combination (a,b,c)=000, and 1 for all other combinations. This behavior matches the function: out = ~(~a & ~b & ~c), i.e., the negation of the AND of the inverted inputs. We can implement this using inverters and a three-input NAND gate to realize the function structurally but differently from the previous OR approach. This design will use three NOT gates and one 3-input AND gate followed by an inverter, or more simply, a 3-input NAND gate with inverted inputs. I'll instantiate primitive NOT and NAND gates as modules and connect them accordingly to illustrate this structural implementation.
