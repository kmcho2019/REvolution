The Karnaugh map can be simplified by carefully analyzing the given map and noting that 'd' inputs are don't-cares and can be chosen to simplify the expression. Observing the map, the output is 1 for all cases except when a=0 and b=0 and c=0, except where don't cares apply. The simplified logic after Karnaugh map minimization is out = a | b | c. Since 'd' is don't care, it can be ignored in the implementation. This reduces the logic to a single OR gate without any intermediate signals or always blocks, improving area, power, and timing.
