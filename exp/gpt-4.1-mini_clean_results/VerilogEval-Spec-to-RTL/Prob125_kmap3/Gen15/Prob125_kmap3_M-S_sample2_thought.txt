Since the original simplified expression is out = a | (c & ~b), and 'a' alone covers many cases, further simplification can be achieved by noticing that when a=1, out=1 regardless of other inputs, and when a=0, out=1 only if c=1 and b=0. The minimal expression is already quite simple and uses just OR and AND gates. However, we can implement this directly with just basic gates (OR and AND with inverter) and inline assignments without intermediate variables or always blocks, which is already done. Any further reduction risks changing functionality. Thus, the existing expression is already minimal and cannot be simplified further in terms of logic complexity or gate count.
