The existing solution uses three overlapping AND terms ORed together: (a & ~c) | (c & ~b) | (c & a). This can be factored to minimize gate count and switching activity by grouping the terms involving 'c' to reduce repeated usage of 'c'. The expression can be rewritten as (a & ~c) | (c & (~b | a)) which uses only two AND terms and one OR term, reducing the total gate count and shared signals. This should reduce power by minimizing the number of repeated signals switching, and area by having fewer gates, while preserving the same logic function.
