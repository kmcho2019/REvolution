Implement the Karnaugh map using a 4-to-1 multiplexer approach by encoding inputs b and a as the select lines and c, d as inputs to the multiplexer. Since d is don't-care, we can treat it as 0 or 1 where convenient. This structure offers a modular and clear way to realize the function by explicitly mapping each combination of a and b to a small logic expression involving c and d. This approach demonstrates combinational multiplexing based on input variables rather than a direct Boolean expression.
