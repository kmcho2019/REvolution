Analyze and simplify the Karnaugh map logic to its minimal Boolean expression. The original terms had overlaps and redundancy. By careful K-map reduction (treating d as don't care), the simplified expression is:

out = a | (c & ~b)

This expression is minimal and can be implemented with a single continuous assignment, eliminating the always block and intermediate wires. This reduces area, power, and potentially improves timing by lowering gate count and logic depth.
