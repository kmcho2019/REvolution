Given the Karnaugh map and the don't-care conditions, the minimal expression is already very simple: out = a | (c & ~b). The input d is don't-care and can be ignored. Since the goal is to simplify further, we notice that "a" alone covers many minterms, and the remaining logic only depends on c and b. Using basic gates and direct boolean expressions is already optimal. Any attempt to simplify the expression further either increases complexity or reduces clarity. Therefore, the simplest and cleanest implementation is to assign out directly as a OR (c AND NOT b).
