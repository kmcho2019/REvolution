- Out is 1 for:
  - ab=10 or 11 and cd=00, 11, 10 (multiple 1s in those columns)

Grouping 1s and ignoring don't-cares, the minimal expression is:
out = c & a | c & ~b | a & ~c
which simplifies to out = a | (c & ~b)
