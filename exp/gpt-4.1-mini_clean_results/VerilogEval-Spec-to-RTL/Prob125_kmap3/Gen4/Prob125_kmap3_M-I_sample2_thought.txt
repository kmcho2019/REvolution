To potentially improve PPA slightly:
- Factor the expression to reduce the number of gates or logic depth, possibly lowering dynamic power by reducing transitions.
- The original expression is out = (c & (a | ~b)) | (a & ~c).
- This can be factored as out = a | (c & ~b), which is logically equivalent:

Check equivalence:
a | (c & ~b) covers all cases where a=1 (covers the rows where a=1)
and when a=0, out=1 only if c=1 and b=0, matching the original truth table.

Simplifying from two terms to one OR with an AND inside reduces logic depth and gate count, potentially improving power and timing.

Therefore, update assign to:
out = a | (c & ~b);
