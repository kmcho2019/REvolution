Implement the Karnaugh map function using a small 4-to-1 multiplexer controlled by two of the inputs, and use a case statement to encode the required outputs for the combinations of the other inputs. Since d is a don't-care, we can simplify the number of entries. Specifically, use inputs c and d as the select signals of the MUX, and inputs a and b to determine the data inputs. This structural approach demonstrates how to realize the logic via multiplexers, providing a different architectural viewpoint than purely boolean equations.
