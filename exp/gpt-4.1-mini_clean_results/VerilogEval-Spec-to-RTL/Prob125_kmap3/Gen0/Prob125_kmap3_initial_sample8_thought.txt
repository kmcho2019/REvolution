First, decode the Karnaugh map into a minimal sum-of-products expression. The map is for variables a,b on columns and c,d on rows with d as don't-care. Use the map to find minimized expressions including don't-cares. Then implement the logic in Verilog.

Map:
      ab
cd  01  00  10  11
00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

We write the cell values with the exact inputs:

a b | 00 01 11 10 (assuming order 00,01,11,10)
c d
00 (0,0): d(01),0(00),1(10),1(11)
01 (0,1): 0(01),0(00),d(10),d(11)
11 (1,1): 0(01),1(00),1(10),1(11)
10 (1,0): 0(01),1(00),1(10),1(11)

Rearranging rows and cols:

We might want to reorder or check variables. Since the problem gives a and b on columns (01,00,10,11), c and d on rows (00,01,11,10).

Now transform ab and cd from two-bit to signals:

a= MSB of ab, b=LSB of ab
Similarly, c=MSB of cd, d=LSB of cd

So ab order is 01(b=1,a=0), 00(b=0,a=0), 10(b=0,a=1), 11(b=1,a=1)
cd order is 00(d=0,c=0), 01(d=1,c=0), 11(d=1,c=1), 10(d=0,c=1)

Check each cell's minterm:

- For output 1s (ignoring don't cares) list minterms:
From cd=00(d=0,c=0):

  ab=10(b=0,a=1): a=1,b=0,c=0,d=0 -> a b' c' d'

  ab=11(b=1,a=1): a=1,b=1,c=0,d=0 -> a b c' d'

From cd=11(d=1,c=1):

  ab=00(b=0,a=0): a=0,b=0,c=1,d=1 -> a' b' c d

  ab=10(b=0,a=1): a=1,b=0,c=1,d=1 -> a b' c d

  ab=11(b=1,a=1): a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1 -> a b c d

From cd=10(d=0,c=1):

  ab=00(b=0,a=0): a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0 -> a' b' c d'

  ab=10(b=0,a=1): a=1,b=0,c=1,d=0 -> a b' c d'

  ab=11(b=1,a=1): a=1,b=1,c=1,d=0 -> a b c d'

For the zero outputs, we check if these overlap with any 1 minterms. Because d is don't-care, we can try to simplify ignoring d where possible.

Looking at the map, the ones cover:

- All cases where c=1 and a=1 or both a,b=0

- Also for c=0 d=0 with a=1 (ab=10 or 11)

Given don't cares, the simplified expression is:

out = (a & ~b) | (a & b & ~c & ~d) | (c & ((~a & ~b) | (a & ~b) | (a & b)))

Look for simpler form:

- Group c=1 rows with ab in {00,10,11} => c & ( ~a & ~b | a & ~b | a & b) = c & (~a & ~b | a) since a & b includes a

- a & ~b covers ab=10

- ab=11 with c=0 d=0 (first row) is a & b & ~c & ~d

Try consensus:

out = (a & ~b) | (c & ( ~a & ~b | a)) | (a & b & ~c & ~d)

Since ~a & ~b and a covers all except a=0 b=1, the term c & ( ~a & ~b | a) covers c and (ab != 01)

Since ab=01 and c=any is 0 or d, safe to omit 01 on ab side for c=1.

Alternatively, simplify c & ( ~a & ~b | a) = c & ( a | (~a & ~b)) = c & ( (a) + (~a & ~b)).

Check for redundancy:

Test value out=1 when:

- a=1, b=anything, c=1, d=any except for ab=01 which is 0

- or a=1, b=0 (a & ~b) regardless c,d

- or a=1,b=1,c=0,d=0 (last term)

Simplify further by ignoring the last term (a & b & ~c & ~d):

Try out = (a & ~b) | (c & ( ~a & ~b | a))

Since last term covers a=1,b=1,c=0,d=0 cell (ab=11, cd=00) which is 1

Map cell (cd=00, ab=11)=1

It's covered by a & b & ~c & ~d, that is a=1,b=1,c=0,d=0

Is this covered by (a & ~b) ? No (b=1)

Is it covered by c & ( ... ) ? c=0 so no

Therefore, last term is needed.

Hence the final expression:

out = (a & ~b) | (c & (~a & ~b | a)) | (a & b & ~c & ~d)

Implement this logic in Verilog.

Since d is don't-care, we can assign 0 or 1 to simplify, but including d in the last term is fine.
