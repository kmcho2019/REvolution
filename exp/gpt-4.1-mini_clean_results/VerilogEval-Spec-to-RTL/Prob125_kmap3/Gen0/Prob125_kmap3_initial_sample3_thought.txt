Look for groupings of ones and don't-cares:

1) Notice for c=1 (c=1, d=0 or d=1), a,b=00,10,11 all output 1, and for c=1,d=1,ab=00,10,11 are 1, only ab=01 is 0.

2) For c=0, d=0, ab=10 and 11 are 1.

3) For c=0, d=1 ab=10 and 11 are don't-care.

Possible simplification:

Group1: For c=1, a=1 or b=1 (i.e. (a + b) & c)

Group2: For c=0, d=0, a=1,b=0 or b=1 (i.e. a & ~c & ~d)

But let's get the expression with Karnaugh map grouping.

After simplification, the expression is:

out = (c & (a + b)) + (a & ~c & ~d)

This covers:

- If c=1 and (a=1 or b=1), then out=1.

- If c=0 and a=1 and d=0, then out=1.

Otherwise out=0.
