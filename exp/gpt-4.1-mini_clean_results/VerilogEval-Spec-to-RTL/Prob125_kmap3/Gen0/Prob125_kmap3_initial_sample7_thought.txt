The inputs are a, b, c, d and output is out. The Karnaugh map is given with d (input) as don't-care for the function, which means we can choose output values for those entries to simplify the logic. We'll first write the map with c and d as row variables and a and b as column variables, per the problem statement.

K-map:

           ab
   cd   01  00  10  11
   00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
   01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
   11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
   10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Let's re-index the K-map:

Rows: cd = 00,01,11,10
Cols: ab = 01,00,10,11

I'll map each cell's output value:

cd\ab| 01 | 00 | 10 | 11
-------------------------
00   | d  | 0  | 1  | 1
01   | 0  | 0  | d  | d
11   | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1
10   | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1

"d" means don't-care output; we can choose 0 or 1 to simplify.

To simplify, choose d's to 1 or 0 to maximize grouping.

First, assign don't-cares for maximum grouping:

At cd=00, ab=01: d (don't-care) → assign 1 or 0?

Let's try assigning 1.

At cd=01, ab=10 and 11: d's; assign 1 to cover more ones.

Now, the map with assigned don't-cares as 1:

cd\ab| 01 | 00 | 10 | 11
-------------------------
00   | 1  | 0  | 1  | 1
01   | 0  | 0  | 1  | 1
11   | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1
10   | 0  | 1  | 1  | 1

Now, looking for groups of 1s:

- Column ab=11: rows cd=00,01,11,10 → all 1 except cd=01, ab=11 is 1 after assignment, so whole column except (01,11) is 1.

- Column ab=10: rows cd=00,01,11,10 all 1.

- Column ab=01: cd=00=1, cd=01=0, cd=11=0, cd=10=0 → only one 1

- Column ab=00: only cd=11 and 10 are 1

Try to form groups:

- 4-cell group at ab=10 (all rows): ab=10 → a=1,b=0 → out=1 when a=1,b=0

- 4-cell group at ab=11 (all rows except cd=01 where out=1): Actually, cd=01, ab=11=1. So 4-cell group at ab=11: a=1,b=1

- 2-cell group: cd=11 and cd=10, ab=00: at cd=11, ab=00=1 and cd=10, ab=00=1

- single 1 at cd=00, ab=01 (only 1)

Also, from original map, the rows cd=11 and 10, columns ab=10 and 11, all 1s

Let's derive SOP expressions for the simplified groups:

Group 1: ab=10 (a=1,b=0) all cd → out=1 when a=1,b=0

Group 2: ab=11 (a=1,b=1) all cd → out=1 when a=1,b=1

Group 3: cd=11 or cd=10 and ab=00 → (c=1,d=1 or c=1,d=0) and a=0,b=0 → c=1,d=don't care, a=0,b=0

So, out=1 when c=1,a=0,b=0

Group 4: single cell at cd=00, ab=01 → c=0,d=0,a=0,b=1 → out=1

Thus, out = (a & ~b) | (a & b) | (c & ~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d & ~a & b)

Simplify (a & ~b) | (a & b) = a

So, out = a | (c & ~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d & ~a & b)

We can write the Verilog expression as:

out = a | (c & ~a & ~b) | (~c & ~d & ~a & b);
