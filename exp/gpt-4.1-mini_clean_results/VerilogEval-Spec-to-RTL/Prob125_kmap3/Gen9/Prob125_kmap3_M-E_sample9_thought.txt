Implement the Karnaugh map logic by explicitly enumerating all minterms that evaluate to '1' and using a priority-encoded style with if-else statements inside a combinational always block. This approach provides clear visibility of which input combinations produce output '1', treats 'd' inputs as don't-cares by excluding those minterms, and leverages behavioral Verilog instead of continuous assignments. The output is computed based on the enumeration of minterms, providing an alternative structural style to the original purely combinational assign expression.
