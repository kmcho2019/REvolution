Improve the existing design by factoring the logic expression to reduce the number of gates and inversions, thereby lowering area and power without affecting delay. The original expression is:

out = (~c & a) | (c & ~b) | (c & a)

This can be factored as:

This eliminates one AND gate and one OR gate inside the main OR, and reduces the number of inverters since ~b and ~c are only computed once. Implementing this factorization reduces redundant logic, improving power and area while maintaining zero critical path delay.
