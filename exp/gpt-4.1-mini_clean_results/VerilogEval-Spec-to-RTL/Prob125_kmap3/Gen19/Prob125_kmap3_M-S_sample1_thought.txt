Analyze the Karnaugh map and minimize the function, considering 'd' as don't-cares for simplification.

From the map:

- The function can be simplified to out = c | (a & ~b)

Because:
- When c=1, output is mostly 1 regardless of other inputs.
- When c=0, output is 1 only when a=1 and b=0.
- d inputs (don't-cares) are chosen to allow this simplification.

This reduces the logic to just one OR and one AND gate with one inverter, minimizing area and power without losing correctness.
