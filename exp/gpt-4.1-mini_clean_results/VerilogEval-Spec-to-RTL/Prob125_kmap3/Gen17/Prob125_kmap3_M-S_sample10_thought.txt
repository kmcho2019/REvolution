Analyze the existing expression: out = (c & a) | (c & ~b) | (a & ~c).

Note that (c & a) and (a & ~c) together simplify to a & (c | ~c) = a (since c | ~c = 1).

So these two terms can be replaced by simply a.

Thus, out = a | (c & ~b).

This is a simpler expression with only two terms.

Implementation will be straightforward:

- Define intermediate signals for inverted b.
- Assign out = a | (c & ~b).
