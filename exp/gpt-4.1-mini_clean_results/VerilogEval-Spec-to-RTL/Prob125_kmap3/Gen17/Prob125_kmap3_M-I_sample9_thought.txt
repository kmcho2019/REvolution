The original design implements the logic in an always block with intermediate wires, resulting in slightly more area and power due to unnecessary registers and additional signals. To optimize for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will:

- Replace the always block with a continuous assignment since the output is purely combinational.
- Remove intermediate wires and combine logic expressions directly for cleaner, minimal RTL.
- Use the don't-care input 'd' to simplify logic by not depending on it explicitly, as allowed.
- Simplify the Boolean expression based on the Karnaugh map to minimize gates and logic levels.

From the Karnaugh map, the simplified function is:

out = a + c & ~b

which matches the original logic. Therefore, a single assign statement implementing out = a | (c & ~b) suffices.

This minimal combinational logic reduces gate count, switching activity, and latency, improving PPA.
