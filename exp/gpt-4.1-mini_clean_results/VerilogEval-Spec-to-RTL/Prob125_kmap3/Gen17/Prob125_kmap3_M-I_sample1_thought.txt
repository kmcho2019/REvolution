Reviewing the current optimized logic expression (a & ~c) | (c & (~b | a)), it already minimizes gate count by factoring terms involving 'c'. However, examining the Karnaugh map more closely shows we can further reduce the logic by leveraging the don't-care conditions (d) to simplify groups in the K-map.

Specifically, looking at the Karnaugh map:

          ab
cd   01  00  10  11
00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

If we treat 'd' as 1 for simplification, the map becomes:

          ab
cd   01  00  10  11
00 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

From this adjusted map, a simpler minimal expression is:

out = c | (a & ~b)

This is because when c=1, output is 1 regardless of other inputs (covers many 1's), and when c=0, output is 1 only if a=1 and b=0.

This new expression uses only one OR gate, one AND gate, and one NOT gate, which reduces gate count and potentially switching activity.

This improves power and area further while maintaining functional correctness given the don't-care condition of 'd'.
