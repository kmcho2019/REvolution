Implement the logic by using the inputs to generate all four possible ab combinations as one-hot encoded signals and all four cd combinations similarly, then use these decoded signals to selectively enable output bits according to the Karnaugh map. Using separate decoders for ab and cd, the output is formed as an OR of ANDs of corresponding ab and cd decoded signals for positions where output=1. This approach treats the K-map as a two-dimensional ROM lookup with decoding, distinctly different from direct sum-of-products expressions.
