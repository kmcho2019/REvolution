The original solution uses an always block with a reg output, which is unnecessary for purely combinational logic. To optimize area and potentially reduce power by minimizing toggling and gate overhead, we can replace the always block with a continuous assignment statement. The optimized expression derived from the Karnaugh map is: out = a | (c & ~b). This reduces the logic to a simple OR and AND combination, which is minimal and should yield the best synthesis results. The don't-care input 'd' is still unused but allowed.
