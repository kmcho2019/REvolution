Since 'a' being high always sets output to 1, and when 'a' is low, output depends on 'b' and 'c', we can simplify by focusing on just a and b. Observing the Karnaugh map, output is 1 whenever a=1 or b=0, ignoring c and d (don't-care). Thus, out = a | ~b simplifies the logic further by removing c, reducing gate count.
