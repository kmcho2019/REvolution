We then create a 4x4 ROM array indexed by {c,d} and {a,b} to get the output. Since 'd' is don't-care, we treat it as 0 in the address but assign outputs such that the don't-cares are taken as zeros.

This approach uses a combinational ROM lookup to implement the function, making the logic structure different from direct combinational logic expressions or multiplexers.

This also illustrates how to use LUT-based design or ROM-based design, useful in FPGA or certain ASIC flows.
