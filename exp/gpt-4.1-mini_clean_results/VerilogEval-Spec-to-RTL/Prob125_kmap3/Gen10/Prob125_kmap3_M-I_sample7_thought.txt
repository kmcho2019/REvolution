The original design is already minimal and optimized for gate count and delay with a clean two-term expression. However, power can be slightly improved by eliminating the intermediate inverted signals since each is used only once, reducing net transitions and wiring capacitance. Inlining the inverted signals reduces net toggles and area for small designs. The logic remains logically equivalent and minimal, so performance and area are not impacted adversely.

Therefore, the improved design inlines the inversions and merges logic terms into a single assignment for the output, maintaining clarity while slightly optimizing power and area.
