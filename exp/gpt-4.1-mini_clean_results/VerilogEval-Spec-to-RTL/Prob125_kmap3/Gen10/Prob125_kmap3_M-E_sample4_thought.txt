Implement the Karnaugh map function using a small lookup table encoded as a 4-bit vector indexed by the inputs a and b. Then use c as a selector between two precomputed output groups, effectively implementing a 2-to-1 multiplexer with inputs coming from combined minterms. The don't-care d is assigned arbitrarily to simplify the logic. This approach modularizes the function into precomputed values based on (a,b), selected by c, avoiding explicit product terms and favoring a data-driven style.
