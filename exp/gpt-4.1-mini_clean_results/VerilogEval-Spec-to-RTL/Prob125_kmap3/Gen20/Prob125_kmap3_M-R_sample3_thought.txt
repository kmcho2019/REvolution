Refactor the original design by expressing the Karnaugh map output as a direct combinational Boolean expression using continuous assignments instead of an always block and reg variable. This approach eliminates the intermediate register and case statement, reducing area and power. By combining the logic derived from the Karnaugh map and treating don't-care conditions as convenient constants, the output is synthesized as a minimized sum-of-products expression, directly assigned to the output port. This results in clearer, more concise code with potentially better synthesis optimizations.
