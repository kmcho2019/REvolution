Implement the logic by using a small ROM (lookup table) indexed by inputs a, b, and c, while ignoring d since it is a don't-care. The ROM stores the output values for all combinations of a, b, and c based on the Karnaugh map with d factored out. This approach clearly separates the input encoding and output decoding, providing a straightforward, easily modifiable, and scalable solution that can be extended for more inputs or modified logic by changing the ROM contents.
