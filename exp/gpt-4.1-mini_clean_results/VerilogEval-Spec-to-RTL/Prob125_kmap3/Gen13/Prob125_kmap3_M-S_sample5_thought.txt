Analyze the Karnaugh map to find the minimal sum-of-products expression ignoring d (don't-care) cells.

The K-map:

         ab
    cd  01 00 10 11
    00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
    01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
    11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
    10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Treating d as convenient, the minimal expression is:

out = a | (c & ~b)

Explanation:
- All cells where a=1 are 1 (columns 10 and 11).
- When a=0, outputs with c=1 and b=0 are 1.
- So out = a + c * ~b

This uses only two literals and is simpler than previous solution, avoiding redundant terms and unnecessary intermediate wires.

Implementing this simple expression reduces gate count and area while maintaining correctness.
