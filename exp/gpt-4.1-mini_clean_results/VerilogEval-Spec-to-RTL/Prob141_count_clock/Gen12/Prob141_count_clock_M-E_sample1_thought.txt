Implement a 12-hour clock using separate BCD digit counters for each digit of hours, minutes, and seconds. This means two digits for each time unit are individually counted in BCD with proper carry propagation on increments. This approach removes the need for binary-to-BCD conversion logic, simplifying combinational logic and potentially improving timing and power by minimizing complex arithmetic operations. The PM flip-flop toggles only during the transition from 11 to 12. All counting and resets are synchronous and triggered on clk's rising edge. The counters increment only when ena is asserted and reset has priority. The output ports directly represent the BCD digits stored in registers, so the output is continuously driven by these registers.
