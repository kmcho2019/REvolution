Implement a fully BCD-based 12-hour clock counter with three cascaded BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each unit and tens digit is stored separately, making increment and rollover logic uniform and modular. For hours, use BCD digits from 01 to 12 with dedicated logic to roll over from 12 back to 01, toggling the pm flag at each rollover. The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 and roll over in the typical BCD fashion. All counters increment synchronously on ena pulses; reset initializes to 12:00:00 AM. This design avoids internal binary to BCD conversion by operating directly in BCD, improving conceptual uniformity and making it straightforward to extend or modify. The pm toggles only when hours transition from 11:59:59 to 12:00:00.
