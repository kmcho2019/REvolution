Simplify the design by using a single binary counter for hours (1 to 12), minutes (0 to 59), and seconds (0 to 59) with direct binary increment and rollover checks. Instead of maintaining each digit as separate BCD registers, perform the BCD conversion only in combinational logic at the outputs, reducing registers and sequential logic complexity. The seconds increment on ena, minutes increment on seconds rollover, and hours increment on minutes rollover with PM toggled on hour rollovers from 11 to 12. Synchronous reset sets the clock to 12:00 AM. This approach minimizes the number of registers and logic while keeping the outputs as required BCD values and functionality intact.
