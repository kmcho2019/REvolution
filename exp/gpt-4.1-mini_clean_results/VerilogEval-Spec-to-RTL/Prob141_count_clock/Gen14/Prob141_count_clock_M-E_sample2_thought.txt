Implement a 12-hour clock by separating seconds, minutes, and hours counters into distinct always blocks triggered by the enable pulse, while keeping synchronous reset in each block. Use two BCD digits for seconds and minutes, and maintain the hours counter directly in BCD. Hours will count from 1 to 12 in BCD, incrementing on a rolling minutes transition. PM toggling will occur only when the hour increments from 11 to 12. Output registers are updated synchronously as well. This modular design divides counting responsibilities, reducing combinational logic complexity per always block and easing timing closure.
