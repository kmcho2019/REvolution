Refactor the 12-hour clock by separating the seconds, minutes, and hours counters into individual always blocks triggered on the clock's positive edge, using enable and carry signals between them. Each counter is implemented as two BCD digits with simple carry logic for rollover, and the hour counter operates in binary but is converted to BCD output externally. The pm flag toggles synchronously when the hour rolls from 11 to 12. This modular approach breaks down the logic into smaller sequential units, improving timing by reducing combinational paths within a single always block and simplifying enable gating with explicit carry signals. Output BCD conversions remain as continuous assignments outside the always blocks for clarity.
