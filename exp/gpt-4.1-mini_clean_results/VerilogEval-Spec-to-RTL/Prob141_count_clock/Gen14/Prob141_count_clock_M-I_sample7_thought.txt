To improve the original design's PPA, I will reduce combinational complexity and critical path length by restructuring the seconds and minutes counters to count directly in BCD with simple carry logic and separate registers for tens and units digits, as before, but modify the seconds and minutes increment logic to use shorter combinational paths.

For the hours counter, I will store hours directly as two BCD digits (4 bits each) instead of binary, eliminating the need for binary-to-BCD conversion logic in the output path, thereby reducing logic depth and switching activity.

The pm toggle logic will be directly linked to the transition from 11:59:59 to 12:00:00, detected by checking the counters, to keep it synchronous and clean.

I will organize the increment logic with early resets and uses clear non-blocking updates with minimum nested ifs, using separate signals for carry propagation to shorten combinational paths.

This approach balances functional correctness while improving timing and power by reducing combinational logic and output path complexity and lowering switching toggles, as hours are stored in BCD directly.

The module remains synchronous to clk with synchronous reset. The outputs are driven from registered BCD values directly with no extra combinational logic.
