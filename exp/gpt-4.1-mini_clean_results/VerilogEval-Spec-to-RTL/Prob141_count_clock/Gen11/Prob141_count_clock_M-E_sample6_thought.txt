Implement the 12-hour clock by using pure BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each as two separate 4-bit digits, incrementing seconds first, then minutes, then hours with proper rollover detection. Instead of holding hours as a binary number internally, directly count hours in BCD from 01 to 12 to simplify output logic. The PM bit toggles exactly when the hours increment from 11 to 12. Use separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours, all synchronous to clk and gated by ena, with synchronous reset overriding. This modular approach isolates the logic of each counter, which can help timing by reducing combinational dependencies inside one block and clarifies carry propagation between counters. The hours counter resets to BCD "12" on reset. By holding hours directly as BCD, no binary-to-BCD conversion logic is needed on output. The 12-hour wrap-around logic requires custom checks since BCD digits go from 01 to 12, not 00 to 99.
