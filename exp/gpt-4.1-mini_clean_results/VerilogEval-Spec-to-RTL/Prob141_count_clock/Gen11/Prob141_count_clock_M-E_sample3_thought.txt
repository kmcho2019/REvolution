Implement the entire clock logic using purely BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds with separate tens and units digits to avoid binary-to-BCD conversion complexity. The hour counter will be handled entirely in BCD with special logic to wrap from 12 back to 1 and to toggle the pm signal accordingly. The seconds and minutes counters are straightforward BCD counters with standard 0-59 ranges. Synchronous reset clears everything to 12:00 AM. The enable signal increments seconds, cascading increments to minutes and hours as needed. All counters and pm toggle are updated on the rising edge of clk, with reset synchronous and prioritized. This approach embraces fully BCD counting for consistency, making outputs direct register values without conversions and simplifying output assignment logic.
