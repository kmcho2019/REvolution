To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) of the existing 12-hour BCD clock, the approach focuses on reducing the number of always blocks and minimizing combinational logic depth and switching activity.

Key improvements:
1. Combine all synchronous counters (seconds, minutes, hours, and pm) into a single always @(posedge clk) block. This reduces overhead of multiple always blocks and improves timing by enabling better resource sharing.
2. Use only non-blocking assignments in the sequential block to improve synthesis quality.
3. Remove the combinational function for hour conversion; instead, convert binary hour directly to BCD within the sequential block and store it in registers to reduce combinational logic depth.
4. Register all outputs (hh, mm, ss) synchronously to reduce glitches and unnecessary toggling.
5. Keep the internal representation the same (seconds and minutes as two BCD digits; hours as a binary 4-bit register) to minimize area but handle the hour to BCD conversion inside the clocked block.
6. Reset all registers synchronously with priority, and update counters only when ena is high, to reduce switching when idle.
7. Use local variables inside the always block to simplify increment logic and avoid redundant evaluations.
This approach reduces logic layers in critical paths, lowers unnecessary switching, and ensures outputs are glitch-free and stable, leading to improved PPA metrics while preserving functional correctness.
