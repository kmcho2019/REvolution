Simplify the design by combining all counters (seconds, minutes, hours) and PM toggling into a single always block triggered on the positive clock edge. Use non-blocking assignments throughout and minimize the number of intermediate signals. Keep seconds and minutes as two BCD digits updated on enable, and maintain hours as a 4-bit binary counter from 1 to 12, converting to BCD only in output assignments. Reset synchronously sets time to 12:00 AM. This reduces module complexity, removes combinational functions, and simplifies output logic without changing functionality.
