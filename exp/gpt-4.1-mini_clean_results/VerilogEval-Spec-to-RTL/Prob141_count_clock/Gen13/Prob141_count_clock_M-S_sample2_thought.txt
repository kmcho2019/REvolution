Simplify the design by using separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours counters, each triggered only when the lower counter rolls over. This modular approach reduces nested combinational logic inside one always block, improving clarity and potentially timing. Each counter increments its lower digit in BCD, rolling over properly from 0-9 or 0-5 as needed. The PM flag toggles synchronously only when hours roll from 11 to 12. Synchronous reset resets all counters and PM to initial state (12:00 AM). Output hours are kept in BCD with direct counting from 1 to 12, avoiding binary to BCD conversion and reducing logic. This clearer separation of counters reduces complexity and eases verification.
