Simplify the design by using pure BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds without separate binary-to-BCD conversion. Handle hour increment and pm toggle by comparing BCD hours directly. For hours, count from 1 to 12 in BCD (tens and units) with simple carry logic, and wrap from 12 back to 1. This avoids the binary hour counter and conversion function, reducing complexity. Use straightforward synchronous logic updating counters on ena pulses. Maintain synchronous reset that sets clock to 12:00 AM (BCD "12" for hours, "00" for minutes and seconds, pm=0). This approach keeps all counters in BCD, simplifies increment conditions, and is easier to understand and verify.
