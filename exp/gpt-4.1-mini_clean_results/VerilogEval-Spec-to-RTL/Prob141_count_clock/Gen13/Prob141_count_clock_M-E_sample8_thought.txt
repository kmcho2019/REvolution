Implement the 12-hour clock by creating separate always blocks for each digit: seconds units, seconds tens, minutes units, minutes tens, and hours in BCD directly (two digits for hours as well), avoiding binary-to-BCD conversion. Each digit counts individually with rollover conditions causing increment in the next digit.

Use a hierarchical ripple carry style counting structure synchronized to the single enable pulse per second. The PM bit toggles precisely when hour increments from 11 to 12.

Use synchronous reset to initialize to 12:00:00 AM (hours=12, minutes=0, seconds=0, pm=0).

This modular approach isolates the counting logic for each digit, simplifying the design and reducing combinational complexity inside any single always block. It avoids runtime binary-to-BCD conversion, improving timing and reducing combinational logic.

Each digit uses non-blocking assignments and only updates on enable to reduce toggling. Outputs are assigned directly from registers without extra combinational logic.

This approach also improves readability and makes verification straightforward, while maintaining all requirements.
