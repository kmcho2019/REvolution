Use binary counters internally for seconds (0-59), minutes (0-59), and hours (1-12), with a single pm flip-flop. Increment seconds on ena; roll over at 60 to increment minutes; roll over minutes at 60 to increment hours; roll hours from 12 to 1 and toggle pm. At reset, initialize to 12:00:00 AM.

Convert binary counters to BCD outputs using simple combinational logic since each value fits within 6 bits for sec/min and 4 bits for hours. This approach reduces combinational logic complexity by avoiding digit-wise BCD counting and complex conditional increments, leading to cleaner and more efficient implementation while preserving the required interface and behavior.
