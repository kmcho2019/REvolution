Implement the 12-hour clock purely with three separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours to directly hold BCD digits and avoid separate conversion logic. Each counter will have two 4-bit BCD digits, incrementing with appropriate carry logic on each enable pulse. The seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, rolling over at 60. The hour counts from 12 down to 1 (BCD encoded as two digits), cycling through 12-hour format and toggling the pm bit at rollover from 11 to 12. The reset sets the clock to 12:00:00 AM synchronously. Using separate digit counters reduces combinational complexity and enables simpler increment and rollover logic, directly maintaining valid BCD digits and minimizing arithmetic operations. All sequential logic is synchronous on clk positive edge, respecting reset and enable priority.
