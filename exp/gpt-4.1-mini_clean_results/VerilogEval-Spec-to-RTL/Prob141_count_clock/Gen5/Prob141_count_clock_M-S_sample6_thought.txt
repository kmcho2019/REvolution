Simplify the 12-hour BCD clock by using binary counters internally for hours, minutes, and seconds, and convert hours to BCD only at output. Minutes and seconds remain BCD since their ranges (0-59) fit nicely with two BCD digits. This avoids complicated BCD hour increment logic and pm toggle complexity. The hour binary counter counts 0-11 representing 1-12, with pm toggled when hour wraps from 11 to 0. Seconds and minutes increment with simple BCD logic. This reduces combinational logic complexity and area, improving clarity and potentially timing and power.
