I will implement the 12-hour clock using three separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each with its own increment and rollover logic. Instead of a binary hour counter and conversion, the hour counter will be two BCD digits from the start, ranging from 01 to 12. The PM flag toggles precisely when the hour rolls from 11 to 12. To simplify logic and improve modularity, each counter (seconds, minutes, hours) is updated only when the lower unit rolls over, with clear, flat logic for BCD increments and rollovers.

Additionally, I will keep synchronous reset with highest priority and synchronous enable gating all counting logic. This approach leverages direct BCD manipulation throughout and minimizes combinational depth by avoiding nested increments inside a single always block. The hour increment logic explicitly handles hour counting and PM toggling with a straightforward condition on BCD hour digits.

This architecture eases verification and debugging, potentially yields shorter critical paths, and can be easily extended or modified.
