Implement the 12-hour clock using a single binary counter for seconds (0 to 43199, representing seconds in 12 hours), incremented on each ena pulse. Convert this total seconds count into hh:mm:ss and pm indicator combinationally. This approach minimizes sequential logic complexity and nesting by handling all increments as a single counter. The pm flag is determined by whether the total seconds count is in the PM half of the 12-hour period. The hour output is derived by converting the seconds count to hours in 1-12 range, minutes and seconds by extracting proper digits and converting to BCD for outputs. Reset synchronously resets the seconds counter to zero (12:00 AM). This centralized time base simplifies state updates, reduces clock domain complexity, and facilitates clean BCD conversion logic in combinational blocks.
