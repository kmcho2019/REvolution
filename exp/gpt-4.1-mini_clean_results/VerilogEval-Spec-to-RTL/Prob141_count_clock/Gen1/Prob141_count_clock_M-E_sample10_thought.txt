Implement the 12-hour BCD clock using separate BCD digit counters for each decimal digit, counting units and tens independently for seconds, minutes, and hours.
- Each digit (units and tens) increments on appropriate conditions and wraps at its max.
- Seconds and minutes tens digit increments when units roll from 9 to 0 (with constraints for max 5 in tens digit).
- Hours digits count from 01 to 12, with hours units digit cycling through 0-9 and tens digit limited to 0 or 1.
- Toggle PM signal when hour changes from 11 to 12 (detected by tens=1 and units=1 before increment).
- On reset, time is set to 12:00 AM.
- Only increment on ena pulse.
This approach modularizes BCD counting at digit level and uses simple combinational logic for increments, easing logic complexity and potentially improving clarity and timing.
