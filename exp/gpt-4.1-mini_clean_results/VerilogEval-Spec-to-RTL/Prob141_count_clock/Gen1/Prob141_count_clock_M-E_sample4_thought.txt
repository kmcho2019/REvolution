Implement the 12-hour clock by splitting each component (hours, minutes, seconds) into separate BCD digits for tens and ones, with explicit carry signals to handle increments and rollovers. Use registers for each digit and a separate pm flag. On each ena pulse, increment seconds ones; when it rolls over from 9 to 0, increment seconds tens; when seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes ones; similarly for minutes tens and hours. For hours, implement a finite state machine for counting from 1 to 12 in BCD with explicit conditions; when hours roll from 11 to 12, hours ones increment to 2; when hours roll from 12 to 1, toggle pm and reset hours to 01. Reset sets time to 12:00:00 AM. All updates happen on the positive clock edge synchronously with reset highest priority. This approach uses explicit digit registers and carry signals to avoid combinational complexity and prevent ambiguous pm toggling.
