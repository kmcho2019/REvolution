Implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator using BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds.
- Seconds count from 00 to 59, incremented when ena pulses.
- Minutes increment when seconds roll over from 59 to 00.
- Hours increment when minutes roll over from 59 to 00.
- Hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD.
- AM/PM toggles when hour rolls over from 11 to 12.
- Reset sets time to 12:00 AM.
All counters are synchronous to clk with synchronous active-high reset.
