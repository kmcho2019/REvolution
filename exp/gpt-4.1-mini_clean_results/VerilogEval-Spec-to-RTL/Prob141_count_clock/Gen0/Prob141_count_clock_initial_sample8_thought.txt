Implement a synchronous 12-hour BCD clock with separate BCD digits for hours, minutes, and seconds. On reset, initialize to 12:00 AM (12:00:00, pm=0). On each ena pulse, increment seconds; when seconds reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment minutes; when minutes reach 59, roll over to 00 and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD; after 12, wrap to 1 and toggle the pm indicator. Use two BCD digits for each time component. The counters update only on positive clock edges, synchronous reset, and when ena is asserted.
