Implement a 12-hour clock using three BCD counters: seconds, minutes, and hours. Each of seconds and minutes has two BCD digits counting from 00 to 59. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD. The clock increments seconds on each ena pulse (once per second). When seconds reach 59 and increment, roll over to 00 and increment minutes. Similarly for minutes to hours. The pm signal toggles every 12 hours; starts low on reset (AM). On reset, clock goes to 12:00 AM. Use synchronous logic on posedge clk with reset taking priority over enable.
