Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters increment on the pulse of the ena signal (once per second). Reset synchronously sets time to 12:00 AM (hh=0x12, mm=0x00, ss=0x00, pm=0). Seconds increment from 00 to 59, then roll over to 00 and increment minutes similarly. Minutes increment from 00 to 59, then roll over and increment hours. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD; after 12, roll over to 1. The pm indicator toggles every 12 hours. All increments occur only when ena is high, and reset overrides ena. The outputs are 8-bit BCD digits for hh, mm, ss, and 1-bit pm.
