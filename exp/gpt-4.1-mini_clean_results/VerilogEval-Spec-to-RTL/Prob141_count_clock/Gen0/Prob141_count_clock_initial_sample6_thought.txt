Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with BCD outputs for hh, mm, and ss, and a pm indicator. On each positive edge of clk, if reset is high, set time to 12:00:00 AM. Otherwise, if ena is high, increment the seconds; if seconds reach 59, reset to 00 and increment minutes; if minutes reach 59, reset to 00 and increment hours. For hours, count from 1 to 12 in BCD; after 12, wrap to 1 and toggle the pm bit. The hh, mm, and ss outputs will be 8 bits representing two BCD digits (most significant nibble and least significant nibble). The pm output is a single bit that is 0 for AM and 1 for PM.
