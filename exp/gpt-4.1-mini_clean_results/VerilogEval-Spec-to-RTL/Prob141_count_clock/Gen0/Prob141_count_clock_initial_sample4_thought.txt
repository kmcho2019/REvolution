Implement a 12-hour clock with separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds and minutes counters count from 0-59, and the hours counter counts from 1-12. The PM indicator toggles every 12 hours. On reset, the clock initializes to 12:00 AM (hh=12, mm=00, ss=00, pm=0). The counters increment only when ena is high. The counting increments seconds first; on seconds wrap from 59 to 00, increment minutes; on minutes wrap, increment hours; on hours wrap from 12 to 1, toggle pm. Use synchronous reset on positive clock edge.
