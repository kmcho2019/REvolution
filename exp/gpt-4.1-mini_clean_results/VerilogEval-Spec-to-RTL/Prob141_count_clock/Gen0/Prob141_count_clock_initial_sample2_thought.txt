Implement synchronous logic that increments the time on the enable pulse (ena) indicating one second elapsed. Use BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59, and hours count from 01 to 12 with a separate pm bit indicating AM/PM. On reset, set time to 12:00 AM. On each second increment (ena), increment seconds; if seconds overflow to 00, increment minutes; if minutes overflow, increment hours; if hours overflow from 12 to 1, toggle pm. All counters are 8 bits representing two BCD digits.
