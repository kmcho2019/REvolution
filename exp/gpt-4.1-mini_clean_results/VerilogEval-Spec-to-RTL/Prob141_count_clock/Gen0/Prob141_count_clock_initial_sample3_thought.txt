Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD outputs for hours, minutes, and seconds. On each positive edge of clk, if reset is asserted, reset the time to 12:00 AM (12 for hours, 00 for minutes and seconds, pm=0). Else if ena is asserted, increment the time by one second. Implement BCD counters for seconds (0-59), minutes (0-59), and hours (1-12). When seconds reach 59 and increment, reset to 0 and increment minutes; similarly for minutes to hours. When hours increment from 11 to 12, toggle pm; when incrementing from 12, roll over to 1. The outputs are BCD coded (8 bits for each of hh, mm, ss), with two digits packed as upper and lower 4 bits each.
