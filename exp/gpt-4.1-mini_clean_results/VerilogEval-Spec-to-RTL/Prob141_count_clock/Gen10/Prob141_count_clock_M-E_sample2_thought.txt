Implement a 12-hour BCD clock using a single 24-hour binary hour counter internally but convert it to 12-hour format for output and AM/PM indication. Instead of separate tens and units BCD counters for seconds and minutes, maintain them as single binary counters (0-59) and convert to BCD for output, thus simplifying counter logic and reducing carry complexity.

Key points:
1. Keep seconds and minutes as 6-bit binary counters (0-59) incremented on 'ena'.
2. Use a 5-bit binary hour counter counting from 0 to 23 internally.
3. On reaching 23:59:59 + 1 sec, roll over to 0.
4. For output, convert binary hours to 12-hour BCD and generate PM signal (PM if hour >= 12).
5. Reset synchronously to 12:00:00 AM (internal hour=0, meaning 12 AM).
6. Minimize combinational logic depth by registering binary counters first and performing BCD conversion outside of sequential blocks.
7. Use simple binary increment with wraparound and straightforward conversion for output.
This reduces the number of BCD increment logic units and carry chains, likely improving timing and area, and simplifying the design.
