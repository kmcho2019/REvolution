1. Register the binary-to-BCD hour conversion output to reduce combinational logic delay on hh outputs, thus improving timing by pipelining the output stage.

3. Implement the BCD seconds and minutes counters as 8-bit packed BCD registers instead of separate digits, simplifying the increment logic with a single increment and BCD adjust step, reducing logic complexity and area.

4. Use a single 8-bit counter each for seconds and minutes, incrementing and wrapping at 0x59 to 0x00, thus minimizing combinational logic and simplifying rollover detection.

5. Preserve the synchronous reset and highest priority, and maintain single always @(posedge clk) block for sequential logic.

6. Keep pm toggling only on 11->12 hour transition for minimal switching.

This approach reduces combinational logic depth, cuts down toggling on outputs by registering them, and simplifies the BCD counting logic, yielding potentially better timing and lower power with modest or improved area.
