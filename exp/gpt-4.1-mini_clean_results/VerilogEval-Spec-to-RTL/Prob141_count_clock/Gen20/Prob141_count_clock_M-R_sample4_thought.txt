Refactor the design to use separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours counters, each triggered on the clock's positive edge with synchronous reset. Instead of using internal wires for carry propagation, perform increment and rollover logic within each always block. The seconds block increments seconds on ena, rolls over at 59 to zero and asserts a carry signal to minutes. The minutes block increments on this carry, rolls over at 59 to zero, asserting a carry to hours. The hour block increments on this carry, counts 1-12, and toggles pm when transitioning from 11 to 12. Outputs are assigned continuously from the registers, where hours are stored as BCD directly, removing the need for conversion logic. This modular structure improves readability by isolating each time unit's logic and avoids complex combinational carry chains.
