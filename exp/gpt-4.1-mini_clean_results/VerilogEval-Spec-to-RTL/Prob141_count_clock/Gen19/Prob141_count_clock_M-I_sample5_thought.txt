To optimize the design for better PPA (performance, power, area), the main improvement is to simplify and reduce combinational logic in the hour output conversion and the seconds/minutes counting.

Key changes:
1. Store hours directly as BCD (tens and units digit) rather than binary 1-12, eliminating the combinational binary-to-BCD conversion logic and thus reducing combinational delay and power.
2. Use the same tens/units BCD structure for hours, with tens digit only 0 or 1, units digit 0-9, and count 12 as "12" (BCD 0x12). This makes hours increment logic simple and symmetric to minutes and seconds.
3. Adjust increment logic for hours tens/units similar to minutes and seconds, with special handling to wrap from 12 to 1.
4. Update pm toggle when hour increments from 11 to 12 (detect units=1 and tens=1 transition).
5. Maintain synchronous reset and enable behavior.
6. Keep all counters as separate small BCD digits with straightforward carry logic to minimize critical paths and power.
7. Use nonblocking assignments consistently and minimize nested conditional complexity by flattening conditions.

This approach reduces combinational logic, avoids arithmetic for BCD conversion, and yields a uniform counting style for all time fields, improving timing and power with minimal area impact.
