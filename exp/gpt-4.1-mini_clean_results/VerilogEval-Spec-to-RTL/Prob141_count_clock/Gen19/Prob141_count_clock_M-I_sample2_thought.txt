To improve the existing design's performance by reducing the critical path delay, separate the counters for seconds, minutes, and hours into individual sequential always blocks that communicate via carry signals registered on each clock. This pipelining breaks the long combinational path from seconds to hours within a single clock cycle, allowing faster clock frequencies. Each counter increments only when its enable is asserted or when a carry-in from the previous counter signals a rollover. The PM toggle is synchronized with the hour rollover, using a separate registered signal to avoid combinational toggling. Hours remain binary internally for area efficiency, converted to BCD on output. This approach improves timing while maintaining low area and power by minimizing combinational logic depth and avoiding unnecessary toggling.
