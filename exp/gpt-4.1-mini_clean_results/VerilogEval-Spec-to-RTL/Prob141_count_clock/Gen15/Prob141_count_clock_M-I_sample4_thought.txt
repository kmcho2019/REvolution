To optimize for better PPA, the strategy is:

1. Register the BCD output values for hours, minutes, and seconds instead of purely combinational conversion to reduce toggling and combinational delay on outputs. Update the outputs only when the internal counters change.

2. Simplify the hour counting by keeping the hour entirely in BCD form, avoiding the binary-to-BCD conversion and thus eliminating the combinational function. This also reduces output logic delay and area.

3. Break down the counting logic into clear, well-pipelined steps inside a single always block, with minimal nested conditionals and explicit carry signals for seconds, minutes, and hours. This enhances timing by flattening the critical path.

4. Use non-blocking assignments consistently and update outputs only on ena or reset, reducing unnecessary toggling when clock ticks but no increment occurs.

5. Keep the synchronous reset with highest priority.

By storing hours also in BCD and updating outputs synchronously only when values change, output glitches and toggle rates reduce, improving power and timing. This trades a bit of area for simpler combinational logic and better timing.

The resulting design should have:
- Hours in BCD (01-12)
- Minutes and seconds in BCD (00-59)
- A pm flip toggling only at the 11->12 hour transition
- Synchronous outputs registered for clean timing and reduced toggling
