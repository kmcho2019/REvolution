Implement the 12-hour clock using three separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each with their own always block triggered by the enable pulse. Each counter handles its own digit increments and rollovers with carry signals passed sequentially from seconds to minutes and minutes to hours. The hour counter operates in BCD from 1 to 12 with special logic to handle the rollover from 12 back to 1. The pm flag toggles only on the hour counter rollover from 11 to 12. This modular approach localizes carry and rollover logic within each counter block, reducing combinational complexity per block and improving timing by breaking the counters into pipelined stages. The reset synchronously sets the clock to 12:00:00 AM by resetting all counters and clearing the pm flag. The outputs are directly assigned from the BCD counters. This architecture improves timing by splitting logic, and enhances modularity and clarity by clearly separating seconds, minutes, and hour logic.
