Implement the 12-hour clock as three separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each counting their respective digits directly without intermediate binary counters. Use a synchronous always block with a clear priority for reset, then enable.  
Seconds and minutes count up in two BCD digits with proper rollover at 60 (tens < 6 and units < 10).  
The hour is represented as two BCD digits counting 01 to 12 directly, incrementing upon minute rollover.  
The pm signal toggles when the hour rolls over from 11 to 12.  
To simplify hour increments, on increment attempt from 12, wrap back to 1 and toggle pm.  
Use a dedicated combinational function to increment a BCD digit with upper limit checks for modularity (e.g., increment BCD with max).  
All counters use non-blocking assignments for clean synchronous logic.  
This design avoids internal binary hour counters and reduces combinational conversions, making the increment logic explicit in BCD domain.  
It trades off a bit more combinational logic in increments for intuitive BCD counting and clear rollover logic, which can be efficiently synthesized and easy to verify.
