Refactor the 12-hour clock by implementing the BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours all as synchronous registers updated on clock edges without separate next-state combinational blocks. Instead, use procedural logic inside a single always @(posedge clk) block with clear nested if-else conditions controlled by ena and reset. This approach simplifies control flow, removes separate combinational next-state signals, and directly increments BCD counters using arithmetic and rollover detection inline. The hour counter is implemented directly in BCD (two digits) rather than binary, toggling pm at the correct rollover. Outputs are assigned directly from these registers without additional functions. This structure reduces combinational logic depth, merges next-state and state registers, and enhances readability and synthesis friendliness without changing functionality.
