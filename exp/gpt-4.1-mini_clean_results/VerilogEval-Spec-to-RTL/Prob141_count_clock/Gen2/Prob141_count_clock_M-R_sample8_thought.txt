Refactor by moving all intermediate calculation signals and variables out of the always block and into module-level regs and wires. Use combinational logic in a separate always_comb block (or an always @* block) to compute the next state of the counters and pm signal based on the current values and ena input. Then in the sequential always @(posedge clk) block, update all registers synchronously with reset priority. This clear separation of combinational next-state logic and sequential state update avoids illegal variable declarations inside procedural blocks. The BCD increment logic is kept similar, but structured through module-level variables. This approach also improves readability and synthesis compatibility.
