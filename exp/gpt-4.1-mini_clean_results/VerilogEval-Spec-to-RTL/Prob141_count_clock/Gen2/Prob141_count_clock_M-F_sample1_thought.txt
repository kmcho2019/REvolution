The fix is to declare all needed variables, including the 9-bit register to hold hour increment result and wrap flag, at the module level instead of inside the always block. Verilog does not permit variable declarations within procedural blocks. Declaring 'reg [8:0] hour_next_and_wrap;' at module scope allows its use inside the always block for combinational function output storage. This resolves the syntax error and allows the hour increment and pm toggle logic to operate as intended. All other logic remains unchanged, ensuring synchronous reset, time increments on ena pulses, and correct BCD counting for the 12-hour clock with AM/PM indication.
