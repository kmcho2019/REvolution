Simplify the 12-hour BCD clock by incrementing BCD digits with straightforward conditional checks instead of a general BCD increment function.  
- Increment seconds units digit (0-9). On rollover from 9 to 0, increment tens digit (0-5).  
- When seconds roll over from 59 to 00, increment minutes similarly.  
- When minutes roll over, increment hours from 01 to 12 in BCD with simple logic: tens digit 0 with units 9 rolls to 10; 10 rolls to 11; 11 rolls to 12; 12 rolls to 01.  
- Toggle pm when hour changes from 11 to 12.  
- Reset synchronously to 12:00 AM.  
This approach avoids complex functions and comparisons, simplifying combinational logic and improving clarity and performance.
