To improve the PPA of the 12-hour BCD clock design while keeping the same functional behavior, I will:
- Replace separate BCD digit counters with binary counters internally for seconds, minutes, and hours, as binary counters are simpler and use fewer resources.
- Only convert to BCD at output ports via combinational logic, reducing combinational logic in the counting path.
- Implement the hour counter as a 1–12 binary counter, toggling PM at rollover from 11 to 12.
- Keep synchronous reset and increment only on ena pulses.
- This approach reduces combinational logic depth and area in the counting logic, improving timing and power by simplifying logic transitions and removing redundant checks.
- Use non-blocking assignments properly and keep all sequential logic within a single always block triggered by clk.
- The PM toggle and reset logic remain, but with simpler conditions due to binary hour counter.
This will improve timing (shorter critical path), reduce area (less combinational and comparator logic), and reduce switching power (simpler logic).
