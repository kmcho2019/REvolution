Implement the 12-hour clock by separating counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each counting in BCD. Use three small BCD counters (seconds units and tens, minutes units and tens, hours units and tens) with combinational logic to handle BCD increment and rollover, tied together with enable pulses. The hour counter treats 12-hour wrap-around explicitly and toggles the PM signal accordingly. The synchronous reset sets the clock to 12:00 AM. The counters increment only on the 'ena' pulse. The design uses modular BCD increment functions and a straightforward always block triggered on clk for sequential updates, avoiding local declarations in procedural blocks by declaring all intermediate signals at the module level.
