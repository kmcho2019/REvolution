Refactor the clock logic by separating combinational next-state logic from sequential state update. Compute next values for seconds, minutes, hours, and pm in combinational always block based on current state and ena. Then update the registers in a simple sequential always block triggered by clk and synchronous reset. This modularizes increment logic explicitly and avoids nested conditions inside the sequential block, improving clarity and potentially aiding synthesis optimization. Also use separate signals for BCD digit increments inline rather than functions to reduce overhead.
