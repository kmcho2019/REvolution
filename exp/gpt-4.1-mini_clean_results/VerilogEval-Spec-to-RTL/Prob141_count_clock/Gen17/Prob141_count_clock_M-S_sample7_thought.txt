Simplify the 12-hour clock by implementing the seconds, minutes, and hours counters in a single always block triggered by clk with synchronous reset. Use BCD logic for seconds and minutes with carry signals, and a straightforward hour counter from 1 to 12 in binary. Update pm on the hour rollover from 11 to 12. Output the hours in BCD by splitting binary hour counter into tens and units using simple logic without subtraction. This reduces the number of always blocks and simplifies carry management, making the design easier to follow and slightly reducing area and complexity while keeping the same behavior.
