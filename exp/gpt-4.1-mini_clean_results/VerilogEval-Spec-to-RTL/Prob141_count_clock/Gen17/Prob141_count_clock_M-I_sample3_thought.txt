To optimize the original design for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), the key is to reduce the long nested conditional logic inside the sequential always block, which creates a critical timing path and potential glitches.

**Improvement Strategy:**

1. Use a **binary counter for seconds** from 0 to 59 instead of separate BCD digits. This reduces the complexity of increment logic and ripple conditions.
2. Similarly, use **binary counters for minutes** from 0 to 59.
3. Keep the **hour counter as a binary 1–12 counter**, as it is already efficient.
4. Convert binary seconds and minutes to BCD outputs combinationally. This moves the complex BCD rollover logic from the clocked process to simpler combinational logic that does not affect timing criticality.
5. The seconds and minutes counters will roll over naturally by comparing against 59 and resetting to zero, reducing nested conditions.
6. PM toggling logic remains unchanged, toggling when hour rolls from 11 to 12.
7. Reset remains synchronous and highest priority.
8. This approach reduces the combinational logic inside the clocked process drastically, improving timing and potentially reducing power by limiting toggling and glitching inside the sequential logic.
9. Area impact is balanced by moving to binary counters but adding combinational BCD converters for outputs, which are lightweight.

Overall, this design balances clarity, correctness, and PPA by minimizing critical path logic inside clocked processes and leveraging combinational conversion for outputs.
