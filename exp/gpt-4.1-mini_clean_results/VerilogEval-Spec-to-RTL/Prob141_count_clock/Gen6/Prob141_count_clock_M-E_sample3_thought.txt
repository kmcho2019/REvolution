Implement the 12-hour clock using three separate BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, but use simple increment logic directly in the sequential always block without helper functions. Represent hours internally as a single binary value (1 to 12) stored in a 4-bit register, then convert to BCD for output to simplify increment and rollover detection for hours. Minutes and seconds are kept as two-digit BCD counters. On each ena pulse, increment seconds; if seconds roll over, increment minutes; if minutes roll over, increment hour with proper wrap from 12 to 1 and toggle the pm flag at this point. Reset synchronously clears the clock to 12:00 AM. This approach separates increment logic cleanly and reduces complexity by handling hour count in binary, converting to BCD only at output, reducing combinational logic complexity and improving clarity.
