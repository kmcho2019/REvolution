Use binary counters internally for seconds (0-59), minutes (0-59), and hours (1-12), incrementing seconds on ena. 
When seconds reach 60, reset to 0 and increment minutes; when minutes reach 60, reset and increment hours.
Hours count from 1 to 12, wrapping from 12 back to 1.
Toggle the PM flag when transitioning from 11 to 12 (i.e., before increment hour is 11 and increment causes 12).
On reset, synchronously set time to 12:00:00 AM.
Convert binary counters to BCD on output using straightforward combinational conversion logic.
This approach simplifies the internal counting logic (binary counters reduce complex BCD carry logic), improving timing and area.
The BCD conversion at outputs is combinational only, preserving clarity.
