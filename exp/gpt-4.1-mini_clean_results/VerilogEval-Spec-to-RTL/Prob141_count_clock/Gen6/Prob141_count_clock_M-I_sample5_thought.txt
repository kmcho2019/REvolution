To improve PPA, refactor the clock counters to use simple binary counters internally for seconds, minutes, and hours, with hour counted 1 to 12 as a binary number. Convert to BCD only at output to reduce combinational logic and area. This reduces the need for multiple nested BCD increment and rollover checks. 

For hours, implement a 4-bit binary counter (1 to 12) instead of separate BCD digits, toggling PM on rollover from 11 to 12. Seconds and minutes can be 6-bit binary counters (0-59). On each ena pulse, increment seconds; if seconds reach 59 then reset to 0 and increment minutes; if minutes reach 59 then reset and increment hours; if hours reach 12 then wrap to 1 and toggle PM.

Use synchronous reset to initialize time to 12:00:00 AM (hours=12, minutes=0, seconds=0, pm=0).

At output, convert hours, minutes, and seconds binary counters to BCD digits to form the 8-bit hh, mm, ss outputs. This conversion is purely combinational and simplifies sequential logic.

This reduces combinational depth in the counting path, lowers area from fewer comparators and less complex rollover detection, and reduces switching in combinational logic, improving power.

Overall, the improved architecture:
- Internal counters: seconds [5:0], minutes [5:0], hours [3:0]
- PM flip-flop toggled on hour rollover from 11 to 12
- Output BCD encoding combinationally
- Synchronous reset sets time to 12:00:00 AM and pm=0
- Only increments on ena pulse

This approach optimizes timing and area while maintaining functional correctness.
