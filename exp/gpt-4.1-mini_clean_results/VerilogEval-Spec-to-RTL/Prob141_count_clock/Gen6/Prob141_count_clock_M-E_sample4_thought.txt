Implement a 12-hour clock by maintaining all time digits (hours, minutes, seconds) as single binary counters internally, then convert to BCD only for output.  
- Use binary counters for seconds (0-59), minutes (0-59), and hours (1-12), plus a PM bit.  
- Increment seconds binary counter on ena pulse. Upon rollover, increment minutes and then hours accordingly.  
- Hours counter wraps from 12 to 1 and toggles PM.  
- On synchronous reset, set to 12:00:00 AM.  
- Use a combinational function to convert the binary counters to BCD digits for outputs hh, mm, ss.  
This design reduces combinational complexity in the clocked always block (simple binary increments and comparisons), isolating the BCD conversion to a purely combinational, straightforward block. This approach simplifies timing and reduces nesting of conditions, likely improving performance and reducing area.
