To improve PPA, implement internal counters for seconds, minutes, and hours in pure binary, counting their natural numeric ranges (seconds and minutes: 0–59; hours: 1–12). Update these counters sequentially on ena. Use a simple binary toggle of the pm flag on hour rollover from 11 to 12. Convert the binary counters to BCD at outputs only, using combinational BCD converters for each 8-bit output (hh, mm, ss). This reduces combinational complexity in the clocking path and shortens critical path length. Clock enables prevent unnecessary toggling. This approach simplifies increment logic, minimizes combinational depth, and reduces area by eliminating multi-level BCD digit rollovers inside sequential logic.
