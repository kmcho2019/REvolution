To optimize PPA while preserving the original functionality, I will simplify hour counting by internally representing hours as a 4-bit binary counter from 1 to 12, then convert this binary hour count to BCD at output. This reduces complex BCD hour increment logic and combinational depth. Seconds and minutes remain as two separate BCD digits with simple chained increment logic. PM toggling is triggered on hour roll from 11 to 12 in binary form, simplifying conditions. I will also gate all counting logic behind ena to minimize unnecessary toggling, and keep synchronous reset logic. This should reduce combinational logic complexity (improving timing and power), simplify area by avoiding complicated BCD hour increments, and maintain correct BCD outputs and AM/PM behavior.
