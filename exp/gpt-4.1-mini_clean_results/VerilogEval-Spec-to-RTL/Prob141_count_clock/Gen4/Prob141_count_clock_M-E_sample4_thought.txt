Implement the 12-hour clock entirely with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds instead of binary counters. Each digit of hours, minutes, and seconds is stored as a separate 4-bit BCD digit (e.g., hh[7:4] for tens, hh[3:0] for units). This way, incrementing the time involves simple BCD increment and rollover logic that respects the decimal boundaries naturally, without requiring binary-to-BCD conversion.

Specifically:
- Seconds and minutes each have two BCD digits counting from 00 to 59.
- Hours count from 12, 01, 02, ..., 11, 12 repeatedly, represented in two BCD digits.
- The pm flag toggles synchronously when the hour rolls over from 11 to 12.
- On reset, time is set to 12:00:00 AM.
- On each ena pulse, seconds increment; if seconds overflow to 00, minutes increment; if minutes overflow, hours increment with correct 12-hour wrapping and pm toggle.

This architecture simplifies combinational logic (no division/modulo) and represents time directly as BCD digits, which can be directly connected to display or BCD bus outputs without conversion. The logic for incrementing and rolling over BCD counters is straightforward and well-structured.
