Fuse the clarity and direct BCD counting of Example 1 with the simpler binary counting and PM toggle logic of Example 2 to optimize both PPA and code clarity.

Strategy:
- Keep internal counters as binary for seconds and minutes (0-59) for simpler increment logic.
- Keep the hour counter as BCD (01-12) to avoid complex binary-to-BCD conversion, reducing division/modulo overhead.
- Implement hour increment in BCD with minimal conditional logic inspired by Example 1.
- Toggle PM only on hour rollover from 11 to 12.
- Increment seconds and minutes counters in binary with simple checks for 59 rollover.
- On reset, synchronously reset counters and PM to 12:00:00 AM.
- Outputs hh, mm, ss driven directly by BCD hour and binary-to-BCD converted minute and second values.
- Minimize combinational logic and switching by updating outputs only on clk.
- Use small helper functions for BCD increment of hours and binary-to-BCD conversion for minutes and seconds.
- This hybrid approach combines the efficient counting of binary seconds/minutes with the natural 12-hour BCD hour counting and clean PM toggle, balancing area, timing, and power.

The design is straightforward, with single always @(posedge clk) block and clear combinational functions for BCD and conversions.
