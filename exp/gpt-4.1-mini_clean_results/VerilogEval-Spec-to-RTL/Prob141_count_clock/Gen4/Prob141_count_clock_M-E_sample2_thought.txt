Implement the 12-hour clock using three separate BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with their own rollover detection signals. The counters increment on ena pulses in a cascaded manner: seconds increment first; if seconds roll over, minutes increment; if minutes roll over, hours increment; if hours roll over from 12 to 1, pm toggles. The hour counter is special: it counts from 1 to 12 in BCD, so the logic handles the 12-hour wrap-around explicitly. The reset synchronously sets the time to 12:00:00 AM. The design uses simple separate always blocks for each counter, with clear increment and rollover logic, emphasizing modularity and clarity, avoiding complex nested conditions or function calls.
