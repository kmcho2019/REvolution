Implement a 12-hour clock using separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each represented as two 4-bit BCD digits stored in registers. Each counter increments on a carry from the lower digit, with the seconds units incremented on ena pulses. To minimize combinational logic in the always blocks, use individual always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours counters, cascading the enable signals as carry flags. Hours count from 1 to 12 in BCD, resetting to 1 after 12. The PM indicator toggles when the hour wraps from 11 to 12. Use synchronous reset to initialize the clock to 12:00 AM. This modular approach isolates each counter's logic, simplifying carry detection and reducing complexity per block while maintaining correct BCD counting and clear pm toggling.
