Simplify the design by directly using BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours to avoid separate binary-to-BCD conversion logic. Implement straightforward BCD increment and carry logic within the always block. The seconds and minutes counters count from 00 to 59, incrementing the tens digit when units overflow, and rolling over after 59. The hours count 1 to 12 in BCD, toggling PM on the 11->12 hour rollover. Using BCD counters keeps all counting in BCD format, eliminating combinational conversion functions and simplifying output logic. This reduces logic complexity and critical path, maintaining synchronous reset and enable behavior.
