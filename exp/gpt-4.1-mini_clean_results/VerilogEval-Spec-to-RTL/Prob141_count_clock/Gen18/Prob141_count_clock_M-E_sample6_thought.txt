Implement a fully BCD-based clock with separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds using cascaded increments with carry signals explicitly generated in combinational logic. The hour counter stores the BCD digits separately as tens and units digits rather than as a binary value, allowing uniform BCD counting and simpler rollover detection. The PM flag toggles on hour rollover from 11 to 12 by detecting that specific transition in the hour BCD counter. The design splits the logic into two always blocks: one synchronous block updating registers on clk, reset and ena signals, and a combinational block generating the next BCD values and carry flags. This approach modularizes the counting logic, reduces combinational complexity in sequential logic, and keeps enable handling clean by gating only when ena is high. The synchronous reset sets the clock to 12:00:00 AM. The outputs are directly the stored BCD digits with no conversion needed. This purely BCD architecture simplifies the handling of each digit, ensures BCD validity at all times, and makes the design easy to extend or modify.
