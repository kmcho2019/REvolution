Implement the 12-hour clock as fully BCD-coded counters for hours, minutes, and seconds to avoid binary-to-BCD conversion and minimize combinational logic.

- Store hours as two BCD digits, ranging from 01 to 12.
- Design dedicated combinational logic that increments BCD seconds and rolls over to minutes, then hours, following 12-hour clock rules.
- Use an AM/PM flip-flop that toggles only when hours roll from 11 to 12.
- Synchronous reset sets time to 12:00:00 AM.
- All increments and rollovers are triggered only on ena pulses, ensuring minimal toggling.
- The counters advance on the positive clock edge.
- This approach yields fully BCD-encoded outputs directly from registers, minimizing conversion logic and simplifying the rollover control.
