Implement a fully binary internal time counter for hours, minutes, and seconds, incrementing once per second on the 'ena' pulse. Use pure binary counters with appropriate wrap-around logic (60 for seconds and minutes, 12 for hours). Maintain an internal PM flag toggled at hour rollover from 11 to 12. Convert the binary counters to BCD outputs on the fly using a straightforward combinational double-dabble (shift-add-3) algorithm for each output (hours, minutes, seconds). This avoids maintaining separate BCD counters and complex BCD increment logic, reducing the amount of sequential combinational logic and making the increment process faster and cleaner.

On synchronous reset, initialize the binary counters and PM flag to 12:00:00 AM (hours=12, mins=0, secs=0, pm=0). All sequential logic on the rising edge of clk and synchronous reset. The output conversion to BCD uses dedicated combinational modules invoked inside always_comb blocks to keep outputs synchronous and glitch-free.

This approach centralizes counting in simple binary counters and offloads BCD encoding to combinational logic, enabling simpler increment logic and more modular design. It also allows easy adjustment of time formats if needed and provides a clean separation of counting and display logic.
