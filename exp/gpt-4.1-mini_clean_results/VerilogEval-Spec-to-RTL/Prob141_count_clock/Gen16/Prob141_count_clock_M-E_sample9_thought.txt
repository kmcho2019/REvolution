Implement the 12-hour clock using separate BCD counters for seconds, minutes, and hours, each with a pair of 4-bit BCD digits. Each counter has its own dedicated always block triggered on clk, enabled by ena. The seconds counter increments first; on rollover (59 -> 00), it asserts a carry signal to enable minutes to increment. Similarly, the minutes counter increments on its enable, and on rollover asserts carry for hours. The hour counter counts in BCD from 01 to 12, resetting to 01 after 12. The PM flag toggles whenever hours roll from 11 to 12. Synchronous reset overrides all logic, setting clock to 12:00 AM. This approach separates counters for better timing, uses minimal combinational logic inside clocked always blocks, and makes increment logic clear and modular. Outputs are directly the concatenation of BCD digits, avoiding binary-to-BCD conversion.
