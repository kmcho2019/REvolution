Simplify the 12-hour clock design by using binary counters internally for hours, minutes, and seconds, then convert these to BCD only at output. Maintain hours in 4-bit binary counting 1-12, minutes and seconds in 6-bit binary counting 0-59. On each ena pulse, increment seconds; if roll over, increment minutes; if minutes roll over, increment hour with wrap from 12 to 1 and toggle pm at 11->12 transition. Reset synchronously sets time to 12:00 AM. This reduces complex BCD digit-by-digit incrementing and nested conditionals, simplifying sequential logic and improving clarity while keeping all functional requirements.
