Refactor the 12-hour clock by separating the counters for seconds, minutes, and hours into distinct always blocks with clear rollover signals and intermediate signals for carry propagation. Instead of a large nested if-else chain, each counter increments only when its enable is asserted, and passes a carry-out to the next counter. The hour counter stores the hour in binary (1 to 12) and toggles the PM bit on roll from 11 to 12. The final BCD outputs for hours, minutes, and seconds are assembled from BCD digit registers and binary-to-BCD conversion is done via combinational logic. This modular approach reduces critical path by breaking combinational logic into smaller segments and improves readability and maintainability. The synchronous reset resets all counters and pm to initial values. This structure uses synchronous logic with non-blocking assignments, reducing combinational depth and potential glitching in outputs.
