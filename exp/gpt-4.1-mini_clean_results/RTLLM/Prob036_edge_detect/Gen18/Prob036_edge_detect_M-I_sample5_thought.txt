The original design is already minimal and efficient with one flip-flop and simple combinational logic for edge detection. To optimize for power without increasing area or hurting timing, I can add a simple clock enable signal that only clocks the flip-flop when the input 'a' changes, reducing unnecessary toggling and thus saving dynamic power. Since 'a' is a slowly changing signal, this simple gating will save power with minimal overhead (only a small combinational gate for the enable). The logic for rise and down remains the same. This approach does not affect timing significantly and keeps area almost unchanged.
