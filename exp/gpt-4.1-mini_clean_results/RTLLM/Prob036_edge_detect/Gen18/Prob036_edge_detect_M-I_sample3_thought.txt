The provided solution is already minimal and efficient, with just one register holding the previous state of 'a' and combinational logic to detect edges. To optimize for power and potentially reduce glitching on outputs, I will register the rise and down signals so that they are synchronous outputs instead of combinational. This avoids glitches on combinational outputs in case of delay mismatch. Also, registered outputs can be better optimized by synthesis tools and might reduce power due to glitch filtering. The logic remains simple with only three flip-flops total and the timing path is unaffected. This offers slightly better signal integrity without increasing area or timing cost meaningfully.
