Implement edge detection by explicitly registering and holding the rise and down outputs until the next edge of the same type appears. This approach uses flip-flops for both the previous input state and the output signals, ensuring rise and down outputs remain asserted until their edges reoccur. On each clock cycle, detect edges by comparing current and previous input states. When a rising edge occurs, set rise to 1 and clear down; when a falling edge occurs, set down to 1 and clear rise; otherwise, maintain the previous state of rise and down. Reset asynchronously clears all registers. This design makes rise and down persistent signals rather than one-cycle pulses, differing from the typical pulse generation and meeting the requirement that rise/down remain asserted until the corresponding edge reappears.
