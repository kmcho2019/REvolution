Implement edge detection by using a two-bit shift register to store the current and previous values of 'a'. This approach tracks the last two sampled values of 'a' in sequence, enabling detection of rising and falling edges by comparing these sequential states. On each positive clock edge, shift in the current 'a' value; then, detect edges by comparing the two bits: a transition from 0 to 1 indicates a rising edge, while a transition from 1 to 0 indicates a falling edge. The rise and down signals are asserted for exactly one clock cycle immediately following the detected edge and then reset. This method eliminates the need for explicit previous state storage as a separate signal, instead using a small shift register, providing a different structural approach.
