To improve the PPA, especially power and timing, I will add an enable signal for the register that delays 'a' (a_dly) so that it only updates when 'a' actually changes. This avoids unnecessary toggling of a_dly and reduces switching power. The edge detection logic remains the same, and outputs continue to pulse for one clock cycle after the edge event. This technique does not add extra latency and helps lower dynamic power while preserving performance and area. The rest of the logic remains clean and minimal.
