To optimize the design for power without affecting performance or area noticeably, I will add a clock enable signal to the edge detection registers to update only when the input "a" changes. Since "a" changes slowly, this reduces unnecessary toggling in the flip-flops and outputs, thereby lowering dynamic power. The rise and down signals will still be generated correctly and will remain asserted for exactly one clock cycle when edges occur. This clock gating is implemented logically by enabling the always block only when "a" differs from its delayed version, minimizing switching activity.
