Use a single flip-flop to store the previous state of 'a' and detect edges by comparing the current and previous values at each clock cycle. To meet the requirement that 'rise' and 'down' signals remain high until the corresponding edge appears again, implement two state-holding registers: when a rising edge occurs, set 'rise' to 1 and clear 'down' to 0; when a falling edge occurs, set 'down' to 1 and clear 'rise' to 0. Thus, after a rising edge, 'rise' stays high until a falling edge occurs, and vice versa. On reset, clear both outputs and the previous input register.

This solution uses edge-triggered detection with explicit output state retention based on the last edge, providing complementary and mutually exclusive 'rise' and 'down' signals that reflect the last edge detected and remain asserted until the opposite edge appears.
