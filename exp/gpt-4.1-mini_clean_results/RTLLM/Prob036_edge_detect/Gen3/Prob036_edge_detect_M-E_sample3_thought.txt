Instead of using a single delayed version of input 'a' to detect edges, use a 2-bit shift register to capture two previous samples of 'a'. On each clock, shift in the current value of 'a'. Then, detect a rising edge when the pattern changes from 0 to 1 between the two most recent samples, and a falling edge when it changes from 1 to 0. Output signals 'rise' and 'down' will be asserted synchronously on the clock cycle after the edge is detected and stay at '1' until the next occurrence of the same edge. This approach inherently stores historical input values and also allows potential future scalability to multi-sample filtering if needed.
