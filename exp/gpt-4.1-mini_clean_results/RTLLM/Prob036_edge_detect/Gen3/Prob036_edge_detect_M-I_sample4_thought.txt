The original design is already minimal and clean, correctly generating one-cycle pulses for rise and down signals synchronously to the clock, using only one register for previous state. To further optimize PPA, I will avoid combinational logic inside the clocked always block by precomputing edge detection signals in a combinational block, then registering outputs. This separates combinational and sequential logic, which might improve synthesis optimizations, timing, and power. Also, I'll add an enable signal for output registers that updates only when 'a' changes, to reduce unnecessary toggling and save power. This leverages the slow-changing nature of 'a'. The enable gating avoids unnecessary updates to rise/down when no edge occurs, potentially reducing switching power and improving timing. Area impact should be minimal or beneficial since combinational logic is simple.
