Implement edge detection using a 2-bit shift register to track the history of signal 'a' over two clock cycles. By comparing the current and previous stored values, detect rising and falling edges on the clock's positive edge. This approach inherently synchronizes the inputs and ensures output signals rise and down are asserted as single-cycle pulses synchronized to clk. This also uses slightly different logic style by storing two consecutive states and comparing them, which can be beneficial in designs requiring more explicit state tracking.
