Fuse the best ideas by registering the input 'a' to synchronize it to the clock and store its previous state in a flip-flop. Then, instead of purely combinational outputs, register the rise and down signals to produce clean, glitch-free, one-clock-cycle pulses aligned exactly with the clock edges. This approach improves signal stability by avoiding any glitches or combinational hazards on output signals. The outputs become registered pulses asserted for one clock cycle immediately following the detected edge. Reset initializes both 'a' and output signals to known states, ensuring clean startup. This fusion combines the minimal resource usage of a single state register with improved output signal reliability by synchronously registering edge indicators.
