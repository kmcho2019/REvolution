Refactor the design by separating the input registering and edge detection logic into two stages. First, register the input signal 'a' into 'a_reg' synchronously with the clock and reset. Then, use continuous assignments to combinationally detect rising and falling edges based on the previous and current registered values. The outputs 'rise' and 'down' are generated by flip-flops capturing these combinational edge detection signals. This approach modularizes the design, clearly separates sequential and combinational parts, and still produces one-cycle pulses synchronized with the clock.
