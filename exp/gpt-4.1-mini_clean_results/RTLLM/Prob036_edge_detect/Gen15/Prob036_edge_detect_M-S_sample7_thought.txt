Since the input "a" is slowly changing and we only need to detect edges synchronized to the clock, we can keep the previous value of "a" in a register and detect rising and falling edges by comparing the current and previous values. To simplify further, directly generate rise and down signals as registered outputs (one cycle wide pulses) on the clock edge instead of combinational assignments, which ensures glitch-free outputs and maintains one-clock-cycle assertion of edge signals. This removes combinational output logic and keeps the design clean and synchronous.
