Implement the edge detection using a two-bit shift register to store the last two samples of 'a'. On each clock, the register shifts in the current 'a' value. By examining the transition from the second-to-last to the last stored sample, detect edges. Then, use separate output registers for rise and down to hold their state, which sets to 1 when the respective edge occurs and resets to 0 on the next clock cycle, ensuring outputs remain asserted for exactly one clock cycle after the edge.
