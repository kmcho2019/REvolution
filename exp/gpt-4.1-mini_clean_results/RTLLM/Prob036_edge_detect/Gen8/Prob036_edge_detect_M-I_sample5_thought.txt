The current design is already optimal for PPA given the simplicity of the function (a single register and combinational logic). To further improve power and glitch immunity, explicitly registering the output signals rise and down can be beneficial. This ensures rise and down are clean, glitch-free pulses synchronized to the clock, each asserted for exactly one clock cycle after the edge. The trade-off is using two additional flip-flops for outputs, but it improves signal integrity and is common practice in synchronous designs. Overall, this small change enhances robustness without significant area or power impact.
