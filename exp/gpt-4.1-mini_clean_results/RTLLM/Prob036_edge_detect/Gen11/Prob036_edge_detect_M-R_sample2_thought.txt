Refactor the design by registering the outputs rise and down signals explicitly in flip-flops instead of driving them purely combinationally. This means storing previous and current 'a' values to detect edges and updating rise and down signals as one-clock-cycle pulses held in registers. This makes the rise/down signals synchronous and explicitly one-clock-cycle wide pulses with clean state handling. The edge detection logic is moved inside a single always block, improving clarity about the timing of output updates while preserving the behavior of generating a pulse on the clock cycle following an edge.
