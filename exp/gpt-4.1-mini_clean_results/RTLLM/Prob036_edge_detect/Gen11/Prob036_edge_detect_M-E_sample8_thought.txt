Instead of using a single delayed register of 'a', this design will use a 2-bit shift register to sample the input signal 'a' over two clock cycles. By examining the two most recent values, the module detects edges. Additionally, the output signals rise and down are registered outputs, holding the edge indication for exactly one clock cycle. This approach cleanly separates edge detection and output signaling, ensuring outputs are synchronous and stable for one clock cycle, automatically returning to zero afterward.
