Fuse the clarity and simplicity of Example 2's single-register approach with Example 1's fully registered, glitch-free output signals approach.

Specifically:
- Register the input signal "a" to a_1d at each clock.
- Generate combinational rise and down signals by comparing the registered previous value (a_1d) and current input (a).
- Register these combinational rise and down signals into output flip-flops (rise_reg and down_reg) so that rise and down signals become synchronous to the clock and remain high for exactly one clock cycle after the detected edge.
- This ensures outputs rise and down are glitch-free and stable for a full clock cycle, as required, while keeping the design minimal with only three flip-flops total.
- The approach reduces logic compared to the FSM and improves output timing and glitch robustness compared to purely combinational outputs.
- Use nonblocking assignments consistently and asynchronous active-low reset for clarity and good synthesis style.

This combination yields a clean, minimal, and robust edge detector meeting all specifications precisely with optimal PPA characteristics.
