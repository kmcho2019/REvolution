Refactor the design to generate registered outputs for rise and down signals that remain asserted for exactly one clock cycle immediately after detecting the respective edges. Store the previous value of 'a' in a register as before, but drive rise and down as registered outputs updated inside a synchronous always block. This aligns with the requirement that rise or down "will be set to 1 on the next clock when the corresponding edge appears, and then return to 0 until the next edge."
