To generate edge detection signals that are set to '1' for exactly one clock cycle when an edge occurs and then return to '0', I will implement the rise and down outputs as registered signals that are set when an edge is detected, and cleared in the next clock cycle. This can be done by detecting the edge from a registered version of 'a', then setting rise or down to '1' on the next clock cycle, and clearing them afterwards. This requires a simple state in rise and down registers to hold the pulse for one clock cycle. The design uses two flip-flops: one to delay 'a', and two registers for rise and down pulses, which makes the outputs synchronous and guaranteed to be a single-cycle pulse at the clock domain.
