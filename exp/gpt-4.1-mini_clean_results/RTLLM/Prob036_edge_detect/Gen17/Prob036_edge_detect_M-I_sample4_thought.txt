To optimize the original edge_detect module for better PPA (performance, power, area), we can keep the general approach but further reduce combinational logic depth by making outputs registered instead of purely combinational. This means "rise" and "down" will be single-cycle pulses generated by registering the edge detection logic, ensuring glitch-free outputs and more stable timing with negligible overhead.

Improvement strategy:
1. Register the previous value of 'a' (a_reg) for edge detection.
2. Generate combinational edge detect signals (rise_comb, down_comb).
3. Register these combinational signals to outputs rise and down on the clock edge.
4. On reset, outputs are cleared to 0.
5. This approach makes the outputs synchronous, glitch-free pulses asserted for exactly one clock cycle after the detected edge.

This approach may slightly increase area due to output registers but improves timing (shortens critical combinational path) and reduces power by eliminating glitches on rise and down signals. It also meets the requirement that rise or down are set to 1 on the next clock after the edge appears and then cleared, exactly one cycle pulse behavior.

This is a common and well-accepted edge detection scheme in synchronous designs.
