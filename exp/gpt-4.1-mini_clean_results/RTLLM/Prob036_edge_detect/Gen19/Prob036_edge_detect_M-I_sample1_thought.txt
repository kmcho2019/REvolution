The original design is already minimal and efficient for detecting edges by storing the previous state of 'a'. To optimize power further without impacting timing or area, we can add a clock enable that updates the previous state register only when 'a' changes. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the flip-flop when 'a' is stable, which can lower dynamic power consumption. Since the clock gating is simple and local, it won't increase area significantly nor hurt timing. The functionality and output timing remain the same, producing 1-cycle pulses aligned to clk.
