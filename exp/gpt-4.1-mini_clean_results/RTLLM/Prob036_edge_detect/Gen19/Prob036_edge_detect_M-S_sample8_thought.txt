Simplify the design by removing the asynchronous reset and instead use a synchronous reset, which simplifies the logic and timing while still reliably initializing the stored signal. The delayed signal a_d will be set to 0 synchronously at the rising edge of clk when rst_n is low. Edge detection signals rise and down remain combinational, asserted for one clock cycle immediately following the detected edge. This approach reduces complexity while maintaining the required functionality and clarity.
