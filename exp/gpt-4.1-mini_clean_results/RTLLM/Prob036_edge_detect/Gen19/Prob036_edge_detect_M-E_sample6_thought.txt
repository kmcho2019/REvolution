Implement edge detection by storing the input 'a' into a 2-bit shift register updated each clock cycle. The two bits represent the current and previous state of 'a', allowing detection of rising (01) and falling (10) edges by comparing consecutive states stored in the shift register. Outputs 'rise' and 'down' will be registered signals set to 1 for exactly one clock cycle after the corresponding edge occurs, then automatically cleared on the next clock. This structure is modular and easily extendable for multi-bit or multi-cycle edge detection if needed.
