Refactor the edge detection by explicitly registering the outputs rise and down as 1-cycle pulse signals, instead of making them purely combinational from the delayed input. This means we will keep a delayed version of 'a' and compare it with the current 'a' to detect edges, but rise and down outputs will be registered signals that set to 1 when edges are detected and then clear back to 0 on the next clock cycle. This makes the outputs registered pulses, which could be preferable in some synchronous designs that need edge pulses aligned with the clock.
