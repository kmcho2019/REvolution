The original solution already efficiently detects edges with minimal logic and a single register. However, to better align with the requirement that "rise" and "down" should be set to 1 on the clock cycle after the edge appears and remain 1 until the next corresponding edge, we need to latch the rise and down signals rather than producing a one-cycle pulse. This requires storing the rise and down flags in registers, setting them when the edge is detected, and clearing them when the opposite edge appears. This approach will keep the signals asserted from the clock after an edge until a new edge clears or sets them. While this adds two registers, it meets the specified behavior exactly and keeps PPA overhead minimal.
