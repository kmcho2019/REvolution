Implement edge detection by using a two-bit shift register to store the current and previous states of the input signal 'a'. On each clock cycle, shift in the new 'a' value. Then, detect rising and falling edges by comparing the two bits: a rising edge occurs if the previous bit was 0 and the current bit is 1; a falling edge occurs if the previous bit was 1 and the current bit is 0. The outputs rise and down are registered to ensure they are asserted for one full clock cycle after the edge detection and then cleared until the next edge appears.
