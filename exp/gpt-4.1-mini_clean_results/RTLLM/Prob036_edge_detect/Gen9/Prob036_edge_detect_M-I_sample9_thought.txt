To improve the original solution for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will ensure that rise and down outputs are registered, producing clean, single-cycle pulses synchronized with clk. This avoids potential glitches on combinational outputs, making timing more robust in high-frequency designs. The registered outputs will be set to 1 exactly one cycle after detecting the edge, then return to 0 until the next edge, satisfying the spec precisely.

This adds two flip-flops but improves timing robustness and avoids glitching, which benefits power (less glitch power) and timing closure at the expense of minimal area increase. The slight increase in registers is justified by improved synchronous behavior.

Implementation:
- Store previous state of 'a' in a_reg
- On clock edge, compare a_reg and current a to detect edges
- Set rise_reg or down_reg accordingly
- Clear them on next clock cycle unless a new edge is detected

This approach cleanly registers all outputs and ensures exactly one clock cycle pulse per edge.
