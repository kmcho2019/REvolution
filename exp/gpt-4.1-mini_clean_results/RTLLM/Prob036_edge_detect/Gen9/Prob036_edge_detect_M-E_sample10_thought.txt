Instead of using an FSM or comparing the previous state explicitly, use two registers to sample the input signal 'a' over two consecutive clock cycles. By comparing these two delayed samples, we can detect edges: if the previous sample is 0 and the current sample is 1, a rising edge occurred; if the previous sample is 1 and the current sample is 0, a falling edge occurred. The rise and down outputs are registered signals driven by this edge detection and will remain high for exactly one clock cycle. This approach is simple, efficient, and inherently synchronized with the clock.
