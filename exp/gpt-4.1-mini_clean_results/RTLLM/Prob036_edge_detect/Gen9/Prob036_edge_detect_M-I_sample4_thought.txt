To improve the original design for better timing closure and reduced glitches on outputs, register the rise and down signals so they assert high for exactly one clock cycle when their corresponding edges are detected. This synchronous pulsing output reduces combinational paths from internal registers to outputs and prevents glitching, which can improve power and timing metrics. The input "a" is still synchronized with a 2-stage shift register to prevent metastability. Outputs rise and down will be driven by registered signals updated on clock edges.
