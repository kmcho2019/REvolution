To detect edges on a synchronous signal "a", store its previous value in a register. On each clock cycle, compare the current and previous values to detect rising (0->1) or falling (1->0) edges. When an edge is detected, set the corresponding output signal (rise or down) to 1 on the next clock cycle and keep it high until the same edge type occurs again (i.e., toggle a latch that remembers the last detected edge to reset the output). Use synchronous reset to initialize registers.
