To optimize for better PPA, the design can be simplified by removing the FSM and instead just registering the previous value of input signal 'a'. On every clock edge, compare the current and previous 'a' values to detect rising or falling edges. This approach reduces logic complexity and area since it only uses one 1-bit register and combinational comparisons. The outputs rise and down can be registered to ensure glitch-free output and are asserted for one clock cycle immediately after detecting edges, then reset to zero. This direct register comparison approach is more power- and area-efficient and yields minimal delay.
