Implement edge detection by explicitly registering the previous input signal 'a' and generating the rise and down signals as registered outputs that are set high for one clock cycle when an edge is detected, then automatically cleared on the next clock. This approach uses two registers for the outputs 'rise' and 'down' instead of combinational logic, ensuring glitch-free, synchronous outputs held for exactly one cycle after detection. It clearly separates the edge detection logic and output signaling stages.
