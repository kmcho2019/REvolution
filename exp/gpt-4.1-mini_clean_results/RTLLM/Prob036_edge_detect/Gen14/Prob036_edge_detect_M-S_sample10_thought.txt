Since the previous design is already minimal with one register and combinational logic for detecting edges, the simplest further step is to remove the explicit output signals as wires and instead register the edge detection signals themselves. This slightly simplifies timing by synchronizing outputs with clk and ensuring rise/down signals are one clock cycle pulses, without additional combinational logic on outputs. Overall, use one register to store 'a' and generate rise/down as registered signals, maintaining the same functionality with very simple code.
