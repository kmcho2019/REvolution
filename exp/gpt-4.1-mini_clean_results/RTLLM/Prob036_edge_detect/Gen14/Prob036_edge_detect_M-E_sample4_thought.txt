Implement edge detection by registering the output signals rise and down separately, rather than generating them combinationally. On every clock cycle, compare the current input 'a' with its previous value. If a rising edge is detected, set rise to 1; if a falling edge is detected, set down to 1. Both rise and down remain asserted only for one clock cycle immediately following the detection of their respective edges. This approach explicitly stores outputs in flip-flops, reducing glitches and ensuring outputs are synchronous and stable.
