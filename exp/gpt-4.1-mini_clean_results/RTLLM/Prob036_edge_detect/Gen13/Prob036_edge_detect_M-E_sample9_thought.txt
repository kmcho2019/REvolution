Implement the edge detector by using a two-flip-flop synchronizer to register the input signal 'a' on the clock, which improves robustness for slow asynchronous signals by synchronizing them safely. Then detect edges by comparing these two successive registered values. Instead of combinational outputs, register the rise and down signals so that the outputs hold '1' for exactly one clock cycle on the detected edge and are zero otherwise. Reset initializes the synchronizer and output registers to zero, ensuring deterministic startup. This method offers a robust and clean edge detection solution with glitch-free registered outputs.
