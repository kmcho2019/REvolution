Implement edge detection using a two-stage synchronizer for signal 'a' to ensure clean sampling of 'a' in the clock domain, then compare these two registered signals to detect edges. Output signals 'rise' and 'down' are registered to hold the indication high until the next corresponding edge occurs, effectively latching the edge event until cleared by the arrival of a new edge in the opposite direction. This ensures 'rise' and 'down' outputs are stable and clear only when a new edge is detected. Reset clears all registers.
