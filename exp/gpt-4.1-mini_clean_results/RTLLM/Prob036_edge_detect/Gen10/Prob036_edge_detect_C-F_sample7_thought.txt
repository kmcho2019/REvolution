Combine the simplicity and minimalism of the previous examples with explicit registered outputs for 'rise' and 'down' signals. This avoids any combinational glitches on the outputs and ensures that the edge indication signals remain asserted synchronously for exactly one clock cycle following the detection. The input 'a' is registered to synchronize it and detect edges by comparing with its previous state. The outputs 'rise' and 'down' are also registered, set to 1 when the respective edge is detected and cleared in the next clock cycle if no new edge occurs. This approach improves output signal stability and reduces switching glitches, beneficial for power and timing, while keeping area overhead minimal by adding only two flip-flops for output registers.
