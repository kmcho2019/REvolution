Use a two-flip-flop synchronizer chain to register the input signal 'a' twice in consecutive clock cycles. By doing so, we get the delayed versions a_d1 and a_d2. Compare a_d2 and a_d1 to detect edges: when a_d2=0 and a_d1=1 it's a rising edge, and when a_d2=1 and a_d1=0 it's a falling edge. Then, use registered outputs rise_reg and down_reg which are set to 1 on the clock cycle following edge detection and automatically cleared on the next clock, thus generating a single-clock pulse for each edge and synchronizing the outputs fully with the clock domain.
