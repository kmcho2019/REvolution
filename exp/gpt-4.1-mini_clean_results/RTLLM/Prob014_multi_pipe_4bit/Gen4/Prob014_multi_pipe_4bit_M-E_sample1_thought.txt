Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline strictly separating pipeline stages to ensure proper timing and data stability. 

Stage 1:
- Register inputs (extended multiplicand and multiplier).
- Generate and register all partial products from the registered multiplier bits and multiplicand.
This stage produces stable partial products at its output registers.

Stage 2:
- Add all partial products sequentially in a combinational adder tree, but register the sums in stage 2 registers.
- Finally, output the sum of all partial products as the final product.

This design guarantees:
- Inputs registered first.
- Partial products computed and registered in stage 1.
- Sums computed from stable stage 1 outputs and registered in stage 2.
- Output registered, providing a clean pipeline with two levels of registers for intermediate values.

The input multiplicand is extended by concatenating zeros at the MSB side before registration.

Use a generate block for partial product generation and arrays of registers for pipeline registers.

This method cleanly isolates combinational partial product generation, pipelined storage of partial products, and a pipelined addition stage producing the final product output.

This satisfies the requirement of two pipeline register stages controlling multiplication and intermediate storage.
