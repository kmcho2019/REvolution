Implement a classic 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline using registers to hold intermediate sums and partial products.

Pipeline Stage 1:
- Register the extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs.
- Generate and register partial products for each bit of the multiplier by checking each multiplier bit and left-shifting the multiplicand accordingly.

Pipeline Stage 2:
- Sum all partial products registered in stage 1 and register the final product output.

This approach clearly separates partial product generation and accumulation into two pipeline stages, with registers at each stage ensuring stable data at clock edges. The extended multiplicand is size bits wider at MSB to accommodate shifting.

By separating the partial product computation and their summation into distinct register stages, the design adheres to pipeline discipline and timing requirements, avoiding combinational loops or race conditions.

This fully synchronous design uses an asynchronous active-low reset and produces a 2*size-bit product output registered at the final pipeline stage.
