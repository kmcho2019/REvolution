Refactor the pipeline multiplier by separating the partial product generation and the addition stages more explicitly. Instead of summing partial products directly within the always blocks, use combinational adders outside the registers and only register the sums. This modularizes the design: generate all partial products combinationally, sum them combinationally in two stages, then register the intermediate sums in pipeline registers. This allows clearer data flow separation between combinational and sequential logic, easing timing analysis and improving readability. Also, use vectors to hold pipeline registers instead of arrays to simplify synthesis and tool compatibility.
