Simplify the 4-bit pipelined multiplier by generating all partial products in a loop and summing them in two pipeline stages:
- Stage 1: Sum the partial products for bits 0 and 1 of the multiplier.
- Stage 2: Sum the partial products for bits 2 and 3.
- Stage 3: Add the two stage sums to produce the final product.

Use a generate loop to create partial products, avoid explicit separate wires for each partial product, and store intermediate sums in registers for pipelining.

Reset registers and output to zero asynchronously on active-low reset, and update synchronously on clock edge.
