Implement a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier with two pipeline stages by splitting the multiplication into two phases:

- Stage 1: Generate and register all shifted multiplicand bits gated by multiplier bits (partial products).
- Stage 2: Accumulate the two halves of partial products sums separately in two registers, then sum these two partial sums into the final product.

Details:
- Inputs (mul_a and mul_b) extended with zeros on MSB side for uniform shift and width (2*size).
- Partial products are generated combinationally by AND-ing each bit of mul_b with the shifted mul_a.
- Stage 1: Register each partial product.
- Stage 2: Partition the partial products into two groups (lower half and upper half), sum each group into intermediate registers, then sum these two intermediate sums into the final product register.
- Using this hierarchical summation reduces combinational addition depth in stage 2.
- Synchronous reset active low.
- Fully parameterized with size=4 default.
This approach creates a neat two-stage pipeline with clear intermediate registers and balanced addition logic to optimize timing.
