Simplify the design by using exactly two pipeline stages of registers:

- Stage 1 registers latch all partial products simultaneously at the rising clock edge.
- Stage 2 registers accumulate the sum of the partial products at the next clock edge.

This means partial products are generated combinationally from inputs, stage1_pp stores these partial products, stage2_sum registers store their sum computed combinationally from stage1_pp.

This approach meets the two-level pipeline requirement and the use of registers for intermediate values and final output.

The multiplication result is output after two clock cycles latency: inputs are registered into partial products, then partial products summed and registered into output.

Input signals are extended by adding "size" zero bits at MSB.

This eliminates the need for control counters or multi-cycle accumulation, simplifying control and ensuring correct synchronization.

Reset clears all pipeline registers and output.

This fully complies with the problem statement in a straightforward way.
