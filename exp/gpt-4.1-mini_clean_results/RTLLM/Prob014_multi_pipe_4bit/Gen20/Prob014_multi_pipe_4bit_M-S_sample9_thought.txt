Simplify the 4-bit pipelined multiplier by:

- Extending multiplicand (mul_a) by size zeros on the MSB side.
- Generating partial products combinationally for each bit of mul_b using shifts and masks.
- Using only two pipeline register levels:
  1. Register all partial products.
  2. Register the sum of the partial products to produce the final product.
- Reset asynchronously clears registers to zero.
- This approach removes the intermediate sum register stage, reducing complexity while meeting the two-level register requirement (one for partial products, one for final output).
