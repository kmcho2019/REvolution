Implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers that store intermediate values.

Steps:
1. Extend multiplicand (mul_a) by adding 'size' zeros on MSB side to make it 2*size bits.
2. Generate partial products combinationally using a generate loop for each bit of mul_b.
3. Stage 1 registers: latch partial products.
4. Stage 2 registers: sum all partial products into a single value.
5. Output register holds the final product (sum from stage 2).
6. Reset asynchronously clears all registers.
7. Use two levels of registers: 
   - stage1 partial product registers,
   - stage2 final sum register.
8. The final output is updated from the stage 2 sum register to maintain pipeline structure.

This reduces the complexity by summing all partial products in one stage 2 register instead of pairwise sums, but still meets the two-level register pipeline requirement.
