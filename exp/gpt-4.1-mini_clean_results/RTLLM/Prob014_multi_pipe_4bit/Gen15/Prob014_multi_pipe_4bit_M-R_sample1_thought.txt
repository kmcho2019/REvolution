Refactoring Idea:

Keep the same two-stage pipeline structure but implement it differently by:
1. Using combinational `assign` statements for generating partial products and summing them.
2. Replacing the array of registers for partial products with a single register holding the sum of partial products at stage 1.
3. Stage 1 register directly captures the sum of partial products, not individual partial products.
4. Stage 2 register captures the final output from the stage 1 sum register.
5. This reduces the number of registers and simplifies the datapath, while maintaining two levels of registers.
6. The inputs are still extended with zeros on the MSBs as required.
7. Reset is synchronous active low reset (on clock's rising edge), aligned with typical FPGA style (but can be changed to async if strictly needed).
8. Using only nonblocking assignments and avoiding loops inside always blocks.
9. The multiplication partial products are combined combinationally before stage 1 register, reducing complexity and potential large combinational adder trees after registers.

This refactor simplifies register usage, reduces register count and critical path, improves synthesizability, and keeps the pipelined multiplier behavior intact.
