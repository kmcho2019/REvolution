To simplify the design while maintaining the required two-level pipelining, we directly generate partial products by shifting the multiplicand based on the multiplier bits. We then group partial products into two sums (for bits 0-1 and bits 2-3) which are stored in the first pipeline stage registers. The second pipeline stage sums those two registers and stores in another register. The output register then holds this final sum. This maintains two levels of registers for pipelining and meets all reset and clocking requirements. The input extension is done simply by zero-padding at MSB. This approach reduces complexity by avoiding separate generate loops and minimizing intermediate signals, while still preserving clear pipeline stages.
