This design takes a different pipelined approach by splitting the multiplication into two pipeline stages with explicit registers for inputs, partial products, and sums. 

First, inputs `mul_a` and `mul_b` are extended by concatenating zeros on the MSB side to get 8-bit values, preserving bit alignment for shifts.

In stage 1 (first clock cycle after reset), the module generates the partial products for each multiplier bit by ANDing the extended multiplicand with the single multiplier bit extended to the operand width, then left-shifting according to the bit index. These partial products are summed in pairs (0+1 and 2+3) and stored in two registers.

In stage 2 (second clock cycle), the two sums are added and stored in a third register. 

Finally, on the third clock cycle, the final register outputs the total product sum.

This structure clearly separates partial product generation, intermediate summation, and final summation, with two pipeline register levels holding sums. Inputs are registered on the first stage to synchronize them. The module resets all pipeline registers asynchronously on active low reset.

This approach emphasizes clear stage separation and data synchronization, using two levels of addition registers for pipelining the 4-bit multiplication.
