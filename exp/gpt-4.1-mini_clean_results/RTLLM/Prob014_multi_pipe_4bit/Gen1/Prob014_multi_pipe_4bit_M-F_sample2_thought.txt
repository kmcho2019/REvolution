To fix the synthesis and elaboration issues, especially related to the use of integer loop variables and temporary regs inside the always block, I will move the summation of partial products outside of the sequential always blocks and implement the addition using a combinational balanced adder tree structure inside a generate block. This avoids procedural for-loops for summation.

The partial products will be split into two groups (half and half). Each group’s sum will be generated combinationally and assigned to wires. These sums are then registered into pipeline registers on the clock. The final product will be calculated by adding the two pipeline registers in another pipeline stage.

This approach eliminates the usage of procedural loops inside always blocks, making the design more synthesis-friendly and easier for tools to infer adders and registers.

The input extension remains as requested (zero-extension by size bits at MSB).

The pipeline has two register levels: 
- Stage 1 registers hold the sum of half partial products each.
- Stage 2 register holds the sum of the two stage 1 registers as final output.

Reset remains synchronous active-low.

No integer or reg variables for looping or accumulation inside always blocks.

This design improves synthesizability, avoids tool-specific synthesis errors, and keeps the pipeline multiplier functionally correct.
