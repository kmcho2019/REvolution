Simplify the pipeline multiplier by using two levels of registers: the first level stores the partial products generated by shifting the multiplicand according to each bit of the multiplier, and the second level stores the sum of all partial products to produce the final output. This avoids multiple addition stages and multiple arrays of registers by summing partial products directly in the second stage. Reset logic is maintained for all registers. The multiplicand is extended by zeros at MSB side before shifting. This approach reduces complexity while keeping the two-level pipeline structure.
