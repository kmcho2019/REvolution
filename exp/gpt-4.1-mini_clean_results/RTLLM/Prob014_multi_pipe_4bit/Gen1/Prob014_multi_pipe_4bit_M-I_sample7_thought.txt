To improve the original design for better synthesis compatibility, performance, and clarity, the following strategy is applied:

1. Avoid loop and integer declarations inside clocked always blocks for summations. Instead, use combinational adder trees implemented with generate blocks to sum partial products before registering the sums.

2. Partition the summation of partial products into two balanced groups and sum each group combinationally with adders; register these sums as the first pipeline stage. Then sum the two registered sums in the next pipeline stage to produce the final output.

3. Keep input extension as specified, but simplify the partial product generation by extending mul_a once and shifting within 2*size width. Use combinational logic for partial products and additions, registered at clock edges.

4. Use registers only to store intermediate sums and final product output, each updated on clk rising edge with async active low reset.

5. This restructuring reduces the size of combinational adders per pipeline stage and eliminates procedural loop usage inside always blocks, improving synthesis compatibility and timing.

6. Keep parameterized design for reusability and straightforward scalability.

This approach yields a clean, fully synthesizable 2-stage pipeline multiplier for 4-bit unsigned inputs with balanced adder trees for partial product summations.
