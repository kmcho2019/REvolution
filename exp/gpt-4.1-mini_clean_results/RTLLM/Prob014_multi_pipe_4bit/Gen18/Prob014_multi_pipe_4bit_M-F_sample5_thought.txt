The previous design is functionally correct but uses SystemVerilog-style arrays of wires and registers, which may cause synthesis issues with some tools expecting pure Verilog-2001 syntax. Also, the combinational addition of multiple wide vectors in an always @* loop with a for-loop is less synthesis-friendly.

To fix these:

1. Replace wire and reg arrays with packed vectors using `generate` and indexing, avoiding multi-dimensional arrays.

2. Sum the partial products in a balanced adder tree implemented explicitly with intermediate registers in pipeline stages.

3. Implement the two-level pipeline with registers storing intermediate sums after the first summation level, then registers for the final summation to output.

4. Avoid `always @*` loops for addition; instead, use combinational assignments with explicit adders, and pipeline registers for intermediate sums.

5. Use pure Verilog syntax that is synthesis-friendly: vectors instead of arrays, standard `always @(posedge clk or negedge rst_n)` blocks.

6. Maintain the extension of multiplicand by `size` zeros on MSB.

This approach keeps the pipeline stages for latency, improves synthesis tool friendliness, and avoids SystemVerilog array usage causing tool errors.

Since `size=4` is fixed here, summations can be hardcoded explicitly for simplicity and clarity. This also avoids generate complexity and is easily extensible.

Overall, the updated design respects the original requirements with better synthesis compatibility.
