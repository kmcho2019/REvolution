New Architectural Idea:

Implement a 2-stage pipelined 4-bit unsigned multiplier by splitting the multiplication and addition into two pipeline stages in a sequential manner instead of fully parallel partial product generation. This architecture performs accumulation of partial products sequentially in pipeline stage 1, and final output register update in pipeline stage 2.

Key points:
- Inputs mul_a and mul_b are zero-extended on MSB side as requested.
- Pipeline stage 1:
  - For each bit of mul_b (multiplier), compute a shifted partial product if mul_b bit is 1.
  - Accumulate these partial products over several clock cycles with an internal counter (0 to size-1).
  - At each clock, take one partial product and add it to a running sum register (stage 1 register).
- Pipeline stage 2:
  - The accumulated sum from stage 1 is registered at the next clock (stage 2 register).
  - Output mul_out is updated with this registered final product.
- Control signals:
  - An internal counter controls which bit of mul_b is processed each clock cycle.
  - On reset, all registers and counter are cleared.
- This design uses two levels of registers:
  1. Accumulator register holding intermediate sum after each partial product addition.
  2. Output register holding final product after all partial products are added.
- Latency is size+1 cycles (5 cycles for size=4): 4 cycles to accumulate partial products, 1 cycle to register output.
- This sequential accumulation with pipelined output allows a simpler datapath without large combinational adders and a balanced pipeline.

This new approach shows a pipelined sequential multiplier that is different from fully parallel partial product addition and registers all partial products, meeting the problem requirements of two levels of registers controlling the multiplication process.
