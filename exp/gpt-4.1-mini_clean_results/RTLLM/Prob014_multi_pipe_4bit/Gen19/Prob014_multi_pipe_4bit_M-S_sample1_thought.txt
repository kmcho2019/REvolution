Implement a 4-bit pipeline multiplier with two register stages as follows:

- Extend multiplicand by size zeros on MSB side.
- Generate all partial products combinationally based on multiplier bits.
- First pipeline stage: register all partial products.
- Second pipeline stage: sum all registered partial products together in one clock cycle and register the final product.
- Use asynchronous active-low reset on all registers.
- Output registered product.
- This approach keeps pipeline simple (two registers stages), avoids complex multiple additions stages, and ensures functionality.
