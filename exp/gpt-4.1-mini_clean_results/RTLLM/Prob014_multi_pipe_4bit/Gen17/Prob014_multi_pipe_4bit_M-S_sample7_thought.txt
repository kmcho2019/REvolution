Simplify the design by generating all partial products in a generate loop and accumulating them in two pipeline stages as required.

- Extend multiplicand by size zeros on MSB side.
- Use generate block to create partial products for each multiplier bit.
- In pipeline stage 1 register the sum of partial products for bits 0 and 1.
- In pipeline stage 2 register the sum of partial products for bits 2 and 3 plus previous stage sum.
- On final stage register the output product.
- Use synchronous reset (active low) to clear pipeline registers and output.
- This reduces code duplication and simplifies the design while maintaining two pipeline stages for intermediate sums.
