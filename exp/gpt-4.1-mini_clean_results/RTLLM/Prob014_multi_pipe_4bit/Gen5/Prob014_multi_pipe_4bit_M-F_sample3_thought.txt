To improve timing and align with the requirement of two pipeline stages, implement a two-level register pipeline that accumulates partial products progressively: in the first stage, sum partial products pairwise into two intermediate registers, and in the second stage, sum these two registers to produce the final product. This breaks down the wide addition into smaller adders, reducing combinational delay and area. Also, maintain synchronous reset and clocking as before. The multiplicand is zero-extended at MSB, and each partial product is conditionally generated and shifted. This approach better pipelines the addition without changing the original interface and functionality.
