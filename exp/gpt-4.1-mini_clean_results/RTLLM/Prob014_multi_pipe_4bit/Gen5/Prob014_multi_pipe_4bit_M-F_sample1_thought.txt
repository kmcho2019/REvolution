To fix the multiplier according to the problem and feedback:

1. **Correct Input Extension**:  
   The problem explicitly states to extend the input signals (mul_a, mul_b) by adding "size" zero bits at the MSB side. This means the multiplicand and multiplier become 2*size bits by concatenating size zeros on the *most significant side*. This is unusual but must be respected literally.

2. **Partial Product Generation via Generate Block**:  
   Use a generate block to create partial products per multiplier bit:  
   For each bit i in [0, size-1]:  
   - If mul_b[i] == 1, partial product = mul_a shifted left by i  
   - Else partial product = 0  
   Since the inputs are extended by size zeros at MSB, the mul_a is 2*size bits wide but only the lower (size + i) bits will hold actual values.

3. **Two Levels of Pipeline Registers**:  
   - Level 1: Register partial products after generation (store intermediate partial products)  
   - Level 2: Register sums of partial products after addition (store accumulated sum)  

   This satisfies the requirement of "two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process."

4. **Addition of Partial Products**:  
   Perform addition of partial products in two steps with registers in between:  
   - First sum the lower half of partial products into stage 1 register.  
   - Then sum the stage 1 register with the upper half of partial products into stage 2 register.  

   This approach pipelines the sum, spreading addition over two stages.

5. **Output Update**:  
   mul_out updates synchronously from the stage 2 register on each clock.

6. **Synchronous Reset**:  
   On rst_n == 0, clear all pipeline registers and output.

This method is fully combinational for partial product generation but pipelined over two registers for addition. It follows the problem statement and solves issues from previous design by:  
- Correct input extension usage  
- Clear generate block for partial products  
- Two stages of registers explicitly holding intermediate partial products and sums  
- Synchronous logic for all registers  
- Fully parameterized  

This approach fits classical pipelined multiplication with partial product registers and sum registers.
