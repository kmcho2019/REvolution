Simplify the pipeline multiplier by separating the process into two pipeline stages:

1. Stage 1: Register the inputs and generate all partial products combinationally based on registered inputs.
2. Stage 2: Register the sum of all partial products and output the final product.

This keeps partial product generation purely combinational, based on registered multiplicand and multiplier from stage 1, and uses registers only for storing inputs and results at pipeline boundaries. The summation is done in a single combinational expression for clarity. The design remains fully synchronous with asynchronous active-low reset.

This approach reduces complexity by eliminating arrays of registers for partial products, instead using wires for partial products and just two pipeline register stages, maintaining clear pipeline separation and correct data timing.
