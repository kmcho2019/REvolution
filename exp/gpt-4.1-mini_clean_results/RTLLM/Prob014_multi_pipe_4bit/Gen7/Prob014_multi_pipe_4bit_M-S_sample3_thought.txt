Use the parameter `size` to generalize bit widths and simplify the pipeline multiplier with two pipeline stages of registers. Extend inputs by `size` zeros on MSB side to create a 2*size-bit width. Generate partial products for each multiplier bit with a simple for-generate loop, and store them in wires. Use two pipeline register stages: first stage sums pairs of partial products, second stage sums the first stage outputs to produce the final product. Registers are updated on the positive clock edge with asynchronous active-low reset. This structure reduces logic complexity while maintaining required pipeline behavior.
