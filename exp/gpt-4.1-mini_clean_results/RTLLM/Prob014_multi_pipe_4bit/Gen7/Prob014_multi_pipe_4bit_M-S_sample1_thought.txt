Simplify the design by implementing a two-stage pipelined multiplier for 4-bit unsigned numbers with these steps:

1. Extend inputs by adding `size` zeros at MSB as required.

2. In the first pipeline stage (first register level), generate all partial products based on each bit of the multiplier:
   - For each bit i of mul_b, partial product = mul_a shifted left by i if bit is 1, else zero.
   - Sum partial products of lower half bits (e.g., bits 0 and 1) into one intermediate register.
   - Sum partial products of upper half bits (e.g., bits 2 and 3) into another intermediate register.

3. The second pipeline stage (second register level) adds the two intermediate sums to get the final product.

4. Use synchronous resets to clear registers and output.

5. This pipeline implements two levels of registers, intermediate sums are stored at stage 1, final product output registered at stage 2.

This approach is straightforward, fully combinational partial product generation but with pipelined add stages, meeting the requirement of two register levels and synchronous operation.
