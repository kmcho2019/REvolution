Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages:

1. Stage 1: Register the extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs.

2. Stage 2: Generate partial products for each bit of the registered multiplier and sum them combinationally, then register this intermediate sum.

Finally, assign the registered sum to the output.

This maintains two pipeline levels: input registers and intermediate sum registers, meeting the problem requirements. Use only allowed declarations outside procedural blocks. Avoid illegal variable declarations inside always blocks by using generate loops and wire arrays for partial products. The design performs multiplication in a pipelined manner with clear synchronous resets.
