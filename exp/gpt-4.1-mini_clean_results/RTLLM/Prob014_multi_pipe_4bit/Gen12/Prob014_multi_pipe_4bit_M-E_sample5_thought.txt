Implement a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier with two pipeline stages that:
1. Extends both multiplicand and multiplier by adding zeros at MSBs to double their width.
2. Generates all partial products combinationally as AND results of the multiplicand bits with each bit of the multiplier, shifted appropriately.
3. The first pipeline stage registers the partial products (one for each bit of the multiplier).
4. The second pipeline stage sums all the registered partial products sequentially across multiple cycles using an internal counter and accumulator register.
5. The final pipeline register stores the completed product output.
6. This design time-multiplexes the addition of partial products over pipeline cycles instead of summing them all at once, reducing combinational logic delay in the second stage at the cost of latency.
7. The design uses a simple FSM to control addition sequence synchronized with the clock and reset.
8. Parameterized for scalability, but fixed to size=4 as per spec.
This novel architecture balances area and timing by pipelining partial product generation and incrementally accumulating the product over cycles.
