To improve the initial 4-bit pipeline multiplier design for better Performance, Power, and Area (PPA), while maintaining correctness and ease of synthesis:

1. **Use register arrays with generate blocks:** This reduces code verbosity and improves scalability while retaining clarity and synthesizability. Modern synthesis tools handle register arrays well.

2. **Add clock enable signals:** Register updates only when needed to reduce switching and dynamic power.

3. **Balance addition tree with pipelined registers:** Insert an additional pipeline register in the adder tree to reduce critical path delay further, improving maximum operating frequency.

4. **Keep asynchronous active-low reset:** For clear reset behavior.

5. **Parameterize the design cleanly and consistently:** Ensure all widths derive from the parameter `size` for easy modification.

6. **Keep zero extension of inputs:** It simplifies shifting and aligns all operands to the same width.

The improved design stages:

- **Stage 1:** Register partial products (size registers).
- **Stage 2:** Pairwise sum partial products (half as many registers).
- **Stage 3:** Sum the results from stage 2 and register final product output.

This 3-stage pipeline reduces combinational path length per clock cycle and increases throughput and max frequency at the expense of 1 extra clock cycle latency, which is acceptable.

By updating registers only when clock enable (derived from an input valid signal or simply always enabled here for simplicity), power is saved.

This approach maintains a clean, parameterized, and scalable design with improved timing and reduced power due to clock gating potential.
