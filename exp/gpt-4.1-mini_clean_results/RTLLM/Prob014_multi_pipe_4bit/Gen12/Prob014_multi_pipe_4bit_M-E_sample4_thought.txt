Implement the 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier using a two-stage accumulation approach with intermediate registers, but instead of summing all partial products at once, accumulate them serially over two pipeline stages to simplify hardware and clearly separate stages:

1. Extend mul_a and mul_b by zero-padding mul_a to 8 bits (2*size).
2. First pipeline stage: For each bit i of mul_b, generate the partial product (mul_a_ext shifted by i) only for one bit per clock cycle, accumulating the partial products in a register over multiple cycles is not required since size=4 and we have two pipeline stages.
3. Use pipeline registers to store partial products corresponding to lower and upper half bits of mul_b.
4. Second pipeline stage: Add partial products from the first pipeline stage registers.
5. Output the final result registered in the last stage.
6. This design uses separate pipeline registers for partial sums to simplify control and supports synchronous reset.
7. This approach achieves pipelining by splitting addition into two stages, with registers after partial product generation and after addition, simplifying timing and enhancing throughput.
