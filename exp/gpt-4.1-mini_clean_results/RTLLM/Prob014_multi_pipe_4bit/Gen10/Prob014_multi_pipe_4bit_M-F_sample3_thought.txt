To address the timing and power issues from the previous design, split the summation of partial products into two pipeline stages, using two levels of registers as required:

- Stage 1: Register the extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs, and generate partial products for bits 0 and 1, summing them and registering the intermediate sum.
- Stage 2: Generate partial products for bits 2 and 3, sum them, add to the registered intermediate sum from stage 1, and register the final product output.

This pipeline breaks the long combinational path of summing all partial products at once, improving timing by distributing addition over two clock cycles.

Also, only generate and register partial products for relevant bits in each stage to reduce switching activity.

This maintains the required two-level pipeline architecture, with clear separation and registers at each stage, and complies with the requirement to extend inputs by "size" zeros on MSB side.

The multiplier latency is 2 cycles: inputs registered and partial products for bits 0-1 combined in stage 1; then partial products for bits 2-3 summed and added to stage 1 sum in stage 2, producing final output.

This approach optimizes the design while adhering strictly to problem specs.
