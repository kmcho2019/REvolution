Refactor the design to use explicit pipeline registers for inputs, partial products, and sums separately, while generating partial products directly from registered inputs outside of always blocks with `assign`. Instead of summing partial products in two groups then final sum, pipeline the addition in two stages with intermediate registers to store partial sums of each stage. Also, explicitly register partial products to maintain pipeline timing and alignment.

Key changes:
- Stage 1: Register extended inputs.
- Stage 2: Register partial products for each bit of the multiplier.
- Stage 3: Sum partial products in pairs combinationally and register the sums.
- Stage 4: Sum the results of Stage 3 combinationally and register the final output.

This creates a clear 3-stage pipeline with two levels of registers storing intermediate values, matching the problem specification. By registering partial products explicitly, timing and data alignment are guaranteed, avoiding combinational feedback. All combinational logic uses `assign` statements outside always blocks.

Reset logic zeroes all registers asynchronously on active-low `rst_n`.

This structured approach ensures the multiplier pipeline stages advance data correctly every clock cycle, with the output valid after three cycles.
