To simplify the 4-bit pipeline multiplier design while maintaining its functionality:

- Keep the parameterized 4-bit width.
- Extend inputs by prepending zeros for consistent width in shifts.
- Generate partial products combinationally for each bit of mul_b by shifting mul_a accordingly.
- Use a first pipeline stage of registers to store each partial product individually (no concatenation).
- Use a second pipeline stage register to store the sum of the registered partial products.
- On reset (active low), all registers and outputs clear to zero.
- On clock edge, register partial products in stage 1, then sum and register the result in stage 2, outputting the final product.
- This keeps two pipeline stages and is easier to understand and synthesize.
- Use asynchronous reset as per original requirement.

This approach reduces complexity by avoiding wide concatenated registers and multiple indexing operations, while keeping a clear two-stage pipeline multiplier.
