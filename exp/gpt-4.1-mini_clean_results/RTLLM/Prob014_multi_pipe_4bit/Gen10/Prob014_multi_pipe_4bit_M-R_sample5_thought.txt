Refactor the two-stage pipelined 4-bit unsigned multiplier by separating partial product generation (combinational) and the two pipeline registers for intermediate values and final sum. 

Key changes:
- Generate all partial products combinationally using a generate block with continuous assignments (`assign`).
- Register all partial products and inputs at stage 1 on clk.
- Sum the registered partial products using a separate combinational adder tree (using a generate block and a reduction loop).
- Register the final sum at stage 2.
- Use only non-blocking assignments in sequential blocks and assign for combinational logic.
- This clean separation reduces the use of arrays in procedural blocks and improves readability.
- Keep the asynchronous active-low reset as requested.

This approach maintains the same two-level pipeline:
- Stage 1: Registers multiplicand, multiplier, and partial products.
- Stage 2: Registers the sum of partial products as the final product output.

Partial products are generated combinationally outside registers to avoid recomputing them in sequential blocks, complying with the requested use of generate blocks and `assign`. This structure keeps a clear data flow and fulfills the problem's extension and pipelining requirements.
