Implement the 4-bit unsigned multiplier using a classic shift-and-add pipelined approach with two pipeline stages. The first stage generates and registers each partial product by ANDing the multiplicand bits with one bit of the multiplier and shifting accordingly. The second stage accumulates the partial products progressively in a register. This design uses one register stage to hold each partial product and another stage to accumulate the sum, fulfilling the two-level pipeline requirement. The multiplicand is extended by appending zeros to the MSB side. Reset logic initializes registers to zero. This approach mimics a hardware multiplier iterative algorithm mapped onto two pipeline stages for efficient partial product generation and accumulation.
