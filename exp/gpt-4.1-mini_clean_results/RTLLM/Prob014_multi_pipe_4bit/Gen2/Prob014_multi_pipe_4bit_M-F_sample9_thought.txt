The main issue in the original design is that the final product output (`mul_out`) is assigned in the same always block and clock cycle as the computation of the final sum (`lvl2_sum`), but it captures the *previous* value of `lvl2_sum` due to non-blocking assignment semantics.

To fix this, I will separate the final summation stage and output register stage into two distinct pipeline stages. This creates a proper 4-stage pipeline:

1. **Stage 0:** Generate partial products combinationally from inputs.

2. **Stage 1:** Register partial products.

3. **Stage 2:** Sum partial products in pairs and register the sums.

4. **Stage 3:** Sum the results of Stage 2 and register into an intermediate register.

5. **Stage 4:** Register the final product output (`mul_out`).

This fixes the timing issue by ensuring `mul_out` is always updated from a stable registered sum at the next clock cycle, eliminating latency mismatches.

Additional improvements:

- I split the final addition and output register into two always blocks with proper registers.

- Keep synchronous active-low reset for all pipeline registers and output.

- Maintain generality and readability for size=4 (default).

This explicit 4-stage pipeline cleanly stages the additions and provides correct timing for `mul_out`.
