To fix the pipeline timing and data dependency issues, we need to strictly separate the pipeline stages by registering all intermediate values before using them in subsequent stages.

Stage 0 (Input registration):  
- Register extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs.

Stage 1 (Partial product generation and first level sum):  
- Using the registered inputs from stage 0, combinationally generate partial products, then sum partial products in pairs (0+1 and 2+3), and register these sums into stage 1 registers.

Stage 2 (Final sum and output):  
- Register the sum of stage 1 sums as final product output.

This ensures that at each clock edge, the pipeline progresses with valid and stable data, and combinational logic only works on stable registered inputs.

Also, inputs are extended by prepending zeros on MSB side as requested.

In detail:  
- Declare stage0 registers for extended multiplicand (mul_a_reg) and multiplier (mul_b_reg).  
- In stage 1, generate partial products combinationally from stage0 registers and sum them into two sums (sum_stage1_0 and sum_stage1_1), which are then registered into stage1 registers.  
- Stage 2 sums the two stage1 sums and registers the result as the output (mul_out).

This approach correctly pipelines the multiplier in two levels, with clear stage boundaries and valid data at each clock edge, fully addressing the feedback.
