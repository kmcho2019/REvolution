I will implement a fully pipelined 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages of registers for intermediate partial product sums.

Architectural idea:
- Inputs mul_a and mul_b (4 bits) are zero-extended to 8 bits by prefixing 4 zeros at MSB.
- Partial products are generated for each bit of mul_b, each a shifted version of ext_mul_a or zero.
- The first pipeline stage registers store the partial product corresponding to bits [0,1] summed, and bits [2,3] summed separately.
- The second pipeline stage register adds the two sums from the first stage registers to produce the final output.
- This approach divides partial products into two groups, sums within each group combinationally, registers results (stage 1), then adds these registers and registers again (stage 2).
- The pipeline allows continuous multiplication every clock cycle after pipeline latency.
- Reset is asynchronous active low and clears pipeline registers and output.

This design meets all requirements, uses two levels of registers, avoids complex summation loops, and cleanly separates partial product summation stages. The code is clear, simple, and synthesizable.
