Simplify the 4-bit unsigned multiplier pipeline by:

1. Extending inputs by concatenating size zeros at the MSBs outside the registers (combinationally), then registering these extended inputs as stage 1 registers.

2. Generating partial products combinationally from the registered inputs, no individual registers per partial product—partial products are summed combinationally.

3. Registering the final sum of partial products as the single stage 2 pipeline register.

This keeps exactly two levels of registers: 
- Level 1: extended inputs registered 
- Level 2: final product registered

Avoid multiple always blocks and arrays for partial products. This approach reduces complexity and still pipelines multiplication into two stages.
