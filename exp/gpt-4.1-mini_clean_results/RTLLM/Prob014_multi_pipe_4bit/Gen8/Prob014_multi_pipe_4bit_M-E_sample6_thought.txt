Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline using the classic shift-and-add multiplication approach distributed over two register stages:

- Stage 1 registers: On the first clock, decode and store partial products corresponding to each bit of multiplier `mul_b`:
  For bit i of `mul_b`, partial product = multiplicand shifted left by i positions if bit i is 1, else zero.
  All these partial products are stored in an array of registers (one per bit).

- Stage 2 registers: On the next clock, sum the partial products pairwise in two steps:
  First sum the lower half partial products, then the upper half partial products in parallel registers.
  Finally, add these two sums together and register the final product output.

This architecture pipelines the multiplication over two register levels:
1. Stage 1 registers hold the partial products.
2. Stage 2 registers hold the final sum of all partial products (the product).

This approach fully pipelines the multiplication by storing intermediate partial products explicitly and then accumulating them in the second pipeline stage, meeting the requirement to have two levels of registers controlling the multiplication process.
