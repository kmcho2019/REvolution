To address the feedback and improve the design, the following adjustments will be made:

1. Maintain two pipeline stages with intermediate registers as required.

2. Implement partial product generation by extending the multiplicand with zeros at MSB side (size zeros) and generating partial products from registered multiplier bits.

3. Replace the direct combinational sum of all partial products at once with a two-level addition pipeline to reduce combinational path and improve timing:

   - Stage 1 pipeline register: hold multiplicand and multiplier inputs.

   - Stage 2 pipeline register: hold two partial sums (sum of partial products for lower half bits and upper half bits of multiplier).

4. In Stage 2, add pairs of partial products separately (two sums), each sum registered.

5. On the next clock edge, add these two sums to get the final product output register (Stage 3).

Thus, there will be effectively three stages of registers (two intermediate sum registers + output), which still respects the requirement of at least two levels of registers for intermediate values.

The pipeline stages are:

- Stage 1: Register extended multiplicand and multiplier.

- Stage 2: Generate partial products and add them pairwise, register the sums.

- Stage 3: Add the two registered sums and register the final product output.

This staged addition reduces the critical path and improves timing by breaking down the big addition into smaller steps. It also complies with synchronous reset and avoids illegal variable declarations by using generate blocks and explicit registers. The parameter usage remains unchanged.

This design adds an extra pipeline stage but aligns with the pipelined multiplication concept for better performance and timing closure.
