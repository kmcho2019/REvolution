Simplify the 4-bit pipeline multiplier by using two levels of registers:

1. Stage 1: Register zero-extended inputs (mul_a extended with size zeros at MSB, mul_b as is).
2. Stage 2: Generate all partial products combinationally from the registered inputs, sum them combinationally, then register the final product output.

This approach keeps two levels of registers as required: input registers and output registers. The intermediate partial products are generated combinationally within the cycle between the two register levels, avoiding multiple registers for partial products to reduce complexity.

Reset (rst_n) clears the input registers and output register asynchronously.

This meets the requirement of two pipeline stages (inputs and outputs) storing intermediate values and controlling multiplication, while simplifying the design by removing per-bit partial product registers.
