Implement a 4-bit pipeline multiplier using two levels of registers to store intermediate sums. Extend inputs by zero-padding on the MSB side. Generate partial products by shifting multiplicand based on multiplier bits. On each clock edge, update the first level register with sum of partial products from lower half bits, and the second level register with sum from upper half bits. Finally, combine the two registers to output the full product. Use synchronous reset (active-low) to clear registers and output.
