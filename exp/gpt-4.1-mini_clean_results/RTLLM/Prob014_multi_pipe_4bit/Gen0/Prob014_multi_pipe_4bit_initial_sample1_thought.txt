The design is a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by adding size zeros to their MSB side. Partial products are generated using a generate block by checking each bit of mul_b, and shifted mul_a accordingly. Two levels of registers store intermediate sums: the first level sums partial products 0 and 1, the second sums partial products 2 and 3. Finally, on the second clock cycle, these intermediate sums are added to produce the final output. Reset logic clears all registers and outputs when rst_n is low.
