Design a 4-bit unsigned multiplier pipeline with two levels of registers to store intermediate sums. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are each extended by 'size' zeros on the MSB side (i.e. to 8 bits) before partial product calculation. Each partial product is generated by checking the bits of mul_b; if bit i is 1, partial product is mul_a shifted by i, else 0. In the first clock cycle, sum the first half partial products into first-level register. In the second clock cycle, sum the second half partial products into second-level register. On the next clock, sum these two registers to get final product in output register. Reset clears registers and output. Use positive edge clk and negative edge rst_n for synchronous reset.
