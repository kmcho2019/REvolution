Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier using a two-stage pipeline of registers. Extend the inputs by padding zeros at the MSB side to double the width, generate partial products via a generate block conditioned on each bit of mul_b, then sum these partial products in two pipeline registers. On each clock edge, registers store intermediate sums; on reset, they clear. Finally, output the sum of these registers as the final product. The module will handle synchronous reset and data flow with pipeline registers, ensuring the multiplication operation is spread across two clock cycles.
