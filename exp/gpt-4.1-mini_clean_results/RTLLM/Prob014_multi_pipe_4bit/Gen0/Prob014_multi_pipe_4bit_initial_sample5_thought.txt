Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier using a pipelined approach with two levels of registers. First, generate partial products by shifting the multiplicand based on each bit of the multiplier. Store these partial products in a first-level register stage on the clock edge. Then, sum these partial products in two stages of registers (pipeline levels). The first register stage stores partial products, the second register stage stores intermediate sums of partial products. Finally, the output register stage sums the values from the second stage to produce the final product output. Use reset logic to clear registers when rst_n is low. This pipeline reduces the critical path and allows timing improvements in clocked operation.
