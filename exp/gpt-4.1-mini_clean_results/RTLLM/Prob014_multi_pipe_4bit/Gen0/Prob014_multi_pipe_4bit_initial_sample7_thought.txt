Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier using pipelined partial product addition. Extend both multiplicand and multiplier to 8 bits by prepending 4 zeros to the MSBs. Generate partial products for each multiplier bit: if mul_b[i] is 1, partial product is mul_a shifted left by i; otherwise 0. Use two pipeline registers to accumulate partial sums in two stages. At clk posedge or rst_n negedge, reset registers if rst_n is low, otherwise update intermediate sums with partial products sums. Finally, output the product as sum of the two pipeline registers. This breaks addition into two stages, distributing logic over two clock cycles, implementing a 2-stage pipeline.
