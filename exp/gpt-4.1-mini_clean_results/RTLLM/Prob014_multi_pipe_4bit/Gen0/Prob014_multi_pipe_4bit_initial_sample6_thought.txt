The design is a 4-bit unsigned multiplier using a pipeline with two levels of registers. The multiplicand and multiplier inputs are zero-extended to 8 bits by adding 4 zeros at the MSB side (size = 4). Partial products are generated for each bit of the multiplier using a generate block. Each partial product is either the multiplicand shifted left by the bit position if the bit is 1, or zero otherwise. The partial products are summed in two stages: first, the lower half of partial products are summed and stored in the first register stage, the upper half are summed and stored in the second register stage. Then these two sums are added in the final stage to produce the output product. The registers are clocked synchronously with asynchronous active low reset. On reset, all registers and output are cleared to zero. This pipeline structure improves throughput by splitting the addition of partial products across two clock cycles.
