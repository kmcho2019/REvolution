The design extends the 4-bit inputs by prepending zeros of width "size" (4 bits), making them effectively 8 bits wide. It uses generate blocks to create partial products for each bit of the multiplier: if mul_b[i] is 1, the multiplicand shifted left by i positions is assigned; otherwise zero. These partial products are added in two pipeline stages with registers to hold intermediate sums. On the first clock edge (if not in reset), partial products for bits 0-1 and 2-3 are summed separately and stored in two registers (stage 1). On the second clock edge, those two sums are added and stored in another register (stage 2). On the third clock edge, the final product register is updated with the sum from stage 2. Reset asynchronously clears all pipeline registers and output. This creates a three-stage pipeline: partial product generation, intermediate addition, and final addition, meeting the requirement of two levels of registers for pipelining.
