Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages. First, extend both inputs by "size" zero bits at the MSB side to have 8-bit width. Generate partial products for each multiplier bit by left-shifting the multiplicand or zeroing based on multiplier bits. In the first pipeline stage (registers), accumulate half of the partial products. In the second pipeline stage (registers), accumulate the remaining partial products. Finally, output the combined sum as the product. Use synchronous reset and clock for register updates. This divides the addition into two levels of pipelined registers for intermediate values and control.
