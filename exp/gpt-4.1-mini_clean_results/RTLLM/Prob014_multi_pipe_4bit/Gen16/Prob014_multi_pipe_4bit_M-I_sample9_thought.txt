Improvement Strategy:

The given solution implements a two-stage pipelined 4-bit multiplier by generating partial products combinationally and summing them before passing through two levels of registers. While functionally correct, the single large combinational sum of all partial products may cause synthesis and timing issues due to the wide adder tree.

To improve:

1. Split the summation of partial products into two pipeline stages with registers after partial product generation, thereby reducing combinational path length and improving synthesis results.

2. First pipeline stage:
   - Generate partial products for bits 0 and 1, sum them, and register the result.
   - Generate partial products for bits 2 and 3, sum them, and register the result.

3. Second pipeline stage:
   - Add the two registered sums from stage 1 and register the final product.

4. Maintain the zero-extension of the multiplicand at the MSB side as before.

5. This approach aligns better with a two-level pipelined design and reduces long combinational adders.

6. Reset remains asynchronous active-low for all registers.

7. Keep the `size` parameter and width definitions consistent.

This approach improves synthesizability and timing by splitting the additions into smaller parts registered at the clock edges, minimizing the critical path and better matching the intended pipelined structure.
