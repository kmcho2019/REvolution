Refactor the pipelined multiplier by using a generate block to create partial products combinationally, each registered at stage 1. Then add these registered partial products via an explicit adder tree split into two pipeline stages, with intermediate sums stored in stage 2 registers before the final sum. This replaces the single combinational sum with a balanced adder tree and two levels of registers to better pipeline the addition, improving timing and clarity. The multiplicand is still extended by prepending zeros, and the entire process is clocked synchronously with asynchronous active-low reset.
