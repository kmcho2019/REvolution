Improvement strategy:
- Maintain the two pipeline stages as requested.
- Instead of storing all partial products individually in stage 1 registers, use a single register to accumulate partial products sequentially over multiple clock cycles to reduce area.
- Use an internal control counter (3-bit) to step through bits of mul_b, adding partial products one by one into an accumulator register in stage 1.
- On completion of processing all bits, transfer the accumulated result to stage 2 register.
- This converts a combinational large adder summing all partial products at once into an iterative addition, reducing critical path and area.
- It effectively creates a pipelined multiplier that uses one addition per clock cycle, spreading multiplication over size cycles.
- To keep pipeline depth two, stage 1 will accumulate partial sums, stage 2 will latch the final product.
- During reset, clear registers and counter.
- Extend mul_a by size zeros at MSB as specified, generate shifted multiplicand for each bit dynamically.
- This approach greatly reduces combinational logic per clock cycle, improves timing and area at a cost of latency = size+1 cycles, which is typical for pipeline multipliers.
