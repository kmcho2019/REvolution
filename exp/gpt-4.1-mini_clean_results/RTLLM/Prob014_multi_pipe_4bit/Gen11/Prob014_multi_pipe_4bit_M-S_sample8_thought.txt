Simplify the 4-bit pipelined multiplier by avoiding arrays of registers and complex generate blocks.

- Extend inputs by zero-padding the multiplicand to 8 bits.
- Generate partial products combinationally for each bit of the multiplier in separate wires without arrays.
- Register partial products in the first pipeline stage using individual registers.
- Add partial products pairwise combinationally.
- Register the sum of the pairs as the second pipeline stage and output the final product.
- Use asynchronous active-low reset as specified.
- This approach maintains two pipeline register levels and matches the requirements with cleaner and simpler code.
