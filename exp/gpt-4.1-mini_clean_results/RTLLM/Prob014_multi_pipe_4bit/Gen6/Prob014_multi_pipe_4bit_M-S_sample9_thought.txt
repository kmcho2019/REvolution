Simplify the 4-bit pipelined multiplier by storing only two pipeline register levels:

1. Level 1 registers: register extended inputs (extended multiplicand and multiplier).

2. Level 2 registers: register the final product output.

Generate partial products combinationally from the registered inputs on each clock cycle, sum them immediately, and register the sum as the final output.

This removes the need to register each partial product separately, reducing complexity and resource usage while maintaining two levels of registers: input registration and output registration.

Reset asynchronously clears registers.

This solution still pipelines the multiplication (partial products are computed from registered inputs, then output is registered in next clock cycle), fulfilling the two-level register requirement, simplifying the design, improving synthesis friendliness, and maintaining correct operation.
