Implement the 4-bit unsigned multiplier as a fully pipelined shift-and-add multiplier with two pipeline stages for intermediate results:

1. Inputs are extended by `size` zeros at MSB as requested, though this does not affect the core algorithm.

2. The multiplier iterates over each bit of the multiplier (mul_b) across pipeline stages by maintaining a pipeline counter and partial sum registers.

3. At stage 1: On the first clock cycle, registers hold the multiplicand (extended), multiplier (extended), a partial product sum (initialized to 0), and the current bit index (starting at 0).

4. At stage 2: On the next clock cycle, if the current multiplier bit is 1, the multiplicand shifted left by the bit index is added to the partial sum.

5. The pipeline registers hold these intermediate sums and indices. After processing all bits (size cycles), the final product is latched into `mul_out`.

6. Two levels of registers correspond to:
   - Level 1: Registers holding multiplicand, multiplier, partial sum, and bit index.
   - Level 2: Registers holding the updated partial sum after addition and incremented bit index.

7. By shifting the bit index through pipeline stages, the design achieves pipelined multiplication over multiple cycles, using two register stages per bit processed.

8. Reset clears all registers and output.

This pipeline performs the multiplication serially in a pipelined manner but uses two levels of registers as requested, staging the data and partial sum updates. The latency will be `size + 1` cycles due to the pipeline.

This approach differs from the previous tree addition style by employing a shift-and-add sequential pipeline style with two registers stages per cycle.
