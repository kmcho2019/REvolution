Implement a 2-stage pipeline multiplier with clean separation of stages and stable data flow.

Stage 1:
- Register inputs (mul_a and mul_b).
- Generate partial products combinationally from the registered inputs.
- Store partial products in an array of registers.

Stage 2:
- Sum all partial products stored in stage 1 registers.
- Register the final sum as output.

Key points:
- Extend mul_a and mul_b by adding 'size' zeros at MSB side before partial product generation.
- Partial products are generated as: if mul_b[i] == 1, then partial product = (extended mul_a) << i; else 0.
- Use a generate block for partial products.
- Use two always blocks: one for stage 1 registers and another for stage 2 registers and output.
- Both blocks sensitive to clk and rst_n (active low reset).
- On reset, clear all registers to zero.
- Pipeline latency is two clock cycles: input registration -> partial product registration -> sum registration -> output.
- This structure ensures stable pipelining and correct synchronization of data.

This approach clearly separates combinational and sequential logic and avoids data dependency issues observed in the previous code.
