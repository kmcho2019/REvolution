To simplify the 4-bit pipelined multiplier with two levels of registers, we will:

- Avoid unnecessary zero extension of inputs.
- Generate partial products directly by shifting the multiplicand based on each multiplier bit.
- Use two pipeline stages: 
  - Stage 1 registers hold sums of partial products for bits 0 and 1.
  - Stage 2 registers hold sums of partial products for bits 2 and 3.
- On the next clock cycle, add these two stage sums to produce the final product output.
- This creates a two-level pipeline where the partial sums are registered first, then combined and registered again.
- Synchronous reset clears all registers.
- This approach preserves the pipeline structure, reduces complexity, and aligns with the problem requirements.

The partial products are combinationally generated each cycle, then their sums are registered over two stages, with the output registered as the final product.
