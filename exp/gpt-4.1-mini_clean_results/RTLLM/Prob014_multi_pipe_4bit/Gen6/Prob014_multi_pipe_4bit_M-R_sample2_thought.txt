Refactor the pipeline multiplier by explicitly registering inputs first, then generating partial products combinationally from registered inputs, followed by two pipeline stages of registers:

1. First stage registers the inputs (mul_a and mul_b extended with zeros).

2. Use combinational logic with a generate block to compute partial products from registered inputs.

3. Second stage pipeline registers sum of partial products.

4. Third stage pipeline registers the final product output.

5. Reset clears all pipeline registers and output.

This structure uses three pipeline levels explicitly: inputs, partial products, sum of partial products, and output register, ensuring clean pipeline stages with clear data flow and no implicit combinational path in always block summations.

This also avoids register arrays by using separate registers, making the design clearer and synthesizable.

Using non-blocking assignments inside sequential blocks for all registers and combinational assign for partial products.

Latency increases by one cycle (3 cycles total) but pipeline stages are clearly defined, improving timing and modularity.
