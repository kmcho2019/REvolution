Simplify the design by directly summing all partial products in two pipeline stages without pairing explicitly. In the first stage, sum all partial products except the last one into a register; in the second stage, add this register with the last partial product to produce the final product. This approach keeps the pipeline depth at two stages, reduces the number of intermediate registers, and maintains the synchronous reset and clocking as required. The multiplicand is zero-extended as before, and partial products are generated using a generate block conditioned on multiplier bits.
