Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-level pipeline using partial product accumulation, but adopt a different approach:

- Instead of generating all partial products at once and then summing, use an iterative accumulation approach over two pipeline stages.

- Stage 1 registers each partial product computed for one multiplier bit at a time.

- Stage 2 accumulates the partial products progressively in an intermediate register.

- Use a counter to indicate which bit of mul_b is being processed each cycle; after processing all bits (4 cycles), output the final product.

- On reset, clear all registers and counter.

- Each clock cycle processes one bit of the multiplier:
  - Generate partial product: if mul_b[counter] is 1, shift mul_a by counter and register it.
  - Add partial product to accumulated sum registered in stage 2.

- After 4 cycles, mul_out is updated with the accumulated sum.

This approach implements a simple pipeline with two registers: one holding the current partial product, the other holding the accumulated sum, mimicking a multiply-accumulate pipeline over multiple cycles, fitting the problem's requirement of two levels of registers for intermediate values.

Also, zero-extension is not needed for mul_a; shifting takes care of alignment. The data widths are carefully assigned.

This design is easy to verify and naturally handles the pipeline and control process with a minimal number of registers.
