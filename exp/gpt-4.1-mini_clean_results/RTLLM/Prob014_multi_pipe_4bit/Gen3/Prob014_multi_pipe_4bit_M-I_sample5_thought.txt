To strictly follow the problem specification and properly pipeline the 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two levels of registers, we should:

1. Extend multiplicand by adding `size` zeros at the MSB side.

2. Use a generate block to produce partial products (each either shifted `a_ext` if mul_b[i] = 1, or 0 otherwise). These partial products will be registered at pipeline stage 1.

3. Then, at pipeline stage 2, register the sum of all partial products.

4. Finally, output the registered sum as mul_out.

This approach respects the requirement of having two pipeline levels of registers: first storing partial products, then storing the accumulated sum.

The summation of partial products will be done combinationally (in a generate block) before the second pipeline register stage, because the problem suggests addition of partial products at clock edge updating intermediate sum registers, i.e., at the second register level.

We avoid summing partial products sequentially over multiple clock cycles to keep to two pipeline stages (as requested). This also avoids combinational loops or complex adders spread across cycles.

Using generate block for partial products improves readability and synthesis friendliness.

The reset is synchronous active-low and clears all registers.

This design ensures clear pipeline stages and is synthesizable with good timing, adhering closely to the problem description.

The output register stage ensures the product is stable and synchronized.

Parameter `size` defaults to 4, ensuring flexible parameterization.

This balances correctness, clarity, and PPA (power, performance, area).
