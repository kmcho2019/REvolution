I will design a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier using the shift-and-add technique with two pipeline register stages.

Design approach:
1. Extend both inputs `mul_a` and `mul_b` by concatenating `size` zeros at their MSBs to align widths to `2*size`.
2. Use a shift-and-add multiplication algorithm split into two pipeline stages:
   - Stage 1: Compute partial products for the lower half bits of the multiplier (bits 0 and 1), and sum them, register the result.
   - Stage 2: Compute partial products for the upper half bits of the multiplier (bits 2 and 3), sum them, then add to the registered stage 1 result to get the final product.
3. The pipeline registers store intermediate sums after each stage, enabling clocked operation and easier timing closure.
4. All partial products are generated by conditionally shifting the multiplicand `mul_a` by the bit index times if that bit in `mul_b` is set, otherwise zero.
5. On reset, registers and output are cleared.
6. This architecture uses addition of grouped partial products in two stages, reducing combinational delay per stage and ensuring pipeline operation.
7. The design is fully parameterized by `size`, default 4, and the output width is `2*size`.
