To address the feedback and improve synthesis compatibility, I will:

- Avoid using arrays of registers for the partial products stage 1 pipeline registers. Instead, I will use a single concatenated register vector to hold all partial products, with each partial product stored contiguously.
- This reduces potential synthesis issues related to multidimensional registers.
- Keep the combinational partial product generation as is, as it is standard and clear.
- Perform the summation combinationally by adding the stage 1 registered partial products, then register the final sum in stage 2.
- Maintain the two-level pipeline register structure.
- Ensure all registers clear to zero on active-low reset.
- The parameter 'size' remains properly declared and used.
  
This design improves synthesizability and maintains the required pipeline structure and behavior.

The latency remains two cycles: one cycle to register partial products, one cycle to register sum and output the product.
