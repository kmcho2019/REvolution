Implement a 2-stage pipelined 4-bit unsigned multiplier using shift-add approach with intermediate registers:

- Stage 1: Register the extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs.
- Stage 2: Perform the accumulation of partial products step-by-step across pipeline registers.
- Instead of generating all partial products combinationally upfront, process one multiplier bit at a time in the pipeline.

Pipeline architecture:
- Input stage registers the extended multiplicand (mul_a extended by 'size' zeros on MSB side) and the multiplier.
- The second stage processes the multiplier bits one per cycle across internal pipeline registers:
  * Use a shift register for multiplier bits.
  * Use an accumulator register to hold partial sum.
  * At each clock cycle, multiply the LSB of the multiplier register by the extended multiplicand shifted by the current bit position, then add to accumulator.
- Since the problem requires 2 levels of registers and the multiplier is 4-bit, the design completes multiplication in 2 cycles:
  * In the first cycle: stage 1 registers inputs.
  * In the second cycle: stage 2 sums all partial products by shifting and adding (unrolled combinationally) and registers the final result.

To respect problem requirements:
- Inputs extended by 'size' zero bits at MSB side.
- Generate partial products per bit (mul_b[i]) shifted by i.
- Register the partial products in stage 1.
- Sum partial products combinationally in stage 2 and register output.
- Use only packed arrays and avoid passing unpacked arrays to functions.
- Use generate block for partial products.
- Final multiplication latency is 2 clock cycles.

This approach avoids function array input problems by summing partial products combinationally with a loop in a single always_comb block, storing partial products in a packed vector array, and registers partial products and the sum at each pipeline stage.
