Simplify the multiplier by directly generating partial products and summing them using two pipeline register levels:

- Stage 1: Register extended multiplicand and multiplier inputs.
- Stage 2: Compute partial products for each bit of the registered multiplier combinationally, sum them up, and register the intermediate sum in an intermediate register.
- Stage 3: Register the final output product as the sum of intermediate sums.

This meets the two-level register requirement by splitting the summation into two register stages:
1) Input registers storing extended inputs.
2) Output registers storing final product.

The intermediate sums are combinational in-between, which simplifies the design and limits combinational delay, but still stores intermediate values in registers.

Use simple shift and conditional generation of partial products and asynchronous reset to zero the registers.
