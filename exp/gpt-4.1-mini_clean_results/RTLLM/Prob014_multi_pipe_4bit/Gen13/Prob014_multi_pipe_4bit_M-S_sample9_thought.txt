Simplify the 4-bit pipeline multiplier by accumulating partial products across two pipeline stages without storing all partial products individually.

- Zero-extend multiplicand by size bits to 2*size width.
- In the first stage, generate partial products for each bit of multiplier and sum them sequentially inside an always block using a loop.
- Register this intermediate sum (first pipeline stage).
- In the second stage, register the final product (second pipeline stage).
- Reset registers asynchronously with active-low reset.
This approach reduces register array usage and avoids multiple partial product registers, simplifying code while maintaining two-level pipelining.
