To correct and improve the given pipelined 4-bit multiplier:

1. Remove illegal `reg` declarations inside the always block by moving intermediate signals to module-level regs or wires.

2. Strictly separate pipeline stages:
   - Stage 1 registers hold the partial products.
   - Stage 2 registers hold the sums of partial products.
   
3. Perform partial product generation combinationally with a generate block.

4. Use stage 1 registers to store partial products on the clock edge.

5. Sum pairs of partial products combinationally from stage 1 registers, then register those sums in stage 2 registers.

6. On the next clock, add the stage 2 sums together and register the final output.

7. Implement synchronous reset (active-low) to clear stage 1 and stage 2 registers and output.

8. This provides a true two-level pipeline:
   - Level 1: Partial products registered
   - Level 2: Summation registered
   - Final output after second pipeline stage

9. This approach improves timing by balancing logic across two pipeline registers, avoiding large combinational additions in one cycle, and adheres strictly to the problem requirements.

10. The design is parameterized, cleanly structured, and synthesizable.
