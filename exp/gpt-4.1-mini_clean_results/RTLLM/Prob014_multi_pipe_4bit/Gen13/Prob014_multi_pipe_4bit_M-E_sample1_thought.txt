Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages as follows:
- Stage 1 registers: generate and register all partial products of the multiplicand shifted by each multiplier bit, but extended to 2*size bits.
- Stage 2 registers: add partial products in pairs in the first half of the clock cycle, storing intermediate sums in registers, then in the next clock cycle add remaining pairs, finally producing the full sum.
- To align with two-stage pipeline requirement, arrange the partial products and their addition in a balanced binary tree manner over two register levels:
  * Stage 1 registers hold partial products.
  * Stage 2 registers hold sums of partial products pairs.
- Final product output is the sum of stage 2 registered pairs.
- Use a generate block to create partial products combinationally.
- Use synchronous reset and clock.
This approach balances the logic over two pipeline stages and avoids multiple additions in one clock edge.
