Simplify the pipelined multiplier by implementing two pipeline stages directly:
- Stage 1: On clock, register the partial products for each multiplier bit.
- Stage 2: On next clock, sum all registered partial products and output the final product.
Avoid sequential accumulation and internal FSM, instead do the summation combinationally in the second stage.
Use separate loop indices for generate and procedural loops to avoid naming conflicts.
This reduces latency to two cycles and simplifies control logic while maintaining pipelined operation.
