Simplify the 4-bit pipeline multiplier by directly calculating partial products and accumulating them over two pipeline stages without separate arrays.

- Extend multiplicand by prepending zeros.
- Generate partial products inside the clocked process based on mul_b bits.
- Store partial products in first stage registers.
- Sum the partial products registered in stage one in the second stage register.
- Use only two pipeline stages: first registers partial products, second registers final output.
- Reset logic asynchronously with active-low reset.
This approach avoids arrays and generate loops, reduces code size, while maintaining two-level pipelining.
