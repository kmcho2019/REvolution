The original solution leverages two's complement signed addition directly, which is the most efficient and standard method for fixed-point addition. This approach inherently covers absolute value addition and subtraction cases without explicit sign checking or conditional logic, simplifying the design and minimizing hardware resources.

The improvement here focuses on:
- Matching the problem's requirement of an internal register `res` storing the result before assigning to output `c`.
- Making the inputs and outputs explicitly signed and of parameterized widths.
- Ensuring the code is clean, synthesizable, and purely combinational.
- Adding comments for clarity.

No explicit absolute value or conditional logic is needed due to two's complement representation.
This approach provides the best PPA: minimal logic, minimal latency, and power efficient.

Thus, the refined design will include:
- Parameterized module.
- Signed inputs/outputs.
- Internal `res` register assigned combinationally.
- Direct addition of signed numbers.
- Output `c` driven by `res`.

This satisfies all given implementation points while adhering to hardware synthesis best practices.
