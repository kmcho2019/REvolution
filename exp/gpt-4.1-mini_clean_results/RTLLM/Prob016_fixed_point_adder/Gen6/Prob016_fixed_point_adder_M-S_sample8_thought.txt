Implement a simplified fixed-point adder that operates directly on two's complement signed inputs without explicit sign-magnitude conversion. Since inputs are in two's complement fixed-point format, adding or subtracting them as signed numbers preserves correct fixed-point addition semantics.

The module will add inputs directly, then saturate or truncate overflow by taking only the lower N bits. Since inputs and outputs are fixed width two's complement values with Q fractional bits, normal integer addition suffices for fixed-point addition.

We use a signed intermediate sum of width N+1 to detect overflow if needed. To keep it simple, we assign the lower N bits as the output. The internal register `res` holds this result combinationally.

This greatly simplifies the design by leveraging Verilog's signed arithmetic and avoids converting between sign-magnitude and two's complement formats, fulfilling the fixed-point addition requirements with clean, concise logic.
