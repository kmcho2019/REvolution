Refactor the fixed-point adder module using a combinational always block with internal registers for intermediate signals to improve clarity and better align with conventional Verilog design styles that emphasize procedural assignments for complex conditional logic. This approach groups the decision-making and arithmetic operations in one always_comb block, making it easier to follow the logical flow and to maintain. The core logic stays the same: compute magnitudes, decide operation based on sign bits, select sum or difference, set the sign of the result accordingly, and output the final two's complement result. This style also allows easier extension for overflow flags or other status signals if needed later. Parameters retain their defaults, and the module remains fully parameterized.
