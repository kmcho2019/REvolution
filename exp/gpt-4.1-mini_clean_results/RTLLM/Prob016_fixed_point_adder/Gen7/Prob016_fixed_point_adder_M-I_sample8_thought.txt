The original solution correctly implements signed fixed-point addition with overflow detection and saturation using two's complement arithmetic. However, it uses two separate always blocks, which is unnecessary and can be merged for cleaner synthesis and reduced logic.

Also, the original saturation constants are correctly defined but could be made more explicit with parameterized constants to increase clarity.

The parameter `Q` (fractional bits) is not used internally but documented to clarify the fixed-point format.

To optimize for PPA (Power, Performance, Area):

- Merge the two always blocks into one combinational block.
- Make the saturation constants `localparam` to adapt automatically for any `N`.
- Add comments clarifying the fixed-point nature, emphasizing that arithmetic is two's complement signed and that `Q` defines the fraction bits (no internal special handling needed).
- Use the signed addition result directly without extension to extra bit but with addition of an overflow bit using simple XOR of carry signals or the standard overflow condition for signed addition.

This approach simplifies the design and keeps it fully synthesizable and efficient, while maintaining the required functionality and parameterization.

The module name, port names, and parameters remain as specified.
