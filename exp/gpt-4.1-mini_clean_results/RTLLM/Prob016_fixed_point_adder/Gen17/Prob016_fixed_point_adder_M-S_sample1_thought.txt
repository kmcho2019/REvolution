Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder handling two's complement inputs with total width N and Q fractional bits. The addition logic:

- Extract sign bits and magnitudes (absolute values) from inputs.
- If signs match, add magnitudes and keep sign.
- If signs differ, subtract smaller magnitude from larger and assign sign of larger magnitude operand.
- If magnitudes equal and signs differ, result zero.
- Assemble result as sign bit + magnitude.
- Preserve full precision (Q fractional bits) by operating at full width.
- Overflow beyond N bits is truncated by slicing.
- Keep code simple and clear without extra functions or intermediate signals beyond necessary.

This simplified design uses direct arithmetic and concise conditional statements inside a combinational always block.
