Simplify the fixed-point adder by directly performing signed addition on the inputs treated as signed fixed-point numbers. Since the inputs and outputs have the same bit width and fractional bits, the fixed-point format naturally aligns. Overflow detection can be done by checking if the sign of the sum differs from the inputs when their signs are equal. If overflow occurs, saturate to the maximum or minimum representable value. This avoids separate absolute value computations and sign manipulations while ensuring correct fixed-point addition with saturation.
