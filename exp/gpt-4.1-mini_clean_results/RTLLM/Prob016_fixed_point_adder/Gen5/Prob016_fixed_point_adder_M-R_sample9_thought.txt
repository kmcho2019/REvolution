Refactor the fixed-point adder to use purely combinational continuous assignments (`assign`) instead of procedural `always` blocks for both the result computation and output assignment. This approach utilizes intermediate wires to hold partial calculations and saturation logic, improving clarity by separating overflow detection, saturation, and final output assignment.
