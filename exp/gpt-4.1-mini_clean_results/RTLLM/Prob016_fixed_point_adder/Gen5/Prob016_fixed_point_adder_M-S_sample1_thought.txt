Simplify the fixed-point adder by performing a direct signed addition of the two N-bit fixed-point inputs, since fixed-point numbers in two's complement can be added directly without manual sign or absolute value handling. Overflow can be detected by checking if the sign bits of inputs are the same but differ from the sign bit of the sum. Upon overflow, saturate the result to the maximum or minimum representable fixed-point value. This approach removes unnecessary sign and magnitude extraction and uses straightforward two's complement arithmetic, preserving fixed-point precision automatically.
