Simplify the fixed-point adder by directly performing signed addition using Verilog's signed arithmetic, leveraging parameterization for bit widths. Since the inputs are two's complement fixed-point numbers with N bits (including sign) and Q fractional bits, adding them as signed values directly yields the correct fixed-point sum.

To handle the different sign cases (same or different signs), we rely on signed addition semantics, avoiding manual absolute value calculations. However, to meet the requirement for internal `res` register holding the result, use a combinational always block to assign the sum.

Overflow is naturally wrapped due to fixed bit width, which is standard in fixed-point operations; explicit saturation is omitted for simplicity. This approach minimizes logic, reduces complexity, and maintains precise fixed-point addition semantics.

- Use signed arithmetic.
- Use an internal register `res` for the result.
