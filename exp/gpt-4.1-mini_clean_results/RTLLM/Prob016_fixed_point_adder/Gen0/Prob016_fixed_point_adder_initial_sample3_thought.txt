Implement a fixed-point adder for signed Q.N fixed-point numbers represented in two's complement with N total bits and Q fractional bits.
The inputs a and b are N-bit signed fixed-point numbers.
If their signs match, sum their magnitudes by adding the two's complement numbers directly; the sign bit remains the same.
If their signs differ, perform magnitude comparison of absolute values, subtract smaller magnitude from larger one, and assign the sign of the operand with larger absolute value.
The internal register res stores the N-bit result of addition or subtraction.
Precision is maintained by using N-bit fixed-point representation.
Overflow is implicitly handled since result is truncated or sign-extended to N bits.
The output c reflects the result of the addition.
