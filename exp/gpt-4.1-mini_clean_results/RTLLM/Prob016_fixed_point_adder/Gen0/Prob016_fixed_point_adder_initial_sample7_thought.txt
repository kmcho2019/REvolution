Design a parameterized fixed-point adder module with inputs a and b, each N bits wide, where Q bits are fractional. The sign bit is the MSB. The adder handles addition or subtraction depending on the sign bits: if signs are same, add absolute values and keep the sign; if signs differ, subtract smaller absolute value from larger one, assigning the sign of the larger operand. The result is stored in an internal register and assigned to output. Overflow is implicitly managed by truncating to N bits. The fixed-point format is preserved by simply performing arithmetic on the fixed-point representation.
