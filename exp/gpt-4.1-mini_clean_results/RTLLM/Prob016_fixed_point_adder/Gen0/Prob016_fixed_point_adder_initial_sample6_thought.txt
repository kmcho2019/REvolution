Design a parameterized fixed-point adder module that performs addition or subtraction depending on the sign bits of the inputs. Since the inputs are signed fixed-point numbers in two's complement form, the approach is:

- Extract the sign bit (MSB) of each input.
- If both have the same sign, add their magnitudes and assign the common sign.
- If signs differ, subtract the smaller magnitude from the larger magnitude and assign the sign of the larger magnitude operand.
- Magnitude is obtained by taking two's complement if negative, else the number itself.
- The result magnitude is limited to N-1 bits (excluding sign bit).
- Compose the result with the sign bit followed by magnitude.
- Use registers to store the result.
- Ensure the output is consistent fixed-point with Q fractional bits.
- Overflow is implicitly handled since result fits in N bits with sign and magnitude.

This solution carefully handles signed fixed-point addition and subtraction at magnitude level, reapplying the sign as per the arithmetic rules.
