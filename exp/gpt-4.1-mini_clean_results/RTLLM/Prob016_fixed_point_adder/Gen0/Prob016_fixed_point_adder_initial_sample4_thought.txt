Implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module with parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits). Inputs a and b are N-bit signed fixed-point numbers. We perform addition considering their sign bits. If signs are equal, we add their absolute values and keep the sign. If signs differ, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger and assign the result sign accordingly. The absolute value is obtained by taking two's complement if MSB is 1. Handle overflow naturally by limiting result to N bits. Use a register res to store intermediate results before assigning to output c.
