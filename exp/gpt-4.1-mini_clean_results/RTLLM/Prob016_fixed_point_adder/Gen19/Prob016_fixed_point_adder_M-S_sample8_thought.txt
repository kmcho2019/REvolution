Use two's complement arithmetic properties to simplify fixed-point addition. Since fixed-point numbers in two's complement form can be added directly without explicit absolute value or sign bit checks, the adder can perform a straightforward signed addition of inputs. This approach inherently handles both addition and subtraction cases, maintaining correct sign and magnitude. Overflow will wrap around naturally in fixed-width arithmetic, which is typical in fixed-point operations.

This significantly reduces complexity and matches fixed-point addition semantics, meeting the problem requirements without conditional logic for absolute values or manual sign handling.
