3. Move all combinational logic into always @* block without function calls.
4. Explicitly declare internal wires/registers with proper widths.
5. Ensure that the final result uses two's complement representation directly without manual sign-magnitude conversions.

7. The sign is implicitly handled by using signed arithmetic with abs values, and result constructed by either addition or subtraction.
