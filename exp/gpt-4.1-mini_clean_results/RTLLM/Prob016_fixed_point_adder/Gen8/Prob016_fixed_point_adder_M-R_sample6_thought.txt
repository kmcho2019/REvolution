Refactor the fixed_point_adder module by replacing the multiple always blocks with continuous assignments (`assign`) for combinational logic, improving clarity and reducing redundant logic. The sign extraction, magnitude conversion, magnitude comparison, addition/subtraction, and final two's complement reconstruction are all expressed as wire assignments. This approach preserves the core algorithm but simplifies dataflow and eases synthesis, as the logic is purely combinational without explicit always blocks. It also clearly separates the steps, and the internal result register is implemented as a reg updated by a single always block for consistent coding style. This version is more idiomatic for purely combinational fixed-point adders and improves readability and maintainability.
