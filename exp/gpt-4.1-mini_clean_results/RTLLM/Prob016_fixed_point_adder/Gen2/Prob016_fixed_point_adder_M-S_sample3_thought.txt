Simplify the fixed_point_adder by consolidating the logic into a single combinational always block and using nonblocking assignments for clarity, although for purely combinational logic blocking assignments are acceptable. Keep the sign and magnitude extraction straightforward. Assign the output directly from the internal result register at the end of the same always block to reduce redundant always blocks. Also, the sign of the result after subtraction should be the sign of the operand with the larger magnitude, or zero if magnitudes are equal. Overflow management remains implicit by fitting result into N bits. Parameter order is kept as (N, Q) with defaults. This simplification improves readability and synthesis friendliness while maintaining all functionality.
