Refactor the fixed_point_adder module to implement the addition purely with combinational logic using continuous assignments without an always block. The design explicitly splits the inputs into sign and magnitude parts, then uses multiplexers and conditional operators to determine whether to add or subtract the magnitudes based on sign equality. Magnitude comparisons select the correct subtraction order and final sign. The zero-result case is handled by forcing the sign bit to zero. The final two's complement output is formed by re-applying the sign to the magnitude using conditional inversion and increment. This approach simplifies the logic flow, reduces register usage, and improves synthesis friendliness by relying entirely on combinational assignments. Overflow handling remains implicit by truncation of results to N bits.
