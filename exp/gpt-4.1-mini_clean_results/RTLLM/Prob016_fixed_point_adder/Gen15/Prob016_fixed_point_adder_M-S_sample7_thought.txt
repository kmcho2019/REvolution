Simplify the fixed-point adder by treating the inputs as signed numbers and directly adding or subtracting them using two's complement arithmetic. Since the inputs and outputs use fixed-point two's complement representation, we can leverage Verilog's signed arithmetic to handle both addition and subtraction with correct sign extension automatically.  
This removes the need for explicit sign and magnitude extraction and manipulation.  
Use a signed addition of the inputs and assign the result directly.  
Parameterize bit widths for flexibility.  
Overflow can be implicitly handled by truncation as per fixed-width output.  
This approach maintains the fixed-point precision and sign handling but greatly reduces complexity.
