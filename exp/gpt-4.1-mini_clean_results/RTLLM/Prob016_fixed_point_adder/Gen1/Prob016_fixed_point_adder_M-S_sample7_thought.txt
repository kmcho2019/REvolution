Create a parameterized fixed-point adder for N-bit two's complement inputs with Q fractional bits. Determine the signs from the MSB of inputs `a` and `b`. If signs match, add inputs directly (signed addition). If signs differ, perform subtraction of the smaller absolute value from the larger absolute value and assign the sign of the operand with the larger magnitude. Use simple two's complement arithmetic and built-in signed types to simplify the design. Store intermediate result in a register before output. Overflow naturally wraps in two's complement and is not explicitly saturated.
