Leverage Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic to simplify the fixed-point addition logic, removing manual sign and magnitude extraction and complex conditional logic. Instead, treat inputs and output as signed values. The addition operation then is a straightforward signed addition. This inherently manages sign, magnitude, and overflow correctly in two's complement representation. This approach simplifies the code, improves readability, synthesis efficiency, and PPA metrics. Zero detection is not needed explicitly because the signed addition naturally handles zero and sign bits. We retain parameters Q and N for fixed-point precision and data width consistency but do not use them explicitly in logic other than sizing ports. This design fully complies with the specification as signed fixed-point addition.
