Refactor the fixed-point adder module to use continuous assignments and internal wires instead of a procedural always block. This approach improves synthesis friendliness and clarity by leveraging Verilog's built-in arithmetic for signed numbers. We'll convert inputs to signed values, perform addition or subtraction on their magnitudes as unsigned numbers, and reconstruct the result based on sign logic using ternary operators. The sign bit extraction, magnitude comparison, and final two's complement reconstruction are done using combinational assignments. Zero result is handled by forcing sign to zero. This removes explicit internal registers and procedural code, simplifying the logic and potentially improving synthesis and simulation performance.
