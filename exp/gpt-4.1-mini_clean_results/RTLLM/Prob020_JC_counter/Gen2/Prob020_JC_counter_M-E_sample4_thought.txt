Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by explicitly capturing the shifting behavior in a for-loop that updates each bit of Q based on the previous bit, while computing the new MSB as the inverse of the previous LSB at every clock edge. On reset, clear Q to zero. This bitwise approach, though functionally equivalent to shifting and concatenation, demonstrates explicit register updates for each bit, which can be instructive for understanding the Johnson counter behavior and may give more control for future bit-level modifications or instrumentation. Use synchronous logic with non-blocking assignments and an active-low asynchronous reset.
