To improve PPA for the 64-bit Johnson counter, the main goal is to reduce the critical path delay caused by shifting and concatenation. Instead of performing a wide 64-bit shift every clock cycle, the update can be restructured as a simple shift register with feedback of a single bit. The new MSB bit can be derived from the LSB of the current value, eliminating the need to shift explicitly.

Specifically:
- Treat Q as a shift register shifting right by one bit.
- The new bit inserted at MSB depends on Q[0]: if 0, insert 1; else insert 0.
- Instead of concatenation with the entire Q[63:1], the design uses a combinational bit for feedback, which simplifies logic.
- This approach reduces combinational logic and potentially shortens the critical path since it only drives the MSB bit with a simple expression rather than concatenating a large vector.
- The reset remains asynchronous active low.

This approach optimizes for better timing and potentially lowers power by simpler logic.
