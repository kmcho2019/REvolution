Refactor the Johnson counter to separate the next-state logic into a combinational always block and use a register to hold the current state. This approach cleanly distinguishes combinational logic from sequential logic, which enhances readability and can facilitate certain synthesis optimizations. The counter state is stored in Q (a reg), updated on the clock edge or asynchronous reset, while the next state is computed based on Q[0] in a combinational block. This preserves the original function of shifting right and inserting the complement of the LSB at MSB, but uses explicit next-state assignment instead of direct concatenation inside the sequential block.
