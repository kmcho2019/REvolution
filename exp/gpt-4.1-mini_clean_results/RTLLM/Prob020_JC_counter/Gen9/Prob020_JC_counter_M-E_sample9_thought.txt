Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by using a straightforward shift right operation combined with a conditional MSB insertion based on the LSB. Instead of a loop, directly assign Q at every clock edge by concatenating the inverted LSB at the MSB and shifting the rest of the bits right. This approach closely follows the counter definition, optimizes the critical path by using simple concatenation, and eliminates the need for intermediate variables. The asynchronous reset initializes Q to zero. This architectural style leads to minimal combinational logic, better timing, reduced power, and less area than bitwise looping.
