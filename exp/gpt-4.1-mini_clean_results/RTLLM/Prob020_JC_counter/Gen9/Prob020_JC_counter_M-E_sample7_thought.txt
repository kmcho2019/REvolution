Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a shift-left approach instead of shift-right, by shifting left and inserting the inverted MSB at LSB position. This approach is functionally equivalent but flips the direction of shifting, giving a different structural perspective. The reset initializes Q to zero, and on each rising clock edge, Q shifts left by one bit and inserts the complement of the previous MSB at LSB. This design maintains simplicity and clarity while providing a new angle on the Johnson counter implementation.
