Instead of performing a simple shift-right by 1 and inserting the inverse of Q[0], implement the Johnson counter with a shift-left operation and inserting the inverse of the MSB at the LSB position. This is a conceptually equivalent Johnson counter operation but shifts bits in the opposite direction. On every rising clock edge or asynchronous reset, if reset is asserted, Q is zeroed. Otherwise, Q shifts left by one, and the inverted MSB (Q[63]) is inserted into Q[0]. This approach reverses the direction of the shift while maintaining the Johnson counter's characteristic sequence, showing a novel architectural perspective. It may offer synthesis tools an alternative mapping or routing option, and conceptually clarifies the inversion of the feedback bit at the least significant position. The logic remains simple, fully synchronous, and uses non-blocking assignments for good sequential design style.
