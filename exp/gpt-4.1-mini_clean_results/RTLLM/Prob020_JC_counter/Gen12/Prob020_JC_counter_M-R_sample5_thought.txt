Refactor the 64-bit Johnson counter by separating the next state logic into a combinational wire and using a synchronous always block only to update the state register Q. This makes the design clearer by explicitly showing the combinational next state calculation outside the sequential process. The next state `next_Q` is computed as the MSB being the inverse of current LSB, and shifting the remaining bits right by one. The reset logic remains asynchronous active-low. This approach modularizes the design by distinguishing between combinational next-state logic and sequential state update.
