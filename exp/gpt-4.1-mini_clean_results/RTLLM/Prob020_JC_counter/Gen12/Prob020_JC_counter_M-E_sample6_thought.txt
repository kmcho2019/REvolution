Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by leveraging the simple and efficient property that the next state is formed by shifting the current state right by one bit and inserting the inverted least significant bit at the most significant bit position. Use a purely combinational right shift plus bitwise negation for the MSB insertion without any loops or temporary variables. The code will use a single non-blocking assignment to update Q each clock cycle, triggered by the positive clock edge or negative asynchronous reset. This approach is concise, synthesizes efficiently, reduces combinational path delay, and uses direct concatenation operations for clarity and performance.
