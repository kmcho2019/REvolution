Instead of performing a direct concatenation of the inverted LSB and shifted bits, we can implement the Johnson counter by explicitly calculating the next state bits using a generate loop. Each bit of the next state depends on the previous bit of the current state, except for the MSB which is the inverse of the LSB. This structural approach emphasizes the bitwise relationship between consecutive states, making the logic more explicit and modular, while preserving the same Johnson counter functionality. The asynchronous reset initializes the counter to zero as specified.
