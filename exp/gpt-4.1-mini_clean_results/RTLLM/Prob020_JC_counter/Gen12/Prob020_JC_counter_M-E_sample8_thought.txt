Instead of performing the right shift and concatenation directly on the full 64-bit register, I will implement the Johnson counter using a simple rotate right operation combined with XOR of the least significant bit with 1 to generate the new MSB. This approach effectively shifts the register right by one bit while computing the new MSB value. The new MSB is the inverted Q[0], which can be done as XOR with 1. This method uses the rotate operation but then overrides the MSB with the inverted LSB, achieving the Johnson counter sequence. It structurally differs from direct concatenation and may help synthesis tools infer rotate operations or simpler muxes.
