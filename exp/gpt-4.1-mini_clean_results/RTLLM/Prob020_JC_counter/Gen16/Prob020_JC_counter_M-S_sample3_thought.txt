Simplify the 64-bit Johnson counter implementation by replacing the explicit for-loop shift with a single vector concatenation assignment. On every clock's rising edge (or reset falling edge), if reset is active, clear the counter; otherwise, shift right by one bit and insert the inverse of the LSB at the MSB position. This concise vector operation is clearer, synthesizes efficiently into shift register hardware, and reduces critical path and area compared to bitwise for-loop shifting. Maintain asynchronous active-low reset and non-blocking assignments for clean sequential logic.
