Instead of shifting the entire 64-bit register at each clock cycle, we can decompose the 64-bit Johnson counter into 16 parallel 4-bit Johnson counters, each operating on a 4-bit slice of the 64-bit output. We update all 16 slices simultaneously based on their own LSB and MSB rules, then combine them into the 64-bit output vector. For the overall 64-bit Johnson behavior, we chain the MSB output of one 4-bit slice as the input for the next slice's shift, effectively creating a pipeline of 4-bit Johnson counters feeding into each other. This hierarchical approach may simplify timing by localizing shifting to 4-bit units while preserving the Johnson pattern across 64 bits.
