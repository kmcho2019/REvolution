Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a rotate-right operation combined with conditional inversion of the input bit. Instead of explicitly shifting and concatenating, perform a rotate-right by one bit on the current state and then conditionally invert the most significant bit based on the current least significant bit. On reset, initialize the counter to zero. This approach conceptually separates the shift and the bit inversion and can be more intuitive while preserving the Johnson counting pattern. Using a rotate operation can also hint synthesis tools to use barrel shifters if supported, or simple wiring, potentially optimizing critical paths.
