A novel approach is to implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by maintaining two registers: one holding the current state and another to generate the next state based on the existing one. Instead of shifting right, shift left by one bit each clock cycle. The new LSB bit is the inversion of the current MSB bit, thus forming a twisted ring counter moving in the opposite direction compared to the right-shift version. This approach emphasizes the left-shift operation, showcasing a different architectural perspective while preserving the Johnson counter behavior. The reset initializes the register to zero asynchronously.
