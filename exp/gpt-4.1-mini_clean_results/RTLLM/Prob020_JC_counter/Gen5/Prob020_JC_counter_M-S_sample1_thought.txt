The original design is already concise and efficient, but we can simplify it slightly by removing the explicit bit range in the right part of the concatenation, since shifting right by one bit is equivalent to assigning Q[63:1] directly. Also, since the MSB is the inversion of the LSB, using ~Q[0] is a neat expression that eliminates the if-else conditional. The reset and clock sensitivity remain the same for asynchronous active-low reset and synchronous update. Overall, the solution is minimal, clear, and maintains full functionality.
