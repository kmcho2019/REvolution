Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by maintaining two 32-bit shift registers that operate in tandem to form the full 64-bit counter. On each clock edge, the rightmost bit of the lower register (Q_low) controls the input to the upper register (Q_high) and vice versa, effectively forming a twisted ring split into two halves. This approach halves the shift register size and can allow better synthesis optimization on some tools. The reset clears both halves to zero. The combined output Q is formed by concatenating Q_high and Q_low. The MSB insertion logic uses the inversion of the appropriate LSB from the other half to propagate the torsional effect.
