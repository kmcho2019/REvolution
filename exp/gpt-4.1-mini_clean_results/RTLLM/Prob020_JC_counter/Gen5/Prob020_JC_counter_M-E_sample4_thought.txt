To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter differently, we can model the shift operation in a combinational manner inside the sequential block. Instead of shifting right and appending the MSB depending on Q[0], we construct the next state by manually assigning each bit: For bits 62 down to 0, assign Q[i+1]; for MSB (bit 63), assign ~Q[0]. This approach leverages the characteristic that Johnson counter outputs are cyclic complements. On reset, Q initializes to all zeros.
