Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a left-shift operation instead of right-shift to demonstrate an alternative architectural approach. On each clock edge, the counter shifts left by one bit, inserting the inverted value of the most significant bit (Q[63]) at the least significant bit (LSB). The asynchronous active-low reset initializes the register to all zeros. This left-shift implementation produces the same 2n unique states characteristic of a Johnson counter but uses the opposite shift direction and inverted MSB insertion, illustrating flexibility in design. Using a separate wire for the feedback bit clarifies the logic and enhances readability. This method may facilitate different synthesis optimizations depending on target architecture and can be easily adapted or expanded.
