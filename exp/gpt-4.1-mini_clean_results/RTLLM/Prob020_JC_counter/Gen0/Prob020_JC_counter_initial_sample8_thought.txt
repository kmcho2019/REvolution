Implement a 64-bit Johnson counter where on every rising clock edge or falling reset edge, 
if reset is active (rst_n == 0), the output Q is cleared to zero. Otherwise, we check the 
LSB of Q: if it's 0, we shift Q right by 1 and insert '1' at the MSB, and if it's 1, 
we shift Q right by 1 and insert '0' at the MSB. This behavior matches the given 4-bit 
example extended to 64 bits.
