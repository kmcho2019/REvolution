Implement a 64-bit Johnson counter by updating the output register Q on the rising edge of clk or the falling edge of rst_n. On reset (rst_n=0), Q is cleared to zero. On each clock, if Q[0] is 0, shift Q right by one and insert '1' at MSB; else if Q[0] is 1, shift right and insert '0' at MSB. This creates the torsional ring counter behavior as described.
