Optimize the Johnson counter implementation by eliminating the explicit for-loop and using simple concatenation to form the next state. The MSB is assigned as the inverse of the current LSB, and the remaining bits are shifted right by one using a slice operation. This approach significantly reduces combinational logic depth and complexity, improving timing and power without changing functionality. The asynchronous active-low reset remains intact for immediate initialization. Non-blocking assignments are maintained for synchronous design. This method yields a cleaner, more efficient design with better PPA metrics.
