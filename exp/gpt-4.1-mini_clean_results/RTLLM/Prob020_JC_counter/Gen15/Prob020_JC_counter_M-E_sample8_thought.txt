Instead of the usual approach of directly shifting and inverting the LSB, implement the Johnson counter by separately tracking the "phase" of the sequence: whether the MSB should be shifted in as '1' or '0'. Use a 1-bit state flip-flop "dir" that toggles whenever the sequence reaches either the all zeros or all ones state. On each clock edge, shift right the register and shift in "dir" as the MSB. On reset, initialize Q to zero and "dir" to 1. This way, the "dir" bit explicitly models whether we are in the ascending (shifting in 1's) or descending (shifting in 0's) part of the Johnson sequence, creating the 2N-length torsional ring count. This method is more explicit and potentially helpful for debugging or further logic extensions.
