Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a simple shift register approach without any loops. On each clock edge, update the register Q by shifting right by one bit and inserting the inverted value of Q[0] at the MSB position. Use a single non-blocking assignment with concatenation for concise, efficient hardware description. Incorporate synchronous logic with asynchronous active-low reset. This approach minimizes combinational logic depth and improves synthesis efficiency compared to explicit loops.
