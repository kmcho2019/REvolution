Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a left-shift approach with conditional feedback at the LSB, instead of right shift with MSB feedback. On each clock edge, if reset is asserted, clear Q. Otherwise, shift Q left by one bit and insert at the LSB the inverted MSB bit (Q[63]). This approach produces the same Johnson counter sequence but with left shift logic. The advantage is conceptual symmetry and potential mapping to shift-register primitives optimized for left-shift or circular shift operations in some FPGAs or ASIC libraries.

This method preserves the torsional ring counter behavior since Johnson counters can be implemented with shifted and inverted feedback at either end. The output Q updates synchronously on the clock's rising edge, with asynchronous active-low reset.

This design is functionally equivalent but architecturally different, offering a fresh perspective on the Johnson counter implementation.
