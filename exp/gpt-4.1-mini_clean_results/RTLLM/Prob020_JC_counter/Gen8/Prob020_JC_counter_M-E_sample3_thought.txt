Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter as a shift register that shifts left instead of right, capturing the inverted MSB bit as the new LSB on each clock cycle. Specifically, on each rising edge of clk (or falling edge of rst_n), if reset is active, initialize Q to zero. Otherwise, if the MSB (Q[63]) is 0, shift left and shift in a '1' at LSB; if Q[63] is 1, shift left and shift in '0' at LSB. This is a complementary approach to the typical shift-right Johnson counter and preserves the Johnson sequence properties. This approach also avoids direct dependence on Q[0], using Q[63] to determine input bit, thus providing architectural variety while meeting the behavior requirements.
