Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a shift-left operation instead of shift-right, but maintaining the same logic: on each clock edge, shift left and insert at LSB the inverted MSB bit. This approach is symmetric to the given example but from the opposite direction, offering an alternative hardware structure. The asynchronous active-low reset clears Q to zero. This design allows the Johnson counting sequence by propagating the complemented MSB into LSB, producing the torsional ring behavior in reverse bit order.
