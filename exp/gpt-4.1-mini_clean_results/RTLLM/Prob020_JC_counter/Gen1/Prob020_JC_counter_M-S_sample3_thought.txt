Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter with a single always block triggered on the rising edge of clk or the falling edge of rst_n. On reset, clear Q to zero. On each clock, shift Q right by one bit and set the MSB to the inverted LSB of the previous Q. This captures the behavior: when Q[0] is 0, MSB=1; when Q[0] is 1, MSB=0, effectively toggling the MSB according to Q[0]. This simplification removes the explicit if-else by using inversion.
