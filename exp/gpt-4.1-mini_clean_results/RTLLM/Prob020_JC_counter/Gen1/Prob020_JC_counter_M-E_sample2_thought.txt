Instead of shifting right each clock cycle, this design uses a left-shift approach combined with a toggle bit generated from the inverted LSB. The counter state Q is updated by shifting Q left by one bit, and the least significant bit is filled with the inverse of Q[0]. This exploits the fact that a Johnson counter cycles through a pattern where the bit entering at one end is the complement of the bit exiting at the other end. On reset, Q is cleared to zero. This approach uses a left-shift and inversion of Q[0], maintaining the Johnson counter property but with a different architectural twist.
