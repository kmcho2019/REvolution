The 64-bit Johnson counter updates synchronously on the rising edge of clk and asynchronously resets on the falling edge of rst_n. On reset, Q is zero. Each clock cycle, the register shifts right by one bit. The MSB is filled based on the current LSB: if LSB is 0, insert 1; if LSB is 1, insert 0. This matches the Johnson counter's torsional ring behavior.
