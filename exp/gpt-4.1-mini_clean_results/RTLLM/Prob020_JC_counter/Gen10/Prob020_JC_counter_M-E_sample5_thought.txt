Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a vector register updated on the positive clock edge with asynchronous reset. Instead of concatenating the inverted LSB and shifted bits, interpret the update as a left shift with feedback from the inverted LSB.

Specifically, the counter state is rotated left by one bit every cycle, inserting the inverted Q[0] bit at the LSB end. This approach keeps the Johnson counter behavior but changes the direction of shifting and the feedback insertion point.

Advantages:
- Conceptually simpler as a left shift and feedback on the LSB side, making it easier to adapt or extend.
- Allows potentially different optimizations in synthesis tools due to bit ordering and shift direction.
- Effectively the same counting sequence but from a different rotation perspective.

Implementation:
- On reset, clear Q to zero.
- Else, Q <= {Q[62:0], ~Q[0]};  // shift left by one, LSB gets inverted Q[0]
This operation implements the Johnson counting logic in an alternative form.
