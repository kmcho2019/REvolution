Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a left shift instead of a right shift to create a different data flow while achieving the same Johnson sequence pattern. Use a synchronous active-low reset to initialize the counter to zero. On each rising clock edge, if reset is inactive, shift the register left by one bit and insert the inverted LSB (Q[0]) at the least significant bit position. This approach differs from the conventional right shift and MSB insertion, giving a fresh perspective on the Johnson counter design.
