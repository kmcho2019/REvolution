Instead of performing a direct shift and insertion on each clock cycle, implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a rotating shift register concept with a stored direction bit that indicates whether to insert a '1' or '0' at the MSB. The direction toggles whenever the LSB changes from 0 to 1 or 1 to 0, emulating the sequence phases (increment or decrement steps). This approach explicitly tracks the counter direction as a state and may help in future extensibility, debugging, or integration with other control logic.

The behavior will be:
- On reset, Q = 0 and direction = 1 (meaning next insert '1' at MSB).
- On each clock:
  - Shift right Q by 1 bit.
  - Insert 'direction' bit at MSB.
  - If LSB changes (from previous cycle), toggle direction.

This method replicates the Johnson counter pattern but separates control (direction bit) from the shift register data. It may help synthesis tools better identify the toggling behavior and potentially optimize power by isolating direction control.

This design still meets the spec (Q is 64-bit output, updated on rising clk or falling rst_n), but with a small additional state bit 'direction' for conceptual clarity.
