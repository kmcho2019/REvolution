Implement the 64-bit Johnson counter by storing the state in a 64-bit register Q. On each rising clock edge or asynchronous reset, update Q depending on Q[0]. Instead of shifting right and appending inverted Q[0], implement the logic as a shift-left operation that shifts the bits left by one and inserts either a 1 or 0 into the least significant bit (LSB) depending on the original Q[0]. If Q[0] is 0, insert a 1 at LSB (increment phase), otherwise insert a 0 (decrement phase). This reversed shift direction approach achieves the same Johnson counting sequence behavior, but complements the feedback logic and may map better to shift-left primitives or certain FPGA architectures. The asynchronous reset initializes Q to zero. This approach clearly separates the feedback decision from the shifting and may simplify timing by using a single direction shift and conditional input at the LSB.
