A different architectural approach for the 64-bit Johnson counter is to treat it as a left-shifting register that inserts the inverted value of the most significant bit (MSB) into the least significant bit (LSB) position. This is logically equivalent but flips the direction of shifting compared to the original approach. On each clock edge, if not in reset, the register shifts left by one bit, and the new LSB is assigned as the inverse of the MSB. On reset, the counter is zeroed. This approach uses a left-shift with feedback from the MSB's inverted value and provides the same Johnson counting sequence behavior.
