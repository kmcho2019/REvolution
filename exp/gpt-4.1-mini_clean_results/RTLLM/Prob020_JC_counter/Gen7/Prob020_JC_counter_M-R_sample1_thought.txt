Refactor the 64-bit Johnson counter to use a purely synchronous always block with simple concatenation and shift operations instead of the bitwise for-loop. This improves readability, synthesis efficiency, and timing by reducing combinational logic depth. On reset, clear Q to zero. At each clock rising edge, update Q by concatenating the inverse of the current Q[0] as the new MSB with the upper 63 bits shifted right by one. This directly implements the Johnson counter behavior as described, while being more concise and efficient.
