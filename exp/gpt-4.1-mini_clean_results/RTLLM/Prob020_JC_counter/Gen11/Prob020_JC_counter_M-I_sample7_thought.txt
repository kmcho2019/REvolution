To optimize the 64-bit Johnson counter for better performance, power, and area (PPA), replace the explicit for-loop and temporary register with a simple and efficient concatenation and shift operation. This approach directly assigns the next state by concatenating the inverse of the current LSB as the new MSB and shifting the rest of the bits right by one, which synthesis tools handle efficiently. This reduces the critical path, gate count, and switching activity, improving timing and lowering power and area without changing functionality. Keep the asynchronous active-low reset and synchronous update on the rising clock edge.
