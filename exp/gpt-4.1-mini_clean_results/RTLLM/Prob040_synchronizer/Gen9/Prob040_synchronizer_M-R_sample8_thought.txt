The original design uses two separate always blocks in clk_a domain for data_reg and en_data_reg and an always block in clk_b domain for synchronization and output update. To refactor, I will combine the clk_a domain registers into a single always block with an enable signal to reduce complexity and consolidate logic. For the clk_b domain, instead of using two flip-flops for enable synchronization explicitly, I will use a shift register style 2-stage synchronizer. Also, I will separate the output data register update into a dedicated always block for clarity, updating dataout only when the synchronized enable is asserted, otherwise holding its value. This modular approach improves readability and better separates synchronization logic from output logic while preserving the original functionality.
