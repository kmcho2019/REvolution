To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will directly register data_in and data_en in the clk_a domain without conditional updates since data_en is stable and data_in changes are infrequent. This avoids added complexity from conditional logic. The enable signal will be passed through a standard two-stage synchronizer in the clk_b domain, and when the synchronized enable is high, dataout is updated with the stored data. On resets, all registers are cleared asynchronously. This minimal approach reduces logic and maintains robust clock domain crossing with clear reset behavior.
