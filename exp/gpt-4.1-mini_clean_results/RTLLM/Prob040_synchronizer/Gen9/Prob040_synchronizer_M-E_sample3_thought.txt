Implement a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer by leveraging a handshake-like approach with an additional pulse detection in the clk_b domain. Instead of synchronizing the entire data_en signal as a level, synchronize its rising edge to create a load pulse for dataout update.

In clk_a domain, latch data_in into data_reg whenever data_en is high.

In clk_b domain, use two-stage synchronizer registers to synchronize data_en from clk_a domain and detect its rising edge (pulse). This pulse triggers a MUX to select data_reg or hold the previous output.

This method avoids gating en_data_reg directly; instead, it creates a clean pulse to update dataout only when new data is ready.

Also, dataout update is controlled by this pulse, ensuring minimal toggling and clear timing.

All resets are asynchronously active low and applied appropriately.

This approach increases robustness and clarity in the synchronization process and clearly controls output updates with a pulse-driven enable, while still obeying the timing requirements.
