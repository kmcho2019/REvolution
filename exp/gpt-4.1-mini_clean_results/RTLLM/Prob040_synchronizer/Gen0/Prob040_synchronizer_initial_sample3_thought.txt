Implement the described multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer with two asynchronous reset domains (arstn for clk_a domain, brstn for clk_b domain). Use data_reg and en_data_reg registers clocked by clk_a and arstn to capture data_in and data_en. Then use two flip-flops (en_clap_one, en_clap_two) clocked by clk_b and brstn to delay the enable signal by two cycles in clk_b domain. Finally, output dataout is updated by clk_b domain, assigned from data_reg when en_clap_two is high, otherwise it holds its value. All resets are active low and synchronous to respective clocks.
