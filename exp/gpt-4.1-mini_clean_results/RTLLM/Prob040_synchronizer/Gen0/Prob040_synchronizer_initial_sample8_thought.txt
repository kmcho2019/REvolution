Implement the synchronizer module with separate always blocks for clk_a domain and clk_b domain as described. In clk_a domain, capture data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg respectively with asynchronous active-low reset arstn. In clk_b domain, create a two-stage synchronizer for en_data_reg with en_clap_one and en_clap_two registers, also asynchronously reset by brstn. Finally, generate dataout based on en_clap_two signal using a mux: when en_clap_two is high output data_reg, else hold previous value. All resets are asynchronous active low, triggered on negedge of the respective reset signals.
