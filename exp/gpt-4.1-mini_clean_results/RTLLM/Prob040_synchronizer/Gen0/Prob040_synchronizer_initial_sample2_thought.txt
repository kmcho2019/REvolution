The design implements a multi-bit synchronizer using two clocks (clk_a and clk_b) and two independent asynchronous resets (arstn and brstn). The input data_in and data_en are sampled on clk_a; the enable signal is then synchronized across to clk_b domain through two flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two). Data output is controlled by en_clap_two in clk_b domain using a MUX to hold previous value when data is not enabled. This guarantees stable data transfer from clk_a domain to clk_b domain with enable filtering and glitch protection.
