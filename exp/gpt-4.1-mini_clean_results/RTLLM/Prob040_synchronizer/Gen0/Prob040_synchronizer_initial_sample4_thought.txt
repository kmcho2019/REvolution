Use separate registers clocked by clk_a to capture data_in and data_en, with asynchronous active-low reset arstn. Then, synchronize the enable signal en_data_reg into clk_b domain using two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two, asynchronously reset by brstn. When en_clap_two is high, output dataout captures data_reg; otherwise, dataout holds its previous value. This design ensures proper crossing of data and enable signals from clk_a domain to clk_b domain with multi-bit data synchronizer using mux-based output enable.
