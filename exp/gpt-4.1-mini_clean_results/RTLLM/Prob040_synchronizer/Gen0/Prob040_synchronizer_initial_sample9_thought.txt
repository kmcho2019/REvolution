Implement a dual-clock domain synchronizer for a 4-bit data signal gated by an enable. In clk_a domain, latch data_in and data_en into data_reg and en_data_reg on rising edge or reset on arstn. Then, synchronize the enable signal across to clk_b domain through two flip-flops en_clap_one and en_clap_two with resets on brstn. On clk_b domain, when en_clap_two is high, latch data_reg into dataout; otherwise retain old value. Reset outputs and internal registers asynchronously on their respective resets.
