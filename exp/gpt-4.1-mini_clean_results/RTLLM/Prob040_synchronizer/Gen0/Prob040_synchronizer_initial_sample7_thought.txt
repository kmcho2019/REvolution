Implement the synchronizer module with two clock domains. In clk_a domain, register data_in into data_reg and data_en into en_data_reg with asynchronous active-low reset arstn. In clk_b domain, implement two-stage synchronization (en_clap_one, en_clap_two) of en_data_reg with asynchronous active-low reset brstn. Use en_clap_two as enable for updating dataout from data_reg; otherwise hold previous value. This design ensures stable multi-bit data synchronization via enable signals delayed by two cycles in clk_b domain.
