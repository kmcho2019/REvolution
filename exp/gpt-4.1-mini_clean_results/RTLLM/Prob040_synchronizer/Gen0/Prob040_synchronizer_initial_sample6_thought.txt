Implement the synchronizer module with two clock domains clk_a and clk_b.
- On clk_a domain: register data_reg stores data_in, and en_data_reg stores data_en, both reset by arstn.
- On clk_b domain: two registers en_clap_one and en_clap_two delay en_data_reg by two clk_b cycles, reset by brstn.
- On clk_b domain: output dataout is updated when en_clap_two is high, otherwise holds previous value, reset by brstn.
