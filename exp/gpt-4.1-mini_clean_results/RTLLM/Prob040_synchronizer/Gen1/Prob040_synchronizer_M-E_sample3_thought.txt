To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer with a novel architecture, we will separate data sampling and synchronization stages and introduce an explicit handshake mechanism to ensure reliable data transfer across clock domains.

In clk_a domain:
- Sample data_in and data_en into registers only when data_en is high to reduce unnecessary toggling, taking advantage of the long stable period.
- Use a small pulse generation mechanism that asserts a single-cycle pulse when data_en transitions from low to high, indicating new valid data ready.

In clk_b domain:
- Synchronize the pulse signal from clk_a domain through a 2-stage synchronizer to avoid metastability.
- Use this synchronized pulse to latch data_reg into a data_latched register, ensuring dataout only updates when a new valid data is signaled.
- Keep dataout stable otherwise.

All resets remain asynchronous active low as per spec.

This approach reduces toggling in clk_a domain registers by gating with data_en, and cleanly synchronizes a handshake pulse to clk_b domain, ensuring dataout updates only when data is stable and signaled. This avoids ambiguous enable signals and clearly separates sampling and transfer logic.
