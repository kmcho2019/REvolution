Refactor the synchronizer by splitting the logic into separate modules for the clock domain crossing (CDC) of the enable signal and the data synchronization logic. Use a dedicated two-stage synchronizer module for the enable signal from clk_a domain to clk_b domain implemented with always blocks. Then use an `assign` statement and a registered output register with a clock enable driven by the synchronized enable to update dataout on clk_b domain. This structure improves modularity and readability, using `assign` to drive the clock enable, and keeping data registers updated only when enable is active, thus reducing unnecessary toggling.
