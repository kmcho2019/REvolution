To simplify while maintaining functionality, we can combine the data_in and data_en registers into one clock domain (clk_a) and synchronize only the enable signal to clk_b domain through the two flip-flop synchronizer chain. The output dataout updates on clk_b domain only when the synchronized enable is high, holding previous value otherwise. We keep asynchronous resets and use non-blocking assignments for clarity and reliability. We remove redundant comments and assignments while preserving the required behavior.
