The original design is functionally correct and minimalistic but can be improved to reduce unnecessary toggling in the clk_b domain registers to save dynamic power. Since data_en remains high for at least 3 clk_b cycles and changes infrequently, we can gate updates of clk_b domain registers (en_clap_one, en_clap_two, and dataout) only when the enable signal is actually active or transitioning. This prevents unnecessary toggling of registers and reduces power consumption. Also, changing the last always block to update dataout only when en_clap_two transitions from 0 to 1 or stays high avoids redundant writes. This preserves functionality while minimizing switching. The reset logic and synchronization stages remain the same to maintain timing and metastability safety.
