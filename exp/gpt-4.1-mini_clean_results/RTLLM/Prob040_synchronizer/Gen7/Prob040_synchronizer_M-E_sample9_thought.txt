Implement a multi-bit synchronizer with a slightly different architecture by introducing an explicit data_valid signal in the clk_b domain derived from the two-stage synchronizer on en_data_reg. This data_valid will gate the dataout update to avoid unnecessary changes. Also, transfer data_reg into clk_b domain through a small dual-clock FIFO style register bank to synchronize the data safely. Specifically, latch data_reg only when data_en is high in clk_a domain, then use a handshake-like approach where en_data_reg delayed twice in clk_b domain (en_clap_two) enables capturing the latched data_reg into a clk_b domain register (data_sync). This ensures clean data transfer and synchronization with controlled enable, improving robustness for data changing at low frequency and stable for multiple cycles. The dataout register updates only when data_valid is asserted, keeping it stable otherwise.
