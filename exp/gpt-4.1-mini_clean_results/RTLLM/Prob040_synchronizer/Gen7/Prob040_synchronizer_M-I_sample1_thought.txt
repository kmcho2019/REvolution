To optimize the original design for power without sacrificing correctness and minimal area, the main improvement is to avoid unnecessary updates to data_reg and en_reg when data_en is low in the clk_a domain. Since data_in remains constant during data_en high and changes infrequently, gating the data_reg and en_reg updates with data_en reduces switching activity, lowering dynamic power. This does not affect functional behavior because data_reg only needs to update when new valid data is presented (when data_en is high). In the clk_b domain, the existing two-flip-flop synchronizer and output logic remain unchanged, preserving the metastability protection and correct timing. This approach reduces toggling on clk_a registers without adding complex gating logic, thus having negligible area impact and no timing penalties.
