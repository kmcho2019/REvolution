A novel approach is to leverage the stable nature of data_in during data_en and avoid continuously updating data_reg in clk_a domain by capturing data_in once at the rising edge of data_en.

Then, in clk_b domain, instead of a simple two flip-flop synchronizer of enable, implement a 3-stage synchronizer chain for the delayed enable to improve metastability robustness.

Use a multi-bit MUX controlled by the last synchronized enable stage in clk_b domain to select between the previous output data and the newly latched data_reg, ensuring dataout updates only when the enable is asserted and synchronized.

This approach reduces unnecessary data sampling in clk_a domain by latching data only once per data_en assertion, and enhances robustness of enable synchronization in clk_b domain.

The dataout register is updated on clk_b domain at each cycle, but uses the MUX output, holding previous data when enable is low.

Asynchronous resets are used as per specification.

This method meets all constraints, including data_en being stable for at least 3 clk_b cycles, and data_in stable during data_en.

Overall, this approach trades a slightly more complex enable synchronization for improved metastability protection and reduced unnecessary sampling in clk_a domain.
