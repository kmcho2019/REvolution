To optimize power and area while maintaining correctness, update data_reg and en_data_reg in clk_a domain only when data_en is high, as data_in is stable only during that period. This conditional update reduces unnecessary toggling of registers when data_en is low, lowering dynamic power. The enable synchronization and data output logic remain unchanged to ensure reliable crossing and output update. This small gating reduces switching without impacting functionality, as per the timing constraints (data_en high at least 3 clk_b cycles and slow data_in change). Additionally, merging en_clap_one and en_clap_two updates and output logic into one always block minimizes logic and eases synthesis. The approach achieves improved power efficiency with minimal area impact, while keeping timing robust and design clean.
