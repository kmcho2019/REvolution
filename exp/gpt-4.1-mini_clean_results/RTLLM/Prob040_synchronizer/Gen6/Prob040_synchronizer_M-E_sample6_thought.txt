Use a handshake-based synchronization approach to cross data and enable from clk_a to clk_b domain. Instead of directly sampling data_en twice on clk_b domain, create a toggle-based handshake signal in clk_a domain that flips whenever data_en is high, indicating new data is ready. Then, on clk_b domain, synchronize this toggle signal through a 2-flip-flop synchronizer and detect edges to latch data_reg safely.

Specifically:

1. In clk_a domain:
   - When data_en is high, latch data_in into data_reg.
   - Toggle a request bit (data_req_toggle) to signal new data is ready.

2. In clk_b domain:
   - Synchronize data_req_toggle through 2 flip-flops to reduce metastability.
   - Detect rising edge of synchronized toggle, indicating new data available.
   - When detected, latch data_reg value into dataout.
   
3. Reset signals arstn and brstn asynchronously reset respective domain registers.

This architecture cleanly separates domains using handshake toggles, removing dependency on direct enable signal crossing, and reduces risk of glitches or partial data sampling. It also cleanly supports the low data update rate (at least 10 cycles apart), as dataout updates only on toggle edges. The handshake toggle approach is widely used for reliable multi-bit data synchronization across clock domains.
