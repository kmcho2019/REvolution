Instead of directly using a two-stage flip-flop synchronizer for the enable signal in clk_b domain, this design uses a 3-bit shift register in clk_b domain to detect that data_en has been stable high for at least two clk_b cycles (to represent the required delay of two cycles). This approach explicitly filters out glitches or short pulses in clk_b domain and only then updates the output data. Meanwhile, in clk_a domain, data_in and data_en are sampled on their rising edge with asynchronous reset. When the shifted enable signal in clk_b domain shows a stable high for two cycles, the output latch updates with the data_reg value latched in clk_a domain. This method provides an explicit multi-cycle enable validation in clk_b domain before dataout updates, enhancing robustness while still using a MUX-like behavior by gating dataout update conditionally.
