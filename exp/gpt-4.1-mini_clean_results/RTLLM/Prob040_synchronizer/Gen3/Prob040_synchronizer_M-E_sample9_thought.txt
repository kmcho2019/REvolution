Use a handshake-like synchronization scheme that captures data_in and data_en in clk_a domain, then passes a toggle signal synchronized into clk_b domain to indicate new data availability. The toggled signal acts as a flag to latch data_reg into clk_b domain after two stages of synchronization to ensure metastability robustness.

In detail:
- In clk_a domain, use registers to store data_in and data_en, and when data_en goes high, update data_reg and toggle a single-bit flag (data_toggle).
- In clk_b domain, use a two-stage synchronizer for data_toggle signal. Detect toggle edges by comparing current and previous synchronized toggle values.
- When an edge on data_toggle is detected (indicating new valid data captured in clk_a domain), latch data_reg into a clk_b domain register for output.
- Data_out register updates only on detected new data, otherwise holds previous output.
- This scheme decouples data and enable synchronization into a toggle handshake, reducing metastability risk and simplifying clock domain crossing logic.
- Asynchronous resets clear all registers in their respective domains.

This approach guarantees new data outputs only after data_en was high for enough cycles in clk_a domain and after synchronization delay in clk_b domain, meeting the timing and data stability conditions. It uses a multi-bit data latch plus a single-bit handshake toggle synchronization to implement the MUX-based synchronizer differently from the previous approach.
