The design can be simplified by combining the data and enable updates in the clk_a domain under a single always block, updating them only when data_en is high, as data_in remains constant when data_en is high and changes slowly. This avoids unnecessary updates, reducing switching activity. The enable signal is then passed through a two-stage synchronizer in the clk_b domain before using it to latch data_reg into dataout. The resets remain asynchronous as specified. This approach keeps synchronization robust while reducing unnecessary toggles and logic complexity.
