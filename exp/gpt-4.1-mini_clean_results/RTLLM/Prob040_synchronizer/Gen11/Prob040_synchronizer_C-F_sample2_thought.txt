Fuse the strengths of both solutions by eliminating the redundant intermediate data_sync register from Example 1 to save area and power, while keeping the explicit two-stage synchronization of the enable signal for robust domain crossing and glitch reduction. Use a single, consolidated always block in clk_b domain to update en_clap_one, en_clap_two and output dataout atomically when the synchronized enable (en_clap_two) is asserted. In clk_a domain, latch data_reg only when data_en is high and also capture data_en in en_data_reg for synchronization. This approach keeps dataout stable when enable is low, minimizes switching, removes redundant registers, and ensures a clean and robust data transfer with minimal logic depth and glitch potential.
