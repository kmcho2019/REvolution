The original design correctly implements a 2-stage synchronizer for the enable signal and uses data_reg to capture data_in in clk_a domain, then outputs dataout in clk_b domain when synchronized enable is asserted. To optimize for power, I will update data_reg and en_data_reg only when data_en is high in clk_a domain, since data_in remains stable and only changes when data_en is asserted. This reduces unnecessary toggling and switching power on these registers. Also, this optimization does not alter functionality because data_en is guaranteed to be high for at least 3 clk_b cycles, providing stable data transfer and synchronization window. The rest of the design is kept as is for reliable clock domain crossing. Asynchronous resets are retained per specification.
