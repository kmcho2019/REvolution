Simplify the design by removing the en_data_reg register in the clk_a domain and directly synchronizing the data_en signal from clk_a to clk_b domain using a two-stage synchronizer. Keep data_reg in clk_a domain capturing data_in when data_en is high. In clk_b domain, delay the synchronized enable signal by two cycles and update the output dataout when the delayed enable is active. This reduces register count and complexity while maintaining correct synchronization and functionality.
