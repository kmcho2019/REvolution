Since data_en is stable and high for at least 3 clk_b cycles and data_in changes infrequently, we can simplify by always capturing data_in and data_en in clk_a domain registers without gating. Then, perform a two-stage synchronization of en_data_reg into clk_b domain, and update output dataout accordingly. This removes conditional updates in clk_a domain, reduces control complexity, and maintains functionality. Combining the two clk_b always blocks into one simplifies code further.
