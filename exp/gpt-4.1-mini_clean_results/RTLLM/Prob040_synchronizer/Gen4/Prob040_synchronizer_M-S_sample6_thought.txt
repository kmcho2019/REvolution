Simplify the design by combining the enable control flip-flops and output data register into a single always block in clk_b domain. Keep asynchronous reset in clk_a domain as required, but use synchronous reset in clk_b domain for consistency and simpler structure. Remove redundant else branches that hold register values implicitly. Since data_in is stable during data_en and data_en is stable for at least 3 clk_b cycles, directly sample data_reg on clk_a and enable its transfer to dataout via the synchronized enable signal with two-stage synchronization in clk_b domain. This reduces logic and keeps the same behavior.
