To simplify the design while maintaining the same functionality, combine related register updates to reduce the number of always blocks and remove redundant explicit holding of register values by relying on default register behavior. Since data_in and data_en are synchronous to clk_a and stable during data_en high period, it is safe to update data_reg and en_data_reg together on clk_a rising edge without additional enable gating — this reduces conditional complexity. In clk_b domain, maintain the two-stage synchronizer for en_data_reg and the output logic in a single always block with synchronous reset, as it is minimal and necessary for reliable synchronization. This approach reduces code complexity, improves readability, and still meets the timing and functionality requirements.
