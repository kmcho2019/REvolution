The existing design is functionally correct and clean but can be improved in power and clarity by gating the clock enabling updates only when necessary. Since data_in and data_en change very slowly, we can reduce unnecessary register toggling by updating data_reg and en_data_reg only when data_en is high in clk_a domain. Similarly, in clk_b domain, we can simplify the enable synchronization by using two flip-flops (as originally requested) rather than a 3-bit shift register, which suffices to delay the enable by two clk_b cycles. We also explicitly use a MUX style update for dataout, retaining previous value when enable is low, which helps synthesis tools optimize power and timing.

In summary:
- Update data_reg and en_data_reg in clk_a domain only when data_en is high, otherwise retain old values (power saving).
- Synchronize en_data_reg into clk_b domain with two-stage flip-flops as per original specification.
- Use en_clap_two to gate the update of dataout with a MUX style assignment for clarity.
- Remove the 3-bit shift register in favor of the required two flip-flop delay.
- Maintain asynchronous active-low resets for both clock domains.
This approach improves power by minimizing toggling in clk_a domain registers and reduces flip-flop usage in clk_b domain, while preserving timing and functionality.
