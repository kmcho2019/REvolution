Use a handshake-based synchronizer to safely transfer data_in from clk_a domain to clk_b domain. In clk_a domain, when data_en is asserted, latch data_in into a register and assert a toggle flag indicating new data is ready. The toggle flag crosses clock domains via a 2-flip-flop synchronizer in clk_b domain. In clk_b domain, detect changes in the synchronized toggle flag to know when new data is available. When new data arrives, sample data_reg from clk_a domain via a double-register handshake latch that holds the stable data for clk_b domain, and update dataout. This approach avoids metastability on multi-bit data signals by synchronizing only a single-bit toggle and using handshake logic for data transfer, ensuring glitch-free data update aligned with clk_b, and fulfilling the enable-delay and synchronization requirements.
