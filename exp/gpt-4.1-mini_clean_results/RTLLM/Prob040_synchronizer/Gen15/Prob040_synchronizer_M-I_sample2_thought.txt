To improve the existing design's power and performance without altering functionality, I will optimize toggling on the clk_a domain by loading data_reg only when data_en transitions from low to high, effectively latching the stable data once per data event, as data_en is low for at least 10 clk_b cycles between changes. This reduces unnecessary data_reg updates when data_en remains high over multiple cycles. Similarly, en_data_reg will capture data_en but only update on the rising edge to reduce toggling. 

In clk_b domain, the two-stage synchronizer for en_data_reg is necessary for metastability mitigation and will be retained. To further reduce power, I will update data_sync and dataout only on the rising edge of en_clap_two (detect enable assertion) rather than every cycle while enable is high, thus avoiding redundant data updates. This uses a simple edge detect technique by storing previous en_clap_two state and updating data only on rising edge.

Also, asynchronous reset in clk_a domain and synchronous reset in clk_b domain are retained as they provide the best reset timing characteristics.

These changes reduce dynamic power by minimizing register toggling and unnecessary data updates, improve performance by reducing critical path load in clk_a domain, and maintain area by not adding complex logic. The design remains clean and fully meets the problem specification.
