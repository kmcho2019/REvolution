Implement the synchronizer by capturing data_in and data_en in clk_a domain into registers only when data_en is asserted, reducing unnecessary updates. Then, transfer the stored enable signal to clk_b domain through a two-flip-flop synchronizer. For data crossing, use a mux-based approach controlled by the delayed enable signal in clk_b domain: when the delayed enable is high, dataout updates with the stored data, otherwise dataout holds its previous value. This approach ensures data stability and synchronization while minimizing updates and uses edge-triggered registers to hold data only when valid enable is present.
