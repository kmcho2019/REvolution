To optimize the given design for better power and area without impacting functionality, I will introduce clock gating to the clk_a domain registers data_reg and en_data_reg. This means these registers will only update (toggle) when data_en is asserted, reducing unnecessary switching and dynamic power consumption. I will also ensure that en_data_reg holds its value only while data_en is high to prevent toggling when data_en is low, thus reducing noise in the synchronization chain.

In the clk_b domain, the 2-stage synchronizer remains unchanged for robust clock domain crossing, but I will explicitly keep the dataout register stable when en_clap_two is low, which may help synthesis tools infer better latch or clock gating behavior to save power.

This approach balances power reduction by gating register updates with design simplicity and robustness, given the long stable intervals between data changes.
