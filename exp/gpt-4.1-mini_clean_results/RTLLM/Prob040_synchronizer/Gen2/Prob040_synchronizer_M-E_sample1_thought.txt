Use a handshake-based double-latching synchronizer with multi-bit MUX to synchronize data_in from clk_a to clk_b domain when data_en is asserted. 
Instead of directly sampling data_in and data_en in clk_a domain and then synchronizing enable signal in clk_b domain, latch the stable input data_in only when data_en is high in clk_a domain to a holding register. Then use a two-stage synchronizer on data_en in clk_b domain to generate a pulse indicating valid data. On clk_b domain, when the synchronized enable pulse is detected, latch the data from the holding register into dataout via a 2:1 MUX that chooses between previous dataout and the new data based on the synchronized enable signal. This approach cleanly separates the data latch (clk_a) and data output update (clk_b) controlled by synchronized enable signals. The 2:1 MUX ensures dataout changes only on valid enable in clk_b domain, minimizing glitches. The two-stage synchronization of data_en provides metastability protection. Reset conditions asynchronously initialize all registers to zero. This design is simple, robust, and cleanly handles synchronization of multi-bit data with enable signals crossing clock domains.
