Use an intermediate handshake signal synchronized from clk_a to clk_b to safely transfer data_en and data_in updates. In clk_a domain, latch data_in and data_en into registers only when data_en rises (rising edge detection), minimizing unnecessary updates. Then, create a pulse (data_en_pulse) indicating valid new data ready, which is then synchronized into clk_b domain using a small pulse synchronizer. In clk_b domain, this pulse enables loading of a multi-bit mux-based synchronizer register from the last captured data_in stored in a dual-clock FIFO-like register. The output dataout is updated only when the synchronized pulse is detected, ensuring safe and glitch-free crossing with minimal toggling. Both resets are synchronous active-low. This handshake with pulse synchronizer avoids direct cross-domain multi-bit data sampling, enhancing robustness.
