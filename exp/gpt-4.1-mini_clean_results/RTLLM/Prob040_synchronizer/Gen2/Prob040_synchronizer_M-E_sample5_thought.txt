Implement a multi-bit synchronizer by first capturing data_in and data_en in clk_a domain, gated by data_en to avoid unnecessary toggling. Then, pass data_en through a 2-stage synchronizer in clk_b domain to safely detect when data is valid in clk_b domain. Additionally, sample data_reg in clk_b domain only when the synchronized enable signal goes high, storing it in a holding register. The output dataout holds its value until the next valid data capture. This approach isolates data capture in clk_a domain, uses gated updates for power saving, and performs clock domain crossing of data_en through a two-flop synchronizer to generate a pulse for data capture in clk_b domain. This pulse triggers the loading of data_reg into a clk_b domain register, ensuring safe and glitch-free data output synchronized to clk_b.
