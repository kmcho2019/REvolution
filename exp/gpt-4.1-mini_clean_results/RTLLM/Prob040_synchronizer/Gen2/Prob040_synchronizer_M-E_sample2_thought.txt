Use a handshake-style multi-bit synchronizer that captures data_in and data_en in clk_a domain only when data_en is asserted, then handshake the data valid signal into clk_b domain through a two-flip-flop synchronizer. In clk_b domain, use the synchronized valid signal to latch the data from a dual-clock FIFO-style register that is updated only on valid data. This approach ensures clean data transfer at low frequency changes and reduces unnecessary toggling of output data. Both resets are asynchronous active low. The output data changes only when data_en was active and the new data is safely transferred into clk_b domain.
