Since data_en and data_in are stable and change infrequently with a guaranteed minimum interval and duration, we can synchronize data_en from clk_a to clk_b domain using a two-stage synchronizer and sample data_reg only when data_en is high in clk_a domain. Then, in clk_b domain, once the delayed enable (after two flip-flops) is high, update the output data with the stored data_reg. This removes redundant enable register in clk_a domain and avoids unnecessary holding of previous data_en. Reset logic remains asynchronous active low.
