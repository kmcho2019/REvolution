Use two parallel data registers in clk_a domain to hold the current and previous stable input data samples when data_en is high. Then, implement a 2-stage enable synchronizer chain in clk_b domain to generate delayed enable signals. A simple 2-to-1 MUX controlled by the last delayed enable signal selects between these two stored data values. The output register in clk_b domain loads the selected data only when the delayed enable signal transitions from low to high, thus avoiding unnecessary toggling and ensuring output updates only after enable has stabilized for two clk_b cycles. This approach leverages a MUX-based synchronizer with edge detection for enable control and double-buffered data capture, improving robustness and reducing unnecessary data register updates and output toggling.
