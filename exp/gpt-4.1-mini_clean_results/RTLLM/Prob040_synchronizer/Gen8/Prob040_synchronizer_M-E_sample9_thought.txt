Implement a single multi-bit 2-stage synchronizer for both enable and data combined by using a latch-based data capture in clk_a domain gated by data_en, then double-registering the data and enable signals in clk_b domain for synchronization. The enable signal is delayed two cycles in clk_b domain via two flip-flops, and the output data is updated only when the delayed enable is asserted. This approach treats the 4-bit data as a bundled signal synchronized together with the enable path, using a parallel 2-stage synchronizer in clk_b domain for the entire data word and the enable, simplifying logic by removing explicit muxes and making data output updates conditional on the delayed enable. As data_en is low most of the time, the data only changes when enabled, and the double flip-flop on clk_b provides metastability protection. Async resets are applied as specified.
