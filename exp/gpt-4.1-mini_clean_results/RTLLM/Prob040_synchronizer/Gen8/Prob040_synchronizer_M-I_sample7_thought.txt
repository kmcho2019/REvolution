To optimize the design for better power and area without sacrificing functionality or timing robustness, I will implement conditional updates for data_reg and en_data_reg in the clk_a domain. Since data_in remains constant during the period when data_en is high, and data changes only with at least 10 clk_b cycles interval, updating data_reg only when data_en is asserted reduces unnecessary register toggling, saving dynamic power. Similarly, en_data_reg updates only when data_en transitions to high or remains high, reducing toggles. This selective clock enable behavior can be implemented by gating the update with data_en. In the clk_b domain, the 2-stage synchronizer and output update logic remain unchanged to ensure metastability protection and correct timing. This balances power and area reduction with robust synchronization.
