To optimize power without impacting functionality, update data_reg and en_data_reg only when data_en is asserted in clk_a domain to reduce unnecessary toggling. This leverages the fact that data_in is stable during data_en high and data changes are infrequent. This gating avoids needless data_reg updates when data_en is low. The clk_b domain logic remains unchanged since it already updates only when enabled, ensuring reliable synchronization. This change reduces dynamic power by minimizing flip-flop toggling on clk_a domain registers without affecting timing or area significantly.
