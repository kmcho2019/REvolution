To simplify the design while preserving functionality, eliminate the intermediate enable register en_data_reg and directly register data_en in clk_a domain along with data_in. Then synchronize this registered enable signal through two flip-flops in clk_b domain. Use the delayed enable signal (after two clk_b cycles) to gate updating the output dataout with the stable data_reg value. This reduces register count and logic, simplifies the path, and maintains metastability protection and proper domain crossing.
