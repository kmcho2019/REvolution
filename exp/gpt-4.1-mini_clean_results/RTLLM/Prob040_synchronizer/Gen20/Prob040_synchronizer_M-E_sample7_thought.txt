Implement a multi-bit synchronizer with a two-domain handshake-like approach to ensure robust transfer of data_in from clk_a domain to clk_b domain. 

In clk_a domain:
- Register data_in into data_reg only when data_en is high, to capture stable data.
- Register data_en as en_data_reg to indicate valid data stored.

In clk_b domain:
- Use a two-stage synchronizer to bring en_data_reg into clk_b domain (en_sync1 and en_sync2).
- Additionally, introduce a data_valid_reg in clk_b domain that latches when en_sync2 rises, indicating new data is ready.
- Use a 4-bit register dataout to hold the synchronized data.
- On clk_b domain, when data_valid_reg asserts (edge detect on en_sync2), latch data_reg into dataout, thus ensuring updated dataout only changes when new data is valid and stable.

This method provides a handshake-like mechanism:
- data_en in clk_a domain controls data_reg latch.
- en_data_reg synchronized into clk_b domain indicates new valid data.
- data_valid_reg detects the enable's rising edge after crossing clock domains, triggering dataout update.
- This architecture improves glitch immunity by using edge detection rather than simple level enable, preventing dataout from toggling unnecessarily.

All async resets are handled as required.
