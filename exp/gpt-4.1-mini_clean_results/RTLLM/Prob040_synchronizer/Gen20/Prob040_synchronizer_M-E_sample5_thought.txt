Implement a 3-stage enable synchronizer flip-flop chain in clk_b domain to further reduce metastability risk and cleanly delay the enable signal. Instead of holding data_reg in clk_a domain, latch the stable data_in into a 4-bit register only on the rising edge of data_en in clk_a domain. This way data_reg only updates at enable assertion edges, not continuously while data_en is high, reducing toggling. Then cross the stable enable signal and the data_reg together into clk_b domain using multi-bit MUX controlled by the third-stage delayed enable signal. Use the delayed enable signal to gate updates of dataout, which picks between previous dataout and synchronized data_reg. This approach ensures minimal data glitches and controlled data transfer triggered strictly by enable edges, with robust multi-stage enable synchronization in clk_b domain and stable data input sampling in clk_a domain.
