Implement a multi-bit synchronizer using a handshake-like protocol between clk_a and clk_b domains to ensure safe data transfer with enable gating. In clk_a domain, latch data_in into data_reg only when data_en is high, and also generate a pulse signal (data_valid) that indicates new data is ready. Then, synchronize this pulse through a 2-flip-flop synchronizer in clk_b domain to detect the rising edge of data_valid safely. When the rising edge is detected, latch the data_reg into data_sync in clk_b domain, and hold it stable until next new data arrives. This approach avoids directly crossing data_reg signals asynchronously and instead crosses a single-bit pulse, improving metastability handling and timing closure. Reset signals remain asynchronous in clk_a domain and synchronous in clk_b domain, matching domain-specific requirements. The output dataout follows clk_b domain timing and holds stable data, updating only when new synchronized data arrives.
