The output dataout is updated when the enable signal delayed by two clk_b cycles (en_clap_two) is active, retaining the previous value otherwise. We'll rename the enable synchronization registers as en_clap_one and en_clap_two as per the specification, and keep the reset and clock edge conditions unchanged.
