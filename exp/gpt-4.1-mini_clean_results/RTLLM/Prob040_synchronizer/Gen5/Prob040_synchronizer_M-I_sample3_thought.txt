To optimize for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), integrate the enable synchronization registers directly into the main synchronizer module instead of a separate module to reduce inter-module routing and area overhead. Also, add clock enables on the data_reg and dataout registers to prevent unnecessary toggling when data_en or en_clap_two is not active, reducing dynamic power. This leverages the knowledge that data_en is stable for at least 3 clk_b cycles and data_in changes slowly (minimum 10 clk_b cycles). These enables avoid redundant register updates. Keep asynchronous resets for simplicity and adherence to specs. The mux logic remains combinational, but is controlled by the synchronized enable signals. Overall, these improvements reduce switching activity and routing area without impacting functional correctness or synchronization integrity.
