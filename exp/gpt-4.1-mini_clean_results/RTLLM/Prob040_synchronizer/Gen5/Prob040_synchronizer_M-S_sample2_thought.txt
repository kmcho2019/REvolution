We can simplify by combining the enable register update with data register update in clk_a domain without separate enable register, as data_en controls data_in stability anyway. Then, in clk_b domain, use a two-stage synchronizer just for data_en to create en_clap_one and en_clap_two. The output updates only when the delayed enable (en_clap_two) is high, otherwise retains its value. This reduces one register in clk_a domain and keeps the synchronizer logic straightforward.
