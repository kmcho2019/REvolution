To improve power efficiency, we can avoid redundant dataout register toggling by updating dataout only when en_sync_2 transitions from low to high or remains high. We add a simple enable signal for the output register to update only when needed. Also, to ensure correct CDC, use the synchronized enable registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) as per the original specification. This matches the requirement more explicitly, maintaining two D flip-flops delay of data_en in clk_b domain, and using a 4-bit mux-based selection for dataout update. The data_reg and en_data_reg remain clocked on clk_a with async reset. This approach respects the specification strictly and adds a gated update for dataout to reduce switching power.
