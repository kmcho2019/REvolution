Use a pulse-based handshake to transfer the enable signal from clk_a to clk_b domain and latch data_in only on the pulse, minimizing unnecessary data updates in clk_b domain.

Key points:
- In clk_a domain, capture data_in into data_reg only on rising edge of data_en, avoiding redundant updates.
- Generate a single-cycle pulse "en_pulse" in clk_a domain when data_en goes high.
- Synchronize this pulse into clk_b domain using a two-flip-flop synchronizer.
- Use pulse detection in clk_b domain to update dataout from data_reg only on the synchronized pulse rising edge.
- This approach guarantees minimal data toggling on clk_b side and clear, glitch-free transfers without needing to hold enable for multiple cycles in clk_b.
- All resets are asynchronous active-low as per spec.
- Dataout holds its value until next valid pulse, ensuring stable output.

This pulse-based handshake reduces toggling and explicitly transfers data updates, improving power and timing while ensuring functional correctness.
