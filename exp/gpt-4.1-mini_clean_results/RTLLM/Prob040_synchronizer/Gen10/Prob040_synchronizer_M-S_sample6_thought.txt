To simplify the design while preserving functionality, I will remove conditional updates inside the clk_a domain and update data_reg and en_data_reg unconditionally on each clk_a rising edge with the asynchronous reset. This removes gating complexity and eases synthesis and timing analysis. The clk_b domain will retain the 2-stage synchronizer for the enable signal and update dataout when the second-stage enable is asserted. This simpler approach ensures clarity, minimal logic, and robust synchronization without changing the intended behavior.
