Simplify the design by removing the conditional update of data_reg and en_data_reg; always capture data_in and data_en in clk_a domain on every clock edge. Use a straightforward two-stage synchronizer to transfer data_en from clk_a to clk_b domain. The output dataout in clk_b domain updates only when the synchronized enable signal is high. This eliminates conditionals and slightly reduces logic complexity while maintaining functional correctness given that data_in changes infrequently and data_en remains high long enough. The reset handling and clock domain crossing remain intact.
