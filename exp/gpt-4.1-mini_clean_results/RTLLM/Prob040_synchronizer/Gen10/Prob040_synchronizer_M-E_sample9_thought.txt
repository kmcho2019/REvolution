Implement a synchronizer that uses a small FIFO-like structure clocked by clk_a to latch data_in and data_en when data_en is high, but only update the data_reg when data_en transitions low, ensuring stable data capture. This reduces unnecessary data_reg updates and better aligns data capture points. Then, in clk_b domain, use a 2-stage synchronizer on a single pulse generated when the enable signal crosses a threshold. Use a MUX controlled by the delayed enables in clk_b domain to select between previously output data and new data_reg content, thus providing stable multi-bit synchronized dataout with minimal toggling. This approach decouples the data capture timing from immediate data_en changes and leverages the known data_en timing constraints to optimize data stability and minimize unnecessary register toggling in clk_b domain.
