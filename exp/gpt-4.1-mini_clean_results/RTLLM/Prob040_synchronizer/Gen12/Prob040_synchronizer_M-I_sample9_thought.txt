To optimize the existing design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA):

1. Performance:
   - The existing design is already minimal and uses only necessary flip-flops.
   - Asynchronous resets might cause timing uncertainty; converting them to synchronous resets can help with timing closure and improve performance.
2. Power:
   - Currently, en_data_reg updates every clk_a cycle even when data_en=0, causing unnecessary switching.
   - Since data_en changes infrequently, gate the update of en_data_reg to only when data_en is high, further reducing toggle rate.
3. Area:
   - The design is minimal; no major area reduction possible without changing functionality.
4. Additional minor optimization:
   - Combine the clk_b domain logic into a single always block to reduce inferred logic.
   - Use synchronous resets instead of asynchronous resets on both clk_a and clk_b domains.
     This reduces metastability risk on reset release and aids PPA.
5. Since data_en is guaranteed high for at least 3 clk_b cycles, two-stage synchronization and one cycle delay is safe.

This approach keeps the design functionally identical while improving timing predictability and lowering dynamic power slightly.

Note: Changing to synchronous resets requires the resets to be held active at least one clock cycle before deassertion.
