To simplify the design while maintaining the functionality, we can remove the conditional update of data_reg and en_data_reg inside clk_a domain and always latch data_in and data_en on clk_a clock edges with reset. This reduces conditional logic and keeps registers updating in a straightforward manner. In clk_b domain, keep the two-stage synchronization of en_data_reg as before, and update dataout only when the delayed enable en_clap_two is high, else hold previous value. The logic remains clear, minimal, and easier to synthesize without any gating or conditional complexity. This simplification still meets the timing and synchronization requirements, leveraging stable data_in during data_en high and the slow data change rate.
