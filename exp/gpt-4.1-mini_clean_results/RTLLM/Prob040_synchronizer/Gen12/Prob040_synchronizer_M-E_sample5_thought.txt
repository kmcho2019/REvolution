Use a handshake-style pulse generation and storage method to synchronize data across domains. First, latch data_in and data_en on clk_a when data_en is asserted. Then generate a single-cycle pulse (strobe) in clk_a domain signaling new valid data is ready. Transfer this strobe through a two-flip-flop synchronizer into clk_b domain to avoid metastability. On detection of the synchronized strobe pulse in clk_b domain, capture the latched data_in into a holding register. The output dataout always presents the last latched valid data captured on clk_b. This approach avoids direct multi-bit signal crossing by using a single-bit pulse and then capturing multi-bit data synchronously in clk_b domain, reducing metastability risks. The two-cycle delayed enable signal is thus implemented by the pulse synchronizer and the data latch on pulse detection. Reset logic ensures known startup state.
