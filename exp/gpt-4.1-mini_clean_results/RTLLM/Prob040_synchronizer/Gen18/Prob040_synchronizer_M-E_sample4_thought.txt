Implement a multi-bit synchronizer by first capturing data_in and data_en in clk_a domain only when data_en is asserted, thus avoiding unnecessary data updates. Then, transfer the enable signal through a 2-stage shift register clocked by clk_b to create a delayed enable in clk_b domain. Use this delayed enable to gate the update of dataout from an intermediate register that is updated asynchronously by combining clk_a data samples into a handshake flag. This handshake flag toggles when new data is latched, and is also synchronized into clk_b domain to trigger dataout update. The main idea is to separate data transfer and enable synchronization paths using handshake logic for safer domain crossing and reduce unnecessary toggling.
