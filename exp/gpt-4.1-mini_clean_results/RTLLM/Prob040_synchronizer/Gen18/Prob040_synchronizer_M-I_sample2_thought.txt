To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will streamline the enable synchronization path and data transfer to reduce register count and unnecessary toggling without compromising metastability protection or functionality. 

Key improvements:
1. Remove the intermediate `data_sync` register in the clk_b domain to save area and power, and reduce latency by directly assigning `data_reg` to `dataout` when the synchronized enable is high. Given data_reg changes only on clk_a when data_en is high, and data_en is low for at least 7 cycles in clk_b domain (due to delay and clock difference), the timing margin is sufficient to ensure stable data is sampled.

2. Make the reset in clk_b domain synchronous as in the original for simplicity and clean reset behavior.

3. Keep the 2-stage synchronizer for `en_data_reg` to maintain metastability robustness on crossing clk_a to clk_b domain.

4. In clk_a domain, keep updating `en_data_reg` every cycle and update `data_reg` only when `data_en` is high to reduce toggling.

5. Use non-blocking assignments carefully to avoid glitches.

This results in a simpler design with fewer registers, less power, and slightly better latency, while preserving correctness and robustness.

The output `dataout` is updated only when the synchronized enable (`en_clap_two`) is high, else it holds its previous value, avoiding unnecessary toggling.

This optimization maintains the design intent and should improve PPA by reducing register count and switching activity.
