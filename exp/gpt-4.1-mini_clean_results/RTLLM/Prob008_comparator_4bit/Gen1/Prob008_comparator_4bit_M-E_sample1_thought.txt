Instead of performing bit-level subtraction with borrow propagation (which is complex and error-prone), implement the 4-bit comparator by chaining simple 1-bit comparators from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). Each 1-bit comparator checks if the bits of A and B at that position are equal, A is greater, or A is less. The final result is derived by evaluating the highest-priority bit where the operands differ. This approach uses pure combinational logic without arithmetic operations and cleanly ensures mutual exclusivity of outputs.
