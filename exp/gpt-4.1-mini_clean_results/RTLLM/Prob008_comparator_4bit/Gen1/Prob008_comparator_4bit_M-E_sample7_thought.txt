Instead of performing a bit-level subtraction with borrow chains, implement the comparator by hierarchically comparing bits from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB) using simple combinational logic. At each bit, if the bits differ, the comparison result is determined by that bit alone. If they are equal, the next lower bit is checked. This cascaded priority approach uses combinational logic without subtractors or borrow chains and ensures mutually exclusive output signals. This method tends to be faster and more area-efficient for small-width comparators.
