To simplify the 4-bit comparator design while preserving its functionality:

- Use ripple borrow subtraction logic to detect borrow (A_less). This approach is simpler and adequate for 4 bits.
- Compute the subtraction result bits and borrow signals step-by-step.
- Determine equality by checking if all bits are equal (bitwise equality using XOR followed by NOR).
- Define outputs mutually exclusive: A_less from final borrow, A_equal from equality check, and A_greater from neither less nor equal.
- This reduces intermediate signals and logic complexity while maintaining clear combinational behavior.
