Simplify by replacing borrow lookahead logic with straightforward ripple borrow subtraction in combinational logic:

- Compute the subtraction A - B bit by bit, generating borrow signals from LSB to MSB.
- The final borrow_out indicates if A < B.
- Equality is directly detected by comparing A and B with a single equality operator.
- Determine A_greater as the case when A is not less and not equal.
- This approach reduces the number of intermediate signals and logic levels, making code simpler but still purely combinational and functionally correct.
