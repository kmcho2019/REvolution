The original design is functionally correct and uses a ripple borrow chain to detect if A < B, combined with a balanced AND-tree for equality. The comparator outputs are mutually exclusive. 

Improvement Strategy:
- Replace the ripple borrow chain with a faster subtractor-based logic: use Verilog subtraction to directly get the borrow and result. Modern synthesis tools efficiently implement subtraction and generate borrow flags with better timing and area than manual borrow chains.
- Use direct subtraction result to decide greater and less signals by checking the sign of (A - B), and check equality by checking if result is zero.
- This removes the explicit borrow chain, reducing logic depth and improving timing.
- Keep outputs mutually exclusive by defining:
    - A_equal = (diff == 0)
    - A_less = (A < B) indicated by sign bit of diff (for unsigned numbers, borrow bit can be used)
    - A_greater = not less and not equal
- This approach leverages the synthesizer's subtractor optimization, leading to improved PPA metrics for timing, power, and area.

Note: Since A and B are 4-bit unsigned numbers, subtraction result is 5-bit to capture borrow (MSB). If the MSB (borrow) is 1, then A < B.

This solution preserves functional correctness, improves timing by eliminating ripple borrow chain, and minimizes area and power by relying on optimized subtraction logic from synthesis tools.
