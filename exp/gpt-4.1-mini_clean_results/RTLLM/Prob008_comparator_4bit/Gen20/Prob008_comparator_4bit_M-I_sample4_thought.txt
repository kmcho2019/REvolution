To improve PPA based on the existing borrow lookahead design, I will:

1. Simplify the borrow lookahead logic by representing generate and propagate signals as 4-bit vectors and computing the borrow out using generate and propagate chains more compactly with vector operations.

2. Use reduction operators and vector expressions for equality checking to reduce gate count and simplify logic.

3. Minimize the depth of logic gates by using a balanced tree approach to compute borrow_out, reducing critical path delay.

4. Reduce the number of intermediate wires for clarity and potentially better synthesis optimization.

This results in a cleaner, more compact, and potentially faster and smaller design without changing the algorithmic essence.
