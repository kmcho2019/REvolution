To optimize the 4-bit comparator for better power, area, and timing (PPA), I focus on:

1. Simplifying borrow logic:
   - Use standard full subtractor borrow expression:
     borrow_out = (~A & B) | ((~(A ^ B)) & borrow_in)
   This is already done, but clarity is improved with explicit wire names.

2. Equality detection:
   - Instead of comparing (A ^ B) == 0, directly implement equality as the AND of bitwise XNOR of each bit.
   - This is simpler and uses less logic than the previous approach.

3. Minimizing logic depth and area:
   - Separate equality detection completely from the borrow chain, as in the previous design.
   - Use a generate block to create per-bit subtractors for clear, scalable, and balanced logic.
   - Since only borrow_out and equality determine outputs, avoid redundant signals.

4. Output encoding:
   - Ensure outputs are mutually exclusive:
     - A_less = borrow_out
     - A_equal = ~borrow_out & equality
     - A_greater = ~borrow_out & ~equality

This approach reduces switching activity (power), logic depth (timing), and gate count (area) compared to a naive subtraction or chained ripple subtractor.

The resulting code is clean, scalable, and synthesizes well for combinational 4-bit comparison.
