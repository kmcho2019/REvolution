Simplify the 4-bit comparator by avoiding the explicit borrow chain and subtraction bit computations. Instead, directly use the Verilog subtraction operator and check the carry-out (borrow) by performing a subtraction with a wider bit-width, capturing the borrow as the MSB. Then use the subtraction result and borrow to determine if A is less, equal, or greater than B.

Equality can be checked simply with an XOR reduction of A and B. This method reduces code complexity and resources while maintaining the required mutually exclusive outputs and purely combinational logic.
