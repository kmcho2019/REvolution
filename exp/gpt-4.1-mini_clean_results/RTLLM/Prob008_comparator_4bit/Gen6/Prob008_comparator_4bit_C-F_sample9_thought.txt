Fusion Strategy:

- Adopt the standardized and clear borrow generation formula: borrow_out = (~A & B) | ((~(A ^ B)) & borrow_in), which balances clarity, efficiency, and synthesis friendliness.
- Implement borrow chain using a generate block for scalability, readability, and maintainability.
- Optimize equality detection with bitwise XNOR reduction (eq_bits = &(~(A ^ B))) for minimal logic depth and gate count.
- Exclude difference calculation since it's not needed for output determination, simplifying the design and reducing unnecessary logic.
- Ensure outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) are mutually exclusive using borrow and equality signals only.
- This combination provides a clean, efficient, and highly synthesizable 4-bit comparator in pure combinational logic.

This fusion preserves clarity and efficiency, yielding a design easy to understand, maintain, and synthesize with good power, area, and performance trade-offs.
