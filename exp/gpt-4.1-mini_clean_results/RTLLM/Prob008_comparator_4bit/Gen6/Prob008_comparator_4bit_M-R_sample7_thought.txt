Refactoring Strategy:

- Retain the borrow-based subtraction logic concept but implement the borrow chain explicitly via combinational continuous assignments rather than a generate loop, for clearer step-by-step signal flow.
- Introduce intermediate wires for borrow signals at each bit explicitly, making the logic easier to follow without generate blocks.
- Use bitwise XNOR followed by an AND reduction for equality detection as before.
- Compute outputs using the final borrow and equality signals, ensuring mutual exclusivity.
- This refactor keeps the combinational, synthesizable nature of the design but uses explicit naming and direct assignment for all intermediate signals, improving readability and debuggability.
