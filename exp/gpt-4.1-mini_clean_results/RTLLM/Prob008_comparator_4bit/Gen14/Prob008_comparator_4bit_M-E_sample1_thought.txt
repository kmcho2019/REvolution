Implement the 4-bit comparator by explicitly performing a 4-bit subtraction (A - B) using combinational full subtractors chained together to generate borrow signals. Each bit subtraction produces a difference bit and a borrow out, which feeds into the next bit subtractor. The borrow out from the MSB indicates if A < B (borrow = 1 means A < B). If borrow is zero and the difference is zero, then A = B; if borrow is zero and difference is non-zero, then A > B.
