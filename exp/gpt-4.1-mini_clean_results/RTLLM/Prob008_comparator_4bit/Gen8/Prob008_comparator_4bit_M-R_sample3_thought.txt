2. Implement borrow logic calculation in a more compact expression using a function that models borrow lookahead, making it clearer and reusable.
3. Use intermediate wires for borrow chain stages with a simple for-generate loop for borrow propagation signals to illustrate another structural style.
4. Explicitly show the borrow chain as a recursive propagate-generate borrow calculation rather than fully expanded expressions.
5. Maintain balanced logic but with clearer hierarchical borrow calculation for easier scaling.
6. Outputs remain mutually exclusive, with A_less = borrow out, A_equal from overall equality, and A_greater as neither less nor equal.

This refactor showcases alternative style using functions and simpler equality logic without generate, and structured borrow calculation via looped stages, which is another clear combinational style maintaining the original functionality.
