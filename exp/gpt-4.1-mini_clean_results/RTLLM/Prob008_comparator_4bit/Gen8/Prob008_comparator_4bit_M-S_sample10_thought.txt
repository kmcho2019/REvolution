Implement the 4-bit comparator by first performing a combinational subtraction of A - B using full subtractors at each bit, generating difference bits and borrow signals. The borrow out of the MSB indicates if A < B (borrow=1 means A < B). If borrow=0 and all difference bits are zero, then A == B; else A > B. This approach uses a ripple borrow subtractor logic to directly produce the outputs, ensuring mutual exclusivity and minimal redundant logic.
