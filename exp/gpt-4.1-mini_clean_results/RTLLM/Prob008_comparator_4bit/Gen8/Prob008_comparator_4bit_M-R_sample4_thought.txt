Refactor the comparator_4bit by avoiding generate loops and expressing all bitwise operations explicitly to increase code readability and make synthesis tool optimizations more transparent. Instead of using generate blocks for per-bit equality, borrow generate and propagate, explicitly define them as simple assign statements for each bit. Also, the borrow out logic will be directly expanded without intermediate signals for clarity.

The equality check can be kept as a reduction AND of bitwise XNOR of inputs, but instead of storing in eq_bits array, assign them directly to wires eq0 to eq3. Similarly, borrow generate and propagate signals are explicitly declared as single-bit wires.

This structure makes the code easier to trace signal-by-signal, facilitates debug and learning, and retains all the combinational borrow-lookahead and equality logic.

Outputs remain mutually exclusive:
- A_less: asserted when borrow_out is 1,
- A_equal: asserted when all bits equal,
- A_greater: asserted otherwise (not borrow_out and not all_equal).

No procedural or sequential blocks used, preserving purely combinational logic.
