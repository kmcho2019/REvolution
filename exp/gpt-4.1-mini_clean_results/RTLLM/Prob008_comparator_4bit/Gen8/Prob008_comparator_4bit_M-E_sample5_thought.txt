Instead of using borrow lookahead subtraction logic, I will implement the comparator by cascading 1-bit comparison blocks for each bit, starting from the MSB to LSB. Each 1-bit comparator compares the corresponding bits and takes into account the result of higher significance bits to decide the final output.

For each bit position, three signals are generated: bit_greater, bit_equal, bit_less. Then the outputs from the most significant bit comparator are combined with the lower bits by propagating equality conditions.

This bit-level hierarchical comparison ensures clear priority from the most significant bit down to the least significant bit, reflecting natural magnitude comparison. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be mutually exclusive by construction.

This architecture uses purely combinational logic with assign statements, no subtraction or borrow logic. It also clearly separates the comparison by bits, making debugging and extension easier.
