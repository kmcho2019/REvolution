Implement a 4-bit comparator by performing a 4-bit subtraction (A - B) using combinational logic full subtractors bit by bit with borrow signals. Track the borrow chain across the bits: if borrow out of the MSB is 1, then A < B; if borrow out is 0 and the subtraction result is zero, then A == B; otherwise A > B. This uses an explicit subtractor architecture to directly reflect the problem's subtraction and borrow-based comparison. The outputs are mutually exclusive by logic construction.
