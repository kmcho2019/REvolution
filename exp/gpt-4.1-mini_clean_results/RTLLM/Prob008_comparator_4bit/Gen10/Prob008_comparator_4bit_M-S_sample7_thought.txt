To simplify the 4-bit comparator design, I'll directly compare A and B using their difference's borrow logic but without explicit borrow lookahead logic. Instead, I'll leverage Verilog's subtraction operator in a combinational manner to detect borrow (underflow) and zero result for equality.

- Perform A - B with a 5-bit result to capture the borrow via the MSB.
- If borrow (MSB) is 1, then A < B.
- If result is zero, then A == B.
- Otherwise, A > B.

This approach eliminates multiple wire signals and complex borrow generate/propagate logic, making the design more straightforward and easier to read while maintaining the mutually exclusive outputs and combinational behavior.
