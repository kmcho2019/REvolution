Implement a 4-bit comparator by cascading four 1-bit comparator modules for each bit position, starting from the MSB to the LSB.

- Each 1-bit comparator compares a bit pair and takes into account previous bits' results (greater, equal, less).
- The 1-bit comparator outputs signals indicating whether A is greater, equal, or less considering bits at and above its position.
- The MSB comparator decides the final result directly for MSB bits, and lower bits only matter if higher bits are equal.
- This cascaded comparator approach creates a hierarchical comparison structure, fully combinational, clear, and easy to extend.
- It leverages simple bitwise comparisons and priority logic to propagate comparison status from MSB to LSB.
- Outputs are guaranteed mutually exclusive because at each step only one of greater, equal, or less is asserted.
- No subtraction or borrow logic needed; this approach bases comparison directly on magnitude relations.
