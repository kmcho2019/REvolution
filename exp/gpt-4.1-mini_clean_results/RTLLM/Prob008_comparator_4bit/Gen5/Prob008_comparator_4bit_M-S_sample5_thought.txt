Simplify the 4-bit comparator by removing the subtraction and borrow chain logic, and instead directly compare inputs bit by bit from MSB to LSB to determine greater, equal, or less conditions. 

Approach:
- Compare bits starting from the most significant bit (bit 3) down to bit 0.
- For each bit:
  - If A[i] > B[i], then A > B.
  - If A[i] < B[i], then A < B.
  - If bits are equal, continue checking lower bits.
- If all bits are equal, then A == B.
  
This avoids implementing explicit subtraction and borrow chains, greatly reducing complexity and combinational depth.

Implementation:
- Use a combinational always_comb block or continuous assignments for priority encoding of bitwise comparison results.
- Ensure outputs are mutually exclusive.
- The comparator is purely combinational and easy to verify.

This approach simplifies logic, reduces area and delay, and directly satisfies the problem requirements.
