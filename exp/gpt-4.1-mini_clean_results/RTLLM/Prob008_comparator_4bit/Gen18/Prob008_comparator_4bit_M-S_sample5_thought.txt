We can simplify the 4-bit comparator by directly comparing bits from MSB to LSB without explicitly computing borrow chains. At each bit, if A[i] > B[i], A_greater is 1; if A[i] < B[i], A_less is 1; if bits are equal, continue checking lower bits. If all bits are equal, then A_equal is 1.

This approach uses straightforward combinational priority encoding and bitwise equality, eliminating intermediate borrow signals and complex borrow logic. The outputs remain mutually exclusive and combinational.

This reduces the number of intermediate signals and simplifies the design while maintaining the correct functionality.
