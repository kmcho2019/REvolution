Implement a 4-bit comparator by explicitly performing a 4-bit subtraction A - B using combinational full subtractors at each bit. Each subtractor produces a difference bit and a borrow out bit. The final borrow out indicates if A < B. If no borrow out and the difference is zero, A = B; else, A > B. This approach directly follows the subtraction and borrow logic hinted in the problem statement, using bit-level subtractors cascaded to generate borrows and difference bits combinationally.
