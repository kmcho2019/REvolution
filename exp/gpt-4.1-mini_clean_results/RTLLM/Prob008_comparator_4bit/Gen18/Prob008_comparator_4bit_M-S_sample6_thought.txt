To simplify the 4-bit comparator while maintaining functionality, I will remove the explicit borrow chain and instead rely on a direct subtraction and comparison approach. Specifically, I will:

- Compute A minus B using the built-in subtraction operator and check the sign (borrow) and zero result.
- Determine A_equal by comparing A and B using simple equality operator.
- Determine A_less by checking if A < B.
- Determine A_greater as the complement of A_equal and A_less.

This approach removes the explicit per-bit borrow calculation, makes the code more concise and readable, and keeps all logic combinational. It relies on synthesis tools to efficiently map the subtraction and comparisons into optimized combinational logic. Outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction.

This simplification reduces explicit intermediate signals and logic gate description while maintaining clear, correct functionality.
