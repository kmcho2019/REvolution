Implement the 4-bit comparator using a cascade of bit-level comparators starting from the most significant bit (MSB). At each bit level, compare A[i] and B[i]. If A[i] > B[i], then A_greater = 1; if A[i] < B[i], then A_less = 1. If bits are equal, proceed to the next less significant bit. If all bits are equal, then A_equal = 1. This approach avoids subtractor borrow chains and uses purely combinational comparison logic with priority encoding.
