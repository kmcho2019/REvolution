Simplify the 4-bit comparator by directly comparing each bit of A and B from MSB to LSB using combinational logic. For each bit, if A[i] > B[i], A is greater; if A[i] < B[i], A is less. If all bits are equal, then A equals B. This removes the need for explicit subtraction and borrow chain, making the design simpler and faster while preserving mutual exclusivity of outputs.
