To optimize the original 4-bit comparator using borrow chain subtraction, I will simplify and improve the equality detection by implementing bitwise XNOR gates followed by an AND reduction instead of the previous equality check. This reduces logic complexity and switching. 

For the borrow chain, I will correct the borrow generation formula to standard full subtractor form:
borrow_out = (~A & B) | ((~A ^ B) & borrow_in)
which is a well-known borrow propagation condition, improving clarity and correctness of borrow signals.

I will keep the bit-level subtraction logic within a generate loop for scalability and maintainability.

By separating equality detection from borrow logic and using clean signals, the outputs remain mutually exclusive and the critical path is simplified.

Overall, the improvement reduces gate count for equality detection, uses standard borrow logic formula for better synthesis and delay, and keeps a clean structure for good PPA balance.
