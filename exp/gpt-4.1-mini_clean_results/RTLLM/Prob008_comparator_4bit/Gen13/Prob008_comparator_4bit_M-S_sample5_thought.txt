Simplify the 4-bit comparator by removing explicit borrow generate and propagate signals and directly use the subtraction result to determine the relation between A and B. Since A - B is a 4-bit subtraction, if the result is zero, inputs are equal; if the subtraction underflows (borrow out is 1), A is less than B; otherwise, A is greater. Using a subtraction operation and its borrow out in combinational logic reduces complexity and improves readability. This also ensures mutually exclusive outputs.
