To improve the original design's PPA metrics:

1. Simplify equality detection by directly using a single 4-bit XNOR reduction with the & operator instead of intermediate signals. This slightly reduces gate count and wiring complexity.

2. Use a ripple borrow approach instead of a full carry-lookahead borrow logic. This reduces logic complexity and area at the expense of slightly increased worst-case delay, which for a 4-bit comparator is acceptable.

3. Optimize internal signal usage by combining borrow computations inline without intermediate wires.

4. Remove redundant intermediate wires to save area and power.

5. Retain mutual exclusivity of outputs and fully combinational logic.

Overall, this yields a simpler and smaller design with lower power and area, with only modest timing impact acceptable at this scale.

The design will:
- Compute bitwise borrow (g) and propagate (p).
- Ripple borrow from LSB to MSB.
- Compute equality using a direct bitwise reduction of XNORed A and B.
- Outputs mutually exclusive signals based on borrow_out and equality.
