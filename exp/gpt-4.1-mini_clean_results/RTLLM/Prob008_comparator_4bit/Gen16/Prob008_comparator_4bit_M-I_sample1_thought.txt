The original design is well-structured with a balanced borrow lookahead and equality detection. To improve power and area, I will:

- Simplify borrow lookahead logic by sharing intermediate terms more efficiently, reducing the number of AND gates.
- Combine repeated propagate terms into intermediate signals for reuse to minimize gate count.
- Use a single 4-input AND gate for equality detection instead of a balanced tree, reducing gate overhead and possibly area.
- Keep output logic mutually exclusive as before.

These changes reduce gate count and switching power with minimal or no impact on timing, given the small bit width.
