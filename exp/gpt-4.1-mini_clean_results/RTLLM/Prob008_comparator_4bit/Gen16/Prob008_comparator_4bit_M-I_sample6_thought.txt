The existing design correctly implements a 4-bit comparator using a ripple borrow chain and a balanced AND-tree for equality detection. While the ripple borrow chain is simple, its delay is linear with bit-width, which is acceptable for 4 bits but can be improved by implementing a parallel borrow logic using direct combinational expressions for borrow generation per bit without ripple propagation.

Improvement strategy:
- Replace the ripple borrow chain with direct borrow generation expressions for each bit based on the borrow lookahead principle:
  borrow_out[i] = (~A[i] & B[i]) | ((B[i] ^ A[i]) & borrow_in)
  For 1-bit subtraction, borrow = (~A & B) | ((~(A ^ B)) & borrow_in) simplifies similarly.
- Since the borrow chain for 4 bits can be expressed with combinational logic without ripple, use combinational borrow logic that computes all borrows in parallel, improving timing.
- Keep the balanced AND-tree for equality detection for fast equality check.
- Outputs remain mutually exclusive.
- This reduces the critical path in borrow propagation improving timing while maintaining low area and power.

This approach trades a slight increase in logic complexity for better performance, especially timing, while preserving correctness and mutual exclusivity of outputs.
