Use a fully parallel bitwise comparator structure that compares each bit from MSB to LSB independently and cascades the results combinationally to determine the final outputs.

Approach:
- For each bit from 3 down to 0, generate three signals:
  * gt[i] = 1 if A[i] > B[i]
  * eq[i] = 1 if A[i] == B[i]
  * lt[i] = 1 if A[i] < B[i]

- Using a hierarchical logic approach, determine the final outputs:
  * A_greater = gt[3] | (eq[3] & gt[2]) | (eq[3] & eq[2] & gt[1]) | (eq[3] & eq[2] & eq[1] & gt[0])
  * A_less = lt[3] | (eq[3] & lt[2]) | (eq[3] & eq[2] & lt[1]) | (eq[3] & eq[2] & eq[1] & lt[0])
  * A_equal = eq[3] & eq[2] & eq[1] & eq[0]

This method uses explicit bit-level comparison signals and a priority encoder style approach that mirrors the natural lexicographical comparison of binary numbers.

The outputs are guaranteed mutually exclusive by construction because only one of greater, less, or equal can be true at once.

This approach avoids borrow and carry logic entirely, uses simple combinational gates, and directly encodes comparator semantics. It is clear, efficient, and easily synthesizable.
