I will simplify the comparator by directly performing the subtraction A - B and inspecting the borrow out and the result. Using the 4-bit subtraction's borrow out signal indicates if A < B (borrow out = 1), if the subtraction result is zero then A == B, else A > B.

Instead of borrow lookahead logic, I will implement a ripple borrow subtractor chain for clarity and simplicity. Each bit subtracts with borrow from previous stage and produces the difference bit and next borrow.

Finally, outputs will be assigned mutually exclusively based on borrow out and zero result.

This method simplifies logic without extra propagate/generate signals, keeping functionality intact and clear for 4 bits.
