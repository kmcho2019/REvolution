Optimize the 4-bit comparator by replacing the subtraction-with-borrow chain with direct hierarchical bit-wise comparison from the most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB). This eliminates the linear borrow chain critical path, improving performance. Use combinational logic to determine if A > B, A == B, or A < B by checking bits in descending order: if A[i] > B[i], then A > B; if A[i] < B[i], then A < B; if equal, continue to next bit. This approach reduces logic depth, toggling activity (less intermediate signals), and gate count, thus improving timing, power, and area. The outputs remain mutually exclusive and combinational.
