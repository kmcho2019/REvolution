To optimize the 4-bit comparator for better PPA (power, performance, area), I will replace the ripple borrow subtractor chain with a borrow lookahead logic, significantly reducing the critical path delay. 

Instead of calculating difference bits, I will directly compute the borrow generate and propagate signals per bit and use these to determine if A < B. This avoids computing the full subtraction result, thus saving logic area and power.

For equality, I will simply compare A and B with a bitwise XNOR and AND reduction.

The outputs remain mutually exclusive:
- A_less is set if borrow out (borrow[3]) is asserted via borrow lookahead.
- A_equal is set if A and B are equal.
- A_greater is set if neither less nor equal is asserted.

This approach reduces gate count, power consumption, and delays from ripple borrow and difference computation, improving overall PPA.
