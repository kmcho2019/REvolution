Implement the 4-bit comparator by decomposing the comparison into per-bit comparisons from MSB to LSB using bit-level comparators combined with priority logic. For each bit position, determine if A[i] > B[i], A[i] < B[i], or A[i] == B[i]. The overall comparison results depend on the highest-order bit where A and B differ. If no difference is found across all bits, then A == B. This approach avoids subtraction and borrow chains, replacing arithmetic with parallel bitwise comparisons and priority logic to determine the first differing bit, which can yield faster combinational paths and simpler control logic.
