I will implement the 4-bit comparator using a bitwise hierarchical comparison approach, where each bit comparison produces signals indicating whether the bits of A and B at that position are equal, A is greater, or A is less. 

Starting from the most significant bit (MSB), the outputs are determined by:
- If bits differ, that bit alone defines the comparison result.
- If bits are equal, the decision defers to the next lower bit.

This approach mirrors a priority encoder logic and is combinational. It avoids subtraction and borrow logic entirely, instead leveraging direct bit-level comparisons chained from MSB to LSB. 

This design naturally ensures mutual exclusivity of outputs since only one path determines the result based on the highest differing bit.

Equality is when all bits are equal.

This method is conceptually simple and directly interpretable from the bit comparisons.
