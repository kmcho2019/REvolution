Implement a 4-bit comparator module by performing the subtraction A - B using combinational logic with full subtractors for each bit. The borrow out of the MSB indicates whether A is less than B (borrow out = 1 means A < B). If no borrow occurs and the result is zero, then A equals B. If no borrow and result is non-zero, then A is greater than B. Generate mutually exclusive output signals accordingly.
