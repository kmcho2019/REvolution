Implement a 4-bit comparator using combinational subtractor logic at the bit-level. For each bit, calculate the difference bit and borrow-out using the borrow-in from the previous less significant bit. After the subtraction (A - B), if a borrow out of the most significant bit occurs, A is less than B. If no borrow and the result is zero, A equals B. If no borrow and the result is non-zero, A is greater than B. Outputs are mutually exclusive and combinational.
