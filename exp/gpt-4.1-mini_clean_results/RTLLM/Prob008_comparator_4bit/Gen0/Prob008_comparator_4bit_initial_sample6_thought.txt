Implement a 4-bit comparator by performing a combinational subtraction A - B using a chain of 1-bit subtractors with borrow. Track the borrow out of the most significant bit: if borrow_out is 1, then A < B. If borrow_out is 0, and the subtraction result is zero, then A == B. If borrow_out is 0 and the subtraction result is nonzero, then A > B. Outputs are mutually exclusive accordingly.
