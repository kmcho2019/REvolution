Implement the traffic light as a Moore state machine with explicit states for red, yellow, and green, each associated with a fixed timer loaded when entering the state. Use separate state and timer registers updated synchronously at clock edges. On pedestrian request during green state, if the remaining green time exceeds 10 cycles, clamp it to 10 cycles immediately. Outputs are registered signals updated each clock based on the current state. This clear separation of state transition and output logic improves modularity and traceability, and uses synchronous reset for predictable initialization.
