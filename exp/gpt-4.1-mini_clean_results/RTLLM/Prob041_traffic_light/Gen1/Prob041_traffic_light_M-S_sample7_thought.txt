Implement a straightforward FSM with states s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. Each state lasts a fixed number of clock cycles: red=10, yellow=5, green=60. The counter counts down each clock cycle. When the pedestrian pass_request is asserted during green and remaining green time > 10, shorten the green time to 10. Transition order: red -> green -> yellow -> red, continuously. Outputs directly reflect the state without using separate previous signal registers, simplifying the design.
