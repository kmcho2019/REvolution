Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore FSM with four states: IDLE, RED, GREEN, YELLOW. Use a single 8-bit countdown timer initialized based on the current state's duration. The pedestrian button affects the green state's timer by shortening the remaining green time to 10 clocks if it is greater than 10. This shortening is handled by monitoring the timer value and the pass_request signal, ensuring that the timer never exceeds 10 during green when a request is active. All state transitions occur when the timer expires (timer reaches zero). The outputs (red, yellow, green) are registered signals updated at state transitions, and the current timer value is output as clock[7:0]. The reset sets the FSM to IDLE with all lights off and a timer of 0. The clock counter is decremented on each clock cycle. This approach ensures a clean separation between state, timer, and outputs, with a simple, easy-to-read FSM and timer logic.
