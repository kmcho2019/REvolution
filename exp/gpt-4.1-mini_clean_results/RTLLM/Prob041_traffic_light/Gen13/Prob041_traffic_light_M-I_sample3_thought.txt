The original solution is functionally correct and meets the specification well. To further optimize for power and area (PPA) without sacrificing readability or functionality, I propose the following improvements:

1. Reduce the width of the timer register and output from 8 bits to 7 bits since max count is 60, saving one bit of register and logic area.
2. Integrate timer decrement only when the timer is not zero, reducing toggling and power consumption.
3. Replace explicit shortening logic in combinational block with a saturating min operation to simplify timer update logic.
4. Combine state and timer updates into a single synchronous block with nonblocking assignments to minimize flip-flop usage and simplify timing.
5. Derive outputs purely from the state to avoid extra registers for outputs and reduce latency.
6. Use `localparam` for state and timing values for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
7. Remove unused states like IDLE to save encoding space and simplify FSM.

These changes lead to a cleaner, more compact FSM with minimal register and logic overhead, reduce switching activity in the timer counter, and maintain the required traffic light timing and pedestrian button shortening behavior.
