Implement the traffic light as a simple FSM with three states (RED, YELLOW, GREEN) and a counter that counts down the duration of each state. On pedestrian button press during the GREEN state, if the remaining time is more than 10, shorten it to 10. Outputs are driven directly from the current state. Use a single always block for synchronous updates to state and counter. This approach reduces complexity by removing extra registers and multiple always blocks while maintaining required functionality.
