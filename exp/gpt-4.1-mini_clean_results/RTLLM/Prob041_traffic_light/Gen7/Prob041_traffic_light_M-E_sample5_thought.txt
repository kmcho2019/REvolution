Implement a traffic light controller using an explicit Moore FSM with separate states for red, yellow, and green, each with a fixed cycle count loaded into a countdown counter at state entry. Unlike the previous solution, here we track the elapsed green time using a separate counter and dynamically reduce the green phase on a pedestrian request by forcing a transition when the remaining green time exceeds 10 cycles; otherwise, let it finish normally. The shortening is handled by immediate transition when the request arrives with more than 10 cycles left, thus no extra flag is needed. Use a synchronous reset and single process for state and timer updates. Outputs are driven directly from the state. This simplifies logic by removing the shortening flag, trading some timing flexibility for architectural clarity.
