Fuse the strengths of both designs by using a binary encoded FSM with clean, simple state transitions and separate state register from counter register to improve timing and clarity. Use synchronous state and counter update with next-state and next-counter combinational logic. Implement pedestrian request logic inside the next counter logic to clamp green time to 10 if requested and remaining green time > 10. Drive outputs directly from the state register to reduce register count and power. This approach keeps simple control and timing logic, minimizes combinational dependencies in sequential blocks, and avoids unnecessary flip-flop switching from one-hot encoding, achieving better area and power while maintaining clear, scalable code. We also register outputs combinationally (nonblocking) inside the sequential always block for glitch-free outputs.
