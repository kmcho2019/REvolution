Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore FSM with explicit states for each light color and countdown timer managed by a single 8-bit counter counting up to the state's duration. Use a separate register to hold the current state's duration (max count). On each clock, increment the counter until it reaches the duration, then transition to the next state. The pedestrian button logic is handled by adjusting the max count for the green state when pressed if the time left is more than 10. Store the current elapsed count and calculate the remaining time dynamically to decide whether to shorten green. Outputs are directly encoded from the state. This design counts up rather than down and uses a max_count register to represent state duration, improving clarity and enabling easy time adjustment.
