Optimize the original FSM by reducing timer width from 8 bits to 7 bits since the maximum timer value is 60, fitting within 7 bits. Introduce clock gating for the timer counter to decrement only when the timer is non-zero, reducing switching activity and power. Also, limit the pedestrian request check only to the green state to minimize unnecessary logic toggling. Use one synchronous always block for state, timer, and ped_shortened updates to simplify logic and reduce flip-flops. Keep outputs directly derived combinationally from the current state for clarity and low latency. These improvements reduce area by shrinking the counter bitwidth, lower dynamic power by reducing counter toggling, and maintain original functionality.
