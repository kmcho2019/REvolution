To improve the PPA of the given traffic light controller, I will implement clock enable gating for the counter to reduce unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. The counter will only decrement or reload when needed, avoiding countdown during steady states without activity. I will also optimize the state encoding by using one-hot encoding to reduce combinational logic delay in state decoding, potentially improving max frequency. The outputs will be generated directly from the one-hot state bits, simplifying output logic. Finally, the state machine and counter logic will be kept clean and modular, maintaining correctness while improving performance and reducing power.
