The original solution is already clear and efficient, but there are some small improvements possible to further reduce area and power without sacrificing clarity or functionality:
1. Remove the ped_shortened flag and replace it with a direct logic check on the counter and pass_request to reduce one register and related logic.
2. Use synchronous reset and always_ff style coding for clarity and better synthesis support.
3. Use an 8-bit counter as requested, which simplifies interfacing and matches the output width.
4. Minimize combinational logic by using simple one-hot output assignments and by directly gating decrement on counter > 0.
5. Make outputs registers updated only on clock edge (Moore outputs) to reduce glitches and transitions, lowering power.
6. Remove redundant default case assignments by initializing signals properly.
7. Keep the state machine and timing constants but encode states with localparam for readability.

This approach reduces register count by one, simplifies combinational logic, and makes the output signals registered, reducing glitches and toggling on outputs, thus improving power. The 8-bit counter matches the spec output width directly.

Overall, this should maintain the same functionality and timing while improving power and slightly reducing area.
