Implement a straightforward finite state machine with three states representing red, yellow, and green lights. Use a single counter to track the remaining time in each state. On each clock cycle, decrement the counter if not zero. When the counter reaches zero, transition to the next state and reload its corresponding time. If a pedestrian button is pressed during green and remaining green time is more than 10, immediately set the counter to 10. Derive output signals directly from the current state, and assign the counter to the output clock. Avoid extra registers for previous outputs to keep the design simple yet fully functional.
