Use a straightforward three-state FSM with a single 8-bit timer counting down. The FSM transitions RED -> GREEN -> YELLOW -> RED. Pedestrian requests shorten the GREEN timer to 10 if it is above 10 and not shortened yet. Keep a flag to ensure shortening happens only once per green phase. Timer decrements each clock if non-zero. Outputs are combinationally driven by the current state. This keeps the design simple, clear, and fully functional with minimal logic.
