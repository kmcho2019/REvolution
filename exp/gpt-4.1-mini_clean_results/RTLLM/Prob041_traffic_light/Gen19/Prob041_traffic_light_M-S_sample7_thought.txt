Simplify the traffic light controller by removing the ped_shortened flag and incorporating pedestrian button logic directly into the timer update. Use a 2-bit state FSM with simple enumerations and an 8-bit counter. When the pedestrian button is pressed during green and remaining green time is more than 10, immediately set timer to 10. Timer counts down every clock cycle if non-zero. States transition when timer hits zero. Outputs are registered signals updated at clock edge for glitch avoidance. This reduces complexity by merging timer shortening logic and eliminates extra flags while keeping the requested functionality and timing.
