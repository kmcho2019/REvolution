Implement a Moore FSM with four explicit states: IDLE, RED, GREEN, YELLOW. Use a separate 8-bit countdown timer that reloads preset durations at each state entry. To handle the pedestrian pass_request, include a flag that, when pressed during GREEN state and remaining time > 10, reduces the countdown to 10. The countdown decrements every clock cycle and triggers state transitions when it reaches zero. Use combinational logic to determine next state and countdown reload values. Outputs (red, yellow, green) are driven by the current state. To avoid glitches, outputs are registered synchronously with the clock and asynchronous active-low reset. The counter reload and pass_request shortening logic occur synchronously, ensuring timing stability and clear separation of logic. The IDLE state initializes outputs and immediately transitions to RED for deterministic startup. This design is modular, explicit, and easily extensible for additional features.
