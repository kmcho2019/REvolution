Implement a traffic light controller using a Moore FSM with explicit states for each light phase: RED, GREEN, and YELLOW. The controller maintains a countdown timer to track the remaining time in each state. The pedestrian button influences the GREEN phase by allowing early transition to YELLOW if the remaining green time is more than 10 clocks, otherwise the green phase completes normally. The counter loads the duration of each state upon entering that state, and counts down every clock cycle. Outputs (red, yellow, green) are combinationally driven by the current state for immediate response. The clock count is output to show the countdown timer. This design uses simple synchronous state transitions and a counter, keeping the logic clear and easily modifiable.
