Implement the traffic light controller as a binary-encoded FSM with explicit states `idle`, `s1_red`, `s2_yellow`, and `s3_green`. The FSM state register and the output logic are separated for clarity. The counter counts down from the preset time associated with each state. To handle the pedestrian button press, when in the green state and pass_request is asserted, the remaining green time is shortened to 10 clocks if it currently exceeds 10 clocks; otherwise, it remains unchanged. This uses a next-state and next-counter combinational logic to manage timing and transitions. The outputs (red, yellow, green) are driven combinationally based on the current state. This design uses clear state encoding, separate synchronous and combinational always blocks, and a concise counter logic ensuring robust and easy-to-follow functionality while meeting all requirements.
