Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore FSM with four states: IDLE (initial), RED, GREEN, YELLOW. The FSM transitions through these states in order: RED -> GREEN -> YELLOW -> RED.

A single 8-bit down counter tracks the remaining time in each state. Upon entering a state, the counter is loaded with the full duration: RED=10, GREEN=60, YELLOW=5.

To handle pedestrian requests, when in GREEN and pass_request is asserted, if the remaining count is above 10, clamp it immediately to 10, effectively shortening green to a minimum of 10 cycles remaining.

The outputs red/yellow/green are driven directly from the state (Moore outputs).

To simplify timing and enhance reliability, the counter only decrements when >0. State transitions happen only when the counter reaches 0.

The IDLE state is transient: on reset, the controller goes to IDLE with all outputs zero, then moves immediately to RED state on next clock cycle.

This design uses a clean FSM with synchronous state and counter updates, clear separation of state and output logic, and avoids any extra flags or multi-cycle enable signals, optimizing for clarity and maintainability.
