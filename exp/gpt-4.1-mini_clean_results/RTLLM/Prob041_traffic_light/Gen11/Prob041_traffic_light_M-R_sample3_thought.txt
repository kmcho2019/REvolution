Refactor the traffic light controller into a Mealy FSM where outputs and timer are updated together in a single always block triggered by clock and reset. Use a single register for state and timer combined with next-state and next-timer signals computed combinationally in a separate always @(*) block. This reduces the number of sequential always blocks to one, combining state and timer updates atomically, improving synchronization and simplifying output assignments by registering output signals directly from the next-state outputs rather than purely combinational assign statements. The pedestrian request logic remains within the combinational logic to clamp the green timer early.
