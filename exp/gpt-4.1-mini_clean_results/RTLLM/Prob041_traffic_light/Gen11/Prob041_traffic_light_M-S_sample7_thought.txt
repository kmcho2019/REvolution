Simplify the traffic light FSM by removing the separate previous signal registers and merging the timer management and state transitions into one synchronous always block. Use a single counter that counts down according to the current state's duration. On pedestrian request, if in the green state and remaining time is greater than 10, shorten the timer immediately to 10. Outputs are directly derived from the current state. This eliminates extra combinational logic and registers, achieving simpler code with the same functionality.
