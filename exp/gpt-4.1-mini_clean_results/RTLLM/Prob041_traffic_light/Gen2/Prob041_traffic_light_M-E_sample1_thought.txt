Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore FSM with explicit states and durations encoded. Use a single timer counter that loads the state's full duration on state entry. For the green state, if a pedestrian request is detected, and the remaining green time is more than 10 cycles, clamp the counter to 10 cycles; otherwise, keep counting down normally. This clamping only happens once per green phase (use a flag to track if shortening has been applied). Transition between states when the counter reaches zero. Drive outputs directly from the state. Reset asynchronously and initialize to red state with full red duration. This design prevents oscillation in the green timer by tracking if the pedestrian request shortening has already been applied.
