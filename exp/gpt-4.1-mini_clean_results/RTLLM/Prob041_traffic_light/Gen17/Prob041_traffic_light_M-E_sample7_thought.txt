Implement a Moore finite state machine with four states representing the traffic light phases: RED, GREEN, YELLOW, and WAIT_FOR_SHORTEN (to manage the pedestrian request). Use a single 8-bit down counter that is initialized according to the state duration. The pedestrian button only affects the GREEN state's duration: if pressed and more than 10 cycles remain, shorten to 10. Store a flag indicating whether the green time has been shortened to prevent repeated shortening. The pedestrian request is sampled synchronously and cleared once the green phase ends. Outputs are registered and driven directly from the state. The FSM updates state and counter synchronously on clock edges with asynchronous reset. This approach cleanly separates timing and control, avoids combinational next-state logic dependencies on timer countdown within the same cycle, and uses a dedicated flag to handle one-time green time shortening.
