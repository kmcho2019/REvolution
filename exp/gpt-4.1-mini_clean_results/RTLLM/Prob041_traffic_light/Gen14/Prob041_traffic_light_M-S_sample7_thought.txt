Implement a straightforward FSM with four states representing the traffic light phases. Use one always block triggered by clk and rst_n to update state and timer counter. The counter loads the time corresponding to each state when entering it and counts down each cycle. On pedestrian request during green, shorten green timer to 10 if remaining time is more than 10. Output signals are directly driven from the current state, eliminating extra registers. This approach keeps logic minimal and clear while meeting all requirements.
