The original design is functional and clean but uses a flag register (shortened_flag) that toggles every cycle in green, which can add switching power. To optimize power, remove the shortened_flag by controlling the green timer purely with combinational logic that clamps the timer value once when pass_request is asserted and timer > 10, ensuring no multiple shortenings without extra state.

Also, separate state transition logic and timer update logic into two always blocks for clarity and better synthesis optimization.

Further, assign outputs from registered state for reduced glitches, and add clock enable for timer decrement to minimize toggling when timer=0.

These refinements reduce power through gating and remove an extra flip-flop, maintain area by avoiding extra logic, and improve timing by simplified logic paths.
