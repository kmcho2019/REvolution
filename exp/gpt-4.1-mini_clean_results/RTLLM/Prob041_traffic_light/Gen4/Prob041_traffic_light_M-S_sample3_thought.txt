Implement a simplified FSM with three states (red, yellow, green), using an 8-bit counter for timing. On reset, start at red with its duration. Transition states when the counter reaches zero. For the green state, if a pedestrian request arrives and the remaining green time is greater than 10, clamp the counter to 10 once per green phase using a simple flag. Outputs are driven directly by the state. This avoids extra next_state variables and multiple condition checks by integrating state transitions and counter updates in a single always block, reducing complexity while maintaining correct behavior.
