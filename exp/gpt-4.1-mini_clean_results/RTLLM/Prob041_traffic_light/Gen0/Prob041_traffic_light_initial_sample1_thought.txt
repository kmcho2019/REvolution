Implement a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. Use an 8-bit counter to track the timing in each state. On reset, initialize the counter to the red light duration (10). Transition through the states with appropriate timing: s1_red (red light for 10 cycles), s3_green (green light for 60 cycles, shortened to 10 if pedestrian button pressed and remaining green time is more than 10), s2_yellow (yellow light for 5 cycles). Control the counter decrement and reset based on state transitions and pedestrian request. Maintain previous output signals to detect rising and falling edges and initialize outputs. Assign counter value to clock output and outputs for red, yellow, green lights.
