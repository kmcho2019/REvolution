Implement a finite state machine (FSM) for the traffic light with states idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The counter (cnt) keeps track of the duration within each state. The green state's duration is normally 60 clocks, but can be shortened to 10 clocks if pass_request is pressed and remaining green time is more than 10. The counter reloads based on state transitions and button press conditions. Output signals are updated with the next values stored in p_red, p_yellow, p_green. The pedestrian light logic is implied through lane light states as per instructions.
