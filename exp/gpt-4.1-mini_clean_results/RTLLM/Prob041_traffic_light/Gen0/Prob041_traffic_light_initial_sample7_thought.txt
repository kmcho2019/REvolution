The design implements a traffic light controller with three states corresponding to red, yellow, and green lights. Each state has a specific duration controlled by an internal 8-bit counter. The pedestrian button can shorten the green light duration to 10 cycles if more than 10 cycles remain. State transitions occur when the counter reaches zero. Outputs red, yellow, and green represent the traffic light status, synchronized with the clock. A reset initializes the state machine and counter.
