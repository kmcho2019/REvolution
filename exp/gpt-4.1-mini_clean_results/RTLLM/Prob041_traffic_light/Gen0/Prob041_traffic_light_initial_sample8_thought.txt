Implement a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. 
The FSM controls red, yellow, and green outputs and a counter (cnt) which keeps track of timing for each state.
- In idle, immediately transition to s1_red.
- In s1_red, red light is on for 10 counts.
- In s3_green, green light is on for 60 counts, but if pass_request is received and remaining green time is > 10, shorten it to 10.
- In s2_yellow, yellow light is on for 5 counts.
We store previous outputs p_red, p_yellow, p_green to detect transitions and to initialize counter accordingly.
The counter decrements each clock cycle, with resets and adjustments as per description.
All outputs update on clk posedge or rst_n negedge.
Assign clock output to current cnt value.
