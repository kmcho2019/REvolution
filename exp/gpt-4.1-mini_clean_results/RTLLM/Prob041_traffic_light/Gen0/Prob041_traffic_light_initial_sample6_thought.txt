Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. 
In idle, initialize all signals to zero and immediately transition to s1_red. 
In s1_red, the red light is on, count 10 clocks, then move to s3_green. 
In s2_yellow, yellow light is on, count 5 clocks, then move to s1_red.
In s3_green, green light is on, count normally up to 60 clocks but if pass_request is pressed and remaining count is more than 10, shorten to 10, else remain unchanged. After the green duration ends, transition to s2_yellow.

Use an 8-bit counter that counts down. On reset, initialize to 10. Update the counter depending on current and previous states of light signals per the requirement.

Use three registers (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to hold the outputs, and update the output ports on clock or reset.

This FSM and counter logic satisfy the timing and pass_request shortening requirements.
