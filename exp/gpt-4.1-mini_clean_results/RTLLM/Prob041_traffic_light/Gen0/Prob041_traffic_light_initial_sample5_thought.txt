Implement a finite state machine (FSM) for traffic light control with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM controls red, yellow, green outputs accordingly, and a counter (cnt) keeps track of timing for each state.

- idle: all lights off, move immediately to s1_red.
- s1_red: red on, count for 10 cycles (sidewalk green), then go to s3_green.
- s3_green: green on, count down from 60 or shortened to 10 if pass_request pressed and remaining green time >10. Then move to s2_yellow.
- s2_yellow: yellow on, count for 5 cycles, then move to s1_red.

Use an 8-bit cnt counter to time states. On reset or state transitions, initialize cnt properly.

Use registers p_red, p_yellow, p_green to store next light signals and update outputs red, yellow, green on clock edge.

Handle pass_request by shortening green time to 10 cycles if remaining green time >10.
