Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore finite state machine with four distinct states: IDLE, RED, GREEN, YELLOW.  
- Use a single 8-bit counter to handle the timing of each state.  
- IDLE state initializes the system and immediately transitions to RED.  
- In RED, GREEN, and YELLOW states, the counter counts down from the respective state's duration.  
- When the pedestrian button (pass_request) is pressed during GREEN, the green duration is shortened to 10 cycles if more than 10 remain, by resetting the counter to 10.  
- Track whether the green phase has been shortened using a flag to avoid repeated shortening.  
- Outputs red, yellow, green are driven combinationally based on the current state.  
- The counter resets appropriately when transitioning between states.  
- The design uses synchronous reset and all sequential logic is on the clock's rising edge for consistent timing and synthesis friendliness.  
- The counter counts down on each clock cycle when enabled and zero triggers state transition.  
This architecture simplifies the logic by using a clear FSM with explicit states, counters, and flags, and avoids complex next-state logic by handling shortening in sequential logic to ensure only one shortening per green phase.
