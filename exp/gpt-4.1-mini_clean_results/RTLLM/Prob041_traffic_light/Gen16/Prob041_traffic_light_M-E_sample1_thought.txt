Implement a traffic light controller using a Moore finite state machine with four states: IDLE (initialization), RED, GREEN, and YELLOW. The state transitions and timing are controlled by a countdown timer that decrements every clock cycle. The pedestrian button shortens the green phase to 10 clock cycles if pressed and more than 10 cycles remain. Outputs (red, yellow, green) are derived directly from the current state. To track shortening of green, introduce a flag that locks the shortened green time once set. The module outputs the current countdown value. All state and timer updates occur synchronously on the clock edge with asynchronous active-low reset.
