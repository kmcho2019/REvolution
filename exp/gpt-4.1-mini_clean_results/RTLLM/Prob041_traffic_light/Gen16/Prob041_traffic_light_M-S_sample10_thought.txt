Implement a simplified FSM with three states (red, yellow, green) and an 8-bit countdown timer. Use a synchronous always block to update state and timer on each clock edge. Handle pedestrian button by shortening the green timer to 10 if remaining time is above 10. Outputs are assigned combinationally from the state. This removes redundant next-state and next-timer registers and previous signals, reducing complexity while preserving all required functionality.
