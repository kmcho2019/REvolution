To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will make the following improvements:

1. Change the output `clock` to be a wire directly assigned from the counter register to avoid unnecessary combinational logic.
2. Reduce the counter width from 7 bits to 6 bits since the maximum count is 60 (which fits in 6 bits), saving some area.
3. Replace the ped_shortened combinational logic with a synchronous set/reset flip-flop updated only on the clock edge, simplifying combinational paths.
4. Optimize the next counter logic by factoring out common decrements and making the shortening check simple.
5. Use non-blocking assignments consistently in always blocks.
6. Encode states with localparams clearly named, keep the logic clean and easy to follow.

These changes keep functionality intact while slightly reducing logic complexity and area, improving timing by simplifying combinational logic, and reducing unnecessary combinational assignments for outputs.
