Implement the traffic light controller as a Moore FSM with four states: idle, red, green, and yellow. Use a single 8-bit down-counter to track the remaining time in the current state. At reset, start in idle and immediately transition to red with a 10-cycle timer. For the green state, start the counter at 60 and decrement each cycle. If the pedestrian button is pressed and the remaining green time exceeds 10, clamp it down to 10 cycles, otherwise leave it unchanged. Transition between states when the counter reaches zero: red → green, green → yellow, yellow → red. Outputs (red, yellow, green) are asserted based on the current state. This clean separation of states with a dedicated down-counter simplifies timer and logic control and avoids storing next signal registers. Outputs derive directly from the current state, ensuring glitch-free transitions. The clock output reflects the current counter value. The counter decrements only when non-zero to avoid underflow.
