To improve the existing design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), we will increase the pipeline depth to reduce the critical path delay from summing all 8 partial products at once. Instead of one combinational large adder, the design will:

1. Generate partial products in parallel (as before).
2. Use a balanced adder tree over multiple pipeline stages to reduce the addition delay:
   - Stage 1: sum pairs of partial products (4 pairs)
   - Stage 2: sum pairs of Stage 1 outputs (2 pairs)
   - Stage 3: sum the final two outputs to produce the final product
3. Pipeline registers will be inserted after each addition stage to enable higher clock frequency.
4. Enable signal pipeline length will be extended to match the data pipeline latency (4 stages total).
5. Input registers sample mul_a and mul_b only when mul_en_in is asserted.
6. Output enable will be asserted in sync with the final product availability.
7. Output mul_out will be zero when output enable is low.

This improvement reduces critical path delay by splitting the large addition into smaller, faster additions across multiple cycles, improving max frequency and potentially lowering power due to reduced glitching. The area overhead of extra registers is minimal compared to timing benefit.
