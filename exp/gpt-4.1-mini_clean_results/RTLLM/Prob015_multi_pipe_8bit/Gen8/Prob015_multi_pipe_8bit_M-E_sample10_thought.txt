Implement the unsigned 8-bit multiplier as a 4-stage pipeline:  
Stage 1: Sample inputs and input enable signal into registers.  
Stage 2: Generate 8 partial products (each 16 bits) by ANDing the multiplicand with each bit of multiplier (shifted accordingly), and register them.  
Stage 3: Pairwise add partial products using 4 16-bit adders, register the intermediate sums.  
Stage 4: Sum the two pairs of sums with one more 16-bit adder, register the final product.  
Enable signal is pipelined along the stages in a 4-bit shift register to track data validity. Output enable is asserted when final product is valid. Output is gated by this signal.  
This balanced pipeline structure reduces the critical path per stage, improving maximum frequency, and distributes addition operations to lower combinational delay while preserving enable gating and synchronous operation.
