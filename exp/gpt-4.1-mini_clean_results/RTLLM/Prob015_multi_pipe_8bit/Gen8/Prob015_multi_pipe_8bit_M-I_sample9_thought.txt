To improve the existing 2-stage pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier for better performance and power, I will:
1. Split the large combinational summation of 8 partial 16-bit operands into two pipeline stages, reducing critical path delay by performing partial sum additions in two separate clock cycles.
2. Introduce an additional pipeline register stage after partial product generation to store intermediate sums.
3. Propagate the enable signal through a 3-stage pipeline aligned with the data path, ensuring correct output enable timing.
4. Retain input sampling only when mul_en_in is high.
5. This approach balances pipeline depth (now 3 cycles), reducing max combinational delay, allowing higher frequency and lower power due to reduced switching at each stage, at the cost of a slightly longer latency.
