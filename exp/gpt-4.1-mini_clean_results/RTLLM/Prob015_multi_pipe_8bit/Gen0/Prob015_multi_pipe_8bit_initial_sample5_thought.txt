Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using pipelined stages. The design captures inputs and input enable on the rising clock edge when active and uses pipelined registers to progressively compute partial products and sums across multiple clock cycles. The mul_en_out is a pipeline register tracking the enable signal through the pipeline. Partial products are generated by ANDing the multiplicand with each multiplier bit (shifted accordingly), then partial sums accumulate those partial products across pipeline registers. Finally, the product output register captures the final sum which is assigned to the output when mul_en_out is active, otherwise outputs zero.
