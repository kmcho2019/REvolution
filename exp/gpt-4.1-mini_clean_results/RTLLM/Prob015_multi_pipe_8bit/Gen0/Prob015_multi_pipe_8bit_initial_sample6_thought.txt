Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using pipelining to improve throughput. The pipeline will be staged to separate input capture, partial product generation, partial sum accumulation, and final product calculation. The module samples inputs when enabled, generates partial products by ANDing multiplicand bits with multiplier bits, shifts and accumulates these partial products in registers across clock cycles. The output enable signal is pipelined alongside the data to indicate when the output product is valid. The reset signal asynchronously resets registers to zero.
