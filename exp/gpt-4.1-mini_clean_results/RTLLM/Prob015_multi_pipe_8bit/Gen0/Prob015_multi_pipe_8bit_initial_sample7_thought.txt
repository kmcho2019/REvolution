Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with a 3-stage pipeline:
1. Stage 1: On mul_en_in, latch inputs and mul_en_in in registers.
2. Stage 2: Generate partial products from mul_a_reg and mul_b_reg, sum them in pipeline registers.
3. Stage 3: Final summation of partial sums into mul_out_reg.
Output enable mul_en_out is registered and indicates valid output.
If output not enabled, output zero.
Active-low reset initializes all registers.
