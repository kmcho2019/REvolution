Design a pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier with input registers for operands and enable signal, partial product generation using bitwise AND between multiplicand and each bit of multiplier, pipeline stages to accumulate partial sums, and registers to store intermediate sums and final product. Synchronize all registers with clk and reset with active-low rst_n. Output enable signal mul_en_out is registered from mul_en_in and delayed accordingly to match pipeline latency. The product output mul_out is valid only when mul_en_out is asserted, else zero.
