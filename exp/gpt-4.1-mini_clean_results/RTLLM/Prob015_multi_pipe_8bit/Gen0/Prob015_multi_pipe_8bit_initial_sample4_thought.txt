Design a pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier with multiple pipeline stages: 
1) Input capture of mul_en_in, mul_a, and mul_b (when mul_en_in is high).
2) Generate partial products for each bit of mul_b and store in wires.
3) In the next pipeline stage, perform addition of partial products in groups to reduce combinational delay and store intermediate sums in registers.
4) In the final stage, sum the partial sums to get the final 16-bit product and store in a register.
Throughout the pipeline, propagate the enable signal in a register mul_en_out_reg so that mul_en_out signals when the product is valid.
Output mul_out is driven by mul_out_reg when mul_en_out is high; else zero.
Active low reset initializes registers to zero.
Clock synchronous operation is used.
