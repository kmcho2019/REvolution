To design an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining, we use multiple pipeline stages to break down the multiplication process into partial product generation, partial sum calculation, and final product summation. The input enable signal is registered and pipelined along to indicate when the outputs are valid. The inputs are registered on input enable, partial products are generated by masking the multiplicand with bits of the multiplier, and partial sums are accumulated in registers each clock cycle. The final product is stored in a register and outputted with an output enable signal derived from the pipeline of mul_en_in. Active-low reset clears all registers. This approach allows throughput improvement by processing multiple multiplication operations in a pipelined fashion.
