Design a pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier with an input enable and output enable signal. Inputs are latched on input enable, and partial products are generated for each multiplier bit. Partial sums are calculated and pipelined to accumulate the final 16-bit product. The pipeline consists of stages corresponding to partial product generation and summing, with registers between stages. Output enable is pipelined in sync with the data, and output product is provided when enabled; otherwise zero.
