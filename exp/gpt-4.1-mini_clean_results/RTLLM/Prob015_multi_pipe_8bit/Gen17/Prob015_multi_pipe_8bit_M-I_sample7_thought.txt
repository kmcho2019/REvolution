To optimize the original 2-stage pipeline for better PPA (performance, power, area), we focus on reducing the critical path delay and switching power:

1. Split the large combinational addition of 8 partial 16-bit products into a balanced 3-stage pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Register inputs and generate partial products.
   - Stage 2: Add partial products pairwise (reduce 8 partial products to 4 sums).
   - Stage 3: Sum remaining partial sums to get final product.

2. This breaks down the large 8-way 16-bit addition into smaller 2-way 16-bit additions over multiple clock cycles, improving timing.

3. Propagate the enable signal through the pipeline stages to gate outputs and registers accordingly, minimizing toggling.

4. Keep input registers updated only when mul_en_in is high, and clear outputs on reset.

This approach improves max clock frequency by balancing additions across pipeline stages and reduces switching power due to shorter combinational paths and enable gating. Area overhead from extra pipeline registers is minimal compared to timing gain.
