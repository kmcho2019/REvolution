Implement a deeper pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a three-stage pipeline: stage one samples inputs and enable; stage two computes the 8 partial products (multiplicand ANDed with each bit of multiplier, shifted accordingly) and stores them; stage three accumulates partial sums in a balanced binary adder tree manner using registers, thereby reducing combinational delay per stage. The output enable signal is pipelined synchronously through the stages. This approach balances the addition logic across multiple clock cycles, improving timing and throughput while maintaining synchronous reset and proper enable control. The final stage outputs the valid 16-bit product when enabled, otherwise zero.
