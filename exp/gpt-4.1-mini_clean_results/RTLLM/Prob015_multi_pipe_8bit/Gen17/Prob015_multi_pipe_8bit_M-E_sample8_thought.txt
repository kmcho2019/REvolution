Implement a three-stage pipeline multiplier that breaks down the multiplication process to shorten the critical path and improve clock frequency. 
- Stage 1: Capture inputs and input enable signal.
- Stage 2: Generate all 8 partial products (each multiplicand shifted by multiplier bits) and store them.
- Stage 3: Use a balanced adder tree in two cycles to sum partial products:
  -- First half of partial products summed and registered.
  -- Then second half summed and registered.
  -- Final addition of the two sums and register the result with output enable.
This staged summation pipeline reduces the critical path of adding eight partial products at once. The enable signal is pipelined accordingly. Output is gated by the output enable to ensure valid results.
