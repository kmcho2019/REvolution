Refactor the design to pipeline partial product accumulation in multiple stages to reduce the combinational path delay. Instead of summing all partial products in one step, split the addition into two pipeline stages: first sum pairs of partial products, then sum the results to get the final product. This balances the adder tree and decreases logic depth per cycle, improving timing. Use registers to hold intermediate sums at each pipeline stage.
