Implement the 8-bit unsigned multiplier as a 4-stage pipeline where partial products are generated and summed progressively across pipeline stages to reduce combinational delay and balance the pipeline. 

Stage 1: Register inputs and generate 8 partial products, each 16-bit wide, corresponding to each bit of the multiplier.
Stage 2: Pairwise add the partial products to get 4 sums.
Stage 3: Pairwise add the 4 sums to get 2 sums.
Stage 4: Add the final 2 sums to get the final product.

At each stage, propagate the enable signal in a pipeline register to indicate data validity. 

This balanced adder-tree pipeline reduces the critical path delay, improving clock frequency, and evenly distributes hardware. The enables ensure proper timing and output gating. If reset is asserted, outputs and pipeline registers clear to zero. 

This approach improves throughput and timing while maintaining clean input/output enable management.
