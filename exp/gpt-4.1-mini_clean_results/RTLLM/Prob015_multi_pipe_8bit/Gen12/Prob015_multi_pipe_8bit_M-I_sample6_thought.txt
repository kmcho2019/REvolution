To improve PPA (Performance, Power, Area) over the prior implementation, restructure the partial product addition into a multi-stage pipelined adder tree.

Strategy:
1. Keep the initial input registers and mul_en pipeline unchanged.
2. Generate partial products combinationally as before.
3. Replace the flat addition of all 8 partial products with a balanced adder tree implemented across two pipeline stages:
   - Stage 1: Pairwise addition of 8 partial products into 4 sums (registered).
   - Stage 2: Pairwise addition of these 4 sums into 2 sums (registered).
4. Stage 3: Final addition of the 2 sums into the final product (registered).
5. Track mul_en_in through a 5-bit shift register to properly sync enable with output latency.
6. Output enable generated from MSB of mul_en pipeline.
7. Multiplex the output product to zero when output enable is inactive.

This breaks the large combinational sum into smaller additions per clock cycle, improving timing.
Registering partial sums reduces glitching and switching power.
The deeper pipeline improves maximum clock frequency and power efficiency at the cost of latency, which is acceptable in pipeline designs.

This approach yields better timing by reducing the critical path delay and lowers power by registering intermediate results and reducing combinational complexity in each stage.
