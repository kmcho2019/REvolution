Implement a 4-stage pipeline unsigned 8-bit multiplier that progressively calculates the product by accumulating shifted partial sums in stages. The pipeline stages are:  
Stage 1: Register inputs and mul_en_in;  
Stage 2: Generate and register partial products for multiplier bits [1:0];  
Stage 3: Generate and register partial products for multiplier bits [3:2] and add to previous sum;  
Stage 4: Generate and register partial products for multiplier bits [7:4] (processed two bits per cycle, combining their partial products with appropriate shifts) and add to cumulative sum from previous stage.  
This staged accumulation reduces the combinational adder width at each cycle, balances the pipeline latency, and allows mul_en signal to be pipelined through 4 registers corresponding to stages. Output enable mul_en_out is taken from the most significant bit of the enable pipeline register. mul_out register holds the final product at the last stage.  
This design trades pipeline latency for improved clock frequency by splitting the additions into smaller steps and carefully pipelining enables and partial products.
