Implement a 4-stage pipeline multiplier that accumulates partial products progressively to reduce combinational delay per stage. Stage 1 registers inputs and enable; Stage 2 generates 4 lower partial products and sums them into a partial sum; Stage 3 generates 4 upper partial products and sums them into another partial sum; Stage 4 adds the two partial sums to form the final product. Each stage registers intermediate results and propagates the enable signal through a 4-bit shift register. This staged accumulation balances hardware and timing, lowering critical path delay and allowing higher clock frequency while maintaining throughput of one multiplication per cycle after pipeline is full.
