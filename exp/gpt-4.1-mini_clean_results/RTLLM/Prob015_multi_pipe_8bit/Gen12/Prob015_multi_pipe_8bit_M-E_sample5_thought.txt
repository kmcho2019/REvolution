Implement a fully pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier with a 3-stage pipeline to improve throughput and reduce critical path delay. Stage 1 captures inputs and the enable signal; Stage 2 generates all eight partial products (each a shifted multiplicand masked by multiplier bits) and partially sums them in pairs to reduce logic depth; Stage 3 sums the partial sums from Stage 2 and registers the final product along with the delayed enable signal.

Each stage is synchronized to the clock with registered outputs, and active-low reset clears all pipeline registers. The output enable is delayed across pipeline stages accordingly. This architecture balances combinational delay across pipeline stages and increases maximum clock frequency while maintaining a steady throughput, as one multiplication result is produced every clock cycle after initial latency.
