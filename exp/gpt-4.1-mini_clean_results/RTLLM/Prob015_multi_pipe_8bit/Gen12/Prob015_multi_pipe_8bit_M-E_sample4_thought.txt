Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a deeper pipeline with 3 stages to balance performance and timing. Stage 1 captures inputs and generates the 8 partial products in parallel. Stage 2 sums the partial products in pairs to reduce the number from 8 to 4 sums. Stage 3 sums those 4 sums pairwise to get 2 sums, then final addition to produce the 16-bit product. Each pipeline stage has registers to hold intermediate sums and the enable signal shifts through a 3-bit register. This reduces the combinational path per cycle, improving timing, and maintains a clean synchronous reset and output enable propagation.
