Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a four-stage pipeline that progressively accumulates partial products with a balanced adder tree approach to reduce the critical path per stage. Stage 1 registers inputs and enable signals. Stage 2 generates partial products for lower 4 bits of mul_b and sums them into a partial sum. Stage 3 does the same for upper 4 bits of mul_b. Stage 4 adds the two partial sums (one shifted by 4 bits) to produce the final 16-bit product. This design balances addition complexity across pipeline stages, improving timing and throughput while providing a registered output enable signal delayed accordingly. All registers update synchronously with clock and active-low reset.
