Implement a straightforward 3-stage pipeline multiplier with an enable shift register to propagate mul_en_in through the pipeline. Sample inputs only when mul_en_in is asserted. Generate partial products combinationally by ANDing multiplicand bits with each multiplier bit and shifting accordingly. Sum all partial products in one pipeline stage using a simple adder tree split into two steps to reduce adder complexity. Register inputs, partial sums, and final product on each clock cycle if the corresponding enable stage bit is asserted. Output enable is the MSB of the enable shift register. Output zero when disabled. This approach simplifies addition by splitting summation into two stages and avoids multiple nested generate loops, keeping design clean and easy to maintain with minimal pipeline latency.
