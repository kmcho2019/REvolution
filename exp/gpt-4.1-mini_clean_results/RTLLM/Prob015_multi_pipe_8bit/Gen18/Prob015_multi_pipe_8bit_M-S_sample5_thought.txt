Implement a straightforward 3-stage pipeline for the unsigned 8-bit multiplier:  
1) Input stage registers inputs and the enable signal.  
2) Partial product generation and summation stage: combinationally generate partial products based on the multiplier bits and sum them together.  
3) Output stage registers the final product and propagates the enable signal to indicate valid output.  

The pipeline registers propagate the enable signal as a shift register aligned to the pipeline latency. The multiplication happens combinationally in one stage with the operands registered beforehand. When the output enable is asserted, the product output is valid; otherwise, output zeros. This keeps the design simple, clean, and functional.
