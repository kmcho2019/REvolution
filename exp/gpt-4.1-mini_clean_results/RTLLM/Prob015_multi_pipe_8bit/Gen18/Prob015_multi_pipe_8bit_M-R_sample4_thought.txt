Refactor the pipeline multiplier to a three-stage pipeline architecture to reduce combinational delay and balance the addition workload.  
Stage 1: Register inputs and input enable.  
Stage 2: Generate partial products and group them into pairs for partial summation, registering the intermediate sums and enables.  
Stage 3: Sum the intermediate partial sums registered in stage 2 and output the final product and enable signal.  
Use combinational assigns for partial product generation and partial sums, and register these sums in always blocks. Output is gated by the output enable signal.  
This structure reduces the critical path delay by splitting large addition into two stages of addition, improves timing and power by minimizing long combinational logic, and keeps enable control aligned with pipeline stages.
