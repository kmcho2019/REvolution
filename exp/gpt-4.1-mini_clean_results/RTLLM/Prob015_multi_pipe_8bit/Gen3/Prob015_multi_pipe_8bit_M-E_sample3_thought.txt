Implement a 3-stage pipeline unsigned 8-bit multiplier using a divide-and-conquer approach that splits the multiplier and multiplicand into high and low 4-bit halves. In the first stage, register inputs and generate the four 4x4 partial products in parallel using combinational multiplication of 4-bit segments. In the second stage, sum the partial products with appropriate shifting to form partial sums, and register these sums. In the third stage, finalize the addition to produce the 16-bit product and register the output. This reduces the number of large adders operating in one clock cycle, balances the pipeline stages, and limits critical path delay. The enable signals are pipelined through 3 flip-flops to track valid data. The multiplication uses small 4-bit multipliers instantiated as separate modules or directly coded combinationally. Input registers only load when input enable is asserted, and outputs are zeroed when not valid.
