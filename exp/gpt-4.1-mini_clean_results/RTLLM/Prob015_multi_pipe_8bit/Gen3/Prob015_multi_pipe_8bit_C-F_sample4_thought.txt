Fuse the best of both designs: use a 4-stage pipeline for high throughput and balanced critical path. 
- Stage 0: Sample inputs and input enable.
- Stage 1: Generate and register partial products individually to avoid repeated combinational delay.
- Stage 2: Perform a staged summation of partial products in pairs with registered intermediate sums. 
- Stage 3: Final addition stage registers the total product and propagates the enable signal.

This breaks down the large combinational addition into smaller steps, improving timing. 
Enable signals are pipelined along with data to indicate valid outputs. 
Only register inputs when enabled, zero outputs otherwise.
This approach combines partial product generation and pipelined addition from example 1, with the enable pipeline and clean register control from example 2, balancing performance, power, and area.
