To improve PPA (Power, Performance, Area) while preserving pipeline structure and functionality, I will refine the design by: 
1) Introducing a more balanced pipeline by adding extra pipeline registers between partial product accumulation steps to reduce combinational logic depth per stage and shorten critical paths, improving timing.
2) Replacing the addition of three operands at once (sum0 + sum1 + sum2) with two-step additions via pipeline registers for better timing.
3) Registering the output product and output enable synchronously to avoid combinational muxing, reducing output timing slack.
4) Adding enable gating to summation registers to minimize switching when multiplication is not active.
5) Keeping partial product generation combinational but isolating pipeline registers from it ensures only required data transitions.
This pipeline restructuring reduces critical path delays and power by controlling switching activity and registers outputs. It slightly increases latency (1 more cycle) but enhances clock frequency and power efficiency.
