Design a 3-stage pipeline unsigned 8-bit multiplier by splitting the multiplication into:

1. Input and partial product generation stage: Register inputs and simultaneously generate all 8 partial products (each 16 bits) directly using bit masking and shifts.

2. Pairwise summation stage: Combine partial products using an adder tree style with three adders to sum pairs and one leftover partial product. This reduces the number of sums to two in one cycle.

3. Final summation and output stage: Add the two remaining sums to produce the final product.

This reduces pipeline stages to 3 (less latency) with a balanced and simple partial product adder tree, simplifying control and improving throughput. The mul_en signal is pipelined accordingly across stages.

Partial products are generated combinationally only once and latched along with the inputs, reducing switching. The output product and enable are registered in the final stage.

Outputs are cleared on reset and when the enable signal is inactive, ensuring glitch-free outputs.

This design trades off slightly deeper combinational adders for fewer pipeline stages and simplified logic, providing a fresh architectural approach.
