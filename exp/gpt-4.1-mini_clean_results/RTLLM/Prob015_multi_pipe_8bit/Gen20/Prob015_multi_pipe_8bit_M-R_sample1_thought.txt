Refactor the pipeline multiplier to use a deeper pipeline with three stages to reduce the critical path and improve timing. 
Stage 1 registers inputs and enable. 
Stage 2 computes all partial products and stores them in registers (breaking combinational partial product generation from final sum). 
Stage 3 accumulates partial sums by using a tree adder structure split into two steps: summing pairs of partial products, then summing those results, all registered to balance delay. 
This design reduces combinational delay by balancing addition over multiple cycles with pipeline registers, while still correctly propagating enables and outputting the final product gated by the output enable.
