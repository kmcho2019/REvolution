Fuse the two designs by implementing a balanced, multi-stage pipelined adder tree to sum partial products in multiple clock cycles, thus reducing the combinational critical path and improving timing. Specifically:

- Stage 1: Register inputs on mul_en_in asserted.
- Stage 2: Generate partial products combinationally.
- Stage 3: Add partial products in pairs and register sums.
- Stage 4: Add pairs of sums again and register.
- Stage 5: Final sum register stage to obtain the product.

This four-stage pipeline for summation greatly shortens the critical path, allowing a higher clock frequency.

Simultaneously, keep the enable signal as a 5-stage shift register to track pipeline latency and indicate valid output.

Conditional registers update only when their respective enables are active, reducing unnecessary switching.

The output product is valid only when the output enable signal is asserted; otherwise zero is output.

This fusion leverages the enable pipeline alignment and balanced adder tree of Example 1 with the straightforward, clean pipeline structure and reset handling of Example 2, yielding an optimized, clean, and efficient pipelined multiplier.
