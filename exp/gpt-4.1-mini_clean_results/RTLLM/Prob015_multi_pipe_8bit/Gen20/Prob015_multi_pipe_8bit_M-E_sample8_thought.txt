Implement the 8-bit multiplier as a 3-stage pipeline where each stage halves the addition complexity, improving timing by distributing the addition of partial products over multiple clock cycles. Stage 1 captures inputs and multiplier bits, generating partial products as shifted multiplicand bits masked by multiplier bits. Stage 2 groups and sums partial products in pairs, reducing eight partial products to four sums. Stage 3 further adds these four sums into two, and then produces the final product output and propagates the enable signal through pipeline registers at each stage. This staged summation lowers combinational delay per stage, enhancing frequency while maintaining clean pipelined control and output enable signaling.
