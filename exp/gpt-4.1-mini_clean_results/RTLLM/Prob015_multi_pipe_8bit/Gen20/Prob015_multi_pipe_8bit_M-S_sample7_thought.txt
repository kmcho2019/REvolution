Simplify the pipeline to three stages:
1. Register inputs mul_a, mul_b, and mul_en_in.
2. Generate all partial products combinationally from registered inputs.
3. Sum all partial products combinationally and register the result.

Use a 3-bit shift register for mul_en_in to track pipeline progress and generate mul_en_out from its MSB. This keeps enable signal timing aligned with the output product.

This approach avoids complex multi-stage addition or partial sum registers while maintaining a clean and functional pipelined multiplier design with 3 cycles latency.
