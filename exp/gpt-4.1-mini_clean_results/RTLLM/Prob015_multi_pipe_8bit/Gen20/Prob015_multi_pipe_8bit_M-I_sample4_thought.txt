To improve the original two-stage pipeline multiplier, focus on reducing the critical path caused by adding eight 16-bit partial products in one cycle. - Stage 2 generates and registers partial products for individual bits.

  * Stage 3 sums partial products in groups (4 partial sums).
  * Stage 4 sums the intermediate sums.
  * Stage 5 registers the final product.
- Propagate enable signals through all pipeline stages.
- Output product is valid when the final enable signal is active.
- Use non-blocking assignments and synchronous reset.
- This approach breaks down the large combinational adder into smaller adders and spreads latency across stages, improving timing and reducing power by limiting switching per cycle.

This 4-stage pipeline (input latch, partial product generation, partial sum tree, final output register) balances delay, power, and area effectively.
