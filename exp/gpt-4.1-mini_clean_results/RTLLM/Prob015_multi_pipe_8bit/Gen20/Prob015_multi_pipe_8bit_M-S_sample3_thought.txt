Implement a simpler 2-stage pipeline multiplier that samples inputs when enabled, generates partial products combinationally, sums them in one addition stage, and registers the final product output with an enable pipeline. This reduces pipeline stages and complexity by using a single addition of all partial products in one step (still combinational, but simpler), with input and output registers synchronized by the enable signals. The enable pipeline shifts the input enable to align with the output. This simplification retains functionality while minimizing complexity and pipeline depth.
