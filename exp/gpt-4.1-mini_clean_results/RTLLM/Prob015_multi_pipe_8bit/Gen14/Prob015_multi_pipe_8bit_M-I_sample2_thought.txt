To optimize the given pipelined 8-bit multiplier for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will split the large summation of eight partial products into a balanced adder tree over multiple pipeline stages.

Improvement strategy:
- Instead of summing all eight partial products in one cycle (which creates a long critical path and large switching activity), perform the addition in multiple pipeline stages, each summing fewer operands.
- Use three pipeline stages for summation:
  - Stage 1: Generate partial products (already done) and register them.
  - Stage 2: Sum partial products pairwise into four sums.
  - Stage 3: Sum the four results pairwise into two sums.
  - Stage 4: Final sum of two sums to produce final product.
- This deepens the pipeline by two additional stages compared to the original design but reduces critical path per stage and switching activity, improving max frequency and lowering power.
- Track enable signals through a 5-stage shift register pipeline accordingly, so that the output enable is correctly aligned.
- Each register update will be gated with the enable signal to prevent unnecessary toggling.
- Output product will be registered and gated by the output enable.
- This hierarchical summation also reduces area slightly due to smaller adders per stage and reduces max combinational delay.
- Maintain clean synchronous reset and nonblocking assignments.

The number of pipeline stages changes from 3 to 5 (input registers + 3 summation stages + output register). Enable pipeline width is 5 bits to keep synchronization.

This approach balances pipeline depth, frequency, power, and area.

Note: The latency increases to 5 cycles, but this is acceptable for improved PPA.
