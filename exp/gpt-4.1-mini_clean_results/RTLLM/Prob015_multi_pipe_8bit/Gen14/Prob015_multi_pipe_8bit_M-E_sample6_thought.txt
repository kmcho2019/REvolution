Implement the unsigned 8-bit multiplier with a deeper, balanced 4-stage pipeline to reduce critical path and improve timing:

1. Stage 0: Register inputs and input enable signal. Inputs are latched only when mul_en_in is high.
2. Stage 1: Generate all partial products (8 partial products from each bit of multiplier).
3. Stage 2: Perform a first layer of partial sum addition by grouping partial products and summing pairs of them, registering the results.
4. Stage 3: Perform the final summation of intermediate sums, register final product.
5. The enable signal is pipelined through a 4-bit shift register to align with final product output.
6. mul_en_out is the MSB of enable pipeline, indicating valid product at output.
7. The output mul_out is driven by the registered final product when mul_en_out is asserted; else zero.

This staged summation approach reduces combinational delay per clock by spreading addition over two cycles. It also balances pipeline stages, potentially improving maximum clock frequency and power efficiency due to shorter combinational paths and reduced switching per stage.
