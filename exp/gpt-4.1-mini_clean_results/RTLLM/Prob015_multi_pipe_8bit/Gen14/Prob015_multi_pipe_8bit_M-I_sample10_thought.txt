To improve the initial design's timing and power efficiency while maintaining pipeline operation, I will restructure the partial product addition into multiple pipeline stages using a balanced adder tree.

Strategy:
1. Register inputs on mul_en_in as before.
2. Generate partial products combinationally from registered inputs.
3. Use a three-stage pipeline for partial sum accumulation:
   - Stage 1: Pairwise addition of partial products (4 adders)
   - Stage 2: Pairwise addition of stage 1 sums (2 adders)
   - Stage 3: Final addition of stage 2 sums (1 adder)
   
Each stage registers its sums and control signals (enable) to improve timing by reducing the adder fan-in.

A 4-bit shift register will track the enable signal through the pipeline, with mul_en_out derived from the MSB.

This staged adder tree reduces the critical path delay per clock cycle and lowers dynamic power by minimizing glitches and excessive combinational logic depth.

The pipeline depth increases to 4 cycles but provides higher frequency and more efficient resource usage.
