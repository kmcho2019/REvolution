Refactor the 8-bit pipelined multiplier by separating each pipeline stage explicitly with registered partial sums instead of summing multiple partial products together in one stage. Use a more systematic pipeline flow: capture inputs and enable, generate partial products in combinational logic, then successively sum pairs of partial products/registers in subsequent pipeline stages with registers between stages. Use a pipeline register for the enable signal at each stage to synchronize output validity. Replace the final combinational output mux with a registered mux to improve timing. This approach clarifies data flow, eases timing closure by balancing addition stages, and reduces large multi-operand adders to simpler two-operand adders per stage.
