Implement a 3-stage pipeline multiplier that exploits an early partial product accumulation approach. On the first stage, inputs and enable are registered. On the second stage, partial products are generated for each multiplier bit in parallel using AND gates and shifted appropriately, then accumulated using a ripple-carry adder implemented as a chain of 8 adders with pipelined carry registers to avoid a long combinational path. This breaks the addition into smaller sequential steps distributed over the pipeline stage. The third stage accumulates the final sum from the previous partial sums and outputs the product. The enable signal is propagated through a 3-bit shift register to gate valid outputs. This approach reduces combinational adder delay per stage, using simple 8-bit adders instead of large 16-bit adders, improving timing and pipeline throughput. Output is gated by the delayed enable signal, and outputs zero when not valid.
