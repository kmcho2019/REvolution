To optimize the existing 8-bit pipelined multiplier for better PPA (performance, power, area), I propose the following improvements:

1. **Balanced Adder Tree with Carry-Save Addition (CSA):**
   Instead of pairwise 16-bit additions in pipeline stages, use carry-save addition to reduce carry propagation delay, improving clock frequency (performance).

2. **Register Clock Enable Instead of Zeroing:**
   Replace explicit zeroing of pipeline registers with clock enables derived from the pipeline enable signals to reduce unnecessary switching (power).

3. **Pipelined Enable Signal Simplification:**
   Keep the enable pipeline but optimize signal naming and usage to cleanly gate the register writes without extra resets, reducing glitches and dynamic power.

4. **Stage Balancing:**
   The pipeline will have four stages: 
   - Stage 0: Register inputs and generate partial products (combinational)
   - Stage 1: CSA addition of partial products into two sums
   - Stage 2: CSA addition of the two sums into final sum and carry
   - Stage 3: Final ripple carry adder to produce the product output

5. **Synthesis-Friendly Coding Style:**
   Use blocking assignments for combinational logic, non-blocking for sequential logic. Modularize partial product generation and addition stages for clarity.

This approach decreases the critical path by using carry-save addition to speed up summation of partial products and reduces power by gating registers instead of zeroing them. Area impact is moderate but acceptable for performance gain.

The output enable will remain synchronized and assigned from the enable pipeline MSB as before.

The code implements a 4-stage pipeline multiplier with CSA and clock-enabled registers for improved PPA.
