Simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier by reducing the number of pipeline stages to three instead of four, merging some addition operations to reduce registers and control signals. Use a single shift register for the enable signal to track input validity. Generate partial products combinationally from the registered inputs. Perform addition of partial products in two steps: first sum adjacent pairs of partial products with registers, then combine the results with a final register stage for the product. Update output enable and product registers synchronously using the enable pipeline register. This reduces pipeline complexity, control logic, and register counts while maintaining the necessary pipeline flow and correct multiplication functionality.
