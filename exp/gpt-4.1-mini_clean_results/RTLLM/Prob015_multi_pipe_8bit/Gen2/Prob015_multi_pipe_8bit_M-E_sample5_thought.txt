Implement a 5-stage pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier using a binary adder tree approach for partial products. First, input operands and enable are registered. Then, generate 8 partial products by ANDing mul_a with each bit of mul_b shifted accordingly. Instead of summing all pairs at once, sum partial products in a balanced binary tree across pipeline stages to reduce the addition fan-in per stage: stage 2 sums pairs of partial products (4 sums), stage 3 sums pairs of these sums (2 sums), stage 4 sums the final two sums to produce the product. Each pipeline stage registers intermediate sums and enable signals. Output enable signal is delayed through the pipeline stages to align with the output product. The final output mul_out is valid only when mul_en_out is high; otherwise zero. This approach reduces addition logic depth per stage and balances pipeline load.
