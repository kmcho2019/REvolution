1. Input and enable signal registering with pipelined enable shifting for precise output enable generation.
2. Partial products generated combinationally using a clean generate loop for clarity and maintainability.
3. Balanced addition tree split into pairwise sums, then summed in subsequent pipeline stages to reduce combinational delay.
4. Each pipeline stage has its own enable signal pipelined in parallel, ensuring data-valid signal alignment throughout the pipeline.
5. Pipeline registers zeroed on reset, and conditional clears on stage enables inactive to minimize glitches and switching.
6. Non-blocking assignments used consistently.
7. Output enable assigned from the MSB of the shifted enable register as in Example 1, ensuring a clean timing reference.
8. The final output is gated by the output enable to prevent invalid data propagation.

This design balances timing, clarity, and enable control while keeping partial product generation and addition stages modular and straightforward, improving maintainability and synthesis friendliness.
