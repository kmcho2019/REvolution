Implement the 8-bit unsigned multiplier with a deeper pipeline that performs partial product generation and accumulation in a sequential step-wise manner, one bit of the multiplier per cycle, allowing a simple and area-efficient datapath with a shift-accumulate approach. This design uses an 8-stage pipeline where each stage processes one bit of the multiplier, accumulating partial sums progressively into a 16-bit register. Input registers latch operands only when mul_en_in is asserted, and an 8-bit shift register pipeline tracks the valid enable signal, shifting along with each cycle. This approach reduces combinational complexity per stage and evenly distributes work across pipeline stages, improving timing and making it friendly to hardware reuse and low power.
