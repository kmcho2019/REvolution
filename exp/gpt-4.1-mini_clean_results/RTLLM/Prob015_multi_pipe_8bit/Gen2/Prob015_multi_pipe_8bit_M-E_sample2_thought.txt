Design a 4-stage pipeline unsigned 8-bit multiplier focusing on balanced computation and staged accumulation:

1. Stage 0 (Input stage): On mul_en_in, latch multiplicand (mul_a) and multiplier (mul_b) into registers and store mul_en_in in a shift register for output enable pipelining.

2. Stage 1: Generate all 8 partial products (each partial product is mul_a ANDed with one multiplier bit, then shifted accordingly).

3. Stage 2: Sum partial products in 4 pairs with registers to pipeline addition.

4. Stage 3: Sum the 4 results in pairs into two 16-bit registers.

5. Stage 4: Final sum of the two results, producing the 16-bit mul_out.

This structure pipelining multiplication and additions to reduce critical path delay. The enable signal is shifted through a 5-bit register (to cover all pipeline stages) so the output enable indicates valid output at mul_out. Outputs zero when output enable is inactive. Active-low reset initializes all pipeline registers.
