Refactor the multiplier pipeline by explicitly pipelining partial product generation and accumulation across multiple stages, instead of summing all partial products combinationally at once. Use a 4-stage pipeline: 
1) Input registers and enable shift register; 
2) Generate partial products for each bit of multiplier (registered); 
3) Pairwise summation of partial products in two pipeline registers to reduce combinational path length; 
4) Final summation register stage for the product. 
This breaks the long combinational addition chain into smaller additions over multiple clock cycles, improving timing and reducing critical path length.  
Use registered partial products and sums rather than large combinational sums.  
Enable signals propagate along pipeline stages via shift registers. 
Outputs are zeroed when enable is not asserted.  
This restructuring keeps the same latency but achieves better timing and possibly power due to reduced combinational logic depth and glitching.
