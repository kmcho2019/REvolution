Implement a straightforward 3-stage pipeline unsigned 8-bit multiplier with input sampling, partial product generation, and accumulation stages.  
- Stage 1: Sample inputs and enable signal on clock edge if mul_en_in is active.  
- Stage 2: Generate and register partial products sum in one step (add all shifted multiplicand masked by multiplier bits).  
- Stage 3: Register final product sum.  
Enable signals are pipelined alongside data to track valid outputs.  
Output enable and product valid only when the enable pipeline's MSB is set.  
Registers hold their values (no reset to zero except on async reset) to reduce unnecessary toggling.  
This keeps the pipeline depth at 3 stages with simple summations for easy timing and minimal complexity, fulfilling the requirements while simplifying the previous design.
