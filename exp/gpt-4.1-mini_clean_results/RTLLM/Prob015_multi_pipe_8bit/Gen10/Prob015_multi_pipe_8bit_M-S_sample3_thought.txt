Implement a straightforward 2-stage pipeline multiplier:
1. On mul_en_in, register inputs mul_a and mul_b.
2. In the next clock cycle, compute the full product combinationally using a single multiplier operator.
3. Register the output product and the enable signal to indicate valid output.
