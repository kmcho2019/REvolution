Simplify the pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier to three pipeline stages:
1. On mul_en_in, sample mul_a and mul_b into registers.
2. Generate partial products combinationally each clock cycle using registered inputs.
3. Sum partial products with a single 16-bit adder tree and register the result as the output product.

Use a 3-bit shift register for mul_en_in to track the pipeline and generate mul_en_out from the MSB. This keeps the enable synchronization simple.

This approach avoids complex multiple pipeline addition stages, yet still pipelines input sampling, multiplication, and output registering, providing throughput improvement over a purely combinational multiplier while significantly reducing complexity.
