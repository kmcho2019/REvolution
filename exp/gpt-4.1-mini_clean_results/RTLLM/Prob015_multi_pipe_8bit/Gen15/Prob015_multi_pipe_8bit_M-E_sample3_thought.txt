Implement a 4-stage pipeline multiplier where each pipeline stage handles a portion of the multiplication to reduce combinational delay and balance pipeline latency with simplicity.

Stages:
1) Input Sampling: Register inputs and mul_en_in.
2) Partial Product Generation: Generate 2 partial products by splitting multiplier into two 4-bit halves, and multiplying each 4-bit half with the entire multiplicand using simple combinational partial product summation.
3) Partial Product Accumulation: Shift and add the two 12-bit partial products to form a 16-bit intermediate sum registered.
4) Output Register: Register the final product and mul_en_out.

Control signals:
- Use a 4-bit shift register pipeline for mul_en_in propagation matching the pipeline stages.
- Register outputs only when the propagated enable is asserted.
- Reset asynchronously active low.

This approach reduces the combinational path by splitting multiplication into two smaller 8x4 bit multiplications per cycle, pipelining the addition in a separate stage, thus achieving better timing and power characteristics than summing all partial products at once. It requires fewer adders per stage and balances performance with design simplicity.

The design carefully synchronizes data and enable signals through the pipeline and uses registers only when enables are active to minimize switching.
