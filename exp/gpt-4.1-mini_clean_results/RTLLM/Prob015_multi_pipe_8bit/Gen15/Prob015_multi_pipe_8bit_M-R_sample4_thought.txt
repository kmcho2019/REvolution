Refactor the multiplier pipeline by explicitly pipelining partial product generation and their accumulation in multiple stages to reduce combinational path delay and improve timing. Instead of a single wide combinational addition of all partial products, break the summation into an adder tree across two pipeline stages: first stage generates partial products, second stage sums them in pairs, and third stage registers the final sum. This approach improves clock frequency by reducing critical path and clarifies the pipeline structure, while preserving functionality.
