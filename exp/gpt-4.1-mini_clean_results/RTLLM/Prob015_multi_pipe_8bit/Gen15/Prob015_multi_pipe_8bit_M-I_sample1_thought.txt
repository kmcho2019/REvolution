Improvement Strategy:
To improve timing and reduce the longest carry chains in the addition stages, we will rebalance the partial product additions into a deeper, more balanced pipeline. Instead of summing three partial products at once in two pipeline stages and two in the third, we will implement a 5-stage pipeline with pairwise 2-input additions to create a balanced binary adder tree.

Key changes:
1. Extend mul_en_pipe to 5 stages to track pipeline validity accordingly.
2. Sample inputs as before.
3. Generate all 8 partial products combinationally (as 16-bit wires).
4. Stage 1: sum partial_products[0] + partial_products[1] (2 operands)
5. Stage 2: sum partial_products[2] + partial_products[3] (2 operands)
6. Stage 3: sum partial_products[4] + partial_products[5] (2 operands)
7. Stage 4: sum partial_products[6] + partial_products[7] (2 operands)
8. Stage 5: sum all results from Stages 1-4 (four operands), done in two steps in the same cycle:
   - sum stage1_result + stage2_result
   - sum stage3_result + stage4_result
   - then sum these two sums
   We can do this with two 16-bit adders plus one final 16-bit adder, effectively making one pipeline stage with three adders.
9. The output enable (mul_en_out) is derived from the fifth stage of mul_en_pipe.
10. Registers update only when the corresponding enable bit is asserted to reduce switching.
11. Output product valid only when mul_en_out is asserted, otherwise 0.
12. This approach reduces each adder's operand count to 2, minimizing carry propagation delay.
13. Latency increases to 5 cycles, but throughput remains 1 result per cycle after pipeline fill.
14. Additional pipeline stage helps timing, with only slightly more registers.
