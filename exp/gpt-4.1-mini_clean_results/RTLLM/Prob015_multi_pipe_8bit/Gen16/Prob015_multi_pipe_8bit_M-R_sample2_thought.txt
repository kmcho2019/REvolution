Refactor the pipeline multiplier by explicitly pipelining partial sums in multiple stages instead of summing all partial products combinationally in one step.  
Use registers to hold the partial products and sum them pairwise across multiple clock cycles, thus balancing the adder tree over several pipeline stages.  
This reduces the critical path per stage, enabling higher clock frequencies.  
The enable signal is propagated through pipeline stages as a multi-bit shift register, matching pipeline latency.  
Output muxing is done combinationally based on the output enable signal.  
The input sampling and enable capturing remain the same, but partial sums are computed progressively with registers.  
This provides a clear multi-stage pipeline, balancing performance and complexity while still fulfilling functional and timing requirements.
