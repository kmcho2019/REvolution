- Sample inputs only when mul_en_in is asserted.
- Generate all 8 partial products combinationally from registered inputs.
- Break the partial product summation into multiple balanced pipeline stages:
  - Stage 1: Sum partial_products[0..1]
  - Stage 2: Sum partial_products[2..3]
  - Stage 3: Sum partial_products[4..5]
  - Stage 4: Sum partial_products[6..7]
- Stage 5: Final sum stage adds the four sums from previous stages to produce the final 16-bit product.
- This 2-input addition pipeline reduces the critical path per cycle, improving max frequency.
- Register sums only when corresponding enable pipeline bit is set, reducing unnecessary switching.
- Output enable mul_en_out is derived from the MSB of the 5-stage enable pipeline, ensuring output validity synchronization.
- Output product is gated by mul_en_out to zero when invalid.
- This approach achieves better timing by limiting adder fan-in per stage, lowering power by selective register enables, and keeps area moderate by balanced adders and pipelining.
