Design a three-stage pipeline multiplier: stage 1 captures inputs and input enable; stage 2 generates partial products (8 partial products of 16 bits each) in parallel; stage 3 performs a balanced adder tree to sum the partial products in three sequential additions over two cycles, storing intermediate sums in registers to reduce combinational delay per cycle. This staged summation lowers critical path delay compared to summing all partial products in one cycle. The output enable signal is pipelined along with data to maintain alignment. On reset, all registers clear. Output is zero when output enable is inactive.
