Implement a 4-stage pipeline multiplier with a 4-bit enable shift register to propagate mul_en_in through the stages. Sample inputs only when mul_en_in is asserted. Generate 8 partial products combinationally by bit-shifting the multiplicand based on multiplier bits. Use a straightforward pipeline addition approach with three stages:

- Stage 1: Register partial products.
- Stage 2: Sum lower and upper halves separately.
- Stage 3: Final sum to produce the product.

At each pipeline stage, registers update only if the respective enable bit is asserted to reduce switching power. The output enable is the MSB of the enable shift register. Output is zero when enable is low. This design simplifies addition steps and enables efficient pipelining without complex grouping or multi-level summations, keeping the design clean and easy to maintain while preserving full functionality.
