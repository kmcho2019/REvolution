Simplify the pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier by reducing the pipeline depth and complexity of partial product accumulation. Use a 3-stage pipeline: input register stage, partial product generation and sum, and output register stage. Instead of a full adder tree pipeline, sum all partial products combinationally and register the final product once. Propagate the enable signal through a 3-bit shift register to align with pipeline latency. This reduces pipeline registers and adds clarity while maintaining throughput with a one-cycle latency after inputs are latched.
