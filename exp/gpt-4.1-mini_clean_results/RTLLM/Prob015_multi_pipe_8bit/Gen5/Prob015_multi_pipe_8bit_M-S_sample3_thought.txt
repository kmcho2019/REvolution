Implement a simplified 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a 3-stage pipeline. On each clock edge, sample the input enable and inputs into registers when enabled. Generate partial products combinationally by bitwise AND and left-shifts. Reduce complexity by summing all partial products in one combinational step rather than multiple intermediate registered sums, then register the final sum. Track the enable signal through a simple 3-bit shift register pipeline to control output validity and zero the output when disabled. This approach reduces the number of registers and summation stages while maintaining correct pipelined multiplication functionality.
