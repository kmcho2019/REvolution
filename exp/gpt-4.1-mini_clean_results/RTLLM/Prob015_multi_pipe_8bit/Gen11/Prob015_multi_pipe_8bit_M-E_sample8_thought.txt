Implement a fully pipelined 8-bit multiplier using an 8-stage pipeline where each stage computes and accumulates one partial product corresponding to each bit of the multiplier. The multiplicand and multiplier inputs are registered when the input enable is asserted. The enable signal is shifted through the pipeline stages to track validity. Each stage computes a partial product by ANDing the multiplicand with one bit of the multiplier, shifted appropriately, and adds it to a cumulative sum passed from the previous stage. This design avoids a large combinational adder summing all partial products at once, distributing addition across pipeline stages and thus reducing critical path delay. The final stage outputs the 16-bit product along with the enable signal indicating output validity.
