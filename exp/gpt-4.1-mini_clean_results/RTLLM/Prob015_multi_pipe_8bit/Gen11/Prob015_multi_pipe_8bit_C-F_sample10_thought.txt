Fuse the two designs by adopting a multi-stage pipelined accumulation approach to reduce the critical path delay caused by summing all eight partial products simultaneously. 

Strategy:
- Stage 1: Register inputs and input enable signal when enabled.
- Stage 2: Generate and register partial products individually in parallel.
- Stage 3: Sum partial products hierarchically in two pipeline stages: first sum pairs of partial products into four partial sums, then sum these partial sums into the final product.
- Pipeline the enable signal through three stages matching data pipeline stages.
- Output the final product and output enable signal synchronously.
- Use non-blocking assignments and asynchronous reset for robust synchronous design.
- This structure shortens the combinational critical path per stage, improving max clock frequency.
- Gating register updates by enable signals reduces unnecessary switching, improving power.
- The hierarchical pipeline with partial product registers and partial sum registers optimizes area by splitting the wide adder into smaller adders.

This balanced pipeline fusion combines input capture and enable tracking from Example 2, partial product generation and enable gating from Example 1, and hierarchical staged accumulation from neither example but inspired by the stated optimization needs in feedback. The result is a three-stage pipeline for better performance, manageable power, and reasonable area.
