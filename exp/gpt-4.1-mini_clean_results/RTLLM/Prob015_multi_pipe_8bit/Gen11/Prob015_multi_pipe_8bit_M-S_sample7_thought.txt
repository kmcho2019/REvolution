Implement a simple pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier with three pipeline registers: 
1) register inputs and enable; 
2) generate and register partial sums for lower and upper halves of the multiplier bits; 
3) register the final product sum and output enable. 
This pipeline breaks the addition of partial products into smaller stages, reducing combinational complexity per stage and improving timing. 
Each stage updates on clock edge with active-low reset. 
Output is gated by the output enable signal.
