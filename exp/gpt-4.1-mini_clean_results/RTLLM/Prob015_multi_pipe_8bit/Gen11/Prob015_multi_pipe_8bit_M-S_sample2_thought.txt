To simplify the 8-bit pipelined multiplier while maintaining functionality, use just two pipeline stages: one to register inputs and generate partial products, and a second to sum all partial products and register the output. Capture the input enable along with inputs, generate partial products as shifted versions of the multiplicand gated by multiplier bits, and sum them in one registered stage. Drive the output enable signal by delaying the input enable through a pipeline register. This approach reduces complexity by using only two pipeline stages without intermediate partial sum registers and keeps the design straightforward and synchronous with active-low reset.
