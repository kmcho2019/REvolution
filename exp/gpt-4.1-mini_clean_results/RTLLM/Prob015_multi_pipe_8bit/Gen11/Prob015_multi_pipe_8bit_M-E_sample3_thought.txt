Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a 3-stage pipeline architecture to improve maximum clock frequency by dividing the multiplication into smaller combinational blocks. 

Stage 1: Register inputs and enable, generate 8 partial products (each 16-bit) in parallel.  
Stage 2: Pairwise add partial products to get 4 intermediate sums (16-bit registers).  
Stage 3: Add intermediate sums pairwise to get 2 sums (16-bit registers).  
Stage 4: Final addition of the last two sums to obtain the product.

Enable signals are pipelined alongside data to indicate valid output at each stage. This reduces the critical path delay by distributing addition across pipeline stages, allowing higher clock frequency while maintaining proper data flow and synchronization. Output is zeroed if output enable is inactive. Active-low reset clears all registers.
