Simplify the pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier by splitting the addition of partial products into two pipeline stages:
1. On input enable, register inputs and enable.
2. Generate partial products for each multiplier bit and add them pairwise into four sums, register these sums.
3. On next clock cycle, add these four sums pairwise into two sums, register again.
4. Finally, add the last two sums on a third stage to produce the 16-bit product output and register it.

Use a 3-bit pipeline register for enable signals to track the valid data flow. Output enable is the MSB of this register.

This staged addition pipeline reduces combinational logic depth per cycle, improving timing and lowering complexity compared to a single-cycle summation of all partial products.
