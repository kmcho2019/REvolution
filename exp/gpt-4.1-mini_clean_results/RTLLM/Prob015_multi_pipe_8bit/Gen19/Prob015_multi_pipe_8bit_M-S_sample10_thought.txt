Implement a single-cycle pipelined multiplier where input registers capture inputs and enable when mul_en_in is asserted. The product is computed combinationally by summing partial products generated via shifts and conditional assignments of the multiplicand based on multiplier bits. The output product and enable are registered to produce mul_out and mul_en_out signals. This design simplifies the pipeline to two stages and reduces logic by avoiding deep adder trees, ensuring functional correctness and proper enable propagation.
