Simplify the pipelined multiplier by using a single pipeline stage: register inputs and enable, then compute the product combinationally in the same cycle. Output enable is delayed by one clock cycle alongside the inputs to maintain timing. This approach eliminates partial sums and intermediate registers, relying on a single stage pipeline that registers inputs and enable, then produces the product immediately in the next cycle. This keeps the design simple, synchronous, and fully pipelined with minimal registers and logic.
