Implement a straightforward 4-stage pipeline for the unsigned 8-bit multiplier to reduce combinational complexity and keep the pipeline balanced. The stages are: 
1) Input latch stage where inputs and enable are sampled; 
2) Partial product generation stage which computes all partial products as 16-bit shifted values using simple bitwise conditions; 
3) Summation stage that adds the partial products using a balanced 3-step addition pipeline to spread the addition load over multiple cycles and registers the intermediate sums; 
4) Final output stage that registers the final product and propagates the enable signal through a 4-bit shift register. This structure balances the combinational load across clock cycles, simplifies partial product handling, and maintains correct output enable timing.
