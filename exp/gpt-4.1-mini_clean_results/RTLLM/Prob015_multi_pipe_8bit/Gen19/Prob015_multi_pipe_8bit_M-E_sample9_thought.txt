Implement a multi-stage pipeline multiplier where each pipeline stage processes one bit of the multiplier. At each clock cycle, the stage examines a single bit of mul_b and adds the multiplicand shifted by that bit position to an accumulated sum if the multiplier bit is set. Input operands and enable are registered initially. Then, for 8 cycles (pipeline stages), the partial sum and bit index are propagated along with the enable signal. This approach fully pipelines the multiplication over 8 stages, spreading out the addition and reducing combinational delay per cycle. The output enable is delayed along with the pipeline stages, asserting when the product is valid. This design reduces critical path by simple shift-and-add per cycle, uses registers for intermediate sums, and keeps control signals synchronized through the pipeline.
