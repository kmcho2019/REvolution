Implement a 4-stage pipeline for the unsigned 8-bit multiplier to reduce the combinational path and balance the workload. The stages are: 
1) Input registers stage (sample operands and enable);
2) Partial product generation stage, where each bit of multiplier generates a partial product shifted accordingly, stored in registers to break the combinational path;
3) Partial sum accumulation stage using a balanced adder tree structure with intermediate registers for partial sums, splitting the addition into smaller steps and pipelining it;
4) Final product register stage to output the result.

The enable signal is pipelined through a 4-bit shift register to align with the data pipeline latency. This architecture minimizes combinational delay per cycle, improves timing and throughput, and balances the pipeline stages for a clean and efficient design.
