Simplify the pipeline to three stages for easier implementation and reduced complexity while maintaining the essential pipelined structure and functionality:
- Stage 1: sample inputs and generate all 8 partial products combinationally (from registered inputs).
- Stage 2: sum all partial products into one register (using a single multi-operand addition done in one clock cycle).
- Stage 3: register the final product output.
- Propagate the mul_en_in signal through a 3-stage shift register pipeline for timing and output enable.
- Output enable (mul_en_out) is derived from the last stage of the enable pipeline.
- When enable signals are low, registers hold zero to reduce switching.
This simpler pipeline reduces the number of pipeline registers and stages but still allows input sampling, partial product generation, summation, and registered output with enable synchronization.
