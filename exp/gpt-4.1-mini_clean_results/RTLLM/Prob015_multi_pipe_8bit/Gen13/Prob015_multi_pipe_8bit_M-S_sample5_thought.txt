To simplify the pipelined unsigned 8-bit multiplier design while maintaining functionality:

- Use a straightforward 3-stage pipeline:
  1. Register inputs and input enable.
  2. Generate partial products from the registered inputs.
  3. Sum all partial products in a single registered stage.

- Track the enable signal through a simple 3-bit shift register pipeline to produce mul_en_out aligned with output.

- Partial products are generated using conditional shifts based on multiplier bits.

- Sum partial products by adding all shifted multiplicand parts in one go at the final pipeline stage.

- Registers only update when mul_en_in is high; otherwise, hold previous value.

- Output mul_out is driven by the registered sum only when mul_en_out is asserted; otherwise, zero.

This approach reduces the number of intermediate pipeline registers and complexity of staged summation, simplifying design and verification while preserving pipelining and output valid signaling.
