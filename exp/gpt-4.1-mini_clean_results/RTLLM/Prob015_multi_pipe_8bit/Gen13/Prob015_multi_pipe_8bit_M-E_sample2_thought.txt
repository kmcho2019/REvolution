Implement a multi-stage pipeline multiplier that processes partial product addition in several pipeline registers to reduce the combinational path delay per cycle.  
Stage 1: Sample inputs and mul_en_in; generate partial products by ANDing mul_a with each bit of mul_b, storing these partial products into registers.  
Stage 2: Pairwise add the partial products in registers to form intermediate sums, reducing the number of sums.  
Stage 3: Final addition stage sums the intermediate sums into the final product register and propagates the enable signal.  
The output enable is taken from the final stage's enable register. The output is zero when the enable is low.  
This approach balances latency and throughput, decreases critical path delay by spreading additions over pipeline stages, and efficiently propagates enable signals.
