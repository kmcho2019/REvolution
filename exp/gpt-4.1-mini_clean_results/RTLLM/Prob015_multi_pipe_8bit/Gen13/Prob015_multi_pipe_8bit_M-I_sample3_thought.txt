To improve timing (reduce critical path) and overall PPA, break the large combinational addition of 8 partial products into multiple pipeline stages with balanced binary tree addition.

Implement a 3-stage pipeline:

1. Stage 1: Register inputs and generate partial products.
2. Stage 2: Sum partial products pairwise into four 16-bit partial sums.
3. Stage 3: Sum the four partial sums pairwise into two sums.
4. Stage 4: Final addition of the two sums and register output.

Track the enable signal through 4 pipeline flip-flops accordingly.

This staged addition reduces combinational depth per cycle, improving max frequency and reducing power and area.

The pipeline registers also register the enable signal to keep control valid through stages.

Outputs will be valid 4 cycles after input enable.

This deeper pipeline increases latency but improves throughput and clock speed.

All registers reset on rst_n, active-low.

The output enable reflects valid mul_out data.
