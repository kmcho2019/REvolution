Implement a fully pipelined 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a classic shift-and-add approach across multiple pipeline stages. On each clock cycle (stage), the design processes one bit of the multiplier, generating a partial product (multiplicand shifted by the bit position if the multiplier bit is set) and accumulating it into a running sum. This approach pipelines the multiplication over 8 stages, each stage performing a single partial product addition, thus evenly distributing the adder's delay and enabling a higher clock frequency.

Registers track the multiplicand, multiplier, enable signals, and partial sums at each pipeline stage. The pipeline registers shift forward the enable signal to assert mul_en_out when the final product is ready. Reset clears all pipeline registers. This design produces one result per clock cycle after an initial latency of 8 cycles.

This method balances power, performance, and area by reducing the combinational adder delay per stage and leveraging simple shift-add logic with a straightforward control scheme.
