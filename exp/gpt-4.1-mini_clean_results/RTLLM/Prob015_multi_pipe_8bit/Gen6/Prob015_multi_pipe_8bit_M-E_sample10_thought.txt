Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a 4-stage pipeline, where partial products are generated and added progressively in stages to reduce combinational delay and improve timing. Stage 1: sample inputs and enable. Stage 2: generate all 8 partial products (each multiplicand bit ANDed with multiplier bits, shifted accordingly) registered in partial product registers. Stage 3 and 4: sum partial products using a balanced adder tree split into two stages, registering intermediate sums to break long carry chains. The enable signal propagates through a 4-bit shift register pipeline to synchronize output validity. Output product is registered and gated by the output enable. This approach distributes addition workload over multiple clock cycles, improving clock frequency and reducing critical path while keeping pipelined throughput.
