Implement an 8-stage pipeline multiplier where each pipeline stage processes one bit of the multiplier sequentially. At each clock cycle, if enabled, the partial sum accumulates the current bit's partial product (multiplicand shifted by the bit position) when the multiplier bit is set. This serial approach spreads the addition across 8 clock cycles, reducing combinational complexity per cycle and increasing maximum frequency. The input enable is registered and shifted through an 8-bit pipeline to produce output enable aligned with the final result. The final product register holds the accumulated sum at the end of the pipeline. This design balances throughput and clock frequency by using fine-grained pipelining with small combinational logic per stage.
