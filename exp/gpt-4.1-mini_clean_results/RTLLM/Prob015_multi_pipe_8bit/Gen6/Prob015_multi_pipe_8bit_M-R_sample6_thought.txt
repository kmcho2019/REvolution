Refactor the pipelined 8-bit multiplier to clearly separate the pipeline stages by using explicit pipeline stage registers for partial products and sums. Instead of summing all partial products combinationally in one step, first generate each partial product and register them to form a pipeline stage. Then, add pairs of partial products stepwise in the next pipeline stage registers to balance the addition tree and reduce combinational delay per stage.
