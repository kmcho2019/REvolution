Implement the 8-bit unsigned multiplier with a classic shift-add multiply algorithm distributed over 8 pipeline stages, one stage per multiplier bit. On each clock cycle, when enabled, the design shifts the multiplier and accumulates the partial sum accordingly. The inputs and enable signal are first registered synchronously, then a pipeline registers the intermediate product and shifted multiplier for each stage. Each pipeline stage conditionally adds the multiplicand shifted by the stage index if the LSB of the shifted multiplier is set, then shifts the multiplier right by one bit. This yields a fully pipelined multiplier with one partial product addition per cycle across 8 cycles. The output enable signal is generated via an 8-bit shift register delayed by 8 cycles. Reset clears all pipeline registers and outputs.
