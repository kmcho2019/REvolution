Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a 5-stage pipeline with a balanced, stepwise partial product accumulation. Instead of summing all partial products in a single stage, this design accumulates partial products progressively with one addition per pipeline stage, shifting partial sums accordingly. It samples inputs and enables synchronously, pipelines the enable signal as a shift register, and outputs the result after 5 cycles. This reduces combinational logic per cycle, improving timing by distributing adders evenly and uses a simple one-adder-per-stage approach with registers storing intermediate sums.
