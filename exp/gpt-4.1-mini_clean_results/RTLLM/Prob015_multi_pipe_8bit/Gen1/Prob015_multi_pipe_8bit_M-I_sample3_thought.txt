   E.g., (mul_a_reg << i) for partial product i.

   This avoids stale or invalid partial sums.

5. **Clear registers on reset for deterministic startup.**

6. **Output mul_out is assigned mul_out_reg only when mul_en_out is high, else zero.**

7. **Ensure no combinational loops or invalid conditions.**

- Stage 1: Sample inputs and enable.
- Stage 2: Compute and sum partial products pairwise.
