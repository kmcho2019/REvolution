Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined over 8 stages, each stage adding 8 bits plus the carry-in. Each pipeline stage registers the operands, the carry-in, the partial sum, and the enable signal. The carry-out and enable propagate to the next stage through registers. At the last stage, assemble all the partial sums and final carry-out into the 65-bit result. This design simplifies indexing by treating pipeline arrays uniformly and assembling the final sum using a simple loop. The pipeline latency is 8 cycles, and the output enable (o_en) reflects the delayed input enable (i_en).
