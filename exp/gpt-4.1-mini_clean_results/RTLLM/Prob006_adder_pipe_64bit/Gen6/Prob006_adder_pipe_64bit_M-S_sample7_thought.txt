Implement an 8-stage pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by dividing the inputs into 8-bit slices. In each stage, add the corresponding 8-bit slices and the carry-in from the previous stage. Pipeline the input slices, carry signals, and enable signal through registers synchronized to the clock. Assemble the final 65-bit result from the pipeline registers once all stages are complete and assert the output enable signal accordingly. This approach uses fixed pipeline stages for simplicity and clear synchronization.
