Simplify the 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelining into 4 stages of 16 bits each by explicitly creating pipeline registers only for the carry, partial sums, and the enable signal. Instead of separate operand pipelines, directly slice inputs per stage on each clock when enabled. Each stage adds its 16-bit slice and the carry-in from the previous stage's pipeline register. This reduces register arrays and complexity while preserving functionality and correct carry propagation. The output enable follows the pipeline stages to indicate valid result availability.
