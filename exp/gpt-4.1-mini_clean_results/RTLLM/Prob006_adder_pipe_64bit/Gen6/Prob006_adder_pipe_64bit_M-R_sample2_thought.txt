Refactor the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by separating the combinational addition from the pipelined registers. The pipeline registers will hold operands and carry-in per stage, and the sum and carry-out will be calculated combinationally each cycle. This approach uses separate registers for operand slices and carries, and combinational logic for addition. The pipeline control signal (enable) is managed by a shift register.

Each clock cycle, operand slices and carry-in registers are updated from the previous stage's registered results. The combinational adder computes sum and carry-out based on the registered inputs. The output result and enable signal are registered at the pipeline exit stage.

This structure allows clean separation of combinational and sequential logic, easing timing analysis and enhancing clarity. No repeated addition inside `always` loops; instead, combinational adders between pipeline registers. The final output is assembled from pipelined sum slices and carry-out. This structure is robust, clear, and synthesizable.

Key points:
- Pipeline registers store inputs and carry-in for each stage
- Addition is combinational: sum and carry-out computed from registered operands and carry-in
- Enable is pipelined as shift register
- Output assembled from pipelined sums and final carry-out, registered on clk
