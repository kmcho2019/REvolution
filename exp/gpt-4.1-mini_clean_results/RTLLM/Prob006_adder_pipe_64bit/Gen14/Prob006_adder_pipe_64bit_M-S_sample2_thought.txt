Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with 4 pipeline stages, each handling 16 bits of the operands. Each pipeline stage registers its inputs and the carry-in, computes a 16-bit sum and carry-out, and forwards them to the next stage on the next clock cycle. The input enable signal (i_en) is pipelined along to generate the output enable (o_en). The final 65-bit result is assembled from the four 16-bit sum registers plus the final carry-out. This approach simplifies the design by using fewer and larger pipeline stages while maintaining the pipelined ripple carry addition functionality.
