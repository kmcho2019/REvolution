Implement a fully synchronous 64-bit ripple carry adder with a pipeline stage every 16 bits (4 pipeline stages total). 

Key points of the architecture:
- Divide the 64-bit addition into 4 stages of 16 bits each.
- Each pipeline stage adds its 16-bit slice plus the carry-in from the previous stage.
- Use registers to store the partial sums and carry bits at each stage, synchronizing with clk and rst_n.
- Propagate the carry from one pipeline stage to the next via registered carry signals.
- The pipeline is enabled by i_en, which is also registered and delayed through the pipeline to generate o_en.
- The final 65-bit sum is formed by concatenating all the pipeline stage sums and the last carry out.
- On reset, all pipeline registers and enable signals are cleared.
- This approach balances pipeline depth (4 stages) and manageable complexity.
- This structure simplifies carry propagation with minimal combinational delay inside each pipeline stage.
