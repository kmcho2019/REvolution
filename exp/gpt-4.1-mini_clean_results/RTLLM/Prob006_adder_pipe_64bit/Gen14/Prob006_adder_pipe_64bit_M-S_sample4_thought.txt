To simplify the design while keeping the pipelined ripple carry adder functionality, I will implement a straightforward 64-bit adder pipeline with 8 stages of 8-bit additions. Each stage will add 8 bits of the inputs along with the carry from the previous stage. Pipeline registers will hold partial sums, carry bits, and enable signals. The design will avoid generate blocks and arrays to reduce complexity, using simple registers and combinational logic inside the sequential block. This approach keeps the pipeline structure, synchronizes the enable signal, and assembles the final 65-bit result with a valid output enable, while making the code more concise and easier to understand.
