To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder, I will:

- Use 8 pipeline stages, each handling 8 bits of addition.
- Store operands and carry-in signals in pipeline registers.
- Calculate sum and carry-out combinationally at each stage.
- Shift the enable signal through pipeline registers to track valid data.
- On `i_en`, load operand slices and start addition with zero carry-in at stage 0.
- Propagate operand slices, carry, sums, and enable signals through pipeline registers on each clock.
- Output the full 65-bit sum and `o_en` when the final stage's data is valid.
- Reset all pipeline registers synchronously on `rst_n`.

This approach maintains the 8-stage pipeline for throughput, but avoids shifting operands multiple times in one cycle and simplifies the enable and carry management logic.
