Refactor the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by removing the use of arrayed pipeline registers for operands and enables, and instead use stage-wise individual registers per pipeline stage to hold operands, enables, sum, and carries. Implement the pipeline entirely using non-blocking sequential registers to hold each pipeline stage’s inputs and outputs explicitly, and use continuous assignments for the stage partial sums and carry computations.

This approach:
- Removes the need for arrayed reg memories and uses simple reg signals per stage, improving clarity.
- Uses explicit pipeline stages with one always block updating all stage registers in a single clock domain.
- Uses combinational adders stage by stage for partial sums and carry-out.
- Enables easier extension and debugging due to flat stage signals.
- Assigns output `result` and `o_en` directly from the final pipeline stage registers.
- Keeps the carry propagation and sum partitioning as per the original segmentation (16-bit stages).
