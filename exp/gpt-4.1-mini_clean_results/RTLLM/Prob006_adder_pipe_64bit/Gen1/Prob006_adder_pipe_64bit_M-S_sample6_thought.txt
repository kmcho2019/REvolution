Simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by dividing it into 4 stages of 16 bits each. Each stage registers its partial sum and carry-out synchronously.

- At each clock cycle with i_en asserted, capture the current 16-bit operand segments for that stage.
- Compute sum and carry-out using the stored operands and carry-in from previous stage.
- Pipeline the carry and enable signals through registers.
- After 4 cycles (pipeline depth), output the concatenated 65-bit sum and assert o_en.
- Use synchronous reset to clear registers and outputs.
  
This removes unnecessary arrays and simplifies control by focusing on registers per stage and pipelining enables and carry signals in separate registers.
