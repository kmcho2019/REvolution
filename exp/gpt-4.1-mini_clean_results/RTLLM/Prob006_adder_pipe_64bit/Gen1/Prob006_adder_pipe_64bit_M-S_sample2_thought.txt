To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder, I will implement it with 2 pipeline stages instead of 4, each handling 32 bits. Each stage adds its 32-bit slice of the inputs plus the carry from the previous stage. The carry is registered and passed to the next stage, pipelining the carry propagation.

The input enable signal (i_en) will be pipelined through 2 registers to generate the output enable (o_en) synchronized with the result availability.

This reduces complexity by halving the number of pipeline stages and registers, while maintaining a balanced pipeline and a 2-cycle latency.
