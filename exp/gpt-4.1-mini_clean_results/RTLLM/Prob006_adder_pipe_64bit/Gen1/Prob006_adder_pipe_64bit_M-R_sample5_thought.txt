Refactor the design by separating combinational addition from sequential pipeline registers. Use intermediate wires to perform the combinational 16-bit additions for each stage with carry-in, then register the sums, carries, operands, and enable signals at the clock edge. This clean separation avoids illegal reg declarations inside always blocks and allows proper pipelining of inputs, sums, and carry signals. The pipeline depth remains 4 stages, each handling 16 bits, with carry propagated through registered stages. The output result and output enable signal are registered at the last pipeline stage.
