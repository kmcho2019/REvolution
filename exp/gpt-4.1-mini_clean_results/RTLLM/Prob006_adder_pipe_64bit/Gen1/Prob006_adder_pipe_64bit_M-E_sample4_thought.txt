I will implement a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder using one bit per pipeline stage for maximum granularity and throughput. Each stage will add a single bit from adda and addb along with the carry-in from the previous stage, registering the sum bit and carry-out at each clock cycle. The pipeline registers will store the carry and sum bits progressively, producing the final 65-bit result after 64 clock cycles of latency. The input enable (i_en) will be pipelined through a shift register of length 64 to generate the output enable (o_en) corresponding to when the result is valid. This fine-grained pipeline achieves maximum clock frequency at the cost of longer latency, but it perfectly demonstrates pipelined ripple carry adder architecture with registers at every bit stage.
