I will refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by splitting the logic into separate pipeline stage modules and using generate blocks to instantiate the stages, improving modularity and clarity. Each stage will be a combinational adder of 16 bits plus carry-in, with registered pipeline registers for partial sums and carry-outs on the clock edge. The enable signal and input operands will be registered stage-by-stage in pipeline registers. The final output and o_en will be registered at the last stage. This approach uses explicit pipeline stage registers, separating combinational adders and pipeline registers cleanly, avoiding complex loops inside always blocks, improving readability and synthesis friendliness.
