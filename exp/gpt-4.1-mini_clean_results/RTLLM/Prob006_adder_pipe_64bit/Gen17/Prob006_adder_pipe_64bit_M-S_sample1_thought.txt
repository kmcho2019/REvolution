Implement a straightforward 64-bit ripple carry adder with 4 pipeline stages (each stage adding 16 bits). Register inputs, partial sums, and carry between stages to form a pipeline. The enable signal is pipelined along the stages to generate the output enable (o_en).

Use arrays of registers for operands and carry signals per stage, updated synchronously on clk with reset. The final 65-bit sum is constructed by concatenating stage sums and final carry-out. Keep the design simple and fully synchronous with no combinational loops.

This simplification keeps fixed parameters and a fixed number of stages for clarity and easy verification.
