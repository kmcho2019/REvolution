A different architectural approach is to design a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder divided into 8 stages, each handling 8 bits with carry input and carry output registers between stages. 

Each stage will have registers to store the inputs, carry-in, sum output, and carry-out, fully synchronizing data and carry signals on the clock with the pipeline. This ensures that every stage completes an 8-bit addition with carry in one clock cycle, passing the carry out and enabling high clock frequency.

The pipeline input registers capture the 64-bit operands and the enable signal. The output stage registers capture the final sum (65 bits including carry-out) and output enable.

This approach avoids combinational complexity per stage, using simple registered 8-bit adders with carry in and carry out signals pipelined stage by stage.

The pipeline is fully balanced, with input operand registers, internal carry and sum registers at each stage, and output registers at the end.

The output enable signal is pipelined through registers alongside data to indicate the valid output cycle.

This clear and modular design maximizes timing closure and eases verification.
