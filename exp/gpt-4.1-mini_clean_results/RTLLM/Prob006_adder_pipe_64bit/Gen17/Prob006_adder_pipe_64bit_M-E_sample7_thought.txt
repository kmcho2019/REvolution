Implement the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder with eight pipeline stages, each handling 8-bit slices of the operands. In this design, the inputs are fully registered at stage 0 to ensure stable propagation through the pipeline. Each pipeline stage computes the partial sum and carry-out using combinational logic based on its 8-bit operand slices and the carry-in from the previous stage. The partial sum and carry-out are stored in pipeline registers at the next clock edge, progressing through the pipeline stages. 

The carry is propagated sequentially stage-by-stage through pipeline registers, realizing a ripple carry behavior but spread over multiple clock cycles, effectively pipelining the carry propagation. The output enable signal (o_en) is pipelined alongside the data through a register chain of length equal to the number of stages, indicating when the output result is valid.

The final output is formed by concatenating the 8-bit sum parts from each stage and appending the final carry-out as the MSB, yielding a 65-bit result. This approach balances pipeline depth and latency by using smaller bit slices (8 bits per stage), which reduces combinational delay per stage, suitable for high-frequency operation. The design also ensures all pipeline registers and control signals are synchronized to clk and reset asynchronously with active low rst_n.
