The main issues in the previous implementation are:
1. The operand slices (`adda_slices` and `addb_slices`) are only loaded at stage 0 and never shifted through the pipeline. This breaks the pipeline flow because each stage should operate on its own slice of operands, advancing together through the pipeline.
2. The final result concatenation manually accesses specific indices and incorrectly orders or misses slices, risking out-of-bounds access and wrong results.
3. Redundant assignments hold pipeline registers unnecessarily.

Improvement strategy:
- Pipeline operand slices similarly to the enable and carry signals, shifting them through each stage on every clock cycle when enabled.
- Use a for-loop or generate a systematic concatenation of sum slices in correct order (from the highest stage down to stage 0) for assembling the output.
- Clean up redundant assignments and better synchronize carry propagation and enable signals.
- Initialize carry at zero at stage 0.
- The pipeline depth is 8 stages (64 bits / 8 bits per stage).
- The final result is 65-bit wide (64-bit sum + carry out).
- On reset, clear all pipeline registers.
- When `i_en` is high, load input operands at stage 0; otherwise, shift operand slices and sum slices down the pipeline to maintain proper pipelining.

This will yield a fully pipelined ripple carry adder with 8 stages, producing the result after 8 cycles, indicated by `o_en`.
