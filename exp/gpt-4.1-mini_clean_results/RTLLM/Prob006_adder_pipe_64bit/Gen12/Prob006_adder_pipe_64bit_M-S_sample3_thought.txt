To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder while preserving functionality:

1. Keep fixed pipeline parameters: 8 pipeline stages each handling 8 bits.
2. Register input operand slices directly each cycle when i_en is asserted.
3. Propagate carry and enable signals through pipeline registers.
4. Compute sum and carry per stage in sequential always block to keep timing and avoid combinational loops.
5. Assemble final 65-bit result from registered sums and final carry at output stage.
6. Use a single always block for synchronous logic to reduce complexity.
7. Hold registers only when i_en is low, otherwise update straightforwardly.
8. Keep code clean, minimal, and readable without unnecessary combinational blocks.

This simpler approach keeps correct carry propagation and pipelining with clearly registered inputs, sums, carries, and enables.
