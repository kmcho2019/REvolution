I will implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with an 8-stage pipeline, each stage handling 8 bits. Each stage will add its 8-bit slice of inputs and the carry from the previous stage, registering the partial sum, carry-out, and enable signal for synchronization. To simplify, I'll use arrays for pipeline registers and a single always block controlling all pipeline stages. The input enable and carry signals will be registered through the pipeline. The final 65-bit result and output enable will be output after the last pipeline stage. This design balances simplicity and pipelined operation.
