Implement a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder with 8 pipeline stages, each processing 8 bits of the input operands. Each pipeline stage has registers to hold its 8-bit operands and carry-in, computes an 8-bit sum and carry-out in one clock cycle, and passes the carry-out to the next stage. The input enable signal is also pipelined along the stages to generate the output enable (o_en). The final 65-bit result is formed by concatenating all 8-bit sums from each stage plus the final carry-out. This design increases pipeline depth for higher clock frequency and clean synchronization of signals through all stages.
