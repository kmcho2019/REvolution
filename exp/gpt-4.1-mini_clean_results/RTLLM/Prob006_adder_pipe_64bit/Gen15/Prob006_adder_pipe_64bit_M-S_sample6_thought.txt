Simplify the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by dividing the addition into 8 stages of 8 bits each, each stage registering its sum and carry out. Instead of storing full 64-bit inputs at each pipeline stage, only propagate partial sums and carries.

On each clock cycle with enable, stage 0 adds bits [7:0] with carry in 0. On subsequent cycles, each stage adds its 8-bit slice plus carry from previous stage's carry register. Pipeline registers store partial sums and carries.

The output is assembled from all 8 sum segments plus the final carry after 8 pipeline stages, with a delayed output enable.

Reset clears all pipeline registers and outputs.

This design reduces register usage and complexity by avoiding propagating full input operands through the pipeline, only partial sums and carries, and cleanly pipeline the carry ripple through 8 stages.
