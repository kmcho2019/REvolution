Refactor the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by structuring it with explicit pipeline registers at each stage that register inputs, carries, and valid signals. Each stage computes its 16-bit sum and carry-out combinationally from registered inputs and carries from the previous stage. The pipeline registers update on the clock edge, controlled by reset and enable signals. This clean separation of combinational addition and sequential registers improves clarity and timing. The output enable signal is pipelined alongside, indicating when the final sum is valid. The final 65-bit result concatenates the sum segments and the final carry-out in correct order.
