Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder in a pipelined fashion by splitting the addition into several stages (e.g., 4 stages of 16 bits each). Each stage adds 16 bits of the input operands along with the carry-in from the previous stage. Pipeline registers store the intermediate sums, carry, and enable signals to synchronize the operation across clock cycles. The final output is the concatenation of all partial sums plus the last carry-out, making a 65-bit result. The output enable is also pipelined to indicate the availability of the result. The reset signal initializes registers, and the inputs are accepted when i_en is high.
