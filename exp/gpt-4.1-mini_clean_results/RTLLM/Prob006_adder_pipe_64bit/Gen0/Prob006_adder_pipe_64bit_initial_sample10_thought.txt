To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelining, I will split the 64-bit addition into multiple pipeline stages, each computing a portion of the sum and carry. Each stage will compute a part of the addition along with carry propagation and store the intermediate results in registers to enable pipelining.

I will use 4 pipeline stages, each adding 16 bits of the input operands, plus the carry from the previous stage. Each stage will register the sum portion and the carry-out for the next stage. The enable signal (i_en) will be pipelined alongside to synchronize the output enable (o_en).

The final output will be formed by concatenating all partial sums and the final carry-out bit, resulting in a 65-bit result.

Reset (rst_n) will clear the pipeline registers and output enable.

This approach balances between complexity and latency, and avoids creating a fully combinational 64-bit ripple carry adder in one cycle, instead breaking it down into pipeline stages.
