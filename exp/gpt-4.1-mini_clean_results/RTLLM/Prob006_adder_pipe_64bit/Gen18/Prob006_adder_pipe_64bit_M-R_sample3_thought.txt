I will implement the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by explicitly defining pipeline registers for inputs, carry, and partial sums stage-by-stage, but instead of a monolithic always block with loops, I will use a clear structural style with separate always blocks per pipeline stage to improve readability and maintainability. The pipeline will consist of 4 stages each handling 16 bits, with registers capturing inputs, sums, and carries. The output enable and result will be registered at the final stage, synchronized with the clock and reset. This refactoring uses separate always blocks and intermediate wires for combinational addition, avoiding complex loops and improve modular clarity. It also ensures correct concatenation of partial sums in order from lowest to highest bits.
