Refactor the 64-bit ripple carry adder pipeline by separating the pipeline registers for operand slices and carry signals from the combinational addition logic. Instead of computing the sum and carry inside the clocked always block, perform combinational addition using `assign` statements from registered operand slices and carry inputs. Then, register the outputs to create pipeline stages.

This approach clearly separates combinational addition from sequential logic, making the pipeline stages more explicit and improving readability. The enable signal is still pipelined as a shift register, and the final output is assembled by concatenating sums and final carry. The carry-in for the first stage is zero, and subsequent carry-outs are pipelined registers clocked from the combinational adder outputs.

This also allows simpler timing analysis because all combinational logic is outside of clocked always blocks, and only registers are in sequential logic.

The pipeline latency remains 8 cycles with 8 stages of 8-bit slices.

Finally, resets clear all pipeline registers and outputs.

This refactoring uses:
- Separate operand slice registers clocked at clk
- Combinational adders producing sum and carry-out for each stage
- Carry registers clocked at clk to feed next stage
- Enable shift register for output enable timing
