Refactor the 64-bit 4-stage ripple carry adder pipeline by separating combinational adders from the pipeline registers. In this design, input operands are registered stage-by-stage, and combinational adders compute sums with carry-ins. Carry signals and sum segments are registered in pipeline registers, allowing clean separation of combinational and sequential logic. The output enable signal is propagated through pipeline registers. This method uses separate always blocks for registering inputs, carries, sums, and enables, and combinational assignments for adders, enhancing clarity and maintainability.
