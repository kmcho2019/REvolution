Simplify the design by using a straightforward 4-stage pipeline, each handling 16 bits. Each stage registers its inputs and carry-in, computes a 16-bit sum with carry-out combinationally within a clock cycle, then registers the sum and carry-out for the next stage. The input operands and enable are registered at the input stage, and the output sum and enable are registered at the output stage.

This reduces pipeline depth and complexity, eases understanding, while preserving the pipelined addition operation with carry propagation through pipeline registers.

Carry is passed as a registered signal between stages, and the output enable signal is pipelined alongside data to indicate valid output.

The final output is a 65-bit sum including the last carry out.
