Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by splitting the pipeline into four explicit pipeline stages, each adding a 16-bit slice plus carry-in, and storing partial sums and carry-outs into pipeline registers at each clock. The input operands, enable, and carry signals are registered at each stage explicitly. The final output result is assembled by concatenating the partial sums and the final carry-out at the last stage. Instead of using arrays and generate blocks for adders inside always blocks, I will use combinational assignments outside of sequential blocks for add operations and then store their results into stage registers. This approach simplifies the design, avoids complex indexing, and clearly separates combinational addition and sequential pipeline registers. The enable signals and carries are pipelined alongside the operands to generate synchronized output enable and result. Reset asynchronously clears all pipeline registers. This structure improves readability and makes timing of pipeline registers explicit.
