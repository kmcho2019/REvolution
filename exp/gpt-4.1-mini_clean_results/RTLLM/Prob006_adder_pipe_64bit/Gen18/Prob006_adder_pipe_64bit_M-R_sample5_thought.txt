Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by clearly separating the pipeline stages and ensuring data and control signals shift every clock cycle, regardless of input enable. Use arrays of registers for operands, sums, and carry signals, and make sure they all advance synchronously.

- Pipeline enable signals track data flow precisely.
- Assemble final result after all stages complete by concatenating sum slices and last carry in a dedicated combinational block, avoiding sequential overwriting.
