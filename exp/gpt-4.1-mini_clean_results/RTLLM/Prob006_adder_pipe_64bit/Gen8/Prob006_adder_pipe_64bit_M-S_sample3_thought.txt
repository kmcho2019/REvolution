I will simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by using 4 pipeline stages instead of 8, adding 16 bits per stage. This reduces the number of registers and logic blocks, making the design simpler but still pipelined.

Key points:

- Each stage adds a 16-bit slice of the inputs along with the carry from the previous stage.

- Pipeline registers hold operand slices, sum outputs, carry-out, and enable signals for synchronization.

- The carry ripples from stage to stage through registers, maintaining the ripple carry behavior.

- The output enable signal (o_en) is delayed through the pipeline stages to indicate when the final sum is valid.

- On reset, registers and signals are cleared.

This approach balances pipeline depth, simplicity, and performance.
