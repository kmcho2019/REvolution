Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by dividing the adder into four pipeline stages, each handling 16-bit slices. For each stage, inputs and control signals (carry-in and enable) are registered on the clock edge with reset. Instead of mixing combinational assignments and sequential blocks, use explicit stage registers to hold partial sums and carries, updating them synchronously. The carry-out of each stage is registered and fed into the next stage. The output enable signal is also pipelined along with carry and partial sums, ensuring proper synchronization. Finally, the 65-bit result is assembled from all the partial sums and the last carry-out, available with a registered output enable, reflecting pipeline latency. This structure clearly separates combinational sum calculations from pipeline registers, providing clean stage boundaries and better timing closure.
