To fix the elaboration errors, declare `DATA_WIDTH` and `STG_WIDTH` as parameters instead of localparams, enabling the testbench to override them during instantiation. Define `DATA_WIDTH` as 64 by default, `STG_WIDTH` as 8, and calculate the number of pipeline stages accordingly. This makes the module flexible, compatible with the testbench, and still implements an 8-bit segmented ripple carry adder pipeline. The rest of the design remains similar, with pipelining of slices, carry signals, sums, and enable signals. The outputs are registered and synchronized with `clk` and `rst_n`.
