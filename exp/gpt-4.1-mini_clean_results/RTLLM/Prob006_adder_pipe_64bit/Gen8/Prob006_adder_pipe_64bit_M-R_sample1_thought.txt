Refactor the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by explicitly implementing eight 8-bit addition stages with registered sums and carries to form a pipeline. Instead of combining zero-padded vectors and single-bit carry with complicated concatenations, perform each stage's addition as a simple addition of an 8-bit operand plus the carry-in bit extended to 9 bits. We use the carry-out bit from the lower stage as the carry-in of the next stage.

Pipeline registers capture sums and carry-outs per stage along with the enable signal pipeline to synchronize output validity. The final result and output enable are registered after the last stage to align timing.

This approach ensures clean, synthesizable Verilog without syntax issues by avoiding ambiguous concatenations and using straightforward arithmetic. It also maintains explicit pipeline stages for clarity and timing control.

We implement the pipeline registers in a single always block triggered by clk and async reset. The initial carry-in to stage 0 is zero on every valid input.

Finally, the 65-bit result concatenates the last carry with all stage sums in order from MSB to LSB.
