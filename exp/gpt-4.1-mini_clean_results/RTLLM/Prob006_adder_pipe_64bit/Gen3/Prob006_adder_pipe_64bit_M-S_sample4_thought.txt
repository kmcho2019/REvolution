Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined with 4 stages, each adding 16 bits plus carry-in. Inputs and carry are registered per stage to create pipeline stages, and i_en is pipelined to generate the output enable o_en synchronized with the final sum output. The 65-bit result is assembled from the partial sums and last carry-out. This reduces pipeline stages and code complexity while preserving pipelined addition functionality.
