I will simplify the design by implementing a 64-bit ripple carry adder with a 4-stage pipeline, each stage handling 16 bits. This reduces the number of pipeline registers while still providing pipelining benefits. The inputs and carry signals will be registered per stage. The i_en signal will be pipelined correspondingly to produce the output enable (o_en). The sum pieces from each stage and the final carry will be concatenated to form the 65-bit result. This design simplifies control logic and pipeline management while maintaining functionality.
