Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined with 4 stages, each stage adding 16 bits and the carry-in from the previous stage. Each stage registers its input operands and carry-in, computes the partial sum and carry-out combinationally within the clock cycle, then registers the partial sum and carry-out for the next stage. The carry-out and partial sums are delayed through pipeline registers to maintain synchronization. The input enable signal (i_en) is also pipelined through registers, so that the output enable (o_en) indicates valid output result after 4 clock cycles. The final output result is formed by concatenating the 4 partial sums and the final carry-out, resulting in a 65-bit sum. This design reduces the pipeline depth compared to 8 stages by using wider adders per stage (16 bits each), offering a tradeoff between latency and throughput.
