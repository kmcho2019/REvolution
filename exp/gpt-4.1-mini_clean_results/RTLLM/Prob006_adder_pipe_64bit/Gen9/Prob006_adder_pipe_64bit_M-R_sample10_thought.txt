Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by explicitly separating combinational addition stages from pipeline registers for sums, carries, and enables.

Instead of performing all additions inside a single always block, implement combinational adders that compute sum and carry-out for each 8-bit stage based on input operands and carry-in from the previous stage. Then, register the sums, carries, and enable signals on clock edges.

This decouples combinational logic from sequential logic, improving clarity and possibly synthesis results.

Pipeline registers are updated each clock cycle with async reset to clear registers. The final output result and output enable signals are registered from the last pipeline stage.

The final 65-bit sum concatenates the carry-out from the most significant stage with all 8-bit sums from LSB to MSB.

This approach maintains the eight-stage 8-bit pipeline structure but uses separate combinational adders and sequential registers, making the data flow more explicit and modular.
