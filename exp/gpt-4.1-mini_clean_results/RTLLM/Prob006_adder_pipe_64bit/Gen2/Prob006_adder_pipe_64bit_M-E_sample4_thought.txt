Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined with 8 stages, each adding 8 bits plus a carry-in. Each stage performs an 8-bit addition, and the carry-out is passed as a registered signal to the next stage on the next clock cycle. Inputs and enables are registered into the pipeline stages, ensuring synchronization. The final 65-bit result is assembled from all stage sums and the last carry-out, all synchronized with the output enable signal o_en. This finer granularity (8 bits per stage) increases the pipeline depth, improving throughput at the cost of latency, and the design cleanly separates combinational add and sequential registers for clarity and timing.
