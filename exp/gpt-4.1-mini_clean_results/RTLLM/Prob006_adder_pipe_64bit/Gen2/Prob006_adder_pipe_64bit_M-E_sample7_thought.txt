I will implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with a coarser-grained 8-stage pipeline, where each stage adds 8 bits plus an incoming carry. The module will register inputs and carry signals at each pipeline stage to form a balanced pipeline with 8 clock cycles latency. This reduces the pipeline depth while still providing throughput improvement over a non-pipelined adder. The carry out of each 8-bit adder stage is registered and forwarded to the next stage. The partial sums from each stage are stored in pipeline registers and concatenated at the output stage to form the full 65-bit result. The input enable (i_en) is also pipelined through 8 registers to produce the output enable (o_en), indicating when the result is valid. This design balances pipeline complexity and latency and is easier to verify and synthesize than a full 64-stage pipeline.
