Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder to use generate blocks and explicit wire assignments for the combinational adders, rather than combinational always blocks. 

We will:
- Divide the 64-bit addition into 4 pipeline stages of 16 bits each.
- Use registers to pipeline the operands and carry signals at each stage.
- Perform addition in combinational assigns, producing sum and carry-out.
- Register the results and carry-outs at each clock edge.
- Shift the input enable signal through pipeline registers to generate output enable.
- Assemble the final 65-bit result from the pipeline sum outputs and final carry-out register.

This clean separation of combinational and sequential logic improves clarity and synthesis, avoids large combinational always blocks, and fits well with a pipelined design.

We also keep the module and parameter names simple and consistent for easy integration with external testbenches that might expect `DATA_WIDTH` and `STG_WIDTH`.

Hence, we:
- Rename parameters to `DATA_WIDTH=64` and `STAGE_WIDTH=16`.
- Use generate blocks to create 4 identical pipeline stages.
- Use arrays of registers and wires to hold pipelined operands, sums, carries, and enables.
- Connect carry-outs of one stage as carry-ins of next stage in the pipeline registers.
