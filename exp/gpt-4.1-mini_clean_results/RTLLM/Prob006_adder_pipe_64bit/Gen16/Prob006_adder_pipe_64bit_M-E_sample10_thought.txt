I will implement the 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined into 4 stages, each stage processing 16 bits. Each pipeline stage will have registers for operands, partial sums, and carry-in signals. The carry-out from one stage is registered and passed to the next stage on the next clock cycle, ensuring carry propagation is pipelined. The input enable (i_en) will be registered and shifted through a pipeline to generate the output enable (o_en) signal in sync with the final sum. The 65-bit result will be assembled from the concatenated partial sums of all stages plus the final carry-out. This design breaks the long carry chain into manageable pipeline stages, providing increased throughput with a simple and clean pipeline control.
