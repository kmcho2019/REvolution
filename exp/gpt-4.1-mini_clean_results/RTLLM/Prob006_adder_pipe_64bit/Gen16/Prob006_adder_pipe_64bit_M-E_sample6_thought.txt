Implement a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder with 16 pipeline stages, each stage adding 4 bits and propagating the carry to the next stage. Each pipeline stage stores registered operands segments, carry-in, partial sum, and the enable signal. The carry-out is generated combinationally per stage and registered for the next stage. The pipeline latency is 16 cycles. The output enable (o_en) is generated by shifting the input enable (i_en) through a 16-bit shift register, reflecting the pipeline delay. The final 65-bit result is assembled from all 16 sum segments and the final carry-out once the pipeline completes.
