Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with 4 pipeline stages, each stage handling 16 bits. Each stage has registers to hold the 16-bit slice operands and carry-in. The stage performs 16-bit addition with the input carry and outputs sum and carry-out. The carry-out and valid signals are pipelined through stages, ensuring synchronization. The partial sums are registered per stage and concatenated at the output stage. This reduces pipeline complexity with fewer stages but larger width per stage, balancing latency and clock frequency. The o_en signal is delayed through the pipeline to indicate valid output.
