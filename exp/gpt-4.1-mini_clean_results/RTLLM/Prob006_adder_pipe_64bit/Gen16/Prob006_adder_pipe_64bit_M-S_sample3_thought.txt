To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder, I will still use an 8-stage pipeline with 8 bits per stage. But instead of using arrays of registers and complex loops, I will explicitly declare registers for each pipeline stage's sum and carry-out. The carry-in to the first stage is zero. Each pipeline register stage will store the sum segment and carry for the next stage, all synchronized on clock and reset. The input enable is pipelined along with the data for proper timing of output enable. This explicit approach reduces indexing complexity and is easier to understand, while still providing the required pipelining and correct output timing.
