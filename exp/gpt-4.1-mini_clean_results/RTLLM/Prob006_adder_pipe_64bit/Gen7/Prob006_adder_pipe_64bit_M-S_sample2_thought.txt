Implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with an 8-stage pipeline by slicing inputs into 8-bit segments. Each pipeline stage adds its slice plus the carry-in from the previous stage. Registers pipeline the slices, carry signals, and enable signals. The final 65-bit result and output enable are registered and output after the last pipeline stage. The design uses fixed local parameters for stage width and count, avoiding parameter overrides for simplicity.
