Refactor the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by implementing each pipeline stage as separate always blocks that latch inputs and propagate carry and enable signals to the next stage. Instead of mixing addition and register updates in the same always block, compute the sum outputs combinationally within each stage, and store only pipeline registers for inputs, carry-ins, and enables. Use registers to hold intermediate carry and sum values, and output the final concatenated sum at the last stage. This modularizes each pipeline stage clearly while maintaining a four-stage pipeline with synchronized control signals.
