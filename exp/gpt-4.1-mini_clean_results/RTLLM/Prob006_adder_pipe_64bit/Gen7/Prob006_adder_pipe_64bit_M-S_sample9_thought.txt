To simplify while maintaining functionality, I will implement a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder with 64 pipeline stages, each handling one bit addition. This approach keeps the ripple carry propagation explicit, with one bit addition per clock cycle.

Key points:
- Each pipeline stage registers the carry-in and corresponding bits of adda and addb.
- Each stage computes the sum bit and carry-out for its bit position.
- The carry-out of stage i becomes the carry-in of stage i+1 in the next clock cycle.
- The input enable signal (i_en) is pipelined through 65 stages to produce o_en synchronously.
- The sum bits are stored in registers per stage and shifted down the pipeline, eventually concatenated into the 65-bit result.
- The final carry-out is the MSB of the result.
- This fully serialized 1-bit per cycle pipeline greatly reduces complexity (no slicing, no arrays indexed by stages), easier to verify.
- Latency = 65 cycles.
- Registers on all intermediate signals ensure timing and pipelining.

This simplification ensures clear, modular, and correct pipelined ripple carry addition.
