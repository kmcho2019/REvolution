To simplify the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder while maintaining correct functionality:

- Use 64 pipeline stages, one per bit, to strictly follow the ripple carry behavior, propagating the carry bit by bit per clock cycle.
- Register each bit of inputs and carry at each stage.
- Maintain pipeline registers for `adda`, `addb`, and carry-in per stage.
- Calculate sum bit and carry-out for each stage in the clocked process.
- Register the output sum bits and propagate carry to the next stage in the next cycle.
- Register and propagate the enable signal through a shift register to generate `o_en`.
- After 64 cycles from input enable, the full 65-bit sum and `o_en` will be valid.
- This design trades latency (64 cycles) for simple and correct ripple carry pipelining with minimal logic per stage.
- Use a single always block synchronous to `clk` and `rst_n` to update pipeline registers.
- The final result is concatenated from sum bits and the last carry.
- Reset all pipeline registers on reset.

This approach eliminates slice-based vector registers and array-of-registers complexities. It uses straightforward per-bit pipelining with clear carry propagation matching ripple carry addition fundamentals.
