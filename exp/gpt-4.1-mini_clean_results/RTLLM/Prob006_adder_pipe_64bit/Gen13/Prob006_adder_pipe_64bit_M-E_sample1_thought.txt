Implement a 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder by dividing the addition into 16 stages, each adding 4 bits. This fine granularity enables balanced pipelining and potentially better clock frequency.

Key design points:
1. Each pipeline stage holds registered inputs (4-bit slices of operands), the carry-in from previous stage, and computes sum and carry-out.
2. The carry signal propagates sequentially through the pipeline stages, registered at every clock cycle.
3. The input enable (i_en) is also pipelined to generate the output enable (o_en) synchronized with the result.
4. All operations are done in sequential always block triggered on clk and async reset (rst_n).
5. At the output stage, the final 65-bit sum is assembled by concatenating the 16 sum parts plus the final carry.
6. The module avoids combinational loops and keeps clean pipelining structure with registered carry and enable signals.
