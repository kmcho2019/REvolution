To refactor the pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder while keeping the same core functionality:

1. Use pipeline registers to store slices of operands and carry signals.

2. Instead of computing all sums combinationally with wires and capturing sums in a single always block, do each pipeline stage addition in a separate always block triggered by the clock, registering both sum and carry at each stage explicitly.

3. Synchronize enable signals through pipeline registers to track valid data.

4. The output stage assembles the full 65-bit result from the registered sums and the final carry.

5. This structure removes the use of arrays of wires for sums and uses explicit per-stage registers, making the data flow clearer and easier to verify.

6. The reset condition clears all pipeline registers.

This approach creates a more modular pipeline structure and separates the computation and registration stages clearly.
