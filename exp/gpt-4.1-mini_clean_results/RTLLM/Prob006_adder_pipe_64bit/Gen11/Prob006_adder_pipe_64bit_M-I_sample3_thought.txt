The original design correctly pipelines a 64-bit ripple carry adder over 65 clock cycles, one bit addition per pipeline stage, producing one valid result per cycle after an initial latency of 65 cycles. 

Improvements for clarity, synthesis friendliness, and minor optimizations:
1. Use one-dimensional packed registers (vectors) instead of arrays of single-bit regs to simplify synthesis and improve readability.
2. Split pipeline registers into shift registers for bits of operands and carries.
3. Maintain a carry register per stage but implement all as vectors shifted per clock cycle.
4. Pipeline the enable signal using a shift register.
5. Assemble the sum after the final stage from the sum bits stored in a vector.
6. Use non-blocking assignments consistently.
7. Reset registers asynchronously on active-low reset.
8. This approach reduces complexity, improves synthesis friendliness, and maintains the same functionality and performance.

Note: The overall latency remains 65 cycles, throughput one per cycle after latency.

This refined design should be synthesizable and easier to maintain or modify.
