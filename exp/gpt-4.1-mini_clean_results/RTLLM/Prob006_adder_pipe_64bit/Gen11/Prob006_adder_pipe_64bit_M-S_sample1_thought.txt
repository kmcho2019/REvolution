Simplify the design by using 4 pipeline stages with 16 bits each for manageable complexity and reasonable latency. Each stage registers 16-bit operand slices, carry-in, and the enable signal. The sum and carry-out for each stage are calculated combinationally and then registered for the next stage.

At each clock, pipeline registers load the operands slices and carry, and propagate the enable. The output result is formed by concatenating all pipeline stage sums plus the final carry-out.

This reduces the number of pipeline registers, simplifies the indexing, and keeps the pipeline approach for throughput and synchronization.
