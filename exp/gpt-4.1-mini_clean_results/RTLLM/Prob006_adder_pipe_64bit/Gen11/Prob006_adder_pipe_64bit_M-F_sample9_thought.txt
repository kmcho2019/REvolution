To fix the pipeline 64-bit ripple carry adder, we must carefully manage carry propagation and pipeline synchronization:

1. **Carry Propagation and Widths:**  
   - Each pipeline stage adds its slice of operands plus the carry-in from the previous stage.
   - The addition must be done with a wider intermediate sum (STG_WIDTH+1 bits) to capture the carry-out correctly.
   - Carry-out (the MSB of the intermediate sum) is registered and passed to the next stage.
   
2. **Pipeline Registers:**  
   - All pipeline stages have registers for sum outputs and carry signals that capture the result synchronously on `clk`.
   - The carry-in for the next stage is the registered carry-out from the previous stage.
   - Input operands are combinational inputs; carry-in and sums are registered.

3. **Enable Signal (o_en) Alignment:**  
   - The input enable signal `i_en` is registered and shifted through pipeline enable registers matching pipeline depth.
   - `o_en` asserts only when the output result corresponds to a valid addition.

4. **Result Assembly:**  
   - The final output result is assembled from all sum pipeline registers plus the last carry-out, concatenated in correct order.
   - This assembly occurs synchronously and uses non-blocking assignments.

5. **Arrays & Tool Compatibility:**  
   - Use `reg` arrays for pipeline registers.
   - Use a generate block for combinational adders and explicit loops for pipeline registers.
   - Use non-blocking assignments inside always blocks.

This design ensures a fully pipelined ripple carry adder with correct carry propagation and synchronized enable and output signals.

We will parameterize DATA_WIDTH=64, STG_WIDTH=8, and STAGES=8 for flexibility and clear logic structure.
