This preserves temporal ordering and ensures the carry propagates correctly through the pipeline.

Key improvements:
- Load the input operands only into stage 0 when `i_en=1`, otherwise shift operands from previous stage downstream.
- Carry-in at stage 0 is reset to zero only on new input load; otherwise, it shifts forward like all other pipeline signals.
- Compute sum and carry for each stage in a single clocked process with nonblocking assignments to avoid combinational loops.
- Pipeline enable signals propagate with the data enabling precise output valid indication.
- Assemble the final 65-bit result by concatenating the sums in correct LSB to MSB order with the final carry as the MSB (no shifting or filling zeros).
- Keep the combinational block simple and deterministic without ambiguous shifting or masking.
- Ensure the entire pipeline updates every clock cycle to maintain timing and throughput.
