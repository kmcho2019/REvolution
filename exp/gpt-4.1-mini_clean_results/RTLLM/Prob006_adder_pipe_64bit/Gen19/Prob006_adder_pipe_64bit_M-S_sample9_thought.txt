To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder, I will implement an 8-stage pipeline, each stage adding 8 bits with a carry input. Each pipeline stage will register its inputs, carry-in, sum, and carry-out. The carry is passed between stages through pipeline registers. The output enable signal and result are registered at the final stage to indicate when the output is valid. This approach reduces the number of pipeline registers and complexity compared to a 4-stage 16-bit block adder, while maintaining functionality and clarity.
