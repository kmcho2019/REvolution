To simplify the 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder, I will implement it using 4 pipeline stages, each handling 16 bits of the addition. Each stage registers its input operands and carry-in, then computes the sum and carry-out. The carry-out propagates as carry-in to the next stage in the following clock cycle. 

I will use simple registers for each pipeline stage's inputs, sums, and carries, and a shift register to track the input enable signal through the pipeline stages, generating the output enable signal after the pipeline latency.

The final 65-bit sum is formed by concatenating the four 16-bit sums and the last carry-out. The design is synchronous, resettable, and avoids complex parameterization to keep it straightforward.
