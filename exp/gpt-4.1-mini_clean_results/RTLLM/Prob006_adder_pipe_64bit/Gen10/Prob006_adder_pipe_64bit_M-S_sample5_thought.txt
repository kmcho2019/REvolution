Implement a straightforward 64-bit ripple carry adder with 8 pipeline stages (8 bits per stage). Each pipeline stage registers slices of the operands and the carry-in, computes the sum and carry-out combinationally, and passes them to the next stage.

Pipeline registers hold operand slices, carry, and enable signals to synchronize the pipeline.

The final result is assembled by concatenating the sums from all stages and the last carry-out. The output enable `o_en` signals when the result is valid.

This approach simplifies the pipeline by computing each stage's addition combinationally from registered inputs and registers only necessary signals per stage, ensuring clean, manageable, and efficient pipeline behavior.
