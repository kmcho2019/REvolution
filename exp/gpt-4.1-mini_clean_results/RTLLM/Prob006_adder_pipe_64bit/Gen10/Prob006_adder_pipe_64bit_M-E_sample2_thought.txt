Implement a fully pipelined 64-bit ripple carry adder by dividing the 64-bit addition into 8 pipeline stages of 8 bits each. Each pipeline stage registers its input operands and incoming carry, performs an 8-bit addition with carry-in to produce an 8-bit sum and carry-out, and passes the carry-out to the next stage in the next clock cycle. The pipeline registers include operand slices, carry, and enable signals to track validity through stages. 

At each clock cycle, a new addition can be started when i_en is asserted. The final result, a 65-bit sum, is built by concatenating the registered sums from each pipeline stage in order and appending the last carry-out bit at the highest bit. The output enable (o_en) indicates when the final result is valid and synchronized to the clock.

This design uses arrays of pipeline registers for operands, sum outputs, carry signals, and enable flags, updated synchronously on the clock edge and asynchronously reset by rst_n. All addition is done inside the pipeline stages sequentially, simplifying timing and improving throughput as results emerge after fixed latency.

This approach emphasizes modular pipelined addition with explicit carry propagation, clean stage separation, and clear output synchronization without complex combinational loops.
