Implement a 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder with eight pipeline stages, each handling 8 bits. Use registers to pipeline both the sum and carry signals along with the enable signal. In each clock cycle, propagate partial sums and carry to the next stage.

This approach balances simplicity and pipelining: each stage adds its 8-bit slice plus carry-in, stores the partial sum and carry-out in registers, and moves them forward. The final output concatenates all sums and the last carry-out. The output enable follows the pipeline delay by registering the input enable signal through the pipeline stages.

Use a single always block for the pipeline registers with synchronous reset. This design avoids combinational arrays and simplifies the structure while meeting the requirements.
