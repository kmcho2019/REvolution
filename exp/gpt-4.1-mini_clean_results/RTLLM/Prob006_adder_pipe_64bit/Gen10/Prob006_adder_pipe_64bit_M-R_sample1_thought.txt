Refactor the 64-bit ripple carry adder pipeline to use a more straightforward approach that avoids multidimensional register arrays indexed by stage and bit. Instead, each pipeline stage corresponds to one bit addition with registered operands and carry signals, forming a shift-register-like pipeline.

Key points in the refactor:
- Use simple single-bit registers for each pipeline stage's operands, carry-in, sum, and carry-out.
- The pipeline depth is 65 stages: 64 for bits, 1 for the final carry out.
- The input operands (adda and addb) are loaded into shift registers that shift out one bit per clock cycle.
- Carry bits propagate along a dedicated carry pipeline.
- A shift register tracks input enable propagation through pipeline stages, producing output enable when the result is valid.
- At the final stage, concatenate the sum bits and carry out to form the 65-bit result.
- This approach models the ripple carry adder pipeline explicitly as shift registers of bits, improving readability, ease of synthesis, and aligns closely with the ripple carry addition concept.
- Avoid complex indexing like adda_reg[stage][stage] by representing each pipeline stage's inputs as single bits shifted through registers.

This design ensures:
- Single clock cycle per bit addition stage.
- Proper synchronization and enable control.
- Latency of 65 cycles from input enable to valid output.
