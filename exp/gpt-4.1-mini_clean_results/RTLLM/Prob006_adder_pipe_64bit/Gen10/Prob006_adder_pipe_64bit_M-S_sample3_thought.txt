Implement a straightforward 64-bit pipelined ripple carry adder divided into 8 stages of 8 bits each. Each stage computes the sum and carry-out from the registered carry-in of the previous stage and registers its sum output. The carry signals and enable signal propagate through pipeline registers.

On each clock edge, if enabled and not in reset, update the pipeline registers for sums, carry, and enable signals. The result is formed by concatenating the final carry-out with all 8-bit sums. This simple approach uses only necessary pipeline registers without separate combinational wire arrays, reducing complexity while maintaining pipeline functionality.
