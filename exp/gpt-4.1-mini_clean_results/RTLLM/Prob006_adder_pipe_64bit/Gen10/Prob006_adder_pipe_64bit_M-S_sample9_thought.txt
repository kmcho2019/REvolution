Simplify the 64-bit ripple carry adder pipelined into four 16-bit stages. Each stage adds its 16-bit slice of the inputs along with the carry-in from the previous stage. Pipeline registers hold intermediate sums, carries, and enable signals to synchronize data flow. The carry ripples through the pipeline stages sequentially, and the output enable (o_en) is asserted when the final pipeline stage has valid data.

This simpler design uses straightforward registers for inputs, sums, carry, and enable signals, with no generate blocks or arrays, making it easier to read and verify while still providing pipelining and synchronization.
