Fuse the shift-register approach from Example 1 with the bit-select approach from Example 2 for an optimized parallel-to-serial converter. Use a 4-bit data register to hold the current parallel data, but avoid shifting it each cycle to reduce toggling and improve power efficiency. Instead, maintain a 2-bit counter that tracks the output bit index from MSB (3) down to LSB (0). Output the bit indexed by the counter, with valid_out asserted only when the counter is zero (indicating the first output bit of a new data word). Load new parallel data into the register when the counter wraps around from 3 to 0. This design minimizes shifting and switching, simplifies the critical path to a mux controlled by the counter, and cleanly generates the valid_out signal. Additionally, use synchronous reset active low to initialize registers properly.
