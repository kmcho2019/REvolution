Use a 4-bit shift register to hold the data and a 2-bit counter to track the output bit position. On reset, clear all registers. On each clock cycle, output the MSB of the shift register. When the counter reaches 3 (last bit), load new parallel data into the register, reset the counter to 0, and set valid_out to 1 to indicate the new valid data output. For other cycles, shift the register left by one bit, increment the counter, and clear valid_out. This straightforward approach reduces complexity by avoiding extra logic and maintains the required functionality.
