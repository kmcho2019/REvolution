Refactor the design to use an internal register to store the parallel input `d` on the cycle when the counter reaches 3, indicating a new load. Use a simple 2-bit counter that increments each cycle, wrapping every 4 cycles. The serial output `dout` is assigned combinationally from the registered data's bit indexed by the counter (from MSB to LSB). The valid_out is asserted when the data is freshly loaded (counter is 0 after load). This separates the input data loading (sequential) from the bit selection (combinational), providing stable output bits and reduced glitching.
