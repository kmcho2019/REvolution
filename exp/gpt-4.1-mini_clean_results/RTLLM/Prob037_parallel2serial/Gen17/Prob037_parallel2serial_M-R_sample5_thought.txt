Refactor the module by registering the input data on a cycle when the counter is at its maximum, then use a shift register to serially output bits from MSB to LSB over four cycles. The valid_out signal will be asserted only on the cycle the new parallel data is loaded (when the counter resets to zero). This approach uses a shift register and a counter to serialize the data, reducing combinational complexity on dout and improving timing by breaking it into registered stages.
