Simplify the design by using a straightforward shift register approach without rotation. Load the 4-bit input data into a shift register when the counter is zero (starting a new serialization). Output the MSB of the shift register each cycle, shift left by one bit after output. The valid_out is asserted only on the first cycle after loading new data (i.e., when counter is zero). Counter counts from 0 to 3 to keep track of bit positions. This method avoids rotation and complex operations, reducing logic complexity while maintaining the required functionality.
