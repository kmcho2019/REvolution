Use a 4-bit shift register loaded with the parallel data when the counter reaches 3, outputting its MSB each cycle as dout. A 2-bit counter tracks the bit position from 0 to 3. valid_out is asserted only when new data is loaded (cnt == 3), indicating the start of a new serial sequence. On other cycles, the register shifts left by one bit filling LSB with zero (since all bits are already output), and the counter increments. This avoids rotate logic, reduces switching since shifting with zero fill prevents re-circulating bits, and simplifies the design while preserving all functionality.
