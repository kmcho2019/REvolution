Eliminate the counter register by using a 2-bit counter implemented as a reg that increments every clock cycle and resets with the asynchronous reset. valid_out can be asserted when cnt is 0, indicating the start of the serialization of a new 4-bit input. The serial output dout is directly assigned as d[3 - cnt] to output bits from MSB to LSB in sequence. This removes the need for additional logic to reset or control valid_out and keeps the design clean and minimal.
