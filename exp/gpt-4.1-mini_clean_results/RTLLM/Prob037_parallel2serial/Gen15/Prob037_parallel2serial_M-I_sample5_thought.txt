To optimize power and timing, avoid shifting the entire 4-bit data register every cycle. Instead, hold the loaded parallel data intact and use a 2-bit counter as a bit pointer to index the current bit to output (from MSB to LSB). This reduces switching activity since data bits remain stable, and only the counter changes. The valid_out is asserted when the MSB bit is output (i.e., counter == 0), matching the requirement that valid_out = 1 when the MSB is output. On cnt==3 (last bit), reload the data and reset counter to 0 for the next frame. This approach improves timing by reducing data path logic and reduces power due to minimized toggling on the data register.
