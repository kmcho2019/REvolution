Refactor the design to avoid shifting the data register every cycle by loading the input data into a register once per 4-bit sequence, and then use a counter to select which bit to output directly through a combinational mux. This reduces switching activity and potentially improves timing by preventing constant data movement. The valid_out signal is asserted as a one-cycle pulse when new data is loaded. The counter increments every clock, and the serial output is driven by the bit indexed by (3 - cnt) from the data register to output MSB first. On reset, all registers are cleared.
