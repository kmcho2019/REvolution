Instead of shifting with a zero fill (which loses original bits and causes extra toggling), perform a left rotate on the data register to maintain the bit pattern, minimizing switching and preserving data integrity. Output the MSB of data each cycle. Assert valid_out only on the first bit (when counter is zero) to indicate the start of serial data. On the last bit (cnt==3), load new parallel data from input d, reset counter, and assert valid_out. On other cycles, rotate the data left by one bit, increment the counter, and deassert valid_out. This rotation reduces switching activity compared to shifting with zeros, preserves data, avoids complex multiplexers, and cleanly generates valid_out, achieving a good balance of performance, power, and area.
