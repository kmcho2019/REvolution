Use an internal 4-bit shift register to hold the parallel input data loaded at the cycle when the 4-bit sequence is completed (i.e., cnt == 3). On this load cycle, valid_out is asserted high to indicate new valid serial data is available. After loading, the shift register shifts left by one bit each clock cycle, outputting its MSB as dout. The counter increments from 0 to 3 to track the 4 output bits. This method ensures stable data output, isolates input changes during shifting, and cleanly separates load and shift phases within the same state machine.
