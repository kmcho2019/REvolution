To optimize timing and power, avoid rotating the entire 4-bit data register every cycle. Instead, load the input data 'd' once into a register and use a 2-bit counter as a bit pointer to select which bit to output, indexing from MSB to LSB. This eliminates shifting data every cycle, reducing toggling and improving timing by shortening the data path. The valid_out signal is asserted only when cnt is zero (start of a new 4-bit data output sequence), matching the requirement. Reset is active low and initializes the counter and data register to zero.
