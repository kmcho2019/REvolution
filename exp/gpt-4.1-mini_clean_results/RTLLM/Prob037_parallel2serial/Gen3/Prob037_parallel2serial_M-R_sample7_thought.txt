Refactor the design by implementing a simple FSM with two states: LOAD and SHIFT. In the LOAD state, the 4-bit parallel input is loaded into a shift register and valid_out is asserted high for one cycle as the MSB is output. Then transition to the SHIFT state, where the shift register shifts left by one bit each clock cycle, outputting the MSB sequentially and valid_out is low during these cycles. After shifting out all bits, transition back to LOAD to accept new data.

This approach explicitly separates data loading from shifting, avoiding a counter-based bit selection and arithmetic indexing. The shift register shifts bits out directly, eliminating the need for bit indexing logic. Valid_out is high only for the cycle when new data is loaded and the first bit (MSB) is output. This method improves clarity and closely follows the original shifting behavior described, while using a simple FSM for control.
