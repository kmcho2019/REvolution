- Load new data into `data` when `cnt == 3` (end of previous 4 bits transmission), output MSB on that cycle, valid_out=1.

- Increment `cnt` every clock cycle when not in reset.
- The initial cycle after reset starts with `cnt=3` so that on first clock cycle data is loaded and outputted.
