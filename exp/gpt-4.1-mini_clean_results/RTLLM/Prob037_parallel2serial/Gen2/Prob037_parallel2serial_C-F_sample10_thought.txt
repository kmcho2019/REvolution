Fuse the strengths of both examples by using a 4-bit shift register to hold the data and a 2-bit counter to track the bits output. On every clock cycle, output the MSB of the shift register. When cnt == 3 (indicating the last bit output), load the new parallel data into the shift register, reset cnt to 0, and set valid_out to 1. Otherwise, shift the data register left by one bit, increment cnt, and set valid_out to 0. This approach outputs bits MSB-first without a complex combinational mux and avoids unnecessary combinational indexing logic. It also reduces switching activity compared to circular shifting since only the shift register shifts, and the valid_out signal pulses only once per 4 cycles. The design balances low power, low area, and good timing by limiting multiplexing and simplifying the output path.
