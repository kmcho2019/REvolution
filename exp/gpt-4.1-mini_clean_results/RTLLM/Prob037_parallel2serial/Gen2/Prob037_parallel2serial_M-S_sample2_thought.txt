Use a 2-bit counter `cnt` to track which bit of the 4-bit input is being output serially, starting from MSB (bit 3) down to LSB (bit 0). On every clock cycle, output the bit `d[3 - cnt]` as `dout`. When `cnt` reaches 3 (last bit), assert `valid_out` to 1 for that cycle and then wrap the counter to 0 for the next data word. This avoids using a shift register, simplifies logic, and meets the requirements.
