Use a 2-bit counter (cnt) from 0 to 3 to track which bit of the 4-bit data is output serially from MSB to LSB. On reset, clear data and counter. At cnt=3, load new parallel data, reset cnt to 0, assert valid_out=1, and output the MSB of the new data. Otherwise, shift data left by 1 bit each cycle, outputting the new MSB, increment cnt, and deassert valid_out. This approach matches the problem requirements directly and is simpler by using shifting and a counter.
