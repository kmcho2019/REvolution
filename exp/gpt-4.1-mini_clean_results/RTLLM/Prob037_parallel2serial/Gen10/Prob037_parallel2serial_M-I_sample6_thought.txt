The original design already meets functional requirements and shows reasonable PPA metrics. To further improve:

1. Power: Minimize switching in the shift register by rotating bits instead of shifting zeros, so each output bit appears sequentially without introducing unnecessary toggling of LSB bits (which are zero in the original shift-in approach). This reduces transitions on data bits.

2. Performance: Keep the logic minimal by maintaining the simple 2-bit counter and a single 4-bit register. No added pipeline stages are needed as the critical path is short.

3. Area: No extra registers or complex logic are introduced; only a minor change from shift-left-with-zero to rotate-left behavior.

Implementation details:

- On load (cnt == 3), load the new parallel data.
- On other cycles, rotate data left by 1 bit, so bits circulate and the MSB always drives dout.
- The valid_out is asserted only when new data is loaded (cnt == 3).
- Counter counts from 0 to 3 as before.

This approach reduces toggling in the data register, as the LSB is never forced to zero and all bits are preserved and circulated, potentially reducing power. The output bit stream remains from MSB to LSB in order.
