Refactor the design by replacing the shift register with a fixed data register that directly stores the 4-bit parallel input, and use a 2-bit counter as an index to select the output bit from MSB to LSB via combinational logic. This approach avoids shifting registers, reduces toggling, and may improve timing by removing the shift logic. The valid_out signal is asserted only when the counter is zero (first bit output), indicating a new 4-bit chunk is being serialized. The counter increments every clock cycle and resets after reaching 3, controlling the bit index. On reset, clear registers and outputs. This simplifies data handling and cleanly separates data storage and bit selection, improving area and power efficiency.
