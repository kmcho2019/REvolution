To optimize for better performance, power, and area, avoid shifting the data register every clock cycle. Instead, load the 4-bit parallel data into a register once every 4 cycles and use a 2-bit counter as an index to select which bit to output, from MSB to LSB. This approach reduces switching activity and logic for shifting. The valid_out signal is asserted only for one cycle when new data is loaded (i.e., when counter is zero). This reduces toggling and clearly indicates valid data availability. Reset initializes the counter and data register, ensuring proper startup state.
