Use a 4-bit shift register that is loaded with the parallel input when a 2-bit counter reaches zero, indicating the start of a new serialization cycle. At the first cycle after loading, set valid_out=1 and output the MSB of the shift register. On subsequent cycles (cnt=1 to 3), right-shift the register by one bit so that the next most significant bit moves to the MSB position for output. valid_out is held high for all four cycles of serialization. The counter increments every clock cycle, wrapping every 4 cycles. This approach keeps valid_out high during the entire serial output period and uses right-shift so that the MSB is always the current output bit. On reset, initialize counter and shift register to zero.
