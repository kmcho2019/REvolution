Use a 4-bit data register to store the parallel input and a 2-bit counter to index which bit to output next. Instead of shifting the data register, keep it constant for the entire 4-cycle serialization, and output the bit selected by the counter starting from MSB (index 3) down to LSB (index 0). On reset, clear all registers. On the last serialized bit (counter=3), load new parallel data and set valid_out to 1 for that cycle to indicate availability of new data on dout. For other cycles, valid_out is 0. This approach eliminates shifting logic, reducing switching activity and timing complexity, and uses simple combinational bit selection based on the counter to output the serial bit.
