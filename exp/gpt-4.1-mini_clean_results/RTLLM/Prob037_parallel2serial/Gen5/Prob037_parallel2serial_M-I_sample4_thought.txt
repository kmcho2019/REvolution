To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, we eliminate the shift register and instead keep the parallel input data constant during the 4-cycle serialization interval. A 2-bit counter tracks which bit to output, and the serial output dout is directly assigned from the bit indexed by the counter, starting from MSB (bit 3) down to LSB (bit 0). This avoids shifting data every cycle, reducing switching activity and combinational logic complexity.

valid_out is asserted only for one cycle when the new parallel data is loaded (i.e., when counter is 0), indicating the availability of the first valid bit output. The counter increments each cycle, wrapping every 4 cycles.

This approach reduces register toggling, simplifies logic by removing shift operations, and uses direct bit indexing which can improve timing and lower power and area.
