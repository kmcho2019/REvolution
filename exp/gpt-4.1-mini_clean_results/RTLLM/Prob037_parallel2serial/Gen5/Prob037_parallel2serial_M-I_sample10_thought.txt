To optimize the existing design for better performance, power, and area, replace the shift register with a fixed data register and use a 2-bit counter as an index to select the output bit directly. This removes the need for shifting logic, reducing switching activity and critical path delay. The valid_out signal will be asserted only on the cycle when a new 4-bit parallel input is loaded and its MSB is output. Output bits are sent MSB to LSB by indexing the fixed data register with the counter value. This design minimizes toggle rate, simplifies combinational logic, and reduces area by eliminating shift hardware.
