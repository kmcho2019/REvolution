Implement an internal 4-bit shift register that loads the parallel input `d` only when the counter reaches 3 (indicating the completion of a serialization cycle). The counter counts from 0 to 3, tracking which bit is being output. On each clock cycle, output the MSB of the shift register as `dout`. Then shift the register left by one bit, inserting zero at the LSB, to prepare for the next output bit. The `valid_out` signal goes high only when the data is freshly loaded (i.e., cnt == 3). This method serializes the data from MSB to LSB with a controlled shift operation, separating input loading and output shifting and minimizing direct dependency on `d` during serialization cycles.
