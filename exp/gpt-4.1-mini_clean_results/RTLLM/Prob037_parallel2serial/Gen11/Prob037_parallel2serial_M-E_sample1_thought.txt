Implement the design using a shift register that loads the 4-bit input 'd' when the counter reaches 3 (end of a serialization cycle). At that moment, valid_out is asserted to indicate the output bit is the MSB of new data. The shift register holds the data and shifts left each cycle, bringing the next bit to the MSB position. The serial output is always the MSB of the shift register. The counter counts from 0 to 3, advancing the serialization progress. valid_out is asserted only on the cycle new data is loaded and output starts.
