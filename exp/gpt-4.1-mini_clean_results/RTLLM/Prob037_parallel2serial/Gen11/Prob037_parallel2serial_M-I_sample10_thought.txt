To improve the design for better power efficiency while maintaining performance and area, introduce a load signal that updates and stores the parallel input data 'd' into an internal register only when starting a new serialization cycle (i.e., when cnt is zero). Then, use this registered data for output selection during serialization. This approach reduces dependency on input 'd' stability and avoids reading 'd' every cycle. Also, add a counting enable signal that is active only during serialization, allowing the counter to stop when no serialization is ongoing, further saving power. The valid_out signal is asserted only at the first cycle of new data output. This optimization reduces switching activity on the counter and output logic, which helps lower dynamic power with minimal area overhead.
