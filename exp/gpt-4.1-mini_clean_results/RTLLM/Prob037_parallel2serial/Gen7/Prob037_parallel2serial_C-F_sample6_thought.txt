Combine the clarity and low switching activity from Example 1's linear left shift (shifting in zeros) with the structured control from Example 2, while adding a slight optimization: instead of circular shifting or shifting in zeros every cycle, keep the 4-bit parallel data loaded at the start of each 4-cycle frame and use a 2-bit pointer (counter) to select the MSB bit to output serially. This avoids shifting the data every clock cycle, minimizing toggling on the data register. The valid_out is asserted only when new parallel data is loaded (once every 4 cycles). The serial output is assigned combinationally from the data register indexed by the bit position decreasing from MSB (3) to LSB (0). This approach combines low switching, simpler data register management, precise timing, and minimal control logic.
