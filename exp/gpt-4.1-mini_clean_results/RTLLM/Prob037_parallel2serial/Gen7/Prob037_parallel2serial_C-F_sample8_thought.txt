To combine the strengths of both examples, I will avoid the power-hungry shift register by keeping the 4-bit input data loaded in a register without shifting it. Instead, I'll use a 2-bit counter to index the bit to output (from MSB to LSB) directly, eliminating bit shifting and reducing switching activity. This will also reduce area by removing the need for a shifting data register and improve timing by having a constant register and a small combinational mux selecting the output bit. The valid_out signal will be asserted as a single-cycle pulse when the parallel input data is loaded (i.e., when cnt is zero), indicating that the first valid serial bit is output. The data register is loaded every 4 cycles (when cnt wraps from 3 to 0). This fusion merges the power and area efficiency of indexing (Example 1 feedback) with the clear and simple sequential control logic of Example 2.
