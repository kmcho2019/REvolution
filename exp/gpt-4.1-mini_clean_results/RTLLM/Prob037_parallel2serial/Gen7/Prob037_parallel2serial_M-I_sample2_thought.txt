To optimize the design for better power and timing, reduce unnecessary toggling in the shift register by avoiding shifting in zeros every cycle. Instead, load the parallel data only once at cnt=3, then simply shift through bits by indexing rather than shifting register contents. This eliminates the shifting operation's toggling.
