Refactor the parallel-to-serial converter by implementing a simple finite state machine (FSM) with two states: LOAD and SHIFT. In LOAD state, the module loads the 4-bit parallel input into a shift register and outputs the MSB with valid_out asserted. In SHIFT state, the module shifts the register left every clock cycle, outputs the next bit, and deasserts valid_out. A 2-bit counter tracks the bit position (0 to 3). When the last bit is output (counter == 3), transition back to LOAD state to fetch new input data. This clear separation of loading and shifting logic improves readability and explicit control flow. Additionally, use continuous assignments to drive dout from the shift register's MSB and valid_out from the state, while registers update only the shift register and counter synchronously.
