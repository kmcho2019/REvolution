To optimize the design for better performance, power, and area, I will avoid shifting the data register on every clock cycle. Instead, the input parallel data will be loaded into a register when the counter is zero, and the output bit will be selected by indexing into the register using the counter (from MSB to LSB). This eliminates continuous shifting and reduces switching activity, lowering dynamic power consumption and improving timing by removing shift logic from the critical path. The valid_out signal will be asserted only for one clock cycle when new data is loaded, as a single-cycle pulse indicating the availability of valid serial data. This approach reduces toggling on valid_out and simplifies logic. Overall, the design will use a 2-bit counter to select bits, a data register to hold the input parallel data, and combinational logic to output the selected bit, achieving the required functionality with less area and power.
