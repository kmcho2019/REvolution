Implement the parallel-to-serial converter using a shift counter and a stored parallel data register without physically shifting the data. On reset or after sending all 4 bits, load the 4-bit input data into a register. A 2-bit counter (0 to 3) indexes the bits to output, starting from MSB (bit 3) down to LSB (bit 0). On each clock cycle, dout outputs the bit at position (3 - cnt), and valid_out is asserted only when cnt is zero, indicating the first (MSB) bit is valid. The counter increments every clock cycle and rolls over after the last bit, triggering a reload on the next cycle. This approach avoids shifting the data, reducing switching activity and potentially improving power and timing.
