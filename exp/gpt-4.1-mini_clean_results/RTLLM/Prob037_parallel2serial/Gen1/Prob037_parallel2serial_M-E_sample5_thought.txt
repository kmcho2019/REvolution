Use a simple 2-bit counter to track the current output bit index (0 to 3). On reset, initialize counter to 0. At each clock cycle, output the bit of input `d` indexed by (3 - counter) to get MSB first, then next bits down to LSB. The `valid_out` signal is asserted only when the counter is zero, indicating the MSB bit is being output. After outputting the 4th bit (counter=3), wrap the counter back to 0 for the next input word. This approach directly indexes bits of `d` without shifting registers, simplifying logic and ensuring proper valid timing.
