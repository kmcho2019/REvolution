Use a simple 2-bit counter to index the bits of input `d` from MSB to LSB and output them serially on `dout`. Assert `valid_out` only when the MSB bit is being output (i.e., when the counter is zero). On reset, clear the counter and valid_out. No need to store `d` internally or perform shifts; directly use `d[3 - cnt]`. This removes extra registers and logic, making the design straightforward and minimal while meeting all requirements.
