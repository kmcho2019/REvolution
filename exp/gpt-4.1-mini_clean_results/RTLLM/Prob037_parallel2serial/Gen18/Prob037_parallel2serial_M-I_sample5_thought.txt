Improve timing and power by registering the input data 'd' at the start of each serialization (when cnt resets to 0) to decouple combinational input-to-output paths. Use a 2-bit counter and an internal 4-bit shift register. On cnt==3, load new data and set valid_out=1; on other cycles, shift left and increment cnt, valid_out=0. This registers 'd' only once per 4-cycle serialization, reducing glitches and switching on dout, improving input setup time and output stability. This approach also aligns closely with the original problem statement of using an internal shift register.
