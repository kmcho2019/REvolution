Refactor the design to separate data loading and shifting into two distinct processes. Use a 2-bit counter as a bit index pointer instead of rotating the data register. The data register is loaded only when all bits have been serialized (i.e., when counter is 0). On each clock, output the bit from data indexed by cnt (MSB first: cnt=0 outputs data[3], cnt=1 data[2], etc.). valid_out is asserted only when cnt==0 indicating the first bit of new data is output. This approach avoids rotating the data register, reducing switching activity and making the bit selection explicit, improving clarity and potentially timing.
