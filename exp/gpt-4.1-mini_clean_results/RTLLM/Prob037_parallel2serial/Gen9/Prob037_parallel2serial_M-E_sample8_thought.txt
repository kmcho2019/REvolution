Instead of shifting the data in the shift register every cycle, use a 2-bit counter as an index to select the bit to output directly from the loaded parallel input. On reset, clear the counter. At the end of each 4-bit serialization (when the counter reaches 3), load the parallel input into a data register and reset the counter. For each clock cycle, output the bit at position (3 - cnt) from the data register, starting with the MSB (bit 3). The valid_out signal is asserted high only when the counter is zero (indicating the first bit of a new data load), and low otherwise. This approach avoids shifting registers and reduces switching activity, since data is stored once and bits are accessed by indexing, possibly improving power efficiency and timing.
