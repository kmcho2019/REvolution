Use a 4-bit shift register to hold the parallel input data. On reset, clear the counter and data. When the counter is 3, load new parallel data, output the MSB, set valid_out high, and reset the counter. For other counts, shift the data left by 1 bit (cycling the MSB to LSB), output the MSB, increment the counter, and set valid_out low. This produces serial output from MSB to LSB over 4 clock cycles.
