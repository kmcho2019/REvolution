Implement a 4-bit shift register to hold the parallel data and a 2-bit counter to track the bit position (0 to 3). On reset (active low), clear the counter and data. When the counter reaches 3, load the new parallel input into the data register, set valid_out to 1, and reset the counter. Otherwise, shift data to the left by one bit (MSB to LSB rotation), increment the counter, and set valid_out to 0. The output dout is the MSB of the current data register.
