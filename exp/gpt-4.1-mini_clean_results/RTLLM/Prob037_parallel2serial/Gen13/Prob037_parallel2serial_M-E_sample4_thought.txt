Implement the parallel-to-serial converter using a down counter from 3 to 0 to index the bit to output directly from the input parallel data without a shift register. The output dout is assigned by selecting the bit of d at position 'cnt' (starting from MSB at 3 down to LSB at 0). When cnt reaches 0 (last bit output), valid_out is set high to indicate that the most significant bit (when cnt=3) has just been output and new data will be loaded next cycle. The counter resets back to 3 at this time to start a new serial output sequence. This avoids shifting registers, reduces switching activity, and allows direct indexing of bits, which may be beneficial for power and area.
