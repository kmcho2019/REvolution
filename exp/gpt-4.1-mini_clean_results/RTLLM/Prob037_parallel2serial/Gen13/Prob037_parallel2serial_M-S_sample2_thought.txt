Use a 2-bit counter and a 4-bit register to hold the data. At reset, clear them. When the counter reaches 3 (last bit output), load the new parallel input and assert valid_out. Otherwise, rotate the data left by one bit each clock cycle, increment the counter, and keep valid_out low. Output is always the MSB of the data register. This uses a rotate operation instead of shift with zero insertion for simplicity and accurate bit ordering.
