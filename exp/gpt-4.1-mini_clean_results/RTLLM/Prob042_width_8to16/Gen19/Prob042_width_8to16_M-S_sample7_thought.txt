Use a simple two-cycle process with one register to store the first 8-bit input.

- When a valid_in arrives and the flag is 0, store data_in in data_lock and set flag to 1. Don't generate output yet.
- When the next valid_in arrives and the flag is 1, concatenate {data_lock, data_in} and set valid_out high on the *next* clock cycle.
- valid_out and data_out should be registered outputs updated on the clock edge.
- On reset, clear all registers and flags.
- This simple sequential logic eliminates the FSM and next-state logic, ensuring clear timing and data path.
