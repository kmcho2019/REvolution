Use a simple 1-bit flag to store the first valid 8-bit input data. When the second valid data arrives, concatenate the stored first byte (higher 8 bits) and current input (lower 8 bits) and generate valid_out and data_out signals in the next clock cycle. Keep all registers fully assigned to avoid latches and reset them properly. The valid_out signal is asserted only for one cycle after the concatenation. This simple approach avoids unnecessary registers and logic while meeting the timing and functional requirements.
