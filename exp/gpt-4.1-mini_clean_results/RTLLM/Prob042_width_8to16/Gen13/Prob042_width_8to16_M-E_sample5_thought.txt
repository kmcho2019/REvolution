Use a small finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and WAIT_SECOND. In IDLE state, when valid_in arrives, store the first byte in a register and move to WAIT_SECOND state. In WAIT_SECOND state, when the second valid_in arrives, concatenate the stored first byte (high 8 bits) and the current byte (low 8 bits), set valid_out and data_out in the next cycle, and return to IDLE. Both valid_out and data_out are registered outputs asserted for one cycle, exactly one clock cycle after receiving the second input. Reset clears all registers and returns FSM to IDLE. This FSM-based approach improves clarity, controls timing explicitly, and avoids overwriting or ambiguous signal assertions.
