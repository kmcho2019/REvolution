Refactor the design using a simple two-state FSM with states IDLE and WAIT_SECOND. On receiving the first valid input in IDLE, store it and move to WAIT_SECOND. When the second valid input arrives in WAIT_SECOND, register the concatenation of the stored first byte and current input, assert valid_out, then go back to IDLE. The valid_out and data_out signals are registered and asserted one cycle after the second input arrives, meeting timing requirements. This approach clearly separates state transitions, stores outputs for one cycle, and avoids combinational logic inside the clocked block that might cause synthesis issues.
