Refactor the design by explicitly modeling a small two-state finite state machine (FSM) that tracks whether we have stored the first 8-bit input or not. The FSM has two states: IDLE (no stored data) and WAIT_SECOND (first data stored, waiting for second). On valid_in in IDLE, store first data and go to WAIT_SECOND. On valid_in in WAIT_SECOND, store second data and prepare output. valid_out and data_out are generated registered on the clock cycle following the arrival of the second data, meeting the one cycle delay requirement. This separation of states clarifies timing and helps avoid race conditions. Also, capture new first data immediately if it arrives concurrently during output cycle, so the circuit can chain inputs seamlessly. This approach cleanly handles the reset, timing, and data order requirements.
