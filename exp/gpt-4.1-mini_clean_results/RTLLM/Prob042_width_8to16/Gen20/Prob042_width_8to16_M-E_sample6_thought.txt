Implement a simple two-state FSM with explicit states IDLE and WAIT_SECOND_BYTE:

- In IDLE state: 
    - When valid_in is high, latch data_in into a register (data_lock), transition to WAIT_SECOND_BYTE.
    - valid_out is deasserted.

- In WAIT_SECOND_BYTE state:
    - When valid_in is high, concatenate the stored data_lock as higher 8 bits and new data_in as lower 8 bits.
    - Register the concatenated 16-bit data_out and assert valid_out.
    - Transition back to IDLE state.

- If no valid_in arrives in WAIT_SECOND_BYTE, remain there without producing output until second byte arrives.

- On reset, clear all registers, set state to IDLE, and deassert valid_out.

This clear FSM ensures data_out and valid_out assert exactly one clock after second valid_in and maintains the first byte in the higher 8 bits as required.

This approach cleanly separates the states, prevents premature output, and ensures the output delay is exactly one clock cycle after the second byte input.
