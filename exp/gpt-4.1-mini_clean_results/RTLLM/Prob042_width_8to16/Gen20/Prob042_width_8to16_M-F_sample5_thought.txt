- On the following cycle, valid_out de-asserts to 0, unless another second byte arrives.
- This ensures valid_out and data_out are valid on the cycle after the second byte arrives.

- No separate *_next signals needed; all outputs registered properly inside one always block, ensuring correct timing and simulation.
