- Use one always block triggered on posedge clk/negedge rst_n to update registers: data_lock, flag, valid_out, data_out.
- Use a combinational always block or combinational logic inside the clocked block to calculate next states/outputs from current inputs and state.
- Do NOT register "next" signals separately inside the same clocked always block.
- The output valid_out and data_out should be registered and only updated one clock cycle after receiving the second valid input.
- On the arrival of the second valid input, store concatenated data into temporary registers and raise a signal to output in the next cycle.
- Implement a simple two-stage pipeline:
  * Stage 1: store first byte if flag=0 and valid_in=1.
