Refactor the design into a simple two-state FSM with explicit states IDLE (waiting for first byte) and WAIT_SECOND (waiting for second byte). Store the first byte when valid_in arrives in IDLE state, then transition to WAIT_SECOND. When second valid_in arrives in WAIT_SECOND, store second byte and in the next cycle assert valid_out and output concatenated data. Use separate registers for first_byte, second_byte, output data, and valid_out, updating them synchronously on clk. This clearly separates input collection and output generation phases and ensures valid_out and data_out are registered one cycle after receiving second byte, fully matching the specification and easing synthesis.
