This approach avoids any ambiguous flag toggling or combinational dependencies. The output valid_out and data_out are registered and aligned correctly one cycle after the second 8-bit data arrives.

- The data_lock register stores the first byte until the second byte arrives.

This FSM and pipeline structure improve clarity, synthesizability, and timing behavior, fully satisfying the requirement that the output signals are valid one cycle after the two inputs have been received.
