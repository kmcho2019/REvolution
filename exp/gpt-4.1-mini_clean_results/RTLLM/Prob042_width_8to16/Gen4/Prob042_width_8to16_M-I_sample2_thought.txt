2. On receiving the first valid data (`valid_in` and state == idle), latch it into `data_lock` and move to `wait_second` state.

4. Output `data_out_next` and `valid_out_next` on the next cycle as `data_out` and `valid_out`.
5. Return to `idle` state after outputting data.
