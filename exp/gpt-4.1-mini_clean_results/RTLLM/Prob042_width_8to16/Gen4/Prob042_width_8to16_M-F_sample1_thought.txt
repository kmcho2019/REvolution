To fix synthesis issues and improve timing, implement a simple 2-state finite state machine (FSM) with explicit states to track whether we have stored the first byte or are ready for output. Separate valid_out and data_out updates in registered logic without unconditional resets every cycle.

Key points:
- Define states IDLE (waiting for first byte) and OUTPUT_READY (have first byte stored).
- On valid_in in IDLE, store the first byte and move to OUTPUT_READY.
- On valid_in in OUTPUT_READY, concatenate stored byte and current input, register data_out and valid_out with one cycle latency, then return to IDLE.
- valid_out is driven by a registered signal valid_out_reg, cleared only when new output is not ready.
- This FSM and registered output signals avoid combinational clearing of valid_out, facilitating synthesis and timing closure.

The output data and valid signal appear one clock cycle after the second input byte arrives, as specified.
Reset clears all states and registers.

This structure improves synthesis friendliness and removes glitches related to the immediate clearing of valid_out.
