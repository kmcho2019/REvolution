To meet the requirement that valid_out and data_out are produced exactly one clock cycle after the second valid input arrives, we separate input data capturing and output generation into two pipeline stages:

- Stage 1 (Input capture):
  Use a register `data_lock` and a flag `has_data` to store the first valid 8-bit input and indicate waiting for the second input.

- Stage 2 (Output generation):
  When the second valid input arrives, concatenate `data_lock` and `data_in` and store the result in an output register `out_reg`.
  Assert a `valid_stage2` flag indicating output data is ready.
  
- Stage 3 (Output register update):
  On the next clock cycle, transfer `out_reg` to `data_out` and `valid_stage2` to `valid_out`, thereby ensuring valid_out and data_out are delayed by one clock cycle after the second input.

This approach strictly meets the timing requirement, cleanly pipelining data and valid signals with simple FSM logic and minimal registers. The reset clears all registers and flags to avoid unexpected states. No combinational assignments are used for outputs to avoid glitches and ensure synchronous behavior suitable for synthesis.

This pipeline also avoids generating valid_out immediately in the same cycle as the second input, eliminating timing issues in the original design.
