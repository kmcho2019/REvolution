- In the WAIT_SECOND state, on receiving a valid input, capture the second byte, concatenate it with the first byte, and assert valid_out and data_out signals on the next clock cycle.

- If no valid_in arrives in WAIT_SECOND state, remain waiting and do not assert outputs.
