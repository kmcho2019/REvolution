Use a simple two-register approach and a one-bit flag to track if the first 8-bit data has arrived.

- On reset, clear all registers and output signals.
- When valid_in is asserted:
  - If the flag is 0 (no first data yet), store data_in in data_lock (high 8 bits) and set flag=1.
  - Else (flag=1), store data_in in data_in_second register (low 8 bits), and assert valid_out and update data_out with concatenated {data_lock, data_in_second} in the next clock cycle.
  - Then clear flag=0 so that the process can restart.
- valid_out and data_out are registered outputs and are asserted only one cycle after the second valid_in arrives, indicating valid concatenated data.
- This approach does not require an explicit FSM and handles any valid_in stream by buffering pairs of inputs before outputting concatenated 16-bit data.

This simplifies the design and ensures valid_out and data_out appear exactly one cycle after the second valid input, as requested.
