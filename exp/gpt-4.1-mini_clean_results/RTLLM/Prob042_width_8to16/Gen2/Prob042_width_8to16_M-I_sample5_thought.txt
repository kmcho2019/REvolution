Improve the original design by removing SystemVerilog-specific features (typedef enum) for better synthesis tool compatibility, use synchronous reset to simplify timing, and optimize FSM encoding using localparam constants. Also, hold valid_out and data_out stable until the next valid concatenation to prevent glitches and reduce unnecessary toggling, improving power efficiency. Instead of using separate *_next signals defaulted to zero, use registered outputs updated only when needed.

The FSM has two states (WAIT_FIRST and WAIT_SECOND) encoded as localparams. On the first valid input, latch data_in; on the second valid input, concatenate and assert valid_out in the next clock cycle. Outputs remain stable otherwise. This aligns with the timing requirement to produce outputs one cycle after the second input. Reset clears all registers synchronously. The approach improves synthesis compatibility, timing closure, and power usage, keeping area minimal.
