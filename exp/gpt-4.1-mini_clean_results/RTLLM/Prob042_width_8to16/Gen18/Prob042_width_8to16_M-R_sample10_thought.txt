To refactor the design, I will maintain the core behavior but simplify the state machine to a two-state FSM:

- STATE_IDLE: Waiting for the first valid input. On valid_in, store it and transition to STATE_WAIT_SECOND.
- STATE_WAIT_SECOND: Waiting for the second valid input. On valid_in, capture the second data, prepare the output, assert valid_out for one cycle, and return to STATE_IDLE.

This FSM has only two states, simplifying control logic.

I will also separate the output data and valid_out register updates clearly:

- data_lock holds the first 8-bit data.
- Upon the second valid_in in STATE_WAIT_SECOND, capture the second 8-bit data_in, concatenate with data_lock to form data_out.
- valid_out will be asserted for exactly one cycle on the clock cycle after the second valid input.
- All registers update synchronously on posedge clk or asynchronously on negedge rst_n.
- The valid_out and data_out signals are cleared on reset and otherwise only asserted for the output cycle.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently to avoid glitches.
- The design ensures that if there is a single valid_in, output waits for the second valid_in before asserting valid_out and data_out.
- The first arriving 8-bit data is placed in the higher 8 bits and the second data in the lower 8 bits of the 16-bit output.
- The FSM transitions and outputs are clearly defined and protected against invalid input timing.

This approach simplifies the design and makes the output timing and data concatenation clearer while matching all requirements.
