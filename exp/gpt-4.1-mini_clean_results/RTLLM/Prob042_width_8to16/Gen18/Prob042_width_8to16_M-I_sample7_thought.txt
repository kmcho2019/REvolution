Improve synthesizability and clarity by separating combinational next-state logic from sequential output/data registers cleanly, and simplifying output register updates.

- Use a two-state FSM with explicit state encoding.
- Implement next state and output valid logic in combinational always block.
- Store the first byte immediately on first valid_in in sequential block.
- Generate valid_out and data_out registers only in sequential block on clock edge.
- Avoid combinational "next_data_out" signals; build output data directly in sequential logic when transitioning states.
- Remove conditional clearing of data_out; hold the output stable until next valid data.
- This approach reduces inferred latches and improves synthesis friendliness.
- Keep the output valid and data_out registers aligned with one-cycle delay after second byte input.
- The first data_in is stored in data_lock on arrival.
- Upon arrival of second valid_in, data_out and valid_out update together on next clock cycle.
- Use parameters for states for better readability and future extensibility.

This structure improves synthesis results by clearly separating combinational next-state logic from sequential state and output register updates, ensuring no inferred latches and straightforward timing.
