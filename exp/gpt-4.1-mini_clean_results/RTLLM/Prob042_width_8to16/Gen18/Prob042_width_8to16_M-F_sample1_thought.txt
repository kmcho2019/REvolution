The main issue is the timing of valid_out and data_out: they must be asserted in the clock cycle *after* the second valid input arrives, not in the same cycle.

To fix this, I will:
- Use a 2-state FSM: IDLE (waiting for first valid input) and WAIT (waiting for second valid input).
- On first valid_in in IDLE, latch the data_in into data_lock, move to WAIT, but do not assert valid_out.
- On second valid_in in WAIT, latch the concatenated 16-bit output ({data_lock, data_in}) into a separate output register and assert valid_out in the *next* clock cycle.
- To generate valid_out and data_out one cycle later, add an extra register stage valid_out_reg and data_out_reg that hold the output signals.
- Implement this by updating data_out_reg and valid_out_reg at posedge clk after transitioning to IDLE from WAIT (i.e., on the cycle after second data arrival).
- Keep FSM and output registers separate to improve synthesis friendliness and clarity.
- Clear output valid_out_reg and data_out_reg on reset and when no valid output is generated.

This ensures valid_out and data_out are delayed by one clock cycle after the second input arrives, meeting the specification and improving synthesis results.
