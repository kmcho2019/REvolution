The design buffers the first 8-bit valid input in a register (data_lock) and sets a flag indicating that the first byte is stored. Upon arrival of the second valid input, it concatenates the stored first byte (higher 8 bits) and the current input byte (lower 8 bits) and outputs the combined 16-bit data along with a valid_out signal in the next clock cycle. The output valid_out and data_out are registered, valid only after two inputs have been received. On reset, all registers and flags clear to zero. The outputs only assert valid_out when two valid inputs are concatenated.
