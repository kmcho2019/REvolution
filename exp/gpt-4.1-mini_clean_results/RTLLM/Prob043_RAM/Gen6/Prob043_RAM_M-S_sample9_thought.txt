Implement a synchronous dual-port RAM with 8 entries of 6 bits each. On the active-low reset, clear all RAM locations and the read_data output to zero synchronously. Write data on the positive clock edge when write_en is high. Read data on the positive clock edge when read_en is high, else output zero. This approach uses two always blocks with synchronous resets to ensure deterministic reset behavior and simpler logic without combinational reads.
