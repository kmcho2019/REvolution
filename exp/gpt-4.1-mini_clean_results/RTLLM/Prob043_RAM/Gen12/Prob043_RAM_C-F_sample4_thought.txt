- Using dual always blocks (one for write and reset of RAM, another for read and reset of read_data) matches the problem specification and ensures independent read and write ports operate simultaneously.

- By registering the read_data output, we gain improved timing and power behavior at the cost of one clock cycle read latency, which is acceptable and often preferred in ASIC/FPGA designs.

- Asynchronous reset (active low) for both RAM initialization and read_data register.
