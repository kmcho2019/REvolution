To improve the original solution's power and reset latency, I will switch from an asynchronous reset (negedge rst_n) to a synchronous reset that initializes the RAM on the rising clock edge. This avoids the reset loop triggered asynchronously, reducing reset complexity and power spikes. Also, I'll keep read logic combinational for low read latency, as it is already efficient. Clock gating can be considered at higher system level, so I'll avoid extra complexity here. This approach enhances timing closure and reduces power during reset while keeping functionality intact.
