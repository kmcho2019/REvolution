To optimize the dual-port RAM design for better power and timing while maintaining the same functionality, I will implement the following improvements:

1. Change the read_data output to be registered on posedge clk instead of combinational. This reduces timing criticality on the read path by breaking combinational chains and improving setup time margin, at the expense of one cycle read latency.

2. Keep synchronous reset only on read_data (to avoid clearing the entire RAM on reset) since clearing all RAM entries synchronously can cause a large reset latency and power spike. Instead, initialize RAM contents at declaration with zero, as 8 entries are small and most synthesis tools support initialization from RTL.

3. Gate the registered read_data update with read_en to avoid unnecessary toggling and reduce power. When read_en=0, keep read_data at zero to indicate no valid data.

4. Keep the write operation synchronous with write_en at clk posedge with no reset clearing of the entire RAM, leveraging initialization.

These steps reduce reset-related power and latency, reduce read_data switching activity, and improve timing on the read path, while maintaining dual-port simultaneous access.

The address width parameter calculation is corrected to use DEPTH for addressing, consistent with requirements.

Overall, this approach better balances timing, power, and area trade-offs for this small dual-port RAM design.
