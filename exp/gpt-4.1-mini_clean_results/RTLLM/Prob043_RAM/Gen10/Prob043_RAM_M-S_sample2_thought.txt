Simplify the dual-port RAM by using a single synchronous process triggered by the clock and asynchronous reset. On reset, clear the read_data output; on write_en, write data synchronously; on read_en, read data synchronously; otherwise, read_data is cleared to zero. Use a simple reg array for RAM with fixed DEPTH and WIDTH, and the read_data is registered rather than combinational. This eliminates the initial block and separate always blocks, and straightforwardly maintains all required functionality.
