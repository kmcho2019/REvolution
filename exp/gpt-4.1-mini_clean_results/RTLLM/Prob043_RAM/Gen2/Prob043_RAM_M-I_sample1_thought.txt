To optimize the given dual-port RAM design for better performance and power (PPA), I will register the read_data output. This reduces the combinational read path critical timing and prevents large glitches on the output, thus improving timing and power by limiting switching to the clock edges. Although this introduces a 1-clock cycle read latency, it is typically acceptable for synchronous designs. 

Additionally, I will keep the synchronous RAM reset for compatibility, but consider that FPGA synthesis tools may optimize the reset loop automatically. The read enable signal will gate the registered output: when read_en is low, read_data will be zero, reducing unnecessary toggling.

The RAM depth and width remain unchanged, and parameterization is kept. The write operation remains synchronous on posedge clk. The read operation uses a synchronous always block clocked on posedge clk and rst_n with output read_data registered. This design offers a good trade-off between performance, power, and area for small FPGA-based RAM blocks.
