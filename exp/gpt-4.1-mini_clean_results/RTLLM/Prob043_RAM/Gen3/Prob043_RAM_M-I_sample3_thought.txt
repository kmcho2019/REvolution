To optimize the design for better performance and power efficiency, implement synchronous read operation instead of combinational read. This reduces combinational path delays and improves timing closure. Also, latch the read_data output, avoiding resetting it to zero when read_en is low; this reduces unnecessary toggling on the output bus, thus lowering dynamic power. 

On reset, clear read_data synchronously and initialize RAM contents to zero inside the reset block to ensure deterministic RAM contents after reset. This approach trades a small reset overhead for improved robustness and clearer behavior. 

Overall, this design uses synchronous write and synchronous read with registered outputs and reset-controlled initialization, improving timing and power metrics over the original combinational read design.
