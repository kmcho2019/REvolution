To optimize the original solution for better power and timing, I will register the read_data output, adding one clock cycle read latency but improving timing and reducing output switching noise. The read process will be synchronous to the clock and gated by read_en. When read_en is low, read_data will hold its previous value to reduce toggling instead of forcing zeros. Additionally, I will keep synchronous reset but only clear read_data register (not entire RAM) for faster reset and lower power. Initializing the small RAM array asynchronously is less portable and often not supported by all tools, so keeping synchronous write with no mass reset of RAM contents reduces reset latency and power. This approach balances power, timing, and area well for a small dual-port RAM.
