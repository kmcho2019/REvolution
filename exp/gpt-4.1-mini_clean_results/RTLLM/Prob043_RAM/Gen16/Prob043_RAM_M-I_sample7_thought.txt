To optimize the original dual-port RAM design for better PPA metrics:

1. Remove the synchronous reset logic that clears the entire RAM array on reset. Instead, rely on power-on initialization (zeros by default) or an explicit asynchronous reset of the read_data register only. This reduces the reset path complexity, improving timing (lower negative slack) and power by avoiding mass toggling on reset.

2. Implement synchronous read with registered output to improve timing closure. Although this introduces a 1-cycle latency on read, it greatly eases timing closure and stabilizes read_data output, beneficial for high-frequency operation.

3. Keep write operation synchronous and gated by write_en.

4. Use a localparam for address width based on DEPTH to ensure scalability and clarity.

5. Initialize RAM contents to zero by defining initial block for simulation purposes (synthesis tools might infer RAM initialization differently).

6. Read enable gating is done inside the synchronous read process: when read_en is low, read_data is zeroed to reduce toggling and power.

This balances timing, power, and area by avoiding costly synchronous RAM-wide reset, stabilizing outputs with registered reads, and maintaining simple structure.
