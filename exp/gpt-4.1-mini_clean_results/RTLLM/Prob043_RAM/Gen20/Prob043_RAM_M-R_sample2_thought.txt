Refactor the original synchronous reset and write logic by separating the synchronous reset into its own always block that initializes RAM on reset, and keep the write operation in a separate always block triggered by the clock and write enable only. Implement the read operation as a registered output with synchronous read for a stable output and easier timing closure. This approach removes the reset logic from the write block, making the design clearer and potentially better for synthesis tools. The registered read output avoids combinational read gating which might introduce glitches or combinational delays.
