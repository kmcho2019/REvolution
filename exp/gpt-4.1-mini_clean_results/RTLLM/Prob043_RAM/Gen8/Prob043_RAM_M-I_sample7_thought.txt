To improve the given dual-port RAM implementation, I will register the read_data output on the clock edge rather than providing combinational read output. This reduces timing critical path on the combinational read and lowers power by reducing output toggling. Also, I will maintain synchronous RAM initialization but optimize the reset logic to reset RAM only once at startup by a reset state and avoid resetting on every clock cycle when rst_n is low. This reduces toggling and power. The design keeps simultaneous read and write operations and parameterization for scalability. Address width is computed via clog2 for safety. Overall, this will trade a one-cycle read latency for improved timing and power metrics.
