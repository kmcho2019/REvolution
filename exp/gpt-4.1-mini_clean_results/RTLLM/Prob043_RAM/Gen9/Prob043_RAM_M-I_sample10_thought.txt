To improve the previous design for better power and timing (PPA), implement synchronous read operation with registered read address and output data to reduce combinational path delay and improve timing closure. Instead of clearing read_data to zero when read_en is low, keep the last read data to reduce unnecessary toggling and switching activity, saving power. Maintain synchronous reset for output register to avoid asynchronous reset in combinational logic. Use a synchronous reset for read_data and initialize RAM at elaboration time. The write port remains synchronous with clock and uses non-blocking assignment. This approach balances power reduction, timing improvements, and functional correctness for dual-port RAM with simultaneous read/write.
