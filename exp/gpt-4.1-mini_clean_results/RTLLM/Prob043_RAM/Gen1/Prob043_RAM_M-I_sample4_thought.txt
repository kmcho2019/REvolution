To optimize the dual-port RAM for better PPA, the main strategy is to reduce unnecessary switching, especially on the read_data output. Instead of clearing read_data to zero when read_en is low, we keep it holding its last valid value, thereby reducing output toggling and power consumption. Additionally, since the RAM depth is small, we keep the zero initialization on reset as is for correctness and simplicity. This avoids complexity that may increase area or timing. The RAM array is maintained as a synchronous dual-port structure, and the write and read addresses remain 3-bit wide as before. Overall, this adjustment improves power consumption by reducing output switching with no loss in functionality.
