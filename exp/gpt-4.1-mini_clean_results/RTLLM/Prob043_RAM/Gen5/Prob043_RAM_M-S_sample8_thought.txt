I will simplify the dual-port RAM by implementing synchronous read and write operations within a single clocked always block, which is straightforward and easy to understand. On reset, the RAM contents and read_data register are cleared to zero. The read operation outputs the stored data registered on the clock edge when read_en is active; otherwise, read_data outputs zero. This method preserves functionality, including the reset initialization, while removing the asynchronous read and multiple always blocks for simplicity.
