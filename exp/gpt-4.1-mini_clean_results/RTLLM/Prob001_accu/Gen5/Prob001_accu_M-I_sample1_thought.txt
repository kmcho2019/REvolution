To optimize the original design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will:

1. Remove the intermediate variable sum_temp and perform the addition directly in non-blocking assignments to avoid mixed blocking/non-blocking usage and help synthesis tools optimize the logic.

2. Combine the addition and count increment in a clean, fully sequential manner with non-blocking assignments to reduce combinational critical paths.

3. Update the accumulator and count only when valid_in is asserted to minimize toggling.

4. Keep valid_out asserted for only one cycle after four inputs are accumulated.

5. Use minimal registers (accumulator and count) and a 10-bit accumulator to accommodate the max sum (4 * 8-bit max = 1020 fits within 10 bits).

This improves timing by avoiding combinational loops or extra registers, reduces switching by conditional updates, and maintains low area by using only essential registers.

The updated implementation follows best Verilog practices with proper reset and sequential logic.
