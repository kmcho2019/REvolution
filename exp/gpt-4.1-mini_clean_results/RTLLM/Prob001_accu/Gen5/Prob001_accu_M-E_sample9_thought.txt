Use a shift register array to store incoming valid data inputs serially and accumulate them only when 4 valid inputs have been collected. Maintain a counter to track how many valid inputs have been received. On each valid_in cycle, shift the new data into a 4-element register array. Once 4 inputs have been collected, calculate the sum by adding all 4 elements in a combinational manner and output the result with valid_out asserted for one cycle. After output, clear the counter and wait for the next 4 inputs. This approach uses storage of inputs explicitly and accumulates them all at once for output, separating input collection from output generation.
