To optimize the existing design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area):

1. Remove the redundant sum_reg register as it is not utilized outside the always block, reducing area and power.
2. Use an enable signal derived from valid_in to update the accumulator and counter only when valid_in is asserted, minimizing toggling power.
3. Drive valid_out only when the accumulation is ready (when 4 inputs are received), and keep it low otherwise, avoiding unnecessary toggling.
4. Separate the accumulator reset and output update into sequential steps to reduce combinational critical paths, improving timing.
5. Retain synchronous active-low reset and register outputs for robust timing.

These changes reduce flip-flop count, minimize switching activity, and improve timing by simplifying the logic without impacting functionality.
