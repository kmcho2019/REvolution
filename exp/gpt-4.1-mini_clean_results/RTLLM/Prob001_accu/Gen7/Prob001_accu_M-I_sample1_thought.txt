Improvement strategy to optimize PPA:

1. Add explicit clock gating to accumulator and count registers to minimize toggling and dynamic power when valid_in is low, reducing unnecessary switching.
2. Register data_in on a valid_in edge to break long combinational path and potentially improve timing.
3. Use blocking assignments for combinational logic and non-blocking for sequential logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
4. Maintain minimal logic and registers without adding pipeline stages, since throughput is input-dependent.
5. Keep valid_out logic simple and assert for one cycle only.
6. Overall, power is reduced by clock gating; timing is improved by registering data_in; area impact of clock gating is minimal and justified by power saving.
