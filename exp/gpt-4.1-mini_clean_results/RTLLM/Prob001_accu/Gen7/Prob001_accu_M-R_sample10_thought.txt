Refactor the accumulator module by introducing an explicit state machine with two states: IDLE and ACCUMULATE. This design separates the accumulation process from the output stage clearly. The accumulator and count are updated only when valid_in is asserted in ACCUMULATE state. Once four data inputs are accumulated, transition to IDLE state in the next cycle, outputting the sum and asserting valid_out. This separation simplifies control logic and ensures valid_out is a one-cycle pulse. Use combinational always block for next-state and output logic, and synchronous block for state and data registers. This structure improves clarity, modularity, and timing predictability, while maintaining the same functionality.
