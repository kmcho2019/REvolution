To improve the existing design's PPA metrics, the main focus will be on power and timing optimizations with minimal area impact.

1. Power Optimization:
   - Replace the combinational enable gating (`if (valid_in)`) inside the always block with a clock enable (CE) signal that gates register updates. This reduces toggling of accumulator and count registers during invalid cycles.
   - Hold `valid_out` low by default with a register that only pulses high for one cycle when needed, preventing unnecessary toggles.
   - Use synchronous reset only for registers that must be cleared; valid_out can be reset asynchronously to minimize glitches.

2. Timing Optimization:
   - Instead of accumulating three inputs then adding the 4th input to produce the output in the same cycle, add the data_in to the accumulator at every valid_in cycle.
   - When count reaches 3 (meaning 4 data points have been accumulated), output the sum immediately.
   - This approach breaks the addition path into a uniform pipeline stage, avoiding adding `data_in` twice in one clock (since previous design adds the 4th input on output cycle).
   - This gives a predictable, short critical path for each cycle's addition, improving timing margin.

3. Area Consideration:
   - The modifications don't add extra registers or combinational logic; the area impact is minimal.
   - Using a clock enable is generally area neutral or slightly better than combinational gating in modern synthesis.

Overall, the design becomes a straightforward accumulator with clock enable and clean output signaling, improving power and timing without area penalty.
