Use a single always block triggered on the clock's rising edge with synchronous active-low reset. Keep a small 2-bit counter to track how many valid inputs have been accumulated, and a 10-bit accumulator for the sum. When valid_in is high, add the input to the accumulator and increment the count. Once four inputs have been accumulated (count reaches 3 before adding the current input), output the sum of accumulator plus current data_in on data_out, assert valid_out for one cycle, then reset accumulator and count for the next batch. Otherwise, just accumulate without output. Set valid_out to 0 by default every cycle. This approach reduces extra conditions and code, while maintaining the required one-cycle pulse and accumulation logic.
