Use a small FIFO-like buffer of 4 registers to temporarily store incoming valid data_in values. When valid_in is asserted, shift data_in into this buffer, and increment a counter. When the counter reaches 4, sum all 4 buffered values simultaneously using a parallel adder tree and output the result with valid_out asserted for one clock cycle. Then reset the counter and buffer. This approach delays addition until 4 inputs are collected, potentially reducing the addition logic toggling each cycle compared to accumulating on each input, which might reduce dynamic power. The addition is performed in one clock cycle on 4 inputs instead of incremental accumulation.
