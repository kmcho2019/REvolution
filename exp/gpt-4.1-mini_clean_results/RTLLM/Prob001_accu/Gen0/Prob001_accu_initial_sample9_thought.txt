Accumulate incoming valid 8-bit data_in values when valid_in is asserted. Use a counter to track the number of valid inputs received (up to 4). Sum the data_in values in an accumulator register. Once 4 valid inputs have been accumulated, output the sum on data_out with valid_out asserted for one clock cycle. Reset accumulator and counter after output. Reset logic clears states on rst_n deassertion.
