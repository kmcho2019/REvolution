Accumulate the 8-bit input data when valid_in is high. Use a counter to count valid data inputs, and accumulate their sum in a register wide enough to hold the sum of four 8-bit numbers (max 4*255=1020, fits in 10 bits). When the count reaches 4, output the accumulated sum on data_out, set valid_out high for one cycle, then reset the accumulator and count. Synchronize all operations on the rising edge of clk and reset asynchronously with rst_n.
