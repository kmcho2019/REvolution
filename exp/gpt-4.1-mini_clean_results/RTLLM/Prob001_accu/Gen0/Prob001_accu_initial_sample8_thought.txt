Accumulate the input data_in values only when valid_in is asserted. Use a counter to track the number of valid inputs received. Once four valid data_in samples have been received and accumulated, output the sum on data_out, set valid_out high for one clock cycle, then reset the accumulator and the counter for the next round. Use a 10-bit register for accumulation since the max sum of four 8-bit numbers (4*255=1020) fits within 10 bits.
