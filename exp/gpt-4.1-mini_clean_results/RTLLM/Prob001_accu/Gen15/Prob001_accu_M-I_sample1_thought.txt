To optimize the design for better PPA, I will implement clock gating by enabling accumulator and count registers to update only when valid_in is asserted, reducing unnecessary switching and dynamic power consumption. Additionally, I will keep the valid_out pulse asserted for only one cycle upon accumulating 4 valid inputs, ensuring correct output timing. The synchronous reset logic will be retained. This approach reduces toggle rate on registers and combinational adders without impacting timing or area noticeably.
