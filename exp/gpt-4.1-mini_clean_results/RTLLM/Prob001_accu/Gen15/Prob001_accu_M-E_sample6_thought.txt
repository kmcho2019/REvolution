Use an approach where we store the four 8-bit inputs in a small shift register array as they come in, gated by valid_in. Once 4 inputs are collected, perform the addition in one clock cycle using a combinational adder summing all four stored values simultaneously. Output the sum and pulse valid_out for one cycle. This method decouples accumulation from addition, potentially reducing the critical path from sequential additions and making the output logic more straightforward.
