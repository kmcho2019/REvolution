To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA):

1. Register the data_in input to reduce the adder's combinational delay by shortening the critical path.
2. Use clock gating-like enable control (via valid_in) for accumulator and count registers to avoid unnecessary toggling and dynamic power when valid_in is low.
3. Simplify control logic to reduce unnecessary signal toggling, ensuring valid_out pulses only once per 4 data inputs.
4. Keep the accumulator width at 10 bits and counter at 2 bits as they are minimal and efficient.
5. Maintain synchronous active-low reset for reliability.

These improvements aim to shorten critical paths and minimize toggling to reduce power without adding area overhead or complexity.
