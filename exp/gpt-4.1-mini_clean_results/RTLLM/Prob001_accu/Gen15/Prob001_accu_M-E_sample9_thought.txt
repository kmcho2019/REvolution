Instead of accumulating data only on the fly, this design uses an internal small FIFO-like shift register to store four consecutive valid inputs. Once four valid inputs are collected, their sum is computed combinationally and output immediately in the next cycle with valid_out pulsed. This pipeline approach separates data storage and addition, potentially shortening the critical path because accumulation is a combinational sum of four 8-bit values rather than iterative additions. It also keeps the accumulation latency constant and the valid_out pulse aligned with output data. When valid_in is low, the module does not shift in new data, preserving previous inputs. Reset clears all registers.
