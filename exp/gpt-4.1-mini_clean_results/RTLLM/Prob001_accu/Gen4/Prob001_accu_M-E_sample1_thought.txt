Implement a serial accumulator using a shift-register style approach to store each incoming 8-bit data in a small buffer of four registers. After collecting four valid inputs, sum the buffered values simultaneously in a combinational block and output the 10-bit result along with a one-cycle valid_out pulse. This decouples input data collection from the addition stage and provides a clean structural separation. The buffer is reset upon rst_n or after output. This design emphasizes clarity and ease of modification, potentially allowing parallel operations on stored data, and can be easily extended to larger block sizes.
