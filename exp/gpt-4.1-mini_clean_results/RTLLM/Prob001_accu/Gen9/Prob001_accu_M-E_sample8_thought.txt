Use a small finite state machine (FSM) approach that separates counting and accumulation explicitly. The design will keep a 10-bit accumulator and a 2-bit counter. On each clock when valid_in is high, add data_in to the accumulator and increment the counter.

When the counter reaches 4, output the accumulator sum through data_out, set valid_out for one cycle, then reset the accumulator and counter synchronously.

To reduce toggling when no valid input arrives, the accumulator and counter update only on valid_in high (use an enable condition), otherwise hold their value.

Additionally, to separate the output stage and make the valid_out pulse generation cleaner, introduce a 'ready' signal to latch the accumulated sum and drive valid_out only for one cycle immediately after count == 4.

This two-stage approach separates accumulation and output timing clearly, simplifying the valid_out pulse generation and making timing easier.

All signals reset synchronously. Use non-blocking assignments for all sequential logic.

This novel FSM and output latching architecture yields clean timing, minimal toggling, and a simple control scheme.
