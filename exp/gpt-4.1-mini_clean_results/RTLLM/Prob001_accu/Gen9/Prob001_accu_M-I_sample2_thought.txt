To optimize the design for better power and timing, add an enable signal to accumulator and count registers to reduce unnecessary switching when valid_in is low. This helps in clock gating internally without modifying the clock tree, which can reduce dynamic power consumption. The valid_out signal is generated combinationally based on the count reaching 3 on valid_in, so the output timing improves slightly. Keeping the code clean, we also initialize outputs synchronously for predictable reset behavior.
