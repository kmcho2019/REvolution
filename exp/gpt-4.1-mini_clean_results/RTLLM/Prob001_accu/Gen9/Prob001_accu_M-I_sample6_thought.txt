To optimize the existing design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA):

- Performance: Reduce the critical path by separating the addition into two stages:
  1) Accumulate partial sums using an internal register updated only when valid_in is high.
  2) On the 4th valid input, output the accumulated sum.
  This pipelining reduces the complexity of the combinational logic in a single cycle, improving timing.
  
- Power: Register updates will only occur when valid_in is asserted. The accumulator and counter are only enabled on valid_in, lowering switching activity when idle.
  
- Area: Minor area reduction by using a one-hot encoded 2-bit counter instead of binary counter can reduce glitches and simplify decode logic, slightly reducing area and dynamic power.

- Maintain synchronous reset and a one-cycle valid_out pulse as before.

These changes collectively improve timing by pipelining the accumulation and reduce power through register enables without increasing complexity or latency.
