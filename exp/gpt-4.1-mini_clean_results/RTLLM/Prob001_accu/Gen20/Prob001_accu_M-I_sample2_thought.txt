To optimize for better power and timing, we can reduce unnecessary toggling by updating accumulator and count registers only when valid_in is asserted, minimizing switching activity. Also, making valid_out a registered output and removing direct assignment in every cycle reduces glitches and helps timing. Since data_out and valid_out are only meaningful when 4 data samples are accumulated, output registers are updated only on that event to reduce switching on outputs. This will lower power and may improve timing due to less switching and cleaner output control. The overall logic remains the same to keep the critical path minimal.
