- Computing the next accumulator value (`acc_next`) combinationally only when `valid_in` is high to avoid unnecessary toggling.
- On the 4th valid input (count == 3), output `acc_next` (which is the sum of the current accumulator and `data_in`), assert a one-cycle `valid_out`, and immediately reset the accumulator and count for the next accumulation.
- Use a single always block triggered on `posedge clk` with synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments to ensure proper sequential behavior.
- Default `valid_out` to 0 every cycle, and only pulse it when the sum of four inputs is output.
- This design minimizes toggling by updating state only on valid inputs, avoids double addition of `data_in` in the same cycle, and keeps the logic simple and efficient.
- The accumulator and counter only update when `valid_in` is asserted, reducing dynamic power.
- The 10-bit adder is only engaged when necessary, preventing glitches or unnecessary toggling.
- Overall, the design maximizes clarity, performance, power-efficiency, and area efficiency.
