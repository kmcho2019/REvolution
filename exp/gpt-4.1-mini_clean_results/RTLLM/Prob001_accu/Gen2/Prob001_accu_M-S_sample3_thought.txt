To simplify the design while maintaining functionality:

1. Remove the redundant sum_reg register since data_out directly holds the accumulated sum.
2. Combine all state updates in a single always block.
3. Keep the synchronous reset, accumulator, and counter.
4. Update accumulator and counter only when valid_in is asserted.
5. Output valid_out and data_out only when 4 valid inputs are accumulated.
6. Keep the logic straightforward for easy understanding and minimal registers.

This approach reduces complexity and resource usage without impacting the required functionality.
