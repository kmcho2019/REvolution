Refactor the design by splitting the accumulation process and the output generation into two always blocks. Use an intermediate wire to hold the next accumulator value to share the addition result and reduce redundant computations. Use a 3-bit counter (counting from 0 to 3) to track the number of valid inputs received. When the count reaches 3 and a new valid_in arrives, output the accumulated sum plus the new data_in, set valid_out high for one cycle, and reset the accumulator and counter. This structure separates counting and accumulation from output logic, improving clarity and enabling more efficient resource sharing.
