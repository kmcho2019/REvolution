Instead of accumulating directly every cycle, use a small shift register to store the four valid inputs and perform the accumulation only when the fourth data arrives. This way, all additions can be done combinationally in one clock cycle without incremental accumulation, potentially reducing the critical path of sequential addition. This also separates input collection and summation phases clearly. valid_out is asserted one cycle after the fourth valid input is stored, and then the shift register clears for the next batch.
