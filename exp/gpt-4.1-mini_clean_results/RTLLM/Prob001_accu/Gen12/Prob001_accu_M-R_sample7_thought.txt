Refactor the accumulator module by implementing a simple finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and ACCUMULATE. 

- When valid_in is asserted, transition to ACCUMULATE state, increment count, and add input to accumulator.
- When count reaches 4, output the accumulated sum, assert valid_out for one cycle, and return to IDLE state (reset accumulator and count).
- Use synchronous reset and separate combinational logic for next-state and next-values to make the structure clear.
- Use registers for count, accumulator, and state.
- This FSM structure improves clarity of control flow compared to the previous approach with conditional checks inside one always block.
