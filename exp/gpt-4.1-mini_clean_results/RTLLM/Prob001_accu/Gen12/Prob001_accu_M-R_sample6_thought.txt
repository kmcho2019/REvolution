Refactor the design by implementing a simple finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and ACCUMULATE. The FSM enables accumulation only when valid_in is high and valid_in was asserted initially. We will use a separate register to hold the accumulator sum and a counter register to track the number of valid inputs received. When the count reaches 4, the FSM moves back to IDLE, outputs the sum with valid_out pulsed for one clock cycle, then resets accumulator and count for the next batch. This separates control from data path logic, uses synchronous reset and outputs valid_out only in the one clock cycle after 4 accumulations, improving readability and maintainability.
