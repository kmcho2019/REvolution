To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics, I will:

1. **Performance:** Avoid adding data_in twice in the same clock cycle by accumulating data_in first and then, on reaching 4 inputs, outputting the accumulator without an extra addition. This shortens the critical path.

2. **Power:** Enable register updates only when valid_in is asserted to reduce unnecessary toggling. Also, suppress updates when rst_n is deasserted.

3. **Area:** The design is already minimal; no extra logic added.

4. **Control signal timing:** Keep valid_out asserted for only one cycle immediately after accumulation.

By updating accumulator and count only when valid_in is high, and outputting the accumulated sum without double addition, the design becomes more efficient with a cleaner critical path.
