Fuse the strengths of both examples by carefully separating the accumulation and output phases to avoid redundant additions in the critical path, while keeping the logic minimal and clean:

- Use a 2-bit counter and a 10-bit sum register to accumulate data_in when valid_in is high.
- On receiving the 4th valid input (count == 2), accumulate that input into sum first, then in the next cycle output the sum with valid_out asserted for one cycle.
- This approach decouples accumulation from output, preventing the need to perform sum + data_in twice in the same cycle, improving timing.
- valid_out and data_out are registered outputs, with valid_out asserted for exactly one cycle after accumulation.
- Reset logic clears sum, count, data_out, and valid_out synchronously on reset.
- The design avoids combinational dependency loops and redundant adders, reducing critical path length.
- valid_out defaults to zero each cycle.
- This approach slightly extends latency by one cycle after the 4th input, but significantly improves timing and clarity.
