2. Use an enable signal to update accumulator and counter only when valid_in is asserted, reducing unnecessary toggling.
3. Register the sum output separately to avoid any combinational race conditions.
4. Use a local wire for the next accumulator value to reduce logic fanout and improve timing.
5. Reset the accumulator and counter immediately after outputting, to minimize logic switching next cycle.
6. Keep the design simple and clean, maintaining the 10-bit width for the accumulator as in the original.
