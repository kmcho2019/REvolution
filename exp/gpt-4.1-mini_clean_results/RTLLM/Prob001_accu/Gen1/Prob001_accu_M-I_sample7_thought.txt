To improve the design's PPA, especially timing and power, optimize the addition by performing the sum of accumulator and data_in only once per clock cycle. Use a temporary wire to hold this sum, and use it both for updating the accumulator and for outputting data_out when the fourth valid input arrives. This eliminates redundant additions, reduces logic depth on the critical path, and reduces switching activity. The rest of the design remains unchanged with a 2-bit counter and synchronous reset. valid_out is asserted only for one cycle when outputting the accumulated sum.
