Use a small FSM with an explicit state to accumulate four valid inputs. Maintain an accumulator register and a 3-bit index counter for inputs received (0 to 3). When valid_in is asserted, add data_in to the accumulator and increment the counter. After the fourth input, transition to an output state where data_out is registered and valid_out pulses high for one clock cycle. Then return to idle to accumulate the next batch. This approach separates accumulation and output phases explicitly, making the control logic clearer.
