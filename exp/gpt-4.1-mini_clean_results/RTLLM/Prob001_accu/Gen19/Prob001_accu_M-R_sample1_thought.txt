Refactor the design to use a two-process FSM approach: one always block for the sequential logic (register updates) and one combinational block to determine the next state and next values of accumulator and count. This separates the state update and next-state logic, improving clarity and ease of modifications. The counter and accumulator are combined into a simple FSM with states encoded by the count variable. The valid_out pulse is asserted when the count reaches 3 and valid_in is high. This structure also explicitly defines next-state logic, which is beneficial for synthesis and understanding.
