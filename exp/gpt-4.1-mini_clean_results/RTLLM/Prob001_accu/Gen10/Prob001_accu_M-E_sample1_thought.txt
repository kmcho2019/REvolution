Implement a sequential accumulator with a small state machine to separate input counting and output generation phases: 

1. Use a 3-bit accumulator to keep sum of four 8-bit inputs (max 1020, fits in 10 bits).
2. Maintain a 2-bit counter to track the number of valid inputs received (0 to 3).
3. On each clock cycle when valid_in is high, add data_in to the accumulator and increment the counter.
4. Once the counter reaches 3 (meaning four data inputs received including current), output the accumulated sum and assert valid_out for one cycle.
5. Use a separate internal register to latch the output sum exactly at the cycle after receiving the 4th input.
6. Reset accumulator and counter immediately after output generation, ready for the next four inputs.
7. valid_out pulse is generated by combinational comparison between counter and 3, combined with valid_in.
8. Data_out updates registered sum to ensure stable output aligned with valid_out.
9. This approach avoids combinational logic on the output path during accumulation and separates concerns cleanly.

The design focuses on a small FSM-like behavior implicitly controlled by counter and valid_in signals, with registered outputs to keep timing clean.
