To simplify the design while maintaining the required functionality, I will:

1. Remove the redundant sum_reg register, as it is not needed for output or internal logic.
2. Use a single always block to accumulate data_in when valid_in is high, increment a counter, and output the sum when 4 inputs are accumulated.
