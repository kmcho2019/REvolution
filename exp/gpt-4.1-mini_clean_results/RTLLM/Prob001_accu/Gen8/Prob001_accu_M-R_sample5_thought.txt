Refactor the design to use a small finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and ACCUMULATE.

- When valid_in is high and we are in IDLE, move to ACCUMULATE and start accumulating data.
- In ACCUMULATE, accumulate valid data_in inputs, counting them with a 2-bit counter.
- When 4 data inputs have been accumulated, output the sum and pulse valid_out for one cycle, then return to IDLE.
- Use combinational logic for valid_out and data_out based on FSM state and count.
- Registers hold accumulator, count, and current state for synchronous updates.
- This separates control (FSM) and data path (accumulator), improving clarity and timing paths.

This approach avoids double addition in one cycle and makes valid_out generation explicit in the FSM state transition.
