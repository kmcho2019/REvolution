Refactor the design using a simple FSM with two states: ACCUMULATE and OUTPUT.

- Accumulate data_in in ACCUMULATE state on valid_in.
- When four inputs are accumulated, transition to OUTPUT state, output the sum, assert valid_out for one cycle.
