Implement a frequency divider for even numbers by counting clock cycles with a 4-bit counter `cnt`. Instead of toggling at `NUM_DIV/2 - 1`, toggle the output `clk_div` every time the counter reaches `(NUM_DIV/2)`. The counter counts from 0 to `(NUM_DIV/2) - 1`, and when it hits `(NUM_DIV/2)`, the counter resets to zero and `clk_div` toggles. On asynchronous active-low reset `rst_n`, reset `cnt` and `clk_div` to zero. This method ensures a clean divide-by-even clock with a simple compare and toggle.
