Implement the frequency divider using a down-counter that counts from half of the division number down to zero. Each time the counter reaches zero, toggle the output clock and reload the counter to half the division factor minus one. This approach cleanly handles the toggling at exact intervals, ensuring the output frequency is the input frequency divided by the even NUM_DIV. Parameter checking is done at compile time with an assertion. The counter width is fixed to 4 bits as requested.
