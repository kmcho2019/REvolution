Implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock by an even number NUM_DIV. The counter counts from 0 up to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). Each time the counter reaches the limit, it resets to zero and toggles clk_div, producing a clock with frequency clk/(NUM_DIV). Use a 4-bit counter as required, and handle active-low reset to initialize counter and clk_div to zero.
