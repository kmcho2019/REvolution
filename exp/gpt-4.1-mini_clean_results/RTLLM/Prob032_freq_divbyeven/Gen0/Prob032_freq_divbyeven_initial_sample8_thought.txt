Implement a frequency divider module named freq_diveven that divides the input clock frequency by an even number parameter NUM_DIV. Use a 4-bit counter to count clock cycles. On reset, initialize counter and output clk_div to 0. Increment counter each clock cycle until reaching half of NUM_DIV-1, then reset counter and toggle clk_div. This toggling every half period results in an output frequency of clk / NUM_DIV.
