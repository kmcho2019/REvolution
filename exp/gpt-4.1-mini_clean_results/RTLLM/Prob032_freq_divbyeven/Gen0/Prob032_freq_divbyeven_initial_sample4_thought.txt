Implement a frequency divider module named `freq_diveven` with a parameter `NUM_DIV` representing an even division factor. Use a 4-bit counter to count input clock cycles. On each positive edge of `clk`, increment the counter until it reaches `(NUM_DIV/2 - 1)`. At that point, reset the counter and toggle the output clock `clk_div`. Reset logic asynchronously initializes the counter and output clock when `rst_n` is low.
