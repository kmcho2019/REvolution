Implement a frequency divider module `freq_diveven` with a parameter `NUM_DIV` which must be even. The module uses a 4-bit counter `cnt` to count clock cycles. On every clock's positive edge, if reset is active low, reset `cnt` and `clk_div`. Otherwise, increment `cnt` until it reaches `(NUM_DIV/2 - 1)`, then reset it and toggle `clk_div`. This halves the frequency with respect to `NUM_DIV`, ensuring the output clock frequency is input frequency divided by `NUM_DIV`.
