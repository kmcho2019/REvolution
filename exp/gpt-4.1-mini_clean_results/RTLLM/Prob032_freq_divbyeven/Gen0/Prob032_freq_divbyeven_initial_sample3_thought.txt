Implement a frequency divider module named freq_diveven that divides the input clock frequency by an even number specified by the parameter NUM_DIV. Use a 4-bit counter to count clock cycles up to half the division factor minus one. On each positive clock edge, increment the counter until it reaches the limit, then reset it and toggle the clk_div output. Reset initializes counter and output to zero. Ensure that NUM_DIV is an even number.
