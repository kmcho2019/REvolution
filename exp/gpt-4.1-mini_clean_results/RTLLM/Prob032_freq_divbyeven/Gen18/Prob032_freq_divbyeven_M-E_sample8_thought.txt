I will implement the frequency divider using a compare-based approach with a counter that counts from zero up to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches NUM_DIV-1, it resets to zero and toggles the output clock signal. This ensures that the output clock frequency is input frequency divided by NUM_DIV (even number). To handle even division correctly, the output toggles every NUM_DIV input clock cycles. The counter width will be automatically sized using $clog2(NUM_DIV) to support larger division factors while conserving resources. The reset will be asynchronous active-low to quickly initialize the state, making the design responsive to reset events. This method counts the full division period before toggling, rather than counting half period and toggling, making it straightforward to understand and verify.
