To refactor the frequency divider with the same core functionality but a different structure, I will implement it using a synchronous process for the counter and generate the divided clock output with an always block that toggles the output when the count reaches half the division count.

Instead of combining the toggle and counter increment logic in the same always block, I will split the counter increment and output toggle logic: the counter increments each clock cycle, and when it reaches half of NUM_DIV, the clk_div toggles and the counter resets. 

This approach clearly separates counting and output toggling steps, improving readability and maintainability.

Also, the output clock `clk_div` will be driven by a register toggling at the event, maintaining a 50% duty cycle for even NUM_DIV.

Finally, I will keep the parameter check for evenness and use a localparam for counter width. Reset logic remains synchronous active-low.

This structure uses two always blocks: one for counting and one for output toggling on the specific count, highlighting the design steps distinctly.
