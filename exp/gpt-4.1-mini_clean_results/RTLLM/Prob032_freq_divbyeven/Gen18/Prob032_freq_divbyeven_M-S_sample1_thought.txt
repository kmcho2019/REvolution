Use a simple 4-bit counter that counts from 0 up to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). On reaching that count, toggle the output clock and reset the counter. This way, the output clock toggles every half period of the division number, achieving an even frequency division. The counter and clk_div initialize on reset.
