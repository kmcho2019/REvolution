Use a toggle flip-flop style approach driven by a full-count counter. The counter counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1, and when it reaches NUM_DIV-1, it resets to zero and toggles the output clk_div. This divides the input clock frequency by NUM_DIV. Since NUM_DIV is even, the output clk_div will have a 50% duty cycle. Use a 4-bit counter and parameter checks. This differs from counting only half the division and toggling; here, counting the full division period per toggle provides a clear and straightforward design.
