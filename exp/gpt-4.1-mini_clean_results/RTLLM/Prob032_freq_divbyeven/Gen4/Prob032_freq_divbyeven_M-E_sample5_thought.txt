Instead of counting up to half the division factor and toggling the output clock, this design uses a counter that counts from zero to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches NUM_DIV/2, the output clock is set high; when the counter reaches NUM_DIV-1, the output clock is set low and the counter resets to zero. This approach generates a clock with a 50% duty cycle for even division factors. The output toggles at half of the division period, precisely matching the required frequency division by an even number. The counter width is parameterized to support larger division factors. Active-low synchronous reset is used to initialize both the counter and output clock signal to zero.
