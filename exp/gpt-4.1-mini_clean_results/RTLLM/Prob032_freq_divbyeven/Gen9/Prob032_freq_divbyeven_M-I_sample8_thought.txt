The original design correctly implements a frequency divider for even division factors using a 4-bit counter and toggling the output clock every half of the division period. To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will:

1. Add a compile-time check for NUM_DIV to ensure it is even and within a valid range (>=2 and <=32), which prevents misuse and synthesis issues.
2. Make the counter width dynamically sized based on NUM_DIV, ensuring no wasted bits for smaller division factors.
3. Convert cnt and clk_div to unsigned logic for clarity.
4. Use a localparam for half_div for cleaner code.
5. Use non-blocking assignments and synchronous reset as before.
6. Keep the counter counting from 0 to half_div-1 then toggle clk_div, as it is the simplest approach.

This will reduce area slightly by fitting the counter width, catch errors at compile time, and maintain clarity and reliability for synthesis and simulation.
