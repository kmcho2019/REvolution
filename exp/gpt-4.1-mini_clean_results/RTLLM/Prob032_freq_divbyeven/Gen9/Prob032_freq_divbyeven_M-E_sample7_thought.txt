Implement a frequency divider by using a toggle flip-flop that flips its output after counting NUM_DIV/2 input clock cycles. Use a 4-bit counter to count the input clocks up to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1), then toggle the output and reset the counter. This approach ensures a 50% duty cycle output clock and clean synchronous reset. The counting and toggling is done entirely inside a synchronous process triggered on the positive clock edge, with an asynchronous active-low reset.
