Refactor the frequency divider by separating the counter logic and the toggle logic. Use a synchronous always block to increment the counter and a separate combinational block to determine when to toggle the output clock. The toggling will be triggered when the counter reaches half the division period minus one. This approach clarifies the counter and toggle conditions.
