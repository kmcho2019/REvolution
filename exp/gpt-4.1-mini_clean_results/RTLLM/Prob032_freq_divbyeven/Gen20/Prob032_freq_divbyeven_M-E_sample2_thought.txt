I will implement a frequency divider for even NUM_DIV using a simple 4-bit counter that counts from 0 up to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). On each positive clock edge, the counter increments until reaching this limit, then resets to zero and toggles the output clock. This approach toggles clk_div once every NUM_DIV/2 input clocks, achieving a total division by NUM_DIV with a 50% duty cycle.

This design strictly uses a 4-bit counter, as requested, and checks the parameter at compile time to ensure NUM_DIV is even and no larger than 16 (to fit in 4 bits). The logic is clean, with a single toggle point synchronized with counter reset, ensuring proper waveform generation without double toggling.

Reset is synchronous active-low; on reset, both counter and clk_div initialize to zero.
