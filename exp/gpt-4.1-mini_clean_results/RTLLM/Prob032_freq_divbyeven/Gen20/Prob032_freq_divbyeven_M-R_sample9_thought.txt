To refactor the frequency divider, I will implement a synchronous counter that increments on each positive clock edge and resets when it reaches NUM_DIV - 1. Instead of toggling the output inside the counting logic conditionally, I will generate the divided clock output by comparing the counter value with half of NUM_DIV. The divided clock output will be high when the counter is less than half of NUM_DIV, and low otherwise, thereby naturally generating a 50% duty cycle without needing to toggle a flip-flop explicitly. This approach avoids asynchronous toggling and makes the output a combinational function of the counter, which is updated synchronously. The counter and output are reset asynchronously with the active-low reset. This structure is clear, synthesizable, and maintains the original functionality of dividing the input clock frequency by an even number.
