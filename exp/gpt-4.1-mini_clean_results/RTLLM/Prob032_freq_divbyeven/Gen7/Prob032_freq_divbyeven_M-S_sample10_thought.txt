Implement a simple frequency divider that toggles the output clock every NUM_DIV/2 input clock cycles. Use a 4-bit counter to count from 0 up to NUM_DIV/2 - 1, then toggle clk_div and reset the counter. This produces an output clock frequency divided by an even number NUM_DIV with a 50% duty cycle. Asynchronous active-low reset initializes the counter and output.
