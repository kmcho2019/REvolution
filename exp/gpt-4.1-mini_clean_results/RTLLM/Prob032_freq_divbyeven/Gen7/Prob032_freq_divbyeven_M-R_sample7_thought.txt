To refactor the frequency divider, I will implement a synchronous design that uses a combinational signal for the toggle condition and separate always blocks for counting and output updating. Instead of toggling `clk_div` inside the same block as the counter, I will generate a pulse signal `toggle_pulse` when the counter reaches `HALF_DIV-1`, and then use this pulse to toggle the output register in a separate always block on the clock edge. This approach separates counter logic from output update logic for better clarity. The parameter check for evenness remains. Also, I will use a standard synchronous reset for all registers.
