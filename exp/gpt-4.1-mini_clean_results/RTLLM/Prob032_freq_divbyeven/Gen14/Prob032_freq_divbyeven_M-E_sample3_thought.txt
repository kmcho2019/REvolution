Instead of counting up to half the division factor and toggling the output clock, this design will use a modulo counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and toggles the output clock exactly when the counter rolls over. 

Since NUM_DIV is even, toggling the output at each full count cycle divides the frequency by NUM_DIV. The counter increments on every clock edge; when it reaches NUM_DIV-1, it resets to zero and toggles the output clock. This approach is simpler conceptually and avoids off-by-one errors related to half periods.

The counter width is parameterized to accommodate larger division factors. On reset, the output clock and counter are initialized to zero.

This solution ensures precise timing and clean transitions by toggling only on complete count cycles.
