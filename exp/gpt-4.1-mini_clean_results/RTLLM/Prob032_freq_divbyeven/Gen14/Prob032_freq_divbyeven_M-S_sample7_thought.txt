Implement a frequency divider with a 4-bit counter and parameterized even division factor NUM_DIV. On reset, clear the counter and output. On each clock edge, increment the counter until it reaches NUM_DIV - 1, then reset it and toggle the output clock. The toggle occurs exactly at half of NUM_DIV cycles to ensure proper frequency division by an even number. The code keeps minimal logic, assumes NUM_DIV is even and >= 2, and uses a fixed 4-bit counter since the problem specifies it.
