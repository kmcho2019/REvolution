Instead of counting up to half the division factor and toggling the output, use a full-range counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches NUM_DIV-1, reset it to zero and toggle the output clock. This simplifies the counting logic and ensures a 50% duty cycle for any even NUM_DIV. A 4-bit counter is used as specified. The toggling on overflow divides the input clock frequency by the even number NUM_DIV.
