I will implement the frequency divider by counting up to half the division factor (NUM_DIV/2 - 1) before toggling the output clock. This way, the output clock's high and low phases are equal in length, resulting in a 50% duty cycle. The counter counts from 0 to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). Each time it reaches that value, it resets to zero and toggles the output clock. The counter is a 4-bit register as specified. The reset (rst_n) is synchronous active low for better timing and synthesis friendliness. This approach splits the division period evenly into two halves, toggling the output at each half-cycle boundary, which is especially suitable for even division factors.
