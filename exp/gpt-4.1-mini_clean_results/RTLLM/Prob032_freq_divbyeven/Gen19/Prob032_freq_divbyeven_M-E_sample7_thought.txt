To design a frequency divider by an even number, I'll create a counter that counts up to NUM_DIV/2 - 1 and toggles the output clock signal each time the count reaches this value. This way, the output clock period corresponds to NUM_DIV input clock cycles. The output toggles every half period, generating a divided clock with a 50% duty cycle. The counter width will be dynamically calculated from NUM_DIV/2 to support larger division values. A synchronous active-low reset initializes the counter and output. Additionally, I'll include a compile-time assertion to ensure NUM_DIV is even, preventing misconfiguration.
