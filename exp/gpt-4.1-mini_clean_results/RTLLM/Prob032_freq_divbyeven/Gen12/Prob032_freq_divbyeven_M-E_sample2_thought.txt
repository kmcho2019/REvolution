Implement the frequency divider using two always blocks: one for counting clock cycles on the positive edge of clk, and another one for toggling the output clk_div on the positive edge of clk when the count reaches half of NUM_DIV. The counter will count from 0 up to (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). When it reaches this value, it resets and toggles clk_div. The reset is synchronous and active low. This separation of counting and toggling logic provides clarity and easy debugging.
