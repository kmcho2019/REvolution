The problem stems from the timing of toggling `clk_div` exactly when the counter reaches `NUM_DIV/2 - 1`. This causes `clk_div` to toggle too early by one clock cycle, resulting in an off-by-one phase error in the divided clock.

To fix this, we should let the counter count from 0 up to `(NUM_DIV/2) - 1` inclusively, and only toggle `clk_div` and reset the counter on the next clock cycle after the full count has been completed.

This means:
- When `cnt == (NUM_DIV/2) - 1`, we increment the counter by 1 to reach `NUM_DIV/2`.
- When `cnt == NUM_DIV/2`, we reset `cnt` to zero and toggle `clk_div`.

This change effectively makes the counter count from 0 to `(NUM_DIV/2)` inclusive, with the toggle happening after the full half period cycles, aligning the output clock phase with the input.

Additionally, we add a synthesis-time assertion to check that `NUM_DIV` is even.

The 4-bit counter is maintained as per the original spec, limiting `NUM_DIV` to at most 32.

This approach ensures the divided clock frequency is input clock frequency divided by `NUM_DIV` (an even number), with a correct 50% duty cycle and correct timing.
