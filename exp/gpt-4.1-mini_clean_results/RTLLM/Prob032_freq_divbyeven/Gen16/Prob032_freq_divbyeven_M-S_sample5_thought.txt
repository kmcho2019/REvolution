To simplify the design, I will implement the frequency divider with a straightforward sequential process that increments a 4-bit counter on every positive edge of the clock. When the counter reaches half the division factor minus one, the output clock toggles, and the counter resets at the full division count minus one. This approach avoids separate next-state logic by embedding all logic in the sequential block, which is sufficient for this simple frequency divider and easier to understand. Parameter checks are retained for safety.
