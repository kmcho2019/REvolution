To refactor the frequency divider with the same functionality, I will separate the counter and output clock toggling into two sequential always blocks for clarity and modularity:

1. The first always block increments the counter synchronously with reset, counting from 0 up to NUM_DIV/2 - 1.
2. The second always block toggles the output clock `clk_div` whenever the counter hits the terminal count.

This approach decouples the counting logic from the output generation, making the design clearer and easier to modify. Also, instead of toggling inside the counter block, toggling happens in a dedicated block triggered on the terminal count condition.

I will keep the compile-time assertion and parameterization for even-number division factor intact.

This design keeps synchronous reset and guarantees output clock toggling exactly every NUM_DIV/2 input clock cycles, thereby dividing the frequency by an even number as required.
