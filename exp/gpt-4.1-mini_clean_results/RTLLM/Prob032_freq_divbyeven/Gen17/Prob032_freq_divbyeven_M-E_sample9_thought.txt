Implement a simple frequency divider by counting clock cycles up to half the desired division factor (NUM_DIV/2). The counter increments each clock cycle and when it reaches the limit, it toggles the output clk_div and resets the counter to zero. This toggling every half cycle produces a divided clock frequency of clk / NUM_DIV. The design includes an active-low reset that initializes both counter and output. Using a straightforward linear counter instead of up/down counting simplifies the logic while maintaining 50% duty cycle and ensures the counter is 4-bit wide, sufficient to handle typical even division factors up to 32.
