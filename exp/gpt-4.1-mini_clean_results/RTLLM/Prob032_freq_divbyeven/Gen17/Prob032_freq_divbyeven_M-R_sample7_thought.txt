I will refactor the frequency divider to implement the counter and toggle logic in a single always block for simplicity, and use a combinational assign statement to generate the divided clock output from a register toggled on the counter reaching the toggle threshold. The counter will count from 0 up to half the division factor minus one, then toggle the output clock and reset the counter. This approach is slightly different by using the counter's half cycle length and toggling on reaching that count rather than counting up to the full NUM_DIV-1, which reduces counter size and keeps the division ratio consistent. Reset logic will remain synchronous and active low.
