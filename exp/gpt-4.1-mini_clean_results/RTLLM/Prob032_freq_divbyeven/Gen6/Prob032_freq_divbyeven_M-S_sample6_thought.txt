I will simplify the frequency divider by combining the counter and clk_div toggle logic into a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk and asynchronous active-low reset. The counter will count from 0 to HALF_DIV-1 (NUM_DIV/2 - 1). When the counter reaches its max value, it resets to 0 and toggles the clk_div output. This single-process approach reduces complexity while maintaining functionality and ensures the output frequency is divided by the even NUM_DIV parameter. I will still include a compile-time check to ensure NUM_DIV is even.
