I will implement the frequency divider by using a single 4-bit counter and generate the divided clock output directly based on the count value. Specifically, the counter increments on every rising edge of the input clock, resetting after reaching NUM_DIV - 1. The output clock toggles its state whenever the counter value equals half of NUM_DIV (i.e., on the midpoint of the counting period). This creates a divided clock with a 50% duty cycle. This approach is slightly different from toggling only at the terminal count and provides a clean and simple architecture that ensures the output toggles exactly once per half period, and is easy to verify and synthesize. The counter and output registers reset when the active-low reset signal is asserted.
