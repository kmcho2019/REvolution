To improve the design for better power efficiency without compromising functionality, add an enable signal that activates counting and toggling only when freq is non-zero. This prevents unnecessary counter toggling and switching activity when freq is zero, which would otherwise cause continuous counting and toggling at max speed. Also, register the freq input to reduce glitches and improve timing stability. The logic of counting and toggling remains the same but gated by the enable signal derived from the registered freq. This approach reduces dynamic power consumption and slightly improves timing and robustness.
