Use a two-stage counter approach where one 8-bit counter increments every clock cycle until it reaches the input freq value, at which point a toggle request is generated. A second 1-bit register captures this toggle request and toggles the wave_out signal. This separates counting and toggling logic, potentially improving timing by minimizing combinational load in the critical path. Also, treat freq==0 as a special case to hold wave_out low to avoid oscillation.
