Implement the square wave generator by using the counter as a down counter from freq to 0, which naturally triggers a toggle when it underflows. Initialize the counter to freq on every toggle, decrement it each clock cycle, and toggle wave_out when the counter reaches zero. When freq is zero, hold wave_out steady and reset the counter to zero. This approach avoids the comparator with subtraction and uses a zero detection, which is simpler hardware-wise. This countdown style is more intuitive and can lead to slightly simpler combinational logic.
