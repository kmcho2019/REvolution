To optimize the design for better timing and potentially power, precompute the terminal count value (freq_minus_1) in a register to avoid subtraction in the comparison during each clock cycle. This reduces combinational delay in the critical path. Also, add handling for freq=0 to prevent unintended behavior (since freq=0 would cause the terminal count to be 255, which is undesired). With freq=0, we can disable toggling to keep wave_out steady. This approach maintains simplicity but improves timing by reducing subtraction in the comparator and adds functional robustness.
