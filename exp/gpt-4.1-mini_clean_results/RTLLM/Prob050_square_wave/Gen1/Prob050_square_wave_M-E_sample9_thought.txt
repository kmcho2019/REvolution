Implement a square wave generator by counting up to half the period specified by freq, then toggling the output and resetting the counter. This approach divides the toggling period into two phases for better clarity. To avoid subtracting freq-1, compare count with a precomputed half_period = (freq >> 1), allowing toggling at every half period for a 50% duty cycle. The counter resets when it reaches half_period, and wave_out toggles accordingly, creating a square wave with frequency inversely proportional to freq.
