To optimize the square_wave module for better power efficiency and timing while maintaining correct functionality, I will implement an enable signal for counting only when freq is nonzero, reducing unnecessary toggling of the counter when freq=0. This decreases dynamic power consumption by disabling the counter when no output frequency is requested.

Additionally, I will register the wave_out output on the rising edge of clk, synchronizing the output to reduce glitches and provide a stable waveform with less combinational logic on the output path, which can improve timing.

To further reduce logic complexity, instead of doing a full 8-bit comparator for count < (freq >> 1), I'll compute half_freq = freq >> 1 in a register to avoid repeated combinational shifting, simplifying the comparison logic.

Overall, these changes trade a small increase in registers (for output and half_freq) for improved timing, glitch reduction, and power saving by disabling counting when freq=0.

No reset on wave_out is needed if we accept initial output as zero after reset.

This approach improves power by gating counting, enhances timing by registering wave_out, and keeps area modest with only a couple of added registers.
