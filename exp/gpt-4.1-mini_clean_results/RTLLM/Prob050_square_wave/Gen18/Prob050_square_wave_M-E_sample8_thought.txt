Implement the square wave generator by using a free-running 8-bit counter that increments every clock cycle and compares it with the frequency input. When the counter reaches the frequency value, it toggles the output and resets the counter. To avoid ambiguity or division by zero, if freq is zero, the output remains constant without toggling. This approach avoids the need to check for freq-1, simplifying comparison to equality with freq. The toggling is edge-aligned with freq counts, generating a square wave with period proportional to freq*2. This architecture is simple and clear, with a direct comparator and synchronous reset for stable initialization.
