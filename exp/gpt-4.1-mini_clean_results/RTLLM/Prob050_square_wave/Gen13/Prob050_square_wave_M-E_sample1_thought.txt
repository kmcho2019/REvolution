Instead of counting up to freq-1, this design uses a down-counter starting from freq, decrementing each clock cycle until zero. When zero is reached, the counter reloads to freq and toggles the output wave_out. This approach can simplify control logic since the toggle occurs precisely at zero, eliminating the need to compare count to freq-1. Additionally, when freq=0, the output wave_out remains constant (no toggling) to avoid invalid operation. This design has no reset input as it wasn't requested, initializing wave_out and count at zero by default. The counting and toggling logic is straightforward, which should lead to a clean and efficient implementation.
