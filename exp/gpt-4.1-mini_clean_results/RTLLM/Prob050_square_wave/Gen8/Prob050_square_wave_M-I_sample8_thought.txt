To optimize for better PPA while keeping the design simple, we can precompute the terminal count (freq - 1) outside the always block to reduce combinational logic in the critical path. Additionally, when freq is zero, we hold count and wave_out stable to minimize toggling and power. To further reduce power, introduce a clock enable signal that only allows the counter to count when freq is nonzero and count hasn't reached terminal count, effectively gating unnecessary counting logic. This approach reduces switching activity without adding complex clock gating circuitry. The design remains minimal and synchronous for easy synthesis.
