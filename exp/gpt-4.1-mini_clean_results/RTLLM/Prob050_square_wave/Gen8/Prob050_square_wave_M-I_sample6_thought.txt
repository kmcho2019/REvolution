The original design is robust and power-aware by gating counting when freq=0, and includes a synchronous reset. To optimize PPA further:

1. Timing: Replace (count == freq) comparison with (count == freq_minus_one) where freq_minus_one is freq-1. This reduces combinational delay as the count increments from 0 to freq_minus_one before toggling. Comparing with freq_minus_one is simpler since count only needs to reach freq-1, avoiding the off-by-one comparator complexity.

2. Power: Keep gating logic by only counting and toggling when freq is non-zero. Further power saving via clock gating is usually tool-dependent; the enable signal suffices here.

3. Area: Introducing freq_minus_one register adds a small combinational subtraction, but synthesis can optimize this easily. It likely improves critical path delay due to simpler equality check.

4. Clarity: Rename signals for better readability.

5. Reset: Maintain synchronous active-low reset for stable initialization.

Overall, the main optimization is to precompute freq_minus_one, and use (count == freq_minus_one) as the toggle condition, reducing critical path delay and improving max frequency achievable without impacting area or power notably.
