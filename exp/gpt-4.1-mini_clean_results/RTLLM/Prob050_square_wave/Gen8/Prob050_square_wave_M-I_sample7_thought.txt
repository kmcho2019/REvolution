To optimize the square_wave generator for better PPA, we focus on power and performance without increasing area or complexity:

1. Clock gating: Instead of always counting and toggling even when freq=0, we effectively gate counting and toggling by freezing count and holding wave_out steady, which reduces switching activity and dynamic power.

2. Register freq_minus_one: By registering freq_minus_one, we can reduce combinational path delay and glitches on the comparator, potentially improving max frequency and reducing power.

3. Simplify logic: Remove the wire freq_minus_one combinational assignment, replacing it with a registered version that updates only when freq changes or at each clock. This may trade off some latency but reduces glitching and power.

4. Keep non-blocking assignments and synchronous active-low reset for clarity and synthesis friendliness.

5. Since freq is 8 bits, a registered subtractor or a small combinational subtractor is inexpensive and benefits timing.

Overall, we trade off a small one-cycle latency in freq_minus_one update for improved timing and lower glitch power on count comparator. The clock gating behavior is maintained by freezing count and wave_out when freq=0.

This approach balances improvements in timing, power, and area with minimal added logic.
