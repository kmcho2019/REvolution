To improve the existing design for better PPA, I will eliminate the subtraction operation inside the always block by precomputing a threshold value (freq_threshold = freq - 1) in a separate combinational always block. This reduces combinational logic on the critical path during counting. Also, to reduce dynamic power consumption, I will freeze the counter and wave_out when freq is zero by gating counting and toggling only when freq is non-zero. The reset input will be included as active-low synchronous reset for reliable initialization. This approach keeps the design simple but improves timing by reducing combinational complexity and saves power by preventing unnecessary toggling.
