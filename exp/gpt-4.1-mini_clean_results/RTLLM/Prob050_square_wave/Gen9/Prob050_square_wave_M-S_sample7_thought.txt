Implement a simple synchronous process triggered on the rising clock edge without reset or additional control signals to keep the design minimal. Use a single 8-bit counter that increments each clock cycle until it reaches freq-1, then reset it and toggle the output wave. When freq=0, the output will not toggle because the counter never reaches freq-1 (255 wraps around), effectively freezing the output. This removes combinational wires and extra conditions, simplifying the design while maintaining correct functionality and behavior.
