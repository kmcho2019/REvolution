To improve the original design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will precompute the threshold value (freq - 1) outside the always block to reduce combinational subtraction inside the clocked process. I will add a signal to freeze counting and toggling when freq is zero to reduce unnecessary toggling and switching activity, lowering power. Additionally, I will use a one-hot style comparator by directly comparing count to the threshold rather than doing subtraction in the always block. This also helps synthesis to optimize. Finally, I will initialize count and wave_out together on reset for clarity, and keep the design minimal with only one always block, balancing performance and power efficiently.
