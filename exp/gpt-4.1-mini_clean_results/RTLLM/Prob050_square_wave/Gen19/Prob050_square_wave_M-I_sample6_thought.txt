To optimize the design for better PPA (performance, power, area), move the wave_out update from combinational logic to a synchronous process, toggling wave_out only when the counter reaches freq - 1. This reduces combinational delays and glitches on wave_out output and simplifies timing. Also, add an enable gating on counter increment to stop counting and toggling when freq is zero, reducing unnecessary switching activity and dynamic power consumption. This preserves functionality exactly but improves timing and power characteristics. The design uses only one register for count and one for wave_out, keeping area minimal.
