Implement the square wave generator using a simple counter that counts up to half of the period corresponding to the input freq, then toggles the output. Since toggling the wave_out every (freq) clock cycles produces a full period of 2*freq clock cycles, the output frequency is effectively half the input clock frequency divided by freq. To realize this, the counter counts up to freq-1, toggles wave_out, then resets. This approach keeps logic simple with a direct comparator for count == freq-1. To handle zero freq (which could cause no toggling or divide by zero), the output stays constant when freq=0. No reset input is included to keep the module minimal, and non-blocking assignments are used for synthesis-friendly RTL.
