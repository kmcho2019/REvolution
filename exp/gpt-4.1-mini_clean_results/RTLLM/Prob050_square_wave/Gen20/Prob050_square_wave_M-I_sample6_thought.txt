To optimize the original design for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will reduce unnecessary counter toggling and minimize combinational logic delays. 

Improvement strategy:
1. Introduce an enable signal that activates counting and toggling only when freq is non-zero to reduce dynamic power consumption.
2. Instead of continuously incrementing a free-running counter, count up to freq - 1 and then toggle the output, resetting the counter—this reduces toggling frequency and makes the waveform period proportional to freq.
3. Register the output wave_out synchronously, reducing glitches and improving timing.
4. Use synchronous reset to initialize registers.

This approach reduces switching activities, removes a large comparator in combinational logic, and simplifies control by using a counter bounded by freq, yielding improved power efficiency and potentially higher maximum frequency.

Overall, the output toggles at clk cycles divided by freq, so a higher freq value results in a slower output wave toggle rate, as desired.
