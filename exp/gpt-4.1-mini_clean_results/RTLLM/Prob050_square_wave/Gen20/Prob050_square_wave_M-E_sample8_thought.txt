Instead of counting continuously up to freq and then toggling wave_out, use a down-counter that counts from freq down to zero. On reaching zero, toggle wave_out and reload the counter with freq. This approach separates the counter and toggling logic, making timing and control clearer. Also, incorporate a synchronous reset to initialize both count and wave_out to zero. This method reduces the number of comparisons per cycle to just a zero-check, which is simpler and can improve timing. The output wave_out is registered and toggles synchronously, avoiding glitches. The frequency control still results in lower toggle rates for larger freq values.
