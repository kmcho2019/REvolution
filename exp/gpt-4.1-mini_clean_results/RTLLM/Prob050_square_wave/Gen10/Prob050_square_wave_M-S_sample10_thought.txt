Implement a straightforward square wave generator toggling wave_out each time count reaches freq-1. The count increments every clock cycle and resets to zero when it reaches freq-1. No clock enable or reset signals are needed as per original spec, simplifying the design. When freq is zero, no toggle occurs since count will never reach freq-1, effectively holding wave_out steady. The design uses a simple equality comparison (count == freq - 1) to decide toggling and counter reset.
