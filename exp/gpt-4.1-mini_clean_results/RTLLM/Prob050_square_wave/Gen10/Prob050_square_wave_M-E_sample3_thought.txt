Instead of directly counting up to freq and toggling on equality, use a down-counter initialized by freq and count down to zero. On reaching zero, toggle the output and reload the counter. This approach inherently uses a zero detection which is simpler than equality comparison, possibly reducing combinational delay and simplifying control logic.

Key ideas:
1. When freq is zero, hold wave_out stable and counter idle.
2. Use a synchronous reset to initialize counter and output.
3. Use a down-counter approach:
   - Load counter with freq on toggle event.
   - Decrement counter each clock cycle.
   - When counter reaches zero, toggle wave_out.
4. This reduces comparator complexity to zero detection.
5. The toggling frequency is inversely proportional to freq as required.

This approach offers simpler control and potentially better timing by using a zero-detection instead of equality comparison.
