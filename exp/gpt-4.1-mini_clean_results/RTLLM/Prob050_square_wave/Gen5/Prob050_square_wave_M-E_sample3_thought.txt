Use an up-counter that counts from 0 to freq/2 - 1, toggling the output wave_out at each terminal count. This halves the counting period to create the correct half-cycle duration of the square wave, giving a full period of freq cycles per toggle cycle. To handle freq=0 gracefully (avoid divide-by-zero), output wave_out will hold its value steady. The counter resets and increments on each clock cycle when freq is non-zero. This approach directly maps freq to the full period length of the wave, providing precise frequency control and allowing simple comparison logic. The half-cycle counting also ensures a 50% duty cycle output.
