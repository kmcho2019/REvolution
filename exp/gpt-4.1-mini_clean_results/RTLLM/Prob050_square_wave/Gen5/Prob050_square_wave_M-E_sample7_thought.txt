Instead of a simple counter that counts up to freq-1 and toggles, implement a down-counter that loads freq into the counter on each toggle event and counts down each clock cycle. When the counter reaches zero, toggle the wave_out and reload the counter with freq again. This approach naturally implements a variable period based on freq. Also, handle the special case of freq=0 by holding wave_out low and disabling counting, which avoids glitches and undefined behavior. This design has a clean toggle event with synchronous reload and ensures predictable output frequency. It also uses only an 8-bit comparator (counter==0), which is simple and efficient.
