The original design uses a countdown counter and toggles wave_out when the count reaches zero. To improve PPA, especially power and performance, I will switch to an up-counter counting from 0 to freq-1. This can be slightly faster for synthesis tools, as increment and comparison are generally simpler than decrement and zero detection. Additionally, to reduce switching power, I will gate the counter increment operation so that when freq is zero, the counter and wave_out do not toggle or change. This avoids unnecessary toggling in the counter when freq is zero.

Further, I will initialize wave_out to zero to have a known startup state.

This design maintains the same behavior: wave_out toggles every 'freq' clk cycles, so frequency is inversely proportional to freq.

In summary, the improvements are:
- Use an incrementing counter with comparison to freq-1.
- Gate the counting and toggling to disable activity when freq=0.
- Initialize wave_out to zero.

This should improve power by minimizing switching when freq=0 and may improve performance due to simpler comparison logic.
