To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will implement clock enable gating using the enable signal to stop unnecessary counting when freq=0, which can help reduce dynamic power. Also, I will slightly simplify the toggle condition by comparing count directly to freq minus one without subtracting each cycle by precomputing freq_minus_one combinationally. This can help timing by avoiding the subtract operation inside the comparator. The synchronous reset is kept for clear startup. Finally, I will remove the extra combinational toggle_flag signal and fold logic into one always block for minimal logic and reduced routing, which benefits area and timing. The output toggling occurs only on counter wrap, minimizing toggling and improving power.
