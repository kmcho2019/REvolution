Implement a simple square wave generator without reset and clock enable. Use an 8-bit counter that increments every clock cycle. When the counter reaches freq-1, reset it and toggle the output wave_out. This straightforward comparator approach (count == freq - 1) reduces complexity and area by avoiding 9-bit adders or clock enable logic, while maintaining the intended functionality. No toggling occurs if freq is 0, since count will never reach freq-1, effectively holding the output steady. This simplification meets the original spec with minimal logic.
