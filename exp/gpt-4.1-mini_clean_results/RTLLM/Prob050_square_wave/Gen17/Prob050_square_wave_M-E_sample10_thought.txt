Instead of toggling the output when a counter reaches (freq-1), use a down-counter that reloads from freq on each zero, producing a toggle every time it counts down to zero. This removes the equality comparison and replaces it with a simple zero detection, which can be slightly faster and more power efficient.

Also, to avoid toggling at freq=0 (which is invalid), clamp freq to minimum 1 internally.

The design:
- Use a countdown register 'count' that initializes to freq.
- On each clk, decrement count.
- When count reaches zero, reload count to freq and toggle wave_out.
- Clamp freq input to min 1 to avoid zero or glitch conditions.

This architecture simplifies the combinational logic (only zero detection), keeps counting logic simple, and produces the desired frequency controlled square wave output.
