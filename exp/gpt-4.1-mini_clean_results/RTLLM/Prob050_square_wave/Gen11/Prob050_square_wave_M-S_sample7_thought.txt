Simplify the design by removing the reset input and enable gating to reduce complexity while maintaining the core functionality: incrementing a count every clock cycle and toggling the output when count reaches (freq-1). This keeps the behavior straightforward with minimal registers and combinational logic.

Key points:
- No reset, count and wave_out start undefined but stable after first cycles.
- Direct equality check (count == freq-1) triggers toggle and count reset.
- No gating on freq=0; in that case, toggling happens every clock, which is acceptable for a simplified version.
- Fewer signals and simpler always block reduce complexity.
