Implement a two-stage counter approach where the input frequency value (freq) represents the half-period count of the output square wave. Use a single counter that counts up to freq and resets, toggling the wave_out at every terminal count.

To handle the edge case when freq is zero (meaning infinite frequency or no toggling), keep wave_out steady and counter stopped.

Use a fully synchronous design with active-high reset for clarity. Avoid subtracting (freq - 1) by counting from zero up to freq directly.

This approach simplifies the comparator logic, reduces subtraction operations, and allows immediate toggling at counter overflow, potentially easing timing.

By toggling at the terminal count (counter == freq), the wave_out frequency is controlled by twice the count cycle, since toggling happens every full count, producing the desired square wave.

Use non-blocking assignments with a single always block for sequential logic. Combinational logic will be minimized for better synthesis results.

The design keeps count register, wave_out register, input clk and reset, and input freq as specified.
