To improve the original design's performance and power characteristics, reduce the comparator complexity by using a direct counter wrap-around at freq instead of comparing count == freq-1. Also, gate the counter increment when freq is zero or one since the wave_out either shouldn't toggle or toggle every cycle (freq=1 means toggling every clock).
