Implement the square wave generator using a decrementing counter that loads the freq value when it reaches zero. This approach eliminates the need for subtracting 1 from freq during comparison. The counter counts down from freq to zero, and when it reaches zero, wave_out toggles and the counter reloads with freq again. If freq is zero, wave_out is held steady and counter is kept at zero to prevent toggling. This design reduces comparator complexity and naturally handles freq=0 as a special case.
