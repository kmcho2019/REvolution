To optimize the existing square wave generator for better PPA (Timing, Power, Area), we can implement clock enable logic to prevent counter and toggle logic activity when freq=0 or when freq=1. This reduces unnecessary toggling and switching activity, lowering power consumption. Also, by replacing the equality comparator (count == freq) with a carry-out from an adder, we can reduce comparator delay, enhancing timing. Adding synchronous reset input allows proper initialization and better control in a system. The design will use a clock enable signal that enables counting and toggling only when freq > 1, otherwise holding the output steady. This reduces switching in low frequency or hold states, thus optimizing power. The comparator optimization with carry-out can slightly improve timing by reducing critical path delays.
