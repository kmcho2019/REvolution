Implement the square wave generator using a countdown approach instead of counting up to freq. Initialize the count register with freq value and decrement it every clock cycle. When the count reaches zero, reset it back to freq and toggle the wave_out signal.

This approach:
- Simplifies the comparator to a zero check, which is typically simpler and faster in hardware than equality with an arbitrary value.
- Avoids subtracting one from freq or complex comparator logic.
- Handles freq=0 by bypassing toggling and keeping wave_out stable.
- Uses synchronous reset to initialize the registers.
- Keeps the code clean and concise with minimal logic for toggling.

This architecture may offer improved timing and power characteristics due to simpler comparison and reduced switching when freq=0.
