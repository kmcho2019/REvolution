Use an up-counter that counts from 0 up to freq/2 - 1 and toggles wave_out each time this count completes. This leverages the fact that a full square wave period equals two toggle intervals. This means wave_out toggles every freq/2 clock cycles, producing the desired frequency. The implementation handles odd freq values by using integer division, resulting in slight frequency approximation but simplifying logic. When freq is 0 or 1, the output is held constant to avoid fast toggling or division by zero. This approach avoids subtracting 1 explicitly on comparisons and uses an up-counter which is often simpler for synthesis. It also decouples the toggle event from freq directly, making the toggle interval explicit and intuitive.
