Use a half-period counting approach to generate the square wave with a cleaner toggle condition.

Instead of counting to freq and toggling at freq, count to freq/2 and toggle the output each time, producing a full period in two toggles. This naturally yields a frequency output inversely proportional to freq.

Advantages:
- The toggle condition becomes count == half_period, where half_period = (freq >> 1).
- When freq is zero, output holds steady.
- This approach handles odd freq values gracefully by truncation.
- No need for complicated comparators; just simple increment and equality check.
- Implement synchronous reset to initialize all registers.
- The output frequency will be roughly clk / freq.

This approach shifts complexity away from counting to full freq cycles and into half cycles toggling, simplifying timing and logic.
