Use an up-counting counter from 0 to freq-1 to determine when to toggle wave_out. When count reaches freq-1, toggle wave_out and reset count to zero. If freq is zero, hold the output steady and the counter at zero to avoid undefined behavior.

This approach:
- Implements straightforward up-counting logic.
- Uses a single comparator against freq-1.
- Does not require a reset input (simpler interface).
- Treats freq=0 as a special case to freeze output.
- Minimizes logic and is easy to understand and synthesize.
