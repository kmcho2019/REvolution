Instead of incrementing a counter up to freq-1 to toggle the output, use a down counter that reloads freq value each time it reaches zero. This approach naturally expresses the countdown to toggle and avoids the subtraction operation when comparing. The counter counts down from freq to zero and toggles wave_out upon reaching zero, then reloads freq again. This method is intuitive and can reduce comparator complexity (just check for zero).

Also, handle the case when freq is zero by holding wave_out constant and not counting. This prevents unwanted toggles and keeps output stable.

The design uses a single always block with synchronous reset, and non-blocking assignments. The input ports remain clk, freq, and wave_out output.

This method can simplify synthesis by eliminating freq-1 calculation, thus reducing logic. It may also improve timing by using a zero detection instead of comparison. The countdown approach aligns well with toggling frequency control and is functionally equivalent to counting up but conceptually cleaner.
