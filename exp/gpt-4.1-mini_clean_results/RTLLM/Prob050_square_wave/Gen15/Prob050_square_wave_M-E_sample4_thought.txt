Use an up-counter that counts from 0 up to freq-1 and then resets to 0, toggling wave_out on each rollover. When freq is zero, keep wave_out stable by preventing the counter from counting. This approach uses a simple equality comparison to detect when to toggle and reset the counter. The design avoids countdown logic, relying on straightforward increment and comparison, which may be more intuitive for some synthesis tools. No reset input is used; wave_out and count initialize to zero on power-up (using Verilog initial blocks for simulation). The frequency control logic is combinational and synchronous, with minimal combinational path delay.
