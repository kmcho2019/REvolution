Implement the square wave generator using a down-counter approach to directly count down from freq to zero. When the counter reaches zero, toggle wave_out and reload the counter with freq.

This method simplifies the comparison logic by checking for zero instead of matching count to freq-1, which might reduce combinational logic and improve timing.

Also, handle the special case of freq=0 by holding wave_out stable and not counting, preventing toggling and counter underflow.

Use non-blocking assignments for sequential logic.

No reset input is included, assuming the module powers up with wave_out=0 and count=0.

This design offers an alternative counting method that can be clearer to synthesize as a decrementing counter with reload and toggle on zero, potentially improving timing and area.
