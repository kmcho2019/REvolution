To improve PPA and correctness, I will refine the counting condition as per the original specification: toggle and reset the counter when count reaches (freq - 1), not freq. This reduces comparator complexity and aligns with problem description, potentially improving timing (WNS).

Also, I will introduce a clock enable signal to gate the counting and toggling only when freq != 0, reducing unnecessary toggling and switching activity, thus lowering dynamic power.

The design will remain fully synchronous with active-high reset. Non-blocking assignments and a single always block are used for clarity and synthesis friendliness.

By counting from 0 to freq-1 and toggling at that point, the effective period is freq * 2 clk cycles, and frequency control behaves as expected.

This change reduces one comparator width bit from 'count == freq' (9-bit compare if freq extended) to 'count == freq-1' (8-bit), and gating the count when freq==0 avoids spurious toggling and switching in counter and output.

These improvements yield better timing and power efficiency, preserving area since no additional registers are added, just combinational gating logic.
