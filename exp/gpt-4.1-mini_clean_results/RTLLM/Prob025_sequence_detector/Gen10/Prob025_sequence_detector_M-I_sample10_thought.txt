To optimize the original one-hot FSM for better power, area, and timing, I will switch to a binary-encoded FSM using a 3-bit state variable instead of one-hot encoding with 5 flip-flops. This reduces flip-flop count and switching activity. The next state logic will be implemented with concise combinational logic, minimizing gate complexity. The output sequence_detected will be asserted synchronously and only for one clock cycle immediately after the sequence 1001 is detected. The asynchronous reset is kept active-low and synchronous reset is used for state and output for consistency and easier timing closure. This strategy reduces area and power while improving timing without changing functional behavior.
