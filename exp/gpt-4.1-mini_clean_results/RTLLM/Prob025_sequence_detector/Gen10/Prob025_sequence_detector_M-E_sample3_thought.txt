This design implements the sequence detector as a Moore FSM with explicit states encoded as parameters, progressing through states IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4 to detect the 4-bit sequence "1001". The FSM transitions on each rising clock edge based on the current state and input bit, carefully handling overlapping sequences by considering partial matches on transitions.

The output `sequence_detected` is asserted high whenever the FSM reaches S4, indicating the full sequence "1001" has been detected. The FSM resets synchronously on `reset_n` being low, ensuring deterministic initialization.

This approach demonstrates a classical FSM-based design with clear state definitions, transition logic, and output signaling, making the design easy to understand and extend if needed. The FSM allows for precise control of the detection process and inherently handles sequence overlaps through explicit state transitions.
