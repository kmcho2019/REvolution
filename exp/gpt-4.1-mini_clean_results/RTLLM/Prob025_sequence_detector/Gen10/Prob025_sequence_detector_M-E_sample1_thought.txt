I will implement the sequence detector using a Mealy FSM approach where the output `sequence_detected` is asserted combinationally based on both the current state and the input `data_in`. The FSM will be encoded with one-hot states for clarity and speed in synthesis. The states represent how many bits of the sequence "1001" have been matched so far. Transition logic will allow overlapping sequence detection by carefully moving between states. The reset will be asynchronous and active low for quicker initialization. The output will be registered to avoid glitches, asserted only for one clock cycle when the full sequence is detected.
