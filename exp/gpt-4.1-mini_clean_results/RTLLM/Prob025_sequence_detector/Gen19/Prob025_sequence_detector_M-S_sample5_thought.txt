I will simplify the sequence detector by using a straightforward binary-encoded FSM with 3 bits representing the states IDLE to S4 in a simple ascending order (0 to 4). The next-state logic will be implemented clearly without any complex encoding or Gray-code style assignments. Output will be a Moore output derived combinationally from the current state, with the sequence_detected asserted when in S4.

The reset will be synchronous active low. The state transitions will handle overlapping sequences naturally. This straightforward approach reduces combinational logic complexity and improves readability while maintaining all required functionality.

This simplification may slightly increase power due to increased toggling but makes the design more maintainable and easier to verify.
