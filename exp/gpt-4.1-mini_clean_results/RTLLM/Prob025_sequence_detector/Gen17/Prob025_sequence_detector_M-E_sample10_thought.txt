I will design the 4-bit sequence detector "1001" using a Mealy state machine with minimal states to reduce logic and improve responsiveness. 

Key ideas:
- Use 4 states that represent how many bits have been matched so far in the sequence.
- Output (sequence_detected) will be combinational and asserted immediately when the sequence is detected, rather than registering it.
- Reset is active-high synchronous reset for clarity and ease of use.
- The next state and output are computed together in a single always block triggered on clock edge to minimize logic stages.
- To handle overlapping sequences, transitions will fallback to appropriate states representing partial matches.
- The Mealy machine style reduces the latency of sequence_detected (asserted in the same cycle the last bit arrives).
- State encoding will be simple 2-bit states, since only 4 meaningful states are needed.
- The module is fully synchronous with synchronous reset.

This approach trades a combinational output for reduced state logic and faster output assertion, which may benefit timing and area slightly.
