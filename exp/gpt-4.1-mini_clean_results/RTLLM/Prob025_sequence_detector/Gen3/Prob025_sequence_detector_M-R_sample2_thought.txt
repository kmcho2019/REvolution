Refactor the sequence detector FSM by implementing a Mealy state machine instead of a Moore machine. The output sequence_detected will be combinational and asserted immediately upon detecting the final bit of the sequence "1001" during the state transitions, allowing a potentially faster detection. Use one always block for state transitions synchronized to the clock and synchronous active-low reset, and combinational logic for the next state and output. This approach simplifies output logic and clearly separates state register and combinational next state/output logic, improving readability.
