To optimize the sequence detector for better timing and power, I will use one-hot encoding for the FSM states, which reduces the combinational complexity of the next-state logic and can improve timing. Also, I will switch to a fully synchronous active-low reset to simplify timing analysis and maintain consistency. The output signal `sequence_detected` will be driven directly by the state register indicating detection, ensuring a one-cycle pulse aligned with the clock. This removes the asynchronous reset on the output register, improving timing and synthesis friendliness. The FSM will still allow overlapping sequence detection as before.
