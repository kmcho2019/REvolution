To simplify the sequence detector while maintaining functionality, I will reduce the FSM to use a 3-bit binary state encoding with four states corresponding to the sequence progress: IDLE, S1, S2, and S3. The final detection is indicated when the FSM transitions from S3 to S1 upon receiving the last bit '1' of the sequence "1001". 

I will also unify the reset naming as "reset_n" for clarity and use synchronous active-low reset. The output "sequence_detected" will be asserted in the cycle the full sequence is detected (on the transition from S3 to S1).

This approach reduces complexity by maintaining simple state encoding and combining output generation with state transitions without extra states or complicated logic, keeping the design minimal and clear.
