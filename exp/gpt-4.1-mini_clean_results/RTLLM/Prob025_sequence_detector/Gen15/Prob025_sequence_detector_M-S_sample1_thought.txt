The original design uses a 4-state FSM encoded with 3 bits, which is more than necessary since only 4 states are needed. We can simplify the FSM by using a 2-bit state encoding, making the design more compact and straightforward.

Also, the sequence_detected output can be asserted combinationally based on the next state to reduce latency and simplify the code.

The reset will be synchronous and active low as specified.

Overall, a simpler FSM with fewer state bits and combinational output logic maintains functionality while reducing complexity.
