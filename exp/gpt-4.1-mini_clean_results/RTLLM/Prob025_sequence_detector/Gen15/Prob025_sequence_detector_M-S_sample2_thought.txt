To simplify the sequence detector design, I will reduce the FSM state encoding from 3 bits to 2 bits because only 4 states are needed. This reduces register width and combinational logic complexity. Also, I will implement the output combinationally within the FSM state transitions to avoid a separate output register and thus reduce logic and latency. The synchronous active-low reset behavior remains unchanged for reliable initialization. Overall, this results in a smaller and cleaner FSM with identical functionality.
