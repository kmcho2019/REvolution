Implement a Moore FSM with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4 representing the progress in detecting the sequence 1001. The FSM transitions on each clock cycle based on data_in. On reset (active high as specified), the FSM returns to IDLE. When the sequence 1001 is detected, sequence_detected is asserted high for one clock cycle. The output is combinational based on the current state.
