Implement a Moore FSM with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The transitions depend on the incoming bit data_in, trying to detect the sequence 1-0-0-1. On each clock edge, the FSM transitions according to the input. When the sequence is completely matched at S4, sequence_detected is asserted for one clock cycle. The FSM then continues to detect overlapping sequences by transitioning to the appropriate state. The reset_n is active low, so when reset_n is 0, FSM returns to IDLE.
