Implement a Moore FSM with five states (IDLE, S1, S2, S3, S4) to detect the sequence 1001 on the serial input data_in. On each clock edge, based on the current state and input bit, transition to the next appropriate state. When the sequence is fully matched (state S4), output sequence_detected is asserted high for one clock cycle. Use active-high reset to initialize the FSM to IDLE state.
