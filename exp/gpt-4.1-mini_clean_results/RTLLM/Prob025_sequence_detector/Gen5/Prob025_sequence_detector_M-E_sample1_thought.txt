Implement a Mealy FSM for the 4-bit sequence detector "1001" with states representing the number of bits matched so far. The FSM transitions on each clock cycle according to the current input bit and the present state. The sequence_detected output is asserted combinationally (in the state transition logic) when the final bit of the sequence is matched, resulting in a pulse synchronized to the clock cycle when the sequence completes. Use one always block triggered by clock and synchronous active-low reset, combining state update and output generation, and design transitions to support overlapping sequences by backtracking to earlier states when partial matches still hold.
