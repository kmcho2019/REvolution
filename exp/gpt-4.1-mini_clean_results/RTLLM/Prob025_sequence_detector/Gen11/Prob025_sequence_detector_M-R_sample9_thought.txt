I will refactor the sequence detector FSM by using binary-encoded states instead of one-hot encoding to reduce area and potentially power consumption. I will implement the FSM with a synchronous reset and separate combinational next-state and output logic blocks. Instead of embedding output logic inside the sequential block, I will use a combinational continuous assignment for `sequence_detected` based on the current state and input. This structure simplifies the output generation and makes the design cleaner. Non-blocking assignments will be used exclusively within sequential blocks for clarity. Overall, the FSM functionality and behavior will remain identical, but the state encoding and logic organization will be more area-efficient and clear.
