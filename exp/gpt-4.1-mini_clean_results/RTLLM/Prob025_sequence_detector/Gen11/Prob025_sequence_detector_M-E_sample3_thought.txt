Implement a Moore-type FSM with explicit one-hot encoded states representing each step of the sequence "1001". Each state indicates how many bits have been matched. The detector progresses state-by-state on matching bits. If an unexpected bit arrives, it jumps to the proper state based on the longest prefix match to support overlapping detection. The output `sequence_detected` is asserted only in the final matched state (S4). Reset is asynchronous active-low to immediately initialize the FSM to IDLE, ensuring a clean start. The one-hot encoding simplifies the next-state logic and enhances clarity in transitions.
