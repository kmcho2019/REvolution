2. Move the sequence_detected output generation from being combinational on next_state to a Moore output decoded purely from the registered current state. This change reduces glitches and dynamic switching power.

3. Simplify next-state logic by using straightforward logic expressions from one-hot states, which can reduce LUT usage and improve performance.

4. Keep synchronous active-low reset and register outputs for timing stability.

This approach trades a slight increase in FF count for simpler combinational logic, better timing, and potentially lower dynamic power, as the output is registered and glitches are minimized.
