To simplify the sequence detector, I will use a binary-encoded FSM with fewer bits for states, which reduces flip-flops and logic complexity compared to one-hot encoding. The FSM will have states IDLE, S1, S2, and S3 representing how many bits of the sequence 1001 have been matched. When the fourth bit is matched, sequence_detected is asserted for one clock cycle.

I will use synchronous reset (active low) and non-blocking assignments uniformly for clarity. The next state transitions are simplified and directly correspond to the input bits and the sequence progress. This approach keeps the functionality intact while reducing area and complexity.
