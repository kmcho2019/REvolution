I will implement the sequence detector using a Moore FSM with explicit states representing how many bits of the sequence "1001" have been matched so far. This FSM will be coded using one-hot encoding for clarity and potentially faster next-state decoding.

The states are:
- IDLE: no bits matched
- S1: matched '1'
- S10: matched "10"
- S100: matched "100"
- S1001: matched full "1001" (used to generate the output)

Transitions depend on the input bit. The output signal sequence_detected will be asserted synchronously in the cycle after matching the full sequence, i.e., when in the S1001 state.

To support overlapping sequences, after detecting "1001", the FSM transitions appropriately based on the last input bit to continue detection without missing sequences.

Reset is synchronous and active-high to align with typical synthesis constraints.

One-hot encoding means each state is represented by a single-bit flag, simplifying next-state logic and potentially speeding up the circuit.

This design emphasizes clarity and ease of understanding with explicit states, plus overlapping sequence detection support.
