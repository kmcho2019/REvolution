Implement a Mealy FSM with four states to detect the 4-bit sequence 1001. The states represent how many bits of the sequence have been matched so far. The output sequence_detected is asserted combinationally based on both the current state and the current input bit, allowing detection in the transition to the final matching condition. The FSM resets to the initial state asynchronously on reset_n de-asserted (active low), and otherwise updates state on the rising clock edge. The design will handle overlapping sequences naturally by transitioning to intermediate states accordingly.
