To improve PPA while maintaining correctness, I will optimize the FSM state encoding from binary to one-hot. One-hot encoding simplifies next-state logic by reducing combinational complexity, potentially improving timing (wns and tns). It may increase area slightly due to more flip-flops, but with only 5 states, the area overhead is minimal. Also, I will implement an asynchronous active-low reset, which can reduce reset path delays and improve timing as well. The sequence_detected output will be generated directly from the state transition to S4, registered on the clock for glitch-free output. These changes should improve timing (smaller logic depth), potentially reduce dynamic power (simpler next state logic), and maintain clear synchronous operation.
