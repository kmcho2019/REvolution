- Derive the output `sequence_detected` directly from the registered state (S4) rather than from next_state, eliminating glitches and reducing switching power.
- Simplify the next-state logic using one-hot encoding, with clear conditional assignments for each next state.
- Retain synchronous active-low reset for consistency.
- Maintain Moore output style, asserting output only when FSM is in S4.
- Ensure the output is a registered signal updated on the clock edge for timing stability.
