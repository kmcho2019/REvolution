Refactor the sequence detector FSM as a Moore machine rather than Mealy, where the output `sequence_detected` depends solely on the current state, simplifying timing analysis and making the output synchronous with the clock. Use enumerated type (via `localparam`) for states with binary encoding instead of one-hot to reduce register width. Implement the state register and output register in a single synchronous always block triggered by the clock and active-low reset. Calculate the next state combinationally using a separate always block. This approach ensures clean separation of sequential and combinational logic and provides registered outputs that are less prone to glitches. The asynchronous reset returns FSM to IDLE on negedge of `reset_n`.
