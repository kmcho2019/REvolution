Implement the sequence detector as a Moore FSM with explicit states representing how many bits of the sequence "1001" have been matched. Use an active-low synchronous reset (`reset_n`) as specified, initializing the state machine on the rising edge of the clock. The output `sequence_detected` is asserted only in the dedicated final state, simplifying output logic and providing clean timing for output signals. The FSM transitions on every clock cycle depending on the current input bit and current state, enabling reliable detection. Overlapping sequences are handled by carefully setting the next state to appropriate partial matches, allowing continuous monitoring of input without missing sequences.
