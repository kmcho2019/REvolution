I will implement a straightforward Moore FSM for detecting the sequence "1001" using simple `parameter` state encoding instead of SystemVerilog enums to ensure broader tool compatibility and eliminate casting issues.

The FSM will have five states: IDLE (no bits matched), S1 (matched '1'), S2 (matched '10'), S3 (matched '100'), and S4 (matched '1001'). The output `sequence_detected` will be asserted high when the FSM is in S4.

The FSM will use synchronous active-low reset (`reset_n`), and output will be registered. To support overlapping sequences, after detection in S4, the next state transitions consider the last input bit.

This approach reduces complexity, uses only Verilog-2001 constructs, and maintains correct sequence detection functionality.
