I will refactor the sequence detector by implementing the FSM with separate combinational and sequential always blocks: one for the next state logic and one for state register updates. Instead of using a SystemVerilog typedef enum, I will use localparam for state encoding to ensure better compatibility with pure Verilog. The output `sequence_detected` will be a combinational signal asserted when the current state matches the final detection state, rather than registered. The reset will remain active low synchronous. This approach clarifies the separation of combinational and sequential logic and avoids SystemVerilog constructs.
