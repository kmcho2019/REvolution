Implement the sequence detector as a Moore FSM with five states, each state representing the count of matched bits of the target sequence "1001". Use one-hot encoding for states to simplify next state decoding and improve clarity. The output `sequence_detected` is asserted whenever the FSM reaches the final matched state (S4). The FSM transitions on each rising clock edge, with an asynchronous active-low reset to initialize the state to IDLE. Overlapping sequences are handled by transitions back to appropriate earlier states based on input, maintaining the ability to detect sequences in a stream continuously.
