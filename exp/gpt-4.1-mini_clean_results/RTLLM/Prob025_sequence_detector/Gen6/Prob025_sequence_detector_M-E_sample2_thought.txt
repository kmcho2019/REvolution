Implement the sequence detector using a Mealy FSM where the output `sequence_detected` is asserted combinationally based on the current state and input, allowing immediate detection without waiting for a full clock cycle. Use one always block for state transitions (synchronous with reset) and another combinational always block for next state and output logic. This separation improves modularity and readability. Employ one-hot encoding for states for simpler transition logic, which can aid synthesis and debugging. The active-low reset asynchronously initializes the FSM to IDLE.
