I will implement the sequence detector using a Moore FSM with binary-encoded states to minimize area and power, unlike the one-hot encoding in the example. The FSM states represent the current matched length of the sequence 1001. On each clock, the FSM transitions based on the input bit and current state to track progress through the sequence. When the FSM reaches the final state indicating the entire sequence matched, the output sequence_detected is asserted synchronously for one clock cycle. The reset is synchronous active-high to simplify timing and implementation. This approach reduces the number of flip-flops to 3 bits, simplifies combinational logic, and cleanly separates state and output logic.
