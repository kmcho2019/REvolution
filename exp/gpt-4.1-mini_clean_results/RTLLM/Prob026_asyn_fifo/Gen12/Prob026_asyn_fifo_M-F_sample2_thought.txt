The Gray code conversion will remain as functions for clarity, but the full and empty conditions will be registered outputs updated synchronously on the respective clocks.  
I will keep the modular structure including separate synchronizer modules for clarity and maintainability.  
The write and read pointers and their Gray-coded versions remain as before, but full will be registered on write clock and empty on read clock, reflecting stable, glitch-free flags.  
This design balances performance, power, and area while preserving correct asynchronous FIFO operation per specification.
