I will keep the asynchronous FIFO design straightforward and modular:

1. Use a single Gray and binary pointer for read and write sides.
2. Implement two-stage synchronizers for crossing clock domains.
3. Use simple Gray-to-binary and binary-to-Gray functions.
4. Instantiate the dual-port RAM as a separate module.
5. Generate full and empty flags directly from the synchronized Gray pointers.
6. Use parameterizable width and depth with appropriate address width.
7. Remove unnecessary intermediate registers while preserving all required functionality.
8. Reset pointers and synchronizers asynchronously with active low resets.

This approach balances simplicity, readability, and correctness for an asynchronous FIFO.
