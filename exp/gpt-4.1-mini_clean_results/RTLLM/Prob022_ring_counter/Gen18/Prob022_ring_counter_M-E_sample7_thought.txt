Instead of storing the one-hot state directly, implement an index register that holds the position (0 to 7) of the '1' bit in the ring counter. On reset, initialize the index to 0. On each clock cycle, increment the index modulo 8. The output is then generated by left-shifting 1 by the index value. This approach avoids manipulating an 8-bit vector directly and does not use bit slicing or concatenation. It is simple, synthesizable, and compatible with all tools.
