To avoid unsupported concatenation and slicing operations that cause synthesis or simulation errors, implement the circular shift of the one-hot bit using a conditional shift operation and simple ternary logic.

Specifically, when shifting left, if the MSB is currently '1', the next state wraps to LSB '1'; otherwise, shift left by one bit.

This approach uses only supported bitwise operations and conditionals, making it compatible with all tools.

The reset condition remains the same, initializing to 8'b00000001.

This method preserves the ring counter functionality, ensures tool compatibility, and avoids complex concatenations.
