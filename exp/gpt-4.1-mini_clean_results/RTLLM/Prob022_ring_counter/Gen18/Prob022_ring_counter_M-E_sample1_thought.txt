Implement the 8-bit ring counter as a shift register with a single '1' bit rotating through the 8 positions. Use a register to hold the current state (one-hot encoding) and on every clock edge shift the bit left by one, wrapping around the MSB back to LSB. Reset sets the register to 8'b00000001. This approach uses a simple rotate-left operation within a sequential always block, fully encapsulated in the state register without separate decoding logic.
