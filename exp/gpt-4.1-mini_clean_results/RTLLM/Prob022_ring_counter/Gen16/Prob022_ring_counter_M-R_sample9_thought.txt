Refactor the ring counter by splitting the state register from the output. Use an 8-bit reg `state` to hold the current one-hot position. On reset, initialize `state` to 8'b00000001. On each rising clock edge, shift `state` left by 1; if MSB was set, wrap around by setting LSB. Output `out` is driven combinationally from `state`. This avoids explicit case statements, keeps simple logic, and should be supported by tools that may have trouble with assigning entire vectors in always blocks, as only one register `state` is updated with simple shift and conditional logic.
