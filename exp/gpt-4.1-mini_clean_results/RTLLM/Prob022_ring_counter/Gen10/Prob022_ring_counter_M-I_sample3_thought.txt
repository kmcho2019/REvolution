The main issue arises from the assignment `out <= 8'b1 << position;` inside a sequential always block, which some tools may not support due to variable shift operations directly into a register vector.

To fix this, I will eliminate the separate `position` register and maintain the one-hot pattern directly in the `out` register. On reset, `out` will be initialized to `8'b00000001`. On each clock rising edge, the pattern will rotate left by 1 bit, cycling the single '1' through all positions. This avoids any variable shift operations and directly updates the one-hot vector, improving synthesis compatibility.

This also simplifies the design by keeping only `out` as the state. Rotation can be done by concatenating the MSB to the LSB, effectively rotating the vector.

This approach is functionally equivalent and should be compatible with all synthesis and simulation tools.
