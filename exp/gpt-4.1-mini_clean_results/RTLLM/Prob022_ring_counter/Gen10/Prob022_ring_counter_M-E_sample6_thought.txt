Use a single 8-bit register `out` to hold the one-hot ring counter state directly and update it on each rising clock edge. On reset, `out` is set to `8'b00000001` (LSB high). On each clock edge, shift `out` left by one bit; if the MSB was previously set, wrap it around to the LSB. This shifting and wrap-around is implemented with a combinational expression that concatenates `out[6:0]` with `out[7]` to perform a rotate-left by 1 bit on every clock. This approach avoids index-based variable bit assignments and uses only standard shift and concatenation operations on vectors supported by all tools. The output `out` is a `reg` updated synchronously with `clk` and asynchronously with `reset`.
