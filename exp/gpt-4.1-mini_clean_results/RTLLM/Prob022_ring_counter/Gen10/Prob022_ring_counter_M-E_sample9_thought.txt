Use a 3-bit counter to track the position of the '1' in the 8-bit output, instead of manipulating the output bits directly. On reset, set the position to zero (corresponding to the LSB). On each clock rising edge, increment the position modulo 8. The output is then generated combinationally by decoding the position into a one-hot 8-bit vector with a single '1' bit at the tracked position. This approach cleanly separates the state (position counter) from the output encoding and avoids shift operations on vectors, improving synthesis compatibility and clarity.
