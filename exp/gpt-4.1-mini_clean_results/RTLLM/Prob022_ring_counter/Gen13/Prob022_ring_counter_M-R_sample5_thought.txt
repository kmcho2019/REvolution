To avoid the tool limitation causing elaboration failures when assigning a shifted vector to a register inside an always block, refactor the design by making the `out` output a wire instead of a reg, and implement it as a continuous assignment using a shift operation from the registered position.

This removes the need for a combinational always block and assignment to a reg vector.

Steps:
- Keep `position` as a 3-bit register updated on clock and reset.
- Declare `out` as an output wire [7:0].
- Implement `out` as a continuous assignment: `assign out = 8'b1 << position;`

This keeps the behavior identical but leverages Verilog best practice of modeling combinational outputs as wires with continuous assignments, sidestepping the tool limitation entirely.
