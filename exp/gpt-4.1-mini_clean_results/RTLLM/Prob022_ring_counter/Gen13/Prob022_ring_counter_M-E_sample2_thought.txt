Instead of tracking the position with an integer and deriving the output via a shift operation, directly implement the ring counter as an 8-bit shift register with a single '1' circulating through it.

Initialize the output register `out` with 8'b00000001 on reset. On each rising clock edge, shift the register left by 1 bit, and feed the bit that overflows from the MSB back into the LSB. This will create a circulating single '1' that cycles through all bits.

This approach avoids using a separate position register or shift operations on constants, making the design simpler and more direct. The output itself is the state register.

Such an 8-bit rotate-left logic on a one-hot value implements the ring counter naturally.
