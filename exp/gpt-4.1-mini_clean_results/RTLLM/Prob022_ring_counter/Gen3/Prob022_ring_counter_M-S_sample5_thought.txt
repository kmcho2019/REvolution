To simplify and ensure full synthesizability and tool compatibility, drive the entire output 'out' synchronously within a single always block triggered by the clock and reset signals. Initialize 'out' to 8'b0000_0001 on reset, and on each clock pulse shift 'out' left by one bit. When the MSB is set, the shift will wrap around naturally by shifting out the MSB and inserting 1 at the LSB by using a rotate operation. This avoids separate index registers and combinational blocks.
