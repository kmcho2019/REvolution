To address the simulation environment's limitation with assigning to the whole vector in a combinational block and to maintain a clean synchronous design, we'll directly update the output 'out' on the rising edge of 'clk'. Instead of using an 'index' and combinational assignment, the ring counter state will be stored in 'out' itself. On reset, 'out' is initialized to 8'b00000001, and on each clock, the '1' bit is rotated left by 1 position using a concatenation shift. This approach is synthesizable, simple, and avoids any unsupported indexed bit assignments or combinational outputs, improving compatibility and performance.
