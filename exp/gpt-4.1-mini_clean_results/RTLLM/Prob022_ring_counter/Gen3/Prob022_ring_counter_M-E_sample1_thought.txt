To avoid any combinational assignment issues and keep the design fully synchronous, implement the ring counter with a single 8-bit shift register that holds the state. On reset, initialize the register to 8'b0000_0001. On each rising clock edge, perform a circular left shift by 1. This means the MSB bit shifts out and re-enters at the LSB position, creating a cyclic one-hot pattern. Since all output updates occur synchronously in the clocked always block, this approach is simple, fully synthesizable, and avoids mixing combinational and sequential logic for the output.
