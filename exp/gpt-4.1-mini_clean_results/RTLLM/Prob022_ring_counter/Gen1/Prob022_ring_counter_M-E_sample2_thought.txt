Implement the 8-bit ring counter using a numeric index to track the position of the active bit instead of manipulating the entire 8-bit vector directly. Store the index (0 to 7) in a 3-bit register, and on each clock cycle, increment this index modulo 8. Generate the output by shifting a single '1' bit left by the stored index position. This avoids concatenation assignments and uses simple arithmetic and bit-shift operations for the output.
