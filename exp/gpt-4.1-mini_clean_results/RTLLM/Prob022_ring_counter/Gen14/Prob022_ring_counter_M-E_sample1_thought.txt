Implement the ring counter using a shift register approach with a registered 8-bit vector representing the one-hot state. On reset, initialize the vector to 8'b00000001, and on each clock rising edge, rotate the '1' bit left by one position by shifting and wrapping the MSB to LSB. This fully registered approach avoids using a separate position counter and shifts the output vector itself, ensuring compatibility with tools that may have issues with shift operations on vectors in combinational assignments. Using always block with posedge clk or reset ensures synchronous reset.
