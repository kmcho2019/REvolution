Implement the ring counter as a shift register initialized with a single '1' in the LSB. Instead of using concatenation to rotate the bits, explicitly shift the bits and wrap the MSB bit back to LSB by using a temporary variable. The output is a register updated synchronously on the clock or reset. On reset, set the output to 8'b00000001. On each clock edge, shift left by one position, and set LSB to the previous MSB to achieve the rotation. This approach avoids slice concatenation and uses simple bitwise operations for rotation, ensuring compatibility with all simulators.
