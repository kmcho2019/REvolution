To avoid the unsupported vector slicing and concatenation operations that cause elaboration issues, we replace the rotate operation with explicit bitwise assignments. We store the ring counter state in an 8-bit register 'out'. On each clock cycle (if not reset), we shift the single high bit left by one position by testing the current 'out' bits and manually assigning the next state's bit high, wrapping the MSB back to LSB. This approach uses straightforward conditional statements and bitwise operations without vector slicing or concatenation, which works around tool limitations while preserving the ring counter functionality.
