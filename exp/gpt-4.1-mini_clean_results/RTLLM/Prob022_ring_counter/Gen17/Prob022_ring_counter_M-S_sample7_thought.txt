Implement the ring counter as an 8-bit register holding the one-hot value directly. On reset, initialize it to 8'b00000001. On each clock rising edge, perform a circular left shift by 1 bit to move the '1' to the next position, wrapping from MSB back to LSB. This approach uses a single register for state, straightforward logic, and directly assigns the register to output, keeping the design simple and easy to synthesize.
