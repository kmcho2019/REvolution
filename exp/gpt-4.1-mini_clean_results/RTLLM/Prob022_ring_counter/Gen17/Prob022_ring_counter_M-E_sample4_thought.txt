Implement the ring counter by directly storing the full 8-bit one-hot state in a register. On each clock rising edge, shift the register left by one bit. When the MSB is 1, wrap around by moving the bit back to LSB on next cycle. On reset, initialize the register to 8'b00000001 (LSB set). This approach uses a single 8-bit register that rotates the '1' bit position cyclically, providing a straightforward and synthesizable ring counter implementation without relying on arithmetic shifts or separate counters.
