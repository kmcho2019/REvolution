Refactor the ring counter to separate the state storage and output logic. Use a simple 3-bit counter to hold the current position (0 to 7) of the '1' bit, and decode this position to a one-hot 8-bit output using a combinational assignment. This eliminates the need for shift operations and vector assignments in sequential always blocks and instead uses a small counter plus a combinational decoder for output. The counter increments modulo 8 on each clock and resets to zero, representing the LSB being set. This structure may improve compatibility with tools sensitive to vector assignments.
