Instead of rotating the entire 8-bit vector with concatenation, maintain an integer index (0 to 7) that points to which bit is set. On each clock edge, increment the index modulo 8 to move the '1' bit position. The output vector `out` is assigned combinationally based on this index by setting only the bit at the index position to 1 and all others to 0. This avoids any complex bitwise rotations or concatenations and relies on simple arithmetic and bit-select assignments, improving compatibility with synthesis tools.
