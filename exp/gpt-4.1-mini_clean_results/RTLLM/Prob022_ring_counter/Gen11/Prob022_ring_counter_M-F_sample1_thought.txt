To avoid the unsupported indexed assignment inside the procedural block, replace the combinational always block that assigns `out` with a continuous assignment statement. The output `out` can be driven by a wire instead of a reg, calculated combinationally as a one-hot encoded value by shifting 1 by `position`. This removes the need for a procedural block to assign bits individually. The `position` register updates on the clock and reset as before. This approach is fully synthesizable and supported by all tools.
