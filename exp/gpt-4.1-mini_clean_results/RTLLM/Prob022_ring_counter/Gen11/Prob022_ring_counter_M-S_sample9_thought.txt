Implement the ring counter by directly storing and updating the output register "out" as an 8-bit one-hot value. On reset, initialize "out" to have the LSB set. On each clock rising edge, shift "out" left by 1 bit; if the MSB is set before shifting, wrap around by setting the LSB after shift. This removes the need for an index variable and avoids the problematic shift assignment to output. The output port is declared as reg and updated synchronously within an always block, simplifying synthesis and simulation.
