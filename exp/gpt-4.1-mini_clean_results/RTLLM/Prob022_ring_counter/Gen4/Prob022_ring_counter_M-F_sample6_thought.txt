To avoid the tool's limitation on assigning concatenations to the entire vector, we will explicitly implement the ring counter shifting using a procedural bit-by-bit update. On each clock edge, we will shift the '1' by one position by assigning `out[i]` from `out[i-1]` for i=1 to 7, and assign `out[0]` from `out[7]` to wrap around. This avoids concatenation and ensures compatibility with the tool while preserving the ring counter behavior. The reset remains asynchronous.
