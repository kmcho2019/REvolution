To simplify the ring counter and avoid slice concatenation in the assignment, represent the ring counter output as a simple 8-bit shift register with a single '1' bit. On reset, initialize the output to 8'b0000_0001. On each clock rising edge, shift the '1' bit left by one position. When the MSB is set (out[7] == 1), wrap around by resetting to 8'b0000_0001. This approach uses only shift and conditional assignment, which are widely supported by synthesis tools and avoids complex bit concatenations.
