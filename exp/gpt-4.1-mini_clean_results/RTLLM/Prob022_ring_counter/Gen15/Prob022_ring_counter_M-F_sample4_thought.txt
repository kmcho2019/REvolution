To avoid the tool limitation with assigning a shifted vector dynamically to 'out', remove the combinational assignment block and instead directly update 'out' synchronously inside the clocked always block. On reset, set out to 8'b00000001. On each clock rising edge, perform a circular left shift on 'out' by shifting the bits left by one and wrapping the MSB to the LSB position. This eliminates the need for an intermediate index and dynamic shifting in a separate combinational block, ensuring all assignments to 'out' happen in a single clocked always block. This approach is fully synthesizable and avoids the reported assignment problem.
