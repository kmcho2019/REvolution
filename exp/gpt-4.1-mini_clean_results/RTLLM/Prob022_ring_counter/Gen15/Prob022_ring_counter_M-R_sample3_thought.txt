To avoid the dynamic bit assignment problem in combinational always blocks, we keep the 3-bit position register `pos` as before, but generate the output `out` using a continuous assignment with a shift operation instead of an always block. By defining `out` as a wire driven by the expression `8'b1 << pos`, we circumvent the synthesis/simulation tool limitation on bit indexing in procedural assignments. This retains the clarity of using `pos` as a state and ensures a clean, synthesizable ring counter.
