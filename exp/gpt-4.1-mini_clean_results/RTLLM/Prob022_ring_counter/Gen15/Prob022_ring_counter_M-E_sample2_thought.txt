Instead of storing the position index and dynamically assigning a bit in a combinational block, use an 8-bit shift register initialized with the LSB set to 1 on reset. On each rising clock edge, perform a left shift with wrap-around: the MSB bit shifted out is fed back into the LSB. This creates the cyclic ring counter behavior directly in the sequential logic with no need for combinational indexing or complex assignment. This approach is fully synthesizable, simple, and elegant.
