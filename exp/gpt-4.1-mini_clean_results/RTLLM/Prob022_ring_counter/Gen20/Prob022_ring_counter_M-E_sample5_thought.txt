Implement the ring counter as a shift register with a single '1' bit circulating through the 8-bit vector. Initialize the output to 8'b00000001 on reset. On each rising clock edge, shift the output left by one bit; if the MSB was 1 before shifting, wrap it around to the LSB by setting the LSB to 1 after the shift. This approach uses only shift and conditional logic directly on the output register without auxiliary position tracking variables, and no full-vector assignments with shifts of variable amounts inside the always block, ensuring compatibility and simplicity.
