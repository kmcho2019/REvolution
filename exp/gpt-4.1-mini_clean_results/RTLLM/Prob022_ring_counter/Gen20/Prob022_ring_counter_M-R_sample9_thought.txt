Refactor the ring counter by keeping an internal position index that indicates which bit is currently high. On each clock edge, increment the position modulo 8 to cycle through the bits. The output `out` is assigned combinationally based on the one-hot encoding of the position. This avoids assigning concatenations inside sequential blocks and removes the need for bitwise rotations within the always block. Instead, the position updates in the sequential block, and the output is continuously assigned via a shift of 1 by the position value, which is synthesizable and usually supported by all tools.
