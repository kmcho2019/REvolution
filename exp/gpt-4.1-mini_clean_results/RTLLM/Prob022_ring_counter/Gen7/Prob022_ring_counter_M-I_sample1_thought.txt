To address the elaboration error related to assigning the entire 8-bit vector 'out' in a combinational always block, I will integrate the output assignment into the clocked always block along with the 'index' state update. This means 'out' becomes a synchronous register updated on the clock's rising edge (or reset), avoiding combinational assignments that might not be supported by the toolchain. The 'out' vector is then updated by shifting a single '1' bit left by 'index'. This approach maintains the one-hot cyclic behavior, simplifies the design, and ensures compatibility with toolchains sensitive to combinational vector assignments.
