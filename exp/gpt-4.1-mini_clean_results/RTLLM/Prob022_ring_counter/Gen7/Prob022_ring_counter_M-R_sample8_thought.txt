To avoid combinational always block assignments to a vector (which some tools reject), I will remove the combinational always block for 'out' and instead generate the one-hot output using a continuous assignment with a left shift operation on a single-bit value. The state index will remain in a clocked always block. Then, 'out' is driven by shifting 1'b1 left by 'index', which is synthesizable and simpler. This eliminates the need for the case statement and avoids assignment issues, while keeping the same logic of the ring counter.
