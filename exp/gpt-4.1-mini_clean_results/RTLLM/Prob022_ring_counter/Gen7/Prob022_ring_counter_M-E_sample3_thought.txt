Implement the 8-bit ring counter as an 8-bit shift register initialized with a single '1' at LSB on reset. On each clock rising edge (if not reset), rotate the bits left by one position: the MSB wraps around to the LSB. This approach uses a single register for the output and directly shifts its bits cyclically, eliminating the need for separate state indexing or complex combinational logic. It is fully synchronous, synthesizable, and straightforward.
