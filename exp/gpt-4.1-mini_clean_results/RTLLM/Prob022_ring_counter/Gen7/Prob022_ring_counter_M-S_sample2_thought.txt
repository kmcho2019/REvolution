I will implement the ring counter as an 8-bit shift register initialized to 8'b0000_0001 on reset. On each rising clock edge, the output shifts left by one bit, with the MSB wrapping around to the LSB to maintain a circulating single high bit. This straightforward shift-register approach uses simple shift and concatenation operations to cycle the one-hot bit, ensuring only one bit is high at a time. This design is simpler and avoids variable indexing or explicit state decoding.
