The failure is due to the unsupported indexed bit-select assignment in the combinational always block (out[index] = 1'b1). To fix this, instead of assigning out as a zero vector and then setting a single bit dynamically, generate the one-hot vector by left-shifting a single 1 by 'index'. This avoids indexed bit-select assignment. Since 'index' is 3-bit, shifting 1 by index (1 << index) produces the desired one-hot output. This approach is fully synthesizable and supported.
