Implement the ring counter by storing the state directly in the 8-bit register 'out'. Initialize 'out' to 8'b00000001 on reset. On each rising clock edge, cyclically rotate 'out' to the left by one bit, which moves the single high bit through the 8-bit vector in a ring fashion. This approach keeps both state and output in a single register and updates it synchronously, avoiding separate count variables and shift operations in separate always blocks.
