The problem arises from the unsupported indexed bit-select assignment inside a combinational always block. To fix this, we can generate the one-hot output by left-shifting a 1 by the 'index' value, which is a synthesizable and tool-friendly operation. This avoids dynamic bit-select assignment. The index counter still updates on the rising edge of clk, resetting to 0 on reset. The output is assigned combinationally as (8'b1 << index), which ensures exactly one bit is high, cycling through all bits.
