To avoid the unsupported slice concatenation in the always block, implement the ring counter by storing the index of the currently set bit in a 3-bit register. On reset, initialize the index to 0 (corresponding to LSB). On each clock edge, increment the index modulo 8, cycling through 0 to 7. The output is then generated combinationally by shifting 1 left by the index value, ensuring exactly one bit is high at a time and cycling correctly. This approach replaces shifting the entire vector with indexing and combinational bit-shifting, which is well supported by all tools.
