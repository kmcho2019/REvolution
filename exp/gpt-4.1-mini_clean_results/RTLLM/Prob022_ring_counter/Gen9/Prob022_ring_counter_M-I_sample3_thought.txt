To improve PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will implement the ring counter using a one-hot encoding with a rotate operation that is naturally synthesized as a rotate circuit instead of a shift and conditional assignment. Also, I will use a reset synchronous to clock (for simpler timing) and implement the rotation with a rotate right operation to keep the "1" cycling through all bits in a cleaner way. This approach can lead to simpler combinational logic and slightly better timing. The output is a reg vector, and the logic is done inside the always block triggered on the rising edge of clk and synchronous reset. The initial state will have the LSB set to 1, and on each clock, the single bit is rotated right by one position.
