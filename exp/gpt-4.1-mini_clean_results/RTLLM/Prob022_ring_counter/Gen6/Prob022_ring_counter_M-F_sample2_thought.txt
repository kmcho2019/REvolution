To fix the syntax elaboration error caused by concatenation in the assignment to `next_out`, I will avoid using concatenations or entire vector assignments inside the always block. Instead, I will use bitwise shift operations which are more widely supported by simulation tools. Specifically, I will implement the circular left shift by shifting `out` left by one bit and OR-ing it with the previous MSB shifted into LSB position. This avoids concatenation and direct array slicing assignments, complying with more restrictive simulators. The reset logic remains synchronous and sets the initial state with LSB high.
