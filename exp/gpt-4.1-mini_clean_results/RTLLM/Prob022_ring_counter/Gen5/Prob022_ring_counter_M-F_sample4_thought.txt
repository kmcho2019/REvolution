To avoid the elaboration failure caused by assigning individual bits of the same register within a single clocked process, I will use a temporary 8-bit register variable to hold the next state of the ring counter. On each clock edge, after reset, I will perform a circular left-shift on this temporary variable (which is allowed as a whole vector operation), and then assign the temporary variable to the output register. This approach avoids multiple bit-wise assignments in a loop and removes write-order conflicts. The reset remains synchronous with the clock to ensure deterministic behavior.
