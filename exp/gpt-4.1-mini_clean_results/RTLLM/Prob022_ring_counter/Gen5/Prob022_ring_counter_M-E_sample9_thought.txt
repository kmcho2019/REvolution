A robust way to implement the ring counter that avoids issues with individual bit assignments is to treat 'out' as a single 8-bit register and perform the rotation using a combination of bitwise operations. On each clock rising edge, if not in reset, we rotate 'out' left by one bit with wrap-around: the MSB moves to the LSB position. On reset, we initialize 'out' to 8'b00000001. This approach uses standard bitwise shift and concatenation operations on the whole vector at once, which is well supported and synthesizable.
