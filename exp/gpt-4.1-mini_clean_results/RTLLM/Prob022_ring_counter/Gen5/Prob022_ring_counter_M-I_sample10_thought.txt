To avoid issues with combinational assignment and improve synthesis compatibility, assign the output `out` synchronously within the clocked always block along with the position update. This ensures the whole `out` register is updated on the clock edge, eliminating the unsupported combinational assignment from the previous design. The logic remains the same: on reset, initialize `out` to 1 in the LSB; otherwise, rotate the single '1' bit by shifting left by one with wrap-around. Using a synchronous update reduces simulator elaboration errors and improves robustness without changing functionality.
