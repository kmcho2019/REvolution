Instead of using an initial block with a forever delay loop, implement the clock generator using an always block sensitive to a free-running simulation timer or an internal counter. This approach uses an internal counter that increments on every simulation time unit (or a given base time) and toggles the clock output when the counter reaches half the PERIOD. This ensures precise toggling even with odd PERIOD values, as the counter controls timing explicitly. The clock output is initialized in an initial block to 0 for deterministic startup. This counter-driven approach gives better control over clock edges and avoids relying on delay constructs, making the design more deterministic and portable.
