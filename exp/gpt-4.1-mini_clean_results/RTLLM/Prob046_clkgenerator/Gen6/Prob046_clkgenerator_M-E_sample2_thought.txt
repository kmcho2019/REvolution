Implement the clock generator using a free-running counter inside an always block triggered by an internal simulation time reference. The counter increments every time unit, and when it reaches half the PERIOD, it toggles the clock and resets the counter. This approach avoids direct delays (#) in always blocks and uses a counter to generate a stable and periodic clock signal. The initial block still initializes clk to 0. This method is commonly used in testbench clock generation and is straightforward for simulation.
