To fix the simulation compatibility and ensure a proper periodic clock generation, the half period delay must be an integer value because Verilog simulators require integral delay values. Using integer division of PERIOD by 2 is standard and safe.

If PERIOD is odd, integer division truncation means the clock period might be slightly off, but this is acceptable for simulation.

The clock output is declared as reg and initialized to 0 in the initial block, then toggled forever every half PERIOD time units.

This approach maintains the original behavior, is fully simulation-compatible, and avoids unsupported real delays.

Since this module is primarily for testbench use (non-synthesizable), this approach is sufficient.
