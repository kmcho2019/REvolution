Refactor the clock generator to use a simple counter-based approach driven by an always block sensitive to an internal time increment. Instead of using delays inside procedural blocks, the clock toggling is controlled by a counter counting simulation time units, toggling the clock every half PERIOD cycles. This approach mimics a frequency divider in hardware and is commonly used in testbenches when a time reference like a free-running counter or clock is not available. Initial clock value is set to 0. This style is slightly more structured and avoids the use of explicit delay (#) statements inside always blocks, making the toggling more explicit and potentially clearer for synthesis tools or for environments that prefer purely procedural timing control.
