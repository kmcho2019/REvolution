The recursive task approach is problematic because infinite recursion in Verilog procedural code is unsafe and can cause simulation failures. A more robust and conventional solution is to use an initial block containing a forever loop with a delay statement to toggle the clock. This approach is simple, reliable, and widely used for testbench clock generation.

Specifically:
- Declare clk as a reg output.
- Initialize clk to 0 inside the initial block.
- Use a forever loop inside the initial block that waits half the PERIOD and toggles clk.
- This ensures a clean, periodic square wave clock without the risk of recursion-induced simulation issues.
- This style is non-synthesizable but ideal for simulation clock generation.

This improves simulation stability and clarity while fully meeting the design requirements.
