To fix the simulation issues caused by recursive task calls, I will replace the recursive task with a straightforward always block that toggles the clock signal every half PERIOD. This is the most common and reliable method in Verilog for generating a periodic clock signal.

Key points:
- Use a reg output `clk`, initialized to 0 in an initial block.
- Use an always block with a `#(PERIOD/2)` delay followed by toggling `clk`.
- The always block runs indefinitely, toggling the clock at fixed intervals without recursion.
- This style is synthesizable for simulation and aligns with best practices for clock generation.
