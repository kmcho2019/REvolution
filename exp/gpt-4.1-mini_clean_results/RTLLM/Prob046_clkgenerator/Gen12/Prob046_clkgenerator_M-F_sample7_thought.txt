To fix the simulation issues caused by the infinite recursion in the clock toggling task, I will revert to a more conventional and robust approach using an initial block with a forever loop.

The design idea:
- Declare `clk` as a reg output, initialized to 0 in the initial block.
- Use a forever loop inside the initial block that waits half of the PERIOD and then toggles clk.
- This approach is simple, synthesizable, and well-supported by all simulators.
- It avoids the pitfalls of infinite recursive calls and ensures stable periodic clock generation.

This structure will generate the intended periodic clock signal toggling every half PERIOD without causing simulation hang-ups or failures.
