Create a clock generator by using an always block that toggles the clk output at fixed time intervals, controlled by a local counter. The counter counts simulation time steps until it reaches half the PERIOD, then toggles clk and resets. This removes the use of initial/forever delay loops and explicitly models the clock period by counting time units in an always block with time units simulated via a clocking event such as a zero-delay event or a time unit cycle. To implement this cleanly in pure Verilog simulation, we'll use an always block triggered by the #1 time unit delay. The counter increments every time unit; once it hits PERIOD/2, clk toggles and counter resets, producing a clock with the desired period.
