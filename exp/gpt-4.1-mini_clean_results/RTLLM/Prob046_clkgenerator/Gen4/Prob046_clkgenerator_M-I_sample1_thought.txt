To ensure simulation correctness and compatibility, use integer delays by performing integer division for half the PERIOD. Initialize clk to 0 in an initial block, then implement a simple always block toggling clk every #(PERIOD/2). If PERIOD is odd, integer division truncates, but the clock period still remains approximately correct. This approach avoids non-standard floating-point delays and ensures stable toggling timing compatible with most simulators and testbenches. Keeping the clock generation simple with procedural toggling is standard for testbench clock generators.
