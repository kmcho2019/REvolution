To strictly adhere to the problem statement, the clock should be initialized to 0 in an initial block. Then, a forever loop inside the initial block toggles the clock every half PERIOD. This approach is very common for simulation-only clock generators and is clear, deterministic, and fully compliant with the requirement. Using a separate always block with delay is less preferred here. The reg clk will be assigned in the initial block using blocking assignment, which is standard practice for testbench-style clock generation. This solution ensures a known initial state and periodic toggling without race conditions.
