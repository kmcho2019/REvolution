Instead of using an always block with delay or an initial block with forever loop, use a single initial block containing a forever loop that toggles the clk signal every half PERIOD. This approach keeps the clock generation self-contained within one procedural block and avoids potential issues with non-standard always blocks without sensitivity lists. The clk is initialized at 0 and then toggled after each half period delay indefinitely, ensuring a stable, square wave clock output for simulation purposes.
