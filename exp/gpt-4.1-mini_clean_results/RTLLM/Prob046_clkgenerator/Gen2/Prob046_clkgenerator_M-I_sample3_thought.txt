The previous design used a time_count integer and event sensitivity on this counter, which is unconventional and problematic for clock generation. A standard, simpler, and reliable approach is to use an initial block with a forever loop containing a delay of half the PERIOD to toggle the clk signal. This directly models a clock with the correct frequency and duty cycle. This method is simple, synthesizable in many simulators, and avoids race conditions or missed toggles. The clk output is initialized to 0, then toggled every half PERIOD delay, producing a clean square wave.
