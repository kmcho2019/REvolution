To improve the clock generator for better reliability and clarity, especially in simulation, we can use an always block instead of an initial block for toggling the clock. This approach better models a continuous process and ensures that the clock toggling is edge-sensitive to time progression, which is the usual convention. We will also add a localparam HALF_PERIOD to clearly define the delay interval. The always block triggers every HALF_PERIOD delay, toggling the clk signal indefinitely. This version is clean, synthesizable (though real clock generation is usually external), and commonly used in testbenches and simulation environments. Additionally, including `timescale for delay precision is good practice.
