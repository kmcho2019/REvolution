The original solution is functionally correct and produces a periodic clock signal with toggling every half PERIOD. However, the compile-time check enforcing an even PERIOD caused failures when an odd PERIOD is used. 

Improvement strategy:

1. Remove the strict even PERIOD check. The clock can toggle every half PERIOD, but if PERIOD is odd, the clock will have a slightly uneven duty cycle, which may be acceptable.

2. Keep the initial block to initialize clk to 0.

3. Use a single initial block to both initialize clk and start the toggling, combining simplicity and clarity.

4. Add comments to clarify behavior and usage.

5. Retain the use of delay-based toggling since this module is for simulation/testbench use only.

This produces a robust clock generator usable with any positive integer PERIOD parameter without forcing 50% duty cycle.
