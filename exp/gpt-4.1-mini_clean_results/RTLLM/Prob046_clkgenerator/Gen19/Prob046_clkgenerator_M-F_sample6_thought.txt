To address the feedback and ensure robust clock generation compatible with more simulators and testbenches, initialize the clock inside an initial block and toggle it within an always block triggered by a delay. This preserves the intended clock behavior but avoids initializing the output register in its port declaration. Also, using an initial block to set `clk = 0` ensures a known stable starting value, while an always block with a delay loop cleanly toggles the clock signal. This approach is standard for simulation clock generators and avoids race conditions with synchronous initializations.
