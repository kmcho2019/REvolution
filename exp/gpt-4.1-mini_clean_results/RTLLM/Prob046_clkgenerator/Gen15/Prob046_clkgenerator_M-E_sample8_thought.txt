Instead of using an initial block with a forever loop, implement the clock generator using an always block with a non-blocking assignment and a delay. Declare the clk as a reg output initialized to zero. The always block will toggle clk after a delay of PERIOD/2 time units, and this process repeats indefinitely. This method models the clock behavior cleanly and avoids potential issues with initial blocks by continuously scheduling toggles in an always block, which some simulators handle more reliably. It remains purely behavioral and suitable for testbench use.
