Refactor the clock generator to use an always block triggered by a sensitivity to a dummy signal (like an internal counter incremented in an always block with no sensitivity list) to toggle the clock at half-period intervals. This approach avoids the infinite loop in an always block without sensitivity and makes the toggling event-driven with delay, which is more simulation-friendly. Alternatively, we use a simple always block with delay, but this time with an explicit initial block to initialize and the always block simply toggling clk after the delay. This matches common clock generation style in testbenches and is synthesizable for simulation.
