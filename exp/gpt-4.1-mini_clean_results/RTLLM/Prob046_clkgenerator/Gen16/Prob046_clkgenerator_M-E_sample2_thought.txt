To create a synthesizable periodic clock generator without relying on delays or infinite initial/always loops with `#` delays, use a free-running input clock and a counter to divide it down to the desired frequency. The module will have an input clock (clk_in) at a known frequency and output a slower clock (clk) toggling every PERIOD input clock cycles. PERIOD parameter defines the full clock period in terms of input clock cycles. The counter counts up to PERIOD/2 - 1, then toggles the output clock and resets the counter. This approach is synthesizable, hardware-friendly, and flexible, as PERIOD can be adjusted according to the input clock frequency and desired output frequency. Initialization of the counter and clk output will be done via a synchronous reset input to ensure deterministic startup. This method avoids delays and initial blocks, making it robust for FPGA/ASIC implementation.
