Implement a radix-2 iterative divider supporting signed and unsigned 8-bit inputs by separating control and data path clearly.  
On a new operation (opn_valid), latch inputs and signs, compute absolute values for signed operations.  
Use two 17-bit registers: remainder (17 bits to hold remainder shifted left by one) and divisor (9 bits) plus a quotient register (8 bits).  
At each clock cycle, shift remainder left, subtract divisor, and set quotient bit based on whether subtraction succeeded (non-negative remainder).  
Perform 8 iterations controlled by a 4-bit counter.  
After completion, apply sign corrections to quotient and remainder before output.  
Manage result valid flag properly, ensuring synchronous operation.  
All internal variables declared at module scope to avoid illegal declarations inside procedural blocks.  
This design uses separate registers for remainder and quotient, which simplifies partial updates, avoids complex combined shift registers, and increases clarity.
