Implement a radix-2 divider using an iterative shift-subtract approach controlled by a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE.  
On opn_valid and when idle, latch inputs and determine the absolute values and signs for signed division.  
Initialize the remainder register (9 bits) with zero and the quotient register (8 bits) with zero.  
In each clock cycle during DIVIDE, shift left the (remainder, quotient) combined register by one bit, bring down the next dividend bit, then subtract the divisor from the remainder.  
If subtraction result is non-negative, update remainder and set quotient bit to 1; else restore remainder and set quotient bit to 0.  
After 8 iterations, adjust quotient and remainder signs if signed operation is selected, then output the concatenated (remainder[7:0], quotient[7:0]) as result.  
Use synchronous reset and manage res_valid signal to indicate valid output.  
The design avoids complex wide shift registers by explicitly shifting remainder and quotient registers separately, improving clarity.  
All combinational operations (subtractions and sign corrections) are handled clearly and separately from sequential logic.
