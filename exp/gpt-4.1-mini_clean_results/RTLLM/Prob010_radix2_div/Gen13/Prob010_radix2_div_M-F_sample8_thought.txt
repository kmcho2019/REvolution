The main issue lies in the incorrect width and bit alignment of the shift register (SR) initialization and the divisor extension, which cause the subtraction to always fail, preventing quotient bits from setting and the counter from reaching the terminal count, thus the design never completes the division cycle.

To fix this:

1. **Shift Register Width and Initialization:**
   - The SR is specified as 17 bits wide to hold remainder and quotient with one extra bit for shifting.
   - We must carefully assign the initial value of SR so that the upper 9 bits hold the remainder (initially 0), the next 8 bits hold the dividend_abs shifted left by 1 (one-bit shifted quotient), and the least significant bit (LSB) is 0.
   - This means SR = {remainder(9'b0), dividend_abs(8 bits), 1'b0}, total 18 bits, but since SR is 17 bits, the concatenation must be {remainder(9 bits), dividend_abs shifted left by 1, i.e., 9 bits total}.
   - To resolve this, shift dividend_abs by 1 (multiply by 2) and assign it to the lower 9 bits (quotient bits + LSB). So dividend_abs shifted left 1 bit fits into 9 bits.
   - SR is then `{9'd0, dividend_abs << 1}` which is 17 bits (9 + 8 bits shifted left 1 => 9 bits).

2. **Divisor Extension and Negation:**
   - The divisor must be aligned with the remainder bits to perform subtraction.
   - The remainder occupies upper 9 bits in SR, so extend divisor to 9 bits and align them with remainder bits.
   - Thus, `divisor_ext = {divisor_abs, 1'b0}` as 9 bits, then negated to 9 bits for subtraction with remainder.
   - This ensures that subtraction `remainder + neg_divisor` is 10 bits (9-bit operands plus one carry bit) correctly aligned.

3. **Subtraction and Update Logic:**
   - Use 10-bit subtraction (remainder + neg_divisor) with MSB indicating borrow (if set, subtraction failed).
   - When subtraction succeeds (no borrow), update remainder bits to sub_res[8:0] and set quotient bit to 1.
   - Otherwise, leave remainder unchanged and set quotient bit to 0.
   - Shift SR left by 1 to incorporate next quotient bit.

4. **Counter and FSM Transitions:**
   - Initialize counter to 1 at start of division and count up to 8 to perform 8 steps.
   - Transition from DIVIDE to DONE when cnt == 8.
   - Ensure counter increments correctly in DIVIDE and resets in other states.
   - `res_valid` asserted in DONE state and cleared when `res_ready` is asserted.

5. **Sign Correction and Result Output:**
   - After division, correct quotient and remainder signs based on initial operand signs.
   - Place remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits of 16-bit result.

This fixes alignment and width mismatches that blocked subtraction success, enabling proper progression of the division process and resolution of the simulation deadlock.
