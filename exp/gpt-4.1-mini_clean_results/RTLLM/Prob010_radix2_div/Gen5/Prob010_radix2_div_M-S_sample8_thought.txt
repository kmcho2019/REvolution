Use a simple iterative radix-2 division approach with a shift register holding remainder and quotient combined. On opn_valid, capture inputs, compute absolute values if signed, and initialize a 17-bit shift register (9 bits remainder, 8 bits quotient). Each clock cycle, shift left, subtract divisor from remainder, set quotient bit accordingly, and increment count.

When 8 cycles are done, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder if signed operation. Provide the combined result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. res_valid signals result ready, cleared when a new operation starts. Keep the design synchronous and straightforward without extra FSM states.
