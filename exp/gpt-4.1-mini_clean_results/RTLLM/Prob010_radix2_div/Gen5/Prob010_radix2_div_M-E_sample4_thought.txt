Implement the radix-2 divider with a synchronous iterative approach controlled by an explicit 'start' signal and a counter. On opn_valid, latch dividend and divisor and compute their absolute values if signed operation is requested. Use a 17-bit shift register SR consisting of the remainder (9 bits) in the upper bits and the quotient (8 bits) in the lower bits. At each clock cycle, shift SR left by one and subtract the divisor from the remainder part; if subtraction is non-negative, keep the subtraction result in the remainder and set the LSB of quotient to 1, else restore remainder and set quotient bit to 0. Repeat for 8 cycles. At end, if signed operation, restore the sign of quotient and remainder according to input sign bits. Provide the result (remainder[7:0] in upper half and quotient[7:0] in lower half) and assert res_valid. Manage ready-to-start logic to avoid starting new operation until previous result consumed.
