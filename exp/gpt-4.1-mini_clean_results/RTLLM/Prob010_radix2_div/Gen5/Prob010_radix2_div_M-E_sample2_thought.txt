Implement the radix-2 divider using a shift-subtract iterative algorithm driven by a synchronous FSM with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE. At IDLE, upon opn_valid, capture inputs, compute absolute values for signed operands, and initialize the 17-bit shift register SR, combining the remainder (9 bits) and quotient (8 bits). Perform division iterations in the DIVIDE state by shifting SR left and conditionally subtracting the divisor, updating the quotient bit accordingly. Use combinational logic for subtraction and quotient bit decision. After 8 iterations, move to DONE state, apply sign corrections on quotient and remainder if the operation is signed, then output the 16-bit result (remainder upper 8 bits, quotient lower 8 bits) and assert res_valid. Wait for result consumption (opn_valid low) before returning to IDLE. This clear FSM and stepwise control with explicit sign handling and a 17-bit shift register simplifies the design and ensures correct signed/unsigned division.
