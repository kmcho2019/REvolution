Simplify the design by using fewer registers and combining operations efficiently. Use a single shift register to hold remainder and quotient and perform subtraction iteratively. Handle sign conversion only once at the start for absolute values, and apply sign correction only after division completes. Use a simple counter for the iteration steps and control signals for start and busy states. Manage res_valid to indicate when the result is ready, clearing it at reset or when a new operation starts. This approach keeps the radix-2 iterative division clear and efficient without redundant signals.
