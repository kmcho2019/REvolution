Implement a straightforward radix-2 division using a single FSM with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, DONE.  
Capture inputs on opn_valid, calculate absolute values and signs for signed operation.  
Use a 17-bit shift register to hold partial remainder and quotient during division steps.  
Perform 8 division iterations, updating the partial remainder and quotient bits per radix-2 algorithm.  
After division, correct the signs of quotient and remainder accordingly.  
Output the combined 16-bit result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.  
All registers and signals are declared at module level; no local declarations inside always blocks to maintain synthesis correctness.  
This simplified approach avoids complex combinational logic blocks and internal combinational variables, making the code easier to read and verify.
