The original design implements a radix-2 non-restoring division for signed and unsigned 8-bit inputs, producing a 16-bit output containing remainder and quotient. It uses an FSM with states IDLE, RUN, FINISH, which is good for clarity and synchronous operation.

Improvement strategy:
1. Remove the non-synthesizable `typedef enum` and use localparam for FSM states for better compatibility across more tools.
2. Simplify the sign handling by calculating absolute values upfront and dealing with sign corrections only once at the end.
3. Replace the signed arithmetic with explicit signed wires to improve synthesis interpretation.
4. Use a single always_ff style sequential block and one always_comb block to separate combinational logic cleanly.
5. Correct width of signals to avoid any sign-extension or mismatch issues.
6. Provide clear reset initialization.
7. Optimize logic to reduce combinational path length by reducing intermediate signed operations.
8. Keep the division counter to exactly 8 iterations, and simplify the control signals.
9. Keep the SR register width at 17 bits: 9-bit partial remainder (including sign bit), plus 8-bit quotient.
10. Remove unnecessary temporary registers inside always blocks and pre-declare all intermediate signals outside always blocks.

This results in a clean, synthesizable, and efficient radix-2 division unit for 8-bit operands supporting signed and unsigned division with proper output formatting and control signals.
