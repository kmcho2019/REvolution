I will implement a radix-2 divider that processes the division iteratively over 8 clock cycles after input is captured. The division algorithm will be:

- On opn_valid, latch inputs and initialize internal states.
- For signed division, extract signs of dividend and divisor, convert operands to absolute values.
- Use a 17-bit shift register to hold remainder (initially zero) and quotient (initially zero).
- Each iteration:
  - Shift the remainder left by 1, bring down the next bit of dividend.
  - Subtract divisor_abs from the remainder.
  - If subtraction result >= 0, set current quotient bit to 1 and update remainder to subtraction result.
  - Else, keep remainder unchanged and quotient bit 0.
- After 8 iterations, apply sign correction on quotient and remainder if signed division.
- Output 16-bit result {remainder[7:0], quotient[7:0]}.
- Use res_valid to indicate output is ready, clear it on start of next operation.
- Ensure synchronous reset clears all states.

This approach strictly separates remainder and quotient parts in registers and manages sign corrections after division is complete. It uses simple sequential logic and is easy to follow.
