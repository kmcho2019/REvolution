Implement a simplified iterative radix-2 divider that handles signed and unsigned 8-bit inputs.  
On opn_valid and res_valid low, latch inputs, convert to absolute values if signed, and initialize SR with dividend magnitude shifted left by one bit.  
NEG_DIVISOR holds the negated divisor magnitude extended to 9 bits for subtraction.  
Each clock cycle, subtract NEG_DIVISOR from SR's upper 9 bits (partial remainder).  
If subtraction yields no borrow, shift left SR and insert '1' as quotient bit; else restore remainder and insert '0'.  
Count 8 iterations for full 8-bit quotient.  
After division, apply sign corrections to quotient and remainder if signed.  
Output result as {remainder[7:0], quotient[7:0]} and assert res_valid.  
Use simple registers and combinational logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
