Refactor the radix-2 divider using an explicit finite state machine (FSM) with clearly defined states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE.  
On opn_valid, latch inputs and prepare operands and signs; then enter the DIVIDE state.  
Use a 4-bit counter counting from 0 to 7 to step through division cycles.  
Use combinational subtraction to decide quotient bit in each cycle, updating a shift register SR accordingly.  
After 8 cycles, perform sign correction on quotient and remainder and move to DONE state.  
In DONE, assert res_valid until acknowledged by new opn_valid.  
This restructuring improves clarity and modularity, avoids complex muxes inside always blocks, and uses synchronous reset and explicit state transitions.  
The result ports remain the same, and interface matches exactly the original spec.
