Implement a radix-2 restoring division algorithm using a synchronous iterative design controlled by a simple FSM.  
On receiving a valid operation, capture inputs and compute absolute values for signed operation support.  
Use a 17-bit register SR to hold the partial remainder in its upper 9 bits and the quotient in its lower 8 bits.  
Initialize SR with the absolute dividend shifted left by 1 bit and quotient zero.  
At each clock cycle, shift SR left by 1, then subtract the divisor from the partial remainder.  
If subtraction result is negative, restore the partial remainder (add divisor back) and set the current quotient bit to 0; otherwise, set the quotient bit to 1.  
Repeat for 8 iterations (8 bits quotient).  
After iterations, apply sign corrections to quotient and remainder based on original signs.  
Output the 16-bit result with upper 8 bits as remainder and lower 8 bits as quotient, assert res_valid.  
Use only module-level registers, no procedural declarations inside always blocks.  
Resets and new operations handled cleanly with FSM states: IDLE, RUN, DONE.
