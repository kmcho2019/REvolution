Implement a straightforward radix-2 divider using a single synchronous always block with a simple FSM: IDLE and DIVIDE states.  
When opn_valid is asserted and the module is idle, latch inputs and compute absolute values if signed.  
Use a 17-bit shift register holding remainder (9 bits) and quotient (8 bits) with the dividend absolute value placed initially in the quotient portion, remainder cleared.  
During each clock cycle in DIVIDE state, perform the following: shift left the SR by 1 bit, subtract divisor_abs from remainder portion, set quotient bit based on borrow from subtraction (if no borrow, quotient bit = 1, remainder updated to subtraction result; else quotient bit = 0, remainder unchanged).  
Count 8 iterations, then transition to DONE state, where res_valid is asserted.  
After DONE, apply sign correction combinationally for quotient and remainder before outputting.  
The design simplifies control with two states, avoids complex enumerated types, and performs division in 8 cycles with a simple shift-subtract cycle.  
Resets clear state and outputs. The result concatenates corrected remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).  
This design is easy to understand, synthesizable, and fits problem specs.
