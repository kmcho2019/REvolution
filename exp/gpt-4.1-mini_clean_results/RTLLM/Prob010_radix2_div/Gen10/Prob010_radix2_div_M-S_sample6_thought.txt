Simplify the radix-2 divider by using a single always block with a minimal state machine. On opn_valid, latch absolute values and signs, initialize the shift register holding remainder and quotient bits. Each clock cycle, perform subtraction of divisor from remainder; shift in quotient bit accordingly. After 8 iterations, apply sign corrections if signed division, then output final result and set res_valid. Reset and ready states managed simply with a busy flag and counter. This approach removes separate functions and intermediate registers for clarity while preserving all features.
