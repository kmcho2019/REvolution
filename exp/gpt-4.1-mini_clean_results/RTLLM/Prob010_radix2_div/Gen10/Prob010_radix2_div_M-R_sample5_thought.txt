Refactor the radix-2 divider design into a clear Finite State Machine (FSM) structure to improve readability and control flow.

- Define explicit states: IDLE (waiting for input), PREPARE (capture inputs and initialize registers), DIVIDE (perform division steps), DONE (output result and wait for acknowledgment).
- Use synchronous registers for state transitions and counters.
- Calculate absolute values and sign flags during PREPARE state.
- Use a 17-bit shift register (SR) for remainder (upper 9 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).
- Perform subtraction and quotient bit update combinationally during DIVIDE state.
- On DONE, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder, output the 16-bit result.
- Manage res_valid in DONE state and clear it when a new operation is started.
- Handle division by zero by immediately skipping division and outputting zero result.
- This structure separates datapath and control clearly, improving maintainability and easing debugging.
