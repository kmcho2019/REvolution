Implement a radix-2 divider using a two-phase pipeline style inside a synchronous process. On receiving opn_valid when idle, latch inputs and prepare absolute values if signed. Use a single 9-bit remainder register initialized with 0, and an 8-bit quotient register initialized to 0. Each clock cycle shifts remainder and quotient left by one, inserts the next dividend bit, and tries to subtract divisor_abs from remainder. If remainder ≥ divisor_abs, set quotient LSB to 1 and update remainder accordingly; else set quotient bit to 0. Count 8 iterations for 8 bits. After completion, apply sign corrections to quotient and remainder based on original signs. Output combined result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits, and raise res_valid. This design uses minimal registers (remainder, quotient, counters), separates sign handling cleanly, and shifts the quotient and remainder in parallel for clarity.
