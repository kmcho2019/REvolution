Refactor the radix-2 divider to a clean multi-cycle synchronous design with a minimal FSM using explicit states IDLE, RUN, and DONE to control operation flow.  
Declare all intermediate signals and registers at module scope to avoid illegal in-block declarations.  
Perform the shift, subtract, and decision logic combinationally with separate signals and update registers synchronously on clock edges.  
Use a 4-bit counter to track iteration count from 0 to 8 for the division cycles.  
On start (opn_valid & idle), latch inputs, convert to unsigned magnitude if signed, and initialize the shift register (SR) holding remainder and quotient bits.  
During RUN state, at each clock cycle perform shift-left on SR, subtract divisor magnitude from remainder portion, and update SR accordingly based on borrow.  
When count reaches 8, go to DONE state where final quotient and remainder are sign-corrected and output is marked valid.  
On reset or new operation, clear outputs and states.  
This clean FSM approach clarifies operation phases, eliminates in-block variable declarations, and simplifies synthesis and simulation.
