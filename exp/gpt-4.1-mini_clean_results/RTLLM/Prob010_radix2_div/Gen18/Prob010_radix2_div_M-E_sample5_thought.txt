Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider using a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, DONE. On opn_valid, the module captures inputs, calculates absolute values and signs, and initializes a 17-bit shift register holding remainder (upper 9 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits). The divider then iteratively shifts left by one and tries to subtract the divisor from the remainder; if the subtraction is non-negative, the quotient bit is set to 1, otherwise 0. After 8 cycles, the module applies sign correction to the quotient and remainder and outputs the result with res_valid asserted. The remainder is kept in the upper 8 bits, and the quotient in the lower 8 bits of the 16-bit result. Division by zero outputs zero quotient and dividend as remainder. The design uses a counter to track iterations and synchronous logic for clean timing.
