Implement a simplified radix-2 divider with a single FSM and minimal states: IDLE, RUN, DONE. Use registers to hold absolute values of dividend and divisor, and track their signs. Perform division over 8 cycles by shifting and subtracting divisor from remainder. At each step, shift remainder and quotient left, subtract divisor if possible, setting quotient bit accordingly.

On reset or new operation, load inputs and initialize registers. After 8 iterations, correct quotient and remainder signs if operation is signed. Set res_valid when done.

This approach avoids overcomplicated state and combinational logic, uses straightforward synchronous updates, and maintains the correct radix-2 division algorithm with sign handling. Result packs remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).
