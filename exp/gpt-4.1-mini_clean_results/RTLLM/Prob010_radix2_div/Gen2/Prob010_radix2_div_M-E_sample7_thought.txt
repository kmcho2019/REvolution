Implement a radix-2 divider using a simple iterative approach controlled by a busy flag rather than an explicit FSM. The module registers the inputs on opn_valid and then performs 8 division steps, one per clock cycle, driven by a 4-bit counter. The division is done on absolute values, using a 9-bit remainder register initialized by shifting the dividend left by 1. The divisor is negated for subtractive trial at each step. Quotient bits are built up by left-shifting and inserting a 1 or 0 based on the subtraction result's sign. After all steps, signed correction is applied to quotient and remainder if needed, using conditional two's complement transformations. The result concatenates the 8-bit remainder in the upper half and 8-bit quotient in the lower half. res_valid signals completion and result readiness. Busy flag prevents new input acceptance until current operation completes. This avoids explicit FSM states and local variable declaration in always blocks, focusing on clear sequential logic and signed handling at final stage.
