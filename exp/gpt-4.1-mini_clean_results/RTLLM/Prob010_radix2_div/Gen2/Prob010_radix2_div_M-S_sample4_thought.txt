Implement a radix-2 division by storing absolute values of dividend and divisor first, handle sign correction after division completes. Use a 17-bit shift register SR holding remainder (upper 9 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits). On each clock cycle, shift SR left by 1 bit, subtract divisor from remainder part; if no borrow, update remainder and set quotient bit to 1, else restore remainder and keep quotient bit 0. Iterate exactly 8 times. When done, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder and output result. Control operation start with opn_valid and produce res_valid when finished. Declare all internal signals at module level to avoid syntax errors and use a single sequential always block for clarity and simplicity.
