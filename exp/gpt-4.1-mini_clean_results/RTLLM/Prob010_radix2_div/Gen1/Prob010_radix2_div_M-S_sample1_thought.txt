Implement a straightforward radix-2 iterative divider supporting signed and unsigned 8-bit division. On opn_valid and if not busy, capture and take absolute values of dividend and divisor, track signs, and initialize a 9-bit shift register (SR) with dividend shifted left by one bit. Use a 4-bit counter counting from 1 to 8 to iterate the division steps. At each step, shift SR left and subtract divisor; if subtraction non-negative, set quotient bit to 1, else restore SR and set quotient bit 0. After 8 iterations, adjust remainder if negative by adding divisor back, then apply sign corrections to quotient and remainder for signed operations. Output concatenates remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits, and assert res_valid. Manage division-by-zero by outputting zero quotient and remainder equal to dividend. This approach avoids local variable declarations inside procedural blocks and keeps the design clean and synthesizable.
