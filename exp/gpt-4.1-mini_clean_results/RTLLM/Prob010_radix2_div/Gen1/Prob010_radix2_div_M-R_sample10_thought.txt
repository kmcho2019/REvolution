Refactor the radix-2 divider by implementing a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, RUN, DONE. Inputs are registered on start, and division iterates synchronously for 8 cycles. The shift register SR holds remainder and quotient bits. Subtraction is done combinationally each cycle to decide quotient bit, and SR is updated accordingly. On completion, sign correction is applied in a separate combinational block to avoid variable declarations inside procedural blocks. Result is output with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. The res_valid signal flags result availability. This FSM structure clarifies control flow and avoids syntax errors caused by improper local declarations or multiple assignments to the same register.
