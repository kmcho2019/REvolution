Implement a radix-2 divider for 8-bit operands supporting signed/unsigned division by explicitly managing the sign and magnitude of inputs at the start. Use a 17-bit shift register SR to hold (dividend_abs shifted left by 8 bits) combined with space for quotient bits. Each cycle, shift SR left by 1 and subtract the divisor_abs from the upper bits to decide the quotient bit: if subtraction is non-negative, keep the result and set quotient bit to 1, else restore previous and set quotient bit to 0. Repeat for 8 cycles. At the end, correct signs of quotient and remainder as needed and output remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits of a 16-bit result. Synchronize operation with clk and rst, manage operation valid and result valid signals for proper handshake.
