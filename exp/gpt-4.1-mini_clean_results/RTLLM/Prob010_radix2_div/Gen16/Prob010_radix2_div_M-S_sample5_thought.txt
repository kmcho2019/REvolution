Implement a radix-2 iterative divider handling 8-bit operands (signed/unsigned) over 8 clock cycles.

Key points:
- On opn_valid, latch inputs, extract signs if signed, convert operands to absolute values.
- Use a 17-bit shift register (SR) to hold remainder and quotient during division:
  SR = {remainder[7:0], quotient[8:0]} initially dividend_abs shifted left by 1.
- NEG_DIVISOR = two's complement of divisor_abs.
- Each cycle:
  - Subtract divisor_abs from the remainder portion (upper bits of SR).
  - If subtraction is >=0 (no borrow), shift left SR and set LSB of quotient = 1.
  - Else shift left SR with quotient bit = 0.
- Repeat for 8 cycles; then sign-correct quotient and remainder if signed.
- Output {remainder[7:0], quotient[7:0]} in a 16-bit result.
- res_valid asserted when division done.
- Synchronous reset initializes all states.

This approach keeps logic simple, uses just one shift register for remainder+quotient, and minimal auxiliary registers.
