This updated design follows the classical radix-2 restoring division:  
- Remainder and quotient start at 0.  
- Dividend bits shift into remainder MSB each cycle.  
- Subtract divisor, if positive remainder then quotient bit=1 else restore remainder and quotient bit=0.  
- Repeat 8 times.

The output contains remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits) after sign correction.
