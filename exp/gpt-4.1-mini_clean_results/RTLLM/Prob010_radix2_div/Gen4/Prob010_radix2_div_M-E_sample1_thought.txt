Implement the radix-2 division using a three-stage synchronous approach controlled by a small finite state machine (FSM) with states: IDLE, CALC, and FINISH. On receiving opn_valid, latch inputs and handle signed division by taking absolute values and saving sign flags. Use a 17-bit shift register combining a 9-bit remainder (initially the dividend shifted left by one) and 8-bit quotient (starting at zero). Each clock cycle in CALC shifts the register left by 1, subtracts the divisor from the remainder portion, and sets the quotient bit based on the subtraction result. After 8 iterations, enter FINISH to restore signs on the quotient and remainder if needed and present the final 16-bit result (remainder upper 8 bits, quotient lower 8 bits). The design handles unsigned or signed division gracefully, uses clear and compact logic for the subtraction and updating of SR, and signals res_valid when output is ready.
