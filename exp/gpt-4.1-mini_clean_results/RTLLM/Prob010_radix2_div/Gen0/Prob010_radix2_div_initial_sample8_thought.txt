Implement a radix-2 iterative divider that supports both signed and unsigned 8-bit division. On opn_valid and if not already busy (res_valid low), it captures and prepares absolute values of dividend and divisor, also storing their signs for result correction. It initializes a shift register (SR) combining remainder and quotient space and a counter to track iterations. In each clock cycle during division, the module shifts left and subtracts the divisor or adds back based on borrow, updating quotient bits. After 8 iterations, it finishes division, applies sign correction to quotient and remainder if signed operation, and asserts res_valid. On reset or new operation start, res_valid and internal states are reset accordingly. The output is a 16-bit result with remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).
