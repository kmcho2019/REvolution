Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed and unsigned integers. On opn_valid and when not busy, latch inputs and convert to absolute values if signed. Initialize the shift register SR with dividend shifted left by 1 bit, NEG_DIVISOR with the negation of absolute divisor, and start the division loop with a counter. At each cycle, perform a subtraction step using NEG_DIVISOR, update SR accordingly, increment counter, and after 8 steps finish. After division, adjust quotient and remainder sign if signed operation. Set res_valid when result is ready. Reset clears all state. The result packs remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.
