Implement a radix-2 division algorithm for 8-bit signed or unsigned integers using a synchronous sequential circuit. On receiving a valid operation (opn_valid), latch the inputs, compute absolute values if signed, initialize the shift register SR with the dividend (shifted left by 1), negate the divisor (absolute) to form NEG_DIVISOR. A counter runs for 8 cycles performing the division step by step: subtract NEG_DIVISOR from the upper 9 bits of SR, update SR by shifting left and inserting the subtraction carry-out bit to form the quotient bits. After 8 cycles, finalize the quotient and remainder, adjusting the signs based on the original sign inputs. The output result packs remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits). The res_valid signal signals when the result is ready.
