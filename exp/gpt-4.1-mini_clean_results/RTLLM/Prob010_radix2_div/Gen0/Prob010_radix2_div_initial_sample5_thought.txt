Implement a radix-2 division algorithm for 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, supporting synchronous operation with a clock and reset. When a valid operation is signaled, the module stores the absolute values of dividend and divisor, prepares the shift register (SR), negated divisor, and control signals. Then the division proceeds bit-by-bit for 8 cycles. Each cycle shifts SR left by 1, tries to subtract divisor, and decides quotient bit based on carry-out. After finishing, it outputs the combined remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits), restoring sign if signed operation was selected. The module also asserts res_valid when the result is ready and waits for the next opn_valid to start anew.
