Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider supporting 8-bit signed/unsigned division. The module will start division on opn_valid when not busy (res_valid low). Inputs are registered on start. For signed division, input operands are converted to absolute values and sign of quotient is determined by XOR of input signs. The division iterates for 8 cycles, shifting the dividend and subtracting the divisor (in negated form) to decide quotient bits. The shift register holds remainder and quotient bits during operation. After completion, the quotient sign is adjusted. The final 16-bit result packs remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits). The res_valid flag indicates completion and result readiness. Reset clears all internal states.
