Implement a synchronous radix-2 division unit for 8-bit operands, supporting signed and unsigned division. On opn_valid and when res_valid is low, capture inputs and start the division. Use a shift register SR initialized with the absolute dividend shifted left by one bit. Calculate NEG_DIVISOR as the negated absolute divisor. Use a counter to perform 8 iterative subtraction and shifts to generate quotient and remainder. After completion, sign-correct the quotient and remainder if the operation is signed. Provide res_valid signal when the result is ready. The output packs the remainder in the upper 8 bits and quotient in the lower 8 bits.
