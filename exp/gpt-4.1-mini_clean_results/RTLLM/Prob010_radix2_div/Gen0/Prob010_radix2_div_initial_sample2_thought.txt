Implement a radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed or unsigned division by performing iterative subtraction and shift operations synchronized on clock edges. On a valid operation input (opn_valid), capture inputs, convert signed inputs to absolute values if sign bit is set, and initialize internal registers including a shift register (SR) and counter. Use a negated divisor for subtraction to decide if quotient bit is 1 or 0 during each iteration. After 8 iterations, output the quotient and remainder, reapplying the correct sign to quotient and remainder as needed. Manage res_valid to indicate when result is ready and can be read. Reset initializes all states. The 16-bit result has remainder (8-bit) in upper bits and quotient (8-bit) in lower bits.
