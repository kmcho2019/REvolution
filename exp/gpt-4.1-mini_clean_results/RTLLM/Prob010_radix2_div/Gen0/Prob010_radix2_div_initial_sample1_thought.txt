Implement a synchronous radix-2 division algorithm for 8-bit dividend and divisor supporting signed and unsigned operations. On opn_valid, latch inputs and convert to absolute values if signed. Initialize shift register SR with dividend shifted left 1 bit (9 bits), NEG_DIVISOR with two's complement of divisor absolute value, and start the division process. On each clock cycle, subtract NEG_DIVISOR from upper bits of SR, update SR based on the borrow (carry-out), and increment counter cnt. When cnt reaches 8, finalize the result with quotient and remainder, and apply sign corrections if needed. Manage res_valid to indicate when result is ready. Provide quotient in lower 8 bits of result, remainder in upper 8 bits.
