Implement a radix-2 division algorithm that supports both signed and unsigned 8-bit division. On opn_valid, load dividend and divisor, convert to absolute values if signed, then start the iterative division process with a counter running 8 cycles. Use a shift register SR initialized with dividend shifted left 1 bit, NEG_DIVISOR as the two's complement of divisor's absolute value. Each cycle, subtract NEG_DIVISOR from the high bits of SR and use the carry-out to decide the next SR update. After 8 cycles, finalize quotient and remainder, restore sign if signed, and assert res_valid. Handle reset and result consumption properly.
