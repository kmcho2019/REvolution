Refactor the radix-2 division module by using an explicit FSM with defined states to manage the division process, which clarifies control flow and separates combinational and sequential logic cleanly. 

Use registers to hold the dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder, and keep track of the iteration with a counter. On opn_valid, capture inputs and start the process. Each clock cycle, perform one step of the division: shift left the remainder and quotient, subtract the divisor if possible, and update quotient bit accordingly.

Manage the signedness separately by storing sign bits and taking absolute values at start. After division completes, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder.

Use only synchronous always blocks for state and data updates. The result is output when ready, with res_valid indicating completion.

This approach simplifies debugging and ensures clean separation of operations while preserving the radix-2 algorithm and requirements.
