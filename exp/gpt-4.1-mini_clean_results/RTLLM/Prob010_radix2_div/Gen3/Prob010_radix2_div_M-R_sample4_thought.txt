Refactor the divider as a finite state machine (FSM) with explicit states IDLE, DIVIDE, DONE, to clarify control flow and separate combinational and sequential logic.

Key points:
- On opn_valid, capture inputs and prepare absolute values and signs.
- Use a 17-bit shift register SR to hold {remainder[8:0], quotient[7:0]}.
- Perform one subtraction per clock cycle:
  * Shift SR left by 1
  * Subtract divisor_abs from upper bits
  * Update SR based on borrow (quotient bit)
- Use combinational logic outside always blocks for subtraction.
- After 8 iterations, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder.
- res_valid is asserted when DONE state is reached and cleared on start of new operation.
- All registers declared at module level, no declarations inside always blocks.
This approach avoids illegal declarations inside always blocks, separates state and data path logic, and makes timing and control explicit.
