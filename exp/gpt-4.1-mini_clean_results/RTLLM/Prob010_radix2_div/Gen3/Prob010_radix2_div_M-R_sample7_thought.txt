Refactor the radix-2 divider as a finite state machine with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, DONE. On opn_valid in IDLE, capture and preprocess inputs (compute absolute values if signed) and initialize registers. During DIVIDE state, perform iterative subtraction and shift operations to calculate quotient and remainder, counting cycles until division completes. On DONE, output the result with sign corrections if signed division, set res_valid high, and wait for acknowledgment (opn_valid low) before returning to IDLE. This approach clarifies control flow, separates datapath and control logic, and uses only synchronous always blocks with registered outputs. It avoids combinational assigns for internal logic and manages res_valid cleanly without conflict.
