   Use a 17-bit shift register:  
   - Upper 9 bits: remainder (to accommodate subtraction and sign bit)

   Each cycle, left-shift the shift register by 1 bit, so the remainder and quotient shift left together, then subtract the divisor from the remainder trial (upper 9 bits).  
   - If subtraction is non-negative (no borrow), update the remainder in shift_reg to subtraction result and set the LSB of quotient to 1.  
   - Else keep the remainder as is and set quotient bit to 0.  
   
   This means the shift_reg is updated with:  
   `{next_remainder[8:0], next_quotient[7:0]}` after shifting and setting the quotient bit.

5. **Sign Handling**:
