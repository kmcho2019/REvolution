Implement a synchronous radix-2 divider with a small state machine controlling the division process. The module handles signed and unsigned division by first extracting absolute values of inputs and sign flags, then performing 8 iterations of subtraction and shifting on a 17-bit shift register which contains the partial remainder and quotient.

Key differences in this design:
- Use an explicit FSM with states IDLE, DIVIDE, DONE for better clarity.
- Separate registers for quotient and remainder during computation.
- After the division is complete, adjust quotient and remainder signs according to input signs.
- When opn_valid is asserted and the module is idle, inputs are latched and division starts.
- Output res_valid is asserted only when the result is ready.
- Handles division by zero by outputting zero quotient and dividend as remainder.
- The remainder is placed in upper 8 bits, quotient in lower 8 bits of the 16-bit result.

This approach makes control flow explicit, eases debug and clarity while meeting the specification.
