Implement the radix-2 divider as a synchronous finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE. When `opn_valid` is high at IDLE, latch inputs and calculate absolute values for signed mode, also record the original signs. Initialize a 17-bit shift register SR (dividend_abs shifted left by 8 bits) to hold remainder and partial quotient. The divisor absolute value is stored, and a 4-bit counter counts from 0 to 7. Each clock cycle in DIVIDE performs a subtraction trial by subtracting the divisor_abs shifted appropriately from the upper bits of SR. If subtraction succeeds (non-negative), update SR by replacing the upper bits with the subtraction result and shift left, setting the least significant bit of quotient to 1. Otherwise, shift left inserting 0 as quotient bit. After 8 iterations, apply sign correction to quotient and remainder if signed mode, then output result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits, and assert `res_valid`. This approach uses a single shift register for the combined remainder and quotient and follows classic restoring division principles with straightforward FSM control.
