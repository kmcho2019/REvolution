Refactor the radix-2 divider by introducing a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, RUNNING, DONE. This improves clarity and control flow:

- Use a state register to track the division progress.
- On IDLE and opn_valid, latch inputs, compute absolute values, determine signs, initialize shift register and counters, and move to RUNNING.
- In RUNNING, perform one iteration per clock: subtract divisor, update shift register accordingly, increment counter.
- When 8 iterations complete, move to DONE and apply sign correction to quotient and remainder.
- In DONE, assert res_valid until next opn_valid triggers new operation and returns to IDLE.
- Use mostly combinational logic for sign handling and next state transitions, and synchronous registers for data and control.
- This FSM structure clarifies operation start, iterative division, and result output, enhancing readability and maintainability.
