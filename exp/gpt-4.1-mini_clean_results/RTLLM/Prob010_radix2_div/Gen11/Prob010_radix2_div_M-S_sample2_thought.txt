Implement a straightforward radix-2 divider using a simple synchronous process with a 4-bit counter from 0 to 8 cycles.  
At start (opn_valid & !res_valid), latch inputs and convert to unsigned magnitude if signed. Initialize the 17-bit shift register SR with the dividend magnitude shifted left (remainder bits zero).  
In each cycle, shift SR left by one, subtract divisor magnitude from the remainder part, and update remainder and quotient bits based on subtraction borrow.  
After 8 iterations, sign-correct the quotient and remainder if signed operation, then set res_valid and output the combined 16-bit {remainder, quotient}.  
Reset clears all registers and output signals.  
Keep the design minimal by avoiding an explicit FSM and using a simple count-based control, handling division sequentially with clear, compact logic.
