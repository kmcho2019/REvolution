Implement a classic radix-2 restoring division algorithm with a simple FSM that includes three states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE.  
When opn_valid is asserted, latch the inputs and prepare for division: convert inputs to absolute values and save their sign info.  
Use a 17-bit shift register to hold the remainder in upper 9 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.  
At each division step, shift left by one and attempt to subtract the divisor.  
If subtraction is successful (no borrow), set quotient bit to 1; otherwise, restore the remainder.  
Count 8 iterations for 8-bit division.  
At the end, restore sign for quotient and remainder as per signed division rules.  
Output the 16-bit result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits.  
Use a single always block for sequential logic and combinational logic for next state and next SR update.  
Keep res_valid high only in DONE state to indicate result ready.  
This approach emphasizes simplicity, easy timing closure, and clear data path separation.
