Implement a radix-2 division for 8-bit inputs supporting signed and unsigned division. On receiving opn_valid while idle, capture inputs and compute absolute values and signs. Initialize a shift register (SR) with dividend absolute shifted left by 1 bit, prepare a negated divisor for subtraction, and begin an 8-step iterative division process synchronized on clk. During each step, subtract divisor from the remainder; if non-negative, shift in quotient bit '1', else '0'. After 8 steps, correct quotient and remainder signs if signed operation. Output 16-bit result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits and raise res_valid. Keep logic straightforward without a formal FSM by controlling states with a simple counter and flags.
