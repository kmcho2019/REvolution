Implement a simple radix-2 divider using a shift-and-subtract algorithm over 8 cycles. On opn_valid, capture inputs and compute absolute values if signed. Use a 17-bit shift register where upper 9 bits hold the remainder and lower 8 bits hold the quotient bits. At each clock cycle during division, shift left, subtract divisor, and set quotient bit accordingly. After 8 iterations, correct the sign of quotient and remainder if signed division is selected. Output remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits with res_valid asserted. Keep the design flat and synchronous with a simple counter-driven process, no FSM states needed.
