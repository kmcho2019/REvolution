Implement a simplified radix-2 divider supporting signed and unsigned 8-bit inputs and outputs a 16-bit result with remainder and quotient. The division is iterative over 8 cycles after capturing inputs when opn_valid is asserted. The algorithm stores absolute values, tracks sign bits for correction, and iteratively shifts and subtracts the divisor from the partial remainder.

Simplifications:
- Use a single FSM state variable with two states: IDLE and RUN.
- Use a simple counter from 0 to 8 for iterations.
- Store dividend and divisor absolute values and signs at the start.
- At each iteration, shift the remainder+quotient left, try to subtract divisor, set quotient bit accordingly.
- Handle divisor=0 by outputting zero quotient and full dividend as remainder immediately.
- Apply sign correction only once after finishing iterations.
- Manage res_valid signal directly with state and counter.
- Keep all logic in sequential always block except combinational subtraction to simplify understanding.
- No separate start signals or extra handshaking signals.
- Clear and reset logic to avoid glitches.
