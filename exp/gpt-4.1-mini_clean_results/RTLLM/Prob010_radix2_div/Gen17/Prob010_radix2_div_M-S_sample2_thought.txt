Simplify the radix-2 division by focusing on the core restoring division algorithm without an explicit FSM. Use a simple counter and state flags to control the process. Handle sign correction upfront by converting operands to absolute values, and adjust the final quotient and remainder signs at the end. The remainder register is 9 bits to hold shifted bits, and dividend bits are shifted in one by one. Each cycle performs subtraction of divisor from remainder; if positive or zero, quotient bit is set to 1, otherwise remainder is restored and quotient bit is 0. After 8 cycles, output the combined remainder (upper 8 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits) with sign correction, and assert res_valid. The module supports synchronous operation with input handshake signal opn_valid and output res_valid.
