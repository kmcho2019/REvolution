Implement a radix-2 restoring division algorithm with 8-bit inputs and support for signed/unsigned operations. 

The key steps are:
- On opn_valid, latch inputs and prepare absolute values for signed operations.
- Use a 17-bit shift register SR to hold remainder (upper 9 bits) and quotient (lower 8 bits).
- Iteratively shift and subtract divisor_abs (9 bits) from remainder part of SR, update quotient bit.
- After 8 iterations, correct signs of quotient and remainder if signed division.
- Output result as {remainder[7:0], quotient[7:0]} with res_valid asserted.
- On reset or new operation, reset internal states.

The design uses a simple FSM with two states (IDLE and DIVIDE). Division by zero outputs zero quotient and dividend as remainder immediately.

This simplification removes extraneous states, counters beyond 4 bits, and avoids complicated signed arithmetic by dealing only with absolute values internally and correcting sign at the end.
