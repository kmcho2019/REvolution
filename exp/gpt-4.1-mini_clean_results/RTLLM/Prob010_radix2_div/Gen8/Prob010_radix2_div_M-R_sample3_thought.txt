Refactor the radix-2 divider by implementing a simple finite state machine (FSM) with clearly defined states: IDLE, DIVIDE, and DONE. On receiving opn_valid, transition from IDLE to DIVIDE, saving inputs and initializing registers. During DIVIDE, perform iterative subtraction and shifts each clock cycle until 8 iterations complete, then move to DONE state where the signed correction is applied and the result is presented with res_valid asserted. Finally, wait for a new opn_valid to restart. This FSM approach improves readability and control flow clarity. Also, compute negated divisor combinationally each cycle, and handle division-by-zero gracefully by setting quotient and remainder to zero. The remainder is kept 9-bit internally for correct subtraction. The design uses synchronous resets and keeps result valid until a new operation arrives. This approach provides a clean modular and maintainable structure while preserving the original algorithm's radix-2 division logic.
