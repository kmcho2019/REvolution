To implement an odd frequency divider with parameter NUM_DIV (odd number), we use two separate counters and two clock dividers toggled on positive and negative edges respectively.

- cnt1 counts on the rising edge of clk.
- cnt2 counts on the falling edge of clk.

Each counter counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1 and resets to zero.

When cnt1 reaches HALF_COUNT (floor((NUM_DIV-1)/2)), clk_div1 toggles.
Similarly, when cnt2 reaches HALF_COUNT, clk_div2 toggles.

The final output clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, thus capturing toggles on both edges, effectively achieving frequency division by an odd number with near 50% duty cycle.

Active low asynchronous reset initializes counters and clock divider signals to zero.

This approach respects the original architectural specification and resolves timing and toggling issues by separating logic for posedge and negedge, avoiding glitches.

Parameter checking is retained to ensure NUM_DIV is odd and at least 3.
