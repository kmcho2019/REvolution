Instead of using two separate counters and toggling outputs on both edges independently, use a single counter clocked on the rising edge of the input clock that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. Generate two toggle points: one at the midpoint (half_count) and one at the terminal count (NUM_DIV-1). Toggle the output clock at these two points to produce a clock with an approximately 50% duty cycle. This approach uses a single counter and toggle register, simplifying timing and eliminating glitches from combining asynchronous signals.
