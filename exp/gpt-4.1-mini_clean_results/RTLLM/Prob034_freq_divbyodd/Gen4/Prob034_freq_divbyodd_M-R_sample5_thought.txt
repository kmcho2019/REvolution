Refactor the design by splitting the counting and toggling logic into two separate always blocks triggered on the posedge and negedge of clk, respectively. 

Each block will have its own counter (cnt1 for posedge, cnt2 for negedge), counting from 0 up to NUM_DIV -1. When each counter reaches the halfway point ((NUM_DIV - 1) / 2), the respective clock divider output (clk_div1 or clk_div2) toggles, creating two signals toggling at half intervals offset by half a clock cycle.

The final divided clock output clk_div is obtained by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively combining toggles from both clock edges to form a frequency division by an odd number with a near 50% duty cycle.

Reset asynchronously clears counters and clock dividers.

This approach matches the original requirement explicitly, is synthesizable, and avoids glitches by separating posedge and negedge logic clearly.
