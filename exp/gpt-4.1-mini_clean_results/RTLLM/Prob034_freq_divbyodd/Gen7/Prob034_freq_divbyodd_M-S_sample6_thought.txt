To simplify the design while correctly dividing by an odd number, use a single counter that counts clock edges up to NUM_DIV and toggles the output clock at the midpoint (half count). For an odd divisor:

- Count from 0 to NUM_DIV -1.
- Toggle the output clock at the halfway point (floor(NUM_DIV/2)).
- This ensures the output clock period is NUM_DIV input clock cycles, and toggling at half count provides a near 50% duty cycle.
- Use a single counter triggered on the positive edge of clk.
- Reset synchronously with active low rst_n.
- This approach avoids the complexity of two counters and ORing outputs, preventing glitches and ensuring clean division.

This is a classic odd divider implementation with a single counter and toggling at half period.
