Simplify the frequency divider by counting on the positive edge only, using a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. Toggle the output clock at the midpoint (half count) and at the counter wrap-around (end of count). This approach produces a divided clock with a duty cycle close to 50%, works well for odd NUM_DIV values, and avoids the complexity of dual-edge counters and separate toggles.

The output clock toggles twice per full count cycle, effectively dividing the frequency by NUM_DIV. Active low reset initializes the counter and output clock.

This simplification ensures correct division by any odd NUM_DIV (>=3), reduces hardware, and makes timing straightforward.
