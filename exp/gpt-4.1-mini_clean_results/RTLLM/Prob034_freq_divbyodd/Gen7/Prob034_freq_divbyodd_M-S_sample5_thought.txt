For frequency division by an odd number using both clock edges, a simpler approach is:

- Use a single counter that increments on each clock edge (both posedge and negedge) effectively counting at twice the input clock frequency.

- The counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and then wraps around.

- Toggle the output clk_div when the counter reaches halfway (floor(NUM_DIV/2)) and at the wrap-around point (NUM_DIV-1). This ensures two toggles per full period, generating the output frequency divided by NUM_DIV with near 50% duty cycle.

- This single-counter, double-edge counting approach avoids the complexity and possible misalignment of separate counters and separate clock domain toggling.

- Use an always block triggered by both posedge and negedge of clk to implement this counting.

- Reset synchronously on rst_n to initialize the counter and output.

- This simplifies the design, provides correct timing, and produces glitch-free clk_div output for any odd NUM_DIV parameter.

- Also include a compile-time check to ensure NUM_DIV is odd.
