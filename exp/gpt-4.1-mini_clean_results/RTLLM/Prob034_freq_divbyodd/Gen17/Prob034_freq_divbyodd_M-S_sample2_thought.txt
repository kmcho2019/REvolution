I will simplify the odd frequency divider by using a single counter and toggling the output clock at two specific counts, corresponding to the edges that approximate dividing by an odd number. Instead of using two separate counters and clock dividers for positive and negative edges, I will generate the divided clock by toggling a single output clock on the positive edge of the input clock whenever the counter reaches 0 or (NUM_DIV-1)/2.

This approach reduces complexity and maintains the correct division ratio, generating an output clock frequency equal to the input clock frequency divided by the odd number NUM_DIV.

The design includes:

- A parameter NUM_DIV that must be an odd number.
- A counter running from 0 to NUM_DIV-1.
- Toggling the clk_div output at two count values.
- Asynchronous active-low reset to initialize the counter and output clock.
- Synchronous logic only on the positive edge of clk.
