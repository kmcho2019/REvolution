To implement an odd frequency divider as specified, I propose a novel approach based on two independent counters running on opposite clock edges and toggling their respective divided clocks independently. Specifically:

- Use one counter (cnt_r) that increments on the rising edge of clk and toggles clk_div_r at count HALF_DIV.
- Use a second counter (cnt_f) that increments on the falling edge of clk and toggles clk_div_f at count HALF_DIV.
- Both counters reset on active low reset.
- The final output clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div_r and clk_div_f.
- This approach cleanly separates the logic for rising and falling edges without sharing a clock domain for both counters, avoiding complex edge detection.
- Counters count modulo NUM_DIV.
- The combination via OR merges the two clock halves to produce a frequency divided clock with a 50% approximate duty cycle.
- This strictly follows the original spec of two counters, two intermediate clocks, and a final OR output.
