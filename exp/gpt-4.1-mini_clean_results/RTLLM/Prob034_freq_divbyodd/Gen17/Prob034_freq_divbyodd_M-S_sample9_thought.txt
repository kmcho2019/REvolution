To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality:

- Use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive edge of clk.
- Toggle one flip-flop clk_div at count zero to generate a divided clock with frequency clk/(2*NUM_DIV).
- For odd divisors, generate the output clk_div by toggling clk_div at count zero, and combine it with a delayed version of the same clock edge to approximate a 50% duty cycle.
- To handle odd division, use a single flip-flop toggled every NUM_DIV counts, and generate clk_div by duplicating this toggling with a simple combinational OR of the current and delayed toggles to mimic the original output.
- Remove the dual edge flip-flop and dual counters, reducing the hardware and complexity.
- The reset initializes the counter and flip-flop synchronously on positive clk edge.
- Keep the parameter check for odd NUM_DIV.
- The output clk_div is generated by ORing clk_div and a delayed clk_div, which is a simpler way to emulate the original clk_div1|clk_div2 logic but only using positive clock domain.
