To simplify the odd frequency divider, use a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive edge of clk.

- Toggle the output clock clk_div exactly once per NUM_DIV input clock cycles, specifically at the midpoint count ((NUM_DIV-1)/2).
- This ensures that clk_div has a frequency of clk / NUM_DIV with a 50% duty cycle as close as possible for an odd divisor.
- Reset asynchronously initializes the counter and clk_div to 0.
- This approach eliminates the need for two separate counters and clock edges, simplifying the design while meeting the frequency division requirements for odd divisors.
