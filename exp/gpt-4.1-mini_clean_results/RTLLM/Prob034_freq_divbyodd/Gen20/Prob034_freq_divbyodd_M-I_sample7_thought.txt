To optimize the existing odd frequency divider for better PPA (power, performance, and area) without losing functional correctness, the following improvements are applied:

- Replace the asynchronous active-low reset with synchronous reset. Synchronous resets generally lead to better timing closure and simpler reset distribution in ASIC/FPGA flows.
- Reduce toggling and glitching by carefully gating the toggling logic, toggling only exactly at the transition counts (0 and HALF_DIV) without redundant toggles.
- Combine the toggling condition into one expression per clock domain to avoid duplicated evaluations.
- Use non-blocking assignments consistently for sequential logic.
- Optimize the logic for toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 by removing redundant conditions.
- The delayed counter is kept but updated only once per posedge clock to synchronize negedge domain, minimizing complexity.
- Add comments on synchronous reset and toggling conditions for clarity.
- Maintain the parameter oddness check.
- Minor code style cleanup (spacing, naming).
- Overall, these changes reduce dynamic power due to fewer toggling events and improve timing robustness by synchronous reset and simpler toggle conditions, while maintaining a nearly 50% duty cycle output.

These changes aim to yield improved timing and power metrics while keeping the same area and functionality.
