Refactor the frequency divider to use a single counter and generate both clk_div1 and clk_div2 combinationally based on counter values rather than explicitly toggling inside always blocks. This approach uses a synchronous counter incremented on the positive clock edge with asynchronous reset. Instead of using separate always blocks for clk_div1 and clk_div2, derive their values combinationally from the counter state, then register the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 into clk_div on the positive edge.

Key points of this refactor:
- Only one always block for the synchronous counter and clk_div register.
- clk_div1 and clk_div2 are generated by combinational comparison of the counter to thresholds.
- This avoids multi-clock domain complexity and potential metastability from using negedge triggered logic.
- Maintains the active-low asynchronous reset.
- Keeps the divide-by-odd logic intact by defining clk_div1 high for the first half of the count and clk_div2 high for the second half.
- The OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 yields the divided clock with approximately 50% duty cycle over an odd divisor.
- This approach simplifies timing and reduces the risk of glitches caused by clock domain crossing.
