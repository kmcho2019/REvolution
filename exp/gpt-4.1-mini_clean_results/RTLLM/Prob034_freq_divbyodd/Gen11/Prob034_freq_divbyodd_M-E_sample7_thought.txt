To accurately divide the input clock frequency by an odd number using the specified two-counter approach, the design employs two separate counters: one incremented on the positive edge (cnt1) and one on the negative edge (cnt2) of the input clock. 

Each counter counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1. When each counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), it toggles its respective divided clock (clk_div1 or clk_div2) and resets to zero. This ensures that clk_div1 toggles at the midpoint of the positive edge counts, and clk_div2 toggles at the midpoint of the negative edge counts.

Finally, clk_div is obtained by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. This merges the two half-frequency clocks staggered on positive and negative edges, producing a clock divided by the odd NUM_DIV with a more balanced duty cycle.

Reset initializes counters and divided clocks. The design strictly enforces an odd divisor via a runtime check. All counters and toggles are synchronous with the clock edges they observe, and asynchronous active-low reset is handled properly.

This architecture fulfills the requirement to use two counters, two divided clocks on different clock edges, and combine them logically to generate the output clock divided by an odd number.
