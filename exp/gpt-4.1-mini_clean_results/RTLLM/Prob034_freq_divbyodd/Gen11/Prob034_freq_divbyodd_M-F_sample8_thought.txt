To fix the clock division by an odd number, both counters should count from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and toggle their respective clk_div signals exactly once per cycle at the same relative count value: the half count (floor(NUM_DIV/2)).

Specifically:
- On the positive edge of clk, cnt1 increments and when it reaches HALF_COUNT, clk_div1 toggles. cnt1 resets after NUM_DIV-1.
- On the negative edge of clk, cnt2 increments similarly and toggles clk_div2 also at HALF_COUNT, then resets after NUM_DIV-1.

This symmetrical toggling ensures that clk_div1 and clk_div2 pulses are staggered and combined to generate a nearly 50% duty cycle divided clock signal when OR'ed together.

The counters reset after counting up to NUM_DIV-1 to keep the full period consistent.

The output clk_div is assigned as the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, combining positive and negative edge toggles.

This design matches the requirement to divide the input frequency by an odd number with good duty cycle, and resolves the prior mismatches and timing inconsistencies.

Reset asynchronously initializes all counters and outputs to zero.
