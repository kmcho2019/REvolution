To meet the original requirement precisely, I will implement the frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV using two separate counters and two divided clocks clk_div1 and clk_div2 driven by the rising edge of clk. The two counters cnt1 and cnt2 will count from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 separately. clk_div1 toggles when cnt1 reaches the midpoint ((NUM_DIV-1)/2), and clk_div2 toggles when cnt2 reaches the end (NUM_DIV-1).

By OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, the output clk_div will have a frequency divided by NUM_DIV, with edges distributed between the positive and negative halves of the input clock, producing an approximately 50% duty cycle output even for odd divisors.

The counters and clk_div outputs are reset asynchronously by the active-low reset rst_n.

This approach strictly follows the problem specification and produces a clean, odd-frequency division with near 50% duty cycle output.
