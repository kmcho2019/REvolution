To improve the PPA of the original odd-frequency divider design, I will focus on:

- Removing the negedge clock domain to simplify timing closure and reduce power consumption. Instead, I will generate both toggling signals on the positive clock edge, using a combinational signal to approximate the half-period delay.

- Use a single counter and two registers (clk_div1, clk_div2) toggled at different counts on the positive edge to emulate the two-edge toggling logic, thus eliminating the need for clock domain crossing and reducing flip-flops.

- Generate clk_div2 by delaying the toggle by one clock cycle, implementing the effect of negedge toggling without actual negedge logic.

- Maintain the OR combination to produce clk_div.

- Keep the asynchronous active-low reset.

- Retain the compile-time odd number check.

This approach reduces logic complexity, removes negedge flip-flops, and uses only positive edge clocking to improve timing and power while preserving the desired odd division functionality.
