To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will use two separate counters (cnt1 and cnt2) running on the positive and negative edges of clk respectively, each counting up to NUM_DIV-1. When cnt1 reaches half the division count, clk_div1 toggles; similarly, clk_div2 toggles on cnt2 reaching half. Finally, clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 to cover both clock edges. This approach directly implements the odd frequency division using two counters and toggling signals without delay registers or complex synchronization logic, reducing the complexity and improving clarity. Active low reset initializes all registers to zero.
