To implement an odd-frequency divider using two counters and two clock dividers for positive and negative edges, the idea is to have two separate counters: cnt1 increments on the rising edge of clk, and cnt2 increments on the falling edge. Both count modulo NUM_DIV. Each clk_div signal toggles when its corresponding counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (rounded down). The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively merging toggles from both edges to produce the divided clock with a nearly 50% duty cycle. Resetting clears counters and output dividers. This dual-edge counting method ensures proper timing and balanced output for odd division ratios.
