To simplify the odd frequency divider, use a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. Toggle the output clock when the counter reaches the midpoint (half_count), where half_count = (NUM_DIV-1)/2, ensuring a 50% duty cycle approximation for odd divisors. The output clock toggles once per cycle, producing the desired divided clock frequency. This approach avoids the complexity of dual counters and edge-based toggling, and still produces a clean odd division with minimal logic.

Ensure parameter NUM_DIV is an odd number >=3. Use synchronous reset to initialize the counter and output clock.

This simplified design uses only one always block triggered on posedge clk, reduces registers, and creates the divided clock with one toggling signal.
