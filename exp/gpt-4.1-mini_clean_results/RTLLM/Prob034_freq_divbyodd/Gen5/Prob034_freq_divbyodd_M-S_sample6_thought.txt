To simplify the odd frequency divider, I will use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the rising edge of clk. The output clock toggles at the counter reaching half the divisor (HALF_DIV), producing a divided clock with frequency clk/NUM_DIV.

This approach eliminates the need for two counters and two separate toggling signals and the OR operation, reducing complexity and potential glitches.

Since NUM_DIV is odd, toggling at HALF_DIV creates the appropriate duty cycle and frequency division.

Reset initializes the counter and output clock synchronously.

This is a straightforward, clean, and reliable method for odd frequency division.
