To simplify the frequency division by an odd number, use a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive edge of the input clock. Toggle the output clock at the midpoint of the count (half_count) and at the terminal count (NUM_DIV-1). This approach ensures a correct frequency division with a near 50% duty cycle for odd divisors. Active low reset initializes counter and output clock. This method removes the need for dual counters and separate posedge/negedge logic while achieving the requested functionality.
