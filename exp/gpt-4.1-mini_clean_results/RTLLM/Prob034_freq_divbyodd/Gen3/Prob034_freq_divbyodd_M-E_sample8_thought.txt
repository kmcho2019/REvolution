A reliable way to implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV (odd) is to use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and generate the output clock with a 50% duty cycle by toggling the output clock at precise counts.

The key is to toggle the output clock twice per complete cycle: once at count = half_count and once at count = NUM_DIV - 1, where half_count = (NUM_DIV - 1)/2.

By toggling the output at these two points within one cycle, the output clock period is NUM_DIV input clocks, and its duty cycle is very close to 50%.

Implementation details:

- Use a synchronous counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1.
- At count = half_count, toggle clk_div.
- At count = NUM_DIV - 1, toggle clk_div again and reset the counter to 0.
- Active low reset initializes counter and clk_div to 0.
- This method yields a stable odd-frequency divided clock with good duty cycle without using two edge counters or ORing signals.
- The design is simple, synchronous, and easy to verify.
