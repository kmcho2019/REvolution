Use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, each incremented on the rising and falling edges of the input clock respectively, both counting modulo NUM_DIV. Each counter toggles its corresponding clock divider output (clk_div1 or clk_div2) when it reaches the midpoint count (half_count). Then OR clk_div1 and clk_div2 to produce clk_div. This method splits the division logic into two halves on opposite clock edges, ensuring a nearly 50% duty cycle for odd divisors with simpler logic. Reset initializes counters and dividers.
