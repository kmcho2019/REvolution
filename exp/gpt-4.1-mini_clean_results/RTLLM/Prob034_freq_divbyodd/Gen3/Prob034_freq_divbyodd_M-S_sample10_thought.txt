To implement an odd frequency divider with a 50% duty cycle, we can use a single counter and toggle the output clock twice per input clock period: once on the rising edge and once on the falling edge.

By counting clock edges instead of clock cycles, and toggling clk_div at counts corresponding to half and full divisor intervals, we effectively divide the frequency by NUM_DIV (odd).

Implementation uses a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV - 1, incremented at every clock edge (both posedge and negedge) using a dual-edge clocking technique (via clocked processes or an internal signal).

To keep design synthesizable and simple, we double the clock domain internally by using two clock enables, one active on posedge and another on negedge, and update the counter and clk_div accordingly.

This avoids multiple counters and complex logic, while achieving the required odd division with near 50% duty cycle.

Reset initializes all registers. The code assumes NUM_DIV >= 3 and odd.
