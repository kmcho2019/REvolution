The key issue is that toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 twice per full counting cycle (at HALF_COUNT and at NUM_DIV - 1) causes the output clock to toggle twice per period, doubling the frequency instead of dividing it by the desired odd number.

To fix this, each counter should toggle its corresponding clk_div signal only once per cycle, either at HALF_COUNT or at NUM_DIV - 1, but not both. Since NUM_DIV is odd, the half count is fractional (floor(NUM_DIV/2)), so toggling at HALF_COUNT on the positive edge and at NUM_DIV - 1 on the negative edge (or vice versa) divides the clock frequency correctly with better duty cycle approximation.

Implement the toggle on cnt1 reaching HALF_COUNT on the positive edge clock, and toggle clk_div1; for cnt2, toggle clk_div2 when cnt2 reaches NUM_DIV - 1 on the negative edge clock. Each counter resets to zero after reaching NUM_DIV - 1.

Finally, OR clk_div1 and clk_div2 to produce clk_div output.

This approach ensures clk_div toggles once every NUM_DIV input clock cycles, producing a frequency divided by the odd number with improved duty cycle.
