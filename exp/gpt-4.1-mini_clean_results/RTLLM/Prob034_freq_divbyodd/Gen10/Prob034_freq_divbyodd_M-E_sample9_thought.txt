I will implement the frequency divider for an odd divisor NUM_DIV using a single counter and a state machine that generates a clock output with a duty cycle close to 50%. 

The main idea is:

- Use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive edge.
- Use a state flip-flop clk_div toggled at two specific counts to create the output clock edges.
- The toggling points are chosen such that the output clock has a high time and low time differing by only one clock cycle, enabling an almost 50% duty cycle even for odd division.
- Specifically, toggle clk_div at counts (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1.
- Reset initializes the counter and output clock.
- This method requires only one always block and no logic on the negative edge, simplifying timing and power.
- The output clk_div frequency is clk frequency divided by NUM_DIV, suitable for any odd NUM_DIV.
- A compile-time check ensures NUM_DIV is odd.
