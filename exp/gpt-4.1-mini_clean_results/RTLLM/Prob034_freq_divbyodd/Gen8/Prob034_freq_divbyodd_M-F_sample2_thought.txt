To implement a frequency divider by an odd number using two counters and two clock dividers as specified:

- Use two separate counters: cnt1 increments on the positive edge of clk, and cnt2 increments on the negative edge of clk. Both count modulo NUM_DIV.

- When cnt1 reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, toggle clk_div1. This toggle divides the frequency by NUM_DIV on the positive clock edges.

- When cnt2 reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, toggle clk_div2. This toggle divides the frequency by NUM_DIV on the negative clock edges.

- The final divided clock, clk_div, is generated by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. Since toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 occurs offset by half the division count, their OR creates a clock with frequency clk/NUM_DIV and improved 50% duty cycle.

- Reset (active low rst_n) initializes counters and internal clock dividers.

- Compile-time check enforces NUM_DIV is odd.

This approach respects the original requirement, separates the logic between posedge and negedge domains, and uses the OR to combine divided clocks correctly, ensuring a glitch-free and correct frequency division by an odd number.
