To simplify the frequency divider by an odd number, I will use a single counter running on the positive edge of the clock. 

The idea:
- Count from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1 modulo.
- Generate the divided clock output by toggling it at count values corresponding to the mid-point and zero count.
- Specifically, toggle clk_div at count zero and at the halfway point (floor(NUM_DIV/2)).
- This single-edge approach reduces complexity by avoiding dual counters and dual always blocks.
- It maintains a 50% duty cycle as close as possible given odd division.
- Asynchronous reset clears the counter and clk_div.
- Also keep the compile-time oddness check to ensure NUM_DIV is odd.

This approach simplifies the design while preserving functionality, reducing area and power, and easing timing closure.
