Use a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive edge of clk. Toggle clk_div at count 0 to generate a clock with half period NUM_DIV cycles. For odd NUM_DIV, this yields a divided clock with frequency clk/(NUM_DIV) and ~50% duty cycle approximated by toggling at counter wrap. Reset initializes counter and clk_div output. This avoids dual counters and negative edge logic, simplifying implementation while maintaining the odd division functionality.
