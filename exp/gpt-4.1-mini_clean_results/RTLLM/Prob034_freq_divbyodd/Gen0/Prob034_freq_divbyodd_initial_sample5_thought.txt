Implement a frequency divider for odd divisors using two counters that count up to NUM_DIV - 1, one triggered on the positive edge of clk (cnt1), and the other on the negative edge of clk (cnt2). Each counter toggles its respective divided clock (clk_div1 or clk_div2) when reaching half of NUM_DIV. The output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively creating a clock with a frequency divided by an odd number. The reset is active low and initializes counters and outputs.
