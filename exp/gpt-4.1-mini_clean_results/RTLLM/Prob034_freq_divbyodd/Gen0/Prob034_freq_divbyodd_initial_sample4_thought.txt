Implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV (default 5). Use two counters: cnt1 increments on the rising edge of clk, cnt2 increments on the falling edge. When cnt1 reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, toggle clk_div1 and reset cnt1; similarly for cnt2 and clk_div2 on the falling edge. The final clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 to combine edges and generate a 50% duty cycle output at the divided frequency. Asynchronous active-low reset rst_n clears counters and clock dividers.
