Implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV (default 5) using two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, which count input clock edges. cnt1 is incremented on the rising edge, cnt2 on the falling edge. When each counter reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2, toggle clk_div1 or clk_div2 respectively and reset the counter. The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, producing a clock divided by the odd number. Asynchronous active-low reset initializes counters and clock outputs.
