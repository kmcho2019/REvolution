To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock by an odd number (NUM_DIV), we use two counters: cnt1 driven by the positive edge of clk, and cnt2 driven by the negative edge of clk. Each counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding clock divider output toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of these two divided clocks, effectively generating a frequency divided by NUM_DIV with a 50% duty cycle. The reset (rst_n) initializes counters and outputs to zero.
