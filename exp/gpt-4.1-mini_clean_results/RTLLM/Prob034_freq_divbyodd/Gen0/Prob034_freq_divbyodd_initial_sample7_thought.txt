To create a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) to handle the rising and falling edges separately. Each counter increments on its respective clock edge and toggles clk_div1 or clk_div2 when it reaches (NUM_DIV - 1)/2. This way, both clk_div1 and clk_div2 have half-period timing offsets on rising and falling edges of the input clock, and the final output clk_div is the OR of these two signals to achieve the desired odd division. Reset logic initializes all counters and outputs.
