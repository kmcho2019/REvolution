To implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV, we will use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 to track counts on positive and negative edges of the input clock respectively. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. When a counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock toggles. Since NUM_DIV is odd, toggling these two clocks on positive and negative edges and then OR-ing them gives a divided clock output with a 50% duty cycle. The reset signal rst_n initializes counters and clk_div outputs. The final output clk_div is clk_div1 OR clk_div2.
