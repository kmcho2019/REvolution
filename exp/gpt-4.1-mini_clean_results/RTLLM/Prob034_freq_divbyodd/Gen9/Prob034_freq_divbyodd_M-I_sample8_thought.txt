The previous implementation toggled clk_div1 and clk_div2 twice per cycle: once at the half-count and once at the full-count, which introduced extra edges and incorrect duty cycles. To fix this, each clk_div signal should only toggle once per complete count cycle. We can achieve this by toggling clk_div1 at half count on the positive edge and toggling clk_div2 at half count on the negative edge, with counters reset after reaching NUM_DIV-1. This produces two toggling edges spaced evenly to form a clean odd division with a more balanced duty cycle when OR-ed together.

Improvement strategy:
- On posedge clk: increment cnt1; toggle clk_div1 only when cnt1 reaches HALF; reset cnt1 after NUM_DIV-1.
- On negedge clk: increment cnt2; toggle clk_div2 only when cnt2 reaches HALF; reset cnt2 after NUM_DIV-1.
- No toggling at full count; full count only resets the counters.
- This guarantees clk_div1 and clk_div2 toggle once each per division cycle, offset by half the period.
- OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 yields a clean divided clock with frequency clk/NUM_DIV and improved 50% duty cycle.
- Retain reset logic and compile-time check for odd NUM_DIV.

This resolves previous timing and glitch issues and produces a proper frequency divider by an odd number.
