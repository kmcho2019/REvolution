To simplify the original dual-counter, dual-clock-divider approach while still supporting odd division, I will use a single counter and generate two toggle signals: one toggled on the rising edge and one on the falling edge of the input clock. 

The main idea:
- Use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1.
- On the rising edge, increment the counter and toggle clk_div1 when counter reaches the half count.
- On the falling edge, toggle clk_div2 when counter reaches zero.
- Combine clk_div1 and clk_div2 using OR to produce clk_div.
- This approach simulates the dual-edge counting with only one counter, simplifying the design.
- Include asynchronous reset to initialize counters and outputs.
- Ensure NUM_DIV is odd by compile-time check.

This reduces hardware by having one counter and two toggle registers without separate counters for rising and falling edges, still giving an approximate 50% duty cycle frequency divided clock for odd NUM_DIV.
