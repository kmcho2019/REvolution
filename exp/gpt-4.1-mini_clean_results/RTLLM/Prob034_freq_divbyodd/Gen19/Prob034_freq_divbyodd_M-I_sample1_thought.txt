Implement the frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV using two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) operating on positive and negative edges of clk, respectively.

- cnt1 increments on posedge clk; cnt2 increments on negedge clk.
- Both counters count modulo NUM_DIV.
- When cnt1 reaches HALF_DIV (NUM_DIV >> 1), toggle clk_div1.
- When cnt2 reaches HALF_DIV, toggle clk_div2.
- The final output clk_div = clk_div1 | clk_div2, capturing toggles from both edges for better duty cycle approximation.
- On active low reset (rst_n), initialize cnt1, cnt2, clk_div1, and clk_div2.
- Add compile-time check to ensure NUM_DIV is odd.
- This meets the original specification precisely and improves duty cycle quality compared to a single-counter solution.
