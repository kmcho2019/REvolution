Implement the dual-edge odd frequency divider by using two counters: one updated on the rising edge (cnt1) and one on the falling edge (cnt2) of clk. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and toggles its respective divided clock (clk_div1 on posedge and clk_div2 on negedge) when reaching half of NUM_DIV (integer division). The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, producing a clock with frequency divided by the odd number NUM_DIV and better duty cycle approximation. Reset initializes counters and clocks. This approach maintains dual-edge tracking but avoids complex logic by simple counter comparisons and toggling.
