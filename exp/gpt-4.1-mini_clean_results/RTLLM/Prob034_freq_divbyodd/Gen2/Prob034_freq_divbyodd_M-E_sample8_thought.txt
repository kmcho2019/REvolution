To implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV using two counters and two divided clocks as requested, the idea is:

- Use two counters: cnt1 counts on the positive edge of clk, and cnt2 counts on the negative edge of clk.
- Both counters count from 0 up to NUM_DIV - 1 and then wrap around.
- When cnt1 reaches half_count (=(NUM_DIV - 1)/2), toggle clk_div1.
- When cnt2 reaches half_count, toggle clk_div2.
- By toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 on opposite clock edges but at the same count value, we achieve a nearly 50% duty cycle divided clock output when clk_div1 and clk_div2 are ORed.
- The combined output clk_div = clk_div1 | clk_div2 produces a clean odd-frequency divided clock.
- Reset asynchronously resets counters and clk_div outputs.
- This approach leverages dual-edge counting for better timing and duty cycle, generating a robust odd division with specified parameters.
