To simplify the odd frequency divider design while keeping the functionality, we can use a single counter that counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1 on every rising edge of clk. The divided clock toggles its output at two distinct points in the count cycle, spaced apart to approximate a 50% duty cycle as closely as possible for odd NUM_DIV.

Specifically, for an odd divisor N:
- The divided clock output clk_div is toggled at counts corresponding to the high and low durations:
  - Toggle at count == (N-1)/2 and at count == N-1
- This approach effectively produces one full clk_div period every N input clk cycles.
- Reset initializes counter and clk_div to zero.
- No need for separate counters or separate positive/negative edge handling.
- This significantly reduces complexity and avoids glitches or timing mismatch from two-edge logic while generating a close-to-50% duty cycle clock divided by an odd number.

This single counter and toggle points method is simple, efficient, and achieves the required odd division with a balanced duty cycle.
