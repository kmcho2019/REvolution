To implement a frequency divider by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters: cnt1 increments on the rising edge and cnt2 increments on the falling edge of clk, each counting modulo NUM_DIV. When cnt1 reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division) on a rising edge, clk_div1 toggles. Similarly, when cnt2 reaches half of NUM_DIV on a falling edge, clk_div2 toggles. This approach interleaves toggling events on both clock edges, enabling an odd division ratio with approximately 50% duty cycle. The final output clk_div is generated by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, capturing the combined toggling on both edges. The asynchronous active-low reset initializes counters and clock outputs. The implementation includes a compile-time check to ensure NUM_DIV is odd.
