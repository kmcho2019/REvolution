For an odd frequency divider, a simple and reliable method is to toggle the output clock once every NUM_DIV input clock edges. Since NUM_DIV is odd, toggling the output every NUM_DIV input clocks results in a divided frequency of clk/NUM_DIV and a 50% duty cycle approximately.

We can implement a single counter that counts input clock edges up to NUM_DIV-1, then resets and toggles the output clock. This removes the complexity of separate counters and toggles on positive and negative edges.

This approach uses only the positive edge of clk, simplifying the design, while still producing a stable, glitch-free divided clock at an odd division ratio.

The reset synchronously initializes the counter and output clock to zero.
