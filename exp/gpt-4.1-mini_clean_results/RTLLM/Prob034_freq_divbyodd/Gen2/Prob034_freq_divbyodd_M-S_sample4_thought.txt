To simplify the design while achieving an odd-frequency division, use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1, incremented on the positive edge of clk. The divided clock toggles when the counter reaches half the divisor (NUM_DIV/2), producing a toggle every half period. For odd division, this naturally creates a nearly 50% duty cycle clock.

The reset initializes the counter and output. This approach avoids dual counters and dual flip-flops, reducing complexity and avoiding edge misalignments.

The output clk_div toggles once per half divisor period, resulting in a clock frequency divided by NUM_DIV with a roughly 50% duty cycle for odd divisors.

This method is clean, easy to understand, synthesizable, and reliable.
