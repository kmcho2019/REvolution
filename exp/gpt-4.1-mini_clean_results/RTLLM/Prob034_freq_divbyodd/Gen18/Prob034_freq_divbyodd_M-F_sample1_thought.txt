The final output clk_div is generated by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, which effectively interleaves toggles on positive and negative edges to approximate an output frequency of input_clk / NUM_DIV with better duty cycle and edge alignment.

The asynchronous active-low reset rst_n initializes counters and clk_div outputs.
