To correctly divide an input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV using both clock edges, the approach is:

1. Use a single counter incrementing on the posedge of clk to count from 0 up to NUM_DIV - 1.

2. Generate two toggles: 
   - clk_div1 toggles when the counter reaches HALF_DIV (mid-point).
   - clk_div2 toggles when the counter reaches NUM_DIV - 1 (end of full cycle).

3. The output clk_div is generated by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. 

4. To correctly align clk_div1 and clk_div2 toggling events with clk edges and avoid glitches, clk_div1 toggles on posedge clk when cnt == HALF_DIV, and clk_div2 toggles on negedge clk when cnt == NUM_DIV - 1. This provides a phase shift and nearly 50% duty cycle.

5. Synchronize the counter to reset asynchronously with rst_n.

6. This single counter approach ensures phase alignment and avoids drift between two independent counters.

7. Implement compile-time check that NUM_DIV is odd.

This approach eliminates the dual independent counters and aligns toggles and counters to produce glitch-free, correctly phased, odd-divided output clock.
