Simplify the frequency divider by using a single counter running on the positive edge and toggle a single output clock (clk_div) twice per NUM_DIV counts to produce the odd division. The toggling points are at count 0 and at the halfway point (floor(NUM_DIV/2)) of the counter. This eliminates the need for two separate flip-flops and two counters, reducing complexity and avoiding negative-edge logic. The reset synchronously initializes the counter and output. This approach maintains functionality and achieves an odd frequency division with a roughly 50% duty cycle, but with simpler logic.
