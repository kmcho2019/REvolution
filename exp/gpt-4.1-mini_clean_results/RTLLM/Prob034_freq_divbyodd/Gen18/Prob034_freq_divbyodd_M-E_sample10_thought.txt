I will design a frequency divider for odd divisors by creating a single modulo-N counter clocked on the positive edge with an explicit state machine to generate a 50% duty cycle output by toggling the output at specific counts. The key is to toggle clk_div once per full cycle (every NUM_DIV counts) but also toggle it halfway through the count (at (NUM_DIV-1)/2 counts) to approximate a 50% duty cycle. 

This method uses one counter and one clock domain, avoiding the complexity and metastability risks of crossing clock edges. The output toggles twice per full division period, once at count = (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and once at count = NUM_DIV-1, ensuring the output frequency is clk frequency divided by NUM_DIV.

Since NUM_DIV is odd, half_period = (NUM_DIV -1)/2 is an integer, and the toggling points are well defined.

Asynchronous active low reset initializes counter and output.

This approach achieves good duty cycle accuracy for odd dividers with minimal hardware and clean synchronous logic on posedge clock only.
