To implement a frequency divider by an odd number using two counters for positive and negative edges, I will use the following approach:

- Use two separate counters: cnt_pos increments on the positive edge of clk, cnt_neg increments on the negative edge of clk.
- Both counters count modulo NUM_DIV.
- When cnt_pos reaches NUM_DIV-1, toggle clk_div_pos.
- Similarly, when cnt_neg reaches NUM_DIV-1, toggle clk_div_neg.
- The final divided clock output clk_div is obtained by OR'ing clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg.
- This produces two pulse trains each toggled at one edge of the input clock, together providing a frequency divided output by the odd number NUM_DIV.
- The output will have a frequency of clk/(NUM_DIV), and the OR logic combines the toggling to produce a near 50% duty cycle output.
- All registers are reset synchronously by an active low reset rst_n.
- This dual-counter, dual-flipflop architecture strictly separates positive and negative edge logic, which suits odd-number division and improves waveform symmetry.
