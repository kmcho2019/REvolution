To fix the issues with asynchronous toggling and uncoordinated counters on positive and negative edges, I will refactor the design to tightly couple the counters and toggling logic while keeping the two-counter structure.

Key points of the refactor:

1. Use two counters, cnt_pos and cnt_neg, both counting modulo NUM_DIV, but one incrementing on posedge clk and the other on negedge clk.

2. Instead of toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 when counters equal MID_VAL inside the always blocks, I will generate combinational toggle signals that become high for exactly one clock edge cycle when the counters reach MID_VAL.

3. Use clk_div1 and clk_div2 registers that toggle only when their respective toggle signals are asserted (one cycle pulse), ensuring precise toggling at the exact count.

4. This approach guarantees each half of the output clock toggles once per NUM_DIV cycles, aligned with its edge, generating a correct odd frequency divider with close to 50% duty cycle.

5. Finally, output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2 as before, combining positive and negative edge toggles.

6. The reset logic remains synchronous and active low, resetting counters and outputs.

7. This method eliminates glitches and race conditions from asynchronous toggling and ensures the divided clocks toggle cleanly at the intended counts.

8. I will also remove the initial block parameter check (non-synthesizable) and instead add a synthesis-time assertion using Verilog `generate` construct for better synthesis compatibility.

This refactor respects the original intent—two counters for positive and negative edges and two toggled outputs combined by OR—but enforces clean toggle pulses and synchronized counting, ensuring a stable and correct odd clock divider.
