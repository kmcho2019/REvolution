I will implement the odd frequency divider using a single up-counter and a toggle flip-flop driven only by the positive edge of the clock. The key idea is to generate a clock enable pulse that toggles the output flip-flop at specific counts, effectively dividing the input frequency by an odd number.

For an odd divisor NUM_DIV:

- The output clock will toggle twice per full cycle of the counter, but unevenly spaced due to the odd count.
- The toggle points are when the counter reaches 0 and (NUM_DIV-1)/2. At these two counts, the output clock is toggled.
- The counter runs from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 and then resets to zero.
- This produces a clock with period NUM_DIV input clock cycles, and a duty cycle close to 50%.
- All logic is synchronous to the positive edge of clk, simplifying timing.
- The asynchronous active-low reset initializes the counter and output clock.
- The parameter NUM_DIV is checked at compile-time to ensure oddness.

This approach eliminates the need for dual-edge triggered logic and separate counters, simplifying the design and improving timing and power efficiency.
