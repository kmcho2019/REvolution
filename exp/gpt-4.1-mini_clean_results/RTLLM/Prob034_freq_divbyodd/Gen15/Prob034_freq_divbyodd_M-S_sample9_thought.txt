To simplify the design while maintaining functionality:

- Use a single counter running on the positive edge of clk, counting modulo NUM_DIV.
- Generate clk_div1 by setting it high at count 0 and clearing at count HALF_DIV.
- Generate clk_div2 by toggling it at the negative edge when the count reaches specific points using a simple enable signal derived from the counter.
- Instead of a delayed counter, use the same counter for both clk_div1 and clk_div2 domains, but clk_div2 is updated on the negative clock edge.
- This reduces registers and logic complexity by having only one counter and two flip-flops without any delayed copies.
- Keep the asynchronous active-low reset.
- Output clk_div as OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2.
- Add compile-time check to ensure NUM_DIV is odd.
- This approach keeps the odd division accuracy, maintains 50% duty cycle approximate timing, and reduces complexity.
