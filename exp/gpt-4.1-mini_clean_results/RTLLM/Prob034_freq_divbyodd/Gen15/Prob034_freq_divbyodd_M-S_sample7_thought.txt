Simplify the design by using a single counter running on the positive clock edge only and generate the divided clock directly by toggling a single flip-flop at two specific counts (0 and half the divisor). This eliminates the second counter and the negedge logic, reducing complexity and making the design fully synchronous. The output clock is the toggled flip-flop itself, providing an odd division with a ~50% duty cycle. The reset asynchronously clears the counter and flip-flop. Also, include a compile-time check for odd NUM_DIV.
