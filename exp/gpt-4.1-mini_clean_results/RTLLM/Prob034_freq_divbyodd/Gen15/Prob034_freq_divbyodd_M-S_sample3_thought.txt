To simplify the odd frequency divider while maintaining functionality, I will use a single counter that counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1 on the positive clock edge. When the counter reaches half the divisor (NUM_DIV/2 rounded down), toggle the output clock. Since NUM_DIV is odd, toggling at half the count gives an approximate 50% duty cycle. The counter resets at NUM_DIV-1 and the output clock toggles at the midpoint, resulting in an output frequency of input clock divided by NUM_DIV. The active-low reset will initialize the counter and output clock to zero. This single-counter method simplifies the design and avoids dual-edge complexity, while still achieving an odd-frequency divide.
