The original solution uses two counters, each counting from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1, toggling clk_div1 and clk_div2 only after a full count cycle, then ORs them to generate clk_div. However, this causes the output frequency to be divided by 2*NUM_DIV, not NUM_DIV, because each divided clock toggles only once per NUM_DIV clock edges, and the output is the OR of two signals toggling at half frequency.

To correct this, each of the two divided clocks (clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg) should toggle at half the divisor count, i.e., when their respective counters reach (NUM_DIV >> 1). By toggling clk_div1 at the posedge counter half-cycle and clk_div2 at the negedge counter half-cycle, then OR-ing these two, the output frequency divides by NUM_DIV with improved 50% duty cycle approximation.

Hence, counters cnt_pos and cnt_neg should count from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. On reaching NUM_DIV-1, reset to 0. On reaching (NUM_DIV >> 1), toggle clk_div1 or clk_div2 respectively.

This approach ensures two toggles per full count, halving the effective period of clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg and producing an overall output frequency of clk / NUM_DIV.
