To implement a frequency divider by an odd number using two counters and two divided clocks triggered on opposite edges of the input clock, the design should count on the positive edge (cnt1) and negative edge (cnt2) separately. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1 and toggles its respective clk_div signal (clk_div1 or clk_div2) when reaching the halfway point ((NUM_DIV-1)/2). The final output clk_div is the logical OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, combining both half-period toggles to form a clock with a frequency divided by an odd number.

On reset (active low), all counters and divided clocks are reset to zero. The two counters increment independently on the respective edges of clk. This ensures the divided clock output edges are well spaced, achieving the odd division with better duty cycle control.
