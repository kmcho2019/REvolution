Implement the frequency divider for an odd divisor NUM_DIV by using two separate counters: cnt_pos triggered on the positive edge of clk, and cnt_neg triggered on the negative edge of clk. Each counter counts from 0 up to NUM_DIV-1 and toggles its respective clk_div signal (clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg) when reaching the halfway point (i.e., (NUM_DIV-1)/2).

The two partial divided clocks clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg are ORed to produce the final clk_div output. This approach ensures that clk_div has rising edges spaced evenly by half the division period, resulting in a near 50% duty cycle for odd divisors.

Resetting (active low) initializes counters and divided clocks to zero.

This design exploits the dual-edge clocking and separate toggling on posedge and negedge to achieve an odd frequency division with balanced waveform symmetry and better timing accuracy.
