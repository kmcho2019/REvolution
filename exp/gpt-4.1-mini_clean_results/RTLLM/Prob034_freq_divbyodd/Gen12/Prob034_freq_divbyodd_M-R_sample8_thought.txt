Refactoring approach:

Maintain the same division by an odd number using a single counter and separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. Instead of explicitly setting/clearing clk_div1 and clk_div2 based on count values inside always blocks, use toggle flip-flops triggered when the counter hits specific values to produce pulses, which toggle the outputs. This reduces combinational conditions within the always blocks and can improve clarity and timing.

Specifically:
- Use one counter counting modulo NUM_DIV on posedge clk.
- On posedge clk, toggle clk_div1 when counter reaches HALF_DIV.
- On negedge clk, toggle clk_div2 when the delayed counter reaches HALF_DIV.
- Initialize both clk_div1 and clk_div2 to 0 on reset.
- Synchronize delayed counter cnt_dly on posedge clk as before.
- The final divided clock is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2.
- This approach replaces explicit set/reset conditions with toggling at half periods, which is typical for clock dividers and often easier for synthesis and timing.
