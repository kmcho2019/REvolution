I will implement the odd-frequency divider by using a single counter incremented on the positive edge of the clock, running modulo NUM_DIV (an odd number). The output clock toggles when the counter hits specific counts that equally split the period into two halves, achieving the desired divide ratio with as close to 50% duty cycle as possible for odd numbers.

Specifically:
- Use a single counter counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1.
- Toggle the output clock (clk_div) at two points in the count cycle:
   1) when counter == HALF_DIV
   2) when counter == NUM_DIV - 1
- This way, one half period lasts HALF_DIV+1 clock cycles, and the other half lasts HALF_DIV clock cycles, giving an accurate odd division with minimal duty cycle distortion.
- Reset asynchronously clears counter and output clock.
- This single-edge approach avoids dual-edge flip-flops and simplifies timing, potentially improving PPA.
- The output clk_div toggles synchronously with clk, preserving timing simplicity.
- Parameterization includes a check for odd NUM_DIV.
