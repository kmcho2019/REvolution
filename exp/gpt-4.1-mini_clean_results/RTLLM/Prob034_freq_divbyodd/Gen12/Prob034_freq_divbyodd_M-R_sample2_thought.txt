To address the issues of glitching and misalignment in the original design, I will refactor the frequency divider by using a single counter clocked on the positive edge of `clk` with synchronous reset.

The key points of the refactoring:

- Use a single up-counter `cnt` counting from 0 to NUM_DIV-1, wrapping around.

- Use the counter value to generate the output clock by setting `clk_div` to '1' for the first (NUM_DIV+1)/2 counts, and '0' for the remaining counts in the period. This approach yields a nearly 50% duty cycle clock for odd divisors.

- This single-counter method avoids complications of synchronizing separate counters on posedge and negedge clocks, thus removing glitches and timing uncertainties.

- The output clock `clk_div` is a simple combinational function of the counter state, ensuring glitch-free output transitions aligned with the input clock edges.

- The design retains the compile-time check that NUM_DIV is odd.

This approach simplifies the implementation and guarantees correct odd-frequency division with a stable output clock.
