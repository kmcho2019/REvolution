Implement the frequency divider by separately counting rising and falling edges of the input clock using two counters: cnt1 increments on the positive edge of clk, and cnt2 increments on the negative edge. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1.

At the moment cnt1 and cnt2 reach the half division point (NUM_DIV >> 1), the corresponding clk_div1 or clk_div2 toggles.

The final output clk_div is formed by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2, producing a waveform with a period of NUM_DIV input clock cycles and a more balanced duty cycle for odd division factors.

Use asynchronous active-low reset for immediate initialization of counters and toggle signals.

Parameterize NUM_DIV and enforce at compile-time that it is odd.

This design respects the problem's requirement of using two counters and two toggles on both clock edges, achieving the odd frequency division with improved output clock quality.
