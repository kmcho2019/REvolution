I will design the 8-bit adder using a single always_comb block with bitwise logic and manual carry propagation instead of instantiating multiple single-bit adders. This approach offers a compact, fully combinational behavioral implementation that clearly shows carry propagation without structural instantiation.

The key idea is to propagate the carry bit through each bit position within a combinational process, using a simple for loop to compute sum bits and carry signals sequentially. This explicit loop emulates a ripple carry behavior but coded behaviorally rather than structurally, making the code concise and easy to maintain.

This method avoids module instantiations, relying on standard Verilog constructs, thus offering clarity and ease of integration, while maintaining the ripple carry functionality. The output cout is the carry out after the 7th bit addition.

This approach is fully combinational, synthesizable, and ideal for quick RTL understanding and debugging.
