To simplify the 8-bit adder with bit-level full adders while keeping the ripple carry structure, I will:

- Use a generate block to instantiate the eight full adders instead of manual instantiation, reducing code verbosity.
- Define the bit_full_adder module with assign statements for a clean combinational implementation.
- Use an internal wire vector for carry signals to streamline the carry chain connections.
- Keep the module purely combinational and maintain the same input/output interface.

This approach balances simplicity and clarity while preserving functionality.
