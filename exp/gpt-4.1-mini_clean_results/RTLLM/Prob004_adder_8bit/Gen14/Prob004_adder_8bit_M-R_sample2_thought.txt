I will refactor the 8-bit adder by directly instantiating eight bit-level full adders connected in series to propagate carry signals, instead of precomputing carry lookahead signals externally. This approach uses pure ripple-carry logic, where each full adder takes the carry-out from the previous adder as carry-in.

This fully modular approach eliminates explicit carry signal computations outside the full adders, clarifying data flow and matching a classic combinational ripple-carry adder architecture.

The carry chain will be formed by wires connecting the internal carry-outs to the carry-ins of successive full adders, with the initial carry-in connected from the input port `cin`. This makes the design straightforward and structurally explicit, suitable for simple educational understanding or ASIC/FPGA synthesis.

The output carry `cout` will come from the last full adder's carry-out.

This retains the original combinational logic style using continuous assignments inside the full adder, but removes the explicit carry lookahead logic in the top module.
