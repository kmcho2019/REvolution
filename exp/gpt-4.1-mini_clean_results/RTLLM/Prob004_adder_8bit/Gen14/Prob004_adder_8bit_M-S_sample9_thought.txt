To simplify the 8-bit adder design while maintaining functionality, I will:

1. Keep the full adder as a simple combinational logic block using concise expressions.
2. Use a generate loop to instantiate eight full adders, reducing repetitive code.
3. Use an internal wire array for carry signals between bits.
4. Maintain a ripple-carry architecture for simplicity and clarity.

This approach reduces code size and improves readability while preserving the structural modularity and combinational nature of the original design.
