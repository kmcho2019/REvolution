To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will implement the 8-bit adder as a simple ripple carry adder composed of 8 bit-level full adders connected sequentially. Each full adder will handle one bit and the carry-out of each will feed into the carry-in of the next. This approach reduces complexity by removing carry lookahead logic and intermediate propagate/generate signals, resulting in a straightforward, easy-to-understand combinational implementation. It sacrifices some speed compared to CLA but meets the functional requirement with minimal code and clear structure.
