The original ripple carry 8-bit adder is functionally correct and simple, but the ripple carry structure inherently limits performance due to linear carry propagation delay.

To improve performance (reduce critical path delay), I will implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure for 8 bits. The CLA reduces carry computation time by generating carry signals in parallel using propagate and generate signals.

Improvement strategy:
- Compute propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for each bit.
- Use carry-lookahead logic to compute all carry bits at once from inputs and cin.
- Use the carry signals to compute sum bits.
- This approach significantly improves performance while keeping the area and power overhead modest for 8 bits.
- Retain a modular design with a reusable single-bit full adder replaced by bit-wise logic for sum generation.
- The module remains purely combinational.

This change should reduce the maximum delay (improve WNS), with a small area increase, yielding better PPA for timing-critical designs.
