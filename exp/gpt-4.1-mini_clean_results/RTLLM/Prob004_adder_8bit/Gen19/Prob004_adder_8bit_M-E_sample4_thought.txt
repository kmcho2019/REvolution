Implement an 8-bit adder as a purely ripple-carry adder composed of 8 instances of a simple 1-bit full adder module. Each full adder computes sum and carry out based on inputs and carry in. This approach uses straightforward combinational logic with minimal overhead and clear dataflow.

Unlike carry-lookahead structures, this ripple carry adder has a simple and regular structure which is easy to understand and debug. Though slower due to carry ripple, it is area-efficient and fully combinational.

The full adder is implemented directly with sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = majority(a, b, cin). The top module chains these bit adders, connecting the carry out of one to the carry in of the next, starting from the external cin.

This design trades timing for simplicity and compactness and demonstrates clean modular design of multiple bit-level adders in a ripple fashion.
