Simplify the 8-bit adder by using a ripple carry approach with 8 instances of a simple bit-level full adder module. Each full adder takes inputs a[i], b[i], and a carry-in from the previous bit and produces sum[i] and carry-out to the next bit. This approach maintains clear modularity and functionality, reduces hierarchy and intermediate signals compared to the previous CLA implementation, and remains purely combinational. It trades some performance for simplicity, which is often acceptable for moderate bit-width adders like 8 bits.
