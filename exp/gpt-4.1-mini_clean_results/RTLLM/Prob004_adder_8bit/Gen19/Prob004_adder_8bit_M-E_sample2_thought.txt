Implement an 8-bit adder purely as a ripple-carry chain of bit-level full adders. Each full adder takes one bit from inputs A and B plus a carry-in, outputs a sum bit and a carry-out which is passed as carry-in to the next full adder. This modular and straightforward approach emphasizes clear, structural combinational logic without carry lookahead, making the design easy to verify and synthesize. Each bit-level full adder is separately instantiated and connected, demonstrating direct carry ripple without hierarchical carry prediction.
