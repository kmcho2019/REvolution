Implement the 8-bit adder using a ripple carry structure composed of eight full adder bit-slices chained sequentially. Each full adder adds corresponding bits of inputs a and b along with a carry input, producing a sum bit and a carry output. The carry out of each full adder is connected as carry in to the next higher bit full adder.

This approach is straightforward, uses simple combinational logic, and directly demonstrates the concept of building an n-bit adder from bit-level adders. While slower than carry lookahead adders in terms of carry propagation delay, it is simple and clear, suitable for understanding fundamental adder design.

The design includes:
- A full adder module that takes bit-level inputs a, b, and cin, and outputs sum and cout.
- The top-level adder_8bit module instantiates eight full adders, connecting their carry chains properly.
