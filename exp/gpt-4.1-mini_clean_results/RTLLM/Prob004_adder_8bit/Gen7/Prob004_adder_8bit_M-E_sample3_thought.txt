Implement the 8-bit adder using a hierarchical approach with two 4-bit ripple carry adders instantiated and connected via carry signals. Each 4-bit adder is built with four bit-level full adders chained internally. This modular approach improves clarity and scalability by dividing the 8-bit addition into two manageable sub-blocks. The carry-out of the lower 4-bit adder feeds the carry-in of the upper 4-bit adder. This structure maintains ripple carry within 4-bit blocks but separates the design for easier extension to larger widths.
