I will implement an 8-bit adder using a flat structure of 8 individual bit-level full adders chained in a ripple carry fashion without hierarchical submodules. Each bit-level full adder will be instantiated explicitly with carry connections passing from the least significant bit to the most significant bit. This approach provides direct visibility of every bit adder instance and carry path in a simple combinational structure, avoiding generate loops or nested modules.

Each full adder will compute sum and carry out using straightforward logic:
- sum = a ^ b ^ cin
- cout = (a & b) | ((a ^ b) & cin)

This direct and explicit bitwise chaining is straightforward, easy to understand, and easy to modify for selective bit changes or extensions. It makes debugging and tracing carries in simulation or hardware straightforward, trading some code verbosity for clarity and explicit control.

The module will have the requested ports and operate fully combinationally to produce sum and cout outputs.

This architecture contrasts the hierarchical and generate-based examples by emphasizing explicit flat bit-level connections.
