Implement the 8-bit adder by defining a single full adder module and using a generate-for loop to instantiate an array of these full adders. This method leverages Verilog generate constructs for compactness and scalability, making the design more concise and easier to modify for different operand widths. The carry chain is still ripple carry but is handled automatically through a carry wire vector. This design improves code maintainability and clarity compared to explicit repeated instantiations.
