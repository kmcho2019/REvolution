The existing 8-bit ripple carry adder using bit-level full adders is already minimal and structurally clean with gate-level full adder implementation. To improve PPA without altering the ripple-carry topology:

- Performance: The linear carry propagation limits speed; we can’t solve that fully here without changing architecture. However, we can minimize internal net capacitance by replacing the generated carry wire array with explicit wires and reducing the fanout on carry signals by using local wires inside the generate block.

- Area & Power: The full adder logic is minimal already. We can slightly reduce power by minimizing signal transitions. One technique is to gate the carry outputs internally with local nets rather than vector wires. Although small, this might help synthesis reduce net loading. 

- Readability & Maintainability: Using a generate block is good. We keep that, but declare internal carry signals explicitly for clarity.

Thus, the improvement is subtle: replacing carry vector wire with individual carry signals inside the generate loop to help synthesis tool map carry signals efficiently, potentially reducing wire length, loading, and glitches.
