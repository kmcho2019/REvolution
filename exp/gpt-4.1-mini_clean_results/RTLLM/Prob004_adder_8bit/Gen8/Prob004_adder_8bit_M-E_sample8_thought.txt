Instead of a traditional ripple carry adder, implement the 8-bit adder as two 4-bit ripple carry adders connected with an intermediate carry signal. This hierarchical approach breaks the 8-bit adder into smaller 4-bit blocks, improving clarity and providing a modular structure conducive for scaling or modification. Each 4-bit adder is itself constructed from bit-level full adders.

This structure maintains combinational logic with chained carry signals but introduces block-level abstraction. While still ripple carry in essence, this two-stage hierarchical design can better localize carry propagation and can be expanded or replaced by carry lookahead blocks in future improvements.

Advantages:
- Modular design with 4-bit building blocks.
- Clear hierarchical structure.
- Facilitates testing or replacement of intermediate blocks.
- Still fully combinational with carry chaining.

The top-level 8-bit adder takes two 4-bit adders:
- Lower 4 bits added with cin.
- Higher 4 bits added with carry out from lower 4-bit adder.

We implement a generic 1-bit full adder with gate-level logic, then use a generate loop for the 4-bit adder blocks.
