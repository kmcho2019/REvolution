Implement the 8-bit adder by splitting it into two 4-bit ripple carry adders connected in series. Each 4-bit adder is composed of bit-level full adders. The carry-out from the lower 4-bit adder becomes the carry-in of the upper 4-bit adder. This modular approach improves readability and scalability by decomposing the 8-bit addition into smaller segments, while still maintaining ripple carry behavior. It provides a clean hierarchical design and makes it easier to extend or modify parts of the adder independently.

Additionally, implement the bit-level full adder using gate-level logic for sum and carry-out to ensure an efficient gate-level implementation. The top-level adder_8bit module instantiates two 4-bit adders and wires the carry between them.

This differs structurally from a flat 8-bit ripple and demonstrates hierarchical decomposition with modularity.
