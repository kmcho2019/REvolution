I will simplify the 8-bit adder design by instantiating eight bit-level full adders directly in the top-level module without intermediate 4-bit adder modules. This reduces module hierarchy and code length, while keeping the ripple carry logic and combinational behavior intact. Each full adder connects its carry-out to the next full adder's carry-in, starting with the external carry-in. The final carry-out is the output carry of the MSB full adder. This approach maintains clear structure and functionality with fewer modules.
