To fuse the strengths of both examples:

- From Example 2, employ a hierarchical carry lookahead approach by grouping bits into 2-bit CLA blocks to speed up carry calculation instead of a simple ripple carry chain. This reduces carry propagation delay by performing carry lookahead in smaller blocks and chaining only 4 blocks.
- Fuse these by implementing a 2-bit CLA block that computes internal carries via p and g signals as in Example 1 but in a dedicated module.
- Then implement the 8-bit adder by chaining four such 2-bit CLA blocks, where each block uses direct p/g signals and carry lookahead.
- This achieves a balance between modular design, reduced gate count (no full adder modules used), and improved timing (shorter carry chain than pure ripple carry).
- The 2-bit CLA blocks calculate carries internally and output carry-out; the top-level 8-bit adder chains these blocks.
- This fusion preserves combinational logic style with continuous assign statements for clarity and ease of synthesis.
- The result is a clean, modular, and efficient 8-bit adder with improved performance and modest area/power.
