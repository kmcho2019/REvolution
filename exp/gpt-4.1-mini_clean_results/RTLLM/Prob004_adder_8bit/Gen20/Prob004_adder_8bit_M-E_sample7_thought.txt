Implement the 8-bit adder as a ripple-carry adder using 8 instances of a 1-bit full adder. Each full adder module adds the corresponding bits of operands a and b along with an input carry, producing a sum bit and a carry out. The carry out of each bit is connected to the carry in of the next bit, forming a simple and classic ripple-carry structure. This approach uses purely combinational logic at the bit level and is easy to understand and verify. Although slower than carry-lookahead, it is straightforward and modular.
