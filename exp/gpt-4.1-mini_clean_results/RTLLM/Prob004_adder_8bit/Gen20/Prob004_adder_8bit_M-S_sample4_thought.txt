To simplify the 8-bit adder design while maintaining full combinational functionality, I will implement a ripple-carry adder composed of eight instantiated 1-bit full adders. Each full adder computes sum and carry-out for one bit. This approach uses a simple carry chain without explicit propagate/generate signals or CLA logic, reducing module hierarchy and intermediate signals. Although ripple carry adders have longer carry propagation delay than CLA, they are straightforward, smaller in code complexity, and easy to verify. The 1-bit full adder will compute sum and carry-out directly from inputs and carry-in. The top-level 8-bit adder will chain these 1-bit full adders to generate the sum and final carry-out.
