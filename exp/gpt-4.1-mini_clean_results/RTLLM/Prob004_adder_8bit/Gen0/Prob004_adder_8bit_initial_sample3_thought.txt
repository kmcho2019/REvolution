Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating eight 1-bit full adder modules in series. Each full adder takes one bit from input A and B and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of one adder connects to the carry-in of the next. The first adder's carry-in is the module input cin, and the last adder's carry-out is the module output cout.
