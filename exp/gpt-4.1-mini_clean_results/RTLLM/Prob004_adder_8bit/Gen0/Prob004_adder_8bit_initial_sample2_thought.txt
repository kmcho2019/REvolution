Implement the 8-bit adder by cascading eight 1-bit full adders in combinational logic. Each full adder takes two single-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each adder connects to the carry-in of the next higher bit adder. The module will have inputs a[7:0], b[7:0], and cin, and outputs sum[7:0] and cout.
