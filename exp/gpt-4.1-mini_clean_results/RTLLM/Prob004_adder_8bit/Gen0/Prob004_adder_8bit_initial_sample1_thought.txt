Implement an 8-bit adder by instantiating eight 1-bit full adder modules connected in series, where each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. Chain the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next, starting with the module's cin input and ending with the final carry-out as cout. This modular approach facilitates bit-level addition in combinational logic.
