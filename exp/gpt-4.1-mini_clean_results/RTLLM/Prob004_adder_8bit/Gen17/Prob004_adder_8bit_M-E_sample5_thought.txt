Implement the 8-bit adder by cascading 8 full adder (bit-level) modules in a ripple carry fashion. Each full adder takes one bit of operands a and b, and a carry-in from the previous less significant bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. This straightforward hierarchical structure clearly shows the bit-level adders connected in series, making it easy to understand and verify. Although ripple carry adders are slower in general than CLA, for 8 bits the delay is manageable and this design strictly uses combinational logic with modular full adders.
