2. Implement the full adder logic directly inside the generate block to save the overhead of module instantiation, potentially reducing area.
3. Use a wire vector for internal carries to keep carry propagation explicit but compact.
4. Keep the ripple carry approach for simplicity since it's still reasonable for 8 bits, but having less module overhead and simpler code should reduce area and power marginally.
5. Optimize the full adder expressions to minimize gate counts by computing intermediate signals once.
6. Keep the design fully combinational.
