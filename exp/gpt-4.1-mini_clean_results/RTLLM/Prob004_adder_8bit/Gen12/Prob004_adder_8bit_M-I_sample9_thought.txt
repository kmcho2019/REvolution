To improve the existing ripple carry 8-bit adder for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will:

1. Use a generate loop to instantiate the eight 1-bit full adders, significantly reducing code verbosity and improving maintainability without changing the architecture.
2. Retain the minimal, balanced full adder logic to keep gate count and power low.
3. Keep the ripple carry structure since for 8 bits it offers a good trade-off between complexity and delay; higher performance architectures like carry lookahead adders introduce more area and power.
4. Use internal wires for the carry chain explicitly generated by the loop.
5. This structural, modular, and concise approach should help synthesis tools optimize better while keeping the design fully combinational and easy to debug.

This approach optimizes for area and code readability, potentially aiding synthesis tools in optimizations, while maintaining the low power and moderate speed suitable for an 8-bit adder.
