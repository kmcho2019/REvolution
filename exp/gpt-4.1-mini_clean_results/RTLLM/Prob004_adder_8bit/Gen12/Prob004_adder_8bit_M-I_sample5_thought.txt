To optimize the 8-bit ripple carry adder for better Performance, Power, and Area (PPA), I will replace the simple ripple carry structure with a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) approach, which significantly reduces the carry propagation delay and improves performance.

Improvement strategy:
- Implement a bit-level full adder that outputs generate (g) and propagate (p) signals.
- Use these g and p signals to compute carry signals in a lookahead manner, reducing the critical path delay to O(log n) instead of linear.
- This approach avoids a ripple chain of 8 carry signals, greatly improving timing.
- It keeps combinational logic, no clock gating needed.
- The area overhead is minimal for 8 bits and the power may slightly improve as carry signals do not propagate sequentially.
- The final sum is computed from A, B, and carry-in signals.

Overall, the design targets better timing (lower WNS), with slight area and power tradeoff acceptable for this small width.

The module ports remain the same, and the full adder logic is embedded combinationally.
