To simplify the 8-bit adder implementation while maintaining full functionality, I will:

1. Use a single-bit full adder module with the simplest and standard full adder logic.
2. Instantiate the 8 full adders in a generate loop instead of explicit individual instantiations to reduce code size and increase scalability.
3. Use an internal wire vector to carry the carry signals between bits.
4. Keep the module fully combinational with a ripple carry structure, ensuring clear carry propagation.
5. Maintain explicit carry-in and carry-out ports.

This approach reduces code verbosity and complexity while preserving the design's modularity and functionality.
