Fuse the two approaches for an optimal 8-bit adder combining clarity, modularity, and performance:

1. Use the minimal, balanced single-bit full adder (bit_full_adder) from Example 1 for best gate-level efficiency and minimal power.
2. Organize the 8-bit adder as two 4-bit ripple-carry blocks chained together, rather than a full 8-bit ripple. This halves the carry chain delay and balances area vs. timing.
3. Within each 4-bit block, instantiate bit_full_adder modules explicitly for clarity and explicit carry chaining, avoiding generate loops to maintain simplicity.
4. Between the two 4-bit blocks, chain carry out of lower block as carry in of upper block.
5. This approach blends the fine-grain minimal adders for low power and area with a hierarchical structure improving delay by reducing maximum carry chain length from 8 to 4.
6. Keep the code fully combinational, easily extendable, and clear for timing and power analysis.

The resulting architecture leverages the simplicity and minimal logic of bit_full_adder while improving performance by limiting carry propagation to 4 bits per block instead of 8.
