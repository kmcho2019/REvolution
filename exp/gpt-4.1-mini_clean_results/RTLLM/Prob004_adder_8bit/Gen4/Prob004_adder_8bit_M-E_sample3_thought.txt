Implement the 8-bit adder using a purely combinational logic approach without defining a separate full adder module, instead explicitly instantiate bit-level full adders by writing out their logic equations directly in the top module using wire arrays for carries.

The carry propagation is done by chaining the carry-out of bit i to the carry-in of bit i+1 in a ripple carry style.

This approach avoids module instantiations, thereby reducing hierarchy depth, which may improve timing slightly.

We implement the full adder logic directly:
- sum[i] = a[i] ^ b[i] ^ carry[i]
- carry[i+1] = (a[i] & b[i]) | (a[i] & carry[i]) | (b[i] & carry[i])

Carry[0] is assigned to cin, and carry[8] is the final cout.

This single-module approach is compact and easy to read and instantiate.

Although it sacrifices modularity, it is straightforward and may give slightly better synthesis results with fewer cells for small widths.
