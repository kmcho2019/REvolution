I will implement the 8-bit adder using a parallel prefix style approach with pairwise carry generation and propagation, but still within combinational logic. Specifically, I will use the concept of propagate and generate signals for each bit, then combine them using hierarchical grouping to quickly compute carries, resembling a simplified carry lookahead logic.

This structure reduces the carry chain delay compared to a pure ripple carry adder. I will compute propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit, then build group generate and propagate signals hierarchically (at 2-bit, 4-bit, and finally 8-bit levels) to determine carry-ins for all bits in parallel.

Then the sum bits will be computed using the propagate and carry-in signals. This approach is purely combinational, uses structural Verilog with explicit generate statements, and avoids instantiating multiple full adders. It is a classic approach to speeding up an adder without going full carry lookahead.

This design is more complex than a ripple carry adder but faster due to parallel carry computations, and still clean and modular.
