To improve the PPA (performance, power, and area) of the 8-bit ripple carry adder, I will:

1. Optimize the full adder logic equations to reduce transistor count and potentially reduce switching power. Use the canonical full adder sum and carry expressions but re-arranged to minimize logic levels.
2. Use assign statements without instantiating a separate module to reduce overhead and area.
3. Avoid generate loops by unrolling the bit-level adders explicitly, allowing finer control and possibly better synthesis results for small fixed width like 8 bits.
4. Minimize the number of wires and simplify the carry chain.
5. The critical path will remain the carry chain, but by simplifying logic and avoiding module overhead, slight improvements in delay and area are possible.
6. These changes are suited for combinational logic and keep the design modular and synthesizable.

This approach balances clarity, maintainability, and PPA improvements without changing the fundamental ripple carry architecture.
