To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in purely combinational logic, I will design a ripple-carry adder using dedicated full adder modules connected in series. Unlike the previous carry-lookahead approach, this design uses a straightforward ripple carry scheme where each bit-level full adder takes a carry-in from the previous stage and generates a carry-out for the next stage. 

This approach emphasizes simplicity and clarity, suitable for educational purposes or when area is more critical than maximum speed. Each full adder computes sum and carry-out combinationally. The top-level 8-bit adder connects eight full adders in a chain, feeding the carry sequentially from LSB to MSB.

The module adheres exactly to the requested interface with inputs `a`, `b`, and `cin` and outputs `sum` and `cout`.

This method is the classic ripple-carry adder design and complements the earlier carry-lookahead design by demonstrating the modular and easy-to-understand structure of bitwise addition.
