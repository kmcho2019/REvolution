Implement the 8-bit adder using a hierarchical carry-lookahead approach instead of a simple ripple carry. 

Key points of this design:
1. Divide the 8-bit inputs into two 4-bit blocks.
2. Each 4-bit block uses a 4-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA) module that generates sum and a block carry out.
3. A top-level module then combines the two 4-bit CLAs, using the carry out of the first 4-bit CLA as the carry in of the second.
4. The 4-bit CLA uses propagate and generate signals to compute carry signals in parallel, reducing carry delay compared to ripple carry.
5. This structure maintains modularity, improves timing performance by reducing carry propagation delay, and still uses combinational logic.
6. The final 8-bit sum and carry-out are obtained from the composition of the two 4-bit CLAs and the carry lookahead logic.

This hierarchical CLA approach balances performance improvement and code clarity without complex generate loops or deeply nested modules.
