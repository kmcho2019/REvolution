To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in a novel way, I will design a fully hierarchical architecture composed solely of 1-bit full adders instantiated individually without generate loops, highlighting explicit carry chain wiring. This approach improves clarity of carry propagation for educational/debugging purposes.

Each full adder is instantiated explicitly with carry connections manually chained from least significant bit to most significant bit. This straightforward, explicit instantiation style emphasizes the ripple carry structure at the bit level, showing exactly how each carry bit flows.

This differs from the parameterized or generate loop approaches by prioritizing explicit structure and direct mapping of each bit-level adder to signals, suitable for small-width adders.

The module 'bit_full_adder' implements a single bit full adder with straightforward logic. The top module 'adder_8bit' instantiates 8 of these, chains their carries explicitly, and wires the sum and carry outputs accordingly.

This explicit architectural style aids understanding, eases small customizations, and suits low-level debugging or instructional contexts.
