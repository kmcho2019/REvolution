To improve the existing 8-bit ripple carry adder, I will unify the instantiation of all 8 full adders into a single generate loop from 0 to 7. This reduces code redundancy and makes the design more scalable and concise while maintaining the same ripple carry structure. I will replace the wire[7:0] carry with wire[7:0] carry, connecting carry[i] as the carry-out of the ith full adder and carry-in of the (i+1)th full adder. The final carry-out will be carry[7]. This approach slightly improves code clarity and maintainability without changing the logic or adding complexity, thereby preserving low area and power consumption. Since ripple carry delay cannot be improved without architectural changes, this is a clean, minimal optimization.
