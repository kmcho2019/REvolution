I will implement the 8-bit adder as a single module using a chain of bit-level full adders instantiated explicitly without generate loops to illustrate a different approach. Instead of a ripple carry formed by carry signals connected through a wire array, I will declare individual carry wires between each full adder. This explicit chaining improves visibility of carry flow and allows fine-grained control or modification if needed. 

The bit_full_adder will remain a clean combinational cell. The top-level adder_8bit will instantiate 8 bit_full_adders, each wired with its own carry signals to propagate the carry-in to the next stage. This approach is straightforward, easy to understand, and good for educational purposes or hand-optimized critical paths. 

The design is purely combinational ripple carry, but with a different explicit style than generate-based modules.
