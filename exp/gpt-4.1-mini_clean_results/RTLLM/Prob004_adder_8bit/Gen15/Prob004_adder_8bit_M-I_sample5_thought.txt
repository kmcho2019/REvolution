To improve the given solution, I will implement the 8-bit adder as a ripple carry adder composed of 8 full adders (bit-level adders), each implemented structurally and chained by carry signals. Although the previous solution used 2-bit CLAs for some speed improvement, this structure reduces the combinational logic overhead and area by using the simplest full adder per bit.

This approach reduces area and power by avoiding extra propagate/generate logic and increases modularity with a standard full adder block. The ripple carry delay is longer but acceptable for an 8-bit adder, and the simpler structure will lower power and area.

Design steps:
1. Implement a full adder module using basic gate-level logic.
2. Instantiate 8 full adders in series for 8 bits.
3. Connect carry-out of one full adder to carry-in of the next.
4. The first full adder's carry-in is cin; the last one's carry-out is cout.
5. The sum bits are collected from each full adder.

This simpler and more classic approach should yield better area and power metrics while maintaining correctness and clarity.
