Refactor the 8-bit adder to use a flat, direct instantiation of eight bit-level full adders in a single module without intermediate 4-bit adder blocks. Each full adder is instantiated explicitly, and carry propagation wires connect them sequentially. This removes hierarchical modules and generate loops for clearer, explicit structural coding, but still uses purely combinational logic.

Advantages:
- Direct, straightforward instantiation improves readability for those familiar with structural modeling.
- No generate or hierarchy; easier for beginners to follow bitwise connections.
- Maintains modular full adder design for easy reuse or modification.
- Keeps ripple-carry structure, same carry chaining behavior, but explicit wiring.

This approach demonstrates a purely structural design style, showing bit-level details explicitly without abstraction layers.
