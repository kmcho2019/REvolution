Implement the 8-bit adder as a ripple carry adder composed of eight full adder modules connected in series. Each full adder computes the sum and carry-out of one bit of operands and the carry-in from the previous stage. This design is straightforward and modular, using only basic combinational logic elements (XOR, AND, OR gates). The carry ripples from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). Although this introduces longer carry propagation delay compared to CLA, it is a classic structural approach useful for educational and simple synthesis purposes.
