To simplify the 8-bit adder implementation while maintaining functionality, I will implement a straightforward ripple carry adder composed of 8 instances of a basic 1-bit full adder module.

This design will:

1. Use a simple full adder without propagate/generate outputs.
2. Chain carry signals directly from one bit adder to the next, resulting in a ripple carry structure.
3. Keep the code modular and combinational for clarity.
4. Avoid extra carry look-ahead logic, thus reducing complexity and area, though at the cost of carry propagation delay.

This approach offers a clear and minimalistic solution suitable for many applications where ultimate speed is not critical.
