Implement the 8-bit adder by instantiating eight 1-bit full adder modules in a ripple carry chain. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder becomes the carry-in of the next higher bit adder. This is a simple and classic approach using bit-level adders connected in series combinationally. The design is fully combinational and modular, clearly illustrating how small full adder building blocks compose an 8-bit adder.
