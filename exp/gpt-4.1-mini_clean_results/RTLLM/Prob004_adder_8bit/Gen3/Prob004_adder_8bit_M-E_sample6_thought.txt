To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic differently from the ripple-carry style, I will use a structural approach based on Half Adders and Full Adders combined explicitly. 

The idea:
1. Implement a half adder module that computes sum and carry for two single bits without carry-in.
2. Implement a full adder module that uses two half adders internally (thus explicitly showing the decomposition of the full adder logic).
3. Use the half adder for the least significant bit addition (since it doesn't have a carry-in).
4. Use the full adder for bits 1 through 7, chaining the carry-out from one stage to the carry-in of the next.
5. The structure emphasizes the hierarchy (half adder + full adder) and clarifies the carry propagation path.
6. This approach contrasts the pure full adder or generate block approach by explicitly showing the composition of the full adder from half adders.
7. The code remains fully combinational, modular, and structurally clean.

This method improves conceptual understanding and facilitates reusing half adder components in other designs.
