Implement the 8-bit adder using a generate block with an array of full adder instances to create a scalable and concise design. Instead of manually instantiating each full adder, a generate-for loop iterates over the bit indices, connecting carries and summing bits in a ripple-carry fashion. This approach improves code compactness and scalability, while maintaining the classic ripple carry chain architecture with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic.
