Implement the 8-bit adder using a parallel prefix adder structure, specifically the Kogge-Stone adder architecture. This approach computes carry generate and propagate signals for all bit positions in parallel, then performs prefix computations to efficiently determine carry signals, reducing the carry propagation delay compared to ripple carry adders. The sum bits are then computed with XOR of propagate signals and carry-in signals. This purely combinational design leverages generate/propagate pairs and prefix operations for fast carry computation, providing a high-performance 8-bit adder.
