To refactor the 8-bit adder design while preserving the ripple carry approach, I will implement the full adder logic directly inside the top module without instantiating a separate full adder module. This approach will use combinational assign statements for sum and carry generation for each bit, chaining carry signals explicitly with wires. This flattens the hierarchy and reveals the logic more directly, demonstrating that a ripple carry adder is essentially a chain of bitwise XOR and carry calculations. The design remains purely combinational and fully synthesizable.
