I will refactor the 8-bit ripple carry adder by explicitly defining the carry chain as a wire vector of length 8 (including the initial carry-in and final carry-out), then connecting each bit adder's carry-in and carry-out using simple assign statements. This avoids using a generate loop with conditionals and makes the carry chain connections more straightforward.

Specifically:
- The carry vector `c` will be 9 bits wide: c[0] is `cin`, c[8] is `cout`.
- Each full adder receives `c[i]` as carry-in and outputs `c[i+1]` as carry-out.
- The sum and carry outputs are directly wired via assign statements.

This approach keeps the combinational logic the same but simplifies carry handling and code readability by avoiding generate and if-else inside the generate block. It is easier to verify and maintain.
