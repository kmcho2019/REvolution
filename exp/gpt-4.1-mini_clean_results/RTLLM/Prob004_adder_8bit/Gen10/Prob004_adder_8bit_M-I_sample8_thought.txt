To optimize the 8-bit adder for better PPA (performance, power, and area), I will:

1. Replace the ripple carry structure with a simple carry look-ahead logic to reduce carry propagation delay, improving performance.
2. Keep the modular single-bit full adder for clarity but add generate logic to create all bits' sum and carry signals at once.
3. Implement a carry look-ahead logic block that calculates carries for all bits in parallel, reducing the critical path from O(n) to O(log n) or constant for 8 bits.
4. This approach slightly increases logic complexity and area but dramatically improves timing and may reduce dynamic power by reducing glitches caused by ripple carry.
5. The design remains combinational and modular, and the top-level interface stays unchanged.
