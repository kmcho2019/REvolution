Implement the 8-bit adder using a hierarchical two-stage approach: first, create a 4-bit ripple carry adder module that can be reused. Then instantiate two of these 4-bit adders in series to form the full 8-bit adder. This partitions the design for better modularity and clarity. Each 4-bit adder internally chains four bit-level full adders. The carry-out of the lower 4-bit adder feeds into the carry-in of the upper 4-bit adder. This approach showcases hierarchical design principles and reuse without generate loops.
