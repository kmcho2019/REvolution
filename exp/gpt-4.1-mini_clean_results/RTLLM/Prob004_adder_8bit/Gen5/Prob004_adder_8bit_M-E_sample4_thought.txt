To create a novel architectural approach for the 8-bit adder using multiple bit-level adders, I will implement a hierarchical two-level carry lookahead adder (CLA) structure built from 1-bit full adders and simple generate/propagate logic. 

The key points of this approach:
1. Each bit-level full adder generates sum, propagate, and generate signals.
2. The 8-bit adder is divided into two 4-bit blocks.
3. Carry lookahead logic is used to compute carries into the blocks and between bits within blocks using generate and propagate signals.
4. This reduces the carry propagation delay by computing carry signals in parallel instead of rippling them through each bit.
5. The sum bits still come from bit-level adders, but carry inputs are computed by CLA logic.
6. The design remains fully combinational, uses modular bit adders, and explicitly shows carry lookahead logic for better timing.

This approach maintains modularity while improving performance over a pure ripple carry adder by introducing carry lookahead at the block level and within the 4-bit blocks.
