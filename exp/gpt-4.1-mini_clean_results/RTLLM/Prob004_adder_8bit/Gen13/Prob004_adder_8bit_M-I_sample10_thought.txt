To optimize the existing 8-bit ripple carry adder for better PPA, I'll implement a carry lookahead adder (CLA) structure which significantly reduces carry propagation delay compared to ripple carry. 

Improvement strategy:
1. Replace the linear carry chain with carry lookahead logic by computing propagate (p) and generate (g) signals for each bit.
2. Use these signals to compute carries in parallel, reducing critical path delay and improving max clock frequency.
3. Maintain combinational logic without adding sequential elements to keep the design simple.
4. Retain modularity by keeping the 1-bit full adder module for sum calculation but move carry generation outside to lookahead logic.
5. This change improves timing by breaking carry chain dependencies, and power improves because of reduced switching on carry signals.
6. Area might slightly increase due to carry logic but is justified by performance gain.

The final module will instantiate one 1-bit adder for sum calculation per bit and a small carry lookahead logic for carry signals.
