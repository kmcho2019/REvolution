The existing solution is functionally correct and uses carry lookahead logic for better performance than ripple carry. However, to improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), we can:

1. Replace the explicit parallel carry computation and separate full adder instantiations with a pure ripple carry chain made of parameterized full adders. For 8 bits, ripple carry delay is modest and this reduces complexity and area.

2. Use a single generate block to instantiate the 8 full adders in a loop, reducing code size and enhancing synthesis optimization.

3. Connect carry signals as a ripple chain within the module, avoiding redundant propagate/generate logic and manual carry assignments, lowering area and switching activity.

4. The full adder module remains unchanged to maintain modularity.

This classic ripple carry implementation reduces gate count and wiring overhead, likely improving area and power, with minimal timing impact on 8-bit width. The simpler wiring also helps synthesis tools optimize better, improving overall PPA for this small adder.
