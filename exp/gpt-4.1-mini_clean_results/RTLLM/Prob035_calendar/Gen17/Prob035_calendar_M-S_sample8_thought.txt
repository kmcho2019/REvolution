Simplify the design by using a single always block to update seconds, minutes, and hours in a straightforward cascading manner. On each clock cycle or reset, increment seconds; if seconds wrap from 59 to 0, increment minutes; if minutes wrap from 59 to 0 simultaneously, increment hours; hours wrap from 23 to 0. This approach reduces the number of always blocks from three to one, making the design easier to understand and maintain while preserving the exact required functionality.
