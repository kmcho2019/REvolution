Implement the perpetual calendar with a modular multi-block approach, using three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block independently handles its own rollover and increment conditions synchronously on the clock edge with asynchronous reset. This design clarifies each unit’s logic and decouples the counters for easy debugging and potential reuse. 

Key features of this novel approach:
1. Three separate always blocks, one per time unit (seconds, minutes, hours).
2. Seconds counter always increments each clock cycle unless reset.
3. Minutes counter increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0.
4. Hours counter increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0.
5. Use synchronous reset for stable reset behavior across all counters.
6. Use non-blocking assignments exclusively.
7. Hours represented by 6 bits for uniformity with minutes and seconds, despite only needing 5 bits, for simpler interface or future expansion.
8. This clear partitioning improves maintainability and scalability, at the potential cost of slightly increased logic, but offers better separation of concerns.

This architecture trades a small increase in logic duplication for improved modularity and comprehensibility.
