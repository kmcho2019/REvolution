Implement the perpetual calendar by decoupling the seconds, minutes, and hours counters into three separate always blocks, each responsible solely for one time unit. Use a synchronous reset and increment seconds at every clock cycle. Minutes increment only when seconds rollover from 59 to 0, and hours increment only when both minutes and seconds rollover from 59 to 0 simultaneously. This separation improves modularity and clarity, potentially allowing independent optimizations or reuse. All counters use non-blocking assignments for proper simulation and synthesis behavior. The bit widths are kept minimal (5 bits for hours, 6 bits for minutes and seconds). This approach aligns well with synchronous design practices and eases testing/debugging by isolating logic per time unit.
