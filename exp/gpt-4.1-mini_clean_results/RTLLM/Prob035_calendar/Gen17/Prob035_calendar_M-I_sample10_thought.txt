To optimize the existing perpetual calendar module for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will implement these improvements:

1. **Separate Always Blocks**: Split the counters into three separate always blocks so that seconds, minutes, and hours update sequentially but independently. This reduces combinational logic depth and critical path, improving timing.

2. **Synchronous Reset Only**: Change to synchronous reset (reset is sampled on clock edge) to simplify timing and reduce asynchronous reset handling overhead.

3. **Use Enable Signals**: Introduce internal enable signals to update minutes and hours only when seconds or minutes roll over. This reduces unnecessary register toggles and power.

4. **Minimize Combinational Logic**: Simplify the combinational logic in each always block by checking only relevant conditions rather than nested if-else structures.

5. **Consistent Bit-widths**: Maintain 6-bit width for minutes and seconds, and 5-bit width for hours, ensuring minimal logic size.

6. **Non-blocking Assignments and No Extra Conditional Register Updates**: Update registers unconditionally in always blocks with non-blocking assignments, avoiding extra compare logic which may add gates.

This approach decreases the critical path delay by partitioning logic, reduces toggle rate on minutes and hours counters, and maintains simple, clean synchronous logic. The synchronous reset reduces complexity and timing uncertainties.

Overall, these changes should improve timing slack (wns), reduce power by gating counter increments naturally, and modestly reduce area by removing extra compare gates.
