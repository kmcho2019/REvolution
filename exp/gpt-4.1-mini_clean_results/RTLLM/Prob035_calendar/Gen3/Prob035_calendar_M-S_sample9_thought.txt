Combine all counters (Secs, Mins, Hours) into a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or RST. Use simple cascading logic: increment seconds each cycle, reset to 0 after 59; if seconds roll over, increment minutes and reset after 59; if minutes roll over, increment hours and reset after 23. This reduces multiple always blocks to one, simplifies condition checks, and keeps the same functional behavior and bit widths.
