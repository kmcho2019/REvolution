Implement the calendar with three independent always blocks, each responsible for updating seconds, minutes, and hours respectively, triggered by the clock and reset signals. Each block uses a synchronous reset and updates its own counter. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and rolls over after 59. The minutes block increments only when seconds roll from 59 to 0, rolling over after 59. The hours block increments only when both seconds and minutes roll over from 59 to 0, rolling over after 23. This modular structure improves readability and matches the problem statement's requirements, allowing clear timing and resource separation. Registers are 6-bit wide as requested.
