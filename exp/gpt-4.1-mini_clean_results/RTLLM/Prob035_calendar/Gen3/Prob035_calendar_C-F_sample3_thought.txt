Fuse the best ideas by combining the clarity and atomic updates of the single always block approach with the clean separation and non-blocking style of the three-block method. Use a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST for synchronous counting and reset, ensuring all counters update together to avoid glitches or race conditions. Employ minimal bit widths (5 bits for Hours, 6 bits for Mins and Secs) to optimize area.

The single always block structure improves timing by reducing cross-block dependencies and ensures synchronous updates, while non-blocking assignments maintain clear and predictable simulation semantics. This fusion eliminates unnecessary self-assignments, reduces condition checks, and sequences increments in a straightforward nested manner: increment seconds; if seconds overflow, reset seconds and increment minutes; if minutes overflow, reset minutes and increment hours; if hours overflow, reset hours.

This balanced design maximizes clarity, correctness, and area efficiency, and tends to offer better timing and power characteristics by minimizing logic duplication and unnecessary toggling.
