Refactor the calendar module by splitting the logic into three separate always blocks, one for each of Seconds, Minutes, and Hours, as originally specified. Each always block updates its respective counter on the clock edge or reset. This separation simplifies individual counter logic, reduces combinational path length per block, and enables easier gating or optimization later. Non-blocking assignments ensure synchronous updates, and inter-block communication is handled via conditions on the current values of counters from previous clock cycle. This structural change helps timing and power by avoiding long nested if-else chains in a single block, and also explicitly matches the original requirements.
