Fuse the advantages of both solutions by keeping the counters in three separate always blocks to reduce the critical path length and improve timing, while ensuring conditional increments only occur when needed to reduce switching activity and save power. Use nonblocking assignments for correct synchronous updates.

Specifically:
- Seconds increment every clock cycle or reset.
- Minutes increment only when seconds wrap (Secs==59), else hold.
- Hours increment only when minutes and seconds wrap (Mins==59 && Secs==59), else hold.

This modular approach balances logic depth and power optimization.

Maintain minimal bit widths: Hours 5 bits, Minutes and Seconds 6 bits.

All blocks are triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST for immediate reset behavior.

This fusion takes the modularity and power-conscious conditional updates from the second solution and the clarity and correctness of the first, leading to superior PPA.
