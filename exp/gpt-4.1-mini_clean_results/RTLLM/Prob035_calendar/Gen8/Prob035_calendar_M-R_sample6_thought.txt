Refactor the calendar module to use a single always block that updates seconds, minutes, and hours together in a cascaded manner. This ensures atomic updates each clock cycle and eliminates possible race conditions between separate always blocks. Use nonblocking assignments and update counters in sequence: increment seconds; if seconds wrap, increment minutes; if minutes wrap, increment hours. Reset synchronously with clock and asynchronously with reset. This structure simplifies timing and may reduce logic and power by centralizing control and minimizing redundant clocked logic.
