To optimize the calendar module for better power and potentially timing, separate the counters into three distinct always blocks. Each counter updates only when necessary, reducing unnecessary toggling and combinational logic depth. Specifically, seconds always increment; minutes increment only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0; hours increment only when minutes and seconds both roll over from 59 to 0. This reduces logic dependency chain on each clock edge and enables better synthesis optimization, lowering switching activity and easing timing closure. The reset remains asynchronous active high. Also, keep the output widths consistent with the specification.
