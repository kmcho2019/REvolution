To improve the power and timing efficiency, we consolidate the minutes and hours counters into a single always block with an enable signal triggered only when seconds wrap (Secs == 59). This avoids redundant always blocks that update registers unnecessarily every clock cycle. Also, by creating enable signals for minutes and hours increments, toggling is reduced, potentially lowering power. The seconds counter remains separate to ensure straightforward incrementation every clock cycle. We keep nonblocking assignments and asynchronous reset for immediate reset response. Hours output width is 5 bits as 0-23 fits within 5 bits, Mins and Secs remain 6 bits.
