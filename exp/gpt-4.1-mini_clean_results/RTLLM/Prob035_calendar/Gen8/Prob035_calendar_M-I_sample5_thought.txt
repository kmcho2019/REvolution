To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will maintain a single always block to keep synchronous updates and reduce complexity. However, I will introduce an enable signal to gate counter increments so that counters only toggle when necessary. This reduces switching power. Also, I will replace nested if-else chains with a cleaner state update using arithmetic and modulus for seconds and minutes, and wrap for hours, which may improve synthesis quality and timing. Additionally, I will keep Hours as 5 bits, Mins and Secs as 6 bits, matching their required ranges, reducing area. This approach can improve timing by simplifying combinational logic, reduce power by limiting toggles, and slightly reduce area with better register and logic optimization.
