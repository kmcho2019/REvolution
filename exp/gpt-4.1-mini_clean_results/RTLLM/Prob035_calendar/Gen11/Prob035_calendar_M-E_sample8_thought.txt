Implement the calendar using a single always block triggered on the clock and reset. Internally maintain a single 17-bit counter representing the total number of seconds elapsed in a day (0 to 86399). On each clock tick, increment this counter and wrap at 86399 back to zero. Derive Hours, Mins, and Secs outputs combinationally from this counter using arithmetic operations: Hours = total_seconds / 3600, Mins = (total_seconds % 3600) / 60, Secs = total_seconds % 60. This method consolidates the counting into one register and eliminates cascading counters and multiple always blocks, simplifying the design and potentially improving timing by reducing sequential dependencies.
