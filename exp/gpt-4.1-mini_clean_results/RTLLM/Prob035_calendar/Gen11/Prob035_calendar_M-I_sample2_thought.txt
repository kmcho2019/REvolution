To optimize for better power and timing, split the calendar logic into three separate always blocks: one each for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block triggers on the clock and reset, updating only its own counter. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and wraps at 59; when seconds wrap, it enables the minutes block to increment; when minutes wrap, it enables the hours block. This reduces combinational logic depth and limits register toggling by updating minutes and hours only on necessary conditions, improving timing and reducing power. Use enable signals derived from seconds and minutes wrap conditions to control increments. Retain nonblocking assignments for proper sequential behavior. Hours remain 5 bits (0-23), minutes and seconds 6 bits (0-59).
