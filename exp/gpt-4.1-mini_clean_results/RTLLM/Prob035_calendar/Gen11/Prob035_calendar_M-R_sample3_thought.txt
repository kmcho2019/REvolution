Refactor the perpetual calendar counter into three separate always blocks, each controlling one time unit: seconds, minutes, and hours. Use an enable signal derived from the rollover condition of the lower unit to trigger increments in the higher unit. This modular approach clarifies the role of each counter and can reduce unnecessary toggling by updating minutes and hours only when their respective enable signals are active. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle, the minutes counter increments when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, and the hours counter increments when minutes roll over from 59 to 0. Reset synchronously clears all counters. This structure aligns with the original problem requirements and improves modularity and potentially power efficiency.
