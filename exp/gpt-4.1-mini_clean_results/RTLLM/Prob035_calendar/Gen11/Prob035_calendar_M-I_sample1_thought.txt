To improve the original single-block implementation for better power and timing, I will split the logic into three separate always blocks, each handling seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. This approach reduces combinational chain length, improving maximum clock frequency (performance). Furthermore, I will update minutes only when seconds roll over (Secs == 59), and update hours only when minutes and seconds roll over (Mins == 59 and Secs == 59). This selective updating reduces unnecessary toggling in minutes and hours registers (power optimization). The reset behavior remains the same. I will keep output widths as specified (Hours: 6-bit, Mins: 6-bit, Secs: 6-bit) for interface consistency despite hours requiring only 5 bits internally. This modular approach also improves code clarity and maintainability.
