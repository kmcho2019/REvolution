To optimize the original design for better PPA, I will consolidate the three always blocks into a single always block. This reduces logic duplication and critical path length by using a single clocked process to update all counters. Within this block, the seconds counter increments every cycle; when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, the minutes increment; when minutes and seconds roll over, the hours increment. This eliminates redundant condition checks and dependencies between multiple always blocks, helping timing closure and reducing power by decreasing switching activity and combinational complexity. I'll keep the same widths (5 bits for hours, 6 bits for minutes and seconds) and synchronous active-high reset. Non-blocking assignments remain for sequential logic clarity. This merged approach keeps the behavior and resets identical but streamlines control flow and optimizes logic sharing, reducing area and power and improving timing.
