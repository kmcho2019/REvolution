Implement the perpetual calendar with separate always blocks for each time unit (Secs, Mins, Hours), each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST, as requested. The seconds counter runs continuously and wraps from 59 to 0. The minutes counter increments only when seconds wrap, and the hours counter increments only when minutes wrap. This approach decouples the counters and their update logic, improving modularity and making the design easier to test and maintain. Outputs Hours, Mins, and Secs are all 6-bit wide as specified, even though Hours only needs 5 bits; this adheres strictly to the requirement. The reset asynchronously clears all counters to zero.
