Refactor the calendar module by using a single always block for all three counters (Secs, Mins, Hours) to reduce interdependent always blocks and improve clarity. Inside this single block, use a nested if-else structure to update seconds, then conditionally update minutes and hours within the same clock cycle. This removes the need to check stale values of seconds and minutes in separate always blocks and potentially improves timing and power by minimizing redundant clocked logic. The reset remains asynchronous for quick clearing. Outputs remain 6-bit for Secs and Mins, and 6-bit for Hours to maintain consistency, although only 5 bits are needed for hours 0-23.
