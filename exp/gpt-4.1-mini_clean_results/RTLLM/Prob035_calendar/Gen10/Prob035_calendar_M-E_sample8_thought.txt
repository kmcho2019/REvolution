Implement a single always block to handle seconds, minutes, and hours updates together synchronously. The block triggers on the rising edge of CLK or RST, resetting all counters when RST is high. On each clock cycle, seconds increment; if seconds roll over from 59 to 0, increment minutes; if minutes also roll over from 59 to 0, increment hours; if hours reach 23 and roll over, wrap to 0. Use nonblocking assignments to ensure correct sequencing of updates. This single-block approach reduces interdependent always blocks and can simplify synthesis and timing. Hours will be 5 bits (0-23), minutes and seconds 6 bits (0-59), all starting from zero after reset.
