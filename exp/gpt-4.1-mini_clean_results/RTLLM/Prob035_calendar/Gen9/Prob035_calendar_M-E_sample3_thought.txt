Implement a single always block that updates all counters (Secs, Mins, Hours) together on each clock edge. This approach centralizes the time-keeping logic and reduces multiple clocked processes to one, potentially improving timing by limiting cross-block dependencies. The logic will increment seconds each clock, then if seconds overflow (59 to 0), increment minutes; if minutes overflow similarly, increment hours and wrap hours at 23 to 0. 

Use synchronous reset only, simplifying timing and avoiding asynchronous reset complications. Hours remain 5 bits, and seconds and minutes are 6 bits each.

This single-block approach improves readability, simplifies state updates, reduces redundant clock domain handling, and might improve performance and power by consolidating logic.
