Use three separate always blocks to handle seconds, minutes, and hours independently but coordinated through flags. Each always block is triggered on posedge CLK or RST. The seconds block increments every clock and signals a rollover flag when seconds wrap from 59 to 0. The minutes block increments only when the seconds rollover flag is asserted, rolling over at 59 and signaling an hours rollover flag similarly. The hours block increments when the minutes rollover flag is asserted and rolls over from 23 to 0. This modular structure separates concerns, potentially improving synthesis and timing by reducing combinational path complexity in each block. Outputs keep 6-bit width for seconds and minutes, 6-bit for hours to follow original specification (even though 5 bits suffice), as requested.
