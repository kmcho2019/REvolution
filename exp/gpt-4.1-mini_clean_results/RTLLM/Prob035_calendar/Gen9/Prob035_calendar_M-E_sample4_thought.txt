Implement the perpetual calendar using three separate always blocks, one for seconds, one for minutes, and one for hours, each triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block handles its own counting with appropriate wrap-around and increment logic, synchronized via enable signals generated from the lower order counters. This modular design improves clarity and timing by distributing logic, allowing the seconds counter to tick every clock cycle, minutes increment only when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and hours increment only when both minutes and seconds wrap around. Non-blocking assignments ensure proper sequencing and reset initializes all counters to zero. Using 6-bit registers for seconds and minutes and 6 bits for hours as required by the problem (though hours only count 0-23, keeping 6 bits for uniformity).
