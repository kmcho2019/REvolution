Implement the calendar module using three separate always blocks for Secs, Mins, and Hours respectively, all triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block handles its own rollover and increment independently by using enable conditions derived from lower-level counters. This approach simplifies each always block's logic, reduces nested conditions, and clearly separates concerns while maintaining the required functionality and bit widths. Use non-blocking assignments for proper sequential logic behavior.
