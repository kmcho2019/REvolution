Implement the calendar using three separate always blocks for Seconds, Minutes, and Hours respectively, each triggered on the posedge of CLK or RST. The seconds block increments every clock cycle or resets to 0 on RST, wrapping from 59 back to 0. The minutes block increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, also resetting on RST and wrapping at 59. The hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0, resetting on RST and wrapping at 23. This separation clarifies the hierarchy of increments and eases timing analysis by localizing logic for each time unit. Hours is a 6-bit output as requested, though only values up to 23 are valid.
