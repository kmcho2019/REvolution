Use three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours counters, each with synchronous reset and clock enable signals derived from lower-level counters. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle and wraps at 59, generating a 'sec_rollover' signal. The minutes counter increments only when sec_rollover is asserted, wraps at 59, and generates a 'min_rollover' signal. The hours counter increments only when min_rollover is asserted, wraps at 23. This modular approach splits timing-critical paths, allowing easier timing closure. Using clock enables for each stage reduces unnecessary toggling, improving power efficiency. The outputs Hours, Mins, and Secs are declared as 6-bit registers for uniformity, though hours only need 5 bits. This approach improves readability, debuggability, and reduces combinational logic depth per always block.
