Implement the perpetual calendar using three independent always blocks as per the original instructions, each triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block maintains its respective counter (Secs, Mins, Hours) and updates based on conditions from the other counters, explicitly reflecting the cascading effect of time counting. This design follows a modular and clear structure for each time unit, enhancing readability and ease of debugging. The Hours, Mins, and Secs outputs are 6-bit registers as specified. This approach emphasizes explicit control and avoids combinational next-state logic.
