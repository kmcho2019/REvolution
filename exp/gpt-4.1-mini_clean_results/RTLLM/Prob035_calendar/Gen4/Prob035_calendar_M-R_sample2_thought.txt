Refactor the perpetual calendar by splitting the time counters into three separate always blocks, each handling seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. Use intermediate signals to indicate rollover conditions to drive increments in the next counters. This modular approach simplifies each block's logic, reduces combinational complexity inside one always block, and can ease timing optimization. Each always block responds to clock and reset and updates its counter based on the rollover signal from the previous unit. The rollover signal is generated combinationally inside each always block. This structure retains the same functionality but uses distributed sequential logic instead of nested conditionals.
