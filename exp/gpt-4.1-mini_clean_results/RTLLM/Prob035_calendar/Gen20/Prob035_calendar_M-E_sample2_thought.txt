Implement the perpetual calendar as a single always block triggered on the positive edge of CLK or RST, using chained conditional updates for seconds, minutes, and hours. 

Instead of separate always blocks or next-state logic, use a single synchronous block that directly updates all counters with non-blocking assignments, maintaining their relations:

- On reset, clear all counters.
- On each clock, increment seconds.
- When seconds overflow from 59 to 0, increment minutes.
- When minutes overflow from 59 to 0 (due to seconds wrapping), increment hours.
- When hours overflow from 23 to 0 (due to minutes and seconds wrapping), wrap hours.

Use 6-bit registers for Secs and Mins, 6 bits for Hours (though 5 bits suffice, using 6 bits here for uniformity and simplicity).

This approach simplifies the design, reduces registers for next states, and easily infers ripple counters for time.

The code remains clear and concise, suitable for synthesis with minimal overhead.
