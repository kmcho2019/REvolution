To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), we can merge the three always blocks into a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or RST. This consolidation removes redundant logic, improves timing by reducing dependencies between separate counters, and potentially reduces area by sharing combinational logic. 

We implement a single synchronous counter that increments seconds every clock cycle. When seconds roll over from 59 to 0, minutes are incremented; similarly, when minutes roll over from 59 to 0 and seconds are 0, hours are incremented. Hours wrap after 23.

Using a single always block minimizes inter-dependent timing paths across multiple always blocks, reduces logic complexity, and provides a natural clock enable for minutes and hours updates only on seconds rollover, cutting switching activity and thus power.

We keep Hours at 5 bits (0–23), Mins and Secs at 6 bits (0–59). Non-blocking assignments ensure correct sequential behavior. Clear comments maintain readability. 

This approach improves timing by eliminating multi-block interlocks, reduces power by limiting toggling to rollover events, and may reduce area by simplifying logic synthesis.
