Use three separate always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or RST for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block resets its counter to zero when RST is asserted. The seconds block increments from 0 to 59 and wraps around. The minutes block increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, and wraps at 59. The hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0, wrapping at 23. This splits the logic to reduce combinational path complexity, simplifies each block, and keeps functionality correct.
