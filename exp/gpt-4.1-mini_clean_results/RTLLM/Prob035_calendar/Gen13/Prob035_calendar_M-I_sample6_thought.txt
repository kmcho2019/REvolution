To improve the original design's PPA metrics, especially power and timing, I will:
- Combine all three counters into a single always block triggered by posedge CLK or RST. This reduces the number of always blocks and the related combinational logic, potentially improving timing and area.
- Introduce clock-enable style gating inside the always block, updating minutes and hours only when seconds roll over to zero, minimizing toggling and logic complexity.
- Maintain non-blocking assignments and reset behavior as specified.
- Keep Hours as 5 bits, Mins and Secs as 6 bits.
- Add clear comments for maintainability.
This merged approach should reduce critical path complexity by eliminating repeated conditional checks across always blocks and lowering power by gating increments inside the single always block.
