Refactor the perpetual calendar by merging the three counters into a single always block triggered by CLK and RST. Use intermediate signals to indicate when seconds and minutes roll over, thus controlling when to increment minutes and hours respectively. This approach centralizes the state update logic, simplifies state transition, and reduces the number of always blocks, potentially improving synthesis clarity and optimization opportunities. Output registers remain 6-bit for seconds and minutes, 6-bit for hours (to match requested width, though hours only go up to 23). This unified block also avoids repeated sensitivity list declarations and better aligns with a synchronous design style.
