Fuse the modularity and power-aware clock enable signals from Example 2 with the concise, single-block update logic from Example 1 to achieve a design that is both clean and efficient. Use a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST for synchronous reset and update to reduce register update complexity and improve timing. Inside the block, implement conditional increments with nested if-else statements: increment seconds every cycle, roll over to zero at 59, increment minutes only when seconds wrap, and increment hours only when both minutes and seconds wrap. This eliminates unnecessary toggling of minutes and hours registers on every clock, reducing power by only updating on rollovers. Using non-blocking assignments preserves correct sequential behavior. Using proper bit widths (5 bits for hours, 6 bits for minutes and seconds) optimizes area. This approach combines the clarity and power efficiency of gated increments with the simpler timing of a single always block, minimizing combinational dependencies and improving critical path.
