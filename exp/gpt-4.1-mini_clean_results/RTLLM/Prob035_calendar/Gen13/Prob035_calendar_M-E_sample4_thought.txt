Implement the perpetual calendar as a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST to ensure synchronous updates with asynchronous reset. Instead of three separate counters, use a combined state update that increments seconds each clock cycle, and upon seconds rolling over from 59 to 0, increment minutes. Similarly, when minutes roll over from 59 to 0, increment hours. Hours roll over from 23 back to 0. This approach simplifies the design by consolidating counter logic, reducing interdependencies between always blocks, potentially improving timing and reducing logic duplication. Outputs remain 6 bits for Secs and Mins (range 0-59) and 5 bits for Hours (0-23). Use non-blocking assignments to preserve synchronous logic behavior.
