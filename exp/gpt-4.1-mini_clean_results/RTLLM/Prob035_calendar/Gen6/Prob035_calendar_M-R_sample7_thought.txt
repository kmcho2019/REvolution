Refactor the perpetual calendar into a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. This centralized approach reduces interdependencies and potential multi-flop delays by updating seconds, minutes, and hours together in one synchronous block. It maintains the same counting logic but improves timing and clarity by having a single source of updates and clear priority of increments. Non-blocking assignments are used to update all outputs simultaneously. The output widths remain the same to match their maximum values.
