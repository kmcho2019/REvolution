Implement the calendar module by dividing the functionality into three separate always blocks, each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block is responsible for one time unit: seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and wraps at 59. The minutes block increments only when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and similarly wraps at 59. The hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds wrap and wraps at 23. This separation clearly reflects the hierarchical time progression and simplifies individual counter logic, making it easier to modify or extend if needed. The design maintains synchronous reset and uses minimal bit widths for each register (Secs and Mins: 6 bits, Hours: 5 bits).
