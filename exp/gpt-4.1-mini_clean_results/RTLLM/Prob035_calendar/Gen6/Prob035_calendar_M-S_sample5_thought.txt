Use a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or RST to synchronously update seconds, minutes, and hours with minimal nested conditions. Reset all counters when RST is high. On each clock, increment seconds; if seconds overflow (59), reset to zero and increment minutes; if minutes overflow (59), reset to zero and increment hours; if hours overflow (23), reset to zero. This flat structure avoids deep nesting and keeps the design simple yet fully functional.
