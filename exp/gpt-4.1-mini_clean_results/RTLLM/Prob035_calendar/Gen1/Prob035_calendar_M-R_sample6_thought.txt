Refactor the perpetual calendar module by merging all counters into a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. This consolidates logic, potentially reducing area and simplifying timing paths. Inside the always block, first handle reset, then increment seconds each clock cycle. When seconds reach 59, reset to 0 and increment minutes. When minutes reach 59 and seconds reach 59, reset minutes and increment hours. When hours reach 23, minutes and seconds reach 59, reset hours. This single-block approach reduces redundant checks and avoids multiple registers updating independently, improving clarity and possibly performance. Also, reduce Hours bit-width to 5 bits since max 23 fits in 5 bits.
