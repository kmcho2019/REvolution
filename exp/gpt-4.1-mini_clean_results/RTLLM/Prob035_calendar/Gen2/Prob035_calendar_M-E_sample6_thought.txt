Implement the calendar module using three separate always blocks as requested, each handling one time unit (Secs, Mins, Hours) independently but synchronously. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle; the minutes counter increments on the falling edge of seconds from 59 to 0, detected by checking if seconds are 59 in the next clock cycle; similarly, hours increment on the falling edge of minutes from 59 to 0, detected when minutes and seconds are both 59 before the clock edge. This staged, modular approach makes logic simple and explicit while adhering strictly to the problem's requirements.
