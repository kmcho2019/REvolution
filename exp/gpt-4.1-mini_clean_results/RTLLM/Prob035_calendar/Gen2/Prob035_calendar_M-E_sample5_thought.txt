Implement the calendar module using three separate always blocks as requested, each dedicated to updating one time unit: seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block is sensitive to posedge CLK and posedge RST. Use flags from the seconds block to signal rollover to the minutes block, and similarly from the minutes block to the hours block. This modular approach allows clear separation of concerns and easy debugging. To support the output widths, Hours will be 6-bit as requested (though only 5 bits are needed), Mins and Secs are 6 bits. The minutes block increments only when seconds roll over, and the hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over, as specified.
