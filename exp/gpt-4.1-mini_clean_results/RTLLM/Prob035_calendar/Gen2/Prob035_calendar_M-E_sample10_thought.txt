Implement the perpetual calendar using a single always block to update all time units synchronously. Use an internal carry logic to propagate increments from seconds to minutes, and from minutes to hours. This consolidates the counting logic into one block, simplifying control flow and potentially improving performance by reducing combinational paths between separate blocks. Hours remain 5-bit as 0-23. On reset, all outputs are cleared to zero. On each clock edge, seconds increment and when wrapping from 59 to 0, a carry triggers minutes increment, and similarly for hours when minutes wrap. This structure minimizes logic duplication and eases timing optimization.
