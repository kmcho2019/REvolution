Implement the calendar module using three separate always blocks, each dedicated to one time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) to clearly isolate their behavior and dependencies. Use the seconds block to increment the seconds counter every clock cycle (or reset). The minutes block increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, detected via a condition on seconds. The hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over from their max to zero, detected via combined conditions. This modular approach increases clarity and eases debugging and verification. Non-blocking assignments ensure correct sequential updates, and asynchronous reset is handled in all blocks. This architecture aligns well with classic RTL design principles, making each counter's logic simple and directly reflecting its counting conditions.
