Refactor the calendar module by separating the counters into three synchronous always blocks, each triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle or resets on RST. The minutes counter increments only when seconds rollover from 59 to 0. The hours counter increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over from 59 to 0. This modular separation reduces combinational logic depth inside each always block, improving timing and clock frequency, while maintaining the same functionality. The outputs are declared reg and updated synchronously. Bit widths remain 6 for seconds and minutes, and 6 bits for hours for uniformity, but hours will be kept as 6-bit for simplicity.
