Fuse the best qualities of both designs by implementing a single always block for all counters (Secs, Mins, Hours) triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST to guarantee atomic and synchronous updates, minimizing combinational cascades and ensuring clear increment dependencies. Use minimal bit widths: 6 bits for Secs and Mins (0-59) and 5 bits for Hours (0-23). Inside the block, on reset, clear all counters. Otherwise, increment seconds every clock cycle; if seconds wrap, increment minutes; if minutes wrap, increment hours; and if hours wrap after 23, reset to zero. This approach reduces toggle activity on minutes and hours by only updating them when needed, reduces combinational path depth for better timing, avoids multiple always blocks that share cross-counter conditions, and preserves clarity. The compact, single-block design also eases synthesis and potential power gating.
