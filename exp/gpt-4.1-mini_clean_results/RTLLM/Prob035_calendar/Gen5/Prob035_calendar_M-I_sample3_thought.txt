To optimize the perpetual calendar for better performance, power, and area, I will combine all counters into a single always block triggered on posedge CLK and posedge RST, thus reducing interdependent combinational logic across multiple always blocks. This consolidation removes multi-always-block dependencies and allows straightforward priority encoding for state updates, likely improving timing by shortening critical paths.

I will also introduce clock enable signals derived from the seconds rollover to update minutes and hours only when needed. This reduces unnecessary toggling in minutes and hours registers, saving dynamic power.

Hours remain 5 bits, minutes and seconds 6 bits for area efficiency. The synchronous reset is preserved for timing closure.

The logic flow:
- On reset, clear all counters.
- Every clock tick, increment seconds; if seconds reach 59, roll over to 0 and enable minute increment.
- If minute increment enabled, increment minutes; if minutes reach 59 and seconds just rolled over, roll minutes to 0 and enable hour increment.
- If hour increment enabled, increment hours; if hours reach 23 and minutes and seconds at max, roll hours to 0.

This single-block design with clock enables reduces logic complexity and switching activity, aiding power and timing improvements, while area is reduced by fewer logic gates and registers in multiple blocks.
