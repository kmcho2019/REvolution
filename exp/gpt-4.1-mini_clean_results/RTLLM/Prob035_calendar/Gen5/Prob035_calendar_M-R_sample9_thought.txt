Refactor the calendar module by splitting the counter updates into three separate always blocks, each handling seconds, minutes, and hours respectively, triggered on the posedge of CLK or posedge of RST. This modular approach reflects the problem description more closely, improving readability and potentially enabling more straightforward timing optimizations since each counter block focuses on a single variable.
