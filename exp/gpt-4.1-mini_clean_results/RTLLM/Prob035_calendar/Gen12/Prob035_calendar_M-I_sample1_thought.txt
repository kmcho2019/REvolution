To improve PPA, I will split the counters into three separate always blocks: one for seconds, one for minutes, and one for hours. This breaks the long combinational logic chain and reduces critical path delay, improving timing (TNS, WNS).

Also, to reduce switching power, I will update minutes only when seconds roll from 59 to 0, and hours only when both minutes and seconds wrap, instead of updating every cycle. This conditional increment reduces toggling in minutes and hours registers.

The counter widths will remain the same (5-bit for hours, 6-bit for minutes and seconds).

This modular approach enhances performance by reducing logic depth and improves power by conditional updates without area penalty.

The reset logic remains synchronous to RST for all counters.
