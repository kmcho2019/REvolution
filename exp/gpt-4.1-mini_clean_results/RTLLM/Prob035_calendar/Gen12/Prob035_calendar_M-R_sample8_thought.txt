Refactor the perpetual calendar by combining the seconds, minutes, and hours counters into a single always block triggered on clock and reset. Use internal next-state signals for each time unit, calculating the increments and wrap-arounds in combinational logic before updating the registers synchronously. This approach centralizes the logic, reduces interdependent always blocks, and improves timing by minimizing inter-block dependencies. Output ports remain registered. The code keeps the same counting ranges and behavior but improves structural clarity and potential synthesis efficiency.
