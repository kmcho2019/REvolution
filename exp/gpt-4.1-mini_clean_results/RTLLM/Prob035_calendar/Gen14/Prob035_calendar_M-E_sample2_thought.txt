Implement the calendar using three separate always blocks, one for each time unit (Secs, Mins, Hours), each triggered on the clock and reset signals. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle, resetting to zero at 59. The minutes counter increments only when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and resets at 59. The hours counter increments only when both minutes and seconds wrap, and resets at 23. This modular approach separates the concerns of each time unit, enabling easier debugging and potentially better timing as the logic is distributed. Use non-blocking assignments and asynchronous reset signals for each block. Define outputs as registers with 6 bits width for Secs and Mins and 6 bits for Hours (even though hours need only 5 bits, 6 bits maintain uniformity and simplicity). This explicit separation adheres closely to the problem description and enhances clarity of timing relationships between units.
