Implement the perpetual calendar using a single always block that sequentially updates seconds, minutes, and hours within one clock cycle. This approach uses a cascading counter logic where seconds increment every cycle; when seconds overflow, minutes increment; when minutes overflow, hours increment; hours wrap around after 23. This consolidation reduces inter-clock-cycle dependencies and simplifies synchronization, potentially improving timing and area by minimizing redundant logic. Also, keep all outputs as 6-bit wide for uniformity, although hours only need up to 23. The asynchronous reset is handled at the beginning of the always block. Non-blocking assignments ensure correct sequential behavior.
