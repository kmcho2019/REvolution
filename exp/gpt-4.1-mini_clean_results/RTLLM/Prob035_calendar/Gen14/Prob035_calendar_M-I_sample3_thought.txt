This decouples the logic, reducing combinational dependencies in each clock cycle, which can improve timing by shortening critical paths. Clock gating at RTL is not generally synthesizable, but by updating minutes only when seconds roll over and hours only when minutes roll over, toggling is naturally reduced, lowering dynamic power. Using nonblocking assignments ensures proper synchronization and timing. The widths remain the same (Hours: 5 bits, Mins and Secs: 6 bits). This modular approach also simplifies the logic, potentially improving both timing and power without area penalty.
