Refactor the perpetual calendar to use three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours, each triggered on posedge CLK or RST. This modularizes the design, improving readability and potentially reducing combinational path length. Each block will handle its own counter and rollover logic, referencing the others for rollovers: seconds increments every cycle and resets at 59; minutes increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0; hours increments only when minutes roll over from 59 to 0 and seconds are zero. This separation preserves the same counting behavior while distributing logic to simpler blocks. The counters keep their widths (Secs and Mins 6-bit, Hours 5-bit). This approach can make debugging and timing optimization easier by isolating counter increments.
