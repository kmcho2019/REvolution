To improve the original single-block calendar design in terms of timing and power, split the functionality into three separate always blocks, each updating seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. This breaks the combinational logic chains between them, thus potentially improving timing. Use enable signals so that minutes increment only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, and hours increment only when both seconds and minutes roll over. This prevents unnecessary toggling of minutes and hours on every clock cycle, thereby reducing switching activity and power consumption. Use nonblocking assignments and synchronous resets in each block to ensure correct and clean design behavior.
