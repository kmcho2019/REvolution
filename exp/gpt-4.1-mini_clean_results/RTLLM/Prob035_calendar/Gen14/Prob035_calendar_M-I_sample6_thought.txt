To optimize for power and timing, split the logic into three always blocks—one each for Secs, Mins, and Hours—using enable signals to update Mins only when Secs rolls over, and Hours only when both Secs and Mins roll over. This reduces unnecessary toggling of Mins and Hours registers and shortens combinational paths per block, improving timing and lowering dynamic power. Keep the widths as specified: Hours at 6 bits for output port consistency (even though 5 bits suffice internally), and Secs and Mins at 6 bits. All always blocks are triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST as per specification.
