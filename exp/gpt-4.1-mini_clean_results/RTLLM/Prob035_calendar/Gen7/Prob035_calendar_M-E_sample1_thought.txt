Use three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours counters, each triggered by the clock and reset signals. The seconds block increments seconds every clock cycle and resets to 0 after 59. The minutes block increments only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0. Similarly, the hours block increments only when both minutes and seconds roll over (minutes and seconds both 59 to 0). This modular approach cleanly separates the counting logic, making it easy to understand and maintain. All counters reset to zero asynchronously on reset. This design follows the exact problem description, splitting functionality across three always blocks for clarity.
