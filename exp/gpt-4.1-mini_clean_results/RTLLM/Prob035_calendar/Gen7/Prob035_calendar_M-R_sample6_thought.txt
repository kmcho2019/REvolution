Refactor the calendar module to use a single always block for all counters, leveraging internal carry signals to propagate increments from seconds to minutes and hours. This approach reduces interdependency issues between always blocks, potentially improving timing and simplifying the control logic. Nonblocking assignments are used to ensure proper sequential behavior. Also, output widths are adjusted as per specification: Hours as 6 bits (even though max is 23), Minutes and Seconds as 6 bits. Reset logic is kept synchronous and active-high asynchronous as required.
