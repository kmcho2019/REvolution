Refactor the calendar module by implementing the counters with separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter updates based on its own logic and input signals derived from the previous counter's rollover condition. This modular approach clarifies the dependency between counters, potentially easing timing analysis by breaking long combinational chains. Use synchronous reset in each always block. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle. When seconds wrap from 59 to 0, the seconds rollover signal enables the minutes counter to increment. Similarly, when minutes wrap from 59 to 0 (and seconds have rolled over), an hours enable signal triggers the hours counter increment. The hours counter wraps at 23. This separation also aligns well with hardware design style and maintains the correctness and timing behavior of the original design.
