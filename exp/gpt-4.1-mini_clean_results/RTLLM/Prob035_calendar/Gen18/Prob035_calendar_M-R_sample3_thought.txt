Refactor the calendar module to use three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours respectively, each triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. This approach matches the user's initial request explicitly for three always blocks.

Each always block handles its counter and rollover logic independently:

- The seconds always block increments seconds on each clock cycle or resets them asynchronously.
- The minutes always block increments minutes only when seconds roll over from 59 to 0, or resets asynchronously.
- The hours always block increments hours only when both minutes and seconds roll over (i.e., minutes = 59 and seconds = 59), or resets asynchronously.

Using separate always blocks makes the logic clearer in modularity and follows the original specification strictly, while the counters remain non-blocking updated for proper sequential behavior.

This design may have slightly more complexity in control signals, but it explicitly meets the requirement for three always blocks, improving readability and maintainability.

All outputs keep the original bit widths (Hours 6-bit, Mins 6-bit, Secs 6-bit) for consistency, although Hours only needs 5 bits to represent 0-23.

Non-blocking assignments are used to maintain proper synchronous semantics.
