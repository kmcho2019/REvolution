Use a single always block triggered by posedge CLK or RST to update all three counters together. This removes the need for multiple blocks and repeated condition checks, simplifying logic and potentially reducing area and power. The counters increment hierarchically: seconds increment every clock; if seconds wrap from 59 to 0, minutes increment; if minutes wrap from 59 to 0 at that time, hours increment and wrap from 23 to 0. Using simple if-else cascaded conditions keeps the logic straightforward and clear while maintaining correct wrap-around behavior.
