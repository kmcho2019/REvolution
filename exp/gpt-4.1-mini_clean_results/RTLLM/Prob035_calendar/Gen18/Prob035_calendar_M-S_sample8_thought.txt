Use a single always block triggered on posedge CLK or RST to synchronously update seconds, minutes, and hours with simple nested if-else statements. On reset, clear all counters. On each clock, increment seconds; if seconds roll over from 59 to 0, increment minutes; if minutes roll over from 59 to 0, increment hours; if hours roll over from 23 to 0, wrap around. This approach reduces complexity by avoiding intermediate variables and conditional register updates while maintaining clear rollover logic.
