Implement three always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each handles the reset condition and increments or resets its respective counter (Secs, Mins, Hours) based on the described conditions. Secs count 0-59; when 59 rolls over to 0 and increments Mins. Mins count 0-59; when both Mins and Secs are 59, increments Hours. Hours count 0-23; when Hours=23, Mins=59, Secs=59 roll over to 0.
