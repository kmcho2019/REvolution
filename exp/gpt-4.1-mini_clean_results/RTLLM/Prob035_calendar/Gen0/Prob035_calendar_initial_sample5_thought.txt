Implement three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. Each block is sensitive to posedge CLK and posedge RST. On reset, set the counter to 0. For seconds, increment every clock cycle until 59, then wrap to 0. For minutes, increment when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping itself after 59. For hours, increment when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, wrapping after 23.
