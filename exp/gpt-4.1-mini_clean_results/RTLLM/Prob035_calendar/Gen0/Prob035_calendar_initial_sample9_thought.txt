Implement a perpetual calendar module named "calendar" with inputs CLK and RST, and outputs Hours, Mins, and Secs as 6-bit registers. Use three separate always blocks triggered on the positive edge of CLK or RST. On reset, all outputs are zeroed. The seconds counter increments every clock cycle, wrapping from 59 to 0. When seconds wrap from 59 to 0, increment the minutes counter, which similarly wraps from 59 to 0. When minutes and seconds both wrap from 59 to 0, increment the hours counter, which wraps from 23 to 0. Each counter only changes on the positive clock edge or reset.
