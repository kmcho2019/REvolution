Implement the perpetual calendar with three always blocks triggered by posedge CLK or posedge RST. Each block handles seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. On reset, each is cleared to 0. Seconds increment every clock cycle and wrap at 59. Minutes increment by 1 when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and wrap at 59. Hours increment by 1 when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and wrap at 23. This design ensures correct cascading increments and proper wrap-around.
