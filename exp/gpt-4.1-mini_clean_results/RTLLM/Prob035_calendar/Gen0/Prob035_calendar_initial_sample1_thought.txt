Implement a calendar module with three always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block triggers on the rising edge of CLK or RST. On reset, outputs are set to 0. Seconds count from 0 to 59, then wrap to 0. When seconds wrap from 59 to 0, minutes increment by 1 unless minutes are 59 and seconds are 59, in which case minutes wrap to 0. Similarly, hours increment by 1 when minutes and seconds both wrap from 59 to 0, and hours wrap from 23 to 0. Each output is 6 bits wide.
