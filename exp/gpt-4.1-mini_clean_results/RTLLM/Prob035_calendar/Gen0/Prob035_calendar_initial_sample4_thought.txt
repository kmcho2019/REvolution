Implement a calendar module with three always blocks that independently update seconds, minutes, and hours on the positive edge of CLK or RST. On reset, all counters reset to zero. Seconds increment every clock cycle from 0 to 59 then wrap. When seconds wrap from 59 to 0, minutes increment similarly from 0 to 59. When both minutes and seconds wrap, hours increment from 0 to 23 and wrap. Hours, minutes, and seconds are all 6-bit outputs to represent their values.
