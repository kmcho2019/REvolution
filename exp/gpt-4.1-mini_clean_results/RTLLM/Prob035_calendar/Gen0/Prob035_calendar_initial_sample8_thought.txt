Use three separate always blocks triggered on posedge CLK or posedge RST for seconds, minutes, and hours respectively. On reset, all counters are cleared to zero. Seconds increment from 0 to 59, wrapping to 0. Minutes increment by 1 when seconds wrap from 59 to 0, and also wrap from 59 to 0. Hours increment by 1 when both minutes and seconds wrap from 59 to 0 and wrap after 23. The outputs are registered 6-bit values for Hours, Mins, Secs.
