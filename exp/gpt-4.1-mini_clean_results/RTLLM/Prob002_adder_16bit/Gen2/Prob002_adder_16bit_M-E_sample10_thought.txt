Implement the 16-bit adder by instantiating four 4-bit ripple carry adder modules hierarchically. Each 4-bit adder internally uses a generate-for loop of full adders for fine granularity and modularity. This allows easy scalability and debugging. The carry-out of each 4-bit adder connects to the carry-in of the next, forming a ripple carry chain at a 4-bit block granularity. This approach breaks the design into smaller building blocks than 8-bit, offering better modularity and clarity on carry propagation through the 16-bit word.
