To optimize the original 16-bit adder design for better performance (reduce delay) and potentially reduce power consumption without increasing area significantly, I will improve the 8-bit adder by implementing a carry-lookahead scheme instead of a ripple carry chain.

Improvement strategy:
1. Replace the ripple-carry logic inside the 8-bit adder with a carry-lookahead logic that calculates the carry signals in parallel using generate (G) and propagate (P) signals.
2. This reduces the carry propagation delay within the 8-bit block from linear to logarithmic complexity, improving timing significantly.
3. Keep the module interface and hierarchical structure the same, to maintain modularity and reusability.
4. The top-level 16-bit adder still cascades two 8-bit blocks, with carry-out from the lower block feeding carry-in of the upper.
5. This carry-lookahead 8-bit adder increases combinational logic slightly but overall yields better performance and can reduce dynamic switching in carries, lowering power.

Thus, the improved design balances a modest increase in complexity inside the 8-bit adder for a better critical path delay, while keeping the modular 16-bit adder structure intact.
