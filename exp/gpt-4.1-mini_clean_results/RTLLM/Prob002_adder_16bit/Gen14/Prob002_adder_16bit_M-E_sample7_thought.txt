Implement the 16-bit full adder using a hierarchical carry lookahead adder (CLA) architecture composed of four 4-bit CLA modules instantiated multiple times. Each 4-bit CLA calculates sum and carry-out with fast carry lookahead logic internally. The 16-bit adder chains the four 4-bit CLAs, passing carry-out to carry-in signals, thus speeding up carry propagation compared to ripple-carry.

This approach improves performance by reducing carry propagation delay logarithmically with adder width. It also demonstrates a modular and hierarchical design approach, different from the ripple carry adder style.

The 4-bit CLA uses generate and propagate signals to compute carries for each bit quickly, avoiding linear carry chain delay.

The top-level 16-bit adder instantiates four 4-bit CLA blocks for bits [3:0], [7:4], [11:8], and [15:12], connecting carry signals between them.

This design is fully combinational, uses no generate loops, and emphasizes hierarchical and structured architecture for clarity and speed.
