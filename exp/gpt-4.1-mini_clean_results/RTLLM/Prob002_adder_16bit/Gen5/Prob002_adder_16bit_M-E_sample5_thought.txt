Implement a 16-bit adder by splitting it into four 4-bit adders connected in a carry-lookahead chain. Each 4-bit adder uses carry-lookahead logic internally for fast carry generation. Between the 4-bit blocks, a simple carry-lookahead logic is used to compute the carry-in for each block in parallel, avoiding ripple delay across blocks.

This hierarchical carry-lookahead design reduces the carry propagation delay significantly compared to a ripple-carry chain or even two 8-bit blocks chained in ripple carry. The design is fully combinational and uses modular 4-bit adders instantiated four times. The carry-lookahead between the 4-bit blocks calculates block carries in parallel from the initial Cin.

Thus, the architecture balances modularity and performance: small fast 4-bit CLA adders and block carry-lookahead for the 16-bit sum.
