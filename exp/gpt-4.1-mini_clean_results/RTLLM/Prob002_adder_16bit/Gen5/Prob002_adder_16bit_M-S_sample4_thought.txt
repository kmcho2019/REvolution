Design a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder without propagate/generate signals for internal carry, since this reduces logic and complexity while still functionally correct for small widths. The carry ripple will be implemented directly by chaining full adders.

Then instantiate two such 8-bit ripple adders in the 16-bit adder module, connecting the carry-out of the lower block to the carry-in of the upper block.

This approach reduces complexity by avoiding propagate/generate signals and carry lookahead logic, keeps the modular structure with reusable 8-bit adders, and still fully combinationally implements the 16-bit adder.
