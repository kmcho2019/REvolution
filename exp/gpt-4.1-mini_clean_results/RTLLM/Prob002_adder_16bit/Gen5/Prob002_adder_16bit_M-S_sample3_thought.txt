Use two 8-bit ripple carry adders instantiated twice to build the 16-bit adder. This reduces module instantiations compared to the previous 4-bit adder approach, simplifying the design while preserving modularity and functionality. The 8-bit adder will implement a similar ripple carry logic using a generate loop internally. The top-level 16-bit adder connects two 8-bit adders in series with carry chaining.
