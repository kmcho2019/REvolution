To combine the strengths of both examples, I will implement a 16-bit adder using two 8-bit adders for better balance between modularity and carry chain length, like Example 1. Within the 8-bit adder, instead of a ripple carry implemented via a generate loop, I will internally use two 4-bit adders chained together as in Example 2. This hierarchical approach reduces the critical path delay by shortening the carry chain within each 8-bit block (max 4-bit carry propagation instead of 8), and keeps modularity and clarity. The 4-bit adders are simple and well-structured with combinational logic. This fusion improves performance by halving ripple length inside the 8-bit adder while maintaining clean hierarchical design and easy instantiation in the 16-bit module.
