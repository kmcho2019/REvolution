Refactor the 8-bit adder by implementing it with a chain of full adder modules instead of a generate block of assign statements. Each full adder is a separate module instantiating the bit-level sum and carry. This hierarchical decomposition enhances clarity and modularity, showing explicitly the carry propagation. The 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit adders as before.

This approach replaces the combinational generate loop with explicit full adder instantiations, facilitating easier extension, debugging, and potential reuse of the single-bit full adder in other designs. The design remains purely combinational and synthesizable.
