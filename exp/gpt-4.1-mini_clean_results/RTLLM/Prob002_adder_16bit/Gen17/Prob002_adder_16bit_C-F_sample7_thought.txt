Fuse the strengths of both examples by implementing the 8-bit adder using propagate and generate signals for internal carry look-ahead within each 8-bit block, improving carry propagation speed inside the block, combined with a clean generate-for loop for scalability and readability. Then, build the 16-bit adder by instantiating two such 8-bit adders connected by a single carry signal.

This design:
- Uses the efficient carry look-ahead logic inside each 8-bit adder to minimize carry propagation delay within the block.
- Retains modular hierarchy by instantiating two 8-bit adders for the 16-bit adder.
- Maintains clean, readable, and synthesizable Verilog with generate loops.
- Is purely combinational and thus suitable for synthesis and easy timing analysis.
- Offers a better timing profile than simple ripple carry by speeding up carry generation inside blocks, while keeping the ripple only between the two 8-bit blocks, which is acceptable given the problem constraints.

This fusion improves performance inside blocks without increasing area or complexity significantly, preserves modularity and clarity, and optimizes carry computation for faster addition.
