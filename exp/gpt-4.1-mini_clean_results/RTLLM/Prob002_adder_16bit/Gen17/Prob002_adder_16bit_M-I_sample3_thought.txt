To optimize the original 16-bit adder design for better performance and area without significantly increasing power, I will replace the ripple carry logic in the 8-bit adder with a carry lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This improves the critical path delay by computing carry signals in parallel rather than serially rippling through each bit, thus increasing maximum operating frequency.

The carry lookahead logic uses generate and propagate signals to efficiently compute carry outputs for all bits simultaneously via hierarchical equations. The sum is then computed based on these carries.

The 16-bit adder hierarchy remains the same, instantiating two 8-bit CLAs in series. The lower 8-bit CLA outputs a carry that becomes the carry-in to the upper 8-bit CLA.

This approach achieves better timing (lower critical path delay) with a modest area increase compared to ripple carry adders and keeps power reasonable by avoiding excessive gating or complex structures.

Overall improvements:
- Replace ripple carry in 8-bit adder with carry lookahead logic.
- Maintain modularity and hierarchical instantiation.
- Use generate-for loop only for sum assignment since carry logic is now parallel.
