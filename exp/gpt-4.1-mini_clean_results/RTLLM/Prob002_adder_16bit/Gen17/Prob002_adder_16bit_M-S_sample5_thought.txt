To simplify the 16-bit adder design while maintaining functionality:

- Use a straightforward ripple-carry approach without propagate/generate signals or for-generate loops.
- Implement an 8-bit adder using basic bitwise XOR and AND for sum and carry generation in a simple ripple chain.
- Instantiate two such 8-bit adders in the 16-bit adder.
- This reduces code complexity by removing carry generate/propagate logic and generate loops, improving readability for basic educational or simple use cases.
- The design remains fully combinational and modular.
