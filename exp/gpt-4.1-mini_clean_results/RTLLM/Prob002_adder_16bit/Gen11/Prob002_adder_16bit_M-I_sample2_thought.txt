To improve the given 16-bit adder design for better Performance, Power, and Area (PPA), I will enhance carry computation by implementing a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure instead of simple ripple carry between two 8-bit adders. 

Specifically:
- Inside each 8-bit adder, keep the existing carry-lookahead logic.
- At the 16-bit level, replace the ripple carry from the lower 8-bit block to the upper block with a carry-lookahead signal computed using the group propagate and generate signals of both 8-bit blocks.
- Compute the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder in parallel by generating group propagate (P) and group generate (G) for each 8-bit block.
- Use these group P and G signals to calculate the carry into the upper block without waiting for the full ripple through the lower 8 bits, thus reducing the critical path delay.

This hierarchical carry-lookahead approach:
- Reduces carry propagation delay between blocks (improves timing).
- Keeps the modular 8-bit block structure for easy reuse and maintainability.
- Keeps combinational logic for synthesis without added complexity.
- Requires minimal additional logic for group P and G signals and their combination.

This optimization focuses primarily on reducing critical path delay while keeping power and area comparable or slightly improved due to less switching delay on carry signals.

Implementation details:
- Modify adder_8bit to output group propagate (P) and group generate (G).
- At the 16-bit level, compute carry_in_upper = G_lower | (P_lower & Cin).
- Use carry_in_upper as carry-in to upper 8-bit adder.
