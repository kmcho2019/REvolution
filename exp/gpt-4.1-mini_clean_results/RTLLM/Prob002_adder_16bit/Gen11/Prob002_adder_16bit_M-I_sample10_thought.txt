To improve the 16-bit full adder design's performance, power, and area, I will replace the ripple carry 8-bit adder with a carry lookahead adder (CLA) for each 8-bit block. The CLA significantly reduces carry propagation delay by generating carry signals in parallel using propagate/generate logic.

This will improve timing by reducing carry chain length, thus enabling a higher clock frequency or lower delay. It also reduces switching power by minimizing the number of sequential carry computations, which reduces glitching.

For area, although CLA logic is slightly more complex, the faster carry computation can allow smaller gate sizes or less buffering in synthesis, potentially reducing area overall.

The top-level 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit CLA blocks connected by a carry signal.

This modular approach preserves code clarity and reusability while optimizing PPA metrics significantly compared to ripple carry adders.
