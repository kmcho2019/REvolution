To optimize the 16-bit adder for better PPA, I will improve the 8-bit adder by implementing a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for the 8-bit block. This reduces the carry propagation delay significantly compared to a ripple carry adder, improving timing (tns, wns, and effective clock period). 

The carry-lookahead logic computes generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for all bits, then quickly derives carries in parallel using group generate and propagate signals. This speeds up carry evaluation, improving delay and power by reducing the long carry chain toggling.

Area may increase slightly due to additional logic for carry lookahead, but overall PPA improves since frequency and power gains outweigh the area cost for a 16-bit adder.

Then, I will instantiate two such 8-bit CLA adders to build the 16-bit adder, cascading via carry-out to carry-in. This keeps modularity and scalability.

In summary:
- Replace the ripple 8-bit adder with an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder.
- Retain the two-block structure for the 16-bit adder.
- Optimize code clarity and maintain a clean interface.
