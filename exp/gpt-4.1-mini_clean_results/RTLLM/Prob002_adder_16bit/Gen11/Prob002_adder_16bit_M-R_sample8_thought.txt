Keep the fundamental design of a 16-bit adder composed of two 8-bit adders, but change the internal carry-lookahead logic implementation of the 8-bit adder by explicitly calculating carry signals using a carry-lookahead prefix-style logic without generate loops. This means precomputing the group propagate and generate signals for 4-bit sub-blocks inside the 8-bit adder to reduce carry chain length internally.

This structure improves readability by explicitly showing carry lookahead stages and removes the generate loop by manual assignment, which can sometimes make debugging or verification easier.

At the top 16-bit level, keep the hierarchical instantiation of two 8-bit adders with carry chaining. 

The sum outputs will be assigned by XORing propagate signals with the corresponding carries.

This refactoring keeps pure combinational logic but uses explicit carry-lookahead equations for clarity and alternative structure.

Advantages:
- Explicit carry-lookahead stages inside 8-bit adder for clarity.
- No generate loops, potentially simpler for some tools or verification.
- Modular and hierarchical design remains.
- Same combinational logic with clear carry computation.

Disadvantages:
- Slightly longer code inside 8-bit adder due to unrolling carry assignments.
