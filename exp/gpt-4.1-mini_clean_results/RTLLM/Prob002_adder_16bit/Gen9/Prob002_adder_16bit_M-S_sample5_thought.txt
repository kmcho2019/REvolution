To simplify the 16-bit adder design, I will instantiate two 8-bit ripple carry adders directly within the 16-bit adder module without further hierarchical 4-bit adder submodules. Each 8-bit adder will be implemented using a straightforward generate loop that instantiates full adders in a ripple carry manner. This reduces the module count and code complexity while preserving correct functionality and carry chaining. The ripple carry approach is simple, easy to understand, and sufficient for basic combinational adders without extra optimization layers.
