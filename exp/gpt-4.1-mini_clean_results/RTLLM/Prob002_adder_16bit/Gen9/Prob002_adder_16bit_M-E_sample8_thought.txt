To design a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic with modular 8-bit adders but with a novel approach, I will:

- Implement an 8-bit adder module based on a carry-lookahead principle to speed up carry computation within each 8-bit block, reducing carry propagation delay compared to pure ripple carry.
- Use generate-propagate signals to quickly compute carry signals internally for the 8-bit block.
- Instantiate two such 8-bit carry-lookahead adders to build the 16-bit adder.
- To connect the two 8-bit blocks, use the carry-out from the lower 8-bit CLA as carry-in for the upper 8-bit CLA.
- This approach maintains modularity but improves performance due to faster carry computation inside blocks, reducing overall delay compared to simple ripple carry.
- Outputs remain the same: 16-bit sum and a final carry out.
