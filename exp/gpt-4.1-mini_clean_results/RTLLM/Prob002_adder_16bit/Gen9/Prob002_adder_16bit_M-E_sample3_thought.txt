I will implement a 16-bit adder by hierarchically instantiating four 4-bit carry lookahead adders. Each 4-bit block uses carry-lookahead logic internally for fast carry calculation within the block. To connect the 4-bit blocks, I will build a simple 4-block carry-lookahead generator that computes the carry inputs of each block from the top-level propagate/generate signals, thus avoiding ripple carry between blocks and achieving faster overall carry computation.

This approach breaks the 16-bit adder into smaller, fast 4-bit carry-lookahead modules with a top-level 4-block carry-lookahead carry generator, balancing speed and modularity. It reduces carry propagation delay by generating all block carries in parallel based on global propagate/generate signals.

The 16-bit adder inputs are split into four 4-bit sections: A[3:0], A[7:4], A[11:8], A[15:12], and similarly for B. The carry into each 4-bit block is computed by the top-level carry-lookahead logic using the propagate and generate signals from all blocks. This avoids ripple carry delays between blocks and results in a faster combinational 16-bit adder.
