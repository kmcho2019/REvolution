Implement the 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit adders as requested, but design the 8-bit adder using a carry-select adder architecture to speed up carry propagation. The 8-bit carry-select adder is composed of two 4-bit ripple carry adders: one assuming carry-in = 0, and another assuming carry-in = 1. Then a multiplexer selects the correct sum and carry-out based on the actual carry-in. This reduces the carry propagation delay roughly by half inside each 8-bit block. Finally, the 16-bit adder cascades these two 8-bit carry-select adders with carry ripple between them. This hierarchical carry-select approach is novel relative to the ripple carry in the example, providing improved speed while still using combinational logic with explicit small adder instantiations.
