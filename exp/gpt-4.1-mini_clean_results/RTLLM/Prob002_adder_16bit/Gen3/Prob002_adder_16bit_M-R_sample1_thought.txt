Refactor the 16-bit adder design by using two 8-bit adders instead of four 4-bit adders, while still building the 8-bit adder from 1-bit full adders. This reduces the number of adder modules instantiated and changes the ripple carry chain to only two stages, potentially reducing delay. The 8-bit adder module will be fully combinational, implemented using assign statements and generate loops for concise code. The top-level 16-bit adder instantiates two 8-bit adders in cascade to form the 16-bit adder. This hierarchical approach keeps modularity but offers a different structure than the original 4-bit blocks.
