Design a 16-bit adder by instantiating four 4-bit ripple carry adders (adder_4bit) instead of two 8-bit adders. Each 4-bit adder computes a 4-bit slice of the sum and passes its carry-out to the next adder's carry-in. This breaks down the problem into smaller modules, improving modularity and potentially easing layout or reuse. The adder_4bit uses a simple carry ripple logic with a carry vector of 5 bits to propagate carry signals. The top-level adder_16bit instantiates four 4-bit adders in series, chaining carry signals appropriately. This differs from the 8-bit adder approach and demonstrates hierarchical design with smaller building blocks.
