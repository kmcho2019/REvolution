Implement the 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit adders connected in a ripple carry chain. The 8-bit adder will be designed by instantiating eight full adders chained internally. This hierarchical design simplifies the 16-bit adder while reducing the number of module instances compared to multiple smaller blocks. It still uses pure combinational logic with ripple carry, but with fewer chained modules, aiding clarity and easy scaling. The two 8-bit adders handle the lower and upper halves of the inputs, connected by the carry-out to carry-in signal.
