To improve PPA, particularly timing, replace the ripple carry 8-bit adders with carry lookahead adders (CLA) for faster carry computation, reducing critical path delay significantly. Implement a hierarchical carry lookahead logic inside the 8-bit adder by computing generate and propagate signals for each bit, then combining these to find carry signals efficiently. This reduces the carry chain delay from linear to logarithmic time, thus improving max clock frequency. Power impact should be minimal since the logic is purely combinational, and area increase is moderate but justified for performance gain. The 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit CLA adders in ripple carry to maintain modularity and minimal complexity at the 16-bit level. This approach balances performance with area and power.
