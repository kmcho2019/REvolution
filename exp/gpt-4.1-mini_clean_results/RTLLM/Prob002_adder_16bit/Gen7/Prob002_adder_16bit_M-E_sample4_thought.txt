Implement the 16-bit adder using four 4-bit adder modules arranged in a hierarchical carry lookahead scheme. Each 4-bit adder will generate local propagate and generate signals alongside sum and carry-out. Then, a higher-level carry lookahead logic block will compute the carry inputs for each 4-bit adder to minimize carry propagation delay across the 16-bit adder.

This approach divides the 16-bit addition into smaller, fast 4-bit blocks, which produce group propagate and generate signals. A carry lookahead unit calculates carries for these blocks in parallel, reducing the overall critical path compared to ripple carry.

The architecture has three module types:
1. adder_4bit: Computes 4-bit sum, carry-out, and group propagate/generate.
2. cla_4block: Takes group propagate/generate from four 4-bit adders and computes intermediate carries.
3. adder_16bit: Instantiates four adder_4bit blocks and the cla_4block to implement the complete 16-bit adder.

This design enhances speed by limiting carry propagation delay to two-level lookahead logic while maintaining modularity and clarity.
