To improve the original 16-bit ripple carry adder design, I will:

- Change the small bit-width adder from 4-bit to 8-bit to reduce the number of carry propagation stages from 4 to 2, thereby reducing the critical path delay and improving performance.
- Implement an 8-bit ripple carry adder module with a generate loop for bitwise addition and carry generation.
- Instantiate the 8-bit adder twice to build the 16-bit adder, connecting the carry-out of the lower 8-bit block to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit block.
- This reduces the number of carry chains and associated wiring, improving timing with minimal area overhead compared to the original design.
- The combinational design remains simple and modular, suitable for synthesis with better PPA metrics, especially improved performance.
