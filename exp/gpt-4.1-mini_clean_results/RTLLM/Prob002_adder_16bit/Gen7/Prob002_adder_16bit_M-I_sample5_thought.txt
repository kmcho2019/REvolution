To optimize the existing 16-bit adder design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will enhance the 8-bit adder block by replacing the ripple carry logic with a carry lookahead adder (CLA) structure. 

The CLA reduces the critical path delay by computing carries in parallel rather than rippling them through each bit, significantly improving maximum frequency (performance). This also reduces switching activity in the carry chain, benefiting power.

The 16-bit top module will still instantiate two 8-bit CLAs to preserve modularity and maintain the specified hierarchy.

Although CLA logic is more complex, it can be efficiently implemented with generate and propagate signals and is a common optimization for mid-size adders. This approach strikes a good balance between complexity, performance, and area.

Key improvements:
- Implement 8-bit adder as carry lookahead adder using generate/propagate signals.
- Maintain hierarchical instantiation for 16-bit adder.
- Use continuous assignments to maximize combinational logic optimization by synthesis tools.
- This change improves timing (reduces carry propagation delay) and power (reduces switching in carry signals), with only modest area increase.

This optimization targets the major bottleneck (carry chain delay) from the previous ripple carry design, improving the design's overall PPA.
