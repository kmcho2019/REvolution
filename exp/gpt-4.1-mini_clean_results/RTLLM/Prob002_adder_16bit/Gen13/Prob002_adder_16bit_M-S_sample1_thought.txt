To simplify the 16-bit adder design while maintaining functionality, I will:

1. Implement a straightforward 8-bit ripple carry adder using full adders directly, avoiding internal decomposition into smaller 4-bit blocks.

2. Instantiate two such 8-bit ripple carry adders in the 16-bit adder module, chaining the carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder.

3. Use simple bitwise addition with carry chaining within the 8-bit adder for clarity and minimal complexity.
