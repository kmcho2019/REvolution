Implement the 16-bit adder by cascading two 8-bit adders similar to before, but internally, each 8-bit adder uses a carry-lookahead scheme to compute the carry signals faster. The adder_8bit module will explicitly compute group propagate and generate signals for the entire 8-bit block to produce carry signals in parallel without ripple carry delay inside the block.

The 16-bit adder then connects two such 8-bit carry-lookahead adders in series, with the carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder feeding the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This approach improves speed inside the 8-bit blocks by parallel carry computation, while maintaining modular hierarchy. This also differs from the previous approach that used ripple carry internally.

This design improves maximum frequency and reduces carry propagation delay significantly by using carry-lookahead logic for the 8-bit blocks.
