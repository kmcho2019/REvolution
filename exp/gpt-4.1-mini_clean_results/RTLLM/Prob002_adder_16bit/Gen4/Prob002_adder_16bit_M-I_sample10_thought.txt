To improve the existing 16-bit adder design while maintaining modularity, correctness, and simplicity, I will optimize the internal 8-bit ripple carry adder by implementing a carry-lookahead structure within each 8-bit block.

This enhancement reduces the critical path delay caused by ripple carry propagation inside the 8-bit adders, thus improving timing without significantly increasing area or power.

Specifically:

1. Replace the internal ripple carry logic in the 8-bit adder with a carry-lookahead scheme using propagate (P) and generate (G) signals and carry equations.
2. Keep the top-level 16-bit adder structure unchanged, still instantiating two 8-bit adders and chaining carries.
3. This approach balances performance improvement by reducing carry chain delay inside the 8-bit blocks while keeping a simple, modular design.
4. The carry chain between the two 8-bit blocks remains ripple-carry to limit complexity, which is acceptable as the critical internal carry is sped up.

This will enhance maximum clock frequency (reduce delay) with minimal area and power overhead compared to a pure ripple carry design.
