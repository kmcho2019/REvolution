Refactor the 8-bit adder to use a simple binary addition operator instead of bitwise gate modeling. The adder_8bit module will perform sum and carry-out by directly adding inputs plus carry-in using Verilog’s addition operator. This approach simplifies the design by leveraging built-in arithmetic, enhancing readability and reducing code complexity. The 16-bit adder remains composed of two 8-bit adders with ripple carry. This keeps modularity intact but shifts from gate-level modeling to behavioral-style combinational logic, which synthesis tools easily optimize.
