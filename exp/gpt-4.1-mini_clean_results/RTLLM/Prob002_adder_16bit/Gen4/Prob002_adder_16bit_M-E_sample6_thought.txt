Implement a 16-bit adder by instantiating four 4-bit adders in a ripple carry manner. Each 4-bit adder module performs addition on a 4-bit slice of the inputs along with a carry-in. This finer granularity improves modularity and reusability compared to the 8-bit block approach. The 16-bit sum is formed by concatenating four 4-bit sums from the 4-bit adders, and the final carry-out is obtained from the last 4-bit adder. The 4-bit adder is implemented using combinational logic with carry chains internally. This approach demonstrates hierarchical design with smaller adders chained together.
