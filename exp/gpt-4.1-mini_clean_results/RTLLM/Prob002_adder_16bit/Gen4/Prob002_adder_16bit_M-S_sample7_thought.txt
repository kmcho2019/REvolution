To simplify the 16-bit adder design while maintaining functionality, I will:

- Implement a 4-bit full adder module instead of 8-bit, which is smaller and simpler to manage.
- Instantiate the 4-bit adder module four times to build the 16-bit adder.
- Use ripple carry between the four 4-bit blocks.
- The 4-bit adder will be implemented using straightforward combinational logic with a generate loop.
- This reduces complexity in carry chain wiring and code length while keeping modular design and correctness.
