To improve the original 16-bit adder design while preserving modularity and combinational logic, I will implement an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) instead of the ripple carry adder for the 8-bit blocks. This will reduce carry propagation delay inside each 8-bit adder, thus improving overall timing for the 16-bit adder.

The 16-bit adder will still instantiate two 8-bit adders. The lower 8-bit block receives the module's carry-in and produces an internal carry-out, which is passed to the upper 8-bit CLA block.

The carry-lookahead adder computes generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for all bits and computes carries in parallel, removing the ripple carry chain inside the 8-bit block and drastically improving delay.

This approach keeps the modular hierarchy, improving performance significantly without increasing area much. Power could also be slightly improved because carry toggling is minimized by faster carry resolution.

In summary:
- Replace the ripple-carry 8-bit adder with an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder.
- Keep the 16-bit adder hierarchy identical, instantiating two 8-bit CLA modules.
- Retain full combinational logic.

This upgrade will reduce the longest carry path delay from 16 ripple stages to approximately 8 CLA stages, providing better timing and potentially lower power consumption.
