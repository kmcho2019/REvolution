The refactoring will keep the same modular approach of using an 8-bit adder submodule instantiated twice to build the 16-bit adder. However, instead of using a generate block with multiple assign statements inside the 8-bit adder, I will implement the 8-bit adder by instantiating eight full adder (1-bit) modules in a chain. This enhances clarity by explicitly representing each bit's full adder and makes carry propagation visually and structurally explicit. The full adder module will be a simple combinational logic block with inputs a, b, cin and outputs sum, cout.

This refactor improves readability and modularity at the bit level, clarifying carry signals and potentially easing further optimizations or replacements of the 1-bit full adder. The interface and functionality remain identical.
