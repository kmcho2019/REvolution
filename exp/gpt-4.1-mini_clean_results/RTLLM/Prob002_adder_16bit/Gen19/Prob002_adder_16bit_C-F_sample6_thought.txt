Key points of the fusion strategy:
- Use an 8-bit adder module instantiated twice in the 16-bit top module for modularity.
- Within the 8-bit adder:
  * Compute propagate (p) and generate (g) signals.
  * Implement a multi-level carry look-ahead to break the carry chain into smaller groups for parallel carry calculation rather than simple ripple carry.
  * Use generate-for loops for concise and scalable code.
  * Carry signals are computed using a hierarchical structure:
    - First-level group propagate/generate signals for 4-bit halves.
    - Final carry-out is computed by combining group generates and propagates.
  * This reduces the carry chain delay significantly compared to purely ripple or simple look-ahead.
- Sum bits calculated by XOR of propagate signals with their corresponding carry-in.
- The 16-bit adder instantiates two 8-bit adders:
  * The lower 8-bit adder takes Cin.
  * The upper 8-bit adder takes the carry-out from the lower block as carry-in.
- This fusion yields:
  * Improved performance: faster carry computation inside the 8-bit blocks.

  * Moderate area and power increase justified by performance gain.
