To simplify the 16-bit adder design while maintaining functionality, I will implement a straightforward ripple carry adder using an 8-bit ripple carry adder as the building block. The 8-bit adder will be a simple combinational adder using a chain of full adders. This reduces complexity compared to carry look-ahead logic, sacrificing some performance but making the design easier to understand and verify.

The 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit ripple carry adders, connecting the carry-out of the lower adder to the carry-in of the upper adder.

This approach uses simple bitwise addition and carries propagation directly, keeping the code concise and clear without explicit propagate and generate signals.
