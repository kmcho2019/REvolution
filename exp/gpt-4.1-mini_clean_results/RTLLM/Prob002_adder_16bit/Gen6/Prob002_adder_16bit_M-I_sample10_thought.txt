The existing design cleanly implements the 16-bit adder using two 8-bit ripple carry adders instantiated hierarchically. To improve PPA (performance, power, area), especially timing, I propose enhancing the 8-bit adder with a simple carry-lookahead scheme internally. This will reduce carry propagation delay within each 8-bit block by computing carries faster than the ripple carry.

Improvement Strategy:
1. Replace the 8-bit ripple carry adder logic with an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA). This accelerates carry computation by calculating carry generate (G) and propagate (P) signals and then computing carries in parallel.
2. Maintain the modular approach and instantiate two improved 8-bit CLA adders to form the 16-bit adder.
3. This will reduce the critical path delay from 16 full carry stages to roughly the delay of two CLA blocks, significantly improving timing.
4. The added logic (for carry-lookahead) introduces some overhead in area and power but overall is more efficient for timing-critical designs.
5. Keep the interface and behavior identical to the original for plug-and-play usability.

This optimization balances timing improvement while maintaining modularity and a clean hierarchical structure. It better suits applications requiring faster combinational adders without major complexity increase.
