Refactor the 8-bit adder to implement a ripple carry adder using a parameterized full adder module instantiated 8 times in a generate block. Each full adder computes a single bit sum and carry out, and the carry chain is formed by cascading carry signals between the stages. This modular approach uses structural style with explicit instantiation of 1-bit full adders to improve code reuse and readability, contrasting the original approach of continuous assignments without generate loops.

The 16-bit adder module remains structurally the same, instantiating two 8-bit adders, but the 8-bit adder is now built from individual 1-bit full adder units, enhancing clarity and modularity while preserving purely combinational logic.
