Implement a 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit adders connected in a ripple carry fashion. Each 8-bit adder performs addition on an 8-bit slice of the inputs along with a carry-in. This halves the number of instantiations compared to the 4-bit adder approach, simplifying the design while maintaining modularity and combinational logic. The final 16-bit sum is formed by concatenating the outputs of the two 8-bit adders, and the final carry-out is from the second 8-bit adder.
