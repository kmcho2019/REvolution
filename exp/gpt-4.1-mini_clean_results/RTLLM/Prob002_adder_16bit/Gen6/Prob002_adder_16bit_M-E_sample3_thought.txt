Implement a 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit adders connected in a ripple carry manner. The 8-bit adder is the basic building block module that performs addition on two 8-bit operands and a carry-in to produce an 8-bit sum and carry-out. The top-level 16-bit adder is constructed by cascading two 8-bit adders: the lower 8 bits added by the first 8-bit adder, and the upper 8 bits added by the second 8-bit adder with the carry-out from the first as carry-in. This hierarchical approach balances modularity and reasonable carry propagation latency, simplifying the design compared to a single 16-bit addition while using larger blocks than 4-bit. Internally, the 8-bit adder uses explicit ripple carry logic through bitwise full adders implemented via combinational logic for clarity.
