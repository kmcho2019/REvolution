Implement a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder that computes sum and carry out directly without propagate/generate signals to reduce complexity. Then instantiate two such 8-bit adders in the 16-bit adder module. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder feeds into the upper 8-bit adder as carry-in. This simplifies the design by removing carry-lookahead logic while still maintaining correctness and modularity.
