To improve the existing 16-bit adder design composed of two 8-bit ripple carry adders, I will optimize the 8-bit adder module for better performance and power by implementing a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) logic instead of simple ripple carry. This reduces carry propagation delay significantly, improving timing (WNS and TNS), thus boosting frequency.

The CLA logic uses generate and propagate signals to calculate carry signals in parallel, minimizing the carry chain delay.

This approach preserves modular hierarchy by keeping the adder_8bit module and instantiating it twice in adder_16bit, but internally, it uses the carry-lookahead technique for better PPA.

Additionally:
- Use vectorized assign statements and no manual repeated code for carry.
- Keep combinational logic style for synthesis friendliness.
- Maintain clarity by naming intermediate signals clearly.

This strategy should improve timing and power (less glitching) by reducing carry chain length and switching activity. Area might slightly increase due to more logic gates for CLA, but the performance gain usually justifies it.
