To simplify the 16-bit adder while maintaining functionality, I will implement an 8-bit ripple carry adder as the submodule instead of the carry look-ahead adder. This reduces complexity by removing propagate/generate calculations and carry look-ahead logic, resulting in straightforward carry chaining via simple assignments.

The 16-bit adder will instantiate two such 8-bit ripple carry adders connected by carry. This simplification sacrifices some performance for clarity and smaller code size, while maintaining modularity and correct behavior in pure combinational logic.
