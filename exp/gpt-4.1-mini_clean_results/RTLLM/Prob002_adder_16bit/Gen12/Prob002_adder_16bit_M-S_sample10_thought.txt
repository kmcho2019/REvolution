To simplify the 16-bit adder design while keeping it fully combinational and modular, I will:

1. Implement a straightforward 8-bit ripple carry adder using a direct bitwise addition with carry chaining, without explicitly computing propagate/generate signals.

2. Implement the 16-bit adder by instantiating two such 8-bit adders connected by carry.

3. Use only the 8-bit adder module instantiated twice, avoiding an additional 4-bit adder hierarchy to reduce complexity.

This approach retains functional correctness and modularity, is simpler and easier to understand, and satisfies the requirement to use an 8-bit adder block for the 16-bit adder construction.
