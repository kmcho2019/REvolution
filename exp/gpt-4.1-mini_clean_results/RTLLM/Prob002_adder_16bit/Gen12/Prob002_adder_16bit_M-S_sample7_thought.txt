To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will implement the 16-bit full adder using only two instantiations of an 8-bit ripple carry adder, without hierarchical 4-bit adders inside the 8-bit adder. The 8-bit adder will directly implement an 8-bit ripple carry adder using generate statements for clarity and compactness. This reduces the design hierarchy and complexity, making the code shorter and easier to understand while preserving modularity and correct carry chaining.
