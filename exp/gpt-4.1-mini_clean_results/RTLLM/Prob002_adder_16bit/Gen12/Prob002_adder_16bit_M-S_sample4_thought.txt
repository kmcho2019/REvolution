To simplify the 16-bit adder implementation, I will use a straightforward ripple-carry approach with an 8-bit ripple-carry adder module as the building block. This module will simply sum its 8-bit inputs and the carry-in using the '+' operator, which infers combinational adder logic in synthesis.

Then, the 16-bit adder instantiates two of these 8-bit adders in series: the first adds the lower 8 bits and the carry-in, outputting a carry to the upper 8-bit adder.

This method reduces complexity by avoiding explicit carry-lookahead logic and generate blocks, making the code more concise and easier to understand, while still fulfilling the requirement of using a small adder instantiated multiple times.

The design remains purely combinational, modular, and synthesizable.
