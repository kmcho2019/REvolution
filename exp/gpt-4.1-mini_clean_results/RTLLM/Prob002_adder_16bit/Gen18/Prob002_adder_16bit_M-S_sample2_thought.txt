Implement a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder module using straightforward bitwise addition and carry propagation with a for-loop. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice in the 16-bit adder module, connecting the carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This reduces the design complexity by avoiding propagate/generate signals and carry lookahead logic while maintaining correct functionality and modularity.
