To optimize the existing 16-bit adder design for better performance (timing) and power, while keeping area reasonable, I propose the following improvements:

1. Replace the ripple carry chain inside each 8-bit block with a carry lookahead adder (CLA) structure. Instead of a simple ripple of carries within the 8-bit block, compute block-level group generate (G) and propagate (P) signals and use them to calculate carries faster. This reduces internal carry latency significantly.

2. Use hierarchical carry lookahead: Each 8-bit adder produces generate and propagate signals to the 16-bit adder level, which then calculates the carry between the two 8-bit blocks using CLA logic instead of ripple carry between blocks.

3. This reduces carry propagation delay inside and across blocks, improving maximum frequency.

4. The design remains modular and purely combinational.

5. The carry signals are computed with minimal logic levels, reducing glitches and power.

The structure:

- adder_8bit module outputs sum, carry out, and also block propagate and generate.

- adder_16bit uses these block P and G to calculate carry_in for upper block with CLA logic:

   C1 = G0 + P0*Cin

where G0 and P0 are from the lower 8-bit adder.

- This keeps carry lookahead hierarchical and efficient.

This approach typically improves timing significantly at a modest area and power cost due to added logic for group generate/propagate signals.
