To improve PPA (Performance, Power, Area) over the given ripple-carry design:

- Performance: Replace the ripple carry in the 8-bit adder with a carry-lookahead scheme to significantly reduce carry propagation delay.
- Power: Carry-lookahead reduces switching toggling on carry chains by generating carries in parallel, lowering dynamic power.
- Area: The carry-lookahead logic adds some area overhead, but it is modest and justified by improved speed.
- Maintain modularity: Keep the 8-bit adder module and instantiate it twice in the 16-bit adder.
- Use generate blocks and explicit intermediate propagate/generate/carry signals inside the 8-bit adder to implement carry-lookahead logic cleanly.
- The overall structure remains combinational and easy to synthesize.

This improvement targets critical path reduction by introducing carry-lookahead within each 8-bit adder block, which is the main contributor to delay in the previous ripple-carry 8-bit adder. The top-level 16-bit adder ripple between two 8-bit blocks is acceptable given their internal speedup.
