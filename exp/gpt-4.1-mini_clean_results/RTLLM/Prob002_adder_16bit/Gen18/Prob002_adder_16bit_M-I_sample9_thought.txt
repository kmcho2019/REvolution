To optimize the 16-bit adder for better performance and potentially power without significantly increasing area, I will improve the 8-bit adder's carry look-ahead logic by implementing a hierarchical carry look-ahead scheme.

Specifically, instead of calculating the carry for each bit sequentially (which forms a linear chain), I will compute group propagate and generate signals for 4-bit subgroups within the 8-bit adder. Then, calculate carries to each 4-bit boundary in parallel, reducing the carry chain length from 7 to 1 for these group calculations. This decreases carry delay and improves the critical path.

The overall approach divides the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit blocks, each with local propagate/generate signals and sums. Then use a higher-level carry look-ahead logic to compute carries between these blocks quickly.

The 16-bit adder instantiates two such enhanced 8-bit adders. This hierarchical structure lowers carry propagation delay and can reduce switching activity in the carry chain, potentially lowering power without significantly increasing area.

I will also use generate blocks to compact repetitive assign statements for cleanliness.

This improves performance by reducing carry propagation stages, slightly reduces dynamic power by limiting carry toggling, and maintains area close to the original.
