I will design a 16-bit adder by instantiating four 4-bit ripple carry adders (RCA). This fine-grained modular approach simplifies the design and makes the code reusable and scalable. 

Each 4-bit adder takes a 4-bit slice of inputs a and b along with a carry-in, and outputs a 4-bit sum and carry-out. The carry-out of each 4-bit adder feeds into the next adder's carry-in. 

This design trades off a slightly longer carry propagation path compared to a carry lookahead adder, but it is straightforward, easy to verify, and good for teaching or low-frequency applications. Also, using a small 4-bit RCA as a building block enables possible future substitution with other optimized 4-bit adder architectures.

Key points:
- Implement a parameterized 4-bit ripple carry adder as a basic block.
- Instantiate it 4 times to cover all 16 bits.
- Chain carry signals between blocks.
- Fully combinational implementation with no registers.
