To improve the 16-bit adder design for better timing (reduced critical path delay) without significantly increasing area, I will upgrade the internal 8-bit adders from simple ripple carry to carry lookahead adders (CLA). A carry lookahead adder generates carries faster by precomputing propagate and generate signals and resolving carries in parallel rather than waiting for ripple.

Thus, each 8-bit adder will implement a carry lookahead structure internally, drastically reducing delay within each 8-bit block, while the 16-bit adder still instantiates two 8-bit CLAs with carry ripple between them (the upper-level carry chain remains single stage).

This approach improves overall timing while retaining modularity and reasonable area overhead. The carry lookahead logic uses standard generate and propagate signals with combinational logic to compute carry signals quickly.

The improved design keeps inputs and outputs the same and is purely combinational.

This improvement targets the critical path, reducing delay from O(n) ripple to O(log n) for each 8-bit block, which benefits the 16-bit addition as a whole.
