Create an 8-bit adder as a purely structural ripple carry adder using 1-bit full adder instances chained together. Then, build the 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit ripple adders and passing the carry output of the lower to the upper. This approach makes the design more modular and explicit, making each 1-bit adder reusable and easy to understand while still combinational. It also separates the complexity clearly into 1-bit adders and their chaining logic.
