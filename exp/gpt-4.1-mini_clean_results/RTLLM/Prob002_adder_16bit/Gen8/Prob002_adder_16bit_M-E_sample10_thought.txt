Implement a 16-bit adder using two instances of an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) module to improve speed over simple ripple carry. The 8-bit CLA will compute internal carries using generate and propagate signals grouped hierarchically, thus reducing carry propagation delay within 8 bits.

For the 16-bit adder, instantiate two 8-bit CLAs: the lower one adds bits [7:0] and the upper one adds bits [15:8]. The carry-out of the lower CLA is used as the carry-in to the upper CLA.

This modular approach provides a faster carry computation than ripple carry inside the 8-bit blocks, and the 16-bit addition is still composed modularly. The design uses purely combinational logic and continuous assignments, maintaining clarity and good timing properties.
