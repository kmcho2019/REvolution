Implement a 16-bit full adder by cascading two 8-bit adders, but this time use behavioral modeling with explicit addition using the '+' operator inside the 8-bit adder. The 8-bit adder takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, and returns an 8-bit sum and carry-out by performing a 9-bit addition internally. Then the 16-bit adder instantiates two 8-bit adders in series, connecting the carry-out of the low 8-bit adder to the carry-in of the high 8-bit adder. This approach leverages the simplicity of '+' operator at the small block level while maintaining modularity and clarity.
