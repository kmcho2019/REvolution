To simplify the design while maintaining functionality, I will keep the hierarchical approach but reduce the small adder size from 8-bit to 4-bit. This breaks the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit adder blocks chained by carry signals. This slightly increases the number of instantiations but simplifies each small adder to a smaller width and possibly a simpler structure. Each 4-bit adder uses a single line of combinational addition. The top-level 16-bit adder instantiates four 4-bit adders, connecting carry outputs to carry inputs in sequence. This design remains modular, combinational, and clear.
