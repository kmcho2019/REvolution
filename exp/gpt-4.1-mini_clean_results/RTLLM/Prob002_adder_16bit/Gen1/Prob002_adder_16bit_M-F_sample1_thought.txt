The original design is correct in terms of structure, but the carry vector in the 8-bit adder was declared as 8 bits wide, while the carry chain requires 9 bits (one extra for the final carry-out). This caused an out-of-range indexing error (`carry[8]`). To fix this, we simply need to increase the width of the carry wire vector from `[7:0]` to `[8:0]`. This allows valid indexing from `carry[0]` through `carry[8]`, resolving the error and correctly propagating carries through the 8-bit adder. The top-level 16-bit adder using two 8-bit adder instantiations remains unchanged.
