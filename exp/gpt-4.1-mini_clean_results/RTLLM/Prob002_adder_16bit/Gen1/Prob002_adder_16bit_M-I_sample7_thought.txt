To improve the existing 16-bit adder design, I will focus on optimizing the 8-bit adder module, which is instantiated twice. Currently, the 8-bit adder uses the '+' operator, which generally synthesizes into a ripple carry adder, potentially limiting performance due to carry propagation delay.

To improve **performance** without significantly increasing area, I will implement the 8-bit adder using a carry lookahead approach. This reduces carry propagation delay, improving speed.

For **power**, reducing unnecessary logic toggling in the carry chain will be helpful, and a carry lookahead adder generally achieves this by quickly resolving carries.

For **area**, carry lookahead logic typically incurs a slight area increase compared to ripple carry adders, but the performance benefits justify it for most use cases.

Thus, I will implement the 8-bit adder explicitly with generate and propagate signals to build a small carry lookahead adder, then instantiate it twice in the 16-bit adder.

This approach keeps the modular design, improves critical path delay, and helps optimize PPA metrics overall.
