Implement a 16-bit full adder by instantiating four 4-bit adders in a ripple-carry chain. Each 4-bit adder handles a 4-bit slice of inputs a and b, propagating the carry-out to the next 4-bit adder's carry-in. This approach demonstrates modular reuse of a smaller bit-width adder and fine-grained hierarchical design different from the previous 8-bit segmentation.
