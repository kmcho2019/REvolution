Implement the 16-bit adder by instantiating four 4-bit adders arranged in a ripple carry chain. Each 4-bit adder sums a quarter of the inputs and propagates carry to the next block. This finer granularity allows better modular reuse and a potentially more balanced carry chain compared to two 8-bit adders. The 4-bit adder will be built from full adders chained internally. This hierarchical design offers clear structure and potential for future replacement of 4-bit adders with faster carry-lookahead blocks.
