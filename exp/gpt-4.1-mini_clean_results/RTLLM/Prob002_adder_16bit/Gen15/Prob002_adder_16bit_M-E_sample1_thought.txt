I will implement a 16-bit full adder using two instances of a 4-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA), with four instances of the 4-bit CLA module combined hierarchically. This approach offers faster carry computation within each 4-bit block via carry lookahead logic, reducing the carry propagation delay compared to ripple-carry adders. Then, I will cascade four 4-bit CLA blocks with internal carry signals connecting them.

This design maintains modularity with a reusable 4-bit CLA as the building block, improving speed by leveraging carry lookahead at the 4-bit block level. The carry signals between blocks form a small ripple chain of only 4 bits, which is faster than 16-bit ripple carry.

The 4-bit CLA computes the carry outputs using propagate and generate signals:
- propagate_i = a_i XOR b_i
- generate_i = a_i AND b_i
and the carries are computed as:
- c1 = g0 + p0*c0
- c2 = g1 + p1*g0 + p1*p0*c0
- c3 = g2 + p2*g1 + p2*p1*g0 + p2*p1*p0*c0
- c4 = g3 + p3*g2 + p3*p2*g1 + p3*p2*p1*g0 + p3*p2*p1*p0*c0

Sum bits are computed as s_i = p_i XOR c_i.

At the top 16-bit adder, the four 4-bit blocks are connected with the carry-out from one block feeding carry-in to the next.

This hierarchical carry lookahead adder architecture improves speed while keeping modularity and clarity.
