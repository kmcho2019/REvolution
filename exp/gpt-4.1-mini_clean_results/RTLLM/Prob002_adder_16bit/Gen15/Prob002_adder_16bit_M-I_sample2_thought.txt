To optimize the 16-bit adder for better PPA while maintaining modularity and clarity, I will implement a carry lookahead adder (CLA) for the 8-bit blocks instead of a simple ripple carry chain. This reduces the carry propagation delay within each 8-bit block, improving timing. Then, I will still chain two 8-bit CLAs to form the 16-bit adder. This hierarchical approach balances complexity and performance. The CLA logic uses generate loops and parallel carry computation for efficiency. This also reduces glitching and power consumption because carry signals are computed in parallel rather than ripple through every bit sequentially.
