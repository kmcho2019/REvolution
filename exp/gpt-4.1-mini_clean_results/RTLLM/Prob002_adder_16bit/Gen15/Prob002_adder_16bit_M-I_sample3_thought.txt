To improve the PPA of the 16-bit adder design that uses two 8-bit ripple carry adders, I will optimize the 8-bit adder to implement a carry lookahead scheme. This reduces the carry propagation delay by computing group propagate and generate signals and resolving carries in parallel within the 8-bit block. This reduces the critical path delay compared to ripple carry inside the block.

The 16-bit adder will still instantiate two 8-bit adders, but each 8-bit adder will now internally compute carries using carry lookahead logic rather than simple ripple carry. This improves timing (better TNS and WNS) and power due to reduced switching activity duration along the carry chain.

The area overhead of carry lookahead within 8 bits is minimal and justified by the timing gain. The logic remains fully combinational without generate or for loops to maintain synthesis friendliness and code clarity.

This approach preserves modularity, clean interface, and simple chaining between blocks with a single carry signal passed between the two 8-bit adders.

Thus, the strategy is:
- Implement a carry lookahead 8-bit adder module internally computing carries.
- Instantiate two such 8-bit adders to build the 16-bit adder.
- Use continuous assignments with no generate loops.

This will provide a better tradeoff between performance, power, and area compared to simple ripple carry 8-bit adders.
