To simplify the 16-bit full adder design while maintaining correct functionality and modularity, I will implement a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder (RCA) as the small bit-width adder block. The 16-bit adder will instantiate two 8-bit RCA blocks in series: the lower 8-bit block takes the module's carry-in, and its carry-out feeds the upper 8-bit block.

This approach avoids the more complex carry-lookahead logic inside each 8-bit adder, reducing code complexity and making the design easier to understand, at the expense of some timing performance. Since the requirement is combinational logic and modular design, this is a reasonable tradeoff.

Summary:
- Implement a simple 8-bit ripple carry adder.
- Instantiate two such adders to build the 16-bit adder.
- Keep all logic purely combinational.
- Maintain clean and concise code.
