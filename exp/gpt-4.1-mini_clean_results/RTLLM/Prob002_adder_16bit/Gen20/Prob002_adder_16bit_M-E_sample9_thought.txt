Design a 16-bit full adder using four instances of a 4-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA) module. The 4-bit CLA computes sum and carry-out faster than ripple carry by generating propagate and generate signals for all bits, enabling carry lookahead logic within the 4-bit block. The 16-bit adder chains four 4-bit CLAs by connecting carry-out of a lower block to carry-in of the next higher block.

This approach:
- Improves carry propagation delay compared to a ripple-carry structure by using CLA blocks.
- Provides modular 4-bit adders for clean hierarchical design.
- Keeps all logic combinational.
- Shows a different hierarchical architecture from the previous example that used 8-bit ripple adders.

Thus, the 16-bit adder is implemented by chaining 4 four-bit carry lookahead adders, which reduces critical path delay for the carry chain compared to ripple carry and gives a more scalable architecture.
