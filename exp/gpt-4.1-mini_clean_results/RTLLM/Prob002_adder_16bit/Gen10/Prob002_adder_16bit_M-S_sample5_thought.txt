To simplify the 16-bit adder design while maintaining functionality, I will instantiate two 8-bit adders in ripple carry fashion instead of four 4-bit adders. This reduces the number of module instances and connections, making the design cleaner and easier to understand. The 8-bit adder will be implemented combinationally using the '+' operator for simplicity and synthesis-friendly behavior. The final 16-bit sum is formed by concatenating the two 8-bit sums, with carry propagated between the two 8-bit adders. This approach preserves modularity and correctness with less complexity.
