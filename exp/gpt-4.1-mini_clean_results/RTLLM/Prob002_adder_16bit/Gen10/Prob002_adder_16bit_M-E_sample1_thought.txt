Implement the 16-bit adder using two instances of an 8-bit ripple carry adder (RCA) module, but explicitly define the 8-bit RCA with a generate loop to create full adder cells for each bit. Each full adder cell computes sum and carry using simple combinational logic.

This approach breaks down the design into the smallest building blocks—full adders—making it highly modular and clear, and uses structural Verilog with generate to create the 8-bit RCA. The 16-bit adder is then formed by chaining two 8-bit RCAs: the carry-out from the lower 8-bit RCA feeds the carry-in of the upper 8-bit RCA. This is a straightforward approach emphasizing modularity, structural clarity, and bitwise logic, providing an alternative to the carry-lookahead style in the example.

This design also enables easy expansion, debugging, or reuse of the full adder module, while keeping all logic combinational and simple to understand.
