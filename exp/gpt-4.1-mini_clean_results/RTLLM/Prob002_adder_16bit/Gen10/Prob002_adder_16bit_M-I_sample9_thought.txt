To improve PPA (Power, Performance, and Area), I'll optimize the 8-bit adder to a carry lookahead adder (CLA) architecture instead of a ripple carry adder. The CLA significantly reduces the carry propagation delay by computing carry signals in parallel using generate and propagate signals, thus improving performance.

For area and power, an 8-bit CLA is a reasonable trade-off: it moderately increases area compared to ripple carry but drastically reduces delay and switching activities in carry chain.

Then, instantiate two 8-bit CLA adders for the 16-bit adder as before.
