To design a 16-bit adder using smaller bit-width adders, I will first implement an 8-bit adder module that performs addition on two 8-bit operands with a carry-in and outputs an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder module twice inside the 16-bit adder module. The first instance will add the lower 8 bits along with the carry-in; its carry-out will be connected to the carry-in of the second instance which adds the upper 8 bits. This modular approach improves design reuse and clarity.
