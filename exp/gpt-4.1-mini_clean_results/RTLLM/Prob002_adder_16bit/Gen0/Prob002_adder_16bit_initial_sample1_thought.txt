To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will create a smaller 8-bit adder module first. This 8-bit adder takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then, the top-level 16-bit adder module instantiates two of these 8-bit adders. The first 8-bit adder adds the lower 8 bits of the inputs and the input carry, producing a lower 8-bit sum and a carry-out. The second 8-bit adder adds the upper 8 bits and the carry-out from the first adder, producing the upper 8-bit sum and the final carry-out. This hierarchical design simplifies the logic and reuses the smaller module.
