To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic and reusing an 8-bit adder, first I will define an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out. Then, the top-level 16-bit adder module will instantiate two 8-bit adders: the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder connects to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. Finally, the 16-bit sum and carry-out are produced by concatenating outputs from the two instances.
