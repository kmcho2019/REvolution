To implement a 16-bit adder using combinational logic, I will first design an 8-bit adder module that takes two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out. Then, the top-level 16-bit adder module will instantiate two 8-bit adders: one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected as the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder. This hierarchical design is clean, modular, and makes the code reusable.
