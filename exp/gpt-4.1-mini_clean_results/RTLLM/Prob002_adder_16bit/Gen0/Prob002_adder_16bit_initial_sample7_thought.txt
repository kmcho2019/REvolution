To implement a 16-bit adder using combinational logic, I will design a reusable 8-bit adder module that adds two 8-bit inputs with a carry-in and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out. Then, in the top-level 16-bit adder module (adder_16bit), I will instantiate two instances of the 8-bit adder: one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected as the carry-in for the upper 8-bit adder. This modular approach promotes code reuse and clarity.
