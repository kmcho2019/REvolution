Implement an 8-bit adder module first, which accepts two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module. The lower 8 bits are added along with the global carry-in, producing a carry-out that is fed as the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder. Finally, the upper 8-bit adder produces the upper sum bits and the final carry-out.
