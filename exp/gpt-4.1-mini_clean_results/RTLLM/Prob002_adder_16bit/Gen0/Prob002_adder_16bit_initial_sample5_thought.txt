To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, I will create an 8-bit adder module first that takes two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out. Then, I will instantiate this 8-bit adder twice in the 16-bit adder module, connecting the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This hierarchical approach simplifies design and reuses smaller modules effectively.
