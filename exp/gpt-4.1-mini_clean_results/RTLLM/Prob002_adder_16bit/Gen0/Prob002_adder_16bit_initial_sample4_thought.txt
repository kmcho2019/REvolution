Design an 8-bit adder module with inputs a, b, and Cin, and outputs sum and Co. Then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice inside the 16-bit adder module: the first for the lower 8 bits with the original Cin, and the second for the upper 8 bits using the carry-out from the lower 8 bits as its carry-in. This structure uses two 8-bit adders to build the 16-bit adder in combinational logic.
