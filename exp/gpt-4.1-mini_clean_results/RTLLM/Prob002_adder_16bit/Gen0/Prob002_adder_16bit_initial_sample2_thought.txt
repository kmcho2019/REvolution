Design a 16-bit adder by instantiating two 8-bit adders in a ripple carry fashion. The first 8-bit adder adds the lower 8 bits of inputs A and B along with the input carry Cin. The second 8-bit adder adds the upper 8 bits of inputs A and B and the carry output from the first adder. The final 16-bit sum is formed by concatenating the two 8-bit sums, and the final carry-out is the carry output from the second adder. The 8-bit adder will be implemented using combinational logic inside a separate module.
