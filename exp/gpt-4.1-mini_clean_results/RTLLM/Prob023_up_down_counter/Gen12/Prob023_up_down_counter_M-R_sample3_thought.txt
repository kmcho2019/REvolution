I will refactor the design by splitting the next state calculation into a separate combinational always block, and then update the counter register in the clocked always block. 

This separates combinational logic (calculating the next count based on up_down) from the sequential logic (register update), which can be clearer for some designers and tools. The counter wraps naturally due to 16-bit arithmetic. The synchronous reset is kept in the clocked process.

This structural change retains exact functionality, but improves modularity of logic and can aid in readability and verification.
