Implement the up_down_counter using a fully synchronous approach but introduce an explicit clock enable (clk_en) signal that only enables counting when reset is inactive. The counting direction is selected inside the always block to avoid unnecessary combinational logic outside the sequential process. This reduces the combinational path between clock edges and can slightly improve timing. 

Also, handle the reset and count update inside the single always block to maintain a simple sequential design. By integrating the counting direction inside the always block, we avoid generating the next_count wire externally, which may reduce logic area and power by eliminating a combinational adder outside the clocked process.

This architectural choice balances clarity, timing efficiency, and reduces toggling caused by redundant logic signals.
