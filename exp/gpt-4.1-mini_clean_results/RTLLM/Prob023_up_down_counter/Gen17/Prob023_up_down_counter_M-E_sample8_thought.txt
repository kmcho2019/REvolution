Implement the 16-bit up/down counter using a single always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock. Instead of precomputing increment and decrement values, perform the counting operation inline within the always block using an if-else construct based on the up_down signal. The reset remains synchronous and sets the count to zero. The design uses non-blocking assignments and hardcoded 16-bit width to match the specification exactly, favoring simplicity and clarity. This direct approach minimizes combinational logic by avoiding extra wires and muxes and makes the code concise and straightforward.
