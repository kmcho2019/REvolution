Refactor the up_down_counter by integrating the counting logic directly inside the clocked always block instead of separating the combinational next-state logic. Use a synchronous reset and update the count register directly based on the up_down input on each clock edge. This simplifies the design by removing the intermediate next_count signal and uses a single always block that handles both reset and counting. The 16-bit arithmetic will still wrap naturally due to fixed bit width.

This approach reduces the number of signals, potentially lowering resource usage and slightly improving timing by avoiding the additional combinational block, at the cost of slightly reduced modularity.
