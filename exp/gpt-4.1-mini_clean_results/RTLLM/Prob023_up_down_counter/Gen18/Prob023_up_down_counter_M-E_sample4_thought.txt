Implement the 16-bit up/down counter using a two-stage approach for counting:

- Split the 16-bit counter into two 8-bit registers: low byte and high byte.
- Perform increment/decrement on the low byte first.
- If the low byte overflows (increment from 0xFF to 0x00) or underflows (decrement from 0x00 to 0xFF), adjust the high byte accordingly.
- This approach mimics a ripple carry architecture but done explicitly in RTL with byte-level operations.
- It can help reveal carry propagation clearly and may allow synthesis tools to optimize for smaller carry chains or enable partial optimizations.
- The counter output combines the high and low bytes.
- This structural approach may assist in exploring architectural trade-offs and may facilitate partial register updates in certain flows.
- Reset logic clears both low and high bytes synchronously.
