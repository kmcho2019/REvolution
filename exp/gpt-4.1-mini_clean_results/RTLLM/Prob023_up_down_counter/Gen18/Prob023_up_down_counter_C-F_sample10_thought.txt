The superior design fuses the modular clarity of separating next-state combinational logic from the sequential register update (from Example 1) with the succinct, readable ternary expression for next_count (from Example 2). 

Additionally, it adds an internal enable signal (defaulted to always enabled here) to make the design more scalable for potential clock gating or conditional counting in the future without a major rewrite. This preserves the modularity and clarity while enhancing maintainability and extensibility.

The synchronous reset remains with the count reset to zero, preserving the clean reset semantics.

By combining these strengths:
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic aids verification and synthesis.
- Use of concise ternary operator improves readability and compactness.
- The internal enable supports design scalability.
- Meaningful comments improve maintainability.

This synthesis-friendly, easily testable structure is optimal for a robust 16-bit synchronous up/down counter.
