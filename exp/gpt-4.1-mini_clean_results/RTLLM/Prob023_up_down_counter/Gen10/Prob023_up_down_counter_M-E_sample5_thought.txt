Implement the 16-bit up/down counter using separate increment and decrement registers combined through a multiplexing stage. On reset, both registers are cleared. On each clock cycle, both increment and decrement values are computed independently from the current count, and a multiplexer selects which updated value to load back into the count register based on the up_down signal. This approach pipelines the increment and decrement computations in parallel, potentially enabling the synthesis tool to optimize the increment and decrement logic separately and allowing for early carry/borrow signals in physical design. It also clarifies the data path by explicitly separating the two operations rather than a conditional arithmetic operation, making the architecture modular and more scalable for extended designs. The entire logic remains synchronous and uses non-blocking assignments for correct sequential semantics.
