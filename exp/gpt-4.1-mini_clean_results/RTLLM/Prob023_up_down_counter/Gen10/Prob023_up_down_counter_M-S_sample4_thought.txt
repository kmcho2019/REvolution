To simplify the up/down counter while maintaining correct functionality, I will eliminate the signed 17-bit arithmetic and instead use a simple synchronous always block that directly increments or decrements the 16-bit unsigned count register based on the up_down control signal. This approach reduces complexity by avoiding signed extension and extra adders, improves timing by using simple 16-bit increment/decrement, and minimizes area and power by limiting switching to the 16-bit count only. The count will naturally wrap around due to 16-bit overflow/underflow behavior in Verilog.
