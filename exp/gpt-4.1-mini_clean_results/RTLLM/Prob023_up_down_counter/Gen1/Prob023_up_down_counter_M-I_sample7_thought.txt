To improve the existing synchronous 16-bit up/down counter for better PPA, introduce a clock enable signal that is asserted only when counting is needed (i.e., up_down or count not at boundary). This reduces switching activity and thus power consumption. Also, by isolating the counter update logic behind this enable, we help the synthesis tool optimize timing and area by reducing unnecessary toggling. The counter wraps naturally, so no additional boundary checks are required. The design remains synchronous with reset and uses a simple always block. This simple gating approach is effective without complex clock gating cells, preserving simplicity and portability.
