To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, and area), I will integrate the increment/decrement logic directly inside the synchronous always block to eliminate the separate combinational block, reducing combinational logic stages and potential delay. 

Since the counter only increments or decrements by 1, I can replace the addition/subtraction operations with the use of the '+' operator conditionally, avoiding a full subtractor and allowing synthesis tools to optimize increment/decrement efficiently. This change can reduce critical path delay (improving timing) and potentially reduce area and power by simplifying logic.

Additionally, keeping a synchronous reset and a single always block preserves clarity while providing an efficient hardware mapping.

Overall, this solution trades a slight loss in modularity for improved PPA while maintaining clear and readable code.
