I will refactor the up_down_counter by splitting the next-state logic into a combinational always block and the state update into a sequential always block. 

Specifically, I will introduce a 16-bit reg variable `next_count` that holds the next value of the counter based on the current count and the up_down control. The combinational block calculates `next_count` by either incrementing or decrementing the current `count`. The sequential block then updates `count` on the rising edge of `clk`, or resets it synchronously when `reset` is asserted.

This approach separates combinational and sequential logic, which improves code clarity and can be helpful for debugging and synthesis tools to recognize distinct logic types. Functionally, the counter behavior remains identical, including natural wrap-around due to 16-bit width.
