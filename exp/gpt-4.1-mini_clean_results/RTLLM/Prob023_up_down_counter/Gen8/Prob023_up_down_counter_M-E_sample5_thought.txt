Implement the 16-bit synchronous up/down counter using two separate registers to hold the incremented and decremented next states, selecting the correct one based on the control signal. This splits the increment and decrement calculations into parallel logic, possibly allowing better timing optimization. Use a single always block triggered on clk for synchronous reset and count update. The design maintains a clean, structural style by separating increment and decrement computations and a mux selecting the next count value.
