To optimize the up_down_counter for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) without changing functionality, I will reduce toggling of bits by adding an enable signal generated internally to update the counter only when up_down changes or when counting progresses. However, since the problem requires counting every clock cycle, gating counting is not allowed.

Instead, I will optimize the increment/decrement logic by explicitly coding the arithmetic using a binary adder and subtractor components merged via a mux to reduce combinational logic depth. This allows the synthesis tool to optimize better than a conditional operator with arithmetic inside. Also, by separating increment and decrement results and selecting between them, critical path delay can be shortened due to simpler datapath muxing.

Further, I will use a clock enable signal derived from the reset and keep the logic synchronous. The explicit style helps synthesis tools map adders and muxes efficiently, potentially improving timing and area.

I will keep the design fully synchronous, reset synchronous to zero, and use non-blocking assignments. The count will wrap naturally due to 16-bit arithmetic. This approach can reduce critical path delay and possibly decrease power due to simpler muxing in datapath.
