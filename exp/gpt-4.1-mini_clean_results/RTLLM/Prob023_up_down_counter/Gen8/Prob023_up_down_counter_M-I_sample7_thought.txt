To optimize the original design for better power and potentially area without impacting performance, I will add an enable input signal that gates counting only when counting is desired. This reduces unnecessary toggling of the counter bits on every clock cycle, thus lowering dynamic power consumption. The enable input defaults to '1' (always count) to maintain original functionality, but can be externally controlled to freeze the counter. This addition improves power efficiency with minimal area overhead and no performance penalty.

Additionally, the counting logic remains simple and synchronous with non-blocking assignments on posedge clk. This retains clean, synthesizable, and timing-friendly structure.
