I will refactor the design to separate the combinational next-state logic from the sequential register update. Instead of directly computing the next count inside the always block, I will use a separate wire for the next count value, computed using a ternary operator outside of the always block. Then, the always block will simply update the count register on the clock edge based on reset or this next count wire. This approach clearly distinguishes combinational logic from sequential logic, enhancing readability and easier formal verification or testing. The functionality remains unchanged.
