I will refactor the up_down_counter by implementing the next count calculation with a continuous assign statement rather than a combinational always block. The sequential always block will remain responsible for updating the count on the clock's rising edge or resetting it.

This approach replaces the explicit combinational always block with an assign statement that computes next_count based on the current count and up_down control. It simplifies the combinational logic coding style by using dataflow modeling, which can be preferred in some coding guidelines or synthesis tools.

Functionally, the design is unchanged: synchronous reset, increment or decrement based on up_down, and 16-bit wrap-around behavior.
