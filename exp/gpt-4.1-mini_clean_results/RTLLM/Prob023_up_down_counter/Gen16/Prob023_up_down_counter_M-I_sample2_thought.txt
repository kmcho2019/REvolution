To improve the PPA metrics, I will simplify the design by removing the separate combinational `next_count` signal and implementing the increment/decrement logic directly inside the synchronous always block. This reduces the critical path delay because the counter updates are done immediately on the rising clock edge without waiting for an intermediate combinational signal. It also reduces switching activity in combinational logic and saves some minimal logic resources. The synchronous reset remains in place. The counter naturally wraps due to 16-bit arithmetic, and the design is straightforward and efficient.
