To implement the 16-bit up/down counter in a different way, I will embed the counting logic directly within the sequential always block rather than separating the next-state logic into a combinational block. 

Specifically, the counter register `count` will be updated on the rising edge of `clk`. When `reset` is asserted, `count` resets to zero. Otherwise, depending on the `up_down` signal, the counter will increment or decrement accordingly. 

This direct approach removes the need for an intermediate `next_count` variable, leading to a compact, straightforward design where state update and counting logic coexist inside the same process. This style is common in simple synchronous counters and is easy to understand.

The counter naturally wraps around due to the fixed 16-bit width: incrementing 16'hFFFF rolls over to 16'h0000, decrementing 16'h0000 rolls under to 16'hFFFF, which matches typical behavior of unsigned counters.
