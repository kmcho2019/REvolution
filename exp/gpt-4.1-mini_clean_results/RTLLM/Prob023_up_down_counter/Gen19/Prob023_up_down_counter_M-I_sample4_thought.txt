To optimize the up_down_counter for better performance, power, and area, replace the two-register (high_byte and low_byte) approach with a single 16-bit register that performs direct increment or decrement based on the up_down control signal. This reduces combinational logic levels and routing complexity, eliminating explicit overflow/underflow checks.

The synchronous always block will:

- On reset, clear the entire 16-bit count to zero.
- On each rising clock edge, increment the entire 16-bit count if up_down is high, else decrement it.
  
Using a single 16-bit register and a straightforward arithmetic operation simplifies the design, reduces muxes, and minimizes toggling in unnecessary intermediate registers, thereby improving critical path delay, lowering dynamic power, and saving area.
