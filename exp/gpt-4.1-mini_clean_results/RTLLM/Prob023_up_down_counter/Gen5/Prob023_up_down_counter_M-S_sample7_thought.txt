Simplify the 16-bit up/down counter by directly handling reset and counting within a single always block without extra intermediate signals. This eliminates redundant signals and keeps the design clean and straightforward. The counter resets to zero synchronously on reset, increments when up_down is 1, and decrements when up_down is 0, all on the rising clock edge. This straightforward approach maintains functionality while reducing logic complexity and potential switching activity from unnecessary signals.
