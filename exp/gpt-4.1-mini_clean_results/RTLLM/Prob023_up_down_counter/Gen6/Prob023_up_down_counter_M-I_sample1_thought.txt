To improve the PPA metrics, especially the timing (wns) and power, I will simplify the logic by removing the intermediate combinational register `next_count` and perform the increment/decrement operation directly within the synchronous always block. This reduces the combinational logic depth, potentially improving the critical path delay. Also, by limiting logic to a single always block and carefully using non-blocking assignments, the synthesizer may produce more optimized hardware. Power can benefit slightly from reducing logic complexity. Since the functionality is simple, this approach keeps the design clean, synchronous, and minimalistic while improving timing and possibly power without sacrificing area.
