Simplify the IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier by splitting the operation into a multi-cycle process controlled by a small 3-bit counter. This approach stages the workflow step-by-step across multiple clock cycles to reduce combinational path complexity per cycle and simplify control logic.

Key ideas:
- Use sequential steps: on different counter values, extract fields, multiply mantissas, normalize product, round, adjust exponent, and generate output.
- Extract sign, exponent, mantissa in the first cycle, compute product in second cycle, normalize and round in third cycle.
- Use 24-bit mantissas with implicit leading 1 for normals.
- Handle special cases such as zero, infinity, and NaN explicitly at input processing.
- Perform 24x24 multiplication with 50-bit product stored in a register.
- Use simple round-to-nearest-even rounding with guard, round, sticky bits.
- Adjust exponent for normalization shifts and rounding overflow.
- On reset, clear counter and output.
- This pipeline-like approach balances clock frequency and resource complexity.
