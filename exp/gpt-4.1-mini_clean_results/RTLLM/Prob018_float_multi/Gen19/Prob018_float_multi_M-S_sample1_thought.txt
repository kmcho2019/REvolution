Implement a simplified single-cycle IEEE-754 floating-point multiplier focusing on clarity and correctness rather than pipelining or extreme optimization.

- Extract sign, exponent, and mantissa directly.
- Handle special cases (NaN, infinity, zero) explicitly.
- Perform 24x24 mantissa multiplication in one step.
- Adjust exponent by adding and subtracting bias.
- Normalize product based on MSB.
- Implement round-to-nearest-even using guard, round, sticky bits.
- Handle overflow and underflow to produce infinity or zero.
- Output final 32-bit IEEE-754 result.
- Use synchronous reset and register output at every clock cycle.
- Keep code straightforward with minimal pipeline stages and no complex flag propagation.

This approach trades throughput for simplicity while maintaining IEEE 754 compliance and correct rounding.
