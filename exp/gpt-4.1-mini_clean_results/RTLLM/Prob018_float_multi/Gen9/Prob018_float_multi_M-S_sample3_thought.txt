To simplify the 32-bit IEEE-754 floating-point multiplier while maintaining full functionality, I will:

- Use a straightforward multi-cycle FSM controlled by a 3-bit counter.
- Keep the overall flow: decode inputs, handle special cases, multiply mantissas, normalize, round, and pack the output.
- Remove explicit named FSM states and just use the counter to sequence steps.
- Combine special case checks succinctly.
- Simplify exponent and mantissa width handling by sticking with fixed widths as required.
- Perform mantissa multiplication in one cycle with a 24x24-bit multiplier.
- Implement rounding to nearest even with guard, round, sticky bits.
- Flush underflows to zero for simplicity.
- Output final IEEE-754 format after rounding and normalization.
- Keep logic fully synchronous with non-blocking assignments.
- Avoid unnecessary intermediate signals and keep the pipeline linear and clean.

This results in simpler, easy-to-read code with well-defined cycle stages, minimal internal signals, and complete IEEE-754 compliant multiplication with special cases, rounding, and normalization.
