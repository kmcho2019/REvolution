To optimize the original 4-cycle floating-point multiplier design for better PPA while maintaining correctness and IEEE-754 compliance, apply the following improvements:

1. **Reduce critical path delay:**
   - Instead of performing the full 24x24 multiplication in one cycle, break the mantissa multiplication into two 12x12 partial products combined with addition. This reduces the single multiplier delay.
   - Alternatively, keep a single 24x24 multiplier but pipeline the design to separate expensive combinational blocks across cycles (already 4 cycles, but more balance can be achieved).
   - Replace blocking assignments inside sequential always blocks with non-blocking assignments to aid synthesis and timing.

2. **Optimize registers and bit-widths:**
   - Exponent registers can be 9 bits instead of 10 bits because max exponent is 8 bits plus a small range for adjustments.
   - Remove redundant registers: For example, avoid duplicate storage of product and product_r; keep product stable in one register.
   - Remove unnecessary pipeline registers that add toggling without clear benefit.

3. **Improve rounding logic:**
   - Implement round-to-nearest-even logic clearly with non-blocking assignments.
   - Avoid recomputing the sign bit multiple times; store it once early and reuse.

4. **Power reduction considerations:**
   - Use clock enable with counter to reduce toggling in cycles without operations.
   - Use localparams for constants instead of literals for clarity.

5. **Code clarity:**
   - Clearly separate combinational calculation in combinational always blocks to help synthesis.
   - Keep sequential logic simple and use registers only for storing states or pipeline registers.

By applying these, timing should improve by easing the longest combinational path, power is saved by reducing register toggling and gate sizes, and area is reduced by smaller bit-width and less register duplication.
