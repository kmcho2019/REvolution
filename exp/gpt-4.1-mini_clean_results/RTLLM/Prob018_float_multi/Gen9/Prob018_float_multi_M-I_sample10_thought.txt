To optimize the original 4-stage pipelined IEEE-754 multiplier for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we apply these improvements:

1. **Power Reduction:**
   - Add operand zero and special case gating before mantissa multiplication to avoid unnecessary multiplication activity when inputs are zero, infinity, or NaN.
   - Calculate sticky bit hierarchically for less switching.

2. **Performance:**
   - Keep the 4-stage pipeline for balanced timing.
   - Move sticky bit calculation to a reduced OR reduction tree for faster timing.

3. **Area:**
   - Reduce internal exponent width from 10 to 9 bits since max exponent sum after bias adjustment won't exceed 255+255-127=383 which fits 9 bits.
   - Use smaller registers where possible to reduce register area.
   - Only register the signals needed at each stage to avoid excess pipeline registers.

4. **Code clarity & correctness:**
   - Use blocking assignments in combinational parts if any, otherwise keep non-blocking.
   - Keep non-blocking assignments in sequential blocks.
   - Add comments to clarify control and datapath.

Overall, the core algorithm and pipeline remain, but gating and signal sizing optimizations reduce switching, register count, and improve timing slack slightly.
