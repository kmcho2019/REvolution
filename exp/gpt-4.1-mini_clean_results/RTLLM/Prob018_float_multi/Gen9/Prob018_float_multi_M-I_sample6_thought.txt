To optimize the given fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier for better PPA, the primary bottleneck is the large combinational delay from the single 24x24 multiplier and combined normalization/rounding logic.

Improvement strategy:
- Break the multiplication process into a multi-cycle pipeline of 3 stages, controlled by a 3-bit counter:
  1. Cycle 0: Extract and preprocess inputs (sign, exponent, mantissa, special cases).
  2. Cycle 1: Perform 24x24 mantissa multiplication and exponent addition.
  3. Cycle 2: Normalize, round the product and produce the final IEEE output.
- This division reduces the combinational logic per cycle, improving clock frequency.
- Use registers between pipeline stages to hold intermediate signals, minimizing critical path delay.
- Keep synchronous reset and registered output.
- This pipeline design slightly increases latency but significantly improves max clock frequency and can reduce power by splitting logic and toggling over cycles.
- Maintain the special cases logic, rounding to nearest even, and IEEE-754 compliance.
- By pipelining the multiplier and rounding, the large multiplier and normalization logic don't combine in a single cycle.

This approach balances latency vs frequency, enabling better PPA metrics without sacrificing correctness.
