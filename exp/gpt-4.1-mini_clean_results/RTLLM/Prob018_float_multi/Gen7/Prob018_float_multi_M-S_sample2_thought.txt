Simplify the floating-point multiplier by using a straightforward 3-cycle sequential approach without extra pipeline registers and minimize the number of temporary registers. 
- Cycle 0: Extract sign, exponent, mantissa, identify special cases.
- Cycle 1: Perform 24x24 mantissa multiplication and exponent addition.
- Cycle 2: Normalize the product, extract rounding bits, perform rounding, adjust exponent, handle special cases, and produce final output.
This reduces register usage and control complexity while keeping the key operations intact and accurate IEEE 754 behavior.
