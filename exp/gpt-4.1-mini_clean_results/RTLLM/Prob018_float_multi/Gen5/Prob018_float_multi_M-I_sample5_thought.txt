To fix the original design and make the IEEE-754 float multiplier fully correct and functional, the following improvements are applied:

1. **Proper exponent handling with bias:**
   - Extract the 8-bit exponent as unsigned.
   - Subtract bias (127) immediately upon extraction to get unbiased signed exponents.
   - Use signed arithmetic thereafter on exponents.

2. **Subnormal input normalization:**
   - Use a safe CLZ function to count leading zeros.
   - Normalize subnormal mantissas by left-shifting mantissa and decrementing exponent accordingly (with signed exponent).
   - Handle zero inputs as a special case.

3. **Product normalization:**
   - After multiplying two 24-bit mantissas, the product is 48 bits.
   - If the top bit (bit 47) is 1, keep product as is and increment exponent by 1.
   - Otherwise, shift product left by one to normalize, exponent remains unchanged.
   - Extract mantissa bits [46:24] (23 bits mantissa + 1 implicit bit), rounding bits (guard, round, sticky) from bits [23:0].

4. **Rounding to nearest even:**
   - Implement round-to-nearest-even logic using guard, round, and sticky bits.
   - If rounding causes mantissa overflow, right-shift mantissa and increment exponent.

5. **Special cases:**
   - Detect NaN, Inf, zero at input and handle per IEEE-754 rules.
   - For operations like Inf * 0 or NaN inputs, output NaN.
   - Zero * anything (except NaN or Inf) produces zero.
   - Inf * non-zero produces Inf.
   - NaN inputs propagate NaN.
   
6. **Final packing:**
   - After rounding and normalization, check exponent overflow → Inf.
   - Check exponent underflow → produce subnormal or zero by right-shifting mantissa.
   - Pack sign, exponent, and mantissa into final output.

7. **Simplify multi-cycle state machine:**
   - Use 6 states for clarity:
     - 0: Extract fields and check special cases.
     - 1: Normalize subnormal inputs.
     - 2: Check special results and multiply mantissas.
     - 3: Normalize product and extract rounding bits.
     - 4: Round mantissa.
     - 5: Pack final result and output.
   - Use non-blocking assignments properly and only update intermediate signals once per cycle.
   
This approach ensures the design meets the IEEE-754 single-precision multiplication standard with correct corner cases, normalization, rounding, and result formatting.
