Design a pipelined floating-point multiplier with a small FSM controlled by a 3-bit counter to sequence operations over several clock cycles. 
On reset, clear the counter. 
On the first clock cycle, extract signs, exponents, and mantissas from inputs a and b, and identify special cases (NaN, infinity, zero).
Compute the product of the mantissas with a hidden leading 1 for normalized numbers.
Add exponents and adjust for bias.
Normalize the product mantissa and adjust exponent.
Generate guard, round, and sticky bits for rounding to nearest even.
Perform rounding and handle overflow, underflow, NaN, infinity, and zero special cases.
Finally, pack sign, exponent, and mantissa into IEEE 754 format and output on z.
Use registers to store intermediate values across cycles, controlled by the counter.
