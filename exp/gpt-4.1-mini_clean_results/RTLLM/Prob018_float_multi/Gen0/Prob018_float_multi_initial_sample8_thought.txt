Design a sequential IEEE-754 single precision floating point multiplier module with inputs registered and processed over multiple clock cycles. On reset, initialize counter to 0. At counter=0, extract sign, exponent, and mantissa from inputs; identify special cases (zero, infinity, NaN). At counter=1, handle special cases and prepare normalized mantissas (add implicit leading 1 if exponent!=0), compute sign XOR, sum exponents minus bias (127). At counter=2, multiply mantissas (24x24=48 bits stored in 50-bit reg), normalize product (shift mantissa and adjust exponent if top bit not set). At counter=3, extract guard, round, sticky bits for rounding; perform round-to-nearest-even rounding; adjust exponent if mantissa overflows. At counter=4, check exponent overflow or underflow; assemble final result considering special cases; register output 'z'. The design uses a 5-cycle pipeline controlled by counter and synchronous reset.
