Implement a pipelined 3-cycle IEEE-754 single-precision floating point multiplier. On reset, clear state. On first clock, extract sign, exponent, mantissa, handle special cases like zero, infinity, NaN. On second clock, multiply mantissas (including implicit leading 1), add exponents, determine sign, normalize product, and prepare rounding bits (guard, round, sticky). On third clock, perform rounding, adjust exponent for overflow/underflow, handle special output cases (NaN, infinity, zero), and assemble final 32-bit result. Use the 50-bit product to capture mantissa multiplication result. The module supports pipelined operation with internal counter and registered intermediate values, producing accurate IEEE-754 results.
