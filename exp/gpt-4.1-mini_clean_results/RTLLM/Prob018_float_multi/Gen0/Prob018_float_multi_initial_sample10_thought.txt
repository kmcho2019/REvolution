Implement a sequential floating-point multiplier that processes inputs over several clock cycles using a counter. In the first cycle, extract sign, exponent, and mantissa fields from the inputs, detect special cases (zero, infinity, NaN). Then perform exponent addition and mantissa multiplication including the implicit leading 1 for normalized numbers. Normalize the product mantissa and adjust the exponent accordingly. Implement rounding using guard, round, and sticky bits. Handle overflow and underflow by saturating to infinity or zero as per IEEE 754. Finally, assemble the sign, exponent, and mantissa fields into the output. Reset clears the state, and the module is synchronous with clock.
