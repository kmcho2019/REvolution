Implement a pipelined 7-cycle IEEE-754 single precision floating-point multiplier with internal states controlled by a counter. On reset, initialize the counter. On cycle 1, extract sign, exponent, and mantissa from inputs. Detect special cases like zero, infinity, and NaN. Adjust mantissas and exponents accordingly. Multiply mantissas with implicit leading one (except for zero/subnormals). Add exponents and subtract bias (127). Normalize the product mantissa result. Apply rounding using guard, round, and sticky bits with round to nearest even rule. Handle exponent overflow (infinity), underflow (zero/subnormal). Generate final IEEE-754 formatted output after all computation cycles. Use registered outputs to sync with clock and reset logic.
