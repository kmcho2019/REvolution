Implement a simplified IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier with a synchronous output register and reset. The design extracts sign, exponent, and mantissa fields from inputs, handles special cases (NaN, infinity, zero) simply, and performs the multiplication and normalization in one clock cycle. The mantissas include the implicit leading 1 for normalized inputs. The product is checked for normalization shift and exponent adjusted accordingly. Round-to-nearest-even is applied using guard, round, and sticky bits. Overflow and underflow saturate to infinity or zero, respectively. The entire operation is done combinationally inside the clocked process, maintaining a simple cycle counter which can be used for possible future extension but currently acts as a single-cycle operation. This reduces complexity while maintaining IEEE-754 compliance for multiplication.
