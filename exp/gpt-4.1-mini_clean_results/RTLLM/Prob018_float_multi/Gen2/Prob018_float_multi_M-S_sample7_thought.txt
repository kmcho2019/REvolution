Implement a pipelined floating-point multiplier that processes the inputs over a few clock cycles, utilizing the given internal signals (counter, mantissas, exponents, signs, product, guard, round, sticky). The first cycle extracts fields and handles special cases, the second cycle performs mantissa multiplication and exponent addition, and the third cycle normalizes, rounds, and prepares the output. This staged approach simplifies logic per cycle and fits the problem's cycle counter requirement, while correctly handling normalization, rounding (round to nearest even), special cases, overflow/underflow, and output formatting in IEEE-754 format.
