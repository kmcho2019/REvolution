Implement a fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier module without multi-cycle logic.  
The design flow:  
1. Extract sign, exponent, mantissa from inputs, handling zero, infinity, and NaN cases immediately.  
2. Determine output sign by XOR of input signs.  
3. Handle special cases (NaN propagation, Inf*0 = NaN, Inf*nonzero = Inf, zero*anything = zero).  
4. For normal/subnormal inputs, normalize subnormal operands by left-shifting mantissa and adjusting exponent accordingly.  
5. Multiply 24-bit mantissas (with implicit leading 1 for normals, 0 for subnormals).  
6. Calculate the exponent sum minus bias, then normalize the product mantissa (check for leading bit) and adjust exponent.  
7. Perform IEEE round-to-nearest-even using guard, round, sticky bits extracted from lower bits of product.  
8. Handle exponent overflow (result infinity) and underflow (result zero or subnormal if supported).  
9. Assemble final 32-bit IEEE754 output: sign, exponent, mantissa.  
10. Use continuous assignments and intermediate wires to keep logic clear and purely combinational for easier testing and synchronous use.  

This approach simplifies control logic, avoids multi-cycle registers, and is suitable for synthesis in pipeline stages or combinational logic blocks. It includes careful normalization for subnormals and adheres to correct rounding.
