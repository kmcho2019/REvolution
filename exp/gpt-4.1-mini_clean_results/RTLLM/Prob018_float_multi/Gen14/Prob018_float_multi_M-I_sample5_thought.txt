Improvement Strategy:

The original design is functionally correct and well-structured with a 3-stage pipeline, but the main timing bottleneck and power/area hotspot is the 24x24-bit multiplier in stage 2, computed combinationally every clock cycle.

To improve PPA (power, performance, area), I will:

1. Split the pipeline into 4 stages (counter wraps at 4) to reduce combinational critical path:
   - Stage 1: Input extraction (sign, exponent, mantissa, special cases)
   - Stage 2: Mantissa multiplication start (use a registered multiplier input)
   - Stage 3: Normalization and rounding calculations (guard, round, sticky bits)
   - Stage 4: Final rounding and output formatting

This pipeline balancing reduces the max combinational logic delay per clock cycle, improving timing (WNS).

2. Insert registers before and after the multiplier to isolate the multiplier stage, enabling use of DSP or vendor multiplier resources efficiently.

3. Reduce the width of intermediate registers when possible to save area.

4. Use non-blocking assignments throughout for clarity and consistency.

5. Add an internal signal to gate the multiplier operation logically on valid inputs (here assumed always valid for simplicity).

6. Minor optimization in sticky bit calculation: perform OR reduction only on 16 bits, then OR with lower bits, to reduce wide fan-in (hierarchical reduction).

These changes will improve the maximum clock frequency and slightly reduce dynamic power by pipelining and gating the multiplier.

The interface remains the same; the latency increases to 4 cycles.

Output remains fully IEEE-754 compliant, with same special cases and rounding.
