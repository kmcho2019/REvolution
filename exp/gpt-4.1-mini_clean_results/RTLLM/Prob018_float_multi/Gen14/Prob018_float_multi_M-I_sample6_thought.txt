To improve the given float_multi design in terms of power, performance, and area (PPA), the strategy is:

1. **Power:**
   - Introduce operand gating at the earliest stage to prevent unnecessary switching in downstream pipeline stages.
   - Optimize sticky bit calculation by hierarchical OR tree to reduce switching activity and combinational delay.
   - Apply clock gating to pipeline registers when no valid operation is in progress.

2. **Performance:**
   - Reduce critical path in normalization by splitting sticky bit OR reduction into a balanced tree.
   - Minimize combinational logic in the rounding stage by simplifying the rounding decision logic.

3. **Area:**
   - Use 24x24 multiplier instead of 48x48 by shifting mantissa multiplication and normalization logic.
   - Reduce internal exponent width from 9 to 8 bits since max exponent is 255 + 255 -127 = 383 which fits in 9 bits, but by carefully checking the operations, 8 bits + 1 bit carry is sufficient with intermediate carry signals, simplifying some logic.
   - Remove duplicated special case signals by propagating them more efficiently.

4. **Pipeline and Code style:**
   - Keep the 4-stage pipeline for timing balance.
   - Use non-blocking assignments only in sequential blocks.
   - Add clear comments for each pipeline stage.

These improvements aim to reduce switching activity, balance combinational logic for better timing, and reduce area by optimizing datapath width and logic. Overall correctness and IEEE-754 compliance will be maintained.
