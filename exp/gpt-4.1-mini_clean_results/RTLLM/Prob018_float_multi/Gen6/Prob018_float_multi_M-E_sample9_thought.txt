Implement a single-cycle combinational IEEE-754 floating-point multiplier with careful step-by-step logic inside a single always block triggered by clock and reset.

This approach:
- Extracts sign, exponent, and mantissa from inputs.
- Handles special cases upfront: NaN, infinity, zero, denormals.
- Uses 24-bit mantissas with implicit leading one for normals, or zero for zeros/denormals.
- Performs 24x24 mantissa multiplication combinationally, resulting in 48-bit product.
- Normalizes the product: if MSB=1 (bit 47) no shift, else shift left until MSB=1 or exponent underflows.
- Adjusts exponent accordingly after normalization.
- Implements round to nearest even with guard, round, sticky bits derived from product bits below the mantissa.
- Handles overflow (sets to infinity) and underflow (sets to zero).
- Constructs the final IEEE-754 single-precision result.
- Outputs result in one cycle (no multi-cycle pipeline or counters).
- Resets output to zero on reset.

This design trades latency for simpler control and lower area by avoiding pipelining and counters, enabling use as a combinational block synchronized to clk.

All steps are done inside a single always block triggered by clk and rst to make it synchronous.

The module is named float_multi, ports as requested.

Note: This approach is simpler and suits synthesis tools that can handle wide combinational logic in one cycle or have high-frequency clocks.

Also, inputs and outputs are registered for timing stability.
