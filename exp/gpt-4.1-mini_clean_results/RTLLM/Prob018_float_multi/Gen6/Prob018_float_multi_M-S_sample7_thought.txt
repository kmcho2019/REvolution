Implement a non-pipelined, single-cycle IEEE-754 32-bit floating-point multiplier to reduce complexity.  
Key simplifications:  
- Combine all processing steps in a single clock cycle with synchronous reset.  
- Extract sign, exponent, and mantissa once per cycle; detect special cases immediately.  
- Perform 24x24 mantissa multiplication with implicit 1's for normalized inputs.  
- Normalize product by checking the most significant bit and adjusting exponent accordingly.  
- Use simple round-to-nearest-even based on guard, round, sticky bits.  
- Handle special cases (NaN, infinity, zero) with priority and assign output accordingly.  
- Output the final IEEE-754 formatted 32-bit float in one cycle.  
This approach trades latency and throughput for simplicity and smaller logic footprint, suitable when pipelining and high frequency are not critical.
