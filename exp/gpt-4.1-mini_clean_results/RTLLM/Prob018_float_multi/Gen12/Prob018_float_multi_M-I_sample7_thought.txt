To improve the PPA (Timing, Power, Area) of the existing float_multi design, we focus on reducing the critical path and dynamic power caused by the large combinational multiplier and normalization logic.

Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipeline the multiplication stage: split the operation into two sequential clock cycles to break the long combinational path of the 24x24 mantissa multiplication.
2. Register intermediate results (product, exponent sum, sign) at the end of the first pipeline stage (CALC1).
3. Perform normalization, rounding, and final exponent adjustment in the second pipeline stage (CALC2).
4. Use three-stage FSM: IDLE -> CALC1 (mantissa multiply & exponent add) -> CALC2 (normalize and round) -> DONE (output register).
5. Register and reuse special case signals across pipeline stages to avoid recomputation.
6. Reduce power by clock gating the logic in IDLE and DONE states implicitly by FSM.
7. Keep clear separation of combinational and sequential logic for synthesis optimization.

This pipelined design reduces the critical path delay of each cycle, enabling a higher clock frequency and lower power. The added registers have minimal area impact compared to large combinational multiplication.

We preserve full IEEE 754 compliance, including correct handling of special cases, rounding to nearest even, and overflow/underflow behavior without subnormals.

The updated code maintains all features with improved timing, balanced logic, and power efficiency.
