Refactor the float_multi module by removing the explicit state machine and instead implementing a simple cycle counter for sequencing operations. Inputs and intermediate values are registered on clk edges to maintain timing. The mantissa multiplication and special-case detection are combinationally assigned from registered inputs. Output generation and rounding logic are also done combinationally with the help of registered intermediate signals. This structure reduces control overhead, keeps pipeline depth at 3 cycles, and uses assign statements and intermediate registered signals instead of a FSM, making the design easier to integrate in different environments while preserving the original functionality and IEEE-754 compliance.
