Implement a fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier with support for special cases (NaN, infinity, zero, denormal), normalization, rounding (round to nearest even), and overflow/underflow handling. This combinational design splits input fields, manages exponent biases, multiplies mantissas including hidden bits, normalizes the result depending on the leading bit of the product, extracts guard, round, sticky bits for rounding, applies rounding, and assembles the final output in one combinational block. This approach avoids sequential FSM and multi-cycle operation, providing immediate result after inputs stabilize.
