Implement a fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier with synchronous output register. On every clock cycle, inputs a and b are decoded into sign, exponent, and mantissa fields. Special cases (NaN, infinity, zero, denormals) are detected combinationally. Mantissas are prepared with implicit leading one for normals, else as-is for subnormals. Mantissas are multiplied to produce a 48-bit product. The exponent sum is adjusted by subtracting bias. The product is normalized by checking the top bits of the product. The result mantissa is rounded using round-to-nearest-even with guard, round, and sticky bits extracted via fixed index based on normalization. Overflow and underflow are handled by saturating to infinity or zero, respectively. The final IEEE-754 32-bit result is updated on the clock's rising edge in a registered output. Reset initializes the output to zero. This design avoids cycle-by-cycle state machine complexity by performing all computations combinationally and latches only the output result, simplifying timing and removing variable part-select indices problems.
