Implement a fully combinational floating-point multiplier module with internal pipeline registers to separate each stage and allow throughput at every clock cycle. The design extracts sign, exponent, and mantissa with implicit leading ones for normalized values, detects special cases (NaN, Infinity, zeros), and then performs mantissa multiplication followed by exponent addition and normalization. The normalization stage detects whether the product's highest bit is set to decide if a right shift and exponent increment are needed. Use explicit wide arithmetic and bitwise operations to safely implement rounding to nearest even with guard, round, and sticky bits. Handle exponent overflow and underflow, saturating outputs to infinity or zero accordingly. The design uses a synchronous pipeline with 3 stages and a valid signal to indicate output readiness. This approach avoids complex arithmetic within concatenations by separating calculations into registers and explicit additions, ensuring clean, synthesizable RTL.
