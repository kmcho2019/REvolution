Implement a multi-cycle floating-point multiplier with a simple 3-stage pipeline controlled by a 3-bit counter. On reset, clear counter and output. On the first cycle, extract sign, exponent, and mantissa, detect special cases. On the second cycle, multiply mantissas and add exponents, normalize if needed. On the third cycle, perform rounding, adjust exponent, handle overflow/underflow, and generate final IEEE-754 output. This divides complex logic across cycles, reducing combinational path complexity per cycle, simplifying timing while preserving IEEE-754 functionality. Register intermediate results for stable operation and straightforward control.
