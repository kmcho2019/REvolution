To simplify the float_multi module while maintaining IEEE-754 compliance and correct functionality, the design will be collapsed into a single clock cycle operation without pipelining. This removes the complexity of multiple pipeline stages and intermediate registers.

Key simplifications:
- Extract sign, exponent, and mantissa directly and perform all operations combinationally at each clock edge.
- Handle special cases (NaN, Infinity, Zero) immediately and output the correct result.
- Perform mantissa multiplication with 24x24 bits, normalization, rounding, and exponent adjustment in one sequential block.
- Use straightforward rounding-to-nearest-even logic.
- Avoid multiple stages and reduce internal signal widths only as needed.

This simplification trades latency and throughput for reduced design complexity and easier verification. It is suitable if a single-cycle multiplier is acceptable, easing integration and maintenance without sacrificing correctness.
