To simplify the 32-bit IEEE-754 floating-point multiplier while maintaining functionality, I will:

- Use a single sequential always block with a cycle counter for staged operations instead of a deep pipeline with many registers.
- Extract inputs, detect special cases, multiply mantissas, normalize, round, and produce output in a multi-cycle manner controlled by the counter.
- Keep internal signals minimal and shared across cycles to reduce register usage.
- Implement rounding and normalization carefully but without separate pipeline stages.
- Handle special cases (NaN, Inf, zero) explicitly with priority.
- Use the counter to sequence through the required steps, updating internal registers each cycle.
- The approach trades latency (multi-cycle) for simpler logic, less area and power, but still functionally correct IEEE-754 multiplication.
