Design a fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier with internal registered inputs and output synchronization by clock and reset. This approach captures inputs on a clock edge, performs all computations combinationally in a single cycle without intermediate state machine steps, and finally registers the output. 

The design pipeline is effectively a 1-stage registered datapath:

- On clk edge, input operands are registered.
- In combinational logic, the multiplier extracts sign, exponent, and mantissa fields.
- Detects special cases (NaN, infinity, zero).
- Calculates product mantissa (24x24 bits = 48 bits) and exponent addition with bias correction.
- Normalizes the result by checking leading bits and shifting mantissa if needed.
- Implements round-to-nearest-even rounding using guard, round, sticky bits.
- Handles exponent overflow/underflow by saturating to infinity or zero.
- Packs the final IEEE-754 output.
- The final result is registered on the next clock cycle.

This architecture simplifies timing by avoiding multi-cycle state machines, easing synthesis and verification. It also avoids complex counters and state logic, allowing the multiplier to behave as a combinational block between registered inputs and outputs, fitting many FPGA and ASIC flows well.

The module includes:
- Registers for inputs and output to break combinational paths across clock cycles.
- Clear combinational signals for intermediate computations.
- Special cases logic integrated in combinational domain.
- A single clocked always block for registering inputs and outputs.
