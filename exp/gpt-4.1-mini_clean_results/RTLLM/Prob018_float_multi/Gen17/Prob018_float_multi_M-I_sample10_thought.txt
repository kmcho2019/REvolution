Improvement Strategy:

The original 4-stage pipeline design is functionally complete and performs accurate IEEE-754 single-precision multiplication with correct handling of special cases and rounding. To improve PPA based on the given metrics and feedback, I will:

1. Increase the pipeline depth to 5 stages to further reduce critical path delay, especially isolating the 24x24 multiplier in a dedicated stage with registered inputs and outputs. This reduces the combinational delay per cycle, allowing higher clock frequency.

2. Add clock enable gating via a valid signal propagated through the pipeline to avoid unnecessary toggling in pipeline registers when inputs are stable or not valid, reducing dynamic power.

3. Optimize sticky bit calculation by hierarchically OR reducing the bits in multiple steps instead of a single two-level OR.

4. Split the rounding and normalization logic into separate stages to balance combinational load.

5. Reduce bit-width where possible: exponent registers are 10 bits (to detect overflow/underflow) which is necessary; mantissas are kept at 24 bits with hidden bit.

6. Use consistent non-blocking assignments.

7. Keep special case handling robust and consistent with IEEE 754.

These changes will increase latency to 5 cycles but improve maximum clock frequency and power efficiency while maintaining IEEE 754 compliance.

Interface remains unchanged.
