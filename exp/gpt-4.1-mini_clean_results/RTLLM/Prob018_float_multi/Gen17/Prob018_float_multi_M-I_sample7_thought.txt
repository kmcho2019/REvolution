Improvement Strategy:

The original 4-stage pipeline float_multi is functionally correct and well-structured, but the main timing bottleneck and power hotspot is the combinational 24x24 multiplier computed every clock cycle in stage 3.

To improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will:

1. Further pipeline the multiplier by inserting a registered pipeline stage for the multiplier inputs and outputs to isolate the multiplier latency, effectively making it a 5-stage pipeline. This reduces the critical path delay of the multiplier and subsequent normalization, increasing the maximum clock frequency.

2. Use non-blocking assignments consistently and split the always block into multiple smaller always blocks to improve synthesis and timing clarity.

3. Optimize sticky bit calculation by splitting the OR reduction hierarchically to reduce fan-in.

4. Maintain IEEE-754 compliance by preserving the handling of special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero), normalization, rounding, overflow, and underflow.

5. Keep exponent as 10-bit for safe overflow detection but use narrower widths where possible.

6. Add clock enables for pipeline registers, allowing potential gating if integrated later.

7. Overall, balance pipeline stage workloads better and isolate the multiplier stage cleanly to improve timing and reduce power consumption due to toggling in long combinational paths.

The interface and functional behavior remain unchanged, but latency increases from 4 to 5 cycles for better timing closure.

This approach should improve maximum frequency, reduce dynamic power, and possibly reduce area by better mapping multiplier to DSP blocks or vendor primitives.
