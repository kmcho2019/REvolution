Simplify the float multiplier by implementing a multi-cycle sequential approach controlled by a 3-bit counter, spreading operations over several clock cycles to reduce combinational complexity per cycle. 

Key simplifications:
- Extraction of sign, exponent, and mantissa on the first cycle.
- Perform the 24x24 mantissa multiplication across one cycle (using a single clock multiplication).
- Normalize and round result in subsequent cycles using registers to hold intermediate values.
- Handle special cases upfront on the first cycle.
- Output final result on last cycle.

This approach reduces critical path per cycle by breaking the process into multiple steps, enabling use of simpler combinational logic and smaller datapaths at each step. It keeps the IEEE 754 compliance intact and uses registered intermediate signals per cycle. 

The counter will sequence through states:
0 - reset/init
1 - extract and prepare
2 - multiply mantissas
3 - normalize and round
4 - generate output

Outputs are registered and updated synchronously on clk. Reset clears all state.

This multi-cycle design trades latency for lower combinational complexity and easier timing closure, simplifying implementation while maintaining full IEEE 754 single-precision multiplication functionality.
