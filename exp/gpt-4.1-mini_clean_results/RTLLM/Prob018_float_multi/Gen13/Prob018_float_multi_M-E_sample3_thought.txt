Implement a fully combinational IEEE-754 single-precision floating-point multiplier without a multi-cycle state machine, targeting a single-cycle latency design with clock gating style reset synchronization. 

Key ideas:
- Extract sign, exponent, and mantissa immediately each clock cycle.
- Handle special cases (NaN, infinity, zero) directly in combinational logic.
- Use a 24x24-bit multiplier implemented combinationally to get a 48-bit product.
- Normalize the product based on the leading bit in the product (bit 47).
- Adjust the exponent accordingly, including bias subtraction and addition.
- Use round-to-nearest-even rounding with guard, round, and sticky bits derived from the lower bits of the product.
- Output the final IEEE-754 formatted 32-bit result registered at clock edge.
- On reset, clear the output register.
- Use a simple 3-stage internal pipeline implemented by registers on sign, exponent, mantissa, product, and rounding stages to balance timing and reduce clock-to-output delay.
- No state machine or explicit counter, instead a pipelined stage counter (3-bit) internally to track cycles, enabling continuous input feeding and throughput of one multiply per 3 cycles.
- This design offers higher throughput by pipelining with a small 3-cycle latency and no FSM overhead.
