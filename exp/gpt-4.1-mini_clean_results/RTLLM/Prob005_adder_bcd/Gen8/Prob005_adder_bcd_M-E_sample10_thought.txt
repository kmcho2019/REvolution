Instead of adding A, B, and Cin directly as a 5-bit sum, first perform the binary addition and then detect if the 4-bit raw sum is invalid as a BCD digit by checking if it is greater than 9 or if there was a carry-out from the 4-bit addition. The correction is then applied only if either condition is true. This method uses the carry-out from the binary addition as an immediate indicator of overflow beyond 9, simplifying the detection logic. Finally, a separate 4-bit adder adds the correction value (6) when needed, avoiding a conditional 5-bit adder. This modular approach isolates addition and correction steps, making the design more understandable and potentially easing timing optimization by splitting combinational logic into smaller blocks.
