To improve the existing 4-bit BCD adder design for better area and power efficiency while maintaining correctness, I will reduce intermediate signal widths and merge the correction logic directly into a single addition stage. Instead of using 5-bit and 6-bit wide signals, I'll keep the addition within 5 bits and use a simple multiplexing correction logic to avoid a second full adder stage. I will also optimize overflow detection by using a simplified minimal expression that directly uses the known BCD overflow condition, reducing logic complexity. This reduces signal toggling and hardware area while keeping a single combinational path for better timing. The final outputs will still be corrected BCD sum and carry-out indicating overflow beyond 9.
