Perform a straightforward 4-bit binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin to produce an intermediate 5-bit sum. Then detect if the sum is invalid BCD (>9) by checking if the 5-bit sum is greater than 9 using standard BCD correction criteria.

If correction is needed, add 6 (binary 0110) to the 4-bit sum to produce the corrected BCD digit and set the carry-out Cout. Otherwise, output the sum directly with no carry.

This solution uses two separate adders: one for the initial sum and one for the correction addition of 6. Using dedicated adders simplifies the logic, improves timing by breaking the path into two shorter adders, and is more intuitive and standard for BCD addition.

Finally, Cout is asserted if correction was applied, indicating a carry to the next decimal digit. The Sum output is guaranteed to be a valid BCD digit (0-9).
