To optimize the original design for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will simplify the BCD correction addition by using a small 4-bit ripple carry adder that adds 6 when correction is needed. Instead of manually computing each bit addition for the correction addition, using a straightforward conditional adder with correction input multiplexing improves timing by reducing logic levels.

Correction detection will still use the efficient boolean logic to avoid magnitude comparison. The corrected sum is produced by either the raw sum (if no correction) or the sum plus 6 (if correction needed). The final carry-out is generated by OR'ing the carry-out from the initial addition and from the correction addition.

This approach simplifies and regularizes the logic (which helps synthesis tools optimize better) and slightly reduces area and power by avoiding redundant logic gates used in bitwise manual additions.

Additionally, it improves timing since the correction addition is a single ripple carry adder rather than multiple conditional logic and mux stages.
