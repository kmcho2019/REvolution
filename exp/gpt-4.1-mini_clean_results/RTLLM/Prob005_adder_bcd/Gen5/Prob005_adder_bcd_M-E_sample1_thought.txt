Instead of performing the full binary addition followed by a conditional correction, this design splits the problem into two parts: first, add A and B without carry-in and apply BCD correction if needed; second, add the carry-in to the corrected sum and again apply correction if needed. This two-step correction approach ensures that carry-in is handled cleanly and that the BCD correction logic is simplified. By using intermediate sums and correction signals, this method breaks down the problem and allows precise control of carry propagation and correction. It also mimics a ripple-carry style handling for BCD addition, which could be beneficial in some hardware implementation scenarios. The design uses simple combinational logic to detect when correction (adding 6) is necessary after each addition phase.
