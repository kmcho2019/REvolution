Implement the BCD adder using a two-step approach with explicit separation of the binary addition and BCD correction logic using combinational boolean expressions. First, perform a binary addition of A, B, and Cin to get a 5-bit sum. Then, detect invalid BCD by checking if the 4-bit sum is greater than 9 or if there is a carry out from the addition itself. Instead of adding 6 directly, use a small combinational circuit (based on correction conditions) to generate the corrected sum bits by adjusting bits selectively, effectively doing the +6 correction through conditional bit manipulations and multiplexers. Finally, the carry-out is generated if a correction happened, which indicates the sum exceeded valid BCD range. This method reduces reliance on full adders for correction and uses minimal logic gates for correction, potentially improving area and power. The carry-out comes from the correction flag.
