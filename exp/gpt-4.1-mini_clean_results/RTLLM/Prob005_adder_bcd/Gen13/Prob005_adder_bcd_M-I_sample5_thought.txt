To optimize the existing 4-bit BCD adder, I will eliminate the second adder stage used for correction by implementing the BCD correction directly with combinational logic. Instead of adding 6 using a full adder on overflow, I will compute each corrected sum bit by logic expressions derived from the known BCD addition properties. This reduces area by avoiding a second adder, lowers power by reducing toggling in the correction addition, and improves timing by shortening the critical path to a single adder plus combinational gates for correction. The overflow detection expression will remain the same, as it is minimal. The final corrected sum and carry out (Cout) will be generated using this optimized combinational approach.
