To improve the original BCD adder design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will merge the correction addition into the raw sum adder by using a conditional increment in the same combinational logic block instead of performing a separate 5-bit addition for correction. Specifically, I will:

1. Perform the initial raw sum of A, B, and Cin as before.
2. Detect correction_needed based on the raw sum bits.
3. Instead of adding 6 via a second adder stage, use a combinational logic block that adds 6 to the raw sum when needed, using smaller logic expressions or a case statement.
4. This avoids a second adder, reducing gate count and area.
5. Keep the correction detection logic simple and minimal.

This approach reduces the critical path by eliminating the second addition stage and lowers area/power by removing the second full adder. The logic for adding 6 to the raw sum can be implemented via simple combinational logic since it is a fixed constant addition. This makes the design more efficient while preserving correctness and readability.
