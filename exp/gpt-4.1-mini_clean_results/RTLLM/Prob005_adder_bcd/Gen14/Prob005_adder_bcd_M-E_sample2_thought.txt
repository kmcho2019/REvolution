Implement the 4-bit BCD adder by first performing a 5-bit binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin to get an initial sum and carry-out. Then, detect if correction is needed (i.e., if the sum is greater than 9) by directly checking the binary sum bits with a simplified condition: sum > 9 if the carry-out of addition is 1 or if the sum is greater than 9 without carry. When correction is needed, instead of adding 6, use a conditional 4-bit multiplexer to select either the original sum (if no correction needed) or the sum minus 10 (which is sum - 6 after adding 6) combined with asserting Cout. This approach reduces the correction addition to a simple subtract-10 operation implemented with bitwise logic and avoids an explicit correction addition stage, simplifying the datapath and potentially reducing critical path delay and area.

The output Sum is the corrected BCD digit, and Cout indicates if the decimal sum exceeded 9, requiring carry propagation.

This approach efficiently combines correction detection and result adjustment in a small, fast combinational block without an explicit correction addition.
