To optimize the existing design for better PPA, we maintain the same algorithmic approach but improve the correction addition logic by using a more concise and regular adder structure. Instead of manually coding bitwise addition with individual wires and complex carry generation, we use a 4-bit adder with the correction (6) masked by the correction_needed signal. This reduces gate-level complexity and improves synthesis optimization.

Moreover, to simplify the correction detection logic, we keep the existing Boolean expression since it's already efficient.

The carry-out after correction will be the OR of the raw carry out (bit 4 of raw sum) and the carry out from the correction addition, ensuring correctness.

This approach balances hardware simplicity and clarity, potentially reducing area and power due to clearer synthesis of adder structures, while maintaining performance.

Key changes:
- Use a dedicated 4-bit adder for sum_low + (correction_needed ? 6 : 0) instead of bitwise manual addition.
- Correction_needed logic remains the same.
- Final carry is OR of raw carry and correction carry.

This will help synthesis tools infer optimized arithmetic cells and possibly reduce delay and area.
