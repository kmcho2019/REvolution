Combine the low-area and clean arithmetic approach from Example 2 with the hardware-friendly, minimal-logic correction detection from Example 1. 

Specifically:
- Use a 5-bit raw sum (A + B + Cin) to capture carry-out.
- Detect correction needed by the efficient Boolean expression from Example 1: correction_needed = raw_sum[4] | (raw_sum[3] & (raw_sum[2] | raw_sum[1])). This avoids a numeric comparator and reduces logic complexity.
- Use the '+' operator to conditionally add 6 to the raw sum's lower 4 bits when correction is needed, enabling synthesis tools to optimize the adder efficiently (better than manual ripple carry).
- Generate the final sum from corrected sum bits and the carry-out from the logical OR of the raw sum carry and correction carry.
- This fusion achieves minimal gate count, reduced switching by avoiding unnecessary correction addition, and improved timing due to shorter carry chains and simpler comparator logic.

The design remains fully combinational, synthesizable, and clear in intent.
