To optimize the BCD adder for better power and area without impacting timing significantly, I will replace the 5-bit comparator with a simpler logic expression for the correction condition. The correction condition (sum > 9) can be implemented by checking if the sum is greater than 9 using a simple combinational logic based on the bits of raw_sum instead of a 5-bit comparator. This reduces gate count and switching activity.

Specifically, the BCD correction is needed if:
- The carry out from the addition (the 5th bit of raw_sum) is 1, or
- The sum is greater than 9 (1001), which can be detected as:
  raw_sum[4] = 1, or
  (raw_sum[3] & (raw_sum[2] | raw_sum[1])) = 1 (which covers decimal sums 10-15)

Also, since we add 6 only when correction_needed is true, the addition of 6 can be done via a conditional adder or using a 4-bit adder with carry. To keep the design simple and efficient, I will implement the sum addition and correction in a single 5-bit adder using the initial sum plus conditional addition of 6, but replacing the comparator with optimized logic.

This reduces power by minimizing toggling in comparator logic and reduces area by avoiding a full 5-bit comparator.

The Cout will remain driven by the correction condition logic.

This approach keeps a single adder and simple combinational logic for correction detection, providing a cleaner and more power/area optimized BCD adder.
