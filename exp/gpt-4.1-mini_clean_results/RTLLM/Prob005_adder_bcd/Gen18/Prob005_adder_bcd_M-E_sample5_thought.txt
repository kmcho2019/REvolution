Implement the 4-bit BCD adder by first performing binary addition of A, B, and Cin resulting in a 5-bit raw sum. Instead of comparing the entire sum to 9, use two simpler conditions that detect BCD overflow: check if the upper nibble of the sum (bits 4 and 3) is greater than 0, or if the lower nibble is greater than 9. If either condition is true, a correction of 6 is added. This approach reduces the complexity of the overflow detection to simple bitwise checks and simple comparisons, avoiding a full comparator for sum > 9. The correction is always a fixed addition of 6 when needed, and carry-out is generated based on whether correction was applied and the carry from the addition. This method balances clarity with simple logic and avoids conditional ternary operators by using multiplexers.
