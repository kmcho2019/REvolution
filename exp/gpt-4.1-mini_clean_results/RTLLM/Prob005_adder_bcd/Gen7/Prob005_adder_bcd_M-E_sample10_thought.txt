Perform a straightforward 4-bit binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin to obtain a 5-bit sum. Instead of implementing a complex correction adder or using Boolean logic to detect when correction is needed, use a simpler approach by explicitly checking if the 4-bit sum is greater than 9 or if the carry-out from the addition is set.

When correction is needed, add 6 (binary 0110) to the lower 4 bits of the sum to convert the binary result into a valid BCD digit, while taking the carry bit into account.

This design uses a 5-bit adder twice: once for the initial addition and once for the conditional correction addition, controlled by the correction-needed signal. This approach simplifies logic at the expense of using an additional small adder. This tradeoff improves design clarity and makes it easy to verify correctness.

The Cout is derived from the carry out of the correction addition, indicating overflow beyond one BCD digit.

This modular structure aids readability and aligns with the concept of BCD addition as a two-step process: add and then conditionally correct.
