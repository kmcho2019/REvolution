Implement the 4-bit BCD adder by first performing the binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin into a 5-bit sum. Then, instead of checking sum > 9 with a single comparator, use combinational logic based on BCD overflow conditions: the sum's upper nibble bit (sum[4]) or sum[3] and sum[2] both set indicate the sum exceeded 9. Based on this overflow signal, add 6 (0110) to the 4-bit sum to correct it. Use two adders internally: the first adds inputs and Cin, the second adds 6 conditionally. Generate Cout from the overflow detection, thus avoiding adding the carry from correction and relying on explicit overflow logic. This separate detection and correction logic clarifies each stage, and the overflow condition is derived from BCD properties rather than a numeric comparator. This modular and explicit design facilitates easy debugging and verification.
