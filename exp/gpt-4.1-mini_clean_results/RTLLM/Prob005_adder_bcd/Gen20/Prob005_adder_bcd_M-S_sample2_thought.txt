Simplify the BCD adder by performing a single 5-bit addition of A, B, and Cin, then directly checking if the 4-bit sum or the carry out from the addition indicates a sum greater than 9. Use a minimal combinational logic expression for the correction condition: correction is needed if the 5-bit sum is greater than 9 (which can be checked by a simple OR of higher bits or direct comparison). Add 6 (0110) only when necessary. Use a simple mux to select between the raw sum and the corrected sum. Generate Cout from the correction condition. This approach avoids complex comparators and multiple adders, minimizing hardware and logic while keeping the design fully combinational and easy to understand.
