Implement the 4-bit BCD adder by first performing the binary addition of A, B, and Cin into a 5-bit sum. Instead of directly comparing the sum to 9, use a combination of the carry out and specific bit patterns to detect BCD overflow:

- A BCD sum is invalid if the sum exceeds 9. This occurs if the carry out from the binary addition (bit 4) is set, OR if the binary sum is greater than 9 without the carry out (i.e., sum[3] & (sum[2] | sum[1])).

If overflow is detected, add 6 (0110) to the lower 4 bits of the sum to correct it. This addition can be done using a small combinational adder.

The carry out of the BCD adder is generated by combining the raw carry out and the carry out of the correction adder.

This approach avoids using a full comparator (>9) and relies on simpler logic for overflow detection, plus one correction adder. It balances logic simplicity with clean, modular code.
