Design a 4-bit BCD adder using a two-step approach: first perform a normal 4-bit binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin to generate a raw 5-bit sum. Then, detect if the raw sum is invalid BCD (>9) by directly comparing the sum against 9 using simple combinational logic. If correction is needed, add 6 to the raw sum. 

Instead of manually coding the addition of 6, implement the correction by reusing a 4-bit full adder instance to add 6 conditionally. This modular approach separates concerns, improves readability, and enables straightforward pipelining or reuse of the full adder module. The carry-out is generated from either the raw sum's carry or the correction adder's carry.

This design is fully combinational, clean, and scalable. It leverages modularity by implementing a reusable 4-bit binary adder to handle both the initial sum and the correction addition. It trades a little area for clarity and easier maintenance while keeping correction logic simple and the critical path short.

The module uses:
- A parameterized 4-bit binary adder submodule
- Direct combinational comparison for correction detection (sum > 9)
- Conditional correction addition by adding 6 via the binary adder
- Correct carry-out logic considering both addition stages
