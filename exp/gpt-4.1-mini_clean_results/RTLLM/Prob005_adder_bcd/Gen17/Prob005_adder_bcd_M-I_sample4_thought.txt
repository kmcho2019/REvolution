To improve the PPA of the existing BCD adder design, I will streamline the BCD correction addition step by replacing the manual bitwise addition with a conditional 4-bit addition using a small 4-bit adder implemented by the '+' operator. This will reduce the ripple carry chain of manually created XOR and AND gates, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the correction addition more effectively for timing and area.

Also, I will perform the correction addition only when needed by using a mux at the output, which avoids switching in the correction logic when no correction is necessary, thus saving dynamic power.

For carry out, I will compute it from the corrected sum overflow (i.e., if the corrected 5-bit sum exceeds 9) or from the raw sum MSB, ensuring correct overflow detection.

Overall, this design balances clarity, reduces manual gate-level logic, minimizes critical path delay in correction addition, and reduces switching activity, improving power, performance, and area without increasing complexity.
