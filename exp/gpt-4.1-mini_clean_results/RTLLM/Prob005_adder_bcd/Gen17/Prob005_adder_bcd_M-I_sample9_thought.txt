To optimize the original design for power and area without sacrificing performance, the correction addition by 6 is replaced with a simple mux selecting between the raw sum and the corrected sum. Instead of always performing the addition of 6 (which causes switching and area overhead), we compute the corrected sum only once and use a multiplexer to select the final sum and carry out based on the overflow condition. This reduces unnecessary switching and gate usage.

Additionally, the overflow detection logic remains the same as it is already minimal and standard for BCD. Using a conditional mux-based output avoids unnecessary adder instantiations for correction and is a common low-power design technique.

The critical path is not worsened because the correction addition and mux are simple and parallel operations.

Overall, this approach improves power by avoiding unnecessary switching and reduces area by not implementing two full adders but rather a single adder and a small mux. Performance remains high.
