To optimize the 4-bit BCD adder for better performance and reduced area/power, eliminate the second full adder that adds the correction (6). Instead, implement the BCD correction logic using combinational logic based on sum bits and carry, and generate corrected sum bits and carry-out directly with simple logic operations.

Approach:
- Perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin to get a 5-bit sum.
- Detect if correction is needed (sum > 9 or carry-out set).
- If correction needed, correct sum = sum + 6.
- Instead of adding 6 with a full adder, compute corrected sum bits directly by bitwise operations:
    - BCD correction +6 (0110) shifts bits and influences sum bits in known pattern.
    - Use the fact that adding 6 to invalid BCD sum produces valid BCD digit.
- Generate final sum and carry-out by muxing between uncorrected sum and corrected sum bits based on correction flag.

This approach reduces the number of adders (no second adder), replacing correction with combinational logic, lowering area and power and potentially improving timing.
