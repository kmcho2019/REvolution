Implement the 4-bit BCD adder by first performing a binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin resulting in a 5-bit sum. Then detect if correction is needed by checking two conditions: either if the 4-bit raw sum is greater than 9 or if there is a carry from the 4-bit addition. To detect sum > 9, compare bits using a simple priority encoder logic rather than a direct comparator.

Once correction is detected, add 6 (0110) to the lower 4 bits using a dedicated 4-bit adder instantiated from a simple ripple carry adder sub-module. This modular approach improves clarity and reusability. The final carry out is the carry of this correction addition or the initial carry out, whichever is high.

This approach trades some additional modules (a small ripple carry adder) for code clarity and modularity. The correction detection is concise and leverages common logic, while the correction addition uses a straightforward ripple carry adder submodule.

Advantages:
- Modularity and clarity via a separate 4-bit ripple carry adder for correction
- Explicit and clear correction detection logic
