The original design is functionally correct and clean. To improve PPA, specifically aiming to reduce area and power, I propose simplifying the overflow detection logic by using a simple comparator-based approach instead of the boolean expression. Although comparators sometimes introduce more logic, here the condition sum > 9 can be easily detected by checking if the upper nibble of sum is nonzero or if the 4-bit sum is greater than 9.

Moreover, we can remove the 5-bit and 6-bit intermediate signals by performing additions directly on 4-bit sums and managing carries carefully, which reduces bit-width and logic complexity.

Also, instead of conditionally adding 6 using a full 5-bit adder, use a simple adder with carry-in from the overflow signal, adding 6 only when overflow is asserted, optimizing the constant addition.

Finally, using an always_comb block with local variables instead of multiple continuous assignments can help the synthesis tool optimize logic better and reduce glitches, which might lower dynamic power.

These changes improve the clarity, reduce signal width, and potentially lower the combinational delay and area, as well as dynamic power due to less toggling on intermediate signals.
