Use a two-step approach: first perform the binary addition of A, B, and Cin to get a 5-bit sum. Then, detect if the sum is invalid as BCD by using a straightforward comparator (sum > 9). If correction is needed, instead of adding 6 via a ripple carry, implement the correction by using a combinational lookup table (case statement) mapping each invalid sum (from 10 to 19) to its corrected BCD value, while sums 0 to 9 pass through directly. This eliminates the need for ripple carry addition of 6 and reduces gate levels, improving timing and area. The carry-out is generated if the sum is greater than 9, indicating overflow beyond a single BCD digit. The lookup table approach offers a clean, easily understandable design that synthesis tools optimize efficiently.
