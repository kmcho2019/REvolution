The original solution uses two sequential additions: first for the raw sum (A + B + Cin), then conditionally adding 6 for BCD correction. This introduces extra delay and area due to two adders and muxing.

Improvement strategy:
- Perform a single 5-bit addition incorporating A, B, Cin, and conditionally add 6 in one step.
- To do this, precompute the condition for correction and use it to add 6 within a single adder via a carry chain or a conditional constant input, avoiding two separate additions.
- Implement the adder and correction logic in one expression by adding (A + B + Cin + 6 * correction) in a single assignment.
- This reduces the number of adders from two to one, reducing critical path, area, and power.
- Keep correction logic minimal as before.
- This approach results in a single adder adding a 5-bit input with a 5-bit input that's either zero or 6, no multiplexing after addition needed.

This single-step approach improves timing by halving the add operations, reduces logic complexity, and leads to better PPA without changing functionality.
