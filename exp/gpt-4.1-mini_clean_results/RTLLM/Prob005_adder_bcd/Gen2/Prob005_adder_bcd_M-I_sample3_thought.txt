To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, I will eliminate the always block and use purely combinational logic with conditional multiplexing for correction. Instead of a two-stage addition (one for sum and one for correction), I will compute the initial sum and then directly select between the uncorrected sum and corrected sum (sum + 6) using a conditional operator. This approach reduces logic depth by avoiding an additional adder instantiated within the always block. Also, using wires and continuous assignments promotes better synthesis optimization and easier timing closure. The 5-bit intermediate sums enable clean carry extraction. This modification preserves the functional correctness while reducing critical path and switching activity, leading to improved overall PPA.
