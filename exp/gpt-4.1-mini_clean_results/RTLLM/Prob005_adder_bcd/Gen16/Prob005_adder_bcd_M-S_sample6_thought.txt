Simplify the BCD adder by performing the initial 5-bit addition of inputs A, B, and Cin, then detect if the sum exceeds 9 using a minimal combinational logic expression without a full comparator. Instead of adding 6 conditionally using an adder, directly add the correction constant (6) to the raw sum using a single combinational expression that adds either 0 or 6 based on the correction condition. The Cout is derived from the correction condition. This approach eliminates extra adders and comparators, reduces logic complexity, and keeps the design fully combinational and compact.
