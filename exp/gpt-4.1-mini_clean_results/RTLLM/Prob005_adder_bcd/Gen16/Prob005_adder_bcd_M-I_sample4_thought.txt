To optimize the 4-bit BCD adder for better performance, area, and power, I will eliminate the second 5-bit adder stage used for correction by implementing the correction step with combinational logic. Instead of adding 6 conditionally through an adder, the corrected sum bits will be derived by applying known BCD correction rules directly with logic gates, using the overflow signal and raw_sum bits. This reduces the critical path by removing a full addition stage and saves area and power by reducing the complexity of the combinational block. The overflow detection logic remains simplified as before, but the correction uses a small combinational logic network to adjust only the required bits, minimizing switching activity and gate count.
