To optimize the existing BCD adder for better performance, power, and area (PPA), we focus on simplifying the BCD correction addition step and reducing the carry chain length.

Improvement strategy:
1. Instead of a ripple-carry manual addition of sum_low + 6, implement a combinational logic for correction addition using minimal gates derived from the known pattern of adding 6.
2. Use conditional correction: if correction_needed, add 6; else keep sum_low unchanged, done via simple combinational logic to reduce toggling and critical path.
3. Compute Cout as (raw_sum > 9) or correction carry out, but simplify to Cout = correction_needed since correction_needed signals sum > 9.
4. This avoids an explicit multi-bit adder for correction and reduces carry propagation delay.
5. The overall design remains purely combinational and clear, but with less gate count and faster correction addition.

This approach reduces critical path delays introduced by multi-bit carry propagation and reduces switching activity by generating the corrected sum bits with minimal logic derived from sum_low bits and correction_needed signal.
