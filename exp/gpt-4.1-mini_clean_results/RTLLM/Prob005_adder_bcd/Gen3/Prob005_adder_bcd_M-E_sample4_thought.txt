Implement the BCD adder using a two-step approach with explicit intermediate wires for the binary sum and then a minimal combinational BCD correction logic without performing a second full addition. The idea is to generate the initial 4-bit binary sum and carry-out from adding A, B, and Cin. Then, instead of adding 6 directly, detect the correction condition and compute the corrected sum bits by using bitwise logic to add 6. This correction logic uses simple logic gates and multiplexers to selectively add 6 to the sum, minimizing addition stages and reducing area and delay. The carry-out is generated from the correction step as usual. This approach avoids two full adders and performs BCD correction through combinational logic that selectively toggles bits, improving power and area efficiency.
