To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will replace the 5-bit comparator (raw_sum > 9) with a more efficient logic expression that detects when correction is needed using only the sum bits. This avoids a full 5-bit comparator and reduces delay and power.

The condition for correction in BCD addition is when:
- The 4-bit sum[3:0] > 9, or
- There's a carry out from the 4-bit addition itself.

We can detect correction with the simplified logic:
correction_needed = (raw_sum[4]) | (raw_sum[3] & (raw_sum[2] | raw_sum[1]));

This expression derives from the fact that a binary sum greater than 9 means the 5-bit sum's highest bit (bit 4) is set, or bit 3 along with either bit 2 or bit 1 is set.

Adding 6 (0110) can be efficiently done using simple logic without a full adder for the correction since 6 is constant.

To reduce area, I will implement the correction by adding 6 using bitwise operations only when correction_needed is asserted, avoiding a second 5-bit addition.

This also simplifies the critical path, reduces logic switching (power), and area since the comparator logic and conditional addition are simpler.

The outputs remain the same, with Sum[3:0] corrected and Cout indicating the carry.
