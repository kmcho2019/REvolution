To optimize the given 4-bit BCD adder for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will reduce the logic levels by eliminating the second full 5-bit addition for BCD correction. Since the correction is always either 0 or 6, I can implement the correction addition combinationally by simple logic that adds 6 when overflow is detected.

The overflow detection logic remains as before since it is already minimal. Instead of adding 6 using a full adder, I will use a combination of gates to generate the corrected sum bits directly from the raw sum and overflow signal, minimizing logic depth and area.

This approach avoids a second addition stage and reduces toggling and area, improving timing and power without compromising functionality.

The final outputs (Sum and Cout) come directly from this optimized correction logic.
