To create a novel 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, let's use a carry-lookahead subtraction approach explicitly by designing a custom 64-bit subtractor using bitwise operations and propagate/generate signals for carry borrow calculation, rather than relying on the '+' operator with two's complement.

The subtraction A - B can be seen as A + (~B + 1), but we'll implement borrow logic explicitly:

1. Define generate (g) and propagate (p) bits per bit for subtraction:
   - g[i] = A[i] & ~B[i] (borrow generate)
   - p[i] = ~(A[i] ^ B[i]) (borrow propagate)
2. Compute borrow chain starting from borrow_in = 0:
   - borrow[i+1] = g[i] | (p[i] & borrow[i])
3. Result bit is:
   - result[i] = A[i] ^ B[i] ^ borrow[i]
4. Overflow detection follows the same principle for signed subtraction:
   - Overflow occurs if the sign of A and B differ and the sign of the result differs from sign of A.
   
This explicit borrow chain subtractor is a classical approach, offering fine-grained control of the carry/borrow logic which might benefit certain synthesis tools or allow future modification for faster carry-lookahead structures. It also avoids using '+' operator directly on large vectors.

This design is fully combinational, uses vectorized signal assignment for efficiency, and produces the 64-bit subtraction and overflow flags as specified.
