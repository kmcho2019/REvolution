To optimize the 64-bit subtractor for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will implement a true hierarchical carry-lookahead adder (CLA) instead of a simple ripple carry generated with a generate loop. Specifically, I will:

- Use a 4-level hierarchical CLA structure dividing 64 bits into 4 blocks of 16 bits, where each 16-bit block is a CLA submodule.
- Implement a 16-bit CLA module with proper propagate and generate signals and local carry-lookahead logic.
- Use a 4-bit CLA to combine the four 16-bit blocks carry signals to generate carries efficiently.
- Internally invert B only once inside the subtractor module, not at multiple places.
- Maintain a fully combinational structure without registers to avoid additional latency.
- Keep overflow detection logic as before.

This hierarchical approach drastically reduces the critical path by computing carries in parallel rather than linearly. It also reduces switching power by avoiding long carry chains. Area may slightly increase due to additional CLA logic but overall should improve efficiency.

The modular design also improves clarity and maintainability.
