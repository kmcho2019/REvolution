Use a direct combinational approach leveraging Verilog's built-in subtraction operator for simplicity and clarity. Compute result as (A - B) using the built-in minus operator. Perform overflow detection by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result: overflow occurs if A and B have different signs and result sign differs from A sign.

This approach:
- Eliminates the need for hierarchical CLA blocks and explicit carry logic.
- Maintains correctness of subtraction and overflow detection.
- Simplifies the code and eases synthesis tool optimization.
- Is suitable for high-level RTL where manual carry optimization is not critical.
