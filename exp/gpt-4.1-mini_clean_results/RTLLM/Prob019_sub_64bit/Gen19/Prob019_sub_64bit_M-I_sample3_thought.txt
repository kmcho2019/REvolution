To improve the original 64-bit subtractor design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), the key enhancement is to replace the linear chain of four 16-bit carry lookahead adders (CLAs) with a hierarchical two-level CLA structure. Instead of cascading four 16-bit CLAs sequentially, the design will:

- First, build 16-bit blocks internally using 4-bit CLAs with their own carry lookahead logic to reduce internal delay and logic complexity.
- Then, construct a 64-bit CLA by composing four 16-bit blocks, using a 4-bit CLA carry lookahead block at the top level for carry propagation among 16-bit blocks. This converts a linear carry chain into a parallel carry lookahead with logarithmic carry computation.
- This hierarchical CLA approach significantly reduces critical path delay compared to the original chained 16-bit CLAs because carry signals propagate faster through grouped generate/propagate signals rather than bitwise linear chains.
- The overflow detection remains unchanged as it is minimal logic based on MSB signs.
- By reducing logic levels and grouping carry computations, the design will reduce switching activity (hence power) and area (due to more balanced logic blocks).
- The modular 4-bit and 16-bit CLA blocks enhance code reusability, clarity, and ease of verification.

This improvement yields faster operation with potentially reduced power and area due to balanced logic depth and fewer transistors in carry chains, effectively optimizing the original design while maintaining correctness.
