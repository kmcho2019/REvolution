The existing design uses eight 8-bit CLA blocks chained serially, resulting in a carry chain length of 8, which limits maximum clock frequency. To improve performance (reduce carry chain delay) and potentially area, a hierarchical CLA structure can be applied.

Improvement strategy:
- Partition the 64-bit subtraction into four 16-bit CLA blocks instead of eight 8-bit blocks.
- Implement each 16-bit CLA subtractor with internal carry lookahead logic, splitting into two 8-bit CLA blocks and a 2-bit block carry lookahead to reduce carry chain length internally.
- Between the four 16-bit blocks, use a 4-bit carry-lookahead for carry propagation to minimize carry chain delay from 8 to 4 blocks.
- This hierarchical approach reduces the number of carry propagation levels and speeds up the critical path, improving timing.
- Overflow detection remains the same based on sign bits.
- The design remains fully combinational and synthesizable.
- For power and area, fewer blocks and hierarchical carry logic can slightly reduce wiring complexity and switching activity.

This hierarchical CLA subtractor balances performance and area better than flat 8-bit block chaining, improving maximum frequency and reducing carry propagation delay.
