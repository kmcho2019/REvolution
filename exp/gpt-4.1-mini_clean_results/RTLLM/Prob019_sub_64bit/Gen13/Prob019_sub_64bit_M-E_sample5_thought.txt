Implement the 64-bit subtractor directly using a single 64-bit two's complement subtraction: result = A + (~B) + 1. This leverages the built-in Verilog addition for simplicity and synthesizability. 

To detect overflow in subtraction for signed numbers, use the standard condition:
Overflow occurs if the sign of A and B differ, and the sign of the result differs from the sign of A. Formally:
overflow = (A[63] != B[63]) && (result[63] != A[63])

This approach eliminates the need for chaining smaller subtractors or building custom CLAs manually, resulting in a compact and efficient RTL description that synthesizes to a fast carry-lookahead or carry-select adder inside the FPGA/ASIC tools. It also reduces area and power compared to manual decompositions.

The module will therefore consist of:
- Computing result = A + (~B) + 1
- Overflow logic as described
- Clean input/output port definitions

This high-level approach exploits vendor synthesis optimizations for large adders/subtractors and is idiomatic Verilog for subtractor implementation with overflow detection.
