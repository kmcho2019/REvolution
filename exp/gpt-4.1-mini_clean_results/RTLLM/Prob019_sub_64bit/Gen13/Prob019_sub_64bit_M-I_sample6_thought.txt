The previous design uses a flat carry-lookahead adder (CLA) with per-bit carry logic in a generate loop, which effectively acts as a ripple carry chain, limiting performance due to carry propagation delay through all 64 bits. To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will implement a hierarchical CLA structure.

Improvement strategy:
- Split the 64-bit CLA into eight 8-bit CLA blocks.
- Each 8-bit CLA computes local sum, local propagate, and local generate signals.
- A top-level 8-block CLA processes the block-level propagate and generate signals to quickly generate block carries, enabling faster carry computation.
- This reduces the critical path from 64 ripple carry stages to 8 ripple stages plus top-level carry lookahead, improving performance and reducing power.
- Area may slightly increase due to extra logic for hierarchy but is offset by faster carry computation and lower glitching.
- Overflow detection remains unchanged.

This hierarchical structure balances clarity and speed, and is well known in adder design.

I will:
- Implement an 8-bit CLA module that outputs sum and block propagate/generate.
- Implement the top-level 64-bit CLA using eight 8-bit CLAs and a higher-level CLA for block carries.
- Use this in the subtractor by feeding A and ~B plus 1 as before.
