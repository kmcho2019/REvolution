The original design splits the 64-bit subtraction into two 32-bit ripple borrow subtractors, which is modular but suffers from linear borrow propagation delays in both halves, affecting timing and increasing power. To optimize for better PPA:

1. Replace the ripple borrow subtractor with a single 64-bit subtractor implemented using the Verilog built-in subtraction operator. This approach leverages synthesis tools' optimized carry look-ahead or carry-select subtractors, reducing critical path delay and overall area.

2. Keep overflow detection logic as is since it is already minimal and correct.

3. Use purely combinational subtraction without explicit borrow chain logic, allowing the synthesis tool to handle carry propagation more efficiently.

This change reduces complexity and borrow propagation stages, improving speed and lowering power and area while maintaining correct functionality and overflow detection.
