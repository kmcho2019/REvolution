Implement the 64-bit subtractor as a single 64-bit ripple borrow subtractor built from a chain of full subtractor (one-bit subtractor) modules. Each full subtractor takes a borrow-in and outputs a borrow-out, enabling precise borrow propagation. The subtraction is performed bitwise as A - B - borrow_in. 

The overflow detection logic uses the classic signed overflow condition for subtraction: overflow occurs if the sign bits of A and B differ, and the sign bit of the result differs from the sign bit of A. This overflow logic is implemented at the final stage.

This fully bit-serial borrow ripple approach is conceptually simple and modular with fully synthesizable logic. Although slower than carry-lookahead, it is easy to understand, verify, and is area-efficient. The single-bit subtractor module is reused 64 times and the borrow is propagated from LSB to MSB. The overflow is computed from the MSB bits only, making it straightforward.

This differs from the provided solution by using borrow logic for subtraction rather than transforming subtraction into addition with carry-lookahead adders. It emphasizes a fundamental borrow ripple subtractor chain and modular design.
