Implement the 64-bit subtractor as a single combinational subtractor using Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic capabilities combined with explicit overflow detection logic. 

Key ideas:
- Perform the subtraction directly as A - B using the Verilog minus operator, interpreting A and B as signed 64-bit values.
- Extract the sign bits of operands and result to detect overflow based on the signed subtraction overflow rules:
  Overflow occurs if:
  - A is positive and B is negative but result is negative (positive overflow)
  - A is negative and B is positive but result is positive (negative overflow)
- Use signed reg or wire types to ensure arithmetic is treated as signed.
- This approach avoids the complexity of multi-level CLA or other carry-lookahead structures, relying on synthesis tools to optimize subtraction efficiently.
- The simplicity improves readability, reduces structural overhead, and lets synthesis target FPGA/ASIC-specific optimizations.
- The design is fully combinational with no clock or registers, suitable for a high-precision arithmetic unit.
