Fuse the strengths of both designs by implementing a 64-bit subtractor using a multi-level hierarchical carry-lookahead structure to improve performance and reduce carry propagation delay compared to a simple ripple or single-level CLA. The subtractor computes A - B as A + (~B + 1).

Key improvements:
- Partition the 64-bit adder into 4 blocks of 16 bits each.
- Each 16-bit block is a CLA with internal carry lookahead.
- A higher-level 4-bit carry-lookahead handles block-level carry between the four 16-bit blocks.
- This hierarchical CLA reduces fan-out and carry propagation delay, improving timing and power.
- Maintain modularity by implementing separate modules for 16-bit CLA and 4-bit CLA for block-level carry.
- Overflow detection is done using the classical signed subtraction overflow condition checking the sign bits of A, B, and result.

This fusion offers:
- Faster carry resolution than ripple borrow or flat 64-bit CLA.
- Lower power due to reduced glitching and shorter carry chains.
- Good modularity and clean structure.
- Minimal additional area overhead compared to flat CLA.
