To improve the original design's performance and area, replace the segmented ripple-borrow subtractors with a single, straightforward 64-bit subtraction using Verilog's built-in operators. This removes the multi-stage borrow chain and associated routing, leading to reduced delay and area.

For overflow detection, retain the clear logic comparing the sign bits of A, B, and the result, which is both correct and minimal.

This approach:
- Reduces complexity by eliminating segmented blocks and borrow propagation.
- Improves timing by avoiding ripple chains.
- Decreases area by using a single subtract operation.
- Maintains clear overflow detection logic.

This design favors synthesis tool optimizations for large adders/subtractors, yielding better overall PPA while ensuring functional correctness.
