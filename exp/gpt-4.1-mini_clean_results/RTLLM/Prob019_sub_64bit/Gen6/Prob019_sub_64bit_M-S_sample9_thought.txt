To simplify the 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, I will use the built-in subtraction operator to directly compute the result (A - B), which is efficiently synthesized by tools and reduces code complexity significantly.

Overflow detection will be performed by checking the sign bits of A, B, and the result:
- Overflow occurs if A and B have different signs and the result's sign differs from A's sign.

This approach maintains correct functionality with fewer lines of code, less intermediate signal declaration, and better readability and maintainability.
