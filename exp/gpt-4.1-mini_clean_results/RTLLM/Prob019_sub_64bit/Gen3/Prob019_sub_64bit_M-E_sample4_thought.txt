To provide a novel solution, I will implement the 64-bit subtractor using an explicit 64-bit carry-lookahead style subtraction by leveraging the generate-and-propagate concept to enhance understanding and potentially allow more explicit control over carry generation. 

The idea is:
- Express subtraction as addition of A and two's complement of B (A + (~B + 1)).
- Instead of using '+' operator directly, implement a 64-bit adder explicitly using generate (G) and propagate (P) signals and a carry-lookahead approach for carry calculation.
- This explicit carry-lookahead addition will clarify carry propagation and may enable synthesis tools to better optimize timing.
- Overflow detection remains the same: overflow occurs if the sign of A and B differ, and sign of result differs from A.
- Inputs and outputs remain the same, with synchronous combinational logic (purely combinational module).
- This solution shows how to design the adder logic manually for subtraction, giving insight and possible optimizations in timing paths.

This approach is different from using the implicit '+' operator for subtraction and demonstrates detailed bit-level adder design, which is useful in custom or educational environments.
