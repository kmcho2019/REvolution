To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, we perform A - B using simple binary subtraction. The overflow occurs in signed arithmetic when the sign of the result does not make sense based on the operand signs. For subtraction, overflow happens when: 
- A is positive, B is negative, but result is negative (positive overflow).
- A is negative, B is positive, but result is positive (negative overflow).
We extract the sign bits (MSB) of A, B, and result to check these conditions and set the overflow flag accordingly.
