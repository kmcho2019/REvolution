To improve the original 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking for better performance and power while maintaining correctness, I will replace the simple bit-wise ripple carry chain in the CLA with a hierarchical carry-lookahead adder that partitions the 64-bit addition into eight 8-bit blocks.

Each 8-bit block will have its own propagate and generate signals and produce a block-level carry-out. A top-level carry lookahead logic will quickly compute carries between these blocks, reducing the carry propagation delay from 64-bit ripple to 8-block ripple, significantly improving max clock frequency.

This hierarchical approach reduces fanout and wiring complexity compared to a flat 64-bit carry chain, which decreases dynamic power and can help area by simpler routing and smaller logic gates.

The overflow detection remains the same, relying on sign bits of inputs and result.

Overall, this optimization preserves functionality while improving timing and power efficiency.

The module uses:
- 8 instances of an 8-bit CLA block for sum computation and local carry.
- A top-level carry lookahead for the block carry signals.
