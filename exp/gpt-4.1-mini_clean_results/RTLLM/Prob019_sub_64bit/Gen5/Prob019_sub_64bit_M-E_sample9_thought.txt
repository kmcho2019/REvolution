Implement the 64-bit subtractor using a layered carry-select adder (CSA) structure optimized for subtraction. The subtraction A - B is performed by adding A to the two's complement of B (~B + 1). Instead of a single large CLA, divide the 64-bit adder into 8 blocks of 8 bits each. For each block, precompute sum and carry-out assuming carry-in = 0 and carry-in = 1.

At block boundaries, use a multiplexer to select the correct sum and carry-out based on the actual carry-in propagated from the previous block. This carry-select approach reduces the worst-case carry propagation delay compared to ripple carry and reduces complexity compared to a flat CLA.

Overflow detection is done by checking the sign bits of A, B, and the result as described:
- Overflow occurs if A and B have different signs, and the result sign differs from A's sign.

This architecture balances performance and area by trading off some area for improved timing over ripple carry, without the large complexity of a full 64-bit CLA. The modular 8-bit block design enables cleaner hierarchical implementation and easier future scaling or modifications.
