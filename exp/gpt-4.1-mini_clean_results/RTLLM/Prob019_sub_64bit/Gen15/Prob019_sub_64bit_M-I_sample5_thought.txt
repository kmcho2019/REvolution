To optimize the existing 64-bit subtractor for better performance, power, and area (PPA), we will:

- Replace the two 32-bit CLAs chained by carry with a single 64-bit CLA. This reduces logic duplication and the carry chain delay, improving timing and saving area.
- Implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure within the 64-bit CLA to efficiently compute carries and reduce critical path.
- Avoid splitting B inversion; instead, invert B inside the CLA to minimize wiring and potential power overhead.
- Maintain the existing overflow detection logic as it's already minimal and efficient.
- Keep the design fully combinational with no redundant signals to reduce switching and power.

This single 64-bit CLA approach simplifies the data path, reduces gate count, and shortens carry propagation delay, addressing the critical path and area concerns raised previously, while preserving correctness and overflow detection.
