Implement the 64-bit subtractor as a single unified 64-bit ripple borrow subtractor built using a chain of 64 1-bit full subtractor cells. Each bit performs subtraction with borrow input from the previous bit.

- The 1-bit full subtractor computes difference = A_bit - B_bit - borrow_in.
- Borrow out is generated if the subtraction requires borrowing.
- The borrow chain propagates serially from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
- This approach is simple and area-efficient though slower in timing compared to CLA, trading speed for simplicity and minimal hardware.

For overflow detection in signed 2's complement subtraction:
- Overflow occurs if the sign bit borrow behavior indicates overflow.
- Specifically, overflow can be detected by examining the borrow into and out of the MSB, or more simply by the standard overflow condition:
  - When subtracting B from A (A - B):
    overflow = (A[63] != B[63]) && (result[63] != A[63])

This ripple borrow subtractor yields a minimal and straightforward design, easier to understand and synthesize, with clearly defined borrow signals per bit and accurate signed overflow detection.
