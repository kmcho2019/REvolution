To improve the original 64-bit subtractor design, I will implement a full 64-bit carry-lookahead subtractor using hierarchical prefix logic rather than chaining four separate 16-bit CLA blocks. This reduces the carry/borrow chain latency significantly, improving the worst negative slack (WNS) and allowing a higher clock frequency (better timing).

- Implement a 64-bit prefix adder structure based on carry-lookahead principles (e.g., Kogge-Stone or Brent-Kung style).

- The subtraction is done as A + (~B) + 1, with cin = 1.
- This removes the long ripple chain of borrow signals between blocks, improving timing.

This approach slightly increases logic area for the parallel prefix tree but significantly reduces critical path delay and power by avoiding ripple carry chains and redundant intermediate signals. Overall, the performance improvement outweighs the area overhead, making it a better design for high-speed 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection.
