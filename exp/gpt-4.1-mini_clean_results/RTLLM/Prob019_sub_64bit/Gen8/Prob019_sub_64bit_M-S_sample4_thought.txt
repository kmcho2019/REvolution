Implement a straightforward 64-bit subtractor using Verilog's built-in subtraction operator for clarity and simplicity. Then, detect overflow by directly comparing the sign bits of the inputs and the result as per the signed subtraction overflow rules:

- Overflow occurs if the sign of A and B differ, and the sign of the result differs from the sign of A.

This approach reduces complexity by avoiding hierarchical CLA blocks and manually handling carry chains, relying on synthesis tools to optimize the subtraction. It keeps the functionality intact and is more concise and easier to maintain.
