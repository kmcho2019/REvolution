Refactor the 64-bit subtractor by implementing a straightforward 64-bit adder-subtractor using a single 64-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA). The subtraction is performed as A + (~B + 1). Instead of carry-select blocks, use a flat CLA for the entire 64 bits with a generate-propagate structure for faster carry computation.

This eliminates duplicated logic and multiplexers from the carry-select approach, improving area and power efficiency while maintaining good performance. The overflow detection logic remains the same, directly comparing sign bits of A, B, and the result.

The design will use continuous assignments (`assign`) and structural approach, avoiding `always` blocks and generate loops for the CLA to clarify the carry calculation. This approach simplifies the design and reduces logic duplication.

The key modules are:
- `sub_64bit`: Top module performing subtraction and overflow detection.
- `cla_64bit`: 64-bit carry-lookahead adder implementing fast addition.
- Internally, propagate/generate signals are computed for each bit, and hierarchical carry-lookahead for all 64 bits is done explicitly.

This refactoring balances clarity, area, and speed, removing carry-select redundancy while keeping the overflow logic simple and efficient.
