Fuse the best of both solutions to create a fast, modular, and synthesis-friendly 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection. 

Fusion strategy:
- Use a hierarchical carry-lookahead adder-based subtractor for A - B = A + (~B) + 1 with optimized 16-bit CLA blocks chained with carry-lookahead, avoiding long ripple chains.
- Each 16-bit CLA block internally uses fast carry lookahead logic with group propagate/generate signals to minimize carry delay.
- Chain four 16-bit CLA blocks with a 4-bit carry-lookahead stage for the carries between blocks, reducing carry chain length significantly.
- This hierarchical CLA reduces critical path delay compared to a flat 64-bit CLA with ripple carry inside.
- Share the complemented B operand at the top level to reduce repeated inversion, reducing power.
- Keep overflow detection logic simple and clear based on sign bits of A, B, and result.
- This design blends Example 1's modularity and hierarchical subdivision with Example 2's fast CLA subtractor approach and clean overflow logic.
- The result is a scalable, low-latency subtractor suitable for high-precision arithmetic with clear and minimal overflow logic.

This fusion provides a balanced tradeoff of speed, area, and power, with a clear and reusable module hierarchy.
