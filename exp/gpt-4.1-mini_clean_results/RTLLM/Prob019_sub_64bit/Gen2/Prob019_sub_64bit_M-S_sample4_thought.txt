To simplify the 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection while maintaining functionality, we can directly use the subtraction operator in Verilog (`A - B`), which is clear and concise. Modern synthesis tools optimize subtraction effectively, so there's no need to manually implement two's complement addition.

The overflow detection logic remains the same: overflow occurs when the sign of A differs from B and the sign of the result differs from A.

This approach reduces intermediate wires and operations, making the code simpler and easier to read without changing behavior.
