To improve timing, power, and area (PPA) of the original ripple borrow 64-bit subtractor, replace the ripple chain of four 16-bit subtractors with a single, straightforward 64-bit subtraction using Verilog’s native subtraction operator. This reduces complexity, removes intermediate borrow signals, and allows synthesis tools to map the operation to optimized carry-lookahead or carry-select structures internally.

For overflow detection, keep the existing sign-bit logic since it is minimal and effective.

This approach significantly improves timing by eliminating the ripple borrow path, reduces area by avoiding multiple submodules and extra wires, and lowers power by minimizing toggling on intermediate signals.

Hence:
- Use a single 64-bit subtraction: `result = A - B;`
- Detect overflow by sign analysis: overflow = (A[63] != B[63]) && (result[63] != A[63]);

This solution is cleaner, more efficient, and synthesis-friendly while meeting the functional requirements.
