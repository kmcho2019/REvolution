Fuse the modular 16-bit segmented subtractor with borrow chaining from Example 1 and the efficient two’s complement subtraction and minimal overflow logic from Example 2, but enhance performance by replacing the ripple borrow chain with a 4-stage hierarchical carry-lookahead generator for borrow signals.

- Partition the 64-bit subtractor into four 16-bit blocks, each performing subtraction via two’s complement (A_seg - B_seg - borrow_in).
- Instead of a ripple borrow chain, implement a hierarchical borrow lookahead across 16-bit blocks using generate/propagate signals for borrow, similar to CLA principles, to reduce delay.
- This approach achieves modularity and clarity, while the borrow-lookahead improves timing and reduces power compared to pure ripple borrow.
- Carry-lookahead internal to 16-bit blocks can be straightforward due to limited bit-width and synthesis tools' optimization of subtractors.
- Overflow detection is done at the top level via sign bits of A, B, and result as before.
- This fusion balances area, power, and speed better than pure ripple or pure full CLA, is structurally clear, and uses well-understood arithmetic primitives.

The final module instantiates four 16-bit subtractors with borrow-lookahead logic that quickly resolves block borrows, assembles the final 64-bit result, and signals overflow by comparing input and result sign bits.
