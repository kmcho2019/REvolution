To optimize the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, I will switch from the ripple borrow approach to a direct subtract operator using Verilog's built-in subtraction. This removes the long borrow chain, reducing critical path delay and power consumption, and yields a more area-efficient design as synthesis tools generate optimal carry-lookahead subtractors automatically.

Overflow detection logic remains unchanged, using sign bits comparison as before.

This approach results in a compact, clear, and synthesizable module with improved performance and area efficiency over the ripple borrow version.
