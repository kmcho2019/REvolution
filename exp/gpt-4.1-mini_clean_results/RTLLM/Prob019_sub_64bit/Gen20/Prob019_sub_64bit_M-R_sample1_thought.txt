Refactor the 64-bit subtractor to use Verilog's built-in subtraction operator directly for the arithmetic operation, which lets synthesis tools optimize the subtractor internally for best performance, area, and power. This reduces the explicit manual bitwise implementation of the ripple-carry subtraction logic and carry propagation. The overflow detection logic remains explicit and unchanged, computed from the sign bits of A, B, and the result.

Key points of this refactor:
- Use a single continuous assignment: result = A - B;
- Compute overflow based on the known signed overflow conditions.
- Simplify the design by removing the explicit bitwise ripple-carry implementation, leveraging synthesis tool optimizations for subtractors.
- The design remains fully combinational and equivalent in functionality.
