Refactor the 64-bit subtractor to use a flat, single-level carry-lookahead structure instead of hierarchical 16-bit and 8-bit blocks. This reduces complexity and nesting of modules, simplifying carry computation and improving readability.

Key points:
- Perform subtraction as A + (~B) + 1 using 64-bit carry lookahead.
- Calculate propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for each bit.
- Compute all carry signals in parallel using carry-lookahead logic to reduce carry chain delay.
- Compute the result bits as sum = P ^ carry.
- Detect overflow by checking the sign bits of A, B, and result exactly as specified.
- Keep all logic combinational and use continuous assignments (`assign`), avoiding internal modules.
- This design trades some modularity for simpler flat structure and potentially faster carry calculation due to parallel carry generation.

The design is fully synthesizable, straightforward, and more concise.
