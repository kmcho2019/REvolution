Implement the 64-bit subtractor using the Verilog built-in subtraction operator for clean, concise, and synthesis-efficient code, combined with explicit overflow detection logic.

The subtraction is done in a single continuous assignment:
    result = A - B;

Overflow detection is explicitly computed by:
- Checking the sign bits of A, B, and result.
- Using the known overflow conditions for signed subtraction:
    - Positive overflow occurs when A is positive and B is negative but result is negative.
    - Negative overflow occurs when A is negative and B is positive but result is positive.

This approach relies on the synthesis tool’s optimized arithmetic operators, which tend to produce better area and performance than manual ripple-carry implementations.

This method also eliminates the need for manually managing carry bits or complex generate loops, making the design simpler and more readable.

Key points:
- Use built-in subtraction operator for efficient arithmetic.
- Compute overflow via combinational logic on sign bits.
- Entire design is combinational, no clocking or state.
