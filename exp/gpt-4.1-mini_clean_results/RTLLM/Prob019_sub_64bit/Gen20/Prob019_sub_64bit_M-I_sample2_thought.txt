To improve PPA (performance, power, area) over the original flat 64-bit ripple carry style subtraction, I will implement the subtraction using a hierarchical carry-lookahead approach by splitting the 64-bit adder into 4 blocks of 16 bits each. Each 16-bit block will use a carry-lookahead adder for internal carries, and a small 4-bit carry-lookahead unit will combine the block carries to generate the top-level carry chain.

This reduces carry propagation delay from 64 levels to approximately 16 + 4 levels, improving timing.

Power and area also improve by localizing carry logic and reducing the number of gates in the critical carry path. Each 16-bit block calculates propagate and generate signals for its bits and a block propagate and generate signal for hierarchical carry calculation.

The overflow detection logic remains the same as it depends only on the sign bits of inputs and result.

This hierarchical carry-lookahead subtractor is fully combinational, modular, and scales well for 64-bit operations.
