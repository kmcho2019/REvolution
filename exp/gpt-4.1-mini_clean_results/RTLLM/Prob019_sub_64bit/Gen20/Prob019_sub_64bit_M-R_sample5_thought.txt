Refactor the 64-bit subtractor design to implement the subtraction as a single 64-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA) instead of two 32-bit CLAs chained. This unifies the carry computation in a single module, potentially reducing carry propagation delay and simplifying hierarchical carry logic. The B operand is still inverted and added with a carry-in of 1 to achieve subtraction via two's complement.

Overflow detection logic remains the same: overflow occurs if A and B have different signs, and the result's sign differs from A's sign.

The 64-bit CLA is implemented using four 16-bit CLA blocks with hierarchical carry computation, balancing modularity and speed. The propagate and generate signals are computed per bit and grouped for fast carry calculation across 16-bit blocks. This approach also improves timing and scalability.

This refactoring reduces the complexity of chaining two large CLA blocks and may improve performance by eliminating intermediate carry dependencies between two separate CLAs.
