To optimize the original design for better PPA (performance, power, area), we can simplify the approach by leveraging Verilog's built-in subtraction operator, which modern synthesis tools handle efficiently, especially for wide datapaths. This eliminates the overhead of multiple CLA blocks and inter-block carry wiring, reducing area and possibly power.

We'll keep overflow detection logic explicit, using the well-known formula for signed overflow in subtraction: overflow occurs if the sign of A and B differ, and the result's sign differs from A's sign.

This simplification achieves:
- Better area utilization by avoiding explicit CLA logic and inter-block carry signals.
- Potentially lower power from fewer combinational gates.
- Simpler, more maintainable code with no hierarchical complexity.
- Performance depends on synthesis tool, but most tools map built-in subtraction efficiently with fast adders.

Overall, this solution is cleaner, likely more optimal in area and power, and keeps the required functional overflow detection.
