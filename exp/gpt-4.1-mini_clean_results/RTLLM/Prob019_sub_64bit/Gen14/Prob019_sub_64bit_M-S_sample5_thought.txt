Perform the 64-bit subtraction directly using Verilog's built-in arithmetic (A - B) and detect overflow by comparing the sign bits of A, B, and the result. Since Verilog handles subtraction and two's complement arithmetic natively, the explicit borrow chains and multi-level adders are unnecessary for functional correctness and overflow detection. 

Overflow in signed subtraction happens if A and B have different signs, and the sign of the result differs from A's sign. We simply compute result = A - B and assign overflow based on this logic. This approach drastically reduces complexity and code size while maintaining functionality.
