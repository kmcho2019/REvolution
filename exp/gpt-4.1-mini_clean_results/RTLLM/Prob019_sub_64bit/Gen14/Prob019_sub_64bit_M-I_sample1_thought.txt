To optimize the 64-bit subtractor for better performance, power, and area, replace the chain of four 16-bit subtractors with a single unified 64-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) configured for subtraction. This removes the 3-stage borrow ripple delay and borrow inversion logic, reducing critical path and gate count.

Perform subtraction as A + (~B) + 1 by setting cin=1. The single 64-bit CLA block will generate the result and the carry out, which is not used here but helps determine borrow if needed.

Overflow detection remains the same: overflow occurs if A and B have opposite signs and the result sign differs from A’s sign.

This approach reduces the logic replication, interconnect overhead, and borrow chain delays, improving timing, lowering dynamic power by reducing switching on intermediate borrow signals, and saving area through a single adder block.

We'll implement a single 64-bit carry-lookahead adder from scratch with generate/propagate signals computed hierarchically for better scalability and manageable complexity. This also improves timing compared to the ripple borrow chain.

Summary of improvements:
- Single 64-bit CLA for subtraction instead of four 16-bit chained subtractors.
- Direct subtraction via A + (~B) + 1 with cin=1.
- Overflow logic remains unchanged.
- Simplified module hierarchy and signal usage.

This yields a cleaner, faster, and more area- and power-efficient design.
