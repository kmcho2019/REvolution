Use a single 64-bit wide subtract operation with explicit two’s complement arithmetic in a purely combinational block without segmented chaining, simplifying the design and eliminating ripple borrow delay.

- Perform subtraction using built-in operators: result = A - B.
- Detect overflow by comparing the sign bits of inputs and output:
  * Overflow occurs when A and B have different signs and the sign of the result differs from that of A.
- Implement overflow flag combinationally to avoid extra latency.
- Use signed types internally to enhance readability but keep interface ports as unsigned wires for synthesis friendliness.
- Keep design minimal and clear for better synthesis and lower area, relying on the synthesis tool to generate optimized arithmetic.

This approach leverages native 64-bit arithmetic units and synthesizes to efficient hardware without manual borrow chains, improving performance and reducing area and power.
