To improve PPA, replace the ripple borrow 32-bit subtractors with a single 64-bit subtractor implemented using Verilog’s built-in signed subtraction operator. This reduces the critical path by leveraging optimized synthesis tool arithmetic units rather than manually constructing borrow chains, which improves timing and reduces area and power.

Overflow detection is directly performed on the signed subtraction using the sign bits of A, B, and result, as before.

This approach simplifies the code, minimizes logic levels, and utilizes synthesis tools' optimized adder/subtractor implementations, improving performance and reducing power and area compared to ripple borrow subtractors.
