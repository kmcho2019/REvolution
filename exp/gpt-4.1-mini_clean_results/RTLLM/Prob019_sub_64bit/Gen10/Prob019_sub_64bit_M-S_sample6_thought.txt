Implement the 64-bit subtractor directly using Verilog's built-in subtraction operator (A - B) for simplicity and clarity. Then detect overflow by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result according to signed subtraction overflow rules.

This approach:
- Eliminates the manual carry-lookahead logic, reducing code complexity and making the design easier to understand and maintain.
- Leverages synthesis tools' optimized subtraction circuitry.
- Retains the correct overflow detection logic based on sign bits.

This solution is fully combinational, concise, and functionally equivalent to the previous complex implementation.
