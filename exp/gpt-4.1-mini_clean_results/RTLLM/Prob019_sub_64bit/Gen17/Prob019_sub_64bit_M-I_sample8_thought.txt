To improve the 64-bit subtractor design for better performance, power, and area, I will leverage the Verilog built-in subtraction operator directly (`result = A - B`) instead of implementing an explicit ripple carry adder. This allows synthesis tools to optimize the subtractor with vendor-specific efficient arithmetic structures, reducing area and power and improving timing.

The overflow detection remains unchanged, as it is a straightforward combinational check on the sign bits of A, B, and the result.

This approach significantly reduces complexity by removing manual carry logic, reduces the critical path by relying on the tool's optimized subtractor implementation, and lowers switching activity and logic area.

Key improvements:
- Use Verilog arithmetic subtraction operator.
- Keep overflow detection logic as before.
- Make the design purely combinational and simple.
