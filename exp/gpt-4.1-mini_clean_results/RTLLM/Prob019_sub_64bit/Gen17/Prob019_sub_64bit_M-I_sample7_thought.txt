To improve timing, power, and area efficiency over the ripple borrow chain architecture, we will eliminate the segmented ripple subtractors and instead perform the subtraction directly using the Verilog built-in 64-bit arithmetic operator. This approach lets synthesis tools infer optimized carry-lookahead or carry-select subtractors, which significantly reduces the critical path and area compared to ripple borrow chains.

For overflow detection, we keep the same logic comparing sign bits of inputs and result. We explicitly cast inputs to signed 64-bit to make the intent clear and help synthesis tools optimize the arithmetic and overflow check.

This fully combinational approach minimizes net count, reduces switching on intermediate borrow signals, and leverages synthesis tools' arithmetic optimizations for better power and area.

Summary of improvements:
- Use one 64-bit subtract operator instead of segmented ripple.
- Rely on signed arithmetic for clarity and efficient synthesis.
- Keep overflow detection logic as before.
- Result is directly assigned; no extra modules or borrow signals.
