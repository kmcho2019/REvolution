Implement the 64-bit subtractor using Verilog's built-in arithmetic subtraction operator directly to perform A - B for clarity and to let synthesis tools optimize the arithmetic efficiently. To detect overflow for signed subtraction, calculate the result as signed, then check overflow by comparing the sign bits of A, B, and result. Overflow occurs if:
- A is positive and B is negative, but the result is negative (positive overflow)
- A is negative and B is positive, but the result is positive (negative overflow)

This approach leverages Verilog's native operators for subtraction and simplifies logic while allowing synthesis tools to generate an optimal subtractor circuit (e.g., carry-lookahead or other fast adders). The overflow logic is explicitly coded based on the sign bits.

This design is purely combinational, concise, easy to read, and synthesis-friendly, likely producing efficient hardware with less manual overhead.
