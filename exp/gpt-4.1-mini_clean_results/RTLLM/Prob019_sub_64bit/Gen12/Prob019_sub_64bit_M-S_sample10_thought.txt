Perform the 64-bit subtraction directly using Verilog's built-in subtraction operator, relying on native hardware subtraction to handle the borrow internally. Then, detect overflow by comparing the sign bits of A, B, and the result according to the given conditions:

- Overflow occurs if A and B have different signs and the result's sign differs from A's sign.

This approach simplifies the design by eliminating the multi-level borrow chain and custom 16-bit CLA modules, resulting in a more compact and readable module with the same functional correctness.
