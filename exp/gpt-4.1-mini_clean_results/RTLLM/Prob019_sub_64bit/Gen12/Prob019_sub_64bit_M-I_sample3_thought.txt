To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and area, I will remove the multi-stage borrow ripple chain by implementing the entire 64-bit subtraction in a single 64-bit carry lookahead adder structure. This avoids the ripple borrow delay across four 16-bit subtractors, improving timing significantly. It also reduces redundant logic and interconnect overhead, lowering power and area.

The subtraction is implemented as A + (~B) + 1, using the 64-bit CLA with a single carry-in of 1. The overflow detection remains the same based on sign bits of inputs and result.

This unified approach reduces complexity, gate count, and critical path length, resulting in improved PPA metrics while keeping functional correctness intact.
