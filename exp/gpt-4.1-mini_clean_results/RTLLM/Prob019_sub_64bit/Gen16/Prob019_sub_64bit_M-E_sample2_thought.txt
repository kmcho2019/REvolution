Implement the 64-bit subtractor using an alternative approach by splitting the 64-bit subtraction into two 32-bit stages to form a two-level subtractor. This two-stage subtractor avoids building a complex full 64-bit carry lookahead adder, potentially reducing area and simplifying timing paths while still supporting 64-bit operands. Borrow handling between stages is explicit, improving modularity.

This approach:
- Implements subtraction as two chained 32-bit subtractors with borrow.
- Each 32-bit subtractor uses basic ripple borrow logic for clarity.
- Overflow detection is done once on the final 64-bit result.
- Borrow propagation between lower and upper halves is explicit and simple.

This two-level subtractor balances clarity and structural modularity with modest performance and area tradeoffs.
