To improve the previous 64-bit subtractor with overflow detection, I will optimize the carry-lookahead adder (CLA) by implementing a hierarchical CLA structure instead of a flat 64-bit ripple carry lookahead.

The hierarchical CLA will:
- Divide the 64 bits into 8 blocks of 8 bits.
- Each 8-bit block computes its own propagate and generate signals locally and outputs a block carry-out.
- A top-level CLA computes the carries between these 8 blocks quickly using the block-level propagate/generate signals.
- This reduces the critical path significantly compared to a flat carry chain, improving maximum frequency and lowering power due to shorter carry chains.
- The sum bits are still computed with the final carries from the hierarchy.

The overflow detection logic remains unchanged.

This approach balances area and speed by breaking down the carry computation hierarchically while keeping the logic straightforward and easy to verify. It reduces both delay and switching activity on carries, which helps with power and performance.

I will provide a single module `sub_64bit` which instantiates a hierarchical `cla_64bit` based on 8-bit CLAs plus an 8-bit block carry lookahead at the top level.
