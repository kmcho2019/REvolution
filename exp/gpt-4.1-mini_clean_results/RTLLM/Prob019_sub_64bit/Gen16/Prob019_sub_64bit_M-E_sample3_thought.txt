Implement the 64-bit subtractor as A - B = A + (~B) + 1 using a parallel prefix adder structure (specifically a Kogge-Stone Adder) to efficiently compute carries in logarithmic time, significantly improving timing performance compared to ripple carry or simple CLA.

Key points:
- Use internal inversion of B inside the adder module for subtraction.
- Implement a 64-bit Kogge-Stone prefix network to generate carries efficiently.
- Compute sum bits as XOR of propagate signals and carry-in bits.
- Detect overflow by comparing the sign bits of inputs and result per the signed subtraction overflow conditions.
- Keep the design fully combinational.
- Modularize the prefix logic for clarity and reuse.

This approach drastically reduces carry computation time from linear to O(log N), improving performance, and also provides a clean structural design beneficial for high-performance, high-precision arithmetic units.
