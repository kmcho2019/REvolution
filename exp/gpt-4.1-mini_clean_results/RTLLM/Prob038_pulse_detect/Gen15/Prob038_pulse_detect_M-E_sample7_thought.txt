Implement the pulse detector using a shift register approach: store the last three samples of data_in synchronized to clk, and detect the pulse pattern "0->1->0" by comparing the contents of this shift register. The output data_out is asserted for one clock cycle when the pattern is detected at the end of the third sample (i.e., at the current cycle). This approach avoids explicit FSM states, using data history comparison instead. On reset, clear the shift register and deassert data_out.
