Implement the pulse detection using a simple shift register to track the last three samples of data_in synchronized to clk. By shifting in each new data_in sample every clock cycle, we detect the pulse pattern "0 1 0" in the last three bits. When the pattern appears, output data_out is asserted for one clock cycle. This approach avoids explicit state machines and relies on pattern matching of recent samples, making the logic straightforward and easily understandable. It also guarantees data_out is aligned with the end of the pulse pattern.
