Instead of using delayed versions of data_in for pattern detection, implement a small finite state machine (FSM) with defined states to explicitly track the pulse progress: IDLE (waiting for rising edge), HIGH (detected data_in=1), and WAIT_FOR_FALL (waiting for data_in to return to 0). This approach offers clear state transitions and makes the pulse detection logic easily extensible. When the FSM detects the sequence 0->1->0 over consecutive cycles, it asserts data_out at the cycle where data_in goes back to 0, then returns to IDLE. The reset puts the FSM in IDLE and data_out low. This explicit FSM method differs architecturally from the delay-line approach and can be easier to maintain or modify.
