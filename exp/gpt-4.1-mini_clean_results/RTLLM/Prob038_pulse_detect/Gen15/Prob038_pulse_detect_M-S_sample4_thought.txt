Use two registers to store the previous and current data_in values. Detect the pulse when data_in transitions from 0 to 1 (rising edge) and then immediately returns to 0 in the next clock cycle. Specifically, when previous is 0, current is 1, and next input is 0, indicate pulse at the last 0. This avoids a 3-bit shift register by using simple sequential comparisons, simplifying logic and maintaining functionality.
