Refactor the pulse detector into a Moore FSM where the output data_out is registered and asserted only on the cycle when the pulse completes. The FSM will have four states explicitly encoding the stages: IDLE (waiting for 0), WAIT_HIGH (waiting for 1), WAIT_LOW (waiting for the falling edge), and PULSE_END (output assertion). The data_out signal is asserted for one cycle when the FSM transitions to PULSE_END, then returns to IDLE. This structure uses a registered output and a clear state transition sequence, simplifying output timing and potentially easing timing closure.
