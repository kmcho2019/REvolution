Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states to detect the pulse pattern 0->1->0 over three cycles. The FSM starts at IDLE waiting for data_in=0, then transitions to WAIT_HIGH when data_in rises to 1, then to WAIT_LOW once data_in goes back to 0. At the transition to WAIT_LOW, we assert data_out=1 for one cycle to indicate pulse detection. If at any point the pattern breaks, the FSM resets to IDLE. This explicit FSM tracks the pattern edges precisely and asserts data_out only at the pulse end cycle.
