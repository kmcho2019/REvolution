Implement the pulse detection by storing the last two samples of data_in in a 2-bit shift register updated every clock cycle. A pulse is identified when the three consecutive samples form the pattern 0 -> 1 -> 0 over three clock cycles. This approach uses a simple shift register and combinational logic to detect the pulse without an explicit FSM, reducing complexity and latency. The output data_out is asserted for one cycle at the moment the pattern completes.
