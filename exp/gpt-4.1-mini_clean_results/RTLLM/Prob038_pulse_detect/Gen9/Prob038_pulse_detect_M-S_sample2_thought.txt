I will implement a simplified FSM using plain Verilog-2001 style without typedef or enums. The FSM will have three states encoded as 2-bit registers:

- S0: Waiting for data_in to be 0 (start of pulse)
- S1: Received 0, waiting for data_in to become 1
- S2: Received 1 after 0, waiting for data_in to go back to 0

When data_in transitions 0 → 1 → 0 over three clock cycles, at the last 0 the output data_out will be asserted high for one clock cycle. After that, the FSM returns to S0 to detect the next pulse.

The design uses synchronous reset and state updates on the positive clock edge. data_out is asserted only for one cycle on pulse detection, simplifying the logic.
