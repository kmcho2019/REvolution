Refactor the original solution by explicitly implementing a three-state finite state machine (FSM) to track the pulse stages: IDLE (waiting for 0), HIGH (data_in=1 detected), and DONE (pulse end detected). On detecting the 0->1->0 pattern across three cycles, the FSM outputs data_out=1 for one cycle at the end of the pulse. This FSM structure makes the state transitions and pulse detection clearer and easier to extend, compared to using just a shift register and combinational logic. The output is registered and synchronous to clk. This approach improves readability and debuggability while maintaining the original detection logic.
