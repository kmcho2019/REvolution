Implement pulse detection by using a simple edge detector that captures the rising and falling edges of the input data_in. The pulse pattern 0->1->0 is detected by identifying a rising edge followed by a falling edge within a sliding 3-cycle window. Use a shift register of length 3 to hold the recent data_in samples and compare them each cycle to recognize the 0-1-0 pattern ending at the current cycle. The output data_out is asserted high for one cycle at the end of the pulse (when the input goes back to 0 after being 1). This approach avoids explicit FSM states and relies purely on sequential logic and combinational detection logic using a small history buffer.
