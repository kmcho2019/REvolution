Refactor the FSM implementation by integrating the state update and output generation inside a single always block, using only synchronous reset and nonblocking assignments. The output signal is registered to assert data_out for one clock cycle exactly when the pulse (0→1→0) completes, i.e., at the falling edge of data_in after detecting the high level. This approach simplifies the design by avoiding separate combinational next-state logic and using a Moore-type output registered with the state, improving timing predictability and removing asynchronous reset complexity.
