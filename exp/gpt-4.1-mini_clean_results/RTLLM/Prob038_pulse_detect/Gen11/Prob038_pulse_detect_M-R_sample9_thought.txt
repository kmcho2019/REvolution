Refactor the design to use a Moore FSM structure with output registered synchronously on clk, instead of a Mealy output. The output data_out is asserted in the state that signifies the pulse end, which simplifies the output logic and timing since outputs change only on clock edges. This involves defining more explicit states representing each phase of the pulse detection: IDLE (waiting for 0), HIGH (detected 1), and PULSE_END (detected the falling edge completing the pulse). data_out is asserted only in PULSE_END state for one cycle. This approach uses a synchronous reset and nonblocking assignments for reliable synthesis and simulation.
