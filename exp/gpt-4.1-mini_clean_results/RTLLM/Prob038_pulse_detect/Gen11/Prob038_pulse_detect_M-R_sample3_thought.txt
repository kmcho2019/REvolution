I will refactor the design by explicitly implementing a finite state machine (FSM) with clearly defined states to detect the 0->1->0 pulse pattern. This FSM will have three states: IDLE (waiting for 0), HIGH (waiting for 1 after seeing 0), and DONE (when 0->1->0 detected). The output data_out will be asserted only in the DONE state for one cycle. I will use enumerated parameters for states and handle state transitions inside a synchronous always block. This makes the design structure more explicit and improves readability, while maintaining the original functionality.
