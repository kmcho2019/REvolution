Simplify the FSM by using only two states: IDLE and PULSE_START. In IDLE, wait for the rising edge of data_in (0 to 1). Once detected, move to PULSE_START state and wait for data_in to go back to 0, which indicates the pulse end. Assert data_out at that cycle and return to IDLE. This reduces states and logic while maintaining correct detection of 0->1->0 pulse pattern with data_out asserted at the pulse end.
