Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states to explicitly track the detection of the pulse pattern: IDLE (waiting for data_in=1), HIGH_DETECTED (data_in went from 0 to 1), LOW_WAIT (waiting for data_in to return to 0), and PULSE_END (indicate the pulse is detected). The FSM transitions through these states based on data_in, asserting data_out only in the PULSE_END state for one clock cycle before returning to IDLE. This approach makes the pulse detection logic very clear and scalable for more complex patterns.
