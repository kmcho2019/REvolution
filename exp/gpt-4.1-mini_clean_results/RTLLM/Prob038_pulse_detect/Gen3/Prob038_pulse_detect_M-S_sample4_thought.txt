Since we only need to detect a 0->1->0 pattern over three consecutive clock cycles, we can simply use a 2-bit shift register to keep track of the current and previous two input values. By shifting data_in through two registers, we have the last three samples available, and we check if they match 0-1-0 pattern. If so, assert data_out for one clock cycle. This removes the explicit state machine and extra registers, achieving the required functionality with minimal logic.
