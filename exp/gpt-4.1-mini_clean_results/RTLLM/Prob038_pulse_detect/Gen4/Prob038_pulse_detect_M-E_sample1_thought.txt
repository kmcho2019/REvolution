Instead of using a multi-state FSM, I will implement the pulse detection using edge detection and a small shift register. The idea is to sample data_in each clock and keep a 3-bit history of the last three samples. A pulse is defined as the pattern 0 -> 1 -> 0 over three consecutive cycles. 

By shifting data_in through a 3-bit register, we can detect the pattern "010" in this history. When this pattern appears, data_out is asserted high for one cycle at the end of the third cycle, then goes low until another pulse is detected.

This approach uses minimal state logic, no FSM, and relies on simple shift register and combinational pattern matching. It is easy to understand, potentially very fast and resource-light.

Reset clears the shift register and output. The pulse detection is synchronous to clk, and the output data_out is a one-cycle pulse indicating the detected pulse pattern.
