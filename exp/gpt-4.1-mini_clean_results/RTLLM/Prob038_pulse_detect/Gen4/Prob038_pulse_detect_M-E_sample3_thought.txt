Instead of using a multi-state FSM explicitly tracking each pulse edge, use a shift register of length 3 to capture the last three samples of data_in on every clock. The 3-bit register forms a sliding window over data_in history. When the shift register matches the bit pattern "010" (which represents a pulse of 0->1->0 in 3 consecutive clock cycles), assert data_out for exactly one cycle. This method leverages simple shift-register and pattern matching logic to detect pulses without a formal FSM, reducing state complexity and improving clarity.
