The previous design is functionally correct and already minimal with a two-state FSM and a delayed data_in register. To optimize power and potentially area slightly, I will add clock gating to the data_in sampling register: data_in_d will only be updated when data_in is high or when state is HIGH (actively detecting a pulse), which reduces unnecessary toggling on data_in_d when data_in is low and no pulse detection is ongoing. This conditional sampling reduces dynamic power while preserving functionality and timing. The FSM and output logic are kept unchanged for clarity and minimal logic. This approach keeps the design lean, improves power efficiency, and minimally impacts timing and area.
