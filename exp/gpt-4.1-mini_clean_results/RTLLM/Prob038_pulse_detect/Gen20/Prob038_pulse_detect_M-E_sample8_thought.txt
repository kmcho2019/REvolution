Use a three-state FSM that explicitly tracks the pulse sequence: IDLE (waiting for rising edge), HIGH (data_in=1), and DONE (pulse detected). The FSM transitions from IDLE to HIGH on data_in=1, then to DONE on data_in=0 following HIGH, indicating the end of a pulse. The output data_out is asserted only in the DONE state for one cycle. This explicit state representation simplifies the pulse detection logic and ensures data_out pulses precisely at the end of each 0-1-0 pattern, matching the requirement. The data_in signal is directly sampled without a delayed register to minimize complexity.
