Instead of a classic FSM tracking states, use a shift register of length 3 to capture the last three cycles of data_in. At every clock cycle, shift in the current data_in bit. Then, check if the 3-bit window matches the pattern 0->1->0 (i.e., 3-bit pattern is 3'b010). If yes, assert data_out=1 for that cycle to indicate the detected pulse at the end of the sequence. This method simplifies the design by relying on a sliding window and direct pattern comparison, requiring minimal state and ensuring accurate pulse detection aligned with the problem statement.
