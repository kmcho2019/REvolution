To fuse the best ideas from both examples, we keep the compact 2-bit binary state encoding from Example 1 for minimal area and reduced switching, and adopt the clean separation of combinational next-state logic and sequential state update with nonblocking assignments for robust synchronous design. We generate a Mealy-type output combinationally based on the current state and input to immediately detect the pulse end without registering output, minimizing latency and logic. Additionally, to reduce power and glitches, we will register the output data_out with a single flip-flop, thus stabilizing output transitions and avoiding glitches caused by combinational logic transitions on data_out. This combines the area and timing efficiency of binary encoding, the clarity and correctness of the FSM, and power/stability improvements from registering the output. The asynchronous active-low reset initializes state and data_out. The state machine detects the pattern 0→1→0 and asserts data_out only at the final cycle of the pulse as requested.
