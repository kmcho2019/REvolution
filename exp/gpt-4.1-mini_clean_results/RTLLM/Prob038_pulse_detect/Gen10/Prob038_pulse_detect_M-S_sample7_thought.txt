I will simplify the design by using a 2-bit state register with fewer states: IDLE (waiting for 0), HIGH (seen 0 then 1), and then detect the falling edge from 1 to 0 directly to assert data_out. Instead of a multi-step next_state logic, I can track data_in transitions directly and generate data_out on detecting the complete 0→1→0 pattern.
