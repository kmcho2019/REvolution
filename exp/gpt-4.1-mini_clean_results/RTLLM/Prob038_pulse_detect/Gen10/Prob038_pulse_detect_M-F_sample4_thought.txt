To improve code clarity and directly capture the three consecutive samples required for pulse detection, replace the 2-bit shift register with a 3-bit shift register that holds the last three samples of data_in explicitly. This makes it straightforward to check the pattern 0->1->0 by examining the bits of the 3-bit register. The output data_out is asserted for one clock cycle when the pattern is detected at the current clock edge. This approach retains the original design’s performance and simplicity while enhancing readability and maintainability.
