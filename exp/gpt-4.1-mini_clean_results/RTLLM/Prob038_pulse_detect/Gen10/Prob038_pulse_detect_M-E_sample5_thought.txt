Use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states to explicitly track the pulse pattern "0 -> 1 -> 0". The FSM states are: IDLE (waiting for data_in to be 0), WAIT_RISE (waiting for data_in to rise to 1), WAIT_FALL (waiting for data_in to fall back to 0), and PULSE_DETECTED (indicating the pulse is complete). At the end of the pulse (when transitioning back to IDLE after detecting the final 0), assert data_out for one cycle to flag pulse detection. This FSM approach is explicit, clear, and easy to extend for more complex pulse patterns.
