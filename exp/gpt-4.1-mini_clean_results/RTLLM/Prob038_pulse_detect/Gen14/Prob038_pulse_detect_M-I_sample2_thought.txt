The original design correctly detects the pulse pattern with a simple 3-state FSM and asserts data_out for one clock cycle. However, data_out is assigned both combinationally and sequentially, causing potential redundant toggling and slightly increased power consumption. To optimize for power and timing, I will move all data_out assignments exclusively into the sequential block.

This ensures that data_out only changes on clock edges, reducing glitches and unnecessary switching. I will also simplify the combinational logic to only compute next_state, while data_out depends solely on the current FSM state.

This approach maintains functional correctness while improving power and timing by minimizing combinational toggling on the output. The FSM remains minimal and clearly coded, with a synchronous reset and registered outputs for better timing closure.
