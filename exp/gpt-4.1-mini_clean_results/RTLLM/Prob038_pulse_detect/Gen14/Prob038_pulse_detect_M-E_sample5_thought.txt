Use a finite state machine (FSM) to track the pulse pattern explicitly. Define states corresponding to waiting for a rising edge (0 to 1), then waiting for a falling edge back to 0. Once the FSM observes the full "0->1->0" transition sequence on data_in over consecutive cycles, it asserts data_out for one cycle, signaling the pulse detection. This FSM approach uses an explicit state encoding, providing clear, sequential logic and easy extensibility.
