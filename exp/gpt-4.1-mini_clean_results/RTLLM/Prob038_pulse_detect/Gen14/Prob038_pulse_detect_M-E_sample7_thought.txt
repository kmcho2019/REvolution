Implement pulse detection using a shift register of length 3 to sample the last three values of data_in on each clock cycle. A pulse "0→1→0" over three consecutive cycles corresponds to the pattern 3'b010 in the shift register. When the pattern is detected at the end of the cycle, assert data_out for one cycle and then clear it until the next pulse appears. This approach uses a simple register and combinational comparison, avoiding FSM state complexity and improving clarity and minimal logic usage.
