Implement pulse detection by storing the previous two samples of data_in in shift registers. When the sampled pattern over three consecutive clock cycles is "0 1 0", a pulse is detected. The output data_out is asserted high for exactly one cycle at the clock when the last 0 in the pattern arrives, then returns to zero. Use synchronous reset. This approach relies on simple sample history comparison, avoiding a state machine, and ensures pulse detection from the input sequence efficiently.
