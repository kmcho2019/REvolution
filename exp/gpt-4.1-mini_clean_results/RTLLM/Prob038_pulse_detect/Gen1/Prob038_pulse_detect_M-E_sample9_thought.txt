Implement pulse detection by storing the previous two samples of data_in using shift registers. A pulse 0->1->0 across three consecutive cycles means the three samples are: previous=0, current=1, next=0. By sampling data_in every clock, we keep track of data_in three cycles ago, one cycle ago, and the current. At each clock cycle, when the triple pattern "0,1,0" appears, assert data_out=1 for that cycle. Otherwise, data_out=0. This approach avoids a formal FSM, using data sampling and pattern detection instead.
