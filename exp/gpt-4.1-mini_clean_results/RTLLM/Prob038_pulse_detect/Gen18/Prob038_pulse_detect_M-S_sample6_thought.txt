Simplify the pulse detection by using a single register to hold the previous value of data_in to detect the rising and falling edges directly. When a rising edge (0->1) is detected, set a flag indicating a pulse start. When a falling edge (1->0) follows and the flag is set, assert data_out for one cycle and clear the flag. This approach uses minimal state (just a flag) and edge detection with one register, reducing complexity while maintaining correct pulse detection.
