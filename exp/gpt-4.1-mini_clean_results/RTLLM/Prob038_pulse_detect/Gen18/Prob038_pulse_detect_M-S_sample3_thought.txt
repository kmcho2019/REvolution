The pulse "0->1->0" over three cycles can be detected by keeping track of the two previous data_in samples using a simple 2-bit shift register. At each clock cycle, we shift in the new data_in bit, and when the stored pattern is '01' and the current data_in is '0', it means the 3-cycle pulse just ended, so output data_out=1 for one cycle. This method requires only one small shift register and a simple combinational check, minimizing logic and state complexity while maintaining correct detection.
