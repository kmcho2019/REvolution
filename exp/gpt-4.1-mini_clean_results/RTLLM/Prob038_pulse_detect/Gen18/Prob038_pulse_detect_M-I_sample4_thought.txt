The original design efficiently uses a 2-bit shift register and simple combinational logic to detect the pulse pattern 0->1->0 over three cycles. To optimize for PPA:

- Performance: The critical path is already minimal. Using non-blocking assignments and simple synchronous logic is suitable.
- Power: The circuit toggles every clock cycle due to continuous shifting. Introducing a clock enable to update the shift register only when data_in changes reduces unnecessary toggling and thus power.
- Area: The logic is minimal already; removing redundant signals or logic will not yield significant gains.

Improvement strategy: Add a clock enable signal that updates the shift register only when data_in differs from the last sample stored (shift[0]). This avoids unnecessary shifts and toggling in shift registers and output logic, reducing dynamic power. The pulse detection remains the same.

This modification keeps performance similar or slightly better due to reduced toggling, lowers power consumption, and keeps the area almost unchanged.

The interface remains the same for compatibility.
