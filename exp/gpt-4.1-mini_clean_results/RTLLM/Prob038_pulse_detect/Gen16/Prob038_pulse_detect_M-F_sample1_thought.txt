Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with three states to detect the pulse pattern 0→1→0 over three cycles. The states will be:
- IDLE: waiting for data_in to be 1 while currently 0
- WAIT_FALL: data_in was 1, waiting for it to return to 0 in the next cycle
- PULSE_DETECTED: data_in transitioned back to 0 after being 1, indicating a pulse completion.

On reset, the FSM returns to IDLE and data_out is cleared. When the FSM reaches the PULSE_DETECTED state, data_out is asserted high for one cycle, then the FSM returns to IDLE to await the next pulse. This ensures data_out is a one-cycle pulse signaling the completion of the pattern "0→1→0".
