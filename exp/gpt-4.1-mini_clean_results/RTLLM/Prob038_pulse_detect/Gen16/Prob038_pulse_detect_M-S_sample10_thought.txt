Simplify the pulse detection logic by directly comparing the current and previous values of `data_in`. Keep a register to store the previous `data_in` value, then detect the pulse as a 0→1→0 sequence over three cycles by checking when `data_in` is 0, the previous was 1, and the one before that was 0. Output `data_out` as 1 for one cycle at the end of this pattern. This approach removes the need for an explicit state machine, reducing complexity while maintaining functionality.
