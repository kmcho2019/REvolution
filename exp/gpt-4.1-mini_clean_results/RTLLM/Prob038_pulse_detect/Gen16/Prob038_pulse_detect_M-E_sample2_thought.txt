Implement the pulse detection using a simple finite state machine (FSM) with four states representing the expected sequence of data_in values: waiting for 0, then 1, then 0 again to complete the pulse. Transition through states only on matching data_in values. When the FSM reaches the final state confirming the pulse (0->1->0), assert data_out for one clock cycle and then reset to the initial state. This method explicitly tracks pulse progress step-by-step rather than using pattern matching, ensuring robustness to noise or glitches and clear state-based design.
