Implement a pulse detector using a simple shift register to track the last three values of `data_in` sampled at each clock cycle. The pulse pattern is 0 → 1 → 0 over three cycles. By shifting in the current data_in bit every clock and comparing the 3-bit pattern to `3'b010`, we can detect the pulse without a formal FSM. At the cycle when the pattern matches, assert `data_out` for one clock cycle. This approach uses minimal state, no explicit FSM, and relies on pattern matching in a small shift register for simplicity and clarity.
