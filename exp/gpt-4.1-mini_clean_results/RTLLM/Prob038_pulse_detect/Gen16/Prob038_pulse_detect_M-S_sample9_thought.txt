Simplify the pulse detection by using a single-bit previous state register to detect the rising and falling edges directly without a state machine. On every clock, compare the current input with the previous sampled input to detect the edges. The pulse (0->1->0) is detected when a falling edge follows a rising edge immediately. At the falling edge, assert data_out for one cycle and then de-assert until the next pulse. This reduces complexity by eliminating explicit FSM states and uses minimal registers.
