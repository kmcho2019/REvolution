Use a shift register of length 3 to continuously track the last three samples of data_in synchronized to clk. Each clock cycle, shift in the current data_in bit. A pulse is detected exactly when the 3-bit register matches the pattern "010" (meaning data_in was 0, then 1, then 0 in three consecutive cycles). On that cycle, assert data_out for one clock cycle to indicate pulse detection.

This method simplifies state handling by leveraging a sliding window of input samples instead of an explicit FSM. It is easy to implement, robust, glitch-free, and outputs data_out exactly at the cycle when the pulse ends (the third bit of the pulse). Reset clears the shift register and output.

This approach also naturally handles overlapping pulses if they occur since each window of three cycles is checked independently.
