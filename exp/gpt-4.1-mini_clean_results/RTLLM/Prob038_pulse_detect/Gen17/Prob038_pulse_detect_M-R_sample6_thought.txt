Refactor the pulse detection FSM to a Moore machine structure with explicit state registers for each step of the pulse detection (IDLE, HIGH, LOW). Implement all state transitions and output assignments inside a synchronous always block triggered on clk and rst_n. The output data_out is a registered output asserted only in the LOW state that follows a complete pulse detection. This approach registers the output, eliminating combinational glitches and maintaining clear timing alignment between the pulse event and data_out signal.
