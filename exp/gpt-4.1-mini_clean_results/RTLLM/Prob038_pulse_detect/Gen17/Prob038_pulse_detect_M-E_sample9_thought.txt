Implement pulse detection using a shift register of three sequential samples of data_in to detect the exact pattern '0->1->0'. On every clock cycle, shift data_in into the shift register and check if the pattern matches 3 bits: previous two samples and the current input. When the pattern '010' is detected in the last three samples, assert data_out for one cycle. This approach avoids explicit FSM and simplifies logic by pattern matching on a shift register.
