Simplify the pulse detection FSM by using only three states with a single always block to handle both state transitions and output. Use Moore FSM style where output is asserted only in the final state indicating pulse end, ensuring synchronous output with minimal logic. The states are IDLE (waiting for rising edge), HIGH (data_in=1), and PULSE_END (indicate pulse complete, data_in=0 after HIGH). This reduces combinational logic blocks and keeps the design straightforward and easy to understand.
