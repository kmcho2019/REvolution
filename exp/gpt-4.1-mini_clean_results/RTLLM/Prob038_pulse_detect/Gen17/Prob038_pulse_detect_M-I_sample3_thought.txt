The original solution efficiently detects the 0->1->0 pulse pattern with minimal states and simple edge detection, which is already quite optimal. To improve power, we can reduce unnecessary toggling by updating the data_in_d register only when data_in changes, using a clock enable technique, thereby potentially lowering switching activity. Also, convert the asynchronous reset to a synchronous reset for better synthesis and timing optimization. 

In addition, we can simplify the FSM to use one register instead of two bits since only two states exist, further reducing area slightly. The data_out generation remains combinational based on falling edge detection from stored data_in_d. Overall, these changes aim for slight power and area improvement without affecting functionality or timing.
