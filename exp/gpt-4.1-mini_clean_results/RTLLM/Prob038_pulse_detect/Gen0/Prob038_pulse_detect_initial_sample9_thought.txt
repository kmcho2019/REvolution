Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with four states to detect the 0->1->0 pulse on data_in. 
States can be: IDLE (waiting for 0), WAIT_HIGH (detected 0, waiting for 1), WAIT_LOW (detected 1 after 0, waiting for 0), PULSE_DETECTED (pulse found, output data_out=1 for one cycle).
On reset, go to IDLE and clear data_out. On clock edge, transition states based on data_in. Output data_out=1 only in the PULSE_DETECTED state for one cycle.
