Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with states representing the sequence of input bits 0->1->0. The states can be: IDLE (waiting for first 0), WAIT_1 (detected first 0, waiting for 1), WAIT_0 (detected 1, waiting for 0). When the FSM detects the sequence 0->1->0 on consecutive clock cycles, output data_out=1 for that cycle and then go back to IDLE to detect new pulses. data_out remains 0 otherwise. On reset, go to IDLE and data_out=0.
