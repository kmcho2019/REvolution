Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with four states to detect the 0->1->0 pulse pattern on data_in over three cycles:
- IDLE: waiting for data_in to go high from 0
- HIGH_DETECTED: data_in has gone 0->1
- LOW_DETECTED: data_in has gone 0->1->0 (a pulse detected over three cycles)
- In the clock cycle where the pulse completes (3rd cycle of the pattern), set data_out to 1, else 0.
On reset, initialize to IDLE and clear data_out.
