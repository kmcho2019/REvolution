Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with states representing the detection of each edge of the pulse: IDLE (waiting for 0 to 1), HIGH (data_in = 1), WAIT_ZERO (waiting for final 0). When a 0->1->0 sequence is detected over 3 cycles, output data_out = 1 in the last cycle, then return to IDLE. Reset initializes state and output to zero.
