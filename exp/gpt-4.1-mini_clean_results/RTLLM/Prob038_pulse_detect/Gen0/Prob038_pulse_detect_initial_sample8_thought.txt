Use a finite state machine with states corresponding to the pulse detection sequence: IDLE (waiting for data_in=1), HIGH (data_in=1), LOW (data_in=0 after high). When the input sequence 0->1->0 is detected, output data_out=1 for that clock cycle, then go back to IDLE. Use a synchronous process with asynchronous reset, and output data_out=1 only for the cycle the pulse ends.
