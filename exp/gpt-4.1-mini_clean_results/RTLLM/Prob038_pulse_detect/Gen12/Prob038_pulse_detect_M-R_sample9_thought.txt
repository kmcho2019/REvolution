Refactor the FSM as a Moore machine using a registered output signal. The state machine will have four states to explicitly represent each phase of the pulse detection: IDLE (waiting for rising edge), HIGH (data_in=1), LOW_DETECT (first low after high), and PULSE_END (pulse detected). The output data_out is registered and asserted for exactly one clock cycle at the end of the pulse, controlled by the state. The state transitions and output generation are handled in a single synchronous always block, simplifying timing and avoiding combinational output logic. This approach improves output stability at the cost of one cycle latency for the data_out signal.
