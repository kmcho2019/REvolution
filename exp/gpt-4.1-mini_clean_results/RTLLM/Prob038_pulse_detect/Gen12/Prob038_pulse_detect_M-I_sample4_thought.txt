To ensure full Verilog-2001 compatibility (no SystemVerilog features like typedef or enum), I will replace the enum state definitions with localparams for states and use a reg variable for state storage. The FSM will have three states: IDLE (waiting for a leading 0), WAIT_HIGH (waiting for 1), and WAIT_LOW (waiting for the falling edge to complete the pulse). The output data_out will be asserted for exactly one clock cycle when the 0->1->0 pattern is detected. The FSM transitions will be handled in a synchronous always block triggered by clk and rst_n, and next state logic will be combinational. This ensures synthesis compatibility and clear, simple FSM design.
