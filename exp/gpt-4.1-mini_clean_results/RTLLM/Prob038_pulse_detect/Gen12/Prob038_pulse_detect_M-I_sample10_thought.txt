To optimize the original FSM for better power and timing without sacrificing correctness, I will reduce the number of states and simplify state transitions by directly detecting edges on data_in using a registered previous sample. This approach eliminates multi-state FSM complexity and reduces combinational logic depth. The module uses a register to hold the previous data_in value and asserts data_out only when detecting the falling edge after a rising edge (i.e., a "0->1->0" pulse).

This edge-detection approach decreases toggling in the FSM and simplifies logic, improving timing and reducing power due to fewer transitions and smaller logic. The output is registered and synchronous to the clock for stable timing, and reset initializes all registers properly. This change is expected to slightly reduce area as well.
