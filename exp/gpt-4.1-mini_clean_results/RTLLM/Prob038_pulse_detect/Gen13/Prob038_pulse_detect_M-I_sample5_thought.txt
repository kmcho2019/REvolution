The given solution is already simple and efficient, using a 3-bit shift register to detect the pulse pattern "0->1->0" and asserting data_out in the cycle this pattern completes. However, we can optimize the design slightly in terms of power by minimizing toggling on data_out. Currently, data_out toggles to 0 every clock cycle except the pulse cycle. To reduce switching, we can register data_out only when the pulse is detected, otherwise hold its value low explicitly. Also, we can separate the shift register update and data_out assignment to improve timing. This doesn't change functionality but slightly reduces switching activity on data_out, which can lead to marginal power savings. Area and performance are already optimal given the simplicity.
