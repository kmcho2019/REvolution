I will implement a simple 3-state finite state machine (FSM) that explicitly tracks the sequence "0 -> 1 -> 0" over exactly three clock cycles on data_in. The FSM states represent waiting for the rising edge (state IDLE), detecting the high level (state HIGH), and detecting the falling edge (state LOW). Upon completing this 3-cycle pulse pattern, data_out is asserted for one clock cycle.

This design uses a compact FSM with well-defined transitions, making the detection explicit and easy to follow. The output is asserted only at the last cycle of the pulse. This approach avoids flags and edge-detection registers by encoding the detection logic in FSM states, ensuring synchronous and glitch-free pulse detection.

Reset initializes the FSM to the IDLE state and clears data_out.
