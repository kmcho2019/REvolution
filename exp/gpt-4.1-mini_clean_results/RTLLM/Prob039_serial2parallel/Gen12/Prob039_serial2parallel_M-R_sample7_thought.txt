Refactor the design by separating the shift register and counter logic into two always blocks to improve clarity and modularity. 

- Use an always block triggered on the clock for the shift register that shifts data only when `din_valid` is high, placing the incoming serial bit at the MSB.
- Use a separate always block for the counter, which increments on each valid input and resets when it reaches 8.
- Generate `dout_valid` combinationally by checking if the counter is at 8, synchronized to the clock by registering the output and valid signals in a third always block.
- Update `dout_parallel` only when 8 bits have been received, capturing the shift register content at that point.
- This separation increases readability and eases debug and verification.

This approach also enables easier insertion of additional control or handshake signals in the future without merging too many functionalities into one always block.
