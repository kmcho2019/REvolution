Implement the serial-to-parallel converter using a single always block triggered on the clock edge with synchronous reset, incorporating both the shift register and the counter in one block to simplify control logic.

The shift register loads the incoming serial bit at the MSB position and shifts previous bits down to LSB on each valid input. A 4-bit counter increments on each valid input and resets after reaching 8.

When the counter reaches 8 (i.e., after 8 valid bits received), the parallel output register is loaded from the shift register and dout_valid is asserted for exactly one cycle.

This approach reduces the number of always blocks and uses a single registered output to hold parallel data, emphasizing simplicity and resource efficiency. The counter counts from 0 to 7, outputting valid on the 8th bit.

Non-blocking assignments maintain synchronous behavior and data coherence.
