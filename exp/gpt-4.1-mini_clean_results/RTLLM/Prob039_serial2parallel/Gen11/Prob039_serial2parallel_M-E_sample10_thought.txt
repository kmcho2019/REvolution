Use a simple shift-left approach with an 8-bit shift register that shifts in serial data at LSB side when din_valid is asserted, placing the newest bit at the least significant bit. To meet the MSB-to-LSB order requirement in output, the bits must be reversed before output. So internally, we accumulate bits LSB-first (shifting left), then after receiving 8 bits, output the reversed bits in dout_parallel.

Maintain a 4-bit counter to count the number of valid input bits received. When the count reaches 8, assert dout_valid for one cycle and output the reversed shift register content as dout_parallel.

This approach differs from Example 1 by shifting left and then reversing bits at output, rather than shifting right and inserting at MSB. It separates input data accumulation from output formatting, making the design conceptually different.

All registers are synchronously reset with active low rst_n.
