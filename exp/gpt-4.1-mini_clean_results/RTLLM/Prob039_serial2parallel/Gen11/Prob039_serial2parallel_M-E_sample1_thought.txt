Use a parallel load shift register approach: On each valid serial input, shift the current register contents right by one bit and insert the new serial bit at the MSB position. This way, the first received bit will end up at dout_parallel[7] (MSB), and the last received bit at dout_parallel[0] (LSB), matching the MSB-first requirement. Count the number of valid serial inputs with a 4-bit counter. When the count reaches 8, assert dout_valid for one clock cycle along with the parallel data output, then reset the counter and shift register for the next byte. Use synchronous reset, and update signals at the rising clock edge.
