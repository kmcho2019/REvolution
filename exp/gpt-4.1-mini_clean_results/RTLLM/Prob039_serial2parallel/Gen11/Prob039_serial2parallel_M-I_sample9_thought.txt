To optimize for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area) while maintaining the original functionality:

- Combine the shift register and output register into a single 8-bit register to save flip-flops and simplify logic.
- Update the shift register only when din_valid is high.
- When 8 bits are received (counter == 7), assert dout_valid for one cycle and output the content of the shift register.
- Use a synchronous reset throughout.
- Remove redundant recombination of bits when outputting; since the shift register already stores data MSB-first, dout_parallel can be directly assigned from it.
- This reduces logic complexity, area (by saving an extra 8-bit register), and switching activity by limiting updates to only din_valid cycles.
- Maintain a 4-bit counter counting from 0 to 7, resetting after 8 bits received.
- The design remains fully synchronous with non-blocking assignments and clear separation of logic.

This approach improves area and potentially power with a minor logic simplification, preserving timing and correctness.
