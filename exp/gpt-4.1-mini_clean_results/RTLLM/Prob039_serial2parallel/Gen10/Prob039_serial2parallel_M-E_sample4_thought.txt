Implement the serial-to-parallel converter using a shift register approach, shifting the serial input bit into the least significant bit of an 8-bit register on each valid data input. The register shifts left by one bit every time a new bit is valid, with the incoming bit placed at LSB. After receiving 8 valid bits (tracked by a 4-bit counter), the entire 8-bit register holds the parallel data with the first received bit at the MSB and the last received bit at the LSB, matching the MSB-first requirement. Once 8 bits are collected, assert dout_valid for one clock cycle, then reset the counter to prepare for the next byte. The design uses synchronous reset and all signals update on rising clock edges.
