Use a single always block triggered on the rising clock edge with synchronous reset. On each valid serial input, shift the received bit into an 8-bit register from MSB to LSB by shifting left and inserting the new bit at LSB. Maintain a 4-bit counter incremented only on valid inputs. When the counter reaches 7 (indicating 8 bits received), output the parallel data and assert dout_valid for one clock cycle. Otherwise, dout_valid is 0. This keeps the design simple and meets the specification.
