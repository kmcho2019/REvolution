To match the requirement that the received serial bits are placed in dout_parallel from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB), the shift register should shift left, inserting new bits at LSB. This means on each valid din_serial, shift the register left by one and place din_serial at bit 0. After receiving 8 bits (when counter reaches 7), output the register and set dout_valid for one cycle. This approach respects the required bit order and allows the first serial bit received to be at MSB (bit 7) and the last at LSB (bit 0). Also, keep all logic in a single synchronous always block with synchronous reset for clear design and better PPA.
