Use an 8-bit shift register that shifts data in from the MSB side on each valid input, placing the new bit at the MSB position so the first bit received ends up in dout_parallel[7]. Maintain a 4-bit counter that increments with each valid input bit. When the counter reaches 8, output the contents of the shift register as dout_parallel and assert dout_valid for one cycle, then reset the counter and shift register to prepare for the next frame. All operations occur on the rising clock edge with synchronous active-low reset. This approach ensures MSB-first loading by shifting right and inserting the new bit at the MSB, different from the common left-shift approach.
