Simplify the design by using a shift register that shifts in the serial input bit from MSB to LSB on each valid input. Use a 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 7 to track received bits. When the counter reaches 7 (meaning 8 bits received), output the shift register as parallel data and pulse dout_valid for one cycle. All registers update synchronously on the clock's rising edge with an active-low reset. This approach avoids redundant assignments and keeps the logic clean and straightforward.
