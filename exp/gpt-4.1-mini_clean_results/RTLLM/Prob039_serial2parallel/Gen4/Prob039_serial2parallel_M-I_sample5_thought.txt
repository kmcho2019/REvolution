To fix the timing issue and ensure the valid output pulse occurs correctly synchronized with the parallel data output, separate the process of capturing the full 8-bit word and asserting the valid signal from the data shifting and counting. Instead of a data_ready flag set combinationally in the same cycle as counting, introduce a registered "full_data" signal that is set when cnt reaches 7 (i.e., after the 8th bit is shifted in). At this point, store the parallel data in a register and assert dout_valid for exactly one clock cycle. The counter and shift register are updated only when din_valid is high, and after outputting data, the counter resets to zero for next 8-bit collection.

This approach avoids overwriting shift register data before it can be latched out and ensures a clean, single-cycle dout_valid pulse that properly signals valid output. The design remains synchronous, minimal, and clear for synthesis and timing closure.
