Use a shift register that shifts bits in from the MSB side on each valid serial input bit. Specifically, on each clock with din_valid high, shift the current 8-bit register left by one position and insert the new serial bit at LSB. This way, the first received bit ends up in the MSB (bit 7) after 8 shifts. Maintain a 4-bit counter that counts how many bits have been received. When the counter reaches 8, output the shift register on dout_parallel and assert dout_valid for one cycle. Reset the counter after output. Use synchronous reset to initialize signals. This approach naturally aligns the serial bits MSB-first without reversing bit order at output.
