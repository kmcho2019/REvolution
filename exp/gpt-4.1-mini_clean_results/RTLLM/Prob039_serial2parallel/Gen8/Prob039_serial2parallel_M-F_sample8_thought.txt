To address the feedback, I will introduce an internal wire to hold the concatenated value `{din_serial, shift_reg[7:1]}`. This avoids recomputing the same concatenation twice in the same clock cycle, which can marginally improve synthesis optimization by sharing logic.

The logic of shifting in the new bit at the MSB remains unchanged, ensuring that after receiving 8 bits, the bits are aligned with the first received bit at MSB and the last at LSB.

I will keep the rest of the design intact: a 4-bit counter increments only on `din_valid`, and when it reaches 7, the output parallel data and valid signal are asserted for one cycle. The reset is active low synchronous reset, clearing all registers.

This micro-optimization improves area and timing slightly without changing the functional behavior or increasing complexity.
