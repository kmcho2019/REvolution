Instead of a traditional shift register, use an 8-bit register indexed by a 4-bit counter to directly place each incoming serial bit into its corresponding position from MSB to LSB. On each valid input, assign din_serial to dout_parallel at the bit position (7 - bit_count). Increment the counter, and when 8 bits have been collected (counter equals 7), assert dout_valid for one clock cycle. This approach avoids shifting entirely, directly mapping incoming bits to their final position, simplifying timing and making the design transparent.
