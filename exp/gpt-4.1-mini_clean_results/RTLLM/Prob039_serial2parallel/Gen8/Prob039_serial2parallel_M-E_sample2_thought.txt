Instead of using a shift register, this design accumulates the serial bits directly into an 8-bit register by shifting the existing bits left and inserting the new serial bit at the least significant position on each valid clock cycle. A 4-bit counter counts the number of bits received, and when 8 bits are accumulated, the parallel output and valid signal are updated. The dout_valid signal is asserted only for one clock cycle immediately after the 8th bit is received. Reset logic asynchronously clears the registers for immediate reset. This approach directly forms the parallel data without storing intermediate shifted versions, reducing combinational logic on each clock edge.
