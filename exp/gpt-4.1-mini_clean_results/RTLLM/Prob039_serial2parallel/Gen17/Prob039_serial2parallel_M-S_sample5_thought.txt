Use a simple shift register that shifts bits left on each valid input, inserting the new serial bit at the least significant bit (LSB). This naturally places the first received bit at the MSB after 8 shifts, matching the problem requirement to have bits sequentially from MSB to LSB. Maintain a 4-bit counter to count valid inputs. When the count reaches 8, output the collected 8-bit data and assert the valid output signal for one clock cycle. Then reset the counter and shift register for the next input sequence.
