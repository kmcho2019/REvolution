Use a 4-bit counter as specified to count from 0 to 7, totaling 8 bits received. To meet the requirement that the earliest serial input bit is placed in the most significant bit of the output, shift the incoming bits into the shift register from the left (MSB) side. That is, on each din_valid clock cycle, shift the existing bits to the right and place the new din_serial bit into the MSB position. This way, the first bit received ends up in the MSB and the last bit received in the LSB. When the counter reaches 7 (indicating 8 bits collected), output the parallel data and pulse dout_valid high for one clock cycle. Use synchronous reset and clear dout_valid otherwise. This approach aligns perfectly with the specification and fixes the bit ordering problem, counter width, and ensures proper timing of dout_valid signal.
