Use a single always block for both the counter and shift register since the design is straightforward. On each clock when din_valid is high, shift din_serial into the MSB of an 8-bit shift register shifting existing bits right. Increment the 4-bit counter. When the counter reaches 7, output the parallel data and assert dout_valid for one clock cycle. Reset logic clears counter, shift register, and dout_valid. This eliminates the explicit FSM and separates control, simplifying the design while maintaining functionality.
