3. Reset the shift_reg to zero after outputting data to clear old data and avoid any unintended holdover.
4. Use a synchronous reset only (i.e., no asynchronous reset) for simpler timing and synthesis. This depends on project requirements but usually is preferred.
5. Add comments and keep the code clean and readable.
6. Keep non-blocking assignments and synchronous design style intact.
7. Use minimal logic and avoid redundant signals.
