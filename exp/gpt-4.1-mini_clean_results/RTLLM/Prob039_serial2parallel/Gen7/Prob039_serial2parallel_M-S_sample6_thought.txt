Use a shift register that shifts in the serial bit at the MSB side on each valid input. Maintain a 4-bit counter counting from 0 to 7 for the received bits. When the counter reaches 7 (meaning 8 bits received), latch the current shift register into dout_parallel and assert dout_valid for one clock cycle.
