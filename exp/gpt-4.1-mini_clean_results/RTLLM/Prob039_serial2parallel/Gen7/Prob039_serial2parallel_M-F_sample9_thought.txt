To ensure the first received serial bit goes to the most significant bit of the parallel output, we need to shift the register right on each new bit: shift_reg <= {din_serial, shift_reg[7:1]}; This puts the newest bit at MSB, pushing previous bits toward LSB. Counting from 0 to 7 bits collected, when cnt reaches 7, dout_parallel is assigned the completed shift_reg, and dout_valid is asserted for one clock cycle. The counter and valid signals reset on rst_n. This approach correctly orders the bits and meets the design requirements.
