Implement the serial-to-parallel converter using a shift register that shifts in the new serial bit at the most significant bit (MSB) position on every valid input, effectively shifting existing bits toward the least significant bit (LSB). Maintain a 4-bit counter that increments on every valid data bit received to track the number of bits collected. When the counter reaches 8, the 8-bit parallel output is captured from the shift register, and the output valid signal is asserted for one clock cycle. Upon reset or output, the counter and shift register reset to zero. This approach uses direct shifting in the main synchronous always block without explicit FSM states, simplifying control logic while fulfilling the requirements.
