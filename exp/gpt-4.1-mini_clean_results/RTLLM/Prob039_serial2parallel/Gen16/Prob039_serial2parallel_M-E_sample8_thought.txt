Use a shift register that inserts each incoming serial bit at the most significant bit position, shifting existing bits to the right. This directly matches the requirement that the first received bit occupies the MSB of the output.

Use a 3-bit counter (cnt) to count valid input bits from 0 to 7. When cnt reaches 7, we have collected all 8 bits.

On every rising clock edge with din_valid asserted:
- Shift the incoming din_serial bit into the MSB of the shift register, shifting other bits right.
- Increment cnt.

When cnt reaches 7 (i.e., after the 8th bit is stored), assert dout_valid for one cycle and update dout_parallel with the shift register.

dout_valid is held low otherwise.

Reset all registers synchronously on active low reset.

This approach avoids an output_flag signal by directly detecting the terminal count, simplifying timing and control. It ensures the correct bit order (MSB-first), synchronous reset, and valid output timing.
