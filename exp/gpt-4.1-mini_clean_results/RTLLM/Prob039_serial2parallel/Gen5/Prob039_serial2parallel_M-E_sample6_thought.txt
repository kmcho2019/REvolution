Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of received bits from 1 to 8. On each valid serial input, shift the new bit into the MSB of an 8-bit register, shifting the register to the right. When the counter reaches 8, the register contains the full 8-bit data, output it, and set dout_valid high for one clock cycle. Then reset the counter and wait for the next 8 bits. This counter design clearly distinguishes between bit counts and synchronizes the output pulse. The shift register input is the serial bit loaded at MSB and the existing bits shifted toward LSB, matching MSB-first placement. The design is fully synchronous with an active low reset.
