Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of received bits (from 0 to 7). On each valid serial input, shift the new bit into a parallel register from MSB to LSB by shifting left and inserting din_serial at LSB. When the counter reaches 7 (meaning 8 bits have been received), transfer the register contents to dout_parallel and assert dout_valid for one clock cycle. Use synchronous reset to initialize registers. This approach ensures the first received bit ends in MSB position and the last bit in LSB, and separates shift register and output register to avoid data race and improve timing clarity.
