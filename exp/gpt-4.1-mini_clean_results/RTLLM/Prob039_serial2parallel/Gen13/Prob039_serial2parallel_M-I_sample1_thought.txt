The existing design is functionally correct and meets the requirements. To optimize for power, performance, and area (PPA):

1. **Power**: Add an enable signal to the counter and shift register logic to prevent unnecessary toggling when `din_valid` is low. This gates the registers from toggling when no valid data arrives.

2. **Performance**: Using nonblocking assignments in sequential always blocks is already done, so no change needed.

3. **Area**: The design is already minimal; however, making clear the one-cycle pulse generation for `dout_valid` improves readability and synthesis friendliness.

Additionally, instead of embedding the `dout_valid` reset in every clock cycle, explicitly clear it at the beginning of the clock cycle. This ensures a clean pulse. 

Also, adding comments clarifying the mapping of the serial input bit order to the output bits aids maintainability.

These small improvements make the design more power-friendly and clearer without changing functionality or timing.
