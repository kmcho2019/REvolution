Refactor the design by removing the SystemVerilog enum and instead use simple parameter definitions for states in traditional Verilog style. Use a synchronous process triggered on the rising edge of clk with active low reset to manage state, counter, and shift register. The incoming bits are shifted into the register from MSB to LSB by shifting the register left and inserting the new bit at LSB, so after 8 bits, the first received bit ends up in MSB position as required. The 4-bit counter counts from 0 to 7 for 8 bits total, and when full, asserts dout_valid for one clock cycle with the parallel data on dout_parallel. Otherwise, dout_valid is low. No asynchronous reset for counters/state beyond synchronous reset. This approach preserves functionality and is compatible with pure Verilog-2001 without SystemVerilog features.
