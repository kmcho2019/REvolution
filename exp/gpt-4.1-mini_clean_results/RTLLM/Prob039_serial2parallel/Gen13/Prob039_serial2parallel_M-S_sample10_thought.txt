Use a single 8-bit shift register that shifts in din_serial at the MSB position each time din_valid is high. Increment a 4-bit counter for each valid bit received. When the counter reaches 7 (indicating 8 bits received, counting from 0), output the shift register as dout_parallel and assert dout_valid for one cycle. Reset the counter and shift register synchronously with rst_n. This simple approach directly aligns with the bit indexing: the first received bit is at dout_parallel[7] (MSB), and the last at dout_parallel[0] (LSB).
