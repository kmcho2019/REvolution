Refactor the serial-to-parallel converter to a simpler synchronous design without an explicit state machine. Use a shift register that shifts left on each valid input, inserting the new bit at the LSB. The first received bit ends up at the MSB after 8 shifts, so finally reverse the bits to place the first bit at MSB in the output. Use a 4-bit counter to count received bits. When the count reaches 8, assert dout_valid for one clock cycle. Use synchronous reset and only registers updated in a single always block. Assign outputs directly from registers. This approach simplifies the design by removing states and uses bit reversal on output for correct bit order.
