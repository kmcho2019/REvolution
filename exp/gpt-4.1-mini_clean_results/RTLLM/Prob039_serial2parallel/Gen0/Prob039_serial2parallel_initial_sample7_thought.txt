Use a 4-bit counter to count valid serial inputs. On each rising clock edge, if din_valid is high, shift the incoming serial bit into an 8-bit shift register from MSB to LSB, decrementing counter towards 0 or counting up to 8. When 8 bits have been collected, output the parallel data and set dout_valid high for one cycle. On reset, clear the counter, shift register, and dout_valid.
