Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of valid serial inputs received. On each rising clock edge, if rst_n is low, reset the counter and output signals. When din_valid is high, shift the incoming serial bit into an 8-bit register from MSB to LSB by shifting left and inserting the new bit at LSB. Increment the counter. When the counter reaches 8, set dout_valid high and output the 8-bit parallel data. Otherwise, dout_valid is low.
