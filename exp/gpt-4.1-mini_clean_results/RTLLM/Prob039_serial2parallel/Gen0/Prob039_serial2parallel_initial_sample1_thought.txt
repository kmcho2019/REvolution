Use a 4-bit counter to count the valid incoming bits. On each rising clock edge, when din_valid is high, shift the incoming din_serial bit into an 8-bit register from MSB to LSB (left shifting the register by one and inserting the new bit at the LSB). When the count reaches 8, output the collected 8 bits to dout_parallel and set dout_valid to 1 for one clock cycle. On reset, clear the register, counter and valid flag.
