Use a simple 8-bit register to collect serial input bits, shifting left and inserting the new bit at the least significant bit position. This way, the earliest received bit becomes the MSB, matching the requirement. Increment a 4-bit counter each time a valid bit is received. When the counter reaches 7 (meaning 8 bits have been collected), output the register and assert dout_valid for one clock cycle, then reset the counter. This approach is straightforward, synchronous, and easy to verify.
