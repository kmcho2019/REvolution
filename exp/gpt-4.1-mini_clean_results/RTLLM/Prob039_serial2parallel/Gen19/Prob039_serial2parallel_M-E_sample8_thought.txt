Use an 8-bit parallel register that captures each incoming serial bit directly into the corresponding bit position indexed by a counter. On each valid input, store the serial bit at the position (7 - count), since the first bit received should be MSB. A 4-bit counter counts from 0 to 7, and when the 8th bit is stored (counter reaches 7), output is latched and dout_valid is asserted for one cycle.

This method avoids shifting the entire register on every cycle, instead performing a direct indexed assignment, which may reduce switching activity and simplify timing. Reset initializes the register and counter. This approach provides clear indexing corresponding to bit positions and straightforward control logic.
