Use a straightforward 4-bit counter to count received bits (0 to 7). Shift the incoming serial bit directly into the parallel output register from the MSB to LSB, i.e., place the first received bit at dout_parallel[7], next at [6], ..., last at [0]. On each clock cycle when din_valid is high, shift the bits left by one and input the new bit at LSB if shifting from LSB side, or better, directly place the incoming bit at the appropriate position determined by the counter. When the 8th bit is received (counter reaches 7), assert dout_valid for one cycle and output the complete 8-bit parallel data. Reset logic initializes all registers synchronously. This approach eliminates bit-reversal and extra functions, providing a clear and simple synchronous design fully aligned with requirements.
