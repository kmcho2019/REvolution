Instead of a pure shift register shifted in from MSB side, use an 8-bit register with a write pointer indexed by a 4-bit counter that counts how many bits have been received. Each valid serial bit is stored directly into the corresponding bit of the register starting from MSB (bit 7) down to LSB (bit 0), matching the problem's MSB-first order. When the counter reaches 8, the parallel data is output and the valid flag is asserted for one cycle. This direct indexed assignment avoids shifting and potential off-by-one issues and clearly maps bits to output positions.
