Instead of shifting bits on each valid input, use a 4-bit counter as an index to directly assign incoming bits to the appropriate position in an 8-bit register. Since the first bit received should go to the most significant bit (bit 7) and the last to the least significant bit (bit 0), we can calculate the bit position as (7 - cnt). On each clock cycle with din_valid high, store din_serial into the calculated bit position, increment the counter, and when the counter reaches 7 (meaning 8 bits received), output the 8-bit parallel data and assert dout_valid for one cycle. Reset clears all registers and counters.
