To improve the original 32-bit CLA design in terms of performance while maintaining good area and power:

- The original 16-bit CLA computes carries sequentially from bit 0 to 15, which effectively is a ripple carry inside the 16-bit block, limiting speed.
- To enhance speed, implement true carry-lookahead within the 16-bit block using a hierarchical carry-lookahead scheme:
  - Divide the 16 bits into 4 groups of 4 bits each.
  - Compute per-bit generate/propagate signals.
  - Compute group generate/propagate signals from the 4-bit groups.
  - Use a 4-bit carry-lookahead logic at the group level to produce group carries quickly.
  - Finally compute the per-bit carries using group carry-in signals.
- This reduces the carry propagation delay to logarithmic instead of linear per block.
- At the top 32-bit level, still instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks with Cin=0 for the lower block, and carry-in = block generate and block propagate from the lower block combined logically to upper block:
  C16 = G_low | (P_low & Cin) => since Cin=0, C16 = G_low.
- Keep the external indexing [32:1] but internally use zero-based.
- This hierarchical approach balances area and speed:
  - Area is slightly increased by extra wires and logic for group generate/propagate.
  - Speed is improved significantly by parallel carry calculation.
- The carry output C32 is the final carry out from the upper block.
- Use only wires and combinational logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
- The indexing in ports and remapping remains unchanged for interface compatibility.

This design improves timing while still controlling area and power by using a 4-bit group carry-lookahead inside the 16-bit block.
