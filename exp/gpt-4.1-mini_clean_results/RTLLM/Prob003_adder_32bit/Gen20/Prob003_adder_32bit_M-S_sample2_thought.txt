To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder, I will keep the hierarchy of two 16-bit CLA blocks and implement each 16-bit block as a simple ripple of carry using generate loops for clarity and minimal code lines. This avoids complex group propagate/generate signals, reduces code complexity, and still maintains correctness by chaining the carry-out of the lower 16-bit block to the carry-in of the upper 16-bit block. The inputs/outputs are properly sliced as 32:1 per the requirement, adjusting indexing accordingly.
