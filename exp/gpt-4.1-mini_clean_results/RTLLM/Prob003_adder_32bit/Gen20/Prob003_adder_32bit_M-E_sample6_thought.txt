To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using two instances of a 16-bit CLA block, I will first design a 16-bit CLA module that computes bitwise propagate (p) and generate (g) signals, and then uses a hierarchical carry-lookahead logic to quickly produce carry outputs for each bit.

The 16-bit CLA module will output a 16-bit sum and a carry-out. Internally, it will calculate group propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for the entire 16-bit block, which will be used by the top 32-bit module.

At the 32-bit top module, two 16-bit CLA blocks will be instantiated: the lower and upper halves. The carry-in to the lower half will be zero, and the carry-in to the upper half will be the carry-out of the lower half.

This hierarchical design maintains the carry-lookahead architecture at both block levels, ensuring logarithmic carry computation delay, and meets the requirement of using 16-bit CLA blocks as building units for the 32-bit adder.

To improve modularity, the 16-bit CLA block will output its group propagate and generate signals, which can be used to implement a 2-level lookahead carry chain in the top module if desired, but here the simple carry chain between two 16-bit blocks suffices for correct functionality.

Bit indexing will be kept as [16:1] and [32:1] for clarity and consistency.
