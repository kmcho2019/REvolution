To optimize the 32-bit CLA for better performance and area while maintaining correctness, I will design an improved 16-bit CLA block using hierarchical carry lookahead logic with group propagate and generate signals.

The idea:
- Divide the 16-bit block into 4 groups of 4 bits each.
- Compute bit-level generate (G) and propagate (P) signals.
- For each 4-bit group, compute group generate (GG) and group propagate (GP).
- Use group-level lookahead logic to compute carries into each group, reducing carry chain length.
- This reduces critical path delay by avoiding ripple carry within the 16-bit block.
- Instantiate two such 16-bit CLA blocks in the 32-bit adder module, connecting carries between lower and upper halves.

This hierarchical structure reduces carry propagation delay significantly compared to a ripple-like carry chain while keeping area reasonable. It also uses generate loops for concise and scalable code.

The sum bits are computed as S = P ^ carry_in. The final carry-out is from the last group's carry.

This approach balances performance and area better than the previous fully ripple-style 16-bit CLA and meets the requirements.
