To improve the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder's performance while maintaining reasonable area and power, I will enhance the 16-bit CLA blocks by implementing a hierarchical CLA structure inside each 16-bit block. Specifically, the 16-bit block will be divided into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks.

Each 4-bit CLA will generate propagate and generate signals and calculate the internal carry signals quickly. Then, the 16-bit block will compute the block propagate and generate signals from the 4-bit blocks and compute the carries for these 4-bit blocks using a second-level carry-lookahead logic.

This two-level CLA hierarchy greatly reduces the carry propagation delay compared to a simple ripple or single-level CLA over 16 bits.

At the 32-bit level, I will keep the same structure: two 16-bit CLA blocks connected with carry chaining.

Additionally, I will maintain the [32:1] indexing on top-level ports and map them internally to [31:0]. The code will remain modular, clean, and parameterized where possible.

This hierarchical design should improve timing significantly, reduce critical path delay, and optimize power by reducing switching activity in carry chains. The area impact is moderate but justified for the timing gain.

Summary of improvements:
- 16-bit CLA block split into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks.
- Second-level CLA carry generation over 4-bit block signals.
- Top-level 32-bit CLA composed of two enhanced 16-bit CLA blocks.
