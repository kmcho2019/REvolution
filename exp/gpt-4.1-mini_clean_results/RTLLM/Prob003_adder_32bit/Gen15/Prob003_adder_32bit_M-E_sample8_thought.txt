I will design a 4-block hierarchical 32-bit carry-lookahead adder composed of four 8-bit CLA blocks. Each 8-bit block computes local generate and propagate signals and produces its own carry-out. Then, a higher-level 4-bit block carry lookahead unit computes the carries between these 8-bit blocks based on their block generate and propagate signals. This hierarchical approach reduces the carry logic delay compared to a flat 16-bit block or simple ripple chaining. The indexing respects the 1-based input/output ports, and the modules are fully parameterized for clarity. The top module connects the four 8-bit CLA blocks with the 4-bit block-level CLA to generate the final sum and carry-out.
