I will implement a 32-bit carry lookahead adder using hierarchical carry lookahead logic. The top-level adder_32bit will instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks for the lower and upper 16 bits, but the carry between these blocks will be generated by a dedicated carry lookahead unit that takes group propagate and generate signals from both 16-bit blocks and computes the carry-in to the upper block (C16) and the final carry-out (C32). This hierarchical approach improves timing by decoupling the carry computation from the sum calculation and uses group generate/propagate signals to accelerate carry propagation over the 32 bits. The 16-bit CLA module will output its group generate and propagate signals for use by the top carry logic. This modular design improves performance and clarity, and better demonstrates the carry lookahead principle.
