To design a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA), I will build a hierarchical structure using 16-bit CLA blocks. First, I'll implement a 16-bit CLA block that takes two 16-bit inputs and a carry-in, and outputs a 16-bit sum and a carry-out. This 16-bit CLA will internally generate propagate and generate signals, compute carries, and produce the sum bits in parallel.

Then, the 32-bit adder top module will instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks:
- The lower 16-bit CLA takes the lower 16 bits of A and B, with carry-in = 0.
- The upper 16-bit CLA takes the upper 16 bits of A and B, with carry-in connected to the carry-out of the lower 16-bit CLA.

This hierarchical approach simplifies design and improves modularity.

The inputs and outputs are specified as [32:1], so the slicing and wiring will respect that indexing.

Note: Since input/output indices are [32:1], the least significant bit is index 1 and most significant is index 32.
