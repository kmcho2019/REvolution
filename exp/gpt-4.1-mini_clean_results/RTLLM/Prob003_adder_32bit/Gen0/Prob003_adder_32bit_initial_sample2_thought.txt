Implement a 32-bit adder using the carry-lookahead approach by instantiating two 16-bit CLA modules. First, design a 16-bit CLA module that calculates sum, generate (G), and propagate (P) signals and uses carry-lookahead logic internally to quickly compute carries for 16 bits. Then, the top 32-bit adder module will instantiate two of these 16-bit CLA modules: the lower 16 bits and the upper 16 bits. The carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA (C16) feeds into the carry-in of the upper 16-bit CLA. The final carry-out (C32) is the carry-out of the upper module. The sum is the concatenation of the two 16-bit sums. Inputs and outputs are 32-bit vectors with bits [32:1].
