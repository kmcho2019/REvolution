To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using 16-bit CLA blocks, we first design a 16-bit CLA module that outputs sum, group propagate and generate signals, and the carry-out. Then, at the top 32-bit adder level, we instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks. The carry-in to the second 16-bit block is generated by the first 16-bit block's carry-out. We use the group propagate and generate signals from both blocks to compute the final carry-out (C32). This hierarchical approach improves speed by generating carry signals in blocks rather than bit-by-bit.
