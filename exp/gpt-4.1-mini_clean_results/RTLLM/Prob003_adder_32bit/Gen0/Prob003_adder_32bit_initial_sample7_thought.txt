To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) using the CLA architecture, we will design a 16-bit CLA block first, which generates sum, propagate, and carry-out signals for 16 bits. Then, in the top-level 32-bit adder module, we instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks: one for the lower 16 bits and one for the upper 16 bits. The carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA is used as the carry-in for the upper 16-bit CLA. This modular approach simplifies the design and ensures fast carry propagation. The inputs and outputs follow the specified port sizes (bits 32 down to 1).

The 16-bit CLA module will calculate the generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for each bit, then use these to compute the carry signals for all bits efficiently, producing the sum bits and the carry-out.

Finally, the top module adder_32bit uses two 16-bit CLA blocks chained via carry signals to produce the final 32-bit sum and carry-out.
