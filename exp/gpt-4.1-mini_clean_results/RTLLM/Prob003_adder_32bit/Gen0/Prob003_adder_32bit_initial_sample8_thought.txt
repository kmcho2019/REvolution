To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using 16-bit CLA blocks, first we design a 16-bit CLA module that computes the sum, generate and propagate signals internally, and outputs the sum and carry-out. Then, we instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks in the 32-bit adder module. The carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA is used as the carry-in for the upper 16-bit CLA. Finally, we combine the sum outputs and generate the overall carry-out (C32). The 16-bit CLA uses internal generate and propagate signals to quickly compute carries for all bits using the carry-lookahead logic.
