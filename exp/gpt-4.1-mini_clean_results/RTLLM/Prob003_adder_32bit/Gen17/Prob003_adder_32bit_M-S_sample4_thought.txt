I will implement the 16-bit CLA module using simple bitwise propagate and generate signals and a carry vector computed iteratively with a generate loop, avoiding explicit intermediate propagate/generate blocks or hierarchical CLA logic to keep it simple. The 32-bit adder will instantiate two 16-bit CLA modules, connecting the carry out of the lower to the carry in of the upper. This approach minimizes code lines and complexity while preserving the carry-lookahead functionality.
