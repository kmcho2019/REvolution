I will simplify the 16-bit CLA module by using straightforward bitwise operations for propagate and generate signals and calculating the carry signals in a linear fashion within a generate loop without complex hierarchical carry lookahead logic. This keeps the implementation easy to understand and verify while maintaining the carry-lookahead principle at the bit level. The top-level 32-bit adder instantiates two such 16-bit CLAs connected by the carry-out to carry-in signal. This approach reduces code complexity and is suitable for moderate speed and area requirements.
