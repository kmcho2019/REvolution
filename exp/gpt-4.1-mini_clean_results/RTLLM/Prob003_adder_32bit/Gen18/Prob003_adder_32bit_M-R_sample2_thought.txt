I will refactor the 16-bit CLA module to explicitly calculate the carry signals using the well-known CLA carry lookahead equations, computing group generate (Gg) and propagate (Pg) signals for four 4-bit blocks to speed up carry computation in parallel rather than sequentially as before. This hierarchical approach reduces carry delay within the 16-bit block. The 32-bit adder still instantiates two 16-bit CLA blocks, connecting carry-out of lower to carry-in of upper. The code uses only assign statements for combinational logic without generate loops, improving readability and maintaining the CLA architecture while optimizing carry calculation.
