The original design is functionally correct and clean but uses a simple ripple carry-like chain inside the 16-bit CLA block. To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead scheme inside the 16-bit CLA. Instead of computing each carry bit from generate/propagate sequentially, I will generate group propagate and generate signals for 4-bit blocks and then use a small CLA logic to compute carries at a block level first. This reduces the carry chain delay from O(16) to roughly O(log4(16)) = 2 levels, improving timing significantly.

This hierarchy breaks the 16 bits into 4 groups of 4 bits:
- Compute 4-bit local generate/propagate signals
- Compute carries between groups using a 4-bit CLA
- Compute final bit carries inside each 4-bit block

This technique leads to a more parallel carry computation, faster critical path, and potentially lower power due to reduced logic depth.

Then, instantiate two such improved 16-bit CLA blocks for the 32-bit adder as before.

Indexing in the top-level is kept consistent (0-based inside 16-bit CLA, and 32-bit inputs use [31:0]) for clarity.

This hierarchical CLA inside 16-bit block is a classic method used in practical CLA adders to balance complexity and speed.
