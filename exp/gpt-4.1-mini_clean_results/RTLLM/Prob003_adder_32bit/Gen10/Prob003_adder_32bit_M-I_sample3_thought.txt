To optimize the existing 32-bit carry-lookahead adder for better performance and area, I will implement a hierarchical CLA structure inside the 16-bit CLA block.

Improvement strategy:
- Replace the flat 16-bit CLA with a 4-block hierarchical CLA:
  - Each 16-bit CLA is made of four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks.
  - Compute generate and propagate signals for each 4-bit block.
  - Use these block-level signals to efficiently compute carry-in signals for each 4-bit block, significantly reducing carry propagation delay.
  - This hierarchical approach limits the carry chain length to 4 bits within each 4-bit block and 4 blocks at the 16-bit level, improving timing.
- Maintain the external 1-based indexing and modular top-level connection between two 16-bit CLA blocks.
- The top-level 32-bit adder instantiates two hierarchical 16-bit CLA blocks as before.
- This structure balances performance gain while keeping area overhead moderate.
