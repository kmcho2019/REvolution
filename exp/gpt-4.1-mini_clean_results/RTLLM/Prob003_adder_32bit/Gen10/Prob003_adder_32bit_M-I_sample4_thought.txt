To optimize the 32-bit CLA adder design for better PPA, I will employ a hierarchical carry lookahead scheme within the 16-bit CLA blocks by breaking them into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks. This reduces the longest carry propagation path and hence improves timing (performance).

Specifically:
- Implement a 4-bit CLA block (cla_4bit) that computes sum and carry-out efficiently.
- Build the 16-bit CLA block (cla_16bit) by instantiating four 4-bit CLA blocks and a 4-bit block-level carry lookahead logic to generate carries between these sub-blocks.
- This hierarchy minimizes the carry chain delay by computing carries at group levels, reducing gate levels for carry propagation.
- Maintain the top-level 32-bit adder structure connecting two 16-bit CLA blocks.
- Keep the 1-based indexing on input/output ports, slicing inputs internally for zero-based indexing.
- This hierarchical approach improves timing by reducing critical path delay without significant area or power penalties.
