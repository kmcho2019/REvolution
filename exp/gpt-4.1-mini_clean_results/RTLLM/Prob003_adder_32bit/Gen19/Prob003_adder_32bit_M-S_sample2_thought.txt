I will simplify the 16-bit CLA by using generate and propagate signals combined at a group level to directly compute the carries with fewer intermediate signals. Instead of enumerating all carry assignments, I will compute carries using a 4-bit block carry lookahead approach internally, reducing the complexity and verbosity. The 32-bit adder will instantiate two of these 16-bit CLAs connected via carry-out to carry-in. This keeps the design clear and functional with less repetitive code.
