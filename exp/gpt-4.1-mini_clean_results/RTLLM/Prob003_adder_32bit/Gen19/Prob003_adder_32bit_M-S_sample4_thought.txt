To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design while maintaining functionality, I will use the two 16-bit CLA blocks but implement each 16-bit block as a simple ripple carry adder inside a generate loop without explicit propagate/generate signals.

This approach reduces complexity by avoiding carry-lookahead inside 16-bit blocks and relies on ripple carry, which is simpler and more compact.

The top-level 32-bit adder then chains two 16-bit adders, with the carry-out of the lower half feeding into the carry-in of the upper half.

This simplification maintains correct addition functionality, keeps the code clean, and reduces logic complexity at the expense of some performance, which fits the requirement to simplify.
