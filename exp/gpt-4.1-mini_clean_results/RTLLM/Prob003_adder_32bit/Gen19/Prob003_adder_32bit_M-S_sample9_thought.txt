To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design, I will keep the hierarchical structure with two 16-bit CLA blocks but remove the explicit carry assignments for each bit inside the 16-bit CLA. Instead, I will use generate and propagate groups to compute the carry-out directly and implement the carries in a single line using an adder-like approach. This reduces repetitive code and improves readability without changing the functionality. The 32-bit adder remains two 16-bit blocks chained by carry.
