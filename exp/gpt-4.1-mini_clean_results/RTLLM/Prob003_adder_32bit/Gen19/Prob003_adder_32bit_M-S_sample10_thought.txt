To simplify the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder design while maintaining correct functionality, I will:

- Keep the hierarchical approach with two 16-bit CLA blocks but simplify the carry calculation inside each 16-bit CLA.
- Implement the 16-bit CLA using only propagate and generate signals with straightforward carry logic without recomputing carries for block generate signal.
- Calculate block propagate and generate signals as simple reductions of bitwise signals (P = AND of all propagates, G = the carry-out with Cin=0).
- Avoid extra zero-Cin carry recomputation for block generate and simply define block generate as carry out with Cin=0.
- Use zero-based indexing internally for clarity but keep input/output ports at [32:1].
- Use continuous assignments and avoid procedural code or generate loops for carry generation inside 16-bit CLA for clarity and brevity.
- Keep the top 32-bit adder simple by instantiating two 16-bit CLAs and connecting the carry-out of the low block to the carry-in of the high block.

This reduces redundant logic and complexity while still providing a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure.
