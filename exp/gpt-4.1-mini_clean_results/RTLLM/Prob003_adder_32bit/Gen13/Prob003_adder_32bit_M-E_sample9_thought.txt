I will design a hierarchical 32-bit carry-lookahead adder by composing four 8-bit CLA blocks. Each 8-bit CLA block computes local sums, propagates, and generates signals. Then, a top-level carry-lookahead logic will combine the 4 blocks' propagate and generate outputs to quickly compute the carry-in signals for each 8-bit block, thus enabling fast carry calculation across all 32 bits. This structure reduces the carry propagation delay by using a two-level hierarchy (block-level and top-level) of carry-lookahead logic, resulting in faster operation than simple ripple or flat CLA. The top-level module adder_32bit instantiates four 8-bit CLA blocks and a carry lookahead logic for the block-level carry chains.
