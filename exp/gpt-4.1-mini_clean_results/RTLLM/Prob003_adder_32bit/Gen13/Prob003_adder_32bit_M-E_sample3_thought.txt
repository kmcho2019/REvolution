Implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using a hierarchical CLA design: divide the 32 bits into four 8-bit CLA blocks. Each 8-bit CLA block computes generate, propagate, sum, and its own carry-out. Then use a 4-bit CLA block at a higher level to combine these four blocks' generate/propagate signals to rapidly determine carries between the 8-bit blocks. This approach fully leverages carry-lookahead logic at two hierarchy levels, reducing carry chain length and improving speed over ripple carry or flat CLA. It also modularizes the design for better readability and scalability.
