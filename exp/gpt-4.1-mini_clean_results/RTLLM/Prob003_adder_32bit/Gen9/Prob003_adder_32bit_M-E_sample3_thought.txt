I will implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder by hierarchically building it from 4-bit CLA blocks. Each 4-bit CLA block computes its sum and group propagate and generate signals. Then, a 8-bit block-level carry-lookahead unit computes carry inputs for each 4-bit block using these group signals, enabling faster carry propagation over the entire 32 bits. This approach balances modularity and speed: small 4-bit blocks simplify carry computations internally, while the higher-level carry generator handles carries across blocks efficiently. Finally, the top 32-bit adder instantiates eight 4-bit CLAs and one 8-block carry lookahead unit, connecting carries accordingly.
