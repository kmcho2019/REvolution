Implement the 32-bit carry lookahead adder (CLA) by hierarchical decomposition into four 8-bit CLA blocks instead of two 16-bit blocks. Each 8-bit CLA computes its internal carry signals locally using propagate and generate signals, and additionally outputs group propagate (PG) and group generate (GG) signals representing the block’s carry behavior.

At the top level, connect the four 8-bit blocks in a CLA manner using their PG and GG signals to quickly generate carries between blocks. This creates a two-level hierarchical CLA where the first level handles carry propagation inside 8-bit blocks, and the second level handles carry propagation across the 4 blocks efficiently.

This method offers a more balanced carry lookahead structure that can potentially reduce carry delay and allows reusing the smaller 8-bit CLA block. The top module converts the 1-based inputs to zero-based for internal processing.

The approach is fully combinational, modular, and shows a different hierarchy and carry computation approach than the original 2x16-bit structure.
