To improve the original 32-bit CLA adder design for better PPA metrics, I will implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead within each 16-bit CLA block to reduce the carry propagation delay and hence improve timing. This is achieved by dividing the 16-bit block into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks with their own propagate and generate signals and then using a block-level carry lookahead logic to compute carries in parallel. This hierarchical approach reduces the critical path from linear (ripple inside 16-bit) to logarithmic by handling carries in groups, speeding up the carry computation and reducing overall delay.

The top-level 32-bit adder still uses two instances of these improved 16-bit CLA blocks with carry chaining.

This hierarchy also reduces switching power by limiting the carry signal fanout and number of sequential carry dependencies and will modestly reduce area by logic sharing of group signals.

I will provide the following:
- A 4-bit CLA submodule to handle small blocks
- A 16-bit CLA module built from four 4-bit CLA blocks with group generate/propagate logic and carry-lookahead for groups
- The 32-bit adder instantiates two 16-bit CLA blocks with proper carry chaining

This improves timing significantly while keeping area and power reasonable.
