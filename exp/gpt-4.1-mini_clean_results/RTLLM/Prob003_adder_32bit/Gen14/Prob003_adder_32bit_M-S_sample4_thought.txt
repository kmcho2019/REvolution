To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design while maintaining functionality, I will create a straightforward 16-bit CLA module that computes generate, propagate, and carries in a simple linear manner using a generate loop. Then, the top 32-bit adder will instantiate exactly two of these 16-bit CLAs, chaining their carry signals. The indexing will use zero-based vectors for simplicity, and the interface ports will be adapted accordingly to match the problem requirements with 1-based indexing internally handled by vector slicing. This reduces the design complexity by avoiding hierarchical carry generation trees and keeps the implementation clean and easy to follow.
