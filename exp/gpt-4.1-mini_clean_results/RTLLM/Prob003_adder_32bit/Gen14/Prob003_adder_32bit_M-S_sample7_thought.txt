To simplify the design while preserving the CLA architecture and 32-bit width, I will keep the 16-bit CLA module as is, but remove the generate loop and instead directly instantiate the carry logic with a simpler assign chain. For the top 32-bit module, I will continue using two 16-bit CLA blocks connected by the carry-out of the lower block. This approach keeps the code concise, easy to understand, and functional, while avoiding the extra complexity of generate loops.
