To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design, I will keep the hierarchical structure with two 16-bit CLA blocks but streamline the carry logic inside the 16-bit CLA block. Instead of explicitly assigning each carry bit one by one, I will use a generate loop to build carries sequentially, making the code more concise and easier to maintain. 

The propagate (P) and generate (G) signals will still be computed for each bit, and the carries will be generated in a for-loop using blocking assignments inside a combinational always block or continuous assignments in a generate loop, depending on style.

This approach keeps the design modular and functional while making the internal carry computation cleaner and reducing repeated code lines.

Note: Inputs and outputs remain with [32:1] indexing with LSB at 1.
