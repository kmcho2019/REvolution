To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder, I will keep the two-stage hierarchy: two 16-bit CLA blocks connected by a carry. However, I will implement the 16-bit CLA without a generate loop by using straightforward continuous assignments to compute carries based on the CLA carry-lookahead formula for each bit. This removes the generate complexity but preserves the lookahead principle and functionality. The 32-bit adder instantiates these two blocks with proper carry chaining.
