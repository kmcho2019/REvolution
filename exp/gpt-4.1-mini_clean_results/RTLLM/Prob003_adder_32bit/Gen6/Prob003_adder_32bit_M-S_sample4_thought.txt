To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design while maintaining functionality, I will implement a straightforward 16-bit CLA module using generate and propagate signals with carry computed sequentially but compactly through a wire vector and loop-generated carry signals. Then, the 32-bit adder will be composed of two instances of this 16-bit CLA connected by carry. Using zero-based indexing for convenience and clean code, this approach removes explicit repeated carry assignments and generates carry signals compactly using a generate-for loop, improving code readability and conciseness without sacrificing functional correctness.
