To simplify the 32-bit CLA adder design while maintaining correct functionality, I'll implement a single 16-bit carry-lookahead adder module that computes generate (G), propagate (P), and carry signals in a straightforward ripple carry manner. This means the internal carry signals are computed serially, but the code is made minimal and clear using simple loops for propagate, generate, carry, and sum computation.

The top-level 32-bit adder module will instantiate two such 16-bit adders, connecting the carry out of the lower to the carry in of the upper block.

This approach trades off true carry-lookahead performance for simplicity and ease of understanding, which is the goal. Indexing will be consistent with the 1-based vector as requested.

The design stays fully combinational and functional, with minimal code and no additional hierarchy inside 16-bit blocks.
