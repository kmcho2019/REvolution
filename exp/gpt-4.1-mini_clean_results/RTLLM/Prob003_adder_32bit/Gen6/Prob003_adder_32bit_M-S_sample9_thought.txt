To simplify the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder design while maintaining functionality, I will implement a straightforward hierarchical structure: the top 32-bit adder instantiates two 16-bit CLA modules connected via carry-in/carry-out.

For the 16-bit CLA module, instead of generating carries using loops and intermediate vectors, I will directly use a generate and propagate pair per bit, then compute the carry for each bit explicitly with simpler logic without internal hierarchical groupings.

This approach reduces structural complexity and keeps the module easy to understand and verify, while still following carry-lookahead principles at the bit level (each carry depends on the propagate/generate of its bit and carry-in). It won't fully optimize timing like a more advanced hierarchical CLA, but significantly simplifies the code.

The sum bits are computed as usual by XOR of propagate and carry-in. The top-level module simply connects two 16-bit blocks with carry propagation.

This balances simplicity and clarity without changing the interface or overall behavior.
