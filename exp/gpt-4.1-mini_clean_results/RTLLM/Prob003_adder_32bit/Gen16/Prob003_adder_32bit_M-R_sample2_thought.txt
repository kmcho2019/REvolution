Refactor the 32-bit CLA adder by explicitly computing the carry signals using hierarchical carry-lookahead logic within the 16-bit block, and propagate the carry-out from the lower 16-bit block to the upper 16-bit block by pre-computing propagate and generate signals for each block.

Inside each 16-bit CLA block, implement the carry-lookahead logic explicitly by grouping bits into 4-bit blocks, computing group propagate and generate signals, then combining these to generate the carry vector.

At the top 32-bit level, compute the two 16-bit blocks’ group propagate and generate signals to produce the carry-in for the upper block using the block-level CLA logic.

This refactor uses continuous assignments for all logic, no always blocks, a hierarchical CLA structure inside 16-bit blocks, and an additional carry-lookahead step between the two 16-bit blocks for improved timing and clarity. The bit ordering is preserved.

This structure improves modularity and allows faster carry computation across the 32 bits compared to simple ripple chaining of two 16-bit blocks.
