To improve the original 32-bit CLA adder implementation, focus on reducing the critical path delay and simplifying the propagate/generate logic by implementing a balanced 4-bit CLA hierarchy inside the 16-bit block. 

Instead of a linear carry chain over 16 bits, divide the 16-bit block into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks, each producing local propagate and generate signals. Then combine these using a 4-bit CLA carry-lookahead at the sub-block level to compute carries between the 4-bit blocks. This reduces the carry chain levels from 16 to roughly log2(4) + 4 = 6 carry stages, improving timing.

The block propagate/generate signals are now the AND/OR of the four sub-block propagate/generate signals, implemented hierarchically.

We retain the top-level structure of two 16-bit blocks for the 32-bit adder, but each 16-bit block internally uses this 4x4 CLA hierarchy for better performance and clearer structure.

Also, maintain the consistent indexing style using [32:1] for external interfaces and zero-based [31:0] internally, mapped with slicing.
