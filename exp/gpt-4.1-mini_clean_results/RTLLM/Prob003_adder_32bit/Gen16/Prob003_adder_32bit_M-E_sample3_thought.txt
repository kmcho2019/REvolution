I will design a hierarchical 32-bit carry-lookahead adder by composing four 8-bit CLA blocks. Each 8-bit CLA block will generate group propagate and generate signals. These group signals will be used in a top-level 4-block carry lookahead logic to quickly compute carry inputs for each 8-bit block. This hierarchical approach improves carry computation speed and modularity. The 8-bit CLA blocks internally compute carries in a lookahead manner. The top-level module then uses the group generate/propagate to calculate carries at block boundaries and feed them into the 8-bit blocks for final sum computation. Input and output ports will use the required [32:1] bit indexing.
