To improve the 32-bit CLA adder, I will enhance the internal 16-bit CLA block by implementing a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure, breaking the 16 bits into four 4-bit blocks. Each 4-bit block generates its group propagate and generate signals, which are used to compute carries between blocks. This reduces the carry chain length and allows parallel carry computation, significantly improving timing (WNS, TNS) and reducing power by minimizing glitching and switching activities.

The carry signals are computed in two stages: 
1. Within each 4-bit block, the 4-bit carries are computed using the bitwise propagate/generate signals.
2. Between blocks, carries are computed using the group propagate/generate signals in a small CLA tree.

This hierarchical approach results in faster carry generation than the flat ripple-style carry chain in the original 16-bit CLA.

For the top 32-bit module, the structure remains two 16-bit CLA instances chained by carry out.

Indexing is kept as [16:1] and [32:1] as per the problem spec for clarity.

This optimization improves timing and power without significantly increasing area by using a well-known, modular CLA design pattern.
