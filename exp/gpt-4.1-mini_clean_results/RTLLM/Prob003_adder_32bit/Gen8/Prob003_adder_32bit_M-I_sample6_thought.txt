To optimize the original 32-bit CLA adder for better performance and power (PPA), I will implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead scheme inside the 16-bit CLA block. Instead of generating all 16 carries sequentially, I will compute group propagate and generate signals for 4-bit sub-blocks and then use these to quickly compute carries for each 4-bit block, reducing the carry chain length and improving timing.

This hierarchical approach reduces critical path delay because carry signals are generated in parallel for groups rather than bitwise. The 32-bit adder will still be composed of two 16-bit blocks, but each 16-bit block will be faster due to this internal hierarchy.

I will also keep the code clean and maintain the zero-based indexing for vectors to improve readability and synthesis optimization.

This optimization should reduce worst negative slack and power due to shorter critical paths and more parallel carry generation while minimally increasing area due to added group generate/propagate signals.
