To improve the original 16-bit CLA block and thus the overall 32-bit adder's PPA metrics, I will implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure inside the 16-bit CLA.

Instead of computing carries sequentially (ripple style), I will:

1. Divide the 16-bit inputs into four 4-bit groups.
2. Compute bit-level propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for each bit.
3. Compute 4-bit group propagate (PG) and group generate (GG) signals.
4. Use the group PG and GG to compute carries into each 4-bit group in parallel.
5. Compute carries inside each 4-bit group using the same CLA logic.
6. Finally compute the sum bits from the carries.

This hierarchical approach reduces the carry chain length from 16 stages to 4 groups of 4 bits, reducing delay and dynamic power due to less switching.

The top 32-bit module remains unchanged, instantiating two 16-bit CLA modules.

I will keep the bit range [16:1] and [32:1] as in the problem.

This design more closely aligns with canonical CLA implementations, balancing complexity, speed, and area.
