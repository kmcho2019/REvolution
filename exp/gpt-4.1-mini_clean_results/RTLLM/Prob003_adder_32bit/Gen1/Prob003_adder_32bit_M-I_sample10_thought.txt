To improve the existing 32-bit carry-lookahead adder design in terms of performance, power, and area, I will restructure the 16-bit CLA into a hierarchical carry-lookahead architecture by splitting it into four 4-bit CLA blocks with an additional 4-bit carry-lookahead generator at the block level.

This approach:

- Breaks the 16-bit carry chain into smaller 4-bit blocks, reducing carry propagation delay within the 16-bit block.
- Uses block propagate and generate signals at 4-bit granularity to compute carries between blocks efficiently.
- Reduces logic depth and the critical path length, improving timing (lower WNS and better clock period).
- Simplifies the carry-generation logic using generate loops and concise parameterized code, reducing area and enhancing power efficiency.
- Retains the hierarchical top-level 32-bit adder with two 16-bit blocks, now internally optimized.

The improved design has:

- A 4-bit CLA block module producing sum, block propagate (P), and generate (G).
- A 16-bit CLA composed of four 4-bit CLAs plus carry-lookahead logic for block carries.
- The top 32-bit adder instantiates two 16-bit CLAs and computes the final carry-out efficiently.

This modular, hierarchical carry-lookahead structure reduces combinational depth and logic duplication, improving performance and power with minimal area overhead.
