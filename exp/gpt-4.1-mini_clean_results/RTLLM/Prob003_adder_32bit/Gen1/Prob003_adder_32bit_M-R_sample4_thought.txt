Refactor the 16-bit CLA module to use a hierarchical grouping strategy by dividing the 16 bits into four 4-bit blocks. Each 4-bit block produces group propagate and generate signals, which are then used to quickly compute carries for the 16 bits using a second-level carry-lookahead logic. This reduces logic depth and redundant repeated terms from the original fully expanded carry logic. The top-level 32-bit adder remains the same, instantiating two 16-bit CLA blocks. The refactored 16-bit CLA uses only `assign` statements, no loops or generate blocks, making the logic clearer and more modular. This approach improves readability, timing, and area by factoring carry computations hierarchically without changing the top-level interface or functionality.
