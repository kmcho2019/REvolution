To optimize the 32-bit carry lookahead adder for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will improve the 16-bit CLA block by implementing a hierarchical carry-lookahead scheme inside the 16-bit block itself. This means instead of a linear carry chain inside the 16-bit block, I will split it into four 4-bit CLA sub-blocks and use the group propagate and generate signals from each 4-bit block to compute carries for the 16 bits in parallel.

This hierarchical approach reduces logic depth of carry calculations from O(16) to O(log16), improving timing (lower critical path). It also reduces glitches and power consumption by balancing the carry generation logic. The area might slightly increase due to extra sub-blocks, but this is usually offset by simpler carry logic inside smaller blocks.

The top-level 32-bit adder remains the same, instantiating two such optimized 16-bit blocks. The 4-bit CLA block is simple and fully combinational, with direct carry calculation using generate/propagate groups. Carry-out and sum signals for the 16-bit block are computed from the 4-bit blocks' carry signals.

This hierarchical CLA architecture improves the overall carry-lookahead speed while maintaining modularity and reusability.
