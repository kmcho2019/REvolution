Refactoring explanation:
I will maintain the core hierarchical CLA architecture: two 16-bit CLA blocks composing the 32-bit adder. However, the internal carry computation inside the 16-bit CLA will be restructured to explicitly generate the carry-lookahead signals (generate and propagate) at multiple levels for clearer hierarchical carry computation. Instead of a simple carry chain with a generate loop, I'll calculate carries using grouped generate and propagate signals at 4-bit granularity within the 16-bit block to demonstrate a small hierarchy internally, improving readability and showing a more structured approach. This will break the internal carry computation into stages:

- First, per-bit generate and propagate signals.
- Then group propagate and generate signals for 4-bit slices.
- Compute carries into each 4-bit slice by applying CLA logic on group G and P.
- Use these carries to compute bit-level carries within each 4-bit slice.
- Finally, compute sums and block-level P_block and G_block signals accordingly.

The top-level 32-bit module will remain structurally similar but will directly connect the two 16-bit blocks with clean carry and block propagate/generate signals, preserving the same interface.

This refactoring:

- Uses assign statements everywhere (no always blocks).
- Avoids iterative carry loops, instead uses parallel logic expressions at multiple levels.
- Makes the hierarchy and carry computation in the 16-bit block more explicit and clear.
- Keeps 1-based indexing on external ports and zero-based internally.
- Provides clear comments to help understand the multi-level CLA.

This also aids future scalability by showing how a recursive or multi-level CLA is structured within a block.
