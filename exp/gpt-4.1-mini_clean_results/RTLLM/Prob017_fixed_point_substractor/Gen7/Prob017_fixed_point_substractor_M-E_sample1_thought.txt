- Extract sign bits and magnitudes (absolute values).

- For different signs, add magnitudes; result sign determined by magnitude comparison.
- After calculation, if result magnitude is zero, explicitly clear sign bit to zero.
- Assemble the result back with sign and magnitude.
- Use registers to hold intermediate results as requested.
