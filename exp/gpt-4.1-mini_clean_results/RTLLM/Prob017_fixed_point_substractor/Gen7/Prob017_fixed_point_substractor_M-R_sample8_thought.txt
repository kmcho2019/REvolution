- Implement separate combinational logic expressions for magnitude operations and sign logic.

- Compute the result sign and magnitude as wires, and convert back to two's complement with a pure combinational function.
- Assign the output `c` directly from the combinational result wire.
- Keep internal intermediate signals as wires to improve clarity and synthesis friendliness.
- Eliminate the internal register `res` by making it a wire representing the combinational result.
