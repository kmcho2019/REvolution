Implement the fixed-point subtractor by directly performing a signed subtraction using two's complement arithmetic, which naturally handles sign and fractional parts. Use continuous assignment for the output to keep the design simple and synthesis-friendly. This avoids using internal registers and always blocks, simplifying the design while maintaining precision and correctness for fixed-point subtraction.
