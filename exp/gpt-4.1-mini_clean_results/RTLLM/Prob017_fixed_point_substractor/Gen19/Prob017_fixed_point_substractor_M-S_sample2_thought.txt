Simplify the fixed-point subtractor by treating inputs as signed two's complement numbers and directly performing subtraction. This naturally handles sign and magnitude internally without explicit sign extraction or separate magnitude arithmetic. To comply with the requirement for explicit sign handling on zero results, explicitly check for zero output and force the sign bit to zero. The output is simply the subtraction result. This approach leverages Verilog's signed arithmetic, reduces complexity, and maintains precise fixed-point subtraction with correct sign handling.
