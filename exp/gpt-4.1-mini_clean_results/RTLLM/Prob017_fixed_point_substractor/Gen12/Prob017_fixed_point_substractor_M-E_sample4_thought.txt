Implement the fixed-point subtractor by explicitly decomposing the inputs into sign and magnitude parts, then perform magnitude operations based on sign conditions to clearly separate logic for same-sign and different-sign cases as specified.

Architecture:
- Extract sign bits and magnitudes (absolute values) of inputs.
- If signs are the same:
  - Subtract magnitudes (a_mag - b_mag).
  - If result is negative, invert and set sign accordingly.
  - The output sign equals input sign if the magnitude difference is positive, else inverted.
- If signs differ:
  - Add magnitudes (a_mag + b_mag).
  - Determine output sign by the sign of the minuend operand (a).
- Explicitly handle zero result to set sign bit to zero.
- Use parameterized widths and fixed-point representation.
- This approach makes the sign and magnitude logic explicit and aligned with the problem description, suitable for designs requiring clear sign handling.
- Use combinational logic with internal registers for magnitude results and sign calculation.

This design is more explicit about sign and magnitude and implements the problem's subtraction rules directly rather than relying on two's complement arithmetic.
