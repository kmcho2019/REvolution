The refactoring keeps the original algorithmic approach of explicit sign and magnitude processing but restructures the logic using continuous assignments (`assign`) and intermediate signed variables for better clarity and improved synthesis friendliness.

Key changes:
1. Declare inputs and outputs as unsigned to extract sign bits and magnitude explicitly.
2. Use signed logic vectors to hold magnitudes and intermediate results.
3. Implement combinational logic via continuous assignments instead of an always block.
4. Separate magnitude subtraction and addition clearly and use signed arithmetic on magnitude variables.
5. Use a ternary conditional operator for the final sign assignment and zero sign-bit enforcement.
6. The output `c` is constructed in two's complement by either zero-extending or taking two's complement of magnitude based on the result sign.
7. This style improves readability and synthesis compatibility, as combinational assignments are straightforward and the logic does not rely on manual bit concatenation in an always block.
8. The testbench is not included here but can remain as before to verify functionality.
