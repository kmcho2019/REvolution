Leverage Verilog signed arithmetic directly for fixed-point subtraction, as two's complement arithmetic naturally handles sign, integer, and fractional parts together. By declaring inputs, outputs, and internal result as signed, a simple combinational subtractor (`a - b`) covers all sign and magnitude cases inherently, including zero handling. The explicit sign fix for zero is unnecessary since two's complement zero has a zero sign bit by definition. Remove the internal register and keep the output as wire for combinational logic simplicity, minimizing area and improving synthesis friendliness. Parameter Q documents the fractional bits but is not used inside hardware. This keeps the implementation straightforward, synthesizable, and precise for fixed-point subtractor use.
