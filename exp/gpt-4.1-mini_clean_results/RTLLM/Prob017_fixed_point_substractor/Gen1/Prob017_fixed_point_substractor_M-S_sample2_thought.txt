Simplify the fixed-point subtractor by leveraging two's complement arithmetic properties directly, since subtraction of two's complement numbers inherently handles sign and magnitude correctly.

- Perform subtraction directly: c = a - b.
- No need to separately handle signs or magnitudes.
- To explicitly clear the sign bit on zero result and avoid negative zero, if the subtraction result is zero, force the sign bit to zero.
- Use parameters Q and N to support fixed-point precision but no special handling needed in the code.
- Implement as a combinational always block with internal register for result.

This approach maintains precision and correctness with much simpler code.
