To fix the syntax errors, all intermediate variables (sign_a, sign_b, abs_a, abs_b, res_sign, mag_sum) must be declared at the module scope, not inside the always block. Inside the always block, only assignments to these pre-declared regs are allowed. This corrects the illegal declaration errors.

The logic remains the same:
- Extract signs from inputs.
- When signs are the same, subtract directly.
- When signs differ, add magnitudes and assign result sign based on magnitude comparison.
- Handle zero result by forcing sign bit to zero.

Declaring all needed regs before the always block ensures legal syntax and correct functionality.
