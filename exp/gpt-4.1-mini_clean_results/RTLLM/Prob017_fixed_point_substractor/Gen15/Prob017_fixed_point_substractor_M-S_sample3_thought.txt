Simplify the fixed-point subtractor by leveraging the fact that inputs and outputs are in standard two's complement format. Since subtraction with signed fixed-point numbers is naturally handled by two's complement arithmetic, directly performing `c = a - b` provides the correct signed result with correct fractional precision. 

To handle the zero edge case where the result might be zero but with a sign bit of 1 due to two's complement representation, explicitly detect zero and force the sign bit to 0.

This approach removes the need for sign and magnitude extraction, manual addition or subtraction of magnitudes, and explicit sign reconstruction. The fixed-point precision is inherently preserved as all bits are uniformly processed.

The module uses a combinational always block to perform the subtraction, detect zero, and set the output accordingly. This simplifies synthesis and maintains correctness and precision.
