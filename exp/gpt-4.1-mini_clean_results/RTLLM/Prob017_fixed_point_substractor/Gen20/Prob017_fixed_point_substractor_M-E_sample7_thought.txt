Implement the fixed-point subtractor by explicitly extracting the sign bits and magnitudes of the inputs, then handling subtraction and addition based on sign comparisons, simulating manual signed arithmetic. This approach separates sign and magnitude logic to fulfill the requirement of sign-dependent operations: if signs are equal, subtract magnitudes; if different, add magnitudes and determine sign by comparing magnitudes. After computation, reconstruct the signed fixed-point result from sign and magnitude, and handle the zero case explicitly by forcing sign bit to zero. This provides precise control over sign logic and clear separation of integer and fractional parts embedded within fixed-point data.
