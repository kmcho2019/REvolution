The fixed-point subtraction can be directly performed using signed arithmetic in Verilog, which inherently manages sign and fractional parts. Thus, the sign-aware logic is naturally handled by the subtract operation itself, simplifying the design. To meet the requirement of explicitly setting the sign bit to zero when the result is zero, a simple conditional assignment can be used. This eliminates any need for separate sign or magnitude checks or internal registers, reducing complexity while preserving exact fixed-point behavior.
