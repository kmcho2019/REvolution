Since fixed-point numbers with the same Q format can be subtracted directly using two's complement arithmetic, the simplest and most efficient solution is to perform a signed subtraction of the inputs. This naturally handles sign and fractional parts together without extra logic. After subtraction, if the result is zero, explicitly clear the sign bit to zero to meet the zero-handling requirement. Using a single internal signed register for the result and parameterizing the bit widths completes the design simply and cleanly.
