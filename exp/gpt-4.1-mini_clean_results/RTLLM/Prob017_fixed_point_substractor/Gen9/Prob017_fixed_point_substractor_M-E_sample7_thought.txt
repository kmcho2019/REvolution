Implement a fixed-point subtractor that explicitly separates sign, integer, and fractional parts of the inputs to handle subtraction more structurally.

1. Extract the sign bits of `a` and `b`.
2. Extract the magnitude parts (absolute values) by masking off the sign bit and reconstructing the full magnitude.
3. Perform addition or subtraction on magnitudes depending on the sign combination:
   - If signs are the same, subtract magnitudes.
   - If signs differ, add magnitudes.
4. Determine the result sign based on inputs and comparison of magnitudes.
5. Handle zero result by forcing sign bit to zero.
6. Recombine sign and magnitude to produce final fixed-point output.

This approach mirrors manual fixed-point signed subtraction with explicit control over sign and magnitude operations. It avoids directly relying on the Verilog signed type arithmetic but carefully implements sign-magnitude arithmetic, making the arithmetic and sign handling explicit, thereby satisfying the problem's detailed conditions.

All operations are combinational. The code parameterizes N and Q, supports varying fixed-point widths, and is synthesizable.
