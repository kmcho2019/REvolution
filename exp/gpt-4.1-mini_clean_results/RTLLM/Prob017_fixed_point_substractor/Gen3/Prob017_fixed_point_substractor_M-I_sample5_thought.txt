3. When signs differ, perform addition of magnitudes, and determine the sign of the result based on magnitude comparison.
4. Ensure that zero result has a cleared sign bit.
5. Use two's complement representation consistently but perform operations on absolute magnitudes explicitly.
6. Implement combinational logic, considering parameterized width for flexibility.
7. Use non-blocking assignments inside an always_comb block or always @* block for clarity.
8. Optimize PPA by minimizing the width of intermediate signals and using clean combinational logic.

This approach explicitly separates sign and magnitude, fulfilling the problem's detailed sign handling while preserving fixed-point precision. The output remains in two's complement form with the correct sign and magnitude. Zero sign bit clearing is also ensured.

Note: In two's complement, the MSB is the sign bit. The absolute value can be computed by conditional inversion plus one if negative.
