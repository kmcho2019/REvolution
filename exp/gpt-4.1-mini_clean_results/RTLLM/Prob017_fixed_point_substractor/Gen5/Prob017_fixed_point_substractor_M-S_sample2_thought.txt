Implement the fixed-point subtractor as a purely combinational module without clock or reset to improve synthesis friendliness.

Leverage two's complement subtraction directly since inputs are in two's complement fixed-point format:
- The output is simply a - b, preserving fixed-point representation.
- To handle the zero sign bit explicitly, detect when the result is zero and force the sign bit to zero.

No explicit magnitude extraction or sign/magnitude operations are needed, which greatly simplifies the logic and synthesis.

This approach maintains precision and correctness for fixed-point arithmetic while meeting the sign and zero handling requirements efficiently.
