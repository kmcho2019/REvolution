Implement a fixed-point subtractor explicitly separating sign and magnitude to handle the detailed sign and zero handling requirements.

1. Extract sign bits and magnitude (absolute value) for inputs a and b.
2. If signs are the same, perform magnitude subtraction; the result sign is the same as inputs.
3. If signs differ, perform magnitude addition:
   - The result sign is sign of the operand with larger magnitude.
4. Use an internal register `res` to store the intermediate result.
5. After computing the magnitude and sign, reassemble the signed fixed-point result in two's complement format.
6. Explicitly detect zero result and force the sign bit to 0.
7. This approach emulates sign/magnitude arithmetic with precise control over sign and zero handling, matching problem requirements.
8. The output is registered for stable timing and synthesis friendliness.

This design is combinational except for the registered output, aligning with typical design methodologies requiring registered outputs for timing closure.
