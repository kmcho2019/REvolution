The fixed-point subtraction is performed considering the sign bits and fractional bits. 
1. Extract the sign bits of a and b.
2. If signs are equal, perform direct subtraction a - b, keeping the sign.
3. If signs differ, convert subtraction to addition of absolute values and determine the result sign by comparing magnitudes.
4. Handle zero case explicitly by setting sign bit to 0.
5. Use a register to store the intermediate result.
6. The design supports parameterized total width (N) and fractional width (Q).
