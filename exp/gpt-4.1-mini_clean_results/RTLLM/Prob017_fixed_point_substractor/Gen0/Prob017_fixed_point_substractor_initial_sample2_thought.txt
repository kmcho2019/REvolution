Implement a fixed-point subtractor module parameterized by total bits N and fractional bits Q. Inputs a and b are fixed-point numbers with signed representation, where the MSB is the sign bit. The subtraction result c should correctly handle cases where inputs have the same or different signs. 

- When signs are the same, perform direct subtraction of a and b, the sign of the result matches inputs.
- When signs differ, perform addition of the absolute values. The result's sign depends on which operand has the larger absolute value.
- The result zero case must clear the sign bit.
- Use an internal register res to store the intermediate result.
- Output c is assigned from res.

This approach respects the fixed-point format and preserves precision and sign handling correctly.
