Implement a parameterized fixed-point subtractor module with inputs a and b and output c of width N bits and fractional bits Q. The subtraction needs to handle same-sign inputs by direct subtraction, and different-sign inputs by addition of absolute values with appropriate sign determination. The result is stored in an internal register 'res' and assigned to output c. The zero result's sign bit is cleared. Sign extension and magnitude extraction are used to determine the magnitude and sign. The module performs all operations combinationally.
