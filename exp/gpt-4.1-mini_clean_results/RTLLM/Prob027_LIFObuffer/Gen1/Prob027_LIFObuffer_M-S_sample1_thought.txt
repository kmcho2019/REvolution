Use a stack pointer (SP) initialized to 0 representing the number of items in the stack. On reset, clear stack and set SP=0 (empty). For a push (write, RW=0) when not full, store dataIn at stack_mem[SP], then increment SP. For a pop (read, RW=1) when not empty, decrement SP and output dataOut from stack_mem[SP]. Update EMPTY and FULL flags based on SP (EMPTY when SP=0, FULL when SP=4). This approach uses SP as a count, simplifying push/pop logic and indexing.
