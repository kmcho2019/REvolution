To fix the timing and indexing issues caused by nonblocking assignments and immediate use of SP within the same always block, we separate combinational logic from sequential updates. We use a temporary variable `next_SP` to calculate the new stack pointer before updating it on the clock edge, and similarly handle data pushes and pops via temporary variables. Flags are updated synchronously after SP update, avoiding stale values. Also, we set `dataOut` combinationally based on the current SP, avoiding direct writes within the pop operation. This clearer separation ensures correct indexing and flag status in the same clock cycle. We maintain the same interface and overall stack architecture (4-entry stack, SP initialized to 4 meaning empty).
