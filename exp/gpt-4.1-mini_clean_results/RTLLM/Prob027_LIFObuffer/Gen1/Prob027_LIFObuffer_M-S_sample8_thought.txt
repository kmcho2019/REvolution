Use a 2-bit stack pointer (SP) that increments on push and decrements on pop for simpler indexing and boundary checks. Initialize SP to 0 on reset (empty). On push (RW=0), write dataIn to stack_mem[SP], then increment SP if not full. On pop (RW=1), decrement SP if not empty, then output stack_mem[SP]. Compute FULL as (SP == 4) and EMPTY as (SP == 0) combinationally from SP for simpler and glitch-free flag signals. Only perform push/pop when enabled and not full/empty. This approach removes complex pointer arithmetic and makes flag outputs purely combinational, reducing logic and improving clarity.
