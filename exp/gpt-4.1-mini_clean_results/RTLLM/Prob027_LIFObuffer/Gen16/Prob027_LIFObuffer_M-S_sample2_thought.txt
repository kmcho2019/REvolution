Simplify the LIFO buffer by using a single always block synchronized on the clock and reset, directly updating the stack pointer and memory based on enable, RW, and buffer status without separate push/pop enable signals. Use the stack pointer SP to indicate the next free position (SP=0 empty, SP=4 full), pushing increments SP and writing dataIn at SP, popping decrements SP and outputs the top data. Flags EMPTY and FULL are combinationally derived from SP. Initialize stack_mem and SP on reset. This reduces control signal logic and simplifies pointer arithmetic, while keeping full functionality and clarity.
