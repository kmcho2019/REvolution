Optimize the existing LIFObuffer by reducing unnecessary register updates and simplifying the control logic to improve power and timing. Specifically, update dataOut and SP only on valid pop or push operations, respectively, rather than on every clock cycle when EN is high. Replace two separate push_valid and pop_valid signals with a single operation_enable flag and use a single always block for next state calculation with clear boundaries. Use a 2-bit stack pointer (0 to 4) but keep SP in binary for simplicity. Also, avoid resetting stack memory to zero explicitly since the functional correctness doesn't require it on reset for synthesis; this can reduce area and power by removing the reset loop. Lastly, make the FULL and EMPTY signals combinationally generated from SP to reduce combinational logic delay. This approach improves power by avoiding needless toggling, trims the logic slightly, and may enhance timing by simplifying conditions.
