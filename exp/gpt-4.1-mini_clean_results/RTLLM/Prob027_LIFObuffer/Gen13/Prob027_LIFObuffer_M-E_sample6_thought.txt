Implement the LIFO buffer using a 4-bit stack pointer (SP) indicating the index of the next free slot (0 to 4). On push (RW=0), store dataIn at stack_mem[SP], then increment SP. On pop (RW=1), decrement SP first, then output stack_mem[SP]. 

To avoid timing issues on pop (using updated SP in the same cycle), separate the pop operation into two steps:
1. On clock edge, if pop requested and buffer not empty, decrement SP.
2. In a second combinational always block, assign dataOut to stack_mem[SP] (after decrement).

Thus, dataOut updates immediately after pop without depending on non-blocking SP updates within one clock cycle.

Also, flags FULL and EMPTY are combinational signals based on SP.

On reset, SP is zero and all stack_mem entries are zeroed.

This approach creates a cleaner timing model with combinational dataOut update and registered SP updates. It also decouples push and pop operations to avoid output glitches or timing hazards, improving maximum clock frequency potential.
