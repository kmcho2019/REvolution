Simplify the LIFObuffer by merging push and pop operations into a single always block with clear priority on operations. Use the stack pointer (SP) as a count of items in the stack (0 = empty, 4 = full) to simplify boundary checks and address calculation. On a push (RW=0), write dataIn to stack_mem[SP] and increment SP if not full. On a pop (RW=1), decrement SP if not empty and read dataOut from stack_mem[SP-1]. Update EMPTY and FULL flags combinationally based on SP. Reset clears SP to 0 and zeroes stack. This reduces address calculations and logic while keeping all functionality intact.
