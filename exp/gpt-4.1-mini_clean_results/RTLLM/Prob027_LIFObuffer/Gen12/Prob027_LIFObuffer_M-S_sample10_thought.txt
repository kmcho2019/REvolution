To simplify the LIFObuffer design, I will remove the separate combinational logic for next_SP and valid_op by directly handling push and pop within a single synchronous always block. The stack pointer (SP) will be a 3-bit register with 0 meaning full and 4 meaning empty, as before. On reset, initialize SP and clear stack memory and output. When enabled, if RW=0 (push) and not full, decrement SP and write dataIn; if RW=1 (pop) and not empty, read dataOut from stack_mem at SP and increment SP. This direct approach eliminates extra combinational signals and branching, making the code easier to follow while preserving correct functionality. Clearing the popped memory location is optional and omitted to reduce toggling. Flags EMPTY and FULL are combinationally derived from SP.
