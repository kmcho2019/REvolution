Implement the LIFObuffer as a circular buffer with a pointer indicating the current top element. Instead of decrementing SP, use a 2-bit pointer (top_idx) that points to the top element index in the 4-entry memory array. Maintain a separate count of valid entries (count) from 0 (empty) to 4 (full) to manage buffer state. On a write (push), increment count and update top_idx (wrap-around), storing dataIn at top_idx. On a read (pop), output the data at top_idx and decrement count and top_idx (wrap-around). Use combinational logic for EMPTY (count==0) and FULL (count==4). This approach uses circular indexing avoiding decrementing SP below zero, and explicitly tracks number of elements for clarity. It cleanly separates the top-of-stack pointer and the count, simplifying push/pop conditions and avoiding negative pointer values. The dataOut is registered on pop only. Reset clears memory, count, top_idx, and dataOut. All operations are synchronous with EN gating the push/pop. This circular buffer design provides a fresh architectural perspective distinct from stack-pointer decrement scheme.
