Use a 16-bit shift register to implement the 4x4-bit LIFO buffer. The 4-bit data words are concatenated as a 16-bit shift register representing the stack contents from top (LSB) to bottom (MSB). A 2-bit stack pointer (SP) counts how many entries are in the stack (0-4). On push (RW=0), if not full, shift the existing stack content left by 4 bits and insert dataIn at the bottom (least significant 4 bits), increment SP. On pop (RW=1), if not empty, output the 4-bit word at the bottom (LSB), shift the register right by 4 bits to remove it, and decrement SP. EMPTY is asserted when SP==0, FULL when SP==4. On reset, clear the shift register and SP. This approach avoids using a memory array, simplifies pointer management with a 2-bit counter, and reduces complexity and area. Flag signals are combinationally driven by SP.
