Simplify by using an up-counting stack pointer (SP) that points to the next free location (0 empty, 4 full). This eliminates the need for subtraction when pushing data. On push, store data at SP then increment SP; on pop, decrement SP then output data from SP. Update EMPTY and FULL flags directly from SP value. Use a single always_ff block with synchronous reset and enable, and keep memory clearing on reset. This approach simplifies pointer arithmetic and indexing, improving readability without changing functionality.
