Simplify the LIFObuffer by removing clock gating logic and extra intermediate signals to make the design straightforward and easy to understand. Use a simple stack pointer that counts from 0 (empty) up to 4 (full). On push (RW=0), data is stored at stack_mem[SP], then SP is incremented; on pop (RW=1), SP is decremented, and dataOut outputs the popped value. On reset, clear SP and dataOut only, without clearing the entire memory for simplicity, as the data is irrelevant when empty. EMPTY and FULL flags are generated based on the SP value. This keeps the design minimal while maintaining all required functionality.
