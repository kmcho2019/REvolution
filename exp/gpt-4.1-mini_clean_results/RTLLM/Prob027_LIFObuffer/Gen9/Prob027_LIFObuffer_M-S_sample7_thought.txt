Simplify the design by removing the unnecessary intermediate pop_data register and directly assign dataOut on pop operations. Use a single always block synchronous to the clock, with non-blocking assignments. Keep SP as a counter of the number of elements (0 to 4), incrementing on push and decrementing on pop. Update EMPTY and FULL flags combinationally from SP. On reset, clear SP and dataOut, but do not clear stack memory to reduce reset complexity. This reduces the number of registers and simplifies timing while maintaining functionality.
