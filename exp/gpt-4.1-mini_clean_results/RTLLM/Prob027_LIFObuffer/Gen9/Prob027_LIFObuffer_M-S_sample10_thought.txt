To simplify the LIFO buffer design while maintaining functionality, I will:

1. Use a 2-bit stack pointer (SP) counting entries from 0 to 4, as the stack depth is only 4.
2. Keep synchronous reset to clear SP and dataOut, but omit initialization of stack memory since it’s unnecessary.
3. Combine push and pop logic in one always block without separate enable signals.
4. Update EMPTY and FULL flags directly from SP in a simple combinational manner.
5. Perform push by writing dataIn at SP and incrementing SP; pop by decrementing SP and reading stack_mem at (SP - 1).
6. Avoid clearing stack_mem on pop to reduce toggling.
7. Remove extra wires or signals to simplify the code further.

This approach results in a straightforward, easy-to-read design that meets all functional requirements with minimal code complexity.
