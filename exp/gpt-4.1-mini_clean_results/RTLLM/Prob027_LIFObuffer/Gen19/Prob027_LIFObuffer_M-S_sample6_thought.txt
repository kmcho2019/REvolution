Implement the LIFObuffer as a simple synchronous design without an explicit FSM: on each rising clock edge when enabled, handle reset by initializing stack pointer and clearing memory; otherwise, perform push if RW=0 and not full or pop if RW=1 and not empty. Keep SP as a 3-bit pointer counting free spaces (4 means empty, 0 full). Update EMPTY and FULL flags combinationally.
