Implement the 4x4-bit LIFO buffer using a 4-entry memory array and a 3-bit stack pointer (SP). SP points to the current top of the stack, starting at 4 indicating empty. On a push (RW=0) when not full, decrement SP and store dataIn at stack_mem[SP-1]. On a pop (RW=1) when not empty, output data at stack_mem[SP], clear that memory location, then increment SP. On reset, initialize SP to 4 and clear all memory. EMPTY and FULL flags are combinationally driven by SP being 4 or 0, respectively. This avoids shifting wide registers, reduces switching, and simplifies access logic.
