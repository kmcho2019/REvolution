Simplify the design by using a stack pointer (SP) that points to the next free location in the stack memory. Initialize SP to 4 on reset to indicate empty (stack grows downward). On push (RW=0), decrement SP and write data at SP; on pop (RW=1), read data at SP and increment SP. Update EMPTY and FULL flags based on SP value. This approach uses fewer conditionals and direct indexing, keeping the code concise and intuitive while preserving functionality.
