Implement the 4-entry 4-bit LIFO buffer using a circular pointer approach where the stack pointer (SP) points to the current top index, initialized at 0xF (an invalid index meaning empty). Use a separate 2-bit count register to track the number of valid entries in the stack for easy FULL and EMPTY detection. On a write (push), increment the SP (wrapping around 0-3) and store dataIn at that position; increment count. On a read (pop), output data from SP position and decrement SP (wrapping), decrement count. All operations happen synchronously on the clock when EN is high and not reset. This decouples SP management from count, allowing simpler wrap-around logic and clearer FULL/EMPTY logic via count. Clearing memory on reset sets all entries to zero. This pointer-and-count circular approach differs architecturally from the usual decrement-on-push, increment-on-pop stack pointer model.
