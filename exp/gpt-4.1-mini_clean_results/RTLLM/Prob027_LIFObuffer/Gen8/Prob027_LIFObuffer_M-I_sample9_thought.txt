To optimize the LIFObuffer design for better PPA without altering its functionality, I will implement these improvements:

1. Pointer Encoding: Convert SP from binary to one-hot encoding to minimize glitches and improve timing on SP-related logic and memory addressing. This reduces dynamic power and glitching in SP logic.

2. Clock Gating: Introduce a clock enable (CE) signal derived from EN and valid operation conditions (can_push or can_pop) to gate register updates, reducing switching power by disabling unnecessary toggling when no operation occurs.

3. Simplify Reset: Only reset SP and dataOut synchronously; avoid clearing the entire memory array on reset since data is irrelevant when empty, which will reduce reset time and area.

4. Modify stack_mem writes: Only update stack_mem when pushing; do not clear on pop to reduce switching.

5. Adjust dataOut update: Update dataOut only on pop to reduce unnecessary toggling.

6. Use explicit non-blocking assignments and registers for SP in one-hot format, convert one-hot to index for stack_mem addressing during push/pop.

7. Maintain EMPTY and FULL as combinational signals for quick flag generation.

These changes will reduce dynamic power, improve timing by reducing glitches on SP logic, and simplify reset logic, improving overall PPA.
