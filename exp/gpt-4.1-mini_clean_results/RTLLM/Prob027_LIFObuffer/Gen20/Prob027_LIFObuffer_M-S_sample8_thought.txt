Simplify the LIFObuffer by:
1. Using a single always block triggered on the clock edge.
2. Implementing the stack pointer (SP) as a 3-bit register counting entries (0 to 4) where SP=0 means empty and SP=4 means full, to avoid subtraction in push.
3. On push (RW=0), if not full, write dataIn to stack_mem[SP], then increment SP.
4. On pop (RW=1), if not empty, decrement SP, then output stack_mem[SP].
5. Update EMPTY and FULL flags directly from SP.
6. Reset sets SP=0 (empty) and clears memory and output.
7. This approach eliminates the need for complex combinational next_SP calculations and makes pointer arithmetic intuitive and simpler.
