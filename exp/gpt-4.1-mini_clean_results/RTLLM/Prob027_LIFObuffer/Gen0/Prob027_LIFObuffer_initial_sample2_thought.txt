Implement a 4-entry 4-bit wide stack with a 3-bit stack pointer (SP) initialized to 4 indicating empty. On reset, clear the stack and set SP to 4. On each clock edge with EN high, perform push if RW=0 and not full by decrementing SP and writing dataIn to stack_mem[SP-1], or perform pop if RW=1 and not empty by reading dataOut from stack_mem[SP], clearing that location, and incrementing SP. Update FULL and EMPTY flags based on SP (FULL when SP=0, EMPTY when SP=4).
