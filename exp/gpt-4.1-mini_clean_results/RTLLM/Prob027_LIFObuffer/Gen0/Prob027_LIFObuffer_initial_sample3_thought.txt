Implement a 4-depth LIFO stack with 4-bit data width using a register array stack_mem and a 3-bit stack pointer (SP). On reset, initialize SP to 4 (empty) and clear memory. On enabled clock edges, if RW=0 (write) and not full, push dataIn by decrementing SP and writing into stack_mem[SP-1]. If RW=1 (read) and not empty, pop data by outputting stack_mem[SP], clearing that memory, and incrementing SP. Update FULL and EMPTY flags according to SP value.
