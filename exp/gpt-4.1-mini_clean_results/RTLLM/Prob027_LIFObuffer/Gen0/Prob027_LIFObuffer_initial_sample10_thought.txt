Implement a synchronous 4-entry 4-bit wide LIFO buffer using a stack pointer that starts at 4 (empty). On reset, clear the stack memory, set SP to 4, and update flags. On each clock when enabled, if RW=0 and not full, push dataIn by decrementing SP and storing data; if RW=1 and not empty, pop data by reading from SP and incrementing it, clearing the popped location. Update FULL and EMPTY flags based on SP value.
