Implement the ALU with a modular approach by separating arithmetic and logic operations into smaller helper functions inside the module, improving clarity and reuse. Use a 33-bit intermediate wire for addition and subtraction operations to capture carry. Compute overflow, carry, negative, zero, and flag signals explicitly and only when necessary to reduce toggling. For shift operations, support both immediate and variable shifts by decoding the opcode and using the correct shift amount. For LUI, shift the lower 16 bits of 'b' by 16 bits to conform to MIPS ISA semantics. The flag output is driven only for SLT/SLTU and is high-impedance (z) for others to clearly reflect its conditional validity, matching the original spec. Default outputs are assigned to high-impedance to explicitly detect invalid control codes in simulation. This approach structures the ALU into clear logical blocks, maintains correctness, and emphasizes selective flag assignment and clean default behavior.
