Refactor the ALU module to a purely combinational design using continuous assignments and intermediate wires instead of a single always block. This approach decouples each operation into separate wires, then selects the output based on aluc through a multiplexer implemented as a combinational expression. Flags such as carry, overflow, zero, negative, and flag (for SLT/SLTU) are computed alongside in similar style. This style improves clarity by isolating operation results and conditions, and is fully synthesizable without latches or high impedance outputs. It also avoids reassignments and allows easier pipeline insertion or further optimization by synthesis tools.
