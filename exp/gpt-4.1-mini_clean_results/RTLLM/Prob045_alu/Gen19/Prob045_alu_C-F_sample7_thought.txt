Fuse strengths of both designs by:
- Using a single combinational always block with blocking assignments for clarity.
- Resetting outputs at the start to avoid latches, but only compute carry and overflow for arithmetic ops to reduce unnecessary toggling and power consumption.
- Calculating carry and overflow precisely for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU using 33-bit arithmetic.
- Using signed wires only once for signed operations to avoid redundant casts.
- Assigning flag to 1 only for SLT and SLTU, and 0 otherwise (avoiding 'z' which is not synthesizable).
- Carefully handling shift amounts as per opcode specification.
- Assigning zero and negative flags based on final result.
- Defaulting outputs to zero rather than high impedance for synthesis friendliness.
- Avoiding redundant or overly complex logic; keeping the implementation minimal yet complete.
This fusion enhances clarity, power efficiency, and synthesis quality.
