Implement the ALU using separate small modules/functions for arithmetic and logic operations to enhance modularity and clarity. Use separate wires for the intermediate arithmetic result with carry-out, and for the arithmetic overflow detection, computed with signed operands for ADD/SUB. The shift operations will use both immediate and variable shift amounts distinctly. The flag output will be explicitly driven low for all operations except SLT and SLTU to avoid high-impedance states, and zero, carry, overflow, and negative flags will be consistently derived from the arithmetic or logical results. The default output will be all zeros. This modular and clear approach improves code readability and facilitates future enhancements or debugging.
