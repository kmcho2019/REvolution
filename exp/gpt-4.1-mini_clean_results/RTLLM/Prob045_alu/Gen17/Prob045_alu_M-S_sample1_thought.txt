Implement the 32-bit ALU as a fully combinational module with a single always_comb block to simplify and clarify logic. Use a 32-bit internal result reg and assign the output r from it directly. Compute carry and overflow only for arithmetic operations, defaulting them to zero otherwise to reduce unnecessary toggling. For shift operations, always use a[4:0] as shift amount. Set flag to 1 only for SLT and SLTU based on comparison results, else zero (avoid 'z'). Compute zero and negative flags by continuous assignment outside always block for clarity. For LUI, shift b (not a) left 16 bits as per MIPS convention. Default result and status flags to zero for undefined opcodes. This streamlined design reduces complexity, avoids redundant assignments, and keeps full functionality.
