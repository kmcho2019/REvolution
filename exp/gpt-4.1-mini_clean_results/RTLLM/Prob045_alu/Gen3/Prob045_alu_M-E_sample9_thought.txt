Implement the ALU as a fully combinational module that computes all possible operations in parallel using internal wires, then selects the final output based on the `aluc` opcode. This parallel computation approach simplifies control flow, reduces conditional logic, and enables easier pipelining or optimization by synthesis tools. 

To determine flags: 
- `zero` is assigned combinationally by checking if the final result is zero.
- `negative` is simply the MSB of the result.
- `carry` and `overflow` are only meaningful for arithmetic operations (ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU) and computed from corresponding internal adders/subtractors.
- The `flag` output is explicitly set only for SLT and SLTU and is high impedance otherwise.

Shift amounts come from `a[4:0]` or immediate zero shift for fixed shift instructions.

This approach avoids multiple procedural blocks and assignments by precomputing results of all instructions and flags, then multiplexing at the output stage. It reduces complexity and increases clarity.

High-impedance outputs are avoided; all outputs are always driven explicitly to stable values, enhancing synthesis friendliness and reducing power.

All operations and flags follow MIPS semantics precisely.
