Implement the ALU using a two-stage approach within a combinational block: first decode the operation type to categorize arithmetic, logic, shift, or compare operations, then perform the operation accordingly. Use intermediate signed and unsigned versions of operands for correct interpretation. Instead of relying on a single 33-bit adder for both addition and subtraction, implement subtraction as addition with two's complement to reuse logic. For shifts, unify handling by computing the shift amount from either the immediate or the variable operand and use a signed arithmetic shift for SRA variants. The carry flag will only be asserted for unsigned addition and subtraction operations to avoid ambiguity. The overflow flag is computed only for signed addition and subtraction. The flag output is high only for SLT and SLTU instructions; otherwise, it is zero (no high impedance). Zero, negative, carry, overflow, and flag signals are derived after operation result computation. All assignments are non-blocking inside an always_comb block to emphasize combinational intent and improve synthesis clarity. This design aims for clarity, reduced redundancy by categorizing operations, and consistent flag handling, differing from the example by structured decoding and unified shift handling.
