To improve PPA, the design will be optimized by:

1. Merging the two always blocks into one, reducing combinational logic duplication and simplifying signal assignments.
2. Using internal signed 33-bit registers for arithmetic operations to clarify carry and overflow computation, aiding synthesis and potentially improving timing.
3. Computing carry only for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU operations; defaulting carry to 0 elsewhere to avoid unnecessary toggling.
4. Setting flag output to 0 for non-SLT/SLTU instructions, avoiding high-impedance states and reducing output toggling.
5. Using non-blocking assignments (`<=`) in the combinational always block is uncommon and avoided; keep blocking for combinational logic.
6. Assign outputs directly from internal registers, avoiding separate always block.
7. Simplify shift operations by reusing b and a[4:0] without unnecessary signed casts except for arithmetic right shift.
8. For LUI, use the operand b as specified (upper 16 bits loaded into result's upper half).
9. Keep default case to zero outputs, preventing unknown states.

This should reduce logic, simplify paths, and improve timing and power.
