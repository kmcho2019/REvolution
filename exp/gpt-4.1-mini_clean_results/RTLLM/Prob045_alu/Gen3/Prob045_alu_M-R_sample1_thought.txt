Refactor the ALU to use combinational continuous assignments (`assign`) and intermediate wires instead of a big always block, improving readability and potentially enabling better synthesis optimizations. Separate arithmetic and logic operations clearly with wires for signed addition/subtraction, carry, and overflow calculations. Compute each operation in parallel with conditional muxing based on the opcode. Assign the outputs directly from these wires without intermediate registers, ensuring outputs are driven combinationally. The flag signal is driven combinationally from the SLT and SLTU comparisons and high-impedance otherwise by using a tri-state style signal (assign flag = ... ? 1'b1 : 1'b0 or 1'bz). This structure clarifies data flow, reduces use of registers, and eliminates the always block while preserving all functionality.
