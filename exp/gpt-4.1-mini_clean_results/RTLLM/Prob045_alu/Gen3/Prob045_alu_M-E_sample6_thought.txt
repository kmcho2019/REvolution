Use a modular design splitting the ALU operations into separate functional blocks for arithmetic, logic, and shifts, then multiplex their outputs based on the opcode. This approach isolates operation types, improving clarity and potential for reuse or parallelism. Compute carry and overflow only in arithmetic module. The SLT and SLTU flag is generated separately based on arithmetic and unsigned comparisons. The zero, negative flags are computed from the final result. The flag output is tri-state ('z') except when performing SLT or SLTU. This design allows a cleaner separation of concerns and could aid in pipelining or optimizing sub-blocks independently.
