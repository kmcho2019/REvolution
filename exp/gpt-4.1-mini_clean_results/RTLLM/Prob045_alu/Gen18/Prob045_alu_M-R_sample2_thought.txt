Refactor the ALU to a purely combinational module using continuous assignments and intermediate wires rather than an always block. Precompute each operation result in separate wires and then use a multiplexer based on `aluc` to select the final result and flags. This can simplify synthesis by allowing better parallelism and easier logic optimization. Carry and overflow are computed only for relevant arithmetic operations using dedicated wires. The `flag` output is driven directly from the comparison results for SLT and SLTU, and zero, negative flags derive directly from the selected result. This approach separates computation from selection, making the design more modular and potentially easier to pipeline or optimize.
