Refactor the ALU to use continuous assign statements for all outputs instead of an always block. This makes the design purely combinational with direct dataflow modeling, improving clarity and potentially reducing inferred latches or unnecessary registers. We'll create intermediate wires for each operation result, then use a combinational multiplexer (via a function or a big assign with a case expression) to select the appropriate output based on aluc. The flags (carry, overflow, negative, zero, flag) are derived combinationally from the selected result and opcode. This approach leverages Verilog's strength in combinational assignment, making the code concise, easy to verify, and potentially enabling better synthesis optimizations. Shift amounts will be extracted from a[4:0], and signed operations will use appropriate casting. The flag output will be 1 for SLT and SLTU, otherwise 0 (not 'z') to avoid high-impedance on outputs.
