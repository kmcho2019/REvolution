I will implement the ALU by splitting the logic into smaller specialized sub-blocks for arithmetic, logic, and shift operations to simplify flag calculations and increase modularity. The carry and overflow flags will be explicitly computed only for arithmetic operations (ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU). For SLT/SLTU, the flag output will be combinationally assigned outside the main case for clarity. Shifts will be handled by a dedicated shift module that takes the shift amount from either 'a' or 'b' depending on the opcode. The zero and negative outputs will be directly derived from the final result. If the opcode doesn't match any known operation, outputs will be driven to high impedance ('z'), per requirement. This modular approach enhances code clarity and maintainability, and isolates flag computations to relevant operations.
