To optimize the ALU for better power and area while maintaining correctness:

1. Update carry and overflow only for ADD, ADDU, SUB, and SUBU operations to avoid unnecessary toggling, which reduces power.
2. Correct the LUI implementation to match MIPS semantics by placing the immediate value in the upper 16 bits and zeros in the lower 16 bits: `r = {b[15:0], 16'b0};` (b is typically the immediate source in MIPS).
3. Use non-blocking assignments in combinational logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness, although blocking is acceptable for combinational logic; this is optional and depends on style.
4. Minimize assignments in the default case to only what is needed.
5. Cache repeated shift amounts (shamt) to reduce complexity.
6. Remove unnecessary casting and use signed arithmetic only where required.
7. Set flag to 'z' (high-impedance) when not SLT or SLTU as originally specified, to explicitly indicate inactive state.
8. Retain the 33-bit adder style for carry detection but only when necessary.

These changes reduce switching activity for non-arithmetic instructions and clarify intent, improving power and area metrics without sacrificing functionality.
