Compute all flags and the result inside this block, storing them in reg variables. The flags zero, negative, carry, overflow, and flag are updated in the always block based on the selected operation and its result. Using always_comb ensures the logic remains purely combinational. This approach is more traditional for ALU designs and makes the flow of control and flag updates explicit, improving readability for some users while maintaining functionality. Carry and overflow calculations remain accurate for ADD and SUB variants. For undefined opcodes, outputs are assigned 'z' to signify high-impedance. This style also centralizes all combinational logic in one procedural block, which may aid debugging and FPGA/ASIC synthesis tools.
