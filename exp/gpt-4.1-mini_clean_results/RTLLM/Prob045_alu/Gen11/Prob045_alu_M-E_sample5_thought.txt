Implement the ALU with a modular approach by separating arithmetic operations, logical operations, shift operations, and set-on-less-than computations into different intermediate wires. The final output is selected by a multiplexer driven by the opcode. Use dedicated logic for carry and overflow only for arithmetic instructions (ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU) to minimize toggling for other operations. For shift instructions, use variable shift amounts extracted from either the instruction or register as required. The flag is assigned only for SLT and SLTU and set to 'z' (high impedance) otherwise, as specified. Zero and negative flags are simple combinational outputs from the final result. For the LUI operation, correctly place the immediate value in the upper 16 bits with lower 16 bits zeroed. For unsupported operations, output all zeros instead of 'z' to avoid undefined outputs. This modularization improves code clarity, limits combinational complexity per operation, and explicitly isolates flag logic, improving maintainability and potential for optimization.
