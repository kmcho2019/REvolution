Simplify the ALU by merging arithmetic and logic operations into a single combinational block with minimal intermediate signals. Use direct signed and unsigned arithmetic operators without separate wires. Compute carry and overflow only for ADD and SUB variants using 33-bit addition/subtraction to capture carry-out. For shift operations, use shift amounts directly from `a[4:0]`. Assign the `flag` output only for SLT and SLTU, otherwise drive it low to avoid high-impedance. Set zero and negative flags by simple checks on the result. Default outputs to zero when opcode is unknown. This reduces complexity, uses fewer wires, and maintains all functional requirements.
