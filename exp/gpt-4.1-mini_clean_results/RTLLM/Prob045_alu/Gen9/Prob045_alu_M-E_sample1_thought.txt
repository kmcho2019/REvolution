I will implement the ALU by modularizing the arithmetic and logical operations and explicitly separating signed and unsigned operations. Instead of performing addition and subtraction in one combined unit, I will create separate adder and subtractor signals with direct addition and subtraction operators, relying on Verilog synthesis to optimize. 

Shift operations will be handled using Verilog shift operators directly, with the shift amount selected from the appropriate operand bits depending on the instruction (immediate shifts use 'a[4:0]', variable shifts use 'a[4:0]' as shift amount and 'b' as the value to shift).

The flag output will be strictly driven only for SLT and SLTU instructions, otherwise zero, avoiding high impedance or unnecessary toggling.

Carry and overflow are only meaningful for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU; for other operations, they will be zero.

Overflow detection will be done explicitly for signed add and sub using standard conditions.

Zero and negative flags will be generated from the result 'r'.

For LUI, the immediate is shifted left by 16 bits, so I will take the lower 16 bits of 'a' and shift them to the upper half of 'r'.

All unrecognized opcodes will output zero rather than 'z' for synthesis friendliness.

This approach yields a clean, readable, and well-structured ALU design with explicit, separated logic for arithmetic, logical, shift, and set operations, and clear flag outputs.
