To improve the PPA of the ALU design, focus on reducing unnecessary parallel computation switching and wiring overhead. Instead of computing all operations in parallel, selectively compute only the necessary operation based on the decoded opcode signals. This can be done by using a combinational always block with a case statement that assigns the result and flags only once based on the operation, rather than generating all results simultaneously and muxing them later. This approach reduces power by disabling unused logic and reduces area and wiring complexity. Additionally, refine the carry flag assignment so that it only updates on operations where it is meaningful, avoiding redundant logic. Keep the design fully combinational for simplicity, but structured for better synthesis. Keep flags consistent and avoid high-impedance outputs by assigning zero when the flag is not valid. This hybrid approach improves power and area with minimal impact on performance.
