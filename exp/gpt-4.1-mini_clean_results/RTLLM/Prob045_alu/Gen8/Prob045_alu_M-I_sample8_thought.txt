To improve the original design for better PPA and synthesis clarity, I will:

1. Replace the large if-else chain with a case statement to clarify operation decoding and enable the synthesis tool to optimize muxing better, potentially improving timing.

2. Compute only the necessary operation within each case to reduce unnecessary toggling, lowering dynamic power.

3. Share the adder-subtractor logic into a single 33-bit module by selecting add or subtract, reducing area.

4. Compute the carry and overflow only for arithmetic operations to avoid spurious toggling in logic and shift instructions.

5. Drive the flag output to 0 when not used (instead of high impedance) for synthesis friendliness and to avoid unnecessary toggling.

6. Combine shift computations by using a variable shift amount extracted from either 'a' or 'b' based on the opcode.

This approach preserves the original functionality while reducing area by shared adder-subtractor, lowering power by conditional computations, and improving timing clarity by a case statement.
