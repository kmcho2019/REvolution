Refactor the ALU to use purely combinational logic with continuous assign statements and intermediate wires instead of an always block. This approach decomposes each operation into separate wires, selects the output using a large multiplexer based on aluc, and calculates carry, overflow, negative, zero, and flag signals separately. This modular style improves readability, eases formal verification, and may help synthesis tools optimize datapaths more efficiently. The signed and unsigned operations use explicitly cast wires to prevent ambiguity. Carry and overflow are computed only for arithmetic ops, zero and negative flags derive from final result combinationally, and the flag is asserted only for SLT and SLTU. Default outputs fall to zero to avoid any high-impedance outputs on results or flags. The shift amounts are consistently extracted from a[4:0]. This structure yields a clean combinational datapath with clear signal dependencies and no latches.
