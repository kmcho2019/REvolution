Simplify the ALU design by merging all operations into a single combinational always block with one case statement, minimizing intermediate registers. Use a single 5-bit shift amount from a[4:0] for all shifts (fixed and variable) as they use the same bits. Compute carry and overflow only for signed ADD and SUB operations. For SLT and SLTU, compute flag and result directly within the case. Assign flag to 0 when not relevant instead of 'z' to reduce switching. Zero and negative flags are assigned directly from the final result. For unsupported opcodes, assign zero to all outputs, avoiding high-impedance states. This reduces complexity, area, and potential power, while keeping clear and concise code.
