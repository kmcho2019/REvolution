Implement the ALU using a modular approach where each operation result is precomputed in parallel with dedicated wires. Use a combinational multiplexer at the end to select the final result based on `aluc`. This approach leverages parallelism to minimize critical path delays. Calculate carry, overflow only for addition and subtraction operations. Use explicit signed and unsigned arithmetic where needed. For SLT/SLTU, derive the `flag` and embed the result in `r`. The zero, negative flags are generated from the selected result. The carry flag is valid only for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU; zero otherwise. The overflow flag is valid only for signed add/sub; zero otherwise. Shift operations use direct shift operators with variable shift amounts from `a` or `a[4:0]`. LUI places the lower 16 bits of `b` in the upper half and zeroes the lower half, as per MIPS convention. Unmatched opcodes result in `r` being zero with no flags set. This design improves timing by parallelizing all operations and then selecting output, reduces glitching by avoiding unnecessary recomputation inside the mux, and cleanly separates flag computations for clarity and maintainability.
