I will simplify the ALU by combining operations into a single combinational always block, using only necessary intermediate signals. Instead of separate carry and overflow for each operation, compute these only for add/sub instructions to minimize logic. The flag will be assigned combinationally for SLT/SLTU and set to zero otherwise as a wire, reducing unnecessary toggling. Shift amounts come from a[4:0] or b[4:0] depending on the instruction. The zero and negative flags will be directly derived from the result. For LUI, the immediate is taken from b's upper 16 bits (per MIPS convention). The default case sets outputs to zero for synthesis friendliness. This simplification reduces complexity, logic toggling, and improves code readability while preserving full ALU functionality.
