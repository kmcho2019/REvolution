Implement the ALU using separate combinational blocks for arithmetic and logic/shift operations, then multiplex the final result based on the opcode group to reduce critical path. Use a 33-bit adder/subtractor with carry out for ADD/ADDU/SUB/SUBU, explicitly handle overflow for signed ops, and separately compute SLT/SLTU flags. For shift instructions, build dedicated shift modules supporting variable and fixed amounts to isolate complexity and improve timing. The flag output is assigned only for SLT/SLTU instructions and high-impedance ('z') otherwise to explicitly reflect undefined state. Zero, carry, negative, and overflow are derived cleanly from the result and arithmetic logic. This modular approach improves readability, facilitates timing optimization by grouping related operations, and cleanly handles flags with tri-state output. The default output is high-impedance for unsupported opcodes to flag errors early.
