I will simplify the ALU by directly implementing operations in a single always_comb block without shared 33-bit arithmetic or explicit carry flags for every instruction. Instead, carry and overflow are only computed for ADD, ADDU, SUB, and SUBU to reduce complexity. For SLT and SLTU, the flag and result are set accordingly; for other operations, flag is zero. Shifts use only the relevant shift amount from `a[4:0]` or `b[4:0]` as specified. The zero, negative outputs are straightforwardly computed from the result. This approach uses less auxiliary signals and no extra signed wires, resulting in simpler, more readable, and smaller code while preserving full functionality.
