To optimize the ALU for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will use a combinational always block with a case statement, so only one operation is computed at a time, eliminating the power cost of all operations running in parallel. I will share the adder/subtractor logic for ADD/ADDU and SUB/SUBU operations to avoid duplicated 33-bit adders. For the LUI instruction, I will correctly place the immediate in the upper 16 bits with lower 16 bits zeroed, matching MIPS ISA semantics. For the flag output, I will drive it low (0) when it is not SLT or SLTU to avoid unnecessary switching caused by high impedance outputs. This approach reduces unnecessary toggling and improves synthesis for timing and power. The carry and overflow flags will be updated only for relevant operations. This design improves clarity, power efficiency, and area without sacrificing correctness.
