Implement the ALU using separate combinational blocks for arithmetic, logical, and shift operations, then select the final output using a multiplexer controlled by aluc. This modular approach clarifies the design and allows isolated optimization of each category. Arithmetic operations use 33-bit operands to capture carry out and detect overflow where applicable. Logical operations are straightforward bitwise operations. Shift operations are performed on the operand b, with shift amounts from either a[4:0] or fixed shift amount (for SLL). The 'flag' output is explicitly driven high only for SLT and SLTU instructions, otherwise low. Zero, negative, carry, and overflow flags are calculated based on the arithmetic output or result as appropriate. Using intermediate wires for arithmetic, logic, and shift results enables independent evaluation and simpler final selection logic. Defaults to zero outputs and zero flags for unsupported instructions, avoiding high impedance. This structure enhances readability and can facilitate future reuse or pipelining.
