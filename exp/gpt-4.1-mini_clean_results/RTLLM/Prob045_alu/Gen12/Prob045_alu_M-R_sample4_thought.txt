Refactor the ALU into purely combinational logic using continuous assignments (`assign`) instead of an always block. Compute all possible operation results in separate wires upfront, and then use a combinational multiplexer to select the final output based on `aluc`. Flags like zero, negative, carry, overflow, and flag are derived from the selected result or based on selected operations. This approach improves clarity by separating operation computation from result selection and avoids implicit latches. It also facilitates easier optimization by synthesis tools and can reduce glitching on outputs by minimizing internal conditional logic inside always blocks.
