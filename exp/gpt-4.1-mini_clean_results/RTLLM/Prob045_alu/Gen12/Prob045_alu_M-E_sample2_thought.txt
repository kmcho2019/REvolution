Implement the ALU using a fully parallel approach that precomputes all possible operation results simultaneously. Store these intermediate results in separate wires and then use a combinational multiplexer (case statement) to select the final output based on aluc. This removes dependencies on sequential logic or extended computations for flags, enabling straightforward flag assignments from precomputed results. 

Carry and overflow are computed only for arithmetic operations using dedicated wires for addition and subtraction extended to 33 bits for carry detection. For SLT and SLTU, flags are derived directly from comparator results rather than embedded in the output. Shifts use two types of shift amounts: fixed shift (lower 5 bits of b for fixed shifts like SLL) and variable shift (lower 5 bits of a for variable shifts like SLLV). LUI loads the immediate into the upper 16 bits as per the specification.

This parallel design keeps the combinational delay relatively balanced and simplifies flag logic, as all results and related flags are available upfront and only routed according to the opcode. Outputs default to zero or low impedance is avoided, improving robustness. This modularized computation also improves code readability and debuggability.
