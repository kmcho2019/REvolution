Simplify the ALU implementation by using a single always @(*) block with a case statement to directly compute the output and flags. Use minimal intermediate signals and unify shift amount extraction. Avoid high-impedance outputs by setting flag to 0 when not SLT/SLTU. Compute carry and overflow only for signed ADD and SUB; for unsigned operations carry is directly from extended addition/subtraction, overflow only applies to signed operations. Compute zero and negative flags directly from the result combinationally. This reduces logic duplication, simplifies control flow, and keeps the design functionally equivalent but easier to synthesize and maintain.
