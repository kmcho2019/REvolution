Implement the ALU by decomposing it into smaller functional blocks to modularize the logic and improve clarity: an adder/subtractor unit, a logic unit, a shift unit, and a comparator unit for SLT/SLTU. Use a multiplexer controlled by `aluc` to select which unit’s output is forwarded to the result. This reduces large monolithic case statements and lets each block be optimized separately. Flags like carry and overflow are computed only by the adder/subtractor; other operations clear those flags. The `flag` output is set only for SLT and SLTU, otherwise high impedance (`1'bz`) to strictly follow the spec. For LUI, the immediate operand is properly assigned to the upper 16 bits. This modular approach enhances readability and maintainability and can allow easier future extensions or optimizations.
