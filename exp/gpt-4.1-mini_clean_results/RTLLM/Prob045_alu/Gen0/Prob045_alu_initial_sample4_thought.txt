Implement a combinational ALU that performs the specified MIPS operations based on a 6-bit aluc control signal. Use parameters to define the opcodes. Perform arithmetic and logical operations, and determine flags such as zero, carry, negative, overflow, and the flag for SLT/SLTU. For arithmetic operations, take care of carry and overflow detection. For shift instructions, use either a fixed shift amount from b[4:0] or variable amount from a[4:0]. For LUI, shift b by 16 bits to the left. If an unknown aluc opcode is received, output high-impedance on r. Assign flag only for SLT and SLTU operations, else high impedance (z). The entire design will be purely combinational with no clock.
