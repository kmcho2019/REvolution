Implement an ALU module that takes two 32-bit inputs and a 6-bit opcode to perform MIPS-ISA operations. Use parameters for opcodes. For arithmetic operations, detect carry and overflow; for logic and shift operations, compute accordingly. For SLT/SLTU, set the flag output based on signed and unsigned comparisons. The result is a 32-bit output with flags zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag indicating SLT/SLTU results or 'z' otherwise. Use an always block with a case statement on aluc for the operations. For shifts, use either immediate shift amounts or variable shifts from 'a'. For LUI, shift 'b' upper 16 bits to left 16 bits. The outputs zero, negative, and flag are combinational, assigned based on the result and operation. Use an internal 33-bit wire for carry detection in addition/subtraction.
