Refactor the ALU module to use continuous assignments and combinational functions instead of a big always block. This approach leverages combinational functions for each operation, improving code modularity and readability. It avoids using blocking assignments and the big case statement inside always. Instead, the ALU operations are computed with dedicated functions and combined using a single multiplexer controlled by aluc. Flags such as carry, overflow, zero, negative, and flag are also assigned with combinational logic. This structure keeps the module purely combinational and functionally identical but uses a declarative and concise style with functions, improving maintainability and potential synthesis optimization.
