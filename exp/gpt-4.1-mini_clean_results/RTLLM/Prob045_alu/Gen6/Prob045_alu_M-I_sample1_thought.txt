To optimize the ALU for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will avoid computing all operations in parallel and instead calculate only the necessary operation based on the opcode. This reduces combinational logic and switching activity, improving power and area. For flags, I will use defined zeros instead of high-impedance to avoid synthesis warnings and reduce glitches. I will fix the LUI implementation to correctly use the upper 16 bits of 'a' concatenated with zeros as per specification. The carry and overflow logic will be computed only for relevant arithmetic ops. I will simplify and unify shift operations using a local variable for shift amount to reduce redundant logic. These changes will reduce resource usage, power consumption, and critical path length, while preserving functional correctness.
