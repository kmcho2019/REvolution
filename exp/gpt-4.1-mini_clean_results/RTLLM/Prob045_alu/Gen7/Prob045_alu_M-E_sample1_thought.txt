Implement the ALU by first computing all possible operation results in parallel using combinational logic. Then, use a multiplexer controlled by `aluc` to select the final output result and flags. This parallel computation approach ensures minimal delay from operand inputs to output result since all results are ready simultaneously, and only multiplexing delay is added. The carry and overflow flags are separately computed for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU. The flag output is only meaningful for SLT and SLTU; otherwise, it drives high-impedance (z) to strictly follow the spec. The zero and negative flags are derived from the selected result. This architecture simplifies debugging and can be friendly for formal verification by clearly separating operation computation and result selection stages.
