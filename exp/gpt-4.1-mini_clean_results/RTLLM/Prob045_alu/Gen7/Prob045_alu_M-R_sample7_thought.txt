Refactor the ALU design to use continuous assign statements combined with intermediate wires instead of a single always block. This approach splits computation into smaller combinational expressions per operation, enabling better parallelism and potentially improved synthesis results. The main output 'r' and flags will be driven by a multiplexer selecting the precomputed operation results based on aluc. Carry and overflow flags are computed only for ADD, ADDU, SUB, SUBU similarly as before. Flag for SLT/SLTU is set accordingly. This structure reduces the use of registers and blocking assignments, promoting a purely combinational and declarative style with clear separation of concerns.
