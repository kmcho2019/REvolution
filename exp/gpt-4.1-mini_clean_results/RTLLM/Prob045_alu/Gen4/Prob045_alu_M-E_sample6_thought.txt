Implement the ALU by splitting arithmetic and logic operations into separate combinational blocks, using intermediate signals for each category and then muxing the final result based on the opcode. Use explicit signed arithmetic for signed operations and carefully compute carry and overflow only for arithmetic instructions. The shift amount is always taken from either the lower 5 bits of 'a' or variable shift encoded in 'a'. The 'flag' output is only asserted (1) for SLT and SLTU; otherwise, it's high-impedance ('z') as originally requested. Zero, negative, carry, and overflow signals are directly derived from the selected final result. This separation enhances clarity and modularity, allowing easier extension or optimization of each operation category independently.
