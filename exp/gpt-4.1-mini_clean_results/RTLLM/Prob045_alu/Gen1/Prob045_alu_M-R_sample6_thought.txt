Refactor the ALU to be purely combinational using continuous assignments instead of an always block with a case statement. Calculate all possible operation results in separate wires and then use a combinational multiplexer (case on aluc) to select the final outputs. This approach clarifies the dataflow, avoids implicit latches, and makes explicit which signals are affected by which operation. Flag and status signals are derived accordingly. Carry and overflow are only set for arithmetic ops; flag is zero when not used instead of 'z' to avoid high-impedance issues. Shift amounts come from a[4:0] as before.
