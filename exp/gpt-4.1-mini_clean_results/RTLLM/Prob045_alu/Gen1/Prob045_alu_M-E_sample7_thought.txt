Design a 32-bit MIPS ALU using a modular approach where arithmetic and logic computations are separated into sub-results first, then a multiplexer selects the final result based on aluc opcode. For carry and overflow, explicitly compute for ADD, SUB, and related instructions using extended width addition/subtraction and signed comparisons. The flag output is conditionally assigned only for SLT and SLTU, otherwise zero to avoid high-impedance states. Shifts are implemented by decoding the aluc and using the corresponding shift amount from either a fixed b[4:0] (for immediate shifts) or variable a[4:0] (for variable shifts). LUI forms the result by concatenating b's upper 16 bits and lower 16 bits zeros (to avoid confusion, since in MIPS LUI loads immediate into upper half, use b[15:0] << 16). The zero flag is combinationally derived from the final result, negative from MSB of result, carry and overflow are registered. Use a single always_comb block to compute all outputs without defaulting to high-impedance, making the module safe and predictable. This architecture is clean, easy to maintain, and avoids multiple signal assignments by gathering all computations then selecting output.
