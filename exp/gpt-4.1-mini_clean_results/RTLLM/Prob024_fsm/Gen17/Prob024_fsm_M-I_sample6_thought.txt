To optimize the FSM for better PPA while maintaining correctness and continuous overlapping detection, I will convert the one-hot FSM to a binary-encoded FSM. This reduces the number of flip-flops from 5 to 3, lowering area and potentially power. 

I will implement the Mealy FSM using a state machine with states numbered 0 to 5, representing the length of the matched prefix of the sequence "10011" (length=5). The transitions follow standard pattern matching logic with overlap support.

The MATCH output will be asserted combinationally when the input completes the full sequence at the right state transition.

Binary encoding simplifies the next state and output logic, reducing combinational complexity and area, improving timing due to less routing and logic depth.

In addition, I will use a synchronous reset and a clean case statement for next state and output logic for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
