To improve the existing one-hot Mealy FSM design for detecting the sequence "10011" and optimize its PPA (performance, power, area), I will:

1. Switch from one-hot encoding (5 bits) to binary state encoding (3 bits), reducing flip-flop count from 5 to 3, thus saving area and dynamic power due to fewer toggling bits.

2. Use a case statement for next state logic to simplify combinational logic and enable better synthesis optimizations.

3. Implement the Mealy output MATCH as a combinational condition derived from the current state and input IN, just like before.

4. Maintain explicit reset and synchronous state update on posedge CLK, asynchronous active-high reset.

This binary encoding reduces area and power, with minimal impact on timing due to smaller register and simpler next state logic. The FSM's correctness will remain intact, and continuous overlapping detection will be supported as required.
