To simplify the FSM design while maintaining correct detection of the sequence "10011" with overlapping support, I will:

1. Use a minimal set of states representing the count of matched bits in the sequence.
2. Simplify the next-state logic using straightforward if-else conditions rather than a case statement.
3. Keep a binary encoding of states to minimize registers.
4. Implement MATCH as a Mealy output, asserted when the last '1' in the sequence is input and the FSM is in the penultimate state.
5. Keep synchronous reset and simple nonblocking assignments.

This approach reduces code verbosity and keeps the design easy to understand and synthesize.
