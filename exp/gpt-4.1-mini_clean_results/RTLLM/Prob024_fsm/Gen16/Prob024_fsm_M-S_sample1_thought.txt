I will implement a binary-encoded Mealy FSM with 6 states representing the progress of matching the sequence "10011". This reduces the state register width and simplifies the next state logic by using a case statement. The FSM will update state on every clock edge, reset synchronously, and output MATCH combinationally when the input completes the sequence at the appropriate state and input. Overlapping is naturally supported by transitions going back to appropriate states. This approach simplifies the design and reduces combinational complexity while preserving full functionality.
