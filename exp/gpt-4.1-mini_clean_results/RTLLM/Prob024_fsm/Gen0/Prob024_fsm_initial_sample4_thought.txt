Implement a Mealy FSM with states representing the progress of detecting the sequence "10011". Each clock cycle, on the positive edge, the FSM transitions based on the input bit IN. If the reset is asserted, the FSM resets to the initial state. The output MATCH goes high when the last bit of the sequence (the 5th bit '1' in "10011") is detected, and the FSM supports overlapping matches by transitioning to the appropriate state after detection. This implementation uses one-hot or encoded states to keep track of progress through the sequence for clarity and efficiency.
