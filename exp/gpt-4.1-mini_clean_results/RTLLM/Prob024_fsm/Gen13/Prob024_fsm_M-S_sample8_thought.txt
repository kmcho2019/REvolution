To simplify the FSM while maintaining functionality, use binary encoding of the states instead of one-hot. This reduces the number of flip-flops from 5 to 3. The next state logic will still clearly represent progress through the "10011" sequence, and MATCH output is asserted in the Mealy style when the last input matches the final required bit and FSM is in the final state. Use synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments for clarity and synthesis friendliness. This approach reduces complexity and area while keeping straightforward code and no redundant states.
