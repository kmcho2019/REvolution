To improve the original one-hot FSM design for better PPA, I will:

1. Switch from one-hot encoding to 3-bit binary encoding:
   - This reduces flip-flop count from 5 to 3, lowering area and dynamic power.
   - Although combinational next-state logic is slightly more complex, it remains straightforward with a case statement.

2. Keep synchronous reset for state register only.

3. Maintain Mealy output assertion on current state and input.

4. Add clear comments for readability and maintainability.

This approach will reduce area and power significantly by minimizing flip-flops while keeping timing reasonable due to a simple FSM and modest combinational complexity. The output MATCH is generated combinationally as before for immediate response. This strikes a good balance among performance, power, and area.
