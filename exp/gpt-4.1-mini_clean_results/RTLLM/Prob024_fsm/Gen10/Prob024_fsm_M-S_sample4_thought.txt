To simplify the FSM while preserving its functionality, I will use a straightforward 5-state Mealy FSM with clear binary encoding and direct state transitions to detect the sequence "10011". I will avoid additional clock enables or delayed input tracking to reduce complexity. The MATCH output will be asserted combinationally when the FSM reaches the final matching state and the input is '1'. The state register will update synchronously on CLK with an active-high synchronous reset.

This design uses a clean case statement for next state logic, straightforward sequential logic for state updates, and a simple Mealy output condition, minimizing the overall logic and improving readability and maintainability.
