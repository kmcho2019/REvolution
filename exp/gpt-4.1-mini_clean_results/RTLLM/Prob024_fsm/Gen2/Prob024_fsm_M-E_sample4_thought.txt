Use a shift register of length 5 to store the last five input bits synchronously on each clock edge. On every clock, shift in the current IN value and compare the stored bits to the target sequence "10011". Because this is a Mealy machine, assert MATCH=1 combinationally when the shifted sequence matches, synchronized with CLK. Also, implement synchronous reset to clear the shift register and the output.
