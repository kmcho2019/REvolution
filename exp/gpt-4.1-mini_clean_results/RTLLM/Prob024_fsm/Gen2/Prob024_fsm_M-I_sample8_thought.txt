To improve the FSM design for better performance, power, and area while maintaining functional correctness:

1. Separate next_state logic and output logic: Use one combinational always block to determine next_state and another purely combinational block for MATCH output. This reduces combinational path complexity and glitch potential.

2. Register MATCH output: Make MATCH synchronous to CLK with asynchronous reset to reduce glitches and minimize power by avoiding combinational toggling of output.

3. Simplify state transitions and use localparam for state encoding for better tool support and minimal overhead.

4. Carefully define state transitions to ensure proper overlap detection and correct sequence detection.

5. Optimize conditional logic in next_state to avoid redundant conditions and improve synthesis quality.

This approach yields a clean two-process FSM with registered outputs, typically better for timing and power without sacrificing functionality.
