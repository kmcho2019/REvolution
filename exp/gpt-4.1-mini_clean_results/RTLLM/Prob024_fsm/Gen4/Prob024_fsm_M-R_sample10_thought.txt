I will refactor the Mealy FSM by implementing the state register using a one always block (sequential logic) and moving the next-state and output (MATCH) logic into continuous assignments (`assign`). The states remain binary encoded with the same encoding and transitions, but by separating combinational next-state logic and output into `assign` statements outside an always block, the design becomes more concise and emphasizes the dataflow nature of combinational logic. This style can improve readability and synthesis interpretation, while preserving the FSM behavior. 

The output MATCH will be an assign statement directly derived from current state and input. The synchronous reset and state update remain inside the always block triggered on positive clock edge. This approach demonstrates a clean separation of sequential and combinational logic.
