To improve PPA, I will implement a sequential shift-and-add multiplier instead of a fully combinational one. This design will use a single adder and an accumulator register updated over 8 clock cycles, reducing area significantly by reusing hardware. It will also reduce switching activity and power since partial sums are computed iteratively. The design requires a clock and synchronous logic, so I will add a start signal and a ready output to indicate when the product is valid. This FSM-based approach trades latency for lower area and power, with simpler combinational logic per cycle and reduced overall switching. The product output is valid after the computation completes. This design is suitable for PPA-sensitive applications.
