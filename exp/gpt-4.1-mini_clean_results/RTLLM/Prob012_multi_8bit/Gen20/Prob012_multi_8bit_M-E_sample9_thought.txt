Implement the 8-bit multiplier as an iterative shift-and-add sequential multiplier using an internal register to accumulate the product. The design uses a clock and reset (added internally for a controlled iterative operation) and processes the multiplier bit-by-bit each clock cycle. For each cycle, if the least significant bit of the multiplier is 1, the multiplicand is added to the product accumulator. Then the multiplicand is shifted left, the multiplier is shifted right, and the process repeats for 8 cycles. The final product is available at the output after processing all bits. This approach reduces combinational logic depth by spreading the addition operations across cycles, optimizing area and potentially power at the cost of latency.
