To optimize the original linear chain of additions, I will implement a balanced adder tree that sums the partial products in a logarithmic depth manner instead of sequentially. This reduces the critical path significantly, improving timing (lower WNS and higher maximum frequency) and power due to less glitching from long carry chains. The area may increase slightly due to more adders running in parallel, but the trade-off is justified for better performance and lower power. The module remains purely combinational and synthesizable. This structured approach also eases timing closure for high-speed designs.
