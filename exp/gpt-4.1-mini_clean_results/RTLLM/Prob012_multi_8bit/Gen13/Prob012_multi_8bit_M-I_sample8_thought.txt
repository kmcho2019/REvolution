To optimize the original combinational multiplier for better performance and potentially lower power/area, I will restructure the summation of partial products into a balanced adder tree. Instead of summing all eight partial products in a single statement, I will pairwise add them in stages, reducing the longest combinational path and decreasing the critical path delay. This approach maintains a fully combinational design with zero latency but better timing characteristics and possibly less simultaneous switching power. The design remains synthesizable and keeps the shift-and-add method clear.
