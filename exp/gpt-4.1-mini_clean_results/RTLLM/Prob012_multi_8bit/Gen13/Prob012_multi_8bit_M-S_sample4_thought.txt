Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a straightforward combinational shift-and-add approach without generating all partial products explicitly or using an adder tree. Iterate through each bit of multiplier B, and if the bit is set, add the multiplicand A shifted left by the bit position to an accumulator. This simple loop-based approach is easy to understand and has less structural complexity, though it may have a longer critical path than a balanced tree. The design is purely combinational and outputs the 16-bit product directly.
