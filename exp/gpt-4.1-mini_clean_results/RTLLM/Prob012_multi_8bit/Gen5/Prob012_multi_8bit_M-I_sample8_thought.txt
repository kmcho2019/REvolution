To optimize the original design for better PPA (performance, power, area), we adopt a balanced adder tree approach for summing partial products instead of a linear chain. This reduces the critical path delay by performing additions in a log-depth manner, improving timing. Additionally, we pre-extend the multiplicand once to avoid repeated concatenations, slightly reducing area. The design remains purely combinational but with a more efficient structure for summing partial products. This optimization reduces the long carry chain of additions and can lower power by reducing switching on the longest path.
