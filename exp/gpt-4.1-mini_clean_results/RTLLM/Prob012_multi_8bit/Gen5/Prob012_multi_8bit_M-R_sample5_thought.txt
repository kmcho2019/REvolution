Refactor the multiplier by using a procedural block that sequentially checks each bit of B and accumulates the shifted multiplicand into the product register. This approach replaces the generate-for block and multiple intermediate wires with a single combinational always block. It models the shift-and-add multiplication explicitly as a loop within an always block, iterating through all bits of B and adding A shifted accordingly when the bit is set.
