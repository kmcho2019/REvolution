Implement the multiplier with a simple procedural block using a for-loop that iterates over each bit of the multiplier B. For each set bit in B, shift the multiplicand A by the bit index and add it to an accumulator variable representing the product. This approach directly models the shift-and-add method in a straightforward, easy-to-understand manner without extra hierarchical adder trees or generate blocks. The output product is assigned combinationally from the accumulator at the end of the loop.
