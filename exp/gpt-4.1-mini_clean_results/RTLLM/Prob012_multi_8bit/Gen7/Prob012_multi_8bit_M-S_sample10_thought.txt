Implement a simpler combinational multiplier by iterating through each bit of multiplier B using a procedural loop inside an always_comb block. For each set bit in B, add the multiplicand A shifted by the bit index to an accumulator (product). This directly models the shift-and-add multiplication with minimal hardware and simpler code, at the cost of a longer combinational path. The output product is assigned from the accumulator after the loop completes. This approach avoids multiple adder stages and generates a straightforward, easy-to-understand multiplier.
