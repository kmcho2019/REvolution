To improve the given shift-and-add multiplier design, I will optimize the addition of partial products by implementing a balanced adder tree rather than a linear chain. This reduces the critical path from 8 sequential additions to approximately log2(8)=3 levels of additions, improving timing (WNS and TNS), potentially lowering power by reducing switching activity propagation delay, and can also reduce area due to less fan-out and better logic sharing.

The strategy:
- Generate partial products similarly by shifting the multiplicand based on the multiplier bits.
- Sum the partial products in pairs recursively to form a balanced binary tree until a single final sum remains.
- This reduces the longest combinational delay path compared to a ripple-like linear summation.
- The final sum is assigned as the product output.

This approach preserves the shift-and-add concept while improving timing and power efficiency for combinational implementation.
