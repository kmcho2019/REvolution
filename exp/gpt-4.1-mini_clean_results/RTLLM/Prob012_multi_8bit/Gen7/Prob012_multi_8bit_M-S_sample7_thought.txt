Implement a straightforward combinational multiplier using a procedural for-loop inside an always block to perform the shift-and-add multiplication. Initialize the product to zero, then iterate through each bit of the multiplier B. If a bit is set, add the appropriately shifted multiplicand A to the product. This approach is simpler, avoids generating multiple partial products and a balanced adder tree, and directly follows the classical shift-and-add algorithm. It remains purely combinational by using an always @(*) block, making the design simpler and easier to understand while maintaining full functionality.
