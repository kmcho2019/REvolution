Fuse the clarity and conditional partial product generation of Example 1 with the explicit and structured addition chaining of Example 2, but improve further by implementing a balanced binary adder tree to sum the partial products. This reduces the critical path compared to both a single multi-operand addition and a long linear addition chain.

Specifically:
- Generate all 8 partial products in a clean generate loop, each a shifted version of A masked by the corresponding bit of B.
- Store partial products in an array of 16-bit wires.
- Sum partial products pairwise in stages (balanced binary tree) to reduce adder depth and improve timing.
- Pre-extend A once outside the generate block to avoid repeated concatenations.
- Keep the design fully combinational, using continuous assignments for synthesis friendliness.

This approach combines the shift-and-add principle, clarity of code, and improved performance (shorter adder tree depth), making it a better solution for PPA while maintaining correctness.
