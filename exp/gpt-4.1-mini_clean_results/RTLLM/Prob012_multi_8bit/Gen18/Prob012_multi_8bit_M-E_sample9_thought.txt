Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add algorithm controlled by a clock and reset signal. This approach mimics a classic hardware multiplier: on each clock cycle, it checks the least significant bit of the multiplier B, adds the multiplicand A to an accumulator if the bit is 1, then shifts A left by one and B right by one. This repeats for 8 cycles until the entire multiplier is processed. Using a sequential approach significantly reduces hardware area compared to fully combinational multiplication. The module uses an internal state machine to perform multiplication in 8 clock cycles, producing the 16-bit product output at the end. The product output is registered and stable after the operation completes. This approach clearly demonstrates shift-and-add with explicit iterative behavior suitable for FPGA or ASIC implementation.
