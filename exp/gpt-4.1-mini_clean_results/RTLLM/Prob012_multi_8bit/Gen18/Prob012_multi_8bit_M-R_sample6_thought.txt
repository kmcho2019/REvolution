Refactor the multiplier to a fully combinational design using a simple for-loop inside an always_comb block to accumulate the shifted multiplicand when the corresponding multiplier bit is set. This approach avoids the generate constructs and intermediate partial product arrays, instead directly accumulating the result in a procedural block. It preserves the shift-and-add concept and is clear, concise, and easy to understand. The product is computed by iterating through each bit of B, adding A shifted by the bit index when that bit is 1.
