I will implement a purely combinational 8-bit multiplier using a sequential iterative shift-and-add method unrolled in combinational logic. Instead of generating all partial products at once and summing them, I will simulate the multiplication process by iterating through each bit of B, and accumulate the partial sums by conditionally adding the shifted multiplicand (A shifted left by the bit index) when that bit of B is set. This approach mimics a loop unrolled in hardware and uses a single accumulate addition chain with conditional adders, preserving clarity and illustrating the shift-and-add principle explicitly. The product will be built by successively adding shifted versions of A based on each bit of B.
