I will implement a sequential 8-bit multiplier using a shift-and-add algorithm controlled by a finite state machine. Instead of a fully combinational approach, this design will use a clock and perform one bit multiplication per clock cycle. The multiplicand is added to an accumulator when the current multiplier bit is 1, then the multiplier is shifted right and the accumulator shifted left accordingly. This iterative method uses fewer hardware resources (a single adder and registers), trading off latency (8 cycles) for area and power efficiency. The final product will be output after all bits of the multiplier are processed. This approach is well suited for low-area and low-power designs.
