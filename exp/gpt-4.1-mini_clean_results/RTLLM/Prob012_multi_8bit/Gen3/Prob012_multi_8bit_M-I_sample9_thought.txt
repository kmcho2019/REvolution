To improve the PPA (power, performance, and area), convert the combinational multiplier into a sequential multiply-accumulate design using a clock and reset. This will spread the computation over multiple clock cycles, significantly reducing the combinational critical path and dynamic power consumption by performing one addition per cycle. The module will have a start signal to begin multiplication, a done signal to indicate completion, and internal registers to hold intermediate results. This sequential approach uses a simple FSM and an 8-bit counter to iterate through each bit of B, adding shifted A to the product when the corresponding bit of B is set, then shifting A left after each step. This balances area and power while improving timing by reducing combinational depth.
