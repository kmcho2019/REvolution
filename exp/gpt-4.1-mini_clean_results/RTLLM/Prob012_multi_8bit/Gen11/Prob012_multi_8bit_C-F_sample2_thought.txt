I will implement a fully combinational 8-bit multiplier using the shift-and-add method that combines the clarity and efficiency of partial product generation with a balanced adder tree summation to optimize timing, power, and area.

Fusion strategy:
- Generate each partial product by conditionally shifting the multiplicand A according to each bit of the multiplier B.
- Use a balanced adder tree that sums partial products in pairs over multiple levels, reducing the addition depth from 7 (linear chain) to 3.
- This approach reduces critical path delay, switching activity, and fan-out, leading to better PPA metrics while keeping the design purely combinational and straightforward.
- I will explicitly declare all wires for readability and synthesis friendliness.
- The implementation avoids any sequential logic or multiplier primitives, maintaining a clear structural style that facilitates optimization and verification.

This design efficiently realizes the shift-and-add multiplication concept with balanced summation, achieving a good tradeoff between performance, power, and area.
