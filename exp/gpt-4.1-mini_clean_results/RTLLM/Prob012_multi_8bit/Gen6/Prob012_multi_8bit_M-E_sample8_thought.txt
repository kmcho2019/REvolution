Implement the multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add algorithm in a purely combinational way by simulating the iterative process with a for-loop inside a combinational always_comb block. We'll initialize product to zero and use a temporary variable to hold the shifted multiplicand. For each bit of B (from LSB to MSB), if the bit is set, add the current shifted multiplicand to the product. Then shift the multiplicand left by one for the next bit. This approach models the classical manual multiplication process in a concise way, avoids generating all partial products separately, and sums progressively in a loop, providing a clean and clear implementation.
