Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add algorithm within a clocked process. The design uses a simple FSM with registers: a product register initialized to zero and a temporary multiplier register initialized to B. On each clock cycle, if the least significant bit of the multiplier register is 1, add the multiplicand A (appropriately shifted) to the product. Then shift the multiplier right by one and the multiplicand left by one, effectively accumulating partial products over 8 cycles. This iterative approach mimics the manual multiplication algorithm, reduces combinational path length, and is efficient for hardware implementation.
