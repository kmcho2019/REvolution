Implement the 8-bit multiplier using an iterative shift-and-add approach in a fully combinational manner. Instead of generating all partial products in parallel, the design will simulate the classical manual multiplication by iterating over each bit of the multiplier B, and conditionally adding the shifted multiplicand A to an accumulator (product) if the current bit of B is 1. This approach will explicitly perform shift and add operations inside a for-loop unrolled at compile time, accumulating the final result combinationally. This method is functionally simple, transparent, and clearly demonstrates the shift-and-add multiplication principle without relying on parallel partial product generation.
