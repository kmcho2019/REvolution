Implement the multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add algorithm controlled by a clock and reset signal inside a simple FSM. The multiplicand (A) is conditionally added to an accumulator if the current bit of the multiplier (B) is 1. Then A is shifted left, and the multiplier is shifted right. This process iterates 8 times, after which the product register holds the final 16-bit product. The module provides a synchronous design with start and done handshaking signals for controlled operation. This approach reduces combinational complexity and can be easily pipelined or clocked for power and area optimization.
