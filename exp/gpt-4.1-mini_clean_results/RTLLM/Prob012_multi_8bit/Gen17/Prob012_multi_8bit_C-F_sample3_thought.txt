Fuse the modular clarity of generating partial products with conditional shifting from Example 1 and the balanced adder tree summation from Example 2, but further improve resource efficiency by avoiding redundant shifting of the multiplicand each time.

- Pre-extend the 8-bit multiplicand A to 16 bits once.
- Generate partial products by ANDing the extended multiplicand with single-bit masks derived from B bits shifted into position, thus avoiding large shifts of A.
- This approach reduces logic complexity for shifting: instead of shifting A by i bits, shift the enable bit of B and mask, then multiply by A once.
- Use a balanced binary tree adder tree to sum partial products, reducing adder depth and improving timing.
- The design remains fully combinational for simplicity and synthesis friendliness.
- This fusion retains correctness, improves clarity, and could reduce switching activity and area by minimizing shifted data generation.

This achieves a neat, balanced design with efficient partial product creation and addition stages, combining the best features of both examples.
