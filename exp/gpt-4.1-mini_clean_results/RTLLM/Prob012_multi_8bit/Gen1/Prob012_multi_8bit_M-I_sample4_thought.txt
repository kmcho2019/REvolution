To improve PPA, implement the multiplier as a sequential shift-and-add multiplier controlled by a clock and reset (synchronous design). This reduces combinational delay by performing one addition and one shift per clock cycle rather than all additions combinationally in parallel. It reduces the critical path, lowering maximum delay, and also reduces dynamic power by limiting switching to one addition per cycle. Area is reduced by reusing a single adder instead of a large parallel adder tree.

The design uses an internal state machine to iterate over 8 cycles, accumulating partial sums when the current bit of B is set. At the end of 8 cycles, the product output is ready. This design is more hardware-friendly for FPGA or ASIC implementation.

Inputs remain combinational; outputs are registered. Added clk and rst inputs for synchronous operation.
