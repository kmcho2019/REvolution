Refactor the combinational multiplier to use a procedural always_comb block to implement the shift-and-add algorithm explicitly. Instead of generating all partial products and summing them at once, perform the multiplication iteratively inside the always block: initialize the product to zero and then, for each bit of B, add the shifted A to the product if the corresponding bit is 1. This approach models the multiplication process sequentially within a combinational block without using generate or continuous assignment of partial sums. It provides a clear, step-by-step hardware description of the shift-and-add method while remaining fully combinational.
