Refactor the design by implementing the shift-and-add multiplication procedurally within an always block, iterating over each bit of the multiplier to add shifted multiplicand selectively. This creates a behavioral, easily understandable implementation of the shift-and-add algorithm, as opposed to generating all partial products simultaneously and summing them in a balanced tree.

Key points:
- Use a combinational always block sensitive to A and B.
- Initialize the product to zero.
- For each bit of B, if the bit is set, add the multiplicand shifted left by the bit index to the product.
- This mimics the traditional shift-and-add multiplication explicitly.
- The result is assigned to the output product register.

This structure is intuitive, easy to modify for iterative or pipelined extensions, and synthesizes efficiently as a combinational multiplier using shift and add operations.
