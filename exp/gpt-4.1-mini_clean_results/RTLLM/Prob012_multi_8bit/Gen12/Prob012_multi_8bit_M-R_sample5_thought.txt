To refactor the original combinational multiplier with balanced adder tree, I will implement the shift-and-add multiplier using a procedural (combinational) always block with a for-loop. This approach directly simulates the shift-and-add multiplication by iterating through each bit of B inside the always block, conditionally adding the shifted A to a running product variable.

Advantages:
- More straightforward and intuitive implementation of shift-and-add logic.
- Eliminates the need for explicitly declaring and summing multiple partial product wires.
- Clear visibility of the multiplication algorithm flow and easy to understand.
- Keeps the design purely combinational without clocking or state machines.

This implementation follows the classic shift-and-add multiplication algorithm by accumulating partial sums as we iterate through multiplier bits.
