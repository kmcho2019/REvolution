To optimize the combinational 8-bit multiplier for better performance and area, I will implement a balanced adder tree to sum the partial products in stages rather than summing all 8 partial products at once. This reduces the critical path length by lowering the number of operands added simultaneously. The adder tree sums pairs of partial products stepwise until the final product is obtained, improving timing and potentially lowering dynamic power since intermediate sums are smaller and more localized. This approach retains the fully combinational, shift-and-add algorithm without using sequential elements, thus keeping zero clock latency but improving the PPA metrics.
