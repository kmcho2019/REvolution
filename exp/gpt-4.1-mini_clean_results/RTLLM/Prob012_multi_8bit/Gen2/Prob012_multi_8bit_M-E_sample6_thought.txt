Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add algorithm within a clocked process, mimicking a classic hardware multiplier. The module includes an enable and clock input, accepts inputs A and B synchronously, and computes the product iteratively over 8 clock cycles. In each cycle, if the least significant bit of the current multiplier is 1, add the multiplicand to an accumulator. Then shift the multiplicand left and the multiplier right by one bit for the next iteration. After all bits are processed, the final product is available. This approach reduces combinational path delay and can easily be pipelined or extended with handshake signals.
