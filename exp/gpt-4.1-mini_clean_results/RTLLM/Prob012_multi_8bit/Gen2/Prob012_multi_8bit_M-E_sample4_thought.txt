Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add approach with a simple FSM in a clocked process. The multiplicand (A) and multiplier (B) are loaded at start. On each clock cycle, check the LSB of the multiplier; if it is 1, add the multiplicand to an accumulator. Then shift the multiplicand left and shift the multiplier right to process the next bit. After 8 cycles, output the 16-bit product. This reduces combinational complexity and spreads the calculation over multiple cycles, allowing for smaller area and potentially higher clock speed.
