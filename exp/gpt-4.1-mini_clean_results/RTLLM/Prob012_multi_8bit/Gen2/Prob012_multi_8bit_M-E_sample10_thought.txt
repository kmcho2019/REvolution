Implement the 8-bit multiplier as a sequential shift-and-add circuit using a finite state machine. The design uses an internal register to accumulate the product, an 8-bit register holding the multiplicand, and an 8-bit register holding the multiplier. On each clock cycle, the least significant bit of the multiplier register is checked; if it's 1, the multiplicand is added to the accumulated product. The product and multiplier are shifted right and left respectively to simulate the multiplication process, iterating 8 times. This approach uses a clocked process to perform multiplication step-by-step, minimizing combinational delay at the expense of latency. This design is suitable for hardware with sequential logic and reduces area compared to a fully combinational multiplier.
