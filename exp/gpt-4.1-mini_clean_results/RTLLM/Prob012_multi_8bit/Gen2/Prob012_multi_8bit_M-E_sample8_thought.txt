Implement the 8-bit multiplier using a procedural always block that iteratively processes each bit of the multiplier B. Initialize the product to zero, then for each bit from LSB to MSB, if the bit is set, add the multiplicand shifted by the bit position to the product. This approach mimics a sequential shift-and-add multiplier algorithm within combinational logic, but using an explicit for-loop inside an always @* block to clearly express the accumulation of partial sums. The design is purely combinational and easy to understand, different from a fully parallel adder tree, yet still synthesizable efficiently.
