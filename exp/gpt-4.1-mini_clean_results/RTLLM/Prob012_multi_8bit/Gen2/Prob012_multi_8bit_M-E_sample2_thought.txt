Implement an iterative shift-and-add multiplier using a sequential approach with a simple FSM. On a start signal (implied by input change), the multiplier accumulates partial sums bit by bit every clock cycle. The multiplicand is held constant, the multiplier bits are scanned one at a time from LSB to MSB, and the product register accumulates shifted partial sums accordingly. This approach uses a clocked process with minimal combinational logic, reducing immediate combinational delay and allowing pipelining or controlled latency in larger systems. The module includes an enable signal for starting the multiplication and a done flag to indicate completion, making it suitable for integration in a sequential design.
