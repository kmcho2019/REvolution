Implement the 8-bit multiplier as a sequential shift-and-add circuit using a simple finite state machine. The design uses a register to hold the partial product and iteratively examines each bit of the multiplier B. If the current LSB of B is 1, the multiplicand A is added to the partial product accumulator. Then the accumulator and B are shifted right each cycle until all bits of B are processed. This approach mimics manual binary multiplication, reduces combinational complexity, and allows for hardware reuse in a small iterative datapath.
