To improve PPA, especially power and area, I will register the output data so that output changes occur on the clock edge rather than combinationally, which reduces glitching and dynamic power. Additionally, I will initialize the ROM inside the initial block with the specified data and keep other addresses zero. This synchronous read ROM is common in FPGA/ASIC designs for better timing and power characteristics. The module will add a clock input, and the dout will be a registered output updated on the rising clock edge from the ROM array.
