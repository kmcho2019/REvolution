To optimize the ROM for better Performance, Power, and Area (PPA), I will replace the combinational always @(*) block with a continuous assignment for the dout output. This reduces unnecessary logic levels and can improve timing by making the read purely combinational without register inference. The memory initialization remains in the initial block, and the rom_style attribute is kept to guide synthesis to infer block RAM, which is area efficient. This approach also minimizes switching activity and power consumption by reducing logic complexity.
