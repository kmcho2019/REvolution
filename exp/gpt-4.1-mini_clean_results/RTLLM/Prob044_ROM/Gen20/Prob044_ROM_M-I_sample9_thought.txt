To improve power and area efficiency, I will partition the 256x16 ROM into four smaller 64x16 ROM blocks with address decoding, so only one smaller memory block is active at a time. This reduces internal switching activity and can help the synthesis tool better optimize block RAM usage. The output multiplexing is purely combinational and small area. Also, I will retain the rom_style="block" attribute to encourage block RAM inference. This hierarchical approach improves power by reducing unnecessary accesses and can slightly reduce area by enabling better resource sharing or selective instantiation.
