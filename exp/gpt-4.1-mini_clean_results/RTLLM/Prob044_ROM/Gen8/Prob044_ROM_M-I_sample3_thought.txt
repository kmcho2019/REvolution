To improve PPA, especially power and performance, convert the combinational ROM output into a registered output synchronized to a clock. This reduces glitches and switching activity on dout and breaks the long combinational path, enabling higher clock frequency. Also, add a clock and synchronous reset input to control when dout updates. Initialize only required ROM contents, and zero out other locations for clarity and potential tool optimizations. This approach is common in FPGA and ASIC designs for ROMs where synchronous read access is preferred.
