To optimize the ROM module for better PPA, I will register the output data with a clock input to reduce combinational path delay and glitches, thus improving timing and power characteristics. Introducing a clock and synchronously outputting the data reduces switching noise and enables better timing closure on the output path. The ROM content itself remains combinationally addressed, but output is stable on clock edges. This technique is common for ROMs in synchronous designs and improves overall PPA without changing memory contents or access semantics.
