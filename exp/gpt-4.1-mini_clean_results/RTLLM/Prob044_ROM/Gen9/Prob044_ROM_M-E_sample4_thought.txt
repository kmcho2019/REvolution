Implement the ROM using a combinational always block with a case statement instead of a memory array. This explicitly enumerates all initialized addresses and assigns their respective values, while defaulting uninitialized addresses to zero. 

This approach eliminates the memory array and initial block, making the ROM content fully visible and fixed at compile time. It can be advantageous for small ROMs or when targeting FPGAs or synthesis tools that better optimize case statements into combinational logic or LUTs rather than block RAM. 

Additionally, defining dout as a reg and driving it combinationally inside an always @(*) block follows traditional Verilog style and ensures clarity. This architectural style emphasizes ROM synthesis as combinational logic and removes dependency on inferred RAMs, making it portable and suitable for ASIC or FPGA LUT-based ROM implementations.
