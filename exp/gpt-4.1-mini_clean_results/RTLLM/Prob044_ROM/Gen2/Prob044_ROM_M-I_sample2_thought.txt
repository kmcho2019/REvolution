To improve PPA while maintaining functionality, I will replace the combinational always block with a continuous assignment to output mem[addr]. This can improve synthesis inference by allowing the tool to treat mem as a ROM and generate optimized logic or memory blocks. Initializing the ROM in the initial block remains unchanged, as it is synthesizable for ROM inference in many flows. Continuous assignment reduces unnecessary register inference and can reduce delay and area. This approach also slightly reduces power by avoiding a combinational process.
