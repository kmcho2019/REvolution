To improve performance and power while maintaining functionality, register the ROM output to reduce combinational path delay and switch activity on dout. This adds a one-cycle latency but improves timing and dynamic power by only changing dout on clock edges. I will add a clock input and use a synchronous read, which is typical for ROMs in FPGA/ASIC to enhance PPA metrics. Initialization remains the same. This tradeoff is beneficial for high-frequency designs.
