Use a synchronous active-low reset and implement the counter with a combinational next-state logic that explicitly checks for the terminal count (11). When valid_count is 0, the counter holds its current state. When valid_count is 1, it increments the count, or resets to zero when it reaches 11. This design separates the combinational next count logic from the sequential register update, which makes the design cleaner and potentially easier to optimize by synthesis tools.
