To improve the original design for better power and performance, I will implement clock gating to disable the clock input to the counter register when counting is not needed (valid_count = 0). This reduces unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. The wrap-around logic remains the same but is now inside a clock-gated domain, potentially improving timing by reducing switching activity. Clock gating will be implemented with a simple AND gate combining clk and valid_count. The synchronous reset remains as-is for reliable reset behavior.
