To simplify the design while maintaining the required functionality, avoid gating the clock at RTL and implement counting with an enable signal inside a single always block. Use asynchronous active-low reset to set the count to zero. On each rising clock edge, if reset is not active and `valid_count` is asserted, increment the count; wrap to zero when the count reaches 11. When `valid_count` is zero, hold the count value. This approach removes clock gating, reducing complexity and improving synthesis and timing friendliness.
