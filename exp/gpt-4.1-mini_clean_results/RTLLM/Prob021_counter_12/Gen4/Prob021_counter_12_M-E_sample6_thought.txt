To provide a different architectural approach, I will implement the counter using an explicit state machine style with two states: counting and paused. Instead of gating the counting logic with a combined enable signal, I will use an enable register that samples the valid_count input on each clock cycle, enabling a clear separation between control and data path. Also, I will perform the reset asynchronously (using negedge rst_n) to immediately reset the output. The counting will be done by an up-counter with explicit conditional logic for wrap-around at 11. This approach differs from combinational conditional operators by using if-else statements in a straightforward manner, making it clearer and easier for formal verification and debugging. The counter holds its value when paused without any extra toggling. This design favors clarity and deterministic control while preserving the specification.
