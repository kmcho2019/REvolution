Implement the counter entirely inside a single synchronous always block using a clock enable style. The counter only increments when valid_count is high, and resets asynchronously on the negative edge of rst_n (since active low reset is typically asynchronous). The counter resets to zero if it reaches 11 on increment. By incorporating the enable signal directly in the clocked process, we avoid combinational next-state logic and reduce complexity. This approach can improve timing by reducing combinational path length and simplify power gating since the registers only toggle on clock edges when valid_count is asserted.
