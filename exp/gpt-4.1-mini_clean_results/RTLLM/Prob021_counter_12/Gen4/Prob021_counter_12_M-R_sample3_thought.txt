Refactor the counter by separating the next-state logic into a combinational always block and using a sequential always block for the register update. This makes the design more explicit and easier to read or extend, by clearly defining the next value of the counter separately from the state update. The reset remains synchronous active low. The counting enable (valid_count) controls whether the counter increments or stays. When count reaches 11, next state resets to 0. This approach is functionally identical but structured as a Moore-style state update for clarity.
