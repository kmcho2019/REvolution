To optimize the counter for better timing closure and power, I will remove the manual clock gating (which can cause glitches and timing issues) and instead implement a synchronous enable signal inside the always block. The counter will increment only when valid_count is high. This approach avoids gating the clock line and allows synthesis tools to infer proper clock gating cells if needed, improving performance and power. The asynchronous reset remains for quick reset response. This modification improves timing predictability, reduces clock glitches, and typically reduces unnecessary switching, benefiting PPA metrics.
