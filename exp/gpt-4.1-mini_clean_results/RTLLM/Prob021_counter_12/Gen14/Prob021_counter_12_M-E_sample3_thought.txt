Implement the counter using a synchronous process with an enable signal derived from valid_count, which acts as a clock enable to control counting. Use a simple equality comparison to detect the terminal count (4'd11), and directly update the output register accordingly. The reset signal is active low and synchronous. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its current value by not enabling the increment logic, eliminating unnecessary toggling and potentially reducing power consumption without explicit clock gating. This approach simplifies the logic by removing the separate next_count combinational logic, leading to a more compact design.
