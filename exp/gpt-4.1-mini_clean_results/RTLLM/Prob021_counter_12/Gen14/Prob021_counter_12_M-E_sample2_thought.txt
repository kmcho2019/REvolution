To implement the counter counting from 0 to 11 with pause capability and synchronous active-low reset, I will use a simple 4-bit register to hold the count. Instead of detecting the max count using an equality or bitwise expression, I will implement the counter as a modulo-12 counter by explicitly comparing the count to 12. When the count reaches 12 (4'd12), the counter will reset to zero on the next increment.

The counting will be gated by valid_count by using it as an enable signal inside the always block. When valid_count is low, the counter will hold its value by not updating the register. This approach prevents unnecessary toggling, reducing dynamic power consumption.

The reset signal rst_n is active low and synchronous, meaning the reset action happens on the clock edge when rst_n is low.

This approach uses a simple compare against 12 to reset the counter, which is easily optimized by synthesis tools, and is conceptually clean. The count outputs values from 0 to 11 inclusive, then resets to 0, and the counting pauses when valid_count is zero.
