Refactor the counter to avoid gated clocks by using the main clock with a synchronous reset and an enable signal. The counter increments only when valid_count is high, otherwise it holds the count value. This is implemented inside a single always block triggered on the rising edge of the clock and asynchronous active-low reset. The comparison and counting logic remain the same, but this structure improves timing and avoids potential clock gating issues. The design also uses a separate combinational next_count signal to clarify next-state logic.
