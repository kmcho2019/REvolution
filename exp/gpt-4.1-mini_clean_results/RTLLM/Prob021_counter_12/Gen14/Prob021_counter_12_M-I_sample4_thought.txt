The current solution uses clock gating by ANDing the clock with valid_count, which may cause glitches and timing closure difficulties. To improve performance and power without complicating timing, I will remove the gated clock and use a synchronous enable (valid_count) inside the always block. This approach prevents glitching on the clock line, simplifies timing analysis, and enables the synthesis tool to infer proper clock gating cells if needed. The asynchronous reset remains unchanged. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its value, reducing unnecessary switching and power consumption. This approach achieves functional correctness with improved PPA metrics.
