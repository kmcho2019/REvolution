Use a straightforward synchronous always block triggered on the rising edge of clk and asynchronous reset (active low). Use the valid_count signal as an enable to control counting without gating the clock. When rst_n is low, reset the counter to zero. When valid_count is high, increment the counter; if it reaches 11, wrap around to zero. Otherwise, hold the counter value. This approach simplifies the design, improves timing, and avoids clock gating issues.
