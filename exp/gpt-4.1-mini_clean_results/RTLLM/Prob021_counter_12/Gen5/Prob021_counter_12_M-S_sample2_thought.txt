The simplest, clean, and recommended approach is to keep the clock clean and use an enable signal (`valid_count`) inside a synchronous always block with an asynchronous active-low reset. This design counts from 0 to 11 inclusive, wraps to 0 after reaching 11, and pauses counting when `valid_count` is low, simply by not updating the count. The code uses a straightforward if-else structure and non-blocking assignments for clear and maintainable RTL.
