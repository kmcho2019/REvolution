Implement a simple synchronous 4-bit counter with asynchronous active-low reset. Use a single always block triggered on the rising edge of clk and asynchronous reset (rst_n). Inside the block, if reset is active, set output to zero. Otherwise, if valid_count is asserted, increment the counter, wrapping back to zero after reaching 11. When valid_count is low, hold the current count. This approach avoids clock gating and uses a standard enable signal for counting, simplifying design and improving synthesis friendliness.
