To improve power efficiency and simplify logic, introduce an explicit clock enable gating the sequential register update, so the register only toggles when valid_count is high. Move the rollover check and increment logic inside the clocked block controlled by the clock enable, removing the combinational next_out logic to reduce unnecessary combinational toggling. This reduces switching activity, as next_out is not continuously computed. The synchronous reset remains active low, resetting the counter to zero. This approach reduces dynamic power by avoiding toggling the register when count is paused and simplifies logic, potentially improving timing due to less combinational logic between registers.
