To refactor the original design without clock gating and still meet all requirements, I will implement the counter using a combinational next-state logic along with a synchronous always block. This approach separates the next state calculation from the state update. The output register 'out' is updated at every clock edge or asynchronously reset by rst_n. When valid_count is high, the counter increments or wraps to 0 at 11. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its value by reusing the current state as the next state. This design is fully synchronous with asynchronous reset, avoids clock gating, and remains clear and easy to understand.
