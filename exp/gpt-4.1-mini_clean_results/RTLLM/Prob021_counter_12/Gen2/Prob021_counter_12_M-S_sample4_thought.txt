To simplify the counter design while maintaining functionality, use a clean clock without gating, and incorporate the `valid_count` signal as an enable inside the always block. This removes the gated clock logic and reduces complexity. The counter increments when `valid_count` is high, resets to zero on active-low reset, and wraps around to zero after counting to 11. This approach keeps the design straightforward and synthesizable, avoiding potential issues related to clock gating at RTL.
