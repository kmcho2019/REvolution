To provide a novel architectural approach, I will implement the counter using a purely synchronous design with a clear and enable signal rather than using clock gating or just an enable signal. The reset will still be active low asynchronous to ensure a fast reset response.

Key points of the new approach:
1. Use an asynchronous active-low reset (rst_n).
2. Use a synchronous clear signal internally derived when the counter reaches its max (11), to reset the counter on the next clock cycle.
3. Use a separate enable signal (valid_count) that controls counting.
4. When valid_count is 0, hold the count value.
5. This approach avoids gated clocks but adds a synchronous clear that resets the counter at max count.
6. Internally the counter increments when enabled and not cleared.
7. The synchronous clear prevents combinational logic for resetting counter value at max count, which may optimize timing by breaking combinational carry chains.
8. It is a little different from the usual approach by isolating count reset as a synchronous clear controlled by a comparator signal.

This design can improve timing closure while keeping simple control logic and clean clocking scheme.
