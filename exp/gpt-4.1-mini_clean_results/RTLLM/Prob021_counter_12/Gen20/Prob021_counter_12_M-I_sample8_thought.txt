To optimize for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will replace the bitwise terminal_count detection with a simple numeric comparison (`out == 4'd11`). This enables the synthesis tool to implement an optimal comparator, potentially improving timing and reducing logic complexity. Also, I will merge the combinational and sequential logic by removing the explicit next_out register and directly updating 'out' in the sequential always block with non-blocking assignments. This reduces logic levels and may improve clock-to-Q delay by minimizing muxes and combinational logic. The counting will still be gated by valid_count, holding the output stable when counting is disabled, thus reducing switching power. The reset remains synchronous active low.

This approach simplifies logic, potentially improves critical path delay, and reduces area, while maintaining clear and maintainable code.
