Refactor the counter using a combinational next-state logic with an always_ff block triggered on the clock and asynchronous reset. Instead of gating the clock, use a clock enable signal controlled by valid_count inside the sequential block. This approach avoids potential glitches from clock gating, improves timing closure, and keeps functional behavior unchanged. The counter increments on each clock cycle when valid_count is high, wraps at 11, and holds value when valid_count is low. Reset asynchronously clears the count to zero.
