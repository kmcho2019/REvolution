To improve the original design for better PPA metrics and avoid potential clock gating glitches, I will implement the counter using a synchronous always block triggered by the clock and an asynchronous active-low reset. The counting will be controlled by using `valid_count` as an enable signal inside the sequential block instead of gating the clock signal. This approach avoids glitches on the clock line and improves timing closure while maintaining correct functionality. The counter resets asynchronously when rst_n is low, increments on each clock cycle when valid_count is high, and wraps from 11 back to 0. When valid_count is low, the count holds its value. This design is synthesis-friendly, easy to verify, and offers improved performance and power characteristics compared to clock gating by ANDing the clock.
