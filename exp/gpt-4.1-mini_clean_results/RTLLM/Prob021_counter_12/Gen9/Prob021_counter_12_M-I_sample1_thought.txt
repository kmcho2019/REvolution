To optimize for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area), I will simplify the terminal count detection by replacing the bitwise combination with a straightforward equality check (`out == 4'd11`) which often synthesizes better. Additionally, I'll add an enable signal in the sequential block to update the counter register only when counting is enabled (valid_count is high) or when reset is active, preventing unnecessary toggling and thus reducing dynamic power. This also helps the synthesis tool infer clock gating or similar optimizations naturally. The rest of the structure is maintained for clarity and correctness.
