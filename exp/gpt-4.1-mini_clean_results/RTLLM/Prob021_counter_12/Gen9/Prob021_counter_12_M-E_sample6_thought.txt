To implement a robust and synthesis-friendly counter without gating the clock, I will use a standard synchronous design with an asynchronous active-low reset. Counting will be controlled using a synchronous enable signal, valid_count. To further optimize the logic, I will use a ternary operator to assign the next count value combinationally, simplifying the logic inside the always block. The counter will count up from 0 to 11, and wrap back to 0 when it reaches 11. When valid_count is low, the counter will hold its current value. This approach eliminates clock gating risks, improves timing closure, and maintains functional correctness.
