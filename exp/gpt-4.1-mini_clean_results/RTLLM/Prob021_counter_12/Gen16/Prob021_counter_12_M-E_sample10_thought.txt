Implement the counter with a single synchronous always block that combines counting and reset logic without separate next-state signals. Use a counter register that increments only when valid_count is high. When the count reaches 11, it wraps back to zero on the next increment. Since reset is active low and synchronous, reset logic is checked inside the clocked process. The design is minimalistic, avoiding combinational next-state logic and reducing logic levels for potentially faster synthesis. The output count is registered, and the counter stops incrementing (holds) when valid_count is low.
