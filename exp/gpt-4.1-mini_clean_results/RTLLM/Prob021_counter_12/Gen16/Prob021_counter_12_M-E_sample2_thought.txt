Implement the counter using a synchronous reset and a synchronous enable (`valid_count`) without gating the clock. Instead of incrementing by one directly, use a combinational next-state logic that determines the next count value based on whether the current count is the terminal value 11 or not. This clean separation of next-state logic and state register improves readability and synthesis friendliness. When `valid_count` is low, the counter holds its value. The asynchronous reset is replaced by synchronous reset (active low reset input is synchronized inside the block), which is often preferred for timing closure. The counter output is 4 bits wide to represent counts from 0 to 11.
