To simplify the counter design while maintaining functionality, avoid clock gating and rely on a single always block triggered by the clock and asynchronous reset. Use the valid_count signal as an enable inside the always block to control counting. This reduces complexity by not generating additional signals or gated clocks and uses straightforward counter logic. The count resets asynchronously when rst_n is low, increments on each clock edge if valid_count is high, and wraps from 11 back to 0. This approach is clean, synthesizable, and easy to understand.
