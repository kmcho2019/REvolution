To simplify the design and maintain functionality without clock gating, I will use a single always block triggered on the rising edge of clk with asynchronous active-low reset. The counting is controlled by the valid_count enable signal inside the always block. On reset, the counter resets to 0. When valid_count is high, the counter increments until it reaches 11, then wraps back to 0. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its current value. This approach avoids clock gating glitches and eases timing closure.
