To optimize the original design for better performance, power, and synthesis friendliness, I will remove the manual clock gating approach, which can cause glitches and complicate timing closure. Instead, I will use a single always block triggered by the rising edge of the clock and an asynchronous active-low reset. The counting will be controlled by a synchronous enable signal (valid_count) inside the always block. This approach improves timing closure by avoiding glitches on the clock line, ensures reliable asynchronous reset behavior, and lets synthesis tools infer appropriate clock gating cells if needed. The wrap-around and counting logic remain unchanged, ensuring functional correctness while improving PPA.
