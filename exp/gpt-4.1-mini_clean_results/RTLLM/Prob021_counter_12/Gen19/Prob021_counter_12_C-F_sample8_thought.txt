Advantages of this fusion:
- The combinational next state logic cleanly expresses the counting and wrap-around behavior, improving readability and modularity.
- Using the bitwise terminal count logic from Example 2 reduces comparator complexity and potential synthesis overhead.
- All sequential updates happen in one always block, ensuring synchronous reset and stable timing.
- Holding the count stable when valid_count is low reduces switching activity, lowering dynamic power.
- Non-blocking assignments and explicit next state variable improve timing and synthesis quality.
- Avoiding clock gating maintains good timing closure and avoids complexity.
