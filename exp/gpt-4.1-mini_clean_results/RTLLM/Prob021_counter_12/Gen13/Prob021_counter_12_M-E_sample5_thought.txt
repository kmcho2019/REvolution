To implement the counter without gating the clock and still efficiently control counting, I will use a synchronous always block triggered on the rising edge of clk with an asynchronous reset (active low). Instead of gating the clock, I use a count_enable signal derived from valid_count to control the counting behavior inside the always block. The counter only increments when valid_count is high. When the count reaches 11, it wraps around to 0. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its value, avoiding unnecessary toggling. This architecture avoids clock gating issues and ensures clean timing while maintaining low power by controlling when the counter increments.
