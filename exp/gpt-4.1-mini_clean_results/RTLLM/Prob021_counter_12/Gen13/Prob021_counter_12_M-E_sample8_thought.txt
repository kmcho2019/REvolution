Instead of using clock gating or a simple enable inside the always block, I will implement the counter as a modulo-12 up-counter using arithmetic and wrap-around logic with a registered enable signal to synchronize the valid_count input. This approach ensures clean timing without gating the clock or combinational glitches. 

I will register valid_count on the clock to generate a synchronous enable signal, avoiding metastability or glitches from asynchronous inputs. The counter will increment only when the registered enable is high. When it reaches 11, it wraps to 0. 

This method avoids clock gating, preserves clean clock paths, and uses a simple arithmetic comparator for wrap-around. The registered enable improves timing by making enable synchronous, allowing easier timing closure in complex designs.

Reset is synchronous and active low, resetting the counter to zero. This approach provides robust timing and functional correctness with minimal area and power overhead.
