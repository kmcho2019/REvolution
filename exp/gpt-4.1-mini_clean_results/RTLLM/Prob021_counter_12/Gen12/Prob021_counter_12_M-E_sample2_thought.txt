Implement the counter by using a synchronous always block with an explicit enable check inside. Instead of a separate combinational next-state logic, directly update the count register only when valid_count is high. On reset (!rst_n), the counter resets to 0. When counting, if the counter reaches 11, it wraps to zero; otherwise, it increments by 1. This straightforward approach reduces logic by eliminating the next_out register and keeps the design simple and easy to read.
