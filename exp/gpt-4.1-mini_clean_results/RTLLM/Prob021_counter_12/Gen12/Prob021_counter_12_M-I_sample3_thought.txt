To improve power efficiency and slightly reduce switching activity, I will add an explicit clock enable (ce) signal derived from valid_count to gate register updates. This can help synthesis tools optimize and reduce toggling in the output register when counting is paused. The counting logic remains the same to ensure correctness and minimal delay by preserving the simple comparison. Using clock enable also aids synthesis tools to generate clock gating cells if supported. This improves power without compromising performance or area noticeably in a small 4-bit counter.
