To optimize the existing counter design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will focus on the following:

1. Simplify the terminal count detection by replacing the bitwise AND with a direct numeric comparison (out == 4'd11). This often synthesizes to optimized hardware and improves readability without increasing delay.

2. Add clock gating internally via an enable signal to the register to reduce power. Instead of gating the clock itself (which requires special handling), I will condition the counter update on valid_count, ensuring the flip-flops only toggle when counting, minimizing switching power.

3. Retain the synchronous active-low reset for robust timing and simplicity.

4. Maintain non-blocking assignments for proper sequential logic.

This approach keeps the logic minimal and allows synthesis tools to implement efficient comparator logic and minimal switching, improving power and area while maintaining or slightly improving timing.

No functional change: The counter resets at rst_n low, increments only when valid_count is high, and rolls over from 11 back to 0.
