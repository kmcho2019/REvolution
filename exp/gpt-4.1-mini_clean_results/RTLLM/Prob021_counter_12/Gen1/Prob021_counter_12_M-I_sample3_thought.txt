To improve the existing counter design for better PPA, we switch from an asynchronous active-low reset to a synchronous active-high reset to improve timing predictability and simplify the reset path. Additionally, we replace the equality comparison with a terminal count signal generated from decoding the bits to potentially reduce critical path delay. Since the count max is 11 (4'b1011), the terminal count condition can be optimized by combinational logic instead of a direct comparator. These changes will keep the same functionality but can aid timing closure and power optimization by better synthesis results.
