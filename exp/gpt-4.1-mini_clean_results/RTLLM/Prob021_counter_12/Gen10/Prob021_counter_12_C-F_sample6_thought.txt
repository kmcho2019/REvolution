Fuse the best ideas from both examples by combining the simplicity and clarity of a single always block synchronous design with an explicit count_enable signal derived from valid_count for readability and potential extensibility. 

Key points of the fusion:
- Use synchronous active-low reset (!rst_n) resetting count to zero.
- Use a count_enable wire assigned from valid_count to clearly gate counting.
- Increment the counter only when count_enable is high.
- When the counter reaches the max value (11 decimal), wrap to zero.
- Maintain non-blocking assignments for proper synchronous behavior.
- Retain simplicity by avoiding separate next_state combinational logic.
- This approach yields clear, minimal, and easily maintainable code with good potential for PPA optimizations.
- Optional: add a parameter for max count (optional, not required here) for future scalability.
- Keep the counter output as reg for synthesis friendliness.

Overall, this design is clean, efficient, and balances readability, simplicity, and future extensibility.
