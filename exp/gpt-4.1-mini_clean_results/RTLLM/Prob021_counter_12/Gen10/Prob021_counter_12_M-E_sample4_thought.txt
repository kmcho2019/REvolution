Implement the counter using a synchronous active-low reset with a separate combinational block to generate the next count value, enabling clearer separation of state update and next-state logic. Use a 4-bit register for the count, and in the combinational block, when valid_count is asserted, increment the counter unless it equals 11, in which case reset to zero; otherwise hold the current count. The count register is updated on each clock edge or reset asynchronously. This modular design eases readability and facilitates formal verification.
