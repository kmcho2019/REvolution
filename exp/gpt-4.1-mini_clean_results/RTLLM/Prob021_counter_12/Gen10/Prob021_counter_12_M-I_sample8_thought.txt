To optimize the counter for better performance and power without risking glitches from manual clock gating, I will remove gated clock and instead use a synchronous enable signal (valid_count) inside the always block. This way, the counter increments only when valid_count is high, otherwise it holds the current value. The reset remains asynchronous and active-low. This approach avoids glitches on the clock line, improves timing closure, and allows synthesis tools to infer proper clock gating cells if needed, enhancing power efficiency. The logic remains simple and area-efficient.
