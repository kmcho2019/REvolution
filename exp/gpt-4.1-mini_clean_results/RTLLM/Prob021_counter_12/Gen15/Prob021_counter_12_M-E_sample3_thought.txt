Implement the counter using a synchronous enable signal without clock gating, but incorporate a small FSM that controls the counting process. The FSM has two states: IDLE (count paused) and COUNT (count enabled). Transition between states is controlled by valid_count. When valid_count is 1, FSM moves to COUNT state, allowing counting; when 0, FSM moves to IDLE, stopping the count. This separation improves clarity and can aid in formal verification and future extendibility. The counter increments on each clock cycle when in COUNT state and resets asynchronously with rst_n. Wrapping at 11 is done with a comparator. This design cleanly separates counting control from counting logic and avoids glitchy clock gating.
