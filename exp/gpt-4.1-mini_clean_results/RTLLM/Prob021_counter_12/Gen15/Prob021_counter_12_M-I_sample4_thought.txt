To improve the existing design for better PPA (performance, power, area), I will:

1. Replace the explicit bitwise detection of terminal count (out == 11) with a simple equality comparison (`out == 4'd11`). Modern synthesis tools typically optimize equality comparisons very efficiently, often better than manual bitwise logic, reducing area and possibly improving timing.

2. Keep the clean modular structure with a separate combinational block for next_out generation for clarity and maintainability.

3. Maintain synchronous active-low reset and non-blocking assignments for proper timing and synthesis behavior.

4. To reduce switching power further, explicitly hold the counter state when valid_count is low, avoiding unnecessary toggling.

5. No clock gating will be added here because it adds complexity and was not requested, and the power saving may be minimal for such a small counter.

This refined design balances clarity, maintainability, and better synthesis optimization opportunities, aiming for slightly better area and power without hurting performance.
