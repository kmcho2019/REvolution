To optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I'll simplify the enable logic by removing rst_n from the count_enable signal because reset forces the counter to zero and naturally stops counting. Including rst_n in count_enable may cause unnecessary switching. Also, I'll keep the counting logic and synchronous reset as is, since it's already minimal.

For power, gating counting only when valid_count is high avoids unnecessary toggling. I won't use explicit clock gating since it may complicate synthesis and timing. For performance, the minimal combinational logic is sufficient.

For area, no extra logic is added. Removing redundant logic can save some area and dynamic power.

So, the main improvement is to use count_enable = valid_count alone, and keep the rest unchanged.
