To improve PPA metrics and avoid potential clock gating issues, I will remove the gated clock and instead keep the clock clean. Counting will be enabled inside the always block via the `valid_count` signal as a synchronous enable.

This approach:
- Improves timing closure by eliminating glitches and skew issues caused by clock gating.
- Maintains functional correctness by incrementing only when `valid_count` is high.
- Retains the asynchronous active-low reset for immediate reset response.
- Keeps the logic minimal and power-efficient since toggling is avoided when counting is disabled.
- Uses a single always block with non-blocking assignments, ensuring proper synthesis and simulation behavior.

This method is a standard good design practice balancing power, performance, and area for small counters like this.
