Use a single always block triggered by the clock and asynchronous reset to implement the counter. Instead of gating the clock, keep the clock signal clean and use the `valid_count` signal as an enable inside the always block. This avoids clock gating complexity and timing issues. When reset is asserted, reset the counter to 0. When `valid_count` is high, increment the counter, wrapping around to zero after 11. If `valid_count` is low, hold the counter value unchanged.
