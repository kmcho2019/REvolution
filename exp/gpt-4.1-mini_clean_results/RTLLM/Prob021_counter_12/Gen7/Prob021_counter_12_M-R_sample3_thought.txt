Refactor the counter design using a Moore-style finite state machine approach with a simple state encoding directly represented by the output `out`. Use a synchronous always block to update the count or reset, and utilize a combinational assign statement for next count calculation outside of the always block. The `valid_count` signal controls if the count advances, and the count wraps around after reaching 11. This structure separates the next count calculation from state updating without needing an explicit `next_out` register, improving clarity and reducing internal registers. The reset logic remains synchronous and active-low.
