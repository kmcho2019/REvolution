To optimize the design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will replace the multiple bitwise assignments with a single nonblocking assignment that uses concatenation and bit-slicing. This approach enables synthesis tools to infer a simple multiplexing/shifting logic with potentially fewer gate levels and lower switching activity. The register q will be assigned as q <= {d, q[7:1]}, effectively performing a right shift by one while inserting the new bit d at the MSB. This concise style improves timing and area while maintaining the same functionality.
