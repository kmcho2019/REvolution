To optimize for power without altering the functional behavior, introduce a clock enable (ce) signal that only updates the register when the input bit `d` differs from the current MSB `q[7]`. This reduces unnecessary toggling of `q` when input `d` equals `q[7]` (since shifting in the same bit results in no data change), thus lowering switching activity and dynamic power. The register still initializes to zero. This simple gating avoids complexity while improving power efficiency with minimal impact on performance and area.
