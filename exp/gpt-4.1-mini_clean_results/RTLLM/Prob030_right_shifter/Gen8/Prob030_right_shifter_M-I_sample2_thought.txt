To improve PPA (timing, power, and area), I will add an active-low synchronous reset to initialize the register to zero. This removes the need for the initial block (which may not be supported or efficient in synthesis), enhancing robustness and synthesis compatibility.

Also, I will add a shift enable input to gate the shifting operation, preventing unnecessary toggling of the register when shifting is not required, reducing dynamic power.

The reset ensures a known initial state on startup, potentially improving timing by avoiding metastability. The enable reduces switching activity, lowering power. This small logic addition has negligible area impact but improves power efficiency and design robustness.

Overall, the changes maintain the original functionality but improve synthesis friendliness and power performance.
