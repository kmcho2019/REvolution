Implement the right shifter using a single 8-bit register `q` initialized to zero. On each rising clock edge, shift the register right by one bit with the Verilog `>>` operator, then explicitly assign the MSB of `q` to the new input bit `d`. 

Instead of a simple vector concatenation or individual bit assignments, leverage a temporary variable to hold the shifted value, then assign the MSB bit separately using bitwise operations combined with masking. This approach clearly separates the shifting operation from the insertion of the MSB bit, making the operation explicit and modular. 

This implementation illustrates usage of bitwise masking and concatenation to combine operations, which may be useful when more complex conditional bit insertions or manipulations are required in future expansions. It also keeps the design fully synchronous and meets initialization requirements.
