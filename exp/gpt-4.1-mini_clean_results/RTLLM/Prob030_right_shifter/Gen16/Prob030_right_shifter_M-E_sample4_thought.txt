Implement the right shifter by combining the shift and insertion into a single nonblocking assignment using concatenation. On each rising clock edge, form the new q by placing the input bit d at the MSB position and concatenating it with the existing bits of q excluding the LSB, effectively shifting q right by one while inserting d at the MSB. This approach avoids multiple nonblocking assignments to the same register, ensuring clearer semantics and potentially cleaner synthesis results. Initialization is still done with an initial statement.
