Refactor the design to implement the right shift operation using a single non-blocking vector assignment inside the synchronous always block, avoiding individual bit-level assignments. Initialize `q` with an initial block to 0. On each rising clock edge, assign `q` the concatenation of input bit `d` and the upper 7 bits of the previous `q`, effectively shifting right by one and inserting `d` at the MSB.
