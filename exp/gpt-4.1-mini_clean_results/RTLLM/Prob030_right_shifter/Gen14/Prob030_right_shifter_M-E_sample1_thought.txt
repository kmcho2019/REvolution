Implement the right shifter purely within the synchronous always block without intermediate wires to reduce combinational logic outside the clock domain. Use a non-blocking assignment where the q register is right-shifted by one bit, and then insert the new input bit d at the MSB in a single statement: q <= {d, q[7:1]}. Initialize the register q to zero using a synchronous reset signal for better synthesis compatibility and deterministic startup behavior instead of an initial block. This design is fully synchronous, simple, and suitable for hardware implementations requiring a clear reset signal.
