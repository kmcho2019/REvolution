The original design is minimal and fully meets functional requirements with excellent PPA characteristics. To optimize power, I can add a clock enable signal internally that only updates the register when the input bit d differs from the current MSB, or when the register content will actually change. This prevents unnecessary toggling of q when the input bit is stable and the shifted content does not cause a change, thus reducing switching activity and dynamic power. This does not increase area significantly since only a simple comparator and gating logic is added, and it does not affect timing critically because the clock enable signal is used at the register stage. Performance is maintained, and power efficiency improves, especially if the input bit remains constant for many cycles.
