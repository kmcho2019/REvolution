Instead of performing the shift and insertion in a single nonblocking assignment, I will implement the shift register behavior by updating each bit of the register individually inside the always block. On each clock rising edge, q[0] will take q[1], q[1] will take q[2], and so forth, and finally q[7] will take the new input bit d. This explicit bit-by-bit assignment demonstrates a straightforward approach to shifting without relying on vector slicing or concatenation. The register is initialized to zero using an initial block as required.
