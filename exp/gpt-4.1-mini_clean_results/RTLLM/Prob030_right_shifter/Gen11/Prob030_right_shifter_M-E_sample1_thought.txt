Implement the right shifter using a synchronous reset that initializes the register q to zero on a reset signal. On each positive clock edge without reset, instead of concatenation, use a bitwise right shift combined with explicitly assigning the MSB with input d. This approach uses two non-blocking assignments inside the clocked always block: one to shift q right by one position, and another to set q[7] to d. This separates the operations clearly and can be helpful for certain synthesis tools. The reset signal enhances hardware initialization clarity and control, making the design more robust and practical for real hardware.
