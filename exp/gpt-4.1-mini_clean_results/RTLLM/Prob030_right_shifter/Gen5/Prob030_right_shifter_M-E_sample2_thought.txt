Implement the right shifter using a sequential approach where the right shift is performed by explicitly shifting bits one by one inside a for-loop on each clock cycle. Initialize the register `q` to zero using an initial block. On the rising edge of the clock, shift all bits of `q` right by one position from LSB to MSB, and then assign the input `d` to the most significant bit. This approach avoids direct concatenation and instead updates each bit individually, showing an alternative to the standard concatenation method.
