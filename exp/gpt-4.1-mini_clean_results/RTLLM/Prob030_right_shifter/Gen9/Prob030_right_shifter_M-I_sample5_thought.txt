To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will simplify the combinational logic by avoiding concatenation and instead using explicit bitwise shift and bit assignment, reducing the muxing complexity. This may help synthesis tools infer simpler hardware and possibly improve timing and power. Also, I will remove the initial block (since not all synthesis tools infer it well) and instead use synchronous reset for initialization, which is more robust and synthesis-friendly. This reset will clear q to zero, enabling reliable power-up state and potentially better timing. The design remains clean, easy to understand, and synthesizable with improved robustness and potential PPA gains.
