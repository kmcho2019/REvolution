To improve PPA, I will replace the initial block with a synchronous reset input for initialization. This approach is more widely supported by synthesis tools and enables deterministic startup behavior. Adding an active-high synchronous reset allows the register to be cleared explicitly, avoiding reliance on initial conditions. This can slightly improve timing and power by avoiding undefined startup states and allowing gating of toggling when reset is asserted. The shifting and insertion logic remains the same to keep minimal logic. This also facilitates design scalability and better synthesis tool support.
