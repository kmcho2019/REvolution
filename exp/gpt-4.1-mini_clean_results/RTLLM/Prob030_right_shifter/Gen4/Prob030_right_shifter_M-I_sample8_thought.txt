To optimize the existing design for better power and timing without changing functionality, we can replace the initial block (which may not be fully supported in all synthesis flows) with a synchronous reset signal to initialize q to zero. This will ensure deterministic startup state and may lead to better synthesis results.

Additionally, combining the shift and insertion into one concatenation assignment is already efficient.

To improve power slightly, gating the update of q only when d differs from q[7] could reduce unnecessary toggling; however, this adds extra logic and may not be beneficial overall.

Given the simplicity and already good metrics, the main improvement is to add a synchronous active-high reset input to initialize the register, which is a common and practical improvement for synthesis and PPA.

This also makes the design more robust and portable across different FPGA/ASIC tools, possibly improving timing and power by avoiding the use of an initial block.

Summary of changes:
- Add synchronous reset input 'rst'
- On reset (rst=1), set q to zero
- On clock edge and no reset, perform the shift operation as before
