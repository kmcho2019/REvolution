The given design is already efficient, implementing a right shift with a single non-blocking assignment that concatenates the new bit `d` at the MSB and shifts the rest right by one bit. The initialization of `q` to zero is also correct for simulation.

To optimize for PPA:
- Introduce a synchronous reset input to reliably initialize `q` in hardware instead of relying solely on the `initial` block, which is ignored by some synthesis tools and target technologies.
- Add an enable input to gate shifting operation to reduce power consumption by avoiding unnecessary shifting.
- Keep the core logic unchanged to maintain minimal area and maximum performance.
- These additions enable better power management and deterministic reset behavior, improving overall design robustness with minimal area and timing overhead.

This enhanced design thus provides:
- Synchronous reset for robust initialization.
- Enable signal for power savings.
- Still minimal logic and timing impact, maintaining short critical path.
