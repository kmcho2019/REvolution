Implement the right shifter by constructing the new shifted value as a single concatenated vector combining the input bit 'd' at the MSB with the upper 7 bits of the current register 'q'. This way, we perform the right shift and insertion in a single non-blocking assignment, avoiding multiple writes to the same register. Initialize the register to zero using an initial block. This approach simplifies the logic, eliminates any ambiguity of multiple assignments to 'q', and should synthesize efficiently.
