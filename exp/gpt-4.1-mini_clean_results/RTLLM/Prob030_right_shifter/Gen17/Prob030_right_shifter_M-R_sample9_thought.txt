To refactor the design without changing functionality, I will separate the register update and the shift logic. Instead of performing the shift and load inside a single always block using concatenation, I will use an intermediate wire to compute the shifted value with the new input bit and then update the register `q` synchronously on the clock edge. This approach decouples combinational shift calculation from the sequential register update, which can make the design conceptually clearer and easier to extend. The register `q` remains initialized to zero with an initial statement.
