To optimize the original design for power and performance without changing functionality, I will implement clock enable signals to avoid unnecessary updates (flip-flop toggles) when the fetch signal does not target a register. This reduces switching activity and thus power. Instead of a case statement with default reassignments, I use individual enable signals for each register. Also, I keep the synchronous active low reset. This approach improves power by reducing toggles and can marginally improve timing by simpler clock gating enabling registers only when needed. The output logic remains combinational and minimal.
