This design introduces a unified 16-bit register to store both instructions simultaneously. On the rising clock edge, if reset is low, the register clears. Based on the fetch signal, the appropriate 8-bit half (upper or lower) of the 16-bit register is updated with the input data. The high 3 bits and low 5 bits are extracted from the upper byte (assuming fetch==2'b01 data is stored there), while the full 8-bit lower byte corresponds to the fetch==2'b10 data. This approach reduces the number of registers by combining both instructions into one register and uses bit-select updates, potentially saving area and making the data path clearer.
