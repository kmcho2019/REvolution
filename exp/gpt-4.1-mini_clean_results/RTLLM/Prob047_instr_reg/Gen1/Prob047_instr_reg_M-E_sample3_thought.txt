Instead of separate registers ins_p1 and ins_p2, use a single 16-bit register to hold both instructions concatenated: upper 8 bits for the second source (ins_p2) and lower 8 bits for the first source (ins_p1). On reset (active low), clear this 16-bit register. On clock rising edge, update the corresponding byte based on fetch: if fetch==2'b01, update lower byte; if fetch==2'b10, update upper byte; else hold previous. Outputs ins and ad1 come from the lower byte, ad2 from the upper byte. This consolidates storage into one register, simplifying register management while preserving functionality.
