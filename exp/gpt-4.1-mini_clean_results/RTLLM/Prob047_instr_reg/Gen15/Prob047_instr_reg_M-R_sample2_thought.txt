Refactor by using two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 as originally specified, each capturing data independently based on fetch signals. On each clock's rising edge with active-low reset, ins_p1 updates if fetch=2'b01, ins_p2 updates if fetch=2'b10, else retains previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1, while ad2 outputs ins_p2 directly. This structure is more explicit about instruction sources, making verification straightforward and improving modularity, at a slight area cost.
