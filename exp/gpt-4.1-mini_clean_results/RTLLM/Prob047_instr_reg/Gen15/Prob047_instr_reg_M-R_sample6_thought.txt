Refactor the design by splitting the two instruction registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 explicitly as separate registers, updating each independently based on fetch signal. This removes the need for a source_tag register and enables direct combinational output assignment from the two registers. Reset initializes both registers to zero. Outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1, while ad2 directly reflects ins_p2. This approach aligns closely with the original problem statement, clarifies the source of each output, and simplifies the conditional logic for outputs by avoiding multiplexers dependent on a tag.
