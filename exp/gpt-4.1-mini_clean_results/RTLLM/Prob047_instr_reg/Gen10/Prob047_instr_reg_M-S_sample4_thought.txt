Use two simple 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to separately store instructions from the two sources based on the fetch input. On each clock rising edge, if reset is low, clear both registers. If fetch is 2'b01, load data into ins_p1; if fetch is 2'b10, load data into ins_p2; otherwise retain previous values. Derive ins and ad1 from ins_p1 and ad2 directly from ins_p2. This approach is straightforward, avoids additional state variables, and clearly separates instructions by source.
