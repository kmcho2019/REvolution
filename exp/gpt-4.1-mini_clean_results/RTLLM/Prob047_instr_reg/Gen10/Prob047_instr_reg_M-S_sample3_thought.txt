Simplify the design by directly implementing two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2, each updated only when their corresponding fetch signal is asserted. Use a synchronous active-low reset and update registers on the rising clock edge. Outputs are directly assigned from the registers: ins and ad1 from ins_p1 (when fetch=01), and ad2 from ins_p2 (when fetch=10). This approach closely follows the problem specification, improves clarity, and avoids extra selector logic.
