Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from the two sources according to fetch. On reset, clear both registers. On clock rising edge, update ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01, and ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10; otherwise hold previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1, ad2 comes from ins_p2, reflecting stored instructions without conditional output multiplexing based on fetch.
