To optimize the design for power and performance, implement synchronous active-high reset instead of asynchronous active-low reset. Synchronous resets generally enable better timing closure and simpler clock gating implementation. Also, add explicit enable signals derived from fetch to update each register only when needed, which helps synthesis tools optimize clock gating or register enables to reduce switching power. Maintain non-blocking assignments for correct sequential logic. Keep outputs as combinational assignments from registers for simplicity and minimal latency. This will improve timing, reduce power, and keep area minimal.
