Implement two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from the two sources as specified. On reset (active low), clear both to zero. On each rising clock edge, if fetch==2'b01, load ins_p1 with data; if fetch==2'b10, load ins_p2 with data; otherwise, hold previous values. Derive outputs directly: ins and ad1 from ins_p1 (opcode and register address), ad2 from ins_p2 (full data). This straightforward separation eliminates the need for source flags or multiplexing and simplifies the output logic.
