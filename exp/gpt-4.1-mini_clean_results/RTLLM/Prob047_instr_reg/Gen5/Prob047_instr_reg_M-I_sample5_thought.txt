To improve the design for better power and timing (PPA), I will convert the asynchronous active-low reset to a synchronous active-low reset. This reduces complexity and potential glitches in reset path, improving timing and synthesis results on many target technologies.

Additionally, I will add explicit clock enables for ins_p1 and ins_p2 updates, so registers only toggle when required, potentially reducing dynamic power.

The outputs will remain combinational for minimal latency.

These changes retain the same functionality but can provide better timing closure and lower power consumption as indicated by typical synthesis behavior.
