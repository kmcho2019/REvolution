Implement two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to independently store instructions from two different sources, updated based on fetch signals. The outputs ins and ad1 are derived only from ins_p1, representing the opcode and register address, while ad2 outputs the full 8-bit data from ins_p2. This separation allows parallel capturing and storage without multiplexing outputs, improving clarity and potentially easing downstream timing constraints.
