Use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to separately store instructions based on the fetch signal. On each clock rising edge, if rst is low, reset both registers to zero. When fetch is 2'b01, update ins_p1; when fetch is 2'b10, update ins_p2. Outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1, and ad2 comes from ins_p2. This straightforward, clear separation matches the spec and reduces complexity by avoiding extra selector logic.
