Implement the instruction register with two independent 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to separately store data fetched from the two sources, as originally described. On reset, clear both registers. On each clock rising edge, update ins_p1 if fetch == 2'b01, or ins_p2 if fetch == 2'b10, otherwise retain previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived solely from ins_p1 (high 3 bits and low 5 bits, respectively), representing the instruction from the register source. The output ad2 reflects the full 8-bit ins_p2 register, representing the instruction from RAM/ROM. This separation avoids the need for a source tracking register and keeps logic simple and direct, simplifying both data path and output decoding.
