Instead of using a single register with a source flag, implement two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to independently store instructions from fetch==01 and fetch==10 sources. On reset, both registers are cleared. On each clock rising edge, the appropriate register is updated based on fetch. The outputs are directly derived combinationally from the respective registers: ins and ad1 come from ins_p1, and ad2 comes from ins_p2. This architecture cleanly separates instruction sources, simplifying timing paths and improving clarity.
