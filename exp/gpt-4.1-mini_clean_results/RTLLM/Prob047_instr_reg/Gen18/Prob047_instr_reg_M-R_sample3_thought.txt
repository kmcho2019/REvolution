Refactor by using two separate 8-bit registers `ins_p1` and `ins_p2` to directly store instructions from their respective fetch sources. The update logic for these registers is synchronous on the rising clock edge with an active-low reset. For each clock cycle, only the register corresponding to the fetch source (01 or 10) updates with the input data; otherwise, they hold their previous values. The outputs are derived combinationally: `ins` and `ad1` come from `ins_p1` (as the primary opcode/address source), while `ad2` directly outputs the full `ins_p2` content. This structure matches the original requirement more explicitly and avoids using a separate `last_fetch` register. It keeps source instructions separated, enabling simpler and clearer control paths and outputs.
