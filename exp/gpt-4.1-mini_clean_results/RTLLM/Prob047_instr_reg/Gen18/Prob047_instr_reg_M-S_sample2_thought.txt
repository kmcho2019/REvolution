Use two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, updated only on their respective fetch conditions to keep track of instructions from two sources separately. On reset, clear both registers. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (which stores fetch == 2'b01 data), and ad2 is directly assigned from ins_p2 (which stores fetch == 2'b10 data). This preserves the original design intent with clear separation and minimal complexity.
