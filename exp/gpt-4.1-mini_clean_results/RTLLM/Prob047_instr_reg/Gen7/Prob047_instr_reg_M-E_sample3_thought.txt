Implement two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 as per the original specification to hold instructions from the two sources. On each positive clock edge, if rst is low, clear both registers. Otherwise, load ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01, ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10, else retain previous values. For outputs, ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1’s high 3 bits and low 5 bits, respectively, reflecting instructions fetched from the first source. The output ad2 reflects the entire 8 bits of ins_p2, corresponding to instructions fetched from the second source. This approach clearly separates data storage for each source and directly maps outputs, reducing output multiplexing and clarifying data paths.
