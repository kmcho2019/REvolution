To improve PPA while maintaining functionality, I will split the combined 16-bit register into two separate 8-bit registers: ins_p1 and ins_p2. This separation allows fine-grained clock enables, which can reduce switching activity and dynamic power because only the targeted register updates on a given clock cycle. Also, I will use synchronous reset instead of asynchronous reset to enhance timing closure and simplify the reset path, which generally helps performance. This approach improves clock gating opportunities and clarifies intent for synthesis tools, potentially reducing area and improving timing without increasing complexity. Outputs are directly driven by registered values as before.
