To improve the existing design for better performance, power, and area (PPA), I will combine the two always blocks into a single synchronous process with a synchronous active-low reset. This will reduce duplication of reset logic and simplify timing paths. I will also implement clock enable signals based on the fetch inputs so registers only update when necessary, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power. The outputs remain combinationally derived from the stored registers. This streamlined design is more compact and efficient while preserving functionality.
