To optimize the original design for better PPA, I will separate the two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) instead of combining them into a single 16-bit register. This allows fine-grained clock enable signals for each register, which reduces unnecessary toggling and switching activity, thereby lowering dynamic power. 

I will implement synchronous reset (active low) for better timing closure and cleaner reset synchronization. By having two separate registers with independent enables based on fetch signal, synthesis tools can optimize better for performance and power. This separation also simplifies timing paths and improves critical path delays.

Outputs remain combinationally derived from the respective registers. This approach balances between area and power, likely reducing unnecessary toggling while maintaining clear logic and improving performance margins.
