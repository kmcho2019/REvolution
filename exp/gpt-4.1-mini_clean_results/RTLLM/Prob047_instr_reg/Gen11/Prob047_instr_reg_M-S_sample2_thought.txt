Use two simple 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to separately store instructions from each source directly on clock edges, with reset clearing both. Retain their values when fetch is neither 2'b01 nor 2'b10. Derive outputs directly from these registers: ins and ad1 from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2. This removes the need for additional state tracking and multiplexing logic, simplifying the design while preserving the original requirements.
