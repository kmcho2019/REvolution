To better align with the problem statement and potentially improve timing and power, I will explicitly implement two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold instructions fetched from the two sources. Each register updates only when its corresponding fetch code (2'b01 for ins_p1 and 2'b10 for ins_p2) is active, otherwise retains its previous value. The outputs will directly map ins and ad1 from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2, without the need for a selector bit or multiplexing at output. 

Additionally, I will make the reset synchronous (active low but asserted on posedge clk) to simplify timing and avoid asynchronous reset issues. Register write-enable signals dependent on fetch reduce unnecessary toggling and power consumption. This design keeps data paths separate and clear, facilitating better synthesis optimizations and improving timing and power metrics.
