Use two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, each updated based on fetch signals (01 or 10) at the clock's rising edge with active low reset initializing both to zero. Outputs ins and ad1 are always derived from ins_p1 (which holds instructions from source 1), and ad2 outputs the full 8-bit from ins_p2 (instructions from source 2). This clearly separates the two sources, avoids tracking the last fetch state, and simplifies output logic while fulfilling all functional requirements.
