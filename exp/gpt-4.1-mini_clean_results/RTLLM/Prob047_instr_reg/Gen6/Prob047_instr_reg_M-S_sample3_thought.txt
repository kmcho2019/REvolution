Use a synchronous active-low reset with a single always block triggered on the clock's rising edge. On reset, clear both instruction registers to zero. On each clock, update ins_p1 when fetch is 2'b01, update ins_p2 when fetch is 2'b10, else retain their values. Outputs are combinationally assigned from stored registers. This reduces complexity by removing asynchronous reset and unnecessary fetch checks, keeping the logic simple and clear.
