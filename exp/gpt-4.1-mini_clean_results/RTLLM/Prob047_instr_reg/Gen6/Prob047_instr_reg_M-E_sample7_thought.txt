Implement two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to independently store instructions fetched from the two different sources. On reset (active low), both registers clear to zero. On each rising clock edge, depending on the fetch input, update ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01 or ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10; otherwise retain their previous values. The outputs ins and ad1 are always derived from the high 3 and low 5 bits of ins_p1, respectively. The output ad2 always reflects the full 8 bits of ins_p2. This separation eliminates the need for a source flag, simplifies data flow, improves timing by isolating data paths, and directly meets the problem's register requirement and output mapping.
