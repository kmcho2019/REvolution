Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from different sources independently. On the rising clock edge, reset clears both registers. Based on fetch, update either ins_p1 or ins_p2 with the data input. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2. This clear separation simplifies the logic and avoids partial register updates.
