To optimize the instruction register for better power and timing, separate the 16-bit combined register into two independent 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2). Each register updates only when its corresponding fetch code is active, which reduces unnecessary toggling and enables simpler clock gating if supported by synthesis tools. Also, remove the redundant self-assignment in the default case by only updating the registers conditionally. This approach simplifies the design and potentially improves timing and power consumption. The outputs ins and ad1 derive from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2 as before.
