Instead of combining the instructions into one large register, I will use two separate 8-bit registers with explicit write enables derived from the fetch signal. This allows clear and simple logic, improving clarity and potentially easing synthesis optimization and timing. The reset will be synchronous to avoid asynchronous reset timing issues. Output signals will be derived directly from the registers, with minimal combinational logic. This modular approach improves maintainability and can help with clock gating if needed later.
