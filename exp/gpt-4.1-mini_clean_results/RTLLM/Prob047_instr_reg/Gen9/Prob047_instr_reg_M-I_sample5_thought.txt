To optimize the design for better PPA (Performance, Power, Area):

- Change the asynchronous active-low reset to a synchronous active-high reset for improved timing closure and simpler clock domain handling.
- Implement clock gating for ins_p1 and ins_p2 registers to minimize unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. This involves generating gated clocks enabled only when their respective register loads are active.
- Keep the separation of registers since it simplifies logic and avoids multiplexer overhead.
- Retain outputs as continuous assignments (wires), which is already optimal.
- This approach can marginally improve timing and power without increasing area significantly.
