Refactor the design by using two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, each capturing data from the respective sources as per fetch signals. On reset, both are cleared. On the clock rising edge, ins_p1 updates only when fetch==2'b01; ins_p2 updates only when fetch==2'b10; otherwise, both hold their values. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1, representing the opcode and register address fields, while ad2 directly reflects ins_p2 (the full 8-bit from source 2). This approach cleanly separates the storage of the two instruction sources without the need for a source selector flag, potentially improving timing by reducing multiplexing and enhancing clarity. Using separate registers aligns better with the problem statement and helps in potentially lower area and power due to simpler control logic.
