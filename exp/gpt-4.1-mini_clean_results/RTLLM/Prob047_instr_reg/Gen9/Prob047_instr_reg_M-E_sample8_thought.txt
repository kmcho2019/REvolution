Implement a single 8-bit instruction register with a 2-bit internal selector to keep track of the source of the last instruction loaded. On the rising clock edge with synchronous active-low reset, the instruction register updates only when fetch signals are 01 or 10, storing data accordingly. The 3-bit opcode (ins) and 5-bit register address (ad1) are always extracted from the stored instruction regardless of source. For fetch=10, the entire 8-bit instruction is also output via ad2; otherwise, ad2 is zeroed. This approach reduces register duplication, eliminates dual always blocks, and simplifies control by using a single register with conditional output multiplexing, improving area and power while maintaining functionality.
