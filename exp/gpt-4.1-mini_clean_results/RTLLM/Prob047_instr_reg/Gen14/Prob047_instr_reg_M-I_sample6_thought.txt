Improve the design by combining the two always blocks into a single synchronous block with a synchronous active-low reset for better timing closure and simplified reset logic. Use clock enable signals to update ins_p1 and ins_p2 registers only when the respective fetch conditions are met, reducing unnecessary toggling and thus power. This consolidation reduces redundant reset paths and improves area. The outputs are still derived combinationally from these registers.
