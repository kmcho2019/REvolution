Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 as originally specified, each dedicated to a different source of instruction data. On every rising clock edge with active reset low, reset both registers to zero. When fetch is 2'b01, load ins_p1 with data; when fetch is 2'b10, load ins_p2 with data. Keep registers unchanged otherwise. The outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1, representing opcode and register address fields, and ad2 outputs the full 8 bits stored in ins_p2, matching the respective sources. This approach closely follows the original specification, clearly separates the two instruction paths, and provides stable registered outputs without conditional multiplexing on outputs, enhancing timing and clarity.
