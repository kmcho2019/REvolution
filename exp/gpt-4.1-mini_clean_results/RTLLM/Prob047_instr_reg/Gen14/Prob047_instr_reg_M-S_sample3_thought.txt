Implement two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from the two sources independently. On the rising clock edge, if reset is active low, clear both registers. When fetch is 2'b01, load data into ins_p1; when fetch is 2'b10, load data into ins_p2. Otherwise, hold previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 come from the high and low bits of ins_p1, and ad2 outputs the full ins_p2. This direct mapping simplifies control logic and output assignments, avoids extra flags or multiplexing, and matches the problem description exactly.
