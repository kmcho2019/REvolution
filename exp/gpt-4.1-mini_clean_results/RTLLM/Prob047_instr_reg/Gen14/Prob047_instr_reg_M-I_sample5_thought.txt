To optimize the design for better performance and clarity, implement two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) exactly as specified. Each register holds instructions from one source, updated only when fetch matches its corresponding source. This reduces combinational multiplexing at output and allows independent clock gating or enable logic per register, improving timing and power efficiency. The outputs `ins` and `ad1` are extracted from ins_p1, and `ad2` directly outputs ins_p2. Reset initializes both registers to zero. This structure aligns exactly with the specification and reduces mux complexity on output paths, improving PPA metrics.
