Simplify the design by using two separate 8-bit registers for ins_p1 and ins_p2 as originally described. Use synchronous active-low reset for easier timing and clarity. On each clock, update ins_p1 if fetch is 2'b01, or ins_p2 if fetch is 2'b10; otherwise, hold previous values. Outputs are simply derived from these two registers. This approach is straightforward, readable, and easy to synthesize without the complexity of partial updates on a combined register.
