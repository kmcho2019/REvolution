Implement a single 8-bit instruction register that loads data on the rising clock edge only when fetch indicates a source (1 or 2), and use a 1-bit selector register to remember which source was last loaded. Outputs are then conditionally derived: if the last fetch was 1, ins and ad1 come from the high and low bits of the register; if the last fetch was 2, ad2 outputs the full register data, with ins and ad1 zeroed. This design reduces register duplication, combines control logic, and clearly distinguishes output fields based on the last fetched source, improving simplicity and resource sharing.
