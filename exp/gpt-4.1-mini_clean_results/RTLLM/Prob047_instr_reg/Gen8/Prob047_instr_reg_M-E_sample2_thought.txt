Implement the instruction register module using two separate always blocks, each dedicated to one of the registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2). This approach isolates the update logic for each register, improving readability and enabling independent timing analysis. Use synchronous active-low reset within the always blocks to ensure deterministic reset behavior at clock edges. Only update each register when the fetch signal matches its condition; otherwise, maintain previous state to avoid unnecessary toggling and reduce dynamic power. Derive outputs directly from these registers continuously. This design trades a slightly increased area due to duplicated always blocks for clarity and potential modular synthesis advantages, and it supports straightforward insertion of clock enables or power-saving techniques later.
