The module instr_reg has two 8-bit internal registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instruction data from different sources. On each positive clock edge, if reset is asserted low, both registers reset to zero. Otherwise, based on the fetch signal, if fetch==2'b01, data is stored into ins_p1; if fetch==2'b10, data is stored into ins_p2; else registers hold their values. Outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1 (split into 3 MSBs and 5 LSBs), and ad2 directly outputs ins_p2. This design cleanly separates instruction fields for further CPU processing.
