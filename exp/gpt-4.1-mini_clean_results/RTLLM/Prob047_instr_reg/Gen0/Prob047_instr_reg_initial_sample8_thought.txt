Implement a synchronous instruction register module with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On reset (active low), both registers clear to zero. On each rising clock edge, if fetch=2'b01, load data into ins_p1; if fetch=2'b10, load data into ins_p2; otherwise hold previous values. The outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1 (ins is upper 3 bits, ad1 is lower 5 bits), and ad2 is the full ins_p2.
