Implement the instr_reg module with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On every rising edge of clk, if rst is low, reset both registers to zero. Otherwise, based on the fetch input, load data into ins_p1 if fetch == 2'b01, or into ins_p2 if fetch == 2'b10. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from the high 3 bits and low 5 bits of ins_p1, respectively, and ad2 is the full 8-bit value of ins_p2.
