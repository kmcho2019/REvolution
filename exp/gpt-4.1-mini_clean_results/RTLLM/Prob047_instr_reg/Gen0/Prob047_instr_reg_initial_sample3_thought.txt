Create a module instr_reg with inputs clk, rst, fetch, and data. Use two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from different sources. On reset (active low), clear both registers. On rising clk edge, update ins_p1 if fetch==2'b01, ins_p2 if fetch==2'b10, else keep the values. Output ins as high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 as low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 as the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
