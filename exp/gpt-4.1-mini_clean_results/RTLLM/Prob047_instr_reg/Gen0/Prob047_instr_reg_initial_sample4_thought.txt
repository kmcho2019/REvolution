Create a module instr_reg with inputs clk, rst, fetch, and data. It contains two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is low, both registers reset to zero. Otherwise, depending on fetch, ins_p1 or ins_p2 captures the data input. The outputs ins (high 3 bits of ins_p1), ad1 (low 5 bits of ins_p1), and ad2 (full 8 bits of ins_p2) are assigned accordingly.
