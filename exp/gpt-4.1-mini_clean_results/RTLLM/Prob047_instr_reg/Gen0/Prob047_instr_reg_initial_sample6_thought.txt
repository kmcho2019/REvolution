Implement a synchronous instruction register with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is low, both registers are reset to zero. If fetch is 2'b01, load ins_p1 with data. If fetch is 2'b10, load ins_p2 with data. Otherwise, retain current values. Derive outputs ins and ad1 from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2. Specifically, ins is the top 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 is the bottom 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 is the entire ins_p2.
