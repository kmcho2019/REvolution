Create a module instr_reg with clk, rst, fetch, and data inputs. Internally maintain two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 that update on the rising edge of clk. On reset (active low), zero both registers. If fetch is 2'b01, load ins_p1 with data; if fetch is 2'b10, load ins_p2 with data. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are derived as follows: ins is the upper 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 is the lower 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 is the entire 8 bits of ins_p2.
