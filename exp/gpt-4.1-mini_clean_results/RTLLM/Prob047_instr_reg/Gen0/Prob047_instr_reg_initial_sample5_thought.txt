The module instr_reg stores instructions from two sources in two separate 8-bit registers: ins_p1 and ins_p2. On a rising clock edge, if reset is active low, both registers are cleared. Depending on the 2-bit fetch signal, data is loaded into either ins_p1 (fetch==2'b01) or ins_p2 (fetch==2'b10). The outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (high 3 bits and low 5 bits respectively), while ad2 outputs the full 8-bit value of ins_p2.
