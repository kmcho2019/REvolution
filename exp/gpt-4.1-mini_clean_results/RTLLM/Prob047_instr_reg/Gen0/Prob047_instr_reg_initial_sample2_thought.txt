The module instr_reg has two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to hold instructions fetched from different sources. On each clock rising edge, if reset is active low, these registers are cleared. Depending on fetch input, ins_p1 or ins_p2 is updated with the input data. The outputs ins and ad1 are extracted from ins_p1 (the high 3 bits and low 5 bits respectively), while ad2 outputs the full 8-bit ins_p2 value.
