Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to hold the instructions from the two sources independently. On the rising edge of clk or active low reset, reset both registers to zero if rst is low. Otherwise, update ins_p1 when fetch is 2'b01 and ins_p2 when fetch is 2'b10, holding their previous values otherwise. The outputs ins and ad1 come from ins_p1 (split into high 3 bits and low 5 bits), while ad2 outputs the full ins_p2 register. This approach uses clearly separated registers for better modularity and potentially easier debug/traceability.
