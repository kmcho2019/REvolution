To optimize the original design for power and timing, I will implement a clock enable (load) signal derived from the fetch input to update the combined register only when necessary, thus reducing unnecessary toggling. Also, I will replace the redundant `instr_combined <= instr_combined;` in the default case with no register update to allow inferred latches or registers with enables, improving synthesis results. Using non-blocking assignments and a clock enable will improve timing and reduce switching power by avoiding register writes when fetch is neither 01 nor 10. Output assignments remain unchanged for clarity. This approach keeps the design functionally the same but reduces dynamic power and improves timing slack.
