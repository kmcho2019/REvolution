Instead of storing the two instruction sources separately in two distinct registers, use a single 8-bit register with an internal selector to latch the latest valid instruction based on fetch. Use an explicit write enable derived from fetch signals to control register update and prevent unnecessary toggling. Additionally, decode outputs dynamically from the stored instruction or the second source register depending on fetch, providing a unified output interface. This reduces register count, simplifies control, and ensures minimal power by gating the register write with an enable signal.
