To optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I will implement a synchronous active-low reset to simplify timing and reduce potential reset-related glitches. I will also remove redundant self-assignments in the always block, since registers naturally hold their value if not assigned, which reduces unnecessary toggling and power. This change preserves functionality but improves power efficiency and possibly timing. The outputs remain combinationally driven from registers as before. This optimization maintains clarity and correctness while improving PPA.
