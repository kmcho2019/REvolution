Use two separate 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to independently store instructions from each source. On a positive clock edge, if reset is active low, clear both registers. When fetch is 2'b01, update ins_p1 with data; when fetch is 2'b10, update ins_p2 with data; otherwise, keep their previous values. The outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (high 3 bits and low 5 bits), while ad2 directly outputs ins_p2. This separate storage simplifies combinational output logic and maintains both source instructions concurrently, enabling parallel access if needed.
