Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 updated on the rising clock edge with synchronous active-low reset, each capturing data based on the fetch signal (01 or 10). Retain their values otherwise. Derive outputs ins and ad1 from ins_p1 (the instruction from the first source), and output ad2 directly from ins_p2 (the full 8-bit data from the second source). This straightforward approach clearly matches the specification without extra state tracking or multiplexing, minimizing logic and making the design easy to understand and verify.
