The original design uses a single register ins_reg to store instructions from either source controlled by fetch signal, which is area-efficient but has combinational output multiplexing depending on fetch, potentially impacting timing and power due to continuous logic switching.

Improvement strategy:
1. Use two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) as specified in the problem, each holding instructions from their respective sources.
2. Registers update only when fetch signals indicate valid data (fetch==01 for ins_p1, fetch==10 for ins_p2).
3. Outputs derived directly from these registers without combinational multiplexing on fetch, eliminating output glitches and reducing power.
4. Registers reset asynchronously on active low reset.
5. This separation reduces output logic switching, improving timing and power at a slight area cost, aligning precisely with problem requirements.

This approach achieves:
- Clear separation of instruction sources.
- Minimizes output switching due to fetch toggling.
- Keeps the design modular and easier for downstream processing.
