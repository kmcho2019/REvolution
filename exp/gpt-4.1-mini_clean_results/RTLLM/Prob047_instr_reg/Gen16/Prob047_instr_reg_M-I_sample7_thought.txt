To optimize PPA further, I will reduce switching activity by introducing a clock enable (ce) signal that enables the instruction register update only when fetch is valid (01 or 10). This avoids unnecessary toggling of the register and the 2-bit fetch state.

Moreover, I will replace the 2-bit `last_fetch` register with a 1-bit flag `ins_p2_sel` to remember only if the last instruction came from source 2 (fetch=10). This reduces area by shrinking state storage and shortens the critical path by simplifying output muxing logic.

The register will update only when `ce` is asserted, ensuring less switching and better power.

Outputs remain combinationally extracted from the stored instruction.

Overall, this approach balances low toggle rate, reduced register count, and simpler control logic for improved PPA.
