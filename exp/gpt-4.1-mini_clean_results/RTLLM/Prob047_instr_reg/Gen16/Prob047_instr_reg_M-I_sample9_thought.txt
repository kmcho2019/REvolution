To improve PPA metrics, split the single register into two dedicated 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 for the two fetch sources. This reduces combinational multiplexing logic on outputs, improving timing and potentially lowering power by localizing updates to each register. Outputs ins and ad1 come only from ins_p1, while ad2 comes only from ins_p2, removing unnecessary gating. The active-low reset initializes both registers. This modular approach enhances timing by shortening critical paths and can slightly increase area but yields better overall PPA.
