Implement the instruction register with a single synchronous always block triggered on the positive clock edge and a synchronous active-low reset. Use an internal state machine with two states corresponding to valid data capture sources: 01 (register) and 10 (RAM/ROM). Store the fetched data in a 9-bit register where the MSB acts as a valid flag indicating which instruction register is active. This eliminates the need for two separate 8-bit registers. Output decoding dynamically extracts ins and ad1 from the lower 8 bits when fetch = 01, or outputs ad2 from the entire 8 bits when fetch = 10, controlled by the valid bit. This approach simplifies storage, synchronizes all logic to clk with synchronous reset, and reduces power by avoiding separate registers and redundant resets.
