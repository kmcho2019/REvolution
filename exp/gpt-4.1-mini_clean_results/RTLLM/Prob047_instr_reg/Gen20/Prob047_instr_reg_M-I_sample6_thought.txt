The original design merges two instruction registers into one, using fetch to select outputs, which can cause combinational output glitches and timing challenges. To improve PPA, especially timing and power, I will explicitly implement two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 as specified originally. Each updates only when fetch matches its source, reducing unnecessary toggling. The outputs will be registered (latched) on clk to minimize combinational logic on output paths, improving timing closure. Active low reset clears both registers. This approach maintains modularity, avoids combinational muxes on outputs, and enables better timing and power optimization by isolating the two instruction sources. The output signals ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2, matching the original specification exactly.
