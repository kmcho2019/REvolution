Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to store instructions from different sources as originally specified. On reset, clear both registers. On each clock edge, update ins_p1 if fetch is 2'b01, update ins_p2 if fetch is 2'b10, otherwise retain previous values. Outputs are derived directly from the registers: ins and ad1 from ins_p1, ad2 from ins_p2. This eliminates the need for a source tag and conditional output logic, simplifying control and reducing combinational logic.
