Use a synchronous reset instead of asynchronous to simplify the reset logic and avoid sensitivity to both clock and reset edges. Use a single always block triggered only on the rising clock edge. On reset, zero both registers; otherwise, update ins_p1 or ins_p2 based on fetch, or retain their values. Outputs are continuously assigned from stored registers, extracting the specified bits. This simplifies the code by removing the asynchronous reset and relying on synchronous reset which is easier to handle in most synthesis tools and improves timing predictability while preserving all functionality.
