Fuse the clarity and directness of maintaining two distinct 8-bit instruction registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) from the original specification with the power and area optimization ideas from the examples by adding clock enables to reduce unnecessary toggling. The design will hold ins_p1 for fetch=01 and ins_p2 for fetch=10, retaining previous values otherwise, with synchronous active low reset. Outputs ins and ad1 derive from ins_p1 (since they represent opcode and register address fields), while ad2 outputs the full ins_p2 data, or zero when ins_p2 is empty. Clock enable signals gate register updates, improving power by reducing switching. This explicit two-register design simplifies combinational output logic and eases timing optimization compared to multiplexed single-register solutions, while clock gating balances power consumption. The reset and fetch logic will be clear and simple, enhancing readability and maintainability.
