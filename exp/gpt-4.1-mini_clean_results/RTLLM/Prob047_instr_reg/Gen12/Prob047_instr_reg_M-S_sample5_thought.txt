Use two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 to directly store data based on fetch signal. On reset, clear both registers. When fetch is 01, update ins_p1 with data; when fetch is 10, update ins_p2 with data; else keep previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1, and ad2 is derived from ins_p2. This matches the original specification clearly and keeps logic simple without multiplexing in outputs.
