Implement the instruction register using a single always block with synchronous active-low reset. Use a multiplexer controlled by the fetch signal inside the always block to selectively update either ins_p1 or ins_p2, while retaining the other register unchanged. This approach centralizes all register updates, simplifies reset logic, and avoids multiple always blocks. Outputs are directly assigned from the stored registers. This architectural style enhances clarity, reduces area by minimizing duplicated reset logic, and improves timing by having a single clock domain update path.
