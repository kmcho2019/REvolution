To optimize PPA (performance, power, area), I will combine both instruction registers into a single 16-bit register to reduce register count and area. Then, use minimal combinational logic to select and output required fields. This reduces the number of flip-flops and associated clock load, potentially improving timing and lowering power. The fetch signal will be decoded as a write enable with mux to select which half of the 16-bit register to update. This approach maintains all functionality but reduces hardware complexity, lowering area and power and potentially improving timing by reducing switching and routing complexity.
