Implement the instr_reg module with a single 16-bit register that concatenates both instructions from the two fetch sources. On reset, the full register clears to zero. On each clock edge, depending on fetch, either the upper or lower 8 bits of the register are updated with the incoming data, while the other half retains its previous value. This approach uses only one register and bit slicing to store and separate instructions, potentially reducing area and simplifying the update logic. The outputs ins and ad1 are extracted from the lower 8 bits (first instruction), and ad2 is taken from the upper 8 bits (second instruction).
