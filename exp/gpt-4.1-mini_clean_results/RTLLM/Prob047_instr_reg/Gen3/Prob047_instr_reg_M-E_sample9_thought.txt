Implement a single always block with a synchronous active-low reset that updates both ins_p1 and ins_p2 registers simultaneously on the rising edge of clk. Use if-else statements to selectively load data into ins_p1 or ins_p2 depending on the fetch signal, and hold previous values otherwise. This simplifies reset handling, ensures consistent synchronous operation, reduces register logic duplication, and avoids asynchronous reset-related timing issues. Outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are directly assigned from the stored registers.
