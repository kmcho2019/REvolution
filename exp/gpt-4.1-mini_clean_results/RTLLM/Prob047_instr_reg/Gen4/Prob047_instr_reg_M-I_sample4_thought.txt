To improve the previous design for better PPA metrics, I will merge the two always blocks into a single synchronous always block with a synchronous active-low reset. This simplifies the reset logic and may reduce area and improve timing by avoiding asynchronous reset paths. I will implement clock enable logic inside the same block to update ins_p1 or ins_p2 only when the corresponding fetch signal matches, preventing unnecessary toggling and saving power. This single-process design is also easier for synthesis tools to optimize. Output logic remains combinationally derived from stored registers.
