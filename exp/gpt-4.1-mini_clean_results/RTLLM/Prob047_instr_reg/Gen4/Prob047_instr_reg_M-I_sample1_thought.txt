To improve PPA, I will split the combined 16-bit register into two separate 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. This reduces mux complexity and shortens the critical path, improving timing (WNS, TNS). It also enables selective updating of each register, reducing dynamic power since only one register toggles per cycle. Area can benefit from simpler control logic and smaller registers. The code will retain the asynchronous active low reset and update ins_p1 on fetch==01 and ins_p2 on fetch==10, else retain their previous values. Outputs are assigned accordingly. This approach maintains original functionality while optimizing performance, power, and area.
