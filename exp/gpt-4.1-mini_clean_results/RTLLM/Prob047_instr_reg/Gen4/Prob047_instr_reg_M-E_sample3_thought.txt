Implement a single 8-bit instruction register that updates depending on the fetch signal with synchronous active-low reset. Use a multiplexer to select which register value to update (ins_p1 or ins_p2) from a combined register bank implemented as a 2-element array. On a clock edge, if fetch indicates source 1 or 2, update the corresponding element; otherwise, hold values. Outputs extract ins and ad1 from the first register and ad2 from the second. This approach leverages indexed register storage, unifies register handling into one always block, and uses array indexing for clarity and compactness.
