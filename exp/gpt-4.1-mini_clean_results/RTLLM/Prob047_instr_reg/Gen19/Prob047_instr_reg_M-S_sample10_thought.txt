Use two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) as originally specified to store instructions from the two fetch sources independently. On reset, clear both registers. On clock rising edge, update ins_p1 if fetch is 2'b01, and update ins_p2 if fetch is 2'b10; otherwise, retain old values. The outputs are directly assigned from the stored registers: ins and ad1 come from ins_p1's high and low bits, and ad2 comes from ins_p2's full bits. This keeps the design straightforward and true to the original specification without extra multiplexing.
