Use two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to directly store instructions from the two different sources indicated by fetch. On the rising clock edge with active low reset, clear both registers. When fetch is 2'b01, load ins_p1 with data; when fetch is 2'b10, load ins_p2 with data. Otherwise, retain previous values. Extract ins and ad1 from ins_p1 to prioritize instructions from source 1, and output the entire ins_p2 as ad2 representing instructions from source 2. This approach clearly separates storage by source, simplifies output logic, and supports simultaneous presence of two instructions for parallel processing.
