To improve the PPA while preserving functionality, I will combine the two always blocks into a single synchronous always block with a synchronous active-low reset. This reduces duplicated reset logic and enables better synthesis optimization. Inside the block, ins_p1 and ins_p2 are updated conditionally based on the fetch value. When fetch is neither 01 nor 10, registers hold their values, minimizing unnecessary toggling for power savings. Outputs remain combinationally assigned from the stored values. This design enhances timing closure, area efficiency, and power by reducing redundant control and flip-flop logic while maintaining clear separation of instruction sources.
