Implement a sequential 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier using three main registers: an accumulator (32-bit signed), a multiplier with appended bit (9 bits), and the multiplicand (16 bits sign-extended). On reset, load the multiplicand and multiplier (with appended zero) into registers and clear the accumulator and cycle counter. Then for 4 cycles (since Radix-4 processes 2 multiplier bits per cycle), at each cycle, form a 3-bit Booth code from the least significant 3 bits of the multiplier register, decode it into a multiplication factor (-2, -1, 0, 1, 2), and add the corresponding multiple of the multiplicand to the accumulator. Then arithmetic right shift the concatenated {accumulator, multiplier} register by 2 bits to prepare for the next cycle. After 4 cycles, the accumulator contains the full 16-bit product in its lower bits. Output this product and assert the ready signal. This method avoids multiple conflicting assignments and uses a clean sequential state machine with clear cycle counting. Only Verilog-2001 constructs are used for broad compatibility.
