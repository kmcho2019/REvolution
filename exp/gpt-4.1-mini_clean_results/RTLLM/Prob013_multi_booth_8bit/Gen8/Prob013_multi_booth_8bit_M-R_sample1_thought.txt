Refactor the Radix-4 Booth multiplier to use a clear state machine with distinct IDLE, CALC, and DONE states for better control and clarity.

- On reset or IDLE, sign-extend inputs `a` and `b` to 16 bits, extend the multiplier by 1 bit appended zero for Booth encoding (17 bits total), initialize the accumulator, counter, and ready signal.

- In CALC state, at each clock cycle:
  - Extract the lowest 3 bits of multiplier_reg to decode Booth encoding.
  - Calculate the Booth factor combinationally.
  - Update the accumulator with multiplicand multiplied by the Booth factor (shifted by 2*counter).
  - Arithmetic right shift the multiplier_reg by 2 bits to move to next Booth group.
  - Increment counter.
- After 4 cycles, move to DONE state, set ready and output the lower 16 bits of accumulator as product.

- In DONE state, wait for reset or new start.

Use separate combinational logic for Booth decoding and factor calculation, avoiding partial assignments inside the clocked block.

This clear state machine and separation of combinational Booth decoding make the design more reliable and maintainable, fulfilling the problem's requirements precisely.
