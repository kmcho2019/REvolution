Implement a simplified 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier with synchronous operation using a 5-bit counter counting up to 8 iterations (each iteration processes 2 bits). On reset, sign-extend the multiplicand and multiplier, initialize the combined register with accumulator=0 and multiplier extended by one bit zero at LSB for Booth recoding. Each clock cycle, extract 3 bits to determine Booth encoding factor (-2, -1, 0, 1, 2), calculate partial product accordingly, add it to accumulator, then arithmetic right shift combined register by 2 bits. After 8 cycles, output the product and set ready signal. This approach is clean, fully synchronous, uses fixed bit widths, and matches the radix-4 Booth algorithm with minimal complexity.
