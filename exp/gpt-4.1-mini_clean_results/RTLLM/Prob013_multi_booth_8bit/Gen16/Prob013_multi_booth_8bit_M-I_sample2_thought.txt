The original design implements a radix-4 Booth multiplier correctly but can be optimized for power and area by reducing unnecessary switching and simplifying sign-extension logic:

Improvement strategy:
1. **Conditional accumulation**: Only update the accumulator when the partial product is non-zero, reducing unnecessary toggling and saving dynamic power.
2. **Simplify sign-extension**: Replace repeated concatenations with Verilog's arithmetic signed extensions using `$signed()` casts to reduce logic area and improve timing.
3. **Reduce width in multiplier shifting**: Use a 10-bit multiplier register to simplify sign extension and shifting since the multiplier is originally 8 bits + 1 LSB for booth recode + 1 MSB sign extension, avoiding complex concatenation at each shift.
4. **FSM clarity**: Remove the separate next_state logic and use a simple state register with direct transitions to simplify the logic.
5. **Hold outputs stable after done state**: Maintain stable output and ready signal without toggling, reducing glitches.
6. **Use blocking assignments in combinational logic** and non-blocking in sequential logic (already good).
7. **Minor coding style improvements** for clarity.

This should reduce switching activity in the accumulator register, simplify the logic for sign extension and shifting, and thus improve power and area without affecting timing significantly.

The number of cycles remains 5 since it’s radix-4 and 8-bit input.
