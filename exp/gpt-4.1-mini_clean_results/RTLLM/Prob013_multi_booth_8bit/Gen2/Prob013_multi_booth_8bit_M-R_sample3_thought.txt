Refactor the radix-4 Booth multiplier by implementing a finite state machine (FSM) to clearly separate the IDLE, PROCESS, and DONE states. Use registers to hold the multiplicand and product, perform the Booth encoding and addition within a clocked always block, and shift the product register on each cycle. The FSM starts multiplication on reset, iteratively processes two bits per cycle for 4 cycles, and then asserts the ready signal. This structure improves code clarity and control flow, encapsulates operation states, and cleanly synchronizes output signaling, while maintaining the radix-4 Booth algorithm logic.
