The primary fix is to move the declaration of the variable `digit` outside the always block to conform with Verilog syntax, as variables cannot be declared inside procedural blocks in Verilog (unlike SystemVerilog). Additionally, I will maintain the radix-4 Booth implementation with 4 cycles as in the original design, which is efficient for 8-bit inputs. The logic for selecting the three bits for Booth encoding will use a fully compatible Verilog part-select method instead of SystemVerilog's indexed part-select. I will implement a function for sign extension to safely handle shifting and multiplication by 2. The ready signal `rdy` will be asserted once all four iterations are done, and the output product `p` will be assigned accordingly. The multiplicand and multiplier will be sign-extended to 16 bits on reset, as specified. All variables will have consistent signed widths. This design strictly follows the radix-4 Booth algorithm, avoids the use of SystemVerilog features, and respects the problem specifications.
