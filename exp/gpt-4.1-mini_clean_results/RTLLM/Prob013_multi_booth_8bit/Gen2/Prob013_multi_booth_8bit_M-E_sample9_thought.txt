Implement a synchronous Radix-4 Booth multiplier that works in multiple clock cycles, processing two bits of the multiplier per cycle. On reset, sign-extend inputs into 16-bit registers and initialize internal registers. Use a 3-bit counter counting up to 4 iterations (since 8 bits / 2 bits per cycle = 4). For each cycle, extract 3 bits (including one overlapping bit from the previous cycle) to perform Booth encoding for multiples of -2, -1, 0, +1, or +2 times the multiplicand. Accumulate the partial products shifted by 2*cycle_count into a 34-bit accumulator to avoid overflow. After 4 iterations, output the lower 16 bits of the accumulator as the product and assert the ready signal. This method avoids declaring variables inside always blocks by pre-declaring all needed registers, and cleanly manages bit extraction with a fixed width multiplier register extended by one LSB zero bit.
