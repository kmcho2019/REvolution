Implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier by representing the multiplier with an appended zero bit, processing two bits of the multiplier at a time over 4 cycles. Instead of shifting the multiplicand variable amounts each cycle, pre-shift the multiplicand once at reset for multiples (1x and 2x), then add or subtract these pre-shifted values shifted by the iteration count. Maintain a 16-bit accumulator as the product register. The multiplier register is shifted right arithmetically by 2 bits each cycle, revealing the next Booth bits. This method reduces variable shifts inside the clocked block by precomputing needed multiplicand multiples and shifting by fixed amounts. Upon completion (after 4 cycles), output the accumulator as product and assert ready. This design uses simpler, smaller shifts and fewer computations per clock cycle, improving timing and resource usage.
