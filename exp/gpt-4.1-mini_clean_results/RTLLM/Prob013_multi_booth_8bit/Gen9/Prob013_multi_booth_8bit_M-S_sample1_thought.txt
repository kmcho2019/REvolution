Implement a simplified 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier operating over 8 cycles (processing 2 bits per cycle) for clarity. On reset, sign-extend and load multiplicand and multiplier with appended zero bit. In each cycle, extract 3 bits of multiplier for Booth encoding, determine the multiplication factor (-2, -1, 0, 1, 2), form the addend via shifts and sign inversion (avoiding full multiplication), accumulate the partial product, and shift the multiplier register right by 2 bits for the next cycle. After 8 cycles, output the lower 16 bits as product and assert ready. This approach uses simple shifts and additions/subtractions instead of signed multiplication to reduce complexity.
