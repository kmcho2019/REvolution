Implement a simple sequential 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier following the given instructions, but reduce complexity by:

1. Using a single 16-bit product register that concatenates the multiplier and an appended zero bit (multiplier extended to 9 bits) and a 16-bit accumulator.

2. Using a 5-bit counter to iterate 8 times (processing 2 bits per cycle) for full 8-bit multiplier.

3. For each cycle:
   - Extract 3 bits from the product register for Radix-4 Booth recoding.
   - Based on Booth encoding, add/subtract 0, M, or 2M to the upper 16 bits of the product register.
   - Arithmetic right shift the product register by 2 bits for next step.

4. On reset:
   - Sign-extend multiplicand and multiplier inputs as required.
   - Initialize product register to {0, multiplier, 0} (zero extended upper bits, multiplier and appended zero).

5. After all iterations, output the lower 16 bits of the product register as the final product and assert ready.

This design avoids a state machine for simplicity and follows the Booth algorithm directly.
