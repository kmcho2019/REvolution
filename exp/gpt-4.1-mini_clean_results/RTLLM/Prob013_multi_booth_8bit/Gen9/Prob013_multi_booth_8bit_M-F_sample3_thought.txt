To optimize the existing radix-4 Booth multiplier, replace the multiplication of the multiplicand by booth_factor (which can only be -2, -1, 0, 1, or 2) with shift and add/subtract operations. This avoids the costly multiplier and improves timing and area. Use conditional logic to generate the partial product by left-shifting the multiplicand by 1 (for factor ±2) or using it directly (for factor ±1), and apply sign based on the factor. Also, introduce a clock enable by updating the product register only when booth_factor is non-zero to reduce switching power. The rest of the design remains unchanged, including sign-extensions, state machine, and output logic.
