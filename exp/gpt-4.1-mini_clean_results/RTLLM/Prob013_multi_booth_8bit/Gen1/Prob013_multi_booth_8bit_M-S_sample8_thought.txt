Implement a simple 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier with a synchronous reset and start-on-reset behavior. On reset, load and sign-extend inputs into internal registers and clear the product, counter, and ready signal. Each clock cycle, process two multiplier bits at a time using Radix-4 Booth encoding rules, update the partial product accordingly, and shift the multiplier register right by two bits. After 4 cycles (since 8 bits processed 2 bits per cycle), output the 16-bit product and set ready high. This approach matches the radix-4 Booth algorithm’s grouping and keeps the design straightforward and synchronous, avoiding asynchronous resets with input-dependent assignments.
