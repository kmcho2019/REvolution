Implement a radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs using an iterative approach over 4 cycles, each processing 2 multiplier bits plus one overlapping bit to determine the Booth encoding. On reset, sign-extend multiplicand (a) and multiplier (b) to 16 bits, initialize product and counter, and set ready low. Each cycle, examine 3 bits from the multiplier (bits 2*i+1 downto 2*i-1) to generate a signed digit (-2,-1,0,1,2) controlling addition or subtraction of multiplicand multiples (multiplicand, 2*multiplicand) shifted appropriately by 2*i. Accumulate result in a signed 17-bit product register to capture sign extension and overflow, then assign final 16-bit output product at end. Set ready high when done. This design faithfully follows radix-4 Booth encoding, improves efficiency by using 4 cycles, and aligns with synchronous reset and clocked operations.
