5. **Registers:**
   - A 33-bit register combining partial product (17 bits) and multiplier extended by 1 bit.

6. **Ready Signal:** Asserted when multiplication completes and held until reset.

7. **Synthesis-Friendly:** Use only non-blocking assignments on clock edge, synchronous reset, and clearly structured code.
