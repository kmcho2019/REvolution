Implement a simplified Radix-4 Booth multiplier with synchronous operation controlled by a 5-bit counter. On reset, sign-extend inputs and initialize registers and counter. Each clock cycle while counter < 16, examine current multiplier bit: if 1, add multiplicand to the product register; then shift the multiplicand left by 1. Increment the counter each cycle. When counter reaches 16, set ready signal. This approach uses basic registers and a straightforward iterative algorithm without complex FSM or booth decoding, closely matching the user's original specification.
