Refactor the Radix-4 Booth multiplier by implementing a simple FSM with explicit IDLE and BUSY states to manage operation flow. On reset, load sign-extended inputs and initialize registers. When starting multiplication, process one Booth cycle per clock: decode Booth factor combinationally outside the sequential block using a current 3-bit window from the multiplier register. Perform addition or subtraction and shift the multiplier register accordingly inside the sequential block. Use a cycle counter to track progress through 4 cycles. Assert ready when done. This cleanly separates control (FSM states) from datapath (arithmetic) and uses combinational logic with `assign` for Booth encoding and partial product selection, improving code modularity and readability.
