To improve the PPA of the original radix-4 Booth multiplier while maintaining functionality, I will:

- Pre-sign-extend the multiplicand and multiplier once on reset, storing them in fixed-width registers. This avoids repeated sign-extension logic in the combinational partial product logic.
- Replace the large combinational partial product calculation with smaller, sign-extended partial products directly derived from the sign-extended multiplicand, avoiding repeated concatenations.
- Reduce accumulator width from 18 to 17 bits by carefully analyzing the maximum bit-width needed, since radix-4 steps shift by 2 bits each step, and inputs are 9 bits signed, 17 bits suffice.
- Use registers for the partial product and accumulator updates to pipeline and reduce critical path within the RUN cycle.
- Separate the multiplier arithmetic shift into a simple register shift operation to reduce muxing logic.
- Add a slight refinement in the FSM to clearly separate load and compute phases, making the ready signal logic cleaner.
- Hold outputs stable in DONE until next reset or start of new operation.
- Keep the design synchronous with all registers updated only on clk rising edge and asynchronous reset for robust synthesis.
This should reduce logic depth and switching, improving timing and power, and marginally reduce area by simplifying sign extension and partial product logic.
