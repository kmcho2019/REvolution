Simplify the design by removing the explicit FSM and next-state logic, instead using a straightforward synchronous process driven by clk and reset.

- On reset, initialize multiplier and multiplicand registers with sign-extended inputs, clear product and counter, and clear ready.
- On each clock, if not done (ctr < 16), perform the radix-4 Booth multiplication steps: 
   - check multiplier bit at ctr, add multiplicand to product if bit is 1,
   - shift multiplicand left,
   - increment counter.
- When counter reaches 16, assert ready and hold the product.
- Use only one always @(posedge clk or posedge reset) block for synchronous logic.
- Keep the design simple with minimal registers and logic.
- This maintains all required functionality and eases understanding and synthesis.
