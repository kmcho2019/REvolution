To improve the PPA (performance, power, area) of the original radix-4 Booth multiplier, I will apply the following optimizations while preserving functionality:

1. Simplify sign extension by using Verilog signed casts and built-in sign-extension rather than manual concatenations, reducing logic depth and area.

2. Apply clock gating to accumulator updates: only update accumulator when partial_product is non-zero, reducing unnecessary switching and dynamic power.

3. Replace manual shift of multiplicand and multiplier with arithmetic right shift operations to clarify intent and enable easier optimization by synthesis tools.

4. Remove the FSM states IDLE and DONE since the original problem states reset initializes the module and multiplication proceeds immediately; instead, use a simple synchronous process with a 5-step counter and an rdy flag, simplifying control logic and area.

5. Use 5-step counter (from 0 to 4) since radix-4 multiplies 8-bit numbers in 5 cycles, removing redundant state encodings and minimizing control registers.

6. Keep partial product combinational logic separated and clean.

7. After multiplication done, hold output and rdy stable until next reset.

This yields a cleaner, more hardware-friendly design with fewer registers and simpler control, lowering area and power, and potentially improving timing.

Note: This design fully respects the original problem ports and behavior, and provides the ready signal as specified.
