To fix the syntax issues and improve clarity, I will refactor the design to use a straightforward synchronous state machine that progresses through the multiplication steps.

Key changes:
- All registers (including temporaries like shifted_multiplicand and add_value) are declared at module scope, avoiding illegal declarations inside always blocks.
- The Booth decoding and partial product calculation are performed inside the sequential always block using the pre-declared registers.
- To avoid shifting signed values by variable amounts inside always blocks, I will use a combinational process to compute the shifted multiplicand based on the current count.
- The design operates over 4 iterations (for 8-bit input with radix-4 Booth).
- The ready signal is asserted once all iterations finish.
- The multiplier (b) is extended with one zero bit LSB outside the always block, for Booth encoding.
- Multiplicand and multiplier inputs are sign-extended at reset.
- The product accumulator is 17 bits to accommodate carry/sign bits.
- The final 16-bit product output is assigned from the lower bits of the product accumulator.

This approach ensures all registers are declared properly, uses a clean FSM approach, and strictly follows the radix-4 Booth algorithm for 8-bit inputs.
