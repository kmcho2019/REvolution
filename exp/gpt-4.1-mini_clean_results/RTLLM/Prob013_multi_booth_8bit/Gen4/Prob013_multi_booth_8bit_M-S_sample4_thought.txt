Implement a simple radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs using a single 16-bit accumulator and multiplier register with an appended zero bit. On reset, load sign-extended inputs into multiplicand and multiplier registers. On each clock cycle, extract 3 bits from the multiplier (including appended zero), decode Booth factor (-2 to 2), add/subtract shifted multiplicand accordingly, then arithmetic right shift multiplier by 2 bits. Repeat for 4 cycles. When done, output the lower 16 bits of accumulator as the product and assert ready. Use minimal bit widths and straightforward signed arithmetic for simplicity while preserving correctness.
