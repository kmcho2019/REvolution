I will simplify the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier by:
- Using a 17-bit multiplier extended with an LSB zero for encoding.
- Performing 8 iterations (processing 2 multiplier bits per cycle).
- Using simple signed arithmetic and precomputing +multiplicand and +2*multiplicand once at reset.
- Generating partial products via a straightforward case statement without repeated shifts.
- Accumulating partial products in a 32-bit signed register shifted by 2*iteration count.
- Using a 4-bit counter and a single always block triggered by clk and reset.
- Setting ready flag when done and outputting the lower 16 bits of the product.
This keeps the design minimal and clear while preserving Radix-4 Booth functionality.
