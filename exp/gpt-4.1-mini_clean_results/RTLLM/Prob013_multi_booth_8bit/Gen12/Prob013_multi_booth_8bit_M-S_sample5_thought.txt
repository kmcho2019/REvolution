Implement a straightforward 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier using a 5-bit counter and registers for multiplicand and multiplier extended and sign-extended to 16 bits. The multiplier register will be extended by one bit at the LSB for Booth encoding. 

At reset:
- sign-extend multiplicand and multiplier to 16 bits.
- initialize product register to 0.
- load multiplier shifted left by 1 bit (appended zero) for Booth processing.
- clear the counter and ready signal.

Each clock cycle, if counter < 8 (since radix-4 processes two bits per cycle for 8-bit multiplier):
- extract 3 bits from multiplier register for Booth encoding.
- determine multiplier to add (+0, +1, +2, -1, -2 times multiplicand).
- update product accordingly.
- arithmetic right shift combined {product, multiplier} by 2 bits to process next two bits.
- increment counter.

Once counter == 8, output product and set ready signal.

This approach maintains clarity and correctness while avoiding complex combined large registers or multiple conflicting assignments per clock cycle.
