Refactor the multiplier by implementing a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, BUSY, and DONE.  
- On reset, move to IDLE and clear registers.  
- On receiving inputs in IDLE, latch inputs into signed registers and start multiplication by moving to BUSY state.  
- In BUSY, perform one step of Booth multiplication per clock:  
  - Check current bit of multiplier, add multiplicand to product if bit=1.  
  - Shift multiplicand left.  
  - Increment counter.  
- When counter reaches 16, move to DONE and assert ready.  
- Remain in DONE until reset.  
This structure separates input latching and computation clearly, improving readability and control flow. The outputs are registered and the module remains synchronous with a single always block for FSM and datapath, avoiding combinational assigns for main logic to ensure proper sequential behavior.
