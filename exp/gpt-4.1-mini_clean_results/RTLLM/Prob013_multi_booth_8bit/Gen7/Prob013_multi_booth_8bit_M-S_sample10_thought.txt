Implement a simplified Radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs with a synchronous process triggered on clk and reset. On reset, load sign-extended inputs into 16-bit registers for multiplier and multiplicand, clear product and counter, and clear ready signal. Each clock cycle, process 2 bits of the multiplier with corresponding Booth encoding (radix-4) to decide on adding 0, ±multiplicand, or ±2*multiplicand to the product. Shift the multiplier right by 2 bits each cycle, and left-shift the partial product accordingly. After 4 cycles (processing 8 bits in radix-4 steps), assert ready and output the final product. Keep design simple with non-blocking assignments, minimal states, and straightforward control.
