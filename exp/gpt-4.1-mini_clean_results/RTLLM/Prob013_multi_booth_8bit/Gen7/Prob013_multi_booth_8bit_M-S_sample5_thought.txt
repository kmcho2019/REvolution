Implement a simplified iterative multiplier for two 8-bit signed inputs using a classic shift-add approach aligned with the user's original instructions, but without full Radix-4 Booth encoding. Use sign extension on inputs and store multiplicand and multiplier as signed 16-bit registers. On each clock cycle, check the current bit of the multiplier at position ctr; if it is 1, add the multiplicand to the product. Shift the multiplicand left by one bit after each cycle to align partial sums correctly. Use a 5-bit counter to track progress through 16 bits. After 16 cycles, signal completion with rdy=1. This approach avoids complicated encoding while preserving correct signed multiplication and synchronous operation.
