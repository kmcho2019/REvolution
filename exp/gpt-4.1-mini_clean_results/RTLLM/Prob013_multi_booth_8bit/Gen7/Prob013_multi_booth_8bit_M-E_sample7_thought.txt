Implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier with a synchronous finite state machine. On reset, initialize a 17-bit register combining the multiplier with an appended zero bit and a 17-bit accumulator initialized to zero. The multiplicand is sign-extended to 16 bits. Instead of a combined large shift, maintain separate registers for accumulator and multiplier (with appended bit). At each cycle, decode the current 3-bit booth code formed from the least two bits of multiplier and the appended bit to generate the partial product. Accumulate the partial product into the accumulator, then arithmetic right shift the accumulator and multiplier registers together by 2 bits. Use a 3-bit counter to track 4 cycles (for 8 bits). When done, concatenate accumulator and multiplier registers to form the 16-bit product output and assert the ready signal. This architectural style uses separate registers and explicit FSM control for clarity and modularity, avoiding a large combined shift register and partial product addition in one step.
