Implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier following the given specification. On reset, sign-extend inputs a and b to 16-bit registers multiplier and multiplicand respectively. Use a 5-bit counter to track steps. On each clock cycle when not reset and counter < 16, shift multiplicand left by 1 and if multiplier[ctr] is 1, add multiplicand to product p. Increment ctr every cycle. When ctr reaches 16, assert ready signal rdy. The operation is synchronous on positive clk or reset edge.
