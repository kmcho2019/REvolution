Implement a synchronous radix-4 Booth multiplier for 8-bit inputs using a stepwise approach controlled by a 5-bit counter. On reset, initialize 16-bit signed extension registers for multiplier and multiplicand. On each clock cycle, if not done, perform radix-4 Booth logic by examining two multiplier bits at a time (plus an extra bit), determine partial product addition or subtraction, and shift registers accordingly. Keep track of progress with a counter; once 8 steps (covering 16 bits effectively) complete, set ready signal high and output the final product.
