Implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier using a synchronous process driven by clk and reset. On reset, initialize the multiplicand and multiplier registers with sign-extended inputs a and b respectively, and reset the product and counter. During operation (reset low), for 16 cycles, examine two bits of the multiplier and decide the Booth operation: add, subtract or no operation based on Booth encoding. Shift multiplicand or product accordingly to simulate Radix-4 Booth multiplication. After 16 cycles, set ready signal high indicating completion. Output the final 16-bit product.
