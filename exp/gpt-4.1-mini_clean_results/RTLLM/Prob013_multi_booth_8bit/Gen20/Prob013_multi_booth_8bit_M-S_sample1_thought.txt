Implement a straightforward Radix-2 Booth multiplier per the user's described algorithm (though named Radix-4, their process inspects one multiplier bit per cycle).

- Use a simple synchronous process triggered on clk or reset.
- On reset, sign-extend and load multiplier and multiplicand into 16-bit registers.
- Maintain a 5-bit counter from 0 to 15.
- On each clock cycle when counter < 16:
  - If multiplier[ctr] is 1, add multiplicand to the product register.
  - Left-shift multiplicand by 1.
  - Increment the counter.
- When counter reaches 16, assert ready and output the product.
- Keep logic simple with minimal FSM: just reset and operation phase.
- Store multiplier unchanged after load; no complex FSM or control signals.
- This matches the problem statement, is clean, and is easier to understand and verify.
