To simplify the original design while maintaining the functionality, we will:

1. Remove the FSM and use a straightforward synchronous process controlled by the reset and a counter.
2. On reset, load sign-extended inputs into multiplicand and multiplier registers and clear product and ready signals.
3. On each clock cycle, if the counter is less than 16, check the multiplier bit at the current counter; if it's 1, add multiplicand to product.
4. Shift multiplicand left by one every cycle.
5. Increment counter; when it reaches 16, set ready high and output the product.
6. Use only synchronous reset on clock edge for simpler synthesis.
7. Use signed registers for arithmetic and keep code clear and concise.

This approach removes the FSM, keeping the logic linear and easier to understand and synthesize.
