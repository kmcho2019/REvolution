This significantly improves performance (timing) and reduces power by reducing switching activity.

Improvement strategy:
- Implement a genuine Radix-4 Booth algorithm by encoding the multiplier bits in overlapping groups of 3 (including an appended zero bit), extracting two bits per cycle.
- Use a 5-bit counter but count up to 8 iterations (since each iteration processes 2 bits).
- The multiplicand is sign-extended to 17 bits to cover the partial product shifts.
- The product register is extended to 17 bits internally to accommodate intermediate sums and shifts, then trimmed to 16 bits at output.
- Use signed arithmetic for correct signed multiplication.
- Shift the product right by 2 bits each cycle (radix-4 shifts the partial product).
- Generate the partial product based on the Booth recode (0, +1, -1, +2, -2).

- Set ready (rdy) high when multiplication is done after 8 iterations.
- The design uses synchronous reset on clock edge.
- This reduces the number of cycles, cutting power and timing demands.
- The encoding logic is combinational and simple, preserving area efficiency.

This approach satisfies the requirement of an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier with synchronous operation and ready signal, improves timing by halving cycles and reduces power by fewer state transitions.
