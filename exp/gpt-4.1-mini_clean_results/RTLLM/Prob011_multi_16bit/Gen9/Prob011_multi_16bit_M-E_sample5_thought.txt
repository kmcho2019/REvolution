Implement a 16-bit unsigned multiplier using the classic shift-and-add algorithm where the multiplier bits (bin) are scanned from least significant bit to most significant bit. Store the multiplicand (ain) in a 32-bit shifted register (shifted left each cycle), and accumulate into a 32-bit product register only when the corresponding multiplier bit is 1. Maintain a shift counter i to track the current bit position. At the start, load ain into areg (shifted 0), store bin in a separate register (breg), and clear the product. For each bit of bin, check if the LSB of breg is 1, if so add areg to the product accumulator. Then shift areg left by one bit, shift breg right by one bit, and increment i. The done signal is asserted when all 16 bits have been processed. This approach differs by explicitly shifting both multiplicand and multiplier registers and incrementing a counter that runs from 0 to 16, simplifying logic and control.
