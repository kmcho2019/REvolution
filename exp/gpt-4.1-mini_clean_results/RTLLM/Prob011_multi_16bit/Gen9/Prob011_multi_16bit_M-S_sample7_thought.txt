Simplify the design by directly using the multiplier register as the shifting operand and the multiplicand register as the add condition without separate shifting of two registers. Use a 5-bit counter `i` from 0 to 16. At `i=0`, load multiplicand and multiplier into registers, and clear the accumulator. For `i` from 1 to 16, check the LSB of the multiplier register: if set, add the multiplicand shifted by (i-1) to the accumulator. Instead of shifting operands each cycle, simply shift multiplicand left by (i-1) during addition, avoiding shift registers and simplifying the logic. Keep the done flag set when `i==16` and clear on reset or start de-assertion. This reduces register usage and shifting hardware complexity while preserving correct multiply-accumulate functionality.
