To optimize the original design for better power and potentially area without sacrificing functionality, I will add clock enable signals to registers to avoid unnecessary toggling when multiplication is not active. This reduces dynamic power consumption by preventing registers from updating when no operation is underway.

Additionally, I will restructure the control so registers update only when start is asserted and the multiplication is ongoing (i between 0 and 16). This avoids extra toggling in the idle state.

Since the original design uses a sequential approach with a single 32-bit adder, I will keep the architecture unchanged for simplicity and area but add clock enables carefully.

This approach improves power consumption with minimal area and timing impact, making the design more efficient for practical use.
