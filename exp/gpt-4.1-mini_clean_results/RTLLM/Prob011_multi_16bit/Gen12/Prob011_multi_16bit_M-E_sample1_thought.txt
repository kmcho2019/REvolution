I will implement a 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a classic shift-and-add algorithm controlled by an explicit counter and registers, but differing from the previous example by:

- Using a left-shift of the accumulator and right-shift of the multiplier each cycle.
- Adding the multiplicand to an accumulator when the LSB of the multiplier is 1.
- Using synchronous reset and synchronous start-triggered load of inputs.
- Keeping the counting and done signaling strictly controlled by the counter reaching 16.
- The done flag is registered and cleared on reset or after completion.
- This approach is a textbook shift-and-add multiplier with the multiplicand and accumulator shifted left together, the multiplier shifted right, accumulating the partial sum in the product register.
- The pipeline has no FSM; control relies on counter and start signal to begin the operation.
- This method minimizes combinational critical path per cycle by separating addition and shifting operations clearly.
- The output is updated every cycle with the current product.
