Use a state machine to control the multiply operation. On start, latch inputs into registers and reset product to zero. In each clock cycle, test the LSB of the multiplier register; if set, add multiplicand to product. Then shift multiplicand left by 1 and multiplier right by 1. Increment a counter until 16 iterations done, then assert done flag. Reset states on rst_n low or when start deasserted. This approach efficiently accumulates partial sums while shifting multiplier right and multiplicand left, avoiding indexing bits directly and providing a neat sequential multiply control.
