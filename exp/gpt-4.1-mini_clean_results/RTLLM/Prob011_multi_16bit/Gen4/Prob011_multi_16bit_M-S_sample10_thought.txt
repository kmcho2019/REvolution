Implement a simplified iterative shift-and-add multiplier by:
- Using a 5-bit counter 'i' to track progress from 0 to 16.
- On reset or when start is low, clear registers and outputs.
- On start, at i=0, load the multiplicand and multiplier into registers.
- For i=1 to 16, check the LSB of areg; if set, add breg to the product.
- Then shift areg right by 1 bit to process the next multiplicand bit.
- Shift breg left by 1 bit to align with the current bit position in product.
- Increment i each clock cycle until done.
- Assert done when i reaches 16.
This approach avoids variable shifts and indexing complexities, uses a straightforward shift-register style multiplier, and reduces combinational complexity, improving clarity and synthesizeability.
