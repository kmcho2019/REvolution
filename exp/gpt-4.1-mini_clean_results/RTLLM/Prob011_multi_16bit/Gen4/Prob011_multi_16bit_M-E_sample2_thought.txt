Implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier using a classic iterative shift-and-add method, but instead of shifting and adding variable shifted versions of the multiplier each cycle, shift the multiplier register right by one bit every clock cycle and conditionally add the multiplicand to an accumulator register if the least significant bit of the multiplier is 1. This approach simplifies the shifting operations, as only one bit of the multiplier is examined each cycle and the multiplicand remains stationary. The process repeats for 16 cycles, after which the accumulator holds the full product. The module controls the process with a shift counter and generates the done flag when complete. Registers are reset on rst_n low and re-initialized upon start. This method reduces variable shifting complexity and hardware cost, improving timing and power efficiency compared to variable shift operations.
