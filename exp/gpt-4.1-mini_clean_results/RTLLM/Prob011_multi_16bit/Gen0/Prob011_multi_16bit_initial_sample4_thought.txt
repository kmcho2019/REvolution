Implement an iterative unsigned 16-bit multiplier using shift and accumulate method controlled by a counter `i`. On reset, clear all registers. On start, load inputs into registers and increment `i` each clock. When `i` is between 1 and 16, check the (i-1)th bit of multiplicand register; if set, accumulate multiplier shifted left by (i-1) bits into product register. The done flag is asserted when `i` reaches 16 and de-asserted at 17. Product output is the 32-bit accumulator value.
