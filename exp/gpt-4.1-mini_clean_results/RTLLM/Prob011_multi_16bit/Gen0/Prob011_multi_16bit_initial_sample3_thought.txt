Implement a shift-and-accumulate 16-bit unsigned multiplier controlled by a shift count (i) and using registers for multiplicand (areg), multiplier (breg), and product (yout_r). On reset, clear registers and flags. On start, load inputs into registers and, for each bit of the multiplicand, if set, add the multiplier shifted by the bit index to the product accumulator. The done flag is asserted when 16 cycles of operation are complete, and cleared after the 17th cycle. The output yout is assigned from the product accumulator register.
