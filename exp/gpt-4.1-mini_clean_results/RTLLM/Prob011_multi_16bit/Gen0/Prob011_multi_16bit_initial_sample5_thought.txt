Implement an unsigned 16-bit multiplier using shift and accumulate method controlled by a shift count register i. The multiplier runs on the clock and reset signals, and starts operation on start signal. The multiplicand and multiplier are loaded at i=0. For each subsequent cycle i from 1 to 16, if the i-1 bit of the multiplicand is set, accumulate the multiplier shifted by i-1 into the product. The done flag is set when i reaches 16 and cleared at 17. The product output is updated from the accumulator register.
