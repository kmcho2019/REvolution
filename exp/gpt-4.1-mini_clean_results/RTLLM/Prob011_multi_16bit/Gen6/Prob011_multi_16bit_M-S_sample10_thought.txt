Implement a straightforward shift-and-add unsigned 16-bit multiplier by tracking the bit position with a 5-bit counter (i) from 0 to 16. At i=0, load the inputs; from i=1 to i=16, check each bit of the multiplier (bin) starting from LSB. If the bit is 1, add the multiplicand (ain) shifted left by i-1 to the accumulator. The done flag asserts at i=16 indicating completion. This approach avoids shifting multiplicand or multiplier registers every cycle, simplifies logic by directly accessing bits of bin, and uses a clean counter for sequencing. It reduces complexity by removing the shifting registers and extended multiplier, only using the original inputs and an accumulator, while maintaining the required start, reset, and done signal behavior.
