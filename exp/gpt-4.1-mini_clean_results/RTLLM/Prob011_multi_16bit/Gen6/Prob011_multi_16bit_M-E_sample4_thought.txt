Implement the 16-bit unsigned multiplier by iteratively shifting the multiplier (bin) and accumulating the multiplicand (ain) into a 32-bit accumulator only when the least significant bit of the multiplier is 1. Instead of shifting the multiplicand, shift the multiplier right by one bit each cycle, and add the multiplicand shifted by the current bit position to the accumulator. This approach uses a single 5-bit counter (0 to 16) to track the bit position. On reset or inactive start, all registers clear. On start, load inputs once at count 0. Each cycle from 1 to 16 checks the multiplier LSB, conditionally adds the shifted multiplicand, and shifts multiplier right. The done flag asserts at cycle 16 and clears on cycle 17, indicating multiplication completion. This method minimizes register usage, reduces the shift operations on the multiplicand (fixed) and shifts only the multiplier (smaller register), and accumulates directly into the product register. This reduces switching by shifting only the multiplier register and preserves all key signals and behaviors as requested.
