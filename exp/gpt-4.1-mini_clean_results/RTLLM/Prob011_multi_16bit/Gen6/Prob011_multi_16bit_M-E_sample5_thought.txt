Use a control state machine approach with a separate phase counter to manage the multiplication steps explicitly. On start, load multiplicand and multiplier into registers; then, for 16 iterations, shift the multiplicand and check each bit. If the bit is set, add the shifted multiplier to the product accumulator. Keep the multiplier fixed and shift the accumulator accordingly. Use separate registers for multiplicand (shifted right every cycle), multiplier (kept constant), and accumulator (32-bit). The done flag is asserted when all bits have been processed. This explicit state-based design enhances clarity and allows easy extension/modification.
