To optimize the original FSM-based 16-bit multiplier for better power and timing without increasing area significantly, I will:

1. Introduce clock enable gating on the product register updates to prevent unnecessary toggling when the multiplier LSB is zero, avoiding the 32-bit addition and register update.
2. Simplify addition logic: only add multiplicand when needed, otherwise just shift right, reducing switching.
3. Maintain the same FSM structure and counter for control to keep control logic simple.
4. Keep non-blocking assignments and synchronous reset for good synthesis.
5. Keep done signal asserted only when multiplication completes.

This approach reduces power by gating the adder and product register updates conditionally, potentially improves timing by reducing unnecessary large-width additions, and has negligible area impact as no extra registers are introduced.
