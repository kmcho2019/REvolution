Refactor the design using a simple FSM with two states: IDLE and BUSY. Use registers with enable signals to reduce unnecessary toggling when start is not asserted. Load inputs at the start, then iterate 16 cycles performing shift-and-add based on the LSB of the multiplier register. Shift the multiplier right each cycle and shift the accumulator left, accumulating the partial product in a single 32-bit register. Update the done flag on completion. This approach uses a more explicit state machine, reduces register toggling by enabling updates only during BUSY state, and manages registers more clearly, improving readability and control.
