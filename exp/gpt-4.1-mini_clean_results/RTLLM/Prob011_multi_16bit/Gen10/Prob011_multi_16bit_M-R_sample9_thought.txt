Refactor the design to use an explicit FSM with three states: IDLE, RUN, and DONE. Use combinational logic for done generation and shift count increment controlled by the FSM states. Registers will hold multiplicand, multiplier, product, and count. On start, load inputs and move to RUN; during RUN, process one bit each clock cycle: if current multiplier bit is 1, add shifted multiplicand to product. After 16 cycles, assert done and move to DONE, then return to IDLE on start deassert. This FSM-based structure improves clarity by separating control and datapath and replaces implicit control in shift count logic with explicit states.
