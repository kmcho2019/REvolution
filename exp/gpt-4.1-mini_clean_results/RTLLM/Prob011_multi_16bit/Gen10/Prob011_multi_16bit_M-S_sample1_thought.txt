Implement a straightforward shift-and-accumulate multiplier controlled by a 5-bit counter (i) indicating the current bit processed (0 to 16). On reset, registers clear. On start, multiplicand and multiplier are loaded into registers. For each bit i (1 to 16), if bit (i-1) of the multiplicand is set, add the multiplier shifted by (i-1) to the product. Use simple synchronous logic with non-blocking assignments and generate done signal when the 16-bit multiplication completes (i == 16). Reset done at i == 17 or when not started. This approach adheres strictly to the given instructions, directly using the multiplicand bits to control accumulation and does not use an FSM, keeping logic simple and easy to understand.
