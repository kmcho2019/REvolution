To optimize the original design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) while maintaining the same functionality:

1. Performance:
- Remove redundant assignments to yout_r when no addition is needed, reducing unnecessary toggling and data movement.
- Directly update done_r outside the main iteration block with clear conditions to avoid glitches.
- Simplify the control logic by incrementing the shift count only when meaningful operations occur.

2. Power:
- Avoid updating registers (areg, breg, yout_r) when values do not change to reduce switching activity.
- Use enable signals inside the always block to gate operations only when needed.

3. Area:
- Use minimal-width registers where possible (i is already 5 bits).
- Share signals efficiently and avoid temporary variables.
  
Additional minor changes:
- Use meaningful comments.
- Keep reset behavior synchronous to negedge rst_n as specified.
- Use blocking assignments for combinational-like updates inside always blocks to reduce logic delay (still nonblocking in sequential always blocks).
  
This approach will reduce toggles on yout_r and registers, thus lowering power, slightly improve max frequency by shortening critical path updates, and maintain area since the data width is fixed by problem requirements.

The design remains a sequential iterative shift-and-add multiplier with done flag and start/reset control, preserving all required functionality and ports.
