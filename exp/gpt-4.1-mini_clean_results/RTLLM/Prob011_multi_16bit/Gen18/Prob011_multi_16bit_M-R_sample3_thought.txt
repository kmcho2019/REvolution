Refactor the original FSM to use an enumerated state type with three states: IDLE, LOAD, and CALC. In IDLE, the module waits for the start signal. Upon start, it transitions to LOAD to register inputs, then to CALC to perform the shift and accumulate over 16 cycles. The counter increments only in CALC state. The product register is updated combinationally inside an always block based on the current product and the LSB of the multiplier portion of the product register. The done signal is asserted at the end of CALC. This structured FSM improves clarity, separates load and calculation phases explicitly, and uses synchronous reset. Also, use localparam for state encoding, and restructure the product update logic into a single always block triggered by the clock, avoiding multiple always blocks for related registers.
