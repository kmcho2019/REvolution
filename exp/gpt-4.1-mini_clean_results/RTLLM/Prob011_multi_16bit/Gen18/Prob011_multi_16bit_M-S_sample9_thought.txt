To simplify the original design while preserving its functionality, I will remove the explicit clock enable signals and gating logic, and use straightforward sequential logic with synchronous reset. The design will use a 5-bit counter `i` to track the shift count from 0 to 17. On reset, registers are cleared. When `start` is asserted, at `i=0` the inputs are loaded into `areg` and `breg`, and at each cycle from 1 to 16 the LSB of `areg` is checked; if it is '1', the shifted `breg` value is added to `yout_r`. Both `areg` and `breg` are shifted every cycle (right and left respectively). The `done` signal is asserted when `i` reaches 16. No complex enable signals or operand gating is used, reducing the control logic complexity and improving clarity while maintaining the same behavior.
