Implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier with shift-and-accumulate by scanning the multiplier bits one by one instead of scanning the multiplicand bits. On start, load inputs into internal registers and clear the product accumulator. Use a 5-bit counter (i) to track the number of processed bits (0 to 16). On each clock cycle while counting from 0 to 15, check the least significant bit (LSB) of the multiplier register; if it is 1, add the current multiplicand register to the product accumulator. Then shift the multiplicand register left by 1 and the multiplier register right by 1 to prepare for the next bit. Once 16 bits are processed, set the done flag; clear done on the next cycle (i=17). Reset clears all registers and flags. This approach processes bits from multiplier LSB upward, simplifying the bit extraction logic and using shift registers in a classic multiplier architecture.
