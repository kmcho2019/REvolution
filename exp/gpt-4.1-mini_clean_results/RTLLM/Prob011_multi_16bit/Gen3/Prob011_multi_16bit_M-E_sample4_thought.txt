Implement a 16-bit unsigned multiplier using an iterative shift-and-add approach with a single accumulator and a shifting multiplier. On start, load multiplicand into a register, load multiplier into another register, reset accumulator and bit count. Then at each clock cycle, check the LSB of the multiplier register; if set, add the multiplicand to the accumulator. Shift the multiplicand left by one bit each cycle and shift the multiplier right by one bit to process the next bit. Count 16 iterations; after that, assert done. This method eliminates the need for variable shifts by shifting multiplicand each cycle, resulting in simpler combinational logic, potentially better timing, and lower power. Control signals handle start, reset, and done generation. The product is the accumulator after 16 cycles. This approach is a classic shift-and-add multiplier with a moving multiplicand instead of variable shifting of multiplier.
