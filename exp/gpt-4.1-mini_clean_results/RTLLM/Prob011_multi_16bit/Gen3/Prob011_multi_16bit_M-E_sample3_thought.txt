Implement an iterative, bit-serial unsigned 16-bit multiplier using shift-and-add with minimal combinational shifting each cycle:

- Use registers to hold multiplicand (ain_reg), multiplier (bin_reg), and product (product_reg) of 32 bits.
- On start, load ain_reg with multiplicand, bin_reg with multiplier, and clear product_reg.
- Each clock cycle, check the LSB of bin_reg (current multiplier bit).
- If LSB is 1, add ain_reg to product_reg.
- Then shift ain_reg left by 1 (multiply by 2), shift bin_reg right by 1 (next multiplier bit).
- Maintain a 5-bit counter to count from 0 to 16 indicating processing of all 16 bits.
- When count reaches 16, assert done.
- This approach avoids large variable shifts and wide adders with big shifting on inputs, simplifying the datapath.
- It's fully sequential, uses only one adder, and handles shifting with registers.
- Control signals (start, reset) properly managed to initialize and run the multiplication.
- The design is clean, power-efficient, and easily synthesizable.
