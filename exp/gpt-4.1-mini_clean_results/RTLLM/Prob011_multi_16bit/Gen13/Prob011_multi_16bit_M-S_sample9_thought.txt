Implement a straightforward shift-and-accumulate multiplier using a shift counter, multiplicand and multiplier registers, and an accumulator for the product. On start, load inputs into registers. On each clock cycle, check the least significant bit of the multiplier register; if set, add the multiplicand (shifted by the current bit index) to the accumulator. Shift the multiplier right by one bit each cycle, increment the count, and after 16 cycles, signal done. Use synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments. This approach is clear, uses minimal registers, and directly follows the given specification without FSM or state encoding, resulting in simpler and easily understandable code.
