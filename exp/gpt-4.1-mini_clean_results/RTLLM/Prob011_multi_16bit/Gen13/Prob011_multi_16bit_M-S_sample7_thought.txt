Implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier with a straightforward shift-and-accumulate approach. Use separate registers for multiplicand, multiplier, and product accumulation. Control the operation with a 5-bit counter (shift count) that increments when start is asserted and counts up to 16. On each cycle (after loading inputs at count=0), check the LSB of the multiplier register; if set, add the multiplicand shifted by the current bit position to the product register. Then shift the multiplier right by 1 to prepare for the next bit. Set the done flag when count reaches 16. Reset all registers on active-low reset. This approach cleanly separates data path and control, avoids complicated packed shifting, and keeps the logic simple and easy to follow.
