Implement a 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a shift-and-accumulate approach controlled by a counter that counts from 0 to 17 (16 multiplication steps plus reset). The design uses three key registers: areg (multiplicand), breg (multiplier), and yout_r (accumulator for product). On start, the inputs are loaded, and for each bit of areg, the design checks if the bit is set; if so, the shifted breg value is added to the accumulator. The shift count i is incremented on each clock cycle during operation. The done signal is asserted when the counter reaches 16. The design resets all internal registers on rst_n low. The product output yout is continuously driven by the accumulator register yout_r. This design operates purely sequentially without FSM, relying on the i counter and control signals for simplicity.
