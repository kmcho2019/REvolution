Refactor the design into a simple finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, RUNNING, and DONE. On start, capture inputs and move to RUNNING state, iteratively accumulate shifted multiplier bits based on the multiplier's bit positions (bin), not multiplicand bits. Use a shift count register to count up to 16 iterations. After completing all steps, signal done and wait for start to deassert to return to IDLE. This clean FSM-based approach improves control flow clarity and ensures done flag is managed correctly, while adhering to shift-and-add multiplication logic.
