Implement a shift-and-add unsigned 16-bit multiplier using a simple finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and RUN. When `start` is asserted, latch inputs into internal registers and clear the product accumulator. Then, in RUN state, iterate 16 times: if the LSB of the multiplier is 1, add the multiplicand to the accumulator; then shift the multiplicand left by 1 and the multiplier right by 1. When 16 cycles are done, assert `done` and output the product. This approach avoids counting beyond 16, reduces logic for done flag, and cleanly separates input capture and processing, improving clarity and correctness.
