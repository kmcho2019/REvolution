Refactor the multiplier design by implementing a simple finite state machine (FSM) to control the multiplication process with states IDLE, LOAD, CALC, and DONE. Use registers to hold the intermediate multiplicand, multiplier, product, and count. At each clock, shift the multiplicand and accumulate the product accordingly. The design avoids large combinational shifters by shifting the product and multiplicand registers iteratively. This sequential approach keeps the critical path short. The done flag is controlled by the FSM. The start signal triggers the FSM from IDLE to LOAD state, then multiplication proceeds for 16 cycles.
