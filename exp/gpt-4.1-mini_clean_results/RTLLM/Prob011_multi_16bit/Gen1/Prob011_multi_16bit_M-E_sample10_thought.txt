Implement an iterative 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a single adder and two shift registers for multiplicand and multiplier. On start, load ain and bin into areg and breg registers. On each clock cycle, check the LSB of the multiplier (breg). If it is 1, add the multiplicand (areg) to the accumulator (yout_r). Then shift areg left by 1 and breg right by 1. Repeat this for 16 cycles. Use a 5-bit counter i to track iteration. Assert done when i reaches 16. Reset clears all registers. This approach uses serial shifts, eliminating a large variable shifter and complex multiplexers, leading to simpler hardware and possibly better timing and area. The output yout is assigned from the accumulator yout_r.
