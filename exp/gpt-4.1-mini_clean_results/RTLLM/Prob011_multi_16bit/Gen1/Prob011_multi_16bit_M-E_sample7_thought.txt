Implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier using a sequential shift-and-add approach controlled by a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BUSY, DONE. On start, load inputs into registers and clear the product register. Each cycle, examine the least significant bit of the multiplier register; if set, add the multiplicand register to the product accumulator. Shift the multiplicand left and the multiplier right on each cycle. Increment a counter to track progress. When 16 cycles are done, set done high and output the product. Reset or start signal clears FSM and registers appropriately. This method directly shifts the multiplier and multiplicand, simplifying logic and avoiding variable left shifts of the multiplier, potentially improving area and timing.
