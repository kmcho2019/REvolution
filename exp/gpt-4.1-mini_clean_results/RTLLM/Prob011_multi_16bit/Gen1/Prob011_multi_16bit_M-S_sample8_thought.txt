Simplify the multiplier by using a single register for the multiplicand (areg), one for the multiplier (breg), and an accumulator for the product (yout_r). Instead of a variable left-shift of breg each cycle, implement breg as a right-shifting register to process each bit sequentially, while conditionally adding areg to the accumulator. This replaces the large variable shifter with simple shifts and reduces combinational logic, making the design smaller and potentially faster. The shift count i controls the number of bits processed. The done flag and control logic remain the same. This serial shift-and-add approach is simpler but functionally equivalent.
