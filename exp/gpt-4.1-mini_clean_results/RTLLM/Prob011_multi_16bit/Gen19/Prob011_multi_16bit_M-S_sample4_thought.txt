Implement a straightforward shift-and-add multiplier controlled by a simple shift count register without FSM states. On start, load inputs; then for 16 cycles, check each bit of the multiplier (areg) from LSB to MSB, accumulating the shifted multiplicand (breg) into the product register (yout_r) whenever the bit is 1. Increment the shift count (i) each cycle. When i reaches 16, assert done. Reset clears all registers. This keeps logic simple and meets all specified control signals and behaviors.
