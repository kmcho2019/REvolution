Implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier using a single accumulator register that holds the product, a shift register holding the multiplier, and a separate multiplicand register. On start, load multiplicand and multiplier into respective registers. Each clock cycle, if the LSB of the multiplier register is 1, add the multiplicand to the accumulator. Then shift the accumulator and multiplier registers to the right together (concatenated) to process the next bit. This uses the classic "shift and add" multiplication, but instead of separate shifting of multiplicand and multiplier, we shift the combined accumulator and multiplier right to keep track of partial sums and bits remaining. The shift count keeps track of the number of bits processed. When all 16 bits are processed, done is asserted. This architecture efficiently merges the multiplier bits and partial product into a single 48-bit register (accumulator + multiplier), simplifying the shift operation and control. The 16-bit multiplicand remains fixed during the process. Reset clears all registers. Start triggers loading and enables the multiplication sequence.
