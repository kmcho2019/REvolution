Implement a straightforward shift-and-accumulate multiplier by explicitly using a 5-bit counter (`i`) to track the progress of multiplication from 0 to 16, and a 32-bit register `yout_r` for the product accumulator. Registers `areg` and `breg` hold the multiplicand and multiplier respectively. On each clock cycle (when `start` is active and `i` < 17), check bit `i-1` of `areg` to conditionally add `breg` shifted left by `i-1` to `yout_r`. Reset and load registers on reset and start signals. Manage `done` flag according to `i`. This method uses clear, separate registers and simple control logic, without packing multiplier and product in the same register, improving readability while fulfilling the given requirements.
