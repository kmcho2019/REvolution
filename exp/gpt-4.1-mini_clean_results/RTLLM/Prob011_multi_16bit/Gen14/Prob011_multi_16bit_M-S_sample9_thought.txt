Implement a straightforward 16-bit unsigned multiplier using shift-and-accumulate logic controlled by a shift counter. On start, registers store inputs; then each cycle checks one bit of the multiplicand, adding a shifted multiplier to the product if that bit is 1. The shift count increments until 16, then done flag asserts. Reset clears all registers and flags. The design avoids FSM and additional states for simplicity, focusing purely on the described logic and control signals.
