To simplify the design while preserving the intended functionality:

- Use a single always block with asynchronous active-low reset.
- Employ a 5-bit shift counter `i` to track multiplication progress from 0 to 17.
- On `start` and when `i` is 0, load inputs into registers and clear accumulator.
- For 1 to 16, check the LSB of `areg`; if set, add `breg` to the accumulator.
- Shift `areg` right by 1 and `breg` left by 1 each cycle during multiplication.
- Set `done` when `i` reaches 16, clear at 17.
- Reset all registers and flags when `start` is low or reset asserted.
- This removes extra conditions and redundant assignments, making the code easier to read and maintain.
