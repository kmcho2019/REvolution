To improve the PPA metrics of the existing 16-bit shift-and-accumulate multiplier, we focus on reducing unnecessary toggling and minimizing critical path delay without changing the core algorithm. The main improvement strategies are:

1. Add clock-enable style gating using the `start` signal and the valid range of `i` to avoid updating registers when multiplication is not active, thereby reducing switching activity and power.

2. Split the shift and accumulation operation into smaller steps using non-blocking assignments carefully to avoid combinational dependency in a single always block, improving timing.

3. Use a registered adder output to pipeline the addition and reduce critical path delay. We add an intermediate sum register for accumulation.

4. Avoid shifting the multiplier register `breg` unnecessarily when the current bit of multiplicand is zero by conditionally shifting only in active cycles.

5. Simplify done flag logic to clear done immediately when `start` goes low or on reset, eliminating an extra cycle of delay, slightly improving responsiveness.

6. Keep the counter `i` increment logic simple and use enable signals to gate counting and register updates.

By implementing clock gating with enables and splitting operations, we reduce toggling and the critical path, leading to better power and timing performance. The area overhead is minimal, as added registers and control signals are small compared to the multiplier datapath.

This refined design maintains correctness and timing but improves overall PPA efficiency.
