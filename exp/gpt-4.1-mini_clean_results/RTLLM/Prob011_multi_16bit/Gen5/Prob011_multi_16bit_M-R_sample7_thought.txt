Refactor the design using a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, RUNNING, and DONE. The counter and done flag are managed within the FSM states rather than separately. Shift and accumulate operations are performed each clock cycle in the RUNNING state, testing the LSB of the multiplier register and adding the multiplicand shifted by the current count when appropriate. This structure improves clarity and control of the multiplication sequence while preserving functionality and timing of the original design.

The shift count register increments in RUNNING state, done flag is set in DONE state, and outputs are registered accordingly. Reset synchronously initializes all registers and FSM to IDLE. This state-machine-driven approach cleanly separates control logic from datapath and avoids asynchronous resets in combinational logic blocks, improving readability and maintainability.
