To optimize the design for better power and slightly improved performance, I will implement clock gating on the registers and done flag so they update only when the multiplier is active (start is high and i < 17). This will reduce unnecessary toggling and dynamic power when idle. Also, to reduce the adder toggling, I will gate the addition operation with the condition that the bit of areg at (i-1) is set before performing the addition. This avoids unnecessary computation when no accumulation is needed. The variable shift remains, as synthesis tools typically optimize it well. These minor changes keep the design functionally the same but improve power efficiency and reduce unnecessary switching.
