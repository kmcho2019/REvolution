To optimize the original design for better PPA, reduce switching activity and critical path delay by avoiding a 32-bit variable shift every cycle. Instead, shift the multiplier (breg) right by one bit each cycle and accumulate it conditionally if the current bit of the multiplicand (areg) is set. This serial shift approach removes the need for a barrel shifter and large variable left shifts, reducing area and improving timing. Also, update done flag logic to clear at cycle 17 as specified. The control counter i still tracks cycles; at i=0 inputs load, from i=1 to 16 the multiply-accumulate loop runs, and at i=17 done resets. This change leverages a right-shift on multiplier, which is simpler and more power efficient, and only one addition of 32-bit width per cycle. The addition is conditional on the current multiplicand bit. This yields a more balanced and practical shift-accumulate multiplier.
