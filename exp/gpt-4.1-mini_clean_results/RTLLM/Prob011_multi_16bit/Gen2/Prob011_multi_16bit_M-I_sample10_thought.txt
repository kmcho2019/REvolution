To optimize the iterative 16-bit multiplier, reduce the large 32-bit variable shift by using a shifting multiplier register (breg) instead of shifting it left by (i-1) bits every cycle. Instead of shifting the multiplier left by (i-1), shift the multiplier register right by 1 each cycle, and conditionally add the multiplicand to the product accumulator when the LSB of the multiplier register is 1. This way, addition involves the multiplicand aligned directly, and shifting operations are single-bit shifts each cycle, simplifying the shifter and adder, reducing critical path delay and power. Also, simplify control by operating on multiplier bits directly, stopping after 16 cycles. The done flag is asserted when count reaches 16. This approach is a classic shift-and-add multiplier optimizing PPA.
