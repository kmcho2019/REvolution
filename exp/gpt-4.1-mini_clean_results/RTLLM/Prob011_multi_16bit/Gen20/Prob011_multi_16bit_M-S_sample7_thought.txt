I will simplify the design by removing some redundant signals and combining conditions to reduce control signals. Instead of a separate breg_ext shifting and areg shifting, I'll only shift areg and directly shift and accumulate bin shifted by (i-1) bits based on areg bit. This removes the need for breg_ext register. Also, I'll merge done flag control into the same always block with shift counter to reduce code duplication. Reset and start controls remain. This simplification reduces register count and logic, while keeping the step-by-step shift and accumulate approach intact.
