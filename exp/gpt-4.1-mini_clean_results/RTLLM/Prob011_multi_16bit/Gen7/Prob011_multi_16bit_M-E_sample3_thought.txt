Implement the 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a shift-and-accumulate method with the multiplicand held constant and the multiplier shifted right on each cycle. At each step, check the least significant bit of the multiplier register; if it is '1', add the multiplicand (appropriately shifted) to the accumulator. Use a 5-bit counter from 0 to 16 to track operation progress. On start, load inputs into registers and clear the accumulator. After 16 cycles, assert done. This approach differs by shifting the multiplier register right each cycle, leaving multiplicand fixed, simplifying partial product generation and accumulation control.
