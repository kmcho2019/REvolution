Implement the 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a classic shift-and-accumulate approach but with a different architecture: Instead of shifting the multiplier and multiplicand registers each cycle, keep the multiplicand fixed and shift a partial product register right. On each clock cycle, examine the least significant bit of the multiplier register; if it's '1', add the multiplicand shifted left by the current bit position to the partial product accumulator. Use a counter (i) that increments each cycle to track which bit is being processed. When i reaches 16, the multiplication is complete. This approach avoids shifting the multiplicand and multiplier, minimizing signal toggling on inputs and better isolates accumulation logic. The done flag is asserted on completion.
