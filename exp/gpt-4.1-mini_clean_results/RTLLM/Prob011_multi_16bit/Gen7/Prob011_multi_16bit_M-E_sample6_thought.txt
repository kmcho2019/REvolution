Use an approach where the multiplicand register (areg) holds the shifted multiplicand instead of the multiplier. The multiplier (breg) is static, and on each cycle the least significant bit of breg is checked to decide if the shifted areg should be accumulated into the product register yout_r. On every cycle, breg is shifted right (to process each bit), and areg is shifted left to represent the multiplicand shifted according to the bit position. The shift count i controls the operation length and done flag. This approach contrasts the example by shifting the multiplicand and iterating over the multiplier bits, which provides a complementary structure and similar control logic.
