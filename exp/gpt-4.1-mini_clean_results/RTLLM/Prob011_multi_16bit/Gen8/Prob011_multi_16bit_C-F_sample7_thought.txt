Fuse the two approaches by leveraging Example 1's simplified control of the shift count and done flag with Example 2's efficient per-cycle shifting of registers. Use a 5-bit counter `i` that increments from 0 to 17. At `i=0`, load the multiplicand into `areg` and the multiplier zero-extended into `breg`. For cycles `1` to `16`, check the LSB of `areg`: if set, add `breg` to the accumulator `yout_r`. Then shift `areg` right by 1 bit and `breg` left by 1 bit. This avoids dynamic wide shifts and complex multiplexing, improving timing. Reset registers when `rst_n` is low or `start` is deasserted to avoid spurious toggling and glitches, lowering power. Keep done flag logic straightforward: set when `i==16`, clear at `i==17`. This design balances minimal logic, low switching activity, and clean control signals for efficient synthesis and good PPA characteristics.
