Implement the 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a shift-and-accumulate approach by shifting the multiplier right every cycle and adding the multiplicand to an accumulator whenever the multiplier's LSB is 1. Maintain three main registers: 
- a 32-bit accumulator (`acc`) for the partial product,
- a 16-bit multiplicand register (`areg`),
- and a 16-bit multiplier register (`breg`).

On `start`, load the multiplicand and multiplier into `areg` and `breg`, clear `acc`, and initialize a 5-bit counter `i` to zero. On each cycle, if the LSB of `breg` is 1, add `areg` shifted left by 0 (simple add to accumulator). Then shift `breg` right by 1 to process the next bit and shift `acc` right by 1 to keep the partial sum aligned correctly.

Repeat this for 16 cycles. When done, output the accumulated product and assert `done`. Use synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments for robust sequential design. The done flag is asserted when the counter reaches 16, indicating all bits processed.

This design uses a separate accumulator and shift registers for multiplicand and multiplier, avoiding complex multi-bit shifts and enables simple addition and shifts, balancing clarity and efficiency.
