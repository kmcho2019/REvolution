Refactor the design by implementing an explicit finite state machine (FSM) with IDLE, RUN, and DONE states, improving clarity of control flow. Use combinational logic with a next-state and next-value approach for registers to better separate sequential and combinational logic. This enhances readability and makes the design modular. The registers will update on clock edges, while combinational blocks determine next values and outputs. The shift and accumulate operations remain the same, iterating over multiplier bits with multiplicand shifted, accumulating partial sums when multiplier bits are set. The done flag is generated from the FSM state.
