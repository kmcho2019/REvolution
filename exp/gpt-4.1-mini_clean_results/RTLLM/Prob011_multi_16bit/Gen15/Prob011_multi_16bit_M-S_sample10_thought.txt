Simplify the multiplier design by removing the separate shift registers for multiplicand and multiplier; instead, keep the multiplicand fixed in a register and shift the product register and the multiplier bits for accumulation. Use a single 5-bit counter to count cycles. Perform the addition only when the corresponding multiplier bit is 1 by testing bin[i].
