Improvement Strategy:

Building on the original well-structured solution, the focus is on reducing power consumption and slightly improving timing without increasing area significantly. Key points:

1. Add clock enables for internal registers (`areg`, `breg`, `yout_r`, and counter `i`) so they only update when needed. This reduces toggling when idle or done.
2. Use operand gating for the adder: compute the addition only when needed (`add_en`), and latch `yout_r` only when accumulation is necessary.
3. Avoid shifting `areg` and `breg` when multiplication is not active (i.e., when `start` is not asserted or outside the valid count range).
4. Keep synchronous active-low reset for stable timing.
5. Hold registers and counter at idle or after done to reduce switching.
6. Improve readability and maintain functional correctness identical to the original design.

This approach minimizes unnecessary switching inside the datapath and control logic, potentially improving power and slightly easing timing closure without architectural changes.

The done flag logic is kept as-is, as it is already efficient and clean.
