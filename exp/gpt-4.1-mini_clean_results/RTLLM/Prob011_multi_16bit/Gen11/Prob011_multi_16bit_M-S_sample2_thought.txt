Implement a straightforward shift-and-accumulate 16-bit unsigned multiplier using a shift counter to control the operation progress.
On reset, all registers are cleared.
When start is asserted, load the multiplicand and multiplier into registers.
For each bit (0 to 15) of the multiplicand, if the bit is set, accumulate the shifted multiplier into the product register.
Use a 5-bit counter to track shifts.
Done signal is asserted when 16 shifts are complete.
Keep logic synchronous with clk and asynchronous active-low reset.
This approach is simple, clear, and meets the design requirements without extra FSM states.
