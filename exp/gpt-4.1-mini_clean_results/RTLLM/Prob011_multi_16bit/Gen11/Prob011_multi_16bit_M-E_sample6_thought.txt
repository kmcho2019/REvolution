Implement an unsigned 16-bit multiplier using an iterative shift-and-add approach but with a different control flow and architecture to clearly separate the control FSM and datapath, and minimize register toggling by updating accumulators only when needed.

Key points:
- Use a state machine with two states: IDLE and CALC.
- On start signal in IDLE, load inputs into registers and clear product and bit count.
- In CALC, process one bit per clock cycle for 16 cycles:
  - Check LSB of multiplier register, if 1, add multiplicand shifted appropriately to accumulator.
  - Shift multiplier right by 1.
  - Increment bit count.
- Once 16 bits processed, assert done, and go back to IDLE.
- On reset, clear all registers and outputs.
- This approach uses a dedicated bit counter separate from a general 5-bit counter.
- The multiplicand is kept static; multiplier shifts right each cycle.
- Accumulator holds the intermediate product.
- This approach avoids shifting multiplicand or building a wide shifted multiplier register.
- Use synchronous active-low reset.
- This architecture eases understanding, potentially lower switching by not shifting multiplicand or large registers unnecessarily.
