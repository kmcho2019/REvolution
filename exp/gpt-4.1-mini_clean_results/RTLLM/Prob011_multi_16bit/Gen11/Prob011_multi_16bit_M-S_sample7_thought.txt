Implement a straightforward shift-and-accumulate 16-bit unsigned multiplier controlled by a 5-bit counter (i) from 0 to 16. On start, load the multiplicand (ain) into a register and the multiplier (bin) into another register. Each clock cycle (i=1 to 16), if the bit (i-1) of the multiplicand is 1, add the multiplier shifted by (i-1) to the product register. Use synchronous logic with active-low reset and control done signal when the count reaches 16. Avoid FSM or complex state logic for simplicity.
