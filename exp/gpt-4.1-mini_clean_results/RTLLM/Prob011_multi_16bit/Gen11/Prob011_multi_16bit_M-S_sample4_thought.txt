To simplify the original solution while maintaining the correct shift-and-accumulate multiplication functionality, I will:

1. Use asynchronous active-low reset as originally requested.
2. Use straightforward logic without additional enable signals to keep the design simple.
3. Keep a 5-bit shift counter `i` counting from 0 to 17 to track the operation phases.
4. On `start` assertion with `i == 0`, load the multiplicand and multiplier registers.
5. From `i == 1` to `i == 16`, if the LSB of `areg` is 1, add the shifted multiplier to the accumulator `yout_r`.
6. Shift `areg` right and `breg` left by one each cycle in this phase.
7. Generate the done flag when `i == 16`.
8. Reset `done` at `i == 17` or when not started.
9. Reset all registers on `rst_n == 0`.
10. When `start` is low, reset the counter and accumulation registers to zero for simplicity.

This approach trades some toggling and power efficiency for easier readability and simpler control logic.
