The original fully combinational design uses a 16-stage generate loop with full subtraction and comparison at each stage, leading to a very long critical path and high area/power due to replicated hardware.

Improvement strategy:
- Retain fully combinational behavior as requested.
- Replace the large 9-bit remainder buses with 8-bit buses plus a carry bit, minimizing bus width.
- Instead of 9-bit subtraction, perform subtraction on 8-bit remainder plus an MSB bit extracted from dividend.
- Use simpler arithmetic by processing the remainder and next dividend bit separately.
- Use non-blocking procedural assignments in a single always_comb block to reduce wire proliferation and improve synthesizability.
- Use a local integer loop inside always_comb rather than generate, to reduce generated hardware complexity.
- This reduces logic duplication, shortens the critical path by removing large multi-level buses, and should reduce power and area while preserving functionality.
- Outputs are registered only combinationally, no sequential logic added.

This approach balances between a fully combinational solution and efficient hardware usage with smaller intermediate buses and simpler arithmetic operations.
