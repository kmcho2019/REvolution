Implement the 16-bit by 8-bit division combinationally by splitting the dividend into upper and lower 8-bit parts. 
First, divide the upper 8 bits of the dividend by the divisor to get an initial quotient and remainder.
Then, concatenate this remainder with the lower 8 bits of the dividend, forming a 16-bit intermediate dividend.
Next, divide this intermediate dividend by the divisor again to get the final quotient and remainder.
Concatenate both quotient parts to form the full 16-bit quotient, and use the last remainder as the overall remainder.
This double-step approach simulates the long division process but reduces complexity by handling two smaller division steps, each can be done combinationally by a simple subtraction loop implemented with generate.
The design uses two combinational divider sub-modules to implement this split process.
The two always blocks are present: one to latch the inputs into internal registers (combinationally), and the second to compute the division steps using these registers.
