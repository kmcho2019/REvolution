- The first combinational always block will latch the inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg, synchronizing the inputs for the main division logic.

- The second combinational always block will perform the division iteratively for all 16 bits of the dividend:

  1. Start with remainder = 0 (width 8 bits), quotient = 0 (16 bits).
  
  2. For each bit from MSB (bit 15) down to LSB (bit 0) of a_reg:

     - Else quotient bit is 0.
  
- By processing this way, we simulate the division where each step extracts the highest dividend bit, concatenates it with the remainder, and compares against the divisor.
  
- This method strictly follows the problem's concept: extract the highest bits of dividend matching divisor width, compare, update quotient bit, and calculate the new remainder.

- The remainder register is 8 bits since divisor is 8 bits; quotient is 16 bits.

- Outputs are assigned after the division loop completes.

- Zero division (B=0) will output quotient and remainder zero, to avoid undefined behavior.
