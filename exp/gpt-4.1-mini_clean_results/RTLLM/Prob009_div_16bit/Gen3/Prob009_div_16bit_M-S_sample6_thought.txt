Simplify the division by focusing solely on the core iterative comparison and subtraction without extra complexities:

- Use only one combinational always block.
- For each of the 16 bits from MSB to LSB:
  - Shift the current remainder (starting at 0) left by 1 bit and bring in the current dividend bit.
  - Compare the remainder with the divisor.
  - If remainder >= divisor, subtract divisor and set the corresponding quotient bit to 1; else quotient bit is 0.
- Output the final quotient and zero-extend the remainder to 16 bits.
- Remove unnecessary intermediate registers and extra always blocks.
- This approach cleanly implements the division using a classical bit-wise restoring algorithm.
