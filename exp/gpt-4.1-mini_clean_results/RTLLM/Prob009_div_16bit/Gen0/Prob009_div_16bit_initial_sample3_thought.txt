Implement a combinational 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor using a restoring division algorithm. Use two always blocks: the first to latch inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg, the second to perform the division process by shifting and subtracting the divisor from the upper bits of the remainder and building the quotient bit-by-bit. The division is done by extracting the top bits of the current remainder equal to the divisor width, comparing them, setting the quotient bit, and updating the remainder accordingly.
