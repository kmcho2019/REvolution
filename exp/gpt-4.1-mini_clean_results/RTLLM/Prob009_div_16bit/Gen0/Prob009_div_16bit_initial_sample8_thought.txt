Implement a combinational 16-bit by 8-bit division using the restoring division algorithm:
- Store inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg on any input change (combinational assignment).
- Use a second combinational block to perform the division:
  - Initialize remainder with the top 8 bits of a_reg.
  - Iterate over the remaining 8 bits of a_reg from MSB to LSB:
    - Compare remainder with divisor b_reg.
    - If remainder >= divisor, set corresponding quotient bit to 1 and subtract divisor from remainder.
    - Otherwise, quotient bit is 0 and remainder stays the same.
    - Shift in next bit of a_reg to the remainder.
  - After processing all bits, the quotient and remainder registers hold the results.
- Output quotient as result and remainder as odd.
