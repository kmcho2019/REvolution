Implement a combinational 16-bit division module with 16-bit dividend and 8-bit divisor. Use two always blocks: the first to register inputs into internal registers (a_reg and b_reg), and the second to perform the division algorithm in combinational logic. Extract 8 bits from the dividend, compare with the divisor, decide the quotient bit and remainder, then shift in the next dividend bit and repeat until all bits are processed. The quotient is 16 bits and the remainder is 16 bits as well (concatenated from remainder and remaining dividend bits).
