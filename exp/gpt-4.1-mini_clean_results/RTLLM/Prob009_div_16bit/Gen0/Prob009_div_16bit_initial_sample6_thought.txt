Implement a 16-bit divider module with an 8-bit divisor using combinational logic. The division algorithm iteratively extracts the top bits of the current remainder (initially the dividend), compares with the divisor, sets the quotient bit accordingly, and computes the new remainder by concatenating the difference with the next dividend bit. Use two combinational always blocks: the first registers inputs into a_reg and b_reg, the second performs the division logic updating quotient and remainder. The quotient is 16 bits, the remainder is 16 bits (though divisor is 8-bit, remainder fits in 16-bit output port as specified).
