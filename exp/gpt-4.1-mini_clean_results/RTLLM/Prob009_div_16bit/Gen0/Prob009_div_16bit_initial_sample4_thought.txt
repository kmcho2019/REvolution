Implement a combinational 16-bit divider module where an 8-bit divisor divides a 16-bit dividend. The approach is a bit-serial division starting from the highest bits of the dividend. Use two always @(*) blocks: the first to register inputs combinationally into a_reg and b_reg, and the second to perform the division using a shift-subtract approach. The division iterates through 16 steps, extracting bits from the remainder concatenated with the next dividend bit, comparing with the divisor, updating the quotient bit and remainder accordingly. Finally, output the 16-bit quotient as result and 16-bit remainder as odd.
