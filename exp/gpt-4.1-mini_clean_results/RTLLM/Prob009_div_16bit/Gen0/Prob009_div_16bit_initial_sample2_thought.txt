Implement a combinational 16-bit by 8-bit divider using a non-restoring or restoring division approach with shifts and comparisons. Use two always blocks: the first to latch inputs into registers a_reg and b_reg; the second to implement the division logic by iterating through each bit of the dividend, comparing the current remainder with the divisor, setting the quotient bits, updating the remainder accordingly. The quotient and remainder are stored in result and odd outputs respectively.
