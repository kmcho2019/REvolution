The division algorithm processes the 16-bit dividend bit-by-bit, starting from the highest bits. First, registers a_reg and b_reg latch the inputs A and B on any input change. Then in a second combinational always block, it performs a restoring division: for each of the 16 bits, extract the current remainder concatenated with the next dividend bit, compare with the divisor, update the quotient bit accordingly, and update the remainder. The quotient is accumulated over 16 steps. Finally, result outputs the quotient, and odd outputs the remainder.
