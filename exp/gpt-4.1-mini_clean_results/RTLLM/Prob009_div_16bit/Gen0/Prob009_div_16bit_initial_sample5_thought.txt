Implement a combinational divider where the division is done by shifting and subtracting the divisor from portions of the dividend. The module uses two always blocks: the first one captures inputs into registers a_reg and b_reg for stable reference; the second one performs the division iteratively by extracting the top bits of the current remainder, comparing with the divisor, updating quotient bits, and shifting to process all bits. Quotient bits are set accordingly, and the final quotient and remainder are output.
