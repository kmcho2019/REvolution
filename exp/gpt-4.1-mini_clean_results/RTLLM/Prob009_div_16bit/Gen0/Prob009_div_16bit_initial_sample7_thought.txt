Implement a 16-bit divider by 8-bit divisor using combinational logic. Use two always blocks as specified: one to latch inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg, and the second to perform the division using the described method. The division logic extracts an 8-bit segment from the current remainder (starting from the MSB of A), compares with divisor b_reg, sets the quotient bit accordingly, and updates the remainder. Repeat this process for all 16 bits. Output the quotient as 16-bit result and the remainder as 16-bit odd (remainder extended or shifted accordingly). All operations are combinational and the outputs reflect inputs immediately.
