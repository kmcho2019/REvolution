Implement a combinational 16-bit divider by processing each bit from the most significant to least significant in a loop, but instead of using the classic restoring division method with a 9-bit remainder register, maintain a separate 8-bit remainder that is always less than the divisor.

At each step:
- Shift the current remainder left by 1 bit, bringing in the next highest bit of the dividend.
- Compare this shifted remainder with the divisor.
- If remainder ≥ divisor, subtract divisor from remainder and set the current quotient bit to 1, else quotient bit is 0.

This approach avoids carrying an extra bit for remainder, making it simpler and more hardware friendly.

The module includes two combinational always blocks:
- The first always block captures inputs into internal registers for stability.
- The second always block implements the division algorithm iterating through all dividend bits.

The remainder output is the final 8-bit remainder zero-extended to 16 bits as per spec.

This design differs by maintaining remainder as 8 bits and performing the shift and comparison in a compact form without extending the divisor or remainder.
