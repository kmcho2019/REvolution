Implement a 16-bit by 8-bit combinational divider using iterative bit-by-bit trial subtraction resembling the restoring division algorithm. 

- First always block: latch inputs into internal registers `a_reg` and `b_reg`.
- Second always block: perform the division through a loop that shifts the remainder left by one, brings down the next dividend bit, compares with divisor, conditionally subtracts divisor from remainder, and sets quotient bits accordingly.
- Quotient and remainder registers (`result` and `odd`) are updated synchronously in the combinational always block.
- This design uses procedural code instead of generate loops, emphasizing clarity and ease of understanding and modification.
- This approach mimics hardware sequential division but fully unrolled into combinational logic (pure combinational output for given inputs).
