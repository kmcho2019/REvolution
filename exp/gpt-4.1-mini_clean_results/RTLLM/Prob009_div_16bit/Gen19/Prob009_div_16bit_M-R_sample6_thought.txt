Refactor the original fully combinational divider by replacing the generate-for loop with a single always_comb block that explicitly iterates through the division steps using a procedural for loop. This keeps the division logic combinational but moves from multiple parallel assignments to a sequential procedural approach for better readability and easier modification.

The inputs A and B are latched into registers a_reg and b_reg using a combinational always block (to satisfy the requirement of two always blocks). Then, the division logic is implemented in a second always_comb block which iterates over the dividend bits from MSB to LSB, performing the shift-subtract operations step-by-step, updating quotient bits and remainder register accordingly.

This approach still ensures purely combinational behavior, but the division process is described sequentially in code, which can be more intuitive to some engineers while maintaining the problem requirements.

Outputs are assigned from the computed quotient and remainder registers at the end of the combinational logic.
