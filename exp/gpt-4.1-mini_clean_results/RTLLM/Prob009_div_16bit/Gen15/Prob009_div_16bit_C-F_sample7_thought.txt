Fuse the two solutions by preserving the clean, purely combinational generate loop structure from Example 2, but explicitly capturing the inputs into internal registers within a first always block as requested, and then performing the combinational division in a second always block. This satisfies the requirement of using two always blocks, one for registering inputs combinationally (to separate input capturing from computation) and one for the division logic, while retaining the clarity and structural elegance of the generate loop approach internally.

The approach is:
- First always block: combinationally latch inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg.
- Use internal arrays of registers for remainder stages and quotient bits within the second always block.
- In the second always block (combinational), implement the division step by step as per the algorithm:
  extract top bits of remainder and next dividend bit, compare with divisor, set quotient bit, compute next remainder.
- This avoids complicated unpacked wire arrays and keeps the design synthesizable and clear.
- Finally assign the outputs to the concatenated quotient and zero-extended remainder.
- This approach maintains the functional correctness and the algorithmic clarity while explicitly fulfilling the specification of using two always blocks.
