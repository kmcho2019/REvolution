Implement a fully combinational 16-bit by 8-bit division using two always blocks as requested:

- The first always block captures inputs A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg combinationally.
- The second always block performs the division iteratively in a combinational way, simulating a shift-subtract division algorithm:
  - Initialize remainder to zero.
  - For each of 16 iterations (from MSB to LSB):
    - Shift remainder left by 1 bit, bring down the next dividend bit.
    - Compare the remainder (width 8 bits) with the divisor.
    - If remainder ≥ divisor, set quotient bit to 1 and subtract divisor from remainder; else quotient bit is 0.
- Quotient and remainder are updated each iteration.
- Output the final quotient and remainder.
- Use reg variables internally to hold intermediate states in the combinational always block.
- This approach uses only combinational logic with no clock or reset and matches the problem description of two combinational always blocks.
