Simplify the 16-bit divider module by using two combinational always blocks as requested: one to register inputs into internal regs, and the second to perform the division logic iteratively in a procedural way inside a single always @(*) block. The division will be implemented as a simple for-loop that simulates the shift-subtract division algorithm: shifting the remainder and bringing in bits from the dividend, comparing with divisor, and updating quotient and remainder accordingly.

This avoids the generate and wire arrays, making the design easier to read and verify, while maintaining fully combinational behavior. The first always block just captures inputs into registers combinationally (modeling input latches), and the second executes the division based on these registered values. The remainder output is zero-extended to 16 bits as before.

This approach matches the original instruction of two always blocks and keeps the logic combinational and concise.
