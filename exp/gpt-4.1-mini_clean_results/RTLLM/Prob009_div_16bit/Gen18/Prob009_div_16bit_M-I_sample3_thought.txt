To optimize the original purely combinational divider for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I will simplify the design by:

- Eliminating the first always block that simply copies inputs combinationally, as it adds no timing or area benefits.
- Using only one always block for the combinational division to reduce the logic duplication.
- Implementing the division logic with only scalar regs to avoid unpacked arrays that may synthesize into large multiplexers.
- Storing intermediate remainder as a single scalar register shifted and updated in each iteration.
- Using a simple for-loop with partial remainder shifting and subtracting to reduce combinational complexity.
- Assigning the quotient bits one-by-one without using a large array.
- This reduces both the logic complexity and the amount of intermediate signals, likely improving area and power.
- The remainder is padded to 16 bits with zeros in the upper bits as before.

This preserves the functionality and the combinational logic but with fewer registers and simpler logic, improving PPA while maintaining clarity.
