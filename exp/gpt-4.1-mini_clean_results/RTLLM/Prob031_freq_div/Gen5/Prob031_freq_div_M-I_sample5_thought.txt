To optimize the frequency divider for better power and area while preserving functionality, I will consolidate the counters into a single larger counter running from 0 to 99 and derive all output clocks by decoding this single counter. This reduces the total number of flip-flops by removing separate counters for 10MHz and 1MHz clocks. The 50MHz clock is derived by toggling a register every clock cycle as before, but the other outputs will toggle on specific counter values, cutting down switching activity on the counters. This approach simplifies the design, reduces power by minimizing counter toggling, and minimizes area by sharing logic.
