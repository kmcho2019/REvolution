Use a single 7-bit counter that increments on every rising edge of the 100MHz clock and resets after 100. Generate all three output clocks by comparing the counter value to specific thresholds:
- CLK_50 toggles every 1 cycle (effectively toggling on every clock edge; divide by 2).
- CLK_10 toggles when counter modulo 10 reaches 0 (count 0, 10, 20, ...), dividing by 10.
- CLK_1 toggles when counter reaches 49 (half of 100), dividing by 100.
This centralized counter reduces hardware by sharing counting logic and minimizes switching activity by toggling outputs only when conditions are met.
