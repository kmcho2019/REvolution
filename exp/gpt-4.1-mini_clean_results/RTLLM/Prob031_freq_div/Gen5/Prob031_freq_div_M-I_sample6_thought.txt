To optimize the frequency divider for better power and area while maintaining timing, I will introduce clock enable signals for the counters controlling CLK_10 and CLK_1 outputs. This way, counters only toggle their registers when needed, reducing unnecessary toggling and dynamic power consumption. I will also combine the counters' comparison and increment logic for cleaner synthesis.

Additionally, since CLK_50 toggles every cycle, it cannot be optimized further without changing the output frequency. But by adding clock enables, the overall register toggling activity is reduced, which benefits power.

The counters remain binary, using minimal bit widths, but gating counting will reduce switching. This approach improves power while keeping area and timing similar.
