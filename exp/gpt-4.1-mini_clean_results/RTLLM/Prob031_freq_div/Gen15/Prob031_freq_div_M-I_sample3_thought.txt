To improve the PPA (power, performance, area) of the original design, I will combine the 10MHz and 1MHz frequency dividers into a single hierarchical counter running up to 100 counts. This shared counter will increment on each CLK_in rising edge and generate both output clocks by toggling their respective outputs at appropriate counts.

Specifically:
- Use one 7-bit counter (cnt_100) counting from 0 to 99.
- Toggle CLK_1 (1MHz) every 50 counts (cnt_100 == 49).
- Toggle CLK_10 (10MHz) every 5 counts (cnt_100 modulo 10 == 4), i.e., when cnt_100%10 == 4.

CLK_50 remains as a simple toggle flip-flop dividing by 2.

This approach reduces area by removing a separate counter for 10MHz, decreases switching activity by sharing the counter, and maintains the asynchronous reset as before. It also simplifies the logic and slightly improves power and area, while keeping the same performance.

All outputs and counters use non-blocking assignments for synchronous design. The counters and toggles reset asynchronously.

This preserves correct functionality with fewer registers and logic.
