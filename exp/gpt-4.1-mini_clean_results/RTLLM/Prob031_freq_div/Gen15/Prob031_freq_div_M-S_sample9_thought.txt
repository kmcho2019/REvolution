We can simplify the design by merging all counters and toggling logic into a single always block triggered by the input clock and reset. This reduces the number of always blocks from two to one, improving readability and compactness without changing functionality.

The 50MHz clock toggles on every clock cycle, the 10MHz toggles every 5 cycles, and the 1MHz toggles every 50 cycles, corresponding to half-period counts. We keep minimal bit-width counters and use non-blocking assignments for synchronous operation.

This single-block approach maintains clarity, functional correctness, and efficiency while reducing code complexity.
