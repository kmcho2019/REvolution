Use a single 7-bit binary counter to count input clock cycles modulo 100. Generate all output clocks from this single counter by toggling output clocks at specific counter values. For CLK_50 (50MHz), toggle at half period (every 1 input clock cycle). For CLK_10 (10MHz), toggle at count 4 and 9 (every 5 input clocks). For CLK_1 (1MHz), toggle at count 49 and 99 (every 50 input clocks). This reduces flip-flop count and eliminates separate counters and multiple always blocks, improving area and power efficiency.
