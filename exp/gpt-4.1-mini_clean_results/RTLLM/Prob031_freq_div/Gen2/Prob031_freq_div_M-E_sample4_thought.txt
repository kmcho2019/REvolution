Use separate always blocks for each output clock to clearly separate the logic and enable independent control and possible parallel synthesis optimization. For CLK_50, simply toggle on every positive edge of CLK_in unless reset. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, use dedicated counters that count up to one less than half the division factor (4 for divide by 10, 49 for divide by 100), then toggle the clock and reset the counter. This modular approach improves readability and allows easier modification of individual frequency dividers.
