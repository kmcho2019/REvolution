I will implement the frequency divider using a single 7-bit counter that counts up from 0 to 99, representing one full period of the slowest divided clock (1 MHz). From this counter, I will derive the three output clocks by toggling them at the appropriate counts. This approach avoids multiple counters by centralizing counting and generating all outputs synchronously. The toggling points are chosen to achieve exact frequency division and 50% duty cycle for each output clock. This architecture simplifies synchronization and reduces the number of registers needed while providing clear timing.
