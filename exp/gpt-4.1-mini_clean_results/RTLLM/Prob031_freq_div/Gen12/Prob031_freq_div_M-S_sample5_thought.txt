To simplify the design, I will separate each clock divider into its own always block. This makes each divider's logic straightforward and easy to follow, reducing code interleaving. The counters and toggle conditions remain the same, but splitting the logic reduces complexity and improves clarity at the cost of slightly more always blocks.

The asynchronous reset is still used in all blocks to ensure immediate reset. The bit widths of counters remain minimal as before. Non-blocking assignments are maintained for proper synchronous updates.

This simplification trades slight increase in the number of always blocks for better readability and modularity without changing functionality or resource usage significantly.
