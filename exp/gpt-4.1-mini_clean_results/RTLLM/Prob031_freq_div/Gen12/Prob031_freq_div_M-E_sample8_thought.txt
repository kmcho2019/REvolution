I will implement the frequency divider by using separate always blocks for each output clock to clearly separate their logic. Each block will have its own counter and toggle logic, but the counters will count to half of the division factor since toggling on the halfway point generates a 50% duty cycle clock. For example, for 10MHz (divide by 10), count to 5 and toggle; for 1MHz (divide by 100), count to 50 and toggle. The 50MHz clock divides by 2, so toggling every input clock edge without a counter suffices. This approach increases modularity and may help timing closure by separating the logic paths, making it easier to optimize and maintain.
