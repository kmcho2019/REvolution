To simplify the frequency divider design, I will separate the logic into three straightforward always blocks for clarity and maintainability, each with their own counters where necessary. The 50MHz clock will be toggled directly on every input clock cycle, while the 10MHz and 1MHz clocks will use counters with clearly defined widths and count limits. I will keep asynchronous reset for simplicity and clear initialization, and avoid localparams for minimalism, directly using constants instead.
