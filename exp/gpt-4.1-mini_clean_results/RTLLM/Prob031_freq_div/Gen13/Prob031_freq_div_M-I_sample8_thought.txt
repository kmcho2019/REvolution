To optimize the design for better power and area, I will merge the two separate counters (cnt_10 and cnt_100) into a single larger counter that counts up to 100. By doing so, I reduce the total number of flip-flops and logic, simplifying the design and reducing switching activity on multiple counters.

The single 7-bit counter increments every clock cycle and resets at 99 (since the maximum count needed is 100 cycles). The output clocks are generated by toggling when the counter reaches specific values:
- CLK_50 toggles every cycle (divide by 2) as before.
- CLK_10 toggles every 5 cycles (counter counts from 0 to 9, toggles at 4).
- CLK_1 toggles every 50 cycles (toggles at 49).

This consolidated counter approach decreases the number of counters and their toggling, thus reducing power and area slightly while maintaining the same timing behavior.

The reset remains asynchronous for immediate response. The code maintains clear synchronous logic and non-blocking assignments.
