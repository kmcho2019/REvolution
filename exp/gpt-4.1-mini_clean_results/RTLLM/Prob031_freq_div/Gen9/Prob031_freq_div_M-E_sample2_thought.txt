To create a different architectural approach for the frequency divider, I will implement three separate always blocks, each dedicated to one output clock domain and its respective counter. This separation improves modularity and clarity by isolating each clock division logic, facilitating easier timing analysis and potential modifications. Each always block will have an asynchronous reset, and counters will count up to half the division factor minus one, toggling the output clock at that point. This method directly reflects the divide-by-N clock generation principle, with counters counting half periods. The widths of the counters are chosen to accommodate the maximum count values. This approach differs from the combined single always block and toggling on final count, and thus provides a clear, separate, parallel architecture for each divided clock.
