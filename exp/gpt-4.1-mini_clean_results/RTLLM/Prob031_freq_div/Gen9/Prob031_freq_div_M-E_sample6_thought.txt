Instead of using separate always blocks for each frequency output, implement a single always block to handle all counters and clock toggling simultaneously, simplifying synchronization and potentially reducing resource usage. Use a single 6-bit counter that increments every CLK_in cycle and resets at 100, and generate the 50MHz and 10MHz clocks by deriving their toggling conditions from this single counter: toggle CLK_50 at count 0 and 50 (effectively dividing by 2), and toggle CLK_10 at every 5 counts (divide by 10). CLK_1 toggles when the counter reaches 49 (divide by 100). This consolidated approach reduces the number of counters and logic blocks, centralizes reset handling, and keeps all operations synchronous.
