To improve power efficiency, minimize unnecessary switching of counters by gating them so they only count when needed. Since CLK_50 toggles every cycle, it must run continuously, but for CLK_10 and CLK_1 counters, counting can be enabled only when not toggling, reducing switching activity.

Performance-wise, splitting the always blocks allows simpler logic per block and may improve synthesis results by reducing fanout and critical path length.

Area impact will be minimal but the design becomes cleaner and more modular.

Hence, implement three separate always blocks: one for CLK_50 toggling, one for CLK_10 with a gated counter, and one for CLK_1 with a gated counter. Use synchronous reset for all.

This reduces power by gating counters and may improve timing and maintain clarity.
