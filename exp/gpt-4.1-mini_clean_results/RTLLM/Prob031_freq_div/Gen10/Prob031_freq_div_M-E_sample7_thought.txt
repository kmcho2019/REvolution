Implement the frequency divider using a single counter that increments on each positive edge of CLK_in. Use combinational logic to generate the three output clocks based on the counter value by comparing against thresholds and toggling each output clock accordingly. The counter resets when it reaches the maximum count needed for the slowest clock division (100 cycles). This approach reduces the number of separate counters by consolidating logic into one counter and combinational toggling logic, making the design more area-efficient and easier to scale. Asynchronous reset initializes all outputs and the counter.
