Refactor the frequency divider by consolidating the three frequency divisions into a single always block for synchronous logic, and generate the divided clocks by comparing counters with thresholds and toggling output signals. Use counters that count up to half the division factor minus one, then toggle the output and reset counters, which naturally creates 50% duty cycles.
