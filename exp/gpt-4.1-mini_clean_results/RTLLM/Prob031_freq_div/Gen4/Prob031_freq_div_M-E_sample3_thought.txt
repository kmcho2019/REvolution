Instead of using separate counters with direct toggle at count thresholds, implement a single scalable modulo-N counter and derive all output clocks from it by comparing the count value against predefined thresholds. The counter counts modulo 100 (the largest division factor times 2 to achieve 50% duty cycle). Outputs are generated by comparing the counter value to half of their full period, effectively generating the three clocks with 50% duty cycles and proper phase alignment.

Specifically:
- Use a 7-bit counter counting from 0 to 99 (since 100 clock cycles = full period for 1 MHz clock).
- For 50 MHz clock: toggle output when counter is less than 50 (high half of period).
- For 10 MHz clock: toggle output high when counter < 5 (half period = 10 cycles, so 5 cycles high).
- For 1 MHz clock: toggle output high when counter < 50 (half period = 100 cycles, so 50 cycles high).
- The counter resets on RST and increments on each CLK_in rising edge.

This centralized approach simplifies the control logic, reduces number of counters, and ensures all derived clocks are phase-aligned and stable with a simple combinational comparison for the output clocks.
