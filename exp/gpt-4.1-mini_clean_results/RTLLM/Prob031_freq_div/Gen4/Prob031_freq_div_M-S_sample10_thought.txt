To simplify the frequency divider, I will keep a single always block triggered by the rising edge of CLK_in or RST to handle all the outputs and counters together. 

For CLK_50, toggling it every clock cycle effectively divides the frequency by 2, so no counter is needed.

For CLK_10 and CLK_1, I'll use minimal counters with the exact widths necessary, incrementing them and toggling the outputs when reaching the specified limits.

This approach removes parameters and slightly reduces code complexity while maintaining clear functionality and synthesis efficiency.
