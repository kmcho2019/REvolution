To optimize power and area further while maintaining timing, I will merge the two counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 into a single counter that counts up to the least common multiple of their toggle counts (50 for cnt_100 and 5 for cnt_10) and derive both CLK_10 and CLK_1 from this single counter. This reduces hardware by avoiding two separate counters. The single counter counts from 0 to 49, resetting thereafter. CLK_10 toggles every 5 cycles and CLK_1 toggles every 50 cycles, so we can generate these by checking the counter value and toggling at appropriate counts.

CLK_50 remains toggled on every clock cycle, which is already minimal hardware.

This approach reduces register usage and switching in extra counters, potentially improving area and power.

The counting and toggling logic remains synchronous and simple, preserving timing performance.
