Use a single 7-bit counter counting from 0 to 99 to generate all divided clocks simultaneously by decoding the counter value for toggling output clocks. This saves registers and logic by sharing counting resources. CLK_50 toggles every 1 count (half period 1 count), CLK_10 toggles every 5 counts (half period 5 counts), CLK_1 toggles every 50 counts (half period 50 counts). The counter resets at 99 and counts up each clock cycle. Outputs toggle when the counter reaches their respective half-period counts. This architecture reduces area by sharing counting logic and uses simple comparison logic to generate the required frequencies.
