To optimize the frequency divider for better power and area efficiency, I will reduce the toggling activity of the CLK_10 and CLK_1 outputs by using clock enable signals and toggling them only when required, instead of toggling or updating every clock cycle.  
Specifically, the CLK_10 and CLK_1 signals will toggle only at their respective count thresholds, not on every clock edge, reducing unnecessary switching.  
Also, counters and toggle registers will be updated conditionally, which reduces switching activity and saves dynamic power.  
This approach keeps the 50MHz clock logic as is because it must toggle every cycle.  
Additionally, I will use minimum required bit-widths for counters for area savings and use blocking assignments in combinational logic (if any) while keeping sequential logic with non-blocking assignments for synthesis clarity.  
This optimization will reduce power consumption, slightly reduce area by lowering toggling in registers, and maintain correct timing behavior with synchronous resets.
