I will implement a frequency divider using a single multi-bit counter to derive all three output clocks, instead of separate counters. The counter will increment on every CLK_in positive edge. For each output clock, a dedicated bit or bits of the counter will be used to produce the desired frequency division by exploiting binary counting properties. For example, the 50MHz clock can be the LSB toggling every cycle (divide by 2), the 10MHz can be generated by toggling when the counter reaches certain counts (divide by 10), and the 1MHz similarly by toggling at certain counts. This centralized counting approach reduces hardware redundancy and simplifies logic, as only one counter and combinational logic are required. An asynchronous reset will reset the counter and all output clocks.
