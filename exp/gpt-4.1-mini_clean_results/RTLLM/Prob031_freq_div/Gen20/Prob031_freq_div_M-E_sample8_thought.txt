An alternative approach is to implement all three frequency dividers using separate counters that count clock cycles and generate output clocks by toggling at half the target division counts. Instead of toggling the output clock on the counter reaching the full count and then resetting, we toggle at half the period and reset at the full period, effectively generating a 50% duty cycle clock. This approach ensures the output clocks have a symmetrical duty cycle even for non-even division factors.

For example:
- CLK_50 divides by 2: toggles every 1 clock cycle.
- CLK_10 divides by 10: toggle every 5 clock cycles, reset at 10.
- CLK_1 divides by 100: toggle every 50 clock cycles, reset at 100.

This design uses separate counters for each clock with appropriately sized counters, and toggles the clocks synchronously on the positive edge of CLK_in. The reset is synchronous.

This approach is modular and scalable, preserves the 50% duty cycle accurately, and cleanly separates the logic for each divided clock.
