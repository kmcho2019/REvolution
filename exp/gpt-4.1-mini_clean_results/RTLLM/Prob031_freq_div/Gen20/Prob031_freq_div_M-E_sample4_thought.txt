Implement a single centralized binary counter that counts clock cycles up to the maximum needed count (for 1 MHz output, up to 100 cycles). Derive the three output clocks by comparing the counter to thresholds and toggling each output at half the division period. This reduces the number of counters and simplifies synchronization between outputs. CLK_50 toggles every 1 cycle (half period = 1), CLK_10 toggles every 5 cycles, and CLK_1 toggles every 50 cycles. This approach uses a single counter and combinational logic to generate enables for toggling outputs, reducing flip-flop count and making all outputs inherently synchronous.
