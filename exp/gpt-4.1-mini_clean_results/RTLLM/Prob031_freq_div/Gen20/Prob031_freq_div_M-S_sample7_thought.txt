To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality, I will:

1. Use a single always block triggered on the positive edge of CLK_in or RST to handle all counters and output clocks.
2. Use zero-based counters directly in the sequential logic without separate combinational next-state logic.
3. Toggle output clocks when their counters reach max values and reset counters inside the always block.
4. Keep counters and outputs minimal in width and logic for clarity and simplicity.
5. Maintain asynchronous reset as specified.

This reduces complexity by avoiding extra combinational blocks and next-state signals, resulting in a straightforward, easy-to-read implementation.
