I will implement a single synchronous always block to handle all three clock divisions together, using separate counters and toggles for each output. Instead of asynchronous reset, I will use synchronous reset for cleaner timing and easier synthesis optimization. For frequency division, the counters will count down from preset values and toggle the output clock signals when they reach zero, then reload the counters. This approach centralizes logic in one block, reduces repetitive always blocks, and can be more resource-efficient in some synthesis tools. Also, counting down improves clarity for reload values. The counters will be sized adequately for their division ratios.
