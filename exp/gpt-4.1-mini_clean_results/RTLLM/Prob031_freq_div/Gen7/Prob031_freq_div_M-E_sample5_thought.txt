Use separate always blocks for each clock output to improve modularity and readability. Implement synchronous resets within each always block. For CLK_50, toggle output on every positive clock edge, dividing by 2. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, use separate counters that count half of the division factor (because toggling the output doubles the division), toggling the output and resetting the counter when the count is reached. This approach isolates each divider logic cleanly, making the design easy to modify and verify.
