To simplify the frequency divider design while maintaining functionality, I will remove the unnecessary clock enable signals since they are always enabled. The counters remain separate as described, but will be implemented straightforwardly without redundant signals or complexity. The 50MHz output toggles every cycle, the 10MHz toggles every 5 cycles, and the 1MHz toggles every 50 cycles. This straightforward approach reduces complexity, improves readability, and is fully functional.
