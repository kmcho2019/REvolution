Implement a frequency divider module with input clock 100MHz and reset. Generate three output clocks:
- CLK_50 toggles every clock cycle (divide by 2).
- CLK_10 toggles every 5 clock cycles (count from 0 to 4, then toggle) to get 10MHz.
- CLK_1 toggles every 50 clock cycles (count from 0 to 49, then toggle) to get 1MHz.

Use separate counters for CLK_10 and CLK_1. On reset, clear the clocks and counters.
On each rising edge of input clock, update counters and toggle output clocks accordingly.
