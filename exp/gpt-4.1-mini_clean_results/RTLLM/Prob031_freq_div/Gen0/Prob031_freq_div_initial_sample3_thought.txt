To implement the frequency divider, I will create a module named freq_div with input clock CLK_in and reset RST. I will generate three output clocks CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1. For CLK_50, it will toggle every clock cycle to divide by 2. For CLK_10, I will use a 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 4, toggling CLK_10 on reaching 4, thus dividing by 10. For CLK_1, I will use a 6-bit counter counting from 0 to 49, toggling CLK_1 on reaching 49, thus dividing by 100. All counters and output clocks reset to 0 when RST is active.
