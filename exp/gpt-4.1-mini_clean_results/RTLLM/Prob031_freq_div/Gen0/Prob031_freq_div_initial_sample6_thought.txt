Implement a frequency divider module with input clock 100MHz and reset. 
- For CLK_50 (50MHz), toggle output on every positive edge of CLK_in unless reset is active.
- For CLK_10 (10MHz), use a 3-bit counter (0 to 4). When counter reaches 4, toggle CLK_10 and reset counter.
- For CLK_1 (1MHz), use a 6-bit counter (0 to 49). When counter reaches 49, toggle CLK_1 and reset counter.
Reset sets all outputs and counters to zero synchronously.
