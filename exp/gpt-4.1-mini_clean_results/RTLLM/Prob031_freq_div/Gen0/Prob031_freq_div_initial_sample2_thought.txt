Use three separate always blocks triggered on the positive edge of CLK_in or asynchronous reset RST. 
For CLK_50, simply toggle the output at every clock edge, achieving a divide-by-2 frequency.
For CLK_10, maintain a 3-bit counter (0 to 4); toggle output when counter reaches 4, effectively dividing by 10 (because output toggles every 5 input clocks).
For CLK_1, maintain a 6-bit counter (0 to 49); toggle output when counter reaches 49, effectively dividing by 100 (because output toggles every 50 input clocks).
Reset all outputs and counters when RST is active.
