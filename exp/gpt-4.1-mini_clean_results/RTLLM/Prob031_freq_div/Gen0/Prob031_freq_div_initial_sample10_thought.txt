Implement a module freq_div with input clock CLK_in at 100MHz and reset RST. The output clocks are CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1.
CLK_50 is generated by toggling on every rising edge (divide by 2).
CLK_10 is generated by counting 5 cycles (count from 0 to 4) then toggling CLK_10 (divide by 10).
CLK_1 is generated by counting 50 cycles (0 to 49) then toggling CLK_1 (divide by 100).
Counters reset with RST, and outputs are reset to 0 on RST.
