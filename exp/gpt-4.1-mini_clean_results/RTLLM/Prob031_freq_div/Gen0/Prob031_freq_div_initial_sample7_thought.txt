To generate the required clock frequencies, implement three separate counters and clock toggling logic:
- For 50 MHz (divide by 2), toggle CLK_50 on every positive edge of the input clock.
- For 10 MHz (divide by 10), use a 4-bit counter cnt_10 that counts from 0 to 4, toggling CLK_10 at count 4.
- For 1 MHz (divide by 100), use a 6-bit counter cnt_100 that counts from 0 to 49, toggling CLK_1 at count 49.
On reset, clear outputs and counters accordingly.
