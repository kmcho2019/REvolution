Implement a frequency divider with three output clocks derived from a 100MHz input clock.
- CLK_50 toggles every clock cycle, effectively dividing by 2.
- CLK_10 toggles every 5 clock cycles (counting from 0 to 4), dividing by 10.
- CLK_1 toggles every 50 clock cycles (counting from 0 to 49), dividing by 100.
Use synchronous reset to initialize outputs and counters.
