Implement a frequency divider module named freq_div that takes a 100MHz clock input and produces three output clocks at 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz respectively. Use counters to generate 10MHz and 1MHz clocks by toggling the output after counting the required number of input clock cycles. The 50MHz clock is generated by toggling on every clock cycle. Reset is asynchronous and synchronous logic is used inside always blocks triggered on posedge of CLK_in or posedge of RST.
