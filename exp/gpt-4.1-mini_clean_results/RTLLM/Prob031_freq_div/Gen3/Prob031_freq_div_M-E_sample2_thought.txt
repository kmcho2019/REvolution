Implement a frequency divider using a single unified counter with a wider bit-width and logic that generates the three output clocks by comparing the counter value to precomputed threshold values corresponding to half the division periods. Instead of separate counters and toggling flip-flops, output clocks are assigned combinationally inside a single synchronous always block, toggling when the counter reaches certain values, then resetting the counter at the overall period (100 cycles for 1MHz). This unified approach reduces the number of counters and simplifies control logic while maintaining synchronous resets and precise timing. The 50MHz output toggles every cycle (divide by 2), the 10MHz output toggles every 5 cycles, and the 1MHz output toggles every 50 cycles, all derived from the single counter. This reduces logic and improves readability while preserving exact output frequencies.
