Simplify the design by removing explicit next-state variables and combining the counter increment and toggle logic directly inside synchronous always blocks. Since the counters are small and logic is straightforward, we can write all logic sequentially without separate combinational always blocks. This reduces code complexity while preserving functionality and readability.

The toggling conditions and counter updates can be handled within the single always block triggered by clock and reset. This approach is more concise and easier to follow for such a simple frequency divider.
