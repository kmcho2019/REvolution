To optimize the frequency divider for better power and area, I'll use hierarchical division: generate the 50 MHz clock by toggling every input clock cycle, then derive the 10 MHz clock by dividing the 50 MHz clock by 5, and finally derive the 1 MHz clock by dividing the 10 MHz clock by 10. This reduces the number of counters toggling at the full 100 MHz rate, thereby lowering switching activity (power) and area. Also, I'll use synchronous reset signals and non-blocking assignments consistently. This approach reduces the toggle frequency on slower clock domains and centralizes division, improving power and area metrics without compromising performance.
