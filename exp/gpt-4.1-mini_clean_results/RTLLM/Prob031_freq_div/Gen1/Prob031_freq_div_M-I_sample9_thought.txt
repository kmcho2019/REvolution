The original design is functionally correct but can be optimized for power and area by reducing switching activity and consolidating counter logic where possible:

- CLK_50 toggles every cycle, which causes maximum switching; this is unavoidable given the divide-by-2 requirement.
- For CLK_10 and CLK_1, instead of separate counters and toggles, combine them into one always block for better area and synthesis efficiency.
- Use one common synchronous reset and non-blocking assignments consistently.
- Since CLK_10 and CLK_1 toggle at different counts, maintain separate counters but inside a single always block to reduce flip-flop overhead and control logic.
- Minimize counter bit widths to the exact needed size.
- Optionally, use parameterization for counters for reuse and clarity.
- No division or multipliers are introduced, keeping combinational logic minimal.
- Keeping separate output registers but sharing clocking logic improves area with minimal impact on timing.
This approach balances clarity, area, and power better without changing the fundamental logic.
