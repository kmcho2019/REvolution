To optimize the frequency divider for better power and area, I will share a single counter for both CLK_10 and CLK_1 since their frequencies are multiples (10 and 100). This reduces redundant counters and logic. CLK_50 remains separate because it toggles every clock cycle (divide by 2) and cannot share counting logic easily. 

The shared counter counts up to 49 (for CLK_1), and inside that cycle, CLK_10 toggles every 5 counts, so the toggling condition for CLK_10 is when the shared counter modulo 5 equals 4. This eliminates one entire counter register and comparison, reducing area and power. 

I will implement the shared counter with a 6-bit register and produce both CLK_10 and CLK_1 toggles based on counter value comparisons. This optimizes area by reducing registers and comparators, and can reduce power by limiting toggling logic. 

Reset logic remains synchronous and all outputs are initialized properly. This approach keeps the design simple while improving PPA over three separate counters.
