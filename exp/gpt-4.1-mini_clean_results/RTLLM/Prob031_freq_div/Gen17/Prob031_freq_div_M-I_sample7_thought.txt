To optimize for better power and area, reduce redundant counters by sharing a single counter that counts to 100. From this counter, derive both 10MHz and 1MHz clocks by toggling their outputs at appropriate counts (every 5 counts for 10MHz toggle, every 50 counts for 1MHz toggle). This eliminates the need for two separate counters and reduces total logic and switching activity.

The 50MHz clock remains a simple toggle flip-flop dividing by 2, as it's already minimal.

This shared counter approach lowers the number of registers and combinational logic, improving area and potentially power, while maintaining performance and functional correctness.
