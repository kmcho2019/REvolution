To optimize the design for better area and power, combine all counters into a single counter that counts from 0 to 99 (for divide-by-100), and generate the output clocks via combinational logic from this single counter value. CLK_50 (divide by 2) can be derived by toggling at half the count (at count 49), CLK_10 (divide by 10) by toggling at every 5 counts, and CLK_1 (divide by 100) by toggling at count 49. This removes multiple counters and reduces flip-flop usage, also minimizing switching activity in counters and registers. Using a single synchronous always block with synchronous reset avoids glitches and reduces power due to less clock toggling inside counters. The combinational logic to generate output clocks will be straightforward and efficient.
