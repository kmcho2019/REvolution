Instead of using separate counters for 10MHz and 1MHz clock outputs, use a single 7-bit counter that counts from 0 to 99 (for 100 clock cycles). Generate 50MHz by toggling on every clock cycle (divide by 2). For the 10MHz output, toggle the clock when the counter reaches every 5 counts (since toggling every 5 cycles results in 10MHz). For the 1MHz output, toggle the clock when the counter reaches 49 counts (dividing by 100). This shared counter approach reduces hardware resources and simplifies synchronization between outputs. The counter resets to zero after reaching 99. All counters and toggling are implemented in a single always block to ensure consistent state updates and minimal logic complexity.
