Implement a single 7-bit counter that counts from 0 up to 99 on each clock cycle. Use combinational logic to derive the three output clocks by toggling them at their respective division points based on the counter value. Specifically, toggle CLK_50 every 1 cycle (divide by 2), toggle CLK_10 every 5 cycles (divide by 10), and toggle CLK_1 every 50 cycles (divide by 100). This centralized counting approach reduces the number of counters to just one, saving area and simplifying clock division logic. The toggling conditions for each output will be derived from the counter value, ensuring all outputs are synchronous to the input clock and reset together.
