To optimize the comparator for better power and area, I will implement a bitwise hierarchical comparison starting from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). This approach allows early resolution of comparison without evaluating all bits fully, potentially reducing gate count and switching activity compared to using the parallel comparison operators. The outputs remain mutually exclusive, and no clock or state is involved to keep it purely combinational. This bitwise chain reduces logic depth and may slightly improve critical path delay and power due to fewer transitions on lower bits when MSB differs.
