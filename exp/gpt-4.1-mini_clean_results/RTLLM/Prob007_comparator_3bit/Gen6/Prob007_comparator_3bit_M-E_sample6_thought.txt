Implement the 3-bit comparator by performing a direct subtraction (A - B) and inspecting the sign of the result and zero condition for equality. Use combinational arithmetic subtraction and detect zero by NOR of all result bits. If the result is zero, then A_equal is high. If the sign bit of the subtraction (most significant bit of the 4-bit subtraction result) is 0 and not zero, then A > B; if sign bit is 1 and not zero, then A < B. This method uses arithmetic to simplify comparison logic, avoids multiple hierarchical bitwise checks, and inherently guarantees mutual exclusivity among outputs.
