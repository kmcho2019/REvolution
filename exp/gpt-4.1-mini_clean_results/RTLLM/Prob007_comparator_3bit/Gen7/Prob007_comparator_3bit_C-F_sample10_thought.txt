To combine the strengths of both examples, implement a hierarchical bitwise comparator logic for clarity, explicit mutual exclusivity, and efficiency, but simplify equality checking using XOR and NOR gates rather than repeated bitwise equality checks. This approach avoids redundant comparisons seen in the direct relational operator method and reduces logic depth from nested conditionals in Example 1. Also, by determining equality first with an XOR reduction and then using a cascading check for greater-than from MSB to LSB, the outputs remain mutually exclusive by design, improving power and area. The less-than output is computed by exclusion from greater and equal, ensuring only one output is high. This fusion yields a compact, clear, and efficient comparator suited for synthesis and low-power design.
