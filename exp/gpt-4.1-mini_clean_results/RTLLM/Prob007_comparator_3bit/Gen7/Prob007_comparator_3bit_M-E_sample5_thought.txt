Implement the 3-bit comparator by performing a bitwise hierarchical comparison from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). At each bit position, determine if A is greater, equal, or less than B. The first bit where A and B differ determines the overall comparison. This approach mimics a priority encoder style logic, ensuring mutual exclusivity and potentially minimizing logic depth by short-circuiting once a decisive bit is found.
