Implement the 3-bit comparator using a straightforward arithmetic difference approach by subtracting B from A as unsigned numbers and analyzing the result along with equality. If the difference is zero, A equals B. If the difference's MSB of the 4-bit result (sign bit in 2's complement) is zero and difference is non-zero, A is greater; otherwise, A is less. This approach uses simple addition/subtraction logic without bitwise comparisons and ensures mutual exclusivity by construction.
