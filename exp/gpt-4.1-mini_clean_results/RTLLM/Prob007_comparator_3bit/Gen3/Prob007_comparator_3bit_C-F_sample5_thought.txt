Fuse the clarity and simplicity of direct relational operators with an explicit hierarchical bitwise comparison for slightly optimized gate-level implementation.  
This approach first compares the MSB down to LSB, reducing redundant evaluation and potentially lowering switching and gate count for a small 3-bit comparator, while still ensuring mutual exclusivity and correctness.  
Outputs are assigned combinationally with priority to A_greater, then A_less, else A_equal, making logic mutually exclusive and clear.  
This yields a concise yet optimized comparator that balances readability, minimal logic, and good PPA characteristics.
