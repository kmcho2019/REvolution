I will implement the 3-bit comparator by constructing a chain of 1-bit comparisons from the most significant bit (MSB) down to the least significant bit (LSB). At each bit position, I compare A[i] and B[i]. If they differ, the decision is made based on that bit; if they are equal, the comparison proceeds to the next less significant bit. This mimics a priority encoder style cascade and can be implemented with simple combinational logic using ternary conditions. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are mutually exclusive by design. This approach clearly shows bitwise cascading and is easy to expand for wider buses.
