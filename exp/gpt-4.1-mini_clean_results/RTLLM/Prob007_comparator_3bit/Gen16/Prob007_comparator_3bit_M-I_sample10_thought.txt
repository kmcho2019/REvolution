The original design is already clear and functionally correct, but we can optimize it slightly to reduce the overall gate count and potentially reduce critical path delay by using the Verilog relational operators directly.

Improvement strategy:
- Use direct comparison operators (>, ==, <) for combinational logic. Modern synthesis tools optimize these efficiently into minimal logic.
- This removes the need for intermediate bitwise XNOR and AND signals, reducing gate levels and possibly power.
- Outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction from comparisons.
- This approach is also succinct, improving readability without sacrificing clarity.

The expected result is the same functionality with potentially lower area and power, and possibly improved performance due to simpler logic.
