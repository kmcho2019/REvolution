Refactor the original combinational comparator by leveraging Verilog's direct comparison operators instead of bitwise logic and intermediate wires. This approach simplifies the design by using the built-in relational operators (`>`, `==`, `<`) on the 3-bit inputs directly, which modern synthesis tools optimize efficiently. 

The outputs remain mutually exclusive and are assigned via continuous assignments. This removes the need for intermediate equality signals and reduces verbosity while maintaining clarity and synthesis friendliness.
