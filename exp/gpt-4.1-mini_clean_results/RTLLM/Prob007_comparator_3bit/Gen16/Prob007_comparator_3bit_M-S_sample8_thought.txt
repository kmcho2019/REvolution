To simplify the 3-bit comparator design, I will leverage Verilog's built-in relational operators instead of explicit bitwise equality and hierarchical greater-than logic. This will drastically reduce complexity and lines of code, while maintaining full functionality.

Specifically:
- Use direct comparison A > B, A == B, and A < B to assign the outputs.
- This ensures outputs are mutually exclusive and the logic is purely combinational.
- The design is more concise and readable, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the comparison efficiently.

This approach trades explicit gate-level detail for concise and clear RTL, suitable for synthesis with optimal PPA results in typical tools.
