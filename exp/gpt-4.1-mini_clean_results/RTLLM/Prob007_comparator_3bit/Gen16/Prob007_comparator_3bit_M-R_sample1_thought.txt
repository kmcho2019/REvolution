Refactor the 3-bit comparator by directly using the built-in comparison operators in Verilog to evaluate A and B relationships without decomposing into bitwise equality signals. This approach keeps the design purely combinational and concise. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are determined by simple comparison operations, ensuring mutual exclusivity naturally. This reduces the intermediate wire signals and relies on synthesizer optimizations for area and performance, while maintaining clarity and correctness.
