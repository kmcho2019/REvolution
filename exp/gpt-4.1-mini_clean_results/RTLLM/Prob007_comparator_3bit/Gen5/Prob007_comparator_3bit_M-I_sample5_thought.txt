To optimize for power and area while preserving performance, use a bitwise hierarchical comparison starting from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). This method avoids the implicit logic synthesis of relational operators which may generate redundant logic. By explicitly encoding the comparison conditions, we can reduce logic complexity and potentially reduce delay and power since the outputs depend on priority conditions of the bits. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction. This is a typical approach used in comparator designs to optimize gate-level implementation.
