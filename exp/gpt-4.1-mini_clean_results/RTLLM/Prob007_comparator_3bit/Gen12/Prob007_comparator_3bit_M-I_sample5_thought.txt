To optimize the 3-bit comparator for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will simplify the equality check by using XOR gates followed by a NOR, which is more hardware-efficient than multiple equality checks with AND. For the greater-than detection, I will implement a clean priority encoder style with minimal logic depth, directly checking the bits hierarchically without nested conditional operators. This approach reduces logic levels, improves readability, and can slightly reduce area and power. The less-than output is derived by exclusion, ensuring mutual exclusivity. This refinement maintains correctness and enhances synthesis friendliness.
