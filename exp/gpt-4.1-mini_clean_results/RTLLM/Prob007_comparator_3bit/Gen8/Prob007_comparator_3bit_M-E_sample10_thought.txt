Implement the 3-bit comparator using a hierarchical approach: compare the most significant bits first, then only proceed to lower bits if the higher bits are equal. This can be efficiently done by cascading 1-bit comparators that output greater, equal, and less signals. The final outputs are derived by combining these signals with enable signals that allow lower bit comparisons only if higher bits are equal. This modular approach clearly shows the flow of comparison and naturally ensures mutual exclusivity of outputs.
