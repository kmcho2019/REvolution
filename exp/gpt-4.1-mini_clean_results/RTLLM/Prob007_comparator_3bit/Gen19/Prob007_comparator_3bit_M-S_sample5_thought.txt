To simplify the 3-bit comparator design while maintaining correct functionality and mutual exclusivity of outputs, I will directly use Verilog's built-in comparison operators. This approach eliminates intermediate wires and explicit bitwise operations, producing clearer and more concise code. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be assigned based on direct comparison expressions on the inputs, which synthesis tools will efficiently optimize. This reduction improves readability and reduces code complexity without losing performance or correctness.
