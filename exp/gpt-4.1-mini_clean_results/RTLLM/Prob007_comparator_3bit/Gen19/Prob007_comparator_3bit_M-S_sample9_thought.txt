To simplify the 3-bit comparator, I will leverage the fact that Verilog supports direct vector comparison operators for small-width signals. Using these operators makes the code more concise, readable, and inherently mutually exclusive without needing intermediate wires or manual bitwise comparisons.

Specifically:
- Use the built-in relational operators `>`, `==`, and `<` directly on the 3-bit vectors.
- Assign outputs based on these comparisons with simple combinational assignments.
- This approach preserves all required functionality, ensures outputs are mutually exclusive, and reduces the amount of code and signals.

This leads to a minimal, clear, and efficient implementation while maintaining correctness.
