I will implement the 3-bit comparator by directly comparing the 3-bit inputs as unsigned values using simple combinational operators. This approach leverages the built-in Verilog relational operators (<, ==, >) for clarity and succinctness, which synthesis tools can easily optimize. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, explicitly encoded as one-hot signals: only one of A_greater, A_equal, or A_less is asserted at a time. This results in a minimal and clean design with straightforward readability and high synthesis efficiency.
