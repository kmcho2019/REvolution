To optimize the 3-bit comparator for better power and area without compromising performance, implement a bitwise priority encoder style comparison from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). This approach compares bits starting from A[2] and B[2]; if they differ, outputs are decided immediately, avoiding redundant logic evaluation on lower bits. If MSBs are equal, move to the next bit, and so on. This structure allows sharing intermediate signals, reduces redundant comparisons, and yields mutually exclusive outputs with minimal logic, thereby improving area and power slightly.
