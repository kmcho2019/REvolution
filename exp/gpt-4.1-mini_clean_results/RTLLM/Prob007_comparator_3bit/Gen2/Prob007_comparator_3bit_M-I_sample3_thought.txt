To optimize the 3-bit comparator for better PPA, replace the direct relational operators with a bitwise hierarchical comparison starting from the MSB to the LSB. This reduces combinational logic depth by evaluating the most significant bits first and deciding the comparison early if possible. It also potentially reduces power by minimizing toggling in lower bits if the higher bits differ, and area by using simple logic gates instead of built-in comparator operators. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by design.
