Optimize the 3-bit comparator by explicitly encoding the comparison with hierarchical combinational logic. Instead of using the direct relational operators (> , ==, <), implement bitwise comparison starting from the most significant bit to the least significant bit. This can reduce the critical path delay by early determination of the comparison outcome, as the highest differing bit dominates the result. This approach can improve timing and slightly reduce power by minimizing unnecessary switching in lower bits when the decision is already known from the higher bits. Outputs remain mutually exclusive by design.
