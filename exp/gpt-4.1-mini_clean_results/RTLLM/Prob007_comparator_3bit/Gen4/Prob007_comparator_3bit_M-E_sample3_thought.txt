Implement the 3-bit comparator using a hierarchical bitwise comparison approach starting from the MSB. The idea is to compare bits from the most significant bit (bit 2) to the least significant bit (bit 0), resolving the comparison at the highest differing bit. This allows building the comparison outputs through combinational logic that cascades decision signals down the bit levels, avoiding direct relational operators. This method can reduce redundant checks by early decision and potentially reduce combinational depth.
