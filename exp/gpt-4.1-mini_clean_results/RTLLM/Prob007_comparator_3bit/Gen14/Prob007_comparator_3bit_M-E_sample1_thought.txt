I will implement the 3-bit comparator using simple arithmetic subtraction and checking the sign and zero results to determine the relationship between A and B. Specifically, I will subtract B from A and analyze the sign bit and zero detection of the result. If the subtraction result is zero, A and B are equal. If the subtraction is positive (MSB of the difference is 0 and not zero), then A > B. If the subtraction is negative (MSB of the difference is 1), then A < B. This approach uses arithmetic operations and simple logic to generate the outputs, providing a compact, uniform, and scalable architecture. Additionally, the design naturally guarantees mutual exclusivity of outputs.
