Refactor the comparator by directly using vector comparisons instead of hierarchical bitwise comparisons and intermediate equality signals. Using Verilog's reduction and relational operators at the vector level simplifies the logic and improves readability. Equality is computed by comparing vectors with the equality operator (==). Greater and less-than are assigned via direct relational comparisons. This approach ensures outputs are mutually exclusive, as the relational operators are exclusive by definition. This refactoring trades explicit bitwise logic for clean vector-level operations while maintaining the same behavior and mutually exclusive outputs.
