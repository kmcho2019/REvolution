To optimize for power and area while maintaining clarity and synthesis friendliness, I will reduce redundant equality checks by sharing intermediate signals. Specifically, precompute equality of MSB and next bit, reuse those signals in greater-than logic, and implement the equality check compactly as bitwise XNORs combined with AND gates. This reduces switching nodes and gate count. Also, by reusing equality signals in the greater-than logic, the design complexity and switching activity decrease slightly, improving power and area. Outputs remain mutually exclusive with minimal logic. This approach balances clarity, synthesis efficiency, and PPA improvements over the previous version.
