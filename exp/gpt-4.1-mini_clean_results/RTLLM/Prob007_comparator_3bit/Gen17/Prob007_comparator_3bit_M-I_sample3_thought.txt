To improve PPA (Power, Performance, Area) beyond the already efficient design, I will leverage Verilog's built-in comparison operators for simplicity and potentially better synthesis results. Using `>` , `==`, and `<` operators allows synthesis tools to generate minimal and optimized comparator logic without manually managing bitwise XNORs and combinational logic.

This approach drastically reduces code complexity, which can reduce area and power due to fewer intermediate signals and gates. It also improves readability and maintainability. Since the problem size is small (3 bits), synthesis tools can easily optimize these operators.

Performance is unaffected or improved because the synthesized comparator from built-in operators typically results in a minimum logic depth, equal to or better than the manually implemented one.

The outputs remain mutually exclusive by definition since exactly one of (A > B), (A == B), or (A < B) is true.

Thus, the improvement strategy is to replace manual combinational logic with Verilog built-in relational and equality operators for minimal and clean code, improving PPA metrics subtly for this small comparator.
