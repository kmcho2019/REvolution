A simpler approach is to leverage Verilog's built-in comparison operators directly, which are well supported by synthesis tools and produce clear, concise code. This eliminates the need for intermediate equality wires and explicit bitwise logic. Since the outputs must be mutually exclusive, assigning outputs based on relational operators (>, ==, <) directly ensures correctness and minimal logic. This also reduces gate count and wiring complexity while maintaining combinational logic and mutual exclusivity.
