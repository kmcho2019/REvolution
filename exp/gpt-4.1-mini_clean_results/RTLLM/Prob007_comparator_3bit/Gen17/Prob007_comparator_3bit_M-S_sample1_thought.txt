To simplify the 3-bit comparator, I will use Verilog's built-in comparison operators to directly derive the outputs. This removes intermediate equality signals and hierarchical bit-wise logic, yielding a concise and readable design. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction, as only one of (A > B), (A == B), or (A < B) can be true. This approach reduces code complexity and aids synthesis optimization without losing clarity or functionality.
