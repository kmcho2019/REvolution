To optimize the 3-bit comparator, fuse the clear, concise direct relational operator approach with logic sharing to avoid redundant evaluations and reduce potential synthesis area and power. First, check equality (A == B) which is cheap and common to all output decisions. If equal, set A_equal high; otherwise, determine A_greater or A_less by a single comparison (A > B) without separately evaluating (A < B). This ensures mutually exclusive outputs, minimizes logic duplication, and maintains combinational simplicity and readability.
