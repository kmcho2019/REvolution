To create a superior 3-bit comparator, we combine the clear hierarchical structure and minimal depth from both examples while leveraging the explicit per-bit equality detection from Example 1, which aids readability and synthesis optimization.

Fusion strategy:
- Use explicit per-bit equality signals via XNOR (~(A[i] ^ B[i])) for clear, granular equality detection.
- Define the overall equality as the AND of per-bit equalities.
- Implement the greater-than logic hierarchically with parallel ORs for each bit priority level, reusing the per-bit equality signals to maintain minimal logic levels and avoid nested ternary operators.
- Derive the less-than signal simply as the negation of both A_equal and A_greater, guaranteeing mutual exclusivity.
- Use minimal intermediate wires to keep the design clean and synthesis-friendly.
- This fusion ensures clear timing paths, low power due to mutual exclusivity and minimal toggling, and compact area with straightforward combinational logic.

The design is fully combinational, easy to understand, and synthesizes efficiently with balanced tradeoffs for performance, power, and area.
