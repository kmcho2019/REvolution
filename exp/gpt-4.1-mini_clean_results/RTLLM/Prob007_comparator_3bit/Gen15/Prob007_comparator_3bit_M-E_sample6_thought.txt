I will implement the 3-bit comparator using a direct priority encoding approach that leverages a case statement on the difference between A and B:

- Calculate the 4-bit signed difference: diff = {1'b0, A} - {1'b0, B} to handle 3-bit inputs properly.
- Based on the sign of diff:
  - If diff > 0, A > B.
  - If diff == 0, A == B.
  - Otherwise, A < B.
- Use combinational always block with case conditions derived from diff to assign outputs.
- This approach uses arithmetic subtraction, making the comparison concise and easy to understand.
- It ensures mutually exclusive outputs and minimal logic levels by synthesizing subtractor and sign check.
- The arithmetic subtraction is natural in hardware and synthesizes efficiently on FPGA/ASIC.
