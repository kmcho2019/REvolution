I will implement the 3-bit comparator using a subtraction-based approach without performing full arithmetic subtraction. The idea is to compute the difference bitwise from MSB to LSB:

- Compare bits from MSB to LSB.
- At the first bit where A and B differ, determine the result by comparing those bits directly.
- If all bits are equal, then A == B.
- Use priority encoding on bit comparisons to decide greater or less.
- This approach minimizes logic by exploiting the significance of bits and early decision, making outputs mutually exclusive by construction.
- The implementation will use combinational logic with conditional (ternary) operators to reflect the decision priority clearly.
