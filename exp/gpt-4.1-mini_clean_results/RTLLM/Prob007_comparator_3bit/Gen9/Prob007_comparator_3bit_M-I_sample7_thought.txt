Optimize the 3-bit comparator by implementing a priority-encoded bitwise comparison from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). Instead of performing three full comparisons independently, cascade comparisons of bits starting from the MSB, using combinational logic to determine outputs. This approach avoids redundant evaluations, reduces switching activity and logic duplication, thus improving power and area while maintaining zero delay. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction.
