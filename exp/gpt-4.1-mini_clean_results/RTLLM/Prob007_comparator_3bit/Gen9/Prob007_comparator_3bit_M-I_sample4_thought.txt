To optimize the 3-bit comparator for power and area, reduce redundant logic by cascading bitwise comparisons from the most significant bit (MSB) down to the least significant bit (LSB). Implement a priority-encoded comparator: compare bits starting from MSB; if they differ, determine output immediately without evaluating lower bits. This approach avoids evaluating all bits three times independently, reducing switching and gate count while preserving mutual exclusivity of outputs.
