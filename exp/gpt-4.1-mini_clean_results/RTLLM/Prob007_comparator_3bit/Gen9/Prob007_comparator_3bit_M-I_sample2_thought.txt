To optimize the existing 3-bit comparator for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will remove the unnecessary intermediate signal `eq_2_1` as it is only used once, and directly embed its logic into the greater-than expression. This reduces one extra wire and gate. Additionally, I will use a cleaner and fully consistent way to compute equality by combining all bitwise equalities with AND gates, as some synthesis tools handle these simpler ANDs better than a reduction XNOR comparison. This also improves readability. The less-than output remains the logical negation of greater and equal, ensuring mutual exclusivity without extra logic. The overall depth of combinational logic stays minimal, thus optimizing timing and minimizing toggling. This simplification slightly reduces area and power with no loss in clarity or correctness.
