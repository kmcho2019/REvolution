To simplify the 3-bit comparator, I will use direct comparison operators (>, ==, <) in a combinational always block or continuous assignments. This avoids manual bitwise equality and hierarchical checks, reducing the number of intermediate signals and making the code more concise and readable. Since the task is purely combinational, continuous assignments are sufficient. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by definition of the comparison operators. This approach leverages Verilog's built-in operators, allowing synthesis tools to optimize the logic efficiently without manual gate-level constructs.
