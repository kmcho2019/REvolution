I will implement a 3-bit comparator using a compact arithmetic subtraction approach combined with priority decoding of the subtraction's sign and zero flags. Specifically:

- Perform a combinational subtraction `diff = A - B`.
- Use the subtraction result to determine the relationship:
  - If `diff` is zero, then A equals B.
  - If the most significant bit of `diff` (sign bit) is 1, then A < B (negative result).
  - Otherwise, A > B (positive result).
- This approach uses a single subtraction operation and simple decoding of the result bits.
- The outputs are mutually exclusive by construction.
- This method is concise, maps well to hardware subtractor circuits, and results in efficient synthesis.

This design avoids explicit bitwise equality and hierarchical comparison by leveraging arithmetic properties, providing a novel and elegant architectural solution.
