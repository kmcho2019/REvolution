Implement the multiplying accumulator using an explicit pipeline stage to separate multiplication and accumulation, reducing the critical path. In this approach, at each clock cycle, multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' and register the product into an intermediate register. On the next clock cycle, accumulate this registered product into a 64-bit accumulator register. This 2-stage pipeline breaks the combinational path, allowing potentially higher clock frequencies. The reset is synchronous and clears both the intermediate product register and the accumulator. The output is assigned as the lower 32 bits of the accumulator. This design trades a 1-cycle latency for improved timing closure and predictable pipelining, useful in high-frequency designs.
