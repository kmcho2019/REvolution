Implement the multiplying accumulator using a two-stage pipeline to improve timing and throughput. The first stage registers the inputs and computes the 64-bit product, while the second stage accumulates this product into a 64-bit accumulator register. The reset is synchronous and only resets the accumulator register. The output is the lower 32 bits of the accumulator. This pipeline reduces the combinational path on the accumulation stage by separating multiplication and accumulation across clock cycles, allowing higher clock frequencies and better timing.
