Refactor the multiplying accumulator by separating the multiplication and addition into distinct steps with intermediate signals. Use a combinational multiplication followed by a registered accumulator updated on the clock's positive edge and synchronous reset. This keeps the pipeline clear: multiplication is done combinationally outside the clocked block, and only accumulation is done inside the always block. The output remains the lower 32 bits of the 64-bit accumulator. This approach clarifies the data flow and potentially helps synthesis tools optimize the multiplier separately.
