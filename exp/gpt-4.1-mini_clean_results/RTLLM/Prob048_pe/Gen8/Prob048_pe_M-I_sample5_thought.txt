To optimize the given 64-bit accumulator MAC_PE design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), we can introduce a few refinements:

1. **Synchronous Reset Only:** The original design uses synchronous reset, which is good for timing. We maintain this.

2. **Use a 48-bit Accumulator:** The maximum product of two 32-bit unsigned integers is 64 bits, but if the inputs are signed or the expected range is smaller, a 48-bit accumulator could be a reasonable compromise to reduce area and power, trading off some headroom. However, since the problem statement doesn't specify input range, we'll keep 64-bit accumulator for correctness.

3. **Registered Multiplier Input:** Splitting the multiplier and accumulator into pipelined stages reduces critical path and allows higher clock frequency. For this, we can introduce a pipeline register stage that stores the multiplication result and then add it to the accumulator in the next clock cycle.

4. **Pipeline the multiply-accumulate operation:** This reduces critical path by breaking the operation into two smaller stages: multiplication stage and accumulation stage.

5. **Output registered:** Register the output to improve timing.

6. **Add clock enable signal:** Although not requested, we can internally gate updates using the reset signal, since when reset is high, no update occurs, which saves power.

7. **Use non-blocking assignments:** Already done correctly.

Given the constraints, the most effective PPA improvement is pipelining the multiplier and accumulator into two stages:

- **Stage 1:** Multiply inputs and store product in a 64-bit register.
- **Stage 2:** Add product to accumulator.

This halves the critical path delay per clock cycle and allows higher clock frequency. Power might slightly increase due to extra registers but overall timing and area (due to smaller adders in each stage) improve.

We keep the output as the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, per spec.
