To optimize the original design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area):

1. Since output `c` is only 32-bit but the full accumulation requires 64 bits to avoid overflow, keep the 64-bit accumulator internally.
2. Reduce critical path delay by registering the product before accumulation (pipelining by one stage). This breaks the combinational path into two smaller parts: multiplication and accumulation, improving max frequency.
3. Use synchronous reset as before.
4. This introduces a one-cycle latency between inputs and output, usually acceptable in MAC designs.
5. The additional register for the product slightly increases area but significantly improves timing and reduces power by balancing pipeline stages.
6. The output remains the lower 32 bits of the accumulator.

This pipelined design achieves better timing closure and lower power by splitting heavy operations over two cycles without changing the functionality.
