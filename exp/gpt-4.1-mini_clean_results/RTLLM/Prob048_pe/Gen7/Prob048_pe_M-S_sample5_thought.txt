To simplify the multiplying accumulator (MAC_PE) while maintaining functionality, I will:

1. Use a 32-bit accumulator register as output width is 32 bits and no requirement was given for full precision accumulation. This reduces complexity and resource usage.

2. Perform 32x32 multiplication, but only accumulate the lower 32 bits of the product into the 32-bit accumulator. This may cause overflow but simplifies the design as per spec.

3. Use synchronous reset and update the accumulator on the clock's positive edge.

4. Use non-blocking assignments to ensure proper sequential behavior.

This simplified design fits minimal requirements with less area and power, trading off precision in accumulation (possible overflow ignored).
