Implement a multiplying accumulator using a 32-bit accumulator register that sums the lower 32 bits of the product each cycle, thus keeping the accumulator width consistent with the output width to save area. To handle the potential overflow in multiplication, the multiplier output is truncated to 32 bits before accumulation. The design uses asynchronous reset (active high) to clear the accumulator. Multiplication and accumulation occur combinationally in an always_ff block triggered by clk and rst, with reset dominating the logic. This approach offers a simpler hardware footprint and reduced area compared to 64-bit accumulation at the cost of potential overflow loss, suitable where exact full 64-bit precision is not mandatory.
