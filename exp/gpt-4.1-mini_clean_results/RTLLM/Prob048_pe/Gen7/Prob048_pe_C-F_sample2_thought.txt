Implement a 64-bit internal accumulator to safely store the full precision of multiple 32x32-bit products and avoid silent overflow. Use a synchronous reset (active high) for ease of synthesis and timing predictability. Separate the combinational multiplication from the sequential accumulation clearly by using a wire for the product and a register for the accumulator. Update the accumulator only on the positive clock edge. Output the lower 32 bits of the accumulator as the accumulated result to match the port size. This design cleanly balances precision, clarity, synthesis friendliness, and modularity, providing a robust foundation for future improvements such as pipelining or power gating.
