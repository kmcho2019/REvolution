The original design is functionally correct and uses a 64-bit internal accumulator to avoid overflow, which is ideal for accuracy. To optimize for better performance, power, and area (PPA), the following improvements are made:

1. Use a clock enable signal inside the module to gate accumulation, which can be externally controlled or tied to 1 if accumulation is always needed. This will reduce switching activity and dynamic power when accumulation is not needed.

2. Add a parameterized enable signal for the MAC operation allowing better power optimization if integrated into a larger system (in this code, hardcoded to 1 for simplicity).

3. Use a synchronous reset (already done) and non-blocking assignments (already done).

4. Keep 64-bit internal accumulator to maintain correctness and output truncated 32-bit sum as required.

5. Provide comments clarifying how to add clock enable easily for further power saving in system integration.

This approach preserves correctness and precision while allowing simple modifications for power savings without impacting area or critical path. The multiplier remains the critical resource and limiting factor, but power gating the accumulation register toggling can meaningfully reduce dynamic power consumption when accumulation pauses.

No complex pipelining is added here to keep the design straightforward and low latency as a basic building block.

Summary of improvements:
- Added clock enable for accumulation gating (fixed to 1 here for always-on but easy to modify).
- Clarified interface and internal comments for easy reuse and power optimization.
