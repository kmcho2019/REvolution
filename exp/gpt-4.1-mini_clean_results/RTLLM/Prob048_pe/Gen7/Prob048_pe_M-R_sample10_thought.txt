Refactor the design by separating the multiplication and accumulation into two always blocks to explicitly pipeline the operations and improve timing clarity. The multiplication of inputs 'a' and 'b' is performed combinationally and stored in an intermediate register. The accumulation then adds this stored product to the accumulator register on the clock edge. This decoupling clarifies the data flow and may allow easier optimization and timing improvement by synthesis tools. Reset remains synchronous and clears the accumulator. The output remains the lower 32 bits of the 64-bit accumulator.
