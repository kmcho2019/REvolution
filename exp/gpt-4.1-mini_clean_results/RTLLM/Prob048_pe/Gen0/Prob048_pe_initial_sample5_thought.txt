Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator that multiplies two 32-bit inputs and accumulates the result in a 32-bit register. On reset, the accumulator is cleared to zero. On each clock edge, if not reset, the accumulator adds the product of inputs a and b to its current value. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64 bits, but the accumulator output is 32 bits, we will only keep the lower 32 bits of the sum. The multiplication and accumulation are done synchronously.
