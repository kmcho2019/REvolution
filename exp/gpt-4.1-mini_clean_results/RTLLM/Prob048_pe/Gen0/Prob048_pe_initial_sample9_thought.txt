Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator module named `pe`. It uses a synchronous always block triggered on the positive edge of clk or rst. When rst is high, the accumulator register `c` is cleared to 0. Otherwise, on each clock cycle, it multiplies the 32-bit inputs `a` and `b` and accumulates the product into `c`. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers is up to 64 bits, and `c` is 32 bits, the accumulator will only keep the lower 32 bits of the sum, effectively allowing overflow. The output `c` is declared as a 32-bit register and outputs the current accumulated value.
