Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit register c holding the accumulated result. On reset (rst high), clear the accumulator to zero. On each clock rising edge when not reset, multiply inputs a and b (both 32 bits), accumulate the product into c. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64 bits, but c is 32 bits, the product will be truncated to 32 bits by taking the lower 32 bits of the product for accumulation. This aligns with the specification of c being 32 bits.
