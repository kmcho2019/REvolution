Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit accumulator register `c`. On each positive clock edge, if reset `rst` is asserted, clear `c` to zero. Otherwise, multiply inputs `a` and `b` (32-bit each) and add the result to `c`. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64 bits, use a 64-bit intermediate accumulator register internally to avoid overflow. The output `c` will be the lower 32 bits of the accumulator.
