Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator module named `pe`. The module will have synchronous reset and accumulate the product of inputs `a` and `b` into a 32-bit register `c`. On reset, the accumulator `c` is cleared. On each positive clock edge, if not in reset, multiply `a` and `b`, then add the product to `c`. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64-bit, we need to consider overflow and how to handle it. The requirement states the output `c` is 32-bit, so we will accumulate only the lower 32 bits of the product and the accumulation result (overflow beyond 32 bits will be truncated). This keeps the design simple and aligned with the problem statement.
