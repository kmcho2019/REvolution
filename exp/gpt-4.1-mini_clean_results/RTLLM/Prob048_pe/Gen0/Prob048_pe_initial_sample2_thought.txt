Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator (MAC) module named "pe" with 32-bit inputs a and b, and a 32-bit output c. The accumulator register c stores the intermediate sum of products. On the positive edge of clk or rst, if rst is high, c resets to 0; otherwise, c accumulates the product of a and b. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64 bits, truncate or handle overflow by keeping only the lower 32 bits of the sum. The module uses a single always block triggered on clk and rst for synchronous reset and accumulation.
