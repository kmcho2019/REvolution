Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator module named "pe". It uses a 32-bit register c to accumulate the product of two 32-bit inputs a and b on every positive clock edge when reset is inactive. On reset (active high), the accumulator is cleared to zero. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers can be up to 64-bit, but output c is specified as 32-bit, we will accumulate only the lower 32 bits of the product for simplicity. The multiplication and accumulation occur inside an always block triggered by posedge clk or posedge rst.
