Implement a synchronous multiplying accumulator that takes 32-bit inputs a and b, multiplies them, and accumulates the 64-bit product into a register. Since the product of two 32-bit numbers is 64-bit wide, and the accumulator should hold the full accumulation result without overflow, use a 64-bit register internally. The output c will be the lower 32 bits of the accumulator for simplicity (since the output port is 32-bit). The register resets to 0 on rst, and on each clk cycle adds a*b to the current value.
