Implement the MAC with a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk or rst. Use a 64-bit accumulator register to store the full accumulated product result. On reset (rst=1), clear the accumulator to zero. Otherwise, accumulate the product of inputs a and b to the accumulator. Output the lower 32 bits of the accumulator as c. This single-process style minimizes complexity, omits the separate combinational next-state logic, and still meets functional requirements.
