Implement a multiplying accumulator (MAC) with a 64-bit internal accumulator register to fully capture the accumulation result without overflow truncation. Instead of saturating on overflow, keep the full 64-bit precision internally.

Each cycle:
- Multiply inputs `a` and `b` (32-bit each), producing a 64-bit product.
- Add the product to a 64-bit accumulator register.

The output `c` exposes the lower 32 bits of the accumulator (the least significant part), reflecting partial sum modularly, consistent with common MAC behavior in fixed 32-bit output systems.

Reset is synchronous active-high, clearing the accumulator to zero.

This design avoids overflow complexity and saturation logic, providing true accumulation with full precision inside, while output truncation is explicit and deterministic.

This architecture enables potential extension for overflow detection or saturation externally and provides more flexible data integrity for downstream processing.

All operations are in a single always block triggered on clock and reset, and outputs are assigned combinationally from the register.

This approach simplifies the hardware and improves accuracy for large accumulations, at the cost of increased internal register width.
