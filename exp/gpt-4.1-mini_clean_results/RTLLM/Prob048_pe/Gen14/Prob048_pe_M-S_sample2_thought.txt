Implement a simple multiplying accumulator with synchronous reset and no overflow detection or saturation. Use a 64-bit internal register for accumulation to avoid overflow issues, but output only the lower 32 bits as specified. On each clock cycle, multiply inputs a and b, add the full 64-bit product to the 64-bit accumulator register, and output the lower 32 bits. Reset clears the 64-bit accumulator. This approach simplifies logic, avoids saturation handling, and still captures accumulation correctly, though output is truncated to 32 bits.
