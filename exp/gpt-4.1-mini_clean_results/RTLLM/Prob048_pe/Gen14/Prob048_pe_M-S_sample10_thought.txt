Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator (MAC) with a 32-bit register 'c' that holds the accumulated sum. Multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' each clock cycle and add the lower 32 bits of the product to 'c'. On reset, clear 'c' to zero. To simplify, do not handle overflow saturation or use 64-bit intermediates—allow wrap-around, which is common for simple MAC designs and reduces logic and area. Use a synchronous reset triggered on the clock's rising edge for clean timing. Assign output 'c' directly from the register.
