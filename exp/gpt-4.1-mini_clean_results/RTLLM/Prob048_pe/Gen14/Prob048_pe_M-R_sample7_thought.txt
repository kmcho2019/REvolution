Refactor the design by implementing the accumulator using a synchronous always block triggered by clk and rst but separating the multiplication into a registered pipeline stage to improve timing. First, register the inputs a and b at clock edge, then perform multiplication combinationally from registered inputs, and finally accumulate the result into a 64-bit register. This pipelined approach separates input sampling from multiplication and accumulation, which can help improve timing by shortening critical paths.
