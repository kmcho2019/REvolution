Implement a pipelined multiplying accumulator with a two-stage pipeline: one stage for multiplication and another for accumulation. Use 64-bit registers to store intermediate multiplication results and partial sums separately, and propagate them through pipeline registers synchronized by the clock. The reset is synchronous and clears all pipeline registers.

This approach splits the heavy multiplication and addition operations across two clock cycles, reducing combinational path delay and improving maximum clock frequency. The output c is the lower 32 bits of the accumulator register after accumulation. Although this adds one cycle latency, it allows higher throughput and better timing, suitable for high-frequency designs.

The design uses separate registers for the multiplier output and the accumulator, and an always block that first registers the multiplier output, then in the next stage accumulates it into the running sum. Reset clears both registers synchronously.
