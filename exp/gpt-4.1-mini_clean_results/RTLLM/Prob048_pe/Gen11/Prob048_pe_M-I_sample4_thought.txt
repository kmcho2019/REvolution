The original design is functionally correct but can be optimized for better timing and simpler logic. Key improvements:

- Remove the combinational always block driving output `c`. Instead, directly assign `c` as a registered output updated in the synchronous always block alongside the accumulator `acc`. This reduces combinational output logic, improving timing.

- Rename the internal accumulator register from `acc` to `c` to reflect the output register, eliminating the need for separate registers and continuous assignments.

- Since the problem states output `c` as 32-bit and overflow truncation is acceptable, keep the truncation approach but remove explicit saturation logic. This reduces the critical path complexity by removing the overflow detection and saturation multiplexer.

- Use synchronous active-high reset as specified, updating the register on positive edge of clk or rst (although in synthesis, reset is often synchronous; here we keep reset synchronous on clk with active high rst as per spec).

- This yields a simpler, single register holding the accumulated value `c`, updated every clock cycle by adding the truncated 32-bit product. The multiplier and adder remain but are streamlined.

This approach reduces logic levels, improves timing by cutting overflow detection logic, and simplifies the design. Power and area should be slightly reduced due to fewer gates and registers.
