I will implement a multiplying accumulator (MAC_PE) with a novel architectural approach by splitting the 64-bit accumulation into two 32-bit registers for high and low parts, using carry-save addition techniques to improve timing by minimizing long carry propagation in the accumulation stage. The multiplication is performed as a full 32x32-bit product (64-bit result), then added into the two-part accumulator (high and low registers) with explicit carry management. This approach may reduce critical path delays due to 64-bit wide addition in a single cycle.

Additionally, the output c will be driven by the concatenation of the low 32 bits from the accumulator (low register) to maintain a 32-bit output as requested.

This design retains synchronous reset and uses non-blocking assignments. The two-register accumulator enables potential pipelining or carry-lookahead optimization in a real implementation.

This architecture is different from a monolithic 64-bit accumulator and helps isolate and manage carry propagation more explicitly for possibly better timing behavior.
