Implement the 32-bit multiplying accumulator with a full 64-bit accumulation register internally to avoid overflow and loss of precision in the accumulation process. Each cycle, multiply inputs a and b to produce a 64-bit product, then add this product to the 64-bit accumulator register. The output c is assigned as the lower 32 bits of the accumulator register, effectively truncating the accumulated value on output.

The reset is synchronous and clears the entire 64-bit accumulator to zero. Multiplication and accumulation occur on the positive clock edge. The output c is a registered output updated every clock cycle, reflecting the truncated accumulator value.

This approach provides precise accumulation with no saturation or overflow in the accumulation itself, and preserves the complete product sum internally while exposing a 32-bit truncated output, suitable for applications requiring accurate summation but a limited output width.

It differs from the previous saturated 32-bit accumulator by maintaining a full 64-bit internal state, which removes saturation or wrap-around issues and maximizes numerical accuracy internally.

This is a straightforward architecture using a 64-bit accumulator register, simple control, and direct truncation on output, which should be easily synthesizable on modern FPGAs or ASICs with 64-bit wide registers.
