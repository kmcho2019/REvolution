Implement the multiplying accumulator with an asynchronous reset instead of synchronous reset, so the register is cleared immediately when reset is asserted, regardless of the clock. Use a 64-bit accumulator register to hold full precision of the 32x32 multiplication accumulation. Perform multiplication in a combinational always @* block, then accumulate in a sequential always @(posedge clk or posedge rst) block. This differs from the typical synchronous reset approach and allows instant reset response. Output the lower 32 bits of the accumulator as c. This style is common in some designs and offers fast reset clearing at the expense of asynchronous reset complexity. Use non-blocking assignments for register updates to avoid race conditions.
