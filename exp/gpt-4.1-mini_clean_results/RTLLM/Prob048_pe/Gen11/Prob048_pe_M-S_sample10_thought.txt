Simplify the design by using a single 32-bit accumulator register that directly accumulates the lower 32 bits of the product of inputs a and b. This reduces the accumulator width from 64 to 32 bits, lowering area and complexity. The multiplication and addition are done synchronously inside the always block. The reset remains synchronous and clears the accumulator. Note that this approach risks overflow and truncation but meets the specification for a 32-bit output and simpler implementation.
