Implement the multiplying accumulator using a dual-register approach to separate the multiplication and accumulation stages, creating a simple pipeline of two clock cycles. The first register stores the multiplication result of 'a' and 'b' as a 64-bit intermediate product. On the next clock cycle, this product is added to a 64-bit accumulator register. This two-stage pipeline breaks the critical path of doing multiplication and accumulation in one clock cycle, improving timing. On reset, both registers clear. The output 'c' continuously reflects the lower 32 bits of the accumulated sum. This approach trades latency (2 cycles) for better clock frequency and allows the multiplier and adder to operate on separate cycles, reducing combinational delay.
