Implement the multiplying accumulator with a 64-bit accumulator register to hold the full precision of repeated 32x32 multiplications without overflow or saturation.  
This approach accumulates the full 64-bit product in a 64-bit register, preventing loss of data from overflow during accumulation.  
The output port 'c' will provide the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, giving the current accumulated sum truncated to 32 bits.  
Use an asynchronous active-high reset that zeroes the 64-bit accumulator on reset.  
Perform all operations (multiplication and addition) in a synchronous always block triggered by the positive clock edge.  
This design trades off output precision (providing lower 32 bits only) for full internal accumulation to avoid overflow errors.  
It is simpler to synthesize and avoids saturation or overflow detection logic, which reduces combinational path complexity.  
If upper bits are needed later, the 64-bit accumulator can be easily accessed or extended.  
This design suits applications tolerant to truncated lower bits output or those that handle overflow externally.
