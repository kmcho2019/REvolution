Implement a straightforward 32-bit multiplying accumulator with synchronous active-high reset.  
- Multiply inputs `a` and `b` (unsigned or signed as 32-bit integers).  
- Accumulate the lower 32 bits of the 64-bit product into a 32-bit register `c`.  
- On reset, clear `c` to zero.  
- Use a single always block triggered by the clock and reset signals.  
- No saturation or overflow detection, allowing natural wrap-around behavior for simplicity.  
This reduces complexity by avoiding overflow logic and extended bit widths, fitting the basic MAC function.
