The previous design is functionally correct with saturation overflow and synchronous reset. To improve PPA, I will focus on these optimizations:

1. Remove the combinational always block for output `c`. Instead, drive `c` directly from the accumulator register, making `c` a registered output updated synchronously with `acc`. This simplifies the output path and reduces unnecessary combinational logic.

2. Integrate intermediate signals inside the always block to reduce wire usage, which can marginally reduce area.

3. Use synchronous reset only on `acc` and assign `c` directly as `acc`. This improves timing by removing unnecessary combinational drivers on output.

4. Maintain the saturation logic for robustness as required by the problem.

These changes aim to improve timing by removing unnecessary combinational output assignments and slightly reduce area and power by removing extra wires and logic without changing functional behavior.
