2. Compute the full 64-bit product of inputs `a` and `b` to avoid premature truncation.

3. Perform 33-bit addition (`acc` extended by 1 bit plus the lower 32 bits of the product) to detect overflow accurately.

4. On overflow, saturate the accumulator to 32'hFFFFFFFF to prevent wrap-around, ensuring robustness.

5. Use synchronous active-high reset for predictable timing and register initialization.

6. Use non-blocking assignments in the sequential block to follow RTL best practices.

7. Avoid output registers to reduce area and dynamic power and simplify timing, as output `c` can directly reflect the accumulator's value.
