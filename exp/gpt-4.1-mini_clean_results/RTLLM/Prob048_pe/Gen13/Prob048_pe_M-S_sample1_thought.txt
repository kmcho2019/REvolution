Implement a straightforward 32-bit multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit register to store the partial sum. On reset, clear the accumulator. On each clock cycle, multiply a and b (32x32 = 64-bit), then accumulate only the lower 32 bits of the product into the register, allowing wrap-around on overflow. Output c is the current accumulated value. This simple approach avoids saturation and additional overflow logic, minimizing complexity while meeting the basic MAC functionality.
