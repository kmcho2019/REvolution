Refactor the design by separating the multiplication and accumulation into two stages. Use a register to store the accumulator and a separate combinational wire for the product. In the always block triggered only by clk and rst, update the accumulator register by adding the product value. This keeps the multiplication combinational but accumulates synchronously.
