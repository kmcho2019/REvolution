Design a synchronous 32-bit multiplying accumulator module "pe" that uses an intermediate 64-bit accumulator register to avoid overflow loss during accumulation. The inputs a and b (32-bit each) are multiplied to produce a 64-bit product, which is added to the 64-bit accumulator register on each clock cycle. On reset, the accumulator clears to zero. The output c provides the lower 32 bits of the accumulator. This approach prevents losing upper product bits during accumulation and only truncates the final output, trading off a slightly larger internal register for correctness and accumulation of full product width.
