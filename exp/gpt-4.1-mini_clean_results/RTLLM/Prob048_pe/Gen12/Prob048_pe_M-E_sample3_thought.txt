Implement the MAC with a 64-bit accumulator register to avoid overflow and preserve full precision of the accumulated products. Inputs a and b are multiplied to produce a 64-bit product, which is then added to the 64-bit accumulator. The output c exposes the lower 32 bits of the accumulator to fit the specified output width. The accumulator is reset synchronously on rst signal. This approach eliminates the need for overflow detection and saturation since the wider accumulator prevents overflow in typical use cases, and also maintains all intermediate results for more accurate accumulation. The multiplication and addition occur combinationally each cycle, with register update on clock edge. This design improves precision and correctness at the expense of a wider register.
