Refactor the accumulating multiplication by separating the product calculation from the accumulation register update. Instead of calculating the sum with a wire and then assigning inside an always block, use a temporary register to store the product in a clocked process, then add it to the accumulator in another always block triggered on the clock. This two-stage process clarifies the operation and keeps the accumulator update synchronous to clk and rst. The accumulator still holds 32-bit results and only accumulates the lower 32 bits of the product.
