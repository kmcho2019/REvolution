Refactor the design by separating the multiplication and accumulation into two steps. Use a register for the accumulator updated on the clock edge, and compute the product as a continuous assign statement. The always block only updates the accumulator with the sum of its current value and the product. Reset is handled synchronously inside the clocked block (instead of asynchronously on rst). This removes the asynchronous reset, which is sometimes preferred for better timing and synthesis results. The product remains a 64-bit wire truncated to 32 bits for accumulation.
