Implement the multiplying accumulator with an asynchronous active-high reset to immediately clear the accumulator register. Use a 64-bit internal register to fully hold the 32x32 product accumulation to avoid overflow. Perform the multiplication and accumulation in a single always block triggered on the rising edge of the clock or the reset. Use blocking assignments for reset and non-blocking for normal operation to ensure deterministic behavior. Output the upper 32 bits of the 64-bit accumulator to reflect the higher order accumulation result, providing a different perspective on the accumulated value compared to outputting the lower 32 bits. This alternative output bit selection may suit applications focusing on higher-order accumulation. The design is kept straightforward for clarity and synthesis friendliness.
