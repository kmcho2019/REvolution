Fuse the best ideas by adopting a synchronous reset (simpler timing and synthesis) with explicit separation of combinational multiplier and sequential accumulation. Use a 64-bit accumulator to avoid overflow and preserve full precision of 32x32 products summed over time. 

To enhance timing and power efficiency, register the product before accumulation to break the critical path: use a pipeline stage that registers the multiplication result before it gets accumulated. This splits the long multiplier-plus-adder path into two shorter paths: multiplier to product register, then product register to accumulator register.

Output the lower 32 bits of the 64-bit accumulator as required.

This design gains:
- Improved timing by pipeline staging the product,
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic,
- Synchronous reset which is simpler for synthesis and timing,
- Full 64-bit accumulation to avoid overflow,
- Power benefits from breaking critical path and easier gating on pipeline registers,
- Maintains compatibility with the original port spec and behavior.

This fusion balances performance, power, and area while retaining correctness and clarity.
