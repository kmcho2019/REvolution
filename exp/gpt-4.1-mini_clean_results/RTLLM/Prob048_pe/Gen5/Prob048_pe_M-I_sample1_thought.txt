To improve PPA (performance, power, area), I will:

1. Extend the accumulator register to 64 bits to avoid data truncation and overflow from multiplication accumulation, improving correctness and result range.
2. Register the product output to break the long combinational path from a * b to c update, effectively pipelining the MAC operation. This can improve the max clock frequency (timing).
3. Use synchronous reset only on the clock's rising edge to simplify timing.
4. Provide the lower 32 bits of the 64-bit accumulator as output `c` to keep interface as specified.
5. This pipelined approach will increase latency by one cycle but enable higher clock frequency and reduce critical path delays.

This balances correctness (no overflow truncation), timing (pipelining), and interface requirements. Power and area are slightly increased due to 64-bit register and pipeline register, but gain timing and correctness overall.
