Implement a multiplying accumulator using an internal 64-bit register for accumulation to avoid overflow and preserve full precision of intermediate results. The inputs 'a' and 'b' are multiplied into a 64-bit product and added to a 64-bit accumulation register on every clock cycle. The output 'c' is assigned as the lower 32 bits of the accumulated 64-bit register, thus providing a clear separation between the internal accumulation and the truncated output. Reset is synchronous and clears the entire 64-bit accumulation register. This approach enables full precision accumulation while providing the 32-bit output interface, reduces the risk of overflow in accumulation, and makes the design easily extensible to output upper bits or overflow flags if needed.
