To improve the initial design for better performance and power, I will implement pipelining by splitting the multiplication and accumulation into two stages. This reduces the critical path: stage 1 performs the multiplication and registers its result; stage 2 adds this product to the accumulator. This reduces the longest combinational delay from multiplier + adder to multiplier or adder alone, increasing max frequency. Also, using asynchronous reset aligns with the original problem description ("posedge rst"). Power consumption can be improved by limiting switching in the adder stage since it only operates on registered inputs. The accumulator width remains 32 bits accepting overflow wrap-around. This pipelined design improves timing significantly while maintaining functionality.
