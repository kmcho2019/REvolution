Implement a 64-bit internal accumulator register to hold the full precision accumulation result of the 32x32 multiplication without overflow, and then truncate the lower 32 bits for output. Use asynchronous reset on the accumulator register for immediate clearing, and perform multiplication and accumulation synchronously on the rising clock edge. This approach preserves full accumulation precision internally, reducing overflow risk while outputting a 32-bit truncated result to meet interface requirements.
