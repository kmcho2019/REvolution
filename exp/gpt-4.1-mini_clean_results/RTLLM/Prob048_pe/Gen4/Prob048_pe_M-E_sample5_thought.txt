Implement the MAC_PE by separating the multiplier and accumulator with pipelining registers to improve timing and throughput. Use a 64-bit accumulator register to preserve full precision of the multiplication result (since 32x32 multiplication produces 64 bits). On each clock cycle, multiply inputs a and b, register the product, then add this registered product to the accumulator register. Reset asynchronously clears both the product register and the accumulator to zero. The output c is assigned from the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, representing the current accumulated value truncated to 32 bits. This pipelined approach reduces the critical path by splitting multiplication and addition into two stages.
