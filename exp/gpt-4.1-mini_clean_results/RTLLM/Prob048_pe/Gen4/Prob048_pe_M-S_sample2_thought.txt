To simplify the multiplying accumulator, we implement a straightforward design that uses a single 32-bit register `c` to accumulate the product results. On each clock cycle, `c` is updated by adding the 32-bit product of `a` and `b`. We use an always block sensitive to the positive edges of `clk` and `rst`. Upon reset, the accumulator clears to zero. This simpler design risks overflow because the 32-bit accumulator may not hold the full 64-bit product sum, but it meets the functional requirement of accumulating 32-bit results and maintaining a 32-bit output, with minimal logic and no intermediate wider registers.
