Implement a multiplying accumulator (MAC) using a 64-bit internal accumulator register to fully hold the intermediate results of the multiplication and accumulation without overflow or saturation.

Key points of this approach:

1. Use a 64-bit accumulator internally to store the full accumulation result, which prevents data loss from overflow and allows seamless accumulation of 32x32 bit multiplications.

2. Output only the lower 32 bits of the accumulator to match the required output port width.

3. Perform synchronous reset on the 64-bit register.

4. Multiplication and accumulation are done in a single clock cycle with non-blocking assignments.

5. This approach avoids saturation logic, trading off by providing the full precision in accumulation, useful in scenarios where overflow should not be masked but retained.

6. This architecture allows extending the output width or further processing the high 32 bits if needed later.

This design is simpler, more accurate in accumulation, and avoids saturation-related complexity, improving arithmetic correctness at the expense of output truncation. This tradeoff is often preferable in DSP and MAC units where full precision accumulation is critical.
