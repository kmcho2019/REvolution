Implement a 64-bit accumulator register to fully capture the 64-bit product of two 32-bit inputs without loss of precision. Use an internal 64-bit register 'acc' to accumulate the results of multiplication, and assign the lower 32 bits of the accumulator to the output 'c'. This approach prevents overflow and preserves the full accumulation internally. Reset and accumulation are synchronous on the positive clock edge. No saturation is implemented, as the internal width covers full multiplication range, and output truncates to 32 bits showing the least significant part of the accumulated sum. This also simplifies overflow handling and increases precision, suitable when the full accumulation is needed internally.
