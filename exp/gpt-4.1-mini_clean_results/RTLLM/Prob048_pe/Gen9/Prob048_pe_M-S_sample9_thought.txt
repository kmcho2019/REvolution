Simplify the design by using a 32-bit accumulator register that adds the product of the two 32-bit inputs directly each cycle. Although this truncates the accumulation and may cause overflow, it matches the requested 32-bit output width and reduces complexity. Use an asynchronous reset for immediate clearing of the accumulator as per the original specification (rst is active high). The multiplier output is directly added combinationally to the accumulator inside a single always block triggered by clk or rst. This simpler approach minimizes width and logic usage while maintaining the multiplying accumulator functionality.
