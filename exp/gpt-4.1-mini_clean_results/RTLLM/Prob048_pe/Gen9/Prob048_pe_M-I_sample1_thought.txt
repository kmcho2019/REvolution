To improve the design's PPA (Power, Performance, Area), focus on reducing the critical path and power consumption without changing functional behavior:

1. Reduce critical path:
   - Currently, multiplication is fully combinational in the same cycle, which can be a long delay.
   - Introduce pipelining by registering the inputs before multiplication to split multiplication delay from accumulation.
   - Register the product before accumulation so multiplication and accumulation are done in two stages.
   
2. Lower power:
   - With pipelining, clock gating can be easier to apply if needed later.
   - Smaller switching activity in each pipeline stage improves power.

3. Area:
   - The pipeline registers add some overhead, but it is minor compared to multiplier complexity.
   - No change in multiplier width or accumulator width to avoid loss of accuracy.

4. Maintain synchronous reset.

This two-stage pipeline breaks the long combinational path of multiply+add into two shorter clock cycles, enabling higher max frequency and potentially lower power.

Output remains the lower 32 bits of the accumulator register.

No asynchronous resets or reset in sensitivity list to ensure easier synthesis and timing.

Final design:
- Stage 1: register inputs a,b, compute product combinationally.
- Stage 2: accumulate the registered product into 64-bit acc.
