Refactor the multiplying accumulator to separate the multiplication and accumulation stages explicitly by using a registered pipeline stage for the product before the accumulation. This pipelining approach still maintains synchronous reset and accumulation but splits the multiply and accumulate operations into two sequential steps within the same clock cycle domain.

Advantages:
- The product is registered first, which can reduce the critical path delay since the multiplier output is stabilized before addition.
- The accumulator updates by adding the registered product, improving timing by shortening combinational paths.
- Reset remains synchronous.
- The internal accumulator is still 64-bit for full precision.
- Output remains the lower 32 bits of the accumulator.

This approach keeps the same functionality but changes the structure for potentially better timing and cleaner separation of operations.
