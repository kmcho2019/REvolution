The original design implements a 32-bit multiplying accumulator with saturation on overflow, which prevents wrap-around but may reduce precision and increase area/power slightly due to overflow detection logic.

To optimize for better PPA:
1. Remove overflow saturation logic to simplify addition and reduce area and power. This allows natural wrap-around arithmetic as is common in fixed-width accumulators.
2. Replace combinational output assignment (`always @(*) c = acc;`) with direct synchronous assignment of output `c` from the accumulator register. This simplifies the output stage and improves timing.
3. Keep reset synchronous and active high.
4. Use a 64-bit accumulator internally to accumulate the full product result without losing bits during accumulation, avoiding precision loss. Then output the lower 32 bits as `c`.
   - This improves accuracy without increasing output width.
   - The accumulator and multiplier use 64-bit width internally, which is typical for MACs.
5. By removing saturation logic and using a 64-bit accumulator internally, the design becomes both accurate and simpler.

This improves power and area by removing saturation logic, improves timing by direct synchronous output, and improves functionality by avoiding truncation of product accumulation. This is a common practical MAC implementation.
