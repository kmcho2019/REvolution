To improve PPA (Power, Performance, Area), the main bottleneck is the large combinational path through the 32x32 multiplier followed immediately by a 33-bit adder before the register update. This limits max clock frequency (negative WNS) and increases power and area.

Improvement strategy:
1. Pipeline the MAC operation by introducing a register stage for the multiplication product. This breaks the critical path into two shorter stages: multiply, then add+accumulate.
2. Use synchronous reset as before.
3. Perform saturation logic in the accumulation stage.
4. Keep the accumulator register width at 32 bits and saturation to 32'hFFFFFFFF.
5. This pipeline adds one cycle latency but allows higher clock frequency and lower dynamic power per cycle.
6. Output `c` reflects accumulator with 1 cycle delay, acceptable for typical designs.

This approach balances throughput and clock frequency, reduces critical path delay, and improves timing slack and power consumption at a modest area cost due to the added register.
