Implement the multiplying accumulator using a 64-bit register to hold the accumulation result internally to avoid overflow during accumulation, since the product of two 32-bit numbers is up to 64 bits. On reset, clear the 64-bit accumulator register. On each clock cycle, multiply inputs 'a' and 'b' (unsigned 32-bit) to get a 64-bit product and add it to the 64-bit accumulator. The output 'c' will be assigned as the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, reflecting the accumulated sum truncated to 32 bits. This avoids saturation logic and allows full precision accumulation internally, while outputting 32 bits for the interface.

This approach:
- Uses a 64-bit register for internal accumulation to prevent overflow.
- Keeps 'c' as a 32-bit output representing the least significant part of the accumulator.
- Uses synchronous reset.
- Does not implement saturation, simplifying design.
- Multiplier and adder are combinational within the clocked always block.
- Provides straightforward and efficient accumulation with full precision internally.
