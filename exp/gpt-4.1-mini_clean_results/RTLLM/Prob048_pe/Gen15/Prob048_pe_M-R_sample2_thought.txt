Refactor the MAC PE by splitting the multiplier and accumulator into separate always blocks to clearly separate the pipelined multiplication from accumulation. Use an internal 64-bit register to hold the product registered at each clock, then accumulate it into a 64-bit accumulator register to avoid losing bits. Output `c` is then the lower 32 bits of the accumulator. Reset is synchronous and clears both product and accumulator registers. This separates combinational multiplication from sequential accumulation, simplifies timing paths, and preserves functionality without saturation. It also uses non-blocking assignments exclusively in clocked always blocks for proper synthesis and timing.
