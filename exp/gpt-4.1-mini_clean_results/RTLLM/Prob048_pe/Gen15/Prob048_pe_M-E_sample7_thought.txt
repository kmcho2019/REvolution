Implement the multiplying accumulator using a 64-bit accumulator register to fully capture the 32x32-bit multiplication result without overflow or truncation.

On each clock cycle, multiply the 32-bit inputs to get a 64-bit product and add it to the 64-bit accumulator register. Output the lower 32 bits of the accumulator as the accumulated value.

Reset is synchronous and clears the accumulator to zero.

This approach ensures no overflow or saturation occurs internally, preserves full precision of accumulation, and only truncates output to 32 bits as required.

Non-blocking assignments in a clocked always block ensure correct sequential behavior and timing.

This design trades a wider accumulator register for accurate accumulation over time without loss or saturation logic, simplifying overflow handling and improving correctness at some area cost.
