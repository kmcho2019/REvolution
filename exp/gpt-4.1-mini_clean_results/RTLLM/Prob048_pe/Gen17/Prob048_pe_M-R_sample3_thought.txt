Refactor the design by separating the multiplication and accumulation stages with a combinational block calculating the next accumulator value and a sequential block updating the register. Use a wire for the next value of 'c', computed by adding the lower 32 bits of the product to the current accumulator, and applying saturation if overflow occurs. This restructuring:
- Moves arithmetic to combinational logic outside always block,
- Makes overflow check and saturation explicit in a combinational assignment,
- Keeps sequential logic minimal with only the register update on clock,
- Potentially helps synthesis tools optimize datapath and timing,
- Maintains the same functionality with clear separation of concerns.
