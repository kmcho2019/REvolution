Refactor by splitting multiplication and accumulation stages: 
1. Perform multiplication in a separate combinational always block with inputs `a` and `b`.
2. Store the product in a registered 64-bit intermediate register.
3. Accumulate the lower 32 bits of the registered product into the 32-bit accumulator register.
4. Detect overflow by extending the sum with a carry bit.
5. Perform accumulation and saturation logic only in the accumulator always block.
6. Use two sequential blocks to pipeline multiplication and accumulation, which also simplifies timing.
7. Reset remains synchronous and resets both intermediate product register and accumulator.
8. This approach improves timing by isolating multiplication from accumulation and structures code with registers to potentially increase frequency.
