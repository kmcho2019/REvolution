Implement a straightforward 32-bit multiplying accumulator without saturation logic or extra overflow detection. Use a 64-bit internal register to store the accumulation result to avoid overflow from repeated 32x32 multiplications. On reset, clear the accumulator; otherwise, accumulate the full 64-bit product each cycle. The output port c will provide the lower 32 bits of the accumulator for simplicity, as requested. This reduces complexity by avoiding saturation and overflow logic, while maintaining accurate accumulation of 32-bit multiplications.
