Implement the multiplying accumulator with a 64-bit accumulator register to hold the full precision result of multiple 32x32 multiplications without overflow. This removes the need for saturation and preserves precision over many cycles. The 64-bit accumulator is updated synchronously on the clock's rising edge or reset, resetting to zero when rst is asserted. The output port 'c' provides the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, representing the accumulated sum's least significant portion. This approach maintains full precision internally and outputs a 32-bit truncated result, offering higher accuracy and flexibility for further scaling or overflow detection outside this module.
