Implement a simple 32-bit MAC unit that accumulates the full 64-bit product internally but outputs only the lower 32 bits, without overflow detection or saturation. Use a 64-bit register to hold the accumulated sum. On reset, clear the accumulator. On each clock cycle, multiply inputs a and b and add to accumulator. Output lower 32 bits of the accumulator as c. This reduces complexity by removing saturation and overflow logic, while still maintaining correct accumulation of the products.
