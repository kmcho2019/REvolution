Simplify the MAC design by directly accumulating the full 64-bit product into a 64-bit register, but output only the lower 32 bits as the accumulated result. Use a synchronous reset and a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk or rst. This removes saturation and overflow checks, reducing complexity and area, while maintaining basic MAC functionality. The output is always the lower 32 bits of the accumulator. This approach is straightforward and suitable when truncation overflow is acceptable.
