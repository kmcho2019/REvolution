Implement the multiplying accumulator using a 64-bit accumulator to capture the full precision of the 32x32 multiplication result without truncation or overflow. This avoids saturation or wrap-around issues and preserves full precision of accumulation over multiple cycles.

- Use a 64-bit register `acc` to hold the accumulated 64-bit result.
- Multiply inputs `a` and `b` to produce a 64-bit product.
- On each clock cycle (except reset), add the 64-bit product to `acc`.
- On reset (synchronous, active high), clear `acc` to zero.
- Output `c` is the lower 32 bits of the accumulator to conform to the output port width.
- This approach prioritizes arithmetic precision by using wider accumulation, trading off output width limitation.
