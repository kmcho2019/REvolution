Implement a simple synchronous multiplying accumulator with a 32-bit accumulator register c. On each clock cycle, if reset is asserted, clear c to zero; otherwise, multiply inputs a and b (32-bit each), then add the full 64-bit product lower 32 bits to c, allowing wrap-around on overflow (no saturation). Output c is registered and updated synchronously. This keeps the design minimal and meets the specification without overflow detection or saturation, reducing logic complexity.
