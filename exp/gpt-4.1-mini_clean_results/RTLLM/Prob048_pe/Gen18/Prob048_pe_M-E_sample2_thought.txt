Implement a 32-bit multiplying accumulator with a wider internal accumulator to avoid frequent overflow and keep full precision over multiple cycles. 

Specifically:

1. Use a 64-bit internal accumulator register `acc` that accumulates the full 64-bit product of inputs `a` and `b`, so no truncation or saturation is needed internally.

2. The output port `c` provides the lower 32 bits of the accumulator, reflecting the least significant part of the accumulated sum.

3. The accumulator is updated synchronously on the rising edge of `clk` or asynchronously reset to zero on `rst` high.

4. No saturation logic is implemented, as the 64-bit accumulator can accumulate large sums without overflow; the user can handle higher bits if needed.

5. Multiplication and addition are done combinationally within an always block, and non-blocking assignments are used for best practice.

This design supports full-precision accumulation of 32x32 products in a 64-bit register, avoiding saturation and overflow complexity, and exposing the lower 32 bits as output, useful for fixed-point or modular arithmetic. The asynchronous reset makes the design responsive to resets immediately.

This design trades off more register width internally to avoid complex overflow and saturation logic, and simplifies the control path.
