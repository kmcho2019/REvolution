Implement the barrel shifter using explicit `mux2X1` submodules as specified, organizing the design into three sequential stages that rotate the bits by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. Each stage is composed of eight `mux2X1` multiplexers, each selecting between a shifted bit and the original bit from the previous stage, controlled by the corresponding bit in `ctrl`.

This modular approach realizes the barrel shifter as a cascade of three layers of multiplexers:
- Stage 0 rotates by 4 bits if `ctrl[2]` is set.
- Stage 1 rotates by 2 bits if `ctrl[1]` is set.
- Stage 2 rotates by 1 bit if `ctrl[0]` is set.

Each mux2X1 is parameterized for bit selection and controlled directly by `ctrl` bits, providing a fully structural, clear, and scalable design aligned with the problem's requirements.
