Simplify the barrel shifter by directly computing the final rotated output based on the combined shift amount from the 3-bit control signal. Since each bit in `ctrl` corresponds to shifts of 1, 2, and 4 positions, the total shift equals the sum of these. Use a single stage rotate-left by this total shift amount, implemented with a simple index wrapping using modulo 8. This eliminates multiple stages and the need for intermediate wires or mux modules, while preserving the exact rotation behavior.
