Implement the 8-bit barrel shifter using three stages corresponding to shifting by 4, 2, and 1 positions. Each stage uses eight instances of a simple 2-to-1 mux module (mux2X1) that selects between the input bit and the bit shifted by the stage's amount, based on the control bit. This structural approach directly reflects the specified design, is clear, modular, and maintains the intended rotate-left behavior with explicit mux2X1 usage.
