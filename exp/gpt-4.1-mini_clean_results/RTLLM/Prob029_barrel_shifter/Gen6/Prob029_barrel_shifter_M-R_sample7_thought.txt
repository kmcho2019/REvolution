Refactor the barrel shifter by eliminating the explicit 2-to-1 mux submodule and instead using conditional continuous assignments (`assign`) to select between the original bit and the shifted bit at each stage. This reduces module overhead and simplifies the code structure. We maintain the three-stage shift (by 4, 2, and 1 positions) controlled by bits of `ctrl`, but implement the multiplexer behavior directly using ternary operators. This approach keeps the logic purely combinational and leverages vector slicing and modular indexing cleanly within `assign` statements, improving readability and synthesis friendliness.
