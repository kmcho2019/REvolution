To simplify the barrel shifter, I will eliminate the use of the separate `mux2X1` module and instead directly implement the multiplexing using conditional (ternary) operators inside always_comb blocks or continuous assignments. This reduces module instantiations and make the code more concise and self-contained while preserving the staged shift-by-4, shift-by-2, and shift-by-1 rotation controlled by each bit of `ctrl`.
