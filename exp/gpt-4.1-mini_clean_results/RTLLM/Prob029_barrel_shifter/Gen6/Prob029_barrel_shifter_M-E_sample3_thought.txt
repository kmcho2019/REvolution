Implement the barrel shifter as a pipeline of three stages, each performing a conditional rotate-by-N operation using mux2X1 units. Each stage corresponds to one bit of `ctrl` and shifts by 4, 2, or 1 positions respectively. At each stage, for each bit of the intermediate data, a 2-to-1 mux selects either the original bit or the bit shifted by that stage's amount, depending on the control bit for that stage. This precisely models the staged shifting architecture and uses mux2X1 modules explicitly. The 8-bit width and rotation semantics mean the shifted bit index wraps around modulo 8. This approach realizes the barrel shifter exactly as specified, with clear modularity and correctness.
