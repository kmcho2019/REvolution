To strictly follow the problem statement and use the `mux2X1` submodule for each bit at every stage, I will implement each of the three shift stages explicitly using arrays of `mux2X1` instances. Each stage will perform a rotation by 4, 2, or 1 bits respectively, controlled by `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]`. For each bit, the `mux2X1` selects between the original bit and the rotated bit depending on the control bit for that stage. This bitwise mux approach exactly matches the design intent and ensures modularity and clarity. The stages will be connected sequentially: output of stage 1 is input to stage 2, output of stage 2 is input to stage 3, and final output is from stage 3.

This approach also allows potential optimization or mapping onto FPGA resources that prefer fine-grained multiplexers, preserving the architecture's intended pipelining and incremental shift capability.
