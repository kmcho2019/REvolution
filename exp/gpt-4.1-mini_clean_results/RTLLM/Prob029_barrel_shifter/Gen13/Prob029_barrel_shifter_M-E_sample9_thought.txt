This design implements the barrel shifter as a three-level hierarchy of explicit 2-to-1 multiplexers, each instantiated as a separate `mux2X1` module to match the problem statement precisely.

At each stage, an 8-bit vector is constructed bitwise by choosing either the current input bit or the bit shifted by 4, 2, or 1 positions, depending on the control bit for that stage. This uses explicit mux2X1 instances (eight per stage) for maximum clarity and modularity.

The data flows through three sequential stages (shift by 4, then by 2, then by 1), each controlled by one bit of the 3-bit control input. Each mux chooses between the bit before shifting and the bit after shifting at that stage, enabling a true hardware barrel shifter composed of explicit muxes, as requested.

This structure helps to visualize and potentially optimize timing in synthesis tools by mapping closely to hardware multiplexers, facilitating fine-grained control over logic and potentially better PPA characteristics in ASIC or FPGA implementations.
