The primary issue causing the failure is the bit-indexing expressions used to select rotated bits in each stage. Using `(i + shift) & 3'b111` mixes integer `i` and a 3-bit vector mask `3'b111` (binary 111) directly, which may cause indexing or synthesis problems due to width mismatches or tool interpretation. Instead, using an integer constant mask `7` or `3'd7` ensures proper arithmetic and indexing.

Since indexing requires an integer, changing `(i + shift) & 3'b111` to `(i + shift) & 7` provides a correct modulo 8 operation.

This correction preserves the intended rotate functionality (rotation by 4, 2, or 1 bit depending on ctrl bits) while resolving functional indexing issues. No other parts of the design need change.

Additionally, comments and formatting are kept clean for clarity, and the mux module is renamed to avoid conflicts.
