Implement the barrel shifter as a pipeline of three stages corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively, each stage realized explicitly by an array of 2-to-1 multiplexers (mux2X1). Each mux2X1 selects between the input bit and the input bit shifted by the stage amount, controlled by the appropriate ctrl bit. This modular approach uses the mux2X1 primitive repeatedly, making the design highly structural and explicit while maintaining clarity and scalability. The data flows through the stages sequentially, and final output is assigned after the last stage. This also satisfies the problem requirement of using mux2X1 submodules at each stage.
