Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter that rotates bits to the left by a variable amount specified by a 3-bit control signal. The control bits indicate shifts by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. Each stage uses a mux2X1 submodule to select between the current bit and the bit shifted by the stage's amount. The shifting is performed in stages, with the output of one stage feeding the input of the next.
