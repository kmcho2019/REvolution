Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter with 3 stages, shifting by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. Each stage uses 8 instances of a 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) that chooses between the original bit and the bit shifted by the current stage amount. The shifting is done in a rotate-left manner, wrapping bits around. The input is passed through these stages sequentially based on the ctrl bits, producing the final rotated output.
