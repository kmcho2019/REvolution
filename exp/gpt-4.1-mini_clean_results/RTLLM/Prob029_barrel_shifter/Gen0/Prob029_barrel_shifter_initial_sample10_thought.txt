Implement the barrel shifter as a sequence of three stages corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively. At each stage, use a vector of mux2X1 modules to select between the shifted version and the input to that stage, based on the corresponding control bit. The mux2X1 module is defined to select one bit out of two inputs based on a single-bit select signal. The shifts are implemented as rotate left operations, so bits rotated out from MSB come back at LSB. The output of each stage becomes the input for the next stage, and the final output is assigned to the output port.
