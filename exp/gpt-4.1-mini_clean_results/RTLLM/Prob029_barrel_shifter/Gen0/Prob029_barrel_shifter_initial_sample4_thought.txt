Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter that rotates bits right based on the 3-bit control signal. The control signal bits correspond to shifting by 4, 2, and 1 positions respectively, using three stages of shifting. Each stage uses 8 mux2X1 modules selecting between the current bit and the bit shifted by that stage amount, effectively combining shifts for any ctrl value. The mux2X1 submodule selects one of two inputs based on sel.
