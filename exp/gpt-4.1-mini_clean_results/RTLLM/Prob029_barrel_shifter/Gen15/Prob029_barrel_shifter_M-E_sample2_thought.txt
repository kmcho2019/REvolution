Implement the 8-bit barrel shifter as a cascade of conditional rotations, each controlled by one bit of the 3-bit control signal `ctrl`. Instead of using many mux2X1 submodules for each bit, use vector-level conditional operators to perform rotate-left operations by fixed amounts (4, 2, 1). The final output is computed by successively rotating the intermediate values only if the respective control bit is set. This approach is clean, compact, avoids repeated submodule instantiations, and is easy to synthesize as combinational logic. It preserves the stage-wise control logic by applying the rotations in the order ctrl[2] (4-bit rotate), ctrl[1] (2-bit rotate), then ctrl[0] (1-bit rotate).
