Implement the barrel shifter using three separate 8-bit 2-to-1 multiplexers arrays for each shift stage (4, 2, and 1 positions). Instead of wiring all muxes individually, define a parameterized mux2X1 module that selects between original and shifted bits. For each stage, use a generate loop to instantiate mux arrays selecting either the input from the previous stage or the input shifted by that stage's amount, controlled by the corresponding ctrl bit. Cascade the stages to achieve the final rotated output. This modular, pipeline-like design strictly follows the staged mux selection methodology and clearly shows how each control bit affects the shift stepwise.
