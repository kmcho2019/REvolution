Refactor the barrel shifter to use purely combinational `assign` statements for each stage of shifting and selection without using the separate `mux2X1` module. Instead, implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer logic directly with ternary conditional operators within the assignments. This reduces module dependency, prevents module duplication issues, and simplifies the design by keeping everything in one module. The shifting still happens in three stages corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 bits controlled by `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]`. Each stage conditionally rotates the bits by selecting either the original bit or the shifted bit.
