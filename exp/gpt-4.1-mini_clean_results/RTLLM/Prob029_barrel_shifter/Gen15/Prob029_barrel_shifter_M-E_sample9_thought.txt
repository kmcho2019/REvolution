Implement the barrel shifter by explicitly instantiating a `mux2X1` submodule array for each shift stage (4, 2, and 1 positions). Each stage consists of eight 2-to-1 multiplexers selecting between the original bit and the bit rotated by the corresponding amount, controlled by the appropriate control bit. This approach models the staged rotation hardware with fine granularity, reflecting the multiplexers described in the problem. The final output is the result of all three cascaded shift stages.
