Refactor the barrel shifter by eliminating the explicit mux2X1 submodule and instead use continuous assign statements with the conditional operator to perform each stage of shifting. This approach simplifies the design by directly implementing the multiplexing behavior with assign statements for each bit at every shift stage. The stages remain the same — shifting by 4, then 2, then 1 — controlled by the respective bits of `ctrl`. The use of modular generate loops is kept to maintain clarity and avoid code repetition, but no separate mux module is used. This method reduces module proliferation and potential duplication issues while preserving the structural clarity of a barrel shifter.
