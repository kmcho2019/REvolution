Simplify the barrel shifter by using continuous assignments and bit slicing instead of explicit mux2X1 instances. Since the barrel shifter is purely combinational and rotating bits, use bit rotations implemented with concatenation and indexing. Shift amounts are controlled by bits in `ctrl`, applied sequentially: first by 4, then 2, then 1. This approach removes the need for a separate mux module and generate blocks, reducing complexity while maintaining the same functionality.
