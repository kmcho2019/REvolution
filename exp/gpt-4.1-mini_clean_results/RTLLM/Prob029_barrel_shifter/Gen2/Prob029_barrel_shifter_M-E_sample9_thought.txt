Implement a barrel shifter by explicitly instantiating mux2X1 modules for each bit and for each shift stage (4, 2, and 1). Each mux2X1 selects between the bit staying in place or the bit rotated from the shifted position. The design rotates bits to the right by the amount specified in the ctrl signal, with each stage applying a rotation of 4, then 2, then 1 positions if the corresponding ctrl bit is set. Using generate loops, the design instantiates mux2X1s in a structural and scalable way, maintaining the problem's requirement to use mux2X1 modules. The intermediate stages are chained, passing the result of one stage as the input to the next.
