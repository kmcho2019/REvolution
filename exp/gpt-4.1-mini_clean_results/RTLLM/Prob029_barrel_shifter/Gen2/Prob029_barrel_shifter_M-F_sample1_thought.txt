To fully meet the problem requirements, the design must implement an 8-bit rotating barrel shifter with rotation to the right, using the `mux2X1` submodule to select between shifted and original bits at each stage. Each stage performs a conditional rotation by 4, 2, or 1 bit based on `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]` respectively. The rotation direction is right-rotation, which means bits rotated out of the right end appear at the left. Using `mux2X1` modules explicitly for each bit at every stage ensures structural clarity and testbench compatibility. The design chains the stages, passing intermediate results to the next. This approach strictly follows the requirements, and the modular mux2X1 reuse helps with area and power optimization.
