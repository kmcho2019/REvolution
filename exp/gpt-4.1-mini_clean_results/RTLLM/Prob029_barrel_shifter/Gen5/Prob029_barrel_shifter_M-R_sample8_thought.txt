To avoid redeclaring the mux2X1 module and reduce complexity, I will refactor the barrel shifter to use purely combinational logic with continuous assignment statements.

Each stage will perform a conditional rotate-right shift by 4, 2, or 1 bits, controlled by the corresponding bits in `ctrl`.

Instead of instantiating mux2X1 modules explicitly, I will use conditional operator `?:` inside a generate-for loop for each bit, selecting between the original bit and the rotated bit at each stage.

The data will flow through three intermediate wires (stage1, stage2), and the final output `out` is assigned similarly.

This approach retains the same logic and functionality but avoids the mux2X1 module entirely, thus eliminating naming conflicts and making the code simpler and more straightforward.
