Instead of cascading stages with individual mux2X1 for each bit and each shift amount, implement the barrel shifter by directly computing the rotated result using bitwise concatenation and shifting based on the control signals. This can be achieved by combining the three shift amounts (4, 2, 1) into a single shift count and then performing a single rotate operation with a combinational expression. This reduces complexity and avoids multiple stages of muxes. The rotation can be done by concatenating `in` twice and selecting an 8-bit slice starting at the shift count, implementing a rotation. This approach is efficient, simple, and easy to understand while meeting the functional requirements.
