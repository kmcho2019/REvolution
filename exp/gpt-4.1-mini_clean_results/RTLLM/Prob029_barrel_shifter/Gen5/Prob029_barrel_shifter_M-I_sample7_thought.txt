The original design is functionally correct and straightforward, using a series of 2-to-1 multiplexers to implement a 3-stage barrel shifter with rotate-right behavior controlled by the bits of `ctrl`.

To improve PPA (performance, power, and area), I will:

1. Inline the mux2X1 functionality directly inside the generate blocks. Since `mux2X1` is a simple 2-to-1 mux, explicit instantiation of many small modules adds synthesis overhead and could increase area and delay. Writing the mux function inline allows the synthesizer to optimize better.

2. Use bit slicing and concatenation to implement rotations compactly, replacing the explicit indexing with modular arithmetic by wiring. This can also help synthesis tools with cleaner logic expressions.

3. Retain the staged shifting structure, which maps well to hardware for timing and power efficiency.

The functionality remains unchanged: the input is rotated right by the sum of the positions indicated by ctrl bits (4, 2, 1). 

This approach simplifies the code and may improve synthesis results by reducing module overhead, while preserving clarity and easy verification.
