Instead of using three sequential stages of 2-to-1 multiplexers, this design will implement the barrel shifter using a single combinational logic block with a parameterized rotation. The shift amount is directly calculated from the control signals (ctrl[2:0]) as a 3-bit value representing the rotate distance (0 to 7). The output is formed by concatenating slices of the input vector rotated by this amount. This approach avoids the step-by-step mux chain and uses bit slicing and concatenation for a clean, efficient, and easily synthesizable barrel rotation.
