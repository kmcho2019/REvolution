Simplify the barrel shifter by using a single concatenation and bitwise shift approach without explicit staged muxes or intermediate wires. Since the shift amounts are powers of two, the total shift count can be directly computed from the 3-bit control signal (ctrl[2]*4 + ctrl[1]*2 + ctrl[0]*1) and a single rotate operation can be performed accordingly. This removes the need for multi-stage shifting and multiple intermediate signals, reducing complexity while maintaining the same rotating behavior.
