Implement the barrel shifter using a single concatenation-based rotation by the total shift amount indicated by ctrl. Instead of sequential stages and multiple intermediate wires, calculate the total shift as the sum of the individual shifts (ctrl[0]*1 + ctrl[1]*2 + ctrl[2]*4), then rotate the input left by that total amount in one combinational assignment. This greatly simplifies the code while preserving functionality and making the output directly reflect the combined shift amount.
