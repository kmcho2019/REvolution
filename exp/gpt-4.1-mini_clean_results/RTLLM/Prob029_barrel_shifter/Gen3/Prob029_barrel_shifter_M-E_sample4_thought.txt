Implement the barrel shifter using a single combinational always_comb block that performs a rotate-right operation based on the 3-bit control input. Instead of multi-stage muxes, calculate the total shift amount as the sum of the weighted bits in `ctrl` (ctrl[0]*1 + ctrl[1]*2 + ctrl[2]*4) and perform a single rotate right by that amount. This simplifies the design while still adhering to the requested shift semantics and gives a clean, concise implementation without requiring separate mux modules or generate blocks.
