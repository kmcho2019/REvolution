The original design is structurally correct and meets the functional requirements using mux2X1 instances at each stage for an 8-bit barrel shifter with rotate-left behavior. However, the explicit instantiation of mux2X1 for every bit at every stage can lead to a relatively large number of mux instances and hence larger area and potential critical path delays.

To optimize for better power, performance, and area (PPA):

1. Replace the explicit mux2X1 submodule instances with conditional operators (`?:`) inside generate loops. This avoids extra module instantiation overhead and allows synthesis tools to implement the multiplexers efficiently as direct logic rather than module boundaries.

2. Use a single always_comb (or continuous assignment) block per stage to express the rotation with conditional operators, improving readability and synthesizability.

3. Maintain the same structural approach with three stages (shift by 4, 2, 1 bits) and rotate-left behavior with wrap-around.

This approach maintains clear staged shifting controlled by the respective control bits and reduces the overhead of many instantiated submodules, improving the design's simplicity and synthesis results.

The improved code follows this strategy, replacing mux2X1 instantiations with direct ternary operators in generate loops.
