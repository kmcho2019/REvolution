We will implement the barrel shifter as a sequence of three stages of 2-to-1 multiplexers (`mux2X1`), each stage corresponding to a shift by 4, 2, and 1 bit respectively. Each bit of the output at a stage selects between the unshifted input of that stage and the input rotated left by the stage's shift amount, controlled by the corresponding control bit. This modular mux-based approach aligns with the problem statement and simplifies the logic without complex modulo expressions, as indices are computed with wrapping using simple conditional expressions.
