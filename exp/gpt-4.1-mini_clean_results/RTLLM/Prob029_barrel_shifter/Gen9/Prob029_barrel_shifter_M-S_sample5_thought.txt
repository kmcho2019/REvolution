The barrel shifter functionality can be implemented more simply by using a single left rotate operation controlled directly by the sum of shift amounts indicated by the bits in `ctrl`. Since each bit of `ctrl` represents a shift amount (1, 2, or 4), the total shift is `ctrl[0]*1 + ctrl[1]*2 + ctrl[2]*4`.

Using a single continuous assignment with a parameterized rotate left operation greatly simplifies the design, removing the need for multiple mux stages. This retains the exact rotate-left behavior and matches the original functionality with less complexity and cleaner code.
