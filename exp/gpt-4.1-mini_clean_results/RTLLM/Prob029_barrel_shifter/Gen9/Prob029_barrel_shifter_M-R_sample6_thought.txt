Refactor the barrel shifter by removing the explicit mux2X1 submodule and instead directly use conditional (ternary) operators to implement the 2-to-1 multiplexing behavior. This approach eliminates module redefinition issues, simplifies the code, and maintains the same rotate-left shift functionality controlled by the `ctrl` bits. The barrel shifter will be implemented in three stages with intermediate wires, each stage conditionally rotating the input by 4, 2, and 1 bits respectively.
