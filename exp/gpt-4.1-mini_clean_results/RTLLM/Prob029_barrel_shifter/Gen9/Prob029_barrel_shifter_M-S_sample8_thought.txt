Instead of implementing the barrel shifter with three stages of mux arrays, simplify by directly computing the total shift amount from `ctrl` as a single 3-bit rotate amount (sum of bits weighted by 4, 2, and 1). Then perform one rotation operation in a single combinational assignment using Verilog's concatenation and slicing.

This approach removes the need for the `mux2to1` module and the three-stage multiplexing structure, greatly simplifying the design while maintaining the exact rotation behavior controlled by `ctrl`.

It is more concise, easier to understand, and likely more efficient in synthesis.
