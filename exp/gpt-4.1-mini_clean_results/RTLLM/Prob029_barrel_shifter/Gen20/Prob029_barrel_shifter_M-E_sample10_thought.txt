Implement the barrel shifter using a single combinational always block with a case statement based on the 3-bit control signal representing the shift amount as a number (0 to 7). Instead of multiple stages of mux2X1 instances, perform a rotate-left by the total shift amount in one step using a straightforward indexing formula.

This approach interprets `ctrl` as an encoded shift amount (sum of the bits weighted 1,2,4), then rotates the input left by that amount. This is efficient, concise, and avoids multiple stages and explicit mux instances, thus providing a fully behavioral but synthesizable design.

The rotation is achieved by assigning each output bit from the input bit rotated by `shift_amount`. This achieves the same functional result as the multi-stage mux design but with clearer and simpler code.
