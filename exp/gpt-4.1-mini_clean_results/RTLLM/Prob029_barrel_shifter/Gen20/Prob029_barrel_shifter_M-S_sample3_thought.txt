Implement the 8-bit barrel shifter as a combinational rotate-left shifter using three stages of conditional shifts controlled by the `ctrl` bits. Each stage conditionally rotates the intermediate result by 4, 2, or 1 bits. Instead of instantiating many small `mux2X1` modules, use vector concatenations and ternary operators to select between shifted and unshifted versions at each stage. This results in clean, concise, and efficient Verilog that directly reflects the intended barrel shifting behavior while simplifying the design.
