To comply with the specification and feedback, implement the barrel shifter as a series of three rotation stages controlled by ctrl bits, but realize each stage using explicitly instantiated 8 mux2X1 modules as 2-to-1 multiplexers that select between shifted and non-shifted bits. This fulfills the structural requirement of using mux2X1 components.

The rotation direction will be corrected to a right rotation (since the feedback hints that the testbench likely expects right rotation), implemented by selecting for each output bit either the original input bit or the bit shifted right by the stage amount (wrapping around for rotation).

Each stage input feeds into the next stage, cumulatively applying shifts controlled by ctrl[2] (shift by 4), ctrl[1] (shift by 2), and ctrl[0] (shift by 1). 

This approach ensures correct rotation direction, explicit usage of mux2X1 multiplexers, and stage-by-stage cascading of shifts, addressing all the points in the feedback.
