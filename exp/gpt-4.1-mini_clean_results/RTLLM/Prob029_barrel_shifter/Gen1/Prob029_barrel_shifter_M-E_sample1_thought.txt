Implement the barrel shifter using a single combinational logic block that performs a left rotate by the total shift amount specified by the 3-bit ctrl signal, interpreted as an integer shift amount from 0 to 7. Instead of cascading multiple stages with mux2X1, directly calculate the rotated output bits using arithmetic index calculation for each output bit. This approach uses continuous assignments with bitwise rotation implemented by indexing, avoiding explicit multiplexers and multiple shift stages.
