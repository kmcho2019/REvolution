Implement an 8-bit right-rotating barrel shifter by directly computing the rotated output using bitwise concatenation and slicing instead of multiple mux stages. The ctrl[2:0] value represents the total shift amount (0-7). This approach computes the rotated output in a single combinational expression by concatenating the input twice and selecting the appropriate 8-bit window, effectively rotating right by ctrl positions. This eliminates the need for sequential mux stages and simplifies the design while achieving the same functionality.
