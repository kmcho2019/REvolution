Implement the barrel shifter as a chain of three stages, each performing a conditional rotate-right by a fixed amount (4, 2, or 1) controlled by the respective bit in `ctrl`. Each stage consists of 8 mux2X1 instances selecting between the input and the input rotated by the stage's shift amount. This modular approach clearly separates the stages and matches the requirement for multiplexed shifting. By chaining the stages, the total rotate amount is the sum of selected shifts, but implemented as stepwise multiplexers, allowing efficient hardware mapping.
