To simplify the 8-bit barrel shifter, I will implement the entire rotation in a single expression instead of multiple stages. Since the ctrl signal encodes the amount to rotate by (1, 2, and 4 positions), the total rotate amount is the sum of those shifts where ctrl bits are set.

This allows me to treat ctrl as a 3-bit binary number representing the shift amount from 0 to 7. I can then perform a single rotate by that amount using concatenation and slicing.

This approach removes the need for multiple conditional stages and muxes, leading to a concise, clean, and easy-to-understand implementation with the same functionality.
