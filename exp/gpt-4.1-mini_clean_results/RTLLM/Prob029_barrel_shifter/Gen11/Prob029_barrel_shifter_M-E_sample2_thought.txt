Implement the barrel shifter as a single combinational expression that conditionally rotates the input based on the 3-bit control signal by composing the shifts using bitwise operations and concatenations, instead of using separate 2-to-1 multiplexers at every bit position. 

Each bit in `ctrl` corresponds to a rotation by 1, 2, or 4 positions. The rotation amount is the sum of the active bits in `ctrl`. Compute the total shift amount from `ctrl`, and perform a barrel rotation by directly concatenating and slicing the input vector. This approach is more compact and efficient in hardware, reducing the logic depth by avoiding multiple layers of muxes. 

This design offers a novel architectural idea relative to the given example by directly using the numeric control value as a shift amount for rotation, enabling a clean and intuitive implementation without auxiliary mux modules.
