Refactor the barrel shifter to eliminate the explicit `mux2X1` submodule and instead use conditional (ternary) operators (`?:`) directly within continuous assignments to implement the mux functionality. The shifting logic remains a cascade of three conditional rotations by 4, 2, and 1 bits respectively, controlled by the bits of `ctrl`. Each stage is represented by a wire vector generated by assigning each bit conditionally from either the original or the rotated version of the previous stage’s output.

This approach removes the need for a separate `mux2X1` module, avoids module naming conflicts, and simplifies the design by using pure combinational assignments while keeping the staged shift structure intact.
