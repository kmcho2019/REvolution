I will simplify the barrel shifter by directly performing the combined rotate-right operation using a single concatenation and a variable shift amount derived from the `ctrl` bits. This eliminates the need for multiple stages and muxes, making the design more concise and easier to understand while maintaining full functionality.

The total rotate amount is the sum of 4*ctrl[2] + 2*ctrl[1] + 1*ctrl[0]. I will use this to perform a rotate-right by that total amount in one step with bitwise concatenation and indexing.

This approach is purely combinational, synthesizable, and efficient.
