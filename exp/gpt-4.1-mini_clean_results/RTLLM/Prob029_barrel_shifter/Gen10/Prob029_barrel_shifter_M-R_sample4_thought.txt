To refactor the barrel shifter, I will remove the dependency on the external `mux2X1` module and instead implement the multiplexing directly with ternary conditional operators. This simplifies the design by eliminating multiple submodule instantiations and potential port mismatch issues.

The design will still perform three stages of rotate-right shifts by 4, 2, and 1 bit, controlled respectively by `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]`. Each stage is implemented by calculating a rotated version of the current input and using a simple assign statement with a conditional operator to select between the rotated and unrotated data.

This approach is fully combinational, concise, easy to understand, and synthesizable. It also resolves any module port mismatch or simulation elaboration errors caused by missing or incompatible `mux2X1` modules.
