Implement the barrel shifter by explicitly instantiating a `mux2X1` submodule for each output bit at every stage (shift by 4, 2, and 1). Each mux selects between the bit shifted by the current stage amount and the unshifted bit from the previous stage, controlled by the corresponding `ctrl` bit. This modular approach directly models the required multiplexer-based design and clearly reflects the stepwise conditional rotations, making the design structurally clear and synthesizable.
