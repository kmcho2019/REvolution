Implement a 4-bit Galois-style LFSR which differs from the Fibonacci LFSR by applying feedback conditionally to specific bits, allowing for a more efficient and potentially faster implementation. Use synchronous active-high reset to initialize the register to a non-zero seed to prevent lock-up. On each clock edge, if not resetting, shift the bits right and update bits conditionally with feedback according to the Galois tap polynomial. The feedback bit is the XOR of the output bit shifted out and the input taps. This approach separates feedback application from the shift operation, making the design modular and easy to extend.
