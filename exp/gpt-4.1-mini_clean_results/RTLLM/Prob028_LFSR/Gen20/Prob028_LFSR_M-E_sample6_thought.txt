Implement the 4-bit LFSR as a shift register with a direct feedback input derived from the XOR of the two specified bits without inverting the result. Use an asynchronous active-high reset to initialize the register to a non-zero seed to avoid lock-up in the all-zero state. The feedback bit is calculated inline within the sequential always block to minimize logic complexity and resource usage. The output port directly reflects the internal register state. This simpler architecture avoids separate combinational logic blocks and uses a straightforward shift and feedback assignment within the clocked process, reducing latency and improving clarity.
