Design a 4-bit LFSR with asynchronous active-low reset for immediate reset response and simplicity in testbench stimulus. The LFSR register will be initialized to 4'b1111 on reset to avoid the all-zero lockup.

Use the characteristic polynomial x^4 + x^3 + 1, which corresponds to XOR feedback from bits 3 and 2 (MSB and next MSB) without inversion (just simple XOR). This yields a maximal length sequence for a 4-bit LFSR.

Implement the feedback as XOR of out[3] and out[2] without inversion, and shift the register right on every rising clock edge.

Provide the output directly from the register state.

This asynchronous reset simplifies immediate reset functionality and initialization, and right shift with XOR feedback is an alternative to the left shift + inverted feedback approach. Both forms are common in LFSR designs but using right shift and direct XOR gives a fresh architectural style compared to the example.

The module uses a single always block with asynchronous reset, and the output is a register for timing clarity.
