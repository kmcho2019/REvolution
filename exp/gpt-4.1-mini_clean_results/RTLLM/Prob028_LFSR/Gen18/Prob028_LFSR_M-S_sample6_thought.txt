Implement a straightforward 4-bit LFSR with feedback defined as the inverted XOR of bits 3 and 2. Use a synchronous active-high reset to initialize the register to a non-zero seed (1) to avoid lock-up at zero. On each clock edge, shift left and insert the feedback at the LSB. Provide a minimal testbench with correctly declared single-bit clock and reset signals that toggle the clock and release reset after a short time, allowing observation of the LFSR output.
