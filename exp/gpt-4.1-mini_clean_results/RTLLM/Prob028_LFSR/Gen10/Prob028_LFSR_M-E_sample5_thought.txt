Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a shift register with an explicit state register and a combinational feedback generator that calculates feedback before the clock edge. Instead of computing feedback from the current 'out' register bits, use a separate intermediate wire that calculates the XOR of the MSB and the next MSB without inversion. Then, use this feedback directly at the LSB on the next clock. The reset initializes the register to 4'b1111 (non-zero) to avoid the all-zero locked state, commonly avoided in LFSRs. This approach ensures the LFSR never locks to zero and clarifies the feedback mechanism by removing inversion and setting a known non-zero start state.
