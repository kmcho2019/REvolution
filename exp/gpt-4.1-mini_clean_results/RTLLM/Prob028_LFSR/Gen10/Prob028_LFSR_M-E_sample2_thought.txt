Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a register that shifts right instead of left, inserting feedback at the most significant bit (MSB) position. Compute feedback as the inverted XOR of bits 3 and 2 of the current register state (same as original). Use asynchronous active-high reset to clear the register to zero. This variant demonstrates a right-shift architecture and asynchronous reset usage while maintaining the specified feedback logic and output behavior.
