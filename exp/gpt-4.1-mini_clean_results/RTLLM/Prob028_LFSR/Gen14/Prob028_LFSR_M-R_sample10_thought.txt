Refactor the 4-bit LFSR by using a shift register implemented as a reg variable updated on the clock edge with synchronous reset. Compute the feedback bit as a combinational wire from XOR and inversion of selected bits. Instead of separate next_state logic, directly assign the shifted and feedback-inserted value to the register on clock edge. Use a non-zero reset seed for proper operation.
