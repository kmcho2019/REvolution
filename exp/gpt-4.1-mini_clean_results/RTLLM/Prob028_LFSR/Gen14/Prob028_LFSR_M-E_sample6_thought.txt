Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a combinational feedback calculation outside the always block with a dedicated wire for feedback, and perform a shift-right operation on each clock cycle. On reset, initialize the register to a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b0001) to avoid the lock-up state common in LFSRs. The feedback bit is obtained by XORing the two highest bits (out[3] and out[2]), then inverting the result. This feedback bit is shifted into the most significant bit position (MSB) as the register shifts right. This approach differs by shifting right and inserting the feedback at MSB, uses a non-zero reset seed, and separates feedback logic cleanly from state update logic.
