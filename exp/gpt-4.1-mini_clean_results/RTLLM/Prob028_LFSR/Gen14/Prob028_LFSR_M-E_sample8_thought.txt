Implement the 4-bit LFSR with a shift register formed by a register array and compute the feedback bit in a continuous assignment based on the XOR of bits 3 and 2, without inversion, but invert the feedback when loading it into the LSB. Use a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk for both reset and shifting. On reset, preload the register with a non-zero seed different from the previous example to avoid lock-up. This approach explicitly models the shift register as separate bits and illustrates an alternative style of feedback insertion with the inversion applied in the sequential part.
