Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a shift register that shifts right instead of left, with feedback computed as the XOR of out[1] and out[0] (the two least significant bits). The feedback is then directly fed into the most significant bit (out[3]) on each clock edge. On reset, initialize the register to a non-zero value to avoid the lock-up state of all zeros (commonly done in LFSRs). This approach changes the tap points and the shift direction, providing a different architecture while still generating a maximal-length pseudo-random sequence.
