Implement the 4-bit LFSR with a right shift instead of left shift. Calculate the feedback as the inversion of XOR between the two least significant bits (out[0] and out[1]). On each clock rising edge, if reset is high, initialize the register to a non-zero seed to avoid lock-up. Otherwise, shift the register right by one bit and insert the feedback at the MSB position. This approach reverses the bit order direction compared to the original design, providing a fresh structural perspective while maintaining the same feedback logic principles.
