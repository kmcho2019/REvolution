To improve the original LFSR design for better power, performance, and area (PPA) while maintaining correctness, I propose the following enhancements:

1. Keep the non-zero seed as a parameter for flexibility and to avoid zero-lock.
2. Use a synchronous reset with non-blocking assignments for clean and glitch-free design.
3. Calculate the feedback signal inside the always block as a local wire to reduce fanout and combinational path length, aiding synthesis optimization.
4. Add an inline function to compute feedback, which some synthesis tools can optimize better by handling it as a combinational logic block with limited scope.
5. Ensure the feedback is explicitly a 1-bit value, avoiding synthesis warnings.
6. Make the design fully parameterized and self-contained for easy reuse and integration.
7. Add comments explaining the feedback logic and the shift operation clearly.

This approach keeps the code clean, synthesizable, and efficient, with potential improvements in timing and area due to limited fanout and scoped combinational logic.
