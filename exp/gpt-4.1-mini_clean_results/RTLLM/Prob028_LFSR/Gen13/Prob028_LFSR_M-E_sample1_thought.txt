Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a separate combinational block to calculate the feedback bit and a sequential always block for shifting and updating the register on the rising edge of clk. The feedback is calculated as the inverted XOR of out[3] and out[2]. Use synchronous reset to initialize the register to a non-zero value (e.g., 4'b0001) to avoid lock-up in zero state. The output is the current register state.
