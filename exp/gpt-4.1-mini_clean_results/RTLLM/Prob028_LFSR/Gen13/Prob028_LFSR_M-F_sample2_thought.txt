The original LFSR module is correct as per specification. The issues arise from how it is instantiated and connected in the testbench, particularly width mismatches and improper signal declarations. To fix this, we will provide a clean, self-contained testbench that declares signals with the correct widths, drives the clock and reset properly, and connects the DUT (Device Under Test) ports matching widths exactly. This will ensure no warnings or errors related to port widths or unresolved signals during simulation.
