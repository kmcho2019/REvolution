Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a combinational function to calculate feedback outside the sequential always block. The feedback is the inverted XOR of bits 3 and 2 of the current state. On each clock edge, if reset is asserted, initialize the register to a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b0001) to avoid lock-up in all zeros. Otherwise, shift left by one bit and input the feedback at the LSB. This approach ensures continuous LFSR operation and clean separation of combinational and sequential logic.
