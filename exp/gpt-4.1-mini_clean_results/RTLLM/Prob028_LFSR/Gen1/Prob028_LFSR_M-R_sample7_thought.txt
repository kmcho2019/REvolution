Refactor the 4-bit LFSR by separating the state register and the feedback calculation more explicitly. Use a combinational always block to compute the next state based on current state and feedback, and a synchronous always block for state update on clock edge and reset. This approach improves readability and modularizes the logic while maintaining the same functionality: on reset, the register is cleared; otherwise, it shifts left with feedback inserted at LSB, where feedback is the inverted XOR of bits 3 and 2.
