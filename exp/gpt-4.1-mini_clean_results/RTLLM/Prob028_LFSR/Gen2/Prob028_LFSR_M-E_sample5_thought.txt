Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a shift-right operation instead of shift-left. Calculate the feedback bit inside the sequential always block by XORing bits 0 and 1 of the current register state and then inverting the result. On the rising edge of clk, if rst is asserted, load a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b1000) into the register. Otherwise, shift right and insert the feedback at the most significant bit (MSB). This approach offers a reversed bit-shifting direction while maintaining correct pseudo-random sequence generation and simplifies feedback computation by using the lower bits of the register.
