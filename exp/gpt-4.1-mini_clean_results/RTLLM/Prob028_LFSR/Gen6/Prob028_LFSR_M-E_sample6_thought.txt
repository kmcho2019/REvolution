Implement the 4-bit LFSR with asynchronous active-high reset and use a single always_ff block (SystemVerilog) triggered on posedge clk or posedge rst. Compute the feedback bit inside the sequential block to keep all logic local. Feedback is the inverted XOR of the two most significant bits of the current state (out[3] and out[2]). On reset, the LFSR state is initialized to 4'b0001 (non-zero seed to avoid locking into all-zero state). On clock edge without reset, shift left by one bit and insert feedback at LSB. This approach uses an asynchronous reset and a local feedback calculation, keeping the design modular and easily synthesizable, while also preventing the all-zero lockup state by using a non-zero seed on reset.
