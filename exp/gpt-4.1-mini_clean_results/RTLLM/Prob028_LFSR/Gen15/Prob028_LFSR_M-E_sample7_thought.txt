Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a single 4-bit shift register stored in a reg. Instead of performing the feedback calculation externally and inverting it only during insertion, directly compute the feedback by XORing bits 3 and 2 of the current state, then invert that result before feeding it back. Use a synchronous reset that initializes the register to zero as specified. On each rising clock edge, shift left and insert the inverted feedback at LSB. This approach simplifies the feedback computation and maintains a straightforward design by embedding the inversion directly into the feedback logic rather than after computing XOR.
