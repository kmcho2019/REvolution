The original LFSR module code is functionally correct; the issue lies in ensuring that the reset sets the register to a non-zero seed value, since resetting to zero causes the LFSR to lock in the zero state and stop producing a sequence. Also, initializing the register to zero is generally not recommended for LFSRs because they do not cycle through states. To fix this, on reset, initialize the register to a non-zero value, such as 4'b0001, to start the pseudo-random sequence properly. The feedback remains the inverted XOR of bits [3] and [2], and shifting is done left with this feedback inserted at LSB. This ensures the LFSR never gets stuck at zero and produces the expected pseudo-random sequence.
