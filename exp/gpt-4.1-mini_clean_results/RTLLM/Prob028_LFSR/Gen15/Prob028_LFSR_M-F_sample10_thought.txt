To strictly follow the problem statement, the LFSR should shift left on each clock cycle, inserting the feedback bit at the least significant bit (LSB). The feedback is calculated as the inverted XOR of the two most significant bits (out[3] and out[2]). On reset (active high), the register is initialized to zero, as specified. To avoid lock-up in all-zero state (which is common in LFSRs but not forbidden here), the design follows the problem exactly. The feedback calculation is done with a wire, and the always block updates the register on the rising clock edge. The code cleanly separates feedback calculation and state update.
