The original LFSR module implementation is functionally correct and matches the specification precisely. The reported issues stem from incorrect signal widths and signal declarations in the testbench environment, not the LFSR module itself. To fix the problem and make the module self-contained and fully verifiable, I will provide a simple, clean testbench that properly declares signals with matching widths and types, drives the clock and reset correctly, and connects the LFSR module ports properly.

This testbench will:
- Declare clk and rst as reg (1-bit).
- Declare out as wire [3:0].
- Drive the clock as a toggling signal.
- Pulse reset high initially.
- Display the LFSR output for several cycles.

This ensures no port width mismatches or driver conflicts and allows proper verification of the module functionality.

The LFSR module itself remains unchanged as it is already correct.
