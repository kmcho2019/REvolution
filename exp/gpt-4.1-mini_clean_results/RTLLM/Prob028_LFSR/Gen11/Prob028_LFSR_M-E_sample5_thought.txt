Implement a 4-bit LFSR with asynchronous active-high reset to zero. Instead of shifting left, shift right so that the feedback bit is inserted at the most significant bit (MSB) position. The feedback is computed as the inversion of the XOR between the MSB (out[3]) and bit 1 (out[1]), providing a different feedback tap configuration. This approach alters the sequence generation pattern, and asynchronous reset allows the register to be cleared immediately on reset. The output always reflects the current state of the LFSR register.
