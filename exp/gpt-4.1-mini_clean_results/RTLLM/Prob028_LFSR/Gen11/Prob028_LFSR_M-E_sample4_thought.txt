Instead of implementing the LFSR with synchronous reset and shift-left with feedback insertion at LSB, I will implement it with asynchronous reset for immediate initialization and shift-right operation. The feedback will be computed as the XOR of the least significant bit (out[0]) and the bit at position 1 (out[1]), then inverted as before. This feedback is shifted into the MSB on each clock cycle. Using asynchronous reset ensures the LFSR initializes immediately to a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b1000), preventing lockup and allowing immediate response to reset. The right-shift direction is a novel alternative that maintains the pseudo-random sequence property given correct feedback taps.
