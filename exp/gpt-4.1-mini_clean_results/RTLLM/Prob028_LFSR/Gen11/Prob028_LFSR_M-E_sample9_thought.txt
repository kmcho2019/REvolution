Implement a 4-bit LFSR with an active-high synchronous reset that sets the register to a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b1001) to avoid lock-up at zero. Use a single always block triggered on the rising edge of the clock, combining state update and output assignment in one register. Calculate the feedback bit as the inverted XOR of the two highest bits (out[3] and out[2]), then shift the register to the right and insert the feedback bit into the MSB (bit 3). This right-shift with inverted feedback is a novel approach compared to typical left-shift LFSRs, providing the same maximal length pseudo-random sequence. The output is assigned directly from this register.
