Implement the 4-bit LFSR using a shift register approach inside a clocked always block with asynchronous reset. Use an internal register to hold the state and output it as `out`. Compute the feedback as the inverted XOR of bits 3 and 2 of the current state. On every rising clock edge, if reset is asserted, initialize to a non-zero seed (e.g., 4'b0001) to avoid the all-zero lockup state; otherwise, shift left and insert the feedback at the LSB. This seed initialization helps the LFSR generate a maximal-length pseudo-random sequence instead of being stuck in zero state.
