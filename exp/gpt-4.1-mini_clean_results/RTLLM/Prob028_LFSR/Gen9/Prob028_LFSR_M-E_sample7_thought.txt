Create a 4-bit LFSR with asynchronous active-high reset initializing the register to 4'b1111 to avoid the all-zero lock-up state. On each rising clock edge, shift the register right by one bit and insert the feedback bit at the most significant bit (MSB). The feedback is generated by XORing the two least significant bits (out[0] and out[1]) and then inverted. This right-shift approach contrasts with the usual left-shift design and uses asynchronous reset for immediate initialization, providing a different architectural style.
