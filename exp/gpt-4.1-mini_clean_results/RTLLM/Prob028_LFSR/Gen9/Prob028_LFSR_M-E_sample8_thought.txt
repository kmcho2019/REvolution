Design the 4-bit LFSR with an asynchronous active-high reset that immediately clears the register to zero when asserted. Use the feedback bit as the inverted XOR of the top two bits (out[3] and out[2]). On each rising clock edge, if not reset, shift the register right (towards LSB) and load the feedback bit into the most significant bit (MSB). This reverses the shifting direction compared to left shift, offering a different architecture while maintaining the required feedback logic. Keep the output a register to hold the current state.
