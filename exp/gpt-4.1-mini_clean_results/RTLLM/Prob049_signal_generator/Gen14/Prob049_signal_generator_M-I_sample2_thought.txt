To improve PPA, simplify the logic by removing the saturate condition and rely solely on state to control counting direction and boundary crossing; this removes redundant holds and boundary checks in next_wave logic. By incrementing or decrementing wave every cycle, and toggling state exactly when wave hits the boundary, the critical path is shortened as there's no need for saturation logic. This minimal combinational logic reduces area and power and improves timing. Also, removing the intermediate next_wave register and directly computing next wave within the sequential block minimizes registers and muxing, slightly reducing area. The updated design keeps asynchronous reset and clear, concise comments for maintainability.
