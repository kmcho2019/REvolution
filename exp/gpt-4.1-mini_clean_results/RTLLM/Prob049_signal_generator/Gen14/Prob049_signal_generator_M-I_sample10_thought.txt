The existing design is efficient and clean, using a direction bit as a 1-bit state to control increment or decrement of the 5-bit wave register. To optimize for better PPA (performance, power, and area), we can slightly refine the logic to reduce unnecessary toggling of the direction signal and minimize conditional checks. Specifically:

1. Update the wave register and direction bit separately to reduce dependency and improve timing.
2. Use local variables for next state/wave values to reduce combinational path and enable easier logic optimization during synthesis.
3. Avoid redundant updates to the wave register and direction bit when at boundary conditions to reduce switching activity and power.
4. Use a synchronous reset for more consistent timing, if allowed, but since the spec mandates async active-low reset, keep it as is.

These improvements slightly reduce the logic toggling and can tighten the critical path by simplifying combinational logic, which may improve clock frequency and reduce power and area by minimizing switching.

The waveform generation remains functionally identical: wave increments on state=0 until 31, then direction flips; decrements on state=1 until 0, then flips direction again.
