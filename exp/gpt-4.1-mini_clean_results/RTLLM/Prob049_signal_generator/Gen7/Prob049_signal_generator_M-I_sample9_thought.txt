The original design uses a direction bit and updates wave and direction conditionally within the clocked block. To improve timing (performance) and potentially reduce power, we can separate the logic for the next wave value and next direction into combinational logic before the clocked register update. This pipelining style reduces combinational path through state registers and may improve critical path timing. Also, simplifying the direction update logic by making direction a one-bit FSM that toggles only at boundary conditions helps minimize switching. We'll use non-blocking assignments consistently and keep the design clean. No extra registers are added, so area stays minimal.
