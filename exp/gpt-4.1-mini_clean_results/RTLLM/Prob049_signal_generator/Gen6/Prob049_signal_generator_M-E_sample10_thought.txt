Implement the triangle wave generator using a direction signal that directly controls whether the wave value is incremented or decremented. Instead of using a single-bit state with a case statement or if-else, maintain an explicit direction register where 0 means increasing and 1 means decreasing. On each clock cycle, update the wave according to the direction. When the wave hits either boundary (0 or 31), toggle the direction. This architecture cleanly separates the control signal from the waveform register, making it explicit and easy to extend. Use synchronous reset with non-blocking assignments for safe sequential logic.
