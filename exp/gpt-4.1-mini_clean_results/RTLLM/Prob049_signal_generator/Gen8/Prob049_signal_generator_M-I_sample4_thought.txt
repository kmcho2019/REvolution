To optimize PPA, replace the explicit state variable and branching logic with a single signed 6-bit counter that increments or decrements automatically. Use the sign bit implicitly to control direction: the counter counts up from 0 to 31, then down from 31 to 0, cycling continuously. This removes conditional state assignments and separate state register, simplifying combinational logic and potentially improving timing. The 6-bit counter can saturate at boundaries by changing its increment sign, implemented with a direction signal derived from the counter value. Use synchronous active-low reset for better timing and power consistency. Output the lower 5 bits as the wave. This solution reduces area, power, and critical path by avoiding multiple conditionals and state updates.
