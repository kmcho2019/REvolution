I will refactor the triangle wave generator by removing the explicit state register and instead using a direction bit stored in a register that indicates whether to increment or decrement the wave value. This direction bit toggles when wave reaches the bounds (0 or 31). The wave value and direction bit are updated synchronously on the positive clock edge or asynchronously reset. This eliminates the state machine case statement and uses a simpler structure with an explicit direction flag. The design thus uses only two registers: wave (5-bit) and dir (1-bit). The direction bit acts as the state (0: increment, 1: decrement), but is represented more directly as a "direction" signal. This improves clarity by focusing on the waveform's direction rather than abstract states.
