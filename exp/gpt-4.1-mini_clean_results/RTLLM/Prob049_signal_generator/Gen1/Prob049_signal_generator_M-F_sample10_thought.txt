To address the feedback regarding the immediate comparison of `wave` after increment/decrement in the same cycle, we can use a temporary signal to calculate the next value of `wave` before updating it. This allows checking boundary conditions on the next value rather than the current, reducing combinational delay and potentially improving timing.

We'll:
- Compute `next_wave` as `wave + 1` or `wave - 1` depending on the state.
- Update `wave` with `next_wave` at the clock edge.
- Update `state` based on `next_wave` reaching 31 or 0.
