Instead of using a 1-bit state register to track increment or decrement, use a direction signal as a 1-bit register where '1' means increment and '0' means decrement. On each clock cycle, the wave value updates according to the direction. When the wave reaches the boundary values 0 or 31, the direction flips. This approach removes the case statement and simplifies control logic into conditional direction toggling, making the design clear and concise.
