Combine the state and wave update into a single always block triggered on posedge clk or negedge rst_n to minimize clocked logic and reduce complexity. Use a simple 1-bit state register to indicate increment/decrement direction. On reset, initialize state and wave to 0. On each clock, increment or decrement wave based on state; when wave reaches the boundary (0 or 31), toggle state to reverse the direction. Avoid redundant holding assignments; wave naturally stays stable if not updated. This simplification reduces logic, improves clarity, and keeps the same waveform functionality.
