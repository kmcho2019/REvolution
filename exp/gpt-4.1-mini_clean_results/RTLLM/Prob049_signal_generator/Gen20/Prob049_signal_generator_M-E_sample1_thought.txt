Implement the triangle wave generator by using a 5-bit counter and a direction flag. Instead of a separate state register, maintain a single 5-bit register for the waveform value and a 1-bit register for direction (0 for ascending, 1 for descending). On each clock cycle, update the waveform by adding or subtracting 1 depending on the direction. When the waveform reaches the upper bound (31) or lower bound (0), toggle the direction bit to reverse the counting direction. Use a synchronous reset to initialize wave and direction to zero. This approach simplifies the logic and avoids a separate state machine by encoding direction in a single bit flag, making the design straightforward, minimal, and easily synthesizable.
