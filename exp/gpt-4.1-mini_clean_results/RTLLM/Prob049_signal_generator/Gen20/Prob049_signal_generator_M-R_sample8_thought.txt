Refactor the design by eliminating the intermediate next_wave register and implementing the wave value update combinationally using an assign statement. Use a simple direction signal instead of a state register, where direction = 0 means increment and direction = 1 means decrement. The direction toggles synchronously when the wave reaches boundaries (0 or 31). This removes the separate always @(*) block, improves code compactness, and maintains clear separation of logic. The wave register updates directly based on direction, and direction updates only at boundaries in the sequential block. This approach keeps functionality identical but presents a clearer flow and slightly reduces registers and complexity.
