Simplify the design by integrating the waveform update and state transition within a single sequential always block without separate combinational logic or an explicit next-wave wire. Use the state register as a direction indicator (0: increment, 1: decrement). On each clock edge, increment or decrement wave based on state, and switch state when hitting the boundary values 0 or 31. This reduces logic by avoiding an extra combinational assignment and keeps the design concise while preserving the original behavior and timing.
