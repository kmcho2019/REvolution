Instead of using a separate state register to track the counting direction, implement the direction as the sign bit of a signed 6-bit counter that ranges between 0 and 31. Use a signed 6-bit register to hold the current waveform value extended by one bit to handle direction implicitly: the upper bit acts as the direction bit (0 for up, 1 for down). On every clock cycle, increment or decrement the waveform value accordingly. When the waveform hits 0 or 31, invert the direction bit to reverse counting. This approach eliminates the separate state variable and case/if statements for direction, simplifying the design. Also, the waveform output is always the lower 5 bits of this signed counter. On reset, initialize the counter to zero and direction to increment. This solution reduces control logic and leverages signed arithmetic for cleaner direction management.
