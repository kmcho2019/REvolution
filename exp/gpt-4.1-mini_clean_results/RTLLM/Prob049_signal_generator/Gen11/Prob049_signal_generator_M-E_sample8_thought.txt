Instead of using a single-bit "state" register to track increment or decrement, use a signed 6-bit counter that can range between 0 and 31, and a 1-bit direction signal (dir). The wave output is simply the 5 LSBs of this counter. The direction bit controls whether the counter increments or decrements. When the counter reaches the boundary (0 or 31), the direction flips. This separates the count value and direction cleanly and reduces conditional complexity. The counter increments or decrements by 1 based on direction on each clock cycle. This design improves clarity by making the count and direction explicit and simplifies the boundary checks. It still uses asynchronous active-low reset as required.
