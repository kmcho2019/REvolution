Use a single 6-bit signed counter register to represent the waveform value extended with direction embedded as the sign of an increment value. The output wave is the absolute value of the signed counter's 5 least significant bits, producing a triangle wave between 0 and 31. On each clock cycle, the signed counter is incremented or decremented by a signed step (+1 or -1). When the waveform reaches either boundary (0 or 31), the direction is reversed by negating the increment step. This approach removes the explicit state register, simplifies the direction control to a signed increment, and uses arithmetic and absolute value operations to generate the triangle wave efficiently.
