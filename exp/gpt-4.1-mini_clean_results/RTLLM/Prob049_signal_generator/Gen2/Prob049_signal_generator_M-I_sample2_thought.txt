To optimize the existing triangle wave generator for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), I maintain the single-bit direction flag and use non-blocking assignments consistently to ensure glitch-free synthesis and simulation. 

I simplify the logic by always updating the wave at each clock, using the direction flag to decide increment or decrement. When the wave reaches either boundary (0 or 31), the direction toggles on the next clock cycle. This removes duplicated code and reduces control complexity. 

This approach reduces the critical path to just one comparator and an addition or subtraction, improving timing. Also, removing the nested if-else inside direction branches optimizes area and power due to simpler muxing and logic. The asynchronous active-low reset and waveform range are preserved as specified.
