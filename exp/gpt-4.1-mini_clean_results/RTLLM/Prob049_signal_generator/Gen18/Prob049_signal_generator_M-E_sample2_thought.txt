Instead of using a single-bit state to track increment/decrement and updating wave sequentially, I will implement the triangle wave generator using a direction signal that explicitly encodes whether the wave is increasing or decreasing. The direction signal will be toggled at the boundaries (0 and 31). To make the design more modular and clearer, I will separate the next value calculation into a combinational always block and then update the wave and direction registers synchronously. This approach also makes it easy to extend or modify behavior (e.g., changing step size or max/min levels). The reset initializes wave and direction to start incrementing from zero. This modular approach improves readability and may help synthesis tools optimize control logic. The module operates asynchronously reset but updates state and wave on clock rising edge.
