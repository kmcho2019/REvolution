Implement the triangle wave generator using a single 5-bit wave register and a direction flag that indicates whether the waveform is currently incrementing or decrementing. On reset, wave and direction are initialized to 0 (start from zero and increment). On each clock cycle, update wave based on direction: if incrementing, add 1 until 31 is reached, then toggle direction to decrement; if decrementing, subtract 1 until 0 is reached, then toggle direction to increment. This approach avoids a separate explicit state machine by using a direction bit, making control logic more compact and directly tied to the wave value.
