Implement the triangle wave generator using a single 5-bit register for the wave and a direction bit that indicates whether the wave is currently incrementing or decrementing. Instead of a separate "state" register, use the MSB (most significant bit) of the direction bit to indicate direction: 0 means counting up, 1 means counting down. On each clock edge, if reset is asserted, clear wave and direction. Otherwise, increment or decrement wave based on direction. When wave reaches the boundaries (0 or 31), flip the direction bit. This approach eliminates the need for a case statement or separate state register with more complex transitions, making the logic concise and fully synchronous with asynchronous reset. The wave updates every cycle, smoothly counting up and down between 0 and 31 inclusively.
