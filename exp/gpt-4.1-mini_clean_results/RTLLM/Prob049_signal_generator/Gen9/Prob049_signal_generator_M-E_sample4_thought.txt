Instead of using a direction state to control incrementing or decrementing, use a single up/down counter approach with a 6-bit counter that counts from 0 to 62 and folds the count into the 0 to 31 range to form a triangle waveform. The waveform is generated by reflecting the count about 31: for counts 0-31, output the count as is; for counts 32-62, output 62-count. This eliminates the need for direction control logic and state transitions, simplifies the design, and allows the waveform to be generated by a single counter and combinational logic. This approach reduces state logic and comparisons, potentially improving timing, power, and area. The 5-bit output wave is derived from the 6-bit counter accordingly.
