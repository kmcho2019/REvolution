Instead of explicitly storing the state, use a direction flag that toggles when the wave hits the boundaries (0 or 31). The wave register will increment or decrement depending on this direction flag on each clock cycle. The direction flag and wave register are updated synchronously with reset asynchronously clearing both. This reduces the logic to a single direction bit and a counter, maintaining the same waveform generation but with a slightly different architectural approach using a direction toggle rather than a state machine.
