Refactor the design by modeling the waveform generation as a simple Moore finite state machine with two states: INCREMENT and DECREMENT. The state and wave registers are updated synchronously on the clock's positive edge or asynchronously reset on negedge rst_n. 

- Use enumerated states for clarity.
- Compute next state and next wave entirely in combinational logic, outside the sequential always block.
- Update the registers directly with the computed next values in a single clocked always block.
- This structure cleanly separates the combinational state transition logic from sequential updates and avoids the wave_update_en signal, simplifying the datapath.
- The waveform increments while in INCREMENT state until reaching 31, then transitions to DECREMENT.
- In DECREMENT state, the waveform decrements until 0, then switches back to INCREMENT.
- Output wave directly from the register.
This approach improves readability and is a classical FSM implementation pattern.
