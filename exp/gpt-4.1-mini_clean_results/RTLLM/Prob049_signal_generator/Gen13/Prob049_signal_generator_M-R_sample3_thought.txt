Refactor the triangle wave generator by implementing the state as a binary direction flag and using a single always block without a combinational next_wave calculation. The direction bit directly controls whether to increment or decrement wave. When wave reaches boundaries (0 or 31), the direction bit flips immediately, which simplifies the logic and removes the need for holding wave constant on bounds. This approach eliminates the separate combinational block, reducing complexity and potential glitches. Also, keep the asynchronous active-low reset as per spec. This compact style improves readability and slightly reduces logic by embedding direction control into one procedural block.
