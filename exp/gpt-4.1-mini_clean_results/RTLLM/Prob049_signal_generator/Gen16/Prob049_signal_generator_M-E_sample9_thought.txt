Implement the triangle wave generator using a counter approach with a signed direction increment variable. 
Instead of tracking a state, use a signed 6-bit accumulator (extended from 5 bits) that increments/decrements by 1 each clock cycle.
When the output hits the upper or lower bound (0 or 31), the direction flips by negating the increment.
This approach reduces logic by avoiding explicit state and conditional branching: update wave and direction in a single always block.
Use synchronous active-low reset to initialize wave and direction.
The direction is represented as a signed 6-bit variable (+1 or -1), which is added directly to the wave value.
Bounds are checked after addition, and direction flips accordingly.
This method results in minimal logic and clean, compact code with smooth waveform generation.
