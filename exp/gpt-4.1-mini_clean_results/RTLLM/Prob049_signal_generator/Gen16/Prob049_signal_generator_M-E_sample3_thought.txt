Implement the triangle wave generator using a directional flag within a single always block triggered by the clock and asynchronous reset. Use a single 5-bit wave register and a direction bit that indicates whether to count up or down. On each clock cycle, if reset is inactive, update the wave by incrementing or decrementing based on direction. Upon reaching the upper (31) or lower (0) bounds, flip the direction bit to reverse counting. This approach eliminates separate combinational next-state logic, simplifies the design, and relies purely on sequential logic with minimal registers, ensuring straightforward synthesis and good timing. The wave continuously counts between 0 and 31, producing the triangle waveform.
