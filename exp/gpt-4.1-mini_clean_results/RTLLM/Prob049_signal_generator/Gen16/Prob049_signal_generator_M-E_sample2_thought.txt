Use a single direction bit as the state to control whether the waveform is incrementing or decrementing. At each clock edge, update the wave value by adding or subtracting 1 according to the direction. When the wave reaches either boundary (0 or 31), invert the direction bit for the next cycle. This removes the need for a case or if-else inside the combinational block, making the update simple and fully synchronous without holding wave values. Initialize wave and direction to zero on reset. This approach maintains the triangle waveform with a minimal, clean design.
