Improvement strategy:

To optimize the given design for better PPA (Performance, Power, and Area), we will:

1. Merge the state and wave registers' update logic into a single always block to reduce register-to-register delay and simplify the control path, improving timing.

2. Eliminate redundant assignments that hold the wave value explicitly at boundaries; instead, update wave only when incrementing or decrementing is valid. This reduces unnecessary toggling and switching power.

3. Use a minimal if-else structure instead of a case statement for state transitions to simplify logic.

4. Keep the asynchronous active-low reset as specified.

5. The single-bit state will toggle at boundaries to switch direction, keeping the state logic minimal and efficient.

This refined approach preserves original functionality, simplifies control flow, reduces logic complexity, and thus should improve critical path, power consumption, and gate count.
