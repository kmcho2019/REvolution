To optimize the original design for better power, performance, and area (PPA), I will simplify the state and waveform update logic by eliminating the combinational next_wave register and integrating the update logic directly inside the sequential always block. This reduces combinational path delay and logic complexity. Additionally, I will use a flag direction signal (state) to control increment/decrement inline, and directly update `wave` accordingly. This reduces toggling at boundaries by holding wave steady and toggling state only when limits are reached, maintaining correct triangle wave behavior. This approach also cuts down on logic area and improves timing by having a minimal clock-to-output path. Comments are retained for clarity.
