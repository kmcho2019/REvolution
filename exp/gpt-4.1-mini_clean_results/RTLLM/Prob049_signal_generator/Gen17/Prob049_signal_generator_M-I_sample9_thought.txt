To optimize the existing triangle wave generator for better performance, power, and area while maintaining correctness, I will:

1. Remove the intermediate combinational next_wave register and directly compute wave next value inside the sequential block. This reduces combinational logic depth, improving timing (WNS, TNS).

2. Integrate the state toggling and wave update logic into a single always block with straightforward non-blocking assignments, minimizing logic duplication and control path complexity.

3. Minimize toggling at the boundary conditions by only updating wave when incrementing or decrementing within the range, and holding steady at the boundaries without changing wave unnecessarily, thus saving dynamic power.

4. Maintain asynchronous active-low reset and clear all state cleanly.

This approach reduces combinational logic and register toggling, leading to lower switching activity (power), simpler logic (area), and improved timing due to reduced path complexity.
