Refactor the design to use a parameterized directional signal (+1 or -1) instead of an explicit state register, thus simplifying state handling. Use a signed direction register to decide whether to increment or decrement the wave. Implement the next wave calculation in a combinational assignment with a single arithmetic operation using the direction. Flip the direction on hitting the waveform boundaries (0 or 31) synchronously on clock. The active-low reset asynchronously initializes wave and direction. This eliminates the explicit state variable and case statements, resulting in clearer, concise, and easily synthesizable code.
