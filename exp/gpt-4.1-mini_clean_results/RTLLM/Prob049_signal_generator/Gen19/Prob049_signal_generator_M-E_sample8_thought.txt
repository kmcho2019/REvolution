Use a single signed 6-bit counter that counts from 0 up to 31 and back down to 0 by incrementing or decrementing the counter each clock cycle. This counter includes the direction implicitly within its value range: it counts up from 0 to 31, then counts down from 31 to 0, forming a triangular waveform naturally. The MSB acts as the direction bit internally. On reset, initialize to 0. The output wave is extracted as the lower 5 bits of the counter. This approach eliminates a separate state register and explicit boundary checks, relying on arithmetic overflow to invert the direction, simplifying the design and potentially improving timing and area.
