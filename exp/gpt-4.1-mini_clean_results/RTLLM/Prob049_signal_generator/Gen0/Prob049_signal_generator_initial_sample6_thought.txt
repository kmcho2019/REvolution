Implement a synchronous module with an active-low reset that uses a 1-bit state register to control increment or decrement of a 5-bit wave register. On reset, state and wave are cleared. When state=0, wave increments until it reaches 31, then state switches to 1. When state=1, wave decrements until 0, then state switches back to 0. The waveform thus forms a triangle wave cycling between 0 and 31.
