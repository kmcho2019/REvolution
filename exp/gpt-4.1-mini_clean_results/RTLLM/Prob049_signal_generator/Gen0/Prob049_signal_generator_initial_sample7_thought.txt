Implement a synchronous process that updates the state and wave registers on the positive edge of clk or negative edge of rst_n. On reset, both registers are cleared to 0. When state is 0, wave increments by 1 until 31, then state switches to 1. When state is 1, wave decrements by 1 until 0, then state switches to 0, creating a triangle wave between 0 and 31.
