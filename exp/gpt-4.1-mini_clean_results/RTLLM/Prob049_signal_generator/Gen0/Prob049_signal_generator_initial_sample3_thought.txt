Implement a synchronous triangle wave generator with a 1-bit state to indicate incrementing or decrementing the 5-bit wave value. On reset, initialize both state and wave to 0. In state 0, increment wave until it reaches 31, then transition to state 1. In state 1, decrement wave until it reaches 0, then transition back to state 0. Update state and wave synchronously on the positive clock edge and asynchronous reset on negedge rst_n.
