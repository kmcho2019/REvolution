Instead of using a separate state register, use a direction flag to indicate whether the wave value is incrementing or decrementing. Use a single always block triggered on the positive edge of clk and asynchronous reset. On reset, initialize wave to 0 and direction to increment (1). During operation, increment or decrement wave based on direction. When the wave reaches the upper or lower bounds (31 or 0), reverse the direction. This approach removes the explicit state machine and leverages a direction signal, resulting in clean and efficient logic.
