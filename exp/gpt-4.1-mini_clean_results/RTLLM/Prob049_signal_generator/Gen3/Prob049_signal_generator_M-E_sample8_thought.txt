Instead of using an explicit state register, use a direction bit named "dir" that directly controls whether the wave is incremented or decremented. The direction is toggled when the wave reaches either boundary (0 or 31). This simplifies control logic by eliminating the need for a case statement on state, replacing it with a simple if-else block, improving readability while preserving functionality. The wave and dir registers are updated synchronously on clock edges and reset asynchronously via rst_n.
