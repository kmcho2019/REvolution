The original design is functionally correct and clear, using a two-state FSM to generate the triangle waveform. To optimize for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), the design can be simplified by merging the combinational next-state logic into the sequential always block, eliminating the separate next_state and next_wave registers. This reduces logic and register usage slightly. Also, we can use the 'state' register as a direction bit with explicit comments and ensure nonblocking assignments for better synthesis results. This approach keeps the logic simple, lowers latency by avoiding extra combinational paths, and reduces power by minimizing toggles in extra signals. The waveform updates are straightforward increments/decrements conditioned on the state and wave value. This optimization balances clarity and resource use without changing behavior.
