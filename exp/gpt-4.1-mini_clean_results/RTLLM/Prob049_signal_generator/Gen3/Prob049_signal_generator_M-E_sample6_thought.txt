Implement the triangle wave generator by using a direction flag register instead of a multi-state register. On reset, initialize the wave to 0 and direction to increment (1). On each clock, update wave by adding or subtracting 1 depending on the direction. When wave hits the boundary (0 or 31), invert the direction flag. This approach avoids using a case statement and leverages arithmetic and conditional operators to achieve the waveform generation, resulting in a compact and efficient design.
