Use a single always block triggered on posedge clk and negedge rst_n to synchronously update both wave and state. Instead of explicitly saturating wave at boundaries, use an increment/decrement step variable (+1 or -1) which flips sign when the wave reaches 0 or 31, creating a natural triangle wave. This eliminates the need for a separate state register and a case statement. On reset, initialize wave and step to zero and +1 respectively. This approach reduces state logic to a single signed step variable, simplifies the control flow, and naturally produces the desired waveform through addition. It also minimizes conditional branches and helps in timing and area efficiency.
