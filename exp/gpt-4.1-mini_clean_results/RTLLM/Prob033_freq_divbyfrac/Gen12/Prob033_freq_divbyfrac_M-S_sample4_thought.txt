Generate clk_intA as high for 4 cycles and low for 3 cycles.

Create clk_intB by delaying clk_intA by one clock cycle (sample clk_intA on the next rising edge). This effectively shifts clk_intA by one input clock period, which approximates a half cycle shift when combined with both edges.

Finally, OR clk_intA and clk_intB to fill in the gaps and generate a smoother fractional clock output with an overall divide-by-3.5 behavior.
