To accurately implement the 3.5x fractional frequency divider with improved duty cycle and uniform output period, the design must leverage double-edge clocking to create a half-cycle phase shift.

- Toggle `clk_raw_pos` at counter values 3 and 6 on posedge clk to produce 4 and 3 cycle intervals.
- Toggle `clk_raw_neg` at the same counts but synchronized on negedge clk, introducing a half-cycle phase shift.

This approach uses the inherent half-cycle phase difference between posedge and negedge clocks to create the required fractional division without relying on delayed versions or XOR tricks. It ensures balanced duty cycle and a clean fractional output clock suitable for FPGA/ASIC implementation.
