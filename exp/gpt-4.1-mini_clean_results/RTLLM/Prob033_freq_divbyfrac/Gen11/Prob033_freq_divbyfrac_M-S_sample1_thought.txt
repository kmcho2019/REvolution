Simplify the fractional divider by using a modulo-7 counter and a flip-flop toggled at specific counts to generate the divided clock.

- Use a 3-bit counter counting 0 to 6 (mod 7).

- Toggle the output clock (clk_div) twice per 7 input cycles to achieve divide-by-3.5 frequency division:
  - Once at count 0 (start of long pulse)
  - Once at count 4 (start of short pulse)

- The output clock thus has two half-periods:
  - High for 4 input clocks (counts 0 to 3)
  - Low for 3 input clocks (counts 4 to 6)

- This approach inherently produces the desired fractional frequency with a 50% duty cycle over the full period (7 input clocks), using a single flip-flop and simpler logic.

- No double-edge clocking or phase-shifted clocks are needed, reducing complexity and improving robustness.

- Reset synchronously clears counter and output clock.
