Refactor the fractional frequency divider by using a single 3-bit counter that increments on the positive edge of `clk` and wraps modulo 7. From this counter, generate two pulse signals: 

- `clk_int_a` is high for counts 0..3 (4 cycles), 
- `clk_int_b` is high for counts 3..5 (3 cycles), but shifted by half a clock period.

To implement the half clock period phase shift for `clk_int_b` without using negedge clock domain, generate `clk_int_b` by sampling the counter at the negative edge of `clk`, synchronizing it back to positive edge domain to avoid asynchronous issues. This creates a deterministic half-clock phase shift.

Combine these two pulses with an OR gate to produce `clk_div` with approximate 3.5x division and roughly 50% duty cycle.

This structure avoids independent counters on posedge and negedge clocks, eliminates asynchronous resets, and uses a single synchronous counter plus registered delayed versions for phase shift, simplifying timing and improving reliability.

All logic and registers use synchronous reset for predictable initialization.
