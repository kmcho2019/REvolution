To implement a fractional frequency divider dividing by 3.5x using double-edge clocking, we double the count period to 7 (to represent 3.5*2). We create a counter that counts from 0 to 6. We generate two clock signals: one toggles after 4 cycles, the other after 3 cycles, alternatingly, to realize the uneven clock cycle lengths needed. Then, by using double-edge clocking, we create two phase-shifted signals: one aligned with the clock and the other delayed by half a clock period. Finally, OR-ing these two signals produces a fractional divided clock with improved duty cycle and uniform period. The reset initializes the counter and output signals.
