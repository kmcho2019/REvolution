To achieve a fractional frequency division by 3.5 using a double-edge clocking technique, the design uses a 7-cycle counter that produces uneven high and low periods (4 and 3 cycles). It then creates two versions of the divided clock: one normal and one phase-shifted by half the input clock period. By combining these two clocks using an OR operation, the output clock achieves an averaged duty cycle closer to 50% and the effective frequency division of 3.5. The phase-shifting is implemented by triggering on opposite clock edges for the two versions.
