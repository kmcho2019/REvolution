To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we use a modulo-7 counter (since 3.5 * 2 = 7). The divided clock period spans 7 input clock cycles: 4 cycles for the high phase and 3 cycles for the low phase, or vice versa, to create a non-50% duty cycle signal. To improve the duty cycle and uniformity, two complementary clocks are generated: one synchronized with the rising edge of the input clock and the other synchronized with the falling edge. By OR-ing these two clocks, the output achieves a more uniform fractional frequency division with reduced duty cycle distortion. The module uses an active low reset, counts input clock cycles, toggles the clock signals at the right counts, and produces the final divided clock output.
