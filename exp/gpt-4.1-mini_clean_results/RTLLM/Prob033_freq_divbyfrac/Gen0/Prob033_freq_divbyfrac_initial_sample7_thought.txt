To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we count 7 input clock cycles and generate two pulse widths: 4 cycles high and 3 cycles high, alternating each period. We create two intermediate signals: one directly from the counter and another phase-shifted by half a clock cycle, implemented by toggling on both rising and falling edges. Then, we OR these two signals to generate the final output clock with an averaged 3.5 division factor and a balanced duty cycle.
