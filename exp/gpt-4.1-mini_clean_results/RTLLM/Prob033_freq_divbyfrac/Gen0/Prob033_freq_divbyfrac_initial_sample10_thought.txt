To create a frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we count the input clock cycles up to 7 (since 3.5 * 2 = 7). The divided clock toggles with uneven high and low periods (4 and 3 cycles). We generate two versions of this divided clock: one aligned with the input clock edges, and another phase-shifted by half a clock cycle (achieved by sampling on opposite clock edges and delaying signals). By OR-ing these two signals, we get a fractional clock output with a uniform period and balanced duty cycle. The reset initializes the counter and output signals properly.
