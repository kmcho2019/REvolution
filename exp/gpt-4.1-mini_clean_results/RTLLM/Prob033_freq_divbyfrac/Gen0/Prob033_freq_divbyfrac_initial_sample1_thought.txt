To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 (i.e., 7/2), use a counter that counts 7 input clock cycles. Generate a divided clock signal that is high for 4 cycles and low for 3 cycles to create a base frequency division by 7. Then use double-edge clocking to produce two phase-shifted versions of this base divided clock by sampling it on both edges of the input clock (one delayed and one advanced by half a clock period). Finally, OR these two signals to create a 3.5x divided output with improved duty cycle and uniform frequency. The asynchronous reset resets the counter and outputs.
