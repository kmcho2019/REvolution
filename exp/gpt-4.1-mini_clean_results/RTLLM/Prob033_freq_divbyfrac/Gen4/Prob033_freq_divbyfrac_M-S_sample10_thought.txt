Implement a fractional frequency divider by using a 3-bit synchronous counter counting from 0 to 6, representing 7 base clock cycles. Generate two pulse signals:

- `clk_int_a`: high when count is 0 to 3 (4 cycles)
- `clk_int_b`: high when count is 4 to 6 (3 cycles)

To achieve the half-clock phase shift for `clk_int_b` without asynchronous sampling, use a 1-bit register toggling at the negative edge of the clock to create a half-cycle delayed enable for `clk_int_b`. This effectively phase-shifts the second pulse by half clock cycle.

Combine `clk_int_a` and `clk_int_b` with an OR operation to produce the final output clock `clk_div`.

This approach uses only synchronous logic with the positive edge clock domain for the counter, and a simple negative edge flip-flop for phase shifting. It avoids metastability, asynchronous sampling, and complex synchronization, providing a clean fractional clock output dividing by 3.5 with near 50% duty cycle.
