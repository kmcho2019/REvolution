Simplify the fractional clock divider by using a single counter that counts 7 input clock cycles. Generate two intermediate clocks with different duty cycles and phase shifts by controlling their toggle points within the cycle. Instead of using separate counters on posedge and negedge clocks, generate both clk_div_rise and clk_div_fall in the posedge clock domain, with clk_div_fall delayed by half a clock cycle using a simple flip-flop.

This avoids mixed clock edge domains and synchronization issues. The clk_div_rise is high for counts 0-3, low for 4-6, and clk_div_fall is a delayed version of clk_div_rise by half clk period (achieved by sampling clk_div_rise on the opposite clock edge). OR-ing these two signals produces a fractional clock divided by 3.5 with balanced duty cycle and minimal complexity.

Advantages:
- Single counter on posedge clk
- clk_div_fall generated by delaying clk_div_rise by half clock period (using a flip-flop clocked on negedge clk)
- No separate counters on negedge clk, reducing complexity and synchronization issues
- Clean, glitch-free fractional clock output with OR of both phases
