To achieve a precise fractional frequency division by 3.5 using a digital-only approach without relying on analog phase shifting or sampling at the negedge, implement a time-multiplexed clock enable scheme.

The idea is to use a modulo-7 counter generating a periodic pattern of enable pulses that create an output clock with one high pulse every 2 input clock cycles on average, but arranged unevenly to yield an effective division by 3.5.

Specifically:
- The counter counts from 0 to 6.
- Generate a clock enable (clk_en) that is asserted on 4 of the 7 counts, spaced to evenly distribute pulses.
- Use this enable signal to toggle the output clock on each input clock edge where clk_en is high.
- This toggling with non-uniform enable intervals produces an output clock with half the frequency of the enable pulses, effectively dividing the input clock by 3.5.
- This approach avoids generating two separate clocks and OR-ing them, improving timing integrity.
- The duty cycle will be close to 50% and frequency will be exactly input_clk / 3.5.

This solution requires only a counter and a toggling flip-flop clocked by the main clock gated by a designed enable pattern.
