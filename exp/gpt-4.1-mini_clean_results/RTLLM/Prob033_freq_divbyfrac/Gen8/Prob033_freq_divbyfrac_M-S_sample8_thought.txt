To simplify fractional division by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, leverage a single 3-bit counter running on the positive edge of clk to cycle through 7 states. Generate two intermediate clocks:

- clk_pos: high for 4 counts (0-3), low for 3 counts (4-6)
- clk_neg: delayed by half a clock cycle relative to clk_pos, realized by sampling clk_pos on the falling edge of clk

This effectively phase-shifts clk_neg by half a clock period relative to clk_pos.

Finally, OR clk_pos and clk_neg to get clk_div with near 50% duty cycle at clk/3.5 frequency.

This approach uses only one counter and two flip-flops clocked on opposite edges to create the needed phase shift and fractional division, significantly reducing complexity while maintaining the desired output clock quality.
