To create the half-cycle phase shift for clk_intB without using negedge triggers or explicit delays, gate clk_intB with the inverted input clock. Then OR clk_intA and the half-cycle shifted clk_intB to form the fractional divide-by-3.5 clock. This method is fully synchronous on the posedge clk, avoids metastability, and uses the input clock's inversion to shift clk_intB by half a clock cycle, resulting in a smooth fractional output.
