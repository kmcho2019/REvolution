To fix the previous design and achieve an accurate fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, the following improvements will be made:

1. **Single Counter Driven on Positive Edge:**  
   Use a single modulo-7 counter driven by the positive edge of the input clock. This avoids issues related to separate counters on posedge and negedge, which may drift or cause misalignment.

2. **Generate `clk_div_even` on Positive Edge:**  
   Generate the first intermediate clock (`clk_div_even`) toggling at counts that create a high phase of 4 cycles and low phase of 3 cycles, toggling at counts 3 and 6 on the positive clock edge.

3. **Generate `clk_div_odd` by Sampling `clk_div_even` on Negative Edge:**  
   Instead of maintaining an independent counter on the negative edge, generate the phase-shifted clock (`clk_div_odd`) by sampling the `clk_div_even` signal at the negative edge of the input clock. This approach effectively creates a half-cycle delayed version of `clk_div_even`, simulating the half-period phase shift without complicated dual counters.

4. **Use a Register to Hold `clk_div_odd`:**  
   Register `clk_div_even` on the negative edge to produce `clk_div_odd`.

5. **OR both intermediate clocks to produce final output:**  
   `clk_div = clk_div_even | clk_div_odd` results in a clock with a doubled number of toggles per period, smoothing the uneven duty cycle and approximating the half-integer division.

6. **Synchronous Reset:**  
   Reset all registers asynchronously to known initial states to avoid startup glitches.

This approach simplifies synchronization, ensures proper phase alignment between the two intermediate clocks, and prevents glitches or non-deterministic timing from independent counters. The sampling of `clk_div_even` at negedge to create `clk_div_odd` provides the required half-cycle phase shift in a clean and robust manner.

Overall, this design cleanly implements the fractional divide-by-3.5 with a nearly 50% duty cycle output clock and matches the problem statement requirements.
