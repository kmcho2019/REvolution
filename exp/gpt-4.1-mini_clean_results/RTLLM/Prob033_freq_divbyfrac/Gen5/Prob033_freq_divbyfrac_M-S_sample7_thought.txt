Implement a fractional clock divider by 3.5 using a simple 3-bit counter cycling through 0 to 6 (7 counts). Generate a base divided clock `clk_int_a` that is high for counts 0 to 3 (4 cycles) and low for counts 4 to 6 (3 cycles), producing an uneven duty cycle.

Create the phase-shifted clock `clk_int_b` by simply inverting `clk_int_a` on every positive clock edge, but delayed by one clock cycle (using a register). This effectively shifts `clk_int_a` by about half a clock period, approximating the needed phase shift without complex negedge sampling.

Finally, OR `clk_int_a` and `clk_int_b` to get the fractional divided clock output `clk_div` with improved duty cycle uniformity and stable timing, avoiding mixed-edge clocking and simplifying the logic.

Reset is synchronous, and all registers update on posedge clk for clean and safe timing.
