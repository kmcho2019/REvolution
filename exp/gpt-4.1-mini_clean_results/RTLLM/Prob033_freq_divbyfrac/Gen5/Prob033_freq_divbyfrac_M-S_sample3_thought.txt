Implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5x using a simple 7-step counter clocked at the input clock's positive edge. Generate two internal clock signals (clk_a and clk_b) with uneven high times of 4 and 3 clock cycles respectively by toggling them at counts 0 and 4 (for clk_a) and counts 3 and 0 (for clk_b). Phase-shift clk_b by half a clock period using a delayed version of clk. Combine clk_a and the phase-shifted clk_b with an OR gate to create a final fractional clock output with a uniform period and improved duty cycle. This approach avoids double-edge triggered logic and asynchronous reset, simplifying design and improving synthesis compatibility while maintaining fractional division accuracy and good duty cycle.
