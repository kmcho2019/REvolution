Implement the fractional frequency divider by using a single counter that counts from 0 to 6 (total 7 cycles for 3.5 division). On the positive edge of clk, increment this counter. Generate two signals toggling at different counts to represent the "high" intervals of the divided clock: one covers 4 cycles, the other 3 cycles offset by half a clock. By combining these two signals (effectively using both edges of the input clock), the output clk_div achieves a 3.5 division with balanced duty cycle. This approach uses minimal logic, avoids separate positive and negative edge counters, and leverages the natural half-clock period offset from the edges to realize the fractional division cleanly.
