Implement fractional division by 3.5 using a 7-cycle counter and generate two complementary intermediate clocks (clk_intA and clk_intB) with uneven high periods (4 and 3 cycles). Then, create two phase-shifted versions of these clocks by sampling them at both the positive and negative edges of the input clock. By XOR-ing or OR-ing these shifted signals properly, we synthesize a final output clock with an effective half-clock phase shift, achieving a smooth 3.5x division with improved duty cycle. This approach uses double-edge clocking to generate the fractional frequency output without requiring PLLs or external phase shifters.
