To improve the fractional divider implementation and fix timing and synchronization issues, the design will be refactored to use a single free-running counter that increments on the rising edge of `clk` and resets asynchronously.

We will then generate the two intermediate clocks (`clk_intA` and `clk_intB`) from this single counter with combinational logic to create exact timing windows for high periods. 

`clk_intA` will be a pulse with high duration over counts [0..3] (4 cycles) and `clk_intB` will be generated by phase-shifting the count by half clock cycle using a 1-bit delayed signal and be high over counts [3..5] (3 cycles), simulating the falling-edge sampling. 

This way, no separate counters on different clock edges are needed; the phase shift is realized by delaying and advancing the signals logically with combinational and registered signals on the same clock domain, ensuring proper phase alignment.

`clk_div` will be the OR of `clk_intA` and `clk_intB`, producing a 3.5x frequency division with a balanced duty cycle.

This approach eliminates asynchronous dual counters, simplifies timing, and improves signal stability and simulation results.
