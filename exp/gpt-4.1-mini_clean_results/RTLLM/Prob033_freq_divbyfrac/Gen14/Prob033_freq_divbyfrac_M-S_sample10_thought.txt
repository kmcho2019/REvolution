Implement the fractional divider by using a single 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 6 (7 counts total). Generate two internal clock phases directly as combinational signals from the counter: 

- clk_intA is high for counts 0 to 3 (4 cycles),
- clk_intB is high for counts 4 to 6 (3 cycles).

Then generate clk_intB as a delayed version of clk_intA by registering clk_intA on the falling edge of the input clock. This produces a half-cycle phase shift.

Finally, OR clk_intA and clk_intB to get a uniformly spaced output clock with 3.5 division ratio.

This simplified approach eliminates unnecessary registers and avoids cross-clock edge counter sampling, keeping phase alignment clean and code minimal.
