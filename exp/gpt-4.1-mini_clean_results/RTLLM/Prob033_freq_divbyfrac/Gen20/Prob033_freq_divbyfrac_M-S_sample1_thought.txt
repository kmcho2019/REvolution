To simplify the fractional divide-by-3.5 clock generation, we leverage the double-edge clocking technique directly by toggling one intermediate clock on the positive edge and the other on the negative edge of the input clock, effectively introducing the required half-clock phase shift without complex counters.

We implement a modulo-7 counter running on the positive edge to track 7 clock cycles, which represent the full period for the division.

- `clk_div_even` toggles every 4 positive edges (counts 0 and 4), producing a signal with a 4-cycle high and 3-cycle low pattern on the posedge clock.
- `clk_div_odd` toggles every 3 negative edges (counts 3 and 6) by using a counter derived from the positive edge counter, but toggled on negedge clk to get the half-cycle phase shift.

The final output is the OR of these two signals, resulting in a stable fractional divided clock at 1/3.5 frequency with uniform duty cycle and smooth edges.

This approach avoids dual counters, complicated clock enables, or asynchronous reset, making the design both conceptually simpler and functionally correct.

Reset synchronously initializes counters and flip-flops to zero for deterministic startup behavior.
