To fix the issues identified in the previous implementation and produce a stable, correct fractional divide-by-3.5 clock output with a near 50% duty cycle, the approach is refined as follows:

1. **Single modulo-7 counter increments on posedge clk with asynchronous reset.**  
   The counter cycles from 0 to 6 to represent the 7 input clock cycles in one output period.

2. **Generate `clk_div_even` toggled at counts 0 and 4 on the posedge clk.**  
   This yields a 4-cycle high and 3-cycle low period in one cycle.

3. **Generate `clk_div_odd` toggled at counts 3 and 0 on the posedge clk, but shifted in time by half a clock cycle using a half-period delayed clock domain.**  
   To achieve half-cycle delay without metastability issues or asynchronous sampling of `cnt_pos` at negedge, create a *half-clock-period delayed version of clk* using a simple inverter or an internal PLL/BUF.  
   
   Because the problem requests a design without special clock management, we implement a **half-cycle delayed domain by using the negedge of clk as the clock for clk_div_odd toggling**, but do **not** sample `cnt_pos` asynchronously at negedge. Instead, we generate `cnt_neg` by re-counting in the negedge domain or use a phase-aligned toggle enable signal derived from posedge logic synchronized to negedge.

4. **Implement a gray-coded or toggling enable signal at posedge clock that is safely transferred to negedge domain** for use in the toggling condition on negedge clk to prevent metastability or timing mismatches. Alternatively, calculate toggling conditions for clk_div_odd directly on negedge clk using a dedicated negedge counter that increments with each negedge clk, modulo 7, aligned with the posedge counter.

5. **Create two independent modulo-7 counters: one on posedge clk (`cnt_pos`), one on negedge clk (`cnt_neg`).**  
   This approach avoids asynchronous sampling and ensures each toggling signal (`clk_div_even` and `clk_div_odd`) is correctly phase-aligned in its respective clock domain.

6. **Toggle `clk_div_even` at counts 0 and 4 on posedge clk, and toggle `clk_div_odd` at counts 3 and 0 on negedge clk.**  
   This produces the required uneven high/low periods in their respective domains, phase shifted by half a clock cycle.

7. **Finally, OR the two clocks to produce the final fractional divided output `clk_div`.**

This fix addresses all issues:

- No asynchronous sampling between clock edges.  
- Stable and synchronized toggling of intermediate clocks in their respective clock domains.  
- The OR-ing of two complementary phase-shifted clocks yields a smooth 3.5 division with near 50% duty cycle.  
- Asynchronous active-low reset initializes all registers.

This design is synthesizable and robust across typical FPGA/ASIC flows and tools without requiring specialized clock generation hardware.
