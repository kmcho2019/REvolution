To implement a robust and accurate fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking and phase-shifted intermediate clocks, I will:

1. Use a modulo-7 counter that increments on each rising edge of the input clock.

2. Generate two intermediate clocks:
   - clk_div1 toggles every 4 and 3 clock cycles alternately (uneven periods), representing uneven cycle lengths (4 and 3 clocks).
   - clk_div2 is the same as clk_div1 but phase-shifted by half a clock period, achieved by toggling on the falling edge of clk instead of rising edge.

3. The final output clk_div is generated by OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. This OR operation effectively produces a clock with half-integer division and a more uniform duty cycle.

4. Synchronize reset to ensure proper initialization.

5. All registers are driven in synchronous always blocks without mixing posedge and negedge in the same block.

6. The toggling points are:
   - For clk_div1 (posedge-driven): toggle when cnt hits values corresponding to 3 and 7 cycles (4 and 3 clock counts in pattern).
   - For clk_div2 (negedge-driven): toggle when cnt hits the alternate values to phase-shift clk_div1 by half a clock.
