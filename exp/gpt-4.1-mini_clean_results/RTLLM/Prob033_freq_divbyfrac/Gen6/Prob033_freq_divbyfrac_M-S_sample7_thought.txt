Implement the 3.5x fractional frequency divider by using a 3-bit counter cycling from 0 to 6. Toggle a single internal clock signal (`clk_int`) at count 0 and count 4 to generate uneven half-cycle lengths (4 and 3 cycles) for the divided clock. Use the negative edge of the input clock to toggle a second internal clock (`clk_int_delayed`), also at count 3 and 6, creating a half-cycle phase shift relative to `clk_int`.

The output clock (`clk_div`) is the OR of these two signals, combining them to produce a uniform 3.5x divided clock with improved duty cycle and correct fractional frequency division.

This approach cleanly separates the phase-shifted clocks by using both edges of `clk` without complex logic, achieving the fractional division with simple counters and toggles, while reducing code complexity.
