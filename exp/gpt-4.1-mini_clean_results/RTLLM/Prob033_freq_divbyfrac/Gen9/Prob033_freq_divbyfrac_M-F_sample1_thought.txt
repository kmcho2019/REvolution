The main issue in the original design arises from sampling the combinational clk_intB signal, which depends on the synchronous counter, on the negedge of clk directly. This causes timing misalignments and glitches because clk_intB_comb transitions only on posedge clk, so its value may change too late or too early relative to the negedge clk sampling, resulting in metastability or incorrect values.

To fix this, the key idea is to decouple the generation of clk_intB from the combinational counter-based signal and create two separate counters/registers that directly track the phases for clk_intA and clk_intB signals, both running synchronously with the clock but shifted in phase. Instead of directly sampling the combinational clk_intB_comb on negedge clk, we create a "shifted" version of the counter for clk_intB that runs on negedge clk, ensuring that clk_intB_neg is a stable, synchronous signal aligned with negedge clk.

Concretely:

1. Use one 3-bit counter counting 0..6 on posedge clk for clk_intA generation, as before.

2. Create a second 3-bit counter counting 0..6 on negedge clk for clk_intB generation, effectively generating clk_intB_neg that is phase shifted by half a clock cycle.

3. Generate clk_intA from the posedge clk counter as high for counts 0..3 (4 cycles).

4. Generate clk_intB from the negedge clk counter as high for counts 0..2 (3 cycles).

5. Finally, OR clk_intA and clk_intB to form the fractional clk_div output.

This way, both clk_intA and clk_intB are generated synchronously on their respective clock edges, avoiding asynchronous sampling of combinational signals.

Handling reset synchronously on both edges also ensures clean initialization without metastability.

This dual-counter double-edge approach yields two clock pulses of 4 and 3 cycles shifted by half a cycle, giving a stable 3.5 division ratio without glitches or metastability.

Thus, the code will have two counters clocked on opposite edges, generating clk_intA and clk_intB, which are ORed to produce the fractional divided clock output.
