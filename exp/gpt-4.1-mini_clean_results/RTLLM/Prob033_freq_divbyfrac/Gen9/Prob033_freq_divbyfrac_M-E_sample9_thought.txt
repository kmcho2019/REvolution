To achieve the half-cycle phase shift needed for double-edge clocking, instantiate a second flip-flop `clk_div_n` clocked on the negative edge of the input clock that follows `clk_div_p` with a half-clock delay.

By OR-ing the outputs of these two registers (`clk_div_p` and `clk_div_n`), a smoother fractional divided clock output is produced with a more uniform duty cycle, effectively implementing a divide-by-3.5 clock using double-edge clocking.
