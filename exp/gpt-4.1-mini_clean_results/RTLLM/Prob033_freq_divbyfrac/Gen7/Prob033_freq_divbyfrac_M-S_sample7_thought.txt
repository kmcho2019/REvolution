To simplify while preserving the fractional divide-by-3.5 functionality, keep the single posedge clock domain and a modulo-7 counter. Generate two non-overlapping clocks representing 4 and 3 cycle pulses directly from the counter. Instead of registering clk_intB at negedge, create a half-cycle delayed version of clk_intB by using the inverted clk as a gating signal, effectively producing a 50% duty cycle fractional clock when OR'ed with clk_intA.

This approach:
- Removes negedge sampling and potential metastability.
- Uses the clock itself and its inversion for phase shifting.
- Keeps all logic synchronous with posedge clk, avoiding clock domain crossing.
- Produces a clean fractional divide clock output with minimal complexity.

The key idea is that clk_intB is gated by the inverted clk, shifting it by half a clock period without extra flip-flops or edges.

This simplification maintains a 3.5 division factor and smooth duty cycle with minimal resource usage.
