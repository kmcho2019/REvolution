To simplify the fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we can use a single 3-bit counter counting from 0 to 6 on the positive clock edge.

- For the positive edge domain, generate clk_div_pos high for counts 0 to 3 (4 cycles).
- For the negative edge domain, sample the counter at the negedge to create clk_div_neg high for counts 4 to 6 (3 cycles).
- Then OR clk_div_pos and clk_div_neg to produce clk_div.

This creates two non-overlapping pulses (4 cycles and 3 cycles) shifted by half clock period, yielding a total period of 7 clock cycles, effectively dividing the input clock by 3.5 with more balanced duty cycle.

Reset synchronously initializes counters.

This approach avoids overlapping high intervals, is simpler with no extra phase shifting, and uses only the counter value sampled on negedge for clk_div_neg generation.
