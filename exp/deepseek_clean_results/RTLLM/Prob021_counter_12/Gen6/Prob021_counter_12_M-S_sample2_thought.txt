The counter can be further simplified by:
1. Removing the explicit reset-to-zero logic since registers initialize to zero by default in Verilog (Xilinx FPGA behavior)
2. Maintaining only the essential counting logic with valid_count enable
3. Letting the counter naturally wrap around from 11 to 0 due to 4-bit overflow
4. This reduces the logic to just a single conditional increment operation

Note: This assumes the target platform initializes registers to zero. For ASICs or platforms requiring explicit reset, the original solution should be kept.
