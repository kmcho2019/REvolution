The current implementation uses clock gating which is not recommended for synchronous designs. Here's the improved strategy:

1. Performance:
   - Remove gated clock and use synchronous enable (valid_count) for better timing
   - Keep the efficient wrap-around logic using pattern matching (4'b1011)
   - Maintain asynchronous reset for predictable initialization

2. Power:
   - Use synchronous enable instead of clock gating to reduce power safely
   - The counter will naturally stop when valid_count=0 without extra logic

3. Area:
   - Simplify the design by removing clock gating logic
   - Let synthesis tool optimize the counter increment and wrap-around logic

The improved version will be more robust while maintaining good PPA metrics.
