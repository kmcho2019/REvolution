The design requires a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 11 (inclusive) and wraps around. The counter should:
1. Reset to 0 when rst_n is low (active low reset)
2. Increment on each clock edge when valid_count is high
3. Pause counting when valid_count is low
4. Automatically wrap around to 0 after reaching 11 (4'd11)

The implementation will use synchronous reset and clocked logic to ensure proper timing. The counter value will be stored in a 4-bit register (out) that updates on the positive clock edge based on the conditions above.
