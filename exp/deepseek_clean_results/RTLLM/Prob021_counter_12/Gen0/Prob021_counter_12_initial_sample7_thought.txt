The design requires a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 11 (inclusive) and wraps around. The counter should only increment when valid_count is high, pause when valid_count is low, and reset asynchronously when rst_n is low. The implementation will use a synchronous always block triggered by the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of reset. Inside the block, we'll first handle the reset condition, then check valid_count to decide whether to increment or hold the current value, making sure to wrap around when reaching 11.
