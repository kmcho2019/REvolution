The counter needs to:
1. Reset to 0 when rst_n is low (active low reset)
2. Increment on each clock cycle when valid_count is high
3. Wrap around to 0 when reaching 4'd11 (binary 1011)
4. Hold its value when valid_count is low

This can be implemented with a synchronous always block that checks rst_n first (highest priority), then checks valid_count to determine whether to increment or hold the count value. The wrap-around condition is handled by checking if the current count equals 11 before incrementing.
