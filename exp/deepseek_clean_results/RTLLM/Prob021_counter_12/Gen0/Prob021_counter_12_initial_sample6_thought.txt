The design implements a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 11 (0xC) and wraps around. The counter is synchronous with the clock (clk) and has an active-low reset (rst_n). The counting is enabled by the valid_count signal. When valid_count is high, the counter increments on each clock edge. When valid_count is low, the counter holds its current value. On reset (rst_n low), the counter synchronously resets to 0.
