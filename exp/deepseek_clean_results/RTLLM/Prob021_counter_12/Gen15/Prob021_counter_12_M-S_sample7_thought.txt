To further simplify the counter design while maintaining all functionality:
1. Keep the natural overflow behavior of 4-bit arithmetic (no explicit comparison needed)
2. Remove redundant logic by combining reset and enable conditions in a single always block
3. Maintain minimal control logic (only reset and enable signals)
4. The counter will automatically wrap from 11 to 0 due to 4-bit width limitation
5. All functionality remains identical to original requirements

Key simplification is recognizing that the 4-bit width inherently handles the 0-11 counting range without additional logic, and the enable/disable behavior can be handled with a simple conditional.
