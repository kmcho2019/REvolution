Refactoring Strategy:
1. Maintain synchronous design principles by removing the gated clock
2. Use a combinational always block for next-state logic
3. Separate the sequential and combinational logic for better readability
4. Keep the efficient wrap-around detection (pattern matching)
5. Use synchronous reset for better timing predictability
6. Implement the valid_count as a synchronous enable signal

Key Improvements:
- Eliminates gated clock issues while maintaining power efficiency
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Maintains all functional requirements
- Better timing characteristics with synchronous design
- More standard and maintainable structure
