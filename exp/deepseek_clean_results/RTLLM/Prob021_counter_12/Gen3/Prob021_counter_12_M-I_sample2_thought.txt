To optimize the counter design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Replace the 4-bit comparator with a simpler terminal count detection (checking for binary '1011' pattern)
   - Use a direct assignment for wrap-around to eliminate the mux delay

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating when valid_count is low to prevent unnecessary toggling
   - Use a simpler comparison that requires fewer gate operations

3. Area Optimization:
   - Simplify the terminal count detection logic
   - Remove redundant logic by combining conditions

The key insight is that for counting to 11 (4'b1011), we only need to check bits 3 and 1 being high (4'b1X1X) since no other number in 0-11 has this pattern. This reduces the comparison logic significantly.
