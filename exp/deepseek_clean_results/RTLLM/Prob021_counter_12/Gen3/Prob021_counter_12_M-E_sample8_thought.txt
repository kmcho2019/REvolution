Here's a novel approach using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) style counter with custom polynomial for counting 0-11. This method offers several advantages:
1. Reduced combinational logic compared to binary counters
2. Natural wrap-around behavior without explicit comparison
3. Lower power consumption due to fewer bit toggles
4. Simplified terminal count detection

The key innovation is using a carefully chosen 4-bit LFSR sequence that covers exactly 12 states (0-11) before repeating. We'll use the polynomial x^4 + x^3 + 1 which generates a maximal length sequence of 15, but we'll modify it to stop at 11 by adding a custom wrap condition.
