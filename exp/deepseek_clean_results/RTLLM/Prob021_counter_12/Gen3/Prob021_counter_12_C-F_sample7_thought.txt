Fusion strategy combining the best aspects of both examples:

2. Timing Optimization: Simplify the comparison logic by using direct numeric comparison (4'd11) instead of binary pattern matching

4. Power Efficiency: Maintain the enable-based counting control (valid_count) for power savings
5. Reset Handling: Keep asynchronous reset for reliable initialization

- Single always block for better area efficiency (from Example 1)
- Numeric comparison for potentially better synthesis (4'd11 vs 4'b1011)

- Clear synchronous enable control
