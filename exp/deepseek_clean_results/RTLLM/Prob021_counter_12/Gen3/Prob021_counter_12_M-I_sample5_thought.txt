Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Eliminate the separate next_out register to reduce critical path
   - Move logic directly into sequential block to reduce combinational delay
   - Simplify comparison logic by using direct 4'd11 constant

2. Area Optimization:
   - Remove redundant next_out register
   - Simplify control logic by combining conditions

3. Power Efficiency:
   - Maintain synchronous design without clock gating (already good power)
   - Keep enable signal (valid_count) simple for synthesis optimization

Key Changes:
- Convert to single always block design for better timing
- Direct comparison with 4'd11 instead of 4'b1011 for clarity
- Remove intermediate next_out register
- Maintain same functionality with more efficient implementation
