The original implementation can be simplified by removing the explicit comparison with 4'd11. Since we're using a 4-bit counter, it will naturally wrap around after reaching 15 (4'b1111). We can modify the counting logic to automatically reset when reaching 12 (4'b1100) by using a bitwise AND operation after incrementing. This eliminates the comparator logic while maintaining the same functionality. The reset and enable conditions remain the same.
