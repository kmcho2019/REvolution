The original implementation is functionally correct but shows minor timing violations (negative WNS). To optimize PPA:

1. Timing Improvement:
- Pre-compute the terminal count (tc) signal combinationally to reduce critical path
- This avoids the comparator in the synchronous path

2. Power Optimization:
- Maintain clock gating through valid_count (already implemented)
- No additional power savings needed for this simple design

3. Area Optimization:
- The design is already minimal (44um²)
- Could use gray coding but not worth complexity for 4-bit counter

The improved version uses a pre-computed terminal count signal to reduce the critical path while maintaining all functionality.
