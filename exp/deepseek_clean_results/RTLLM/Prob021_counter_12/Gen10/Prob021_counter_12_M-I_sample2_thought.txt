   - Use synchronous enable (valid_count) with main clock
   - Replace pattern detection with direct comparison (== 4'b1011) for better timing

2. Power Optimization:
   - Implement synchronous enable instead of clock gating
   - Simplify terminal count logic to reduce unnecessary switching

3. Area Optimization:
