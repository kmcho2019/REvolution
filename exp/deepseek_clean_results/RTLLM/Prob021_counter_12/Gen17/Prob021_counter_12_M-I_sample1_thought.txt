The current implementation has good power and area metrics but suffers from timing issues due to the gated clock approach. Here's the improved strategy:

1. Performance:
   - Replace gated clock with synchronous enable for better timing closure
   - Maintain the efficient wrap-around logic using pattern matching
   - Keep asynchronous reset for predictable initialization

2. Power:
   - Use synchronous enable (valid_count) to control counting
   - Maintain low-power characteristics by only updating registers when needed

3. Area:
   - Keep the compact implementation of counter logic
   - Remove gated clock circuitry to save area
   - Let synthesis tool optimize the increment and wrap-around logic

The improved version will:
- Remove timing risks from gated clock
- Maintain excellent power characteristics
- Potentially improve area by removing clock gating logic
- Keep the same functionality
