The current implementation has good power and area metrics but suffers from timing issues due to the gated clock approach. Here's the improved strategy:

1. Performance Improvement:
   - Replace gated clock with synchronous enable (valid_count) to eliminate clock skew risks
   - Maintain the efficient wrap-around logic using pattern matching
   - Keep the asynchronous reset for clean initialization

2. Power Optimization:
   - The synchronous enable approach still prevents unnecessary toggling when valid_count is low
   - Simplified comparison logic reduces switching activity

3. Area Efficiency:
   - Remove gated clock logic to save area
   - Let synthesis tool optimize the counter increment and wrap-around logic

The improved version will maintain the power and area benefits while solving the timing issues of the gated clock approach.
