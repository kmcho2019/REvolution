   - This reduces the critical path by removing wire delays

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - Remove the intermediate next_out wire and its associated logic
   - Simplify to a single always block with direct register updates

3. **Power Consideration**:
   - Keep the synchronous enable (valid_count) approach
   - Avoid clock gating since power is already better than reference

4. **Functionality Preservation**:
   - Maintain same reset behavior (async low)
   - Keep same counting sequence and wrap-around at 11
