Here's a novel approach using a Johnson counter with decode logic for counting to 12. This offers several advantages:

1. Novel Architecture:
   - Uses a 4-bit Johnson counter (ring counter with inverted feedback) that naturally cycles through 8 states
   - Additional decode logic extends this to 12 states by skipping 4 unused states
   - The Johnson counter's inherent glitch-free operation improves reliability

2. Performance Benefits:
   - Single-bit shifts are faster than binary increments
   - Decode logic operates in parallel with the counter
   - No carry propagation delay like in binary counters

3. Power Advantages:
   - Only one bit toggles per clock cycle (except during reset)
   - Simplified decode logic reduces switching activity

4. Area Efficiency:
   - Johnson counter requires minimal sequential logic
   - Decode logic is simple combinatorial gates
   - No need for complex comparators or adders

The key insight is that a 4-bit Johnson counter normally cycles through 8 states, but by carefully designing the decode logic, we can map this to our desired 12-count sequence while maintaining all the benefits of a Johnson counter.
