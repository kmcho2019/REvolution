The FSM approach in the original solution is unnecessarily complex for a simple counter. We can simplify this by using a standard binary counter with wrap-around logic. The counter will:
1. Reset to 0 when rst_n is low
2. Increment on each clock cycle when valid_count is high
3. Wrap around to 0 when reaching 11 (4'b1011)
4. Hold its value when valid_count is low
This approach uses simple arithmetic comparison and increment operations, which are more efficient in both area and timing compared to the FSM implementation.
