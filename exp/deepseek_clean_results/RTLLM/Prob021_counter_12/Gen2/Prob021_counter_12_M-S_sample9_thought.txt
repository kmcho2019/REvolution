The optimized design simplifies the counter logic by:
1. Removing the redundant "else out <= out" statement since registers hold their value by default
2. Using a simpler wrap-around check by directly comparing to 4'b1011 (11 in binary)
3. Combining the reset and increment logic more efficiently
4. Eliminating unnecessary mux logic by letting the register hold its value when valid_count is low
This reduces both area and power while maintaining the same functionality.
