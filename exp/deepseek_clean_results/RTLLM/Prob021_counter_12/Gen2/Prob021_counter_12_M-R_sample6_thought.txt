The refactored design will maintain identical functionality while improving readability and potential synthesis results by:
1. Using a separate combinational always block for next state logic
2. Moving the sequential logic to a separate always block
3. Using explicit if-else statements instead of ternary operators for clearer logic flow
4. Maintaining the same reset and count enable behavior
5. Keeping the 4-bit counter with wrap-around at 11 (4'b1011)
This structure makes the design intent clearer and may help synthesis tools optimize better.
