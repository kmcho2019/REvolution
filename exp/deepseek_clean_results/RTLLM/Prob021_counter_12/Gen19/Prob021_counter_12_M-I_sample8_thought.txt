Based on the feedback, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Remove clock gating and use synchronous enable for better timing and synthesis:
   - Eliminate gated clock to prevent skew issues
   - Use valid_count as a synchronous enable signal

2. Optimize terminal count detection:
   - Replace pattern matching with direct comparison (== 4'd11)
   - This simplifies the logic and improves timing

3. Separate reset logic from counting logic:
   - Make reset behavior more explicit
   - Allow synthesis tools to better optimize the design

4. Maintain power efficiency:
   - The synchronous enable still prevents unnecessary toggles
   - Simplified logic reduces switching activity

5. Area optimization:
   - Direct comparison may use fewer gates than pattern matching
   - Cleaner structure allows better synthesis optimizations

The improved design will be more robust for timing closure while maintaining good power and area characteristics.
