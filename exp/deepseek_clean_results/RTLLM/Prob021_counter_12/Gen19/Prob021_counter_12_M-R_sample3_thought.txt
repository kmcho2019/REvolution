This separates the combinational logic (next state calculation) from the sequential logic (register update), which can improve synthesis results and timing analysis. The key changes are:

1. Split the design into combinational next-state logic and sequential state update
2. Use an explicit next_state wire calculated with continuous assignments

- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Better timing predictability
- Easier synthesis optimization
