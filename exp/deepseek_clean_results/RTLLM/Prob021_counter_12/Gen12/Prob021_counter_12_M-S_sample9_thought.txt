Simplified design approach:
1. Remove clock gating (not recommended in synchronous designs)
2. Use synchronous reset for better consistency
3. Maintain simple increment and wrap-around logic
4. Keep the enable (valid_count) as a synchronous control signal
5. Use direct comparison with 4'd11 for clarity
6. All operations occur on clock edges for predictable timing

This maintains all required functionality while being simpler and more robust for synchronous designs.
