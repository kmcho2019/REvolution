The current implementation has good PPA metrics but can be further optimized based on the feedback:

1. Remove clock gating (gated_clk) to eliminate potential timing risks and reduce area
2. Use synchronous enable (valid_count) instead of clock gating for safer power reduction
3. Simplify wrap-around logic using a single conditional comparison
4. Keep synchronous reset for modern design practices
5. Maintain parameter for MAX_COUNT for flexibility

Key improvements:
- Better timing by removing combinational clock gating
- Reduced area by eliminating gated_clk register
- Maintained low power through synchronous enable
- Simpler and more reliable implementation
