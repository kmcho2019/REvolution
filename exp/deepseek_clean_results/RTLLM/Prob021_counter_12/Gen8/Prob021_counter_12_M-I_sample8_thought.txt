To optimize the counter design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality:

1. Timing Optimization:
- Remove the implicit wrap-around detection (which creates a long combinational path)
- Implement explicit comparison with 4'd11 (4'b1011) for clearer timing paths
- This makes the critical path more predictable for synthesis tools

2. Power Optimization:
- Add clock gating when valid_count is low to prevent unnecessary toggling
- Use a registered comparison to break timing paths

3. Area Optimization:
- Keep the simple sequential logic structure
- The explicit comparison adds minimal area overhead while improving timing

The improved version will:
- Reset to 0 when rst_n is low
