Novel Architectural Idea: Parallel-Prefix Radix-2 Division with Sign Preprocessing

Key Innovations:
1. Sign preprocessing stage that handles all sign-related calculations upfront
2. Parallel-prefix subtraction approach for faster carry propagation
3. Integrated quotient prediction logic to reduce critical path
4. Single-cycle result formation after computation
5. Optimized for minimal latency (8 cycles + 1 cycle sign correction)
6. Explicit divide-by-zero handling with special result encoding
7. Zero remainder early termination detection

Implementation Highlights:
- Stage 1: Sign extraction and absolute value conversion
- Stage 2: Parallel-prefix subtraction core with quotient prediction
- Stage 3: Final remainder adjustment and sign correction
- Uses 3-bit counter (optimized from 4-bit)
- Combines remainder and quotient in single shift register
- Includes pipeline registers for better timing
- Strictly follows specified interface without extra signals
