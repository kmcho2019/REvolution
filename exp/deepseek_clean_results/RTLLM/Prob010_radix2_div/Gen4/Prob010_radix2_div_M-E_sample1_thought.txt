Novel Architecture:
1. Pipelined Non-Restoring Division: Implement division in a pipelined fashion to improve throughput
2. Early Termination: Detect completion when remainder becomes zero to save power
3. Sign Pre-processing: Handle sign conversion in the first stage
4. Two-stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and sign handling
   - Stage 2: Division core with early termination
5. Optimized Datapath: Use carry-save arithmetic for faster subtraction
6. Result Formatting: Final stage handles sign correction and result packing

Key Innovations:
- Pipelined operation allows new operations to start before previous ones complete
- Early termination reduces power consumption for easy divisions
- Carry-save arithmetic improves critical path timing
- Clear separation between datapath and control logic
