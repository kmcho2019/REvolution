This design introduces a predictive carry-select architecture for division with these key innovations:

1. **Dual-Path Calculation**: Maintains both possible remainder paths (positive and negative) in parallel, selecting the correct one each cycle based on the sign prediction.

2. **Sign-Preserving Logic**: Implements early sign determination and propagation to minimize correction cycles at the end.

3. **Early Termination Detection**: Detects division completion conditions (like division by 1 or 0) within the first cycle to save power.

4. **Adaptive Shift Control**: Dynamically adjusts shift amounts based on leading zero detection to reduce unnecessary operations.

5. **Pipelined Result Formation**: Final quotient and remainder are assembled in parallel with the last division step.

The architecture provides better timing closure and lower average latency compared to traditional non-restoring dividers.
