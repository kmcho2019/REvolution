Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Dual-Path Processing: Separate signed and unsigned processing paths for better timing optimization
2. Early Termination: Detect division completion when remainder becomes zero
3. Parallel Subtraction: Use carry-save adder for faster subtraction in critical path
4. Sign Handling: Process signs in parallel with division operation
5. Pipelined Output: Separate result formation from core division logic

Key Innovations:
- Dual datapaths allow signed/unsigned operations to proceed concurrently
- Early termination reduces average division time
- Carry-save adder improves timing closure
- Sign processing doesn't stall the main division
- Output pipelining improves throughput

Implementation Highlights:
- Uses two separate shift registers for signed/unsigned cases
- Implements carry-save subtraction for faster iteration
- Maintains sign flags throughout operation
- Special handling for division by zero and overflow
- Optimized control logic with minimal states
