Here's a novel architectural approach using a non-restoring division algorithm with parallel partial remainder calculation:

Key innovations:
1. **Parallel Remainder Calculation**: Instead of sequential subtraction, we calculate both possible remainder paths (add/subtract) in parallel and select the correct one based on the sign.

2. **Early Sign Prediction**: We predict the next remainder sign in advance to reduce critical path delay.

3. **Booth-like Encoding**: Implement a modified Booth encoding for the divisor to reduce the number of operations needed.

4. **Variable Latency**: The design can complete in fewer cycles for smaller dividends by detecting leading zeros.

5. **Pipelined Sign Correction**: Final sign adjustments are done in parallel with the last division step.

This approach reduces average latency while maintaining the same worst-case timing, improving throughput for typical cases.
