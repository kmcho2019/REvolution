The radix-2 divider implements a sequential division algorithm for 8-bit signed or unsigned integers. The key steps are:
1. Convert inputs to absolute values if signed operation
2. Initialize shift register with dividend (shifted left by 1)
3. Perform iterative subtraction and shifting for 8 cycles
4. Handle sign correction for final quotient/remainder
5. Output combined result when complete

The design uses:
- A 17-bit shift register (SR) to hold intermediate results
- A counter to track iteration progress
- Sign handling logic for signed operations
- Synchronous operation with clock and reset
