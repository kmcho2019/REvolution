The design implements a radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed/unsigned integers using a shift-and-subtract algorithm. Key points:
1. Convert inputs to absolute values for signed division
2. Use a 16-bit shift register (SR) to hold intermediate results (dividend initially shifted left by 1)
3. Perform 8 iterations (one per bit) of:
   - Subtract divisor from upper bits
   - Shift result and update quotient bit based on carry
4. Handle sign correction for final quotient/remainder
5. Output combined result with remainder in upper byte and quotient in lower byte
6. Control logic manages operation sequencing and result validity
