The design implements a radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed/unsigned integers. Key aspects:
1. Convert inputs to absolute values if signed operation
2. Use shift-and-subtract algorithm (non-restoring division)
3. Maintain a 16-bit shift register (SR) for intermediate results
4. Control flow with counter (cnt) and state (start_cnt)
5. Handle sign correction for final quotient/remainder
6. Output format: remainder[15:8], quotient[7:0]

The design operates synchronously with clock and reset. Division starts when opn_valid is asserted and completes in 8 cycles.
