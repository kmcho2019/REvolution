Novel Architecture:
1. Non-restoring division algorithm - more efficient than restoring division
2. Early termination when remainder becomes zero (saves cycles)
3. Dual-phase operation:
   - Phase 1: Convert inputs to absolute values if signed
   - Phase 2: Perform division with sign correction at end
4. Optimized datapath with combined remainder/quotient register
5. Special handling for division by zero (returns max values)
6. State machine with 3 states (IDLE, DIVIDE, FINISH)

Key Innovations:
- Early termination when remainder hits zero
- Combined remainder/quotient shift register
- Parallel sign conversion and absolute value calculation
- Single-cycle state transitions for faster operation
- Clear separation of control and datapath logic
