Novel Architectural Idea: Pipelined Radix-2 Division with Early Termination

Key Innovations:
1. Dual-stage pipeline: Separates sign handling and core division for better timing
2. Early termination detection: Checks for division completion in parallel
3. Optimized remainder calculation: Uses carry-save addition for faster subtraction
4. Dynamic shift control: Adjusts shift amount based on leading zeros
5. Unified result formation: Combines quotient and remainder with sign correction in final stage
6. Error handling: Explicit divide-by-zero and overflow detection

Implementation Highlights:
- Stage 1: Input conditioning and sign handling
- Stage 2: Core radix-2 division with variable shifting
- Early exit when remainder becomes zero
- Final stage handles sign correction and result packing
