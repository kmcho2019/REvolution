Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Pipelined Radix-2 Division: Implement the division operation in a pipelined fashion to improve throughput
2. Early Termination: Detect division completion early when remainder becomes zero
3. Dual-Path Processing: Handle signed and unsigned operations in parallel paths
4. Optimized Shift Register: Use a combined 16-bit register for both remainder and quotient with smart shifting
5. Status Flags: Add flags for divide-by-zero and overflow conditions

Key Innovations:
- Pipeline stages for better timing closure
- Early termination saves cycles when possible
- Parallel signed/unsigned processing reduces critical path
- Smart shifting reduces hardware complexity
- Additional status flags enhance functionality
