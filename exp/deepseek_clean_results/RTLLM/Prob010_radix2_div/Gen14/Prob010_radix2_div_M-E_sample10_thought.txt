Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Dual-Phase Processing: Separate the division into two distinct phases - initialization and computation - to simplify control logic
2. Early Sign Resolution: Compute and store all sign-related information during initialization
3. Parallel Subtraction: Use a dedicated subtractor that operates in parallel with the shift operation
4. Smart Counter: Utilize a 4-bit counter with bitwise checks for state transitions (bit 3 indicates completion)
5. Unified Result Formation: Combine remainder and quotient in a single operation at the end
6. Optimized Reset Handling: Clear only necessary registers on reset to reduce fanout

Key Innovations:
- Phase-based operation reduces control complexity
- Parallel arithmetic improves timing
- Bitwise counter checks simplify state logic
- Early sign resolution prevents timing bottlenecks
- Unified result formation reduces final cycle complexity
