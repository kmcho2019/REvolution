Simplified Radix-2 Divider Design:

Key Improvements:
1. Combined quotient and remainder calculation into single shift register
2. Removed redundant registers by using direct computation
3. Simplified control logic to just use a counter
4. Maintained signed operation support with minimal overhead
5. Streamlined state management with just calculating flag
6. Direct sign application without intermediate storage

Core Algorithm:
1. Take absolute values for signed operations
2. Initialize shift register with dividend (left-shifted by 1)
3. Perform 8 iterations of non-restoring division:
   - Subtract divisor from upper bits
   - Shift result and insert quotient bit
4. Final remainder correction if needed
5. Apply signs to results
6. Output combined quotient and remainder
