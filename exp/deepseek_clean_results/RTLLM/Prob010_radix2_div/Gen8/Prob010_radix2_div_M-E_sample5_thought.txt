Novel Architecture Idea: Pipelined Radix-2 Divider with Early Termination

Key Innovations:
1. Pipelined approach with 3 stages (Input, Calculation, Output) for better timing
2. Early termination detection for division by zero and power-of-two divisors
3. Optimized subtraction using carry-save arithmetic
4. Dual-phase calculation (shift and subtract in parallel)
5. Sign handling integrated into pipeline stages

Pipeline Stages:
1. Input Stage: 
   - Register inputs
   - Calculate absolute values
   - Detect special cases (div by zero, power-of-two)
   - Determine result signs

2. Calculation Stage:
   - Perform radix-2 division steps
   - Early exit if divisor is power-of-two
   - Parallel shift and subtract operations

3. Output Stage:
   - Apply sign correction
   - Handle special cases
   - Output final result

Advantages:
- Higher clock frequency due to pipelining
- Reduced latency for special cases
- More efficient resource utilization
- Clear separation of concerns between stages
