Novel Architecture:
1. Pipelined Radix-2 Division - Processes multiple bits per cycle for faster operation
2. Dual-Path Datapath - Separates sign handling from core division logic
3. Predictive Subtraction - Uses carry-save adders for faster partial remainder calculation
4. Early Exit Optimization - Detects division completion in fewer cycles when possible
5. Multi-Stage Result Formation - Builds quotient and remainder in parallel
6. Error Handling - Special cases for division by zero and overflow

Key Innovations:
- Two-stage pipeline for sign conversion and division
- Parallel remainder and quotient update logic
- Carry-select adder for faster subtraction
- Dynamic cycle reduction based on operand magnitude
- Optimized register usage with shared intermediate results
