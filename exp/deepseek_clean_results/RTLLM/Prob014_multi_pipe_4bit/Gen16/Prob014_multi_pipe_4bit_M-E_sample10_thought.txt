Novel Architecture: "Bit-Serial Pipeline Multiplier with Shift-Accumulate"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Processes multiplier bits sequentially to reduce hardware complexity
2. **Shift-Accumulate Technique**: Uses right-shift for multiplicand and conditional addition
3. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: 
   - Stage 1: Bit extraction and shift preparation
   - Stage 2: Conditional accumulation
4. **Reduced Register Usage**: Only stores intermediate sum and shifted multiplicand
5. **Early Termination**: Skips zero multiplier bits to save power

Architectural Benefits:
1. Lower area than parallel multiplier (no need for multiple partial product generators)
2. Natural pipeline structure with clear stage separation
3. Power-efficient due to conditional operation
4. Scalable to larger bit widths with minimal changes
5. Clear data flow with minimal intermediate storage
