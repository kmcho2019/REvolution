Simplified 2-Stage Pipelined 4-bit Multiplier Design:

Key Improvements:
1. **Proper Pipeline Implementation**: Maintains required two pipeline stages for intermediate values
2. **Efficient Partial Product Generation**: Uses simple bitwise AND and shifts instead of generate blocks
3. **Balanced Pipeline**: Divides the multiplication into two stages:
   - Stage 1: Calculate first two partial products and sum
   - Stage 2: Calculate remaining partial products and final sum
4. **Simplified Reset Logic**: Clear all pipeline registers on reset
5. **Compact Implementation**: Uses minimal registers while meeting requirements

Operation Flow:
1. First Pipeline Stage:
   - Calculate partial products for bits 0-1 of multiplier
   - Store intermediate sum in first pipeline register
2. Second Pipeline Stage:
   - Calculate partial products for bits 2-3 of multiplier
   - Combine with previous sum for final result
3. Output Register:
   - Stores final product
