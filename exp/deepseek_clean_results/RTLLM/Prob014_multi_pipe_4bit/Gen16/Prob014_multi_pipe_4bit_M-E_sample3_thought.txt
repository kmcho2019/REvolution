Novel Architecture: "Bit-Serial Pipeline Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Processes multiplier bits sequentially to reduce hardware
2. **Carry-Save Technique**: Uses carry-save adders between pipeline stages for faster addition
3. **Rotating Accumulator**: Implements a barrel-shifter based accumulator for partial products
4. **Two-Phase Pipeline**: Splits operation into shift and accumulate phases
5. **Early Termination**: Detects when remaining bits are zero to skip unnecessary cycles

Advantages:
1. **Reduced Area**: Processes one bit per cycle instead of parallel processing
2. **Higher Frequency**: Carry-save addition reduces critical path
3. **Power Efficiency**: Early termination saves dynamic power
4. **Scalable Design**: Easily extendable to larger bit widths
5. **Clear Pipeline Stages**: Explicit shift and accumulate phases
