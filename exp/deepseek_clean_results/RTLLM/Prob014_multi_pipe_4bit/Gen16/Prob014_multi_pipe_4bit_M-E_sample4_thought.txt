Novel Pipelined Multiplier Architecture: "Bit-Serial Accumulation with Staggered Pipeline"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Process multiplier bits sequentially across pipeline stages
2. **Staggered Data Flow**: Each pipeline stage handles a different bit position
3. **Accumulation Pipeline**: Partial products accumulate through the pipeline
4. **Two-Stage Implementation**:
   - Stage 1: Processes bits 0-1 with initial accumulation
   - Stage 2: Processes bits 2-3 with final accumulation
5. **Efficient Resource Usage**: Minimizes hardware while maintaining pipelining

Operation Flow:
1. First Pipeline Stage:
   - Processes LSBs (bits 0-1) of multiplier
   - Generates and accumulates partial products for these bits
   - Stores intermediate result in first register

2. Second Pipeline Stage:
   - Processes MSBs (bits 2-3) of multiplier
   - Accumulates with previous stage's result
   - Produces final product in output register

Reset Behavior:
- Both pipeline registers clear synchronously on reset
- Clean restart of multiplication process
