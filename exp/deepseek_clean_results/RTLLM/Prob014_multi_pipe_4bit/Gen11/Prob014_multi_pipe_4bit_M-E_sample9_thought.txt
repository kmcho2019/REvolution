Here's a novel pipeline architecture for the 4-bit unsigned multiplier:

1. **Bit-Serial Pipeline Approach**:
   - Instead of processing all bits in parallel, we'll process one multiplier bit per pipeline stage
   - Each stage handles one bit of the multiplier (mul_b) and accumulates the partial product
   - This creates a natural 4-stage pipeline (one stage per bit)

2. **Pipeline Structure**:
   - Stage 0: Processes LSB (bit 0) of mul_b
   - Stage 1: Processes bit 1 of mul_b and accumulates with previous result
   - Stage 2: Processes bit 2 of mul_b and accumulates
   - Stage 3: Processes MSB (bit 3) of mul_b and produces final result

3. **Key Features**:
   - Each pipeline stage is identical in structure
   - Uses shift-and-add approach in each stage
   - Properly registered between stages for correct pipelining
   - Reset clears all pipeline registers

4. **Advantages**:
   - Clear pipeline progression with one bit per stage
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
   - Easily scalable to larger bit widths
   - Intuitive understanding of multiplication process
