Novel Architecture: "Bit-Serial Pipeline Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Process multiplier bits sequentially to reduce hardware complexity
2. **Carry-Save Technique**: Use carry-save adders between pipeline stages to avoid carry propagation delays
3. **Accumulation Pipeline**: Implement two-stage accumulation with intermediate result storage
4. **Dynamic Partial Product Selection**: Generate partial products on-the-fly based on current multiplier bit
5. **Efficient Resource Utilization**: Minimize hardware by reusing the same adder across pipeline stages

Operation Flow:
1. First Stage (Cycle 1):
   - Process LSB of multiplier (mul_b[0])
   - Generate partial product (mul_a or 0)
   - Store in carry-save format (sum + carry)

2. Second Stage (Cycle 2-4):
   - Process next multiplier bit (mul_b[i])
   - Generate shifted partial product
   - Accumulate with previous result
   - Maintain carry-save format between stages

3. Final Stage (Cycle 5):
   - Combine final sum and carry
   - Output complete product
