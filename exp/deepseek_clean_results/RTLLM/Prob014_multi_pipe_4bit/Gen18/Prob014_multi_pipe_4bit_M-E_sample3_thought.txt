Novel Pipeline Architecture: Bit-Serial with Shift-Accumulate

Key Innovations:
1. Bit-serial processing of multiplier (mul_b) to reduce combinational logic
2. Shift-accumulate approach where multiplicand is shifted each cycle
3. Two-stage pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Register inputs and prepare shifted multiplicand
   - Stage 2: Conditionally accumulate based on current multiplier bit
4. Progressive shifting eliminates need for multiple shift operations
5. Reduced register count compared to traditional approaches

Operation Flow:
1. Cycle 0: Register inputs, initialize accumulator and shift counter
2. Cycle 1: Process LSB (bit 0) of multiplier
3. Cycle 2: Shift multiplicand, process bit 1
4. Cycle 3: Shift multiplicand, process bit 2
5. Cycle 4: Shift multiplicand, process MSB (bit 3) and output result

Advantages:
1. Minimal combinational logic between pipeline stages
2. Natural alignment of partial products through shifting
3. Clear separation of pipeline stages
4. Efficient use of registers
5. Easily scalable to larger bit widths
