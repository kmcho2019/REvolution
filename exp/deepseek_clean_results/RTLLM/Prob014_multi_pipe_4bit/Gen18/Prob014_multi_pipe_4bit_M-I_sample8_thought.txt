Improved Strategy for PPA Optimization:

1. **Parameter Handling**: Ensure 'size' parameter is properly accessible to testbench by making it visible at module instantiation.

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - Add clock gating during reset to reduce dynamic power
   - Use enable registers to prevent unnecessary toggling

3. **Area Optimization**:
   - Replace individual registers with packed arrays
   - Use a single register array for partial products
   - Combine first-level sum registers

4. **Performance Enhancement**:
   - Use carry-save adder structure for first stage reduction
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing

5. **Code Structure**:
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
   - Explicit pipeline stage boundaries
   - Reduced redundant logic
