To simplify the 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove unnecessary input extensions since 4-bit * 4-bit multiplication naturally fits in 8 bits
2. Generate partial products directly from original inputs
3. Use a single pipeline stage to accumulate partial sums:
   - First cycle: sum of pp0 and pp1
   - Second cycle: add pp2 and pp3 to previous sum
4. Maintain synchronous reset and clocking
5. Reduce register usage by combining operations
This approach maintains the pipeline nature while simplifying the logic and reducing area.
