Here's a novel approach using a bit-serial multiplication technique with pipelining:
1. Instead of generating all partial products in parallel, we'll process one multiplier bit per clock cycle
2. Use a shift-and-add approach with accumulator
3. Implement two pipeline stages:
   - First stage: Shift multiplicand and conditionally add based on current multiplier bit
   - Second stage: Accumulate the shifted/add result
4. This reduces combinatorial logic and improves timing
5. The design naturally handles the 4-bit multiplication in 4 clock cycles with pipelining
6. Reset clears all pipeline registers
