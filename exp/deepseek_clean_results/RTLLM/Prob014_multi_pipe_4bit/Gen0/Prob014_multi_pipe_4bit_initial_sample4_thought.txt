To implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extend both inputs (mul_a and mul_b) by 4 bits (size parameter) with zeros at MSB
2. Generate partial products for each bit of mul_b
3. Use two pipeline stages (registers) to store intermediate sums
4. Accumulate the partial products through the pipeline stages
5. Output the final product after the second pipeline stage

The design will:
- Use generate blocks to create partial products
- Implement pipeline registers for intermediate sums
- Handle reset conditions properly
- Perform all operations on clock edges
