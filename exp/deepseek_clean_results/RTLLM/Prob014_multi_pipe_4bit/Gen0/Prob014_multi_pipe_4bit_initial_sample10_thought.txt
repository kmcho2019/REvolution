To implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extend both input operands by 4 bits (since size=4) to handle overflow during partial product calculations
2. Generate partial products for each bit of the multiplier (mul_b)
3. Use a two-stage pipeline:
   - First stage: Calculate and register partial products
   - Second stage: Accumulate partial sums and register final sum
4. Control the pipeline with clock and reset signals
5. The final output will be available after two clock cycles (pipeline latency)

The design will use combinational logic for partial product generation and addition, with registered stages to pipeline the computation.
