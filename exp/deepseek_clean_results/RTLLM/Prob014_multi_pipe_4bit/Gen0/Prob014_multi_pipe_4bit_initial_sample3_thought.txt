The design implements a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier with two levels of registers. The key steps are:
1. Extend inputs by 4 zero bits at MSB
2. Generate partial products for each bit of multiplier
3. Use pipeline registers to store intermediate sums
4. Accumulate partial products in two stages
5. Output final product after second pipeline stage

The pipeline registers help improve timing by breaking the addition into stages. Reset clears all registers and outputs.
