To implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extend both input operands by adding 4 zero bits at MSB positions (creating 8-bit values)
2. Generate partial products for each bit of the multiplier (mul_b)
3. Use two pipeline stages to accumulate the partial sums:
   - First stage: Sum of first two partial products
   - Second stage: Sum of remaining partial products and previous stage result
4. Register all intermediate values and final output
5. Handle synchronous reset properly for all registers
