To implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extend both input operands by 4 bits (size=4) to handle intermediate calculations
2. Generate partial products for each bit of mul_b
3. Use two pipeline stages (registers) to store intermediate sums
4. Accumulate the partial products through the pipeline stages
5. Output the final product after all pipeline stages

The design will use synchronous reset and clocked registers to ensure proper pipelining. Each pipeline stage will add more partial products to the accumulated sum.
