The design implements a 4-bit unsigned pipelined multiplier with two levels of registers. Here's the conceptual approach:
1. Extend both inputs by 4 bits (size parameter) with zeros at MSB
2. Generate partial products for each bit of multiplier (mul_b)
3. Use two pipeline stages:
   - First stage: Sum of first two partial products
   - Second stage: Sum of remaining partial products
4. Final stage adds results from both pipeline stages
5. All operations are clock-synchronized with active-low reset
6. Output is registered to maintain pipeline consistency
