To implement a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extend both input operands (mul_a and mul_b) by adding 4 zeros at MSB positions
2. Generate partial products for each bit of mul_b (shifted versions of mul_a)
3. Use two pipeline stages:
   - First stage: Sum the first two partial products
   - Second stage: Sum the next two partial products and add to previous sum
4. Register intermediate results at each stage
5. Final output is registered and available after pipeline stages complete
6. All operations are synchronous to clock with active-low reset
