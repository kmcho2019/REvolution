Novel Architecture: "Parallel Partial Product Generator with Two-Stage Wallace Tree Reduction"

Key Innovations:
1. **Parallel Partial Product Generation**: Generates all partial products simultaneously in first pipeline stage
2. **Wallace Tree Compression**: Uses 3:2 compressors in first reduction stage for efficient partial product summation
3. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: First stage generates and compresses partial products, second stage completes final addition
4. **Conditional Partial Product Masking**: Zeroes out partial products when multiplier bit is 0
5. **Registered Output**: Ensures clean timing closure with final registered output

Architectural Benefits:
1. **High Throughput**: Processes one multiplication per clock cycle after initial latency
2. **Area Efficiency**: Wallace tree reduces adder resources compared to ripple-carry
3. **Timing Optimization**: Balanced pipeline stages for maximum clock frequency
4. **Power Efficiency**: Only activates necessary compression logic
5. **Scalability**: Structure easily extends to larger bit widths
