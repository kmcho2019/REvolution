Simplified Pipeline Multiplier Design:

Key Simplifications:
1. **Direct Partial Product Generation**: Generate partial products directly based on multiplier bits without pre-shifting
2. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: 
   - Stage 1: Generate and register partial products
   - Stage 2: Accumulate partial products
3. **Simplified Control**: Remove complex carry-save logic and use straightforward addition
4. **Efficient Reset**: Simple synchronous reset for all registers
5. **Bitwise AND for Partial Products**: Use bitwise AND with shifted multiplicand for clean partial product generation

Advantages:
1. **Clear Data Flow**: Easier to understand and verify
2. **Reduced Logic**: Eliminates unnecessary intermediate registers
3. **Correct Functionality**: Meets specification requirements
4. **Maintained Pipelining**: Still achieves pipeline benefits with simpler implementation
