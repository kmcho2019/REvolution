Here's a novel architectural approach for the 4-bit pipelined multiplier:

1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding: Instead of simple partial products, we'll use Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products by half, improving efficiency.

2. Carry-Save Addition: Implement carry-save adders in the first pipeline stage to reduce critical path delay.

3. Balanced Pipeline Stages: Distribute operations more evenly between stages to maximize clock frequency.

4. Optimized Partial Product Selection: Use multiplexers for Booth recoding rather than conditional shifts.

Key innovations:
- Booth encoding reduces the number of partial products from 4 to 3
- Carry-save addition in stage 1 avoids full carry propagation
- Final addition happens in stage 2 with carry propagation
- More balanced pipeline stages for better throughput
