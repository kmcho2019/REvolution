Here's a novel approach using a Wallace Tree multiplier with pipelining for improved performance:

1. Partial Product Generation:
   - Generate all partial products in parallel
   - Use AND gates for each bit combination (a[i] & b[j])

2. Wallace Tree Compression:
   - First pipeline stage: 3:2 compressors (carry-save adders)
   - Reduce all partial products to two vectors (sum and carry)
   - Register compressed results after first stage

3. Final Addition:
   - Second pipeline stage: Carry-propagate adder
   - Combine sum and carry vectors
   - Register final result

4. Key Innovations:
   - Wallace tree structure reduces critical path
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better throughput
   - Efficient compression reduces adder stages
   - Clear separation between compression and final addition

5. Reset Handling:
   - Synchronous reset clears all pipeline registers
   - Proper initialization of intermediate values
