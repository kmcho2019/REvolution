Here's a novel approach using a bit-serial multiplication technique with pipeline registers:

1. Bit-Serial Processing:
   - Process one multiplier bit per clock cycle
   - Accumulate partial products sequentially
   - Reduces combinational logic complexity

2. Shift-Accumulate Architecture:
   - Multiplicand is shifted left each cycle
   - Conditional addition based on current multiplier bit
   - Uses only one adder per pipeline stage

3. Two-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Shift multiplicand and compute current partial product
   - Stage 2: Accumulate partial product with running sum
   - Final result available after 4+1 cycles (4 bits + initialization)

4. Advantages:
   - Lower area than parallel multiplier
   - Easily scalable to larger bit widths
   - Clear pipeline separation between shift and add operations
