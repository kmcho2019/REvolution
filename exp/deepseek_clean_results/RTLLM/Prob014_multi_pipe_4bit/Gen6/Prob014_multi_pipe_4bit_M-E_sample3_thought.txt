My novel approach implements a radix-4 Booth encoded pipeline multiplier that offers several advantages:

1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 4 to 2 for 4-bit)
   - Improves power efficiency by reducing switching activity
   - Simplifies partial product generation logic

2. Optimized Pipeline Structure:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace-tree compression of partial products
   - Stage 3: Final carry-propagate addition

3. Advanced Features:
   - Sign extension handling built into Booth encoding
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
   - Reduced hardware complexity compared to conventional approach

4. Implementation Benefits:
   - Fewer adders required due to Booth encoding
   - Smaller critical path in each pipeline stage
   - Better scalability for larger bit-widths
