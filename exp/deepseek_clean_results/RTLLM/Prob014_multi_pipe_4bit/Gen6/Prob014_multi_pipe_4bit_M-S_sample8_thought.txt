Here's a simplified approach for the 4-bit pipelined multiplier:

1. **Basic Shift-Add Architecture**:
   - Eliminate Booth encoding to reduce complexity
   - Use straightforward partial product generation
   - Each bit of multiplier generates a shifted multiplicand

2. **Two-Stage Pipeline**:
   - First stage generates all partial products
   - Second stage accumulates the partial products
   - Simple register-based pipeline control

3. **Direct Implementation**:
   - Partial products created by ANDing multiplicand with each multiplier bit
   - Left-shift applied based on bit position
   - Simple adder tree for accumulation

4. **Clean Reset Logic**:
   - Single reset signal clears all registers
   - Synchronous reset for predictable behavior

Advantages:
- Simpler logic than Booth encoding
- Easier to understand and verify
- Maintains full functionality
- Fewer potential points of failure
