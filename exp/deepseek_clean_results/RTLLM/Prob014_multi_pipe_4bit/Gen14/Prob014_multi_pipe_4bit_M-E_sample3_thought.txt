Novel Architecture: "Bit-Serial Pipeline Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Processes multiplier bits sequentially to reduce area
2. **Carry-Save Technique**: Uses carry-save adders to avoid propagation delay
3. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: First stage generates shifted multiplicand, second stage accumulates
4. **Dynamic Shift Register**: Efficiently handles variable shifts without large multiplexers
5. **Early Output Register**: Minimizes critical path by registering output early

Architectural Benefits:
1. **Area Efficiency**: Bit-serial approach reduces logic resources
2. **Timing Improvement**: Carry-save technique breaks long adder chains
3. **Power Savings**: Only activates necessary shift/add operations
4. **Scalability**: Easily extends to larger bit widths
5. **Throughput**: Maintains single-cycle throughput after initial latency
