Simplified Design Approach:

1. **Streamlined Pipeline**: 
   - Reduce to two essential pipeline stages (partial product generation and accumulation)
   - Eliminate unnecessary intermediate registers while maintaining the required pipeline depth

2. **Simplified Partial Products**:
   - Generate partial products directly without separate wire arrays
   - Combine partial product generation with first-stage addition

3. **Optimized Register Usage**:
   - Use only necessary registers for pipeline stages
   - Combine reset logic for cleaner implementation

4. **Direct Computation**:
   - Calculate final sum directly from stage registers
   - Remove redundant combinational signals

5. **Maintained Functionality**:
   - Keep same 4-bit unsigned multiplication behavior
   - Preserve two-clock-cycle latency
