Simplified 4-bit Pipelined Multiplier Design:

Key Simplifications:
1. **Basic Pipeline Structure**: Maintain two-stage pipeline but with straightforward implementation
2. **Direct Partial Products**: Generate partial products without clock gating for simplicity
3. **Simple Accumulation**: Use sequential addition of partial products rather than carry-save
4. **Clear Timing**: Separate pipeline stages clearly with registers
5. **Reset Handling**: Maintain synchronous reset behavior

Operation Flow:
1. First Stage (Cycle 1):
   - Generate all partial products
   - Store in pipeline register

2. Second Stage (Cycle 2):
   - Sum all partial products
   - Register final output

Reset Behavior:
- All registers clear synchronously on active-low reset
- Simple and predictable behavior
