Simplified 4-bit Pipelined Multiplier Design:

Key Simplifications:
1. Remove unnecessary intermediate registers (b_reg) that aren't used
2. Eliminate the adder tree structure since for 4-bit multiplication, a simple sequential addition is sufficient
3. Combine partial product generation and first stage addition into a single pipeline stage
4. Maintain clear two-stage pipeline structure:
   - Stage 1: Generate and sum first two partial products
   - Stage 2: Sum remaining partial products and produce final result
5. Keep clean reset logic for all pipeline registers

Benefits:
- Reduced register usage
- Simpler control flow
- Maintains same pipeline latency (2 cycles)
- Easier to understand and verify
- Still meets all functional requirements
