Simplified Design Approach: "Basic Shift-and-Add Pipeline Multiplier"

Key Features:
1. **Direct Partial Product Generation**: Generate each partial product by AND-ing multiplicand with each multiplier bit and shifting appropriately
2. **Two-Stage Pipeline**:
   - Stage 1: Generate and store all partial products
   - Stage 2: Accumulate partial products to form final result
3. **Simple Adder Tree**: Use basic addition for accumulation (no need for complex parallel prefix adders at 4-bit scale)
4. **Proper Zero Extension**: Follow problem specification by zero-extending inputs
5. **Reset Handling**: Clear all registers on active-low reset

Advantages:
- Matches exact problem requirements
- Simpler and more maintainable
- Lower power consumption
- Easier to verify correctness
- Still maintains 2-cycle pipeline structure
