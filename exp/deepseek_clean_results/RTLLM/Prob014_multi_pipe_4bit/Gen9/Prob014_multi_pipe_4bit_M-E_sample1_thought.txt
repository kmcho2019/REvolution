Novel Architectural Idea: "Bit-Serial Pipeline Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition"

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Process multiplier bits one at a time to reduce hardware complexity
2. **Carry-Save Technique**: Use carry-save adders to avoid carry propagation delays
3. **Accumulative Pipeline**: Accumulate results over multiple cycles with intermediate storage
4. **Dynamic Shift Control**: Automatically adjust multiplicand shift based on bit position
5. **Compact Implementation**: Requires fewer registers than traditional parallel approaches

Operation Flow:
1. Extend inputs to 8-bit width with zero padding
2. Process each multiplier bit sequentially over 4 cycles
3. For each active bit, shift and accumulate the multiplicand
4. Use carry-save format for intermediate results
5. Final addition converts carry-save to binary output
