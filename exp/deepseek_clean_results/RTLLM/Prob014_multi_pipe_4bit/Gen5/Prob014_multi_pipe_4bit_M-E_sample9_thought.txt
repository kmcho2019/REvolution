Here's a novel approach using a digit-serial multiplier with pipelined accumulation:

1. Digit-Serial Architecture:
   - Processes multiplier bits in pairs (2 bits per cycle)
   - Reduces partial product count by 50%
   - More efficient than bit-serial but simpler than full parallel

2. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Encodes multiplier bits to reduce partial products
   - Supports 0, ±1, ±2 multiplicand operations
   - More efficient than simple AND-based partial products

3. Two-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product selection
   - Stage 2: Accumulation of partial products
   - Balanced pipeline stages for optimal throughput

4. Pipelined Accumulation:
   - Uses carry-save adder for intermediate sums
   - Final carry-propagate adder in second stage
   - Registered outputs for clean timing

5. Advantages:
   - Reduced hardware complexity vs full parallel
   - Higher throughput than bit-serial
   - Easily scalable to larger sizes
   - Power efficient due to reduced switching
