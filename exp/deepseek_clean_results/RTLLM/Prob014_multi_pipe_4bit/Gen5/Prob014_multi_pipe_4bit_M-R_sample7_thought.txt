Here's a refactored version with improved pipeline structure and clearer partial product accumulation:

1. **Simplified Pipeline Stages**:
   - Stage 1: Register inputs and generate all partial products
   - Stage 2: First level of addition (sum of first two partial products)
   - Stage 3: Second level of addition (sum of next two partial products)
   - Stage 4: Final addition and output

2. **Balanced Addition**:
   - Each pipeline stage handles exactly two operands
   - Clear progression from partial products to final result

3. **Continuous Assignments**:
   - Use assign statements for combinational logic
   - Keep registers only for pipeline stages

4. **Symmetric Treatment**:
   - All partial products go through same number of pipeline stages
   - No special treatment for any particular partial product

Key improvements:
- Balanced pipeline stages (2 additions, 3 clock cycles total)
- Clear separation between combinational and sequential logic
- Symmetric handling of all partial products
- Easier to verify correct operation
