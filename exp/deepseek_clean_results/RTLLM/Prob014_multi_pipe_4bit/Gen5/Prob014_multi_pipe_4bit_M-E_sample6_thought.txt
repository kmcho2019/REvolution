Here's a novel architectural approach for the 4-bit pipelined multiplier:

1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**:
   - Implement Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products from 4 to 3
   - This reduces pipeline stages while maintaining accuracy

2. **Asymmetric Pipeline**:
   - First stage handles Booth encoding and generates partial products
   - Second stage performs parallel addition of all partial products using carry-save adders
   - Final stage converts carry-save format to binary

3. **Efficient Partial Product Selection**:
   - Use multiplexers to select among 0, ±A, ±2A based on Booth encoding
   - Sign extension handled automatically through proper bit-width management

4. **Optimized Reset Logic**:
   - Single reset control for all pipeline stages
   - Clear signals propagate through all stages synchronously

Key Advantages:
- Fewer pipeline stages than conventional approach
- Reduced logic complexity through Booth encoding
- Better performance with same clock frequency
- Maintains full precision and correctness
