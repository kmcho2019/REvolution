Simplified 4-bit Pipelined Multiplier Design:

Key Improvements:
1. **True Pipeline Implementation**: Properly clocked registers for both pipeline stages
2. **Simplified Logic**: Direct partial product generation without intermediate wires
3. **Consistent Reset**: Synchronous reset for all pipeline stages
4. **Clear Data Flow**: Single always block for each pipeline stage
5. **Efficient Summation**: Direct accumulation in pipeline stages

Simplifications:
- Removed unnecessary zero-extension wire (handled in partial products)
- Combined partial product generation with shifting
- Single always block per pipeline stage
- Eliminated redundant constants

Operation Flow:
1. First Stage: Sum of pp0 and pp1 (registered)
2. Second Stage: Sum of pp2 and pp3 (registered)
3. Final Output: Sum of both stages (registered)
