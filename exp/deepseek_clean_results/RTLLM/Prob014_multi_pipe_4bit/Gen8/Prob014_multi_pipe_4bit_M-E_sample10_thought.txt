Here's a novel architectural approach for the 4-bit pipelined multiplier:

1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**: 
   - Implement Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products by half (from 4 to 2)
   - This reduces the adder stages needed while maintaining the same throughput

2. **Two-Stage Pipeline Architecture**:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding + partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression + final addition

3. **Optimized Partial Product Handling**:
   - Use sign-extension for proper arithmetic handling
   - Implement dedicated registers for each pipeline stage
   - Include pipeline valid signals to maintain data integrity

4. **Power Optimization**:
   - Clock gating through the enable signal
   - Reduced switching activity via Booth encoding

5. **Scalability**:
   - The architecture easily scales to larger bit-widths
   - Maintains clean timing closure with balanced pipeline stages
