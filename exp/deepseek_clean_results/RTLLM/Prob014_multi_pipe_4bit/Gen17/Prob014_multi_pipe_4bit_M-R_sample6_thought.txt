Refactored Architecture: Two-Stage Pipeline Multiplier with Explicit Partial Products

Key Improvements:
1. **True Two-Stage Pipeline**: Strict separation of partial product generation (stage 1) and accumulation (stage 2)
2. **Explicit Partial Products**: Generate all partial products simultaneously using bitwise AND and shifts
3. **Registered Accumulation**: Intermediate sums are properly registered between stages
4. **Simplified Control**: Eliminates complex state machine in favor of direct pipeline registers
5. **Clear Data Flow**: Each pipeline stage has well-defined inputs/outputs

Operation Flow:
1. First Stage (Cycle 1):
   - Generate all 4 partial products in parallel
   - Register partial products and multiplier LSBs
   - First level of reduction (sum pairs of partial products)

2. Second Stage (Cycle 2):
   - Final accumulation of intermediate sums
   - Output registered result

Advantages:
- Maintains original bit-serial concept but with proper pipelining
- Clear separation of combinational and registered logic
- Easier timing closure
- More predictable latency (2 cycles)
- Better matches testbench expectations
