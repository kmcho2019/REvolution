Simplified 4-bit Pipeline Multiplier Design:

Key Simplifications:
1. **Direct Partial Product Generation**: Eliminate complex state machine by directly generating all partial products in one cycle
2. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: Use simple registers to store intermediate sums rather than carry-save technique
3. **Parallel Addition**: Add partial products in parallel rather than bit-serial approach
4. **Simplified Control**: Remove phase logic and early termination for cleaner implementation
5. **Direct Output Assignment**: Final product calculation happens in second pipeline stage

Advantages:
1. **Reduced Complexity**: Fewer states and control signals
2. **Maintained Functionality**: Still produces correct 4-bit multiplication result
3. **Clear Pipeline**: Two distinct stages for partial product generation and final sum
4. **Easier Debugging**: Straightforward data flow
5. **Maintained Performance**: Still benefits from pipelining
