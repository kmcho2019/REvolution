Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Two-stage fully pipelined Wallace tree multiplier
2. First stage: Generate all partial products in parallel
3. Second stage: Use carry-save adders for efficient reduction
4. Final stage: Combine carry and sum vectors
5. Key innovations:
   - Balanced pipeline stages for maximum throughput
   - Optimized partial product generation with sign extension
   - Carry-save architecture reduces critical path
   - Registered intermediate results for clean timing

Advantages:
- Higher throughput than sequential approaches
- Balanced pipeline stages (equal delay in each stage)
- Efficient hardware utilization
- Easily scalable to larger bit widths
- Clear separation of partial product generation and reduction
