Novel Architecture: "Dual-Stage Parallel Partial Product Accumulator"

Key Innovations:
1. **Parallel Partial Product Generation**: Generate all partial products simultaneously in the first stage
2. **Two-Stage Balanced Pipeline**: First stage generates partial products, second stage performs accumulation
3. **Weighted Shift Register**: Uses position-aware shifting for proper bit alignment
4. **Early Zero Optimization**: Skips addition for zero partial products to save power
5. **Symmetric Pipeline**: Balanced latency between stages for maximum throughput

Operation Flow:
1. First Stage (Cycle 1):
   - Generate all 4 partial products in parallel
   - Apply proper bit shifts based on multiplier bit position
   - Store in intermediate registers

2. Second Stage (Cycle 2):
   - Accumulate all non-zero partial products
   - Final result is ready after exactly 2 clock cycles
   - Maintains full throughput of one multiplication per cycle after initial latency
