Corrected Design Approach: Two-Stage Pipelined Multiplier with Proper Partial Product Accumulation

This design implements a proper 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages:
1. First stage (partial product generation):
   - Extends inputs with zeros for proper bit alignment
   - Generates all partial products in parallel
   - Each partial product is either shifted multiplicand or zero
   - First pipeline register stores intermediate sum of first two partial products

2. Second stage (final accumulation):
   - Adds remaining partial products to the intermediate sum
   - Second pipeline register stores final product
   - Proper reset handling for all registers

Key Improvements:
- Correct partial product generation with proper left shifts
- Two-stage pipeline for better timing
- Simple and clear accumulation logic
- Proper handling of reset conditions
- Direct implementation of specified algorithm
