Simplified 4-bit Pipelined Multiplier Design:

Key Simplifications:
1. **Eliminate Redundant Registers**: Instead of storing all partial products separately, accumulate them progressively
2. **Single Pipeline Stage**: Use just one intermediate register for accumulation
3. **Direct Calculation**: Compute partial products and accumulate in one step per cycle
4. **Simplified Reset Logic**: Clear all registers with single reset condition
5. **Reduced Wiring**: Minimize internal signal routing by combining operations

Operation Flow:
1. First Cycle:
   - Generate all partial products
   - Accumulate first two partial products (pp0 + pp1)
   - Store intermediate sum in pipeline register

2. Second Cycle:
   - Add remaining partial products (pp2 + pp3)
   - Combine with previous sum
   - Output final result

Reset Behavior:
- All registers clear synchronously on active-low reset
- Pipeline naturally refills in two cycles after reset
