Improvement Strategy:
1. Performance Optimization:

   - Pipeline the adder to break the critical path into two stages
   - Use registered outputs for better timing closure

2. Power Optimization:

   - Only compute next_accum when valid_in is high
   - Use synchronous reset with clock gating

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share the adder between accumulation and final sum
   - Reduce register width where possible (maintain 10-bit accumulator for safety)
   - Simplify control logic by combining related operations

Key Improvements:
- Better timing through pipelining and one-hot counter
- Lower power through conditional computation and clock gating
- Compact area through resource sharing and simplified control
