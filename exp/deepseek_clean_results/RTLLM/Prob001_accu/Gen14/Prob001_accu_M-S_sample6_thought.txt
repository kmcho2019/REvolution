To simplify the accumulator module while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a single register for both accumulation and output (data_out) to reduce area
2. Eliminate the separate accumulator register by reusing data_out during accumulation
3. Simplify the control logic by combining the count check and accumulation
4. Maintain the same reset behavior and valid_out timing
5. Reduce power by only updating registers when needed (valid_in)
