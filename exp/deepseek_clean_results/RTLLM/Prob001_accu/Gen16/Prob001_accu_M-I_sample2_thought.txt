Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipeline the accumulation to break the critical path:

   - This reduces combinatorial delay at the cost of 1 cycle latency
2. Optimize the control logic:
   - Use a dedicated state machine for clearer control flow
   - Separate the counter logic from accumulation
3. Reduce power consumption:
   - Only update registers when necessary
   - Gate the adder inputs when not accumulating
4. Area optimization:
   - Share resources where possible
   - Simplify comparison logic
