New architecture idea:
1. Instead of sequential accumulation, use a 4-element shift register to store inputs
2. Perform parallel addition of all 4 values when the shift register is full
3. This eliminates the critical path of sequential accumulation
4. Use register gating to reduce power - only update shift register when valid_in is high
5. Separate the counting logic from the addition logic for better timing
6. Use a state machine to clearly manage the accumulation and output phases
