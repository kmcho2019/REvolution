Instead of using a counter and sequential accumulation, we can:
1. Use a 4-stage shift register to store the last 4 inputs
2. Implement parallel adders to sum all 4 values simultaneously
3. Generate valid_out when the shift register is full
4. This approach eliminates the sequential dependency of the accumulator, improving timing
5. The shift register naturally handles the 4-input window requirement
6. Parallel addition reduces critical path compared to sequential accumulation
