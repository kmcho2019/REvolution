To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:
1. Pipeline the accumulator by splitting into two stages (0-1 and 2-3 inputs) to break the critical path
2. Add clock gating for the accumulator when valid_in is low to reduce power
3. Optimize register usage by sharing the output register with the final accumulation stage
4. Use a 3-bit counter for better state encoding and future scalability
5. Implement early reset of the accumulator to reduce unnecessary switching
6. Optimize valid_out generation to only toggle when needed

The improved design will:
- Reduce timing violations by shortening the critical path
- Lower power consumption through clock gating and reduced switching
- Maintain the same functionality while using resources more efficiently
