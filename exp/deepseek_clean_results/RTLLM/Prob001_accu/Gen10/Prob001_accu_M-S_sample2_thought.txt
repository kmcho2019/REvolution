To simplify the accumulator module while maintaining functionality:
1. Reduce the counter to 1-bit since we only need to track odd/even pairs (we'll detect every 2nd input)
2. Use two-stage accumulation: 
   - First stage adds pairs of inputs (8+8=9 bits)
   - Second stage adds the pairs (9+9=10 bits)
3. This reduces the critical path by breaking the 4-input addition into two 2-input additions
4. Maintain same interface and functionality with valid_out pulsing every 4 inputs
5. Reset clears all registers
