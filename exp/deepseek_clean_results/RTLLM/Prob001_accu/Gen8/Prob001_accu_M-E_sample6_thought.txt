Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Instead of sequential accumulation, use a 4-stage shift register to store the last 4 inputs
2. Perform parallel addition of all 4 values when the shift register is full
3. This eliminates the long combinational adder chain from the critical path
4. Use a circular buffer pointer instead of a counter to track valid inputs
5. Power optimization by only updating the shift register when valid_in is high

Key Advantages:
- Better timing: Parallel addition reduces critical path
- Lower power: Only shift register updates when valid_in is high
- Simpler control logic: No need for complex counter comparisons
- Scalable: Easily extendable to larger accumulation windows
