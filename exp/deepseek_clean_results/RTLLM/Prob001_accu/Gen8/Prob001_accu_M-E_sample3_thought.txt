New Architecture Idea:
1. Instead of sequential accumulation, use a 4-stage shift register to store the last 4 inputs
2. Perform parallel addition of all 4 values when the shift register is full
3. This eliminates the critical path of chained additions in the original design
4. Use a finite state machine to track input sequence and control output timing
5. Implement clock gating for the shift register to reduce power when valid_in is low
6. Use carry-save addition technique to optimize the parallel adder
