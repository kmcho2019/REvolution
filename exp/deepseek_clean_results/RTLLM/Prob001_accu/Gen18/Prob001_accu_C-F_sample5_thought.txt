Fusion Strategy:

   - Pre-compute next accumulator value to reduce critical path
   - Separate accumulator and output registers for better timing

2. Power Optimization:

   - Only update valid_out during output cycle
   - Use synchronous reset for better power characteristics

3. Area Optimization:
   - Keep minimal 2-bit counter and 10-bit accumulator
   - Reuse adder between accumulation and final sum
   - Simplify control logic by removing redundant states
