To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics:

1. Performance Improvement:
   - Pipeline the accumulator by splitting into two stages (current sum + new input)
   - Use one-hot encoding for state machine to reduce comparison logic delay
   - Register the final output to break critical path

2. Power Reduction:
   - Add clock gating for accumulator when valid_in is low
   - Minimize toggling of valid_out by making it purely combinatorial

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share adder resources between accumulation and final output
   - Use smaller registers where possible (state machine)

4. Timing Fixes:
   - Break the long combinational path from accumulator to output
   - Ensure setup/hold times are met with proper pipelining
