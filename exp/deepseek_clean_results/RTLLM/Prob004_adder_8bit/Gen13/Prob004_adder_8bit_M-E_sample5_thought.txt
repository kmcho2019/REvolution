Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Adder with 4-bit Blocks
1. Instead of a pure ripple-carry approach, implement a carry-select adder architecture
2. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit blocks:
   - Lower 4 bits (0-3): Standard ripple-carry adder
   - Upper 4 bits (4-7): Two parallel ripple-carry adders (one assuming carry-in=0, one assuming carry-in=1)
3. Use the carry-out from lower 4 bits to select the correct upper sum
4. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - Maintains reasonable area overhead
   - No need for complex carry-lookahead logic
   - Good trade-off between speed and area for 8-bit adders
5. Implementation:
   - Create separate modules for 4-bit ripple-carry adders
   - Implement multiplexers for carry selection
   - Combine results from both blocks
