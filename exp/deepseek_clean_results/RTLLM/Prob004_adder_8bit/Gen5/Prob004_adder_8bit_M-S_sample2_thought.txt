To simplify the 8-bit adder while maintaining functionality:

1. Remove parameterization to focus on the core 8-bit functionality
2. Eliminate the generate block since we have a fixed width (8 bits)
3. Use direct instantiation of 8 full adders with explicit carry chaining
4. Keep the efficient XOR-based sum and majority-function carry logic
5. Maintain clear signal naming and comments for readability

This simplification reduces abstraction overhead while keeping the same logical functionality and performance characteristics.
