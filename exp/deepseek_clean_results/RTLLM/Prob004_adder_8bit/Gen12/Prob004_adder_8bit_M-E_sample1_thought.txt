Hybrid Carry-Select Adder Architecture:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit sections (lower and upper)
2. Implement lower 4 bits as ripple-carry adder (simple and area-efficient)
3. Implement upper 4 bits as carry-select adder (faster parallel paths)
4. The carry-select portion computes both possible sums (for carry=0 and carry=1)
5. Final output selects between precomputed sums based on actual carry from lower bits
6. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - More area-efficient than full carry-select
   - Balanced trade-off between speed and area
7. Key optimizations:
   - Shared logic between carry=0 and carry=1 paths
   - Early carry resolution for lower bits
   - Parallel computation of upper bits
