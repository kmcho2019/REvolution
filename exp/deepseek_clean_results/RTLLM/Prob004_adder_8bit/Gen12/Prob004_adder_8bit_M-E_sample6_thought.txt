Novel Architecture: Carry-Skip Adder with Variable Block Size
Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid adder combining ripple-carry and carry-skip techniques
2. Variable block sizes (3-2-3 bit partitioning) for optimal delay
3. Skip logic detects when carry propagation can be bypassed
4. Maintains combinational nature while improving critical path
5. Balanced partitioning reduces worst-case delay

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (especially for mid-range additions)
- Simpler than full carry-lookahead while still providing speedup
- Efficient area/power tradeoff
- Particularly effective for 8-bit width where carry chains are manageable
