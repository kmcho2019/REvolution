To implement an 8-bit adder, we'll chain together 8 full adders (one for each bit position). Each full adder takes the corresponding bits from inputs A and B, along with the carry from the previous stage, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each stage becomes the carry-in for the next stage. The first carry-in comes from the module's cin input, and the final carry-out becomes the module's cout output.
