I propose a hybrid carry-select adder that:
1. Divides the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks
2. Uses ripple-carry adders for each chunk
3. Implements carry-select logic between the chunks
4. Provides better critical path delay than pure ripple-carry
5. Maintains reasonable area overhead compared to pure carry-select

Key features:
- First 4 bits use standard ripple-carry
- Upper 4 bits compute both possible sums (carry=0 and carry=1)
- MUX selects correct upper sum based on actual carry from lower bits
- Reduces critical path by computing upper sums in parallel
- More area efficient than full 8-bit carry-select
