To improve upon the traditional ripple-carry adder, I propose a hybrid carry-select/predict architecture that:
1. Divides the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments
2. Uses carry-select for the upper 4 bits (computing both possible carry scenarios)
3. Implements carry prediction for the lower 4 bits to reduce delay
4. Merges results based on the actual carry from lower bits
This approach reduces critical path delay while maintaining reasonable area overhead compared to pure ripple-carry. The prediction logic helps avoid waiting for the full lower-bit carry propagation.
