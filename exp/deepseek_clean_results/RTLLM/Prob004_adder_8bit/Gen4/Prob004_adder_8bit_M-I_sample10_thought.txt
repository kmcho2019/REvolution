To optimize the 8-bit adder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics, I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This will:
1. Reduce critical path delay by computing carry signals in parallel rather than ripple
2. Group bits into 4-bit lookahead units (optimal balance between complexity and speed)
3. Use generate blocks for scalable implementation
4. Maintain the same interface while improving performance
5. Reduce power consumption by minimizing switching activity in carry chain

The CLA approach significantly reduces propagation delay while maintaining reasonable area overhead. The 4-bit grouping provides a good tradeoff between speed and complexity.
