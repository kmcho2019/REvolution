Improvement strategy:
1. Replace ripple-carry with carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure to reduce critical path delay
2. Implement 2-level CLA (4-bit blocks) for better PPA trade-off
3. Use group propagate/generate logic to parallelize carry computation
4. Maintain same interface while improving performance
5. Optimize logic equations for area efficiency

Benefits:
- Significantly reduced critical path (from 8 FA delays to ~4 gate delays)
- Better performance with minimal area overhead
- Still fully combinational
- Scalable to wider adders with same approach
- Power efficient due to reduced switching activity on long carry chains
