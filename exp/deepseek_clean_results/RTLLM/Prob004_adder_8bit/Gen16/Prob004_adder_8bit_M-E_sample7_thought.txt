Hybrid Carry-Select Adder Approach:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit sections (lower and upper)
2. Implement lower 4 bits as ripple-carry for area efficiency
3. Implement upper 4 bits as carry-select for better performance
4. The carry-select section computes both possible sums (for carry=0 and carry=1)
5. Use the actual carry-out from lower section to mux the correct upper sum
6. Benefits:
   - Better critical path than pure ripple-carry (only 4-bit ripple + mux delay)
   - More area efficient than full carry-select
   - Balanced trade-off between speed and area

Key Features:
- First 4 bits use optimized ripple-carry structure
- Upper 4 bits compute both possible sums in parallel
- Final output selection based on lower carry-out
- Expected to have better timing than pure ripple-carry with modest area increase
