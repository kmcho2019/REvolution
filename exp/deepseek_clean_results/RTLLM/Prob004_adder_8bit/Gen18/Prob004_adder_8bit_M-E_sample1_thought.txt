Novel Hybrid Carry-Select Adder Architecture:
1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks (lower and upper nibbles)
2. For the lower nibble (bits 0-3), use ripple-carry for area efficiency
3. For the upper nibble (bits 4-7), implement carry-select logic:
   - Compute both possible sums (with carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Use the actual carry-out from lower nibble to select correct result
4. This reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry while maintaining better area efficiency than full carry-select
5. Optimized majority function for carry computation
6. Explicit bit-level operations with clear comments

Advantages:
- Reduced critical path (4-bit ripple + 1 mux delay vs 8-bit ripple)
- Better area than full carry-select (only duplicate upper nibble)
- Maintains combinational logic simplicity
- Clear hierarchical structure for readability
