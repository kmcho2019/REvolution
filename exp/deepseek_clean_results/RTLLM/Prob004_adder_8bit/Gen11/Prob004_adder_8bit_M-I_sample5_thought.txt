Improvement Strategy:
1. Implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure to reduce critical path delay
2. Group bits into 4-bit blocks for balanced performance/area tradeoff
3. Use generate/propagate logic to compute carries in parallel
4. Maintain full combinational operation while improving timing
5. Optimize power by reducing unnecessary switching in carry chain
6. Keep area efficient by sharing generate/propagate logic

The CLA approach will significantly improve performance (timing) while maintaining reasonable area and power characteristics. The 4-bit grouping provides a good balance between complexity and speed improvement.
