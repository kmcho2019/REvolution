Novel Hybrid Carry-Select/Predict Architecture:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit sections (lower and upper)
2. For the lower 4 bits: Use ripple-carry for area efficiency
3. For the upper 4 bits: Implement carry-select with prediction
   - Compute both possible sums (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Use the actual carry-out from lower section to select correct sum
4. Carry prediction:
   - Generate a 'carry likely' signal from lower bits to pre-select probable sum
   - Correct if mispredicted (rare case)
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - Better area efficiency than full carry-select
   - Power savings from prediction mechanism
6. Implementation:
   - Explicit ripple-carry for lower 4 bits
   - Dual sum computation for upper bits
   - MUX-based selection with prediction
