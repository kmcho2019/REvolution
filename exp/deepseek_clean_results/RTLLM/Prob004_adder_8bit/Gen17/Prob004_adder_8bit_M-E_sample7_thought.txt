Novel Hybrid Carry-Select/Predict Architecture:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit chunks (lower and upper nibbles)
2. For the upper nibble, compute both possible sums (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use a carry-predict circuit to determine the actual carry-in to upper nibble
4. Mux the correct upper sum based on the predicted carry
5. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path by parallel computation of upper sums
   - Carry prediction is faster than ripple propagation
   - Only one 4-bit ripple chain remains in critical path
   - Good balance between speed and area
6. Implementation:
   - Lower 4-bit ripple carry adder
   - Upper 4-bit dual-path computation
   - Fast carry predictor using lookahead-like logic
   - Final output muxing
