To create a novel 8-bit adder implementation, I'll use a hybrid carry-select/predict architecture that combines the benefits of both approaches:

1. Divide the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments
2. For the lower 4 bits, use a conventional ripple-carry adder
3. For the upper 4 bits, implement two parallel ripple-carry adders assuming carry-in=0 and carry-in=1
4. Use the actual carry-out from the lower 4 bits to select the correct upper sum
5. This provides faster propagation than pure ripple-carry while being more area-efficient than full carry-select

Key advantages:
- Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
- More area-efficient than full 8-bit carry-select
- Balanced approach suitable for medium-speed applications
- Clear separation of lower and upper bit processing
- Easily extensible to wider adders by adding more segments
