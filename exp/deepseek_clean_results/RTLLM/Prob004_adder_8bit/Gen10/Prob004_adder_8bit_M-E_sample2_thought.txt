Novel architectural approach:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit conditional sum adders (CSA)
2. Each 4-bit CSA computes both possible sums (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use a carry-select mux to choose the correct sum based on actual carry-in
4. The first 4-bit block computes its own carry-out to feed the second block
5. This reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area

Benefits:
- Faster than ripple-carry (critical path reduced by ~50%)
- More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
- Regular structure that synthesizes well
- Balanced delay between sum and carry paths
