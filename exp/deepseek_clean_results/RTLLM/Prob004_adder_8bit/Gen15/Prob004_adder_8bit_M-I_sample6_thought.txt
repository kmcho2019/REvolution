Improvement Strategy:
1. Implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure to reduce critical path delay
2. Group bits into 4-bit blocks to balance between complexity and performance
3. Generate propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for each bit
4. Compute carry signals in parallel using lookahead logic
5. Maintain combinational nature while significantly improving timing
6. Keep area efficient by sharing common terms in lookahead logic
7. Optimize power by reducing unnecessary switching activity

Key Benefits:
- Dramatically reduces carry propagation delay from O(n) to O(log n)
- Maintains reasonable area overhead compared to ripple-carry
- Reduces power by minimizing glitching in carry chain
- Scales better for wider adders
- More balanced PPA tradeoff than pure ripple-carry
