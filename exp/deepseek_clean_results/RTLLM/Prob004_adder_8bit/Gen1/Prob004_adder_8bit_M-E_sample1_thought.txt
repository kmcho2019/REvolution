The carry-select adder improves upon the ripple-carry approach by computing two versions of each sum bit in parallel: one assuming carry-in=0 and another assuming carry-in=1. When the actual carry from the previous stage arrives, it simply selects the correct version using multiplexers. This architecture reduces the critical path delay compared to a simple ripple-carry adder. For an 8-bit adder, we'll implement it with two 4-bit carry-select blocks.
