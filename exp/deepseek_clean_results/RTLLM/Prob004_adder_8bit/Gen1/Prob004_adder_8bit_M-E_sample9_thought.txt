Instead of using a traditional ripple-carry adder, we'll implement a carry-select adder architecture for better performance. The 8-bit adder will be divided into two 4-bit blocks. The lower 4 bits will use a ripple-carry adder, while the upper 4 bits will have two parallel adders (one assuming carry-in=0 and one assuming carry-in=1). A multiplexer will select the correct upper sum based on the actual carry-out from the lower block. This reduces the critical path delay compared to a pure ripple-carry adder.
