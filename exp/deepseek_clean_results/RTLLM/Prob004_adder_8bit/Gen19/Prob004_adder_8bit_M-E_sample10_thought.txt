Novel Architecture: Carry-Select Adder with 4-bit Blocks
Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid approach combining ripple-carry and carry-select techniques
2. Divides 8-bit addition into two 4-bit blocks for parallel computation
3. First 4 bits use ripple-carry (simple and efficient for lower bits)
4. Upper 4 bits compute both possible sums (assuming carry=0 and carry=1)
5. Final output mux selects correct upper sum based on actual carry from lower bits
6. Reduces critical path by parallelizing upper bit computations

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced by ~50%)
- More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
- Balanced trade-off between speed and area
- Scales well to wider adders by adding more blocks
- Maintains purely combinational logic
