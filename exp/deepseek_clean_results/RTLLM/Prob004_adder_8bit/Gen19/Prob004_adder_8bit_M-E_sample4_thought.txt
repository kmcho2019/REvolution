Hybrid Carry-Select/Ripple-Carry Architecture:
1. Split the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit sections (lower and upper)
2. Use ripple-carry for the lower 4 bits (simple and area-efficient)
3. Implement carry-select for the upper 4 bits (faster critical path)
4. Pre-compute both possible carry scenarios (0 and 1) for upper bits
5. Select correct result based on actual carry-out from lower section
6. This reduces critical path while maintaining reasonable area

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced by ~50%)
- More area-efficient than full carry-select
- Balanced PPA tradeoff
- Easily scalable to wider adders
