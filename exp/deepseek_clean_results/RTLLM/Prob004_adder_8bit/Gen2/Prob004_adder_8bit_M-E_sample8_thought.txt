To improve upon the traditional ripple-carry adder, I propose a hybrid carry-select adder architecture that combines the simplicity of ripple-carry with the performance benefits of carry-select. The key innovations are:

1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks
2. For each chunk, compute both possible sums (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use the actual carry-out from the previous stage to select the correct sum
4. This reduces the critical path while maintaining reasonable area overhead

The design benefits:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced from 8 to 4 full adders)
- Simpler than full carry-select (only two chunks instead of more)
- Maintains combinational nature
- Balanced trade-off between speed and area
