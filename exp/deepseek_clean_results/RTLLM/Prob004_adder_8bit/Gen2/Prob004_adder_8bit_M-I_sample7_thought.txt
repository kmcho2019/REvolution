To optimize the 8-bit adder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This approach:
1. Reduces critical path delay by computing carry signals in parallel
2. Uses generate blocks for scalable implementation
3. Implements group propagate/generate logic to minimize carry propagation delay
4. Maintains the same interface while improving performance
5. Balances logic depth for better power characteristics

The CLA approach will significantly improve performance (timing) while potentially reducing power due to shorter critical paths and less glitching. Area may increase slightly due to additional lookahead logic, but the tradeoff is favorable for most applications.
