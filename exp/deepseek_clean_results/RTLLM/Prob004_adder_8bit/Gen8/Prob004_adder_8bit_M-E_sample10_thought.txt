Novel Architecture: Carry-Select Adder with 2-bit Blocks
Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid approach combining ripple-carry and carry-select techniques
2. Divide 8-bit addition into four 2-bit blocks
3. Each block computes two possible sums in parallel (assuming carry=0 and carry=1)
4. Use actual carry from previous stage to select correct sum
5. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - Maintains reasonable area overhead
   - Better timing than pure ripple-carry while simpler than full carry-lookahead
   - Naturally pipelinable structure
Implementation Details:
- First block is conventional ripple-carry (bits 0-1)
- Subsequent blocks perform carry-select (bits 2-7)
- Final carry chain uses multiplexers for carry propagation
