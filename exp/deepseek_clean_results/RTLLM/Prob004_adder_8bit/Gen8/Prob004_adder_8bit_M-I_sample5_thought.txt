Improvement Strategy:
1. Carry-Lookahead Optimization: Implement a 2-level carry-lookahead structure to break the linear critical path of the ripple-carry adder while maintaining reasonable area overhead.
2. Balanced Grouping: Divide the 8-bit adder into two 4-bit carry-lookahead groups to balance timing and area.
3. Optimized Majority Function: Keep the efficient majority function for carry generation within groups.
4. Hierarchical Carry: Use group propagate/generate signals to compute higher-level carries in parallel.
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse intermediate terms (P,G) for both sum and carry computations where possible.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced critical path from O(n) to O(√n) for 8-bit adder
- Maintains combinational nature and readability
- Better timing performance while keeping area reasonable
- Still fully parameterizable for different widths
