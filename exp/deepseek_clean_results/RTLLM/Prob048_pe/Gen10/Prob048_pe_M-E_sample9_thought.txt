The design implements a 32-bit MAC with:
1. Two-stage pipelined multiplier (16x16 partial products per stage)
2. Carry-save accumulator to break the critical path
3. Early termination for zero inputs to save power
4. Register retiming for better timing closure

Key innovations:
- Multiplier is split into two 16x16 stages with intermediate registers
- Carry-save format reduces adder propagation delay
- Zero-detection logic gates the clock to save power
- Output uses final carry-propagate adder only when needed
