This design implements a 2-stage pipelined MAC with the following innovations:
1. Split the operation into two pipeline stages:
   - Stage 1: Register inputs and compute multiplication
   - Stage 2: Perform accumulation and register output
2. Operand isolation during reset to reduce power
3. Early reset propagation to minimize timing impact
4. Registered inputs to improve timing closure
5. Explicit pipeline registers with enable control

Key benefits:
- Better timing due to pipeline stages (critical path split)
- Reduced power via operand isolation
- Maintains same functionality with 1-cycle latency
- Easier timing closure with registered inputs
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
