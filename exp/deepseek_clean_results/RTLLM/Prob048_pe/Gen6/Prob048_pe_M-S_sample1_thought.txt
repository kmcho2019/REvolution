The simplified design maintains core functionality while optimizing for area and power:
1. Remove the 64-bit product calculation since we only need 32-bit results
2. Directly compute and accumulate the 32-bit product
3. Keep synchronous reset functionality
4. Eliminate intermediate wires by computing product within the always block

This reduces:
- Area by removing unused 64-bit computation
- Power by avoiding unnecessary bit calculations
- Complexity by combining operations
While maintaining the same MAC functionality with 32-bit precision
