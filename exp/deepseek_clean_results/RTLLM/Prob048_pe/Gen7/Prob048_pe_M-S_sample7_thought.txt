The simplified design maintains the core MAC functionality while reducing complexity through:
1. Removing the explicit 64-bit product calculation since we only need 32-bit results
2. Performing direct 32-bit multiplication and accumulation
3. Keeping synchronous reset for clean state initialization
4. Eliminating unnecessary intermediate signals
This reduces area and power by removing unused bits and simplifying the data path, while maintaining the same functional behavior (with potential overflow which is acceptable for many MAC applications).
