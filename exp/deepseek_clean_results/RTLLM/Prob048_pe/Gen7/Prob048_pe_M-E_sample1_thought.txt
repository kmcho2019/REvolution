Here's a novel approach using a pipelined MAC design with overflow detection and saturation logic:

1. Two-stage pipeline architecture:
   - Stage 1: Performs multiplication (registered output)
   - Stage 2: Performs accumulation with saturation logic
2. Overflow handling:
   - Detects when accumulation exceeds 32-bit signed range
   - Saturates to MAX/MIN values instead of wrapping around
3. Registered inputs/outputs for better timing
4. Separate enable signals for each pipeline stage
5. Uses full 64-bit precision for intermediate calculations

Key benefits:
- Improved timing due to pipelining
- More reliable operation with saturation
- Better power efficiency with stage enables
- Full precision maintained until final stage
