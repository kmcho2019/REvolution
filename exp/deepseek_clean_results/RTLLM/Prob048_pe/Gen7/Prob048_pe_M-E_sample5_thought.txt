This design implements a 3-stage pipelined MAC with carry-save accumulation:
1. Stage 1: Partial product generation using radix-4 Booth encoding
2. Stage 2: Carry-save addition of partial products
3. Stage 3: Final accumulation with carry propagation
Key innovations:
- Pipelined multiplier improves clock frequency
- Carry-save accumulation reduces critical path
- Early termination when inputs are zero
- Configurable precision control
- Power gating for idle cycles
