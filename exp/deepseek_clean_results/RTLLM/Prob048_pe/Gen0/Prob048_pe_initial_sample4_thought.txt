The design implements a 32-bit multiplying accumulator (MAC) with synchronous reset. The key components are:
1. A 32-bit register 'c' to store the accumulated result
2. On each clock edge:
   - If reset is active (rst=1), clear the accumulator
   - Else, accumulate the product of inputs a and b into the register
3. The multiplication is done in parallel (combinational logic)
4. The accumulation is registered (sequential logic) to maintain state
