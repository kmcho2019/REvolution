Novel Architecture: Pipelined Power-Optimized MAC with Precision Scaling
1. Two-stage pipeline design to improve timing (separates multiply and accumulate)
2. Dynamic precision scaling - can operate in 16-bit or 32-bit modes
3. Power optimization features:
   - Clock gating when inputs are zero
   - Operand isolation for unused pipeline stages
4. Overflow protection with saturation arithmetic
5. Configurable rounding modes (truncate or round-to-nearest)

Key Innovations:
- The precision_mode input allows dynamic switching between full 32-bit and reduced 16-bit precision
- Zero-input detection automatically gates computations when either operand is zero
- The pipeline registers include enable signals to prevent unnecessary toggling
- Saturation logic prevents overflow wrap-around
- Rounding control allows flexible output behavior
