This design implements a 2-stage pipelined MAC with carry-save accumulation for better PPA:
1. Stage 1: Pipelined Booth-encoded multiplier (reduces critical path)
2. Stage 2: Carry-save accumulator (reduces adder delay)
3. Zero-skipping logic (reduces power when inputs are zero)
4. Registered outputs for better timing closure
5. Early reset propagation to minimize power during reset

Key innovations:
- The multiplier uses radix-4 Booth encoding for area/power efficiency
- Carry-save form reduces adder critical path
- Zero detection skips unnecessary operations
- Separate pipeline registers for better timing
- Optimized reset handling reduces switching activity
