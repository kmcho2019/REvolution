Novel Architecture: Pipelined Booth-Encoded MAC with Carry-Save Accumulation

Key Innovations:
1. Booth Encoding: Implements radix-4 Booth encoding for more efficient multiplication (reduces partial products by half)
2. Two-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree reduction and carry-save accumulation
3. Carry-Save Accumulator: Uses carry-save format to break the critical path between multiplier and accumulator
4. Early Zero Detection: Bypasses computation when either input is zero
5. Overflow Protection: Implements saturation arithmetic for overflow cases

Advantages:
- Better timing due to pipeline stages
- Lower power from Booth encoding and zero detection
- Smaller area from reduced partial products
- More reliable results with overflow protection
