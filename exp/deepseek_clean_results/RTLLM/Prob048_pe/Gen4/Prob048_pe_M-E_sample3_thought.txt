Novel Architecture: Pipelined Multiplier with Early Accumulation

Key Innovations:
1. Two-stage pipeline: Separates multiplication and accumulation into distinct clock cycles
2. Operand pre-processing: Uses Booth encoding for the multiplier to reduce switching activity
3. Carry-save accumulation: Reduces critical path by using carry-save form for intermediate results
4. Power-optimized reset: Only resets necessary registers
5. Overflow protection: Maintains 32-bit output while using extended precision internally

The design splits the operation into:
- Stage 1: Booth-encoded multiplication (partial product generation)
- Stage 2: Final addition and accumulation (using carry-save technique)

This approach improves timing by breaking the long combinational path while reducing power through operand encoding and careful clock gating.
