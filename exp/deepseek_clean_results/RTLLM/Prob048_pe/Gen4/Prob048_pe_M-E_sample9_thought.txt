Here's a novel architectural approach for the MAC unit:

1. **Pipelined Multiplier-Accumulator**: Split the operation into two pipeline stages to improve timing:
   - Stage 1: Register inputs and perform multiplication
   - Stage 2: Perform accumulation and register output

2. **Input Buffering**: Register inputs to prevent glitches from propagating through combinational logic

3. **Early Reset Propagation**: Reset both pipeline stages simultaneously for clean state initialization

4. **Explicit Pipeline Registers**: Clearly separate the multiplier and accumulator stages for better synthesis optimization

This approach provides:
- Better timing closure due to reduced combinatorial paths
- Higher maximum clock frequency
- Clear separation of operations for synthesis tools
- Maintains single-cycle throughput (with 2-cycle latency)
