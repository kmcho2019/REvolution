This design introduces a carry-save accumulator architecture that:
1. Uses carry-save form to break the critical path by separating carry propagation from addition
2. Implements a 2-stage pipeline (multiply stage and accumulate stage)
3. Employs operand gating to reduce dynamic power
4. Uses explicit saturation arithmetic to handle overflow
5. Implements clock-gating for the accumulator register when inputs are zero

The key innovation is the carry-save accumulator which allows the multiply and accumulate operations to be partially parallelized. The pipeline stages are:
- Stage 1: Multiply inputs A and B
- Stage 2: Convert carry-save to binary and accumulate

This reduces the critical path while maintaining single-cycle throughput with 2-cycle latency.
