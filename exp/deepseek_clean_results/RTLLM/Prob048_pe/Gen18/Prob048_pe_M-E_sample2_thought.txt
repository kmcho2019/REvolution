Novel Architecture: Split-Precision MAC with Dynamic Scaling

Key Innovations:
1. Split-Precision Computation:
   - Break 32-bit multiply into two 16-bit segments (high and low)
   - Compute partial products separately
   - Accumulate with proper bit alignment

2. Dynamic Scaling:
   - Detect input magnitude to skip unnecessary computations
   - Scale down computation when inputs are small
   - Preserve full precision only when needed

3. Power Optimization:
   - Clock gating for inactive segments
   - Operand isolation for zero inputs
   - Early termination for trivial cases

4. Timing Optimization:
   - Parallel computation of segments
   - Balanced pipeline stages
   - Carry-select adder for final accumulation

Implementation Benefits:
- Reduced switching activity for power savings
- Shorter critical paths through segmentation
- Maintains full precision when required
- Better PPA tradeoffs than conventional MAC
