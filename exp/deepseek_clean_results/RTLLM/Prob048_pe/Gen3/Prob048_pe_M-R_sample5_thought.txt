The refactored design maintains the same functionality but uses continuous assignments for the combinational logic and separates the sequential elements. Key changes:
1. Move the multiplication to a continuous assignment
2. Use an explicit adder for accumulation (rather than + operator)
3. Separate the combinational and sequential logic
4. Add operand isolation during reset to reduce power
5. Use explicit truncation of the product to 32 bits

This structure makes the datapath clearer and allows for easier pipelining in future optimizations. The synchronous reset and accumulation behavior remains identical to the original.
