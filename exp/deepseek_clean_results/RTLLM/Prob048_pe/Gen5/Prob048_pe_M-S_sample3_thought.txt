The simplified design maintains core functionality while reducing complexity:
1. Remove unnecessary operand isolation logic since reset already handles clearing
2. Eliminate intermediate signals that don't add value
3. Perform direct signed multiplication and accumulation
4. Keep only essential registers and combinational logic
5. Maintain synchronous reset behavior
6. Use blocking assignment for reset to ensure clear initialization

This simplification reduces area and power while maintaining identical functionality. The critical path remains the multiplier-adder chain, but with fewer intermediate signals.
