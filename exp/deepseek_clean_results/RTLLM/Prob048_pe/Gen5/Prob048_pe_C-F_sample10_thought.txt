   - This creates a 2-stage pipeline (multiply then accumulate)
4. Power optimization:
   - Remove unused 64-bit product calculation
   - Keep clean synchronous reset implementation
5. Area optimization:
   - Use only necessary bit-widths
   - Maintain simple, efficient structure
