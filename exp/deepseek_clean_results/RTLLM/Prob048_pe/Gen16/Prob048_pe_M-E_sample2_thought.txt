Innovative Architecture: Pipelined Carry-Save MAC
1. Split the 32-bit multiplication into two 16-bit partial products (Karatsuba-like decomposition)
2. Use carry-save format for accumulation to avoid long carry chains
3. Implement 3-stage pipeline for better timing:
   - Stage 1: Partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Partial product reduction
   - Stage 3: Final accumulation with carry resolution
4. Use redundant number representation during intermediate stages
5. Add pipeline registers to improve clock frequency
6. Include bypass logic for single-cycle operation when needed

Key Advantages:
- Higher clock frequency due to pipelining
- Reduced critical path through carry-save arithmetic
- Flexible operation modes (pipelined or single-cycle)
- Better power efficiency through operand isolation
