This design implements a 2-stage pipelined MAC with saturation arithmetic:
1. Stage 1 (Multiplier Stage):
   - Computes the 32x32 multiplication in first clock cycle
   - Registers the product for next stage
2. Stage 2 (Accumulator Stage):
   - Adds the registered product to accumulator with saturation
   - Implements overflow detection and clamping
3. Features:
   - Pipelined operation improves timing (better WNS/TNS)
   - Saturation arithmetic prevents wraparound on overflow
   - Explicit overflow flag output
   - Registered inputs for better timing
   - Synchronous reset clears all pipeline registers
