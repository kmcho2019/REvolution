The refactored design maintains the same functionality but improves readability and potential synthesis optimization by:
1. Separating the combinational logic (multiplier and accumulator) from sequential elements
2. Using continuous assignments for the product calculation
3. Moving the accumulation logic to a separate always block
4. Maintaining synchronous reset behavior
5. Using blocking assignments for combinational logic and non-blocking for sequential

Key changes:
- Product calculation moved to continuous assignment
- Accumulation logic split into combinational and sequential parts
- Clear separation of timing domains
- More explicit data flow
