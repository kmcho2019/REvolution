The simplified design maintains core functionality while optimizing for area and power:
1. Remove the 64-bit product calculation since we only need 32-bit results
2. Directly compute a*b and accumulate in one step
3. Keep synchronous reset functionality
4. Eliminate unnecessary intermediate signals
5. Use blocking assignments for simpler synthesis

Key simplifications:
- Single 32-bit multiplication and accumulation operation
- No intermediate product storage
- Direct register update in always block
- Maintains same interface and functionality
