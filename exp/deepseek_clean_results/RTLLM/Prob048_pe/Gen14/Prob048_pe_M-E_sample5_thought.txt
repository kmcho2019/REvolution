Novel Architecture: Pipelined Carry-Save MAC
1. Two-stage pipeline for better timing:
   - Stage 1: Booth-encoded partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Carry-save accumulation
2. Power optimization:
   - Dynamic operand isolation for zero inputs
   - Clock gating for inactive cycles
3. Area efficiency:
   - Shared carry-save adders for both multiply and accumulate
   - Bit-serial accumulation for lower bits
4. Precision handling:
   - Full 64-bit intermediate product
   - Configurable output width with saturation
5. Novel features:
   - Early termination for zero operands
   - Configurable pipeline depth
   - Optional overflow protection
