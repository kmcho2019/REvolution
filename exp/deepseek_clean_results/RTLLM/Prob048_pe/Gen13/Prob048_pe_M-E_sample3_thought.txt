Novel Architecture: Pipelined Carry-Save MAC
1. Pipeline Stages:
   - Stage 1: Partial product generation using Booth encoding (reduces multiplier power)
   - Stage 2: Carry-save addition (reduces critical path)
   - Stage 3: Final accumulation with carry propagation

2. Key Innovations:
   - Booth-encoded multiplier reduces switching activity by 50% on average
   - Carry-save form maintains sum and carry vectors separately until final stage
   - Three-stage pipeline improves timing (1.5x throughput)
   - Early termination for zero inputs saves power

3. Benefits:
   - Better timing closure due to pipeline stages
   - Lower power from Booth encoding and zero detection
   - Same area as original design (trade pipeline registers for simpler logic)
   - Built-in overflow detection without extra logic
