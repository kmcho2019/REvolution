The simplified design maintains core functionality while reducing complexity through:
1. Direct synchronous accumulation without intermediate combinational assignments
2. Single always block handling both reset and accumulation
3. Elimination of explicit next_acc calculation by performing operation directly in register update
4. Removal of unnecessary 64-bit product wire since we only need lower 32 bits
5. Same truncation behavior (implicit through register width)

This approach reduces:
- Number of internal signals from 3 to 1
- Combinational logic paths
- Register-to-register paths
While maintaining identical functionality and timing characteristics.
