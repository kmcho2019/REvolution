The simplified design focuses on:
1. Removing redundant operand isolation logic since reset already zeros the accumulator
2. Using direct 32-bit multiplication and accumulation without intermediate 64-bit product
3. Simplifying the combinational logic path
4. Maintaining the same synchronous reset behavior
5. Using blocking assignment for reset to ensure immediate clearing

This reduces area and power by eliminating unnecessary logic while keeping the same functionality. The timing may improve slightly due to simpler combinational paths.
