Novel Architecture: Pipelined Carry-Save MAC
1. Two-stage pipelined multiplier to break critical path:
   - Stage 1: Partial product generation (Booth encoded)
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression
2. Carry-save accumulation:
   - Maintain sum and carry vectors separately
   - Final addition only during readout
3. Precision-aware truncation:
   - Dynamic scaling based on operand magnitudes
   - Saves power by avoiding unnecessary full-width operations
4. Early termination:
   - Detect zero operands to skip computation
5. Power gating:
   - Clock enable for idle cycles

Key Advantages:
- Better timing from pipelined multiplier
- Lower power from carry-save and early termination
- Smaller area from optimized multiplier structure
- Maintains full precision when needed
