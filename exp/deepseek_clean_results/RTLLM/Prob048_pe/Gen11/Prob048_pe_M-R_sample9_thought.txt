The refactored design maintains the same functionality but uses continuous assignments and combinational logic where possible to potentially improve synthesis results. Key changes:
1. Replace the always block with continuous assignments for the product calculation
2. Use a ternary operator for the reset condition
3. Maintain synchronous behavior for the accumulator register
4. Explicitly handle the 32-bit truncation of the product

The approach:
- The multiplier is combinational (continuous assignment)
- The accumulator update is synchronous with reset

- This structure may allow better optimization during synthesis
