This design implements a 3-stage pipelined MAC with carry-save accumulation for better timing closure:
1. Stage 1 (Multiplier Decomposition): 
   - Break 32x32 multiplication into four 16x16 partial products using Karatsuba decomposition
   - This reduces critical path while maintaining precision

2. Stage 2 (Partial Product Reduction):
   - Use 4:2 compressor trees to sum the partial products
   - Generate sum and carry vectors in carry-save form

3. Stage 3 (Accumulation):
   - Use carry-save adder for accumulation to avoid carry propagation delay
   - Convert to final result only when needed (output stage)
   - Includes synchronous reset and overflow protection

Key innovations:
- Pipelined multiplier with balanced stages
- Carry-save accumulation reduces critical path
- Overflow detection and saturation logic
- Power-optimized clock gating for zero inputs
