Novel Architecture: Pipelined Power-Optimized MAC with Precision Scaling
1. Two-stage pipeline design separates multiplication and accumulation for better timing
2. Dynamic precision scaling reduces power when full precision isn't needed
3. Clock gating implementation for power savings during idle cycles
4. Overflow protection with saturation arithmetic
5. Optional bypass path for pure multiplication mode

Key Features:
- Stage 1: Registered inputs and multiplication
- Stage 2: Accumulation with saturation
- Precision control via input ports (4 modes: 8/16/24/32 bit)
- Power gating when not in use
- Overflow protection prevents incorrect accumulation
- Clean separation of pipeline stages for better PPA
