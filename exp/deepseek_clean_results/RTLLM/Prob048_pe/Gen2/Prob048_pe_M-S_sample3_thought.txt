The simplified design maintains the core MAC functionality while optimizing for area and power:
1. Remove the 64-bit product wire since we only need 32-bit accumulation
2. Perform direct 32-bit multiplication and accumulation
3. Keep synchronous reset for clean state initialization
4. Eliminate intermediate signals to reduce area
5. Maintain same clocked accumulation behavior
This simplification reduces area by removing unused bits while keeping the same functional behavior for the 32-bit accumulation case.
