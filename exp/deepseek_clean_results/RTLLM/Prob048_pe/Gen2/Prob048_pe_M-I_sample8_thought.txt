Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipelining: Split the MAC operation into two stages to improve timing:
   - Stage 1: Register the inputs and perform multiplication
   - Stage 2: Perform accumulation
2. Power Optimization:
   - Add operand isolation during reset
   - Implement clock gating when inputs are zero
3. Area Optimization:
   - Use 32-bit product with overflow detection
   - Remove unnecessary 64-bit product calculation
4. Timing Closure:
   - Register multiplier outputs to break critical path
   - Add pipeline registers for better timing

The improved design will:
- Achieve better timing with 2-stage pipeline
- Reduce power with smarter gating
- Maintain similar area while improving performance
- Handle overflow more efficiently
