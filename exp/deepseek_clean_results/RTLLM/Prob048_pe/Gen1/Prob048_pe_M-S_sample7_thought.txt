The simplified design maintains core functionality while reducing complexity:
1. Eliminate the explicit 64-bit product wire since we only need 32-bit result
2. Perform multiplication and accumulation in a single step within the always block
3. Keep synchronous reset and clocked behavior
4. Remove unnecessary intermediate signal (product) to reduce wiring

This simplification reduces:
- Wire routing (no separate product signal)
- Register bits (only final 32-bit result stored)
- Synthesis complexity while maintaining identical functionality
