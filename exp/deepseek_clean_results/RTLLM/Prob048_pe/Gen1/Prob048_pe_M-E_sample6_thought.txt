Novel Architecture: Pipelined Booth Multiplier with Staggered Accumulation

Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth Multiplier: Reduces number of partial products by half compared to standard multiplication, improving speed and area.
2. Two-Stage Pipeline: 
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree reduction and final addition
3. Staggered Accumulation: 
   - Accumulator updates every other cycle to allow full multiplier timing
   - Uses a shadow register to maintain intermediate results
4. Dynamic Clock Gating: 
   - Multiplier stage is clock-gated when inputs are zero
   - Accumulator is clock-gated when no new product is available

Benefits:
- Better timing closure due to pipelining
- Reduced power consumption from clock gating
- Smaller area from Booth encoding
- Maintains throughput with staggered accumulation
