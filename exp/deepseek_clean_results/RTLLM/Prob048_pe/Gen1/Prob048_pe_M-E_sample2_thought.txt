This design implements a 2-stage pipelined MAC to improve timing closure:
1. Stage 1 (Multiply Stage):
   - Performs the a*b multiplication in the first clock cycle
   - Registers the product result
2. Stage 2 (Accumulate Stage):
   - Adds the registered product to the accumulator in the second clock cycle
   - Handles reset synchronously

Benefits:
- Breaks the critical path into two stages (multiply and accumulate)
- Maintains same functionality but with better timing
- Uses registered intermediate results for better timing predictability
- Still maintains synchronous reset behavior
- Output is registered for clean timing

The tradeoff is one extra cycle of latency, but this is often acceptable in DSP applications where throughput is more important than latency.
