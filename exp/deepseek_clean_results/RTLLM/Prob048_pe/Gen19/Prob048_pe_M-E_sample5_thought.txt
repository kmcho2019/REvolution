Novel Architecture: Pipelined Carry-Save MAC
1. Split the 32-bit multiplication into 4 parallel 8x8 multiplications (partial products)
2. Use carry-save adders for intermediate accumulation to reduce critical path
3. Implement 2-stage pipelining:
   - Stage 1: Partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Final accumulation with carry propagation
4. Add operand gating during reset to reduce power
5. Use sign-extension for correct signed arithmetic handling
6. Implement saturation arithmetic to prevent overflow

Key Benefits:
- Reduced critical path through partial product parallelism
- Carry-save technique minimizes propagation delay
- Pipelining enables higher clock frequency
- Power-efficient through operand gating
- More robust with saturation arithmetic
