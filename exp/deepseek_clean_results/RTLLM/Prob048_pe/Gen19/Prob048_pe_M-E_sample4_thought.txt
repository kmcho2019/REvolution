This design implements a pipelined MAC with carry-save accumulation to break the critical path:
1. 2-stage pipeline: First stage computes partial products, second stage handles accumulation
2. Carry-save accumulation: Uses separate sum and carry registers to avoid full adder propagation delay
3. Early reset propagation: Reset clears all pipeline stages immediately
4. Optimized multiplier: Uses radix-4 Booth encoding for partial product generation
5. Final merge: Only converts carry-save to binary on output read when needed

Key advantages:
- Pipelining improves clock frequency by breaking long combinatorial paths
- Carry-save reduces adder delay during accumulation
- Booth encoding reduces number of partial products
- Maintains single-cycle throughput with 2-cycle latency
- Better power efficiency due to reduced switching in carry-save form
