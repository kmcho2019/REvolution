Novel Architectural Approach: Pipelined Multiplier with Carry-Save Accumulation

Key Innovations:
1. Two-stage pipelined multiplier to break critical path
   - Stage 1: Partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Final product summation
2. Carry-save accumulator to reduce adder delay
   - Maintains sum and carry vectors separately
   - Final addition only performed when needed
3. Early zero detection to skip unnecessary operations
   - Bypasses multiplier when either input is zero
4. Configurable output precision with saturation logic

Benefits:
- Improved timing due to pipelined multiplier
- Reduced power via zero detection
- Flexible output handling
- Better area efficiency through optimized accumulation
