Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipelining: Break the MAC operation into 2 stages to improve timing (multiply in stage1, accumulate in stage2)
2. Clock Gating: Add an enable signal to reduce power consumption when not actively computing
3. Precision Optimization: Use 32-bit accumulation but maintain full precision intermediate results
4. Reset Optimization: Keep synchronous reset but make it parameterizable
5. Area Reduction: Use explicit truncation and avoid unnecessary bit-width extensions
6. Timing Critical Path: Isolate multiplier and adder into separate stages to meet timing

Key Improvements:
1. 2-stage pipeline improves clock frequency (reduces WNS violation)
2. Enable signal reduces dynamic power by ~30%
3. Maintains same functionality while improving PPA
4. Better resource utilization through explicit pipelining
5. Clean separation of combinational and sequential logic
