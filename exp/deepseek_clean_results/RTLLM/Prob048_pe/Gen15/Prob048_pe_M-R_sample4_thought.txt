The refactored design will maintain identical functionality but optimize the structure for better clarity and potential synthesis results. Key changes:
1. Use explicit continuous assignments for all combinational logic
2. Separate the multiplier and adder into distinct operations
3. Remove redundant operand isolation since reset already handles clearing
4. Use blocking assignments for intermediate calculations
5. Make the truncation explicit for clarity

This version eliminates unnecessary muxes and makes the dataflow more transparent while maintaining the same cycle-accurate behavior. The synchronous reset and accumulation behavior remains identical.
