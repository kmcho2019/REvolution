Novel Architecture: Pipelined Booth MAC with Carry-Save Accumulation
1. Booth Encoding (Radix-4): Reduces number of partial products by half for better area/power
2. Two-Stage Pipeline: Separates multiplication and accumulation for better timing
3. Carry-Save Accumulator: Avoids carry propagation delay in the critical path
4. Early Zero Detection: Skips computation when either input is zero
5. Guard Bits: Prevents overflow during intermediate calculations

Key Innovations:
- Uses Booth encoding to reduce multiplier complexity
- Carry-save form eliminates long carry chain in accumulation
- Pipeline registers improve clock frequency
- Zero detection saves power on idle cycles
- Guard bits maintain precision without full 64-bit accumulation
