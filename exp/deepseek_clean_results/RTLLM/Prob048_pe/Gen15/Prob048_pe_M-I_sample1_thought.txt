Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipeline the design to break the critical path (multiplier + adder) into two stages:
   - Stage 1: Multiplication only (registered output)
   - Stage 2: Accumulation only
2. Remove redundant operand isolation logic since reset already handles clearing
3. Use 32-bit multiplication directly instead of 64-bit when overflow isn't a concern
4. Add explicit reset synchronization for better timing closure
5. Optimize power by gating the multiplier inputs during reset

This approach will:
- Improve timing by reducing combinatorial path length
- Reduce area by eliminating unnecessary 64-bit operations
- Lower power by minimizing unnecessary switching
- Maintain same functional behavior
