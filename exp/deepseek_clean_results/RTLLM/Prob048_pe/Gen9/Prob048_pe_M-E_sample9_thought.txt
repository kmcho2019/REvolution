The new design employs a dual-register accumulation scheme with carry-save optimization:
1. Split the 32-bit accumulation into two 16-bit halves (low and high) with separate registers
2. Use carry-save addition for the high half to break the critical path
3. Implement ripple-carry addition only in the low half
4. Use a conditional carry propagation scheme that only updates the full sum every N cycles
5. Add operand gating to prevent unnecessary switching during idle cycles

This approach provides:
- Better timing: The 16x16 multipliers and split adders reduce critical path
- Lower power: Carry-save reduces switching activity
- Same functionality: Final result remains mathematically equivalent
- Scalability: Easy to extend to pipelined versions
