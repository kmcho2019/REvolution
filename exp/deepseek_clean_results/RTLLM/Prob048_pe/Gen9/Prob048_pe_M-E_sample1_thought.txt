Novel Architecture: Pipelined Segmented MAC with Dynamic Precision
1. Two-stage pipeline for better timing (separate multiply and accumulate stages)
2. Configurable precision support (16/32-bit) via mode signal
3. Power optimization features:
   - Operand isolation to prevent unnecessary toggling
   - Clock gating for the accumulator
4. Overflow protection with saturation arithmetic
5. Early termination for zero operands to save power
6. Uses carry-save adder structure for the multiplier to reduce critical path

Key Innovations:
- Dynamic precision reduces power when full 32-bit isn't needed
- Pipeline improves throughput to 1 MAC/cycle with 2-cycle latency
- Power-aware design reduces switching activity
- Saturation prevents arithmetic overflow issues
- Zero operand detection skips unnecessary operations
