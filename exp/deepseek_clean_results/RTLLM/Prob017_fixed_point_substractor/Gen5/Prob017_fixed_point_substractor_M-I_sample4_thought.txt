2. **Zero Detection**: Optimize the zero detection logic by:
   - Using a simple equality comparator instead of bitwise OR reduction

4. **PPA Optimization**:

   - Power: Reduced switching activity in the zero detection logic
   - Area: Simpler comparator uses less resources than OR reduction tree
