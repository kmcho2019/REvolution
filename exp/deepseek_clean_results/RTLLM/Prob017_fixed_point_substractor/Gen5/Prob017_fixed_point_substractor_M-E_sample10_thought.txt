My novel approach implements a pipelined fixed-point subtractor with overflow/underflow detection and saturation. The key innovations are:

1. **Two-stage Pipeline**: Separates the subtraction operation and sign handling into distinct stages for better timing closure
2. **Overflow Protection**: Detects and handles overflow/underflow conditions by saturating to max/min values
3. **Early Sign Comparison**: Performs sign comparison in parallel with subtraction for faster critical path
4. **Zero Flag Generation**: Produces a dedicated zero flag output for easier system integration
5. **Parameterized Precision**: Maintains flexible Q.N format support with runtime checks

The architecture:
- Stage 1: Performs raw subtraction and sign comparison in parallel
- Stage 2: Handles overflow/underflow, zero detection, and final result selection
- Special Cases: Explicitly handles zero result and boundary conditions
