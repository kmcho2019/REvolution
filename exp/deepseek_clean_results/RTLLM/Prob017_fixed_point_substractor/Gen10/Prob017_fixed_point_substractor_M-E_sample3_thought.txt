Novel Architectural Idea: "Sign-Magnitude Hybrid Subtractor"

Key Innovations:
1. Dual-Path Processing: Separate logic paths for same-sign and different-sign operands
2. Early Sign Determination: Compute result sign before arithmetic operation
3. Magnitude Comparison: Use a dedicated comparator for absolute values
4. Selective Inversion: Only invert negative operands when needed
5. Zero Detection: Built into the sign computation logic

Advantages:
- More explicit handling of sign cases as required
- Better synthesis characteristics due to separated logic paths
- Clearer representation of the mathematical operations
- Easier to verify and debug
- More efficient hardware mapping for FPGAs

Operation Flow:
1. Extract signs and magnitudes of inputs
2. Determine if signs are same/different
3. For same signs: direct magnitude subtraction
4. For different signs: magnitude addition with sign from larger operand
5. Handle zero case explicitly
