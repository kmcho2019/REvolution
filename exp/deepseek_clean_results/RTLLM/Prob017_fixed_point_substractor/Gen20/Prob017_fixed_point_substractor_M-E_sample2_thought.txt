Novel Architecture Idea: Pipelined Sign-Magnitude Subtraction with Early Zero Detection

Key Innovations:
1. Sign-Magnitude Separation: First stage separates inputs into sign and magnitude components
2. Early Zero Detection: Parallel zero check to quickly identify zero results
3. Magnitude Comparison: Dedicated comparator to determine larger operand
4. Two-Stage Pipeline: 
   - Stage 1: Sign processing and magnitude comparison
   - Stage 2: Selective magnitude operation (add/sub) based on signs
5. Registered Output: Ensures clean timing closure

Advantages:
- Better timing due to pipeline stages
- Clear separation of sign and magnitude processing
- Early zero detection reduces critical path
- More scalable for larger bit widths
- Explicit handling of all edge cases
