The simplified design focuses on:
1. Removing pipelining and associated control signals (clk, reset_n, enable) for combinational operation
2. Eliminating overflow/underflow flags since fixed-point arithmetic naturally wraps around
3. Simplifying zero detection to a direct comparison
4. Maintaining core subtraction logic with proper sign handling
5. Keeping parameterized width support (Q and N)

Key simplifications:
- Pure combinational logic instead of pipelined
- Direct subtraction without intermediate registers
- Removed overflow/underflow detection (relying on natural fixed-point behavior)
- Simplified zero flag generation
- Reduced total lines of code by ~50%
