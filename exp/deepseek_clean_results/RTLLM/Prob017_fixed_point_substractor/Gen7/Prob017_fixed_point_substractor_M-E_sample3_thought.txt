Here's a novel approach using a sign-magnitude representation for the fixed-point subtraction:

1. **Sign-Magnitude Conversion**: Convert both inputs to sign-magnitude format first, separating sign and magnitude calculations.
2. **Magnitude Comparison**: Compare the magnitudes to determine the result's sign and whether we need to add or subtract magnitudes.
3. **Controlled Subtraction/Addition**: Perform either magnitude subtraction or addition based on input signs and comparison result.
4. **Result Construction**: Combine the computed sign with the processed magnitude to form the final result.
5. **Zero Handling**: Explicitly check for zero result and normalize the sign bit.

Advantages:
- Clear separation of sign and magnitude processing
- More intuitive handling of different sign cases
- Easier to verify and debug
- Better matches the mathematical operations
