Novel Architecture: Bitwise Sign-Magnitude Subtraction
1. Convert inputs to sign-magnitude representation to explicitly handle sign logic
2. Perform magnitude comparison and subtraction in separate steps
3. Implement magnitude subtraction using a carry-lookahead approach for better timing
4. Reconstruct final result with proper sign based on comparison and original signs
5. Explicit zero detection for clean sign handling

Key Advantages:
- Explicit sign handling makes design intent clear
- Separates magnitude and sign logic for better optimization
- Carry-lookahead improves critical path timing
- Easier to modify for different rounding modes
- More transparent handling of special cases
