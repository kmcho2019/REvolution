The fixed-point subtractor needs to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases correctly while maintaining proper precision. The key aspects are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: subtract magnitudes and keep the sign
3. For different signs: add magnitudes and determine result sign based on which input is larger
4. Handle zero result explicitly
5. Use parameterized Q and N to maintain proper fixed-point precision
