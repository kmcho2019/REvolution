The fixed-point subtractor needs to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases properly while maintaining the correct sign in the result. The key points are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: subtract magnitudes and keep the sign
3. For different signs: add magnitudes and determine sign based on which input is larger
4. Handle zero result case explicitly
5. Maintain fixed-point precision by keeping N total bits and Q fractional bits
