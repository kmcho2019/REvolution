The fixed-point subtractor needs to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases properly while maintaining the fixed-point precision. The key aspects are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: perform direct subtraction and maintain the same sign
3. For different signs: perform addition of absolute values and determine the result sign based on magnitude comparison
4. Handle zero result explicitly by setting sign bit to 0
5. The operation should respect the fixed-point format defined by Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits)
