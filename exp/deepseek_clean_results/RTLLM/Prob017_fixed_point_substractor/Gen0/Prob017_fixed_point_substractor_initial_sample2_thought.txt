The fixed-point subtractor needs to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases properly while maintaining the fixed-point precision. The key aspects are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: perform direct subtraction and maintain the sign
3. For different signs: perform absolute value addition and determine result sign based on magnitude comparison
4. Handle zero result explicitly by setting sign bit to 0
5. Maintain fixed-point precision by keeping Q fractional bits and N total bits
