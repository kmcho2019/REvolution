The fixed-point subtractor needs to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases properly while maintaining the correct sign for the result. The key points are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: perform regular subtraction and keep the sign
3. For different signs: add absolute values and determine result sign based on magnitude comparison
4. Handle zero result case explicitly
5. Maintain fixed-point precision by properly handling Q fractional bits
