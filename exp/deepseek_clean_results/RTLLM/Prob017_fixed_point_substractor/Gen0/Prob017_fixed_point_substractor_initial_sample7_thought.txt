To implement a fixed-point subtractor, we need to handle both same-sign and different-sign cases properly. The key points are:
1. Determine the signs of inputs a and b
2. For same sign: perform direct subtraction and maintain the sign
3. For different signs: add absolute values and determine result sign based on magnitude comparison
4. Handle zero result explicitly
5. Maintain fixed-point precision by keeping Q fractional bits and (N-Q) integer bits
6. Use parameterized N and Q for flexibility
