Novel Architecture Idea: Sign-Magnitude Based Fixed-Point Subtractor

Key Innovations:
1. Sign-Magnitude Representation: Process numbers in sign-magnitude format to simplify sign handling and overflow detection
2. Early Sign Determination: Compute result sign before arithmetic operation based on input magnitudes
3. Dual-Path Processing: Separate logic paths for same-sign and different-sign cases to optimize critical path
4. Magnitude Comparator: Use dedicated comparator to determine which operand is larger for sign determination
5. Zero Detection: Built-in zero detection with priority encoding for efficient implementation

Advantages:
- Clear separation of sign and magnitude processing
- Optimized critical path by parallelizing sign and magnitude calculations
- More intuitive overflow handling
- Better synthesis results due to explicit magnitude comparison
