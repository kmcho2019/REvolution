Novel architectural approach for fixed-point subtraction:
1. Use sign-magnitude representation internally for clearer arithmetic operations
2. Implement magnitude comparison before subtraction to determine result sign
3. Separate the logic for same-sign and different-sign cases explicitly
4. Add overflow/underflow detection with saturation logic
5. Include parameterized rounding option for fractional bits
6. Use parallel processing for sign determination and magnitude calculation

Key innovations:
- Early sign determination pipeline stage
- Parallel magnitude comparison and subtraction
- Configurable rounding modes
- Saturation arithmetic for overflow protection
