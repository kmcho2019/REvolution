Novel Architectural Idea: Bitwise Subtraction with Sign Handling

1. Bitwise Operation: Instead of relying on Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic, implement subtraction at the bit level to have more control over the process.

2. Explicit Sign Handling: 
   - Separate the sign bits from the magnitude
   - Convert to two's complement if negative
   - Perform magnitude comparison to determine final sign

3. Precision Control:
   - Maintain full precision by operating on all N bits
   - Handle overflow by saturating to maximum/minimum values

4. Zero Detection:
   - Explicitly check for zero result to ensure consistent representation
   - Force positive zero when result is zero

Key Advantages:
- More transparent operation than relying on Verilog's signed arithmetic
- Better control over edge cases
- Easier to modify for specific rounding modes
- Clear separation of sign and magnitude processing
