Here's a novel approach using magnitude comparison and conditional inversion for subtraction:

1. Instead of relying on signed arithmetic, we'll explicitly handle the sign and magnitude separately
2. For same sign inputs: Subtract magnitudes directly and keep the common sign
3. For different signs: Compare magnitudes and conditionally invert the subtraction
4. Zero detection is simplified by checking the raw subtraction result
5. This approach avoids signed arithmetic while maintaining correct behavior
6. The design uses parallel magnitude comparison and subtraction for potentially better timing

Key innovations:
- Explicit sign handling without signed data types
- Parallel magnitude comparison and subtraction
- Conditional inversion based on sign comparison
- Simplified zero detection logic
- Better control over arithmetic implementation
