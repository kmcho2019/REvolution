Pipeline Stages:
1. Stage 1: Input registration and sign/magnitude separation
2. Stage 2: Magnitude comparison and operation selection
3. Stage 3: Final computation and result assembly

Advantages:

- Explicit overflow detection
- Early zero prediction reduces critical path
- Clearer arithmetic in sign-magnitude domain
