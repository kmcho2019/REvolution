2. Perform magnitude subtraction based on the relative sizes of the absolute values
3. Determine the result sign based on original signs and magnitude comparison
4. Handle special cases (zero result, overflow) explicitly
5. Use parameterized width for both integer and fractional parts (Q and N)
6. Implement proper sign extension for correct arithmetic operations

- Separate sign and magnitude processing pipelines

- Proper handling of all sign combinations
