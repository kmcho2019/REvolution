Here's a simplified approach to the fixed-point subtractor:

1. Core Operation:
   - Use simple signed subtraction as the fundamental operation
   - Let Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic handle sign extension and overflow

2. Sign Handling:
   - Eliminate separate sign comparison logic since subtraction inherently handles signs
   - Remove redundant magnitude comparison logic

3. Zero Detection:
   - Keep simple equality check for zero result
   - No need for special handling of zero sign since 0 is naturally positive

4. Simplifications:
   - Remove unnecessary intermediate signals
   - Reduce conditional logic to just the zero case
   - Let the subtraction result flow through otherwise

Key Benefits:
- Much simpler implementation
- Fewer logic gates
- Maintains correct functionality
- Better synthesis results
- Lower power consumption
