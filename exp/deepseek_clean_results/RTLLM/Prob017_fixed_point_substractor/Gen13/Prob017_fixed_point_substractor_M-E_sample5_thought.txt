Novel Architecture Idea: Sign-Magnitude Based Fixed-Point Subtractor

Key Innovations:
1. Sign-Magnitude Representation: 
   - Convert inputs to sign-magnitude format before operation
   - Allows explicit handling of sign bits and magnitudes separately
   - Simplifies comparison and sign determination logic

2. Early Sign Resolution:
   - Determine result sign before magnitude calculation
   - Enables parallel computation paths for magnitude and sign

3. Magnitude Comparator:
   - Dedicated comparator to determine which input has larger magnitude
   - Used for both sign determination and overflow detection

4. Conditional Magnitude Operations:
   - Perform either addition or subtraction of magnitudes based on sign conditions
   - Avoids unnecessary arithmetic operations

5. Zero Detection Optimization:
   - Special case handling for zero results
   - Early termination of magnitude calculations when inputs are equal

Benefits:
- More intuitive sign handling
- Reduced critical path for sign determination
- Clear separation of magnitude and sign operations
- Better testability and debug visibility
