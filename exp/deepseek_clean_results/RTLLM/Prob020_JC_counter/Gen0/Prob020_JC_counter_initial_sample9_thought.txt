To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter:
1. The counter operates synchronously with the clock (clk) and asynchronously resets when rst_n is low.
2. On reset, all bits are cleared to 0.
3. During normal operation:
   - If Q[0] is 0 (ascending phase), right-shift and insert 1 at MSB
   - If Q[0] is 1 (descending phase), right-shift and insert 0 at MSB
4. This creates the characteristic Johnson counter pattern that cycles through all states before repeating.
