A Johnson counter is a type of shift register where the complement of the output is fed back to the input. For a 64-bit Johnson counter:
1. On reset (rst_n low), initialize Q to 64'b0
2. On each clock edge:
   - If Q[0] is 0: Right shift and insert 1 at MSB (Q <= {1'b1, Q[63:1]})
   - If Q[0] is 1: Right shift and insert 0 at MSB (Q <= {1'b0, Q[63:1]})
This creates the characteristic "walking" pattern of the Johnson counter.
