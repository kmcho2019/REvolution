Novel Architectural Idea: Parallel-Load Johnson Counter
1. Dual-Register Approach: Use two 32-bit registers instead of one 64-bit register to enable parallel loading of halves, reducing critical path.
2. Phase-Shifted Operation: The upper and lower halves operate with opposite phases (inverted LSB feedback) to create natural symmetry.
3. Optimized Shift Logic: Implement barrel-shifter style operation where we only need to compute the new MSB value rather than full shifts.
4. Power Gating: Add enable signals for each half to reduce unnecessary toggles when not needed.
5. Reset Optimization: Use synchronous reset only on the active half during operation to minimize reset power.

Benefits:
- Improved timing from reduced fanout (32-bit vs 64-bit paths)
- Potential power savings from selective half-register operation
- Maintains full Johnson counter sequence
- Enables future partial-update optimizations
