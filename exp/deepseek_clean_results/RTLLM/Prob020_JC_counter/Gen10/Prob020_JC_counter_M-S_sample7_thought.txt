The Johnson counter can be further simplified by recognizing that:
1. The entire operation is just a right shift with the MSB being the inverse of the LSB
2. The reset condition can be handled first, then a single operation handles all other cases
3. The shift operation is the same regardless of current state

This allows us to:
- Remove any conditional logic for the shift operation
- Use a single line of logic after reset check
- Maintain identical functionality with minimal operations
The simplified version reduces logic complexity while keeping the same behavior.
