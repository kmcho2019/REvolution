The Johnson counter can be further simplified by recognizing that:
1. The entire operation is just a right shift with the MSB being the inverse of the current LSB
2. The reset condition can be handled with a simple conditional assignment
3. The shift operation can be expressed concisely using concatenation and bit inversion

This leads to an even more compact implementation that:
- Uses minimal operators (just concatenation and bit inversion)
- Maintains the same functionality
- Reduces the logic to a single line of assignment after reset
