Novel Architectural Idea: Dual-Edge Triggered Johnson Counter with Precomputation

Key Innovations:
1. Dual-edge triggering: Utilize both rising and falling edges of the clock to effectively double the counter speed without increasing clock frequency
2. Precomputation: Calculate next state in parallel with current state to reduce critical path
3. Split-phase operation: Alternate between shift directions on different clock phases to optimize power
4. Early evaluation: Compute the MSB value (Q[63]) in the first half of the clock cycle

This design achieves better timing by:
- Distributing the computation across both clock edges
- Reducing the critical path through parallel precomputation
- Maintaining the same Johnson counter sequence but with improved timing characteristics
