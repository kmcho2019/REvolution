Novel Architectural Idea: Dual-Edge Triggered Johnson Counter
1. Instead of using only the rising clock edge, utilize both edges of the clock to double the effective counting speed
2. On positive clock edge: Perform the standard Johnson counter operation
3. On negative clock edge: Perform an additional shift operation with inverted logic
4. This creates a more complex but faster counting pattern while maintaining the Johnson counter properties
5. Reset remains synchronous to the positive edge for stability
6. The counter will cycle through states twice as fast as conventional implementation
7. The pattern will be: 0000→1000→0100→1010→1101→0110→0011→1001→1100→...
