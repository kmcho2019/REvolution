Novel Architectural Idea: 
1. Implement a segmented Johnson counter using 8 parallel 8-bit Johnson counters for better timing closure and potential power savings
2. Each segment operates independently but synchronously, reducing the critical path length
3. Use a circular dependency between segments to maintain the Johnson counter behavior
4. The MSB of each segment controls the LSB of the next segment, creating a distributed ring structure
5. This approach enables better pipelining opportunities and reduces fan-out on the critical path

Key Advantages:
- Improved timing due to shorter critical paths (8-bit vs 64-bit shifts)
- Potential power savings from reduced switching activity in each segment
- Scalable architecture that can be easily extended to wider counters
- Better placement and routing characteristics due to distributed structure
