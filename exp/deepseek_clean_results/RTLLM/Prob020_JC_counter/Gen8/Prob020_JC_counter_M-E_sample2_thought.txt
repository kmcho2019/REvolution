Novel Architecture Idea: Parallel Precompute with Lookahead
1. Instead of the traditional sequential shift-and-invert approach, we'll precompute all possible next states in parallel.
2. We'll use a multiplexer to select between increment and decrement paths based on Q[0].
3. The increment path shifts right and sets MSB to 1, while the decrement path shifts right and sets MSB to 0.
4. This approach exposes more parallelism to the synthesis tool and may enable better PPA optimization.
5. The reset remains synchronous for consistency with standard design practices.
