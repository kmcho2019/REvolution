A novel approach to implement the Johnson counter is to use a circular shift register with a toggle flip-flop that controls the shift direction. The key innovations are:
1. Instead of calculating the MSB from LSB each cycle, we maintain a direction flag
2. The direction toggles when we reach all 1s or all 0s states
3. This reduces the critical path by eliminating the need for LSB inversion in the shift path
4. The design uses parallel load for reset condition

Benefits:
- Reduced critical path (no inversion in shift path)
- Predictable power consumption (consistent shift operation)
- Easier timing closure for high-frequency designs
- Clear state machine behavior
