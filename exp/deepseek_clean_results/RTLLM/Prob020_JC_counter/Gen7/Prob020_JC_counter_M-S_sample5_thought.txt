The Johnson counter can be simplified by recognizing that the shift operation and MSB setting can be combined into a single operation. The key observations are:
1. The MSB is always the inverse of the current LSB during normal operation
2. The shift operation is consistent regardless of state
3. Reset behavior remains the same

This allows us to:
- Eliminate the conditional statement for shift direction
- Use a single shift operation with MSB = ~LSB
- Maintain the same reset functionality
The simplified version reduces control logic while preserving identical behavior.
