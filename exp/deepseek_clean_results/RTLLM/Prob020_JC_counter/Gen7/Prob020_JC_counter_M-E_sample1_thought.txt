Novel Architectural Idea: 
1. Instead of using a single 64-bit register, implement a hierarchical Johnson counter structure using smaller 8-bit Johnson counters (8 counters of 8 bits each)
2. Each 8-bit counter operates independently but synchronizes its MSB with the LSB of the next higher counter
3. Benefits:
   - Better timing due to smaller critical paths (8-bit vs 64-bit)
   - Lower power consumption as only active counters toggle
   - Modular design that's easier to verify and scale
   - Natural pipelining potential between segments
4. Reset propagates through all counters
5. The final output is the concatenation of all 8 counter outputs
