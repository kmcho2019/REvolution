Simplified Design Approach:
1. Single Register Implementation: Use a single 64-bit register instead of pipelined stages to reduce complexity and area.
2. Direct Feedback: The MSB is simply the inverse of the LSB, following standard Johnson counter behavior.
3. Synchronous Operation: All operations occur on clock edges with synchronous reset.
4. Optimized Shift: Perform the shift operation in one step rather than breaking it into stages.
5. Reset Handling: Clear all bits on active-low reset.

Benefits:
- Reduced area (64 flops vs original 64+ pipeline registers)
- Simpler timing path (single register stage)
- Lower power (fewer intermediate toggles)
- Maintains identical functionality
