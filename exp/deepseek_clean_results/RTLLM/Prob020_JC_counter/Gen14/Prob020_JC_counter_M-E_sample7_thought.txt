Novel Architectural Idea: Dual-Edge Triggered Johnson Counter with Predictive Shift

1. Dual-Edge Operation: Utilize both clock edges to effectively double the counting speed while maintaining the same clock frequency
2. Predictive Shift Logic: Pre-compute the next state during the first half cycle to reduce critical path delay
3. Phase-Split Architecture: 
   - Even bits update on positive edge
   - Odd bits update on negative edge
4. Reset Strategy: Maintain synchronous reset but with distributed reset logic for better timing
5. Power Optimization: 
   - Split the counter into two 32-bit halves to reduce switching activity
   - Use conditional inversion to minimize toggling

Key Advantages:
- Effectively 2x speed with same clock frequency
- Reduced critical path through predictive computation
- Better power characteristics from distributed updates
- Maintains full Johnson counter functionality
