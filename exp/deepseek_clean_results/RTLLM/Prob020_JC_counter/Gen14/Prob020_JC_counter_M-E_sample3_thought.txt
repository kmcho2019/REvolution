Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Sliced Parallel Johnson Counter
1. Instead of a single 64-bit shift register, implement 8 parallel 8-bit Johnson counters (slices)
2. Each slice operates independently but synchronously
3. Add a control mechanism to maintain proper Johnson counter behavior across slices
4. Benefits:
   - Improved timing due to shorter critical paths (8-bit vs 64-bit)
   - Potential power savings from localized bit transitions
   - Better scalability for larger counters
5. Implementation:
   - Each 8-bit slice follows standard Johnson counter behavior
   - The slices are chained through their MSB/LSB to maintain overall sequence
   - Reset initializes all slices to 0
   - Transition logic handles the wrap-around between slices
