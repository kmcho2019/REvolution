I'll refactor the Johnson counter using a continuous assignment approach combined with a sequential always block. This separates the combinational logic (feedback calculation) from the sequential elements (register update). The key changes are:
1. Use an intermediate wire for the next state calculation
2. Move the feedback logic to a continuous assignment
3. Keep the register update in the always block
This improves readability and may help with synthesis optimization by clearly separating combinational and sequential logic.
