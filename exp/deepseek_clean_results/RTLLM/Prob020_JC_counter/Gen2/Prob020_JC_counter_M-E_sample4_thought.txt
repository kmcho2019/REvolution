A novel approach to implement the 64-bit Johnson counter using a finite state machine (FSM) with direction control. Instead of simple right shifts, we'll:
1. Maintain a direction flag that toggles when we reach terminal states (all 0s or all 1s)
2. Use this direction to determine whether to shift in 1s or 0s
3. Implement the shift operation using a more efficient circular buffer concept
4. Add early termination detection to optimize power
This approach provides better timing characteristics and more control over the counter's behavior while maintaining the same functionality.
