To simplify the 64-bit Johnson counter implementation:
1. Maintain the same synchronous operation with clock and asynchronous reset
2. The key observation is that the Johnson counter behavior can be expressed as a single conditional shift operation
3. Instead of separate if-else blocks for increment/decrement phases, we can use a bitwise XOR operation to toggle the MSB based on Q[0]
4. This reduces the logic to a single shift operation with a conditional MSB
5. The reset functionality remains unchanged
