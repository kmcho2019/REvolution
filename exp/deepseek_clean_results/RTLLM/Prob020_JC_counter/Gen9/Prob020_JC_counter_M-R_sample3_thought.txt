I'll refactor the Johnson counter using a continuous assignment approach combined with a clocked register. The key changes are:
1. Separate the combinational logic (shift operation) from the sequential element (register)
2. Use an assign statement for the shift operation
3. Keep the register update in a separate always block
This approach provides better visibility into the combinational vs sequential parts of the design and may help with synthesis optimization. The functionality remains identical to the original implementation.
