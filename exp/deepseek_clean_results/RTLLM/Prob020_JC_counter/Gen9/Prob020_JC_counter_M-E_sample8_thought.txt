Novel Architectural Idea:

2. Boundary Synchronization Logic: Add logic to ensure the two halves remain synchronized when crossing between the upper and lower halves of the counter.
3. Power Optimization: Implement explicit clock gating when the counter reaches its extreme states (all 0s or all 1s) to reduce dynamic power consumption.
4. State Prediction: Add a direction predictor that anticipates the next shift direction to potentially reduce setup time requirements.

- Better timing due to reduced critical path
- Lower power through explicit clock gating
- Maintains full Johnson counter functionality
