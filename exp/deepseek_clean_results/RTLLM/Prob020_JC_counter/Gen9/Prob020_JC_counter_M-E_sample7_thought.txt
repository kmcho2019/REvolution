Novel Architectural Idea: Parallel Segment Johnson Counter
1. Instead of a single 64-bit shift register, divide into 8 parallel 8-bit Johnson counters
2. Each segment operates independently but synchronously
3. Add synchronization logic to maintain proper Johnson counter behavior across segments
4. Benefits:
   - Improved timing due to shorter critical paths (8-bit vs 64-bit)
   - Potential power savings from reduced switching activity
   - Better scalability for larger counters
5. Implementation:
   - Use generate blocks for parallel segments
   - Add segment synchronization when crossing boundaries
   - Maintain same external interface as original design
