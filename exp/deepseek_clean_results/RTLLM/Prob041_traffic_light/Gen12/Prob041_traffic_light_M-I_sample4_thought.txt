Improvement Strategy:
1. Power Optimization: Implement clock gating for the counter to reduce dynamic power when the counter isn't changing state.
2. State Encoding: Switch to Gray coding for state transitions to minimize switching activity and reduce power.
3. Output Encoding: Combine the three output signals into a single 2-bit encoded register to save area.
4. Counter Logic: Optimize the counter reset logic to use synchronous load for better timing.
5. Parameterization: Make all timing parameters configurable at instantiation for better flexibility.
