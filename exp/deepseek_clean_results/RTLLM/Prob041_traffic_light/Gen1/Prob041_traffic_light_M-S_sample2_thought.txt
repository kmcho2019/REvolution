The simplified traffic light controller will:
1. Use a single always block for state and counter management
2. Eliminate intermediate registers (p_red, p_yellow, p_green)
3. Directly assign outputs based on current state
4. Simplify pass_request handling by immediately setting counter to 10 if >10 cycles remain
5. Maintain proper timing (60 green, 5 yellow, 10 red)
6. Reset to red state with 10-cycle count

The design combines state transitions and counter logic in one block for clarity, while maintaining all required functionality.
