The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine with three main states: red, yellow, and green. The state transitions are controlled by a counter that counts down clock cycles for each state. When the pedestrian button is pressed during green light, the remaining time will be shortened to 10 cycles if it's currently more than 10. The counter values for each state are: green=60, yellow=5, red=10. The outputs will be synchronized to the clock and reset properly.
