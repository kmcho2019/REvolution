The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine with four states: idle, red, yellow, and green. The state transitions are controlled by a counter that counts down from preset values (10 for red, 5 for yellow, and 60 for green). When the pedestrian button is pressed during green phase, if remaining time is >10 cycles, it will be shortened to 10. The outputs are synchronized with the clock and reset signals. The counter value is also output for monitoring purposes.
