The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine with three main states: red, yellow, and green. The state transitions will be controlled by a counter that counts down from preset values (10 for red, 5 for yellow, and 60 for green). When the pedestrian button is pressed during green light, if there are more than 10 clock cycles remaining, the counter will be shortened to 10. The outputs will be properly synchronized with the clock and reset signals.
