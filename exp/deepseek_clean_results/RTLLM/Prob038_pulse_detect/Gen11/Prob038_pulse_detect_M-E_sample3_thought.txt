Here's a novel approach using a shift register and edge detection:

1. Instead of a traditional state machine, we'll use a 3-bit shift register to track the last three samples of data_in
2. We'll detect both rising and falling edges separately
3. The pulse is detected when we have:
   - A rising edge (0->1) two cycles ago
   - data_in was high one cycle ago
   - A falling edge (1->0) in the current cycle
4. This approach is more robust against glitches and easier to verify
5. The output is purely combinational based on the shift register contents

Advantages:
- No explicit state machine to manage
- Clear relationship between input pattern and output
- Easily extensible to detect different pulse patterns
- Lower latency as output appears immediately when pattern matches
