Instead of using a traditional state machine, we'll implement a shift register approach that tracks the last three values of data_in. This allows us to directly detect the 0->1->0 sequence by examining the stored history. The shift register will be 3 bits wide, storing data_in from cycles [n-2, n-1, n]. When the pattern "010" is detected (which represents 0->1->0 over three cycles), we'll assert data_out. This approach is more direct and eliminates the need for explicit state management.
