This solution uses a finite state machine (FSM) approach with explicit states to track the pulse progression. The FSM has four states: IDLE (waiting for first 0), GOT_0 (received first 0), GOT_1 (received subsequent 1), and PULSE_DETECTED (completed 0->1->0 sequence). The state transitions precisely follow the pulse detection requirements, and data_out is only asserted when we reach the PULSE_DETECTED state. This approach provides more explicit control over the detection sequence compared to the shift register method.
