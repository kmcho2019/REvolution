Novel Approach: Shift Register with Pattern Matching
1. Instead of using a traditional state machine, implement a 3-bit shift register to track the last three samples of data_in
2. Detect the pulse pattern by directly comparing the shift register contents to the desired 3-cycle pattern (010)
3. The output is generated combinatorially when the pattern is matched
4. This approach simplifies the design by eliminating complex state transitions
5. The shift register naturally handles consecutive pulses and maintains timing accuracy
6. Reset initializes the shift register to all zeros
7. The output is synchronous to avoid glitches
