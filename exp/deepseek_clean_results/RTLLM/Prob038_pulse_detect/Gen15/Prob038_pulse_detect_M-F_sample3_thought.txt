To properly detect the 3-cycle pulse pattern (0->1->0), I'll implement the following changes:

1. Use a 3-bit shift register to track the last three values of data_in, which is necessary to detect the complete pattern
2. The output will be asserted when:
   - The oldest sample (shift_reg[2]) is 0 (first cycle)
   - The middle sample (shift_reg[1]) is 1 (second cycle)
   - The newest sample (shift_reg[0]) is 0 (third cycle)
3. Reset will clear the shift register
4. The output is purely combinationally derived from the shift register contents
5. This approach maintains the required timing where data_out is high only on the final 0 of the sequence

The 3-bit shift register provides the complete history needed to detect the pattern, and the combinational output logic ensures correct timing of the pulse detection signal.
