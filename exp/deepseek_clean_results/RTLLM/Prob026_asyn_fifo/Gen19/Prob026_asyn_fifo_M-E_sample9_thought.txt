Innovative Architecture: Pointer Mirroring with Early Full/Empty Prediction

Key Concepts:
1. Pointer Mirroring:
   - Maintain mirrored copies of pointers in both clock domains
   - Update mirrored pointers only when needed (reduces metastability risk)
   - Use these for early full/empty prediction

2. Hybrid Gray/Binary Comparison:
   - Compare Gray codes for empty condition (safe across domains)
   - Compare binary values (within same domain) for full condition
   - Reduces comparison logic complexity

3. Predictive Flag Generation:
   - Generate "almost full" and "almost empty" signals
   - Use these to trigger early synchronization
   - Prevents actual full/empty conditions in most cases

4. Optimized Memory Interface:
   - Single-cycle read with registered output
   - Write-through with byte enables (though not used here)
   - Separate address generation for better timing

Advantages:
- Reduced synchronization latency
- Lower power due to less frequent pointer updates
- More predictable timing behavior
- Early warning of full/empty conditions
