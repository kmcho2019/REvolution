Novel architectural features:
1. Pointer mirroring technique - maintains mirrored copies of pointers in both domains
2. Four-stage synchronizers for robust metastability protection
3. Early full/empty prediction to reduce latency
4. Separate pointer management for better timing closure
5. Optimized Gray code conversion with pre-computation

Key innovations:
- Mirror pointers eliminate need for full pointer synchronization
- Additional synchronizer stages improve reliability
- Predictive logic anticipates full/empty conditions
- Separate pointer update and sync logic simplifies timing
- Optimized Gray conversion reduces combinational paths
