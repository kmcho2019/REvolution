Parallel Pattern Matching with Bit History:
1. Instead of using a traditional FSM or shift register, track the last 4 bits using a compact history buffer
2. Implement parallel comparison of all possible sequence starting points
3. Key innovations:
   - 4-bit circular buffer to store recent input history
   - Pointer-based tracking of buffer position
   - Parallel comparison of all 4-bit windows in the buffer
   - Early termination logic to reduce power
4. Advantages:
   - Constant-time detection (single cycle after 4 cycles of warm-up)
   - No complex state transitions to manage
   - Efficient use of comparators
   - Naturally handles overlapping sequences
5. Reset behavior clears the history buffer and detection flag
