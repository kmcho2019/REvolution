I'll implement a novel "parallel window" approach that checks for the sequence in overlapping 4-bit windows simultaneously. Instead of shifting bits, we'll maintain four 1-bit registers that represent potential sequence starts at different positions. This creates a sliding window effect without actual shifting, reducing register count and power consumption.

Key features:
1. Four 1-bit registers (p0-p3) track potential sequence starts
2. Each register represents a different phase of the sequence detection
3. Combinatorial logic checks for complete sequence across the phases
4. Eliminates shift register power overhead
5. Reduces critical path by breaking the 4-bit comparison into smaller chunks
