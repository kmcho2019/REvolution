The current shift-register implementation is already quite simple and efficient. To further simplify while maintaining functionality:

1. Keep the 4-bit shift register approach as it's minimal and effective
2. Remove the reset condition since initial state doesn't affect functionality (sequence detection starts working after 4 clocks regardless)
3. Make output purely combinational from the shift register
4. Eliminate reset logic reduces area and complexity slightly

This maintains all functionality while being slightly simpler. The design will:
- Still detect '1001' sequences
- Handle overlapping sequences naturally
- Have minimal logic (just shift register and comparator)
- Work immediately after power-on (after 4 clock cycles)
