The shift register approach is already quite simple and efficient. To further simplify while maintaining functionality:

1. Keep the 4-bit shift register as it's the most straightforward way to track the sequence
2. Remove the reset condition since Verilog registers initialize to 0 by default (matches our IDLE state)
3. Make the output purely combinational without any additional registers
4. The design now has minimal components: just a shift register and comparator

This simplification:
- Removes reset logic while maintaining same behavior (initial state is all zeros)
- Keeps the efficient parallel comparison
- Reduces gate count by eliminating reset circuitry
- Maintains single-cycle detection capability
- Still handles overlapping sequences naturally
