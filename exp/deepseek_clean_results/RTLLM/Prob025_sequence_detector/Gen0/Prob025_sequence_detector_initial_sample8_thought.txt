To implement a sequence detector for the pattern "1001", we'll use a Moore finite state machine (FSM) with 5 states: IDLE (initial state), S1 (first '1' detected), S2 (second '0' detected), S3 (third '0' detected), and S4 (final '1' detected). The FSM will transition between these states based on the current state and input bit. The output 'sequence_detected' will be high only in state S4. The design will be synchronous with positive-edge clock and active-low reset.
