To implement a sequence detector for the pattern "1001", we'll use a Moore finite state machine (FSM) with 5 states: IDLE (initial state), S1 (first '1' detected), S2 (next '0' detected), S3 (next '0' detected), and S4 (final '1' detected). The FSM transitions between states based on the current state and input bit. The output 'sequence_detected' is only high in state S4 when the complete sequence is detected. The FSM is synchronous with positive-edge clock and active-low reset.
