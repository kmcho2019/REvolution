Here's a novel approach using a counter-based sequence detector with state compression:

1. Instead of tracking all bits, we'll use a counter to track partial matches
2. The counter increments when we get a correct next bit in sequence
3. Resets to 0 on incorrect bits
4. When counter reaches 3 (4 bits matched), detection occurs
5. This reduces storage needs to just a 2-bit counter instead of 4-bit shift register
6. State transitions are simplified to increment/reset logic
7. Output is generated when counter reaches maximum value
8. Overlapping sequences are naturally handled by the counter logic
9. Lower area than shift register approach (only 2 flip-flops needed)
10. Power efficient as only counter updates rather than full shift
