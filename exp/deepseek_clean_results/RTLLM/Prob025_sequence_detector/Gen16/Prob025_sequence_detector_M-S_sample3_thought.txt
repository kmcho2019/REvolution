The shift register approach is already quite simple and efficient. To further simplify while maintaining functionality:

1. Keep the 4-bit shift register for tracking input bits
2. Replace the parallel comparison with a simple AND of the relevant bits
3. Directly assign the output based on the shift register contents
4. Maintain the same reset behavior

This simplification:
- Eliminates the explicit comparator operation
- Uses direct bit matching which is more hardware-friendly
- Reduces gate count slightly
- Maintains identical functionality
- Still handles overlapping sequences naturally
