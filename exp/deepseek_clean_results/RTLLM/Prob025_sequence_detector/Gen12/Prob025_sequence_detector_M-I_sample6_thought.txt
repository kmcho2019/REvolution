To optimize the sequence detector for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose these improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Keep the shift register approach for its natural pipelining benefits
   - Maintain the simple combinational logic for pattern matching
   - The current timing is already good (0.14ns clock period) so no major changes needed

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add an enable signal to freeze the shift register when not processing data
   - This reduces unnecessary switching activity in the shift register
   - Only update the shift register when new data is available

3. Area Optimization:
   - The current area (25 um²) is already better than reference
   - Maintain the minimal combinational logic structure
   - No further area reduction needed as it would likely impact timing

Key improvements:
- Added enable signal for power savings
- Maintained all original functionality
- Kept the efficient shift register approach
- Preserved the simple pattern matching logic
