Instead of using a traditional Moore FSM with explicit state definitions, I'll implement a shift register approach combined with combinatorial pattern matching. This design will use a 4-bit shift register to store the last 4 input bits and compare them against the target pattern "1001" in parallel. The shift register approach is more compact and leverages Verilog's bitwise operations for efficient pattern matching. The output will be combinatorial based on the current register contents. Reset will clear the shift register.
