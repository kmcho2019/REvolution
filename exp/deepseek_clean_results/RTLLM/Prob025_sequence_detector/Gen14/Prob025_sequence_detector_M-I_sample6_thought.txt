To optimize the sequence detector for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose these improvements:

1. Timing Optimization:
   - Pipeline the detection logic by adding a register stage for the output
   - This will improve clock frequency capability by breaking the critical path

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add an enable signal to freeze the shift register when not needed
   - This reduces unnecessary switching activity

3. Area Optimization:
   - Maintain the efficient shift register approach
   - Keep the simple combinational logic for pattern matching
   - The area is already minimal at 25 um^2

Key improvements:
- Pipelined output improves timing (reported WNS of -0.13ns)
- Enable signal reduces dynamic power
- Maintains the same small area footprint
- Still detects sequences in 4 cycles (with 1 cycle output latency)
- Fully synchronous design for better timing closure
