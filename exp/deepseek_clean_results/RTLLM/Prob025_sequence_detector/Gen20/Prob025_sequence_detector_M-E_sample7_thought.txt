Here's a novel approach using a bitmask comparison technique with dynamic pattern matching:

1. Instead of a shift register or traditional FSM, we'll use a 4-bit history buffer that updates circularly
2. Implement a rotating mask that aligns with the current window of 4 bits
3. Use XOR-based pattern matching to detect the sequence
4. Include a counter to track consecutive matches for robustness
5. Add early termination logic to reset on mismatches

Key Innovations:
- Circular buffer reduces register toggling compared to shift register
- XOR-based matching is more hardware-efficient than direct comparison
- Counter adds noise immunity (optional)
- Early termination saves power by avoiding unnecessary comparisons
- Naturally handles overlapping sequences without extra logic
