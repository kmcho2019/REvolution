The refactored ALU will use continuous assignments (assign statements) instead of procedural blocks (always) for better readability and combinational logic clarity. Key changes include:

1. Convert all operations to assign statements for purely combinational behavior
2. Simplify flag generation by directly computing them based on operation results
3. Use nested conditional operators for operation selection
4. Optimize shift operations by combining similar cases
5. Remove redundant internal wires by computing values directly in output assignments
6. Maintain the same parameter definitions and functionality

The design will be functionally equivalent but more straightforward in its combinational implementation. All flag outputs will be computed directly from the operation results without intermediate registers.
