The refactored ALU design will:
1. Replace the always block with continuous assignments using ternary operators
2. Separate arithmetic and logical operations into distinct assignment blocks
3. Compute flags directly from intermediate results
4. Use function-like expressions for complex operations
5. Maintain the same functionality but with purely combinational logic

Key changes:
- Arithmetic operations computed in separate assign statements
- Shift operations handled with concatenation and sign extension
- Flag generation simplified with direct expressions
- Operation selection through nested ternary operators
- No procedural blocks (always) used
