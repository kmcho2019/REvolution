Optimized ALU Implementation with PPA Improvements

Key Optimizations:
1. Arithmetic Unit: Separated adder and subtractor paths to reduce unnecessary computations
2. Flag Logic: Added gating for flags that are only relevant for specific operations
3. Barrel Shifter: Implemented logarithmic shifting for better timing
4. Power Optimization: Added operation-specific enable signals to reduce switching power
5. Area Optimization: Shared logic between similar operations (AND/OR/XOR/NOR)
