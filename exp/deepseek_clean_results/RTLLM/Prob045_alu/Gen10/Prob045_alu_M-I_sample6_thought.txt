   - Use logarithmic barrel shifter for faster shift operations

   - Use clock gating for sequential elements (if pipelined)

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share resources between adder and comparator
   - Optimize flag generation logic
   - Simplify control logic

- Faster adder implementation
- More efficient shifter
