The improved ALU design focuses on PPA optimization while maintaining full functionality:

1. Performance:
   - Separate the critical path (ADD/SUB) from other operations to reduce timing pressure
   - Simplify overflow detection logic using direct sign bit comparisons
   - Pre-compute common comparison results for SLT/SLTU

2. Power:
   - Remove unnecessary 33-bit operations where 32-bit suffices
   - Consolidate shift operations to share hardware
   - Optimize flag generation to avoid intermediate storage

3. Area:
   - Share adder/subtractor hardware between signed/unsigned versions
   - Combine shift operation logic
   - Simplify flag generation logic

Key improvements:
- Use parallel computation for status flags
- Optimized overflow detection with direct sign checks
- Shared arithmetic unit for ADD/SUB operations
- Consolidated shift operations with multiplexed inputs
- Direct flag assignment for comparison operations
