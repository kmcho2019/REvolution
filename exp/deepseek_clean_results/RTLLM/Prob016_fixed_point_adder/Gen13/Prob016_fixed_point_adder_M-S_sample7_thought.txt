Simplified Fixed-Point Adder Design:
1. Core Concept: Direct two's complement arithmetic without sign-magnitude conversion
2. Simplifications:
   - Remove sign-magnitude conversions to reduce logic
   - Perform arithmetic directly on two's complement numbers
   - Let Verilog handle the sign bits naturally
3. Implementation:
   - Simply add the two numbers (Verilog handles sign extension)
   - Rely on natural overflow behavior of N-bit arithmetic
   - No need for explicit magnitude comparisons
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic complexity
   - Smaller area footprint
   - Maintains correct fixed-point behavior
5. Note:
   - Overflow still occurs naturally within N-bit range
   - Fixed-point position (Q) ensures proper alignment
