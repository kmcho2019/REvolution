Novel architectural approach for parameterized fixed-point adder:
1. Dual-path architecture: Separate logic for positive and negative operands
2. Sign-magnitude representation: Convert inputs to sign-magnitude format before arithmetic
3. Early sign determination: Compute result sign before arithmetic operation
4. Magnitude comparison: Use a dedicated comparator for absolute values
5. Conditional inversion: Use XOR-based sign inversion for subtraction cases
6. Unified result assembly: Combine sign bit with computed magnitude
7. Overflow prevention: Use guard bits during arithmetic to detect overflow before truncation

Key innovations:
- Avoids direct signed arithmetic, reducing synthesis complexity
- Explicit magnitude comparison enables precise result sign determination
- Sign-magnitude conversion makes overflow detection more intuitive
- Guard bits provide overflow detection without extra logic
- Modular design with clear separation of sign and magnitude processing
