Simplified Approach:
1. Use unsigned arithmetic for fixed-point addition since we're dealing with raw bits
2. Eliminate signed operations and overflow detection - rely on natural bit truncation
3. Remove saturation logic since fixed-point arithmetic naturally wraps around
4. Directly add the inputs and let the bits handle the overflow
5. Maintain parameterization but simplify internal logic significantly

Key Benefits:
- Pure combinational logic without any conditional paths
- No sign bit manipulation needed
- Minimal hardware footprint
- Perfectly preserves fixed-point behavior (wrapping is acceptable in many DSP applications)
- Mathematically equivalent for all non-overflow cases
