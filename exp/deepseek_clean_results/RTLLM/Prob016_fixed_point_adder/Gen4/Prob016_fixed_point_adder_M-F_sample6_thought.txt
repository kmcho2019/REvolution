Improved fixed-point adder design addressing feedback:

   - Keep Q and N as parameters but document their relationship (N > Q)

2. Synthesis Improvements:
   - Replace direct magnitude comparison with explicit bit-by-bit comparison
   - Use intermediate registers for arithmetic operations
   - Break down complex conditional assignments

3. Overflow Handling:
   - Add explicit overflow detection by checking carry-out
   - Implement saturation logic for overflow cases
   - Maintain sign consistency during overflow

4. Structural Changes:
   - Separate magnitude and sign processing
   - Use registered output for better timing
   - Explicit width management for all operations

5. Verification Considerations:
   - Ensure all arithmetic operations maintain N-bit width
   - Properly handle sign extension
   - Clear separation of fractional and integer parts
