Novel Architectural Approach: Sign-Magnitude Fixed-Point Adder
1. Core Concept: Convert inputs to sign-magnitude format before arithmetic operations
2. Benefits:
   - Simplifies overflow detection (magnitude comparison only)
   - Enables cleaner saturation logic
   - Reduces critical path by separating sign and magnitude processing
3. Implementation Steps:
   a) Convert both inputs to sign-magnitude format
   b) Compare magnitudes and signs to determine operation type
   c) Perform magnitude addition/subtraction based on sign conditions
   d) Handle overflow by comparing against maximum representable magnitude
   e) Convert result back to two's complement format
4. Optimization:
   - Parallel processing of sign and magnitude paths
   - Early magnitude comparison for operation selection
   - Simplified saturation using magnitude bounds check
