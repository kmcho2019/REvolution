Novel architectural approach:

2. Implement a magnitude comparator that works directly on two's complement numbers
3. Perform all arithmetic operations in two's complement space without explicit conversion
4. Use a priority-encoded overflow detection scheme that considers both addition and subtraction cases
5. Implement a zero-detection circuit for proper sign handling of zero results
6. Optimize the design for minimal logic depth by combining sign determination and magnitude comparison
7. Use parameterized width throughout to maintain flexibility

- Combines sign comparison and magnitude comparison in single operation
- Uses carry-save arithmetic for efficient overflow detection
- Implements early zero detection to optimize critical path
