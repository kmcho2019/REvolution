Novel architectural approach: Sign-Magnitude Based Fixed-Point Adder

Key Innovations:
1. Sign-Magnitude Representation: Operate directly on sign-magnitude format to simplify arithmetic operations
2. Early Sign Resolution: Determine result sign upfront based on input signs and magnitudes
3. Unified Magnitude Path: Single magnitude calculation path for both addition and subtraction cases
4. Efficient Overflow Handling: Use bit-width extension during calculations to prevent overflow
5. Zero Detection: Special handling for zero results to maintain correct sign representation

Advantages:
- Eliminates redundant magnitude calculations
- Reduces comparison logic complexity
- Simplifies overflow detection
- More intuitive handling of sign bits
- Better suited for pipelined implementations

Operation Flow:
1. Extract signs and magnitudes
2. Determine effective operation (add/sub) based on signs
3. Perform magnitude operation with extended precision
4. Normalize result back to N bits
5. Combine with pre-determined sign
