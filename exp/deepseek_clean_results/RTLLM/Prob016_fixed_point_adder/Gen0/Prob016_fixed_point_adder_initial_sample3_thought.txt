The fixed-point adder needs to handle both addition and subtraction of signed fixed-point numbers with configurable precision. The key aspects are:
1. Determine operation type based on sign bits (same = add, different = subtract)
2. For addition: sum absolute values and maintain sign
3. For subtraction: find larger absolute value, subtract smaller from larger, and set correct sign
4. Handle overflow by keeping result within N-bit range
5. Maintain Q fractional bits precision throughout
