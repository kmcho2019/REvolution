The fixed-point adder needs to handle both addition and subtraction of signed fixed-point numbers while maintaining proper sign and precision. The key aspects are:
1. Determine if operands have same sign (both positive or both negative)
2. For same signs: add magnitudes and keep sign
3. For different signs: subtract smaller magnitude from larger and set sign based on comparison
4. Handle overflow by keeping result within N-bit range
5. Maintain Q fractional bits precision throughout
