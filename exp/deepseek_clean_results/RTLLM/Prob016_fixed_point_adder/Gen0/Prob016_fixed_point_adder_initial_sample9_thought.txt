The fixed-point adder needs to handle both addition and subtraction of signed fixed-point numbers while maintaining proper sign and precision. The key steps are:
1. Check the signs of inputs to determine operation (addition or subtraction)
2. For same signs: add magnitudes and keep the sign
3. For different signs: subtract smaller magnitude from larger and set sign accordingly
4. Handle the fixed-point precision by keeping Q fractional bits
5. Manage overflow by ensuring the result fits in N bits
