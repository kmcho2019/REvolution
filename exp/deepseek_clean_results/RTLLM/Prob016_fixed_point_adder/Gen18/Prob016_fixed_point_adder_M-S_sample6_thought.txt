Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove explicit overflow detection - rely on natural fixed-point arithmetic behavior
2. Eliminate saturation logic - let the addition naturally wrap around (common in fixed-point DSP)
3. Simplify to pure addition without sign checks - fixed-point addition is identical to integer addition
4. Maintain parameterization but remove unused Q parameter (kept for interface compatibility)
5. Reduce to single assignment statement for minimal logic

Key Improvements:
- Pure combinational design with direct addition
- No overflow/saturation handling (simpler and often preferred in DSP)
- Minimal logic footprint
- Maintains core fixed-point addition functionality
- Better synthesis characteristics
