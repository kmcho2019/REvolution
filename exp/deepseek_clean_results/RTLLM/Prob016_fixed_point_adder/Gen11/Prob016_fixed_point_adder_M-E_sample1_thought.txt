Novel Architectural Idea: Sign-Magnitude Based Fixed-Point Adder

Key Innovations:
1. Sign-Magnitude Representation: Operate directly on sign and magnitude components instead of two's complement
2. Early Sign Resolution: Determine result sign before magnitude calculation
3. Parallel Magnitude Processing: Compute both possible magnitude operations (add/sub) simultaneously
4. Conditional Selection: Use sign comparison to select correct magnitude path
5. Zero Detection: Special handling for zero results to maintain correct sign

Advantages:
- Eliminates need for overflow detection by design (magnitude never exceeds N-1 bits)
- Parallel computation reduces critical path
- Clear separation of sign and magnitude logic
- Natural handling of zero results with correct sign
- More intuitive fixed-point arithmetic implementation
