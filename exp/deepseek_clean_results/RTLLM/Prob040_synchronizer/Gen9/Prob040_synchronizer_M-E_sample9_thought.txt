Novel Handshake Synchronizer Architecture:
1. Instead of direct enable synchronization, implement a request/acknowledge protocol between clock domains
2. When data_en goes high in clk_a domain, generate a pulse (req) to clk_b domain
3. clk_b domain responds with an acknowledge (ack) after capturing data
4. Data is only updated when both request and acknowledge are active
5. This provides better metastability protection and clear handshake signaling
6. The protocol ensures data is stable during transfer and prevents glitches

Key Advantages:
- More robust synchronization with explicit handshaking
- Clear separation of control and data paths
- Better metastability protection through pulse synchronization
- Maintains all original requirements while adding reliability
