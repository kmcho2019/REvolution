This design implements a handshake-based synchronization mechanism with edge detection:
1. Instead of directly synchronizing the enable signal, we detect the rising edge of data_en in clk_a domain
2. The edge detection generates a pulse that gets synchronized to clk_b domain
3. In clk_b domain, we generate an acknowledge signal that gets synchronized back to clk_a
4. This creates a full handshake protocol ensuring safe data transfer
5. The data is only updated when the handshake completes
6. Includes protection against fast consecutive requests

Key advantages:
- More robust synchronization with explicit handshake
- Clear separation of clock domains
- Built-in protection against back-to-back requests
- Maintains data stability during transfer
- Still meets all original requirements
