This design implements a toggle-based handshake synchronization mechanism:
1. Instead of directly synchronizing the enable signal, we convert it to a toggle signal in clk_a domain
2. The toggle signal is synchronized through two flip-flops in clk_b domain
3. A pulse generator detects the toggle edge in clk_b domain to create a synchronized enable
4. The data is captured only when this synchronized pulse occurs
5. This approach provides better metastability protection and clear handshaking between domains

Key advantages:
- More robust synchronization using toggle signals
- Clear handshake protocol between clock domains
- Lower probability of metastability issues
- Still meets all timing requirements
