The design implements a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer that safely transfers data from clock domain A (clk_a) to clock domain B (clk_b). The key aspects are:
1. Data capture in clk_a domain when data_en is high
2. Two-stage synchronization of the enable signal in clk_b domain for metastability protection
3. Output data is only updated when the synchronized enable signal is active
4. Proper reset handling for both clock domains
The design ensures data stability by requiring data_en to be active for at least 3 clk_b cycles and having sufficient spacing between data changes.
