The design involves synchronizing data from clock domain A (clk_a) to clock domain B (clk_b) using a MUX-based approach. The key aspects are:
1. Capture data_in and data_en in clk_a domain with async reset arstn
2. Synchronize the enable signal through two flip-flops in clk_b domain (metastability protection)
3. Use the synchronized enable to gate the data output in clk_b domain
4. All resets are asynchronous and active-low
5. Data is only transferred when the synchronized enable is high
