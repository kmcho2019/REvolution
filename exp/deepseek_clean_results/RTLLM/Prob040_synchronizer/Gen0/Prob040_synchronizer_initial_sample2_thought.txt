The design implements a multi-bit synchronizer that safely transfers data from clock domain A (clk_a) to clock domain B (clk_b). The key aspects are:
1. Data capture in clk_a domain when data_en is high
2. Two-stage synchronization of the enable signal in clk_b domain
3. Final data output in clk_b domain only when synchronized enable is active
4. Proper reset handling for both clock domains
5. The design ensures metastability protection through the two flip-flop synchronizer for the enable signal
