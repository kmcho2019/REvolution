Novel Handshake Synchronization Architecture:
1. Edge Detection: Detect rising edge of data_en in clk_a domain to trigger synchronization
2. Handshake Protocol: Implement a simple req/ack handshake between clock domains
3. Data Latching: Only capture data when handshake is complete
4. Pulse Synchronizer: Use pulse synchronizer for the req signal to cross domains safely
5. Output Stability: Maintain output until new data is fully synchronized

Key Advantages:
- More robust than simple two-stage synchronizer for infrequent data changes
- Explicit handshake ensures data is only transferred when both domains are ready
- Lower power due to minimal switching activity (only during handshake)
- Clear state machine-like behavior makes timing easier to analyze
- Better metastability protection through explicit acknowledge mechanism
