The original implementation is functionally correct but can be optimized for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area). Here's the improvement strategy:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Remove en_data_reg since data_en can be directly synchronized to clk_b domain (given data_en is stable for 3 clk_b cycles)
   - This saves one flip-flop's power consumption

2. Performance Optimization:
   - Keep the two-flip-flop synchronizer for metastability protection
   - The direct path from data_reg to dataout is already optimal

3. Area Optimization:
   - Remove en_data_reg reduces area by 1 flip-flop
   - No other area reductions possible without compromising functionality

4. Functional Guarantees:
   - Still meets all original requirements:
     - data_in stable during data_en high
     - data_en high for ≥3 clk_b cycles
     - data_in change interval ≥10 clk_b cycles
     - Proper clock domain crossing synchronization
     - Independent reset handling
