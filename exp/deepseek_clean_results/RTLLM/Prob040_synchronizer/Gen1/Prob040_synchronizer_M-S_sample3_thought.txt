The simplified design maintains the core functionality while reducing complexity by:
1. Removing redundant registers - data_reg isn't needed since data_in is stable during data_en
2. Simplifying the enable path by directly synchronizing data_en
3. Maintaining the two-stage synchronization for metastability protection
4. Keeping the output gated by the synchronized enable signal
5. Preserving proper reset handling for both clock domains

The simplification works because:
- data_in remains constant during data_en (so we don't need to capture it separately)
- The change interval is long enough (10 clk_b cycles) to ensure proper synchronization
- data_en duration (3 clk_b cycles) is sufficient for the two-stage sync to work
