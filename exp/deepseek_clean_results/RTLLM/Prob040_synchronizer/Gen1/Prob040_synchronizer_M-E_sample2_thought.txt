This design implements a request-acknowledge handshake protocol for safer clock domain crossing:
1. Instead of direct enable synchronization, we use a request signal (req) from clk_a domain
2. The clk_b domain responds with an acknowledge (ack) after safely capturing the data
3. Data is only updated when both request and acknowledge are aligned
4. The handshake ensures data stability without strict timing requirements on data_en
5. Built-in protection against metastability through dual-flop synchronization of control signals
6. Feedback mechanism prevents data overwrite during transfer
