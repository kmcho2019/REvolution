This design implements a request-acknowledge handshake protocol for safer cross-clock domain transfer:
1. Instead of direct enable signal synchronization, we use a request-acknowledge mechanism
2. When data_en goes high in clk_a domain, a request is generated
3. The request is synchronized to clk_b domain (2 FF stages)
4. When acknowledged in clk_b domain, the data is captured
5. The acknowledge signal is synchronized back to clk_a domain (2 FF stages)
6. Data stability is guaranteed by the handshake protocol
7. The design includes early request termination to prevent unnecessary synchronization

Key advantages:
- More robust synchronization with explicit handshaking
- Clear data validity signaling
- Lower power due to reduced switching activity
- Better timing closure with clear request/ack paths
