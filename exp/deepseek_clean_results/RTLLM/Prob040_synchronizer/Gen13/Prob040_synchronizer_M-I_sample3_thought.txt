Optimization Strategy: Simplified Synchronization with Reduced Logic

Key Improvements:
1. Remove redundant data pipeline stages since data_in is stable during data_en
2. Simplify enable synchronization to single-stage with proper timing constraints
3. Eliminate output change detection since data changes are infrequent (10+ cycles)
4. Combine enable signals to reduce register count
5. Maintain metastability protection while reducing latency

Benefits:
- Reduced area by eliminating unnecessary registers and comparators
- Lower power consumption by minimizing switching activity
- Maintained reliability with proper synchronization stages
- Simplified timing paths for better performance
