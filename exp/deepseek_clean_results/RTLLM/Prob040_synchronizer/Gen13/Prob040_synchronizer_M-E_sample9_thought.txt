Novel Architecture: Pulse-Stretching Synchronizer with Data Hold

Key Innovations:
1. Pulse Stretching Technique: Instead of traditional two-flop synchronization, we stretch the enable pulse to guarantee it's captured across clock domains
2. Data Hold Register: Implement a dedicated hold register that only updates when new valid data is available
3. Glitch-Free Transition: Use a carefully designed state machine to prevent any potential glitches during domain crossing
4. Power-Optimized Design: Minimize unnecessary toggling by only updating registers when absolutely necessary

Advantages:
- Guaranteed capture of enable signal due to pulse stretching
- Reduced metastability risk through controlled enable timing
- Lower dynamic power consumption from minimized register updates
- Simplified timing analysis with clear handshake boundaries
