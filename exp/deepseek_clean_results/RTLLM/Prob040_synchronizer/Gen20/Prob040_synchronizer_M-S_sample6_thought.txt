Simplified design approach:
1. Remove redundant data_sync register since data_in remains stable during data_en period
2. Directly synchronize data_in when data_en is detected in clk_b domain
3. Maintain two-stage enable synchronization for metastability protection
4. Use single always block for clk_b domain operations
5. Keep separate reset domains for each clock domain

Key improvements:
- Eliminate intermediate data_sync register since data_in is stable during transfer
- Reduce total flip-flop count by 4 (from 10 to 6)
- Maintain same functionality with simpler structure
- Still meets all timing and stability requirements
- Takes advantage of problem constraints (data stability during transfer)
