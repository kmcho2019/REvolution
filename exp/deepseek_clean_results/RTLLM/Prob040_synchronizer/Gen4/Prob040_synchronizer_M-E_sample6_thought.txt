This solution employs a pulse-stretching technique combined with stability verification:

1. Key Innovations:
- Uses a pulse stretcher to ensure data_en remains detectable across clock domains
- Implements a stability checker to verify data remains unchanged during transfer
- Employs a voting mechanism for robust synchronization

2. Operation Principle:
- When data_en is detected in clk_a domain, it stretches the pulse to cover 3 clk_b cycles
- The stretched pulse triggers synchronization in clk_b domain
- Data stability is verified by comparing multiple samples
- Output only updates when 3 consecutive samples match

3. Advantages:
- Naturally handles the 3-cycle requirement by design
- Built-in data stability verification prevents glitches
- More tolerant to clock domain crossing variations
- Lower latency than traditional handshake approaches
