This design implements a novel request-acknowledge handshake mechanism between clock domains:

1. Instead of directly synchronizing the enable signal, we detect the rising edge of data_en in clk_a domain
2. Generate a pulse (req) that gets synchronized to clk_b domain
3. When the synchronized pulse is detected in clk_b domain, we capture the data and generate an acknowledge signal
4. The acknowledge signal gets synchronized back to clk_a domain to complete the handshake
5. This ensures safe data transfer while minimizing metastability risks
6. Edge detection prevents unnecessary synchronization when data_en stays high
7. The design naturally handles the 3-cycle minimum requirement through the handshake protocol

Key advantages:
- More robust synchronization through explicit handshaking
- Only synchronizes when needed (on data_en edges)
- Clear separation of concerns between clock domains
- Built-in flow control through acknowledge
- Better power efficiency due to reduced toggling
