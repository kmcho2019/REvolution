Novel Architecture: Edge-Triggered Pulse Synchronizer with Data Stability Window
1. Utilize edge detection on data_en to generate a pulse in clk_a domain
2. Synchronize this pulse to clk_b domain using a toggle synchronizer
3. Implement a data stability window that ensures data_in remains stable during synchronization
4. Use a feedback mechanism to confirm successful synchronization
5. Optimize for minimal latency while maintaining reliability

Key Advantages:
- Explicit pulse-based synchronization for clear domain crossing
- Built-in data stability verification
- Feedback path ensures completion of transfer
- Lower latency than traditional 2-FF synchronizers
- Reduced power consumption through event-driven operation
