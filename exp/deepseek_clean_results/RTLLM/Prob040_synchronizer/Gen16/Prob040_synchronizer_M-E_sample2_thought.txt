Novel Architecture: Dual-Stage Synchronization with Data Stability Detection

Key Innovations:
1. Added a stability checker that verifies data_in remains constant during data_en
2. Implemented a handshake mechanism between clock domains
3. Used a dual-path synchronization approach for better metastability protection
4. Added a data valid signal to ensure only stable data is transferred
5. Optimized for low-power by gating unnecessary operations

Operation Flow:
1. In clk_a domain: Monitor data_in stability during data_en
2. Generate a stable_data signal when conditions are met
3. Synchronize both data and stability signals to clk_b domain
4. Use synchronized stability signal as a qualifier for final output
5. Implement clean handshake to prevent data corruption

Advantages:
- Higher reliability with stability checking
- Better metastability protection with dual-path sync
- Lower power consumption through qualified updates
- Clear handshake protocol between domains
- Maintains all original requirements while adding robustness
