**Self-Adjusting LFSR with Dynamic Feedback:**

1. **Adaptive Feedback Selection**: Instead of fixed feedback taps, the design automatically selects between two different feedback polynomials based on the current state's parity (even/odd number of 1s).

2. **State Preservation**: The LFSR maintains a history of its previous state to prevent short cycles and ensure better randomness properties.

3. **Auto-Seeding**: If the LFSR enters the forbidden all-zero state (which should never happen with proper feedback), it automatically reseeds itself with a non-zero pattern.

4. **Output Masking**: The raw LFSR output is XORed with a rotated version of itself to break linearity while maintaining good statistical properties.

Key Advantages:
- Dynamic feedback improves sequence quality
- Built-in protection against lock-up states
- Simple yet effective output transformation
- Maintains all original interface requirements
- Fully synthesizable with minimal overhead
