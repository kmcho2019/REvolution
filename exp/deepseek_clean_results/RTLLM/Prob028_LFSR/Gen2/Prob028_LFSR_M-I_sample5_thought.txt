Improvement Strategy:
1. Simplify the design to match the original specification (4-bit LFSR with fixed feedback)
2. Remove unnecessary features (parallel load, enable signal) to reduce area
3. Optimize feedback calculation using direct XOR and inversion
4. Maintain registered output only (remove combinational next_out)
5. Keep parameter for potential future flexibility
6. Ensure proper reset initialization (synchronous or asynchronous based on requirements)

The improved version will:
- Be more area-efficient
- Have lower power consumption
- Maintain the same functionality
- Be easier to verify
- Still allow some configurability through parameters
