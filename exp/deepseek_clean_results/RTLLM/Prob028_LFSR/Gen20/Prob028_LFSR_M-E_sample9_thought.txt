Novel LFSR Architecture: Dual-Feedback Parallel LFSR

1. Core Innovation:
   - Implements two parallel feedback paths instead of one
   - Primary feedback: XOR of bits [3] and [2] (standard)
   - Secondary feedback: XOR of bits [1] and [0] (novel addition)
   - Combined feedback uses majority voting for enhanced randomness

2. Operational Benefits:
   - Increased sequence complexity while maintaining 4-bit width
   - Better pseudo-randomness properties
   - Still maintains maximal length sequence (15 states)
   - More uniform bit distribution

3. Implementation Details:
   - Three-phase operation: feedback calculation, majority decision, shift
   - Reset initializes to 4'b0001 (non-zero to avoid lock-up)
   - Clock-edge triggered with synchronous reset

4. Verification Approach:
   - Self-checking testbench verifies sequence periodicity
   - Monitors for repeated patterns
   - Checks all states are visited exactly once in full cycle
