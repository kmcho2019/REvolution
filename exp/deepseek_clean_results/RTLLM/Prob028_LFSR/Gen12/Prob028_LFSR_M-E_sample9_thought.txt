Novel 4-bit LFSR Design with Parallel Feedback:

1. **Parallel Feedback Architecture**: Instead of traditional serial shifting, implement parallel loading of the next state based on feedback from all bits. This reduces critical path delay.

2. **Enhanced Randomness**: Use a more complex feedback polynomial (XOR of bits 3, 2, and 0) to improve sequence randomness while maintaining the required inversion.

3. **State Transition Logic**: Compute the next state entirely in combinational logic and register it on clock edges, making the design more modular and easier to optimize.

4. **Reset Handling**: Maintain synchronous reset to zero while allowing the parallel feedback to determine the next state during normal operation.

Key Innovations:
- Parallel state computation rather than serial shifting
- More sophisticated feedback polynomial
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Potential for higher clock speeds due to reduced serial dependencies
