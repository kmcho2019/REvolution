Simplified LFSR design with minimal essential components:
1. Maintain 4-bit fixed width for simplicity
2. Keep only clock and reset inputs (no enable/valid)
3. Use direct XOR of MSBs without inversion for simpler feedback
4. Initialize to 4'b0001 to ensure non-zero operation
5. Single always block for sequential logic
6. Remove unnecessary comments for cleaner code

This version reduces logic operations while preserving the pseudo-random sequence generation capability. The feedback is simplified to just XOR of the two MSBs (no inversion) which still provides good randomness for a 4-bit LFSR.
