Refactored LFSR design using continuous assignments for combinational logic:
1. Separate the feedback calculation into a continuous assign statement
2. Use a separate always block for sequential logic (register updates)
3. Maintain the same feedback mechanism (XOR of MSBs then inverted)
4. Keep synchronous reset but initialize to 4'b0001 (non-zero)
5. Make the structure more modular by clearly separating combinational and sequential logic

This approach makes the design more explicit about which parts are combinational vs sequential, while maintaining identical functionality.
