Here's a novel approach for the 4-bit LFSR design:
1. Implement a right-shifting LFSR instead of left-shifting for variety
2. Use a different feedback polynomial: XOR of bits 0 and 3 (taps at positions 1 and 4)
3. Make the feedback non-inverted to create a different sequence pattern
4. Add a seed input for initial state configurability
5. Implement asynchronous reset for immediate initialization
6. Include a valid output signal that goes high when the LFSR is initialized
7. The output will cycle through all 15 non-zero states (maximal length sequence)
