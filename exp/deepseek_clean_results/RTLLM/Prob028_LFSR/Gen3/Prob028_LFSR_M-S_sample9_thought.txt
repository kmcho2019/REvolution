Simplified LFSR design focusing on core functionality:
1. Remove parameterization to keep it simple 4-bit
2. Eliminate enable and valid signals to reduce complexity
3. Keep optimized feedback logic (XOR of two MSBs then invert)
4. Maintain synchronous reset but initialize to 4'b0001 (non-zero)
5. Simplified documentation for clarity

This maintains the essential LFSR behavior while removing non-essential features. The feedback mechanism remains the same for pseudo-random sequence generation.
