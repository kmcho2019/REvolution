To refactor the LFSR while maintaining the same functionality:
1. Use continuous assignments for all combinational logic (feedback and output)
2. Implement the state update using a clocked assignment in a separate always block
3. This creates a clear separation between combinational and sequential logic
4. The feedback is still calculated as inverted XOR of the two MSBs
5. Maintain synchronous reset behavior
6. The shifting operation remains identical (left shift with feedback in LSB)
7. Makes the design more modular and easier to verify
