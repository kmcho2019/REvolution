To simplify the LFSR design while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the separate wire for feedback and calculate it directly in the always block
2. Eliminate the continuous assignments since output can be directly assigned from reg
3. Keep the core functionality: XOR of two MSBs, inverted, as feedback
4. Maintain synchronous reset and shifting behavior
5. Reduce the design to a single always block for both state and output
6. Output is now directly the state register, no need for separate assignment
