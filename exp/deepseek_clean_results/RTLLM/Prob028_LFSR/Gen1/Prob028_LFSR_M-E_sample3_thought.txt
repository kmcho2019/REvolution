Novel approach for a 4-bit LFSR with enhanced randomness properties:
1. Instead of standard left shift, implement a bidirectional shift register that alternates direction
2. Use two different feedback taps (bits 3-2 and bits 1-0) selected alternately
3. Add a mode bit that toggles every cycle to control shift direction and feedback selection
4. Reset initializes to a non-zero seed (4'b0001) to avoid lock-up state
5. The output is the current register value plus the feedback bit for better mixing
