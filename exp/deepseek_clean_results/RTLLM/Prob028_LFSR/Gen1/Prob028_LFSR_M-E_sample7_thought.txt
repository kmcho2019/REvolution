This alternative LFSR design uses a different feedback polynomial (x^4 + x^3 + 1) which provides a maximal-length sequence of 15 states (excluding all zeros). Instead of inverting the XOR result, we'll use the raw XOR of bits 3 and 0 (tapping different positions) for feedback. The reset initializes to a non-zero seed (4'b0001) to ensure the register doesn't get stuck in the all-zero state. The design also includes an optional output enable signal for better control.
