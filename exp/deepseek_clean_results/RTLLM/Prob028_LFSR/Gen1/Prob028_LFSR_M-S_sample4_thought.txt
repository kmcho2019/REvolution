To simplify the 4-bit LFSR while maintaining functionality:
1. Keep the same 4-bit register for state storage
2. Remove the inversion of the XOR feedback since it doesn't affect sequence randomness (just shifts the sequence)
3. Maintain synchronous reset to zero
4. Use simple XOR feedback between bits 3 and 2 (without inversion)
5. Still shift left and insert feedback at LSB
This reduces logic operations while preserving the pseudo-random sequence generation property.
