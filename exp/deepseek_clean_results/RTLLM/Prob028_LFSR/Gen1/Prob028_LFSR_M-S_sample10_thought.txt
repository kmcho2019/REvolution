To simplify the 4-bit LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Keep the core functionality of shifting and feedback
2. Remove the inversion of the XOR result to simplify the feedback logic
3. Use a standard polynomial feedback (XOR of bits 3 and 2 without inversion)
4. Maintain the same reset behavior
5. The output remains the current state of the register
This simplification removes one operation (inversion) while still producing a pseudo-random sequence.
