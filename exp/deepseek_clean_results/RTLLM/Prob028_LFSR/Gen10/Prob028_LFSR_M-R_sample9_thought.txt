Refactoring approach:
1. Maintain the same LFSR functionality (4-bit, taps at bits 3 and 2, inverted XOR feedback)
2. Replace the sequential always block with continuous assignments
3. Use a wire for the next state calculation
4. Implement the register using a separate always block for cleaner separation of combinational and sequential logic
5. Keep the same reset behavior (active high, resets to 0)
6. The feedback calculation remains identical (~(out[3] ^ out[2]))
7. The shift operation becomes a continuous assignment for the next state
8. This makes the design more modular and easier to understand the data flow
