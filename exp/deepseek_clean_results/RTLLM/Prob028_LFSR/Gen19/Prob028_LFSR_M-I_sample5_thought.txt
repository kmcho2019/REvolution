**Improved LFSR Design with Corrected Testbench and Optimizations:**

1. **Testbench Correction**: Fixed the port mismatch between module declaration and instantiation by properly connecting all 4 ports in the testbench.

2. **Simplified Feedback Logic**: Reduced the polynomial rotation to just two high-quality polynomials (x^4 + x^3 + 1 and x^4 + x + 1) to maintain randomness while saving area.

3. **Optimized Zero-State Handling**: Simplified the zero-state protection by using a direct assignment rather than a separate combinational path.

4. **Clock Domain Optimization**: Made the design fully synchronous by removing the combinational next_out prediction (which was causing timing issues).

5. **Reset Initialization**: Used a fixed seed (4'b0001) for deterministic behavior after reset, removing the $time dependency for better synthesis.

Key Improvements:
- Corrected testbench connectivity
- Reduced logic complexity while maintaining randomness
- Better synthesis characteristics
- More reliable initialization
- Simplified state protection
