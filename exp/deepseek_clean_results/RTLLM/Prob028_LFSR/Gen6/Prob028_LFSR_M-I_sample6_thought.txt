Improvement strategy for the 4-bit LFSR:
1. Simplify the interface to match original requirements (only clk, rst, and 4-bit out)
2. Keep the core LFSR functionality with proper feedback (XOR of bits 3 and 2, inverted)
3. Remove unnecessary features (quality metrics, gray coding) to focus on core operation
4. Maintain synchronous reset behavior
5. Ensure the LFSR never gets stuck at all-zeros by forcing to 4'b0001 when reset
6. Optimize for area by removing unused logic
7. Keep the design fully synchronous for better timing characteristics
