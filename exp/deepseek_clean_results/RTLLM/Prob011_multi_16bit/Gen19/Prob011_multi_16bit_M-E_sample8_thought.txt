Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Multiplier
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces required operations from 16 to 8 by examining 3 bits at a time
2. Uses signed-digit arithmetic for partial product generation
3. Implements carry-save addition for faster accumulation
4. Optimized state machine with early termination detection
5. Reduced power consumption through operand isolation

Key Features:
- Booth encoder generates partial products (0, ±A, ±2A)
- Carry-save adder tree for efficient accumulation
- Early termination when remaining bits are zero
- Power gating for inactive logic blocks
- Compact 4-bit counter (0-8 states)
