Novel Architecture: Parallel Partial Product Generation with Early Termination

Key Innovations:
1. Parallel Processing: Instead of sequential bit-by-bit processing, generate all partial products in parallel using combinational logic
2. Early Termination: Detect when the multiplier (bin) is zero to immediately terminate calculation
3. Optimized Accumulation: Use a tree of adders to sum partial products efficiently
4. Zero-Case Optimization: Special handling when either input is zero for immediate result
5. Pipelined Output: Register final output to improve timing

Advantages:
- Faster computation (single-cycle for zero case, fixed latency otherwise)
- More efficient power usage (no unnecessary shifting operations)
- Better timing characteristics (parallel structure reduces critical path)
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
