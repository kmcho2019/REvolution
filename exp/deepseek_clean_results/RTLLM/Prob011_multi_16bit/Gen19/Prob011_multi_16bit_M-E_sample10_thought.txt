Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Multiplier
Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces number of partial products by half (from 16 to 8)
2. Uses multiplexer-based partial product generation instead of shift-and-add
3. Implements carry-save adder tree for faster accumulation
4. Optimized control FSM with only 9 states (vs 17 in original)
5. Early termination when multiplier is zero
6. Power gating for inactive stages

Advantages:
- 2x speed improvement (8 cycles vs 16)
- Lower power due to reduced switching
- Smaller area from reduced adder stages
- Maintains same interface and functionality
