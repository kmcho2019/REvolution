Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Multiplier
Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces required additions by ~50% compared to simple shift-add
2. Three-bit overlapping window scans multiplier bits to determine operation type
3. Optimized control FSM with only 9 states (vs 17 in original)
4. Pre-computed partial products for all Booth cases (-2A, -A, 0, +A, +2A)
5. Early termination when remaining bits are zero
6. Simplified done logic using FSM state

Advantages:
- Faster completion (average case ~8 cycles vs 16)
- Lower dynamic power due to fewer additions
- Same interface and functionality
- Better scalability for larger multipliers
