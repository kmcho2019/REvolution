This design implements a modified Booth's algorithm (Radix-4) for 16-bit unsigned multiplication. Key innovations:
1. Uses Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products from 16 to 9
2. Implements a Wallace tree for efficient partial product accumulation
3. Uses a carry-select adder for final sum
4. Optimized control FSM with early termination detection
5. Pipelined architecture for better throughput

Advantages:
- Reduced number of addition operations (9 vs 16 in naive approach)
- Parallel partial product generation
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Early termination when multiplier is zero
- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
