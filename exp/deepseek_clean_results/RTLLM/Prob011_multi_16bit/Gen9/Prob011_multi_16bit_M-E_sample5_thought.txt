This design implements a radix-4 Booth encoded multiplier which:
1. Processes 2 bits per cycle (radix-4) to reduce total cycles from 16 to 9 (including setup)
2. Uses Booth encoding to minimize the number of additions needed
3. Implements a pipelined accumulation stage to improve timing
4. Includes early termination when multiplier bits are exhausted
5. Uses one-hot state encoding for better timing

Key innovations:
- Booth encoding reduces average additions by 50%
- Pipelined accumulation separates shift and add operations
- Early termination when remaining multiplier bits are zero
- Optimized state machine with clear separation of control and datapath
