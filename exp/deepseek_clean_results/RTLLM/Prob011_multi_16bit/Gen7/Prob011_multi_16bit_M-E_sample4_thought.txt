This design implements a modified Booth algorithm with parallel prefix addition for faster multiplication. Key innovations:
1. Booth encoding reduces the number of partial products by half (from 16 to 8)
2. Wallace tree structure for efficient partial product accumulation
3. Parallel prefix adder (Kogge-Stone) for final addition
4. Pipelined operation for better timing closure
5. Early termination detection when multiplier is zero

The design operates in three phases:
1. Booth Encoding: Converts the multiplier to signed-digit representation
2. Partial Product Generation: Creates 8 partial products
3. Wallace Reduction: Accumulates partial products using carry-save adders
4. Final Addition: Uses Kogge-Stone adder for fast final sum
