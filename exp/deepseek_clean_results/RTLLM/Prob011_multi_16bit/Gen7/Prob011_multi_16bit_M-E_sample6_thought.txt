This design implements a radix-4 Booth encoded multiplier with three pipeline stages:
1. Booth Encoding Stage: Encodes the multiplier (bin) into Booth digits (-2, -1, 0, +1, +2)
2. Partial Product Generation: Generates partial products based on Booth digits
3. Wallace Tree Reduction: Uses 3:2 compressors to reduce partial products
4. Final Addition: Performs the final addition of reduced terms

Key innovations:
- Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces number of partial products by half (from 16 to 8)
- Pipelined implementation improves throughput
- Wallace tree structure optimizes critical path
- Early termination when multiplier is zero
- Power-optimized clock gating for idle cycles
