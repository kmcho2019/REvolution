Novel Architecture: Parallel Partial Product Generation with Early Termination

Key Innovations:
1. Parallel Processing: Instead of sequential shifting, generate all 16 partial products in parallel
2. Early Termination: Detect when multiplier is zero to complete operation in 1 cycle
3. Balanced Tree Reduction: Use 4-level adder tree for efficient partial product summation
4. Zero-Case Optimization: Special handling for zero multiplicand or multiplier
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse adders between levels to minimize area

Advantages:
- Worst-case latency reduced to 5 cycles (vs 16 in sequential)
- Average-case latency improved with early termination
- Better timing due to balanced adder tree
- Lower power when inputs are zero
- Scalable to larger bit-widths
