My novel approach implements a radix-4 Booth encoded multiplier with a 3-stage pipeline:
1. Stage 1 (Input): Register inputs and generate Booth encoded partial products
2. Stage 2 (Compression): Use (4:2) compressors to reduce partial products
3. Stage 3 (Final Add): Perform final addition with carry propagation

Key innovations:
- Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
- (4:2) compressors provide efficient partial product reduction
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing closure
- Optimized enable signal propagation through pipeline
- Power-efficient design with conditional computation
