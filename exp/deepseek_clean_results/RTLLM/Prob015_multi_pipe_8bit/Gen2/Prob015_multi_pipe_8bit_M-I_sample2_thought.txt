The improved design implements a proper 3-stage pipeline architecture for better throughput and timing closure:

1. Stage 1 (Input Register): Captures inputs and enables signal
2. Stage 2 (Partial Product Generation): Generates all shifted partial products
3. Stage 3 (Accumulation): Sums all partial products with proper alignment

Key improvements:
- Proper 3-stage pipeline with correct enable signal propagation
- Registered partial products for better timing
- Balanced pipeline stages for equal stage delays
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Optimized partial product generation using shift-and-add
- Proper reset behavior with all registers initialized

The pipeline ensures:
- New inputs can be accepted every cycle
- Results appear after 3 clock cycles
- Maximum operating frequency is improved
- Enable signals properly synchronized through pipeline
