Here's a novel approach using a Wallace Tree multiplier architecture with pipelining:

1. **Wallace Tree Structure**:
   - Uses 3:2 compressors (full adders) to efficiently reduce partial products
   - Naturally parallel structure reduces critical path
   - Better suited for pipelining than simple shift-and-add

2. **Pipeline Stages**:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace Tree reduction (first level)
   - Stage 3: Wallace Tree reduction (second level) and final addition
   - Stage 4: Output registration

3. **Key Innovations**:
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
   - Efficient partial product reduction
   - Optimized enable signal propagation
   - Reduced adder stages compared to traditional approach

4. **Advantages**:
   - Lower latency (4 cycles total)
   - Better timing characteristics
   - More scalable for larger bit widths
   - Power-efficient due to reduced switching activity
