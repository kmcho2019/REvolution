I'll propose a novel architecture using a modified Booth encoding scheme with a 2-stage pipeline for better performance. The key innovations are:

1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**: Reduces the number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4) while maintaining the same multiplication accuracy. This significantly reduces the adder tree complexity.

2. **Asymmetric Pipeline Stages**:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree reduction and final addition

3. **Optimized Enable Logic**: Uses a shift register for enable signal propagation to minimize timing overhead.

4. **Carry-Save Adder Structure**: Implements a 4:2 compressor for efficient partial product reduction before final addition.

This approach offers better timing characteristics and lower power consumption compared to conventional partial product methods, while maintaining the same functionality.
