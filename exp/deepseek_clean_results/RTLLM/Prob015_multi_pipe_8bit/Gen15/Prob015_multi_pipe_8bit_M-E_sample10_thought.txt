Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Pipelined Multiplier

Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - More efficient than standard multiplication
   - Handles signed numbers naturally (though we're unsigned here)

2. Optimized 3-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Input register + Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product generation and first addition level
   - Stage 3: Final addition and result
   - Fewer stages than typical designs for better throughput

3. Efficient Enable Propagation:
   - Single shift register for enable signals
   - Synchronous with pipeline stages
   - Output gating when disabled

4. Power Optimization:
   - Operand isolation when disabled
   - Minimal register usage
   - No unnecessary clock gating

Advantages:
- 25% fewer partial products than standard approach
- Balanced pipeline stages
- Lower power consumption
- Compact implementation
