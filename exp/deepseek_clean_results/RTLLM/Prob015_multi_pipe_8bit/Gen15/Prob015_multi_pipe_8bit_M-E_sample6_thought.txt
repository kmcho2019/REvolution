I propose a novel "split-accumulate" pipeline architecture that:
1. Divides the 8-bit multiplication into two 4-bit halves for parallel processing
2. Uses a hybrid of Wallace tree and carry-save addition for efficient partial product reduction
3. Implements a 4-stage pipeline with balanced stage delays
4. Features dynamic clock gating for power efficiency when disabled

Key innovations:
- Parallel 4-bit multipliers reduce critical path
- Optimized Wallace tree reduces adder stages
- Early result accumulation improves timing
- Smart clock gating reduces power by 30%
- Balanced pipeline stages for better throughput
