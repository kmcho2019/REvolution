The new design implements a Wallace Tree multiplier with 3-stage pipelining for better performance. Key innovations:

1. **Wallace Tree Structure**:
   - Uses carry-save adders to reduce partial products in logarithmic time
   - More efficient than simple ripple-carry addition
   - Naturally breaks computation into pipeline stages

2. **Optimized Pipeline**:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace Tree reduction (4:2 compressors)
   - Stage 3: Final carry-propagate addition

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - Gated clock enable for pipeline registers
   - Conditional partial product generation
   - Early termination when inputs are zero

4. **Area Efficiency**:
   - Shared compression logic
   - Optimized register usage
   - Balanced pipeline stages
