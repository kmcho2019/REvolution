This design implements a radix-4 modified Booth multiplier with 2-stage pipelining:
1. Stage 1 (Decode): 
   - Encodes the multiplier using Booth algorithm (reduces partial products by half)
   - Generates signed partial products with proper sign extension
2. Stage 2 (Accumulate):
   - Uses carry-save adders (CSA) for fast partial product reduction
   - Final addition done with carry-propagate adder (CPA)
3. Key innovations:
   - Booth encoding reduces number of partial products from 8 to 5
   - Carry-save adder tree reduces critical path
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
   - Optimized sign extension handling
4. Power savings:
   - Reduced switching activity due to fewer partial products
   - Clock gating on unused Booth encoders
