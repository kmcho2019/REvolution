Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Pipelined Multiplier

Key Innovations:
1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**: 
   - Reduces number of partial products from 8 to 4
   - More efficient than standard multiplication
   - Handles sign extension automatically

2. **Optimized Pipeline Stages**:
   - Stage 1: Input registration + Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product accumulation + final addition
   - Fewer stages than conventional designs (2 vs 3)

3. **Efficient Partial Product Handling**:
   - Uses Booth encoding to generate ±0, ±A, ±2A
   - Simplified sign extension handling
   - Balanced adder tree for better timing

4. **Power Optimization**:
   - Conditional partial product generation
   - Gated clock enable propagation
   - Operand isolation when not enabled

5. **Area Efficiency**:
   - Reduced number of adders (4 vs 8 partial products)
   - Shared resources between stages
   - Compact Booth encoder logic
