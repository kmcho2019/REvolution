The new architecture employs Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products and a 2-stage pipeline for better throughput:

1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**:
   - Encodes the multiplier (mul_b) to reduce partial products from 8 to 4
   - Each encoded digit selects from [-2A, -A, 0, +A, +2A] operations
   - Sign extension handled properly for negative multiples

2. **2-Stage Pipeline**:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product generation and summation
   - Enables higher clock frequency due to reduced combinational path

3. **Optimized Summation**:
   - Uses carry-save adders for partial product reduction
   - Final addition performed in the second stage
   - Properly aligned partial products with sign extension

Key advantages:
- Fewer partial products (4 vs 8) reduces adder complexity
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Booth encoding provides inherent power savings
- Maintains same latency (2 cycles) with better performance
