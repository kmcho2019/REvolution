This design implements a radix-4 Booth encoded multiplier with 2-stage pipelining for better PPA:

1. **Radix-4 Booth Encoding**:
   - Encodes the multiplier (mul_b) in groups of 3 bits to reduce the number of partial products from 8 to 4
   - Each encoded partial product can be 0, ±A, or ±2A
   - Reduces adder stages and improves timing

2. **Optimized 2-Stage Pipeline**:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product accumulation and final result
   - Fewer pipeline stages reduce latency while maintaining good timing

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - Partial products are only generated when needed (based on Booth encoding)
   - Clock gating for inactive pipeline stages
   - Operand isolation for unused paths

4. **Area Efficiency**:
   - Uses carry-save adders for partial product reduction
   - Shares resources between pipeline stages
   - Minimizes register overhead

5. **Timing Improvement**:
   - Balanced adder tree structure
   - Early carry propagation
   - Critical path optimization through Booth encoding
