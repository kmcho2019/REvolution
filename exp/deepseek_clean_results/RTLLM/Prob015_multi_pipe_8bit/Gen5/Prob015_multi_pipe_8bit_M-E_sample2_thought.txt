The Wallace Tree multiplier offers better performance than the traditional shift-and-add approach by reducing the number of addition stages through parallel processing. My design implements a 3-stage pipelined Wallace Tree multiplier:

1. Stage 1 (Input & Partial Product Generation):
   - Register inputs
   - Generate all partial products in parallel using AND gates
   - Organize them into groups for efficient reduction

2. Stage 2 (Wallace Tree Reduction):
   - Use carry-save adders (3:2 compressors) to reduce partial products
   - Pipeline the intermediate reduction results
   - Maintain proper alignment of bit weights

3. Stage 3 (Final Addition):
   - Use a fast carry-propagate adder (CPA) for final summation
   - Register the final result
   - Pipeline the enable signals properly

Key innovations:
- Parallel partial product generation
- Efficient 3:2 compression tree
- Balanced pipeline stages
- Optimized enable signal propagation
