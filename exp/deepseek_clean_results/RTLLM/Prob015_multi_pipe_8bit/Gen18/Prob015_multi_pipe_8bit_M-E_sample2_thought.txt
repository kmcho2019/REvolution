Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoding with 2-Stage Pipeline

Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - More efficient than standard multiplication
   - Handles signed numbers inherently (though we're unsigned here)

2. Optimized 2-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression and final addition
   - Reduced latency compared to traditional approaches

3. Efficient Enable Handling:
   - Single-cycle enable propagation
   - Output valid signal generation

4. Wallace Tree Compression:
   - 4:2 compressors for efficient partial product reduction
   - Balanced tree structure for better timing

Advantages:
- Fewer partial products mean less hardware
- 2-stage pipeline offers good throughput with lower latency
- Booth encoding provides better power efficiency
- Wallace tree provides faster addition
