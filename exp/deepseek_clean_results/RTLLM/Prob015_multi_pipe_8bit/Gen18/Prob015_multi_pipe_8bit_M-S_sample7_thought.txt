To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll focus on these key optimizations:

1. **Reduced Pipeline Stages**: Combine the partial product generation and initial sums into a single stage since the additions are simple enough to not require separate pipelining.

2. **Simpler Partial Product Generation**: Instead of generating all 8 partial products separately, use a more compact representation that combines them logically.

3. **Optimized Enable Logic**: Reduce the enable pipeline to just track when valid data is present at each stage.

4. **Cleaner Register Structure**: Use fewer intermediate registers by combining related operations.

The simplified design will have:
1. Stage 1: Input registration
2. Stage 2: Partial product generation and accumulation
3. Stage 3: Final output registration
