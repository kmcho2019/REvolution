To implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier with pipelining, we'll break down the multiplication into stages where each stage handles a portion of the computation. The key steps are:
1. Register inputs when enabled
2. Generate partial products in parallel
3. Accumulate partial products through pipeline stages
4. Output final result when computation completes

The pipeline will have 3 stages:
- Stage 1: Register inputs and generate all partial products
- Stage 2: First level of addition (reducing 8 partial products to 4)
- Stage 3: Final addition and output registration

The enable signal will be pipelined along with the data to track valid operations.
