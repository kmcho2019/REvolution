To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a 2-stage pipeline:

1. **Stage 1 (Input Register)**:
   - Register inputs and enable signal
   - Generate all partial products in parallel
   - Sum first 4 and last 4 partial products separately

2. **Stage 2 (Final Sum)**:
   - Add the intermediate sums from stage 1
   - Register final result and output enable

Key simplifications:
- Reduced from 3 to 2 pipeline stages
- Combine partial product generation and initial summation in first stage
- Eliminate intermediate registers between partial product sums
- Maintain proper enable signal propagation
- Keep reset synchronization
