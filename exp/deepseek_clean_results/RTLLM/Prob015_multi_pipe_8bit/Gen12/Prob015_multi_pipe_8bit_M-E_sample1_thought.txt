Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoding with 3-Stage Pipeline

Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces the number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
2. Three-stage pipeline for better throughput:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product generation and compression
   - Stage 3: Final addition and output registration
3. Optimized enable signal propagation through pipeline stages
4. Carry-save adder structure for faster partial sum accumulation
5. Balanced pipeline stages for improved timing closure

Advantages:
- Reduced partial products mean fewer adders and better area efficiency
- Booth encoding provides inherent sign handling (though unused here for unsigned)
- Balanced pipeline stages improve timing (better WNS/TNS)
- Carry-save structure reduces critical path in partial sum accumulation
- Clear separation of concerns in each pipeline stage
