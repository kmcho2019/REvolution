To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll implement these changes:

1. **Simplified Pipeline Structure**:
   - Reduce to 2 stages (Input + Partial Products, Final Sum)
   - Eliminate intermediate registers that don't significantly improve timing

2. **Streamlined Partial Product Generation**:
   - Use direct bitwise AND with shifted multiplicand
   - Remove conditional generation since modern tools optimize zeros well

3. **Simplified Control Logic**:
   - Single enable propagation path
   - Remove clock gating complexity

4. **Optimized Addition**:
   - Single final addition stage
   - Maintain registered outputs for clean timing

The simplified design will:
- Reduce register count and control complexity
- Maintain same functionality with proper enable propagation
- Keep clean timing with registered outputs
- Be more straightforward to understand and verify
