The new architecture employs Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products from 8 to 4, significantly optimizing the critical path. Key features:

1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Encodes the multiplier (mul_b) to generate only 4 partial products instead of 8
   - Reduces addition stages and improves timing

2. Optimized 2-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression and final addition
   - Fewer pipeline stages reduce latency while maintaining throughput

3. Efficient Partial Product Generation:
   - Uses Booth selectors to generate ±0, ±A, ±2A
   - Sign extension handled automatically through proper bit-widths

4. Wallace Tree Compression:
   - 4:2 compressors efficiently reduce partial products
   - Final carry-propagate adder for clean result

This approach achieves better PPA metrics by:
- Reducing critical path through fewer additions
- Lowering power via reduced switching activity
- Decreasing area with fewer arithmetic components
