To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll focus on these key optimizations:

1. **Simplified Pipeline Structure**:
   - Reduce from 3 stages to 2 stages (input registration + computation)
   - Combine partial product generation and summation in one stage

2. **Reduced Register Usage**:
   - Eliminate intermediate partial sum registers
   - Use single product register instead of separate low/high registers

3. **Streamlined Enable Logic**:
   - Single enable register instead of 3-bit shift register
   - Direct enable propagation with one cycle delay

4. **Efficient Computation**:
   - Use simple shift-and-add approach
   - Maintain parallel partial product generation but with simpler structure

The simplified design will have:
- Stage 1: Input registration
- Stage 2: Parallel computation and result registration
