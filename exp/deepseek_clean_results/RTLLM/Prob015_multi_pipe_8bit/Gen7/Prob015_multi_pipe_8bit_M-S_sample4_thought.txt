To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a straightforward 2-stage pipeline:

1. **Stage 1 (Input Register)**:
   - Register inputs and enable signal
   - Generate all partial products in parallel using bitwise AND and shifts
   - Sum all partial products in one step

2. **Stage 2 (Output Register)**:
   - Register the final sum
   - Propagate the enable signal
   - Output the result when enabled

Key simplifications:
- Single addition stage instead of two-step addition
- Direct partial product generation without generate blocks
- Simplified enable signal propagation
- Reduced register usage
- Maintains reset synchronization
