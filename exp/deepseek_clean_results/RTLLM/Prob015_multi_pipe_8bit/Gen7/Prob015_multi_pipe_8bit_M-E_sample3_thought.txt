Novel Architecture: Wallace Tree with Pipelined Reduction
1. Partial Product Generation: Generate all partial products in parallel using AND gates (similar to traditional approach)
2. Wallace Tree Structure: Organize the reduction in 3 pipeline stages:
   - Stage 1: Group partial products into sets of 3 and perform 3:2 compression (carry-save adders)
   - Stage 2: Further reduce using 3:2 and 2:2 compressors
   - Stage 3: Final addition using carry-propagate adder
3. Enable Propagation: Pipeline the enable signal through all stages
4. Power Optimization: Gate the compression logic when not enabled
5. Timing Optimization: Balanced pipeline stages with similar logic depth

Key Advantages:
- More efficient reduction than simple adder tree (Wallace Tree is optimal for partial product reduction)
- Better timing characteristics due to balanced pipeline stages
- Lower power consumption due to gated compression when disabled
- Scalable architecture that could be extended to larger multipliers
