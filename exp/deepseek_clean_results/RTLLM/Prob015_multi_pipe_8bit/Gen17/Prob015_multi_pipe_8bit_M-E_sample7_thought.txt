Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Pipelined Multiplier with Carry-Save Adders

Key Innovations:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - More efficient than standard multiplication
   - Handles signed multiplication inherently (though we use unsigned here)

2. Carry-Save Adder Pipeline:
   - 3-stage pipeline with carry-save adders for faster operation
   - Avoids carry propagation until final stage
   - Better timing characteristics than ripple-carry adders

3. Optimized Pipeline Control:
   - Single clock domain with enable-based pipeline
   - Compact enable shift register (1-bit per stage)
   - Output isolation when disabled

4. Power Optimization:
   - Operand isolation for unused pipeline stages
   - Zero partial products when disabled
   - Gated clock enable signals

Implementation Details:
- Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
- Stage 2: Carry-save addition of first two partial products
- Stage 3: Carry-save addition of remaining partial products
- Stage 4: Final carry-propagate addition and result
