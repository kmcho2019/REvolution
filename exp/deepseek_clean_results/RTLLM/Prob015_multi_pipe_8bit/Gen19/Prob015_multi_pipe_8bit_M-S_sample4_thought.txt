To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality:

1. Reduce to 2 pipeline stages (input registration and computation) for simpler control
2. Remove intermediate sum registers and compute final product directly
3. Simplify enable signal propagation with just 2 stages
4. Maintain operand isolation but implement it more directly
5. Use single-stage partial product generation and accumulation
6. Remove unnecessary intermediate registers while keeping critical path reasonable

The simplified design will:
- Use 2 pipeline stages (input and computation)
- Have straightforward register updates
- Maintain the same interface and functionality
- Be easier to verify and synthesize with less area
