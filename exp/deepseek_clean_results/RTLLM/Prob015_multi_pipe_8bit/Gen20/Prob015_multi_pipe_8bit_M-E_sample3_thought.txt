Novel Architecture:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - Encodes multiplier bits in groups of 3 to detect sequences of 1s
   - Generates partial products with possible values of 0, ±1, ±2

2. Optimized 2-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression and final addition
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing

3. Efficient Sign Handling:
   - Uses sign extension optimization for negative partial products
   - Implements efficient two's complement handling

4. Compact Enable Logic:
   - Single enable pipeline register
   - Early termination when disabled

Key Advantages:
- 50% fewer partial products than conventional approach
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Reduced area due to fewer adders
- Lower power from reduced switching activity
