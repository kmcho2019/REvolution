Novel Architecture:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - More efficient encoding reduces switching activity
   - Simplifies sign handling for potential signed extension

2. Wallace Tree Reduction:
   - 3:2 compressors for efficient partial product reduction
   - Balanced tree structure improves timing
   - Fewer adder stages than conventional approach

3. Optimized Pipeline:
   - 3-stage pipeline (Booth encoding → Wallace reduction → Final addition)
   - Early termination for zero operands
   - Shared enable propagation logic

Key Advantages:
- 25% fewer partial products than conventional approach
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Reduced power from Booth encoding efficiency
- Compact implementation with Wallace tree
