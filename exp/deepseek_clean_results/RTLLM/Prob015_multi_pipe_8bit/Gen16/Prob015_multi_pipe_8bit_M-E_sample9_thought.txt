This design implements a radix-4 modified Booth encoding multiplier with a 2-stage pipeline:
1. **Stage 1 (Booth Encoding & Partial Product Generation)**:
   - Encodes the multiplier using radix-4 Booth algorithm to reduce the number of partial products by half
   - Generates signed partial products with proper sign extension
   - Includes input register stage

2. **Stage 2 (Wallace Tree Reduction & Final Addition)**:
   - Uses a simplified Wallace tree structure for efficient partial product reduction
   - Implements a carry-select adder for final sum computation
   - Includes output register stage

Key Innovations:
- Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces partial products from 8 to 4
- Optimized Wallace tree structure minimizes critical path
- Carry-select adder improves final addition speed
- Efficient enable signal propagation through pipeline
- Reduced register usage compared to conventional designs
