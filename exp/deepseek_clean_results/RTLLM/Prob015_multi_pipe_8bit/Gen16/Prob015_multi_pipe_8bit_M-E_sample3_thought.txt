Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Pipelined Multiplier
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Encodes multiplier bits in groups of 3 (overlapping) to reduce partial products by half
   - Generates partial products of 0, ±1x, ±2x multiplicand
   - Reduces adder stages and improves timing

2. Optimized 3-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product generation and first-level compression
   - Stage 3: Final addition and result registration
   - Fewer stages than conventional designs for better throughput

3. Efficient Enable Propagation:
   - Shift register tracks valid data through pipeline
   - Output gating when disabled to save power
   - Early termination when disabled

4. Power Optimization:
   - Operand isolation when disabled
   - Zero partial products when not enabled
   - Gated clock enable signals
