To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll:

1. Reduce pipeline stages from 4 to 3 (input, partial products, final sum) to decrease latency and complexity
2. Eliminate clock gating to simplify timing analysis
3. Remove operand isolation since it adds complexity with minimal benefit for this small design
4. Use a simpler enable propagation scheme with single-bit registers
5. Combine partial product sums into fewer registers
6. Maintain the same interface and core functionality
