This design implements a radix-4 modified Booth encoding scheme with a 3-stage pipeline:
1. Stage 1: Input sampling and Booth encoding (reduces partial products by half)
2. Stage 2: Partial product generation and first level compression (4:2 compressors)
3. Stage 3: Final addition and result output

Key innovations:
- Uses Booth encoding to reduce number of partial products from 8 to 4
- Implements 4:2 compressors for efficient partial product reduction
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing closure
- Optimized enable signal propagation through pipeline
- Reduced switching activity through Booth encoding
