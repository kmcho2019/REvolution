To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll focus on:

1. Reducing pipeline stages to just 2 (input + calculation) instead of 3
2. Combining partial product generation and summation in one stage
3. Simplifying enable signal propagation
4. Using direct bitwise operations instead of separate partial product wires
5. Eliminating intermediate registers where possible

Key simplifications:
- Single pipeline stage for calculation instead of separate partial sum stages
- Direct computation of final product in one step
- Simplified enable signal handling with just 1 pipeline stage
- Reduced register count by combining operations
- Maintain same functionality with less complexity
