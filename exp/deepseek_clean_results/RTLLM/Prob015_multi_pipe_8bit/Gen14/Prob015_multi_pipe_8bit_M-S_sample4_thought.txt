To simplify the pipelined 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll make these key changes:

1. **Reduce Pipeline Stages**: Combine the partial product generation and summation into a single stage since 8-bit multiplication doesn't require deep pipelining.

2. **Simplify Partial Products**: Generate all partial products in one step using shift operations rather than separate wires.

3. **Streamline Enable Logic**: Use a single-bit enable pipeline register instead of 3 bits.

4. **Optimize Summation**: Perform the final addition in one step rather than splitting into low/high parts.

The simplified design will have:
1. Input registration stage
2. Combined partial product generation and summation stage
3. Output stage with enable signal
