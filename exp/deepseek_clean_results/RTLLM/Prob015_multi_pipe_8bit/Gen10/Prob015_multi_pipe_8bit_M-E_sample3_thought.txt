Novel Architecture: Bit-Serial Pipelined Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition

Key Innovations:
1. **Bit-Serial Processing**: Instead of parallel partial products, process one multiplier bit per cycle, reducing area and power
2. **Carry-Save Addition**: Use carry-save form to avoid carry propagation delays in intermediate stages
3. **Rotating Accumulator**: Implement a barrel-shifter based accumulator for efficient partial product accumulation
4. **Early Termination**: Skip processing when multiplier bits are zero to save power
5. **Compact Pipeline**: 4-stage pipeline optimized for throughput vs area tradeoff

Advantages:
- Reduced area from bit-serial processing
- Lower power from early termination and reduced switching
- Maintained throughput via efficient pipeline
- Better timing from carry-save addition
