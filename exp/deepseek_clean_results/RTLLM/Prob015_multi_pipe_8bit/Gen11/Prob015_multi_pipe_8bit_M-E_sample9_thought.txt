Novel Architecture:
1. Wallace Tree Structure: Uses 3:2 compressors (carry-save adders) to reduce partial products efficiently
2. Pipelined CSA: Implements 2-stage pipelining within the Wallace Tree for better timing
3. Smart Partial Product Generation: Uses AND gates with enable gating to reduce switching activity
4. Final Carry-Propagate Adder: Uses a pipelined Kogge-Stone adder for fast final addition
5. Optimized Enable Logic: Implements precise enable propagation through the pipeline stages

Key Innovations:
- Wallace Tree with built-in pipelining reduces critical path
- Carry-save adders minimize carry propagation delay
- Kogge-Stone final adder provides high-speed addition
- Fine-grained enable control reduces power consumption
- Balanced pipeline stages for maximum throughput
