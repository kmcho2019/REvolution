Novel Architecture: Radix-4 Booth Encoded Pipelined Multiplier
1. Booth Encoding: Use radix-4 Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products from 8 to 4, significantly reducing adder stages.
2. Three-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Input registration and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product generation and compression
   - Stage 3: Final addition and output
3. Efficient Compression: Use 4:2 compressors for partial product reduction instead of simple adders for better timing.
4. Enable Propagation: Carefully pipeline the enable signal to match the data flow.
5. Output Control: Only update output when valid data is available through the pipeline.

Key Advantages:
- Fewer partial products (4 vs 8) reduces adder stages
- 4:2 compressors provide better timing than ripple-carry adders
- Balanced pipeline stages for maximum clock frequency
- Power-efficient due to reduced switching activity
