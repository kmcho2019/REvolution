The new design implements an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a radix-4 Booth encoding scheme with a 3-stage pipeline. This approach:
1. Uses Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
2. Implements a balanced 3-stage pipeline (Booth encoding → Wallace tree reduction → Final addition)
3. Includes proper enable signal propagation through all stages
4. Uses carry-save adders in the reduction stage for better timing
5. Maintains full 16-bit precision throughout the pipeline

The radix-4 Booth encoding provides better efficiency than simple partial product generation, while the 3-stage pipeline offers good throughput with reasonable latency.
