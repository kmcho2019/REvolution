To simplify the design while maintaining functionality:
1. Eliminate the case statement by using a shift register approach
2. Use a single 4-bit register to store the parallel input and shift it out
3. Maintain the 2-bit counter for tracking output bits
4. Generate valid_out only when new data is loaded (counter=0)
5. Simplify the bit selection logic by always outputting the MSB of the shift register
6. Shift left on each clock cycle to bring the next bit to MSB position
This approach reduces logic complexity and improves timing by eliminating the multiplexer from the case statement.
