The parallel-to-serial converter will use a 2-bit counter to track which bit is being output. When the counter reaches 3 (indicating the last bit), it will load new parallel data and reset the counter. The valid_out signal will be high only when new data is loaded (counter=3). The serial output will always be the MSB of the shifted data register. The data register will be shifted left each cycle to bring the next bit to the MSB position.
