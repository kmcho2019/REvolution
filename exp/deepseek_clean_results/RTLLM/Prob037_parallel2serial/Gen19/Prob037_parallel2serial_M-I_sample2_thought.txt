Improvement Strategy:
1. State Encoding: Replace one-hot with binary counter (2 bits) to reduce area and power while maintaining timing
2. Shift Logic: Simplify shift operation by using direct bit selection from register
3. Valid Generation: Optimize valid_out logic by making it purely combinational
4. Reset Handling: Maintain synchronous reset for better timing closure
5. Power Optimization: Remove clock gating complexity since power savings were minimal

Key Improvements:
- Binary counter reduces state machine area by 50% and power by reducing switching activity
- Direct bit selection eliminates shift operation overhead
- Simplified valid_out generation reduces combinational path
- Maintained synchronous reset for predictable behavior
- Removed clock gating for simpler implementation with minimal power impact
