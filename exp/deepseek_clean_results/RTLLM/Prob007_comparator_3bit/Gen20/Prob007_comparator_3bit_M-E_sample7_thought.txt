My novel approach implements a hierarchical comparator with early termination logic to optimize both power and performance. The key innovations are:

1. **Hierarchical Comparison:** Break the 3-bit comparison into two stages:
   - First compare MSB (bit 2)
   - Only proceed to lower bits if MSBs are equal

2. **Early Termination:** 
   - The comparison stops as soon as a definitive result is found
   - This reduces switching activity in lower bits when not needed

3. **Output Encoding:** 
   - Use a priority encoder style approach
   - Directly generate mutually exclusive outputs without extra logic

4. **Power Optimization:**
   - Minimize logic depth for common cases
   - Reduce unnecessary bit comparisons when possible

This approach is particularly efficient when input numbers are frequently different in their MSBs, which is common in many applications.
