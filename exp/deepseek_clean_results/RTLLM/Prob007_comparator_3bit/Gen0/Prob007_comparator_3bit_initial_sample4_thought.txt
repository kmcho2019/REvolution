To implement a 3-bit comparator, we'll compare the two 3-bit numbers A and B bit by bit from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). The comparison starts at the MSB (bit 2), and if the bits are equal, we move to the next lower bit until we find a difference or all bits are equal. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are mutually exclusive and determined based on the first non-equal bit pair or if all bits are equal.
