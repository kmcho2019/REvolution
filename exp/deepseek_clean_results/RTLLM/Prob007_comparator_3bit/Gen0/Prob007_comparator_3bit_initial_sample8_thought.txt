To implement a 3-bit comparator, we need to compare the two 3-bit inputs A and B bit by bit from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). The comparison starts at the MSB, and if the bits are equal, we move to the next lower bit until a difference is found or all bits are compared. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are mutually exclusive and are determined based on the comparison results.
