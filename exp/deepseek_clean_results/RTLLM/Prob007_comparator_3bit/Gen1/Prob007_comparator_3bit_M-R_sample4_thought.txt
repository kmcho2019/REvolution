To optimize the 3-bit comparator, I'll replace the procedural always block with continuous assignments using assign statements. This approach:
1. Eliminates the need for procedural blocks, reducing area
2. Makes the comparison logic purely combinational and parallel
3. Reduces power by minimizing redundant logic evaluations
4. Maintains the same functionality while improving PPA metrics
The outputs will be directly assigned based on the comparison results of A and B, with mutually exclusive conditions.
