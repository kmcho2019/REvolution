A novel approach to implement the 3-bit comparator is to use a hierarchical comparison method that breaks down the comparison into smaller sub-comparisons and combines their results. This method:
1. First compares the most significant bits (MSBs) to get an initial result
2. Only if MSBs are equal, proceeds to compare the middle bits
3. Only if both MSBs and middle bits are equal, compares the least significant bits (LSBs)
4. Uses intermediate signals to store partial comparison results
5. Generates final outputs based on the hierarchical comparison results
This approach mimics how humans compare numbers digit by digit and can potentially optimize the critical path in hardware.
