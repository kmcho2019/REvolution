To simplify the comparator while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the 4-bit subtraction since 3-bit inputs can be compared directly without overflow concerns
2. Implement hierarchical bit-by-bit comparison starting from MSB
3. Use simple combinational logic for each comparison case:
   - A > B when any higher bit is greater while previous bits are equal
   - A < B when any higher bit is smaller while previous bits are equal
   - A == B when all bits are equal
4. This approach reduces area by eliminating the subtractor and uses minimal logic gates
5. Outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction
