Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Parallel Magnitude Comparison with Early Termination

Key Innovations:
1. Bit-parallel comparison: Compare all bits simultaneously rather than hierarchically
2. Early termination: Use OR reduction to detect any inequality at each bit position
3. Priority encoding: Generate comparison results based on the most significant differing bit
4. Optimized logic structure:
   - Single XOR layer for equality detection
   - AND-OR logic for magnitude comparison
   - Shared intermediate signals between outputs

Advantages:
- Reduced critical path by parallel bit comparison
- Lower power consumption through minimized switching activity
- Compact area from shared logic and reduced gate count
- Clear structural representation of the comparison logic
