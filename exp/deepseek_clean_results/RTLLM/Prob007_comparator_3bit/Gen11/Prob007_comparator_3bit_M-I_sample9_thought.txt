To optimize the comparator for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical bitwise comparison approach that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB, stopping at the first unequal bit
2. Uses simple AND/OR/XOR gates for comparison rather than arithmetic subtraction
3. Eliminates the need for 4-bit operations since 3-bit comparison doesn't need overflow handling
4. Structures the logic to minimize switching activity and glitches
5. Uses shared logic for equality detection to reduce area

Key improvements:
- Direct bitwise comparison is faster than subtraction
- Hierarchical structure reduces unnecessary switching
- Simpler gates reduce area and power
- Maintains mutual exclusivity of outputs
- No arithmetic operations means no carry propagation delay
