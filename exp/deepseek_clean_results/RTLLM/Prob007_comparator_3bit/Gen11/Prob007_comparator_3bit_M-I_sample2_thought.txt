To optimize the comparator for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical bitwise comparison approach that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB, stopping at the first unequal bit
2. Uses simple AND/OR gates for magnitude comparison
3. Shares logic between equality and magnitude checks
4. Eliminates the subtractor to reduce area and power
Key improvements:
- No arithmetic operations (subtraction removed)
- Early termination in comparison logic
- Shared XOR gates for equality check
- Direct magnitude comparison without intermediate subtraction
This should reduce both dynamic power and area while maintaining or improving timing.
