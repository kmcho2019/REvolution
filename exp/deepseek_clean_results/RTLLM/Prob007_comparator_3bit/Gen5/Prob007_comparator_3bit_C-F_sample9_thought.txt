Fusion Strategy:
1. Combine the simplicity and clarity of direct comparison operators from Example 1 with the hierarchical bit-slice approach from Example 2
2. Maintain mutual exclusivity through structural design rather than explicit logic
3. Optimize for both power and area by:
   - Using continuous assignments for clean synthesis
   - Implementing MSB-first comparison for potential early termination
   - Minimizing redundant logic operations
4. Keep the implementation purely combinational while making it more parameterizable

The fused solution features:
- Clear priority encoding from MSB to LSB (like Example 2)
- Simple continuous assignments (like Example 1)
- No generate blocks to reduce overhead for this small design
- Direct derivation of all outputs without intermediate negation
