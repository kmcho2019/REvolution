To further simplify the 3-bit comparator while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Recognize that the three outputs are mutually exclusive, so we only need to compute two outputs and derive the third
2. Compute A_equal first since it's the simplest comparison (bitwise equality)
3. Use A_equal result to simplify the greater/less comparisons
4. This reduces the number of comparison operations needed from three to two
5. The implementation remains purely combinational with continuous assignments
