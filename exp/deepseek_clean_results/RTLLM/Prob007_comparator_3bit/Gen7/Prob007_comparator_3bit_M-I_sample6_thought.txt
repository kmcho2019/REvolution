To optimize the comparator for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical bit-wise comparison approach that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB, stopping at the first unequal bit
2. Uses simple AND/OR/XOR gates instead of arithmetic subtraction
3. Eliminates the need for 4-bit operations since 3-bit comparison doesn't need overflow handling
4. Reduces switching activity by only evaluating necessary bits
5. Shares logic between the equality and magnitude checks

This approach will:
- Reduce area by using simpler gates and eliminating the subtractor
- Improve power by minimizing unnecessary switching
- Maintain good timing through parallel bit comparisons
- Keep the outputs mutually exclusive by design
