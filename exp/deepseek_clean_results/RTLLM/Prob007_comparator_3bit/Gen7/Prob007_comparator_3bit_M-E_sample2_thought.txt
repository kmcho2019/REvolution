I'll implement a hierarchical bit-slice comparator that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB in a cascaded fashion
2. Uses propagate/generate terms similar to carry-lookahead adders
3. Employs priority encoding to terminate comparison early when a difference is found
Key innovations:
- Parallel evaluation of bit significance with early termination
- Shared logic for equality and magnitude comparison
- No arithmetic operations, pure combinational logic
- Optimized for both speed and area through hierarchical structure
This approach mimics how humans compare numbers digit-by-digit from left to right.
