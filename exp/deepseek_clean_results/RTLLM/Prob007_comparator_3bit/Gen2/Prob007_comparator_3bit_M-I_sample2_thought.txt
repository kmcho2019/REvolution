Improvement Strategy:
1. Maintain the current efficient continuous assignment structure which provides good PPA
2. Optimize area by combining the comparison logic into a single 2-bit encoded output, then decode to the three signals
3. This reduces the number of comparators needed from three to one
4. The encoding/decoding logic is simple and area-efficient for this small design
5. Maintain mutual exclusivity by construction in the decoder
6. Keep the clean interface and readability while improving area efficiency

The new implementation will:
- First encode the comparison result into 2 bits (00: equal, 01: less, 10: greater)
- Then decode these 2 bits into the three output signals
- This reduces comparator logic while maintaining all functionality
