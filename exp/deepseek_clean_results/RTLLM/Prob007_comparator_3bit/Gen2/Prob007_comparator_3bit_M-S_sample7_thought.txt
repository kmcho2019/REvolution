The original solution uses three separate comparison operations (>, ==, <) which is functionally correct but can be simplified. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive, we can:
1. First check for equality (A == B) as it's the simplest case
2. Then use a single comparison (A > B) to determine the other two states
3. Derive A_less from the negation of A_greater when they're not equal
This reduces the number of comparison operations while maintaining the same functionality and mutual exclusivity.
