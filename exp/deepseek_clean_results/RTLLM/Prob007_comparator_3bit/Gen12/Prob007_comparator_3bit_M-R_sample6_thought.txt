The refactored implementation will maintain the same functionality but use a different structural approach. Instead of using nested ternary operators, we'll:
1. Use arithmetic comparisons directly (A > B, A == B)
2. Derive A_less from the other two outputs to ensure mutual exclusivity
3. Keep all outputs as simple assign statements
4. This approach is more readable and may allow synthesis tools to better optimize the implementation
5. The arithmetic comparisons will naturally handle the bit-by-bit comparison from MSB to LSB
