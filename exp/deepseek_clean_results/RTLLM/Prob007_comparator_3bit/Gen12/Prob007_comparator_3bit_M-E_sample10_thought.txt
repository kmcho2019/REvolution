I'll implement a hierarchical comparator that:

2. Uses a priority-based resolution scheme where higher bits dominate

4. Employs a unique "comparison vector" approach for efficient encoding

- Hierarchical comparison reduces logic depth
- Priority resolution eliminates redundant comparisons
- Early termination when MSB difference is detected
- Comparison vector provides a compact representation
- Optimized for both speed and power efficiency
