This innovative design breaks down the 3-bit comparison into bit-slice units that generate local comparison results, then combines them hierarchically. Each bit slice computes:
1. Local equality (P)
2. Local greater-than (G)
3. Local less-than (L)

The hierarchical combination follows these rules:
- Equality propagates only if all bits are equal (P_total = P2 & P1 & P0)
- Greater-than propagates if either:
  - Current bit is greater and higher bits are equal, or
  - Higher bits are already greater
- Less-than follows similar propagation rules

This approach offers:
1. Better timing for wide comparators (scalable beyond 3 bits)
2. Clear propagation of comparison results from MSB to LSB
3. Modular design that's easy to extend
4. Potential area savings through shared propagate logic
