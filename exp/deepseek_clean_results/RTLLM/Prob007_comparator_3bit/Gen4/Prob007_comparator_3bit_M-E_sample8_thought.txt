The hierarchical bit-slice comparator breaks down the 3-bit comparison into individual bit comparisons and then combines the results in a structured way. This approach offers:

1. Better scalability for wider comparators (easy to extend to N-bits)
2. Clear priority encoding from MSB to LSB
3. Potential area savings through shared intermediate logic
4. Explicit propagation of comparison states between bit positions

The design works by:
1. Comparing bits from MSB to LSB
2. Generating local greater/equal/less signals for each bit position
3. Propagating the comparison result once a definitive difference is found
4. Using priority encoding to ensure only one output is active
