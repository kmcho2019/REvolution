A novel approach to implement the 3-bit comparator is to use a hierarchical comparison method that breaks down the comparison into smaller 1-bit comparators and then combines their results. This method provides better scalability and clearer logical structure:

1. First, implement three 1-bit comparators (for each bit position)
2. Each 1-bit comparator generates local greater, equal, and less signals
3. Combine these local signals hierarchically:
   - For equality: All bits must be equal (AND of all equal signals)
   - For greater/less: Use a priority encoder approach where higher bits dominate
4. This method makes the comparison logic more modular and easier to extend to wider buses
5. The outputs remain mutually exclusive by construction
