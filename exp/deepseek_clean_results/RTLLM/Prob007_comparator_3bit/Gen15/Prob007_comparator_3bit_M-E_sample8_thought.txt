I'll implement a hierarchical bitwise comparator that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB in a cascaded fashion
2. Uses priority encoding to determine the final result
3. Incorporates early termination when inequality is detected
Key features:
- No arithmetic operations (pure combinational logic)
- Minimal logic depth (3 levels for 3-bit comparison)
- Natural mutual exclusivity through priority encoding
- Power-efficient due to early termination
- Scalable to any bit width with linear logic depth increase
The design compares MSBs first and only proceeds to lower bits if higher bits are equal, optimizing both performance and power.
