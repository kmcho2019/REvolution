To optimize the comparator for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical bit-wise comparison approach that:
1. Compares bits from MSB to LSB, stopping at the first unequal bit
2. Uses simple AND/OR logic gates instead of arithmetic subtraction
3. Shares common logic between the three output conditions
4. Ensures mutual exclusivity through structural design

This approach:
- Eliminates the need for a subtractor, reducing area and power
- Provides balanced timing paths since all bits are compared in parallel
- Naturally enforces mutual exclusivity through the comparison logic
- Scales efficiently to wider bit widths
- Minimizes switching activity by only evaluating necessary bits
