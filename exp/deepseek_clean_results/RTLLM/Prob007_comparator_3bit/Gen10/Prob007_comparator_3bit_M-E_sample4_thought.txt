Novel Approach: Bit-Serial Comparator with Early Termination
1. Instead of parallel bit comparison, implement a serial comparison from MSB to LSB
2. Use a state machine-like approach with combinational logic to terminate early when inequality is found
3. Each bit comparison stage depends on the previous stage's equality result
4. This reduces redundant comparisons when higher-order bits already determine the result
5. The design naturally ensures mutual exclusivity through the serial decision-making process
6. While functionally equivalent, this approach may offer better power efficiency in some technologies due to reduced switching activity
