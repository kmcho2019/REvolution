Novel Architectural Idea: Parallel Prefix Division
1. Instead of sequential bit-by-bit processing, implement a parallel prefix approach that processes multiple bits at once
2. Use a tree structure to compute partial quotients and remainders in logarithmic time
3. Break the 16-bit dividend into 4 segments of 4 bits each (nibbles)
4. For each nibble, pre-compute possible quotients and remainders based on divisor
5. Combine results using carry-propagation between segments
6. Final remainder is computed by combining segment remainders with proper weighting

Advantages:
- Reduced critical path compared to sequential implementation
- More parallel operations enable better synthesis results
- Clear separation of quotient and remainder computation
- Scalable to wider bit-widths with same structure
