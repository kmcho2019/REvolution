Novel Architecture:
1. Parallel Prefix Division: Implement a parallel prefix structure similar to carry-lookahead adders to compute multiple bits simultaneously
2. Carry-Save Remainder: Maintain remainder in carry-save form to avoid long carry chains
3. Overlapped Comparison: Perform divisor comparisons in parallel with remainder updates
4. Bit-Sliced Processing: Process 4 bits at a time (quad-bit slicing) to balance speed and area
5. Early Termination: Detect completion conditions to skip unnecessary operations

Key Innovations:
- Eliminates sequential dependencies through parallel prefix computation
- Carry-save form reduces critical path for remainder updates
- Quad-bit processing provides good area-speed tradeoff
- Combinational implementation maintains single-cycle operation
- Proper remainder handling through full-width operations
