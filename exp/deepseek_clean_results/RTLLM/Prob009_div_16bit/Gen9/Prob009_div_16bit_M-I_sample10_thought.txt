2. **Resource Sharing**: Instead of having separate subtraction logic for each stage, I'll share a single subtractor unit.

3. **Power Optimization**: I'll minimize unnecessary switching activity by only performing operations when needed and optimizing the comparison logic.

4. **Area Reduction**: By sharing resources and optimizing the remainder calculation, I can reduce the overall area.

5. **Parallel Processing**: I'll process multiple bits in parallel where possible to improve throughput.

The improved design will:
- Maintain combinational behavior
- Reduce critical path length
- Lower power consumption
- Decrease area utilization
