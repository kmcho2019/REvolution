I'll implement a novel "parallel prefix division" approach that combines aspects of carry-lookahead addition with division. The key innovations are:

1. Hierarchical comparison: Break the 16-bit division into 4 segments of 4 bits each, processing them in parallel where possible
2. Early termination: If the divisor is larger than the current remainder segment, skip unnecessary computations
3. Remainder propagation: Use a tree structure to combine partial remainders efficiently
4. Quotient generation: Build the quotient bits in parallel using prefix computation

This approach reduces the critical path by:
- Processing 4 bits at a time where possible
- Minimizing dependency chains through parallel computation
- Using hierarchical comparison to eliminate unnecessary operations

The design maintains combinational behavior while significantly improving performance and reducing area compared to the bit-serial approach.
