To create a novel divider architecture, I'll implement a parallel prefix approach inspired by carry-lookahead adders. The key innovations are:

1. **Parallel Comparison Tree**: Instead of sequential bit processing, we'll build a binary tree of comparators to determine quotient bits in logarithmic time complexity.
2. **Remainder Prediction**: Use speculative subtraction with carry-select logic to compute possible remainders in parallel.
3. **Early Termination**: Detect when remaining bits won't affect the quotient (when divisor > remaining dividend) to skip unnecessary operations.
4. **Bit-Sliced Processing**: Process groups of bits in parallel rather than single bits, reducing the critical path.

The design will:
- Maintain combinational behavior
- Reduce critical path compared to sequential approaches
- Exploit parallelism in the division operation
- Use speculative computation to hide comparison latency
