To optimize the 16-bit divider module for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Single always block**: Merge the two always blocks to reduce register overhead and simplify the design.
2. **Pre-compute zero-extended divisor**: Calculate this once outside the loop to avoid repeated computations.
3. **Optimize remainder width**: Since divisor is 8-bit, we only need 9 bits (8+1) for comparison at each step.
4. **Remove unnecessary registers**: Eliminate a_reg and b_reg since they're just copies of inputs.
5. **Simplify loop structure**: Make the loop more straightforward while maintaining functionality.

These changes will:
- Reduce area by eliminating redundant registers and computations
- Improve power by minimizing switching activity
- Maintain performance by keeping the combinational path similar
- Keep the design purely combinational as required
