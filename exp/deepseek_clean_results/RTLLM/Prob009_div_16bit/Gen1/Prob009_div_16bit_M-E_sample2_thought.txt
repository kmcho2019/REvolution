The new approach implements a pipelined combinational division algorithm that processes 1 bit per clock cycle in a fully unrolled fashion. Instead of using loops, we explicitly unroll all 16 comparison and subtraction steps. This eliminates any potential loop-related synthesis issues and makes the timing behavior completely deterministic. 

Key features:
1. No loops - all 16 steps are explicitly written out
2. Uses a carry chain of 16 comparison/subtraction units
3. Each stage processes one bit of the dividend
4. Maintains full 16-bit precision throughout
5. Pure combinational logic with no registers
6. Directly implements the restoring division algorithm in hardware
