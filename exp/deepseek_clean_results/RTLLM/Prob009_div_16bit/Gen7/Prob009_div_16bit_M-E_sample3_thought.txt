Here's a novel approach using a binary search algorithm for division in combinational logic:

1. Instead of the traditional shift-and-subtract method, we'll implement a binary search to find the quotient.
2. For each bit position in the quotient (starting from MSB), we'll test if the divisor multiplied by 2^i fits into the remaining dividend.
3. This approach reduces the number of comparison operations from linear to logarithmic complexity.
4. We'll use a tree structure to compute partial products and comparisons in parallel.
5. The remainder is calculated by subtracting the product of quotient and divisor from the dividend.

Key advantages:
- Fewer comparison operations than traditional methods
- Parallel evaluation of multiple bit positions
- More balanced critical path
- Better scalability for wider datapaths
