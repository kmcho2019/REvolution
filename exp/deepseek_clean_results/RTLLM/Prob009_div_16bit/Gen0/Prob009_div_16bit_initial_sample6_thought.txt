To implement a 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor in combinational logic, we'll use a non-restoring division algorithm. The key steps are:
1. Initialize a working register (remainder) with the upper bits of the dividend
2. For each bit in the dividend (16 iterations):
   - Shift the remainder left by 1 and bring in the next dividend bit
   - Compare the current remainder with the divisor
   - If greater or equal, subtract divisor and set quotient bit to 1
   - Else, keep remainder and set quotient bit to 0
3. After all iterations, the quotient and remainder will be ready

This is implemented purely combinationally by unrolling the loop into 16 stages of comparison and subtraction.
