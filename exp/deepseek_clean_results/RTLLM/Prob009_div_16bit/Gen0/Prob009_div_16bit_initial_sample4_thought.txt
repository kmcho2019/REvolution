To implement a 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor in combinational logic, we'll use a non-restoring division algorithm. The key steps are:
1. Initialize registers for the dividend (extended to 32 bits for remainder tracking), divisor, and quotient
2. For each bit (16 iterations):
   a. Shift the dividend/remainder left by 1
   b. Compare the upper bits with divisor
   c. If greater or equal, subtract divisor and set quotient bit
   d. Else, keep the value and clear quotient bit
3. The final remainder will be in the upper bits, and quotient in the lower bits
4. Handle division by zero case by setting outputs to zero
