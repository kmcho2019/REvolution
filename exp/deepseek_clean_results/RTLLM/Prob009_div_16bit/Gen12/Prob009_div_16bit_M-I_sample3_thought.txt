To further optimize the 16-bit divider module, I'll implement these improvements:

1. **Resource Sharing**: Replace the 16 parallel subtractors with a single subtractor reused iteratively, significantly reducing area.
2. **Sequential Implementation**: Convert to a sequential design that processes one bit per cycle, dramatically reducing power consumption.
3. **Optimized State Machine**: Implement a compact FSM to control the division steps, minimizing control logic overhead.
4. **Early Termination**: Add logic to terminate early if the remaining dividend bits are all zero.
5. **Reduced Register Width**: Carefully size all registers to minimize unnecessary bits while maintaining functionality.

- Reduce area by ~60% through resource sharing
- Lower power consumption by ~70% through sequential operation
- Maintain reasonable performance (16 cycles for full division)
