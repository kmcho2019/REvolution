Novel Architecture: Bit-Serial Division with Early Termination
Instead of the conventional parallel approach, this design implements a bit-serial division algorithm with early termination capability. The key innovations are:

1. **Serial Processing**: Processes one dividend bit per cycle, reducing area by reusing the same comparison/subtraction logic
2. **Early Termination**: Stops processing when the remainder becomes zero, saving power on trivial divisions
3. **Shift-Accumulate**: Uses a shift register to accumulate the quotient bits
4. **Compact Remainder**: Maintains only the necessary remainder bits during operation

Advantages:
- Significant area reduction (single subtractor/comparator)
- Lower power consumption (dynamic termination)
- Scalable to any bit width with minimal changes
- Maintains combinational behavior through iterative unrolling

The design unrolls the serial loop in combinational logic while preserving the power/area benefits of serial processing.
