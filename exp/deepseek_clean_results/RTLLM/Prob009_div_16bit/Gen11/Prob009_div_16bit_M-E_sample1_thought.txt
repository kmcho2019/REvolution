Novel Architecture: Parallel Prefix Division
Key Innovations:
1. Instead of serial bit processing, use a parallel prefix approach inspired by carry-lookahead adders
2. Break the division into 4-bit chunks processed in parallel
3. Use a hierarchical comparison structure to propagate partial results
4. Implement remainder prediction to enable parallel computation
5. Final stage combines partial results with carry-like propagation

Advantages:
- Dramatically reduces critical path compared to bit-serial approach
- Enables better timing closure for high-frequency designs
- More efficient hardware utilization through parallel processing
- Scalable architecture that can be extended to wider divisions
- Maintains pure combinational logic while improving performance
