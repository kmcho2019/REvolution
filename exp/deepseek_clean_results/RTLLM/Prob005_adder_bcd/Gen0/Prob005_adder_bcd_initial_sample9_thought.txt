To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to:
1. First perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin
2. Check if the binary sum exceeds 9 (1001) or generates a carry
3. If either condition is true, add 6 (0110) to correct the sum to BCD format
4. Generate Cout if the sum exceeds 9 or the initial addition generates a carry
5. The corrected sum should be a valid BCD digit (0-9)
