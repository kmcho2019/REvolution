To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to:
1. First perform a regular 4-bit binary addition of A, B, and Cin
2. Check if the result exceeds 9 (1001) or generates a carry-out
3. If either condition is true, add 6 (0110) to correct the sum to BCD format
4. Generate Cout if the original sum exceeds 9 or the corrected sum generates a carry
