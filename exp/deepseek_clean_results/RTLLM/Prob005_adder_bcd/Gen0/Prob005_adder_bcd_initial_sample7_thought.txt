To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to:
1. Perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (1001 in binary) or if there was a carry-out from the binary addition
3. If either condition is true, add 6 (0110 in binary) to correct the sum to BCD format
4. Generate Cout if the sum exceeds 9 or if the binary addition produced a carry
5. Ensure the final sum is a valid BCD digit (0-9)
