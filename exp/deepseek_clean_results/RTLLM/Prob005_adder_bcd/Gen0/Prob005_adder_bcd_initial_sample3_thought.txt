To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to:
1. First perform a regular 4-bit binary addition of A, B, and Cin
2. Check if the intermediate sum exceeds 9 (1001) or if there was a carry-out from the addition
3. If either condition is true, add 6 (0110) to correct the sum to BCD format
4. Generate the final sum and carry-out signals
5. The correction step may produce another carry-out which needs to be considered
