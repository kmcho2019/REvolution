Refactoring Strategy:
1. Switch from carry-lookahead to ripple-carry adder for simplicity since we're only dealing with 4 bits
2. Combine the binary addition and BCD correction into a single continuous assignment block
3. Simplify overflow detection by checking the raw sum and carry directly
4. Use arithmetic expressions instead of bitwise operations for better readability
5. Maintain same functionality with reduced gate count

Key Changes:
- Ripple-carry adder replaces CLA for smaller area
- Single continuous assignment for sum and carry generation
- Direct arithmetic comparison for overflow detection
- More readable arithmetic expressions
- Reduced intermediate wires
