Optimization Strategy:
1. Switch from carry-lookahead to ripple-carry adder for better area efficiency in 4-bit case
2. Combine overflow detection and correction into single continuous assignments
3. Eliminate intermediate wires by computing everything in output assignments
4. Use bitwise operations for more efficient overflow detection
5. Maintain same functionality while reducing gate count and power consumption

Key Improvements:
- Ripple-carry adder is more area-efficient for small bit widths
- Direct computation of sum and carry reduces intermediate logic
- Bitwise overflow check is more compact than arithmetic comparison
- Reduced switching activity should lower power further
- Maintains same timing characteristics for this small design
