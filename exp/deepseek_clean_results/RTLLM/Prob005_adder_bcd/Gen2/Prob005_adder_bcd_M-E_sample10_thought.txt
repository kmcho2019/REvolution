Novel approach using pre-correction logic:
1. Instead of performing correction after addition, predict when correction will be needed before addition
2. Use the MSBs of A and B to determine if their sum will exceed 9
3. Add 6 (correction) in parallel with the regular addition
4. Use a multiplexer to select between corrected and uncorrected results based on the prediction
This reduces critical path by performing correction in parallel rather than sequentially
