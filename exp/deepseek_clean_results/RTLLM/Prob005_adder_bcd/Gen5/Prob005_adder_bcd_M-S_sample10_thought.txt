To simplify the BCD adder while maintaining functionality:
1. Perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin in one step
2. Determine if correction is needed by checking if the sum exceeds 9 (1001)
3. The carry-out can be directly determined from the same condition as the correction
4. Use a single conditional assignment for both sum correction and carry generation
5. Eliminate redundant checks since temp_sum[4] is already covered by (temp_sum > 9) for 4-bit inputs
