Novel Architectural Idea: Predictive BCD Correction
1. Instead of first performing binary addition and then checking for correction, we predict the need for correction before the addition
2. Use a lookahead approach to determine if the sum of A and B will exceed 9, considering all possible combinations
3. Combine this with the actual carry-in to make the final correction decision
4. Perform the addition and correction in parallel paths, then select the correct result
5. This approach reduces the critical path by moving the correction decision logic before the addition

Key Advantages:
- Correction decision is made in parallel with addition
- Potentially shorter critical path as correction doesn't wait for sum result
- More balanced logic distribution
- Better timing characteristics for high-frequency designs
