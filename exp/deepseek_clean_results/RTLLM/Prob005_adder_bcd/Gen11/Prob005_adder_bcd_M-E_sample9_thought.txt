Novel Architecture Idea: Pre-corrected Addition with Parallel Paths
1. Instead of performing binary addition followed by conditional correction, we'll pre-correct the inputs by adding 6 to B when Cin=1
2. This creates two parallel addition paths: one for normal addition and one for pre-corrected addition
3. A multiplexer selects the correct result based on whether the normal addition would have required correction
4. This approach eliminates the need for a separate correction adder stage, potentially reducing critical path delay
5. The carry-out is generated by detecting when either path produces a carry or when the sum exceeds 9

Key Advantages:
- Parallel computation of both possible results
- Elimination of sequential correction step
- Potentially faster critical path as selection happens in parallel with addition
- Maintains correct BCD behavior while optimizing timing
