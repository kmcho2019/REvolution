To simplify the BCD adder while maintaining functionality:
1. Perform 4-bit binary addition of A, B, and Cin (result may be up to 5 bits)
2. Determine if correction is needed by checking if either:
   - The raw sum exceeds 9 (invalid BCD)
   - There's a carry out (sum[4] is set)
3. The correction can be combined into a single operation:
   - If correction needed: Sum = raw_sum + 6 (mod 16) and Cout = 1
   - Else: Sum = raw_sum and Cout = 0
This reduces the logic by combining the comparison and addition operations
