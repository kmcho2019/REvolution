To simplify the BCD adder while maintaining functionality:
1. Perform 4-bit binary addition of A, B, and Cin (result may be up to 19)
2. Detect if correction is needed by checking either:
   - The sum is >9 (using bit pattern detection: if MSB is set OR sum[3] AND (sum[2] OR sum[1]))
   - There's a carry out from the addition (sum[4])
3. If correction needed, add 6 and set Cout
4. Otherwise, pass through the sum and clear Cout

Key optimizations:
- Replace >9 comparison with simple bit pattern check
- Use 4-bit operations where possible
- Combine correction logic more efficiently
