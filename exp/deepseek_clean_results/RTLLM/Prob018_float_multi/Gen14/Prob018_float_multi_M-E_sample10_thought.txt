Novel Architecture:
1. Pipelined Wallace Tree: Implement a 3-stage pipelined Wallace tree multiplier for efficient partial product reduction
2. Early Normalization: Predict normalization shift during multiplication to reduce critical path
3. Exponent Pre-adjustment: Calculate exponent adjustments in parallel with multiplication
4. Dual-path Rounding: Separate rounding paths for normalized and non-normalized cases
5. Smart Bypass: Direct path for special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero) to skip computation

Key Innovations:
- Wallace tree with carry-save stages for faster multiplication
- Early normalization prediction using leading-zero detection on partial products
- Parallel exponent adjustment calculation
- Reduced latency through pipelining and early decision making
- Power savings through computation bypass for special cases
