Refactored design approach:
1. Convert sequential logic to combinational where possible using assign statements
2. Eliminate the clock and reset since floating-point multiplication can be purely combinational
3. Restructure special case handling into a priority encoder style
4. Simplify exponent calculation by combining steps
5. Use continuous assignments for all intermediate calculations
6. Maintain same IEEE-754 compliance but with reduced logic depth
7. Remove redundant registers and rely on wire connections
