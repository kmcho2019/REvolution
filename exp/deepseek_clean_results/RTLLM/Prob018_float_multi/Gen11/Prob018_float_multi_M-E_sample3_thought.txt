Novel pipelined floating-point multiplier design:
1. Three-stage pipeline for better timing closure (Input stage, Multiply stage, Normalize stage)
2. Modified Booth encoding for the 24x24 mantissa multiplier to reduce area and power
3. Early exponent calculation with overflow/underflow prediction
4. Combined sign and special case handling in first pipeline stage
5. Guard bits for improved rounding accuracy
6. Two-level normalization with priority encoder for leading-one detection
7. Optimized NaN/infinity handling with parallel path
