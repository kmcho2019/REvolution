Improvement Strategy:
1. Critical Path Optimization: Break the 24x24 multiplication into two stages to improve timing (reduce WNS)
2. Power Reduction: Implement clock gating for the multiplier and reduce bit-width of intermediate registers
3. Area Optimization: Reduce product register to 47 bits (sufficient for normalization) and exponent handling to 8 bits
4. Special Case Handling: Move special case detection to first stage to enable early termination
5. Rounding Logic: Simplify rounding condition by pre-computing sticky bit during multiplication
6. Resource Sharing: Combine exponent adjustment calculations to reduce redundant logic

Key Improvements:
- 5-stage pipeline (vs original 4-stage) with better balanced stages
- Reduced register sizes (47-bit product, 8-bit exponent)
- Early special case detection and handling
- Optimized rounding logic with pre-computed sticky
- Clock gating for power reduction
- Better timing with split multiplier
