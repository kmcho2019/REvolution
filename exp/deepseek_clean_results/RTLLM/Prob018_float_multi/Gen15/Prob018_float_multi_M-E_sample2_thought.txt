Pipelined floating-point multiplier with early termination:
1. Three-stage pipeline for better throughput
2. Early termination detection for special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero)
3. Shared exponent calculation path
4. Optimized mantissa multiplication with implicit normalization
5. Lazy rounding - only perform rounding when needed
6. Power-optimized design with clock gating for idle stages
7. Reduced bit-width operations where possible
