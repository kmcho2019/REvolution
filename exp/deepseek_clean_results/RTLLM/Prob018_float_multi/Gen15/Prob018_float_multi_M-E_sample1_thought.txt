Novel Architecture: Pipelined Dual-Path Multiplier with Early Special Case Detection

Key Innovations:
1. Dual-Path Processing: Separate paths for special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero) and normal operations to reduce critical path
2. Early Special Case Detection: Identify special cases in the first cycle to bypass normal computation
3. 4-Stage Pipeline: 
   - Stage 1: Input decode and special case detection
   - Stage 2: Exponent processing and mantissa alignment
   - Stage 3: Mantissa multiplication (split into upper/lower halves)
   - Stage 4: Normalization and rounding
4. Predictive Exponent Handling: Pre-compute possible exponent adjustments to reduce final stage logic
5. Guarded Multiplier: Only enable full multiplier when needed (non-special cases)

Advantages:
- Reduced critical path through pipelining
- Power savings from early termination of special cases
- Better timing closure with distributed computation
- More efficient resource utilization
