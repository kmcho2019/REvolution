Simplified floating-point multiplier design:
1. Remove redundant registers and use direct combinatorial logic for most operations
2. Simplify special case handling by combining conditions
3. Reduce exponent calculation width to minimum required (8 bits)
4. Eliminate explicit normalization step by using multiplication properties
5. Use simple truncation rounding (no guard/round/sticky bits)
6. Combine sign, exponent, and mantissa processing into single operations
7. Maintain IEEE-754 compliance for basic operations while removing complex features
