Novel Architecture: Split-Mantissa Multiplier with Early Normalization
1. Mantissa Splitting: Split 24-bit mantissas into upper 12 and lower 12 bits to enable smaller 12x12 multipliers
2. Partial Product Generation: Compute four 12x12 products in parallel
3. Early Normalization: Detect leading 1 position during partial product summation
4. Parallel Rounding Path: Calculate rounding bits in parallel with final summation
5. Exponent Pre-adjustment: Adjust exponent based on early normalization info
6. Hybrid Pipeline: 3-stage pipeline with parallel operations in each stage

Key Advantages:
- Smaller multipliers reduce area and power
- Early normalization enables faster critical path
- Parallel rounding path eliminates wait cycles
- Better timing balance between stages
- Reduced register requirements
