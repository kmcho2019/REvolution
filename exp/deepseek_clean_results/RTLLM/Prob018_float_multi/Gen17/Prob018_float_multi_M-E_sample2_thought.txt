Innovative floating-point multiplier design:
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding for efficient mantissa multiplication (reduces partial products by half)
2. Parallel special case detection in first cycle to bypass computation when possible
3. Predictive exponent adjustment to reduce critical path
4. Combined normalization and rounding using leading-one prediction
5. Single-cycle path for special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero)
6. Optimized datapath with variable-width operations
7. Early overflow/underflow detection with guard bits
8. Power-aware design with operand isolation
