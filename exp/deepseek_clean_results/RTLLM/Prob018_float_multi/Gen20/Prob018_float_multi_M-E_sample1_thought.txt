3. Early normalization prediction to reduce critical path
4. Advanced rounding with guard, round, and sticky bits
5. Dual-path exponent calculation (normal and subnormal cases)
6. Optimized special case handling with parallel detection
7. Shared logic for exponent overflow/underflow detection
8. Power-optimized clock gating for inactive pipeline stages
