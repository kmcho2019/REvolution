Novel Architecture: Logarithmic Floating-Point Multiplier
1. Conversion to LNS: Convert inputs to logarithmic domain where multiplication becomes addition
2. Sign Handling: Process signs separately
3. Exponent Combination: Add exponents in log domain
4. Antilog Conversion: Convert back to linear domain for final result
5. Special Cases: Handle NaN, infinity, and zero cases in parallel
6. Pipeline: 3-stage pipeline (input conversion, log addition, antilog conversion)
7. Precision: Use lookup tables for log/antilog with linear interpolation for accuracy

Key Advantages:
- Multiplication becomes simple addition in log domain
- Reduced combinatorial complexity compared to traditional multipliers
- Efficient handling of special cases
- Potential power savings due to simpler arithmetic operations
- More regular structure than conventional floating-point multipliers
