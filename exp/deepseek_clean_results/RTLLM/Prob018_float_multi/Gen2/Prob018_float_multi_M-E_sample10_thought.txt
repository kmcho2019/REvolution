Novel Architecture Features:
1. Early Normalization Pipeline: Normalize mantissa products in parallel with exponent calculation to reduce critical path
2. Parallel Special Case Detection: Dedicated combinatorial logic for special cases that bypasses the main pipeline
3. Two-Stage Rounding: First stage handles obvious cases, second stage handles tie-breaking
4. Exponent Pre-Adjustment: Adjust exponents before multiplication to simplify overflow detection
5. Optimized Multiplier: Split 24x24 multiplication into four 12x12 multiplications with carry-save addition

Key Innovations:
- Special cases are detected and processed in parallel with normal operations
- Normalization starts as soon as partial products are available
- Two-cycle rounding reduces timing pressure
- Exponent pre-processing simplifies overflow logic
- Multiplier decomposition improves timing and area
