Novel Architecture:
1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding for mantissa multiplication (reduces partial products by half)
2. Two-stage pipeline with optimized timing:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding, partial product generation, and exponent calculation
   - Stage 2: Partial product reduction, normalization, and rounding
3. Advanced special case handling:
   - Early detection of denormals
   - Parallel processing of special cases
4. Predictive exponent adjustment with overflow/underflow lookahead
5. Optimized rounding logic with:
   - Guard, round, and sticky bit generation
   - Support for all IEEE rounding modes
6. Zero-latency sign calculation

Key Innovations:
- Radix-4 Booth encoding reduces multiplier complexity
- Predictive exponent handling prevents pipeline bubbles
- Parallel special case processing improves throughput
- Optimized rounding unit with minimal critical path
- Balanced pipeline stages for maximum clock speed
