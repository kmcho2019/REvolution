Novel Architecture:
1. Pipelined Wallace Tree: Uses a 3-stage pipelined Wallace tree multiplier for efficient partial product reduction
2. Early Rounding Prediction: Predicts rounding needs during multiplication to reduce final rounding latency
3. Parallel Special Case: Handles special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero) in parallel with normal operations
4. Exponent Pre-adjustment: Adjusts exponents early to detect overflow/underflow before multiplication completes
5. Sign-Magnitude Processing: Processes sign and magnitude separately for better timing closure

Key Innovations:
- Wallace tree with carry-save adders reduces critical path
- Early rounding prediction eliminates one pipeline stage
- Parallel special case handling improves throughput
- Exponent pre-processing enables early overflow detection
- Separate sign processing simplifies logic
