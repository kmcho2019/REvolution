Novel Architecture Approach:
1. Early Special Case Detection: Detect special cases (NaN, Inf, Zero) in the first pipeline stage to bypass unnecessary computations
2. Pipelined Multiplier: Split the 24x24 multiplication into two stages (partial products and final sum) for better timing
3. Exponent Pre-computation: Calculate exponent sum in parallel with mantissa multiplication
4. Optimized Rounding: Implement a simplified rounding scheme that reduces logic depth
5. Dual-Path Normalization: Handle normalization and exponent adjustment in parallel paths
6. Predictive Overflow: Detect potential overflow early to optimize critical path

Key Innovations:
- Three-stage pipeline (Input/Detect, Multiply, Normalize/Round)
- Early termination for special cases
- Parallel exponent and mantissa processing
- Reduced rounding logic complexity
- Predictive overflow handling
