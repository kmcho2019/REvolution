The simplified LIFO buffer maintains core functionality while reducing complexity:
1. Remove unnecessary stack clearing during pop operations since new writes will overwrite the data
2. Simplify flag logic by directly comparing SP values without ternary operators
3. Remove redundant stack initialization during reset (Verilog registers initialize to X by default)
4. Use more efficient SP comparisons for flag generation
5. Keep only essential operations to reduce power and area while maintaining correct behavior
