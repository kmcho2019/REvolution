Instead of traditional stack implementation, we can use a circular buffer with head and tail pointers where:
1. The head pointer always points to the next write location
2. The tail pointer follows the head for read operations
3. Push operation writes to head location and increments head
4. Pop operation reads from head-1 location and decrements head
5. Buffer is empty when head == tail
6. Buffer is full when head+1 == tail (with wrap-around)
This approach offers better pointer management and avoids the SP-1 operation in the traditional stack implementation.
