The LIFO buffer will be implemented as a stack with a stack pointer (SP) that tracks the current position. The stack pointer starts at 4 (empty) and decrements on push operations and increments on pop operations. The EMPTY flag is set when SP=4, and FULL flag when SP=0. Synchronous operations occur on the rising clock edge when EN is high. Reset clears the stack and sets SP to 4. Read/write operations only proceed if the buffer isn't full/empty respectively.
