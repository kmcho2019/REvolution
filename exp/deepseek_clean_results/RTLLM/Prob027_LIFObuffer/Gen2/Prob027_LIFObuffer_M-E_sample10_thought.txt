Instead of using a traditional stack pointer approach, this design implements the LIFO buffer using dual pointers (head and tail) with wrap-around logic. The key innovation is using the head pointer for both push and pop operations while maintaining the tail pointer as a fixed reference point. This allows for simpler full/empty detection logic and more efficient pointer arithmetic. The buffer appears as a circular structure but operates in LIFO fashion by always modifying the head pointer. Full/empty conditions are determined by the relative positions of head and tail pointers.
