Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove clock gating (derived clock) to eliminate timing violations
   - Use synchronous enable signal instead for better timing closure
   - This eliminates the negative WNS by removing the gated clock domain

2. Area Optimization:
   - Remove intermediate wire declaration
   - Perform shift operation directly in always block
   - This reduces area by eliminating redundant logic

3. Power Optimization:
   - Maintain synchronous enable for power savings
   - Simplified structure reduces switching activity

4. Functional Enhancement:
   - Keep same functionality but with cleaner implementation
   - Better separation of concerns (no clock manipulation)

Key Changes:
- Remove gated_clk wire
- Move shift operation into always block
- Use enable as synchronous control
- Maintain same interface
