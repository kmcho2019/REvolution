Here's a novel approach using a circular buffer implementation with pointer arithmetic instead of traditional shifting. This method:
1. Uses a write pointer to track the insertion position
2. Maintains the buffer contents without physical shifting
3. Calculates the output by reading from the pointer position
4. Offers potential power savings by reducing toggling bits compared to full-register shifts

The key innovation is that we're not actually shifting data - we're just moving a pointer and doing smart indexing. This could be more efficient in certain implementations where pointer arithmetic is cheaper than physical shifting.
