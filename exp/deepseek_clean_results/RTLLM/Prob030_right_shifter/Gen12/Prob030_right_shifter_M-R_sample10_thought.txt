Refactoring approach:
1. Replace the always block with continuous assignment for the shift operation
2. Use a wire for the shifted value combined with a DFF for the register
3. This separates the combinational logic (shift operation) from the sequential element (register)
4. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
   - Easier timing analysis as paths are more explicit
   - Potentially better synthesis optimization as components are distinct
