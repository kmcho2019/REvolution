Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove unnecessary clock gating to reduce complexity
2. Perform shift and insert operation directly in sequential block
3. Maintain same core functionality with minimal logic
4. Initialize register to 0 as specified in requirements

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates intermediate wires and clock gating logic
- Simpler and more direct implementation
- Easier to understand and maintain
- Reduced area and power consumption
- Better timing without gated clock domain
