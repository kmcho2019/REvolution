Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove clock gating (derived clock) to eliminate timing violations
   - Use synchronous enable signal instead to maintain functionality
   - This simplifies the clock path and improves setup/hold timing

2. Area Optimization:
   - Eliminate intermediate wire (shifted_val)
   - Perform shift operation directly in always block
   - Reduce unnecessary logic elements

3. Power Optimization:
   - Maintain synchronous design for better clock tree synthesis
   - Enable signal will still prevent unnecessary toggling

4. Functional Enhancement:
   - Keep same core functionality (8-bit right shift with MSB insertion)
   - Make enable signal optional (default to always enabled)

Key Benefits:
- Better timing from simplified clock path
- Reduced area from streamlined logic
- Maintained power efficiency
- More standard and synthesis-friendly design
