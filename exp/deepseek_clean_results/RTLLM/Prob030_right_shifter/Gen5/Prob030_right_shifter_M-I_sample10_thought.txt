Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove clock gating (derived clock) to eliminate timing violations
   - Use synchronous enable signal instead to maintain functionality
   - This simplifies the clock path and improves setup/hold margins

2. Power Optimization:
   - Maintain power efficiency by keeping the enable functionality
   - Reduced clock tree complexity from removing gated clock helps power

3. Area Optimization:
   - Eliminate intermediate wire for shifted value
   - Perform shift operation directly in always block
   - Remove clock gating logic to reduce area

4. Key Changes:
   - Replace gated clock with synchronous enable
   - Combine shift and insert operations in single always block
   - Simplify overall structure while maintaining same functionality
