Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove clock gating from the clock path to eliminate combinational delay
   - Move enable logic into the sequential block as a condition
   - This maintains functionality while improving timing characteristics

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add synchronous reset capability to initialize the register
   - This allows power savings when the circuit is not in active use

3. Area Optimization:
   - Eliminate the separate next_shift wire by merging the shift operation into the sequential block
   - Reduce routing overhead by combining combinational and sequential logic

Key Changes:
- Replace clock gating with enable condition in always block
- Add reset functionality
- Simplify logic by removing intermediate wire
- Maintain same interface and behavior
