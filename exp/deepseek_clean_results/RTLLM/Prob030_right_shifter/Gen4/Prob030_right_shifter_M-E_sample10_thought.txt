Here's a novel approach using a circular buffer implementation:
1. Instead of shifting bits, we'll use a write pointer to track insertion position
2. The pointer wraps around after reaching the end (circular buffer behavior)
3. Output is generated by reading from the current pointer position to pointer-7 (with wrap-around)
This approach eliminates the need for actual shifting operations, potentially reducing power consumption and improving timing since we're just updating a pointer and doing simple muxing.
