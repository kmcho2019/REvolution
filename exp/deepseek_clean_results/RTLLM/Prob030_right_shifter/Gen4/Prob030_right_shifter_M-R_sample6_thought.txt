To refactor the right shifter while maintaining the same functionality, we can:
1. Use a continuous assignment with a ternary operator to handle the shift operation
2. Move the initialization to a reset condition triggered by the initial clock edge
3. Replace the always block with an assign statement for the shift operation
4. Use an internal register to maintain state, updated only on clock edges

This approach separates the combinational logic (shift operation) from the sequential elements (register update), making the design more modular. The initialization is now handled through a reset-like mechanism on the first clock edge.
