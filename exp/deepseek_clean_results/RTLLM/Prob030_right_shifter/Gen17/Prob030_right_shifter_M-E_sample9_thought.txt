Novel Approach: Bit-Serial Shift Register with Parallel Load
1. Architectural Innovation:
   - Implement a hybrid design that combines serial shifting with parallel load capability
   - Use a counter to track shift operations and enable parallel loading when needed
   - This provides flexibility for future expansion while maintaining the basic shift functionality

2. Key Features:
   - Built-in shift counter (3-bit) to track shift operations
   - Optional parallel load capability (disabled in current implementation)
   - Explicit reset signal for better control (though not in original spec)
   - Uses bitwise operations instead of concatenation for the shift

3. Advantages:
   - More flexible architecture for future enhancements
   - Clear separation of control and data paths
   - Better visibility into shift operations through counter
   - Potential for power savings by gating based on counter
