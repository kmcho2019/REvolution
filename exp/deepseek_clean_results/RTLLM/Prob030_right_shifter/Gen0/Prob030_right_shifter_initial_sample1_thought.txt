The design implements an 8-bit right shifter that operates synchronously with the clock. On each rising clock edge:
1. The current value of q is right-shifted by 1 position (using >> operator)
2. The input bit d is inserted into the MSB (bit 7) of the shifted value
3. The register is initialized to 0 at startup
This creates a right-shifting register where new bits enter from the left and old bits shift out to the right.
