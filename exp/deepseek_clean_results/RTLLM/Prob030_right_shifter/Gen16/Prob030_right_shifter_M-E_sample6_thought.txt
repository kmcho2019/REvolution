Novel Architecture Idea: Pipeline-Staged Right Shifter with Parallel Load
1. Instead of a single 8-bit register, use two 4-bit registers in a pipelined structure
2. Each stage handles half of the shift operation
3. First stage (upper 4 bits) gets the new input and shifts right
4. Second stage (lower 4 bits) receives the overflow from first stage
5. Benefits:
   - Potential for higher clock speeds due to reduced critical path
   - Clear separation of shift phases
   - Easier timing closure with smaller register chunks
   - More flexible for future width extensions
6. Implementation:
   - Two always blocks for each pipeline stage
   - Explicit bit transfer between stages
   - Same functionality but distributed logic
