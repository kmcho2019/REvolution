Refactoring Strategy:
1. Move the shift logic to combinational always block
2. Separate the sequential element (flip-flop) into its own always block
3. Use non-blocking assignments for the sequential part
4. Keep the same functionality of right-shifting while inserting new bit at MSB
5. Maintain initial reset to 0 for the register

Key Changes:
- Split the design into combinational and sequential parts
- Explicitly show the next-state logic
- Potentially better for synthesis optimization as it separates combinational and sequential logic
- Maintains identical functionality to original
