Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Parallel Load with Shift:
   - Instead of sequential shifting, implement a parallel load mechanism that can shift and load simultaneously
   - Use a multiplexer to select between maintaining current value or performing the shift operation
   - This approach allows for potential future expansion to support parallel load operations

2. Shift Control:
   - Introduce an explicit shift enable signal for better control
   - When disabled, the register maintains its value (power saving)
   - When enabled, performs the right shift with new bit insertion

3. Optimization:
   - Directly implement the shift in the sequential block without intermediate wires
   - Eliminate clock gating to avoid potential timing issues
   - Use synchronous reset for better initialization control
