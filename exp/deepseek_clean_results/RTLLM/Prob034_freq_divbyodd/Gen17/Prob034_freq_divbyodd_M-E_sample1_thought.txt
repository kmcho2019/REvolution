Innovative shift-register based odd frequency divider:
1. Use a circular shift register with NUM_DIV stages
2. Initialize with single '1' and rest '0's (thermometer pattern)
3. Rotate the pattern on each clock edge
4. Generate output clock by XOR-ing specific register stages
5. Key innovation: The XOR points are mathematically determined to create perfect 50% duty cycle
6. Reset initializes the shift register pattern
7. No separate edge domains needed - works purely on positive edges
8. Scalable for any odd division factor with consistent behavior

Advantages:
- Single clock domain operation
- Perfect 50% duty cycle guaranteed by mathematical construction
- No complex counter logic
- Symmetrical output generation
- Easily parameterizable for any odd division
