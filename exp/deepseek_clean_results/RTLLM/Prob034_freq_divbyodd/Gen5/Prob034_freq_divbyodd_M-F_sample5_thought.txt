Corrected odd frequency divider approach:
1. Use two separate counters (cnt1 for posedge, cnt2 for negedge) to track both clock edges
2. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1 (odd number)
3. Generate two intermediate clocks (clk_div1 on posedge, clk_div2 on negedge)
4. Toggle each intermediate clock when counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2
5. Combine the two intermediate clocks with OR operation for final output
6. Reset initializes both counters and intermediate clocks
7. This ensures proper 50% duty cycle by utilizing both clock edges
8. Maintains exact odd division ratio as required
