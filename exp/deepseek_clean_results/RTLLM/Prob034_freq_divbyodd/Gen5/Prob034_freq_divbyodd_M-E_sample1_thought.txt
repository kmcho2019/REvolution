Novel phase-shifted dual-counter architecture:
1. Use two counters operating on opposite clock edges (posedge and negedge)
2. Each counter counts to (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and generates a pulse
3. The pulses from both counters are combined to create a 50% duty cycle
4. A phase detector ensures proper alignment between the two counters
5. The output is generated by XOR-ing the two counter outputs
6. This creates a clean 50% duty cycle for any odd division factor
7. The architecture naturally handles clock domain crossing between the two counters
8. Reset synchronizes both counters to maintain phase alignment
