Simplified odd frequency divider approach:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges (dual-edge counter)
2. Generate toggle signals at (N-1)/2 and (N-1) points of the counter
3. Use a single clock divider register that toggles at these points
4. This eliminates the need for separate positive/negative edge counters and clock dividers
5. Maintain 50% duty cycle by toggling at the midpoint and end of the period
6. Keep parameter validation for odd numbers
7. Use synchronous reset for consistency
8. Simplified logic reduces resource usage while maintaining same functionality
