Novel approach for odd frequency division with 50% duty cycle:
1. Use two separate counters - one triggered on positive edge and another on negative edge
2. Each counter counts up to (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and toggles its respective clock divider
3. The positive edge counter generates clk_div_p on rising edges
4. The negative edge counter generates clk_div_n on falling edges
5. Combine the two divided clocks using an XOR operation instead of OR to achieve precise 50% duty cycle
6. Reset synchronously initializes both counters and divider outputs
This approach provides better phase alignment and more precise duty cycle control compared to traditional methods.
