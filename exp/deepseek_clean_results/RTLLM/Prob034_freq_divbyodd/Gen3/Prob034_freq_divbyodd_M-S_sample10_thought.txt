To simplify the odd frequency divider while maintaining correct functionality:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges (dual-edge counting)
2. Generate the divided clock by toggling at both (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1 points
3. This eliminates the need for two separate counters and clock dividers
4. The single toggle approach naturally creates a 50% duty cycle for odd divisions
5. Reset initializes the counter and output clock
This approach reduces complexity while maintaining the same functionality with proper timing.
