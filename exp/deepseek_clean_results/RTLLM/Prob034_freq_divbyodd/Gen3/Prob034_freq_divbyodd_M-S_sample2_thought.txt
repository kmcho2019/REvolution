To simplify the odd frequency divider while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges (dual-edge counter)
2. Toggle the output clock when counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and (NUM_DIV-1)
3. Reset initializes counter and output clock to 0
4. This approach eliminates the need for two separate counters and intermediate clocks
5. The single counter approach still maintains the 50% duty cycle requirement
6. The simplification reduces logic resources while preserving correct odd division behavior
