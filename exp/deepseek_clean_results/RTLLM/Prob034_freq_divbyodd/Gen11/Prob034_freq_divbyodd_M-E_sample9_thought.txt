Novel architectural approach for odd frequency division with 50% duty cycle:
1. Use two separate counters synchronized to opposite clock edges
2. First counter (pos_cnt) triggers on rising edges, second (neg_cnt) on falling edges
3. Each counter counts to (NUM_DIV-1)/2 before toggling their respective clock signals
4. The divided clock is generated by XOR-ing the two edge-aligned clock signals
5. This creates perfect 50% duty cycle by combining phase-shifted waveforms
6. Reset synchronously initializes both counters and clock signals

Key advantages:
- True 50% duty cycle guaranteed by construction
- Clear separation of rising and falling edge domains
- Symmetric implementation that scales for any odd division factor
- No race conditions between edge domains
- Predictable timing behavior
