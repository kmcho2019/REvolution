Simplified odd frequency divider with 50% duty cycle:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges (double the counting rate)
2. Generate the divided clock by toggling at (NUM_DIV-1) counts
3. Reset the counter when it reaches NUM_DIV-1
4. This approach eliminates the need for two separate counters and clock signals
5. Still maintains parameterized design with NUM_DIV defaulting to 5
6. Produces perfect 50% duty cycle for any odd divisor
7. Simplified reset handling with single counter and clock signal
