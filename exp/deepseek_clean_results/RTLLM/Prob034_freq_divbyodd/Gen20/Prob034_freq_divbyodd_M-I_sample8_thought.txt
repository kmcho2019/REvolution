Improved Strategy for Odd Frequency Division:
1. Dual-edge synchronization is essential for 50% duty cycle in odd dividers
2. Use two separate counters:
   - pos_cnt for positive edge tracking
   - neg_cnt for negative edge tracking
3. Generate two phase-shifted clocks:
   - clk_div1 toggles at (NUM_DIV-1)/2 on positive edge
   - clk_div2 toggles at (NUM_DIV-1)/2 on negative edge
4. Combine clocks with OR operation to create final output
5. Key improvements:
   - Proper dual-edge synchronization
   - Accurate 50% duty cycle generation
   - Clear separation of positive/negative edge logic
   - Maintains parameterized odd division
   - Robust reset behavior

Implementation Details:
- Both counters reset to 0
- Counters increment until NUM_DIV-1
- Toggle signals change at midpoint of count
- Final output combines both edge contributions
