Revised Odd Frequency Divider Design:
1. Dual counter approach: Separate counters for positive and negative edges
2. Each counter counts up to (NUM_DIV-1)/2 to maintain 50% duty cycle
3. Generate two intermediate clock signals (clk_div1 and clk_div2) for each edge domain
4. Combine the signals with an OR gate to create the final output
5. Reset initializes both counters and intermediate signals
6. Maintain parameter validation for odd NUM_DIV

Key Improvements:
- Fully synthesizable using standard flip-flops
- Maintains 50% duty cycle requirement
- Clear separation of edge domains
- Proper parameter validation
- Follows original problem specification more closely
