Revised odd frequency divider using dual counters:
1. Use two separate counters for rising and falling edges to achieve odd division
2. Counter1 (pos_cnt) counts on rising edges, Counter2 (neg_cnt) counts on falling edges
3. Each counter counts up to (NUM_DIV-1)/2 before toggling its respective clock
4. Generate two intermediate clocks (clk_div1 from rising, clk_div2 from falling)
5. Combine the intermediate clocks with OR operation for final 50% duty cycle output
6. Reset initializes both counters and clock signals
7. More reliable than state machine approach with proper edge handling
8. Maintains precise timing by tracking both clock edges separately
