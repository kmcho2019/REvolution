Simplified odd frequency divider using dual counters:
1. Use two counters - one for rising edge, one for falling edge
2. Each counter counts up to (NUM_DIV-1)/2
3. Toggle intermediate clocks when counters reach their max
4. Combine intermediate clocks with OR operation for final output
5. Reset initializes counters and intermediate clocks
6. Maintains 50% duty cycle by symmetric counting on both edges
7. Simpler than state machine approach while achieving same functionality
