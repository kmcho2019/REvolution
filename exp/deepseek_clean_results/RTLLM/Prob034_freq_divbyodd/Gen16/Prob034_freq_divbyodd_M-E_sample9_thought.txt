Novel approach for odd frequency division using edge-aligned pulse generation:
1. Generate two pulse trains with 50% duty cycle but phase-shifted by 180°
2. First pulse train (pos_pulse) triggers on positive edges, second (neg_pulse) on negative edges
3. Each pulse train has period of NUM_DIV input clock cycles
4. Combine pulses using OR operation to create final divided clock
5. Use simple up-counters for each edge domain
6. Generate pulses when counters reach (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
7. Maintain strict separation between posedge and negedge domains
8. Implement clean reset synchronization for both domains

Key advantages:
- Clear separation of edge domains
- No dual-edge triggering issues
- Simple counter logic
- Maintains 50% duty cycle
- Easily scalable for any odd division factor
