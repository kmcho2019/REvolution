2. Generate two phase-shifted toggle signals:
   - Main toggle at counter wrap (NUM_DIV-1)
   - Secondary toggle at (NUM_DIV-1)/2 with 1 clock delay
3. Combine these toggles using a T-flipflop to create the divided clock
4. The delayed secondary toggle ensures proper phase alignment
5. Maintain 50% duty cycle by precisely controlling toggle timing
6. Simplified design with fewer registers and cleaner state transitions
7. Reset synchronously initializes all state elements
8. Parameter validation ensures NUM_DIV is odd and >=3
