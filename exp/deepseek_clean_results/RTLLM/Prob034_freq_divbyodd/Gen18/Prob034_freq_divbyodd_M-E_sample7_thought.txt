Novel single-counter odd frequency divider architecture:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both clock edges (double-rate counting)
2. Implement a symmetric toggle pattern around the counter midpoint
3. For odd division N, toggle at (N-1)/2 and (N-1) points
4. This creates two symmetric pulses per division period
5. Key advantages:
   - Eliminates need for dual counters and synchronization
   - Naturally maintains 50% duty cycle
   - Single counter simplifies design and reduces area
   - More robust timing characteristics
   - Clear mathematical relationship between count values and output
6. Reset initializes counter and output to known state
