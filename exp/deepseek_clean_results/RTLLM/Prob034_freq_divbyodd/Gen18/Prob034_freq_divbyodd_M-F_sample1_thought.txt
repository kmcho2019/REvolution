Corrected dual-counter odd frequency divider implementation:
1. Use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) for tracking positive and negative edges respectively
2. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1 (parameter must be odd)
3. Generate two intermediate clocks (clk_div1 and clk_div2):
   - clk_div1 toggles when cnt1 reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 on positive edges
   - clk_div2 toggles when cnt2 reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 on negative edges
4. Combine the intermediate clocks with OR operation to create final output
5. Reset initializes counters to 0 and intermediate clocks to 0
6. Maintains 50% duty cycle by using both clock edges
7. Strictly follows the problem specification's architecture
