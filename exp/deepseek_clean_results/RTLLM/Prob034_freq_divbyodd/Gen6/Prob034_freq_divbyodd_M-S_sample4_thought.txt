Simplified odd frequency divider with 50% duty cycle:
1. Use single counter that counts on both edges (dual-edge counting)
2. Toggle output when counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
3. Reset initializes counter and output
4. Eliminates need for separate positive/negative edge counters and clock combiners
5. Maintains 50% duty cycle by toggling at mid-point and end of count
6. More efficient implementation with fewer registers and simpler logic
