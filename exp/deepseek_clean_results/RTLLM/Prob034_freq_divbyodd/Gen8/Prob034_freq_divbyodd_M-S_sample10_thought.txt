Simplified odd frequency divider with 50% duty cycle:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges (dual-edge counter)
2. Generate the divided clock by toggling when counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
3. This eliminates the need for two separate counters and clock signals
4. Maintain parameterized design with NUM_DIV defaulting to 5
5. Reset initializes counter and output clock
6. Simplified logic achieves same functionality with fewer registers
