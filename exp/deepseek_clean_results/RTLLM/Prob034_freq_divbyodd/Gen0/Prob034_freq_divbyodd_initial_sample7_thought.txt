To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use two counters and two intermediate clock signals:
1. One counter (cnt1) and clock (clk_div1) for positive edge detection
2. Another counter (cnt2) and clock (clk_div2) for negative edge detection
3. The counters count up to NUM_DIV-1 (default 4 for division by 5)
4. Each intermediate clock toggles when its counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2
5. The final output is the OR of both intermediate clocks to create 50% duty cycle
6. Reset initializes counters to 0 and clocks to 0
