To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use a dual-counter approach that tracks both rising and falling edges of the input clock. The key points are:
1. Use two counters (cnt1 for rising edge, cnt2 for falling edge) that count up to NUM_DIV-1
2. Generate two intermediate clocks (clk_div1 on rising edge, clk_div2 on falling edge)
3. Toggle each intermediate clock when its counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2
4. Combine the two intermediate clocks with an OR operation to get the final output
5. Reset synchronously initializes all counters and clock signals
6. The parameter NUM_DIV must be odd (we'll add a check for this)
