To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use two counters and two intermediate clock signals:
1. One counter (cnt1) and clock (clk_div1) for positive edge detection
2. Another counter (cnt2) and clock (clk_div2) for negative edge detection
3. Each counter will count up to NUM_DIV-1
4. The intermediate clocks toggle when counters reach (NUM_DIV-1)/2
5. The final output is the OR of both intermediate clocks to create a 50% duty cycle
6. Reset initializes all counters and clock signals to 0
