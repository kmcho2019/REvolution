To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use two counters and two intermediate clock signals:
1. cnt1 counts on positive edges and toggles clk_div1 when reaching (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
2. cnt2 counts on negative edges and toggles clk_div2 similarly
3. The final output is OR of both intermediate clocks to create 50% duty cycle
4. Reset initializes counters to 0 and clocks to 0
