To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use two counters and two intermediate clock signals:
1. One counter (cnt1) and clock (clk_div1) for positive edge detection
2. Another counter (cnt2) and clock (clk_div2) for negative edge detection
Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1. The intermediate clocks toggle when counters reach (NUM_DIV-1)/2.
The final output is generated by OR-ing the two intermediate clocks to create a symmetric output waveform.
Reset initializes all counters and clock signals to 0.
