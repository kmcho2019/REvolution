To create an odd frequency divider, we need to handle both rising and falling edges of the input clock. The approach involves:
1. Using two counters - one for rising edge (cnt1) and one for falling edge (cnt2)
2. Generating two intermediate clock signals (clk_div1 and clk_div2) that toggle at (NUM_DIV-1)/2 counts
3. Combining these signals with an OR operation to create the final divided clock
4. The counters reset to 0 when they reach NUM_DIV-1
5. All signals are asynchronously reset when rst_n is low
