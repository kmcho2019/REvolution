To implement an odd frequency divider, we'll use two counters and two intermediate clock signals:
1. One counter (cnt1) and clock (clk_div1) for positive edges
2. Another counter (cnt2) and clock (clk_div2) for negative edges
Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV-1. The clocks toggle when counters reach (NUM_DIV-1)/2.
The final output is OR of both intermediate clocks to create 50% duty cycle.
Reset initializes counters to 0 and clocks to 0.
