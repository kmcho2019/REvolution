Simplified Approach with Single Edge Counting:
1. Use a single counter that increments only on positive edges for simplicity
2. Generate two toggle signals based on counter value comparisons
3. First toggle signal (toggle_pos) controls the rising edge transitions
4. Second toggle signal (toggle_neg) controls the falling edge transitions, delayed by half a cycle
5. Combine both toggle signals with OR logic to create the final divided clock
6. Key improvements:
   - Single edge counting eliminates timing complexity
   - Clear and predictable counter behavior
   - Maintains 50% duty cycle for odd division
   - Fewer edge-triggered processes
   - Easier to verify and synthesize
