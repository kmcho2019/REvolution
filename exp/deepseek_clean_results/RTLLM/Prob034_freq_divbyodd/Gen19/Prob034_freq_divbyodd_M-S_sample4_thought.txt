Simplified odd frequency divider design:
1. Use single counter approach but count only on positive edges for stability
2. For odd division N, toggle output at (N-1)/2 and N-1 counts
3. This creates two symmetric transitions per period (rising at midpoint, falling at end)
4. Maintains approximate 50% duty cycle for odd divisions
5. Key simplifications:
   - Single edge counting eliminates glitch risks
   - Reduced logic complexity
   - Clear and predictable timing
   - Still meets odd division requirement
6. Reset initializes counter and output to known state
