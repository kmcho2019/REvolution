A novel approach for odd frequency division using a single counter and edge-triggered toggling:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both rising and falling edges (double-rate counting)
2. Toggle the output clock when the counter reaches both (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
3. This creates a symmetric duty cycle by effectively distributing the toggles across both clock phases
4. The counter resets after reaching NUM_DIV-1
5. The output toggles are synchronized to the input clock edges for clean transitions
6. This approach reduces hardware complexity by using a single counter instead of two
