To properly implement an odd frequency divider with 50% duty cycle, we need to:
1. Use two counters (posedge and negedge) that count full cycles (NUM_DIV)
2. Toggle each divided clock at exactly half the period (NUM_DIV/2)
3. For odd division, the positive edge clock toggles at (NUM_DIV-1)/2
4. The negative edge clock toggles at the same point but offset by half a cycle
5. Combine both clocks with OR to create the final 50% duty cycle output
6. Reset synchronizes both counters and forces both divided clocks low
7. Use integer division which naturally rounds down for odd numbers
