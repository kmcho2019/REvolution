To simplify the odd frequency divider while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a single counter that counts on both edges of the clock
2. Toggle the output clock when the counter reaches (NUM_DIV-1)/2 and NUM_DIV-1
3. This eliminates the need for two separate counters and intermediate clocks
4. The single counter approach still achieves 50% duty cycle by toggling at both half-period points
5. Reset initializes the counter and output clock to 0
