Instead of using two separate counters for rising and falling edges, we'll use a single counter with a duty cycle control mechanism:
1. Use one counter that counts full cycles (both edges) of the input clock
2. Generate two control signals: one for the high period and one for the low period
3. The high period will be (NUM_DIV+1)/2 counts
4. The low period will be (NUM_DIV-1)/2 counts
5. This ensures a near 50% duty cycle output
6. The output clock toggles when either period completes
7. Reset synchronously initializes the counter and output
8. The approach uses fewer resources while maintaining precise odd division
