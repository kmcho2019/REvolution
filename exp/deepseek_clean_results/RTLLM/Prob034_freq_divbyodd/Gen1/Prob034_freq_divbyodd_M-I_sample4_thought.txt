2. Simplified Counter Logic:

3. Edge-Aligned Toggle:
   - Toggle output when counter reaches (N-1)/2 and (N-1)
   - This ensures proper phase alignment and duty cycle

4. Reset Synchronization:
   - Clear counter and output on reset
   - Ensure clean startup behavior
