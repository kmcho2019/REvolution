To optimize the 8-bit multiplier for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Use Booth Encoding (Radix-2) to reduce the number of partial products from 8 to 5 on average, which will:
   - Reduce the number of additions required
   - Decrease power consumption by activating fewer adders
   - Reduce area by needing fewer partial product generators

2. Implement carry-save addition for the partial product accumulation to:
   - Reduce critical path delay by avoiding carry propagation at each stage
   - Improve performance by allowing parallel addition operations

3. Optimize the final adder to use a carry-lookahead structure for:
   - Faster final sum computation
   - Better timing characteristics

4. Use conditional generation of partial products to:
   - Minimize switching activity when multiplier bits are zero
   - Reduce dynamic power consumption

The improved design will maintain the same functionality while achieving better PPA metrics.
