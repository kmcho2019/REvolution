Novel approach using Booth encoding with optimized shift-add:
1. Implement Radix-4 Booth encoding to reduce the number of partial products from 8 to 4
2. Use a modified shift-add structure that combines sign extension handling with accumulation
3. Key innovations:
   - Booth encoding reduces number of additions by half
   - Integrated sign extension handling simplifies the adder structure
   - Parallel evaluation of all partial products with optimized shifting
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to basic shift-add
   - Lower power consumption due to fewer operations
   - Maintains simplicity while improving performance
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Slight increase in control logic for Booth encoding
   - Requires careful handling of sign bits
