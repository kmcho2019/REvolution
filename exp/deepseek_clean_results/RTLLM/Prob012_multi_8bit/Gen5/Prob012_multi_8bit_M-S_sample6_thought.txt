To simplify the 8-bit multiplier while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a straightforward shift-and-add approach. The key simplifications are:

1. Remove complex partial product reduction stages (4:2 compressors, CSAs)
2. Use a simple accumulator-based approach with shifting
3. Eliminate specialized adder modules (CLA)
4. Perform additions sequentially in a loop-like structure
5. Maintain the same interface and functionality

The simplified design will:
- Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B)
- Shift the multiplicand (A) left for each bit position
- Add to the accumulator when the multiplier bit is 1
- Use basic Verilog operations without complex submodules
