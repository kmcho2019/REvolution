2. **Partial Product Gating**: Only generate partial products when the corresponding multiplier bit is '1', reducing unnecessary switching activity.

4. **Balanced Pipeline**: Add a single pipeline stage after the partial product generation to improve clock frequency without significantly increasing latency.
