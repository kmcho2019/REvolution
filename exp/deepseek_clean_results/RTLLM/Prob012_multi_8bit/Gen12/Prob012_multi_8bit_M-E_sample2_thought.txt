My novel approach implements an 8-bit multiplier using a radix-4 Booth encoding scheme combined with a carry-select adder structure. This design offers several advantages:

1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding:
   - Reduces the number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4)
   - Handles signed numbers naturally
   - Simplifies the reduction tree

2. Carry-Select Adder Structure:
   - Provides parallel computation of sum bits
   - Balances the critical path
   - Offers better performance than ripple-carry or carry-lookahead for this bit width

3. Optimized Partial Product Generation:
   - Uses multiplexers to select between 0, ±A, ±2A
   - Implements sign extension efficiently
   - Minimizes switching activity

The multiplier works in three stages:
1. Booth encoding of the multiplier (B)
2. Generation and alignment of partial products
3. Parallel addition using carry-select structure
