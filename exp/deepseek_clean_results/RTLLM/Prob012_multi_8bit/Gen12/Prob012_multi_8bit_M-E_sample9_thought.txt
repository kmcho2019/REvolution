Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Serial Multiplier with Early Termination
1. Instead of parallel processing all bits, process one multiplier bit per clock cycle (bit-serial)
2. Use a shift-accumulate approach where we:
   - Right-shift the multiplier (B) to examine one bit at a time
   - Left-shift the multiplicand (A) to align with current bit position
   - Conditionally add to an accumulator when multiplier bit is 1
3. Early termination optimization: Stop processing when remaining multiplier bits are all 0
4. Benefits:
   - Lower power consumption (only active when needed)
   - Smaller area (sequential rather than parallel implementation)
   - Natural handling of sparse multipliers (faster when many 0 bits)
   - Scalable to any bit-width with same control logic
