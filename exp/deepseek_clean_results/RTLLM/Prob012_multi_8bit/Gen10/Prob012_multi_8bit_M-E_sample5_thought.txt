Novel Architectural Idea: Serial-Parallel Hybrid Multiplier with Early Termination

1. Serial-Parallel Approach:
   - Process 4 bits of the multiplier (B) in parallel at each cycle (2 cycles total)
   - Each cycle handles a nibble (4 bits) of B, reducing critical path compared to full parallel
   - Combines benefits of serial (lower area) and parallel (higher speed) approaches

2. Early Termination Optimization:
   - Detect when remaining B bits are zero to skip unnecessary cycles
   - Uses a leading-zero detector to determine effective bit-width
   - Saves power by avoiding redundant operations

3. Pipelined Accumulation:
   - Uses a registered accumulator to break critical path
   - First cycle: processes lower nibble of B
   - Second cycle: processes upper nibble with shifted A
   - Final result available after 2 cycles (with optional early termination)

4. Power Gating:
   - Shuts down shifting logic when early termination occurs
   - Dynamic power reduction when multiplying with small numbers
