I'll implement a novel hybrid multiplier architecture that combines the benefits of both array and tree multipliers while optimizing for area and power. The key innovations are:

1. Radix-4 Booth Encoding: Reduces the number of partial products by half (from 8 to 4) for better area and power efficiency.
2. Asymmetric CSA Tree: Uses different compression ratios for lower and higher bits to balance timing and area.
3. Segmented Final Adder: Implements a hybrid ripple-carry/carry-lookahead adder where lower bits use simpler ripple-carry and critical upper bits use carry-lookahead.
4. Early Termination: Detects when remaining partial products are zero to skip unnecessary additions.

This approach provides better area-power tradeoff while maintaining reasonable performance.
