Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Serial Multiplication with Parallel Accumulation

This design implements an 8-bit multiplier using a bit-serial approach with parallel accumulation, offering several advantages:
1. Reduced area by reusing a single adder for all bit operations
2. Lower power consumption due to minimized switching activity
3. Pipelined operation potential for higher throughput
4. Simple control logic with predictable timing

Key Features:
- Processes one multiplier bit per clock cycle (8 cycles total)
- Uses a shift register for the multiplier (B) to examine bits sequentially
- Accumulates shifted versions of multiplicand (A) in parallel
- Final product available after 8 cycles
- Can be easily extended to pipelined operation
