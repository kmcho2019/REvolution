To create a novel 8-bit multiplier implementation, I propose a hybrid approach combining:
1. Booth encoding for reduced partial products (better for signed numbers)
2. Carry-save addition for efficient partial product accumulation
3. Final carry-propagate adder for result computation

Key innovations:
- Uses radix-4 Booth encoding to reduce number of partial products from 8 to 4
- Implements carry-save addition tree for faster intermediate sums
- Maintains full 16-bit precision
- More efficient than basic shift-and-add for both signed and unsigned numbers
- Better PPA characteristics due to reduced number of operations
