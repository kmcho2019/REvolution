To optimize the 8-bit multiplier, I'll focus on improving the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality. The key improvements will be:

1. Performance: Replace the final multi-operand addition with a dedicated carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for faster critical path.
2. Power: Optimize partial product generation by using AND gates directly instead of conditional assignments.
3. Area: Simplify the CSA tree structure by combining some operations and reducing bit-width where possible.

The improved design will:
- Use direct AND gates for partial product generation (lower power)
- Maintain the CSA tree but with optimized bit-widths
- Implement a 16-bit CLA for final addition (better performance)
- Combine some partial products earlier to reduce stages (better area)
