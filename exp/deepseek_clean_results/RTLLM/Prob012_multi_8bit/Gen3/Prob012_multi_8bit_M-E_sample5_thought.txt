Here's a novel approach using a binary tree reduction structure with carry-save adders:

1. Instead of a linear addition chain, we'll use a balanced binary tree of carry-save adders (CSAs) to reduce the partial products
2. Each CSA level reduces three partial products to two (sum and carry)
3. The final level uses a conventional adder to combine the last sum and carry
4. Partial products are generated in parallel using shifted versions of A masked by B bits
5. This approach reduces the critical path from O(n) to O(log n) for n-bit multiplication

Key advantages:
- Faster critical path due to logarithmic depth
- Better timing characteristics for high-speed designs
- More regular structure that's easier to pipeline if needed
- Efficient use of hardware resources
