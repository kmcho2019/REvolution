Let's implement a Booth-encoded multiplier with radix-4 encoding to reduce the number of partial products by half. This approach offers better performance and area efficiency compared to the basic shift-and-add method.

Key features:
1. Radix-4 Booth encoding to reduce partial products from 8 to 4
2. Sign extension handling for negative partial products
3. Wallace tree for efficient partial product reduction
4. Final carry-propagate adder for result computation

Advantages:
- Fewer partial products mean less addition operations
- Efficient handling of signed numbers
- Better performance due to reduced critical path
- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
