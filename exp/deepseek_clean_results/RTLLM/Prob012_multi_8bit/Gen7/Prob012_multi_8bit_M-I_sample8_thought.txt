Improvement Strategy:
1. Power Optimization:
   - Add operand isolation to disable partial product generation when multiplier bits are zero
   - This prevents unnecessary switching activity in the AND gates and adders

2. Area Optimization:
   - Replace the 3-level adder tree with a carry-save adder (CSA) structure
   - CSAs reduce area by eliminating carry propagation in intermediate stages
   - Final addition done with a single carry-propagate adder

3. Performance:
   - Maintain the same critical path length (3 adder levels)
   - CSA structure naturally balances the adder tree

Key Changes:
- Added operand isolation using AND gates with B bits as enables
- Implemented 4:2 compressor (CSA) for first reduction stage
- Used carry-save format until final addition
- Final addition with fast carry-propagate adder
