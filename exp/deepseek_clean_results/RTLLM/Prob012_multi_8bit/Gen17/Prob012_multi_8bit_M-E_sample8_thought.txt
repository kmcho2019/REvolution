Novel Bit-Serial Multiplier with Parallel Accumulation:
1. Instead of processing all bits in parallel, we'll process the multiplier (B) one bit at a time
2. For each bit of B, we'll conditionally add a shifted version of A to an accumulator
3. The shifting is done dynamically by rotating the accumulator contents
4. Key innovation: We use a rotating register for the accumulator to avoid explicit shift operations
5. Benefits: Reduced dynamic power (only active when B bits are 1), smaller area (no large adder tree), and simpler control logic
6. Tradeoff: Requires 8 clock cycles to complete (but can be pipelined for throughput)
