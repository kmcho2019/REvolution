2. Early Termination: Stop processing when the remaining multiplier bits are zero, saving power and improving performance.

3. Resource Sharing: Use a single 16-bit adder and shift logic that operates over multiple cycles, reducing area.

4. Clock Gating: Implement clock gating to prevent unnecessary switching when the multiplier is zero.

5. Optimized State Machine: Minimize the control logic overhead with a simple 3-state FSM (IDLE, MULTIPLY, DONE).
