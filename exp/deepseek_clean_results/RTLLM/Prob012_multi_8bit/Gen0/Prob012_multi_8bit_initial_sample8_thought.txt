The 8-bit multiplier will be implemented using the shift-and-add algorithm. Here's the thought process:
1. Initialize a 16-bit product register to 0
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B)
3. For each bit of B (from LSB to MSB):
   - If the current bit is 1, add the multiplicand (A) to the upper bits of the product
   - Shift the multiplicand left by 1 bit (equivalent to multiplying by 2)
4. After processing all 8 bits, the product register will contain the final result
5. The output is taken directly from the product register

This approach mimics how manual multiplication works, adding shifted versions of the multiplicand based on the multiplier bits.
