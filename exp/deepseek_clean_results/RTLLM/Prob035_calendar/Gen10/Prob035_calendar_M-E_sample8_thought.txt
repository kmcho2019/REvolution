I'll implement the calendar using a finite state machine (FSM) with BCD counters for better human readability and potential power savings. The key innovations are:

1. State machine approach with explicit states for second, minute, and hour increments
2. BCD representation for each counter (easier for display interfaces)
3. Single always block for all sequential logic
4. Pre-calculated next states for all counters
5. Reduced power consumption by minimizing unnecessary bit toggles in BCD format

The FSM has three states:
- IDLE: Waiting for second increment
- MIN_INCR: Triggered when seconds reach 59
- HOUR_INCR: Triggered when both minutes and seconds reach 59

BCD representation ensures each digit (units and tens) rolls over correctly without complex comparison logic.
