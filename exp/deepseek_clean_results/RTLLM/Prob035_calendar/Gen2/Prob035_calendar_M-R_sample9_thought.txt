The refactored design will use a single always block with enable signals for minutes and hours updates to improve timing and reduce power consumption. Instead of separate always blocks, we'll:
1. Generate enable signals when seconds reach 59 (min_en) and when both minutes and seconds reach 59 (hour_en)
2. Use these enables to conditionally update minutes and hours
3. Maintain the same rollover behavior but with cleaner logic
4. Remove reset from the sensitivity list to reduce power during normal operation
This approach reduces redundant comparisons and creates a more streamlined data path.
