To improve the design, we'll implement the calendar using a combination of always blocks and continuous assignments. The key changes are:
1. Separate the second, minute, and hour counters into individual always blocks for better timing closure
2. Use continuous assignments for rollover conditions to simplify the control logic
3. Generate enable signals for minute and hour increments to reduce unnecessary operations
4. Maintain the same functionality while improving timing and power characteristics
This approach reduces the critical path by parallelizing operations and minimizes switching activity by only updating minutes/hours when needed.
