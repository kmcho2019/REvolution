The perpetual calendar module needs to track time in hours, minutes, and seconds with proper rollover behavior. We'll implement this using three separate always blocks for each time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) that trigger on clock edges. Each block will handle its respective counter's increment and rollover logic, with proper reset functionality. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and rolls over at 59, triggering the minutes block. The minutes block increments when seconds=59 and rolls over at 59, triggering the hours block. The hours block increments when both minutes and seconds are 59 and rolls over at 23.
