The perpetual calendar module needs to track seconds, minutes, and hours with proper rollover behavior. We'll implement this using three separate always blocks, each handling one time unit (seconds, minutes, hours). Each block will:
1. Reset to 0 when RST is active
2. Increment when appropriate (seconds always increment, minutes when seconds reach 59, hours when both minutes and seconds reach 59)
3. Roll over when reaching their maximum values (59 for seconds/minutes, 23 for hours)
All operations are synchronized to the positive clock edge.
