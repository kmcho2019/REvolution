The perpetual calendar module needs to track seconds, minutes, and hours with proper rollover behavior. We'll use three separate always blocks to handle each time unit:
1. Seconds block: increments every clock cycle, rolls over at 59
2. Minutes block: increments when seconds=59, rolls over at 59
3. Hours block: increments when both minutes and seconds=59, rolls over at 23
All counters reset to 0 when RST is active. Each counter is 6 bits wide to accommodate values up to 59 (for seconds/minutes) and 23 (for hours).
