The perpetual calendar module needs to track time in hours, minutes, and seconds with proper rollover behavior. We'll use three separate always blocks to handle each time component (seconds, minutes, hours) with appropriate reset and increment conditions. The seconds block increments every clock cycle and rolls over at 59, triggering the minutes increment. Similarly, minutes roll over at 59 and trigger hours increment. Hours roll over at 23. All counters reset to 0 when RST is active.
