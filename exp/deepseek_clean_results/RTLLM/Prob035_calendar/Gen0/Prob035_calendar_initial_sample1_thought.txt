The perpetual calendar module needs to track seconds, minutes, and hours with proper rollover behavior. We'll use three separate always blocks to handle each time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) with the following logic:
1. Seconds increment on every clock cycle, rolling over to 0 after 59
2. Minutes increment when seconds reach 59, rolling over to 0 after 59
3. Hours increment when both minutes and seconds reach 59, rolling over to 0 after 23
All counters reset to 0 when RST is active.
