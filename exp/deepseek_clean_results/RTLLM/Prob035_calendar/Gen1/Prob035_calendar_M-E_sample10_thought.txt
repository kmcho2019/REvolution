Here's a novel approach using a single always block with a state machine and unified counter logic:

1. Instead of separate counters, we'll use a single 17-bit counter (enough for 24*60*60 counts) that increments continuously
2. We'll decode this master counter into hours, minutes, and seconds using division/modulo operations
3. The counter automatically rolls over after 86400 counts (24 hours)
4. Reset sets all outputs to zero and resets the master counter
5. This approach reduces logic complexity and eliminates cascaded conditions
6. Division/modulo operations are implemented using bit shifts and masks for efficiency

Benefits:
- Single always block reduces area and power
- No cascaded conditions improve timing
- Unified counter simplifies verification
- Division/modulo via bit operations is hardware-friendly
- Natural rollover at 24 hours
