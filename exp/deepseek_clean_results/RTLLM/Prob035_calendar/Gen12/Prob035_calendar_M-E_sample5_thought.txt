Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Counter with Pre-computed Rollover Signals

Key Innovations:
1. Predictive rollover signaling - calculates next rollover conditions one cycle in advance
2. Hierarchical counter structure with independent enable signals
3. Shared comparison logic between minutes and hours
4. Early termination of increment logic when not needed
5. Optimized bit-width usage with parameterized constants

Design Approach:
1. Use a 3-stage counter hierarchy (sec → min → hour) with enable signals
2. Pre-compute rollover conditions for the next cycle
3. Implement conditional increment with early termination
4. Use parameterized constants for all time limits
5. Single always block for synchronous updates with clear priority

Advantages:
1. Reduced critical path by pre-computing rollover signals
2. Lower power consumption from conditional increment logic
3. Better timing from hierarchical enable structure
4. Clear separation of concerns between counter stages
5. Easier to modify time limits with parameters
