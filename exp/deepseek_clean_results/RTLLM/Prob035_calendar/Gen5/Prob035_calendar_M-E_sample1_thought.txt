Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Counter with Pre-computed Rollover
1. Instead of cascaded rollover conditions, use a hierarchical counter structure where each stage is conditionally enabled
2. Pre-compute all rollover conditions in combinational logic before the sequential block
3. Implement a unified state update mechanism using a single always block
4. Use explicit enable signals for each counter stage (sec_en, min_en, hour_en)
5. Optimize bit widths: 6 bits for sec/min (0-59), 5 bits for hours (0-23)
6. Zero-extension handled at output port declaration

Key Advantages:
- Eliminates timing dependencies between rollover conditions
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Enables better synthesis optimization through explicit enable signals
- More scalable for future extensions (like day/month counters)
- Reduced glitching power due to pre-computed enables
