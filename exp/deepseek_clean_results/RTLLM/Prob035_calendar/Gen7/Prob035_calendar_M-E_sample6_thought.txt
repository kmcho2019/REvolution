Innovative hierarchical counter architecture:
1. Uses a unified 17-bit counter (seconds since midnight) as the primary timebase
2. Implements time decomposition through division operations
3. Features preemptive rollover prediction to eliminate comparison chains
4. Employs power-optimized division via bit selection
5. Uses synchronous clear signals for each counter domain

Key advantages:
- Eliminates ripple-carry delay between time units
- Single comparison operation for all rollovers
- Division operations are free in hardware (just bit selection)
- Predicts rollovers one cycle in advance
- Reduces switching activity through hierarchical enables
