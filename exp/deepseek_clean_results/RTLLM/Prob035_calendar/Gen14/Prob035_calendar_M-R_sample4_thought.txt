Refactoring Strategy:
1. Use continuous assignments (assign) instead of always blocks for combinational logic
2. Separate the sequential and combinational parts clearly
3. Create next-state logic for each counter (sec, min, hour)
4. Use a single always block for sequential updates
5. Simplify rollover conditions by pre-computing them
6. Maintain the same functionality but with clearer signal flow

Key Improvements:
- Clear separation between combinational and sequential logic
- Next-state signals make the design more modular
- Single clocked always block reduces potential for race conditions
- Rollover conditions computed in parallel for better timing
- Maintains same PPA characteristics while being more readable
