Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Counter with Pre-computed Rollover
1. Instead of checking rollover conditions sequentially, pre-compute all possible rollover states in parallel
2. Use a 3-level hierarchical counter structure where each level has its own independent counter
3. Implement a "carry propagation" mechanism between counters using enable signals
4. Optimize bit widths precisely (5 bits for hours, 6 bits for mins/secs)
5. Use synchronous reset for better timing characteristics
6. Separate combinational and sequential logic for better synthesis results

Key Advantages:
- Parallel rollover detection improves timing (no cascaded conditions)
- Clear separation of concerns between counter levels
- Minimal logic between clock edges
- Efficient carry propagation reduces switching activity
- Precise bit-width usage saves power and area
