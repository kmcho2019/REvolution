Refactoring Strategy:
1. Use continuous assignments for combinational logic to simplify the design
2. Break down the counter logic into separate always blocks for better timing
3. Use enable signals to control counter increments
4. Implement rollover conditions as separate combinational logic
5. Maintain same functionality but with clearer signal flow

Key Improvements:
- Separates combinational and sequential logic clearly
- Uses enable signals to reduce unnecessary operations
- Easier to understand and modify individual counter behaviors
- Better timing characteristics due to separated always blocks
- More modular structure for potential reuse
