Novel Architecture: Unified Counter with Time Extraction
1. Use a single 17-bit counter (enough for 24*60*60 = 86400 counts) to track total seconds
2. Extract hours, minutes, seconds through division/modulo operations
3. Benefits:
   - Eliminates cascaded rollover logic
   - Single always block simplifies timing analysis
   - Division/modulo can be optimized during synthesis
   - Easier to extend for additional features (days, months)
4. Trade-offs:
   - Slightly larger counter (17 bits vs separate counters)
   - Division operations may require more area but modern synthesizers optimize well
   - More intuitive mathematical relationship between components
