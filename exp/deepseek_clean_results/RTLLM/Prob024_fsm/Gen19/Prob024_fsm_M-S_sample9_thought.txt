2. Combinational Output: Keep MATCH as combinational logic for immediate response, but simplify the condition to only check when we're in state GOT10 and have seen the correct sequence.

3. Simplified Transition Logic: Merge state transitions by recognizing that after seeing "100", we only need to check for "11" to complete the sequence.

4. Reset Handling: Maintain synchronous reset for clean initialization.
