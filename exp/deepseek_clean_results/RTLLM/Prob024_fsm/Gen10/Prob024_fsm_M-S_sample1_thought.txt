2. Output Logic: Move MATCH assignment directly into combinational block to eliminate redundant register
3. State Transitions: Simplify transitions by using a counter-like approach for zero sequences

5. Continuous Detection: After match, immediately transition to GOT1 state to begin new detection

- Fewer states (3 vs 4) reduces logic complexity
- Direct output assignment in combinational block
- Clear state progression with implicit zero counting
