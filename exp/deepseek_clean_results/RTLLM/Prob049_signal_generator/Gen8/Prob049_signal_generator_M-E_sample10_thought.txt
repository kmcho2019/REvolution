Novel Architecture: Dual-Counter Approach with Direction Flag
1. Instead of using a state machine, we'll use a direction flag to control counting direction
2. Implement two separate counters: one for up-counting and one for down-counting
3. Use a multiplexer to select between the two counters based on the direction flag
4. The direction flag toggles when either counter reaches its boundary (0 or 31)
5. This approach reduces combinatorial logic by:
   - Eliminating the need for state transitions in the critical path
   - Simplifying boundary condition detection to simple equality checks
   - Allowing parallel counter operation
6. Reset initializes both counters and sets direction to increment
7. Power optimization: Only one counter is active at any time (the other holds its value)
