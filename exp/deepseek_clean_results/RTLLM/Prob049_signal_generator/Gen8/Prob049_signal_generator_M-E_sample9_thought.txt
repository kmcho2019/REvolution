Here's a novel approach using a bidirectional counter with direction control, eliminating the explicit state machine:

1. Instead of maintaining a separate state register, we'll use the MSB of the counter as a natural direction indicator
2. When wave reaches 31 (5'b11111), the MSB will naturally cause the counter to roll over and start decrementing
3. When wave reaches 0 (5'b00000), the MSB will naturally cause the counter to roll over and start incrementing
4. This creates a self-regulating triangle wave without explicit state transitions
5. The counter will automatically alternate between incrementing and decrementing based on overflow/underflow

Advantages:
- Eliminates state register and associated logic
- Simpler control flow with just one arithmetic operation
- Natural boundary detection through counter overflow
- Reduced area and power from fewer registers and logic gates
