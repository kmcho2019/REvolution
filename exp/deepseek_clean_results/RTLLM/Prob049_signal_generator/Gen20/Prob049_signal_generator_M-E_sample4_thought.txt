Novel architectural approach:
1. State Machine Design: Use a 3-state FSM (INCR, DECR, HOLD) for explicit control
2. Boundary Prediction: Compute next_wave value in advance to detect boundaries one cycle early
3. Early Transition: Change direction one cycle before hitting boundaries to maintain smooth waveform
4. Registered Output: Pipeline the wave output for better timing closure
5. Advantages:
   - Predictable state transitions
   - No glitches during direction changes
   - Better timing due to pipelining
   - Clear separation of control and datapath
