This design implements a triangle wave generator using a bidirectional shift register approach:
1. Instead of arithmetic operations, we use bit shifting to generate the waveform
2. A 32-bit shift register holds all possible waveform values (0-31)
3. The direction of shift determines increment/decrement
4. The MSB/LSB act as natural boundaries to change direction
5. We extract the upper 5 bits as our output waveform
6. This eliminates arithmetic logic and uses simple shift operations
7. The pattern naturally wraps at boundaries without explicit checks

Advantages:
- No arithmetic operations (replaced by shifts)
- No explicit boundary checks needed
- Simple direction control
- All operations are single-cycle
- Naturally handles overflow/underflow
