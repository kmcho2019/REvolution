Innovative approach using:
1. A single 6-bit counter that naturally overflows/underflows to create the triangle wave
2. The MSB serves as the direction indicator (0=up, 1=down)
3. Boundary detection is simplified to checking specific bit patterns
4. The 5-bit wave output is derived from the lower 5 bits of the counter
5. Reset puts the counter at midpoint (16) to avoid startup glitches

Advantages:
- Eliminates separate direction register
- Uses simple arithmetic overflow for direction change
- More efficient boundary detection logic
- Midpoint reset provides cleaner startup
- Single counter reduces register count
