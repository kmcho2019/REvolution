The design can be further simplified by:
1. Eliminating the explicit direction register entirely
2. Using the MSB of an extended counter to naturally handle overflow/underflow
3. Implementing a single arithmetic operation (addition) with a sign-controlled delta
4. Letting the counter naturally wrap around at boundaries
5. Clamping the output to maintain the 0-31 range

This achieves:
- Removal of all control logic (no state/direction registers)
- Single arithmetic operation per cycle
- Automatic direction change at boundaries
- Same functional behavior with fewer resources
