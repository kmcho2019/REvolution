Innovative Solution: Bidirectional Shift Register with Mirror Pattern

Key Concepts:
1. Instead of arithmetic counting, use a shift register that can shift both left and right
2. Implement a "mirror" pattern where the bits represent the triangle wave shape
3. The shift direction automatically reverses at boundaries by detecting the MSB/LSB

Advantages:
- Eliminates arithmetic logic (no adders/subtractors)
- Uses simple shift operations which are hardware-efficient
- Boundary detection is implicit in the shift pattern
- Naturally creates symmetric triangle wave
- Lower power due to fewer logic transitions
- Better timing as shifts are simpler than arithmetic

Implementation:
- 5-bit shift register initialized to 00001
- Direction is implicit based on current pattern
- Shifts left until MSB is 1 (pattern 10000)
- Then shifts right until LSB is 1 (pattern 00001)
- The shift pattern naturally creates a triangle wave
- Reset clears to 00001 (starting pattern)
