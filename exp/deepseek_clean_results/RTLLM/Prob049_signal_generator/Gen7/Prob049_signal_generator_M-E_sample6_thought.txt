Innovative approach using parallel arithmetic and saturation logic:
1. Instead of tracking direction, we'll use a 6-bit counter (allowing overflow detection)
2. The MSB of the counter will determine the counting direction (0=up, 1=down)
3. We'll implement a saturation circuit that converts the 6-bit counter to our 5-bit triangle wave
4. Key advantages:
   - Eliminates explicit direction control logic
   - Uses simple arithmetic overflow for direction change
   - Parallel conversion from counter to triangle wave
   - Reduced control logic complexity
   - Potential for better timing due to simpler critical path
5. The conversion logic:
   - When counter is counting up (MSB=0): output = counter[4:0]
   - When counter is counting down (MSB=1): output = ~counter[4:0]
   - This automatically creates the triangle waveform
