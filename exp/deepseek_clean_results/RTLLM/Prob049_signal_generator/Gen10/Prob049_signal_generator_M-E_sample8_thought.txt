Innovative approach using a bidirectional counter with direction control:
1. Instead of a state machine, we use the MSB of the wave+1 and wave-1 operations to determine direction
2. When incrementing, if wave+1 overflows (carry out), we switch to decrement
3. When decrementing, if wave-1 underflows (borrow out), we switch to increment
4. This eliminates the explicit state register and reduces logic complexity
5. The direction is implicitly stored in the carry/borrow signals
6. Boundary conditions are automatically handled by the arithmetic properties
