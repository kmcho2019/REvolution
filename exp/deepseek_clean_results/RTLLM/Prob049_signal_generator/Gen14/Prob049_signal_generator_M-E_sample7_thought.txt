Novel Architectural Idea: Predictive Direction Control
1. Instead of using a state machine, we'll use arithmetic prediction to determine direction
2. The counter will always calculate both next increment and decrement values
3. Boundary conditions are detected by looking at the MSB and LSB patterns
4. Direction is controlled by a single flip-flop that toggles at boundaries
5. Key innovation: The direction flips one cycle before hitting the boundary, allowing continuous counting without hold cycles

Advantages:
- Eliminates the need for explicit state machine
- Removes the one-cycle hold at boundaries
- Uses simpler boundary detection logic
- More efficient arithmetic path since both increment/decrement are always calculated
- Naturally handles wrap-around cases
