Novel approach: Implement a carry-lookahead inspired subtraction with early overflow prediction. Instead of performing full subtraction first, we:
1. Pre-compute sign information and potential overflow conditions
2. Use this to predict overflow before the actual subtraction completes
3. Perform the subtraction in parallel with overflow prediction
4. Use hierarchical carry generation to optimize the critical path

Key innovations:
- Early overflow prediction reduces critical path by running in parallel with subtraction
- Hierarchical carry generation improves timing for large bit-width operations
- Optimized sign bit handling reduces power consumption
- Modular design allows for better synthesis optimization
