To optimize the 64-bit subtractor for better PPA metrics, I'll implement these improvements:
1. Replace the default subtraction with a carry-lookahead structure to reduce area and power
2. Simplify the overflow detection logic by:
   - Combining the sign checks into a single expression
   - Using XOR operations which are more area-efficient
3. Use explicit intermediate signals to avoid redundant computations
4. Maintain the same functionality while reducing gate count

The key insight is that overflow occurs when the signs of A and B differ AND the result's sign differs from A's sign. This can be expressed more efficiently with XOR operations.
