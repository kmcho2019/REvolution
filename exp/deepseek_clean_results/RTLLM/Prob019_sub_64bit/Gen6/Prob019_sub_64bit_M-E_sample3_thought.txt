Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Save Subtraction with Parallel Overflow Detection

1. **Carry-Save Subtraction**:
   - Instead of using a direct subtractor, implement a carry-save approach that breaks the 64-bit operation into smaller chunks (16-bit in this case)
   - This allows for parallel computation of partial results and early overflow detection

2. **Hierarchical Overflow Detection**:
   - Detect potential overflow at each 16-bit boundary before propagating carries
   - Combine local overflow signals to determine the final overflow condition

3. **Optimized Sign Handling**:
   - Compute sign bits independently for each segment
   - Use these to predict overflow conditions in parallel with the subtraction

Key Advantages:
- Potentially better timing due to reduced critical path
- Early overflow detection can be useful for speculative execution
- More scalable for larger bit-widths
- Better power characteristics due to localized computation
