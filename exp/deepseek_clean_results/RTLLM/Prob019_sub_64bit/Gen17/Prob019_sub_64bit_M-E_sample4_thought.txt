Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Subtracter with Early Overflow Prediction

1. **Hierarchical Subtraction**:
   - Divide the 64-bit operation into 8-bit segments (8 segments total)
   - Implement carry-select architecture where each segment computes both possible results (with carry=0 and carry=1)
   - Select correct result based on actual carry-in from previous segment

2. **Early Overflow Prediction**:
   - Compute potential overflow conditions in parallel with subtraction
   - Use sign extension properties to predict overflow without waiting for full result
   - Special handling for most significant segment to detect final overflow

3. **Optimized Implementation**:
   - Segment-based design enables better timing closure
   - Early overflow detection reduces critical path
   - Balanced pipeline-like structure for improved performance
