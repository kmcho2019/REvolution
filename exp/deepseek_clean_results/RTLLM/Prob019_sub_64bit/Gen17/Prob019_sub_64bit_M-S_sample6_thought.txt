Simplification Strategy:
1. **Basic Subtraction**: Use simple arithmetic subtraction (A - B) which automatically handles two's complement and carry propagation

- Much simpler implementation (reduced from 40+ lines to ~10 lines)
- Maintains identical functionality
- Leverages Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic
- Easier to understand and maintain
- Still synthesizes to efficient hardware
