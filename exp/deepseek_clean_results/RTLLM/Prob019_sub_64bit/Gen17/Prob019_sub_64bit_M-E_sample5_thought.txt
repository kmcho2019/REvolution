Novel Architecture: Hybrid Carry-Select with Conditional Sum and Early Overflow Detection

Key Innovations:
1. **Hybrid Adder Structure**:
   - Combines carry-select (for upper bits) with conditional sum (for lower bits) to optimize both area and speed
   - Lower 32 bits use conditional sum for compact implementation
   - Upper 32 bits use carry-select for faster propagation

2. **Speculative Overflow Detection**:
   - Computes potential overflow in parallel with addition
   - Uses sign prediction to detect overflow conditions early
   - Reduces critical path by not waiting for final sum

3. **Optimized Two's Complement**:
   - Implements B complement using XOR with subtraction control
   - Eliminates explicit inversion step

4. **Resource Sharing**:
   - Reuses intermediate results for both sum and overflow calculation
   - Minimizes redundant logic

Benefits:
- Faster critical path through parallel overflow detection
- Smaller area than pure carry-select implementations
- Lower power through reduced switching activity
- Maintains full precision and correct overflow detection
