Novel Architectural Idea: Pipelined Subtraction with Staged Overflow Detection

1. Split the 64-bit subtraction into two 32-bit operations to reduce critical path
2. First stage computes lower 32 bits and detects potential overflow conditions
3. Second stage computes upper 32 bits with carry-in from first stage
4. Overflow detection is performed in parallel with the arithmetic operations
5. Uses carry-lookahead concept for faster propagation between stages
6. Benefits:
   - Better timing due to reduced combinatorial path
   - More efficient overflow detection by breaking it into stages
   - Potential for higher clock frequencies in pipelined implementations
   - Clear separation of arithmetic and control logic
