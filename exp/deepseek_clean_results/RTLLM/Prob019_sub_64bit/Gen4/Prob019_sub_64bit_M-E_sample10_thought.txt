Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Save Subtractor with Early Overflow Prediction

Key Innovations:
1. Carry-Save Subtraction: Break the 64-bit subtraction into 16x4-bit carry-save blocks for better timing and area efficiency
2. Early Overflow Prediction: Compute potential overflow conditions in parallel with subtraction using sign-bit analysis
3. Hierarchical Carry Propagation: Use ripple-carry between 4-bit blocks but with optimized carry lookahead within each block
4. Dual-Path Result Calculation: Compute both possible results (with and without overflow correction) and select based on overflow flag

Benefits:
- Improved timing due to parallel computation of overflow condition
- Better area efficiency from 4-bit block reuse
- Early overflow detection reduces critical path
- Maintains full precision while optimizing implementation
