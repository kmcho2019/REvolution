To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we'll need to:
1. Perform the subtraction operation (A - B) using Verilog's built-in arithmetic
2. Check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits:
   - Positive overflow: A is positive (MSB=0), B is negative (MSB=1), result is negative (MSB=1)
   - Negative overflow: A is negative (MSB=1), B is positive (MSB=0), result is positive (MSB=0)
3. Combine these conditions to generate the overflow flag
4. Output both the result and overflow status
