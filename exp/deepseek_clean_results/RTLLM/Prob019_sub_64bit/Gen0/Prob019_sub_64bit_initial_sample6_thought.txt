To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we need to:
1. Perform binary subtraction of A - B using a simple assign statement (Verilog will handle the 2's complement arithmetic)
2. Detect overflow by checking the sign bits:
   - Positive overflow occurs when A is positive (MSB=0), B is negative (MSB=1), and result is negative (MSB=1)
   - Negative overflow occurs when A is negative (MSB=1), B is positive (MSB=0), and result is positive (MSB=0)
3. Combine these conditions to set the overflow flag
