Novel Architecture: Hybrid Carry-Select with Conditional Sum
1. **Hierarchical Subtraction**:
   - Split the 64-bit operation into 8x 8-bit segments
   - Each segment computes both possible results (with carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Final carry chain selects correct results in parallel

2. **Conditional Sum Optimization**:
   - Each 8-bit block generates sum for both carry scenarios
   - Carry propagation only needed between blocks (reduces critical path)
   - Mux trees select final results based on actual carries

3. **Early Overflow Detection**:
   - Monitor MSB segment's carry behavior
   - Combine with sign bit analysis for precise overflow
   - Uses carry look-ahead principles for early flag generation

Key Advantages:
- Faster than pure carry-lookahead for wide adders
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Lower power than ripple-carry alternatives
- Scalable to even wider bit-widths
