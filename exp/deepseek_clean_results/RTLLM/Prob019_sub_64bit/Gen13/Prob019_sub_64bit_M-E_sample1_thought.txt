Novel Architecture: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Detection

Key Innovations:
1. **Segmented Carry-Select Adder**:
   - Divide the 64-bit operation into 8-bit segments for balanced performance/area
   - Each segment computes both possible results (with carry=0 and carry=1)
   - Final carry chain selects between segment results

2. **Early Overflow Prediction**:
   - Detect potential overflow by examining only the MSB segment (bits [63:56])
   - Parallel computation of overflow conditions during subtraction
   - Eliminates need for full-width result examination

3. **Optimized Sign Handling**:
   - Special handling for sign bits to minimize critical path
   - Direct comparison of segment boundaries for faster overflow detection

4. **Resource Sharing**:
   - Reuse intermediate results between subtraction and overflow logic
   - Shared carry propagation between adjacent segments

Advantages:
- Faster than pure carry-lookahead for 64-bit widths
- Lower area than full carry-select implementations
- Early overflow detection reduces critical path
- Balanced pipeline potential for high-frequency designs
