Refactoring Strategy:
1. **Explicit Intermediate Signals**: 
   - Break down the subtraction and overflow detection into intermediate signals for better readability and potential optimization
   - Use wire declarations for all internal signals

2. **Parallel Computation**:
   - Compute the result and overflow conditions in parallel rather than sequentially
   - Use separate assign statements for each logical component

3. **Sign Bit Extraction**:
   - Extract sign bits once at the beginning for cleaner overflow logic
   - Use these extracted signals consistently throughout the design

Key Improvements:
- Clear separation of arithmetic and overflow logic
- Potential for better synthesis optimization due to explicit signal dependencies
- More maintainable code structure with logical grouping
- Same functionality with identical timing characteristics
