Simplification Strategy:
1. Leverage Verilog's built-in signed arithmetic to handle subtraction directly
2. Use a single expression for both subtraction and overflow detection
3. Eliminate intermediate wires by computing everything in output assignments
4. Simplify overflow logic by checking if signs of inputs and result indicate overflow

Key Improvements:
- Single-line subtraction operation
- Direct overflow detection without separate sign bit comparisons
- No intermediate wires needed
- Maintains identical functionality with minimal logic
