Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Hierarchical Subtraction: Break down the 64-bit subtraction into four 16-bit segments with carry propagation between them. This allows for better timing optimization and potential area savings.
2. Early Overflow Detection: Compute overflow in parallel with subtraction by examining the MSB segments first, enabling faster critical path.
3. Carry Lookahead Optimization: Implement carry lookahead between 16-bit segments to reduce propagation delay.
4. Sign Extension Handling: Properly handle sign extension between segments for correct signed arithmetic.

Key Benefits:
- Improved timing due to segmented approach and carry lookahead
- Potential area savings from optimized 16-bit subtractors
- Early overflow detection can be useful in pipelined designs
- More scalable for larger bit-widths
