Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Prediction

1. **Parallel Subtraction with Segmentation**:
   - Divide the 64-bit operation into 8 segments of 8 bits each
   - Each segment computes both possible results (with carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Final carry chain only needs to propagate through 8 segments instead of 64 bits

2. **Early Overflow Prediction**:
   - Predict overflow by examining only the MSB segment's behavior
   - Compare sign bits of A and B with predicted result sign
   - Eliminates need to wait for full 64-bit subtraction completion

3. **Optimized Carry Propagation**:
   - Each 8-bit segment uses carry-lookahead internally
   - Segment selection uses multiplexers driven by previous segment's carry
   - Combines benefits of carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path through segmented approach
- Early overflow detection improves timing
- Balanced power/area tradeoff
- Scalable architecture (can adjust segment size)
