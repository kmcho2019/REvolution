Novel Architectural Approach: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Detection

Key Innovations:
1. Combines carry-select subtraction for speed with parallel overflow prediction
2. Divides the 64-bit operation into 8-bit blocks (8x8 structure) for balanced performance
3. Implements speculative overflow detection at each block level
4. Uses hierarchical carry resolution to minimize critical path
5. Early termination for overflow cases to save power

Advantages:
- Faster than ripple-carry (critical path reduced from 64 to ~8 stages)
- Early overflow detection can halt unnecessary computations
- Balanced pipeline-friendly structure
- Power-efficient through block-level gating
- Maintains full precision while optimizing timing
