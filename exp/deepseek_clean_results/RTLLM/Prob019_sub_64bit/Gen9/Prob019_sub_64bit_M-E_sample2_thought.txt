Novel Architecture: Carry-Save Subtractor with Parallel Overflow Prediction

Key Innovations:
1. **Carry-Save Subtraction**: 
   - Break the 64-bit subtraction into 16-bit chunks (4 stages) using carry-save technique
   - Each stage computes partial sum and carry independently
   - Final stage combines results for complete subtraction

2. **Parallel Overflow Prediction**:
   - Compute overflow conditions in parallel with subtraction
   - Use sign bit analysis of input operands to predict overflow early
   - Combine with final result sign for accurate detection

3. **Optimized Datapath**:
   - Separate logic for magnitude and sign computation
   - Early termination for special cases (like A=B)
   - Balanced pipeline stages for better timing

Advantages:
- Potentially faster than ripple-carry implementation
- Parallel processing enables better timing closure
- Modular design allows for easy bit-width scaling
- Power-efficient due to localized computation
