Novel Architecture: Hybrid Carry-Select with Conditional Sum Adder (CSA)

Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid Approach:
   - Uses carry-select for upper 32 bits (reducing critical path)
   - Implements conditional sum adder for lower 32 bits (optimizing area)

2. Overflow Detection Optimization:
   - Early overflow prediction using sign bits before full computation
   - Parallel computation of overflow condition with subtraction

3. Power Reduction:
   - Segmented computation reduces unnecessary switching
   - Conditional execution blocks minimize dynamic power

4. Area Efficiency:
   - Shared resources between carry-select paths
   - Compact conditional sum implementation

Advantages:
- Faster than pure carry-lookahead for 64-bit operations
- Lower power than traditional approaches
- More area-efficient than hierarchical CLA
- Early overflow detection improves timing
