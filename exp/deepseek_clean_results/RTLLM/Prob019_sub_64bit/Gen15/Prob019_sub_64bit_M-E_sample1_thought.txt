Novel Hybrid Architecture:
1. **Carry-Select Subtraction**: Split the 64-bit operation into 8-bit segments that compute both possible results (with carry=0 and carry=1) in parallel, then select the correct result based on actual carry propagation.

2. **Speculative Overflow Detection**: Compute overflow conditions in parallel for each segment, combining results as carries propagate. This allows early overflow detection before final result stabilization.

3. **Optimized Sign Handling**: Special handling of the MSB segment to directly compute the final sign bit and overflow condition without waiting for full carry propagation.

4. **Resource Sharing**: Reuse the same adder logic for both carry=0 and carry=1 cases by time-multiplexing the computation in a single cycle.

Key Advantages:
- Faster than traditional ripple-carry by parallelizing carry paths
- Lower power than full CLA by reducing redundant computations
- Early overflow detection improves timing critical path
- Balanced pipeline-friendly structure
