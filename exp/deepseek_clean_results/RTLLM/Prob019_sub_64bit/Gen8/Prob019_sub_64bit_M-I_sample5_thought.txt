Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipelining: Split the 64-bit subtraction into two 32-bit stages to improve timing

4. Carry Lookahead: Implement carry lookahead for faster carry propagation
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse intermediate signals between subtraction and overflow detection

- XOR-based overflow detection reduces gate count
- Enable signal reduces dynamic power by 30-40%
- Carry lookahead improves worst-case timing
- Shared logic between operations saves area
