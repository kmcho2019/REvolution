Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Subtracter with Early Overflow Prediction

Key Innovations:
1. Hierarchical Carry-Select Architecture:
   - Split the 64-bit operation into 16-bit chunks for better timing
   - Each chunk computes both possible results (with/without carry-in)
   - Final result selected based on actual carry chain

2. Early Overflow Prediction:
   - Parallel overflow detection for each chunk
   - Combines local overflow signals with carry propagation
   - Eliminates need for full result computation before overflow check

3. Power Optimization:
   - Gated clock domains for each 16-bit chunk
   - Dynamic operand isolation for inactive sections

Advantages:
- Better timing due to parallel chunk computation
- Early overflow detection improves critical path
- Power savings from selective activation
- Scalable to even wider bit-widths
