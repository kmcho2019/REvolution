Novel architectural idea: Pipelined subtraction with speculative overflow detection

Key innovations:
1. Two-stage pipeline for better timing closure:
   - Stage 1: Compute partial results (upper and lower 32 bits separately)
   - Stage 2: Combine results and final overflow check
2. Speculative overflow detection:
   - Pre-compute potential overflow conditions in parallel with subtraction
   - Use carry lookahead principles to anticipate overflow
3. Optimized sign bit handling:
   - Single shared sign computation block
   - Early termination for certain overflow cases
4. Resource sharing:
   - Reuse adder logic for both subtraction and overflow prediction
   - Shared sign comparison logic

Benefits:
- Better timing due to pipelining
- Potential power savings from early termination
- More balanced logic distribution
- Scalable to wider datapaths
