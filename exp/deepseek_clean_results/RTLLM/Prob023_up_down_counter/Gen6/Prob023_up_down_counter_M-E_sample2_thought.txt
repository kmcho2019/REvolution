This design implements a segmented counter that divides the 16-bit count into 4 parallel 4-bit counters with carry propagation:
1. The 16-bit counter is split into four 4-bit segments (nibbles)
2. Each segment operates independently with its own increment/decrement logic
3. Carry/borrow signals propagate between segments only when needed
4. This reduces critical path by limiting carry propagation to 4 bits at a time
5. Each segment has its own direction control to handle borrow conditions
6. The design includes early termination logic to prevent unnecessary segment updates
7. Reset clears all segments simultaneously
8. This approach offers better timing characteristics while maintaining full functionality
