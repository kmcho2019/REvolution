The fusion strategy combines the best aspects of both examples while addressing their limitations:

1. From Example 1: 
   - Adopt the clean separation of combinational and sequential logic
   - Use explicit register declaration for better readability and control
   - Maintain the direct output assignment from the register

2. From Example 2:
   - Incorporate the concise ternary operator for direction selection
   - Keep the compact synchronous reset implementation
   - Maintain the efficient single always block structure

3. Additional improvements:
   - Add parameterization for counter width to enhance reusability
   - Include an enable signal for power optimization opportunities
   - Use blocking assignments for simulation consistency

The resulting design achieves:
- Better timing through simplified control logic
- Improved power efficiency with enable control
- Enhanced readability and maintainability
- Greater flexibility through parameterization
