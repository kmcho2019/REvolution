Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove the segmented approach and use a simple 16-bit counter for better area and timing
2. Eliminate the look-ahead carry logic since it's unnecessary for a basic counter
3. Use a single always block with straightforward increment/decrement logic
4. Maintain the same interface and functionality
5. Simplify the reset logic to directly assign zero

Key Benefits:
- Reduced complexity with single counter register
- Improved timing with simpler combinatorial path
- Smaller area by removing intermediate wires and segment logic
- Easier to understand and maintain
