Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove unnecessary optimizations (Gray code, clock gating) that complicate the design without clear benefits
2. Use a single 16-bit adder/subtractor instead of segmented logic for cleaner implementation
3. Maintain synchronous reset and counting direction control
4. Keep the core functionality intact while reducing complexity
5. Let synthesis tools handle arithmetic optimizations

The simplified design will:
- Use standard Verilog arithmetic operations
- Have a single always block for clear synchronous behavior
- Eliminate unused logic and complex control signals
- Be more maintainable and easier to understand
