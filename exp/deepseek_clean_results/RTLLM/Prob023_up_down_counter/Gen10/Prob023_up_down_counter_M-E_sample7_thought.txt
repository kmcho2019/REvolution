Novel Hybrid Counter Architecture:
1. Segmented Counting:
   - Split the 16-bit counter into 4x4-bit nibbles for localized carry propagation
   - Each nibble operates independently with its own carry chain
   - Only active nibbles toggle, reducing power consumption

2. Conditional Carry Propagation:
   - Implement carry/borrow prediction to enable nibble skipping
   - When a nibble isn't at boundary (0x0000 or 0xFFFF), adjacent nibbles remain stable
   - Reduces switching activity by up to 75% for small count ranges

3. Smart Reset Distribution:
   - Hierarchical reset that only affects nibbles needing reset
   - Prevents unnecessary toggling of stable nibbles during reset

4. Direction-aware Optimization:
   - Separate increment/decrement logic paths for each nibble
   - Eliminates unnecessary muxing at the bit level

Key Advantages:
- Reduced power consumption through localized counting
- Faster carry propagation (4-bit vs 16-bit)
- Better timing due to segmented architecture
- Lower area than full carry-lookahead implementations
- Maintains full 16-bit functionality
