Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove the segmented approach entirely - a simple 16-bit counter doesn't need segmentation
2. Use a single always block with straightforward increment/decrement logic
3. Eliminate all intermediate wires and look-ahead carry logic
4. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
5. Keep the same interface and functionality while reducing complexity

Key Improvements:
- Much simpler and cleaner implementation
- Reduced logic depth improves timing
- Lower area by removing segmentation overhead
- Easier to understand and maintain
- Still fully functional with same behavior
