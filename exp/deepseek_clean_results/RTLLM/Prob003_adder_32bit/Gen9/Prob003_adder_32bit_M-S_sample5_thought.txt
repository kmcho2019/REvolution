Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove intermediate 16-bit CLA module to reduce hierarchy levels
2. Directly instantiate 8x 4-bit CLA blocks in the 32-bit adder
3. Chain carries between 4-bit blocks linearly for simplicity
4. Maintain same interface and functionality
5. Eliminate redundant intermediate signals

Key Benefits:
- Reduced module hierarchy improves clarity
- Linear carry chaining is simpler to implement and verify
- Still maintains carry lookahead benefits within each 4-bit block
- Fewer modules means less synthesis overhead
