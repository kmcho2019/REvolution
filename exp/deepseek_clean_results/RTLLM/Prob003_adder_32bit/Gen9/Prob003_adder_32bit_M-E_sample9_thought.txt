Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Carry Lookahead with Super Group Propagation

Key Innovations:
1. Introduces a 3-level hierarchy (4-bit → 8-bit → 32-bit) for better carry propagation balance
2. Implements "Super Group" propagate/generate terms at the 8-bit level
3. Uses parallel carry computation for upper and lower 16-bit segments
4. Optimizes critical path by computing high-order carries independently when possible

Advantages:
- Reduced worst-case carry propagation delay compared to standard 4-bit CLA chains
- Better scalability for wider adders
- Balanced logic depth across all bit positions
- Modular design allows easy parameterization for different bit widths
