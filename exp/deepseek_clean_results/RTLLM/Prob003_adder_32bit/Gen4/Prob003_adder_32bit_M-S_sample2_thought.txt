To simplify the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Remove the intermediate 4-bit CLA module and implement the logic directly in the top module
2. Eliminate redundant wiring by computing generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for all bits at once
3. Compute carry signals in a single level rather than hierarchical blocks
4. Maintain the carry lookahead principle but with a flatter structure
5. Reduce module hierarchy to just one module while keeping the performance benefits

Key simplifications:
- Single module implementation
- Direct carry computation without sub-modules
- Parallel generation of all G and P signals
- Simplified carry chain calculation
