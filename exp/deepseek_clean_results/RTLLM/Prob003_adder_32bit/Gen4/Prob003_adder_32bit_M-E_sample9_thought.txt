Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Lookahead (HCSL) Adder
1. Combine the benefits of carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques
2. Divide the 32-bit adder into 8-bit blocks with carry-select
3. Use lookahead carry generation between blocks to eliminate ripple delay
4. Each 8-bit block computes both possible sums (Cin=0 and Cin=1) in parallel
5. Final sum selection happens when the actual carry is available
6. Key advantages:
   - Parallel computation of multiple carry paths
   - Reduced critical path compared to pure CLA
   - Balanced area-performance tradeoff
7. Implementation:
   - 4x 8-bit carry-select blocks
   - Lookahead carry unit to predict block carries
   - Multiplexers for sum selection
