2. Eliminate the separate 16-bit CLA module since we only need it once in the 32-bit adder
3. Directly instantiate four 8-bit CLA blocks instead of two 16-bit blocks, reducing the carry propagation depth
4. Simplify the carry computation by combining it directly in the top module
5. Maintain the same interface while reducing internal complexity
