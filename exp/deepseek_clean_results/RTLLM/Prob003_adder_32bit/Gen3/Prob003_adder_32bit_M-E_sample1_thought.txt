I propose a hybrid carry-select/carry-lookahead architecture that combines the benefits of both approaches:
1. Divide the 32-bit adder into 8 segments of 4-bits each (instead of 4 segments of 8-bits)
2. Implement each 4-bit segment as a standard CLA block
3. Use carry-select technique between segments to predict both possible sums (for Cin=0 and Cin=1)
4. Implement a hierarchical carry-lookahead unit that computes the actual carry-in for each segment in parallel
5. This reduces the critical path by:
   - Limiting CLA blocks to efficient 4-bit sizes
   - Parallelizing carry computation across segments
   - Using carry-select to eliminate ripple-carry delay between segments
6. The design maintains O(log n) delay while reducing fanout on critical paths
