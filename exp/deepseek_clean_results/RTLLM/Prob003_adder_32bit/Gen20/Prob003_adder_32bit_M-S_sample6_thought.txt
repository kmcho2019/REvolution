Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove intermediate 16-bit CLA module since it's just a wrapper for 4-bit CLAs
2. Directly instantiate 8x 4-bit CLA blocks in the 32-bit adder
3. Simplify carry chain by directly connecting carry-out of one block to carry-in of next
4. Maintain same functionality with fewer hierarchy levels
5. Reduce module count while keeping same CLA performance

Key Benefits:
- Fewer hierarchical levels (2 instead of 3)
- Simpler carry propagation
- Same performance characteristics
- Reduced code complexity
- Easier to understand and maintain
