Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove intermediate 16-bit CLA module since it's just a wrapper for 4-bit CLAs
2. Directly instantiate four 8-bit CLA blocks in the 32-bit adder for better balance
3. Each 8-bit CLA will internally use two 4-bit CLAs with carry chaining
4. Simplify signal naming and reduce hierarchy levels
5. Maintain same functionality with fewer modules and cleaner structure

Key Benefits:
- Reduced hierarchy levels (from 3 to 2)
- More balanced carry propagation (8-bit blocks vs 16-bit)
- Fewer modules to maintain
- Same carry-lookahead performance
- Clearer direct implementation
