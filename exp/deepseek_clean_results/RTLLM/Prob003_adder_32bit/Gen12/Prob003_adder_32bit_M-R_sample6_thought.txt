3. Maintain the same 4-bit CLA logic but flatten the hierarchy to show direct 32-bit carry lookahead

5. Use consistent bit numbering (31:0) instead of (32:1) for better Verilog convention
6. Simplify the top-level structure while maintaining the same functionality

- Direct 32-bit implementation shows the complete carry chain

- More conventional bit numbering
