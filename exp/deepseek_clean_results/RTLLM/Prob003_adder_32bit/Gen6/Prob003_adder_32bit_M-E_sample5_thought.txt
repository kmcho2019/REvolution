The novel hybrid architecture combines:
1. Carry-lookahead for local fast carry propagation (4-bit blocks)
2. Carry-select for global carry prediction (8-bit segments)
3. Variable block sizing (4-8-4-8-4-4) to balance critical paths
4. Early carry resolution for the most significant bits

Key innovations:
- Uses carry-select for upper 16 bits where timing is most critical
- Implements smaller 4-bit CLA blocks in LSBs where carry chains are shorter
- Special 8-bit blocks use parallel carry computation
- Optimized carry merging logic reduces fan-out on critical paths
- Balanced block sizes minimize worst-case delay
