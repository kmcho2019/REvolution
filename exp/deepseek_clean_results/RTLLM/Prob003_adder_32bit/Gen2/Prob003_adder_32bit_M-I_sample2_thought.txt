To optimize the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, I propose these improvements:

1. Simplify the 8-bit CLA structure:
   - Reduce from 3-level Kogge-Stone to 2-level Brent-Kung prefix network
   - This maintains good performance while reducing area and power

2. Optimize group propagate logic:
   - Compute group propagate only when needed for higher-level carries
   - Use simpler AND trees with balanced structure

3. Improve carry lookahead between blocks:
   - Use a hybrid approach with 4-bit and 8-bit blocks
   - First level uses 4-bit CLAs for better granularity
   - Second level combines them into 8-bit groups

4. Power optimization:
   - Remove redundant computations
   - Simplify logic trees to reduce switching activity

The improved design will have:
- Better area efficiency (reduced logic levels)
- Similar or better timing (optimized critical path)
- Lower power consumption (simpler logic)
