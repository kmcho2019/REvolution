Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Lookahead
1. Combine carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques for better performance
2. Divide the 32-bit adder into 8-bit segments with carry-select between them
3. Each 8-bit segment uses internal 4-bit CLA blocks for fast carry propagation
4. Implement carry prediction between segments using lookahead logic
5. Key advantages:
   - Reduces critical path by parallelizing carry computation
   - Maintains lookahead benefits within segments
   - Better area-performance tradeoff than pure CLA
   - Scales well to 32 bits
