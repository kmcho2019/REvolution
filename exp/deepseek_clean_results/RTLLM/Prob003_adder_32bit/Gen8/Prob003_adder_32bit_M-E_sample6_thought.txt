Novel Hybrid Architecture:
1. Combines benefits of carry-select (fast parallel paths) and carry-lookahead (reduced carry propagation)
2. Uses variable block sizes (4-bit, 8-bit, 16-bit) based on carry criticality
3. Implements speculative carry computation for middle blocks
4. Optimized for both worst-case and typical-case timing
5. Reduced fanout on critical carry paths by hierarchical lookahead

Key Features:
- 4-bit CLA blocks at LSB (where carries are most frequent)
- 8-bit carry-select blocks in middle (with parallel carry paths)
- 16-bit CLA block at MSB (where carries are less frequent)
- Two-level carry lookahead network to reduce critical path
- Shared P/G term computation between adjacent blocks
