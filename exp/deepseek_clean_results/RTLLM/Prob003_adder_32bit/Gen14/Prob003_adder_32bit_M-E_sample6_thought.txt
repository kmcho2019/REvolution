Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Carry-Lookahead with 8-bit Super Blocks

Key Innovations:
1. Two-level hierarchy: 8-bit super blocks containing 4-bit CLA sub-blocks
2. Group Generate/Propagate logic at 8-bit level for faster carry propagation
3. Balanced approach between granularity and performance
4. Optimized carry chain with both local (4-bit) and global (8-bit) lookahead
5. Reduced critical path compared to flat 4-bit approach while maintaining reasonable complexity

Advantages:
- Faster than pure 4-bit chaining due to reduced carry propagation stages
- More area-efficient than full 32-bit lookahead
- Modular design allows easy parameterization
- Better timing for high-frequency applications
