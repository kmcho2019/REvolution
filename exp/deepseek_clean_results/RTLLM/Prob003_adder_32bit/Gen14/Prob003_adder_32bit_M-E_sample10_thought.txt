Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Carry-Lookahead with 8-bit Super Blocks
1. Use a two-level hierarchical CLA structure for better timing
2. First level: 8x 4-bit CLA blocks (same as before)
3. Second level: 4-bit super block CLA that computes group generate/propagate
4. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path by computing higher-level carry lookahead
   - Maintains modularity while improving performance
   - Scales better for wider adders
5. Implementation:
   - 4-bit super CLA computes carry for each 16-bit segment
   - Regular 4-bit CLAs handle local sums
   - Final carry chain combines super block outputs
