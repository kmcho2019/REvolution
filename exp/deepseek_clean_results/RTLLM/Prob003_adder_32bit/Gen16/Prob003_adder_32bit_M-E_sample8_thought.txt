Novel Hybrid Architecture:
1. Combine carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques for better performance
2. Divide the 32-bit adder into 8-bit segments with carry-select between segments
3. Each 8-bit segment uses hierarchical 4-bit CLA blocks
4. Implement speculative carry computation for the next segment
5. Use multiplexers to select between precomputed sums based on actual carry-in
6. Optimize critical path by computing both carry possibilities (0 and 1) in parallel
7. Reduce overall delay by overlapping carry computation and sum generation

Key Advantages:
- Faster than pure CLA due to parallel carry computation
- More area-efficient than full carry-select
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Scalable to wider bit-widths
- Reduced fan-out compared to flat CLA
