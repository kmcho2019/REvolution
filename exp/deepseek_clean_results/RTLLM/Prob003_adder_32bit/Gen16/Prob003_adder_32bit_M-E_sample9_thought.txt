Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid CLA-Ripple Carry Adder with Optimized Carry Bypass

Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid Architecture: Combines CLA blocks with ripple carry for optimal PPA trade-off
2. Variable Block Sizes: Uses larger 8-bit CLA blocks for better carry lookahead efficiency
3. Carry Bypass Optimization: Implements bypass logic for zero-carry propagation cases
4. Hierarchical Carry Network: Two-level lookahead (8-bit → 32-bit) with optimized inter-block propagation
5. Balanced Critical Path: Equalizes delay across all bit positions through careful block sizing

Advantages:
- Reduced logic levels compared to pure 4-bit CLA hierarchy
- Better area efficiency than full 32-bit CLA
- Lower power consumption through carry bypass
- Maintains fast worst-case timing through optimized lookahead
- Scalable architecture for wider adders
