To create a novel 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, I propose a hybrid architecture that combines:
1. A 4-bit CLA at the lowest level for fast local carry propagation
2. A novel 8-bit super-block that uses parallel prefix computation (Brent-Kung structure)
3. Hierarchical composition to build the 32-bit adder with optimized carry lookahead

Key innovations:
- Uses Brent-Kung parallel prefix network for logarithmic carry computation
- Combines the benefits of CLA and parallel prefix adders
- Reduces critical path by computing carries in parallel
- Maintains regular structure for better synthesis
- Optimized carry chain with only O(log n) delay
