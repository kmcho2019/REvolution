To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA), we'll break it down into two 16-bit CLA blocks. The first block computes the lower 16 bits and generates a carry-out (C16). This carry-out is then fed into the second 16-bit CLA block which computes the upper 16 bits. Each 16-bit CLA block internally uses 4-bit CLA groups to efficiently compute carry propagation and generation. The final carry-out (C32) comes from the second 16-bit block.
