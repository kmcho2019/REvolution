To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA), we'll break it down into two 16-bit CLA blocks connected hierarchically. Each 16-bit CLA will generate its own propagate (P) and generate (G) signals, and the carry between the two blocks will be handled by a higher-level lookahead unit. Here's the approach:

1. Design a 4-bit CLA module that handles basic lookahead logic
2. Use the 4-bit CLA to build a 16-bit CLA module
3. Use two 16-bit CLA modules to build the 32-bit adder
4. The higher-level lookahead unit will calculate the carry between the two 16-bit blocks
5. The final carry (C32) comes from the carry chain of the second 16-bit block
