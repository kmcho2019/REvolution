To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA), we'll break it down into two 16-bit CLA blocks connected in series. Each 16-bit block will generate its own propagate (P) and generate (G) signals, and the carry-out from the first block will be used as the carry-in for the second block. The 16-bit CLA itself will be implemented using 4-bit CLA blocks with carry-lookahead logic to compute the carries quickly.

The design hierarchy will be:
1. 4-bit CLA blocks (basic building blocks)
2. 16-bit CLA blocks (composed of four 4-bit CLAs)
3. 32-bit adder (composed of two 16-bit CLAs)
