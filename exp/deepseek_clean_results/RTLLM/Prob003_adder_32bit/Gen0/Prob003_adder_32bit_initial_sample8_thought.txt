To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA), we'll break it down into two 16-bit CLA blocks. Each 16-bit block will generate its own propagate (P) and generate (G) signals, and the carry-out from the first 16-bit block will be used as the carry-in for the second 16-bit block. The 16-bit CLA itself will be implemented using 4-bit CLA blocks with carry-lookahead logic to compute the carries quickly.

The design hierarchy will be:
1. 4-bit CLA block with P, G generation
2. 16-bit CLA composed of four 4-bit CLAs
3. 32-bit CLA composed of two 16-bit CLAs
