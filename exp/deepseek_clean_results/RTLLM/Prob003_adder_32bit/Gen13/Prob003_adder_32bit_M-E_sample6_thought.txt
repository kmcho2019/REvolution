Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Lookahead (HCSL) Adder
1. Combine benefits of carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques
2. Divide 32-bit adder into 8-bit segments with carry-select between them
3. Each 8-bit segment uses internal 4-bit CLA blocks for fast carry propagation
4. Implement speculative carry calculation for next segment (carry-select)
5. Key advantages:
   - Reduces critical path by parallelizing carry computation
   - Maintains lookahead benefits within segments
   - Better area-time tradeoff than pure CLA
   - Simpler carry chain than full 32-bit lookahead
6. Implementation:
   - Two 4-bit CLA blocks per 8-bit segment (for carry=0 and carry=1 cases)
   - Multiplexer selects correct sum based on actual carry-in
   - Carry-out from each segment drives next segment's mux select
