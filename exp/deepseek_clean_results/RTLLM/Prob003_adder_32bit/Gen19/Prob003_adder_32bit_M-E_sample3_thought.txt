Novel Architecture: Hybrid Parallel-Prefix Carry-Lookahead Adder
Key Innovations:
1. Combines 8-bit Brent-Kung parallel prefix trees with 4-bit CLA blocks for optimal balance between speed and area
2. Uses hierarchical carry generation with:
   - First level: 4-bit CLA blocks for local sum generation
   - Second level: Brent-Kung tree for global carry computation
3. Features:
   - Reduced logic levels compared to pure CLA
   - Better fanout management than pure Brent-Kung
   - Optimized carry propagation path
4. Implementation:
   - 32-bit adder divided into 8 segments of 4 bits each
   - Brent-Kung tree computes segment carry-in signals
   - Final sum computed using segment carries and local P/G
5. Advantages:
   - Faster than traditional hierarchical CLA
   - More area-efficient than full parallel-prefix
   - Scalable to wider adders
