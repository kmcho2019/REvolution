The ring counter can be implemented more simply by:
1. Using a single always block with clock and reset in sensitivity list
2. Directly assigning the initial value on reset (8'b00000001)
3. For normal operation, just using a left shift with wrap-around when reaching MSB
4. Eliminating the explicit if-else for wrap-around by using a concatenation trick
5. Maintaining the one-hot property throughout

This approach reduces the logic to its simplest form while preserving all required functionality.
