Simplified approach using a shift register with wrap-around:
1. Use a simple 8-bit register initialized to 8'b00000001
2. On each clock edge, shift left with wrap-around (MSB becomes LSB)
3. Reset sets the register back to initial state
4. Eliminates need for case statements and parameters
5. More compact and easier to understand
6. Maintains same functionality: one-hot encoding with cyclic behavior
