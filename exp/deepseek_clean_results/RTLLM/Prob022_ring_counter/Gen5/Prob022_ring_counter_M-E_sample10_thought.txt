Alternative approach using a 3-bit counter with decoder:
1. Use a 3-bit binary counter to track the current position (0-7)
2. Decode the counter value to one-hot output
3. Reset initializes counter to 0 (LSB set)
4. Counter increments on clock, wrapping around at 7
5. Decoder converts binary count to one-hot pattern

Advantages:
- Avoids direct bit shifting operations
- Uses standard counter+decoder pattern
- More flexible for future modifications
- Clear separation of counting and decoding logic
