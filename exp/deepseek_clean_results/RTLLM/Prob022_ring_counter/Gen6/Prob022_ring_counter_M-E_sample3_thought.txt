Novel approach using a circular shift register with a parameterized width:
1. Instead of explicit state transitions, use a rotating shift register
2. Implement with a simple shift-left operation and wrap-around logic
3. Use a parameter to make the design more flexible (though fixed at 8 bits for this problem)
4. Reset initializes to 1 in LSB position
5. The shift operation automatically handles the cyclic behavior
6. More concise than case statement approach while being equally clear
7. Better for synthesis as it uses fundamental shift operations
