Improvement strategy:
1. Direct implementation using shift register approach for simpler logic and better PPA
2. Eliminate the counter+decoder to reduce logic levels
3. Use a simple shift register with wrap-around
4. Maintain one-hot encoding by shifting a single '1' bit
5. Reset initializes to 8'b00000001
6. Each clock shifts left with wrap-around from MSB to LSB

Advantages:
- Fewer logic gates (just DFFs and simple muxes)
- Lower power (no binary counter toggling)
- Smaller area (no decoder logic)
- More intuitive implementation matching the ring counter concept
- Guaranteed one-hot output without decoding
