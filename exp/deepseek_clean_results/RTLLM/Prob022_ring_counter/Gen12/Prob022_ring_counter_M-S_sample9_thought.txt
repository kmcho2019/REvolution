To simplify the ring counter implementation:
1. Use a single 8-bit shift register instead of a separate state counter and decoder
2. Implement direct shifting with wrap-around behavior
3. Maintain synchronous reset to initialize to 8'b00000001
4. Eliminate the need for case statements by using simple shift operations
5. Keep the one-hot output property where only one bit is high at a time

This approach reduces the design to its simplest form while maintaining all required functionality. The shift register approach is more direct and eliminates intermediate state variables.
