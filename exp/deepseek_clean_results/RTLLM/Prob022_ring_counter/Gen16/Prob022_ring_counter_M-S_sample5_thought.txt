To simplify the ring counter implementation while maintaining functionality:

1. Use a direct shift register approach with synchronous reset
2. Initialize to 8'b00000001 on reset
3. On each clock cycle, perform a left shift with wrap-around
4. This eliminates the need for a separate position counter and decoder
5. The output is purely sequential logic, avoiding combinational glitches

The simplified approach:
- Single 8-bit register for the state
- Reset sets the LSB to 1
- Each clock shifts left with MSB wrapping to LSB
- No intermediate counters or decoders needed
