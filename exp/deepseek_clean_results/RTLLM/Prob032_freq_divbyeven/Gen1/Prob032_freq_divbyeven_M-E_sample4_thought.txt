Novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Instead of counting clock cycles, use a shift register with NUM_DIV/2 bits
2. Initialize the shift register with a single '1' (one-hot encoding)
3. On each clock cycle, rotate the '1' through the shift register
4. The output clock toggles when the '1' wraps around to the first bit
5. This eliminates the need for comparison operations, potentially reducing logic
6. Reset initializes the shift register to its starting state
