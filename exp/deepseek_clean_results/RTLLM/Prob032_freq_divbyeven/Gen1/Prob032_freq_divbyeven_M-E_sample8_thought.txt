Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Instead of a traditional counter, use a shift register with NUM_DIV/2 bits
2. Initialize the shift register with a single '1' and the rest '0's
3. On each clock cycle, rotate the shift register right
4. The output clock toggles whenever the '1' wraps around
5. This eliminates the need for comparison logic (== operator)
6. The shift register naturally handles the division factor without arithmetic
7. Reset initializes the shift register pattern and output clock
