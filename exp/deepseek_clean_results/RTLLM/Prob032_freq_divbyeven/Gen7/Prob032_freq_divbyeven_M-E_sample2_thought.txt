Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a traditional counter:
1. Instead of counting clock cycles, we'll use a shift register that circulates a single '1' bit
2. The shift register length is half of NUM_DIV (since we toggle on both rising and falling edges)
3. The MSB of the shift register directly drives the clock divider output
4. Reset initializes the shift register with a '1' in the LSB position
5. This approach eliminates the need for comparison logic and reduces gate count
6. The shift register naturally wraps around, simplifying the control logic

Advantages:
- No comparison operations needed (reduces critical path)
- Simple, regular structure good for synthesis
- Easily scales to large division ratios
- Glitch-free output by design
