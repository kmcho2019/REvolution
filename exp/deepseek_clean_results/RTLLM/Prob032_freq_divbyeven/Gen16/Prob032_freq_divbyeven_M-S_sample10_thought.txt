Simplified design approach:
1. Remove the separate toggle register to reduce complexity
2. Use the MSB of the counter directly as the divided clock output
3. Adjust counter width and limit to maintain even division
4. Reset counter and output to 0 on active-low reset
5. Increment counter each clock cycle, wrapping at NUM_DIV-1
6. Since NUM_DIV is even, MSB toggles at exactly half the frequency
7. This eliminates one register and associated logic while maintaining functionality
