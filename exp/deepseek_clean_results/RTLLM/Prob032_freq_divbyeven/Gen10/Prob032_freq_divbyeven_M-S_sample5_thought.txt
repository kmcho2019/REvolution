To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality:

1. Remove the explicit counter variable and use the output clock itself as a toggle indicator
2. Utilize a single always block for both reset and clock division logic
3. Still maintain parameterized even division capability
4. Keep the same interface (ports and parameters)
5. Simplify the logic by counting toggles rather than maintaining a separate counter
6. Reset behavior remains identical (active-low reset initializes output to 0)

Key simplifications:
- Eliminate the separate counter register
- Reduce logic operations by directly toggling based on clock edges
- Maintain same division ratio but with fewer registers
- Still produces clean 50% duty cycle output
