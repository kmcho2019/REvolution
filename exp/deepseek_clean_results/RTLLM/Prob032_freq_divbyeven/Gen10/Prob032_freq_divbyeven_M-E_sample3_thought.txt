Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:

1. Instead of using a counter and comparator, implement a circular shift register with NUM_DIV/2 bits
2. Initialize the shift register with a single '1' and the rest '0's
3. On each clock edge, rotate the bits right
4. The MSB of the shift register drives the clock divider output
5. This eliminates the need for comparison operations and reduces logic complexity
6. The shift register naturally wraps around, creating the desired frequency division
7. Still maintains parameter validation for even division factors
8. Synchronous reset initializes both the shift register and output
