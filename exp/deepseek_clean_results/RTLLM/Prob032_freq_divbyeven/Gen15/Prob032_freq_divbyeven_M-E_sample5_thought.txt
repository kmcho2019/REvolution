Novel design approach:
1. Instead of using a standard counter, implement a shift register-based approach for frequency division
2. The shift register will have NUM_DIV/2 stages to achieve even division
3. A single '1' will circulate through the shift register, creating the divided clock output
4. Reset initializes the shift register with '1' in the LSB and '0's elsewhere
5. On each clock edge, the '1' shifts right (with wrap-around)
6. The MSB of the shift register drives the divided clock output
7. This approach eliminates comparison logic and uses simple shifting
8. The division ratio is still controlled by the NUM_DIV parameter
