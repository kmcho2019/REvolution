To correctly implement the even frequency divider:

2. Use the parameter NUM_DIV to determine division ratio (must be even)
3. Toggle output clock only when counter reaches (NUM_DIV/2)-1
4. Reset both counter and output clock on active-low reset
5. Ensure 50% duty cycle by toggling at half the division period
