Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter for frequency division:
1. Instead of counting cycles, we'll use a shift register that circulates a single '1' bit
2. The shift register length will be NUM_DIV/2 bits (since we toggle on half cycles)
3. When the '1' reaches the end of the shift register, we toggle the output clock
4. This eliminates the need for comparison logic (== operator) and uses simpler shift operations
5. The approach is particularly efficient for larger division factors as it requires only a single bit comparison
6. Reset initializes the shift register with a '1' in the LSB position
