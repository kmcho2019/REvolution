Novel approach using a down-counter with edge detection:
1. Instead of counting up to half the division factor, we'll use a down-counter initialized to NUM_DIV/2
2. The counter decrements on each clock cycle and reloads when it reaches zero
3. The clk_div toggles only when the counter reaches zero
4. This approach reduces comparison logic since we only need to detect zero
5. The down-counter naturally handles the division factor without needing to calculate (NUM_DIV/2 - 1)
6. Reset initializes the counter to NUM_DIV/2 and clk_div to 0
