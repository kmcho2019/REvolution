Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Instead of counting cycles, we'll use a shift register of length NUM_DIV/2
2. The shift register will circulate a single '1' bit
3. The output clock toggles whenever the '1' bit wraps around
4. This eliminates the need for comparison logic (== operator)
5. Reset initializes the shift register with '1' in the LSB position
6. The design naturally enforces even division since the shift register length is NUM_DIV/2
This approach reduces logic complexity and may offer better timing characteristics.
