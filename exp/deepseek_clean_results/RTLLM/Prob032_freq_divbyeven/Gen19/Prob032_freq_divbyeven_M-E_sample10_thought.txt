New Approach: Phase-Aligned Even Frequency Divider

1. Instead of using a simple counter and toggle, this design employs a phase-aligned approach that ensures the divided clock edges align precisely with the input clock edges.

2. Key features:
   - Uses two counters: one for counting full cycles (full_cnt) and one for tracking half cycles (half_cnt)
   - Generates two phase-shifted clock signals (clk_div_p and clk_div_n)
   - Combines them to create a perfectly aligned 50% duty cycle output
   - The divided clock transitions occur exactly at the input clock edges

3. Advantages:
   - More precise edge alignment
   - Better handling of reset conditions
   - Clear separation of counting and phase generation
   - Easier to verify timing relationships

4. Implementation:
   - full_cnt counts complete division periods
   - half_cnt tracks the midpoint of each period
   - Two flip-flops generate complementary phases
   - Final output is OR combination for clean transitions
