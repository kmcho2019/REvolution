Novel approach using shift register for frequency division:

1. Instead of a traditional counter, use a shift register approach
2. Initialize shift register with alternating pattern for even division
3. Rotate the pattern on each clock cycle
4. Output is taken from MSB of shift register
5. Number of bits in shift register equals NUM_DIV/2
6. Automatically ensures 50% duty cycle
7. More efficient for larger division factors (no comparison needed)
8. Reset initializes pattern to all zeros (output starts low)
9. Each bit represents one clock cycle of the divided output
