Novel approach using shift register for frequency division:

1. Instead of a traditional counter, use a shift register to track division cycles
2. The shift register length equals NUM_DIV/2 bits (half the division factor)
3. A single '1' circulates through the shift register to mark division points
4. Output toggles when the '1' reaches the end of the shift register
5. Benefits:
   - No arithmetic comparisons needed (just shift operations)
   - Naturally enforces even division (shift register length must be integer)
   - Simple reset behavior (just load initial pattern)
   - Easily scalable to large division factors
   - Clear visual representation of division process
6. Still maintains parameter validation for even division
7. Uses minimal logic for implementation
