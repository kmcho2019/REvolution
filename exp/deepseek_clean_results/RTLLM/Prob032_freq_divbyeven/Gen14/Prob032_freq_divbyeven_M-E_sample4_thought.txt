Novel architectural idea:
1. Use a down-counter approach instead of up-counter for more efficient comparison
2. Implement a preloadable counter that reloads when reaching zero
3. The divided clock toggles only when counter wraps around
4. Counter is initialized to NUM_DIV/2 - 1 (since we toggle every N/2 cycles)
5. Uses XOR feedback for the counter to create a pseudo-random division pattern
6. Advantages: simpler comparison logic (just zero detection), more power efficient
7. Added benefit of glitch-free output due to register-based toggling
