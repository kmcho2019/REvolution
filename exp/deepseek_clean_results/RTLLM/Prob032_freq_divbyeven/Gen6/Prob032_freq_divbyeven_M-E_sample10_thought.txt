Novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Instead of using a traditional counter, implement a circular shift register that rotates a single '1' through its bits
2. The length of the shift register equals half the division factor (NUM_DIV/2)
3. The divided clock toggles whenever the '1' wraps around from the MSB back to the LSB
4. Benefits:
   - Eliminates comparator logic (no need to compare counter value)
   - Simple wrap-around detection (just monitor the MSB)
   - Naturally glitch-free output since it's registered
   - Scales well for larger division factors
5. Reset initializes the shift register with '1' in LSB position
6. Still maintains parameter validation for even division factors
