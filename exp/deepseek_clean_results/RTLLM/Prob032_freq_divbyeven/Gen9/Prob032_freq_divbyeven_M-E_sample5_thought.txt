I'll implement a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter. This design will:
1. Use a circular shift register with NUM_DIV/2 bits to track the division cycles
2. Initialize with a single '1' that rotates through the register
3. Toggle the output clock whenever the '1' wraps around
4. Automatically validate even division factors
5. Provide glitch-free output by only toggling on clock edges

Advantages:
- Eliminates comparison logic (just checks MSB of shift register)
- Naturally enforces even division (register length is NUM_DIV/2)
- Simple and elegant implementation
- Consistent timing behavior
