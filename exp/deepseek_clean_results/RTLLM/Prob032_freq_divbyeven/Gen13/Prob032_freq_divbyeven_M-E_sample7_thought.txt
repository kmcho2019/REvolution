Novel approach using shift register with pattern generation:
1. Instead of using a counter, implement a shift register that circulates a specific pattern
2. The pattern length equals NUM_DIV and contains exactly two transitions (0->1 and 1->0)
3. For even division, the pattern will have equal number of 1s and 0s (50% duty cycle)
4. The MSB of the shift register directly drives the clk_div output
5. On each clock edge, the pattern rotates right by one bit
6. Reset initializes the pattern with a single '1' in the middle position
7. This approach eliminates comparison logic entirely and uses simple shifting
8. More efficient for larger division factors as it doesn't require wide comparators
