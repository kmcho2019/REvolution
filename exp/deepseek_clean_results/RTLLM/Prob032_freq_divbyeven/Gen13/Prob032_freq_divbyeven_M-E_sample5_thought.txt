Novel architectural idea:
1. Use a shift register approach instead of a conventional counter
2. Implement a circular shift register with width equal to NUM_DIV/2
3. The divided clock is generated from the MSB of the shift register
4. On each clock edge, shift left with circular feedback
5. Reset initializes the shift register to have a single '1' at LSB
6. This creates a natural divide-by-N behavior where N is the register width*2
7. Advantages: simpler logic, no comparators needed, scales easily with division factor
