Novel Architecture Idea: Cascaded Clock Divider with Shared Counter

Key Concepts:
1. Hierarchical division: First divide by 2 (50MHz), then use this as base for 10MHz (divide by 5), then use 10MHz as base for 1MHz (divide by 10)
2. Shared counter logic: Only need two counters instead of three
3. Phase-aligned outputs: All divided clocks maintain consistent phase relationship
4. Reduced power: Lower frequency clocks toggle less frequently
5. Minimal logic: Eliminates redundant counter structures

Advantages:
- 33% fewer counters compared to parallel implementation
- Natural phase alignment between clocks
- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
- Simpler timing analysis with clear clock hierarchy
- More efficient use of flip-flops
