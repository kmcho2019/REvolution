Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove unnecessary parameters and localparams since division ratios are fixed
2. Use simple up-counters with direct comparison for easier understanding
3. Eliminate clock enable signals as they add complexity without significant benefit here
4. Keep separate always blocks for each clock domain for clear timing paths
5. Use minimum required counter widths (5 bits for 1MHz counter)
6. Maintain synchronous reset for all outputs

Key simplifications:
- Direct counter comparisons instead of enable signals
- Fixed division ratios instead of parameters
- Up-counters instead of down-counters
- Removed unnecessary calculations
