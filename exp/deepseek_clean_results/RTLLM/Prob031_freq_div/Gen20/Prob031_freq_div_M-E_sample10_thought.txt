Novel Architecture:
1. Use a single shared counter to minimize area and power
2. Implement cascaded clock enables to reduce redundant counting logic
3. Generate phase-aligned outputs by deriving all clocks from the same counter
4. Use gray coding for the counter to minimize switching power
5. Implement glitch-free clock generation with synchronous toggling
6. Add parameterized division ratios for flexibility

Key Benefits:
- Reduced area from shared counter
- Lower power from gray coding and minimized transitions
- Phase-aligned outputs for synchronous systems
- Glitch-free operation
- Easily scalable to other frequencies
