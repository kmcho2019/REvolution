Novel Architecture: Cascaded Divider with Clock Gating
1. Hierarchical Division: Implement a cascaded frequency divider where each stage divides the output of the previous stage, reducing counter sizes and power consumption.
2. Clock Gating: Use clock gating for the lower frequency dividers to minimize unnecessary toggling and reduce dynamic power.
3. Minimal Counters: Optimize counter sizes by leveraging the cascaded structure (only need 5-bit counter for 1MHz instead of 6-bit).
4. Synchronous Design: Maintain synchronous reset and edge-triggered behavior for reliable operation.
5. Balanced Duty Cycle: Ensure 50% duty cycle by toggling at midpoint of each division period.

This approach reduces power by minimizing high-frequency counter toggles and optimizes area through smaller cascaded counters.
