To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality:
1. CLK_50 can remain a simple toggle flip-flop (divide by 2)
2. CLK_10 can be derived from CLK_50 by dividing it further by 5 (using a 2-bit counter)
3. CLK_1 can be derived from CLK_10 by dividing it further by 10 (using a 3-bit counter)
This hierarchical approach reduces counter sizes and logic complexity while achieving the same frequency division ratios.
