Novel Architecture Idea: Hierarchical Clock Division with Cascaded Counters

Key Concepts:
1. Instead of independent counters, use a cascaded approach where higher frequency clocks generate lower frequency ones
2. The 50MHz clock (div-by-2) is generated first
3. The 10MHz clock (div-by-5) is derived from the 50MHz clock
4. The 1MHz clock (div-by-10) is derived from the 10MHz clock
5. This reduces total flip-flop count and power consumption
6. Uses synchronous reset for all registers
7. Implements minimal-width counters for each division stage

Advantages:
- Reduced logic since higher frequency clocks are reused
- Lower power due to fewer toggling flip-flops
- Natural phase relationship between clocks
- Easier timing closure with staged clock domains
