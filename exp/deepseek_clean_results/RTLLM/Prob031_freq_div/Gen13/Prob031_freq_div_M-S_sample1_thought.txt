Simplification Strategy:
1. Direct Implementation: Remove parameterization since division factors are fixed (2, 10, 100)
2. Counter Optimization: Use simple up-counters with direct comparison instead of down-counters
3. Reset Logic: Keep synchronous reset for all outputs and counters
4. Clock Generation: Maintain separate always blocks for each clock domain
5. Counter Widths: Use minimum required bits (1-bit for 50MHz, 4-bit for 10MHz, 7-bit for 1MHz)

This simplified version reduces unnecessary complexity while maintaining the exact same functionality.
