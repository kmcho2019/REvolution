Novel Architectural Idea: Cascaded Divider with Glitch-Free Outputs
1. Instead of using separate counters for each output frequency, implement a cascaded divider structure where each stage divides the output of the previous stage.
2. The 50MHz clock is generated first by dividing the input by 2.
3. The 10MHz clock is then generated by dividing the 50MHz clock by 5.
4. The 1MHz clock is generated by dividing the 10MHz clock by 10.
5. This approach reduces overall switching activity and power consumption since higher frequency divisions are derived from lower frequency clocks.
6. Glitch-free outputs are ensured by using registered outputs at each stage.
7. The design maintains synchronous reset behavior for all outputs.
