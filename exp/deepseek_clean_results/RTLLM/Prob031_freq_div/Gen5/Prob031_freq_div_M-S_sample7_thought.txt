To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality:
1. For CLK_50 (50MHz): Keep simple toggle flip-flop (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10 (10MHz): Reduce counter size by using a 4-bit counter (0-9) and toggle when counter equals 9 (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1 (1MHz): Derive from CLK_10 by adding another 10:1 divider (total 100:1 division)
This reduces counter complexity and leverages existing divided clocks for further division.
