Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Clock Division with Cascaded Counters

Key Innovations:
1. Cascaded counter structure where higher frequency clocks generate lower frequency clocks
2. Uses Gray code counters to minimize switching activity and power consumption
3. Separate always blocks for each clock domain to improve timing closure
4. Parameterized design with calculated counter widths for flexibility
5. Synchronous reset with independent control for each clock domain

Advantages:
- Reduced power consumption due to Gray coding and hierarchical division
- Better timing closure with separated always blocks
- More scalable architecture for adding additional divided clocks
- Clear signal relationships between clock domains
- Lower area overhead through cascading
