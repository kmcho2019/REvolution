Novel Architectural Idea: Cascaded Clock Gating with Enable Signals
1. Instead of using a single large counter, implement a cascaded counter structure where each stage divides the clock by a smaller factor
2. Use clock enable signals to create the divided clocks, reducing unnecessary toggling
3. First stage divides by 2 (50MHz) using simple toggle
4. Second stage divides the 50MHz clock by 5 to get 10MHz
5. Third stage divides the 10MHz clock by 10 to get 1MHz
6. Each stage has its own independent counter, reducing critical path
7. Clock gating reduces power consumption by preventing unnecessary transitions

Key Advantages:
- Reduced power consumption due to clock gating
- Shorter critical paths as each counter is smaller
- More modular and scalable design
- Clear separation of clock domains
- Lower switching activity in counters
