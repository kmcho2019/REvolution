Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Use a cascaded counter approach where each stage divides the previous clock
2. First stage divides by 2 (100MHz → 50MHz)
3. Second stage divides the 50MHz clock by 5 (50MHz → 10MHz)
4. Third stage divides the 10MHz clock by 10 (10MHz → 1MHz)
5. Benefits:
   - Natural clock tree structure matches frequency hierarchy
   - Each stage operates at lower frequency, reducing power
   - Clear separation of timing domains
   - Smaller counters needed at each stage
6. Implementation:
   - Three separate always blocks for each divider stage
   - Synchronous reset for all registers
   - Minimal counter sizes for each division
