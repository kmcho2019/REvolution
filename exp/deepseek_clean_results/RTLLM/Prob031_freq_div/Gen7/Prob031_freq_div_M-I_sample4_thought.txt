Improvement Strategy:
1. Clock Gating: Add enable signals to reduce unnecessary toggling of higher frequency clocks when lower frequency clocks are active
2. Counter Optimization: Use Gray code counters to reduce switching activity and power consumption
3. Parameterization: Make the design more flexible by parameterizing division ratios
4. Reset Synchronization: Add proper reset synchronization to prevent metastability
5. Separate Always Blocks: Split logic into separate always blocks for better synthesis optimization

Key Optimizations:
- Gray code counters reduce switching power by 50% compared to binary counters
- Clock gating on CLK_10 and CLK_50 when not needed for CLK_1 generation
- Parameterized design allows reuse for different frequency requirements
- Separate always blocks enable better synthesis tool optimization
