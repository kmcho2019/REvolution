Novel Architecture: Cascaded Clock Divider with Gated Counters
1. Hierarchical Division: Implement a master counter that counts to 100 (for 1MHz), then derive all other clocks from this counter's intermediate values to minimize redundant counting logic.
2. Clock Gating: Use the master counter's bits to generate enable signals for the higher frequency clocks, reducing unnecessary toggling.
3. Phase Alignment: Ensure all output clocks have aligned rising edges by deriving them from the same counter state.
4. Resource Sharing: Reuse counter bits for multiple clock divisions to minimize area.
5. Power Optimization: Gate the clock enables to prevent unnecessary transitions in the higher frequency dividers.

This approach reduces area by sharing counter logic and improves power efficiency by minimizing unnecessary toggles.
