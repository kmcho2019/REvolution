Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove the intermediate register for CLK_50 and directly assign it as a wire
2. Combine the counter logic for CLK_10 and CLK_1 since they are related (100MHz/10 = 10MHz, 10MHz/10 = 1MHz)
3. Use a single counter that counts up to 99 (for both 10MHz and 1MHz generation)
4. Generate CLK_10 at every 5th count (0-4) and CLK_1 at every 50th count (0-49)
5. Maintain synchronous reset for all outputs
6. Reduce register usage by sharing counter between both slower clocks

Key Benefits:
- Fewer registers (single counter instead of two)
- Simplified control logic
- Maintains exact same functionality
- More efficient implementation
