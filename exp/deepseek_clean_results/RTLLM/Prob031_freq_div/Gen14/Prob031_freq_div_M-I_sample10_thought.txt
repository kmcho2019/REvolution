Improvement Strategy:
1. Power Optimization: 
   - Implement clock gating for counters when their outputs aren't needed
   - Use clock enable signals to minimize unnecessary toggles
2. Area Optimization:
   - Replace generic counter with fixed-width counters to eliminate $clog2() overhead
   - Share common counter logic where possible
3. Performance:
   - Maintain synchronous reset for reliable timing
   - Keep balanced clock generation paths
4. Modularity:
   - Keep clean separation of concerns while reducing redundant logic

Key Changes:
- Fixed-width counters for known division ratios
- Shared counter logic for CLK_10 and CLK_1 generation
- Optimized enable conditions to reduce switching activity
- Maintained same functionality with improved PPA
