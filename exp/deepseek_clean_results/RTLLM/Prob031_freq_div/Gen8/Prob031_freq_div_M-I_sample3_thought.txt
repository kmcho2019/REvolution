Optimization Strategy:
1. Reduce counter bit-widths to minimum required (2-bit for 10MHz, 6-bit for 1MHz remains optimal)
2. Separate always blocks for different frequency domains to improve timing
3. Use Gray coding for the 1MHz counter to reduce switching activity
4. Implement clock gating for the 50MHz output when not needed
5. Maintain synchronous reset for all outputs
6. Use parameterized constants for better maintainability
