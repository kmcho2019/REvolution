Novel Architecture: Cascaded Divider with Clock Gating
1. Hierarchical Division: Implement a cascaded frequency divider where each stage divides the output of the previous stage, reducing counter sizes and power consumption.
2. Clock Gating: Use clock gating for the lower frequency dividers to minimize unnecessary switching activity.
3. Synchronous Design: Maintain synchronous operation with clean reset behavior.
4. Optimal Counter Sizing: Use minimal counter sizes for each division stage (1-bit for /2, 3-bit for /5, and 4-bit for /2).
5. Duty Cycle Correction: Ensure 50% duty cycle by toggling at the midpoint of each division period.

This approach reduces power by:
- Minimizing high-frequency counter operations
- Gating clocks to lower frequency dividers
- Using smaller counters through cascading
