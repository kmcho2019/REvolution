Novel approach using a single unified counter with phase-aligned outputs:
1. Use a single 7-bit counter (0-99) that serves as the base for all three output clocks
2. Generate CLK_50 from bit 0 (divide by 2)
3. Generate CLK_10 from bit 2 (divide by 10) 
4. Generate CLK_1 from bit 6 (divide by 100)
5. All outputs are perfectly phase-aligned since they derive from the same counter
6. Reduced logic and improved timing by eliminating separate counters
7. Reset synchronously initializes all outputs to 0
