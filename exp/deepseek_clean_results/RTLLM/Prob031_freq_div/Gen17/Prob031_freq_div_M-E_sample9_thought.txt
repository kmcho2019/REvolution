Novel Architecture: Cascaded Clock Divider with Enable Propagation
1. Instead of independent counters, create a cascaded structure where each slower clock is derived from the faster one
2. The 50MHz clock is generated first (direct toggle)
3. The 10MHz clock is generated from the 50MHz clock (5x slower)
4. The 1MHz clock is generated from the 10MHz clock (10x slower)
5. This reduces overall switching activity and power consumption
6. Use synchronous enables to propagate clock edges through the hierarchy
7. Maintain synchronous reset for all registers

Key Advantages:
- Reduced power consumption due to lower switching activity in higher dividers
- Natural clock domain hierarchy improves timing closure
- Fewer counters needed (only 2 counters instead of 3)
- Clean clock enable propagation through the hierarchy
- Minimal logic depth for each clock generation
