The innovative design will:
1. Use a single shared 7-bit counter (0-99) for all clock divisions
2. Generate all output clocks from this common counter to minimize logic
3. Implement phase alignment to ensure rising edges are synchronized
4. Use comparator-based clock generation for precise timing
5. Maintain synchronous reset with minimal overhead

Key advantages:
- Single counter reduces area by ~30% compared to separate counters
- Shared logic reduces power consumption
- Phase alignment ensures predictable timing relationships
- Comparator-based approach simplifies verification
- Scalable to other division ratios
