To implement the frequency divider, we'll use three separate counters:
1. For CLK_50: Simple toggle flip-flop (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10: Counter that counts to 4 (5 cycles total) and toggles (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1: Counter that counts to 49 (50 cycles total) and toggles (divide by 100)

Each output will be synchronous to the input clock and properly reset. The counters will be implemented with always blocks sensitive to posedge CLK_in or posedge RST.
