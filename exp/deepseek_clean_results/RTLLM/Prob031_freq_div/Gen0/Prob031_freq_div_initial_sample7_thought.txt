To implement the frequency divider, we'll use three separate counters:
1. For CLK_50 (divide by 2): Simple toggle flip-flop that inverts on every input clock cycle
2. For CLK_10 (divide by 10): Counter that counts to 4 (5 cycles) and toggles (total 10 input cycles per output cycle)
3. For CLK_1 (divide by 100): Counter that counts to 49 (50 cycles) and toggles (total 100 input cycles per output cycle)

All outputs are synchronous to the input clock and reset synchronously.
