To implement the frequency divider, we'll use three separate counters:
1. For CLK_50 (divide by 2): Simple toggle flip-flop that inverts on every clock cycle
2. For CLK_10 (divide by 10): Counter that counts 5 cycles (since we toggle at midpoint)
3. For CLK_1 (divide by 100): Counter that counts 50 cycles (since we toggle at midpoint)

Each output will be generated by toggling when the respective counter reaches its midpoint value. Reset will synchronously clear all counters and output signals.
