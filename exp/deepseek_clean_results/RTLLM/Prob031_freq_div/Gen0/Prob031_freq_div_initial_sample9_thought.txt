To implement a frequency divider with three output clocks (50MHz, 10MHz, 1MHz) from a 100MHz input clock, we need:
1. For CLK_50: Simple toggle flip-flop that inverts every clock cycle (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10: Counter that counts 5 cycles (0-4) and toggles output (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1: Counter that counts 50 cycles (0-49) and toggles output (divide by 100)
All outputs are synchronous with reset functionality.
