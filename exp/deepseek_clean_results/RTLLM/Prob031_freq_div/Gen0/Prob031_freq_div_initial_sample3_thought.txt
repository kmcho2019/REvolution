To implement the frequency divider, we'll use three separate counters:
1. For CLK_50 (50MHz): Simple toggle flip-flop that inverts every clock cycle (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10 (10MHz): Counter that counts to 4 (5 cycles total) and toggles (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1 (1MHz): Counter that counts to 49 (50 cycles total) and toggles (divide by 100)

All outputs will be synchronous with positive-edge triggered flip-flops and synchronous reset.
