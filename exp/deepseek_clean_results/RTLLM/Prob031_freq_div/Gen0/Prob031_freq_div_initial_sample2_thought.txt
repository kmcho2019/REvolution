To implement the frequency divider, we'll use three separate counters:
1. For CLK_50 (50MHz): Simple toggle flip-flop that inverts on every input clock edge (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10 (10MHz): Counter that counts to 4 (5 cycles) and toggles (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1 (1MHz): Counter that counts to 49 (50 cycles) and toggles (divide by 100)

All outputs will be synchronous with the input clock and properly reset.
