To implement a frequency divider with three output clocks (50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz) from a 100MHz input clock, we need:
1. For CLK_50: A simple toggle flip-flop that inverts its value every clock cycle (divide by 2)
2. For CLK_10: A counter that counts to 4 (5 cycles total) and toggles the output (divide by 10)
3. For CLK_1: A counter that counts to 49 (50 cycles total) and toggles the output (divide by 100)
All outputs should be synchronous and properly reset.
