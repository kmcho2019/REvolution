Novel Architecture: Cascaded Gated-Enable Counters
1. Hierarchical division: First divide by 2 (50MHz), then use that as base for 10MHz (divide by 5), then use 10MHz as base for 1MHz (divide by 10)
2. Each stage generates its own clock enable signal rather than toggling a clock
3. Actual clock gating happens only at the final output stage
4. Benefits:
   - Reduces switching activity in downstream counters
   - More power efficient as higher frequency counters are smaller
   - Clear separation of timing domains
   - Easier timing closure as each stage operates at lower frequency
5. Implementation:
   - First stage: Simple toggle flip-flop for 50MHz
   - Second stage: 3-bit counter with period control for 10MHz
   - Third stage: 4-bit counter with period control for 1MHz
   - Output clocks are gated versions of input clock
