Novel approach using a single unified counter and clock enables:
1. Instead of separate counters for each clock division, use one master counter that counts to 99 (for the least common multiple)
2. Generate clock enables at specific counter values:
   - CLK_50 toggles every cycle (counter LSB)
   - CLK_10 toggles every 5 cycles (when counter[2:0] == 4)
   - CLK_1 toggles every 50 cycles (when counter == 49)
3. This reduces area by sharing counter logic and simplifies timing analysis
4. Added benefit of synchronous clock enables rather than direct clock division
