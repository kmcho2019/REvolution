To simplify the frequency divider while maintaining functionality:
1. Keep CLK_50 as a simple toggle flip-flop (most efficient implementation)
2. Combine the counters for CLK_10 and CLK_1 into a single 7-bit counter (0-99)
   - CLK_10 toggles every 5 counts (0-4)
   - CLK_1 toggles every 50 counts (0-49)
3. Use the same counter for both outputs to reduce area
4. Maintain synchronous reset for all outputs
This reduces the number of counters from 2 to 1 while keeping the same functionality.
