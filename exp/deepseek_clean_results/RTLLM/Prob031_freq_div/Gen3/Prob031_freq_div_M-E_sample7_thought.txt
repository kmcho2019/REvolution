Novel approach using a single unified counter with phase-aligned outputs:
1. Use a single 7-bit counter (0-99) to track all division ratios simultaneously
2. Generate all output clocks from this counter's value:
   - CLK_50: Toggle every counter increment (effectively /2)
   - CLK_10: Toggle when counter[2:0] == 3'b100 (every 5 cycles)
   - CLK_1: Toggle when counter == 7'd49 (every 50 cycles)
3. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic compared to separate counters
   - All outputs are phase-aligned to the same counter
   - Easier to extend for additional divided clocks
   - More power efficient as only one counter toggles
