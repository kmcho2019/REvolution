To simplify the fractional frequency divider while maintaining 3.5x division:
1. Use single-edge triggering (positive edge only) for simpler timing
2. Implement a 3-bit counter that counts 7 cycles (for 3.5x division)
3. Generate two clock phases with different duty cycles (4 and 3 cycles)
4. Combine these phases with an OR gate to create the final output
5. The counter naturally creates the required phase relationship without explicit phase shifting
This approach eliminates double-edge complexity while achieving the same fractional division through simple cycle counting and phase combination.
