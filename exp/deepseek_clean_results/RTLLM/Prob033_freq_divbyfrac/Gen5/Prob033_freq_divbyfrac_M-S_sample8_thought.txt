To simplify the fractional frequency divider while maintaining 3.5x division:

1. Use a single 3-bit counter (0-6) to track cycles
2. Generate two clock phases:
   - Main phase: high for first 4 cycles (0-3)
   - Shifted phase: high for next 3 cycles (4-6), delayed by half cycle
3. Combine phases with OR operation
4. Key simplifications:
   - Single counter instead of dual counters
   - Simplified clock generation logic
   - Clear reset handling
   - Maintains correct 4:3 duty cycle ratio
   - Proper half-cycle phase relationship through edge-triggered flip-flop
