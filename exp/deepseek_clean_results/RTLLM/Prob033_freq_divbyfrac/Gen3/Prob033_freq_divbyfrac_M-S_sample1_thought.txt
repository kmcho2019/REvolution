Simplified approach for 3.5x frequency division:

1. **Single Counter Approach**: Use a single 3-bit counter that cycles through 7 states (0-6) to track the division cycle.

2. **Dual-Edge Triggering**: Generate the divided clock by toggling on both rising and falling edges of the input clock to achieve the fractional division.

3. **Phase Generation**:
   - Main phase (4-clock high, 3-clock low) generated on rising edges
   - Complementary phase (3-clock high, 4-clock low) generated on falling edges
   - Phases are offset by exactly half a cycle (180°)

4. **Output Combination**: OR the two phases together to create the final divided clock with uniform period.

5. **Simplified Reset**: Initialize all registers to zero for consistent startup behavior.

This approach eliminates the need for separate state machines and phase toggle logic while maintaining the correct fractional division ratio.
