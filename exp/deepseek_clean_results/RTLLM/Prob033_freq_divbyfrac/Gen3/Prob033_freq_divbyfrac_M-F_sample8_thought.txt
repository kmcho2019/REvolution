Revised solution addressing the feedback:

1. **Phase Alignment**: 
   - Phase A (4-clock period) starts immediately at reset and toggles every 4 positive edges
   - Phase B (3-clock period) starts after a half-cycle delay and toggles every 3 negative edges
   - Precise 180° phase offset maintained between the two phases

2. **Simplified State Machine**:
   - Removed the complex 7-state counter in favor of direct cycle counting
   - Separate counters for each phase (4 and 3 cycles respectively)
   - Counters synchronized to opposite clock edges for proper phase relationship

3. **Improved Output Generation**:
   - Direct OR combination of both phases without toggle logic
   - Ensures proper pulse stretching for uniform periodicity
   - Glitch-free output by maintaining all transitions synchronized to clock edges

4. **Reset Behavior**:
   - Clear initialization of all counters and phase signals
   - Immediate generation of valid divided clock after reset

5. **Duty Cycle Correction**:
   - Phase A high for 4 cycles, low for 3
   - Phase B high for 3 cycles, low for 4
   - Combined output produces uniform 3.5x division with balanced duty cycle
