To achieve a simplified yet accurate 3.5x fractional divider:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6)
2. Generate two phase-shifted clocks:
   - clk_div1: Toggles every 3.5 cycles (alternating between 3 and 4 cycles)
   - clk_div2: Same as clk_div1 but delayed by half cycle
3. Combine them with OR logic to create uniform output
4. Reset initializes all signals to zero
This maintains the correct average frequency while simplifying the phase generation logic by using a single counter and delayed version.
