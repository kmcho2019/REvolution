2. Generate two phase-shifted clocks:
   - clk_pos: active for 4 cycles (0-3) on positive edges
   - clk_neg: active for 3 cycles (0-2) but delayed by half cycle (using negedge)
3. The phase shift is achieved by:
   - clk_pos leading by 1/2 cycle
   - clk_neg lagging by 1/2 cycle
4. Combine them with OR to create uniform periods
5. Reset synchronously to ensure clean startup
