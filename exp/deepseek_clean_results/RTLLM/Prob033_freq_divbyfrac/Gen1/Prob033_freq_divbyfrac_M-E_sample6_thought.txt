Novel approach using edge-triggered pulse generation:
1. Instead of simple counter-based division, we'll generate precisely timed pulses
2. Use two separate counters to track rising and falling edges independently
3. Create four control signals that trigger at specific fractional intervals
4. Combine these signals with careful edge alignment to produce the 3.5x divided clock
5. The design maintains exact timing by:
   - Counting 7 input clocks per output cycle
   - Generating rising edges at 0 and 3.5 input clock intervals
   - Generating falling edges at 1.75 and 5.25 input clock intervals
6. Uses synchronized flip-flops to prevent glitches
