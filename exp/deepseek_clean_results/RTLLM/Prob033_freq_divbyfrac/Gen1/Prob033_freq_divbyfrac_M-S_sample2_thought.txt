To simplify the 3.5x fractional divider while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6) for the base period
2. Generate two clock phases:
   - Phase A: High for first 4 cycles (0-3), low for next 3 (4-6)
   - Phase B: High for first 3 cycles (0-2), low for next 4 (3-6)
3. Combine these phases with OR logic to create the final output
4. The phase difference naturally creates the required fractional division
5. Reset initializes all signals synchronously
This approach eliminates the need for explicit phase shifting while maintaining the correct division ratio through simple counter-based control.
