1. Maintain the 7-cycle counter (0-6) for the base division ratio
2. Generate two complementary clock phases with precise timing:
   - First phase (clk_phase1) high for 3.5 cycles (0-3, falling at 3.5)
   - Second phase (clk_phase2) high for 3.5 cycles (3.5-7, falling at 7)
3. Implement half-cycle phase shifting by:
   - Using both clock edges (double-edge triggering) for phase generation
   - Creating precise transitions at half-cycle intervals
4. Combine the phases with an OR operation to produce the final output
5. Ensure non-overlapping high periods between phases to prevent continuous high output
