To simplify the 3.5x fractional divider while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6)
2. Generate two complementary clock phases:
   - Phase1: High for first 4 cycles (0-3)
   - Phase2: High for next 3 cycles (4-6)
3. Shift Phase2 by half a clock cycle using the negative edge
4. Combine phases with OR operation to create final output
Key simplifications:
- Eliminate complex conditional logic for half-cycle timing
- Use simple range checks for phase generation
- Leverage clock edge directly for phase shifting
- Maintain clear separation of phase generation
