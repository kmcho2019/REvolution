Simplified and corrected design for 3.5x fractional divider:
1. Maintain 7-state counter (0-6) for basic division ratio
2. Generate two clock phases:
   - Primary phase (clk_0) high for 4 cycles (0-3)
   - Secondary phase (clk_180) high for 3 cycles (4-6)
3. Use simple OR combination of both phases
4. Key improvements:
   - Eliminate negedge-triggered logic (potential timing issues)
   - Use same counter for both phases (simpler synchronization)
   - Clear phase relationship (4+3 cycles = 7 total)
   - Maintains 50% duty cycle in combined output
