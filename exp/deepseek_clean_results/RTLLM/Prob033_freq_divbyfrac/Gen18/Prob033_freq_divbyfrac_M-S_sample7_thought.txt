Simplified design approach for 3.5x fractional division:

1. Maintain the fundamental 7-cycle period (0-6) for 3.5x division
2. Generate two complementary clock phases:
   - Phase A: High for first 4 cycles (0-3)
   - Phase B: High for next 3 cycles (4-6)
3. Implement phase shifting by sampling Phase A on negative edge and Phase B on positive edge
4. Combine the two phase-shifted signals with OR operation to create final output
5. Key simplifications:
   - Remove separate advanced phase logic
   - Use direct counter comparisons for phase generation
   - Single always block for counter and phase sampling
   - Clearer timing relationships with minimal registers
