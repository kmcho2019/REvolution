Simplified approach for 3.5x frequency division:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6) for the base period
2. Generate two clock phases:
   - Phase A: High for first 4 cycles (0-3), low for next 3 cycles (4-6)
   - Phase B: High for first 3 cycles (0-2), low for next 4 cycles (3-6)
3. Combine these phases with OR operation to create the final divided clock
4. Reset initializes all signals to known states
Key simplifications:
- Eliminated separate toggle logic for phases
- Directly generate each phase based on counter value
- Simplified phase combination to basic OR operation
- Maintains correct 3.5x division ratio with proper duty cycle
