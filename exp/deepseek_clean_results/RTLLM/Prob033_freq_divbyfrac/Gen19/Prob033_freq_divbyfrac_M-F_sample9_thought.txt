Corrected Fractional Divider Design:

1. Core Concept:
   - Implement true 3.5x division using double-edge technique
   - Generate two phase-shifted clocks (0° and 180°)
   - Combine them with OR to create fractional division

2. Implementation Approach:
   - Maintain 7-cycle counter (0-6) as base period
   - Create two intermediate clocks:
     * clk_div1: Toggles every 3 or 4 cycles (0° phase)
     * clk_div2: Same as clk_div1 but phase-shifted by 180°
   - Final output is OR of both intermediate clocks

3. Duty Cycle Correction:
   - First period: 4 cycles high, 3 low
   - Second period: 3 cycles high, 4 low
   - Phase shift creates uniform 50% duty cycle when combined

4. Reset Behavior:
   - Synchronous reset initializes counters and outputs
   - Output starts low after reset

- Proper phase generation (0° and 180°)
