Improved design strategy for 3.5x fractional divider:

1. Maintain the 7-cycle period (0-6) for accurate 3.5x division ratio
2. Generate two precisely timed clock phases:
   - Phase A: High for cycles 0-3 (4 cycles)
   - Phase B: High for cycles 4-6 (3 cycles)
3. Implement proper phase shifting:
   - Delay Phase A by half cycle (negative edge sampling)
   - Keep Phase B as is (positive edge aligned)
4. Combine phases with OR operation to create uniform output period
5. Key improvements:
   - Correct phase generation timing (exact cycle counts)
   - Clear separation of phase generation and combination
   - Reset synchronization for clean startup
   - Minimal register usage for better PPA
