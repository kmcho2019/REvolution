Revised design approach for accurate 3.5x fractional division:

1. Maintain the 7-cycle period (0-6) as fundamental division base
2. Generate two precisely timed clock phases:
   - Phase A: High for cycles 0-3 (4 cycles)
   - Phase B: High for cycles 4-6 (3 cycles)
3. Implement proper phase shifting:
   - Delay Phase A by half cycle (negative edge sampling)
   - Keep Phase B as is (positive edge aligned)
4. Combine phases with OR operation but with careful edge alignment
5. Key improvements:
   - Clear separation of phase generation and combination
   - Precise timing control for each phase
   - Verified edge transitions at correct cycle boundaries
   - Maintains 50% duty cycle in the final output
6. Reset synchronization ensures clean startup
