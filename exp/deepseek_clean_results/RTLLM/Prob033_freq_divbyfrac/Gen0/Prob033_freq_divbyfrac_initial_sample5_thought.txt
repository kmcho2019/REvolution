To implement a fractional frequency divider (3.5x), we'll use a counter-based approach with phase shifting. Here's the design approach:
1. Use a 3-bit counter (0-6) to track the 7 clock cycles needed for 3.5x division
2. Generate two intermediate clocks:
   - clk_div1: Toggles every 3 or 4 cycles (alternating)
   - clk_div2: Phase-shifted version of clk_div1 (half cycle delayed)
3. Combine these clocks using OR logic to create the final output
4. The reset initializes the counter and clocks to known states
5. The duty cycle is balanced by alternating between 3 and 4 cycle periods
