To implement a fractional frequency divider (3.5x), we'll use a counter-based approach with phase shifting:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 clock cycles (since 3.5 = 7/2)
2. Generate two intermediate clocks:
   - clk_div1: Toggles every 3 or 4 cycles (alternating)
   - clk_div2: Phase-shifted version of clk_div1 (delayed by half cycle)
3. Combine these clocks using OR operation to get the final output
4. The counter alternates between 4 and 3 cycles to achieve the 3.5 division
5. Reset synchronizes the counter and outputs
