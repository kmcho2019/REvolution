Novel Fractional Divider Architecture:

1. Dual-counter system:
   - Main counter (0-6) tracks full cycles
   - Sub-counter (0-1) tracks half-cycle phases

2. Phase interpolation:
   - Generate two base clocks with 3x and 4x division
   - Interpolate between them using sub-counter

3. Dynamic period adjustment:
   - Alternate between 3 and 4 cycle periods
   - Use sub-counter to maintain precise timing

4. Phase-locked output:
   - Combine base clocks with precise timing
   - Ensure smooth transitions between periods

5. Key innovations:
   - Explicit half-cycle tracking
   - Dynamic period selection
   - Phase interpolation instead of OR-combination
   - No edge-triggered phase generation
   - More deterministic behavior

Advantages:
- Precise 3.5x division ratio
- Balanced duty cycle
- No glitches or race conditions
- Clear state transitions
- Easily scalable to other fractional divisions
