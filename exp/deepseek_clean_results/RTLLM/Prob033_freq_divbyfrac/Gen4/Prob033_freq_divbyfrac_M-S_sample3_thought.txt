To simplify the 3.5x fractional divider while maintaining accuracy:
1. Use a 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6) for the base period
2. Generate two clock phases:
   - clk_div1: High for first 4 cycles, low for next 3 cycles
   - clk_div2: Same pattern but offset by 1 cycle (delayed)
3. Combine them with XOR logic to create the final output
4. Reset initializes all signals synchronously
This approach eliminates complex toggle logic and uses simple pattern generation with a single counter, while the XOR combination naturally creates the required fractional division.
