Novel Rotating Shift Register Approach:

1. **Pattern Generation**:
   - Use a 7-bit shift register (for 3.5x division) that rotates a specific pattern
   - Pattern alternates between 4 active cycles and 3 inactive cycles (1000100)

2. **Double-Edge Sampling**:
   - Sample the shift register on both clock edges to achieve half-cycle precision
   - Maintain two parallel shift registers for phase alignment

3. **Dynamic Pattern Rotation**:
   - Rotate the pattern by 1 bit every full clock cycle
   - This creates the necessary phase relationship between the two edges

4. **Output Combination**:
   - Combine the sampled values from both edges using OR logic
   - This produces the fractional division effect with proper duty cycle

5. **Reset Synchronization**:
   - Initialize the shift register to a known pattern
   - Ensure immediate valid output after reset release

Key Advantages:
- Eliminates complex counter logic
- Naturally maintains phase relationship through bit rotation
- Simple implementation with predictable timing
- Easily scalable to other fractional divisions
