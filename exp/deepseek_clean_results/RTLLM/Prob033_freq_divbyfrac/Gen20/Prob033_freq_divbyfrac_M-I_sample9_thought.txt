Improved fractional divider with 3.5x division:

1. Core Improvements:
   - Use OR operation instead of XOR to properly combine phases
   - Adjust counter comparison values for precise 4/3 cycle alternation
   - Maintain proper phase relationship between posedge and negedge clocks
   - Ensure clean transitions with proper reset handling

2. Implementation Strategy:
   - Keep 7-cycle counter (0-6) as base
   - Generate clk_div1 high for cycles 0-3 (4 cycles)

   - Combine with OR to create final output
   - Reset synchronously for clean startup
