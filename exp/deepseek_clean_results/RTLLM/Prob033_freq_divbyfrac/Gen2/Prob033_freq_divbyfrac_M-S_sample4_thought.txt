To simplify the 3.5x fractional divider while maintaining correct functionality:
1. Use a single 3-bit counter to count 7 cycles (0-6)
2. Generate the divided clock by toggling every 3 or 4 cycles alternatively
3. Implement this by:
   - First period: 4 cycles high, 3 cycles low
   - Second period: 3 cycles high, 4 cycles low
   - Repeat this pattern
4. Use a toggle flip-flop to alternate between the two patterns
5. Reset initializes all signals synchronously
This approach eliminates the need for multiple phases and OR logic while maintaining the correct average frequency through simple pattern alternation.
