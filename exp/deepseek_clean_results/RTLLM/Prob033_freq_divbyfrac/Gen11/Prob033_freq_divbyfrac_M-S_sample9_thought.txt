Simplified Fractional Divider Approach:

1. **Core Concept**:
   - Single counter approach (0-6) for 3.5x division (7 clock cycles total)
   - Generate two intermediate clocks with 50% phase shift:
     * clk_a: toggles every 3 or 4 cycles (alternating)
     * clk_b: same as clk_a but delayed by half clock cycle
   - OR both clocks to create final output with correct fractional division

2. **Key Simplifications**:
   - Eliminates dual counters and complex synchronization
   - Uses single counter with simple toggle logic
   - Implements true phase shifting through delay elements
   - Maintains correct 3.5x division ratio with balanced periods

3. **Operation**:
   - Counter increments 0→6 (7 cycles total)
   - clk_a toggles at count 3 (first period) and count 5 (second period)
   - clk_b is clk_a delayed by 180°
   - OR operation combines both clocks for uniform output
