Revised Fractional Divider Solution:

1. **Problem Analysis**:
   - Current implementation fails due to improper phase alignment between main and phase-shifted clocks
   - Simple OR combination doesn't account for the precise timing needed for 3.5x division
   - Need to generate two complementary clocks with precise phase relationship

2. **New Approach**:
   - Maintain 7-cycle counter (0-6) for accurate period measurement
   - Generate two independent clocks:
     * Clock A: High for first 4 cycles, low for next 3
     * Clock B: High for last 3 cycles, low for first 4 (phase-shifted version)
   - Use XOR instead of OR to combine clocks for proper fractional division
   - This creates a clock with:
     * 3.5 source clock periods per output cycle
     * 50% duty cycle (after multiple cycles)
     * Correct phase alignment

3. **Implementation Details**:
   - Keep 3-bit counter for cycle tracking
   - Generate Clock A on positive edge
   - Generate Clock B with inverted pattern and half-cycle delay
   - XOR combination ensures proper pulse timing
   - Reset properly initializes all signals

4. **Key Improvements**:
   - Correct phase relationship between clock components
   - Proper pulse timing through XOR combination
   - Maintains accurate 3.5x division ratio
   - Better duty cycle distribution
   - Verified reset behavior
