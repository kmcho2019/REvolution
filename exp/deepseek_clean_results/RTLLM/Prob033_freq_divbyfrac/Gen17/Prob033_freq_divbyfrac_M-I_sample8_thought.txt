Improved design strategy for 3.5x fractional divider:

1. Maintain the 7-cycle counting approach (0-6) for 3.5x division
2. Key improvements:
   - Generate two complementary clock phases with proper phase alignment
   - Phase A: High for first 3.5 cycles (4 positive edges)
   - Phase B: High for next 3.5 cycles (3 positive edges + 1 negative edge)
   - Use both positive and negative edge triggering for precise timing
   - Combine phases with XOR instead of OR for better duty cycle
3. Benefits:
   - Correct fractional division ratio (3.5x)
   - Better duty cycle (50% target)
   - Precise phase alignment
   - Reduced glitches in output
4. Operation:
   - Each complete cycle takes 7 input clock periods
   - Output transitions occur at both positive and negative edges
   - Phase relationship ensures smooth fractional division
