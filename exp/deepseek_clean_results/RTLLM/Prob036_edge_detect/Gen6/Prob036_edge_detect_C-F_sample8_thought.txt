Fusion Strategy:
1. Maintain the clear separation of combinational and sequential logic for better modularity and readability
2. Use the optimized combinational logic expressions (~a_prev & a for rise, a_prev & ~a for down) for minimal gate count
3. Keep the synchronous active-low reset for reliable initialization
4. Use non-blocking assignments for sequential logic to ensure proper synthesis behavior
5. Add parameterization for potential future flexibility
6. The result is a clean, efficient implementation with:
   - Clear code structure
   - Minimal logic depth for best timing
   - Low power and area
   - Easy maintainability and verification
   - Potential for future extension
