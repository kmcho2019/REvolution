The current solution is already quite efficient, but we can make some minor optimizations to further improve PPA:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating for the a_prev register when 'a' is stable
   - This reduces dynamic power by preventing unnecessary clock toggles

2. Area Optimization:
   - Share the inverter between rise and down paths
   - This reduces gate count by eliminating duplicate inverters

3. Timing Optimization:
   - The design already has excellent timing (0ns WNS/TNS)
   - No changes needed for timing

The improved design will:
- Maintain the same functionality
- Reduce power consumption through clock gating
- Slightly reduce area through logic sharing
- Keep the same excellent timing characteristics
