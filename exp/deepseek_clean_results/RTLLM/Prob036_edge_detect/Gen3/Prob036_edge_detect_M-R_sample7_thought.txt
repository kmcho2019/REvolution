The original solution uses a registered version of 'a' (a_prev) and combinational logic for edge detection. We can refactor this to:
1. Move the edge detection logic into the always block
2. Register the rise and down signals to ensure clean, glitch-free outputs
3. This makes the outputs synchronous to the clock which may be preferable in some designs
4. The functionality remains identical but the outputs are now registered
5. This approach may be better for timing closure in some cases since all signals are registered
