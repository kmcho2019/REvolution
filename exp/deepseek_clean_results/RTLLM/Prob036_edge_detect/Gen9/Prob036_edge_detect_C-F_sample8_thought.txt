Fusion strategy:
1. Maintain the minimal register approach from both examples (single a_prev flip-flop)
2. Keep the simple combinational logic for edge detection
3. Add output registers to:
   - Prevent glitches on rise/down signals
   - Reduce switching power by only updating outputs on clock edges
   - Improve timing by registering outputs
4. Use consistent signal naming and formatting
5. Keep the efficient reset handling from both examples

The resulting design combines the area efficiency of both examples with improved power characteristics from registered outputs.
