Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator DDS
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally becomes our square wave output
3. Benefits:
   - Linear frequency control (output freq = (freq * clk_freq) / 2^N)
   - No explicit comparison operations needed
   - Naturally glitch-free output transitions
   - Easily scalable to higher precision
4. Implementation:
   - 8-bit phase accumulator (matches input freq width)
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - No explicit counter reset logic needed
   - Frequency resolution determined by accumulator width
