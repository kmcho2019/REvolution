Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Approach
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Benefits:
   - Smoother frequency transitions (no glitches when changing freq)
   - Higher frequency resolution (can implement fractional frequencies)
   - More efficient hardware implementation (simple adder vs comparator)
   - Easily scalable to different frequency ranges
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Use accumulator's overflow as the square wave output
   - Simple synchronous reset for freq=0 case
   - No need for explicit counter comparisons
