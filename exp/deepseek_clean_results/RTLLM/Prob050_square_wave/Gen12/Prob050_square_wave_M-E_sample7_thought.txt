Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency value to an accumulator register
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally becomes our square wave output, automatically toggling at the desired frequency
3. Benefits:
   - More precise frequency control (especially for low frequencies)
   - No need for explicit comparison operations
   - Naturally handles frequency changes smoothly
   - Scales better for higher resolution frequency control
4. Implementation:
   - Use a wider accumulator (16-bit) for better frequency resolution
   - The accumulator overflow creates the square wave transitions
   - Frequency input directly controls the phase increment rate
