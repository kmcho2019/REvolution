Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency value to an accumulator register
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally toggles at a rate proportional to the frequency input, creating our square wave
3. Benefits:
   - More precise frequency control (especially for lower frequencies)
   - No need for comparison operations (freq-1)
   - Naturally handles freq=0 case (accumulator won't increment)
   - Single always block implementation
   - Scales well for higher resolution frequency control
4. Tradeoffs:
   - Slightly larger accumulator register (16-bit vs original 8-bit)
   - More sophisticated concept but cleaner implementation
