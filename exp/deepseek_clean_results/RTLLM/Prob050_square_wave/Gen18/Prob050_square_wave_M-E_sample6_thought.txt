Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Approach
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Benefits:
   - Smoother frequency transitions (no glitches when changing freq)
   - More precise frequency generation (especially for lower frequencies)
   - Easier to extend to higher resolution frequencies
   - No explicit counter comparison needed
4. Implementation:
   - Use a wider accumulator (16-bit) for better frequency resolution
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - Automatically handles freq=0 case (output stays low)
