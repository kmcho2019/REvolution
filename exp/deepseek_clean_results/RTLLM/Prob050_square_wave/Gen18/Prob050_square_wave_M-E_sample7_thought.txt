Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator for more precise frequency control
2. The accumulator adds the frequency value each clock cycle, overflowing at the maximum value
3. The MSB of the accumulator directly serves as the square wave output
4. Benefits:
   - More accurate frequency generation (especially for low frequencies)
   - No need for explicit comparison operations
   - Naturally handles freq=0 case (output stays low)
   - Scales well for higher resolution frequency control
5. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - No explicit counter reset logic needed
