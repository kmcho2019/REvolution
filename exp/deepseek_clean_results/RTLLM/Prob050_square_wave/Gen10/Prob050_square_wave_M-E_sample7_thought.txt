Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Core Concept: Use a phase accumulator (like DDS) to track waveform phase instead of simple counting
2. Frequency Control: Implement frequency tuning word (FTW) calculation for precise frequency steps
3. Phase Wrapping: Automatically handle phase overflow to generate square wave transitions
4. Power Optimization: Dynamic clock gating based on required output frequency
5. Edge Cases: Special handling for freq=0 (constant low) and freq=255 (maximum toggle rate)

Key Advantages:
- More precise frequency control with linear frequency steps
- Easier frequency scaling if needed (just change accumulator width)
- Lower power at high frequencies due to dynamic clock gating
- Clean mathematical relationship between input freq and output
- Scalable architecture for future enhancements
