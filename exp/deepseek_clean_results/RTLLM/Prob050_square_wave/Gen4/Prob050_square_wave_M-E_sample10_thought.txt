Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency value
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Benefits:
   - Linear frequency control (output frequency directly proportional to input freq value)
   - No explicit comparison needed (MSB toggles automatically when accumulator overflows)
   - Higher frequency resolution (can generate very low frequencies precisely)
   - Easily scalable to higher precision by increasing accumulator width
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator provides good frequency resolution
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - Frequency control is the phase increment value
   - No explicit counter reload logic needed
