Here's a novel approach using a phase accumulator technique commonly used in DDS (Direct Digital Synthesis) implementations:

1. Instead of a simple counter, we'll use a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency value to an accumulator register
2. The MSB of the accumulator will naturally toggle at the desired frequency, creating our square wave
3. This approach provides several advantages:
   - Smoother frequency transitions as the phase is preserved during frequency changes
   - Higher frequency resolution as we can use more bits in the accumulator
   - More efficient implementation for certain frequency ranges
4. Special handling for freq=0 case remains, but now it's simpler as we just force the output low
5. The design uses a single always block for all sequential elements
