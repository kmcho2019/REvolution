Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator for finer frequency control
2. The accumulator adds the frequency value each clock cycle, overflowing at the MSB
3. The MSB of the accumulator directly becomes the square wave output
4. Benefits:
   - Linear frequency control (output freq = (freq * clk_freq)/2^N)
   - No comparison operations needed (reduces critical path)
   - Naturally produces 50% duty cycle
   - Easily scalable to higher precision
5. Implementation:
   - 8-bit phase accumulator (matches input freq width)
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - No explicit counter reload logic needed
