Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally becomes our square wave output
3. Benefits:
   - More precise frequency control (especially for higher frequencies)
   - No need for explicit comparison operations
   - Naturally handles frequency changes without glitches
   - Scales better for higher resolution frequency control
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Frequency input is scaled to be the phase increment per clock
   - MSB of accumulator directly drives output
   - Automatic rollover behavior eliminates need for explicit reset logic
