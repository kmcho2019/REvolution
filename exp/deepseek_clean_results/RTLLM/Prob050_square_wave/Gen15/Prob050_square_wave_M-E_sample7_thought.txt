Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator DDS-style Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally becomes our square wave output
3. Benefits:
   - Smoother frequency transitions (no glitches during freq changes)
   - Higher frequency resolution
   - More efficient hardware implementation
   - Easily extendable to other waveforms
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit phase accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Use top bit as square wave output
   - Automatically handles freq=0 case (output stays low)
   - No need for explicit comparison operations
5. Optimization:
   - Single always block for clean timing
   - No combinational logic in clock path
   - Minimal control logic
