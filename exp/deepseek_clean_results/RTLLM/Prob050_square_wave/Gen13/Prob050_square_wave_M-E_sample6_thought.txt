Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator that adds the frequency value each clock cycle
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Benefits:
   - Linear frequency control (output period ∝ 1/freq)
   - No explicit comparison needed (MSB toggles automatically)
   - Higher frequency resolution
   - Easier to extend to more complex waveforms
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator provides better frequency resolution
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
   - No explicit counter reload logic needed
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Slightly larger area due to wider accumulator
   - More precise frequency control
   - No timing-critical comparison operations
