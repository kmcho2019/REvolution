Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator with Edge Detection
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator that continuously sums the frequency value
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Edge detection on the MSB provides clean transitions
4. Benefits:
   - More precise frequency control (sub-clock cycle resolution possible with wider accumulator)
   - No need for explicit counter resets
   - Frequency changes take effect immediately without phase discontinuity
   - Scales better for higher frequency ranges
5. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Edge detector ensures clean transitions
   - Special handling for freq=0 case
