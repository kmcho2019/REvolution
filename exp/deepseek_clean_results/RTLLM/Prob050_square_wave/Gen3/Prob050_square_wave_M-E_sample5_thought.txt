Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator Square Wave Generator
1. Instead of using a simple counter, implement a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally becomes our square wave output, automatically toggling at the desired frequency
3. Benefits:
   - No explicit comparison operation needed (reduces critical path)
   - Naturally handles frequency changes smoothly without glitches
   - Provides better frequency resolution
   - Scales well to higher frequencies
4. Implementation:
   - Use a wider accumulator (16-bit) for better frequency resolution
   - The frequency input becomes the phase increment value
   - Output is simply the MSB of the accumulator
