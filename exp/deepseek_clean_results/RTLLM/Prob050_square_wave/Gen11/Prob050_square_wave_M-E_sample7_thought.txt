Novel Architecture: Phase Accumulator DDS Approach
1. Instead of a simple counter, use a phase accumulator that continuously adds the frequency control word
2. The MSB of the accumulator naturally creates a square wave with precise frequency control
3. Benefits:
   - More precise frequency control (no quantization at freq boundaries)
   - Smoother frequency transitions
   - No need for explicit counter comparisons
   - Easily scalable to higher resolutions
4. Implementation:
   - 16-bit accumulator for better frequency resolution
   - Use top bit as square wave output
   - Frequency control word calculated as (desired_freq * 2^16) / clk_freq
   - Handle freq=0 case with synchronous clear
