My novel approach implements a hierarchical barrel shifter using a tree of multiplexers with configurable shift directions. Instead of fixed left/right rotation, it supports both directions based on an additional control bit. The key innovations are:

1. **Bidirectional Rotation**: Added a direction control bit (MSB of ctrl) to select left/right rotation
2. **Balanced Mux Tree**: Uses a logarithmic-depth multiplexer tree for better timing
3. **Pre-rotation**: First rotates the input by 4 positions if needed, then conditionally by 2 and 1
4. **Resource Sharing**: Reuses intermediate results between shift stages
5. **Explicit Wiring**: Clearly shows the bit movement patterns for verification

The design maintains efficiency while adding flexibility and improving readability through explicit bit wiring patterns.
