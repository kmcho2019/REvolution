1. Implement proper cascaded shifting where each control bit adds to the total shift amount
2. Perform shifts in stages (4-bit, then 2-bit, then 1-bit) as specified
3. Use intermediate signals to properly accumulate the shifts
4. Maintain the same interface and functionality as specified
5. Keep the design purely combinational for maximum performance
