The barrel shifter will be implemented using a hierarchical approach with three stages of shifting (4-bit, 2-bit, and 1-bit). Each stage will be controlled by a corresponding bit in the ctrl signal. We'll use multiplexers to select between shifted and unshifted versions of the data at each stage. The shifts will be implemented as rotates (circular shifts) where bits that fall off one end are reintroduced at the other end.
