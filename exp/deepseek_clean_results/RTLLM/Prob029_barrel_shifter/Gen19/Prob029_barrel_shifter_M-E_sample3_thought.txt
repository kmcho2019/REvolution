My novel approach implements a hierarchical barrel shifter using a tree of multiplexers with configurable shift amounts. Instead of the traditional staged approach, this design:

1. Uses a binary-weighted shift selection where each control bit enables a specific shift amount (1, 2, or 4 bits)
2. Implements parallel shift paths that are combined using a final multiplexer stage
3. Each shift path is independent and can be enabled/disabled by the control bits
4. The final output is selected based on the OR combination of all active control bits

Key advantages:
- Parallel processing of all possible shifts
- Clear separation of shift amounts
- Easily extensible to larger bit widths
- Lower critical path delay compared to serial implementations
- Maintains the specified control bit behavior (independent shift amounts)
