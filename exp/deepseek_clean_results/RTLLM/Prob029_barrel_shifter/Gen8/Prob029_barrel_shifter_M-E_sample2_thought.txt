Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with pre-computed shift patterns:

Key innovations:
1. Instead of cascaded stages, we pre-compute all 8 possible shift patterns (0-7 positions)
2. Use a 3-level hierarchical multiplexer to select the final output
3. Each level of hierarchy corresponds to one control bit
4. The first level (ctrl[2]) chooses between 0-3 or 4-7 shifts
5. The second level (ctrl[1]) chooses between even or odd shifts within the range
6. The third level (ctrl[0]) makes the final selection between adjacent shifts

Advantages:
- More balanced timing path compared to cascaded stages
- All shift amounts are equally fast regardless of control pattern
- Clear separation of selection logic
- Easier to pipeline if needed
- More intuitive mapping between control bits and shift amounts
