To simplify the barrel shifter while maintaining functionality:

1. Key insight: The three-stage approach can be condensed into a single operation by directly calculating the final shift amount from the control bits
2. The control bits represent shift amounts in powers of 2 (1, 2, 4), so we can combine them into a single shift value (0-7)
3. Use a single right rotation operation based on the combined shift amount
4. This eliminates intermediate stages and wires, reducing complexity
5. The rotation is implemented using concatenation and part-select with the calculated shift amount
6. Maintains all functionality while being more concise and easier to understand
