I'll implement a barrel shifter using a hierarchical multiplexer approach with explicit shift stages. This design:

1. Uses three distinct shift stages (4-bit, 2-bit, 1-bit) controlled by ctrl[2:0]
2. Each stage is implemented using a dedicated module with proper parameterization
3. Employs a clean pipeline of shift operations where each stage's output feeds into the next
4. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of shift operations
   - Easier to understand and modify
   - Better synthesis results due to explicit structure
   - More maintainable than deeply nested ternary operations
   - Scalable to larger bit widths
