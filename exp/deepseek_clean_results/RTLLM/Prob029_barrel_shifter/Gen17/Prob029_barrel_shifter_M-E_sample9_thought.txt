Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure that directly selects the appropriate rotated output based on the combined control signal value. Instead of cascading shift stages, we'll:

1. Pre-compute all 8 possible rotation variants (0-7 positions)
2. Use the 3-bit control signal as a selector for an 8:1 multiplexer
3. Implement the multiplexer using a case statement for clarity

Advantages:
- Single-stage selection rather than cascaded operations
- All rotation variants are computed in parallel
- Clear mapping between control value and rotation amount
- Easily extensible to larger bit widths
- No intermediate wires needed between stages

This approach trades some area (more pre-computation) for potentially better timing (single selection stage).
