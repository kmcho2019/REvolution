Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical selection tree with parallel shift operations:

1. Instead of cascading shifts sequentially, generate all possible shift combinations in parallel
2. Use a 3-level multiplexer tree to select the appropriate shift amount:
   - First level selects between no shift (0) or shift by 4
   - Second level selects between previous result or additional shift by 2
   - Final level selects between previous result or additional shift by 1
3. This approach exposes more parallelism in the design
4. The shift operations are computed independently and then selected
5. Benefits:
   - More balanced critical path (all shift operations happen in parallel)
   - Easier to pipeline if needed
   - Clear separation between shift generation and selection
   - Potentially better for synthesis optimization
6. Still maintains the same functionality but with a different implementation style
