   - Stage 3: 1-bit rotation of the previous stage's result when ctrl[0] is high
3. The rotations are implemented using concatenation with wraparound
4. Each stage's output feeds into the next stage, allowing shifts to accumulate properly
5. The control bits work independently, with their effects combining as expected
