To simplify the barrel shifter while maintaining functionality, we'll:

1. Break down the shifting into three clear stages (4-bit, 2-bit, 1-bit)
2. Use intermediate wires for each stage to improve readability
3. Implement each shift stage with simple concatenation operations
4. Control each stage with its respective ctrl bit
5. Maintain the rotation property by wrapping bits around

This approach:
- Is much more readable and maintainable
- Clearly shows the shifting stages
- Uses simple concatenation operations
- Still maintains efficient hardware implementation
- Preserves all functionality of the original
- Avoids deeply nested ternary operations
