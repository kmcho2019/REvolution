The feedback indicates the original implementation had issues with handling certain control signal combinations and rotation direction. Here's the improved approach:

1. Maintain the efficient 3-stage design (4-2-1 shifts) but ensure correct bit ordering in concatenations
2. Implement left rotation (more common in barrel shifters) where bits wrap around to the right
3. Verify all control combinations work correctly:
   - ctrl=0: no shift
   - ctrl=1: shift by 1
   - ctrl=2: shift by 2
   - ctrl=3: shift by 3 (1+2)
   - etc. up to ctrl=7 (4+2+1=7)
4. Each stage should properly combine with previous shifts when multiple control bits are set
5. Use proper bit selection in concatenations to ensure correct rotation
