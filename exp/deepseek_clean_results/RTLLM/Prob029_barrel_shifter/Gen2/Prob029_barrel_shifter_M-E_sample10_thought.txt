Here's a novel approach using a single-stage parallel multiplexer array instead of cascaded stages. The key innovations are:
1. Pre-compute all possible shift combinations in parallel (8 possibilities for 8-bit input)
2. Use the 3-bit control signal as a direct selector for the final output
3. Eliminate intermediate stages by generating all shifted versions simultaneously
4. Use a case statement for clean selection of the correct shift amount
This approach trades some area (more parallel logic) for potentially better timing (single-stage selection) and simpler control logic.
