The original implementation had a logical flaw where the shifts were applied sequentially rather than independently. To fix this, we need to:
1. Calculate each shift amount independently based on control bits
2. Combine the shift amounts to get the total rotation
3. Perform a single rotation operation based on the combined shift amount
4. Use modulo arithmetic to handle wrap-around for rotation
5. Maintain the same interface and functionality but with correct behavior

This approach ensures that each control bit independently contributes to the shift amount, and the rotation is performed in one operation rather than stages.
