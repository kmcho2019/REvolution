My novel approach implements a hierarchical barrel shifter using a tree of multiplexers with configurable shift amounts. Instead of the traditional 4-2-1 fixed stages, this design:

1. Uses a binary-weighted shift architecture where each control bit enables a different shift magnitude
2. Implements parallel shift paths that are combined at the output
3. Employs a hierarchical selection scheme that minimizes critical path
4. Features:
   - Each control bit independently controls its corresponding shift amount
   - Shift operations are performed in parallel and combined
   - No intermediate registers needed
   - Clean separation of shift magnitudes
5. Advantages:
   - More balanced timing paths
   - Clearer correspondence between control bits and shift amounts
   - Easier to extend to larger widths
   - Better testability as each shift can be verified independently
