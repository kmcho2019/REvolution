Novel architectural approach:
1. Implement a pipelined read operation to improve timing at high frequencies
2. Use a shadow register technique to maintain read data stability during writes to same address
3. Add write-through capability for simultaneous read/write to same address
4. Benefits:
   - Better timing closure with registered read output
   - No read-write conflicts due to shadow register
   - Maintains data coherency during concurrent operations
   - More realistic for high-performance applications
   - Reduced metastability risk on read outputs
