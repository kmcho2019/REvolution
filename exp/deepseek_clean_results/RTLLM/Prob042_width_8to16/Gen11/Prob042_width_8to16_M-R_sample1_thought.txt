Refactored Design Strategy:

2. Separated combinational and sequential logic for better synthesis

6. Maintained identical functionality and timing behavior
7. Improved readability with clear signal naming

Key Improvements:
- Single-bit state flag reduces area and simplifies synthesis
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic prevents synthesis issues
- More straightforward output generation logic
- Maintains all original specifications while being more synthesis-friendly
- Reduced redundant logic for better PPA
