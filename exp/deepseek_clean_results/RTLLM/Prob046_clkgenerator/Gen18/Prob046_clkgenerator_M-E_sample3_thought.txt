Novel Design Approach:
1. Implement a hybrid counter/delay-line oscillator for precise timing
2. Use a digital delay chain to achieve sub-counter resolution
3. Incorporate automatic jitter reduction through statistical averaging
4. Add phase interpolation for smooth period adjustments
5. Implement a self-test mode for period verification
6. Use a fractional-N divider approach for flexible period control

Key Innovations:
- Combines counter and delay-line for high resolution
- Digital jitter reduction without analog components
- Runtime period tuning with minimal glitches
- Built-in self-test capability
- Fractional period control for non-integer periods
- No dependency on simulation time steps
