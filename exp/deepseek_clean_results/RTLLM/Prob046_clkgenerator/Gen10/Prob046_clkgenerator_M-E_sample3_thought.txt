Novel clock generator design using a counter-based approach:
1. Instead of using delays, employs a free-running counter
2. Counter increments on every positive edge of a base clock (simulation time step)
3. Clock toggles when counter reaches PERIOD/2
4. Automatically resets counter after full period
5. Benefits:
   - More deterministic behavior in simulation
   - Easier to synchronize with other events
   - More flexible for future modifications
   - Avoids potential issues with delay-based approaches
6. Still maintains parameterized period control
7. Generates perfect 50% duty cycle
