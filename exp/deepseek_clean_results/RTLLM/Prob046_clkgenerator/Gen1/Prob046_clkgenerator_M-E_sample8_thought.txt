This design implements a clock generator using a counter that:
1. Uses a free-running counter that increments every time unit
2. Compares the counter value against PERIOD/2 to determine when to toggle
3. Resets the counter after each full PERIOD
4. Generates a clean 50% duty cycle clock
5. Is more simulation-friendly and potentially synthesizable
6. Provides better control and observability of the clock generation process
