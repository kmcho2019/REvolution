A novel approach would be to implement the clock generator using a counter-based design rather than simple delay-based toggling. This provides better control and observability of the clock generation process. The design will:
1. Use a counter that increments every time unit
2. Toggle the clock when the counter reaches half the PERIOD value
3. Reset the counter after each full PERIOD
4. Include an initialization phase with explicit clock startup behavior
This method offers more precise control over clock edges and makes it easier to synchronize with other modules.
