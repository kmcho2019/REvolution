Counter-based clock generator with precise timing:
1. Uses a simple counter to divide the simulation time steps
2. Toggles clock output when counter reaches half-period
3. Fully synthesizable implementation
4. Precise timing control through parameterized period
5. Lower power consumption than delay-line approach
6. Minimal area overhead (just a counter and comparator)
7. Maintains 50% duty cycle
8. Easily extendable for clock gating or frequency modulation
