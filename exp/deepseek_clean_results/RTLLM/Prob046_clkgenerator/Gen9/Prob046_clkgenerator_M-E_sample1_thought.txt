Innovative counter-based clock generator design that:
1. Uses a free-running counter to track time instead of direct delays
2. Implements period control through counter comparison
3. Provides better simulation stability by avoiding direct delay statements
4. Allows for runtime period adjustments (though not exposed in this simple version)
5. Uses synchronous design principles for better integration
6. Includes automatic reset handling

Key advantages:
- More reliable simulation behavior
- Easier to integrate with other synchronous logic
- Foundation for future enhancements like dynamic period changes
- Clear separation of timing control and clock generation
- Better visibility into internal timing for debugging
