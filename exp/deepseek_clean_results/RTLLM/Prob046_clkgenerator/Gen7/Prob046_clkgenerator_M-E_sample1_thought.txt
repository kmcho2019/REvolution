Innovative FSM-based clock generator with self-timed control:
1. Use a 2-state FSM (HIGH and LOW) to represent clock phases
2. Implement state transitions based on elapsed time counters
3. Each state maintains its own duration counter for precise timing control
4. State transitions occur when counters reach PERIOD/2 thresholds
5. Eliminates need for continuous counter comparisons
6. Provides clear separation of clock phases in the design
7. Easily extensible for non-50% duty cycles
8. More robust timing control through explicit state management
