The simplified design maintains the same functionality with these optimizations:
1. Remove the separate shift register and counter, using dout_parallel directly as the shift register
2. Eliminate intermediate variables to reduce area
3. Keep the same MSB-first ordering by shifting left and OR-ing new bits
4. Maintain synchronous reset and valid signal timing
5. Simplify the counter logic by directly checking when to output
6. Reduce the number of registers from 3 to 2 (counter and parallel output)
