The improved design maintains the same functionality but adds some optimizations:
1. Keep using a 3-bit counter (0-7) as it's sufficient for counting 8 bits
2. Add clock gating for the shift register to reduce power when din_valid is low
3. Optimize the shift operation to only occur when din_valid is high
4. Maintain MSB-first ordering as specified
5. Clear dout_valid immediately after one clock cycle
6. Keep synchronous reset for all registers
7. The shift register now only updates when din_valid is high, reducing unnecessary toggles
