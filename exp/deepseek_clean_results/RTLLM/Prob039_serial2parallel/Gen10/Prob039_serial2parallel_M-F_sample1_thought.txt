Improved design strategy based on feedback:
1. Maintain core functionality of serial-to-parallel conversion with MSB-first ordering
2. Optimize power by gating shift register updates only when din_valid is active
3. Keep synchronous reset for better timing closure
4. Use efficient concatenation for shift operation
5. Maintain one-cycle assertion of dout_valid
6. Add Gray code counter to minimize bit transitions and reduce power
7. Keep area-efficient design with minimal registers
8. Ensure all outputs are in known state during reset
9. Improve readability with clear signal naming
