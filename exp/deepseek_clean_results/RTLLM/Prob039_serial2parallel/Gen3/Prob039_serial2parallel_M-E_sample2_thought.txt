A novel approach using a shift register with early completion detection:
1. Instead of counting bits, use a thermometer-coded shift register to track received bits
2. The shift register's MSB serves as the completion indicator
3. Each valid input shifts a '1' into the tracking register
4. When the tracking register's MSB goes high, we've received 8 bits
5. This eliminates the need for a separate counter and reduces logic complexity
6. The data is shifted in MSB-first naturally by the shift operation
7. Output valid is simply the tracking register's MSB delayed by one cycle
