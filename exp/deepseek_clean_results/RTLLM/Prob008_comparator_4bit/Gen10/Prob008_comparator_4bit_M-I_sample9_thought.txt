Optimization Strategy: Carry-Lookahead Inspired Comparison
1. Key Improvements:
   - Replace hierarchical AND-OR chains with carry-lookahead style logic for faster propagation
   - Use XNOR for equality comparison (lower power than XOR)
   - Optimize output logic to reduce redundant computations
   - Maintain mutual exclusivity while simplifying logic paths

2. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic depth (faster critical path)
   - Lower power consumption (XNOR, reduced switching)
   - Smaller area (shared intermediate terms)
   - Clearer bit significance progression

3. Implementation Approach:
   - Generate propagate (equality) and generate (greater) signals per bit
   - Cascade comparisons MSB-first with carry-lookahead
   - Compute outputs directly from final comparison state
   - Eliminate redundant intermediate signals
