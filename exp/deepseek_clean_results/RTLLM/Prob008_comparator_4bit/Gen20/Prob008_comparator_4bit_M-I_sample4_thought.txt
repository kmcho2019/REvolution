1. Timing Optimization:

   - Maintain parallel equality check for fastest path
2. Power Optimization:
   - Replace XNOR with XOR+NOT for propagate signals (potentially lower power)
   - Simplify generate signal logic
3. Area Optimization:
   - Share common sub-expressions between greater/less calculations
   - Use direct wiring where possible to reduce gate count
4. Mutual Exclusivity:
   - Maintain structural guarantee of single-hot output encoding
   - Use complementary signals where possible
