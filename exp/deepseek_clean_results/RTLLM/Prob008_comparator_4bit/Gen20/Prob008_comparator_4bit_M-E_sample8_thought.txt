Innovative thermometer-encoded comparison approach:
1. Convert the 4-bit comparison into a thermometer scale where each bit represents a magnitude relationship
2. Create a "difference vector" that captures the signed difference between A and B at each bit position
3. Use a priority encoder to find the most significant bit where A and B differ
4. The sign of this difference determines the final comparison result
5. Key advantages:
   - Efficient encoding of magnitude relationships
   - Naturally prioritizes MSB differences
   - Simple final decision logic
   - Scales well to larger bit widths
   - Minimizes redundant comparisons
