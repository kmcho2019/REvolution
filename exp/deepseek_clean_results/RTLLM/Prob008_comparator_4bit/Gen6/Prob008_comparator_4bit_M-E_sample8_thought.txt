Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Serial Comparison with Early Termination

This design implements a bit-serial comparison approach that:
1. Starts comparison from the MSB (bit 3) and proceeds to LSB (bit 0)
2. Uses early termination - stops comparing once inequality is detected
3. Employs a state machine-like behavior without actual registers
4. Uses minimal logic gates by reusing comparison results
5. Maintains mutual exclusivity through careful logic construction

Key Advantages:
- Reduces switching activity (lower power) since lower bits aren't evaluated when MSB comparison decides outcome
- Minimizes logic depth (better timing) by avoiding long AND-OR chains
- Uses simple gates (XOR, AND, OR) for efficient implementation
- Naturally handles the priority of higher bits over lower bits
