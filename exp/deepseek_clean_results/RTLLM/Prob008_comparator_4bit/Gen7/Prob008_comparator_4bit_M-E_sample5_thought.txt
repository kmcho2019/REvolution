Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Parallel Prefix Comparator
1. Instead of traditional hierarchical or subtraction-based comparison, implement a parallel prefix network that propagates comparison results across all bits simultaneously.
2. Use a prefix computation structure similar to carry-lookahead adders, but adapted for comparison operations.
3. Key features:
   - Each bit position generates local greater/less signals
   - Prefix network combines these signals in logarithmic depth
   - Early propagation of comparison decisions
   - Optimized for both power and area through shared intermediate computations
4. Advantages:
   - More balanced logic depth than hierarchical approaches
   - Better scalability for wider comparators
   - Efficient sharing of intermediate results
   - Natural mutual exclusion of outputs
