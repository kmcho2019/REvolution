Refactoring Strategy:
1. Replace the always block with continuous assignments (assign statements) for purely combinational logic
2. Compute intermediate comparison signals first, then derive outputs from them
3. Maintain the same functionality and mutual exclusivity of outputs
4. Use bitwise operations for equality check and magnitude comparison
5. Structure the logic to clearly show the priority of comparisons (equality first)

Key Changes:
- Use assign statements instead of procedural blocks
- Create intermediate signals for equality and greater-than comparisons
- Derive outputs directly from these signals
- The structure will be more parallel and potentially more efficient for synthesis
