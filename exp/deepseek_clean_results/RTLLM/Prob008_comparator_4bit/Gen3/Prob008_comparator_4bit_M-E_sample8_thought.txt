A novel approach using hierarchical bit-wise comparison with priority encoding:
1. Compare bits from MSB to LSB (priority order)
2. For each bit position, generate 3 signals: greater, equal, less
3. Use priority encoding to determine final output - the first non-equal bit determines the result
4. This mimics how humans compare numbers digit by digit from left to right
5. Benefits: 
   - Early termination potential (don't need to compare lower bits if MSB differs)
   - Clear critical path (MSB comparison is most critical)
   - Modular design that scales well to larger bit widths
