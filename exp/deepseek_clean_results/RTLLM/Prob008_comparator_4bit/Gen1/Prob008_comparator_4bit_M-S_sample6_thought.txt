To simplify the 4-bit comparator while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Eliminate the full subtraction operation since we only need comparison results
2. Compare bits from MSB to LSB, stopping at the first unequal pair
3. Use bitwise equality checks and priority encoding for the comparison
4. Generate outputs directly from the comparison results without intermediate subtraction
This approach reduces logic complexity by:
- Removing the subtractor and borrow propagation
- Using simple AND/OR gates for comparison
- Maintaining mutually exclusive outputs
