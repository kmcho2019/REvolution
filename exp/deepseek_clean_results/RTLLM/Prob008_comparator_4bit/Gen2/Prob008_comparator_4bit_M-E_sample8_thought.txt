Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical bit-slice comparison technique:
1. Break down the 4-bit comparison into individual bit comparisons starting from MSB
2. Use a priority encoder approach where higher bits override lower bits
3. Implement a cascading structure where each bit stage generates partial results
4. Propagate comparison state through the bit slices
5. Final outputs are determined by the highest priority non-equal bit pair

Advantages:
- Early termination potential (can determine result before checking all bits)
- Clear hierarchical structure that maps well to physical layout
- Naturally maintains mutual exclusivity of outputs
- Efficient for synthesis as it avoids arithmetic operations
