A novel approach to implement the 4-bit comparator is to use a hierarchical bit-sliced comparison technique. Instead of a direct comparison, we'll:
1. Break the comparison into individual bit slices (MSB to LSB)
2. For each bit position, generate local greater/equal/less signals
3. Combine these local signals in a priority encoder fashion where higher bits dominate
4. Use a final decision logic that considers the most significant non-equal bit position
This method provides better scalability for wider comparators and clearer timing paths.
