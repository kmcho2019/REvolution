Here's a novel approach using bit-sliced comparison with priority encoding:
1. Compare bits from MSB to LSB (priority order)
2. Use a hierarchical comparison structure where higher bits dominate
3. Implement with parallel generate blocks for each bit position
4. Use a priority encoder to select the final result
5. This mimics how humans compare numbers (left-to-right digit comparison)

Advantages:
- Early termination potential (though synthesis may not exploit this)
- Clear hierarchical structure that's easy to understand
- Naturally handles the priority of more significant bits
- No arithmetic operations needed (pure logical comparison)
