Refactoring Strategy:
1. Replace the hierarchical bitwise comparison with a more straightforward subtraction-based approach
2. Use continuous assignments (assign) for all outputs instead of intermediate wires
3. Simplify the logic by directly computing the subtraction result and analyzing its sign
4. Maintain mutual exclusivity of outputs through careful Boolean expressions
5. Optimize power by minimizing switching activity through direct computation

Key Changes:
- Subtraction result is computed once and reused
- Output logic is simplified by deriving all conditions from the subtraction
- More readable structure while maintaining similar PPA characteristics
- Eliminates redundant XOR operations from previous implementation
