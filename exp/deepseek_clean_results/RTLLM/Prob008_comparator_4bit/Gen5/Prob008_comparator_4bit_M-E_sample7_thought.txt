Novel Architecture: Carry Lookahead Comparator
1. Concept: Adapt carry lookahead adder principles for comparison
2. Key Innovation: 
   - Generate propagate (P) and generate (G) signals for comparison
   - P indicates bit equality (propagates comparison to next bit)
   - G indicates bit-wise greater condition (generates final result)
3. Benefits:
   - Constant time comparison (O(1) critical path)
   - Efficient shared intermediate terms
   - Naturally produces mutually exclusive outputs
4. Implementation:
   - Compute P and G for each bit position
   - Combine using carry lookahead structure
   - Final outputs derived from combined terms
