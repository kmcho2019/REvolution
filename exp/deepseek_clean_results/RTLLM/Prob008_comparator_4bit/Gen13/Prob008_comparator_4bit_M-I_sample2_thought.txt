Optimization strategy:
1. Replace complex priority encoder with direct arithmetic comparison for better PPA:
   - Use simple subtraction (A-B) and examine the sign bit (MSB) of result
   - This reduces logic depth and eliminates redundant bit-wise comparisons
2. Simplify output logic:
   - A_equal can be directly computed as (A == B)
   - A_less is simply the borrow from subtraction (sign bit)
   - A_greater is when not equal and not less
3. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic depth (critical path now just one subtraction)
   - Lower power from eliminating intermediate signals
   - Smaller area by leveraging synthesis tool optimizations
   - Maintains mutually exclusive outputs
