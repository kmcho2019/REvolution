Simplified approach using direct arithmetic comparison:
1. Instead of bit-wise comparison, leverage Verilog's built-in comparison operators
2. These operators will be synthesized into efficient combinational logic
3. The outputs are mutually exclusive by definition (a number can't be both greater and less than another)
4. Benefits:
   - Much simpler and cleaner code
   - Synthesis tools can optimize the implementation automatically
   - Reduced logic depth compared to manual bit-wise comparison
   - Easier to understand and maintain
5. The implementation will be functionally identical but more efficient in terms of PPA
