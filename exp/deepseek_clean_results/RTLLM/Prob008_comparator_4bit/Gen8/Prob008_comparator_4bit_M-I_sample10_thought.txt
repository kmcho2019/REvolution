Optimization strategy based on PPA feedback:
1. Replace complex priority encoder with direct arithmetic comparison for better timing and area
2. Use simple magnitude comparison operators (>, ==, <) to let synthesis tool optimize
3. Eliminate redundant bit-wise operations to reduce power consumption
4. Maintain mutually exclusive outputs through careful signal generation
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic depth (better timing)
   - Fewer gates (better area)
   - Less switching activity (better power)
   - Simpler and more readable code
   - Synthesis tool can better optimize the standard operators
