Simplified Design Idea: Parallel Comparison with Single-Level Logic

This simplified design:
1. Uses parallel comparison for all bits simultaneously
2. Computes equality and greater-than signals in one step
3. Eliminates intermediate hierarchical comparison wires
4. Uses basic Boolean operations for minimal logic
5. Maintains mutual exclusivity through direct derivation of outputs

Key Simplifications:
- Single 4-input AND gate for equality check (A_equal)
- Bitwise greater-than signals OR'ed together (A_greater)
- A_less derived as neither equal nor greater
- No early termination logic (simpler but slightly less power efficient)
- Reduced wire count and logic levels
