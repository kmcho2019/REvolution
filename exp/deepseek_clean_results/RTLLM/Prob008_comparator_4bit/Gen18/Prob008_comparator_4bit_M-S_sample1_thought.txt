Simplified Strategy: Direct Bitwise Comparison with Priority Encoding

1. Key Simplifications:
   - Remove intermediate wire arrays (bit_eq, bit_gt) to reduce area
   - Use direct bitwise comparison with priority encoding for greater/less detection
   - Simplify mutual exclusivity by deriving A_less directly from other outputs

2. Architectural Changes:
   - Single parallel equality check (AND of XNORs)
   - MSB-first comparison with simple OR reduction for greater detection
   - Eliminate redundant logic by deriving A_less as !(A_greater OR A_equal)

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced logic levels (faster critical path)
   - Fewer intermediate signals (lower area)
   - Maintains correct functionality and mutual exclusivity
   - Clearer and more maintainable code
