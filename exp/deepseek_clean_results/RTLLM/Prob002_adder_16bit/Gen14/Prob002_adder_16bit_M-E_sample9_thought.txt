Novel Hybrid Architecture:
1. Hierarchical Division: Split the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit blocks for better granularity.
2. Carry-Select Optimization: Implement the upper 12 bits (3 blocks) using carry-select to parallelize carry propagation.
3. Ripple-Carry Base: Keep the lowest 4-bit block as ripple-carry for simplicity and area efficiency.
4. Carry Prediction: Each 4-bit block generates both possible sums (with carry=0 and carry=1) then selects the correct path.
5. Balanced PPA: This hybrid approach reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry while avoiding the area overhead of full carry-lookahead.

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (O(n/4) instead of O(n))
- More area-efficient than full carry-select
- Modular design allows easy parameterization
- Balanced power consumption
