Novel Architecture: Carry-Select Adder with Bit-Sliced Optimization
1. Divide the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit slices for better timing
2. Implement carry-select logic to parallelize carry propagation
3. Use multiplexers to select between precomputed sums with carry=0 and carry=1
4. Optimize power by gating unused carry paths
5. Maintain modularity with parameterized bit-widths for reusability

Key Advantages:
- Faster than ripple-carry by parallelizing carry computation
- Balanced critical path across all bits
- Power efficient through conditional carry computation
- Scalable architecture for different bit-widths
- Better timing closure potential
