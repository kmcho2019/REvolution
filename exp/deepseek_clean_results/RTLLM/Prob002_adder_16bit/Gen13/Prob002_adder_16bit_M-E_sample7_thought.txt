Innovative Hybrid Adder Architecture:
1. Parallel Prefix Concept: Split the 16-bit adder into 4-bit blocks for partial parallel processing.
2. Carry-Select Optimization: Use carry-select for the upper 12 bits (3x4bit blocks) while keeping the lower 4 bits as ripple-carry.
3. Early Carry Resolution: Compute both possible carry chains (0 and 1) for middle blocks in parallel.
4. Resource Sharing: Reuse common logic between carry chains to minimize area overhead.
5. Balanced Design: Achieves better timing than pure ripple-carry while using less area than full carry-select.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
- Lower area than full carry-select implementation
- Scalable to wider bit widths
- Maintains combinational logic purity
