To simplify the 16-bit adder implementation while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Eliminate the intermediate 8-bit adder module since it's just a wrapper around 1-bit adders
2. Directly instantiate 16 1-bit full adders in the 16-bit adder module
3. Use a carry chain where each adder passes its carry-out to the next adder's carry-in
4. This reduces hierarchy levels while keeping the same ripple-carry functionality
5. The implementation becomes more straightforward and easier to understand
