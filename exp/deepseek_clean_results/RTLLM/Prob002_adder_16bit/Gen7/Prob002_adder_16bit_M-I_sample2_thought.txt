To optimize the 16-bit adder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure at the 8-bit level. This approach:
1. Reduces carry propagation delay by generating carry signals in parallel
2. Maintains the same hierarchical 16-bit structure but with faster 8-bit blocks
3. Uses group propagate/generate logic to minimize critical path
4. Balances between complexity and performance gains
5. Still keeps the design combinational and synthesizable

Key improvements:
- Replace ripple-carry with carry-lookahead in 8-bit adders
- Use 4-bit lookahead units for better granularity
- Optimize group propagate/generate logic
- Maintain clean hierarchy for better synthesis
