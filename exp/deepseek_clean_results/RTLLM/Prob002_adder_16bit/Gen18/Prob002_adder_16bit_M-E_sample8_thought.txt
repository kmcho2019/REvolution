Novel Hybrid Carry-Select Adder Architecture:
1. Hierarchical Design: Split the 16-bit adder into two 8-bit blocks, but implement them differently for optimization.
2. Lower 8-bit: Use a standard ripple-carry adder for the LSBs where timing is less critical.
3. Upper 8-bit: Implement as a carry-select adder that computes both possible sums (for carry=0 and carry=1) in parallel.
4. Early Carry Resolution: The lower adder's carry-out selects between the pre-computed upper sums, reducing critical path delay.
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse the same 8-bit adder module for both ripple and select implementations to maintain modularity.

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
- Lower area than full carry-select implementation
- Maintains clean hierarchy and modularity
- Balanced performance and area tradeoff
