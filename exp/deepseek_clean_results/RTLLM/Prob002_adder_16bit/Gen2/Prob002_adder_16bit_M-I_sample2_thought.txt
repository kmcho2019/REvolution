To optimize the 16-bit adder, we'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure for the 8-bit submodules. This will significantly reduce the critical path delay compared to ripple-carry, while maintaining reasonable area overhead. The 16-bit adder will still use two 8-bit CLA blocks, but with faster carry propagation. We'll also optimize the logic expressions for better area efficiency.

Key improvements:
1. Replace ripple-carry with carry-lookahead in 8-bit adders
2. Optimize generate/propagate logic for better timing
3. Maintain hierarchical structure for modularity
4. Use more efficient Boolean expressions for carry generation
