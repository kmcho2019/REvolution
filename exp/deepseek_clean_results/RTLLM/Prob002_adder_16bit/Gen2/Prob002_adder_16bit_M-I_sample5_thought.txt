To optimize the 16-bit adder, we'll implement a carry-lookahead architecture for the 8-bit submodules. This will significantly reduce the critical path delay compared to ripple-carry, while maintaining reasonable area and power consumption. The improvement strategy includes:

1. Replace ripple-carry 8-bit adders with carry-lookahead adders (CLAs)
2. Maintain the hierarchical 16-bit structure but with faster 8-bit blocks
3. Use generate blocks for scalable implementation
4. Optimize carry propagation between the two 8-bit blocks

The CLA approach calculates carry signals in parallel, reducing the longest path from O(n) to O(log n) for n-bit adders. For 8-bit blocks, this means carry propagation delay is reduced from 8 gate delays to approximately 3-4 gate delays.
