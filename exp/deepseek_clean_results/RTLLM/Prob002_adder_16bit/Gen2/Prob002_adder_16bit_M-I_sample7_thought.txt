To optimize the 16-bit adder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure with 4-bit blocks. This approach:
1. Reduces critical path delay by computing carry signals in parallel
2. Maintains reasonable area overhead compared to ripple-carry
3. Reduces power consumption by minimizing unnecessary switching in carry chain
4. Uses hierarchical design with 4-bit CLA blocks for better modularity
5. Implements generate/propagate logic to compute carries more efficiently
