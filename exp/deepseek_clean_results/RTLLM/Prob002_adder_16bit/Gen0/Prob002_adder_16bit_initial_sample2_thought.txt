To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical design approach:
1. First create an 8-bit full adder module (adder_8bit) that takes two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out
2. Then instantiate two of these 8-bit adders in the 16-bit adder (adder_16bit):
   - First adder handles bits 0-7 with the external carry-in
   - Second adder handles bits 8-15 with the carry-out from the first adder
3. The final carry-out comes from the second 8-bit adder
This approach provides modularity and reusability of the 8-bit adder component.
