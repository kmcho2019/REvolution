To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical approach by first designing an 8-bit adder module, then instantiating it twice in the 16-bit adder module. The 16-bit adder will split the input into two 8-bit segments, with the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder feeding into the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This approach provides modularity and reusability of the 8-bit adder component.
