To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical approach by breaking it down into smaller 8-bit adders. This promotes modularity and reusability. The 16-bit adder will consist of two 8-bit adders connected in series, where the carry-out of the first 8-bit adder becomes the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder. The final carry-out comes from the second 8-bit adder.
