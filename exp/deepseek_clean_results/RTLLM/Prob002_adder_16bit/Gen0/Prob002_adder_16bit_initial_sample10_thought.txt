To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical approach by creating an 8-bit adder module first, then instantiating it twice in the 16-bit adder module. The 8-bit adder will handle the lower and upper bytes separately, with the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder feeding into the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. This approach efficiently reuses the 8-bit adder logic while maintaining clean design hierarchy.
