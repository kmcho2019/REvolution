To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical design approach. First, we'll create an 8-bit full adder module that can be instantiated twice to form the complete 16-bit adder. The 8-bit adder will itself be built using 1-bit full adders. 

The 16-bit adder will consist of:
1. A lower 8-bit adder (bits 0-7) that takes Cin as its carry-in
2. An upper 8-bit adder (bits 8-15) that takes the carry-out from the lower adder as its carry-in
3. The final carry-out comes from the upper 8-bit adder
