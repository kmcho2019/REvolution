To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical approach by breaking it down into smaller 8-bit adders. This improves modularity and reusability. The 16-bit adder will be constructed by cascading two 8-bit adders, where the carry-out of the first adder becomes the carry-in of the second adder. Each 8-bit adder will be implemented using a simple ripple-carry approach for clarity.
