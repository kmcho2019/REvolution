To implement a 16-bit full adder, we'll use a hierarchical design approach:
1. First create a basic 8-bit adder module (adder_8bit) that takes two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produces an 8-bit sum and carry-out
2. Then in the top-level adder_16bit module, we'll:
   - Split the 16-bit inputs into two 8-bit segments (lower and upper)
   - Use the first 8-bit adder for the lower bits (bits 7:0)
   - Use the second 8-bit adder for the upper bits (bits 15:8), with its carry-in connected to the carry-out of the first adder
   - Concatenate the two 8-bit sums to form the 16-bit output
   - The final carry-out comes from the second 8-bit adder
