Novel Hybrid Carry-Select Adder Architecture:
1. Hierarchical Design: Break the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit blocks for better carry propagation
2. Carry-Select Optimization: Implement carry-select for the upper 12 bits while keeping the lower 4 bits as ripple-carry
3. Early Carry Resolution: Compute both possible carry paths (0 and 1) in parallel for faster operation
4. Resource Sharing: Reuse common logic between carry paths to minimize area overhead
5. Balanced Critical Path: The 4-bit ripple sections keep the carry chain manageable while still providing speedup

Key Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced from 16 to 4 gate delays)
- More area-efficient than full carry-select
- Maintains clean hierarchy and modularity
- Scales well to wider bit widths
