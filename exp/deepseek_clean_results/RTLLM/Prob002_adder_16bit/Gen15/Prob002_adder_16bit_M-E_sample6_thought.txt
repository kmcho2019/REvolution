Novel Hybrid Architecture:
1. Divide the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit carry-select blocks
2. Each 4-bit block uses carry-lookahead internally for fast propagation
3. Implement carry-select by computing both possible sums (Cin=0/1) in parallel
4. Use multiplexers to select correct sums based on actual carry-in
5. Benefits:
   - Combines advantages of carry-select (parallel computation) and carry-lookahead (fast local propagation)
   - Reduces critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - Maintains reasonable area overhead compared to pure carry-lookahead
   - Scales better than pure carry-select for wider adders
