To implement a 16-bit full adder with improved performance, we'll use a carry-select adder architecture. This approach computes both possible outcomes (with carry-in 0 and 1) in parallel for the upper bits, then selects the correct result once the actual carry from the lower bits is known. This reduces the critical path compared to a simple ripple-carry adder. We'll split the 16-bit adder into two 8-bit sections, where the lower 8 bits use a ripple-carry adder and the upper 8 bits use the carry-select approach.
