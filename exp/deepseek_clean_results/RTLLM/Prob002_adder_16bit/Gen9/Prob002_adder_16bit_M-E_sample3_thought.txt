To implement a 16-bit full adder with improved performance, we'll use a carry-select adder architecture. This approach computes both possible sums (with carry-in 0 and 1) in parallel for the upper 8 bits, then selects the correct result once the carry-out from the lower 8 bits is known. This reduces the critical path delay compared to a simple ripple-carry adder while maintaining reasonable area overhead. The lower 8 bits will use a standard ripple-carry adder to generate the initial carry-out.
