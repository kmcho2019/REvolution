Innovative Strategy:
1. Hybrid Architecture: Combine ripple-carry and carry-select techniques for balanced performance and area.
2. Segmented Calculation: Split into 4-bit blocks for better carry propagation control.
3. Conditional Carry: Pre-compute both carry possibilities (0 and 1) for upper blocks.
4. Multiplexer Optimization: Use shared logic for carry selection to reduce area.
5. Bit-wise Parallelism: Process all 4-bit segments in parallel for improved timing.

Key Advantages:
- Better critical path than pure ripple-carry (O(n/4) instead of O(n))
- Lower area overhead than full carry-select
- Balanced PPA trade-off
- Scalable to wider adders with consistent benefits
