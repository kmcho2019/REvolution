Improvement Strategy:
1. Carry-Lookahead Optimization: Replace ripple-carry with carry-lookahead to reduce critical path delay from O(n) to O(log n).
2. Hierarchical CLA: Implement 4-bit CLA blocks within the 8-bit adder for better granularity and timing.
3. Group Propagation: Use group propagate/generate signals to accelerate carry computation across bit positions.
4. Balanced Logic: Structure the CLA logic to balance fanout and minimize gate delays.
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse intermediate propagate/generate signals for both carry and sum computation.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced critical path delay through parallel carry computation
- Maintained modularity while improving performance
- Better scalability for wider adders
- Improved timing without significant area/power overhead
