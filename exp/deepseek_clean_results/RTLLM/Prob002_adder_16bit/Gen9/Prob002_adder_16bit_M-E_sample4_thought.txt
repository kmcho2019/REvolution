I'll implement a hybrid carry-select adder architecture that combines the benefits of ripple-carry and carry-select approaches. This design:
1. Divides the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit blocks
2. Uses ripple-carry within each 4-bit block for simplicity
3. Implements carry-select between blocks for faster propagation
4. Reduces hardware compared to pure carry-select by sharing common sum logic
5. Optimizes the critical path by pre-computing both possible sums (carry=0 and carry=1) for upper blocks

Key advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry due to parallel carry computation
- More area-efficient than full carry-select
- Balanced delay across all bit positions
- Scalable to wider bit widths
