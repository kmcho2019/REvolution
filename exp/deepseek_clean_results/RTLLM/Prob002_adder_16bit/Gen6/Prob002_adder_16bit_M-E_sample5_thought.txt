I'll implement a carry-select adder architecture for the 16-bit adder, which offers better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead. The key ideas are:
1. Split the 16-bit adder into 4-bit chunks for better trade-off between speed and area
2. For each 4-bit chunk (except the first), compute both possible sums (with carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use multiplexers to select the correct sum once the actual carry-in is known
4. This provides parallel computation of sums while keeping carry propagation local to 4-bit blocks
5. The first 4-bit adder remains a simple ripple-carry to generate the initial carry

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced)
- More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
- Regular structure that scales well to wider adders
- Predictable timing characteristics
