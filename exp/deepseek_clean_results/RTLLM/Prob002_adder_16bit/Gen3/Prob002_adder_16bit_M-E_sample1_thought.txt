I'll implement a 16-bit adder using a hybrid architecture that combines the benefits of carry-select and carry-lookahead techniques. The design splits the 16-bit adder into four 4-bit blocks. Each 4-bit block uses carry-lookahead internally for fast carry generation. Between blocks, we use carry-select to speculatively compute both possible carry scenarios (0 and 1), then select the correct result when the actual carry is known. This provides better timing than pure ripple-carry while being more area-efficient than full carry-lookahead.
