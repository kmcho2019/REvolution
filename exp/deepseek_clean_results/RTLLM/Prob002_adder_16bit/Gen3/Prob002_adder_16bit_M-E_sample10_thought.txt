To implement a 16-bit full adder with improved performance, we'll use a carry-select adder architecture. This approach computes both possible outcomes (with carry-in 0 and 1) in parallel for the upper bits, then selects the correct result once the actual carry is known from the lower bits. This reduces the critical path compared to ripple-carry adders. We'll implement this using two 8-bit ripple-carry adders for the lower bits and two 8-bit adders for the upper bits (computing both carry possibilities), plus a multiplexer for selection.
