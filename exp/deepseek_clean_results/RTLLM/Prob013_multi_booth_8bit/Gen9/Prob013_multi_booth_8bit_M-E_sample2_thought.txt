Novel Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier with Early Termination:

1. **Dual-Stage Pipeline Architecture**:
   - Separation of Booth encoding (stage 1) and accumulation (stage 2)
   - Allows parallel processing of current and next operations

2. **Dynamic Bit-Width Processing**:
   - Adaptive processing based on leading zeros detection
   - Early termination when remaining bits are all 0 or 1

3. **Hybrid Radix-2/Radix-4 Operation**:
   - Default Radix-4 operation with automatic fallback to Radix-2
   - When remaining bits < 2, switches to single-bit processing

4. **Predictive Shifting**:
   - Pre-calculates required shifts for next cycle
   - Eliminates one clock cycle delay in critical path

5. **Smart Ready Signal Generation**:
   - Early rdy signal when product stabilizes
   - Continuous monitoring of result convergence
