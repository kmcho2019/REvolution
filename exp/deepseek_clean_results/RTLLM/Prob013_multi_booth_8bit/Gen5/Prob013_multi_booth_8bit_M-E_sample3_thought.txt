My novel approach implements a fully pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with parallel partial product generation and carry-save addition. Key innovations:

1. **Parallel Booth Encoding**: Generate all possible partial products (0, ±A, ±2A) in parallel at start
2. **4-stage Pipeline**: 
   - Stage 1: Sign extension and Booth encoding
   - Stage 2: Partial product selection
   - Stage 3: Carry-save addition tree
   - Stage 4: Final addition and output
3. **Early Ready Signal**: Predict completion time based on pipeline depth
4. **Resource Sharing**: Reuse adders between pipeline stages
5. **Bypass Logic**: Handle back-to-back multiplications efficiently

This design achieves higher throughput (one multiplication every 4 cycles) while maintaining correct Booth algorithm implementation and providing precise ready signaling.
