My novel approach implements a parallel Radix-4 Booth multiplier with these key features:
1. **Parallel Partial Product Generation**: All possible partial products (0, ±A, ±2A) are precomputed simultaneously
2. **Pipelined Selection**: Booth encoding selects from precomputed values in a single clock cycle
3. **Accumulation Tree**: Uses a 4-stage adder tree for efficient partial product accumulation
4. **Early Completion Detection**: Monitors multiplier bits to potentially finish early for sparse inputs
5. **Signed Extension Handling**: Properly maintains sign extension throughout all operations

The design processes 2 bits per cycle (Radix-4) and completes in a fixed 5 cycles (1 initialization + 4 processing). The parallel computation reduces critical path delay compared to sequential approaches.
