My novel approach implements a pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with parallel partial product generation and a Wallace tree reduction stage. Key innovations:

1. **Pipelined Architecture**:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Wallace tree compression
   - Stage 3: Final carry-propagate addition
   - Enables higher clock speeds and throughput

2. **Parallel Partial Products**:
   - Generate all 4 partial products simultaneously
   - Use multiplexers to select based on Booth encoding
   - Proper sign extension handling for negative multiples

3. **Optimized Wallace Tree**:
   - 4:2 compressors for efficient partial product reduction
   - Balanced tree structure for minimal critical path
   - Registered intermediate results for pipelining

4. **Smart Control Logic**:
   - Single-cycle multiplication (after pipeline fill)
   - Valid signal indicates when output is ready
   - Clean reset behavior with pipeline flush

This design achieves better performance than sequential implementations while maintaining area efficiency through careful compressor optimization.
