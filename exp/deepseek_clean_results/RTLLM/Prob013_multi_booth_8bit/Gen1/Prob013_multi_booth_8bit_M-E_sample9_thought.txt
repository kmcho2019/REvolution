Here's a novel approach using a pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with parallel partial product generation:

1. **Parallel Booth Encoding**: Instead of sequential processing, we'll generate all Booth encodings in parallel for the 8-bit multiplier, creating 4 partial products simultaneously.

2. **Wallace Tree Reduction**: Use a Wallace tree structure to efficiently sum the partial products in logarithmic time rather than sequential accumulation.

3. **Two-Stage Pipeline**: 
   - Stage 1: Generate all partial products in parallel
   - Stage 2: Reduce partial products using Wallace tree and final addition

4. **Optimized Control**: 
   - Only 2 clock cycles needed (1 for generation, 1 for reduction)
   - Ready signal asserts after pipeline completes
   - Reset initializes all registers

This approach significantly improves performance by leveraging parallelism while maintaining the Radix-4 Booth algorithm's benefits.
