Here's a novel pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier design that improves throughput by processing in stages:

1. **Three-Stage Pipeline**: 
   - Stage 1: Sign extension and initialization
   - Stage 2: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 3: Accumulation and completion

2. **Optimized Booth Encoding**:
   - Process 3 bits (current group + previous LSB) for correct Radix-4 encoding
   - Generate control signals for ±A, ±2A operations

3. **Parallel Partial Products**:
   - Compute all possible partial products in advance
   - Select appropriate version based on Booth encoding

4. **Early Ready Signal**:
   - Predict completion one cycle early
   - Final result available immediately when rdy asserts

This design achieves better timing and throughput while maintaining correct Booth algorithm implementation.
