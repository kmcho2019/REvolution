My novel approach implements a pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with these key innovations:

1. Three-stage pipeline architecture:
   - Stage 1: Operand preparation and sign extension
   - Stage 2: Parallel Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 3: Accumulation and final result computation

2. Optimized Booth encoding:
   - Uses combinational logic for immediate 2-bit group encoding
   - Generates all possible partial products in parallel

3. Efficient accumulation:
   - Uses carry-save adder structure for fast partial product summation
   - Final adder only activates in last stage

4. Smart control logic:
   - Pipeline stages synchronized with counter
   - Early termination detection for negative numbers

5. Resource sharing:
   - Reuses arithmetic units between pipeline stages
   - Minimizes register usage through careful scheduling

This architecture achieves higher throughput than conventional sequential designs while maintaining area efficiency.
