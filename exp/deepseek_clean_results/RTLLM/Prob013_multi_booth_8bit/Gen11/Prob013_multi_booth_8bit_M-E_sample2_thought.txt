Here's a novel pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier architecture with these key innovations:

1. Three-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Operand preparation and sign extension
   - Stage 2: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 3: Accumulation and final result computation

2. Parallel Booth Encoders:
   - Four parallel Booth encoders process 2-bit groups simultaneously
   - Each encoder handles a different bit pair of the multiplier

3. Shift-Add Tree:
   - Partial products are properly shifted before addition
   - Carry-save adders reduce critical path delay

4. Early Ready Signal:
   - Ready signal asserts after fixed pipeline latency
   - No need for completion detection logic

5. Optimized Datapath:
   - Minimal register usage between stages
   - Balanced pipeline stages for maximum clock speed

This architecture achieves higher throughput than sequential implementations while maintaining area efficiency.
