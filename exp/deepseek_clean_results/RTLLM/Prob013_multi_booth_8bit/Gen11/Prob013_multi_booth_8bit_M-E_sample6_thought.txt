My novel approach implements a pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with parallel partial product generation and a Wallace tree reduction stage. Key innovations:

1. Pipelined Architecture:
   - Split into 3 stages: Booth encoding, partial product generation, and Wallace tree reduction
   - Enables higher clock speeds by reducing critical path

2. Parallel Processing:
   - Generate all 4 partial products simultaneously in stage 2
   - Use multiplexers to select ±M, ±2M based on Booth encoding

3. Optimized Wallace Tree:
   - 4:2 compressor based reduction for efficient partial product summation
   - Final carry-propagate adder for clean result

4. Smart Control Logic:
   - Single-cycle multiplication when not pipelined
   - Proper handling of sign extension and overflow

This design achieves better performance than traditional sequential implementations while maintaining area efficiency.
