Novel Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier Architecture:

1. Key Innovation:
   - Two-stage pipeline design separating Booth encoding and accumulation
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Accumulation with carry-save adder optimization

2. Architectural Improvements:
   - Parallel Booth encoders for all 4 possible positions
   - Carry-save adder tree for efficient partial product accumulation
   - Early termination detection for power savings

3. Performance Enhancements:
   - Reduced critical path through pipelining
   - Parallel partial product generation
   - Optimized carry propagation with carry-save technique

4. Power Optimization:
   - Clock gating for inactive pipeline stages
   - Dynamic operand isolation
   - Zero detection for early completion

5. Area Efficiency:
   - Shared Booth encoder logic
   - Optimized adder tree structure
   - Minimal register overhead for pipelining
