Novel Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier with Early Termination:

1. Architectural Innovation:
   - 2-stage pipeline: Booth encoding (stage1) and accumulation (stage2)
   - Early termination detection when remaining bits are all 0 or 1
   - Dynamic stage control to skip unnecessary cycles

2. Key Features:
   - Parallel Booth encoding for current and next 2-bit pairs
   - Sign prediction logic to detect completion before full 4 cycles
   - Pipeline registers with bypass for single-cycle operations

3. Advantages:
   - Average-case performance improvement through early termination
   - Maintains throughput of 1 multiply per 4 cycles in worst case
   - Reduced power consumption from skipped cycles
   - Clear separation of pipeline stages for better timing

4. Implementation Details:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and shift amount calculation
   - Stage 2: Multiplicand shifting and product accumulation
   - Early termination when multiplier bits become uniform (all 0 or 1)
   - Bypass path for single-cycle operations when possible
