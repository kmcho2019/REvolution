Simplified Radix-4 Booth Multiplier Design:

1. **Core Operation**:
   - Maintain 16-bit registers for both multiplicand and multiplier with proper sign extension
   - Process 2 bits per cycle (4 cycles total for 8-bit multiplication)
   - Use a 2-bit counter (since we only need 4 cycles)

2. **Simplified Datapath**:
   - Single product register (p) accumulates results
   - Multiplicand remains constant during operation
   - Multiplier shifts right by 2 bits each cycle

3. **Booth Encoding**:
   - 3-bit window (current 2 bits + previous LSB)
   - Simple case statement handles all 8 possible encodings
   - Direct arithmetic operations (add/subtract multiplicand or 2×multiplicand)

4. **Control Logic**:
   - Reset initializes all registers
   - Ready signal asserts after 4 cycles
   - No complex state machine needed
