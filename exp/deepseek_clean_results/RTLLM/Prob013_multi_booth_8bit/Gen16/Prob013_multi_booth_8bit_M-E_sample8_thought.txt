Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier with Carry-Save Addition:

1. Novel Architecture:
   - 2-stage pipeline for better timing closure
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Carry-save adder tree for accumulation
   - Optimized for high-frequency operation

2. Key Innovations:
   - Parallel processing of all Booth encodings in first stage
   - Carry-save addition reduces critical path in accumulation
   - Early termination detection for constant multipliers
   - Dynamic shift adjustment based on Booth encoding

3. Advantages:
   - Higher clock frequency due to pipelining
   - Lower power from reduced switching activity
   - Smaller area from shared arithmetic resources
   - Maintains same interface and functionality
