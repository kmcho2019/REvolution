Novel Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier with Early Termination:

1. Architectural Innovation:
   - 2-stage pipeline: Booth encoding stage and accumulation stage
   - Early termination when multiplier becomes zero (for sparse inputs)
   - Parallel partial product generation with carry-save addition

2. Key Features:
   - Stage 1: Concurrently computes all possible partial products (x0, x1, x2, x3)
   - Stage 2: Selects and accumulates the correct partial product based on Booth encoding
   - Early termination logic detects when remaining shifts won't affect result

3. Advantages:
   - Higher throughput with pipelining (can accept new inputs every 2 cycles)
   - Power savings from early termination
   - Reduced critical path by separating encoding and accumulation
   - Maintains Radix-4 efficiency while improving performance
