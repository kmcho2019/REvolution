Novel Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier with Carry-Save Optimization:

1. Architectural Innovation:
   - Three-stage pipeline for better timing closure (Decode, Accumulate, Finalize)
   - Carry-save adder structure to break critical path
   - Early termination detection for power savings

2. Key Features:
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Carry-save accumulation of partial products
   - Stage 3: Final carry-propagate addition and result output
   - Dynamic clock gating when early termination detected

3. Performance Optimizations:
   - Balanced pipeline stages for maximum clock frequency
   - Carry-save reduces critical path in accumulation stage
   - Early termination saves power for small operands

4. Implementation Details:
   - Separate always blocks for each pipeline stage
   - Registered intermediate results between stages
   - Optimized Booth encoding with direct partial product selection
   - Configurable pipeline depth (easily extendable)
