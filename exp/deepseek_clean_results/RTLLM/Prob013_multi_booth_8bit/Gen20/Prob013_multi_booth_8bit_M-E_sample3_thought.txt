Novel Parallel-Pipelined Radix-4 Booth Multiplier Design:

1. Hybrid Parallel Processing:
   - Split 8-bit multiplication into two 4-bit parallel Radix-2 operations
   - Combine partial results with proper weighting
   - Reduces critical path by 50% compared to sequential approach

2. Dual-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Parallel Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Weighted accumulation of partial products
   - Enables single-cycle throughput after initial latency

3. Optimized Encoding Logic:
   - Shared Booth encoder for both parallel units
   - Common subexpression elimination in arithmetic paths
   - Early termination detection for zero operands

4. Advanced Ready Signaling:
   - Two-phase ready signal (rdy_pre and rdy_final)
   - Early indication when computation begins
   - Final valid signal when result is stable

Key Innovations:
- Parallel processing halves computation time
- Pipeline enables higher clock frequency
- Shared logic reduces area overhead
- Early ready signals improve system integration
