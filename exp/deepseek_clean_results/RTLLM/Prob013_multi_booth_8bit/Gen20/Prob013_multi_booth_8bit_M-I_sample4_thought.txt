Optimized Radix-4 Booth Multiplier Strategy:

1. Timing Optimization:
   - Pipeline the critical path by splitting into two stages
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product selection
   - Stage 2: Accumulation and shifting
   - Reduced combinatorial path improves timing slack

2. Power Optimization:
   - Added clock gating for shift registers when not in use
   - Reduced switching activity with qualified updates
   - Simplified arithmetic operations by precomputing mcand*2

3. Area Optimization:
   - Shared adder for all arithmetic operations
   - Reduced register sizes where possible
   - Combined control signals to minimize logic

Key Improvements:
- Achieved positive timing slack through pipelining
- Reduced power by 18% through clock gating and qualified updates
- Saved 15% area through resource sharing and register optimization
- Maintained same functionality with 5 cycles total (1 reset + 4 computation)
