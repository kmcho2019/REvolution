Here's a novel pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with parallel partial product generation and carry-save addition:

1. **Pipelined Architecture**:
   - 3-stage pipeline for higher throughput
   - Stage 1: Booth encoding and partial product generation
   - Stage 2: Carry-save addition of partial products
   - Stage 3: Final carry-propagate addition

2. **Parallel Partial Products**:
   - Generates all 4 partial products simultaneously
   - Uses multiplexers for Booth selection
   - Includes sign extension handling

3. **Optimized Addition**:
   - Uses carry-save adders to reduce critical path
   - Final stage uses ripple-carry adder for simplicity

4. **Control Logic**:
   - State machine manages pipeline stages
   - Handles reset and completion signaling
   - Includes pipeline stall logic

5. **Resource Sharing**:
   - Reuses adders between stages
   - Minimizes hardware footprint
