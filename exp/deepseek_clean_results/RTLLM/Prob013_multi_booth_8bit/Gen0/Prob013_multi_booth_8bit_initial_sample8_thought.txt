The Radix-4 Booth multiplier reduces the number of partial products by examining two bits of the multiplier at a time (plus one overlapping bit). Here's the design approach:
1. Sign-extend both inputs to 16 bits for proper handling of negative numbers
2. Use a 5-bit counter (0-15) to track 8 iterations (since we process 2 bits per iteration)
3. At each iteration:
   - Examine 3 bits of multiplier (b) to determine Booth encoding
   - Perform appropriate arithmetic shift and addition/subtraction
   - Shift multiplicand (a) left by 2 bits for next iteration
4. Set ready signal when counter reaches 8 (for 8 iterations)
5. Reset all registers when reset is high
