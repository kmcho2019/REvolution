The Radix-4 Booth multiplier reduces the number of partial products by examining 3 bits at a time (current bit and previous bit) to determine the operation (add, subtract, or shift). Here's the design approach:
1. Sign-extend both inputs to 16 bits for proper handling of signed numbers
2. Use a 5-bit counter (0-15) to track the 8 steps (since we process 2 bits at a time)
3. At each step, examine 3 bits of the multiplier (b) to determine the operation:
   - 000 or 111: no operation
   - 001 or 010: add multiplicand (a)
   - 011: add 2*multiplicand
   - 100: subtract 2*multiplicand
   - 101 or 110: subtract multiplicand
4. Accumulate the partial results with proper shifting
5. Set rdy when counter completes (reaches 8 for 8-bit inputs)
