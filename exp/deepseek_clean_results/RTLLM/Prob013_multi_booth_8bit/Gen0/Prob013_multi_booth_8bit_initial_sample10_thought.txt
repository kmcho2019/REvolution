The Radix-4 Booth multiplier is an efficient algorithm for signed multiplication. Here's the design approach:
1. Extend both inputs to 16-bit (sign extension) for proper signed arithmetic
2. Use a 5-bit counter (0-16) to control the multiplication steps
3. At each step, examine 3 bits of the multiplier (current and previous) to determine the Booth encoding
4. Perform the appropriate arithmetic operation based on the Booth encoding
5. Shift the multiplicand and multiplier appropriately
6. Set rdy signal when counter reaches 16 (completion)
7. Reset all registers when reset is high
