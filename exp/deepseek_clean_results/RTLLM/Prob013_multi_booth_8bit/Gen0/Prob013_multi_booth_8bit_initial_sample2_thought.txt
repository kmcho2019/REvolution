The Radix-4 Booth multiplier reduces the number of partial products by examining two bits of the multiplier at a time. Here's the design approach:
1. Sign-extend both inputs to 16 bits for proper handling of negative numbers
2. Use a 5-bit counter (0-15) to track 8 iterations (since we process 2 bits per iteration)
3. In each iteration:
   - Examine 3 bits of multiplier (current pair + previous LSB)
   - Perform appropriate operation based on Booth encoding (add/subtract multiplicand or do nothing)
   - Right shift the multiplier and arithmetic right shift the product
4. Set ready signal when counter reaches 16
5. Reset initializes all registers
