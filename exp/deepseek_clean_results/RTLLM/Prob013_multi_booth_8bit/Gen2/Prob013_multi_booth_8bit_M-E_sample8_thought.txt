Here's an innovative state-machine based Radix-4 Booth multiplier with optimized partial product selection:

1. **State Machine Architecture**:
   - IDLE: Waits for reset deassertion
   - INIT: Performs sign extension and initialization
   - COMPUTE: Processes each Radix-4 group
   - DONE: Signals completion

2. **Parallel Booth Encoding**:
   - Pre-computes all possible partial products (0, ±A, ±2A)
   - Uses multiplexer tree for fast selection

3. **Early Termination**:
   - Detects when remaining bits are all 0
   - Jumps to DONE state early

4. **Optimized Shifting**:
   - Uses arithmetic right shifts for sign extension
   - Maintains proper alignment throughout

5. **Cycle-Accurate Ready Signal**:
   - Precisely tracks completion
   - No false early assertions
