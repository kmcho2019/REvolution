Here's a simplified and corrected Radix-4 Booth multiplier design:

1. Maintain 16-bit registers for multiplicand and multiplier with proper sign extension
2. Use a 2-bit counter (since 8 bits / 2 bits per step = 4 steps)
3. At each clock cycle:
   - Examine 3 bits of multiplier (current pair + previous LSB)
   - Perform the appropriate Booth operation (0, ±A, ±2A)
   - Right shift multiplier by 2 bits (Radix-4)
   - Left shift multiplicand by 2 bits for next step
4. Complete in exactly 4 cycles (for 8-bit inputs)
5. Set ready signal when counter reaches 3 (since we count 0-3)

Key improvements:
- Reduced counter to 2 bits (0-3)
- Simplified case statement to only necessary operations
- Clear separation of reset and operation phases
- Direct use of multiplier[0] for previous bit
- Fixed shift operations for Radix-4
