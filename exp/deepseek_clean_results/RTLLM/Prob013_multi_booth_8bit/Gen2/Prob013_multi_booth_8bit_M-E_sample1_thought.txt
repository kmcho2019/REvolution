This novel architecture implements a 3-stage pipelined Radix-4 Booth multiplier with:
1. Parallel Booth Encoders: Processes all 4 bit-groups simultaneously in stage 1
2. Carry-Save Addition: Uses CSA trees for efficient partial product reduction
3. Pipelined Final Adder: Separates the critical path into stages
4. Early Ready Signal: Generates 'rdy' after fixed pipeline latency

Key innovations:
- Eliminates sequential iteration by processing all bit-groups in parallel
- Uses 4:2 compressors for partial product reduction
- Implements sign-extension handling in the encoder stage
- Pipelined design enables higher clock frequencies
- Fixed 3-cycle latency regardless of input size
