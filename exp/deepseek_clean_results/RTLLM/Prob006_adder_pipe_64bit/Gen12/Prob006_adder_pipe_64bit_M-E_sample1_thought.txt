Novel architectural approach: Carry-Save Pipeline Adder with Staggered Enable

Key innovations:
1. Three-stage pipeline with carry-save technique to reduce critical path
2. Staggered enable signals for each pipeline stage to ensure proper timing
3. Optimized 22-22-20 bit partitioning for balanced stage delays
4. Dedicated carry registers between stages for clean propagation
5. Explicit result composition stage for final sum calculation
6. Synchronized enable propagation with data flow

Advantages:
- Better timing due to carry-save technique
- Balanced pipeline stages
- Clear separation of partial sum and carry paths
- Robust enable signal synchronization
- Easier timing closure with explicit stage boundaries
