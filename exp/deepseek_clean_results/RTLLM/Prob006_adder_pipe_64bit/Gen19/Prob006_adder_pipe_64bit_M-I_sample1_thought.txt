Improvement Strategy:
1. Pipeline Optimization:
   - Reduce from 4 to 3 stages (22-bit chunks) for better PPA balance
   - Eliminate redundant pipeline registers for operands
   - Simplify carry propagation with direct chaining

2. Register Optimization:
   - Use single register arrays for more compact implementation
   - Combine sum and carry storage
   - Reduce total flip-flop count

3. Timing Improvement:
   - Balanced stage widths (22-21-21) for better clock speed
   - Early output calculation to reduce critical path

4. Power Optimization:
   - Gated clock enable for pipeline registers
   - Reduced switching activity through smarter staging

5. Area Reduction:
   - Eliminate separate operand pipelines
   - Shared enable logic
   - Compact carry handling
