Simplified Design Idea:
1. Key Improvements:
   - Reduce pipeline stages to 2 (32-bit chunks) for better balance between latency and throughput
   - Eliminate separate arrays for pipeline registers by using direct chaining
   - Simplify carry propagation with direct wiring between stages
   - Combine sum calculation and register updates in single always block
   - Streamline enable signal with simple 2-stage shift register

2. Maintained Functionality:
   - Full 64-bit addition with carry out (65-bit result)
   - Pipeline behavior with proper output enable timing
   - Reset and clock synchronization
   - Same interface and behavior as original

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced latency (2 cycles vs 4)
   - Fewer registers and simpler control logic
   - Clearer data flow with direct stage connections
   - More efficient synthesis while maintaining performance
   - Easier to understand and maintain
