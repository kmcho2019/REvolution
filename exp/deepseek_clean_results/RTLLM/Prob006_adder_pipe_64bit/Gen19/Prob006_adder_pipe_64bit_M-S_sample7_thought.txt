2. Basic ripple carry implementation without carry-save optimization
3. Simplified enable signal propagation
4. Direct register storage of inputs and results
5. Clear two-cycle latency (register inputs, then register outputs)

Advantages:

- Lower resource utilization
- Predictable timing (fixed 2-cycle latency)
- Still meets basic pipelining requirements
- Clear separation between combinational logic and registers
