Simplified Design Idea:
1. Basic Ripple Carry Adder with Pipeline:
   - Use a traditional 64-bit ripple carry adder structure
   - Split into 4 pipeline stages (16 bits per stage)
   - Each stage calculates partial sum and carry out
   - Pipeline registers store intermediate results and propagate carry
   - Enable signal is pipelined through shift register

2. Simplifications:
   - Remove speculative carry-select logic (use simple ripple carry)
   - Reduce number of pipeline registers (only store necessary intermediate values)
   - Simplify control logic (single enable shift register)
   - Straightforward carry propagation between stages

3. Benefits:
   - Smaller area than previous solution
   - Clear and simple implementation
   - Maintains pipelined throughput
   - Easier to verify and debug
   - Still meets functional requirements
