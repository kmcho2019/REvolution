Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Hybrid carry-select and ripple-carry structure for better performance
2. Three pipeline stages with uneven bit distribution (24-24-16) to balance critical paths
3. Carry prediction logic to reduce propagation delay
4. Early carry generation for the final stage
5. Optimized register placement for better timing closure
6. Dual-edge triggered pipeline registers for higher throughput

Key Features:
- First stage: 24-bit ripple-carry with carry prediction
- Second stage: 24-bit carry-select (using predicted carry)
- Final stage: 16-bit ripple-carry with early carry resolution
- Pipeline registers on both clock edges for 2x throughput
- Optimized enable signal propagation with clock gating awareness
