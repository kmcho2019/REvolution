Simplified design approach:
1. Use a single-stage pipeline instead of two stages to reduce complexity
2. Perform full 64-bit addition in one combinational step
3. Register the result and pipeline the enable signal
4. Maintain same interface and 3-cycle latency (input reg -> addition -> output reg)
5. Eliminate intermediate carry calculations by using direct 64-bit addition
6. Simplify control logic by tracking enable signal through pipeline stages
7. Keep reset functionality identical
