Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Hybrid Carry-Select with Speculative Execution:
   - Divide the 64-bit adder into 16-bit blocks (4 blocks total)
   - Each block computes two sums in parallel: one assuming carry-in=0 and one assuming carry-in=1
   - Use pipeline registers between blocks to store both speculative sums
   - Final carry selection happens in the next pipeline stage
   - Enable signal is pipelined synchronously with data

2. Key Innovations:
   - Speculative computation eliminates carry propagation delay between blocks
   - Carry selection becomes simple muxing in the next stage
   - Balanced pipeline stages (4 stages total)
   - Each stage has identical latency
   - Simple, regular structure with clear timing

3. Benefits:
   - Faster than pure ripple-carry (O(1) carry selection per block)
   - More area-efficient than full carry-select (only 4 blocks)
   - Pipeline naturally absorbs the carry-select mux delay
   - Easy to scale to different widths
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
