Improved 4-Stage Pipelined Adder with Balanced Timing:

Key Improvements:
1. Simplified carry propagation with pure pipeline approach (no carry-select)
2. Balanced 16-bit stages for better timing closure
3. Optimized register usage with direct chaining
4. Reduced logic complexity by using simple ripple-carry between stages
5. More efficient enable signal propagation
6. Clear separation of pipeline stages for better synthesis

Advantages:
- Simpler and more predictable timing
- Reduced area from eliminating carry-select logic
- Lower power consumption
- Easier to meet timing constraints
- Maintains same throughput with simpler implementation
