To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we'll divide the addition into smaller chunks (16 bits each) to create 4 pipeline stages. Each stage will:
1. Process a 16-bit segment of the 64-bit inputs
2. Propagate the carry to the next stage
3. Store intermediate results in registers
4. Synchronize the enable signal through pipeline registers

The output will be available after 4 clock cycles, with o_en indicating valid output.
