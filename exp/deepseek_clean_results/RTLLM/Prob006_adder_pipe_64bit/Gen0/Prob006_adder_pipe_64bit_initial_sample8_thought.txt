To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we'll break down the addition into smaller chunks (16 bits each) to create 4 pipeline stages. Each stage will:
1. Process a 16-bit segment of the addition
2. Propagate the carry to the next stage
3. Register intermediate results and control signals
4. Synchronize the output enable signal through the pipeline

The design will use registered inputs/outputs between stages to maintain proper pipeline timing. The output enable (o_en) will be delayed through the pipeline to match the result latency.
