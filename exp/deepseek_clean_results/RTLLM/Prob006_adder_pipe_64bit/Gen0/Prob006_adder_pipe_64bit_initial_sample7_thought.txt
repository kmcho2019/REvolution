To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we'll break down the addition into smaller chunks (16 bits each) to create 4 pipeline stages. Each stage will:
1. Process 16 bits of addition with carry-in from previous stage
2. Register the partial sum and carry-out for the next stage
3. Pipeline the enable signal to synchronize with the computation

The output will be available after 4 clock cycles, with o_en indicating valid output. The 65-bit result includes the final carry-out.
