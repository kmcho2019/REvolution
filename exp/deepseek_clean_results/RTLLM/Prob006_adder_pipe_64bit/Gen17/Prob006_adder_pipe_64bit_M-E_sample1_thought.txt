Novel 8-Stage Carry-Skip Pipelined Adder Architecture:

Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid carry-skip/ripple architecture reduces critical path
2. 8-stage pipeline with 8-bit chunks for better throughput
3. Carry-skip logic every 8 bits to accelerate propagation
4. Optimized register usage with shared intermediate results
5. Early carry-out prediction to reduce pipeline bubbles
6. Balanced stage delays for improved clock frequency
7. Power-aware design with clock-gating potential

Advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (skip logic helps long carries)
- More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
- Better timing closure with balanced 8-bit stages
- Lower latency than 4-stage design (more parallelism)
- Flexible for power/performance tradeoffs
