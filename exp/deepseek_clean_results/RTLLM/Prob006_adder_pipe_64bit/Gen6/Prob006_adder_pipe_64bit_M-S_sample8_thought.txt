To simplify the 64-bit pipelined adder while maintaining functionality:

1. Reduce pipeline stages from 4 to 3 for simpler implementation:
   - Stage 1: Input registration
   - Stage 2: Lower 32-bit addition
   - Stage 3: Upper 32-bit addition with carry

2. Remove carry-lookahead logic to reduce complexity:
   - Use simple ripple carry between stages
   - Still meets timing requirements with 3 stages

3. Simplify enable signal propagation:
   - Single pipeline for enable signals
   - Fewer intermediate registers

4. Cleaner result assembly:
   - Combine results in final stage
   - Clear output on reset

5. Maintain same interface and functionality:
   - 65-bit result with proper carry out
   - Synchronized o_en signal
