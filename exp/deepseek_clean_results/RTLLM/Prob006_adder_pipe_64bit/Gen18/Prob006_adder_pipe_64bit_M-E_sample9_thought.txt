Novel Architecture: Carry-Save Pipelined Adder with Early Result Formation

Key Innovations:
1. Carry-Save Technique: Instead of propagating carries between stages, we preserve both sum and carry bits at each stage, reducing critical path.

2. Two-Phase Pipeline:
   - Phase 1: Parallel partial sum generation (16-bit chunks)
   - Phase 2: Carry resolution and final sum formation

3. Early Result Formation:
   - Lower bits available immediately after Phase 1
   - Upper bits computed in Phase 2 with proper carry handling

4. Smart Enable Propagation:
   - Separate enable chains for each pipeline phase
   - Precise timing control for result validity

5. Balanced Pipeline:
   - Equal stage delays for better timing closure
   - Reduced register count compared to traditional ripple-carry

Advantages:
- Lower latency for lower bits
- Better timing characteristics
- More efficient resource utilization
- Clear separation of computation phases
