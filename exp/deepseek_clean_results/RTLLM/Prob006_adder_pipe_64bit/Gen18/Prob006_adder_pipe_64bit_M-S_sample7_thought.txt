Simplified Design Idea:
1. Key Simplifications:
   - Reduce pipeline stages to minimum (2 stages) while maintaining functionality
   - Use simple ripple carry adder structure without complex block segmentation
   - Simplify enable signal pipeline with just 2 flip-flops
   - Combine all combinational logic into single always block for clarity
   - Remove unnecessary intermediate registers

2. Benefits:
   - Reduced area and power consumption
   - Easier to understand and maintain
   - Still maintains pipelined operation
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
   - Lower latency (2 cycles vs original 4)
