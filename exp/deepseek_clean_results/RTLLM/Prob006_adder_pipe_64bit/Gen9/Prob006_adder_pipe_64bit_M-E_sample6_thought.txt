Novel Design Approach:
1. Implement a 4-stage pipelined 64-bit adder using hierarchical carry-lookahead architecture
2. Divide the 64-bit addition into 4 segments of 16 bits each
3. Generate propagate (P) and generate (G) signals in stage 1
4. Compute segment carry-lookahead in stage 2
5. Calculate intermediate sums in stage 3
6. Combine final results in stage 4
7. Use shift registers to pipeline the enable signal (i_en) through all stages
8. Implement proper pipeline registers between each stage to maintain timing
9. Output valid signal (o_en) synchronized with final stage completion

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path through hierarchical carry-lookahead
- Balanced pipeline stages for better throughput
- Clear separation of computation and pipeline control
- Easily scalable to different bit widths
