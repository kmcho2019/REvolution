Novel Architecture Idea: Hierarchical Carry-Skip with Balanced Pipeline Stages

This design introduces a 3-stage pipelined adder with:
1. Bit-sliced computation (8-bit slices) with carry-skip optimization
2. Two-level carry resolution (local and global)
3. Balanced pipeline stages for optimal throughput
4. Early carry termination detection

Key innovations:
- 8-bit ripple-carry slices for local computation
- Carry-skip logic between slices to accelerate propagation
- Hierarchical carry network (local within 16-bit blocks, global between)
- Pipeline registers placed to balance critical paths
- Early termination detection to save power on known results

Advantages:
- Better worst-case timing than pure ripple-carry
- Lower area than full carry-lookahead
- Balanced pipeline stages (3 cycles total)
- Power savings from early termination
- Scalable to wider adders
