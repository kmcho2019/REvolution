Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Hierarchical Carry-Skip Adder with Pipeline: 
   - Divide the 64-bit adder into 8-bit segments (8 segments total)
   - Implement carry-skip logic between segments to reduce worst-case delay
   - Add pipeline registers between segments for throughput optimization
   - Use a shift register for enable signal propagation

2. Key Innovations:
   - Combines carry-skip efficiency with pipelined throughput
   - Each segment computes its own carry-propagate (P) and carry-generate (G) signals
   - Skip logic determines when to bypass the ripple carry chain
   - Pipeline registers store intermediate sums and carry status
   - Output stage combines all segment results with proper timing

3. Benefits:
   - Better worst-case delay than pure ripple-carry
   - Maintains simple, regular structure
   - Balanced pipeline stages
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
