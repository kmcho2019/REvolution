Novel 4-Stage Carry-Select Pipelined Adder Architecture:

Key Innovations:
1. Hybrid carry-select and ripple-carry approach for better performance
2. 4-stage pipeline (16-bit chunks) for improved throughput
3. Parallel carry calculation for each stage
4. Early carry resolution reduces critical path
5. Optimized enable signal propagation through pipeline

Advantages:
- Better performance than pure ripple-carry
- More balanced pipeline stages
- Reduced worst-case delay through carry-select
- Maintains simple, understandable structure
- Efficient enable signal handling
