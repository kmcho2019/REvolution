Novel Architecture: Carry-Select Pipelined Adder
1. Divide the 64-bit adder into 4 pipeline stages (16 bits each)
2. Each stage uses carry-select technique to compute both possible sums (carry=0/1)
3. Actual carry from previous stage selects correct sum in current stage
4. Pipeline registers store intermediate results and propagate carries
5. Enable signal pipeline ensures result validity synchronization
6. Benefits:
   - Faster than pure ripple-carry (parallel carry computation)
   - Still maintains pipelined throughput
   - Balanced stage delays
   - Clean separation between pipeline stages
