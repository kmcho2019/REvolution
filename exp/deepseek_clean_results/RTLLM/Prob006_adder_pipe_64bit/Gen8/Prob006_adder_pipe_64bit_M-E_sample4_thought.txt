Novel Architecture Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select and Ripple-Carry Pipeline

This design combines the benefits of carry-select and ripple-carry adders in a pipelined structure. The key innovations are:

1. Hierarchical Segmentation: Divide the 64-bit addition into 4 segments (16+16+16+16 bits) with carry-select between segments
2. Two-Phase Pipeline: 
   - Phase 1: Compute all segment sums in parallel (both carry=0 and carry=1 cases)
   - Phase 2: Select correct results based on actual carry propagation
3. Smart Carry Resolution: Use carry-lookahead logic only between segments to minimize critical path
4. Efficient Enable Pipeline: Matches the 2-cycle latency with proper synchronization

Advantages:
- Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
- Balanced pipeline stages
- Lower area than full carry-select
- Maintains simple control flow
- Better timing closure potential
