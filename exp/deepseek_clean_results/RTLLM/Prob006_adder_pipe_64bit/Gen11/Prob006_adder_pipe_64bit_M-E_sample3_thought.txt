Novel Design Approach:
1. Implement a 64-bit adder using a hierarchical carry-lookahead structure
2. Divide into 4 levels of hierarchy (4-bit, 16-bit, 64-bit) for optimal carry propagation
3. Use a 4-stage pipeline to maximize throughput:
   - Stage 1: Precompute 4-bit block generate/propagate terms
   - Stage 2: Compute 16-bit group generate/propagate terms
   - Stage 3: Compute final carry signals
   - Stage 4: Combine sums with final carries
4. Implement speculative carry computation to reduce critical path
5. Use a shift register for enable signal propagation matching pipeline depth
6. Optimize register placement for balanced stage timing

Key Advantages:
- Hierarchical carry-lookahead reduces worst-case delay
- Balanced 4-stage pipeline improves throughput
- Speculative carry computation enables early evaluation
- Modular structure simplifies verification and timing closure
- Clear separation between carry computation and sum generation
