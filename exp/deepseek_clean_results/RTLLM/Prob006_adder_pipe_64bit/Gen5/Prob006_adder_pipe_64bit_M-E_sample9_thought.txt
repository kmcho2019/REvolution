Novel approach: Hybrid Carry-Lookahead/Ripple-Carry Adder with Staggered Pipeline
1. Combine carry-lookahead (CLA) for local fast carry generation with ripple-carry for global propagation
2. Divide the 64-bit adder into 4 segments (16-bit CLA blocks)
3. Each CLA block computes its own group propagate/generate terms
4. Implement staggered pipeline: CLA blocks compute in parallel while carry ripples between them
5. Use two-stage pipeline: first stage computes CLA sums, second stage resolves inter-block carries
6. Optimize register usage by only storing necessary intermediate terms
7. Synchronize enable signals with pipeline stages

Key advantages:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry due to CLA blocks
- More area-efficient than full CLA
- Balanced pipeline stages (computation + carry resolution)
- Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
- Flexible timing with staggered pipeline
