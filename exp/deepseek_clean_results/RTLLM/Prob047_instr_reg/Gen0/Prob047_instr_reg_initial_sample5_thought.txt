The instruction register needs to store instructions from different sources based on the fetch control signal. We'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold the instructions. On each clock edge, we'll check the reset signal first. If not reset, we'll update the appropriate register based on the fetch signal. The outputs are derived from these registers: ins and ad1 from the high and low bits of ins_p1 respectively, and ad2 directly from ins_p2.
