The instruction register needs to store instructions from two different sources based on the fetch control signal. We'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold these instructions. On each clock edge, we'll check the reset signal first - if active, we'll clear both registers. Otherwise, we'll update the appropriate register based on the fetch signal. The outputs will be derived from these registers: ins[2:0] from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1[4:0] from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2[7:0] directly from ins_p2.
