The instruction register needs to store instructions from different sources based on the fetch control signal. We'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold the instructions. On each clock edge, we'll check the reset signal first. If not reset, we'll update the appropriate register based on the fetch signal. The outputs are derived from the stored values: ins and ad1 come from the high 3 and low 5 bits of ins_p1 respectively, while ad2 outputs the full ins_p2 value.
