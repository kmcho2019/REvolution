The instruction register needs to store instructions from different sources and provide specific fields as outputs. We'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions based on the fetch control signal. On reset, both registers are cleared. The outputs are:
- ins: bits [7:5] of ins_p1 (opcode)
- ad1: bits [4:0] of ins_p1 (register address)
- ad2: full 8 bits of ins_p2 (second source data)
The registers update on clock edges when their respective fetch signals are active.
