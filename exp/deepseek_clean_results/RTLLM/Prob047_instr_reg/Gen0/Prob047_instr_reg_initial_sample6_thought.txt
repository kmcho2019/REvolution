The instruction register module needs to:
1. Store instructions from different sources in two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2)
2. Reset both registers to 0 when rst is low
3. Update ins_p1 when fetch is 01, ins_p2 when fetch is 10
4. Maintain current values when fetch doesn't match either condition
5. Output:
   - ins: high 3 bits of ins_p1 (opcode)
   - ad1: low 5 bits of ins_p1 (register address)
   - ad2: full 8 bits of ins_p2 (second source data)
