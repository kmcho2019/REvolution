The instruction register module needs to:
1. Store instructions from different sources in two separate 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2)
2. Reset both registers when rst is low
3. Update registers based on fetch signal (01 for ins_p1, 10 for ins_p2)
4. Output:
   - ins: upper 3 bits of ins_p1
   - ad1: lower 5 bits of ins_p1
   - ad2: full 8 bits of ins_p2
5. Maintain current values when no fetch condition is met
