Simplified Design Approach:
1. Single-edge triggering: Use only rising clock edge for simpler timing and better PPA
2. Remove redundant priority logic: Since fetch signals are mutually exclusive per spec
3. Basic register storage: Maintain two 8-bit registers for instruction parts
4. Direct output assignment: Outputs derived directly from stored registers
5. Simplified reset: Clear all registers synchronously on active-low reset

Key Improvements:
- Eliminates complex dual-edge triggering
- Removes unnecessary priority encoder
- Simplifies clock gating
- Reduces area by removing redundant logic
- Maintains same functionality with better timing
