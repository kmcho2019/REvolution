Based on the PPA metrics and feedback, here's the optimized strategy:

1. Timing Improvement:
   - Remove clock gating from clock path to eliminate combinational delay
   - Use synchronous enable signals instead to maintain timing closure
   - Simplify register update logic with direct assignments

2. Power Optimization:
   - Keep power savings by using enable-based register updates
   - Maintain output isolation during inactive cycles
   - Remove unnecessary clock switching activity

3. Area Optimization:
   - Merge register update conditions into single always block
   - Remove redundant intermediate signals
   - Use direct bit slicing for outputs

Key changes:
- Eliminated clock gating that was causing timing issues
- Simplified control logic while maintaining functionality
- Reduced area by combining update conditions
- Maintained power efficiency through enable-based updates
