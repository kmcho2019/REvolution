Novel Architecture Concept:
1. Dual-edge Triggering:
   - Utilize both rising and falling edges of the clock to potentially double throughput
   - Rising edge captures instruction part 1 (ins_p1)
   - Falling edge captures instruction part 2 (ins_p2)

2. Output Pipelining:
   - Add pipeline registers for outputs to improve timing
   - Separate the output generation from instruction capture
   - Enables higher clock frequencies at the cost of 1-cycle latency

3. Power-Aware Design:
   - Use clock enables to prevent unnecessary toggling
   - Implement output isolation during reset
   - Separate power domains not needed for this small design

Key Advantages:
- Potential throughput improvement with dual-edge operation
- Better timing characteristics due to pipelined outputs
- Maintains clean reset functionality
- Power efficient with proper clock gating
