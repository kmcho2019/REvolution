My novel approach features:
1. Dual-edge triggering (both rising and falling clock edges) to effectively double the throughput
2. A pipelined structure with two stages for better timing closure
3. Separate enable signals for each pipeline stage
4. Output registers to improve timing on critical paths
5. Power optimization through clock gating when fetch signals are inactive

The design maintains the same functionality but achieves better performance by:
- Processing instructions on both clock edges
- Breaking the critical path with pipeline stages
- Reducing clock power consumption with gating
- Improving output timing with registered outputs
