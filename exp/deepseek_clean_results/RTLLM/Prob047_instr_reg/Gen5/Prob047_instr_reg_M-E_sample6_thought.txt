Novel Dual-Buffered Pipeline Architecture:

1. Dual-buffer design: Uses two sets of registers (primary and shadow) to enable seamless instruction swapping without pipeline stalls
2. Output pipelining: Adds one pipeline stage to outputs for better timing closure
3. Power optimization: Implements clock gating and operand isolation
4. Smart reset: Different reset behavior for different registers (full reset for primary, partial for shadow)
5. Source validation: Adds a validity check for fetch signals to prevent unintended updates

Key Innovations:
- Shadow registers allow for speculative instruction prefetch
- Pipelined outputs improve timing at minimal area cost
- Advanced power management through conditional clocking
- Error-resistant design with fetch signal validation
- Separate reset domains for different register types
