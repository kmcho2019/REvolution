Based on the PPA metrics and feedback, here's the optimization strategy:
1. Timing Improvement:
   - Simplify the fetch decoding logic by using direct bit checks instead of full decode
   - Remove redundant else block to flatten the control flow

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating for the registers when fetch signals are inactive
   - Use power-optimized register assignments

3. Area Optimization:
   - Remove intermediate wires for outputs and assign directly from registers
   - Use simpler continuous assignments for bit slicing

The improved design maintains functionality while addressing:
- Negative slack by simplifying critical path
- Power consumption through clock gating
- Area by removing unnecessary logic
