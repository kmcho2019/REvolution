I propose a pipelined instruction register design with these key innovations:
1. Two-stage pipeline to improve timing (fetch stage and output stage)
2. Registered outputs to break critical paths
3. Early decoding of instruction fields in the first stage
4. Power-optimized clock gating for the second stage
5. Separate enable signals for each pipeline stage

The design splits the operation into:
- Stage 1: Instruction capture and field separation
- Stage 2: Output registration with clock gating

This approach provides better timing margins while maintaining the same functionality, and the clock gating reduces dynamic power when outputs aren't changing.
