The main issue in the original design is the race condition between register updates and output generation. To simplify:
1. Remove the separate output registers (ins, ad1, ad2) and make them combinational outputs derived directly from the internal registers
2. Keep the two 8-bit internal registers (ins_p1, ins_p2) for storage
3. Update registers only on clock edges based on fetch signal
4. Make outputs continuous assignments from the stored values
This eliminates the timing ambiguity while maintaining the same functionality.
