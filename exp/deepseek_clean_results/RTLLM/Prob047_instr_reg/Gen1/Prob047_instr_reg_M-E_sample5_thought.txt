This solution employs a two-stage pipeline approach with explicit state separation:
1. First stage: Capture and store instructions from different sources into separate registers
2. Second stage: Process and output the instruction components
The key improvements are:
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Output registers that maintain stability between clock cycles
- Explicit handling of instruction components
- Better synthesis predictability by avoiding continuous assignments in clocked blocks
The design ensures outputs reflect the stored values from the previous cycle while new instructions are being captured.
