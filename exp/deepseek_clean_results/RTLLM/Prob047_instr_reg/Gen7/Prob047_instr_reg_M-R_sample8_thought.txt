Refactoring approach:
1. Use a single always block for sequential logic to improve timing and reduce power
2. Remove intermediate combinational assignments for cleaner implementation
3. Maintain the same functionality but with more direct register updates
4. Keep output assignments combinational for simplicity
5. This approach reduces unnecessary logic and potential glitches

- Direct reset handling in the always block
