Hierarchical Speculative Instruction Register:
1. Three-stage pipeline: Fetch -> Pre-decode -> Final-decode
2. Speculative execution: Always pre-decode incoming data regardless of fetch signal
3. Shadow registers: Maintain speculative and confirmed instruction copies
4. Dynamic output selection: Choose between speculative or confirmed outputs based on validity
5. Error recovery: Rollback mechanism for incorrect speculation

Key Innovations:
- Parallel processing of all potential instructions
- Early availability of pre-decoded outputs
- Reduced latency through speculation
- Automatic correction of mis-speculated paths
- Power-efficient through selective register updates

Advantages:
- 1-cycle earlier availability of instruction fields
- Better utilization of bus bandwidth
- Graceful handling of fetch signal timing variations
- Reduced power through minimized register toggling
