Simplified Design Approach:
1. Single-edge triggering: Use only rising clock edge for simpler timing and better predictability
2. Remove redundant logic: Eliminate priority encoder since fetch signals are mutually exclusive
3. Optimize register usage: Maintain only necessary internal registers
4. Simplified power management: Remove complex clock gating in favor of standard enable logic
5. Direct output assignment: Derive outputs directly from input data during fetch operations

Key Improvements:
- Single clock edge simplifies timing analysis
- Reduced logic complexity improves area efficiency
- Maintains full functionality per specifications
- Cleaner separation of control and data paths
- More straightforward implementation
