To optimize the D flip-flop design for better PPA metrics, particularly focusing on power reduction while maintaining the same functionality:

1. **Power Optimization**:
   - Remove the hierarchical structure to eliminate module instantiation overhead
   - Use direct implementation in TopModule to reduce power consumption from inter-module connections
   - Keep the same positive-edge triggered behavior

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - The flattened design will naturally have slightly better area characteristics
   - No additional logic is needed beyond the basic DFF implementation

3. **Performance**:
   - Maintain the same timing characteristics (0ns violations)
   - The direct implementation may slightly improve clock-to-Q timing

This simplified approach should reduce power consumption while keeping all other metrics at their optimal values.
