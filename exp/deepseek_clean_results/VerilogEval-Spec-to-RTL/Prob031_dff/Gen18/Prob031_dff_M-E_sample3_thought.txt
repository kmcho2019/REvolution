This solution implements the D flip-flop using a master-slave latch configuration, which is how flip-flops are typically constructed at the transistor level. The design:
1. Uses two level-sensitive latches (master and slave)
2. The master latch is transparent when clock is low
3. The slave latch is transparent when clock is high
4. Together they create positive edge-triggered behavior
5. Demonstrates the internal working of a flip-flop rather than just behavioral description

This approach provides better insight into the actual hardware implementation while maintaining identical functionality to a standard D flip-flop.
