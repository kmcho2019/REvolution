Here's a novel approach: Implement the D flip-flop using a master-slave latch configuration with explicit enable signals. This provides:
1. Better glitch immunity by separating the sampling and update phases
2. Potential for future power optimization through clock gating
3. More transparent operation that could aid in debugging
4. The same functional behavior as a standard DFF but with different internal structure

The design uses two level-sensitive latches (master and slave) with complementary clock phases to create the edge-triggered behavior. The master latch captures the input when clock is low, and the slave latch updates the output when clock goes high.
