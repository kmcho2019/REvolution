A novel approach is to implement the D flip-flop using a master-slave latch configuration with explicit structural modeling. This mimics the internal structure of edge-triggered flip-flops found in many standard cell libraries. The design uses two level-sensitive latches (master and slave) with opposite clock phases to create edge-triggered behavior. The master latch is transparent when clock is low, while the slave latch is transparent when clock is high, effectively creating a positive-edge triggered flip-flop.
