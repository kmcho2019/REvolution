Here's a novel approach using a master-slave latch configuration to implement the D flip-flop, which provides better glitch immunity and clearer separation of timing domains:

1. Instead of a direct edge-triggered flip-flop, we'll use two level-sensitive latches
2. The master latch (negative level-sensitive) captures data when clock is low
3. The slave latch (positive level-sensitive) passes data when clock is high
4. This creates a positive edge-triggered behavior through the combination
5. The implementation shows the internal working of a flip-flop more explicitly
6. This approach can have better metastability characteristics in some technologies
