The original implementation uses a procedural always block to model the D flip-flop behavior. We can refactor this to use a continuous assignment with a clock-gated expression. While this isn't typically recommended for synthesis (as it may not infer a proper flip-flop in all tools), it demonstrates an alternative structural approach. The key idea is to use an assign statement that updates 'q' only at the positive clock edge by making the assignment conditional on the clock transition.
