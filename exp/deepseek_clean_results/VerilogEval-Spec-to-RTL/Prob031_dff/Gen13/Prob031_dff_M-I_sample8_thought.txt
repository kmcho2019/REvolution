To optimize the D flip-flop design for better PPA metrics, particularly focusing on power reduction while maintaining the same functionality:

1. **Power Optimization**:
   - Remove the hierarchical structure to eliminate module call overhead
   - Use direct implementation in TopModule to reduce power consumption from inter-module connections
   - Keep the same positive-edge triggered behavior

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - The single flip-flop implementation is already minimal, but flattening removes the module instantiation overhead

3. **Performance**:
   - Maintain the same timing characteristics (0ns violations) since the core flip-flop implementation remains unchanged
   - The flattened design might have slightly better performance due to reduced hierarchy

This simplified implementation should reduce power consumption while maintaining all other PPA metrics.
