Here's a novel approach: implement the D flip-flop using a master-slave latch configuration with transparent latches. This provides the same functionality but demonstrates an alternative internal implementation:

1. Use two level-sensitive latches (positive and negative enable)
2. First latch (master) captures data when clock is low
3. Second latch (slave) captures master's output when clock goes high
4. Combined behavior creates edge-triggered flip-flop
5. This shows the underlying structure of how flip-flops are often built from latches
