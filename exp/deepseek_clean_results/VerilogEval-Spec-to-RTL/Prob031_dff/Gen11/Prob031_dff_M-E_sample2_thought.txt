Here's a novel approach using a master-slave latch configuration to implement the D flip-flop:
1. Instead of a single always block, we'll use two transparent latches
2. The master latch is active when clock is low, capturing the input
3. The slave latch is active when clock is high, holding the output
4. This creates edge-triggered behavior through the interaction of the two level-sensitive latches
5. The implementation shows the underlying structure of how flip-flops are often built from latches
