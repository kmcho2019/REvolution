Instead of using a standard flip-flop implementation, we can construct the flip-flop using two level-sensitive latches in a master-slave configuration:
1. Master latch (negative level-sensitive) captures input when clock is low
2. Slave latch (positive level-sensitive) captures master's output when clock is high
3. Together they create positive edge-triggered behavior
4. This demonstrates the underlying latch-based construction of flip-flops
