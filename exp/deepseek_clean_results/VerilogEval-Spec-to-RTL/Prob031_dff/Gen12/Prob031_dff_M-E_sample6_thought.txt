Instead of using a direct positive-edge triggered flip-flop, we can implement the same functionality using two level-sensitive latches in a master-slave configuration. The master latch is transparent when clock is low, capturing the input data. On the positive clock edge, the master latch closes and the slave latch opens, transferring the captured value to the output. This approach demonstrates the underlying mechanism of how edge-triggered flip-flops are typically constructed from latches in actual hardware implementations.
