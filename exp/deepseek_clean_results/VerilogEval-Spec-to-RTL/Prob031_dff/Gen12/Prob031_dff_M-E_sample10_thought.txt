This implementation uses two level-sensitive latches (master and slave) to create edge-triggered behavior:
1. The master latch is transparent when clock is low
2. The slave latch is transparent when clock is high
3. Together they create a positive edge-triggered flip-flop
4. This approach demonstrates the underlying mechanism of how edge-triggered flip-flops are typically built from latches in actual hardware
5. The implementation shows explicit data flow through both stages
