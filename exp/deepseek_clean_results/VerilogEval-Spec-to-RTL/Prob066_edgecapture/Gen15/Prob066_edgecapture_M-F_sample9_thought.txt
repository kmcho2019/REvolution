The feedback highlights three key issues to address:

1. **Independent Bit Handling**: Each bit should independently detect and remember its falling edge, without being affected by other bits' transitions.

2. **Sticky Capture Logic**: Once a bit detects a falling edge (1→0 transition), its corresponding output bit should remain 1 permanently until reset, regardless of subsequent transitions.

3. **Unconditional Update**: The captured register should update whenever a falling edge occurs on any bit, without requiring a global condition.

Solution Approach:
1. Remove the conditional update (`if (|(prev_in & ~in))`) since we want each bit to update independently
2. Maintain the parallel comparison of current and previous input
3. Use OR operation to combine new falling edges with previously captured ones
4. Keep the synchronous reset functionality

This ensures:
- Each bit operates independently
- All falling edges are captured permanently
- Reset clears all captured states
- Minimal hardware overhead
