Refactored approach with clearer edge detection and capture:

1. **Separate Edge Detection**: Use continuous assignments for combinational logic to clearly show the falling edge detection (1->0 transitions).

2. **Explicit Capture Logic**: Maintain the sticky capture behavior through a simple register that ORs new detections with previous state.

3. **Simplified State**: Reduce to just one state register (captured) while keeping previous input as a temporary for edge detection.

Key improvements:
- Clear separation of combinational (edge detection) and sequential (capture) logic
- More readable structure with explicit falling edge wires
- Maintains identical sticky capture behavior
- Easier to debug and verify
