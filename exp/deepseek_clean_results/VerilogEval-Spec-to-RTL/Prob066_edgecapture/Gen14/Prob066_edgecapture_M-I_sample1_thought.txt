1. **Proper Edge Detection**:

   - Detect falling edges when prev_in=1 and current in=0
   - This ensures edge detection happens exactly at clock boundaries

2. **Capture Logic**:
   - Once a falling edge is detected, maintain the output until reset
   - Use OR operation to preserve previously detected edges
   - This meets the requirement of "capturing" transitions

3. **Reset Handling**:
   - Synchronous reset clears both prev_in and captured registers
   - Ensures clean state initialization

4. **Optimization**:
   - Minimal register usage (only prev_in and captured)
   - Simple combinational logic for edge detection
