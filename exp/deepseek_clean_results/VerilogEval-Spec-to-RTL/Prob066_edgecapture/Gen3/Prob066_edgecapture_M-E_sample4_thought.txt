Here's a novel approach using edge detection with a two-stage pipeline for each bit:

1. **Double Buffering**: For each bit, maintain two flip-flops to store the previous two states (prev1 and prev2). This creates a proper pipeline for edge detection.

2. **Edge Detection Logic**: A falling edge is detected when prev2 is 1 and prev1 is 0 (indicating a 1->0 transition occurred between two cycles ago and one cycle ago).

3. **Capture Memory**: Use a separate register to maintain the captured state. Once a falling edge is detected for any bit, its corresponding bit in the capture register is set and remains set until reset.

4. **Synchronous Operation**: All state updates occur on the positive clock edge, with synchronous reset.

This approach provides more reliable edge detection by ensuring we're comparing values from two distinct previous cycles, avoiding any potential race conditions in the comparison logic.
