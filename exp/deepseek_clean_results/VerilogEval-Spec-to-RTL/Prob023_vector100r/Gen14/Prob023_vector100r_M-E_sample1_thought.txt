A novel approach to bit reversal that leverages hierarchical grouping and recursive-like structure:
1. Divide the 100-bit input into 5 groups of 20 bits each
2. Reverse the order of these groups (outer reversal)
3. Within each group, reverse the bit order (inner reversal)
4. Combine the results to form the final output
This approach:
- Creates a more structured hierarchy in the implementation
- Potentially improves routing efficiency by breaking down the large fan-out problem
- Maintains the same functionality while offering a different implementation strategy
- Could be more scalable for even wider buses
