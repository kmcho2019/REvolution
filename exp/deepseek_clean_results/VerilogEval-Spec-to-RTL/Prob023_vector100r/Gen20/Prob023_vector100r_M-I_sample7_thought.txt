To optimize the solution, we'll use a generate loop to reverse the bit order. This approach offers several advantages:
1. Better scalability - works for any bit width without manual expansion
2. Improved readability and maintainability
3. Gives synthesis tools more optimization opportunities for better PPA
4. Reduces potential for human error in manual bit mapping
5. Creates a more regular structure that may lead to better routing

The generate loop will iterate through each bit position and make the appropriate reversed connection, which should result in similar functionality but with potential PPA improvements.
