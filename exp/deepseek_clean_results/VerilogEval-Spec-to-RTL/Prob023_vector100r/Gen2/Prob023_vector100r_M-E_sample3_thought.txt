Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical bit-reversal structure that divides the 100-bit vector into smaller chunks for potentially better synthesis results:

1. Divide the 100-bit input into 5 chunks of 20 bits each
2. Reverse each 20-bit chunk individually
3. Reverse the order of the chunks themselves
4. Combine the reversed chunks to form the final output

This hierarchical approach might offer better routing and placement opportunities for the synthesizer, especially in deep submicron technologies where wire delays dominate. The 20-bit chunk size was chosen as it's a good balance between hierarchy depth and chunk size.
