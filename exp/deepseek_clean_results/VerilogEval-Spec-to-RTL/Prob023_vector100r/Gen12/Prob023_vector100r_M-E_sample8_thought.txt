A novel approach using a recursive parameterized module to reverse the bits. This method:
1. Breaks down the 100-bit vector into smaller chunks recursively
2. Reverses each chunk independently
3. Combines the reversed chunks in reverse order
4. Uses a base case for the smallest chunk size (2 bits)
5. Offers better scalability and potential for synthesis optimization
6. Demonstrates hierarchical design principles
7. Can be easily adapted for different vector sizes
