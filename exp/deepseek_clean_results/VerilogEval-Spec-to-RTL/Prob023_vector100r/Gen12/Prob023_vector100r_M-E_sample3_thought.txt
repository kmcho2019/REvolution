Here's a novel approach using a concatenation-based reversal instead of individual bit assignments. This method:
1. Uses a single concatenation operation to reverse all bits at once
2. Eliminates the need for generate blocks or loops
3. Potentially reduces synthesis overhead by treating the operation as a single vector operation
4. Maintains the same functionality while being more concise
5. Still allows easy parameterization for different bit widths
6. May offer better optimization opportunities for synthesis tools as it's a single operation
