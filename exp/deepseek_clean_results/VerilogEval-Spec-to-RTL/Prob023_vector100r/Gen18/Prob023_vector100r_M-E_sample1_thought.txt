I'll implement a barrel-shifter inspired approach that:
1. Uses a logarithmic reversal pattern (similar to FFT bit-reversal)
2. Performs pairwise bit swaps in stages:
   - Stage 1: Swap adjacent bits (distance 1)
   - Stage 2: Swap bits with distance 2
   - Stage 4: Swap bits with distance 4
   - Stage 8: Swap bits with distance 8
   - Stage 16: Swap bits with distance 16
   - Stage 32: Swap bits with distance 32
3. This creates a butterfly network that gradually builds the reversed output
4. Benefits include:
   - Regular structure that may be more synthesis-friendly
   - Potential for pipelining if timing becomes critical
   - Clear visualization of the bit reversal process
5. The approach is mathematically equivalent to direct reversal but offers different implementation characteristics
