Here's a novel approach using parameterized bit-slicing and concatenation:
1. Instead of using a loop, we'll split the 100-bit input into smaller chunks (10 bits each)
2. Reverse each 10-bit chunk individually
3. Then reverse the order of the chunks themselves
4. Finally concatenate all reversed chunks
This method:
- Shows a different architectural approach to bit reversal
- Demonstrates hierarchical reversal (bits within chunks and chunks themselves)
- Could potentially have better synthesis results for certain technologies
- Makes the reversal operation more modular and potentially reusable
