This solution takes a different architectural approach by:
1. Dividing the 100-bit vector into 5 slices of 20 bits each
2. Reversing each 20-bit slice individually
3. Concatenating the reversed slices in reverse order
4. Using a parameterized approach that could be easily adapted to different vector sizes

Key advantages:
- Reduces the number of individual assignments compared to bit-by-bit reversal
- More hierarchical organization of the reversal operation
- Potentially better for synthesis tools to optimize routing
- Maintains pure combinational logic with zero timing overhead
- Scalable approach that could be parameterized for different vector widths
