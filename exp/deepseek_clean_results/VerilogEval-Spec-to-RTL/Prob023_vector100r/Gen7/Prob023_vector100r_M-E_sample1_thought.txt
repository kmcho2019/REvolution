This solution implements a divide-and-conquer approach to bit reversal:
1. Instead of processing bits individually, we divide the 100-bit vector into smaller chunks
2. First reverse the order of chunks (macro-level reversal)
3. Then reverse bits within each chunk (micro-level reversal)
4. Uses parameterized chunk size for potential optimization
5. Benefits include:
   - More regular structure that synthesis tools might optimize better
   - Potential for parallel processing of chunks
   - Clear hierarchical organization of the reversal operation
   - Easier to pipeline if needed for larger designs
