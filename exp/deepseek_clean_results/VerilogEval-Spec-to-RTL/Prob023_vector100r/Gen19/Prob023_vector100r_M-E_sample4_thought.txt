Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical bit-reversal technique:
1. Divide the 100-bit input into 5 segments of 20 bits each
2. Reverse each 20-bit segment individually
3. Then reverse the order of the segments themselves
4. This creates a two-level reversal that achieves the same result
5. Benefits:
   - More structured approach than direct bit assignments
   - Potentially better for physical implementation due to localized wiring
   - Easier to scale for larger bit widths
   - Clear separation of concerns in the design
6. The implementation uses intermediate wires to hold the segment reversals
