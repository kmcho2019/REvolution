I'll implement a hierarchical bit reversal approach that:
1. Divides the 100-bit vector into smaller chunks (10 groups of 10 bits)
2. Reverses the bits within each chunk
3. Then reverses the order of the chunks themselves
4. Uses generate blocks for modularity and scalability
5. Creates a more structured implementation than direct assignments
6. Potentially offers better physical implementation characteristics
7. Maintains the same functionality while being more architecturally interesting
