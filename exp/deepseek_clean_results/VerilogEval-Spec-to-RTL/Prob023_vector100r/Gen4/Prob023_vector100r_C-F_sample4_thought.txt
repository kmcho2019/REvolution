The fusion strategy combines the conciseness of the generate loop approach (Example 2) with the explicit bit-reversal clarity of the concatenation approach (Example 1). The key improvements are:

1. Maintain the generate loop structure for better synthesis optimization and scalability
2. Use direct bit assignments for clarity and parallel execution
3. Keep the implementation compact while being fully explicit about the bit reversal operation
4. This approach offers the best balance between readability, maintainability, and synthesis efficiency

The solution will use a generate loop with direct bit assignments, making it:
- More scalable than manual concatenation (easier to modify for different bit widths)
- More explicit than function-based approaches
- Equally efficient in terms of PPA metrics
