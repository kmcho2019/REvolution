Novel Architecture Idea: "Direction-Preserving Fall State Machine"

Key Innovations:
1. State Separation: Use three distinct states (LEFT, RIGHT, FALL) instead of combined encoding for clearer semantics
2. Direction Preservation: Maintain a separate direction register that persists during falls
3. Edge-Triggered Bump Detection: Only respond to bump signals on the rising edge to prevent glitches
4. Output Optimization: Generate outputs directly from state and direction registers with minimal logic

Advantages:
- Clear separation of walking direction and falling state
- More robust bump handling with edge detection
- Easier to extend for additional behaviors
- Reduced glitch potential in output signals
