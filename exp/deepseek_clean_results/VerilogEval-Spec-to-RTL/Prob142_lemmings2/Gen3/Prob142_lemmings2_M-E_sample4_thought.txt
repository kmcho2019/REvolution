Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical state machine design:
1. Separate the walking and falling behaviors into two distinct state machines that interact
2. The walking FSM handles direction changes and bump responses
3. The falling FSM manages the falling state and ground detection
4. Use a handshake protocol between the FSMs to maintain direction during falls
5. This separation allows for cleaner state transitions and easier debugging

Key advantages:
- Clear separation of concerns between walking and falling behaviors
- Each FSM is simpler with fewer transitions
- Explicit direction preservation during falls
- Easier to extend with additional behaviors
