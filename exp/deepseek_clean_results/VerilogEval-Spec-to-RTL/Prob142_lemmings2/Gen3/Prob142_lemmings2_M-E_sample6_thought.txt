Here's a novel approach using a direction register and a falling flag instead of explicit states for walking directions. The key ideas are:
1. Use a single direction bit (dir) to track walking direction (0=left, 1=right)
2. Use a falling flag (is_falling) to track falling state
3. Outputs are derived combinatorially from these registers
4. Direction changes only occur when grounded and bumped
5. Falling state is entered when ground is lost and exited when ground returns

This reduces the state space and simplifies transitions while maintaining the same behavior. The direction register persists through falling states, eliminating the need for separate fall-left/fall-right states.
