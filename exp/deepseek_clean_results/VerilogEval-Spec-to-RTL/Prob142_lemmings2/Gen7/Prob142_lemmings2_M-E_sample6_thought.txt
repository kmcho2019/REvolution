Here's a novel approach using a 3-state FSM with explicit direction tracking:

1. Use three states: LEFT, RIGHT, and FALL to clearly represent each behavior
2. Eliminate the separate direction register by encoding direction in the state
3. Implement a "bump buffer" to handle simultaneous bumps more cleanly
4. Use a registered output approach for better timing closure
5. The FALL state will remember the previous walking direction implicitly

Key differences from the original:
- More states but simpler transitions
- No separate direction tracking needed
- Cleaner handling of bump conditions
- Registered outputs for better timing
