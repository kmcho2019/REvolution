Simplified Design Approach:
1. State Representation: Use 2 states (WALK and FALL) with a direction register
2. Bump Handling: Combine left/right bump conditions into a single expression
3. Ground Handling: Direct state transitions based on ground signal
4. Output Logic: Simple combinational outputs based on current state and direction
5. Reset: Async reset initializes to walking left
6. Key Simplifications:
   - Single always block for state and direction updates
   - Minimal conditional logic
   - Clear separation of walking and falling behaviors
   - No unnecessary optimizations that complicate readability
