Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical state machine with direction tracking:

1. Main states: WALKING and FALLING (Moore outputs)
2. Nested direction states: LEFT and RIGHT (Mealy-like behavior)
3. Key innovation: Direction changes are handled in a separate always block that's only active during WALKING state
4. State transitions are purely based on ground signal
5. Outputs are derived directly from the current state and direction
6. Asynchronous reset initializes to WALKING-LEFT

This separation of concerns makes the design more modular and potentially easier to optimize for PPA metrics. The direction logic only operates when relevant (during WALKING state), reducing unnecessary switching.
