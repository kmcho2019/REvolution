Here's a novel approach using a direction flag and fall state tracking:
1. Instead of separate states for left/right falling, track direction separately
2. Use a direction register (1=right, 0=left) and a falling flag
3. State transitions become simpler:
   - When not falling, update direction based on bumps
   - When ground=0, set falling flag
   - When ground returns, clear falling flag
4. Outputs are derived combinatorially from direction and falling state
This reduces state space and simplifies transitions while maintaining the same behavior.

Key advantages:
- Fewer states (just tracking direction + falling status)
- Clear separation of direction logic from falling logic
- More efficient encoding (2 bits total)
- Easier to extend with additional behaviors
