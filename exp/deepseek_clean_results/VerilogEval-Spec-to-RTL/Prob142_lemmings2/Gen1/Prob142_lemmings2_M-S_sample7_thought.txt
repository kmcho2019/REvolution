To simplify the Lemming's state machine, we can:
1. Reduce the number of states from 4 to 3 by combining the falling states into one FALL state and tracking the previous walking direction separately.
2. Use a separate register to store the walking direction during falls.
3. Make the output logic simpler by directly using the state and direction register.
4. Maintain the same functionality while reducing state transitions and logic complexity.

The simplified states are:
1. WALKING: Active when on ground (output depends on direction)
2. FALLING: Active when not on ground (output aaah=1)

The direction register:
- Stores the walking direction (left or right)
- Updated only when walking and bumped
- Preserved during falls
