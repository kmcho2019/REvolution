To implement the Lemming behavior, we need a Moore state machine with the following states:
1. WALK_LEFT: Lemming walks left (walk_left=1)
2. WALK_RIGHT: Lemming walks right (walk_right=1)
3. FALL_LEFT: Lemming is falling from left (aaah=1)
4. FALL_RIGHT: Lemming is falling from right (aaah=1)

Transitions:
- Reset (areset) goes to WALK_LEFT
- In walking states, ground=0 transitions to falling states
- In falling states, ground=1 transitions back to walking states
- In walking states, bump signals change direction
- Falling states ignore bump signals
- Falling states remember their previous walking direction
