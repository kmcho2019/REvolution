To implement the Lemming's behavior, we need a Moore state machine with the following states:
1. WALK_LEFT: Lemming is walking left (walk_left=1)
2. WALK_RIGHT: Lemming is walking right (walk_right=1)
3. FALL_LEFT: Lemming is falling from left (aaah=1)
4. FALL_RIGHT: Lemming is falling from right (aaah=1)

Transitions:
- When ground=0, transition from WALK_LEFT to FALL_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALL_RIGHT
- When ground=1, transition back from FALL_LEFT to WALK_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT to WALK_RIGHT
- When bumped on left in WALK_LEFT, switch to WALK_RIGHT
- When bumped on right in WALK_RIGHT, switch to WALK_LEFT
- Reset initializes to WALK_LEFT

The key is to handle the falling state properly and ensure bumps don't affect direction during falls.
