The given solution is already optimal for this trivial combinational pass-through requirement. Since it's a direct wire connection:
1. There's no timing path to optimize (tns/wns = 0)
2. Power consumption is minimal (just the wire capacitance)
3. Area is already at the absolute minimum (1.0 um^2)

No meaningful PPA improvements are possible because:
- We can't reduce area below a single wire connection
- We can't improve timing beyond zero propagation delay
- We can't reduce power below the inherent wire capacitance

The only possible "improvement" would be stylistic - using an explicit wire declaration for clarity, though this doesn't affect PPA.
