The given solution is already optimal for this trivial combinational pass-through requirement. The direct wire connection between input and output achieves:
1. Perfect timing with zero delay (tns/wns = 0.0)
2. Minimal power consumption (2.11e-05 W)
3. Minimal area utilization (1.0 um²)

No meaningful improvements can be made because:
- Any additional logic would only degrade the metrics
- The design is at theoretical minimum for area and power
- The timing cannot be improved beyond zero delay

The only possible "improvement" would be to use different Verilog syntax that synthesizes to identical hardware, but this would be purely stylistic rather than a functional or PPA improvement.
