Here's a novel approach using a transmission gate implementation for the buffer. While functionally equivalent to a direct wire connection, this implementation:

1. Uses CMOS transmission gates for potential power savings in certain technologies
2. Provides symmetrical rise/fall times
3. Can be useful in mixed-signal designs where transmission gates are preferred
4. Offers potential for better noise immunity
5. Demonstrates an alternative implementation style that could be extended to more complex circuits

The implementation uses both NMOS and PMOS transistors (modeled as Verilog primitives) to create a full transmission gate. While this may not be better in PPA for this simple case, it shows a different architectural approach that could be beneficial in certain contexts.
