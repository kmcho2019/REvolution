Here's a novel approach using a transmission gate implementation:
1. Models the behavior of a CMOS transmission gate (pass transistor)
2. Demonstrates switch-level modeling in Verilog
3. Shows how to implement a bi-directional pass element
4. Includes explicit strength modeling for more realistic simulation
5. Can be extended to model more complex analog behaviors

The transmission gate acts as a perfect conductor when enabled (which it always is in this case), passing the input signal to the output with minimal distortion. This approach is commonly used in mixed-signal and switch-level designs.
