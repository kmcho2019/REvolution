To simplify the LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Instead of using a full 32-bit mask and XOR operation, only implement the necessary XOR operations at the specific tap positions (31, 21, 1, 0)
2. The shift operation can remain a simple right shift
3. Only apply XOR with q[0] at the tap positions
4. Maintain the same reset functionality (active high synchronous reset to 32'h1)
5. This reduces complexity by:
   - Eliminating the full 32-bit mask operation
   - Only performing 4 specific XOR operations
   - Reducing the number of gates needed
6. The simplified version will have the same functionality but with less logic
