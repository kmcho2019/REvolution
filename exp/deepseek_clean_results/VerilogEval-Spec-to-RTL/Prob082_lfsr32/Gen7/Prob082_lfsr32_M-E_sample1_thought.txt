Innovative Parallel Computation Approach:
1. Instead of sequential right shifts with XOR taps, compute all bits in parallel
2. Pre-calculate the feedback term (q[0]) first
3. For each bit position, determine its next value in parallel:
   - MSB (bit 31) always takes feedback directly
   - Tap positions (21,1,0) compute their next value as XOR of their neighbor and feedback
   - Other bits simply take their left neighbor's value
4. This approach may improve timing by:
   - Reducing the critical path to just one XOR operation per bit
   - Eliminating the ripple effect of sequential shifts
   - Potentially allowing better synthesis optimization
5. Still maintains synchronous reset behavior
