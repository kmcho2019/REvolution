New approach for Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Use 1-based indexing for clarity (matching problem specification)
2. Implement taps as a parameterized mask for flexibility
3. Instead of separate XOR operations, use a bitwise AND with tap mask followed by XOR
4. Shift operation combined with feedback in one step
5. Reset initializes to 32'h1 (0x00000001 in 1-based indexing)
6. All operations in single always block for clean sequential logic

Key insights:
- The feedback is always the LSB (bit 1 in 1-based)
- Taps are at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1
- Can represent taps as a 32-bit mask where tap positions are 1
- XOR feedback with all tap positions simultaneously using mask
- Shift right occurs naturally in the assignment
