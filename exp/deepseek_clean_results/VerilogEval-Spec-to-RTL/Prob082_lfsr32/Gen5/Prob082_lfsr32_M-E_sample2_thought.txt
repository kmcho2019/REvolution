Here's a novel approach using a bitwise conditional XOR operation during the shift:
1. Instead of precomputing a single feedback value, we'll process each bit position individually during the shift
2. For each bit position that has a tap (32, 22, 2, 1 in 1-based indexing), we'll XOR it with the LSB (q[0]) as it shifts right
3. Untapped bits simply shift right unchanged
4. This approach:
   - More accurately models the Galois LFSR structure
   - Explicitly shows the tap modification at each relevant position
   - Avoids the feedback calculation error in the previous solution
   - Maintains clear visibility of the LFSR polynomial implementation
5. Still uses synchronous reset and positive edge triggering
