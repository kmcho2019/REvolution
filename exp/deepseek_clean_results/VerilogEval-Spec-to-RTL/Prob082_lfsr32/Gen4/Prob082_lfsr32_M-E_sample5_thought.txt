Here's a novel approach using a bitmask technique for the Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Instead of handling each tap position separately, create a tap mask that identifies all tap positions
2. Use this mask to selectively XOR the feedback with only the tap bits in parallel
3. The shift operation becomes a simple right shift combined with the masked XOR operation
4. This approach reduces the explicit bit-by-bit assignments and leverages bitwise operations for cleaner code
5. Maintain the same synchronous reset and positive edge triggering behavior
6. The tap mask (32'b11000000000000000000010000000011) represents positions 32, 22, 2, and 1
