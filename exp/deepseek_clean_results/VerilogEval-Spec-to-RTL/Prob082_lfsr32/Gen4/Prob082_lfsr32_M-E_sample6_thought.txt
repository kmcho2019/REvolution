Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Instead of using a traditional Galois LFSR structure, implement a "split-feedback" design that:
   - Separates the feedback computation from the shift operation
   - Uses intermediate registers to break the critical path
   - Implements a two-stage pipeline for better timing

2. Key innovations:
   - Pre-compute all XOR operations in parallel during the first clock cycle
   - Store intermediate results in flip-flops
   - Perform the final shift and update in the second clock cycle
   - This approach trades off a small latency increase (1 cycle) for significantly better timing closure

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path by separating XOR operations from the shift
   - Better timing slack due to pipelining
   - More balanced power distribution across clock cycles
   - Maintains same LFSR sequence but with improved PPA metrics
