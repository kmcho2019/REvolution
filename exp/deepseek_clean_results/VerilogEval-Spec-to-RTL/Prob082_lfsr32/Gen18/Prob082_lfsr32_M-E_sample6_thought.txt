Innovative segmented LFSR architecture:
1. Divide the 32-bit register into 4 segments (8 bits each) with tap points at segment boundaries
2. Implement local XOR feedback within each segment to reduce wire length
3. Use a hierarchical feedback structure where:
   - Each segment computes its own feedback term
   - Global feedback from LSB (q[0]) is distributed only to tap segments
4. Key advantages:
   - Reduced fanout of q[0] by localizing feedback paths
   - Shorter critical paths due to segmented computation
   - Better physical implementation characteristics
5. Maintain same reset functionality but implement with parallel load
6. The design should have improved timing and power characteristics
