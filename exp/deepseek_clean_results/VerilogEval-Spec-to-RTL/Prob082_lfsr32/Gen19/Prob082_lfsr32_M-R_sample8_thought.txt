Refactoring Strategy:
1. Separate the combinational logic from sequential logic for better clarity
2. Use continuous assignments (assign) for the next state calculation
3. Keep the synchronous reset behavior
4. Break down the LFSR update into explicit components:
   - The MSB comes from q[0]
   - Untapped bits shift right normally
   - Tapped bits are XORed with q[0]
5. This approach makes the tap positions more visible and might help synthesis tools optimize better
6. The functionality remains identical while improving readability
