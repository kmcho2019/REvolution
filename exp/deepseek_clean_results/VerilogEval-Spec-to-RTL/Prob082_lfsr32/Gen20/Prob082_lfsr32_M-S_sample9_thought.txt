To simplify the LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Directly implement the 4 required XOR operations at tap positions (31,21,1,0) instead of using a full 32-bit mask
2. Remove the unnecessary mask generation and replication logic
3. Perform the XOR operations only on the tapped bits during the shift
4. Keep the same reset functionality with direct assignment
5. The simplified approach will:
   - Reduce logic complexity by eliminating unused mask operations
   - Maintain the same LFSR behavior with proper taps
   - Potentially improve timing by reducing unnecessary operations
