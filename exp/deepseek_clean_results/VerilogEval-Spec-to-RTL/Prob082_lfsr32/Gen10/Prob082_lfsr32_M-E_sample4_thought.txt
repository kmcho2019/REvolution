Novel Architectural Idea: Pipelined XOR Tree with Early Computation
1. Instead of computing all XOR operations in the same cycle, pipeline them across two clock cycles to improve timing
2. First stage computes XOR for taps 22 and 2 in parallel
3. Second stage computes XOR for tap 1 and combines with previous results
4. This reduces the critical path by splitting the XOR operations
5. Maintain same functionality with 1 cycle latency penalty
6. Use a shadow register to store intermediate results
7. Reset initializes both main and shadow registers
8. The pipelining allows higher clock frequencies at the cost of increased latency
