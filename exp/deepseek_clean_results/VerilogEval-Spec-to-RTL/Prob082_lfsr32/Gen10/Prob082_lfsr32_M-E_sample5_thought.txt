Novel Architectural Idea: Parallel XOR Network with Pre-computation
1. Instead of performing XOR operations during the shift operation, pre-compute all XOR results in parallel
2. Use a dedicated XOR network that calculates all tap modifications simultaneously
3. This approach reduces the critical path by parallelizing the XOR operations
4. The shift operation becomes a simple selection of pre-computed values
5. Maintains the same functionality with potentially better timing characteristics
6. Still implements synchronous reset to 32'h1 as specified
