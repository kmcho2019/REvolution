Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Precompute Feedback: Instead of calculating XOR operations separately for each tap, precompute the feedback term that combines all tap contributions in parallel.
2. Parallel XOR Network: Create a dedicated XOR network that processes all taps simultaneously, reducing the critical path.
3. Shift with Precomputation: Use the precomputed feedback to drive the shift operation in a single cycle.
4. Optimized Reset: Maintain the synchronous reset but implement it with a conditional operator for potential synthesis optimization.

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path by parallelizing XOR operations
- Clean separation of feedback computation and shift operation
- Potential for better timing due to parallel processing
- Maintains same functionality with improved PPA characteristics
