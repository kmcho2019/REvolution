To simplify the 32-bit Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Maintain the same core functionality with taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1
2. Instead of using explicit bit slicing and concatenation, we can:
   - First calculate the feedback bit (q[0])
   - Then compute each tap position by XORing with feedback when needed
   - Finally shift all bits right and insert feedback at MSB
3. This approach reduces the visual complexity of the bit manipulation while maintaining identical functionality
4. The synchronous reset behavior remains unchanged
