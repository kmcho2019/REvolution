To correctly implement the 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1:
1. The LFSR should shift right on each clock cycle (q[i] <= q[i-1])
2. For tap positions (which correspond to bits 31, 21, 1, and 0 in 0-based indexing):
   - These bits should be XORed with the outgoing LSB (q[0])
   - The result of q[0] XOR q[31] becomes the new MSB
   - Other tap positions (21, 1) get q[i-1] XOR q[0]
   - Non-tap positions simply shift right (q[i] <= q[i-1])
3. Maintain synchronous reset to 32'h1
4. The key correction is to handle each tap position separately rather than combining all taps in one XOR
5. Bit 0 (LSB) always shifts out and doesn't need special handling
