To simplify the 32-bit Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Use a single always block with synchronous reset
2. For taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1 (1-based indexing):
   - These bits should XOR with q[0] (feedback)
   - Other bits simply shift right
3. Reset initializes q to 32'h1
4. Key simplification:
   - Use concatenation with explicit XOR operations only for tap positions
   - Non-tap positions can be shifted directly
   - Position 1 (q[0]) always becomes the feedback (MSB XOR result)
