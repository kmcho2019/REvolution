To implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1:
1. The LFSR will shift right on each clock cycle
2. The MSB (q[31]) is always XORed with the output bit (q[0])
3. Additional taps at positions 21 (q[21]), 1 (q[1]), and 0 (q[0]) will also be XORed with q[0]
4. All other bits simply shift right
5. On synchronous reset (active high), initialize q to 32'h1
6. The XOR operations are performed in parallel for the tapped bits
