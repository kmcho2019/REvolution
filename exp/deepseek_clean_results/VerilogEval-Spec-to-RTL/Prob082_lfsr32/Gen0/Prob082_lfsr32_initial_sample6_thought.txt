To implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with specified taps:
1. Use a 32-bit register 'q' as the shift register
2. On each clock edge:
   - If reset is high, initialize q to 32'h1
   - Else, perform the Galois LFSR operation:
     * The LSB (q[0]) is always shifted out
     * For tap positions (32,22,2,1), XOR with q[0]
     * All other bits simply shift right
3. The taps are at positions 32,22,2,1 (note: in Verilog, these would be q[31], q[21], q[1], q[0] since indexing starts at 0)
4. The new MSB (q[31]) is calculated as q[31] ^ q[0]
