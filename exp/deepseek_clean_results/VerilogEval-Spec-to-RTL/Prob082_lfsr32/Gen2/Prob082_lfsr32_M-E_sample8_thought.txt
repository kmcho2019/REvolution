Novel Parallel Computation Approach:
1. Instead of calculating each bit sequentially, precompute all XOR operations in parallel
2. Use a mask to identify tap positions (bits 31, 21, 1, 0)
3. Generate a feedback mask where only tap positions are set to feedback value
4. XOR the entire register with this mask in one operation
5. Then perform the right shift in a separate step
6. This approach may reduce critical path by:
   - Parallelizing XOR operations
   - Separating the XOR and shift operations
   - Potentially allowing better synthesis optimization
7. Reset remains synchronous with same behavior
