Novel Approach: Split-Counter Architecture
1. Instead of a single 10-bit counter, use two separate counters:
   - A 3-bit counter (0-7) for the lower bits
   - A 7-bit counter (0-124) for the upper bits
2. The 7-bit counter increments only when the 3-bit counter rolls over
3. Combine the outputs to form the 10-bit count (7-bit * 8 + 3-bit)
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced power consumption as upper bits toggle less frequently
   - Simplified terminal count detection (just check both counters reach max)
   - Better timing as comparators work on smaller bit widths
   - Natural reset synchronization between counters
5. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
6. The combined count still covers 0-999 (125*8=1000 cycles)
