Novel Architecture Idea: Binary-to-BCD Conversion Counter
1. Instead of using a standard binary counter, implement a BCD (Binary Coded Decimal) counter that counts from 000 to 999
2. Use three 4-bit BCD counters (for hundreds, tens, and units digits)
3. Implement synchronous carry propagation between digits
4. Reset all digits to 0 on active high reset
5. Combine the BCD digits to form the 10-bit output (packed BCD format)
6. Advantages:
   - Natural decimal counting (matches human-readable output)
   - No need for terminal count detection logic (automatic rollover at 999)
   - Potential power savings as higher digits change less frequently
   - Clear separation of digit logic
