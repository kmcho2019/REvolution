Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Divide the counting into two stages:
   - A prescaler counter that counts every 10 cycles (0-9)
   - A main counter that increments every 10 cycles (0-99)
2. This reduces the maximum counter width from 10 bits to:
   - 4 bits for the prescaler (counts 0-9)
   - 7 bits for the main counter (counts 0-99)
3. Combine the outputs to form the final 10-bit count value
4. Benefits:
   - Smaller adders/comparators reduce critical path
   - Less switching activity in the main counter
   - Easier timing closure due to smaller bit-width operations
5. Reset synchronously resets both counters
6. Terminal count detection becomes simpler (just check if both counters reach max)
