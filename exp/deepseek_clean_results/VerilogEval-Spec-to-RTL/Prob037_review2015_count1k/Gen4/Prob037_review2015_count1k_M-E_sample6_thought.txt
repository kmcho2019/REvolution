I'll implement a hierarchical counter that uses two separate counters: one for the hundreds digit (0-9) and one for the tens/units digits (00-99). This approach reduces the maximum count value each counter needs to handle, potentially improving timing. The hundreds counter increments only when the tens/units counter wraps around. The outputs are combined to form the final 10-bit count value. This structure may offer better timing characteristics as each counter has simpler comparison logic.
