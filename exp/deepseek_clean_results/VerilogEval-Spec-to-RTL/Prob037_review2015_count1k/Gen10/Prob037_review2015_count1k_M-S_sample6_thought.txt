The solution can be further simplified by:
1. Removing the explicit comparison with 999 since the counter will naturally wrap around at 1000 due to being a 10-bit register (max value 1023)
2. Letting the counter free-run and relying on the synchronous reset for initialization
3. The wrap-around at 1000 is handled automatically by the register overflow
This reduces logic complexity while maintaining the same counting behavior from 0-999.
