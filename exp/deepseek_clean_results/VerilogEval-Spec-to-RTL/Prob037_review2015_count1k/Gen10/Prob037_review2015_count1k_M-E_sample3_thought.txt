Novel Architectural Idea: Decomposed Parallel Counter
1. Instead of a single 10-bit counter, break it down into three parallel counters:
   - A 4-bit counter for hundreds (0-9)
   - A 4-bit counter for tens (0-9)
   - A 4-bit counter for units (0-9)
2. Each counter increments independently when the lower counter rolls over
3. Benefits:
   - Smaller adders/comparators reduce critical path
   - Natural BCD representation
   - Lower power as only active counters toggle
   - Easier to pipeline if needed
4. Reset synchronously clears all counters
5. Combine outputs to form final 10-bit count value
