Novel Architecture Idea: Split Counter with Parallel Counting
1. Instead of a single 10-bit counter, split into two parallel counters:
   - A 3-bit counter for hundreds (0-9)
   - A 7-bit counter for tens/units (0-99)
2. Benefits:
   - Reduced comparator complexity (compare 3 bits and 7 bits separately)
   - Lower power consumption as only one counter increments most cycles
   - Faster terminal count detection (parallel comparisons)
   - More efficient reset handling
3. Operation:
   - The 7-bit counter increments every cycle
   - When 7-bit counter reaches 99, it resets and hundreds counter increments
   - Terminal count when hundreds=9 and tens/units=99
   - All counters reset synchronously
4. Advantages over previous solution:
   - Smaller comparators (3-bit and 7-bit vs 10-bit)
   - Less frequent toggling of hundreds counter
   - Clearer separation of digit positions
