To simplify the counter implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the explicit comparison with 999 since the counter will naturally wrap around from 999 to 0 due to 10-bit overflow (1000 in binary is 1111101000, but only lower 10 bits are kept)
2. Use a single conditional assignment that handles both reset and increment cases
3. This eliminates the need for a comparator, reducing logic complexity
4. The counter will still maintain the same period of 1000 cycles (0-999)
