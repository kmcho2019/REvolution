Novel Approach: Split Counter with Parallel Counting
1. Instead of a single 10-bit counter, split into two parallel counters:
   - A 5-bit counter (0-31) for the lower bits
   - A 5-bit counter (0-31) for the upper bits
2. The upper counter increments only when the lower counter rolls over
3. Terminal count is detected when both counters reach their maximum values (31 and 7 respectively)
4. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path by having smaller adders in parallel
   - Simplifies terminal count detection (simple AND of individual terminal counts)
   - More power efficient as upper counter toggles less frequently
5. Maintains synchronous reset functionality
