Instead of a single 10-bit counter, we'll use three separate counters:
1. A modulo-10 counter (units digit)
2. A modulo-10 counter (tens digit)
3. A modulo-10 counter (hundreds digit)

This BCD-style decomposition eliminates the need for a large 10-bit comparator. Each digit rolls over independently, and we only need simple equality checks for digit boundaries. The reset and rollover logic becomes simpler as we only need to compare single digits against 9. The output is formed by concatenating the three digits.
