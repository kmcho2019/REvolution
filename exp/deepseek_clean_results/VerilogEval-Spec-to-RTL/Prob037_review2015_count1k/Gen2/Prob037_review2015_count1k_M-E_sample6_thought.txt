Instead of using a single 10-bit counter, we can decompose the counter into three separate counters:
1. A ones counter (0-9)
2. A tens counter (0-9)
3. A hundreds counter (0-9)
This reduces the comparator complexity since each digit only needs to compare against 9. The counters cascade when they roll over, and we combine the digits to form the final 10-bit output. This approach reduces switching activity and comparator complexity while maintaining the same functionality.
