To optimize the counter for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:

1. Performance: Split the counter into 3 BCD digits (3x4-bit counters) to reduce comparator size from 10-bit to 4-bit comparisons. This reduces the critical path.

2. Power: Add clock gating for the higher digits when they don't need to increment (when lower digits aren't at max value). This reduces switching activity.

3. Area: The BCD implementation uses slightly more flops (12 vs 10) but simpler comparators, which may balance out area-wise.

The improved design:
- Maintains same functionality (0-999 count)
- Uses synchronous reset
- Has better timing due to smaller comparators
- Reduces power through clock gating
- Potentially similar or better area due to simpler logic
