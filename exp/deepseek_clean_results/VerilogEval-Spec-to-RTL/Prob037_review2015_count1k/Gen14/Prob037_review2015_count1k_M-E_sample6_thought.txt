Novel Approach: Binary to BCD Conversion Counter
1. Instead of using a standard binary counter, implement a BCD (Binary Coded Decimal) counter that counts from 000 to 999
2. Use three 4-bit BCD counters (one for each digit) that roll over independently
3. The hundreds digit increments when the tens digit rolls over from 9 to 0
4. The tens digit increments when the units digit rolls over from 9 to 0
5. This approach naturally handles the 0-999 range with clear digit boundaries
6. Benefits:
   - More intuitive visualization of decimal counting
   - Potential power savings as higher digits toggle less frequently
   - Modular design with clear digit separation
   - Same functionality but different implementation approach
