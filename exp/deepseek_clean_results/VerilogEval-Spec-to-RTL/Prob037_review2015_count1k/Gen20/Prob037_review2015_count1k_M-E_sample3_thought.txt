Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Break the 0-999 counter into three decade counters (units, tens, hundreds)
2. Each counter is a 4-bit BCD counter (0-9)
3. Cascade the counters with ripple carry logic
4. Benefits:
   - More intuitive structure matching human counting
   - Easier to modify for different counting ranges
   - Potential power savings as higher digits change less frequently
   - Clear terminal count detection (when all digits are 9)
5. Implementation:
   - Three 4-bit registers for each digit
   - Carry logic between digits
   - Synchronous reset and terminal count handling
   - Combine digits for final 10-bit output
