Novel Architectural Idea: Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) Counter
1. Instead of a pure binary counter, implement a BCD counter that counts each digit separately
2. Use three 4-bit counters for hundreds, tens, and units digits (0-9 each)
3. The hundreds counter increments only when tens and units reach 9
4. The tens counter increments only when units reach 9
5. Reset sets all digits to 0
6. Benefits:
   - Natural decimal representation
   - Simplified terminal count detection (just check for 9 in all digits)
   - Potential power savings as higher-order digits toggle less frequently
   - Clearer correspondence between counter value and human-readable numbers
