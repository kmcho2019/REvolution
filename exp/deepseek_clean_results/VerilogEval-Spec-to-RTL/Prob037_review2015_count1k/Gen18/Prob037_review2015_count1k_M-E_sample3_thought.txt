Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Implement a BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) counter instead of a binary counter
2. Use three 4-bit counters to represent each decimal digit (units, tens, hundreds)
3. Each digit rolls over independently when reaching 9
4. Benefits:
   - More human-readable counting pattern
   - Natural terminal count detection (9-9-9)
   - Potential power savings as lower digits toggle more frequently than higher ones
   - Different implementation approach from standard binary counters
5. Still maintains synchronous reset and 1000-cycle period
