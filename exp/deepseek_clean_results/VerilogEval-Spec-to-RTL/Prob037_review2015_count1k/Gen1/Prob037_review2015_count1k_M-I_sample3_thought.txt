To optimize the counter design for better PPA metrics, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Timing Optimization:
   - Replace the full 10-bit comparison (q == 10'd999) with a terminal count signal that only checks the necessary bits (999 is 1111100111 in binary)
   - This reduces the comparator size from 10 bits to effectively 8 bits (since bits 4 and 3 are always 0 in 999)

2. Power Optimization:
   - Use a registered terminal count signal to reduce switching activity in the comparator
   - This prevents the comparator from evaluating every cycle when not needed

3. Area Optimization:
   - Maintain the simple binary counter structure which is already area-efficient
   - The optimized comparator will slightly reduce area by eliminating unnecessary comparison bits

The improved design should achieve better timing (reduced WNS) while maintaining similar power and area characteristics.
