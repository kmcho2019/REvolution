I'll implement a hierarchical counter that breaks down the counting into decades (units, tens, hundreds). This approach:
1. Uses three 4-bit counters (BCD style) for each digit place
2. Implements carry propagation between digits
3. Only uses 12 flip-flops (3x4) instead of 10 for the binary counter
4. Has simpler comparison logic (just checking digit values)
5. Resets each digit independently
The advantage is simpler comparison logic and potential power savings since lower digits toggle more frequently than higher ones.
