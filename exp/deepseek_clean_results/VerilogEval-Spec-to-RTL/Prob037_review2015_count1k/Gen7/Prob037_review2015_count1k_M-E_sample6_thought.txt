1. Instead of a single 10-bit counter, implement three separate counters:
   - A 4-bit counter for units (0-9)
   - A 4-bit counter for tens (0-9)
   - A 2-bit counter for hundreds (0-9)
2. Each counter only increments when the lower counter rolls over
3. This reduces switching activity since higher-order bits toggle less frequently
4. The terminal count detection is distributed across the counters
5. Reset synchronously clears all counters
6. Combine the outputs to form the final 10-bit count
7. This approach reduces power by minimizing high-frequency bit toggles and improves timing by breaking the long carry chain
