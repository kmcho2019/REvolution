Innovative Solution Concept:
1. Dual-Port PHT: Implement separate read/write ports for concurrent prediction and training
2. Speculative History Tracking: Maintain both committed and speculative GHR states
3. Shadow PHT: Keep a shadow copy of PHT entries being trained to avoid read-after-write hazards
4. Pipeline-aware Updates: Explicitly separate prediction and training phases in the clock cycle

Key Innovations:
- Dual GHR registers (committed and speculative) for precise misprediction recovery
- Shadow PHT entries for training to prevent prediction contamination
- Explicit write-back stage for PHT updates
- Priority-aware history update logic
