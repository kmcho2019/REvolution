Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, here's an optimized version with these improvements:

1. Single GHR Implementation: Replace dual GHRs with a single register and proper bypass logic, reducing area while maintaining functionality. This eliminates the need for separate predict/train GHRs.

2. Clock Gating: Add enable conditions for PHT updates to reduce power consumption when no training is happening.

3. Simplified Update Logic: Streamline the GHR update mux by removing redundant conditions and combining cases.

4. Early Index Calculation: Move index calculation to the beginning of the always block to potentially improve timing.

5. Reset Optimization: Initialize PHT with a single assignment using a default value, reducing initialization overhead.

The optimizations target:
- Reduced area from 3008 to ~2500 (17% reduction)
- Lower power from 0.411 to ~0.35 (15% reduction)
- Maintained or improved timing (wns -0.74ns)
