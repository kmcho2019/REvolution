Novel architectural idea: Segmented Speculative GShare
1. Split the 7-bit GHR into two segments: 4-bit speculative (for in-flight predictions) and 3-bit committed (for trained branches)
2. Maintain two PHTs: one for speculative predictions and one for committed state
3. Predictions use speculative GHR and PHT, while training updates committed PHT
4. On misprediction, roll back speculative state to committed state
5. Reduces contention between predict/train paths by separating concerns
6. Allows more aggressive speculation while maintaining correct recovery

Key benefits:
- Parallel prediction and training operations with less interference
- Faster recovery from mispredictions (only need to reset speculative segment)
- Better branch pattern isolation between speculative and committed paths
- Reduced critical path for predictions (smaller speculative PHT)
