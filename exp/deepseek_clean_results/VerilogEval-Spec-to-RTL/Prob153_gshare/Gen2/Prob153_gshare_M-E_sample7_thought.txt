Here's a novel architectural approach for the gshare predictor:

1. **Dual-Ported PHT**: Implement the PHT with separate read/write ports to avoid timing conflicts between prediction and training. Predictions always see the current state while training updates occur on the next cycle.

2. **Shadow GHR for Mispredictions**: Maintain a shadow copy of the GHR that's only updated during mispredictions. This allows instant recovery without disrupting the main GHR update pipeline.

3. **Priority Encoder for Updates**: Use a clear priority scheme where:
   - Misprediction recovery has highest priority (uses shadow GHR)
   - Training updates come next
   - Prediction updates have lowest priority

4. **Early Prediction Output**: Calculate predict_taken immediately using current GHR and PHT state, while predict_history reflects the GHR state used for the prediction.

Key innovations:
- Physical separation of prediction and training paths
- Shadow GHR enables single-cycle misprediction recovery
- Clear priority handling through dedicated logic
- Maintains correct timing relationships between operations
