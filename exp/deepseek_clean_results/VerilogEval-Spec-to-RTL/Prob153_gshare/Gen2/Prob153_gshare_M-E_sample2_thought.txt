My novel approach introduces a dual-buffer architecture for the GHR to handle concurrent prediction and training operations more effectively. Here's the key innovation:

1. **Shadow GHR Register**: Maintains a separate copy of the GHR specifically for training operations. This allows clean separation between prediction and training paths.

2. **Three-Phase Update Logic**:
   - Phase 1: Predictions use current GHR and PHT state
   - Phase 2: Training updates are calculated based on shadow GHR
   - Phase 3: Misprediction recovery has absolute priority and can override both registers

3. **Atomic PHT Updates**: Implement a write-through cache style update where training writes go directly to PHT but predictions always see the pre-update state.

4. **History Forwarding**: When a misprediction occurs, we forward the corrected history to both GHR and shadow GHR immediately.

This architecture ensures:
- No timing conflicts between prediction and training
- Correct behavior during concurrent operations
- Proper misprediction recovery
- Single-cycle latency for all operations
