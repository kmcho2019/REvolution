Novel Idea: "Speculative History Update with Shadow PHT"
1. Introduce a speculative GHR that updates immediately during prediction
2. Maintain a shadow PHT that gets updated speculatively during prediction
3. On training, either commit or rollback the speculative updates based on actual branch outcome
4. Key benefits:
   - Reduces branch prediction latency by eliminating GHR update delay
   - Maintains accuracy by correcting speculative updates during training
   - Simplifies timing by making prediction path purely combinational
5. Implementation:
   - Two GHRs: speculative (for prediction) and committed (for training)
   - Two PHTs: main (committed state) and shadow (speculative state)
   - Training logic reconciles speculative and committed states
