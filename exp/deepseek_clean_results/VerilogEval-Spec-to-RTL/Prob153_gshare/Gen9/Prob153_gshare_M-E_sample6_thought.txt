Novel Architecture: "Speculative G-Share with Segmented History"
Key Innovations:
1. Segmented History Register: Splits the 7-bit GHR into 3-bit recent and 4-bit older history segments for better pattern capture
2. Speculative PHT Updates: Updates PHT speculatively during prediction to reduce training latency
3. Dual-Ported PHT with Shadow Entries: Maintains shadow copies of frequently accessed PHT entries for conflict-free training
4. History Recovery Stack: Implements a small stack to handle mispredictions without stalling the pipeline
5. Power-Optimized Indexing: Uses partial XOR to reduce switching activity

Advantages:
- Better pattern recognition with segmented history
- Reduced training latency via speculative updates
- No PHT access conflicts between predict/train paths
- Efficient misprediction recovery
- Lower power consumption
