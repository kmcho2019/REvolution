Novel Architecture: Pipelined Prediction with Shadow PHT
1. Shadow PHT Concept: Maintain two identical PHTs - one for prediction (front-end) and one for training (back-end)
2. Pipeline Synchronization: Predictions use current PHT state while training updates propagate to prediction PHT on next cycle
3. History Shadow Buffer: Store speculative history updates in a FIFO that aligns with pipeline stages
4. Misprediction Recovery: Implement a rollback mechanism that can revert both PHT and history state
5. Atomic Updates: Ensure training updates appear atomically in the prediction PHT

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates read-after-write hazards between prediction and training
- Enables true simultaneous operation without priority logic
- Simplifies timing by decoupling prediction and training paths
- Provides clean recovery from mispredictions
- Maintains architectural correctness while improving performance
