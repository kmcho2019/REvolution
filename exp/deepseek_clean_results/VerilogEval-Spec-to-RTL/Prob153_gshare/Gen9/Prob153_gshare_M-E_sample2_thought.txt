Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Speculative History Tracking: Maintain both committed and speculative GHR states to handle concurrent predict/train scenarios more efficiently
2. Delayed PHT Updates: Buffer training requests for one cycle to simplify timing and reduce critical path
3. Dual-Ported PHT: Use separate read/write ports for prediction and training to avoid conflicts
4. Early Prediction: Compute prediction index in previous cycle to reduce critical path
5. Optimized Counter Update: Use simpler saturation logic with bitwise operations

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates timing conflicts between predict/train operations
- Reduces critical path by precomputing prediction index
- Simplifies PHT update logic with delayed writes
- Maintains high prediction accuracy with speculative history
