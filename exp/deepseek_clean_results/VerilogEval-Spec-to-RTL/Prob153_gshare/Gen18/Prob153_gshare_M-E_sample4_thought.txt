Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Speculative GHR Update: Instead of waiting for training to confirm predictions, maintain two GHRs:
   - "Speculative GHR" tracks predicted branches (updated immediately)
   - "Architectural GHR" tracks confirmed branches (updated during training)
2. Dual-PHT Access: Read both current and next potential PHT states in parallel
3. Early Misprediction Recovery: When a misprediction occurs, we can immediately restore the correct GHR state
4. Priority Handling: Training always overrides speculative updates
5. Power Optimization: Only update PHT entries when training occurs

Key Benefits:
- Reduces pipeline bubbles by speculatively updating GHR
- Maintains accurate architectural state for training
- Parallel PHT access improves timing
- Clear priority handling between prediction and training
