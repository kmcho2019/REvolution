Novel Architectural Idea: "Speculative History Update with Rollback"

Key Concepts:
1. Speculative GHR Update: Update GHR immediately during prediction for lower latency, but maintain a shadow copy for rollback if misprediction occurs
2. Dual-Ported PHT: Implement separate read/write ports to allow simultaneous prediction and training
3. Training Queue: Buffer training requests to handle conflicts more gracefully
4. History Recovery Stack: Maintain a small stack of recent GHR states for faster misprediction recovery

Advantages:
- Reduces prediction latency by eliminating GHR update delay
- Handles training/prediction conflicts more elegantly
- Faster misprediction recovery with history stack
- Better timing due to separated PHT access paths
- More power efficient with conditional updates

Implementation Approach:
1. Use a dual-ported RAM for PHT (read for prediction, write for training)
2. Maintain speculative GHR and confirmed GHR
3. Small 4-entry stack for GHR recovery
4. Priority encoder for training queue handling
