Novel Architecture: "Speculative GShare with Segmented History"
1. Split the 7-bit GHR into two segments (3-bit recent, 4-bit older) for better locality
2. Implement speculative PHT updates - predict path updates PHT speculatively while train path confirms/corrects
3. Use a shadow PHT for misprediction recovery to avoid stalling pipeline
4. Add a small (8-entry) prediction cache for frequently accessed branches
5. Implement history folding for better utilization of PHT entries

Key Innovations:
- Segmented history improves pattern recognition for both short and long loops
- Speculative updates reduce prediction latency by 1 cycle
- Shadow PHT enables instant recovery from mispredictions
- Prediction cache reduces PHT access energy for hot branches
- History folding increases effective PHT size without adding entries
