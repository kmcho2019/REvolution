2. PHT Banking: Split the PHT into two 64-entry banks to reduce power consumption by only activating the needed bank during accesses.

3. Clock Gating: Add clock gating for PHT updates to prevent unnecessary switching when no training occurs.

4. Simplified Update Logic: Optimize the counter update function to use more efficient encoding and reduce LUT usage.

5. Early Mux Resolution: Restructure the GHR update logic to resolve the priority mux earlier in the timing path.

- Lower power via banking and clock gating
