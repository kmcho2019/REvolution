Novel Architectural Idea: "Decoupled Prediction-Training Pipeline with Shadow PHT"
1. Decouple prediction and training into separate pipeline stages
2. Maintain two PHTs: main PHT for predictions and shadow PHT for training updates
3. Use a FIFO to track in-flight predictions and apply training updates to shadow PHT
4. Periodically merge shadow PHT updates into main PHT when no conflicts exist
5. Implement GHR as a circular buffer with commit/rollback capability

Key Advantages:
- Eliminates prediction-training conflicts by physical separation
- Training updates don't stall predictions
- Shadow PHT allows speculative training updates
- Circular GHR reduces update complexity
- Better timing due to decoupled operations
- More accurate predictions through non-blocking training
