Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Segmented History Register: Split the 7-bit GHR into two parts (4+3 bits) to reduce aliasing and improve prediction accuracy for different branch types.
2. Speculative PHT Updates: Perform PHT updates speculatively during prediction to hide training latency, then confirm/cancel based on actual training.
3. Dual-Ported PHT: Use a dual-ported memory structure to allow concurrent read (prediction) and write (training) operations.
4. History Recovery Cache: Small buffer to store recent GHR states for quick misprediction recovery without stalling.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced aliasing from segmented history
- Lower prediction latency via speculative updates
- Better handling of concurrent predict/train operations
- Faster misprediction recovery
