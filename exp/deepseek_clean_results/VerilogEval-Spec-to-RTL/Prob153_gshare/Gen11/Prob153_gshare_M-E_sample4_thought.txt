Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Speculative History Update: Instead of waiting for training to update the GHR, we maintain two copies - one speculative (for predictions) and one confirmed (from training). This allows faster feedback loops.
2. Two-Level Prediction: First level uses standard gshare, second level adds a small buffer of recent misprediction patterns to improve accuracy.
3. Early Training: Begin training counters immediately after prediction, before actual outcome is known, using predicted outcome as initial training.
4. Priority-based Update: Clear priority scheme where training updates override speculative updates.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced misprediction penalty through speculative updates
- Improved accuracy with two-level prediction
- Faster adaptation to branch patterns
- Maintains single-cycle prediction latency
