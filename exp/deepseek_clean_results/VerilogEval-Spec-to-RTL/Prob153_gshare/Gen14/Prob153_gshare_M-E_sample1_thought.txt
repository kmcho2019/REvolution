Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Segmented History Register: Split the 7-bit GHR into two parts (3-bit recent + 4-bit older) to better capture both short-term and long-term patterns
2. Banked PHT: Divide the PHT into 4 banks (32 entries each) accessed by different PC bits to reduce access conflicts
3. Early Prediction: Compute prediction index in previous cycle to reduce critical path
4. Lazy PHT Update: Only update PHT entries when they would actually change (not at saturation)
5. History Shadow Register: Maintain a speculative GHR for predictions while training uses the committed GHR

Key Benefits:
- Reduced bank conflicts between prediction and training
- Shorter critical path by pre-computing prediction index
- Lower power from lazy updates and banked structure
- Better pattern capture with segmented history
