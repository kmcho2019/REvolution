Novel Architecture: Segmented Dynamic-Hash GShare (SDGShare)
1. Segmented History Register: Split the 7-bit GHR into 3 segments (2-2-3 bits) for better pattern capture
2. Dynamic Hash Selection: Use PC bits to dynamically select which segments to XOR for index generation
3. Dual-Phase Update: Separate PHT updates into speculative (prediction) and confirmed (training) phases
4. History Recovery Cache: Small buffer to store intermediate GHR states for misprediction recovery
5. Power-Optimized Access: Banked PHT structure with gated access based on prediction/training activity

Key Advantages:
- Better pattern capture through segmented history
- Dynamic hash adapts to PC patterns
- Reduced aliasing from selective segment XORing
- Faster misprediction recovery with history cache
- Lower power from banked PHT access
