Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Pipelined Prediction: Split the prediction into two stages (index calculation and PHT lookup) to improve clock frequency
2. Speculative History Update: Maintain two GHR copies - one for current predictions and one speculative for future updates
3. Early Misprediction Recovery: Add a bypass path to quickly correct the speculative GHR when mispredictions are detected
4. Banked PHT: Split the PHT into two banks (odd/even indices) to allow concurrent read/write operations
5. Precomputed Training: Calculate training index in parallel with PHT lookup to reduce critical path

Key Benefits:
- Higher clock frequency due to pipelining
- Reduced penalty from mispredictions via speculative updates
- Better throughput with banked PHT structure
- Lower power from gated clock domains for inactive banks
