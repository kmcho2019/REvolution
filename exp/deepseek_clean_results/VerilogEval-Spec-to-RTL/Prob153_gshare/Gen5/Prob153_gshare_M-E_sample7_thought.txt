Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Speculative GHR Update: Update the GHR immediately during prediction (speculatively) while maintaining a shadow GHR for misprediction recovery
2. Dual-port PHT: Separate read/write ports for prediction and training to avoid conflicts
3. Early Training: Process training requests in parallel with predictions to minimize pipeline bubbles
4. History Shadow Buffer: Maintain a small buffer of recent GHR states for quick recovery on mispredictions

Key Benefits:
- Reduces prediction latency by eliminating GHR update from critical path
- Enables parallel prediction and training operations
- Faster misprediction recovery using shadow buffer
- Maintains correct timing semantics while improving performance
