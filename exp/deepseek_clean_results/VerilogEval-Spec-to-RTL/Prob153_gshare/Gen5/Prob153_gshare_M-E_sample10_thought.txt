Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Speculative GHR Update: Instead of waiting for training feedback, update GHR speculatively during prediction and maintain a shadow GHR for recovery on mispredictions.
2. Two-Level Prediction: Use a secondary PHT that tracks patterns in the GHR itself, combining with primary PHT via majority vote.
3. Early Training: Begin training entries as soon as predictions are made, reducing training latency.
4. Banked PHT Structure: Split PHT into two banks (odd/even indices) to allow simultaneous predict+train to different banks.

Key Benefits:
- Reduces misprediction penalty by speculating GHR updates
- Improves accuracy with two-level prediction
- Enables more parallel operations with banked structure
- Maintains single-cycle prediction latency
