Here's a novel approach using a speculative GHR (Global History Register) to handle the prediction/training conflict:

1. **Dual GHR System**: Maintain two GHRs - one for current predictions (speculative_ghr) and one for training recovery (committed_ghr). This separates the prediction and training paths.

2. **Speculative Updates**: Predictions update the speculative GHR immediately, while training updates affect the committed GHR. On misprediction, we restore the speculative GHR from the committed one.

3. **PHT Shadowing**: Use a shadow PHT that gets updated one cycle after training, ensuring predictions see the pre-training state when they occur simultaneously.

4. **Priority Handling**: Training always takes precedence - when both occur, we use the training GHR for PHT access and update both GHRs based on training.

5. **Efficient Recovery**: Mispredictions trigger an immediate GHR recovery from the training history, while normal training updates both GHRs in sync.

Key innovations:
- Eliminates prediction/training conflicts via dual GHRs
- Maintains correct timing with shadow PHT
- Clear separation of speculative and committed state
- Efficient recovery mechanism
