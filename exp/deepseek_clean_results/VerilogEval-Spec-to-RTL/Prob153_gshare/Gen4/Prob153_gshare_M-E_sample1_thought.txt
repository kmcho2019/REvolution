Novel Architectural Idea: "Shadow GHR with Delayed PHT Update"

Key Concepts:
1. Dual GHR System: Maintain two GHRs - one for predictions (predict_ghr) and one for training (train_ghr)
2. Shadow Update: Training updates go to train_ghr immediately, while predict_ghr follows one cycle later
3. Delayed PHT Writes: Buffer training requests and apply PHT updates one cycle later to match spec timing
4. Priority Resolution: Training always takes precedence by updating train_ghr immediately
5. Misprediction Recovery: train_ghr is restored from train_history on mispredictions

Advantages:
- Clear separation of prediction and training paths
- Natural handling of concurrent predict/train operations
- Guaranteed timing compliance with PHT updates
- Simplified priority handling through architectural separation
- Efficient misprediction recovery through dedicated train_ghr
