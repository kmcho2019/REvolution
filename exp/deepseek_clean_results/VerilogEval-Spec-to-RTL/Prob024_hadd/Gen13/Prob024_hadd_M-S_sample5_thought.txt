The half adder implementation can't be simplified further while maintaining correct functionality because:
1. The sum (XOR) and carry-out (AND) operations are the minimal logical expressions needed for a half adder
2. Any attempt to combine or reduce these operations would either:
   - Lose functionality (incorrect results)
   - Actually increase complexity by requiring more operations
3. The current implementation uses the fewest possible logic gates (1 XOR and 1 AND)
4. Continuous assignments are already the simplest way to express combinational logic in Verilog
