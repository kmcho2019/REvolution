An alternative implementation of a half adder can be achieved using a multiplexer-based approach. The sum output can be viewed as selecting between the inputs based on their values: when a and b are different, sum is 1; when they're same, sum is 0. The carry-out is simply the case when both inputs are 1. This can be implemented using a 2:1 mux for the sum and a simple AND gate for carry-out.
