The simplest implementation of a half adder uses direct bitwise operations:
1. Sum is calculated using XOR (a ^ b)
2. Carry-out is calculated using AND (a & b)
3. No need for procedural blocks or case statements
4. Continuous assignments provide the most straightforward and efficient implementation
5. This matches exactly the textbook definition of a half adder
6. Will synthesize to minimal gates (1 XOR and 1 AND)
