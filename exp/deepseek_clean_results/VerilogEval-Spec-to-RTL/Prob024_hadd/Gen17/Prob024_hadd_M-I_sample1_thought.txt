To optimize the half adder implementation for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), we should:

1. Replace the mux-based implementation with direct gate-level logic:
   - Sum output can be implemented with a single XOR gate (sum = a ^ b)
   - Carry output can be implemented with a single AND gate (cout = a & b)

2. Benefits of this approach:
   - Reduced power consumption: Eliminates the need for muxes and inverter
   - Improved performance: XOR and AND gates typically have lower propagation delay than mux-based implementations
   - Smaller area: Basic gates require fewer transistors than muxes
   - Simpler and more straightforward implementation

3. Expected improvements:
   - Lower power due to reduced switching activity
   - Better timing due to simpler critical path
   - Smaller area from using primitive gates
   - More standard implementation that matches typical half adder designs
