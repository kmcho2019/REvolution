To optimize the half adder implementation for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA), we should:
1. Replace the mux-based implementation with direct gate-level logic
2. Use XOR for sum calculation (sum = a ^ b)
3. Use AND for carry-out calculation (cout = a & b)
4. Eliminate unnecessary intermediate signals and modules
5. Benefit from simpler logic structure with fewer gates

This optimization will:
- Reduce power consumption by eliminating redundant logic
- Improve performance with shorter critical path (just one gate delay)
- Decrease area by using minimal primitive gates
- Maintain correct functionality while being more efficient
