Simplification Strategy:
1. The half adder can be implemented with just two basic logic operations: XOR for sum and AND for carry-out
2. Using direct bitwise operations is clearer and more efficient than using addition operation for this simple case
3. Remove unnecessary complexity by avoiding the concatenation operation used in the previous solution
4. Maintain minimal gate count (1 XOR + 1 AND) for optimal area and power
5. Keep the implementation straightforward and easy to understand
