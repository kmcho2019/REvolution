This innovative approach uses a multiplexer to compute the sum and a clever carry generation method that exploits the observation that carry occurs only when both inputs are 1. The key innovations are:
1. Sum is calculated using a 2:1 mux controlled by 'b', selecting between 'a' and its complement
2. Carry is generated by detecting the '11' case through a NOR of the inverted inputs
3. Uses only 2 logic levels (inversion + mux/NOR) for both outputs
4. Demonstrates alternative logic decomposition that might be beneficial in certain technology libraries
5. Shows equivalent functionality through different logical relationships
