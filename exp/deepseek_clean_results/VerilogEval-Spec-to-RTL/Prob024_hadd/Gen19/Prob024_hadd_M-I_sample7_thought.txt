To optimize the half adder implementation for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I propose the following improvements:

1. Remove the intermediate gate modules (XOR_Gate and AND_Gate) and implement the logic directly in TopModule:
   - Eliminates hierarchy overhead, reducing potential propagation delays
   - Reduces area by removing module instantiation overhead
   - Maintains the same logical functionality

2. Use continuous assignments for both sum and cout:
   - Keeps the implementation simple and straightforward
   - Ensures minimal gate count (still just XOR and AND operations)
   - Makes the design more compact and easier to read

3. Benefits:
   - Potentially better timing due to reduced hierarchy
   - Slightly better area utilization
   - Maintains the same low power characteristics
   - Simpler verification and synthesis flow

This optimized version should achieve the same functionality while being more efficient in terms of PPA metrics.
