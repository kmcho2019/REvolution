The simplest and most efficient way to implement a half adder is to use basic logic gates:
1. Sum is the XOR of inputs a and b (sum = a ^ b)
2. Carry-out is the AND of inputs a and b (cout = a & b)
This approach:
- Uses only two basic gates (XOR and AND)
- Eliminates the need for multiplexers and inverters
- Matches the standard textbook implementation of a half adder
- Provides optimal power, performance, and area characteristics
