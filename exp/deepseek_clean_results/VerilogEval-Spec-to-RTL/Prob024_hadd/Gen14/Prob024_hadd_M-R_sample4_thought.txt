Here's a refactored version that maintains the same MUX-based logic but:
1. Uses continuous assignments with explicit XOR and AND operations
2. Separates the logic into distinct assign statements for clarity
3. Shows an alternative way to express the same functionality without conditional operators
4. Makes the XOR relationship more explicit while keeping the MUX concept
5. Could potentially map better to certain standard cell libraries

The key changes are:
- Expressing sum as a direct XOR operation (which is equivalent to the MUX implementation)
- Keeping cout as a simple AND operation
- This version might be more intuitive for readers familiar with basic gate-level operations
