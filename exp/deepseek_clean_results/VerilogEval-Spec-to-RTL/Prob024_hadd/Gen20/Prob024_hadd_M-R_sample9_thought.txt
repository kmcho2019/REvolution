Refactoring Approach:
1. Maintain the same logical functionality (XOR for sum, AND for cout)
2. Simplify the hierarchy by removing separate gate modules
3. Use direct continuous assignments in the top module
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced hierarchical overhead improves power efficiency
   - Simplified structure makes the design more readable
   - Maintains optimal timing characteristics
   - Easier for synthesis tools to optimize
5. The refactored version will be functionally identical but with:
   - Flatter hierarchy
   - More concise implementation
   - Better power characteristics
   - Same area and timing performance
