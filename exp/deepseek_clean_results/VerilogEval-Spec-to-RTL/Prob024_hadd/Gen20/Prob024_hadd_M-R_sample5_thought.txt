Refactoring Approach:
1. Maintain the same half-adder functionality (XOR for sum, AND for cout)
2. Simplify the hierarchy by removing separate gate modules
3. Use direct continuous assignments in the top module
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced hierarchical overhead improves power efficiency
   - Simpler structure while maintaining same functionality
   - Easier for synthesis tools to optimize
   - More readable as all logic is in one place
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Less modular than original (though appropriate for this simple case)
   - Still maintains clear arithmetic intent
   - Better PPA characteristics expected due to flattened structure
