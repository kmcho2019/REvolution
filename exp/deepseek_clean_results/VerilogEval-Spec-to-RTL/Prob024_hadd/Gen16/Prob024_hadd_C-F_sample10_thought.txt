Fusion Strategy:
1. Take the explicit gate-level implementation from Example 2 (XOR for sum, AND for cout) for its simplicity and efficiency
2. Incorporate the modular approach from Example 1 by creating separate gate modules
3. Benefits:
   - Maintains the optimal PPA characteristics of direct gate implementation
   - Provides better modularity and reusability of gate components
   - Clear separation of logic functions makes the design more maintainable
   - Still maintains the simplicity and efficiency of the original gate-level solution
4. This hybrid approach gives us the best of both worlds: efficient primitive operations with clean modular structure
