The simplest and most efficient way to implement a half adder is by using basic logic gates:
1. Sum output is the XOR of inputs a and b (sum = a ^ b)
2. Carry output is the AND of inputs a and b (cout = a & b)
This approach:
- Uses minimal logic gates (just 1 XOR and 1 AND)
- Has no unnecessary intermediate signals
- Matches the canonical definition of a half adder
- Provides optimal power, performance, and area characteristics
