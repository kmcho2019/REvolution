The new architecture employs:
1. Dual history registers (main and speculative) to separate prediction updates from training updates
2. The speculative register handles ongoing predictions while the main register holds confirmed history
3. On misprediction, we roll back by combining the main history with actual branch outcome
4. This reduces critical path by separating prediction and training paths
5. The design naturally handles concurrent predict/update cases without priority logic
6. Asynchronous reset initializes both registers
