Novel Architectural Idea: "Dual-Phase History Update with Predictive Shift"

This approach separates the history update into two distinct phases:
1. Prediction Phase: Maintains a speculative history that gets updated immediately with predictions
2. Validation Phase: Holds a confirmed history that only updates when predictions are validated

Key Innovations:
- Uses two separate registers: speculative_history (for immediate updates) and confirmed_history (for validated state)
- On misprediction, rolls back to confirmed_history + actual outcome
- Eliminates the need for storing intermediate prediction states
- Provides better timing by separating the prediction and validation paths
- Reduces power by minimizing unnecessary updates to the confirmed history

Advantages:
- Clean separation of speculative and confirmed states
- Reduced critical path compared to reconstruction approaches
- Lower power consumption as confirmed history updates less frequently
- Simpler rollback logic using confirmed history as anchor point
