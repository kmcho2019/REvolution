Simplified design approach:
1. Remove unnecessary segmentation of the 32-bit shift operation - let synthesis tools optimize this naturally
2. Eliminate the reg_enable signal since it adds complexity without clear benefit
3. Use straightforward concatenation for both misprediction and prediction cases
4. Maintain the priority of misprediction over prediction
5. Keep the asynchronous reset functionality

Key simplifications:
- Single concatenation operation for both update cases
- Direct use of conditions in always block without intermediate enable signal
- Cleaner priority handling with if-else structure
