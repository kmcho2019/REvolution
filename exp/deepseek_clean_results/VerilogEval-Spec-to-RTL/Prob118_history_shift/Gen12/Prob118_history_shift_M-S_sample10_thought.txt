Simplified approach using a single register with conditional updates:
1. Use only one 32-bit history register to store the current state
2. On misprediction (highest priority), reconstruct history directly from train_history and train_taken
3. On prediction (lower priority), shift in predict_taken
4. Eliminate shadow_history since we can directly use train_history for rollback
5. Maintain same functionality with reduced area and power
6. Reset clears the single register
7. Output directly connects to the history register
