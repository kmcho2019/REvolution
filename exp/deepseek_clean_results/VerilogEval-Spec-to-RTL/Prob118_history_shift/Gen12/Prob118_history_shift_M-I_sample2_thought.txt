To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:
1. Replace the circular buffer with a simple 32-bit shift register to reduce area and power
2. Eliminate the separate saved_history register and pointer logic
3. Handle misprediction by directly loading train_history concatenated with train_taken
4. Maintain predict_valid updates as simple shifts when no misprediction occurs
5. Keep the asynchronous reset functionality
6. Make misprediction handling take precedence as specified
This reduces storage from 101 bits to just 32 bits, eliminates pointer arithmetic, and simplifies the data path.
