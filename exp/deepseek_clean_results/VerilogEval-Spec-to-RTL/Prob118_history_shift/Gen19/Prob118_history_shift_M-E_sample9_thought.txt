Novel Circular Buffer Architecture:
1. Instead of shifting bits, maintain a circular buffer with head/tail pointers
2. Use a write pointer to track the current insertion point (youngest branch)
3. On misprediction, directly load the buffer from train_history and append train_taken
4. For normal predictions, simply overwrite the oldest entry with new prediction
5. Benefits:
   - Eliminates large shift operations (better timing)
   - Reduces switching activity (only 1 bit changes per prediction)
   - Simplifies misprediction recovery (direct pointer manipulation)
   - More power efficient (only active bits change)
6. Tradeoff: Slightly more complex control logic
