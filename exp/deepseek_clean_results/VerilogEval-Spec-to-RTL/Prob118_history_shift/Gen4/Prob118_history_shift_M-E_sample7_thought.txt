Here's a novel approach using a two-stage history buffer to optimize for misprediction recovery:

1. Maintain two history registers:
   - current_history: tracks the actual committed branch history
   - speculative_history: tracks in-flight predictions

2. On prediction:
   - Only update speculative_history by shifting in predict_taken
   - current_history remains unchanged until branch resolution

3. On misprediction:
   - Roll back to the known good state (current_history)
   - Then apply the correct branch outcome (train_taken)

4. On normal operation (no misprediction):
   - Merge speculative updates into current_history
   - This happens automatically as branches retire correctly

This approach reduces unnecessary updates to the committed history and provides faster misprediction recovery since we always maintain a clean checkpoint. The speculative history can be quickly discarded on misprediction without complex rollback logic.
