Instead of a single history register, we'll implement:
1. A main history register (committed_history) that always reflects correct state
2. A speculative history register (spec_history) for in-flight predictions
3. On prediction: update spec_history speculatively
4. On misprediction: restore committed_history and apply correct update
5. This reduces critical path by separating speculative and committed updates
6. Also enables power savings by only updating committed_history when needed
