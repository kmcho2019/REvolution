Novel Dual-Buffer Architecture:
1. Maintain two history registers - one for committed state (safe_history) and one for speculative updates (spec_history)
2. On prediction: Update spec_history with predict_taken while keeping safe_history unchanged
3. On misprediction: Rollback by copying safe_history and appending train_taken to both registers
4. On commit (no misprediction): Copy spec_history to safe_history
5. Benefits:
   - Clear separation between speculative and committed state
   - More natural handling of rollbacks
   - Potential for better timing as critical path is split
   - Easier to extend for out-of-order execution scenarios
