Instead of a single history register, implement a dual-buffer system:
1. Main history buffer (committed state)
2. Speculative history buffer (uncommitted predictions)
3. On prediction: update speculative buffer only
4. On misprediction: merge train_history with train_taken into main buffer
5. On clock edge: commit speculative to main if no misprediction
6. This reduces critical path by separating prediction and training paths
7. Also enables power savings by gating speculative updates

Key advantages:
- Parallel prediction and training paths improve timing
- Reduced switching activity when no predictions occur
- Clean separation of speculative and committed states
- Easier to extend for more complex prediction schemes
