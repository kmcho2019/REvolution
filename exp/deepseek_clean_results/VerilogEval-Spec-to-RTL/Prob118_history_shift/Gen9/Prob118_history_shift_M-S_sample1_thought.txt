Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove unnecessary optimizations that complicate the design
2. Maintain core functionality with minimal logic:
   - Asynchronous reset
   - Misprediction recovery (highest priority)
   - Normal prediction update
3. Direct output assignment
4. Single always block for sequential logic
5. No additional signals or clock gating to keep it simple

The simplified version focuses on:
- Clear priority handling (misprediction > prediction)
- Basic shift operations
- Minimal control logic
While removing:
- Any speculative optimizations
- Unnecessary intermediate signals
- Complex power/area optimizations that aren't critical
