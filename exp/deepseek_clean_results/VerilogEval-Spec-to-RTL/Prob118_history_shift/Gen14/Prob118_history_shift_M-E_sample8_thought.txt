Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Dual-register approach: Maintain both current and previous history states to enable instant rollback without complex concatenation
2. Shadow register concept: The 'previous_history' register always holds the state before any prediction update
3. Simplified update logic: 
   - On prediction: current_history shifts, previous_history holds current value
   - On misprediction: instantly restore from previous_history + train_taken
4. Power optimization: Only update registers when needed (clock gating conceptually)
5. Timing improvement: Eliminate concatenation operation from critical path
6. Area tradeoff: Additional 32-bit register for faster recovery

Key Benefits:
- Faster misprediction recovery (single cycle)
- Simplified critical path (no wide mux for concatenation)
- Clean separation of prediction and recovery paths
- More intuitive state management
- Better timing closure potential
