The new architecture employs:
1. Dual history buffers (main and shadow) to separate prediction and correction paths
2. The main buffer handles normal prediction updates
3. The shadow buffer maintains a copy that can be quickly swapped in on misprediction
4. Reduced critical path by parallelizing prediction and correction operations
5. Clock gating for the main buffer when no prediction is being made
6. Simplified mux logic by using buffer swapping instead of complex concatenation
