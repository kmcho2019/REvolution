Novel Architectural Idea: "Rollback Buffer with History Reconstruction"

Instead of maintaining a shadow register, we can implement a more efficient rollback mechanism by:
1. Using a single history register (current_history) for normal operation
2. Storing only the necessary reconstruction information (train_taken) during predictions
3. Reconstructing the correct history on misprediction by combining:
   - The older history bits from train_history (provided by the pipeline)
   - The actual branch outcome (train_taken)
   - The newer history bits that were correctly predicted after the mispredicted branch

Key Advantages:
- Eliminates the shadow register entirely, saving area
- Still maintains correct rollback functionality
- Power efficient as no redundant updates are needed
- Simple priority logic remains unchanged
- Reconstruction happens in a single cycle when needed
