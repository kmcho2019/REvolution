Novel Dual-Buffer Architecture:
1. Maintain two history registers - one for committed state (safe_history) and one for speculative updates (spec_history)
2. On prediction: Update spec_history while keeping safe_history unchanged
3. On misprediction: Rollback by copying safe_history and applying the correct branch outcome
4. On idle cycles: Synchronize spec_history to safe_history when no predictions are pending
5. Benefits:
   - Eliminates critical path from misprediction handling (no need to reconstruct history)
   - Enables better power gating opportunities (speculative updates can be isolated)
   - Provides cleaner separation between committed and speculative states
   - Reduces fanout on the history register compared to single-register designs
