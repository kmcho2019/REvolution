The design requires a 32-bit shift register for branch history with two main operations:
1. Normal prediction update: shift in predict_taken when predict_valid is high
2. Misprediction recovery: restore history from train_history and append train_taken when train_mispredicted is high

Key points:
- Misprediction has higher priority than normal prediction
- Asynchronous reset clears the history
- Output predict_history always reflects current history register value
- On misprediction, new history is {train_history[30:0], train_taken}
