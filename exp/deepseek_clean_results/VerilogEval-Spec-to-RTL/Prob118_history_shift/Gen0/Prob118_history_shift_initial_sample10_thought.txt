The design requires a 32-bit shift register to maintain branch history. The key aspects are:
1. Asynchronous reset (areset) sets history to 0
2. On misprediction (train_mispredicted), load history with {train_history, train_taken}
3. On prediction (predict_valid), shift in predict_taken (unless misprediction occurs)
4. Output current history at predict_history

Priority handling:
- Reset has highest priority
- Misprediction takes precedence over normal prediction
- Normal prediction happens only if no misprediction
