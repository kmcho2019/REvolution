The new architecture employs two history registers:
1. Main history register - holds committed branch history
2. Speculative history register - tracks in-flight predictions

Key features:
- Speculative updates happen immediately to the speculative register on predictions
- Mispredictions trigger a rollback from the main register
- Committed updates (non-mispredicted predictions) are merged from speculative to main register
- This reduces critical path by separating prediction and misprediction handling
- Power savings by only updating main register when needed
- Reduced multiplexer complexity compared to single-register approach
