Here's a novel approach using a dual-buffer system to handle mispredictions more efficiently:

1. Instead of a single history register, maintain two buffers:
   - current_history: tracks the actual committed branch history
   - speculative_history: tracks in-flight predictions

2. Normal operation:
   - Update speculative_history by shifting in predict_taken
   - current_history remains unchanged

3. Misprediction recovery:
   - Discard speculative_history
   - Update current_history with the correct history (train_history + train_taken)

4. Output selection:
   - predict_history always shows speculative_history during normal operation
   - During misprediction, it shows the newly updated current_history

This approach reduces critical path by separating the committed and speculative histories, allowing parallel updates. The misprediction recovery becomes simpler as we just need to discard the speculative buffer.
