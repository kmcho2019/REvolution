Innovative Pipeline Architecture with Speculative History Tracking:

1. Dual-Register Approach:
   - Maintain two history registers: committed_history (stable) and speculative_history (in-flight)
   - committed_history reflects known-good state
   - speculative_history tracks predicted branches not yet verified

2. Parallel Update Logic:
   - Prediction updates only affect speculative_history
   - Misprediction correction updates both registers atomically
   - This allows prediction and training to operate in parallel when not conflicting

3. Key Advantages:
   - Reduced critical path: prediction updates don't wait for training validation
   - Better power efficiency: committed_history only updates on mispredictions
   - Clean separation of concerns between speculative and committed state

4. Operation:
   - Reset clears both registers
   - Predictions shift into speculative_history
   - Mispredictions update both registers with corrected history
   - Output always shows speculative_history (most recent predictions)
   - Committed_history serves as rollback point
