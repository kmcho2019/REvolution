Here's a novel approach using a circular buffer implementation for the history register:
1. Instead of shifting bits, maintain a pointer to track the "head" of the history
2. Use a write pointer to handle new entries
3. For mispredictions, directly load the train_history and append train_taken
4. This reduces shift operations to pointer updates, potentially saving power
5. The circular nature allows efficient rollback without full register shifts
6. Output generation becomes a simple indexed read operation
7. Reset initializes both pointers and clears the buffer
