Novel approach using a two-stage history register with rollback capability:
1. Maintain two 32-bit registers: current_history and shadow_history
2. On prediction (predict_valid), update current_history by shifting in predict_taken
3. On misprediction (train_mispredicted), reconstruct correct history by:
   - Using shadow_history which stores the pre-prediction state
   - Concatenating with actual train_taken
4. Shadow_history always tracks the state before any prediction update
5. This eliminates need for circular buffers while maintaining single-cycle rollback
6. Reset clears both registers
7. Output always reflects current_history
