Simplified Single-Register Design:
1. Use a single history register to reduce area and power
2. Prioritize misprediction handling over prediction updates
3. On misprediction: directly load the corrected history (train_history + train_taken)
4. On prediction: shift in predict_taken when no misprediction occurs
5. Reset clears the history register
6. Output always reflects current history state
This approach eliminates the dual-register overhead while maintaining correct functionality by properly prioritizing misprediction handling.
