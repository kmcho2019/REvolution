The current implementation is already optimal in terms of area and timing, but we can slightly improve power consumption by using an assign statement instead of an always block. This change reduces the overhead of the always block sensitivity list while maintaining the same functionality. The assign statement is more concise and typically results in slightly lower power consumption in synthesis.

Key improvements:
1. Replace always block with continuous assign statement
2. Maintain same XNOR functionality
3. Reduce power by eliminating unnecessary always block overhead
4. Keep minimal area (still just one XNOR gate)
5. Maintain zero timing slack (no critical path)
