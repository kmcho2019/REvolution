The waveform shows that z is 1 in three cases:
1. When both x and y are 0
2. When both x and y are 1
3. After a pattern where x was 1 and y was 0, followed by x 0 and y 1, then both become 1

This suggests a stateful behavior where the module remembers previous inputs. We can implement this as a simple state machine that tracks the last two input pairs and outputs z=1 when either:
- Current inputs are equal (00 or 11)
- The sequence was 10 -> 01 -> 11
