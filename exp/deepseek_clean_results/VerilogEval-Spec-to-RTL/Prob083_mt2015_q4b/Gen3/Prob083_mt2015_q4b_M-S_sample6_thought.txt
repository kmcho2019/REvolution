The waveform shows that the output z is 1 in two cases:
1. When both inputs x and y are 0 (XNOR behavior)
2. When both inputs x and y are 1 (XNOR behavior)

For all other input combinations (x=1,y=0 and x=0,y=1), z is 0. This matches exactly with an XNOR gate behavior. Therefore, the simplest and most optimal implementation is a direct XNOR operation between the inputs.
