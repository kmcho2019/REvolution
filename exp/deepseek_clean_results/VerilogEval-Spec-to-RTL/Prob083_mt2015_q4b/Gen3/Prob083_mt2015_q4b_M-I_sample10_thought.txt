The current XNOR implementation is indeed optimal for this simple combinational logic. The only potential improvements would be:

1. Using alternative XNOR implementations that might better map to specific standard cell libraries:
   - Option 1: z = (x & y) | (~x & ~y)
   - Option 2: z = ~(x ^ y) (current implementation)

2. For some technologies, the first option might have better PPA due to:
   - Better mapping to available standard cells
   - Potential for sharing inverted signals if used elsewhere
   - More balanced rise/fall times

However, for such a simple function, the improvements would be marginal. The current implementation is already optimal in most cases.
