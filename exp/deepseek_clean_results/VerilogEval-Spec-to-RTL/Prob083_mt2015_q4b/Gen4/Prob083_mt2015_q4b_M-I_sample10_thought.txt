The current implementation correctly implements an XNOR function but can be optimized for better PPA metrics. The improvement strategy is:

1. Use a direct XNOR operator (~^) which:
   - Typically maps to a single standard cell in most libraries
   - Reduces the logic depth from 2 levels to 1
   - May have better power characteristics due to fewer internal nodes
   - Likely has smaller area as it's a single primitive

2. This change should:
   - Maintain the same functionality
   - Reduce power consumption (fewer gates switching)
   - Potentially reduce area (single gate vs compound gates)
   - Improve timing (single level logic)
   - Maintain zero timing violations
