The waveform shows that the output z is 1 in three cases:
1. When both x and y are 0 (z=1)
2. When both x and y are 1 (z=1)
3. Otherwise z is 0

This behavior matches an XNOR gate, where z is the logical equivalence of x and y (z = ~(x ^ y)). The XNOR gate outputs 1 when both inputs are equal and 0 when they are different.
