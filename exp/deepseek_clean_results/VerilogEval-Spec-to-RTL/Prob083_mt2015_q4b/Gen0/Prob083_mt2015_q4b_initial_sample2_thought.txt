From the waveform, we can observe that the output z is 1 in three cases:
1. When both x and y are 0
2. When both x and y are 1
3. When x is 0 and y is 1, but only for the first cycle after a change (this doesn't match the waveform)
However, looking closely at the waveform, z is actually 1 only when x and y are equal (either both 0 or both 1). This matches an XNOR gate behavior where z = ~(x ^ y).
