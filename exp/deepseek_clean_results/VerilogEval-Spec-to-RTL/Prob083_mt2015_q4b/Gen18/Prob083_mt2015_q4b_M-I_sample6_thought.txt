The current XNOR implementation is already optimal for this simple logic function. However, we can explore alternative implementations that might offer marginal improvements in specific PPA metrics:

1. **Alternative Logic Expression**: Using an OR of ANDs (z = (x&y) | (~x&~y)) might map better to certain standard cell libraries
2. **Transistor-level Optimization**: While not applicable at RTL, this would be considered during physical design
3. **Technology Mapping**: Let the synthesis tool choose the most optimal standard cell implementation

The simplest and most robust approach is to maintain the current XNOR implementation since:
- It's the most direct representation of the function
- Synthesis tools are excellent at mapping this to optimal standard cells
- Any alternative would likely result in identical implementation after synthesis
- The current metrics show perfect timing and minimal area/power
