The given waveform shows that the output z is 1 when both inputs are equal (either both 0 or both 1), and 0 when they are different. This is exactly the behavior of an XNOR gate. The simplest possible implementation is indeed a single XNOR operation, which can be written either as ~(x ^ y) or as x ~^ y in Verilog. Both forms are equally optimal in terms of PPA metrics.

The solution cannot be simplified further because:
1. It already uses the minimal logic operation (single XNOR)
2. It maps directly to a single standard cell
3. Any attempt to "simplify" would either be functionally equivalent or would break the required behavior
