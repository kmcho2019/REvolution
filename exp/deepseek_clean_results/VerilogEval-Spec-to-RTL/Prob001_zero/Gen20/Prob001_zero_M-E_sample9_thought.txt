Here's a novel approach that uses a clocked register to maintain the zero output. While this is less efficient than a direct assignment, it demonstrates:
1. Using sequential logic for a constant output
2. Initializing a flip-flop to maintain state
3. Showing how even simple outputs can be implemented with registers

The register will be initialized to 0 and never change, effectively creating a constant LOW output through sequential logic. This is intentionally over-engineered to demonstrate an alternative implementation method.
