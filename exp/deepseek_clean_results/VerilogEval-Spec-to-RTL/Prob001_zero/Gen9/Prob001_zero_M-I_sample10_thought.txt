The original solution uses unnecessary sequential logic (a flip-flop) to generate a constant LOW output, which is inefficient in terms of power, area, and timing. We can optimize this by:
1. Removing the flip-flop and clock infrastructure completely
2. Using a simple continuous assignment to tie the output directly to LOW
3. This eliminates all dynamic power consumption from clock toggling
4. Reduces area to nearly zero (just a wire connection)
5. Simplifies timing analysis by removing unnecessary sequential elements
