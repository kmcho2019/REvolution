1. Current state is B (001) and w=1 (transition to D)
2. Current state is C (010) and w=1 (transition to D)
3. Current state is E (100) and w=1 (transition to D)
4. Current state is F (101) (transitions to either C or D, both have y[1]=1)
5. Current state is D (011) and w=0 (transition to F)

1. Combining all cases where w=1 leads to y[1]=1 (B, C, E)
