1. Analyze all state transitions where y[1] changes:
   - From B (001) to C (010): y[1] becomes 1 (when w=0)
   - From C (010) to E (100): y[1] becomes 0 (when w=0)
   - From C (010) to D (011): y[1] becomes 1 (when w=1)
   - From D (011) to F (101): y[1] becomes 0 (when w=0)
   - From E (100) to D (011): y[1] becomes 1 (when w=1)
   - From F (101) to C (010): y[1] becomes 1 (when w=0)
   - From F (101) to D (011): y[1] becomes 1 (when w=1)

   - State B (001) maintains y[1]=0 when w=1
   - State D (011) maintains y[1]=1 when w=1 (transition to A)
   - State E (100) maintains y[1]=0 when w=0
   - State F (101) maintains y[1]=0 (but transitions always change it)
