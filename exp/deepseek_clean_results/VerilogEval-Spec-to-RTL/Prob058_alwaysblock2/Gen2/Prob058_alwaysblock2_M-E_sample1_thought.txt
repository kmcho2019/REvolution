Novel approach to implement the XOR gate three ways:
1. For out_assign: Use a ternary conditional operator to implement XOR without using the XOR operator
2. For out_always_comb: Use a function inside the combinational always block to calculate the XOR result
3. For out_always_ff: Use a clocked always block with a direct assignment, but introduce a clock divider that makes the output update every 2 clock cycles (just for demonstration of a different approach)
