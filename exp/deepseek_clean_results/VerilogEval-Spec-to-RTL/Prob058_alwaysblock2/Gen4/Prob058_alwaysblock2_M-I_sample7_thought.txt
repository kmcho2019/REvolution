The original implementation is already quite optimal for this simple XOR functionality, but we can make one minor improvement by:
1. Removing the intermediate wire (xor_result) since it's only used once in each context
2. Directly assigning the XOR operation to each output
3. This eliminates one level of hierarchy in the netlist while maintaining identical functionality
4. The change is purely structural and doesn't affect timing or functionality
5. This might result in marginally better synthesis results due to simpler direct connections
