Here's a novel architectural approach that leverages hierarchical design and conditional clock gating:

1. **Hierarchical XOR**: Implement the XOR operation in a separate submodule to promote modularity and reuse. This allows for independent optimization of the XOR logic.

2. **Clock Gating**: For the sequential output, implement conditional clock gating where the flip-flop only updates when either input changes. This reduces unnecessary clock toggles and saves power.

3. **Output Buffering**: Use a buffer register for the combinational outputs to balance timing paths, which could help in larger designs where output loading varies.

Key Innovations:
- Modular design with reusable XOR component
- Power-saving clock gating technique
- Balanced output paths through buffering
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
