Innovative XOR implementation with:
1. A traditional continuous assignment for baseline XOR
2. A combinational always block with optional inversion capability (though not used here)
3. A sequential implementation with configurable delay stages (set to 1 cycle)
4. Added parameterization for future flexibility
5. Explicit reset handling for the sequential logic
6. Using blocking assignments in combinational logic for clarity
7. Maintaining minimal overhead while adding architectural flexibility
