The original solution can be simplified while maintaining all required functionality:

1. Remove the enable parameter (ENABLE_SEQ) since it's not required by the specification and adds unnecessary complexity for this simple XOR implementation.

2. Remove the shared xor_result wire since it's only used once per output and doesn't provide significant benefit for such a simple operation.

3. Keep the three required implementations:
   - Continuous assignment (assign)
   - Combinational always block (always_comb)
   - Sequential always block (always_ff)

4. Maintain the same interface and functionality while reducing the code to its simplest form.
