Innovative shift register FSM implementation:
1. Instead of traditional state encoding, use a 4-bit shift register where only one bit is high at any time (one-hot like but with shifting)
2. Each state corresponds to a specific bit position in the shift register
3. The shift direction and operation are controlled by the input 'in'
4. Output is simply the MSB of the shift register (no additional combinational logic needed)
5. Reset initializes to '0001' (state A)
6. This approach minimizes logic complexity and provides deterministic timing
