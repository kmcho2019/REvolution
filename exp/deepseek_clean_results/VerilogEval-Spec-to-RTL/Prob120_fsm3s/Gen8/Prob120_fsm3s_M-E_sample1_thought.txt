A novel approach to implement this Moore FSM using a shift register with feedback:
1. Represent states as positions in a 4-bit shift register (one-hot encoding)
2. Use the input to control the shift direction and feedback logic
3. Each state transition becomes a predictable shift operation
4. Output is simply the MSB of the shift register
5. This approach leverages hardware-efficient shift operations and minimizes combinational logic
6. Reset initializes the shift register to '0001' (state A)
