To optimize the FSM implementation, I'll focus on improving timing and reducing power while maintaining the area efficiency. The key improvements are:

1. State Encoding: Switch to Gray coding to minimize bit transitions between states, which reduces switching power and improves timing.
2. Output Logic: Keep the simple AND gate for output since it's already area-efficient.
3. Reset Strategy: Maintain synchronous reset for consistency with the original design.
4. Transition Logic: Optimize the case statement by grouping similar transitions to potentially reduce logic depth.

The Gray code encoding will be:
A: 00
B: 01
C: 11
D: 10

This ensures only one bit changes between adjacent states (A<->B and C<->D), reducing glitches and power consumption.
