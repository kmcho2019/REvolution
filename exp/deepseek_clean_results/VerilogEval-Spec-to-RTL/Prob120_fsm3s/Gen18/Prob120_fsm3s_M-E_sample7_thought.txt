I'll implement the FSM using a shift register approach with conditional shifting logic. The key insights are:
1. States are represented by a 4-bit one-hot encoding (A=0001, B=0010, C=0100, D=1000)
2. State transitions are implemented by shifting bits left or right based on input:
   - A->A: No shift (hold)
   - A->B: Shift left once
   - B->C: Shift left once when in=0
   - B->B: No shift when in=1
   - C->A: Reset to A when in=0
   - C->D: Shift left once when in=1
   - D->C: Shift right once when in=0
   - D->B: Shift right twice when in=1
3. The output is simply the MSB of the shift register (state D)
4. Reset initializes to state A (0001)

This approach leverages shift operations for state transitions, which can be efficiently implemented in hardware. The one-hot encoding provides simple output logic and potentially better timing characteristics.
