Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Shift Register with Early Stop Detection:
   - Instead of counting bits, use a 9-bit shift register to capture the entire frame (start + 8 data + stop)
   - Detect start bit by monitoring the LSB of the shift register
   - Validate frame when MSB (stop bit) is high and LSB (start bit) is low
   - This eliminates the need for a separate bit counter and simplifies state transitions

2. Predictive State Machine:
   - Only 2 states needed: IDLE (waiting for start) and CAPTURE (collecting frame)
   - Transition to IDLE immediately when a valid stop bit is detected
   - Stay in CAPTURE until stop bit is found (handles framing errors)

3. Edge Detection:
   - Use falling edge of start bit to trigger capture
   - This makes the design more robust to glitches during idle state

Key Advantages:
- Reduced state complexity (2 states vs 4 in original)
- No explicit bit counting needed
- Early detection of framing errors
- More efficient use of shift register
- Lower power due to simpler control logic
