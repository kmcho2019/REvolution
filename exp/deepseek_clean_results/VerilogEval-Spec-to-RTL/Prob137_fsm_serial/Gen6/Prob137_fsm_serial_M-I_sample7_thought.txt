1. Timing Optimization:

   - Use registered outputs for done signal to improve timing closure
   - Simplify state transition conditions by merging similar states

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add enable signals to prevent unnecessary toggling of data_reg and bit_count
   - Reduce switching activity by only updating registers when needed
   - Minimize combinational logic in always blocks

3. Area Optimization:

   - Keep efficient shift operation but make it conditional
   - Remove redundant logic in state transitions
