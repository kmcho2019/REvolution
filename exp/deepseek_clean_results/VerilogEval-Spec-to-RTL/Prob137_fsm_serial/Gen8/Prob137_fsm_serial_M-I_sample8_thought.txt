Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, here's my optimization strategy:

1. State Encoding Optimization:
   - Switch from one-hot to binary encoding (2 bits) to reduce area and power
   - This reduces flip-flop count from 4 to 2 while maintaining functionality

2. State Machine Simplification:
   - Merge WAIT and STOP states into a single STOP_WAIT state
   - This reduces total states from 4 to 3, further optimizing area

3. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove pipelined done signal to reduce latency
   - Generate done combinatorially when valid stop bit is detected

4. Power Optimization:
   - Simplify state transitions to reduce switching activity
   - Maintain clock gating for shift register

5. Area Optimization:
   - Reduce state register bits from 4 to 2
   - Simplify control logic by merging states

The improved design should maintain functionality while reducing area and power, with comparable timing performance.
