Simplification Strategy:
1. State Reduction: Combine STOP and WAIT states into a single CHECK_STOP state since their behavior is similar (both wait for stop bit)
2. Counter Optimization: Use a 3-bit counter that naturally wraps around after 8 bits, eliminating explicit comparison
3. Data Handling: Keep simple shift register but remove redundant assignments
4. Done Logic: Generate pulse directly in combinational logic to reduce registers
5. Reset Handling: Maintain synchronous reset but simplify initialization
6. State Encoding: Keep binary encoding but reduce to 2 states (IDLE and ACTIVE) with counter tracking progress

Key Simplifications:
- Fewer states (2 instead of 4)
- Natural counter wrap-around
- Direct done pulse generation
- Reduced register updates
- Cleaner state transitions
