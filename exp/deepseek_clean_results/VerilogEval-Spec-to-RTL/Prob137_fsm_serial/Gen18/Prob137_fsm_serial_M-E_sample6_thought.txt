Novel Architecture:
1. Unified Shift-State Register: Combine the data shift register with state tracking bits to reduce register count
2. Bit-Position Encoding: Use the shift register's position to implicitly track bit count, eliminating explicit counter
3. Early Stop Detection: Check for stop bit validity during reception to fail fast
4. Compact FSM: Reduce to just 2 states (IDLE and ACTIVE) by leveraging shift register metadata
5. Parallel Processing: Process start/stop conditions in parallel with data shifting

Key Innovations:
- Eliminates separate bit counter by using shift register position
- Combines state and data storage for area efficiency
- Early stop bit checking reduces latency on bad frames
- Simplified FSM with fewer transitions
- Parallel processing improves timing
