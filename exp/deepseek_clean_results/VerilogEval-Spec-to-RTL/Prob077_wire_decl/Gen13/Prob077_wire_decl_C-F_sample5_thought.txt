Fusion strategy combining the best aspects of both examples:
1. Maintain the flat, efficient structure from Example 1 for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area)
2. Keep the clear intermediate wire naming from Example 1 for readability
3. Preserve the direct continuous assignments for simplicity and efficiency
4. Include the inverted output generation directly from the main output
5. While not using hierarchical modules (like Example 2), we maintain their logical clarity through well-named intermediate wires

This hybrid approach achieves:
- Better PPA than hierarchical design (lower power, same area)
- Clearer structure than pure flat design (through named wires)
- Optimal timing with minimal logic levels
- Maximum readability while maintaining efficiency
