The circuit can be implemented by first creating two AND gates for the first layer:
1. AND gate with inputs a and b
2. AND gate with inputs c and d
Then in the second layer, we OR the outputs of these two AND gates to produce 'out'.
Finally, we create an inverted version of 'out' to produce 'out_n'.
We'll use two intermediate wires (and1_out and and2_out) to connect the AND gates to the OR gate.
