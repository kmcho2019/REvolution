The circuit can be implemented by first creating two AND gates for the first layer:
1. AND gate 1 takes inputs a and b
2. AND gate 2 takes inputs c and d
Then the outputs of these AND gates are fed into an OR gate for the second layer, producing 'out'.
Finally, 'out_n' is simply the inverted version of 'out'.
We'll use two intermediate wires (and1_out and and2_out) to connect the AND gates to the OR gate.
