The circuit can be implemented by first creating two AND gates for the first layer: one for inputs a & b, and another for inputs c & d. The outputs of these AND gates will then be ORed together in the second layer to produce the 'out' signal. The 'out_n' signal is simply the inverted version of 'out'. We'll use two intermediate wires (and1_out and and2_out) to connect the AND gate outputs to the OR gate.
