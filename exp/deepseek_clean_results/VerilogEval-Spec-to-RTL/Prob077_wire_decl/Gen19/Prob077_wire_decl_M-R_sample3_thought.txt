Refactored approach using continuous assignments in a single module:
1. Eliminate separate AND gate modules to reduce hierarchy and improve PPA
2. Use direct wire assignments for the AND operations (a&b and c&d)
3. Combine these with the OR operation in a single expression for 'out'
4. Generate the inverted output directly from 'out'
This simplifies the design while maintaining identical functionality, reducing power and area by removing module overhead.
