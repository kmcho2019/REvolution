Refactored approach using direct continuous assignments:
1. Eliminate the separate AND gate modules to reduce hierarchy and improve PPA
2. Use direct wire assignments for the AND operations (a&b and c&d)
3. Combine these with the OR operation in a single module
4. Generate the inverted output directly from the OR result
This flatter structure reduces power by eliminating module interface switching, improves area by removing instantiation overhead, and marginally improves performance by reducing hierarchical delays.
