Simplified approach:
1. Eliminate separate AND gate modules to reduce hierarchy and overhead
2. Use direct continuous assignments for both logic layers
3. Create two internal wires (and1_out, and2_out) for the first layer AND operations
4. Combine these with an OR operation for 'out'
5. Generate inverted output 'out_n' directly from 'out'
This flat design reduces power and area while maintaining the same functionality.
