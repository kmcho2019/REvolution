Here's an alternative approach using a hierarchical design with submodules:
1. Create two separate AND gate modules (AndGate1 and AndGate2) to encapsulate the first layer logic
2. Instantiate these AND gates in the top module
3. Use the outputs of these AND gates directly in the OR operation
4. Generate the inverted output by connecting to the OR output
This approach demonstrates modular design principles and could be more scalable for complex circuits.
