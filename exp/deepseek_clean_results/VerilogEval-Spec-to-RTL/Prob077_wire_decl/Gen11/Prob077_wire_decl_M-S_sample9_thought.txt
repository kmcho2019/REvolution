Simplified approach:
1. Eliminate separate AND gate modules to reduce hierarchy and overhead
2. Use direct continuous assignments for both logic layers
3. Create two internal wires (and1_out, and2_out) to connect the AND operations
4. OR these wires to produce 'out'
5. Generate 'out_n' by simply inverting 'out'
This maintains the same functionality while being more efficient in terms of power, area, and performance.
