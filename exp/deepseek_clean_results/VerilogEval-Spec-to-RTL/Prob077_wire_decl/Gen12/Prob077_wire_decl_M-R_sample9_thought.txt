Refactored approach using direct continuous assignments:
1. Eliminate the separate AND gate modules to reduce hierarchy and improve PPA
2. Use direct wire assignments for the AND operations (a&b and c&d)
3. Combine the results with an OR operation for the output
4. Generate the inverted output directly from the main output
This flat implementation reduces power by eliminating module switching overhead and improves area by removing instantiation overhead while maintaining the same logical functionality.
