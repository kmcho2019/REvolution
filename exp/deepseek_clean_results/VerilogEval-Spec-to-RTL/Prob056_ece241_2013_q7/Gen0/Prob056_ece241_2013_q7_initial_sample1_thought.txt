To implement a JK flip-flop, we need to create a sequential circuit that updates its output Q on every positive edge of the clock signal. The behavior follows the standard JK flip-flop truth table:
- When J=0 and K=0, Q maintains its previous value (Qold)
- When J=0 and K=1, Q becomes 0
- When J=1 and K=0, Q becomes 1
- When J=1 and K=1, Q toggles (becomes ~Qold)

We'll use a simple always block triggered on the positive edge of clk to implement this behavior.
