To implement a JK flip-flop, we need to create a sequential circuit that updates its output Q on every positive clock edge according to the JK flip-flop truth table. The behavior can be described as:
- When J=0 and K=0: Q remains the same (Qold)
- When J=0 and K=1: Q becomes 0
- When J=1 and K=0: Q becomes 1
- When J=1 and K=1: Q toggles (becomes ~Qold)

We'll use an always block triggered on the positive edge of clk to implement this behavior.
