To implement a JK flip-flop, we need to create a sequential circuit that updates its output on the positive edge of the clock according to the JK truth table. The output Q should:
- Remain unchanged when J=0 and K=0
- Reset to 0 when J=0 and K=1
- Set to 1 when J=1 and K=0
- Toggle when J=1 and K=1

We'll use an always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock to implement this behavior.
