To refactor the JK flip-flop implementation while maintaining the same functionality, I'll use a continuous assignment (assign) with a combinational next-state logic and a separate D flip-flop for the state storage. This approach:

1. Separates combinational logic from sequential elements for better modularity
2. Uses a wire for the next-state calculation instead of a reg
3. Implements the flip-flop behavior with a simple D flip-flop
4. The combinational logic remains functionally equivalent but is now in a continuous assignment
5. May offer better synthesis optimization opportunities as the combinational and sequential parts are clearly separated

The next-state logic is: Q_next = (j & ~k) | (~j & ~k & Q) | (j & k & ~Q)
