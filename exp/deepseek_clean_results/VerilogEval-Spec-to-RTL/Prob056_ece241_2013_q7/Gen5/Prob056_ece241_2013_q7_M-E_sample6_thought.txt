Here's a novel approach using a state transition concept:

1. Instead of directly implementing the truth table, I'll model the JK flip-flop as a state machine with two possible states (0 and 1)
2. The state transitions will be determined by a combination of current state (Q) and inputs (J,K)
3. I'll use a 4-to-1 multiplexer concept where:
   - The select lines are the J and K inputs
   - The data inputs represent all possible next states based on current Q
4. This approach makes the state transitions explicit and might offer better synthesis optimization
5. The mux implementation could potentially reduce critical path compared to nested conditionals
