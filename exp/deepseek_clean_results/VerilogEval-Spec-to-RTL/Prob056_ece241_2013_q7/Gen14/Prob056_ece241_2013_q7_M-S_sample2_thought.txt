To simplify the JK flip-flop implementation while maintaining functionality, I'll focus on directly implementing the truth table with minimal logic. The key observations are:
1. When J and K are different (01 or 10), Q simply takes the value of J
2. When J and K are same (00 or 11), Q either holds or toggles
3. This can be implemented with a single multiplexer selecting between J and the toggled Q when J==K

The simplified logic will be:
Q_next = (j == k) ? (j ^ Q_reg) : j;
This reduces the implementation to just one conditional assignment and one XOR operation, which is both simple and efficient.
