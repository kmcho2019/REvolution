To further optimize the JK flip-flop implementation for better PPA, I'll focus on:

1. Performance: Replace the ternary operator with direct logic expressions to potentially reduce critical path delay. The XOR operation can be maintained for the toggle case (J=1,K=1).

2. Power: Keep the synchronous design but simplify the combinational logic to reduce switching activity.

3. Area: Use a more straightforward logic expression that requires fewer gates while maintaining functionality.

The improved logic will be:
Q_next = (J & ~K) | (~J & K & Q_reg) | (J & K & ~Q_reg);
This breaks down the truth table explicitly and may allow better optimization during synthesis.
