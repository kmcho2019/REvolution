To simplify the JK flip-flop implementation, I'll:
1. Combine the combinational and sequential logic into a single always block
2. Directly implement the truth table using case-like conditions
3. Eliminate the intermediate wire (Q_next) since it's not necessary for this simple design
4. Use a more straightforward conditional structure that maps directly to the truth table

This reduces the design to its essential components while maintaining identical functionality. The simplification comes from removing the separation between combinational and sequential logic which isn't needed for such a small state element.
