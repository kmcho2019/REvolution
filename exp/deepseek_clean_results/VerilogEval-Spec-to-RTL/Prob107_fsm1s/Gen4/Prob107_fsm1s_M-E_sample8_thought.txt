Novel architectural idea: Implement the state machine using a shift register approach where the state is represented by the position of a single '1' bit in a 2-bit register. This creates a "walking one" encoding that naturally matches the state transitions.

Key features:
1. Uses a 2-bit shift register where only one bit is ever '1' at a time
2. State transitions become simple shift operations
3. Output is derived directly from the MSB of the shift register
4. Reset initializes to '01' (state B)
5. State transitions:
   - B (01) with in=0: shift left becomes 10 (A)
   - B (01) with in=1: no shift remains 01 (B)
   - A (10) with in=0: shift left with wrap becomes 01 (B)
   - A (10) with in=1: no shift remains 10 (A)

Benefits:
- Simple shift operations reduce combinational logic
- Natural one-hot encoding
- Easy to extend if more states are added later
- Clear physical meaning of state representation
