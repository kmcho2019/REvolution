Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation for the state machine:
1. Instead of traditional state encoding, use a 2-bit shift register where the current state is represented by the MSB
2. The shift register will circularly rotate based on input conditions
3. The output is directly the MSB of the shift register
4. Reset initializes the shift register to '11' (state B)
5. This implementation leverages the shift register's natural behavior to implement the state transitions:
   - When in=0: Rotate left (state changes)
   - When in=1: Maintain current value (state stays)
This approach may offer better timing as shift registers are highly optimized in most FPGA/ASIC libraries.
