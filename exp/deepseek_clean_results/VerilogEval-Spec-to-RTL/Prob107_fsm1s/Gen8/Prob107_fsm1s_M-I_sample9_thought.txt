To optimize the design for better timing (reduce negative slack) while maintaining the same functionality:

1. **State Transition Logic Simplification**:
   - Replace ternary operators with direct logical expressions to reduce logic levels
   - The state transition can be expressed as: next_state = (state & in) | (~state & ~in)

2. **Output Logic Optimization**:
   - Keep the simple inversion since it's already optimal
   - The output is purely combinational from state

3. **Register Implementation**:
   - Maintain single flip-flop for state storage
   - Keep synchronous reset as specified

4. **Timing Improvement Strategy**:
   - The new transition logic uses only AND/OR operations which may map better to standard cells
   - Eliminates the mux-like structure from ternary operators
   - Should reduce the critical path delay

This change maintains identical functionality while potentially improving timing closure.
