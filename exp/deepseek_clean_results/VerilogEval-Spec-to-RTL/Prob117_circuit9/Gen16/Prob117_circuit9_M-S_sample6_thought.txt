Simplified Design Approach:
1. The circuit has two clear modes based on input 'a':
   - When 'a' is high (1), output 'q' stays constant at 4
   - When 'a' is low (0), 'q' increments and wraps around from 6 to 0
2. We can implement this with a simple register and minimal combinational logic
3. No need for complex clock gating - the mux selection handles the mode switching
4. The wrap-around can be detected by checking if q == 6 (110) directly
5. This reduces logic depth and area while maintaining the same functionality
