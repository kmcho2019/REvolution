Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove the comparator from the critical path by using a direct bit pattern match for reset condition (q[2] & q[1])
   - Simplify the mux structure by separating the constant assignment and counter logic
   - Use parallel paths instead of priority structure

2. Power Optimization:
   - Eliminate the counter_en signal to reduce switching activity
   - Use clock gating implicitly by structuring the logic to only update when needed

3. Area Optimization:
   - Replace full comparator with simple bit checks
   - Simplify the increment logic by combining with reset condition
   - Remove intermediate wires where possible
