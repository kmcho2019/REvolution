Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Shift Register with Conditional Reset: Implement a 3-bit shift register that normally increments its value, but resets to 4 when 'a' is high.

3. Minimal State Logic: Use just 3 flip-flops and simple combinational logic to achieve the desired behavior, reducing area and power.
4. Efficient Encoding: The shift operation automatically handles the wrap-around from 6 to 0, eliminating the need for explicit wrap-around logic.
