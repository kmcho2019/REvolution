Refactored Design Idea:
1. Separate Combinational Logic: Move the next-state logic to a continuous assignment
2. Sequential Element: Keep only the register update in the always block
3. Explicit Next-State: Calculate next_q separately for clarity
4. Same Behavior: Maintain identical functionality while improving readability

This approach separates combinational and sequential logic clearly, which can help with synthesis optimization and timing analysis. The continuous assignment makes the next-state logic more visible and potentially easier to optimize during synthesis.
