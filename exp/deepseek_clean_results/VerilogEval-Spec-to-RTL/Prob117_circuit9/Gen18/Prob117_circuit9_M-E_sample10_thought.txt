Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Implement a hybrid approach combining a counter with a priority selector
2. Use a saturating counter that freezes at 4 when 'a' is high
3. When 'a' is low, the counter increments normally but wraps around after 6
4. The design uses a single always block with conditional increment logic
5. This approach minimizes state transitions when 'a' is high, saving power

Key Features:
- Counter-based implementation with conditional increment
- 'a=1' acts as a synchronous hold at value 4
- Simple arithmetic for increment and wrap-around
- Reduced switching activity when 'a' is high
- Straightforward timing path with predictable behavior
