Simplified Design Approach:
1. Maintain same functionality: q=4 when a=1, increment when a=0 with wrap-around at 6
2. Use a single always block for both combinational and sequential logic (more compact)
3. Simplify the reset condition by checking for q==6 directly (more readable)
4. Remove intermediate wires since we can directly assign to q in the always block
5. The increment operation is simple enough to include directly in the always block
6. This reduces logic depth and area while maintaining clear functionality
