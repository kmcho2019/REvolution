Novel Design Idea: Shift Register with Conditional Feedback
1. Instead of a conventional counter, use a 3-bit shift register with XOR feedback
2. When 'a' is high, force the register to "100" (4)
3. When 'a' is low, implement a pseudo-random sequence generator that cycles through 4-5-6-0-1
4. The feedback pattern is carefully chosen to match the required sequence
5. This approach demonstrates how shift registers can be used for sequence generation

Key Advantages:
- Different implementation style from standard counters
- Shows alternative sequential circuit design
- Feedback logic is simple and efficient
- Easily extensible to other sequences by changing feedback taps
