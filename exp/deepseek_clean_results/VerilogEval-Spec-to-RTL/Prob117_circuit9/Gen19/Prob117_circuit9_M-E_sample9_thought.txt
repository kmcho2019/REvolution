Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Shift Register with Conditional Load: Implement a 3-bit shift register that normally rotates its bits, but loads a fixed value (4) when 'a' is high.
2. Bit Rotation Logic: When 'a' is low, the register rotates its bits in a specific pattern that generates the counting sequence 0-6.
3. Fixed Value Injection: The value 4 (100) is directly loaded when 'a' is asserted, overriding the rotation behavior.
4. Efficient Encoding: The rotation pattern is carefully chosen so that the natural shift behavior produces the desired counting sequence when 'a' is low.

This approach leverages the simplicity of shift operations while maintaining the required functionality, potentially offering better area and power efficiency compared to a traditional state machine implementation.
