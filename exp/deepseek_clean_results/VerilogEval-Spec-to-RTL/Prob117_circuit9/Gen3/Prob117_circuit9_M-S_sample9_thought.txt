The key observations from the waveform are:
1. q holds value 4 when a=1 (at all times, not just clock edges)
2. When a=0, q increments by 1 each clock cycle with wrap-around (4→5→6→0→1)
3. Initial value is 4

Simplifications:
1. Remove the initial block (not synthesizable) and use reset instead
2. Make the a=1 behavior combinational (q=4) since it's immediate in waveform
3. Keep sequential increment only when a=0
4. Use synchronous reset for initialization

This matches the waveform exactly while being simpler and more hardware-friendly.
