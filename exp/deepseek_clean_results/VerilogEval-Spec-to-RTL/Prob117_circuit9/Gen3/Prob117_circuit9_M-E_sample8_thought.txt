Here's a novel approach using a down-counter with conditional hold:
1. Implement a 3-bit down-counter that normally decrements on each clock edge
2. Use input 'a' as a hold signal - when high, it prevents the counter from changing
3. The counter wraps around from 0 back to 6 (since the waveform shows 4→5→6→0)
4. Initialize the counter to 4 to match the waveform
5. This approach is simpler than a state machine and directly implements the observed behavior
