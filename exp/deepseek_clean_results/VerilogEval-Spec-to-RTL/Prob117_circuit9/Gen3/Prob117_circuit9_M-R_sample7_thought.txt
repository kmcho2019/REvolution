Refactored approach:
1. Separate the combinational and sequential logic for better clarity
2. Use an always block for sequential part (register)
3. Use continuous assignment for combinational logic (next state)
4. Maintain same functionality:
   - Hold at 4 when a=1
   - Increment with wrap-around (4→5→6→0→1) when a=0
5. Initialize register to 4 (3'b100)

Key changes:
- Next state logic moved to assign statement
- Register update remains in always block
- More modular design with clear separation of concerns
