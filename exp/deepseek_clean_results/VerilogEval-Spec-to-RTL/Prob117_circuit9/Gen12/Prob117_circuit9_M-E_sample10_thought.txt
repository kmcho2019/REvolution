Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Shift Register with Conditional Reset: Implement a 3-bit shift register that normally rotates its bits left, but resets to 4 (100) when 'a' is high.
2. Bit Rotation Logic: When 'a' is low, the register rotates left (MSB becomes LSB), creating a sequence that counts through 0-6.
3. Reset Priority: The 'a' input has higher priority than the rotation operation, ensuring immediate transition to 4 when asserted.
4. Minimal State: Uses only 3 flip-flops with simple combinational logic, optimizing for area and power.

This approach leverages the natural counting behavior of a rotating shift register while maintaining the required reset-to-4 functionality, resulting in a compact and efficient implementation.
