Simplified Design Approach:
1. The circuit has two clear modes based on input 'a':
   - When a=1: q stays at 4 (constant value)
   - When a=0: q increments from current value, wrapping around to 0 after 6
2. Can implement this with a simple mux that selects between:
   - Constant 4 when a=1
   - Incremented value (with wrap-around) when a=0
3. Wrap-around can be detected by checking if current value is 6 (110)
4. No need for complex clock gating or power optimization in this simple case
5. Direct implementation with minimal logic operations
