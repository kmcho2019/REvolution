From the simulation waveforms, we can observe the following behavior:
1. The output q changes only on the rising edge of clk (positive edge-triggered)
2. When a=1, q maintains its current value (4 in this case)
3. When a=0, q increments by 1 each clock cycle (4→5→6→0→1)
4. The counter wraps around after reaching 6 (goes back to 0)

The module is essentially a 3-bit counter with enable:
- When a=1: counter holds its value
- When a=0: counter increments by 1
- The counter wraps around after 6 (binary 110)
