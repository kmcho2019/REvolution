Simplified Design Idea:
1. Direct Counter Approach: Instead of a state machine, use a simple counter that:
   - Resets to 4 when 'a' is high
   - Increments from 0-6 (wrapping around) when 'a' is low
2. Single Register: Use just one 3-bit register for the counter
3. Minimal Logic: Implement with simple if-else and arithmetic
4. Same Behavior: Maintain identical functionality to original solution but with fewer states and simpler logic

This approach eliminates the need for state encoding and case statements, reducing both area and power while maintaining the same timing characteristics.
