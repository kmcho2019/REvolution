Refactored Design Approach:
1. Maintain identical functionality: q=4 when a=1, increment when a=0 with wrap-around at 6
2. Use purely combinational logic with continuous assignments
3. Eliminate the explicit register by using a feedback loop through the clock edge
4. Simplify the reset condition by directly comparing with 6 (3'b110)
5. Use a single ternary operator for the entire next state logic
6. This approach reduces hierarchy and may improve synthesis results
