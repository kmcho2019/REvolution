Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Shift Register with Conditional Reset: Implement a 3-bit shift register that normally rotates its bits left, but resets to 4 (100) when 'a' is high.
2. Bit Rotation Logic: When 'a' is low, the register rotates left (MSB becomes LSB), creating a sequence that counts from 0 to 6 in binary.
3. Direct Reset Control: The input 'a' directly controls whether the register resets or rotates, eliminating complex state transition logic.
4. Minimal Logic: This approach uses simple bit operations instead of a full state machine, reducing area and power.

Key Insight:
- The binary representations of 0-6 form a pattern that can be generated by rotating bits
- 4 (100) is a fixed point when 'a' is high
- Rotation creates the sequence: 000→001→010→011→100→101→110→000...
