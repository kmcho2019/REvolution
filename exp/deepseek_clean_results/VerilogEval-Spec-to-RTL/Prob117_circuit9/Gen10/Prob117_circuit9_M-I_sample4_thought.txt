The current implementation works correctly but has timing violations (negative WNS). To improve PPA:

1. Performance Improvement:
   - The critical path is in the increment/wrap logic. We can pipeline this by splitting into two stages:
     - Stage 1: Compute next value (q + 1)
     - Stage 2: Handle wrap-around (if q == 6)

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating when 'a' is high since q remains constant (4)
   - This prevents unnecessary register toggles

3. Area Consideration:
   - The pipelining will add a small amount of area but significantly improve timing
   - The clock gating logic adds minimal area overhead

The improved design will:
- Meet timing constraints by reducing critical path
- Reduce power consumption through clock gating
- Maintain correct functionality
