The previous solution uses a ternary operator for increment/wrap logic which adds some complexity. We can simplify this by:
1. Maintaining the synchronous reset to 4 when a=1
2. For a=0, just use a simple increment that naturally wraps around due to 3-bit overflow
3. This achieves the same behavior with simpler logic since:
   - 4+1=5
   - 5+1=6
   - 6+1=7 (but we want 0)
   - However, 7+1=0 due to natural 3-bit overflow
4. The only adjustment needed is to start counting from 4 when a transitions from 1 to 0
