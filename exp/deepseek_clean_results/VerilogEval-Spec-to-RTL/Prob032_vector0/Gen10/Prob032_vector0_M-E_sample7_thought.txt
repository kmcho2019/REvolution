Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation:
1. Instead of direct wiring, we'll use a 3-bit shift register that loads the input vector
2. The shift register will have parallel outputs for outv
3. We'll tap individual bits from the register for o2, o1, o0
4. This adds a clocked element which could be useful in synchronous systems
5. While less efficient for this simple task, it demonstrates an alternative implementation strategy that could be extended for more complex operations
