To optimize the PPA metrics, we should:
1. Eliminate unnecessary buffer gates since they add delay and area without functional benefit
2. Use direct assignments for both the vector output and individual bit outputs
3. This reduces:
   - Timing: Removes buffer gate propagation delay
   - Power: Eliminates unnecessary switching in buffers
   - Area: Removes buffer gate cells
4. Maintain the same functionality through simpler, more direct connections
