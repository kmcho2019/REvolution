Here's a novel approach using a structural implementation with tri-state buffers:

2. The vector output will be driven by three buffers enabled by a constant '1'
3. Each individual bit output will use its own buffer
4. This demonstrates an alternative implementation style that could be useful in more complex designs where output enable control is needed
