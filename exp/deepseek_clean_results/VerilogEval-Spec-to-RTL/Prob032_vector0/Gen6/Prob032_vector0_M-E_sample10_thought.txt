This implementation uses a structural approach with explicit bit extraction:
1. Instead of using a register or direct assignments, we'll use bit-select operations
2. The vector output is simply passed through
3. Each individual output is connected to its corresponding bit using hierarchical bit selection
4. This approach emphasizes the structural nature of the bit extraction
5. The design clearly shows the relationship between vector and scalar outputs
6. No intermediate signals are used, making it fully combinational and efficient
