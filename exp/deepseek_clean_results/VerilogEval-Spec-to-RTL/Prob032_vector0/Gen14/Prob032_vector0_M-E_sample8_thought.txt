This novel approach implements the functionality using a shift register concept:
1. Instead of direct bit assignments, we'll use a right-shift operation to isolate each bit
2. The vector output remains a direct assignment
3. Each individual output is obtained by shifting and masking:
   - o2: shift right by 2 and mask with 1'b1
   - o1: shift right by 1 and mask with 1'b1
   - o0: no shift needed, just mask with 1'b1
4. This demonstrates bit extraction through shifting rather than direct selection
