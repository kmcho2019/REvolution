To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality:

1. Timing Improvement:
- Implement a logarithmic barrel shifter structure to reduce critical path
- Break down the 100-bit rotation into smaller, balanced stages (5 stages of 2x rotation since 2^5=32 > 100)
- This reduces the mux depth from O(n) to O(log n)

2. Power Optimization:
- Add clock gating for the default case (no rotation)
- Only enable clock when there's actual rotation or load operation

3. Area Optimization:
- The barrel shifter structure will naturally reduce area by sharing common logic
- Remove redundant muxing in the default case

The improved design will:
- Use a 5-stage barrel shifter for both left and right rotation
- Maintain synchronous load functionality
- Implement clock gating for power savings
