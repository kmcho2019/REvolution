Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Break down the 100-bit rotation into two stages to reduce critical path
   - First stage handles load vs rotate selection
   - Second stage handles direction selection
   - This reduces the fanout and combinational depth of the rotation logic

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add explicit clock gating for the no-rotation case (ena=2'b00/2'b11)
   - This prevents unnecessary toggling of the register when no rotation occurs

3. Area Optimization:
   - Maintain the current efficient mux structure
   - The two-stage approach may slightly increase area but significantly improves timing
