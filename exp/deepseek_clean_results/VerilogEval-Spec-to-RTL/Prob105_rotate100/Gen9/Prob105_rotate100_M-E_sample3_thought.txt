Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Instead of physically shifting bits, maintain a fixed register and use a 7-bit pointer (since 100 needs 7 bits) to track the rotation offset
2. The pointer acts as a virtual starting point for the circular buffer
3. For reads (q), we output the 100-bit window starting from the pointer position
4. Benefits:
   - Rotation becomes a simple pointer increment/decrement (O(1) operation)
   - No physical bit shifting means lower dynamic power
   - Load operation remains simple register write
   - Scales better for larger registers (100+ bits)
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Slightly more complex output muxing
   - Requires modulo arithmetic for pointer wrapping
