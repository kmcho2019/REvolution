To implement a 100-bit rotator with synchronous load and enable, we need to:
1. Use a 100-bit register to store the current value (q)
2. On each positive clock edge:
   - If load is high, load data into the register
   - Else if ena indicates rotation (01 for right, 10 for left), perform the rotation
   - For right rotation (ena=01), shift right and bring the LSB to MSB
   - For left rotation (ena=10), shift left and bring the MSB to LSB
   - For other ena values, keep the register unchanged
