Novel Architecture Idea: Barrel Rotator with Pre-decoded Control
1. Instead of using multiplexers for rotation selection, implement a barrel rotator structure that can perform both left and right rotations in a single operation
2. Pre-decode the control signals to generate rotation amount and direction
3. Use a single large multiplexer stage that selects between:
   - Load value (data)
   - Left rotation (concatenate LSB to MSB-1)
   - Right rotation (concatenate MSB to LSB+1)
   - Current value (hold)
4. This approach reduces the logic depth compared to cascaded multiplexers and may improve timing
5. The control logic is simplified to direct selection of rotation paths
