Novel Circular Buffer Approach:
1. Instead of physically rotating bits, maintain a fixed register and track rotation position with a pointer
2. Use a 7-bit pointer (since 100 < 128) to track current rotation offset
3. Implement virtual rotation by adjusting the pointer and using modular arithmetic
4. For output, generate the rotated view by combining parts of the register based on pointer
5. Benefits:
   - Avoids large mux networks of traditional shifters
   - Pointer arithmetic is simpler than full-width shifting
   - More efficient for large widths (scales better than 100-bit muxes)
   - Enables future multi-bit rotation extensions
