Instead of physically rotating bits, we can implement a virtual rotation using a pointer-based approach:
1. Maintain a 100-bit register and a 7-bit rotation pointer (since 100 needs 7 bits)
2. The pointer tracks the virtual starting position of the rotated sequence
3. For left/right rotation, simply increment/decrement the pointer modulo 100
4. The output is generated by selecting bits [pointer:99] concatenated with [0:pointer-1]
5. Load operation resets the pointer to 0 and loads the data
This approach reduces the physical bit movement during rotation to just pointer updates.
