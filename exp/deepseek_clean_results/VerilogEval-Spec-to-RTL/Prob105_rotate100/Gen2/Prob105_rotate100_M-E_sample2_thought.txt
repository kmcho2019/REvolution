Here's a novel approach using a circular buffer implementation with pointer arithmetic instead of direct bit manipulation:
1. Instead of physically rotating bits, maintain a 100-bit register and track rotation state with a 7-bit pointer (since 100 needs 7 bits)
2. The pointer indicates the current "start" position of the circular buffer
3. For right rotation: decrement pointer (with wrap-around)
4. For left rotation: increment pointer (with wrap-around)
5. The output is generated by selecting the 100-bit window starting at the pointer position
6. Load operation resets the pointer to 0 and loads the data
This approach reduces the physical bit movement and may offer better PPA for certain technologies
