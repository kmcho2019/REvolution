Novel Architectural Idea:
Instead of using a single large register and performing the rotation in one cycle, we can implement a barrel shifter structure with a hierarchical approach. The key insights are:

1. Divide the 100-bit register into smaller segments (e.g., 10 segments of 10 bits each) to reduce fanout and improve timing.
2. Implement rotation at two levels:
   - First level: Rotate within each 10-bit segment
   - Second level: Rotate the segments themselves
3. Use a pipelined approach where the segment rotation and segment ordering are handled in separate stages to break the critical path.
4. This hierarchical approach allows for better timing closure while maintaining the same functionality.

The benefits are:
- Reduced fanout on each rotation path
- Better timing due to smaller local operations
- Potential power savings from localized switching
- More scalable for larger bit widths
