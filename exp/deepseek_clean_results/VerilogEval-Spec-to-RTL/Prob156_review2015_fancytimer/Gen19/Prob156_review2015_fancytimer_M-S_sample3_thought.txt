To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, I'll make these key changes:

1. **Single Counter Approach**: Instead of separate cycle and phase counters, use one large counter that counts up to (delay+1)*1000 cycles directly. This eliminates the need for phase tracking logic.

2. **Simpler State Machine**: Reduce to just 3 states (IDLE, COUNTING, DONE) by combining pattern detection and delay capture into the IDLE state.

3. **Direct Delay Calculation**: Calculate the total cycles needed immediately after capturing all delay bits.

4. **Cleaner Output Logic**: Generate outputs directly from the current state and counter values without intermediate registers.

The simplified design maintains all required functionality with fewer states and less complex control logic.
