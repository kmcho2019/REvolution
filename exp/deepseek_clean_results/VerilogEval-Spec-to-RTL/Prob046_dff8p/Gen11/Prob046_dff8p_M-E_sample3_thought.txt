My novel approach leverages a parameterized shift register implementation with built-in reset value. Instead of individual flip-flops, I'll use:
1. A parameterized reset value (8'h34) defined at module level
2. A single always block for all 8 bits to improve area efficiency
3. Conditional reset assignment using bitwise AND with reset mask
4. Negative edge triggering maintained as required
This approach reduces wiring complexity and improves power efficiency by eliminating constant reset_val signals while maintaining the same functionality.
