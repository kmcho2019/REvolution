Here's a novel approach using a parameterized shift register implementation with a custom reset value:
1. Instead of individual DFFs, we'll use a single always block for all 8 bits
2. The reset value 0x34 will be hardcoded as a parameter for better optimization
3. We'll implement the negative edge triggering at the module level
4. This approach reduces hierarchy and potential synthesis overhead
5. The design is more compact and potentially more area-efficient
6. The reset value is directly applied without bitwise calculations
