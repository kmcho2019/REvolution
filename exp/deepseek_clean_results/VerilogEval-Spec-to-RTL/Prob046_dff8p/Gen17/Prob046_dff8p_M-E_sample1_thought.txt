Novel Approach: Shift Register with Reset Injection
1. Instead of individual DFFs, use a shift register structure with parallel load capability
2. Implement a custom reset mechanism that injects the reset value (0x34) when reset is asserted
3. Leverage the shift register's inherent serial nature to reduce area while maintaining functionality
4. Use negative-edge triggering by inverting the clock internally
5. Key advantages:
   - Potential area savings from shared logic
   - Unique reset injection method
   - Maintains all specified requirements
   - Novel architecture different from standard solutions
