To implement a TopModule with 8 D flip-flops that reset to 0x34 (binary 00110100) on synchronous active-high reset, we'll:
1. Use a synchronous always block triggered on the negative edge of clk
2. When reset is high, assign the output q to 8'h34
3. Otherwise, pass the input d to output q
4. Each bit will be handled by its own DFF in the vector
