To implement a TopModule with 8 D flip-flops that reset to 0x34 on synchronous reset, we'll:
1. Use a synchronous always block triggered on the negative edge of clk
2. When reset is high, assign the output q to 8'b00110100 (0x34 in binary)
3. Otherwise, pass the input d to output q
4. Each bit of the 8-bit output will be handled by individual DFF behavior
