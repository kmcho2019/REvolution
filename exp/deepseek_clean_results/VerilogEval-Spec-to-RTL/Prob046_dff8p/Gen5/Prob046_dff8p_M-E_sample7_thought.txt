Here's a novel approach using a shift register concept with a twist:
1. Instead of individual DFFs, implement a circular shift register that loads the reset value (0x34) when reset is active
2. On negative clock edges without reset, the register shifts left by 1 bit and loads the input d at LSB
3. The reset value is hardcoded as a parameter for better power efficiency
4. This approach reduces wiring complexity and leverages sequential behavior for more compact implementation
5. The circular nature allows for potential future expansion to shift operations
