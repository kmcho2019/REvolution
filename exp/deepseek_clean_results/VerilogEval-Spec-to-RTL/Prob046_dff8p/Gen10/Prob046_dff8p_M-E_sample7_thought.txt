Here's an innovative approach using a hierarchical design with individual DFF components:
1. Instead of a monolithic register, we'll instantiate 8 separate DFF modules
2. Each DFF will have its own reset value corresponding to the appropriate bit in 0x34
3. We'll use a generate block to efficiently create all 8 instances
4. This approach demonstrates modular design and makes individual flip-flop behavior more explicit
5. The negative edge triggering is maintained at each individual flip-flop level
