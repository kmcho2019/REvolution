To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Replace the AND-OR structure with a case statement:
   - Case statements are more readable and often synthesize more efficiently
   - The synthesizer can optimize case statements better for both area and power
   - Eliminates the need for separate selection signal generation

2. Combine the selection and default (invalid sel) logic:
   - Remove the separate valid signal generation
   - Include the default case (output 0) directly in the case statement
   - This reduces logic levels and eliminates the final masking operation

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced area by eliminating redundant logic
   - Lower power consumption due to fewer switching nodes
   - Maintains good timing performance (case statements typically synthesize well)
   - More readable and maintainable code
