To implement the TopModule with 8 D flip-flops, we'll create a register that updates its output on the positive edge of the clock. Each bit of the 8-bit input 'd' will be connected to a D flip-flop, and all flip-flops will share the same clock and reset signals. The synchronous reset will clear all flip-flops to 0 when active (high). The output 'q' will be the collective output of all 8 flip-flops.
