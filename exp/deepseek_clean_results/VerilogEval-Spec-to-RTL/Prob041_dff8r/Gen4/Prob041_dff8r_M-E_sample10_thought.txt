Here's a novel approach that implements the 8-bit register using individual DFF instantiations rather than a single always block. This provides:
1. Explicit visibility of each flip-flop in the design
2. Potential for better optimization in some synthesis tools
3. Easier debugging of individual flip-flops
4. More flexibility for clock gating individual bits
5. Clear mapping between input and output bits

The design still maintains synchronous reset functionality for each flip-flop, triggered on the positive clock edge.
