To optimize the PPA metrics, particularly focusing on area reduction while maintaining the excellent power and timing characteristics, I'll:
1. Use a single always block for all 8 flip-flops instead of individual instances
2. Keep the synchronous reset functionality
3. Maintain positive edge triggering
4. Use a packed register for the output to enable better synthesis optimization
This approach should reduce area by:
- Eliminating module instantiation overhead
- Allowing synthesis tools to potentially share reset logic
- Enabling better register packing optimizations
The power should remain excellent as we're still using synchronous design practices, and timing should remain optimal with zero violations.
