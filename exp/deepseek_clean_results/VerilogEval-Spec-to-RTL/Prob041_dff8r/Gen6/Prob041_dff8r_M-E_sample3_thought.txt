Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation with a twist:
1. Instead of individual flip-flops, we'll use a circular shift register structure
2. The reset will clear all bits synchronously
3. The input data will be loaded in parallel when not in reset
4. This approach demonstrates an alternative way to implement parallel loading while maintaining the same functionality
5. The circular nature (though not used here) could be useful for future expansion into a rotating register
