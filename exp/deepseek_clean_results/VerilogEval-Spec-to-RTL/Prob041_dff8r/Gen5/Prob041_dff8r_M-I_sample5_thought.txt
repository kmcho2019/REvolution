To optimize the PPA metrics, particularly focusing on reducing area while maintaining power efficiency and performance, we can:
1. Use a single always block with vector operations instead of individual flip-flop instances
2. Maintain synchronous reset behavior and positive edge triggering
3. Let the synthesis tool optimize the flip-flop implementation
This approach should:
- Reduce area by allowing better optimization of the flip-flop array
- Maintain good power characteristics due to simpler structure
- Keep zero timing violations as the basic structure remains similar
The vectorized implementation is more concise and typically synthesizes more efficiently in modern tools.
