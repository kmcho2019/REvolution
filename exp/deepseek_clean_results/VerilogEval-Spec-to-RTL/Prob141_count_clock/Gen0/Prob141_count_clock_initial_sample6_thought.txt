To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we need to create three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with BCD encoding. The design involves:
1. A seconds counter (0-59) that rolls over to increment minutes
2. A minutes counter (0-59) that rolls over to increment hours
3. A hours counter (1-12) with AM/PM toggle at 12:00
4. Synchronous reset to 12:00 AM with highest priority
5. All counters increment only when enabled (ena)
6. BCD encoding for each digit pair (tens and units)
