To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we need to create three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter should:
1. Reset to 12:00:00 AM when reset is high
2. Increment only when ena is high (once per second)
3. Use BCD encoding for each digit (two digits per time unit)
4. Handle rollover correctly:
   - Seconds roll over at 59 to 00 and increment minutes
   - Minutes roll over at 59 to 00 and increment hours
   - Hours roll over at 12 to 01 and toggle PM/AM
5. The PM signal should be toggled when hours change from 11 to 12
