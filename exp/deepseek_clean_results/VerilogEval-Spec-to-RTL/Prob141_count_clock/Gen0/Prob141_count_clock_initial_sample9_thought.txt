To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we need to create three counters (hours, minutes, seconds) that increment on the enable signal. Each counter should:
1. Reset to 12:00:00 AM when reset is high
2. Increment seconds from 00 to 59, then roll over to 00 and increment minutes
3. Increment minutes from 00 to 59, then roll over to 00 and increment hours
4. Increment hours from 01 to 12, then toggle AM/PM and reset to 01
5. Use BCD encoding for each digit (4 bits per digit, 8 bits total per time unit)
6. Handle the PM indicator (high for PM, low for AM)
7. All operations should be synchronous to the positive clock edge
