To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we need to create three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter should be in BCD format (two digits each). The design will:
1. Use synchronous reset (highest priority) to initialize to 12:00 AM
2. Increment seconds when ena is high, rolling over to minutes when seconds reach 59
3. Increment minutes when seconds roll over, rolling over to hours when minutes reach 59
4. Increment hours when minutes roll over, handling the 12-hour format and AM/PM toggle
5. The PM signal will be high when the time is PM (12:00 to 11:59)
6. All counters will be implemented with BCD digits to maintain proper decimal counting
