Refactoring Strategy:

3. Use continuous assignments for all increment and rollover conditions

- Makes PM indicator purely combinational for better timing
- Simplifies hour counter by using direct BCD encoding
- Reduces register usage by making more signals combinational
- Maintains identical functionality with cleaner structure
