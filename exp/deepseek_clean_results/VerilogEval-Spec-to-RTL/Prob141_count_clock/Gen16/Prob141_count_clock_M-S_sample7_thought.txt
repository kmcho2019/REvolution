Simplification Strategy:
1. Direct BCD counting for all counters to eliminate conversion logic
2. Single always block for all counters to reduce redundant logic

- Single always block improves readability
- Direct BCD counting simplifies hour handling
- PM toggling at 12:00 is more intuitive
- Maintains same functionality with less complexity
