Novel Architectural Idea: "Decoupled State Machine with Predictive Rollover"

Key Concepts:
1. Decoupled Control: Separate the timekeeping logic into independent state machines for each time unit (hours, minutes, seconds) that communicate through handshake signals
2. Predictive Rollover: Each counter predicts when it will roll over and pre-asserts a signal one cycle in advance
3. Early PM Calculation: Compute the PM indicator combinatorially based on current hour and pending increment
4. Unified BCD Encoding: Use a shared encoding scheme that handles both 1-12 (hours) and 00-59 (minutes/seconds) with consistent logic

Advantages:
- Better timing: Predictive signals break long combinational paths
- Lower power: Reduced glitching due to early signal stabilization
- Clearer state transitions: Each counter manages its own rollover independently
- Scalable: Easy to extend to additional time units (e.g., days)
