To optimize the 12-hour clock design, I'll focus on improving PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Simplify the PM toggle logic to only check at 11:59 -> 12:00 transition
   - Combine rollover detection into the counter blocks to reduce combinatorial paths
   - Remove intermediate rollover wires to reduce routing

2. Power Optimization:
   - Gate all counter logic with 'ena' to prevent unnecessary switching
   - Make PM toggle logic edge-triggered only when needed
   - Simplify conditions in always blocks

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share reset logic where possible
   - Remove redundant comparisons
   - Optimize BCD counter implementations

The improved design will maintain the same interface but with better timing closure, lower power consumption, and reduced area.
