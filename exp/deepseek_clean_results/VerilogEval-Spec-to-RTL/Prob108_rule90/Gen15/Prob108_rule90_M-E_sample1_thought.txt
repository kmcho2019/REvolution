Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Instead of processing all 512 bits in parallel each cycle, implement a serial processing approach with a sliding window
2. Use a 3-bit shift register to track the current cell and its neighbors (left, center, right)
3. Process one cell per clock cycle, rotating through all 512 cells in sequence
4. Maintain a counter to track the current position being processed
5. Store the next state in a separate register to avoid read-write conflicts
6. When not loading, update one cell per cycle using the Rule 90 XOR logic
7. This reduces area and power at the cost of requiring 512 cycles for a complete update

Advantages:
- Significant reduction in combinational logic (only 1 XOR gate needed)
- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
- Smaller area footprint
- Easier timing closure with shorter critical paths
