Novel approach using shift registers and pipelining:

1. Instead of calculating all 512 XOR operations in parallel, use a serial processing approach with shift registers
2. Implement a 3-stage shift register to track current cell and its neighbors
3. Process one cell per clock cycle, rotating through all 512 cells
4. Use a counter to track current processing position
5. Maintain two separate registers:
   - One for current state (updated only after full cycle)
   - One for next state (built incrementally)
6. Advantages:
   - Dramatically reduces combinatorial logic
   - Lower power consumption (only one XOR active per cycle)
   - Easier timing closure
   - Scales better to larger arrays
7. Trade-off:
   - Requires 512 cycles to complete one full update
   - But allows much higher clock frequency
8. Boundary conditions handled by masking the shift register inputs
