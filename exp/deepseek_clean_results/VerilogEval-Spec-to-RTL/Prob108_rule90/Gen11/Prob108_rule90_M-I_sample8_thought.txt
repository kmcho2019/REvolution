Improvement strategy for better PPA:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Pipeline the design by splitting into 4 stages (128 bits each) to reduce critical path
   - Register intermediate results to break long combinational paths
2. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating for inactive stages when load isn't asserted
   - Use enable signals to reduce unnecessary switching
3. Area Optimization:
   - Share boundary condition logic between adjacent stages
   - Use more compact XOR implementations where possible

The improved design will:
- Maintain same functionality with correct boundary conditions
- Reduce critical path by 4x through pipelining
- Lower power through clock gating and enable logic
- Slightly reduce area through shared boundary logic
