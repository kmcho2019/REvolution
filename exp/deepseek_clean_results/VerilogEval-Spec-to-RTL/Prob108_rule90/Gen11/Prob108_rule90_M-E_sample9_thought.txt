Novel approach using shift registers and parallel XOR computation:
1. Instead of individual neighbor connections, use two shift registers to capture left and right neighbors simultaneously
2. The main shift register holds current state (q)
3. Left neighbor shift register stores q shifted right by 1 (with 0 inserted at MSB)
4. Right neighbor shift register stores q shifted left by 1 (with 0 inserted at LSB)
5. Compute next state by XORing corresponding bits from left and right shift registers
6. This approach reduces wiring complexity and may improve timing
7. Boundary conditions are naturally handled by the shift operations
8. Maintain the same clocked behavior with load functionality
