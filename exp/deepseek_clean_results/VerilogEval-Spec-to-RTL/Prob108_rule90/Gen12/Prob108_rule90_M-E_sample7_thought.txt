Novel Architecture Idea: Pipelined Rule 90 with Shift Register Approach

Key Innovations:
1. Shift Register Implementation:
   - Instead of parallel XOR gates, use a shift register approach that naturally handles neighbor relationships
   - This reduces combinatorial logic depth and improves timing

2. Two-Stage Pipeline:
   - Stage 1: Capture left and right neighbors through shift operations
   - Stage 2: Compute XOR of neighbors
   - This breaks the long combinatorial path into two shorter stages

3. Boundary Handling:
   - Use register concatenation for clean boundary conditions (0's automatically inserted)
   - Eliminates need for conditional logic at boundaries

4. Power Optimization:
   - Only shift registers when not loading (reduces unnecessary toggling)
   - Smaller clock domain for shift operations

Advantages:
- Better timing due to pipelining
- Reduced combinatorial logic
- Cleaner boundary handling
- Lower power when idle
- More scalable for larger arrays
