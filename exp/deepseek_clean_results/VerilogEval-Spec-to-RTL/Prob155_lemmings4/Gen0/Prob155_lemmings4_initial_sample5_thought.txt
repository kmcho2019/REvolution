To implement the Lemming behavior, we need a finite state machine (FSM) with multiple states and transitions. The key states are:
1. WALK_LEFT - walking left
2. WALK_RIGHT - walking right
3. FALL - falling (aaah)
4. DIG - digging
5. SPLAT - splattered (dead)

The FSM needs to handle:
- Direction changes when bumped (unless falling or digging)
- Falling when ground=0 (highest priority)
- Digging when dig=1 and walking (medium priority)
- Counting fall duration (20 cycles max)
- Splattering if falling too long
- Resetting to WALK_LEFT on areset

We'll use a counter to track fall duration and state transitions based on the priority rules.
