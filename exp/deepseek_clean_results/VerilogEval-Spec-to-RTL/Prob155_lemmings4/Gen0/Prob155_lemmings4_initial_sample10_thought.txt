To implement the Lemming behavior, we need a finite state machine (FSM) with multiple states and transitions. The key states are:
1. WALK_LEFT - walking left
2. WALK_RIGHT - walking right
3. FALL_LEFT - falling while previously walking left
4. FALL_RIGHT - falling while previously walking right
5. DIG_LEFT - digging while previously walking left
6. DIG_RIGHT - digging while previously walking right
7. SPLAT - splattered (dead)

The FSM transitions between these states based on inputs:
- bump_left/bump_right change walking direction
- ground=0 causes falling
- dig=1 starts digging (if on ground)
- Falling for >20 cycles causes splatter when ground returns

We'll also need a counter to track fall duration. The outputs are determined by the current state.

The priority of conditions is:
1. Falling (highest)
2. Digging
3. Bumping
