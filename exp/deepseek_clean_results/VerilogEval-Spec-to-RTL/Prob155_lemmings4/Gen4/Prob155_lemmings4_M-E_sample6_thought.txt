Here's a novel approach using separate state machines for direction and action:

1. Split the design into two parallel state machines:
   - Direction FSM: tracks left/right walking direction
   - Action FSM: handles walking/falling/digging/splatter states

2. Key advantages:
   - Clear separation of concerns
   - Simplified transition logic in each FSM
   - Easier to maintain priority rules (fall > dig > direction change)
   - More modular design

3. Special features:
   - Direction is preserved during falls/digs
   - Fall timer only counts during actual falls
   - Clean handling of splatter condition
   - Explicit priority encoding in transition logic

4. The two FSMs communicate through:
   - Current direction (left/right)
   - Action state (walk/fall/dig/splatter)
   - Shared ground/dig/bump inputs
