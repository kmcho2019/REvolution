The original implementation correctly implements an AND gate but uses an always block with a registered output. For a purely combinational circuit like this, a continuous assignment would be more appropriate and slightly more power efficient. The improvement strategy is to:
1. Replace the always block with a continuous assignment
2. Use a wire output instead of reg
3. Maintain the same AND functionality
This change will eliminate the implicit latch behavior of the always block and reduce power consumption slightly, while maintaining the same timing and area characteristics.
