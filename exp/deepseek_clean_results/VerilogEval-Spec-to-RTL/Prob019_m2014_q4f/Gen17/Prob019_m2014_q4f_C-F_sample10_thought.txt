The fusion strategy combines the strengths of both examples while maintaining optimal PPA metrics:

1. From Example 1: The ternary operator approach is elegant and concise, demonstrating how selection logic can implement Boolean operations efficiently. This approach often synthesizes well and is readable.

2. From Example 2: The explicit bitwise operation (& ~) clearly shows the intended logic function (AND with inverted input), making the design intent immediately obvious to readers.

The superior fused solution:
- Uses the explicit bitwise operation for clarity of intent (from Example 2)
- Maintains the continuous assignment style (from Example 1) which is most appropriate for simple combinational logic
- Results in identical PPA metrics (since all implementations synthesize to the same basic gates)
- Provides the clearest expression of the design intent while maintaining optimal implementation

This fusion gives us the best of both worlds: clarity of the logic operation and the conciseness of continuous assignment.
