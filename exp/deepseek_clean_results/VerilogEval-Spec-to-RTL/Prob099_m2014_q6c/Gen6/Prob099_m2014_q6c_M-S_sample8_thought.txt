The key simplifications are:
1. Focus only on implementing Y1 (next state A) and Y3 (next state D) as specified
2. Recognize that Y1 only needs to be active in two cases (A->A and D->A)
3. Recognize that Y3 is simply active whenever w=1 in states B, C, E, or F
4. Use direct bitwise operations without parentheses since Verilog operator precedence makes them unnecessary
5. Keep the one-hot encoding benefits by checking individual y bits

The simplified implementation will:
- Make Y1 just the OR of y[0]&w and y[3]&w
- Make Y3 just w ANDed with the OR of states B,C,E,F (y[1], y[2], y[4], y[5])
- Use minimal logical operations while maintaining correctness
