   - Y1 is active in states E and F (y[4] and y[5])
   - Y3 is active when:
     * In state A and w=1 (stays in A)
     * In state B and w=1 (goes to D)
     * In state C and w=1 (goes to D)
     * In state D and w=1 (goes to A)
     * In state E and w=1 (goes to D)
     * In state F and w=1 (goes to D)
