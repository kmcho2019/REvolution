Fusion Strategy:
1. Combine the clarity of structural implementation (Example 1) with the conciseness of continuous assignment (Example 2)
2. Use the fundamental mux logic (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) but implement it directly in a single continuous assignment
3. Eliminate intermediate wires to reduce area and power while maintaining readability
4. Keep the Boolean expression form which clearly shows the mux functionality
5. This approach provides:
   - Single assignment for better timing (shorter critical path)
   - No intermediate wires (lower area and power)
   - Clear hardware mapping (still shows AND-OR structure)
   - Likely to map to optimal MUX primitive in synthesis
   - More readable than gate-level but equally efficient
