Instead of using a ternary operator, we can implement the multiplexer using basic logic gates (AND, OR, NOT). The idea is to:
1. Generate the complement of sel (sel_n)
2. AND a with sel_n (selects a when sel=0)
3. AND b with sel (selects b when sel=1)
4. OR the results of these two AND operations to get the final output
This gate-level implementation demonstrates the fundamental logic behind multiplexer operation.
