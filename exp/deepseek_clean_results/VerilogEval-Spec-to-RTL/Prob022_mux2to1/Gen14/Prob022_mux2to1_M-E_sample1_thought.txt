Novel Implementation Idea:
1. Instead of using a ternary operator or if-else, implement the mux using bitwise operations
2. The approach uses AND gates to mask the inputs based on sel, then ORs the results
3. When sel=0: (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) = (a & 1) | (b & 0) = a
4. When sel=1: (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) = (a & 0) | (b & 1) = b
5. This is a gate-level implementation that shows the underlying hardware more explicitly

Advantages:
- Demonstrates the fundamental logic gates behind a mux
- Doesn't rely on higher-level operators
- Shows how selection works at the bit level
- May map more directly to some FPGA architectures
