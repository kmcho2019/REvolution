Novel Approach: Gate-Level Multiplexer with Explicit AND-OR Structure
1. Instead of using high-level operators, implement the mux using fundamental logic gates
2. Break down the mux function into its canonical form: out = (a AND NOT sel) OR (b AND sel)
3. Benefits:
   - Explicitly shows the underlying gate-level implementation
   - May offer better control for synthesis optimizations
   - Demonstrates understanding of digital logic fundamentals
   - Can be more power-efficient in some technologies by avoiding ternary operators
4. The implementation uses:
   - One NOT gate for sel inversion
   - Two AND gates for the selection paths
   - One OR gate to combine the paths
