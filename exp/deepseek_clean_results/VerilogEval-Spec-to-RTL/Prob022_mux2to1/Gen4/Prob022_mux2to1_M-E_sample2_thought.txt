Let's implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a gate-level approach with AND-OR logic. The idea is to use sel and its complement to gate the inputs a and b, then OR the results together. This creates a hardware structure where:
- When sel=0: b is blocked (AND with 0) and a passes (AND with 1)
- When sel=1: a is blocked and b passes
The OR gate combines these gated signals to produce the final output.
