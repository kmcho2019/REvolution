The original implementation is already optimal for a simple 2-to-1 multiplexer in terms of PPA (Performance, Power, Area). The ternary operator implementation maps directly to a single multiplexer primitive in hardware, resulting in:
1. Minimal propagation delay (best possible performance)
2. Lowest possible power consumption (single gate)
3. Smallest possible area (single gate)

No further optimization is possible at this level of abstraction. The only potential improvements would come from:
- Technology-specific optimizations (which would be handled by the synthesis tool)
- Architectural changes at a higher level (if this mux were part of a larger system)

Therefore, the original solution should be maintained as it's already optimal.
