The simplest implementation of a 2-to-1 multiplexer can be achieved with a single ternary operator assignment. This approach:
1. Directly implements the mux functionality in one line
2. Requires no additional wires or parameters
3. Is both human-readable and synthesis-friendly
4. Results in optimal PPA metrics (as shown in the feedback)
5. Matches exactly the required behavior: out = (sel) ? b : a
