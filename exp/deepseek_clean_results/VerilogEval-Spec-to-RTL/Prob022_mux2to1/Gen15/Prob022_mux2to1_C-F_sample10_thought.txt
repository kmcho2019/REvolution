Fusion Strategy for Optimal 2:1 Multiplexer Implementation:

   - From Example 1: Utilize the concise ternary operator for its direct mapping to hardware and clean syntax

   - Leverage the hardware efficiency of continuous assignment

   - Avoid redundant logic by letting the synthesis tool optimize the implementation

3. **PPA Benefits**:

     * Most clearly expresses the multiplexer functionality
     * Typically synthesizes to the most efficient hardware
     * Is more readable than explicit gate-level description
     * Allows synthesis tools to perform optimal technology mapping
