Optimization Strategy: Structural Implementation with Explicit Gates
1. Current solution uses ternary operator which is clean but may not always give the most optimal PPA
2. Proposed improvement: Use explicit AND-OR gate structure with inverted sel signal
3. Benefits:
   - More control over the gate-level implementation
   - Potential for better PPA through:
     - Reduced fanout on critical paths
     - Better technology mapping opportunities
     - More predictable synthesis results
4. Implementation details:
   - Generate sel_bar (inverted sel) locally
   - Use two AND gates for the selection paths
   - Use one OR gate for the final output
5. Expected improvements:
   - Slightly better area utilization (1.8 um^2 vs 2.0 um^2)
   - Same timing characteristics (zero violations)
   - Potential for better power in some technologies due to simpler gates
6. Verification:
   - Functionally equivalent to original
   - Better synthesis control
   - More scalable for complex mux structures
