Novel Approach: Bitwise Operation Mux
1. Instead of using a ternary operator or gate-level logic, implement the mux using bitwise operations
2. The key insight is that we can use the sel signal to mask the inputs:
   - When sel=0: (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) = (a & 1) | (b & 0) = a
   - When sel=1: (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) = (a & 0) | (b & 1) = b
3. Benefits:
   - Demonstrates an alternative mathematical approach to mux implementation
   - Shows how bitwise operations can achieve selection logic
   - Still synthesizes efficiently to standard mux structures
   - Provides a different perspective on multiplexer functionality
   - Maintains single-cycle operation with minimal logic depth
