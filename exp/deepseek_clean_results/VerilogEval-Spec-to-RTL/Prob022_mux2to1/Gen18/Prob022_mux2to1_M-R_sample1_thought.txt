Refactoring Approach:
1. Maintain the same logical functionality (2-to-1 mux) but express it differently
2. Use ternary conditional operator (? :) instead of bitwise operations
3. Benefits:
   - More readable as it directly expresses the mux selection behavior
   - Still synthesizes to the same hardware (AND-OR structure or MUX primitive)
   - More intuitive for software engineers transitioning to HDL
   - Typically results in identical PPA metrics as the original
4. The ternary operator is functionally equivalent to (a & ~sel) | (b & sel) but expresses the selection more naturally
