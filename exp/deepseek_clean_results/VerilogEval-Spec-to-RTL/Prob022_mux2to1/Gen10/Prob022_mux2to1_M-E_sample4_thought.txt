Let's implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a novel approach based on XOR-based selection logic. This method:
1. Uses XOR gates to create a selection mechanism that doesn't require explicit AND/OR gates
2. The output is derived from (a & ~sel) XOR (b & sel), which mathematically reduces to the mux function
3. Offers potential advantages in certain technologies where XOR gates are more efficient
4. Provides a different propagation delay characteristic that might be beneficial in some timing scenarios
5. Could offer better testability due to the XOR structure
