A novel approach to implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer is to use a transmission gate-based design. Transmission gates are particularly efficient for multiplexers as they allow bidirectional signal flow and have lower capacitance compared to standard logic gates. In this implementation:
1. When sel=0, the transmission gate for 'a' is enabled (using sel and its complement)
2. When sel=1, the transmission gate for 'b' is enabled
3. The output is driven by whichever transmission gate is active
This approach reduces the number of active devices and can potentially improve power efficiency.
