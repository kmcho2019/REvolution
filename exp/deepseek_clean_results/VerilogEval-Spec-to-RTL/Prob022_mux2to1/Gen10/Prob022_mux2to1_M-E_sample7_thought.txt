Here's a novel approach using transmission gates for a 2:1 mux implementation. This method:
1. Uses pass-transistor logic instead of standard logic gates
2. Implements the mux with two transmission gates controlled by complementary select signals
3. Requires an inverter to generate sel_bar, but eliminates the need for AND/OR logic
4. Can potentially offer better performance in certain technologies due to reduced transistor count

The key advantages are:
- Only 4 transistors needed (2 for transmission gates + 2 for inverter)
- Potentially lower power consumption as there's no direct path to VDD/GND
- Symmetrical rise/fall times when properly sized
