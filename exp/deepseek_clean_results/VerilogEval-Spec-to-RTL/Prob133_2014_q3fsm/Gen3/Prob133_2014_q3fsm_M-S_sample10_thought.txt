To simplify the FSM implementation:
1. Reduce to just 2 states (A and B) as originally designed
2. Eliminate separate cycle counter by tracking state transitions
3. Use a 2-bit shift register to track last 3 w values instead of counting
4. Check for exactly two 1's in the shift register when complete
5. Output z is simply whether we have exactly two 1's in the shift register
6. Reset clears all state and returns to A
