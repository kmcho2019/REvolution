The simplest and most efficient way to implement a NOT gate in Verilog is to use the bitwise NOT operator (~). This approach:
1. Directly implements the logical NOT function with minimal syntax
2. Results in the most optimized hardware implementation (typically a single transistor pair)
3. Clearly expresses the design intent without unnecessary constructs
4. Provides identical functionality while being more readable and maintainable
5. Will synthesize to the most area and power efficient implementation
