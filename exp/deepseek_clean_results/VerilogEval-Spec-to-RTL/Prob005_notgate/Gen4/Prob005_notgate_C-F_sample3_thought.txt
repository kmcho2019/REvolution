Both examples demonstrate optimal implementations of a NOT gate using the Verilog negation operator (~). The fusion strategy here is straightforward since both solutions are essentially identical and already optimal. The NOT gate is a fundamental logic operation, and the most efficient implementation is a direct continuous assignment using the ~ operator. This approach provides:
1. Minimal timing (single gate delay)
2. Lowest power consumption (single gate operation)
3. Smallest area (single transistor pair)
No further optimization is possible at the RTL level while maintaining the NOT gate functionality.
