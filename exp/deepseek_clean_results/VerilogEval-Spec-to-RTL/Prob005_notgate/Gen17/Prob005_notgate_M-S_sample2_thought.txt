1. A single continuous assignment
2. One bitwise NOT operator (~)
3. Minimal module declaration syntax

- No registers are needed (pure combinational)
- No additional logic operations are required
- No hierarchical structure is beneficial
- No parameters or generate statements are applicable

- Changing the required functionality
- Violating the interface specification
