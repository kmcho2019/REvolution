The original solution is already optimal in terms of functionality and nearly optimal for PPA. However, we can make one minor improvement by eliminating the intermediate wire in Alternative 3 to slightly reduce power consumption and simplify the design. The direct continuous assignment without an intermediate wire is the most efficient implementation for a NOT gate in Verilog.

Improvement strategy:
1. Remove the unnecessary intermediate wire declaration
2. Use a single continuous assignment for the NOT operation
3. This reduces the number of nodes that could toggle, slightly improving power
4. Maintains the same timing and area characteristics
