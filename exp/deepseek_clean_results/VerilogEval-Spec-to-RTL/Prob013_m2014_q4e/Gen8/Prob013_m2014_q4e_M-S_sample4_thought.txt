1. A single continuous assignment
2. The minimal logical expression (~(in1 | in2))
3. No unnecessary hierarchy or complexity

- It's already a one-line implementation
- The logical expression is the most direct representation of a NOR operation

- Code length (shortest possible)
- Readability (clear NOR operation)
