The current implementation is already optimal for a basic 2-input NOR gate in terms of PPA metrics. However, we can make one minor improvement in readability and maintainability while maintaining the same excellent PPA:

1. Use the alternative NOR operator (~|) which is more idiomatic in Verilog for NOR operations
2. Keep the truth table comment for documentation
3. Maintain the continuous assignment style for optimal synthesis

This change makes the code slightly more readable to experienced Verilog designers while maintaining identical synthesis results and PPA metrics. The ~| operator is the most direct representation of a NOR operation in Verilog.
