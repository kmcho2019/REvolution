Simplification Strategy:
1. Eliminate separate AND gate modules since the logic is simple enough to implement directly
2. Use continuous assignments for both AND and OR operations in a single module
3. Remove intermediate wires since they're not necessary for this simple logic
4. Maintain the exact same functionality by keeping the same gate connections
5. Reduce hierarchy levels to improve timing and area

The simplified design will:
- Have identical functionality to the original
- Be more compact and readable
- Potentially have better timing due to flattened logic
- Use less area by eliminating module instantiations
- Maintain good power characteristics
