To optimize the PPA metrics, I'll implement a flatter design by:
1. Eliminating the hierarchical sub-modules (AND3, AND2, OR2) and implementing the logic directly in the TopModule
2. Using continuous assignments (assign statements) for all outputs
3. Removing intermediate wire declarations by combining the AND and OR operations in single expressions
4. Maintaining the same logical functionality while reducing hierarchy levels and potential routing overhead

This approach should:
- Reduce area by eliminating module boundaries and unused ports
- Improve timing by reducing hierarchy levels
- Maintain or slightly improve power by reducing switching activity on intermediate nets
- Keep the same logical functionality while being more concise
