Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Use a single module with direct continuous assignments for all logic operations
2. Eliminate all submodules to reduce hierarchy overhead
3. Implement both AND and OR operations in one level using Verilog operators
4. Use concatenation for parallel operations where possible
5. Optimize for minimal logic depth and maximum clarity

Key Advantages:
- Single-level implementation reduces synthesis overhead
- Direct assignments minimize propagation delay
- Clear and concise implementation shows all operations at once
- No intermediate wires needed for such simple logic
- Easier to understand at a glance while maintaining efficiency
