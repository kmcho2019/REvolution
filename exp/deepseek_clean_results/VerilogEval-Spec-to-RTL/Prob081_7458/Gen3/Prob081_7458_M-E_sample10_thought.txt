Here's a novel approach using hierarchical design with submodules to represent the logical groupings. Instead of direct assignments, we'll create separate modules for the AND and OR functions, then instantiate them to build the complete circuit. This approach:
1. Better reflects modular design principles
2. Makes the design more scalable for complex logic
3. Allows for easier debugging and modification of individual logic blocks
4. Provides clearer separation of concerns
5. Could enable technology-specific optimizations for different logic blocks
