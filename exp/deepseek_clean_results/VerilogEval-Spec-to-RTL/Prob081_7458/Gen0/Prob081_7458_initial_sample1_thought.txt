To implement the 7458 chip functionality, we need to create two main outputs:
1. p1y: OR of two 3-input AND gates (p1a&p1b&p1c) and (p1d&p1e&p1f)
2. p2y: OR of two 2-input AND gates (p2a&p2b) and (p2c&p2d)

We'll use intermediate wires for the AND gate outputs to make the design clearer and more modular.
