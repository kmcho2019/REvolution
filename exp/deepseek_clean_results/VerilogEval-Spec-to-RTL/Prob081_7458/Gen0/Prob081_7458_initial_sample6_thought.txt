The problem requires implementing a module that mimics the behavior of a 7458 chip, which consists of four AND gates and two OR gates. The solution involves:
1. Creating two 3-input AND gates for p1y (combining p1a,p1b,p1c and p1d,p1e,p1f)
2. Creating two 2-input AND gates for p2y (combining p2a,p2b and p2c,p2d)
3. OR-ing the outputs of each pair of AND gates to produce p1y and p2y respectively
4. Using intermediate wires for the AND gate outputs to make the design clearer
