The 7458 chip functionality can be implemented by creating four AND gates (two 3-input and two 2-input) and two OR gates. For p1y, we'll AND p1a, p1b, p1c together and p1d, p1e, p1f together, then OR these two results. For p2y, we'll AND p2a with p2b and p2c with p2d, then OR these two results. We'll use intermediate wires for the AND gate outputs to make the design clearer.
