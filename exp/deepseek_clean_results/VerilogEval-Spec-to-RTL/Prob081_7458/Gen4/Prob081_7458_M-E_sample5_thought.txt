Hierarchical Strategy:
1. Break down the problem into fundamental gate components (AND and OR)
2. Create reusable submodules for 2-input and 3-input AND gates
3. Create a submodule for the 2-input OR gate
4. Instantiate these submodules to build the complete circuit
5. Benefits:
   - Explicit representation of each logical operation
   - Better modularity and reusability
   - Clear separation of concerns
   - Easier debugging and modification
   - Potential for gate-level optimization
