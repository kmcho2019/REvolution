Simplification Strategy:
1. Eliminate separate AND modules since they're simple enough to implement directly
2. Use direct assign statements for both AND and OR operations
3. Remove intermediate wires since they're not necessary for this simple logic
4. Keep all functionality intact while reducing hierarchy and module count
5. Maintain clear signal naming for readability

The simplified design will:
- Have identical functionality to the original
- Be more compact with fewer lines of code
- Reduce hierarchy overhead
- Maintain good timing characteristics
- Be easier to understand at a glance
