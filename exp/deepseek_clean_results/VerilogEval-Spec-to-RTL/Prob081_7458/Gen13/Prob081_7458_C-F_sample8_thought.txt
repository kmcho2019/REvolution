Fusion Strategy:
1. Combine the clarity of hierarchical modules (from Example 1) with the efficiency of direct assignments (from Example 2)
2. Keep the intermediate wire declarations for better readability and debugging
3. Use direct AND operations in the wire declarations to avoid unnecessary module hierarchy
4. Maintain separate assign statements for OR operations to clearly show the logical structure
5. This approach provides both readability and optimization potential

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates module hierarchy overhead while keeping logical structure clear
- Uses direct wire assignments for AND operations (more efficient than submodules)
- Maintains explicit OR operations for output clarity
- Provides better synthesis optimization potential than hierarchical approach
- Easier to debug than fully flattened design due to named intermediate signals
