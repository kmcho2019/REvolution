Novel Architecture Idea: Bitwise Parallel Processing with Shared Logic
1. Instead of separate AND modules, implement all logic within TopModule using continuous assignments
2. Use bitwise operations to compute all AND results in parallel
3. Eliminate intermediate wires by directly computing the OR results from the AND operations
4. This approach minimizes hierarchy and propagation delay while maintaining clarity
5. The design will be more compact and potentially faster due to reduced logic levels
6. Power consumption may improve due to fewer signal transitions between modules
