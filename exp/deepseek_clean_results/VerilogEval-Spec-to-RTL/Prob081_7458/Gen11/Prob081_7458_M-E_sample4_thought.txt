Novel architectural idea: Hierarchical AND-OR structure with explicit gate-level modeling
1. Instead of using direct assign statements, implement each logical gate as a separate module instance
2. Create reusable AND and OR gate modules for better modularity and potential reuse
3. Use named intermediate nets for better debugging and waveform visibility
4. This approach provides clearer gate-level representation and better matches the physical chip structure
5. While slightly more verbose, it offers better hierarchical organization for complex designs
