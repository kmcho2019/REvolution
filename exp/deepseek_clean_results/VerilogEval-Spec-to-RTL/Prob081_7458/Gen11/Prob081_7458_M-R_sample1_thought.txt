Refactoring approach:
1. Instead of using direct assign statements, we'll use intermediate wires to represent the AND gate outputs
2. This makes the design more modular and easier to debug/analyze
3. The logical functionality remains identical but the structure is more explicit
4. Each AND operation gets its own named wire, making the design more self-documenting
5. The OR operations then combine these intermediate signals
6. This approach might be preferable in more complex designs where intermediate signals need to be probed or reused
