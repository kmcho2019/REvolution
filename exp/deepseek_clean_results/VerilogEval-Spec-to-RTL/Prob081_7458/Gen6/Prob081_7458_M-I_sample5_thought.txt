To optimize the PPA metrics, we'll implement a flat design using direct assign statements instead of hierarchical modules. This approach will:
1. Reduce hierarchy levels, improving timing (performance)
2. Eliminate intermediate wire declarations, reducing area
3. Potentially reduce switching activity by combining operations, improving power
4. Maintain the same logical functionality while being more efficient

The direct implementation will use compound logical expressions in assign statements to compute the outputs without intermediate modules or wires.
