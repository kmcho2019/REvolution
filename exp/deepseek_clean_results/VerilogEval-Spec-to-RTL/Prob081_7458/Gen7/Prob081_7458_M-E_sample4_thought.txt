Novel Approach: Hierarchical Design with Submodules
1. Break down the design into smaller functional blocks for better modularity
2. Create separate AND and OR gate modules to promote reuse
3. Instantiate these modules to build the complete circuit
4. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of concerns
   - Easier debugging and testing of individual components
   - Potential for parameterization if needed in future
   - Better reflects the actual chip's internal structure
5. While slightly more verbose, this approach provides better scalability for more complex designs
