Refactoring Approach:
1. Goal: Maintain identical functionality while demonstrating alternative coding styles
2. Key changes:
   - Eliminate intermediate wires to show direct assignment approach
   - Use continuous assignments exclusively (no wire declarations)
   - Combine AND-OR operations in single expressions for each output
3. Benefits:
   - Shows more compact implementation
   - Demonstrates how Verilog can express logic directly in output assignments
   - Still maintains good readability for this simple case
   - May lead to slightly better synthesis results as it gives the optimizer more flexibility
4. Tradeoffs:
   - Less explicit documentation of intermediate steps
   - Still clear enough for this simple logic
   - Maintains same PPA characteristics
