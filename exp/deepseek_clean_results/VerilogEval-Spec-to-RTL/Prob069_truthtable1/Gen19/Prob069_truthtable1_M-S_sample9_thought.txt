The truth table shows that f is 1 in two distinct cases:
1. When x2 is 1 and x3 is 0 (rows 3,4)
2. When x1 is 1 and x3 is 1 (rows 6,8)

We can observe that these conditions can be directly expressed as:
f = (¬x3 ∧ x2) ∨ (x3 ∧ x1)

This is already the minimal sum-of-products form. The original solution is already optimal in terms of gate count (2 ANDs and 1 OR). However, we can make the code slightly more concise by eliminating the intermediate wires and combining the expressions directly in the assign statement.
