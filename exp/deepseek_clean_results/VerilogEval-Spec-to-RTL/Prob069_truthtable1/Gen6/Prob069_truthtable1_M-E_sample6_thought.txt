An alternative approach is to recognize that the output 'f' can be expressed as a combination of AND-OR logic where:
1. f is true when (x3=0 AND x2=1) OR (x3=1 AND x1=1)
2. This can be implemented using basic AND and OR gates
3. The implementation will have two AND gates (one for each condition) and one OR gate to combine them

This approach is different from the mux-based solution as it explicitly shows the logical conditions from the truth table and uses fundamental logic gates. It provides better visibility into the actual logical relationships between inputs and output.
