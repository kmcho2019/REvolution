An alternative approach is to use a multiplexer-based implementation. We can treat x3 as the selector for a 2:1 mux:
- When x3=0, the output follows x2 (since f=1 when x2=1 regardless of x1)
- When x3=1, the output follows x1 (since f=1 when x1=1 regardless of x2)

This results in a clean mux implementation: f = x3 ? x1 : x2
