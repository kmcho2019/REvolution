The truth table shows that f can be expressed as a simple Boolean function of the inputs. Analyzing the patterns:
- f is 1 when either:
  - x2 is 1 and x3 is 0 (rows 3-4), or
  - x1 is 1 and x3 is 1 (rows 6,8)
This can be implemented with just two AND gates and one OR gate: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)
This is more efficient than the mux implementation while maintaining the same functionality.
