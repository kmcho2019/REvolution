The truth table shows that the output f is 1 in three cases:
1. When x2=1 and x3=0 (rows 3 and 4)
2. When x1=1 and x3=1 (rows 6 and 8)

This can be expressed as a simple logical OR of these two conditions:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)

This implementation:
1. Uses minimal logic gates (just 2 ANDs, 1 OR, and 1 NOT)
2. Is more efficient than the case statement approach
3. Clearly shows the logical relationship between inputs and output
4. Has identical functionality to the original truth table
