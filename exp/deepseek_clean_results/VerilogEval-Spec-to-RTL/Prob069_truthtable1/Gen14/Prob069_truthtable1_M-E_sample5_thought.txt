An alternative approach is to recognize that the output f is true when:
1. x2 is true and x3 is false (rows 3-4), OR
2. x1 is true and x3 is true (rows 6,8)
This can be expressed as a logical OR of two AND conditions:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)
This implementation uses basic logic gates (NOT, AND, OR) to achieve the same functionality.
