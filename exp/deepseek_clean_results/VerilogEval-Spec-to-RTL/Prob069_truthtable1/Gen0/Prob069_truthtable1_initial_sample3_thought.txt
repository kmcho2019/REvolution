The truth table shows that the output f is 1 in four cases:
1. When x3=0 and x2=1 (regardless of x1)
2. When x3=1 and x2=0 and x1=1
3. When x3=1 and x2=1 and x1=1

This can be simplified to the logical expression: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & ~x2 & x1) | (x3 & x2 & x1)
Further simplification using Boolean algebra:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1) because (x3 & ~x2 & x1) | (x3 & x2 & x1) = x3 & x1
