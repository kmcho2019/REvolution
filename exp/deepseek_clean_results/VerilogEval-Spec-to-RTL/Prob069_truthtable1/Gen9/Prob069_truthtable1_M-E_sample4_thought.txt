An alternative approach is to implement the truth table using a sum-of-products (SOP) form derived from the minterms where the output f is 1. From the truth table, we can see that f is 1 in three cases:
1. When x3=0, x2=1 (regardless of x1)
2. When x3=1, x2=0, x1=1
3. When x3=1, x2=1, x1=1

This can be expressed as: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & ~x2 & x1) | (x3 & x2 & x1)
We can simplify this to: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1) since the last two terms share x3 & x1
This results in a compact SOP implementation using basic logic gates.
