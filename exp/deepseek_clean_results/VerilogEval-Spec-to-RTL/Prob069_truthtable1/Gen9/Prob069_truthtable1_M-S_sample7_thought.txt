The truth table shows that the output f is 1 in two cases:
1. When x3=0 and x2=1 (rows 3 and 4)
2. When x3=1 and x1=1 (rows 6 and 8)

This can be expressed as a simple OR of these two conditions:
f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1)

This implementation:
- Uses only basic logic operations
- Has minimal gate count (two ANDs and one OR)
- Maintains the same functionality as the original
- Is more straightforward than the mux-based approach
