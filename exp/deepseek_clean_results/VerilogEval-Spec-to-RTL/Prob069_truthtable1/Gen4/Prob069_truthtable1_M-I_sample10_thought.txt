The current implementation is already optimal in terms of gate count and logic structure. However, we can make one minor improvement by eliminating the explicit NOT gate through De Morgan's laws, which might slightly reduce power consumption by removing one gate operation. The logic can be rewritten as:

f = (x3' & x2) | (x3 & x1)
   = (x3' & x2) + (x3 & x1)

This maintains the same functionality while potentially reducing power slightly. The area remains the same since we're still using two ANDs and one OR gate, just rearranged slightly.
