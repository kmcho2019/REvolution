The optimal solution combines the best aspects of both examples while improving clarity and maintainability:

1. **Boolean Expression Insight (Example 1)**:
   - The expression (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1) is mathematically minimal
   - Clearly shows the logical relationship between inputs
   - Easy to verify against the truth table

2. **MUX Insight (Example 2)**:
   - The observation that x3 acts as a selector between x1 and x2
   - Hardware-efficient implementation concept

3. **Fusion Strategy**:
   - Use the Boolean expression form for its clarity and direct mapping to logic
   - Maintain the MUX concept in the thought process for understanding
   - The expression form is more readable than ternary operator for this case
   - Explicitly shows the two cases (x3=0 and x3=1) for better documentation

4. **Improvements**:
   - More maintainable than pure MUX syntax as the logic is explicit
   - Same excellent PPA metrics as both examples
   - Easier to modify if requirements change
   - Better documentation value as the logic is clearly visible

5. **Implementation Choice**:
   - Pure combinational logic with continuous assignment
   - No unnecessary complexity
   - Optimal for both ASIC and FPGA implementations
   - Clear mapping to standard cells
