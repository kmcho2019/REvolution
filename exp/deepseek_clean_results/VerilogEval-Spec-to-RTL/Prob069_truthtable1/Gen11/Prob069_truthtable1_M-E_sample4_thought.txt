An alternative approach is to recognize that the output f can be expressed as a combination of minterms. Looking at the truth table, f is true in four cases:
1. When x3=0 and x2=1 (regardless of x1)
2. When x3=1 and x2=0 and x1=1
3. When x3=1 and x2=1 and x1=1

This can be implemented using OR gates combining these conditions. The first condition covers two cases (x1=0 and x1=1) when x3=0 and x2=1. The other two conditions are specific cases when x3=1. This implementation uses basic logic gates instead of a multiplexer.
