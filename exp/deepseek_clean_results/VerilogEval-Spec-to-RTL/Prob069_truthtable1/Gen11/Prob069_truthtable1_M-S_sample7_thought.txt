The truth table shows that the output f is 1 when either:
1. x2 is 1 and x3 is 0, or
2. x1 is 1 and x3 is 1

This can be expressed more concisely using a multiplexer-like structure where x3 selects between x2 and x1. The expression becomes:
f = x3 ? x1 : x2

This implementation:
- Uses a single ternary operator (equivalent to a 2:1 mux)
- Has minimal logical operations (just one selection operation)
- Maintains identical functionality
- Is more readable and intuitive
