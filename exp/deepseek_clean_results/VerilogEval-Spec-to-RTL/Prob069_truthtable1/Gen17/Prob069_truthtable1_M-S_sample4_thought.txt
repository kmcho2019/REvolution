The truth table shows that the output f is 1 in two cases:
1. When x2 is 1 and x3 is 0 (rows 3 and 4)
2. When x1 is 1 and x3 is 1 (rows 6 and 8)

This can be expressed as a simple Boolean expression: f = (~x3 & x2) | (x3 & x1). This implementation:
1. Uses minimal logic gates (2 ANDs, 1 OR, and 1 NOT)
2. Eliminates the need for a case statement
3. Matches the truth table exactly
4. Is more efficient in terms of power and area
