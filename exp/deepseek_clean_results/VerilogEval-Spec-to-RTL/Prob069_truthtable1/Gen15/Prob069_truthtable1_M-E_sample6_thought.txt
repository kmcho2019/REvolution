Instead of using a mux, we can implement the function using a 3-to-8 decoder with output gating:
1. First, decode the 3-bit input (x3,x2,x1) into 8 possible minterms
2. Then OR together the minterms where f=1 in the truth table (rows 3,4,6,8)
3. This approach:
   - Explicitly shows the canonical sum-of-products form
   - Makes it easy to modify if truth table changes
   - Can be efficiently implemented in FPGAs using LUTs
   - Shows the fundamental relationship between decoders and logic functions
4. The decoder outputs are:
   - d0 = ~x3 & ~x2 & ~x1 (row 1)
   - d1 = ~x3 & ~x2 &  x1 (row 2)
   - d2 = ~x3 &  x2 & ~x1 (row 3)
   - d3 = ~x3 &  x2 &  x1 (row 4)
   - d4 =  x3 & ~x2 & ~x1 (row 5)
   - d5 =  x3 & ~x2 &  x1 (row 6)
   - d6 =  x3 &  x2 & ~x1 (row 7)
   - d7 =  x3 &  x2 &  x1 (row 8)
5. f is then the OR of d2, d3, d5, d7
