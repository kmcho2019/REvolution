The fusion strategy combines the strengths of both examples while addressing potential weaknesses:

1. From Example 1:
   - The logical expression clearly shows the relationship between inputs
   - Minimal gate count (2 ANDs, 1 OR, 1 NOT)

2. From Example 2:
   - Mux-based approach offers regular structure
   - Potentially better technology mapping (especially for FPGAs)
   - Single-level logic path

The optimal fusion is to use the mux implementation (Example 2) because:
- It's functionally equivalent to the logical expression but more readable
- Modern synthesis tools will optimize either implementation similarly
- The mux structure is more maintainable and scalable
- It directly represents the observed pattern in the truth table (x3 selects between x2 and x1)
- Typically maps efficiently to both ASIC and FPGA technologies

The fused solution maintains all advantages while being the most readable and technology-neutral implementation.
