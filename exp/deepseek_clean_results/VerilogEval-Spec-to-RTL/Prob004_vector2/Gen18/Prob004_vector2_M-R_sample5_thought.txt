The refactored solution maintains the same byte-reversal functionality but simplifies the structure by:
1. Eliminating the hierarchical ByteReverser module since it adds unnecessary overhead for such a simple operation
2. Using direct assignment in the TopModule itself
3. Keeping the efficient concatenation approach but making it more immediately visible in the top level

This change reduces module hierarchy while maintaining identical functionality and PPA characteristics. The direct implementation makes the design more straightforward and easier to understand at a glance.
