Here's a novel approach using a shift-register based byte reversal:

1. **Shift Register Concept**: Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a shift register that processes the input in byte-sized chunks. This provides a more hardware-oriented solution that could be beneficial in certain pipelined scenarios.

2. **Byte-wise Processing**: The 32-bit input will be processed 8 bits at a time, with each byte being shifted into its reversed position.

3. **Parallel Loading**: We'll use parallel loading of bytes into their reversed positions in a single clock cycle, maintaining combinational behavior while demonstrating an alternative approach.

This method:
- Shows a different architectural perspective
- Could be extended to pipelined implementations
- Demonstrates byte-level manipulation
- Maintains single-cycle operation
