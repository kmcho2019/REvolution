Here's a novel approach using a parameterized for-loop to reverse the bytes, which provides:
1. Scalability - easily adaptable to different vector widths
2. Maintainability - single point of change for different byte orders
3. Potential synthesis optimization - some tools may unroll loops efficiently
4. Clear intent - shows the byte-reversal pattern explicitly
5. Flexibility - could be extended to support configurable endianness

The implementation will:
- Use a generate-for loop to swap byte pairs
- Maintain the same functionality as the direct concatenation method
- Potentially offer better readability for some use cases
- Still result in optimal hardware after synthesis
