Here's a novel approach using a shift register-based byte reversal mechanism:

1. **Shift Register Concept**: Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a shift register that processes the input in 8-bit chunks.
2. **Parallel Loading**: Load the 32-bit input into a 4-stage shift register (each stage holding 8 bits).
3. **Reverse Shifting**: Shift out the bytes in reverse order by selecting from different stages of the shift register.
4. **Advantages**:
   - More scalable for pipelined implementations
   - Easier to extend to different word sizes
   - Potential for lower power in some scenarios due to sequential nature
   - Clear separation of byte boundaries in the implementation
5. **Implementation**: We'll use a combinational always block to perform the reversal without actual clocked shifting.

This approach provides a different architectural perspective while maintaining the same functionality.
