Here's a novel approach using a priority-encoded selection scheme:
1. Instead of using a direct AND operation for the select signals, we'll implement a priority encoder
2. The encoder will treat sel_b1 as the primary select and sel_b2 as a secondary confirmation
3. This creates a more fault-tolerant design where sel_b2 acts as a "confirmation" bit for sel_b1
4. The implementation uses this priority scheme in both continuous and procedural styles
5. This approach could be beneficial in systems where selection signals may have timing skew
