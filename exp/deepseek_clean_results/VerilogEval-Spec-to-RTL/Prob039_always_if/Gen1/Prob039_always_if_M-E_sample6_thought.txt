To implement a 2-to-1 multiplexer with a novel approach, we can:
1. Use a hierarchical design with two sub-modules: one for the assign-based mux and one for the always-based mux.
2. Each sub-module will have its own selection logic, making the design more modular.
3. The top module will instantiate both sub-modules and connect their outputs to the final outputs.
4. This approach demonstrates modular design and separation of concerns in Verilog.
