Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation instead of traditional state encoding. The key idea is:

1. Represent states using a 2-bit shift register where:
   - "01" represents OFF state
   - "10" represents ON state
2. State transitions are implemented by shifting bits:
   - From OFF to ON: Shift left (01 becomes 10)
   - From ON to OFF: Shift right (10 becomes 01)
3. The output is simply the MSB of the shift register
4. Reset initializes to "01" (OFF state)

This approach has several advantages:
- Uses simple shift operations instead of conditional logic
- Naturally prevents invalid states (only 01 and 10 are possible)
- Output is directly available from the register
- Can be easily extended to more states by increasing register width
