2. **Clock Gating**: Add conditional clock enable to reduce power during state retention
3. **Output Optimization**: Keep output directly tied to state (already optimal for Moore machine)
4. **Reset Optimization**: Maintain synchronous reset for better timing characteristics
5. **Logic Minimization**: Use direct assignment without case statement for this simple FSM
