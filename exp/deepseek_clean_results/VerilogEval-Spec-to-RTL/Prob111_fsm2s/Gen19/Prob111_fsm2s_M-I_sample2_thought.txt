Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, here's the optimization strategy:

1. **Simplify State Transition Logic**:
   - Remove clock gating (state_en) as it adds unnecessary complexity for this small FSM
   - Use direct state transitions with simple conditional logic

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - Remove reset from clock gating condition to reduce switching activity
   - Keep output directly assigned from state register (no extra logic)

3. **Area Reduction**:
   - Remove parallel_case directive (not needed for 2-state FSM)
   - Simplify next-state logic to minimal AND/OR operations

4. **Timing Improvement**:
   - Use straightforward conditional assignments for state transitions
   - Maintain synchronous reset but with simpler implementation

The improved version should reduce area and power while maintaining or improving timing.
