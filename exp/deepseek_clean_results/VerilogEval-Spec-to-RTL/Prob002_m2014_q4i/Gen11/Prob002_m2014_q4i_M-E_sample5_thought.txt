Here's a novel approach that implements the constant 0 output using a more complex but functionally equivalent structure:

1. Use a 4-input AND gate where all inputs are driven by inverted versions of a single input (which could be tied to 1)
2. This creates a structure where: (1'bx & ~1'bx & ~1'bx & ~1'bx) will always evaluate to 0
3. The implementation shows that even complex logic can be optimized to a constant 0 during synthesis
4. This demonstrates how synthesis tools can optimize complex expressions to their simplest form

The PPA metrics will be slightly worse than the direct assignment due to the initial logic structure, but synthesis will optimize it to the same minimal implementation.
