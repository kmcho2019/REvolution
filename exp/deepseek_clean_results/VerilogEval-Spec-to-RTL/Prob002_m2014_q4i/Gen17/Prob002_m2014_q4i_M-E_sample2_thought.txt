An alternative approach that achieves the same functionality but with a different architecture is to use a registered output. This involves:
1. Creating a flip-flop that always loads 0
2. Using a clock signal (even though not required in the interface)
3. The output will be stable after one clock cycle
While this is less efficient than direct assignment, it demonstrates:
- Sequential logic implementation
- Potential for future expansion if timing control is needed
- Different synthesis characteristics
