The current implementation uses unnecessary sequential logic (reg and always block) to maintain a constant 0 output. This creates redundant switching activity and consumes extra power and area. The optimal solution should:
1. Remove all unnecessary logic elements (reg, always block)
2. Use a simple continuous assignment to directly drive the output to 0
3. This will reduce both power consumption and area to their minimum possible values
4. Maintain the same functionality of always outputting 0
