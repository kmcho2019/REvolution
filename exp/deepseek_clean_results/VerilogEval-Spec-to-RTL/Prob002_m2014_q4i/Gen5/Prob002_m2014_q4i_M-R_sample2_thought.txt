The original implementation uses a continuous assignment to hardwire the output to 0. An alternative approach would be to use an always block with procedural assignment, though this would be unnecessarily complex for such a simple case. Another approach could be to instantiate a primitive gate (like a buffer with constant input), but that would also be overcomplicating. The cleanest alternative is to use a parameter with a constant value, though this still achieves the same result with slightly different syntax.
