The given solution is already optimal for this trivial case where we need a constant 0 output. Since there are no inputs or internal logic, the simplest and most efficient implementation is a direct hardwired assignment to 0. This approach:

1. Uses minimal area (likely just a direct connection to ground)
2. Has zero timing paths (no logic to propagate through)
3. Consumes minimal power (only leakage from the output driver)

No further optimization is possible at the RTL level since:
- There are no inputs to create conditional logic
- No sequential elements are needed
- The output is purely combinational and constant

The synthesis tool will implement this most efficiently as a direct ground connection in the target technology.
