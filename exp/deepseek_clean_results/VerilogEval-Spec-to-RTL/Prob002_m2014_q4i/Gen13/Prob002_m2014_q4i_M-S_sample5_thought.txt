The simplest possible implementation is to directly hard-wire the output to 0. This requires no logic gates, no sequential elements, and minimal synthesis resources. The solution is optimal because:
1. It uses the absolute minimum hardware (just a ground connection)
2. Has zero propagation delay
3. Consumes no dynamic power (static power only)
4. Takes zero area in most technologies (can share ground with other circuits)
5. Is completely timing-independent
