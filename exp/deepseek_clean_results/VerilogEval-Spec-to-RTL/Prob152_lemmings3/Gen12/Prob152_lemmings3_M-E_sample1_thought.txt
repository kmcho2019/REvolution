Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical state machine with direction tracking:

1. Main states: WALKING, FALLING, DIGGING (3 states)
2. Direction tracking: Separate register for left/right (1 bit)
3. Key innovations:
   - Use a "pending_direction" register to remember direction during falls/digs
   - Implement state transitions as a pipeline:
     * First stage: Detect fall conditions
     * Second stage: Detect dig conditions (if not falling)
     * Third stage: Handle bumps (if neither falling nor digging)
   - Outputs are purely combinational functions of state and direction
4. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of concerns between movement and actions
   - Efficient handling of direction persistence
   - Simplified output logic
   - Better timing due to staged condition evaluation
