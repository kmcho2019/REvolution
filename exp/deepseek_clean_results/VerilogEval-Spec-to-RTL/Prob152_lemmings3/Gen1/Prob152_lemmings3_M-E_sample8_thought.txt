Instead of separate states for left/right variants of each action, we can:
1. Use a direction register to track current walking direction (left/right)
2. Have three main states: WALKING, FALLING, DIGGING
3. The direction register persists through state changes
4. Bump signals only affect the direction in WALKING state
5. Priority is handled by checking conditions in order: fall > dig > bump

This reduces the state space from 6 to 3 states while maintaining all functionality. The direction is stored separately, making state transitions cleaner and more maintainable.
