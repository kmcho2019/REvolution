A novel approach is to separate the walking direction tracking from the current action state. We'll use two registers:
1. A 1-bit register to track the walking direction (0=left, 1=right)
2. A 2-bit register for the current action state (walking, falling, digging)

This separation allows us to:
- Preserve the walking direction during falls and digs
- Simplify state transitions by decoupling direction from action
- Make the FSM more maintainable with clear separation of concerns

The action states are:
- 00: WALK
- 01: FALL
- 10: DIG

The outputs are derived from both the direction and action states.
