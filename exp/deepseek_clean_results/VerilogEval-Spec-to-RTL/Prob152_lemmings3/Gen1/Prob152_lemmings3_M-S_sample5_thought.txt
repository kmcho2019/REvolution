2. Track walking direction separately from current action (falling/digging)
3. Use a 2-bit state: 1 bit for direction (left/right) and 1 bit for falling/digging
4. Make state transitions more straightforward by separating direction logic from action logic
5. Maintain Moore outputs by deriving them from current state

- Separate direction tracking from action state
