To simplify the Lemming state machine, we can:
1. Reduce the number of states by combining left/right variants into single states with direction tracking
2. Use a direction register to track walking direction instead of separate states
3. Prioritize state transitions clearly: falling > digging > bumping
4. Simplify output logic by using the direction register

Key states:
- WALKING: handles both left/right walking with direction tracking
- FALLING: handles falling behavior
- DIGGING: handles digging behavior

The direction register (dir) tracks left/right direction during all states.
This reduces state count from 6 to 3 while maintaining all functionality.
