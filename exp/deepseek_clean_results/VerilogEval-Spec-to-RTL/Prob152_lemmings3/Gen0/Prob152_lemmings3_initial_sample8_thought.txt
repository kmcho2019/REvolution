To implement the Lemming behavior, we need a Moore state machine with multiple states to handle walking, falling, and digging. The key states are:
1. WALK_LEFT - walking left
2. WALK_RIGHT - walking right
3. FALLING_LEFT - falling while previously walking left
4. FALLING_RIGHT - falling while previously walking right
5. DIGGING_LEFT - digging while previously walking left
6. DIGGING_RIGHT - digging while previously walking right

The state transitions are governed by:
- Bump signals cause direction change only in walking states
- Ground=0 causes transition to falling states
- Dig=1 causes transition to digging states if ground=1
- Falling has highest priority, then digging, then bumping

Outputs are determined by current state:
- walk_left/walk_right in walking states
- aaah in falling states
- digging in digging states
