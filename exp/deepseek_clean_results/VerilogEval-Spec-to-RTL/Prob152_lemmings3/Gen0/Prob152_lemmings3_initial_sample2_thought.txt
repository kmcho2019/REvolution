To implement the Lemmings behavior, we need a finite state machine (FSM) with multiple states to handle walking, falling, and digging. The FSM will have the following states:
1. WALK_LEFT: Lemming is walking left
2. WALK_RIGHT: Lemming is walking right
3. FALL_LEFT: Lemming is falling while previously walking left
4. FALL_RIGHT: Lemming is falling while previously walking right
5. DIG_LEFT: Lemming is digging while previously walking left
6. DIG_RIGHT: Lemming is digging while previously walking right

The state transitions will be as follows:
- From WALK_LEFT/WALK_RIGHT:
  - If ground=0, transition to FALL_LEFT/FALL_RIGHT
  - Else if dig=1, transition to DIG_LEFT/DIG_RIGHT
  - Else if bumped, switch walking direction
- From FALL_LEFT/FALL_RIGHT:
  - If ground=1, transition back to WALK_LEFT/WALK_RIGHT
- From DIG_LEFT/DIG_RIGHT:
  - If ground=0, transition to FALL_LEFT/FALL_RIGHT

The outputs are determined by the current state:
- walk_left: 1 in WALK_LEFT or DIG_LEFT states
- walk_right: 1 in WALK_RIGHT or DIG_RIGHT states
- aaah: 1 in FALL_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT states
- digging: 1 in DIG_LEFT or DIG_RIGHT states
