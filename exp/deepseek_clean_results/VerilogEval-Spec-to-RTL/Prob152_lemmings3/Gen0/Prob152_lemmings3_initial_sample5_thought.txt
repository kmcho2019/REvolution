To implement the Lemmings behavior, we need a Moore state machine with multiple states to handle walking, falling, and digging. The key states are:
1. WALK_LEFT - walking left (default after reset)
2. WALK_RIGHT - walking right
3. FALL_LEFT - falling while previously walking left
4. FALL_RIGHT - falling while previously walking right
5. DIG_LEFT - digging while previously walking left
6. DIG_RIGHT - digging while previously walking right

The state transitions follow these rules:
- Falling has highest priority when ground=0
- Digging has next priority when dig=1 and ground=1
- Bumping changes direction only when walking normally
- After falling, return to previous walking direction
- After digging ends (ground=0), transition to falling
- Reset always returns to WALK_LEFT

The outputs are determined by the current state:
- walk_left: active in WALK_LEFT or DIG_LEFT
- walk_right: active in WALK_RIGHT or DIG_RIGHT
- aaah: active in FALL_LEFT or FALL_RIGHT
- digging: active in DIG_LEFT or DIG_RIGHT
