Simplified Design Approach:
1. Use a standard 4-bit binary counter for minimal hardware
2. Replace the optimized overflow detection (q[3] & q[0]) with a direct comparison to 9 (4'b1001)
3. Maintain synchronous reset and enable functionality
4. Advantages:
   - More readable and maintainable code
   - Direct comparison to 9 is more intuitive and easier to verify
   - Same hardware efficiency (4 flip-flops)
   - Similar timing characteristics (single comparison operation)
5. The simplification comes from using a more straightforward comparison while maintaining all functional requirements
