To simplify the decade counter while maintaining functionality:
1. Keep the 4-bit register but optimize the comparison logic
2. Instead of checking for 9 (1001), we can check when q[3] is set (since only 8 and 9 have q[3] set) and q[0] is set (for 9)
3. This reduces the comparison from checking all 4 bits to just checking 2 bits
4. Maintain the same synchronous reset and enable functionality
5. The increment operation remains simple as it's just +1 until wrap-around
