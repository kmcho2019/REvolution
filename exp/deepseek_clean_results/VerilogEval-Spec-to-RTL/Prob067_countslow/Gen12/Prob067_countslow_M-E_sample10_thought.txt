Here's a novel approach using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) style counter with custom feedback:
1. Instead of a standard binary counter, implement a 4-bit LFSR that cycles through 10 distinct states
2. The feedback polynomial is carefully chosen to create a decade counter sequence
3. The sequence will be: 0000→0001→0011→0111→1111→1110→1100→1001→0010→0100→0000 (repeats)
4. This approach reduces combinational logic complexity compared to binary counters
5. Maintain synchronous reset and enable functionality
6. The feedback is simply the XOR of specific bits when in terminal state
