Refactoring strategy:
1. Separate the combinational next-state logic from sequential elements
2. Use continuous assignments for the overflow detection and next-state calculation
3. Keep the synchronous reset and enable functionality
4. The overflow detection still uses the efficient (q[3] & q[0]) check
5. This structure makes the design more modular and potentially easier to optimize during synthesis
6. The sequential part remains simple with just a register update
