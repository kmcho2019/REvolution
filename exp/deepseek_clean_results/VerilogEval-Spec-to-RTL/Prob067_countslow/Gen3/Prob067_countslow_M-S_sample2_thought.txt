To simplify the decade counter while maintaining functionality:

1. Remove the intermediate wire declarations and combine logic directly in the always block
2. Keep the basic synchronous reset and enable functionality
3. Use direct comparison with 9 (4'b1001) for clarity and simplicity
4. Eliminate unnecessary intermediate signals to reduce logic levels

The simplified design will:
- Maintain the same functionality (0-9 counting with enable and reset)
- Reduce gate count by eliminating intermediate signals
- Keep the code more readable with direct operations
- Still meet all timing requirements for typical clock frequencies
