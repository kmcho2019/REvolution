Novel Hybrid Encoding Approach:
1. Use a 4-bit binary counter for states 0-7 (efficient storage)
2. Switch to one-hot encoding for states 8 and 9 (simpler overflow detection)
3. Implement a state machine that:
   - Uses binary counting for 0-7 (3 LSBs)
   - Sets a dedicated MSB for states 8 and 9
   - Detects overflow by checking when binary count would exceed 9
4. Advantages:
   - Reduced comparison logic for overflow (only need to check when binary count is 8)
   - Maintains small area (only 4 bits total)
   - Simplifies wrap-around condition
   - Lower power than pure binary for states 8-9 (fewer bit toggles)
5. Key innovation:
   - Hybrid encoding automatically handles the decade wrap-around
   - No need for complex comparison logic
   - Natural progression from binary to one-hot at higher counts
