Refactoring Approach:
1. Separate combinational and sequential logic for better clarity and potential synthesis optimization
2. Use an always block only for the sequential part (register update)
3. Move the next-state logic to a continuous assignment (assign statement)
4. Maintain the same functionality (decade counter with enable and reset)
5. The rollover detection remains the same (q[3] & q[0] for detecting 9)
6. This structure may help with timing by separating the combinational path from the register update
