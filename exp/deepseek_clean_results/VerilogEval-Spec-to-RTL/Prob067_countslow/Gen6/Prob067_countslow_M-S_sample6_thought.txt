To simplify the decade counter further while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the explicit comparison with 9 (1001) since the counter will naturally wrap around from 9 to 0 when incremented beyond 9
2. Use a simple increment with overflow handling - when q reaches 9, next increment will automatically wrap to 0 due to 4-bit overflow
3. Keep synchronous reset and enable functionality
4. This eliminates the comparator entirely, reducing logic complexity
5. The design remains correct because 9+1 in 4 bits is 0 (1001 + 1 = 1010, but we only keep lower 4 bits: 0000)
