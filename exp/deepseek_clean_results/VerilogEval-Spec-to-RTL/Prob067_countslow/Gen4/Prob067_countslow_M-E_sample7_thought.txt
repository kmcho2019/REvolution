Here's a novel approach using a ring counter with one-hot encoding for the decade counter. The key ideas are:
1. Use one-hot encoding (10 bits) where only one bit is active at any time
2. Implement as a circular shift register that rotates when enabled
3. The output is encoded to 4 bits using a priority encoder
4. Benefits:
   - No arithmetic operations needed (simpler logic)
   - Natural wrap-around behavior
   - Each state change only involves flipping two bits
   - Potentially better power characteristics due to reduced switching
5. Trade-off is slightly larger area due to 10-bit register vs 4-bit
