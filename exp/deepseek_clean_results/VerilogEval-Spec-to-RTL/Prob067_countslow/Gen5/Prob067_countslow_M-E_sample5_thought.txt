Novel approach using a Johnson counter with decode logic:
1. Use a 5-bit Johnson (twisted ring) counter that naturally cycles through 10 states
2. The Johnson counter has inherent glitch-free behavior and requires fewer flip-flops than a binary counter
3. Decode the Johnson counter state to produce the 4-bit binary output
4. The Johnson counter will rotate left on each enabled clock cycle
5. Synchronous reset initializes the counter to 00001 (first Johnson state)
6. This approach reduces combinational logic complexity compared to binary increment/decode
