To refactor the TopModule while maintaining identical functionality, we'll:
1. Separate the multiplexer and flip-flop into distinct logical components
2. Use continuous assignment for the multiplexer (combinational logic)
3. Keep the flip-flop as a synchronous element using always block
4. This makes the design more modular and explicit about the two distinct operations
5. The mux selects input based on L, while the flip-flop handles clocked storage

Key improvements:
- Clear separation of combinational (mux) and sequential (flip-flop) logic
- More explicit representation of the two fundamental operations
- Maintains same behavior while being more structurally clear
