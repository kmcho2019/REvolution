Simplified approach:
1. Implement TopModule as a basic D flip-flop with a 2:1 mux at its input
2. The mux selects between r_in (parallel load) and q_in (feedback path) based on L
3. No need for tri-state buffers - use a simple conditional assignment
4. The feedback XOR logic will be handled in the higher-level full_module
5. Each TopModule instance handles just one bit of the 3-bit register
6. Clean and straightforward implementation with no contention issues
