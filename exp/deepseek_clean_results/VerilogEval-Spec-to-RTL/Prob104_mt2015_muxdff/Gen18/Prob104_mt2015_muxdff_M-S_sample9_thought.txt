The key insight is that TopModule should remain a simple, generic building block - just a flip-flop with a 2:1 mux at its input. The complex feedback pattern ({q[1]^q[2], q[0], q[2]}) should be handled at the full_module level by appropriately connecting the instances, not within TopModule itself.

TopModule should:
1. Select between synchronous load (r_in) and state update (q_in) based on L
2. Maintain its state with a flip-flop
3. Output its current state (Q)

The transformation logic is achieved in full_module by wiring the instances together correctly. Each TopModule instance just needs to store either its input or perform a simple state update, without knowing about the system-level feedback pattern.
