My novel approach implements a self-contained, edge-triggered unit with built-in feedback capability. Instead of just being a simple loadable flip-flop, each TopModule instance will:

1. Include its own XOR gate for feedback operations
2. Have a configurable bit position (parameterized)
3. Generate its own feedback term when in shift mode
4. Still maintain the basic load functionality

This makes each instance more intelligent and reduces the wiring complexity in the full_module. The key innovation is that each flip-flop "knows" its role in the feedback network. The full_module would simply need to chain these together appropriately.

The parameter 'POS' determines the bit position (0=LSB, 2=MSB) and controls the feedback logic. The MSB (POS=2) uses XOR feedback, while others just pass their state.
