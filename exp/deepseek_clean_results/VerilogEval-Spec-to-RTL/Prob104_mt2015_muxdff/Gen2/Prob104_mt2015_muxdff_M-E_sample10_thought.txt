My novel approach is to implement a clock-gated version of the TopModule that reduces power consumption by only clocking the flip-flop when necessary. The key ideas are:
1. Use L as a clock enable signal - only allow clock transitions when either L is high or q_in differs from current state
2. This creates a power-efficient design that avoids unnecessary clock toggles
3. Maintain same functionality by still implementing the 2:1 mux logic
4. Add a clock gating cell (simulated here with an AND gate) to control clock propagation
5. The design will still work correctly when instantiated three times in full_module
