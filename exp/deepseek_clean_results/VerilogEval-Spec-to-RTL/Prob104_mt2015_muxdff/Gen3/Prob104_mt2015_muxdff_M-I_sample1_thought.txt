The original solution attempted clock gating which can lead to timing issues. Here's a better approach:
1. Use a standard synchronous design with a single clock
2. Implement a 2:1 mux to select between r_in (load) and q_in (feedback)
3. The flip-flop updates on every clock edge
4. The mux selection is controlled by L
5. This maintains clean synchronous design principles
6. The implementation is simpler and more reliable
