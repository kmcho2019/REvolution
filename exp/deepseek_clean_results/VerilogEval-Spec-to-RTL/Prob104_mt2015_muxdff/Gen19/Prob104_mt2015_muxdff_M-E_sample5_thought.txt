Novel approach using explicit multiplexer and separate flip-flop:
1. Implement the 2:1 mux and D flip-flop as distinct components within TopModule
2. Use continuous assignments for the mux to clearly separate combinational and sequential logic
3. The mux output drives the flip-flop input
4. This provides better visibility into the internal operation and timing
5. Allows potential for more complex mux logic in future extensions
6. Maintains same interface but with more modular internal structure
