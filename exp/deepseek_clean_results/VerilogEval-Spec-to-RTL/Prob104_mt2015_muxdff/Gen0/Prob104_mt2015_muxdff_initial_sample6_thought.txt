To create the TopModule submodule, we need to implement a single bit storage element (flip-flop) with a 2:1 multiplexer at its input. The multiplexer selects between the load input (r_in) and a feedback path (q_in) based on the load control signal L. The flip-flop updates its output Q on the positive edge of the clock. This submodule will be instantiated three times in the full_module to replace the original register implementation.
