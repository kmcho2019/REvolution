Here's an alternative approach that implements the same functionality but with a different architecture:

1. Instead of using a continuous assignment for the mux, we'll use an always block to make the mux behavior more explicit
2. We'll combine the mux and flip-flop into a single always block for better timing characteristics
3. The design will still maintain the same interface and functionality, but with a different internal structure
4. This approach eliminates the intermediate wire and combines the selection and storage into one procedural block

The key difference is that we're moving the mux logic inside the clocked always block, which can sometimes lead to better synthesis results in certain technologies. This is particularly useful when the mux and flip-flop are meant to be tightly coupled in the final implementation.
