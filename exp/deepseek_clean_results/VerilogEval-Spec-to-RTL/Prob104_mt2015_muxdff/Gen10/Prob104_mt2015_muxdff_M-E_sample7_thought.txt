Here's a novel approach that leverages clock gating for the load operation while maintaining the feedback path:

1. Instead of using a traditional multiplexer, we'll use clock gating controlled by the L signal
2. When L=1, the clock is enabled and the flip-flop loads r_in
3. When L=0, the clock is disabled and the flip-flop holds its value while the feedback logic is applied externally
4. This approach separates the load and hold functionality more distinctly
5. The Q output is always driven by the flip-flop, ensuring clean timing
6. The feedback logic (XOR operation) will be handled in the parent module through q_in connections

This implementation is more power-efficient since the clock is gated when not loading, and it maintains a clear separation between load and hold operations.
