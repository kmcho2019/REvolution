The key realization is that TopModule should be a simple building block - just a loadable flip-flop without any feedback logic. The feedback and XOR operations should remain in the higher-level full_module where they can properly coordinate between the three instances. Here's the corrected approach:

1. TopModule should be a basic loadable D flip-flop
2. It should select between r_in (when L=1) or q_in (when L=0) at the input
3. The XOR and bit manipulation should stay in full_module where it can properly handle the interconnections
4. This makes TopModule a simple, reusable component while keeping the complex feedback logic at the higher level

The refactoring uses a continuous assignment for the mux followed by a simple D flip-flop, making the design clearer and more modular.
