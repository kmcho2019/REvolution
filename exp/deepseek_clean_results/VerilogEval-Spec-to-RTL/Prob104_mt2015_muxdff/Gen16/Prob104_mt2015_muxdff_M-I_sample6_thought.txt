The original solution is already quite optimal for PPA (Power, Performance, Area) as it implements a simple 2:1 mux followed by a D flip-flop. However, we can make a few minor improvements:

1. Remove the initialization (Q = 1'b0) since initial values are often not preserved during synthesis and can lead to mismatches between simulation and hardware
2. Use non-blocking assignment consistently for flip-flop behavior
3. Keep the minimal structure that exactly matches the requirements

The improved version will:
- Maintain the same functionality
- Be more synthesis-friendly
- Have slightly better PPA by removing unnecessary initialization
- Still be instantiable three times in the full_module

The XOR operation remains at the full_module level as intended in the hierarchical design.
