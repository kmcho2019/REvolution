Here's an alternative approach that implements the TopModule with a more explicit structural design. Instead of using a behavioral always block, we'll:
1. First create the 2:1 mux explicitly using gate-level modeling
2. Then connect the mux output to a D flip-flop
3. The mux selects between r_in (when L=1) and q_in (when L=0)
4. The flip-flop captures this value on the clock edge

This approach makes the internal structure more visible and might be preferable for certain synthesis or verification scenarios where explicit gate-level modeling is desired.
