The feedback indicates that the original TopModule was too simplistic and didn't account for the inter-bit dependencies needed for the full_module's shift/XOR functionality. However, the key realization is that the TopModule itself shouldn't implement the specific feedback logic - that's the responsibility of how the three instances are connected in full_module.

The correct approach is:
1. Keep TopModule as a simple loadable flip-flop with mux
2. The feedback patterns (q[1]^q[2], q[0], q[2]) will be created by:
   - Wiring appropriate q_in signals to each instance in full_module
   - The first instance gets q[1]^q[2]
   - The second gets q[0]
   - The third gets q[2]
3. TopModule remains generic - the specific bit manipulation happens at the full_module level through proper wiring

This maintains the hierarchical design while correctly implementing the required functionality.
