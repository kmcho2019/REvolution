The TopModule should be a simple building block that:
1. Acts as a single D flip-flop with a 2:1 mux at its input
2. When L=1, loads r_in (synchronous load)
3. When L=0, takes q_in (feedback path from other flip-flops)
4. No need for complex logic - the feedback pattern is handled by full_module's interconnections
5. Just needs to properly clock the selected input
