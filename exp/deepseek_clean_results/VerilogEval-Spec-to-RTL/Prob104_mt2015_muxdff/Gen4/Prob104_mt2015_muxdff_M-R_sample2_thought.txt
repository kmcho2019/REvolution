The refactored version will maintain identical functionality but use continuous assignments for the mux logic while keeping the flip-flop behavior in the always block. This separates the combinational (mux) and sequential (flip-flop) logic more explicitly. The key changes are:
1. Replace the ternary operator in the always block with a wire assignment
2. Use an intermediate wire for the mux output
3. Keep the flip-flop behavior cleanly in the always block
This makes the design more modular and easier to understand the separation between combinational and sequential logic.
