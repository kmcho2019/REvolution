The previous solution overcomplicates the feedback logic by introducing a parameter. Since each instance of TopModule will be connected differently in the full_module, we can simplify by:
1. Removing the parameter and making the module purely structural
2. Letting the full_module handle the feedback connections externally
3. Implementing just a basic mux + flip-flop structure
This makes TopModule simpler and more generic, while still allowing the full_module to implement the correct feedback logic through proper wiring of the q_in inputs.
