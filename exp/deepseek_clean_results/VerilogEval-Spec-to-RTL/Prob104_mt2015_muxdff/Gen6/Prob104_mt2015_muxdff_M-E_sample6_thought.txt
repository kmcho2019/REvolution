Here's a novel approach that implements the TopModule with a more explicit separation of the mux and flip-flop functionality:

1. Create a combinational always block to handle the 2:1 mux logic separately from the flip-flop
2. The mux selects between r_in (when L=1) and q_in (when L=0)
3. The flip-flop then simply registers the mux output on the clock edge
4. This makes the design more modular and easier to understand
5. The XOR logic remains in the full_module's wiring as before
6. This approach clearly separates the combinational and sequential logic
