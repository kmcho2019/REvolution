The TopModule should be a simple building block that can be instantiated three times to recreate the full_module's functionality. Since the complex feedback logic (XOR) is handled at the full_module level through proper wiring of q_in ports, the TopModule itself should remain simple:

1. It's just a D flip-flop with a 2:1 mux at its input
2. When L=1, it loads r_in
3. When L=0, it loads q_in (feedback from other flip-flops)
4. The XOR operation will be handled by how we connect these modules in full_module

This matches exactly with the original full_module behavior when properly instantiated three times with correct connections.
