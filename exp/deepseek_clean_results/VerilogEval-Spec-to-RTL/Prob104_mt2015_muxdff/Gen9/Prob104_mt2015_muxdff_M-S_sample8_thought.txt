The simplified approach focuses on implementing a basic D-flipflop with a 2:1 mux at its input:
1. Remove all clock gating logic as it's unnecessary for this application
2. Use standard synchronous design with the original clock
3. Implement a simple mux that selects between r_in (when L=1) and q_in (when L=0)
4. The flipflop updates on every positive clock edge
5. This matches the original full_module's behavior exactly while being simpler and more reliable
