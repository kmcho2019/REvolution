Here's a novel approach using a T-flipflop with enable logic:
1. Instead of tracking states explicitly, we'll use a T-flipflop that toggles based on specific conditions
2. The T-flipflop will toggle when:
   - Current state is OFF (0) and j=1 (transition to ON)
   - Current state is ON (1) and k=1 (transition to OFF)
3. The output is simply the flipflop's Q output
4. Reset initializes the flipflop to 0 (OFF state)
This approach eliminates explicit state comparisons and uses the flipflop's natural toggle behavior.
