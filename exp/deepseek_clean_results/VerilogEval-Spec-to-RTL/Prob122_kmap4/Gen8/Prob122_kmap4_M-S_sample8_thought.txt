The Karnaugh map shows a perfect checkerboard pattern that can be implemented with just two XOR operations. The output is 1 when either:
1. a and b are different and c and d are same, OR
2. a and b are same and c and d are different
This is exactly equivalent to (a XOR b) XOR (c XOR d). We can implement this directly without intermediate wires since the expression is simple enough to understand at a glance.
