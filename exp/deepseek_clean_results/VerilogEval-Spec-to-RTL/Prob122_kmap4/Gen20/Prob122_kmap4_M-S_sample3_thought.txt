The Karnaugh map shows a clear XOR pattern between inputs b and d, with the output inverted when a and c are equal. This can be implemented with just two XOR operations:

1. First XOR between b and d creates the base pattern
2. Second XOR between (b^d) and (a^c) handles the inversion when needed
3. This reduces the logic to just two XOR gates in series, eliminating the need for explicit mux and NOT operations

Advantages:
- Simpler implementation with only 2 XOR gates
- Maintains same functionality with reduced gate count
- Potentially better timing due to reduced logic levels
