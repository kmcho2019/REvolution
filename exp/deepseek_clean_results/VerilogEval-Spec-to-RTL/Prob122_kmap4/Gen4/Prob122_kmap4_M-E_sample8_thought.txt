An alternative approach is to recognize that the Karnaugh map shows a checkerboard pattern that repeats every two cells. This suggests we can implement the logic by:
1. Grouping the inputs into two pairs (a,b) and (c,d)
2. Creating a pattern where the output toggles based on the XOR of these pairs
3. The output is 1 when either:
   - Both pairs have odd parity (XOR=1)
   - Both pairs have even parity (XOR=0)
This is equivalent to an XNOR between the XOR results of the two pairs
