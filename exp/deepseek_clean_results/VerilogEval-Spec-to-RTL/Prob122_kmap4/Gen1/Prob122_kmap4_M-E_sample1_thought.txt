After analyzing the Karnaugh map, I notice a checkerboard pattern that repeats every two cells. This suggests we can implement the logic by checking the parity (odd/even) of the input combinations. Specifically:
1. Group inputs into pairs (a,b) and (c,d)
2. Count the number of 1s in each pair (0, 1, or 2)
3. The output is 1 when both pairs have an odd count (1) or both have an even count (0 or 2)
This can be implemented using XOR for parity checks and XNOR to compare the parities
