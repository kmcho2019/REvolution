After analyzing the Karnaugh map more carefully, I notice that the output pattern repeats every two rows and columns. The output depends on whether the sum of the input pairs ab and cd is odd or even. Specifically:
- When (a XOR c) is 0, output is b XOR d
- When (a XOR c) is 1, output is ~(b XOR d)
This can be simplified to: out = (a ^ c) ^ (b ^ d)
