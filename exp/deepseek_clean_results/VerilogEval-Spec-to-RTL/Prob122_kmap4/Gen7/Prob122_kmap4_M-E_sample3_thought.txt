A novel approach is to recognize that the Karnaugh map pattern resembles a 2D parity check. We can implement this by:
1. First computing row parity (c XOR d)
2. Then computing column parity (a XOR b)
3. Finally, XORing these two parities together to get the output
This creates a hierarchical parity structure that matches the checkerboard pattern while potentially offering better testability and modularity than a flat XOR chain.
