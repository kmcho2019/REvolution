A novel approach is to recognize that the Karnaugh map exhibits a checkerboard pattern that can be interpreted as a 2D parity check. The output is 1 when either:
1. The row (cd) and column (ab) parities are both even, or
2. The row and column parities are both odd.

This can be implemented by:
1. Calculating row parity (c XOR d)
2. Calculating column parity (a XOR b)
3. Comparing these parities with an XNOR gate
4. The output is 1 when the parities match (both even or both odd)
