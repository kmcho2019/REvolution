The current implementation is already optimal in terms of functionality and PPA metrics. However, we can make one minor improvement for better readability and potential synthesis optimization:

1. Use a single continuous assignment for the XOR chain instead of intermediate wires. This:
   - Makes the code more concise
   - May allow synthesis tools more flexibility in gate-level optimization
   - Maintains identical functionality
   - Doesn't affect the critical path (still 3 XOR delays)
   - Keeps the same power and area characteristics

The improvement is purely stylistic as the PPA metrics are already excellent (0 timing violations, minimal power and area). The single-line version may be preferable for:
- Clearer expression of the XOR relationship between all inputs
- Potential for synthesis tools to recognize the 4-input XOR pattern
- Easier maintenance and readability
