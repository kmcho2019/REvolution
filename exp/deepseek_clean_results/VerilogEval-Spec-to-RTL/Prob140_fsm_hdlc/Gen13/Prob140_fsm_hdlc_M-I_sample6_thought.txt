To further optimize the design based on PPA metrics and feedback:

1. Timing Improvement:
   - Replace binary counter with one-hot encoding for count states (5,6,7) to simplify comparisons
   - Pre-compute err condition in parallel to break timing dependency
   - Use gray coding for count transitions to reduce switching

2. Power Reduction:
   - Only update outputs when they change (remove default clears)
   - Use enable signals to reduce register toggling
   - Implement saturation for count at 7 to prevent unnecessary increments

3. Area Optimization:
   - Reduce count register to 2 bits (since we only need to track up to 7)
   - Combine related output conditions to share logic
   - Use simpler equality checks for output generation

The improved design will:
- Achieve better timing by reducing comparison logic depth
- Lower power consumption through reduced switching activity
- Maintain or reduce area through more efficient encoding
