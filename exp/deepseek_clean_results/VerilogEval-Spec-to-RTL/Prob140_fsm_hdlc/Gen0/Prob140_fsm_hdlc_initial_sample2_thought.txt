To implement the HDLC framing decoder, we'll use a Moore FSM with states representing the count of consecutive 1s seen. The states are:
- STATE_0: initial state (previous bit was 0)
- STATE_1: seen one 1
- STATE_2: seen two 1s
- STATE_3: seen three 1s
- STATE_4: seen four 1s
- STATE_5: seen five 1s (next 1 would trigger discard)
- STATE_6: seen six 1s (flag condition)
- STATE_ERR: seen seven or more 1s (error condition)

The FSM transitions between states based on the input bit. Outputs are generated based on the current state and input:
- disc: when in STATE_5 and input is 1
- flag: when in STATE_6 and input is 0
- err: when in STATE_6 and input is 1 (transition to STATE_ERR)
