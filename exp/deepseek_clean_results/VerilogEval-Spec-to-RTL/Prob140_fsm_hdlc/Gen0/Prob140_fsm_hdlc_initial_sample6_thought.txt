To implement the HDLC framing decoder, we'll create a Moore FSM with states representing the count of consecutive 1s. The FSM will track sequences of 1s and detect the three required patterns:

1. After 5 consecutive 1s followed by 0 (0111110), assert 'disc' in next cycle
2. After 6 consecutive 1s followed by 0 (01111110), assert 'flag' in next cycle
3. After 7 or more consecutive 1s, assert 'err' in next cycle

The states are:
- S0: Initial state (0 consecutive 1s)
- S1: 1 consecutive 1
- S2: 2 consecutive 1s
- S3: 3 consecutive 1s
- S4: 4 consecutive 1s
- S5: 5 consecutive 1s
- S6: 6 consecutive 1s
- S7: 7+ consecutive 1s (error state)

Outputs are registered to appear in the cycle after detection.
