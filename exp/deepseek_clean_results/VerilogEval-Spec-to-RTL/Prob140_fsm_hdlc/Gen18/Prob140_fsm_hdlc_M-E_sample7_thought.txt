Instead of using a counter or traditional state machine, I'll implement a shift register that tracks the last 7 bits received. This allows:
1. Direct pattern matching on the shift register contents
2. Parallel detection of all conditions (disc, flag, err)
3. No need for complex state transitions - just shift and compare
4. Simplified reset behavior (clear shift register)

Key advantages:
- All conditions checked in parallel (better timing)
- No arithmetic operations (simpler logic)
- Clear relationship between input history and outputs
- Easily extensible if pattern requirements change
- Outputs are purely combinational functions of shift register
