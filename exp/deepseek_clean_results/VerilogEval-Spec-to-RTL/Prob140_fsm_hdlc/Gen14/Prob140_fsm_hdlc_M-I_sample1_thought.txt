2. Output Logic:
   - Maintain registered outputs for clean timing

   - Add clock gating for the state registers when input is 0

3. Power Optimization:

   - Clear outputs immediately when not needed to reduce switching

4. Area Optimization:

   - Combine related output conditions where possible

Key improvements:
- One-hot encoding reduces comparison logic delay
- Direct state-to-output mapping simplifies logic
- Maintains synchronous reset behavior
- Better power characteristics through output management
- Potentially smaller area by eliminating comparators
