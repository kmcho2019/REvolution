To correctly implement the Karnaugh map using multiplexers, we need to analyze each output bit (mux_in[3:0]) based on the cd inputs for each ab case:

1. mux_in[0] (ab=00):
   - 1 when cd=00 or cd=10 → (~c & ~d) | (c & ~d) → ~d

2. mux_in[1] (ab=01):
   - Always 0 → direct assignment

3. mux_in[2] (ab=11):
   - 1 when cd=00 or cd=11 → (~c & ~d) | (c & d) → c XNOR d

4. mux_in[3] (ab=10):
   - 1 when cd=00, 11, or 10 → (~c & ~d) | (c & d) | (c & ~d) → ~d | (c & d)

Key simplifications:
1. mux_in[0] simplifies to just ~d
2. mux_in[1] remains constant 0
3. mux_in[2] uses XNOR
4. mux_in[3] can be implemented with OR and AND
5. Total logic operations minimized while maintaining correctness
