A novel approach would be to separate the arithmetic operation and zero detection into two distinct always blocks. This creates a clear sequencing where:
1. The first always block computes the arithmetic result (out)
2. The second always block generates the zero flag based on the stable output value

This separation ensures:
- Cleaner timing relationships between operations
- More predictable behavior in simulation and synthesis
- Explicit dependency of zero flag on the computed result
- Maintains the same functionality while being more robust
