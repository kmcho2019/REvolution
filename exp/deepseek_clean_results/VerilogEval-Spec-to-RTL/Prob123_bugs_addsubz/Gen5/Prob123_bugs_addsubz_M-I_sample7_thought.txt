   - Use reduction NOR instead of reduction OR with inversion for slightly better synthesis

2. Arithmetic Operation Optimization:

3. Output Assignment:

These changes should:
- Reduce power by eliminating redundant logic
- Maintain or improve timing by reducing logic levels
- Potentially reduce area slightly by combining operations
