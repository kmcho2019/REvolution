This solution implements a carry-save adder-subtractor with early zero detection:

1. Carry-Save Architecture:
   - Uses separate sum and carry vectors to potentially reduce critical path
   - Performs addition/subtraction in parallel with zero detection

2. Early Zero Prediction:
   - Computes potential zero conditions in parallel with arithmetic
   - Uses bitwise equality checks between operands for subtraction case
   - Combines this with actual result check for robust zero detection

3. Optimized Logic:
   - Avoids full ripple-carry propagation for zero detection
   - Uses bitwise operations that may synthesize more efficiently
   - Minimizes switching activity by computing only necessary terms

Benefits:
- Potential power savings from reduced switching activity
- Parallel operations may improve timing in some implementations
- More regular structure could lead to better area optimization
