Here's a novel approach using a carry-save adder structure with early zero detection:

1. Architectural Innovation:
   - Implement a carry-save adder (CSA) structure that performs addition and subtraction in parallel
   - Use the do_sub signal to conditionally invert 'b' and set the carry-in
   - This allows for faster operation as the carry propagation is reduced

2. Early Zero Detection:
   - Instead of checking the final result, detect potential zero conditions during computation
   - Combine this with the final result check for more efficient zero flag generation

3. Power Optimization:
   - Use bitwise operations to minimize unnecessary toggling
   - Structure the logic to prevent glitches in the zero flag output

The key advantage is the parallel computation of sum and carry, which could lead to better timing characteristics in synthesis.
