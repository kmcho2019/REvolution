Instead of computing each next_state bit independently, I'll implement a priority-based transition scheme that:
1. Uses the one-hot property to create a priority chain where higher-numbered states take precedence
2. Implements the transitions as a cascaded decision tree
3. Reduces logic depth by making early decisions for higher-priority states
4. Maintains the output simplicity by directly tapping the state bit

Key innovations:
- Hierarchical decision making based on state priority (D > C > B > A)
- Early termination of logic evaluation for higher-priority states
- Reduced fan-out on critical paths by structuring the logic as a tree
- Maintains one-hot correctness through mutually exclusive conditions
