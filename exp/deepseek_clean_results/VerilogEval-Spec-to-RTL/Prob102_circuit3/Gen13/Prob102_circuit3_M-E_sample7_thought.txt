An alternative approach is to use a multiplexer-based implementation. We can treat the combination of a and b as a selector for different conditions on c and d. Specifically:
- When neither a nor b is active (a=0, b=0), q should be 0 regardless of c and d
- When either a or b is active (a=1 or b=1), q should follow the OR of c and d
This can be implemented using a 2:1 mux where the select signal is (a OR b), one input is 0, and the other input is (c OR d).
