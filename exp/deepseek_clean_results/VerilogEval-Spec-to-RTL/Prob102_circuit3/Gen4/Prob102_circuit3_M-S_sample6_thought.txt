Looking at the truth table, we can observe that:
1. When either a or b is 1, q equals (c OR d)
2. When both a and b are 0, q is 0

This can be simplified to a single AND operation between (a OR b) and (c OR d):
q = (a | b) & (c | d)

This implementation uses only two OR gates and one AND gate, which is more efficient than the mux-based solution.
