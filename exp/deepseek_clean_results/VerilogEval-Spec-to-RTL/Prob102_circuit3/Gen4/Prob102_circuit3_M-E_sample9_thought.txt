Novel Approach: Priority-Encoded AND Gate
1. Instead of traditional OR-AND logic, implement a priority-based approach where the output is only high when at least one input from each pair (a/b and c/d) is high.
2. This can be viewed as a "dual-requirement" check where both conditions must be met simultaneously.
3. The implementation uses nested conditional operators to emphasize the priority nature of the logic.
4. This approach conceptually separates the two conditions more clearly than the OR-AND implementation.
