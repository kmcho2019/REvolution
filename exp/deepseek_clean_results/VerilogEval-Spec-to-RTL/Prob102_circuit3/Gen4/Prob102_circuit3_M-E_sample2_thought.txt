An alternative approach is to recognize that the output q is 1 when at least two conditions are met simultaneously:
1. Either a or b is active (a | b)
2. Either c or d is active (c | d)

We can implement this using multiplexers to create a more structural representation. The first MUX selects between b and a based on a control signal (which we'll set to always prefer a when both are active), and the second MUX similarly selects between d and c. Then we AND the results of these two MUXes.
