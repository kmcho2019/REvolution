An alternative approach is to use a multiplexer-based implementation where we treat the inputs as a 4-bit select signal. We can observe from the truth table that q is 1 when at least two of the inputs are 1, with specific patterns. This approach demonstrates how truth table logic can be directly mapped to multiplexer logic.
