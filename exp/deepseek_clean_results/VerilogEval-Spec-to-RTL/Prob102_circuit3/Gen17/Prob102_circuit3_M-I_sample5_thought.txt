However, we can explore a different implementation style that might offer marginal power savings by using NAND-NAND logic instead of OR-AND logic. This approach leverages De Morgan's laws and might result in slightly better power characteristics in some technologies since NAND gates are often more power-efficient in CMOS implementations.

The equivalent NAND-NAND implementation would be:
q = NOT( (NOT(a AND b)) AND (NOT(c AND d)) )
