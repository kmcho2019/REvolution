After analyzing the truth table, I notice that q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1 (rows where b=1 and q=1: 0101, 0110, 0111)
2. a is 1 and d is 1 (rows where a=1 and q=1: 1001, 1011, 1101, 1111)
3. a is 1 and c is 1 (rows where a=1 and q=1: 1010, 1110)

This can be expressed as: q = (b & (c | d)) | (a & d) | (a & c). This implementation uses fewer logic operations while maintaining the same functionality.
