Looking at the truth table, we can observe that q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1, OR
2. a is 1 and d is 1

This can be expressed as a simple Boolean expression: q = (b & (c | d)) | (a & d). This implementation requires only 3 logic gates (1 OR, 2 ANDs) and is much more efficient than the mux-based approach.
