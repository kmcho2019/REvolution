After analyzing the truth table, I notice that q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1 (rows 5-7), OR
2. a is 1 and d is 1 (rows 9-11,13-15)
This can be expressed as a simple Boolean expression: q = (b & (c | d)) | (a & d). This implementation will be more efficient than the mux-based approach as it uses fewer logic gates and has a shorter critical path.
