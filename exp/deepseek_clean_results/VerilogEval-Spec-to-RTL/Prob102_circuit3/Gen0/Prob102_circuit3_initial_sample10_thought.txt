Analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can see that the output q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1, or
2. a is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1.

This can be simplified to: q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d). The output is only 1 when either a or b is 1, and at least one of c or d is also 1.
