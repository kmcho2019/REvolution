Analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can observe that the output q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1 (rows where b=1 and c or d=1 have q=1)
2. a is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1 (rows where a=1 and c or d=1 have q=1)

This can be simplified to: q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d)
