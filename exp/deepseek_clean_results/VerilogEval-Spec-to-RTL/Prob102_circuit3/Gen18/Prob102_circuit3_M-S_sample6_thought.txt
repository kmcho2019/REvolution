The truth table shows that q is 1 when either:
1. Both a and d are 1 (rows where a=1,d=1)
2. Both b and c are 1 (rows where b=1,c=1)
3. Both b and d are 1 (rows where b=1,d=1)

This can be simplified to q = (a & d) | (b & (c | d)). This implementation:
1. Uses only one OR and two AND operations
2. Has the same functionality as the original
3. Reduces gate count from 3 to 2 (since c|d can be shared)
4. Maintains the same single-level critical path
