Simplified Approach: Direct Logical AND of OR Gates
1. The truth table shows q is high only when both conditions are met:
   - At least one of a or b is high
   - At least one of c or d is high
2. This can be directly implemented as the logical AND of two OR operations
3. The simplest expression is (a|b) & (c|d)
4. Using bitwise operators instead of logical operators since all signals are 1-bit
5. This reduces the implementation to a single line with minimal operations
