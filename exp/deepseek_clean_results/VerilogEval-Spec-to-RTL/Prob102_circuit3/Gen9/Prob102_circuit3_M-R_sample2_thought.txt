The original implementation uses a multiplexer-based approach with intermediate wires. We can refactor this to use a single continuous assignment that directly expresses the logical relationship without intermediate variables. The truth table shows that q is 1 only when both (a OR b) AND (c OR d) are true. This can be implemented as a single AND of two OR expressions, which is logically equivalent but more concise and potentially more efficient in terms of gate-level implementation.
