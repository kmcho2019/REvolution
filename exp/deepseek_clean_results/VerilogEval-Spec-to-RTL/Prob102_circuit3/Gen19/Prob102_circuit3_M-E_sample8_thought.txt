An alternative approach is to use a multiplexer-based implementation where we treat the inputs as a 4-bit select signal. The output q is 1 when the binary value of {a,b} is greater than or equal to 1 and the binary value of {c,d} is greater than or equal to 1. This can be implemented by checking if either a or b is 1 (using OR) and similarly for c and d, then ANDing these conditions together.
