An alternative approach is to recognize that the output q is true when exactly two conditions are met: (1) at least one of the first pair (a,b) is true, and (2) at least one of the second pair (c,d) is true. Instead of using standard OR and AND gates, we can implement this using multiplexers (MUX) for a different architectural approach. The first MUX will select between b and 1 based on a (effectively OR operation), and the second MUX will select between d and 1 based on c (another OR). Then we'll AND these results together. This MUX-based implementation provides an alternative dataflow path that might have different PPA characteristics in certain technologies.
