After analyzing the truth table, I notice that q is 1 only when both of these conditions are met:
1. At least one of c or d is 1 (c OR d)
2. At least one of a or b is 1 (a OR b)

However, instead of using standard OR and AND gates, we can implement this using multiplexers for a different architectural approach. We'll use a hierarchical MUX structure where:
1. First level selects between c and d (OR function)
2. Second level uses this result to gate the OR of a and b
This gives the same functionality but with a different implementation style.
