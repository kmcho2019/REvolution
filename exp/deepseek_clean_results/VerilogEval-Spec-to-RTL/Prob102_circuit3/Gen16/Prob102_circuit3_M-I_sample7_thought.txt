Looking at the truth table, we can observe that q is 1 when either:
1. b is 1 and at least one of c or d is 1, OR
2. a is 1 and d is 1

This can be expressed more concisely as: q = (b & (c | d)) | (a & d). This optimized Boolean expression will reduce the number of logic gates needed, improving both area and power consumption while maintaining the same functionality.
