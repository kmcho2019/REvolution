Refactoring Strategy: Use Bitwise Operations with Concatenation
1. Instead of using separate OR and AND operations, we can leverage bitwise operations on concatenated inputs
2. By concatenating a and b, then c and d, we can perform bitwise OR operations on these pairs
3. The final output is the logical AND of these two OR results
4. This approach maintains the same functionality while showing an alternative implementation style
5. The gate-level implementation would be identical to the original, just expressed differently in Verilog
