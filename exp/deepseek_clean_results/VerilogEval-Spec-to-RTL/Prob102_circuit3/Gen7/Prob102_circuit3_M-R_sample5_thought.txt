The original implementation uses a single continuous assignment with logical OR and AND operations. For refactoring, we can:
1. Break down the logic into intermediate signals to show clearer functional blocks
2. Use separate assignments for each logical operation
3. Maintain the same functionality while making the structure more modular

This approach makes the design more readable and easier to modify if needed, while keeping the same logical behavior. The intermediate signals (ab_or and cd_or) clearly show the two main components of the logic: the OR combinations of (a,b) and (c,d), followed by their AND combination.
