Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical decoder structure:

1. First, create a 2-to-4 decoder for inputs a and b to identify which of the four possible combinations (00, 01, 10, 11) is active
2. Similarly, create another 2-to-4 decoder for inputs c and d
3. The output q should be true when both decoders have at least one active output (meaning at least one of a/b is true AND at least one of c/d is true)
4. This approach makes the logic more modular and scalable, though it uses more gates than the minimal solution

The advantage is that this structure could be easily extended to wider inputs or more complex conditions while maintaining clear modularity.
