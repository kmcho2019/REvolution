The original implementation uses continuous assignments with intermediate wires for OR operations followed by an AND. For refactoring, we can:
1. Eliminate the intermediate wires by combining the logic into a single expression
2. Use parentheses to clearly show the precedence of operations
3. Maintain the same functionality while making the code more compact

This approach reduces the number of internal signals while keeping the logic clear through proper grouping. The single assignment shows the direct relationship between inputs and output without intermediate steps.
