Simplified Approach: Direct Boolean Expression
1. Observing the truth table, q is true when at least one of a or b is true AND at least one of c or d is true
2. This can be directly expressed as a single Boolean expression using logical OR and AND operations
3. The minimal expression is (a|b) & (c|d), which uses the fewest operators possible
4. This maintains the exact same functionality while being maximally simplified
