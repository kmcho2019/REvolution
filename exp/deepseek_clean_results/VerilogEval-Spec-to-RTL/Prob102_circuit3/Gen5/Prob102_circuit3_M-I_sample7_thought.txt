The current solution is already optimal in terms of logic implementation, using just 2 OR gates and 1 AND gate (3 gates total). However, we can potentially improve PPA by:

1. Using a single AOI (AND-OR-Invert) cell which combines the OR and AND operations into one compound gate, reducing:
   - Area: From 3 gates to 1 compound gate
   - Delay: Single gate delay instead of two-level logic
   - Power: Reduced switching activity and capacitance

The logic can be implemented as:
q = (a OR b) AND (c OR d)
This matches exactly with AOI21 gate functionality where:
AOI21(A,B,C) = (A OR B) AND C
We'll need to pre-compute (c OR d) and use it as the third input
