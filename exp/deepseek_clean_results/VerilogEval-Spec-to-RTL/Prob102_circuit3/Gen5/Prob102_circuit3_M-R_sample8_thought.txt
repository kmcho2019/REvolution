The original implementation uses a single continuous assignment with combined OR and AND operations. For refactoring, I'll:

1. Break down the logic into intermediate signals to show clearer intent
2. Use separate assignments for each logical operation
3. Maintain the same functionality while making the structure more modular

This approach:
- Makes the design more readable by separating the OR and AND operations
- Allows easier debugging of intermediate signals
- Maintains the same combinational behavior
- Keeps the same optimal gate count (3 gates) and logic levels
- Provides better documentation of the design intent
