Simplified approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter instead of a shift register to count exactly 4 cycles
2. Initialize counter to 0 on reset
3. Increment counter each clock cycle while it's less than 3
4. Assert shift_ena when counter value is less than 4 (0-3)
5. Counter stops at 3, maintaining shift_ena low thereafter
6. Fewer bits needed (2 vs 4) and simpler logic than shift register approach

Benefits:
- Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset
- Simple counter logic
- Minimal hardware (2 flip-flops)
- Clear, maintainable implementation
