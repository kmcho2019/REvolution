Improved design approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to count exactly 4 cycles after reset (0 to 3)
2. The counter will increment on each clock cycle when not in reset
3. shift_ena is asserted when counter value is less than 4 (0-3)
4. After reaching count 3, shift_ena stays low forever until next reset
5. Advantages:
   - Explicit counting ensures exactly 4 cycles
   - No shifting logic needed, simpler implementation
   - Clear state transition (count increments until done)
   - Guaranteed to meet timing requirements
   - Lower power consumption than shift register approach
6. Reset behavior:
   - Synchronous reset sets counter to 0
   - shift_ena immediately goes high (cycle 0)
   - Counts through cycles 1, 2, 3
   - After cycle 3, shift_ena stays low
