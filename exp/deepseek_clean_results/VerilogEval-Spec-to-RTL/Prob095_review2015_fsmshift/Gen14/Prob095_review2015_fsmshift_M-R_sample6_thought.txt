Refactored design approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter instead of shift register for cycle counting
2. Counter increments on each clock cycle after reset
3. shift_ena is high when counter value is less than 4 (0-3)
4. Reset sets counter to 0 and enables shift_ena immediately
5. After 4 cycles, counter stays at 4 (or wraps) but comparison ensures shift_ena stays low
6. Key improvements:
   - More explicit cycle counting
   - No shifting operations needed
   - Clear relationship between counter value and shift_ena
   - Guaranteed exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena
   - Simpler state tracking
