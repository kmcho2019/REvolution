Novel approach using a down counter with explicit cycle counting:
1. Instead of a shift register, use a 2-bit down counter to track remaining enable cycles
2. Initialize counter to 3 (binary 11) on reset, representing 4 cycles (0-3)
3. Decrement counter each clock cycle while not zero
4. shift_ena is asserted when counter hasn't reached zero
5. After 4 cycles (when counter underflows), shift_ena stays low
6. Advantages:
   - Explicit cycle counting makes timing clear and verifiable
   - No shift patterns to manage
   - Simple comparison logic for output
   - Scales easily if cycle count needs to change
   - Clear reset behavior with defined countdown
