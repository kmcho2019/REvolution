Refactored design approach:
1. Maintain the shift register concept but initialize to 4'b1111 (all ones) on reset
2. Shift left with 0 input (becomes 4'b1110, 4'b1100, 4'b1000, then 4'b0000)
3. Make shift_ena the OR of all shift register bits
4. This ensures:
   - shift_ena is high immediately after reset (cycle 0)
   - Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (cycles 0-3)
   - Automatically turns off after 4 cycles
   - No arithmetic operations needed
   - Clear visual representation of timing
5. Key improvements:
   - Correct initialization value ensures exactly 4 cycles
   - Shift operation naturally counts down the cycles
   - Simple and efficient hardware implementation
   - Meets specification exactly
