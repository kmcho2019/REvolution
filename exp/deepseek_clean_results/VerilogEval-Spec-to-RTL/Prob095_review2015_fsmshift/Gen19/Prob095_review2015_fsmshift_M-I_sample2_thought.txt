Improvement strategy:
1. The original solution fails to meet the 4-cycle requirement because:
   - 2-bit counter can only count 0-3 (4 states)
   - shift_ena is deasserted when counter reaches 3 (after 3 cycles)
2. Correct approach:
   - Use a 2-bit counter but modify the comparison logic
   - shift_ena should be high for counter values 0-3 (4 cycles)
   - After 4 cycles, counter should stop incrementing
   - Reset should initialize counter to 0
3. Key changes:
   - Change comparison to (counter < 2'd4) - covers all 4 states
   - Counter will naturally stop at 3 (max value) after 4 cycles
   - Maintains minimal hardware (still only 2 flip-flops)
   - Guarantees exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset
