Simplified approach using a 2-bit counter:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to count 4 cycles (0 to 3)
2. Initialize counter to 0 on reset
3. shift_ena is high when counter < 4 (i.e., while counter[2] is 0)
4. Counter stops incrementing after reaching 3 (natural rollover)
5. Advantages:
   - Fewer bits needed (2 vs 4 in original)
   - Still maintains exact 4-cycle enable
   - Simpler comparison logic
   - More intuitive counting approach
   - Eliminates shift register overhead
