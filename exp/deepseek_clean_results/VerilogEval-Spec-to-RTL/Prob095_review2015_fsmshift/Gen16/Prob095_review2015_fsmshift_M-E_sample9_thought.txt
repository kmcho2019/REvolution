Novel approach using a shift register pattern:
1. Instead of a counter, use a 4-bit shift register initialized to '1111' on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with '0' input (becomes '0111', '0011', '0001', '0000')
3. The leftmost bit directly serves as shift_ena
4. Benefits:
   - Naturally counts exactly 4 cycles (4 bits)
   - No comparison logic needed (shift_ena is direct output)
   - Simple implementation with minimal logic
   - Clear visual representation of the enable pattern
   - Automatically holds at '0000' after 4 cycles
5. Reset behavior:
   - Initialize shift register to '1111'
   - Each cycle shifts in a '0' from left
   - After 4 cycles, stays at '0000' forever
