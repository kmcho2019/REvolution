Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:

1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with 0 input (becomes 4'b0111, 4'b0011, etc.)
3. The MSB of the shift register directly controls shift_ena
4. This naturally creates exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (while any '1's remain)
5. After 4 shifts, the register becomes 4'b0000 and stays there until next reset

Advantages:
- No explicit counter or done flag needed
- Shift register naturally counts down the 4 cycles
- shift_ena is asserted immediately in first cycle after reset
- Very simple and elegant implementation
