Here's a refactored version maintaining the one-hot shift register concept but fixing the timing issues:

1. Initialize shift register to '1111' on reset to cover all 4 required cycles
2. Each clock cycle, shift in a '0' from the right while shifting left
3. This creates a moving window of '1's that naturally extinguishes after 4 cycles
4. The shift_ena output is simply the OR of all register bits
5. After 4 cycles, the register becomes '0000' and stays there until next reset

Key improvements:
- Correct 4-cycle assertion by starting with all bits set
- Simplified shifting logic - just shift left with zero fill
- Maintains the one-hot advantage of simple decoding
- Clear state progression visible in the register pattern
