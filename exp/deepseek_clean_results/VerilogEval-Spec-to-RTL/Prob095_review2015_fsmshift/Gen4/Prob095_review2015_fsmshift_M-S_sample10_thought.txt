To simplify while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a 2-bit counter (values 0-3) since we only need to count 4 cycles
2. Initialize counter to 0 on reset
3. Assert shift_ena when counter is less than 4 (values 0-3)
4. Stop counting after reaching 3 (binary 11) to maintain shift_ena at 0
5. This gives exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (counts 0,1,2,3)

Simplifications:
- Reduced counter width from 3 to 2 bits
- Simplified terminal condition (count < 4 becomes implicit when using 2 bits)
- Same functionality with less hardware
