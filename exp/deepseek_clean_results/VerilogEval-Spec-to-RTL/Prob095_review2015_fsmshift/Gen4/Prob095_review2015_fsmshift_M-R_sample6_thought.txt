Here's a refactored version maintaining the one-hot encoding concept but with improved clarity and correctness:

1. Still use 4-bit one-hot encoding (0001, 0010, 0100, 1000) to represent the 4 active cycles
2. On reset, initialize to 0001 (first cycle active)
3. Each clock cycle, rotate left (0001→0010→0100→1000→0000)
4. After reaching 1000, next state becomes 0000 (stays there until next reset)
5. shift_ena is simply the OR of all state bits (high when any bit is set)
6. Explicitly handle the transition from 1000 to 0000 to ensure exactly 4 cycles

Key improvements:
- Clear termination condition after 4 cycles
- Explicit state transition for the final cycle
- Maintains one-hot encoding benefits while fixing timing issues
- More straightforward state progression
