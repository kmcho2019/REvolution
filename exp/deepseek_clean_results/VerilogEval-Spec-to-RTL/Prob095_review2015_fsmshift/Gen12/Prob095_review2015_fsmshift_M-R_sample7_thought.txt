Refactored design with improved timing:
1. Keep the shift register concept but modify the initialization and output logic
2. Initialize shift register to 4'b1110 (instead of 4'b1111) to ensure:
   - shift_ena is high immediately after reset (cycle 0)
   - Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (cycles 0-3)
3. Shift in zeros from the right as before
4. The output is now OR of all shift register bits:
   - This gives '1' when any bit is '1' (first 4 cycles)
   - Automatically goes to '0' when all bits are '0'
5. Reset behavior:
   - Synchronously loads 4'b1110
   - First shift creates 4'b1100, then 4'b1000, then 4'b0000
6. Advantages:
   - Maintains simple shift register approach
   - Corrects timing to match specification
   - Uses OR operation for clean output generation
   - Still avoids complex arithmetic
