Novel approach using a shift register:
1. Instead of a counter, use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111
2. On each clock cycle, shift in a 0 from the right
3. shift_ena is simply the leftmost bit of the shift register
4. Reset behavior:
   - Synchronously load 4'b1111 into the shift register
   - This naturally produces 4 cycles of shift_ena=1 as the 1's shift out
5. After 4 cycles, the register becomes 4'b0000 and stays that way
6. No need for explicit done signal - shift register state implicitly tracks completion

Advantages:
- More elegant implementation using shift register's natural behavior
- Exactly matches the 4-cycle requirement (4 initial 1's)
- No complex counting logic needed
- Self-terminating after 4 cycles without additional control logic
