Improved design approach:
1. Maintain the shift register concept but adjust initialization and output selection
2. Initialize shift register to 4'b0001 on reset (instead of 4'b1111)
3. Shift left with 0 input (becomes 4'b0010, 4'b0100, 4'b1000, then 4'b0000)
4. Make shift_ena the OR of all shift register bits
5. This ensures:
   - shift_ena is high immediately after reset (cycle 0)
   - Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (cycles 0-3)
   - Automatically turns off after 4 cycles
   - No arithmetic operations needed
   - Clear visual representation of timing
6. Benefits:
   - Correct timing alignment with reset
   - Simple implementation
   - Efficient hardware (just a shift register and OR gate)
   - Meets specification exactly
