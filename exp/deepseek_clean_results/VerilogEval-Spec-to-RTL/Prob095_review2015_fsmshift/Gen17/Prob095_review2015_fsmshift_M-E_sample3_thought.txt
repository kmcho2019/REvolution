Novel approach using a shift register for cycle counting:
1. Instead of a binary counter, use a 4-bit shift register initialized to '0001'
2. On each clock cycle, left-shift the register (with 0 fill)
3. shift_ena is the OR of all bits except the MSB
4. Reset behavior:
   - Initialize shift register to '0001' on reset
   - After 4 shifts, register becomes '0000' and stays there
5. Advantages:
   - Naturally counts exactly 4 cycles (when '1' shifts out)
   - No explicit counter or done flag needed
   - Simple combinatorial logic for output
   - Intuitive visual representation of timing
   - Easily extensible to different cycle counts
