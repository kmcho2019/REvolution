Corrected approach:
1. Maintain the 2-bit counter (0-3) for counting 4 cycles
2. Add a 'done' flag to track when we've completed the 4 cycles
3. shift_ena should be high only when:
   - We're in reset (immediately starts counting)
   - Counter is 0-3 AND we're not done yet
4. The 'done' flag should:
   - Be set when counter reaches 3
   - Be cleared on reset
5. This ensures:
   - Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset (counts 0,1,2,3)
   - shift_ena stays low forever after until next reset
   - No counter rollover issues
   - Minimal hardware (2-bit counter + 1-bit flag)
6. Key improvements:
   - Prevents unwanted reassertion of shift_ena
   - Clearly separates counting phase from done phase
   - Maintains synchronous reset behavior
