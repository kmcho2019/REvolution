2. New approach:

   - shift_ena is high when counter <= 3 (inclusive)

   - Counter increments each cycle until 3, then freezes
3. Implementation details:

   - Add a 'done' flag to freeze counter after 4 cycles
   - Reset clears both counter and done flag
4. Benefits:

   - Clean termination after 4 cycles
   - No continuous counting loop
   - Fully synchronous design
