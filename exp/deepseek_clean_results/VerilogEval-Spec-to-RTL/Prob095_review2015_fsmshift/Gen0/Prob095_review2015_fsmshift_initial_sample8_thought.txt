To implement this module, we need a finite state machine (FSM) that:
1. On reset (active high), starts counting 4 clock cycles and asserts shift_ena during this period
2. After 4 cycles, keeps shift_ena low until next reset
3. Uses a 2-bit counter to track the 4 cycles
4. Has synchronous reset behavior (all state changes on clock edges)

The FSM will have two states:
- COUNTING: when we're in the 4-cycle period after reset
- IDLE: when we're waiting for next reset
