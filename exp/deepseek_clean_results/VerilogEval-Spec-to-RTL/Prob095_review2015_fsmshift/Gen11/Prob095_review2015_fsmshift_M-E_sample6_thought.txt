Novel design approach using a shift register:
1. Instead of a counter, use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with 0 input (becomes 4'b0111, 4'b0011, etc.)
3. shift_ena is simply the MSB of the shift register
4. After 4 shifts, the register becomes 4'b0000 and stays there
5. Reset behavior:
   - Synchronously load 4'b1111 into the shift register
   - This makes shift_ena high for exactly 4 cycles (as the '1's shift out)
6. Advantages:
   - No arithmetic operations needed
   - Simple, direct implementation of the 4-cycle requirement
   - Naturally stops after 4 cycles without additional logic
   - Clear visual representation of the timing
