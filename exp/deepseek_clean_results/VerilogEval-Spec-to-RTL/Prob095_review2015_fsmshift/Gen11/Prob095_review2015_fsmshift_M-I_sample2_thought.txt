The original solution had the right concept but contained syntax errors due to duplicate declarations. Here's the improved approach:

1. Use a single state_t enum declaration with 4 states (S0-S3) to represent the 4 cycles
2. Maintain a single current_state register
3. Reset transitions directly to S0 (first active cycle)
4. Each state advances to next state on clock edge
5. S3 loops back to itself (shift_ena=0)
6. shift_ena is active in states S0-S2 (3 cycles) plus the transition into S0 (total 4 cycles)
7. All logic is synchronous to positive clock edge

Improvements:
- Eliminated duplicate declarations
- Clear state progression for exactly 4 cycles
- Simple combinational output logic
- Maintains all original functionality
- More efficient than counter-based approach for small cycle counts
