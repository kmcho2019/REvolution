Here's a novel state machine approach using explicit states instead of a counter:

1. Implement a 4-state FSM (S0-S3) to track the 4 active cycles
2. Add a terminal state (S_DONE) to hold shift_ena low forever
3. Transition through states S0→S1→S2→S3→S_DONE on each clock
4. Reset forces immediate transition to S0
5. shift_ena is high in S0-S3, low in S_DONE
6. No counters needed - pure state transitions

Advantages:
- Explicit state tracking makes behavior very clear
- No arithmetic operations needed
- Easy to modify if timing requirements change
- Clearly separates active and inactive periods
