Here's a refactored version that maintains the same core functionality but with a cleaner structure:

1. Still use a 2-bit counter (0-3) to track the 4 cycles
2. Make shift_ena purely combinational based on counter value
3. Counter stops incrementing after reaching 3 (0b11)
4. Reset synchronously clears the counter
5. Key improvements:
   - Clear separation between counter logic and output generation
   - Counter stops after 4 cycles (doesn't wrap around)
   - shift_ena is low after 4 cycles regardless of reset state
   - More explicit state management
