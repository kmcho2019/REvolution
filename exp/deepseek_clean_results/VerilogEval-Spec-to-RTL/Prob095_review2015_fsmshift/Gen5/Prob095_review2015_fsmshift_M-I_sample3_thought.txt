The original solution is conceptually correct but can be optimized for better power and area efficiency while maintaining the same functionality. Here's the improved strategy:

1. Replace one-hot encoding with a simple 2-bit counter (more area efficient)
2. Use a counter to track cycles (0-3) instead of state bits
3. Clear counter on reset and increment each cycle until reaching 3
4. Generate shift_ena when counter value is less than 4
5. This reduces flip-flop count from 4 to 2 while maintaining same behavior
6. Simplified logic with direct comparison for shift_ena generation

- 50% reduction in state storage (2 bits vs 4)
- Simpler comparison logic for shift_ena

- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
