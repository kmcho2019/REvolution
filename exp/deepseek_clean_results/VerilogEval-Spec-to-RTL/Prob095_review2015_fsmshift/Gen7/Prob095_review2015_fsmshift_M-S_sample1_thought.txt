To simplify while maintaining correct functionality:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to track 4 cycles (0 to 3)
2. Reset the counter to 0 on reset
3. Keep counting until we reach 3 (binary 11)
4. Generate shift_ena when counter is active (0-3)
5. The shift_ena will be high for exactly 4 cycles after reset (including reset cycle)
6. After 4 cycles, shift_ena stays low until next reset

Key improvements:
- Simple counter logic with clear 4-cycle count
- Direct mapping between counter value and enable
- Meets all original requirements precisely
