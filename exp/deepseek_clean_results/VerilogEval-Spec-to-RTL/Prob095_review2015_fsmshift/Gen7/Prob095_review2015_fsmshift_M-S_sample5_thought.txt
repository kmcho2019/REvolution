Simplified solution approach:
1. Use a 3-bit counter instead of state machine to count exactly 4 cycles after reset
2. Counter starts at 0 on reset and increments each cycle until it reaches 3
3. shift_ena is asserted while counter value is less than 4 (0-3)
4. After 4 cycles, counter stops and shift_ena remains low
5. Synchronous reset behavior matches requirements
6. More efficient than FSM approach with fewer states and simpler logic
