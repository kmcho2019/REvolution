Here's a novel approach using a down counter instead of a traditional state machine:

1. Use a 2-bit counter to count down from 3 (covering 4 cycles including the initial state)
2. Initialize counter to 3 on reset
3. Decrement counter each cycle while it's not zero
4. shift_ena is simply the "not zero" signal of the counter
5. After reaching zero, counter stays at zero until next reset

Advantages:
- More compact implementation than state machine
- Clear relationship between counter value and cycles remaining
- Easily extensible if cycle count needs to change
- Simple logic with minimal gates
- Naturally handles the 4-cycle requirement
