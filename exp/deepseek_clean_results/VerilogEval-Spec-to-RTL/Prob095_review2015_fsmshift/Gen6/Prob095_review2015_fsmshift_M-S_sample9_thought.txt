Simplified solution approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to track cycles since reset (0-3)
2. Reset should synchronously start the counting sequence
3. Counter increments every clock cycle (including during reset)
4. shift_ena is simply the inverted MSB of the counter (count[1])
   - This gives exactly 4 cycles of enable (count=0,1,2,3) since count[1] becomes 1 at count=4
5. After 4 cycles, shift_ena stays low until next reset

Key simplifications:
- Single bit comparison for shift_ena (just count[1])
- Counter always increments, no conditional logic
- Minimal logic while maintaining exact 4-cycle requirement
