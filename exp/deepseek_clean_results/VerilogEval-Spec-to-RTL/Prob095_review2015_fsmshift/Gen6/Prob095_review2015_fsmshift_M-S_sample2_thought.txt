To simplify while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to track 4 cycles (0 to 3)
2. Reset the counter to 0 on reset
3. Keep counting until we reach 3 (binary 11)
4. Generate shift_ena when counter is active (0-3)
5. This requires only 2 flip-flops and simple comparison logic
6. The shift_ena will be high for exactly 4 cycles after reset (including reset cycle)

Key improvements:
- Removes the OR with reset in shift_ena assignment
- Counter naturally stops at 3 (no need for additional logic)
- Clear direct mapping between counter value and enable
- Still meets all original requirements
