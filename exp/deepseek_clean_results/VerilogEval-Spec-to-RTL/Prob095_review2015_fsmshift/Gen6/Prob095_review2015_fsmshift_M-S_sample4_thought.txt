To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality:

1. Instead of using a full FSM with multiple states, we can use a 2-bit counter to count the 4 cycles needed.
2. The counter will be reset to 0 when the system reset is asserted.
3. shift_ena will be high when the counter value is less than 4 (0 to 3).
4. After reaching 3, the counter will stop incrementing and shift_ena will stay low.
5. This approach reduces the state machine complexity to just a simple counter.
6. The synchronous reset ensures clean timing alignment with the clock.
