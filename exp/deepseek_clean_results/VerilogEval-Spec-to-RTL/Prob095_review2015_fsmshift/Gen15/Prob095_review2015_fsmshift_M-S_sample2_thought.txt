Simplified design approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to track cycles (0-3)
2. shift_ena is active when counter is less than 4 (0-3)
3. Reset initializes counter to 0
4. Counter increments each cycle until it reaches 3, then stops
5. No need for reset in shift_ena logic since counter starts at 0
6. After 4 cycles (when counter=3), shift_ena automatically goes low
7. Counter remains at 3 until next reset
Key simplifications:
- Remove reset from shift_ena logic
- Fixed counter width (still 2 bits)
- Clear stopping condition
- Guaranteed 4 cycles of shift_ena
