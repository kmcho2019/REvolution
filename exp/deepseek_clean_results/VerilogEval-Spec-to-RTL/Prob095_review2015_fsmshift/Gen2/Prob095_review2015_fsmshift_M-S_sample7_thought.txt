Here's a simplified approach that maintains the exact same functionality:
1. Use a 2-bit counter that counts from 0 to 3 (4 cycles)
2. Reset initializes the counter to 0
3. shift_ena is simply the MSB of the counter (bit 1)
4. Counter stops incrementing after reaching 3 (binary 11)
This reduces logic by using the counter's natural overflow and eliminates the need for comparison operations.
