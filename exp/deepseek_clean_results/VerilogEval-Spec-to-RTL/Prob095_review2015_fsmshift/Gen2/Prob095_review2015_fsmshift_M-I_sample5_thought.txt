The original solution had a fundamental issue with the counter comparison logic that caused shift_ena to be asserted for only 3 cycles instead of 4. Here's the improved approach:

1. Still use a 2-bit counter (range 0-3) to count 4 cycles
2. Make shift_ena high when counter is less than 4 (0-3 inclusive)
3. Reset should immediately set counter to 0 and keep shift_ena high
4. After counting to 3, the counter should freeze (prevent wrap-around) until next reset
5. shift_ena should go low only after the 4th cycle completes

This ensures:
- Exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset
- No unnecessary toggling after the initial 4 cycles
- Proper synchronous reset behavior
