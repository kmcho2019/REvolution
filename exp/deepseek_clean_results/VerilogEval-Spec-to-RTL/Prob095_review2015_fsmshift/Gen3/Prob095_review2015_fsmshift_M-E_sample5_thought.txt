Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter to control the shift_ena signal:

1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to '1111' on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with a '0' input
3. OR all bits of the shift register to generate shift_ena
4. This creates exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset:
   - First cycle: 1111 (shift_ena = 1)
   - Second: 0111 (shift_ena = 1)
   - Third: 0011 (shift_ena = 1)
   - Fourth: 0001 (shift_ena = 1)
   - Fifth: 0000 (shift_ena = 0)
5. The shift register holds its value until next reset

Benefits:
- No need for explicit counting logic
- Simple shift operation with fixed pattern
- Naturally produces exactly 4 enable pulses
- Easy to verify timing visually
