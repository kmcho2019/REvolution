Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with 0 input (becomes 4'b0111, 4'b0011, etc.)
3. The leftmost bit of the shift register directly controls shift_ena
4. This ensures exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset (one for each '1' shifted out)
5. After 4 cycles, the register becomes 4'b0000 and stays there until next reset
6. Reset synchronously loads 4'b1111 into the shift register
This method eliminates the need for comparison logic and provides precise cycle counting through the shift pattern.
