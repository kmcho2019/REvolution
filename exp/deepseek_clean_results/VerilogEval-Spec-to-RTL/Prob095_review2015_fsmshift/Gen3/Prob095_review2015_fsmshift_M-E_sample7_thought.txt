Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with 0 as input
3. The shift_ena output is the OR of all shift register bits
4. This ensures exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena after reset
5. After 4 cycles, all bits become 0 and shift_ena stays 0
This approach eliminates comparison logic and uses simple shifting operations.
