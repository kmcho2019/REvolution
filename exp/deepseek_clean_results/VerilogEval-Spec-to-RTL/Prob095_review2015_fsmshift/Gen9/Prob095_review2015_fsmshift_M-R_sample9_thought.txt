Refactoring strategy:

2. Implement shift_ena as a combinational output using assign statement
3. Simplify the sequential logic to only handle counter increment and reset
4. Make reset behavior explicit - counter starts at 0 and counts to 3 (4 cycles)
5. shift_ena is high when counter < 4 (0-3) and low otherwise

- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic

- More maintainable and verifiable structure
