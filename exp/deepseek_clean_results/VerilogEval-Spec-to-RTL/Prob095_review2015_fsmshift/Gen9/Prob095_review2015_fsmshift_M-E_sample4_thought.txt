Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:

1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. On each clock cycle, shift in a 0 from the right
3. The shift_ena output is the leftmost bit of the shift register
4. This naturally creates exactly 4 cycles of shift_ena (one for each '1' in the initial pattern)
5. After 4 shifts, the register becomes 4'b0000 and shift_ena stays 0
6. Reset synchronously reloads the 4'b1111 pattern

Advantages:
- No explicit counting logic needed
- Simple shift operation achieves the timing requirement
- Clear visual representation of the 4-cycle enable period
- Naturally stops asserting shift_ena after exactly 4 cycles
