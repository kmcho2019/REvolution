Alternative approach using a down counter with synchronous reset:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to count down from 3 to 0 (4 cycles total)
2. Initialize counter to 3 on reset
3. Decrement counter each clock cycle while not zero
4. shift_ena is asserted when counter is not zero
5. Benefits:
   - More compact than FSM approach (only 2 flip-flops needed)
   - Clear relationship between counter value and remaining cycles
   - Easily adjustable cycle count by changing initial value
   - Naturally stops counting at zero
6. Reset behavior:
   - Counter loads 3 on reset (first cycle after reset)
   - Decrements each cycle until zero
   - Stays at zero until next reset
