Simplified approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter instead of shift register for precise cycle counting
2. Counter increments on each clock cycle after reset
3. shift_ena is asserted when counter value is less than 4 (0-3)
4. Reset synchronously clears the counter and starts the 4-cycle count
5. After 4 cycles, counter stops incrementing (holds at 4) until next reset
6. This guarantees:
   - shift_ena asserted exactly 4 cycles (including reset cycle)
   - Simple and clear state tracking
   - Minimal hardware resources (only 2 flip-flops needed)
