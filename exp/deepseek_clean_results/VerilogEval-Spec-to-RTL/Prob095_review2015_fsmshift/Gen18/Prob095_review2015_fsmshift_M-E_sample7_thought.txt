Novel approach using a shift register instead of a counter:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register initialized to 4'b1111 on reset
2. Each clock cycle, shift right with 0 input (becomes 4'b0111, then 4'b0011, etc.)
3. The MSB of the shift register directly serves as shift_ena
4. This naturally provides exactly 4 cycles of enable after reset
5. After 4 shifts, the register becomes 4'b0000 and stays there until next reset
6. Advantages:
   - No explicit counter or done flag needed
   - Simple and elegant implementation
   - Exactly matches the timing requirement
   - Clear visual correspondence between register state and enable duration
