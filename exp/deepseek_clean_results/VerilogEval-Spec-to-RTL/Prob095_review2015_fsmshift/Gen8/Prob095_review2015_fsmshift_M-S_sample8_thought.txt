Simplified solution approach:
1. Use a 2-bit counter to count exactly 4 cycles (0 to 3)

3. shift_ena is active while counter is counting (values 0-3)
4. After reaching count=3, shift_ena stays permanently low
5. Reset synchronously restarts the counting

- Simple comparison for shift_ena generation
- Clear 4-cycle count (including reset cycle)
