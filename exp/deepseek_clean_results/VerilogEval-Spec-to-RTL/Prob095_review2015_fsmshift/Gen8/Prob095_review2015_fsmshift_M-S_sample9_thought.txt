Here's a simplified approach that maintains the required functionality:

1. Use a 2-bit counter to count exactly 4 cycles (0-3) after reset
2. The counter increments on each clock cycle after reset
3. shift_ena is active when counter value is less than 4 (i.e., bits [1:0] are any value)
4. The counter stops counting once it reaches 3 (binary 11)
5. This ensures shift_ena is active for exactly 4 cycles (0,1,2,3) after reset
6. All logic is synchronous to the positive clock edge

Advantages:
- Simpler counter control (just increment until max value)
- Clear 4-cycle count (0-3 is more intuitive than 3-0)
- No need for complex comparison logic
- Naturally stops counting after 4 cycles
