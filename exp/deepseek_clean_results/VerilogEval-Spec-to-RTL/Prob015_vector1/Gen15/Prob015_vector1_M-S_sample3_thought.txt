The simplest way to split a 16-bit value into upper and lower bytes is through direct bit selection:
1. out_hi can be directly assigned to bits [15:8] of the input
2. out_lo can be directly assigned to bits [7:0] of the input
3. This eliminates unnecessary shift and mask operations
4. Benefits:
   - Most straightforward implementation
   - Minimal logic required
   - Clearly shows the intent of byte splitting
   - More readable and maintainable
