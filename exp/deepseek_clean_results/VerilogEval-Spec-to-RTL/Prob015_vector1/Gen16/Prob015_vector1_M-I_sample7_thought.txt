The original solution works correctly but can be optimized for better PPA by:
1. Replacing the arithmetic right shift (>>>) with simple bit slicing for the upper byte
2. Using direct part-select for the lower byte instead of masking
3. This eliminates unnecessary operations and reduces gate count
4. The improved version will be functionally identical but more efficient in:
   - Timing: No shift/mask operations needed
   - Power: Simpler logic reduces switching activity
   - Area: Direct wiring requires fewer gates
