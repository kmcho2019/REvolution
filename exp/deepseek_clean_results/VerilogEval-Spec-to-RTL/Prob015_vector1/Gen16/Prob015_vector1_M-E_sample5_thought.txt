This alternative implementation uses a shift register approach to demonstrate how the byte splitting could be done sequentially (though still combinational in this case). The key ideas are:
1. Uses a barrel shifter concept to "rotate" the bits into position
2. Implements the split through explicit bit manipulation rather than direct part-select
3. The implementation could be easily extended to support dynamic splitting positions
4. Makes the bit-level operations more visible, which could be useful for educational purposes

While less efficient than direct part-select, this approach demonstrates:
- Alternative bit manipulation techniques
- Potential for parameterization (split position could be made configurable)
- Clear visualization of how each output bit is derived
