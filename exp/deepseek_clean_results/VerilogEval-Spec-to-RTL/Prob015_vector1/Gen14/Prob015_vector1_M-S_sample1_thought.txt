The simplest way to split a 16-bit value into upper and lower bytes is through direct bit selection, which is more straightforward than using shift operations. This approach:
1. Directly selects bits [15:8] for the upper byte (out_hi)
2. Directly selects bits [7:0] for the lower byte (out_lo)
3. Eliminates unnecessary masking operations since the bit selection inherently gives us the correct 8 bits
4. Results in cleaner, more readable code that clearly expresses the intent
5. Maintains the combinational nature of the circuit
