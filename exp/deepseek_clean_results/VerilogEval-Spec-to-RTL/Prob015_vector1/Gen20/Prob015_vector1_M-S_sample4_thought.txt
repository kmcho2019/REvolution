The simplest and most straightforward way to split a 16-bit input into upper and lower bytes is to use direct bit-slicing. This approach:
1. Directly assigns the upper 8 bits (bits 15:8) to out_hi
2. Directly assigns the lower 8 bits (bits 7:0) to out_lo
3. Eliminates unnecessary generate loops and individual bit assignments
4. Is more readable and maintainable
5. Will synthesize to the same efficient hardware as the loop version but with clearer intent
