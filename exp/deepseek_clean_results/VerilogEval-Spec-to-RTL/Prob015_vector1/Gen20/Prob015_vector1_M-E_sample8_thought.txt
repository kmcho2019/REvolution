Here's a novel approach using a shift register concept without actually implementing sequential logic. The key idea is to:
1. Treat the 16-bit input as two separate bytes conceptually
2. Use concatenation and masking operations to extract the bytes
3. Implement this through bitwise operations that mimic a parallel shift operation
4. This approach demonstrates an alternative way of thinking about bit slicing while maintaining combinational behavior

The implementation uses:
- Bitwise AND with masks to isolate bytes
- Right shift operation for the high byte (though implemented through slicing)
- No generate loops or parameters for maximum simplicity
- Clear separation of the two output paths
