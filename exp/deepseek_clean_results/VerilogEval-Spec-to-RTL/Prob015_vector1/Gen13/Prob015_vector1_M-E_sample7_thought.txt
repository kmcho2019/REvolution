Instead of direct bit slicing, we'll implement a shift-based approach that:
1. Uses arithmetic right shift to isolate the upper byte
2. Masks the lower byte using bitwise AND
3. Demonstrates an alternative way to achieve the same result
4. While less efficient than direct slicing, shows different implementation options

This approach is less optimal for PPA but demonstrates:
- Alternative bit manipulation techniques
- How the same result can be achieved through different operations
- The flexibility of Verilog in expressing solutions
