The original solution is already optimal for this simple bit-slicing operation. Since:
1. It uses direct wire connections (assign statements) with zero combinational delay
2. Consumes minimal power (only wire switching)
3. Uses minimal area (just routing)

No meaningful PPA improvements are possible because:
- The operation can't be made faster than direct wire connections
- Power can't be reduced below wire switching
- Area can't be reduced below simple routing

The only possible "improvement" would be stylistic - using concatenation to show the relationship between outputs, though this has no actual PPA impact.
