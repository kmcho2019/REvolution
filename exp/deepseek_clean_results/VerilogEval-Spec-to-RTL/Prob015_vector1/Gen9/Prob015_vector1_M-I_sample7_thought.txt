The current solution works correctly but has unnecessary operations that impact PPA. Here's the optimized approach:
1. Remove the shift operation completely - we can directly access the upper byte [15:8]
2. Remove the masking operation - simply selecting bits [7:0] is sufficient
3. Use direct bit selection which requires no logic gates, just wiring
This optimization will:
- Reduce area by eliminating the shift and mask operations
- Reduce power by removing unnecessary logic operations
- Maintain or potentially improve timing as the critical path is now just wire connections
