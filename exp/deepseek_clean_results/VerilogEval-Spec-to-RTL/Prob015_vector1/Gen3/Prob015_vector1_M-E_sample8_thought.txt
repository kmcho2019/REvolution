Instead of direct bit-slicing, we can implement this as a conceptual shift operation where:
1. The lower byte is obtained by masking the input with 0xFF (11111111)
2. The upper byte is obtained by right-shifting the input by 8 bits then masking
This approach demonstrates the byte separation concept through operations rather than direct wiring, while still being combinational. It's less efficient than direct wiring but shows an alternative implementation method.
