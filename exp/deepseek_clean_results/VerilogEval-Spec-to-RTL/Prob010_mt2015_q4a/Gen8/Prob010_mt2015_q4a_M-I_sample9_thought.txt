The current implementation can be significantly optimized by recognizing that the boolean function z = (x^y) & x simplifies to z = x & ~y through boolean algebra. This simplification offers several advantages:

1. **Performance Improvement**: Reduces logic depth from 2 stages (XOR + AND) to just 1 stage (AND with inverted input)
2. **Power Reduction**: Eliminates unnecessary switching activity from the XOR gate and redundant AND operation
3. **Area Savings**: Replaces three logic elements (XOR, MUX, AND) with just one AND gate and an inverter

The optimized implementation will directly compute z = x & ~y, which is mathematically equivalent to the original function but more efficient in all PPA metrics.
