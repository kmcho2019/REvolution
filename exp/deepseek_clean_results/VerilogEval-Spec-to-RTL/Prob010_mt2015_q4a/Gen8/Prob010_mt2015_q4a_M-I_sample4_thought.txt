To optimize the PPA further, we can consider the following improvements:

1. Direct Implementation: The original function z = (x^y) & x simplifies to x & ~y. Implementing this directly with a single AND-NOT gate could be more efficient than the mux approach.

2. Gate-Level Optimization: 
   - The mux implementation requires an inverter and a mux (2 gates)
   - A direct AND-NOT implementation uses just 1 compound gate or 2 basic gates (inverter + AND)
   - Some standard cell libraries have optimized AND-NOT compound gates

3. Power Reduction:
   - Fewer gates mean less switching power
   - Direct implementation has shorter critical path (1 gate delay vs 2)

4. Area Reduction:
   - Potentially uses fewer transistors
   - May leverage library's most area-efficient AND-NOT cell
