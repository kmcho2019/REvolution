The simplest implementation would be to directly express the boolean function z = (x^y) & x using basic logical operators. This can be further simplified by observing that (x^y) & x is equivalent to x & ~y, since x^y is x&~y | ~x&y, and when ANDed with x, only the x&~y term remains. This reduces the logic to a simple AND of x and NOT y, which is the most straightforward implementation possible.
