The original function z = (x^y) & x can be simplified using boolean algebra:
1. Expand the XOR: (x^y) = x&~y | ~x&y
2. AND with x: (x&~y | ~x&y) & x = x&~y (since ~x&y & x = 0)
3. The simplified form is z = x & ~y
This implementation requires just one NOT gate for y and one AND gate, which is more efficient than the mux-based approach.
