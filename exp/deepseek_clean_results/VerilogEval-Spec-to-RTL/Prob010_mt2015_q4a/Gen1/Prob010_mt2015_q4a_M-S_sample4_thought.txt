The boolean function z = (x^y) & x can be simplified using Boolean algebra:
1. (x^y) is equivalent to x&~y | ~x&y
2. When we AND this with x: (x&~y | ~x&y) & x
3. The ~x&y term becomes 0 when ANDed with x, leaving only x&~y
4. Therefore, the entire expression simplifies to x & ~y

This simplification reduces the logic from requiring both XOR and AND operations to just requiring an AND and a NOT operation.
