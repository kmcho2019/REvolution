Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based implementation. Instead of directly computing the boolean expression, we'll use the x input as a selector for a mux that chooses between 0 and ~y. This is functionally equivalent to z = x & ~y but demonstrates an alternative way to think about the logic. The mux approach might be more intuitive for some designers and could potentially map better to certain FPGA architectures that have dedicated mux resources.
