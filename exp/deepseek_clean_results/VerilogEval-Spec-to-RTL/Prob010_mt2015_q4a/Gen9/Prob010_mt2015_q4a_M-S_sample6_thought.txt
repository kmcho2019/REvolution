The given function z = (x^y) & x can be simplified using Boolean algebra:
(x^y) & x = (x & ~y | ~x & y) & x
           = (x & ~y & x) | (~x & y & x)
           = (x & ~y) | 0
           = x & ~y

This shows the function is equivalent to a simple AND operation between x and the complement of y. We can implement this directly with a single AND gate and an inverter, which is the minimal implementation possible for this function.
