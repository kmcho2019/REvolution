The boolean expression z = (x^y) & x can be simplified using boolean algebra. The XOR operation x^y can be expanded to (x & ~y) | (~x & y). When ANDed with x, the second term (~x & y) & x becomes 0 (since x & ~x = 0), leaving only x & ~y. Therefore, the entire expression simplifies to z = x & ~y, which requires just one NOT gate and one AND gate instead of an XOR and AND gate.
