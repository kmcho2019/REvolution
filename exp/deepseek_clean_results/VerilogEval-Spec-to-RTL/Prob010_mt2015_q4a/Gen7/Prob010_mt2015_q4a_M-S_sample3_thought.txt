The original function z = (x^y) & x can be simplified through boolean algebra:
1. Expand XOR: x^y = x&~y | ~x&y
2. AND with x: (x&~y | ~x&y) & x = x&~y (since ~x&y & x = 0)
3. Final simplified form: z = x & ~y

This requires only one AND gate with an inverted y input, which is:
- More area-efficient (single gate)
- Faster (single gate delay)
- Lower power (less switching activity)
