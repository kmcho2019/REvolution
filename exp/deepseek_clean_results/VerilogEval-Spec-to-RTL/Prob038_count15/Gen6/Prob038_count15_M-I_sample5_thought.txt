To optimize the counter implementation, we should:
1. Use binary encoding instead of one-hot to reduce register width from 16 to 4 bits
2. Implement standard binary increment logic which is more area-efficient
3. Remove the complex output encoding logic since the counter value is the output
4. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
5. Keep the same counting behavior (0-15 with period of 16)
This approach will significantly reduce area and power while improving timing.
