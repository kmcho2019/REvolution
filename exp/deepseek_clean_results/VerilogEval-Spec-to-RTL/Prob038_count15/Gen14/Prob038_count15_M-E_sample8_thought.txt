Here's a novel approach using a rotating shift register with feedback to implement the counter:

1. Instead of traditional binary counting, we'll use a circular shift register that rotates left with each clock cycle
2. The feedback logic will ensure the pattern cycles through all 16 states (0000 to 1111)
3. Special feedback cases handle the transitions between states to maintain proper counting sequence
4. The reset initializes to 0000
5. This approach offers:
   - Unique state encoding (not pure binary)
   - All bits change predictably each cycle
   - Simple feedback logic
   - Potential power savings from reduced switching activity compared to LSB toggling
   - Novel architecture that's different from standard counters
