To optimize the 4-bit binary counter for better PPA metrics while maintaining functionality:

1. Performance Improvement:
   - Replace simple increment with carry-lookahead logic to break the ripple carry chain
   - This reduces the critical path delay by computing carry bits in parallel

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating when counter reaches 15 (0xF) to prevent unnecessary toggling
   - This reduces dynamic power when the counter would normally wrap around

3. Area Consideration:
   - Keep the basic 4-bit register structure as it's already minimal
   - The carry-lookahead logic adds some area but improves timing significantly

The improved design will:
- Maintain synchronous reset functionality
- Count from 0 to 15 with period of 16
- Have better timing performance
- Lower power consumption
- Slightly increased area for performance gain
