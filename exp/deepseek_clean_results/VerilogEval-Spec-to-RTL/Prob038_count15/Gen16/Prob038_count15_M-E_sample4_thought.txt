I'll implement a 4-bit counter using an LFSR (Linear Feedback Shift Register) with additional conversion logic. This approach:
1. Uses a 4-bit LFSR that cycles through all 15 non-zero states (Galois configuration)
2. Adds special handling for the zero state to complete the 16-state cycle
3. Includes a binary decoder to convert the LFSR state to binary count
4. Maintains synchronous reset functionality
5. Offers potential advantages in testability and pattern generation
The LFSR uses taps at positions 3 and 4 (x^4 + x^3 + 1 polynomial) to generate a maximal-length sequence. The binary decoder converts the pseudo-random sequence to a binary count.
