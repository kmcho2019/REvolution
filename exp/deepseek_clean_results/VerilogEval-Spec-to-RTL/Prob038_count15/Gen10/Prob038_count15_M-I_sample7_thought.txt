To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Remove the intermediate next_count wire and combine the logic in the always block
   - This reduces one level of combinational logic, improving timing

2. Power Optimization:
   - Directly use the reset condition in the always block to prevent unnecessary logic
   - This reduces switching activity compared to the wire-based approach

3. Area Optimization:
   - Remove the separate q assignment and directly output the count register
   - This eliminates one unnecessary assignment

The improved design will maintain the same functionality while being more efficient in all PPA metrics.
