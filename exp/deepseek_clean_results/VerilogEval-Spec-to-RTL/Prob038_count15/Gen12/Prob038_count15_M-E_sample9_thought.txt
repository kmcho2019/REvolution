I'll implement a parallel-load counter with a twist: instead of a simple increment, I'll use a rotating shift register approach combined with a pattern generator. This creates a counter that:
1. Uses a 4-bit shift register that rotates left
2. Adds a fixed pattern (0001) when rotating to maintain the counting sequence
3. Still maintains the 0-15 counting range with period 16
4. Benefits:
   - Novel architecture that's different from standard counters
   - Potential for lower power due to localized bit changes
   - Interesting trade-off between area and timing
   - Demonstrates alternative counting approaches
