To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Performance: The current design has minor timing violations (-0.24ns WNS). I'll combine the combinational and sequential logic into a single always block to reduce the critical path. The increment operation will be done directly in the sequential block.

2. Power: I'll add clock gating when the counter reaches 15 (0xF) to prevent unnecessary toggling during the rollover cycle. This reduces dynamic power consumption.

3. Area: The combined logic approach will slightly reduce area by eliminating the separate next_state wire and combinational logic. I'll also make the reset conditional to potentially save area if synthesis can optimize it.

These changes should improve timing closure, reduce power, and potentially save area while maintaining the same functionality.
