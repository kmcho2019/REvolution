Instead of using a standard binary counter, let's implement a linear-feedback shift register (LFSR) based counter. While LFSRs are typically used for pseudo-random number generation, we can configure a 4-bit LFSR to cycle through all 16 states (0-15) in a specific order. This approach offers potential power savings since fewer bits toggle on average compared to a binary counter. The LFSR will use the polynomial x^4 + x + 1 which generates a maximal-length sequence for 4 bits.
