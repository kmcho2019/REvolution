This solution implements a 4-bit counter using a rotating shift register with XOR feedback, inspired by ring counters but modified for binary counting. The key ideas are:

1. Use a 4-bit shift register that rotates left with each clock
2. Add XOR feedback from specific bits to create the counting sequence
3. The feedback pattern is designed to produce a binary count sequence
4. Reset initializes to 0001 (start of sequence)
5. After 16 cycles, the pattern repeats correctly

Advantages:
- Novel architecture different from standard binary counters
- Potentially lower power due to reduced switching activity
- Interesting alternative implementation that still meets requirements
- Demonstrates creative use of shift registers for counting
