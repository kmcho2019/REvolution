I'll implement the counter using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) approach instead of a traditional binary counter. This will create a pseudo-random sequence that cycles through all 16 states (0-15) but in a non-sequential order. The LFSR will use the polynomial x^4 + x^3 + 1 (tap positions 4 and 3) which guarantees a full cycle of 15 states plus the all-zero state. I'll add special handling to include the all-zero state in the sequence while maintaining the period of 16. The reset will synchronously initialize the register to 4'b0001.
