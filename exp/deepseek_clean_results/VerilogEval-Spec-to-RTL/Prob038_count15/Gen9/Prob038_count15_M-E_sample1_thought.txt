I'll implement a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) style counter that uses XOR feedback to generate the counting sequence. This approach:
1. Uses a 4-bit shift register with specific feedback taps
2. Implements a maximum-length sequence that cycles through all 15 non-zero states (0001 to 1111)
3. Adds special logic to include the zero state (0000) to complete the 16-state cycle
4. Maintains synchronous reset functionality
5. Offers potential advantages in speed and area due to simpler feedback logic compared to binary counters
The feedback taps are chosen to create a maximal-length sequence (using polynomial x^4 + x^3 + 1), with additional logic to inject the zero state when needed.
