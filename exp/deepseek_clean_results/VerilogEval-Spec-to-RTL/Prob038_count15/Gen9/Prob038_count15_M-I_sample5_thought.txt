Optimization Strategy:
1. Maintain the same functionality (4-bit counter with synchronous reset)
2. Focus on power optimization since timing and area are already minimal
3. Add clock gating when counter reaches maximum value (15) to prevent unnecessary toggling
4. Keep the design clean and readable while adding minimal complexity
5. Use a combinational always block for clock gating logic to avoid timing issues

The improvement adds clock gating to reduce power consumption when the counter saturates at 15, while maintaining all original functionality. This is particularly useful if the counter might remain at 15 for multiple cycles.
