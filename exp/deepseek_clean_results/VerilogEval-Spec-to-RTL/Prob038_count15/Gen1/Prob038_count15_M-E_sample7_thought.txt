Instead of a standard binary counter, we can implement a 4-bit maximal-length LFSR counter. This will cycle through all 16 possible states (including 0) in a pseudo-random order, while maintaining the same period of 16. The LFSR uses XOR feedback to generate the next state, which typically has better timing characteristics than a binary counter. The reset initializes the register to 0, and we include special detection to ensure it doesn't get stuck in the all-zeros state during normal operation.
