Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Instead of using a combinational always block, implement the logic using continuous assignments (assign statements)
2. This approach is more concise and eliminates the need for sensitivity lists
3. The logic becomes purely structural rather than procedural
4. Each output is directly driven by its logical expression
5. This method is more hardware-accurate as it directly represents the gate-level implementation
6. The design becomes more readable and maintainable with clear one-to-one mapping between logic and implementation
7. No risk of latch inference since all assignments are continuous
