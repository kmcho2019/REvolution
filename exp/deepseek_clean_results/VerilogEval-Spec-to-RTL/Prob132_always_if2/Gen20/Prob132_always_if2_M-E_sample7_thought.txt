Hierarchical Control with Explicit Defaults Approach:
1. **Explicit Defaults**: Clearly define all output states for better readability and maintainability
2. **Separate Control Logic**: Split into distinct always blocks for thermal control and vehicle control
3. **Positive Logic**: Use positive logic for vehicle control (drive when not arrived AND has fuel)
4. **Defensive Coding**: Include all inputs in sensitivity lists for robustness
5. **Modular Structure**: Organized to easily extend each control domain independently

Key Innovations:
- Clear separation of concerns between computer and vehicle systems
- Explicit default assignments prevent latch inference
- Positive logic makes the driving condition more intuitive
- Future-proof structure for adding more conditions to either system
