1. Power Optimization:
   - Remove default assignments that cause unnecessary switching when conditions aren't met
   - Only assign outputs when needed, reducing dynamic power consumption

2. Maintainability:
   - Keep the single always block structure for compactness
   - Add clear comments explaining the intentional lack of default assignments

3. Timing/Area:
   - The design is already minimal in terms of gates (1 inverter and 1 AND gate)
   - No further area reduction is possible without changing functionality

The improved version will eliminate unnecessary switching while maintaining the same functionality and readability.
