Novel Architectural Approach:
1. Instead of using procedural blocks, implement the logic using continuous assignments for better clarity and synthesis optimization
2. Separate the two independent output signals into distinct assign statements
3. Use ternary conditional operators for concise expression of the logic
4. This approach eliminates the need for sensitivity lists and ensures all outputs are always driven
5. The design becomes purely combinational with no risk of latches
6. Each output's logic is clearly visible at the point of declaration
7. This style is particularly suitable for simple combinational logic like this case
