Here's a novel approach using a structural implementation with basic logic gates:
1. Instead of direct wire assignments, we'll use buffer gates (implemented as NOT-NOT pairs) for each connection
2. This creates explicit buffering in the design
3. The architecture demonstrates how simple wire connections could be implemented at a gate level
4. While functionally equivalent, this approach shows an alternative implementation strategy
5. The NOT gates will cancel each other out, effectively creating buffered wire connections
