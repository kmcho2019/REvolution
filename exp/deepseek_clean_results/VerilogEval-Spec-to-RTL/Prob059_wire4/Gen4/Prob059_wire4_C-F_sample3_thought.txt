The optimal solution combines the simplicity and clarity of direct assignments from Example 1 with the compactness of concatenated assignment from Example 2. The fusion strategy is:
1. Maintain the direct wire connection semantics which are most readable
2. Use concatenation for outputs that share the same input source (b -> x,y)
3. Keep individual assignments for unique connections (a->w, c->z)
This approach provides both clarity (explicit connections) and compactness (grouping same-source outputs) while maintaining optimal PPA characteristics.
