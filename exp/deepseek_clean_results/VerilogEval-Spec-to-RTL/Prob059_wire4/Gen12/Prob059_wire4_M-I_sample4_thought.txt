The current solution works correctly but can be optimized for better PPA by:
1. Removing the WirePair sub-module to eliminate hierarchy overhead
2. Using direct assign statements for all connections
3. This simplifies the design while maintaining identical functionality
4. The direct approach should slightly reduce power and area by:
   - Eliminating module instantiation overhead
   - Reducing routing complexity
   - Maintaining the same optimal timing characteristics

The improved version will be functionally equivalent but more efficient in terms of power and area.
