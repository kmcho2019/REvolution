The decade counter can be further simplified by recognizing that counting from 1 to 10 is equivalent to counting from 0 to 9 and then adding 1 to the result. This allows us to use a standard 4-bit binary counter (0-15) and simply reset when we reach 10 (1010). The reset condition remains the same (q == 4'b1010), but we can eliminate the explicit increment logic by letting the counter naturally overflow from 9 to 0 and then adjusting the output. This reduces the logic complexity while maintaining the same functionality.
