Here's a novel approach using a shift register with feedback to implement the decade counter. The key ideas are:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register where bits circulate with modifications
2. Implement the counting sequence by carefully designing the feedback logic
3. The pattern resets to '0001' on reset and automatically cycles through 1-10
4. This approach eliminates the need for a traditional adder and uses simpler logic for state transitions
5. The wrap-around is handled naturally by the shift pattern
