Here's a novel approach using a shift register with feedback to implement the decade counter. This design eliminates the need for a traditional adder and instead uses a rotating pattern:

1. Instead of incrementing, we'll use a 4-bit shift register that rotates left
2. We'll add feedback logic to create the sequence 1-10 by:
   - Starting with 0001 (1)
   - Rotating left until we reach 1010 (10)
   - Then resetting back to 0001
3. The rotation is controlled by XOR feedback when specific patterns are detected
4. This approach minimizes combinational logic and reduces critical path
5. The synchronous reset initializes the shift register to 0001

Benefits:
- No adder needed, reducing combinational logic
- Simple shift operations are generally faster than additions
- Feedback logic is minimal and optimized for the specific sequence
- Easily scalable to other counting sequences with pattern modification
