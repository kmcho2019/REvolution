To simplify the decade counter while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the explicit comparison logic for 10 (1010) since we can rely on natural overflow behavior
2. Use a simple increment with conditional reset to 1 when reaching 11 (since 10+1=11)
3. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
4. This approach eliminates the need for any bitwise comparisons, reducing logic complexity
5. The counter will naturally count 1-10 and reset to 1 on the next cycle
