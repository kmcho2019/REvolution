To simplify the decade counter while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the optimized wrap-around detection logic and use a simple equality comparison (q == 4'b1010) which is more readable and equally efficient
2. Eliminate the enable signal since it adds complexity without significant benefit for this simple counter
3. Keep the basic synchronous reset and increment logic
4. Maintain the same counting sequence (1 through 10) with synchronous reset to 1

The simplified design will:
- Use straightforward comparison for wrap-around detection
- Keep the same reset behavior
- Maintain the counting sequence
- Be easier to understand and maintain
