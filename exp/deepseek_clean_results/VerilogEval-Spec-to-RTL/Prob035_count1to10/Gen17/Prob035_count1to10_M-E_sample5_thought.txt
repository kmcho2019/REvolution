Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Implement a Johnson counter (twisted ring counter) with additional logic to create a decade counter
2. Use a 4-bit shift register with inverted feedback to generate 10 unique states
3. Add combinational logic to convert Johnson counter states to 1-10 sequence
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced switching activity (only one bit changes per clock)
   - Potential power savings
   - Interesting alternative to binary counter approach
5. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
6. Use non-blocking assignments for sequential logic

Key Features:
- Johnson counter naturally cycles through 2N states (8 for 4 bits)
- Additional logic extends this to 10 states
- Simple state transitions (single bit changes)
- Unique encoding that may offer better timing in some cases
