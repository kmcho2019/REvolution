To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10, we'll use a 4-bit register to store the current count value. The counter will increment by 1 on each clock edge, wrapping around to 1 after reaching 10. A synchronous reset will force the counter back to 1. The key points are:
1. Use a 4-bit register to store the count
2. Increment count on each positive clock edge
3. Reset to 1 when reset is high
4. After reaching 10 (1010 in binary), wrap around to 1
