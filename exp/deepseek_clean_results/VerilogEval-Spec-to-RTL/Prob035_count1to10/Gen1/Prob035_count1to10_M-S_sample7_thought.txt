To simplify the decade counter implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Remove the explicit comparison with 10 (4'b1010) by using modular arithmetic
2. Use a simple increment and conditional wrap-around
3. Maintain synchronous reset to 1
4. The key insight is that after 10, we can subtract 9 to wrap around to 1 (since 10+1-9=2, 9+1-9=1, etc.)
This reduces the comparator logic while keeping the same behavior.
