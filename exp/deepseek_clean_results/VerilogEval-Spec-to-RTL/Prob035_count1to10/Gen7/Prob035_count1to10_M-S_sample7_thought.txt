To simplify the decade counter while maintaining functionality, I'll make these improvements:

1. **State Representation**:
   - Use a 4-bit binary counter instead of one-hot encoding to reduce flip-flop count from 10 to 4
   - Directly output the counter value, eliminating the need for conversion logic

2. **Counting Logic**:
   - Implement simple increment with wrap-around from 10 to 1
   - Use synchronous reset to initialize to 1

3. **Optimizations**:
   - Remove redundant state conversion logic
   - Simplify the design to just a counter and reset logic
   - Maintain same functionality with less area and power

This approach reduces complexity while keeping all required functionality, and will have better PPA metrics than the one-hot solution.
