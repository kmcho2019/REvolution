To further optimize the design for better PPA metrics, particularly focusing on timing (WNS) and power:

1. **State Encoding Optimization:** Switch to one-hot encoding (6 bits for 6 states) which typically improves timing by simplifying the state decoding logic, though at a small area cost. This is justified given our current negative WNS.

2. **Output Logic Optimization:** Maintain the simple output logic but ensure it's synthesized efficiently with the new encoding.

3. **Timing Improvement:** One-hot encoding reduces fan-out on state bits and simplifies the next-state logic, which should help with timing closure.

4. **Power Consideration:** Despite increased state register bits, the simpler decoding logic may offset power consumption. We'll also add clock gating for stable states (E and F).

5. **Reset Handling:** Maintain synchronous reset for consistency.
