Based on the PPA metrics and feedback, here's my optimization strategy:

1. **State Encoding Optimization**:
   - Switch to one-hot encoding (6 states = 6 bits) despite slightly larger area because:
     * Eliminates decoding logic for output (z)
     * Simplifies state transition logic (each bit directly represents a state)
     * Better timing due to simpler next-state logic

2. **Timing Improvement**:
   - Break down complex state transitions into simpler parallel conditions
   - Use priority-encoded case statements for better synthesis optimization
   - Remove unique case directive since one-hot ensures mutually exclusive states

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - Maintain the direct output assignment from state bits
   - One-hot encoding reduces switching activity (only one bit changes per transition)

4. **Area Trade-off**:
   - Accept modest area increase (6 bits vs 3) for better timing and power
   - Simplified combinational logic compensates for larger state register

5. **Reset Strategy**:
   - Keep synchronous reset for consistency
   - Initialize to state A (000001) with all other bits low
