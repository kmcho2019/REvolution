Here's a novel approach using individual flip-flop instantiations with explicit clock gating:

1. Instead of using a single always block, we'll instantiate 8 separate D flip-flop primitives
2. Each flip-flop will have its own enable signal derived from the clock
3. The output will be a concatenation of all individual flip-flop outputs
4. This demonstrates a more structural approach to building the register
5. The design maintains positive edge triggering and parallel storage of all bits
