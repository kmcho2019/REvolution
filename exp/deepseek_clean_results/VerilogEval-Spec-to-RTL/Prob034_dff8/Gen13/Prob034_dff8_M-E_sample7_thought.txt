Here's a novel approach using individual flip-flop instantiations with explicit clock-to-q delays:

1. Instead of a single always block, instantiate 8 separate DFF primitives
2. Each DFF has a small delay (1ns) from clock to output to model real hardware behavior
3. Use gate-level modeling with explicit timing control
4. This provides:
   - More realistic timing behavior
   - Better visibility into individual flip-flop operations
   - Explicit modeling of clock-to-q delays
   - Clear separation of each bit's storage element
5. The delay helps avoid potential simulation race conditions
