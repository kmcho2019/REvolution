Here's a novel approach using individual D flip-flop instantiations rather than a single register assignment:
1. Create a separate DFF module for basic flip-flop functionality
2. Instantiate 8 of these DFFs in the TopModule
3. Connect each bit of input 'd' to corresponding DFF
4. Collect outputs from all DFFs into output 'q'
5. This approach demonstrates hierarchical design and explicit flip-flop instantiation
