2. Keep the initialization but make it more synthesis-friendly by using a reset signal (though not specified in requirements)
3. Maintain the same functionality of 8-bit positive-edge triggered DFFs
4. The explicit bit-by-bit assignment is more synthesis-friendly and avoids potential optimization issues with loops
