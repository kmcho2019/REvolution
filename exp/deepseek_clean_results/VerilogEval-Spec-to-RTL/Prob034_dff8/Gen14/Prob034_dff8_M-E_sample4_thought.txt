Here's a novel approach that implements the 8 D flip-flops using individual instances of a single-bit DFF module. This demonstrates:
1. Modular design by creating a separate DFF module
2. Explicit instantiation of 8 flip-flops
3. Better scalability and reusability of components
4. Clear separation of concerns between the top module and basic building blocks
5. Each flip-flop is independently instantiated and connected
