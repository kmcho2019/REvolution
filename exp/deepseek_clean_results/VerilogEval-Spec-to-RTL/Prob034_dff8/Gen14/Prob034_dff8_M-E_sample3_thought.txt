To create a novel solution while meeting the requirements, I propose a shift register approach with parallel loading capability. Here's the key innovation:
1. Instead of simple parallel DFFs, implement a configurable shift register
2. Normally operates as parallel-in parallel-out (PIPO) registers
3. Includes a mode where data can be serially shifted (though unused in this case)
4. Uses a more structured hierarchical design with individual DFF components
5. Provides better scalability and potential for future expansion

This approach maintains the required functionality while offering more flexibility in the architecture. The hierarchical design makes it clearer and more maintainable.
