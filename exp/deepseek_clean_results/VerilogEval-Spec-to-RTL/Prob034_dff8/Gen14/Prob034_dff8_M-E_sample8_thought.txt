Here's a novel approach using individual D flip-flop instantiations rather than a single always block:
1. Create a separate D flip-flop module (DFF) with standard input/output
2. Instantiate 8 of these DFFs in the TopModule
3. Connect each bit of input 'd' to corresponding DFF
4. Collect outputs from all DFFs into 'q'
5. This structural approach explicitly shows the 8 flip-flops
6. No reset needed as per requirements
7. Each DFF is positive-edge triggered
