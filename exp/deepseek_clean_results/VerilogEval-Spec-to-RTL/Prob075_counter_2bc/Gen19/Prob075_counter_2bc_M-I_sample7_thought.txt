To optimize this design, I'll focus on improving timing (wns) while maintaining or reducing power and area. The key improvements will be:

1. Flatten the nested conditionals into a case statement for better synthesis optimization
2. Pre-calculate both increment and decrement paths in parallel to reduce critical path
3. Add an enable condition to reduce unnecessary switching activity
4. Use direct state transitions for better timing predictability

The new approach will:
- Reduce logic depth by eliminating nested conditionals
- Improve timing by parallel path calculation
- Reduce power by gating unnecessary computations
- Potentially reduce area through more efficient synthesis
