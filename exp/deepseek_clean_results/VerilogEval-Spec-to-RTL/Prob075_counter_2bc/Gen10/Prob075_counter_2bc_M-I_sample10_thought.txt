   - Use simple addition/subtraction with min/max bounds for state transitions
   - Eliminate the combinational next_state block by moving logic directly to sequential block

- Arithmetic approach with saturation bounds is more direct and typically synthesizes better
- Single always block reduces hierarchy and potential synthesis overhead
- Maintain same reset behavior and power characteristics
- Simplified logic structure should improve both timing and area
