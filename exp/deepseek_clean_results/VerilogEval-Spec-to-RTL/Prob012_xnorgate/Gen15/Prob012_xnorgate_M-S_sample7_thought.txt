The simplest and most efficient implementation of an XNOR gate is to use the built-in XNOR operator (^~). This approach:
1. Uses a single gate operation
2. Has minimal propagation delay (single gate level)
3. Results in the smallest possible area
4. Consumes least power
5. Is the most readable and maintainable implementation
6. Leverages any technology-specific optimizations for XNOR gates in the target library
