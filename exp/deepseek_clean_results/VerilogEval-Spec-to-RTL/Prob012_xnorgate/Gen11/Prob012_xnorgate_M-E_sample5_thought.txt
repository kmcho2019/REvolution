Novel Time-Domain XNOR Gate:
1. **Concept**: Implement XNOR using propagation delay differences. The idea is that when inputs match, their delayed versions will align to produce a high output, while mismatched inputs will cause cancellation.

2. **Implementation**:
   - Use two delay lines (one for each input) with carefully matched delays
   - The delayed signals are ANDed with the original signals
   - A final OR gate combines the cases where both inputs stayed the same
   - The delay is set to be longer than the signal transition time but shorter than the clock period

3. **Advantages**:
   - Novel approach that doesn't use traditional logic gates
   - Demonstrates alternative implementation styles
   - Could have different power/EM characteristics than standard implementations

4. **Considerations**:
   - Requires careful delay matching
   - Sensitive to PVT variations
   - More of a conceptual demonstration than practical implementation
