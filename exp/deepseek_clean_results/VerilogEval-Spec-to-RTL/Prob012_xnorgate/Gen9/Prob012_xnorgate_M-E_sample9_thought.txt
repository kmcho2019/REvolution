Novel Architectural Idea:
Implement the XNOR gate using a transmission gate approach combined with complementary logic. This method:
1. Uses pass transistors to selectively route signals based on input combinations
2. Employs complementary pull-up and pull-down networks
3. Demonstrates a switch-level implementation that could be more efficient in custom CMOS designs
4. Shows how basic logic can be implemented at the transistor level for potential power/area savings

This approach provides insight into how logic gates are actually implemented in transistor-level designs, offering potential advantages in certain low-power or custom IC scenarios.
