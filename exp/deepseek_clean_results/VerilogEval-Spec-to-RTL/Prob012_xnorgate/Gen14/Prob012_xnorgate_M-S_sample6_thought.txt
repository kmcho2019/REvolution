Simplification Approach:
1. **Direct Implementation**: Use the built-in XNOR operator (~^) for the most straightforward and efficient implementation.
2. **Remove Intermediate Signals**: Eliminate unnecessary intermediate wires to reduce complexity and improve readability.
3. **Minimalist Design**: Implement the functionality with a single continuous assignment for optimal simplicity and efficiency.
4. **Maintain Functionality**: Ensure the simplified version produces exactly the same output as the original.

This simplification reduces the gate count to just one operation while maintaining identical functionality, resulting in better area and potentially power efficiency.
