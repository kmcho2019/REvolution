An alternative approach to implement an XNOR gate is to use its logical equivalence to an OR of two AND gates with inverted inputs. Specifically, XNOR can be expressed as (a AND b) OR (~a AND ~b). This implementation uses basic logic gates and demonstrates how XNOR can be built from first principles. While this approach uses more gates than the direct ~^ operator, it provides insight into the underlying logic and could be useful in educational contexts or when gate-level control is needed.
