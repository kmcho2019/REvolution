Fusion Strategy:
1. Maintain the simplest and most efficient implementation - direct continuous assignment of '1'
2. Use explicit 1-bit width specification (1'b1) for clarity and consistency
3. Remove all unnecessary constructs to achieve minimal implementation
4. Ensure the solution is:
   - Most area-efficient (single assignment)
   - Lowest power (constant driver)
   - Best timing (direct connection)
   - Most readable (clear intent)

- It uses the minimal Verilog constructs needed
- Has zero timing paths (direct connection)
- Consumes minimal power (constant output)
- Takes minimal area (single assignment)
