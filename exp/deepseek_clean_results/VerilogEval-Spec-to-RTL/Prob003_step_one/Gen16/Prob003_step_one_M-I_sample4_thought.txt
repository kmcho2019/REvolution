The given solution is already optimal in all PPA metrics (Power, Performance, Area) for this trivial case. Here's why no improvement is possible:

1. Power: The design has zero dynamic power (no switching) and minimal static power (just driving a constant '1')

2. Performance: There are no timing paths (wns/tns = 0) since it's a direct constant assignment

3. Area: The implementation uses the absolute minimum possible area (1.0 um²) - just a single wire connection to VDD

Any attempt to "optimize" would actually worsen PPA by:
- Adding unnecessary complexity (registers, parameters)
- Making it technology-dependent (pull-ups)
- Increasing area/power without benefit

The only theoretical improvement would be to eliminate the module entirely and connect directly to VDD at a higher level, but that's a system architecture decision, not a Verilog optimization.

Therefore, the original solution remains the most optimal implementation possible.
