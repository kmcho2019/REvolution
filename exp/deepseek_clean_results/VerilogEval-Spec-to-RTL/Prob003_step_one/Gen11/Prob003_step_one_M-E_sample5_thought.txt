Here's a novel approach using a ring oscillator concept to generate the constant '1' output. While this is clearly over-engineered for the simple requirement, it demonstrates an alternative architectural approach:

1. Create a minimal ring oscillator (3 inverters in loop)
2. Sample the oscillator output with a D-flipflop
3. Initialize the flipflop to 1
4. The oscillator will keep the output stable at 1 since it's initialized and never toggled

This approach is intentionally complex to showcase a different methodology, though it's not practical for this simple requirement. The key insight is that an initialized flipflop fed by an untriggered oscillator will maintain its initial state.
