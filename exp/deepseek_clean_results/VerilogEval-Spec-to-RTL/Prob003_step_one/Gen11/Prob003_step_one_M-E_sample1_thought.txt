Here's a novel approach using a ring oscillator to generate the constant '1' output. While this is significantly more complex than needed, it demonstrates an alternative way to produce a constant high signal:

1. Create a 3-stage ring oscillator using inverters (this would normally oscillate)
2. Sample the oscillation using a D-flipflop clocked by the oscillator itself
3. The metastable state will eventually resolve to a stable '1' due to the positive feedback
4. Use this stable output to drive the 'one' signal

Note: This is intentionally over-engineered to demonstrate an alternative approach, though in practice the simple assign statement is vastly superior.
