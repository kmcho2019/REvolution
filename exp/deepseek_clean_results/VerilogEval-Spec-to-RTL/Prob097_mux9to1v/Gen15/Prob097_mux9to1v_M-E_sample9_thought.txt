I'll implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure using a tree of 2-to-1 muxes. This approach:

1. Divides the 9 inputs into groups that are progressively selected
2. First level selects between groups of inputs
3. Second level makes the final selection within the chosen group
4. Uses a priority encoder to handle the unused cases (sel > 8)
5. Benefits:
   - Potentially better timing due to balanced tree structure
   - Clear hierarchy that may be easier to optimize during synthesis
   - Explicit handling of invalid cases through priority encoding
   - Modular design that could be parameterized for different input sizes
