Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure to optimize both area and power:

1. **Two-Level Mux Structure**: Instead of a single large 9:1 mux, we'll use two levels of smaller muxes (3:1 followed by 3:1) to reduce the total number of large transistors needed.

2. **Binary Grouping**: Group inputs in powers of 2 (though not perfectly balanced here) to minimize selection logic complexity.

3. **Early Termination**: Use the MSB of sel to quickly detect invalid cases (sel >= 9) and bypass the mux logic entirely for power savings.

4. **Output Gating**: Gate the final output with the valid selection signal to prevent unnecessary toggling when sel is invalid.

This approach should provide better area efficiency while maintaining good timing characteristics.
