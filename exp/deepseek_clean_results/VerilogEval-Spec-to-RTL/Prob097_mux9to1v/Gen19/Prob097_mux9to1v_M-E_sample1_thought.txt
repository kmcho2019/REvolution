Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with bitwise selection:

1. **Hierarchical Muxing**: Instead of a flat 9:1 mux, implement two levels of smaller muxes (3:1 and 4:1) to reduce fanout and improve timing.

2. **Bitwise Selection**: Process each bit independently to allow for potential bit-level optimizations in synthesis.

3. **Early Default Handling**: Use the MSB of sel to quickly detect invalid cases (sel >= 9) and set outputs to '1' without going through the full mux logic.

4. **Parallel Processing**: Maintain full 16-bit parallelism while benefiting from the hierarchical structure.

5. **Power Optimization**: The hierarchical structure naturally reduces switching activity compared to a single large mux.

Key advantages:
- Better timing due to reduced fanout
- Lower power from hierarchical structure
- Clean separation of valid/invalid cases
- Potential for bit-level optimizations
