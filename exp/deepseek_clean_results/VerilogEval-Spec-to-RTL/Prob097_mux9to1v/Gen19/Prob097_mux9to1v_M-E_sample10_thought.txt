1. **Hierarchical Selection**: Instead of a single large 9:1 mux, implement two levels of smaller muxes (3:1 and 3:1) followed by a final 2:1 mux. This reduces fan-in and improves timing.

2. **Pre-decoding**: Use the MSB of sel to split the selection space into two groups (0-7 and 8-15), then use the remaining bits for the actual mux selection. This simplifies the control logic.

3. **Default Handling**: Implement the 'all 1s' default using a simple OR gate that activates when sel[3] is high (cases 8-15), combined with the actual mux output.

4. **Parallel Processing**: Maintain full 16-bit parallel processing while benefiting from the hierarchical structure's better timing characteristics.

This approach offers better timing at high frequencies and more balanced logic distribution compared to a flat 9:1 mux implementation.
