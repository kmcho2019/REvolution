Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with priority encoding:

1. **Hierarchical Muxing**: Instead of a flat 9-to-1 mux, implement a tree of smaller muxes (3 levels of 3-to-1 muxes). This reduces fan-in and improves timing.

2. **Priority Encoding**: Use the upper bits of 'sel' to determine which level of the hierarchy to activate, reducing the number of active muxes at any time.

3. **Output Gating**: For invalid selections (9-15), gate the output directly to '1's without propagating through the mux hierarchy, saving power.

4. **Bit-sliced Processing**: Process all 16 bits in parallel but share the control logic between them for area efficiency.

This approach should provide better timing due to reduced fan-in at each stage, lower power due to hierarchical activation, and reasonable area due to shared control logic.
