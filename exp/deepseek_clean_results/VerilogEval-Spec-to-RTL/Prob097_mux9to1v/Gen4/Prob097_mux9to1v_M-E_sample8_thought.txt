My novel approach leverages a hierarchical multiplexer structure to optimize both area and timing. Instead of a flat 9-to-1 mux or parallel 1-bit muxes, I'll implement a two-level hierarchy:

1. First level: Divide the 9 inputs into 3 groups of 3 inputs each (a-c, d-f, g-i)
2. Second level: Use the MSB of sel to choose between these 3 groups
3. Final selection: Use the LSBs of sel to choose within each group

This reduces the fan-in at each stage, improving timing. For the default case (sel >= 9), we'll use a simple comparator to detect invalid selects and force the output to all 1's. This approach should:
- Reduce critical path by breaking the large mux into smaller ones
- Minimize area through hierarchical sharing of selection logic
- Maintain clean code structure for better maintainability
