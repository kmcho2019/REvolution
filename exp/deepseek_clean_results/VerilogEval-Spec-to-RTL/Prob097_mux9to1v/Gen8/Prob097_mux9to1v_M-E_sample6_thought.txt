Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical tree of smaller multiplexers to improve timing while maintaining readability:

1. First stage: Use three 3:1 muxes to reduce the 9 inputs to 3 intermediate signals
2. Second stage: Use a final 3:1 mux to select from the intermediate signals
3. Special handling for sel > 8 using a simple OR gate to force outputs high
4. This structure provides better timing characteristics by balancing the critical path
5. The design naturally handles the default case by gating the final stage

The key innovation is using a balanced tree structure with 3:1 muxes (instead of more common 2:1) which perfectly fits our 9-input case. This reduces the maximum fan-in and creates a more balanced delay path.
