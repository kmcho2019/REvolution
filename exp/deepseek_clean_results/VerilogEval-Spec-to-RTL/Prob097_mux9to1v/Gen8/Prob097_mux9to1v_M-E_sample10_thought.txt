Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure:
1. First stage: Use two 4-to-1 multiplexers to reduce 9 inputs to 2 intermediate signals
2. Second stage: Use a final 2-to-1 multiplexer to select between the intermediate signals
3. Special handling for sel values 9-15 by OR'ing the selection logic with a range detection
4. This approach may offer better timing characteristics in some technologies by breaking the large mux into smaller ones
5. The range detection for sel 9-15 is done once and used to force the output to all 1's
