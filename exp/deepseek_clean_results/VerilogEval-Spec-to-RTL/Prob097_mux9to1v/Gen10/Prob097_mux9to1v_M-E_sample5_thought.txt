Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with bitwise selection:

1. **Bitwise Parallel Processing**: Instead of selecting the entire 16-bit word at once, we'll process each bit position independently in parallel. This enables better optimization for area and power.

2. **Two-Level Selection**:
   - First level: Use the lower 3 bits of sel (sel[2:0]) to choose between 8 inputs (a-h)
   - Second level: Use sel[3] to choose between the first level output or input i
   - This reduces the fan-in of each multiplexer stage

3. **Invalid Selection Handling**: For sel values 9-15, we'll use a simple OR reduction of sel[3:0] to detect invalid cases and force outputs high.

4. **Power Optimization**: The hierarchical structure minimizes switching activity by only activating the necessary portions of the mux tree.

This approach should provide better area and power efficiency while maintaining good timing characteristics.
