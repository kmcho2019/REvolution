To optimize the design for better Power and Area (PPA) metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Hierarchical Mux Structure**: Break down the 9-to-1 mux into two levels of smaller muxes (4-to-1 and 2-to-1) to reduce switching activity and area. This creates a more balanced tree structure.

2. **Bit-sliced Implementation**: Process each bit separately to potentially allow better optimization by the synthesis tool.

3. **Efficient Default Handling**: Implement the default case (all 1's) using a simple OR gate structure at the output stage rather than routing through the mux.

4. **Reduced Fanout**: The hierarchical approach reduces fanout on the selection signals compared to a single large mux.

This approach should reduce both dynamic power (from reduced switching) and area (from more efficient mux structures), while maintaining the same functionality and timing characteristics.
