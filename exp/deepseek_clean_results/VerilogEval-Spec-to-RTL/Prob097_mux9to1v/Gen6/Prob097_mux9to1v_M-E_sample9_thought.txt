Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure:
1. First stage: Use three 3-to-1 multiplexers to reduce the 9 inputs to 3 intermediate signals
2. Second stage: Use a final 3-to-1 multiplexer to select between the intermediate signals
3. The selection logic is split between the stages to efficiently handle the 4-bit sel input
4. Default case (sel > 8) is handled by a simple comparator that overrides the output
5. This approach potentially reduces critical path and improves area efficiency by breaking down the large 9-to-1 mux into smaller components
