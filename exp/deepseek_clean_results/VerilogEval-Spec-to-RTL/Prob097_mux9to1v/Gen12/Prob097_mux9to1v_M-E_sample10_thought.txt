Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with binary tree decomposition:

1. **Hierarchical Mux Structure**: Instead of a flat 9-to-1 mux, we'll implement it as a tree of smaller muxes. First level will have three 3-to-1 muxes, and the second level will be a 3-to-1 mux selecting between the first level outputs.

2. **Efficient Encoding**: Use the lower 3 bits of sel for first level muxes and the 4th bit for final selection. This naturally handles the unused cases (sel >= 9) by setting the final mux to '1's when the MSB is set.

3. **Resource Sharing**: The 3-to-1 mux structure allows sharing of common selection logic across all bits.

4. **Power Optimization**: The hierarchical structure reduces switching activity as only parts of the tree are activated based on the selection.

This approach should provide better timing due to the balanced tree structure and potentially lower power due to reduced switching activity in unused branches.
