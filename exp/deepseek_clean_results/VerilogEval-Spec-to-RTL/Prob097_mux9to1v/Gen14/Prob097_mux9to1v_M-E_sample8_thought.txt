Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure with bitwise operations:

1. **Hierarchical Selection**:
   - Break the 9-to-1 mux into two levels: first 3-to-1 muxes, then a final 3-to-1 mux
   - Use the two LSBs of sel for the first level, MSB for the final selection

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - Gate the inputs to the second level mux when sel is invalid
   - This prevents unnecessary toggling in the second level when sel is invalid

3. **Area Efficiency**:
   - The hierarchical structure reduces the total number of large multiplexers
   - Bitwise operations allow parallel processing of all 16 bits

4. **Default Handling**:
   - Use a single OR reduction of sel[3:2] to detect invalid cases
   - This is more efficient than comparing to 9

The implementation will have better fanout characteristics and potentially better timing for deep submicron processes.
