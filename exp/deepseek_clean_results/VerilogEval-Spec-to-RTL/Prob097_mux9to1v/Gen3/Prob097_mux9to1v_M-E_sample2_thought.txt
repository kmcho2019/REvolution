Instead of using a single large 9-to-1 multiplexer, we can implement a two-level hierarchical structure. First level: three 3-to-1 muxes that each select among three inputs. Second level: a 3-to-1 mux that selects among the outputs of the first level muxes. This reduces fan-in and may improve timing. The unused cases are handled by a separate condition that checks if sel > 8.
