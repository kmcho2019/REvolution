Instead of a single large case statement, we'll implement a two-level hierarchical multiplexer. First level selects between groups of inputs (a-d, e-h, i+default), and second level selects between these groups. This reduces fan-in at each stage and may offer better timing characteristics. The default case (sel=9-15) is handled by forcing the final output to all 1's when sel[3] is high (values 8-15) and sel[2] is high (values 4-15) simultaneously.
