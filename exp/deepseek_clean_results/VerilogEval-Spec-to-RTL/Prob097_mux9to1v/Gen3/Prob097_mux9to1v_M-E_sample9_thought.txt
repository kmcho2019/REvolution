Instead of using a traditional case statement, we'll implement the multiplexer using a bitwise OR operation of masked inputs. Each input will be ANDed with a 16-bit mask that's either all 1's (selected) or all 0's (not selected). The masks are generated by decoding the select signal. This approach leverages parallel bitwise operations and may offer better optimization opportunities for synthesis tools. For unused cases (sel=9-15), we'll generate a constant 16'hFFFF by OR'ing with a default mask.
