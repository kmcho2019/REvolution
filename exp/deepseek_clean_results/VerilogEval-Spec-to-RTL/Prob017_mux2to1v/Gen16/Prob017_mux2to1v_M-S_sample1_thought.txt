The simplest and most efficient implementation for a 2:1 multiplexer in Verilog is to use a single continuous assignment with a ternary operator. This approach:
1. Directly expresses the mux functionality in one line
2. Is easily understandable
3. Results in optimal synthesis (same as case statements for modern tools)
4. Has minimal gate count (just 100 2:1 muxes)
5. Maintains all functionality while being maximally concise
