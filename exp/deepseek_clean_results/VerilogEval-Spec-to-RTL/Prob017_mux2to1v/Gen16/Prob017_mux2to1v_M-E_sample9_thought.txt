Instead of a flat or segmented implementation, I'll design a binary tree of 2:1 muxes that:
1. Recursively divides the 100-bit input into smaller chunks
2. Uses a balanced tree structure to minimize critical path
3. Naturally scales to any bus width (not just 100 bits)
4. Provides better timing characteristics for large buses
5. Enables potential power gating at different tree levels

The tree will have:
- Leaf nodes handling 1-bit muxes
- Intermediate nodes combining results from lower levels
- The sel signal propagates through all levels
- Total depth of log2(100) ≈ 7 levels

This approach offers better timing scalability for very wide buses while maintaining reasonable area overhead.
