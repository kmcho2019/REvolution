Instead of a flat 100-bit mux, we'll implement a hierarchical structure that:
1. Divides the 100-bit input into 10 groups of 10 bits each
2. Uses a first-level mux to generate group enable signals
3. Each group has its own 10-bit mux that only activates when its enable is high
4. This reduces switching activity since only the selected group's muxes are active
5. The approach saves power by minimizing unnecessary bit transitions while maintaining performance
