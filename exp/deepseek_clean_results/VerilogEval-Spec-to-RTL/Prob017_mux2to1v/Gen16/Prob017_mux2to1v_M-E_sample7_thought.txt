Instead of a single 100-bit mux, we'll implement 25 smaller 4-bit muxes with individual enable signals. This approach:
1. Reduces switching activity by only enabling mux slices when their inputs change
2. Allows for potential clock gating at the 4-bit level
3. Provides better power granularity - unused slices can be completely disabled
4. Maintains similar timing characteristics since the critical path remains a single mux level
5. Enables potential area savings through shared control logic
