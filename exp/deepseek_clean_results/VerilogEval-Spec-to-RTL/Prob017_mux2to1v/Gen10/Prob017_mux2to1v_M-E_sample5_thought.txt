Innovative hierarchical bit-slice multiplexer design:
1. Divide the 100-bit mux into 10 slices of 10 bits each
2. Each slice has its own enable signal derived from the global sel
3. This enables potential power savings by:
   - Only activating slices when needed (if we add clock gating later)
   - Allowing for partial updates if needed in future extensions
4. The design maintains the same functionality but provides:
   - Better modularity for future modifications
   - Potential power optimization points
   - Clear hierarchical structure for verification
5. Uses generate blocks for scalable implementation
