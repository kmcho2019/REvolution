Instead of a flat 100-bit mux, we'll implement a hierarchical structure:
1. Divide the 100 bits into 10 slices of 10 bits each
2. Use a common enable signal derived from 'sel'
3. Each slice has its own 2:1 mux with shared control
4. The enables are generated once and distributed
5. Benefits:
   - Potentially better physical implementation (localized routing)
   - Enables power gating at slice level if needed
   - More scalable for very wide buses
   - Clear hierarchy for timing analysis
