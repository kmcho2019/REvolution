To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose the following improvements:

1. Simplified Structure:
   - Replace the generate loop with a single continuous assignment for the entire 100-bit bus
   - This reduces potential routing complexity and allows synthesis tools more optimization freedom

2. Power Optimization:
   - The single assignment means the mux logic is more centralized, potentially reducing switching power
   - The synthesis tool can now optimize the entire mux as one unit rather than 10 separate slices

3. Area Optimization:
   - Eliminates the overhead of the generate loop structure
   - Allows the synthesis tool to potentially use wider mux primitives if available in the target library

4. Timing Consideration:
   - The critical path remains the same (sel to out)
   - The simplified structure may reduce routing delays
   - Still maintains parallel processing of all bits
