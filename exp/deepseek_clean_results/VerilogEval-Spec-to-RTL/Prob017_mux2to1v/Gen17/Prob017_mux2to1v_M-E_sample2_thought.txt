Novel Architectural Idea: Bitwise Parallel Processing with Conditional Masking
1. Instead of using a traditional multiplexer structure, implement a parallel bitwise masking approach
2. Generate two 100-bit masks based on the select signal:
   - One mask is all 1s when sel=0, all 0s otherwise
   - The other mask is all 1s when sel=1, all 0s otherwise
3. Perform bitwise AND operations between inputs and their respective masks
4. Combine the results using bitwise OR to produce the output
5. This approach may offer better optimization opportunities for certain synthesis tools and technologies
6. The implementation remains purely combinational while providing an alternative structure
