To optimize the 100-bit 2:1 multiplexer for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I propose the following improvements:

1. Hierarchical Multiplexing:
   - Break the 100-bit mux into smaller chunks (e.g., 25-bit slices)
   - This reduces fanout and improves timing by localizing the select signal distribution
   - Enables better placement and routing

2. Select Signal Buffering:
   - Buffer the select signal to drive all slices equally
   - Prevents signal degradation across large fanout

3. Power Optimization:
   - The hierarchical approach reduces switching capacitance
   - Smaller slices have lower dynamic power consumption

4. Area Optimization:
   - Standard cells can be better utilized with smaller mux slices
   - Potential for better placement density

This approach maintains functionality while improving timing, power, and potentially area.
