Here's a novel approach using bitwise operations instead of traditional conditional selection:
1. Use bitwise AND and OR operations to implement the mux functionality
2. For each bit position, compute: (a & ~sel_mask) | (b & sel_mask)
3. The sel_mask is created by replicating the sel bit 100 times
4. This approach eliminates branching and uses pure combinational logic
5. May have better synthesis results in some technologies due to uniform bitwise operations
