The simplest and most efficient way to implement a 2:1 multiplexer in Verilog is to use a conditional operator (?:). This approach directly expresses the multiplexer functionality without any intermediate operations or mask generation. The synthesis tool can easily map this to optimal hardware MUX primitives, resulting in better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) characteristics. The conditional operator clearly shows the selection between 'a' and 'b' based on 'sel' in a single line of code.
