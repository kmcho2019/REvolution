Instead of implementing 100 individual 2:1 muxes, we can:
1. Divide the 100-bit input into smaller groups (e.g., 10 groups of 10 bits)
2. Use a hierarchical mux structure where:
   - First level selects between groups using a single sel signal
   - Second level selects within each group
3. This approach may offer:
   - Better power efficiency by reducing sel signal fanout
   - Potential area savings through shared control logic
   - More balanced timing paths
4. The grouping factor (10 bits) can be parameterized for flexibility
