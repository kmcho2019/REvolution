The original implementation is already quite optimal, but we can explore a different structural approach using bitwise operations. This alternative implementation:

1. Uses bitwise AND and OR operations to implement the mux functionality
2. Still maintains purely combinational logic
3. May offer different optimization opportunities during synthesis
4. Provides the same functionality (sel=0 chooses a, sel=1 chooses b)
5. Could potentially lead to different PPA tradeoffs in some technology libraries

The formula used is: out = (~sel & a) | (sel & b)
This is mathematically equivalent to the ternary operator but expressed differently.
