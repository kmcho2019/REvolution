Instead of using traditional multiplexers, we can:
1. Exploit the mathematical property that a 2:1 mux can be implemented as:
   out = (a & ~sel) | (b & sel)
2. Further optimize by recognizing this is equivalent to:
   out = a ^ ((a ^ b) & sel)
3. This allows us to:
   - Precompute (a ^ b) once for all bits
   - Only need one AND operation per bit with sel
   - Then XOR with original a
4. Benefits:
   - Potentially fewer gates than traditional mux implementation
   - More balanced timing paths since XOR/AND operations have similar delays
   - Better technology mapping as XOR/AND are primitive cells in most libraries
5. The implementation uses bitwise operations across the entire 100-bit width
