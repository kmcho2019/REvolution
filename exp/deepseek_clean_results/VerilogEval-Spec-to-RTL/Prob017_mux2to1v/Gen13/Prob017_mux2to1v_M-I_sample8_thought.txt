To optimize the PPA metrics further, I propose the following improvements:
1. Remove the intermediate wire array to reduce area overhead (synthesis tools can handle direct assignment)
2. Use a single continuous assignment for the entire 100-bit bus to potentially improve synthesis optimization
3. Maintain the same functionality while simplifying the structure
4. This approach should:
   - Reduce area by eliminating unnecessary intermediate wires
   - Maintain or improve timing by keeping the parallel mux structure
   - Potentially reduce power slightly by minimizing wiring

The key insight is that modern synthesis tools are very good at optimizing simple, direct assignments, and the segmented approach might actually introduce unnecessary overhead.
