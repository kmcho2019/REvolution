An alternative approach is to implement the multiplexer using a bitwise AND-OR structure. This leverages the property that:
out = (a & ~sel_mask) | (b & sel_mask)
where sel_mask is a 100-bit vector with all bits equal to sel. This approach explicitly shows the logical operations behind multiplexing and might offer better optimization opportunities for certain synthesis tools. The sel_mask can be created by replicating the sel bit 100 times.
