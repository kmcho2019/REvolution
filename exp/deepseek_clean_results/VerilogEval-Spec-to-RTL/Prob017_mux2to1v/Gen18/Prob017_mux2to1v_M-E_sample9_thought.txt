Novel Architectural Idea: Bitwise Mask-Based Multiplexer with Parallel Processing

Instead of using a traditional ternary operator or case statement, we can implement the multiplexer using a bitwise masking approach that:
1. Creates two 100-bit masks based on the select signal
2. Uses parallel AND operations to apply these masks to inputs a and b
3. Combines the results with an OR operation

Key Advantages:
- Explicitly shows the bitwise operations happening in parallel
- May offer better optimization opportunities for certain synthesis tools
- Clearly demonstrates the masking concept which can be useful for educational purposes
- Potentially better for power optimization as it reduces unnecessary switching
- The parallel nature of operations might be more intuitive for hardware implementation
