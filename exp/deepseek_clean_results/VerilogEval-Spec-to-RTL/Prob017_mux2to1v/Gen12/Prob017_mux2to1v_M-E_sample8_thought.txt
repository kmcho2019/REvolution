Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure:
1. Divide the 100-bit input into 10 segments of 10 bits each
2. Use a first-level mux to select between corresponding segments of a and b
3. Then use a second-level mux to combine all segments
4. Benefits:
   - Potentially better timing due to hierarchical selection
   - More scalable for very wide buses
   - Allows for partial selection if needed in future extensions
   - Clear separation of selection logic
