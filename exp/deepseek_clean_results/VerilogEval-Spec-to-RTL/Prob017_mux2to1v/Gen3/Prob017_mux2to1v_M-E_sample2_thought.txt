Instead of a single 100-bit wide mux, implement a hierarchical structure with smaller 10-bit muxes. This approach:
1. Divides the 100-bit inputs into 10 groups of 10 bits each
2. Uses smaller 10-bit muxes for each group
3. Shares the common select signal
This reduces fanout on the select signal and may improve timing in some technologies by breaking the large mux into smaller, more manageable chunks. The functionality remains identical to a flat implementation.
