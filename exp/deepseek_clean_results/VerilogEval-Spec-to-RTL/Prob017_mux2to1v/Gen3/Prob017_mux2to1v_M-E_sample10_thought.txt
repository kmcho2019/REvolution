Instead of a flat 100-bit mux, we can implement a hierarchical approach that divides the 100-bit input into smaller segments (e.g., 20 segments of 5 bits each). Each segment has its own mux, and we use a common select signal. This approach:
1. May offer better physical implementation characteristics
2. Could potentially reduce fan-out on the select signal
3. Makes the design more modular and scalable
4. Could enable power gating of unused segments in future extensions
