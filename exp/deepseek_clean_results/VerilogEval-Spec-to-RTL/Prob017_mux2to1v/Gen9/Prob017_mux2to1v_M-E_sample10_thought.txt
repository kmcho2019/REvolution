This solution implements the 100-bit mux as 25 parallel 4-bit muxes, each implemented with transmission gates. The key innovations are:
1. Hierarchical decomposition for better physical implementation (easier placement/routing)
2. Transmission gate implementation for potentially lower power consumption
3. Bit-sliced organization that could enable partial power gating
4. The design could be more area-efficient in some technologies due to better transistor-level optimization
5. The hierarchical structure makes timing constraints easier to manage for large buses
