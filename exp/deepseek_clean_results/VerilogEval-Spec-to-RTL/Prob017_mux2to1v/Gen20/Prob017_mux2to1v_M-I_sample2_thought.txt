To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose the following improvements:

1. Simplified Structure:
   - Replace the sliced generate approach with a single continuous assignment
   - This reduces potential routing complexity from slice selection operators
   - Allows synthesis tools to better optimize the entire mux as one unit

2. Power Optimization:
   - The single assignment means no intermediate signals between slices
   - Reduces potential glitching power from multiple parallel muxes

3. Area Optimization:
   - Lets synthesis tools choose the most efficient implementation (either wide mux or bit-wise)
   - Removes generate overhead which might prevent some RTL optimizations

4. Timing Consideration:
   - Modern synthesis tools handle wide muxes efficiently
   - Single assignment may result in better routing and placement
   - Maintains the parallel selection advantage of the original design
