This implementation uses a hierarchical structure of 4-bit multiplexers to build the 100-bit multiplexer:
1. Divides the 100-bit inputs into 25 groups of 4 bits each
2. Uses 25 parallel 4-bit 2:1 muxes
3. The select signal is common to all sub-multiplexers
4. Benefits include:
   - Potentially better physical implementation (shorter wires)
   - More regular structure for place-and-route
   - Easier timing closure for very wide buses
   - Scalable to even wider buses
