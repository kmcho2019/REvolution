Instead of a flat 100-bit mux, implement a tree-structured hierarchical mux:
1. Divide the 100-bit inputs into 10 groups of 10 bits each
2. First level: 10 small 10-bit muxes (each handling one group)
3. Second level: Single select signal distribution to all muxes
4. Benefits:
   - Reduces fanout of the select signal (only drives 10 muxes instead of 100)
   - Better timing characteristics due to reduced capacitive load
   - More scalable for wider buses
   - Potential for better physical implementation (localized routing)
   - Easier to pipeline if needed
