To optimize the PPA metrics, I'll focus on the following improvements:

1. **Redundant Comparison Elimination**: Since a~^b is identical to b~^a, we can compute each unique comparison only once and reuse it. This reduces the number of XNOR operations from 20 to 10 (plus 5 self-comparisons).

2. **Intermediate Signal Storage**: Store all unique pairwise comparisons in wires before concatenation to potentially help synthesis tools optimize better.

3. **Constant Propagation**: Keep the self-comparisons as 1'b1 since they're always true.

4. **Structural Organization**: Group related comparisons to potentially help with placement and routing.

This approach reduces the number of logic operations while maintaining the same functionality, which should improve both power and area metrics.
