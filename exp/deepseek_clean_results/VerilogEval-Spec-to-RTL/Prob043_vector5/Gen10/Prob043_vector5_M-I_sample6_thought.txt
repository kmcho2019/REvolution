The current implementation is already optimal in terms of PPA metrics (0 timing violations, minimal power and area). However, we can make one minor improvement for better readability and maintainability while maintaining the same PPA:

1. Use explicit bit ordering (MSB to LSB) in the assignments for clearer visualization of the comparison matrix
2. Group the assignments by output bit ranges to make the 5x5 matrix structure more visually apparent
3. Maintain all the PPA advantages of the original solution (parallel XNOR operations, minimal gate count)

The improved version will be functionally identical but more readable, showing the symmetric nature of the comparisons more clearly. This is particularly helpful when verifying the correctness of all 25 comparisons.
