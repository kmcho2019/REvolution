Improvement strategy:
1. Remove redundant self-comparisons (a^a, b^b, etc.) by hardwiring them to 1'b1
2. Eliminate duplicate comparisons by recognizing that ~(x^y) == ~(y^x)
3. Reduce total number of XNOR operations from 25 to 10 unique comparisons
4. Use wire declarations for intermediate results to share common subexpressions
5. Maintain same output bit ordering for compatibility
6. This should reduce both area and power while maintaining zero timing violations
