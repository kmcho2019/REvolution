The original solution already implements the minimal required logic for this problem - 25 XNOR operations (implemented as ~x ^ y) arranged in a specific order. There's no meaningful way to simplify this further while maintaining the exact same functionality, as:

1. Each output bit requires its own independent comparison operation
2. The operations are already as simple as possible (single XNOR per bit)
3. The ordering is fixed by the specification

The only potential "simplification" would be to use XNOR operators directly instead of the equivalent ~x ^ y, but this is just syntactic sugar and doesn't change the underlying logic complexity.
