To optimize this design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Remove redundant comparisons**: Since (a==b) is the same as (b==a), we can eliminate half the comparisons by only computing unique pairs and reusing results.

2. **Use XNOR instead of ==**: XNOR is more direct for equality comparison in hardware and typically synthesizes more efficiently than the == operator.

3. **Group assignments logically**: Maintain clear grouping by operand while reducing total number of operations.

4. **Direct assignment for self-comparisons**: Keep the 1'b1 assignments for self-comparisons as they require no logic.

5. **Optimize bit ordering**: Arrange the output bits to group related comparisons together for potential synthesis optimization.

This approach reduces the number of unique comparisons from 25 to 15 (10 unique pairs + 5 self-comparisons), which should significantly reduce area and power while maintaining the same functionality.
