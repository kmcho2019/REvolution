To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Constant Optimization**: Replace self-comparisons (like a-a, b-b, etc.) with constant 1'b1 values since they always evaluate to 1. This reduces logic gates and power consumption.

2. **Symmetric Optimization**: For symmetric comparisons (like a-b and b-a), we'll keep both since they might be needed for the output ordering, but the synthesis tool might optimize them during technology mapping.

3. **Concise Implementation**: Maintain the single concatenation approach for readability while incorporating the above optimizations.

4. **Power Reduction**: By reducing the number of active logic gates (especially for the constant terms), we'll lower dynamic power consumption.

5. **Area Reduction**: The constant terms and reduced logic operations should result in a smaller area footprint.
