Here's a novel approach using a matrix-style comparison:
1. Create two 5-bit vectors by replicating each input 5 times
2. Perform an equality comparison between these two vectors in a structured way
3. Use a generate block to systematically create all 25 comparisons
4. The comparisons are ordered such that we first compare a with all inputs (a-e), then b with all inputs, etc.
5. This approach is more scalable and systematic than individual assignments
6. We'll use XOR-NOT (XNOR) operations for the comparisons as specified
