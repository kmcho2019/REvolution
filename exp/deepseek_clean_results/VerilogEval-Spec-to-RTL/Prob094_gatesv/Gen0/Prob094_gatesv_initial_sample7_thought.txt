To implement the TopModule, we need to process the 4-bit input vector to generate three different 4-bit output vectors based on specific neighbor relationships:

1. For out_both: Each bit (except the MSB) should be the AND of itself and its left neighbor. The MSB is irrelevant.
2. For out_any: Each bit (except the LSB) should be the OR of itself and its right neighbor. The LSB is irrelevant.
3. For out_different: Each bit should be the XOR of itself and its left neighbor, with wrap-around (so in[0] is compared with in[3]).

We'll implement these operations using bitwise operations and proper indexing.
