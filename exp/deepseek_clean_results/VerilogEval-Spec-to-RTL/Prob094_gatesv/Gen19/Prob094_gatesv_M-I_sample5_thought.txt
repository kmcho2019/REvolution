The current implementation is already quite efficient, but we can make a few minor optimizations to further improve PPA:

1. For out_both[3] and out_any[0], instead of assigning constant 0, we can leave them unconnected (high-impedance) since they're not required by the spec. This saves a small amount of power and area.

2. We can combine the left-shift and circular rotation operations for out_different into a single operation for slightly better timing.

3. Use more explicit bit ranges to make the intent clearer while maintaining the same functionality.

The improvements will:
- Reduce power slightly by eliminating unnecessary constant drivers
- Maintain the same timing (critical path remains the same)
- Potentially save a tiny amount of area
- Keep the same functionality where outputs are required
