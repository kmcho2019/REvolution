Novel Approach: LFSR-based Decade Counter
1. Instead of a conventional binary counter, use a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) configured to count through 10 distinct states
2. The LFSR will cycle through a pseudo-random sequence that covers exactly 10 states before repeating
3. Add a mapping function to convert the LFSR state to a binary count value (0-9)
4. Benefits:
   - Potentially lower power due to reduced switching activity
   - Can achieve the same functionality with different area/power tradeoffs
   - Interesting alternative to traditional binary counters

Implementation Details:
- Use a 4-bit LFSR with taps at positions [3,2] (polynomial x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + 1)
- The LFSR naturally cycles through 15 states, but we'll force it to reset after 10 counts
- Add a combinational mapping block to convert LFSR state to binary count
- Maintain synchronous reset functionality
