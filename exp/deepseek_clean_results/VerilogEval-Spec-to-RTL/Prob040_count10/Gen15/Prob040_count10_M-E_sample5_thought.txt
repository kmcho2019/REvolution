Novel Approach: Shift Register Counter with Feedback
1. Instead of a traditional binary counter, use a 10-bit shift register where only one bit is high at any time
2. The single '1' circulates through the register, effectively counting from 0-9
3. Feedback logic resets the counter after the 9th position (bit 9)
4. Output encoding converts the one-hot shift register to binary
5. Advantages:
   - Simple state transition logic (just shifting)
   - Natural decade period without complex terminal count detection
   - Glitch-free outputs
   - Easy to extend for other modulo counts
