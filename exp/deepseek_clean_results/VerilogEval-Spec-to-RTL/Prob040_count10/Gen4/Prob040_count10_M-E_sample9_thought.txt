Novel approach using a shift register with feedback for decade counting:
1. Implement a 4-bit linear feedback shift register (LFSR) modified to count sequentially
2. Use a custom feedback polynomial to create a decade counter sequence
3. The LFSR will naturally cycle through 10 unique states (0-9) before repeating
4. Advantages:
   - Lower power consumption due to fewer bit toggles
   - Potential for better timing as only one bit changes per clock
   - Interesting alternative to traditional binary counting
5. Reset initializes to 0 (0000) and the sequence progresses through binary 0-9
