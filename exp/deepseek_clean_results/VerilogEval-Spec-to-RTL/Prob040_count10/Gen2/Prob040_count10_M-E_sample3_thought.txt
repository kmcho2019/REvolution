Alternative approach using LFSR-style decade counter:
1. Instead of a standard binary counter, implement a modified LFSR that cycles through 10 states
2. Use a 4-bit register with custom state transitions to create the 0-9 sequence
3. The state transitions are hardcoded based on current state:
   - 0→1, 1→2, ..., 8→9, 9→0
4. This approach may have better glitch characteristics and more uniform delay paths
5. Synchronous reset still initializes to 0
