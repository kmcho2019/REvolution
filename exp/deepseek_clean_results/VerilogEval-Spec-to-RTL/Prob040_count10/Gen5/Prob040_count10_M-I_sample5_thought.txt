Improvement strategy for better PPA:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Pre-compute the terminal count condition one cycle earlier to break the critical path
   - Use registered comparison to reduce combinational logic delay

2. Power Optimization:
   - Implement clock gating when counter reaches 8 (1000) to prevent unnecessary toggles
   - Use a dedicated terminal count signal to minimize switching activity

3. Area Optimization:
   - Maintain the efficient 2-bit terminal count detection (q[3] & q[0])
   - Avoid additional registers except for the critical path optimization
