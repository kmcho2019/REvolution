To optimize the decade counter for better PPA metrics:
1. Pre-compute the rollover condition to reduce critical path delay
2. Use clock gating when counter reaches 9 to reduce power consumption
3. Maintain simple 4-bit binary encoding for minimal area
4. The rollover signal can be generated in parallel with the increment operation
5. Clock gating will prevent unnecessary toggles when counter is at 9

Key improvements:
- Parallel rollover detection reduces critical path
- Clock gating reduces power during idle cycles
- Maintains same area footprint while improving timing
