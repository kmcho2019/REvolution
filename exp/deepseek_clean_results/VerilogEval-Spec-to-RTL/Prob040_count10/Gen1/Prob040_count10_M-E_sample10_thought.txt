Instead of a traditional binary counter, we can implement the decade counter using a modified LFSR approach:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register with XOR feedback
2. Carefully design the feedback taps to create a sequence of exactly 10 states (0-9)
3. The feedback polynomial is chosen to cycle through the required values
4. This approach can potentially offer better performance in some implementations as it reduces the carry chain of a traditional counter
5. The reset initializes the register to 0 (0000)
6. The sequence will be: 0→1→2→4→8→3→6→12→5→10→0 (but we mask to keep only 0-9)
