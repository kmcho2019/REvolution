Novel Approach: Johnson Counter with Decoding Logic
1. Use a 4-bit Johnson counter (twisted ring counter) which naturally cycles through 8 states
2. Add additional logic to extend the cycle to 10 states by detecting and injecting two extra states
3. The Johnson counter provides glitch-free outputs and requires less combinational logic than a binary counter
4. Special state detection logic handles the extended counting sequence
5. Reset initializes the counter to 0000 (first state of Johnson sequence)

Advantages:
- Reduced combinational logic compared to binary increment approach
- Glitch-free outputs during counting
- Novel state sequencing that differs from standard binary counting
- Efficient implementation with good timing characteristics
