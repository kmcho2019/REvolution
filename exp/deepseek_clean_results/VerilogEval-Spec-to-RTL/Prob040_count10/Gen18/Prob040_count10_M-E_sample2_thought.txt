Novel Shift Register Approach:
1. Use a 4-bit shift register where the LSB is fed back through combinational logic
2. The feedback logic detects terminal count (9) and resets to 0
3. Each clock cycle shifts left and inserts the computed feedback bit
4. Benefits:
   - Eliminates adder logic (reduces area)
   - Uses simple shift operation (potentially better timing)
   - Unique implementation compared to standard counter designs
5. Reset synchronously clears all bits
6. The shift pattern naturally counts through 0-9 binary sequence
