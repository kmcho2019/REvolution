Simplified Decade Counter Design:
1. Basic Functionality:
   - Maintain synchronous decade counter (0-9) with synchronous reset
   - Keep 4-bit output register
   - Positive edge triggered clock

2. Simplification Approach:
   - Remove the combined terminal count detection (q[3] & q[0])
   - Use simple equality comparison (q == 9) for wrap-around
   - This makes the logic more straightforward and readable
   - Still maintains all required functionality

3. Benefits:
   - Clearer intent (direct comparison with 9)
   - Slightly easier to understand for maintenance
   - Same area and timing characteristics in practice
   - Maintains synchronous reset behavior
