Novel architectural idea: LFSR-based decade counter with custom polynomial
1. Instead of a traditional binary counter, use a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) approach
2. Select a 4-bit LFSR polynomial that cycles through exactly 10 states (0-9)
3. The polynomial x^4 + x^3 + 1 cycles through 15 states, but we'll modify it to reset early
4. Add synchronous reset functionality
5. Benefits: potentially lower power (fewer bit toggles) and unique state encoding

Key features:
- Uses LFSR's efficient shift operation instead of increment
- Custom state mapping to achieve decade count
- Maintains synchronous reset
- Interesting alternative to traditional binary counter
