To optimize the solution for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I propose the following improvements:

1. Performance: Maintain parallel bitwise operations which already provide excellent timing characteristics (wns/tns = 0).

2. Power Optimization:
   - Group related operations to minimize switching activity
   - Use more power-efficient XOR implementation for the wrap-around case

3. Area Optimization:
   - Eliminate the generate blocks since they don't provide area benefits for this simple case
   - Use direct vector operations which synthesize more efficiently
   - Combine similar operations where possible
