Refactored design approach:

1. Replace the always block with continuous assignments (assign statements) for better readability and potential synthesis optimization.

2. Maintain the same logical operations but express them directly with assign statements:
   - out_both: Use concatenation and bitwise AND as before
   - out_any: Use concatenation and bitwise OR as before
   - out_different: Use XOR with rotated input as before

3. Benefits of this refactoring:
   - More declarative style shows direct relationships between inputs and outputs
   - Eliminates the need for procedural blocks when simple combinational logic suffices
   - May allow synthesis tools more flexibility in optimization
   - Maintains identical functionality while being more concise

Key changes:
- Converted from procedural always block to continuous assignments
- Kept all bitwise operations identical
- Preserved all edge case handling
- Simplified module structure
