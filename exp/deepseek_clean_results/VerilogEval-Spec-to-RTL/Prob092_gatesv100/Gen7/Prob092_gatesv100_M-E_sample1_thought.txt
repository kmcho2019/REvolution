Here's a novel approach using shift registers and parallel processing:

1. For out_both: Instead of direct bitwise AND, we'll shift the input left by 1 and then perform AND with original input. This automatically handles the neighbor relationship. The MSB is explicitly set to 0.

2. For out_any: We'll shift the input right by 1 and perform OR with original input. The LSB is explicitly set to 0.

3. For out_different: We'll create a circular shift left by 1 and XOR with original input to handle the wrap-around requirement.

Key advantages:
- Uses shift operations which are hardware-efficient
- More compact representation of the neighbor relationships
- Clear separation of boundary conditions
- Potentially better for synthesis as shift operations are well-optimized in most tools
