Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Hierarchical Processing: Divide the 100-bit input into 10 segments of 10 bits each for parallel processing
2. Segment Processing Units: Each segment handles its own 10-bit chunk independently
3. Boundary Handling: Special logic for segment boundaries to maintain correct neighbor relationships
4. Output Aggregation: Combine results from all segments to form final outputs
5. Optimized XOR for Difference: Use carry-chain-like XOR propagation for efficient wrap-around difference calculation

Key Benefits:
- Better scalability for larger bit widths
- Potential for pipelining in future extensions
- More structured synthesis optimization opportunities
- Clear separation of boundary cases from regular processing
