Novel Shift-and-Compare Architecture:

1. Instead of direct bitwise operations, implement a parallel shift-and-compare mechanism:
   - For out_both: Right-shift the input and AND with original
   - For out_any: Left-shift the input and OR with original
   - For out_different: Circular left-shift and XOR with original

2. Key innovations:
   - Uses implicit shift operations through concatenation
   - Processes all bits in parallel without explicit loops
   - Handles edge cases naturally through shift direction
   - More hardware-friendly as it maps well to FPGA shift registers

3. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of the three operations
   - Easier timing closure due to regular structure
   - Potential for pipelining if needed
   - More intuitive mapping to physical hardware
