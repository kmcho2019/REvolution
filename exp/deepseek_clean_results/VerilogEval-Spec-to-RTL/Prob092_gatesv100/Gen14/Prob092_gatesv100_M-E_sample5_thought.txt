Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Instead of computing all outputs in parallel, use a sequential approach with a small state machine to reduce area and power
2. Process the 100 bits in chunks (e.g., 4 bits at a time) using a shift register approach
3. Use a counter to track which bits are being processed
4. Store intermediate results in registers and accumulate the final outputs
5. This trades off some speed for potential area and power savings

Key Benefits:
- Reduced combinational logic by processing bits sequentially
- Lower switching activity since not all bits are processed simultaneously
- Potential for clock gating when outputs aren't needed
- More scalable for larger bit widths
