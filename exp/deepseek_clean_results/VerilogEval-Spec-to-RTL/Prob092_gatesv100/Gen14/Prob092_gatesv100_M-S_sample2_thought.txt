1. All outputs are computed in parallel with minimal bitwise operations
2. Boundary conditions are handled elegantly through concatenation

4. Each output is computed in a single clean line
5. No unnecessary variables or intermediate steps exist

- Break functionality (e.g., incorrect boundary handling)
