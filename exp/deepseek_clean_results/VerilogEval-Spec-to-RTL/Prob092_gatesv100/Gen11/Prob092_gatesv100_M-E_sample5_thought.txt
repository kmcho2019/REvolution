Novel Architectural Idea:
Instead of processing all bits in parallel, we'll implement a hierarchical bit-slice architecture where:
1. We divide the 100-bit input into 10 segments of 10 bits each
2. Each segment has a dedicated processing unit that handles its local operations
3. Boundary conditions are handled by special edge units
4. A final combiner merges results from all segments

Advantages:
- More scalable for larger bit widths
- Easier to pipeline if needed
- Clear separation of boundary and internal logic
- Potential for power gating unused segments
- Modular design allows for easier verification
