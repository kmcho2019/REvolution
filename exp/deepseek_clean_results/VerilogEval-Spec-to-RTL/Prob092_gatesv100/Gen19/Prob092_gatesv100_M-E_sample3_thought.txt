Novel Architectural Idea: Bitwise Processing with Circular Shift Registers

Instead of using direct bitwise operations, this solution employs circular shift registers to handle the neighbor comparisons more elegantly. The key innovations are:

1. For out_both and out_any: Use circular right and left shifts to align neighbor bits, then perform the required logic operations. This eliminates the need for explicit concatenation.

2. For out_different: Leverage the inherent properties of XOR with circular shifts to detect differences, including the wrap-around case.

3. Edge cases are handled naturally by the shift operations without special conditions.

4. The implementation uses temporary shift registers to improve readability while maintaining efficiency.

This approach provides a more systematic way to handle the neighbor comparisons and might be more intuitive for certain applications where shift operations are preferred.
