Here's a novel approach using a circular shift register technique:
1. For out_both: Create a left-shifted version of the input and perform bitwise AND with original
2. For out_any: Create a right-shifted version of the input and perform bitwise OR with original
3. For out_different: Use a circular left shift and XOR with original for wrap-around behavior
4. Special boundary bits are handled by masking the shift results appropriately

Key advantages:
- Uses shift operations which can be efficiently implemented in hardware
- Clear separation of the three different operations
- Maintains parallel processing of all bits
- Explicit handling of boundary conditions
