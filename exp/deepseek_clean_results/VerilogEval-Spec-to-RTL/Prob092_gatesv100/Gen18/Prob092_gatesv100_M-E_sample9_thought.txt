Novel Architectural Idea:
Instead of using direct bitwise operations, implement a hierarchical approach using smaller sub-modules for better modularity and potential reuse. The 100-bit operations will be broken down into 10 segments of 10 bits each, processed by identical submodules, then combined. This approach offers:
1. Better scalability for larger bit widths
2. Potential for pipelining if timing becomes critical
3. Clear separation of concerns for each output type
4. Easier verification through modular testing
5. Potential for power gating individual segments if needed

Key Features:
- 10 identical 10-bit processing units (SegmentModule)
- Each segment handles its own boundary conditions
- Final output combines all segment outputs
- Wrapped XOR handled within each segment plus one cross-segment connection
