To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Performance (Timing):
   - The critical path is in the decrement operation (subtraction). We'll replace it with a simpler decrement logic using a ripple-borrow approach that typically has better timing characteristics.
   - Add a bypass register for the decrement result to break the critical path.

2. Power:
   - Implement clock gating for the register when neither shift_ena nor count_ena are active to reduce dynamic power.
   - Use conditional assignments to minimize unnecessary toggling.

3. Area:
   - The ripple-borrow decrementer will be more area-efficient than the subtractor.
   - Remove redundant logic by combining control signals.

The improved design will maintain the same functionality while achieving better timing and lower power consumption.
