The simplified design will:
1. Combine the shift and count operations directly in a single always block
2. Eliminate the separate next_q calculation to reduce logic levels
3. Maintain the same functionality with shift having priority over count
4. Use simpler conditional statements without intermediate wires
5. Reduce area by removing unnecessary combinational logic

Key simplifications:
- Direct register update in always block
- No intermediate next_q calculation
- Fewer logic levels by combining operations
- Same edge-triggered behavior preserved
