Instead of a traditional linear shift register, we'll implement a circular design where:
1. For shift operations: The MSB is always the input data, and other bits rotate right
2. For count operations: We'll use a clever bit manipulation trick where we:
   - Invert all bits (equivalent to 1's complement)
   - Add 1 (which gives us 2's complement negative)
   - This effectively performs a decrement operation
3. The circular nature reduces wiring complexity and the bit manipulation approach simplifies the decrement logic

This approach offers potential PPA benefits:
- Reduced critical path by eliminating the full subtractor
- More balanced shift/count operations
- Potential area savings from shared inversion logic
