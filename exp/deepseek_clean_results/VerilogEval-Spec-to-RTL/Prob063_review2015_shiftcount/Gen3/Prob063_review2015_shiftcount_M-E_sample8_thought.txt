Instead of a traditional linear shift register, we'll implement a circular design with two separate data paths:
1. Shift path: Data circulates while inserting new bits during shift operations
2. Count path: Dedicated decrement logic that feeds back to the register

Key innovations:
- Circular architecture reduces wire length and improves timing
- Separate paths for shift and count operations minimize critical path
- Shared register reduces area while maintaining functionality
- The circular nature allows efficient bit rotation during shifts
