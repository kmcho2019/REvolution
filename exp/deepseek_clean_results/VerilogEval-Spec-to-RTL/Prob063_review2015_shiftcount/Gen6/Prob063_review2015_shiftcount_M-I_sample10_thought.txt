Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Remove the enable signal to eliminate one level of logic in the critical path
   - Simplify the mux structure by using parallel case evaluation
   - Keep operations separate to avoid unnecessary muxing

2. Power Optimization:
   - Maintain operand isolation by keeping shift and count operations separate
   - Remove clock gating since it adds complexity with minimal power benefit for this small design

3. Area Optimization:
   - Simplify the decrement operation by using direct subtraction
   - Use standard cell-friendly operations

Key Changes:
- Separate always blocks for clarity and timing optimization
- Direct assignment of operations without intermediate enable signal
- Simplified mux structure with parallel case evaluation
- Maintain clean separation of shift and count functionality
