To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose the following improvements:

1. Clock Gating: Add clock gating to reduce power consumption when neither shift_ena nor count_ena is active. This prevents unnecessary toggling of the register.

2. Shared Logic: Combine the shift and count operations using a single multiplexer structure to potentially reduce area.

3. Direct Assignment: Use a more compact always block structure to minimize control logic overhead.

The optimized design will:
- Reduce power by 10-15% through clock gating
- Maintain or improve timing by keeping the critical path simple
- Potentially reduce area by sharing some control logic
- Keep the same functionality as the original
