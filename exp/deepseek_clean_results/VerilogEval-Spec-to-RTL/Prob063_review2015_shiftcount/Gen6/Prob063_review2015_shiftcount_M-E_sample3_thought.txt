This alternative design:
1. Uses separate combinational logic paths for shift and count operations
2. Implements a 2:1 mux to select between shift and count results
3. Reduces fanout on the enable signals by decoding them first
4. Potentially improves timing by parallelizing the shift and count operations
5. May offer better power characteristics by gating unused paths

Key innovations:
- Pre-computes both shift and count results in parallel
- Uses a single mux selection rather than priority logic
- Could potentially reduce glitching power by having stable paths
- May offer better physical implementation characteristics
