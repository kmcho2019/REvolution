To optimize this design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I propose the following improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Pipeline the decrement operation by splitting it into two stages to meet timing requirements
   - Use carry-lookahead style logic for faster decrement operation

2. Power Optimization:
   - Add explicit clock gating for the hold state to prevent unnecessary register toggles
   - Use conditional assignments to minimize switching activity

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share common logic between shift and count operations where possible
   - Use more efficient encoding for the decrement operation

The improved design will:
- Meet timing constraints by reducing the critical path
- Reduce power consumption through clock gating and conditional operations
- Maintain or slightly reduce area through logic sharing
