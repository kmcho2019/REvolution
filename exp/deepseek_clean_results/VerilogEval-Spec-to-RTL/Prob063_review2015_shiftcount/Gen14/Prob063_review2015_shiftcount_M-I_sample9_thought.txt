To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:

1. Performance: Replace the ternary operator chain with a priority-encoded case statement in an always block to reduce the critical path. This eliminates the cascaded mux structure.

2. Power: Move the next state logic into the same always block as the register update to prevent unnecessary combinational switching when enables are inactive.

3. Area: Keep the minimal register implementation but ensure the arithmetic/logic operations are optimally synthesized.

Key changes:
- Combine next state and register logic in one always block
- Use casez priority encoding for clearer operation selection
- Maintain same functionality but with better timing characteristics
- Reduce power by eliminating redundant combinational evaluations
