The feedback indicates the shift operation was incorrectly implemented as a right shift when it should be a left shift for MSB-first operation. For MSB-first shifting:
1. New data should enter at the LSB position
2. Existing bits should shift left (toward MSB)
3. The MSB should be discarded during shift

The corrected implementation will:
- Perform left shift when shift_ena is high: {q[2:0], data}
- Still decrement when count_ena is high
- Maintain the same priority (shift over count)
- Keep all other aspects of the original design
