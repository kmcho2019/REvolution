Novel architectural idea: "Parallel Precompute with Final Mux"
1. Instead of conditionally performing operations, precompute both possible outcomes (shift and count) in parallel
2. Use a simple multiplexer to select the final result based on control signals
3. This approach:
   - Eliminates priority concerns by computing both paths simultaneously
   - May improve timing by parallelizing computations
   - Makes the control flow more explicit and potentially more synthesis-friendly
   - Could enable better pipelining if needed for higher frequencies
4. The mux selection gives clear priority (shift over count) while maintaining simplicity
