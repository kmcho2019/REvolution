The design requires a 4-bit register that can perform two operations:
1. Shift operation (MSB first) when shift_ena is high
2. Decrement operation when count_ena is high

The operations are mutually exclusive in normal operation, so priority doesn't matter. We'll implement this as a simple always block that checks the control signals and performs the appropriate operation on the positive clock edge. The shift operation will shift in the new data bit into the MSB, while the decrement operation will subtract 1 from the current value.
