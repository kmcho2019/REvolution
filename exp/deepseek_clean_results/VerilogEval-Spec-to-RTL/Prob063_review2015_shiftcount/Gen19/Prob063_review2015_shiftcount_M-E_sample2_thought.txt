Novel Arithmetic-Based Implementation:
1. Instead of separate shift and count logic, use a unified arithmetic approach
2. For shift: Multiply by 2 (left shift) and add new bit (equivalent to {q[2:0], data})
3. For count: Simply subtract 1
4. Use a multiplexer to select between these operations based on control signals
5. This approach may offer better timing as it uses fundamental arithmetic operations that synthesis tools can optimize well

Advantages:
- Single arithmetic operation per cycle simplifies datapath
- Potential for better synthesis optimization
- Clear mathematical relationship between operations
- Easier to extend to wider bit widths if needed
