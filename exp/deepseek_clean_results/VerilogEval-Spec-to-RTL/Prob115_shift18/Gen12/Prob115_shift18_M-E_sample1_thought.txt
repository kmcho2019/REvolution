Novel Barrel Shifter Architecture:
1. **Parallel Shift Computation**:
   - Pre-compute all possible shift results in parallel (left1, left8, right1, right8)
   - Use a single 4:1 multiplexer to select the final result
   - Eliminates nested conditionals and reduces critical path

2. **Sign Extension Optimization**:
   - Compute arithmetic right shifts using dedicated sign extension wires
   - Avoids repetitive sign bit replication in the datapath

3. **Efficient Control Logic**:
   - Decode shift amount into one-hot control signals
   - Simplify multiplexer selection logic

4. **Clock Gating**:
   - Only update shift register when load or ena is active
   - Reduces unnecessary clock transitions

Key Advantages:
- Balanced timing paths for all shift operations
- Reduced multiplexer depth from 3 levels to 1
- Clear separation of shift computation and selection
- More scalable for additional shift amounts
