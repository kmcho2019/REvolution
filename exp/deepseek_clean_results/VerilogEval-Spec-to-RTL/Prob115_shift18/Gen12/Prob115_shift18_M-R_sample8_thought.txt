1. **Shift Logic Simplification**:

   - Eliminate intermediate wires for shift operations to reduce logic levels
   - Maintain arithmetic right shift by directly handling sign extension

2. **Register Implementation**:

   - Simplify next-state logic by moving shift operations directly into the always block

3. **Control Signal Optimization**:
   - Remove redundant control signals (do_load, do_shift)
   - Use direct expressions in the always block for clearer intent

4. **Output Assignment**:
   - Keep simple continuous assignment for output
   - Remove unnecessary intermediate wires

- More direct implementation of shift operations
- Reduced logic levels in critical path
- Clearer control flow
- Maintains same functionality with potentially better timing
