Here's a novel approach that uses a hierarchical shift structure with pre-computed shift options:
1. Instead of nested conditionals, use parallel shift computation
2. Pre-compute all possible shift results in separate wires
3. Use a single 4:1 mux to select the appropriate shift result
4. This reduces critical path by eliminating cascaded logic
5. The arithmetic right shift maintains sign extension by replicating MSB
6. The synchronous load and enable remain unchanged
7. This approach may improve timing by reducing logic depth
