Improvement Strategy:
1. Parallel Shift Paths: Replace nested ternary operators with parallel shift computations and a final multiplexer to reduce critical path depth.
2. Gated Computation: Only compute the selected shift operation to minimize unnecessary switching activity.
3. Shared Shift Logic: Implement 8-bit shifts using concatenation of smaller shifts (1-bit) to reduce area.
4. Optimized Sign Extension: Simplify right shift logic by pre-computing sign extension bits.
5. Early Muxing: Move the load/data selection before shift computation to simplify the critical path.

Key Optimizations:
- Reduced logic depth by eliminating nested conditionals
- Power savings through gated computation
- Area reduction via shared shift logic
- Improved timing with parallel paths and early muxing
- Maintained functionality while optimizing PPA
