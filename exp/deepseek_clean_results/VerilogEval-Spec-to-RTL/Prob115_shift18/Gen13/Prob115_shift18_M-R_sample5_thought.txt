Refactoring Strategy:
1. **Structural Changes**:
   - Replace the always block with continuous assignments for better clarity and potential synthesis optimization
   - Separate shift direction and amount selection into distinct logic blocks
   - Use arithmetic shift operator (>>>) for right shifts to simplify sign extension

2. **Logic Optimization**:
   - Pre-compute all possible shift results in parallel
   - Use a single 4:1 mux for shift selection instead of nested if-else
   - Make shift operations conditional only on 'ena' to simplify control flow

3. **Power/Area Benefits**:
   - Continuous assignments may lead to cleaner synthesized logic
   - Arithmetic shift operator reduces explicit sign extension logic
   - Parallel computation of shift options may enable better resource sharing

Key Improvements:
- More readable and maintainable code structure
- Potential for better synthesis optimization with parallel paths
- Reduced control complexity with direct shift amount selection
- Clear separation of shift direction logic
