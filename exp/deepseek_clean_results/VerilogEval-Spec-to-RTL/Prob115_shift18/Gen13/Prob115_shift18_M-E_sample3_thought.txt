Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Implement a barrel shifter with configurable shift direction and amount
2. Use a hierarchical shift approach:
   - First level selects between 1-bit and 8-bit shifts
   - Second level handles direction (left/right)
3. For arithmetic right shifts, implement sign extension through a conditional bit replication
4. Optimize for minimal logic depth by:
   - Pre-computing all possible shift variants
   - Using a single 4:1 multiplexer for final selection
5. Include power optimization by:
   - Gating the shift computation when not enabled
   - Minimizing unnecessary bit toggling

Key Innovations:
- Hierarchical shift selection reduces critical path
- Conditional sign extension only activates during right shifts
- All shift variants pre-computed in parallel
- Efficient multiplexer structure for final selection
- Power-aware design with enable gating
