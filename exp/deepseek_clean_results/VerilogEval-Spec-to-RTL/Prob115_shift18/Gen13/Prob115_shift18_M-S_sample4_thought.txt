Simplification Strategy:
1. **Reduce Control Logic**:
   - Use a single case statement for all shift operations instead of nested if-else
   - Combine direction and amount selection into one operation

2. **Minimize Signal Transitions**:
   - Remove intermediate next_q signal and compute directly in clocked block
   - Only perform shift operations when ena is active

3. **Simplify Arithmetic Shift**:
   - Use concatenation with sign bit directly in right shift cases
   - Keep left shift operations simple with zero padding

4. **Streamline Register Update**:
   - Handle load and shift operations in a single always block
   - Eliminate unnecessary signal assignments
