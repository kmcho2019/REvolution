Here's a novel architectural approach for the 64-bit arithmetic shift register:

1. Precompute all possible shift operations in parallel using dedicated shift circuits
2. Use a 4:1 multiplexer to select the appropriate shifted result
3. Implement arithmetic right shift by sign-extending the MSB during right shifts
4. Separate the shift amount decoding from the shift operations for better timing
5. Use a single always block for clean synchronous operation
6. Optimize for power by only computing shifts when enabled (ena)

Key innovations:
- Parallel shift computation reduces critical path compared to serial conditional logic
- Explicit shift circuits make the design more synthesis-friendly
- Clear separation of control and datapath improves readability and optimization
- Sign extension handled directly in the shift operations
