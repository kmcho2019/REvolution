To optimize the design while maintaining the same functionality, I'll:
1. Replace nested ternary operators with a case statement for better readability and potentially better synthesis
2. Separate the shift amount decoding from the shift operation itself
3. Use continuous assignments for all combinational logic
4. Keep the register update in a single always block
5. Optimize arithmetic right shift by reusing the sign bit more efficiently
6. Maintain the same synchronous load and enable functionality

This approach should improve timing by reducing logic depth and make the design more maintainable while preserving the same behavior.
