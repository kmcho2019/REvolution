This design implements a multi-stage shift architecture with the following innovations:
1. Uses a two-stage shift approach: first by 0/8 bits, then by 0/1 bit
2. Implements dynamic sign extension that only activates during right shifts
3. Employs a priority-encoded shift selection to reduce multiplexer depth
4. Uses bitmask-based shifting for left operations to reduce logic complexity
5. Implements sign extension as a conditional operation rather than pre-computing
6. Optimizes the critical path by separating the shift amount and direction control
7. Reduces power consumption by minimizing unnecessary bit toggles during shifts
