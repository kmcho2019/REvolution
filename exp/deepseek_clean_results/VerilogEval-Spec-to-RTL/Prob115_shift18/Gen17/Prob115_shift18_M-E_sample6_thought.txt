New architectural approach:
1. Pre-compute all possible shift outcomes in parallel (left1, left8, right1, right8)
2. Use a 4:1 multiplexer to select the appropriate shift result
3. For arithmetic right shifts, implement sign extension by replicating the MSB
4. The synchronous load has priority over shifting
5. This approach minimizes the critical path by:
   - Eliminating sequential shift operations
   - Reducing logic depth compared to barrel shifter
   - Making shift selection a simple mux operation
6. The parallel computation allows for constant-time shift operations regardless of shift amount
