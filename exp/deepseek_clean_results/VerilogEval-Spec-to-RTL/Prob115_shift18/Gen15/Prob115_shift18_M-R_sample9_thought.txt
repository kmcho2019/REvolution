Refactoring Approach:
1. **Structural Changes**:
   - Replace the always block with continuous assignments for better readability and potential synthesis optimization
   - Move the shift operation calculations into separate wires for clearer logic separation
   - Use a more explicit selection structure for the shift operations

2. **Optimization Focus**:
   - Maintain the parallel shift computation but make the structure more hierarchical
   - Keep the efficient sign extension logic but make its usage more explicit
   - Preserve the gating logic while making it more visible in the code flow

3. **Key Improvements**:
   - Explicit shift direction selection before shift amount selection
   - Clear separation of load vs shift logic
   - Direct assignment to output q without intermediate next_q register

The core functionality remains identical but the structure is more transparent and potentially more optimization-friendly for synthesis tools.
