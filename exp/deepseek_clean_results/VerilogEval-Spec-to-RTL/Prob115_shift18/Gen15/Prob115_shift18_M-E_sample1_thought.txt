Novel Pipelined Shift Architecture:
1. **Two-Stage Pipeline**:
   - Stage 1: Decode shift operation and prepare shift amount
   - Stage 2: Execute the actual shift operation
   - This breaks the critical path into smaller segments

2. **Pre-computed Shift Options**:
   - Compute all possible shift amounts in parallel
   - Use a barrel shifter approach but with arithmetic right shift support
   - Sign extension is pre-computed for right shifts

3. **Dynamic Clock Gating**:
   - Only enable pipeline stages when needed (load or ena active)
   - Reduces power consumption during idle cycles

4. **Efficient Sign Handling**:
   - Store and propagate the sign bit separately for arithmetic shifts
   - Eliminates redundant sign extension calculations

5. **Optimized Control Path**:
   - Simplified control logic with early decoding
   - Reduced multiplexer depth through pre-selection

Key Advantages:
- Improved timing through pipelining
- Lower power through dynamic clock gating
- Reduced area through shared shift logic
- Maintains single-cycle operation for load
- Better scalability for larger shift amounts
