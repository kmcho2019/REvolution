Novel Architectural Idea: Barrel Shifter with Pre-computed Shift Options

Key Concepts:
1. **Parallel Shift Computation**: Compute all possible shift operations simultaneously in parallel, then select the appropriate result using a multiplexer. This eliminates sequential logic dependencies and reduces critical path.

2. **Arithmetic Right Shift Optimization**: For arithmetic right shifts, use sign extension masks that are pre-computed based on the shift amount, avoiding repetitive bit replication logic.

3. **Efficient Multiplexing**: Implement a 4:1 multiplexer to select between:
   - No shift (load operation)
   - Left shift by 1
   - Left shift by 8
   - Right shift (arithmetic) by 1 or 8

4. **Clock Gating**: Only update the register when either load or ena is active, reducing unnecessary power consumption.

Advantages:
- All shift computations happen in parallel, improving timing
- Clean separation of shift operations simplifies verification
- Reduced logic depth compared to nested conditionals
- Easier to extend for additional shift amounts
