This design employs a segmented shift strategy with pre-computed shift patterns:
1. Instead of traditional barrel shifting, we pre-compute all possible shift outcomes in parallel
2. The register is conceptually divided into segments to optimize shift operations
3. For left shifts: Implement as simple bit shifts with zero fill
4. For right shifts: Use sign-extension by replicating the MSB during shift
5. A 4:1 multiplexer selects the appropriate pre-computed shift result
6. This approach minimizes critical path by eliminating sequential shift logic
7. The synchronous load has priority over shift operations
