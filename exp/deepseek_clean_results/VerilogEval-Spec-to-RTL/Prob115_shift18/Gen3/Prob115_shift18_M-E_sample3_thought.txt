Novel Barrel Shifter Architecture:
1. Pre-compute all possible shift results in parallel to eliminate multiplexer critical path
2. Use a hierarchical sign extension approach:
   - First extend the sign bit to 8 bits when needed
   - Then replicate this for the 8-bit right shift case
3. Implement enable gating at the shift operation level to reduce power
4. Use a priority-encoded selection scheme for the shift amount to optimize timing
5. Separate the left and right shift logic paths for better physical implementation

Key Innovations:
- Parallel computation of all shift options eliminates serial multiplexer delay
- Hierarchical sign extension reduces replication logic
- Early enable gating prevents unnecessary computations
- Physical separation of left/right shift paths improves placement
