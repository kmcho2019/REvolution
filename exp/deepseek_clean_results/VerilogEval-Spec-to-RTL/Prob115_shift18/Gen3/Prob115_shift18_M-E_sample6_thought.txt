The new architecture features:
1. A barrel shifter structure that pre-computes all possible shift amounts in parallel
2. Optimized sign extension logic that only computes the necessary sign bits
3. Early enable gating to reduce unnecessary switching activity
4. Resource sharing between left and right shift operations
5. A priority-encoded multiplexer for shift amount selection

Key innovations:
- Parallel computation of all shift amounts enables faster selection
- Sign extension is computed on-demand only for right shifts
- The enable signal gates the shift computation early to save power
- The barrel shifter structure is more area-efficient for multiple shift amounts
- The design maintains single-cycle operation while improving PPA metrics
