Here's a novel approach using a barrel shifter with configurable shift amounts:
1. Instead of pre-computing all shift possibilities, implement a single configurable barrel shifter
2. Use a control unit to decode the 'amount' into shift direction and magnitude
3. For arithmetic right shifts, dynamically generate the sign extension mask
4. The barrel shifter will use multiplexers to select the appropriate shifted bits
5. This approach should reduce area by sharing shift logic while maintaining flexibility
6. The synchronous load is implemented as a priority operation
