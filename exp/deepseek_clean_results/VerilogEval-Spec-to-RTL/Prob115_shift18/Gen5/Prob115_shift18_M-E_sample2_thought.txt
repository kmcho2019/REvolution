Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Implement a barrel shifter with configurable direction and shift amount
2. Use a hierarchical shift approach:
   - First stage handles 8-bit shifts when needed
   - Second stage handles 1-bit fine adjustment
3. Optimize sign extension by only applying it during right shifts
4. Use a pre-computation approach where all possible shift results are calculated in parallel
5. Implement a power-optimized selection mechanism using one-hot encoding

Key Innovations:
- Hierarchical shifting reduces critical path
- Parallel pre-computation enables single-cycle selection
- Efficient sign extension handling
- One-hot selection reduces switching power
- Clean separation of shift magnitude and direction logic
