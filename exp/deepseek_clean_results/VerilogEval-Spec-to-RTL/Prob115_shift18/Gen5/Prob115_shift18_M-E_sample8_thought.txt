Novel Architectural Idea: "Pipelined Multi-Stage Shift Register with Dynamic Sign Extension"

Key Innovations:
1. Two-stage pipeline for improved timing:
   - Stage 1: Decode and prepare shift operation
   - Stage 2: Execute shift and handle sign extension

2. Dynamic sign extension:
   - Only computes sign extension when right shifts are selected
   - Uses parameterized replication with late binding

3. Shift amount pre-decoding:
   - Pre-decodes shift amount into control signals
   - Reduces critical path in shift selection logic

4. Power-optimized design:
   - Gated clock enable for pipeline registers
   - Conditional computation of shift operations

5. Area-efficient implementation:
   - Shared shift logic for similar operations
   - Minimal redundant computation

Advantages:
- Better timing due to pipelining
- Lower power due to gated computation
- Cleaner separation of control and datapath
- More scalable for larger shift amounts
