Here's a novel architectural approach for the 64-bit arithmetic shift register:

1. **Pre-compute all shift variants**: Instead of selecting between shift operations, pre-compute all four possible shift results in parallel. This trades area for potentially better timing as the selection becomes a simple 4:1 mux.

2. **Sign extension optimization**: For arithmetic right shifts, pre-extend the sign bit to 8 positions, allowing both 1-bit and 8-bit right shifts to use the same extended sign value.

3. **Early enable gating**: Apply the enable signal before the shift operations to prevent unnecessary computation when not shifting.

4. **Explicit case structure**: Use a case statement for shift selection to improve readability and potentially help synthesis tools optimize better.

This approach should:
- Reduce critical path by parallelizing shift computations
- Minimize redundant sign extension logic
- Improve power efficiency by gating computations early
- Maintain clean synchronous load behavior
