Refactoring Strategy:
1. **Structural Changes**:
   - Replace always blocks with continuous assignments for combinational logic
   - Use ternary operators for more compact shift selection
   - Separate shift direction and magnitude decisions into distinct signals

2. **Optimization Goals**:
   - Reduce logic depth by flattening the nested if-else structure
   - Improve readability with explicit shift amount calculations
   - Maintain same functionality while using different coding style

3. **Key Improvements**:
   - Shift operations now computed in parallel and selected via mux
   - Clear separation of control signals (direction and amount)
   - More direct implementation of arithmetic right shift
   - Reduced redundant computations through signal reuse
