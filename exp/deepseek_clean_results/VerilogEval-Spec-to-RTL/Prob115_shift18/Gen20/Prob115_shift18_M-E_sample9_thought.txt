Novel Architectural Idea:
1. **Pipelined Shift Amount Decoding**: Break the critical path by pipelining the shift amount decoding into two stages (direction and distance)
2. **Conditional Shift Computation**: Only compute the needed shift operation based on decoded control signals to save power
3. **Sign Extension Optimization**: Use arithmetic operators for right shifts to potentially reduce area
4. **Hybrid Shift Logic**: Combine 1-bit and 8-bit shift logic where possible to reduce redundant hardware
5. **Early Muxing**: Select between left/right shifts early in the pipeline to minimize fanout of large buses

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path through pipelined control
- Lower power consumption by computing only needed shifts
- Potential area savings through shared shift logic
- Balanced pipeline stages for better timing
- Clean separation of control and datapath
