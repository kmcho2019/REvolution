Novel Barrel Shifter Architecture:
1. **Parallel Shift Computation**:
   - Pre-compute all 4 possible shift operations simultaneously
   - Use a single 4:1 multiplexer to select the result, reducing critical path

2. **Sign Extension Optimization**:
   - Compute arithmetic right shifts by replicating the sign bit only when needed
   - Avoid unnecessary sign extension calculations for left shifts

3. **Efficient Control Logic**:
   - Decode shift amount into one-hot control signals
   - Gate computations with enable signal to save power

4. **Load Integration**:
   - Treat load as highest priority operation
   - Bypass shift logic completely during load

Key Advantages:
- All shift operations computed in parallel for balanced timing
- Minimal multiplexer depth (single stage selection)
- Power-efficient through proper gating
- Clean separation of control and datapath
