After carefully re-analyzing the waveform with the feedback, I realize the correct behavior is:
1. The state flip-flop toggles when:
   - Both a and b are high (a & b), OR
   - Both a and b are low (~a & ~b)
2. The q output is high when:
   - b is high and state is low (b & ~state), OR
   - a is high and b is low (a & ~b), OR
   - a is high, b is high, and state is high (a & b & state)

This explains all the waveform observations:
- State toggles at both (a&b) and (~a&~b) conditions
- q has additional condition when all three (a,b,state) are high
- Initial state of 0 matches waveform
