The circuit behavior is more complex than initially thought. Key observations:
1. State transition occurs when:
   - a=1 and b=1 (toggle)
   - a=0 and b=0 (reset to 0)
2. Output q depends on:
   - When a=0: q = b
   - When a=1: q = state XOR b
3. Special cases:
   - When a=1 and b=0, q = state
   - When a=1 and b=1, q = ~state

This explains all waveform behavior:
- At 45ns: a=0,b=1 → q=1
- At 55ns: a=1,b=0 → q=state=0
- At 65ns: a=1,b=1 → q=~state=0
- At 95ns: a=1,b=1 → state toggles, q=~state
