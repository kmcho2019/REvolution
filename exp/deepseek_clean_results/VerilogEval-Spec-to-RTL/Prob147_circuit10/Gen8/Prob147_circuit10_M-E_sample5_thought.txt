After carefully analyzing the waveform patterns, I propose a new approach based on the following observations:

1. The state behaves like a toggle flip-flop that's conditionally enabled:
   - Toggles when a=1 and previous state was 0 (with some exceptions)
   - Resets under certain conditions when b=1

2. The output q appears to be:
   - 1 when (state XOR b) is true, except when a=1 and b=1
   - 0 otherwise

Key innovations in this new design:
1. State transitions are controlled by a combination of current inputs and previous state
2. Output logic has special cases for specific input combinations
3. The design maintains minimal hardware (one flip-flop) while capturing the complex behavior
