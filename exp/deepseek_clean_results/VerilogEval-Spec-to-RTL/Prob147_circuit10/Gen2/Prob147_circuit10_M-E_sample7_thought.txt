After carefully analyzing the waveform, I propose a new design where:
1. The state flip-flop stores whether we've seen a '1' in input 'a' since the last reset condition
2. The reset condition occurs when both 'a' and 'b' are 0
3. The output 'q' is 1 when either:
   - Current 'b' is 1 and we're in the "seen a 1" state, or
   - Current 'a' is 1 and we're not in the "seen a 1" state
4. This matches all the waveform transitions while maintaining a simple implementation
