The waveform reveals a more complex state machine behavior:
1. The state (flip-flop) toggles when a=1 and b=1 (at 95ns)
2. The state also sets to 1 when a=0 and b=1 (at 75ns)
3. The output q is 1 when either:
   - b=1 and state=0 (at 45ns)
   - b=0 and state=1 (at 115ns)
   - a=1 and b=1 (at 95ns)

This suggests:
- State updates when (a & b) or (~a & b)
- Output q is (b ^ state) | (a & b)
We'll implement this as a simple state machine with combinational next-state and output logic.
