After carefully re-examining the waveform, I realize the behavior is more complex than initially thought. The circuit appears to be a state machine where:

1. The state flips when (a & b) is true (as before)
2. Output q is 1 in two cases:
   - When (a & b) is true (regardless of state)
   - When state is 1 and (a | b) is true
3. Otherwise, q is 0

This suggests we can implement it with:
1. A flip-flop for state that toggles on (a & b)
2. Combinational logic for q that combines (a & b) with (state & (a | b))
3. All sequential logic triggered on positive clock edge
