Novel Architecture Idea:
1. State Storage:
   - Use a T-flip-flop with enable instead of standard DFF
   - Toggle state only when (a & b) | (~a & ~b) (XOR condition)
   - This reduces switching activity compared to standard approach

2. Output Logic:
   - Implement q as (a & ~b & state) | (~a & b & ~state) | (a & b)
   - This creates a priority encoder-like behavior that matches waveforms
   - Reduces unnecessary toggles in the output path

3. Power Optimization:
   - The T-flip-flop only toggles when needed (11 or 00 inputs)
   - Output logic gates are minimized by the priority structure
   - No unnecessary intermediate signals

4. Timing Benefits:
   - Parallel evaluation of output conditions
   - Shorter critical path than XOR chain in some technologies
   - Better glitch suppression due to priority encoding
