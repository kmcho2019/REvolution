After carefully analyzing the waveform, I realize the circuit behaves as follows:
1. The state flip-flop toggles when both a and b are high (a & b)
2. The q output is high when:
   - b is high and state is low (b & ~state), OR
   - a is high and b is low (a & ~b)
This can be implemented more cleanly by:
1. Using a simple toggle flip-flop for state that only toggles when (a & b)
2. Implementing q as purely combinational logic based on current inputs and state
3. Removing unnecessary next_state logic since we only need to toggle
