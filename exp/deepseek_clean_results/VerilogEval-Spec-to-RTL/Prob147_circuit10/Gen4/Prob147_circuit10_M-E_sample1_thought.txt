After carefully re-examining the waveform, I notice a more complex pattern:
1. The state behaves like a toggle flip-flop that toggles when (a & b) is true
2. The output q is high when either:
   - b is high and state is low (b & ~state), or
   - b is low and state is high (~b & state)
3. Essentially, q is the XOR of b and state, but the state toggles only when both a and b are high

This suggests a different implementation:
- State toggles only when both a and b are 1 at the clock edge
- Output q is simply the XOR of b and current state
This creates a more interesting interaction between inputs and state.
