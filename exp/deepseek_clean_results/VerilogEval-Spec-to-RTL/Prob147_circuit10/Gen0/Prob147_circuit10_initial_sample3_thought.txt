Analyzing the waveform, we can observe that:
1. The state (flip-flop output) changes only when a=1 and b=1 (at 95ns)
2. The q output is 1 when either:
   - b is 1 and state is 0 (at 45ns)
   - a is 0 and state is 1 (at 75ns, 135ns)
3. Otherwise q is 0

This suggests:
- The flip-flop toggles only when both a and b are 1 (AND condition)
- q is driven by combinational logic: (b & ~state) | (~a & state)
