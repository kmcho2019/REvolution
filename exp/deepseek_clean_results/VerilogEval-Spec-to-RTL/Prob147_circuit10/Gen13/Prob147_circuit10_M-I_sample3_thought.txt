The key insights are:
1. State transition logic is correct (matches waveform):
   - Set to 1 when a=1 and b=1
   - Reset to 0 when a=0 and b=0
   - Otherwise maintains current state

2. The output q has a different relationship:
   - When state=0: q is the OR of a and b (a | b)
   - When state=1: q is the AND of a and b (a & b)

This matches all cases in the waveform:
- At 45ns: state=0 → q=0|1=1 (matches)
- At 55ns: state=0 → q=1|0=1 (matches)
- At 65ns: state=0 → q=1|1=1 (but waveform shows 0 - discrepancy)
  
Wait, this doesn't match all cases. Looking deeper:

Final correct pattern:
q = (state ? (a ~^ b) : (a ^ b))  // ~^ is XNOR, ^ is XOR
This matches all cases perfectly:
- At 45ns: state=0 → 0^1=1 (matches)
- At 55ns: state=0 → 1^0=1 (matches)
- At 65ns: state=0 → 1^1=0 (matches)
- At 75ns: state=1 → 0~^0=1 (matches)

- At 115ns: state=1 → 1~^0=0 (matches)
