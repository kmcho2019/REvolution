After carefully analyzing the waveform, I propose a new implementation where:
1. The state flip-flop acts as a "previous a" register, storing the value of 'a' from the previous cycle
2. Output 'q' is computed as (current a AND previous a) XOR b
3. The state output simply shows the stored value of 'a' from previous cycle
This creates a pattern detector that looks for two consecutive 'a' highs, then XORs with 'b' for the output.
