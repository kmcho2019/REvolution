To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Flatten the hierarchy**: Remove the separate XNOR2 and XOR2 modules and implement the logic directly in TopModule. This eliminates module instantiation overhead and reduces the critical path.

2. **Single continuous assignment**: Combine both operations (XNOR followed by XOR) into a single assign statement. This reduces intermediate switching activity and simplifies the logic.

3. **Eliminate intermediate wire**: Remove the xnor_out wire by directly using the XNOR result in the XOR operation, which reduces power consumption from unnecessary signal transitions.

This approach should maintain the same functionality while improving power efficiency and potentially reducing area by eliminating module boundaries and intermediate signals.
