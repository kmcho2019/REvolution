The best solution should combine the clarity of hierarchical design with the efficiency of direct implementation. Here's the fusion strategy:

1. Maintain the logical clarity by keeping the XNOR and XOR operations conceptually separate
2. Improve PPA by implementing the logic directly in the TopModule without submodules
3. Use a single continuous assignment to reduce power by minimizing intermediate switching
4. Keep the implementation clean and readable while optimizing for performance

This approach eliminates module instantiation overhead while maintaining the logical structure of the design. The single assign statement allows the synthesizer to optimize the gate-level implementation while clearly showing the XNOR followed by XOR operation.
