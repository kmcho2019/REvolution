Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with sub-modules to explicitly represent the logical components:
1. Create separate modules for XNOR and XOR operations
2. Instantiate these modules in the TopModule
3. Use an internal wire to connect the XNOR output to the XOR input
4. This approach makes the design more modular and easier to debug/analyze
5. While slightly more verbose, it clearly shows the logical structure and could be beneficial for more complex designs
