To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we should:
1. Eliminate the hierarchical modules (XNOR2 and XOR2) and implement the logic directly in TopModule
2. Combine both operations into a single assign statement to reduce intermediate signal switching
3. Let the synthesis tool optimize the combined expression for better area/power
This approach reduces module instantiation overhead and gives the synthesizer more flexibility to optimize the gate-level implementation.
