Here's a novel approach that implements the AND gate functionality using:
1. A continuous assign statement for out_assign (standard approach)
2. A more complex always block implementation that:
   - Uses a temporary variable to store the AND result
   - Implements the logic through a case statement for demonstration purposes
   - Shows alternative ways to express the same logic
This demonstrates that there are multiple ways to implement even simple logic in Verilog while maintaining the same functionality.
