This novel approach implements the 8-bit 2-to-1 mux using a hierarchical tree of smaller muxes:
1. First splits the 8-bit inputs into two 4-bit halves
2. Uses two 4-bit 2-to-1 muxes to select between the upper and lower halves
3. Then combines the results using another 4-bit 2-to-1 mux
4. This architecture demonstrates a divide-and-conquer approach that could be beneficial for timing optimization in larger designs
5. The output is properly maintained as 8-bit wide
