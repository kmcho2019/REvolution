Here's a novel approach using a bitwise multiplexer implemented with XOR gates, which can be more efficient in some technologies. The key ideas are:
1. Use XOR-based mux implementation: (a & ~mask) | (b & mask) where mask is sel replicated 8 times
2. Avoid sequential elements (registers) for pure combinational logic
3. Handle undefined sel by propagating 'x' without forcing to 0
4. Use bitwise operations that may map well to FPGA LUTs or ASIC standard cells
5. The solution remains purely combinational with no latches
