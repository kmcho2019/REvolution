Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with bit-sliced 2-to-1 muxes:
1. Instead of a single operation on the full 8-bit vectors, we'll instantiate 8 individual 1-bit muxes
2. Each mux will select between corresponding bits of 'a' and 'b' based on 'sel'
3. This approach demonstrates modular design and could be more easily extended to pipelined implementations
4. The output is properly maintained as 8-bit wide
