Here's a novel approach using a different architectural style - a bit-sliced implementation where we:

1. Break down the 8-bit mux into 8 individual 1-bit muxes
2. Use generate-for to create the bit-sliced structure
3. Each bit slice gets its own dedicated mux logic
4. This approach:
   - Makes the design more modular and scalable
   - Could have better timing characteristics in some technologies
   - Explicitly shows the parallel nature of the operation
   - Allows for potential partial reconfiguration
   - Makes debugging individual bits easier
   - Could be extended to more complex per-bit operations
