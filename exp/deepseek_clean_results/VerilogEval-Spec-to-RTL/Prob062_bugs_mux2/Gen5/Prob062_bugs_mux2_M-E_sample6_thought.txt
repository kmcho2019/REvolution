Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with 8 individual 1-bit muxes:
1. Instead of operating on the entire 8-bit bus at once, we'll instantiate 8 single-bit muxes
2. Each mux will select between corresponding bits of a and b based on the sel signal
3. This approach provides better modularity and makes the design more scalable
4. Each mux uses a simple ternary operator for clean selection logic
5. The hierarchical structure makes it easier to debug individual bit paths
