To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Recognize that the LFSR can be implemented with a single concatenation operation
2. The feedback taps are at positions 5 (MSB) and 3 (q[2])
3. The next state can be computed as:
   - MSB (q[4]) becomes q[0]
   - q[3] shifts normally from old q[4]
   - q[2] is the XOR of old q[3] and q[0]
   - q[1] shifts normally from old q[2]
   - q[0] shifts normally from old q[1]
4. This reduces to a compact concatenation with embedded XOR
5. Reset initializes to 1 (5'b00001) as specified
