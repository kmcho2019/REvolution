To refactor the LFSR implementation while maintaining the same functionality:
1. Separate the combinational logic from sequential elements
2. Use continuous assignments (`assign`) for the next state calculation
3. Keep the synchronous reset behavior in a separate always block
4. The taps remain at positions 5 and 3 (q[4] and q[2] in 0-based)
5. The XOR operations are now in combinational logic
6. The register update remains synchronous with positive clock edge
This approach makes the design more modular and potentially easier to optimize during synthesis.
