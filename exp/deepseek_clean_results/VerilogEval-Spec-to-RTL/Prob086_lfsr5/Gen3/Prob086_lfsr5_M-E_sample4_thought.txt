To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-based), we'll use a different architectural approach:
1. Instead of individual bit assignments, we'll use a concatenation-based approach for the shift operation
2. The feedback will be calculated separately and injected into the appropriate tap positions
3. We'll use a temporary wire to compute the feedback (XOR of output bit q[0])
4. The shift operation will be:
   - Bits without taps shift right normally
   - Tap positions (q[4] and q[2]) XOR with the feedback
5. Reset will initialize to 5'b00001 as before
6. This approach makes the tap positions more explicit and easier to modify
