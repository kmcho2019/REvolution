To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Recognize that taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-based) mean we only need XOR at position 3 (q[2] in 0-based)
2. The MSB (q[4]) simply shifts into q[0] without XOR (position 5 is not actually a tap in Galois configuration)
3. All other bits shift right normally
4. Maintain synchronous reset to 1
5. Combine the feedback logic directly in the always block to eliminate separate combinational assignments
