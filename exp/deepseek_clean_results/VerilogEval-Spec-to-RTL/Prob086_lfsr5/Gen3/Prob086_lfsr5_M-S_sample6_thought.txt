To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Keep taps at positions 5 and 3 (0-based: bits 4 and 2)
2. For Galois LFSR:
   - All bits shift right by 1
   - Tapped bits (4 and 2) XOR with the output bit (q[0])
   - New MSB (bit 4) is simply the output bit (q[0])
3. Reset initializes to 1 (5'b00001)
4. Simplified logic:
   - q[4] = q[0]
   - q[3] = q[4]
   - q[2] = q[3] ^ q[0]
   - q[1] = q[2]
   - q[0] = q[1]
