Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization: The current implementation has a negative WNS (-0.17ns). To improve timing:
   - Move the XOR operation into the sequential block to potentially reduce combinational path length
   - This may allow better register retiming during synthesis

2. Power Optimization:
   - Maintain the same functionality but with potentially lower switching activity
   - Keep the synchronous reset minimal

3. Area Optimization:
   - The design is already minimal, but we can make it more readable while maintaining the same area

Key Changes:
- Move feedback logic into sequential block
- Maintain same Galois LFSR structure with taps at positions 5 and 3
- Keep synchronous reset to 5'b00001
- Simplify the structure while maintaining functionality
