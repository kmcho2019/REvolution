To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing):
1. Use 0-based indexing for Verilog implementation (positions 4 and 2)
2. For Galois LFSR, XOR the tap positions with the output bit (q[0]) during right shift
3. Non-tap positions simply shift right unchanged
4. The MSB (q[4]) gets filled with q[0] (feedback)
5. Implement synchronous reset to initialize to 1 (5'b00001)
6. This configuration will produce a maximal-length sequence of 31 states (2^5-1)
