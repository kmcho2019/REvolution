To simplify the LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Use direct assignment in the always block without intermediate signals
2. Implement the Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (which correspond to bits [4] and [2] in 0-based indexing)
3. Reset to 1 as specified (5'b00001)
4. The XOR operation is only needed for the tapped bits
5. All other bits simply shift right
6. This maintains maximum-length sequence while being minimal and clear
