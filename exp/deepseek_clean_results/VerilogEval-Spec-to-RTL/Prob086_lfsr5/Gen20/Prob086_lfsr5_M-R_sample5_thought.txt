Refactoring Strategy:
1. Separate the combinational logic (LFSR feedback) from sequential elements (register)
2. Use continuous assignments (assign) for the combinational part to make the feedback path explicit
3. Keep the synchronous reset behavior in a dedicated always block
4. This approach makes the design more modular and potentially improves synthesis optimization
5. The XOR operations remain the same but are now clearly separated from the register update

Benefits:
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Potential for better synthesis optimization as the feedback path is explicit
- Maintains identical functionality while presenting a different structural approach
- May improve readability by separating the LFSR logic from the register update
