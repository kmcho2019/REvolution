Alternative approach using left-shifting LFSR with different tap points:
1. Instead of right-shifting, implement a left-shifting LFSR for variety
2. Use taps at positions 0 and 2 (equivalent to right-shift taps 5 and 3 when considering bit ordering)
3. The feedback will be XOR of q[4] (MSB) with taps at q[0] and q[2]
4. Reset to 1 remains the same but shifts happen in opposite direction
5. This maintains maximal-length properties while demonstrating an alternative implementation
6. The XOR operations are consolidated into a single expression for potential optimization
