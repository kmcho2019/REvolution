Here's a novel approach using a parallel computation technique for the LFSR:

1. Instead of the traditional sequential shift-and-XOR operation, we'll pre-compute all next state bits in parallel
2. We'll use a combinational block to calculate the entire next state at once
3. The feedback is still based on taps at positions 5 and 3 (indices 4 and 2)
4. This approach may offer better timing characteristics as all next state bits are computed simultaneously rather than through a chain of dependencies
5. The reset initialization remains the same (5'b00001)
6. This method might be more amenable to pipelining if needed for higher clock frequencies
