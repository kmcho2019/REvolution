To refactor the LFSR implementation while maintaining the same functionality:
1. Use continuous assignments (assign statements) for combinational logic
2. Separate the next state logic from the register update
3. Keep the same tap positions (bits 4 and 2 in 0-based indexing)
4. Maintain synchronous reset behavior initializing to 1
5. This approach makes the design more modular by separating combinational and sequential logic
