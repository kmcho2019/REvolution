To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-indexed):
1. Understand that position 5 refers to the MSB (q[4] in 0-indexed) and position 3 refers to q[2]
2. The feedback polynomial is x^5 + x^3 + 1
3. In Galois configuration:
   - The MSB (q[4]) is XORed with the output bit (q[0])
   - Bit q[2] is XORed with the output bit (q[0])
   - Other bits simply shift right
4. Reset should initialize to 1 (00001)
5. The sequence will cycle through all 31 non-zero states (2^5-1)

The improved implementation will:
- Correctly identify tap positions as q[4] and q[2]
- Properly implement Galois feedback only on tapped bits
- Maintain simple right-shift for non-tapped bits
- Keep synchronous reset functionality
