To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing), we'll use a different architectural approach:

1. Instead of directly computing each bit's next state, we'll first calculate the feedback value (XOR of taps and LSB)
2. Then perform the shift operation while selectively applying the feedback to tap positions
3. The feedback polynomial for maximal length is x^5 + x^3 + 1
4. This translates to XORing bits q[4] (position 5) and q[2] (position 3) with q[0] (LSB)
5. The shift operation moves all bits right by 1, with the new MSB being the feedback value
6. Reset initializes to 1 (00001) as specified

This approach clearly separates the feedback calculation from the shifting operation, making the design more modular and easier to verify.
