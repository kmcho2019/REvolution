1. Convert to 0-based indexing: taps are at bits 4 and 2 (q[4] and q[2])
2. The Galois LFSR structure should:
   - XOR each tap bit with the output bit (q[0])
   - Shift right by 1 position
   - The new MSB (q[4]) becomes the XOR of q[0] with the original q[4]
   - Bit position 2 (q[2]) also gets XORed with q[0]
3. The correct feedback polynomial for maximal-length is: q[4] ^ q[2] ^ q[0]
