To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing), we need to:
1. Understand that Verilog uses 0-based indexing, so tap positions 5 and 3 correspond to q[4] and q[2] respectively
2. Implement the feedback mechanism where:
   - Each tap position XORs its current value with the LSB (q[0])
   - Non-tap positions simply shift right
3. Ensure the reset initializes to 1 (5'b00001) as specified
4. The feedback polynomial should be x^5 + x^3 + 1 for maximal length
5. The LSB (q[0]) is always shifted in from q[1], while other bits either shift or XOR based on tap positions
