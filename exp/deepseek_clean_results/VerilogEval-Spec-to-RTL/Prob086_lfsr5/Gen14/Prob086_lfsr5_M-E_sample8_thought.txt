Instead of the conventional bit-by-bit assignment, we can implement the LFSR using:
1. A combined XOR operation for all taps at once
2. A single right shift operation
3. A mask to selectively apply XOR only to tap positions
4. This approach may reduce logic depth by combining operations
5. The reset remains synchronous active-high setting to 1
6. The tap mask is 5'b10100 (positions 5 and 3, 1-based)
7. The feedback is still q[0], but applied through the mask
