To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. For a 5-bit LFSR, position 5 refers to the feedback input (not a register bit)
2. The correct tap positions for a 5-bit LFSR are bits 4 and 2 (0-based indexing)
3. The feedback polynomial should be: x^5 + x^3 + 1
4. In Galois configuration:
   - The MSB (q[4]) is XORed with q[0] and fed back
   - Bit q[2] is also XORed with q[0]
   - Other bits shift right normally
5. Reset should initialize to 1 (00001)
6. The sequence will cycle through all 31 non-zero states
