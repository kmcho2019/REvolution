To correctly implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. For a 5-bit LFSR, position 5 refers to the feedback input (not a register bit)
2. The correct polynomial for a 5-bit maximal-length LFSR is x^5 + x^3 + 1
3. In Galois configuration:
   - The feedback is XORed with the LSB (q[0])
   - Taps are at positions corresponding to the polynomial terms (positions 3 and 5)
   - For implementation:
     - XOR q[2] (position 3) with q[0] to get the new value for q[2]
     - XOR q[4] (position 5) with q[0] to get the new value for q[4]
     - Other bits shift right normally
4. On reset, initialize to 1 (00001)
5. This will generate the maximal sequence of 31 states
