To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3, I propose a different approach that clearly separates the feedback logic from the shifting operation:

1. Use a 5-bit register for the current state
2. Implement the feedback as a separate operation before the shift:
   - Calculate feedback as q[0] (LSB)
   - XOR the feedback with tapped bits (q[4] and q[2])
3. Perform the shift operation:
   - Right shift all bits by 1
   - Insert the feedback result at the MSB
4. Reset initializes to 1 (00001)
5. This approach makes the feedback and shift operations more explicit and modular

The key difference from the previous solution is that we first compute all the XOR operations needed for the tapped bits, then perform a clean shift operation, making the data flow more visible.
