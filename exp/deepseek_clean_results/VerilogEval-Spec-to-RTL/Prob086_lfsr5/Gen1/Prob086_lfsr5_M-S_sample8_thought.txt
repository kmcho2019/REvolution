To implement a correct 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. Use a 5-bit register for the state (q)
2. On each clock edge:
   - If reset is high, initialize to 1 (00001)
   - Else:
     - XOR q[0] with q[2] (tap at position 3) for feedback
     - Shift right and insert the feedback at MSB
     - Also XOR q[0] with q[4] (tap at position 5)
3. This creates a proper maximal-length sequence of 31 states
4. The implementation is simplified by combining the feedback operations
