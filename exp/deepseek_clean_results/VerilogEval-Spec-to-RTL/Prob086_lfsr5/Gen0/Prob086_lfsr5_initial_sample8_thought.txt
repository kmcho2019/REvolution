To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. We'll use a 5-bit register to store the current state (q)
2. On each clock cycle:
   - If reset is high, set q to 1 (00001)
   - Else, perform the Galois LFSR operation:
     - XOR q[4] (position 5) and q[2] (position 3) with q[0]
     - Shift all bits right by 1
     - The new MSB (q[4]) becomes the XOR of q[0] with the original q[4]
