To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3, we need to:
1. Use a 5-bit register to store the current state (q)
2. On each clock edge:
   - If reset is high, initialize q to 1 (00001)
   - Otherwise:
     - XOR q[4] (bit position 5) with q[0] (LSB) to get the new MSB
     - XOR q[2] (bit position 3) with q[0] to get the new q[2]
     - Shift all bits right by 1
     - The new MSB comes from the XOR of q[4] and q[0]
3. The taps are at positions 5 and 3 (note: for a 5-bit register, position 5 would be q[4] since we count from 0)
