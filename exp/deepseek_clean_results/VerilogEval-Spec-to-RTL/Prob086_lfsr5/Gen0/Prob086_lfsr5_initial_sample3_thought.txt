To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. We'll use a 5-bit register to store the current state (q)
2. On each clock edge:
   - If reset is active, set q to 1 (00001)
   - Otherwise, perform the Galois LFSR operation:
     * The new MSB (q[4]) is q[0] XOR q[2] (since taps are at positions 5 and 3, which correspond to bits 4 and 2 in 0-based indexing)
     * Other bits shift right (q[3] becomes q[4], etc.)
     * The LSB (q[0]) is discarded
