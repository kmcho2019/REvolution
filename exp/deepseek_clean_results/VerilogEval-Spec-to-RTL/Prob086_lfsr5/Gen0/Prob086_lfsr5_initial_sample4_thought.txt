To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at positions 5 and 3:
1. The LFSR will have 5 bits (q[4:0])
2. On each clock cycle:
   - The MSB (q[4]) is XORed with the LSB (q[0]) to form the feedback
   - Bit q[2] (position 3) is also XORed with q[0]
   - All bits shift right by 1
   - The feedback value becomes the new MSB
3. On reset, initialize the register to 5'b00001 (1)
4. The taps at positions 5 and 3 mean we XOR q[4] and q[2] with q[0]
