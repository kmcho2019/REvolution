To refactor the LFSR implementation while maintaining the same functionality:
1. Move the combinational logic to continuous assignments using 'assign'
2. Keep the sequential part in a separate always block
3. This separates combinational and sequential logic clearly
4. The feedback calculation becomes more explicit in the continuous assignment
5. Reset behavior remains identical (synchronous reset to 5'b00001)
6. The tap positions (5 and 3) are still XORed with q[0] as before
7. This approach may offer better readability and potential synthesis optimizations
