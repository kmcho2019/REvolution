To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Recognize that in a Galois LFSR, the feedback is applied directly to tapped bits during the shift
2. The new state can be computed in a single assignment:
   - MSB (q[4]) becomes q[0]
   - q[3] shifts normally (from old q[4])
   - q[2] is the XOR of old q[3] and q[0] (tap position)
   - q[1] shifts normally (from old q[2])
   - q[0] shifts normally (from old q[1])
3. This reduces to a single concatenation with one XOR operation
4. Reset still initializes to 1 (5'b00001)
