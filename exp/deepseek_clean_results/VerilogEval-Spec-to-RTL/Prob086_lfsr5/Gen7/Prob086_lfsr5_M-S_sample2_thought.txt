To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR:
1. Maintain the same basic structure with clock and reset
2. Keep the reset value as 5'b00001 (active high sync reset)
3. For the LFSR operation:
   - The taps are at positions 5 and 3 (1-based), which correspond to bits q[4] and q[2] (0-based)
   - Only these tap positions need XOR with q[0]
   - Other bits simply shift right
4. Can express the next state more concisely using a single concatenation
5. This maintains the maximal-length sequence (31 states) with minimal logic
