To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR:
1. Keep the same basic structure with clock and reset
2. Maintain reset value as 5'b00001 (active high sync reset)
3. For the LFSR operation:
   - Only need to XOR tap position 3 (q[2] in 0-based) with q[0]
   - The MSB (q[4]) is always fed by q[0]
   - Other bits simply shift right
4. This reduces the operation to a single XOR gate while maintaining maximal-length sequence
5. The feedback is simplified to just the MSB being q[0] and position 2 being XORed
