To simplify the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR implementation:
1. Maintain the same feedback polynomial (x^5 + x^3 + 1)
2. Instead of individual bit assignments, use a more compact concatenation approach
3. The key operations are:
   - Shift right (>> 1)
   - XOR operation at tap position 2 (q[2] ^ q[0])
4. Reset still initializes to 5'b00001
5. The simplified version reduces the number of explicit assignments while maintaining identical functionality

The simplification comes from using a single assignment with concatenation and XOR, rather than separate assignments for each bit.
