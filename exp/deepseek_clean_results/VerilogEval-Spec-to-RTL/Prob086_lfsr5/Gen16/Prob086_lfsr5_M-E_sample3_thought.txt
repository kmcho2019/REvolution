Instead of the traditional Galois LFSR structure, we can implement a Fibonacci LFSR that shifts in the opposite direction (left) while maintaining the same maximal-length sequence properties. This approach:
1. Uses left shifting instead of right shifting
2. Pre-computes the feedback term using all tap positions at once
3. Implements the same maximal-length sequence (2^5-1 states) but with different internal wiring
4. Maintains identical reset behavior and output sequence
5. The taps are still at positions 5 and 3 (now affecting bits 4 and 2 in 0-based indexing)
6. The feedback is XORed with the MSB before being inserted at the LSB position
