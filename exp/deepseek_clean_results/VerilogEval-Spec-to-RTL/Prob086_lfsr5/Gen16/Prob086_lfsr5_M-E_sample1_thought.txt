Instead of the traditional Galois LFSR structure, we can implement a Fibonacci LFSR that shifts left and uses pre-computed XOR logic. This approach:
1. Uses left shifting instead of right shifting
2. Pre-computes all XOR operations in parallel
3. Implements the same maximal-length sequence (2^5-1 states)
4. Maintains taps at equivalent positions (bit 0 and 2 for left shift)
5. Still resets to 1 but implements it differently
6. May offer better timing due to parallel XOR computation

The key innovation is using a left-shifting Fibonacci structure while maintaining the same sequence as the Galois version, potentially improving timing at the cost of slightly more XOR gates.
