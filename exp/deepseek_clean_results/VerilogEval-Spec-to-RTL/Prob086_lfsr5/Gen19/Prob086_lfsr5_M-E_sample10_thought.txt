Instead of the traditional bit-by-bit assignment approach, we can implement the LFSR using:
1. A single XOR operation for the feedback
2. A clever bit manipulation technique that combines shifting and XOR in one operation
3. Using a concatenation operation to form the new state
4. The feedback is calculated as (q[0] ? {q[3:0],1'b0} ^ 5'b00100 : {q[3:0],1'b0})
   - This effectively implements the tap at position 3 when q[0] is 1
5. Reset still initializes to 1, but the implementation is more compact
6. This approach may have better synthesis results as it combines operations

The key insight is that we can express the Galois LFSR operation as a conditional XOR with a mask when the LSB is 1, combined with the standard shift operation.
