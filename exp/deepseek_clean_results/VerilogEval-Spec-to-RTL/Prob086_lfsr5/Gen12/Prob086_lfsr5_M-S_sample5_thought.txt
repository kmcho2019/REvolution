To simplify the 5-bit Galois LFSR implementation while maintaining functionality:
1. Recognize that the taps are at positions 5 and 3 (bits q[4] and q[2] in 0-based indexing)
2. The feedback is solely based on q[0] (LSB)
3. Instead of assigning each bit individually, we can:
   - Use a concatenation for the standard shift right
   - Only modify the tapped bit (q[2]) with XOR operation
4. Maintain the same reset behavior (initialize to 5'b00001)
5. This reduces the code to just 2 operations: concatenation for shifting and XOR for feedback

The simplified version maintains identical functionality but is more concise and easier to understand.
