Innovative approach using a "mirrored" LFSR structure:
1. Instead of traditional right-shift Galois LFSR, implement a left-shift variant
2. Use equivalent tap positions (mirrored for left-shift): original taps at 5 and 3 become 1 and 3
3. XOR operations now happen with MSB (q[4]) instead of LSB
4. Reset still initializes to 1 (5'b00001)
5. New logic:
   - q[4] = q[3]
   - q[3] = q[2] ^ q[4]
   - q[2] = q[1]
   - q[1] = q[0] ^ q[4]
   - q[0] = q[4]
6. This maintains the same sequence length (31 states) but with different internal timing paths
7. Benefits: potentially better timing due to different critical path structure
