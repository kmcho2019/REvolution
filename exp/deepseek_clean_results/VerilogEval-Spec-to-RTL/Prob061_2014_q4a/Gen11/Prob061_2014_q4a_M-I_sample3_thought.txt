The original solution is functionally correct but can be optimized for power efficiency by adding clock gating when neither load (L) nor shift (E) operations are active. This prevents unnecessary clock toggles of the Q register when no operation is being performed. The improvement strategy is:

1. Maintain the same functionality and priority (load over shift)
2. Add clock gating condition when both L and E are low
3. Use a clock gating cell (integrated in modern synthesis tools) to reduce dynamic power
4. Keep the same timing characteristics since the critical path remains unchanged
5. The area impact will be minimal as clock gating cells are small
