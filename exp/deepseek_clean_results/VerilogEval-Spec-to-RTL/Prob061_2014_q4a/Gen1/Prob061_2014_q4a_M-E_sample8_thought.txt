This implementation uses a hierarchical design with sub-modules to separate concerns:
1. A control unit that generates the mux select signals based on E and L
2. A 2:1 mux for selecting between shift and load inputs
3. A D flip-flop with enable for the storage element
The control logic prioritizes load over shift, and only enables the flip-flop when there's an actual operation to perform (E or L is high), reducing power consumption.
