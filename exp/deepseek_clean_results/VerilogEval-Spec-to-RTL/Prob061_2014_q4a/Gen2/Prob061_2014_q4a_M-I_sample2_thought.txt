The original solution is functionally correct but can be optimized for power efficiency by adding clock gating when neither load (L) nor shift enable (E) is active. This reduces unnecessary clock toggling and dynamic power consumption. The improved design will:
1. Maintain all original functionality
2. Add clock gating when neither L nor E is asserted
3. Use a simple AND gate for clock gating to minimize area impact
4. Keep the same timing characteristics since the critical path remains unchanged
