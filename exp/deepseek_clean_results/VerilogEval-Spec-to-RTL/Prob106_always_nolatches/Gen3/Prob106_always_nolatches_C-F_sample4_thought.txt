The optimal fusion strategy combines the strengths of both examples while addressing their minor weaknesses:

1. From Example 1, we retain the hierarchical comparison (checking 0xe0 prefix first) which reduces power by avoiding unnecessary lower byte comparisons for non-extended keys.

2. From Example 2, we maintain the simple parallel structure for maximum timing efficiency, as all outputs can be determined simultaneously.

3. We optimize area by:
   - Sharing the extended key detection logic
   - Using 8-bit comparators instead of 16-bit where possible
   - Keeping the clean direct assignment structure

4. We ensure mutual exclusivity naturally through the design without needing explicit checks.

This approach provides:
- Better power efficiency than Example 2 (fewer comparisons)
- Similar timing to both examples (parallel paths)
- Potentially better area than either (shared prefix check)
- Maintained readability and simplicity
