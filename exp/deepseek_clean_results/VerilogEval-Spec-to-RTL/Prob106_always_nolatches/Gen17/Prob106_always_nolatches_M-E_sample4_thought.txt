Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical priority encoder structure:

1. Instead of checking all bits simultaneously, we'll use a hierarchical comparison:
   - First stage checks the high byte (0xE0) using a simple equality comparator
   - Second stage uses a priority encoder for the low byte to detect arrow keys

2. Key innovations:
   - Uses a thermometer-coded priority scheme for the low byte comparisons
   - Implements the output logic as a 4-bit one-hot decoder
   - Reduces comparison logic by leveraging the known prefix
   - Minimizes switching activity by checking the prefix first

3. Benefits:
   - Potentially lower power since invalid codes are rejected early
   - More regular structure that may synthesize more efficiently
   - Clear separation of prefix and keycode matching stages
   - Outputs remain mutually exclusive by design
