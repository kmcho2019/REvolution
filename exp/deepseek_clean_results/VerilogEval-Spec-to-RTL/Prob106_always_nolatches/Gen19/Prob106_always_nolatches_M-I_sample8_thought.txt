The current implementation works correctly but can be optimized further for PPA:
1. Remove the hierarchical `any_arrow` detection since it adds unnecessary logic and switching activity
2. Simplify output logic by directly assigning each output based on its condition
3. Maintain parallel comparison structure for timing efficiency
4. Since scancodes are mutually exclusive, no priority encoding is needed
5. This reduces both power (less switching) and area (simpler logic)
