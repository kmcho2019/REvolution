The current implementation uses XOR operations and a NOT gate to check for even parity. However, looking at the truth table, we can see that q is simply the XNOR of all inputs. The XNOR operation naturally gives 1 when there's an even number of 1s (which is exactly what even parity checking does). 

We can simplify this to a single XNOR operation of all inputs, which:
1. Combines all XOR and NOT operations into a single logical operation
2. Maintains the same functionality
3. Reduces gate count (from 5 gates to 1 multi-input XNOR)
4. Keeps the balanced tree structure for timing
