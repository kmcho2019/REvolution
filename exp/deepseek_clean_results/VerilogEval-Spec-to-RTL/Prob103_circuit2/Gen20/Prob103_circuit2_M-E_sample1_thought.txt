Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical parity calculation with intermediate results. Instead of a single XOR chain, we'll:
1. First compute parity of a,b and c,d separately
2. Then combine these intermediate parities
3. This creates a balanced tree structure that may have better timing characteristics in some technologies
4. The final output is the XNOR of these intermediate parities
This approach maintains the same functionality while potentially offering better timing paths in certain implementations.
