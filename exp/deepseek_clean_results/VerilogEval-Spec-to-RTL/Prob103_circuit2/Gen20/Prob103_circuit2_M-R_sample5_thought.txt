The original implementation uses a single XNOR operation to compute even parity. We can refactor this to use a more explicit structural approach that:
1. First computes the XOR of pairs of inputs (a^b and c^d)
2. Then XORs these intermediate results
3. Finally inverts to get the XNOR (even parity) result

This approach breaks down the operation into smaller steps while maintaining the same functionality. It may offer better readability and could potentially help with synthesis optimizations in some tools.
