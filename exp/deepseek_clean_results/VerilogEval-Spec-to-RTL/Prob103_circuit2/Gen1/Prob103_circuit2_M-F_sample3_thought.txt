After carefully analyzing the truth table again, I notice the output pattern doesn't follow simple XOR behavior. Instead, the output q is 1 in these specific cases:
1. When all inputs are 0 (0000)
2. When exactly two adjacent inputs are 1 (0011, 0110, 1001, 1100)
3. When all inputs are 1 (1111)

This can be implemented by:
1. Checking for all zeros case (NOR of all inputs)
2. Checking for adjacent pairs being equal (a==b and c==d, or b==c with a and d being opposite)
3. Checking for all ones case (AND of all inputs)
4. OR-ing all these conditions together
