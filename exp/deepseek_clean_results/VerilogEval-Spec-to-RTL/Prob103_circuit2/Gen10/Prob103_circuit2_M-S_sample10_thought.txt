After carefully analyzing the truth table, I notice that the output q is 1 when the number of 1s in the inputs is even (including zero). This is exactly the behavior of an XNOR function across all inputs. 

Simplified approach:
1. The XNOR function naturally gives 1 when there's an even number of 1s in its inputs
2. We can implement this directly with a single XNOR operation on all inputs
3. This reduces the logic to just one XNOR gate (which is more efficient than XOR + inverter)
