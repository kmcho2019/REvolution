The truth table reveals that the output q is 1 when there's an even number of 1s in the inputs (0, 2, or 4). This is exactly the behavior of an XOR parity checker. We can optimize the implementation by:

1. Using XOR operations to count parity instead of a full case statement
2. Chaining three XOR gates to compute the parity of all four inputs
3. Inverting the final result since q=1 for even parity
4. This reduces area from 8um² to approximately 3-4um² (3 XOR gates)
5. Maintains the same functionality while improving power and area
6. The XOR chain will have slightly better timing than the balanced mux structure
