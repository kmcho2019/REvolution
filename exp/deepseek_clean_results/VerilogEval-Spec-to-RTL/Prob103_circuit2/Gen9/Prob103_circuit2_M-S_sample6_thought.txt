After analyzing the truth table, I notice that the output q follows the pattern of an XOR function across all inputs. The output is 1 when there's an even number of 1s in the inputs (including zero), which is exactly what an XOR chain computes. This is simpler than using an adder and checking the LSB.

Simplified approach:
1. XOR all inputs together
2. The result will be 1 for odd parity and 0 for even parity
3. Since we want q=1 for even parity, we just need to invert the XOR result
4. This reduces the logic to just 3 XOR gates and 1 inverter
