The original implementation uses a single XOR chain with inversion for even parity detection. To refactor while maintaining the same functionality, we can:
1. Use a tree structure of XOR operations to reduce the critical path depth
2. Break down the logic into intermediate steps for better readability
3. Still use continuous assignments (assign) but with a more structured approach

The tree structure will first XOR a+b and c+d in parallel, then XOR those results, and finally invert. This reduces the critical path from 3 gate delays (serial XORs) to 2 gate delays (parallel then combine).
