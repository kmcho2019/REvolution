The current implementation works correctly but can be optimized for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area). Here's the improvement strategy:
1. Replace the adder-based implementation with XOR gates since parity checking is more efficiently done with XOR operations
2. XOR operations are naturally power-efficient and require fewer gates than adders
3. The XOR chain will have the same functionality (output 1 when even number of 1s) but with:
   - Lower power consumption (XOR gates are more power-efficient than adders)
   - Smaller area (XOR chain requires fewer gates than a 3-bit adder)
   - Same performance (still single-level combinational logic)
4. The implementation will be a simple cascade of XOR operations
