Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based design with explicit priority encoding. Instead of separate AND gates, we'll use a 2:1 mux to select between the two output states. The mux select line will be the vibrate_mode input, and the data inputs will be the ring signal (for motor) and its complement (for ringer). This ensures:
1. Only one output can be active at a time
2. The selection is made in a single decision point (the mux)
3. The design is easily extensible if more modes are added later
4. The mux implementation may offer better timing characteristics in some technologies
