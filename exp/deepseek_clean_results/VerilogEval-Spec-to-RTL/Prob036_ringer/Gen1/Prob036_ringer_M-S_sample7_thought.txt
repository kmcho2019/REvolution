The original solution is already quite minimal, using just two AND gates. However, we can make it slightly more straightforward by recognizing that the motor output is simply the AND of ring and vibrate_mode, while the ringer is the AND of ring and NOT vibrate_mode. This is already optimal in terms of gate count and logic complexity. The only potential simplification would be to make the assignments more explicit about their mutually exclusive nature, though this doesn't reduce actual hardware complexity.
