The simplest implementation directly maps the requirements to logic gates without any intermediate structures:

1. The ringer should be on when there's a ring AND we're NOT in vibrate mode
2. The motor should be on when there's a ring AND we ARE in vibrate mode
3. This can be implemented with just two AND gates:
   - One AND between ring and inverted vibrate_mode for ringer
   - One AND between ring and vibrate_mode for motor
4. This is the most minimal implementation possible that meets all requirements
