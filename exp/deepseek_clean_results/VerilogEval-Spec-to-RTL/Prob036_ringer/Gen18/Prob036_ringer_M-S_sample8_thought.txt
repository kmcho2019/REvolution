The solution can be simplified by recognizing that the motor output is simply the AND of ring and vibrate_mode, while the ringer output is the same as motor but with vibrate_mode inverted. This means we can compute motor first and then derive ringer from it using an XOR operation with vibrate_mode. This reduces the logic to one AND gate and one XOR gate, which is slightly more optimal than two AND gates in terms of gate count and potential area savings.
