1. Current implementation uses:
   - 1 inverter (for ~vibrate_mode)
   - 2 AND gates (for the final outputs)
2. We can eliminate the inverter by using NOR logic:
   - ringer = ~(~ring | vibrate_mode) = ring & ~vibrate_mode (same as original)
   - motor = ring & vibrate_mode (unchanged)
3. While this doesn't reduce gate count in standard cell libraries (where inverters are free), it might help in some FPGA implementations where LUTs can implement NOR more efficiently
