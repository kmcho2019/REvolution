Simplified Approach: Direct Combinational Logic
1. Remove unnecessary local parameters since they don't provide significant benefit in this simple case
2. Use direct Boolean expressions without any intermediate states or parameters
3. Implement the logic as simple AND operations that clearly show the conditions for each output
4. Maintain the mutual exclusivity of outputs through the logical structure
5. Keep the design purely combinational for maximum simplicity

Key Benefits:
- Fewer lines of code
- More direct implementation of the specification
- Same functionality with less overhead
- Still clearly readable due to simplicity
