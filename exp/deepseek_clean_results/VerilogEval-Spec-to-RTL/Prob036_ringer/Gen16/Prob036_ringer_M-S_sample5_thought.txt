The current solution can be further simplified by recognizing that ringer and motor are mutually exclusive outputs controlled by vibrate_mode when ring is active. We can:
1. Use a single multiplexer-like structure where vibrate_mode selects between activating ringer or motor
2. Only need one AND gate to check the ring condition
3. This reduces the logic to just 2 gates total (1 AND + 1 NOT or similar)
4. Maintain the exact same functionality but with simpler implementation
5. The outputs will still never be active simultaneously
