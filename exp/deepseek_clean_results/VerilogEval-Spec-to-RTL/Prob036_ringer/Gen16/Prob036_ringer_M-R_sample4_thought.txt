Refactoring Approach:
1. Replace the procedural always block with continuous assignments for simpler combinational logic
2. Use direct logical expressions to determine ringer and motor outputs
3. Maintain the same functionality where:
   - ringer activates when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=0
   - motor activates when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=1
4. Ensure mutual exclusivity by construction (motor is simply the inverse of ringer when ring is active)
5. This approach reduces the sequential logic overhead and makes the design more straightforward

Benefits:
- Eliminates procedural block overhead
- Clearer direct relationship between inputs and outputs
- Maintains same PPA characteristics (or potentially improves them)
- Easier to understand at a glance
