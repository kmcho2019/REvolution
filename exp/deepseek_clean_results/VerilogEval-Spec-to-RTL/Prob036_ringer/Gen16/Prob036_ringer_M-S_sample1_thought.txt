Simplified Approach: Direct Combinational Logic
1. Remove unnecessary local parameters since they don't provide significant benefit in this simple case
2. Use direct Boolean expressions without any intermediate states or parameters
3. Leverage the mutual exclusivity of outputs (ringer and motor) by deriving one from the other
4. Make the logic as straightforward as possible while maintaining all functionality

Key Benefits:
- Fewer lines of code
- More direct implementation of the specification
- Still maintains clear relationship between inputs and outputs
- No impact on performance or area
