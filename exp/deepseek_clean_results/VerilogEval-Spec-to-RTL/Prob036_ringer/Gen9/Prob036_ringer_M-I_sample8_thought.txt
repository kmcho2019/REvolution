The original solution is already optimal in terms of PPA (Power, Performance, Area) for this simple combinational logic. However, we can make one micro-optimization by:
1. Using continuous assignments instead of procedural blocks to potentially save a tiny amount of area (though the difference will be negligible for such a small design)
2. Removing the sequential logic structure (registers) since this is pure combinational logic
3. Maintaining the exact same functionality where:
   - motor is active when both ring and vibrate_mode are high
   - ringer is active when ring is high but vibrate_mode is low
   - both outputs are low when ring is low

This change might save a tiny amount of area by eliminating the need for procedural blocks and registers, though the impact will be minimal given the small size of the design.
