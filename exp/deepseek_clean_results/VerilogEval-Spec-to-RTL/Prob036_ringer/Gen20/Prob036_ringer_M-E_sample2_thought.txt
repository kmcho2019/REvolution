Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Implement a priority-encoded output selection mechanism
2. Use a 2-bit internal state representation to explicitly encode all possible output combinations
3. Apply a truth-table based approach with direct mapping from inputs to outputs
4. Utilize bitwise operations and masking to enforce mutual exclusion
5. Structure the logic to minimize gate delays by parallelizing output generation

Key Features:
- Internal state encoding makes the design intent more explicit
- Outputs are derived from a centralized decision point rather than separate paths
- Uses bitwise OR operations to combine conditions
- Explicit handling of all input combinations through a truth table approach
- Potential for better optimization during synthesis due to clear state representation
