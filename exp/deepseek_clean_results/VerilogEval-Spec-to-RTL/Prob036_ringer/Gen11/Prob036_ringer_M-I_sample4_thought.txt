The existing solution is already optimal in terms of PPA metrics (zero timing violations, minimal power consumption, and small area). However, we can make one minor improvement by using a single AND gate and an inverter for the ringer output, which might save a tiny amount of area in some technologies where inverters are cheaper than AND gates. The motor output still requires an AND gate. This change maintains the same functionality while potentially reducing area slightly.

The improved strategy:
1. Keep the motor output logic unchanged (requires one AND gate)
2. For the ringer output, use an inverted vibrate_mode signal combined with the ring signal
3. This reduces the total gate count from 2 AND gates to 1 AND gate + 1 inverter
