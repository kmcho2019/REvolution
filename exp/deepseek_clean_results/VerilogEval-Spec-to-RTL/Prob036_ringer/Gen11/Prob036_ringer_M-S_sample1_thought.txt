The solution can be further simplified by recognizing that the ringer and motor outputs are mutually exclusive and directly controlled by the vibrate_mode signal when ring is active. We can:
1. Eliminate the explicit NOT operation for ringer by swapping the terms in the AND operation
2. Maintain the same logical functionality with slightly simpler expressions
3. Keep the minimal gate count (still 2 AND gates)
4. Preserve all PPA advantages of the original solution
