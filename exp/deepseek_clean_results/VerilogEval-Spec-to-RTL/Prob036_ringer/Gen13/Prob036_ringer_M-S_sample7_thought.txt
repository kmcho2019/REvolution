The original solution is already minimal with just two AND gates. However, we can simplify further by recognizing that:
1. The outputs are mutually exclusive (only one can be active at a time)
2. The ringer is simply the ring signal when not in vibrate mode
3. The motor is the ring signal when in vibrate mode
4. This can be implemented with a single multiplexer structure where:
   - vibrate_mode selects between the two outputs
   - ring enables/disables both outputs

This reduces the logic to:
- ringer = ring AND (NOT vibrate_mode)
- motor = ring AND vibrate_mode
Which is exactly equivalent to a 2:1 mux controlled by vibrate_mode between ringer and motor, with ring as the enable.

The implementation remains just 2 gates (AND + NOT) but is conceptually simpler.
