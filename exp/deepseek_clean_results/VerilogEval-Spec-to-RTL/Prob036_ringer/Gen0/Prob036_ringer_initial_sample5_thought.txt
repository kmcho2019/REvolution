The problem requires a simple combinational logic circuit that controls either the ringer or vibration motor based on two inputs: ring and vibrate_mode. The logic is:
1. When ring is active (1), we need to activate either ringer or motor
2. If vibrate_mode is active (1), we activate motor (motor = 1)
3. If vibrate_mode is inactive (0), we activate ringer (ringer = 1)
4. We must ensure ringer and motor are never both active at the same time
