The problem requires a simple combinational logic circuit that controls either the ringer or vibration motor based on two inputs: ring and vibrate_mode. The logic is:
1. When ring is active (1), check vibrate_mode:
   - If vibrate_mode is 1, activate motor (1) and keep ringer off (0)
   - If vibrate_mode is 0, activate ringer (1) and keep motor off (0)
2. When ring is inactive (0), both outputs should be 0 regardless of vibrate_mode
