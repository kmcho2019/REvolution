The design needs to control two outputs (ringer and motor) based on two inputs (ring and vibrate_mode). The ringer should activate when ring is high and vibrate_mode is low, while the motor should activate when both ring and vibrate_mode are high. The outputs should never be active simultaneously. This can be implemented with simple combinational logic where:
- ringer = ring & ~vibrate_mode
- motor = ring & vibrate_mode
