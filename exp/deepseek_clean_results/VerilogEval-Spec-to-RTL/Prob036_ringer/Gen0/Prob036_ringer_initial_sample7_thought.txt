The design needs to control two outputs (ringer and motor) based on two inputs (ring and vibrate_mode). The ringer should activate when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=0, while the motor should activate when both ring=1 and vibrate_mode=1. The outputs should never be active simultaneously. This can be implemented with simple combinational logic where:
- ringer = ring AND NOT vibrate_mode
- motor = ring AND vibrate_mode
