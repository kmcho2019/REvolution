The existing solution is already optimal in terms of functionality and PPA metrics. However, we can make two micro-optimizations:

1. Split the combined assignment into separate assignments for each output to potentially reduce power by minimizing unnecessary switching activity between the outputs.

2. Use direct logical expressions instead of the ternary operator to make the implementation more explicit and potentially more synthesis-friendly for some tools.

These changes maintain the same functionality while potentially offering slight improvements in power and area in certain technology nodes or synthesis tools.
