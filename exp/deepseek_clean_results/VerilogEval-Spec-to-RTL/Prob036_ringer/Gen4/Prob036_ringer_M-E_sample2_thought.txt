Here's an alternative approach using a multiplexer-based design. Instead of directly computing the outputs with AND gates, we'll use the vibrate_mode signal to select between two possible configurations (ringer on/motor off or motor on/ringer off). This approach makes the control logic more explicit and could be more scalable if additional modes were to be added in the future. The multiplexer effectively routes the ring signal to either the ringer or motor output based on vibrate_mode.
