The simplest solution uses direct combinational logic with minimal gates. The key observations are:
1. The ringer should be active when ring=1 and vibrate_mode=0
2. The motor should be active when both ring=1 and vibrate_mode=1
3. The outputs are mutually exclusive by design since vibrate_mode can't be 0 and 1 simultaneously

This can be implemented with just two AND gates, making it both simple and efficient.
