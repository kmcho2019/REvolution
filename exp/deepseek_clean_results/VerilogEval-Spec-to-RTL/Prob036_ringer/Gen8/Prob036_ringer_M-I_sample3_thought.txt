The existing solution is already optimal in terms of PPA metrics, but we can consider a slight architectural improvement for better power efficiency. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive, we can implement them using a single multiplexer structure. This won't reduce gate count (still requires equivalent logic), but makes the design intent clearer and could potentially lead to better technology mapping in some synthesis tools. The key insight is that we're essentially selecting between two states (ringer or motor) based on vibrate_mode when ring is active.
