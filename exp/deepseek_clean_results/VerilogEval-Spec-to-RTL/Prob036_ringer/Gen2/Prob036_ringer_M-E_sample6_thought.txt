Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based design. Instead of directly computing the outputs with AND gates, we'll use the vibrate_mode signal to select between two configurations:
1) When vibrate_mode=0: ringer follows ring signal, motor is always off
2) When vibrate_mode=1: motor follows ring signal, ringer is always off

This approach centralizes the control logic into a single multiplexer decision, potentially reducing routing complexity and making the design more scalable if additional modes are needed in the future.
