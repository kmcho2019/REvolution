To implement a 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gate, we can use reduction operators in Verilog. These operators apply the specified operation across all bits of a vector:
1. &in will perform a bitwise AND of all bits in 'in'
2. |in will perform a bitwise OR of all bits in 'in'
3. ^in will perform a bitwise XOR of all bits in 'in'
This approach is efficient and concise for implementing wide-input logic gates.
