To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality:
1. Use Verilog's built-in reduction operators for all three operations (AND, OR, XOR)
2. Eliminate the generate blocks and intermediate wires since reduction operators handle this internally
3. Make the code more concise and readable by directly assigning the reduction operations to outputs
4. Keep the parameter for potential future width modifications
5. The reduction operators will automatically create efficient implementations during synthesis

Benefits:
- Much simpler and cleaner code
- Maintains full functionality
- Synthesis tools will optimize the reduction operations efficiently
- Easier to understand and maintain
