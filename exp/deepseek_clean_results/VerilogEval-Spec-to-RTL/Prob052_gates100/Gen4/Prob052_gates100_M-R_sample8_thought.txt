To improve the timing performance while maintaining the same functionality, I'll implement a tree structure for the logic operations. This approach:
1. Reduces the critical path depth from O(n) to O(log n)
2. Uses hierarchical reduction with intermediate stages
3. Maintains combinational behavior
4. Improves timing while keeping area and power efficient

For a 100-bit input, I'll use a 4-level binary tree structure (7 levels would be needed for full binary, but 4 levels gives good balance):
- Level 1: Process 25 groups of 4 bits
- Level 2: Process 7 groups (25 reduced to 7 groups of ~4)
- Level 3: Final reduction to 1 bit
- This reduces critical path from 100 to ~4 levels
