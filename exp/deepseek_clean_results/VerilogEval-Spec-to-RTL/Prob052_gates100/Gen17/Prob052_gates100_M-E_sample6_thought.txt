Here's a novel approach using hierarchical reduction with intermediate stages:
1. Divide the 100 inputs into groups of 10 (10 groups total)
2. First compute intermediate AND/OR/XOR results for each group
3. Then combine the intermediate results in a second stage
4. This creates a balanced two-level hierarchy instead of a linear chain
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path (from 100 to 20 levels max)
   - More balanced timing
   - Better power characteristics due to reduced glitching
   - Clear modular structure that's easy to scale
