Hierarchical Reduction Approach:
1. Instead of using direct reduction operators, implement a tree-structured hierarchical reduction
2. Break down the 100-bit operations into smaller chunks (10 groups of 10 bits)
3. First perform reduction operations on each 10-bit chunk
4. Then perform final reduction on the intermediate 10 results
5. This approach mimics how large gates might be physically implemented in hardware
6. Provides better scalability for very wide inputs and potential timing benefits

Key Features:
- Uses intermediate wires for hierarchical results
- Employs parameterized chunk size for flexibility
- Maintains same functionality while demonstrating alternative implementation
- Could potentially offer better timing characteristics in some technologies
