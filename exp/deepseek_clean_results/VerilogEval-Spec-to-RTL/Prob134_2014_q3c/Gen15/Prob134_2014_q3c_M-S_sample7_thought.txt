1. For Y0 (next state's LSB):
   - When x=0: Y0 is simply y[0] for all states except 000 (where it's 0)
   - When x=1: Y0 is ~y[0] for states 000 and 011, y[0] for others

2. For output z:
   - z is 1 only when y is 011 (3) or 100 (4)
   - This can be detected by checking if y[2] or (y[1] & y[0]) is true
