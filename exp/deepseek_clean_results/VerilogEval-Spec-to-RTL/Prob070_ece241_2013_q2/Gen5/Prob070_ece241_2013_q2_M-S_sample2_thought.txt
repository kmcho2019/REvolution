Simplified approach using direct combinational logic:
1. Analyze the truth table to derive minimal SOP and POS expressions
2. For out_sop (sum-of-products):
   - Output is 1 for inputs 2 (0010), 7 (0111), and 15 (1111)
   - SOP form: (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (~a & b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d)
3. For out_pos (product-of-sums):
   - Output is 0 for inputs where out_sop is 0 (invert the condition)
   - POS form can be derived by complementing the SOP of the inverted function
4. Implement both forms using simple assign statements
5. Benefits:
   - Eliminates the need for case statements and internal variables
   - Pure combinational logic implementation
   - More efficient in terms of gate-level implementation
   - Avoids procedural assignment issues
