To simplify the solution while maintaining correct functionality:

1. First clearly define the input mapping (assuming d is LSB):
   - 2: 0010 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0)
   - 7: 0111 (a=0,b=1,c=1,d=1)
   - 15: 1111 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1)

2. For SOP form (output 1 cases):
   - Directly implement the OR of the three valid input patterns

3. For POS form (output 0 cases):
   - Implement the AND of NOTs of all invalid patterns
   - Since there are many invalid cases, we can think of POS as NOT(SOP of invalid cases)

4. Remove intermediate wires since the expressions are simple enough without them
