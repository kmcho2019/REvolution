To implement the TopModule, we need to create two outputs:
1. out_sop: Minimum sum-of-products form for when the input represents 2, 7, or 15
2. out_pos: Minimum product-of-sums form for when the input represents 0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13, or 14

First, we'll create a truth table based on the given conditions. Then:
- For out_sop: We'll OR the minterms for 2, 7, and 15
- For out_pos: We'll AND the maxterms for the complement cases (which are 2,7,15)

The minterms are:
- 2: a'b'cd'
- 7: a'bcd
- 15: abcd

The maxterms are:
- out_pos = (a+b+c'+d)(a'+b+c+d)(a'+b'+c'+d')
