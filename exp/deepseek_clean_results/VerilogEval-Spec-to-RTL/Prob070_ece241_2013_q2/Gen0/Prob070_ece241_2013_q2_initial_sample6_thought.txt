2. For out_sop (sum-of-products), identify all minterms where output is 1 (2,7,15) and OR them together.
   - 2: a'b'cd'
   - 7: a'bcd
   - 15: abcd
3. For out_pos (product-of-sums), identify all maxterms where output is 0 (0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14) and AND them together in POS form.
   - Convert each maxterm to its POS representation (sum terms where output is 0).
4. Implement both forms in Verilog using continuous assignments.
