Refactored Design Strategy:

1. Truth Table Re-analysis:
   - Keep same minterms (2,7,15) and maxterms (0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14)
   - Don't-cares remain (3,8,11,12) but will be used more carefully

2. Improved SOP Form:
   - Original minterms: 2(~a~bc~d), 7(~abcd), 15(abcd)
   - Don't-care 3(~a~bcd) can help simplify term 2 to ~a~bc (covers both 2 and 3)
   - Don't-care 11(~ab~cd) doesn't help with 7, so keep original ~abcd term
   - Final SOP: ~a~bc + ~abcd + abcd

3. Improved POS Form:
   - Original maxterms cover all output=0 cases
   - Group maxterms by similar patterns:
     * 0,1: (a|b|c|d) & (a|b|c|~d)
     * 4,5: (a|~b|c)
     * 6: (a|~b|~c)
     * 9,10: (~a|b|d)
     * 13,14: (~a|~b|d)
   - No don't-cares used in POS to avoid incorrect coverage

4. Implementation Changes:
   - Use more precise SOP terms that don't over-cover
   - POS implementation uses exact maxterms without don't-care optimization
   - Both forms now strictly match required functionality
