Novel Approach: K-map Based Optimization with Complementary Outputs

1. Key Insight:
   - Instead of treating SOP and POS separately, recognize they are complementary forms of the same function
   - Use K-map visualization to simultaneously optimize both forms while ensuring consistency

2. K-map Analysis:
   - Create 4-variable K-map with inputs a,b,c,d
   - Mark 1's for outputs 2,7,15
   - Mark 0's for specified false cases
   - Leave don't-cares (3,8,11,12) as X for optimization

3. SOP Optimization:
   - Identify prime implicants covering all 1's:
     - Term for 2: ~a & ~b & c & ~d
     - Combined term for 7 and 15: b & c & d (since a is don't-care when b=1)
   - Final SOP: (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (b & c & d)

4. POS Optimization:
   - Work with 0's in K-map to find maximal groupings
   - Essential prime implicants for 0's:
     - (a | b | c)
     - (a | ~b | ~c | d)
     - (~a | b | ~c | ~d)
     - (~a | ~b | c | d)
   - These cover all specified 0 cases while properly handling don't-cares

5. Verification Strategy:
   - Ensure both forms produce identical outputs for all valid inputs
   - Confirm don't-cares don't affect specified cases
   - Cross-validate with edge cases

6. Implementation Benefits:
   - Minimal gate count for both forms
   - Clear separation of terms
   - Symmetrical handling of input conditions
