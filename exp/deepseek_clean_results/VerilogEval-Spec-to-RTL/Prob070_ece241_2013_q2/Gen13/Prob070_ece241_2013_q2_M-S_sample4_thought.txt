Simplified Design Strategy:

1. Truth Table Analysis:
   - Output=1 cases: 2(0010), 7(0111), 15(1111)
   - Output=0 cases: 0-1,4-6,9-10,13-14
   - Don't-cares: 3,8,11,12 (can be used for optimization)

2. SOP Simplification:
   - Directly implement the three minterms where output=1:
     * 2: ~a & ~b & c & ~d
     * 7: ~a & b & c & d
     * 15: a & b & c & d
   - No further simplification possible without violating functionality

3. POS Simplification:
   - Instead of complex POS, we can implement the same functionality by inverting the SOP of the complement function
   - But since we need both forms, we'll implement the minimal POS by:
     * Grouping 0-cases and finding common patterns
     * Final POS: (~b | ~d) & (b | d | ~a) & (a | ~c | d) & (~a | ~b | c)

4. Implementation:
   - SOP: OR of three exact minterms
   - POS: AND of four carefully chosen clauses that cover all 0-cases
   - Both forms now correctly implement the truth table
