Refactored design approach:

1. For out_sop (Sum-of-Products):
   - Directly implement the minimal SOP expression covering required outputs (2,7,15)
   - The correct minimal SOP is: (b & c & d) | (a & b & c & d) | (~a & b & ~c & d)
   - This covers:
     - 7 (0111) with (b & c & d)
     - 15 (1111) with (a & b & c & d)
     - 2 (0010) with (~a & b & ~c & d)

2. For out_pos (Product-of-Sums):
   - Implement the minimal POS expression that excludes prohibited outputs
   - The correct minimal POS is: (b + d)(a' + b + c)(a + b' + d')
   - This excludes all prohibited cases while allowing required outputs
