Novel approach using a hybrid LUT/decoder design:

1. Key Insight:
   - Instead of traditional SOP/POS implementations, use a more efficient lookup-table approach that leverages the specific input conditions
   - Since we know certain input combinations (3,8,11,12) never occur, we can treat them as don't-cares for optimization

2. Optimized SOP:
   - Notice that all true cases (2,7,15) have c=1 and d=1 for 7/15
   - Can factor out common terms: (~a & ~b & c & ~d) OR (c & d & (~a & b OR a & b))
   - This reduces to: (~a & ~b & c & ~d) | (c & d & b)

3. Optimized POS:
   - Observe that output is 0 when either:
     - c=0 (for 0,1,4,5,6) OR
     - a=1 with specific patterns (9,10,13,14)
   - Can be expressed as: (c) & (a | patterns)
   - Further refined by combining terms

4. Implementation:
   - Use these optimized Boolean expressions
   - Takes advantage of don't-care conditions for minimization
   - Results in significantly simpler logic than original solution
