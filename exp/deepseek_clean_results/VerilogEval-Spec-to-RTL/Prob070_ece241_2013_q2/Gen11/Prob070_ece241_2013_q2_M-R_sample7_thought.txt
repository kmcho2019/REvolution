Refactored approach using minimal Boolean expressions:

1. For out_sop (Sum of Products):
   - Identify minterms where output is 1 (2,7,15)
   - Convert to canonical SOP: (¬a¬bc¬d) + (¬abcd) + (abcd)
   - Minimize using Boolean algebra/K-map:
     - 2 (0010) cannot be combined
     - 7 (0111) and 15 (1111) share abc → d can be don't-care
     - Final SOP: (¬a¬bc¬d) + (abc)

2. For out_pos (Product of Sums):
   - Identify maxterms where output is 0 (0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14)
   - Convert to canonical POS and minimize:
     - Group adjacent zeros in K-map
     - Final POS: (a+b+c)(a+¬b+c)(¬a+b+¬c)

3. Implementation:
   - Use continuous assignments (assign) for combinational logic
   - Explicitly show the minimized forms
   - Don't-cares are naturally optimized in the expressions

Advantages:
  - Matches exact minimal forms required
  - More efficient than case statement
  - Clearly shows the logic optimization
  - Properly utilizes don't-care conditions
