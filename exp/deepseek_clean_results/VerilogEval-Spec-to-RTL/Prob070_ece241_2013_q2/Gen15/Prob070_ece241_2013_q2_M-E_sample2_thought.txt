Novel Architectural Idea: "Pattern Matching with Shared Sub-expressions"

1. Key Insight:
   - Instead of traditional SOP/POS implementations, recognize that all output=1 cases (2,7,15) share common patterns:
     * 2 (0010) and 7 (0111) share ~a
     * 7 (0111) and 15 (1111) share b & c & d
   - This suggests we can build the logic by combining these shared patterns

2. Implementation Strategy:
   - Create intermediate signals for shared sub-expressions
   - For SOP: Combine patterns using OR of the following:
     * Pattern for 2 (unique to this case)
     * Shared pattern between 7 and 15
   - For POS: Derive by inverting the complement function built from:
     * Common characteristics of output=0 cases
     * Specifically target cases where either:
       - a & ~b (covers 8,9,10,11,12,13,14)
       - ~a & ~c (covers 0,1,4,5)

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced gate count by sharing sub-expressions
   - Natural handling of don't-cares through pattern overlaps
   - More efficient than traditional minterm/maxterm approaches
   - Clear logical structure that's easy to verify
