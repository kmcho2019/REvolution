Simplified design approach:

1. For out_sop (Sum of Products):
   - Identify that minterms 2 (0010), 7 (0111), and 15 (1111) can be simplified by observing patterns:
     - 2: c is high while others are low
     - 7: a is low while b,c,d are high
     - 15: all inputs are high
   - This allows us to express the logic without full pattern matching

2. For out_pos (Product of Sums):
   - Instead of checking all 0-output cases, recognize that the output should be 1 unless:
     - All inputs are 0 (0)
     - Only d is 1 (1)
     - b is 1 without a or c (4,5,6)
     - a is 1 without b or with specific c,d patterns (9,10,13,14)
   - This can be expressed more concisely using logical combinations

3. Key optimizations:
   - Eliminate full pattern matching in favor of simpler logical expressions
   - Leverage don't-care conditions to minimize logic
   - Use more fundamental logic operations for cleaner implementation
