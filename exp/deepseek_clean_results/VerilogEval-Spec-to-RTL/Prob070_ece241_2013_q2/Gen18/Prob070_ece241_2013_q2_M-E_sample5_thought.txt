Novel Design Strategy:

1. Truth Table Reinterpretation:
   - Instead of traditional SOP/POS, use a hierarchical decision structure
   - First identify critical distinguishing features:
     * For output=1 cases (2,7,15), c is always 1
     * For output=0 cases, c is sometimes 0 (0,1,4,5,6,9,10,13,14)
     * When c=1, need additional checks to distinguish between 1/0 outputs

2. Hierarchical Logic:
   - Primary gate: AND of c with secondary logic
   - Secondary logic:
     * For 2: ~a & ~b & ~d
     * For 7: ~a & b & d
     * For 15: a & b & d
   - Combine these with OR for SOP
   - For POS, implement as NOR of inverted conditions

3. Implementation Benefits:
   - Clear structural correspondence to truth table
   - Easy to verify each condition separately
   - Natural handling of don't-care cases
   - Efficient gate-level implementation

4. Verification:
   - Each output=1 case has unique path through logic
   - All output=0 cases naturally fall through
   - Don't-cares don't affect functionality
