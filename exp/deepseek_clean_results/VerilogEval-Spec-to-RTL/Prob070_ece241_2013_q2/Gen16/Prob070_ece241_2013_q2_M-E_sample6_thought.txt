Novel architectural idea: Instead of traditional SOP and POS implementations, we'll use a hybrid approach that combines:
1. A priority encoder for the output=1 cases (2,7,15)
2. A zero detector for the output=0 cases
3. A don't-care handler that defaults to 0 for unspecified cases

Key features:
- Uses hierarchical logic with intermediate signals
- Implements truth table through pattern matching
- Explicitly handles don't-care conditions
- More scalable for larger truth tables
- Clear separation of 1-output and 0-output logic paths

Logic implementation:
1. For out_sop: Pattern match the exact minterms (2,7,15)
2. For out_pos: Implement as the complement of the SOP terms plus explicit 0-output cases
3. Use intermediate signals for better readability and optimization
