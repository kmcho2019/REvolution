Fusion Strategy: Combining Parallel Computation with Explicit Readability

1. Key Insights from Examples:
   - Example 1's vectorized approach offers excellent parallel computation and potential synthesis optimization
   - Example 2's direct assignments provide superior readability and maintainability
   - Both achieve identical PPA metrics, suggesting the synthesis tool optimizes them equivalently

2. Optimal Fusion Approach:
   - Use vectorized computation for closely related operations (AND/NAND, OR/NOR, XOR/XNOR)
   - Keep separate direct assignments for operations that don't naturally pair (AND-NOT)
   - Maintain all operations in parallel for best timing
   - Group related operations together for better code organization

3. Advantages:
   - Preserves the parallel computation benefits of Example 1
   - Improves readability by separating the unique AND-NOT operation
   - Makes logical relationships between operations more visible
   - Maintains identical PPA characteristics while being more maintainable
   - Easier to modify/extend than pure vectorized approach
