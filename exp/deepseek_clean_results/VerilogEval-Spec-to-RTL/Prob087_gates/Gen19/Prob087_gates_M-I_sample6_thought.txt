Improvement Strategy:
1. Common Subexpression Elimination:
   - Identify and reuse intermediate results (a&b and a|b) that are used multiple times
   - This reduces redundant logic and saves area

2. Output Derivation:
   - Compute XNOR from XOR (one less inverter)
   - Compute NAND from AND (one inverter)
   - Compute NOR from OR (one inverter)

3. Optimized Structure:
   - Maintain parallel computation for critical paths
   - Use minimal number of logic operations
   - Keep the vectorized assignment for clarity

Expected Benefits:
- Reduced area by eliminating redundant logic
- Maintained or improved timing (still single-level logic depth)
- Slightly reduced power due to fewer gates switching
