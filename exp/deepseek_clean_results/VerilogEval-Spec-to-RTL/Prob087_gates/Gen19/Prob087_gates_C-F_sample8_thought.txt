Fusion Strategy: Optimal Readability with Efficient Implementation

1. Key Insights:
   - Example 1's vectorized assignments efficiently group complementary operations (AND/NAND, OR/NOR, XOR/XNOR)
   - Example 2's direct assignments with derived outputs (nand from and) improve readability and show logical relationships
   - Both achieve identical PPA metrics, so we can optimize for maintainability

2. Optimal Fusion:
   - Use vectorized assignments for complementary operations to show their relationship clearly
   - Keep direct assignment for the unique AND-NOT operation for clarity
   - Group related operations together for better code organization
   - Add descriptive comments to explain each operation

3. Advantages:
   - Maintains excellent PPA characteristics (0 timing violations, minimal power/area)
   - Shows logical relationships between complementary operations
   - More readable than pure vectorized approach
   - Easier to modify and maintain than separate assignments
   - Clear organization of related operations
