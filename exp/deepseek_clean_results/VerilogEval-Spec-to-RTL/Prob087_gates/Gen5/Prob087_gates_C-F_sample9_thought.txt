The fusion strategy combines the best aspects of both examples while maintaining optimal PPA metrics:

1. From Example 1: Leverage logical relationships between outputs to reduce redundant operations (using inversions of base outputs for complementary gates)
2. From Example 2: Maintain clarity and directness for operations that don't benefit from sharing (like out_anotb)
3. Additional optimization: Precompute ~b once to potentially save one inverter in the technology mapping

- out_nand derived from out_and (saves one AND operation)
- out_nor derived from out_or (saves one OR operation) 
- out_xnor derived from out_xor (saves one XOR operation)
- ~b computed once for both out_xnor and out_anotb
