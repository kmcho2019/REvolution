2. Inverted Signal Optimization:
   - Compute 'not_b' only once (from both examples) and use it for both out_anotb and out_nor/out_nand when beneficial
   - This minimizes power consumption by reducing switching activity

3. Direct Assignments:
   - Use direct assignments for complementary operations (from Example 2)
   - This maintains clarity while being synthesis-friendly

4. Logical Grouping:
   - Group related operations together (from Example 2)
   - Basic operations first, then their complements, then special cases
