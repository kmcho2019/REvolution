Improvement Strategy:
1. Maintain all required logic functions while optimizing for area
2. Leverage shared intermediate signals to reduce redundant logic:
   - a_and_b = a & b is used for both out_and and out_nand
   - a_or_b = a | b is used for both out_or and out_nor
   - a_xor_b = a ^ b is used for both out_xor and out_xnor
3. Use inverted versions of these shared signals where possible
4. Keep the anotb output separate as it doesn't share logic with others
5. This reduces the number of distinct logic operations from 7 to 4 (plus inversions)

Expected Benefits:
- Small area reduction by eliminating redundant AND/OR/XOR operations
- Same power characteristics since the same number of gate operations occur
- No impact on timing (still purely combinational)
- Improved readability by showing logical relationships between outputs
