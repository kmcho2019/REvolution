Refactoring Strategy:
1. Maintain the same functionality but reorganize the implementation
2. Use direct gate-level primitive instantiations instead of assign statements
3. Group outputs by their logical relationship (positive logic first, then complemented)
4. Use explicit gate primitives for better readability and potential synthesis optimization
5. Keep the same interface and combinational nature

Key Changes:
- Replace assign statements with Verilog gate primitives (and, or, etc.)
- Group basic gates together (AND, OR, XOR)
- Group complemented gates together (NAND, NOR, XNOR)
- Implement AND-NOT as a separate primitive
- Maintain same logical functionality but with different structural implementation
