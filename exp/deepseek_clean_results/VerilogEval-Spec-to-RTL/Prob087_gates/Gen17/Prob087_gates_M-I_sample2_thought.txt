Improvement Strategy: Common Subexpression Elimination and Output Derivation
1. Key Observations:
   - Several outputs share common subexpressions (AND, OR, XOR)
   - Some outputs can be derived from others (XNOR from XOR, NAND from AND)
   - This reduces total gate count and area

2. Optimizations:
   - Compute AND, OR, XOR first and store as intermediate wires
   - Derive NAND, NOR, XNOR from these intermediates
   - Maintain separate computation for A-AND-NOT-B as it's unique
   - This reduces the number of primitive operations from 7 to 4

3. Expected Benefits:
   - Reduced area by eliminating redundant logic
   - Same performance (still single-level combinational logic)
   - Slightly lower power due to fewer gates switching
   - Maintains clean, readable structure
