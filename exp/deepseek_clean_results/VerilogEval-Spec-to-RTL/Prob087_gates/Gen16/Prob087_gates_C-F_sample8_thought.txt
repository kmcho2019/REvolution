The optimal fusion strategy combines the clarity and directness of Example 1 with the power optimization of Example 2, while further improving area efficiency:

1. Maintain direct input-to-output assignments for clarity (from Example 1)
2. Introduce shared inverted signals only where they provide clear benefits (from Example 2)
3. Optimize area by:

   - Using the same AND gate for both out_and and out_nand
4. Group operations logically for better readability
5. Keep all paths single-gate delay for maximum performance
6. Use most efficient implementation for each operation (e.g., direct AND-NOT)

- Fewer total gates by sharing inversion where possible
- Clearer logical grouping than Example 2
- More area-efficient than Example 1
- Maintains all performance benefits
- Better power profile than Example 1
