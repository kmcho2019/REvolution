The original solution is already optimal in terms of functionality and basic PPA metrics. However, we can make two minor improvements:

1. Group related operations to potentially help synthesis tools optimize shared logic (like computing AND first, then using it for NAND)
2. Use parentheses for clarity in compound operations (though synthesis would optimize this anyway)
