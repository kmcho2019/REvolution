The current implementation is already optimal in terms of timing with single-level gate operations. However, we can optimize for power and area by:
1. Sharing common sub-expressions to reduce redundant computations
2. Using intermediate signals to avoid recalculating the same operations
3. Maintaining the same logic depth while reducing gate count

Key optimizations:
- Compute 'a_and_b' once and reuse for out_and and out_nand
- Compute 'a_or_b' once and reuse for out_or and out_nor
- Compute 'a_xor_b' once and reuse for out_xor and out_xnor
- Compute 'not_b' once and reuse for out_anotb and potentially other operations
This reduces the total number of gate operations from 7 to 5 while maintaining the same functionality.
