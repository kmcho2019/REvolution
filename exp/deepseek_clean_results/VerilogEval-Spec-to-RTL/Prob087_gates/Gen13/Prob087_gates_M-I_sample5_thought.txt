The current implementation is correct but can be optimized for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) by:

1. Logic Sharing: Since some outputs are inversions of others (nand vs and, nor vs or, xnor vs xor), we can share these operations to reduce gate count and area.

2. Direct Implementation: For operations that are naturally inverted (like nand, nor), we'll use their native Verilog operators rather than computing and then inverting, which may lead to better synthesis results.

3. Grouping Similar Operations: Keeping related operations together for better readability and potential synthesis optimization.

4. Power Optimization: By reducing the number of operations through sharing, we can potentially reduce switching activity and dynamic power.

The improved version will:
- Keep basic operations (AND, OR, XOR) as is
- Implement NAND, NOR, XNOR using their native operators rather than inversion
- Maintain the special AND-NOT case
- Result in fewer total logic operations while maintaining the same functionality
