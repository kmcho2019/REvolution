The current implementation is already optimal in terms of timing and power, but we can explore minor area optimizations by:
1. Sharing inverters between complementary operations (nand/nor)
2. Deriving xnor from xor to save one gate
3. Maintaining the same clean structure while reducing transistor count

The improvements will:
- Keep all timing characteristics identical (wns/tns = 0)
- Maintain the same power profile
- Potentially reduce area slightly by gate sharing
- Preserve readability while being more gate-efficient
