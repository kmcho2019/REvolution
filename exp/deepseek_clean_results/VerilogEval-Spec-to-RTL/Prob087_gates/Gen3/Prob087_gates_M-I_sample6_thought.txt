To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll focus on the following improvements:

1. Remove redundant intermediate signals that are only used once (like not_b)
2. Directly implement operations where intermediate signals don't provide reuse benefits
3. Maintain the same logical functionality while reducing gate count
4. Group related operations for better readability
5. Keep all operations as continuous assignments for clarity

The key optimizations are:
- Eliminate the not_b intermediate signal since it's only used once
- Keep the shared intermediate signals (a_and_b, a_or_b, a_xor_b) as they're used multiple times
- Directly compute out_anotb without an intermediate signal
- Maintain the same combinational behavior with minimal gates
