The original implementation uses direct continuous assignments for each output, which is clear and straightforward. For refactoring, we can group related operations together to potentially improve readability and maintainability. We'll organize the outputs into three categories:
1. Basic operations (AND, OR, XOR)
2. Negated operations (NAND, NOR, XNOR)
3. Special operations (A AND-NOT B)

This grouping makes the logical relationships between operations more explicit while maintaining the same functionality.
