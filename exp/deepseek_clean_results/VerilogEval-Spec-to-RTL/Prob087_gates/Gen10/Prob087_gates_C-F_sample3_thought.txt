The optimal fusion strategy combines the clarity and simplicity of direct continuous assignments from Example 2 with the truth-table visibility of Example 1. Here's the approach:

2. Keep the explicit truth table visibility by organizing outputs in a logical order (basic gates first, then their complements)

Key advantages of this fusion:
- Clear mapping between spec and implementation
- Optimal gate-level synthesis

- No timing concerns
- Easy to verify correctness by inspection
