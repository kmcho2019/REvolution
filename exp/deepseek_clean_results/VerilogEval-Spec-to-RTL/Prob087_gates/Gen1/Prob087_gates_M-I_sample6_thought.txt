The original solution is functionally correct but can be optimized for area by sharing intermediate signals. Since `a & b` and `a | b` are used multiple times, we can compute them once and reuse the results. This reduces redundant logic and saves area. The optimization maintains the same functionality while potentially reducing the number of gates.

Key optimizations:
1. Compute `a_and_b` and `a_or_b` once and reuse them
2. Derive other outputs from these intermediate signals
3. The `out_anotb` still needs separate computation as it uses `~b`
