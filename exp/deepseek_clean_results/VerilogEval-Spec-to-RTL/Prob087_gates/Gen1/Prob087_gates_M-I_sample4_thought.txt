The original solution is functionally correct but can be optimized for power and area by sharing common sub-expressions. The key observations are:
1. out_nand is simply the inverse of out_and
2. out_nor is the inverse of out_or
3. out_xnor is the inverse of out_xor
4. out_anotb can be expressed as out_and & ~b

By computing intermediate results first and reusing them, we can reduce the total number of logic gates. This optimization trades a small amount of performance (adding one extra gate delay for some outputs) for reduced area and power.
