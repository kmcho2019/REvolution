The current implementation is functionally correct but can be optimized for power by sharing intermediate results. Here's the improvement strategy:
1. Compute common sub-expressions first (a&b and a|b) and store them in wires
2. Reuse these intermediate results for derived operations (nand, nor, etc.)
3. This reduces redundant logic computations, lowering switching activity
4. Maintain the same functionality while potentially improving power efficiency
5. Keep the area minimal by not adding unnecessary registers
