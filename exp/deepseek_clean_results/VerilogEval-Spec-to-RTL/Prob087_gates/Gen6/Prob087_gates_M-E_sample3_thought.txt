Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical gate structure with shared intermediate signals. Instead of computing each gate independently, we'll first generate common intermediate signals (like a AND b, a OR b) and then derive other outputs from these intermediates. This reduces redundant computations and creates a more efficient fan-out structure. The key innovation is recognizing that many outputs are logical inversions or combinations of other outputs, allowing us to share computation resources. For example, NAND is just the inversion of AND, NOR is inversion of OR, etc. This approach minimizes gate count while maintaining clear logical relationships between outputs.
