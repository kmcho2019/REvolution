The refactored version will maintain the same functionality but reorganize the code for better readability and potential synthesis optimization. Key changes:

1. Group operations by their logical relationship (basic operations first, then their complements)
2. Use direct assignment for all outputs without intermediate wires since they're simple enough
3. Order operations from simplest to most complex
4. Place related operations (like AND and NAND) close together
5. Keep the AND-NOT operation last as it's the most specialized

This structure makes the logical flow clearer while maintaining the same PPA characteristics. The synthesis tool will still recognize and optimize the basic logic operations.
