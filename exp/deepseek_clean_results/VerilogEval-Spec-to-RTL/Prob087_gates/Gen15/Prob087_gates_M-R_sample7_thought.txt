The original solution uses direct assign statements for each output, which is clear and efficient. For refactoring, we can group related operations together to potentially improve readability and show logical relationships between outputs. We'll maintain the same functionality but organize the assignments differently by:
1. Grouping complementary operations (AND/NAND, OR/NOR, XOR/XNOR)
2. Calculating intermediate signals where it makes logical sense
3. Keeping the same gate-level implementation but with a different presentation structure
