Novel Shift Register Architecture:
1. **Shift Register Implementation**: Instead of parallel computation, use a shift register to sequentially process each cell and its neighbors. This reduces area by reusing computation logic.

2. **Pipelined Rule Computation**: Implement the Rule 110 logic in a pipelined fashion to improve timing. The computation is split across two clock cycles to meet timing constraints.

3. **Smart Boundary Handling**: Use a circular buffer approach for boundary cells, eliminating special case logic for edges. The leftmost and rightmost cells are treated as neighbors in a circular fashion.

4. **Power Gating**: Add enable logic to skip computation when the system is in a stable state (all 0s or repeating pattern).

Key Advantages:
- Significant area reduction through sequential processing
- Better timing due to pipelining
- Simplified boundary handling
- Lower power consumption through conditional computation
