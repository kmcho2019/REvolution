To simplify the Rule 110 implementation while maintaining correctness:
1. Use a single always block for both load and state update
2. Implement Rule 110 as a concise case statement for each cell
3. Handle boundary conditions by treating non-existent neighbors as 0
4. Use bitwise operations to simplify the logic expressions
5. The next state for each cell can be expressed as:
   - For left boundary: next = (center & right) | (~left & right) | (~left & center)
   - For middle cells: next = (center & right) | (~left & right) | (~left & center)
   - For right boundary: next = (center & ~left) | (right & ~left) | (center & right)
   (Note: left=0 for left boundary, right=0 for right boundary)
