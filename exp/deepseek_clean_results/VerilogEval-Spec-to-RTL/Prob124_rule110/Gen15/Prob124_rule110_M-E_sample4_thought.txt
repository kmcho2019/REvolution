Novel Architecture: Shift Register with Parallel Computation
1. **Shift Register Core**: Instead of treating the 512 bits as independent, we'll use a shift register approach where each cell's neighbors are naturally adjacent in the register.
2. **Pipelined Computation**: Break the Rule 110 computation into two pipeline stages to improve timing:
   - Stage 1: Compute neighbor pairs (left+center and center+right)
   - Stage 2: Combine results with Rule 110 logic
3. **Optimized Boundary Handling**: Use dedicated boundary registers that always hold 0, eliminating the need for conditional logic at the edges.
4. **Parallel Processing**: Still process all bits in parallel but with better timing characteristics due to pipelining.
5. **Load Optimization**: Direct parallel load into the shift register when requested.
