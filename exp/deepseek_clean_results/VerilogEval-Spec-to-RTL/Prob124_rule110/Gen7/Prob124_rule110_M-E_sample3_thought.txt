The new architecture employs a "sliding window" approach to reduce area and power while improving timing:

1. **Serialized Computation**: Instead of computing all 512 bits in parallel, we process the array in 64-bit chunks (8 cycles per full update). This significantly reduces area and power.

2. **Overlap Buffers**: Maintain 64-bit overlap buffers at each end to correctly compute boundary conditions when processing chunks.

3. **Pipelined Processing**: Each chunk computation is pipelined across multiple clock cycles to improve timing.

4. **Double-Buffering**: Use two 512-bit registers (current and next) to maintain state while computing updates.

Key Benefits:
- 8x reduction in combinational logic (64 bits vs 512)
- Better timing due to pipelining
- Lower power from reduced switching activity
- Maintains correct boundary conditions through overlap buffers
- Still completes full update in deterministic time (8 cycles)
