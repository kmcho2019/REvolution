Novel Architectural Idea:
1. **Shift-Register Based Processing**: Instead of processing all 512 bits in parallel, use a shift-register approach to process the array in chunks. This reduces area by reusing logic units.

2. **Sliding Window Technique**: Implement a 3-bit sliding window that moves across the array, calculating the next state for each cell sequentially but pipelined for throughput.

3. **Boundary Handling Optimization**: Use circular boundary conditions by connecting the ends of the shift register, eliminating the need for special boundary logic.

4. **Pipelined Processing**: Process the array in 4 parallel pipelines (128 bits each) to maintain throughput while reducing area.

Key Benefits:
- Significant area reduction through logic reuse
- Maintains high throughput with pipelining
- Simplified boundary conditions
- Better power efficiency due to reduced switching activity
