To optimize the Rule 110 implementation, I propose a parallel computation approach with optimized boundary handling:

1. Pre-compute boundary conditions separately to avoid repetitive checks
2. Use bitwise operations to compute next states in parallel
3. Implement Rule 110 logic using Boolean expressions rather than case statements
4. Group the computation into three parts:
   - Left boundary cell (special case)
   - Middle cells (uniform computation)
   - Right boundary cell (special case)
5. This reduces redundant logic and enables better synthesis optimization

The key insight is that Rule 110 can be expressed as:
next_state = center ^ (left | (center & ~right))
This Boolean expression captures all cases from the truth table.
