Novel Architecture: Shift Register with Lookahead Computation

Key Innovations:
1. **Shift Register Core**: Instead of parallel computation, use a serial shift register approach with lookahead computation to reduce area and power.
2. **Pipelined Processing**: Break the 512-bit computation into 64-bit chunks with pipeline registers to improve timing.
3. **Boundary Optimization**: Implement circular boundary conditions to eliminate special cases and simplify logic.
4. **Computation Sharing**: Compute common subexpressions once and share across multiple bits.

Advantages:
- Reduced area by eliminating parallel computation units
- Lower power due to reduced switching activity
- Improved timing through pipelining
- Simplified boundary handling with circular conditions
