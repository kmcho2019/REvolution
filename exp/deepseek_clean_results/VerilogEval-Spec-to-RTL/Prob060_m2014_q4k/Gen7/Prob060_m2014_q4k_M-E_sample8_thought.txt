Here's a novel approach using a circular shift register with pointer tracking:
1. Instead of shifting bits through a linear register, we'll implement a circular buffer
2. Use a 2-bit pointer to track the current 'head' position
3. On each clock cycle, we'll update the appropriate bit based on the pointer
4. The output will always be the bit at (pointer + 3) % 4 position
5. This approach reduces the fan-out of the input signal and may offer better timing characteristics
6. The synchronous reset clears both the register and pointer
