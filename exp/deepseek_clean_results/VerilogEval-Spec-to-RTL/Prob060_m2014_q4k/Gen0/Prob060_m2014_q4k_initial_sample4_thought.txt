To implement a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset, we'll need:
1. Four D flip-flops connected in series (output of one feeds input of next)
2. Synchronous reset (when resetn=0, all flops reset to 0 on clock edge)
3. First flop takes 'in' as input
4. Last flop's output connects to 'out'
5. All flops update on positive clock edge
