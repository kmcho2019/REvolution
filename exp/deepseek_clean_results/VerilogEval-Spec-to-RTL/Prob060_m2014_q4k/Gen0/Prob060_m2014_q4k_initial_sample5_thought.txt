To implement a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset, we'll need:
1. Four D flip-flops connected in series (each output feeds into next input)
2. Synchronous reset that clears all flip-flops when resetn is low
3. The input 'in' goes to the first flip-flop
4. The output 'out' comes from the last flip-flop
5. All flip-flops update on positive clock edge
