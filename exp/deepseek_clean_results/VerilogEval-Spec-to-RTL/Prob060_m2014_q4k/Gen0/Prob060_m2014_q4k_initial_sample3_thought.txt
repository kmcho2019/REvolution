To implement a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset, we'll need:
1. Four D flip-flops connected in series (each output feeds into next input)
2. Synchronous reset (when resetn is low, all flops reset to 0 on clock edge)
3. The input 'in' connects to first flop, output 'out' comes from last flop
4. All flops update on positive clock edge
