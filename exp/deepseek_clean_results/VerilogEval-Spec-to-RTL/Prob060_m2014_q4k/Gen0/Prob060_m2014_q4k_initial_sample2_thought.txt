To implement a 4-bit shift register with synchronous active-low reset, we'll need:
1. Four D flip-flops connected in series
2. The first flip-flop takes the input 'in'
3. Each subsequent flip-flop takes the output of the previous one
4. All flip-flops are clocked on the positive edge and reset synchronously when resetn is low
5. The output 'out' comes from the last flip-flop in the chain
