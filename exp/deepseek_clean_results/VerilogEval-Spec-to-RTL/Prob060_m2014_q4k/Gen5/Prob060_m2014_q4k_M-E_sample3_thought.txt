Novel approach using a circular shift register with parallel load capability:
1. Implement a 4-bit circular shift register that can also load values in parallel
2. Use a single always block for all flip-flops to reduce area
3. Add a control signal to switch between shift and load modes (though only shift is used here)
4. Maintain synchronous active-low reset
5. The circular nature allows for potential future expansion to more complex operations
6. Output is taken from the last bit of the register
7. This approach provides flexibility for future modifications while maintaining the basic shift register functionality
