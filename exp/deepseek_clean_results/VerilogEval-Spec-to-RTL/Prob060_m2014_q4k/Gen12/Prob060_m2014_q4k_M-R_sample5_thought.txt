Refactoring approach:
1. Replace the always block with continuous assignments using D flip-flop primitives
2. Create individual DFF instances for each bit of the shift register
3. Maintain synchronous active-low reset functionality for each flip-flop
4. Chain the flip-flops to form the shift register
5. Connect the output to the last flip-flop's output
6. This structural approach makes the design more explicit and may be preferred for certain synthesis or verification scenarios
