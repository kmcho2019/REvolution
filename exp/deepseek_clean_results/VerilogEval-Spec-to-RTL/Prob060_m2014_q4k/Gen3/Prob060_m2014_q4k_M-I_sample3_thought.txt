The original solution is already quite efficient for a simple 4-bit shift register. However, we can make a few minor optimizations:

1. Remove the intermediate vector register to save a small amount of area
2. Use individual flip-flops with direct chaining to potentially improve timing
3. Maintain the same synchronous active-low reset functionality
4. The output is still taken from the last flip-flop

This approach might offer slightly better timing as each flip-flop directly drives the next, and eliminates the small overhead of the vector register. The area savings would be minimal for 4 bits, but the design becomes more explicit about the shift register structure.
