Novel approach using a circular shift register with parallel load capability:
1. Instead of a simple linear shift register, implement a 4-bit circular shift register
2. Use a counter to track the number of shifts and control the output selection
3. Maintain synchronous active-low reset functionality
4. The output is taken from the last bit of the shift register (same as original)
5. This approach could be extended to support parallel load and other operations
6. The circular nature makes it reusable for other applications
7. The counter adds flexibility for future expansion
