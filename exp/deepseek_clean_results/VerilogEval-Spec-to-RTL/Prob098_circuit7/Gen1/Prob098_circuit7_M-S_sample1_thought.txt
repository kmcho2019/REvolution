From the waveform analysis:
1. q starts as unknown (x) and becomes 1 at the first positive clock edge (15ns)
2. q remains 1 until a rising edge where a=1 is detected (35ns)
3. At the next rising edge after detecting a=1 (45ns), q becomes 0 and stays 0 permanently

Simplified approach:
- Use a single flip-flop to store whether we've seen a=1
- Output q is 1 until we've seen a=1, then 0
- No need for separate initial blocks - reset behavior is handled by initialization
- The seen flag only needs to be set, never cleared
