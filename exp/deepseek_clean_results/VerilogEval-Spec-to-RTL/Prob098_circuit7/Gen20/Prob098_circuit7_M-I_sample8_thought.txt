The given solution is already optimal in terms of PPA (Power, Performance, Area) for this simple sequential circuit. However, we can make one minor improvement by eliminating the separate DFF module and implementing the flip-flop directly in the TopModule. This will:

1. Remove one level of hierarchy (slight area improvement)
2. Reduce one module instantiation (minor power improvement)
3. Maintain identical functionality
4. Keep the same timing characteristics
5. Simplify the design slightly while maintaining readability

The improvement is minimal since the original design was already very efficient, but this makes it slightly more compact without any downsides.
