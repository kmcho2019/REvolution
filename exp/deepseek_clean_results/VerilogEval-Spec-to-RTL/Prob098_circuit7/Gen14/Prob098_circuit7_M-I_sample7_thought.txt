The waveform shows that:
1. q starts as unknown (x) until first positive edge where a=0 (q becomes 1)
2. q remains 1 while a=0

1. Store the previous value of 'a'
2. Detect the 0->1 transition
3. Toggle q when this transition occurs
4. Keep q stable otherwise
