The waveform shows that q toggles from 1 to 0 only when a rising edge occurs while a=1. Otherwise, q maintains its previous value. This behavior can be implemented with a simple D flip-flop where:
1. q is initialized to 1 (from waveform at time 15ns)
2. q clears to 0 when a=1 at posedge clk
3. Otherwise q holds its value
We can implement this directly with a single always block and reset logic.
