The fusion strategy combines the clarity of Example 1's modular design with the conciseness of Example 2. Key improvements:
1. Maintain explicit combinational/sequential separation (good for readability and synthesis)
2. Use direct register output (reduces wire count)
3. Keep minimal code structure while being self-documenting

This solution offers the best balance between readability and efficiency, with identical PPA metrics to both examples since the underlying hardware is fundamentally the same.
