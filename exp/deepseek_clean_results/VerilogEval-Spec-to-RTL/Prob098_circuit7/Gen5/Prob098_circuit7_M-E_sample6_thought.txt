After analyzing the waveform, I notice that the output 'q' behaves as follows:
1. Initially undefined (x)
2. On first positive clock edge (5ns), q remains x (since a=0)
3. On next positive edge (15ns), q becomes 1 (when a=0)
4. Then q changes to 0 only when a=1 at the positive edge (45ns)
5. After that, q stays 0 as long as a remains 1

This suggests the circuit is detecting the first occurrence of a=1 after initialization. Once a=1 is seen, q latches to 0 and stays there regardless of subsequent a values. Before seeing a=1, q remains 1 when a=0.

This can be implemented as a simple state machine with two states:
- State 1: Waiting for first a=1 (output q=1)
- State 2: After seeing a=1 (output q=0)
The state transitions only happen at positive clock edges.
