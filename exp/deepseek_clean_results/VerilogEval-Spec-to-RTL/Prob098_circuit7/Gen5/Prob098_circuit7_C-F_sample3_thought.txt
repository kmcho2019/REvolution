The fusion strategy combines the best aspects of both examples while optimizing for power, performance, and area (PPA):

1. From Example 1: The clear separation of combinational and sequential logic (modularity)
2. From Example 2: The direct implementation of the behavior without unnecessary inversion (efficiency)
3. Optimization: Move all logic inside the clocked block to minimize unnecessary switching activity (power reduction)

The resulting design:
- Uses a single always block for sequential logic (cleaner timing)
- Implements the exact required behavior without extra gates (area efficient)
- Only evaluates on clock edges (power efficient)
- Maintains clear functionality mapping (readable)
