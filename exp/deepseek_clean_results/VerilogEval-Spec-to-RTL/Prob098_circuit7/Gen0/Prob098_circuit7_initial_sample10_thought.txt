From analyzing the simulation waveforms:
1. The output q changes only on positive clock edges (5ns, 15ns, 25ns, etc.)
2. q becomes 1 when a is 0 at the clock edge (seen at 15ns)
3. q becomes 0 when a is 1 at the clock edge (seen at 45ns)
4. q maintains its value when a doesn't change (25ns-35ns, 55ns-85ns)

This suggests the circuit is a simple D flip-flop where q takes the inverse of a on each clock edge.
