From analyzing the simulation waveforms:
1. The output q changes only on positive clock edges (5ns, 15ns, 25ns, etc.)
2. When a=0 at the positive clock edge (15ns), q becomes 1
3. When a=1 at the positive clock edge (45ns), q becomes 0
4. q maintains its value when a doesn't change at the clock edge (25ns, 35ns, etc.)

This suggests the circuit is a simple D flip-flop where:
- q gets 1 when a is 0 at clock edge
- q gets 0 when a is 1 at clock edge
- Essentially, q is the inverted value of a, but only updated at clock edges
