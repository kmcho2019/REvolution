The circuit appears to detect the rising edge of input 'a' and toggles the output 'q' accordingly. Here's the improved design strategy:
1. Store the previous value of 'a' to detect changes
2. Only toggle 'q' when a rising edge (0->1 transition) is detected
3. Maintain all other functionality from the original waveform
4. This reduces unnecessary toggling of 'q' when 'a' is stable or falling
5. The design remains simple with just one additional flip-flop for 'a' history
