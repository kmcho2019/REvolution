The given solution is already quite optimal for this simple sequential circuit. However, we can make one minor improvement by combining the inverter and D flip-flop into a single always block, which might provide slight PPA benefits by:
1. Eliminating the explicit wire and assign statement
2. Potentially allowing better synthesis optimization since the inverter and flip-flop are treated as a single unit
3. Reducing routing resources between the inverter and flip-flop

The functionality remains identical - q outputs the inverted value of 'a' from the previous clock cycle.
