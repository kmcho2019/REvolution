Here's a novel approach that leverages a circular shift register with parallel load capability and a hierarchical multiplexer structure:

1. Circular Shift Register:
   - Instead of a traditional linear shift register, implement a circular one that can rotate bits
   - This allows for potential power savings since bits aren't always shifting through all stages
   - Include a parallel load capability (though not used here) for future extensibility

2. Hierarchical Multiplexer:
   - Implement the 8:1 mux as two 4:1 muxes followed by a 2:1 mux
   - This hierarchical structure can potentially reduce fan-out and improve timing in some technologies
   - The selection is still based on ABC but now with two levels of muxing

3. Clock Gating:
   - Add simple clock gating to the shift register to reduce power when not enabled
   - This reduces dynamic power consumption when the circuit is idle

Benefits:
- Circular architecture provides flexibility for future enhancements
- Hierarchical mux may offer better timing in some technology nodes
- Clock gating reduces power consumption
- Still maintains all required functionality
