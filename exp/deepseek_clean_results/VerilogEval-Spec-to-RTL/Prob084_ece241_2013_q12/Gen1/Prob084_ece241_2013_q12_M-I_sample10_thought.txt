To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:

1. Performance:
   - Replace the cascaded ternary operators with direct array indexing (Q[{A,B,C}]) for the multiplexer to reduce critical path delay
   - This eliminates the long chain of conditional checks and uses a simple lookup

2. Power:
   - Keep the shift register implementation as is since it's already power-efficient when not enabled
   - The direct indexing method reduces switching activity in the selection logic

3. Area:
   - The array indexing approach is more compact and will synthesize to a more efficient multiplexer structure
   - Eliminates redundant comparison logic from the original ternary operator chain

The improved design will:
- Maintain the same functionality
- Reduce critical path delay by simplifying the output selection
- Use less area through more efficient multiplexer implementation
- Maintain similar power characteristics while potentially improving timing
