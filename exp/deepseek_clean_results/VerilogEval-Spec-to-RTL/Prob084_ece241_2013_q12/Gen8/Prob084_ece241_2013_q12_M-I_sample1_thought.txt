To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Clock Gating:
   - Add clock gating for the shift register when enable is low to reduce dynamic power
   - This will prevent unnecessary clock toggles in the flip-flops when not shifting

2. Mux Optimization:
   - Replace the direct indexing mux with a binary tree structure
   - This reduces fanout and improves timing characteristics

3. Shift Register Optimization:
   - Use a parameterized width for better reusability
   - Implement reset functionality (though not required, good practice)

4. Power Optimization:
   - Use don't-care optimization for unused states
   - Group related signals to minimize switching activity

The improved design will maintain the same functionality while achieving better PPA metrics.
