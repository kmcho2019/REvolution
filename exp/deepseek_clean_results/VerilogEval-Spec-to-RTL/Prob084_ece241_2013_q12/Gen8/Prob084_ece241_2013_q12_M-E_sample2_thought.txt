Here's a novel approach using a circular shift register with parallel load capability and a decoder-based multiplexer:

1. Shift Register Innovation:
   - Implement a circular shift register that can rotate bits when enabled
   - This allows for potential power savings as bits aren't always shifting linearly
   - Include a parallel load path that could be useful for future expansion

2. Multiplexer Innovation:
   - Use a decoder to generate one-hot select signals
   - Combine with AND-OR mux structure for potentially better timing
   - This approach may offer better power characteristics when outputs are stable

3. Control Logic:
   - Add a mode control (though unused in current spec) for future flexibility
   - The circular nature could reduce switching activity in some use cases

Benefits:
- Novel circular architecture offers potential power savings
- Decoder-based mux may have better timing characteristics
- More flexible design for future modifications
- Different implementation style shows alternative thinking
