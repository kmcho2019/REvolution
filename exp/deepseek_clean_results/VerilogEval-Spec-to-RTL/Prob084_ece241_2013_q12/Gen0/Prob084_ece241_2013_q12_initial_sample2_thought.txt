To implement the TopModule, we need:
1. An 8-bit shift register using D flip-flops that shifts on positive clock edges when enable is high.
2. A 3-to-8 multiplexer that selects one of the 8 flip-flop outputs based on the 3-bit address ABC.
3. The shift register input comes from S, with Q[0] being the first bit shifted in (MSB first).
4. The output Z is the selected flip-flop output based on ABC.
