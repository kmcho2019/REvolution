To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics while maintaining functionality:

1. **Performance Improvement**:
   - The negative WNS indicates timing violations in the shift register path
   - Replace the linear shift register with a circular buffer implementation to break the long combinational path
   - This reduces the critical path from O(n) to O(1) for shift operations

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - Add clock gating to the shift register to prevent unnecessary toggling when enable=0
   - This reduces dynamic power consumption during idle periods

3. **Area Optimization**:
   - Keep the efficient direct array indexing for the mux
   - The circular buffer implementation doesn't require additional area compared to the shift register

The improved design should:
- Maintain the same functionality
- Improve timing closure (reduce WNS violation)
- Reduce power consumption
- Keep area similar or better
