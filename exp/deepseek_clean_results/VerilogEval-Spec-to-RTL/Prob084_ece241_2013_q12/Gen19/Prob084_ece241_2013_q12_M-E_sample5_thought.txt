Here's a novel architectural approach that leverages a circular shift register with parallel load capability and a hierarchical multiplexer structure:

1. Shift Register Innovation:
   - Implement a circular shift register that can rotate bits when enabled
   - This reduces wire length and fanout compared to linear shift registers
   - Each flip-flop directly connects to its neighbor in a ring topology

2. Multiplexer Optimization:
   - Use a two-level hierarchical mux (4:1 followed by 2:1) instead of single 8:1
   - This reduces critical path delay by balancing the mux tree
   - The hierarchy matches natural binary encoding of ABC inputs

3. Power Optimization:
   - Implement clock gating at the flip-flop level using the enable signal
   - Only active flip-flops receive clock pulses when shifting

4. Area Efficiency:
   - The circular structure eliminates long routing wires
   - Hierarchical mux uses smaller standard cells
   - Combined control logic reduces overhead

Key Advantages:
- Better timing due to balanced mux hierarchy
- Lower power from clock gating and reduced switching
- Compact layout from circular structure
- Scalable to larger sizes
