Refactoring Strategy:

   - Each flip-flop will be implemented as a separate register with explicit enable logic
   - This makes the shift operation more visible at the gate level

2. Multiplexer:

   - This makes the selection logic more explicit while maintaining efficiency

3. Top Module:

   - Use hierarchical naming for the flip-flops to maintain clarity
