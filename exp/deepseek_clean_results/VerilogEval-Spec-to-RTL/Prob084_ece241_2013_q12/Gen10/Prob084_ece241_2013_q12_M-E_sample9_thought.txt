Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Shift Register with Parallel Load Option:
   - While maintaining the shift functionality, add a parallel load capability that can be triggered when enable is high
   - This allows for faster initialization if needed, though we'll keep it disabled in normal operation

2. Hierarchical Multiplexer Structure:
   - Instead of a direct 8:1 mux, implement a two-level hierarchical mux (4:1 followed by 2:1)
   - This reduces fanout and may improve timing in some technologies
   - The hierarchy matches the bit significance (C selects between first-level mux outputs)

3. Integrated Control Logic:
   - Combine the address bits into a control word that drives both the shift register and mux
   - While not strictly necessary, this makes the design more extensible

Key Advantages:
- More flexible shift register with potential for parallel load
- Better timing characteristics for larger implementations due to hierarchical mux
- Clean separation of shift and read operations
- More scalable architecture
