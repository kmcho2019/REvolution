Here's a novel approach that uses a circular shift register with parallel load capability, combined with a clever multiplexing scheme:

1. Circular Shift Register:
   - Instead of a traditional linear shift register, implement a circular one where the last bit wraps around
   - This allows for more flexible data movement and potential power savings
   - Include parallel load capability (though not used here) for future extensibility

2. Smart Multiplexing:
   - Rather than a standard 8:1 mux, implement a hierarchical selection scheme
   - First level selects between upper and lower nibbles
   - Second level selects the specific bit within the nibble
   - This reduces fanout on the select signals

3. Power Optimization:
   - Use clock gating for the shift register when enable is low
   - Implement the mux using transmission gates for reduced power consumption

Benefits:
- Circular architecture enables future functionality extensions
- Hierarchical mux reduces critical path delay
- Explicit power management improves energy efficiency
- Maintains same interface and functionality as original spec
