Here's a novel architectural approach that leverages hierarchical decoding and distributed multiplexing:

1. Shift Register Innovation:
   - Instead of a single 8-bit shift register, use 8 individual DFFs with a shared enable
   - Each DFF has its own input mux that selects between:
     - The shift input (S) for the first bit
     - The previous DFF's output for other bits
     - Maintains current value when not enabled

2. Multiplexing Strategy:
   - Implement a hierarchical 2-level mux structure:
     - First level: 4x 2:1 muxes selecting between Q[0:3] and Q[4:7]
     - Second level: 2:1 mux selecting between the first level outputs
     - Final 2:1 mux selects based on LSB (C)
   - This reduces fanout compared to a single 8:1 mux

3. Control Logic:
   - Use ABC as one-hot decoded select signals
   - Each DFF input is controlled by its position in the shift chain
   - Enable signal gates the shift operation

Benefits:
- Reduced critical path through hierarchical muxing
- Better clock gating potential with individual DFF control
- More granular power management
- Potential area savings in some technologies
