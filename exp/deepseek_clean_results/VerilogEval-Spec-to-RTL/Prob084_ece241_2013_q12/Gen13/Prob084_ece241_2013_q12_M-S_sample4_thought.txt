Simplified Design Approach:
1. Basic Shift Register:

2. Direct 8:1 Multiplexer:
   - Use a single-level 8:1 multiplexer for address decoding
   - ABC inputs directly select the appropriate Q output
   - More straightforward and efficient than hierarchical approach

Key Simplifications:

- Removed redundant hierarchical mux structure
- Direct connection from shift register to output mux
- Minimal logic while maintaining full functionality
