The current solution already shows excellent PPA metrics, particularly in power consumption. However, we can make some targeted improvements:

1. Shift Register Optimization:
   - Keep the efficient concatenation approach but add explicit reset capability
   - Maintain clock gating through enable signal

2. Multiplexer Optimization:

   - Remove unnecessary hierarchy to reduce routing

3. Top Module:
   - Combine shift register and mux into single module to reduce hierarchy
   - Use localparam for constants
   - Optimize wire declarations

Key Improvements:
- Simplified hierarchy for better routing
- Direct mux implementation for potentially better timing
