To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Performance**: Use a carry-select adder structure for the decrementer to reduce critical path delay. Break the 10-bit counter into two 5-bit chunks for parallel operation.

2. **Power**: 
   - Add clock gating to the counter logic when it reaches zero to prevent unnecessary toggling.
   - Use a hierarchical zero detection (tree structure) to reduce switching activity.

3. **Area**:
   - Share logic between the decrementer and zero detection.
   - Use a more efficient encoding for the counter when it's near zero.

The improved design will:
- Maintain the same functionality
- Reduce critical path delay by parallelizing operations
- Lower power consumption through clock gating and optimized logic
- Minimize area through resource sharing
