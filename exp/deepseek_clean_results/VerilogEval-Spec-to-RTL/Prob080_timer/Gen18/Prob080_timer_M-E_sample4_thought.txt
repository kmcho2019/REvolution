Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Instead of a pure down-counter, implement a saturating counter that stops at zero but with a different control mechanism
2. Use a state machine approach with two states: IDLE (counter at zero) and COUNTING (active counting)
3. The state transitions are:
   - Load always takes priority and moves to COUNTING state
   - In COUNTING state, decrement until zero, then transition to IDLE
4. Terminal count is simply the IDLE state indicator
5. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of control and datapath
   - More explicit state management
   - Easier to extend with additional states if needed
   - Potentially better timing as state logic is simpler than wide OR reduction
