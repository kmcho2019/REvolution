Refactoring Strategy:
1. Structural Changes:
   - Replace the always block with continuous assignments for the counter logic
   - Use a combinational next_counter calculation and a single DFF for state storage
   - Simplify control logic by making load have unconditional priority

2. Optimization Goals:
   - Reduce sequential logic to just the counter register
   - Move all combinational logic to continuous assignments
   - Maintain clear priority of load over decrement operation
   - Keep zero detection simple and efficient

Key Changes:
- Next counter value calculated combinationally before the register
- Single always block just for the register update
- Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
- Simplified tc generation
