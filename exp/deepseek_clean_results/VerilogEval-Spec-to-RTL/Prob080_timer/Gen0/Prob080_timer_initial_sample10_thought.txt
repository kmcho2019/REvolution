The design involves a 10-bit down-counter with synchronous load capability. The counter will:
1. Load the 10-bit data when 'load' is high
2. Decrement by 1 each clock cycle when 'load' is low and counter isn't zero
3. Output 'tc' (terminal count) when counter reaches zero
4. Hold at zero until next load operation

The single D flip-flop requirement implies we should use a register for the counter state and no other sequential elements.
