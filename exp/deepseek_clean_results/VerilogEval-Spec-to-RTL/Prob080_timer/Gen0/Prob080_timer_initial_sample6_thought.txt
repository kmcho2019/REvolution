The design involves a 10-bit down-counter with synchronous load capability. The counter will:
1. Load the input data when 'load' is high
2. Decrement by 1 when 'load' is low and counter isn't zero
3. Output 'tc' (terminal count) when counter reaches zero
4. Maintain zero count until next load
The single D flip-flop requirement implies we should use a registered output for 'tc'.
