Here's a novel approach using a shift register instead of a traditional counter. The key idea is:
1. Represent the countdown using a shift register where the number of '1's corresponds to the remaining count
2. Initialize the shift register with 'data' number of '1's when loaded
3. Each clock cycle, shift right and insert '0' to effectively count down
4. The terminal count (tc) is simply the inverse of the LSB of the shift register
This approach eliminates the need for a subtractor and comparator, potentially improving timing and area.
