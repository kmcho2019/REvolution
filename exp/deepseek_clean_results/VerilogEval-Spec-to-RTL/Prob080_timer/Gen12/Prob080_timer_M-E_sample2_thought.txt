Novel architectural idea: Zero-Count Detection with Parallel Load
1. Instead of continuously decrementing the counter, we'll use a comparator to detect when the counter reaches zero
2. The counter will only decrement when not zero and not being loaded
3. A separate zero detection flag will be maintained to prevent unnecessary decrements
4. This approach reduces switching activity when the counter is idle (at zero)
5. The terminal count (tc) is simply the zero detection flag

Key advantages:
- Reduced power consumption when counter is at zero (no unnecessary decrement operations)
- Cleaner separation of load and count logic
- Potential for better synthesis optimization due to simpler always block
- Maintains single D flip-flop requirement
