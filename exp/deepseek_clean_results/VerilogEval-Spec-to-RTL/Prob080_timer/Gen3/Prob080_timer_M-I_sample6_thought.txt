1. Performance:
   - Register the 'tc' output to break the critical path from counter to output
   - This reduces timing pressure on the comparator logic

2. Power:
   - Add clock gating when counter is zero to prevent unnecessary switching
   - Only update the counter when needed (not zero or load asserted)

3. Area:
   - Keep the basic structure but optimize the implementation
   - The registered output adds one flip-flop but improves timing

The improved design will:
- Still maintain the same functionality
- Have better timing due to registered output
- Reduce power consumption through clock gating
- Keep area impact minimal while improving other metrics
