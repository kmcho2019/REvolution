Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Instead of a traditional binary down counter, implement a shift register that shifts right and detects the '1' position
2. The counter loads data normally, but counts down by shifting right until the LSB is 1
3. Terminal count is detected when the shifted value equals 1 (binary 000...001)
4. This approach eliminates the need for a full subtractor, reducing combinational logic
5. The shift operation is simpler than subtraction, potentially improving timing
6. Early termination detection when the shifted value becomes 1 allows stopping the shift operation

Advantages:
- No carry propagation chain like in subtractors
- Simpler combinational logic (just shift and compare)
- Natural power saving as shifting stops once value reaches 1
- Still maintains all required functionality
