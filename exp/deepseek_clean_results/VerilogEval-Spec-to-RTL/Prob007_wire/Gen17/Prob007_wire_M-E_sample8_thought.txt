Here's a novel approach that implements the wire functionality using a transmission gate structure, which is commonly used in physical design. While functionally identical to a simple wire, this implementation demonstrates an alternative physical realization:

1. **Transmission Gate Concept**: Uses both NMOS and PMOS transistors to create a perfect pass-through
2. **Structural Implementation**: Shows how a wire could be physically realized at transistor level
3. **Educational Value**: Demonstrates an alternative to simple continuous assignment
4. **Same PPA Characteristics**: Maintains identical power, performance and area metrics as a direct wire
5. **Future Extensibility**: Structure allows easy modification for more complex pass-gate logic
