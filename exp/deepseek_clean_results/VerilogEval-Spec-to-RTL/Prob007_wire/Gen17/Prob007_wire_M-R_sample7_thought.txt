The simplest and most efficient way to implement a wire connection is through a direct continuous assignment. This approach:
1. Uses minimal syntax for maximum clarity
2. Eliminates any unnecessary intermediate signals
3. Results in identical synthesized hardware (a direct connection)
4. Maintains zero-delay wire behavior
5. Optimizes all PPA metrics by having just one driver

This refactoring removes the intermediate wire and uses a single continuous assignment, which is the most straightforward implementation of a wire connection in Verilog.
