The given solution is already optimal for a simple wire connection in terms of PPA metrics. Since it's just a direct assignment with:
- Perfect timing (tns/wns = 0.0)
- Minimal power (2.11e-05 W)
- Minimal area (1.0 um^2)

There is literally no room for improvement at the RTL level. The only potential optimizations would be at the physical implementation level (placement/routing), but those would be handled by the synthesis tool rather than requiring RTL changes.

The current implementation represents the theoretical minimum for this functionality in Verilog. Any alternative implementation would either be functionally equivalent or worse in terms of PPA metrics.
