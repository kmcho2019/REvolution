- Zero timing impact (no propagation delay)
- Minimal power consumption (only port switching)
- Minimal area (just the port connections)

1. Add unnecessary complexity (like buffers) that would worsen PPA
2. Be functionally equivalent but syntactically different (no PPA improvement)
