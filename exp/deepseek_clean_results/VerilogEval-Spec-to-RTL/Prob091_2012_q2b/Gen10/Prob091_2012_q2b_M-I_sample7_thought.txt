Improvement Strategy:
1. **Y1 Logic Analysis**: 
   - Current implementation (y[0] & w) is already optimal - single AND gate
   - No possible simplification since it directly encodes the A→B transition

2. **Y3 Logic Optimization**:
   - Current implementation (~w & ~y[0] & ~y[3]) uses 3-input AND with inverters
   - Can leverage one-hot encoding property (exactly one y bit is active)
   - Alternative expression: ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])
   - This uses OR of active states that transition to D (B, C, E, F)
   - Same logical function but may synthesize differently
   - Potential benefits:
     * Fewer inverters needed (only ~w)
     * OR gate may map better to target technology
     * Maintains one-hot safety

3. **PPA Considerations**:
   - Both implementations are correct under one-hot encoding
   - New version may have better power/area depending on cell library
   - Timing remains excellent in both cases
   - Choose version that gives best synthesis results
