To simplify the solution, we can optimize the logic for Y3 by factoring out the common ~w term. This reduces both the number of gates and potential switching activity. 

For Y1 (next state of y[1]):
- Remains the same: Y1 = y[0] & w (only state A with w=1 transitions to B)

For Y3 (next state of y[3]):
- Instead of four separate AND operations with ~w, we can OR all relevant states first then AND with ~w
- Y3 = ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])

This simplification maintains the same functionality while reducing gate count and power consumption.
