   - y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5] is equivalent to ~(y[0] | y[3]) in one-hot encoding
   - This reduces the OR gate from 4 inputs to 2 inputs

   - Reduces area (smaller OR gate)
   - May reduce power (fewer transistor switches)
   - Maintains the same timing characteristics
