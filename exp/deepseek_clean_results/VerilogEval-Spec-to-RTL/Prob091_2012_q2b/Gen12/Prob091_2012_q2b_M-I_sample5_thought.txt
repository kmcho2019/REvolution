Improvement Strategy:
1. **Y1 Optimization**: The current AND gate implementation is already optimal for the A→B transition (y[0] & w). No further optimization needed.

2. **Y3 Optimization**:
   - Current NOR implementation (~(y[0] | y[3])) is good but can be slightly improved
   - Observe that D state (y[3]) is entered when:
     * In B (y[1]) and w=0
     * In C (y[2]) and w=0
     * In E (y[4]) and w=0
     * In F (y[5]) and w=0
   - Can be expressed as: ~w & (y[1] | y[2] | y[4] | y[5])
   - This replaces the NOR with an OR of fewer terms

3. **Power Reduction**:
   - The new OR implementation will have lower switching activity
   - Fewer gates in the critical path

4. **Area Consideration**:
   - The new implementation uses one OR gate instead of NOR
   - May result in slightly better area due to simpler gate structure

5. **Maintainability**:
   - Keep clear documentation of state transitions
   - Explicitly show the one-hot encoding assumption
