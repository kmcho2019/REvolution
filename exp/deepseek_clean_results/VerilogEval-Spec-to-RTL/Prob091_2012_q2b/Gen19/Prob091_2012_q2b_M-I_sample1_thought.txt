Optimization Strategy:
1. **Y3 Logic Simplification**: 
   - Current implementation uses ~(w | y[0] | y[3]) which requires 1 OR and 1 NOT gate
   - Can be implemented more efficiently as a single NOR gate: ~w & ~y[0] & ~y[3]
   - This reduces gate count and potentially improves timing

2. **Output Gating**:
   - Add enable conditions to outputs to prevent unnecessary switching
   - Only update outputs when relevant state transitions occur

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - The NOR implementation reduces switching activity compared to OR+NOT
   - Maintain minimal logic depth to keep power consumption low

4. **Area Optimization**:
   - The new implementation uses fewer gates (1 AND and 1 NOR instead of 1 AND, 1 OR, and 1 NOT)
   - No additional flip-flops or complex logic needed
