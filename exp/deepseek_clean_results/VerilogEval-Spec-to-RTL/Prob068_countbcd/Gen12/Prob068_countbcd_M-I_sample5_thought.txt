Optimization strategy for better PPA:
1. Timing Improvement:
   - Register enable signals to break combinational path
   - Parallelize digit increment logic to reduce critical path
   - Use synchronous reset for better timing characteristics

2. Power Optimization:
   - Gate enable signals when not needed
   - Simplify combinational logic to reduce switching activity

3. Area Reduction:
   - Share adder resources across digit positions
   - Simplify enable generation logic
   - Use parameterized digit width for flexibility
