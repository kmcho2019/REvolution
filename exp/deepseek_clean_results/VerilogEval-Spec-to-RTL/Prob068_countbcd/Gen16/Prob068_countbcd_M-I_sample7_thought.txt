Improvement strategy for better PPA:
1. Break down the 16-bit counter into four 4-bit BCD counters with cascaded enable signals to:
   - Reduce comparator width (from 8/12-bit to 4-bit)
   - Minimize unnecessary bit toggling
   - Enable better resource sharing
2. Use registered enable signals to break timing paths
3. Implement ripple-carry style increment with registered enables to:
   - Reduce combinatorial logic depth
   - Improve timing (reduce WNS/TNS)
   - Lower dynamic power by reducing glitching
4. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
5. Optimize area by sharing 4-bit increment logic
