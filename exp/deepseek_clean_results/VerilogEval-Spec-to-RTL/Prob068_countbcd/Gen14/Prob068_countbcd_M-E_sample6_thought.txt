Novel approach using a unified counter with digit extraction:
1. Use a single 16-bit counter for the entire counting range (0-9999)
2. Implement BCD conversion through digit extraction logic
3. Generate enable signals by detecting digit boundaries (9->0 transitions)
4. This approach eliminates ripple-carry delays and reduces hierarchical complexity
5. Synchronous reset clears the entire counter at once
6. Digit extraction happens in parallel rather than sequentially
