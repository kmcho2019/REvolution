Simplified BCD counter design:
1. Use a single 16-bit register for all digits (compact representation)
2. Generate enable signals in parallel using simple digit comparisons
3. Implement digit increment logic with cascaded conditions:
   - Always increment ones digit
   - Higher digits increment only when lower digits reach 9 and are about to roll over
4. Reset clears all digits to zero
5. Enable signals are just the current digit being 9 (no chained dependencies)
Key simplifications:
- Parallel enable generation avoids timing chain
- Clear increment logic with simple conditions
- Reduced comparison operations
- Maintains same functionality with simpler structure
