Novel approach using a single counter with digit extraction and enable generation:
1. Use a single 16-bit counter instead of separate digit counters
2. Implement BCD behavior by detecting when each digit exceeds 9 and correcting it
3. Generate enable signals by comparing current digit values with their previous values
4. This approach reduces area by eliminating redundant digit counters
5. Uses synchronous reset and clock gating for power optimization
6. Digit extraction and correction happens in parallel for better timing
