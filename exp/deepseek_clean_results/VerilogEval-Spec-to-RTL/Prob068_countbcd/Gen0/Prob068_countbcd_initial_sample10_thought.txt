To implement a 4-digit BCD counter:
1. Use a 16-bit register (q) to store the 4 BCD digits (4 bits each)
2. For each digit, increment when:
   - ones digit: every clock cycle (unless reset)
   - tens digit: when ones digit rolls over from 9 to 0
   - hundreds digit: when tens digit rolls over from 9 to 0
   - thousands digit: when hundreds digit rolls over from 9 to 0
3. Generate enable signals (ena) for the upper three digits when their lower digit rolls over
4. Synchronous reset should clear all digits to 0
