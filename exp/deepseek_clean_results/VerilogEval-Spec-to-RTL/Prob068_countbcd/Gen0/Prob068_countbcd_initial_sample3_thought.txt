To implement a 4-digit BCD counter:
1. Use a 16-bit register (q) to store the 4 BCD digits (4 bits each)
2. For each digit, increment when:
   - For ones digit: every clock cycle (when not reset)
   - For tens digit: when ones digit rolls over from 9 to 0
   - For hundreds digit: when tens digit rolls over from 9 to 0
   - For thousands digit: when hundreds digit rolls over from 9 to 0
3. Generate enable signals (ena) for the upper three digits when their lower digit rolls over from 9 to 0
4. Reset all digits to 0 when reset is high
5. All operations should be synchronous to the positive clock edge
