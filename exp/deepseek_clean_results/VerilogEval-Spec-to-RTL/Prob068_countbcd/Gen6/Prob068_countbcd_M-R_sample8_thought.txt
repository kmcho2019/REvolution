Refactored design approach:

2. Use cascaded enable signals to trigger digit increments
3. Implement digit rollover logic using simple conditional assignments
4. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
5. Key improvements:

   - More modular structure for easier modification
   - Reduced nesting depth in control logic
   - Explicit digit overflow conditions
