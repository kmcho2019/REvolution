Novel approach using a hierarchical counter structure with distributed control:
1. Implement each BCD digit as an independent 4-bit counter with local enable logic
2. Use a token-passing scheme where each digit generates a 'carry' signal for the next higher digit
3. Each digit maintains its own rollover detection and increment logic
4. The enable signals are derived directly from the carry signals
5. This creates a more modular and scalable design with better timing characteristics
6. Synchronous reset clears all counters simultaneously
