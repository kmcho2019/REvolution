Novel approach using a single counter with digit extraction:
1. Use a single 16-bit counter instead of separate digit counters
2. Implement BCD behavior by detecting when any digit exceeds 9 and correcting it
3. Generate enable signals by comparing digit values to 9
4. This approach reduces area by eliminating redundant digit counters
5. Simplifies control logic by having a single counter state
6. Synchronous reset clears the entire counter at once
