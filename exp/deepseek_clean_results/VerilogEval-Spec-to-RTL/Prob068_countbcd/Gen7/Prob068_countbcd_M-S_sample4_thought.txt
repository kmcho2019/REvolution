Simplified BCD counter design:
1. Use a single 16-bit register for all digits (more compact than separate registers)
2. Generate enable signals sequentially to avoid complex combinational logic
3. Implement digit increment logic in a more straightforward way:
   - Always increment ones digit
   - Higher digits only increment when lower digits roll over
4. Reset clears all digits simultaneously
5. Enable signals are simply the rollover conditions of lower digits
Key simplifications:
- Single register reduces area
- Sequential enable generation avoids complex AND gates
- Clear, straightforward increment logic
