Novel unified BCD counter with parallel enable generation:
1. Implement the counter as a single 16-bit register with digit-wise increment logic
2. Generate enable signals in parallel rather than cascading
3. Key innovations:
   - Single always block for all digits reduces area
   - Parallel enable calculation improves timing
   - Conditional increment logic minimizes switching activity
4. Reset synchronously clears all digits
5. Enable signals are generated combinatorially based on digit values
6. Each digit increments only when all lower digits are at 9 and enabled
Advantages:
- Eliminates ripple-carry delay of cascaded design
- More compact implementation
- Lower power due to reduced switching
- Better timing characteristics
