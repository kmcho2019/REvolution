Improvement strategy targeting power and area optimization while maintaining performance:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Remove redundant comparisons by sharing max detection logic
   - Use clock gating for higher digits when not counting
   - Simplify enable logic to reduce switching activity

2. Area Optimization:
   - Combine comparison logic for all digits
   - Remove intermediate wires where possible
   - Use direct concatenation for outputs

3. Performance Maintenance:
   - Keep parallel enable generation
   - Maintain clean hierarchical structure
   - Preserve synchronous reset functionality
