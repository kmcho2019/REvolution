Novel approach using a single counter with digit extraction and enable generation:
1. Use a single 16-bit counter instead of separate digit counters
2. Implement BCD behavior by detecting digit rollovers (9->0 transitions)
3. Generate enable signals by comparing current and previous counter values
4. Extract individual digits through bit selection and modulo operations
5. This approach reduces area by eliminating redundant digit counters
6. Enable signals are generated combinatorially from counter transitions
7. Synchronous reset clears the entire counter
