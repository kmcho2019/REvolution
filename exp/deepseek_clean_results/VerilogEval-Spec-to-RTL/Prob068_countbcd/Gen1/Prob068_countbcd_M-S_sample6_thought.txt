To simplify the 4-digit BCD counter:
1. Use a single 16-bit register for all digits instead of separate registers
2. Generate enable signals in parallel rather than cascading
3. Increment logic becomes simpler by:
   - Always incrementing ones digit
   - Using direct rollover detection for upper digits
   - Only updating upper digits when their lower digit rolls over
4. Maintain synchronous reset functionality
5. Reduced logic depth by eliminating cascaded enable dependencies
