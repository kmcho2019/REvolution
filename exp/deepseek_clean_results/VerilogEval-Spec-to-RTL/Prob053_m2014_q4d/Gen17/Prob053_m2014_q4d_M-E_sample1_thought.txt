Here's a novel approach using a T flip-flop (toggle flip-flop) instead of a D flip-flop with XOR feedback:

1. The T flip-flop toggles its state when its input is 1
2. We can use 'in' as the toggle enable signal
3. The output is simply the state of the T flip-flop
4. This creates equivalent behavior to the XOR feedback circuit since:
   - When in=1: output toggles (equivalent to XOR with 1)
   - When in=0: output remains same (equivalent to XOR with 0)
5. The flip-flop is positive-edge triggered by clk with no reset
6. This approach eliminates the explicit XOR gate, implementing the same logic more directly
