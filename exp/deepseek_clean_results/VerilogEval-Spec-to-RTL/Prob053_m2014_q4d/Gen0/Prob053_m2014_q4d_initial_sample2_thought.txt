The problem requires implementing a simple sequential circuit where:
1. The output 'out' is the result of a D flip-flop
2. The D input of the flip-flop comes from an XOR of the current input 'in' and the current output 'out' (feedback)
3. The flip-flop is clocked on the positive edge of 'clk' with no reset

This creates a circuit where the output toggles whenever the input is high (since XOR with 1 toggles the value).
