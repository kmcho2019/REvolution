The problem requires implementing a circuit where:
1. A D flip-flop is clocked on the positive edge of 'clk' (no reset)
2. The D input of the flip-flop comes from a 2-input XOR gate
3. The XOR gate takes two inputs: the external input 'in' and the flip-flop's output 'out'

This creates a feedback loop where the flip-flop's output is XORed with the input and fed back to itself. The circuit essentially toggles its output when 'in' is high.
