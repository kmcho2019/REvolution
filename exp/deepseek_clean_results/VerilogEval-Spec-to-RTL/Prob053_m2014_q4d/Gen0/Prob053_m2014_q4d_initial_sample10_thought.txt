The problem requires implementing a simple feedback circuit where:
1. A D flip-flop stores the output of an XOR gate
2. The XOR gate takes two inputs: the external input 'in' and the flip-flop's output 'out'
3. The flip-flop is clocked on the positive edge of 'clk' with no reset

This creates a circuit where the output toggles whenever the input is high, effectively behaving like a toggle flip-flop when 'in' is held high.
