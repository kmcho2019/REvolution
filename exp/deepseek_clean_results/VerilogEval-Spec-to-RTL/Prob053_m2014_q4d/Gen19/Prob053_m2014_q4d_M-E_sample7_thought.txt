Here's a novel approach using a clock-gated XOR feedback loop:

1. Instead of directly feeding the XOR output to the flip-flop, we'll create a clock-gated feedback path
2. The XOR operation will be split into two parts:
   - A combinational XOR of the current 'in' and 'out'
   - A gated version that only updates when the clock is high
3. This creates a pseudo-latched feedback loop that's more tolerant to glitches
4. The flip-flop still captures the XOR result on the positive edge
5. Benefits:
   - More robust against timing violations
   - Clear separation of combinational and sequential logic
   - Easier timing closure
   - Maintains identical functionality but with better timing characteristics
