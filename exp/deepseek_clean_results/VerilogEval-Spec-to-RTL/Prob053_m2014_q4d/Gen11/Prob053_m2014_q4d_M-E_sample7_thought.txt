Here's a novel approach using two flip-flops to break the potential combinational loop and ensure proper timing:
1. Use two cascaded D flip-flops (a 2-stage pipeline)
2. First flip-flop stores the current state
3. Second flip-flop stores the XOR result of 'in' and the first flip-flop's output
4. This creates a one-cycle delay in the feedback path
5. The output comes from the second flip-flop
6. This approach guarantees stable operation by preventing any direct combinational feedback

Advantages:
- Eliminates any potential race conditions
- Provides clean separation between combinational and sequential logic
- More robust timing characteristics
- Easier to meet timing constraints in physical implementation
