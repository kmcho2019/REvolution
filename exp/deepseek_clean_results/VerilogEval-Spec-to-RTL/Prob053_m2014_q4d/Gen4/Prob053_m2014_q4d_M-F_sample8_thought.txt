The feedback correctly identifies the race condition in the original implementation. Here's the corrected approach:
1. The XOR operation should be purely combinational, operating on the current 'in' and the flip-flop's current output
2. The flip-flop should store this XOR result at the positive clock edge
3. The flip-flop's output then feeds back to the XOR for the next cycle's computation
4. This creates a proper synchronous circuit where:
   - The combinational XOR operates between clock edges
   - The sequential flip-flop captures stable values at clock edges
5. The key fix is separating the combinational and sequential parts clearly
