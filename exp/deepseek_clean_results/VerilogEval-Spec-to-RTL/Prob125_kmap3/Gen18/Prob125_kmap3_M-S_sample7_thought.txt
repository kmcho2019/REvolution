The Karnaugh map shows that the output is primarily dependent on inputs a and c, with b being relevant only in certain cases. By carefully analyzing the map and leveraging don't-care conditions, we can simplify the logic to:

1. Output is 1 when either:
   - a is 1 (right half of K-map)
   - c is 1 and b is 0 (top-left and bottom-left quadrants when a=0)

2. This reduces to: out = a | (~b & c)
3. This implementation uses only 3 gates (1 NOT, 1 AND, 1 OR) but with a more direct expression
4. The don't-care conditions allow us to ignore d completely and simplify the b terms
