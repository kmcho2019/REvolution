The current implementation is already quite efficient, but we can explore a slight optimization by observing the K-map more carefully:

1. Looking at the K-map patterns:
   - When a=1, output is always 1 (all entries in the right two columns)
   - When a=0, output is 1 when either:
     * c=1 and b=0 (top-left corner)
     * b=1 (bottom two rows except first column)

2. This leads to an equivalent but potentially more optimized expression:
   out = a | (c & ~b) | (b & ~a)

3. Benefits of this approach:
   - Same logical coverage as original
   - May map better to standard cell libraries
   - Could potentially reduce gate count
   - Maintains same minimal logic depth (1 level)
   - Still handles don't-cares optimally

4. Expected improvements:
   - Slight area reduction possible
   - Same or better timing
   - Similar power characteristics
