The Karnaugh map shows that the output is primarily determined by combinations of a, b, and c, with d being a don't-care. The minimal logic expression that covers all the 1s in the map while properly handling don't-cares is: out = a | (~b & c). This requires just one NOT, one AND, and one OR operation, which is simpler than the previous solution that used two ORs and one AND. The d input can be completely ignored as it doesn't affect the output.
