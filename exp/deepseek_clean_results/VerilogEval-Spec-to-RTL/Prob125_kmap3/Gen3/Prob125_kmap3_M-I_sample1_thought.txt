To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, let's analyze it systematically:

1. First, identify all cases where output is definitely 1:
   - When c=0: (ab=10 or ab=11) except cd=00,ab=00
   - When c=1: (ab=10 or ab=11) and also cd=11,ab=01

2. Identify cases where output is definitely 0:
   - cd=00,ab=00
   - cd=01,ab=00
   - cd=01,ab=01
   - cd=11,ab=00

3. Don't-care cases (marked 'd') can be treated as 1 to simplify the logic:
   - cd=01,ab=10
   - cd=01,ab=11

The optimal expression is:
out = (~c & (a ^ b)) | (c & (a | b))

This covers:
- When c=0: output is 1 when a and b are different (XOR)
- When c=1: output is 1 when either a or b is 1
- All specified 0 outputs are preserved
- Don't-care conditions are used to minimize the logic
