The Karnaugh map shows that the output is primarily determined by inputs a, b, and c, with d being a don't-care. The minimal logic expression derived from the map is:
out = a | (~b & c)

This is simpler than the previous solution (a | c) & (a | ~b) because:
1. It uses only one OR and one AND operation instead of two ORs and one AND
2. The expression directly matches the minimal sum-of-products form from the K-map
3. It maintains the same functionality while reducing gate count
4. The d input remains unused as specified
