The original solution uses a single continuous assignment with a minimal logical expression. For refactoring, we can:
1. Break down the logic into intermediate signals for better readability and potential synthesis flexibility
2. Use explicit wire declarations to make the design hierarchy clearer
3. Maintain the same logical function (a OR (NOT b AND c)) but express it differently
4. Keep the optimization benefits while making the code more modular
