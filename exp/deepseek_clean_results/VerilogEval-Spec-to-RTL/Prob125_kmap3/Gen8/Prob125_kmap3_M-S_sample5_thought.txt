The Karnaugh map shows that when c=0, the output follows 'a' (with some don't-care conditions). When c=1, the output is 1 except when a=0 and b=1. This can be further simplified by observing that:
1. When c=0: output = a (regardless of b or d)
2. When c=1: output = ~(~a & b) which is equivalent to (a | ~b)
The ternary operation can be replaced with a simple OR operation by recognizing that:
out = (c & (a | ~b)) | (~c & a)
This can be simplified to:
out = a | (c & ~b)
This reduces the logic to just one OR and one AND operation, eliminating the need for a ternary operator.
