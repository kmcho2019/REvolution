To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll analyze the Karnaugh map more carefully and derive a minimal logic expression:

1. Observing the K-map:
   - When a=1 (right two columns), output is always 1 except for cd=01 case (which is don't-care)
   - When a=0 (left two columns):
     * Output is 0 when b=1 (from cd=01 and cd=11 rows)
     * Output follows c when b=0

2. Key optimization insights:
   - The output can be expressed as: out = a | (~a & ~b & c)
   - This reduces to a single OR and AND gate with inverters
   - Eliminates the mux hierarchy, reducing logic depth
   - Uses fewer gates (3 total: NOT, AND, OR) compared to mux implementation
   - Better exploits don't-cares by choosing simplest implementation

3. Expected improvements:
   - Reduced area (fewer gates)
   - Lower power (simpler logic, fewer transitions)
   - Similar or better timing (shorter critical path)
