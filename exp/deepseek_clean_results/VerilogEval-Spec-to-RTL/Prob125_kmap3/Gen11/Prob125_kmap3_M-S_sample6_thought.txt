After analyzing the Karnaugh map and the current implementation, we can simplify the logic by observing:
1. The output is always 1 when a=1 (from K-map columns where a=1)
2. When a=0, the output equals c except when both b=1 and c=1 (from K-map analysis)
3. This can be expressed as: out = a OR (c AND NOT b)
4. This single Boolean expression replaces the two-level mux structure while maintaining the same functionality
5. The don't-care conditions are handled naturally by this simplified expression
