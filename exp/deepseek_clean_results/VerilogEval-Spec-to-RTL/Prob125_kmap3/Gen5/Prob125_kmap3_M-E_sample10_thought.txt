After analyzing the Karnaugh map, I propose a novel approach that:
1. Observes that the output is 1 in most cases when c=1 (bottom two rows)
2. For c=0, output is 1 only when a=1 and cd=00 (first row)
3. Uses a clever combination of XOR and AND operations to minimize logic
4. Exploits don't-care conditions to simplify the expression
The key insight is that when c=1, output is 0 only for ab=01, otherwise 1
When c=0, output is 1 only when a=1 and d=0 (cd=00)
This leads to: out = c ? (a | ~b) : (a & ~d)
This implementation uses fewer gates and has better power characteristics
