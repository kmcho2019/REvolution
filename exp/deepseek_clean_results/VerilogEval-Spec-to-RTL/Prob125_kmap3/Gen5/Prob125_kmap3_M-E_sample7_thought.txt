Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based implementation:
1. Observe that the output depends heavily on the value of 'c'
2. When c=1, the output is primarily determined by (a OR ~b)
3. When c=0, the output depends on a AND ~d
4. This suggests using 'c' as a selector for a 2:1 mux
5. The mux will select between (a OR ~b) when c=1 and (a AND ~d) when c=0
6. This implementation reduces logic depth and potentially improves timing
