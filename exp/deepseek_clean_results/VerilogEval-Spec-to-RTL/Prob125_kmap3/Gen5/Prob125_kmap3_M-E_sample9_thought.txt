After analyzing the Karnaugh map and considering PPA optimization, I propose a novel approach:
1. Recognize that the output is primarily determined by 'a' and 'b' when 'c' is high
2. When 'c' is low, the output depends on 'a' and 'd' only for the first row (cd=00)
3. Implement a more optimized logic by:
   - Using 'c' to select between two simplified cases
   - For c=1: Output is 1 unless ab=01 (a=0 and b=1)
   - For c=0: Output is 1 only when a=1 and (d=0 or we don't care)
   - This reduces the logic to just AND/OR operations without explicit NOT gates
4. The final expression becomes: out = c ? (a | ~b) : (a & ~d)
