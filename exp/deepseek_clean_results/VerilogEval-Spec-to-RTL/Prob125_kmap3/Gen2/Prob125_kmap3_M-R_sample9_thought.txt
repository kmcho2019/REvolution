To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, I'll analyze the patterns more carefully:
1. The output is 1 in most cases when (a XOR b) is true, except when cd=00 and ab=01
2. For (a XOR b)=false cases (ab=00 or 11), output is 1 only when cd=10 or 11 and b=1
3. Don't-cares can be used to simplify the cd=01 cases when ab=10 or 11 to 1
The minimal expression is: out = (a ^ b) ? (~(c==0 & d==0 & a & ~b) & (c | d | a | b)) : (b & (c | d))
This ensures:
- When a≠b, output is 1 unless special case (cd=00 and ab=01)
- When a=b, output follows b AND (c OR d)
