Let's refactor the mux-based implementation to use direct boolean logic expressions instead, while maintaining the same functionality. Here's the approach:
1. Analyze the original mux structure: when a=1, output is 1; when a=0, output is (b ? 0 : c)
2. Convert this to a boolean expression: out = a OR (NOT a AND NOT b AND c)
3. Simplify using boolean algebra: out = a OR (NOT b AND c) since (a OR NOT a) covers all cases
4. This results in a single-level logic expression that's functionally equivalent but potentially more efficient
5. The advantage is reduced logic depth (single expression vs two mux levels) and better utilization of don't-cares
