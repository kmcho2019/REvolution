The Karnaugh map shows that the output is primarily determined by 'a' and 'c', with 'b' acting as a modifier only when a=0. We can simplify the logic to:
1. When a=1, output is always 1 (matches all 1's in right half of K-map)
2. When a=0, output equals c AND NOT b (matches left half patterns)
This reduces the two-level mux to a single AND-OR structure, saving area and power while maintaining the same functionality.
Don't-cares are handled by choosing the most convenient logic configuration.
