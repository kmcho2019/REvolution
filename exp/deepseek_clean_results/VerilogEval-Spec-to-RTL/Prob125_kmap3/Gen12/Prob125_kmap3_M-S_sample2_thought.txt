The current implementation is already minimal with just 3 gates. However, we can observe that when a=1, the output is always 1 regardless of other inputs. When a=0, the output is c AND NOT b. This can be expressed more simply as: out = a OR (c AND NOT b). This reduces the logic to just 2 gates (AND and OR) plus one inverter, while maintaining the exact same functionality as the original K-map specification.
