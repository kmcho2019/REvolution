To implement the given Karnaugh map, we can observe that the output is 1 in the following cases:
1. When c=0 and ab=10 or ab=11 (top-right two cells of first row)
2. When c=1 and (ab=00 or ab=10 or ab=11) except when cd=01 (second row)
3. Don't cares (d) can be treated as 1 to simplify the logic

The simplified logic equation is: out = (~c & (a ^ b)) | (c & (~d | b))
This covers all the 1's in the K-map while optimally using the don't-care conditions.
