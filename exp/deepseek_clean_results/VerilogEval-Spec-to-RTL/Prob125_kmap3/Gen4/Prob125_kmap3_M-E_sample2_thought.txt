Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical decision structure that prioritizes the most significant bits first:

1. First, observe that when c=0 and d=0 (cd=00), the output depends primarily on ab:
   - ab=10 or 11 → out=1
   - ab=00 → out=0
   - ab=01 → don't-care (we'll choose 0 for consistency)

2. For cd=01, the output is 0 for ab=00 or 01, and we'll choose 1 for ab=10,11 to simplify logic

3. For cd=11 or cd=10, output is 1 for all ab except 00 (where it's 0)

4. This suggests a priority-based implementation:
   - First check if cd=00 (special case)
   - Then check if ab=00 (output 0)
   - Otherwise output 1

5. The logic can be implemented as:
   out = ~(~c & ~d) & ~(~a & ~b)
   This means output is 1 unless:
   - cd=00 (first term becomes 0)
   - or ab=00 (second term becomes 0)

This implementation uses only 4 gates (2 ANDs and 2 NOTs) and properly handles all cases and don't-cares.
