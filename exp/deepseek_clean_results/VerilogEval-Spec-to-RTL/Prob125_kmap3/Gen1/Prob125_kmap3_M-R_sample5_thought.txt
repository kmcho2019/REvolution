After carefully re-examining the Karnaugh map, I realize the original solution missed some key patterns. The correct implementation should:
1. Output 1 when (a XOR b) is true AND (c OR d is 1) - this covers most 1s in the map
2. Specifically handle the cd=00 case where output is 0 when ab=00
3. Use don't-care conditions to simplify the logic
The minimal expression is: out = (a ^ b) & (c | (a & b)) | (b & ~c & ~d)
This properly covers all cases while utilizing don't-cares optimally.
