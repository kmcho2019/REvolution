After carefully analyzing the Karnaugh map again, I realize the previous solution missed some key patterns. The correct approach is to identify all the 1s in the map and group them optimally, using don't-cares to maximize grouping. The output should be 1 when:
1. (a XOR b) is 1 (covers most 1s in the map)
2. OR when (c is 1 AND d is 0) for the remaining cases
This gives us a simpler and correct implementation: out = (a ^ b) | (c & ~d)
