The current implementation is already quite efficient, but we can explore a slight optimization by rearranging the Boolean expression to potentially reduce the number of operations:

1. Original expression: out = a | (~a & ~b & c)
2. We can apply Boolean algebra to factor out ~a:
   out = a | (~a & (~b & c))
3. This can be rewritten using the identity (x | (~x & y)) = x | y:
   out = a | (~b & c)
4. The new expression is mathematically equivalent but uses one fewer operation (eliminates the ~a term)
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced gate count (from 3 operations to 2)
   - Potentially better synthesis results
   - Maintains exact same functionality
6. PPA impact expected:
   - Same timing (still single-level logic)
   - Slight area reduction
   - Possibly lower power due to fewer operations
7. Don't-cares remain handled the same way
