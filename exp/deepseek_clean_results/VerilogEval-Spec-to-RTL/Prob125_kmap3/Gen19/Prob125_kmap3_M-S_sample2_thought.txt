Looking at the Karnaugh map more carefully, we can observe that the output is 1 in all cases where either:
1. a is 1 (right half of the map), or
2. c is 1 (bottom two rows of the map)

This leads to an even simpler logical expression: out = a | c. The don't-care conditions allow us to make this simplification while still maintaining correct functionality for all specified cases. This reduces the logic to just a single OR gate.
