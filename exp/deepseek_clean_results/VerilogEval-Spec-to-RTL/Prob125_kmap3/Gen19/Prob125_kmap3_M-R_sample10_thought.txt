Refactoring approach:
1. Maintain the same logical function (a OR (NOT (a OR b) AND c))
2. Change implementation style from single continuous assignment to structural modeling
3. Break down the expression into intermediate wires for better readability and potential optimization
4. Use named intermediate signals to make the logic flow clearer
5. Potential benefits:
   - More explicit gate-level representation
   - Easier to modify individual logic components
   - Better for hierarchical designs
   - May allow synthesis tools to optimize differently
6. The refactored version uses:
   - An OR gate for (a OR b)
   - A NOT gate for inversion
   - An AND gate for the final term
   - A final OR gate for the output
