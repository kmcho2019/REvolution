The Karnaugh map can be simplified to the expression: out = a | (~b & c). This captures all the essential cases:
1. When a is 1, out is always 1 (covering the right two columns of the map)
2. When a is 0, out is 1 only when both ~b and c are true (covering the top-right and bottom-left cells when a=0)
This eliminates one operation (the ~a term) compared to the previous solution while maintaining correct functionality.
