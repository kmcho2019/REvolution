Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer tree structure that leverages the don't-care conditions to optimize the logic:

1. Key Insight: The output depends primarily on 'a' and 'c' with 'b' playing a secondary role
2. Novel Structure:
   - First level mux selects between c=0 and c=1 cases
   - For c=0: Output is simply 'a' (from K-map observation)
   - For c=1: Use a second mux to handle the b-dependence
3. Optimization:
   - Exploit don't-cares in the c=1 case to simplify the b logic
   - When a=1, output is always 1 regardless of b (from K-map)
   - When a=0, output equals ~b
4. Benefits:
   - Clear hierarchical structure
   - Efficient use of don't-cares
   - Only requires 2 muxes and 1 NOT gate
   - Natural implementation using ternary operators
