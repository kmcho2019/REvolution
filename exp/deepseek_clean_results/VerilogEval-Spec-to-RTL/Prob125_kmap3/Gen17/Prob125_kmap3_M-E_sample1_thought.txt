Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based implementation that leverages the don't-care conditions more aggressively:

1. Observation:
   - The output is 1 in most cases when a=1 (except cd=00)
   - When a=0, the output depends on b and c
   - This suggests a hierarchical decision structure

2. New Architecture:
   - Use a as the primary selector for a 2:1 mux
   - When a=1, output is 1 (simplifying many cases)
   - When a=0, use a secondary mux based on b and c
   - This reduces the logic complexity by exploiting the don't-cares more effectively

3. Benefits:
   - Potentially better timing as the critical path is through just two mux levels
   - Clear hierarchical structure that might map well to FPGA LUTs
   - More explicit handling of don't-care conditions

4. Implementation:
   - First mux selects between 1 (when a=1) and a secondary expression
   - Secondary expression is (~b & c) which covers all required cases when a=0
