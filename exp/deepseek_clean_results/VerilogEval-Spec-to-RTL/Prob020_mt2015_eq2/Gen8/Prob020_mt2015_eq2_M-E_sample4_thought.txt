Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based equality checker. Instead of directly comparing bits, we'll:
1. Concatenate A and B to form a 4-bit selector
2. Use this selector to choose between 1 (equal) or 0 (not equal) from a 16-entry lookup
3. The lookup pattern is designed such that only the diagonal entries (where A=B) output 1

This method demonstrates how equality checking can be implemented using selection logic rather than direct comparison. While less efficient than the standard approach, it showcases an alternative architectural perspective.
