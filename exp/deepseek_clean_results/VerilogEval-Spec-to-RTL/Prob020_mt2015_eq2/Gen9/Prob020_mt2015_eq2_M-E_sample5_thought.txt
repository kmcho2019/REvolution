This implementation uses a creative combination of bitwise operations to determine equality:
1. First, we perform a bitwise XNOR between A and B (each bit pair)
2. Then we use a reduction AND operator to check if all bits are equal
3. The XNOR acts as an equality gate (outputs 1 when inputs are equal)
4. The reduction AND ensures all bit pairs must be equal for z to be 1

This approach is elegant because:
- It directly maps to the mathematical definition of equality
- Uses minimal logical operations
- Has excellent timing characteristics (single gate delay path)
- Scales well to wider buses
- Shows how basic logic operations can solve comparison problems
