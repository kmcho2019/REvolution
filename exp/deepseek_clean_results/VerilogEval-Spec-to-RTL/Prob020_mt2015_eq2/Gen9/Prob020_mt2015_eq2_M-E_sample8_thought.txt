This alternative implementation decomposes the equality check into its fundamental logic operations:
1. Compares each bit pair individually using XNOR gates (bit equality)
2. Combines the bit-wise results with an AND gate
3. This approach:
   - Makes the bit-level operations explicit
   - Could be more flexible for future modifications
   - Shows the underlying hardware structure clearly
   - Might offer better optimization opportunities in some synthesis tools
   - Demonstrates an alternative way to think about equality comparison
