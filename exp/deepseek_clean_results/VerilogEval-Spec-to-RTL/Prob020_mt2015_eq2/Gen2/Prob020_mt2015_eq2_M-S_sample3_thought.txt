The simplest possible implementation is already achieved with the direct equality comparison (A == B). This is:
1. A single-line assignment that clearly expresses the intended functionality
2. The most readable and maintainable form
3. Automatically optimized by synthesis tools to the most efficient gate-level implementation
4. Mathematically equivalent to (A[1] == B[1]) & (A[0] == B[0]) but more concise
No further simplification is possible while maintaining full functionality.
