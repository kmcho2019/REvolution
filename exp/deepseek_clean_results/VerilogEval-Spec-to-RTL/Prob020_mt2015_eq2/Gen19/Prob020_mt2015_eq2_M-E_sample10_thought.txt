Novel Approach: Bitwise XOR with Parallel Reduction
1. Use XOR gates to compare each bit pair individually (A[1] vs B[1] and A[0] vs B[0])
2. The XOR results will be 0 when bits match, 1 when they differ
3. Combine the XOR results using a NOR gate instead of the traditional AND approach
4. This exploits De Morgan's laws in a novel way for equality checking
5. The NOR gate will output 1 only when both XOR results are 0 (all bits equal)
6. This approach demonstrates how logical equivalences can lead to alternative circuit implementations
7. Potentially offers better delay characteristics in some technologies due to NOR implementation
