The simplest and most efficient way to implement a 2-bit equality comparator is to use a direct equality operator (==). This approach:
1. Directly compares the two 2-bit inputs A and B
2. Produces output z=1 when they are equal, z=0 otherwise
3. Is more concise and readable than the case statement approach
4. Will likely synthesize to optimal hardware (typically a dedicated comparator structure)
5. Has better PPA characteristics (performance, power, area) than the LUT-based approach
