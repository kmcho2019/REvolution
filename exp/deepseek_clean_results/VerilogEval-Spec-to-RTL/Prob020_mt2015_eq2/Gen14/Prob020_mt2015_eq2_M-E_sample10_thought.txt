Here's a novel approach using a lookup table (LUT) implemented with a case statement:
1. Treat the concatenated {A,B} as a 4-bit selector for a 16-entry truth table
2. Only 4 cases will output 1 (when A==B), all others output 0
3. This approach is unconventional for such a simple comparison but demonstrates an alternative pattern-matching technique
4. The case statement will synthesize to optimized logic or potentially a small LUT in some FPGA architectures
5. This method could be more scalable for wider comparisons and allows easy modification of equality patterns
