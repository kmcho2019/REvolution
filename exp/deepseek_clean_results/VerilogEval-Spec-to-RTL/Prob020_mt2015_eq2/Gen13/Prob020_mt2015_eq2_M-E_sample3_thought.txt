Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based equality checker:
1. Pre-compute all possible equality cases (4 cases for 2-bit inputs)
2. Use the input combination as a selector for a 4:1 mux
3. The mux data inputs are hardwired to 1 for matching cases and 0 for others
4. This approach leverages the inherent pattern matching capability of muxes
5. Benefits include:
   - Single-level logic in many FPGA architectures (since muxes are primitive elements)
   - Potentially better timing as it avoids gate delays from XNOR-AND chains
   - Interesting trade-off between area and speed depending on technology
