The fusion strategy combines the strengths of both examples while optimizing for PPA:
1. Use XNOR gates for bitwise comparison (from Example 1) as they directly implement equality check
2. Use AND combination (from Example 1) instead of NOR (from Example 2) to eliminate the need for inversion
3. This approach:
   - Maintains minimal logic depth (2 levels: XNOR + AND)
   - Uses fewer gates than Example 2 (2 XNOR + 1 AND vs 2 XOR + 1 NOR)
   - Avoids the power overhead of inversion in Example 2
   - Provides clear bit-level visibility like Example 1
   - Could potentially map better to standard cell libraries
   - Offers better power characteristics by reducing switching activity
