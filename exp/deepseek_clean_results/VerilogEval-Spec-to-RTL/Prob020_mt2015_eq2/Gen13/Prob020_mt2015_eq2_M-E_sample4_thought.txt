This implementation uses a creative combination of bitwise operations to detect equality:
1. First, we perform bitwise XNOR between A and B (each bit pair)
2. Then we use a creative OR-NOR combination to detect when all bits are equal
3. The key insight is that (A XNOR B) gives 1 for equal bits, and we need ALL bits to be 1 for equality
4. Instead of a standard AND reduction, we use De Morgan's law implemented through OR and NOR for variety
5. This demonstrates how basic logic operations can be combined in non-obvious ways to achieve the same result
