Novel Approach: Bitwise XNOR with Reduction AND
1. **Key Insight**: Use XNOR gates for bitwise equality comparison, followed by a reduction AND to combine results.
2. **Implementation**: 
   - First, perform bitwise XNOR between A and B (XNOR gives 1 when bits are equal)
   - Then use a reduction AND to check if all bits are equal
3. **Advantages**:
   - More hardware-oriented implementation that directly maps to common digital logic gates
   - Shows an alternative way to think about equality comparison
   - Demonstrates use of bitwise operations and reduction operators
4. **Result**: Same functionality but with different underlying logic structure
