Here's a novel approach using a time-multiplexed architecture:

1. Instead of three separate flip-flops, use a single register to store all three states
2. Process each gate operation sequentially over three clock cycles using a counter
3. Maintain the feedback paths by storing previous results
4. Use a small state machine to cycle through the operations
5. Output the NOR result only after all three operations complete

Advantages:
- Reduced flip-flop count (3→1)
- Sequential processing saves area
- Clear separation of operations
- Maintains correct feedback relationships
