The problem requires implementing a finite state machine with three parallel paths:
1. XOR path: x XOR with its flip-flop's output
2. AND path: x AND with its flip-flop's complemented output
3. OR path: x OR with its flip-flop's complemented output

Each path goes through a D flip-flop, and the outputs of all three flip-flops are NOR'ed together to produce z. The flip-flops are initially reset to 0 and update on the positive clock edge.

The solution involves:
1. Creating three D flip-flops (DFFs) with synchronous reset
2. Implementing the three gates (XOR, AND, OR) with their respective inputs
3. Connecting the gate outputs to DFF inputs
4. NOR'ing the three DFF outputs to produce z
