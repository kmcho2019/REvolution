The problem requires implementing a finite state machine with three parallel paths, each consisting of a two-input gate (XOR, AND, OR) followed by a D flip-flop. The outputs of these flip-flops are then NOR'ed together to produce the final output z. The second inputs of the gates are derived from their respective flip-flop outputs (or their complements). Here's the design approach:

1. Create three D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the outputs of the three gates.
2. The XOR gate takes input x and its own DFF's output as inputs.
3. The AND gate takes input x and the complement of its own DFF's output as inputs.
4. The OR gate takes input x and the complement of its own DFF's output as inputs.
5. The outputs of all three DFFs are NOR'ed together to produce z.
6. All DFFs are synchronous and reset to 0 initially.
