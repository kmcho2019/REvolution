The problem requires implementing a finite state machine with three parallel paths:
1. XOR path: x XOR with its flip-flop output
2. AND path: x AND with complement of its flip-flop output
3. OR path: x OR with complement of its flip-flop output

Each path goes through a D flip-flop, and the outputs of all three flip-flops are NOR'ed together to produce z. The flip-flops are initially reset to 0 and update on positive clock edges.

The solution involves:
1. Creating three flip-flops with their respective input logic
2. Connecting their outputs to a 3-input NOR gate
3. Ensuring all flip-flops are initially reset to 0
