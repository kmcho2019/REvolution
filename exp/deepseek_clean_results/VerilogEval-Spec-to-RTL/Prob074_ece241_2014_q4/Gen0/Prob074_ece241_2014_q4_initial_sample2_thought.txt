The design involves three parallel paths, each consisting of a two-input gate followed by a D flip-flop. The outputs of these flip-flops are then NOR'ed together to produce the final output z. The key points are:
1. XOR gate: x XOR q_xor (feedback from its own flip-flop output)
2. AND gate: x AND ~q_and (feedback from complemented flip-flop output)
3. OR gate: x OR ~q_or (feedback from complemented flip-flop output)
4. All flip-flops are initially 0 and update on positive clock edges
5. Final output z is NOR of all three flip-flop outputs
