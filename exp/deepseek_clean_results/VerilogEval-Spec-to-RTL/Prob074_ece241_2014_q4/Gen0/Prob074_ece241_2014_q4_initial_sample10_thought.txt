1. Instantiate three D flip-flops (DFFs) with synchronous reset (initially 0)
2. Create the three gates with their respective inputs:
   - XOR: x and DFF output (feedback)
   - AND: x and complemented DFF output
   - OR: x and complemented DFF output
3. Connect the DFF outputs to a 3-input NOR gate for z
