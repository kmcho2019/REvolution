The design requires implementing three parallel paths where each path consists of:
1. A two-input logic gate (XOR, AND, OR)
2. A D flip-flop that stores the gate's output
3. The flip-flop outputs feed into a three-input NOR gate to produce z

For each path:
- XOR gate: input x and its flip-flop's output (feedback)
- AND gate: input x and complement of its flip-flop's output
- OR gate: input x and complement of its flip-flop's output

All flip-flops are clocked on the positive edge and initially reset to 0.
