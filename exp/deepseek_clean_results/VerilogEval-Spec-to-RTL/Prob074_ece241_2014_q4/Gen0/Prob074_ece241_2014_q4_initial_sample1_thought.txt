The problem requires implementing a finite state machine with three flip-flops whose inputs are driven by different logic gates (XOR, AND, OR) that each take the current input x and a feedback from their respective flip-flop outputs (or complemented outputs). The outputs of these flip-flops are then NOR'ed together to produce z. Here's the design approach:
1. Instantiate three D flip-flops with synchronous reset (initially 0).
2. The XOR gate takes x and the output of its flip-flop.
3. The AND gate takes x and the complemented output of its flip-flop.
4. The OR gate takes x and the complemented output of its flip-flop.
5. The outputs of all three flip-flops are fed into a 3-input NOR gate to produce z.
