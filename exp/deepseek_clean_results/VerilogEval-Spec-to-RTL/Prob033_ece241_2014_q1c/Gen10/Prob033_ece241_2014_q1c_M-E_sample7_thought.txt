Novel Architecture: Hybrid Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Prediction
1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit carry-select blocks to parallelize computation
2. Compute both possible sums (with carry=0 and carry=1) for the upper 4 bits in parallel
3. Use the lower 4-bit sum to select the correct upper sum, reducing critical path
4. Implement early overflow prediction by:
   - Checking MSB sign consistency during parallel sum computation
   - Using intermediate carry signals to predict overflow before final sum is ready
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Early overflow detection (parallel with sum computation)
   - Balanced timing between sum and overflow outputs
   - Scalable to wider datapaths by adding more select stages
