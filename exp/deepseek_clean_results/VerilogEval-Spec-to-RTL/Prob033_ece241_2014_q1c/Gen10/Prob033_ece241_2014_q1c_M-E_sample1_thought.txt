Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection
1. Instead of traditional carry-lookahead, use carry-select adder structure for better area-time tradeoff
2. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks (low and high nibbles)
3. Compute both possible results for high nibble (with carry=0 and carry=1)
4. Use the actual carry-out from low nibble to select correct high nibble result
5. Implement early overflow detection by:
   - Checking MSB sign bits of inputs and potential results
   - Determining overflow condition before final result selection
6. Benefits:
   - Parallel computation reduces critical path
   - Early overflow detection improves timing
   - Modular structure is scalable
   - Balanced area and performance
