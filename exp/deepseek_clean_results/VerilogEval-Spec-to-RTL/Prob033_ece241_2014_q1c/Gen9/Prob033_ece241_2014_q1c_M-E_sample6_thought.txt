Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection

Key Features:
1. Uses a carry-select adder structure for parallel computation of possible sums
2. Implements early overflow detection by examining MSB propagation before full addition
3. Combines the benefits of carry-select (speed) with optimized overflow logic
4. Divides the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments with carry prediction
5. Computes overflow in parallel with sum computation for critical path optimization

Advantages:
- Potentially faster critical path than traditional approaches
- Early overflow detection reduces timing pressure on overflow output
- Modular structure allows for easy scaling to wider datapaths
- Balanced logic distribution between sum and overflow computation
