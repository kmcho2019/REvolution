Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection

1. Instead of a pure carry-lookahead approach, use a hybrid carry-select adder that:
   - Divides the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks
   - Computes both possible sums (with carry-in 0 and 1) for the upper 4 bits in parallel
   - Selects the correct sum based on the actual carry-out from the lower 4 bits

2. Early Overflow Detection:
   - Compute overflow conditions in parallel with the addition
   - Use sign bit analysis to detect overflow without waiting for full sum computation
   - Special handling for the boundary case where carry propagates across bit 6 to 7

3. Benefits:
   - Potentially faster critical path since upper bits don't wait for carry propagation
   - Early overflow detection can be useful in pipelined designs
   - Balanced logic depth between sum and overflow computation
