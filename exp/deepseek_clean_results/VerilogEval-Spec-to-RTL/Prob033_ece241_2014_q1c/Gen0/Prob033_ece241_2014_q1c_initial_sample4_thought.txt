To implement an 8-bit signed adder with overflow detection:
1. Perform regular addition of the two 8-bit inputs a and b
2. The sum s is simply the 8-bit result of this addition
3. For signed overflow detection, we need to check if both inputs have the same sign but the result has a different sign
4. Specifically, overflow occurs when:
   - Both a and b are positive (MSB=0) and sum is negative (MSB=1)
   - Both a and b are negative (MSB=1) and sum is positive (MSB=0)
