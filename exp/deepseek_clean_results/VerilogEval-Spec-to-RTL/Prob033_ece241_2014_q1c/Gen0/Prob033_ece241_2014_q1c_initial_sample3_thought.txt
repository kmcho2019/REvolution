To implement an 8-bit signed adder with overflow detection:
1. Perform standard 8-bit addition of inputs a and b
2. The sum s is simply the 8-bit result of this addition
3. For signed overflow detection, we need to check if:
   - Adding two positive numbers (MSB=0) results in a negative number (MSB=1)
   - Adding two negative numbers (MSB=1) results in a positive number (MSB=0)
4. Overflow occurs when the carry into the MSB differs from the carry out of the MSB
