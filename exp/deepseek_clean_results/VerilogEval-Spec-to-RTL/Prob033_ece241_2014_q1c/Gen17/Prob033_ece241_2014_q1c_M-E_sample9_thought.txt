Novel Approach: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection
1. Divide the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments (lower and upper nibbles)
2. Implement carry-select logic for the upper nibble (computing both possible carry-in scenarios)
3. Use the lower nibble's carry-out to select the correct upper nibble result
4. Detect overflow early by examining the MSB carry behavior and sign bits
5. Benefits:
   - Parallel computation of both carry scenarios reduces critical path
   - Early overflow detection by comparing sign bits and carry behavior
   - Balanced logic depth improves timing
   - Modular structure enables easy scaling to wider datapaths
