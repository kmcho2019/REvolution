Alternative approach using carry signals for signed overflow detection:
1. Perform 8-bit addition while keeping track of the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB
2. For signed numbers, overflow occurs when the carry-in to the MSB differs from the carry-out of the MSB
3. This method is more efficient as it reuses the carry chain from the adder rather than performing separate sign checks
4. The implementation will use a full 8-bit adder and examine the carry bits at the MSB position
