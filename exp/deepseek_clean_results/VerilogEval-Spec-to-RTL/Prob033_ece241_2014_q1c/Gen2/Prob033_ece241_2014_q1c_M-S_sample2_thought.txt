To simplify the 8-bit signed adder with overflow detection:
1. Keep the basic 8-bit addition operation as it's fundamental
2. Optimize the overflow detection logic by:
   - Recognizing that overflow can be determined by comparing the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB
   - This eliminates the need for separate sign checks on inputs and output
3. The simplified overflow condition is:
   - Overflow occurs when carry-in to MSB ≠ carry-out from MSB
