Novel Architecture Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Detection
1. Instead of traditional carry-lookahead, use a carry-select approach with 2-bit blocks for better area-time tradeoff
2. Compute overflow in parallel with sum computation by:
   - Detecting potential overflow conditions during intermediate stages
   - Using sign bits and intermediate carry information
3. Key innovations:
   - 2-bit blocks for finer-grained carry selection
   - Early overflow prediction using block-level sign information
   - Shared logic between sum and overflow computation
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Early overflow detection (doesn't wait for final sum)
   - Balanced area and performance
   - Scalable to wider datapaths
