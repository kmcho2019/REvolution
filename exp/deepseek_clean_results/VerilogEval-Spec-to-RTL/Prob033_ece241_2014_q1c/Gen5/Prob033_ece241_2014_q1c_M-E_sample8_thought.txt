Novel Strategy: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Prediction
1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments (lower and upper)
2. Perform parallel additions for the upper segment with both carry-in assumptions (0 and 1)
3. Use the actual carry-out from lower segment to select the correct upper sum
4. Predict overflow early by:
   - Comparing upper segment additions when signs match
   - Detecting if either potential sum would cause overflow
5. Benefits:
   - Faster critical path than ripple-carry (4-bit add instead of 8)
   - Early overflow determination (can be ready before final sum selection)
   - Power savings from gating unused adder paths
   - More regular structure for better physical implementation
