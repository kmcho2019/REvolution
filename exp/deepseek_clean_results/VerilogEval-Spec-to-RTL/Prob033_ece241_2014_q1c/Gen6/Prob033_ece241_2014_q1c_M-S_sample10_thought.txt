Simplification Strategy:
1. The sum calculation is already minimal - keep as is
2. Overflow detection can be expressed more concisely by recognizing that:
   - Overflow occurs when carry-in and carry-out of the MSB differ
   - This can be detected by XORing the carry-in and carry-out of the sign bit
3. This reduces the overflow logic to a single XOR operation on the carries

Key optimizations:
- Maintain same sum calculation
- Replace 3 operations (2 XORs and 1 AND) with just 1 XOR operation
- More direct implementation of the overflow condition
