Simplification Strategy:
1. Use a simple ripple-carry adder instead of carry-lookahead for 8-bit width:
   - For small bit widths (8 bits), ripple-carry is simpler and nearly as fast
   - Reduces logic complexity significantly
2. Simplify overflow detection:
   - Keep the same logical expression but implement it directly
   - Remove intermediate signal declarations where possible
3. Combine operations where feasible:
   - Compute sum and carry in a single always block
   - Use continuous assignments for clarity
4. Maintain correct signed overflow detection:
   - Overflow occurs when signs of inputs are same but result sign differs

The simplified solution will:
- Use less area by eliminating carry-lookahead logic
- Be easier to understand and maintain
- Maintain correct functionality
- Have comparable performance for 8-bit operations
