Simplification Approach:
1. Use a simple ripple-carry adder structure instead of carry-lookahead to reduce complexity
2. Compute sum and carry bits sequentially using a loop
3. Maintain the same overflow detection logic (sign bits comparison)
4. Benefits:
   - Much simpler implementation
   - Reduced area and power due to simpler logic
   - Maintains correct functionality
   - Easier to understand and modify
