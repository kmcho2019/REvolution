Improvement Strategy:
1. Power Optimization:
   - Replace the standard adder with a carry-select adder structure to reduce switching activity
   - This reduces power by minimizing unnecessary bit transitions during addition

2. Area Optimization:
   - Share comparator logic between the adder and overflow detection
   - Use a single XOR gate for sign comparison instead of separate comparators

3. Timing Consideration:
   - Maintain the single-cycle operation
   - The carry-select adder structure actually improves timing for wider bit widths (though minimal impact for 8-bit)

4. Implementation Approach:
   - Implement a 4+4 bit carry-select adder (optimal for 8-bit)
   - Compute overflow using shared sign comparison logic
   - Keep the design purely combinational for maximum speed

This approach should reduce both power and area while maintaining or improving timing characteristics.
