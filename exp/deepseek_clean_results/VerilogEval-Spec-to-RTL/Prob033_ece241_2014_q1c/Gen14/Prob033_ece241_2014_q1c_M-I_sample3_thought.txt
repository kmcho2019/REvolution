Improvement Strategy:
1. Replace ripple-carry with carry-lookahead adder (CLA) to reduce critical path delay
2. Group bits into 4-bit CLA blocks for better area-delay tradeoff
3. Maintain the same overflow detection logic (most efficient for signed addition)
4. Optimize generate/propagate terms to reduce unnecessary switching
5. Benefits:
   - ~30% faster critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Better timing (reduced WNS/TNS)
   - Slightly increased area for better performance
   - Maintains same power characteristics
