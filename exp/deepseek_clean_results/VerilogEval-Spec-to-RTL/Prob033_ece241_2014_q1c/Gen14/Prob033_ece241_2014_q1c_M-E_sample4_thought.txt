Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection

Key Features:
1. Hierarchical Carry-Select Structure:
   - Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks (lower and upper nibbles)
   - Compute both possible results for the upper nibble (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Select correct result based on actual carry-out from lower nibble

2. Early Overflow Detection:
   - Detect overflow conditions in parallel with addition
   - Uses sign bit analysis and carry information to determine overflow
   - Avoids waiting for full carry propagation

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Parallel computation enables faster operation
   - Early overflow detection improves timing
   - Modular structure scales well to wider adders

4. Implementation Details:
   - Lower 4-bit ripple-carry adder
   - Upper 4-bit dual-path computation
   - Multiplexer for final upper nibble selection
   - Overflow computed from sign bits and carries
