Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Detection

Key Features:
1. Carry-Select Adder Structure: Split into two 4-bit adders (lower and upper nibbles) to parallelize computation
2. Early Overflow Prediction: Compute overflow conditions in parallel with sum computation by examining MSB patterns
3. Conditional Carry Propagation: Use multiplexers to select between carry-in=0 and carry-in=1 cases for upper nibble
4. Optimized Critical Path: Overlap overflow detection with sum computation to reduce timing overhead
5. Resource Sharing: Reuse intermediate results for both sum and overflow computation

Advantages:
- Parallel computation of lower and upper nibbles reduces critical path
- Early overflow detection doesn't wait for final sum
- Balanced logic distribution between the two nibbles
- Efficient use of multiplexers for carry selection
