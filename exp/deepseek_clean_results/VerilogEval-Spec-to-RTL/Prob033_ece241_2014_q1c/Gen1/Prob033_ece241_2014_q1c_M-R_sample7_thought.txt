Refactored design approach:
1. Maintain the same signed addition logic but streamline the overflow detection
2. Eliminate separate sign bit wires since they're only used once
3. Perform the addition directly on 8-bit inputs and detect overflow using the carry-in and carry-out of the sign bit
4. This reduces redundant signal assignments and should improve PPA metrics
5. The overflow condition can be detected by comparing the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB (sign bit)
