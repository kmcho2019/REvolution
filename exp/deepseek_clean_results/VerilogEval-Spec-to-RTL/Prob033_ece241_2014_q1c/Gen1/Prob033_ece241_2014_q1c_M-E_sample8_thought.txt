Alternative approach using carry-lookahead principles:
1. Split the addition into sign-bit and magnitude handling
2. Compute the sum of lower 7 bits (magnitude) separately
3. Handle the sign-bit (MSB) addition with explicit overflow detection
4. Use the carry-out from magnitude addition to determine final sign
5. Overflow occurs when:
   - Sign bits differ but magnitude addition produces carry-out
   - Sign bits are same but result sign differs
This approach provides better visibility into the overflow conditions and could be more efficient in certain implementations.
