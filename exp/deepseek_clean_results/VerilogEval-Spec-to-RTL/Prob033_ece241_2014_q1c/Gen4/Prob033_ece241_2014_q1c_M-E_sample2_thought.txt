Novel Approach: Carry Look-Ahead Inspired Overflow Detection
Instead of using the traditional sign-bit comparison method for overflow detection, we can leverage the carry bits from the addition operation. For signed overflow in 2's complement, overflow occurs when:
1. The carry into the MSB (bit 7) differs from the carry out of the MSB
2. This is equivalent to checking if the carry-in and carry-out of the sign bit position don't match

Key advantages:
- Uses the natural carry propagation of the adder
- May have better timing characteristics as it doesn't depend on the sum result
- Conceptually cleaner as it directly relates to the mathematical definition of overflow
- Could potentially share some logic with the adder implementation
