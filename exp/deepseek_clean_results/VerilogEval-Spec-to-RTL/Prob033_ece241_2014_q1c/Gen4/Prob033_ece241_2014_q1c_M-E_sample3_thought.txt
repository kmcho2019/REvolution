Novel approach using carry-select addition with early overflow detection:
1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks (lower and upper nibbles)
2. Perform parallel additions for both possible carry-in scenarios (0 and 1) for the upper nibble
3. Use the actual carry-out from the lower nibble to select the correct upper sum
4. Detect overflow by comparing the carry-in and carry-out of the sign bit
5. This method provides potential timing benefits by parallelizing critical path operations
6. The overflow detection is simplified by observing the carry chain behavior at the MSB
