Novel Approach: Carry-Save Adder with Early Overflow Detection
1. Instead of traditional ripple-carry, use carry-save technique to parallelize carry computations
2. Compute generate (g) and propagate (p) terms in parallel for all bits
3. Use a tree structure to combine carries, reducing critical path
4. Implement early overflow detection by monitoring MSB behavior during addition
5. Key innovation: Predict overflow during intermediate stages rather than waiting for final carry

Advantages:
- Parallel processing of carry terms improves timing
- Early overflow detection reduces critical path
- Maintains simplicity while offering better performance
- Suitable for both ASIC and FPGA implementations
