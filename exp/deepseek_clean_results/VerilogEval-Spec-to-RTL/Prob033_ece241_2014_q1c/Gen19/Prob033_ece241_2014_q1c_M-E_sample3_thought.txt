Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection
1. Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments for better timing
2. Use carry-select approach to compute both possible sums (with carry=0/1) for upper bits
3. Implement early overflow detection by:
   - Checking sign bits of inputs and result
   - Combining with traditional carry-based detection
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Early overflow detection can be done in parallel with sum computation
   - Better timing for high-frequency operation
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Slightly increased area due to duplicate adders
   - More complex control logic
