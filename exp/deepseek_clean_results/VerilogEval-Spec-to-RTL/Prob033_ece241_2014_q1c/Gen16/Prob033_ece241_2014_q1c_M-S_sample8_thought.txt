Simplified Strategy:
1. Use basic ripple-carry adder structure for simplicity since we're optimizing for reduced complexity
2. Compute sum by XORing inputs with carry chain
3. Detect overflow by comparing the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB
4. Benefits:
   - Much simpler implementation with clear logic flow
   - Reduced area and power from eliminating hierarchical carry computation
   - Maintains correct functionality for 8-bit signed addition
   - Easier to understand and verify
5. Tradeoffs:
   - Slightly slower than hierarchical approaches (but acceptable for many applications)
   - Still maintains correct signed overflow detection
