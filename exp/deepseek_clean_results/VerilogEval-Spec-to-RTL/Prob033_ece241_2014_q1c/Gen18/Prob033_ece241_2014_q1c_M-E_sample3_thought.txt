Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection
1. Divide the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit chunks for parallel processing
2. Use carry-select approach to compute both possible results (carry=0 and carry=1) for upper 4 bits
3. Implement early overflow detection by:
   - Checking sign bits of inputs and result
   - Comparing with carry propagation between chunks
4. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to ripple-carry
   - Parallel computation of possible results
   - Early overflow determination without waiting for full carry chain
   - Better timing while maintaining reasonable area
5. Key Features:
   - Combines carry-select and overflow prediction
   - Optimized for 8-bit signed operations
   - Clear separation of lower and upper bit processing
