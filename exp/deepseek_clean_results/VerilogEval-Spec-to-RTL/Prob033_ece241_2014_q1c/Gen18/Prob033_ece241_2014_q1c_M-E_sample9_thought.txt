Novel Architecture Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection

Key Innovations:
1. Combines carry-select and ripple-carry techniques for balanced performance/area
2. Splits the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments (lower and upper nibbles)
3. Computes both possible carry scenarios (0 and 1) for upper nibble in parallel
4. Uses ripple-carry for lower nibble to maintain area efficiency
5. Early overflow detection by examining both possible upper nibble results
6. Benefits:
   - Reduced critical path compared to pure ripple-carry
   - Better area efficiency than full carry-select
   - Early termination of overflow calculation
   - Parallel computation of possible results

Implementation Details:
- Lower 4 bits use standard ripple-carry
- Upper 4 bits compute both carry=0 and carry=1 cases simultaneously
- Final carry selection happens after lower nibble computation
- Overflow detected by comparing MSB signs before final selection
