Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select with Early Overflow Detection

1. Core Concept:
   - Split the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments
   - Use carry-select approach for the upper 4 bits (computing both possible carry-in scenarios)
   - Implement early overflow detection by examining sign bits and carry propagation

2. Key Innovations:
   - Parallel computation of both possible carry scenarios (0 and 1) for upper bits
   - Early overflow prediction by comparing sign bits before full addition completes
   - Reduced critical path by eliminating carry propagation between segments
   - Optimized multiplexer selection based on lower segment's carry-out

3. Advantages:
   - Faster than ripple-carry due to parallel computation
   - More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
   - Early overflow detection reduces timing pressure on critical path
   - Balanced logic distribution between segments

4. Overflow Detection:
   - Check if the sum of two positive numbers yields negative result
   - Check if the sum of two negative numbers yields positive result
   - Special case handling for mixed-sign operands (never overflows)
