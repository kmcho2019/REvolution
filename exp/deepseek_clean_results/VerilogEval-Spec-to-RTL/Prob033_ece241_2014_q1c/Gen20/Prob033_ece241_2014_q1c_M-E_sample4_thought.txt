Novel Architectural Idea: Hybrid Carry-Select Adder with Early Overflow Detection

Key Features:
1. Combines carry-select and ripple-carry techniques for balanced performance/area
2. Splits the 8-bit addition into two 4-bit segments (lower and upper nibbles)
3. Computes both possible outcomes (carry=0/1) for the upper nibble in parallel
4. Uses ripple-carry for the lower nibble to determine actual carry-in for upper nibble
5. Early overflow detection by comparing MSB signs before full addition completes
6. Benefits:
   - Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced by parallel computation)
   - More area-efficient than full carry-select
   - Early overflow detection can potentially save power
   - Modular structure scales well to wider datapaths

Implementation Details:
- Lower 4 bits use ripple-carry
- Upper 4 bits compute both carry=0 and carry=1 cases in parallel
- Final output mux selects correct upper result based on lower carry-out
- Overflow detected by comparing input signs and result sign
