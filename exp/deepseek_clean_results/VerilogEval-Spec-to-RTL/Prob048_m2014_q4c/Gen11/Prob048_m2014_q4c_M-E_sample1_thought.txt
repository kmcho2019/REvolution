Instead of using a mux or conditional assignment, we can implement the D flip-flop with synchronous reset using:
1. Clock gating technique where we effectively disable the clock when reset is active
2. A transparent latch to hold the reset value when clock is gated
3. This approach reduces switching activity during reset conditions
4. The design separates the reset logic from the data path completely
5. Potentially better power efficiency since the flop doesn't toggle during reset
