To optimize the design, I'll implement a more power-efficient solution by:
1. Eliminating the hierarchical structure to reduce area overhead
2. Moving the reset logic inside the always block to prevent unnecessary combinational switching
3. Using a single always block for both reset and data capture to minimize power consumption
4. Removing intermediate wires to reduce routing resources

This approach will maintain the same functionality while improving power efficiency by reducing glitching and minimizing area by flattening the design.
