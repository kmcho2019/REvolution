I'll implement a D flip-flop with synchronous reset using a single always block approach, but with a novel twist: I'll use a ternary operator inside the sequential block to handle the reset condition. This eliminates the need for any combinational logic outside the flip-flop, reducing potential glitching power and making the reset path purely synchronous. The design will be more compact and have better timing characteristics since the reset logic is incorporated directly into the flip-flop's state update logic.
