Instead of using a mux, we can implement the synchronous reset by conditionally gating the clock signal:
1. When reset (r) is active, we suppress the clock to the flip-flop
2. This prevents the flip-flop from updating its state, effectively holding the reset value (0)
3. When reset is inactive, the clock passes through normally
4. This approach can potentially save power by reducing unnecessary clock toggles during reset
5. The flip-flop itself becomes a simple DFF without reset logic
