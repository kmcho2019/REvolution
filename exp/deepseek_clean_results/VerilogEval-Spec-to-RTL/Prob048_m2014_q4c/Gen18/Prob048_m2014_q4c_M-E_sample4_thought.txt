I'll implement a D flip-flop with synchronous reset using a single always block approach, but with a twist: I'll use a ternary operator inside the always block to handle the reset condition. This eliminates the need for any separate combinational logic or hierarchical modules. The design will be more compact and efficient since the reset logic is integrated directly into the flip-flop's behavior. This approach minimizes power by reducing unnecessary signal transitions and simplifies the design by keeping everything in one always block.
