I'll implement a D flip-flop with synchronous reset using a different approach that leverages clock gating for power optimization. Instead of gating the data input, I'll use the reset signal to control the clock input to the storage element. When reset is active, the clock is blocked, maintaining the output at 0. This approach reduces switching activity in the data path during reset conditions, potentially saving power. The design will use a simple AND gate for clock gating and a transparent latch to prevent glitches on the gated clock.
