Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Circular Buffer Implementation:
   - Instead of traditional shift operations, use a 4-bit circular buffer with head/tail pointers
   - The 'q' output is always the current 4-bit window of the buffer
   - Shifting becomes a matter of moving the window pointer rather than physical bit movement

2. Pointer-based Control:
   - Maintain a 2-bit pointer to track the current window position
   - Reset sets both buffer and pointer to zero
   - Load operation fills the entire buffer with new data and resets pointer
   - Shift operation increments the pointer (with wrap-around)

3. Advantages:
   - More efficient for potential future extensions (like adding left-shift capability)
   - Reduced physical bit movement during shifts (only pointer changes)
   - Easier to extend to larger widths
   - Interesting side-effect: Can implement circular rotation with minor modifications
