Innovative Approach:
1. Circular Buffer Concept: Instead of traditional shifting, implement a 4-position circular buffer with a pointer
2. Pointer-based Access: Maintain a write pointer to track the current position, enabling efficient rotations
3. Dual-Purpose Operation: Load operation writes to all positions while shift operation only moves the pointer
4. Power Optimization: Minimize bit toggles by only updating necessary bits during shifts
5. Timing Advantage: Reduced fanout compared to traditional shift implementations
6. Future Scalability: Easily extendable to larger widths without increasing critical path

Key Benefits:
- More efficient for frequent shift operations (only pointer update needed)
- Lower dynamic power during shifts (only one bit changes)
- Clear separation of control and data paths
- Potential for pipelining in more complex versions
