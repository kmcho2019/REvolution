Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Circular Buffer Approach: Instead of traditional shifting, use a pointer-based circular buffer to track the "start" position
2. Virtual Rotation: Maintain the register contents in a fixed storage with a dynamic offset to represent shifts
3. Explicit State Machine: Use a small FSM to handle the different modes (reset, load, shift)
4. Zero-Insert Optimization: For shifts, we can simply move the pointer and force MSB to zero rather than physical shifting
5. Edge Cases Handling: Special handling for full rotations (though not needed for 4-bit)

Advantages:
- Reduced physical shifting operations (only pointer updates)
- Potential power savings since fewer bits change state during shifts
- Easier extension to larger register sizes
- Clear separation of control and data paths
