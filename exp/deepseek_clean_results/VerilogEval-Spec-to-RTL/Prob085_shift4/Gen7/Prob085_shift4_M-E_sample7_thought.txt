Instead of using a traditional shift register implementation, we can:
1. Implement a 4-bit register with explicit bit-wise control
2. Use a rotating pointer to track the "start" of the register
3. For shift operations, we simply move the pointer rather than physically shifting bits
4. Only perform actual bit movement during load operations
5. This reduces switching activity during shifts since only the pointer changes

The key advantages are:
- Reduced power consumption during shift operations (only 1 bit changes)
- Potential timing improvement as the critical path is simplified
- More flexible architecture that could be extended to other operations
