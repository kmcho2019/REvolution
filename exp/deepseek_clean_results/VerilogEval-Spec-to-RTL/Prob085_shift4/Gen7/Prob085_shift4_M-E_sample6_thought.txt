Instead of directly implementing the shift register with conditional assignments, we can:
1. Separate the next-state logic from the register update
2. Use a 2-bit control signal to explicitly encode the operations (reset/load/shift/hold)
3. Implement a 4:1 multiplexer for the next-state selection
4. This approach makes the priority logic more explicit and may offer better synthesis results
5. The state encoding is:
   - 00: reset (highest priority)
   - 01: load
   - 10: shift
   - 11: hold
