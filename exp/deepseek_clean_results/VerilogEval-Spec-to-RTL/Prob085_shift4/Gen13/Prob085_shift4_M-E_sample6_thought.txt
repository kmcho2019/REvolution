Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Instead of traditional right-shift with zero-fill, implement a rotating shift register that naturally preserves bits
2. Use a mask control to selectively zero out the MSB during shift operations
3. This reduces the critical path by combining shift and zero-insertion into one operation
4. The mask control also simplifies the priority logic by making load and shift operations more orthogonal
5. Additional benefit: this structure could easily be extended to support both left and right shifts with minimal modification

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path by eliminating separate zero-insertion logic
- More efficient implementation of the shift operation
- Clean separation of load and shift functionality
- Potential for future extension to bidirectional shifting
