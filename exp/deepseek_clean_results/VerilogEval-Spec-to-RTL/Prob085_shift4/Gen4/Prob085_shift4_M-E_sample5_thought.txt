Instead of using traditional shift register implementation, we can:
1. Use a 4-bit register with a 2-bit pointer to track the "start" position
2. Implement the shift operation by moving the pointer rather than shifting bits
3. For load operations, reset the pointer to 0 and store the data
4. For shift operations, increment the pointer (mod 4)
5. The output is generated by rotating the register contents based on the pointer
6. This approach reduces physical bit movement during shifts, potentially saving power

Advantages:
- Reduced switching activity during shifts (only pointer changes)
- Constant-time access to all positions
- Easier to extend to larger widths
