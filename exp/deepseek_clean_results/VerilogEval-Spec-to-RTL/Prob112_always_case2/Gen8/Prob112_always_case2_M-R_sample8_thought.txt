The refactored design maintains the same priority encoding logic but restructures it to:
1. Use a more compact encoding scheme by combining the priority detection and position encoding in single expressions
2. Eliminate intermediate wires (p0-p3) to reduce area
3. Share common sub-expressions (like the NOR operations) to reduce power
4. Use direct assignment statements for better readability and potential synthesis optimization

The key improvements are:
- Combining the two logic stages into single expressions
- Reusing the NOR operations for multiple bits
- Removing unnecessary intermediate signals
- Maintaining the same logical functionality while potentially improving PPA
