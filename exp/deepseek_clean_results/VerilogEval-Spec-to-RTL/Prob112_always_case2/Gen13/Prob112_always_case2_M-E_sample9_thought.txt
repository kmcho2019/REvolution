Here's a novel approach using a binary tree structure for the priority encoder. The idea is to divide the 4-bit input into two 2-bit chunks and process them in parallel, then combine the results. This approach:

1. Processes both halves of the input simultaneously (bits [1:0] and bits [3:2])
2. Uses smaller 2-bit priority encoders for each half
3. Combines results with priority given to the lower bits
4. Potentially offers better timing characteristics due to parallel processing
5. Scales better for larger encoders (though not needed here)

The architecture detects activity in the lower half first. Only if the lower half is all zeros does it consider the upper half. This maintains the priority encoding behavior while potentially improving performance through parallelism.
