Here's a novel approach using a binary search technique for the priority encoder. Instead of checking bits sequentially or using bit manipulation, we'll divide the input into halves and recursively determine the highest priority bit. This approach has logarithmic complexity in terms of logic depth, which could be beneficial for larger input widths. For a 4-bit input, we'll:

1. First check the upper 2 bits (bits 3 and 2)
2. If any of them are set, we know the answer is in the upper half
3. Otherwise, we look in the lower 2 bits (bits 1 and 0)
4. Within each 2-bit segment, we apply the same logic to determine the exact position

This creates a hierarchical decision structure that may offer better timing characteristics for larger implementations while maintaining a clean and scalable design.
