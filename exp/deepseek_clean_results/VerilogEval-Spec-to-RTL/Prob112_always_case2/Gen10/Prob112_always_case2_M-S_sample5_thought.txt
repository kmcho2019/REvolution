To simplify the priority encoder, we can:
1. Remove the complex bit manipulation (in & ((~in) + 1)) which adds unnecessary logic
2. Use a straightforward priority encoding approach with if-else statements
3. Check bits from highest to lowest priority (bit 3 down to bit 0)
4. Default to 0 when no bits are set
This approach is simpler, more intuitive, and likely more efficient in terms of both power and area since it eliminates the intermediate bit manipulation operations.
