To simplify the priority encoder, we can:
1. Remove intermediate wires (p0-p3) since they're not needed for the final output
2. Directly compute the output bits based on input patterns
3. Use simple Boolean expressions that capture the priority encoding logic
4. Maintain the same functionality where higher bits have priority over lower bits
5. Output 0 when all inputs are 0

The simplified approach uses:
- pos[1] is high when input[3] is 1 OR (input[2] is 1 AND input[3] is 0)
- pos[0] is high when input[1] is 1 OR (input[3] is 1 AND input[1:0] are not both 1)
