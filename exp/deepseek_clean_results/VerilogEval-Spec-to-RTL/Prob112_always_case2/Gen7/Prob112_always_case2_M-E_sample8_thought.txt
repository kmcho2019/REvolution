Here's a novel approach using a binary tree structure for the priority encoder. Instead of sequential if-else or case statements, we'll implement a parallel decision tree that:
1. Divides the 4-bit input into two 2-bit groups
2. Processes each group in parallel to find the highest priority '1'
3. Combines the results using the group priority information

This approach offers potential advantages:
- More balanced logic depth (better timing)
- Parallel processing of groups (potential power savings)
- Scalable to larger encoders
- Clear hierarchical structure that may map well to FPGA or ASIC architectures
