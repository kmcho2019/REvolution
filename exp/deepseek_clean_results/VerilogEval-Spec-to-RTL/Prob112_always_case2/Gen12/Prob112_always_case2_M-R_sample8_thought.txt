I'll refactor the priority encoder to use continuous assignments (assign statements) instead of an always block. This approach:

1. Maintains the same functionality but expresses it more concisely
2. Eliminates the need for a procedural block, making it purely combinational
3. Uses ternary conditional operators to implement the priority encoding
4. Still explicitly handles the all-zero case first
5. May result in slightly different synthesis outcomes while maintaining the same logical behavior

The refactored version will be more compact and may potentially lead to better synthesis results in some tools due to its purely combinational nature.
