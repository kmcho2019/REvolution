Instead of using traditional priority encoder logic, we can:
1. First convert the input into a thermometer code where all bits to the left of the first '1' become '1's
2. Then XOR adjacent bits to identify the transition point
3. Finally, use a logarithmic encoder to convert the transition position to binary

This approach has these advantages:
- The thermometer conversion can be done in parallel for all bits
- The XOR operation efficiently detects the transition point
- The logarithmic encoding is simple for small widths
- Potentially better timing for larger encoders due to parallel operations
