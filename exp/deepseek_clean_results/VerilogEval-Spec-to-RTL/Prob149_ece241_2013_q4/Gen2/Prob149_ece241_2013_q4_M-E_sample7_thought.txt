I'll implement a novel "threshold-based" approach that doesn't use a traditional state machine. Instead, I'll:
1. Directly decode the sensor inputs to determine the current water level zone
2. Use registers to track the previous sensor readings to detect rising/falling levels
3. Generate outputs based on both current zone and transition direction
4. Implement a "history" mechanism that remembers if we came from a lower level
5. Use priority encoders for zone detection to handle invalid sensor combinations

The key innovation is eliminating explicit states in favor of continuous zone detection and transition tracking. This makes the design more direct and potentially more efficient in hardware.
