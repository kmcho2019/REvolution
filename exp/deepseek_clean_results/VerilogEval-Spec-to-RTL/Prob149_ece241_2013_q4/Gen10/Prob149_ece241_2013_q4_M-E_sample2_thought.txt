My novel approach uses a priority encoder to determine the highest active sensor, combined with edge detection for level changes. Here's the key innovation:

1. **Priority Encoding**: Convert sensor inputs into a 2-bit encoded value representing the highest active sensor (or no sensors). This simplifies level comparison.

2. **Level Tracking**: Maintain current and previous encoded levels to detect transitions.

3. **Direction Detection**: Compare current and previous encoded levels to determine if water is rising (needing supplemental flow).

4. **Output Generation**: Use the encoded level directly to generate nominal flow outputs (fr2, fr1, fr0), and the direction detection for dfr.

This architecture reduces complexity by:
- Encoding sensor patterns into discrete levels
- Simplifying transition detection through encoded value comparison
- Making output generation more systematic
