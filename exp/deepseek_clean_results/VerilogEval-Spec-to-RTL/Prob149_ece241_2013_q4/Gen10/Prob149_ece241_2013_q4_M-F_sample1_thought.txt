The key issues in the previous implementation were:

1. The supplemental flow (dfr) logic had reversed comparison - it should trigger when water level is rising (current zone > previous zone), not falling.

2. The zone encoding was numerically inverted (higher numbers for lower physical levels), which made the comparison confusing.

Here's the corrected approach:

1. Maintain the same zone encoding but clarify the comparison logic for dfr.
2. Fix the dfr condition to trigger when:
   - Current zone is numerically greater than previous zone (indicating rising water level)
   - OR when in the lowest zone (below all sensors)
3. Keep the nominal flow outputs unchanged as they were correct.
4. Handle undefined sensor patterns by defaulting to previous zone (maintaining last known state).

The reset condition remains correct - it initializes all outputs to 1 and sets the state as if water level was below all sensors.
