Here's a novel approach using a priority encoder and direct level comparison:

1. Instead of a traditional state machine, use a priority encoder to determine the highest active sensor
2. Maintain a previous level register to track water level changes
3. Generate nominal flow outputs directly from current sensor readings
4. Calculate dfr by comparing current and previous water levels
5. Use parallel comparators for immediate level detection rather than sequential states

Key advantages:
- More direct mapping from sensors to outputs
- Eliminates state encoding issues
- Clearer relationship between physical levels and logic
- Easier to extend for more sensors
- More efficient implementation with parallel comparators
