Here's a novel approach using a state machine with explicit level tracking and transition detection:

1. **State Encoding**: Use a 4-state FSM to represent the water levels explicitly (BELOW, BETWEEN_LOW, BETWEEN_MID, ABOVE) instead of just tracking previous sensors.

2. **Transition Detection**: Maintain both current and previous states to detect rising/falling water levels.

3. **Output Logic**:
   - Flow rates (fr2, fr1, fr0) are purely combinational based on current state
   - Supplemental flow (dfr) is asserted when we detect a rising transition
   - Reset initializes to BELOW state with all outputs high

4. **Advantages**:
   - Clear state representation makes the logic more readable
   - Explicit transition detection avoids complex pattern matching
   - Easier to extend for more sensors or different intervals
   - More robust handling of intermediate states

The state machine will transition based on sensor inputs while properly handling all edge cases.
