Novel Approach: Time-Based Flow Rate Adjustment

Instead of using a traditional state machine, this solution employs:
1. A priority encoder to determine the highest active sensor
2. A history register to track previous water level
3. A time-based flow adjustment mechanism where:
   - Nominal flow rates are set immediately based on current level
   - Supplemental flow (dfr) is activated for one clock cycle after detecting a rise
   - This eliminates the need for complex state transitions

Key Benefits:
- Simpler logic with direct sensor-to-output mapping
- Precise one-cycle pulse for dfr when water rises
- Reduced state complexity while maintaining all requirements
- Clear separation between level detection and flow control
