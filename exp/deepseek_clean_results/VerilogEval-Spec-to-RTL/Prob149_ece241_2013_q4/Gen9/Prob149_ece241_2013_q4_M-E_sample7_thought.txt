Here's a novel approach using a priority encoder and transition detection logic:

1. Instead of using explicit state encoding, we'll directly decode the sensor inputs to determine the current water level zone (0-3).
2. We'll maintain a history of the previous water level zone to detect rising/falling transitions.
3. The nominal flow outputs (fr2, fr1, fr0) will be generated combinatorially based on the current zone.
4. The supplemental flow (dfr) will be triggered when:
   - The current zone is numerically lower than the previous zone (water level rising)
   - Or when we're in the lowest zone (below s[0])
5. Reset will force all outputs high and set the history to the lowest zone.

This approach eliminates explicit state machines and instead uses direct sensor decoding with transition history, making the logic more straightforward and potentially more area-efficient.
