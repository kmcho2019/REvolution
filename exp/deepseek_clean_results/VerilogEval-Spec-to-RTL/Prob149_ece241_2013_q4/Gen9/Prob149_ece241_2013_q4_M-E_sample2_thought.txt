Here's a novel approach using a history-based flow control system:

1. **Level Detection with Priority Encoder**: Instead of explicit states, use a priority encoder to directly determine the current water level from sensors. This simplifies level detection logic.

2. **History Register**: Maintain a 2-bit history register to track the previous water level, enabling accurate detection of rising/falling levels.

3. **Flow Rate Calculation**: 
   - Base flow rate (fr0-fr2) is determined by current level
   - Supplemental flow (dfr) is activated when current level is higher than previous level
   - Special handling for reset condition (all outputs high)

4. **Synchronous Operation**: All logic updates on clock edges with proper synchronous reset behavior.

This approach eliminates explicit state machines in favor of direct level comparison and history tracking, making the design more straightforward and easier to verify.
