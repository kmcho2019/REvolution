My novel approach uses a hierarchical priority encoder with dynamic flow adjustment. The key innovations are:

1. **Priority-Based Level Detection**: Uses a cascaded priority encoder to determine the highest active sensor, providing clear water level boundaries.

2. **Dynamic Flow Adjustment**:
   - Base flow rates are determined by current level
   - Supplemental flow (dfr) is triggered by upward level transitions

3. **Transition History Tracking**: Maintains a 2-bit history of level changes to accurately detect upward movement patterns.

4. **Reset Integration**: Reset forces all outputs active while maintaining clean transition to normal operation.

This architecture offers:
- Precise level boundary detection
- Accurate transition history tracking
- Clear separation between steady-state and transitional behavior
- Minimal state while maintaining all requirements
