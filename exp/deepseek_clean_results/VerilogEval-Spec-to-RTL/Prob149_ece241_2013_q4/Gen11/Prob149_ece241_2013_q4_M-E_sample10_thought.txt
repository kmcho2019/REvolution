My novel approach uses a hierarchical priority encoder with dynamic flow adjustment. The key innovations are:

1. **Priority-Based Sensor Processing**: Uses a cascaded priority structure to determine the highest active sensor level, providing clear water level classification.

2. **Dynamic Flow Adjustment**:
   - Base flow rates are determined by current water level
   - Supplemental flow (dfr) is calculated by comparing current and previous levels

3. **Transition History Tracking**: Maintains a 2-bit history of previous water level to accurately detect upward movement patterns.

4. **Reset Integration**: Reset forces all outputs to maximum flow while maintaining clean transition to normal operation.

This architecture offers:
- Clear hierarchical water level classification
- Precise flow rate determination based on both current state and movement trends
- Minimal state tracking (only previous level needed)
- Efficient implementation with priority encoding
