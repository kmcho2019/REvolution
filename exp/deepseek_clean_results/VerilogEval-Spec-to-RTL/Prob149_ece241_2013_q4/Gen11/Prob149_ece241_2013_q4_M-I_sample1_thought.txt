The key issue in the original solution is the incorrect detection of rising water levels, leading to incorrect dfr outputs. Here's the improved approach:

1. Maintain a 3-bit previous sensor state to track all sensor values (not just s[2:1])
2. Implement proper state encoding for all possible water levels (4 states)
3. Detect rising water levels by comparing current and previous states
4. Generate dfr when:
   - Water level is rising (current state > previous state)
   - OR when below all sensors (all outputs should be high)
5. Reset initializes to below-sensor state with all outputs high
