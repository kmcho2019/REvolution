Here's a novel approach using a state machine with explicit state encoding for each water level zone, combined with edge detection for level changes:

1. **State Encoding**: Use 4 distinct states representing each water level zone, with binary encoding matching their natural order (00=highest, 11=lowest).

2. **Transition Detection**: Implement a dedicated edge detector that identifies when the highest asserted sensor changes, indicating a water level movement.

3. **Direction Detection**: Track the direction of level changes (rising/falling) separately to determine when to activate supplemental flow.

4. **Output Logic**: Generate outputs combinatorially based on current state and direction, with registered outputs for clean timing.

This approach provides clear separation between level detection, transition analysis, and output generation while maintaining synchronous design principles.
