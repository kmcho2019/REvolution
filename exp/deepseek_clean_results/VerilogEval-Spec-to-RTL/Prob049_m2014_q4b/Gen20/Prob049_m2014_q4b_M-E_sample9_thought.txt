Novel Architectural Idea: Dual-Edge Triggered Flip-Flop with Asynchronous Reset
1. Instead of standard positive edge triggering, implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop that captures data on both rising and falling edges
2. Maintain asynchronous reset functionality
3. Use a toggle-based approach to achieve dual-edge sensitivity without complex clock manipulation
4. Benefits:
   - Effectively doubles the data rate without increasing clock frequency
   - Reduces clock tree power by operating at half the frequency for same throughput
   - Maintains simple asynchronous reset behavior
   - Uses standard Verilog constructs for portability
5. Implementation:
   - Internal toggle flip-flop tracks clock edges
   - Main data flip-flop captures on both edges
   - Reset remains fully asynchronous
