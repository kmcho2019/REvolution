Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with two modules:
1. Create a basic latch module (LatchModule) with async reset
2. Use two instances of this latch in master-slave configuration to create the flip-flop
3. The master latch captures data on clock low, slave on clock high
4. This mimics the internal structure of a standard flip-flop but makes it explicit
5. The async reset propagates through both latches immediately
