Here's a novel approach using a master-slave latch configuration to implement the D flip-flop with asynchronous reset. Instead of using a single always block, we'll decompose the flip-flop into its fundamental components:
1. A master latch that's transparent when clock is low
2. A slave latch that's transparent when clock is high
3. Asynchronous reset that overrides both latches
This implementation provides better insight into the internal workings of a flip-flop and could potentially offer different timing characteristics in certain technologies.
