Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with two latches to implement the D flip-flop:
1. Use a master-slave latch configuration to create edge-triggered behavior
2. The master latch is transparent when clock is low
3. The slave latch is transparent when clock is high
4. Asynchronous reset overrides both latches
5. This mimics the internal structure of many physical flip-flop implementations
6. Provides better insight into the flip-flop's internal operation
7. May offer different PPA characteristics in some technologies
