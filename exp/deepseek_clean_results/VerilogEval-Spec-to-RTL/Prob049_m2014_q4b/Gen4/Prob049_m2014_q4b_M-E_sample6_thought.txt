Alternative implementation using master-slave latches:
1. Create two level-sensitive D latches (master and slave)
2. The master latch is transparent when clock is low
3. The slave latch is transparent when clock is high
4. Asynchronous reset overrides both latches when active
5. This forms a positive edge-triggered flip-flop through the latch pairing
6. Reset is implemented at both levels for proper asynchronous behavior
