Here's a novel approach using a master-slave latch configuration to implement the D flip-flop with asynchronous reset:
1. Instead of using a single always block, we'll decompose the flip-flop into its fundamental latch components
2. The master latch is transparent when clock is low, capturing the input data
3. The slave latch is transparent when clock is high, holding the output stable
4. The asynchronous reset will override both latches when active
5. This approach demonstrates the internal working of a flip-flop at the latch level
