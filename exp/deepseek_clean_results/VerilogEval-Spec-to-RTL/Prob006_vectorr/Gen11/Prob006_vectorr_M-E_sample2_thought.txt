Novel approach using a generate loop for bit reversal:
1. Instead of direct concatenation or manual bit mapping, use a generate loop to dynamically reverse the bits
2. This approach is more scalable and parameterizable
3. The loop creates a reversed index mapping without hardcoding bit positions
4. Benefits:
   - More flexible for different bit widths
   - Easier to maintain for larger vectors
   - Still results in optimal combinational logic
   - Clearer intent for bit reversal operation
5. The generate loop will unroll during synthesis into efficient hardware
