The fusion strategy is to maintain this optimal approach while potentially improving readability by using a single concatenation statement instead of individual bit assignments. This:
1. Preserves the perfect timing characteristics (tns/wns = 0.0)
2. Maintains the minimal power consumption (0.000169W)
3. Keeps the small area footprint (6.0 um^2)
4. Potentially improves readability by showing the complete bit reversal in one statement
5. May slightly reduce switching power by treating the operation atomically
