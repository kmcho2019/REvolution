Refactored approach using a more direct multiplexer implementation:
1. Instead of concatenating all inputs into a wide bus, we'll use a direct 4:1 mux structure
2. The selection will still be based on the lower 2 bits of 'c'
3. For invalid cases (c >=4), we'll force output to 'f' by making it the default case
4. This eliminates the need for masking logic and reduces the bus width
5. The implementation will be more straightforward and likely more area-efficient
6. Power consumption should improve as we're not maintaining a wide intermediate bus
