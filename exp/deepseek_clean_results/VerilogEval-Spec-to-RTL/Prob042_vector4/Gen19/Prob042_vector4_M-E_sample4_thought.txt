Here's a novel approach that avoids using the replication operator entirely. Instead, we'll:
1. Use a case statement to detect the sign bit
2. For negative numbers (sign bit = 1), fill the upper 24 bits with 1's
3. For positive numbers (sign bit = 0), fill the upper 24 bits with 0's
4. Concatenate with the original 8 bits

This approach demonstrates:
- Alternative logic implementation without replication operator
- Explicit handling of positive/negative cases
- Same functionality through different control flow
