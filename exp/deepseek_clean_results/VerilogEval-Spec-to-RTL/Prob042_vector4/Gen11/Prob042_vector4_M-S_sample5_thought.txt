The optimal solution for sign-extension is to directly replicate the sign bit (MSB) 24 times and concatenate it with the original 8-bit input. This approach is both simpler and more efficient than the two-stage extension, as it:
1. Uses a single concatenation operation
2. Eliminates intermediate signals
3. Reduces logic depth
4. Maintains identical functionality
This direct approach is the standard way to implement sign-extension in Verilog.
