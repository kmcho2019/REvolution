The current solution is already optimal for sign-extension from 8-bit to 32-bit. The implementation uses the minimal and most efficient Verilog construct for this operation - a single replication and concatenation. The synthesis metrics confirm this with perfect timing (0.0 tns/wns) and minimal power/area.

For this simple operation, there's no meaningful way to improve PPA further because:
1. The replication operator synthesizes to direct wiring in hardware
2. No arithmetic operations are involved that could be optimized
3. The design has no timing paths to optimize
4. The area is already minimal for this functionality

The only potential "optimization" would be to use the system function $signed() for clarity, but this wouldn't change the synthesized hardware or PPA metrics. Therefore, the original solution remains the best implementation.
