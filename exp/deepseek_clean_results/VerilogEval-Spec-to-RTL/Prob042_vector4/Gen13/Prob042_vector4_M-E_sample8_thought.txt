A novel approach to sign extension could leverage the observation that the sign bit replication can be viewed as a bitwise OR operation between the input and a mask of sign bits. This approach might offer better optimization opportunities for synthesis tools by expressing the operation differently. Instead of direct concatenation, we'll:
1. Create a 32-bit mask where bits [31:8] are all equal to the input's MSB
2. Combine this with the original input in the lower 8 bits
This method might reveal different optimization paths during synthesis while maintaining the same functionality.
