Here's a novel approach using a lookup table (LUT) for sign extension. Instead of direct bit replication, we'll:
1. Use the sign bit to select between two pre-defined extension patterns
2. When sign bit is 0 (positive), extend with 24 zeros
3. When sign bit is 1 (negative), extend with 24 ones
4. Concatenate with the original 8 bits
This demonstrates an alternative implementation that might be useful in FPGA designs where LUTs are abundant and can sometimes offer better routing.
