Both examples demonstrate optimal implementations for sign-extension using Verilog's replication and concatenation operators. The key strengths are:
1. Concise syntax using continuous assignment (assign)
2. Efficient bit replication operator {{24{in[7]}}}
3. Minimal PPA impact (zero timing violations, low power/area)

The fusion strategy is to maintain these optimal characteristics while potentially improving readability. Since both solutions are functionally identical and equally efficient, the superior solution would be the most readable/maintainable version - which in this case is the first example with its clear comment about the replication count (24 copies of sign bit).

The resulting solution remains identical in functionality and PPA metrics, but with slightly better documentation.
