The current solution uses a two-stage sign extension which is unnecessary for this simple operation. We can optimize both power and area by:
1. Using a single concatenation operation to directly extend from 8 to 32 bits
2. Eliminating the intermediate 16-bit wire to reduce switching activity and area
3. Maintaining the same functionality while simplifying the logic

This direct approach will:
- Reduce area by eliminating intermediate signals
- Improve power efficiency by minimizing unnecessary transitions
- Maintain or improve timing due to simpler logic structure
