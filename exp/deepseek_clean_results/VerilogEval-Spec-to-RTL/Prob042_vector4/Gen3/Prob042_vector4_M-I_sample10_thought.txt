The original solution is already optimal for this simple sign-extension operation, as evidenced by the excellent PPA metrics (0ns timing, minimal power, and small area). The replication operator approach is the most efficient way to implement sign-extension in Verilog. However, we can make one minor improvement by explicitly declaring the output as signed, which doesn't change the hardware implementation but makes the design intent clearer to other engineers reading the code. This also ensures consistent behavior if the output is used in signed operations elsewhere in the design.
