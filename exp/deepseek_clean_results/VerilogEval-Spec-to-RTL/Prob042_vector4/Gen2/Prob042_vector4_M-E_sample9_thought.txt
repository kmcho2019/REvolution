Instead of using direct replication or multiplexer-based sign extension, we can implement a shift-and-mask approach. The idea is to first left-shift the 8-bit input by 24 positions to align it with the output's least significant bits, then perform an arithmetic right shift by 24 positions. This cleverly exploits Verilog's arithmetic right shift behavior which preserves the sign bit during shifting. The result will automatically have the correct sign extension without explicit bit replication.
