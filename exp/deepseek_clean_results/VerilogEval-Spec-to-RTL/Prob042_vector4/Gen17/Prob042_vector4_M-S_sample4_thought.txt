The simplest and most efficient way to implement sign extension is to directly replicate the sign bit (MSB) and concatenate it with the original input. This approach:

1. Uses the built-in Verilog replication operator {n{bit}} to create 24 copies of the sign bit
2. Concatenates these copies with the original 8-bit input
3. Eliminates any unnecessary multiplexer logic
4. Results in minimal hardware (just wiring) with zero logic gates
5. Maintains perfect functionality while being the most area and power efficient solution
