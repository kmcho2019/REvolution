3. **Timeout Mechanism**: Use a 2-bit counter for the y-input monitoring phase, with explicit handling of both success (y=1) and timeout (2 cycles) cases.

4. **State Encoding**: Implement a minimal state machine with only necessary states to reduce complexity.

5. **Output Control**: Separate the output logic into distinct always blocks for better readability and maintainability.
