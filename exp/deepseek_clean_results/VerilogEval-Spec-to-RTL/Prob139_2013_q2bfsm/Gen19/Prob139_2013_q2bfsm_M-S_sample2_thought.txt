   - MONITOR_X: Watch for 101 pattern on x
   - MONITOR_Y: After pattern match, watch y for 1 within 2 cycles

2. **Pattern Detection**: Use a 3-bit shift register for x inputs to detect '101'

3. **Timeout Handling**: Simple counter for y monitoring with clear timeout condition

4. **Output Control**:
   - f pulses high only in PULSE_F state
   - g goes high after pattern match and stays high if y=1 within 2 cycles, else goes low permanently
