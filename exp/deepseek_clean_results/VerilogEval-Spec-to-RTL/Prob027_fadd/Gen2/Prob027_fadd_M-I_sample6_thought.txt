The given implementation is already optimal for a single full adder in terms of PPA metrics. However, we can make two minor improvements:

1. **Code Readability**: Split the combined assignment into two separate assignments for better clarity and maintainability, while maintaining identical functionality.

2. **Potential Technology Mapping**: By separating the assignments, synthesis tools might have more flexibility in gate-level optimization during technology mapping, though this likely won't change the fundamental PPA metrics for such a small circuit.

The improvements maintain:
- Same optimal XOR-based sum calculation
- Same optimal majority-function carry-out
- Identical functionality
While potentially offering:
- Better code readability
- Slightly better synthesis tool flexibility
