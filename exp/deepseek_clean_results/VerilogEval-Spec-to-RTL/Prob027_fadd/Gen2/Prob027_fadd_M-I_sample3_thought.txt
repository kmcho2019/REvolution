The given solution is correct but can be optimized for better performance (timing) by reducing the logic levels. Instead of using two half adders with intermediate signals, we can implement the full adder directly with XOR and AND gates in a single level. The sum will be a 3-input XOR of a, b, and cin, while the carry-out will be the OR of two terms: (a AND b) and ((a XOR b) AND cin). This reduces the critical path delay by eliminating intermediate steps while maintaining the same functionality.
