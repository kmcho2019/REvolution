Let's implement the full adder using a majority function approach. The carry-out (cout) can be derived from the majority of the three inputs (a, b, cin), while the sum can be computed as the XOR of all three inputs. This approach is more direct and leverages the observation that carry occurs when at least two of the three inputs are 1. The sum is simply the odd parity of the three bits. This implementation uses fewer logic levels compared to the hierarchical half-adder approach.
