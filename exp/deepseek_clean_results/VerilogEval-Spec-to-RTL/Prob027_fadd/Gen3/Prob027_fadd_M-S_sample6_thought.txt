The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic gates while maintaining full functionality:

1. Sum calculation:
   - Use XOR operations which naturally implement modulo-2 addition
   - The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)

2. Carry-out calculation:
   - The carry occurs when at least two of the three inputs are 1
   - This can be expressed with just two operations:
     - First compute if a and b both are 1 (a & b)
     - Then OR with cases where either a or b is 1 and cin is 1 ((a ^ b) & cin)

This implementation reduces the carry-out logic from three ANDs and two ORs to two ANDs and one OR while maintaining the same functionality.
