The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by directly using the standard Boolean equations for sum and carry-out. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs, while the carry-out is the OR of two conditions: when both a and b are 1, or when at least one of a or b is 1 and cin is 1. This implementation uses the fewest possible gates (2 XOR, 2 AND, 1 OR) and maintains clear, straightforward logic.
