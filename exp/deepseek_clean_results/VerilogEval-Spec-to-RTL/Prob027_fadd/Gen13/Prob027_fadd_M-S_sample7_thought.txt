The full adder can be implemented more efficiently by recognizing that:
1. The sum calculation remains optimal as a 3-input XOR (a ^ b ^ cin)
2. The carry-out can be simplified using the observation that:
   - When a and b are both 1, carry-out is 1 regardless of cin
   - Otherwise, carry-out equals cin when either a or b is 1
   - This reduces the logic to: cout = (a & b) | ((a ^ b) & cin)
3. This simplification:
   - Uses one fewer AND gate (2 ANDs instead of 3)
   - Maintains the same OR gate
   - Reuses the (a ^ b) term already needed for sum calculation
   - Results in better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics
