1. **Carry Logic Optimization**:
   - The current cout implementation uses 3 AND gates + 1 OR gate (4 gates total)
   - We can reduce this to 2 AND gates + 1 OR gate by factoring: cout = (a & b) | (cin & (a ^ b))
   - This maintains the same functionality while potentially reducing area and power

2. **Balanced Timing Paths**:
   - The XOR chain for sum and optimized cout logic now share the (a ^ b) term
   - This creates more balanced timing paths between sum and cout outputs
   - May help in certain timing-critical applications
