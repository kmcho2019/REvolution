The refactored implementation will maintain the same full adder logic but use a different structural approach. Instead of using intermediate wires, we'll:
1. Directly compute the sum using a single XOR expression (a XOR b XOR cin)
2. Compute carry-out using a single expression combining AND and OR operations
This approach eliminates the intermediate wires while keeping the logic identical. The direct computation may lead to slightly different synthesis results while maintaining the same functionality.
