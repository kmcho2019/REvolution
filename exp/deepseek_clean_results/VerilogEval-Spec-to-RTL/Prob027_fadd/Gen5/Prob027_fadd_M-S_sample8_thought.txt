The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic while maintaining full functionality:

1. Sum calculation:
   - The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
   - This requires two XOR operations chained together

2. Carry-out calculation:
   - The carry-out is 1 when at least two inputs are 1
   - This can be implemented with just three AND operations and two OR operations
   - Alternatively, we can think of it as (a AND b) OR (cin AND (a XOR b))

The implementation is already optimally simple with just two continuous assignments. No further simplification is possible without changing the fundamental logic of a full adder.
