Here's a novel approach using majority and parity functions implemented with XOR and AND gates:
1. The sum output can be derived from the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
2. The carry output can be computed using a majority function - it's 1 when at least two of the three inputs are 1
3. This implementation uses only basic gates (XOR and AND) which can be efficiently mapped to standard cells
4. The architecture is symmetric and has balanced paths for sum and carry generation
5. This approach mimics how full adders are often built at the transistor level in standard cell libraries
