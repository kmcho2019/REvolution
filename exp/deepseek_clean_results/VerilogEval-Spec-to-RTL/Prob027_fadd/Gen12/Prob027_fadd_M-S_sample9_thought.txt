The simplest implementation of a full adder can be achieved by directly expressing the sum and carry-out logic in their most fundamental forms:

1. Sum Calculation:
   - The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
   - This is the most straightforward way to compute the sum bit

2. Carry-out Calculation:
   - The carry-out occurs when at least two of the three inputs are 1
   - This can be expressed as (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin)

3. Simplification Benefits:
   - Eliminates intermediate signals
   - Uses minimal, direct logic expressions
   - Maintains clear relationship to the full adder truth table
   - Still maintains optimal gate count (3 ANDs + 1 OR for cout, 2 XORs for sum)
