The full adder can be implemented with even simpler logic by recognizing that:

1. Sum calculation remains optimal as XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
2. Carry-out can be expressed more simply as:
   - cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin)
   - This is equivalent to the majority function of the three inputs
   - It requires only three AND operations and two OR operations, but is more straightforward to understand
   - This form eliminates the need to compute (a ^ b) separately for the carry-out

The implementation maintains full functionality while using slightly more symmetric and intuitive logic for the carry-out calculation.
