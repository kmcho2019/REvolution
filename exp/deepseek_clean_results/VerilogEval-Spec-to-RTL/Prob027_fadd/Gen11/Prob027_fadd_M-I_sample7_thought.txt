The current implementation is already quite optimal for a simple full adder. However, we can make a minor optimization by leveraging the observation that the carry-out can be expressed using the majority function of the three inputs. This reduces the logic depth slightly by combining the AND and OR operations into a single expression.

Improvements:
1. Maintain the same XOR-based sum calculation (optimal)
2. Express carry-out as (a&b) | (b&cin) | (a&cin) - this is the majority function
3. Benefits:
   - Slightly reduced logic depth for carry-out (one less gate level)
   - Symmetrical expression may lead to better routing in some technologies
   - Same area and power characteristics
   - Functionally identical but with potentially better timing
