To optimize the full adder further, we'll focus on:
1. Timing Optimization:
   - Maintain the shared (a^b) signal to reduce redundant logic
   - Keep the critical path balanced (both sum and cout have 2 gate delays)
   
2. Power Optimization:
   - Use the same intermediate signals to minimize switching activity
   - No unnecessary recomputation of common terms

3. Area Optimization:
   - While keeping 4 gates (2 XOR, 2 AND, 1 OR), ensure optimal gate sharing
   - The implementation is already minimal for standard cell libraries

The key insight is that this is already the canonical optimal implementation for a full adder in standard CMOS. Any further optimizations would require:
- Technology-specific cells (like compound gates)
- Different logic styles (pass-transistor, dynamic logic)
- Architectural changes (when used in larger adders)

Since we're constrained to standard cells, this is the optimal implementation.
