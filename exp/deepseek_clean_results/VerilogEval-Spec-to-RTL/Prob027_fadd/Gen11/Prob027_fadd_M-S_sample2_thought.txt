The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by directly expressing the sum and carry-out using basic boolean operations. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin), while the carry-out is the OR of two cases: when both a and b are 1, or when the carry-in is 1 and either a or b is 1. This implementation uses the most straightforward boolean expressions for a full adder while maintaining optimal PPA characteristics.
