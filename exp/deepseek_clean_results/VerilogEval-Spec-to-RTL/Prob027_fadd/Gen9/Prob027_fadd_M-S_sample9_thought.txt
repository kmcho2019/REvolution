The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by directly expressing the sum and carry-out functions without intermediate wires. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out is when at least two of the three inputs are 1. This can be expressed most concisely by:

1. Sum: Direct XOR of all three inputs (a ^ b ^ cin)
2. Carry-out: OR of three AND cases where any two inputs are 1 (a&b | b&cin | a&cin)

This implementation:
- Uses only 4 basic gates (3 AND, 1 OR for cout, plus XOR chain for sum)
- Has no intermediate wires
- Matches the canonical textbook implementation
- Is functionally identical but more straightforward
