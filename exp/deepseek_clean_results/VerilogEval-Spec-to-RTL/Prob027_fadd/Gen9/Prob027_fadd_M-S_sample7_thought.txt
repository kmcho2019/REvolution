The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by directly expressing the sum and carry-out functions:

1. Sum calculation:
   - Simply XOR all three inputs (a, b, cin) since XOR is the parity function
   
2. Carry-out calculation:
   - Carry occurs when at least two of the three inputs are 1
   - This can be expressed as (a&b) | (a&cin) | (b&cin)
   
This implementation uses the most straightforward logic expressions while maintaining full functionality. The carry-out logic is symmetric and easy to understand.
