This implementation uses a hierarchical approach by decomposing the full adder into two half adders and an OR gate. The key idea is:
1. First half adder computes partial sum (a XOR b) and generate (a AND b)
2. Second half adder adds the partial sum with cin to get final sum
3. Carry-out is the OR of both generate terms (from first half adder and second half adder)

This approach demonstrates modular design and reuses smaller components (half adders) to build a larger function. While it uses slightly more gates than the minimal implementation, it shows a different structural organization that might be beneficial in certain design contexts (like standard cell libraries or educational purposes).
