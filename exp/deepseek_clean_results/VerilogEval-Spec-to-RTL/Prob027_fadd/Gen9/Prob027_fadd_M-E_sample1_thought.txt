This implementation uses a hierarchical approach by decomposing the full adder into two half adders and an OR gate. The key idea is:
1. First half adder computes partial sum (a XOR b) and carry (a AND b)
2. Second half adder adds the partial sum with cin to get final sum
3. The carry-out is the OR of both half adder carries (either a AND b, OR partial sum AND cin)

This approach demonstrates modular design and reusability of smaller components (half adders) to build more complex logic. While functionally equivalent, it provides better structural clarity and could be advantageous in certain hierarchical designs or when half adders are already available as library components.
