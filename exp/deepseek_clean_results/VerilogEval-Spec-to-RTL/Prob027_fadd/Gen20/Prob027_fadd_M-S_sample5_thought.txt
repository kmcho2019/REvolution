To simplify the full adder implementation while maintaining functionality:

1. Direct Implementation Approach:
   - Eliminate intermediate signals and half-adder structure since they're not reused
   - Compute sum and carry-out directly from inputs using minimal logic expressions

2. Optimized Logic:
   - Sum remains XOR of all three inputs (a XOR b XOR cin)
   - Carry-out can be expressed as majority function of inputs (when 2 or more inputs are 1)
   - This reduces the gate count and logic levels while maintaining correct functionality

3. Benefits:
   - Fewer internal signals/wires
   - Reduced gate count (3 XORs reduced to 2 for sum, 3 ANDs and 2 ORs reduced to 3 ANDs and 1 OR for cout)
   - Same propagation delay for critical paths
   - Clearer direct implementation of full adder truth table
