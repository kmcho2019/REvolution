Here's a novel approach using multiplexer-based logic for the full adder implementation:

1. For the sum output:
   - The sum can be viewed as a controlled inversion: when cin=0, sum is a^b; when cin=1, sum is ~(a^b)
   - This can be efficiently implemented using a 2:1 mux selecting between (a^b) and its complement based on cin

2. For the carry output:
   - The carry can be expressed as: when a=b, carry follows cin; otherwise carry follows a (or b)
   - This can be implemented with a 2:1 mux selecting between cin and a, controlled by (a^b)

This approach uses different logical relationships that might offer better optimization opportunities in certain technology libraries or synthesis scenarios.
