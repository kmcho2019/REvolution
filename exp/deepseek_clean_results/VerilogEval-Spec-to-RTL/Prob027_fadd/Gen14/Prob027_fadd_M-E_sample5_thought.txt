An alternative approach to implementing a full adder is to use a hierarchical structure with two half adders. The first half adder computes the sum and carry of inputs a and b. The second half adder then adds this intermediate sum with the carry-in (cin) to produce the final sum. The carry-out is the OR of the carries from both half adders. This method explicitly shows the modular construction of a full adder from simpler components while maintaining correct functionality.
