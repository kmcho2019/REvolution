Instead of directly computing the sum and carry-out with parallel logic gates, we can decompose the full adder into two half adders. The first half adder adds 'a' and 'b', producing an intermediate sum and carry. The second half adder adds this intermediate sum with 'cin', generating the final sum. The carry-out is the OR of the carries from both half adders. This approach demonstrates modular design and reusability of smaller components.
