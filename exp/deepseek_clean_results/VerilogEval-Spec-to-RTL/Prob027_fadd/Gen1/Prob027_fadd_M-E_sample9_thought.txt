Instead of directly computing the full adder outputs, we can decompose it into two half-adder stages:
1. First half-adder adds a and b, producing intermediate sum (s1) and carry (c1)
2. Second half-adder adds s1 with cin, producing final sum and intermediate carry (c2)
3. Final carry-out is OR of c1 and c2 (since either half-adder could generate a carry)
This approach demonstrates modular design and might offer better optimization opportunities in some technologies.
