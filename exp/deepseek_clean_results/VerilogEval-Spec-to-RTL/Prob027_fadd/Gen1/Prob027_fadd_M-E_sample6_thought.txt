Instead of directly computing the sum and carry-out with single-level logic, we can decompose the full adder into two half adders. The first half adder adds inputs a and b, producing an intermediate sum (s1) and carry (c1). The second half adder adds s1 with cin, producing the final sum and another carry (c2). The final carry-out is the OR of c1 and c2. This approach demonstrates modular design and reusability of smaller components.
