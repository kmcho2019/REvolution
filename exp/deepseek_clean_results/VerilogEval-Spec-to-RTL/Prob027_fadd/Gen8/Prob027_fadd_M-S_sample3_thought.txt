The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by directly expressing the sum and carry-out functions. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs, while the carry-out is the majority function of the inputs (when at least two of the three inputs are 1). This results in the most straightforward and efficient implementation possible:

1. Sum calculation: XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
2. Carry-out calculation: OR of two cases:
   - Both a and b are 1 (generates carry regardless of cin)
   - Either a or b is 1 AND cin is 1 (propagates carry)

This maintains full functionality while using the fewest possible logical operations.
