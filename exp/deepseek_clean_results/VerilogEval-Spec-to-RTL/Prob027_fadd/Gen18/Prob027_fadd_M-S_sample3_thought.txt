The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by recognizing that:
1. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
2. The carry-out can be expressed as majority function of the three inputs
This reduces the logic to just 3 XOR gates for sum and a combination of AND/OR for carry-out, but we can make it even simpler by:
- Using continuous assignments for both outputs
- Let the synthesis tool optimize the logic further
- The sum remains XOR of all inputs
- The carry-out is when at least two of the three inputs are 1
This maintains functionality while being extremely concise.
