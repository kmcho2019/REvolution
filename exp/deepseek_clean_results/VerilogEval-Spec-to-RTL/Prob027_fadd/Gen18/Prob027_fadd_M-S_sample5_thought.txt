The full adder can be implemented with minimal logic by recognizing that:
1. The sum is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin)
2. The carry-out occurs when at least two of the three inputs are 1
This leads to a very straightforward implementation with:
- A 3-input XOR for the sum
- A majority function (OR of AND pairs) for the carry-out
This implementation is both simple and efficient, with no intermediate wires needed.
