Here's a simplified approach that directly implements the full adder logic without intermediate half-adders:

1. The sum output is simply the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin) - this gives the odd parity which is exactly what we need for the sum bit
2. The carry-out can be computed as the majority function of the three inputs - if two or more inputs are 1, we get a carry-out
3. This implementation:
   - Uses only two logic operations (XOR for sum, AND-OR combination for cout)
   - Eliminates intermediate signals and wires
   - Mathematically equivalent but more direct
   - Potentially faster in some implementations as it reduces logic levels

Key advantages:
- Fewer logic operations (2 vs 4 in the original)
- No intermediate signals needed
- More straightforward to understand
- Mathematically elegant representation of the full adder
