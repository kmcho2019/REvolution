To implement a full adder, we need to compute both the sum and carry-out based on the three input bits (a, b, and cin). The sum is the XOR of all three inputs, while the carry-out is 1 if at least two of the three inputs are 1. This can be expressed as:
- sum = a ^ b ^ cin
- cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin)
