Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation:
1. Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a shift-based approach to distribute the bits
2. We'll create a 32-bit shift register initialized with the concatenated inputs and two 1's
3. Then we'll shift out the bits in 8-bit chunks to each output
4. This demonstrates an alternative sequential approach that could be useful in streaming applications
5. The implementation uses a temporary register and sequential assignments for educational purposes
