Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation:
1. Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a 32-bit shift register
2. The inputs will be loaded sequentially into the register with padding
3. The outputs will be tapped from fixed positions of the shift register
4. This approach could be beneficial if we wanted to add pipeline stages later
5. The shift operation is done in a single cycle for efficiency
6. The padding bits are explicitly handled during the shift operation
