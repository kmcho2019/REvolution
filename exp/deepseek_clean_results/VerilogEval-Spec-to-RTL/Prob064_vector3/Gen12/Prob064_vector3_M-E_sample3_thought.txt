Here's a novel approach using a shift register concept:

1. Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a pipelined approach that processes the inputs in stages
2. First stage combines inputs a-c into a 15-bit register
3. Second stage combines inputs d-f into another 15-bit register
4. Final stage merges these with the two 1's and splits into outputs
5. This approach could be beneficial in scenarios where inputs arrive at different times
6. The staged processing might help with timing closure in more complex designs
7. While not necessarily better for this simple case, it demonstrates an alternative architectural approach
8. The outputs are still combinational, but structured differently
