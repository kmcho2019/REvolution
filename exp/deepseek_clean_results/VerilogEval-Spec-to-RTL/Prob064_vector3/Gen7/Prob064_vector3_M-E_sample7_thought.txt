Here's a novel approach that uses a shift register-like operation to distribute the input bits across the outputs. Instead of simple concatenation, we'll:
1. Create a 32-bit register initialized with the padding bits (11) in LSBs
2. Shift in the input bits from MSB to LSB in a circular fashion
3. Assign the register contents to outputs in 8-bit chunks
This method demonstrates an alternative way to distribute bits while maintaining the same functionality, and could be useful in scenarios where bit ordering needs more control.
