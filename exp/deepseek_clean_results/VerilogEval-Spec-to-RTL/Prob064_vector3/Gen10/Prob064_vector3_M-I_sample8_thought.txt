The original solution is already quite optimal for PPA (Power, Performance, Area) given the simple nature of the task. However, we can make one minor optimization:

1. Remove the intermediate wire declaration and directly assign the outputs from the concatenation operation. This:
   - Saves one wire declaration (minor area improvement)
   - Reduces one level of hierarchy in the netlist (minor routing improvement)
   - Maintains identical functionality
   - Doesn't affect timing since it's still purely combinational
   - The synthesis tool can still optimize this effectively

The improvement is marginal but follows the principle of keeping designs as simple and direct as possible.
