Here's a novel approach that uses a shift register implementation:
1. Instead of direct concatenation, we'll use a 32-bit shift register
2. The inputs will be loaded in parallel into the shift register
3. The outputs will be extracted from the shift register in 8-bit chunks
4. This approach could be beneficial if we later need to add sequential processing or pipelining
5. The two LSB 1's are added during the initial load
6. This demonstrates an alternative implementation that could be extended for more complex operations
