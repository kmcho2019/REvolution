Here's an alternative approach that avoids creating a large intermediate concatenated wire:
1. Instead of concatenating all inputs first, we'll build each output byte by directly selecting the relevant bits from inputs
2. For w[7:0], we'll take bits from a, b, and c (since 5+5+5=15 bits, but we only need 8)
3. For x[7:0], we'll take remaining bits from c, plus d and e
4. For y[7:0], we'll take remaining bits from e plus all of f
5. For z[7:0], we'll use the remaining bits from f plus the two padding 1's
This approach minimizes intermediate wiring and directly routes input bits to their final destinations.
