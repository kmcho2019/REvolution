The refactored version will maintain the same functionality but implement it differently by:
1. Using a single concatenated wire to hold all input bits plus padding
2. Splitting this concatenated wire into the output vectors
3. Maintaining the same padding bits (2'b11) in the LSB positions
4. Making the bit selection more explicit by showing the exact bit ranges
5. Keeping the purely combinational nature of the design

This approach makes the bit distribution more visible and may be easier to understand and modify. The single concatenation clearly shows the complete input-to-output mapping.
