Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation for the Moore machine:
1. Instead of traditional state encoding, use a 2-bit shift register to represent states
2. The shift register will circularly shift based on input conditions
3. State transitions are implemented through shift operations
4. Output is derived from a specific bit position
5. Asynchronous reset initializes the shift register pattern

Key advantages:
- Eliminates combinational next-state logic by using shift operations
- Potential for lower power due to reduced switching activity
- Simple and regular structure that may synthesize well
- Maintains same functionality while being architecturally different
