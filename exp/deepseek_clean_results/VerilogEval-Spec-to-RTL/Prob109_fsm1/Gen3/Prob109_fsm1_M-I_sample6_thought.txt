Optimization strategy based on PPA metrics:
1. Reduce state representation from 2 bits to 1 bit (0=A, 1=B) to minimize area and power
2. Simplify next-state logic to a single XOR operation (state ^ in) which perfectly matches the state transition requirements
3. Maintain asynchronous reset to state B (1)
4. Output remains directly tied to the state bit (Moore machine)
5. This reduces the critical path, switching activity, and area while maintaining correct functionality
