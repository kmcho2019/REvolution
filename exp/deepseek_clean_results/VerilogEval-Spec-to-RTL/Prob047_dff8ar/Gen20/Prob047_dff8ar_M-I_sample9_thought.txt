   - Keep the same asynchronous reset behavior and positive-edge triggering

2. **Power Consideration:**

   - Vector operations shouldn't impact power since the switching activity remains the same

   - The simplified structure shouldn't affect critical paths

- Eliminating hierarchical boundaries that might prevent some synthesis optimizations
- Removing the generate loop overhead
