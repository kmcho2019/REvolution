To implement the TopModule differently, we'll use a hierarchical approach:
1. Create a single-bit DFF submodule with asynchronous reset
2. Instantiate 8 of these DFFs in the top module
3. Connect each bit of input 'd' to a separate DFF
4. Collect outputs from all DFFs to form the 8-bit 'q'
This approach demonstrates modular design and makes the individual flip-flop behavior more explicit.
