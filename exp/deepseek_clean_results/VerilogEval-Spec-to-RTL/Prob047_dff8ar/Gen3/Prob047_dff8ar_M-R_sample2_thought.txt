To refactor while maintaining the same functionality, we can:
1. Use individual D flip-flop instantiations instead of a single always block
2. This makes each flip-flop explicit and might be preferred in some design methodologies
3. Still maintain asynchronous reset and positive edge triggering
4. Keep the same reset behavior (active high, reset to 0)
5. This approach is more verbose but can be useful for:
   - Better visibility in synthesis reports
   - Easier ECO modifications of individual flip-flops
   - More direct mapping to physical layout
