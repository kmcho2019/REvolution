Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with individual DFF instances:
1. Instead of a single always block, we'll instantiate 8 separate DFF modules
2. Each DFF will be a basic D flip-flop with async reset
3. The TopModule will connect the individual bits of 'd' to each DFF
4. The outputs of the DFFs will be concatenated to form 'q'
5. This modular approach allows for better scalability and individual control of each flip-flop
