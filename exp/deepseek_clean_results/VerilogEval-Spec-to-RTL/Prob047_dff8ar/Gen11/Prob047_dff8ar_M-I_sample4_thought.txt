To optimize this design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Area Optimization**: Replace the generate loop with direct instantiation of 8 DFFs to potentially allow better synthesis optimization. While generate loops are more readable, direct instantiation can sometimes lead to better packing in some synthesis tools.

2. **Power Optimization**: Add clock gating control to reduce dynamic power when inputs are stable. This will be implemented with a simple stability detector that disables clock toggling when inputs don't change.

3. **Performance Maintenance**: Keep the same positive-edge triggered design with async reset, ensuring timing characteristics remain excellent.

4. **Reset Optimization**: Use a common reset signal for all flip-flops to minimize reset tree complexity.

The improved design will:
- Maintain all functional requirements
- Potentially reduce area through better packing
- Reduce power through clock gating
- Keep excellent timing characteristics
