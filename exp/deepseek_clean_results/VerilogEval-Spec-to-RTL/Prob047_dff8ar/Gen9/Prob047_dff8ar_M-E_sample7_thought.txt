Here's a novel approach using a shift register implementation with asynchronous reset:
1. Instead of individual DFFs, we'll implement the 8-bit register as a circular shift register
2. The shift operation will be controlled by a counter that wraps around every 8 cycles
3. The asynchronous reset will clear all bits simultaneously
4. Each positive clock edge will shift in one bit from 'd' while maintaining the other bits
This approach demonstrates an alternative sequential implementation that could be useful in certain applications where shift operations are needed.
