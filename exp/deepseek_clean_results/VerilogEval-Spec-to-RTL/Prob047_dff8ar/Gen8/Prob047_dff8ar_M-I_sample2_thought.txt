To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose these improvements:
1. Replace individual DFF instances with a single 8-bit register in one always block - this reduces area by eliminating redundant control logic
2. Use vector operations for the entire 8-bit bus - simplifies the design and allows better synthesis optimization
3. Keep the same asynchronous reset functionality but implement it more efficiently for the vector case
4. Maintain the same positive-edge triggered behavior
5. This approach should reduce area while maintaining or improving timing and power characteristics
