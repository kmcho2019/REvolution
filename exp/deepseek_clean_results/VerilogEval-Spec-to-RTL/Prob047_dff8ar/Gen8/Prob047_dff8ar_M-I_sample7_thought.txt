2. **Power Optimization**: Add clock gating control to reduce dynamic power when the input data is stable. This will be implemented with a simple stability detector.

3. **Performance Maintenance**: Keep the positive-edge triggered design and asynchronous reset, but ensure the clock gating doesn't introduce timing issues.

4. **Reset Optimization**: Use a single reset signal for all bits to maintain simplicity while ensuring proper reset functionality.

- Use a more compact 8-bit register implementation
- Reduce power consumption through clock gating
