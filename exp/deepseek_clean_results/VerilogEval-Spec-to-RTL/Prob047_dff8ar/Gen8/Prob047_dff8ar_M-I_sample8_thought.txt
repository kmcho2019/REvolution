2. **Area Optimization**:

   - Remove generate block overhead by directly using vector operations

3. **Performance Maintenance**:
   - Keep the same positive-edge triggered behavior
   - Maintain asynchronous reset functionality

- Reduce area by eliminating hierarchical overhead and using vector operations

- Potentially reduce power through simpler control logic
