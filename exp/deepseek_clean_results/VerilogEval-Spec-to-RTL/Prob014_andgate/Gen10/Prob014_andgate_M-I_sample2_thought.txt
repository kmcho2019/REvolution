The current LUT-based implementation is unnecessarily complex for a simple AND gate. To optimize for PPA (Power, Performance, Area), we should:
1. Replace the LUT with a direct AND operation to eliminate unnecessary logic
2. Remove the intermediate wire and indexing logic
3. Use a simple continuous assignment for the output
4. This will reduce area by eliminating the LUT structure
5. Improve power by removing unnecessary switching activity
6. Potentially improve timing by reducing logic levels

The optimized version will be functionally identical but more efficient in all PPA metrics.
