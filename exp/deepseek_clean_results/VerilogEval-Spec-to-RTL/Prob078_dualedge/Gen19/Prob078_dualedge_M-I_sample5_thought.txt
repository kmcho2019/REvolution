Optimization Strategy for Dual-Edge Flip-Flop:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Add clock gating to reduce dynamic power when input is stable
   - Use a stability detector to disable clock toggling when 'd' remains unchanged

2. Area Optimization:
   - Replace the mux with an XOR-based output selection (reduces LUT usage)
   - Combine the stability detector with existing logic

3. Performance Maintenance:
   - Keep the critical path through only one flip-flop and simple logic
   - Ensure all timing constraints are still met

Key Improvements:
- Reduced dynamic power through conditional clocking
- Smaller area footprint with XOR-based output
- Maintained glitch-free operation
- Fully synthesizable and portable
