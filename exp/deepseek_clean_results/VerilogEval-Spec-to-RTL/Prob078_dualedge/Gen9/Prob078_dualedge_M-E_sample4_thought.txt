Novel Architectural Idea: Clock Phase Sampling with XOR Detection
1. Instead of using two separate flip-flops, we'll use a single flip-flop and detect clock edges
2. Store the previous clock state to detect transitions (both rising and falling edges)
3. When a transition is detected (using XOR of current and previous clock), we'll sample the input
4. This approach reduces the number of flip-flops needed while maintaining dual-edge behavior
5. The design uses edge detection logic to trigger updates, making it more efficient in terms of area
