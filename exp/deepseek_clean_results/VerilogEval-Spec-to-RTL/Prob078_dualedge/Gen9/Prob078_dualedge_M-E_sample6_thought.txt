A novel approach to implement dual-edge triggered behavior is to use a transparent latch-based design. The key insight is that a latch transparent during high phase captures the rising edge behavior, while one transparent during low phase captures falling edge behavior. By combining these with proper synchronization, we can achieve dual-edge triggering without explicit clock edge sensitivity. This approach uses level-sensitive latches instead of edge-triggered flip-flops, which might offer better timing characteristics in some technologies.
