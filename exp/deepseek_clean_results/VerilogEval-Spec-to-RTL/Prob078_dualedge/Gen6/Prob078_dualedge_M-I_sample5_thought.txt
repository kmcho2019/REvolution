To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining correct functionality, I propose the following improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Register the final output to break the combinational path from clock to output
   - This adds one cycle of latency but improves timing closure

2. Power Optimization:
   - Use clock gating to prevent unnecessary toggles in the inactive flip-flop
   - Only update the relevant flip-flop based on current clock state

3. Area Optimization:
   - Maintain the same basic structure (2 FFs + mux) as it's already efficient
   - The slight area increase from output registration is offset by timing benefits

The improved design will:
- Have better timing due to registered output
- Reduce power by gating unnecessary flip-flop updates
- Maintain similar area while improving other metrics
