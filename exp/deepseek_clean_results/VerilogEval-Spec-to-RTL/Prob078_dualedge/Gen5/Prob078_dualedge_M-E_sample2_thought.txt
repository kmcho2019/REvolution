Here's a novel approach using a transparent latch-based design to achieve dual-edge triggering without using two flip-flops:
1. Use two transparent latches - one active high and one active low
2. The high latch captures data when clock is high, the low latch when clock is low
3. Combine their outputs with an OR gate since only one latch will be transparent at any time
4. This creates a pulse-capture behavior on both clock edges
5. Add a final flip-flop to synchronize the output and prevent glitches

This approach reduces the number of storage elements compared to the dual flip-flop solution while maintaining the dual-edge capture functionality. The latches naturally handle both clock edges without explicit edge detection.
