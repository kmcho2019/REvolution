To properly implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using illegal sensitivity lists, we'll use two separate flip-flops:
1. One triggered on the positive edge of the clock
2. One triggered on the negative edge of the clock

We'll then combine their outputs using a multiplexer that selects between them based on the current clock state. This approach:
- Uses only legal sensitivity lists (@posedge and @negedge separately)
- Avoids clock domain crossing issues
- Properly captures data on both clock edges
- Is fully synthesizable on FPGAs
The multiplexer ensures the output updates immediately when the clock changes, providing the dual-edge behavior we want.
