To refactor the dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can eliminate the explicit clock gating logic and instead use clock enables with a single always block for each flip-flop. This makes the design more straightforward and reduces unnecessary logic. The positive-edge FF will only update on rising edges, and the negative-edge FF will only update on falling edges, controlled by their respective enable signals. The output multiplexing remains the same but is now more clearly separated from the clock generation logic.
