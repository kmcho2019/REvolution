To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog without using the illegal sensitivity list, we can use two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and one on the negative edge. Then, we'll use a multiplexer to select between their outputs based on the current clock state. When the clock is high, we use the negative-edge flip-flop's output, and when it's low, we use the positive-edge flip-flop's output. This creates the illusion of dual-edge triggering.
