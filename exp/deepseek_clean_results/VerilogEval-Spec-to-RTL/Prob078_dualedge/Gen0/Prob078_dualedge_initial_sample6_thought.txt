To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and another on the negative edge of the clock. The output of the positive-edge flip-flop is sampled on rising edges, while the output of the negative-edge flip-flop is sampled on falling edges. The final output q is selected based on the current clock state - when clock is high, we use the negative-edge flip-flop's output (since it was just updated on the falling edge), and when clock is low, we use the positive-edge flip-flop's output (since it was just updated on the rising edge).
