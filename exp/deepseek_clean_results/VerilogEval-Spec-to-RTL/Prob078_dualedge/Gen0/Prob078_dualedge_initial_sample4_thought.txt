To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and another on the negative edge of the clock. The outputs of these flip-flops are then combined using a multiplexer that selects between them based on the current clock state. When the clock is high, we use the negative-edge triggered flip-flop's output, and when the clock is low, we use the positive-edge triggered flip-flop's output. This approach effectively captures data on both clock edges while using standard single-edge triggered flip-flops.
