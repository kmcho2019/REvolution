To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog without using the illegal sensitivity list, we can use two separate flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and another on the negative edge of the clock. Then, we'll use a multiplexer to select between their outputs based on the current clock state. The multiplexer will choose the positive-edge flip-flop's output when the clock is high and the negative-edge flip-flop's output when the clock is low. This effectively captures data changes on both clock edges.
