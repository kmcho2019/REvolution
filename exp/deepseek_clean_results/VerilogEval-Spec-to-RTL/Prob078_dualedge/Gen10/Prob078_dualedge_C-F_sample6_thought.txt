Fusion Strategy:
1. Maintain the core dual flip-flop approach (positive and negative edge) as it's the most reliable FPGA-compatible method.
2. Keep the simple clock-based mux for output selection to minimize latency.
3. Add output registration to improve timing characteristics by breaking the direct clock-to-output path.
4. Use non-blocking assignments consistently for all sequential elements.
5. Optimize naming for clarity (q_pos/q_neg) and add brief comments for maintainability.

The improved solution combines the reliability of the dual flip-flop approach with better timing characteristics through output registration, while maintaining clarity and simplicity. The output register eliminates potential timing issues from the clock-to-output combinational path while adding only minimal latency.
