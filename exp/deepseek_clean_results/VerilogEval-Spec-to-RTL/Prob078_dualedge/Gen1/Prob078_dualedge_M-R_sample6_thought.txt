The core issue with the original implementation is the potential for glitches during clock transitions when selecting between the positive and negative edge-triggered flip-flops. To solve this, we can:
1. Still use two separate flip-flops (positive and negative edge-triggered)
2. But instead of directly muxing their outputs based on the clock state, we'll:
   - Register the clock signal to create a delayed version
   - Use this delayed clock to safely select between the flip-flop outputs
   - This ensures the mux selection changes only after the flip-flops have stabilized
3. The delayed clock acts as a synchronization signal to prevent glitches during transitions
