To properly implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop while avoiding race conditions and glitches, we need to:
1. Keep the two single-edge triggered flip-flops (positive and negative edge)
2. Instead of directly muxing their outputs based on the clock, we'll use the clock to sample the input data at different times
3. The key insight is to use the flip-flop outputs to drive the final output through a combinational path that doesn't depend on the current clock state
4. This ensures the output changes only when the respective flip-flop has stabilized after a clock edge
5. The output becomes the OR of both flip-flop outputs, which effectively combines their behavior without clock-dependent muxing
