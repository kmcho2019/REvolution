To simplify the dual-edge triggered flip-flop implementation while maintaining functionality, we can eliminate the explicit multiplexer by directly using the clock to select between the two flip-flop outputs. The key insight is that the positive-edge flip-flop updates on rising edges and holds its value during high clock, while the negative-edge flip-flop updates on falling edges and holds its value during low clock. By using the clock as the selector, we naturally get the correct output without additional logic.
