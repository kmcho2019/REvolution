To create a reliable dual-edge triggered flip-flop that synthesizes correctly:
1. Use two standard single-edge triggered flip-flops - one for positive edge and one for negative edge
2. Avoid using the clock signal directly in combinational logic (which caused synthesis issues)
3. Instead, use the outputs of both flip-flops directly in a XOR gate to detect changes
4. The output will be the XOR of both flip-flop outputs, effectively capturing transitions on both edges
5. This approach avoids clock domain crossing issues and maintains synthesizability
