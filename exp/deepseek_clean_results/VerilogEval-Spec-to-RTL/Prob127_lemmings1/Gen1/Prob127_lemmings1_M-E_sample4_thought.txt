Here's an alternative approach using a toggle flip-flop concept instead of explicit state encoding. The key idea is:
1. Represent the walking direction as a single bit (0=left, 1=right)
2. Use XOR operations to toggle the direction when bumps occur:
   - bump_left toggles the direction to right
   - bump_right toggles the direction to left
   - Both bumps together effectively cancel out (toggle twice = no change)
3. The output signals are derived directly from the direction bit
4. Asynchronous reset initializes to walking left (direction=0)

This approach eliminates explicit state parameters and reduces the state transition logic to simple XOR operations, potentially improving PPA metrics.
