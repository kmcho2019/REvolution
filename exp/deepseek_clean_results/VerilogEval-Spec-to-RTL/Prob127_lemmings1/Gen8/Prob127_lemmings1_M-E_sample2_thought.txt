Here's a novel approach using a toggle-based state machine:
1. Instead of explicit states, we'll use a single flip-flop that toggles direction on bumps
2. The flip-flop value directly represents direction (0=left, 1=right)
3. Bump inputs act as toggle signals - any bump causes direction to flip
4. Asynchronous reset initializes to walk left (0)
This reduces the state machine to a simple toggle register with minimal logic, while maintaining the same behavior.
