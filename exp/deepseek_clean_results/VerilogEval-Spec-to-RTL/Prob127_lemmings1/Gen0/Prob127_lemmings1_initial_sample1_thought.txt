The problem requires implementing a Moore state machine with two states (LEFT and RIGHT) to model a Lemming's walking behavior. The Lemming changes direction when it bumps into obstacles. The key points are:
1. Two states: LEFT (walking left) and RIGHT (walking right)
2. State transitions:
   - From LEFT to RIGHT when bump_left is 1
   - From RIGHT to LEFT when bump_right is 1
   - If both bumps occur simultaneously, the Lemming switches direction
3. Asynchronous reset (areset) initializes the state to LEFT
4. Outputs (walk_left and walk_right) are directly derived from the current state
