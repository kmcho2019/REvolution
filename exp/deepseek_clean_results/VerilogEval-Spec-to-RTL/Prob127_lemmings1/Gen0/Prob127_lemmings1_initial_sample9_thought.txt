The problem requires implementing a Moore state machine with two states (LEFT and RIGHT) to control a Lemming's movement. The Lemming starts walking left (initial state) and changes direction when it bumps into obstacles. The state transitions are:
1. From LEFT to RIGHT when bump_left is 1
2. From RIGHT to LEFT when bump_right is 1
3. If both bumps occur simultaneously, the direction still switches
The outputs walk_left and walk_right are directly derived from the current state.
