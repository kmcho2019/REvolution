Novel Architectural Idea: "Directional Preference with History"

Instead of a simple state machine that just toggles direction on bumps, let's implement a more sophisticated behavior where the Lemming remembers its preferred direction. The key innovations are:

1. State Encoding: Use a 2-bit state register to track both current direction and preferred direction
   - Bit 0: Current direction (0=left, 1=right)
   - Bit 1: Preferred direction (0=left, 1=right)

2. Behavior Rules:
   - On reset: Prefers left, walks left
   - When bumped on current walking side: 
     * Switches direction immediately
     * Updates preferred direction to the opposite
   - When bumped on opposite side while walking:
     * Continues walking in same direction
     * Doesn't change preferred direction
   - This creates more "determined" behavior where Lemmings tend to stick to their preferred direction

3. Benefits:
   - More interesting behavior than simple toggle
   - Still maintains all required functionality
   - Only slightly more complex than original solution
   - Potential for easier extension to more complex behaviors
