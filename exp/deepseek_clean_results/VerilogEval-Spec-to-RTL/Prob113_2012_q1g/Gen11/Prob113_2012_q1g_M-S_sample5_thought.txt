To simplify the implementation while maintaining correctness, let's analyze the Karnaugh map more carefully:

1. First, observe that the output is 1 in these specific cases:
   - When x[3:2] is 00 and x[1:0] is 00 or 10 (top-left and top-right corners)
   - When x[3:2] is 11 and x[1:0] is 00, 01, or 10 (bottom-left, bottom-middle, and bottom-right)
   - When x[3:2] is 10 and x[1:0] is 00, 01, or 10 (middle-left, middle-middle, and middle-right)

2. We can express this as:
   - f = 1 when:
     (x[3:2] == 2'b00 && (x[1:0] == 2'b00 || x[1:0] == 2'b10)) OR
     (x[3:2] == 2'b11 && (x[1:0] != 2'b11)) OR
     (x[3:2] == 2'b10 && (x[1:0] != 2'b11))

3. This can be simplified to:
   f = (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & ~x[0]) |  // 0000
       (~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0]) |   // 0010
       (x[3] & x[2] & ~(x[1] & x[0])) |   // 11xx except 1111
       (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~(x[1] & x[0]))    // 10xx except 1011
