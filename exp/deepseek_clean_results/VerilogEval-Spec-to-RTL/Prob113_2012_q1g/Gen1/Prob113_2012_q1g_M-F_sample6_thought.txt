After carefully reviewing the Karnaugh map and the feedback, I realize the previous implementation had incorrect minterms. Let's re-analyze the map to identify exactly where f=1:

1. First row (x[3:2]=00):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (0000) or 10 (0010)

2. Third row (x[3:2]=11):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (1100), 01 (1101), or 11 (1111) - but according to the map, only 00 and 01 should be 1 (1111 should be 0)

3. Fourth row (x[3:2]=10):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (1000), 01 (1001), or 10 (1010)

The corrected minterms are:
- 0000 (x[3:0]=0000)
- 0010 (x[3:0]=0010)
- 1100 (x[3:0]=1100)
- 1101 (x[3:0]=1101)
- 1000 (x[3:0]=1000)
- 1001 (x[3:0]=1001)
- 1010 (x[3:0]=1010)

Note that 1111 (x[3:0]=1111) should NOT be included as the map shows f=0 for this case.
