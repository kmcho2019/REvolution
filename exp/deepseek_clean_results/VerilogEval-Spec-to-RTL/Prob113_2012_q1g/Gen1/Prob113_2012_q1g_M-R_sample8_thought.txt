After carefully re-examining the Karnaugh map, I'll implement a more accurate sum-of-products expression that correctly captures all the '1's in the map. The correct groupings are:

1. Top row (x[3:2]=00): f=1 when x[1:0]=00 or 10
2. Bottom-left quadrant (x[3:2]=11): f=1 when x[1]=0
3. Bottom-right quadrant (x[3:2]=10): f=1 when x[1:0]=00, 01, or 10

This gives us the minimal expression:
f = (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0]) | 
    (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & x[0]) | 
    (x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1]) | 
    (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1]) | 
    (x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0])
