To improve the implementation, we'll take a more systematic approach by:
1. Grouping the 1s in the Karnaugh map to find optimal product terms
2. Implementing the simplified Boolean expression using these groups
3. Using a single assign statement with ORed product terms

From the K-map, we can identify three main groups of 1s:
1. Top-left and bottom-left corners (x[3]x[2]=00 and 10 with x[1]x[0]=00)
2. Entire x[3]x[2]=11 row except x[1]x[0]=10
3. Bottom row (x[3]x[2]=10) with x[1]x[0]=01 and x[1]=0

This gives us the simplified expression:
f = (~x[2]&~x[0]) | (x[3]&x[2]) | (x[3]&~x[2]&~x[1])
