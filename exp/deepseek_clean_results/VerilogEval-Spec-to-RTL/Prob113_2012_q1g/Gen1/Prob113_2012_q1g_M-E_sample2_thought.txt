A more efficient approach would be to recognize patterns in the Karnaugh map that can be expressed using simpler Boolean expressions. Observing the map:

1. f=1 when x[3:2]=00 and x[1:0]=00 or 10 (top row)
2. f=1 when x[3:2]=11 and x[1] is 0 (middle-left 3 cells)
3. f=1 when x[3:2]=10 and x[0] is 0 (bottom row except 11)

This can be expressed as:
f = (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0]) | 
    (~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0]) |
    (x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1]) |
    (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0])

This implementation reduces the number of terms while maintaining correctness.
