To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to carefully analyze each cell and create proper groupings:

1. First, let's list all minterms where f=1 based on the K-map:
   - 0000 (x3=0,x2=0,x1=0,x0=0)
   - 0010 (x3=0,x2=0,x1=1,x0=0)
   - 1100 (x3=1,x2=1,x1=0,x0=0)
   - 1101 (x3=1,x2=1,x1=0,x0=1)
   - 1110 (x3=1,x2=1,x1=1,x0=0)
   - 1000 (x3=1,x2=0,x1=0,x0=0)
   - 1001 (x3=1,x2=0,x1=0,x0=1)
   - 1010 (x3=1,x2=0,x1=1,x0=0)
   - 1011 (x3=1,x2=0,x1=1,x0=1)

2. Now, identify optimal groupings:
   - Group A: x3x2x1x0 = 00-0 (covers 0000 and 0010)
   - Group B: x3x2x1x0 = 11-0 (covers 1100 and 1110)
   - Group C: x3x2x1x0 = 110- (covers 1100 and 1101)
   - Group D: x3x2x1x0 = 10-- (covers 1000,1001,1010,1011)

3. The minimal SOP expression is:
   f = (~x3 & ~x2 & ~x0) | (x3 & x2 & ~x0) | (x3 & x2 & ~x1) | (x3 & ~x2)

4. We can further optimize by noticing that (x3 & x2 & ~x0) | (x3 & x2 & ~x1) = x3 & x2 & (~x0 | ~x1)
   This reduces the number of gates needed.
