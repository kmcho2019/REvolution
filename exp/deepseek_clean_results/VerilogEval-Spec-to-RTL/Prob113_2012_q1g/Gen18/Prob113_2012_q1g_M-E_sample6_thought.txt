An alternative approach is to implement the Karnaugh map using hierarchical logic gates derived from the map's groupings. This method:
1. Identifies prime implicants from the K-map (three 1-cell groupings visible)
2. Creates AND-OR logic for the SOP (Sum of Products) form
3. Implements each product term separately
4. Combines them with an OR gate
5. Specifically:
   - Group 1: x[3]x[2]'x[1]' (top-left corner and bottom-left corners)
   - Group 2: x[3]x[2]x[0]'
   - Group 3: x[3]'x[2]'x[0]'
   - Group 4: x[3]x[2]x[1]
This gate-level implementation is more efficient than LUT for small functions and demonstrates understanding of K-map optimization.
