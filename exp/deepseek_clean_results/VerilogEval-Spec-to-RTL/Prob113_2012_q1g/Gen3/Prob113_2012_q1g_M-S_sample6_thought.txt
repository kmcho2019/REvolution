1. Analyze the Karnaugh map to identify prime implicants (largest groups of 1s)
2. Observe that the output is 1 in three main cases:
   - When x[3:2] is 00 and x[1:0] is 00 or 10 (top-left and top-right corners)
   - When x[3:2] is 11 and x[1] is 0 (left two columns of bottom row)
   - When x[3:2] is 10 and x[0] is 0 (left and right columns of second bottom row)
3. Express these conditions as Boolean expressions:
   - (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & ~x[0]) | // 0000
   (~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0]) |    // 0010
   (x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1]) |             // 11x0
   (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0])              // 10x0
4. Combine these terms with OR operations
