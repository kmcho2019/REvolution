Let's carefully analyze the Karnaugh map again and find the optimal grouping:

1. For x[3:2]=00 (first row):

   - This can be expressed as ~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & ~x[0] (0000)
   - OR ~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0] (0010)
   - Simplified to ~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0] (since x[1] doesn't matter)

2. For x[3:2]=11 (third row):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00, 01, or 11
   - This is x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1] (covers 1100 and 1101)
   - Plus x[3] & x[2] & x[1] & x[0] (1111)
   - But according to the map, 1111 should be 0 (conflict in original solution)
   - Actually, the map shows 1111 is 0, so we only need x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1]

3. For x[3:2]=10 (fourth row):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00, 01, or 10
   - This can be expressed as x[3] & ~x[2] & (~x[1] | ~x[0])

Final simplified expression:
f = (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[0]) |       // First row
    (x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1]) |         // Third row
    (x[3] & ~x[2] & (~x[1] | ~x[0])) // Fourth row
