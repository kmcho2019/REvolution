A different approach would be to use a hierarchical design with smaller sub-modules that implement partial functions, then combine their outputs. Here's the plan:
1. Create a module to detect when x[3:2] is 00 or 10 (left and right columns)
2. Create another module to detect when x[3:2] is 11 (middle rows)
3. Create a final module to combine these with x[1:0] conditions:
   - For x[3:2]=00 or 10: f=1 when x[1:0] is 00 or 10
   - For x[3:2]=11: f=1 when x[1:0] is not 10
   - For x[3:2]=01: always 0
This approach breaks down the problem into more manageable pieces and makes the logic clearer.
