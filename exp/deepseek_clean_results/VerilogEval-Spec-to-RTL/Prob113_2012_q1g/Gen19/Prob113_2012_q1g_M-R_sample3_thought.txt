To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to:
1. Identify all the cells where f=1 in the K-map
2. Group adjacent 1s to form product terms (implicants)
3. Implement these groups using OR operations
4. The K-map shows f=1 in:
   - Top-left and top-right corners (x3'x2' terms)
   - Entire third row (x3x2 terms)
   - Bottom-left and middle-bottom (x3x2' terms)
5. We'll implement this as:
   - f = (x3'x2') & (x0' ⊕ x1) OR
         (x3x2) OR
         (x3x2') & (x1' + x0')
