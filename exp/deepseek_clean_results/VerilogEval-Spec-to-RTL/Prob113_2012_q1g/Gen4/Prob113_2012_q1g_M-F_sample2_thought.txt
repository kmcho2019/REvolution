To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to:
1. Properly identify the row conditions (x[3:2]):
   - 00 (top row)
   - 10 (bottom row)
   - 11 (third row)
   - 01 (second row, always 0)

2. For each row, implement the exact column conditions (x[1:0]):
   - For x[3:2]=00: f=1 when x[1:0] is 00 or 10
   - For x[3:2]=10: f=1 when x[1:0] is 00, 01, or 10
   - For x[3:2]=11: f=1 when x[1:0] is 00, 01, or 11
   - For x[3:2]=01: always 0

3. Combine these conditions without simplification that would lose the specific patterns.
