To correctly implement the Karnaugh map:
1. Carefully analyze each cell of the K-map to identify all minterms where f=1
2. Identify the correct groupings (prime implicants) that cover all 1s without covering any 0s
3. The correct groupings are:
   - Top-left corner: ~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & ~x[0] (0000)
   - Top-right corner: ~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0] (0010)
   - Bottom-left 2x2 square: x[3] & ~x[1] & ~x[0] (1x00)
   - Middle-bottom pair: x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1] (110x)
   - Bottom-right corner: x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0] (1010)
4. Combine these terms with OR operations to get the complete function
5. Note that some terms can be simplified by observing don't-care conditions
