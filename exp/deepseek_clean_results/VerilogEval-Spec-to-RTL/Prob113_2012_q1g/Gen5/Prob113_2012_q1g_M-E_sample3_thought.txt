To implement the Karnaugh map more efficiently, I'll use a hierarchical approach:

1. First, decode the x[3:2] and x[1:0] pairs separately into one-hot encoded signals
2. Then create a 4x4 grid of AND gates representing each cell in the K-map
3. Only enable the AND gates corresponding to '1's in the K-map
4. OR together all the enabled AND gate outputs to get the final result

This approach visually mirrors the K-map structure and makes it easy to verify correctness by inspection. The one-hot decoding ensures we precisely match each K-map cell without any simplification errors.
