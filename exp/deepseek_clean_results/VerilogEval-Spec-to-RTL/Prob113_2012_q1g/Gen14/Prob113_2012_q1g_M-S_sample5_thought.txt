   - f=1 occurs in specific patterns when x[3:2] (rows) and x[1:0] (columns) match certain combinations
   - The ones appear in clusters that can be expressed with simple logical conditions

   - f=1 when x[3:2] is 11 and x[1:0] is 00 or 01 or 11
   - f=1 when x[3:2] is 10 and x[1:0] is 00 or 01 or 10

3. Simplified logic:
