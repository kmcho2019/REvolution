After carefully analyzing the Karnaugh map, we can implement the function with just three product terms by:
1. Grouping the four corners (x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 0000, 0010, 1000, 1010) which share the pattern ~x[1]&~x[0]
2. Adding the entire x[3]x[2]=11 row except x[1]x[0]=10 (1100,1101,1111)
3. Including the two middle cells in the bottom row (1001,1011)

This gives us the simplified expression:
f = (~x[1]&~x[0]) | (x[3]&x[2]) | (x[3]&~x[2]&x[0])
