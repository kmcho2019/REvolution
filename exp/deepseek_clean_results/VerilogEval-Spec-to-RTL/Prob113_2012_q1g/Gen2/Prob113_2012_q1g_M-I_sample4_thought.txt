To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to:
1. Carefully identify all minterms where f=1 from the Karnaugh map
2. Group adjacent 1s to find optimal logic expressions
3. Implement the minimal sum-of-products expression

From the Karnaugh map, the minterms where f=1 are:
- 0000 (0)
- 0010 (2)
- 1000 (8)
- 1001 (9)
- 1010 (10)
- 1011 (11)
- 1100 (12)
- 1101 (13)
- 1111 (15)

We can see these form several prime implicants:
1. ~x[3]&~x[2]&~x[0] (covers 0000,0010)
2. x[3]&~x[2]&~x[1] (covers 1000,1001)
3. x[3]&~x[2]&x[1] (covers 1010,1011)
4. x[3]&x[2]&~x[1] (covers 1100,1101)
5. x[3]&x[2]&x[0] (covers 1101,1111)

This gives us the minimal expression:
f = (~x[3]&~x[2]&~x[0]) | (x[3]&~x[2]&~x[1]) | (x[3]&~x[2]&x[1]) | (x[3]&x[2]&~x[1]) | (x[3]&x[2]&x[0])
