Looking at the Karnaugh map again:

1. First row (x[3:2]=00):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (0000) or 10 (0010)

2. Second row (x[3:2]=01):
   - All zeros (no minterms)

3. Third row (x[3:2]=11):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (1100), 01 (1101), and 11 (1111)
   - But according to the map, only 00 and 01 should be 1 (1111 is 0)

4. Fourth row (x[3:2]=10):
   - f=1 when x[1:0]=00 (1000), 01 (1001), and 10 (1010)

The correct minterms are:
- 0000 (x[3:0]=0000)
- 0010 (x[3:0]=0010)
- 1100 (x[3:0]=1100)
- 1101 (x[3:0]=1101)
- 1000 (x[3:0]=1000)
- 1001 (x[3:0]=1001)
- 1010 (x[3:0]=1010)

We can optimize this by grouping adjacent 1s in the K-map:
1. For x[3:2]=00: ~x[3]&~x[2]&~x[0] (covers both 0000 and 0010)
2. For x[3:2]=11: x[3]&x[2]&~x[1] (covers 1100 and 1101)
3. For x[3:2]=10: x[3]&~x[2]&(~x[1]|~x[0]) (covers 1000, 1001, 1010)
