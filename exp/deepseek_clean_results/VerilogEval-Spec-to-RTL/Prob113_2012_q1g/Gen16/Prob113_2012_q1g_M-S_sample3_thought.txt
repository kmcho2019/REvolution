To correctly implement the Karnaugh map with minimal complexity:

1. Analyze the K-map to identify essential prime implicants:
   - Top row (x[3:2]=00): f=1 when x[1:0]=00 or 10
   - Third row (x[3:2]=11): f=1 when x[1:0]=00,01,11
   - Bottom row (x[3:2]=10): f=1 when x[1:0]=00,01,10

2. Notice these patterns can be expressed as:
   - f=1 when x[3:2]=00 and x[1:0] matches 0x0 (00 or 10)
   - f=1 when x[3:2]=11
   - f=1 when x[3:2]=10 and x[1] is 0

3. This leads to the simplified expression:
   f = (~x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1] & ~x[0]) |  // 0000
       (~x[3] & ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0]) |   // 0010
       (x[3] & x[2]) |                    // Covers all 11xx cases
       (x[3] & ~x[2] & ~x[1])             // 1000,1001

4. Further simplification:
   - The first two terms can be combined as (~x[3]&~x[2]&~x[0]&(~x[1]|x[1])) = (~x[3]&~x[2]&~x[0])
   - But we must exclude 0001 case, so keep original terms
   - The x[3]&x[2] covers all cases where x[3:2]=11
   - The last term covers x[3:2]=10 with x[1]=0
