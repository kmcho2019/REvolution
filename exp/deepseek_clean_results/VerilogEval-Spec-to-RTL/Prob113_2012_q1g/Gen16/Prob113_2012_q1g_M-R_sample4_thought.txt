To refactor the solution while maintaining the same functionality, I'll switch from using a case statement in an always block to a continuous assignment with logical OR of all minterms where f=1. This approach:

1. Directly implements the sum-of-products form from the Karnaugh map
2. Uses bitwise comparisons to check for each minterm where f=1
3. Combines all conditions with OR operations
4. Is more concise and potentially more efficient than the case statement approach
