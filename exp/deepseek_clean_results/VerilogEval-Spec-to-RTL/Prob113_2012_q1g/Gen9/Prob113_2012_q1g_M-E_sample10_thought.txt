Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical AND-OR structure based on the K-map groupings:

1. First, identify all the 1's in the K-map and group them into maximal prime implicants
2. For this K-map, we can identify four distinct groups of 1's:
   - Group 1: x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 00-0 (covers 0000 and 0010)
   - Group 2: x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 11-- (covers 1100, 1101, 1110)
   - Group 3: x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 100- (covers 1000 and 1001)
   - Group 4: x[3]x[2]x[1]x[0] = 1011 (single cell)
3. Implement each group as a separate AND term
4. Combine all AND terms with an OR gate to get the final output
5. This approach is more efficient than the mux solution and directly implements the minimal SOP form
6. The hierarchical structure makes it easy to verify against the K-map
