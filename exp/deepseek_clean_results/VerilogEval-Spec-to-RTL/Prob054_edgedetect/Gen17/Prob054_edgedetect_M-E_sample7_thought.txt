Here's a novel approach using XOR-based edge detection with delayed sampling:
1. Instead of tracking two clock cycles of history, we'll use a single register and XOR operation
2. The key insight is that a positive edge creates a '1' in the XOR of current and previous values
3. We'll sample the XOR result one cycle later to ensure stable detection
4. This reduces register usage while maintaining accurate edge detection
5. The design uses bitwise operations for parallel processing of all 8 bits
