Alternative approach using shift registers and XOR logic:
1. Instead of storing just the previous value, maintain a 2-bit history for each input bit using shift registers
2. Use XOR between the two history bits to detect any change (both 0->1 and 1->0)
3. AND the change detection with the current value to isolate only positive edges
4. This method provides more flexibility for future modifications (like detecting both edges)
5. The shift register approach could be extended to detect glitches or longer patterns if needed
