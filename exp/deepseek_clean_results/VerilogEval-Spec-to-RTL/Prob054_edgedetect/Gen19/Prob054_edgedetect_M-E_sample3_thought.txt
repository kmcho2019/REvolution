Here's a novel approach using a shift register and XOR-based edge detection:
1. Instead of tracking just the previous cycle, we'll use a 2-bit shift register for each input bit
2. This gives us a 3-cycle history window (current + previous 2 cycles)
3. We'll detect edges by looking for specific patterns in this history:
   - 0 in cycle N-2
   - 0 in cycle N-1
   - 1 in cycle N (current)
4. This provides more robust edge detection by requiring two consecutive 0s before a 1
5. The XOR operations will efficiently detect the transition patterns
6. This method inherently filters out single-cycle glitches
