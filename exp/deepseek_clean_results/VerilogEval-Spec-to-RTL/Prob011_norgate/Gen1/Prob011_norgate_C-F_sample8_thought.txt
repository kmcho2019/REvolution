Both examples demonstrate optimal implementations of a NOR gate using simple combinational logic. The key insights are:
1. Direct use of the NOR operation (~|) or its equivalent (~(a | b)) is most efficient
2. The continuous assignment (assign) is the cleanest implementation method
3. For such a simple gate, the RTL implementation is already optimal

The fusion strategy is to maintain the simplicity and clarity of the implementation while ensuring it remains synthesis-friendly. Both examples are essentially identical in functionality and efficiency, so we'll adopt the most readable form while maintaining optimal PPA characteristics.
