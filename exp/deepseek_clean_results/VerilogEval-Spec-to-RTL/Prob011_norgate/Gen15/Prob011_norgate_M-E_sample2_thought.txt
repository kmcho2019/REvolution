Novel Approach: Hierarchical NOR Gate Implementation
1. Instead of direct NOR operation, decompose it into fundamental gates (OR + NOT)
2. Use separate modules for OR and NOT operations to demonstrate hierarchical design
3. Benefits:
   - Shows modular design approach
   - Easier debugging and testing of individual components
   - Potential for reuse of basic gate modules
   - Demonstrates hierarchical synthesis flow
4. Maintain same functionality while showing alternative implementation style
5. Keep interface identical to original specification
