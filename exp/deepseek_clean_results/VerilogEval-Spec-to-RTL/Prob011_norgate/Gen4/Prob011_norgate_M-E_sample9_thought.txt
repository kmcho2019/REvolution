Here's a novel approach using transmission gate logic to implement the NOR function. This implementation:
1. Uses pass transistor logic instead of standard CMOS gates
2. Employs a complementary pair of NMOS and PMOS transistors
3. Implements the NOR function through conditional signal passing
4. May offer potential power savings in certain technologies due to reduced switching activity

The design works by:
- When a=0 and b=0, the output is pulled high through the PMOS network
- When either a or b is 1, the output is pulled low through the NMOS network
- This matches the truth table of a NOR gate while using a different transistor-level implementation
