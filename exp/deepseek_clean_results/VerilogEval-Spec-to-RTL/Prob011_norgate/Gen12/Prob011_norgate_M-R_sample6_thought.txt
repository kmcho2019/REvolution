Refactoring Approach:
1. Maintain the same NOR gate functionality but express it differently
2. Instead of a direct continuous assignment, use an intermediate wire to show the OR operation explicitly
3. Keep all the documentation and parameterization benefits
4. The logic remains identical but is now broken into two explicit steps for better readability and debugging

This refactoring makes the design more explicit about each operation while maintaining identical functionality and PPA characteristics. The two-step approach (OR then NOT) might be more intuitive for some readers and makes it easier to probe intermediate signals during debugging.
