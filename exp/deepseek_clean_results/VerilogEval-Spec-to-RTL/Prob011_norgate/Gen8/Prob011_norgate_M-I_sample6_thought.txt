The feedback suggests that while the current implementation works correctly, it could be optimized for better power and area by using a continuous assignment instead of a registered output. Here's the improvement strategy:

1. Switch from behavioral style (always block) to continuous assignment style
2. Remove the output reg declaration since we don't need sequential logic
3. Use a direct assign statement for the NOR operation
4. Maintain identical functionality while reducing power and area

This change will:
- Eliminate the small overhead of a registered output
- Potentially reduce power consumption slightly
- Maintain the same excellent timing characteristics
- Use purely combinational logic which is more appropriate for a simple gate
