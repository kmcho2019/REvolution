To implement a D latch with a different approach, we can use a structural implementation with basic logic gates (NAND gates) instead of behavioral modeling. This creates a more fundamental representation of the latch's internal structure. The design uses two cross-coupled NAND gates to form the memory element, with additional NAND gates for input conditioning. When 'ena' is high, the input 'd' propagates through to 'q', and when 'ena' is low, the cross-coupled NAND gates maintain the previous state.
