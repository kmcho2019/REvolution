To optimize the D latch implementation, we'll use an always_latch block which better expresses the design intent and may lead to better synthesis results. This approach:
1. Explicitly indicates latch behavior to synthesis tools
2. Potentially reduces power by minimizing unnecessary switching
3. May improve area by allowing better technology mapping
4. Maintains the same functionality while being more readable
The always_latch construct is preferred in SystemVerilog for latch implementations as it helps tools optimize and verify the design more effectively.
