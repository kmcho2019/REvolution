The simplest possible implementation of a D latch can be achieved with a single continuous assignment using a ternary operator. This directly models the latch behavior: when 'ena' is high, 'q' follows 'd'; when 'ena' is low, 'q' retains its previous value. The feedback in the assignment (q appears on both sides) implicitly creates the latch behavior without needing any additional logic or declarations.

This is already the minimal implementation - the continuous assignment with ternary operator is the simplest way to express a D latch in Verilog. No further simplification is possible while maintaining functionality.
