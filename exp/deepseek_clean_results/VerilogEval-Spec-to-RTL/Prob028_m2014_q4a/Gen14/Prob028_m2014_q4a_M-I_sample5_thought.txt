To optimize the D latch implementation for better PPA, we should:
1. Use an always_latch block instead of continuous assignment to explicitly indicate latch behavior to the synthesis tool
2. This gives the synthesis tool more optimization opportunities for power and area
3. The functionality remains identical - transparent when ena=1, holds when ena=0
4. This approach may reduce power by preventing unnecessary switching when ena=0
5. The area might be further optimized as the synthesis tool recognizes the latch structure
