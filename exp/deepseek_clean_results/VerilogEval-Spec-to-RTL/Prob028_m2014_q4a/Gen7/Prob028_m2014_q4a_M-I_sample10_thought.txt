To optimize the D latch implementation, we'll use an 'always_latch' block instead of a continuous assignment. This approach has several advantages:
1. Makes the latch behavior more explicit to the synthesis tool, potentially enabling better optimizations
2. May reduce power consumption by more clearly indicating the latching behavior
3. Could lead to better technology mapping for area optimization
4. Maintains the same functionality while potentially improving PPA metrics

The 'always_latch' block is specifically designed for latch inference and will help the synthesis tool better understand our intent.
