The previous solution uses two registers to track state, but we can implement this with just 3 states (as required) while maintaining the Mealy machine behavior. The states will represent:
1. Initial state (waiting for first '1')
2. Got '1' (waiting for '0')
3. Got '10' (waiting for '1')

The output z is asserted immediately when we're in state '10' and see a '1' input. This approach eliminates the need for shift registers and directly implements the FSM with minimal states.
