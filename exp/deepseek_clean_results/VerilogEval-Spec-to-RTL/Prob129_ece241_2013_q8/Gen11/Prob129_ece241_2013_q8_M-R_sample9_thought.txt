The original solution uses a history register to track the last two bits. We can refactor this into an explicit 3-state Mealy FSM while maintaining the same functionality. The states will represent:
1. IDLE: No relevant history
2. GOT1: Received '1' (first bit of sequence)
3. GOT10: Received '10' (first two bits of sequence)

The output z will be high when we're in GOT10 state and receive a '1'. This makes the state transitions more explicit while keeping the same behavior and overlapping sequence detection.
