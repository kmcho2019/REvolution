My novel approach uses a compact state machine with just 3 states (IDLE, GOT1, GOT10) to track the sequence detection:
1. IDLE: Initial state, waiting for first '1'
2. GOT1: Received first '1', waiting for '0'
3. GOT10: Received '1' followed by '0', waiting for final '1'
The output z is asserted immediately when we're in GOT10 state and receive a '1', making it a Mealy machine.
This approach minimizes state transitions and leverages the Mealy property for immediate output.
The asynchronous reset initializes to IDLE state.
Overlapping sequences are handled naturally by the state transitions.
