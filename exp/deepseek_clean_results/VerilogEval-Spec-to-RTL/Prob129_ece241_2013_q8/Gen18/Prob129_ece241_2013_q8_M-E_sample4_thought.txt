Here's a novel approach using a binary counter-based state machine:
1. Instead of traditional states, use a 2-bit counter to track progress through the sequence
2. Counter increments when matching partial sequences:
   - 00: initial state
   - 01: saw '1' (first bit)
   - 10: saw '10' (first two bits)
   - 11: saw '101' (complete sequence)
3. Reset counter to 00 on mismatch
4. Mealy output is asserted when counter transitions to 11
5. Benefits:
   - Uses arithmetic progression instead of shift registers
   - Naturally handles overlapping sequences
   - Clear state progression visible in counter value
   - Minimal logic operations
