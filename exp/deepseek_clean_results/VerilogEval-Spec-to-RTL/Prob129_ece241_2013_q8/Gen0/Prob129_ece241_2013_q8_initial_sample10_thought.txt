To implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "101" with overlapping sequences, we need 3 states:
1. IDLE: Initial state, waiting for the first '1'
2. GOT1: Received '1', waiting for '0'
3. GOT10: Received '10', waiting for '1'

The output z is asserted immediately when we receive the final '1' in the sequence while in GOT10 state. The FSM transitions between these states based on the input x, and resets asynchronously when aresetn is low.
