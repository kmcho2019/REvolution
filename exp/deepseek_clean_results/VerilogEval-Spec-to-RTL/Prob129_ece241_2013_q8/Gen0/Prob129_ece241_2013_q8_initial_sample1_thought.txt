To implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "101" with overlapping sequences, we need 3 states:
1. IDLE: Initial state, waiting for first '1'
2. GOT1: Received first '1', waiting for '0'
3. GOT10: Received '10', waiting for final '1'

The output z is asserted immediately when we receive the final '1' in the sequence while in GOT10 state. The FSM transitions back to GOT1 after detecting the complete sequence to allow overlapping detection (since the last '1' could be the start of a new sequence).

The asynchronous reset (active low) will reset the FSM to IDLE state.
