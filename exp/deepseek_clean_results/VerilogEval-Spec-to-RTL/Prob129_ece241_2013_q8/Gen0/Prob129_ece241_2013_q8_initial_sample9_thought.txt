To implement a Mealy FSM that detects the sequence "101" with overlapping sequences, we need 3 states:
1. IDLE: Initial state, waiting for first '1'
2. GOT1: Received first '1', waiting for '0'
3. GOT10: Received '1' followed by '0', waiting for final '1'

The output z is asserted immediately when we receive the final '1' in GOT10 state. The FSM transitions back to GOT1 state after detecting a complete sequence to allow overlapping detection (the last '1' could be the start of a new sequence).
