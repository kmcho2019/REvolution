Instead of using a shift register approach, let's implement a proper 3-state Mealy FSM that explicitly tracks the sequence detection progress. The states will represent:
1. IDLE: No relevant bits detected
2. GOT1: First '1' detected
3. GOT10: '1' followed by '0' detected

The output z will be asserted when we're in GOT10 state and receive a '1' input. This maintains the Mealy property where output depends on both state and input. The FSM will handle overlapping sequences naturally by transitioning appropriately.
