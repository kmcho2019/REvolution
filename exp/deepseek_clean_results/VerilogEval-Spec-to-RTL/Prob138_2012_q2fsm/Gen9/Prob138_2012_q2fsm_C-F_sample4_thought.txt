Optimized State Encoding:
A: 000 (reset state)
B: 001 (1-bit change from A)
C: 010 (1-bit change from B)
D: 011 (1-bit change from C)
E: 110 (output state, MSB=1)
F: 111 (output state, MSB=1)

Benefits:
