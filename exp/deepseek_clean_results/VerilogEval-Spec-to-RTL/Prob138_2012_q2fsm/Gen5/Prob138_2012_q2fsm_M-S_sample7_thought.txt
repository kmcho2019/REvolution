Simplification Strategy:
1. State Encoding: Use simple binary encoding since the FSM is small (6 states) and the hybrid approach doesn't provide significant benefits here.
2. Output Logic: Keep the continuous assignment for z since it's already minimal.
3. Architecture: Maintain the two-always-block style for clarity.
4. Reset Handling: Keep synchronous reset as specified.

Simplified State Encoding (Binary):
A: 000
B: 001
C: 010
D: 011
E: 100
F: 101

This reduces encoding complexity while maintaining all functionality. The output z can still be derived from bit 2 of the state.
