To optimize the population count circuit for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:

1. Balanced Adder Tree Structure:
   - Replace the linear summation with a binary tree of adders to reduce critical path
   - This improves timing by reducing the maximum adder depth from 32 to log2(32)=5 levels

2. Uniform Group Handling:
   - Pad the last 7-bit group with a zero to make it 8 bits
   - This simplifies the counting logic and enables more regular adder tree implementation

3. Efficient Counting:
   - Maintain the 8-bit group counting approach but implement it more cleanly
   - Use consistent 4-bit intermediate sums to help with synthesis optimization

4. Power Optimization:
   - The balanced tree reduces switching activity on high-fanout nets
   - Smaller intermediate sums help with power reduction

The improved design will have:
- Better timing due to reduced critical path
- Lower power due to reduced switching activity
- Potentially smaller area due to more regular structure
