The new approach uses a logarithmic-depth binary tree structure to accumulate the population count, significantly reducing the critical path compared to the linear accumulation. The key ideas are:

1. First-level counting: Divide the 255-bit input into 85 groups of 3 bits each
2. Second-level counting: Combine these counts into 21 groups of 4 counts plus 1 remaining
3. Third-level counting: Further combine into 5 groups of 4 counts plus 1 remaining
4. Final accumulation: Combine the remaining counts in a balanced tree structure

This hierarchical approach provides better timing characteristics while maintaining the same functionality. The 3-bit grouping at the first level allows efficient counting with minimal logic depth before the tree accumulation begins.
