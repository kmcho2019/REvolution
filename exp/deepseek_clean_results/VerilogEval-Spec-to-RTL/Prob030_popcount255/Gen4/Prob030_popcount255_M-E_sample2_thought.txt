A novel approach to implement the population count is to use a hierarchical tree structure with intermediate registers. This method:
1. Divides the 255-bit input into 3-bit groups (85 groups) for initial counting
2. Uses 2-bit adders to sum adjacent 3-bit counts into 5-bit intermediate sums
3. Further combines these sums in stages using wider adders
4. Includes pipeline registers between stages to improve timing
5. Uses carry-save addition in early stages to reduce critical path

This approach provides better timing characteristics through pipelining while maintaining a compact area footprint. The hierarchical structure allows for natural clock domain partitioning and better power characteristics due to reduced switching activity in later stages.
