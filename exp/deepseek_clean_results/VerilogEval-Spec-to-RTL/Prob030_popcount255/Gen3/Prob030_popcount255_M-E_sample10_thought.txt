Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical counting structure with parallel processing:

1. Divide the 255-bit input into 17 groups of 15 bits each (255/15=17)
2. Each group counts its own 1s using a 4-bit counter (since 15 bits max is 15 '1's)
3. Sum all 17 group counts in a balanced binary tree structure
4. Use carry-save adders for intermediate sums to reduce critical path

Advantages:
- Parallel processing of groups reduces critical path
- Balanced tree structure improves timing
- 4-bit counters are small and efficient
- Carry-save adders help with fast accumulation
- Scales well to larger bit widths
