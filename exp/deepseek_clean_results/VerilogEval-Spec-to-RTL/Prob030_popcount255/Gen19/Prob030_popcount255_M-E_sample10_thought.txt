Here's a novel approach using a logarithmic counting tree with carry-save adders:

1. **Carry-Save Addition**:
   - Use carry-save adders (CSAs) to reduce the 255 bits to three numbers (sum, carry)
   - CSAs are efficient for population count as they reduce addition delay

2. **Hierarchical Reduction**:
   - First level: Process input in 3-bit groups (85 groups) using CSAs
   - Second level: Combine results in a logarithmic fashion
   - Final stage: Use a fast adder to combine the final sum and carry

3. **Optimizations**:
   - No need for padding - handle partial groups naturally
   - Carry-save structure minimizes critical path
   - Logarithmic depth ensures O(log n) delay
   - Final adder uses carry-lookahead for fast summation

Key advantages:
- Faster than traditional methods due to carry-save structure
- Regular structure simplifies layout
- Efficient hardware utilization
- Scales well to larger bit widths
