To optimize the population count circuit for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area):

1. Performance Optimization:
- Reduce the critical path by adding one more level of hierarchy to balance the final summation
- Use 4-bit groupings at each level for more balanced reduction (4 is a power of 2)

2. Area Optimization:
- Eliminate the 2D arrays and use flattened wires
- Simplify the edge case handling by padding with zeros instead of conditional assignments

3. Power Optimization:
- Reduce switching activity by using consistent grouping sizes
- Minimize redundant logic in edge cases

4. Implementation Strategy:
- First level: Sum bits in groups of 4 (64 groups, with last group padded)
- Second level: Sum first level results in groups of 4 (16 groups)
- Third level: Sum second level results in groups of 4 (4 groups)
- Final sum of the 4 third-level results
