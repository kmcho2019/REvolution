A novel approach to implement the priority encoder is to use a binary tree structure with OR gates to propagate the presence of '1's and multiplexers to select the correct position. This method breaks down the 8-bit input into smaller chunks and processes them in parallel, then combines the results. The key innovation is using the OR gates to quickly determine which chunk contains the first '1', and then using that information to select between pre-computed positions. This approach offers better scalability for larger encoders and potentially better timing characteristics.
