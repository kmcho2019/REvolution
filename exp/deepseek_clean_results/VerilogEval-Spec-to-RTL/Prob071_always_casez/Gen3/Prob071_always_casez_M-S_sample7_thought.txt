To simplify the priority encoder while maintaining functionality:
1. Use a casez statement which is more natural for priority encoding in Verilog
2. The casez allows us to treat 'z' and '?' as don't-cares, perfect for our priority matching
3. List cases in order of priority (LSB to MSB)
4. Include a default case for when no bits are set
5. This approach is more readable and may synthesize more efficiently
