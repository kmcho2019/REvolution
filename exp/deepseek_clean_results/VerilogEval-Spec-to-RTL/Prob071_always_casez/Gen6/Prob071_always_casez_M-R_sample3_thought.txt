To refactor the priority encoder while maintaining the same functionality, we can use a continuous assignment with nested conditional operators (?:). This approach eliminates the procedural block (always) and expresses the same priority logic more concisely. The conditional operators naturally implement the priority encoding by evaluating conditions from LSB to MSB, stopping at the first true condition. This style is more compact and may lead to slightly better synthesis results in some tools, though the underlying logic remains similar.
