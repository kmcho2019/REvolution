To optimize the priority encoder, we'll use a structural approach with basic gates and a binary decision tree. This should reduce area and power compared to the casez implementation. The idea is to:
1. Create a cascade of OR gates to detect the presence of '1's in higher bits
2. Use these detection signals to mask lower priority bits
3. Combine the masked bits to determine the final position

This approach eliminates the parallel pattern matching of casez and reduces switching activity, while also potentially using fewer gates.
