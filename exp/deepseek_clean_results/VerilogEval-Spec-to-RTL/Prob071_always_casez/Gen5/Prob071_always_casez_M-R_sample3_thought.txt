To refactor the priority encoder, we'll switch from an if-else cascade to a casez statement. This approach:
1. Uses pattern matching to identify the first '1' from LSB to MSB
2. Provides clearer intent of priority encoding through the casez syntax
3. May allow synthesis tools to optimize the logic structure differently
4. Maintains identical functionality while potentially improving readability

The casez statement with '?' wildcards naturally expresses the priority encoding behavior we need, where the first matching pattern determines the output.
