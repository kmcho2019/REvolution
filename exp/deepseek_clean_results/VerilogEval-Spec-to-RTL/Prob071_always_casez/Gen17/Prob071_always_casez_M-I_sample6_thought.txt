To optimize the priority encoder for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Performance Optimization**:
   - Replace the ternary operator chain with a `casez` statement in an `always_comb` block
   - This allows synthesis tools to better recognize and optimize the priority encoding structure
   - Eliminates the multiplexer chain from ternary operators

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - Remove the nibble hierarchy to simplify logic
   - Use a single unified priority encoder structure
   - Reduce redundant logic from separate nibble encoders

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - Maintain the early termination behavior (checking from LSB to MSB)
   - Keep the zero-check optimization to prevent unnecessary switching

The improved design will:
- Have cleaner synthesis results with `casez`
- Potentially reduce critical path delay
- Use fewer logic gates
- Maintain the same functionality
