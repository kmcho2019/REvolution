Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical enable structure with clock gating to optimize power and timing:

1. Instead of individual byte enables, implement a two-level hierarchical enable:
   - First level: Global enable (OR of both byteena bits)
   - Second level: Byte-specific enables
2. Use clock gating when neither byte is enabled to save power
3. Implement the flip-flops in two separate 8-bit groups with shared enable logic
4. Use a synchronous reset with priority over enables
5. Optimize the enable logic to reduce critical path delay

This approach reduces unnecessary clock toggling and simplifies the enable logic path.
