Novel Architecture Idea: Segmented Clock-Gated Register Bank
1. Instead of a monolithic 16-bit register, implement two independent 8-bit register banks
2. Each bank has its own clock gating controlled by byteena signals
3. Benefits:
   - Power savings by gating clocks to inactive byte segments
   - Clear physical separation between byte groups
   - Potential for better placement/routing in physical design
4. Implementation:
   - Create two 8-bit registers (upper and lower)
   - Generate gated clocks for each bank
   - Use synchronous reset for both banks
   - Combine outputs for final q
5. Advantages over original:
   - Explicit clock gating reduces dynamic power
   - Better modularity for hierarchical designs
   - More physically aware implementation
