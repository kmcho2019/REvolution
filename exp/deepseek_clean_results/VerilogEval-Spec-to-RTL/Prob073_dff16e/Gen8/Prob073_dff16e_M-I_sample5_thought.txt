Improvement Strategy:
1. Maintain functionality while optimizing for area and power
2. Replace individual DFFs with a single 16-bit register with byte-wise enable
3. Use a more efficient enable structure that:
   - Reduces redundant logic by grouping bits by byte
   - Minimizes fanout of enable signals
4. Keep the synchronous reset behavior
5. Benefits:
   - Reduced area by eliminating individual DFF instantiations
   - Lower power consumption through shared enable logic
   - Maintains same timing characteristics
6. Key changes:
   - Single always block for all 16 bits
   - Byte enable logic applied to respective byte groups
   - Clear separation of upper and lower byte control
