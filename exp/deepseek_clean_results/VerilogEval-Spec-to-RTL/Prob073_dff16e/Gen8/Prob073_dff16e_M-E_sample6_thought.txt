This new architecture implements each flip-flop independently with its own enable logic, providing:
1. Finer-grained control over individual bits while maintaining byte-level enables
2. Potential for better power gating at bit-level precision
3. More flexible modification options for future enhancements
4. Clear visibility into each bit's behavior during simulation/debug
5. Better utilization of modern FPGA/ASIC architectures that favor distributed registers

The design uses generate blocks to instantiate 16 individual DFFs, with enable logic derived from the byteena signals combined with their position in the byte. This maintains the byte-level control while allowing for future bit-level modifications if needed.
