Novel Bit-Sliced Masked Enable Architecture:
1. Instead of byte-level granularity, implement individual flip-flop enables for maximum flexibility
2. Use a mask generation circuit that expands the 2-bit byteena into 16 individual enable signals
3. Each flip-flop has its own enable signal derived from byteena and its position
4. Implement synchronous reset with priority over enables
5. Benefits:
   - Enables future expansion to individual bit control without structural changes
   - More transparent implementation showing exactly how each bit is controlled
   - Potential power savings by gating only needed flip-flops
   - Clear separation of control logic from storage elements
