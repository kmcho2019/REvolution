Improvement Strategy:
1. Clock Gating: Add clock gating to reduce dynamic power when byte enables are inactive. This will significantly cut power consumption during idle periods.
2. Shared Reset Logic: Combine the reset logic for both byte registers to reduce area overhead.
3. Parameter Optimization: Remove the parameter since we only need 8-bit registers, simplifying the implementation.
4. Direct Instantiation: Use direct DFF instantiation for better synthesis control and potential area optimization.
5. Power-Aware Structure: Structure the design to enable power gating at synthesis level.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced power consumption through clock gating
- Slight area reduction by removing parameter and sharing reset
- Maintains same timing characteristics
- Better synthesis optimization potential
