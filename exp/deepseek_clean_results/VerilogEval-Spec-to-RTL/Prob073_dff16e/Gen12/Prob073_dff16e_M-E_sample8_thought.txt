Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Register Banking: Split the 16-bit register into two 8-bit banks (upper and lower bytes)
2. Clock Gating: Use hierarchical clock gating to disable clock to inactive banks
3. Benefits:
   - Power savings by gating clock to inactive byte banks
   - Simplified enable logic by banking
   - Maintains same functionality with potentially better PPA
4. Implementation:
   - Two 8-bit registers instead of 16 individual DFFs
   - Clock gating cells for each byte bank
   - Synchronous reset propagated to all banks
5. Key Advantages:
   - Reduced clock switching power
   - Cleaner hierarchical structure
   - Easier to scale to wider buses
