The original implementation uses a chain of ternary operators for the combinational logic. While functional, we can improve readability and potentially synthesis results by using a case statement inside an always block. This approach:
1. Makes the mapping more explicit and easier to read
2. Groups all conditions together in a structured way
3. May lead to better synthesis results as case statements are well-optimized in most tools
4. Still maintains pure combinational behavior
5. Explicitly lists all possible cases (including default) for completeness
