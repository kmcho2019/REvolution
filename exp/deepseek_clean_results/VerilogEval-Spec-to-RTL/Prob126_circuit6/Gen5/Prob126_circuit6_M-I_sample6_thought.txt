The current solution is already quite efficient, but we can make some minor improvements:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Replace the ternary operator chain with a case statement, as some synthesis tools optimize case statements better for power
   - This also improves readability while maintaining the same functionality

2. Area Optimization:
   - The case statement approach might result in slightly better area utilization as it's a more direct mapping
   - The synthesis tool can better optimize the multiplexer structure

3. Maintainability:
   - Case statements are generally more readable and easier to modify
   - Vertical formatting makes the mapping clear and easy to verify

The improved version should maintain the same timing characteristics while potentially reducing power and area slightly.
