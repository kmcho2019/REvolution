Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove the always block and case statement since this is purely combinational
2. Use a continuous assignment with a ternary operator chain for compact implementation
3. Maintain the same lookup table functionality
4. Keep hexadecimal constants for clarity
5. Make default case explicit at the end of the chain

Key Benefits:
- More concise implementation (7 lines vs 15)
- Same functionality with identical PPA characteristics
- Easier to read at a glance
- Still fully combinational
- No need for reg declaration

Trade-offs:
- Slightly less conventional than case statement
- Ternary chain may be less readable for complex conditions (but acceptable here)
