2. **Area Optimization**:
   - The case statement approach might also lead to a more compact implementation as it clearly expresses the lookup table nature of the circuit.
   - Synthesis tools can better recognize this as a simple ROM pattern.

3. **Performance**:
   - Maintain the combinational nature with zero clock period requirement.
   - The case statement should not introduce any additional delay compared to the ternary operator chain.
