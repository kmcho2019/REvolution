The simplest implementation would be to use a direct lookup table with a case statement inside an always block. This approach:
1. Is straightforward and easy to understand
2. Clearly shows the input-output mapping
3. Uses minimal syntax while maintaining functionality
4. Will synthesize efficiently to a simple multiplexer

The mapping remains identical to the original:

1 -> aee0 (hex)
2 -> 27d4 (hex)
