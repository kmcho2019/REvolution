To optimize the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics of the minimum-finding module, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. Performance Optimization:
   - Use a balanced comparison tree structure to reduce critical path
   - Break the operation into two stages with intermediate registers (pipelining)
   - This allows higher clock frequencies by reducing combinational delay

2. Power Optimization:
   - Only perform comparisons when inputs change (using clock gating)
   - Eliminate redundant comparisons by storing intermediate results

3. Area Optimization:
   - Share comparison resources between stages
   - Use registered outputs to reduce glitching power

The new design will:
- First find min(a,b) and min(c,d) in parallel
- Register these intermediate results
- Then find the final min between the two intermediate results
- Use clock gating to only update when inputs change
