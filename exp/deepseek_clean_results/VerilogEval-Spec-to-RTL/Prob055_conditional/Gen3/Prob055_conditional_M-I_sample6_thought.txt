To optimize the minimum-finding circuit, we'll implement a balanced tree comparison structure. This approach:
1. Compares a vs b and c vs d in parallel (first level)
2. Compares the winners from the first level (second level)
This reduces the critical path from 3 comparisons to 2 comparisons, improving timing.
The parallel comparisons also reduce switching activity since all comparisons happen simultaneously rather than cascaded.
We'll maintain the same functionality while improving both performance and power characteristics.
