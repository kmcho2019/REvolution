To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, we can:
1. Eliminate redundant comparisons by first finding the minimum of a and b, and separately finding the minimum of c and d
2. Store these intermediate minimums in wires to avoid recomputation
3. Then compare these two intermediate minimums to get the final result
4. This approach reduces the number of comparison operations from 3 to 2 (since a<b and c<d are each only computed once)
5. The logic remains equally clear while being more efficient
