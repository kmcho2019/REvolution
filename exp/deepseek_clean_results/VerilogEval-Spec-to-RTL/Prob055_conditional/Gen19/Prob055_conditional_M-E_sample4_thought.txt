Here's a novel approach using a bit-serial comparator that finds the minimum value by examining bits from MSB to LSB. This method:
1. Processes one bit at a time starting from the most significant bit
2. Eliminates candidates that can't be the minimum at each bit position
3. Uses a priority encoder to track remaining candidates
4. Only requires one full comparison at the end for the final selection
5. Potentially reduces power consumption by early elimination of candidates
6. Uses fewer comparison operations than the hierarchical approach
