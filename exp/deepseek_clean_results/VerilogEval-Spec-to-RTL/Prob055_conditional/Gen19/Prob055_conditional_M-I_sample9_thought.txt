To optimize the solution further for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I propose the following improvements:

1. Performance:
   - Maintain the two-level comparison structure for optimal timing
   - Keep parallel comparisons of input pairs (a vs b, c vs d) in the first level
   - Final comparison of intermediate results in the second level

2. Power:
   - Use continuous assignments instead of module instantiations to reduce hierarchy overhead
   - Eliminate intermediate wires to minimize switching activity
   - Directly compute the final result in a single expression

3. Area:
   - Remove the Min2 module to reduce hierarchy and interface overhead
   - Implement the comparisons directly in the TopModule
   - Use nested ternary operators for compact implementation

The improved design will:
- Reduce power by eliminating unnecessary hierarchy
- Maintain the same performance with parallel comparisons
- Decrease area by removing module instantiations
- Keep the same functionality while being more efficient
