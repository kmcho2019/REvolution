A novel approach to find the minimum of four 8-bit values is to use a parallel comparison tree structure. Instead of cascading comparisons sequentially, we can first compare a vs b and c vs d in parallel (level 1), then compare the results of these two comparisons (level 2). This reduces the critical path from 3 comparisons to just 2 comparisons, potentially improving timing performance. The parallel structure also allows for better power characteristics as the comparisons happen simultaneously rather than sequentially.
