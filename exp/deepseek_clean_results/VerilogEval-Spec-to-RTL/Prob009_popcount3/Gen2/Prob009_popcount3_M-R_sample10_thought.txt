The original implementation uses a simple continuous assignment with a direct sum of bits. We can refactor this to use a more structured approach by:
1. Breaking down the addition into two steps (first add two bits, then add the third)
2. Using intermediate wires to make the computation more explicit
3. Maintaining the same functionality while potentially improving readability and synthesis optimization opportunities

This approach might help with power optimization by reducing simultaneous switching and could give the synthesizer more flexibility in implementing the adder structure.
