The current solution is already quite optimized, but we can make it slightly simpler by recognizing that:
1. The LSB (out[0]) can be computed as the XOR of all input bits, which remains optimal
2. The MSB (out[1]) can be computed using just two AND gates and one OR gate by observing that we don't need all three pairwise AND terms - any two will suffice because if any two bits are 1, the count is at least 2 regardless of the third bit

This reduces the number of AND operations from 3 to 2 while maintaining correct functionality.
