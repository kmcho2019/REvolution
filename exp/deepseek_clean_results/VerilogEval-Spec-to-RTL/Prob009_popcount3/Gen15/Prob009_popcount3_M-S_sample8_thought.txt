1. The population count of a 3-bit input can be directly calculated by summing the bits
2. The least significant bit (out[0]) is the XOR of all input bits (odd parity)
3. The most significant bit (out[1]) is 1 when two or more bits are 1 (majority function)
4. We can implement this more directly by:
   - Calculating out[0] as the XOR of all bits
   - Calculating out[1] as the OR of all pairwise ANDs (majority detection)
