The population count for a 3-bit input can be simplified by recognizing that:
1. The LSB (out[0]) is simply the XOR of all bits, which detects odd counts
2. The MSB (out[1]) is true when at least two bits are set, which can be expressed as the OR of all pairwise ANDs

This leads to a very straightforward implementation with minimal logic operations:
- out[0] = in[0] ^ in[1] ^ in[2]
- out[1] = (in[0] & in[1]) | (in[1] & in[2]) | (in[0] & in[2])
