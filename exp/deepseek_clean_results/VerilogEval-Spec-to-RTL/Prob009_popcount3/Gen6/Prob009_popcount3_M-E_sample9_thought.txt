Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical adder structure:
1. First, count the number of '1's in the first two bits using a half-adder (sum and carry)
2. Then add this partial count to the third bit using another half-adder
3. The final count is the sum of these operations

This approach mimics how a full adder works but optimized for our specific case of counting '1's. The advantage is that it uses a standard digital arithmetic structure that's well-optimized in most synthesis tools.
