Here's a novel approach using a lookup table (LUT) implemented as a case statement. While less efficient for 3-bit inputs, this approach demonstrates how population count could be scaled to wider inputs where manual bit summation becomes impractical. The case statement acts as a 3-bit to 2-bit decoder that directly maps each input pattern to its population count.

Key advantages:
1. Conceptually simple and easy to understand
2. Scales better to wider inputs (though not optimal for 3-bit)
3. No arithmetic operations needed
4. Demonstrates an alternative architectural approach
