Novel LUT-based Approach:
1. Pre-compute all possible 3-bit input combinations (8 cases) and their corresponding population counts
2. Implement this as a direct lookup using a case statement
3. Benefits:
   - Extremely straightforward implementation
   - No complex logic gates required
   - Easy to understand and verify
   - Naturally extends to wider inputs if needed
   - All outputs computed in parallel with equal delay
4. Tradeoffs:
   - Uses more area than minimal gate implementations
   - But provides excellent timing characteristics
   - Very regular structure that synthesizes well
