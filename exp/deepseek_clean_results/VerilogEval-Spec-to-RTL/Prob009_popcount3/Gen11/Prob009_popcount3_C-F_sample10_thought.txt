The fusion strategy combines the strengths of both examples while addressing their minor limitations:

1. From Example 1, we keep the explicit bit-width declaration for robustness
2. From Example 2, we adopt the efficient bitwise operations for area optimization
3. We combine both approaches by:
   - Using the XOR-based LSB calculation (more efficient than addition for single bit)
   - Using the OR-of-ANDs for MSB (more direct than addition)
   - Maintaining explicit bit-widths for clarity
4. This hybrid approach should:
   - Maintain excellent timing (single-level logic)
   - Potentially reduce area (fewer gates than adder implementation)
   - Keep power consumption low
   - Remain highly readable and maintainable

The solution is optimal for both ASIC and FPGA implementations.
