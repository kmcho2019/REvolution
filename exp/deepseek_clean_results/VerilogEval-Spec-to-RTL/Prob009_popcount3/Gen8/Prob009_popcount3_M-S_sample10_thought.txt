Simplification Approach:
1. Recognize that for a 3-bit input, the population count can be directly computed by summing the bits
2. The output is simply the binary representation of this sum (0-3)
3. The LSB (out[0]) is the XOR of all bits (odd/even detection)
4. The MSB (out[1]) is true when two or more bits are set
5. Can compute MSB more directly by checking if the sum of any two bits is 2 (using OR of pairwise ANDs)
