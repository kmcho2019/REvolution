The current implementation is already quite optimal for a 3-bit population count. However, we can make a slight optimization in the majority function calculation for the MSB (out[1]):

1. The LSB (out[0]) calculation using XOR is optimal and can't be improved further.
2. For the MSB (out[1]), we can observe that we only need to check any two pairs of bits being '1' since if any two are '1', the count will be at least 2 (which sets the MSB).
3. We can reduce the OR operation from three terms to two terms by strategically choosing which pairs to check, while still covering all cases where two or more bits are '1'.

This reduces the number of OR inputs from 3 to 2 while maintaining correct functionality, potentially saving a small amount of power and area.
