The current implementation is already optimal for a 3-bit population count in terms of functionality and PPA metrics. However, we can make one minor improvement by:
1. Using a continuous assignment with explicit bit-width casting to ensure clean synthesis
2. Maintaining the same simple adder structure but making it more explicit
3. This makes the intent clearer to synthesis tools while maintaining identical PPA characteristics

The improvement is purely stylistic/readability - the actual implementation remains just as efficient in terms of gates and timing.
