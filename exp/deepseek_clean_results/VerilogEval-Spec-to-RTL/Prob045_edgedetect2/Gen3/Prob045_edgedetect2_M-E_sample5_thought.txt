Instead of using a simple XOR-based edge detection, this solution implements a parallel history buffer approach:
1. For each bit, maintain a 2-bit shift register to track the last two states
2. Use combinational logic to detect when the two most recent states differ
3. This provides better isolation between bits and allows for potential future expansion to detect specific edge types (rising/falling)
4. The parallel structure may offer better timing characteristics for high-frequency designs since each bit's edge detection is independent
5. The 2-bit history also provides robustness against potential glitches
