Novel approach using shift registers:
1. Instead of storing just the previous value, maintain a 2-bit history for each input bit using shift registers
2. Detect edges by comparing the two most recent values in the history
3. This approach provides more flexibility for future extensions (like detecting specific edge patterns)
4. The shift register implementation naturally pipelines the edge detection logic
5. Additional benefit: can be easily extended to detect glitches or specific edge sequences if needed

Key advantages:
- More extensible architecture
- Clear separation of history tracking and edge detection
- Potential for higher clock speeds due to pipelining nature
- Can be parameterized for different history depths
