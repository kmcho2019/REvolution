Improvement Strategy:
1. Maintain the core XOR-based edge detection logic as it's already optimal for this functionality
2. Focus on power optimization by adding clock gating for bits that remain stable
3. Implement a stability detector to identify static bits and gate their clock signals
4. Use hierarchical clock gating to minimize overhead
5. Keep the same minimal flip-flop count (8 for prev_in) and registered output
6. Maintain the clean synchronous design with non-blocking assignments

Expected Benefits:
- Reduced dynamic power consumption by gating clock to stable bits
- Maintain excellent timing characteristics (wns -0.16ns)
- Slight area increase for gating logic (~5-10 um²) but significant power savings
- Preserve the single-cycle latency and parallel processing
