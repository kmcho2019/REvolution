   - No need for pipelining as the timing is already good

2. Power Optimization:

   - Only update the prev_in register when inputs actually change
   - This reduces clock tree power and register switching power

3. Area Optimization:
   - The design is already minimal (85.0 um²)
   - We'll keep the same structure but add the power optimization logic
   - The additional comparator adds minimal area overhead
