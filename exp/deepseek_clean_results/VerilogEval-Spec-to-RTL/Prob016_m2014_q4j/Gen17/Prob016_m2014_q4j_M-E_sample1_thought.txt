Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Precomputation
1. **Parallel Computation**: Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks that compute results for both possible carry-in scenarios (0 and 1) in parallel.
2. **Carry Propagation**: Use the actual carry-out from the lower 2-bit block to select the correct result from the upper 2-bit block.
3. **Timing Advantage**: This approach reduces the critical path compared to ripple-carry, as the upper bits don't wait for the full carry propagation.
4. **Resource Tradeoff**: Uses slightly more area (extra adders) but provides better timing performance.
5. **Final Overflow**: The carry-out from the upper block becomes the overflow bit (sum[4]).
