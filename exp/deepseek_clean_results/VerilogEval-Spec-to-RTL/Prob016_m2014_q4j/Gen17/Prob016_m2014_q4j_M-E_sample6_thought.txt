Here's a novel approach using a carry-skip adder architecture, which offers a good balance between performance and area for 4-bit addition:

1. **Carry-Skip Concept**: 
   - Divide the 4-bit adder into two 2-bit blocks
   - Each block computes its own carry-out and group propagate signal
   - Skip carry propagation when all bits in a block are propagators (P = x ^ y)

2. **Advantages**:
   - Reduces worst-case carry propagation delay compared to ripple-carry
   - More efficient than carry-lookahead for small widths (4-bit)
   - Maintains reasonable area overhead

3. **Implementation**:
   - First block (bits 0-1) computes sum and generates carry1
   - Second block (bits 2-3) computes sum and generates carry2
   - Skip logic determines final carry based on block propagate signals
   - Final sum combines all bits and overflow

This provides better performance than ripple-carry while avoiding the complexity of full carry-lookahead for this width.
