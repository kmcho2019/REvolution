A novel approach using carry-select adder architecture:
1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks (bits 1:0 and 3:2)
2. Compute both possible sums for the upper bits (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use the actual carry-out from the lower bits to select the correct upper sum
4. Combine results and maintain the overflow bit
This approach reduces critical path delay compared to ripple-carry while using slightly more area. The carry-select structure provides better timing for larger adders.
