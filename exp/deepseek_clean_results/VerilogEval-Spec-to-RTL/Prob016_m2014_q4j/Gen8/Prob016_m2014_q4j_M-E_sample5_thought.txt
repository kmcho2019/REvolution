Instead of using a traditional ripple-carry adder, we can implement a carry-select adder structure. This approach divides the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks that compute their sums in parallel with both possible carry-in scenarios (0 and 1). A multiplexer then selects the correct result based on the actual carry-out from the previous stage. This provides better performance than ripple-carry while still being relatively simple to implement for 4 bits.
