Instead of using a traditional ripple-carry adder, I'll implement a carry-skip adder which offers better performance for moderate bit widths like 4 bits. The key idea is to detect when the carry can skip over a group of bits when all bits in that group would propagate the carry. For 4 bits, we'll use one skip group covering all bits. The architecture will:
1. Compute generate/propagate signals for each bit
2. Calculate group propagate (AND of all individual propagates)
3. Use the group propagate to potentially skip the carry chain
4. Combine results to get the final sum and carry-out
This approach reduces the worst-case carry propagation delay.
