To simplify the 4-bit adder while maintaining functionality, I'll make these optimizations:

1. **Direct Implementation**: Instead of instantiating separate FullAdder modules, implement the logic directly in the TopModule to reduce hierarchy overhead.

2. **Carry Chain**: Maintain the ripple-carry approach but express it as continuous assignments rather than module instantiations.

3. **Compact Logic**: Combine the sum and carry calculations into single expressions for each bit position.

4. **Overflow Handling**: Keep the final carry as the overflow bit in sum[4].

5. **Benefits**:
   - Eliminates module instantiation overhead
   - Reduces interconnect complexity
   - Maintains same functionality with cleaner code
   - Still meets timing for 4-bit operation
   - Easier to read and maintain
