Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Skip Adder with Early Termination

Instead of a standard ripple-carry or carry-lookahead approach, I'll implement a carry-skip adder that:
1. Divides the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit blocks
2. Uses ripple-carry within each block
3. Implements skip logic between blocks to bypass ripple when possible
4. Includes early termination detection when no carry propagation occurs

Key Benefits:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (O(√n) average case)
- Simpler than full carry-lookahead
- Power-efficient due to early termination
- Maintains clean hierarchy with FullAdder modules
- Naturally handles the overflow bit as the final carry-out
