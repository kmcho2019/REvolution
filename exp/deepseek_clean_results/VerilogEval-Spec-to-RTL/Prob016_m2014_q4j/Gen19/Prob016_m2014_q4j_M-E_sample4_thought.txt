A novel approach using carry-select adder architecture:
1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks (bits 0-1 and bits 2-3)
2. Compute both possible sums for the upper bits (with carry=0 and carry=1)
3. Use the carry-out from the lower bits to select the correct upper sum
4. Combine results and maintain the overflow bit as the final carry
This approach provides better timing characteristics than ripple-carry while still being straightforward to implement.
