Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Skip Adder with Optimized Grouping

Instead of the conventional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead approaches, I propose a 4-bit carry-skip adder with optimized grouping. This design:
1. Divides the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit groups
2. Uses ripple-carry within each group
3. Implements skip logic between groups when all bits in a group would propagate carry
4. Provides better average-case performance than ripple-carry while being simpler than full carry-lookahead
5. Naturally extends the carry-out to become the overflow bit
6. Optimizes power by reducing unnecessary carry propagation
7. Maintains good area efficiency with only slight overhead over ripple-carry

The skip logic detects when P0&P1=1 (both bits in first group would propagate carry) and allows the carry to skip the first group's ripple delay when possible.
