Simplification Strategy:
1. **Uniform Ripple-Carry**: Use simple ripple-carry structure for all bits (including MSB) since the performance difference is negligible for 4-bit adders
2. **Direct Carry Chain**: Eliminate separate carry-lookahead logic for MSB to reduce complexity
3. **Minimal Wiring**: Use a single carry chain array and connect directly between full adders
4. **Overflow Handling**: Maintain direct carry-out as sum[4] for overflow detection

Key Advantages:
- Simpler and more uniform implementation
- Easier to understand and maintain
- Still efficient for 4-bit operations
- Maintains correct overflow handling
