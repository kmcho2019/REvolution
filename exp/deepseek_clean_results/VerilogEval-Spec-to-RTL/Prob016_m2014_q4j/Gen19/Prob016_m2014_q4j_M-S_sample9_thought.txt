Simplification Strategy:
1. Pure ripple-carry implementation: While slightly slower, this is simpler and meets the basic requirements.
2. Remove carry-lookahead logic: Eliminate the separate g/p signals for MSB to reduce complexity.
3. Consistent full adder usage: Use the same FullAdder module for all bits including the MSB.
4. Direct carry chain: Let the final carry-out naturally become the sum[4] bit.

Key Advantages:
- Simpler implementation with fewer components
- Maintains correct functionality
- Easier to understand and verify
- Still reasonably efficient for 4-bit addition
