Refactoring Strategy:
1. **Structural Change**: Replace hierarchical FullAdder instances with continuous assignments to flatten the design
2. **Wire Reduction**: Eliminate intermediate carry wires by directly computing each stage's sum and carry
3. **Direct Computation**: Calculate each bit's sum and carry using Boolean expressions in a single level
4. **Maintain Functionality**: Preserve the ripple-carry behavior while making the implementation more compact

Benefits:
- Reduced hierarchy improves synthesis optimization potential
- Direct assignments may lead to better area utilization
- Cleaner code with all logic visible at the top level
- Maintains same timing characteristics (still ripple-carry)
