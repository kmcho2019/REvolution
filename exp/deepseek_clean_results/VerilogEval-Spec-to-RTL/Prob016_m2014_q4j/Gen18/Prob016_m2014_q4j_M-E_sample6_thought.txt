Here's a novel approach using a carry-select adder architecture with a twist - we'll implement it using conditional carry propagation with multiplexers rather than traditional duplicate adders. This provides a good balance between performance and area efficiency for a 4-bit adder:

1. **Parallel Carry Paths**: Instead of waiting for carry propagation, we'll compute both possible outcomes (carry=0 and carry=1) for the upper bits simultaneously.

2. **Conditional Carry Selection**: Use the actual carry from lower bits to select between the pre-computed sums, reducing the critical path.

3. **Optimized Structure**:
   - Bits 0-1: Standard ripple carry (small enough for minimal delay)
   - Bits 2-3: Carry-select with conditional propagation
   - Final overflow: Directly from the selected carry

Benefits:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (critical path reduced by parallel computation)
- More area-efficient than traditional carry-select (avoids duplicate adders)
- Maintains clean hierarchy and readability
