Instead of using a traditional ripple-carry adder, we'll implement a carry-select adder architecture. This approach divides the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks that compute in parallel. The lower 2 bits use a ripple-carry adder, while the upper 2 bits compute both possible results (with carry-in 0 and 1). A multiplexer then selects the correct upper result based on the actual carry-out from the lower bits. This provides a good balance between speed and area for a 4-bit adder.
