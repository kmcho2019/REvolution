To optimize the 4-bit adder for better PPA metrics, we'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This will:
1. Reduce the critical path by calculating carry bits in parallel rather than sequentially
2. Maintain good power efficiency by minimizing switching activity
3. Keep area reasonable by leveraging shared generate/propagate logic

The CLA calculates generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for each bit position, then computes carries using these signals. This reduces the carry chain depth from linear to logarithmic.
