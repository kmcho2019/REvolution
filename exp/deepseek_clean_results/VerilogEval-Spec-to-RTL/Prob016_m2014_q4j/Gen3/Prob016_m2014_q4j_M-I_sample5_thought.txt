To optimize the 4-bit adder, I'll implement a simple ripple-carry adder structure. This approach:
1. Eliminates the carry-select overhead since for 4 bits, ripple-carry provides sufficient performance
2. Reduces area by using a single chain of full adders instead of parallel computation
3. Lowers power by avoiding redundant computations
4. Simplifies the design by flattening the hierarchy
5. Maintains correct functionality while improving PPA metrics
