The original solution uses a carry-select adder which is overkill for a 4-bit adder and results in unnecessary area and power consumption. We can optimize this by:
1. Implementing a simple ripple-carry adder structure which is more area-efficient for small bit-widths
2. Flattening the hierarchy to remove module boundaries and enable better synthesis optimizations
3. Directly implementing the full adders in the top module to reduce overhead
4. Maintaining the same functionality while reducing area and power
5. The ripple-carry approach will be sufficient for 4 bits with minimal timing impact
