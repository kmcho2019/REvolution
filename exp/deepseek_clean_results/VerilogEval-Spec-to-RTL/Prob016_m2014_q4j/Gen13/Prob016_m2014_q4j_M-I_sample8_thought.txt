To optimize the 4-bit adder for better PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This approach will:
1. Reduce critical path delay by computing carry bits in parallel (O(log n) delay)
2. Maintain similar power consumption by using optimized lookahead logic
3. Keep area efficient through shared carry generation logic

The CLA will use propagate (P) and generate (G) signals to compute carries in advance, significantly improving timing performance while keeping power and area reasonable.
