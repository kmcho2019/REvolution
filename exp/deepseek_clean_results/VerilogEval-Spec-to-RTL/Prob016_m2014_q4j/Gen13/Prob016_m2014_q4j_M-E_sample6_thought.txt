I'll implement a 4-bit carry-skip adder that combines ripple-carry efficiency with partial carry-skip logic. The key innovations are:

1. **Carry-Skip Logic**: When all propagate conditions are met (P[3:0] all 1), the carry skips the intermediate stages, improving worst-case delay.

2. **Parallel Computation**: The propagate (P) and generate (G) terms are computed in parallel for all bits.

3. **Hybrid Structure**: For 4 bits, we only need one skip block, making it area-efficient while still providing timing benefits.

4. **Optimized Overflow**: The final carry-out naturally becomes the overflow bit (sum[4]).

5. **Advantages**:
   - Better worst-case timing than pure ripple-carry
   - More area-efficient than carry-lookahead for 4 bits
   - Simple skip logic implementation
   - Maintains full functionality with overflow detection
