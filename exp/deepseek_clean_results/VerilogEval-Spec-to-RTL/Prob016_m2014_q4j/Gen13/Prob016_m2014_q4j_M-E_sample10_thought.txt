To create a novel solution, I'll implement a carry-select adder architecture which provides better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable complexity for 4-bit addition. The key ideas are:
1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks
2. Compute both possible results (with carry=0 and carry=1) for the upper bits in parallel
3. Use the carry-out from the lower bits to select the correct upper result
4. This approach reduces the critical path compared to ripple-carry
5. The overflow bit is naturally included as the final carry-out
