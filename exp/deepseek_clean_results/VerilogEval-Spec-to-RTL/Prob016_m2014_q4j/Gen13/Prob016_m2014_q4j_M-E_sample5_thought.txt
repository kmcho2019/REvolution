Here's a novel approach using a carry-skip adder architecture, which offers a good balance between ripple-carry simplicity and carry-lookahead performance:

1. **Carry-Skip Concept**: The adder is divided into blocks where carry propagation is either rippled through or skipped if all bits in a block would propagate the carry.
2. **4-bit Implementation**: For our 4-bit case, we'll use two 2-bit blocks with carry-skip logic between them.
3. **Efficiency**: This reduces the worst-case carry propagation path while maintaining reasonable area/power.
4. **Key Components**:
   - Two 2-bit ripple-carry adders
   - Carry-skip logic that detects when all bits would propagate carry (P0&P1)
   - Multiplexer to select between rippled carry or skipped carry
5. **Output**: The final carry becomes the MSB of the sum, handling overflow naturally.

This approach provides better timing than pure ripple-carry while being more area-efficient than full carry-lookahead for 4-bit addition.
