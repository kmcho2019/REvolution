Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Skip Adder with Optimized Grouping

Instead of the traditional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead approaches, I propose a carry-skip adder with optimized 2-bit grouping. This provides a balance between performance and area:
1. **Grouping Strategy**: Divide the 4-bit adder into two 2-bit groups
2. **Carry Skip Logic**: Implement carry propagation between groups only when both bits in a group would propagate a carry
3. **Performance**: Reduces worst-case carry propagation path compared to ripple-carry
4. **Area Efficiency**: Requires less logic than full carry-lookahead while still improving speed
5. **Power Consideration**: Skip logic reduces unnecessary switching in intermediate carry chains

The design will use:
- Standard full adders for bit-level operations
- AND-OR logic for carry skip conditions
- Direct connection of final carry-out to sum[4] for overflow
