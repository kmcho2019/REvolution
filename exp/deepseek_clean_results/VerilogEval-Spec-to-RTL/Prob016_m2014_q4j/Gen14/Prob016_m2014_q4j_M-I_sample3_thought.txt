To optimize the 4-bit adder while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This improves performance by calculating carry bits in parallel rather than waiting for ripple propagation. The CLA reduces the critical path from O(n) to O(log n) while keeping area reasonable. Key improvements:

1. Generate and propagate signals computed in parallel
2. Carry bits calculated using lookahead logic
3. Maintains same interface and functionality
4. Better timing with similar area/power tradeoff
5. Overflow still naturally captured in sum[4]
