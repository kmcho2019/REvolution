A novel approach using carry-select adder architecture:
1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit segments
2. Compute both possible sums (with carry=0 and carry=1) for the upper 2 bits in parallel
3. Use the carry-out from the lower 2 bits to select the correct upper sum
4. This reduces critical path delay compared to ripple-carry while maintaining full functionality
5. The overflow is determined by the final carry-out
This approach provides better timing performance while keeping the design clean and modular.
