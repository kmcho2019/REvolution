Instead of using the traditional ripple-carry adder approach, we can implement the 4-bit adder using a carry-select architecture. This approach divides the adder into two 2-bit sections. The lower 2 bits use a regular ripple-carry adder, while the upper 2 bits compute both possible sums (assuming carry-in of 0 and 1). The actual carry-out from the lower section then selects the correct upper sum. This provides some parallelism and can potentially improve timing compared to a pure ripple-carry approach.
