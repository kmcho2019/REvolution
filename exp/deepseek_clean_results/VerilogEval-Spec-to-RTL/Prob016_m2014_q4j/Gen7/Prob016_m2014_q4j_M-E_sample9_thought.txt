Here's a novel approach using a carry-skip adder architecture, which offers a good balance between ripple-carry simplicity and carry-lookahead performance:

1. **Carry-Skip Concept**: The adder is divided into blocks where the carry can either propagate through or skip over blocks when all bits in a block are propagate conditions (P = x XOR y).
2. **4-bit Implementation**: For a 4-bit adder, we'll use two 2-bit blocks with carry-skip logic between them.
3. **Advantages**:
   - Better critical path than ripple-carry (O(√n) vs O(n))
   - Simpler than full carry-lookahead
   - Maintains reasonable area/power tradeoff
4. **Implementation**:
   - First 2 bits use standard ripple-carry
   - Skip logic detects if all bits in first block are propagate
   - Second 2 bits can either take ripple-carry or skip the first block's carry
   - Final carry-out becomes MSB of sum
