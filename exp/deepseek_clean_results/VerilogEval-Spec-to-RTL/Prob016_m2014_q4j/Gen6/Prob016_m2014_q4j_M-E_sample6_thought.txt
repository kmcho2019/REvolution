Here's a novel approach using a carry-select adder architecture, which provides better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead for a 4-bit adder:

1. **Carry-Select Concept**: 
   - Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks
   - Compute both possible sums for the upper bits (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
   - Use the actual carry-out from lower bits to select the correct sum

2. **Benefits**:
   - Parallel computation reduces critical path delay compared to ripple-carry
   - Still maintains full customizability at the full-adder level
   - Scales better than pure ripple-carry for wider adders

3. **Implementation**:
   - Use two 2-bit ripple-carry adders for the lower and upper bits
   - The upper bits adder computes both possible sums (carry0 and carry1 cases)
   - Mux selects correct upper sum based on lower carry-out
   - Final overflow bit comes from the selected upper carry
