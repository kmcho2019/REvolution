Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, here's an improved strategy for the 4-bit adder:

1. **Simplification**: For a 4-bit adder, the carry-select approach is overkill. A ripple-carry adder provides better PPA tradeoffs at this width.
2. **Power Optimization**: Remove redundant logic (dual adders for upper bits) to reduce switching activity.
3. **Area Reduction**: Use a single carry chain, eliminating duplicate logic and intermediate wires.
4. **Performance**: While ripple-carry has longer critical path, for 4 bits the difference is negligible compared to area/power savings.
5. **Implementation**:
   - Use a simple ripple-carry structure with 4 full adders chained together
   - Directly propagate the carry through all stages
   - Final carry-out becomes the MSB of the sum

This approach maintains correctness while optimizing for area and power with minimal impact on timing for a 4-bit adder.
