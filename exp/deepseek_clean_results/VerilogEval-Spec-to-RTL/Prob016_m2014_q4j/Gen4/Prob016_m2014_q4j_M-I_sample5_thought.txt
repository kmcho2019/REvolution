Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, I'll optimize the design with these considerations:

1. **Simplification**: For a 4-bit adder, the carry-select approach's benefits don't outweigh its overhead. A ripple-carry adder is more efficient.

2. **Power Reduction**: Eliminating the duplicate adders will significantly reduce dynamic power.

3. **Area Optimization**: A single ripple-carry chain will use fewer gates and interconnects.

4. **Timing**: While ripple-carry has longer critical path, for 4 bits it's still acceptable and meets timing constraints.

5. **Implementation**:
   - Use a single chain of 4 full adders
   - Carry propagates naturally between stages
   - Final carry becomes the overflow bit
   - Maintains same functionality with better PPA

6. **Tradeoffs**:
   - Simpler and more area-efficient
   - Lower power consumption
   - Slightly longer critical path but still meets timing
   - Easier to verify and maintain
