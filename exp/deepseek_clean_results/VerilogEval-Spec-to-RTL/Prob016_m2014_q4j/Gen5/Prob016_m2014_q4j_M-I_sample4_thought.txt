Based on the feedback and PPA metrics, I'll optimize the design with these considerations:

1. **Simplification**: For a 4-bit adder, the carry-select approach's benefits don't outweigh its area/power costs. A ripple-carry adder is more efficient.

2. **Power Reduction**: Eliminating duplicate adder logic will significantly reduce dynamic power.

3. **Area Reduction**: Single carry chain implementation will use fewer gates and routing resources.

4. **Maintained Performance**: The ripple-carry delay for 4 bits is still acceptable for most applications.

Implementation Strategy:
- Use a single chain of 4 full adders
- Carry propagates naturally from LSB to MSB
- Final carry becomes the overflow bit (sum[4])
- This reduces area by ~50% and power by eliminating redundant computations
