This solution implements a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) with optimized lookahead logic:

1. **Concept**: 
   - Instead of waiting for carries to ripple through each stage, we compute all carry signals in parallel using generate (G) and propagate (P) terms
   - For 4 bits, we can compute all carries directly without hierarchical lookahead
   - The overflow bit naturally becomes the final carry-out

2. **Advantages**:
   - Constant-time carry computation regardless of bit width (for small adders)
   - Eliminates the linear carry propagation delay of ripple-carry adders
   - Clear separation of generate/propagate logic from sum computation

3. **Implementation**:
   - First compute all G (generate) and P (propagate) terms
   - Then compute carries using the lookahead equations
   - Finally compute sums using the precomputed carries
   - The overflow is simply the final carry-out (C4)

4. **Tradeoffs**:
   - More gates than ripple-carry for small adders
   - Excellent performance with predictable timing
   - Scales better than ripple-carry for wider adders
