To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we'll chain together 4 full adder modules. Each full adder takes two input bits (x and y) and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and carry-out. The carry-out of each stage becomes the carry-in of the next stage. The final carry-out becomes the overflow bit (MSB of the 5-bit sum output). We'll instantiate 4 full adders and connect them sequentially.
