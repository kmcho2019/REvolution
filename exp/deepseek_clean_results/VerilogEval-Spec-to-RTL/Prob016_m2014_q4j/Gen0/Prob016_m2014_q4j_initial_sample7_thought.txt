To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we'll need to chain together 4 full adder modules. Each full adder takes a carry-in from the previous stage and produces a sum bit and carry-out. The carry-out from each stage becomes the carry-in for the next stage. The final carry-out will be the overflow bit (MSB of the 5-bit sum). We'll instantiate 4 full adders and connect them properly.
