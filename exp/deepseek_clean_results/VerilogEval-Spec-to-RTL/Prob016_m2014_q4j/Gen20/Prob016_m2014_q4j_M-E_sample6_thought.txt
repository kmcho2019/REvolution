Novel Architectural Idea: Carry-Skip Adder with Early Termination
1. **Hybrid Approach**: Combine ripple-carry with carry-skip logic to potentially reduce critical path
2. **Early Termination Detection**: Add logic to detect when carry propagation can be skipped
3. **Optimized MSB Handling**: Special case for MSB to handle overflow efficiently
4. **Parallel Computation**: Compute propagate signals in parallel to enable faster carry skipping

Key Advantages:
- Potential speedup over pure ripple-carry when carry propagation is limited
- Still maintains simple structure for 4-bit case
- Interesting middle-ground between ripple-carry and more complex adders
- Preserves correct overflow handling
