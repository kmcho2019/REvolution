Novel Approach: Carry-Save Adder with Final Carry-Propagate Stage

1. **Parallel Processing**: Use carry-save addition for the first 3 bits to compute sum and carry bits in parallel, reducing critical path delay compared to ripple-carry.

2. **Final Ripple Stage**: Combine the saved carries in a final ripple-carry stage for the last bit and overflow detection.

3. **Efficient Overflow**: The final carry from the last stage naturally becomes the overflow bit (sum[4]).

4. **Resource Sharing**: Reuse the same FullAdder module for both carry-save and final stages.

Benefits:
- Faster than pure ripple-carry (3-bit parallel processing)
- Still maintains simple structure
- Efficient overflow handling
- Better timing with only partial ripple
