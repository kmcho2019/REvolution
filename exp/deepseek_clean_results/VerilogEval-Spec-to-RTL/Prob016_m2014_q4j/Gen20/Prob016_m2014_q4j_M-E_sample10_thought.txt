Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Select Adder with Precomputation
1. **Parallel Computation**: Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks that compute results for both carry-in possibilities (0 and 1) in parallel.
2. **Carry Selection**: Use the actual carry-out from the lower chunk to select the correct result from the upper chunk.
3. **Early Carry Resolution**: The carry from the lower 2 bits resolves quickly, allowing the correct upper sum to be selected with minimal delay.
4. **Optimized Implementation**: Use efficient full adder designs and minimize logic levels in the carry selection path.

Key Advantages:
- Faster than ripple-carry due to parallel computation
- More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
- Balanced critical path for all input combinations
- Scalable to wider adders by adding more stages
