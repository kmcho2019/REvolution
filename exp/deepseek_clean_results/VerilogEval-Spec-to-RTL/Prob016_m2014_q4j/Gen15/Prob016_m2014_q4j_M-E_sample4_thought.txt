Novel Architecture Idea: Carry-Skip Adder with Optimized Grouping

Instead of a standard ripple-carry adder, I'll implement a carry-skip adder that provides better timing performance while maintaining reasonable area overhead. The key innovations are:

1. **Grouping Strategy**: Divide the 4-bit adder into two 2-bit groups with carry-skip logic between them. This balances timing improvement vs area cost.

2. **Skip Logic**: Implement carry propagation detection (P = a XOR b) to determine when carry can skip a group, reducing worst-case delay.

3. **Optimized Full Adders**: Use modified full adders that output both sum and propagate signals to support the skip logic.

4. **Overflow Handling**: The final carry-out naturally becomes the overflow bit (sum[4]) as in the original design.

This approach provides better timing performance (O(√n) delay) than ripple-carry while avoiding the area overhead of full carry-lookahead.
