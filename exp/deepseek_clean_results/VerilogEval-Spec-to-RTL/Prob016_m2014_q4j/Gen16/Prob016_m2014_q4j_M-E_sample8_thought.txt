I'll implement a novel carry-skip adder architecture that offers better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable complexity. The key ideas are:
1. Divide the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit blocks
2. Each block computes its own carry-out and generate/propagate signals
3. Implement carry-skip logic between blocks to bypass ripple propagation when possible
4. The skip condition occurs when all bits in a block propagate their carry (P=1)
5. This reduces worst-case carry propagation delay while using less hardware than carry-select
6. The overflow bit is naturally included as the final carry-out
