To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a basic ripple-carry adder using full adders. This approach:
1. Uses a chain of full adders where the carry-out of each stage feeds into the next
2. Naturally handles the 4-bit addition with overflow
3. Is straightforward and minimal in terms of components
4. The final carry-out becomes the overflow bit in the sum output
5. Uses only 4 full adders, reducing area and power compared to the carry-select approach
