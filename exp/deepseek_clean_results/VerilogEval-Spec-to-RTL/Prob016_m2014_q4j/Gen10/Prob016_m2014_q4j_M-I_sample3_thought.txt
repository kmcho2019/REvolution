To optimize the 4-bit adder for better PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure. This provides:

1. **Performance Improvement**: 
   - CLA reduces carry propagation delay from O(n) to O(log n) by computing carry bits in parallel
   - Eliminates the ripple-carry critical path bottleneck

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - Reduced switching activity in the carry chain
   - More efficient logic implementation compared to ripple-carry

3. **Area Consideration**:
   - While CLA requires additional generate/propagate logic, the overall area impact is minimal for 4-bit
   - Better area-delay product than ripple-carry for this size

4. **Implementation Strategy**:
   - Compute generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for each bit
   - Calculate carry bits using lookahead equations
   - Sum bits remain XOR operations
   - Final overflow is naturally handled by the carry-out
