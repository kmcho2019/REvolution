Here's a novel approach using carry-select adder architecture, which provides better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead for a 4-bit adder:

1. **Carry-Select Concept**: 
   - Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks
   - Compute each chunk twice: once assuming carry-in=0 and once assuming carry-in=1
   - Use the actual carry from the previous stage to select the correct result

2. **Benefits**:
   - Parallel computation reduces critical path delay compared to ripple-carry
   - For 4 bits, this provides a good balance between speed and area
   - The overflow bit (sum[4]) is naturally generated as the final carry

3. **Implementation**:
   - First 2 bits use a regular ripple-carry adder
   - Upper 2 bits have dual adders (for carry=0 and carry=1 cases)
   - 2:1 mux selects between the two upper results based on actual carry
