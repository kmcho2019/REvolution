A novel approach using carry-select adder architecture:
1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit segments
2. Compute both possible sums for the upper bits (assuming carry=0 and carry=1)
3. Use the actual carry from the lower bits to select the correct upper sum
4. This reduces critical path delay compared to ripple-carry while maintaining full functionality
5. The overflow bit is naturally included as the final carry
This approach provides better timing performance while keeping area reasonable.
