Here's a novel approach using a carry-skip adder architecture, which offers better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead:

1. **Carry-Skip Concept**: The adder is divided into blocks where carry can either propagate through or skip over blocks when all bits in a block are propagate conditions (P = x XOR y).

2. **Implementation Details**:
   - Divide the 4-bit adder into two 2-bit blocks
   - Each block computes its own group propagate (P_group) and generate (G_group)
   - The carry either propagates through the block or skips it when P_group is true
   - This reduces the worst-case carry propagation delay

3. **Advantages**:
   - Better performance than ripple-carry (shorter critical path)
   - More area-efficient than full carry-lookahead
   - Scales better for wider adders
   - Maintains clear modular structure

4. **Optimizations**:
   - Directly compute sum bits using propagate signals
   - Efficient carry chain with skip logic
   - Clean hierarchical design
