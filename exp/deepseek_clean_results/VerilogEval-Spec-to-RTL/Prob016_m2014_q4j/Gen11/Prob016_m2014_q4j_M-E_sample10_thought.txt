Here's a novel approach using a carry-select adder architecture, which provides better performance than ripple-carry while still being straightforward to implement. The key idea is to:

1. Split the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks
2. Compute both possible sums for the upper bits (assuming carry-in 0 and 1)
3. Use the actual carry-out from the lower bits to select the correct upper sum
4. Combine the results to get the final 5-bit output

This approach reduces the critical path compared to ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead.
