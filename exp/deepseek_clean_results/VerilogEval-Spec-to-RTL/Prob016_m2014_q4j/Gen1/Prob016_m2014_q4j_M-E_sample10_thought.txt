Instead of using a ripple-carry adder, we'll implement a carry-select adder structure. This approach divides the 4-bit addition into two 2-bit chunks that are computed in parallel with both possible carry-in assumptions (0 and 1). The correct result is then selected based on the actual carry-out from the previous stage. This provides better performance than ripple-carry while maintaining reasonable area overhead for a 4-bit adder.
