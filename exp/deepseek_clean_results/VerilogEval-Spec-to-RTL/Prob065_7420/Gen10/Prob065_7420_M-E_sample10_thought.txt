Here's a novel approach that implements the 7420 functionality using a lookup table (LUT) based design. Instead of traditional gate-level implementation, we'll:
1. Precompute all possible 16 combinations of 4-input NAND outputs
2. Use the 4 inputs as an address to select the correct output
3. Implement this for both NAND gates in the chip
4. This approach is unconventional for simple gates but demonstrates an alternative digital design methodology
5. Benefits include uniform delay paths and potential area savings in FPGA implementations
