The current implementation is already optimal for basic functionality, but we can make minor improvements for better power efficiency and timing robustness:

1. **Input Buffering**: Add input buffers to improve signal integrity and reduce input capacitance effects, which helps with timing and power in larger designs.

2. **Balanced Gates**: Use a balanced gate structure (two 2-input NANDs followed by a NOR) to implement the 4-input NAND, which may offer better timing characteristics in some technology libraries.

3. **Explicit Wire Declarations**: Make internal connections explicit for better readability and potential optimization by synthesis tools.

The area will increase slightly due to additional gates, but this may be offset by better timing and power characteristics in some scenarios.
