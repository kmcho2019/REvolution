The original implementation uses direct continuous assignments for the NAND operations. Let's refactor this to:
1. Use an intermediate wire for each NAND gate's AND operation before inversion
2. Group related signals together for better readability
3. Maintain the same functionality while making the structure more explicit

This approach breaks down the operation into two clear steps (AND followed by NOT) and groups related signals, which can improve readability and potentially help with debugging in more complex designs.
