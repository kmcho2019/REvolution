Simplified design approach:

1. **State Machine**:
   - Use 4 distinct states: IDLE, SHIFT, COUNTING, DONE
   - Simple binary encoding for minimal logic

2. **Pattern Detection**:
   - Maintain 4-bit shift register only in IDLE state
   - Clear pattern only on reset or after full cycle completion

3. **Shift Control**:
   - Use simple 2-bit counter for exact 4-cycle shift duration
   - Counter automatically rolls over after 4 cycles

4. **State Transitions**:
   - Clear transitions based on explicit conditions
   - No complex conditions or data-dependent transitions

5. **Outputs**:
   - Direct mapping from states to outputs
   - No additional combinational logic needed

Key simplifications:
- Minimal state encoding
- Explicit cycle counting for shift phase
- Clear state transition conditions
- Reduced sequential logic
