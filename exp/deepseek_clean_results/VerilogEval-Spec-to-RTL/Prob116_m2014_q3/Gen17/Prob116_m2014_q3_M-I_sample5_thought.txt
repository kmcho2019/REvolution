- The output is 1 when (x[3]x[4] = 11) regardless of x[1]x[2] in most cases
- The output is also 1 when (x[3]x[4] = 10) and (x[1]x[2] = 00 or 01)
- The don't-care conditions allow us to simplify the expression to: f = x[3] | (~x[4] & ~x[2])

This new expression:
- Uses only 3 gates (1 NOT, 1 AND, 1 OR)

- Should maintain the same functionality while being more area-efficient
