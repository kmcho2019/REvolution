After analyzing the current implementation and PPA metrics, here's an optimized approach:

1. Key Observations:
   - The current NAND-only implementation already shows good PPA metrics (0.0 timing violations, low power, small area)
   - The 3-input NAND in term2 is the largest component (x[0] & x[1] & x[3])
   - We can potentially reduce this to 2-input gates by leveraging more don't-care conditions

2. Optimization Strategy:
   - Notice that when x[0]=1 and x[1]=1, f is 1 regardless of x[3] in some cases (from K-map)
   - We can split the 3-input AND into two 2-input NANDs
   - This might slightly increase gate count but could improve area due to using smaller standard cells

3. New Implementation:
   - First term remains (~x[0] & x[2])
   - Second term becomes (x[0] & x[1]) then AND with x[3]
   - Convert all to NAND operations
   - Total gates: 4 NANDs (but all 2-input) instead of 1 3-input and 2 2-input NANDs

This change maintains the same functionality while potentially improving area and power due to using only 2-input gates which are typically more area-efficient in standard cell libraries.
