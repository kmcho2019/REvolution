Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure that leverages the K-map's don't-care conditions:

1. Observation:
   - The output depends heavily on x[2] (middle bit)
   - When x[2]=1, output is primarily determined by x[0] (with some exceptions)
   - When x[2]=0, output depends on combination of other bits

2. Novel Architecture:
   - Use x[2] as the primary selector for a 2:1 mux
   - The mux inputs will be:
     * When x[2]=1: A simplified expression focusing on x[0] and x[3]
     * When x[2]=0: A simplified expression focusing on x[1] and x[3]
   - This structure naturally handles don't-cares by choosing the simpler path in each case

3. Benefits:
   - Clear hierarchical structure
   - Efficient use of multiplexer logic
   - Better physical implementation properties (regular structure)
   - Potentially better timing due to balanced paths
