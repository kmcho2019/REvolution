A novel approach using a hierarchical decoder structure:
1. First decode the x[3:4] inputs (rows) into one-hot representation
2. Then decode the x[1:2] inputs (columns) into one-hot representation
3. Use the decoded signals to implement the K-map function through a priority encoder-like structure
4. This approach leverages the regularity of digital decoders and provides a clear mapping from the K-map to hardware

Advantages:
- Highly modular and scalable for larger K-maps
- Clear separation of row and column decoding logic
- Easy to modify if K-map specifications change
- Potential for better optimization in synthesis
