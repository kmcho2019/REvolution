Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure:
1. Instead of traditional logic minimization, implement the K-map as a 4:1 mux where x[3] and x[4] are the select lines
2. Each mux input will be a 2:1 mux controlled by x[2], implementing the columns of the K-map
3. This leverages Verilog's built-in mux optimization and provides a regular structure
4. Carefully choose don't-care values to simplify the mux inputs
5. The hierarchical approach may offer better routing and placement characteristics

Key benefits:
- Regular structure may improve physical implementation
- Explicit muxes can be efficiently mapped to FPGA or ASIC resources
- Clear correspondence to original K-map structure
- Potential for better timing due to balanced paths
