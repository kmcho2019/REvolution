Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical decoder structure:

1. First, decode the x[3:4] inputs into 4 possible states (00, 01, 11, 10)
2. Then for each decoded state, implement the required logic for x[1:2]
3. This approach is more systematic and scales better for larger K-maps
4. We'll use the don't-cares to minimize each sub-logic block
5. The final output is a 4:1 mux selecting between the sub-logic outputs based on x[3:4]

Benefits:
- Clear separation of row and column logic
- Easier to modify individual sections
- Potentially better for synthesis optimization
- More readable and maintainable structure
