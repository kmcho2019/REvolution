Looking at the Karnaugh map, we can identify the essential prime implicants to simplify the logic:

1. The '1's in the K-map can be covered by three essential terms:
   - x[3] & ~x[4] (bottom two rows when x[1]x[2] is 00 or 01)
   - x[3] & x[4] (top two rows when x[1]x[2] is 00 or 01)
   - ~x[3] & x[4] & x[1] & x[2] (middle when x[3]x[4] is 01 and x[1]x[2] is 11)

2. However, we can further optimize by:
   - Noticing that when x[3] is high, f is high for most cases except when x[4] is high and x[1]x[2] is 10
   - Using don't-cares to our advantage to simplify the expression

3. The simplified expression becomes:
   f = x[3] | (x[4] & x[1] & x[2])

This covers all required '1's while being much simpler, using just one OR and one AND operation.
