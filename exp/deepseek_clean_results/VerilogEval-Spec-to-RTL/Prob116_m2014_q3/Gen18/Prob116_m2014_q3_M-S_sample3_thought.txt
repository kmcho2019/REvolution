To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, I analyzed the Karnaugh map and identified that:
1. When x[2] is 1 and x[0] is 0, f is always 1 (from the 11 column)
2. When x[3] is 1 and both x[0] and x[1] are 1, f is also 1 (from the 11 row)
3. All other cases where f must be 0 are covered by these conditions

The simplification comes from:
- Removing redundant parentheses
- Maintaining the same logical functionality with minimal operators
- Keeping the same efficient use of don't-care conditions
- The simplified expression is logically equivalent but more concise
