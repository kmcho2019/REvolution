   - x[3]x[4] = 10 and x[1]x[2] = 00 or 01
   - x[3]x[4] = 01 and x[1]x[2] = 11

2. The simplest implementation comes from recognizing that:
   - f is 1 when either:
     * x[3] is 1 AND x[4] is 1 (top two rows)
     * OR x[3] is 1 AND x[4] is 0 AND x[2] is 0 (left two columns)
     * OR x[3] is 0 AND x[4] is 1 AND x[1] is 1 AND x[2] is 1 (middle cell)
