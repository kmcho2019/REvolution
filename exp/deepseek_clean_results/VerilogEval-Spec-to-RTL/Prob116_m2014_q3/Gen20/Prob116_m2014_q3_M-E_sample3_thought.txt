Here's a novel approach using a multiplexer-based implementation:
1. Instead of traditional logic gates, we'll use a 4:1 multiplexer to select between different output values based on x[3] and x[4]
2. The select lines will be x[3] and x[4], grouping the K-map by rows
3. Each multiplexer input will implement the simplified logic for that row, taking advantage of don't-care conditions
4. This approach may offer better area/power tradeoffs in some technologies due to efficient multiplexer implementations

Key insights:
- The 00 row can be optimized to always output 0 (taking advantage of don't-cares)
- The 01 row needs careful handling of the 11 column case
- The 11 and 10 rows can share some logic patterns
