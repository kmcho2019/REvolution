Here's a novel approach using a lookup table (LUT) implemented with a case statement. This method:
1. Explicitly maps all input combinations to outputs based on the K-map
2. Clearly shows the relationship between inputs and outputs
3. Makes optimal use of don't-care conditions by choosing values that minimize logic
4. Provides a straightforward implementation that's easy to verify against the K-map
5. May offer better synthesis results for certain technologies that optimize case statements well

The case statement will cover all 16 possible input combinations, with don't-cares filled in to simplify adjacent groupings.
