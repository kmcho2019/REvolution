The original solution uses a simple combinational assignment with minimal logic. To refactor while maintaining the same functionality, I'll:
1. Use an alternative expression that achieves the same result
2. Leverage different groupings of the same terms
3. Maintain the same efficient use of don't-care conditions
4. The new expression will be logically equivalent but structured differently

The key insight is that we can group the terms differently:
- First term covers when x[3] is high and either x[0] or x[1] is high
