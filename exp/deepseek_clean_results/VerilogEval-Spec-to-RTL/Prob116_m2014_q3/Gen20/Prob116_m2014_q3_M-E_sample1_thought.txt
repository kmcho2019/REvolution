Instead of traditional K-map minimization, I'll implement a hierarchical decoder-based solution that:
1. First decodes the x[3:4] (rows) and x[1:2] (columns) separately
2. Then combines these decoded signals with the don't-care conditions
3. Uses a priority encoder-like structure to select the output value
4. Implements the function with a unique combination of multiplexers and decoders

This approach offers:
- Better scalability for larger K-maps
- More regular structure for physical implementation
- Potential power savings through hierarchical decoding
- Clear separation of row/column logic
