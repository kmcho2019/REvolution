1. Analyzing the K-map:

   - When x[2] is 0 (left two columns), f is 1 when both x[3] and x[1] are 1 (from term2)
   - All other cases can be covered by these conditions

   - This reduces one AND gate while maintaining correct functionality due to don't-care conditions

   - This maintains all required 1s in the K-map while potentially using don't-cares more aggressively
