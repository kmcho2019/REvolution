To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, I'll analyze the Karnaugh map more carefully to find the minimal expression:

Key Observations:
1. The output is 1 when:
   - x[3]x[4] = 11 (regardless of x[1]x[2] in most cases)
   - x[3]x[4] = 10 and x[1]x[2] ≠ 11
   - x[3]x[4] = 01 and x[1]x[2] = 11

2. The output is 0 when:
   - x[3]x[4] = 00 and x[1]x[2] = 01
   - x[3]x[4] = 01 and x[1]x[2] = 00 or 10

Simplification Approach:
- We can cover all 1s by looking at x[3] first (since 11 and 10 rows mostly give 1)
- Then handle the special case in x[3]x[4] = 01 when x[1]x[2] = 11
- The minimal expression becomes: f = x[3] | (x[1] & x[2] & ~x[4])

This uses just 3 gates (OR, AND, NOT) instead of the original 4 gates.
