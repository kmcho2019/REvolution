After carefully analyzing the issues in the previous implementation, here's the corrected approach:

1. Bit Order Correction:
   - The input x[3:0] should be interpreted as x[3], x[2], x[1], x[0] (MSB to LSB)
   - The K-map uses x[3]x[2] as rows and x[1]x[0] as columns

2. K-map Reinterpretation:
   - Rows (x[3]x[2]): 00, 01, 11, 10
   - Columns (x[1]x[0]): 00, 01, 11, 10

3. New Grouping Strategy:
   - Essential 1s grouping:
     * x[3] (covers all 1s in rows 10 and 11)
     * ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0] (covers the 1 at 0101)
   - Essential 0s grouping:
     * ~x[3] & ~x[1] (covers 0 at 0001)
     * ~x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1] (covers 0s at 0100 and 0110)

4. Optimized Logic:
   - f = x[3] OR (~x[2] AND x[1] AND ~x[0])
   - This covers all specified 1s while maintaining correct 0s
   - Don't-cares are used to minimize the logic

5. Verification:
   - 0001: 0 (via ~x[3] & ~x[1])
   - 0100: 0 (via ~x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1])
   - 0110: 0 (via ~x[3] & x[2] & ~x[1])
   - 0101: 1 (via ~x[2] & x[1] & ~x[0])
   - All x[3]=1 cases: 1 (via x[3] term)
