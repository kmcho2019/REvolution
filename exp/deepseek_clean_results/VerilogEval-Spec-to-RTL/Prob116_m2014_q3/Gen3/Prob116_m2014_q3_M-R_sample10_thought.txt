To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we'll:

1. Properly interpret the input bits (x[3:0] where x[3] and x[4] are row selectors)
2. Group the 1s in the map to form minimal logic expressions:
   - For x[3]x[4] = 11 (row 3): f=1 except when x[1]x[2]=10 (but marked as don't-care)
   - For x[3]x[4] = 10 (row 4): f=1 when x[1]x[2]=00 or 01, 0 when 10
   - For x[3]x[4] = 01 (row 2): f=1 only when x[1]x[2]=11
   - For x[3]x[4] = 00 (row 1): f=0 when x[1]x[2]=01

We'll use don't-cares to simplify:
- f = x[3] OR (x[4] AND x[1] AND x[2]) OR (~x[3] AND ~x[4] AND ~x[1] AND x[2])
