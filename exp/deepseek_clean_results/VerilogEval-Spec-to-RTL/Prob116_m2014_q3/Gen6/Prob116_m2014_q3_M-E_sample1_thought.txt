To implement the Karnaugh map correctly with inputs x[3:0]:
1. First, recognize that the K-map uses x[3]x[4] for rows and x[1]x[2] for columns, but since x is only 4 bits, we'll assume x[4] is a typo and should be x[0].

2. Analyze the K-map patterns more carefully:
   - f=1 when:
     * x[3]x[0]=11 (rows) with any x[1]x[2] except 10 (bottom-left 1's)
     * x[3]x[0]=10 (rows) with x[1]x[2]=00 or 01 (top-right 1's)
     * x[3]x[0]=01 (rows) with x[1]x[2]=11 (middle 1)

3. The simplified expression is:
   f = (x[3] & x[0]) | 
       (x[3] & ~x[0] & ~x[1]) | 
       (~x[3] & x[0] & x[1] & x[2])

This covers all required 1's while optimally using don't-cares.
