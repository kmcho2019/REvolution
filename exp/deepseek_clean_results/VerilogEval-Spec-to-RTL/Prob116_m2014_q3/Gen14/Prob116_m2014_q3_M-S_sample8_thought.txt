To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, I'll analyze the Karnaugh map to find the most minimal expression. Key observations:
1. The output is 1 when:
   - x[3]x[4] = 11 (regardless of x[1]x[2] in most cases)
   - x[3]x[4] = 10 and x[1]x[2] ≠ 11
   - x[3]x[4] = 01 and x[1]x[2] = 11
2. The output is 0 when:
   - x[3]x[4] = 00 and x[1]x[2] = 01
   - x[3]x[4] = 01 and x[1]x[2] = 00 or 10
   - x[3]x[4] = 10 and x[1]x[2] = 11

Simplified approach:
- Output is 1 when either:
  - x[3] is 1 and (x[4] is 1 OR x[1] is 1)
  - x[1]x[2] is 11 and x[3]x[4] is 01
This can be expressed as: f = x[3] | (x[1] & x[2] & ~x[4])
