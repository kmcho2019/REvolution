Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical decision tree structure:
1. First, check the most significant bits (x[3] and x[4]) to divide the problem space
2. Then, implement optimized logic for each quadrant of the K-map
3. Use multiplexers to select between these optimized solutions based on x[3:4]
4. This approach leverages the natural grouping in the K-map and may lead to better area/power tradeoffs

Key advantages:
- Hierarchical structure matches the K-map's natural grouping
- Each sub-module can be optimized independently
- Potentially better power characteristics due to localized switching
- Clear separation of concerns in the implementation
