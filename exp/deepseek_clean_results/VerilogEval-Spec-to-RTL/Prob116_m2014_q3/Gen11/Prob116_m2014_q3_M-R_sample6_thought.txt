Refactoring approach using direct sum-of-products implementation:

1. Key Observations:
   - The original multiplexer approach is logical but may not be optimal for PPA
   - Looking at the K-map, we can identify essential prime implicants:
     * x[3]x[4]=11 covers two 1's (when x[1]x[2]=00 and 01)
     * x[3]x[4]x[2]=101 covers another 1 (when x[1]x[2]=11)
     * x[3]x[1]x[0]=110 covers the remaining 1's

2. Refactoring Strategy:
   - Replace the mux structure with a direct sum-of-products implementation
   - Combine the essential implicants using OR operations
   - Carefully select don't-care values to minimize the logic expression
   - This should reduce gate count and potentially improve timing

3. Benefits:
   - Simpler combinatorial logic
   - Fewer logic levels (better timing)
   - Reduced area (fewer gates)
   - More straightforward implementation
