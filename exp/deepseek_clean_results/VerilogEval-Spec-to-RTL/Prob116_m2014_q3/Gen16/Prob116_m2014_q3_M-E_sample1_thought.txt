Innovative hierarchical MUX-based solution:
1. First-level MUX selects between x[3]x[4] groups (rows in K-map)
2. Second-level MUX implements the column logic within each row
3. Key innovation: Uses don't-cares to simplify MUX control logic
4. Employs priority encoding for the most frequent output patterns
5. Optimizes by collapsing some MUX levels where possible

Advantages:
- More scalable for larger K-maps
- Clear separation of row/column logic
- Better utilization of don't-care conditions
- Potential for better timing in deep submicron technologies
- Easier to modify if specifications change
