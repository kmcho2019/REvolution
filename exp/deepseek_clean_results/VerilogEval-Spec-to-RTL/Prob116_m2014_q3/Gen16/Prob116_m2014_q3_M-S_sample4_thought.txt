After carefully analyzing the Karnaugh map, I can see that the function can be simplified by better utilizing the don't-care conditions. The key observations are:

1. When x[3]x[4] = 11 or 10 (x[3]=1), f is mostly 1 except when x[1]x[2] = 10 (x[1]=1, x[2]=0)
2. When x[3]x[4] = 01, f is 1 only when x[1]x[2] = 11
3. When x[3]x[4] = 00, we can set f=0 for all cases (using don't-cares)

This leads to a simpler implementation:
f = x[3] & ~(x[1] & ~x[2]) | (x[3] & x[1] & x[2]) | (~x[3] & x[2] & x[1])

This can be further simplified to:
f = x[3] ? (~x[1] | x[2]) : (x[1] & x[2])
