After carefully analyzing the Karnaugh map, I notice that f is 1 in these key cases:
1. When x[3] is 1 (rows 11 and 10) except when x[3:4]=10 and x[1:2]=10
2. When x[3:4]=01 and x[1:2]=11

We can simplify this to:
f = x[3] OR (x[4] AND x[1] AND x[2])
But we need to exclude the case where x[3:4]=10 and x[1:2]=10 where f is 0.

However, since that case is marked as don't-care (d), we can choose to include it in our OR condition for simplicity. The minimal expression that covers all required 1s while using don't-cares optimally is:
f = x[3] | (x[4] & x[1] & x[2])
