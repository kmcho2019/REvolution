A hierarchical tree-based multiplexer approach can be more efficient than a flat implementation. We'll divide the 256 inputs into groups of 16 (16 groups total), then use a two-level selection process:
1. First level: Use the upper 4 bits of sel to select one of the 16 groups
2. Second level: Use the lower 4 bits to select within the chosen group
This reduces the fanout and critical path compared to a single 256:1 mux while maintaining the same functionality.
