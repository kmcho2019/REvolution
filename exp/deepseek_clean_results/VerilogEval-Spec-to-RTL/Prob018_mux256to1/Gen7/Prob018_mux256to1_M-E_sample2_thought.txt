Here's a novel hierarchical approach that divides the 256:1 mux into two levels:
1. First level: 16x 16:1 muxes (each handling 16 inputs)
2. Second level: 1x 16:1 mux (selecting between the first level outputs)
3. The 8-bit sel is split into:
   - Upper 4 bits (sel[7:4]) for second level selection
   - Lower 4 bits (sel[3:0]) for first level selection
4. This reduces fan-in at each stage while maintaining functionality
5. The approach may offer better timing/area tradeoffs in some technologies
