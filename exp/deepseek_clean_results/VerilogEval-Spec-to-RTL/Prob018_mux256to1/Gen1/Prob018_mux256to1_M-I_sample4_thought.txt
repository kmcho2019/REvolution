To optimize the 256-to-1 multiplexer for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical approach using two levels of 16:1 multiplexers. This reduces the fan-in of each multiplexer stage, potentially improving timing and area. The strategy is:
1. First level: 16x 16:1 muxes (each selecting one bit from a 16-bit chunk)
2. Second level: 1x 16:1 mux (selecting from the first level outputs)
This tree structure reduces the maximum fan-in from 256 to 16, which should improve timing and potentially reduce area. The power may also benefit from reduced switching activity in smaller muxes.
