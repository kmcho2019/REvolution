Innovative Crossbar Switch with Priority Encoding:
1. Instead of traditional tree structures, implement a crossbar switch that directly connects inputs to output
2. Use priority encoding to convert the 8-bit sel into a one-hot 256-bit control signal
3. The crossbar performs a bitwise AND between the one-hot control and input vector
4. A reduction OR combines all bits to produce the final output
5. Key advantages:
   - Single level of logic for selection (better timing)
   - No recursive hierarchy (simpler structure)
   - Natural power gating (only selected path activates)
   - Scales well with wider inputs
   - More regular layout for better physical design
