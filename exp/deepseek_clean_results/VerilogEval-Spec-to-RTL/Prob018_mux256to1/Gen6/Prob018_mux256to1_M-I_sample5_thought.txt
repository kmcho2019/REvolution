To optimize the 256-to-1 multiplexer for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement a hierarchical approach using two levels of 16-to-1 multiplexers. This reduces the maximum fan-in from 256 to 16, improving timing performance. The strategy is:

1. First level: 16x 16-to-1 muxes (each selecting 1 bit from a 16-bit group)
2. Second level: 1x 16-to-1 mux (selecting from the first level outputs)
3. Use the upper 4 bits of sel for the second level and lower 4 bits for first level

This approach:
- Reduces critical path by breaking the large mux into smaller ones
- Maintains the same functionality while being more area-efficient
- Potentially reduces power by limiting the active selection logic
