My innovative approach transforms the multiplexer problem into a shifting operation:
1. **Barrel Shifter Concept**: Treat the 256-bit input as a circular buffer and rotate it left by 'sel' positions
2. **Priority Extraction**: After rotation, the LSB (bit 0) will contain the selected value
3. **Efficient Implementation**: Use a hierarchical shift structure that combines smaller shifts (powers of 2) to achieve the full rotation
4. **Power Optimization**: Gate the shift operations to prevent unnecessary toggling of upper bits

Key advantages:
- Reduces critical path to log2(256)=8 levels of 2:1 muxes
- Eliminates recursive module instantiation overhead
- Enables potential power gating of unused shift stages
- More regular structure for better physical implementation
