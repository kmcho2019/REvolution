Novel Approach: Bit-Sliced Priority Selection
1. Instead of traditional hierarchical multiplexing, use a bit-sliced selection approach
2. Treat the 256-bit input as 8 slices of 32 bits each (8x32=256)
3. First stage: Use upper 3 bits of sel to select one of 8 slices (32-bit chunks)
4. Second stage: Use lower 5 bits of sel to select 1 bit from the selected 32-bit chunk
5. Key advantages:
   - Only two levels of selection (vs 4-8 in hierarchical approaches)
   - Simple, direct implementation with minimal logic depth
   - No recursion or complex parameterization needed
   - Easy to understand and verify
   - Potentially better timing due to flatter structure
