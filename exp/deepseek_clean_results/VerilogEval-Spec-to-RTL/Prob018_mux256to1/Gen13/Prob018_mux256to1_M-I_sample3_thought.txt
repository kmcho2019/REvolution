To optimize the PPA further, I'll implement a hierarchical tree-based multiplexer structure that:
1. Reduces fan-in by splitting the 256:1 mux into two levels of 16:1 muxes (16x16=256)
2. Only activates the necessary branches during operation, reducing switching power
3. Potentially improves timing by breaking the critical path into smaller segments
4. Maintains the same functionality while being more synthesis-friendly
5. Uses intermediate registers for better pipelining if needed (though not shown here)
