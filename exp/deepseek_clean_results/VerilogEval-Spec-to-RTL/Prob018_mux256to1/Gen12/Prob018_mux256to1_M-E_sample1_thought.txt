Innovative Solution: Hierarchical Transmission-Gate Multiplexer

Key Features:
1. **Transmission-Gate Based Selection**: 
   - Uses transmission gates for input selection instead of logic gates
   - Reduces power consumption by eliminating unnecessary switching
   - Provides better signal integrity for high-speed operation

2. **Two-Level Hierarchical Decoding**:
   - First level: 16x16 decoder (8-bit sel split into two 4-bit fields)
   - Second level: Final selection using decoded signals
   - Reduces fanout compared to single-level decoding

3. **Power Gating**:
   - Only activates the selected 16-input block
   - Significant power savings by disabling unselected blocks

4. **Low-Swing Signaling**:
   - Optional voltage scaling for internal signals
   - Further reduces dynamic power consumption

Advantages:
- Lower power than conventional mux implementations
- Potentially faster due to reduced fanout
- Better signal integrity from transmission gates
- Scalable to larger mux sizes
