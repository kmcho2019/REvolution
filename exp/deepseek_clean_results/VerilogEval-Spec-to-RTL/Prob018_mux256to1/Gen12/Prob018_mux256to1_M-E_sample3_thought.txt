Here's a novel hierarchical approach using a tree of smaller multiplexers:
1. Break down the 256:1 mux into two levels of 16:1 multiplexers (16x16=256)
2. First level: 16 16:1 muxes each selecting one bit from a 16-bit segment
3. Second level: 1 16:1 mux selecting between the outputs of first level muxes
4. Use sel[3:0] for first level selection and sel[7:4] for second level
5. This reduces fanout and creates a more balanced timing path
6. The hierarchical structure may offer better PPA in some technologies
