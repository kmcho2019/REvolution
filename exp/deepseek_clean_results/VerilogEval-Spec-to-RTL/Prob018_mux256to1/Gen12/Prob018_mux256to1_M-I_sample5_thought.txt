To optimize the 256-to-1 multiplexer further, I'll implement these improvements:

1. **Flat Implementation**:
   - Replace the recursive tree structure with a direct selection approach
   - Use a single assign statement with indexed selection
   - Eliminates hierarchical logic delays and reduces area

2. **Power Optimization**:
   - The flat structure naturally minimizes switching activity since it directly selects one bit
   - No intermediate muxes means no unnecessary power consumption

3. **Area Optimization**:
   - Single assignment statement is more compact than recursive instantiations
   - No need for partial width handling logic
   - Better utilization of standard cell libraries

4. **Timing Improvement**:
   - Single-level selection reduces critical path
   - No propagation delays through multiple mux levels

The flat implementation will provide better timing (single selection operation), lower power (no intermediate logic), and reduced area (simpler structure) while maintaining full functionality.
