To simplify the 256-to-1 multiplexer while maintaining functionality, I'll:

1. Use a direct selection approach instead of recursive hierarchy
2. Eliminate the parameterization since we know the exact size (256:1)
3. Simply select the bit indicated by the 8-bit sel input
4. This makes the implementation flat and straightforward

The simplified version will:
- Be easier to understand and maintain
- Have similar timing characteristics (selection is still just wire routing)
- Potentially reduce area by eliminating hierarchical structures
- Maintain the same functionality as originally specified
