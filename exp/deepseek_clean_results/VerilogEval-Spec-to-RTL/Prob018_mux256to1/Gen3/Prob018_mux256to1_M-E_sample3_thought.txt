Instead of the hierarchical approach, we'll implement a balanced binary tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers. This structure:
1. Uses 255 2-to-1 muxes arranged in 8 levels (log2(256))
2. Each level halves the number of signals until we get the final output
3. The select bits are used at different levels of the tree (LSB at leaf level, MSB at root)
This approach provides better timing characteristics (O(log n) delay) and more balanced fan-out. The tree structure is particularly efficient for large multiplexers in FPGAs where 2-to-1 muxes are primitive elements.
