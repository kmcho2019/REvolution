The optimal fusion strategy combines the simplicity of direct indexing with hierarchical optimization potential:

1. Maintain the clean, readable direct indexing approach (in[sel]) as the primary implementation
2. Add synthesis optimization hints through careful structuring:
   - Use a packed array input for clarity
   - Explicit wire declaration for outputs
   - Let synthesis tool infer optimal hierarchical structure
3. Benefits:
   - Clear intent like Example 1's direct approach
   - Allows synthesis tool to implement hierarchical optimization like Example 2 when beneficial
   - Single assignment maintains simplicity while giving synthesis freedom
   - Better PPA potential through tool-driven optimization

This approach gives us the best of both:
- Readability and maintainability of direct indexing
- Performance optimization potential of hierarchical structure
- Flexibility for synthesis tools to choose optimal implementation
- No unnecessary complexity in RTL
