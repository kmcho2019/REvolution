Here's a novel hierarchical approach using a two-level multiplexer structure:
1. First level: Divide the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 bits each
2. Use the upper 4 bits of sel to select one of the 16 groups
3. Second level: Use the lower 4 bits to select one bit from the chosen 16-bit group
4. This reduces the fanout compared to a flat 256-to-1 mux and may offer better PPA characteristics
5. The implementation uses intermediate 16-to-1 muxes and one final 16-to-1 mux
