To optimize the PPA metrics, particularly power and area, I'll implement a hierarchical 2-level multiplexer structure. This approach:
1. Divides the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 bits each (first level)
2. Uses the upper 4 bits of sel to select one of these 16 groups
3. Uses the lower 4 bits to select the final bit from the chosen group
This reduces the complexity from one large 256:1 mux to smaller 16:1 muxes, which typically have better PPA characteristics in standard cell libraries.

The improvement strategy focuses on:
- Reducing power by minimizing the active selection logic at any time
- Decreasing area through hierarchical structure and shared intermediate logic
- Maintaining good timing through balanced selection paths
