To optimize the PPA metrics further, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Timing Optimization**:
   - Use a 4-level hierarchical structure (4x4x4x4) instead of 2-level (16x16) to reduce the critical path
   - Each level will have 4-to-1 muxes, which typically have better timing characteristics in standard cell libraries

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - The 4-to-1 mux structure may reduce overall area as it better matches common standard cell sizes
   - Fewer intermediate wires compared to the 16-to-1 approach

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - Smaller muxes at each level reduce switching capacitance
   - Hierarchical structure naturally limits signal propagation to only the active path

The new structure will be:
- Level 1: 64 4-to-1 muxes (selecting from 4-bit chunks)
- Level 2: 16 4-to-1 muxes (selecting from Level 1 outputs)
- Level 3: 4 4-to-1 muxes (selecting from Level 2 outputs)
- Level 4: 1 4-to-1 mux (final output)
