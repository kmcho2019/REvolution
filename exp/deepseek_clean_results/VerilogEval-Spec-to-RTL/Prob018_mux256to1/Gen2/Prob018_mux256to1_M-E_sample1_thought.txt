Instead of a single large 256-to-1 mux, we'll implement a two-level hierarchical structure:
1. First level: 16x 16-to-1 muxes (each selecting one bit from a 16-bit chunk)
2. Second level: 1x 16-to-1 mux (selecting from the first level outputs)
This reduces the maximum fan-in from 256 to 16, improving timing. The 8-bit sel is split into:
- Upper 4 bits for the second level selection
- Lower 4 bits for each first level selection
This approach balances the load and reduces critical path delay.
