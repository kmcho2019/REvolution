This implementation uses a LUT-based approach with hierarchical selection:
1. Pre-decode the 8-bit selector into 256 individual enable signals (one-hot)
2. Use a large AND-OR structure where each input is ANDed with its corresponding enable
3. OR all the AND outputs together to get the final result
4. Benefits:
   - Single-level selection logic after pre-decoding (potentially better timing)
   - Parallel evaluation of all paths (no series multiplexer delays)
   - Simple structure that maps well to FPGA LUTs or standard cell libraries
   - Easily pipelinable if needed
5. The one-hot decoder is implemented using a shift operation
