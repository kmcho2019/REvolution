My novel approach uses a hierarchical tree structure to implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer, which offers better PPA characteristics:

1. Divide the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 inputs each (16x16=256)
2. First stage: 16 parallel 16-to-1 muxes (each selecting one bit from their group)
3. Second stage: 1 final 16-to-1 mux that selects between the outputs of the first stage
4. Use the upper 4 bits of sel to choose between groups (second stage)
5. Use the lower 4 bits of sel to choose within each group (first stage)

This approach:
- Reduces critical path by breaking the large mux into two smaller stages
- Potentially saves power by only activating one 16-to-1 mux in each stage
- May reduce area through better resource sharing
- Maintains the same functionality while being more scalable
- Can be easily pipelined if needed
