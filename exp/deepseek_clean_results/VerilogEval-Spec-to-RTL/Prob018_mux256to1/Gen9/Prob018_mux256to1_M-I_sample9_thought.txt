To optimize the 256:1 multiplexer for better PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I propose a hierarchical approach that breaks down the large mux into smaller, more manageable chunks. This strategy:

1. Divides the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 inputs each (16x16=256)
2. Uses a first-level 16:1 mux to select between the groups
3. Uses a second-level 16:1 mux to select the final bit within the selected group
4. Uses the upper 4 bits of sel for group selection and lower 4 bits for bit selection
5. Potentially improves timing by reducing fanout and critical path
6. May reduce area through better structure optimization
7. Maintains the same functionality while potentially improving PPA
