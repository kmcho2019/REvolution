To optimize the PPA metrics, I'll implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure that:
1. Breaks down the 256:1 mux into two levels of 16:1 muxes (16x16=256)
2. First level: 16 parallel 16:1 muxes, each selecting one bit from a 16-bit chunk
3. Second level: 1 final 16:1 mux to select between the first level outputs
4. Uses the upper 4 bits of sel for the second level and lower 4 bits for first level
5. Benefits:
   - Reduces critical path by splitting into smaller muxes
   - Potentially lower power as only portions of the circuit are active
   - More regular structure may lead to better area optimization
   - Maintains full functionality while improving timing
