1. From Example 1: The hierarchical multiplexer structure provides good timing characteristics by breaking down the large selection into smaller stages. However, it activates all first-stage muxes unnecessarily.

Fusion Strategy:

- It balances the fanout better than 2-stage approaches
- The stages get progressively smaller (64x8-to-1, 8x8-to-1, 1x4-to-1)
- One-hot encoding reduces switching activity in unused paths
