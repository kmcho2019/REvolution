Here's a novel hierarchical approach that reduces fan-in and improves timing:
1. Split the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 bits each (16x16=256)
2. First stage: Use upper 4 bits of sel to select one 16-bit group
3. Second stage: Use lower 4 bits to select 1 bit from the chosen group
4. This creates a balanced two-level multiplexer structure
5. The design reduces critical path by breaking the large 256:1 mux into smaller 16:1 muxes
6. Power is reduced since only one 16-bit group is active at any time
7. Area may increase slightly but with better timing characteristics
