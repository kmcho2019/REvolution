Here's a novel approach using a tree of XOR gates implemented through a generate block. This method:
1. Uses a hierarchical XOR structure that mimics how parity might be computed in hardware
2. Breaks down the 8-bit input into pairs and computes intermediate parity bits
3. Combines these intermediate results in stages
4. Offers better visibility into the computation steps
5. Could potentially offer better timing in some synthesis scenarios due to balanced paths
6. Makes the computation more explicit while maintaining the same functionality
