Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure with intermediate registers. This implementation:
1. Uses a 2-stage pipeline to break the XOR chain
2. Computes parity in two phases (lower and upper nibbles)
3. Stores intermediate results in registers
4. Final XOR combines the nibble parities

Advantages:
- Potentially better timing in high-frequency designs due to pipeline
- Clear separation of nibble computations
- Could be extended to wider data paths
- Demonstrates an alternative structural approach
