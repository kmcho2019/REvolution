Instead of using a single reduction XOR, we can implement a balanced XOR tree. This approach:
1. Breaks down the 8-bit XOR into smaller 2-bit XOR operations
2. Creates a balanced binary tree structure
3. May offer better timing characteristics in some technologies due to balanced paths
4. Makes the implementation more explicit and potentially more scalable for wider inputs
5. Could be more power-efficient in certain scenarios by localizing switching activity
