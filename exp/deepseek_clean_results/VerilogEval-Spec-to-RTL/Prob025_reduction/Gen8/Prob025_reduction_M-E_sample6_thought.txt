Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure that processes bits in pairs. The idea is to:
1. First XOR adjacent bit pairs (0-1, 2-3, etc.)
2. Then XOR the results of those operations
3. Continue this hierarchical reduction until we get the final parity bit

This approach demonstrates an alternative implementation that:
- Shows how XOR operations can be structured differently
- Might have different synthesis characteristics in some technologies
- Makes the reduction operation more explicit and visible in the code
- Could potentially have better routing characteristics in certain physical layouts
