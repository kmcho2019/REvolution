The current solution is already optimally simple for calculating even parity. The reduction XOR operator (^) is the most concise and efficient way to implement this functionality in Verilog. No further simplification is possible while maintaining the correct functionality, as:
1. It's a single continuous assignment
2. Uses the minimal built-in operator for the task
3. Has zero timing violations and minimal area/power
4. Directly expresses the parity calculation without any unnecessary constructs
