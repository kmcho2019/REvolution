Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR structure with intermediate registers. While less efficient in area and power than the direct XOR reduction, this implementation demonstrates an alternative pipelined architecture that could be beneficial in high-frequency systems where timing closure is challenging. The design breaks the 8-bit XOR into two stages of 4-bit XORs, with optional pipelining registers between stages.

Key features:
1. Two-stage XOR computation (4 bits each stage)
2. Optional pipeline registers (commented out by default)
3. Parameterized width for flexibility
4. More verbose but illustrates an alternative structural approach

This approach trades off area/power for potential timing benefits in high-frequency designs, though for this simple case the direct XOR reduction remains superior.
