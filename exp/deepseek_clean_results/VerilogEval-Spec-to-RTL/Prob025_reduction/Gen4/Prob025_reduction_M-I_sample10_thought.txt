The feedback suggests the LUT approach is inefficient for PPA metrics. We can optimize by:
1. Using a simple XOR reduction operation instead of a LUT
2. This eliminates the need for 256-bit storage and initialization
3. The XOR tree implementation will use fewer gates (7 XOR gates for 8 bits)
4. Maintains the same timing characteristics (single-level XOR operation)
5. Reduces both area and power consumption significantly
6. The solution remains functionally identical (even parity calculation)
