Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree with intermediate registers. This design:
1. Uses a pipelined XOR reduction for better timing closure
2. Divides the 8-bit input into two 4-bit groups
3. Computes parity for each group in parallel
4. Registers intermediate results to break critical path
5. Final XOR combines the two group parities
This approach provides:
- Better timing characteristics for high-frequency designs
- Balanced logic depth
- Potential power savings through reduced glitching
- Scalability to wider inputs
