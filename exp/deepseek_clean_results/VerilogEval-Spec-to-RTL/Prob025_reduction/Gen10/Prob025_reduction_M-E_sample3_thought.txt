Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure:
1. Instead of a single XOR operation, break it into two levels
2. First compute XOR of lower and upper nibbles separately
3. Then XOR these intermediate results
4. This mimics how a hardware XOR tree might be physically laid out
5. Theoretically could have better timing in some technologies due to balanced paths
6. Still maintains the same functionality while providing a different implementation
