The existing solution is already optimal in terms of PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics:
1. Performance: The reduction XOR operation has zero timing violations (tns/wns = 0.0)
2. Power: Extremely low at 0.000941W
3. Area: Minimal at 11.0 um²

The implementation cannot be improved further at the RTL level because:
1. The reduction XOR is the most direct implementation of even parity
2. It maps perfectly to hardware XOR trees
3. Any alternative implementation would either:
   - Increase area (e.g., using sequential logic)
   - Degrade performance (e.g., adding pipeline stages)
   - Increase power (e.g., using more gates)

Therefore, the optimal solution is to maintain the current implementation.
