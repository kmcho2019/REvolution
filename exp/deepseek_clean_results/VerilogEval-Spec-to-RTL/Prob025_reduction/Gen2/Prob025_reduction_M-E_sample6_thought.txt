Here's an alternative approach using a tree structure of XOR gates to compute the even parity bit. While functionally equivalent to the reduction operator, this implementation explicitly shows the hierarchical XOR computation:
1. First level XORs pairs of bits (bit0⊕bit1, bit2⊕bit3, etc.)
2. Second level XORs the results from first level
3. Final XOR combines all intermediate results
This method demonstrates how parity calculation can be parallelized and is more scalable for wider buses.
