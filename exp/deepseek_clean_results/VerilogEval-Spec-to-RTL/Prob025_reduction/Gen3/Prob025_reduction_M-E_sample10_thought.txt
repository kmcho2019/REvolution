Instead of using a single XOR reduction operation, we can implement a balanced XOR tree structure. This approach:
1. Breaks down the 8-bit XOR into smaller 2-bit XOR operations
2. Creates a balanced binary tree of XOR gates
3. Potentially improves timing in some technologies by reducing fan-out
4. Makes the critical path more predictable (log2(N) levels)
5. Could be more power-efficient in certain scenarios due to reduced glitching

The tree structure first XORs bits in pairs, then combines results until we get the final parity bit. This is functionally equivalent but structurally different from the reduction operator approach.
