Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure:
1. Instead of a single XOR reduction, break it down into stages
2. First XOR pairs of bits (4 XOR operations)
3. Then XOR the results in pairs (2 XOR operations)
4. Finally XOR the last two results (1 XOR operation)
5. This mimics how a physical XOR tree might be implemented in hardware
6. The approach demonstrates pipelining potential and better visualization of the XOR propagation
7. While functionally identical, this shows an alternative implementation strategy
