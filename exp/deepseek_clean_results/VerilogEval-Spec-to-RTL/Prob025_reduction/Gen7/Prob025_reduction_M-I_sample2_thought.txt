The feedback suggests the LUT approach is inefficient for PPA metrics. We can optimize by:
1. Using a simple XOR reduction operation instead of a LUT
2. This eliminates the need for 256-bit storage and initialization
3. The XOR tree approach will:
   - Reduce area significantly (only 7 XOR gates needed)
   - Lower power consumption (no LUT access)
   - Maintain the same timing characteristics (still just one level of logic)
4. The implementation becomes simpler and more efficient while maintaining functionality
