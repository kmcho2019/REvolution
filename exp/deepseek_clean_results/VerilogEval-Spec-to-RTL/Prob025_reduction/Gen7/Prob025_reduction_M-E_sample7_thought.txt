Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure:
1. Instead of a single reduction XOR, we'll build the parity bit through a binary tree of XOR operations
2. First XOR pairs of bits, then XOR the results in successive stages
3. This mimics how a carry-lookahead adder structures its logic
4. While functionally identical to the reduction XOR, this approach:
   - Makes the timing path more balanced
   - Could be more layout-friendly for physical design
   - Explicitly shows the gate-level structure
   - May have better characteristics for certain technologies
5. The 8-bit input is processed in 3 stages (log2(8))
