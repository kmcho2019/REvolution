Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure with pipelining:
1. Instead of a flat XOR reduction or LUT, we'll use a 3-stage pipeline
2. First stage: XOR pairs of bits (4 parallel XORs on bits [1:0], [3:2], [5:4], [7:6])
3. Second stage: XOR the results from first stage (2 parallel XORs)
4. Final stage: XOR the last two results to get the parity bit
5. This approach provides better timing characteristics for high-frequency designs
6. The pipelined structure allows for higher clock frequencies at the cost of latency
7. Each stage has balanced logic depth, improving timing predictability
