An alternative approach to compute even parity is to use a hierarchical XOR tree structure. Instead of using the reduction operator directly, we can break down the XOR operations into multiple stages. This approach demonstrates how the parity calculation can be implemented using a more explicit, structural method that might be useful in educational contexts or when targeting specific hardware architectures. The 8-bit input is divided into pairs, each pair is XORed in the first stage, then the results are combined in subsequent stages until we get the final parity bit.
