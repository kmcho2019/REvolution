Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical XOR tree structure:
1. Instead of using the reduction operator, we'll explicitly build a balanced XOR tree
2. First stage: XOR pairs of bits (bit0^bit1, bit2^bit3, etc.)
3. Second stage: XOR the results from first stage
4. Final stage: Combine intermediate results
5. This approach demonstrates how the reduction operator would be implemented in hardware
6. Benefits include better visibility of the logic structure and potential for pipelining in more complex designs
