The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 in all cases except when a=0 AND b=0 AND c=0. This can be directly implemented using a simple OR operation between 'a' and the AND of 'b' and 'c'. 

Key observations:
1. When a=1, output is always 1 (matches OR behavior)
2. When a=0, output is 1 if either b or c is 1 (which is equivalent to (b & c) being 1)
3. The only case where output is 0 is when all three inputs are 0

This simplifies to: out = a | (b & c)
This implementation uses just one AND and one OR gate, which is more efficient than the previous mux-based solution.
