The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 in all cases except when a=0, b=0, and c=0. This can be directly implemented with a simple OR logic: the output should be 0 only when all inputs are 0, otherwise it should be 1. This reduces to a NOR gate followed by an inverter, or equivalently, a 3-input OR gate. The simplest implementation is to use a continuous assignment with a logical OR of all inputs.
