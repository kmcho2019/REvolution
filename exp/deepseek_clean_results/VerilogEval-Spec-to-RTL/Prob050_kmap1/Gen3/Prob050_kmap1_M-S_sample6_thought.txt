The Karnaugh map shows the output is 1 in all cases except when a=0, b=0, and c=0. This is equivalent to a 3-input OR operation. We can implement this directly with a single OR gate, which is the most straightforward and efficient implementation. This will:
1. Use minimal logic (just one OR operation)
2. Eliminate unnecessary inverters
3. Maintain the same functionality
4. Potentially improve power and area metrics
