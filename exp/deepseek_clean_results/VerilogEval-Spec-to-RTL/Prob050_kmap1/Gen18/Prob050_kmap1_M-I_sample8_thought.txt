The original solution correctly implements the Karnaugh map but can be optimized for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area). Here's the improved strategy:

1. **Boolean Optimization**: 
   - From the Karnaugh map, we can see the output is 1 for all cases except when a=0, b=0, c=0
   - This can be expressed as: out = a | b | c
   - This is simpler than the original case statement approach

2. **PPA Benefits**:
   - **Performance**: Direct OR operation is faster than a lookup table
   - **Power**: Fewer gates switching means lower dynamic power
   - **Area**: Single OR gate is smaller than a 3-bit address decoder and lookup table

3. **Implementation**:
   - Replace the case statement with a simple OR operation
   - Remove unnecessary registers and wiring
   - Maintain same functionality with minimal logic
