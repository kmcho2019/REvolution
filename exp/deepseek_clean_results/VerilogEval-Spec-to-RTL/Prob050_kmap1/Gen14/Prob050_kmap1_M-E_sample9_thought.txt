An alternative approach is to recognize that the output is 1 when either:
1. a is 1 (right half of K-map), or
2. b and c are not both 0 (all cases except bc=00)

This can be implemented using a combination of AND and OR gates. Specifically, we can create a signal that is 1 when b or c is 1 (using OR), and then OR that with a. This is logically equivalent but demonstrates a different implementation approach than the simple 3-input OR.
