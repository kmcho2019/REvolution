The original solution correctly implements the Karnaugh map's logic, but we can potentially optimize it slightly for power and area by removing the explicit parentheses in the OR operation. Since OR operations are associative, we can write it as a single OR of all three inputs. This might allow the synthesis tool to potentially use a single 3-input OR gate instead of two 2-input OR gates, though in practice most synthesis tools would optimize this automatically.
