The current implementation is already optimal in terms of functionality and PPA metrics. The OR operation (a | b | c) perfectly matches the Karnaugh map requirements with:
- Minimal timing (single gate delay)
- Minimal area (single 3-input OR gate)
- Reasonable power consumption

1. Be functionally equivalent but more complex (no benefit)
2. Potentially increase area by using more gates
3. Increase power consumption through additional switching
