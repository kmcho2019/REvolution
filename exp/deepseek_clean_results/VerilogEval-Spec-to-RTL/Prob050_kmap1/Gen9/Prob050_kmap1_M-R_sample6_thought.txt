Refactoring Approach:
1. The Karnaugh map shows the output is 1 in all cases except when a=0 and bc=00
2. This is equivalent to (a OR (b OR c)) which can be simplified to (a OR b OR c)
3. Instead of nested OR operations, we'll use a single continuous assignment with all inputs ORed together
4. This makes the implementation more straightforward and easier to read while maintaining identical functionality
5. The single-level OR operation might also have slight timing advantages in synthesis
