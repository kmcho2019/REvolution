The Karnaugh map shows that the output 'out' is 1 in all cases except when a=0, b=0, and c=0. This can be expressed as the logical OR of a with the AND of b and c (a + (b & c)). However, looking at the K-map more carefully, we can see the output is 1 in all cases except when all inputs are 0. Therefore, the simplest expression is out = a | b | c.
