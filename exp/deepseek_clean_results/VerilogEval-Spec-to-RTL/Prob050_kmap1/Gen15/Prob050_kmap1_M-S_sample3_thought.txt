The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 in all cases except when all inputs (a, b, c) are 0. This can be implemented most simply as a 3-input NOR gate followed by an inverter, or more directly as an OR of all inputs. The simplest implementation is just OR-ing all three inputs together, since the output is 1 whenever any input is 1, and 0 only when all inputs are 0.
