The current implementation is already optimal in terms of PPA metrics, but we can make one minor improvement by flattening the OR operations into a single level. This will:

1. Reduce the logic depth from 2 to 1 (single gate delay)
2. Potentially save a tiny amount of area by using one 3-input OR gate instead of two 2-input OR gates
3. Maintain exactly the same functionality as the original K-map specification
4. Slightly improve timing by eliminating one gate delay
5. Not affect power consumption since the switching activity remains identical

The improvement is marginal but follows good design practice of minimizing logic depth where possible.
