The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 0 only when all inputs are 0 (a=0, b=0, c=0), and 1 in all other cases. This is exactly the behavior of a 3-input OR gate. The minimal implementation is already achieved with a single OR operation of all inputs. No further simplification is possible while maintaining correct functionality since we cannot reduce the number of terms or operations without changing the output behavior.
