The current implementation is correct but can be optimized for power by reducing unnecessary switching activity. The key observation is that the output is 1 in all cases except when a=0 AND b=0 AND c=0. Therefore, we can:
1. Directly detect the single case where output should be 0 (a=0, b=0, c=0)
2. Use this detection to drive the output, with all other cases defaulting to 1
3. This eliminates the intermediate bc_zero signal and reduces switching activity

This approach uses a single AND-NOR structure (equivalent to detecting ~a & ~b & ~c) followed by an OR with 1, which is more power-efficient as it only toggles when in the critical case.
