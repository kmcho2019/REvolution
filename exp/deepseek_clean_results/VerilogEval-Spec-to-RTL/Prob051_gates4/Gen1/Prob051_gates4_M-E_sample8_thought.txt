Instead of using reduction operators, we can build the 4-input gates hierarchically from 2-input gates. This approach:
1. Creates out_and by cascading three 2-input AND gates
2. Creates out_or by cascading three 2-input OR gates  
3. Creates out_xor by cascading three 2-input XOR gates
This demonstrates how complex gates can be built from simpler components, which might be useful in certain technology mappings or educational contexts.
