3. Maintains identical functionality while showing a different structural approach

- Compute each output by explicitly ANDing/ORing/XORing all input bits
- Show the same timing characteristics (0ns violations)
- Consume slightly more area due to explicit gate construction

- Serve as an educational example of alternative implementations
