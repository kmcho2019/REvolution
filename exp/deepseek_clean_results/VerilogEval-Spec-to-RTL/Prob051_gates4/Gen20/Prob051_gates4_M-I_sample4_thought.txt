To optimize the PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics, we should:
1. Use Verilog reduction operators which synthesize more efficiently to standard cells
2. Eliminate intermediate wires to reduce switching power
3. Simplify the logic to a single level of operation for better timing
4. Maintain the exact same functionality while reducing area and power

The improved implementation will:
- Use &, |, and ^ reduction operators for direct 4-input operations
- Remove all intermediate wires and gates
- Result in a more compact and power-efficient design
- Potentially improve timing by reducing logic levels
