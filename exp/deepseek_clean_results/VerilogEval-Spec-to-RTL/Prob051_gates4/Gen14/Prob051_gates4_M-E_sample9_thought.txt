Instead of using reduction operators or chained bitwise operations, we'll implement the 4-input gates by structurally instantiating primitive logic gates in a tree structure. This approach:

1. Provides more control over the gate-level implementation
2. Creates a balanced tree structure for potentially better timing
3. Makes the gate-level structure explicit in the RTL
4. Could be more power-efficient for certain technologies
5. Allows easier technology mapping for specific libraries

The implementation uses:
- Two 2-input AND gates feeding into a final AND for the 4-input AND
- Two 2-input OR gates feeding into a final OR for the 4-input OR
- Two 2-input XOR gates feeding into a final XOR for the 4-input XOR
