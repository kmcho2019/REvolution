Instead of using reduction operators, we can implement the logic gates using a hierarchical approach with 2-input gates. This demonstrates how complex gates can be built from simpler components. For the 4-input operations:
1. out_and: Chain three 2-input AND gates
2. out_or: Chain three 2-input OR gates  
3. out_xor: Chain three 2-input XOR gates
This approach shows the fundamental building blocks of digital logic while achieving the same functionality.
