The optimal fusion strategy combines the clarity of explicit bitwise operations from Example 1 with the conciseness of reduction operators from Example 2. Here's the rationale:

1. **Readability vs. Conciseness**: While reduction operators (&in, |in, ^in) are concise, explicit bitwise operations (in[3]&in[2]...) make the 4-input nature immediately visible to readers.

2. **PPA Considerations**: Both approaches synthesize to identical hardware since reduction operators are just syntactic sugar for bitwise operations. The PPA metrics will be equivalent.

3. **Maintenance**: Explicit bitwise operations make it easier to modify individual bit connections if needed later.

4. **Documentation**: Adding brief comments improves maintainability without affecting PPA.

The fused solution uses:
- Explicit bitwise operations for clarity
- Separate assign statements for each output
- Minimal comments for documentation
- Same optimal PPA characteristics as both examples
