The optimal solution combines the conciseness of reduction operators with the balanced tree structure concept. Here's the fusion strategy:

1. For AND and OR operations, use reduction operators because:
   - They are the most concise and readable in Verilog
   - They synthesize efficiently to standard cells
   - They clearly express the intended functionality

2. For XOR operation, maintain the tree structure because:
   - XOR trees typically have better timing characteristics than long XOR chains
   - Some technology libraries implement XOR more efficiently as trees
   - The balanced structure ensures equal path delays

3. This hybrid approach gives us:
   - Minimal and clear code for AND/OR operations
   - Better XOR implementation for timing and technology portability
   - Overall optimal PPA characteristics
