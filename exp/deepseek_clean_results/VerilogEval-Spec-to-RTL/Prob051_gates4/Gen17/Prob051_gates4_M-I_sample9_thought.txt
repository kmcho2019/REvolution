1. Keep the efficient reduction operators (&, |, ^) which provide the best implementation for multi-input gates
2. Add parameterization to make the bit-width configurable for better reusability
3. Include more detailed comments explaining the reduction operators
4. Maintain the same direct assignment structure for zero-delay paths
5. The parameterized version won't affect PPA when instantiated with WIDTH=4, but adds flexibility
