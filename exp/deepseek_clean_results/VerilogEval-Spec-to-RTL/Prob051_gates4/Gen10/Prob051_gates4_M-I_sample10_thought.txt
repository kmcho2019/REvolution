The current implementation is already quite optimal, but we can make a few minor improvements:

1. **XOR Implementation**:
   - Replace the explicit tree structure with a reduction operator (^in) since:
     * Modern synthesis tools can optimize this perfectly
     * It's more concise and readable
     * May yield slightly better power/area with same timing

2. **Maintain Other Operations**:
   - Keep the reduction operators for AND and OR as they're already optimal
   - No benefit in changing these implementations

3. **Additional Optimizations**:
   - Remove intermediate wires since they're not needed with reduction operator
   - Keep the purely combinational style
   - Maintain the same interface and functionality

This change maintains the same timing characteristics (still 2 gate delays for XOR) while potentially improving power and area slightly through simpler code that the synthesizer can optimize more effectively.
