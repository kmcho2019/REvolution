1. Break down the 4-input operations into pairs of 2-input operations
2. Compute intermediate results for each pair
3. Combine the intermediate results to get final outputs
4. This demonstrates a tree-like computation structure that could be beneficial in certain physical layouts

The advantages of this approach:
- Shows an alternative implementation strategy
- Could potentially offer better physical placement in some technologies
- Demonstrates hierarchical thinking for more complex circuits
