The optimal solution combines the conciseness of reduction operators (from Example 2) with the balanced tree structure concept (from Example 1). Here's the fusion strategy:

1. Use reduction operators for AND and OR operations since they are:
   - Most concise in Verilog
   - Synthesize efficiently to standard cells
   - Have clear semantics

2. For XOR operation, maintain the tree structure because:
   - XOR trees have better timing characteristics than long XOR chains
   - Some technologies implement XOR more efficiently as trees
   - Maintains balance in critical paths

3. This hybrid approach gives us:
   - Minimal code for AND/OR (like Example 2)
   - Better XOR implementation (like Example 1)
   - Overall optimal PPA characteristics
