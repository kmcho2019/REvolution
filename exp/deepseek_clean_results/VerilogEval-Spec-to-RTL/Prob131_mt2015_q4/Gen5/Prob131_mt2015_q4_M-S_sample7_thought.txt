To simplify the design while maintaining functionality:
1. Recognize that ModuleB's truth table can be expressed as z = x & y (same as AND gate)
2. Since ModuleA implements z = (x^y) & x, which simplifies to x & ~y
3. Remove redundant module instantiations since all submodules share identical inputs
4. Directly implement the logic using simplified Boolean expressions:
   - OR path: (x & ~y) | (x & y) = x
   - AND path: (x & ~y) & (x & y) = 0
   - Final XOR: x ^ 0 = x
5. The entire circuit simplifies to just outputting input x
