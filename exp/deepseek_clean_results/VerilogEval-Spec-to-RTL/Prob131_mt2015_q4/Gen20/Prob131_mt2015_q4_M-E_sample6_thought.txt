Novel Architectural Idea: 
1. Temporal Pipeline with Phase-Shifted Evaluation:
   - Instead of evaluating all modules simultaneously, stagger their computation across clock phases
   - Uses a 2-phase clock system to evaluate ModuleA and ModuleB in alternating phases
   - Reduces peak current draw by spreading computations over time

2. Dynamic Reconfiguration via Shared Logic:
   - Implement both functions using a shared XOR core with configurable output conditioning
   - The same hardware computes both functions at different times through dynamic reconfiguration

3. Output Composition with Wave-Pipelining:
   - Use the natural propagation delays through gates to create a wave-pipelined output stage
   - The OR and AND operations happen naturally through signal timing rather than explicit gates

Key Benefits:
- Lower simultaneous switching noise due to phase-shifted computation
- Hardware reuse through dynamic reconfiguration
- Reduced peak power consumption
- Maintains combinational behavior while introducing temporal aspects
- Novel application of wave-pipelining to combinational logic composition
