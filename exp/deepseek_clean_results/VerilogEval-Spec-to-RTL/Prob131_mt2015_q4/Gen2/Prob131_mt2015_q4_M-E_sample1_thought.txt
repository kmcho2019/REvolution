Here's a novel architectural approach that leverages time-multiplexing and sequential logic to reduce hardware resources while maintaining functionality:

1. **Time-Multiplexed Computation**:
   - Instead of instantiating four parallel modules (2xA + 2xB), use a single instance of each module and time-multiplex their outputs
   - Add a simple 2-bit counter to control the multiplexing sequence

2. **Pipelined Processing**:
   - Process ModuleA and ModuleB results sequentially in two clock cycles
   - Store intermediate results in registers

3. **Final Logic**:
   - Combine the stored results in the third cycle using the required OR/AND/XOR operations
   - This reduces area by sharing hardware across time rather than space

Key Benefits:
- Significant area reduction (only 1xA + 1xB instead of 2x each)
- Maintains correct functionality through proper sequencing
- Introduces pipelining which could be beneficial in some applications
- Power reduction due to fewer switching elements
