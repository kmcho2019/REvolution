Refactoring Approach:
1. Logic Consolidation:
   - Instead of using separate modules for A and B, implement their logic directly in the top module using continuous assignments
   - This eliminates module instantiation overhead while maintaining the same functionality

2. Expression Simplification:
   - Recognize that the final XOR operation between a_out and b_out can be expressed directly in terms of x and y
   - The original structure (OR/AND/XOR) is mathematically equivalent to a simple XOR of the two intermediate results

3. Direct Implementation:
   - Compute ModuleA's output (x & ~y) as a wire
   - Compute ModuleB's output (~(x ^ y)) as another wire
   - Final output is the XOR of these two wires

4. Benefits:
   - Reduced hierarchy (no submodules)
   - Same logical behavior
   - Potentially better synthesis results due to flatter structure
   - Maintains readability through clear signal naming
