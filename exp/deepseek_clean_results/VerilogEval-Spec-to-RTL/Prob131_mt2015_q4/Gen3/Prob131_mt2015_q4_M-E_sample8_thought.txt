Alternative architecture idea:
1. Instead of separate OR and AND gates, combine the logic into a single stage
2. Use the observation that ModuleB's truth table matches x AND y (from the waveform)
3. Simplify ModuleB implementation using direct AND operation
4. Merge the logic operations by recognizing that:
   - OR of A1 and B1 outputs = ((x^y)&x) | (x&y)
   - AND of A2 and B2 outputs = ((x^y)&x) & (x&y)
5. The XOR of these can be simplified using Boolean algebra to: x & (y | ~(x & y))
This reduces the logic depth and eliminates intermediate wires
