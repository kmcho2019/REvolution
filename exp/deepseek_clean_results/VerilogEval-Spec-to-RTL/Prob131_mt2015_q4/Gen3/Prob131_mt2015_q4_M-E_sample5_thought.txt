Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Logic Sharing: Observe that both ModuleA and ModuleB compute (x^y) as an intermediate result. We can compute this once and share it.
2. Output Remapping: Instead of instantiating duplicate modules, compute each unique output once and route as needed.
3. Direct Output Calculation: The final output z can be expressed as:
   z = [(x^y & x) | ~(x^y)] ^ [(x^y & x) & ~(x^y)]
   This simplifies to z = (x & ~y) ^ (x & y & ~x) which further reduces to z = x & ~y
4. Implementation: Single XOR for shared term, then direct logic for outputs, eliminating redundant computations.

Key Advantages:
- Eliminates all duplicate module instances
- Reduces gate count by sharing common sub-expressions
- Maintains same functionality with simpler implementation
- Potentially better timing due to reduced logic levels
