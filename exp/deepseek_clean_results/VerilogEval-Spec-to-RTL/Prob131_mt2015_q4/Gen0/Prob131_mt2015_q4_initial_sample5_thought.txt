To implement the top-level module, we need to:
1. Instantiate two Module A instances (A1, A2) and two Module B instances (B1, B2)
2. Connect all their first inputs to 'x' and second inputs to 'y'
3. Connect outputs of A1 and B1 to an OR gate
4. Connect outputs of A2 and B2 to an AND gate
5. Connect the outputs of the OR and AND gates to an XOR gate for final output 'z'
