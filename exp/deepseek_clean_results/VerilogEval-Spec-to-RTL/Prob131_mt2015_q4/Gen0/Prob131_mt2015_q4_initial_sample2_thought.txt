To implement the top-level module, we need to:
1. Instantiate two Module A instances (A1 and A2) and two Module B instances (B1 and B2)
2. Connect all their first inputs to 'x' and second inputs to 'y'
3. Connect A1 and B1 outputs to an OR gate
4. Connect A2 and B2 outputs to an AND gate
5. Connect the outputs of OR and AND gates to an XOR gate which produces final output 'z'
