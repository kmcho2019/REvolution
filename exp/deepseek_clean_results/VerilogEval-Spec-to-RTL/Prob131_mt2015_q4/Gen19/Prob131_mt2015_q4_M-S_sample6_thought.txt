1. Boolean Analysis:

   - ModuleB: Truth table matches XNOR (same as original)

2. Key Insight:
   - Since all submodules have identical inputs, we can compute each function once
   - OR and AND operations on identical values are redundant:
     * (A OR B) XOR (A AND B) simplifies to A XOR B
   - Therefore, the entire circuit reduces to computing ModuleA XOR ModuleB

3. Final Optimization:
   - Directly compute (x&~y) XOR ~(x^y)
   - No need for separate OR/AND/XOR stages
   - Single expression gives correct output

Benefits:
- Eliminates intermediate gates (OR, AND)
- Reduces logic depth
- Maintains exact functionality
- Minimal implementation (2 operations)
