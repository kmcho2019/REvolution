Novel Time-Multiplexed Architecture:
1. Key Insight:
   - Since both ModuleA and ModuleB instances receive identical inputs, we can compute their outputs sequentially in alternating clock cycles
   - This reduces hardware by 50% while maintaining functionality through temporal reuse

2. Implementation Strategy:
   - Use a clock divider to create two-phase operation
   - Phase 1: Compute ModuleA outputs and store in registers
   - Phase 2: Compute ModuleB outputs and combine with stored ModuleA results
   - Final XOR operates on the combined results from both phases

3. Benefits:
   - 50% reduction in combinational logic area
   - Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
   - Maintains same logical function with minimal latency penalty
   - Scalable approach for larger systems with repeated modules
