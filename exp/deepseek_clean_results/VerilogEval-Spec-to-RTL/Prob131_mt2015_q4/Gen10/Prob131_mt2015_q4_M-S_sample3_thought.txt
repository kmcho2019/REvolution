To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality:
1. Recognize that Module A's output (x & ~y) and Module B's output (x ~^ y) can be directly computed
2. The top-level logic consists of:
   - OR between (x & ~y) and (x ~^ y)
   - AND between another (x & ~y) and (x ~^ y)
   - XOR between these two results
3. Since all submodules process the same inputs, we can eliminate redundant instantiations
4. Compute the intermediate results once and reuse them in the final logic
5. This reduces the design to just the essential logic operations without redundant modules
