Novel Time-Multiplexed Architecture:
1. Instead of instantiating multiple parallel modules, use a single instance of each module type and time-multiplex their outputs
2. Use a clock divider to alternate between ModuleA and ModuleB computations
3. Store intermediate results in flip-flops to maintain correct timing
4. Combine results in the final cycle using sequential logic
5. Benefits:
   - Reduces module instances (area savings)
   - Potentially lowers dynamic power by reducing simultaneous switching
   - Maintains same logical function but spread over multiple cycles
   - Enables potential clock gating opportunities
