Novel Time-Multiplexed Architecture:
1. Instead of instantiating multiple modules, use a single instance of each module type and time-multiplex their outputs
2. Implement a simple state machine to alternate between computing ModuleA and ModuleB results
3. Store intermediate results in flip-flops to maintain correct timing
4. Use clock gating to reduce power consumption when modules aren't active
5. The final output is computed in a pipelined fashion to maintain throughput
6. This approach saves area by reusing logic blocks at the cost of slightly increased latency

Key advantages:
- Significant area reduction by sharing hardware resources
- Potential power savings through clock gating
- Maintains correct logical functionality
- Scalable to more complex operations
