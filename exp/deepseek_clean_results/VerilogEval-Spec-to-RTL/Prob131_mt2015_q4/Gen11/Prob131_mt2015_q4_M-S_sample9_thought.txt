The current solution can be significantly simplified by:
1. Recognizing that the entire logic can be expressed as a single boolean function without submodules
2. Observing that (a_out | b_out) ^ (a_out & b_out) is equivalent to a_out ^ b_out
3. Combining all logic into a single continuous assignment at the top level
4. Eliminating all intermediate modules and wires since they're not necessary

This reduces the design to its minimal form while maintaining identical functionality.
