Improvement Strategy:
1. Boolean Optimization:
   - Recognize that ModuleA's output (x&~y) and ModuleB's output (~(x^y)) can be combined directly
   - The final XOR operation (a_out ^ b_out) can be expanded and simplified

2. Logic Reduction:
   - (x&~y) ^ (~(x^y)) = (x&~y) ^ (~x&~y | x&y) = x&~y ^ ~x&~y | x&~y ^ x&y
   - This simplifies to (~y) | (x&y) = x|~y

3. Implementation Optimization:
   - Replace all submodules with a single direct implementation
   - Use only OR and NOT operations which typically have better PPA characteristics than XOR/XNOR
   - Eliminate intermediate wires and redundant logic

4. PPA Benefits:
   - Reduced gate count (from 5 gates to 2)
   - Eliminated power-hungry XOR/XNOR operations
   - Single-level logic for faster operation
   - Smaller area due to simpler gates

Key Improvements:
- Complete elimination of submodules
- Direct implementation using only OR and NOT
- 60% reduction in gate count
- Lower power consumption
- Potentially better timing due to reduced logic depth
