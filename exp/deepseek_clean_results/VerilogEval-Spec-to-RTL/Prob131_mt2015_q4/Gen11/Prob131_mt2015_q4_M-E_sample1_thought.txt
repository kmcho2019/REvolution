Novel Time-Multiplexed Architecture:
1. Instead of instantiating multiple parallel modules, use a single instance of each module type and time-multiplex their outputs
2. Add a simple 2-bit counter to cycle through the four required operations (A1, B1, A2, B2)
3. Use registers to store intermediate results and a multiplexer to select the final output
4. This reduces the combinatorial logic footprint by reusing the same modules across clock cycles
5. The design trades off some speed (now requires 4 clock cycles for full operation) for potential area savings

Key advantages:
- Significant area reduction by reusing module instances
- Lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity
- Scalable to more complex operations by increasing counter size
- Maintains same logical functionality over multiple clock cycles
