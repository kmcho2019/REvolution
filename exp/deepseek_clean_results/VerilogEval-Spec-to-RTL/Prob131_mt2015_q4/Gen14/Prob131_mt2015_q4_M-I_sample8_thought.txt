To further optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement these improvements:

1. **Logic Consolidation**:
   - Eliminate redundant submodules since all instances process the same inputs
   - Combine the functionality of ModuleA and ModuleB into direct expressions in the top module

2. **Expression Simplification**:
   - The final XOR operation can be merged with the previous logic
   - Use Boolean algebra to reduce the number of operations

3. **Resource Sharing**:
   - Compute common sub-expressions once and reuse them
   - Remove intermediate wires where possible

4. **Power Optimization**:
   - Reduce switching activity by minimizing the number of logic operations
   - Eliminate unnecessary hierarchy that adds to power consumption

The optimized design will maintain identical functionality while reducing area and power consumption.
