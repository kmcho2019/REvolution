Improvement Strategy:
1. Boolean Optimization:
   - Recognize that ModuleA (z = x & ~y) and ModuleB (z = ~(x ^ y)) are already minimal implementations
   - Final output z = a_out ^ b_out is mathematically equivalent to (a_out XOR b_out), which is optimal

2. Gate-Level Optimization:
   - Replace explicit ModuleA and ModuleB with direct logic expressions in TopModule
   - Eliminate intermediate wires and module instantiations to reduce hierarchy overhead
   - Use technology-independent primitives for maximum portability

3. Power Optimization:
   - Combine all logic into a single expression to minimize switching activity
   - Remove unnecessary hierarchy levels that could cause additional power consumption

4. Area Optimization:
   - The direct implementation uses only 3 basic gates (AND, NOT, XOR)
   - No further area reduction possible without changing functionality

5. Performance Optimization:
   - The critical path remains at 3 gate delays (AND/NOT → XOR)
   - Maintain same timing characteristics while simplifying structure

Key Improvements:
- Eliminates module hierarchy overhead
- Reduces wire transitions for power savings
- Maintains identical functionality with minimal logic
- Simplifies synthesis and place-and-route
