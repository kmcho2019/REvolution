Refactoring Strategy:
1. Module Consolidation:
   - Eliminate separate ModuleA and ModuleB by implementing their logic directly in the top module
   - This reduces hierarchical overhead while maintaining the same functionality

2. Expression Optimization:
   - Combine the XOR and AND operations into single continuous assignments
   - Use direct Boolean expressions instead of intermediate wires where possible

3. Structural Simplification:
   - Replace the submodule instances with direct logic implementations
   - Maintain the same logical flow but flatten the hierarchy

4. Power Optimization:
   - Minimize signal transitions by combining operations
   - Reduce gate count by eliminating unnecessary intermediate signals

Key Changes:
- Removed submodule hierarchy while preserving identical functionality
- Combined all logic into continuous assignments
- Maintained mathematical equivalence through Boolean algebra
- Reduced overall gate count by eliminating redundant structures
- Improved readability through direct expression of the logic
