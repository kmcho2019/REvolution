Simplification Strategy:
1. Direct Boolean Implementation:
   - Recognize that the entire system can be expressed as a single Boolean function
   - Eliminate submodules entirely since their outputs are immediately combined

2. Boolean Algebra Reduction:
   - ModuleA implements z = x & ~y
   - ModuleB implements z = ~(x ^ y) = x XNOR y
   - Final output is (ModuleA_out OR ModuleB_out) XOR (ModuleA_out AND ModuleB_out)
   - This simplifies to ModuleA_out XOR ModuleB_out through Boolean algebra

3. Final Expression:
   - Substitute the submodule functions into the final XOR
   - Result: z = (x & ~y) ^ (~(x ^ y))
   - Further simplification possible but maintaining clarity

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates all submodule hierarchy
- Single continuous assignment
- Fewer internal signals
- Mathematically equivalent to original
- More efficient for synthesis
