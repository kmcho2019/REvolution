Novel Time-Multiplexed Architecture:
1. Instead of parallel computation, use a sequential approach where we evaluate the function over two clock cycles
2. First cycle computes ModuleA's result and stores it in a register
3. Second cycle computes ModuleB's result and combines it with the stored ModuleA result
4. This reduces simultaneous switching activity by staggering computations
5. Uses a simple FSM to control the computation sequence
6. Output is registered for clean timing
7. Benefits:
   - Reduced peak power by spreading computation over time
   - Potential for clock gating during idle periods
   - Simpler physical implementation with fewer simultaneous signal transitions
   - Better EMI characteristics due to staggered switching
