Simplification Strategy:
1. Recognize that all ModuleA and ModuleB instances receive identical inputs, so their outputs will be identical. We only need one instance of each module.
2. The original logic (a1_out | b1_out) ^ (a2_out & b2_out) can be simplified since a1_out = a2_out and b1_out = b2_out.
3. The expression reduces to (a_out | b_out) ^ (a_out & b_out), which is equivalent to a_out ^ b_out (XOR operation).
4. Final implementation needs just one instance each of ModuleA and ModuleB, with their outputs XORed together.

Key Benefits:
- Eliminates redundant module instances
- Reduces power consumption by minimizing switching activity
- Maintains identical functionality
- Simplifies the design while keeping the modular structure
