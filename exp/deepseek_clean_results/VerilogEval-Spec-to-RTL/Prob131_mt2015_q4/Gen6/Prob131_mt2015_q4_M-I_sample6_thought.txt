To optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area):
1. First identify redundant computations - the XNOR operation is calculated twice
2. Compute shared terms once and reuse them to reduce area and power
3. Simplify the logic algebraically to reduce gate count
4. Maintain the same functionality while reducing combinational path depth
5. Use more efficient gate implementations where possible

The key optimizations will be:
- Compute x XNOR y once as an intermediate signal
- Simplify the OR and AND operations using the precomputed XNOR
- Reduce the number of logic levels in the critical path
- Eliminate redundant gate computations
