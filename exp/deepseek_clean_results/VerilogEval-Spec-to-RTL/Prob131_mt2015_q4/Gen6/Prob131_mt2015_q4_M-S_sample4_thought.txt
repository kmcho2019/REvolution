The key insight is that we can completely eliminate the module hierarchy and express the entire logic as a single minimized Boolean expression. By analyzing the truth tables and combining the operations, we find that the final output z can be expressed simply as x NOR y. This is because:
1. ModuleA's output (x^y)&x = x&~y
2. ModuleB's output (~x&~y)|(x&y) = x XNOR y
3. The top-level logic (a_out OR b_out) XOR (a_out AND b_out) simplifies to ~(x OR y)

This eliminates all intermediate logic and modules, reducing the implementation to just one simple gate operation.
