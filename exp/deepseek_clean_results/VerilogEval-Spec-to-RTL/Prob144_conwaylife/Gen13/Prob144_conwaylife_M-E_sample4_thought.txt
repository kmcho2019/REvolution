Novel Architecture:
1. Parallel Neighbor Counting: Instead of calculating neighbors individually for each cell, we'll use shift operations on entire rows to efficiently compute neighbor counts for all cells simultaneously.
2. Toroidal Shift Logic: Implement wrap-around using circular shifts on the 256-bit vector, treating it as a 16x16 grid.
3. Pipelined Processing: Split the neighbor counting into two stages (horizontal and vertical) to improve timing.
4. Early Termination: Skip full neighbor counting for cells that are dead with no live neighbors (common case optimization).

Key Innovations:
- Bulk shift operations for efficient neighbor calculation
- Two-stage pipelined counting for better timing
- Early termination optimization for power savings
- Minimal state updates through change detection
