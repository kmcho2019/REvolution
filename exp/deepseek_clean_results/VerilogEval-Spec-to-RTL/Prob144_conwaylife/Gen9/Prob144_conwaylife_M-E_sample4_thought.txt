Novel Architecture: Hierarchical Neighbor Counting with Pipelining
1. Hierarchical Counting: Divide the grid into 4x4 blocks (16 total). Each block computes neighbor counts for its 16 cells first, then combines with adjacent blocks' counts.
2. Pipelined Processing: Use a 2-stage pipeline where stage 1 computes intra-block counts and stage 2 combines inter-block counts.
3. Toroidal Wrapping: Implement wrap-around at the block level first, then at the cell level within blocks.
4. State Update: Only recalculate cells that changed or whose neighbors changed from previous cycle (delta computation).

Key Advantages:
- Reduced critical path through hierarchical counting
- Better timing with pipelined neighbor calculation
- Lower power through delta-based updates
- Scalable to larger grids
