Innovative Shift-Register Architecture:
1. Instead of calculating neighbors for each cell independently, use shift registers to propagate neighbor information
2. Implement a "sliding window" approach where each cell's neighbors become available through sequential shifts
3. Use a pipelined design where neighbor counts are calculated in stages
4. Leverage the toroidal nature by circularly shifting the edge rows/columns
5. Optimize by processing cells in a wavefront manner, reusing intermediate calculations

Key Advantages:
1. Dramatically reduces redundant neighbor calculations
2. Minimizes arithmetic operations by sharing intermediate sums
3. Better suited for FPGA implementations with efficient shift register primitives
4. Naturally handles the toroidal wrapping through circular shifts
5. Enables potential power savings by reducing switching activity
