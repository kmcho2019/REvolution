Improvement Strategy:
1. Timing Optimization:

   - Precompute row sums to share calculations between adjacent cells
   - Pipeline the neighbor count calculation across two clock cycles

2. Power Optimization:

   - Add enable signals for entire rows when no changes are detected

3. Area Optimization:

   - Use more compact counting structures (parallel counters)
   - Remove redundant cell_stable storage by computing on-the-fly

- Hierarchical clock gating (4x4 blocks)
