Simplification Strategy:
1. **Eliminate Submodules**: Remove the hierarchical submodules (BitwiseOR, LogicalOR, VectorNOT) and implement all operations directly in TopModule to reduce overhead.
2. **Direct Assignments**: Use simple continuous assignments for all operations without intermediate modules.
3. **Maintain Functionality**: Ensure the simplified implementation still correctly computes:
   - Bitwise OR of inputs a and b
   - Logical OR (reduction OR) of all bits in a and b
   - Concatenated NOT of b and a in the specified order
4. **Combinational Logic**: Keep the design purely combinational for zero timing violations.
