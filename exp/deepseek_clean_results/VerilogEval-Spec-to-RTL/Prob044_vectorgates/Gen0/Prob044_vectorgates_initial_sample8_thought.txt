To implement the TopModule:
1. For out_or_bitwise: Perform bitwise OR between corresponding bits of a and b (3 bits each)
2. For out_or_logical: Perform logical OR between the entire a and b vectors (treat them as single values)
3. For out_not: 
   - Take bitwise NOT of b and place in bits [5:3] of out_not
   - Take bitwise NOT of a and place in bits [2:0] of out_not
