To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations:
1. Bitwise OR between the two 3-bit inputs a and b, which will be a 3-bit result where each bit is the OR of corresponding bits in a and b.
2. Logical OR between the two inputs, which will be a single bit that is 1 if any bit in a or b is 1.
3. NOT operation on both inputs, concatenated into a 6-bit output where the upper 3 bits are the inverse of b and the lower 3 bits are the inverse of a.
