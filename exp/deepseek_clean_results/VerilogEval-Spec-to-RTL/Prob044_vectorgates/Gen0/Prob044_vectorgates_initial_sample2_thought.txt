To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three operations:
1. Bitwise OR between the 3-bit inputs a and b, resulting in a 3-bit output.
2. Logical OR between the 3-bit inputs a and b, which is true if any bit in a or b is 1.
3. NOT operation on both inputs, with b's inverse in the upper 3 bits and a's inverse in the lower 3 bits of a 6-bit output.
