Refactoring Approach:
1. **Flatten the Hierarchy**: Remove submodules and implement all logic directly in TopModule to reduce overhead from module instantiation and interconnects.
2. **Use Continuous Assignments**: Replace module instantiations with direct assign statements for simpler and more compact code.
3. **Maintain Operation Separation**: Keep the logical separation of operations (bitwise OR, logical OR, NOT) through comments for clarity.
4. **Optimize Power and Area**: By eliminating submodules, we reduce routing resources and potential power consumption from interconnects.
5. **Same Functionality**: Ensure all outputs maintain identical behavior to original implementation.
