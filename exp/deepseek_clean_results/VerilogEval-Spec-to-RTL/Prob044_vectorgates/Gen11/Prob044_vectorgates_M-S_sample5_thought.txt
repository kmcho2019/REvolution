Simplified Design Approach:

1. Remove unnecessary modularity since operations are simple and don't benefit from separate modules
2. Implement all operations directly in the TopModule using continuous assignments
3. Maintain the same functionality:
   - Bitwise OR of inputs a and b
   - Logical OR (reduction of bitwise OR)
   - Concatenated NOT operations with b in upper bits and a in lower bits
4. Eliminate parameterization since bit-widths are fixed per specification
5. Use direct assignments for cleaner and more efficient implementation
