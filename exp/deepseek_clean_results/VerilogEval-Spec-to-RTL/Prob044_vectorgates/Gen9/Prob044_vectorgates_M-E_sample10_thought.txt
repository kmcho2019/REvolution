Novel Architectural Approach:

1. Unified Operation Processing:
   - Instead of separate modules, implement all operations within a single always block for better optimization potential
   - Use continuous assignments for combinational logic to maintain clarity

2. Bitwise OR Optimization:
   - Direct parallel bitwise operation for maximum speed

3. Logical OR Innovation:
   - Implement as a reduction OR of the bitwise OR result (reusing computation)
   - More efficient than separate reduction operations on a and b

4. NOT Operation Optimization:
   - Compute NOT operations in parallel
   - Use concatenation for final output with explicit bit ordering
   - Eliminates need for separate NOT modules

5. Benefits:
   - Reduced hierarchical overhead
   - Potential for better synthesis optimization
   - Clear data flow within single module
   - Maintains full functionality with minimal operations
