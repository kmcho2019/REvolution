Novel Architectural Idea: 
1. Compute-Once Principle: Instead of separate modules, implement all operations in a single module to minimize interconnect delays and area overhead.
2. Bitwise Operations First: Compute bitwise operations first since they are the foundation for other operations.
3. Logical OR Optimization: Derive the logical OR directly from the bitwise OR result using reduction OR, avoiding redundant computation.
4. Concatenated NOT: Compute NOT operations in parallel and concatenate them in the required order in a single step.
5. Direct Wiring: Use direct wiring for outputs to minimize logic levels and improve timing.
