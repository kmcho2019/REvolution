Refactoring Approach:
1. **Flatten the Hierarchy**: Remove submodules and implement all logic directly in TopModule for better synthesis optimization potential.
2. **Use Continuous Assignments**: Replace module instantiations with direct assign statements for simpler combinational logic.
3. **Optimize Logical OR**: Keep the efficient reduction OR implementation but move it inline.
4. **Simplify NOT Operation**: Maintain the same concatenation approach but implement directly.
5. **Remove Redundant Wires**: Eliminate intermediate signals by assigning directly to outputs.

Key Benefits:
- Reduced hierarchical overhead
- More direct mapping to hardware
- Same functionality with potentially better PPA
- Cleaner single-module implementation
