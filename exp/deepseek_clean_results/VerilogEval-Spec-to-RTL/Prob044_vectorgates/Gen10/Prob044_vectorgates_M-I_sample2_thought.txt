Improvement Strategy:
1. **Module Elimination**: Remove separate submodules to reduce instantiation overhead and interconnect power. This will improve both area and power metrics.
2. **Direct Implementation**: Implement all operations directly in TopModule using continuous assignments, maintaining the same logic but with fewer hierarchical levels.
3. **Operation Consolidation**: Keep the optimized single reduction OR for logical OR and concatenated NOT operations, but now in a single module.
4. **PPA Benefits**: This approach will:
   - Reduce area by eliminating module boundaries
   - Lower power consumption by minimizing interconnect
   - Maintain the same timing characteristics (still single-level combinational logic)
   - Simplify verification and synthesis flow
