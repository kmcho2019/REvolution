Innovative approach using a unified design with parameterized operations:
1. Eliminate submodules and implement all operations directly in TopModule
2. Use clever bit manipulation and concatenation for the NOT operation
3. Compute logical OR by first OR-ing the vectors then reducing
4. This approach minimizes hierarchy overhead while maintaining readability
5. Uses parallel continuous assignments for maximum combinational efficiency

Advantages:
- Single-module design reduces synthesis overhead
- Parallel operations ensure minimal critical path
- Clear, concise implementation without unnecessary hierarchy
- Efficient bit manipulation for the NOT operation
- Easier to optimize as a whole during synthesis
