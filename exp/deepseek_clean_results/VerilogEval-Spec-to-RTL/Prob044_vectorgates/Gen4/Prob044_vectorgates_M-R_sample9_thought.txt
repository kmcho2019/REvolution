Here's a refactored version that maintains the same functionality but uses a more streamlined approach:
1. Eliminate the submodules and implement all operations directly in TopModule
2. Use continuous assignments (assign) for all operations
3. Compute the logical OR by first OR-ing the vectors then applying reduction OR
4. Maintain the same bit ordering for the NOT operation
5. This approach reduces hierarchy overhead while keeping the same logical structure

Benefits:
- Reduced module hierarchy simplifies synthesis
- All operations are visible at the top level
- Still maintains clear separation of different operations
- More compact representation while preserving readability
