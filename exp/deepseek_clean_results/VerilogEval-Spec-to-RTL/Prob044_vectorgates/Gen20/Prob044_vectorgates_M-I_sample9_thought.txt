Improvement Strategy:

1. PPA Optimization Focus:
   - Maintain current excellent timing (0ns violations)
   - Further reduce power consumption (already 11x better than reference)
   - Explore area reduction opportunities

2. Key Optimizations:
   - Flatten hierarchy to eliminate submodule overhead
   - Use direct assignments instead of module instantiations
   - Combine NOT operations into single expression
   - Simplify logical OR to use reduction operator directly

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced area by removing module boundaries
   - Slightly better power from simpler structure
   - Maintains same functionality and timing
   - More compact and readable implementation

4. Trade-offs:
   - Loss of modularity (but acceptable for this small design)
   - Still maintains good readability
   - No impact on timing characteristics
