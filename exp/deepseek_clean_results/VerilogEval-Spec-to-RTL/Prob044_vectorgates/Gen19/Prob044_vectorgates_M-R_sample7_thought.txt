Refactoring Approach:

1. Key Changes:
   - Eliminate separate submodules and implement all operations directly in TopModule
   - Use continuous assignments (assign) for all operations
   - Maintain same functionality but with flatter hierarchy
   - Keep parameterization for future flexibility

2. Benefits:
   - Reduced module hierarchy simplifies verification
   - Direct implementation may improve synthesis optimization
   - Maintains same PPA characteristics while being more concise
   - Easier to modify individual operations

3. Implementation Notes:
   - Bitwise OR remains parallel operation
   - Logical OR uses reduction OR of the bitwise OR result (optimized)
   - NOT operations use concatenation with same bit ordering
   - All operations are purely combinational
