Simplified Design Approach:
1. Eliminate separate modules since operations are simple and don't need modularization
2. Implement all operations directly in the TopModule using continuous assignments
3. Maintain same functionality with bitwise OR, logical OR, and NOT operations
4. Keep the output organization with b's inverse in upper bits and a's inverse in lower bits
5. Remove parameterization since we're working with fixed 3-bit widths
6. Use minimal, straightforward syntax for all operations
