Novel Architecture Idea: Bit-Sliced Processing with Shared Logic
1. **Bit-Sliced Processing**: Process each bit position independently for better scalability and potential pipelining
2. **Shared OR Logic**: Reuse the same OR gate for both bitwise and logical OR operations to save area
3. **Parallel NOT Operations**: Compute NOT operations in parallel with the OR operations for better timing
4. **Direct Output Mapping**: Eliminate submodules to reduce hierarchy overhead
5. **Optimized Bit Ordering**: Place the NOT results in the output vector during computation to avoid extra wiring

Key Advantages:
- More scalable architecture suitable for wider vectors
- Shared logic reduces area
- Parallel computation improves timing
- Simplified hierarchy improves PPA
- Maintains full functionality while being more efficient
