Novel Architectural Idea: Distributed Processing with Parallel Submodules
1. Instead of a single module, break down operations into specialized submodules
2. Create three independent processing units:
   - Bitwise OR unit (3 parallel OR gates)
   - Logical OR unit (reduction OR tree)
   - NOT unit (6 parallel inverters with smart concatenation)
3. Benefits:
   - Parallel processing improves timing
   - Clear separation of concerns
   - Potential for power gating individual units
   - Easier verification and testing
4. Key features:
   - Explicit instantiation of basic gates for transparency
   - Structured wiring for better physical implementation
   - Modular design allows independent optimization
