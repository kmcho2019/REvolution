Novel Bit-Sliced Architecture:
1. Implement operations at the bit level for maximum flexibility and potential power savings
2. Use a bit-sliced approach where each bit is processed independently
3. Compute logical OR by combining individual bit ORs with a custom priority encoder
4. Generate NOT operations in a distributed manner across the output bits
5. Key innovations:
   - Bit-level processing enables clock gating opportunities
   - Custom priority encoder for logical OR reduces critical path
   - Explicit bit ordering for NOT operations improves routing
   - No parameterization needed for this specific 3-bit case
6. Benefits:
   - Finer-grained control over power consumption
   - Potential for better timing on critical paths
   - Clear physical implementation mapping
   - Simpler verification due to explicit bit handling
