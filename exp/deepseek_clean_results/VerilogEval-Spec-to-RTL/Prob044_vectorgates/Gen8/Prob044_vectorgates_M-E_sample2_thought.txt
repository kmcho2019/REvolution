Novel Architectural Idea: Bit-Sliced Processing with Shared Inversion Logic

1. Core Concept:
   - Implement a bit-sliced architecture where each bit pair (a[i], b[i]) is processed independently
   - Share inversion logic between a and b vectors to reduce area
   - Use hierarchical OR reduction for logical OR to minimize power

2. Key Innovations:
   - Shared NOT gates for both vectors (reduces transistor count)
   - Parallel bit-slice processing (improves timing)
   - Early termination for logical OR (saves power when result is known)
   - Compact wiring through direct bit assignments

3. Advantages:
   - Reduced area from shared inversion logic
   - Better power efficiency from hierarchical OR
   - Maintained parallel processing benefits
   - Clear signal flow through direct bit assignments
