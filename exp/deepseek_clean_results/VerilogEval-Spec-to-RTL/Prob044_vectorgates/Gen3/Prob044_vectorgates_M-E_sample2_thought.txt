Novel Architecture Idea: Distributed Processing with Sub-modules
1. Instead of performing all operations in the main module, we'll break them into specialized sub-modules
2. Create three sub-modules:
   - BitwiseOR: Handles 3-bit bitwise OR operation
   - LogicalOR: Computes logical OR of two 3-bit vectors
   - VectorNOT: Handles the NOT operations and concatenation
3. This approach demonstrates modular design principles and could be beneficial for:
   - Reusability of components
   - Easier debugging and testing
   - Potential for independent optimization of each operation
4. The main module becomes a simple wrapper that instantiates and connects these sub-modules
