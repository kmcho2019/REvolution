Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical design with submodules:
1. Create separate modules for each operation (bitwise OR, logical OR, and NOT)
2. Instantiate these modules in the TopModule
3. The NOT module will handle both a and b inversions and concatenation
4. This modular approach improves reusability and makes the design more scalable for future modifications

Benefits:
- Clear separation of concerns
- Each operation is encapsulated in its own module
- Easier to modify or optimize individual operations
- Better for large-scale designs where operations might be reused
