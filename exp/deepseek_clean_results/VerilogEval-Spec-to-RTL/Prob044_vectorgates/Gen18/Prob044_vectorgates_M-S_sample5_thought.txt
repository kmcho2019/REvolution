Simplified Design Approach:
1. Remove modular hierarchy since operations are simple and don't need separate modules
2. Implement all operations directly in the TopModule using continuous assignments
3. Maintain same functionality:
   - Bitwise OR: simple bitwise operation between inputs
   - Logical OR: reduction OR of the bitwise OR result
   - NOT operations: concatenate inverted b and a vectors
4. Eliminate parameterization since bit-widths are fixed in requirements
5. Keep all original port definitions and behaviors
