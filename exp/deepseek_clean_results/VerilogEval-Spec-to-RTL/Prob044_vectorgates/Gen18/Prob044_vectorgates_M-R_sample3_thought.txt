Refactoring Approach:

1. Key Changes:
   - Eliminate submodules and implement all operations directly in TopModule
   - Use continuous assignments (assign) for all operations
   - Maintain same functionality but with flatter hierarchy
   - Keep parameterization for flexibility
   - Preserve optimal bit ordering for NOT operations

2. Benefits:
   - Reduced hierarchy improves simulation performance
   - Simpler verification with all logic in one module
   - Maintains same PPA characteristics
   - Easier to understand at a glance
   - Still parameterizable for different bit widths

3. Implementation Notes:
   - Bitwise OR remains parallel operation
   - Logical OR uses reduction OR on the bitwise OR result
   - NOT operations use direct inversion with concatenation
   - All outputs driven by continuous assignments
