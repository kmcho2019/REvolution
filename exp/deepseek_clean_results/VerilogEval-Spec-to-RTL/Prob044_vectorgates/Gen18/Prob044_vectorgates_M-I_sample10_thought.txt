Improvement Strategy:

1. PPA Optimization Focus:
   - Maintain functionality while reducing area and power
   - Eliminate module hierarchy to reduce interconnect overhead
   - Use direct assignments for simpler synthesis
   - Keep operations parallel for best timing

2. Key Changes:
   - Flatten the hierarchy by implementing all operations directly in TopModule
   - Remove parameterization since width is fixed (3 bits)
   - Use simpler logical OR implementation (|a || |b)
   - Maintain same bit ordering for NOT operations

3. Expected Benefits:
   - Reduced area by eliminating module instances and ports
   - Slightly lower power from reduced interconnect
   - Maintain same excellent timing characteristics
   - Simpler synthesis and place-and-route

4. Trade-offs:
   - Loss of modularity/reusability (acceptable for this fixed-width case)
   - Slightly less readable but still clear for this simple case
