Improvement Strategy:

1. PPA Optimization Analysis:
   - Current implementation already shows excellent PPA metrics (0ns timing violations, 2.89μW power, 10μm² area)
   - Further optimization opportunities:
     * Remove parameterization since width is fixed (3 bits) to eliminate overhead
     * Combine all operations into single module to reduce hierarchical overhead
     * Use direct OR implementation instead of De Morgan's for potential technology mapping benefits
     * Simplify logical OR to basic OR-reduction for minimal gate count

2. Key Changes:
   - Eliminate submodules and implement all operations directly in TopModule
   - Use direct bitwise OR operation for clarity and potential synthesis optimization
   - Simplify logical OR to its most basic form
   - Maintain clean concatenation for NOT operations

3. Expected Benefits:
   - Slight area reduction by removing module hierarchy
   - Potential timing improvement with direct OR implementation
   - Maintained or improved power efficiency
   - Simplified code structure while preserving functionality
