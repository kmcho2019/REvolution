Simplification Strategy:
1. Remove parameterization since we're dealing with fixed 3-bit widths
2. Eliminate the submodule hierarchy for direct implementation
3. Keep the same efficient operations:
   - Bitwise OR as direct operation
   - Logical OR as reduction of bitwise OR
   - NOT operations with concatenation
4. Maintain all functionality while reducing module count
5. Simplify structure while keeping optimal operations

Benefits:
- Reduced hierarchy improves readability
- Maintains same performance characteristics
- Simpler verification with single module
- Still maintains optimal power/area/timing
