To further optimize the design, we can implement a three-level hierarchical multiplexer structure. This approach:
1. Divides the 256 inputs into 16 groups of 16 (same as before)
2. First level: 16 16-to-1 muxes (same as before)
3. Second level: 4 4-to-1 muxes that each select from 4 first-level mux outputs
4. Third level: Final 4-to-1 mux that selects from the second-level outputs

This reduces the fanout on the higher selection bits (sel[7:6]) and may improve timing further. The area might increase slightly due to additional muxes, but the timing improvement could justify it. We'll also use one-hot encoding for the final selection to potentially reduce power consumption.
