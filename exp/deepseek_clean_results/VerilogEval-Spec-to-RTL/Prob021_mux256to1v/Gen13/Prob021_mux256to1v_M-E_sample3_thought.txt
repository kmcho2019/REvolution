Instead of treating the 4-bit outputs as atomic units, we can process each bit position independently:
1. Slice the 1024-bit input into 4 separate 256-bit vectors (one for each output bit)
2. Implement four 256-to-1 single-bit multiplexers in parallel
3. Each mux selects one bit from its 256-bit slice based on the 8-bit sel
4. Combine the selected bits to form the 4-bit output
This approach leverages parallel processing and may offer better routing and timing characteristics
