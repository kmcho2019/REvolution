Instead of using a hierarchical or direct selection approach, we can implement the multiplexer as a binary tree of 2-to-1 multiplexers. This structure is highly regular and scales well with the number of inputs. The 256 inputs are processed in log2(256) = 8 levels of multiplexing, with each level halving the number of candidates. The selection bits control which path to take at each level. This approach offers excellent timing characteristics as the critical path is balanced and predictable.
