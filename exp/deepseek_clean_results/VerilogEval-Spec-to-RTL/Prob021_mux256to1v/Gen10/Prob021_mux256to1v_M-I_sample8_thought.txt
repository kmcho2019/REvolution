The current solution uses a two-stage hierarchical approach, which is good for timing but introduces some overhead in area and power due to the intermediate `group` wires. We can optimize this by:
1. Using a single-stage selection with direct bit-slicing, which eliminates the need for intermediate wires and reduces area.
2. The direct approach `out = in[sel*4 +: 4]` is simpler and more efficient for synthesis, as it maps directly to a single multiplexer operation in hardware.
3. This change will reduce power by eliminating unnecessary switching activity in the intermediate `group` wires and reduce area by removing the extra logic for grouping.
4. The timing should remain good since modern synthesis tools can optimize large multiplexers effectively.
