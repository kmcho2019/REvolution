Instead of using a hierarchical multiplexer structure, we can implement the 256-to-1 mux using a barrel shifter approach. The key idea is to rotate the input vector based on the selector value and then pick the first 4 bits. This approach leverages the observation that selecting a 4-bit group is equivalent to rotating the input until the desired group appears at the least significant position. We'll use a logarithmic barrel shifter that performs shifts in stages (8 stages for 256 positions). Each stage shifts by a power-of-two amount controlled by one bit of the selector. This approach may offer better regularity and potentially better timing characteristics.
