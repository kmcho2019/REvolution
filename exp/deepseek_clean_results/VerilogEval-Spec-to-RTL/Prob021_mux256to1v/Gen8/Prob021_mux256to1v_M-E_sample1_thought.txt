Instead of using traditional multiplexing logic, we can implement the selection using a barrel-shifter-like approach. The key idea is to:
1. Right-shift the entire 1024-bit input by (sel*4) bits to align the desired 4-bit chunk at the LSB positions
2. Mask out all but the lowest 4 bits
This approach eliminates the need for hierarchical multiplexing and reduces the logic depth to just a shift and mask operation. The shift amount is computed as sel*4, and we only need the bottom 4 bits after shifting.
