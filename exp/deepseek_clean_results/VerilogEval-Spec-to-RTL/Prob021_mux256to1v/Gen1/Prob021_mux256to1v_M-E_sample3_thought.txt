Instead of using a direct part-select operation, we can implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure that breaks down the 256-to-1 selection into two stages: first selecting among groups of 16 inputs (16-to-1), then selecting within the chosen group (16-to-1). This approach might offer better synthesis optimization opportunities and potentially better timing characteristics for certain technologies. The 1024-bit input is divided into 16 groups of 64 bits each (16 groups × 16 4-bit inputs per group). The upper 4 bits of 'sel' choose the group, and the lower 4 bits choose within the group.
