The fusion strategy combines the best aspects of both examples while addressing their limitations:

1. From Example 1: Maintain the two-level hierarchical approach for better timing and scalability
2. From Example 2: Simplify the implementation by directly computing the selection without intermediate wires
3. Key improvements:
   - Eliminate the intermediate wire arrays to reduce area and power
   - Use direct bit selection with proper indexing for cleaner implementation
   - Keep the hierarchical structure for balanced timing
   - Optimize the selection logic to use a single calculation for the final output

The solution calculates the final output in one step by:
1. Using sel[7:4] to determine the 64-bit group offset
2. Using sel[3:0] to select the specific 4-bit chunk within that group
This maintains the hierarchical benefits while being more efficient.
