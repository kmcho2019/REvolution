Instead of traditional multiplexing, we can use a barrel shifter approach where we:
1. Treat the 1024-bit input as a continuous circular buffer
2. Use the selector to determine the rotation amount (sel*4 bits)
3. Extract the 4 LSBs after rotation
This eliminates the need for hierarchical selection and reduces the logic depth to a single rotation operation followed by bit extraction. The rotation is implemented using a concatenation trick that makes it efficient in hardware.
