To optimize the design further, we can eliminate the intermediate grouping wires and implement a single-level selection. This approach:
1. Removes the hierarchical structure to reduce area by eliminating unnecessary multiplexing logic
2. Simplifies the selection to a direct bit selection operation, improving timing
3. Reduces power by minimizing switching activity in intermediate wires
4. Uses a single continuous selection expression (in[sel*4 +: 4]) which is more efficient for synthesis
The key insight is that modern synthesis tools can efficiently handle large bit selections directly, making the hierarchical approach unnecessary for this case.
