The current solution uses a two-stage hierarchical approach, which is good for large multiplexers but introduces some overhead in area and power due to the intermediate `group` array. We can optimize this by:
1. Using a single-stage selection with direct bit-slicing, which eliminates the need for intermediate wires and reduces area.
2. The direct approach `out = in[sel*4 +: 4]` is simpler and more efficient for synthesis, as it maps directly to hardware multiplexers without extra logic layers.
3. This change will reduce power by minimizing switching activity (no intermediate `group` wires) and area by removing unnecessary storage.
4. Timing should remain good since modern synthesis tools can handle large bit-slicing operations efficiently.
