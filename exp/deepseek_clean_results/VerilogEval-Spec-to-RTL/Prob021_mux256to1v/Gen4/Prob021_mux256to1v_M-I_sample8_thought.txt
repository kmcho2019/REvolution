The current solution uses a two-stage hierarchical approach, which is good for large multiplexers but introduces intermediate wires (the `group` array) that consume area and may cause unnecessary switching activity. We can optimize this by:

1. **Flattening the selection logic** to a single-stage multiplexer using direct bit-slicing. This eliminates the intermediate `group` wires, reducing area and power.
2. **Simplifying the indexing** by directly calculating the output position from `sel` and `in`. The formula `out = in[sel*4 +: 4]` achieves this in one step.
3. **Maintaining readability** while minimizing logic overhead. The direct indexing approach is concise and avoids unnecessary hierarchical structures.

This optimization trades off the hierarchical structure for a simpler, flatter design that should improve PPA metrics further.
