Instead of using a hierarchical or direct part-select approach, we can leverage a shift register technique combined with masking. The idea is to shift the input vector by the selection index multiplied by 4 (since each selection is 4 bits wide), then extract the least significant 4 bits. This approach eliminates the need for intermediate storage or complex selection logic, potentially offering better area and power efficiency for certain implementations. The shift operation can be implemented using a barrel shifter structure optimized for power and area.
