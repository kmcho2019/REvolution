To optimize the PPA further, I'll implement a single-stage 256-to-1 multiplexer using a direct indexing approach. This strategy:
1. Eliminates the hierarchical structure to reduce area and potentially improve timing
2. Uses a simple bit-select operation with concatenated indexing
3. Avoids intermediate wires and generate blocks for cleaner synthesis
