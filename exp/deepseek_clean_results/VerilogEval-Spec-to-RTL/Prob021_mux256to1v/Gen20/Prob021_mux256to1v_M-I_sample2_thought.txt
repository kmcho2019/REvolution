To further optimize the design, we can implement a three-level hierarchical multiplexer structure. This will:
1. Break down the 256-to-1 mux into three levels of 4-to-1 muxes (4^4 = 256)
2. Reduce fanout at each level by having smaller muxes
3. Potentially improve timing by having more balanced path delays
4. Reduce area through more efficient decoding logic

The sel[7:0] will be divided into four 2-bit groups:
- sel[1:0] for the first level (4-to-1)
- sel[3:2] for the second level (4-to-1)
- sel[5:4] for the third level (4-to-1)
- sel[7:6] for the final level (4-to-1)

This logarithmic approach should provide better PPA metrics while maintaining functionality.
