To optimize the design further while maintaining good timing characteristics, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Single-stage MUX Implementation**: 
   - Replace the two-level hierarchy with a single-stage 256:1 mux using direct bit selection
   - This eliminates intermediate wires and reduces area while maintaining similar timing
   - The direct calculation of the selected bits using a single expression may allow better optimization by synthesis tools

2. **Optimized Bit Selection**:
   - Calculate the starting bit position using a multiplication-free approach (left shift by 2 instead of multiply by 4)
   - This reduces the logic complexity for address calculation

3. **Simplified Structure**:
   - Remove the generate block and intermediate wires
   - Use a single continuous assignment for the output

The single-stage approach should maintain good timing while potentially reducing area and power further.
