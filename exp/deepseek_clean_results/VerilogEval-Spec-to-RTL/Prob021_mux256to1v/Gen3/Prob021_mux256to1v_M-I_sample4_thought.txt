To optimize the design further while maintaining good timing characteristics, I'll implement the following improvements:

1. **Single-Level Mux with Direct Indexing**: 
   - Replace the two-level hierarchy with a single-level 256:1 mux using direct bit selection
   - This eliminates intermediate wires and reduces area while maintaining similar timing
   - The direct indexing approach leverages Verilog's efficient bit selection capabilities

2. **Optimized Bit Selection**:
   - Calculate the starting bit position directly from the sel input
   - Use the +: operator for clean 4-bit selection
   - This results in simpler routing and potentially better timing

3. **Power Optimization**:
   - The simpler structure reduces switching activity
   - Fewer intermediate signals mean less dynamic power consumption

This approach maintains the zero timing violations while potentially reducing both area and power further.
