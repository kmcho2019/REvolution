Instead of using a direct part-select operation, we can implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure. This approach breaks down the 256:1 selection into two levels: first 16:1 selection of 64-bit chunks, then a 16:1 selection of 4-bit outputs within each chunk. This reduces fan-out and may improve timing/power in some technologies, though it requires more explicit logic. The trade-off is slightly more complex code for potential PPA benefits.
