To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure. Instead of a single large 256:1 mux, I'll break it down into two levels:
1. First level: 16x 16:1 muxes (each selecting one 4-bit group from 16 possible)
2. Second level: 1x 16:1 mux (selecting the output from the first level)

This approach reduces fanout and improves routing efficiency. The sel[7:0] will be split:
- sel[3:0] for first-level mux selection
- sel[7:4] for second-level mux selection

This hierarchical structure should reduce area and power while maintaining timing performance.
