Instead of using a direct part-select approach, I'll implement a hierarchical multiplexer structure. The idea is to break down the 256:1 selection into two stages: first a 16:1 selection of 64-bit chunks, then a 16:1 selection within the chosen chunk. This reduces the fanout and potentially improves timing. The 8-bit sel is split into two 4-bit fields (upper and lower nibbles) to control each stage respectively.
