To optimize the PPA metrics while maintaining functionality, I'll implement a hierarchical multiplexer approach:
1. First level: Break the 256:1 selection into two 16:1 stages (16x16=256)
2. Second level: Use a final 16:1 mux to select between first stage outputs
3. This reduces the critical path by balancing the selection logic
4. The hierarchical structure reduces area by sharing intermediate selection logic
5. Power is optimized by minimizing the active portion of the large input bus

The improvement strategy focuses on:
- Better timing through balanced hierarchy
- Reduced area via shared intermediate selection
- Lower power by activating smaller portions of the input bus
