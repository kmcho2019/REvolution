Novel Architecture Idea:
1. Use a compact 3-state FSM instead of shift register to save area
2. Implement "lookahead" comparison to detect sequence in fewer cycles
3. Add early termination when partial sequence is broken
4. Use one-hot encoding for states to simplify comparison logic
5. Optimize power by disabling state transitions after detection

Key Advantages:
- Reduced area (only 3 states vs 4-bit shift register)
- Faster detection (can identify sequence in 3 cycles with lookahead)
- Lower power consumption (stops evaluating after detection)
- Clear state progression for easier debugging
- Efficient comparison using one-hot encoded states
