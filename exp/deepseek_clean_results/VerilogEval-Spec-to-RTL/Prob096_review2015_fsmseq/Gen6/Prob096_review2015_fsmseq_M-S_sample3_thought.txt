Simplification Strategy:
1. State Encoding: Use minimal 2-bit binary encoding (4 states) since we only need 5 states (including reset). This reduces area and improves timing.
2. State Machine: Combine GOT11 and GOT110 states since they both lead to FOUND on next correct bit. This reduces state count.
3. Output Logic: Keep combinational assignment for simplicity and better timing.
4. Reset Handling: Maintain synchronous reset for consistency.
5. Found State: Keep permanent output behavior until reset as specified.
