Here's a simplified approach that maintains functionality while reducing complexity:
1. Use a 3-bit shift register instead of 4-bit, tracking only the necessary history
2. Combine the shift and compare operations into a single step
3. Eliminate the separate 'found' register by using the shift register's MSB as the detection flag
4. The pattern 1101 is detected when:
   - Current data bit is 1 (rightmost bit)
   - Previous 3 bits are 110 (stored in shift_reg)

This reduces:
- Register bits from 5 (4 shift + 1 flag) to 3
- Comparison logic complexity
- Power consumption by stopping shifts after detection
