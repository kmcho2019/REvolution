Here's a novel approach using a binary decision tree with early termination:
1. Instead of storing all bits, we track partial matches in parallel
2. Use a tree of comparators that check for the sequence in stages
3. Each level of the tree checks for a portion of the pattern
4. Early termination occurs when any branch fails to match
5. This reduces switching activity compared to shift registers
6. The design naturally pipelines the comparison logic
7. Only active when not yet matched (start_shifting=0)
