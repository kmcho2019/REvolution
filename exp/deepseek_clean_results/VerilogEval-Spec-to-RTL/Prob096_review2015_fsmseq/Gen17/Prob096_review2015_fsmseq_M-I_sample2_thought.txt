To optimize the design further while maintaining functionality, I'll focus on:
1. Reducing the critical path by simplifying the comparison logic
2. Minimizing power consumption by adding an enable condition to the shift register
3. Maintaining the same area efficiency while improving timing

Key improvements:
- Use a 2-bit shift register plus current input for sequence detection (1101)
- Separate the state tracking into distinct states for better clarity and potential power savings
- Add an enable signal to stop shifting once the sequence is found
- Simplify the comparison logic to reduce critical path delay
