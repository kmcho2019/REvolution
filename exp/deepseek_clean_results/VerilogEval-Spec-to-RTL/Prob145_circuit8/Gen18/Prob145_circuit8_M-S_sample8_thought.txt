1. Analyzing the waveform reveals simpler behavior:
   - p is 1 when both clock and a are high (AND condition)
   - q is a delayed version of p, becoming 1 when p was 1 in the previous cycle

2. Key simplifications:
   - No need for edge detection - just simple combinational logic for p
   - q can be implemented as a single-cycle delay of p
   - Both outputs can be synchronous to the clock

3. Solution approach:
   - p = clock AND a (combinational)
   - q = p from previous cycle (1-bit shift register)
   - All logic synchronous to clock edges
