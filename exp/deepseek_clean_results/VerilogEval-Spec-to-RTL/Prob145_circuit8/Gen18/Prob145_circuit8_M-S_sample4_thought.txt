Simplified approach:
1. Use a single state bit to track whether we're in "counting" mode (after first 'a' seen)
2. p directly follows 'a' when clock is high (no state needed for this)
3. q becomes 1 one cycle after p was 1, and stays 1 until reset condition
4. Reset occurs when 'a' is high during clock low (clears both p and q)

Key simplifications:
- Eliminated explicit state machine states
- Reduced to simple sequential logic with conditional updates
- p follows 'a' immediately during clock high
- q tracks previous p value
- Reset condition is simple combinational check
