After carefully analyzing the waveform, I've identified the correct behavior:
1. p becomes 1 when:
   - Clock is high AND
   - a is high AND
   - This is the first cycle where a has been high since the last p=1

2. q becomes 1 when:
   - p was 1 in the previous cycle AND
   - a is low at the current cycle

3. The circuit needs to track whether we've seen a rising edge on 'a' since the last p=1

Implementation strategy:
- Use a state machine with 2 states:
  * WAIT: Waiting for a=1
  * PULSE: p=1 has occurred, waiting for a=0 to allow next cycle
- p is high only in PULSE state when clock is high
- q is set when leaving PULSE state and a is low
- All transitions happen on clock edges
