After carefully analyzing the waveform, here's a new approach:

1. p behavior: p is high for one clock cycle whenever 'a' transitions from low to high (rising edge detection)
2. q behavior: q becomes high when p was high in the previous cycle and stays high until the next positive clock edge where 'a' is low
3. The outputs should only update on positive clock edges
4. Need to detect rising edges of 'a' to generate p pulses

Key differences from previous approach:
1. Uses edge detection for p generation
2. q is set based on previous p and cleared when a is low at clock edge
3. No continuous reset - outputs maintain state until specific conditions are met
4. Simpler state tracking with just one previous a value
