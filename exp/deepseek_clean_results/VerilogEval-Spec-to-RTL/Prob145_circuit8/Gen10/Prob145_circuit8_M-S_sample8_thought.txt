After analyzing the waveform and the previous solution, here's a simpler approach:

1. Key observations:
   - p is simply a synchronous capture of 'a' on the positive clock edge
   - q follows p with a one-cycle delay
   - Both outputs reset to 0 when a=0 on a positive clock edge

2. Simplified approach:
   - Use a single flip-flop for p that samples 'a' on clock rise
   - Use another flip-flop for q that samples p with one cycle delay
   - No need for complex state machines - just two registers

3. Benefits:
   - Fewer states (no state machine needed)
   - Clearer relationship between inputs and outputs
   - Matches the waveform behavior perfectly
