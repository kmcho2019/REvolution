1. Simplified Behavior Analysis:
   - p is high when 'a' is high during a clock high period (level-sensitive, not edge-sensitive)
   - q is high when p was high in the previous clock cycle and stays high until reset by specific conditions
   - Both outputs are synchronous to the clock

2. Simplification Strategy:
   - Remove edge detection logic (simpler than original solution)
   - Make p directly follow 'a' during clock high
   - Make q remember previous p state until 'a' goes high again
   - Single always block for cleaner implementation

3. Advantages:
   - Fewer registers than original solution
   - No edge detection complexity
   - Still matches waveform behavior
   - Clear and minimal state tracking
