The waveform reveals a pattern where:
1. p acts as a synchronous detector for 'a' being high during rising clock edges
2. q becomes high only after p has been high for at least one cycle and stays high until a specific reset condition
3. The reset condition for q occurs when 'a' is high during a low clock phase

Key insights for the new architecture:
- Use a 2-bit shift register to track recent 'a' values at clock edges
- Implement q as a latch that sets when we see consecutive 'a' highs and resets on the asynchronous condition
- p simply reflects the current 'a' value at rising edges
- Add a small state machine to manage the q output behavior
