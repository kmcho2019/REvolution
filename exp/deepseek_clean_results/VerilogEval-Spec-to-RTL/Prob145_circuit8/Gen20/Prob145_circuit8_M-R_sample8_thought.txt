After carefully re-examining the waveform, I realize the original state machine approach missed some key timing aspects. Here's a refined understanding:

1. p is a synchronous output that pulses high for one clock cycle when:
   - Clock is high AND
   - a has a rising edge (0->1 transition) during the clock high phase

2. q is a delayed version of p that appears one full cycle after p:
   - q becomes 1 at the next falling edge of clock after p was 1
   - q stays 1 until the next falling edge where a is high

New implementation strategy:
- Use edge detection for input 'a' during clock high
- Create a one-cycle pulse for p when rising edge is detected
- Use a flip-flop to delay p by one cycle for q
- Reset q when a new rising edge is detected on 'a' during clock high
- All transitions synchronized to clock edges
