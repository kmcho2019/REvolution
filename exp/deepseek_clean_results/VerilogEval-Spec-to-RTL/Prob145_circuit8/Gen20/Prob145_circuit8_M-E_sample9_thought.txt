After carefully re-examining the waveform, I've developed a new approach using edge detection and delayed outputs:

1. Key observations:
   - p pulses high for one clock cycle when a rising edge on 'a' occurs during clock high
   - q becomes high one cycle after p's pulse and stays high until the next p pulse
   - Both outputs are synchronous to clock edges

2. New architecture:
   - Use an edge detector to capture rising edges of 'a' during clock high
   - Implement a one-cycle delay for q generation
   - Use registers to maintain state between cycles
   - Output p is combinatorial based on current a and previous state
   - Output q is registered version of p delayed by one cycle

3. Implementation:
   - Detect rising edges of 'a' while clock is high
   - Generate p pulse when edge is detected
   - Use a flip-flop to delay p for q generation
   - Clear q when a new p pulse occurs
