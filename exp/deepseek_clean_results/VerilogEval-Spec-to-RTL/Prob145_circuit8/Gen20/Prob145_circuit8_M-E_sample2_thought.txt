2. Implement p as a pulse generator that triggers on detected a edges during clock high
3. Use q as a falling-edge capture register that stores p's value when clock goes low

   - Clear separation of rising/falling edge behaviors
   - Minimal sequential elements for efficient implementation
   - Matches waveform timing exactly:
     * p updates immediately on rising clock edge when a has rising edge
     * q captures p's value on falling clock edge
