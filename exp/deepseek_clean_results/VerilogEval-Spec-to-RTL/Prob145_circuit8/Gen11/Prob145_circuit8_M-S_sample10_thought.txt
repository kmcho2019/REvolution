After carefully analyzing the waveform, here's a simpler implementation:

1. Key Observations:
   - p simply follows input 'a' but only updates on positive clock edges
   - q is set to 1 one cycle after p becomes 1 (delayed version of p)
   - q resets to 0 when 'a' is 1 during a negative clock edge

2. Simplified Approach:
   - Remove unnecessary state tracking
   - p directly follows 'a' at clock edges
   - q tracks p with a one-cycle delay using a single flip-flop
   - Clear q on negedge when 'a' is high

3. Benefits:
   - Fewer registers needed (only one for q's state)
   - Clearer direct relationships between signals
   - Maintains all waveform requirements
   - Easier to understand and verify
