1. Analyzing the waveform reveals a more complex pattern:
   - p acts as a synchronous pulse detector, going high when a rising edge of 'a' is detected during clock high
   - q acts as a toggle flip-flop that changes state when:
     * p was high in the previous cycle AND
     * current clock is low
2. This requires:
   - Edge detection for input 'a' (to generate p)
   - State tracking for both p and q
   - Proper synchronization to clock edges
3. Implementation approach:
   - Use a register to store previous 'a' value for edge detection
   - Generate p synchronously when rising edge of 'a' occurs during clock high
   - Toggle q on falling clock when p was active
4. This matches the waveform behavior where:
   - p pulses when 'a' rises during clock high
   - q toggles one half-cycle after p pulses
