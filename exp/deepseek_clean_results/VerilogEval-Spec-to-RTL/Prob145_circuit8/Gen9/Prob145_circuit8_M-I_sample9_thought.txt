1. Correct Behavior Analysis:
   - p is high for one clock cycle when a rising edge is detected on 'a' during a clock high period
   - q is a delayed version of p, becoming high one full clock cycle after p was high
   - Both outputs are synchronous to the clock

2. Improvement Strategy:
   - Use a registered version of 'a' to detect edges
   - Implement p as a pulse on rising edge of 'a' during clock high
   - Implement q as a one-cycle delayed version of p
   - All operations are synchronous for better timing

3. Advantages:
   - Fully synchronous design (better for timing analysis)
   - Matches waveform behavior exactly
   - Simple and efficient implementation
   - Clear state tracking with minimal registers
