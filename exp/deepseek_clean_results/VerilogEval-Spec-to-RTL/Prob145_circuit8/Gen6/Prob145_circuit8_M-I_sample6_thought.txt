After carefully analyzing the waveform and the feedback, here's the improved design approach:

1. 'p' behavior:
   - Updates only on positive clock edges
   - Becomes 1 when current 'a' is 1 (immediately responds to 'a' on clock edge)
   - Stays 1 until next positive clock edge where 'a' is 0

2. 'q' behavior:
   - Exactly follows 'p' but updates on negative clock edges
   - Essentially a negative-edge triggered version of 'p'

Key improvements:
1. Remove unnecessary gating of 'p' with clock
2. Simplify state tracking - only need to remember if we're in a "held high" state
3. Make 'q' a simple negative-edge triggered flip-flop of 'p'
4. Use clearer state transition logic
