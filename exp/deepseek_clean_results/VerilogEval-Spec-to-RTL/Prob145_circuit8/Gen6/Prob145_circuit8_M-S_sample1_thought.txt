After carefully re-examining the waveform, I realize the behavior can be simplified to:

1. Output p is simply the input 'a' sampled at positive clock edges (no conditional)
2. Output q is the previous value of p (one cycle delayed version)
3. Both outputs are synchronous to the clock edge

This matches the observed behavior:
- p directly follows 'a' at clock edges (90ns, 100ns, 110ns)
- q follows p with one cycle delay (p=1 at 110ns → q=1 at next cycle)
- Values only change at clock edges
