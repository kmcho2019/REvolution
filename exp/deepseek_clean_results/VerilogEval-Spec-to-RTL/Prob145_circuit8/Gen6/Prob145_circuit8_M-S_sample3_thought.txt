After carefully analyzing the waveform, here's a simplified approach:

1. p behavior: p is high when 'a' is high during the clock's high phase (simple AND of clock and a)
2. q behavior: q is set when p was high in the previous clock cycle and stays set until the next clock low phase
3. Only need to track one previous state (whether p was high last cycle)
4. Outputs update on clock edges, with q clearing during clock low phase when a is low

Key simplifications:
1. Eliminated separate a_prev register
2. Simplified p generation to just clock AND a
3. q now depends only on previous p state and current a during low phase
4. Reduced state tracking to just remembering previous p
