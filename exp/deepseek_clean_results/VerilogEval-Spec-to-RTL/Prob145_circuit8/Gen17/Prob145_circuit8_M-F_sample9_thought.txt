1. Analyzing the waveform reveals a more complex state machine behavior:
   - p becomes 1 only when both clock is high and a transitions to 1 (rising edge of a during high clock)
   - p stays 1 until the next positive clock edge where it resets to 0
   - q becomes 1 when p was 1 at the previous positive clock edge and the current negative clock edge occurs
   - q stays 1 until the next negative clock edge where it resets to 0

2. Key observations:
   - Need to detect rising edges of 'a' during high clock for p
   - Need to remember p's state at positive edges for q's behavior
   - Both outputs have memory and depend on previous states

3. Solution approach:
   - Use edge detection for 'a' during high clock
   - Add a flip-flop to store p's value at positive edges
   - Generate q based on stored p value at negative edges
   - Properly initialize all states
