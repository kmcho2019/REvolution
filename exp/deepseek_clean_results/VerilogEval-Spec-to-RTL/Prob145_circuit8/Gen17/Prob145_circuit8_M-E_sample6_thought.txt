New approach:
1. Implement a 2-bit state machine that tracks the circuit's behavior
2. Use the state to control when p follows 'a' and when q gets updated
3. State transitions:
   - STATE_IDLE (00): p follows 'a' on posedge, moves to STATE_P when p=1
   - STATE_P (01): sets q=1 on next cycle, moves to STATE_Q
   - STATE_Q (10): waits for 'a' during clock low to reset
4. Reset condition: when 'a' is high during clock low while in STATE_Q
5. Outputs:
   - p = a when in STATE_IDLE
   - q = 1 when in STATE_Q
This creates a clean separation of states and clearly defines the transition conditions.
