New approach: Implement a state machine with explicit states to track the behavior shown in the waveform:
1. IDLE state: Both p and q are 0, waiting for 'a' to be 1 during clock high
2. P_ACTIVE state: p follows 'a' when clock is high (q remains 0)
3. Q_ACTIVE state: q becomes 1 after p was 1, and both reset when 'a' is 1 during clock low

Key features:
- Uses explicit states for clearer behavior
- Only updates outputs on clock edges
- Properly handles the delayed q activation after p
- Correctly implements the reset condition when 'a' is high during clock low
- More closely matches the waveform timing relationships
