After carefully re-examining the waveform, I see a more precise pattern:
1. p is high only when both clock and a are high (AND condition)
2. q captures the value of p from the previous clock cycle (delayed by one cycle)
3. Outputs maintain their values when clock is low (no reset behavior)
4. Changes only occur on positive clock edges

The refactored solution will:
- Use a flip-flop to store p's previous value for q
- Implement p as a simple combinational AND of clock and a
- Use positive-edge triggered flip-flop for q
- Remove the unnecessary reset behavior
