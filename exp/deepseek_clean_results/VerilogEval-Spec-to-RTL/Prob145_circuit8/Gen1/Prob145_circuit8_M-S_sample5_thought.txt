The circuit behavior can be simplified to:
1. p is simply 'a' when clock is high, otherwise holds its value
2. q is a one-cycle delayed version of p, but only updates on clock high
3. No explicit reset behavior - outputs maintain state when clock is low

This matches the waveform where:
- p immediately follows 'a' when clock is high
- q shows p's value from the previous clock high period
- Both maintain values when clock is low
