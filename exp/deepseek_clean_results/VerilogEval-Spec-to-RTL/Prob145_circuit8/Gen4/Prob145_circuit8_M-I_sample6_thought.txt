The improved implementation requires:
1. A state machine for 'p' that remembers if 'a' was high during the previous clock cycle
2. 'p' should be high when either:
   - Current 'a' is high AND clock is high (immediate response)
   - Previous 'a' was high (state retention)
3. 'q' remains a flip-flop capturing 'p' on negative edges
This matches the waveform where 'p' stays high for multiple cycles when 'a' pulses are close together.
