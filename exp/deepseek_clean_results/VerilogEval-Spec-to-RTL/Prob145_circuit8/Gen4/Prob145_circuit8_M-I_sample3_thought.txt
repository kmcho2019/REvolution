After carefully re-examining the waveform, I observe a more complex behavior than initially thought:

1. Output p:
   - Goes high only when 'a' is high during a positive clock edge
   - Stays high until the next positive clock edge (capture and hold behavior)
   
2. Output q:
   - Goes high when p was high in the previous cycle AND the current 'a' is low
   - Essentially detects when p transitions from high to low
   - This creates a one-cycle pulse one cycle after p goes low

The improved design requires:
1. A flip-flop to store the value of 'a' at each positive clock edge (for p)
2. Additional logic to detect the falling edge of p (for q)
3. Proper synchronization to clock edges
