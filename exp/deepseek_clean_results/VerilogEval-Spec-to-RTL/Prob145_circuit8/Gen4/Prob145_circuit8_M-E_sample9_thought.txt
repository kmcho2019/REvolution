The circuit appears to be a two-stage state machine where:
1. p acts as an indicator that's set when 'a' is high during a clock high phase, and remains set until the next negative clock edge
2. q captures the value of p at the negative clock edge and holds it until the next negative edge
3. The system resets when 'a' is high during a clock low phase (seen at 175ns)

This requires:
- A state register for p that's set on positive clock edges when a=1
- p is cleared on negative clock edges
- q simply follows p with a half-cycle delay
- A reset condition when a=1 during clock low
