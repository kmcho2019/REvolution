After carefully re-examining the waveform and feedback, I realize the circuit has more complex behavior:
1. Output p acts as a synchronous pulse detector - it goes high only when 'a' is high during a positive clock edge
2. Output q is a stateful signal that:
   - Gets set when p has been high for at least one cycle
   - Gets cleared when a new pattern begins (a goes high during clock low)
3. The circuit requires tracking previous states to determine outputs

Key observations:
- p is simply the registered version of 'a' (latched on posedge)
- q is set when p was high in the previous cycle
- q is cleared when a is high during clock low phase

The design needs:
1. One flip-flop for p (registered 'a')
2. One flip-flop for q with conditional setting/reset
3. Additional combinational logic for q's behavior
