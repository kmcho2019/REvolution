After carefully re-examining the waveform, I observe more complex behavior than initially thought:

1. Output p is high only when 'a' is high during a positive clock edge (not continuously)
2. Output q becomes high when p was high in the previous cycle AND the current cycle's a is low
3. Both outputs maintain their values until the next positive clock edge
4. There's a clear state transition pattern where q follows p's previous state under certain conditions

The improved design requires:
1. Tracking the previous value of p
2. Conditional assignment for q based on previous p and current a
3. Synchronous updates only on positive clock edges
4. No reset needed as initial state is don't-care
