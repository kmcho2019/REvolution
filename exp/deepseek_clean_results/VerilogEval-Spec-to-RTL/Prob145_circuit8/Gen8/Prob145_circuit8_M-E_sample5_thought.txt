After carefully re-examining the waveform, I propose a novel pulse-detection and propagation architecture:

1. Key observations:
   - p mirrors 'a' but only during clock high phases and only when certain conditions are met
   - q follows p with a one-cycle delay during clock low phases
   - The system has memory of previous states but doesn't require complex state encoding

2. Novel approach:
   - Use edge detection on 'a' to identify valid transitions
   - Implement a pulse stretcher to maintain p for the required duration
   - Create a delayed version of p to generate q
   - Use clock phase separation for clean timing

3. Implementation strategy:
   - Detect rising edges of 'a' during clock high
   - Maintain p active for the duration of clock high when conditions are met
   - Propagate p to q on the following clock low phase
   - Clear outputs based on specific conditions
