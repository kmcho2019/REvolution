1. Analyzing the waveform reveals:
   - p is high only when both clock and a are high (AND operation)
   - q is a delayed version of p that toggles when:
     * p was high in the previous cycle AND
     * current clock is low
2. This can be implemented with:
   - p as a simple combinational AND of clock and a
   - q as a register that toggles when p was high in previous cycle and current clock is low
3. No need for complex state tracking - just remember if p was high last cycle
4. Reset behavior occurs naturally with this simpler implementation
