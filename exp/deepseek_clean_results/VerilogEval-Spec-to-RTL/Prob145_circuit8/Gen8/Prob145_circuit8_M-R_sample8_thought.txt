After carefully analyzing the waveform, I realize the behavior is more complex than initially thought. The key observations are:

1. p becomes 1 when 'a' is 1 at a positive clock edge and stays 1 until the next positive edge (regardless of 'a')
2. q becomes 1 only after p has been 1 at a positive edge and stays 1 until the next positive edge
3. Both outputs maintain their values between clock edges and only change at positive edges

The refactored solution uses:
- Two flip-flops to store the current state of p and q
- Combinational logic to determine next state based on current state and input
- Output assignments directly from the flip-flops
- All state changes synchronized to positive clock edges

This approach better matches the waveform by explicitly maintaining state between clock edges.
