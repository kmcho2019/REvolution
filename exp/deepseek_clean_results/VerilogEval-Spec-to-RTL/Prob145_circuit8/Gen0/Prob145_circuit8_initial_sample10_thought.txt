From analyzing the waveform, we can observe the following behavior:
1. Output p is 1 only when both clock is high and input a is high (rising edge detection of 'a' during clock high)
2. Output q appears to be a delayed version of p, specifically q becomes 1 one full clock cycle after p was 1
3. Both outputs reset to 0 when clock is low and a rising edge is detected on 'a' (as seen at 175ns)

The implementation requires:
- A flip-flop to store the previous value of 'a' for edge detection
- A flip-flop to delay p by one clock cycle to generate q
- Logic to detect rising edges of 'a' during clock high
- Reset behavior when clock is low and 'a' has a rising edge
