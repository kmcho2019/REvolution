From analyzing the waveform, we can observe the following behavior:
1. Output p is high only when input a is high during a positive clock edge (p follows a but only updates on clock edges).
2. Output q is high when p was high in the previous clock cycle (q is a delayed version of p by one clock cycle).
3. Both outputs are synchronous to the positive clock edge.

This suggests we need:
- A flip-flop to capture input 'a' on positive clock edges (for p)
- Another flip-flop to delay p by one cycle (for q)
- The outputs should be registered to maintain synchronous behavior
