After analyzing the waveform and the issues in the previous solution, here's a simplified approach:

1. Key Observations:
   - p simply follows input 'a' when q is 0 (latched at rising clock edge)
   - q becomes 1 when p was high for a full clock cycle (seen at both edges)
   - q resets to 0 when 'a' is high during a falling edge
   - p is forced to 0 when q is 1

2. Simplified Implementation:
   - Use two always blocks: one for rising edge and one for falling edge
   - Rising edge block handles p and q setting
   - Falling edge block handles q reset
   - Track p's state across both edges to detect full-cycle high
   - Remove unnecessary intermediate registers

3. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of edge-triggered behaviors
   - No complex state machine
   - Minimal registers needed
