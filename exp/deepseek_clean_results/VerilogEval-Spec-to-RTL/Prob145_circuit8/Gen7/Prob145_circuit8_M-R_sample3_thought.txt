The waveform reveals a more complex state behavior:
1. p becomes 1 only when 'a' is 1 at a positive clock edge, and stays 1 until the next positive edge
2. q becomes 1 only after p has been 1 at a positive edge, and stays 1 until the next positive edge
3. Both outputs are synchronous to the clock and hold their values between edges

Key insights:
- p acts as a one-cycle pulse when 'a' is high
- q acts as a one-cycle delayed version of p's pulse
- Need to properly synchronize both outputs to clock edges
- Can implement this with a simple state register and combinational logic
