After carefully analyzing the waveform, here's a simplified approach:

1. p behavior: p is simply the AND of clock and a - it's high only when both clock is high and a is high
2. q behavior: q remembers if p was high during the previous clock high phase, and clears when a is low during clock low phase
3. Simplified implementation:
   - p remains combinational (clock & a)
   - q uses a single flip-flop to remember p's state from previous clock high
   - q clears only when clock is low and a is low
4. Removed unnecessary edge detection and clock tracking logic
