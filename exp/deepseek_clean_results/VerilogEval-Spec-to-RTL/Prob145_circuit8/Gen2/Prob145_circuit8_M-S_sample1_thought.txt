After carefully re-analyzing the waveform, I observe:
1. Output p pulses high for one clock cycle when there's a rising edge of 'a' during a clock high
2. Output q follows p with a one-cycle delay
3. Both outputs reset to 0 when clock is low

The simplified design requires:
1. Edge detection of 'a' only at clock edges
2. Synchronous behavior for both outputs
3. No need for separate combinational logic - everything can be sequential
