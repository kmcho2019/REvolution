Refactoring Approach:
1. Structural Change:
   - Instead of using a single complex NOR-NOR structure (via De Morgan's), break the logic into intermediate signals for better readability and potential optimization
   - Use separate assignments for each product term, then combine them

2. Optimization Benefits:
   - Clearer expression structure makes it easier for synthesis tools to optimize
   - Intermediate signals may help with common subexpression elimination
   - Maintains the same logical function while potentially improving PPA through better tool optimization

3. Implementation:
   - Create four intermediate signals for each product term
   - Combine them with OR operation
   - Final inversion maintains the NOR-NOR structure
   - This approach makes the logic more modular and easier to modify
