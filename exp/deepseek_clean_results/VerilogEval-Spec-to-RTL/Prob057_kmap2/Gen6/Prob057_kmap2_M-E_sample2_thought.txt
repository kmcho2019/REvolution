This solution implements a direct sum-of-products implementation from the Karnaugh map, but with an optimized approach that minimizes logic operations. The key insight is to recognize that the output can be expressed as the OR of several product terms that cover all the '1's in the K-map, while avoiding the '0's.

Approach:
1. Identify all minterms (product terms) where the output is 1 in the K-map
2. Group adjacent '1's to form larger product terms where possible
3. Implement each product term as a logical AND of the appropriate inputs
4. Combine all product terms with logical OR operations

Benefits:
- Direct implementation of the K-map logic
- Minimal number of logic operations (4 ANDs and 1 OR)
- Clear correspondence between the K-map and the code
- No priority encoding issues
- Efficient synthesis to standard cells
