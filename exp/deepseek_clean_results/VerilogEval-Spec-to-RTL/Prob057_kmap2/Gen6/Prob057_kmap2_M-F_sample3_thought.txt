To correctly implement the Karnaugh map:
1. Re-analyze the K-map to identify all minterms where output is 1:
   - 0000, 0001, 0010 (first row)
   - 0100, 0110 (second row)
   - 0111, 1111, 1110 (third row)
   - 1000, 1001 (fourth row)

2. Identify optimal groupings:
   - ~a~b covers first two rows when ab=00
   - ~a~d covers left two columns when cd=00
   - ~b~c covers top-right corner (ab=10, cd=00,01)
   - bcd covers middle (ab=01, cd=11)
   - abc covers bottom-right (ab=11, cd=11,10)

3. The correct minimal SOP form is:
   out = ~a~b + ~a~d + ~b~c + bcd + abc

This covers all 1's in the K-map without including any 0's.
