To correctly implement the Karnaugh map:
1. Carefully analyze each cell in the 4x4 Karnaugh map
2. Identify all minterms where output is 1:
   - ab=00: cd=00,01,10 (but not 11)
   - ab=01: cd=00,10
   - ab=11: cd=01,10,11
   - ab=10: cd=00,01
3. Derive the minimal SOP expression that covers all these cases:
   - ~a~b~d (covers ab=00, cd=00,01,10 except ab=00 cd=11)
   - ~a~c~d (additional coverage for ab=00 cd=00)
   - a~b~c (covers ab=10 cd=00,01)
   - bc (covers ab=11 cd=11 and helps with other cases)
   - b~d (covers ab=01 cd=10 and ab=11 cd=10)
4. Combine these terms with OR operations
5. Final expression: out = (~a&~b&~d) | (~a&~c&~d) | (a&~b&~c) | (b&c) | (b&~d)
