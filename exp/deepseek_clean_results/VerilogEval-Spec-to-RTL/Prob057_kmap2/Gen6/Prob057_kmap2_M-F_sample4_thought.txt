To correctly implement the Karnaugh map:
1. Re-examine the K-map carefully, noting all positions where output is 1:
   - Top-left quadrant (ab=00, cd=00/01/10)
   - Middle-right (ab=11, cd=01/10/11)
   - Bottom-left (ab=10, cd=00/01)
   - Top-right (ab=01, cd=00/10)

2. Identify optimal groupings:
   - ~a~b covers first column (ab=00)
   - ~a~d covers top-left and bottom-left when cd=00
   - ~b~c covers top row when cd=00
   - b~cd covers (ab=11, cd=01)
   - a~bc covers (ab=10, cd=11)
   - ab~d covers (ab=11, cd=10)

3. The correct minimal SOP form is:
   out = ~a~b + ~a~d + ~b~c + b~cd + a~bc + ab~d
This covers all 1's without including any 0's from the K-map.
