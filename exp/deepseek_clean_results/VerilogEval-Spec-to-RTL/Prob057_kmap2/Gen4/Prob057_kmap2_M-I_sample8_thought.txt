To optimize the solution, we'll derive the minimal Sum of Products (SOP) expression from the Karnaugh map. This will reduce the number of gates and improve PPA metrics compared to the case statement approach. 

From the K-map:
1. Group all 1s to find minimal cover:
   - Term covering cd=00, ab=00 and 10: ~c & ~d & ~b
   - Term covering cd=00, ab=00 and 01: ~c & ~d & ~a
   - Term covering cd=11, ab=01,11,10: c & d & (a | b)
   - Term covering cd=10, ab=00,01: c & ~d & ~a
   - Term covering cd=00,01,10,11 with ab=01: b & ~a & d

After combining terms and eliminating redundancies, the minimal SOP is:
out = (~c & ~d) | (c & d & (a | b)) | (c & ~d & ~a) | (b & ~a & d)

This implementation should reduce area and power while maintaining or improving timing.
