After carefully analyzing the Karnaugh map, I notice that the output can be expressed more simply using XOR operations and basic logic. The key observations are:

1. When c=0, out is 1 except when (a AND b) AND (d=1)
2. When c=1, out is 1 except when (~a AND ~b) OR (a AND b AND ~d)

This leads to a simpler implementation using:
- out = (~c & (~(a & b & d))) | (c & (~(~a & ~b) & ~(a & b & ~d)))
Which can be further simplified to:
- out = (~c & (~(a & b & d))) | (c & (a | b) & (~(a & b) | d))
