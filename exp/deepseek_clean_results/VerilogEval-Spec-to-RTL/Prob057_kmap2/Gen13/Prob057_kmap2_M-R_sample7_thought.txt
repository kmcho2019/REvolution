Refactored approach focusing on direct K-map implementation with improved efficiency:

1. Key Changes:
   - Eliminate intermediate one-hot decoders to reduce area and power
   - Implement the minimal Sum of Products (SOP) form directly from the K-map
   - Use parallel AND-OR structure for better timing
   - Group terms logically based on the K-map's visual patterns

2. Optimizations:
   - Directly implement the 6 product terms that make output 0
   - Combine them using a single OR operation followed by inversion
   - Remove redundant decoding logic
   - Maintain same functionality while reducing gate count

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced area by eliminating intermediate decoding logic
   - Improved timing with flatter logic structure
   - Lower power consumption due to fewer switching nodes
   - Maintains readability while being more efficient
