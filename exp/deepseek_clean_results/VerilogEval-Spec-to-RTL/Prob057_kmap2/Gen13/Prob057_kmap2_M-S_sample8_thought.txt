Simplification Approach:
1. K-map Analysis:
   - The K-map shows that the output is 1 in most cases except for specific patterns
   - The zeros are clustered in a way that suggests a simpler expression can be derived

2. Key Observations:
   - Output is 0 when (a=1 & b=1 & c=1 & d=1) - but this case isn't in the K-map
   - Main 0 cases are when (a=0 & b=1 & c=1 & d=0) and (a=1 & b=0 & c=1 & d=1)

3. Simplified Logic:
   - Output is 1 unless both (b & ~c) or (~a & c & d) are true
   - This can be expressed as out = ~((b & ~c) | (~a & c & d))

4. Implementation:
   - Use just two intermediate terms instead of four
   - Single OR and NOT operation at the output
   - Eliminates redundant logic from previous solution
