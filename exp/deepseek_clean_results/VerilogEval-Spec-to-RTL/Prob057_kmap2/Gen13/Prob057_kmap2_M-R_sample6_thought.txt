The refactored solution will use continuous assignments with Boolean expressions derived from the Karnaugh map, but with a more optimized grouping of terms. Here's the approach:

1. K-map Analysis:
   - Identify groups of 1s in the K-map to find minimal cover
   - The output can be expressed as OR of three product terms:
     - Term1: !a OR !c (covers top-left and bottom-left quads)
     - Term2: !b OR !d (covers left and right edges)
     - Term3: a XOR b (covers diagonal pattern)
   - Final expression: out = Term1 OR Term2 OR Term3

2. Implementation:
   - Use intermediate wires for each term
   - Combine terms with OR operation
   - This structure reduces the number of operations compared to the original

3. Benefits:
   - Fewer logic operations (4 operations vs original's 6)
   - Better potential for technology mapping
   - More readable structure showing the logical grouping
   - Maintains same functionality as original solution
