To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to:
1. Carefully analyze each '1' in the map and group them optimally
2. The K-map shows output '1' for:
   - ab=00 when cd=00,01,10
   - ab=01 when cd=00,10
   - ab=11 when cd=01,10,11
   - ab=10 when cd=00,01
3. The minimal correct expression is:
   out = (~a & ~b & ~d) | (~a & ~b & ~c) | (~a & c & d) | (a & b & ~d) | (a & ~b & ~c & d)
4. This covers all '1's without covering any '0's:
   - First term covers ab=00, cd=00,10
   - Second term covers ab=00, cd=01
   - Third term covers ab=11, cd=11
   - Fourth term covers ab=11, cd=10 and ab=01, cd=10
   - Fifth term covers ab=10, cd=01
