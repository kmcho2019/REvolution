After carefully analyzing the feedback and re-examining the Karnaugh map, here's the corrected approach:

1. The main issue was incorrect grouping and overlapping terms. We need to precisely cover all 1s without covering any 0s.

2. Looking at the K-map:
   - cd=00: 1s at ab=00,01,10 → (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b))
   - cd=01: 1s at ab=00,10 → (~c & d & (~a | ~b))
   - cd=11: 1s at ab=01,11 → (c & d & (b))
   - cd=10: 1s at ab=00,01 → (c & ~d & (~a | b))

3. Breaking this down:
   - For cd=00 and cd=01, we can combine the cases where either a or b is 0
   - For cd=11, we simply need b=1
   - For cd=10, we need either a=0 or b=1

4. The correct Boolean expression is:
   out = (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b)) | (~c & d & (~a | ~b)) | (c & d & b) | (c & ~d & (~a | b))

5. This can be simplified to:
   out = (~c & (~a | ~b)) | (c & d & b) | (c & ~d & (~a | b))
