To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we'll:
1. Identify all the '1' positions in the K-map and create minterms for each
2. Group adjacent '1's where possible to simplify the expression
3. The K-map shows output '1' for these distinct cases:
   - When ab=00 and cd≠11 (top-left quadrant except cd=11)
   - When ab=10 and cd=00 or 01 (top-right two cells)
   - When ab=01 and cd=00 or 11 (middle-left top and bottom)
   - When ab=11 and cd=01,10,11 (bottom-right three cells)
4. The minimal expression covering all '1's is:
   out = (~a & ~b & ~(c & d)) | (~a & b & c) | (a & ~b & ~c) | (a & b & ~d) | (a & ~b & d)
5. This ensures all '1's are covered while avoiding '0's
