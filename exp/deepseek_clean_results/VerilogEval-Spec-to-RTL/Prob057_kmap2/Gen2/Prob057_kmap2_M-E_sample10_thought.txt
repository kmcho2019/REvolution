A novel approach is to implement the Karnaugh map using a hierarchical structure of smaller multiplexers. This method breaks down the 4-input problem into two stages of 2-input multiplexers, leveraging the inherent structure in the Karnaugh map. 

First stage:
- Use inputs c and d to select between four 2-input multiplexers (each handling ab combinations)
- Each 2-input mux corresponds to one row of the K-map (cd combinations)

Second stage:
- The outputs of these four muxes are then fed into a final 4:1 mux controlled by cd
- This creates a tree-like structure that may offer better area/power tradeoffs than a flat implementation

This approach could potentially reduce the number of gates compared to a direct case statement implementation, while maintaining readability and correctness.
