Simplification Strategy:
1. Analyze the Karnaugh map to identify essential prime implicants
2. Observe that the output is 1 in most cases except specific patterns
3. Instead of OR-ing multiple minterms, implement the inverse logic by NOR-ing the cases where output is 0
4. This reduces the number of terms needed in the expression
5. The simplified expression only needs to cover the three cases where output is 0 in the K-map

Key Observations:
- Output is 0 only when:
  - cd=11 and ab=00 (!a & !b & c & d)
  - cd=01 and ab=11 (a & b & !c & d)
  - cd=10 and ab=11 (a & b & c & !d)
- All other cases output 1
- Can implement this with a single NOR gate of these three terms
