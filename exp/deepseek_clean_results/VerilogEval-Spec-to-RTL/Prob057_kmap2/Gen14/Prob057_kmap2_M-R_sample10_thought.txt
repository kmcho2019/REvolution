Refactoring Approach:
1. Structural Change:
   - Convert the single NOR-of-ANDs implementation into a hierarchical AND-OR structure
   - Break down the logic into intermediate wires for each product term
   - This improves readability and may help with synthesis optimization

2. Logic Representation:
   - Keep the same minimized sum-of-products form
   - Express the inversion (NOR) as a separate operation at the end
   - This makes the logic structure more explicit and easier to modify

3. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of product terms and final combination
   - Easier to modify individual terms if needed
   - Potential for better synthesis optimization as the structure is more explicit
   - Maintains same PPA characteristics while improving code organization
