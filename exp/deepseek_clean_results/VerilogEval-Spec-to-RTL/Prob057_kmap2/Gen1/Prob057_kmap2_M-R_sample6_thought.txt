To improve the implementation, we should analyze the Karnaugh map more carefully and derive a more accurate Boolean expression. The key observations are:

1. The output is 1 when:
   - cd=00 and ab≠11 (first two columns of first two rows)
   - cd=01 and (ab=00 or ab=10) (first and last columns of second two rows)
   - cd=11 and (ab=01 or ab=11 or ab=10) (middle three columns of third two rows)
   - cd=10 and ab=00 or ab=01 (first two columns of last two rows)

This can be expressed as:
out = (~c & ~d & ~(a & b)) |  // cd=00, ab≠11
      (~c & d & (~a | ~b)) |  // cd=01, ab=00 or 10
      (c & d & (a | b)) |     // cd=11, ab=01,11,10
      (c & ~d & (~a | b))     // cd=10, ab=00 or 01

This covers all 1s in the map without covering any 0s.
