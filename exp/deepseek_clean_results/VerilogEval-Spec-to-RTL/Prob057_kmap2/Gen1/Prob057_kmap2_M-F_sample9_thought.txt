To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to:
1. Carefully analyze each '1' in the map and ensure it's covered by our expression
2. Avoid covering any '0' positions
3. The K-map shows output '1' for:
   - First column (ab=00) except cd=11
   - Last column (ab=10) when cd=00 or cd=01
   - Middle two columns have scattered '1's:
     - ab=01: cd=00, cd=11
     - ab=11: cd=01, cd=10, cd=11
4. The minimal correct expression is:
   out = (~a & ~b) | (~b & c & d) | (a & ~b & ~c) | (a & b & ~d) | (~a & b & c)
5. This covers all '1's without covering any '0's
