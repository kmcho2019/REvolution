To correctly implement the given Karnaugh map, we need to carefully analyze each cell and derive a more accurate Boolean expression. The previous implementation missed several cases and included some incorrect ones. Here's the improved approach:

1. First, identify all minterms where output is 1:
   - cd=00: 1 for ab=00,01,10 (but not 11)
   - cd=01: 1 for ab=00,10
   - cd=11: 1 for ab=01,10,11
   - cd=10: 1 for ab=00,01

2. The correct Boolean expression can be written as:
   out = (~c & ~d & ~(a & b)) |        // cd=00 and not ab=11
         (~c & d & (~a | ~b)) |        // cd=01 and not ab=01
         (c & d & (a | b)) |           // cd=11 and not ab=00
         (c & ~d & ~(a & ~b))          // cd=10 and not ab=10

3. This can be further simplified to:
   out = (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b)) |
         (~c & d & (~a | ~b)) |
         (c & d & (a | b)) |
         (c & ~d & (~a | b))

4. Notice common terms can be factored:
   out = (~d & (~a | ~b)) |            // When c=0
         (d & (a | b)) |               // When c=1
         (c & ~d & (~a | b))           // Additional term for cd=10

This implementation will correctly cover all 1's in the K-map while excluding the 0's.
