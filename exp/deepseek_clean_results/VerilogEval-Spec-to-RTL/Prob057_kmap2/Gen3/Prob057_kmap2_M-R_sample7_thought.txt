1. First, let's properly analyze the Karnaugh map by identifying all minterms where output is 1:
   - When cd=00: out=1 for ab=00,01,10 (but not 11)
   - When cd=01: out=1 for ab=00,10
   - When cd=11: out=1 for ab=01,10,11
   - When cd=10: out=1 for ab=00,01

2. We can derive the minimal expression by:
   - Grouping the four corners (cd=00,ab=00; cd=00,ab=10; cd=10,ab=00; cd=10,ab=10) which gives ~d & ~b
   - Adding the cd=00,ab=01 case with ~c & ~d & a & ~b
   - Adding the cd=01,ab=00 case with ~c & d & ~a & ~b
   - Adding the cd=01,ab=10 case with ~c & d & a & ~b
   - Adding the cd=11,ab=01 case with c & d & ~a & b
   - Adding the cd=11,ab=10 case with c & d & a & ~b
   - Adding the cd=11,ab=11 case with c & d & a & b
   - Adding the cd=10,ab=01 case with c & ~d & ~a & b

3. After combining terms and simplifying, we get:
   out = (~d & ~b) |                     // Corner grouping
         (~c & ~d & a & ~b) |            // cd=00,ab=01
         (~c & d & ~a & ~b) |            // cd=01,ab=00
         (~c & d & a & ~b) |             // cd=01,ab=10
         (c & d & ~a & b) |              // cd=11,ab=01
         (c & d & a & ~b) |              // cd=11,ab=10
         (c & d & a & b) |               // cd=11,ab=11
         (c & ~d & ~a & b)               // cd=10,ab=01

4. This covers all 1's in the K-map exactly without any overlaps with 0's.
