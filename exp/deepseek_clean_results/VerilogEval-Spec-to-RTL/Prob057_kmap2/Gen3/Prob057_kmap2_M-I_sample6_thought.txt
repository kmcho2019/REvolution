To correctly implement the Karnaugh map, we need to carefully analyze each cell and ensure our logic covers all 1s without covering any 0s. Here's the improved approach:

1. Analyze each group of 1s in the K-map:
   - For cd=00: 1s at ab=00,01,10 → (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b | (a & ~b)))
   - For cd=01: 1s at ab=00,10 → (~c & d & (~a | ~b))
   - For cd=11: 1s at ab=01,11 → (c & d & b)
   - For cd=10: 1s at ab=00,01 → (c & ~d & (~a | b))

2. The key improvements are:
   - More precise grouping to avoid covering 0s
   - No overlapping of incorrect terms
   - Clear separation of cases based on cd values

   out = (~c & ~d & (~a | ~b | (a & ~b))) |
         (~c & d & (~a | ~b)) |
         (c & d & b) |
         (c & ~d & (~a | b))
