Novel Approach: Hierarchical Logic Decomposition
1. Key Insight:
   - Instead of direct SOP minimization, decompose the problem into two stages:
     * Stage 1: Detect patterns in the cd and ab domains separately
     * Stage 2: Combine these patterns with strategic masking

2. Implementation Strategy:
   - Create intermediate signals that detect specific cd patterns (rows)
   - Create intermediate signals that detect specific ab patterns (columns)
   - Use these intermediates to construct the final output through logical combinations
   - This approach mimics how human vision processes grid patterns by first detecting rows/columns

3. Benefits:
   - More scalable for larger K-maps
   - Clear separation of row/column logic makes debugging easier
   - Potential for better technology mapping in some synthesis tools
   - Natural parallelism in detecting row/column patterns

4. Verification:
   - Each intermediate signal corresponds to a clear visual pattern in the K-map
   - Final combination logic matches all 1s in the K-map through systematic coverage
