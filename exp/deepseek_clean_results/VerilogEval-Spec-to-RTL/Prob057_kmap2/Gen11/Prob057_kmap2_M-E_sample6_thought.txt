Here's a novel approach using a hierarchical multiplexer structure that leverages the symmetry in the K-map:

1. Observation:
   - The K-map shows that when cd=00 or cd=10, the output is mostly 1 except for ab=11
   - When cd=01 or cd=11, the pattern is more complex but shows symmetry in ab

2. Key Idea:
   - Use cd as primary select lines for a 4:1 mux
   - Each mux input implements a simplified function of ab
   - This hierarchical approach reduces logic depth and may improve timing

3. Implementation:
   - For cd=00: out = !(a & b) [all 1s except ab=11]
   - For cd=01: out = !b [matches the pattern]
   - For cd=11: out = a | b [matches the pattern]
   - For cd=10: out = !a [matches the pattern]

4. Benefits:
   - Clear separation of concerns (cd vs ab logic)
   - Potentially better timing due to reduced logic depth
   - Modular structure that's easy to understand and modify
   - Efficient implementation using standard cells
