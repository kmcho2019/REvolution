The current implementation is already quite efficient, but we can optimize it further by:

1. Expression Simplification:
   - Notice that several terms share common sub-expressions (like a&b, c&d, etc.)
   - Factor these common terms to reduce logic operations
   - The expression can be rewritten as:
     out = !( (!a & d & (!b&c | b&!c)) | (a & b & (!c&d | c&!d)) | (a & !b & c & !d) )

2. Implementation Benefits:
   - Reduced number of logic operations (from 6 OR terms to 4)
   - Shared intermediate terms reduce gate count
   - Better fan-out characteristics
   - Potentially lower power due to reduced switching activity
