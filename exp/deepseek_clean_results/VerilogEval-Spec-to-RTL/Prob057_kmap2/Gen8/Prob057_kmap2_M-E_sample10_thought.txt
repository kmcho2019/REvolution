A novel approach is to implement the Karnaugh map using a direct sum-of-products (SOP) implementation derived from the K-map. This method identifies all the 1's in the K-map and creates OR terms for each group of adjacent 1's that can be combined. 

From the given K-map, we can identify four prime implicants:
1. ~a & ~d (covers top-left 2x2 square)
2. ~b & ~c (covers left two columns in top and bottom rows)
3. b & c (covers middle two cells in third row)
4. a & ~b & d (covers two cells in first and second rows)

The output will be the OR of these four terms. This implementation minimizes the number of gates while maintaining a single level of logic (after the input inversions), which should provide good timing characteristics.
