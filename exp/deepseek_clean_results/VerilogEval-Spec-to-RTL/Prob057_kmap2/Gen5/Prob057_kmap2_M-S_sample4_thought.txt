To simplify the implementation while maintaining correct functionality:
1. Analyze the Karnaugh map to identify essential prime implicants
2. Group adjacent 1's to find minimal cover:
   - ~a~b covers all 1's in first two rows when ab=00 or 01
   - ~a~d covers leftmost column (cd=00)
   - ~b~c covers top-right corner (ab=10, cd=00,01)
   - b~cd covers middle-right (ab=11, cd=01)
   - abc~d covers bottom-right (ab=11, cd=10)
3. Combine these terms with OR operations
4. The minimal SOP form is: out = ~a~b + ~a~d + ~b~c + b~cd + abc~d
This covers all 1's in the K-map without redundancy.
