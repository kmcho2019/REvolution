An alternative approach is to recognize that the Karnaugh map can be decomposed into simpler sub-functions that can be combined using basic logic gates. By analyzing the map, we can see that the output is 1 in most cases except when:
1. (c=1,d=1,a=0,b=0) - top-left corner of the 11 cd group
2. (c=0,d=0,a=1,b=1) - middle of the 00 cd group
3. (c=1,d=0,a=1,b=1) - middle of the 10 cd group
4. (c=0,d=1,a=0,b=1) - middle of the 01 cd group
5. (c=1,d=0,a=1,b=0) - bottom-right of the 10 cd group

We can implement this by creating the inverse conditions (when output is 0) and then inverting the result. This approach uses fewer gates than the case statement method while maintaining readability.
