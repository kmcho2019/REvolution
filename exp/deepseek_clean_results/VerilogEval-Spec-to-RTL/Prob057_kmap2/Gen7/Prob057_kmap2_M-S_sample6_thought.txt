The Karnaugh map can be simplified using boolean algebra to reduce the logic complexity. Looking at the map, we can identify prime implicants and group the 1s to form a minimal sum-of-products expression. 

From the K-map:
1. The term a'b' covers the top-left corners (cd=00/01, ab=00)
2. The term a'c covers the left and right edges (cd=00/10, ab=00 and cd=11, ab=10)
3. The term bd covers the middle-right (cd=11, ab=01/11)
4. The term ac'd' covers the top-right (cd=00, ab=10)

The simplified expression is: out = a'b' + a'c + bd + ac'd'

This implementation uses 4 AND gates and 3 OR gates, which should be more efficient than the 16-case lookup table while maintaining the same functionality.
