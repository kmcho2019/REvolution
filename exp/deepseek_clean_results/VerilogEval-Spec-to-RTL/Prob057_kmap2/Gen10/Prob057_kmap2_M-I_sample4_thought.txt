To optimize the PPA metrics, I'll derive a minimized Boolean expression directly from the Karnaugh map. This approach will:

1. Reduce logic levels by implementing the function directly rather than through nested case statements
2. Minimize the number of gates to reduce area and power
3. Maintain the same functionality while improving timing

The K-map shows the following prime implicants:
- ~c & ~d (covers all 1s in cd=00)
- ~a & ~d (covers top-left corners)
- b & c & d (covers the 1s in ab=01,11 when cd=11)
- a & ~b & ~d (covers the 1 in ab=10 when cd=00)

The minimized expression is: out = (~c & ~d) | (~a & ~d) | (b & c & d) | (a & ~b & ~d)
