To simplify the implementation while maintaining functionality, we can derive a minimized Boolean expression from the Karnaugh map. The K-map shows clear groupings that can be expressed with fewer logic operations:

1. The output is 1 when:
   - (a XOR b) is true AND (c AND d) is false (top-left and bottom-right quadrants)
   - OR when (c AND d) is true AND (a OR b) is true (middle-right quadrant)
   - OR when (c XNOR d) is true AND (a NAND b) is true (specific cells)

2. This can be simplified to:
   out = (~c | ~d) & (a ^ b) | (c & d & (a | b)) | (~(a & b) & (c ~^ d))

3. Further simplification yields:
   out = (a ^ b) & (~c | ~d) | (a | b) & (c & d) | ~(a & b) & (c ~^ d)

This reduces the logic to a few basic operations while maintaining the exact functionality.
