Novel Architectural Idea:
1. Observation: The K-map shows a checkerboard-like pattern with 0s and 1s that suggests potential XOR/XNOR relationships between variables.
2. Approach: Instead of traditional sum-of-products, implement a hierarchical XOR-based structure that captures the pattern's symmetry.
3. Key Insight: The output can be expressed as a combination of XOR operations between pairs of variables, reducing gate count and critical path.
4. Implementation: 
   - First layer computes XOR/XNOR relationships between variable pairs
   - Second layer combines these intermediate results with additional logic
   - Final output is derived from these combined signals
5. Benefits: 
   - Fewer gates than SOP implementation
   - Balanced critical path
   - Potential for better power efficiency due to reduced switching activity
