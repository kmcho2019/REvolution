Improvement Strategy:
1. Critical Path Optimization:
   - Restructure the logic to reduce gate levels by moving the inversion earlier
   - Use De Morgan's laws to transform the NOR structure into a NAND-based implementation
   - This reduces the critical path from 3 levels (AND-OR-INV) to 2 levels (NAND-NAND)

2. Term Consolidation:
   - Combine terms that share common literals to reduce gate count
   - Specifically, merge (a & b & ~c) and (a & b & ~d) into (a & b & (~c | ~d))
   - This maintains functionality while reducing implementation complexity

3. Area Optimization:
   - Use compound gates where possible (AOI/OAI)
   - Share common sub-expressions between terms
   - Eliminate redundant logic paths

4. Power Optimization:
   - Structure the logic to minimize switching activity on high-probability paths
   - Place simpler terms earlier in the evaluation chain
