Refactored approach using direct K-map implementation with minimized logic:

1. Key Changes:
   - Eliminate intermediate one-hot decoders to reduce area and power
   - Implement the minimal Sum of Products (SOP) form directly from the K-map
   - Use parallel AND-OR structure for better timing
   - Group terms to minimize gate count

2. Optimization Insights:
   - From the K-map, identify the minimal cover of 1s:
     - ~a~b (ab=00) covers three 1s (except cd=11)
     - a~b~d (ab=10, cd=00,01)
     - ab~c (ab=11, cd=01,10)
     - ~acd (ab=01, cd=11)
   - This gives us 4 product terms instead of 6 in the original
   - Direct implementation reduces logic levels and fan-out

3. Benefits:
   - Reduced area (fewer gates)
   - Lower power (less switching activity)
   - Potentially better timing (shorter critical path)
   - More efficient synthesis
