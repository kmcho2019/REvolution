Refactoring Approach:
1. Structural Change: Instead of using a single NOR gate with OR-ed product terms, we'll break it into separate AND terms and combine them with OR gates first, then invert the final output. This makes the logic more explicit and easier to optimize during synthesis.

2. Term Grouping: Group the product terms based on common variables to potentially help synthesis tools with better optimization. We'll keep the same 4 product terms but express them separately.

3. Output Inversion: Move the inversion to a separate assign statement for clarity. Some synthesis tools might optimize this differently when separated.

4. Benefits:
   - More readable and modular structure
   - Same logical functionality
   - Potential for better synthesis optimization due to explicit term separation
   - Maintains single-level logic depth
