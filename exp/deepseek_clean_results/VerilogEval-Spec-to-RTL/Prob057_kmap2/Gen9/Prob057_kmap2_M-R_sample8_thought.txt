The refactored solution will use continuous assignments with Boolean expressions derived from the Karnaugh map, but with a different grouping approach to potentially optimize the logic further. Here's the approach:

1. K-map Analysis:
   - Identify alternative groupings in the K-map that might lead to simpler logic
   - Instead of looking at where output is 0, look at where it's 1 and express as sum-of-products
   - The output is 1 when:
     * (!c & !d) OR
     * (!a & !d) OR
     * (a & b & d) OR
     * (!a & b & !c) OR
     * (a & !b & c)

2. Implementation:
   - Use continuous assignment with the alternative Boolean expression
   - Group terms to minimize logic operations
   - The expression can be written as a sum of these simpler terms

3. Benefits:
   - Different logic structure that might synthesize differently
   - Potentially better area optimization in synthesis
   - Clear relationship to original K-map
   - Maintains same functionality as original solution
   - Uses positive logic which might be more intuitive
