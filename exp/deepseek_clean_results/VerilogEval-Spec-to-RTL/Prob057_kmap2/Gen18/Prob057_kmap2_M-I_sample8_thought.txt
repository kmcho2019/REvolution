To optimize the solution for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area), I'll implement the minimal Sum of Products (SOP) form derived directly from the Karnaugh map. This approach:

1. Identifies all the 1s in the K-map and creates product terms for them
2. Combines these terms using OR operations
3. Benefits:
   - Eliminates unnecessary intermediate decoding logic
   - Reduces gate count and critical path
   - Minimizes power consumption by avoiding redundant switching
   - Uses only essential logic gates needed for the minimal expression

The K-map shows the following essential product terms:
1. ~a~b (ab=00) for cd=00,01,10
2. a~b~d (ab=10, cd=00)
3. ab~c (ab=11, cd=01)
4. abc (ab=11, cd=11)
5. ~ab~c (ab=01, cd=11)
6. a~bc (ab=10, cd=11)
