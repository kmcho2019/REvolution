The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating and cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should activate when it's too cold, while in cooling mode, the aircon should activate when it's too hot. The fan should turn on whenever either the heater or aircon is active, or when explicitly requested via fan_on. The key is to properly decode the mode and condition signals to generate the correct outputs without any conflicting states (heater and aircon should never be on simultaneously).

1. heater = mode AND too_cold
2. aircon = NOT mode AND too_hot
3. fan = heater OR aircon OR fan_on
