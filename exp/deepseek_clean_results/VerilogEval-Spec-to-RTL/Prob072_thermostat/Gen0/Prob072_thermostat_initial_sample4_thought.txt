The thermostat controller needs to operate in two modes (heating/cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should turn on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the aircon should turn on when it's too hot. The fan should turn on whenever either the heater or aircon is on, or when explicitly requested via fan_on. The key is to properly decode the mode and generate the appropriate control signals while ensuring the fan logic covers all cases.
