The thermostat controller needs to operate in two modes (heating/cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should turn on when too_cold is high, and in cooling mode, the aircon should turn on when too_hot is high. The fan should turn on whenever either heater or aircon is on, or when explicitly requested via fan_on input. The key is to properly decode the mode and temperature inputs to control the outputs while preventing heater and aircon from being on simultaneously.
