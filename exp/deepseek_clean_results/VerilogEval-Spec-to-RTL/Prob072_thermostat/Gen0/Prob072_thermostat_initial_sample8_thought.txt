The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating and cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, it should activate the heater when too_cold is high, and in cooling mode, it should activate the aircon when too_hot is high. The fan should be activated whenever either the heater or aircon is on, or when explicitly requested via fan_on. The key is to use the mode input to select between the two operational modes and generate the appropriate control signals for heater, aircon, and fan based on the temperature sensors and fan request.
