The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating and cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should activate when it's too cold, while in cooling mode, the aircon should activate when it's too hot. The fan should turn on whenever either the heater or aircon is active, or when explicitly requested via the fan_on input. The key is to properly decode the mode and temperature inputs to generate the correct output signals without any conflicting states (heater and aircon should never be on simultaneously).
