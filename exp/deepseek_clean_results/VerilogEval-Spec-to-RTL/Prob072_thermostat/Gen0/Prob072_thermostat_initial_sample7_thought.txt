The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating/cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, the heater should activate when it's too cold, while in cooling mode, the aircon should activate when it's too hot. The fan should turn on whenever either the heater or aircon is active, or when explicitly requested via fan_on. The key is to properly decode the mode and condition signals to generate the correct output controls without conflicting heater/aircon activation.

1. Heater control: active only in heating mode (mode=1) when too_cold=1
2. Aircon control: active only in cooling mode (mode=0) when too_hot=1
3. Fan control: active when either heater, aircon, or fan_on is active
