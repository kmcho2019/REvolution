The thermostat controller needs to operate in two distinct modes (heating and cooling) based on the 'mode' input. In heating mode, it should activate the heater when too_cold is high, and in cooling mode, it should activate the aircon when too_hot is high. The fan should be turned on whenever either the heater or aircon is active, or when explicitly requested via fan_on. The key is to use the mode input to select between heating and cooling behaviors while ensuring the fan logic properly combines all activation conditions.
