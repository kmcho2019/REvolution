The solution can be simplified further by recognizing that:
1. The fan output is simply the OR of all possible activation conditions (heater, aircon, or direct fan request)
2. The heater and aircon outputs are mutually exclusive based on mode, so no additional logic is needed to prevent both from being on simultaneously
3. We can directly use the input signals in the output assignments without any intermediate logic
