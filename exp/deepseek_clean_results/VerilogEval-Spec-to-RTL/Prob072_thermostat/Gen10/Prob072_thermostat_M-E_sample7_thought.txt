Here's a novel approach using a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the thermostat states explicitly. The FSM will have four states:
1. IDLE - No heating or cooling needed
2. HEATING - Heater is on
3. COOLING - Aircon is on
4. FAN_ONLY - Only fan is requested

This approach provides more explicit state control and could be easier to extend with additional features like timers or safety checks. The FSM transitions are based on the input signals (mode, too_cold, too_hot, fan_on).
