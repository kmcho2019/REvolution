The solution can be further simplified by recognizing that:
1. The heater and aircon outputs are mutually exclusive (can't be on simultaneously) due to mode control
2. The fan logic can directly use the conditions that activate heater/aircon instead of their outputs
3. This eliminates one level of logic by combining conditions

Simplified logic:
- heater = mode & too_cold (unchanged)
- aircon = ~mode & too_hot (unchanged)
- fan = (mode & too_cold) | (~mode & too_hot) | fan_on (direct condition check)

This reduces the fan logic's dependency on intermediate signals while maintaining identical functionality.
