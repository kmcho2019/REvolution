Here's a novel state-machine based approach that introduces hysteresis to prevent rapid toggling of outputs:

1. Instead of pure combinational logic, use a simple state machine to manage transitions between heating/cooling states.

2. Implement hysteresis by requiring the temperature to cross a threshold before switching states (e.g., won't switch back immediately after turning on heater/aircon).

3. Separate the fan control into its own logic block that can be independently controlled while still responding to system needs.

4. Use registered outputs to prevent glitches and ensure clean transitions.

Key advantages:
- More stable operation by preventing rapid cycling of heater/aircon
- Cleaner transitions between states
- Better modeling of real-world thermostat behavior
- Still maintains all required functionality
