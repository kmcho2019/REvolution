The current solution is already optimal in terms of PPA metrics, but we can make one minor improvement:
1. The fan logic uses an OR operation which is slightly more area/power intensive than needed
2. We can optimize the fan logic by observing that heater and aircon are mutually exclusive (can't both be 1)
3. This allows us to replace the OR with a simple wire connection to either heater or aircon when active, plus the fan_on signal
4. The improvement is marginal but reduces gate count slightly
