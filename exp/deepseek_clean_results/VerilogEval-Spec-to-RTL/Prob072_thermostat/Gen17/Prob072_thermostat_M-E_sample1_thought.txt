Here's a novel approach using a finite state machine (FSM) implementation:
1. Instead of pure combinational logic, we'll use a simple FSM with 4 states to explicitly track the system's operational mode
2. The FSM will have states for IDLE, HEATING, COOLING, and FAN_ONLY
3. This provides better debuggability and potential for future expansion (like adding hysteresis or timing controls)
4. Outputs are registered, which can help with glitch reduction in physical implementation
5. The FSM transitions are designed to prioritize user fan requests while maintaining correct heating/cooling behavior
