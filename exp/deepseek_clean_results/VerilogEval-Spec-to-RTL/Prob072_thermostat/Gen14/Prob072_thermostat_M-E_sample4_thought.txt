Here's a novel approach using a finite state machine (FSM) implementation instead of pure combinational logic. The FSM will have 4 states:
1. IDLE: No heating/cooling needed
2. HEATING: Heater is on
3. COOLING: Aircon is on
4. FAN_ONLY: Only fan is requested

Benefits of this approach:
1. More explicit state representation makes the design more maintainable
2. Easier to extend with additional features (like timers, hysteresis)
3. Clear separation of control logic
4. Potential power savings by avoiding unnecessary switching

The FSM will transition between states based on inputs, and outputs will be determined by current state.
