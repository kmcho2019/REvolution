The solution can be simplified by recognizing that:
1. The heater and aircon outputs are completely independent of each other
2. The fan output can be expressed as a simple OR of three conditions:
   - User-requested fan (fan_on)
   - Heater being on (mode & too_cold)
   - Aircon being on (~mode & too_hot)
This eliminates the need for a ternary operator in the fan logic while maintaining the same functionality.
