The solution can be further simplified by recognizing that:
1. The heater and aircon are mutually exclusive (can't be on simultaneously due to mode control)
2. The fan logic is simply the OR of all possible activation conditions
3. We can directly express outputs without intermediate signals

Simplified approach:
- heater = mode & too_cold
- aircon = ~mode & too_hot
- fan = (mode & too_cold) | (~mode & too_hot) | fan_on
This eliminates the need for internal signal declarations while maintaining the same functionality.
