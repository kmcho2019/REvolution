1. Keep the direct mode-based control for heater/aircon
2. Simplify the fan logic by recognizing that fan_on is a standalone override
3. This maintains the same functionality while potentially reducing switching activity when fan_on is active (since it doesn't need to evaluate heater/aircon states)
