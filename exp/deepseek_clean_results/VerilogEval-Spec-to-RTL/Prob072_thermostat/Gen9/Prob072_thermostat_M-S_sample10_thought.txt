The original solution is already quite simple, but we can make it even more straightforward by observing that:
1. The heater and aircon outputs are completely independent of each other (mutually exclusive based on mode)
2. The fan output can be expressed as a simple OR of all possible activation conditions (heater, aircon, or fan_on)
3. We can directly embed the mode conditions in the heater/aircon assignments without intermediate signals

This results in a minimal implementation with just three continuous assignments, each being a single logical expression.
