The current implementation is already quite efficient, but we can make a few targeted improvements to optimize PPA further:

1. Remove localparam definitions since they're only used once and don't provide significant readability benefit in this small module, saving a tiny bit of area.

2. Reorder the fan logic to place the most likely condition (fan_on) last, as OR gates typically evaluate left-to-right. This might save power when fan_on is less frequent than heater/aircon.

3. Use parentheses in the output assignments to ensure optimal synthesis (though modern tools would handle this anyway).

4. Keep the mutual exclusion property between heater and aircon by maintaining the mode-based gating.

These changes should maintain the same functionality while potentially improving power and area slightly.
