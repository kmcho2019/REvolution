The solution can be further simplified by observing that:
1. The heater and aircon outputs are completely determined by mode and their respective temperature signals
2. The fan output is simply the OR of all possible activation conditions (heater, aircon, or manual fan_on)
3. We can express all outputs directly as combinational logic with minimal gates

The most straightforward implementation is:
- heater = mode AND too_cold
- aircon = NOT mode AND too_hot
- fan = heater OR aircon OR fan_on (same as before)
This is already optimally simple - no further meaningful simplification is possible without changing functionality.
