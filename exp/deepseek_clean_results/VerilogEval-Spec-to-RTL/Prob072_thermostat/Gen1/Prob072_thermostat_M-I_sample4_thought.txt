To optimize the design, I'll focus on reducing power consumption and area while maintaining correct functionality. Key improvements:

1. **Fan Logic Optimization**: 
   - The fan can be driven directly by fan_on when neither heater nor aircon is active, reducing unnecessary switching activity.
   - This creates a more power-efficient path for fan control.

2. **Mode Signal Handling**:
   - Eliminate the explicit inversion of mode by restructuring the aircon logic to use positive logic with mode.
   - This reduces gate count by removing the inverter.

3. **Output Isolation**:
   - Ensure heater and aircon remain mutually exclusive by construction, not just by mode.
   - This provides better design robustness.

The improved version will have fewer logic levels and gates while maintaining the same functionality.
