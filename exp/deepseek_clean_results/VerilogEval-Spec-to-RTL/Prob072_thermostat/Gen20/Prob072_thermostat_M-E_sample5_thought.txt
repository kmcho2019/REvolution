Here's a novel approach using a finite state machine (FSM) with explicit states for each operating mode. The FSM will:
1. Have 4 states: IDLE, HEATING, COOLING, FAN_ONLY
2. Transition between states based on mode, temperature, and fan inputs
3. Output heater, aircon, and fan signals based on current state
This approach makes the control logic more explicit and modular, potentially making it easier to extend with additional features in the future.
