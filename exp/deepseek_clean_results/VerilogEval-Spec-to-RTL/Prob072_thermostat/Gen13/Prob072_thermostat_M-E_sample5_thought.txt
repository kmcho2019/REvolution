This solution implements the thermostat as a finite state machine (FSM) with 4 distinct states:
1. IDLE: No temperature control active
2. HEATING: Heater is on (mode=1 and too_cold)
3. COOLING: Aircon is on (mode=0 and too_hot)
4. FAN_ONLY: Only fan is on (user request)

Key advantages:
- Explicit state transitions make the behavior very clear
- Easier to extend with additional states/features
- More robust against glitches (state transitions are explicit)
- Fan control logic is centralized in state decoding
- Could be easily modified to add timing constraints (minimum on-times, etc.)

The FSM approach provides better maintainability and extensibility while maintaining the same functionality. It's slightly more complex but offers better design structure for potential future enhancements.
