   - Add clocked registers for outputs to reduce unnecessary switching activity
   - Implement a simple clock gating mechanism for the fan control to reduce dynamic power when fan isn't needed

2. **Area Optimization**:
   - Maintain the current minimal gate count while making the design more robust
   - Use registered outputs which may help with timing closure in larger designs

3. **Readability/Maintainability**:
   - Keep clear comments and signal naming
   - Group related logic together
