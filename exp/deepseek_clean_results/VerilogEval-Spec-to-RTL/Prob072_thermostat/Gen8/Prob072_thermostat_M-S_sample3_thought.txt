The solution can be simplified by recognizing that:
1. Heater and aircon are mutually exclusive based on mode
2. Fan needs to be on when either heater/aircon is on OR when fan_on is active
3. The logic can be directly expressed with simple assignments without any need for complex optimization

The simplified version maintains all functionality while being more straightforward:
- heater = mode AND too_cold
- aircon = NOT mode AND too_hot
- fan = heater OR aircon OR fan_on
