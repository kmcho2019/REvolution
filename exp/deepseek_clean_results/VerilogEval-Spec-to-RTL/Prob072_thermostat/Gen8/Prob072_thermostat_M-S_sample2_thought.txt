The solution can be simplified by recognizing that:
1. The heater and aircon outputs are completely independent and each depends on just two inputs
2. The fan output can be expressed as a simple OR of three conditions (fan_on, heater, aircon)
3. No intermediate signals are needed - all outputs can be direct combinational assignments

This maintains the exact same functionality while making the code more straightforward and easier to understand. The logic depth remains the same (2 levels for fan) but the implementation is cleaner.
