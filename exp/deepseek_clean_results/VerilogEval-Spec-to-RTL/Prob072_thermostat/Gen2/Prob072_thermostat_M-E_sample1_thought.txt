An alternative approach is to use a state machine representation where the thermostat's behavior is explicitly modeled as distinct states. While this is a combinational problem, thinking in terms of states can make the logic more modular and potentially easier to extend. We'll define two states (HEATING and COOLING) based on the mode input, and then generate outputs based on these states and the temperature inputs. The fan control remains the same as before. This approach makes the mode selection more explicit and could be easier to modify if additional modes are added later.
