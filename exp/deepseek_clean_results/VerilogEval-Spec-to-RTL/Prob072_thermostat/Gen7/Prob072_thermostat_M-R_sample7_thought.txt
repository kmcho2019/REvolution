The refactored solution maintains the same functionality but reorganizes the logic to:
1. Use an always block with case statement for mode selection (instead of separate assign statements)
2. Group related outputs together in the case structure
3. Keep the fan logic separate since it depends on all conditions
4. Maintain the same localparam definitions for readability

This approach makes the mode-specific behavior more explicit and could be easier to extend if additional modes are added later. The fan logic remains combinational for optimal timing.
