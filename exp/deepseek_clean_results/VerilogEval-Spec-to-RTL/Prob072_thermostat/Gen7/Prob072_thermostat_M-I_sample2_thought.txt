The current implementation is already highly optimized for PPA, but we can make a few minor improvements:

1. Power Optimization:
   - Reorder the fan logic to potentially reduce switching activity (fan = heater | aircon | fan_on)
   - This puts the heater/aircon signals first which may be more stable than fan_on

2. Area Optimization:
   - Use a single NOT gate for mode inversion shared between aircon and any potential future logic
   - Though minimal, this prevents duplicate inversion logic

3. Maintainability:
   - Keep the simple combinational structure
   - Use clear signal names and minimal logic

The improvements will:
- Maintain identical functionality
- Slightly reduce potential power consumption
- Keep the same excellent timing characteristics
- Potentially save a tiny amount of area
- Remain just as readable
