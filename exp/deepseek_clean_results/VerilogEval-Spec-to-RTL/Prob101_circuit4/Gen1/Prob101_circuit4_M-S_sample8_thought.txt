Analyzing the truth table, we can see that q is 1 in all cases except when:
1. All inputs are 0 (a=0, b=0, c=0, d=0)
2. When a=1, b=0, c=0 (regardless of d)

This can be simplified to: q is 0 only when (a & ~b & ~c) is true or when all inputs are 0. Therefore, q = ~(a & ~b & ~c) & (b | c | d). However, we can further optimize by noticing that when b or c is 1, the first term is automatically satisfied. The simplest expression is q = b | c | (a & d).
