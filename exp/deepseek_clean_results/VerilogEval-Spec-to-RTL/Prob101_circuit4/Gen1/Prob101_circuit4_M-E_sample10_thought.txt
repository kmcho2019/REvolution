After carefully analyzing the truth table, I noticed that the output q is 1 whenever:
1. Either b or c is 1 (regardless of other inputs)
2. Or when a and b are both 1 (regardless of c and d)
This can be represented more efficiently by recognizing that the condition where both a and b are 1 is already covered by b being 1. Therefore, the minimal expression is simply q = b | c. This covers all cases in the truth table while being the most optimized implementation.
