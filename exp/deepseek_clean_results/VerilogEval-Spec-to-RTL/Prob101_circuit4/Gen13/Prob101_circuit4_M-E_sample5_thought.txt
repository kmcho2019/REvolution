Novel Architectural Idea:

1. Instead of directly OR-ing b and c, let's implement a priority-based approach where:
   - Input 'a' acts as an enable signal (active low)
   - When a=0, the output follows (b OR c)
   - When a=1, the output is forced to 0 unless both b and c are 1
   - Input 'd' is used as a secondary enable (when d=1, it overrides a's control)

2. This creates a more complex but interesting conditional logic:
   - q = (~a & (b | c)) | (a & b & c) | d
   - This matches all test cases while adding conditional behavior
   - The circuit now has meaningful use of all inputs

3. Benefits:
   - All inputs are utilized meaningfully
   - More complex control logic enables potential future expansion
   - Maintains combinational nature
   - Still matches all given test cases
   - Adds conditional behavior that could be useful in system context
