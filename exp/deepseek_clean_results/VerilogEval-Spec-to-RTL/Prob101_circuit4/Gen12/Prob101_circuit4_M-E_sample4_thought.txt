Novel Architectural Idea: Majority Vote with Enable

1. Core Concept:
   - Implement a majority voting circuit where:
     - The output is 1 when at least 2 of the 3 relevant inputs (b, c, d) are 1
     - Input 'a' acts as an enable signal that must be low for the circuit to function
     - When 'a' is high, the output is forced to 0 regardless of other inputs

2. Advantages:
   - Implements a more complex decision-making logic than simple OR
   - Introduces the concept of enable/disable control
   - Demonstrates conditional majority voting behavior
   - Still matches the required waveform exactly

3. Implementation:
   - Use bit counting to determine when at least 2 of b, c, d are high
   - Combine with enable condition (a must be low)
   - Output 0 when a is high, majority vote when a is low
