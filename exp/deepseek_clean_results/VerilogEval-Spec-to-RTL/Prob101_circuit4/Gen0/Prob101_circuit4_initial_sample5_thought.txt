Analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can observe that the output q is 1 in all cases except when:
1. All inputs (a, b, c, d) are 0
2. When a=1 and b=0 and c=0 (regardless of d)
3. When a=0 and b=0 and c=0 and d=0 or d=1

This behavior can be summarized as: q is 1 when either b is 1, or c is 1, except when a is 1 and b is 0 and c is 0. Essentially, q = (b | c) & ~(a & ~b & ~c).
