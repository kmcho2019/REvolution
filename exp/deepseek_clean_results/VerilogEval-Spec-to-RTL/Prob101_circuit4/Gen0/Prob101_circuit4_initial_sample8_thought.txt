Analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can observe that the output q is 1 in all cases except when:
1. All inputs (a, b, c, d) are 0
2. When a is 1 and b, c are 0 (regardless of d)
3. When a is 0, b is 0, and d is 0 (regardless of c)

This behavior can be summarized as: q is 1 when either (c is 1) OR (b is 1), except when a is 1 and b is 0. The exact logic can be expressed as: q = (b | c) & ~(a & ~b)
