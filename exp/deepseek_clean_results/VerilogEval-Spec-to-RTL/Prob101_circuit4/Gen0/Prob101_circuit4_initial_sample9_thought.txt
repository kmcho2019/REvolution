Analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can observe that the output q is 1 in all cases except when:
1. All inputs (a, b, c, d) are 0
2. When a=1 and b=0 and c=0 (regardless of d)
This suggests the logic can be expressed as: q = (b OR c) OR (a AND c) OR (a AND b). Alternatively, we can see that q is 1 whenever either b or c is 1, or when both a and b are 1. This simplifies to q = b | c | (a & b).
