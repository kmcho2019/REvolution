An alternative approach is to recognize that q is 1 in all cases except when both b and c are 0. This can be implemented using a NAND gate on the inverted inputs of b and c, which effectively gives the same functionality as an OR gate but demonstrates a different logic implementation. The NAND of inverted inputs (De Morgan's theorem) is equivalent to OR operation. This approach shows how the same functionality can be achieved with different gate-level implementations.
