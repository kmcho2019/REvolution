Refactored Implementation Idea: Boolean Expression with Explicit OR

1. Core Concept:
   - Maintain the same logical functionality (q = b OR c)
   - Replace the ternary operator with an explicit OR operation
   - This makes the intent clearer while maintaining identical behavior
   - Still ignores 'a' and 'd' as they don't affect the output

2. Advantages:
   - More readable and explicit about the OR operation
   - Same minimal logic depth and timing characteristics
   - Potentially more intuitive for other designers to understand
   - Still maintains the same PPA characteristics

3. Implementation:
   - Direct OR operation between b and c
   - Simple assign statement for combinational logic
   - No change in functionality, just clearer expression
