To design the instruction register module `instr_reg`, we will first declare the module with its input and output ports. We will then define two 8-bit registers, `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`, to store the instructions from different sources. The design will include a reset mechanism to initialize both registers to zero when the `rst` signal is low. We will use an always block to capture the incoming instructions based on the `fetch` control signal and update the `ins_p1` and `ins_p2` registers accordingly. Finally, we will derive the output signals `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` from the stored instructions in the registers.
