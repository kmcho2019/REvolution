To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to capture incoming instructions from various sources and separate them into distinct fields for further processing. The module will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. Based on the fetch signal, instructions will be fetched from the data input into the respective registers. We will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the register. The outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions.
