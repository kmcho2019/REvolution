To design the instruction register module, we'll use a combination of conditional statements and assignments to manage the flow of instructions from different sources. We will utilize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. The clock signal and reset will be used for synchronization and initialization. The fetch signal will determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. Finally, we will derive the output signals (ins, ad1, ad2) from the stored instructions based on their bit positions.
