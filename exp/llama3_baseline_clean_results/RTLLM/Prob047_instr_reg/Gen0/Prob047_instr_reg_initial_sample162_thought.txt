To implement the instruction register module, we will first define the module with its input and output ports. Then, we will declare two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store the instructions from different sources. We will use the fetch signal to determine which register to update. The ins, ad1, and ad2 outputs will be derived from the stored instructions. We will also include a reset mechanism to initialize both registers to zero when the reset signal is low. This will ensure the module starts in a known state.
