To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use a clock signal (clk) and an active low reset signal (rst) to synchronize and initialize the registers. The fetch signal will determine which register to update with the incoming instruction from the data input. We will then derive the output ports (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions in ins_p1. The ins output will hold the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 will hold the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and ad2 will hold the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
