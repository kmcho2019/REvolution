To implement the instruction register module `instr_reg`, we need to follow these steps: 
- Initialize two 8-bit registers, `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`, to store instructions from different sources.
- Use a clocked always block to capture the instruction from the `data` input based on the `fetch` control signal and update the corresponding register (`ins_p1` or `ins_p2`) on the rising edge of the clock `clk`.
- Apply the active low reset `rst` to initialize both registers to zero when `rst` is low.
- Derive the output ports `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` from the stored instructions in `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`.
- Ensure that the outputs are updated based on the values of `ins_p1` and `ins_p2` after the instruction fetch operation.
