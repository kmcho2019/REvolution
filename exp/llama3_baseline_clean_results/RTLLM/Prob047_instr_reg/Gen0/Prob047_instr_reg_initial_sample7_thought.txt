To design the instr_reg module, we will create two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. We will use the fetch signal to determine which register to update on the rising edge of the clock. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with ins taking the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 taking the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 taking the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
