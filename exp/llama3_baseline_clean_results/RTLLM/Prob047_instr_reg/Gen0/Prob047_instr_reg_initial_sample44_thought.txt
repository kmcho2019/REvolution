To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will utilize a clocked always block to capture the rising edge of the clock (clk) and synchronize the register updates with the clock. Inside this block, we will first check for the reset condition (rst == 0) to initialize the registers to zero. Then, based on the fetch signal, we will update the corresponding register (ins_p1 or ins_p2) with the incoming instruction from the data input. Finally, we will derive the output signals (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions in the registers.
