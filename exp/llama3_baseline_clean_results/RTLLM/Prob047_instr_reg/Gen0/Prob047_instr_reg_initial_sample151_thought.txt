To implement the instruction register module, we'll first define the inputs and outputs as specified. We'll then use two 8-bit registers to store instructions from different sources. The reset signal will be used to initialize these registers. Based on the fetch signal, we'll update the appropriate register with the incoming instruction data. Finally, we'll derive the output signals from the stored instructions. The ins signal will be derived from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, the ad1 signal will be derived from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and the ad2 signal will be the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
