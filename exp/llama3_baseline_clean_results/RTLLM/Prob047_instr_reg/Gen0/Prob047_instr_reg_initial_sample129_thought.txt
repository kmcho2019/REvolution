To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will first declare the input and output ports according to the specification. Then, we will define the two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store the instructions from different sources. We will use a always block to handle the clock signal, reset signal, and the fetch control signal. Based on the fetch signal, we will decide which register to update. Finally, we will assign the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 based on the stored instructions in the registers.
