To implement the instruction register module, we need to design a digital circuit that can store and process CPU instructions from various sources. The module should have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The circuit should be able to capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address, and data) for further processing. The module should also have a reset mechanism to initialize the registers to zero when the reset signal is low.
