To design the instruction register module 'instr_reg', we'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will implement a clocked process to handle the reset and the fetching of instructions based on the 'fetch' signal. The 'ins', 'ad1', and 'ad2' outputs will be derived from the stored instructions in these registers. We'll use conditional statements to determine which register to update based on the 'fetch' signal and to assign the outputs accordingly.
