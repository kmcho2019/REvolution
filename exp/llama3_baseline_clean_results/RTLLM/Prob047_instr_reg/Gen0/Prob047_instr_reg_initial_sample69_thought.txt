To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to follow these steps: 
1. Initialize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources.
2. Use the reset signal (rst) to reset both registers to zero when it's active low.
3. Based on the fetch signal, update the corresponding register with the input data on the rising edge of the clock.
4. Derive the outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions in the registers.

The ins output will take the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 will take the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 will take the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
