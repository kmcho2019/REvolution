To design the instr_reg module, we need to create two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and a reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers. The fetch signal will control which register to update with the incoming instruction from the data input. We will then derive the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions in ins_p1. Specifically, ins will be the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 will be the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 will be the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
