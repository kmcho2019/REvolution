The instruction register module will be designed with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will have a clock signal (clk) for synchronization, an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the register, a control signal (fetch) to indicate the source of the instruction, and an 8-bit data input (data) representing the instruction to be fetched. The module will output the high 3 bits of the instruction (ins), the low 5 bits of the instruction (ad1), and the full 8-bit data from the second source (ad2). The implementation will be based on the specified conditions for the fetch signal.
