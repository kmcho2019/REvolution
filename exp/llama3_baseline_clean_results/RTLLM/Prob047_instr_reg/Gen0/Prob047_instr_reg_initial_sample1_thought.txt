To implement the instr_reg module, I will first declare the input and output ports as specified. Then, I will define two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store the instructions from different sources. The module will have a reset functionality that initializes both registers to zero when the rst signal is low. Based on the fetch signal, the module will update the corresponding register with the incoming instruction. Finally, the outputs will be derived from the stored instructions by selecting the appropriate bits.
