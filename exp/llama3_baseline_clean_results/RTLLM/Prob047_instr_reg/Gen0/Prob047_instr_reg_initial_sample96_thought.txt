The instruction register module, instr_reg, will be designed using two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. It will use a clock signal, clk, and an active low reset signal, rst, to synchronize and initialize the registers. The fetch signal will determine which register to load with the incoming instruction. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1. Specifically, ins will be the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 will be the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 will be the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
