To implement the instruction register module, we will first define the two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. We will then use a clocked always block to capture the instruction from the input data based on the fetch signal. The reset signal will be used to initialize both registers to zero on the rising edge of the clock. Finally, we will use continuous assignments to derive the output ports ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions in the registers.
