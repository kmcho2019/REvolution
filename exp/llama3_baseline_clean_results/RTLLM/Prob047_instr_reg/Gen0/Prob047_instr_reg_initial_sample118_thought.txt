The instruction register module (instr_reg) will utilize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. It will have a clock signal (clk) for synchronization, an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the register, and a control signal (fetch) to determine the source of the instruction. Based on the fetch signal, the instruction will be fetched from the data input into either ins_p1 or ins_p2. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions. The module will be implemented using Verilog's always block for combinational and sequential logic.
