To implement the instruction register module `instr_reg`, we need to follow a step-by-step design process. 
First, we declare the module and its ports according to the given specification. 
Then, we define two internal registers, `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`, to hold the instructions from different sources. 
The next step involves implementing the reset logic to initialize both registers to zero when the `rst` signal is low. 
After that, we add the clock edge detection logic to update the internal registers based on the `fetch` signal. 
Finally, we assign the outputs `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` based on the stored instructions in `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`.
