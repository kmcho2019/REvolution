The instruction register module, instr_reg, will be designed as a sequential circuit that responds to the clock and reset signals. It will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The fetch signal will determine which register is updated with the incoming data. The outputs (ins, ad1, ad2) will be derived from the contents of these registers. We will use a case statement inside an always block to handle the different conditions based on the fetch signal.
