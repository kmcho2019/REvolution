To implement the instr_reg module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We'll use the fetch signal to determine which register to update with the incoming data. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, both registers will be initialized to zero. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with ins being the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 being the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 being the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
