To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will first define the internal signals and registers required to store the instructions. Two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, will be used to hold instructions from different sources. We will use a case statement to determine which register to update based on the fetch control signal. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the values stored in ins_p1. The module will be reset when the rst signal is low, and the clock signal clk will be used to synchronize the register updates.
