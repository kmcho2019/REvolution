To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will be synchronized with a clock signal (clk) and will have an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers. The fetch signal will control which register to load with the incoming instruction. We will use if-else statements to determine which register to update based on the fetch signal. The outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers.
