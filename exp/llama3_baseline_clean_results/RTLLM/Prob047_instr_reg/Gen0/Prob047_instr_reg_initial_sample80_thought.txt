To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to implement two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold instructions from different sources. The module should capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address, and full 8-bit data) for further processing. We will use the clk signal for synchronization and the rst signal for initialization. The fetch signal will determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers.
