To design the instr_reg module, we need to create two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to hold instructions from different sources. We will use a case statement to handle the fetch signal and determine which register to update. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, we will reset both registers to zero. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1. We will use bit slicing to extract the required fields from the instruction.
