To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we'll start by defining the input and output ports according to the problem description. We'll use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields for further processing. The ins output will hold the high 3 bits of the instruction from ins_p1, representing the opcode, ad1 will hold the low 5 bits of ins_p1 as the register address, and ad2 will directly take the value of ins_p2.
