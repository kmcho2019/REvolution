The instruction register module (instr_reg) will be designed with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address) for further processing. On the rising edge of the clock, the module will either initialize the registers to zero if the reset signal is low or update the registers according to the fetch signal. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers.
