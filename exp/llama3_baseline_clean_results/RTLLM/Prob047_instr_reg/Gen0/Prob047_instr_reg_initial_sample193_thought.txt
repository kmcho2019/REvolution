To design the instruction register module, we'll first define the module and its ports according to the given specifications. We will then use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will be sensitive to the clock signal and the reset signal. Based on the fetch signal, the module will decide which register to update with the incoming data. Finally, we will derive the output signals ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions. The high 3 bits of ins_p1 will be used for the opcode, the low 5 bits of ins_p1 will represent the register address, and the full 8 bits of ins_p2 will be used as the second source data.
