To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will be designed to capture incoming instructions based on the control signal (fetch) and reset (rst) signal. We will use a synchronous reset to initialize both registers on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the reset signal is low. The outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers. We will use a case statement to determine which register to update based on the fetch signal. The ins output will take the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 will take the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and ad2 will take the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
