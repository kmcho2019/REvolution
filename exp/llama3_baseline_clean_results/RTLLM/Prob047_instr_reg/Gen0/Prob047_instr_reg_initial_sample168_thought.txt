To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address) for further processing. We will use a clock signal for synchronization and a reset signal to initialize the registers. Based on the fetch signal, we will load the instruction into the corresponding register and derive the output signals from the stored instructions.
