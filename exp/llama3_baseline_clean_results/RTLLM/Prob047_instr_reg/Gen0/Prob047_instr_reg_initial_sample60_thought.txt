The instruction register module, instr_reg, will be designed as a finite state machine with two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers. The fetch signal will determine which register to load with the incoming instruction. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers. The design will utilize always blocks for combinational and sequential logic.
