The module `instr_reg` is designed to capture and process CPU instructions from various sources. It contains two 8-bit registers (`ins_p1` and `ins_p2`) to store instructions. The `fetch` signal determines which register to update with the incoming instruction. The `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` outputs are derived from the stored instructions. To implement this, we'll use Verilog's synchronous reset and conditional assignment to update the registers based on the `fetch` signal and derive the output signals accordingly.
