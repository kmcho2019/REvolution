To design the instruction register module 'instr_reg', we will use two 8-bit registers 'ins_p1' and 'ins_p2' to store instructions from different sources. The module will be sensitive to the rising edge of the clock signal 'clk'. If the reset signal 'rst' is low, both registers will be reset to zero. Based on the 'fetch' signal, the instruction will be fetched from the 'data' input into either 'ins_p1' or 'ins_p2'. The outputs 'ins', 'ad1', and 'ad2' will be derived from the stored instructions in 'ins_p1' and 'ins_p2'. We will use always blocks to describe the sequential behavior of the registers.
