To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to consider the clock synchronization, reset functionality, and the control logic based on the fetch signal. The module will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. The ins output will be derived from the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 from the full 8 bits of ins_p2. 
We will use always blocks to describe the combinational and sequential logic. The module will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock and the reset signal to handle initialization and instruction updates.
