To design the instruction register module, we will utilize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The design will include a reset mechanism to initialize the registers and a control mechanism based on the fetch signal to load instructions into the registers. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions. We will use the always @(posedge clk) block to capture the rising edge of the clock and perform the necessary operations.
