The instruction register module, instr_reg, will be designed to capture and process CPU instructions. It will have two internal registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will use a clock signal, clk, for synchronization, an active low reset signal, rst, to initialize the register, a control signal, fetch, to indicate the source of the instruction, and an 8-bit data input, data, representing the instruction to be fetched. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with ins representing the opcode or operation, ad1 representing the register address, and ad2 representing the full 8-bit data from the second source. The module's functionality will be implemented using always blocks in Verilog, handling the reset condition and the fetch control signal.
