The instruction register module (instr_reg) will be designed as a digital circuit that captures and processes incoming instructions. It will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the register. Based on the control signal (fetch), the module will store the incoming instruction in one of the two registers. The high 3 bits of the instruction will be extracted as the opcode (ins), and the low 5 bits will be extracted as the register address (ad1). The full 8-bit data from the second source will be extracted as ad2.
