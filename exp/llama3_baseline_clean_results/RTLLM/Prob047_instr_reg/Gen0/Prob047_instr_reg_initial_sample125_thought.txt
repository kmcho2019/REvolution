The instruction register module, `instr_reg`, will have two 8-bit registers, `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`, to store instructions from different sources. On the rising edge of the clock, it will check the reset signal and fetch signal to determine whether to update the registers. Based on the fetch signal, it will either store the instruction from the data input into `ins_p1` or `ins_p2`, or retain the previous values. The outputs `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` will be derived from the stored instructions. 
The `ins` output will be the high 3 bits of `ins_p1`, `ad1` will be the low 5 bits of `ins_p1`, and `ad2` will be the full 8-bit data from `ins_p2`.
