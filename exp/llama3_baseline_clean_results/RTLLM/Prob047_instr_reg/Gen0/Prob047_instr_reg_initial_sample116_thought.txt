The instr_reg module will be designed with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. It will have a clock signal for synchronization, an active low reset signal, a 2-bit control signal to determine the source of the instruction, and an 8-bit data input. The module will capture incoming instructions and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address, and the full 8-bit data from the second source) for further processing. 
The ins output will be derived from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 from ins_p2.
The registers will be initialized to zero on the rising edge of the clock when the reset signal is low.
Based on the fetch signal, the instruction will be fetched from the data input into either ins_p1 or ins_p2.
