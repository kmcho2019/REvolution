The instruction register module (instr_reg) is designed to capture and process incoming CPU instructions from various sources. It uses two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. The module is controlled by the clock signal (clk), active low reset signal (rst), and a control signal (fetch) that determines the source of the instruction. Based on the fetch signal, the instruction is stored in either ins_p1 or ins_p2. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1. The ins output represents the high 3 bits of the instruction (opcode or operation), the ad1 output represents the low 5 bits of the instruction (register address), and the ad2 output represents the full 8-bit data from the second source (ins_p2).
