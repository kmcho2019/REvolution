To design the instruction register module, we will first define the inputs and outputs as per the problem statement. Then, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store the instructions from different sources. We will use the fetch signal to determine which register to load with the incoming instruction. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions by extracting the required bits. We will use a clocked always block to ensure that the registers are updated on the rising edge of the clock. We will also include a reset mechanism to initialize the registers when the reset signal is low.
