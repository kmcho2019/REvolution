To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to follow these steps:
1. Initialize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources.
2. Implement a reset mechanism to clear both registers when the reset signal (rst) is low.
3. Based on the fetch signal, update the corresponding register (ins_p1 or ins_p2) with the data input on the rising edge of the clock (clk).
4. Derive the output ports (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions in ins_p1.
The ins output will hold the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 will hold the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and ad2 will hold the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
