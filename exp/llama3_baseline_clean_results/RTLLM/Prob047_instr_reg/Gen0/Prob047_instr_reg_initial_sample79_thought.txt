To implement the instruction register module, we will first define the input and output ports according to the given specifications. Then, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. Based on the fetch signal, we will update the corresponding register on the rising edge of the clock. Finally, we will derive the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions. The outputs will be driven directly from the stored values in ins_p1.
