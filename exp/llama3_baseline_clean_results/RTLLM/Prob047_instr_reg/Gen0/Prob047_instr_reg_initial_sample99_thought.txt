To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we'll design a Verilog module that includes two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the control signal 'fetch' and synchronize these operations with the clock signal 'clk'. It will also include an active low reset signal 'rst' to initialize the registers. The outputs 'ins', 'ad1', and 'ad2' will be derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1.
