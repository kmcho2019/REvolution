To design the instr_reg module, we need to consider the following steps: 
1. Declare the module and its ports according to the given specifications.
2. Initialize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources.
3. Implement the clock-edge triggered logic to update the registers based on the fetch signal and reset condition.
4. Derive the output signals (ins, ad1, ad2) from the stored instructions in the registers.
