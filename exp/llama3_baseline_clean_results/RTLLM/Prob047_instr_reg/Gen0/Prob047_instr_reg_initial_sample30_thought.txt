To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (opcode, register address, and full 8-bit data) for further processing. 
The design will utilize a clock signal for synchronization and an active low reset signal to initialize the register. 
The output ports will provide the high 3 bits of the instruction (opcode), the low 5 bits of the instruction (register address), and the full 8-bit data from the second source.
