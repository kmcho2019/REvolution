To implement the instruction register module, I will use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will be designed with a clocked reset to initialize the registers. Based on the fetch signal, the module will capture instructions from the data input into the corresponding register. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with the opcode extracted from the high 3 bits, the register address from the low 5 bits, and the full 8-bit data from the second source. This implementation will ensure proper synchronization with the clock signal and correct handling of the reset and fetch signals.
