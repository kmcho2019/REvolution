To implement the instruction register module, we need to define a Verilog module that captures the specified inputs and produces the required outputs. The module will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. Based on the fetch signal, we will update these registers on the rising edge of the clock. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1 and ins_p2.
