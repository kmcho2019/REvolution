To implement the instruction register module, we'll use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The control logic will be based on the fetch signal, which determines the source of the instruction. We'll use a case statement to handle the different conditions of the fetch signal. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with the opcode extracted from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, the register address extracted from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
