To design the instruction register module, we will first define the input and output ports according to the given specification. We will then use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. Based on the fetch signal, we will update the corresponding register with the incoming instruction. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1. If the reset signal is low, we will initialize both registers to zero on the rising edge of the clock.
