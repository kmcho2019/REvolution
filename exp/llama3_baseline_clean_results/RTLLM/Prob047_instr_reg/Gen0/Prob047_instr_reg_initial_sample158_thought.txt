To design the instruction register module, we will first define the input and output ports according to the specifications. We will then implement two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers. Based on the fetch signal, the module will capture incoming instructions and separate them into distinct fields for further processing. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions.
