To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers to store instructions from different sources. The module will be sensitive to the rising edge of the clock signal and will perform the following operations: 
- On reset, both registers will be initialized to zero.
- Based on the fetch control signal, instructions will be loaded into the corresponding register.
- The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions.
We will use the `always_ff` block for the sequential logic and the `always_comb` block for the combinational logic.
