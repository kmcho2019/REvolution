The instruction register module will be designed using two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will have a clock signal (clk), an active low reset signal (rst), a control signal (fetch) to determine the source of the instruction, and an 8-bit data input (data) representing the instruction. The module will also have three output ports: ins (high 3 bits of the instruction), ad1 (low 5 bits of the instruction), and ad2 (the full 8-bit data from the second source). Based on the fetch signal, the instruction will be fetched into either ins_p1 or ins_p2. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions.
