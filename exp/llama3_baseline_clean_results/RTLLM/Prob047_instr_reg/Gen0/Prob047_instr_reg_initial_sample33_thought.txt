To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will utilize two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The design will be based on a finite state machine (FSM) that responds to the fetch control signal to determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. The outputs (ins, ad1, ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions in the registers. We will use the rising edge of the clock signal for synchronization and the active low reset signal to initialize the registers.
