To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use the fetch signal to determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. The ins output will be derived from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 from the full 8 bits of ins_p2. We will use a synchronous reset to initialize both registers to zero when the reset signal is low.
