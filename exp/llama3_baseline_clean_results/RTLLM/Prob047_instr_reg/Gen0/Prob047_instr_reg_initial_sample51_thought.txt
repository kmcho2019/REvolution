To design the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will capture incoming instructions based on the fetch control signal and update the corresponding register on the rising edge of the clock. We will then derive the output signals (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions. The reset signal will be used to initialize both registers to zero when it is low.
