Based on the rising edge of the clock (clk) and the reset (rst) signal, we'll implement the reset functionality to initialize both registers to zero when rst is low. 

Next, we'll use the fetch signal to determine which register to update with the incoming instruction from the data input. If fetch is 2'b01, we'll update ins_p1; if fetch is 2'b10, we'll update ins_p2. 

Finally, we'll derive the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions. The ins output will be the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 will be the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and ad2 will be the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
