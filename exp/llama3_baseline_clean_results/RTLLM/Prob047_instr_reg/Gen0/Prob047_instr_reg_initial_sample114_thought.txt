To implement the instruction register module, we can use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers. Based on the fetch signal, we will either load the instruction into ins_p1 or ins_p2. The outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions. We will use if-else statements to handle the different conditions and assignments to update the registers and outputs accordingly.
