The instr_reg module will be designed as a synchronous digital circuit with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module will have a clock signal (clk) for synchronization, an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers, a control signal (fetch) to determine the source of the instruction, and an 8-bit data input (data) to represent the instruction to be fetched. The module will output the high 3 bits of the instruction (ins), the low 5 bits of the instruction (ad1), and the full 8-bit data from the second source (ad2). The registers will be updated on the rising edge of the clock based on the fetch signal.
