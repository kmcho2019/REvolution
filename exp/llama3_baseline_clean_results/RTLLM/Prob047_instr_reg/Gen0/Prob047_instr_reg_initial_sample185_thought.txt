To design the instruction register module 'instr_reg', we need to consider the following steps:
- Create two 8-bit registers 'ins_p1' and 'ins_p2' to store instructions from different sources.
- Use the 'rst' signal to reset both registers to zero at the rising edge of the clock 'clk'.
- Implement the logic for storing instructions into 'ins_p1' and 'ins_p2' based on the 'fetch' signal.
- Derive the outputs 'ins', 'ad1', and 'ad2' from the stored instructions in 'ins_p1'.
- Use always block with posedge condition to capture the clock and reset signals.
- Use if-else statement to handle different conditions of the 'fetch' signal.
