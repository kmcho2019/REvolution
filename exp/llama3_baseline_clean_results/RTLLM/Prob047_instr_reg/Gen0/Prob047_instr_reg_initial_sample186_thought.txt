To design the instr_reg module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use a reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers to zero on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when rst is low. The fetch signal will determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. We will then derive the output signals ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions.
