To design the instruction register module (instr_reg), we first need to declare the input and output ports according to the given specifications. We will use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. The module's behavior will be controlled by the fetch signal and the clock (clk) and reset (rst) signals for synchronization and initialization, respectively. Based on the fetch signal, the module will load the incoming instruction into the appropriate register. The outputs (ins, ad1, ad2) will be derived from the stored instructions. The ins output will take the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, ad1 will take the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and ad2 will be the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
