To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will use the fetch signal to determine which register to update on the rising edge of the clock. The ins output will be derived from the high 3 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, the ad1 output will be derived from the low 5 bits of the instruction in ins_p1, and the ad2 output will be derived from the full 8-bit data in ins_p2. We will also implement an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize both registers to zero when it is low.
