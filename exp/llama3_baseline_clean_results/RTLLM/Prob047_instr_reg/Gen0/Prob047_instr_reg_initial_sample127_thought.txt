To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to capture incoming instructions from various sources and separate them into distinct fields for further processing. The module should have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. Based on the fetch signal, the instruction is fetched from the data input into either ins_p1 or ins_p2. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are derived from the stored instructions in ins_p1. We will use a clock signal (clk) for synchronization and an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the register.
