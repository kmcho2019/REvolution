To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to consider the clock signal (clk) and the reset signal (rst) for synchronization and initialization. The fetch signal determines which register (ins_p1 or ins_p2) to load with the incoming instruction (data). 
We will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. 
The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions based on their bit positions.
The module will have a conditional statement to check the fetch signal and update the registers accordingly.
We will also handle the reset condition to initialize the registers when the rst signal is low.
