To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. We will use a case statement to determine which register to update based on the fetch signal. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, both registers will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the registers will be updated according to the fetch signal. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 will be derived from the stored instructions.
