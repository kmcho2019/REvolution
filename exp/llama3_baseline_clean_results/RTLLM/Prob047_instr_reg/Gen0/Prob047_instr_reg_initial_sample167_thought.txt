To implement the instruction register module, we will first define the module with its input and output ports. We will then use two 8-bit registers to store instructions from different sources. The clock signal will be used to synchronize the operations, and the reset signal will be used to initialize the registers. Based on the fetch signal, we will either load new instructions into the registers or retain the previous values. Finally, we will derive the output signals from the stored instructions. The opcode or operation (ins) will be the high 3 bits of the instruction, the register address (ad1) will be the low 5 bits of the instruction, and the full 8-bit data from the second source (ad2) will be the instruction stored in the second register.
