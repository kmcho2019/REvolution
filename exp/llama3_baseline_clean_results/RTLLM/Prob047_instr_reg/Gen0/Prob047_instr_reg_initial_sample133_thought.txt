The instruction register module (instr_reg) will be designed as a digital circuit with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. It will utilize a clock signal (clk) for synchronization, an active low reset signal (rst) to initialize the registers, and a control signal (fetch) to determine the source of the instruction. The module will capture incoming instructions from various sources, separate them into distinct fields, and output the opcode (ins), register address (ad1), and the full 8-bit data from the second source (ad2).
