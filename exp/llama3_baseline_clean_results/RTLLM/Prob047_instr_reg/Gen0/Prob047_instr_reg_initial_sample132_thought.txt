To implement the instruction register module, we will first define the module and its ports according to the given specifications. Then, we will declare two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store the instructions from different sources. We will use an always block to capture the rising edge of the clock and update the registers based on the fetch signal. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions by bit-slicing and assigning them to the respective output ports. We will also include a reset mechanism to initialize the registers when the reset signal is low.
