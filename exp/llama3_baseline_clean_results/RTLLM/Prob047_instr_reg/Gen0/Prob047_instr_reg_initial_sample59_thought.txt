To design the instr_reg module, we need to create two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. 
We will use the fetch signal to control which register to load with the incoming data. 
The ins output will be derived from the high 3 bits of ins_p1, the ad1 output from the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and the ad2 output from the full 8 bits of ins_p2.
We will use a reset signal (rst) to initialize both registers to zero on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the reset signal is low.
We will use a case statement or if-else conditions to implement the control logic based on the fetch signal.
