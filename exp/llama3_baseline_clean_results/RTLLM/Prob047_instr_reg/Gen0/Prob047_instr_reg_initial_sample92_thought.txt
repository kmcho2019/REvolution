To design the instr_reg module, we'll use two 8-bit registers, ins_p1 and ins_p2, to store instructions from different sources. We'll utilize the fetch signal to determine which register to update with the incoming instruction. On the rising edge of the clock, we'll check the reset signal and initialize both registers if it's low. Otherwise, we'll update the registers based on the fetch signal. Finally, we'll derive the output signals ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions.
