To design the instruction register module, we will first create two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We will then use the fetch signal to determine which register to update with the incoming data on the rising edge of the clock. Based on the stored instructions, we will derive the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 by extracting the corresponding bits. We will also implement the reset functionality to initialize both registers to zero when the reset signal is low.
