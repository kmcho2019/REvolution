To implement the instruction register module, I will design a Verilog module named 'instr_reg' with the specified input and output ports. The module will contain two 8-bit registers, 'ins_p1' and 'ins_p2', to store instructions from different sources. Based on the 'fetch' signal, the module will capture incoming instructions and update the registers accordingly. The outputs 'ins', 'ad1', and 'ad2' will be derived from the stored instructions. The module will be synchronized with the 'clk' signal and reset by the 'rst' signal.
