To implement the instruction register module (instr_reg), we need to design a Verilog module that can capture and process incoming CPU instructions. This involves creating two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. The module should be sensitive to the clock signal (clk) and reset signal (rst). Based on the fetch signal, it should update the registers accordingly and derive the outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 from the stored instructions. The ins output should be the high 3 bits of the instruction, indicating the opcode or operation, ad1 should be the low 5 bits of the instruction, representing the register address, and ad2 should be the full 8-bit data from the second source.
