The instruction register module (instr_reg) will be designed as a sequential digital circuit with two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. It will capture incoming instructions from various sources based on the fetch control signal and separate them into distinct fields (ins, ad1, ad2) for further processing. The design will be implemented using Verilog HDL, utilizing always blocks for combinational and sequential logic.
