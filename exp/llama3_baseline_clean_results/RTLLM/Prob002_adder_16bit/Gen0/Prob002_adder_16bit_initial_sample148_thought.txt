To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we can design a smaller 8-bit full adder module and then instantiate it twice to form the 16-bit adder. Each 8-bit adder will handle 8 bits of the input operands. We'll use the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder to ensure proper carry propagation. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design process and reduces the complexity of the overall circuit.
