To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can design a smaller 8-bit full adder and then instantiate it twice to form the 16-bit adder. The smaller 8-bit adder will be used to add the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits of the input operands A and B. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the upper 8-bit adder. This hierarchical approach allows us to simplify the design and make it more manageable.
