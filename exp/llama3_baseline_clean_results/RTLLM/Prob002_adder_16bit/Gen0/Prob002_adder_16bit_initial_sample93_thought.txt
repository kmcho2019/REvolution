To design a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we can start by creating a smaller 8-bit full adder module and then instantiate this module twice in the main 16-bit adder module. The first 8-bit adder will handle the least significant 8 bits of the inputs, and the second 8-bit adder will handle the most significant 8 bits of the inputs. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and makes it more manageable.
