To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This smaller adder will be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module. The first 8-bit adder will handle the least significant 8 bits of the input operands A and B, and the second 8-bit adder will handle the most significant 8 bits. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. This approach allows us to break down the problem into smaller, more manageable parts, and then combine them to achieve the desired functionality.
