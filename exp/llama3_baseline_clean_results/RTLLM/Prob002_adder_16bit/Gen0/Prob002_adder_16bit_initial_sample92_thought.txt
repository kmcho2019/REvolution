To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can first design an 8-bit full adder and then use two instances of it to build the 16-bit adder. Each 8-bit adder can handle the lower and upper 8 bits of the 16-bit operands, and the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder can be connected to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder to properly handle the carry propagation. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design process and reduces the complexity of the combinational logic.
