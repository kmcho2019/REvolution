To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. The 8-bit adder will be used as a building block for the 16-bit adder. The 8-bit adder will have input ports for two 8-bit operands and a carry-in bit, and output ports for an 8-bit sum and a carry-out bit. We will then instantiate this 8-bit adder twice in the 16-bit adder module, chaining them together to form a 16-bit adder. This approach allows for a hierarchical design and easier handling of carry propagation.
