To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first create a smaller 8-bit full adder module. This 8-bit adder will be designed using the same principle but with 8 bits instead of 16. The 16-bit adder will then instantiate two of these 8-bit adders, one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will serve as the carry-in for the upper 8-bit adder, thus chaining them together to form a 16-bit full adder.
