To implement a 16-bit full adder, we first need to design a smaller, more manageable adder, such as an 8-bit adder. This 8-bit adder can then be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module. Each 8-bit adder will handle half of the bits from the two input operands. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits). This approach simplifies the design by breaking it down into smaller, more manageable components.
