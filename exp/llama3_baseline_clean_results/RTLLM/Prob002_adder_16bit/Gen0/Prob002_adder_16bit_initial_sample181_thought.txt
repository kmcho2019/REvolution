To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit full adder will be used as a building block and instantiated twice in the 16-bit full adder module. The 8-bit adder will take two 8-bit inputs and a carry-in, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out. In the 16-bit full adder, the first 8-bit adder will add the lower 8 bits of the two input numbers and the carry-in. The second 8-bit adder will add the upper 8 bits of the input numbers and the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder. The carry-out of the second 8-bit adder will be the final carry-out of the 16-bit full adder.
