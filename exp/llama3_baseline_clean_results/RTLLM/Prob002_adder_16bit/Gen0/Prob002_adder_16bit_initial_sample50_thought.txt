To design a 16-bit full adder, we can create a smaller 8-bit full adder module and instantiate it twice. The first 8-bit adder will handle the lower 8 bits of the input operands, and the second 8-bit adder will handle the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder will be connected to the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder. This way, we can efficiently design a 16-bit full adder by reusing the 8-bit adder module.
