To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can start by designing an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit adder can then be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module to handle the two 8-bit halves of the 16-bit input operands. The carry-out of the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits) will be connected to the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits), thus propagating the carry from the lower bits to the upper bits. This approach simplifies the design and leverages the modularity of hardware description languages like Verilog.
