To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder module. This 8-bit adder will be instantiated twice to create the 16-bit adder. The 8-bit adder module will take two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produce an 8-bit output and a carry-out. We will then use two of these 8-bit adders to create the 16-bit adder, handling the carry propagation from the lower 8 bits to the upper 8 bits.
