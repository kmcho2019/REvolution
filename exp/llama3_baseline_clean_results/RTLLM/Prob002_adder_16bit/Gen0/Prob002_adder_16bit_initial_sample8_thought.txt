To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we'll first design an 8-bit full adder module, which will be instantiated twice to form the 16-bit adder. Each 8-bit adder will handle half of the 16-bit input. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits). This way, the carry chain is properly propagated through the entire 16-bit operation.
