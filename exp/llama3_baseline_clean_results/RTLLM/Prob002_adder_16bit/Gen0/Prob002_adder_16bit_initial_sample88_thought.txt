To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit adder can then be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module. The first 8-bit adder will add the lower 8 bits of the two input operands (a[7:0] and b[7:0]) along with the carry-in input (Cin). The second 8-bit adder will add the upper 8 bits of the two input operands (a[15:8] and b[15:8]) along with the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder. The carry-out from the second 8-bit adder will be the carry-out of the 16-bit adder. This approach allows us to reuse the 8-bit adder design, simplifying the implementation.
