To design a 16-bit full adder, we will first create a smaller, 8-bit full adder module (adder_8bit) that can be instantiated multiple times. The 8-bit adder module itself will be composed of multiple 1-bit full adder modules (adder_1bit). The 1-bit full adder module will take two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The 8-bit adder will then be used in the 16-bit adder module, where two instances of the 8-bit adder will be used to handle the lower and upper 8 bits of the 16-bit input operands, with the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder serving as the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder. This approach allows for a hierarchical and modular design.
