To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can break down the problem into smaller parts. First, we will design an 8-bit full adder module. Then, we will use this 8-bit adder module to construct the 16-bit adder. The idea is to cascade two 8-bit adders where the carry-out of the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits of the inputs) is used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits of the inputs). This way, we can efficiently compute the sum and the final carry-out for the 16-bit addition.
