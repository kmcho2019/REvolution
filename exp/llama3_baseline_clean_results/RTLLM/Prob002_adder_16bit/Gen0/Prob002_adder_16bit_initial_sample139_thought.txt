To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can break it down into smaller, manageable parts. One approach is to create a smaller 8-bit adder and then instantiate it twice to form the 16-bit adder. This smaller 8-bit adder itself can be designed using another even smaller adder, such as a 1-bit full adder, but for simplicity, we can directly design the 8-bit adder using bitwise operations in Verilog. The 16-bit adder will then be constructed by cascading two 8-bit adders. The carry-out of the first 8-bit adder (which adds the lower 8 bits of the inputs) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which adds the upper 8 bits of the inputs). This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and reduces complexity.
