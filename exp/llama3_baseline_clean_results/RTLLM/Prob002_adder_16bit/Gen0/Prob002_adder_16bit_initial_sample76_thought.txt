To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit adder will be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module. The first 8-bit adder will handle the lower 8 bits of the input operands (a[7:0] and b[7:0]), and the second 8-bit adder will handle the upper 8 bits (a[15:8] and b[15:8]). The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. The final carry-out will be the carry-out of the second 8-bit adder.
