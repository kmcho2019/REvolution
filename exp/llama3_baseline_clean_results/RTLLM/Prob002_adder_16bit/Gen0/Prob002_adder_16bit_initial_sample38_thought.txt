To design a 16-bit full adder, we can first create a smaller 8-bit full adder module and then instantiate it twice within the 16-bit adder module. The 8-bit adder will handle the lower and upper 8 bits of the 16-bit operands separately. We will also need to propagate the carry from the lower 8-bit addition to the upper 8-bit addition. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of the overall adder.
