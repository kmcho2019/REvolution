To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, we will first design a smaller 8-bit adder module and then instantiate it twice in the main adder_16bit module to handle the 16-bit addition. The idea is to break down the problem into smaller, manageable parts. Each 8-bit adder will handle 8 bits of the inputs, and the carry from the first 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. This approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of handling 16 bits at once.
