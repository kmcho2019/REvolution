To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we first need to design a smaller bit-width adder, such as an 8-bit adder. This 8-bit adder can then be instantiated multiple times to form the 16-bit adder. The 8-bit adder itself can be built using the basic full adder cell, which adds two bits along with a carry-in to produce a sum bit and a carry-out. By cascading these basic cells, we can form the 8-bit adder, and subsequently, the 16-bit adder by combining two 8-bit adders with appropriate carry handling.
