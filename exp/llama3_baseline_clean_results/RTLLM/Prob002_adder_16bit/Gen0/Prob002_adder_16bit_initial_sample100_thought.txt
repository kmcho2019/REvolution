To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit full adder will then be instantiated twice in the 16-bit full adder module to handle the two 8-bit halves of the 16-bit input operands. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits of the input operands) will be connected to the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits of the input operands). This way, the carry propagation from the lower 8 bits to the upper 8 bits is correctly handled.
