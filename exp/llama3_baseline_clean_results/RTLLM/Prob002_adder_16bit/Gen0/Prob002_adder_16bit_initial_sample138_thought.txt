To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder module. This 8-bit adder will be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder module, once for the lower 8 bits and once for the upper 8 bits, with the carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder connected to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder. The 8-bit adder itself can be designed using a combination of half adders or by directly implementing the full adder logic for each bit.
