To design a 16-bit full adder, we will first create a smaller 8-bit full adder module, which can be easily instantiated to form the larger 16-bit adder. This approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of the overall implementation. The 8-bit adder itself can be composed of two 4-bit adders or directly from full adder cells (1-bit adders). For simplicity and minimal propagation delay, we'll consider using 1-bit full adders. Each 1-bit full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum and a carry-out. The 8-bit adder will then be used as a building block to form the 16-bit adder by cascading two 8-bit adders.
