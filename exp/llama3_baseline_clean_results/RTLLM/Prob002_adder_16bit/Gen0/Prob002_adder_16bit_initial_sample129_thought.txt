To implement a 16-bit full adder, we first need to design a smaller, basic adder module, in this case, an 8-bit adder. This 8-bit adder will then be instantiated twice within the 16-bit adder to handle the two halves of the 16-bit input operands. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (handling the lower 8 bits) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (handling the upper 8 bits). This approach ensures that the carry propagation is correctly handled across the entire 16-bit addition operation.
