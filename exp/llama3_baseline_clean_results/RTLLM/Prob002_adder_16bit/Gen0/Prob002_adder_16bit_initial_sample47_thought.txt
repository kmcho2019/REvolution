To design a 16-bit full adder, we can break it down into smaller 8-bit adders, which can then be combined to achieve the 16-bit addition. This approach simplifies the design and minimizes the amount of code needed. We will first create an 8-bit full adder module and then instantiate two of these modules in the 16-bit adder. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which adds the lower 8 bits of the operands) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which adds the upper 8 bits of the operands). This ensures that the carry propagates correctly through the entire 16-bit addition.
