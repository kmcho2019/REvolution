To design a 16-bit full adder, we can first create a smaller, 8-bit full adder module. This module will take two 8-bit inputs, a carry-in, and produce an 8-bit output along with a carry-out. We can then instantiate this 8-bit adder module twice within the 16-bit adder, connecting them in a way that the carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder becomes the carry-in for the higher 8-bit adder. This way, we efficiently utilize the smaller adder to build the larger one, following a hierarchical design approach.
