To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, I will first create a smaller, 8-bit full adder module. This 8-bit adder will be designed using the basic principle of a full adder, where the sum of two bits and a carry-in produces a sum bit and a carry-out. The 16-bit adder will then be constructed by instantiating two of these 8-bit adders in a hierarchical manner, connecting them in a way that allows the carry from the first 8-bit adder to be passed into the second 8-bit adder as the carry-in. This approach allows for a modular and efficient design.
