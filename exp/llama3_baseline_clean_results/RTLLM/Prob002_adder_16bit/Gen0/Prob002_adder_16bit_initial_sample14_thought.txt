To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can design a smaller 8-bit full adder and then instantiate it twice to create the 16-bit adder. The 8-bit adder will be used for the lower and upper 8 bits of the input operands. We will also need to connect the carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder to the carry-in of the upper 8-bit adder to propagate the carry signal. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of the combinational logic.
