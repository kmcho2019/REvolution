To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we can create a smaller 8-bit adder module and then instantiate it twice within the 16-bit adder module. This 8-bit adder will take care of the lower 8 bits and the upper 8 bits of the 16-bit input separately. The carry-out of the lower 8-bit adder will serve as the carry-in for the upper 8-bit adder. This approach allows us to efficiently reuse the 8-bit adder design, simplifying the implementation of the 16-bit adder.
