To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design a small bit-width adder (8-bit adder) and then instantiate it multiple times in the main adder_16bit module. This approach allows for better modularity and reusability of the code. The 8-bit adder will be designed using a simple full adder cell for each bit position, where each cell will have two input bits and a carry-in input, and will produce a sum bit and a carry-out. 
We can use a similar hierarchical approach to combine two of these 8-bit adders with an additional full adder cell to handle the carry between the two halves.
This way, the overall 16-bit addition is performed by combining the results of two 8-bit additions.
