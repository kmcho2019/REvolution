To implement a 16-bit full adder using combinational logic, we first need to design a smaller, manageable adder, in this case, an 8-bit adder. This 8-bit adder will be instantiated twice to handle the 16-bit input. Each 8-bit adder will handle 8 bits of the input operands (A and B) and their respective carry-in signals. The carry-out of the first 8-bit adder will serve as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. This approach allows us to break down the complex 16-bit addition into more manageable parts, leveraging the concept of hierarchical design in digital logic.
