To design a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we can create a smaller 8-bit full adder and then instantiate it twice in the main 16-bit adder module. Each 8-bit adder will handle half of the 16-bit input operands. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits). This approach allows us to build a larger adder from smaller, more manageable components, following a hierarchical design methodology.
