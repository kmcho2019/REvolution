To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design a smaller, 8-bit full adder module. This module will be instantiated twice within the 16-bit full adder to handle the most significant 8 bits and the least significant 8 bits separately. Each 8-bit adder will produce a sum and a carry-out. The carry-out from the least significant 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the most significant 8-bit adder. This approach allows us to break down the problem into smaller, more manageable parts and then combine them to achieve the desired 16-bit addition functionality.
