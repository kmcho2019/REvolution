To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will design a smaller 8-bit adder module first. This smaller module will be instantiated twice within the main 16-bit adder module. Each 8-bit adder will handle the lower and upper 8 bits of the input operands A and B. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will be connected as the carry-in to the upper 8-bit adder. This approach will effectively create a ripple-carry adder architecture for the 16-bit operation. We will also ensure proper connection of carry-in and carry-out signals to maintain the full adder functionality.
