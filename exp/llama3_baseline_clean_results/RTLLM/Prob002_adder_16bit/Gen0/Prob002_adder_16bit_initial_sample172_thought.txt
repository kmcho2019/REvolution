To implement a 16-bit full adder, we first need to design a smaller bit-width adder, such as an 8-bit adder, and then instantiate it multiple times to achieve the desired 16-bit operation. The 8-bit adder will be responsible for adding two 8-bit numbers and handling the carry-in and carry-out. We'll use this 8-bit adder as a building block to create the 16-bit adder by instantiating it twice: once for the lower 8 bits and once for the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the lower 8-bit adder will serve as the carry-in for the upper 8-bit adder, ensuring proper carry propagation.
