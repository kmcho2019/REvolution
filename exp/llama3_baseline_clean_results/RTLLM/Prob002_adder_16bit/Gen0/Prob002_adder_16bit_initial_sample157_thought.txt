To implement a 16-bit full adder, we first need to design a smaller, more manageable adder, such as an 8-bit full adder. This 8-bit adder will then be instantiated twice within the 16-bit adder module. Each 8-bit adder will handle half of the 16-bit operands. The first 8-bit adder will add the lower 8 bits of the two operands along with the carry-in input. The second 8-bit adder will add the upper 8 bits of the two operands along with the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and makes it more modular.
