To implement a 16-bit full adder, we will first design an 8-bit full adder. This smaller adder will be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder, with the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (which handles the lower 8 bits) connected to the carry-in of the second 8-bit adder (which handles the upper 8 bits). Each 8-bit adder will be designed using the standard full adder logic but applied bit-wise across 8 bits. The final carry-out from the second 8-bit adder will serve as the overall carry-out of the 16-bit adder.
