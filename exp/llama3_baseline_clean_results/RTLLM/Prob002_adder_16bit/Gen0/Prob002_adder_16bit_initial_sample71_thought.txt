To design a 16-bit full adder, we can start by creating a smaller 8-bit full adder module. This module will be instantiated twice in the 16-bit adder to handle the two 8-bit halves of the 16-bit operands. Each 8-bit adder will take two 8-bit operands and a carry-in input, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out output. The carry-out of the first 8-bit adder (handling the lower 8 bits) will serve as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (handling the upper 8 bits). This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and reduces complexity.
