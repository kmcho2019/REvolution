To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first create a smaller 8-bit full adder module. This 8-bit adder will be instantiated twice within the 16-bit adder module. Each 8-bit adder will handle 8 bits of the input operands A and B. The carry-out from the first 8-bit adder (handling the lower 8 bits) will be used as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder (handling the upper 8 bits). This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and makes it easier to understand and test.
