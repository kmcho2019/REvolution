To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first design a smaller, 8-bit full adder. This smaller adder will be instantiated twice within the 16-bit adder module. The first instance will handle the lower 8 bits of the input operands, and the second instance will handle the upper 8 bits. The carry-out from the first instance will be used as the carry-in for the second instance. This approach allows us to easily extend the adder to 16 bits while minimizing the complexity of the design.
