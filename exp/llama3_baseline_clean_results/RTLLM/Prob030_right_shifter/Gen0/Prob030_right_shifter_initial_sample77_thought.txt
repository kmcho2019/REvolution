To implement a right shifter in Verilog, we will create a module named right_shifter with input ports clk and d, and an output port q. We will use a reg [7:0] q to store the shifted bits and initialize it to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will shift the contents of q to the right by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. However, the direct assignment of q[7] <= d after the right shift operation would not work as expected because in Verilog, the right shift operation (q >> 1) does not directly allow us to insert a bit into the most significant position in the same statement. Instead, we should first perform the right shift and then assign the new bit to the most significant position.
