The right_shifter module will be implemented as a sequential circuit with synchronous operation, utilizing the rising edge of the clock signal for shifting and updating the register q. The q register will be defined as an 8-bit register and initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will perform a right shift operation on the q register by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position. This will be achieved using the >> operator for right shift and assignment statement for updating the most significant bit.
