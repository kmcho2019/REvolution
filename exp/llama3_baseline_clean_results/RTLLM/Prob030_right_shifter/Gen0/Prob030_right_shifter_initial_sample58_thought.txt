The right shifter module will be implemented as a sequential circuit with a clock signal (clk) controlling the shift operation. On each rising edge of the clock, the module shifts the contents of the q register to the right by one bit and inserts the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. We will use the always block to describe the sequential behavior, and the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to assign the new value to the q register. The initial statement will be used to initialize the q register to 0.
