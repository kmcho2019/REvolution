The right_shifter module will be designed with an 8-bit register q. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will perform a right shift operation on the q register by shifting its contents one bit to the right and inserting the new input bit d into the most significant position. This is achieved using the always block to capture the rising edge of the clock and the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the register q. The right shift operation is performed using the >> operator, and the most significant bit of the register is assigned the value of the input signal d.
