The module right_shifter will be designed with an 8-bit register q that is initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock signal clk, the value of q will be right-shifted by one bit and the most significant bit will be assigned the value of the input signal d. This is achieved by using the always block to capture the rising edge of the clock, and then using the shift operator (>>) to right-shift the register q. After the shift operation, the most significant bit of q (q[7]) will be assigned the value of d.
