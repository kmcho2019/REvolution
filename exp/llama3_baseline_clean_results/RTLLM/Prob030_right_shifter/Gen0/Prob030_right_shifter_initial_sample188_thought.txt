The right_shifter module will be designed with an 8-bit register q that is initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will right shift the contents of q by one bit, inserting the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. This will be achieved using a non-blocking assignment for the right shift operation (q <= (q >> 1)) and a separate non-blocking assignment to insert the new bit (q[7] <= d) within a clocked always block.
