The module will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits, initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will perform a right shift operation on 'q' and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'. This will be achieved using the always block with a sensitivity list including the clock signal, and utilizing the right shift operator (>>).
