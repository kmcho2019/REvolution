To implement the right shifter module, we will first initialize an 8-bit register `q` to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will perform a right shift operation on the `q` register by shifting its contents to the right by one bit and then insert the new input bit `d` into the most significant position of the register. This is achieved using the always block with a sensitivity to the clock signal and the non-blocking assignment operator (`<=`) for updating the register value synchronously.
