To implement the right shifter module, we will use a register (q) to store the current state of the shift operation. On each rising edge of the clock, we will perform a right shift operation on the register (q) by one bit and insert the new input bit (d) into the most significant position of the register. This can be achieved using a non-blocking assignment (<=) in a always block that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock (posedge clk).
