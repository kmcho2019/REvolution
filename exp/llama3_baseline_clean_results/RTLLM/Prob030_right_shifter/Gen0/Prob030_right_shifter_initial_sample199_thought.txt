The right_shifter module will be designed as a synchronous sequential circuit, utilizing the clk signal for operation. An 8-bit register q is initialized to 0. On each rising edge of clk, the module right-shifts the contents of q by 1 bit and inserts the input signal d into the most significant position of the register. This is achieved by first shifting the contents of q to the right by 1 bit using the >> operator and then assigning the value of d to the most significant bit of q. However, a straightforward approach to shift and then insert would require a temporary variable or a more complex single-line assignment. Instead, we can directly construct the new value of q by shifting the current value and then assigning d to the most significant bit. The key is to remember that in Verilog, the >> operator performs a logical shift, filling the most significant bits with zeros.
