To implement the right shifter module, we first define the module with the given name and ports. We then declare the register q as an 8-bit reg and initialize it to 0. Inside the always block, which is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, we shift the contents of q to the right by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position. This is achieved by using the right shift operator (>>) to shift the bits of q and then assigning the value of d to the most significant bit of q.
