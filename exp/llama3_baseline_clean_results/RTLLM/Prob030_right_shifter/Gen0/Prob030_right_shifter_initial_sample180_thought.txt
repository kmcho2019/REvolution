To design the right shifter module, we first need to define the module and its ports. The module name is 'right_shifter' and it has three ports: 'clk' for the clock signal, 'd' for the input signal, and 'q' for the output signal. We will use a reg [7:0] to store the output and initialize it to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will right shift the contents of the register by one bit and insert the new input bit into the most significant position of the register.
