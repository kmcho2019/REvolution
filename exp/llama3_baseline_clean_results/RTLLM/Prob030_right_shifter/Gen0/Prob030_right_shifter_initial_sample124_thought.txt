To implement the right shifter module, we need to first initialize the 8-bit register q to 0. Then, on each rising edge of the clock, we shift the contents of q to the right by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. This can be achieved using the always block in Verilog, which is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal. Within this block, we first shift the register q to the right by one bit and then assign the input signal d to the most significant bit of q.
