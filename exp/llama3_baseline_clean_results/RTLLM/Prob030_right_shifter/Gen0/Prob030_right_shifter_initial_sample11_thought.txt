To implement the right shifter module, we need to define a register 'q' of size 8 bits and initialize it to 0. On every rising edge of the clock, we will right shift the contents of 'q' by one bit and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'. We can achieve this by using the always block with a posedge condition for the clock and performing the shift operation using the >> operator. The input 'd' will be assigned to the most significant bit of 'q' after the shift.
