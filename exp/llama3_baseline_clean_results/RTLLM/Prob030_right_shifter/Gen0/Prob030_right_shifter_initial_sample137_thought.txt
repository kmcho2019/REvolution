The design of the right shifter module involves creating a register that shifts its contents to the right by one bit on each rising edge of the clock and inserts the new input bit into the most significant position. This can be achieved using a combination of the initial statement to initialize the register, the always block to handle the clock signal, and the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the register value. The >> operator will be used for the right shift operation, and bit assignment will be used to insert the new input bit.
