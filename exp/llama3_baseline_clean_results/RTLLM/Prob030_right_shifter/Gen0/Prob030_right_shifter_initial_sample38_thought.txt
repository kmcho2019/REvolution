To implement the right shifter module in Verilog, we will define the module right_shifter with input ports clk and d, and output port q. The q register will be defined as an 8-bit reg and initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will shift the contents of q to the right by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. This will be achieved using a non-blocking assignment to prevent race conditions and ensure synchronous operation.
