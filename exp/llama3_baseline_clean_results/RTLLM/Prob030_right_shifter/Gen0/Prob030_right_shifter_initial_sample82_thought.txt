To implement the right shifter module, we will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits, initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will shift the contents of 'q' one bit to the right and assign the most significant bit of 'q' the value of the input signal 'd'. This is achieved by using the always block for clock edge detection and the shift operator (>>) to right shift the 'q' register. After shifting, we assign the input 'd' to the most significant bit of 'q'.
