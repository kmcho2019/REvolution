To implement the right shifter module, we will use a Verilog module with a clock signal (clk) for synchronous operation, an input signal (d) to be shifted, and an output signal (q) representing the result of the shift operation. The q register will be initialized to 0 and will be updated on each rising edge of the clock by shifting its contents to the right by one bit and inserting the new input bit into the most significant position.
