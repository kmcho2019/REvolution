To implement the right shifter module in Verilog, we will use a sequential design approach. The module will have a clock signal (clk) for synchronous operation and an input signal (d) to be right-shifted. The output of the module will be an 8-bit signal (q) representing the result of the right shift operation. We will initialize the q register to 0 and on each rising edge of the clock, we will right shift the contents of the q register by one bit and insert the new input bit (d) into the most significant position of the register.
