The right shifter module will be designed using a register 'q' of size 8 bits. The module will be synchronous, meaning it will operate on the rising edge of the clock. At each rising edge, the module will shift the contents of 'q' one bit to the right and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'. This can be achieved using a combination of the right shift operator (>>) and bit assignment.
