The right_shifter module will be designed as a sequential circuit, utilizing a clock signal (clk) for synchronous operation. It will take an input signal (d) and perform an 8-bit right shift operation. The q register will be initialized to 0 and updated on each rising edge of the clock. The update will involve shifting the current value of q to the right by one bit and inserting the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register.
