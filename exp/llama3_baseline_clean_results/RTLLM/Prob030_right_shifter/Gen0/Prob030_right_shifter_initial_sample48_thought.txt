The module should have an output signal 'q' representing the result of the right shift operation. We will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits and initialize it to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will shift the contents of 'q' to the right by one bit and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'.
