To implement the right shifter module, we'll use a register 'q' that is 8 bits wide and initialize it to 0. On every rising edge of the clock 'clk', we'll right shift the contents of 'q' by one bit and then assign the input 'd' to the most significant bit of 'q'. This process effectively shifts the input 'd' into the 'q' register from the most significant bit position, achieving the desired right shift operation over time. The output 'q' will represent the result of the right shift operation.
