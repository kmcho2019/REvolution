To implement the right shifter module, we will first initialize an 8-bit register 'q' to 0. On each rising edge of the clock 'clk', we will shift the contents of 'q' one bit to the right and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'. This will be achieved using a non-blocking assignment within a sequential always block, ensuring synchronous operation with respect to 'clk'.
