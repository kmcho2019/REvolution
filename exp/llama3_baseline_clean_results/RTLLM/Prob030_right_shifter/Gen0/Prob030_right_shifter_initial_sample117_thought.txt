To implement the right shifter, we will define a module named 'right_shifter' with input ports 'clk' and 'd', and an output port 'q'. The 'q' register will be initialized to 0 and then on each rising edge of the clock, its value will be right-shifted by 1 bit, and the most significant bit will be updated with the new input 'd'. We will use a always block with a posedge condition to capture the clock signal and perform the shift operation.
