The output port 'q' will represent the result of the right shift operation. We will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits, initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will shift the contents of 'q' to the right by one bit using the '>>' operator and then insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'.
