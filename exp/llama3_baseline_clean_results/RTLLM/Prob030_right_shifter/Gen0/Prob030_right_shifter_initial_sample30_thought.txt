To implement the right shifter module, we'll define a Verilog module named 'right_shifter' with input ports 'clk' and 'd', and an output port 'q'. The module will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits, initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, the module will shift the contents of 'q' one bit to the right and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of 'q'. This will be achieved using the always block with a clock edge condition, and the right shift operator '>>' to shift the bits of 'q'.
