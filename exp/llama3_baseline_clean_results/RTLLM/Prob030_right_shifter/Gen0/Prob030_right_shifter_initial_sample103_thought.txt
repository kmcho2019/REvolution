To implement the right shifter module, we first initialize an 8-bit register q to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we right shift the contents of the register q by one bit and insert the new input bit d into the most significant position of the register. This can be achieved using a synchronous always block in Verilog. Inside the block, we check for the rising edge of the clock and then perform the right shift operation on the register q and assign the new input bit d to the most significant bit of q.
