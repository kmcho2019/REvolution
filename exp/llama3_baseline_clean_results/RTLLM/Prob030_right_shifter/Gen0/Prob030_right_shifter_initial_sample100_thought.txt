To implement the right shifter module, we will utilize a register to store the current state of the shift operation. The module will be designed to shift the register's contents one bit to the right on each rising edge of the clock and insert the new input bit into the most significant position. This operation will be achieved through the use of a non-blocking assignment to the register within a always block that is sensitive to the clock's rising edge. The new input bit will be assigned to the most significant bit of the register before the shift operation.
