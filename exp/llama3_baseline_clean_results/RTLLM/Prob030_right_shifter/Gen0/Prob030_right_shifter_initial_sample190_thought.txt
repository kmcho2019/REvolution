The right shifter module can be implemented using a register 'q' of size 8 bits. On every rising edge of the clock, the value of 'q' is shifted one bit to the right, and the most significant bit of 'q' is replaced with the input signal 'd'. We can use the always block with the posedge of the clock to achieve this. Inside the always block, we first shift the value of 'q' one bit to the right and then replace the most significant bit of 'q' with 'd'. However, since we cannot directly modify the bits of 'q' after shifting, we should assign 'd' to the most significant bit of 'q' before shifting.
