To implement the right shifter module, we will define a module named 'right_shifter' with input ports 'clk' and 'd', and output port 'q'. We will use a register 'q' of size 8 bits, initialized to 0. On each rising edge of the clock, we will right shift the contents of 'q' by one bit and insert the new input bit 'd' into the most significant position of the register.
