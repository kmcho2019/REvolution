To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we can utilize a shift-and-add approach. The multiplicand (A) is shifted and added to a running product based on the bits of the multiplier (B). The process starts from the least significant bit of B and moves towards the most significant bit. For each '1' bit in B, A (shifted accordingly to its position) is added to the product. This method simulates the traditional long multiplication process but is more efficient in hardware implementation due to the use of shifts and adds, which are basic operations in digital logic. The final product, which can be up to 16 bits wide, is accumulated in the output port.
