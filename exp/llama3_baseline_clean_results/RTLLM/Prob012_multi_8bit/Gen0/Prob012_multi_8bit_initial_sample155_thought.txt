To implement the 8-bit multiplier module 'multi_8bit', we'll use the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. The multiplicand (A) is logically shifted left by one bit after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port 'product'.
