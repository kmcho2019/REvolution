To implement the 8-bit multiplier module named "multi_8bit", we will use the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit (1), adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand (A) is logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. The process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product will be stored in a 16-bit output port to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers.
