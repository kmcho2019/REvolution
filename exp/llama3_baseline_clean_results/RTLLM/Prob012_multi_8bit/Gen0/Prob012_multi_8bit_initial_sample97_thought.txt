To implement the 8-bit multiplier module using shifting and adding operations, we need to iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and add the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever a bit is set. We will use a temporary variable to hold the product and update it in each iteration. The multiplicand will be shifted left by one bit in each iteration to prepare for the next addition. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
