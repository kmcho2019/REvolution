To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This method involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand (A) will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. The process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product will be stored in a 16-bit output port to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers. The module will be designed using a combinational logic approach to perform the multiplication in a single clock cycle or without any clock dependency.
