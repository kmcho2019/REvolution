To implement the 8-bit multiplier module 'multi_8bit', we will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output, 'product', which represents the result of multiplying A and B. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier B, and for each bit set to 1, we will add the multiplicand A (shifted appropriately based on the bit position) to the product. After each addition, we will shift the multiplicand A one bit to the left to prepare for the next iteration. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier B have been processed, resulting in the final product.
