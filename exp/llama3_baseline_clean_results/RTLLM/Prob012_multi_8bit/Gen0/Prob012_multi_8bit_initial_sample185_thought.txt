To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The algorithm will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, it will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. We will use a loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier, shifting the multiplicand left by one bit after each addition. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
