To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of shifting and adding operations. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit in each iteration, and if the corresponding bit in the multiplier (B) is set, the shifted multiplicand will be added to the product. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product.
