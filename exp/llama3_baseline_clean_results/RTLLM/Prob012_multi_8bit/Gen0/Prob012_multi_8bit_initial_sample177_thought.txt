To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add multiplication algorithm. We start with the least significant bit (LSB) of the multiplier (B) and move towards the most significant bit (MSB). For each bit set in the multiplier, we add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the corresponding bit position to the product. After processing each bit, we shift the multiplicand left by one bit for the next iteration. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port.
