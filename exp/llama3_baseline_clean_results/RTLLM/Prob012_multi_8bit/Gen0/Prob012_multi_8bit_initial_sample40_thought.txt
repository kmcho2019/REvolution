To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add algorithm. The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output, product. We'll iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, we'll add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, we'll shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will be repeated until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port.
