To implement the 8-bit multiplier module (multi_8bit) based on shifting and adding operations, we can utilize a combination of shifting and addition to achieve the multiplication. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit that is set (1), we will add the multiplicand (A) shifted to the corresponding position to the product. This process will be repeated until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port (product).
