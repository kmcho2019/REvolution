To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of shifting and adding operations. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit in each iteration, and if the corresponding bit in the multiplier (B) is 1, the shifted multiplicand will be added to the product. This process will be repeated for all bits in the multiplier, resulting in the final product. We will utilize a for loop to iterate through each bit of the multiplier and a temporary variable to accumulate the product.
