To implement the 8-bit multiplier module (multi_8bit), we will utilize the shift-and-add method. The process starts with the least significant bit (LSB) of the multiplier (B) and moves towards the most significant bit (MSB). For each '1' bit encountered in the multiplier, the multiplicand (A) is added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. This position is determined by the current bit's position in the multiplier, which dictates how many places the multiplicand should be shifted left. The multiplicand is logically shifted left by one bit after each addition to prepare for the next iteration, simulating the traditional multiplication process. This iterative process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in a 16-bit product stored in the output port (product).
