To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize a behavioral approach, using a combination of loops and conditional statements to perform the shift-and-add multiplication algorithm. The design will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), checking for set bits and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product accordingly, shifting A left after each iteration. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port.
