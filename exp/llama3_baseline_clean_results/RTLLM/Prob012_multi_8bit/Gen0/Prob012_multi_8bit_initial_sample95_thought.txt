To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow the shift-and-add method. The process starts by initializing the product to zero. Then, for each bit in the multiplier (B), if the bit is set (1), the multiplicand (A) is added to the product. After each addition, the multiplicand (A) is shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product is then stored in the 16-bit output port.
