To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize a combination of shifting and adding operations. The design will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), checking if the bit is set. If the bit is set, the multiplicand (A) will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. We will utilize a 16-bit wide output port to store the final product, accommodating the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process.
