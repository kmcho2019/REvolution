To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll use a combination of conditional statements and shift operations in Verilog. We will iterate over each bit of the multiplier (B) and check if the bit is set. If the bit is set, we add the multiplicand (A) shifted by the current bit position to the product. After processing each bit, we shift the multiplicand one position to the left for the next iteration. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the output port.
