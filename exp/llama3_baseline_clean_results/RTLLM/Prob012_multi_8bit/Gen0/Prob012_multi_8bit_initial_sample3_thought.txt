To implement the 8-bit multiplier module 'multi_8bit', we will utilize a combinational logic approach with a shift-and-add algorithm. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left and added to the product whenever a '1' is encountered in the multiplier (B), effectively performing the multiplication operation. This process will be achieved through the use of a loop that iterates over each bit of the multiplier, and a conditional statement to check for '1's in the multiplier.
