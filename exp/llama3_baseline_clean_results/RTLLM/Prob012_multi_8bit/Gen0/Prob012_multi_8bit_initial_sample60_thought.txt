To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the product to zero.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit that is set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After processing all bits of the multiplier, the final product will be stored in the output port.

We will use a for loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier and perform the shift-and-add operation accordingly.
