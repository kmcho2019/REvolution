To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize a combination of shifting and adding operations. The multiplier will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and check if the bit is set. If the bit is set, the multiplicand (A) will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product will be stored in the output port, which is 16 bits wide to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers.
