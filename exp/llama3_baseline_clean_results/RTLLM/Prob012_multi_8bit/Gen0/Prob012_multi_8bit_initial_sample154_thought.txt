To design the 8-bit multiplier module, we will implement the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The process involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit set to 1, adding the multiplicand (A) shifted by the corresponding position to the product. This process is repeated until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port. We will utilize a combinational logic approach to achieve this.
