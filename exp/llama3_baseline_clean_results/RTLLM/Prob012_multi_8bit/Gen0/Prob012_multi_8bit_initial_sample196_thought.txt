The implementation of the 8-bit multiplier module 'multi_8bit' will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position whenever the bit is set. The multiplicand will be logically shifted left after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port 'product'. The design will be implemented using a always_comb block in Verilog to ensure combinational logic.
