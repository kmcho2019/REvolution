To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add method. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever a bit is set. We will use a loop to iterate through each bit of the multiplier, and within the loop, we will check if the current bit is set. If it is, we will add the multiplicand (shifted to its correct position) to the product. After each iteration, we will shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare it for the next addition. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
