The 8-bit multiplier module will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product if the current bit is set. After each addition, the multiplicand is logically shifted left by one bit. The process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product. This will be implemented using a for loop to iterate over the bits of the multiplier and conditional statements to perform the additions. The product is accumulated in the output port.
