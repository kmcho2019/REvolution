To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize the shift-and-add method. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever a '1' bit is encountered in the multiplier. The multiplicand is shifted left by one bit after each addition to simulate the multiplication process. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, and the final product is stored in the output port.
