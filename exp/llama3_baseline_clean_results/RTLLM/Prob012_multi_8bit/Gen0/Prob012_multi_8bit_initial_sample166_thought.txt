To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted and added to a running product based on the bits of the multiplier (B). We'll iterate through each bit of B, and for each bit that is set (1), we'll add the shifted version of A to the product. After each addition, we'll shift A one bit to the left to prepare for the next iteration. This process will continue until all bits of B have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
