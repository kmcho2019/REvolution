1. Initialize the product to 0.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit that is set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After each iteration, shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 until all bits of the multiplier have been processed.
6. The final product is stored in the output port.
