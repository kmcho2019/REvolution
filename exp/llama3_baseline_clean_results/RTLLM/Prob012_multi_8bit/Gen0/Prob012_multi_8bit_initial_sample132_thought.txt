To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow the shift-and-add algorithm. We'll iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). For each bit set in the multiplier, we'll add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product. We'll use a variable to keep track of the product and another to hold the multiplicand as it's shifted. The process repeats until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
