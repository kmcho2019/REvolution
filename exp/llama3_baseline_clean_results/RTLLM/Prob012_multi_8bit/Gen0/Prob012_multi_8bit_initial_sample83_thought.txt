To implement an 8-bit multiplier based on shifting and adding operations, we need to follow the shift-and-add algorithm for multiplication. The multiplicand (A) is added to a running product whenever a bit in the multiplier (B) is 1. After each addition, the multiplicand is shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next potential addition. This process iterates through all bits of the multiplier. The result is accumulated in a 16-bit product to handle the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers. The module will be implemented in Verilog, utilizing a procedural block (always_comb) to describe the combinational logic of the multiplier.
