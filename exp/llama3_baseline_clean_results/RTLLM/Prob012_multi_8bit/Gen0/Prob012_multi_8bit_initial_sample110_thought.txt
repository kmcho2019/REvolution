To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a sequential approach, iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position if the bit is set. We will utilize a register to store the product and another to store the multiplicand as it is shifted. The iteration will be controlled by a counter that increments until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. At each step, we check the current bit of the multiplier, and if it's 1, we add the shifted multiplicand to the product. After each operation, we shift the multiplicand to the left to prepare for the next potential addition.
