To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever a bit is set. After each addition, we shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product. The module will be designed with input ports for the two 8-bit operands (A and B) and an output port for the 16-bit product.
