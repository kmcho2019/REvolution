To implement the 8-bit multiplier module (multi_8bit), we will use a combination of shift and add operations. 
The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output representing their product.
We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and for each set bit, add the multiplicand (A) shifted to the corresponding position to the product.
After processing all bits of the multiplier, the final product will be stored in the output port.
The algorithm will be implemented using a always_comb block to ensure combinational logic and avoid latches.
