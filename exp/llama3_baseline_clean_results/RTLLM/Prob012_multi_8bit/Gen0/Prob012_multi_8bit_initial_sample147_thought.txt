To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll utilize the shift-and-add method. The process starts with initializing the product to zero. Then, for each bit in the multiplier (B), we check if the bit is set. If it is, we add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product. After each addition, we shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. This process repeats until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port.
