To implement the 8-bit multiplier module using shifting and adding operations, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the product to zero.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit to the most significant bit.
3. For each bit that is set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After each addition, shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 until all bits of the multiplier have been processed.
6. Store the final product in the output port.

We will use a for loop in the always block to iterate through the bits of the multiplier, and a temporary variable to hold the multiplicand shifted left by the current bit position.
