To implement an 8-bit multiplier based on shifting and adding operations, we will use a procedural approach within the module. The idea is to iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit (1), add the multiplicand (A) shifted by the corresponding bit position to the product. This process involves using a loop to scan the multiplier, shifting the multiplicand left by one bit after each iteration, and accumulating the product. The final product is a 16-bit value because the maximum product of two 8-bit numbers requires 16 bits to be represented accurately.
