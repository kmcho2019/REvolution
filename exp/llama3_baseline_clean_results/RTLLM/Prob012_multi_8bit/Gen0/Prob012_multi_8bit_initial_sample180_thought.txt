To implement an 8-bit multiplier module based on shifting and adding operations, the approach involves iteratively checking each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit to the most significant bit. If a bit is set (1), the multiplicand (A) is added to the product at the corresponding position, shifted left by the bit position. After each iteration, the multiplicand is shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process simulates the traditional multiplication method and ensures that the final product, stored in the 16-bit output port, accurately represents the result of multiplying the two 8-bit inputs.
