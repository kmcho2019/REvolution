To implement the 8-bit multiplier module named "multi_8bit", we will follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. 
The multiplicand (A) will be added to the product whenever a bit in the multiplier (B) is 1, with A being shifted left after each addition to simulate the multiplication process.
We will use a procedural block (always block) to iterate through each bit of the multiplier, performing the necessary additions and shifts.
The product will be accumulated in a variable and finally assigned to the output port "product" after all bits of the multiplier have been processed.
