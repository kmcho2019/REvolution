To implement the 8-bit multiplier module using shifting and adding operations, we will use a procedural approach within a Verilog module. The module, named "multi_8bit", will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output, product. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit set (1), we will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, we will shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, effectively performing the multiplication of A and B.
