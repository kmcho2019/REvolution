The design idea is to create a multiplier module named 'multi_8bit' that takes two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 16-bit product. The multiplication is performed using the shift-and-add method. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, we add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, we shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process is repeated until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, and the final product is stored in the output port.
