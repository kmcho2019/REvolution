To implement the 8-bit multiplier module named "multi_8bit", we will use a combination of always block and wire signals in Verilog. The multiplicand (A) and multiplier (B) will be input to the module. The output will be the 16-bit product of A and B. We'll iterate through each bit of the multiplier B using a for loop, shifting A to the left based on the bit position and adding it to the product if the current bit of B is 1. This process will continue until all bits of B have been processed.
