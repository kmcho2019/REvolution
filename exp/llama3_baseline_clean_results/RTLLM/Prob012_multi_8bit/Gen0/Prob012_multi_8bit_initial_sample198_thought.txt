To implement an 8-bit multiplier based on shifting and adding operations, we will use a combinational logic approach where the multiplier (B) is processed bit by bit. For each '1' bit encountered in the multiplier, the multiplicand (A) is added to the product at the position corresponding to the current bit's place value. This process involves shifting A to the left by one bit after each addition to align with the next place value in the multiplier, thus effectively multiplying A by 2 for each bit position. The final product is accumulated over all bits of the multiplier and stored in a 16-bit output port to accommodate the maximum product of two 8-bit numbers.
