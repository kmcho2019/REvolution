To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The algorithm will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B). If a bit is set (1), the multiplicand (A) will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the output port.
