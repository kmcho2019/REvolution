To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of shifting and adding operations. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and add the multiplicand (A) to the product if the current bit is set. After each addition, we will shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
