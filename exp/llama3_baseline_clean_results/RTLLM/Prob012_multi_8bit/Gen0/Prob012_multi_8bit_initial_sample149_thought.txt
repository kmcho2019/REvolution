To implement an 8-bit multiplier module in Verilog, we'll use a behavioral approach. The module, named multi_8bit, takes two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 16-bit output (product). The multiplication will be performed using the shift-and-add method, where each bit of the multiplier (B) is checked. If the bit is 1, the multiplicand (A) is added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand is logically shifted left by one bit. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product is then stored in the output port.
