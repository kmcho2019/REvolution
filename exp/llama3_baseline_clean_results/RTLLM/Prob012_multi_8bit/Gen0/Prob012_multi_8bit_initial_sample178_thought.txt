To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the product to zero.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit set in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted by the corresponding bit position to the product.
4. After processing all bits of the multiplier, store the final product in the output port.

We'll use a for loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier and a temporary variable to hold the multiplicand shifted by the current bit position.
