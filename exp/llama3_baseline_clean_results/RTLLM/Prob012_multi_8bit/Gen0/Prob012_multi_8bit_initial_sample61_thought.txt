The 8-bit multiplier module, 'multi_8bit', will be designed using the shift-and-add method. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier, adding the multiplicand to the product when a '1' bit is encountered, and shifting the multiplicand left for the next iteration. The process is repeated until all bits of the multiplier are processed. The final product will be stored in a 16-bit output port to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers.
