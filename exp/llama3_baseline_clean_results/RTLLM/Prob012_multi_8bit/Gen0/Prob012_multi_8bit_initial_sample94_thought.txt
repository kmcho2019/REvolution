To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a sequential approach. The module will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from least significant bit (LSB) to most significant bit (MSB). For each bit set (1) in the multiplier, the multiplicand (A) will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
