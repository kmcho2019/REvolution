To design the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize a combination of iterative shifting and addition operations to compute the product of the two 8-bit input operands, A and B. The module, named 'multi_8bit', will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output, 'product', representing the result of the multiplication. The algorithm involves checking each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). If a bit is set (1), the multiplicand (A) is added to the product, with A being shifted left by the position of the current bit in B. This process effectively implements the shift-and-add multiplication technique.
