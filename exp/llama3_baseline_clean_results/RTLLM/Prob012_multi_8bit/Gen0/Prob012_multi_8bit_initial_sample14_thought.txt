To implement an 8-bit multiplier based on shifting and adding operations, we will follow these steps:
1. Initialize a 16-bit product register to zero.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit that is set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After processing each bit, shift the multiplicand (A) one bit to the left to prepare for the next iteration.
5. Once all bits of the multiplier have been processed, the final product is stored in the output port.
We will use a for loop in Verilog to iterate through each bit of the multiplier, and we will use the "+" operator to add the shifted multiplicand to the product and the "<<" operator to shift the multiplicand left.
