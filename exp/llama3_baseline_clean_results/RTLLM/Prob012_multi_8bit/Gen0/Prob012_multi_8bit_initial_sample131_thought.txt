To implement an 8-bit multiplier using shifting and adding operations, we can follow these steps:
1. Initialize the product to zero.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit that is set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After processing each bit, shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition.
5. Continue this process until all bits of the multiplier have been processed.
6. The final product is stored in the output port.

We will use a for loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier and perform the necessary operations. We will also use the shift operator to shift the multiplicand and the product.
