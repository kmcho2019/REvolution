To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, I will utilize a combinational logic approach using Verilog. The module will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and perform a conditional addition of the multiplicand (A) to the product based on the current bit of the multiplier. This process will be achieved using a combination of bitwise operations and conditional statements. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit after each iteration to prepare for the next addition. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
