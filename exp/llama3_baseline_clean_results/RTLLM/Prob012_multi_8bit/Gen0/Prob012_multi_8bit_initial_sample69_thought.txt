To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow the shift-and-add approach for multiplication. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left and added to a running product based on the bits of the multiplier (B). We'll use a for loop in the initial block to iterate through each bit of the multiplier. If a bit is set, we'll add the shifted multiplicand to the product. After each iteration, we'll shift the multiplicand left to prepare for the next addition.
