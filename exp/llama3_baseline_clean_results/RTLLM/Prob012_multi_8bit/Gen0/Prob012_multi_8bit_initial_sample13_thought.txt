To implement an 8-bit multiplier using shifting and adding operations, we will design a Verilog module named 'multi_8bit'. This module will take two 8-bit inputs, 'A' and 'B', and produce a 16-bit output representing the product of 'A' and 'B'. The shift-and-add method will be utilized, where for each bit set in the multiplier 'B', the multiplicand 'A' will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, 'A' will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier 'B' have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
