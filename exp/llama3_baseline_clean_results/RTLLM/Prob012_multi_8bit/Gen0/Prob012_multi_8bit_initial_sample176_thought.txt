To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit that is set (1), adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. We will use a for loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier and utilize the shift operator to shift the multiplicand left by one bit after each addition. The final product will be stored in a 16-bit output port.
