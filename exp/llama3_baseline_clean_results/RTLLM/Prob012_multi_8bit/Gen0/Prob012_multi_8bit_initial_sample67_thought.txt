To implement an 8-bit multiplier module based on shifting and adding operations, we will design a module named 'multi_8bit' that takes two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produces a 16-bit output representing their product. The multiplication process will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), adding the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever a bit is set, with A being shifted left after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process.
