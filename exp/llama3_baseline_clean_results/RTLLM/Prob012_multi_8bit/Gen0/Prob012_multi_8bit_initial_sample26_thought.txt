To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the product to 0.
2. Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
3. For each bit set (1) in the multiplier, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
4. After processing all bits, the final product is stored in the 16-bit output port.

We will use a for-loop to iterate over the bits of the multiplier and perform the shift-and-add operations accordingly.
