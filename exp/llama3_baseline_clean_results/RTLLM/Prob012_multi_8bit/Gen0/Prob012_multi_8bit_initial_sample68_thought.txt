To implement an 8-bit multiplier based on shifting and adding operations, we will use a loop to iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B). For each bit that is set (1), we will add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product. This process simulates the traditional multiplication method where each digit in the multiplier determines how many times the multiplicand is added to the product, shifted according to its position. We will use a temporary variable to hold the shifted multiplicand and another to accumulate the product. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port, which is sufficient to hold the result of multiplying two 8-bit numbers.
