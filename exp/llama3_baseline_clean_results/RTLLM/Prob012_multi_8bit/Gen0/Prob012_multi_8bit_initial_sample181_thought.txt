To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, I will follow the shift-and-add method. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit in each iteration, and added to the product if the corresponding bit of the multiplier (B) is set. The process will be repeated for all bits of the multiplier. I will use a for loop to iterate through each bit of the multiplier, checking if the bit is set and performing the addition if necessary. The final product will be stored in the 16-bit output port.
