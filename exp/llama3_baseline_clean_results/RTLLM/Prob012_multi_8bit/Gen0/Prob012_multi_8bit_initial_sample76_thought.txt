To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The multiplicand (A) will be added to a running total (product) whenever a bit in the multiplier (B) is set. After each operation, the multiplicand will be shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next potential addition, simulating the multiplication process. This process will iterate through all bits of the multiplier. The result will be a 16-bit product that represents the multiplication of the two 8-bit inputs.
