To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll use the shift-and-add method. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted and added to the product based on each bit of the multiplier (B). We'll iterate through each bit of B from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). If a bit of B is 1, we'll add the corresponding shifted value of A to the product. After each operation, we'll shift A one bit to the left to prepare for the next iteration. This process will continue until all bits of B have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
