To implement an 8-bit multiplier using shifting and adding operations, we will design a module named 'multi_8bit' that takes two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 16-bit output (product). The module will use a shift-and-add method for multiplication, where for each '1' bit in the multiplier (B), the multiplicand (A) will be added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration, simulating the traditional multiplication process. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed.
