To implement an 8-bit multiplier module, I will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit product. The algorithm will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, it will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, and the final product will be stored in the output port.
