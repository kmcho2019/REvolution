To implement the 8-bit multiplier module (multi_8bit), we will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit set to 1, adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand (A) will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier (B) have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product stored in the output port (product).
