To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of shifting and adding operations. The module will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B) and add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position if the bit is set. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in a 16-bit product stored in the output port.
