To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of always block, if-else statements, and a for loop. The module will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit that is set, it will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. We will use a temporary variable to hold the product during the calculation, and finally, we will assign the result to the output port.
