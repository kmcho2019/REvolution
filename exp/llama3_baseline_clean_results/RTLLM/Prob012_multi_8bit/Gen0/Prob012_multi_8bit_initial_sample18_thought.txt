To implement the 8-bit multiplier module (multi_8bit), we'll follow the shift-and-add method. The design will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit set to 1, it will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, the multiplicand (A) will be logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in a 16-bit product stored in the output port.
