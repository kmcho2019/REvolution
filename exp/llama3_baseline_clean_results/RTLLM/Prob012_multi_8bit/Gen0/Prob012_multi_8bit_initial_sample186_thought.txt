To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize a combinational logic approach. The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output representing their product. The shift-and-add method will be employed, where each bit of the multiplier B is checked. If a bit is set (1), the multiplicand A is added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, A is logically shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. This process continues until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, and the final product is stored in the 16-bit output port.
