The 8-bit multiplier module, multi_8bit, will be designed using the shift-and-add method. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B) and adding the multiplicand (A) to the product whenever the current bit of the multiplier is 1. After each addition, the multiplicand will be shifted left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration. The process will be repeated until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in a 16-bit product.
