To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a simple iterative approach based on the shift-and-add method. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit for each bit position in the multiplier (B) that is set (1), and then added to the product at the corresponding position. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final 16-bit product.
