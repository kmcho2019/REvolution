To design the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process in Verilog that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal. This process will check the reset signal and the up_down control signal to determine whether to reset, increment, or decrement the counter. We will use a reg variable to hold the counter value and assign it to the output port. The counter should be able to handle a 16-bit value, thus we will declare the count variable as a 16-bit reg.
