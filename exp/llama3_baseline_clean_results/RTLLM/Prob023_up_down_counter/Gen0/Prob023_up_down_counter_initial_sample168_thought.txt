The up_down_counter module will be implemented as a synchronous digital circuit, utilizing a 16-bit register to store the current count value. The counting direction is controlled by the up_down signal, and the counting process is synchronized with the rising edge of the clk signal. If the reset signal is active, the counter will be reset to zero. The counter will either increment or decrement based on the up_down signal, and the current count value will be reflected on the count output port.
