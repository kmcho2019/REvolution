To design the up_down_counter module, I will use a synchronous process triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). The counter will be reset to zero when the reset signal is active. Based on the up_down control signal, the counter will either increment or decrement on each clock cycle. I will utilize a reg variable to hold the counter value and assign it to the output port count. The counter will be designed to handle 16-bit values ranging from 0 to 65535.
