To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal. We will utilize an if-else statement to check the reset condition and reset the counter to zero if the reset signal is active. Then, we will use another if-else statement to determine the counting direction based on the up_down control signal.
