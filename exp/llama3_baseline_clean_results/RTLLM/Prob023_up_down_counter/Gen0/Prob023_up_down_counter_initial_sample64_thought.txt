To design the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process that responds to the rising edge of the clock signal. We will use an if-else statement inside the process to check the reset signal and the up_down control signal. If the reset signal is high, the counter will be reset to zero. Otherwise, we will check the up_down control signal and either increment or decrement the counter accordingly. The current value of the counter will be assigned to the count output port.
