To implement the up_down_counter module, I will use a synchronous process triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`). Inside the process, I will first check if the reset signal (`reset`) is active. If it is, I will reset the counter to zero. Then, I will check the `up_down` control signal to determine the counting direction. If `up_down` is high, I will increment the counter; if it's low, I will decrement the counter. The `count` output will be updated accordingly. I will use a 16-bit reg to store the counter value and assign it to the `count` output port.
