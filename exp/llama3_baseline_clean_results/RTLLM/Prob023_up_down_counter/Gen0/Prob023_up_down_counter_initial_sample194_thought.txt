To design the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). We will first check for the reset signal and reset the counter to zero if it is active. Then, we will check the up_down control signal and increment or decrement the counter accordingly. The updated counter value will be assigned to the count output. We will use a reg variable to hold the current counter value and assign it to the count output.
