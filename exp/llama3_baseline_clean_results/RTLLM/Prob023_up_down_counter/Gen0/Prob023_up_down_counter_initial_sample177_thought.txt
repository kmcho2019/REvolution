To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). This process will check the reset signal and the up_down control signal to decide whether to reset, increment, or decrement the counter. We will use a reg data type to declare the counter variable and assign its value to the output port count. The counter will be 16 bits wide to accommodate values ranging from 0 to 65535.
