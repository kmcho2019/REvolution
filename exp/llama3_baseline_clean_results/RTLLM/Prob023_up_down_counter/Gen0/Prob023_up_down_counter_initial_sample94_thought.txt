To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`). We will utilize an if-else statement to check the `reset` signal and reset the counter to zero when it is active. Then, we will use another if-else statement to check the `up_down` control signal and either increment or decrement the counter based on its value. The `count` output will be assigned the current value of the counter.
