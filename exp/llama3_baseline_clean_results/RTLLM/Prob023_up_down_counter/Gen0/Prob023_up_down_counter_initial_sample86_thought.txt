To implement the up_down_counter module, we need to use a synchronous process that checks the reset signal and the control signal (up_down) to determine whether to reset, increment, or decrement the counter. The counter should be 16 bits wide to accommodate values from 0 to 65535. The process will be triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). If the reset signal is active, the counter should immediately reset to zero. Otherwise, the counter's action (increment or decrement) depends on the state of the up_down signal.
