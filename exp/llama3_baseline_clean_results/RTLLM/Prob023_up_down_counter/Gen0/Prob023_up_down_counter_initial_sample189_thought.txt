The up_down_counter module will utilize a synchronous process to handle the counting logic based on the control signals and clock. The module will be designed as follows:
- It will have an internal 16-bit register to hold the current count.
- On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is active, the counter will reset to zero.
- If the reset signal is not active, the counter will either increment or decrement based on the value of the up_down control signal.
- The updated count will be reflected at the output port 'count' after each clock cycle.
