To design the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process in Verilog that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`). The counter will be reset to zero when the `reset` signal is active. Based on the `up_down` control signal, we will either increment or decrement the counter on each clock cycle. The current value of the counter will be reflected in the `count` output. We will use a 16-bit reg to store the counter value and use non-blocking assignments to update the counter value.
