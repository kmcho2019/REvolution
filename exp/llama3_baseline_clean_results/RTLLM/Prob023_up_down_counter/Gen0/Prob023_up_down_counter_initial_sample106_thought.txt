The design idea is to create a 16-bit up/down counter that responds to control signals. The counter will be implemented as a synchronous process, triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal. It will have a reset mechanism to set the counter to zero when the reset signal is active. The counting direction will be determined by the up_down control signal, where a high signal causes the counter to increment and a low signal causes it to decrement. The current counter value will be reflected in the 16-bit output port named count.
