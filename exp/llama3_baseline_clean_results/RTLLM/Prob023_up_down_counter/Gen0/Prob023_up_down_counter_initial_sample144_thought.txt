To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process in Verilog that is triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). The counter's behavior will be controlled by the reset and up_down signals. If the reset signal is active (high), the counter will be reset to zero, regardless of the up_down signal. If the reset signal is inactive (low), the counter will either increment or decrement based on the value of the up_down signal. We will use a reg variable to hold the current count and assign this value to the output port count.
