The up_down_counter module will utilize a synchronous process to update the counter value based on the control signals. It will be triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal. The reset signal will have priority over the counting process. If the reset signal is active, the counter will reset to zero regardless of the control signals. The up_down control signal will determine whether the counter increments or decrements. The counter value will be stored in a 16-bit register, and its value will be updated accordingly on each clock cycle.
