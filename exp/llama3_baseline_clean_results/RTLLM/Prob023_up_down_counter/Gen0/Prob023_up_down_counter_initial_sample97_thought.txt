The up_down_counter module will utilize a synchronous process triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`). We will use an if-else statement inside the process to check the `reset` signal. If `reset` is high, the counter will reset to zero. Otherwise, we will check the `up_down` control signal to determine whether to increment or decrement the counter. If `up_down` is high, we will increment the counter; if `up_down` is low, we will decrement the counter. The updated counter value will be assigned to the `count` output.
