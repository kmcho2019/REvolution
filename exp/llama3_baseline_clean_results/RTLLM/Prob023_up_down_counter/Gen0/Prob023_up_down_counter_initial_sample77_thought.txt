To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a synchronous process in Verilog that responds to the rising edge of the clock signal. We will use an if-else statement inside the process to handle the reset condition and the increment/decrement logic based on the up_down control signal. The count output will be updated accordingly, either by incrementing or decrementing it by 1 on each clock cycle, depending on the state of the up_down signal.
