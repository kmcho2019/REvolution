The up_down_counter module will be designed as a synchronous digital circuit, utilizing a 16-bit register to store the counter value. The counter's behavior will be controlled by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk), and its value will be updated based on the up_down control signal. If the reset signal is active, the counter will be reset to zero, ensuring proper initialization. The up_down signal will determine whether the counter increments or decrements, allowing for bidirectional counting functionality.
