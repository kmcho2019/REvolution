To implement the up_down_counter module, we will use a 16-bit register to store the current count and update it based on the up_down control signal and the reset signal. The module will be triggered by the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). When the reset signal is high, the counter will reset to zero. When the up_down signal is high, the counter will increment; otherwise, it will decrement. We will use a case statement or if-else statements to handle the different conditions.
