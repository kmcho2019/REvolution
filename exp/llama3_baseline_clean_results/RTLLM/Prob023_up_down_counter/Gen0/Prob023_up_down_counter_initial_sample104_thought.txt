The up_down_counter module will utilize a synchronous process, which is sensitive to the rising edge of the clock signal (`clk`), to update the counter value. The reset signal (`reset`) will be used to asynchronously reset the counter to zero. Based on the `up_down` control signal, the counter will either increment or decrement on each clock cycle, and the current value will be reflected on the `count` output. 
We will use a 16-bit reg type to hold the counter value and update it within the always block that is triggered by the rising edge of `clk`. We also need to ensure the reset condition takes precedence over any counting operation.
