The edge_detect module will use a simple yet effective approach to detect rising and falling edges in the input signal a. It will utilize a previous_state variable to keep track of the state of signal a at the previous clock cycle. By comparing the current state of signal a with the previous state, the module can determine whether a rising or falling edge has occurred. The edge detection will be performed on the positive edge of the clock signal clk, and the output signals rise and down will be updated accordingly. The reset signal rst_n will be used to reset the previous_state and output signals when necessary.
