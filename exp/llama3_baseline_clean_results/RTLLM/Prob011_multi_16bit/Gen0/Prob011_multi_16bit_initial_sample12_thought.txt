The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. This is achieved through a finite state machine that controls the flow of the multiplication operation based on the input control signals such as clock, reset, and start. The module starts the multiplication operation when the start signal is active and increments a shift count register (i) on every positive edge of the clock signal until the shift count reaches 16. The multiplication completion flag (done) is set to 1 when the shift count reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication operation, and reset to 0 when the shift count reaches 17. The shift and accumulate operation involves storing the multiplicand and multiplier in respective registers, shifting the multiplier left by the current shift count, and accumulating the result in the product register when the corresponding bit in the multiplicand is high.
