It utilizes a finite state machine approach to manage the multiplication operation, which includes the control of the shift count register (i), the multiplication completion flag (done_r), and the shift and accumulate operation. This design incorporates a combinational logic for the product output calculation and a sequential logic for the control of the multiplication process. The use of an internal product register (yout_r) and registers for the multiplicand (areg) and multiplier (breg) allows for a synchronized multiplication operation.
