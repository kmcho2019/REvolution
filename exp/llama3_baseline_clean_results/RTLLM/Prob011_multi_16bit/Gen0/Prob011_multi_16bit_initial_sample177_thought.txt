To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the shift and accumulate operations. The FSM will have two states: idle and multiply. In the idle state, the module waits for the start signal to initiate the multiplication operation. In the multiply state, the module performs the shift and accumulate operation. We will use a counter to keep track of the shift count. The multiplication completion flag (done) will be set to 1 when the shift count reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The multiplicand and multiplier will be stored in registers, and the product will be accumulated in a separate register.
