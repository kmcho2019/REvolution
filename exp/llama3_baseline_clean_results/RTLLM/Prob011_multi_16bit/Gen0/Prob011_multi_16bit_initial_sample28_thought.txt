The module will have control signals such as clock (clk), reset (rst_n), and start (start), along with a completion flag (done) indicating the completion of the multiplication operation.

The multiplication completion flag generation will update the multiplication completion flag (done_r) on every positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
The shift and accumulate operation will be performed on every positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
The output assignment will assign the product output (yout) the value of the product register (yout_r).
