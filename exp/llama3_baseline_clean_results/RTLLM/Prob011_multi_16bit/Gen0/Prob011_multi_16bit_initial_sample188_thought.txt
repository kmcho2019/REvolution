The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier will be implemented using the Verilog hardware description language (HDL). The module will utilize shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. It will also include control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The design will be divided into several sections: control logic, shift and accumulate operation, and output assignment. The control logic will handle the clock, reset, and start signals to control the shift count register and multiplication completion flag. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed based on the control signals and the values of the multiplicand and multiplier registers. The output assignment will assign the product output the value of the product register.
