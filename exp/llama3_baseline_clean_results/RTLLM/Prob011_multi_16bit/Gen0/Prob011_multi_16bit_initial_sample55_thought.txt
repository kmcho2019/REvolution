To implement the design of an unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach, which will manage the control signals and perform the shift and accumulate operations. The FSM will handle the reset, start, and completion flag signals. It will also manage the shift count register, multiplicand register, multiplier register, and product register. The module will use the shift count register to track the number of bits processed during the multiplication operation. The multiplicand and multiplier will be stored in separate registers, and the product will be accumulated in the product register based on the shift count and the value of the multiplicand bits.
