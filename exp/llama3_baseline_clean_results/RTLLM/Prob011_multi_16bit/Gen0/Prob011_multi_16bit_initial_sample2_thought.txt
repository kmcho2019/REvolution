To implement the 16-bit unsigned multiplier using shift and accumulate operations, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the shift count, multiplicand, multiplier, and product registers. The FSM will be updated on every positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n). The multiplication operation starts when the start signal is active and the shift count register (i) is less than 17. The multiplication completion flag (done_r) is generated when the shift count register (i) reaches 16. The shift and accumulate operation is performed based on the bits of the multiplicand register (areg) and the multiplier register (breg).
