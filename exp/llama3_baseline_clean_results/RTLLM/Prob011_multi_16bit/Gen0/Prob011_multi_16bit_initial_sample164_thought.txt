The design of an unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizing shift and accumulate operations can be implemented using a finite state machine that controls the multiplication process. The multiplier consists of a shift count register, multiplicand register, multiplier register, and product register. The control signals, including clock, reset, start, and completion flag, manage the multiplication operation. On every positive edge of the clock signal, the shift count register is updated based on the start signal and the current shift count. The multiplication completion flag is generated when the shift count reaches 16. The shift and accumulate operation is performed on every positive edge of the clock signal, where the multiplicand and multiplier are stored in their respective registers, and the product register accumulates the shifted value of the multiplier based on the bit position in the multiplicand.
