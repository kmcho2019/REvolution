The 16-bit unsigned multiplier utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. It includes control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The design involves a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the multiplication process. The shift count register is updated based on the clock edge and reset condition. The multiplication completion flag is generated based on the shift count register value. The shift and accumulate operation is performed based on the start signal and shift count register value. The product output is assigned the value of the product register.
