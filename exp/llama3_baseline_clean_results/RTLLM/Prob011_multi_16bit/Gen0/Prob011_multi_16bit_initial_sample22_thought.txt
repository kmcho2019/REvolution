To design an unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize a shift and accumulate approach. The multiplier will have a 16-bit multiplicand (a) and a 16-bit multiplier (b) as inputs, with a 32-bit product output (yout). The control signals include a clock (clk), reset (rst_n), start (start), and a completion flag (done). We will implement the multiplier using a finite state machine (FSM) that manages the shift and accumulate operations, updates the multiplication completion flag, and resets the registers as needed.
