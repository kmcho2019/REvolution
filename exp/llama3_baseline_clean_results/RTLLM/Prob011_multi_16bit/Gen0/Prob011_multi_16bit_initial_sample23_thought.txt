To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we'll utilize the shift and accumulate method. We will have a control unit that manages the shift count register (i), the multiplication completion flag (done_r), and performs the shift and accumulate operation based on the input signals. The shift count register (i) will be updated on every positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n). We will also have separate registers to hold the multiplicand (areg) and the multiplier (breg), and another register (yout_r) to accumulate the product. The module will also generate a completion flag (done) when the multiplication operation is complete.
