To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we need to design a module that performs the shift and accumulate operation to generate the product output. The module will have control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. We will use a counter to keep track of the shift count and update the multiplicand, multiplier, and product registers accordingly. The multiplication completion flag will be generated based on the shift count. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed on every positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
