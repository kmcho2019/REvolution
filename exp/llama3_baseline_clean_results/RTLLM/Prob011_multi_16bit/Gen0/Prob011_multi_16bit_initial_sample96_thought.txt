The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier module (multi_16bit) involves utilizing shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output (yout). It also includes control signals such as clock (clk), reset (rst_n), and start (start), along with a completion flag (done) indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The module operates as follows:
- It resets the shift count register (i) and other registers (areg, breg, yout_r, done_r) when the reset signal (rst_n) is low.
- It increments the shift count register (i) when the start signal is active and i is less than 17.
- It generates the multiplication completion flag (done_r) when the shift count register (i) is equal to 16 and resets it when i is equal to 17.
- It performs the shift and accumulate operation by shifting the multiplier register (breg) left by i-1 positions and accumulating it into the product register (yout_r) when the bit at position i-1 in the multiplicand register (areg) is high.
- It assigns the product output (yout) the value of the product register (yout_r).
The Verilog implementation of the multi_16bit module will be designed using a finite state machine (FSM) approach with a single state, incorporating the required control and data signals.
