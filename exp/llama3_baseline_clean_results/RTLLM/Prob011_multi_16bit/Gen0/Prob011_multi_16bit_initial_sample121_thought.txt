The unsigned 16-bit multiplier module multi_16bit utilizes a shift and accumulate approach to generate the product of two input numbers. The module includes control signals such as clock (clk), reset (rst_n), and start (start), along with a completion flag (done) indicating the end of the multiplication operation. The multiplication process involves shifting and accumulating operations based on the bits of the multiplicand and multiplier. A reset condition is handled by setting all registers to zero and the completion flag to zero. On every positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal, the module updates its internal registers accordingly. The product output (yout) is assigned the value of the product register (yout_r) at the end of the multiplication operation.
