To design an unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we need to create a module that takes the multiplicand and multiplier as inputs, and produces the product output. The module will utilize shift and accumulate operations to generate the product. It will also include control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. We will use a shift count register to keep track of the current bit position during the multiplication operation. The module will perform the shift and accumulate operation on every positive edge of the clock signal, and the product register will be updated accordingly.
