To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize shift and accumulate operations. The design involves control signals for clock, reset, start, and completion flag. We will update the shift count register based on the clock edge or reset condition and use it to control the multiplication process. The multiplication completion flag will be generated based on the shift count register. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed using the multiplicand and multiplier registers, and the result will be stored in the product register.
