To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize the shift and accumulate operations. The module will be designed with control signals such as clock, reset, start, and a completion flag. The shift count register (i) will be updated based on the clock signal and the start signal. The multiplication completion flag (done_r) will be generated based on the shift count register. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed based on the multiplicand and multiplier registers. The product output will be assigned the value of the product register.
