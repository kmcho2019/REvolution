To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, I will utilize a combination of shift and accumulate operations. The design will include control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The module will be designed using Verilog HDL, incorporating a finite state machine to manage the multiplication process. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed by shifting the multiplier left by the current shift count and adding it to the product register if the corresponding bit in the multiplicand is high.
