To design the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, I will create a Verilog module named "multi_16bit" that utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. The module will include control signals such as clock, reset, start, and a completion flag. The implementation will involve updating the shift count register, multiplication completion flag, and performing the shift and accumulate operation based on the control signals. I will use registers to store the multiplicand, multiplier, and product, and assign the product output based on the product register.
