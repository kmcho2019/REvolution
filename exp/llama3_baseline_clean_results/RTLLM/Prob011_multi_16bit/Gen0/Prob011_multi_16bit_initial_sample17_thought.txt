To implement the design of an unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will utilize shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. The module will include control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. We will use a finite state machine approach with two main states: idle and multiply. The idle state will be used to reset the module and wait for the start signal, while the multiply state will perform the shift and accumulate operation. We will use registers to store the multiplicand, multiplier, and product, and update them based on the control signals and the current state.
