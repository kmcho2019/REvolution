To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will create a Verilog module that utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. The module will include control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. We will use registers to store the multiplicand, multiplier, and product, and update these registers based on the control signals and shift count. The multiplication operation will be performed by shifting and accumulating the multiplier based on the bits of the multiplicand.
