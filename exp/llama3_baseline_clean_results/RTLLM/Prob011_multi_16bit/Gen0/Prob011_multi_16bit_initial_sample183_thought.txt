The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. It consists of control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The module will be implemented using Verilog, incorporating always blocks to handle the clock and reset signals. The shift count register is updated on every positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal. The multiplication completion flag is generated based on the shift count register. The shift and accumulate operation is performed on every positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal. The product output is assigned the value of the product register.
