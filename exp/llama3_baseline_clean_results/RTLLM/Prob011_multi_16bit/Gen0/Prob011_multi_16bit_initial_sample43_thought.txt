The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier can be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) with a single state, and the shift and accumulate operations are performed based on the current state of the shift count register (i). The module takes the multiplicand (a) and multiplier (b) as inputs and produces the product (yout) as output. The start signal initiates the multiplication operation, and the done flag indicates the completion of the operation. The reset signal resets the module to its initial state.
