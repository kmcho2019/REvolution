To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will use a shift and accumulate approach. This involves shifting the multiplier to the left based on the bits of the multiplicand and accumulating the results. We will use a counter (i) to keep track of the current bit position in the multiplicand. On every clock cycle, we check the current bit of the multiplicand. If it is high, we add the shifted multiplier to the product. We will also generate a completion flag (done) when the multiplication is finished. The multiplication is considered complete when the counter (i) reaches 16, indicating that all bits of the multiplicand have been processed.
