To design the unsigned 16-bit multiplier module, we will utilize the shift and accumulate method. The module will have a clock signal (clk), an active-low reset signal (rst_n), a start signal (start), two input signals representing the multiplicand (ain) and the multiplier (bin), an output signal representing the product (yout), and a completion flag (done). The shift count register (i) will be used to control the number of shifts and accumulations. On every positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal, the shift count register (i) will be updated. The multiplication completion flag (done_r) will be generated based on the value of the shift count register. The shift and accumulate operation will be performed based on the value of the shift count register and the bit value at the corresponding position in the multiplicand register.
