To design the unsigned 16-bit multiplier module, we will follow these steps:
1. Initialize the shift count register (i), multiplication completion flag (done_r), multiplicand register (areg), multiplier register (breg), and product register (yout_r) on the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n) or the positive edge of the clock signal (clk).
2. Update the shift count register (i) based on the start signal (start) and the current shift count.
3. Generate the multiplication completion flag (done_r) based on the shift count register (i).
4. Perform the shift and accumulate operation to calculate the product.
5. Assign the final product to the output port (yout) and indicate the completion of the multiplication operation using the output flag (done).
