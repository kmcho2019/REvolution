The unsigned 16-bit multiplier module will be designed using a combination of shift and accumulate operations. The control signals, including the clock (clk), reset (rst_n), and start (start), will be used to manage the operation of the multiplier. The module will have an internal shift count register (i) to track the number of shifts performed during the multiplication operation. The multiplicand (ain) and multiplier (bin) will be stored in internal registers (areg and breg) and used in the shift and accumulate operation. The product output (yout) will be generated by accumulating the shifted values of the multiplier register (breg) based on the bits of the multiplicand register (areg). The multiplication completion flag (done) will be set to 1 when the shift count register (i) reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication operation.
