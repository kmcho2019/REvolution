The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizes a shift and accumulate approach, where the multiplicand and multiplier are shifted and added to produce the product. The design includes control signals such as clock, reset, start, and done. On every clock cycle, the design checks if the reset signal is active, and if so, resets the shift count register and multiplication completion flag. If the start signal is active, the design initializes the multiplicand and multiplier registers, and then performs the shift and accumulate operation for 16 clock cycles. After the 16th clock cycle, the design sets the multiplication completion flag. The product output is assigned the value of the product register.
