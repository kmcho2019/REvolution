To design the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the control signals and a shift-accumulate approach to perform the multiplication. The design will consist of several registers to store the multiplicand, multiplier, and product, as well as a counter to track the shift count. The multiplication completion flag will be generated based on the shift count. We will implement the shift and accumulate operation using a combination of bitwise operations and arithmetic shifts.
