To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier module, I will utilize a combination of shift and accumulate operations. The design will include a control unit to manage the multiplication process, along with registers to store the multiplicand, multiplier, and product. The control unit will update the shift count register, generate the multiplication completion flag, and perform the shift and accumulate operation based on the input signals and current state. I will use a sequential design approach, with the multiplication process taking place over multiple clock cycles. The module will reset the registers and flags upon detection of a low reset signal or an inactive start signal.
