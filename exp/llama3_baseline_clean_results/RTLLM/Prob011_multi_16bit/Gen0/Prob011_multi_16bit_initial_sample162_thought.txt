The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizes a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to control the multiplication process. On every positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the FSM increments the shift count register (i) when the start signal is active and the shift count is less than 17. The multiplicand (ain) and multiplier (bin) are stored in their respective registers (areg and breg) when the shift count is 0. For each shift count, the FSM performs the shift and accumulate operation by shifting the multiplier register (breg) left by i-1 positions and accumulating the result in the product register (yout_r) if the corresponding bit in the multiplicand register (areg) is high. The multiplication completion flag (done_r) is set to 1 when the shift count reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication operation.
