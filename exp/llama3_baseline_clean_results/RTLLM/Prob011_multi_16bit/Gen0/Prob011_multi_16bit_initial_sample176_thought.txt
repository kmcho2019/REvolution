To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier module (multi_16bit), we will utilize the shift and accumulate operation to generate the product output (yout). The module includes control signals such as clock (clk), reset (rst_n), and start (start), along with a completion flag (done) indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. We will implement the data bit control logic to update the shift count register (i), generate the multiplication completion flag (done_r), and perform the shift and accumulate operation based on the control signals and the multiplicand (ain) and multiplier (bin) inputs.
