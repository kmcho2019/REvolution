We start by defining the module with the given input and output ports. We then declare the necessary registers and wires to hold the multiplicand, multiplier, and product values. The multiplicand and multiplier registers are initialized based on the reset signal. We use a counter (ctr) to track the number of clock cycles and control the multiplication process. In each clock cycle, we check the current bit of the multiplier register and perform the necessary operations according to the Booth algorithm. Once the counter reaches 16, we set the ready signal (rdy) to 1 to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.
