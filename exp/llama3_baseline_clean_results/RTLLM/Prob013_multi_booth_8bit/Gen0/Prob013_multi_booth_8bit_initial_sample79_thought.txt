The Booth algorithm is a multiplication algorithm that allows for faster multiplication by shifting the bits of the multiplicand instead of the multiplier. The Radix-4 variant of the Booth algorithm processes two bits of the multiplier at a time.

- The multiplicand register will be left-shifted by 1 to simulate the Booth algorithm's shifting operation.
