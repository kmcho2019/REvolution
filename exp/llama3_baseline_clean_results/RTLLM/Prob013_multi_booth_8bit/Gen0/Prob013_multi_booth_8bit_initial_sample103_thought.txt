The design idea is to implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier in Verilog. The Booth multiplier performs the multiplication of two 8-bit inputs using the Booth algorithm. The algorithm utilizes a series of left shift and add operations to calculate the product of two numbers. In this implementation, we use a clock signal (clk) and a reset signal (reset) to control the operation of the multiplier. The product output (p) and a ready signal (rdy) are provided as outputs. When the reset signal (reset) is high, the multiplicand and multiplier are initialized. When the reset signal (reset) is low, the multiplier performs the multiplication process by shifting the multiplicand and accumulating the product. The ready signal (rdy) is set to 1 when the multiplication process is complete.
