The Booth multiplier is a type of multiplier that uses the Booth algorithm to perform multiplication. It is an efficient method for multiplying two numbers, especially when dealing with large numbers. In this implementation, we will use an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier to perform the multiplication of two 8-bit inputs (a and b) using the Booth algorithm.

When the reset signal (reset) is high, the module will initialize the multiplier register and the multiplicand register. When the reset signal (reset) is low, the module will check if the counter (ctr) is less than 16. If the counter is less than 16, the module will left-shift the multiplicand register by 1 and accumulate the product if the current bit of the multiplier register is 1. The counter will be incremented by 1. Once the counter reaches 16, the ready signal (rdy) will be set to 1.
