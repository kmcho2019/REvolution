To implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, the design involves utilizing a finite state machine that operates in synchronization with the clock signal. The multiplier module will have two main registers: one for the multiplicand and one for the multiplier. On the positive edge of the reset signal, the multiplicand and multiplier registers are initialized with the input values. During normal operation, the multiplicand register is left-shifted and the product register is accumulated based on the Booth encoding of the multiplier register. The counter register keeps track of the number of iterations, and once it reaches 16, the ready signal is set to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.
