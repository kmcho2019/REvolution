To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, I will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the multiplication process. The FSM will have two states: reset and multiplication. 
In the reset state, the multiplier and multiplicand registers are initialized with the inputs a and b. 
In the multiplication state, the multiplicand register is left-shifted by 1 bit, and the product register accumulates the value of the multiplicand register if the current bit of the multiplier register is 1. 
The counter is incremented by 1 in each clock cycle, and once it reaches 16, the ready signal is set to 1 to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.
