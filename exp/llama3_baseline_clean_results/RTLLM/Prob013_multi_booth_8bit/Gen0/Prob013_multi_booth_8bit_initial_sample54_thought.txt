To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the multiplication process. The FSM will have two main states: reset and multiplication. In the reset state, the multiplicand and multiplier are initialized. In the multiplication state, the multiplicand is shifted, and the product is accumulated based on the Booth encoding of the multiplier bits. A counter will keep track of the number of bits processed, and once all bits are processed, the ready signal will be set to indicate completion.
