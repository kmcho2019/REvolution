To implement an 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the multiplication process. The FSM will be in one of two states: reset or multiply. In the reset state, the multiplier and multiplicand registers are initialized with the input values. In the multiply state, the multiplicand register is left-shifted, and the product register accumulates the value of the multiplicand register based on the current bit of the multiplier register. The multiplication process is completed when the counter reaches 16, and the ready signal is set to 1.
