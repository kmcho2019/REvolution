- Use two 16-bit registers to store the multiplicand and multiplier.

- In the multiplication process, use a counter to iterate over the bits of the multiplier.
- For each bit of the multiplier, shift the multiplicand register to the left by 1 bit, and if the current bit of the multiplier is 1, accumulate the multiplicand register in the product register.
