To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we will follow the steps of the Booth algorithm. The multiplier and multiplicand will be represented as 16-bit numbers, with the 8-bit inputs a and b extended to 16 bits by sign extension. On the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, the module will perform the multiplication process. If the reset signal is high, the multiplier and multiplicand registers will be initialized. If the reset signal is low, the module will check if the counter is less than 16 and perform the Booth algorithm's shifting operation and accumulation of the product register accordingly. Once the counter reaches 16, the ready signal will be set to 1, indicating the completion of the multiplication process.
