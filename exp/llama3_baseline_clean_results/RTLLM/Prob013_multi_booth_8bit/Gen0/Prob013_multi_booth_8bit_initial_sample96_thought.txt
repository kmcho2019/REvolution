To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: idle and multiply. The multiplier will be reset to the idle state when the reset signal is high. In the idle state, the multiplicand and multiplier registers will be loaded with the input values. In the multiply state, the Booth algorithm will be performed. The multiplicand will be left-shifted, and the product will be accumulated based on the current bit of the multiplier. The counter will be incremented until it reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication process. The ready signal will be set to 1 to indicate the completion.
