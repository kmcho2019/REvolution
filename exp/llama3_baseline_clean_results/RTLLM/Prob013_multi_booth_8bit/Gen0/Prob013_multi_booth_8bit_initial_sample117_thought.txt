To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we will use the Booth algorithm to perform the multiplication of two 8-bit inputs (a and b) and provide the product output (p) and a ready signal (rdy). We will utilize a clock signal (clk), a reset signal (reset), and a counter (ctr) to keep track of the multiplication process. When the reset signal is high, we will initialize the multiplicand and multiplier registers. During normal operation, we will check the current bit of the multiplier register and accumulate the product accordingly. Once the counter reaches 16, we will set the ready signal to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.
