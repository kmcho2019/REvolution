To implement the 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier, we will follow these steps:
1. Initialize the multiplicand and multiplier registers on the positive edge of the reset signal.
2. On each positive edge of the clock signal, check if the counter (ctr) is less than 16.
3. If the counter (ctr) is less than 16, left-shift the multiplicand register by 1 bit.
4. Check the current bit of the multiplier register (multiplier[ctr]). If it's 1, accumulate the multiplicand value in the product register (p).
5. Increment the counter (ctr) by 1.
6. Once the counter (ctr) reaches 16, set the ready signal (rdy) to 1 to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.

We will use Verilog's non-blocking assignments to update the registers on each clock cycle and ensure synchronous operation.
