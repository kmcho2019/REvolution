2. If the reset signal is low, we will check if the counter (ctr) is less than 16.
3. If the counter (ctr) is less than 16, we will perform the Booth algorithm's shifting operation by left-shifting the multiplicand register (multiplicand) by 1.
4. We will check the current bit of the multiplier register (multiplier[ctr]) and accumulate the product register (p) with the multiplicand register (multiplicand) if the bit is 1.
5. We will increment the counter (ctr) and repeat steps 2-4 until the counter (ctr) reaches 16, indicating the completion of the multiplication process.
6. Once the counter (ctr) reaches 16, we will set the ready signal (rdy) to 1.
