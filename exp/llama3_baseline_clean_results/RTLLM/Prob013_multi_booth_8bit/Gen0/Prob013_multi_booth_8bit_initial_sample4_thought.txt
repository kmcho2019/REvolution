The Booth multiplier module multi_booth_8bit can be implemented using a Finite State Machine (FSM) that controls the multiplication process. The FSM has two main states: idle and multiply. In the idle state, the multiplier and multiplicand are initialized when the reset signal is high. In the multiply state, the Booth algorithm is performed by shifting the multiplicand and accumulating the product based on the multiplier bits. The multiplication process is completed when the counter reaches 16, and the ready signal is set to 1. The Verilog implementation will utilize a 5-bit counter (ctr) to keep track of the current bit being processed in the multiplier register.
