The ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU can be designed using a case statement that checks the control signal (aluc) to perform the desired operation. The module will first assign the input operands to signed wires and then use these wires to perform the operation. For each operation, the result is assigned to the output port (r), and the flag, zero, carry, negative, and overflow outputs are determined accordingly. The shift amount for shift operations is determined by the value of 'a' or 'a[4:0]'. For the LUI operation, the upper 16 bits of 'a' are concatenated with 16 zeros to form the result.
