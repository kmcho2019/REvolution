To implement the ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, we will first define the parameters for the control signals of various operations. We will then use an always block to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal. Inside the always block, a case statement will be used to determine the operation to be performed. The result will be assigned to the output port 'r' and other status signals such as 'zero', 'carry', 'negative', 'overflow', and 'flag' will be updated accordingly.
