To implement the ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, I will first define the control signals for various operations using parameters. Then, I will use an always block with a case statement to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal (aluc). The result will be assigned to the register (res) accordingly. I will also handle the output ports, including zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag, based on the result of the operation.
