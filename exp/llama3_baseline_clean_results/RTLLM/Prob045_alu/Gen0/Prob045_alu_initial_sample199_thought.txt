The problem is to design an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU. 
The ALU should support various operations such as addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, OR, XOR, NOR, 
shift left, shift right, shift left logical, shift right logical, shift right arithmetic, 
and load upper immediate (LUI) operations. 
The ALU should also generate flags for zero, carry, negative, overflow, and a general flag.
The control signal "aluc" will determine which operation to perform.
We can use a case statement to handle different operations and assign the result to the output "r".
We will also generate the flags based on the result of the operation.
