The ALU module will be designed to perform a variety of arithmetic, logical, and shift operations based on the provided opcode. 
The module will use parameters to define the control signals for various operations, and it will utilize an always block with a case statement to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal.
For each operation, the result will be assigned to the register accordingly, and the status flags (zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag) will be updated accordingly.
