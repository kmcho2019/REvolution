To design an Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU) for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, we will utilize Verilog parameters to define control signals for various operations and a case statement to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal. The ALU will handle operations such as ADD, SUB, AND, OR, SLT, SLTU, shift operations, and LUI. We will also generate status flags like zero, carry, negative, overflow, and a general flag for specific operations.
