To design an ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, we first define parameters for the control signals of various operations. We use a case statement inside an always block to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal (aluc). The result is assigned to the register (res) accordingly, and output ports are updated based on the result. For shift operations, the shift amount is determined by the value of 'a' or 'a[4:0]'. For the LUI operation, the upper 16 bits of 'a' are concatenated with 16 zeros to form the result.
