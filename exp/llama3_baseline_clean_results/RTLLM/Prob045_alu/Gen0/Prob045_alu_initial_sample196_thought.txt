The ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) is a critical component of the CPU, responsible for performing various arithmetic and logical operations. To implement the ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, we need to define the supported operations and their corresponding opcodes. We will use parameters to define these opcodes and then use a case statement to perform the appropriate operation based on the control signal (aluc). We will also calculate the zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag outputs based on the result of the operation.
