Then, we will use these parameters to control a case statement inside an always block to perform the corresponding operations on the two input operands 'a' and 'b'. The result will be assigned to the output 'r', and the flags 'zero', 'carry', 'negative', 'overflow', and 'flag' will be updated accordingly.
