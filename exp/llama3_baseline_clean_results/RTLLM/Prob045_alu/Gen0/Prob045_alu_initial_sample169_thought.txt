To implement the ALU, we need to consider the different operations supported by the MIPS-ISA CPU. We will use a case statement to determine the operation based on the control signal (aluc). For each operation, we will calculate the result and assign it to the output register (res). We will also update the flags (zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag) accordingly. 
For shift operations, we will use the shift amount from the operand 'a' or 'a[4:0]'. For the LUI operation, we will concatenate the upper 16 bits of 'a' with 16 zeros. 
If the control signal does not match any defined operation, we will assign the result as 'z' (high-impedance).
