To implement the ALU for a 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU, I will define a module named "alu" that takes two 32-bit operands "a" and "b", a 6-bit control signal "aluc", and generates a 32-bit result "r" along with several flag outputs. The module will use a case statement to determine the operation to be performed based on the control signal "aluc". The result will be assigned to the register "res" accordingly. I will handle each operation separately, including arithmetic, logical, shift, and load immediate operations. Additionally, I will generate the flag outputs "zero", "carry", "negative", "overflow", and "flag" based on the result and the control signal.
