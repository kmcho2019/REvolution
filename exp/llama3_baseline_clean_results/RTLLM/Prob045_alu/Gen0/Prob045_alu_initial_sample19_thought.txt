The ALU for the 32-bit MIPS-ISA CPU is implemented using a case statement within an always block in Verilog. The module takes in two 32-bit operands 'a' and 'b', a 6-bit control signal 'aluc', and outputs the 32-bit result 'r', along with flags 'zero', 'carry', 'negative', 'overflow', and 'flag'. The 'aluc' signal determines the operation to be performed, and the result is assigned accordingly. The module handles various operations such as addition, subtraction, bitwise AND, OR, XOR, NOR, shift operations, and load upper immediate (LUI). The flags are updated based on the operation and the result.
