The fixed_point_subtractor module can be designed using Verilog by performing the following steps:

1. Determine the sign of the input operands a and b based on the most significant bit (MSB) of each.
2. Perform subtraction or addition based on the signs of the operands.
3. For same-sign subtraction, directly subtract b from a and maintain the same sign.
4. For different-sign operations, compare the absolute values of a and b, add them, and assign the sign based on the comparison.
5. Handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
6. Ensure that the subtraction operation maintains the required fixed-point precision defined by parameters Q and N.

The Verilog implementation will involve conditional statements to handle these different cases and perform the necessary arithmetic operations while maintaining the specified precision.
