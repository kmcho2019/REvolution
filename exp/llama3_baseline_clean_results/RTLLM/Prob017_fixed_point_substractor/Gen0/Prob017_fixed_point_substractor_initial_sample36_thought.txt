The fixed_point_subtractor module will be implemented as a parameterized Verilog module. It will take in two N-bit fixed-point numbers, 'a' and 'b', and produce an N-bit output, 'c', representing the result of the subtraction operation. The module will handle same sign and different sign subtractions, as well as the edge case where the result is zero. To maintain precision, the module will use the given parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits) to accurately perform the subtraction operation.

The module will first check the signs of 'a' and 'b'. If the signs are the same, it will subtract the fractional and integer parts directly. If the signs are different, it will add the absolute values of 'a' and 'b' and determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of 'a' and 'b'. The result will be stored in the internal register 'res' and then assigned to the output 'c'. To handle the edge case where the result is zero, the module will explicitly set the sign bit to 0.
