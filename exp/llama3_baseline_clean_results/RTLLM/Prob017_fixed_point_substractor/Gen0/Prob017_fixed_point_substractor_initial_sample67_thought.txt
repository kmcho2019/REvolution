The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed with parameterized values Q and N. The inputs a and b will be processed based on their signs to perform the subtraction operation. The internal register res will store the result of the subtraction operation. Same sign subtraction will be performed by directly subtracting the fractional and integer parts of a and b. Different sign subtraction will involve adding the absolute values of a and b, and the result's sign will depend on the relative sizes of a and b. Handling of zero will involve explicitly setting the sign bit to 0 when the result is zero. The fixed-point precision will be maintained by adhering to the parameters Q and N during the subtraction operation.
