The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed using Verilog, taking into account the specified parameters, inputs, and outputs. The subtraction operation will be performed based on the signs of the input operands. If the signs are the same, a straightforward subtraction will be performed. If the signs are different, the absolute values of the operands will be added, and the result's sign will depend on the relative sizes of the operands. The result will be stored in the internal register 'res' and then assigned to the output port 'c'. The fixed-point precision will be maintained throughout the operation.
