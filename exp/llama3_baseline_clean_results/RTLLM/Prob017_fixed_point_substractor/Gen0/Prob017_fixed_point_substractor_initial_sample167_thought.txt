The fixed_point_subtractor module will be implemented with parameterized values Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits) to maintain precise arithmetic operations. It will handle two N-bit fixed-point inputs, a and b, and produce an N-bit output, c. The subtraction operation will consider the signs of the inputs and perform same sign or different sign subtraction accordingly. The result will be stored in an internal register, res, and then assigned to the output port, c. To ensure accurate results, the module will handle edge cases, such as zero results and maintain the fixed-point precision.
