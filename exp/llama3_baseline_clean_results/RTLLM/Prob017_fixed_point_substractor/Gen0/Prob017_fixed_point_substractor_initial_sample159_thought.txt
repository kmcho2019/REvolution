The fixed_point_subtractor module will perform subtraction operations on two fixed-point input operands, 'a' and 'b', with precise arithmetic operations and maintaining fixed precision defined by parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits). The module will handle same sign subtraction, different sign subtraction, and edge cases such as zero result. It will utilize internal registers to store the result of the subtraction operation. The implementation will consider the sign bits and the fractional and integer parts of the input operands during the subtraction process.
