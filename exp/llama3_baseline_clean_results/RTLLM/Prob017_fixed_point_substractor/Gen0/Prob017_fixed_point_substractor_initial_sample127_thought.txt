To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we will first determine the signs of the input operands a and b. Based on the signs, we will perform either same sign subtraction or different sign subtraction. The result of the subtraction operation will be stored in the internal register res. We will handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The fixed-point precision will be maintained by considering the parameters Q and N for both integer and fractional parts.
