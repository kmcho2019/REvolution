To implement a fixed-point subtractor in Verilog, we need to consider the cases of same sign subtraction and different sign subtraction. For same sign subtraction, we can directly subtract the two numbers. For different sign subtraction, we need to determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of the two numbers. We also need to handle the edge case where the result is zero. The precision of the fixed-point representation is defined by the parameters Q and N, which represent the number of fractional bits and the total number of bits, respectively.
