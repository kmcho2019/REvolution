To implement a fixed-point subtractor in Verilog, we first need to understand the representation of fixed-point numbers. The number of fractional bits (Q) determines the precision of the fractional part, and the total number of bits (N) defines the overall size of the fixed-point number, including both integer and fractional parts. We will handle subtraction based on the signs of the inputs and ensure that the result maintains the same precision. For same sign subtraction, we simply perform bit-wise subtraction. For different sign subtraction, we compare the absolute values of the inputs to determine the sign of the result. We also need to handle the case where the result is zero to ensure the sign bit is correctly set. The result is stored in an internal register before being output.
