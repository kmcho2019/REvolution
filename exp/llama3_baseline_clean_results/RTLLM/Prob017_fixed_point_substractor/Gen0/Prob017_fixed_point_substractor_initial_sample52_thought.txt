To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we will follow these steps:
- Determine the sign of the inputs a and b.
- If the signs are the same, perform subtraction directly.
- If the signs are different, add the absolute values and determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of a and b.
- Use internal registers to store the result of the subtraction operation.
- Handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
- Ensure the fixed-point precision is maintained according to the parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits).
