To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we first need to understand the requirements for the fixed-point representation and subtraction logic. The module takes two N-bit fixed-point inputs, a and b, and produces an N-bit fixed-point output, c, which is the result of subtracting b from a. We must handle cases where the signs of a and b are the same (same sign subtraction) and different (different sign subtraction), ensuring the correct sign for the result based on the relative magnitudes of a and b. Additionally, we must explicitly handle the case when the result is zero to ensure the sign bit is correctly set to 0. The parameters Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits) define the precision of the fixed-point arithmetic. We will use these parameters to guide our implementation, ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the specified precision.
