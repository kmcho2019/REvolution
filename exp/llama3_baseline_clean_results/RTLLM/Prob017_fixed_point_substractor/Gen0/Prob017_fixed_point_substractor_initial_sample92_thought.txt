To implement a fixed-point subtractor module in Verilog, we'll first define the module with its parameters, input and output ports, and internal registers. The module's logic will handle same sign subtraction, different sign subtraction, and the edge case of a zero result. We'll use Verilog's parameterization to allow for flexibility in terms of the number of fractional bits (Q) and the total number of bits (N) used in the fixed-point representation. The subtraction logic will consider the signs of the inputs and compute the result accordingly, ensuring the correct sign and precision in the output.
