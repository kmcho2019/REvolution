To design the fixed_point_subtractor module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Define the module with parameters Q and N, and input/output ports a, b, and c.
2. Determine the sign bits of the input operands a and b.
3. Perform same-sign subtraction when the signs of a and b are the same.
4. Perform different-sign subtraction (essentially addition with sign adjustment) when the signs of a and b differ.
5. Handle the zero result case by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
6. Use an internal register res to store the result of the subtraction operation.
7. Assign the result to the output port c.
We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators and bit manipulation to achieve these steps.
