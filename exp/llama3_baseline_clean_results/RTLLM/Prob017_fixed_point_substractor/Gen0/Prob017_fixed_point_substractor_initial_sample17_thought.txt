To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we need to consider the rules for same sign and different sign subtraction in fixed-point arithmetic. The same sign subtraction involves a straightforward subtraction of the fractional and integer parts, maintaining the sign of the inputs. Different sign subtraction requires comparing the absolute values of the inputs and determining the result's sign based on their relative sizes. Handling zero as a special case ensures the sign bit is correctly set. The fixed-point precision, defined by parameters Q and N, must be maintained throughout the operation.
