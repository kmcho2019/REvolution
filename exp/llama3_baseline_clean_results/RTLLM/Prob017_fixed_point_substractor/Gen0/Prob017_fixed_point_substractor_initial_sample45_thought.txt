We'll then define the input and output ports, as well as an internal register to store the result. The subtraction operation will be performed based on the signs of the input operands. If the signs are the same, we directly subtract the absolute values of the operands. If the signs are different, we add the absolute values of the operands and determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of the absolute values. We will also handle the zero result case by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
