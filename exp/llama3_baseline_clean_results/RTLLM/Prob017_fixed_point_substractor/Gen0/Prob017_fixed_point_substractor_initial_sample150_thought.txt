To implement a fixed-point subtractor in Verilog, we need to consider the rules for same sign subtraction and different sign subtraction, ensuring that the fractional and integer parts are handled correctly while maintaining the specified precision. The module will be parameterized by Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits), allowing for flexibility in the representation of the fixed-point numbers. The subtraction operation will be performed based on the signs of the input operands, and the result's sign will be determined accordingly. Special care will be taken to handle the case when the result is zero.
