To implement a fixed-point subtractor module, we first need to understand the rules of fixed-point arithmetic, especially considering the handling of signs, integer and fractional parts, and edge cases like zero results. 
The module will take two N-bit fixed-point inputs, a and b, and produce an N-bit output, c, representing the result of the subtraction a - b. 
We'll use a parameterized approach with Q representing the number of fractional bits and N representing the total number of bits, ensuring flexibility in the precision of the arithmetic operations.
The subtraction logic will consider same sign subtraction, different sign subtraction, and handle the zero result case by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
