To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, I will first determine the sign of the inputs a and b by checking the most significant bit (MSB) of each input. Based on the signs, I will perform either same sign subtraction or different sign subtraction. I will use a temporary register res to store the result of the subtraction operation. To handle the edge case where the result is zero, I will explicitly set the sign bit of the result to 0. The parameters Q and N will define the precision of the fixed-point representation, ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the necessary precision for both integer and fractional parts.
