The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed using Verilog, incorporating parameterization for the number of fractional bits (Q) and total bits (N). It will handle same-sign and different-sign subtraction, considering the integer and fractional parts separately. The module will utilize a single internal register, res, to store the result. To maintain precision, the fixed-point representation will be carefully managed during the subtraction process. The sign of the result will be determined based on the signs and magnitudes of the input operands. Special consideration will be given to handling the zero result case to ensure the sign bit is correctly set to 0.
