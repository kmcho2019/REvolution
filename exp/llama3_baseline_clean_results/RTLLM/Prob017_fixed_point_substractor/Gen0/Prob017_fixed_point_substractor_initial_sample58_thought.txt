The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed to handle fixed-point arithmetic operations with precise control over the precision. It will take into account the signs of the input operands and perform the subtraction accordingly, handling cases where the signs are the same or different. The module will also handle the case when the result is zero. The fixed-point representation will be parameterized by the number of fractional bits (Q) and the total number of bits (N), ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the necessary precision for both integer and fractional parts.
