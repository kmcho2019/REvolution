To implement a fixed-point subtractor module, we first need to understand the representation of fixed-point numbers. In this case, we are using Q fractional bits and N total bits. The module will take two N-bit fixed-point inputs, a and b, and produce an N-bit output, c, representing the result of the subtraction. We will handle same sign and different sign subtractions separately, considering the cases where the signs of a and b are the same or different. For different sign subtractions, we will compare the absolute values of a and b to determine the sign of the result. We will also handle the edge case where the result is zero.
