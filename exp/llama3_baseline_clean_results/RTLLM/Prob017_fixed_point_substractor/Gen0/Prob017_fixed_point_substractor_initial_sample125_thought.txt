To implement the fixed-point subtractor module, we will first determine the signs of the input operands and then perform the subtraction based on these signs. If the signs are the same, we will directly subtract the two operands. If the signs are different, we will compare the absolute values of the operands and perform addition or subtraction accordingly. We will also handle the zero result case by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The fixed-point precision will be maintained by ensuring that the total number of bits (N) and the number of fractional bits (Q) are used correctly throughout the operation.
