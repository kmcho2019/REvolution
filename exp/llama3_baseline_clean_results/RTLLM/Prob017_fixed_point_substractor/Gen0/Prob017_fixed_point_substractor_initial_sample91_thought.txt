To implement the fixed-point subtractor module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Determine the sign of the input operands and the operation to be performed based on their signs.
2. Perform subtraction when the signs are the same and addition when the signs are different.
3. Handle the case when the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
4. Ensure that the result maintains the specified fixed-point precision defined by parameters Q and N.

The fixed-point representation assumes that the most significant bit (MSB) is the sign bit, and the remaining bits are divided into integer and fractional parts based on the parameter Q.
