The fixed_point_subtractor module is designed to perform precise fixed-point subtraction operations with a specified number of fractional bits (Q) and total bits (N). To achieve this, we first need to identify the signs of the input operands 'a' and 'b' and then perform the subtraction accordingly. If the signs are the same, we directly subtract 'b' from 'a'. If the signs are different, we determine the sign of the result based on the relative magnitudes of 'a' and 'b'. We also need to handle the case when the result is zero to ensure the sign bit is correctly set to 0.
