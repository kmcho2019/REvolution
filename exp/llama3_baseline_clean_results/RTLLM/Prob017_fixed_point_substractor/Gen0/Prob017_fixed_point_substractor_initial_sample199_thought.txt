The fixed_point_subtractor module will perform subtraction on two fixed-point numbers, a and b, with the same sign and different signs. It will also handle zero results by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The module's parameters Q and N will define the fixed-point precision, ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the necessary precision for both integer and fractional parts. The module's input ports a and b will be N-bit fixed-point numbers, and the output port c will also be an N-bit fixed-point number. The internal register res will store the result of the subtraction operation.
