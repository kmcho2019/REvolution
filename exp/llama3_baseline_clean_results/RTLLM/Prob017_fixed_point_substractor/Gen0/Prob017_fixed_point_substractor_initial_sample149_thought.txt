To implement a fixed-point subtractor, we need to consider the cases where the signs of the input operands are the same or different. When the signs are the same, we simply perform subtraction, maintaining the sign of the result as the same as the inputs. When the signs are different, we compare the absolute values of the inputs to determine the sign of the result. Additionally, we handle the case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The precision of the fixed-point arithmetic is defined by the parameters Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits), ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the necessary precision for both integer and fractional parts.
