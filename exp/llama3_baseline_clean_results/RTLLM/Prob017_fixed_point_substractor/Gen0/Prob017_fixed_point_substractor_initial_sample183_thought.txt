To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we first need to determine the signs of the input operands a and b. We then perform subtraction or addition based on the signs, handling the cases for same sign and different sign separately. For same sign subtraction, we directly subtract the fractional and integer parts of b from a, keeping the same sign as the inputs. For different sign subtraction, we compare the absolute values of a and b, determine the sign of the result based on which is greater, and perform the addition of their absolute values. We need to handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The result is then stored in the internal register res and assigned to the output port c.
