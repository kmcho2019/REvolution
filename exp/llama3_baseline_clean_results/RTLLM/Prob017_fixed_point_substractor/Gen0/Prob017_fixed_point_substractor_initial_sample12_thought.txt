To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we first need to understand the arithmetic operations involved in fixed-point subtraction. The module should handle same sign subtraction and different sign subtraction. It should also account for the precision defined by the parameters Q and N. For same sign subtraction, we simply perform a standard subtraction, while for different sign subtraction, we add the absolute values of the inputs and determine the sign based on their relative sizes. The result should be stored in an internal register and then assigned to the output port. We must also ensure proper handling of the zero result case by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
