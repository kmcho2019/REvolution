To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we will use the following approach:
- Determine the sign of the input operands a and b by checking their most significant bits (MSBs).
- Perform same sign subtraction when the signs of a and b are the same. This involves subtracting the fractional and integer parts directly.
- For different sign subtraction, compare the absolute values of a and b to determine the sign of the result.
- Use the parameters Q and N to ensure accurate fixed-point representation and precision throughout the subtraction operation.
- Handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
- Utilize internal registers to store the result of the subtraction operation.
