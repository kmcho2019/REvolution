To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we need to first identify the signs of the input operands a and b. We then perform the subtraction based on the signs. If the signs are the same, we directly subtract b from a. If the signs are different, we add the absolute values of a and b and determine the sign of the result based on their relative sizes. We also need to handle the case when the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The subtraction operation should maintain the fixed-point precision defined by the parameters Q and N.
