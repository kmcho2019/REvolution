The module fixed_point_subtractor will be implemented with parameterized values Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits). It will perform fixed-point subtraction based on the signs of the input operands a and b. If a and b have the same sign, their integer and fractional parts will be subtracted directly. If they have different signs, the absolute values of a and b will be added, and the result's sign will depend on the relative sizes of a and b. The result will be stored in the internal register res and then outputted as c. The module will handle zero results by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
