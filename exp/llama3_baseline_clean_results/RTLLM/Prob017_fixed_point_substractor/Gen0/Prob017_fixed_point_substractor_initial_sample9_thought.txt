To implement the fixed-point subtractor, we need to first check the signs of the input operands. If they have the same sign, we simply perform a straightforward subtraction. If the signs are different, we compare the absolute values of the operands to determine the sign of the result. We must also handle the edge case where the result is zero. The fixed-point precision is maintained by ensuring that the subtraction is performed accurately for both the integer and fractional parts as defined by the parameters Q and N.
