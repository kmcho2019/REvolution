To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, I will first determine the sign bits of the input operands a and b. Then, based on the signs, I will perform either same sign subtraction or different sign subtraction. For same sign subtraction, I will directly subtract b from a. For different sign subtraction, I will add the absolute values of a and b and then determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of a and b. I will handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. I will use parameterized values Q and N to ensure accurate fixed-point precision.
