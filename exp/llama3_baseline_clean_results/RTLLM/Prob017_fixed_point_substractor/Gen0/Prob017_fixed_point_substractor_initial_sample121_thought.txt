The fixed_point_subtractor module will perform subtraction on two fixed-point numbers, a and b. It will take into account the signs of the input operands and handle the subtraction accordingly. The result will be stored in the internal register res and then assigned to the output port c. The module will maintain the specified fixed-point precision defined by parameters Q and N. The signs of the input operands will be used to determine whether to perform a same-sign or different-sign subtraction. The result's sign will be determined based on the relative sizes of the input operands and their signs. The module will also handle the edge case where the result is zero.
