The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed with a focus on handling same sign and different sign subtraction cases separately. For same sign subtraction, it will simply perform the subtraction operation. For different sign subtraction, it will determine the sign of the result based on the comparison of the absolute values of the input operands. The module will also handle the edge case when the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The fixed-point precision will be maintained according to the defined parameters Q and N. 
The design will utilize internal registers to store the result temporarily before assigning it to the output port.
To ensure accurate arithmetic operations, the module will be designed to work within the specified fixed-point representation, maintaining the required precision for both integer and fractional parts.
