The fixed_point_subtractor module will perform fixed-point subtraction based on the signs of the input operands a and b. It will handle same sign subtraction by directly subtracting the two numbers, and different sign subtraction by adding the absolute values of a and b and determining the sign of the result based on their relative sizes. The module will also explicitly handle the case where the result is zero to ensure the sign bit is correctly set. The fixed-point precision is defined by the parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits), ensuring accurate subtraction while maintaining the necessary precision.
