The fixed_point_subtractor module will be designed to handle precise arithmetic operations with fixed precision. It will take two N-bit fixed-point inputs, a and b, and produce an N-bit output representing their difference. The module will be parameterized by Q, the number of fractional bits, and N, the total number of bits. It will correctly handle same sign and different sign subtractions, as well as the edge case where the result is zero. The implementation will ensure that the subtraction is performed accurately while maintaining the necessary precision for both integer and fractional parts.
