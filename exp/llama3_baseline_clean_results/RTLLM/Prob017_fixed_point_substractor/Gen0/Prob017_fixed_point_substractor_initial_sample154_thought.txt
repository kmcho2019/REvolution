To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we need to follow these steps:
1. Determine the signs of the input operands a and b.
2. Perform subtraction based on the signs of a and b.
   - If the signs are the same, subtract b from a and maintain the sign.
   - If the signs are different, compare the absolute values of a and b, add them if necessary, and determine the sign of the result based on the comparison.
3. Handle the case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0.
4. Ensure that the subtraction is performed while maintaining the fixed-point precision defined by the parameters Q (fractional bits) and N (total bits).

We will use the built-in Verilog operators for arithmetic operations and conditional statements for handling different sign scenarios and zero result cases.
