To implement the fixed-point subtractor, we will first identify the sign bits of both inputs and then perform the subtraction based on the signs. If the signs are the same, we can directly subtract the inputs. If the signs are different, we will compare the absolute values to determine the sign of the result. We will handle the case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. We will use the parameters Q and N to ensure accurate precision for both integer and fractional parts.
