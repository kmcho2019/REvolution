To implement the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we need to follow these steps: 
1. Extend the input signals by adding 4 zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
2. Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products.
3. Store the intermediate sum values in registers.
4. On the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal, perform add operations and update the registers.
5. On the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal, calculate the final product and update the product output.
