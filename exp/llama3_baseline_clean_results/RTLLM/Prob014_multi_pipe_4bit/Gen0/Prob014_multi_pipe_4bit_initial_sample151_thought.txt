The design of the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier (multi_pipe_4bit) will be implemented using two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process. 
The multiplicand (mul_a) and multiplier (mul_b) are first extended by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions. 
Then, the multiplication operation is performed for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) using a generate block to calculate the partial products.
The module uses registers to store the intermediate sum values and performs add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
Finally, the module calculates the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
