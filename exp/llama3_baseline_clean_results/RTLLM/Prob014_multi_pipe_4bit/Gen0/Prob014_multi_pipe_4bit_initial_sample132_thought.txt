To design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we will implement a module named "multi_pipe_4bit" with two levels of registers. The multiplier uses a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products. We will use registers to store the intermediate sum values and update them on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
