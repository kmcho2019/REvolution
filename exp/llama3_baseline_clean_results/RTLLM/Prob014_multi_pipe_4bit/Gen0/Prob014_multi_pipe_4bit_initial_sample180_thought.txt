- Extend the input signals (mul_a and mul_b) by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
- Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products.
- Use registers to store the intermediate sum values.
- Perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
- Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).

The module will be named multi_pipe_4bit, and it will have input ports clk, rst_n, mul_a, and mul_b, and an output port mul_out.
