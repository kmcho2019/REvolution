The approach to designing a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier is to create a module with two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process. The multiplication operation involves generating partial products for each bit position of the multiplier and then adding these partial products to obtain the final product. This will be achieved using a generate block to perform the multiplication and addition operations. We will use two registers to store the intermediate sum values. The module will be designed with synchronous reset and will operate on the positive edge of the clock signal. The output will be a 8-bit product.
