3. Use registers to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
4. Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
