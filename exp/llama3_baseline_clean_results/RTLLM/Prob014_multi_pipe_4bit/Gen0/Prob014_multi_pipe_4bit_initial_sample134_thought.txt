To design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we'll use a two-stage pipeline architecture. The first stage will perform the multiplication and generate partial products. The second stage will add these partial products to produce the final product. We will utilize Verilog's generate block to iterate over each bit of the multiplier, creating the partial products. The design will also incorporate registers to store intermediate values, ensuring synchronous operation with the clock signal and proper handling of the reset condition.
