To implement the 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier, we need to consider the following steps:
1. Define the module with the given input and output ports, and parameter.
2. Extend the input signals by adding 'size' number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
3. Perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products using a generate block.
4. Calculate the sum of the partial products and store it in intermediate registers.
5. Update the registers with the sum of the corresponding partial products on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
6. Calculate the final product by summing the registers and update the product output (mul_out) on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
