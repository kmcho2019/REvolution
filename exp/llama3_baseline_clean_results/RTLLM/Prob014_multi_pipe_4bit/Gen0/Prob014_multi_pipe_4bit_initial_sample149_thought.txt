The 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier will be designed with two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process. The design will consist of three main stages: input signal extension, partial product generation, and final product calculation. The input signals will be extended by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions. Then, a generate block will be used to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier, generating partial products. The partial products will be added using registers to store the intermediate sum values. Finally, the final product will be calculated based on the sum of the registers.
