The 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier can be implemented using two stages of registers and a generate block to perform the multiplication. 
In the first stage, the partial products are generated and added using the generate block. 
In the second stage, the intermediate sum values are stored in registers. 
The final product is calculated by adding the sum of the registers. 
The reset signal (rst_n) is used to reset the registers and the product output (mul_out) when it is low. 
The module should be designed to handle the multiplication of two 4-bit numbers and produce an 8-bit product.
