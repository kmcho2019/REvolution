The module multi_pipe_4bit will be designed as a pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers. The input signals mul_a and mul_b will be extended by adding 4 number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions. Then, the module will perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products using a generate block. The partial products will be added and stored in registers. Finally, the module will calculate the final product by summing up the registers and output it as mul_out.
