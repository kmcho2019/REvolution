The 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier will be designed using a two-level register pipeline. 
The module multi_pipe_4bit will be implemented with two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process. 
The input signals mul_a and mul_b will be extended by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions. 
A generate block will be used to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products. 
The partial products will be added using registers to store the intermediate sum values. 
The final product will be calculated and updated on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
