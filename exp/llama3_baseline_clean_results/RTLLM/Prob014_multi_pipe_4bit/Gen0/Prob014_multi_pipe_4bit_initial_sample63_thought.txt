To implement the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we will use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate partial products. We will use registers to store intermediate sum values and control the multiplication process. The design will have two levels of registers. The first level will store the partial products and the second level will store the sum of the partial products. The final product will be calculated by adding the values stored in the second level of registers. The design will be implemented using Verilog HDL.
