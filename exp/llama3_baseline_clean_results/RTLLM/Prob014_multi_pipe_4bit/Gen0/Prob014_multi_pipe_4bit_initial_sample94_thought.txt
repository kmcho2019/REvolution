To design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier with two levels of registers, we need to follow these steps:
- Define the module `multi_pipe_4bit` with the required input and output ports.
- Implement the extension of input signals by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
- Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products.
- Use registers to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
- Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal and update the product output.
