To design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we can follow these steps:
1. Define the module with the given name, input ports, output ports, and parameter.
2. Extend the input signals (mul_a and mul_b) by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
3. Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products.
4. Calculate the partial products based on the corresponding bits in the multiplier.
5. Use registers to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
6. Update the registers with the sum of the corresponding partial products when the reset signal (rst_n) is high.
7. Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
8. Update the product output (mul_out) with the sum of registers when the reset signal (rst_n) is high.

The module will have two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process.
