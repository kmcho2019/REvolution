The 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier will be designed using a 2-stage pipeline. 
The first stage will generate the partial products for each bit position of the multiplier, 
and the second stage will add these partial products to produce the final product.
The design will utilize a generate block to instantiate the multiplication logic for each bit position.
Two levels of registers will be used to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process.
The reset signal will be used to clear the registers and output during a reset condition.
