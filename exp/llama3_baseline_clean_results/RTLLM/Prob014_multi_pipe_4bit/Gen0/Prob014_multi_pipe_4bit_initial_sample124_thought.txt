To design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we need to implement the following steps: 
1. Extend the input signals by adding 4 zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
2. Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products.
3. Use registers to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
4. Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
We will use two levels of registers to store the intermediate values and control the multiplication process.
