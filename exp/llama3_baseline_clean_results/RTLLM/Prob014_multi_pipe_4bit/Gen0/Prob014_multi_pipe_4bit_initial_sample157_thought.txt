To implement the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we will first extend the input signals by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions. Then, we will use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products. The partial products will be added using registers to store the intermediate sum values. The final product will be calculated on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
