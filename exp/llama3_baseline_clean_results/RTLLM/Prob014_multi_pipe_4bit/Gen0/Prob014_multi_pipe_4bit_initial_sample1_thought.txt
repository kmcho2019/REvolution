The 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier module, multi_pipe_4bit, will be designed using two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process. 
The input signals mul_a and mul_b will be extended by adding 4 number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
A generate block will be used to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and generate the partial products.
Registers will be used to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
On the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n), the module will calculate the final product and update the product output (mul_out) accordingly.
