The 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier can be designed using Verilog. The design will consist of a module named "multi_pipe_4bit" with the specified input and output ports. The multiplicand and multiplier will be extended by adding 4 zero bits at the most significant bit positions. A generate block will be used to perform the multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products. The partial products will be added using registers to store the intermediate sum values. The final product will be calculated and updated on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
