The module multi_pipe_4bit will be designed as a 4-bit unsigned pipeline multiplier. 
It takes two 4-bit inputs (mul_a and mul_b) and produces an 8-bit output (mul_out) after two clock cycles.
The module uses two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process.
On the first clock cycle, the module calculates the partial products for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) and stores the sum in the first level of registers.
On the second clock cycle, the module calculates the final product by adding the partial products stored in the first level of registers and stores the result in the second level of registers.
The final product is then output from the second level of registers.
The module also includes an active-low reset signal (rst_n) that resets the registers and the output to 0 when low.
