To design the fixed_point_adder module, we first consider the parameterization for the number of fractional bits (Q) and the total number of bits (N). The module will handle addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. Absolute value addition is performed when the signs are the same, and absolute value subtraction is performed when the signs are different. We must also manage the precision and handle potential overflows. The result is stored in an internal register (res) before being output.
