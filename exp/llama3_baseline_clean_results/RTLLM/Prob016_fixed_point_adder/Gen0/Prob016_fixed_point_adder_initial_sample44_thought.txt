To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we first need to determine the sign of the input operands `a` and `b`. If both have the same sign (either both positive or both negative), we proceed with absolute value addition. If the signs are different, we perform absolute value subtraction. The result's sign is determined based on the signs of `a` and `b` and the comparison of their absolute values. To handle precision and overflow, we ensure that the operands are treated as fixed-point numbers with `Q` fractional bits and that the result fits within the `N`-bit range.
