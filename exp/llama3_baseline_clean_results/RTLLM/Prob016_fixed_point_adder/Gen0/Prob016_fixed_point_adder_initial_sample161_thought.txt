2. Perform absolute value addition or subtraction based on the signs of a and b.
3. Ensure the precision of the operands and the result by maintaining consistency in the fractional part, as defined by parameter Q.
4. Handle overflow by monitoring the MSB to guarantee the result stays within the N-bit range.
