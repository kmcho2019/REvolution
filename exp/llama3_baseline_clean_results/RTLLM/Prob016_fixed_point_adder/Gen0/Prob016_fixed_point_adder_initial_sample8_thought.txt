To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder in Verilog, we first need to understand the requirements of fixed-point arithmetic, including handling of sign bits, integer and fractional parts, and overflow. The module should take two input parameters: Q (the number of fractional bits) and N (the total number of bits). 

We will implement the adder with the ability to perform absolute value addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. For addition, if the signs of the operands are the same, we add their absolute values and keep the sign consistent. For subtraction, we compare the absolute values of the operands and perform the subtraction based on which operand is larger, setting the sign of the result accordingly.

To manage precision, we ensure that the operations are consistent with the specified number of fractional bits (Q) and that the result fits within the N-bit range, handling overflows by observing the most significant bit (MSB) of the result.

Our implementation will use Verilog's parameterized module feature to make the adder flexible and reusable for different bit widths and precisions.
