To design a parameterized fixed-point adder, we need to consider the precision, overflow handling, and the rules for addition and subtraction based on the signs of the operands. The module will take two N-bit fixed-point numbers as input and produce an N-bit result. We will use a register to store the result internally. For addition and subtraction, we will compare the most significant bits of the operands to determine whether to add or subtract their absolute values and set the sign of the result accordingly. We will also manage overflow by ensuring the result fits within the N-bit range.
