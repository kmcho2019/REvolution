To design a parameterized fixed-point adder, we need to consider the different cases for addition and subtraction based on the sign bits of the input operands. The adder will perform absolute value addition or subtraction based on the sign bits. The result's sign bit will be determined accordingly. We will also manage overflow internally to ensure the result fits within the N-bit range. The use of parameters Q and N allows for the flexible configuration of the adder's precision and bit width.
