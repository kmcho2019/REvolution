To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder, we need to consider the different scenarios for addition and subtraction based on the sign bits of the input operands. The design will utilize a combinational logic approach to perform the operations and manage precision. We will first determine if the operation is an addition or subtraction based on the sign bits, then perform the operation, and finally handle any potential overflows. The result will be stored in an internal register `res` before being assigned to the output port `c`. The design will ensure the precision of the fixed-point numbers is maintained throughout the operation.
