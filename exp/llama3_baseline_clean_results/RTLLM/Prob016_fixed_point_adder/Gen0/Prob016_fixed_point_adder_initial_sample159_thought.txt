The fixed_point_adder module will be designed with a focus on handling fixed-point arithmetic operations, taking into account the precision specified by the parameter Q. The module will differentiate between addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. It will also manage overflow by ensuring the result stays within the N-bit range. The implementation involves determining the larger absolute value when the signs of the operands differ and setting the sign bit of the result accordingly. Precision will be maintained by considering the Q fractional bits during arithmetic operations.
