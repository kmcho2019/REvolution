To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we need to first check the signs of the two input operands 'a' and 'b'. Based on their signs, we decide whether to perform addition or subtraction. We use the 'res' register to store the result of the operation. For addition, we simply add the two operands and set the sign bit of the result accordingly. For subtraction, we determine the larger absolute value and perform the subtraction. We also need to handle overflow by checking the MSB of the result.
