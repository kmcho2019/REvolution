To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder, we need to first identify the MSBs of the input operands `a` and `b` to determine whether to perform addition or subtraction. We then compare the absolute values of `a` and `b` if their MSBs are different to decide the operation and the sign of the result. The result is stored in an internal register `res`, and the precision is ensured by maintaining consistency in the number of fractional bits `Q` across inputs and output. Overflow handling is implicit in the fixed-point representation, as the MSB will indicate whether the result exceeds the representable range.
