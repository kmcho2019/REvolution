To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we need to consider both addition and subtraction cases based on the signs of the input operands `a` and `b`. The operation to be performed depends on whether the most significant bits (MSBs) of `a` and `b` are the same or different. For addition, we simply add the absolute values of `a` and `b` and set the MSB of the result to match the MSBs of `a` and `b`. For subtraction, we determine the larger absolute value and perform the subtraction, setting the MSB of the result based on whether the result is positive or negative. We must also handle overflow by observing the MSB to ensure the result fits within the N-bit range. The precision of the fixed-point arithmetic is determined by the parameter `Q`, which represents the number of fractional bits.
