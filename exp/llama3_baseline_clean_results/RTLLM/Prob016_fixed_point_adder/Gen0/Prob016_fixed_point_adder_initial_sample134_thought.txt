The fixed_point_adder module will handle arithmetic operations for fixed-point numbers with a specified precision. It will take two N-bit inputs, a and b, and produce an N-bit output, c. The number of fractional bits (precision) is determined by parameter Q. The module will perform absolute value addition or subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. It will also manage overflow by observing the most significant bit (MSB) to ensure the result fits within the N-bit range.
