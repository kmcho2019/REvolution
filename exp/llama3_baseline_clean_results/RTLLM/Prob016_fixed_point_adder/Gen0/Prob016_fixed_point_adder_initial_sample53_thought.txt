To implement the parameterized fixed-point adder, we will first determine the sign of the inputs and then perform either addition or subtraction based on the signs. The addition or subtraction will be done on the absolute values of the inputs. After obtaining the result, we will adjust the sign of the result based on the original signs of the inputs and manage any potential overflow. We will use parameterized Verilog to allow for flexibility in the number of bits (N) and the number of fractional bits (Q).
