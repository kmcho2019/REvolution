1. Determine the sign bits of the input operands `a` and `b` to decide whether to perform addition or subtraction.
2. Compare the absolute values of `a` and `b` to determine the result of the operation.
3. Based on the result, set the sign bit of the output `c`.
4. Perform the required arithmetic operation (addition or subtraction) while maintaining the specified precision `Q`.
5. Handle overflow by ensuring the result fits within the `N`-bit range.
