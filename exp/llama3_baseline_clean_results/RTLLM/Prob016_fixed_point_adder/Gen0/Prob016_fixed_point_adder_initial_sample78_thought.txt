To implement the parameterized fixed-point adder, I will design a module that takes two N-bit fixed-point numbers as input and produces their sum as output. The module will handle both addition and subtraction, depending on the signs of the input operands. It will also ensure that the result is represented with the correct precision and handle overflow conditions. The design will be implemented in Verilog, utilizing a parameterized module to accommodate different numbers of bits (N) and fractional bits (Q).
