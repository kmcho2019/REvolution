To implement the fixed-point adder module, we need to consider several key aspects: 
- Determine if the operation is an addition or subtraction based on the signs of the inputs.
- Perform the absolute value addition or subtraction based on the comparison of the inputs' absolute values.
- Manage the sign of the result based on the signs of the inputs and the outcome of the operation.
- Ensure the precision of the fixed-point arithmetic is maintained consistently across the inputs and output.
- Handle overflow by checking the result's most significant bit to ensure it fits within the defined N-bit range.

We will use Verilog's built-in operators and conditional statements to achieve these requirements. We will also utilize parameters for N and Q to make the module parameterized and flexible for different fixed-point representations.
