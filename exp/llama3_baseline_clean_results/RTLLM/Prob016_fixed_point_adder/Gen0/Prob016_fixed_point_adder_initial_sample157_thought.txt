To implement the fixed-point adder module, we need to consider the sign bits of the input operands `a` and `b` to determine whether to perform addition or subtraction. 
The operation is then carried out based on the absolute values of `a` and `b`, considering their fractional parts and the specified precision `Q`. 
The result is then checked for overflow and the MSB is adjusted accordingly to maintain the correct sign. 
We use internal registers to store the result temporarily before assigning it to the output port `c`.
