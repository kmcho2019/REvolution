To implement the parameterized fixed-point adder, I will use Verilog's parameterization feature to make the design flexible and reusable. The module will take parameters Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits) to define the precision and size of the operands and the result. 

Inside the module, I will use conditional statements to determine whether to perform absolute value addition or subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. I will also handle overflow by observing the MSB of the result and ensuring it fits within the N-bit range.

To maintain precision, I will ensure that the fractional part of the result is correctly represented by the Q least significant bits.

The use of internal registers (res) will help in storing the result temporarily before it is assigned to the output port (c).
