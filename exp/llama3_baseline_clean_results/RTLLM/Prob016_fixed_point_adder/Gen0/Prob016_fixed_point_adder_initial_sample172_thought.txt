The fixed_point_adder module will be designed with a parameterized architecture to accommodate varying numbers of bits (N) and fractional bits (Q). It will first determine whether to perform addition or subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. For addition, it will directly add the absolute values of the operands and set the sign bit accordingly. For subtraction, it will compare the absolute values of the operands, perform the subtraction, and set the sign bit based on the result. The precision of the arithmetic operations will be maintained by ensuring that the fractional parts of the operands are correctly aligned and handled. Overflow handling will be implicitly managed by the Verilog arithmetic operations, which will wrap around if the result exceeds the N-bit range.
