To design the parameterized fixed-point adder module, we first consider the structure of the inputs and output, which includes the total number of bits (N) and the number of fractional bits (Q). We then implement the logic for absolute value addition and subtraction, handling the sign bits and ensuring the result's precision and range are consistent with the input parameters. Finally, we manage overflow by observing the MSB of the result and ensuring it fits within the N-bit range.
