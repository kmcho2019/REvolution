To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder in Verilog, we need to consider the precision of the fixed-point numbers, which is determined by the number of fractional bits (Q). The adder should handle addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands and manage overflow by ensuring the result fits within the specified total number of bits (N). We will use a register to store the result of the addition or subtraction. The design will be parameterized to allow for flexibility in the choice of Q and N.
