To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder, we'll create a Verilog module that takes two fixed-point numbers, `a` and `b`, and produces their sum, `c`, with consistent precision and handling for overflows. The module will be parameterized by `Q` (number of fractional bits) and `N` (total number of bits). We will handle absolute value addition and subtraction based on the signs of `a` and `b`. Precision will be maintained as per the `Q` parameter, ensuring the fractional parts of the inputs and output are correctly represented.
