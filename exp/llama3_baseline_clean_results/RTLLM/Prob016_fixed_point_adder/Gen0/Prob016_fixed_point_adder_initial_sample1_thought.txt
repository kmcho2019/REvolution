To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we need to follow these steps:
1. Determine the MSBs of the input operands `a` and `b` to decide whether to perform addition or subtraction.
2. Compare the absolute values of `a` and `b` to determine the result of the subtraction.
3. Set the MSB of the result to match the sign bit of the operands in the case of addition, or according to the result of the subtraction.
4. Ensure the precision of the result by considering the fractional part determined by parameter `Q`.
5. Handle overflow by observing the MSB of the result to ensure it fits within the N-bit range.
