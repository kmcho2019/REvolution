To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we first need to determine if the two input numbers have the same sign or not. We can do this by comparing their most significant bits (MSBs). If they are the same, we perform an addition of their absolute values. If they are different, we determine which number has a larger absolute value and perform a subtraction. The result's sign is then determined based on the signs of the input numbers and the result of the comparison. To handle precision, we consider the Q parameter, which defines the number of fractional bits, ensuring consistent precision across inputs and output. Finally, we manage overflow by observing the MSB of the result to ensure it fits within the N-bit range defined by the parameter N.
