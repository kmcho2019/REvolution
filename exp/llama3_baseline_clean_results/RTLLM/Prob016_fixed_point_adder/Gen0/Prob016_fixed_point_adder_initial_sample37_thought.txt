The design of the fixed_point_adder module requires a parameterized approach to handle variable bit widths (N) and fractional precision (Q). To implement the addition and subtraction logic while maintaining the sign consistency and handling overflow, the module will use internal registers and conditional statements to evaluate the MSBs of the input operands. The addition and subtraction operations will be performed based on the absolute values of the operands, and the result's MSB will be set accordingly to maintain sign consistency. To ensure precise fixed-point arithmetic, the fractional part of the operands and the result will be consistently represented by the Q parameter.
