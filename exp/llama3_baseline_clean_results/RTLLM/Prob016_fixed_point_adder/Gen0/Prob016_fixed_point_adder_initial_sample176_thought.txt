To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder, we first need to understand the requirements of fixed-point arithmetic. The module should handle both addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. It must also manage the precision and handle overflows properly. We will use Verilog's parameterized module feature to make the design flexible and reusable. The module will have two input ports, `a` and `b`, and one output port, `c`. The internal register `res` will store the result of the operation. We will use the most significant bit (MSB) to determine the sign of the operands and the result. For addition, if the MSBs of `a` and `b` are the same, we add their absolute values. For subtraction, if the MSBs are different, we determine the larger absolute value and perform the subtraction accordingly. We will also handle overflow by observing the MSB of the result.
