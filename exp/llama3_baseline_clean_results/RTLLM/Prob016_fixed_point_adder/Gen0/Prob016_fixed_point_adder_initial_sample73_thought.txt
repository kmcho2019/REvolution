To design a parameterized fixed-point adder in Verilog, we'll first define the module with parameters Q and N, and input/output ports as specified. The module will handle absolute value addition and subtraction based on the most significant bits (MSBs) of the input operands. We will also manage the precision based on the parameter Q, ensuring consistent integer and fractional parts across inputs and output. To handle overflow, we'll monitor the MSB of the result to ensure it fits within the N-bit range.
