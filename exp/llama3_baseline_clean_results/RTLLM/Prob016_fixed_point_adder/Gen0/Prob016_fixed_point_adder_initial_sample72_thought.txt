The fixed_point_adder module will be implemented with a focus on handling both addition and subtraction based on the signs of the input operands. It will utilize parameterized values for Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits) to ensure flexibility and precision in fixed-point arithmetic. The design will involve conditional logic to determine whether to perform addition or subtraction based on the signs of the operands, manage the precision of the result, and handle overflow by ensuring the result fits within the N-bit range.
