To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we first need to consider the inputs and their formats. We will use the most significant bit (MSB) to determine the sign of each number. The addition/subtraction logic will depend on whether the MSBs of `a` and `b` are the same or different, indicating the same or different signs. To handle precision, we ensure that the output maintains the same format (Q fractional bits and N-Q-1 integer bits).
