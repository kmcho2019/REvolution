To implement the parameterized fixed-point adder module, we need to consider the following steps:
1. Determine the sign of the inputs `a` and `b` based on their most significant bits (MSBs).
2. Perform absolute value addition or subtraction based on the signs of `a` and `b`.
3. Manage the precision of the fixed-point arithmetic by ensuring that the fractional part of the result is consistent with the parameter `Q`.
4. Handle overflow by observing the MSB of the result to ensure it fits within the N-bit range.

We will use Verilog's parameterized module feature to make the design flexible and reusable. The module will take parameters `Q` (number of fractional bits) and `N` (total number of bits), and ports `a`, `b`, and `c` for the two input operands and the result, respectively.
