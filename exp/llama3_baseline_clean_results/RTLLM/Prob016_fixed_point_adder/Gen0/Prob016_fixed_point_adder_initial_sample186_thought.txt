To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder, we need to follow a step-by-step approach considering the requirements:

1. Determine the sign of the input operands `a` and `b` by checking their most significant bits (MSBs).
2. Based on the signs, decide whether to perform absolute value addition or subtraction.
3. For addition, simply add the absolute values of `a` and `b`.
4. For subtraction, determine the larger absolute value and subtract the smaller one from it. Then, set the sign of the result accordingly.
5. Ensure that the result is within the N-bit range to handle overflow.
6. Use the parameter `Q` to maintain consistent precision across inputs and output.

This process involves conditional statements, arithmetic operations, and bit manipulation.
