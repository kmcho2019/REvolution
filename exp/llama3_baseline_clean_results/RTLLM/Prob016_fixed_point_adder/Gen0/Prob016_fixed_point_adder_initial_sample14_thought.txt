To implement the fixed_point_adder module, we will follow these steps:
1. Determine the sign bits of inputs a and b.
2. Compare the absolute values of a and b.
3. Based on the comparison result, decide whether to perform absolute value addition or subtraction.
4. After performing the addition or subtraction, manage the overflow by adjusting the result according to the sign bit.

The module will utilize Verilog's parameterization to dynamically adjust the total number of bits (N) and the number of fractional bits (Q) for the fixed-point arithmetic.

To handle the addition or subtraction, we'll use a temporary register to store the result. The result's MSB will be determined based on the signs of a and b.

For precision, we will ensure that the operations respect the fractional part defined by Q.
