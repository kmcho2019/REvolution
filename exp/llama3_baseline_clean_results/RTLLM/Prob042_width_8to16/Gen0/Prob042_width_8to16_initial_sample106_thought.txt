The idle state represents the initial condition where the circuit is waiting for the first valid data. Once the first valid data arrives, it transitions to the waiting_for_second_data state, where it waits for the second valid data. When the second valid data arrives, the circuit concatenates the two data inputs and generates the valid_out and data_out signals. The circuit will also have a flag signal to track whether the first data has been received.

The data_out register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. On reset, the data_out register will be cleared to 0.
