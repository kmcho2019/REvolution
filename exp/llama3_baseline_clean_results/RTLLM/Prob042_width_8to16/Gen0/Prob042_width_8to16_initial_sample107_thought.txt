To implement the data width conversion circuit, we will use two registers, data_lock and data_out, along with a flag signal to track whether the first 8-bit data has been stored. The data_lock register will hold the first arriving 8-bit data, and once the second valid 8-bit data arrives, the two will be concatenated to form the 16-bit output data. The valid_out signal will be asserted in the same clock cycle as the data_out is generated, indicating the validity of the output data.
