To implement the width conversion circuit, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) approach with two main states: IDLE and VALID. The FSM will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal. When the input data is valid, it will be stored in a temporary register. Once two valid data inputs are received, they will be concatenated and outputted as 16-bit data. The valid_out signal will be asserted when the 16-bit output data is valid. The circuit will be reset when the reset signal is active-low.
