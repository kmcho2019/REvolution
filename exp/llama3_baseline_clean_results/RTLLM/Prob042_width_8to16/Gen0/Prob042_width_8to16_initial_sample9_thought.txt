To accomplish this, we will use two registers: 'data_lock' to store the first arriving 8-bit data and 'data_out' to store the final 16-bit output. We also need a flag signal to track whether the first data has arrived. The module will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. Once the second valid data arrives, it will be concatenated with the data in 'data_lock' to form the 16-bit output and stored in 'data_out'. The 'valid_out' signal will be asserted in the same clock cycle as 'data_out'.
