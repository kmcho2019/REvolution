To implement the data width conversion circuit, we will use a combination of flip-flops and combinational logic. We'll have two 8-bit registers to store the two incoming 8-bit data. A flag signal will be used to track whether the first data has arrived. The circuit will be designed to operate synchronously with the clock signal. When the first data is valid, it will be stored in one of the 8-bit registers. Then, when the second data is valid, the two stored 8-bit data will be concatenated to form a 16-bit output, which will be registered and outputted. The valid_out signal will be asserted in the same clock cycle as the data_out.
