To design a 4-bit wide LIFO buffer, we will utilize a stack-based approach. The buffer's functionality is controlled by the RW signal, where a low signal indicates a write operation and a high signal indicates a read operation. We'll use a stack pointer (SP) to track the current position in the stack, incrementing it when popping data and decrementing when pushing data. The EMPTY and FULL flags will be updated based on the stack pointer's value. The buffer will be initialized and cleared when the reset signal is high. This design ensures that the buffer operates as expected, performing push and pop operations according to the provided control signals.
