To implement the LIFO buffer, we'll use a stack memory array (stack_mem) to store the data and a stack pointer (SP) to track the current position in the stack. The buffer operations (push and pop) will be controlled by the RW signal, and the buffer status (EMPTY and FULL) will be indicated by flags. The enable (EN) signal will activate buffer operations, and the reset (Rst) signal will initialize the buffer. We'll use a synchronous design, with all operations occurring on the rising edge of the clock (Clk).
