To implement the LIFO buffer, we'll use a register array (stack_mem) to simulate the stack and a pointer (SP) to track the current position. The design will be sensitive to the clock edge and will perform operations based on the RW and EN signals. On reset, the stack will be cleared, and the SP will be set to indicate an empty buffer. During normal operation, we'll check for full or empty conditions and perform push or pop operations accordingly, updating the flags and output as necessary.
