To design the LIFO buffer in Verilog, I will use a reg array to simulate the stack memory and a reg variable to track the current stack pointer. The module will be synchronous, with operations performed on the rising edge of the clock signal. The reset signal will be used to clear the stack and initialize the stack pointer. I will also use conditional statements to control the read and write operations based on the RW signal and the status of the buffer (EMPTY or FULL).
