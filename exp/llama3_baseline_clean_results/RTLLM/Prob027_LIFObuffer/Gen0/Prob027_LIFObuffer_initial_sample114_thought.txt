To implement the LIFO buffer in Verilog, I will use a combination of sequential logic for the stack pointer, combinational logic for the flag generation, and a register array to represent the stack memory. The design will utilize a synchronous reset to clear the stack and reset the stack pointer. The read and write operations will be controlled by the RW signal, with the enable signal EN governing the activation of these operations. The EMPTY and FULL flags will be generated based on the stack pointer's position relative to the stack's bounds.
