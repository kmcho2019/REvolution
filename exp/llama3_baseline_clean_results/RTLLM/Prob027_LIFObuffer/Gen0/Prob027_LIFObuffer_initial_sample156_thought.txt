To design the LIFObuffer module, we first need to declare the module and its ports. Then, we can define the internal signals and variables required for the implementation, such as the stack memory array (stack_mem), the stack pointer (SP), and flags for EMPTY and FULL. 
We will use a always_ff block to handle the synchronous operations at the rising edge of the clock (Clk) and an always_comb block to handle combinational logic for updating the flags. 
Inside the always_ff block, we will check for the reset condition and handle the buffer operations based on the RW signal and the status of the buffer (EMPTY or FULL).
