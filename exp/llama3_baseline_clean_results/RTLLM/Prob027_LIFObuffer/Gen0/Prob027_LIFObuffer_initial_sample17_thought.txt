To implement the LIFO buffer in Verilog, we will use a register array (stack_mem) to store the data. A stack pointer (SP) will be used to track the current position in the stack. The buffer will be controlled by the RW, EN, Rst, and Clk signals. On the rising edge of the clock, if the enable signal is high, the buffer will perform the desired operation based on the RW signal. If the RW signal is low (write operation), the data from dataIn will be pushed onto the stack, and the stack pointer will be decremented. If the RW signal is high (read operation), the data will be popped from the stack into dataOut, the corresponding stack memory will be cleared, and the stack pointer will be incremented. The EMPTY and FULL flags will be updated based on the stack pointer status.
