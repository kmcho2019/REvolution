To implement the LIFO buffer, we will use a stack memory array (stack_mem) with 4 entries, each 4 bits wide, and a stack pointer (SP) to track the current position in the stack. We will use the RW signal to determine whether to perform a push or pop operation. When the EN signal is high, we will check the Rst signal to decide whether to reset the stack or perform the operation based on the RW signal. We will update the EMPTY and FULL flags based on the stack pointer status.
