The LIFObuffer module will utilize a stack-based data structure to store and retrieve 4-bit data. We will employ a reg array (stack_mem) to represent the stack memory, and a reg variable (SP) to track the current stack position. The buffer will support synchronous push and pop operations based on the RW signal. On each clock cycle, the buffer will check for reset, enable, and RW conditions to perform the corresponding actions. We will also generate flags (EMPTY and FULL) to indicate the buffer's status.
