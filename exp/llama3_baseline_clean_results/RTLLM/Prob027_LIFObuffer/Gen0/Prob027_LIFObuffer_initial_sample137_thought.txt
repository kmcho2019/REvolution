To design the LIFObuffer module, we'll use a combination of synchronous logic and sequential statements in Verilog. The stack memory will be implemented as a 4x4-bit register array, with the stack pointer tracking the current position. The RW signal will control the push and pop operations, while the EN signal will enable the buffer operations. The Rst signal will reset the buffer to its initial state. The EMPTY and FULL flags will be updated based on the stack pointer's value.
