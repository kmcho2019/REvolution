To design the LIFObuffer module, we will first declare the necessary signals and variables for the stack memory, stack pointer, and flags. We will then use an always block to handle the clock edge and the reset signal. Within the always block, we will implement the logic for pushing and popping data based on the RW signal and the status of the buffer (empty or full). We will also update the flags accordingly. The dataOut will be assigned based on the popped data from the stack.
