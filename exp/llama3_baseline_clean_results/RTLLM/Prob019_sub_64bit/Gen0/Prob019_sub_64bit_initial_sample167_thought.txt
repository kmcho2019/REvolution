To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will first perform the binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B. Then, we will examine the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect any overflow conditions. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), which is bit 63. If A is positive (A[63] = 0) and B is negative (B[63] = 1), and the result is negative (result[63] = 1), we have a positive overflow. If A is negative (A[63] = 1) and B is positive (B[63] = 0), and the result is positive (result[63] = 0), we have a negative overflow. The overflow flag will be set accordingly based on these conditions.
