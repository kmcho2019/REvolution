To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will first perform the binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B. Then, we will analyze the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect any overflow conditions. We can determine the sign of a number by checking its most significant bit (MSB). In 2's complement representation, a negative number has its MSB set to 1, while a positive number has its MSB set to 0. We will use these properties to identify potential overflows.
