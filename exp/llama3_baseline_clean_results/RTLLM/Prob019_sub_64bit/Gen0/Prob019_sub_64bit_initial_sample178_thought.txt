To design the 64-bit subtractor module with overflow checking, we need to follow these steps:
1. Define the module with input ports A and B, and output ports result and overflow.
2. Perform binary subtraction of A and B to obtain the result.
3. Check the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect overflow conditions.
4. Set the overflow flag based on the detected overflow conditions.

We can use Verilog's built-in subtraction operator to perform the subtraction operation. 
For overflow detection, we'll examine the most significant bits (MSBs) of A, B, and the result to determine the signs of the numbers.
If the signs of A and the result are different, and the sign of B is different from A, we have an overflow condition.
