To design the 64-bit subtractor module with overflow checking, we will first perform the subtraction operation using Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators. Then, we will examine the sign bits of the inputs (A and B) and the result to detect overflow conditions. 
The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), i.e., bit 63. 
We will use this bit to determine whether each number is positive (MSB = 0) or negative (MSB = 1). 
After performing the subtraction, we will check for overflow by comparing the signs of A, B, and the result. 
If the signs of A and B are different and the sign of the result is the same as B, it indicates an overflow.
