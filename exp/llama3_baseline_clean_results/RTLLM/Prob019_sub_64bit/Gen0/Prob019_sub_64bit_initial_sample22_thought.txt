To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will utilize the following steps:
1. Perform binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B to obtain the result.
2. Examine the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect overflow conditions.
3. Set the overflow flag based on the detected overflow condition.
