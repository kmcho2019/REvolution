To implement the sub_64bit module, we need to consider the following steps: 
1. Perform binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B.
2. Determine the sign of the input operands A and B by examining their most significant bits (MSBs).
3. Determine the sign of the result by examining its MSB.
4. Based on the signs of A, B, and the result, check for overflow conditions and set the overflow flag accordingly.

We will use the built-in Verilog operators for subtraction and comparison, as well as logical operations to determine the sign of the operands and result.
