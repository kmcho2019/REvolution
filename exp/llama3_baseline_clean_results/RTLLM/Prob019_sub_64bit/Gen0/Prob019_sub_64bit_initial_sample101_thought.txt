To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we need to perform binary subtraction between the two input operands A and B. For overflow detection, we need to examine the sign bits of A, B, and the result. We can use the most significant bit (MSB) of each operand and the result to determine the sign. A positive number has a MSB of 0, while a negative number has a MSB of 1. We can then use these sign bits to check for overflow conditions.
