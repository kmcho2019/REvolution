To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we need to design a module that performs binary subtraction and checks for overflow conditions. The subtraction operation can be performed using the built-in subtraction operator in Verilog. For overflow detection, we will examine the sign bits of the inputs (A and B) and the result. We will set the overflow flag to 1 if the signs of A and B are different and the sign of the result is different from the sign of A, indicating an overflow condition. Otherwise, the overflow flag will be set to 0.
