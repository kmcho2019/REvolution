To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will create a Verilog module that performs binary subtraction and then checks for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of the input operands and the result. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), which is bit 63. We will use this information to detect positive and negative overflows. The module will have two input ports, A and B, and two output ports, result and overflow. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for subtraction and bit manipulation to achieve this.
