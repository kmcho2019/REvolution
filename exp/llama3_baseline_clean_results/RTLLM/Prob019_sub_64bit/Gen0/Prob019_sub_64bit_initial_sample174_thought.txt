1. Performing the subtraction operation between the two 64-bit inputs A and B to obtain the result.
2. Examining the most significant bits (MSBs) of A, B, and the result to determine the sign of each.
3. Applying the rules for overflow detection: checking if a positive A minus a negative B results in a negative result (positive overflow), or if a negative A minus a positive B results in a positive result (negative overflow).
4. Setting the overflow flag accordingly based on these conditions.
