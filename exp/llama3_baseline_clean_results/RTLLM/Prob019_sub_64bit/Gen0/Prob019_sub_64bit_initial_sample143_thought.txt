The 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking will be implemented as a Verilog module named sub_64bit. It will have two 64-bit input ports (A and B) and two output ports: a 64-bit output port (result) to store the difference of A and B, and a 1-bit output port (overflow) to indicate whether an overflow occurred. 
The subtraction operation will be performed using the built-in Verilog subtraction operator (-). 
The overflow condition will be detected by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result. 
We will consider the most significant bit (MSB) of each operand and the result to determine the sign. 
If the signs of A and B are different and the sign of the result is different from the sign of A, then an overflow has occurred.
