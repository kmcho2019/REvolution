To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we'll use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators to perform the subtraction operation. We'll then analyze the sign bits of the input operands and the result to detect overflow conditions. The sign bit is the most significant bit (MSB) of a signed binary number. In this case, since we're working with 64-bit numbers, the MSB is bit 63. We'll use conditional statements to check for overflow and set the overflow flag accordingly.
