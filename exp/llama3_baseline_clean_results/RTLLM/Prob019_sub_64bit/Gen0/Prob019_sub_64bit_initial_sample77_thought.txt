To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will first perform the binary subtraction of A and B to obtain the result. Then, we will examine the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect any overflow conditions. 
We will set the overflow flag to 1 if the result's sign bit is different from the sign bit of A (the first operand) when A and B have different signs.
