To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will first perform the binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B. Then, we will examine the sign bits of A, B, and the result to detect overflow conditions. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), which is bit 63. We can use this bit to determine the sign of the numbers. If the signs of A and B are the same and the sign of the result is different, then an overflow has occurred. We will use this logic to set the overflow flag.
