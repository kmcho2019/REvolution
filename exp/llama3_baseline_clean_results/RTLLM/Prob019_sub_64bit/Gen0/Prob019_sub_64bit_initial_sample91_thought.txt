To design a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we need to perform binary subtraction and then examine the sign bits of the operands and the result to detect overflow conditions. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), i.e., bit 63. We will use this to determine the sign of A, B, and the result. Overflow detection involves checking for two conditions: (1) when A is positive and B is negative, and the result is negative (positive overflow), and (2) when A is negative and B is positive, and the result is positive (negative overflow). We will use these conditions to set the overflow flag.
