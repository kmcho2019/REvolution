To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will perform binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B to obtain the result. 
We will then check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result. 
If the sign of the result is different from the expected sign based on the signs of A and B, it indicates an overflow condition, and the overflow flag will be set accordingly.
The module will consist of two main components: a subtractor to perform the binary subtraction and a logic circuit to detect overflow conditions.
The subtractor will be implemented using the built-in Verilog subtraction operator, and the overflow detection logic will be implemented using logical operators to examine the sign bits of the operands and the result.
