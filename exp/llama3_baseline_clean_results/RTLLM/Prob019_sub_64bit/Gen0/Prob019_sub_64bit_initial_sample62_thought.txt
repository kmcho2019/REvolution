To design a 64-bit subtractor module with overflow checking, we need to follow these steps:
1. Perform binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B to obtain the result.
2. Detect overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result.
   - Positive overflow occurs when A is positive, B is negative, and the result is negative.
   - Negative overflow occurs when A is negative, B is positive, and the result is positive.
3. Set the overflow flag accordingly based on the detected overflow condition.

The Verilog implementation will utilize arithmetic operators for subtraction and logical operators for overflow detection.
