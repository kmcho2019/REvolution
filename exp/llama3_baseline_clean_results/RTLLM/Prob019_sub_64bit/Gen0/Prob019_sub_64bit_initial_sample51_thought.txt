To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will first perform the subtraction operation using the built-in subtraction operator in Verilog. Then, we will check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of the input operands and the result. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), which is bit 63 in this case. We can use the MSB to determine the sign of each number.
