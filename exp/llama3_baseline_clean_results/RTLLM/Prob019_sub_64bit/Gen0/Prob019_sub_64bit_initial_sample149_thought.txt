To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we'll follow these steps:
1. Define the module with input and output ports.
2. Perform binary subtraction of A and B to obtain the result.
3. Check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result.
4. Set the overflow flag based on the detected overflow condition.

We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators to perform the subtraction and logical operators to check for overflow conditions.
