To implement the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the built-in subtraction operation in Verilog and manually check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of the input operands and the result. 

The key steps are:
1. Perform the binary subtraction of the two 64-bit operands A and B.
2. Determine the sign bits of A, B, and the result by checking the most significant bits (MSBs) of each.
3. Check for overflow conditions based on the signs of A, B, and the result. 
   - If A is positive (MSB = 0) and B is negative (MSB = 1), and the result is negative (MSB = 1), then a positive overflow has occurred.
   - If A is negative (MSB = 1) and B is positive (MSB = 0), and the result is positive (MSB = 0), then a negative overflow has occurred.
4. Set the overflow flag accordingly based on the detected overflow conditions.
