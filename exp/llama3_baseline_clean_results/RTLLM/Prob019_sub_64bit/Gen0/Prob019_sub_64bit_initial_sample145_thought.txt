To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we first perform the binary subtraction of the two input operands, A and B. Then, we check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result. We consider a positive overflow when A is positive, B is negative, and the result is negative. Similarly, a negative overflow occurs when A is negative, B is positive, and the result is positive. The overflow flag is set based on these conditions. We utilize Verilog's built-in arithmetic and logical operators to achieve this functionality.
