To implement the 64-bit subtractor module with overflow checking, we will first perform the binary subtraction of the two input operands A and B. Then, we will check for overflow conditions by examining the sign bits of A, B, and the result. We will set the overflow flag to 1 if an overflow condition is detected; otherwise, it will be set to 0. The overflow condition is met when the sign of the result is different from the expected sign based on the signs of A and B. We can use the most significant bit (MSB) of each number to determine its sign.
