The overflow condition will be checked by examining the sign bits of the operands and the result. The sign bit of a 64-bit signed integer is the most significant bit (MSB), i.e., bit 63. If the sign bits of the operands and the result are different, it indicates an overflow. Specifically, we will check for two types of overflow conditions: (1) positive overflow, where a positive number is subtracted by a negative number and the result is negative, and (2) negative overflow, where a negative number is subtracted by a positive number and the result is positive. We will use these conditions to set the overflow flag accordingly.
