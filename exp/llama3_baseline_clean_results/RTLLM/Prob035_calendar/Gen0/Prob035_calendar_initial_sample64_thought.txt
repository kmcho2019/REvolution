To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design a digital circuit that updates the seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The module will have three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours separately. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use conditional statements to check the values of seconds, minutes, and hours, and update them accordingly.
