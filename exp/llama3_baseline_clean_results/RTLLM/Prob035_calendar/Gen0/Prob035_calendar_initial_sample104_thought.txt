To create a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will utilize three always blocks that update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will handle the wrapping around of the respective time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) when it reaches its maximum value. The blocks will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, allowing for a reset of the calendar when needed. The output ports will display the current hours, minutes, and seconds.
