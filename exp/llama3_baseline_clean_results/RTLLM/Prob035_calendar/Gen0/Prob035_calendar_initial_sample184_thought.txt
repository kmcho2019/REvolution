To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to create a module named 'calendar' with the specified input and output ports. The module will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The seconds, minutes, and hours values will be updated according to their respective conditions, and the values will wrap around when they reach their maximum values.
