To design a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to implement three always blocks that update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block handles the seconds value, wrapping it around to 0 when it reaches 59. The second always block handles the minutes value, wrapping it around to 0 when both minutes and seconds are 59. The third always block handles the hours value, wrapping it around to 0 when hours, minutes, and seconds are all at their maximum values. 
We will use non-blocking assignments to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to avoid race conditions and ensure that the values are updated simultaneously.
