To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we'll use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, allowing for synchronous updates and reset functionality. 
We will use non-blocking assignments (<=) to update the values to avoid race conditions and ensure the correct ordering of operations within each always block.
