To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds, the second block will handle the minutes, and the third block will handle the hours. Each block will check for the reset condition and the wrap-around condition for its respective time unit. If the reset signal is active, all time units will be reset to 0. The seconds will wrap around from 59 to 0 and trigger the minute to increment when it reaches 59. Similarly, the minutes will wrap around from 59 to 0 and trigger the hour to increment when it reaches 59, and the hours will wrap around from 23 to 0 when all three reach their maximum values.
