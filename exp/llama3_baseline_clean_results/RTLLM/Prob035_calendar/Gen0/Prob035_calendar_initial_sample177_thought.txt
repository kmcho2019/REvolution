To implement the perpetual calendar module, I will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The reset signal will be used to reset the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to 0. The values will wrap around when they reach their maximum values (59 for seconds and minutes, 23 for hours).
