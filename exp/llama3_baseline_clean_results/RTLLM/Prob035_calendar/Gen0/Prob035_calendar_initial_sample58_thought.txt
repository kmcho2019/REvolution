To create a perpetual calendar module, we can use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will implement conditional statements to handle the wrapping around of seconds, minutes, and hours when they reach their maximum values. 
For seconds, we simply check if it has reached 59 and wrap it around to 0, incrementing minutes if necessary. 
For minutes, we check if both minutes and seconds are at 59, and if so, wrap minutes around and increment hours if necessary. 
For hours, we check if hours, minutes, and seconds are all at their maximum and wrap hours around if so.
