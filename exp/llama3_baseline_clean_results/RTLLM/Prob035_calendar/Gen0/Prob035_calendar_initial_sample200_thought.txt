To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will handle the respective time unit, ensuring continuous looping from 0 to 59 for seconds and minutes, and from 0 to 23 for hours. We will also implement the necessary conditional statements to handle the wrap-around and increment logic for each time unit.
