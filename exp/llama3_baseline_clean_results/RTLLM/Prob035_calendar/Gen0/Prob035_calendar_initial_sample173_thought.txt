To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will utilize three always blocks that are triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (CLK) or the positive edge of the reset signal (RST). The first block will handle the seconds (Secs), the second block will handle the minutes (Mins), and the third block will handle the hours (Hours). 
Each block will check for the reset condition and update the respective time component based on its current value and the values of other time components. 
We will use non-blocking assignments (<=) to update the registers to ensure that the values are updated in a predictable and synchronized manner.
