The perpetual calendar module will be designed using three always blocks in Verilog, each handling the seconds, minutes, and hours values. These blocks will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The reset signal will be used to initialize the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to 0. The seconds value will be incremented every clock cycle, and when it reaches 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes value. Similarly, when the minutes value reaches 59 and the seconds value is 59, the hours value will be incremented, and when the hours value reaches 23 and the minutes and seconds values are 59, it will wrap around to 0.
