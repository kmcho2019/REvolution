The perpetual calendar module will utilize three always blocks to manage the seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The seconds block will handle the wrapping around of seconds from 59 to 0 and the incrementing of minutes when seconds reach 59. The minutes block will handle the wrapping around of minutes from 59 to 0 and the incrementing of hours when minutes and seconds both reach 59. The hours block will handle the wrapping around of hours from 23 to 0 when hours, minutes, and seconds all reach their maximum values.
