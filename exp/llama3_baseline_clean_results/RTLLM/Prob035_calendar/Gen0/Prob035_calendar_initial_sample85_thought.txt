To implement the perpetual calendar module, we can use three always blocks in Verilog to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second will handle the minutes value, and the third will handle the hours value. Each block will check for the reset signal and update the corresponding value accordingly. We will use non-blocking assignments to ensure that the values are updated correctly at the positive edge of the clock signal or reset signal.
