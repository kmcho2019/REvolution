To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The seconds value will be updated first, followed by the minutes and then the hours. The always blocks will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use conditional statements to check for the reset signal, seconds value, minutes value, and hours value to determine the next state of the calendar.
