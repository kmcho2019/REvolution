To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog. The first block will handle the seconds counter, incrementing it every clock cycle and resetting it to 0 when it reaches 59. The second block will handle the minutes counter, incrementing it when the seconds counter resets and resetting it to 0 when it reaches 59 and the seconds counter resets. The third block will handle the hours counter, incrementing it when the minutes and seconds counters both reset and resetting it to 0 when it reaches 23 and the minutes and seconds counters both reset. We will use non-blocking assignments to update the counters.
