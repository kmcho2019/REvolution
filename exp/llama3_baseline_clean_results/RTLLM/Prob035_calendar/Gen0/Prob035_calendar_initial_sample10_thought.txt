To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to create a module that takes a clock signal (CLK) and a reset signal (RST) as inputs and outputs the current hours, minutes, and seconds. The module will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will check for the reset condition and the wrap-around conditions for seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds will increment every clock cycle, minutes will increment when seconds reach 59, and hours will increment when minutes and seconds reach 59.
