To design a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will create a module named 'calendar' with input ports 'CLK' and 'RST', and output ports 'Hours', 'Mins', and 'Secs'. The module will utilize three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will implement the logic for resetting, wrapping around, and incrementing the values of seconds, minutes, and hours according to the given conditions.
