To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will handle the wrap-around and increment logic for its respective time unit. We will use if-else statements to check the conditions for wrap-around and increment. The module will have a clock input (CLK) and an active high reset signal (RST), and it will output the current hours, minutes, and seconds as 6-bit values.
