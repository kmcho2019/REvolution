To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will handle the seconds, the second always block will handle the minutes, and the third always block will handle the hours. Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The reset signal will be used to reset the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to 0. We will use if-else statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours and update them accordingly.
