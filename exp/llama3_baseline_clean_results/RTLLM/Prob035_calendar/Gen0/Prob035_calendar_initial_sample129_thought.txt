The perpetual calendar module will be designed using three always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will update the seconds value based on the clock signal and reset signal. The second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. The always blocks will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The module will have three output ports: Hours, Mins, and Secs, each representing the current hours, minutes, and seconds respectively.
