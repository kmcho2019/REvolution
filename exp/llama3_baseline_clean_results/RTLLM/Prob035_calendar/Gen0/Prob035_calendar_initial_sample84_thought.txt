To implement the perpetual calendar module, we need to design a digital circuit that can keep track of time in hours, minutes, and seconds. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. We will use conditional statements to check for wrap-around conditions and increment the values accordingly.
