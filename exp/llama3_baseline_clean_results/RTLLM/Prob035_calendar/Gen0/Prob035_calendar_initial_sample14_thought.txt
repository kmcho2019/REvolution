To design the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. We will trigger these blocks on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. Each block will handle the update logic for its respective time unit (seconds, minutes, hours), considering wrap-around conditions and reset functionality. The goal is to ensure that the seconds and minutes loop continuously from 0 to 59, and the hours loop from 0 to 23.
