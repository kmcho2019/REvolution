The perpetual calendar module will be designed using three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The seconds, minutes, and hours values will be updated based on their current values and the reset signal. When the seconds value reaches 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes value. Similarly, when the minutes value reaches 59 and the seconds value is 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the hours value. When the hours value reaches 23 and the minutes and seconds values are both 59, it will wrap around to 0.
