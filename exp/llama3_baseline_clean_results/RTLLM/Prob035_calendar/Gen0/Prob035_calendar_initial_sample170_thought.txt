To implement the perpetual calendar module, we can use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. We will use if-else statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours and update them accordingly. We will also use the posedge keyword to trigger the always blocks on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal.
