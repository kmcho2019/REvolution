To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will create a Verilog module named "calendar" that takes a clock signal (CLK) and a reset signal (RST) as inputs and outputs the current hours, minutes, and seconds. 
The module will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. 
Each always block will handle the respective time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) and will check for the reset signal and the wrap-around conditions to update the values accordingly.
We will use non-blocking assignments (<=) to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to avoid race conditions.
