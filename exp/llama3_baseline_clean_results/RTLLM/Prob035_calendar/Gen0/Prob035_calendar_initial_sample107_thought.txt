The perpetual calendar module will have three always blocks to handle seconds, minutes, and hours. Each always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. Inside these blocks, we will check for the reset condition and handle the wrap-around and increment conditions for seconds, minutes, and hours accordingly. We will use non-blocking assignments to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to ensure that the changes are visible at the next clock cycle.
