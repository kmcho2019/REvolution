To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we can use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The values of seconds, minutes, and hours will be updated based on the specified conditions.
