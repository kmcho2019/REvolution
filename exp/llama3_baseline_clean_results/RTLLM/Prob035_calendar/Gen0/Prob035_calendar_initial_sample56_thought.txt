To design the perpetual calendar module, we need to consider the behavior of seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds should loop from 0 to 59 and when it reaches 59, it should reset to 0 and increment the minutes by 1. The minutes should also loop from 0 to 59 and when it reaches 59, it should reset to 0 and increment the hours by 1. The hours should loop from 0 to 23 and when it reaches 23, it should reset to 0. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The always blocks will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal.
