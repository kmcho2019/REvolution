The perpetual calendar module can be designed using three always blocks that update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The seconds always block will handle the seconds counter, the minutes always block will handle the minutes counter, and the hours always block will handle the hours counter. The reset signal will be used to reset all counters to 0. 
Each always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. 
We will use if-else statements to implement the logic for updating the seconds, minutes, and hours values.
