The perpetual calendar module will be designed using three always blocks in Verilog. The first block will handle the seconds, the second block will handle the minutes, and the third block will handle the hours. Each block will check for the reset signal and update the respective time unit accordingly. The seconds will loop from 0 to 59, the minutes will also loop from 0 to 59, and the hours will loop from 0 to 23. When the seconds reach 59, the minutes will increment, and when both the minutes and seconds are 59, the hours will increment.
