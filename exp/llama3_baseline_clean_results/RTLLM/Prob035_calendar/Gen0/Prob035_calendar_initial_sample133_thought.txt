To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design a digital circuit that updates the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. We can use three always blocks to achieve this. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use if-else statements to check the conditions and update the values accordingly.
