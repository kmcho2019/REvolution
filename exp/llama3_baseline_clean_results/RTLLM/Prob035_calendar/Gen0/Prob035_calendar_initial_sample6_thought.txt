To implement the perpetual calendar module, we need to design three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use if-else statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours and update them accordingly. When the reset signal is active, all values will be reset to 0. The seconds value will increment by 1 every clock cycle, and when it reaches 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes value. Similarly, when the minutes value reaches 59 and the seconds value is 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the hours value. The hours value will also wrap around from 23 to 0.
