To implement the perpetual calendar module, we can utilize three always blocks in Verilog. These blocks will handle the seconds, minutes, and hours, respectively. Each block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. This allows the values to be updated based on the clock and reset signals. The seconds will loop from 0 to 59, and when reaching 59, the minutes will increment. The minutes will also loop from 0 to 59, and when both minutes and seconds are 59, the hours will increment, looping from 0 to 23.
