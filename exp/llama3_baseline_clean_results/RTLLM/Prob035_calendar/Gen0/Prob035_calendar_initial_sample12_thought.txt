The calendar module will be implemented with three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours values. 
Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. 
The seconds value will wrap around to 0 when it reaches 59, and increment the minutes value. 
The minutes value will wrap around to 0 when both the minutes and seconds values are 59, and increment the hours value. 
The hours value will wrap around to 0 when the hours, minutes, and seconds values are all at their maximum (23, 59, and 59 respectively), and reset the hours value to 0 when the reset signal is active.
