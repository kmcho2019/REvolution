To implement the perpetual calendar module, we can utilize three always blocks that are sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. Each block will handle the update of the seconds, minutes, and hours values based on the given conditions. We will use conditional statements to check for the reset signal, wrap-around conditions, and increment conditions for each of the time units. The output ports will be updated accordingly to reflect the current hours, minutes, and seconds.
