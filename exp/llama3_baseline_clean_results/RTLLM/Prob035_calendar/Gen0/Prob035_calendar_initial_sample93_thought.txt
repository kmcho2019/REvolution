To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use if-else statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours, and update them accordingly. We will also use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to assign values to the output ports.
