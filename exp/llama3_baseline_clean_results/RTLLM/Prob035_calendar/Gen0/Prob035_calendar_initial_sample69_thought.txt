To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to create a module that can keep track of seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds, minutes, and hours should loop continuously from 0 to 59, 0 to 59, and 0 to 23 respectively. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds, the second always block will handle the minutes, and the third always block will handle the hours. We will use if-else statements to check for the conditions when the seconds, minutes, or hours should wrap around or increment. We will also use the posedge keyword to trigger the always blocks on the positive edge of the clock signal or the reset signal.
