To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to consider the timing requirements and the looping behavior of seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds and minutes should loop from 0 to 59, while the hours should loop from 0 to 23. When the seconds value reaches 59, it should wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes value. Similarly, when the minutes value reaches 59 and the seconds value is 59, it should wrap around to 0 and increment the hours value. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal.
