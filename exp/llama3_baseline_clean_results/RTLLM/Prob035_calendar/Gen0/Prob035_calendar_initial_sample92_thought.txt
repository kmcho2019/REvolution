To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds, the second block will handle the minutes, and the third block will handle the hours. We will use if-else statements to check for the reset signal and the maximum values of seconds, minutes, and hours to update their values accordingly. We will also use the posedge of the clock signal to trigger the updates.
