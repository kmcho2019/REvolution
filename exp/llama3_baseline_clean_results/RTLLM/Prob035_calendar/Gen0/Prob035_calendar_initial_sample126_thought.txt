The perpetual calendar module is designed to keep track of time in hours, minutes, and seconds. The module has three main components: seconds, minutes, and hours. Each component has a specific behavior when its value reaches its maximum (59 for seconds and minutes, 23 for hours). The module uses three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The seconds value is incremented every clock cycle, and when it reaches 59, it wraps around to 0 and increments the minutes value. The minutes value behaves similarly, and when it reaches 59, it wraps around to 0 and increments the hours value. The hours value also wraps around to 0 when it reaches 23.
