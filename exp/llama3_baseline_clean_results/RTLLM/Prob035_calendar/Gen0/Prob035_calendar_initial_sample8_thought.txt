To design a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to consider the requirements for seconds, minutes, and hours. 
The seconds and minutes will loop continuously from 0 to 59, and when both reach 59, the hours will increment. 
The hours will cycle from 0 to 23. 
We can achieve this using three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours separately, 
each triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal.
