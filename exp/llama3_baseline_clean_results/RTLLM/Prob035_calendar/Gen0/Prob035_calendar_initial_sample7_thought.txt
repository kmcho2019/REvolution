To implement the perpetual calendar module, we'll use three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the reset signal. We'll use conditional statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours, and update them accordingly. The seconds will loop from 0 to 59, minutes will loop from 0 to 59, and hours will loop from 0 to 23. When the seconds reach 59, the minutes will increment, and when both minutes and seconds are 59, the hours will increment. If the reset signal is active, all values will be reset to 0.
