To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks to update the seconds, minutes, and hours values based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will handle the wrapping around of the values when they reach their maximum limits. We will also use the positive edge of the clock signal and the reset signal to trigger these blocks. This approach ensures that the module accurately simulates a perpetual calendar, updating the time values in real-time based on the clock signal and resetting them when the reset signal is active.
