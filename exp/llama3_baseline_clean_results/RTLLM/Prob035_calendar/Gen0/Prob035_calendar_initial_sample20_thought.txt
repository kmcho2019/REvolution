To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. We will use if-else statements to check the conditions for incrementing or wrapping around the seconds, minutes, and hours values. The seconds and minutes will loop continuously from 0 to 59, and the hours will loop from 0 to 23.
