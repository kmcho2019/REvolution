To implement the perpetual calendar module, we need to design three always blocks that handle the seconds, minutes, and hours counters. Each block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The seconds counter will increment by 1 on each clock cycle unless it reaches 59, at which point it will wrap around to 0 and trigger an increment in the minutes counter. The minutes counter will follow a similar pattern, wrapping around to 0 when it reaches 59 and triggering an increment in the hours counter. The hours counter will also wrap around to 0 when it reaches 23 and both minutes and seconds counters are at 59.
