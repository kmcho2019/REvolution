To design a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will implement three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. We will use the positive edge of the clock signal and the positive edge of the reset signal to trigger these blocks. Each block will handle the respective time unit (seconds, minutes, hours) and perform the necessary operations such as incrementing, wrapping around, or resetting based on the conditions provided. 
We will use non-blocking assignments to update the time unit values.
