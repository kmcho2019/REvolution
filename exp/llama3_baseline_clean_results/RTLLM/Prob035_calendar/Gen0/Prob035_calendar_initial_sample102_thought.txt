To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design three always blocks that update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will handle the logic for wrapping around and incrementing the respective time units. We will use the posedge of the clock signal and the reset signal to trigger these blocks. The seconds block will increment and wrap around every 60 seconds, the minutes block will increment and wrap around every 60 minutes, and the hours block will increment and wrap around every 24 hours.
