To design the perpetual calendar module, we can utilize three always blocks to handle the seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The seconds block will handle the wrapping around of seconds from 59 to 0. The minutes block will handle the wrapping around of minutes from 59 to 0 when seconds reach 59. The hours block will handle the wrapping around of hours from 23 to 0 when both minutes and seconds reach 59.
