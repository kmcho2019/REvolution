To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will create a Verilog module named 'calendar' with input ports 'CLK' and 'RST', and output ports 'Hours', 'Mins', and 'Secs'. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. Inside these blocks, we will check for the reset condition and update the seconds, minutes, and hours accordingly.
