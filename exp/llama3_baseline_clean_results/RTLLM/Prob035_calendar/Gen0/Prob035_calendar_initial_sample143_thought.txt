To create a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we can utilize three always blocks to update the seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each block will check for the reset condition and update the respective time unit accordingly. The seconds will loop from 0 to 59, the minutes will loop from 0 to 59, and the hours will loop from 0 to 23. When the seconds reach 59, the minutes will increment, and when both minutes and seconds reach 59, the hours will increment. The use of posedge for both the clock and reset signals ensures synchronization and correct resetting of the time units.
