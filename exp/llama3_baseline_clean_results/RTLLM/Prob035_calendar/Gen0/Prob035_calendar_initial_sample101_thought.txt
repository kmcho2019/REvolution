To create the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we can utilize three always blocks that trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, ensuring it increments or wraps around as necessary. The second always block will manage the minutes value, considering both the reset condition and the wrap-around logic based on the seconds value. The third always block will control the hours value, taking into account the reset, and the wrap-around conditions based on the minutes and seconds values. This approach ensures a modular and straightforward implementation of the perpetual calendar's logic.
