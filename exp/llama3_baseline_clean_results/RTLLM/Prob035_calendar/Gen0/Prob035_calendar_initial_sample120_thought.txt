To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design a module that updates the seconds, minutes, and hours based on a clock signal and a reset signal. The seconds, minutes, and hours will loop continuously from 0 to 59 for seconds and minutes, and 0 to 23 for hours. We will use three always blocks to handle the updates of seconds, minutes, and hours. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, allowing us to reset the values when needed or update them based on their current state.
