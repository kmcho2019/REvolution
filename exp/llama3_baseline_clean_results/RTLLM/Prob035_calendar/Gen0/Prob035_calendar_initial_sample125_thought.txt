To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will handle the seconds, the second always block will handle the minutes, and the third always block will handle the hours. Each block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. The values of seconds, minutes, and hours will be updated based on the conditions specified in the problem. We will use non-blocking assignments to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours to avoid race conditions.
