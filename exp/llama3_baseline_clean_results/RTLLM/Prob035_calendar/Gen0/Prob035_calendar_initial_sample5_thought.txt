To implement the perpetual calendar module, we will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. 
Each always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. 
We will use if-else statements to check the current values of seconds, minutes, and hours, and update them accordingly. 
When the reset signal is active, all values will be set to 0. 
When seconds reach 59, minutes will be incremented by 1. 
When minutes and seconds are both 59, hours will be incremented by 1. 
When hours, minutes, and seconds are all at their maximum (23, 59, and 59 respectively), hours will wrap around to 0.
