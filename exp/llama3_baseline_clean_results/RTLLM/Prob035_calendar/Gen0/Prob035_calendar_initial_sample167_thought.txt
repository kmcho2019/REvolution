To implement the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. The first always block will handle the seconds value, the second always block will handle the minutes value, and the third always block will handle the hours value. We will use non-blocking assignments (<=) to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours. We will also use if-else statements to check the conditions for wrapping around or incrementing the values.
