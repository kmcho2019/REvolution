To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we can use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2 to account for both clock edges. We will also use the active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
