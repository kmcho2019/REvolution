To implement the frequency divider module, we will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. Each counter will count up to the odd divisor value minus one, and two separate clock dividers will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two dividers to account for both clock edges. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
