To design the freq_divbyodd module, we need to create two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock signal. We will also create two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, which will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. 
We will use an always block to implement the counters and clock dividers. The counters will be reset when the active low reset signal rst_n is low. 
We will use a parameter NUM_DIV to make the module flexible for different odd divisor values.
