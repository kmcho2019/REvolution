To implement the frequency divider module that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, I will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers to handle the positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The module will also be reset by the active low reset signal rst_n.

Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the implementation:
1. Define the module with the required input and output ports.
2. Declare two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
3. Initialize two clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to handle the positive and negative edges of the clock.
4. Use the counters to toggle the clock dividers when the counters reach half of the divisor value.
5. Derive the final divided clock output by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers.
6. Add a reset mechanism to initialize the counters and clock dividers when the active low reset signal rst_n is asserted.
