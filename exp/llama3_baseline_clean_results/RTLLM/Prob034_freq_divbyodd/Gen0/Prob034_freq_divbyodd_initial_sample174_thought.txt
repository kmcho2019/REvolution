To implement the frequency divider module, we need to design a module that takes an input clock signal and divides its frequency by an odd number specified by the parameter NUM_DIV. The module should generate a divided clock output that accounts for both the rising and falling edges of the input clock. We will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock outputs for positive and negative edges, respectively. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The module will also have an active low reset signal that initializes the counters and clock divider outputs.
