To design the frequency divider module, we'll start by defining the module with the given name, input and output ports, and parameter. The parameter NUM_DIV will be used to control the division ratio, which should be an odd number. We'll use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, each counting up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two dividers. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
