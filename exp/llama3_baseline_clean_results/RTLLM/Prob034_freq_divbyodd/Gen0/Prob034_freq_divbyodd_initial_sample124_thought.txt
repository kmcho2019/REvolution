To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we need to use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. These counters count up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. We will also use the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. The parameter NUM_DIV will be used to determine the divisor value.
