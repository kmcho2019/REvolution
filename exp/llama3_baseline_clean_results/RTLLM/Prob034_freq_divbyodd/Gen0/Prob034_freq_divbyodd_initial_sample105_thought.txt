To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The module will also have an active low reset signal that initializes the counters and the clock divider outputs.
