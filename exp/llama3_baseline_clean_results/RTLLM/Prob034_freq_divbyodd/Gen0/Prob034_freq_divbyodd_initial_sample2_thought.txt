To design the frequency divider module freq_divbyodd, we first define the module with the specified input and output ports. We use the parameter NUM_DIV to set the divisor value, which defaults to 5 if not specified. Two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, are used to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters are reset by the active low reset signal rst_n. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, are generated based on the counters and are toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2 to account for both clock edges.
