To implement the frequency divider, we will first define a module named freq_divbyodd with input ports for the clock signal, reset signal, and output port for the divided clock. We'll use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. These dividers will be toggled based on a counter value that reaches half of the divisor NUM_DIV. The final divided clock will be obtained by logically OR-ing the outputs of the two dividers. The module will also include an active low reset mechanism to initialize the counters and dividers.
