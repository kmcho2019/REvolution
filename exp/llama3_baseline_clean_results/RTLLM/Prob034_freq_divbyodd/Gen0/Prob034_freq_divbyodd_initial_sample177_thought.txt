To create a frequency divider module in Verilog that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will increment until they reach half of the specified odd divisor value (NUM_DIV), at which point two separate clock dividers will be toggled. The final divided clock output will be the logical OR of these two dividers, ensuring that both the positive and negative edges of the clock are accounted for. The module will also include an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
