To design the frequency divider module (freq_divbyodd), we'll first define the parameters and input/output ports as per the requirements. The module will utilize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. We will then derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. Finally, we will implement the active low reset logic to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
