To implement the freq_divbyodd module, we need to design a frequency divider that can divide the input clock frequency by an odd number specified by the NUM_DIV parameter. The module will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. When these counters reach half of NUM_DIV, two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be toggled for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. We also need to consider the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
