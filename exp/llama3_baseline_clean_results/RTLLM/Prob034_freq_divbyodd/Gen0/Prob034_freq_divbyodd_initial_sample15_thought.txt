To implement the freq_divbyodd module, we first define the module with the given input and output ports. We use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock signal. We then use these counters to generate two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. We utilize the parameter NUM_DIV to define the divisor value, which defaults to 5.
