The module freq_divbyodd will be designed as a digital circuit that takes an input clock signal and divides its frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. It uses two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock output for positive and negative edges. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is obtained by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The module will also be reset by the active low reset signal rst_n, which initializes the counters and the clock divider outputs.
