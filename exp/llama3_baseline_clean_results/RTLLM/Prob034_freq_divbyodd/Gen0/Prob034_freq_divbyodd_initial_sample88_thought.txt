To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can utilize two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, where NUM_DIV is the odd divisor value provided as a parameter. Two separate clock dividers, one for positive and one for negative edges of the clock, will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers to account for both clock edges. The active low reset signal will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
