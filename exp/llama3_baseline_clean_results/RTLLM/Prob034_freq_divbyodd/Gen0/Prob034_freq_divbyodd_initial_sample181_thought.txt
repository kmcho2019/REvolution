To implement the frequency divider module, we will define a Verilog module named freq_divbyodd that takes an input clock clk, an active low reset signal rst_n, and generates a divided clock output clk_div. We will utilize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The parameter NUM_DIV will be used to specify the odd divisor value, defaulting to 5.
