To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we will implement a module named freq_divbyodd with two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock, clk. The counters will count up to the divisor value (NUM_DIV) - 1. We will use two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, which will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. An active low reset signal, rst_n, will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
