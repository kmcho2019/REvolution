To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we'll use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers (clk_div1 and clk_div2) are used for the positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers are toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value (NUM_DIV). The final divided clock output (clk_div) is derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. We will utilize the active low reset signal (rst_n) to initialize the counters and clock divider outputs.
