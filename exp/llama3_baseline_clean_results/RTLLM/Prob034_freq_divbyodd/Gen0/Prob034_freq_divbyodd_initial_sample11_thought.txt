To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we will design a module that utilizes two counters and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges of the clock. The counters will track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and the clock dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The module will be parameterized to allow for different divisor values, defaulting to 5. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
