To design a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we need to create a module with a parameterized divisor value. The module will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and generate two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The module will also have an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
