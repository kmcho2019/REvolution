To implement the freq_divbyodd module, we need to follow these steps:
- Define the module with the given input and output ports, and a parameter NUM_DIV for the divisor.
- Initialize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
- Use the counters to generate two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, for positive and negative edges of the clock.
- Derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
- Implement the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
