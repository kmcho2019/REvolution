To implement the frequency divider module (freq_divbyodd) that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV, I will use two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers (clk_div1 and clk_div2) will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These dividers are toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output (clk_div) will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
