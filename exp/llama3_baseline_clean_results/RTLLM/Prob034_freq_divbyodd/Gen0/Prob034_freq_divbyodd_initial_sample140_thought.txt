To design the frequency divider module 'freq_divbyodd' that divides the input clock by an odd number, we first define the module with input ports 'clk' and 'rst_n', and an output port 'clk_div'. We use two counters, 'cnt1' and 'cnt2', to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. We also use two separate clock dividers, 'clk_div1' and 'clk_div2', for the positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers are toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value 'NUM_DIV'. The final divided clock output 'clk_div' is derived by logically OR-ing 'clk_div1' and 'clk_div2'. We initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs when the active low reset signal 'rst_n' is asserted.
