To implement the frequency divider module freq_divbyodd, we first define the module with its input and output ports, and the parameter NUM_DIV that determines the division factor. We then declare two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, are used for the positive and negative edges, respectively, and are toggled when their respective counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is obtained by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and clock dividers. We utilize a parameterized approach to allow the user to specify the division factor as needed.
