To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and generate two separate clock dividers. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output can be obtained by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. We will use a parameterized module to make the design flexible and reusable for different divisor values. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
