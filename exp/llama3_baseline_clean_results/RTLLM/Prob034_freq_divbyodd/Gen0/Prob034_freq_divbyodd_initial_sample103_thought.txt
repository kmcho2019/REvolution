To implement the frequency divider module, we need to design a circuit that can divide the input clock frequency by an odd number. The module will utilize two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and the clock dividers will toggle when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
