The freq_divbyodd module will be designed with two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and the clock dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2 to account for both clock edges. The module will also include an active low reset signal, rst_n, to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
