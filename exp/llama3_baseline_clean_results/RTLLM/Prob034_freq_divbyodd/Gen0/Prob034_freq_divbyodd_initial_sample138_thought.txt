To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges of the clock. The divided clock output is then derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. We will use a parameterized module to make the design flexible and reusable. The parameter NUM_DIV will be used to specify the odd divisor value, defaulting to 5 if not provided. We will also implement an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
