To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we will design a Verilog module that utilizes two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. The counters will count up to the divisor value minus one, which is an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. We will use two separate clock dividers for the positive and negative edges of the clock, and the final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two dividers. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
