To design the frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, we need to implement the following steps:
1. Define the module with input ports for the clock signal, active low reset signal, and output port for the divided clock.
2. Use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock signal. These counters should count up to NUM_DIV - 1, where NUM_DIV is the odd divisor value provided as a parameter.
3. Use two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock for positive and negative edges of the clock signal. These dividers should toggle when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV.
4. Derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2 to account for both clock edges.
5. Implement the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
