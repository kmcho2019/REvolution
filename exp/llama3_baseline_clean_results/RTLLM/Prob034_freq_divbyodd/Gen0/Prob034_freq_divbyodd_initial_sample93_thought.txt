To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we can utilize two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Each counter counts up to the odd divisor value minus one. Two separate clock dividers are used for the positive and negative edges of the clock, toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value. The final divided clock output is derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers to account for both clock edges. The design will also incorporate an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
