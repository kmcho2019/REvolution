The frequency divider module freq_divbyodd will be implemented using two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock signal clk. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output clk_div will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The module will be reset by the active low reset signal rst_n, which will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
