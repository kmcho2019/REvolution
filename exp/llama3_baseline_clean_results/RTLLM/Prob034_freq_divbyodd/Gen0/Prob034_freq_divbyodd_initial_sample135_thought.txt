To implement the freq_divbyodd module, we need to create a design that can divide the input clock frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. This can be achieved by using two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
