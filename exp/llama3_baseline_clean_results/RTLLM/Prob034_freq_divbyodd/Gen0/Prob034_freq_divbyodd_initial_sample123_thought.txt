To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, I will create a Verilog module named freq_divbyodd. The module will utilize two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, with each counter counting up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, and the final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two dividers. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. I will use parameters to make the module reusable with different odd divisor values.
