To design the frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, we first define the module with the specified input and output ports. We then declare two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used to generate the divided clock outputs for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is obtained by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. The parameter NUM_DIV is used to determine the divisor value, which defaults to 5 if not specified. We use always blocks to implement the counter logic, clock divider logic, and the reset functionality.
