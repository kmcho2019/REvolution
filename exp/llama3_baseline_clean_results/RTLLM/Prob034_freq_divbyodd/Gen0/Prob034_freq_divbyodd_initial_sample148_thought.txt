The freq_divbyodd module will be designed using two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. This design will ensure that the input clock frequency is divided by an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. The active low reset signal rst_n will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
