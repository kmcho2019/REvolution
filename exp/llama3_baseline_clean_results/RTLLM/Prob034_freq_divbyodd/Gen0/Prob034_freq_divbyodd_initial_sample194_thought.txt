To implement the frequency divider module, we first define the module with the specified input and output ports and a parameter for the divisor value. We then declare the internal counters and clock divider signals. The counters are used to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and the clock dividers are toggled based on these counters. The final divided clock output is generated by OR-ing the two clock dividers. We use always blocks to describe the behavior of the counters and the clock dividers, and we use the parameter NUM_DIV to determine when to toggle the clock dividers.
