To implement the frequency divider, we will create a module that takes an input clock and an active low reset signal. We'll use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. The divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. The active low reset signal will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. The divisor value will be defined by a parameter NUM_DIV, which defaults to 5.
