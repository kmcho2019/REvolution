To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and the clock dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers to account for both clock edges. The active low reset signal will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
