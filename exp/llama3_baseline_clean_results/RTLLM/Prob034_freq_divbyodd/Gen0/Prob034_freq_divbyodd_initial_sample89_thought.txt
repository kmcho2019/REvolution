The freq_divbyodd module will be designed as a parameterized Verilog module. It will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock signal clk. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and the dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
