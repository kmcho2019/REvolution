To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we will design a module named freq_divbyodd. The module will have two input ports, clk and rst_n, and one output port, clk_div. We will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock for positive and negative edges. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. We will use a parameter NUM_DIV to define the odd divisor value, which defaults to 5.
