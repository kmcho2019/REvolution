To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we'll implement a module with two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. These counters will be used to toggle two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges of the clock, which will then be logically OR-ed to derive the final divided clock output. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. We'll utilize Verilog's parameter feature to define the divisor value, defaulting it to 5.
