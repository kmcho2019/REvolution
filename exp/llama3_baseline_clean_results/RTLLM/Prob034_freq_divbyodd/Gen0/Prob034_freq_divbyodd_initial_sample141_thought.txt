We will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Each counter will count up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers will be toggled when their respective counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
