To design the freq_divbyodd module, we will implement two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. These counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1. We will then use two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock signals for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
