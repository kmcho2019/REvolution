The frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, will be designed using two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used to generate the divided clock for the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These will be toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
