To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and two separate clock dividers will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two dividers to account for both clock edges. 
We will use the active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs. The design will be implemented as a Verilog module with the specified input and output ports.
