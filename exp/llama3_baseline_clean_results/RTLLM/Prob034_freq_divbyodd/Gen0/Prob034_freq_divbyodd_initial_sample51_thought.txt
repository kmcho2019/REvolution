To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we can utilize two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. By employing two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges, we can ensure that the final divided clock output accurately accounts for both clock edges. The use of an active low reset signal allows for initialization of the counters and clock divider outputs. This approach enables us to achieve a frequency divider that meets the specified requirements.
