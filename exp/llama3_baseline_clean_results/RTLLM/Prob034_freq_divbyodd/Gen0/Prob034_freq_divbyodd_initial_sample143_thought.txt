To create a frequency divider module that can divide the input clock frequency by an odd number, we need to design a system that can track the clock edges and generate the divided clock output accordingly. The module will have two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two dividers. We will also implement an active low reset to initialize the counters and the clock dividers. The module will be parameterized to allow for different odd divisor values.
