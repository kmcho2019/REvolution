To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we need to follow these steps:
1. Define the module freq_divbyodd with input ports clk and rst_n, and output port clk_div.
2. Declare the parameter NUM_DIV with a default value of 5.
3. Initialize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
4. Use two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, for positive and negative edges of the clock.
5. Toggle clk_div1 and clk_div2 when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV.
6. Derive the final divided clock output clk_div by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
7. Use the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
