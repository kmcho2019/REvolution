To implement the frequency divider, we will design a module called freq_divbyodd with the specified input and output ports. The module will have two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
