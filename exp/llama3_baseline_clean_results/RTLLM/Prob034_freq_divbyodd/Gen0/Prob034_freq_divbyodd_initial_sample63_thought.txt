To design the freq_divbyodd module, we first define the module with the given parameters and ports. We then initialize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, are used for positive and negative edges of the clock. We use the counters to toggle these dividers when they reach half of NUM_DIV. Finally, we derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
