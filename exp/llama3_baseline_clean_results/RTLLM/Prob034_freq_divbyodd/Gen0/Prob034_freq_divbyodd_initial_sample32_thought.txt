To design the freq_divbyodd module, we first need to understand the requirements: the module should divide the input clock frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. This involves using two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock and generating two separate clock dividers that are toggled based on these counters. The final divided clock output will be the logical OR of these two dividers. We'll implement this using Verilog, ensuring that the counters reset properly when the active low reset signal rst_n is asserted.
