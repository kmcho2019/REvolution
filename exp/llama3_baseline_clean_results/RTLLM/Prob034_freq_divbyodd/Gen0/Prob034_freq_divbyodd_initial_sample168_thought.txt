To implement the frequency divider module, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock signal. We'll use these counters to toggle two separate clock dividers, one for the positive edge and one for the negative edge, when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two clock dividers. We'll also include an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and clock dividers.
