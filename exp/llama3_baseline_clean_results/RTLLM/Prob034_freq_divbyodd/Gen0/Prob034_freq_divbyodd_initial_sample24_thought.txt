The design involves creating a frequency divider module that can divide an input clock signal by an odd number specified by the parameter NUM_DIV. To achieve this, two counters are used to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock, and two separate clock dividers are used for the positive and negative edges. These dividers are toggled when the counters reach half of the specified odd divisor. The final divided clock output is obtained by logically OR-ing the two divider outputs to account for both clock edges. The module also includes an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the divider outputs.
