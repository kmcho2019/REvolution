To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we will design a Verilog module named freq_divbyodd. This module will have two input ports, clk and rst_n, and one output port, clk_div. The module will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. It will also utilize two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to divide the clock frequency at positive and negative edges, respectively. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value, NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be obtained by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal, rst_n, will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
