To implement the frequency divider module, we need to create a module named freq_divbyodd that takes in the input clock signal clk, the active low reset signal rst_n, and provides the divided clock output clk_div. The module will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the NUM_DIV value. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
