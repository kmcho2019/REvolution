To design the frequency divider module freq_divbyodd, we will first define the module with the specified input and output ports, and a parameter for the odd divisor value. We will then declare two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges, respectively. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two dividers. We will also implement the active low reset logic to initialize the counters and the dividers.
