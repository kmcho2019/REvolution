To implement the frequency divider, we can utilize two counters, one for tracking the rising edges and another for the falling edges of the input clock. These counters will increment until they reach half of the odd divisor value, at which point the corresponding divided clock output will toggle. By logically OR-ing the divided clock outputs for the positive and negative edges, we can derive the final divided clock output that accounts for both edges. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the divided clock outputs.
