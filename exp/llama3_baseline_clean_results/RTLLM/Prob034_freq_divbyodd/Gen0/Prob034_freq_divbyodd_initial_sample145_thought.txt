To implement the frequency divider module, we need to follow these steps: 
- Define the module with the specified input and output ports.
- Initialize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. 
- Implement two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, for the positive and negative edges of the clock.
- Toggle clk_div1 and clk_div2 when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV.
- Derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
- Use the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
