To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, we will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Each counter will count up to NUM_DIV - 1. We will then use two separate clock dividers for the positive and negative edges of the clock, toggling them when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. We will also include an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
