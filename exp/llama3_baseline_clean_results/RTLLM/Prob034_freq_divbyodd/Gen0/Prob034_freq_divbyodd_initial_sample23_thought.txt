The freq_divbyodd module is designed to divide the input clock frequency by an odd number, defined by the parameter NUM_DIV. It utilizes two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to handle positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is obtained by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The module is reset by the active low signal rst_n, which initializes the counters and the clock divider outputs.
