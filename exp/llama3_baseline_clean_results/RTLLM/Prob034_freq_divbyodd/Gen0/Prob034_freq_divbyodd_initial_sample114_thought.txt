To implement the frequency divider module, we will follow these steps:
1. Define the module with the given ports and a parameter NUM_DIV for the odd divisor value.
2. Initialize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
3. Implement the logic to count up to NUM_DIV - 1 for each counter.
4. Create two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, that toggle when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV.
5. Derive the final divided clock output, clk_div, by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2 to account for both clock edges.
6. Incorporate the active low reset signal rst_n to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.

We will use a parameterized module to make the design flexible and reusable. The counters will be implemented using always blocks, and the clock dividers will be updated based on the counter values.
