To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we can use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. We will use a parameter NUM_DIV to define the odd divisor value. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, and two separate clock dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. We will also implement an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
