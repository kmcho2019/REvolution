To implement the frequency divider module, we'll use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. We'll then use these counters to toggle two separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing these two dividers. We'll also include an active low reset signal to initialize the counters and dividers. The module will be designed using Verilog, with a parameterizable odd divisor value.
