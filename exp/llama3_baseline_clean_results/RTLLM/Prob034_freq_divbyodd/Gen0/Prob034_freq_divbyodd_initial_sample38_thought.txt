To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_divbyodd', we will utilize two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1, where NUM_DIV is an odd number defined by the parameter. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. These dividers will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
