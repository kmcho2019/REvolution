The design of the freq_divbyodd module involves using two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock and generating two separate divided clock signals, one for each edge, which are then logically OR-ed to produce the final divided clock output. The counters and the clock divider outputs are reset when the active low reset signal rst_n is asserted. The division factor is determined by the parameter NUM_DIV, which defaults to 5 and must be an odd number.
