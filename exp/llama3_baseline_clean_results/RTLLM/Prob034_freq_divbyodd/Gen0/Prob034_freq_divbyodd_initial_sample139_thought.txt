To create the freq_divbyodd module, we will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. We'll utilize two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, which will be toggled when the counters reach half of the odd divisor value (NUM_DIV). The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
