To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by odd numbers, we will use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. The counters will count up to the odd divisor value minus one. Two separate clock dividers will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock, and these will be toggled when the counters reach half of the divisor value. The final divided clock output will be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers to account for both clock edges. The active low reset signal will be used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
