To implement the dual-port RAM, we will use two always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will handle the write operation and the reset of the RAM array. The second always block will handle the read operation and reset of the read_data register. We will use a reg array to represent the RAM and initialize it with zeros. The write and read operations will be enabled by the write_en and read_en signals, respectively, and will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal.
