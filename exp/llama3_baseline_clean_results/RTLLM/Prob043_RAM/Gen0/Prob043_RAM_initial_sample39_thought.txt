The design idea is to create a dual-port RAM module with a depth of 8 and a bit width of 6 bits. The module will have two groups of ports: one for reading data and one for writing data. The module will use two always blocks to handle write and read operations. The first always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal and will handle the write operation. The second always block will also trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal and will handle the read operation.
