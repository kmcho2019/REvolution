The module RAM will be implemented as a dual-port RAM. This will involve defining a register array RAM to hold the data. The write operation will be handled in one always block, triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. The read operation will be handled in another always block, also triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. The write and read operations can occur simultaneously. All memory locations will be initialized to 0 on reset.
