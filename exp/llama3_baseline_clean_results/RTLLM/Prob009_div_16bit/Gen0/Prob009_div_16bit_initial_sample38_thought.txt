To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use two always blocks. The first block will update the register values with the input values. The second block will perform the division operation by comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly, and repeating the process until all dividend bits are processed. We will use temporary registers to hold the values of the dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder during the division process.
