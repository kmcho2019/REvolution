The 16-bit divider module will use two combinational always blocks to perform the division operation. The first always block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation by iteratively comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed. The division operation will be implemented using a simple iterative approach, where the remainder is updated at each iteration by concatenating it with the next bit of the dividend.
