To implement a 16-bit divider module, we'll follow the described algorithm. We'll start by extracting the higher bits of the dividend, matching the bit width of the divisor (8 bits in this case), and then compare these bits with the divisor. Based on the comparison, we'll update the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed. We'll use two always blocks in the module, one to update the input registers and the other to perform the division operation. The division will be performed bit by bit, starting from the most significant bit of the dividend.
