To implement the 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will utilize two always blocks. The first always block will be responsible for updating the internal registers a_reg and b_reg with the input values A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation by iteratively comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. We will use a temporary variable to store the remainder and quotient during the division process.
