The module div_16bit will be designed using two always blocks. The first always block updates the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B, respectively. The second always block performs the division operation. It extracts the higher bits of the dividend, compares them with the divisor, calculates the remainder, and updates the quotient. This process is repeated until all dividend bits are processed. The final quotient and remainder are assigned to the output ports result and odd, respectively.
