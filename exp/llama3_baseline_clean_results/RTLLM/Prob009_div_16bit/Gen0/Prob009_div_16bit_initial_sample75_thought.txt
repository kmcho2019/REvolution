To implement the 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use two always blocks. The first always block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation.

The division operation will be performed by extracting the higher bits of the dividend, matching the bit width of the divisor, comparing these bits with the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed.

The use of combinational logic ensures that the division operation is performed without any clock signal, and the output is available as soon as the input values change.
