To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use a sequential approach. The module will have two main always blocks: one for capturing the input values into registers and another for performing the division operation. The division operation will involve comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder, and then processing the remaining bits of the dividend. This process will be repeated until all bits of the dividend are processed. The output of the module will be the quotient and the remainder.
