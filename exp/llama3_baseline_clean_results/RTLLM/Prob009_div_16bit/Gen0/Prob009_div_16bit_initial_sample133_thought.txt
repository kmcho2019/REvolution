To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we need to break down the division process into smaller steps. The main idea is to compare the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor and then update the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process is repeated until all bits of the dividend are processed.

The first always block is used to update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of the input dividend A and divisor B, respectively. This ensures that the division operation is performed on the latest input values.

The second always block performs the division operation by comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder, and then shifting the dividend to process the next bit. This process is repeated 8 times, once for each bit in the divisor.

We use the variables a_reg, b_reg, result_reg, and odd_reg to store the values of the dividend, divisor, quotient, and remainder, respectively. We also use a variable, temp, to store the temporary result during the division process.
