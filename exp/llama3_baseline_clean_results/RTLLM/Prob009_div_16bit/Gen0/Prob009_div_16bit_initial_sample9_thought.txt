The div_16bit module is designed to perform a 16-bit division operation with an 8-bit divisor. It uses two combinational always blocks to achieve this. The first block updates the internal registers with the input values, while the second block performs the actual division operation. The division process involves comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, setting the quotient accordingly, and repeating the process until all bits are processed. The final quotient and remainder are then outputted.
