To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we'll design a module named `div_16bit` with two input ports `A` (16-bit dividend) and `B` (8-bit divisor), and two output ports `result` (16-bit quotient) and `odd` (16-bit remainder). 

We will use two always blocks. The first always block will update two registers `a_reg` and `b_reg` with the input values `A` and `B`. 

In the second always block, we will implement the division logic. We will compare the most significant bits of `a_reg` with `b_reg`, and update the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will be repeated for all bits of the dividend.

However, Verilog doesn't directly support non-constant delays or iterative operations like loops in combinational logic, so we will have to unroll the division process and perform it in a single always block.
