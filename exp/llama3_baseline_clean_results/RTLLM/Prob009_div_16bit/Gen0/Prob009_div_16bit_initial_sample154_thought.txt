The idea here is to implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic. The approach will be to use two always blocks: one to capture the input values and the other to perform the division. In the division block, we will shift and compare the dividend bits with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will continue until all bits of the dividend are processed.

Here's a step-by-step breakdown:
1. Capture the input values A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg in the first always block.
2. Initialize variables to store the quotient and remainder.
3. Compare the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, and update the quotient and remainder based on the comparison.
4. Shift the dividend to the left and repeat the comparison until all bits are processed.
5. Output the final quotient and remainder.

This process will be implemented using two always blocks in the Verilog module div_16bit.
