To implement the 16-bit divider module, we will utilize two always blocks in combinational logic. The first block will register the inputs, ensuring they are stable before the division operation begins. The second block will perform the division by iteratively comparing and subtracting the divisor from the dividend, constructing the quotient and remainder bit by bit. We will use a temporary register to hold the current state of the division process and update it based on the comparison results. The final quotient and remainder will be assigned to the output ports after all bits have been processed.
