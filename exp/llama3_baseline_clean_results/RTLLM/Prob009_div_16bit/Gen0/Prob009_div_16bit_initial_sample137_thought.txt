To implement the 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we can utilize a shift-and-compare approach. We first extract the higher bits of the dividend, matching the bit width of the divisor, and compare them with the divisor. Based on the comparison, we determine the quotient bit and calculate the remainder. We then concatenate the remainder with the highest remaining bit of the dividend and repeat the process until all dividend bits are processed. The process is divided into two always blocks. The first always block is used to update the register values, while the second always block performs the division operation.
