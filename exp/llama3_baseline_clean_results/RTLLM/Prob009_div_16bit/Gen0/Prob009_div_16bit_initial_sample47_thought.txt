To implement the 16-bit divider module, we need to use two always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B, respectively. The second always block will perform the division operation using the values of a_reg and b_reg. We will use a temporary variable to store the quotient and remainder during each iteration of the division process. The division process involves comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, setting the quotient bit, calculating the remainder, and repeating the process until all dividend bits are processed.
