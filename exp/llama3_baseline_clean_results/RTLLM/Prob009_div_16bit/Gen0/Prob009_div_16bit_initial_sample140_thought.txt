To implement the 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will follow the standard division algorithm. The main idea is to compare the most significant bits of the dividend with the divisor, and based on this comparison, decide the quotient bit and update the remainder for the next iteration. We will use two always blocks: one for updating the register values with the input values and another for performing the division operation. The division operation will involve a series of comparisons and shifts to update the quotient and remainder until all bits of the dividend are processed. We will use a temporary variable to hold the remainder and the quotient during the division process.
