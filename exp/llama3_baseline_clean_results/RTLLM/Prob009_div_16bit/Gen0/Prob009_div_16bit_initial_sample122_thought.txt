To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we'll use a two-stage approach. 
The first stage involves registering the input values to ensure they are stable for the division operation. 
The second stage performs the division using a simple iterative comparison and subtraction process, 
extracting and concatenating the remainder at each step until all dividend bits are processed.
We'll utilize two always blocks to achieve this: one for registering the inputs and the other for the division logic.
