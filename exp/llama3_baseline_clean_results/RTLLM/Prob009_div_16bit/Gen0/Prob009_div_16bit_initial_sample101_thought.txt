To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use two always blocks. The first block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of the dividend (A) and the divisor (B), respectively. The second block will perform the division operation by extracting the higher bits of the dividend, comparing them with the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. We will use a temporary variable to store the remainder and quotient during the division process. We will repeat the comparison and update process until all bits of the dividend are processed.
