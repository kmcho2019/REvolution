To implement the 16-bit divider module, we'll use two always blocks in combinational logic. The first always block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation by extracting the higher bits of the dividend, comparing them with the divisor, and then updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. This process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed. We will also implement the necessary logic to handle the case when the divisor is zero.
