To implement the 16-bit divider module, we'll use two always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will be a combinational block that updates the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B, respectively, whenever the inputs change. The second always block will perform the division operation using the extracted higher bits of the dividend and the divisor, and update the quotient and remainder accordingly. The division process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed.
