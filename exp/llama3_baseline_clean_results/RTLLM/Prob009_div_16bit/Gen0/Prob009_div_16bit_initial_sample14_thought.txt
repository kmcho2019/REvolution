The div_16bit module can be implemented using two always blocks in Verilog. The first always block will be used to latch the input values A and B into registers a_reg and b_reg. The second always block will perform the division operation. We will use a temporary register, temp, to store the remainder and the quotient. We will iterate over each bit of the dividend, comparing it with the divisor and updating the remainder and quotient accordingly. The final quotient and remainder will be assigned to the output ports result and odd, respectively.
