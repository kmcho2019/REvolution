To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will follow the standard division algorithm. The module, named div_16bit, will take a 16-bit dividend (A) and an 8-bit divisor (B) as inputs and produce a 16-bit quotient (result) and a 16-bit remainder (odd). We will use two always blocks: the first to update registers with input values and the second to perform the division operation. In the division operation, we compare the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, update the quotient and remainder accordingly, and repeat this process until all bits of the dividend are processed.
