To implement the 16-bit divider module, we will use two always blocks in combinational logic. The first always block will update two internal registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the input values A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation. 
We will extract the higher bits of the dividend, matching the bit width of the divisor, and compare them with the divisor. 
Based on the comparison, we will set the quotient to 1 or 0 and calculate the remainder. 
Then, we will concatenate the remainder with the next bit of the dividend and repeat the process until all bits are processed. 
The quotient and remainder will be calculated and updated at each iteration.
