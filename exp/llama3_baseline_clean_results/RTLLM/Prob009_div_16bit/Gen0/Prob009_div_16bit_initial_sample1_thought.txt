To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use two always blocks. The first always block will update the registers with the input values. The second always block will perform the division operation by comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly, and then concatenating the remainder with the next bit of the dividend. This process will be repeated until all bits of the dividend are processed. We will use a temporary variable to store the remainder and another variable to store the quotient. We will also handle the case where the divisor is zero to avoid division by zero errors.
