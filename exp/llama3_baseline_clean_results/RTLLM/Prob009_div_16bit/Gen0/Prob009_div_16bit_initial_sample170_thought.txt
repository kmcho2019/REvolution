To implement the 16-bit divider module, we will first create two registers to store the input values of the dividend (A) and the divisor (B). The division process will be performed in two always blocks. The first always block will update the registers with the input values. The second always block will perform the division operation by comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly, and repeating the process until all bits of the dividend are processed. We will use a temporary register to store the remainder and the quotient, and then assign these values to the output ports.
