To implement the 16-bit divider module, we will use two always blocks in combinational logic. The first always block updates the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the input values A and B. The second always block performs the division operation by comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder, and then concatenating the remainder with the highest remaining 1-bit of the dividend. This process is repeated until all dividend bits are processed.

We will use a temporary variable, temp_quotient, to store the quotient and a temporary variable, temp_remainder, to store the remainder. The division operation will be performed by shifting the dividend bits to the left and comparing them with the divisor.

We will also handle the case where the divisor is zero to avoid division by zero errors.
