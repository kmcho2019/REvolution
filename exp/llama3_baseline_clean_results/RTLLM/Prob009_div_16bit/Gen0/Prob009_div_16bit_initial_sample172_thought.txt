To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we will use two always blocks. The first always block will update the values of two registers, a_reg and b_reg, with the values of A and B. The second always block will perform the division operation by repeatedly comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. We will use a temporary variable to store the result of each comparison and update the dividend for the next iteration. The division process will continue until all bits of the dividend have been processed.
