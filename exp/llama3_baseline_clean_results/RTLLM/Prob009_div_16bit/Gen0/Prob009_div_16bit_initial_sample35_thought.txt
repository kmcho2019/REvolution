The 16-bit divider module can be designed using two combinational always blocks. The first block will update the internal registers a_reg and b_reg whenever there's a change in the inputs A and B. The second block will perform the division operation by iteratively comparing and updating the remainder and quotient. The division process involves comparing the most significant bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly, and then shifting the dividend to process the remaining bits.
