To implement the 16-bit divider module, we need to perform a series of subtractions between the dividend and the divisor. The process starts with the higher bits of the dividend and continues until all bits are processed. We use two always blocks: one to update the input registers and the other to perform the division. We compare the bits of the dividend with the divisor, update the quotient and remainder accordingly, and repeat this process for all bits. This approach allows us to implement the division operation in a combinational logic circuit.
