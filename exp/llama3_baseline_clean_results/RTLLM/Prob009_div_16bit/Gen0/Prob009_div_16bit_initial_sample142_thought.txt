To implement a 16-bit divider module in combinational logic, we can utilize the concept of repeated subtraction, where the divisor is subtracted from the dividend as many times as possible without resulting in a negative value. We will compare the most significant bits of the dividend with the divisor and use the difference as the remainder. The process will be repeated until all dividend bits are processed. We will use two always blocks: one for updating the input values and the other for performing the division operation.
