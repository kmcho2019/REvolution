To design a 16-bit divider module, we can break down the division process into smaller steps. First, we need to extract the higher bits of the dividend that match the bit width of the divisor, which is 8 bits in this case. We compare these bits with the divisor and update the quotient accordingly. The difference between the extracted dividend bits and the divisor is used as the remainder. We then concatenate this remainder with the next bit of the dividend and repeat the process until all bits of the dividend are processed. We will use two always blocks in the Verilog module: one to update the input registers and another to perform the division operation. We will use a temporary register to hold the intermediate results during the division process.
