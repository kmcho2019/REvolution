The second always block will perform the actual division operation. We will iterate through each bit of the dividend, from most significant bit to least significant bit, and compare it with the divisor. If the dividend bits are greater, we will set the corresponding quotient bit to 1; otherwise, we will set it to 0. We will then update the remainder with the difference between the dividend bits and the divisor. This process will be repeated until all bits of the dividend are processed.
