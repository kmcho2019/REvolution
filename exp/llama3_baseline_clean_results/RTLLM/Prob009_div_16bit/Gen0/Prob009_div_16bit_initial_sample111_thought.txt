The second always block will perform the division operation. It will extract the higher bits of the dividend, compare these bits with the divisor, and update the quotient and remainder accordingly. We will use a while loop to repeat the process until all dividend bits are processed.

We need to be careful with the bit width of the dividend and the divisor. Since the dividend is 16-bit and the divisor is 8-bit, we can extract the higher 8 bits of the dividend and compare them with the divisor. We will use a temporary variable, temp, to store the remainder.
