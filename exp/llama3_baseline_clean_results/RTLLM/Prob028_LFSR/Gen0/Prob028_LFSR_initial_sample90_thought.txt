To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will design a module named LFSR with input ports for the clock signal (clk) and the active high reset signal (rst), and an output port (out) to display the current 4-bit state of the LFSR. The LFSR will operate based on the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback calculated by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register will be reset to zero; otherwise, the register bits will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit.
