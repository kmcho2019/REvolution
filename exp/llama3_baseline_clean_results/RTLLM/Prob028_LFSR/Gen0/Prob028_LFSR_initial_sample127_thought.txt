To implement the 4-bit LFSR, we can utilize a 4-bit register to store the current state. The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to generate the new least significant bit (LSB) on each clock cycle. The LFSR will be reset to zero when the active high reset signal is asserted. The shifting and feedback operations will occur on the rising edge of the clock, ensuring synchronization.
