To implement the LFSR, we need to create a 4-bit shift register that shifts its bits to the left on the rising edge of the clock signal. The new bit inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) position is determined by the feedback mechanism, which involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. We will use a always block to describe the behavior of the register. When the reset signal is high, the register should be initialized to zero. Otherwise, it shifts the bits and applies the feedback on the rising edge of the clock.
