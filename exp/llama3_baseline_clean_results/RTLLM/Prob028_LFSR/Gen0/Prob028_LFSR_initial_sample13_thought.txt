To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to follow these steps:
1. Initialize the register with zeros when the active high reset signal (rst) is asserted.
2. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result.
3. On the rising edge of the clock signal (clk), shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) position if the reset signal is not asserted.
We will use a reg type to hold the current state of the LFSR and always block to describe the sequential behavior.
