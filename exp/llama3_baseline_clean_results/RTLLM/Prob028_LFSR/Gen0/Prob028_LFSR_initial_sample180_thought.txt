To design a 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism will be implemented by calculating the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to get the new least significant bit (LSB). On the rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, we will initialize the register to zero. Otherwise, we will shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the LSB.
