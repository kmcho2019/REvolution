To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a procedural block to handle the clock and reset signals. The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, inverting the result, and using it as the new least significant bit (LSB) on each clock cycle. We will utilize a reg type to hold the current state of the LFSR and update it accordingly.
