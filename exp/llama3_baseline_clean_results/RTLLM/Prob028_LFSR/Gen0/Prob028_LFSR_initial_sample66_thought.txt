To implement the LFSR module, we will first define the module with its input and output ports. Then, we will use a reg variable to store the current state of the LFSR. We will apply the feedback mechanism to generate the next state of the LFSR. The feedback signal will be calculated as the XOR of the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, and then inverted. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register will be reset to zero; otherwise, the bits will be shifted left and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit.
