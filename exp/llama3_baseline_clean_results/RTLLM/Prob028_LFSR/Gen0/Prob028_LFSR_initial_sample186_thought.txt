To design the LFSR module, we need to implement a 4-bit shift register with a specific feedback mechanism. The feedback is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register should be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the bits in the register should be shifted left, and the new feedback value should be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
