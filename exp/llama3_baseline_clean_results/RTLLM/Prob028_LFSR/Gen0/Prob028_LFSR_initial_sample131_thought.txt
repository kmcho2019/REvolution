To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will utilize the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback based on the XOR of specific bits in the register. The feedback calculation involves XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. This will be implemented using Verilog's bitwise operators. We will also incorporate a synchronous reset mechanism to initialize the register to zero when the reset signal is high.
