To design the LFSR module, we first identify the key components and operations involved: 
1. A 4-bit register to store the current state, 
2. A feedback mechanism based on the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, 
3. A clock signal to synchronize the shifting operation, and 
4. A reset signal to initialize the register.
The feedback is calculated by XORing the MSB and the second MSB, then inverting the result to produce the new bit to be inserted at the LSB during each clock cycle. 
We will use a combinational logic expression for the feedback calculation and a sequential logic block (always block) for the clock-synchronized shifting operation.
