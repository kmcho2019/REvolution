To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we'll implement a module that takes a clock signal and a reset signal as inputs and produces a 4-bit output representing the current state of the LFSR. The LFSR will operate based on the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback calculated by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On each rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to zero; otherwise, the bits will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit.
