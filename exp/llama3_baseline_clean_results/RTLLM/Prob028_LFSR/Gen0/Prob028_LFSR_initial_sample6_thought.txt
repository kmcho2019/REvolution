To implement the LFSR module, we first need to understand the feedback mechanism. The feedback signal is generated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. This feedback signal will be used as the new least significant bit (LSB) when shifting the register left on the rising edge of the clock. The module will also have a reset functionality that initializes the register to zero when the reset signal is high. This can be achieved using a combination of Verilog's built-in logical operators and a shift operation.
