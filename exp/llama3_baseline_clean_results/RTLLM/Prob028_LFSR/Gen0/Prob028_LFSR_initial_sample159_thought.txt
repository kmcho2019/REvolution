To design the LFSR module, we need to implement the following steps:
1. Initialize the register with a reset value when the reset signal is high.
2. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), and then inverting the result.
3. On the rising edge of the clock signal, shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) position.
We can achieve this using Verilog's reg and always blocks to define the register and the clock-driven logic, respectively.
