To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will utilize a reg type to hold the current state of the LFSR and assign it to the output port. We will use a feedback mechanism where the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit are XORed, and the result is inverted. On each rising edge of the clock, the register is either reset to zero if the reset signal is high or shifted left and updated with the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) if the reset signal is low.
