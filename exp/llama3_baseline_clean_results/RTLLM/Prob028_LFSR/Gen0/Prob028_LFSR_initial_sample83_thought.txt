The LFSR module can be designed by using a 4-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism is implemented using the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), and then inverting the result. The LFSR operates in two modes: reset and normal operation. In reset mode, the register is initialized to zero when the reset signal (rst) is high. In normal operation, the register is shifted left on the rising edge of the clock (clk), and the new feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
