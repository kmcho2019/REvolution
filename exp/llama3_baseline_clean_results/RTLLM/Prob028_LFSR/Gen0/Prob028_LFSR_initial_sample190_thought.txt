To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a reg to store the current state of the LFSR and update it based on the clock and reset signals. The feedback mechanism involves calculating the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, inverting the result, and using it as the new least significant bit (LSB) when shifting the register left. This process will create a pseudo-random sequence at the output.
