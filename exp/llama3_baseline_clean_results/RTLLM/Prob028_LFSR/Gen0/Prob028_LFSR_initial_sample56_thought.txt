The Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) can be designed using a 4-bit register with a feedback mechanism based on the XOR of specific bits. The operation of the LFSR can be summarized as follows: 
1. Initialize the register to zero on the rising edge of the clock when the reset signal is high.
2. On the rising edge of the clock when the reset signal is low, shift the bits in the register to the left.
3. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit of the current state, then inverting the result.
4. Insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit of the register.

This process will produce a sequence of bits that appears random due to the feedback mechanism.
