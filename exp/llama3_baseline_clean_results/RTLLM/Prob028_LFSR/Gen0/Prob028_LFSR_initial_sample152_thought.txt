To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we can use a combination of always block for synchronous operations and basic bitwise operations. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock (clk) and the reset (rst) signal. If the reset is high, the register should be reset to zero. Otherwise, on each clock cycle, the bits should be shifted left and the feedback value, calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]) and then inverting the result, should be inserted at the least significant bit position.
