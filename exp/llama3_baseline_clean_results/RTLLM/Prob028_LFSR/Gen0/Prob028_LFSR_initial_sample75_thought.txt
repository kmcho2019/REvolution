The feedback mechanism will involve calculating the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. This value will be inserted as the least significant bit (LSB) on each clock cycle. We'll also implement an active high reset that clears the register to zero. The LFSR's operation can be captured using a combination of always blocks for handling the clock and reset, and a procedural assignment to calculate the new state of the register.
