To design the LFSR module, we will utilize the principles of digital logic and Verilog HDL. The LFSR's behavior will be described using a combination of sequential and combinatorial logic. We'll use the XOR operator (^) to calculate the feedback signal and a reg type to store the current state of the LFSR. On the rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal and either initialize the register or shift its contents based on the feedback.
