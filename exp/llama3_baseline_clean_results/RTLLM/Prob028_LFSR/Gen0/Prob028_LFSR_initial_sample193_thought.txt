The design involves creating a 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) that generates pseudo-random sequences based on a specific feedback mechanism. The LFSR operates by shifting bits and applying feedback calculated from the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The implementation will use a clock signal for synchronization and an active high reset signal for initialization. The main steps include: 
1. Declaring the module with its ports, 
2. Defining the internal register to hold the state of the LFSR,
3. Implementing the reset logic to initialize the register when the reset signal is high,
4. Implementing the shift and feedback logic on the rising edge of the clock when the reset signal is low, 
5. Assigning the output based on the current state of the LFSR.
