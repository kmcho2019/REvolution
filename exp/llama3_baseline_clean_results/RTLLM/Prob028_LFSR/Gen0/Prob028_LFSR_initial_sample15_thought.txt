The design of the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) will involve creating a module named LFSR with input ports for the clock signal (clk) and the active high reset signal (rst), and an output port (out) to represent the current state of the LFSR. The LFSR's operation will be based on shifting bits and applying feedback calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. This feedback will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) when the register is shifted. The LFSR will be reset to zero when the rst signal is high on the rising edge of the clk.
