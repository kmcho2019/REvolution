To design the LFSR module, we first need to understand the basic principle of how it operates. The LFSR generates a pseudo-random sequence by shifting the bits of its internal register and using a feedback mechanism. In this case, the feedback is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The design should include a reset mechanism to initialize the register to zero when needed. The module should be implemented using Verilog, incorporating the specified inputs (clk and rst) and output (out[3:0]), and should accurately reflect the operation described.
