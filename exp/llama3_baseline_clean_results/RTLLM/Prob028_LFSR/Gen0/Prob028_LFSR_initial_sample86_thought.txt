To design the 4-bit LFSR, we first need to understand the principle of operation. The register's feedback is determined by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On each clock cycle, if the reset is not high, the bits are shifted left, and the new feedback value is added to the least significant bit (LSB). This process continues to generate a pseudo-random sequence. The design will involve a combinational logic block to calculate the feedback and a sequential logic block (a flip-flop array) to hold the current state of the LFSR.
