To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to follow these steps: 
1. Initialize the LFSR with a reset signal: When the reset signal is high, the LFSR should be set to zero.
2. Calculate the feedback: The feedback signal is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result.
3. Shift the bits: On the rising edge of the clock signal, shift the bits in the LFSR to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
We will use a reg to store the current state of the LFSR and use a always block to handle the synchronous reset and the shift operation.
