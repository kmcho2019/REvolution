To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will first identify the feedback mechanism, which involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The LFSR will be implemented as a 4-bit register with a clocked shift operation. The feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) after shifting the bits left. An active high reset signal will be used to initialize the register to zero.
