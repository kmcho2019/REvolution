To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will first identify the key components and operations involved: 
1. The LFSR has a 4-bit register that can be initialized to zero when the reset signal is high.
2. The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to generate the new least significant bit (LSB).
3. On each rising edge of the clock, the LFSR either resets to zero or shifts its bits left and inserts the new feedback value at the LSB.
Given these steps, we will implement the LFSR using Verilog, focusing on capturing the reset condition, the feedback calculation, and the shifting operation in a single always block that is sensitive to the clock edge and reset signal.
