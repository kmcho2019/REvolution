To implement the LFSR, we will design a module that takes the clock signal (clk) and active high reset signal (rst) as inputs and generates a 4-bit output (out[3:0]) representing the current state of the LFSR. The LFSR will operate based on the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to zero; otherwise, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
