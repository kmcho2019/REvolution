To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will create a module named LFSR with input ports for the clock signal (clk) and the active high reset signal (rst), and an output port for the 4-bit output (out). We will utilize the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback based on the XOR of specific bits in the register. The feedback will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
