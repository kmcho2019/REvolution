To implement the LFSR, we need to create a 4-bit register and use a feedback mechanism based on the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit. The result is inverted to produce the feedback signal. We will use a clock signal to synchronize the shifting operation and a reset signal to initialize the register. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register is initialized to zero; otherwise, the bits in the register are shifted left, and the new feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
