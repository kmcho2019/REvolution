To design the LFSR module, we need to implement a 4-bit shift register with a feedback mechanism. The feedback signal will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On each clock cycle, if the reset is not high, the register will shift its bits to the left, inserting the feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) position. This will generate a pseudo-random sequence of bits. We can use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the register's state synchronously with the clock.
