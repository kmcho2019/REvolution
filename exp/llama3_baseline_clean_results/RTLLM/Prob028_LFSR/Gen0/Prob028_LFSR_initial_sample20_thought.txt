To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a register to hold the current state and a combinational logic block to generate the feedback signal. The feedback signal will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal. If it's high, we reset the register to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits in the register left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
