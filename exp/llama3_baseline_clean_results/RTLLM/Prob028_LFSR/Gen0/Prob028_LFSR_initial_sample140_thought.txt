To implement the LFSR, we need to consider the specific feedback mechanism and the shifting operation. We will use a 4-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback signal is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, and then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, we check if the reset signal is high. If it is, we initialize the register to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits in the register left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
