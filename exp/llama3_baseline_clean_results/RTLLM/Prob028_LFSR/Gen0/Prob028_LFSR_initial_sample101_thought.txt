To design the LFSR module, we need to consider the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback based on the XOR of specific bits in the register. The feedback signal is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, we check if the reset signal is high. If it is, we initialize the register to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits in the register left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit. This process is repeated to produce a sequence of pseudo-random bits.
