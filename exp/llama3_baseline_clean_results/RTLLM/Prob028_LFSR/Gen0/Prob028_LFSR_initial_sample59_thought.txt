To implement the LFSR, we will design a module named LFSR that takes a clock signal (clk) and an active high reset signal (rst) as inputs. The module will have a 4-bit output (out) representing the current state of the LFSR. The LFSR will operate by shifting bits left and applying feedback based on the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
