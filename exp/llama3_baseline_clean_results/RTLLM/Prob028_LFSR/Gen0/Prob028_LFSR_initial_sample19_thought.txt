To implement the LFSR, we need to follow these steps:
1. Initialize the register to zero when the reset signal (rst) is high.
2. On the rising edge of the clock (clk), if the reset signal (rst) is low, shift the bits in the register to the left.
3. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result.
4. Insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) of the register.
We will use a reg variable to hold the current state of the LFSR and assign it to the output port out.
The feedback calculation and the shifting operation will be implemented using Verilog's built-in operators and bitwise operations.
