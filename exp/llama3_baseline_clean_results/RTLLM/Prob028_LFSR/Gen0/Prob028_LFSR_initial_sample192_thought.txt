The LFSR module will be designed as a 4-bit shift register with a feedback mechanism based on the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit. The feedback is inverted to produce the new least significant bit (LSB). 
The module will have two inputs: a clock signal (clk) and an active high reset signal (rst). 
When the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to zero. 
On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the LSB.
