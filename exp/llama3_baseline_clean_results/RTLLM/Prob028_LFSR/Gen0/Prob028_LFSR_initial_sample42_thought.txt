To implement the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to produce the new least significant bit (LSB) for the next clock cycle. On the rising edge of the clock, we check the reset signal. If it's high, the register is reset to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits left and insert the calculated feedback at the LSB. We will utilize Verilog's procedural assignment within an always block to achieve this.
