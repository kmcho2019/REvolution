To design the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state. The register will be updated on the rising edge of the clock signal. We will calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit of the current state, and then inverting the result. We will use a reset signal to initialize the register to zero when it is high. The out port will be used to output the current state of the LFSR.
