To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a combination of always blocks to handle the reset and clock edges, and internal wires to calculate the feedback value. The LFSR will have a 4-bit output representing its current state, and it will operate based on the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback. The feedback is determined by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the LFSR will reset to zero; otherwise, it will shift its bits left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
