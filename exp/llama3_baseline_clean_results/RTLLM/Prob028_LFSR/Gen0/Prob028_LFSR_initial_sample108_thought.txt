To implement the LFSR, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state. The feedback mechanism will be based on the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit. This result will be inverted to produce the new least significant bit (LSB) on each clock cycle. The design will include an asynchronous active-high reset to initialize the register to zero. We will utilize the non-blocking assignment operator (<:) in the always block to ensure proper shift register behavior.
