To design the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state. We will calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit (2nd MSB), and then inverting the result. On each rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, we will initialize the register to zero. Otherwise, we will shift the bits in the register left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
