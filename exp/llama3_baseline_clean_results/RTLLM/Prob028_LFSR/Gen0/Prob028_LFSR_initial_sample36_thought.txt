The design of the 4-bit LFSR involves creating a digital circuit that can generate a pseudo-random sequence. The key to this is in the feedback mechanism, where the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit are XORed, and the result is inverted to provide the new least significant bit on each clock cycle. This process will create a sequence that appears random due to the nature of the XOR operation and the shifting of bits. To implement this in Verilog, we'll use a register to hold the current state and update it based on the feedback mechanism at each positive edge of the clock signal, unless the reset signal is high, in which case the register should be reset to zero.
