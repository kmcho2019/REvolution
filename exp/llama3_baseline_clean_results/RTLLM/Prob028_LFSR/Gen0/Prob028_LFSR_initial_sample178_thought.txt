To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a combination of combinational logic for the feedback calculation and sequential logic for the register update. The LFSR will utilize a synchronous reset (rst) to initialize the register to zero. On every rising edge of the clock (clk), the register will either be reset if rst is high or shifted left with the calculated feedback inserted at the least significant bit. The feedback is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]) and then inverting the result.
