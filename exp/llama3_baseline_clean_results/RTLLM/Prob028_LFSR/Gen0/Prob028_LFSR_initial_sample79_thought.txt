To implement the LFSR, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state. On the rising edge of the clock, we will check if the reset signal is high. If it is, we will reset the register to zero. If not, we will calculate the feedback by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit (second MSB), then inverting the result. We will shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the feedback at the least significant bit (LSB). This process will continue until the LFSR is reset or the simulation is stopped.
