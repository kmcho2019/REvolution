To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to consider the following steps:
- Initialize the LFSR with a reset signal (rst) to ensure a known starting state.
- Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result.
- On each rising edge of the clock (clk), shift the bits in the LFSR to the left and insert the feedback signal at the least significant bit (LSB) position, unless the reset signal is high, in which case the LFSR should be reset to zero.
This design ensures that the LFSR generates a pseudo-random sequence based on the specified feedback mechanism.
