To design the LFSR module, we will first define the 4-bit register that holds the current state of the LFSR. We will use a procedural block (always) to handle the clock and reset signals. When the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, on the rising edge of the clock, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, then inserted at the least significant bit. This feedback mechanism will produce a pseudo-random sequence.
