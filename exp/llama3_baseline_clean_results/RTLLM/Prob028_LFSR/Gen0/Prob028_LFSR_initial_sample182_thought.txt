To design the LFSR module, we need to follow the specified implementation. The key components involve: 
1. Initializing the register to zero when the active high reset signal (rst) is asserted.
2. Calculating the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result.
3. Shifting the bits in the register to the left and inserting the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) on the rising edge of the clock (clk) when the reset signal is not asserted.
This will involve using Verilog's procedural assignments within an always block to handle the clock and reset logic, and continuous assignments to handle the feedback calculation.
