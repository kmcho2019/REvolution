To design the LFSR module, we will implement a 4-bit register that shifts its bits left on the rising edge of the clock signal (clk) and inserts a new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB). The feedback value is generated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. We will use the always_ff block in Verilog to model the sequential behavior of the LFSR, and handle the active high reset signal (rst) to initialize the register to zero when asserted.
