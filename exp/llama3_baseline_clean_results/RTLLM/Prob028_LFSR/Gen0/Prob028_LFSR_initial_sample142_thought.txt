To design the 4-bit LFSR, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the register with a reset signal (rst) that sets all bits to zero when high.
2. On the rising edge of the clock (clk), shift the bits in the register one position to the left.
3. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result.
4. Insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) of the register after shifting.
5. Output the current state of the LFSR as a 4-bit vector (out[3:0]).
