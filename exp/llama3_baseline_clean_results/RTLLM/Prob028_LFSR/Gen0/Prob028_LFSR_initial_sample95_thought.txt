To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a synchronous reset mechanism to clear the register when the reset signal is high. Otherwise, on each rising edge of the clock, we shift the bits to the left and generate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The feedback signal is then inserted at the least significant bit position to generate the next state of the LFSR. We will use a Verilog module to encapsulate this functionality, utilizing a reg to hold the state of the LFSR and an always block to handle the clock and reset operations.
