To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will follow the principles of digital circuit design, focusing on the shift operation, feedback mechanism, and reset functionality. The main steps involve:
1. Initializing the register with a reset signal.
2. Implementing the shifting operation on the rising edge of the clock.
3. Calculating the feedback signal as the XOR of the most significant and second most significant bits.
4. Inserting the feedback value at the least significant bit position after shifting.

We will utilize Verilog's reg and wire data types to represent the LFSR's internal state and connections. The module will be defined with the specified input and output ports. The feedback calculation and shifting logic will be implemented within an always block, which will be sensitive to the clock and reset signals.
