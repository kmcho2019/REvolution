To implement the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state. The feedback mechanism will be implemented by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to generate the new least significant bit (LSB). On the rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal. If it is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, we will shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the LSB. This process will generate a sequence of bits that appears random.
