To design the LFSR module, we will first define the module's ports according to the given specifications. Then, we will implement the reset logic to initialize the register to zero when the reset signal is high. Next, we will implement the logic for shifting the bits to the left and generating the feedback signal based on the XOR of the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, inverting the result to produce the new least significant bit. This logic will be executed on the rising edge of the clock signal.
