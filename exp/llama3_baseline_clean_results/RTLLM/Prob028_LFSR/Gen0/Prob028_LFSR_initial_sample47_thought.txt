To design the LFSR module, we first need to define the 4-bit register and the feedback mechanism. The feedback is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, we check if the reset signal is high. If it is, we reset the register to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB). This operation will produce a sequence of bits that appears random.
