To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to implement a digital circuit that shifts bits to the left on each clock cycle and inserts a new bit at the least significant bit (LSB) position based on the feedback mechanism. The feedback mechanism involves calculating the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), inverting the result, and using it as the new bit to be inserted. We also need to handle the reset condition where the LFSR is initialized to zero when the reset signal (rst) is high.
