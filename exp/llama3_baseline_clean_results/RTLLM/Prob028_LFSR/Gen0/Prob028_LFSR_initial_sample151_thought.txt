To implement the LFSR, we first need to define the module with its input and output ports. The LFSR will have an internal register to hold its current state. On every rising edge of the clock, we check if the reset signal is high. If it is, we reset the register to zero. If not, we calculate the feedback by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, inverting the result, and then shifting the bits in the register to the left while inserting the feedback at the least significant bit (LSB).
