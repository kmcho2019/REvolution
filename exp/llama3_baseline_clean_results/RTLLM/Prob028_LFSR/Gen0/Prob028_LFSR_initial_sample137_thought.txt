The LFSR design will utilize a 4-bit register to store the current state. The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. This inverted result will be used as the new least significant bit (LSB) during the shifting operation. The shifting operation occurs on the rising edge of the clock signal, and the register is reset to zero when the reset signal is high. This implementation will utilize Verilog's synchronous reset and assignment statements to model the behavior.
