To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will utilize the specified feedback mechanism. The register's bits will be shifted left on the rising edge of the clock, and the feedback value, calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result, will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB). An active high reset signal will initialize the register to zero when asserted. We will implement this logic using a combination of always blocks for sequential logic, handling the reset and clock edge conditions.
