To implement the LFSR module, we'll start by defining the input and output ports. The module will have a clock signal (clk) and an active high reset signal (rst) as inputs, and a 4-bit output (out) representing the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism will be implemented using a combination of bitwise operations, specifically XOR and inversion, on the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]) of the register. The new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) after shifting the bits in the register left on the rising edge of the clock.
