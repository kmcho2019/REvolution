To design the LFSR module, we'll use a 4-bit register to store the current state. The feedback mechanism will be implemented by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the bits in the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
