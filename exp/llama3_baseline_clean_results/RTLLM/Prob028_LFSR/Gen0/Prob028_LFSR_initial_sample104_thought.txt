To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a register to store the current state and a combinational logic to calculate the feedback signal. The LFSR's operation will be synchronized by the clock signal (clk) and reset by the active high reset signal (rst). The feedback mechanism involves XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), inverting the result, and using this value as the new least significant bit after shifting the register's contents to the left. This process generates a pseudo-random sequence of bits.
