To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we first need to understand the shifting and feedback mechanism. The LFSR's operation involves shifting the bits to the left and inserting a new bit at the least significant position based on the feedback. The feedback is generated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. We will use a D flip-flop for each bit of the register to store the state. The reset signal will be used to initialize all the flip-flops to zero when it is high. Otherwise, on each rising edge of the clock, the flip-flops will be updated according to the shifting and feedback rules.
