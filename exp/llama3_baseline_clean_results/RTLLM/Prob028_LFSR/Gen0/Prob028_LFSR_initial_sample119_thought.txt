To implement the LFSR module, we can use a 4-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism is based on the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), followed by inversion. We will use a always_ff block to capture the rising edge of the clock and apply the necessary operations (reset or shift) based on the state of the reset signal. The output port out[3:0] will be continuously assigned the value of the internal register.
