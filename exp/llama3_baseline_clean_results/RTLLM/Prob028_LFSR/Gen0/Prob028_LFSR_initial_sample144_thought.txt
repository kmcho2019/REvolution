To implement the LFSR module, we first need to define the feedback mechanism based on the given XOR operation between the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit (second MSB) of the register. Then, we'll use a always block to describe the behavior of the register on the rising edge of the clock signal. If the reset signal is high, the register should be reset to zero; otherwise, the bits in the register are shifted left, and the new feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit. The feedback value is calculated by inverting the result of the XOR operation between the MSB and the second MSB.
