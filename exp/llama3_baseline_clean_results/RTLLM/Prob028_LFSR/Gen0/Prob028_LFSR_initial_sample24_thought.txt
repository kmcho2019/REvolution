To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we'll use the principle of shifting bits and applying feedback based on the XOR of specific bits in the register. We will initialize the register with zeros when the active high reset signal is asserted. Otherwise, on the rising edge of the clock, the bits in the register are shifted left, and the new feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB). The feedback is calculated as the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), and then the result is inverted.
