To design the LFSR module, we will first define the internal state of the register and how it is updated based on the clock and reset signals. The feedback mechanism will be implemented using the XOR operation between the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, followed by an inversion. On each rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal; if it's high, the register will be reset to zero. If the reset signal is low, the register will shift left, and the feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit.
