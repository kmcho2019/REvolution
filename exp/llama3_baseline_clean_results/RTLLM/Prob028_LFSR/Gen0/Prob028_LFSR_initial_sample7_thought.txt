To implement the LFSR module, we first need to understand the basic operations involved: shifting bits, calculating feedback, and resetting the register. The key concept here is that the feedback is calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second MSB, then inverting the result. This feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) position when the bits are shifted left on the rising edge of the clock, provided the reset signal is not active high. The design will use a reg type to hold the current state of the LFSR and always blocks to handle the synchronous reset and the shift operation based on the clock edge.
