To implement the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism will be implemented using the XOR operator to calculate the new least significant bit (LSB) based on the most significant bit (MSB) and the second MSB. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to zero. Otherwise, the register will be shifted left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the LSB.
