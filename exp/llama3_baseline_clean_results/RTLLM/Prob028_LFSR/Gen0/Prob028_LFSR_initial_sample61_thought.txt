To design the 4-bit LFSR, we first identify the key components and operations involved: a 4-bit shift register, a feedback mechanism based on the XOR of the most significant and second most significant bits, and an active high reset. The module will have a clock input (clk) for synchronizing the shift operation and a reset input (rst) to initialize the register to zero. The output will be a 4-bit vector (out) representing the current state of the LFSR. On each rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register resets to zero; otherwise, it shifts left, inserting the calculated feedback at the least significant bit.
