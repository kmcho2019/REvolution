To implement the 4-bit LFSR, we will utilize the concept of shifting bits and applying feedback based on the XOR of specific bits in the register. The feedback mechanism will involve XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result to produce the new bit that will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB) after shifting. This operation will be synchronized by the clock signal and reset by the active high reset signal. We will use Verilog's non-blocking assignment to capture the new state of the register on the rising edge of the clock, ensuring proper synchronization and avoiding race conditions.
