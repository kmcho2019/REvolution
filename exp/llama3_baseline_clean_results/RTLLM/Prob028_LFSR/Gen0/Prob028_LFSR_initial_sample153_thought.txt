To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to follow these steps:
1. Initialize the output register with a reset value when the reset signal (rst) is high.
2. Calculate the feedback signal by XORing the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), then invert the result.
3. On the rising edge of the clock (clk), if the reset signal (rst) is low, shift the bits in the register one position to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
We will use a combination of always block, conditional statements, and bitwise operations to achieve this functionality in Verilog.
