To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we'll first identify the feedback mechanism which involves XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The register's operation will be controlled by a clock signal (clk) and an active high reset signal (rst). On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset is high, the register will be reset to zero. Otherwise, the bits will be shifted left, and the calculated feedback will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB). This will generate a pseudo-random sequence based on the LFSR's initial state and the feedback mechanism.
