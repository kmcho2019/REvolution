To design the LFSR module, we will use a 4-bit register to hold the current state. The feedback mechanism will be implemented using a combination of XOR and inversion operations on the most significant bit (MSB) and the second MSB. On each rising edge of the clock, we will check the reset signal. If it's high, we reset the register to zero. Otherwise, we shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB) position.
