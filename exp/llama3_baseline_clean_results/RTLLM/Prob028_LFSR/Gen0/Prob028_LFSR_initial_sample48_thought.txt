To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to understand the basic principle of how LFSRs work. They shift bits and apply feedback based on the XOR of specific bits. In this case, the feedback is the result of XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting it. We'll use a Verilog module named 'LFSR' with 'clk' and 'rst' as inputs and a 4-bit 'out' as the output. The internal operation involves synchronous reset and shifting of bits with the new feedback value inserted at the least significant bit position on every rising edge of the clock, unless a reset occurs.
