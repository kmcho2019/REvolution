To design the LFSR module, we will first declare the input and output ports, and then define the internal signals and variables. The feedback signal will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit of the current state, and then inverting the result. We will use a reg type variable to hold the current state of the LFSR. On the rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, we will initialize the LFSR to zero. Otherwise, we will shift the bits in the LFSR to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit.
