To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to consider the feedback mechanism, the shifting operation, and the reset functionality. The LFSR's feedback is based on the XOR of the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, and this result is inverted. We will use a 4-bit register to hold the current state of the LFSR. On each rising edge of the clock, if the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to zero. Otherwise, the bits will shift left, and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
