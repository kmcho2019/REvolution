To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we need to follow the specified operation:
1. Initialize the LFSR to zero when the reset signal (rst) is high.
2. Calculate the feedback by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result.
3. On the rising edge of the clock signal (clk), if the reset signal is not high, shift the bits in the register to the left and insert the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB).
This process will generate a pseudo-random sequence of bits from the LFSR.
