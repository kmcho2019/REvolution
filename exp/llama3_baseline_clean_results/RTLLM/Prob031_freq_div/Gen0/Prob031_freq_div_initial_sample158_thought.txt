To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use a combination of counters and conditional statements to generate the desired output clock frequencies. The module will have three counters: one for each output clock frequency. On every positive edge of the input clock or reset signal, the counters will be updated based on their current values and the reset signal. If the reset signal is active, all counters and output clocks will be reset. Otherwise, each counter will be incremented, and when it reaches its target value, the corresponding output clock will be toggled, and the counter will be reset.
