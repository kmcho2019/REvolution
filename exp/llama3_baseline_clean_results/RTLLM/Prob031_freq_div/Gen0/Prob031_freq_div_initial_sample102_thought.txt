To design the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. We'll use two counters, cnt_10 and cnt_100, to generate the 10MHz and 1MHz clock signals respectively. The 50MHz clock signal can be generated by simply toggling the output on every positive edge of the input clock. We will also use the RST signal to reset the counters and output signals as required.
