We will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, we will simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. 
For CLK_10, we will use a counter (cnt_10) that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in and toggles CLK_10 when it reaches a value of 4, then resets the counter. 
For CLK_1, we will use another counter (cnt_100) that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in and toggles CLK_1 when it reaches a value of 49, then resets the counter.
