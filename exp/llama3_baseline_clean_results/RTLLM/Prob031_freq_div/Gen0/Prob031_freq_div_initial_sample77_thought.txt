To implement the frequency divider module named freq_div, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. The module will have input ports for the input clock signal (CLK_in) and reset signal (RST), and output ports for the divided clock signals (CLK_50, CLK_10, CLK_1). 
For CLK_50 generation, it will be toggled on every positive edge of CLK_in, effectively dividing the input frequency by 2.
For CLK_10 and CLK_1 generation, counters (cnt_10 and cnt_100) will be used. cnt_10 will be incremented on every positive edge of CLK_in, and when it reaches 4 (for 10MHz), CLK_10 will be toggled and cnt_10 reset to 0. Similarly, cnt_100 will be incremented on every positive edge of CLK_in, and when it reaches 49 (for 1MHz), CLK_1 will be toggled and cnt_100 reset to 0. 
All counters and output clocks will be reset when the RST signal is active.
