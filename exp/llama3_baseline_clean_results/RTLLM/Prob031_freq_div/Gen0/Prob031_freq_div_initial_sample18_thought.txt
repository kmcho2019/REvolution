To implement the frequency divider module, we'll use three counters: one for each output clock frequency. The module will be sensitive to the positive edge of the input clock signal and the reset signal. On every positive edge, we'll check if the reset signal is active. If it's active, we'll reset the output clock signals and the counters. Otherwise, we'll update the output clock signals and the counters according to their respective frequencies. For CLK_50, we'll simply toggle its value on every positive edge. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we'll use counters to divide the input clock frequency by 10 and 100, respectively.
