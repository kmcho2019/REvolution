To implement the frequency divider, we will create a Verilog module named 'freq_div' that takes in two inputs, 'CLK_in' and 'RST', and generates three output clocks, 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1', with frequencies divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. We will use three counters, one for each output clock, to achieve this division. The counters will be incremented on every positive edge of 'CLK_in' and reset when 'RST' is active. When each counter reaches its respective threshold (1 for 'CLK_50', 4 for 'CLK_10', and 49 for 'CLK_1' for the first toggle after reset, then 50 for 'CLK_1' for subsequent toggles), the corresponding output clock will be toggled.
