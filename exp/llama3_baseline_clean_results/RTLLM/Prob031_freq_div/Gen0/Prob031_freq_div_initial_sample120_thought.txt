To implement the frequency divider module named "freq_div", we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. 
For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we will use counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 respectively. 
When these counters reach their threshold values (4 for cnt_10 and 49 for cnt_100), we toggle the corresponding clock signals and reset the counters.
The module will also be reset when RST is active.
We'll use always @(posedge CLK_in or posedge RST) to capture the positive edge of both CLK_in and RST.
