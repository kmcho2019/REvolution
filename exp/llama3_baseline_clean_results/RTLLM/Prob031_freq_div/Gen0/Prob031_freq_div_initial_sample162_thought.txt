To implement the frequency divider module, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
We will use a simple toggle mechanism for the CLK_50 generation, where the signal is inverted on every positive edge of the input clock.
For the CLK_10 and CLK_1 generation, we will use counters that reset when they reach a certain value, and the corresponding clock signal is toggled at that point.
The counters will be reset to 0 when the reset signal RST is active.
We will use a Verilog always block to implement the counters and the clock signal toggling logic.
