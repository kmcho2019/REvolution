The frequency divider module, freq_div, will utilize three separate counters to generate the desired output clock frequencies. For CLK_50, a simple toggle counter will be used to divide the input clock frequency by 2. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters will be used to count up to a specific value (4 for CLK_10 and 49 for CLK_1) before toggling the output clock signal and resetting the counter. The module will also include reset logic to reset the counters and output clock signals when the RST signal is active.
