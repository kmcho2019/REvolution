To implement the frequency divider module `freq_div`, we will utilize three counters to divide the input clock frequency into the desired output frequencies: 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz. The module will have two input ports: `CLK_in` for the input clock signal and `RST` for the reset signal. It will have three output ports: `CLK_50`, `CLK_10`, and `CLK_1`, corresponding to the divided clock frequencies. We will use the counters `cnt_10` and `cnt_100` to keep track of the clock cycles for generating `CLK_10` and `CLK_1`, respectively. On every positive edge of `CLK_in` or when `RST` is active, the corresponding counters will be reset or the output clocks will be toggled according to the specified conditions.
