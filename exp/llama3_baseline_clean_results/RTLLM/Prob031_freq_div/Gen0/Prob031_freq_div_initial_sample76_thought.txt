To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to create a Verilog module that takes in the input clock signal 'CLK_in' and the reset signal 'RST', and outputs the three divided clock frequencies 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1'. 
The module uses three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For 'CLK_50' generation, it is simply a matter of toggling the output on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. 
For 'CLK_10' generation, we use a counter 'cnt_10' that increments on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. When 'cnt_10' reaches 9 (since we want to divide by 10 and it is 0-indexed, so 10 - 1 = 9), 'CLK_10' is toggled. 
For 'CLK_1' generation, we use a counter 'cnt_100' that increments on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. When 'cnt_100' reaches 99 (since we want to divide by 100 and it is 0-indexed, so 100 - 1 = 99), 'CLK_1' is toggled.
However, according to the implementation description, the counters are compared to 4 and 49 respectively. Hence, we should use these values instead.
The counters and 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1' are all reset when 'RST' is active.
