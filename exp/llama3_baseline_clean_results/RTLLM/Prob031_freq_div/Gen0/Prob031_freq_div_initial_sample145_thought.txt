To implement the frequency divider module, we will use three counters to generate the output clock frequencies. The CLK_50 will be generated by simply toggling its value on every positive edge of the input clock. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters will be used to achieve the desired frequency division. The counters will be reset to zero when the reset signal is active. The output clocks will be toggled when the respective counters reach their target values, and the counters will be reset to zero after each toggle.
