The problem requires designing a frequency divider module 'freq_div' that takes an input clock signal 'CLK_in' and a reset signal 'RST', and generates three output clock signals 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1' with frequencies divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. The module will use three counters to achieve this division. For 'CLK_50', it will simply toggle on every positive edge of 'CLK_in' or 'RST'. For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', it will use counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' to count the number of 'CLK_in' cycles before toggling the output signals.
