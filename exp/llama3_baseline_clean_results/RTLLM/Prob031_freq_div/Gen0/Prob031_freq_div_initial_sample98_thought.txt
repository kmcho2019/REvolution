To implement the frequency divider module, we can use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. We'll use a reg type to store the output clock signals and counters. The counters will be reset on the positive edge of RST or CLK_in when RST is active. The output clock signals will be updated based on the counter values. For CLK_50, we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we check the counter values and update the output clock signals accordingly.
