To implement the frequency divider, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in, effectively dividing the frequency by 2. 
For CLK_10, we use a counter cnt_10 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_10 reaches 4 (i.e., 100MHz / 10 = 10MHz), we toggle CLK_10 and reset cnt_10 to 0. 
For CLK_1, we use a counter cnt_100 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_100 reaches 49 (i.e., 100MHz / 1 = 100, and 100 / 2 = 50, 50 * 2 - 1 = 99, which is equivalent to 100 - 1, and in verilog, we start counting from 0), we toggle CLK_1 and reset cnt_100 to 0.
We will also handle the reset condition where if RST is active, all output clocks and counters are reset to their initial values.
