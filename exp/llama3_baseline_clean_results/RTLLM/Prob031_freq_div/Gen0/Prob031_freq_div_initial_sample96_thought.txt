To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will utilize a combination of counters and conditional statements to generate the desired output clock frequencies. 
The module will take an input clock signal 'CLK_in' and a reset signal 'RST', and produce three output clock signals: 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1', with frequencies equal to 'CLK_in' divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. 
This will be achieved by employing separate counters for each output clock signal, and using these counters to determine when to toggle the output clock signals.
