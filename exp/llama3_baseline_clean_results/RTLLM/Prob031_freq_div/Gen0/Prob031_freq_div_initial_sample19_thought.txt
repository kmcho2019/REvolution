The frequency divider module, freq_div, will be designed using three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The module will have an input clock signal, CLK_in, and a reset signal, RST. 
Three output clock signals will be generated: CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1, with frequencies of CLK_in divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively.
The module will use a simple counter-based approach to achieve the desired frequency division.
For CLK_50 generation, the output will be toggled on every positive edge of CLK_in.
For CLK_10 generation, a counter, cnt_10, will be used to count up to 4 and then toggle the output.
For CLK_1 generation, a counter, cnt_100, will be used to count up to 49 and then toggle the output.
The reset signal, RST, will reset all counters and output clocks when active.
