To implement the frequency divider, we will design a module named 'freq_div' that takes in a 100MHz clock signal 'CLK_in' and a reset signal 'RST'. The module will generate three output clock signals: 'CLK_50' at 50MHz, 'CLK_10' at 10MHz, and 'CLK_1' at 1MHz. We will use counters to divide the input clock frequency. The 'CLK_50' will be generated by toggling its value on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', we will use counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' respectively to divide the frequency. When the counter reaches the desired value, the corresponding clock signal will be toggled and the counter will be reset.
