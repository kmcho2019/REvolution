To implement the frequency divider, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency and generate the required output frequencies. 
For CLK_50, we simply need to toggle the signal on every positive edge of CLK_in.
For CLK_10, we will use a counter cnt_10 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_10 reaches 4, we toggle CLK_10 and reset cnt_10 to 0.
For CLK_1, we will use a counter cnt_100 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_100 reaches 49, we toggle CLK_1 and reset cnt_100 to 0.
We will also use the RST signal to reset the counters and output clocks when it is active.
