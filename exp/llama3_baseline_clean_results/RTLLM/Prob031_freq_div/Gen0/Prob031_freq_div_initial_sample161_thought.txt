To implement the frequency divider module, we will utilize three counters to divide the input clock frequency. For the CLK_50 output, we will simply toggle its value on every positive edge of the input clock. For the CLK_10 output, we will increment a counter (cnt_10) until it reaches 9 (since we need to divide the frequency by 10, and 100/10 = 10, we toggle every 10 cycles), then toggle the CLK_10 value and reset the counter. Similarly, for the CLK_1 output, we will increment another counter (cnt_100) until it reaches 99 (since we need to divide the frequency by 100, and 100/1 = 100, we toggle every 100 cycles), then toggle the CLK_1 value and reset the counter. We also need to handle the reset condition, where all outputs and counters are reset when the RST signal is active.
