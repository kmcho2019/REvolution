The freq_div module is designed to take an input clock signal CLK_in and produce three output clock signals with frequencies that are divisions of the input frequency. 
The module uses three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, it is generated by toggling its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. 
For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 are used respectively to divide the frequency. 
When the counter reaches the desired value, the corresponding clock signal is toggled and the counter is reset to 0.
