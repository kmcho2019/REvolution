The frequency divider module freq_div will utilize three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The counters will be used to generate the desired output frequencies by toggling the output clock signals at specific intervals. 
The module will also incorporate a reset mechanism to ensure that the counters and output clock signals are properly initialized.
We will use a single always block to handle the generation of all output clock signals and the counters, with conditional statements to manage the reset and counting logic.
