To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
We will use a single process to handle all the counters and output signals, 
synchronized with the positive edge of the input clock signal 'CLK_in' or the reset signal 'RST'. 
For 'CLK_50', we will simply toggle its value at every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. 
For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', we will use counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' to count the number of 'CLK_in' cycles, 
and toggle 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1' when the respective counters reach their target values (4 and 49). 
When 'RST' is active, all output signals and counters will be reset to their initial states.
