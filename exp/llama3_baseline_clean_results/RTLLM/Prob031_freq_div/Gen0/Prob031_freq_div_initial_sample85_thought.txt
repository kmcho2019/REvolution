To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three separate counters to divide the input clock frequency of 100MHz to generate output clock frequencies of 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz. 
The module will have four ports: 'CLK_in' for the input clock signal, 'RST' for the reset signal, and 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', 'CLK_1' for the output clock signals.
