To implement the frequency divider, we will create a module named `freq_div` with the specified input and output ports. We will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. For CLK_50, we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we use counters cnt_10 and cnt_100, respectively, to divide the frequency. When the counter reaches the desired value, we toggle the corresponding clock signal and reset the counter. The RST signal will reset all clock signals and counters to their initial states.
