To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to design a digital circuit that takes the input clock signal 'CLK_in' and the reset signal 'RST' and generates three output clock signals: 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1'. The module will utilize three counters to divide the input clock frequency. The 'CLK_50' signal will be toggled on every positive edge of 'CLK_in', while 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1' will be toggled when their respective counters reach the desired values.
