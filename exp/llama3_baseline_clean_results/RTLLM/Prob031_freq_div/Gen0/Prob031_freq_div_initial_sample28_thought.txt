To implement the frequency divider, we can use a single always block to handle all the logic. 
We will use three counters (cnt_2, cnt_10, cnt_100) to divide the input clock frequency. 
The counters will be reset when RST is active. 
When the counters reach their respective thresholds, the corresponding output clocks will be toggled.
We will use non-blocking assignments to update the counters and output clocks to avoid race conditions.
