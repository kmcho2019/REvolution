To design the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to create three separate counters to generate the desired output frequencies. The first counter will be used to generate the 50MHz clock by toggling its value on every positive edge of the input clock. The second counter will generate the 10MHz clock by counting up to 4 and then toggling its value. The third counter will generate the 1MHz clock by counting up to 49 and then toggling its value. We will also include a reset mechanism to set all output clocks to 0 and reset the counters when the reset signal 'RST' is active.
