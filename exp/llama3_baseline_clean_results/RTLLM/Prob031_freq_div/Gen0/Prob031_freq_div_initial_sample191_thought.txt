The problem requires designing a Verilog module named freq_div that takes an input clock signal (CLK_in) of 100MHz and generates three output clock signals: CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1. The frequencies of these output clocks are derived by dividing the input clock frequency by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. The module also includes a reset signal (RST) to reset the output clocks and their respective counters. The counters are used to achieve the desired frequency divisions. 
For CLK_50, a simple toggle on every positive edge of the input clock is sufficient due to its division by 2.
For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters are used to accumulate the input clock edges until the desired division factor is reached, then the output clock is toggled, and the counter is reset.
This approach ensures that the output clocks are synchronized with the input clock and are properly reset when the RST signal is active.
