To implement the frequency divider module, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The first counter is used to generate the 50MHz clock signal by toggling its value every clock cycle. 
The second counter is used to generate the 10MHz clock signal by toggling its value every 5 clock cycles (100MHz / 10MHz = 10, but since we are dividing by 2 to get 50MHz, we need to divide by 5 to get 10MHz from 50MHz, and further divide 100MHz by 2 and then by 5 to get 10MHz).
The third counter is used to generate the 1MHz clock signal by toggling its value every 50 clock cycles (100MHz / 1MHz = 100, but since we are dividing by 2 to get 50MHz, we need to divide by 50 to get 1MHz from 100MHz, and further divide 100MHz by 2 and then by 50 to get 1MHz).
We will use the always block to handle the reset and the edge-triggered behavior.
