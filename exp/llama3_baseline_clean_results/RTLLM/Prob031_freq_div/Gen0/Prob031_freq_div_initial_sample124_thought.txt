To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to create a Verilog design that takes into account the requirements for generating the three output clock frequencies (50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz) from the input 100MHz clock signal. The module will utilize counters to achieve the desired frequency divisions. For CLK_50, a simple toggle on every positive edge of CLK_in will suffice due to the divide-by-2 requirement. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters (cnt_10 and cnt_100) will be used to count the number of input clock cycles before toggling the respective output clocks, effectively dividing the input frequency by 10 and 100, respectively.
