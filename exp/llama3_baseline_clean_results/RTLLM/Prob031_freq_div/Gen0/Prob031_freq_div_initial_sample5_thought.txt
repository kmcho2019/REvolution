The frequency divider module freq_div can be designed using three counters: one for each output clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, a simple toggle operation can be used to divide the clock frequency by 2. 
For CLK_10 and CLK_1, counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 are used to divide the clock frequency by 10 and 100 respectively. 
The counters are reset when the RST signal is active. 
On every positive edge of CLK_in or RST, the counters are updated and the corresponding output clocks are toggled when the counter reaches the desired value.
