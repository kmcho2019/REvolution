The frequency divider module, freq_div, is designed to take an input clock signal CLK_in and produce three output clock signals, CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1, with frequencies that are 1/2, 1/10, and 1/100 of the input frequency, respectively. The module uses counters to achieve the desired frequency divisions and resets the counters and outputs when the reset signal RST is active. The division of the clock frequencies is achieved by using separate counters for each output clock signal.
