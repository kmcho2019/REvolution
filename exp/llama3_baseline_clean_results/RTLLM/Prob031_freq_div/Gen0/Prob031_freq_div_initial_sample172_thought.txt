The module freq_div will be designed using three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The input clock signal CLK_in is divided by 2, 10, and 100 to generate CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1 respectively.
Each output clock signal is generated by either toggling or inverting its current value based on the counter values and the reset signal RST.
The counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 are used to track the number of clock cycles for generating CLK_10 and CLK_1 respectively.
When the counters reach their respective threshold values (4 for cnt_10 and 49 for cnt_100), the corresponding output clock signals are toggled, and the counters are reset to 0.
The reset signal RST is used to reset the output clock signals and counters to their initial states.
