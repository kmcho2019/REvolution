To implement the frequency divider module, we will design a Verilog module named freq_div that takes an input clock signal (CLK_in) and a reset signal (RST) and generates three output clock signals (CLK_50, CLK_10, CLK_1) with frequencies divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. 
We will use three counters to keep track of the clock cycles for each output signal. 
The counter for CLK_50 will be toggled on every positive edge of the input clock. 
The counters for CLK_10 and CLK_1 will be incremented on every positive edge of the input clock and will toggle their respective output signals when they reach their target values (4 and 49 respectively).
The RST signal will be used to reset all output signals and counters to their initial states when it is active.
