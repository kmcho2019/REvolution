To implement the frequency divider module freq_div, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. For CLK_50, we will toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we will use counters cnt_10 and cnt_100, respectively, to count the number of CLK_in cycles. When the counters reach the desired values (4 for cnt_10 and 49 for cnt_100), we will toggle the corresponding clock signals. On reset, all clock signals and counters will be reset to their initial values.
