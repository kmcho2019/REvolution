To implement the frequency divider, we will use three separate counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The first counter will be used to generate the 50MHz clock by simply toggling its value on every positive edge of the input clock.
The second counter will be used to generate the 10MHz clock by counting up to 4 and then toggling the clock value.
The third counter will be used to generate the 1MHz clock by counting up to 49 and then toggling the clock value.
All counters will be reset when the reset signal is active.
