The frequency divider module, freq_div, will be implemented with three separate counters to generate the desired output frequencies. 
The module will have input ports for the input clock signal (CLK_in) and a reset signal (RST), and output ports for the divided clock signals (CLK_50, CLK_10, CLK_1).
The counters will be used to divide the input clock frequency by the desired ratios, and the output clock signals will be generated by toggling their values when the respective counters reach their target values.
The reset signal will be used to reset the counters and output clock signals to their initial values.
