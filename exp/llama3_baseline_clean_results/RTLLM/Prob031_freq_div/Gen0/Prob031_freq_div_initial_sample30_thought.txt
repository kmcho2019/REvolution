To implement the frequency divider module `freq_div`, we will use three counters to generate the desired output clock frequencies. The module will have two input ports: `CLK_in` for the input clock signal and `RST` for the reset signal. The module will have three output ports: `CLK_50`, `CLK_10`, and `CLK_1`, which will be generated by dividing the input clock frequency by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. We will use counters `cnt_10` and `cnt_100` to generate `CLK_10` and `CLK_1` respectively. `CLK_50` will be generated by simply inverting its current value on every positive edge of `CLK_in`. On the positive edge of `RST`, all counters and output clocks will be reset to 0.
