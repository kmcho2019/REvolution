To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to use three counters to generate the desired clock frequencies (50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz) from the 100MHz input clock signal. 
We will utilize a toggle strategy for CLK_50, and counter-based strategies for CLK_10 and CLK_1. 
In each case, the counters and the output clocks will be reset when the reset signal 'RST' is active.
