The frequency divider module, freq_div, will be designed with three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The counters will be implemented using always blocks that are sensitive to the positive edge of the input clock signal, CLK_in, and the reset signal, RST. 
For CLK_50 generation, a simple toggle on every positive edge of CLK_in will achieve the desired frequency division by 2. 
For CLK_10 and CLK_1 generation, counters will be used to keep track of the number of cycles and toggle the output clock signals when the desired division is reached.
