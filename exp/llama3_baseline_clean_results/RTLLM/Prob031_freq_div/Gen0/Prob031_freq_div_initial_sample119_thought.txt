The problem requires implementing a frequency divider module named `freq_div` that takes an input clock signal `CLK_in` of 100MHz and a reset signal `RST`, and generates three output clock signals: `CLK_50` at 50MHz, `CLK_10` at 10MHz, and `CLK_1` at 1MHz. The module uses three counters to achieve the frequency division. On every positive edge of `CLK_in` or `RST`, the counters are reset or incremented accordingly to generate the desired output clock frequencies.
