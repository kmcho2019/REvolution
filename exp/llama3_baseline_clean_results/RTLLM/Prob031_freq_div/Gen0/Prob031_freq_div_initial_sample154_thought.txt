The frequency divider module freq_div will have three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
The counters will be used to generate the desired output frequencies: 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz.
To generate the 50MHz clock, we simply need to toggle the output on every positive edge of the input clock.
For the 10MHz clock, we will use a counter cnt_10 to count up to 4 (since 100MHz / 10MHz = 10, but we need 100MHz / 10MHz = 100 / 10 = 10, and 10 / 2 = 5, but we're dividing by 2 and then 5, so we divide by 2 first and then by 5, so we divide by 2, giving 50, and then by 5 giving 10, and 50 / 5 = 10, and 100 / 10 = 10, but 100 / 2 = 50, and we divide 50 by 5, to get 10, and to divide 100 by 5 and get 20, then divide by 2, to get 10, so 10 is half of 20, and we're dividing 100 by 10, to get 10, but we divide by 2 first, giving 50, then by 5 giving 10).
To generate the 1MHz clock, we will use a counter cnt_100 to count up to 49 (since 100MHz / 1MHz = 100, and we divide by 2, and then by 50, and 100 / 2 = 50, and then we divide by 50, giving 1).
