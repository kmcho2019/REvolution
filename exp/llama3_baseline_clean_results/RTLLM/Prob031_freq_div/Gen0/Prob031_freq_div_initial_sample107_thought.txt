To implement the frequency divider, we'll use a single module named 'freq_div' with input ports 'CLK_in' and 'RST', and output ports 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1'. We will utilize three counters to generate the desired clock frequencies. 
For 'CLK_50', we will simply invert its value on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. 
For 'CLK_10', a counter 'cnt_10' will be used to count the number of 'CLK_in' cycles and toggle 'CLK_10' every 5 cycles. 
For 'CLK_1', a counter 'cnt_100' will be used to count the number of 'CLK_in' cycles and toggle 'CLK_1' every 50 cycles. 
The 'RST' signal will be used to reset all the counters and output signals to their initial values.
