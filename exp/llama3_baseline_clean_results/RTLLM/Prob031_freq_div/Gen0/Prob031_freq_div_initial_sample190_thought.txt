To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to design a Verilog module that takes an input clock signal 'CLK_in' and a reset signal 'RST', and generates three output clock signals 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1' with frequencies divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. We will use three counters to achieve this. The counters will be reset when the 'RST' signal is active. For 'CLK_50', we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', we use counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' to divide the frequency by 10 and 100 respectively. When the counters reach their target values (4 for 'cnt_10' and 49 for 'cnt_100'), we toggle the corresponding output clock signal and reset the counter.
