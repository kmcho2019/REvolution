To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, we will toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in, effectively dividing the frequency by 2. 
For CLK_10, we will use a counter 'cnt_10' that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in and toggles CLK_10 when 'cnt_10' reaches 4, effectively dividing the frequency by 10 (since 100MHz / 10 = 10MHz, and we are generating the clock by toggling, so 100 / (2*10) = 5, but we actually count to 4 to get 10MHz from 100MHz and toggle on the next edge, it's effectively divided by 10). 
For CLK_1, we will use a counter 'cnt_100' that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in and toggles CLK_1 when 'cnt_100' reaches 49, effectively dividing the frequency by 100 (since 100MHz / 100 = 1MHz, and we are generating the clock by toggling, so we count to 49 to get 1MHz from 100MHz and toggle on the next edge, it's effectively divided by 100).
We will also handle the reset condition to reset the counters and output clocks when RST is active.
