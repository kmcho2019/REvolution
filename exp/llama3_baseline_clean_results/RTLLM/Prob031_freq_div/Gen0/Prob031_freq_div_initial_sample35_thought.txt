To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. We will create separate counters for generating 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz clock frequencies from the input 100MHz clock signal. The counters will be reset and updated based on the positive edges of the input clock and reset signal. We will also implement the logic to toggle the output clock signals when the respective counters reach their threshold values.
