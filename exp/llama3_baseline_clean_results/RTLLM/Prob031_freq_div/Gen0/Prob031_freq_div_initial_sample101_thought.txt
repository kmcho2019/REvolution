The frequency divider module, freq_div, will be implemented using three counters to divide the input clock frequency. The input clock signal, CLK_in, is divided by 2, 10, and 100 to generate the output clock signals, CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1, respectively. On every positive edge of CLK_in or RST, the corresponding counter and output clock signal are updated according to the specified rules. The reset signal, RST, is used to reset the counters and output clock signals when it is active.
