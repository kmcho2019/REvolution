To implement the frequency divider, we will create a Verilog module named freq_div. This module will take in a 100MHz clock signal (CLK_in) and a reset signal (RST), and output three clock signals with frequencies of 50MHz (CLK_50), 10MHz (CLK_10), and 1MHz (CLK_1). The module will use counters to divide the input clock frequency. For CLK_50, it will toggle on every positive edge of CLK_in. For CLK_10, it will toggle every 10 cycles of CLK_in. For CLK_1, it will toggle every 100 cycles of CLK_in.
