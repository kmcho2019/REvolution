To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use a combination of counters and conditional logic to achieve the desired clock frequencies. For CLK_50, we can simply toggle the signal on every positive edge of the input clock or reset. For CLK_10 and CLK_1, we will use counters to divide the input clock frequency by 10 and 100, respectively. We will reset the counters and the corresponding clock signals when the reset signal 'RST' is active.
