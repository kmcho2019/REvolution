To implement the frequency divider module named 'freq_div', we need to design a Verilog module that takes an input clock signal 'CLK_in' and a reset signal 'RST', and generates three output clock signals 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1' with frequencies of 50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz respectively. 
The module will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For 'CLK_50', it will be toggled on every positive edge of 'CLK_in' unless 'RST' is active, in which case it will be reset to 0. 
For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' will be used to divide the frequency of 'CLK_in' by 10 and 100 respectively, and will be reset when 'RST' is active.
