To implement the frequency divider, we will use a Verilog module named 'freq_div' with input ports 'CLK_in' and 'RST', and output ports 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1'. 
We will utilize three counters, one for each output clock frequency. 
For 'CLK_50', we simply need to toggle its value on every positive edge of 'CLK_in'. 
For 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1', we use counters 'cnt_10' and 'cnt_100' respectively to divide the input clock frequency. 
When 'RST' is active, all output clocks and counters are reset to their initial values.
