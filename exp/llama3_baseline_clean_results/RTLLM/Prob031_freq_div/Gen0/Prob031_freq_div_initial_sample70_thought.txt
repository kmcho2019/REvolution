To implement the frequency divider module freq_div, we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. The counters are used to generate the output clock signals CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1 with frequencies of CLK_in divided by 2, 10, and 100 respectively. We will use always blocks in Verilog to define the behavior of the counters and the output clock signals. The counters cnt_10 and cnt_100 will be used to keep track of the number of cycles of the input clock signal CLK_in. When the counters reach the desired values (4 for cnt_10 and 49 for cnt_100, and 1 for CLK_50), the corresponding output clock signals will be toggled.
