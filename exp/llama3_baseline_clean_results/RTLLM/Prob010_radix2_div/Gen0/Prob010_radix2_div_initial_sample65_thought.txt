The radix-2 divider will be implemented as a sequential digital circuit using Verilog HDL. The design will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the division process, and will perform the division operation based on the inputs provided.

The division process involves shifting the dividend left by one bit, subtracting the divisor from the shifted dividend, and repeating this process until the counter reaches 8. The result will be updated at each step based on the subtraction result and the carry-out.

The design will also handle signed and unsigned division operations based on the 'sign' input. If 'sign' is high, the inputs will be treated as signed numbers; otherwise, they will be treated as unsigned numbers.

The 'res_valid' output will be managed based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed. When 'res_valid' is high, the result is valid and ready for consumption.

The module will be implemented using a combination of combinational and sequential logic, and will utilize registers to store the intermediate results.
