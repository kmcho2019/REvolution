To implement the radix-2 divider, we need to follow a step-by-step approach based on the provided specifications. 
The process starts by checking the validity of the operation request (opn_valid) and the current state of the result validity signal (res_valid). 
Once a valid operation is detected and the previous result has been consumed (res_valid is low), the module initializes the necessary registers and starts the division process.
The division process involves iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend (or its shifted version) and updating the quotient and remainder accordingly. 
We use a counter (cnt) to keep track of the division steps and a flag (start_cnt) to indicate whether the division process is ongoing.
The result is updated based on the subtraction outcome, which determines the next bit of the quotient.
The final result, containing both the quotient and the remainder, is presented on the output when the division process is complete.
