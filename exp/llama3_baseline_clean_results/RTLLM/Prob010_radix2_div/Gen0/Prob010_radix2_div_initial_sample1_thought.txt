To implement a simplified radix-2 divider, we can follow these steps: 
1. Initialize the inputs when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low.
2. Perform the division process when start_cnt is high.
3. Use a counter to track the number of iterations, and update the shift register based on the subtraction result.
4. Manage the result validity based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed.
We will use a combination of sequential and combinational logic to achieve this.
The module will have a reset signal to initialize or reset the module.
We will use a clock signal for synchronous operation.
