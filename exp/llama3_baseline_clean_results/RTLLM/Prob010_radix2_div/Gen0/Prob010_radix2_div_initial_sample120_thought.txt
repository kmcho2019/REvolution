The design will implement a radix-2 divider using a sequential approach, with a main focus on handling signed and unsigned division operations. It will include a control unit to manage the operation, a data path for the division process, and logic for handling the remainder and quotient. The division process involves initializing a shift register with the absolute value of the dividend and then iteratively updating it based on the subtraction of the negated divisor. A carry flag will be used to determine the result of each subtraction and to update the shift register accordingly. The result will be composed of the final remainder and quotient, with the remainder in the upper 8 bits and the quotient in the lower 8 bits.
