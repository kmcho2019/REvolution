The design will involve a control unit to manage the state of the divider and a datapath to perform the actual division. The control unit will handle the start of the operation, the counting of the division steps, and the generation of the valid result signal. The datapath will perform the subtraction, shifting, and updating of the shift register (SR) with the remainder and quotient. 
We will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two main states: IDLE and DIVIDE. 
The datapath will include a subtractor, a multiplexer, and a shifter.
To handle signed and unsigned division, we will determine the sign of the result based on the sign of the inputs and adjust accordingly.
