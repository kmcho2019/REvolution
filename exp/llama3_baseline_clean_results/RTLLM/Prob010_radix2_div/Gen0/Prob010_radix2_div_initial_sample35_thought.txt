To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we'll follow these steps:
1. Initialize the module: save the inputs, initialize the shift register SR, and set NEG_DIVISOR based on the inputs when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low.
2. Perform the division process: update the shift register SR, counter cnt, and start_cnt based on the subtraction result and carry-out until the counter cnt reaches 8.
3. Manage result validity: update res_valid based on the reset signal, counter, and whether the result has been consumed.
We'll use a finite state machine (FSM) to implement these steps and manage the module's states.
