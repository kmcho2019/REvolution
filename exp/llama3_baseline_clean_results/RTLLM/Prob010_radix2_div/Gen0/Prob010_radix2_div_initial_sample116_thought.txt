To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we will follow these steps:
1. Initialize the module with the inputs when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low.
2. Perform the division process by iterating through each bit of the dividend and updating the shift register SR based on the subtraction result.
3. Once the division is complete, update the result with the final remainder and quotient.
4. Manage the res_valid signal to indicate when the result is valid and ready for consumption.
We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the division process and manage the signals.
