The radix2_div module is designed as a synchronous radix-2 divider that can handle both signed and unsigned division operations. The division process starts when a valid operation is detected (opn_valid is high and res_valid is low), and it involves a series of shift and subtract operations on the dividend and divisor, respectively. The NEG_DIVISOR is used for the two's complement representation in case of signed division. The result is valid and ready when the counter cnt reaches 8, indicating the completion of the division process. 
We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the entire process, which includes the initialization, division, and result validation stages.
