To implement the radix-2 divider, we will use a combination of sequential logic to handle the division process, including initialization, division steps, and result validity management. 
The design involves a shift register (SR) to hold the dividend and quotient, a counter (cnt) to track the number of division steps, and control signals (start_cnt, res_valid) to manage the operation flow.
The division process will involve repeated subtraction and shifting, with the result being updated based on the carry-out of each subtraction operation.
We will also need to handle the signed and unsigned division based on the input 'sign' signal, which affects how the dividend and divisor are interpreted.
