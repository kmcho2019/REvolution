To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we will use a combination of sequential logic and combinatorial logic. The sequential logic will handle the synchronization of the inputs, the initialization of the registers, and the generation of the result validity signal. The combinatorial logic will handle the division process, including the subtraction, the update of the shift register, and the selection of the result based on the carry-out. The module will have three main states: 
- Idle: waiting for a valid operation request
- Division: performing the division
- Done: result is valid and ready
