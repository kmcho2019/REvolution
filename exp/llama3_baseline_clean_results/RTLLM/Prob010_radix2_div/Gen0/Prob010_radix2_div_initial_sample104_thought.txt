The radix-2 divider will be implemented as a finite state machine with two main states: idle and division. 
The division process involves shifting the dividend left, subtracting the divisor, and updating the remainder and quotient based on the result.
The module will use a counter to keep track of the number of iterations and a shift register to store the remainder and quotient.
The result will be valid when the division is complete and will be indicated by the res_valid signal.
