To implement a simplified radix-2 divider, we will create a Verilog module named radix2_div. This module will take in two 8-bit operands (dividend and divisor), a clock signal, a reset signal, a sign indicator, and an operation validity signal. It will then output a 16-bit result containing both the quotient and the remainder, along with a result validity signal. The design will support both signed and unsigned division operations. The division process will be performed using a series of subtractions, with the dividend being shifted left and the divisor being subtracted in each step. The result will be updated based on the carry-out of the subtraction operation. The process will continue until the counter reaches 8, indicating the completion of the division.
