To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we will use a synchronous design approach. The module will have two main states: idle and division. When the operation is valid and the result is not valid, the module will transition from idle to division state. In the division state, the module will perform the division process by shifting the dividend and checking for borrow. If the divisor is greater than the current dividend part, the quotient bit will be 0; otherwise, it will be 1. The remainder will be updated accordingly. The division process will continue until all bits of the dividend have been processed or the counter reaches 8. After completing the division, the result will be made valid, and the module will wait for the result to be consumed before returning to the idle state.

To handle signed and unsigned division, we will use the absolute values of the inputs and keep track of the signs separately. If the signs of the dividend and divisor are different, the result will be negated.

The design will include a reset signal to initialize the module and a clock signal for synchronous operation. The inputs will be saved when a valid operation is requested, and the result will be made valid when the division is complete.
