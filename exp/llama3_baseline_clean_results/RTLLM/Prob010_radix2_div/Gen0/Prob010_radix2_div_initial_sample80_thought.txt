To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we need to follow the steps outlined in the problem statement. The design will involve several key components: 
- A control unit to manage the operation flow based on input signals like `opn_valid` and `res_valid`.
- A shift register `SR` to hold the intermediate results during the division process.
- A counter `cnt` to track the number of division steps.
- Logic to compute the subtraction and update the shift register based on the carry-out.
- Management of the `res_valid` signal to indicate when the result is ready.

The Verilog implementation will involve the use of always blocks for the combinational and sequential logic, and the management of the control signals to ensure the correct flow of the division operation.
