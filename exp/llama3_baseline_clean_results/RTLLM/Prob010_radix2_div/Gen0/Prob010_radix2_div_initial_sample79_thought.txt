To implement a simplified radix-2 divider, we'll use a combination of sequential logic and arithmetic operations. The design will be divided into several stages: 
1. Initialization: When a new operation is requested (opn_valid is high), we save the inputs and initialize the necessary registers. 
2. Division Process: We use a counter to control the number of iterations. In each iteration, we perform a subtraction and update the shift register based on the carry-out. 
3. Result Validity: We manage the res_valid signal to indicate when the result is ready and valid.

We will utilize Verilog's arithmetic operators and bit manipulation capabilities to implement these stages. The design will be sensitive to the clk and rst signals to ensure proper synchronization and reset functionality.
