The implementation of the radix-2 divider in Verilog involves several key steps. First, the module needs to capture the input values when a valid operation is requested and the result is not yet valid. Then, it enters the division process, which involves iterative subtraction and shifting of the dividend. The division process continues until all 8 bits of the dividend have been processed, at which point the final quotient and remainder are computed. The quotient and remainder are then output on the result port, with the remainder in the upper 8 bits and the quotient in the lower 8 bits. The design also needs to handle signed and unsigned division based on the sign input. Additionally, the module must manage the validity of the result to ensure that it is only considered valid when the division operation is complete and the result has not been consumed.
