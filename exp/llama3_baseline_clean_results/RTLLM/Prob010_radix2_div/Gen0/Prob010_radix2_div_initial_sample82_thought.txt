To implement a simplified radix-2 divider for 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, we need to follow these key steps:
1. Check the validity of the operation and the reset signal to determine when to start a new operation or reset the module.
2. Upon receiving a valid operation request, save the inputs (dividend and divisor) and initialize necessary registers for the division process.
3. Perform the radix-2 division by iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend (or its shifted versions), keeping track of the quotient and remainder.
4. Manage the result validity based on the operation's completion and consumption of the result.
We will use a finite state machine approach to handle the different states of the division process, including initialization, division, and result output.
