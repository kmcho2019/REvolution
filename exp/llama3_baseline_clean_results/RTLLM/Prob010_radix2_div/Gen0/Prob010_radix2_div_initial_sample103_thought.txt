To implement the radix-2 divider, we first initialize the shift register SR with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left by one bit, and set NEG_DIVISOR to the negated absolute value of the divisor. Then, we enter a division loop where we repeatedly subtract NEG_DIVISOR from SR and update SR based on the result. The loop continues until the counter reaches 8. After each subtraction, we shift SR left and insert the borrow bit. Once the loop completes, we update the result with the final remainder and quotient. We manage the res_valid signal based on the reset, counter, and result consumption.
