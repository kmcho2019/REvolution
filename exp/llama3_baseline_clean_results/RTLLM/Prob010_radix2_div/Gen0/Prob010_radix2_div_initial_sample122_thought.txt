To implement the radix-2 divider, we can utilize a combination of sequential logic and combinational logic. We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different states of the division process, including initialization, division, and result output. The division process itself will be performed using a series of subtractions and shifts. We will use a multiplexer to determine the result of each subtraction step based on the carry-out.
