The radix2_div module is designed to perform division operations on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers using the radix-2 division algorithm. This algorithm iteratively subtracts the divisor from the dividend, shifting the dividend left and inserting the borrow or carry-out into the least significant bit. The module maintains a shift register (SR) for the dividend, a counter for tracking the number of iterations, and a flag (start_cnt) to indicate the start of the division process. It handles both signed and unsigned division by taking the absolute value of the inputs and adjusting the result accordingly. The result is composed of the quotient in the lower 8 bits and the remainder in the upper 8 bits.
