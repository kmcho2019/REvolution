To implement a simplified radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, we will design a Verilog module named radix2_div. This module will perform the division operation based on the radix-2 division algorithm, which iteratively subtracts the divisor from the dividend and shifts the remainder to the left to generate the quotient. The division process is controlled by a counter, and the result is made valid after the division is complete. We will handle both signed and unsigned division operations by considering the sign bit and adjusting the dividend and divisor accordingly. We will also implement the necessary logic to manage the input and output signals, such as opn_valid, res_valid, dividend, divisor, and result.
