To implement a simplified radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, we'll follow these steps: 
1. Save the inputs when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low.
2. Initialize the shift register SR with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left by one bit.
3. Set NEG_DIVISOR to the negated absolute value of the divisor.
4. Set the counter cnt to 1 and start_cnt to 1 to begin the division process.
5. Perform the division process by incrementing the counter cnt, computing the subtraction of NEG_DIVISOR, and updating the shift register SR based on the subtraction result.
6. Manage res_valid based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed.
7. Once the division is complete, clear cnt and start_cnt, and update the shift register SR with the final remainder and quotient.
