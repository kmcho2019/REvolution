The implementation involves a radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers. The design uses a synchronous approach with a clock signal and reset. The key steps include initializing the shift register with the dividend, performing the division based on the subtraction result, and managing the result validity.

The division process starts when a valid operation request is present, and the result is not ready. The module then performs the division by iteratively subtracting the negated divisor from the shift register and updating the shift register based on the carry-out.

The result is updated when the division is complete, and the validity of the result is managed based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed.

The design will utilize Verilog's always block for the combinational and sequential logic, and the case statement for the multiplexer.
