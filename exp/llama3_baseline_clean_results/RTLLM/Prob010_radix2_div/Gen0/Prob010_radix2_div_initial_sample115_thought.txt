The design of the radix-2 divider module, radix2_div, involves managing the division process through a state machine that handles the initialization, the iterative division steps, and the result output. The module will utilize registers to hold the dividend, divisor, and the intermediate results. The division process involves iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend and updating the quotient and remainder based on the result. We will use a counter to keep track of the division steps. The sign of the operation (signed or unsigned) will be considered during the initialization and final result phases.
