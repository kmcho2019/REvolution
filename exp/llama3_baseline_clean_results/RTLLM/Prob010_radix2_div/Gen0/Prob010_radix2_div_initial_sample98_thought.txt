To implement the simplified radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, we will design a module named 'radix2_div' that takes in the necessary inputs (clk, rst, sign, dividend, divisor, opn_valid) and produces the required outputs (res_valid, result). We will use a combination of registers, counters, and multiplexers to manage the division process, including the handling of signed and unsigned operations. The division process involves a series of subtractions and shifts, managed by a counter, to produce the quotient and remainder. The result validity signal 'res_valid' is managed to indicate when the result is ready and has not been consumed.
