The radix2_div module will perform a simplified radix-2 division on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers. The design utilizes a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the division process. When the operation is valid, the module saves the inputs and initializes the shift register SR with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left by one bit. It then performs the division by iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend, shifting the result, and updating the remainder and quotient accordingly. The division process is controlled by a counter cnt and the start_cnt signal. Once the division is complete, the result is made available and res_valid is set high.
