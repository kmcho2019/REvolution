To implement a simplified radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, the design involves several key steps: 
- It starts by saving the inputs (dividend and divisor) when a valid operation request is detected (opn_valid is high and res_valid is low).
- It initializes a shift register (SR) with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left by one bit and sets a flag (NEG_DIVISOR) to the negated absolute value of the divisor.
- The division process involves iteratively shifting and subtracting the divisor from the dividend using a counter (cnt) to track the number of iterations.
- The result, containing the remainder in the upper 8 bits and the quotient in the lower 8 bits, is updated based on the subtraction result and the carry-out.
- Finally, the result validity (res_valid) is managed based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed.
The Verilog implementation will use a combination of combinatorial and sequential logic to achieve these steps, including the use of registers, counters, and multiplexers.
