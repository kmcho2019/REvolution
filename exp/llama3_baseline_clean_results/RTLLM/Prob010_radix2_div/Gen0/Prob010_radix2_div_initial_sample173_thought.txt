To implement the radix-2 divider, we will create a finite state machine (FSM) with two main states: idle and division. In the idle state, the module waits for a valid operation request. Once a request is received, the module transitions to the division state, where it performs the radix-2 division algorithm. The algorithm involves repeatedly subtracting the divisor from the dividend and shifting the result left. The quotient and remainder are updated accordingly. The division process is controlled by a counter, and the result is valid when the counter reaches its maximum value.
