To implement the radix-2 divider, we first need to capture the inputs when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low. Then we perform the division process by iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend and updating the shift register SR with the remainder and quotient. The process is controlled by the counter cnt, which is incremented until it reaches 8 (the number of bits in the dividend). We also need to handle the signed and unsigned operations based on the sign input.
