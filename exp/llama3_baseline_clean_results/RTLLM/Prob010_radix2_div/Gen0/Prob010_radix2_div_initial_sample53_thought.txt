The design of the radix-2 divider module, named radix2_div, involves several key steps. It first checks for valid operation requests and resets the module when required. Once an operation is initiated, it saves the inputs and initializes the necessary registers for the division process. The division process itself involves iterative subtraction and shifting, with the quotient and remainder being updated based on the carry-out of the subtraction. The module handles both signed and unsigned division by considering the sign bit and adjusting the divisor and dividend accordingly. The result is made valid once the division is complete, and it remains valid until the result is consumed or the module is reset.
