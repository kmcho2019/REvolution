To design the radix-2 divider module, I will follow the given specification. The module will have two main states: idle and division. In the idle state, it waits for a valid operation request (opn_valid high and res_valid low). Once the inputs are saved, it transitions to the division state, where it performs the radix-2 division algorithm. The division process involves shifting the dividend left, subtracting the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder based on the carry-out. The process repeats until the counter reaches 8. After completion, the result is updated, and res_valid is set high to indicate the result is ready. The module also handles reset and result consumption to manage res_valid correctly.
