To implement the radix-2 divider, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that handles the division process in multiple clock cycles. The FSM will have states for initialization, division, and result preparation. We will use a shift register to store the dividend and divisor values, and a counter to keep track of the division steps. The NEG_DIVISOR and SR will be updated based on the subtraction result and the carry-out. The res_valid signal will be managed to indicate when the result is ready and valid.
