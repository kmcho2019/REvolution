To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we need to manage the division process through a finite state machine (FSM) that handles the initialization, division steps, and result validation. We'll use the opn_valid and res_valid signals to control the operation and output validation. The design will handle both signed and unsigned division by appropriately managing the signs of the dividend and divisor.

Key steps include:
1. Saving the inputs and initializing the shift register and counter when a valid operation is requested.
2. Performing the division process by iteratively subtracting the divisor from the dividend and updating the quotient and remainder.
3. Managing the result validity based on the reset signal and the completion of the division process.
