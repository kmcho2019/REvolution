To implement the simplified radix-2 divider in Verilog, we will follow these steps:
1. Define the inputs and outputs of the module, including the clock signal, reset signal, sign indicator, dividend, divisor, operation validity, result validity, and result.
2. Initialize the shift register SR with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left by one bit when opn_valid is high and res_valid is low.
3. Perform the division process by iterating through each bit of the dividend, updating the shift register SR, and computing the subtraction of NEG_DIVISOR.
4. Use a multiplexer to select the appropriate result based on the carry-out of the subtraction.
5. Update the result validity signal res_valid based on the reset signal, the counter, and whether the result has been consumed.
6. Handle the case when the division is complete (i.e., when the counter cnt reaches 8) by clearing the counter and start_cnt, and updating the result.
