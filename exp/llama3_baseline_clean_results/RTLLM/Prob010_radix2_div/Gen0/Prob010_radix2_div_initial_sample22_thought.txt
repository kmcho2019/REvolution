To implement the radix-2 divider, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) that handles the operation start, division process, and result validity. The module will have several states to manage the division process: idle, initial, and division states. When opn_valid is high and res_valid is low, the module will transition from the idle state to the initial state to save the inputs and initialize the shift register SR and counter cnt. Then, it will transition to the division state, where it will perform the division process based on the subtraction result and the carry-out. After completing the division process, it will transition back to the idle state and set res_valid high to indicate that the result is valid and ready.
