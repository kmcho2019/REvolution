When the operation is valid and the result is not yet ready, the module saves the inputs, initializes the shift register with the absolute value of the dividend shifted left, and sets the negated absolute value of the divisor. The division process then starts by subtracting the negated divisor from the shift register, updating the shift register based on the carry-out, and incrementing the counter until all 8 bits have been processed.

To handle signed division, we need to consider the sign of the inputs and adjust the result accordingly.
