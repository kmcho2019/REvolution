To implement the radix-2 divider, I will design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the division process. The FSM will handle the initialization of the shift register and the counter, the division steps, and the result validity. 

The module will be divided into several main components: 
- Input handling and initialization 
- Division process 
- Result calculation and validity management 

For the input handling, I will use the opn_valid signal to capture the inputs when they are valid and the reset signal to initialize the module. 

The division process involves shifting and subtracting the divisor from the dividend. I will use a counter to track the number of shifts performed.

The result will be calculated based on the final state of the shift register, which contains the quotient and remainder.
