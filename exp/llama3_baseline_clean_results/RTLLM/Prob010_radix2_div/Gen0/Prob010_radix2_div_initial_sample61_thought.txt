The design of the radix-2 divider can be achieved by breaking down the problem into several key components. Firstly, the module needs to accept and store the dividend and divisor when a valid operation is requested. It should handle both signed and unsigned division by considering the sign bit. The division process involves iterative subtraction and shifting, where the remainder and quotient are updated based on the subtraction result and carry-out. A counter tracks the number of iterations, and once the division is complete, the final result (quotient and remainder) is updated and made valid. The result validity is managed to ensure it is only considered valid when the division is complete and not consumed yet. This can be implemented using a finite state machine or by using control signals such as start_cnt and res_valid to orchestrate the division process.
