To implement the traffic light controller, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM will transition between these states based on the internal counter and the pass_request signal. The counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will be adjusted based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be updated based on the current state and the previous state.
