To implement the traffic light controller, we need to consider two main components: the state machine and the counter. The state machine will handle the different states of the traffic light (red, yellow, green), while the counter will be responsible for the timing of each state. We will use three always blocks: one for the state transition logic, one for the counting logic, and one for the output signals. The counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will determine the state of the traffic light. The pedestrian button press will affect the counter value when the green light is active.
