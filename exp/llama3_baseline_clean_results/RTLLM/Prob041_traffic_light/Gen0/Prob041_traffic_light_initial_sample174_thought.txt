To solve this problem, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the traffic light. The FSM has three main states: red, yellow, and green. The duration of each state is determined by the count value of an internal counter (cnt). When the pedestrian button is pressed, if the remaining green time is greater than 10 clocks, it is shortened to 10 clocks. The lane light and the sidewalk light are paired, with the sidewalk light being red when the lane light is green or yellow, and green when the lane light is red. We will use three always blocks to implement the state transition logic, the counting logic, and the output signals.
