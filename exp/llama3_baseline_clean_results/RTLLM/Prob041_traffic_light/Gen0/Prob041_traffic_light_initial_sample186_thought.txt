The traffic light controller can be designed using three always blocks: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signals. The first always block will use a case statement to handle different states (idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green) based on the current state and internal counter value. The second always block will handle the counting logic, adjusting the internal counter value based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light. The third always block will assign the previous values of the output signals to the current output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
