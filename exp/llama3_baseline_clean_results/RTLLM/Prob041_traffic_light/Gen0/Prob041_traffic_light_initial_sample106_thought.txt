To design the traffic light controller, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) approach with three states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block, and the counting logic of the internal counter will be handled in the second always block. The output signals will be updated in the third always block. We will use a case statement to handle different states and conditional statements to adjust the counter values based on various conditions. The traffic light controller will operate according to the specified timing requirements, and the pedestrian button press will be handled accordingly.
