To design the traffic light controller, we will follow a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions will be based on the count value of the internal counter and the pass_request signal. We will use two always blocks to handle the state transition logic and the counting logic of the internal counter. The output signals will be assigned based on the current state of the FSM.
