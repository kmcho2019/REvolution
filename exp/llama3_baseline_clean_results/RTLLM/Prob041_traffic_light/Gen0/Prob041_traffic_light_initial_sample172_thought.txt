The traffic light controller can be designed using a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM transitions between these states based on the internal counter and the pass_request signal. The counter is decremented on every clock cycle and reset to specific values based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are updated based on the current state and the previous values of the signals.
