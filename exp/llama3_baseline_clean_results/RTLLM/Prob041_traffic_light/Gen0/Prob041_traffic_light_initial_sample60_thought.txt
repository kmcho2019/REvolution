The traffic light system is designed as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The system starts in the idle state and immediately transitions to the s1_red state. The s1_red state sets the red signal to 1 and waits for a count of 10 before transitioning to the s3_green state. The s2_yellow state sets the yellow signal to 1 and waits for a count of 5 before transitioning to the s1_red state. The s3_green state sets the green signal to 1 and waits for a count of 60 before transitioning to the s2_yellow state. However, if the pedestrian button is pressed during the green state, the green time is shortened to 10 clocks if the remaining time is greater than 10 clocks. The lane light and the sidewalk light are paired, with the sidewalk light being red when the lane light is green or yellow, and green when the lane light is red. The internal counter (cnt) is used to keep track of the time in each state, and the output signals (red, yellow, green) are updated based on the current state and the counter value.
