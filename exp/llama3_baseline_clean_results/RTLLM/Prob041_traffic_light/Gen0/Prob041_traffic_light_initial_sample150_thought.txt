The design idea involves implementing a finite state machine (FSM) to control the traffic light. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions are based on the current state and the internal counter value. The internal counter (cnt) is used to time the duration of each state. The pass_request signal is used to adjust the green light duration when it is active. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are assigned based on the current state.
