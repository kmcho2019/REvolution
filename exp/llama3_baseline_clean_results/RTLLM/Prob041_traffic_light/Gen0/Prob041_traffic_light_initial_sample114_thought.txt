To design the traffic light controller, we'll follow a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The traffic light has three main states: red, yellow, and green. The pedestrian button press affects the duration of the green state. We'll use two always blocks to manage the state transitions and the internal counter. The first always block will handle the state logic, while the second will handle the counter logic. The output signals will be updated based on the current state and the counter value.
