To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we will use three always blocks and several conditional statements to manage the state transitions, counting logic, and output signals. The first always block will handle the state transition logic, the second always block will handle the counting logic, and the third always block will handle the output signals. We will use a case statement to manage different states and assign the next values for the red, yellow, and green signals. The counter values will be adjusted based on various conditions, and the output signals will be updated accordingly.
