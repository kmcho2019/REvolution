The traffic light controller will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM will transition between these states based on the internal counter and the pass_request signal. The internal counter will be used to implement the timing for each state, and the pass_request signal will be used to adjust the timing of the green state. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be updated based on the current state and the internal counter.
