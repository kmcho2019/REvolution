To design the traffic light controller, we need to create a finite state machine (FSM) that handles the different states of the traffic light. The states include idle, red, yellow, and green. The FSM will also handle the pedestrian button press by shortening the green time to 10 clocks if the remaining green time is greater than 10 clocks. We will use two always blocks, one for the state transition logic and another for the counting logic. The output signals will be assigned based on the current state of the FSM.
