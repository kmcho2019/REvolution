To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we can use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with different states representing the traffic light signals. The state transition logic will be implemented in the first always block. The second always block will handle the counting logic of the internal counter. Finally, the output signals will be handled in the third always block. We will also use assign statements to connect the internal counter to the output clock. Additionally, we will define parameters for the different states and initialize them accordingly.
