To implement the traffic light controller, we'll create a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block. The second always block will handle the counting logic of the internal counter, and the third always block will handle the output signals. The counter values will be adjusted based on various conditions, such as the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic lights.
