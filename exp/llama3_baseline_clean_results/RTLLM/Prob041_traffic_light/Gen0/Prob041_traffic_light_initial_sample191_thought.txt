To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we'll follow a finite state machine (FSM) approach with three states: idle, red, yellow, and green. The state transition logic will be handled in one always block, the counting logic in another, and the output signal logic in the third. We'll use a case statement to handle different states and conditional statements to handle the counting and output signal logic. The FSM will transition between states based on the internal counter and the pedestrian button press. The traffic light signals will be updated accordingly, and the internal counter will be adjusted based on the current state and the pedestrian button press.
