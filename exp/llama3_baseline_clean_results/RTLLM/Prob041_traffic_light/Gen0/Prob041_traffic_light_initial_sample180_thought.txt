To implement the traffic light controller, we need to consider the different states of the traffic light (red, yellow, green) and the pedestrian request signal. The controller should transition between these states based on the internal counter and the pedestrian request signal. We will use a 2-bit register to represent the current state of the traffic light controller and a case statement to handle the different states. The counting logic will be handled in a separate always block, where the internal counter is decremented or set based on various conditions. The output signals will be assigned the previous values on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
