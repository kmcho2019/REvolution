To implement the traffic light controller as per the given specifications, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) approach with three states: idle, red, yellow, and green. The traffic light controller transitions between these states based on the count value of the internal counter (cnt). The internal counter (cnt) is decremented by 1 on every positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The counter values are adjusted based on various conditions. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are updated based on the current state and the count value of the internal counter.
