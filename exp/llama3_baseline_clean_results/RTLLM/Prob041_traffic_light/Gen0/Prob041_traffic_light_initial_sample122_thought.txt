To implement the traffic light controller, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions will be controlled by a counter that decrements on every clock cycle. When the pedestrian button is pressed, the controller will check the remaining green time and adjust it accordingly. The lane light and the sidewalk light will be paired, with the sidewalk light being green when the lane light is red, and red when the lane light is green or yellow. We will use three always blocks to implement the state transition logic, the counting logic, and the output signal logic.
