To design the traffic light controller, we need to follow the specified state transition logic and counting logic. The first always block will handle the state transitions based on the current state and the count value. The second always block will handle the counting logic, including decrementing the counter and adjusting its value based on various conditions. The assign statement will assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock. The final always block will handle the output signals, assigning the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
