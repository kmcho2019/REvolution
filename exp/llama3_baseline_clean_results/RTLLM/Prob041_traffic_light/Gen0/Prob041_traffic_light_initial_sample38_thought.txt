The traffic light controller is designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic is implemented using a case statement in the first always block. The counting logic of the internal counter (cnt) is handled in the second always block, which decrements the counter on every positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The counter values are adjusted based on various conditions, such as the pass_request signal, green signal, yellow signal, and red signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are assigned the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The assign statement assigns the value of the internal counter (cnt) to the output clock.
