To implement the traffic light controller, we need to follow the given design track. We will first define the states and their transitions, then handle the counting logic, and finally assign the output signals. The traffic light controller has four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions are handled based on the internal counter (cnt) and the pass_request signal. The counting logic is responsible for decrementing the internal counter and adjusting its value based on various conditions. The output signals are assigned based on the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) and the current state.
