The traffic light controller can be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: idle, red, yellow, and green. The state transition logic is handled by a case statement in the first always block. The counting logic of the internal counter is handled by the second always block. The output signals are assigned the previous values on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal in the final always block. We use a 2-bit register to represent the current state and a 8-bit register as the internal counter. The pedestrian button is handled by checking the pass_request signal and adjusting the counter value accordingly.
