The given problem describes a traffic light system with red, yellow, and green indicators, along with a pedestrian button. Under normal circumstances, the traffic light follows a cycle of 60 clock cycles of green, 5 clock cycles of yellow, and 10 clock cycles of red. However, when the pedestrian button is pressed, the remaining green time is shortened to 10 clocks if it is greater than 10 clocks. Otherwise, the remaining green time remains unchanged. To implement this system, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. We will use two always blocks to handle the state transition logic and the counting logic of the internal counter. Additionally, we will use an assign statement to assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock and another always block to handle the output signals.
