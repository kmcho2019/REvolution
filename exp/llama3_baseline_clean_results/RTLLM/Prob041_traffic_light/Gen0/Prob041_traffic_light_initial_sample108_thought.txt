To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we will follow the recommended design track. The first always block will handle the state transition logic, the second always block will handle the counting logic of the internal counter, and the third always block will handle the output signals. We will use a case statement to handle different states in the first always block and adjust the counter values based on various conditions in the second always block. The assign statement will be used to assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock.
