To implement the traffic light controller, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the different states of the traffic light. The states include idle, red, yellow, and green. We will also use a counter to handle the timing for each state. The counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will determine the state transitions. The pedestrian button will be used to interrupt the green state and shorten its duration if necessary. The output signals for the traffic light will be updated based on the current state and the counter value.
