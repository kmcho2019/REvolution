The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block, where the controller transitions between these states based on the current state and the count value of the internal counter. The second always block will handle the counting logic, where the internal counter is decremented or reset based on various conditions. The assign statement will assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock. Finally, the third always block will handle the output signals, assigning the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
