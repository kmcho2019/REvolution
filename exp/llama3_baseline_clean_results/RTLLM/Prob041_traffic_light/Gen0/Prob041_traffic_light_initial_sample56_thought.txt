To implement the traffic light controller, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block, and the counting logic of the internal counter will be handled in the second always block. The output signals will be assigned in the third always block. We will also use a case statement to handle different states and adjust the counter values based on various conditions.
