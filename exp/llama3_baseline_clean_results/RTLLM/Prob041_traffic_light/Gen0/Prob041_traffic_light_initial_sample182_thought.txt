The traffic light controller is designed as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic is implemented in the first always block, which uses a case statement to handle different states. The counting logic of the internal counter is handled in the second always block, where the counter is decremented by 1 on every positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The counter values are adjusted based on various conditions, such as the pass_request signal and the previous values of the red, yellow, and green signals. The assign statement assigns the value of the internal counter to the output clock. The final always block handles the output signals, assigning the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
