The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in one always block, and the counting logic of the internal counter will be handled in another always block. The output signals will be assigned in a third always block. The pedestrian button press will be handled by adjusting the counter value when the green signal is active. The lane light and sidewalk light pairing will be implemented by setting the sidewalk light to red when the lane light is green or yellow, and setting it to green when the lane light is red.
