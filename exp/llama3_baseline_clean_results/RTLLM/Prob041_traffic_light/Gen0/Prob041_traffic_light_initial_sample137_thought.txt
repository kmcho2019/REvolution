To implement the traffic light controller, we will follow the recommended design track. The controller has four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The first always block will handle the state transition logic using a case statement. The second always block will handle the counting logic of the internal counter. The counter values will be adjusted based on various conditions, such as the pass_request signal, the green signal, and the previous values of the signals. The assign statement will assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock. The final always block will handle the output signals, assigning the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
