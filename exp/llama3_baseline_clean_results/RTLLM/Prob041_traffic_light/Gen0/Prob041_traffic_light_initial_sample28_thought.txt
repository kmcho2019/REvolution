To implement the traffic light controller, we need to define the state machine with different states for the traffic light, handle the internal counter for timing purposes, and manage the output signals based on the current state and counter value. The state machine will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The internal counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will be adjusted based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal, the current state, and the previous state. The output signals will be updated on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
