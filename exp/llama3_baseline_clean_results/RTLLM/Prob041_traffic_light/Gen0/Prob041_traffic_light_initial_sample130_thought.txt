The traffic light module should have three main states: red, yellow, and green. The module should also consider the pedestrian button press. When the pedestrian button is pressed, if the remaining green time is greater than 10 clocks, it should be shortened to 10 clocks. Otherwise, the remaining green time should remain unchanged. The traffic light module should also have a counter to keep track of the time spent in each state. The counter should be decremented on every positive edge of the clock. The output signals should be updated based on the current state and the counter value.
