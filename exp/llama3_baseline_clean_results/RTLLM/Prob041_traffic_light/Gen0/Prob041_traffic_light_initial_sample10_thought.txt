To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we'll use three always blocks: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signal handling. The state transition logic will be based on a case statement that handles different states (idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green) and their respective transitions. The counting logic will be responsible for adjusting the internal counter (cnt) based on various conditions, such as the reset signal, pass_request signal, and the current state of the traffic lights. The output signal handling block will assign the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to the output signals (red, yellow, green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
