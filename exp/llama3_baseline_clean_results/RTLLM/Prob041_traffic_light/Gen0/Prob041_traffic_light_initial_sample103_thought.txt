To implement the traffic light controller, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM will transition between these states based on the internal counter value and the pass_request signal. We will also use a separate always block to handle the counting logic of the internal counter and adjust its value based on various conditions. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be assigned based on the current state of the FSM. We will also include an assign statement to output the internal counter value.
