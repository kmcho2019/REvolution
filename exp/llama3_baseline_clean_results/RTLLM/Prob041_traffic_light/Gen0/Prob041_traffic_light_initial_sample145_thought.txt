To implement the traffic light controller, we need to define the states and transitions based on the given requirements. The controller will have four main states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The transition between these states will be based on the counter value and the pass_request signal. The counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will be adjusted based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be assigned based on the current state.
