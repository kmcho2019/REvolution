To design the traffic light controller, we will create a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions will be based on the internal counter (cnt) and the pass_request signal. We will use three always blocks to handle the state transition logic, counting logic, and output signal assignment. The assign statement will be used to assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock. We will also use parameters to represent the different states of the traffic light controller.
