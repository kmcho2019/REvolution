To design the traffic light controller, we need to follow the given specifications. The controller has four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions are based on the current state and the count value of the internal counter. The counter is decremented on every positive edge of the clock, and its value is adjusted based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are assigned the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) on the positive edge of the clock. The internal counter (cnt) is assigned to the output clock.
