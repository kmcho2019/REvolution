To implement the traffic light controller, we need to consider the different states of the traffic light and the counting logic of the internal counter. We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to handle the state transitions and the counting logic. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. Each state will have its own set of conditions for transitioning to the next state. The counting logic will be handled by a separate always block that decrements the internal counter based on various conditions. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be assigned the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
