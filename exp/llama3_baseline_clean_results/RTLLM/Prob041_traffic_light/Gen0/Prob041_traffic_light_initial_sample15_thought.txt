The traffic light controller should be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions between different states based on the internal counter value and the pass_request signal. The FSM should have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic should be handled in one always block, while the counting logic should be handled in another always block. The output signals should be assigned based on the previous values of the signals and the current state of the FSM.
