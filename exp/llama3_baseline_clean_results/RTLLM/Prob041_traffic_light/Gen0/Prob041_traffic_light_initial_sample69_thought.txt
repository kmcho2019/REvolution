The traffic light controller is designed as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic is implemented using a case statement. The counting logic of the internal counter is handled based on various conditions, including the pass_request signal, the current state of the traffic light, and the previous state. The output signals are updated on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The traffic light controller also considers the pedestrian request and adjusts the green time accordingly.
