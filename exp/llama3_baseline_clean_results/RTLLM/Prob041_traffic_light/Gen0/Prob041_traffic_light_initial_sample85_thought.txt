To implement the traffic light controller, we will follow the recommended design track. 
We will use three always blocks: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signal handling. 
The state transition logic will handle the different states of the traffic light controller based on the current state and the counter value. 
The counting logic will handle the internal counter (cnt) based on various conditions such as reset signal, pass_request signal, and previous signal values. 
The output signal handling will assign the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
