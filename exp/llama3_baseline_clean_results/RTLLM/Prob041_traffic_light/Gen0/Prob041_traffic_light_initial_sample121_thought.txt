The traffic light controller is designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic is implemented in the first always block, which uses a case statement to handle different states. The counting logic of the internal counter is handled in the second always block, where the counter values are adjusted based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal and the previous state of the traffic lights. The output signals are assigned in the final always block, where the previous values are assigned to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
