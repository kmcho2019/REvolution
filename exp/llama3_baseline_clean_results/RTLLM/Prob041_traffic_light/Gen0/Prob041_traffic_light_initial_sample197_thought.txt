To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we need to follow the given design track. The module will have three always blocks: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signals. The state transition logic will handle different states of the traffic light controller, the counting logic will handle the internal counter, and the output signals will be assigned based on the previous state. The counters and state transitions will be adjusted based on the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light controller.
