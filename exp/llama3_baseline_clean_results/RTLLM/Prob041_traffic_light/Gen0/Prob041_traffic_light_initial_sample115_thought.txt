To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we will use three always blocks: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signal handling. The state transition logic will handle the different states of the traffic light controller based on the current state and the count value. The counting logic will handle the internal counter (cnt) based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light. The output signal handling will assign the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to the output signals (red, yellow, green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
