To design the traffic light controller, we will create a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block, and the counting logic for the internal counter will be handled in the second always block. The output signals will be assigned based on the current state and the previous values of the signals. We will also consider the pedestrian button press and adjust the green time accordingly.
