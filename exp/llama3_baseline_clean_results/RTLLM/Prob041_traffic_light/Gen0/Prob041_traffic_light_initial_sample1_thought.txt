The traffic light controller will be implemented as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in one always block, the counting logic in another, and the output signal assignment in the third. The counting logic will be adjusted based on the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light. The output signals will be assigned based on the previous state and the current state.
