To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we will follow the recommended design track. The first always block will handle the state transition logic using a case statement to manage different states (idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green). The second always block will handle the counting logic of the internal counter (cnt) based on various conditions. The assign statement will assign the value of the internal counter (cnt) to the output clock. The final always block will handle the output signals, assigning the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to the output signals (red, yellow, green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
