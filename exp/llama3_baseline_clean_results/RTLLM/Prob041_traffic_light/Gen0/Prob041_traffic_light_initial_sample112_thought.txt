The traffic light controller can be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The FSM transitions between these states based on the current state and the count value of the internal counter. The internal counter is decremented on every positive edge of the clock and reset to specific values based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals (red, yellow, green) are assigned the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) on the positive edge of the clock.
