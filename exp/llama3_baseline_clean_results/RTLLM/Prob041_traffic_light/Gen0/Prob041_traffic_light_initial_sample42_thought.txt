The problem requires implementing a traffic light controller with a pedestrian button press feature. The traffic light has three indicators: red, yellow, and green. Under normal circumstances, the motor vehicle lane indicator light follows a 60 clock cycles of green, 5 clock cycles of yellow, and 10 clock cycles of red pattern. When the pedestrian button is pressed, if the remaining green time is greater than 10 clocks, it is shortened to 10 clocks, and if it is less than 10 clocks, it remains unchanged.

To implement this, we will use three always blocks in Verilog: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signal handling. We will also use a case statement to handle different states in the state transition logic block.

The parameters idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green will be used as enumeration values representing different states of the traffic light controller. Registers and wires such as cnt, state, p_red, p_yellow, and p_green will be used to implement the traffic light controller's functionality.
