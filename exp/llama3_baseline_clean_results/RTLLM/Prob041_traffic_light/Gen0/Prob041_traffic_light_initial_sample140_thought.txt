The traffic light controller will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic and counting logic will be implemented in separate always blocks. The counting logic will handle the internal counter based on the current state and the pass_request signal. The output signals will be updated based on the previous state and the current state. The pedestrian button press will be handled by adjusting the counter value when the green signal is active.
