To implement the traffic light controller, we need to consider the state transition logic, counting logic, and output signal assignments. The state transition logic will be handled by the first always block, which uses a case statement to transition between different states based on the current state and count value. The counting logic will be handled by the second always block, which decrements the internal counter (cnt) and adjusts its value based on various conditions. The output signal assignments will be handled by the final always block, which assigns the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to the output signals (red, yellow, green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The traffic light will have three states: red, yellow, and green, and the pedestrian button will be considered only when the lane light is green.
