The traffic light controller is designed with a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions between four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The internal counter (cnt) is used to keep track of time spent in each state. The pass_request signal is used to shorten the green time to 10 clocks when pressed. The output signals red, yellow, and green are updated based on the current state and previous state values.
