To implement a traffic light controller in Verilog, we can break down the problem into three main components: state transition logic, counting logic, and output signal assignment. The state transition logic will be handled by the first always block, which uses a case statement to manage the different states of the traffic light controller. The counting logic will be implemented in the second always block, which adjusts the internal counter based on various conditions. Finally, the output signal assignment will be handled by the third always block, which assigns the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
