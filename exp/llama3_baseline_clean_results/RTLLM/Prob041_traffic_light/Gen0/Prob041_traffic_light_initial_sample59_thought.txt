To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in one always block, the counting logic in another, and the output signals will be assigned in a third always block. We will also use a case statement to handle different states in the state transition logic. The counter will be decremented by 1 on every positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal, and its values will be adjusted based on various conditions.
