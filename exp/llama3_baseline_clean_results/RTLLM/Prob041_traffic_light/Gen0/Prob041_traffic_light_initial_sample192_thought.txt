To design a traffic light controller in Verilog, we'll create a finite state machine (FSM) that handles the different states of the traffic light (red, yellow, green) and a pedestrian button press request. The FSM will transition between these states based on the internal counter and the pedestrian button press. We'll also implement the counter logic to manage the timing of each state. Additionally, we'll handle the output signals for the traffic lights and the clock output.
