To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we need to follow the recommended design track. The first step is to define the state transition logic, which involves using a case statement to handle different states such as idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. Then, we need to implement the counting logic of the internal counter, adjusting its values based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal, green signal, yellow signal, and red signal. Finally, we need to assign the previous values to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
