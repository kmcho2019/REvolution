To implement the traffic light controller, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be implemented using a case statement. The counting logic of the internal counter will be handled based on various conditions such as reset, pass_request, and state transitions. The output signals will be assigned based on the previous values of the signals. The traffic light will follow the sequence of red (10 clocks), green (60 clocks), and yellow (5 clocks). When the pass_request signal is active during the green state, the remaining green time will be shortened to 10 clocks if it is greater than 10 clocks.
