The traffic light controller can be designed using a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transitions will be based on the count value of the internal counter. The counter will be decremented on every clock cycle, and its value will be adjusted based on the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be assigned based on the current state of the FSM. The internal counter value will be assigned to the output clock signal.
