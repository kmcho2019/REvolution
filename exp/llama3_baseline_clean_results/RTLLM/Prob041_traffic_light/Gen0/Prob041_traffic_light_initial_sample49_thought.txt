The traffic light controller will be designed as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block. The counting logic of the internal counter will be handled in the second always block. The output signals will be handled in the third always block. The assign statement will be used to assign the value of the internal counter to the output clock. The pedestrian button press will be considered when the green signal is active.
