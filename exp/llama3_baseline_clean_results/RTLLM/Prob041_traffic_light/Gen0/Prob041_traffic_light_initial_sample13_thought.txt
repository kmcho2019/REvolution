To implement a traffic light controller in Verilog, we need to define the states of the traffic light (idle, red, yellow, and green) and the transitions between these states. The controller should also handle the pedestrian button press by shortening the remaining green time to 10 clocks if it is greater than 10 clocks. We can use a case statement to handle different states and assign the next values for the red, yellow, and green signals. The internal counter (cnt) will be used for timing purposes, and its value will be assigned to the output clock. We will also handle the counting logic of the internal counter and the output signals in separate always blocks.
