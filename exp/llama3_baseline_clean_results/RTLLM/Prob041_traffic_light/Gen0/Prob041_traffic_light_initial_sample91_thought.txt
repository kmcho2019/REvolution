To implement the traffic light controller, we will follow the provided design track. The controller will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The first always block will handle the state transition logic based on the current state and the count value. The second always block will handle the counting logic, adjusting the counter value based on various conditions such as the pass_request signal and the previous signal values.
