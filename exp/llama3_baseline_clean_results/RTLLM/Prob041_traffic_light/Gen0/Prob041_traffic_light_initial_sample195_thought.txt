The design will use three always blocks to implement the traffic light controller: one for state transition logic, one for counting logic, and one for output signal handling. The state transition logic will use a case statement to handle different states, and the counting logic will handle the internal counter (cnt) based on various conditions. The output signal handling will assign the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) to the output signals (red, yellow, green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
