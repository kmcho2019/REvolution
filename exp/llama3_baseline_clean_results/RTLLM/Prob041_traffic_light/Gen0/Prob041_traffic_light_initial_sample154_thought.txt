The traffic light controller will be designed as a finite state machine with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be implemented using a case statement in the first always block. The counting logic of the internal counter will be handled in the second always block, where the counter will be decremented or set to specific values based on the current state and input signals. The output signals will be updated in the third always block, where the previous values of the signals will be assigned to the output signals on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal.
