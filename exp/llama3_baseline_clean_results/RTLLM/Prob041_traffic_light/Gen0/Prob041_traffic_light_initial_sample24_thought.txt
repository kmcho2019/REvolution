To implement the traffic light controller, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that handles different states of the traffic light, including red, yellow, and green, and responds to the pedestrian button press by adjusting the green time. The FSM should transition between states based on the internal counter and the pedestrian request signal. The internal counter is used to keep track of the time for each state. The output signals, red, yellow, and green, are determined by the current state of the FSM. The pedestrian button press is handled by checking if the remaining green time is greater than 10 clocks and adjusting it accordingly.
