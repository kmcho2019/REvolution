To create a clock generator module in Verilog, we will utilize an always block to continuously toggle the clock signal at regular intervals. The interval will be defined by the PERIOD parameter. Since the clock needs to toggle at half of the specified period to maintain a square wave shape, we will use a variable to track the passage of time and toggle the clock when this time variable reaches half of the period. We will use the initial block to set the initial state of the clock to 0.
