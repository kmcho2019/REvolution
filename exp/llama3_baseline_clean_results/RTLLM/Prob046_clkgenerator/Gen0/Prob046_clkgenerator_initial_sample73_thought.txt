To design the clkgenerator module, we can utilize an initial block to initialize the clock signal to 0. Then, we use a forever loop to create a periodic clock signal. Inside the loop, we first set the clock signal to its current state and then wait for half of the specified PERIOD. After that, we toggle the clock signal by assigning its inverse to the clock signal and wait for the remaining half of the PERIOD. This process repeats indefinitely, creating a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency.
