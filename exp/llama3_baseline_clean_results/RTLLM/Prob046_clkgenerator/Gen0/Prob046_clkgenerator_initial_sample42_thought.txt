To create a clock generator module in Verilog, we can utilize the concept of a forever loop within an initial block to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals. The module will have a parameter PERIOD that determines the total duration of the clock cycle, with the clock toggling every half of this period to create a square wave. We will use the $time variable in Verilog to keep track of the simulation time and the # delay operator to introduce delays between clock toggles.
