To create a clock generator module in Verilog, we need to define a module named "clkgenerator" with a parameter "PERIOD" that determines the frequency of the generated clock signal. The output of the module will be a clock signal "clk" that toggles at regular intervals defined by the PERIOD parameter. We can use an initial block to initialize the clock signal to 0 and then use a forever loop to toggle the clock signal at the desired frequency.
