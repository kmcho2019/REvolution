To design the clkgenerator module, we will utilize an always block instead of an initial block to create a continuous toggling effect for the clock signal. The always block will be sensitive to the posedge of the clk signal itself, allowing it to toggle at the desired interval defined by the PERIOD parameter. We'll use a variable to keep track of the time elapsed since the last toggle and compare it with half of the PERIOD to determine when to toggle the clock signal. This approach ensures the generation of a square wave clock signal with the specified frequency.
