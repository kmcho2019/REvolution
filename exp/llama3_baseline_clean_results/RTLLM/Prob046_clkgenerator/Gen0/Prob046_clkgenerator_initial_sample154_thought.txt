To implement the clkgenerator module, we will utilize a parameterized module that accepts the PERIOD as a parameter. Inside the module, an initial block will be used to initialize the clock signal to 0. A forever loop will then be used to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals defined by the PERIOD parameter, effectively generating a square wave clock signal. The forever loop will utilize the # operator to introduce a delay, allowing the clock signal to change state every half of the specified PERIOD.
