To create a clock generator module in Verilog that produces a periodic clock signal, we can utilize an initial block to set the initial state of the clock signal and then use a forever loop to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals defined by the PERIOD parameter. Since the clock needs to toggle every half of the specified PERIOD to create a square wave, we will use the # operator to introduce a delay in the forever loop. This will ensure the clock signal changes its state (from 0 to 1 or 1 to 0) at the desired intervals, thus generating a clock signal with the specified frequency.
