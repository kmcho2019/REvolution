To create a clock generator module in Verilog, we can utilize an initial block to initialize the clock signal and a forever loop to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals. The period of the clock signal will be defined by the PERIOD parameter, which determines how often the clock signal toggles. The clock signal will toggle every half of the specified PERIOD, creating a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency. The initial block sets the initial state of the clock signal to 0, ensuring a consistent starting point for the clock signal.
