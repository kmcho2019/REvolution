To create a clock generator module in Verilog, we will utilize an initial block to set the initial state of the clock signal and a forever loop to continuously toggle the clock signal at the desired interval. The PERIOD parameter will define the time between each toggle, effectively determining the frequency of the generated clock signal. We will use the `$time` and `$display` system tasks for debugging and verification purposes, although they are not strictly necessary for the operation of the clock generator.
