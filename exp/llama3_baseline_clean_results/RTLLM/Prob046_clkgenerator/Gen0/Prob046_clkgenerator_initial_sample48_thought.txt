To create a clock generator module in Verilog that produces a periodic clock signal, we can utilize an initial block to initialize the clock signal and an always block to continuously toggle the clock at specified intervals. The PERIOD parameter determines the length of each clock cycle, with the clock toggling every half cycle to generate a square wave. We'll use the `#` symbol to specify the delay, ensuring the clock toggles at the correct frequency. Given the PERIOD is set to 10 time units, the clock will toggle every 5 time units, creating a square wave with a period of 10 time units.
