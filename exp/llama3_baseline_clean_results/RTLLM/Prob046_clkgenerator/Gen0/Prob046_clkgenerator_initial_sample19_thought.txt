To create a clock generator module in Verilog that produces a periodic clock signal, we will use an initial block to initialize the clock signal and then utilize a forever loop to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals. The interval at which the clock toggles will be determined by the PERIOD parameter. Since we want to create a square wave, the clock will toggle every half of the specified period. This can be achieved by using the # operator to introduce delays within the forever loop. The clock signal will be defined as a reg type and will be the output of the module.
