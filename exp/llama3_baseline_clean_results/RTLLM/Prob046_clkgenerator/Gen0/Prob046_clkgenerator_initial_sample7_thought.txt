To design the clkgenerator module, we need to consider the following key points:
- The module should have a parameter PERIOD to define the clock period.
- The module should have an output port clk to generate the clock signal.
- We use an initial block to initialize the clock signal to 0.
- We use a forever loop to continuously toggle the clock signal at regular intervals, creating a square wave.
- The clock signal should toggle every half of the specified PERIOD to achieve the desired frequency.
