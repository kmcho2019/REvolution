The frequency divider module (freq_diveven) will be designed using a counter-based approach. It will utilize a 4-bit counter to keep track of the input clock cycles and divide the frequency by a specified even number (NUM_DIV). The counter will be reset to zero when the reset signal (rst_n) is low. On each positive edge of the input clock, the counter will increment until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output signal (clk_div).
