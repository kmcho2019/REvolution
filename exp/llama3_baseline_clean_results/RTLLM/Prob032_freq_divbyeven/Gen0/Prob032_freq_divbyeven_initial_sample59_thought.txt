The frequency divider module, `freq_diveven`, will be designed using a counter-based approach. The counter, `cnt`, will count the number of input clock cycles and toggle the `clk_div` output every `NUM_DIV` cycles. The `NUM_DIV` parameter should be an even number, and the counter will reach its limit at half of `NUM_DIV - 1` to ensure the output toggles at the correct frequency. 
The module will use an active-low reset signal (`rst_n`) to initialize the counter and the `clk_div` output to zero. 
To ensure correct operation, the counter will be implemented as a 4-bit register, and the `NUM_DIV` parameter will be a module parameter that must be set to an even number.
The module will use a simple if-else statement to check the counter value and toggle the `clk_div` output accordingly.
