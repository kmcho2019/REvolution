To design the freq_diveven module, we need to use a counter to track the number of clock cycles and a parameterizable division factor (NUM_DIV) that must be an even number. We'll use a 4-bit counter to handle division factors up to 16. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it will reset and toggle the divided clock output. The reset signal will initialize both the counter and the divided clock signal to zero.
