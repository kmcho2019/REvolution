To implement the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we'll use a parameterized Verilog module with a 4-bit counter to track the clock cycles. The `NUM_DIV` parameter will determine the division factor. We'll handle the active-low reset signal `rst_n` to initialize the counter and the divided clock output `clk_div`. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock `clk` until it reaches the specified limit (half of `NUM_DIV - 1`), at which point it resets and toggles `clk_div`. This design ensures the divided clock frequency is exactly the input clock frequency divided by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`.
