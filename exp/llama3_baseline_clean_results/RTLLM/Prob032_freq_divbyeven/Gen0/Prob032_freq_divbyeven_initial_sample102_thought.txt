The freq_diveven module can be designed using a counter to track the number of clock cycles. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it resets and toggles the divided clock output. The module uses a parameterizable division factor (NUM_DIV) that must be an even number. The reset signal (rst_n) is used to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal to zero. This approach ensures that the divided clock signal is toggled at the desired frequency.
