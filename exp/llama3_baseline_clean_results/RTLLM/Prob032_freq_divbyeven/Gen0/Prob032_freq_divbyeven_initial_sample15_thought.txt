The freq_diveven module will be designed as a parameterized module with a 4-bit counter to track the number of clock cycles. The division factor will be specified by the NUM_DIV parameter. On every positive edge of the input clock, the counter will be checked against the half of NUM_DIV - 1. If the counter is less than this value, it will be incremented without changing the divided clock output. When the counter reaches the specified limit, it will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
