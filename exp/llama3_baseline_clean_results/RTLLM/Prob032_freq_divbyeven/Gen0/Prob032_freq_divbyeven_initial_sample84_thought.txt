The freq_diveven module can be designed using a counter-based approach. It will take the input clock signal (clk) and divide its frequency by a specified even number (NUM_DIV). This will be achieved by counting the input clock cycles using a 4-bit counter (cnt). The counter will reset when it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, effectively dividing the clock frequency. The divided clock output signal (clk_div) will be toggled on every reset of the counter, maintaining the divided frequency.
