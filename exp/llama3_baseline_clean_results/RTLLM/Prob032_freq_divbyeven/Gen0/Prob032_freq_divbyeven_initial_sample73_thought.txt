The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be designed as a parameterized module with a 4-bit counter to track the number of clock cycles. The NUM_DIV parameter will specify the division factor. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, the counter resets to zero and toggles the clk_div output signal. This will effectively divide the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
