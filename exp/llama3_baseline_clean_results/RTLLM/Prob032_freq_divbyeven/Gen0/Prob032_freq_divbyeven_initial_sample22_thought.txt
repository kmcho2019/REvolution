The module freq_diveven will be designed with a parameterizable division factor NUM_DIV, which must be an even number. A 4-bit counter (cnt) will be used to track the number of clock cycles. On each positive edge of the input clock (clk), the counter will be checked against half of NUM_DIV - 1. If the counter is less than this value, it will be incremented. When the counter reaches the specified limit, it will be reset to zero, and the divided clock signal (clk_div) will be toggled. The module will also have an active-low reset signal (rst_n) to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal.
