To implement the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we need to define a parameter `NUM_DIV` to specify the division factor. Since `NUM_DIV` must be an even number, we'll use a 4-bit counter `cnt` to keep track of the clock cycles. On each positive edge of the input clock `clk`, the counter increments until it reaches half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, at which point it resets to zero and toggles the divided clock output `clk_div`. The module also includes an active-low reset signal `rst_n` to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal.
