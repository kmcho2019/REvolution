To implement the freq_diveven module, I will use a parameterized Verilog module with a 4-bit counter to divide the input clock frequency by an even number specified by the NUM_DIV parameter. The module will have an active-low reset signal (rst_n) to initialize the counter and the divided clock output (clk_div) to zero. On each positive edge of the input clock (clk), the counter will increment until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it will reset to zero and toggle the clk_div output signal. This will effectively divide the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
