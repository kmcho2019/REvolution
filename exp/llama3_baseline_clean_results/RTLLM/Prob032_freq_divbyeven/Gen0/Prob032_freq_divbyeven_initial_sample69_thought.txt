We use a parameter `NUM_DIV` to specify the division factor, which must be an even number. We then declare a 4-bit register `cnt` to act as the counter. In the always block, we check for the active-low reset signal `rst_n`. If `rst_n` is low, we reset the counter `cnt` and the divided clock signal `clk_div`. On each positive edge of the input clock `clk`, we check the value of the counter. If the counter is less than half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, we increment the counter without changing the `clk_div` output. When the counter reaches the specified limit, we reset the counter to zero and toggle the `clk_div` output signal, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`.
