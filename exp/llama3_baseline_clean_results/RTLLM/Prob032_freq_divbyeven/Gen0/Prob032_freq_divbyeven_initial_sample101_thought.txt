To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by even numbers, we can utilize a counter-based approach. We will use a parameter `NUM_DIV` to specify the division factor, which must be an even number. The counter will be a 4-bit register (`cnt`) that increments on each positive edge of the input clock (`clk`) until it reaches half of `NUM_DIV - 1`. At this point, the counter resets to zero and toggles the `clk_div` output signal, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`. We will also incorporate an active-low reset signal (`rst_n`) to initialize the module.
