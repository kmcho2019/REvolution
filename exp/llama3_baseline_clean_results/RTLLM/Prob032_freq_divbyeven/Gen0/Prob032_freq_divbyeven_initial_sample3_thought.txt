To implement the freq_diveven module, we will use a parameterizable Verilog module with a counter that increments on each positive edge of the input clock. The counter's limit will be determined by the NUM_DIV parameter, ensuring that the divided clock signal toggles every NUM_DIV/2 clock cycles. This means the counter will reset to zero and toggle the clk_div output when it reaches (NUM_DIV/2 - 1), effectively dividing the frequency by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
