The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will utilize a parameterized approach to allow for variable division factors. It will include a counter to track clock cycles and a divided clock output that toggles at the specified division rate. The module will also have an active-low reset to initialize the counter and output. Given that the division factor (NUM_DIV) must be even, the counter will compare against half of (NUM_DIV - 1) to determine when to toggle the output and reset the counter.
