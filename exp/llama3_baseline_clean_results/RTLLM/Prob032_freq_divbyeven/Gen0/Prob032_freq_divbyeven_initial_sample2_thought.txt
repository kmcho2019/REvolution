To implement the freq_diveven module, we will use a parameterized Verilog module with a 4-bit counter to divide the input clock frequency by even numbers. The NUM_DIV parameter will determine the division factor. Inside the module, we will use a reg to store the current counter value and another reg to store the divided clock output. The counter will be reset when the active-low reset signal is asserted. On each positive edge of the input clock, the counter will increment until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1. Once the counter reaches this limit, it will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output.
