To implement the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we will use a parameterized approach to allow for flexibility in the division factor. The module will utilize a counter (`cnt`) to keep track of the number of clock cycles. The counter will be reset to zero and the divided clock output (`clk_div`) will be toggled when the counter reaches half of the division factor minus one. This ensures the divided clock output toggles at the desired frequency, effectively dividing the input clock by the specified even number.
