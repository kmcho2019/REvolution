To create the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we'll define a 4-bit counter (`cnt`) and use a parameter `NUM_DIV` to specify the division factor. The module will be reset when `rst_n` is low, and the counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock `clk`. When the counter reaches half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, it resets and toggles the `clk_div` output signal. This will effectively divide the frequency of the input clock by the specified even number.
