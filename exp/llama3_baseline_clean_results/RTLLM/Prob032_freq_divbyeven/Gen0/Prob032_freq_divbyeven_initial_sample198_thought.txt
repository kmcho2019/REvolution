The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be designed with a parameterized division factor NUM_DIV. It utilizes a counter (cnt) to track clock cycles. When the counter reaches a specific limit based on NUM_DIV, it resets and toggles the divided clock output (clk_div), effectively dividing the input clock frequency by an even number. To ensure NUM_DIV is even, a check can be implemented at the start of the module.
