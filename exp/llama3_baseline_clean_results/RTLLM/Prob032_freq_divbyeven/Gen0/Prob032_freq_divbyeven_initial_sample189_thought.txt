To design the freq_diveven module, I will use a 4-bit counter to track the number of clock cycles. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of the division factor minus one (NUM_DIV - 1) / 2. Once the counter reaches this limit, it will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output signal. This will effectively divide the input clock frequency by the even number specified by NUM_DIV. I will also include an active-low reset signal to initialize the module.
