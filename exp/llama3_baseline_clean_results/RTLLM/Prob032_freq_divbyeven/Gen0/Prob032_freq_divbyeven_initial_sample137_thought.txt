To implement the frequency divider, I will design a Verilog module named `freq_diveven` that takes an input clock `clk` and an active-low reset `rst_n`, and generates a divided clock output `clk_div`. The division factor will be specified by the `NUM_DIV` parameter, which must be an even number. I will use a 4-bit counter (`cnt`) to track the number of clock cycles. The counter will reset to zero and toggle the `clk_div` output when it reaches half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the specified even number.
