To implement the frequency divider module (freq_diveven), we'll use a parameterized approach to allow flexibility in the division factor (NUM_DIV), ensuring it's an even number. The module will have an input clock (clk), an active-low reset (rst_n), and a divided clock output (clk_div). We'll utilize a 4-bit counter (cnt) to keep track of clock cycles. When the counter reaches a specified threshold based on NUM_DIV, it resets and toggles the divided clock output, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the specified even factor.
