To design the freq_diveven module, we need to create a counter that increments on each positive edge of the input clock signal (clk) and resets to zero when it reaches half of the division factor minus one (NUM_DIV - 1). The divided clock output signal (clk_div) will be toggled when the counter resets, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by NUM_DIV. We will use a parameterizable design to make the division factor flexible and enforce the constraint that it must be an even number. The module will also include an active-low reset signal (rst_n) to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal to zero.
