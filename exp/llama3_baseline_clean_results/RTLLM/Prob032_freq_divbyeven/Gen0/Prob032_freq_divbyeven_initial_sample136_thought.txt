The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be designed with a counter (cnt) that increments on each positive edge of the input clock (clk) until it reaches half of the specified even division factor (NUM_DIV - 1). Once this condition is met, the counter resets to zero, and the divided clock output (clk_div) is toggled. This process effectively divides the input clock frequency by the specified even number. We will use a parameterizable module to allow for flexibility in the division factor. The reset signal (rst_n) will be used to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal when it is asserted low.
