The module freq_diveven will be designed as a parameterized Verilog module, allowing the division factor (NUM_DIV) to be specified during instantiation. The module will utilize a 4-bit counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles. On each positive edge of the input clock, the counter will be incremented until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1. At this point, the counter will reset to zero and the divided clock output (clk_div) will be toggled, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
