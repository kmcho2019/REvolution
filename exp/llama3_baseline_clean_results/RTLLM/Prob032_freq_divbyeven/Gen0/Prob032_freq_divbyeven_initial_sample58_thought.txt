To design the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we need to create a counter that increments on each positive edge of the input clock `clk` and resets to zero when it reaches a certain limit based on the division factor `NUM_DIV`. The divided clock signal `clk_div` should toggle every time the counter resets. The `rst_n` signal will be used to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal when it is low. The division factor `NUM_DIV` must be an even number, so the limit for the counter will be half of `NUM_DIV - 1`. This ensures that the divided clock toggles at the correct frequency.
