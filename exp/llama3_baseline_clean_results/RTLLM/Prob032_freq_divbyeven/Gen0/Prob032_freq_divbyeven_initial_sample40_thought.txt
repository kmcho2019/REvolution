To implement the frequency divider module (freq_diveven) that divides the input clock frequency by even numbers, I will use a counter (cnt) to count the number of clock cycles. The division factor is specified by the parameter NUM_DIV, which must be an even number. The counter and the divided clock signal (clk_div) will be initialized to zero when the reset signal (rst_n) is low. On each positive edge of the input clock (clk), the counter will increment until it reaches the specified limit (half of NUM_DIV - 1), at which point it resets to zero and toggles the clk_div output signal, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
