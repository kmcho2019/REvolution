The freq_diveven module will be designed with a 4-bit counter to keep track of the clock cycles. It will utilize a parameterized approach to specify the division factor (NUM_DIV), ensuring it's an even number. The module will reset the counter and the divided clock signal when the active-low reset signal (rst_n) is asserted. On each positive edge of the input clock (clk), it will increment the counter until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it resets the counter and toggles the divided clock output (clk_div), thus achieving the desired frequency division.
