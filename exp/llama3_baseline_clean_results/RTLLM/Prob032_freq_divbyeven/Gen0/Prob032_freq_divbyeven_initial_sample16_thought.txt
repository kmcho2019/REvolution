The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be designed with a parameterized division factor (NUM_DIV) that must be an even number. It uses a 4-bit counter (cnt) to track the number of input clock cycles. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, it resets and toggles the divided clock output (clk_div), effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the specified even number. The module is reset when the active-low reset signal (rst_n) is low, initializing both the counter and the divided clock output to zero.
