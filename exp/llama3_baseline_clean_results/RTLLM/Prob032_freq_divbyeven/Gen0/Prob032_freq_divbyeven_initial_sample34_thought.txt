The division factor will be specified by the `NUM_DIV` parameter, which must be an even number. On each positive edge of the input clock (`clk`), we will check if the counter has reached the specified limit (half of `NUM_DIV - 1`). If it has, we will reset the counter to zero and toggle the `clk_div` output signal. Otherwise, we will increment the counter.
