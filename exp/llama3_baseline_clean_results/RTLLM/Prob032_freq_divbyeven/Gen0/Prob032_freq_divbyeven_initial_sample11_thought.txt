The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be implemented using a 4-bit counter to keep track of the clock cycles. A parameter, NUM_DIV, will be used to specify the division factor. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1. Once this limit is reached, the counter will reset to zero and the divided clock signal will be toggled. To ensure that the division factor is an even number, we will use the modulus operator to check if NUM_DIV is even during the instantiation of the module. If NUM_DIV is not even, the module will not be instantiated correctly.
