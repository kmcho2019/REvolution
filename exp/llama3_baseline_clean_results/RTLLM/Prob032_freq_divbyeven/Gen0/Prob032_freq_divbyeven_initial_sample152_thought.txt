To implement the frequency divider module (freq_diveven), we'll use a counter (cnt) to track the number of clock cycles. The division factor, specified by the NUM_DIV parameter, must be an even number. We'll reset the counter and the divided clock signal (clk_div) to zero when the active-low reset signal (rst_n) is low. On each positive edge of the input clock (clk), we'll increment the counter if it's less than half of NUM_DIV - 1. Once the counter reaches the specified limit, we'll reset it to zero and toggle the clk_div output signal, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the even number specified by NUM_DIV.
