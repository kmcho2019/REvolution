To implement the frequency divider module (freq_diveven), we'll define a parameterized Verilog module that takes the input clock (clk) and active-low reset (rst_n) signals and generates a divided clock output (clk_div). The division factor is specified by the parameter NUM_DIV, which must be an even number. Inside the module, we'll use a 4-bit counter (cnt) to track the number of clock cycles and toggle the clk_div signal when the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1. We'll also include a reset mechanism to initialize the counter and clk_div signal when rst_n is low.
