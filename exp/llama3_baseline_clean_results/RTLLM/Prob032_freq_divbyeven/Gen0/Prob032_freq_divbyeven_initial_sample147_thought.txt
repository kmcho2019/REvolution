The freq_diveven module will be implemented as a parameterized module in Verilog, allowing for the division factor to be specified at instantiation time. The division factor, NUM_DIV, will determine how often the output clock, clk_div, is toggled. The counter, cnt, will be used to keep track of the number of clock cycles and will reset to zero when it reaches the specified limit, which is half of NUM_DIV - 1. The active-low reset signal, rst_n, will be used to initialize the counter and the divided clock signal when it is low.
