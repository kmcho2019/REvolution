To implement the frequency divider module, we will use a counter to keep track of the input clock cycles. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches the specified limit, which is half of the division factor minus one. Once the limit is reached, the counter will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output signal. This process effectively divides the frequency of the input clock by the specified even number. We will use a parameter `NUM_DIV` to specify the division factor and ensure it is an even number. We will also include an active-low reset signal to initialize the module.
