To design the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we will use a counter (`cnt`) to keep track of the number of clock cycles. The division factor, specified by the `NUM_DIV` parameter, must be an even number. We will initialize the counter and the divided clock signal (`clk_div`) to zero when the reset signal (`rst_n`) is low. On each positive edge of the input clock (`clk`), we will increment the counter if it is less than half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, and reset the counter to zero and toggle the `clk_div` output signal when the counter reaches the specified limit.
