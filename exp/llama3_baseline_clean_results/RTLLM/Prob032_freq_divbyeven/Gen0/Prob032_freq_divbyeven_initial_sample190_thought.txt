The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be implemented using a counter (cnt) to track the number of clock cycles. A parameter, NUM_DIV, will be defined to specify the division factor. The module will have an input clock signal (clk), an active-low reset signal (rst_n), and a divided clock output signal (clk_div). The counter will be reset to zero when the reset signal is low, and it will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1. At this point, the counter will reset, and the divided clock output signal will toggle, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the specified even number.
