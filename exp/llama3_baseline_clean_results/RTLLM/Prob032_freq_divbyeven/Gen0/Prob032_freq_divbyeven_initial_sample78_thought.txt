To implement the frequency divider, we'll use a counter to track the number of clock cycles and toggle the output clock signal when the counter reaches the specified limit. The counter will be reset when the reset signal is low. We'll also use a parameter (`NUM_DIV`) to specify the division factor, which must be an even number. The divided clock output will be toggled every time the counter reaches half of `NUM_DIV - 1` clock cycles, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the specified even number.
