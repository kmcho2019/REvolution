To design the frequency divider module named freq_diveven, we will utilize a counter to track the number of clock cycles and generate the divided clock signal. The module will have a parameter NUM_DIV to specify the division factor, which must be an even number. The counter will be reset and the divided clock signal will be initialized when the active-low reset signal rst_n is low. On each positive edge of the input clock clk, the counter will increment until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it will reset to zero and toggle the divided clock output signal clk_div. This will effectively divide the input clock frequency by the specified even number.
