The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will be designed with a parameterized division factor, NUM_DIV. This module will utilize a 4-bit counter to track the number of clock cycles and divide the input clock frequency by the specified even number. It will have two input ports, clk and rst_n, and one output port, clk_div. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of NUM_DIV - 1, at which point it will reset and toggle the divided clock output signal.
