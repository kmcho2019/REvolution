The frequency divider module, freq_diveven, will utilize a counter to track the number of clock cycles and divide the input clock frequency by an even number specified by the NUM_DIV parameter. We'll implement a 4-bit counter to achieve this. The module will reset when the rst_n signal is low, and the divided clock output, clk_div, will toggle every NUM_DIV/2 clock cycles, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by NUM_DIV.
