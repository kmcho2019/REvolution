The key components include input control, input registers, partial product generation, partial sum calculation, final product calculation, and output assignment. The module, multi_pipe_8bit, will take in the clock signal (clk), active-low reset signal (rst_n), input enable signal (mul_en_in), and input signals representing the multiplicand (mul_a) and multiplier (mul_b). The final product will be obtained by summing up all the partial sums.
