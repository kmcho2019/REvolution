To design an unsigned 8-bit multiplier using pipelining, we will break down the multiplication process into stages. The first stage will be the input control and register stage where the input enable signal and the multiplicand and multiplier are sampled and stored in registers. The next stage will involve generating partial products based on the bits of the multiplier. Then, we calculate partial sums by adding the partial products and accumulate these sums to obtain the final product. Finally, we assign the final product to the output based on the output enable signal. The entire process is synchronized with a clock signal (clk) and an active-low reset signal (rst_n) is used to reset the design.
