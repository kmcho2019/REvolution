To design an unsigned 8-bit multiplier based on pipelining, we will break down the multiplication process into several stages. The first stage involves sampling the input enable signal and storing the input multiplicand and multiplier in registers. The second stage generates partial products by performing bitwise operations between the multiplicand and the individual bits of the multiplier. The third stage calculates the partial sums by adding up the partial products. The fourth stage accumulates the partial sums to obtain the final product. Finally, the output product is assigned based on the output enable signal. We will use a clock signal to synchronize these operations and an active-low reset signal to reset the module. The output enable signal will indicate whether the multiplication operation is enabled.
