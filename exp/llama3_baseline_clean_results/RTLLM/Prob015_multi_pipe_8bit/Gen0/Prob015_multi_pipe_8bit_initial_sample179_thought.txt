To implement the 8-bit unsigned multiplier using pipelining, we need to follow a multi-stage approach. The design will include input control, input registers, partial product generation, partial sum calculation, final product calculation, and output assignment. The multiplier will be designed to perform the multiplication operation when the input enable signal is active, and the output enable signal will indicate when the product is valid. We will utilize registers to store the intermediate results and wires to propagate the signals between the stages.
