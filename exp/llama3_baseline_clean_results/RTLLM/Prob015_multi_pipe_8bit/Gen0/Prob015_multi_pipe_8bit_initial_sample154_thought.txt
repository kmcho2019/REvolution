Next, we will create registers to store the input multiplicand (mul_a) and multiplier (mul_b) values.
Then, we will generate partial products by performing bitwise operations between the multiplicand and the individual bits of the multiplier.
After that, we will calculate the partial sums by adding the partial products.
Finally, we will sum up all the partial sums to obtain the final product and assign it to the output (mul_out) based on the output enable signal.
