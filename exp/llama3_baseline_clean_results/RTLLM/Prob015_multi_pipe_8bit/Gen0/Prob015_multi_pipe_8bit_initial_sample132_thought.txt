The unsigned 8-bit multiplier based on pipelining processing will be implemented as a sequential digital circuit using Verilog. The design will consist of several stages: input control, input registers, partial product generation, partial sum calculation, and final product calculation. Each stage will be carefully designed to ensure proper pipelining and correct operation. The module will utilize a clock signal, active-low reset signal, input enable signal, and provide an output enable signal along with the 16-bit product output.
