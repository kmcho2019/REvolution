To design the unsigned 8-bit multiplier using pipelining, we'll break down the process into several stages: input control, input registers, partial product generation, partial sum calculation, final product calculation, and output assignment. Each stage will be synchronized with the clock signal (clk) and will consider the input enable signal (mul_en_in) for controlling the multiplication operation. We'll use registers to store intermediate results and wires to connect the different stages. The output enable signal (mul_en_out) will be derived from the input enable signal, and the product output (mul_out) will be assigned based on the output enable signal and the final product value.
