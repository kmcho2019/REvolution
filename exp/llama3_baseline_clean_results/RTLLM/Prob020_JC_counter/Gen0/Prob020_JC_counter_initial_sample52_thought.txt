The Johnson counter implementation involves a 64-bit register Q. The counter is updated on every rising edge of the clock signal clk or the falling edge of the reset signal rst_n. 
When the reset signal rst_n is low, the counter is reset to 0. 
Otherwise, the counter increments or decrements based on the least significant bit (LSB) of Q. 
If the LSB is 0, the counter increments by shifting the current value (Q[63:1]) to the right and appending a 1 at the most significant bit position. 
If the LSB is 1, the counter decrements by shifting the current value (Q[63:1]) to the right and appending a 0 at the most significant bit position.
This can be implemented using a single clocked always block, where we use if-else statements to handle the reset and the increment/decrement logic.
