The implementation of the 64-bit Johnson counter involves a combination of shifting and appending bits based on the state of the least significant bit. The key steps are to check the state of the reset signal and the least significant bit of the counter value. If the reset signal is low, reset the counter to 0. Otherwise, check the least significant bit and perform the corresponding shift operation (right shift with appending 1 if Q[0] is 0, right shift with appending 0 if Q[0] is 1) to update the counter value.
