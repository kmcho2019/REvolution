To implement the 64-bit Johnson counter, we first need to understand the operational principle of a Johnson counter. It is a type of ring counter where the most significant bit is fed back to the least significant bit position with an inversion, creating a sequence of states that eventually returns to its initial state. For a 64-bit Johnson counter, we will utilize a similar principle, updating the state on every rising edge of the clock signal and resetting the counter when the reset signal is low. We'll use an if-else statement within an always block to check the conditions for incrementing or decrementing the counter value based on the least significant bit (Q[0]).
