To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter, we can use a combination of conditional statements and shift operations in Verilog. The counter's operation depends on the state of the least significant bit (Q[0]) and the reset signal (rst_n). On every rising edge of the clock signal, we will check the state of the reset signal and the least significant bit, and update the counter accordingly. If the reset signal is low, the counter will be reset to 0. If the least significant bit is 0, the counter will increment by shifting the current value to the right and appending a 1 at the most significant bit position. If the least significant bit is 1, the counter will decrement by shifting the current value to the right and appending a 0 at the most significant bit position.
