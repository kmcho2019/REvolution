To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter, we can utilize a simple shift register with a conditional append operation based on the least significant bit (LSB) of the current count value. The counter should be updated on every rising edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal. When the reset signal is low, the counter should be reset to 0. The counter's increment and decrement operations are determined by the LSB of the current count value. This can be achieved using a combination of always blocks for sequential logic and conditional statements to determine the next state of the counter.
