When the LSB is 1, the counter decrements by shifting the current state one bit to the right and appending a 0 at the MSB position.

We also need to handle the reset condition. When the reset signal (rst_n) is low, the counter should be reset to its initial state, which is all zeros.

The module will be designed as a synchronous sequential circuit, meaning it will update its state on the rising edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
