When Q[0] is 1, the counter decrements by shifting the current value one bit to the right and setting the most significant bit (Q[63]) to 0. This process continues, and the counter wraps around to its initial state (0000...0) after reaching the maximum count.

We'll use a synchronous reset to initialize the counter. The counter value (Q) is updated on every rising edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
