To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter, we will utilize a synchronous design approach. The counter's next state will be determined based on its current state and the values of the input signals (clk and rst_n). When the reset signal is low, the counter will be reset to 0. On every rising edge of the clock signal, the counter will update its value according to the rules of a Johnson counter: if the least significant bit is 0, the counter increments by shifting the current value to the right and appending a 1 at the most significant bit position; if the least significant bit is 1, the counter decrements by shifting the current value to the right and appending a 0 at the most significant bit position.
