To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter, we need to design a synchronous sequential circuit that updates its state based on the current state and the clock signal. The counter has 64 bits, and its state transition depends on the least significant bit (LSB) of the current state. We can use a combination of conditional statements and bitwise operations to achieve the desired behavior. The circuit will be reset when the active-low reset signal (rst_n) is low. On every rising edge of the clock signal (clk), the counter will update its state according to the rules specified.
