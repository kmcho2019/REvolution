To design a 64-bit Johnson counter, we will use a Verilog module named JC_counter with input ports clk and rst_n, and an output port Q. The counter will be updated on every rising edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal. If the reset signal is low, the counter will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the counter will either increment or decrement based on the least significant bit of the current count value. This will be achieved by shifting the current value and appending either a 1 or a 0 at the most significant bit position, depending on the least significant bit.
