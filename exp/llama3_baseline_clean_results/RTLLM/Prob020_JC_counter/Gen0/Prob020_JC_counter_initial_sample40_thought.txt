To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter in Verilog, we will first initialize the counter to 0 when the reset signal is low. Then, on every rising edge of the clock signal, we check the least significant bit (LSB) of the counter. If the LSB is 0, we shift the current value to the right and append a 1 at the most significant bit position, effectively incrementing the counter. If the LSB is 1, we shift the current value to the right and append a 0 at the most significant bit position, effectively decrementing the counter. We can achieve this behavior using a combination of conditional statements and bit manipulation in Verilog.
