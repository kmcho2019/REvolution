To implement the pulse detection module, we'll use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, PULSE_START, PULSE_PEAK, and PULSE_END. The IDLE state is the initial state where we wait for the rising edge of the pulse. The PULSE_START state is entered when a rising edge is detected, PULSE_PEAK when the pulse is at its peak (high), and PULSE_END when the falling edge is detected, indicating the end of the pulse. The data_out signal is asserted only when we are in the PULSE_END state, indicating the completion of a pulse. This approach ensures that data_out is 1 only at the end cycle of a pulse and returns to 0 until another pulse is detected.
