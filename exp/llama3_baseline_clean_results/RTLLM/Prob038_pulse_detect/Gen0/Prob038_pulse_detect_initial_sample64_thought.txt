The design idea is to create a finite state machine (FSM) that tracks the input signal data_in and outputs a pulse signal data_out whenever a pulse is detected. The FSM has three states: IDLE (initial state), RISING (data_in goes from 0 to 1), and FALLING (data_in goes from 1 to 0). The data_out signal is asserted when the FSM transitions from the FALLING state to the IDLE state, indicating the end of a pulse. The FSM is reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal rst_n is asserted low.
