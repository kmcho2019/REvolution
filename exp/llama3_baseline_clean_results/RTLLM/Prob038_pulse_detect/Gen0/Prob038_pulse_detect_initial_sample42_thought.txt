The pulse detection module will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) to track the state of the input signal data_in. The FSM will have four states: IDLE (initial state), RISING (data_in transitions from 0 to 1), HIGH (data_in remains 1), and FALLING (data_in transitions from 1 to 0). The module will detect a pulse when the FSM transitions from the RISING state to the HIGH state and then to the FALLING state. The data_out signal will be set to 1 at the end of the pulse (i.e., when the FSM is in the FALLING state) and will remain 0 otherwise.
