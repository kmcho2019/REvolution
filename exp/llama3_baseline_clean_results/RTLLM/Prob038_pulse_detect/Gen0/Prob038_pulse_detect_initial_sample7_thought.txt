The pulse detection module can be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, PULSE_START, and PULSE_END. The module starts in the IDLE state and transitions to PULSE_START when data_in rises from 0 to 1. It then transitions to PULSE_END when data_in falls from 1 to 0 after being high for at least one cycle, indicating the end of a pulse. The data_out signal is set to 1 in the PULSE_END state to indicate the presence of a pulse. The FSM returns to the IDLE state after a pulse is detected.
