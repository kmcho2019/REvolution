To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we'll utilize the double-edge clocking technique. This involves creating two intermediate clock signals with uneven periods, then phase-shifting and combining them to achieve a smooth fractional division. The module, freq_divbyfrac, will include counters to manage the clock cycles, a mechanism to generate the intermediate clocks, and logic to phase-shift and combine these clocks. The active low reset signal (rst_n) will be used to initialize the module. The divided clock output (clk_div) will reflect the fractional frequency division.
