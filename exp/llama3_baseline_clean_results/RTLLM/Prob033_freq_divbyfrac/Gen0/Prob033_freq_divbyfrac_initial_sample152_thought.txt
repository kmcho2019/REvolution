The freq_divbyfrac module will be designed to divide the input clock frequency by a fractional value of 3.5. This will be achieved by utilizing the double-edge clocking technique to generate two uneven clock periods, which are then phase-shifted and combined to produce a smooth fractional clock output. The module will consist of a counter to count the clock cycles, a logic block to generate the intermediate divided clock signals, and a phase-shifting block to adjust for duty cycle imbalance. The final fractional divided clock output will be generated by logically OR-ing the two intermediate clocks.
