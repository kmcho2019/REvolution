To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, we will utilize a counter to track the input clock cycles and generate the intermediate divided clock signals. The double-edge clocking technique will be employed to achieve the fractional frequency division. We will use a 3-bit counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7). Based on the counter value, we will generate the intermediate clock signals with uneven periods (4 and 3 source clock cycles). Then, we will phase-shift these signals by half a clock period to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
