To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we will use the double-edge clocking technique to achieve half-integer division while adjusting for duty cycle imbalance. The module, freq_divbyfrac, will count clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. We will use two counters, one for counting the uneven clock periods (4 and 3 source clock cycles) and another for generating the phase-shifted versions of the divided clock. The final fractional divided clock output will be produced by logically OR-ing the two intermediate clocks, ensuring a uniform period. The implementation will involve a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different states of the frequency divider.
