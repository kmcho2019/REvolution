To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we'll utilize the double-edge clocking technique. The approach involves counting clock cycles, generating intermediate divided clock signals with uneven periods, phase-shifting these signals, and then logically OR-ing them to produce a smooth fractional clock output. 
The key steps are: 
1. Counting clock cycles to determine when to generate the divided clock.
2. Creating uneven clock periods (4 cycles and 3 cycles for 3.5x division).
3. Phase-shifting the divided clocks by half a cycle to ensure uniformity.
4. Logically OR-ing the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final output.
