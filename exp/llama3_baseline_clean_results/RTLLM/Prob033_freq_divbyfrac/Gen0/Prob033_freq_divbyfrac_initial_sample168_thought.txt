To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, I will design a digital circuit that utilizes the double-edge clocking technique to achieve a fractional frequency division of 3.5x. The module will consist of a counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods. The counter will be used to generate phase-shifted versions of the divided clock, which will then be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output. I will use Verilog's always block to implement the counter and the phase-shifting logic.
