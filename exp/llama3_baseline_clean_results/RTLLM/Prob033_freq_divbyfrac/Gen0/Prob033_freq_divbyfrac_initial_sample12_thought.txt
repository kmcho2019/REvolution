The frequency divider module, freq_divbyfrac, is designed to divide the input clock frequency by a fractional value, specifically 3.5x in this case. It utilizes the double-edge clocking technique to achieve half-integer division and correct for duty cycle imbalance. The module operates by counting clock cycles and generating an intermediate divided clock signal with uneven periods, then phase-shifting and combining these signals to produce a smooth, fractionally divided clock output. Key steps include: 
1. Implementing a counter to cycle through a specified number of clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7 for 3.5x division).
2. Generating two intermediate clock signals with uneven periods (4 source clock cycles and 3 source clock cycles) based on the counter state.
3. Phase-shifting these clock signals by half a clock period in opposite directions.
4. Logically OR-ing the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractionally divided clock output, ensuring a uniform period.
The Verilog implementation will include a finite state machine (FSM) or a counter to control the generation of the intermediate clock signals and their phase-shifted versions, and logic to combine these signals for the final output.
