To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, we need to use a counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles. We then generate two intermediate clock signals with uneven periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. These clocks are then phase-shifted to ensure a smooth output. The phase-shifted clocks are logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output. We will use the double-edge clocking technique to achieve half-integer division and adjust for duty cycle imbalance.
