The freq_divbyfrac module will utilize the double-edge clocking technique to achieve fractional frequency division. It will employ a counter to keep track of the clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. For 3.5x division, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, producing two uneven clock periods of 4 and 3 source clock cycles. The phase-shifting technique will be used to generate two versions of the divided clock, one delayed and one advanced by half a clock period, which will then be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output. This approach ensures a uniform period for the divided clock signal.
