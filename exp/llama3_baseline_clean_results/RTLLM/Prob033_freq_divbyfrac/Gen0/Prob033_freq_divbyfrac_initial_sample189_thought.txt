To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values, we will utilize the double-edge clocking technique to achieve half-integer division while adjusting for duty cycle imbalance. The module, freq_divbyfrac, will take in an input clock signal, clk, and an active low reset signal, rst_n, and produce a fractionally divided clock output, clk_div. The fractional division will be achieved by counting clock cycles and generating an intermediate divided clock signal. Specifically, for a 3.5x division, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. Phase-shifted versions of the divided clock will then be generated, and the two intermediate clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring a uniform period.
