To achieve a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by a fractional value (3.5x), we will implement the following steps:
1. Define the multiplication factor and division factor for the fractional frequency division. 
2. Create a counter to cycle through the multiplication factor number of clock cycles.
3. Generate two intermediate clock signals with uneven clock periods based on the division factor.
4. Apply phase shifting to the intermediate clocks to ensure a smooth fractional clock output.
5. Logically OR the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
We'll use a counter, conditional statements, and logical operations to implement these steps in Verilog.
