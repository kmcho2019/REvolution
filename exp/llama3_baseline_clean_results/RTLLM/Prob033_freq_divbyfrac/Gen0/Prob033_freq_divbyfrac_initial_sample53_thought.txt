To design a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by a fractional value (3.5x), we'll use the double-edge clocking technique. The approach involves counting clock cycles to generate an intermediate divided clock signal. For 3.5x division, we'll have two uneven clock periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. These will be phase-shifted and logically combined to produce the final fractional divided clock output. The Verilog implementation will include counters, flip-flops for phase-shifting, and combinational logic for the final clock generation.
