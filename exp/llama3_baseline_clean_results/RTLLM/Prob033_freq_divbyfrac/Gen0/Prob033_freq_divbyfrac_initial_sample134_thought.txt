To design a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values, we need to utilize the double-edge clocking technique. This involves generating two intermediate clocks with uneven periods and then phase-shifting them to achieve a smooth fractional clock output. For a 3.5x division, we will use a counter that cycles through 7 clock cycles, generating one clock period with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. Then, we will phase-shift these clocks by half a clock period and logically OR them to produce the final output.

The key components of this design will be:
1. A counter to track the clock cycles.
2. Logic to generate the uneven clock periods based on the counter value.
3. Phase-shifting logic to delay and advance the intermediate clocks.
4. A final OR gate to combine the phase-shifted clocks into the fractional divided clock output.
