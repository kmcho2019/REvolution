To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, we'll utilize a counter to keep track of the input clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. We will employ the double-edge clocking technique to achieve the fractional division. For a 3.5x division, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods. We'll then phase-shift these periods to produce a smooth fractional clock output. The divided clock signal will be generated by logically OR-ing the phase-shifted versions of the intermediate clocks.
