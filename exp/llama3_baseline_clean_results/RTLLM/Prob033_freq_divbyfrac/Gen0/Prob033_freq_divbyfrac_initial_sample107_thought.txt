To design the freq_divbyfrac module, I will utilize the double-edge clocking technique to achieve a fractional frequency division of 3.5x. The module will count clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. It will then phase-shift the intermediate clocks and logically OR them to produce the final fractional divided clock output. This will involve using a counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating uneven clock periods, and implementing phase-shifted versions of the divided clock. The key components will be a counter, a signal generator for the intermediate clocks, and a logical OR gate to produce the final output.
