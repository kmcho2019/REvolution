The freq_divbyfrac module will utilize a combination of a counter and a state machine to achieve the desired fractional frequency division. It will count 7 clock cycles, generating two intermediate clocks with uneven periods (4 and 3 cycles), and then produce phase-shifted versions of these clocks. Finally, it will logically OR the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring a uniform period. The double-edge clocking technique will be employed to achieve half-integer division and adjust for duty cycle imbalance.
