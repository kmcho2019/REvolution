To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, I will use a counter to keep track of the clock cycles and generate the intermediate divided clock signals. The module will have two main components: a counter to cycle through the clock periods and a logic block to generate the phase-shifted clocks and the final output. The counter will be used to generate the uneven clock periods (4 source clock cycles and 3 source clock cycles) and to determine when to phase-shift the clocks. The logic block will use the counter output to generate the two intermediate clocks and then logically OR them to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
