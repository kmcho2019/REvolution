To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we'll employ the double-edge clocking technique. The idea is to generate two uneven clock periods and then phase-shift them to create a smooth fractional clock output. 1. Define the parameters for the frequency division ratio.
2. Initialize a counter to keep track of the clock cycles.
3. Generate two uneven clock periods based on the division ratio (4 source clock cycles and 3 source clock cycles for 3.5x division).
4. Phase-shift the divided clock signals to create a smooth fractional clock output.
5. Use logical operations (OR) to combine the phase-shifted clocks and produce the final fractional divided clock output.
