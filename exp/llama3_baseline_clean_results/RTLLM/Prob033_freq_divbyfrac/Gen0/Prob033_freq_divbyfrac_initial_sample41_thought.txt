To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values, I will use a counter-based approach with double-edge clocking to achieve half-integer division. The design will involve the following steps:

1. Define the parameters for the frequency division, such as the numerator and denominator for the fractional division ratio (3.5x).
2. Implement a counter that cycles through the specified number of clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7) to generate an intermediate divided clock signal.
3. Generate two uneven clock periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles.
4. Implement phase-shifting for the divided clock signals, delaying one phase by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period.
5. Logically OR the two phase-shifted divided clock signals to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring the divided clock signal has a uniform period.

The Verilog implementation will utilize a modular design with parameters for flexibility and testability.
