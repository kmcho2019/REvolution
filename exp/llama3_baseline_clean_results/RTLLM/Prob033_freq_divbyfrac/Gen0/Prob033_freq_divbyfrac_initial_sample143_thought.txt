To implement the frequency divider module, we will use the following approach:
- Initialize a counter to track the clock cycles.
- Set the counter to count up to 7 clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7) for 3.5x division.
- Generate an intermediate clock signal with two uneven periods: 4 clock cycles high and 3 clock cycles low.
- Create phase-shifted versions of the intermediate clock by delaying one by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period.
- Logically OR the two phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
- Use an active low reset signal to initialize the module.
