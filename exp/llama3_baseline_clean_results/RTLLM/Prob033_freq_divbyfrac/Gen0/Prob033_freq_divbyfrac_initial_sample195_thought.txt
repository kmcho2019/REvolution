To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), I will use the double-edge clocking technique. The module, freq_divbyfrac, will utilize a counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. This will be achieved by creating two uneven clock periods, one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. Then, phase-shifted versions of the divided clock will be generated by delaying one phase by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period. Finally, the two intermediate clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring the divided clock signal has a uniform period.
