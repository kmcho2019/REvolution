The freq_divbyfrac module will implement a fractional frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by 3.5. This will be achieved by utilizing the double-edge clocking technique to generate a smooth fractional clock output. The module will count clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. For 3.5x division, it will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. In the next cycle, phase-shifted versions of the divided clock will be generated, and finally, the two intermediate clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
