Finally, we'll logically OR the intermediate clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output with a uniform period.

1. A counter to cycle through 7 clock cycles.
2. Logic to generate the intermediate divided clock signals with uneven clock periods.
3. Logic to generate the phase-shifted versions of the intermediate clocks.
4. Logic to OR the intermediate clocks and produce the final fractional divided clock output.
