To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, we will use a counter to track the clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. The double-edge clocking technique will be utilized to achieve half-integer division. For 3.5x division, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods. We will then generate phase-shifted versions of the divided clock and logically OR them to produce the final fractional divided clock output. The implementation will consist of a finite state machine (FSM) to handle the different states of the counter and generate the desired clock outputs.
