To achieve a fractional frequency division of 3.5x, the module 'freq_divbyfrac' will use the double-edge clocking technique. The design will involve a counter that cycles through 7 clock cycles. Two uneven clock periods will be generated: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. Then, phase-shifted versions of these clocks will be produced by delaying one by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period. The final fractional divided clock output will be obtained by logically OR-ing these two phase-shifted clocks. This approach ensures the divided clock signal has a uniform period.
