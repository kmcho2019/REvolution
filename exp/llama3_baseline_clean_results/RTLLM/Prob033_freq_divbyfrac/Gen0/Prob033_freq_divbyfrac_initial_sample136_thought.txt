The frequency divider module, freq_divbyfrac, will utilize a counter to track the input clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. To achieve the fractional frequency division of 3.5x, we will divide the clock cycles into two uneven periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. The counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles. Double-edge clocking technique will be used to generate phase-shifted versions of the divided clock. The final divided clock output will be obtained by logically OR-ing the two intermediate clocks, ensuring a uniform period.
