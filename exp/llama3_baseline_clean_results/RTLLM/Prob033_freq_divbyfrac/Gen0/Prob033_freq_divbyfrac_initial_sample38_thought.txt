To implement the freq_divbyfrac module, I will utilize a counter to track the clock cycles and generate the intermediate divided clock signals. The double-edge clocking technique will be applied to achieve half-integer division and correct duty cycle imbalance. This involves counting through 7 clock cycles, producing uneven clock periods of 4 and 3 cycles, and then generating phase-shifted versions of these clocks. The phase-shifted clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output with a uniform period. 
The main components of this design include a counter, signal generators for the uneven clock periods, phase-shifting logic, and an OR gate to combine the phase-shifted clocks.
