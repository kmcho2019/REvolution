To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we will utilize the double-edge clocking technique. This involves creating two uneven clock periods, one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles, which are then phase-shifted and logically OR-ed to produce a smooth fractional clock output. The module will have a counter to keep track of the clock cycles, and the intermediate divided clock signals will be generated based on this counter. The phase-shifting will be achieved by delaying one signal by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period. The final fractional divided clock output will be the logical OR of these two phase-shifted signals.
