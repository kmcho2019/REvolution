The freq_divbyfrac module will be designed to achieve a 3.5x frequency division. It will utilize a counter to track the clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. The module will implement the double-edge clocking technique to divide the clock by uneven cycles (4 and 3) and adjust for duty cycle imbalance by phase-shifting the intermediate clocks. The final divided clock output will be generated by logically OR-ing the two phase-shifted intermediate clocks.

To implement this, the following steps will be taken:
- Define the parameters for the frequency division.
- Create a counter to track the clock cycles.
- Generate the intermediate divided clock signals with uneven clock periods (4 and 3 source clock cycles).
- Phase-shift the intermediate clocks by half a clock period.
- Logically OR the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.

The module will be designed to reset when the rst_n signal is low, ensuring proper initialization.
