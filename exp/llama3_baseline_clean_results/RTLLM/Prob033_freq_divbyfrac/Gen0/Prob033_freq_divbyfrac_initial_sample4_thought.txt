To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values (3.5x), we will utilize the double-edge clocking technique. The key steps involve:
1. Defining constants for the division ratio and the number of clock cycles to achieve the fractional division.
2. Implementing a counter to track the clock cycles and generate intermediate clock signals based on the division ratio.
3. Phase-shifting the intermediate clocks by half a clock period to ensure a uniform output period.
4. Logically combining the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractionally divided clock output.
The Verilog module will incorporate these steps to generate the desired fractional clock output.
