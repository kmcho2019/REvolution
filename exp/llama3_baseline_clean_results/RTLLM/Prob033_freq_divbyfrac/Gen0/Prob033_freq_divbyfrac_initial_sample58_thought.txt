To design the freq_divbyfrac module, we need to implement the following steps:
- Initialize a counter to track the input clock cycles.
- Generate an intermediate divided clock signal based on the fractional division ratio (3.5x).
- Create phase-shifted versions of the divided clock signal to achieve a smooth fractional clock output.
- Logically combine the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.

The key components are:
- A counter (cnt) to cycle through the required number of clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7).
- Two signals to track the generation of the uneven clock periods (div_clk_4 and div_clk_3).
- Two phase-shifted versions of the divided clock signal (div_clk_ph1 and div_clk_ph2).
- The final fractional divided clock output (clk_div) obtained by logically OR-ing the phase-shifted clocks.
