- Initialize the module with an active low reset.
- Create a counter that cycles through 7 clock cycles to achieve 3.5x frequency division.
- Generate two intermediate clocks with uneven periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles.
- Phase-shift these clocks by half a clock period in opposite directions to create two intermediate clocks.
- Logically OR the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
