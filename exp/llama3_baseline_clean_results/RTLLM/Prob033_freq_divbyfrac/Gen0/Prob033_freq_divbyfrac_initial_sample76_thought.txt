The frequency divider module, freq_divbyfrac, will utilize a counter to track the clock cycles. It will count up to MUL2_DIV_CLK (7 for 3.5x division) and then reset. Two intermediate clock signals will be generated with uneven periods (4 and 3 clock cycles for 3.5x division). To achieve the fractional division, these clocks will be phase-shifted by half a clock cycle and then logically OR-ed to produce the final divided clock output. The module will also include an active-low reset signal to initialize it.
