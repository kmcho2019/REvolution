To achieve fractional frequency division, we'll implement a counter to track the clock cycles and generate the intermediate divided clock signals. We will then use these intermediate clocks, along with their phase-shifted versions, to produce the final fractionally divided clock output. The key steps involve: 
1. Counting the clock cycles to determine when to generate the divided clock pulses.
2. Generating the intermediate divided clocks with uneven periods.
3. Phase-shifting these intermediate clocks by half a clock period.
4. Logically combining the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional clock output.

The division by 3.5 is achieved by having a total cycle of 7 clocks, with 4 clocks in one period and 3 in the next, effectively averaging out to 3.5 over the entire cycle. This is implemented using two counters and logic to handle the phase-shifting and ORing of the signals.
