The module freq_divbyfrac will be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: one for the 4-clock cycle period and another for the 3-clock cycle period. The FSM will use a counter to keep track of the clock cycles. To achieve the fractional frequency division of 3.5x, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles. The divided clock will be generated based on the current state of the FSM. For the double-edge clocking technique, the module will use two intermediate clock signals: one for the original divided clock and another for the phase-shifted divided clock. These two clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
