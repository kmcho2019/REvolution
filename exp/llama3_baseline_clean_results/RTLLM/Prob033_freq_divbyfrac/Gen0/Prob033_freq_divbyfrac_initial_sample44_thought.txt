The Verilog module freq_divbyfrac will be designed to perform fractional frequency division. It will count the input clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. For a 3.5x division, the counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating two uneven clock periods. The phase of the divided clock will be shifted to produce a smooth fractional clock output. The final output will be obtained by logically OR-ing the phase-shifted intermediate clocks.

The module will have the following components:
- A counter to count the input clock cycles.
- A finite state machine (FSM) to generate the uneven clock periods and control the phase shifting.
- A phase-shifting circuit to delay and advance the divided clock phases.
- A logical OR gate to combine the phase-shifted clocks and produce the final output.

The module will be reset using the active low reset signal rst_n.
