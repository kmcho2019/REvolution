To achieve the fractional frequency division of 3.5x, the design utilizes a double-edge clocking technique. This involves counting the input clock cycles and generating intermediate divided clock signals with uneven periods. By phase-shifting these intermediate clocks and logically OR-ing them, the final fractional divided clock output is produced. 
The implementation includes the following key components: 
1. A counter to track the clock cycles and generate the intermediate divided clock signals. 
2. A phase-shifting mechanism to delay and advance the intermediate clocks by half a clock period. 
3. A logical OR operation to combine the phase-shifted clocks and produce the final fractional divided clock output.
To maintain a uniform period for the divided clock signal, the design adjusts for duty cycle imbalance by using the double-edge clocking technique.
