To achieve the fractional frequency division of 3.5x, the freq_divbyfrac module utilizes a double-edge clocking technique. This technique involves counting clock cycles and generating an intermediate divided clock signal. The counter cycles through 7 clock cycles, producing two uneven clock periods of 4 source clock cycles and 3 source clock cycles. Then, it generates phase-shifted versions of the divided clock by delaying one phase by half a clock period and advancing the other by half a clock period. Finally, the two intermediate clocks are logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring a uniform period.

The design requires the following steps:

1. Initialize counters for the clock cycle count and the phase-shifting mechanism.
2. Generate the intermediate divided clock signals based on the counter values.
3. Create phase-shifted versions of the divided clock signals using the double-edge clocking technique.
4. Combine the phase-shifted clocks to produce the final fractional divided clock output.
