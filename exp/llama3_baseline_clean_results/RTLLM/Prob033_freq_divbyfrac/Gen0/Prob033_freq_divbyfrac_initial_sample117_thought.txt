The frequency divider module, freq_divbyfrac, will utilize the double-edge clocking technique to achieve a fractional frequency division of 3.5x. It will count 7 clock cycles and generate two uneven clock periods: one with 4 source clock cycles and another with 3 source clock cycles. To maintain a uniform period for the divided clock, it will create phase-shifted versions of the divided clock signals. One signal will be delayed by half a clock period, and the other will be advanced by half a clock period. The final fractional divided clock output will be produced by logically OR-ing these two intermediate clocks.
