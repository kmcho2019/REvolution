To implement the frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values, we will use a counter to count the clock cycles and generate an intermediate divided clock signal. The double-edge clocking technique will be employed to achieve half-integer division while adjusting for duty cycle imbalance. The two intermediate clocks will be logically OR-ed to produce the final fractional divided clock output, ensuring the divided clock signal has a uniform period.
