The float_multi module will be designed as a synchronous digital circuit, utilizing the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision floating-point representation. It will perform high-precision multiplication of 32-bit floating-point numbers. The module will handle special cases like NaN and infinity, and it will also perform normalization, rounding, and adjustment of the result to ensure accurate representation. The overall architecture will be divided into several stages, including input processing, special case handling, normalization, multiplication, rounding, and output generation. Each stage will be carefully implemented to adhere to the IEEE 754 standard.
