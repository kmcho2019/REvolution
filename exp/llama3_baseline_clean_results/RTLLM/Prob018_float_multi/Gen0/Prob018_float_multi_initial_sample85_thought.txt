To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for the IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we will design a Verilog module named float_multi. The module will perform high-precision multiplication of 32-bit single-precision floating-point numbers following the IEEE 754 standard. 

The float_multi module will take two 32-bit floating-point numbers (a and b) as input, a clock signal (clk), and a reset signal (rst). It will produce the result of the multiplication (z) in IEEE 754 format.

The module's operation will be divided into several stages: 
1. Initialization: Reset the counter on the rst signal.
2. Input Processing: Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of inputs a and b.
3. Special Cases Handling: Identify special cases like NaN and infinity based on the inputs.
4. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if needed.
5. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents.
6. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent.
7. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

To achieve accurate arithmetic operations, we will utilize various internal signals such as counter, mantissas, exponents, sign bits, product, and rounding control bits.
