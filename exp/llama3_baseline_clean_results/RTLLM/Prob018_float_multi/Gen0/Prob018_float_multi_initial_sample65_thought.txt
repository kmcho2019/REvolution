To implement the float_multi module for 32-bit single-precision floating-point multiplication following the IEEE 754 standard, we will break down the process into several key steps. 
1. Initialization: Reset the internal state upon receiving a reset signal.
2. Input Processing: Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input operands.
3. Special Cases Handling: Identify and handle special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
4. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if they are denormalized.
5. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas and adjust the exponents accordingly, considering the signs of the operands.
6. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the product to fit the 24-bit mantissa and adjust the exponent to ensure the result is normalized.
7. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 single-precision format, handling overflow and underflow scenarios.

Given the complexity of floating-point arithmetic, each step must adhere to the IEEE 754 standard for accuracy and compatibility.
