First, we initialize the internal signals and extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers. We then handle special cases such as NaN and infinity, and proceed to normalize the mantissas if necessary. The multiplication of the mantissas is performed, and the signs and exponents are adjusted accordingly. After rounding the result to ensure precision, we adjust the exponent further if necessary and finally format the output in IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow scenarios.
