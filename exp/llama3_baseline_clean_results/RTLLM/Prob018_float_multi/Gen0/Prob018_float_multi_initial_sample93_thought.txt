To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision arithmetic, the design should involve several key steps: 
1. Extraction of mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input operands.
2. Handling of special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
3. Normalization of mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiplication of mantissas, combining signs, and adjusting exponents.
5. Rounding and adjustment of the result to ensure accurate representation.
6. Formatting the final result in IEEE 754 standard, considering overflow and underflow scenarios.

The module will utilize a state machine (controlled by a counter) to sequence these operations across multiple clock cycles.
