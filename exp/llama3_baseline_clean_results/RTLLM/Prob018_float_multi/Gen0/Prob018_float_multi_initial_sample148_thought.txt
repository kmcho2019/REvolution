The module float_multi will be implemented in several stages, starting with input processing and extraction of mantissas, exponents, and signs from the input operands a and b. It will handle special cases like NaN and infinity, followed by normalization of the mantissas if necessary. The mantissas will be multiplied, and the signs and exponents will be combined accordingly. The result will be rounded and adjusted to fit the IEEE 754 single-precision format, handling overflow and underflow conditions. The output will be generated in the IEEE 754 format, ensuring accurate representation of the multiplication result.
