To implement the 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we need to break down the operation into several stages: 
1. Input Processing: Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Special Cases Handling: Identify and handle special cases such as NaN and infinity.
3. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if needed to ensure accurate multiplication.
4. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents according to the IEEE 754 standard rules.
5. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent to maintain precision and representation accuracy.
6. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, considering overflow and underflow scenarios.

We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to sequence these operations across multiple clock cycles, utilizing a counter to keep track of the current operation stage. 
The module will also maintain internal signals for the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of both inputs and the result, as well as control signals for rounding and normalization.
