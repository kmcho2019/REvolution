It should then handle special cases, normalize the mantissas, perform multiplication, round the result, and adjust the exponent to ensure the output is in the IEEE 754 standard format. The internal operations are sequenced using a cycle counter to manage the pipeline of operations.

The code must include:
1. Extraction of mantissa, exponent, and sign bits from the input operands.
2. Handling of special cases (NaN, infinity).
3. Normalization of mantissas.
4. Multiplication of mantissas and adjustment of the exponent.
5. Rounding of the result.
6. Adjustment of the exponent for the final output.
7. Output formatting according to the IEEE 754 standard.
