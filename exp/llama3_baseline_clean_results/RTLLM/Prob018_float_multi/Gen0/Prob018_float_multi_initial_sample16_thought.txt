To implement the 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we need to break down the operation into several steps:
1. Input Processing: Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input numbers.
2. Special Cases Handling: Identify special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the inputs.
3. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if needed.
4. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents.
5. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
6. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.
We will use a cycle counter to sequence these operations and ensure proper timing.
