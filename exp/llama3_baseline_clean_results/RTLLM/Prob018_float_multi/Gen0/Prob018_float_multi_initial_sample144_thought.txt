To implement the float_multi module, we will follow a step-by-step approach:
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handle special cases such as NaN and infinity according to the IEEE 754 standard.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary to ensure correct multiplication.
4. Multiply the mantissas, combining their signs and adjusting the exponents.
5. Perform rounding and adjust the exponent to maintain accurate representation.
6. Format the result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow conditions.
We will use Verilog's built-in data types and operators to perform these steps, utilizing always blocks for sequential logic and combinational logic for the multiplication and rounding processes.
