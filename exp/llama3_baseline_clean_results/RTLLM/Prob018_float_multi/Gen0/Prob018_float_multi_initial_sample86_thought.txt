To implement the float_multi module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input numbers.
2. Check for special cases like NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas, combining the signs and adjusting the exponents.
5. Round the result and adjust the exponent.
6. Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard.

We'll use Verilog's arithmetic and logical operators to perform the necessary operations. The module will have an internal counter to sequence the operations over multiple clock cycles.
