The design involves several key steps to implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier in compliance with the IEEE 754 standard. Initially, the module extracts the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers, a and b. It then checks for special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity. Following this, the mantissas are normalized if necessary. The multiplication of the mantissas is performed, considering the signs and adjusting the exponents accordingly. The result is then rounded and the exponent is adjusted to ensure accurate representation. Finally, the output is formatted according to the IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow scenarios appropriately.
