To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE 754 standard, the design involves several key steps. First, the inputs are processed to extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits. Special cases like NaN and infinity are identified and handled accordingly. The mantissas are then normalized if necessary. After normalization, the mantissas are multiplied, and the signs and exponents are combined and adjusted. The result is then rounded and adjusted to ensure it conforms to the IEEE 754 standard. Finally, the result is formatted and outputted, taking care of overflow and underflow scenarios.
