To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for the IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, the design should follow these steps:
1. **Initialization**: Reset the internal state upon receiving the reset signal.
2. **Input Processing**: Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input operands.
3. **Special Cases Handling**: Identify special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
4. **Normalization**: Normalize the mantissas if they are not already in the normalized form (i.e., the most significant bit is 1).
5. **Multiplication**: Multiply the mantissas and adjust the exponent accordingly.
6. **Rounding and Adjustment**: Apply rounding rules and adjust the exponent as necessary to ensure the result is accurately represented.
7. **Output Generation**: Format the final result according to the IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow scenarios.
The implementation will utilize the clock signal for synchronization and the reset signal to reset the module's state.
