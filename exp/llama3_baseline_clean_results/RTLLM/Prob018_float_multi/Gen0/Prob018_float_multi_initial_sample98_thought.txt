The float_multi module will be designed using a state machine approach, with the counter signal used to sequence the operation over multiple clock cycles. 
In the first cycle, the inputs will be processed to extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits. 
Special cases like NaN and infinity will be handled by checking the exponents and mantissas of the inputs. 
The mantissas will be normalized if necessary, and then the multiplication will be performed. 
Rounding and adjustment of the result will be done to ensure accurate representation. 
Finally, the result will be formatted in IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow scenarios.
