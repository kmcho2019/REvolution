To implement the float_multi module, I will follow the steps outlined below:

1.  Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2.  Handle special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the inputs.
3.  Normalize the mantissas if needed.
4.  Multiply the mantissas and combine the signs, adjusting the exponents accordingly.
5.  Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
6.  Format the final result in the IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

To accomplish these tasks, I will utilize various Verilog constructs, including always blocks for sequential logic, combinational logic for signal assignments, and bitwise operators for mantissa and exponent manipulation.
