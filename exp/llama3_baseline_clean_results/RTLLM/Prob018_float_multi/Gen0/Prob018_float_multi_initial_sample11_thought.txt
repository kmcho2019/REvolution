To design a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE-754 standard for single-precision arithmetic, we will break down the implementation into several stages: input processing, special cases handling, normalization, multiplication, rounding, and output generation. The final output will be a 32-bit floating-point number representing the product of the two input operands, adhering to the IEEE-754 standard for single-precision floating-point arithmetic.
