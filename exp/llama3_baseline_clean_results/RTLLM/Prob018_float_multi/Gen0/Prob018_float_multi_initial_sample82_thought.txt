To implement the float_multi module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handle special cases such as NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas and combine the signs.
5. Adjust the exponent based on the multiplication result.
6. Round the result and adjust the exponent accordingly.
7. Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow scenarios.
We'll use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to sequence these operations over multiple clock cycles.
