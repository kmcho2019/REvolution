To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE 754 standard, we will break down the process into several steps:
1. Input Processing: Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Special Cases Handling: Identify special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
3. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combining their signs and adjusting the exponents.
5. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
6. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

The module will utilize a finite state machine to control the sequence of operations, ensuring that each step is completed before proceeding to the next one.
