4. **Multiplication**: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs of the two input numbers to determine the sign of the result, and calculate the new exponent. The new exponent will be the sum of the exponents of the two input numbers minus a bias (127 for single precision).

5. **Rounding and Adjustment**: Round the product to 24 bits (the size of the mantissa in single-precision floating-point format), and adjust the exponent accordingly if necessary. This involves checking for overflow and underflow conditions.

6. **Output Generation**: Finally, format the result in IEEE 754 standard, including handling any overflow or underflow by adjusting the exponent and possibly the mantissa.
