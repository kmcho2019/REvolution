The float_multi module will be designed to handle IEEE 754 single-precision floating-point multiplication. 
The module will first extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input operands. 
Then, it will check for special cases such as NaN and infinity. 
After that, the mantissas will be normalized if needed. 
Next, the mantissas will be multiplied, and the signs will be combined. 
The exponents will be adjusted accordingly. 
Finally, the result will be rounded and adjusted to ensure accurate representation, and the final result will be formatted in IEEE 754 standard.
