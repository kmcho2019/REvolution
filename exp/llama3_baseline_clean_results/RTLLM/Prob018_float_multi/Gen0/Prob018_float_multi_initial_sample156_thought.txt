To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we will follow the steps outlined in the problem statement. 
The design will involve several stages: 
1. Initialization: Reset the internal counter and registers on the reset signal.
2. Input Processing: Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers during the first clock cycle.
3. Special Cases Handling: Identify special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the inputs.
4. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if necessary to ensure accurate multiplication.
5. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combining the signs and adjusting the exponents.
6. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
7. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

The module will utilize various internal signals to manage these stages, including a cycle counter, mantissas, exponents, sign bits, and rounding control bits.
