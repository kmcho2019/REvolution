To design the float_multi module, we will follow the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision floating-point numbers. The design involves several steps: 
1. Extraction of the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handling of special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
3. Normalization of the mantissas if needed.
4. Multiplication of the mantissas, combining the signs, and adjusting the exponents.
5. Rounding the result and adjusting the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
6. Formatting the final result in the IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.
