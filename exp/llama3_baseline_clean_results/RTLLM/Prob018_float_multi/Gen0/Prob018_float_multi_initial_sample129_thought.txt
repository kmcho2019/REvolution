To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we will follow these steps:
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input numbers.
2. Handle special cases like NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents.
5. Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
