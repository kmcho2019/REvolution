The implementation of a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE 754 standard involves several key steps: 
1. Initialization: Reset the counter and internal signals upon receiving the reset signal.
2. Input Processing: Extract the mantissa, exponent, and sign bit from the input floating-point numbers.
3. Special Cases Handling: Identify and handle special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
4. Normalization: Normalize the mantissas if necessary to ensure proper representation.
5. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas and adjust the exponents accordingly, taking into account the signs of the inputs.
6. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the product and adjust the exponent to maintain accuracy and adhere to the IEEE 754 format.
7. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing potential overflow and underflow scenarios.

To achieve this, the module will utilize internal registers to store the extracted components of the inputs and the intermediate results. It will also employ a counter to sequence the operations across multiple clock cycles.
