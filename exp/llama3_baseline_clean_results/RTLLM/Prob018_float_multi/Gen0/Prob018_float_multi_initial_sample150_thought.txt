To implement the float_multi module, we need to follow the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision floating-point numbers. This involves several steps: extracting mantissas, exponents, and sign bits; handling special cases; normalizing mantissas; multiplying mantissas and adjusting exponents; rounding the result; and generating the final output. We will utilize Verilog's built-in data types and operators to perform these operations efficiently. The design will be highly sequential, with each step occurring on a specific clock cycle, to ensure accurate and synchronized operation.
