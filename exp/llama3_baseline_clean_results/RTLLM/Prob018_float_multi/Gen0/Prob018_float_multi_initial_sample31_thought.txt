To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier in Verilog for the IEEE-754 standard, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the inputs.
2. Identify special cases such as NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents.
5. Round the result and adjust the exponent for accurate representation.
6. Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, handling overflow and underflow.

This design requires several internal signals to manage the operation sequencing, mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of the inputs and output, as well as intermediate results and control bits for rounding.
