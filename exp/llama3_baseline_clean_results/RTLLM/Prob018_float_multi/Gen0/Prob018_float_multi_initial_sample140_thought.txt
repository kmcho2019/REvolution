The float_multi module will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with several states to handle different stages of the multiplication process, including input processing, special case handling, normalization, multiplication, rounding, and output generation. The module will utilize various internal signals to extract and manipulate the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of the input operands. The multiplication of mantissas will be performed using a multiplier, and the result will be rounded and adjusted according to the IEEE 754 standard. The final result will be formatted in IEEE 754 standard and output through the z port.
