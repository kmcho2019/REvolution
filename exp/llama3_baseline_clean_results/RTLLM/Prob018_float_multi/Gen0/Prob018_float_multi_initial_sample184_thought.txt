The float_multi module will be designed as a finite state machine with multiple stages for input processing, special cases handling, normalization, multiplication, rounding, and output generation. 
The mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of inputs a and b will be extracted during the first clock cycle. 
The module will identify special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the inputs.
The mantissas will be normalized if needed.
The mantissas will be multiplied, combining the signs and adjusting the exponents.
The module will round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
The final result will be formatted in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.
