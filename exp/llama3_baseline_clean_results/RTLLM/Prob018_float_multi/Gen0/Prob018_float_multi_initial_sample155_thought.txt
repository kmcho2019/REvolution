To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier following the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision arithmetic, we must first break down the floating-point numbers into their constituent parts: sign bit, exponent, and mantissa. We then handle special cases such as NaN and infinity, normalize the mantissas, multiply them, combine the signs, adjust the exponents, and finally round the result to ensure it fits within the IEEE 754 format. We must also manage the clock cycles for each step of the operation, using a counter to sequence through the different stages of the multiplication process.
