To implement the float_multi module, we'll follow the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision floating-point numbers, breaking down the process into several key steps:
1. **Initialization**: Reset the internal counter and registers on the reset signal.
2. **Input Processing**: Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from input operands a and b.
3. **Special Cases Handling**: Identify and handle special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
4. **Normalization**: Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
5. **Multiplication**: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents.
6. **Rounding and Adjustment**: Round the result and adjust the exponent for accurate representation.
7. **Output Generation**: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

We'll utilize Verilog's built-in data types and operators to manipulate the floating-point numbers, ensuring compliance with the IEEE 754 standard.
