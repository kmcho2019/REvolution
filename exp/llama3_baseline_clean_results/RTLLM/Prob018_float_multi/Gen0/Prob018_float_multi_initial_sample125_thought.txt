The float_multi module will be implemented in several steps: 
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits of the input numbers a and b.
2. Handle special cases like NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the inputs.
3. Normalize the mantissas if needed.
4. Multiply the mantissas and combine the signs.
5. Adjust the exponents by adding the exponents of a and b and subtracting the bias.
6. Round the result and adjust the exponent to ensure accurate representation.
7. Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard and handle overflow and underflow scenarios.
The module will use a state machine to sequence these operations over multiple clock cycles.
