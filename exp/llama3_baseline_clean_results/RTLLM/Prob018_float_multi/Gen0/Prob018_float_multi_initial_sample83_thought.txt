To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic, we will break down the operation into several steps. These include input processing, handling special cases like NaN and infinity, normalization of mantissas, multiplication of mantissas, rounding, and finally, formatting the result according to the IEEE 754 standard. The module will have a cycle counter for sequencing these operations and will use internal signals to hold intermediate results. The focus will be on correctly handling the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits to ensure accurate multiplication following the IEEE 754 standard.
