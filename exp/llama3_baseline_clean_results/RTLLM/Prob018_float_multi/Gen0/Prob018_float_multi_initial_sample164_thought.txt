To implement the float_multi module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handle special cases such as NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas and adjust the exponents.
5. Round the result and adjust the exponent.
6. Generate the final output in IEEE 754 standard format, considering overflow and underflow scenarios.
