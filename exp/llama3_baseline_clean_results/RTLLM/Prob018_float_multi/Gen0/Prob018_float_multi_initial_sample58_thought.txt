To implement the float_multi module, we will follow the IEEE 754 standard for single-precision floating-point numbers. The design involves several key steps: 
1. Extracting mantissa, exponent, and sign from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handling special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity.
3. Normalizing the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiplying the mantissas and adjusting the exponents.
5. Rounding the product and adjusting the exponent.
6. Formatting the result according to the IEEE 754 standard, including handling overflow and underflow.
We will use a cycle counter (counter) to sequence these operations across multiple clock cycles, ensuring that each step completes before proceeding to the next.
