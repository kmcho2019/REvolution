To implement the float_multi module, we first need to extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers a and b. Then, we handle special cases like NaN and infinity. After that, we normalize the mantissas if necessary and perform the multiplication, adjusting the exponents accordingly. We also need to implement rounding and adjustment of the result to ensure accurate representation in IEEE 754 format. Finally, we format the final result, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.
