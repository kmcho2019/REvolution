1. Initialize the module by resetting the counter on the reset signal.
2. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers during the first clock cycle.
3. Identify special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the input values.
4. Normalize the mantissas if necessary to ensure they adhere to the IEEE-754 representation.
5. Multiply the mantissas, considering the signs of the inputs to determine the sign of the result, and adjust the exponent accordingly.
6. Perform rounding on the product to fit the result into the 32-bit format, which may involve adding a guard bit, round bit, and sticky bit for proper rounding.
7. Finally, format the result in the IEEE-754 standard, handling any overflow or underflow conditions that may have arisen from the multiplication.
