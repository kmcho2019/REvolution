To implement the float_multi module, we will follow these steps:
1. Extract the mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the input floating-point numbers.
2. Handle special cases such as NaN and infinity.
3. Normalize the mantissas if necessary.
4. Multiply the mantissas, combining the signs and adjusting the exponents.
5. Round the result and adjust the exponent for accurate representation.
6. Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.

The module will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to sequence the operations over multiple clock cycles.
