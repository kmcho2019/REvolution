To implement a 32-bit floating-point multiplier in Verilog for the IEEE-754 standard, we will follow a structured approach:

1. **Initialization**: Reset the module's internal state, including the cycle counter and output registers, when the reset signal (rst) is high.

2. **Input Processing**: Extract the sign bits, exponents, and mantissas from the input floating-point numbers a and b during the first clock cycle.

3. **Special Cases Handling**: Identify special cases such as NaN (Not a Number) and infinity based on the input values and handle them according to the IEEE 754 standard.

4. **Normalization**: Normalize the mantissas if necessary to ensure they are in the correct format for multiplication.

5. **Multiplication**: Multiply the mantissas, combining their signs and adjusting the exponents accordingly.

6. **Rounding and Adjustment**: Round the product to the nearest representable floating-point number, adjusting the exponent as necessary to maintain accuracy and handle overflow or underflow conditions.

7. **Output Generation**: Format the final result in the IEEE 754 standard, taking into account any special cases, overflows, or underflows that may have occurred during the operation.
