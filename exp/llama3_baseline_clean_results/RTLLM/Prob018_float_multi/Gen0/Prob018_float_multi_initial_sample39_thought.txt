To design the float_multi module for 32-bit floating-point multiplication following the IEEE 754 standard, we will break down the implementation into several key stages:
1. Initialization: Reset the internal counter and registers on the rst signal.
2. Input Processing: Extract mantissas, exponents, and sign bits from the inputs a and b during the first clock cycle.
3. Special Cases Handling: Identify and handle special cases such as NaN and infinity.
4. Normalization: Ensure mantissas are normalized if necessary.
5. Multiplication: Multiply the mantissas, combine the signs, and adjust the exponents accordingly.
6. Rounding and Adjustment: Round the result and adjust the exponent to maintain accurate representation.
7. Output Generation: Format the final result in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios.
We will utilize Verilog's reg and wire data types to declare the necessary signals and variables, and employ the always block for combinatorial and sequential logic.
