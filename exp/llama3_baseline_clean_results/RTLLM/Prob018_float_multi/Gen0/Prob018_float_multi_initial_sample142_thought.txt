The float_multi module is designed to perform high-precision multiplication of 32-bit single-precision floating-point numbers following the IEEE 754 standard. The module will be implemented with a finite state machine (FSM) to sequence the operation. In the first state, the inputs are processed, and special cases like NaN and infinity are handled. In the subsequent states, the mantissas are normalized, multiplied, and rounded. The result is then formatted in IEEE 754 standard, addressing overflow and underflow scenarios. The FSM will ensure accurate arithmetic operations essential for various computational applications.
