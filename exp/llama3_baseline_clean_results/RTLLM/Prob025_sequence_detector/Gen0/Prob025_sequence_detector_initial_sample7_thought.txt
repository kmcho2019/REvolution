To implement the sequence detector module, we'll use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. We'll start in the IDLE state and transition between states based on the input bitstream data_in. We'll check for a match of the specific sequence '1001' and assert the output sequence_detected when the complete sequence is detected. The detector will be synchronized with the clock signal clk and reset to the IDLE state when reset_n is low. The state transitions will be as follows:
- IDLE to S1: when data_in is '1'
- S1 to S2: when data_in is '0'
- S2 to S3: when data_in is '0'
- S3 to S4: when data_in is '1'
- S4 to IDLE: sequence detected, assert sequence_detected
- Reset: transition to IDLE when reset_n is low
