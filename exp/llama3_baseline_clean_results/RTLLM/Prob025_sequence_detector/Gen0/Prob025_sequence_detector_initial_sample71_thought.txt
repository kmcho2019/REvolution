The sequence detector will be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The FSM will transition between states based on the input data_in. The detector will start in the IDLE state, waiting for the first '1' to transition to S1. Then, it will wait for a '0' to transition to S2, followed by another '0' to transition to S3, and finally, a '1' to transition to S4, where the sequence_detected signal will be asserted. The reset_n signal will be used to reset the FSM to the IDLE state when it is high.
