The sequence detector will be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The FSM will transition through these states based on the input data_in. On each clock cycle, the detector will check for a match of the specific sequence 1001. The sequence_detected output will be asserted when the FSM reaches the S4 state, indicating that the complete sequence has been detected. The reset_n signal will be used to reset the FSM to the IDLE state when it is high.
