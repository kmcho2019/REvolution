To implement the sequence detector module, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The FSM will transition through these states based on the input data_in signal. When the reset signal reset_n is low, the FSM will reset to the IDLE state. The sequence_detected signal will be asserted when the FSM reaches the S4 state, indicating that the specific sequence 1001 has been detected. We will use a case statement to handle the different states and transition logic.
