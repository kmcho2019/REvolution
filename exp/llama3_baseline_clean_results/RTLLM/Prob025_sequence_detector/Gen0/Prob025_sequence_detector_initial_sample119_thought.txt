To implement the sequence detector module, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions between states based on the input bitstream. The FSM will have five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The detector will start in the IDLE state and transition to subsequent states as it detects the specific sequence 1001. When the complete sequence is detected, the output sequence_detected will be asserted high. The detector will also include a reset mechanism to return to the IDLE state when reset_n is low.
