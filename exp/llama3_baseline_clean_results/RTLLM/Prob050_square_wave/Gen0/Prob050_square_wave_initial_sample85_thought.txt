To design the square_wave module, we will first initialize the count register and wave_out signal. We'll use a counter that increments on each rising edge of the clk. When the count reaches (freq - 1), we'll reset the count to 0 and toggle the wave_out signal. Otherwise, the count simply increments by one on the next clock cycle. The wave_out signal will be toggled at the specified frequency, thus generating a square wave. We'll also handle the case where freq is 0 to prevent division by zero.
