To implement the square_wave module, we will utilize a counter-based approach. The counter register, 'count', will increment on every rising edge of the clock signal 'clk'. Once 'count' reaches the specified frequency value 'freq' minus one, it will be reset to zero and the output square wave signal 'wave_out' will be toggled. This effectively creates a square wave with a frequency determined by the 'freq' input. We'll also include a sensitivity list to ensure proper synthesis and simulation behavior.
