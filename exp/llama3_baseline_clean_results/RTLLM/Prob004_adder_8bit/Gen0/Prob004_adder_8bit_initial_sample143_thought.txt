To design an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum and a carry-out. The carry-out of one full adder serves as the carry-in for the next full adder in the chain. This approach allows us to perform the addition operation bit by bit, from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). We'll use this methodology to implement the 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit'.
