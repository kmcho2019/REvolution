To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple carry configuration. Each full adder will handle the addition of one bit from each of the input operands (a and b) along with the carry input from the previous bit addition. The carry output from each full adder will be passed as the carry input to the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. This will enable the addition of the two 8-bit operands bit by bit.
