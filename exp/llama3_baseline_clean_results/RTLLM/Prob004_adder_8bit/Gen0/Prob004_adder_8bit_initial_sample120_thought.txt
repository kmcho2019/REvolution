To implement an 8-bit adder, we will use a series of full adders, each performing a single bit addition. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each full adder is used as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder. We'll start from the least significant bit (LSB) and move towards the most significant bit (MSB), using the carry-in for the first full adder as the module's cin input.
