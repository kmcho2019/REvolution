To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with the carry from the previous bit position, and produce the corresponding bit of the sum along with the carry to the next bit position. The module will have 8 full adders, one for each bit position, and the carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. The final carry output will be the carry-out of the most significant bit full adder.
