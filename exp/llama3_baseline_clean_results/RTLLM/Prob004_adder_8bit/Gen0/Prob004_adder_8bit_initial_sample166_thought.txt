To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will add corresponding bits of the two input operands, 'a' and 'b', along with the carry-in from the previous bit position. The sum and carry-out of each full adder will be used to generate the final sum and carry-out of the 8-bit adder. This approach allows us to leverage the basic full adder logic to perform multi-bit addition in a straightforward manner.
