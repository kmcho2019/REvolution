To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform a bit-level addition of the corresponding bits from the two input operands, along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder to compute the correct sum. The module will have two 8-bit input ports for the operands, a 1-bit input port for the carry-in, an 8-bit output port for the sum, and a 1-bit output port for the carry-out.
