To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can use a series of full adders, where each full adder is responsible for adding one bit from each input operand (a and b) along with a carry-in bit. The carry-out from one full adder becomes the carry-in for the next full adder. This forms a ripple-carry adder architecture. The module will have input ports for the two 8-bit operands (a and b), a carry-in port, output ports for the 8-bit sum, and a carry-out port. We will use Verilog's built-in full adder module or design our own full adder module to simplify the implementation.
