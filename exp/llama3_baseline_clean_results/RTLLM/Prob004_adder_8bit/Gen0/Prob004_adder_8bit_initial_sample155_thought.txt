To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in input. The carry-out output from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder to perform the addition operation. 
We will design a full adder module first, which will be instantiated eight times in the main adder_8bit module, each handling the addition of one bit from the operands A and B.
The full adder module will have three inputs (a, b, cin) and two outputs (sum, cout), representing the sum and carry-out respectively.
In the adder_8bit module, we will connect the full adders in series, with the carry-out output from each full adder connected to the carry-in input of the next full adder.
