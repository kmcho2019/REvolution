To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of bit-level adders, also known as full adders, in a combinational logic structure. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum output bit and a carry-out output. The carry-out from one full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the addition to ripple through the bits. We will define a separate module for the full adder and then instantiate it eight times within the adder_8bit module to handle each bit of the input operands.
