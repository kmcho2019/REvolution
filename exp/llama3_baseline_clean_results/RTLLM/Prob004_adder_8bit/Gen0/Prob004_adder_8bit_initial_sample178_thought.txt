To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in input, producing a sum bit and a carry-out output. The carry-out output from each full adder is connected to the carry-in input of the next full adder, thus propagating the carry through the adder chain. This approach allows us to perform the addition operation bit by bit, from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
