To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a chain, where each full adder performs the addition of two bits from the input operands A and B along with a carry-in input. The carry-out of each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This approach allows us to compute the sum and carry-out for each bit position from least significant bit (LSB) to most significant bit (MSB). We will start with the LSB (bit 0) and propagate the carry through each full adder until we reach the MSB (bit 7).
