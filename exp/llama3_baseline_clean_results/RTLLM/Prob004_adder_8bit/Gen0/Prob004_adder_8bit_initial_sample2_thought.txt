To implement an 8-bit adder module in Verilog, we can use a series of full adders to perform the addition operation bit by bit. Each full adder takes in two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from one full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder to propagate the carry through all the bits. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed correctly, considering the carry from each bit position to the next.

The module will have two 8-bit input ports (a and b), a 1-bit carry-in port (cin), an 8-bit output port (sum), and a 1-bit carry-out port (cout). We will instantiate eight full adders, one for each bit position, connecting them in a chain to propagate the carry from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).
