To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders to perform the addition operation bit by bit. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through all 8 bits. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed correctly, handling both the sum and carry for each bit position.
