To implement an 8-bit adder, we will utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. Each full adder will take two input bits from the 8-bit operands A and B, along with a carry-in input. The full adder will then produce a sum output bit and a carry-out output bit. We will chain these full adders together, with the carry-out of each full adder serving as the carry-in for the next full adder, thus allowing the carry to propagate through the entire 8-bit adder. The final carry-out from the most significant bit full adder will serve as the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
