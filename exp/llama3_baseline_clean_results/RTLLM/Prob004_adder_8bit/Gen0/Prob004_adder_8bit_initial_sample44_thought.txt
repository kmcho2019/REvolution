To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry input from the previous bit position, and produce a sum output and a carry output. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder in the series. This configuration allows the carry to "ripple" through the adder, enabling the correct calculation of the sum and carry-out.

The module will have input ports for the 8-bit operands A and B, a carry-in input, output ports for the 8-bit sum, and a carry-out output. The design will use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators to implement the full adders and connect them in the desired configuration.
