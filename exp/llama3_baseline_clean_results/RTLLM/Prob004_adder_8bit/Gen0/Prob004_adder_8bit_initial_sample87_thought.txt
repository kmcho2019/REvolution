To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will add one bit from operand A, one bit from operand B, and the carry-in from the previous bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be passed as the carry-in to the next full adder. The final carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) position will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.

The module will be designed using Verilog, and we will use a hierarchical approach by defining a separate module for the full adder and then instantiating it within the 8-bit adder module.
