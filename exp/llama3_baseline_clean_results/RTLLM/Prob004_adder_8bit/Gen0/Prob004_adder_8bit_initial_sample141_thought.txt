To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands (a and b) along with a carry-in bit. The carry-out from each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next most significant bit's full adder. This way, we can propagate the carry through all bits to achieve the correct sum and final carry-out.
