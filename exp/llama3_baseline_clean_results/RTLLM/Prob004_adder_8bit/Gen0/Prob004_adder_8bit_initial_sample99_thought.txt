To implement an 8-bit adder module in Verilog, we can use a series of bit-level full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from operands A and B, along with a carry input, producing a sum bit and a carry output. The carry output from each full adder will serve as the carry input for the next full adder. This ripple-carry approach allows us to compute the sum of the two 8-bit operands A and B, as well as the final carry-out.
