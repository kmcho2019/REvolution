To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders (bit-level adders) to perform the addition operation. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We will chain these full adders together to form the 8-bit adder, where the carry-out of one full adder serves as the carry-in for the next full adder. This approach ensures that the adder module can handle the addition of two 8-bit numbers and produce the correct sum and carry-out.
