To design an 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit', we can utilize a series of bit-level full adders. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum and a carry-out. We will chain these full adders together, with the carry-out of each adder serving as the carry-in to the next. This approach ensures that the carry is propagated through all bits, allowing for accurate 8-bit addition. We start with a half adder for the least significant bit (LSB) since we only need to consider the external carry-in at this point. However, for simplicity and consistency, we can implement all bits using full adders, where the carry-in for the LSB full adder comes from the external cin input, and each subsequent bit's full adder uses the carry-out from the previous bit's full adder as its carry-in.
