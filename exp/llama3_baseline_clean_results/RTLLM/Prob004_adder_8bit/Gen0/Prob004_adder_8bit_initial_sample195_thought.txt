To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders, each handling a single bit of the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit addition. The carry-out from each full adder (except the last one) will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, effectively propagating the carry through each bit position. The final carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) addition will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder. The sum output will be the collection of the sum outputs from each full adder.
