To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous stage (or the external carry-in for the first stage), and produce a sum bit and a carry-out that will be passed to the next stage. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed bit by bit, allowing us to easily extend the module to support 8-bit operands. We'll use a structural design approach, instantiating a full adder module for each bit position and wiring them together to form the 8-bit adder.
