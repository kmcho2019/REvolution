To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can use a series of full adders, each responsible for adding two corresponding bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry bit. The carry bit for the first full adder is the carry-in input (cin), and for subsequent full adders, it's the carry-out from the previous full adder. Each full adder will produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The sum bits from all full adders will form the final sum output (sum[7:0]), and the carry-out from the last full adder will be the final carry-out output (cout).
