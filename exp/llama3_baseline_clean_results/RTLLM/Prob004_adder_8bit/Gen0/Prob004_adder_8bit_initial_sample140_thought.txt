To implement an 8-bit adder using multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we can utilize a series of full adders. The full adder is a basic digital circuit that adds two one-bit numbers along with a carry-in bit. For an 8-bit adder, we will connect eight full adders in a ripple-carry configuration, where the carry-out of each full adder serves as the carry-in for the next most significant bit's full adder. The least significant bit's carry-in is the input cin, and the most significant bit's carry-out is the output cout. Each full adder will add corresponding bits from the two input operands (a and b) along with the carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out.
