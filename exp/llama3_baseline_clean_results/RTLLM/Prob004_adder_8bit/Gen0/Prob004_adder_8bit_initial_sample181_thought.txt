To design an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders, each responsible for adding two bits from the input operands A and B along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be passed as the carry input to the next full adder. This way, we can chain eight full adders to handle the 8-bit addition. The overall module will take two 8-bit inputs (a and b), a carry-in (cin), and produce an 8-bit sum and a carry-out (cout).
