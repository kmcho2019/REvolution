To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum and a carry-out. We will chain these full adders together, using the carry-out of each adder as the carry-in for the next, starting with the least significant bit (LSB) and moving towards the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit addition.
