To implement the 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit', we will utilize a series of bit-level full adders to perform the addition operation. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum output bit and a carry-out output. The carry-out from each bit-level adder will be used as the carry-in for the next bit-level adder, effectively propagating the carry through the 8-bit addition operation. The sum output bits from each bit-level adder will be collected to form the 8-bit sum output, and the carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) adder will be used as the final carry-out output.
