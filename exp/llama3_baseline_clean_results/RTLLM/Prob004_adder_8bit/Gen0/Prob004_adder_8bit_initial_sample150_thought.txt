Each full adder will take two input bits (one from each operand) and the carry-in from the previous bit position. The full adder will then produce a sum bit and a carry-out that is used in the next bit position. The 8-bit adder module will have input ports for the two operands, a carry-in input, output ports for the sum, and a carry-out output. We will use a hierarchical approach, defining a separate module for the full adder and then instantiating it within the 8-bit adder module.
