To design the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform a single-bit addition operation, taking into account the carry input from the previous stage. The module will have 8-bit input ports for operands A and B, a 1-bit carry-in input, an 8-bit output port for the sum, and a 1-bit carry-out output. The full adders will be connected in a chain, where the carry output of each stage is used as the carry input for the next stage.
