To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry style. Each full adder will perform the addition of one bit from the two input operands A and B, along with a carry input from the previous bit. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. This approach will allow us to propagate the carry through all the bits, ensuring accurate addition. We will define a separate module for the full adder to make the design more modular and reusable.
