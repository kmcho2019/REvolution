To implement an 8-bit adder, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry-in from the previous adder. The carry-out from each adder will be used as the carry-in for the next adder. We will start with a half adder for the least significant bit (LSB) to handle the initial carry-in, and then use full adders for the remaining bits.
