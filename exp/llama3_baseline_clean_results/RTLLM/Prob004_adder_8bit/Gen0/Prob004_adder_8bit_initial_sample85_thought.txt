To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders, one for each bit position. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, and a carry input from the previous full adder (except for the first full adder which will take the cin input). The sum and carry outputs from each full adder will be connected to the next full adder. The final carry output will be the cout of the module. We will use a hierarchical approach to simplify the design by first defining a full adder module and then instantiating it within the adder_8bit module.
