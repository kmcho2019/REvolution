To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take a bit from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit, and produce a sum and carry-out. The carry-out from one full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This configuration allows the adder to handle the addition of two 8-bit numbers and produce a correct sum and carry-out. We will use the built-in Verilog operators to implement the full adder logic.
