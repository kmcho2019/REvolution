To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from the operands A and B and a carry input. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder to propagate the carry through the bits. We'll use a hierarchical approach, defining a full adder module first and then using it within the 8-bit adder module.
