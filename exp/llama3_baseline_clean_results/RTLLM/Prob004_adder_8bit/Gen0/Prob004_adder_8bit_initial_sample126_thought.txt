To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of bit-level full adders. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in signal, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out signal. The carry-out from each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing us to propagate the carry through all bits. The final carry-out will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit addition operation.
