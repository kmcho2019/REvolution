To design the 8-bit adder module, we'll use a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of one bit from the two input operands (A and B) and the carry from the previous addition. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. The final carry-out will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder. We'll start from the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry through each full adder up to the most significant bit (MSB).
