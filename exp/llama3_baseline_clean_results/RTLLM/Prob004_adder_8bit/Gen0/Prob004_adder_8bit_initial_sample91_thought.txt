To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level full adders in combinational logic. The full adder is a basic digital circuit that adds three one-bit numbers (two operands and a carry-in) and produces a two-bit output (sum and carry-out). By connecting these full adders in a chain, we can perform an 8-bit addition operation. The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. This design will allow us to calculate the sum of two 8-bit numbers and also produce a carry-out.
