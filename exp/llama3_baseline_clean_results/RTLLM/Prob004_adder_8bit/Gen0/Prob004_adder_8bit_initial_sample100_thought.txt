To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input for the next full adder. The first full adder will receive the carry-in input, and the last full adder's carry output will be used as the carry-out output of the 8-bit adder module. This approach allows the module to compute the sum and carry-out by propagating the carry through the series of full adders.
