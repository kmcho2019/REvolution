To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of 8 bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit position. The carry-out of each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next bit position. This approach allows us to compute the sum and carry-out bits for the overall 8-bit addition operation. The module will have input ports for the two 8-bit operands A and B, a carry-in input, and output ports for the 8-bit sum and carry-out.
