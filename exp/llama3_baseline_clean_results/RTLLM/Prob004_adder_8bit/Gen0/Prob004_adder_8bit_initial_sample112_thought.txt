To design an 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit', we will use a chain of 8 full adders in combinational logic. Each full adder will perform a single-bit addition of the corresponding bits from the two input operands 'a' and 'b', along with a carry input. The carry output from one full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder to propagate the carry through all bits. The module will have two 8-bit input ports 'a' and 'b', a 1-bit carry-in input 'cin', an 8-bit output port 'sum', and a 1-bit carry-out output 'cout'.
