To implement the 8-bit adder module, we'll use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in, and produce a sum and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. We'll also need to handle the initial carry-in and the final carry-out.

Here's a step-by-step breakdown:

1. Define the module with the specified input and output ports.
2. Declare internal wires for the carry signals between full adders.
3. Instantiate a full adder for each bit position, connecting the inputs and outputs accordingly.
4. Connect the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next full adder.
5. Assign the final carry-out to the cout port.

This approach ensures that the addition is performed bit-by-bit, with the carry propagating through the chain of full adders.
