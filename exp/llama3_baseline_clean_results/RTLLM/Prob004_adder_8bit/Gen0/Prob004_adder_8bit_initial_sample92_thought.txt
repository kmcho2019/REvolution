To implement an 8-bit adder using bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will be used as the carry-in bit for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. The final carry-out bit from the last full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
