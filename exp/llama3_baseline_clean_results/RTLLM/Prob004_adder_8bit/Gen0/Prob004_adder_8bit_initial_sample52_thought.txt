To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we can use a series of full adders. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will be used as the carry-in bit for the next full adder. This process will be repeated for all 8 bits of the operands. The final carry-out from the last full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
