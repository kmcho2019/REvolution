To design the 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit', we will utilize a series of full adders to perform the addition operation bit by bit. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in signal, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out signal. The carry-out signal from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, thus propagating the carry through the bits. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed correctly, taking into account any carry generated during the addition of each bit pair.
