To implement an 8-bit adder, we can chain together eight full adders, each handling one bit position from the inputs A and B, along with a carry input from the previous bit position (except for the least significant bit, which receives the external carry-in). Each full adder's sum output contributes to the final sum, and the carry output of each full adder (except the most significant bit) is connected to the carry input of the next full adder to the left. The carry output of the most significant bit's full adder becomes the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
