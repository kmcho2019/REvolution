To design an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input for the next full adder. This way, the addition operation will be performed bit by bit, and the final sum and carry-out will be obtained.
