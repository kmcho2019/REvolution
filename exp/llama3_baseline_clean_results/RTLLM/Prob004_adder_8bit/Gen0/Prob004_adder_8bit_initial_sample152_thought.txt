To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in input of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. This implementation will enable the module to perform the addition operation on the 8-bit input operands A and B, producing an 8-bit sum and a carry-out output.
