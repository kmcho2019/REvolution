To design an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder takes in two bits from the input operands and the carry-in from the previous full adder, producing a sum bit and a carry-out to the next full adder. The carry-out from the most significant bit's full adder will serve as the final carry-out of the 8-bit adder module. We start by defining the full adder module, which takes in three inputs (two data bits and a carry-in) and produces two outputs (a sum bit and a carry-out). Then, we instantiate eight instances of the full adder module within the 8-bit adder module, chaining them together to perform the 8-bit addition.
