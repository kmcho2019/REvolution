To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. The adder will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and a carry-in input. It will produce an 8-bit output representing the sum of A and B, and a carry-out output. Each bit-level adder will calculate the sum and carry for its corresponding bit position, using the inputs from A and B, and the carry from the previous bit position.
