To design the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders in combinational logic. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in signal, and produce a sum and a carry-out signal. The carry-out signal from each full adder will serve as the carry-in signal for the next full adder. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed bit by bit, from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out signal from the most significant full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
