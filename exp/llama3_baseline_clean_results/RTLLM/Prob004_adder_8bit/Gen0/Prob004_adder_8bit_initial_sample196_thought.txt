To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, and a carry-in bit from the previous full adder (for the least significant bit, the carry-in is the module's cin input). The output of each full adder will be the corresponding bit of the sum and the carry-out to the next full adder. The final carry-out from the most significant bit's full adder will be the module's cout output.
