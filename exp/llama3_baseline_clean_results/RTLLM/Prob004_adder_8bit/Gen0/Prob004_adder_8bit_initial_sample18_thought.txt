To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. The full adder is a combinational circuit that adds three one-bit binary numbers (A, B, and carry-in) and produces a two-bit binary output (sum and carry-out). We will chain these full adders together, with the carry-out of one full adder serving as the carry-in for the next. This approach will allow us to add the two 8-bit input operands (A and B) bit by bit, from the least significant bit to the most significant bit, while properly handling the carry propagation.
