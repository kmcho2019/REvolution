To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders, each of which takes two input bits and a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. The module will have eight instances of a full adder, each handling one bit position of the 8-bit operands. The carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the bits.

We'll define a module for a full adder and then instantiate it eight times in the main adder_8bit module, connecting them in a chain to handle the carry propagation. The sum output of each full adder instance will contribute to the final sum[7:0] output, and the carry-out of the most significant bit's full adder will be the final carry-out output (cout).
