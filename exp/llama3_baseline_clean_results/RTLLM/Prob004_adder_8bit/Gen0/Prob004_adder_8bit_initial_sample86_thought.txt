To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry architecture. Each full adder performs a bit-level addition of the corresponding bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder is propagated to the next full adder to compute the sum of the adjacent bits. The final carry output from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder is the overall carry-out. We will design a full adder module and then instantiate it eight times within the adder_8bit module, connecting them in a way that ripple-carry addition is achieved.
