To implement an 8-bit adder, we can use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry input, and produce a sum and a carry output. The carry output from one full adder will serve as the carry input for the next full adder. The final carry output from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder will be the carry-out output of the 8-bit adder.
