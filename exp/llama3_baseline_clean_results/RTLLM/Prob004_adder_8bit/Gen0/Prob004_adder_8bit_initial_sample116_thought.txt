To implement an 8-bit adder, we can use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder takes in two input bits from the operands A and B, and a carry-in bit from the previous full adder. It then produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit, which is used as the carry-in for the next full adder. The process starts with the least significant bit (LSB) and propagates to the most significant bit (MSB). The carry-in for the first full adder is the external carry-in input (cin), and the carry-out of the last full adder is the external carry-out output (cout).
