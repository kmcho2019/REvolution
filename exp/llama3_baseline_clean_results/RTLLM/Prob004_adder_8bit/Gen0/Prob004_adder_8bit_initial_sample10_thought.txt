To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will create a series of bit-level adders, specifically full adders, to perform the addition operation. The module will have two 8-bit input operands, A and B, a carry-in input, and will produce an 8-bit output sum and a carry-out output. Each bit-level adder will take two bits from A and B, along with a carry input, and produce a sum bit and a carry output. The carry output from one bit-level adder will be connected to the carry input of the next bit-level adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder.
