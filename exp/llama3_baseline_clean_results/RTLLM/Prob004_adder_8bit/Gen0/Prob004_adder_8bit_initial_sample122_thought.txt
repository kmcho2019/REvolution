To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders, where each full adder will perform the addition operation for one bit of the input operands. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the ripple carry effect to propagate through the bits. We'll use a for loop to instantiate the full adders for each bit position, ensuring a concise and efficient implementation.
