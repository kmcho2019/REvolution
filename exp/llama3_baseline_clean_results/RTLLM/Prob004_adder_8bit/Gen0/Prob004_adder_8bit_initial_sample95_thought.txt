To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will be responsible for adding one bit from operand A, one bit from operand B, and the carry from the previous bit. The carry-out from each full adder will be passed to the next full adder. This design ensures that the addition operation is performed bit by bit, and the final sum and carry-out are obtained.
