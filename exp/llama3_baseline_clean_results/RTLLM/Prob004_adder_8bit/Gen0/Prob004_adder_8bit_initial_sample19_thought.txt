The adder_8bit module will utilize a series of full adders to perform the 8-bit addition. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry input, and produce a sum output and a carry output. The carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. This approach will enable the module to correctly calculate the sum of the two 8-bit input operands and produce a carry-out output.
