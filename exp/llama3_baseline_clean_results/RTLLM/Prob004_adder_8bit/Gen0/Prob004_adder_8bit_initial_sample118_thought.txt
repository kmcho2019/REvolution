To implement an 8-bit adder using multiple bit-level adders, we can chain full adders together, where the carry-out of one full adder becomes the carry-in for the next full adder. Each full adder takes two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. We start with the least significant bit (LSB) of the operands and proceed to the most significant bit (MSB), connecting the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next one. The final carry-out from the MSB full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
