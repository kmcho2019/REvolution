To design an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders, where each full adder performs a bit-level addition. We will chain the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next full adder, thus allowing the carry to propagate through all bits. The adder_8bit module will have two 8-bit input ports (a and b), a 1-bit carry-in port (cin), an 8-bit output port (sum), and a 1-bit carry-out port (cout).
