To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder. The final carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) position will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder. We will define a separate module for the full adder and then instantiate it within the 8-bit adder module.
