To design an 8-bit adder module in Verilog, we can break down the problem into smaller parts by using full adder modules for each bit. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. We can then chain these full adders together, with the carry-out of one full adder becoming the carry-in for the next full adder. This way, we can perform the addition of two 8-bit numbers, A and B, along with a carry-in, and obtain the sum and a carry-out.
