To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of 8 bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, and the carry-in from the previous adder (except for the first adder which takes the global carry-in), and produce a sum bit and a carry-out. The sum bits from each adder will form the final sum output, and the carry-out from the last adder will be the final carry-out.
