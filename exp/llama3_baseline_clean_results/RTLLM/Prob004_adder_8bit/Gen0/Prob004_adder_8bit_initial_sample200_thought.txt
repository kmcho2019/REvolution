To design an 8-bit adder, we'll use a series of full adders, each of which takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out from one full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the addition to propagate from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out from the MSB full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed bit by bit, handling the carry propagation correctly.
