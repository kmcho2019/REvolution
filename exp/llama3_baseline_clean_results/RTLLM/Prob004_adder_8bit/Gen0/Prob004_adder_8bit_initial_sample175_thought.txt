To implement the 8-bit adder module, we'll create a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform the addition of one bit from each input operand and the carry from the previous bit. The sum and carry-out of each full adder will be used to compute the sum and carry for the next bit. We'll use the built-in Verilog operators for addition and logical operations to simplify the implementation.
