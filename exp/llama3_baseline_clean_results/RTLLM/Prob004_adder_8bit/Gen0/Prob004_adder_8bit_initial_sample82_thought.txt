To design an 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit', we will use a series of full adders for each bit position. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, and a carry-in signal. The carry-out of one full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder to propagate the carry through the bits. The sum of each full adder will form the output 'sum'. The final carry-out will be the overall carry-out 'cout' of the 8-bit adder.
