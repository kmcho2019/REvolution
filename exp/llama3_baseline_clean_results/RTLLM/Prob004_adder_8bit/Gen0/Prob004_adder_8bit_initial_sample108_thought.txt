To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry from the previous adder (or the carry-in for the first adder), and produce an output bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each adder is then used as the carry-in for the next adder. This process is repeated for all 8 bits of the operands. The final carry-out from the last adder is the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
