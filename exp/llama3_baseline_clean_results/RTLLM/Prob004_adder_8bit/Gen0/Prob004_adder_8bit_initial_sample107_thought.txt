To design an 8-bit adder using bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder to calculate the sum and carry for the next bit position. This chain of full adders will ensure that the addition operation is performed correctly, taking into account any carry values generated during the process. We will use the built-in bitwise operators in Verilog to simplify the implementation.
