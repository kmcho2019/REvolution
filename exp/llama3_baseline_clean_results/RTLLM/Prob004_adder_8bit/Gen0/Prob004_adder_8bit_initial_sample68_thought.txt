To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders, each capable of adding two single bits and a carry-in bit. The full adder's output will be the sum of the two input bits and the carry-in, as well as a carry-out to be used in the next full adder. This way, the carry propagates through each bit position, allowing the addition of the two 8-bit operands. The sum of each bit position will be collected to form the final 8-bit result.
