To implement the 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit', we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take a bit from the two input operands 'a' and 'b', and the carry from the previous bit position. The sum of the two input bits and the carry will be calculated and the result will be the sum bit at the current position and the carry to the next position. 
The first full adder will take the carry-in input 'cin' as the carry, and the last full adder's carry output will be the final carry-out 'cout'. 
We will use Verilog's built-in full adder module or implement it using logic gates to perform the bit-level addition.
