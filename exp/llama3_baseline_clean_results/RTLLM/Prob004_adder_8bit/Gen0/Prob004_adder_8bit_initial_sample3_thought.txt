To implement an 8-bit adder module in Verilog, we will use a series of full adders, where each full adder is responsible for adding two input bits from the operands and a carry-in bit. We will chain these full adders together, passing the carry-out from one full adder to the carry-in of the next, to handle the carry propagation across the bits. The adder_8bit module will take two 8-bit operands (a and b) and a carry-in (cin) as inputs and produce an 8-bit sum and a carry-out (cout) as outputs.
