To implement an 8-bit adder, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands (a and b) and the carry-in from the previous bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. This way, the carry will propagate through the series of full adders, allowing the correct calculation of the sum. 
We will start from the least significant bit (bit 0) and proceed to the most significant bit (bit 7).
