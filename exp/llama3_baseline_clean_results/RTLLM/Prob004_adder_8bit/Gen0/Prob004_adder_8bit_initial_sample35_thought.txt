To implement an 8-bit adder module using multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we'll design the module 'adder_8bit' with input ports 'a' and 'b' for the two 8-bit operands, 'cin' for the carry-in input, and output ports 'sum' for the 8-bit sum and 'cout' for the carry-out. The adder will utilize a series of full adders to perform the bit-by-bit addition. Each full adder will take two input bits from 'a' and 'b' and a carry-in (initially 'cin' for the least significant bit and the carry-out from the previous full adder for subsequent bits), producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The final carry-out from the most significant bit's full adder will be 'cout'.
