To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders, each performing a single-bit addition. The full adders will be connected in a ripple-carry fashion, where the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. This allows the carry to propagate through the adders, enabling the module to perform an 8-bit addition. We will define a separate module for the full adder and then instantiate it eight times within the 8-bit adder module.
