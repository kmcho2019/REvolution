To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will create a series of full adders, each of which takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The full adders will be connected in a ripple-carry fashion, where the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. This way, the carry propagates through the adder, allowing us to perform the addition operation. We will use a separate full adder module to simplify the design and improve readability. The top-level adder_8bit module will instantiate eight full adders and connect them appropriately to perform the 8-bit addition.
