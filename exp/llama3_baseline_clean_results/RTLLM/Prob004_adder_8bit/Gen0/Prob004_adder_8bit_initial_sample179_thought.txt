To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from operands A and B, and the carry-in from the previous full adder. The carry-out of each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This allows us to propagate the carry from one bit position to the next, enabling the correct addition of the two input operands. The final carry-out will be used as the cout output of the 8-bit adder module.
