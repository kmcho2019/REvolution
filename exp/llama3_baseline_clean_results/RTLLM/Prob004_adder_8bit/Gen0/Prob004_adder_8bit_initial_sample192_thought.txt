To implement an 8-bit adder using multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we can utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder takes in two bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. By chaining these full adders together, where the carry-out of one adder becomes the carry-in of the next, we can add two 8-bit numbers. The final carry-out of the last full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit addition operation.
