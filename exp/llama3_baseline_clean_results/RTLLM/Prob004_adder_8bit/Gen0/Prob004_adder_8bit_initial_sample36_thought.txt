The 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit' will be implemented using a series of 8 full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder performs the addition of two input bits and a carry input, producing a sum bit and a carry output. The carry output from each full adder is connected to the carry input of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. The sum bits from each full adder are collected to form the final 8-bit sum output. The final carry output from the last full adder is used as the carry-out output of the module.
