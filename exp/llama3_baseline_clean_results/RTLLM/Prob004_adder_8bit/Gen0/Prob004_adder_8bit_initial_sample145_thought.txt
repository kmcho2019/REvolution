To implement an 8-bit adder using bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will create a series of full adders where each bit of the input operands A and B is added along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. This way, the carry propagates from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). We will define a full adder module separately and instantiate it eight times within the adder_8bit module to perform the addition of the 8-bit operands A and B.
