To implement the 8-bit adder module, we'll use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, and a carry input. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. The final carry output of the last full adder will be the carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
