To implement an 8-bit adder, we can use a series of bit-level full adders. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in input, producing a sum output and a carry-out output. The carry-out output of each full adder will serve as the carry-in input for the next full adder, allowing the addition operation to propagate through all 8 bits. This way, the module will be able to handle the addition of two 8-bit numbers with a carry-in input and produce an 8-bit sum output along with a carry-out output.
