To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. Each full adder will take in two bits from the input operands A and B, and the carry-in from the previous bit position. The sum and carry-out of each full adder will be used to compute the sum and carry-in for the next bit position. This process will be repeated for all 8 bits of the input operands. 
The module will have input ports for the 8-bit operands A and B, the carry-in input, and output ports for the 8-bit sum and the carry-out.
