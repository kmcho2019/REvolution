To implement an 8-bit adder module, we'll use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from the operands A and B along with a carry bit from the previous addition. The carry-out from one full adder will be the carry-in for the next full adder. This approach allows us to add the two 8-bit numbers bit by bit, handling the carry from one bit position to the next, ultimately producing the 8-bit sum and a final carry-out.
