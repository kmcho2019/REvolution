To implement the 8-bit adder module named "adder_8bit", we will use a series of bit-level adders (full adders) in combinational logic. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will serve as the carry-in bit for the next most significant bit position. We will use a chain of eight full adders, each handling one bit position of the 8-bit operands, to compute the sum and carry-out for the entire 8-bit addition operation.
