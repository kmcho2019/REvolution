To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will create a series of full adders that are connected in a way that the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. This creates a ripple-carry adder, which is a basic implementation of a multi-bit adder. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous adder (or the external carry-in for the first adder), and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The sum bits from each full adder are collected to form the 8-bit sum output, and the carry-out from the last full adder is the carry-out output of the module.
