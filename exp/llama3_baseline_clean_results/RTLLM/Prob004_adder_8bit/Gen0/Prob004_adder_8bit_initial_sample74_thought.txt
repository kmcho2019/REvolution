To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits and a carry-in, and produce a sum output and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This will allow us to perform the addition of the two 8-bit input operands A and B. We will also need to handle the final carry-out of the most significant bit full adder as the overall carry-out of the module.
