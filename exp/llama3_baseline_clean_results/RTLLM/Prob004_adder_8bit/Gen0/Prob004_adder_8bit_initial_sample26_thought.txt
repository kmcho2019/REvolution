To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry input. The sum bit will be generated for each position, and the carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input for the next full adder. This way, the carry is propagated from the least significant bit to the most significant bit.
