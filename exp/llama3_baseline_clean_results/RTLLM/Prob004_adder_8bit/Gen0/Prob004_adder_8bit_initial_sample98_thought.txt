To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry-in from the previous full adder (except for the first full adder which takes the external carry-in input). The sum and carry-out of each full adder are then passed to the next stage. The final carry-out from the last full adder serves as the carry-out of the 8-bit adder module.
