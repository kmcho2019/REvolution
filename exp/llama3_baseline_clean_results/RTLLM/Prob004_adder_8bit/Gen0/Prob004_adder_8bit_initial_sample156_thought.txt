To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in input, and produce a sum output and a carry-out output. The carry-out output from each full adder will be used as the carry-in input for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the series of adders. This will enable us to perform the addition operation bit-by-bit, from the least significant bit to the most significant bit.
