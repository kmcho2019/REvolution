To design an 8-bit adder module using multiple bit-level adders, we can utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the entire 8-bit adder. We'll use a chain of 8 full adders to implement the 8-bit addition operation.
