To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry from the previous bit position. The full adder's output will provide the sum bit and the carry for the next bit position. We'll start from the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry through each full adder to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry output will be the carry-out of the MSB full adder.
