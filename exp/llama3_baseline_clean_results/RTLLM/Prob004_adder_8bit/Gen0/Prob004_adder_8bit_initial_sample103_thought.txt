To implement an 8-bit adder, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry input from the previous full adder, and produce a sum bit and a carry output. The carry output from each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for bitwise operations to implement the full adders. The final carry output will be the carry-out of the most significant bit's full adder.
