The adder_8bit module will be designed using a series of 8 full adders. Each full adder will be responsible for adding the corresponding bits from the two input operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the bits. The sum output of each full adder will be connected to the corresponding bit of the output sum.
