To design the 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit', we will utilize a series of full adders, where each full adder performs the addition of two input bits and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out of each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder. The 8-bit adder module will consist of 8 instances of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. The sum bits produced by each full adder will form the output sum, and the carry-out of the most significant bit (MSB) full adder will be the final carry-out output.
