The 8-bit adder module can be designed using a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder takes in two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in signal, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out signal. The carry-out signal from one full adder is passed as the carry-in signal to the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the entire adder. The final carry-out signal from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder is the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
