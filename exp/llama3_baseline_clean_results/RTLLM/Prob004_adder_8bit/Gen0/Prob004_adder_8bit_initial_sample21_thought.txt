To implement the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. The design involves creating a full adder module that takes in two bits and a carry-in, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out. We then instantiate this full adder module eight times within the 8-bit adder module, connecting the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next one. This allows us to propagate the carry from one bit position to the next, performing the complete 8-bit addition.
