To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry input from the previous full adder (or the external carry-in for the first full adder). The sum and carry outputs of each full adder will be used as inputs for the next full adder and the final sum output, respectively. This approach allows us to perform the 8-bit addition operation using bit-level adders in combinational logic.
