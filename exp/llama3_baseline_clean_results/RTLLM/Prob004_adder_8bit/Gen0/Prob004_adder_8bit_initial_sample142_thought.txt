To implement an 8-bit adder module using multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will employ a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from the operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input to the next full adder, thus allowing the propagation of carry from the least significant bit to the most significant bit. The sum output from each full adder will contribute to the final sum output. The carry-out output will be the carry output from the most significant bit's full adder.
