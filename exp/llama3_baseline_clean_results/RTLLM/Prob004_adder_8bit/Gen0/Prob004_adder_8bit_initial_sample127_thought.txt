To implement an 8-bit adder using bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from each operand) and the carry from the previous addition, producing the corresponding sum bit and the carry for the next addition. We start with the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry through each stage up to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out is the carry from the MSB addition.
