To implement an 8-bit adder module, we'll use a series of full adders, each performing a single-bit addition. The carry-out from one full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This ripple-carry approach will allow us to calculate the sum and carry-out for the entire 8-bit operation. We'll define a separate module for the full adder and instantiate it within the adder_8bit module.
