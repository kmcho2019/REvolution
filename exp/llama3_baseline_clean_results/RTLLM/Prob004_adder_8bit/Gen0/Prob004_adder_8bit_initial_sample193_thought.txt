To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands (a and b) along with the carry from the previous bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. The final carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder. We will use the standard full adder logic, which can be described by the following equations: sum = a XOR b XOR cin and cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin).
