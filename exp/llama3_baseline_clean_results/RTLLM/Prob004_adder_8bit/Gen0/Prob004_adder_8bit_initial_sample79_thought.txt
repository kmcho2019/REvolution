To design the 8-bit adder module, we'll create a series of full adders that will perform bit-level additions. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum and a carry-out. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, effectively propagating the carry through the 8-bit adder. We will start from the least significant bit (LSB) and move towards the most significant bit (MSB), ensuring that the carry from each bit position is correctly propagated to the next.
