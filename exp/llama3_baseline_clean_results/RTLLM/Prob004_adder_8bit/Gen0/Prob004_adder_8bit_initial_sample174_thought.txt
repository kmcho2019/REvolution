To design the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from one full adder will serve as the carry-in bit for the next full adder, allowing the carry to ripple through the adder chain. The final carry-out bit from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
