To implement an 8-bit adder using multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, we will design a module that instantiates eight full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from the 8-bit operands A and B, along with a carry input. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder to propagate the carry bit through the entire 8-bit addition process. The final carry output will be the cout of the most significant bit's full adder.
