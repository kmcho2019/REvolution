To implement an 8-bit adder, we'll use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, and the carry-in from the previous adder. The first full adder will use the carry-in input provided to the module, and subsequent adders will use the carry-out from the previous adder. The sum bits from each full adder will be collected to form the final 8-bit sum. The carry-out from the last full adder will be the carry-out of the module. We'll use a separate module for the full adder to keep the design modular and reusable.
