To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. The full adder is a basic digital circuit that adds three one-bit binary numbers. In this case, we will use eight full adders to add the corresponding bits of the two 8-bit input operands (A and B) along with a carry-in bit. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder. We will use a hierarchical approach by first defining a full adder module and then instantiating it eight times in the main adder_8bit module.
