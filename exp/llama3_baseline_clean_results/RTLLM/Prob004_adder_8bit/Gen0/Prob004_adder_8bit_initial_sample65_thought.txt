To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, and a carry input from the previous full adder (or the external carry-in for the first adder), and produce a sum bit and a carry output. The sum bits from each full adder will form the output sum, and the final carry output will be the carry-out of the last full adder. This design can be efficiently implemented using a parameterized module for the full adder and a generate statement in Verilog to instantiate the series of full adders.
