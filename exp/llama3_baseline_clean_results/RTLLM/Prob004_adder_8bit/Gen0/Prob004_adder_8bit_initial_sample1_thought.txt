To implement an 8-bit adder, we'll create a module named "adder_8bit" with input ports "a" and "b" for the 8-bit operands, "cin" for the carry-in, and output ports "sum" for the 8-bit result and "cout" for the carry-out. The adder will utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration, where each full adder calculates the sum of two input bits and the carry from the previous full adder.
