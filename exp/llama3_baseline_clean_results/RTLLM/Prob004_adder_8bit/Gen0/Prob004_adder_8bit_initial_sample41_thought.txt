To implement an 8-bit adder, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous adder, and produce the corresponding bit of the sum and the carry-out to the next adder. The first full adder will use the external carry-in, and the last full adder's carry-out will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
