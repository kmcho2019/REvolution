To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will utilize a series of bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take in two bits from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit position. The sum and carry-out of each full adder will be used to compute the sum of A and B and the carry-out for the next bit position. The module will have 8 input bits for each operand, a carry-in bit, 8 output bits for the sum, and a carry-out bit.
