To design an 8-bit adder, we'll use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B and the carry from the previous adder, producing a sum bit and a carry bit. The carry bit from each adder will be passed to the next adder, enabling the ripple-carry effect. We'll start by defining a full adder module, then use it within our 8-bit adder module to perform the addition operation.
