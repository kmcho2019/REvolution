To design an 8-bit adder module in Verilog, we can use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take in a bit from each of the input operands A and B, and a carry-in input. The carry-out of each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder. This design allows us to calculate the sum and carry-out for each bit position, ultimately giving us the final sum and carry-out for the entire 8-bit operation.
