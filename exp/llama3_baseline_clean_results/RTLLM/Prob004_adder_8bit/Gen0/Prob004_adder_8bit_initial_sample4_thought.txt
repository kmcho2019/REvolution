To implement an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take in two input bits (one from each operand) and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. This approach enables the addition of two 8-bit operands with a carry-in and produces a carry-out.
