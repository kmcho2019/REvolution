To implement an 8-bit adder module, we'll utilize a series of bit-level adders (full adders) to perform the addition operation. The module will have two 8-bit input ports (a and b), a 1-bit carry-in input port (cin), an 8-bit output port (sum), and a 1-bit carry-out output port (cout). The addition will be performed bit by bit, with each bit-level adder's carry-out connected to the next bit-level adder's carry-in, starting from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). This approach allows the carry to propagate through each bit position, enabling the correct calculation of the sum and the final carry-out.
