To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will create a series of full adders, each responsible for adding corresponding bits from the two input operands (A and B) along with a carry-in bit. The full adder for the least significant bit (LSB) will use the provided cin as its carry-in, while each subsequent full adder will use the carry-out from the previous full adder. This way, we propagate the carry through the bits, allowing for the correct addition of the two 8-bit numbers. The sum of the addition will be represented by the sum output, and the final carry-out will be captured in the cout output.
