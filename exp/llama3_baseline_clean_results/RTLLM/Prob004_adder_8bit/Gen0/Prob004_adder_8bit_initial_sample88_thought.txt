To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a chain, where the carry-out of one full adder serves as the carry-in for the next full adder. Each full adder will perform the addition of one bit from operand A, one bit from operand B, and the carry-in from the previous bit position. The output of each full adder will be one bit of the sum and the carry-out to the next bit position.
