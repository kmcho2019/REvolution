To implement an 8-bit adder, we will utilize a series of full adders, where each full adder will handle one bit of the input operands A and B. We will start from the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry to the next full adder. The carry-in input (cin) will be used as the initial carry for the LSB full adder. The carry-out output (cout) will be the carry from the most significant bit (MSB) full adder. We will define a separate module for the full adder to keep the design modular and reusable.
