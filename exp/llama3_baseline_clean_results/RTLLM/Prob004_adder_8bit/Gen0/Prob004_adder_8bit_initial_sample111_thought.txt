To implement the 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will perform the addition of one bit from the input operands A and B, along with the carry-in from the previous bit position. The output of each full adder will be the sum of the current bit position and the carry-out to the next bit position. We will start with the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out from the MSB full adder will be the overall carry-out of the 8-bit adder.
