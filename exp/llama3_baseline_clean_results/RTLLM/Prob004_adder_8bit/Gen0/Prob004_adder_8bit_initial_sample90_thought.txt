To implement the 8-bit adder, we'll design a module named 'adder_8bit' that utilizes a series of full adders (bit-level adders) to perform the addition operation. Each full adder will take in two input bits (one from each operand) and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We'll chain these full adders together, with the carry-out of one adder serving as the carry-in for the next, allowing the carry to propagate through the bits. We'll start with the least significant bit (LSB) and move towards the most significant bit (MSB).
