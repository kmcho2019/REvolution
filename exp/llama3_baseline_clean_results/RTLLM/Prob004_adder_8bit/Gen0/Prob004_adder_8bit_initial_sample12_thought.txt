To design an 8-bit adder module using bit-level adders in combinational logic, we can utilize a series of full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of two input bits from the operands A and B along with a carry input. The carry output from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder to ensure the correct addition operation. We will start by defining a full adder module, and then use this module within the adder_8bit module to perform the 8-bit addition.
