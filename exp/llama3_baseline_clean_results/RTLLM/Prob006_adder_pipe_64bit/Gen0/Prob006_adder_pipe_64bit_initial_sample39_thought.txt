To implement the 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will use a combination of Verilog's always block and assign statements. The design will include several registers to store the intermediate results of the addition operation at each pipeline stage. The input enable signal (i_en) will be synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals. The output enable signal (o_en) will be updated based on the pipeline stages and synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals.
