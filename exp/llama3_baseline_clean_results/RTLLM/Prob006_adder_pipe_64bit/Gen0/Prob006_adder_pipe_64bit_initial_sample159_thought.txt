The module will have the following pipeline stages:
1. Input stage: Store the input operands (adda and addb) and the enable signal (i_en) in registers.
2. Addition stage: Perform the addition operation on the input operands and calculate the sum and carry signals.
3. Output stage: Store the final result (sum and carry) in a register and update the o_en signal.
