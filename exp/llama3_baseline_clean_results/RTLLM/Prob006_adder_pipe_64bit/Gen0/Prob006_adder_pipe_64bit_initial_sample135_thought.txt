To design a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we can break down the process into several stages. Each stage will include a register to hold the input operands and a full adder to calculate the sum and carry. The carry from each stage will be propagated to the next stage, and the final result will be available after all stages have completed. We will use the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals to control the registers and synchronize the input enable signal (i_en). The output enable signal (o_en) will be updated based on the pipeline stages and synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals.
