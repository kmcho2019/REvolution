To implement the 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will first design a 1-bit full adder that calculates the sum and carry-out for two input bits and a carry-in. We will then use this 1-bit full adder as a building block to construct the 64-bit ripple carry adder. The input operands (adda and addb) will be propagated through the pipeline stages, and the corresponding sum and carry signals will be calculated at each stage. The output enable signal (o_en) will be synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals to indicate the availability of the result.
