To design a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we can use a series of full adder modules. Each full adder will calculate the sum of two input bits and a carry bit. The output of each full adder will be connected to the next full adder to propagate the carry signal. To implement the pipeline stages, we will use registers to store the intermediate results and synchronize the input enable signal (i_en). We will also use a flag to track the availability of the result, which will be indicated by the output enable signal (o_en).
