The 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages can be designed using a series of full adders and registers. The adder will have several stages, each of which includes a full adder and a register to store the sum and carry-out from the previous stage. The input enable signal (i_en) is synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals to control the pipeline stages. The output enable signal (o_en) is updated based on the pipeline stages and is synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals.
