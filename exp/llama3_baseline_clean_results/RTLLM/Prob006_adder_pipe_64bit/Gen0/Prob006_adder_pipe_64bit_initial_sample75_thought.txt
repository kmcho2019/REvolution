To implement the 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will break down the design into several stages, each consisting of a full adder and a register to hold the input and output values. We will use the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals to control the flow of data through the pipeline stages. The input enable signal (i_en) will be synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals to ensure proper operation. The output enable signal (o_en) will be updated based on the pipeline stages and synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals.
