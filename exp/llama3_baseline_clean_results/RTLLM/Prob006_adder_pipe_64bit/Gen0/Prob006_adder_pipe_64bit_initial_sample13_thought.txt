To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will first define the module with the given input and output ports. We will then declare the necessary internal signals and registers to hold the intermediate results of each pipeline stage. The adder will be implemented using a combination of full adders and registers to enable the pipeline stages. We will also include a mechanism to synchronize the input enable signal (i_en) and update the output enable signal (o_en) based on the pipeline stages. The entire design will be controlled by the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals.
