To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will use a combination of registers and combinational logic. The design will have several stages, each consisting of a full adder and a register to store the sum and carry. The input enable signal (i_en) will be synchronized with the clock (clk) and reset (rst_n) signals, and the output enable signal (o_en) will be updated based on the pipeline stages. We will use a hierarchical approach, first designing a single-bit full adder and then instantiating it 64 times to create the 64-bit ripple carry adder.
