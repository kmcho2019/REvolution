To design a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we'll break down the addition operation into multiple stages. Each stage will perform a partial addition and store the intermediate results in registers. The output will be available after all pipeline stages have completed. We will use a series of full adders to perform the addition and registers to store the intermediate results. The input enable signal (i_en) will be synchronized with the clock and reset to control the pipeline. The output enable signal (o_en) will be updated based on the pipeline stages to indicate when the result is valid.
