To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipeline stages, we will first design a basic ripple carry adder that takes two 64-bit inputs and produces a 65-bit output. Then, we will add pipeline stages by inserting registers at appropriate points to synchronize the input enable signal and the output result. The pipeline stages will be controlled by the clock and reset signals. We will also include an output enable signal that is updated based on the pipeline stages and synchronized with the clock and reset signals.
