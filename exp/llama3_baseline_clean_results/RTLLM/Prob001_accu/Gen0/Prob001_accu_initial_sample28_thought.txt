To implement the accu module, we will use a counter to keep track of the number of valid data inputs received. When the counter reaches 4, we will output the accumulated sum and set valid_out to 1 for one cycle. We will use a register to accumulate the sum and reset it when the counter reaches 4 or when the reset signal rst_n is asserted. The module will be sensitive to the rising edge of the clock signal clk and will reset when rst_n is 0.
