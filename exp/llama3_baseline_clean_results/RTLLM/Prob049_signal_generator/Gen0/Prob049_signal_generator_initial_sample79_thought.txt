To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we can use a finite state machine with two states: one for incrementing the waveform and another for decrementing it. The state transitions will occur when the waveform reaches its maximum or minimum value. We'll utilize a 5-bit register for the waveform and a 1-bit register for the state. The module will be designed using Verilog HDL, with an always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal.
