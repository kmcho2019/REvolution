To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The FSM will control the 5-bit output waveform signal "wave" by either incrementing or decrementing it based on the current state. The module will have a clock signal "clk" and an active-low reset signal "rst_n". On reset, the state and waveform will be reset to 0. The waveform will cycle between 0 and 31, incrementing or decrementing by 1, based on the state transitions.
