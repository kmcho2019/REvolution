To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use two registers: one for the state and another for the waveform. The state register will control the direction of the waveform generation (incrementing or decrementing). We will use a case statement to determine the next state based on the current state and the waveform value. The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. On reset, both the state and waveform registers will be cleared to 0. The waveform will cycle between 0 and 31 based on the state transitions.
