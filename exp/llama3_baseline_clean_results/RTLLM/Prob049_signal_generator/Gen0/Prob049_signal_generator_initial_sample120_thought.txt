To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use two registers, 'state' and 'wave', and control the waveform generation based on the current state of 'state'. We will utilize an always block to update these registers synchronously with the clock signal and asynchronously with the reset signal. The 'state' will be used to determine whether to increment or decrement the 'wave', and we will use a case statement to handle the different states. When the 'wave' reaches its maximum or minimum value, the 'state' will be updated to change the direction of the waveform.
