To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: 0 (incrementing) and 1 (decrementing). The module will use a 5-bit register, wave, to generate the waveform, which will cycle between 0 and 31. The state transitions will be controlled by a case statement, and the waveform generation will be updated in the always block triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. On reset, the state and waveform registers will be cleared to 0.
