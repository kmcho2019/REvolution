The signal_generator module is designed to produce a triangle wave by incrementing and decrementing a 5-bit signal, wave. This is achieved by utilizing a state machine with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The module's operation is controlled by a clock signal (clk) and an active-low reset signal (rst_n). On reset, the state and wave registers are reset to 0. The waveform generation is then controlled based on the current state, ensuring that the wave signal cycles between 0 and 31. The state transitions occur when the wave signal reaches its maximum (31) or minimum (0) values.
