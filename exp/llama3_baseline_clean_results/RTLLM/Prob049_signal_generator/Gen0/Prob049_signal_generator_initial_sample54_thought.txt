To implement a Triangle Wave signal generator module, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The state machine controls a 5-bit register "wave" that represents the generated waveform. The FSM transitions between the two states when the "wave" reaches its maximum (31) or minimum (0) value. This can be achieved using a case statement within an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal and the negative edge of the reset signal.
