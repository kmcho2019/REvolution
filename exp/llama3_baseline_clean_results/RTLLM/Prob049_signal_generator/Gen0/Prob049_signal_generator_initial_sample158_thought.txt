The design involves creating a module named "signal_generator" that produces a triangle wave signal by controlling the state of the waveform generation. 
The module uses two registers: "state" for state control and "wave" for the waveform output. 
These registers are updated based on the clock signal and the reset condition. 
A case statement within the always block determines the state transitions and the waveform updates.
The waveform cycles between 0 and 31 by incrementing or decrementing the "wave" signal based on the current state.
