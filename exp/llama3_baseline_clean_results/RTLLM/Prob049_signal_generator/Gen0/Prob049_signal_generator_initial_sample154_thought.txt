The design approach involves creating a finite state machine with two states (0 and 1) to control the direction of the waveform generation (incrementing or decrementing). We will utilize a 5-bit register 'wave' to store the current value of the waveform and a 1-bit register 'state' to track the current state of the waveform generation. The state transitions and waveform updates will be performed within an always block, sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal and the negative edge of the reset signal. The waveform will cycle between 0 and 31 based on the current state.
