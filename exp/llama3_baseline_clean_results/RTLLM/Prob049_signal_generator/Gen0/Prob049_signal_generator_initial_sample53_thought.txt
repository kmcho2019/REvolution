To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use a register named 'state' to control the state of the waveform generation and another register named 'wave' to represent the generated waveform. We will utilize an always block to update these registers based on the clock signal and reset. The waveform generation will be controlled using a case statement to determine whether to increment or decrement the 'wave' register based on its current value and the 'state' register.
