To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The FSM will control the 5-bit signal "wave" to generate the desired waveform. We will use two registers: one for the state and one for the waveform. The state register will determine whether to increment or decrement the waveform register. The waveform register will be updated based on the current state. We will use a case statement to implement the state transitions and waveform updates.
