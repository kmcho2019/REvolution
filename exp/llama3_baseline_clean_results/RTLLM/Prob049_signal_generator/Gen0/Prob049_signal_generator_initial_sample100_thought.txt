The design involves creating a module named "signal_generator" that generates a triangle waveform by incrementing and decrementing a 5-bit signal "wave". The waveform generation is controlled by a state machine with two states: 0 for incrementing the waveform and 1 for decrementing it. The state transitions occur when the waveform reaches its maximum value (31) or minimum value (0). The design uses an always block to update the state and waveform registers on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal.
