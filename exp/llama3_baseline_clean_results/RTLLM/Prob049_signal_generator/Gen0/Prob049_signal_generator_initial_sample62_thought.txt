To design the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) approach with two states: one for incrementing the waveform and another for decrementing it. The FSM will be implemented using a case statement within an always block that triggers on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal. We will also use two registers: one for the state and another for the waveform. The waveform register will be updated based on the current state and its value.
