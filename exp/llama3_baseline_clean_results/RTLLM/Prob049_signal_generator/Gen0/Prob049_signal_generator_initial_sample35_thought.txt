To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use two registers: state and wave. The state register will be used to control the state of the waveform generation, and the wave register will represent the generated waveform. The module will have two states: incrementing the waveform (state = 0) and decrementing the waveform (state = 1). The waveform generation will be controlled using a case statement based on the current state. The state and waveform registers will be updated in the always block, triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal.
