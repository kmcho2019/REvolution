To design the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will implement a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The FSM will control the direction of the waveform based on the current state and the value of the waveform. We will use a 5-bit register to represent the waveform and update it on the positive edge of the clock signal. The state transitions will be based on the value of the waveform, transitioning from incrementing to decrementing when the waveform reaches 31, and from decrementing to incrementing when the waveform reaches 0.
