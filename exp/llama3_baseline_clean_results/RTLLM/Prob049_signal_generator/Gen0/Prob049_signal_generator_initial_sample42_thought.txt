The Triangle Wave signal generator module can be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the generation of the waveform. The FSM consists of two states: incrementing and decrementing. The module uses two registers, state and wave, to keep track of the current state and the generated waveform, respectively. The state register is used to determine whether to increment or decrement the waveform register. The waveform register is updated based on the current state, and the state is updated when the waveform reaches its maximum or minimum value.
