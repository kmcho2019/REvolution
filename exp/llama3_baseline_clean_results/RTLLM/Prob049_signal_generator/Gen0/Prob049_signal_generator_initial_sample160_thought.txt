To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The wave signal will be incremented or decremented based on the current state. We will use a 5-bit register to represent the wave signal and another register to represent the state. The module will be synchronous, with all operations triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. The reset signal will be used to initialize the state and wave registers.
