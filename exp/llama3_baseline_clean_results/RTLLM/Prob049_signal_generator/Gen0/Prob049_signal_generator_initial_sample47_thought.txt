To design the signal_generator module, we will use two registers: state and wave. The state register will control the direction of the waveform generation (increment or decrement), and the wave register will store the generated waveform. We will use a case statement to determine the next state based on the current state and waveform values. The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal, allowing us to update the state and waveform registers synchronously.
