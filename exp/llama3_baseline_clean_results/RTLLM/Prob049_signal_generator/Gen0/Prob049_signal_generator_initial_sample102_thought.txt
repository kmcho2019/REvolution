The signal_generator module will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The module uses two registers: state and wave. The state register controls whether the waveform is being incremented or decremented, and the wave register holds the current value of the waveform. The FSM transitions between the two states based on the current value of the waveform. When the reset signal is active, both registers are reset to 0. The waveform is updated on every positive edge of the clock signal.
