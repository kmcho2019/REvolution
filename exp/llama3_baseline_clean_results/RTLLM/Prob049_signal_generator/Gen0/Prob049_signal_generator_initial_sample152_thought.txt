To implement a Triangle Wave signal generator module in Verilog, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the waveform generation. The FSM will have two states: incrementing and decrementing. We will use a 5-bit register to store the current waveform value. On every clock cycle, we will check the current state and the waveform value to determine whether to increment or decrement the waveform. If the waveform reaches its maximum value (31), we will transition to the decrementing state. If the waveform reaches its minimum value (0), we will transition to the incrementing state.
