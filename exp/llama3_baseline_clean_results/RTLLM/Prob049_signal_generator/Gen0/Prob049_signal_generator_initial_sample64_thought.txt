The signal_generator module will be designed with two registers: state and wave. The state register will control the direction of the waveform generation (incrementing or decrementing), and the wave register will store the generated waveform. The module will use a case statement to determine the next state and update the waveform accordingly. The waveform will cycle between 0 and 31 based on the state transitions. The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal or the negative edge of the reset signal, ensuring synchronous operation.
