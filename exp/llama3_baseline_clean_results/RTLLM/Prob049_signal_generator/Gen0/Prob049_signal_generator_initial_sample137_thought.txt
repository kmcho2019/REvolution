To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the generation of the waveform. The FSM will have two states: incrementing and decrementing. We will use a case statement to handle the state transitions and waveform updates. The waveform generation will be based on a 5-bit signal that increments or decrements by 1. The state and waveform registers will be updated synchronously with the clock signal.
