To solve this problem, we can use two different clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, to synchronize the data_in signal. The data_in signal is stored in a register, data_reg, on the positive edge of clk_a. The enable signal, data_en, is also stored in a register, en_data_reg, on the positive edge of clk_a. Then, we use two control registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clk_b. Finally, we assign the output data value based on the control signal en_clap_two.
