To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, I will follow these steps:
1. Create a data register to store the input data signal, data_in, which is updated on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn).
2. Create an enable data register, en_data_reg, to store the input enable signal, data_en, which is also updated on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn).
3. Create two enable control registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to control the selection of the input data. These registers are updated on the positive edge of clock signal B (clk_b) or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn).
4. Assign the output data value based on the control signal (en_clap_two) on the positive edge of clock signal B (clk_b) or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn).

This approach ensures that the data change frequency of data_in is handled correctly, and the enable signal data_en is delayed by two cycles as required.
