To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will use a combination of registers and clock domain crossing (CDC) to transfer data from the clk_a domain to the clk_b domain. The synchronizer will have the following components:
1. Data Register: Stores the input data signal, data_in, on the positive edge of clk_a or the falling edge of arstn.
2. Enable Data Register: Stores the input enable signal, data_en, on the positive edge of clk_a or the falling edge of arstn.
3. Enable Control Registers: Two registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to control the selection of the input data. These registers are updated on the positive edge of clk_b or the falling edge of brstn.
4. Output Assignment: Assigns the output data value on the positive edge of clk_b or the falling edge of brstn based on the control signal en_clap_two.

The CDC will be implemented using two D flip-flops to delay the enable signal, data_en, by two cycles in the clk_b domain.
