The synchronizer module will be implemented using two separate clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, with corresponding reset signals arstn and brstn. 
The module will first capture the input data signal, data_in, and the enable signal, data_en, on the rising edge of clk_a into data_reg and en_data_reg, respectively.
The enable signal, en_data_reg, will then be synchronized to the clk_b domain through two D flip-flops, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to ensure that the enable signal is stable for at least two clk_b cycles.
The output data signal, dataout, will be assigned the value of data_reg on the rising edge of clk_b when en_clap_two is active, indicating that the enable signal has been stable for at least two clk_b cycles.
