The problem involves designing a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer that synchronizes input data from one clock domain (clk_a) to another (clk_b) based on an enable signal (data_en). The design includes several key components: 
1. Data Register (data_reg): Stores the input data signal (data_in) on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn).
2. Enable Data Register (en_data_reg): Stores the input enable signal (data_en) on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn).
3. Enable Control Registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two): Control the selection of the input data based on the enable signal. They are updated on the positive edge of clock signal B (clk_b) or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn), introducing a 2-clock cycle delay in the enable signal from the clk_a domain to the clk_b domain.
4. Output Assignment: The output data (dataout) is assigned the value of the data register (data_reg) on the positive edge of clock signal B (clk_b) or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn) when the control signal (en_clap_two) is active. 

This approach ensures that the data is synchronized from the clk_a domain to the clk_b domain, with a guaranteed minimum delay of two clk_b clock cycles after the data_en signal becomes active.
