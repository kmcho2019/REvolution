To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will use a combination of registers and clock signals to ensure proper synchronization of the input data. The module will have two main sections: one for registering the input data and enable signal with respect to clock A, and another for controlling the output selection based on clock B. The use of two D flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) will allow for a two-clock-cycle delay in the enable signal, ensuring that data_in remains constant during the period when data_en is high. We will utilize the arstn and brstn reset signals to initialize the registers accordingly.
