The synchronizer module is designed to transfer data from one clock domain (clk_a) to another (clk_b) using a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer. The data_in remains constant during the period when data_en is high, and data_en is high for at least 3 clk_b clock cycles. To achieve this, the module uses several registers to store the input data and enable signals, and control the output data based on the enable signals. The data register (data_reg) and enable data register (en_data_reg) are updated on the positive edge of clk_a or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn). The enable control registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) are updated on the positive edge of clk_b or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn), and are used to control the selection of the input data. The output data (dataout) is assigned based on the value of en_clap_two.
