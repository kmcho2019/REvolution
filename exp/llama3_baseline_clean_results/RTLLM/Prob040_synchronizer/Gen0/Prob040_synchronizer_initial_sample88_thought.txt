To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will follow the provided specification. The design involves two clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. The input data_in is captured by a register data_reg on the positive edge of clk_a, and the input enable signal data_en is captured by a register en_data_reg also on the positive edge of clk_a. The enable control is implemented by two registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, which are updated on the positive edge of clk_b. The output dataout is assigned based on the value of en_clap_two and the data stored in data_reg, with the assignment occurring on the positive edge of clk_b.
