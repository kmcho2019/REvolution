To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we'll first design the data register and enable data register that operate on clock A (clk_a) and reset signal A (arstn). Then, we'll design the enable control registers that operate on clock B (clk_b) and reset signal B (brstn). These control registers will introduce a delay of two clock cycles of clk_b to the enable signal. Finally, we'll assign the output data based on the control signals and clock B.
