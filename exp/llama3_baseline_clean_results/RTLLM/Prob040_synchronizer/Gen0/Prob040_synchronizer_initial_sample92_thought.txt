We will use two clock domains (clk_a and clk_b) to manage the data synchronization. The data_in and data_en signals are referred to clock_a, while the output dataout is referred to clock_b. We will also introduce two enable control registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to delay the enable signal by two cycles of clock_b.
