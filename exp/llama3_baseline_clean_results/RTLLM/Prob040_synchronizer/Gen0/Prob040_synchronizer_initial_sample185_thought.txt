The multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer module is designed to synchronize input data from one clock domain (clk_a) to another (clk_b) while ensuring that the data is stable and valid during the synchronization process. This is achieved through a combination of data registers and enable control registers that are updated based on the input enable signal (data_en) and the clock signals. The module uses two clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, with the input data and enable signal referring to clk_a, and the output data referring to clk_b. The enable signal is delayed by two cycles of clk_b through two D flip-flops (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to ensure proper synchronization. The data is output only when the delayed enable signal (en_clap_two) is active, ensuring that the data has been stabilized across the clock domain boundary.
