The synchronizer module will be designed using two clocks (clk_a and clk_b) and two reset signals (arstn and brstn). 
We will first create registers to store the input data (data_in) and the enable signal (data_en) synchronized with clock A.
Then, we will create two additional registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clk_b.
Finally, we will assign the output data (dataout) based on the delayed enable signal (en_clap_two) and the stored input data (data_reg), using clk_b for synchronization.
