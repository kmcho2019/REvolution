To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer as described, we will follow these steps:
- Create a data register (data_reg) that captures the input data (data_in) on the positive edge of clock A (clk_a) when enable signal data_en is high. 
- Create an enable data register (en_data_reg) to capture the input enable signal (data_en) on the positive edge of clock A (clk_a).
- Use two enable control registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) clocked by clock B (clk_b) to delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clk_b. 
- Finally, output the data (dataout) on the positive edge of clock B (clk_b) when the delayed enable signal (en_clap_two) is high.
We ensure that the design is resettable using the active-low reset signals arstn and brstn for clock domains A and B, respectively.
