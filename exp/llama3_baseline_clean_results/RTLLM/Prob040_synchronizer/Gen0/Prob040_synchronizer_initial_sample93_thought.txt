The module synchronizer is designed to synchronize the input data_in signal with the clock clk_b, ensuring that the data is stable for at least 3 clk_b clock cycles when data_en is high. The module includes several registers to store the input data signal, the enable signal, and control signals to manage the synchronization process. The data register and enable data register are updated on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn). The enable control registers are updated on the positive edge of clock signal B (clk_b) or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn). The output data is assigned based on the control signal en_clap_two and the reset signal B (brstn).
