The design concept involves creating a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer with two clocks, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. The module has input data (data_in) and enable signal (data_en) referenced to clock A, and the output (dataout) is referenced to clock B. 
The implementation includes several registers: data_reg to store the input data, en_data_reg to store the input enable signal, and two control registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to control the selection of the input data based on the enable signal delayed by two clock cycles of clk_b. 
The output data (dataout) is updated on the positive edge of clk_b if the delayed enable signal (en_clap_two) is active; otherwise, it retains its previous value.
