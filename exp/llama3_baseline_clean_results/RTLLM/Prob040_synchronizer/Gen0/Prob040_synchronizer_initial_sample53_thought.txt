To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we'll create a Verilog module named synchronizer that includes several registers to store the input data signal and enable signals. The module will update these registers based on the clock signals and reset conditions. We'll use two clocks, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. The data_in signal will be stored in the data_reg register, and the data_en signal will be stored in the en_data_reg register. Two additional registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, will be used to control the selection of the input data based on the enable signal. The output dataout will be assigned based on the control signal en_clap_two.
