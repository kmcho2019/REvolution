The goal is to design a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer that transfers data from one clock domain to another while ensuring synchronization. The module will have two main sections: one clocked by clk_a and responsible for capturing the input data and enable signal, and another clocked by clk_b that delays the enable signal by two cycles and uses it to control the output data. The synchronizer will update its registers based on the provided clocks and reset signals, ensuring proper synchronization and data transfer.
