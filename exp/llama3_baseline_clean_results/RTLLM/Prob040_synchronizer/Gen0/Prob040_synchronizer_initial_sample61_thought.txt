2. Creating an enable data register (en_data_reg) to store the input enable signal (data_en), also updated on the positive edge of clk_a or the falling edge of arstn.

4. Finally, assigning the output data (dataout) based on the value of en_clap_two and the data stored in data_reg, ensuring that the output is synchronized with clk_b.
