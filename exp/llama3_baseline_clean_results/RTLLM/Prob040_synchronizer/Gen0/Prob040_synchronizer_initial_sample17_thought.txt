To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer module, I will follow the given requirements and create a design that includes data and enable registers, as well as control registers for synchronization. The data register (data_reg) and enable register (en_data_reg) will be updated based on the clock signal A (clk_a) and reset signal A (arstn). The control registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) will be updated based on the clock signal B (clk_b) and reset signal B (brstn), and will be used to control the output data assignment. The output data (dataout) will be assigned based on the control signal (en_clap_two) and the clock signal B (clk_b).
