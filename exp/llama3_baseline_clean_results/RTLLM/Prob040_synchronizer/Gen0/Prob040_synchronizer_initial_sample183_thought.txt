To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer module in Verilog, we need to follow the provided specification. The module has two clocks, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. The enable signal, data_en, controls the selection operation and is also synchronized with clock clk_a. We will use two D flip-flops to delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clk_b.

The module includes three main components: 
1. Data Register: stores the input data signal, data_in.
2. Enable Data Register: stores the input enable signal, data_en.
3. Enable Control Registers: include two registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to control the selection of the input data.

The data register and enable data register will be updated on the positive edge of clock signal A (clk_a) or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn).
