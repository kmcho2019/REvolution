To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will use two separate clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. The input data signal, data_in, and enable signal, data_en, will be synchronized across these clock domains using two D flip-flops for the enable signal. We will use registers to store the input data and enable signals, and update them based on the clock edges and reset conditions. The output data, dataout, will be assigned based on the control signals generated from the enable signal.
