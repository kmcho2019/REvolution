To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer module, we will follow the given specifications. The module will have two clock domains, clk_a and clk_b, and two reset signals, arstn and brstn. It will include several registers to store the input data and enable signals. The data register and enable data register will be updated based on clk_a and arstn, while the enable control registers will be updated based on clk_b and brstn. The output data will be assigned based on the control signal en_clap_two.
