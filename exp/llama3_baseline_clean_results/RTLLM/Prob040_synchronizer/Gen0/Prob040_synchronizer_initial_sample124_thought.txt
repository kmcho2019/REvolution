The synchronizer module will include several registers to handle the input data and enable signals across two different clock domains. It will use a data register and an enable data register to capture the input data and enable signals on the rising edge of clock signal A (clk_a). Then, it will use two enable control registers, en_clap_one and en_clap_two, to delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clock signal B (clk_b). The output data will be assigned based on the delayed enable signal and the captured data. The module will also handle reset conditions for both clock domains.
