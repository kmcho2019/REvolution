The design involves creating a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer. It includes several registers to handle data and enable signals across two different clock domains (clk_a and clk_b). The main idea is to capture input data (data_in) and its enable signal (data_en) in one clock domain and then transfer this information to the other clock domain with proper synchronization. The data is only updated when the enable signal (data_en) is active for at least three clock cycles of clk_b and data_in remains constant during this period. The synchronizer ensures data stability and proper enable signal delay before outputting the synchronized data (dataout) in the clk_b domain.
