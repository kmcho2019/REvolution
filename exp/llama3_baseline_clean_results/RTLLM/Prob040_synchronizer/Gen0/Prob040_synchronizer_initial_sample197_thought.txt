To implement the synchronizer module, we will follow the given specifications:
- We will use a register `data_reg` to store the input data signal `data_in`, updated on the positive edge of `clk_a` or the falling edge of `arstn`.
- We will use a register `en_data_reg` to store the input enable signal `data_en`, updated on the positive edge of `clk_a` or the falling edge of `arstn`.
- Two enable control registers `en_clap_one` and `en_clap_two` will be used to control the selection of the input data, updated on the positive edge of `clk_b` or the falling edge of `brstn`.
- The output data `dataout` will be assigned based on the control signal `en_clap_two` and the value of `data_reg`.

The main steps involve:
1. Defining the input/output ports and internal registers.
2. Writing the logic for updating the registers based on the clock and reset signals.
3. Assigning the output data based on the control signal.
