To design the synchronizer module as per the given requirements, we will implement it using Verilog HDL. The module will include two main registers: one for data (data_reg) and one for the enable signal (en_data_reg), both operating on clock A. For clock B, we will have two registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) that control the output data selection based on the enable signal delayed by two clock cycles of clock B. The output dataout will be assigned based on the state of en_clap_two and the reset signal brstn.
