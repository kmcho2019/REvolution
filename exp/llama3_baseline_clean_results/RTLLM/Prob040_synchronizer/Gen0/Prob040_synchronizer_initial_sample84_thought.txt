The module 'synchronizer' implements a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer with two clocks (clk_a and clk_b) and two active-low reset signals (arstn and brstn). 
It includes several registers: data_reg to store the input data signal (data_in), en_data_reg to store the input enable signal (data_en), 
en_clap_one and en_clap_two to control the selection of the input data. 
When data_en is high for at least 3 clk_b clock cycles and data_in remains constant during this period, data can be synchronized from clk_a domain to clk_b domain.
