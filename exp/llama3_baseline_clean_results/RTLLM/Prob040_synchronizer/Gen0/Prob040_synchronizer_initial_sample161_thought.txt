To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we need to follow the specifications given. The module will have two main parts: one working with clock A (clk_a) for input data and enable signal, and another part working with clock B (clk_b) for delayed enable signal and output. The data_in will be stored in a register (data_reg) on the positive edge of clk_a or the falling edge of reset signal A (arstn). The enable signal data_en will be delayed by two cycles of clk_b using two registers (en_clap_one and en_clap_two) to ensure the data can be synchronized correctly. The output dataout will be assigned the value of data_reg when en_clap_two is high and the previous value of dataout when en_clap_two is low, all on the positive edge of clk_b or the falling edge of reset signal B (brstn).
