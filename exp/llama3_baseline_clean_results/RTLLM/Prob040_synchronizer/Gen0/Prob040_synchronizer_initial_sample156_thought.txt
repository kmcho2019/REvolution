To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will first design the data register and enable data register that operate on clock signal A (clk_a) and are reset by the active-low reset signal A (arstn). Then, we will create the enable control registers that operate on clock signal B (clk_b) and are reset by the active-low reset signal B (brstn). These enable control registers will delay the enable signal by two clock cycles of clk_b. Finally, we will assign the output dataout based on the delayed enable signal and the data register. The output will be synchronized to clock signal B (clk_b).
