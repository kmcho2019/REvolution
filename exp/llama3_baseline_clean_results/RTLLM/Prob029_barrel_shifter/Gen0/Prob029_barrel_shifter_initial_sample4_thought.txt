To design the barrel_shifter module, we'll first create a submodule for the 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) that selects between two inputs based on a select signal. Then, we'll use this submodule to create the stages of the barrel shifter. Each stage will shift the input by a specific amount based on the control signals. We'll chain these stages together to achieve the final shift amount. The control signals `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]` will determine whether to shift by 4, 2, or 1 position, respectively, at each stage.
