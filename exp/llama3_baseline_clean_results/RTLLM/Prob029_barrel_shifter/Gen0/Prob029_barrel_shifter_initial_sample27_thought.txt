The barrel shifter will be implemented using a hierarchical approach, where each stage of shifting is achieved through a series of 2-to-1 multiplexers (mux2X1). The 3-bit control signal `ctrl` determines how many positions to shift the input `in`. Each bit of `ctrl` corresponds to a specific shift amount: `ctrl[2]` for a 4-position shift, `ctrl[1]` for a 2-position shift, and `ctrl[0]` for a 1-position shift. The shifts are performed in a cascaded manner, with each stage's output serving as the input to the next stage. The final output `out` will be the shifted version of `in` based on the `ctrl` signal.
