To implement the barrel shifter module, we first define the submodule `mux2X1` that acts as a 2-to-1 multiplexer. This submodule selects between two input values based on the `sel` signal. Then, in the main `barrel_shifter` module, we use a series of these multiplexers to achieve the shifting in stages. The control signal `ctrl` is used to determine which multiplexers are enabled to perform the shifting by 1, 2, or 4 positions. The output of each stage of multiplexers is fed into the next stage, allowing for the combination of shifts to achieve the final desired rotation.
