1. **Stage 1 (Shift by 4):** If `ctrl[2]` is high, shift `in` by 4 positions.
2. **Stage 2 (Shift by 2):** If `ctrl[1]` is high, shift the result of Stage 1 by 2 positions.
3. **Stage 3 (Shift by 1):** If `ctrl[0]` is high, shift the result of Stage 2 by 1 position.

For each stage, we will use instances of the `mux2X1` module to select between the shifted input and the original input, based on the corresponding bit of the `ctrl` signal.
