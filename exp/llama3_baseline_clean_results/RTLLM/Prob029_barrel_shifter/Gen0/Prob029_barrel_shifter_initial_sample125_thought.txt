To design the barrel shifter module, we first need to define the 2-to-1 multiplexer submodule (`mux2X1`) that will be used throughout the shifter. This submodule selects between two input values based on a select signal. Then, in the `barrel_shifter` module, we will use these multiplexers in stages to achieve the shifting. The shifting process will be performed in three stages, corresponding to the control bits `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]`, each responsible for shifting by 4, 2, and 1 positions, respectively. We will use a hierarchical approach to implement the multiplexers and then connect them in the barrel shifter module to achieve the desired shifting behavior.
