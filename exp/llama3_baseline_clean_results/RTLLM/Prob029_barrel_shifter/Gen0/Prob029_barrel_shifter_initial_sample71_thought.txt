To implement the barrel shifter, we will first design a 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) that selects between two input values based on the `sel` signal. Then, we will create the `barrel_shifter` module, which consists of a series of these multiplexers. Each stage of multiplexers will perform the shifting by 4, 2, or 1 position based on the control signal `ctrl`. The shifts are performed in a hierarchical manner, where the result of one stage is the input to the next stage. This approach ensures that the input is shifted by the total number of positions specified by the control signal.
