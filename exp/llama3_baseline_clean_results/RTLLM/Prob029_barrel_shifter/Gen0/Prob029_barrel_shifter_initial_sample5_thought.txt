To implement the barrel shifter, we will first define the 2-to-1 multiplexer submodule `mux2X1`. This submodule will select between two inputs based on a control signal. Then, in the main module `barrel_shifter`, we will use the `mux2X1` submodule in a series of stages to achieve the required shifts. The first stage will handle the shift by 4 positions based on `ctrl[2]`, the second stage will handle the shift by 2 positions based on `ctrl[1]`, and the third stage will handle the shift by 1 position based on `ctrl[0]`. Each stage will use the output of the previous stage as one of its inputs and the original input shifted accordingly as the other input.
