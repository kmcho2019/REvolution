To design the barrel_shifter module, we'll first define a 2-to-1 multiplexer submodule `mux2X1` that selects between two input values based on a `sel` signal. Then, in the `barrel_shifter` module, we'll use this `mux2X1` submodule in a series of stages to achieve the shifting. Each stage will shift the input by 4, 2, or 1 positions based on the `ctrl` signal. The shifted outputs from each stage will be passed to the next stage for further shifting until all control bits have been considered.
