To design the barrel shifter module, we first define the basic 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) that will be used throughout the shifter to select between shifted and unshifted bits. The main `barrel_shifter` module then utilizes these multiplexers in stages to achieve the desired shifting based on the control signal. Each bit of the control signal (`ctrl`) controls whether a shift by 1, 2, or 4 positions occurs, with the shifts happening sequentially. The input is shifted by 4 positions first (controlled by `ctrl[2]`), then by 2 positions (controlled by `ctrl[1]`), and finally by 1 position (controlled by `ctrl[0]`). Each stage of shifting passes its result to the next stage for further shifting if required.
