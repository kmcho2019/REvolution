To implement the barrel shifter module, we will create a hierarchical design with multiple stages of 2-to-1 multiplexers. The top-level module, `barrel_shifter`, will instantiate the `mux2X1` submodule for each stage of shifting. The `ctrl` signal will be used to select the shift amount at each stage, starting with the most significant bit controlling the largest shift (4 positions) and progressing to the least significant bit controlling the smallest shift (1 position). This approach allows for efficient shifting of the input bits based on the `ctrl` signal.
