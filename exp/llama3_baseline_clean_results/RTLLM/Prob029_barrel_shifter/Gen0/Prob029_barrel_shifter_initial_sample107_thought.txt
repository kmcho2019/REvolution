To design the barrel shifter module, we will use a hierarchical approach with a series of 2-to-1 multiplexers (`mux2X1`) that perform the shifting at each stage. The control signals (`ctrl[2:0]`) will select the input to the multiplexers at each stage, effectively shifting the input bits. We will start by designing the `mux2X1` submodule and then use it in the barrel shifter module to perform the required shifts.
