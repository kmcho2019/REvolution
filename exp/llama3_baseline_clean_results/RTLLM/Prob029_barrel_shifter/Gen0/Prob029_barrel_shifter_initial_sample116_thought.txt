To implement the barrel shifter, we'll first define the submodule `mux2X1` that acts as a 2-to-1 multiplexer. This submodule will select between two inputs based on a select signal. Then, we'll use this submodule to create the stages of the barrel shifter. Each stage will apply a shift by 1, 2, or 4 positions based on the control signals `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]`, respectively. The output of each stage will be fed into the next stage, allowing for the cumulative shift as specified by the control signals.
