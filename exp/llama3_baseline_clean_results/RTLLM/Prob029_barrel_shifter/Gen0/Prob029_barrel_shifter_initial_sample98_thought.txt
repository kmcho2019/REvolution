To implement the barrel shifter, we'll first define a 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) that selects between two input values based on the `sel` signal. Then, we'll design the main `barrel_shifter` module, which uses a series of `mux2X1` instances to achieve the bit shifting in stages controlled by the `ctrl` signal. We'll utilize the `ctrl[2]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[0]` bits to determine whether to shift the input by 4, 2, or 1 position, respectively, in each stage.
