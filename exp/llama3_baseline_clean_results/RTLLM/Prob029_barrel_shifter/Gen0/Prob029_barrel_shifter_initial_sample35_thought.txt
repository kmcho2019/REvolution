To implement the barrel shifter, we'll design a hierarchical module that consists of a series of 2-to-1 multiplexers (`mux2X1`) to perform the bit shifting based on the control signal. The design will involve three stages of shifting: by 4, 2, and 1 position, controlled by the respective bits of the `ctrl` signal. Each stage will use the `mux2X1` submodule to select between the shifted input and the original input, effectively performing the shift operation. The outputs from each stage will be passed to the next stage for further shifting until the final output is generated.
