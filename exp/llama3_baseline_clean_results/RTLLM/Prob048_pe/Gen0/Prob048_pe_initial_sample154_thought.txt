To implement a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) for 32-bit integers in Verilog, we will design a module named 'pe'. This module will have a register 'c' to store the partial sum, and in each cycle, it will accumulate the result of 'a' multiplied by 'b' into this register. The design will utilize a parallel multiplier and accumulator, leveraging registers and an always block for synchronous operations. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and the reset signal 'rst'. When 'rst' is high, the register 'c' will be reset to 0. When 'rst' is low, the module will update the register 'c' by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value, effectively performing the accumulation.
