To implement the Multiplying Accumulator, we will design a module named 'pe' that takes in a clock signal 'clk', a reset signal 'rst', and two 32-bit input operands 'a' and 'b'. The module will output the accumulated result 'c'. We will use an always block to update the accumulator register 'c' on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands to its current value.
