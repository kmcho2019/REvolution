To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers, we will design a module named 'pe' with input ports for clock, reset, and two 32-bit operands 'a' and 'b', and an output port 'c' to display the accumulated result. The module will utilize a register to store the partial sum and perform multiplication and accumulation operations based on the clock and reset signals. The always block will be used to update the register on the positive edge of the clock or reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0; otherwise, it will accumulate the product of 'a' and 'b' into the register.
