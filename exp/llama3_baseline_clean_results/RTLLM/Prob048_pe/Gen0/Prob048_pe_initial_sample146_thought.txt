The module pe will be designed with a register to store the accumulated result. It will have an always block that checks for the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the positive edge of the reset signal (rst). If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0. If the reset signal is low, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands (a and b) to its current value. This will be achieved by using a non-blocking assignment to the register, ensuring that the update is done synchronously with the clock signal.
