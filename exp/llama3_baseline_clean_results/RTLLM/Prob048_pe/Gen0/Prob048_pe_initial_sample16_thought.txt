To implement a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) for 32-bit integers, we will use a Verilog module named "pe". This module will have a register to store the partial sum (intermediate accumulation result). The module will perform the multiplication of input operands "a" and "b" and accumulate the result into the register "c". The accumulation operation will be performed on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or when the reset signal (rst) is high. If the reset signal is high, the register "c" will be reset to 0. If the reset signal is low, the register "c" will be updated by adding the product of "a" and "b" to its current value.
