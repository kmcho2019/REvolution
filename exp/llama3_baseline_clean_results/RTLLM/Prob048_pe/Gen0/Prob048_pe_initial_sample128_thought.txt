To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers, we will design a module named 'pe' with input ports for clock, reset, and two operands (a and b), and an output port for the accumulated result (c). We will use an always block to update the register 'c' based on the reset signal and the product of 'a' and 'b'. If the reset signal is high, the register 'c' will be reset to 0; otherwise, it will accumulate the product of 'a' and 'b' in each clock cycle.
