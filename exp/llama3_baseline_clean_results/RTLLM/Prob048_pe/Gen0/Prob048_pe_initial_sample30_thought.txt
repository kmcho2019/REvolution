The design idea here is to create a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module named "pe" that takes in two 32-bit operands "a" and "b" and accumulates their product in a register "c". The module will have a clock signal "clk" for synchronous operations and a reset signal "rst" to reset the accumulator register "c". On each positive edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, the module will update the register "c" by adding the product of "a" and "b" to its current value. If the reset signal is high, the register "c" will be reset to 0.
