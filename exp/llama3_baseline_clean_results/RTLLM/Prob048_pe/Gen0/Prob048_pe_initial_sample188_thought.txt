1. On the positive edge of the clock signal (clk), if the reset signal (rst) is high, the register "c" should be set to 0.
2. If the reset signal (rst) is low, the register "c" should be updated by adding the product of the input operands "a" and "b" to its current value.
3. The module should output the accumulated result "c".
