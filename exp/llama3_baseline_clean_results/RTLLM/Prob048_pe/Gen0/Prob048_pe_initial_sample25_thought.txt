To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers, we will design a module named 'pe' that includes a register 'c' to store the partial sum (intermediate accumulation result). We will use an always block to update the register on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the positive edge of the reset signal (rst). If the reset signal (rst) is high, the register 'c' will be set to 0. If the reset signal (rst) is low, the register 'c' will be updated by adding the product of the input operands (a and b) to its current value. The multiplication and accumulation operation will be performed inside the always block.
