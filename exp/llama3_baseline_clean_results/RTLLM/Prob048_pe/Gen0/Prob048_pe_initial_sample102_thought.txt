To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) in Verilog, we will design a module named 'pe' that takes in a clock signal 'clk', a reset signal 'rst', and two 32-bit input operands 'a' and 'b'. The module will have an internal register 'c' to store the accumulated result. On the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, the module will update the register 'c' by either resetting it to 0 if 'rst' is high or by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value if 'rst' is low.
