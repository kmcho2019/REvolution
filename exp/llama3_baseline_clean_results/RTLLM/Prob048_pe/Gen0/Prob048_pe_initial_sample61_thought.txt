To implement a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) in Verilog, we will use a combination of registers and an always block. The design will include a register to store the partial sum (c) and will update this register based on the clock signal (clk) and the reset signal (rst). In each cycle, the result of "a multiplied by b" will be accumulated in this register. When the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0; otherwise, it will accumulate the product of the input operands a and b.
