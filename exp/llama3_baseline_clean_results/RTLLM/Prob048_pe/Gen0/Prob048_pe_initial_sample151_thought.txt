To implement the Multiplying Accumulator, we will use a combination of registers and an always block to perform the multiplication and accumulation operation. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal and the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the accumulator register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands to its current value. We will use the '*' operator to perform the multiplication and the '+' operator to perform the accumulation.
