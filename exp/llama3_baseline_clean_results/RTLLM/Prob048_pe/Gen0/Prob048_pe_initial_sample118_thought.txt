To implement the Multiplying Accumulator, we will design a Verilog module named 'pe' with input ports 'clk', 'rst', 'a', and 'b', and an output port 'c'. The module will use a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. On the positive edge of 'clk', if 'rst' is low, the module will update 'c' by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value. If 'rst' is high, 'c' will be reset to 0.
