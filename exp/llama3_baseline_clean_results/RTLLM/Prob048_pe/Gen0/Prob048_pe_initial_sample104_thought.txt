To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) for 32-bit integers, we'll use a parallel multiplier and accumulator design. The module, named 'pe', will include a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. We'll utilize an always block to update the register on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. When the reset signal is high, the register will be set to 0. Otherwise, it will be updated by adding the product of the input operands to its current value. This approach ensures a synchronous operation with reset functionality.
