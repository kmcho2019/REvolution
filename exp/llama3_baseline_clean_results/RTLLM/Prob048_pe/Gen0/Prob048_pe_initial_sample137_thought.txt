To implement a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) in Verilog, we need to design a module that can multiply two 32-bit integers and accumulate the result in a register. The module will have a clock signal (clk) and a reset signal (rst) for synchronous operations. The inputs will be two 32-bit operands (a and b), and the output will be the accumulated result (c). We will use an always block to update the register on the positive edge of the clock signal or the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands to its current value.
