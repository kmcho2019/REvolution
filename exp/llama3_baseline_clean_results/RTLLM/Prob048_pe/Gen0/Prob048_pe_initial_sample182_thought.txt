To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE), we will create a Verilog module named "pe" that takes in two 32-bit operands (a and b) and a clock signal (clk) along with a reset signal (rst). The module will accumulate the product of "a" and "b" into a 32-bit register (c). On the positive edge of the clock, if the reset signal is low, the module will update the register "c" by adding the product of "a" and "b" to its current value. If the reset signal is high, the register "c" will be reset to 0. We will utilize an always block to describe this behavior, ensuring the operation is synchronous with the clock signal.
