To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE), we need to design a module 'pe' that takes in two 32-bit operands 'a' and 'b', a clock signal 'clk', and a reset signal 'rst'. The module will multiply 'a' and 'b', accumulate the result in a register 'c', and output the accumulated result. The register 'c' will be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal or reset signal. If 'rst' is high, 'c' will be reset to 0; otherwise, 'c' will be updated by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value.
