To implement the Multiplying Accumulator, we need to design a module that includes a register to store the accumulated result, and an always block to update this register. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0. If the reset signal is low, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands to its current value. We can use the '*' operator in Verilog to perform the multiplication.
