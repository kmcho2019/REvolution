To implement the Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers in Verilog, I will use a synchronous design approach. The design will include a register to store the accumulated result, which will be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal or when the reset signal is active. Inside the always block, I will first check for the reset condition. If the reset signal is high, the accumulator register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, I will multiply the input operands and accumulate the result into the register. The output of the accumulator register will be driven to the output port.
