To design the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) module in Verilog, we will use an always block to handle the synchronous operations based on the clock and reset signals. The multiplication of the input operands 'a' and 'b' will be performed within this block, and the result will be accumulated in the register 'c'. On reset, the register 'c' will be set to 0. Otherwise, on each positive edge of the clock, the new product of 'a' and 'b' will be added to 'c', effectively accumulating the result over time.
