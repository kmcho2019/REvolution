To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE), we will create a Verilog module named 'pe'. This module will have a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. The 'always' block will be used to update the register on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the positive edge of the reset signal (rst). Inside the always block, we will perform the multiplication of the input operands 'a' and 'b' and accumulate the result into the register 'c'. If the reset signal (rst) is high, the register 'c' will be reset to 0.
