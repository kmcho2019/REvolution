To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers, we will design a module named 'pe' with input ports 'clk', 'rst', 'a', and 'b', and an output port 'c'. The module will utilize a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. On each positive edge of the clock signal 'clk', the module will update the register 'c' by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value, unless the reset signal 'rst' is high, in which case the register 'c' will be reset to 0.
