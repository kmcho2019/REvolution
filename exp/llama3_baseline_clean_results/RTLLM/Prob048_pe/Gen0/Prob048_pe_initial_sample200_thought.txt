To implement a Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) in Verilog, we can use a combination of registers and an always block to perform the multiplication and accumulation operations. The design will include a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. On the positive edge of the clock signal (clk), the register will be updated. If the reset signal (rst) is high, the register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the register will be updated by adding the product of the input operands 'a' and 'b' to its current value. This can be achieved using a non-blocking assignment inside an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal.
