To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module named 'pe', I will use a Verilog module with input ports for the clock (clk), reset (rst), and two 32-bit operands (a and b). The output will be a 32-bit register (c) that accumulates the product of 'a' and 'b' on each clock cycle. The accumulation will be done within an always block that checks for the positive edge of the clock or the reset signal. If the reset signal is high, the register 'c' will be reset to 0; otherwise, it will accumulate the product of 'a' and 'b'.
