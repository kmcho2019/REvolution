The comparator_4bit module will be designed using combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers, A and B. 
To achieve this, a subtraction operation A - B will be performed. The result of this operation will help determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. 
If a borrow occurs during the subtraction (i.e., a carry-out from the most significant bit), it indicates that A is less than B. 
If no borrow occurs and the result of subtraction is non-zero, it means A is greater than B. 
Lastly, if the result of subtraction is zero, A and B are equal.
The outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) are mutually exclusive, ensuring only one of the three outputs is high (1) at any given time.
