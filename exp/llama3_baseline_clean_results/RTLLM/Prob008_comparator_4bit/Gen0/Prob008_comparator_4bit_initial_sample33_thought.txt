To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will use a combination of bit-level subtraction and comparison logic. The subtraction operation will be performed by creating a ripple-borrow subtractor, where each bit position subtracts the corresponding bit of B from A and generates a borrow for the next position if necessary. The borrow generation will be key to determining if A is less than B. The result of the subtraction will be used to determine if A is greater than or equal to B. Since we are dealing with 4-bit numbers, we will perform the subtraction bit by bit, starting from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB). The final result and borrow status will determine the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
