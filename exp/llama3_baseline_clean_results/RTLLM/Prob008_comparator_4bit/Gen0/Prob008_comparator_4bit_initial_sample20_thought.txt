The 4-bit comparator module will be designed using combinational logic. It will compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B by performing a subtraction operation (A - B). The result of this subtraction will determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one of the three outputs is high (1) at any given time.
