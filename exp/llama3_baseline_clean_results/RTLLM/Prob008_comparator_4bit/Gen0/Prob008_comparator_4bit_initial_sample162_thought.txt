The design of the comparator_4bit module will utilize combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers, A and B. The comparison logic will perform a subtraction operation (A - B) to determine the relationship between A and B. 
The result of this subtraction will be used to generate the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less, which are mutually exclusive, ensuring only one output is high at any given time.
The module will use a full subtractor circuit at each bit position to handle borrow propagation.
The final output will be determined based on the result of the subtraction and the borrow generated during the subtraction process.
