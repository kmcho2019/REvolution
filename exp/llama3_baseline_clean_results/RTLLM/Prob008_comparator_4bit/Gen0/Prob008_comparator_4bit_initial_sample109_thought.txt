To implement a 4-bit comparator using combinational logic, we'll design a module named `comparator_4bit` that takes two 4-bit inputs `A` and `B`, and produces three outputs: `A_greater`, `A_equal`, and `A_less`, which indicate whether `A` is greater than, equal to, or less than `B`, respectively. We will use bit-level subtraction to determine the relationship between `A` and `B`. The subtraction operation will help us detect if a borrow occurs (indicating `A` is less than `B`), if the result is non-zero without borrow (indicating `A` is greater than `B`), or if the result is zero (indicating `A` is equal to `B`). The outputs will be encoded such that only one of `A_greater`, `A_equal`, or `A_less` is high at any given time, ensuring mutual exclusivity.
