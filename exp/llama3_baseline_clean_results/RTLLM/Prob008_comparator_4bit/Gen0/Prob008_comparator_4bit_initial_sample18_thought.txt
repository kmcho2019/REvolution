To implement the comparator_4bit module, we will use bit-level comparators in combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B. We will perform a subtraction operation A - B to determine the relationship between A and B. The result of this subtraction will be analyzed to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We will also generate a borrow signal to detect if A is less than B. The outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one of the three outputs is high (1) at any given time.
