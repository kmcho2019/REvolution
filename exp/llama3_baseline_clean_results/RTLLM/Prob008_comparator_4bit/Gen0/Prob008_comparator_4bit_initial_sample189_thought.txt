The comparator_4bit module will utilize bit-level combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B. It performs a subtraction operation A - B and analyzes the result to determine if A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are mutually exclusive, ensuring that only one output is high at a time. The module will be designed using basic digital logic components such as full subtractors to perform the subtraction operation.
