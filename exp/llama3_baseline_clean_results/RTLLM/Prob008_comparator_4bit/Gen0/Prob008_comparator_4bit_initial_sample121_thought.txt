To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will use combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B. We will perform a subtraction operation (A - B) and use the result to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one of the three outputs is high (1) at any given time. 
The logic for the comparator can be broken down into three main parts:
1. Subtraction logic to compute A - B.
2. Borrow detection to determine if A is less than B.
3. Result decoding to set the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the subtraction result and borrow status.
We will use a combination of bitwise operators and conditional statements to implement this logic in Verilog.
