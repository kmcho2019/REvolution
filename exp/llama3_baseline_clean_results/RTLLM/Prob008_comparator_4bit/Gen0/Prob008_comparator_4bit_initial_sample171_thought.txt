To implement the comparator_4bit module, we can use a combination of subtractors and logical gates to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We can perform a bit-level subtraction operation between A and B, and use the result along with borrow signals to generate the comparison outputs. Since the module uses combinational logic, there's no need for any sequential elements like flip-flops. The output encoding is designed such that only one of the three outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) is high at any given time, making them mutually exclusive.
