If no borrow occurs, we then check the result of the subtraction to determine if A is greater than or equal to B. The result of the subtraction being zero indicates that A and B are equal. 

We will use a full subtractor for each bit position, which can handle the borrow from the previous bit position, allowing us to perform the subtraction A - B. The full subtractor will give us the difference and the borrow information. 

After performing the subtraction, we analyze the borrow and the result to set the A_greater, A_equal, and A_less signals accordingly.
