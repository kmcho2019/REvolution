To implement a 4-bit comparator with multiple bit-level comparators in combinational logic, we will perform a subtraction operation (A - B) and use the result and the borrow flag to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We will use a full subtractor for each bit, generating the difference and the borrow. The borrow from the most significant bit will be used to determine if A is less than B. If no borrow occurs during the subtraction and the result is non-zero, A is greater than B. If the result is zero, A is equal to B. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one is high at any given time.
