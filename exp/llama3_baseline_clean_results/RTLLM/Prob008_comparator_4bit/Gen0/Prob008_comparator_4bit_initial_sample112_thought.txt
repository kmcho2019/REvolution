To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we can utilize a combinational logic approach. The comparison logic will perform a subtraction operation (A - B) to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We will generate a borrow to detect if A is less than B and check the result of subtraction to determine if A is greater than or equal to B. The outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one of the three outputs is high (1) at any given time.
