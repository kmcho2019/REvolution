To implement a 4-bit comparator in Verilog, we can utilize the concept of subtraction between two binary numbers and observe the borrow and result to determine the relationship between the numbers. The comparator module will have two 4-bit input ports (A and B) and three 1-bit output ports (A_greater, A_equal, A_less). The comparison logic will perform a subtraction operation (A - B), and based on the result and borrow, it will set the output ports accordingly. The output ports will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high at any given time.
