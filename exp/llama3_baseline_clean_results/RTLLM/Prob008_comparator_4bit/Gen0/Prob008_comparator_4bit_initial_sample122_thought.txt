The module comparator_4bit will compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B using combinational logic. It performs a subtraction operation (A - B) and analyzes the result to determine if A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The output ports (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high (1) at any given time, indicating the result of the comparison.
