To design the 4-bit comparator module, we can utilize the concept of subtraction to compare the two input operands A and B. The subtraction operation A - B will be performed bit by bit, considering the borrow from the previous bit. We will use a combination of full subtractors to achieve this. 

The comparator module will generate three output signals: A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. These signals will be mutually exclusive, ensuring that only one of them is high (1) at any given time, indicating whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B, respectively.

We will use the following steps to implement the comparator:

1. Perform the subtraction A - B using full subtractors.
2. Analyze the borrow and result of the subtraction to determine the relationship between A and B.
3. Assign the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison result.

By following these steps, we can create a 4-bit comparator module that accurately compares two binary numbers and produces the desired output signals.
