To implement the 4-bit comparator, we will utilize bit-level comparators in combinational logic to compare the input operands A and B. The comparison logic will be based on a subtraction operation, A - B, to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. This can be achieved by checking for borrow during subtraction. We will use a ripple borrow subtractor or a similar approach to implement the subtraction. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be generated based on the result of the subtraction and the borrow. These outputs will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high at any given time.
