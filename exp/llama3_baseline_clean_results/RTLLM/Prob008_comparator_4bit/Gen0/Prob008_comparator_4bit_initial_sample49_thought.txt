To implement a 4-bit comparator module, we need to perform subtraction and analyze the result along with any borrow that occurs. The comparison logic will determine the relationship between the two input operands A and B. 
The combinational logic for the comparator will involve full subtractors or a similar approach to handle the borrow and the result of the subtraction. 
We will then use this result and the borrow status to generate the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less, ensuring they are mutually exclusive.
This can be achieved by checking for the most significant bit (MSB) of the result for determining greater or less than, and all zeros in the result for equality.
