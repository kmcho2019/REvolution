To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we can follow these steps:
1. Define the module with input ports A and B, and output ports A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
2. Perform a subtraction operation between A and B. Since Verilog does not support subtraction directly, we can use the addition of the two's complement of B to A.
3. Determine the carry-out of the most significant bit (MSB) after the subtraction, which indicates if a borrow occurred.
4. Compare the result of the subtraction with zero to determine if A is equal to B.
5. Use the carry-out and the result of the subtraction to generate the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
