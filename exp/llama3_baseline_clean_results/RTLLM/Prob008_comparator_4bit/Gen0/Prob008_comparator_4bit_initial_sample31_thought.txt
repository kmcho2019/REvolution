To implement the comparator_4bit module, we'll first perform a subtraction operation A - B. We'll use the result and the borrow bit to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. In Verilog, we can achieve the subtraction and borrow using a full subtractor or by utilizing the built-in arithmetic operators. For simplicity and clarity, we'll use the latter approach. The result of the subtraction and the borrow will be used to assign values to the output ports A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. We'll ensure that the outputs are mutually exclusive.
