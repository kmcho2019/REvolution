To implement the 4-bit comparator module 'comparator_4bit', we'll use bitwise operations and combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. The comparison will be based on a subtraction operation A - B, analyzing the borrow and the result to determine if A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We will utilize a full subtractor for each bit position to generate the borrow and the difference.
