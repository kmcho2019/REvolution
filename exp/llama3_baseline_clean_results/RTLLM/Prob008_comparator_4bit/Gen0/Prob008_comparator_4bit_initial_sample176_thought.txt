To implement a 4-bit comparator with multiple bit-level comparators in combinational logic, we can leverage the properties of binary subtraction. The idea is to perform a subtraction operation (A - B) and analyze the result to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. 
We will utilize the borrow signal generated during subtraction to identify if A is less than B. 
If no borrow occurs and the result of the subtraction is non-zero, it indicates that A is greater than B. 
If the result of the subtraction is zero, it means A and B are equal.
This approach ensures that the outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) are mutually exclusive, with only one being high at any given time.
