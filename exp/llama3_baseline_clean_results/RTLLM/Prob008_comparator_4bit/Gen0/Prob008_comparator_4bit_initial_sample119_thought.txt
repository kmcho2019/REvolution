To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will first create a full subtractor to perform the subtraction operation A - B. This full subtractor will generate a borrow output for each bit position. The final borrow output (from the most significant bit) will indicate if A is less than B. We will also use the result of the subtraction to determine if A is greater than or equal to B. By combining these conditions, we can assign the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less accordingly.
