To implement a 4-bit comparator with multiple bit-level comparators in combinational logic, I will design a module named 'comparator_4bit' with input ports 'A' and 'B', each 4 bits wide, and output ports 'A_greater', 'A_equal', and 'A_less'. The comparison logic will be based on the subtraction operation A - B. I will use a full subtractor for each bit position, taking into account the borrow from the previous bit position. The final output will be determined by the borrow and the result of the subtraction.
