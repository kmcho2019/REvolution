The key points are:
1. Perform bitwise subtraction between A and B, taking into account any borrow from the previous bit.
2. If a borrow occurs at any point during the subtraction, it indicates that A is less than B.
3. If no borrow occurs and the result of the subtraction is not zero, A is greater than B.
4. If the result of the subtraction is zero, A is equal to B.
