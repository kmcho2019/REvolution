To implement the comparator_4bit module, we will utilize a combination of bitwise operations and arithmetic logic units (ALUs) to perform the comparison between the two 4-bit input operands A and B. The design will employ a subtract-and-compare approach to determine the relationship between A and B. We will use a full subtractor for each bit position, considering the borrow from the previous bit. Based on the result of the subtraction operation, we will generate the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less, ensuring that only one of them is high at any given time. The module will be designed using combinational logic to provide a straightforward and efficient comparison mechanism.
