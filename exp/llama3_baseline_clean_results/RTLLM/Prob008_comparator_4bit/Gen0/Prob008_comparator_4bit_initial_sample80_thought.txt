To implement the comparator_4bit module, we will first perform a subtraction operation between the two input operands A and B. We will then analyze the result and the borrow generated during the subtraction to determine the relationship between A and B. We will use combinational logic to generate the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the result of the subtraction and the borrow. The output encoding ensures that only one of the three outputs is high at any given time.
