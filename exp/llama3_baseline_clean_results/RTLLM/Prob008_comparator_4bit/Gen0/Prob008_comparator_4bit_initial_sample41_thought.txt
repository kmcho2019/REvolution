To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will use bit-level comparators in combinational logic. The comparison logic will be based on the subtraction operation A - B. We will analyze the borrow and the result of the subtraction to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The output encoding will ensure that only one of the three outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) is high at any given time.
