The module comparator_4bit will be implemented using combinational logic to compare two 4-bit binary numbers A and B. 
The comparison will be performed by subtracting B from A. 
To determine if A is greater than, equal to, or less than B, we will examine the result of the subtraction and any borrow generated during the operation.
We will use a full subtractor for each bit to simplify the comparison and borrow generation process.
The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one output is high at any given time.
