To design the 4-bit comparator module, we will use combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. The comparison will be done by performing a subtraction operation (A - B) and analyzing the result and borrow to determine the relationship between A and B. We will use the borrow to detect if A is less than B, and the result of the subtraction to check for equality or if A is greater than B. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be mutually exclusive, ensuring that only one output is high at any given time.
