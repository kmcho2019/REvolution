To implement a 4-bit comparator, we will use a combinational logic approach. The comparator will perform a subtraction operation (A - B) to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We'll utilize the borrow signal to detect if A is less than B. If no borrow occurs and the result of the subtraction is non-zero, A is greater than B. If A and B are equal, the result of the subtraction will be zero. To ensure the outputs are mutually exclusive, we'll use a combination of logic gates to generate the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
