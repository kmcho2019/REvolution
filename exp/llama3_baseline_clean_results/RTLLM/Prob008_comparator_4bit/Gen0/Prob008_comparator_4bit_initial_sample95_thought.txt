To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we'll design a combinational logic circuit that performs a subtraction operation (A - B) to determine the relationship between the two input operands. The result of this subtraction will indicate whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We'll use a full subtractor for each bit position to generate the borrow and result signals. The outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) will be encoded based on the borrow and result signals to ensure mutual exclusivity.
