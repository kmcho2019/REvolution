To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we'll utilize a subtraction-based approach. We can represent the subtraction A - B as a series of full subtractors, where each bit of A is subtracted from the corresponding bit of B, and borrow logic is implemented between each bit position. 
The output of this subtraction (result and borrow) will be used to determine the relationship between A and B (greater, equal, or less).
To encode the output, we will use a simple combinational logic to generate the A_greater, A_equal, and A_less signals based on the subtraction result and borrow.
