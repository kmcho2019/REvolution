To implement the comparator_4bit module, we need to design a digital circuit that can compare two 4-bit binary numbers. The comparison will be done by performing a subtraction operation (A - B) and analyzing the result. We will use combinational logic to generate the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. The key idea is to use the borrow generated during the subtraction to determine whether A is less than B. If no borrow occurs, we check the result of the subtraction to determine whether A is greater than or equal to B. The outputs will be encoded such that only one of them is high at any given time, ensuring mutual exclusivity.
