To implement the comparator_4bit module, we'll design a combinational logic circuit that compares two 4-bit binary numbers A and B. We'll use a subtraction approach (A - B) to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. The subtraction operation will be performed bit by bit, considering borrow for each bit position. Based on the result of the subtraction, we'll set the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less accordingly. The output signals will be mutually exclusive, ensuring that only one of them is high (1) at any given time.
