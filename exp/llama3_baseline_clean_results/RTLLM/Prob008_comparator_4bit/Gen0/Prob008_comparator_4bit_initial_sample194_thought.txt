The module comparator_4bit will be designed using combinational logic. It takes two 4-bit inputs A and B, and performs a subtraction operation A - B to determine the comparison result. The result of the subtraction and the borrow flag are used to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. We will use a ripple borrow subtractor to generate the borrow flag. The output encoding ensures that only one of the three outputs (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) is high at any given time.
