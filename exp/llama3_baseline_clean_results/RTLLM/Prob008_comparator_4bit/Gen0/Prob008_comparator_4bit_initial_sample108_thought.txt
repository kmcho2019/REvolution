To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will utilize combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. 
The comparison logic involves performing a subtraction operation (A - B) and analyzing the result to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B.
We will generate a borrow (carry-out) signal during the subtraction operation to help with the comparison.
Based on the result of the subtraction and the borrow signal, we will assert one of the three output signals: A_greater, A_equal, or A_less.
To ensure the outputs are mutually exclusive, we will use logical operations to set only one output high at any given time.
