To design the comparator_4bit module, we can utilize the concept of subtraction in combinational logic to compare two binary numbers, A and B.

The module will have three output signals (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) that are mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them can be high at any given time.

- A is greater than B if no borrow occurs and the result is non-zero.
- A is equal to B if the result of subtraction is zero.
