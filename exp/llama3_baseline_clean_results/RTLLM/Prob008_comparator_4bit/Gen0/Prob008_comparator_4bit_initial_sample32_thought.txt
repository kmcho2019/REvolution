To implement the 4-bit comparator module, we will use a combination of full subtractors to perform the subtraction operation between the two input operands A and B. 
The borrow out of each full subtractor stage will be used to determine the comparison result.
We will also generate the borrow and result of the subtraction using the full subtractor logic.
Based on the borrow and the result of the subtraction, we can determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B, and assign the corresponding output signals (A_greater, A_equal, A_less) accordingly.
