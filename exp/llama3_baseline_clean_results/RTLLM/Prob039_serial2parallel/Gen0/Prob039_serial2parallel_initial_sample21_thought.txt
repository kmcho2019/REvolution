The design idea for this series-parallel conversion circuit is to utilize a synchronous design that operates on the rising edge of the clock signal. It will have a 4-bit counter to track the number of serial input data bits received. The input serial data will be shifted into an 8-bit register from the most significant bit to the least significant bit as the counter counts from 0 to 7. Once 8 bits have been received (i.e., the counter reaches 7), the parallel output will be valid, and the valid output signal will be set to 1. The circuit will reset the counter and clear the valid output signal when the counter reaches its maximum value (7) and a new set of serial data can be received. The circuit also resets when the active low reset signal is asserted.
