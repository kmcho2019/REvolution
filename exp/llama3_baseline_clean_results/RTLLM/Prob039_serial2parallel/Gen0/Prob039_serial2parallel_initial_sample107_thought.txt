The series-parallel conversion circuit can be designed using a synchronous finite state machine. The circuit will have a counter to keep track of the number of serial input bits received. When the counter reaches 8, the parallel output will be generated, and the valid output signal will be set to 1. The circuit will reset the counter and valid output signal when the counter reaches 8 or when the reset signal is asserted.
