The series-parallel conversion circuit will be implemented as a synchronous digital circuit using a 4-bit counter to track the number of serial input data bits received. When the counter reaches 8 (or 1000 in binary), the 8-bit parallel output will be generated and the dout_valid signal will be set to 1. The module will utilize a shift register to construct the parallel output from the serial input data. The circuit will reset when the reset signal (rst_n) is low.
