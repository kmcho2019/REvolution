To implement the series-parallel conversion circuit, we will utilize a synchronous design with a 4-bit counter to track the number of serial input data bits received. The circuit will operate on the rising edge of the clock signal and reset when the reset signal is low. We will use a shift register to store the incoming serial data and output the 8-bit parallel data when all 8 bits have been received. The validity of the output data will be indicated by the dout_valid signal.
