To implement the ring_counter module, I will utilize a combination of always block for the sequential logic, and a variable to keep track of the current state of the counter. The output will be an 8-bit signal where only one bit is set at a time. The state transition will follow a cyclic pattern, and the reset signal will initialize the counter to its starting state. I will use a binary encoding where the position of the '1' bit represents the current state, cycling from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB) and then wrapping around to the LSB.
