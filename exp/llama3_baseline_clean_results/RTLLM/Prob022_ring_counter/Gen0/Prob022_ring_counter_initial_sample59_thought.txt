To design the ring_counter module, we will use an 8-bit register to store the current state of the counter. The state transition will be controlled by the clock signal (clk) and the reset signal. On each rising edge of the clock signal, we will shift the 1 in the current state to the next bit position, wrapping around to the least significant bit (LSB) when the most significant bit (MSB) is reached. When the reset signal is high, we will initialize the counter to its starting state, setting the LSB to 1 and all other bits to 0.
