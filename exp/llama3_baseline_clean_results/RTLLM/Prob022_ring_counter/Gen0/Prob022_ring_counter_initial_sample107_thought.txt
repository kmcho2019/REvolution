To implement the 8-bit ring counter, we will use a single 8-bit register to store the current state of the counter. The state transition is achieved by shifting the 1 bit to the next position on each clock pulse, wrapping around to the least significant bit (LSB) after reaching the most significant bit (MSB). We will use a concatenation operation to shift the bits and create the cyclic sequence. The reset signal will be used to initialize the counter to its starting state. We will also ensure that the counter only updates on the rising edge of the clock signal.
