To design an 8-bit ring counter, we will use a register to hold the current state of the counter. The state transition will be controlled by the clock signal. On each rising edge of the clock, the current state will be updated by shifting the '1' to the next position. When the '1' reaches the most significant bit (MSB), it will wrap around to the least significant bit (LSB) to create a cyclic sequence. The reset signal will be used to initialize the counter to its starting state.
