To design an 8-bit ring counter, we will create a module named `ring_counter` with the specified input and output ports. The internal logic of the counter will be based on a cyclic shift of a single '1' bit through the 8-bit output, triggered by the clock signal. We will use a combination of conditional statements and shift operations to implement this behavior. The reset signal will be used to initialize the counter to its starting state when it is high. The state transition logic will ensure that the counter cycles through all 8 bits, wrapping around to the least significant bit after reaching the most significant bit.
