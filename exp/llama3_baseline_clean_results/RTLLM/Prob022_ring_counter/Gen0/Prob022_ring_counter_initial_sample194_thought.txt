The internal logic will utilize a flip-flop or a register to store the current state and update it on each clock pulse. The output will be an 8-bit vector where only one bit is high at any given time. The state transition will follow a cyclic pattern, shifting the high bit to the next position with each clock pulse and wrapping around to the least significant bit after reaching the most significant bit. The reset signal will be used to initialize the counter to its starting state.
