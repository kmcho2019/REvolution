To design an 8-bit ring counter, we will use a register to hold the current state and a clock signal to trigger state transitions. The ring counter starts in an initial state where the least significant bit (LSB) is 1 and the rest are 0. On each rising edge of the clock signal, we shift the 1 to the next position in a cyclic manner. When the 1 reaches the most significant bit (MSB), we wrap it around to the LSB. To implement this, we can use a simple shift operation in Verilog, combined with a conditional statement to handle the wrap-around. The reset signal will be used to asynchronously reset the counter to its initial state.
