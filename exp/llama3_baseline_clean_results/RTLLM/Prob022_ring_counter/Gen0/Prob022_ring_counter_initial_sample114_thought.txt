The ring counter should be reset to its starting state (typically with the least significant bit (LSB) of out set to 1) when the reset signal is high. This can be achieved by using a shift register that shifts the output to the right by one bit on each rising edge of the clock signal and then wraps around to the most significant bit (MSB) after reaching the LSB.
