To implement an 8-bit ring counter, we'll use a combination of a register to hold the current state, and a simple logic to shift the '1' bit to the next position on each clock cycle. The ring counter will reset to its initial state when the reset signal is high. The main concept here is to shift the bits in a cyclic manner, wrapping around from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB) after each complete cycle. This can be achieved by checking the current state and shifting the '1' to the next position based on that state.
