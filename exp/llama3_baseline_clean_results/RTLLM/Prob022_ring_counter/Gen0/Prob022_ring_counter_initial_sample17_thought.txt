To implement the 8-bit ring counter module, we will utilize a shift register approach. We'll define an 8-bit output vector `out` and manage its bits to follow a cyclic pattern. The internal logic will be based on the clock signal `clk` and the reset signal `reset`. On every rising edge of the clock, we'll shift the 1 bit to the next position, wrapping around to the least significant bit (LSB) after reaching the most significant bit (MSB). The reset signal will initialize the counter to its starting state, where the LSB of `out` is set to 1. We can use the non-blocking assignment operator (`<=`) to ensure the output bits are updated correctly at each clock edge.
