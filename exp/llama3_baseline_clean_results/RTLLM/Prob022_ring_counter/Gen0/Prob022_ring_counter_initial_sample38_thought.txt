To implement the 8-bit ring counter, we can utilize a register to store the current state of the counter. On each clock cycle, we will shift the bits of the register to the left, effectively moving the '1' to the next position. When the '1' reaches the most significant bit, we will wrap it around to the least significant bit. To handle the reset, we will check the reset signal and if it's high, we will initialize the register to its starting state. We can use a basic shift operation combined with a conditional statement to achieve the desired behavior.
