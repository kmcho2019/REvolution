To implement the 8-bit ring counter, we will use a simple sequential logic design in Verilog. The counter's state will be represented by an internal 8-bit register, and the state transition will be controlled by the clock signal and the reset signal. When the reset signal is high, the counter will be reset to its initial state. On each rising edge of the clock signal, the state will be updated according to the cyclic pattern of the ring counter. We will use a case statement to handle the state transitions, shifting the 1 to the next bit in the sequence and wrapping around to the LSB after reaching the MSB.
