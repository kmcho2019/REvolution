To design an 8-bit ring counter, we will utilize a sequential logic approach, where the output at each clock cycle depends on the previous state. The key idea is to detect the current position of the '1' in the 8-bit output and shift it to the next position on each clock pulse. The counter will be reset to its initial state when the reset signal is high. The ring counter's cyclic nature can be achieved by using a simple shift operation combined with a conditional statement to handle the wrap-around from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB).
