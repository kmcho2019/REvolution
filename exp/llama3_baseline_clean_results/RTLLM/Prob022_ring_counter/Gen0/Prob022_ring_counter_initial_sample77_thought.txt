To implement the 8-bit ring counter, we'll use a simple shift register concept where the output of the previous bit is connected to the next bit, creating a cyclic pattern. On each rising edge of the clock signal, we'll shift the 1 to the next bit in the sequence. When the reset signal is high, we'll initialize the counter to its starting state. We can use the concatenation operator to shift the bits and a conditional statement to handle the reset and cycling behavior.
