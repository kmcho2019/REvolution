To design the ring_counter module, we'll use a simple 8-bit shift register where each bit represents a state. The register will shift its contents one position to the right on each rising edge of the clock signal, effectively moving the '1' to the next position in the cyclic sequence. When the reset signal is high, the register will be initialized to its starting state, which is 8'b0000_0001. This approach ensures that only one bit is set high at any time and that the set bit cycles through the 8-bit output in a ring-like pattern.
