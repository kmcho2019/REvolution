To design an 8-bit ring counter, we'll use a sequential logic approach, utilizing a register to store the current state and a combinational logic circuit to determine the next state. The counter will be driven by a clock signal and reset signal. The ring counter will have 8 bits, with only one bit set to 1 at any time. The 1 will shift to the next bit with each clock pulse, wrapping around to the least significant bit (LSB) after reaching the most significant bit (MSB). When the reset signal is high, the counter will be initialized to its starting state, with the LSB set to 1.
