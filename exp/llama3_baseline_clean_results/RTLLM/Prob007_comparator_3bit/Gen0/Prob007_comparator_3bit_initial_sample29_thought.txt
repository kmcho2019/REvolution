To implement a 3-bit comparator, we will use a straightforward combinational logic approach. The comparison between two 3-bit binary numbers A and B can be done by comparing them bit by bit from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). We will use logical operators to compare the bits of A and B and determine the output based on the comparison result. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high (1) at any given time, indicating whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B, respectively.
