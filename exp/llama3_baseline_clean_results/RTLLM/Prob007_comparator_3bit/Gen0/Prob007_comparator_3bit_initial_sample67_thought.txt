To design the comparator_3bit module, we will use combinational logic to compare the two 3-bit binary numbers A and B. We can achieve this by comparing the bits of A and B from most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB). If A's MSB is greater than B's MSB, then A is greater than B. If A's MSB is equal to B's MSB, we then compare the next bits, and so on. The output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be set based on the comparison results. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high (1) at any given time.
