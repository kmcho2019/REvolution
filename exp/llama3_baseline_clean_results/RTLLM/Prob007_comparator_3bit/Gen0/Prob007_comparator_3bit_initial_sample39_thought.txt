To design the comparator_3bit module, we will use a combination of conditional statements and logical operations to compare the two 3-bit binary numbers A and B. We will compare the numbers bit by bit, starting from the most significant bit (MSB) down to the least significant bit (LSB). Based on the comparison results, we will set the output ports A_greater, A_equal, and A_less accordingly.
