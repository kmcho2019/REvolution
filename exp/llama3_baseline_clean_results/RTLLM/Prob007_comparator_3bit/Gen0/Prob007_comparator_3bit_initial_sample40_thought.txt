The comparator_3bit module can be designed using simple combinational logic, comparing the bits of A and B from most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB). The comparison can be broken down into three cases: A greater than B, A equal to B, and A less than B. This can be implemented using if-else statements or logical operations in Verilog. We will use the logical operations approach to determine the values of A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison of A and B.
