To implement the comparator_3bit module, we will use combinational logic to compare the two 3-bit binary numbers A and B. The comparison logic will be based on the most significant bits first, and then the less significant bits. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be determined based on the comparison of A and B, and will be mutually exclusive.

Here's a step-by-step breakdown of the thought process:
1. Compare the most significant bits (MSB) of A and B.
2. If the MSBs are equal, compare the next bits, and so on.
3. Based on the comparison, determine the values of A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
4. Ensure that the outputs are mutually exclusive, meaning only one of the three outputs will be high (1) at any given time.

The logic can be implemented using simple Verilog code with conditional statements.
