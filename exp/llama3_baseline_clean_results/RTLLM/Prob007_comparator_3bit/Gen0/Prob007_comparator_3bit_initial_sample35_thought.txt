To implement the comparator_3bit module, we will use combinational logic to compare the two 3-bit binary numbers A and B. We will first compare the most significant bits (MSB) of A and B. If the MSBs are different, we can determine the greater number. If the MSBs are the same, we will compare the next bits, and so on. We will use this logic to generate the A_greater, A_equal, and A_less outputs. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of the three outputs will be high (1) at any given time, based on the comparison results.
