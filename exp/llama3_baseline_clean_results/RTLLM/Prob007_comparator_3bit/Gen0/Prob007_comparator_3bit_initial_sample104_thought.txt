To implement a 3-bit comparator module in Verilog, we need to design a combinational logic circuit that compares two 3-bit binary numbers (A and B) and produces three output signals: A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. These signals are mutually exclusive and indicate whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B, respectively. We can use simple logical operations to compare the bits of A and B from most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB), setting the output signals accordingly.
