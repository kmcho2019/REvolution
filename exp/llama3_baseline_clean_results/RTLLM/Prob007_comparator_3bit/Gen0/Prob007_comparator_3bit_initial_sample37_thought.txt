If the MSBs are different, we can determine the result based on the MSB comparison. If the MSBs are the same, we move to the next bit and repeat the process until we find a difference or all bits are compared. The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be determined based on this comparison logic. We will use combinational logic to achieve this.
