To design a 3-bit comparator, we will use a hierarchical approach. 
First, compare the most significant bits (MSBs) of A and B. If they are different, 
we can immediately determine the relationship between A and B. 
If the MSBs are the same, compare the next bits, and so on, until we find a difference or all bits are compared.
We will use simple combinational logic gates (AND, OR, NOT) to implement this comparison.
The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less will be determined based on the comparison results.
We will ensure that the outputs are mutually exclusive, meaning only one of the three outputs will be high (1) at any given time.
