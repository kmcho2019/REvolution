To design a 3-bit comparator module named comparator_3bit, we will utilize combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. The comparison will result in three outputs: A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. The logic for these outputs will be based on the binary values of A and B. We can achieve this by comparing the bits from most significant to least significant and setting the appropriate output based on the comparison. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, ensuring only one output is high at any given time, reflecting the result of the comparison between A and B.
