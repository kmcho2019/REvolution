To implement a 3-bit comparator, we will use combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. We will determine the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison results. The comparison can be performed by checking the bits of A and B from most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB). If A's MSB is greater than B's MSB, then A is greater than B. If A's MSB is less than B's MSB, then A is less than B. If the MSBs are equal, we move to the next bit and perform the same comparison. This process continues until we find a bit where A and B are not equal or until we have compared all bits. If all bits are equal, then A is equal to B. We will use this logic to determine the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
