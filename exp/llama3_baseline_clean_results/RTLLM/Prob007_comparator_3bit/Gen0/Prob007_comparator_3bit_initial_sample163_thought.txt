The comparator_3bit module can be implemented by comparing the input binary numbers A and B bit by bit. We start from the most significant bit (MSB) and work our way down to the least significant bit (LSB). The comparison logic is implemented using combinational logic with if-else conditions to determine the outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison result. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive, we can use a priority-based approach where A_greater has the highest priority, followed by A_equal, and then A_less.
