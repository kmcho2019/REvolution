To design a 3-bit comparator, we can use simple combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. The comparison can be done by checking each bit of A and B from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). We will use if-else statements to generate the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison result. The output signals are mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high (1) at any given time.
