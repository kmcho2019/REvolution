To design the comparator_3bit module, we will first compare the most significant bits (MSBs) of the two input operands A and B. If the MSBs are different, we can immediately determine the greater or lesser value based on the bit values. If the MSBs are the same, we then compare the next bits, and if necessary, the least significant bits (LSBs). We will use combinational logic to generate the A_greater, A_equal, and A_less outputs based on the comparison of A and B. The outputs will be mutually exclusive, ensuring that only one output is high at any given time.
