To implement a 3-bit comparator module, we can use simple combinational logic to compare the two input binary numbers A and B. 
We start by comparing the most significant bits of A and B. If they are not equal, we can immediately determine the greater or lesser value. 
If the most significant bits are equal, we then compare the next most significant bits, and so on, until we find a difference or determine that the numbers are equal. 
The outputs A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are then set based on the comparison result, ensuring they are mutually exclusive.
