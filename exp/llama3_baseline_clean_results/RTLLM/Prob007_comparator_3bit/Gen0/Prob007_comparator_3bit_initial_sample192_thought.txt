To implement a 3-bit comparator, we will use combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. We will have three output signals: A_greater, A_equal, and A_less. The comparison logic can be broken down into three main cases: 
1) A is greater than B, 
2) A is equal to B, and 
3) A is less than B. 
For each case, we will set the corresponding output signal to 1 and the other two signals to 0. 
The comparison can be performed by using the greater-than, less-than, and equality operators in Verilog.
