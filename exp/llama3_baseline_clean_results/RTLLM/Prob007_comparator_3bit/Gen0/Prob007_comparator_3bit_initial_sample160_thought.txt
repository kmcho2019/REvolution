To design a 3-bit comparator module in Verilog, we need to implement combinational logic that compares the two input binary numbers, A and B, and generates output signals indicating whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. This comparison can be achieved by using if-else statements or by designing a digital circuit that compares bits from most significant to least significant, setting the output signals accordingly. Since the outputs are mutually exclusive, we can use simple conditional statements to assign the output values based on the comparison of A and B.
