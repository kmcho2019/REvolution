To implement a 3-bit comparator, we can compare the two input binary numbers A and B bit by bit, starting from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). 
We will use combinational logic to determine the comparison results and generate the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
The output signals will be mutually exclusive, meaning only one of them will be high (1) at any given time, based on the comparison results.
