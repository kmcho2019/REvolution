To design a 3-bit comparator module in Verilog, we can use simple combinational logic to compare the two input operands A and B. The comparison can be performed bit by bit from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). We will generate the output signals A_greater, A_equal, and A_less based on the comparison result. The module will have three output signals, and only one of them will be high at any given time, ensuring mutually exclusive outputs.
