To design the counter_12 module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the count value. The counting process will be controlled by the valid_count signal. When valid_count is 1, the counter will increment on each clock cycle. If the current count value reaches 4'd11, it will wrap around to 0. The reset signal (rst_n) will be used to reset the counter to 0 when it is active low. We will use a synchronous reset to ensure the counter resets on the next clock cycle after the reset signal is asserted.
