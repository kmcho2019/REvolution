The counter_12 module is designed to count from 0 (4'b0000) to 11 (4'd11) based on the valid_count input signal. It has a reset feature when the rst_n signal is active low, and it wraps around to 0 after reaching the maximum count of 11. The counter is synchronous, meaning it only updates on the rising edge of the clock signal (clk) when valid_count is 1. 
To implement this, we'll use a simple if-else statement within a clocked always block to check the conditions for reset, increment, or no change in the count value.
