The counter_12 module will be designed as a sequential circuit with a 4-bit output register (out) that stores the current count value. The circuit will have an asynchronous reset input (rst_n) to reset the counter to 0. The counting will be controlled by the valid_count signal. When valid_count is 1, the counter will increment on each clock cycle. If the count reaches the maximum value (4'd11), it will wrap around to 0. If valid_count is 0, the counter will pause, and the output will remain unchanged.
