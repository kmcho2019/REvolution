The counter_12 module will be designed as a synchronous digital circuit with a reset feature. It will utilize an always block to handle the reset and counting functionality. The reset signal (rst_n) is active low, meaning the counter resets when rst_n is 0. The counting functionality will be enabled by the valid_count signal. When valid_count is 1, the counter will increment on each clock cycle until it reaches the maximum count value (4'd11), at which point it will wrap around to 0. If valid_count is 0, the counter will pause and retain its current value.
