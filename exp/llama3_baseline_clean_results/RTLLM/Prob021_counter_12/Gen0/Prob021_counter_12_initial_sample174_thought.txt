The counter_12 module will utilize an always_ff block to handle the sequential logic based on the clock signal (clk) and the reset signal (rst_n). The valid_count signal will control whether the counter increments or remains unchanged. If the reset signal is active, the counter will reset to 0. If the valid_count signal is high, the counter will increment unless it has reached its maximum value (4'd11), at which point it will wrap around to 0. Otherwise, if the valid_count signal is low, the counter will hold its current value.
