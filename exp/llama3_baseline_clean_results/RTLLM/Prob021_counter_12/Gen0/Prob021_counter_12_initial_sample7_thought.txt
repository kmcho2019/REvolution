To design the counter_12 module, we will use a combinational logic approach to create a synchronous 4-bit up-counter with a reset feature. The counter will increment when the valid_count signal is high, and it will pause when the valid_count signal is low. The counter will also reset to 0 when the reset signal (rst_n) is low. This will be achieved using a conditional statement to check the reset and valid_count signals, and then either incrementing the counter or keeping it the same.
