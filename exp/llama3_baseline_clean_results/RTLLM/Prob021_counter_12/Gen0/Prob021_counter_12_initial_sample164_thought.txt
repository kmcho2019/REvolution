The counter_12 module will utilize a 4-bit register to store the current count value. The counter's operation is controlled by the valid_count signal and the reset signal (rst_n). On each positive edge of the clock signal (clk), if valid_count is high and rst_n is high, the counter checks its current value. If the current count is less than 4'd11, it increments by 1. If the count is 4'd11, it resets to 0. If valid_count is low or rst_n is low, the counter either pauses or resets to 0, respectively.
