To implement the counter_12 module, we will use a 4-bit register to store the count value. The counting process will be controlled by the valid_count signal. On each clock cycle, if the valid_count signal is high (1), the counter will increment by 1. When the counter reaches the maximum count value (4'd11), it will wrap around and reset to 0. If the reset signal (rst_n) is active low (0), the counter will be reset to 0. We will use a sequential logic (always block) to implement this behavior.
