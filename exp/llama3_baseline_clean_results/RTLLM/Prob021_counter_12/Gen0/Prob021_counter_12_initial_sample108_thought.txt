To implement the counter_12 module, I will use a synchronous reset approach with an if-else statement to control the counting behavior based on the valid_count signal. The counter will reset to 0 when the reset signal (rst_n) is active low. When the valid_count signal is high, the counter will increment on each clock cycle. If the counter reaches the maximum value (4'd11), it will wrap around to 0. When the valid_count signal is low, the counter will pause, maintaining its current value.
