The counter_12 module will be designed using a simple 4-bit counter with an enable signal (valid_count) to control the counting process. It will have an active low reset signal (rst_n) to reset the counter to 0. On every positive clock edge, the counter will check if the valid_count signal is active. If it is, the counter will increment by 1. If the count value reaches the maximum count (4'd11), it will wrap around and reset to 0. If the valid_count signal is inactive, the counter will remain unchanged.
