To implement the 4-bit BCD adder module, we will first perform a binary addition of the two BCD inputs A and B along with the carry-in Cin. We then check if the result exceeds 9 (the maximum value in BCD). If it does, we add 6 (0110 in binary) to the sum to correct it to a valid BCD digit. During this correction, if the sum after correction still exceeds 9, we should generate a carry-out. The process involves bit manipulation and conditional checks that can be efficiently handled in Verilog.
