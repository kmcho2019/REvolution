To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). Then, we check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum decimal value that can be represented by a 4-bit BCD number). If it does, we add 6 to the sum to bring it within the valid BCD range (0-9) and generate a carry-out (Cout). We use a binary adder to perform the initial addition and then implement the BCD correction and carry generation logic.
