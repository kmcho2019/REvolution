To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we'll follow these steps:
1. Perform binary addition of inputs A, B, and Cin.
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001).
3. If the sum exceeds 9, apply a correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum.
4. Generate a carry-out (Cout) signal if the result of the addition exceeds 9.
