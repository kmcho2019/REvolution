To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to follow these steps:
1. Perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, apply a BCD correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum.
3. After applying the correction, generate a carry-out (Cout) if the result still exceeds 9, indicating that the sum cannot be represented by a single BCD digit.
We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators for addition and conditional statements for the BCD correction and carry generation.
