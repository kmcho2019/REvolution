To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform a binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). Then, we check if the sum exceeds 9. If it does, we add 6 to the sum to correct it and generate a carry-out (Cout). This process ensures the result is a valid BCD digit (0-9). The correction and carry generation are based on the properties of BCD arithmetic, where exceeding 9 requires a correction to stay within the 0-9 range and generate a carry for the next decimal place.
