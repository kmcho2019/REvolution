To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). Then, we check if the sum exceeds 9, which is the maximum valid decimal digit in BCD. If it does, we add 6 to the sum to correct it back to a valid BCD range and generate a carry-out. This process ensures that the output Sum remains within the valid BCD range (0 to 9) and Cout is asserted whenever the sum of two BCD digits exceeds 9.
