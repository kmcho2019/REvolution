To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform binary addition of the two inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). Then, we'll check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum value in BCD representation). If it does, we add a correction factor of 6 to the sum to bring it within the valid BCD range (0-9) and generate a carry-out (Cout) to indicate that the result exceeds a single decimal digit.
