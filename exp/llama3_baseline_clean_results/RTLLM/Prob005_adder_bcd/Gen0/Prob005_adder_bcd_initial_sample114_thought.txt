1. Perform binary addition of the two 4-bit inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in input (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001), which would require BCD correction.
3. If the sum exceeds 9, add 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to ensure it stays within the valid BCD range (0-9).
4. Generate a carry-out (Cout) if the result of the addition (before or after correction) exceeds 9.
