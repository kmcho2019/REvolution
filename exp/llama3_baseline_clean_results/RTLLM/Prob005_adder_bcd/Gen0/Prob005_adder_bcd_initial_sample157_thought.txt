To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, the design involves two main steps: 
1. Binary addition of the two input BCD numbers and the carry-in bit.
2. BCD correction: If the sum of the binary addition exceeds 9 (binary 1001), a correction of 6 (binary 0110) is added to the sum to ensure it remains within the valid BCD range (0-9). 
Additionally, if the final sum (after correction) exceeds 9, a carry-out signal is generated. 
The module will use a combination of arithmetic and logical operations to achieve these steps.
