To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform a binary addition of the two 4-bit BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). Then, we will check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, we add a correction of 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to ensure it stays within the BCD range (0-9). The carry-out (Cout) will be generated if the result of the addition exceeds 9, indicating that the BCD sum exceeds a single decimal digit.
