To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will follow these steps:
1. Perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum valid BCD digit). If it does, we apply the BCD correction by adding 6 to the sum. This correction ensures the result stays within the valid BCD range (0-9).
3. Determine the carry-out (Cout). If the sum after correction (if any) is greater than 9, a carry-out is generated.

The design will involve using Verilog's arithmetic operators for addition and conditional statements (if-else) for applying the BCD correction and determining the carry-out.
