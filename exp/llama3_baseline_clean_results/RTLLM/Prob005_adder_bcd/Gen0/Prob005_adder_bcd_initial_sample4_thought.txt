To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we first need to perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in input (Cin). Then, we check if the sum exceeds the decimal value of 9 (binary 1001). If it does, we add a correction of 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to bring it within the valid BCD range (0-9). Additionally, if the sum exceeds 9 after the initial addition or after the correction, we generate a carry-out (Cout) to indicate that the result exceeds a single decimal digit. The corrected sum will be the final output, ensuring it represents a valid BCD digit.
