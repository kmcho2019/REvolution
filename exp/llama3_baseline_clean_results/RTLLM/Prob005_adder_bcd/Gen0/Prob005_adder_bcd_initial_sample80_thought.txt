The 4-bit BCD adder can be implemented using two main steps: 
1. Perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Apply BCD correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum if it exceeds 9 (binary 1001). 
This correction ensures the result stays within the valid BCD range (0-9).
We also need to generate a carry-out (Cout) if the sum before correction exceeds 9.
We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators to perform these operations.
