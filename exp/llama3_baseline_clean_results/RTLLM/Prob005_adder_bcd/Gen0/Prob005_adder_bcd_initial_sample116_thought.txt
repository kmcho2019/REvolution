To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we'll perform the following steps:
1. Add the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin) using binary addition.
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, apply the BCD correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum.
3. Determine the carry-out (Cout). If the sum exceeds 9 after the correction, Cout should be 1; otherwise, it should be 0.
