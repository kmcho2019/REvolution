To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we'll follow these steps:
1. Perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (which is 1001 in binary). If it does, we add 6 (0110 in binary) to the sum to correct it to a valid BCD digit.
3. Generate a carry-out (Cout) if the sum after correction exceeds 9, indicating that the result is greater than a single decimal digit.
We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators for addition and comparison, and conditional statements for the BCD correction and carry generation.
