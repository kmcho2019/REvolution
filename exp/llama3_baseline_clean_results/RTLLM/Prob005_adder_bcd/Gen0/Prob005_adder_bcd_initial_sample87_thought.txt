The sum from this addition is then checked to see if it exceeds 9 (binary 1001), which is the maximum valid BCD digit. If the sum is greater than 9, a correction of 6 (binary 0110) is added to the sum. This correction ensures that the result is within the valid BCD range (0-9). The carry-out (Cout) is generated based on whether the final result exceeds the decimal value of 9.
