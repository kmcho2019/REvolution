To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD digits and the carry-in. Then, we check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum valid BCD digit). If it does, we add 6 (0110 in binary) to the sum to correct it to a valid BCD digit and generate a carry-out. This process ensures that the result is always a valid BCD digit (0-9) and the carry-out is properly generated when the sum exceeds 9.
