To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) and the carry-in (Cin). The sum of the binary addition is then checked to see if it exceeds the decimal value of 9 (binary 1001). If it does, a correction value of 6 (binary 0110) is added to the sum to bring it within the valid BCD range (0-9). The carry-out (Cout) is generated if the result of the addition, before correction, exceeds 9, indicating that the BCD sum exceeds a single decimal digit.
