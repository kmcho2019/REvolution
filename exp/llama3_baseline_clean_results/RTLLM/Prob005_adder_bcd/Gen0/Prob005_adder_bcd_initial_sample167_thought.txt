The 4-bit BCD adder module will first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). After obtaining the sum, it will check if the sum exceeds the decimal value of 9. If the sum does exceed 9, the module will apply a correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum. This correction is necessary to ensure the result is a valid BCD digit. Additionally, if the result of the addition (before or after correction) exceeds 9, the module will generate a carry-out (Cout) to indicate that the BCD sum is greater than a single decimal digit (0-9).
