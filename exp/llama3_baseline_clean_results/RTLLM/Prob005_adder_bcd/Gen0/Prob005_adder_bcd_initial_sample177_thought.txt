To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will follow these steps:
1. Perform binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, we add 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to bring it within the valid BCD range (0-9).
3. Generate a carry-out (Cout) if the sum after correction exceeds 9, indicating that the result cannot be represented by a single decimal digit.
We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators for the addition and comparison operations, and conditional statements (if-else) for the BCD correction and carry generation logic.
