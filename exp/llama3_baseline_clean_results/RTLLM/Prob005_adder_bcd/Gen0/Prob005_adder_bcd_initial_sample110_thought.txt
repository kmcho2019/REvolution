The 4-bit BCD adder module 'adder_bcd' will perform binary addition of the two 4-bit BCD inputs A and B along with the carry-in Cin. To ensure the result is a valid BCD digit, a correction of 6 (binary 0110) is added if the sum exceeds 9. Additionally, a carry-out (Cout) is generated when the sum of A and B exceeds 9, indicating that the result requires more than one decimal digit.
