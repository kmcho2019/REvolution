Then, we check if the sum exceeds 9 (which is the maximum decimal value that can be represented by a 4-bit BCD number). If the sum is greater than 9, we apply a correction by adding 6 (which is equivalent to 0110 in binary) to the sum to ensure it remains within the valid BCD range (0-9). Additionally, we need to generate a carry-out (Cout) signal whenever the result of the addition (before correction) exceeds 9, indicating that the sum is greater than a single decimal digit. We will use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators for addition and conditional statements (if-else) for the BCD correction and carry generation logic.
