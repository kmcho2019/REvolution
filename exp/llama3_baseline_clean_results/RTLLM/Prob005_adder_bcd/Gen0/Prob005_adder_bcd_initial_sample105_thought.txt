The 4-bit BCD adder module, 'adder_bcd', will be designed using Verilog. It takes two 4-bit BCD numbers, A and B, and a 1-bit carry-in, Cin, as inputs and produces a 4-bit BCD sum and a 1-bit carry-out. 
The module will first perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin. 
Then, it will check if the sum exceeds 9. If it does, the module will add 6 to the sum to correct it to be within the valid BCD range and set the carry-out. 
Otherwise, it will simply assign the sum and the carry-out.
