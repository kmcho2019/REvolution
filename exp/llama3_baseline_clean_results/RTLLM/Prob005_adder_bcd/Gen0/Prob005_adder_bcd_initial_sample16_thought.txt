To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). The result of this addition will be a 5-bit value (to accommodate carry). We then check if the sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, we add a correction of 6 (binary 0110) to ensure the result is a valid BCD digit (0-9). The final sum is then the lower 4 bits of the corrected sum, and the carry-out (Cout) is generated if the original sum before correction exceeded 9.
