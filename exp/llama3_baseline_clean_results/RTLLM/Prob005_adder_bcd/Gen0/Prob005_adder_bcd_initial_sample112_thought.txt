To design a 4-bit BCD adder, we first need to perform binary addition of the two input BCD numbers along with a carry-in bit. After obtaining the binary sum, we check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum value for a BCD digit). If it does, we add a correction value of 6 to the sum to bring it back within the valid BCD range (0-9) and generate a carry-out signal. This process ensures that the output of the adder is always a valid BCD digit.
