To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin) bit. This will give us a raw sum. We then check if this raw sum exceeds the decimal value of 9. If it does, we apply a correction by adding 6 to the sum and generate a carry-out. The corrected sum, after ensuring it fits within the 4-bit BCD representation, is then assigned to the Sum output, and the carry-out is assigned to the Cout output.
