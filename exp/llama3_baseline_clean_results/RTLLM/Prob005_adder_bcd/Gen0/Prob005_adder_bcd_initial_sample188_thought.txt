The module adder_bcd will be designed with the following logic: 
1. Perform binary addition of inputs A and B along with the carry-in (Cin) to get a temporary sum.
2. Check if the temporary sum exceeds 9 (binary 1001). If it does, add a correction of 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to ensure it stays within the BCD range (0-9).
3. After applying the correction (if any), determine the carry-out (Cout). If the final sum (after correction) exceeds 9, Cout should be set to 1; otherwise, it should be 0.
This approach ensures that the module correctly performs BCD addition and generates a carry-out when the sum exceeds a single decimal digit.
