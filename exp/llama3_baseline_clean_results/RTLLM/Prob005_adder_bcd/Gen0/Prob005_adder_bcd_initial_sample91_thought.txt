The 4-bit BCD adder module will perform the addition of two 4-bit BCD inputs (A and B) along with a carry-in input (Cin). It will then apply a correction to ensure the sum is a valid BCD digit (0-9) and generate a carry-out (Cout) if the sum exceeds 9. This can be achieved by first performing a binary addition of A, B, and Cin, then checking if the result exceeds 9. If it does, adding 6 to the sum to correct it to a valid BCD range and setting the carry-out accordingly.
