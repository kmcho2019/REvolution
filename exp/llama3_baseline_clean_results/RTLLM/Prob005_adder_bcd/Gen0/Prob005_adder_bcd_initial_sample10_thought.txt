To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we need to perform binary addition of the two input BCD numbers and the carry-in, and then apply the necessary corrections to ensure the result is a valid BCD digit. If the sum exceeds 9, we add 6 to the sum to bring it within the BCD range and generate a carry-out. The design involves combinational logic to calculate the sum and the carry-out based on the inputs.
