To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). The sum and carry-out from this addition are then checked. If the sum exceeds 9 (which is represented by the binary value 1001), we add 6 (binary 0110) to the sum to correct it and make it a valid BCD digit. The carry-out is generated if the sum of A, B, and Cin exceeds 9. This process ensures that the output sum remains within the valid BCD range (0 to 9) and correctly generates a carry-out when necessary.
