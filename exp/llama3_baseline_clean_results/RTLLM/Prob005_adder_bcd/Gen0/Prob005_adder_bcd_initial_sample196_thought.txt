To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we can follow a two-step process:
1. Perform binary addition of the two BCD inputs (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin).
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9 (the maximum value in BCD), and if so, add 6 (0110 in binary) to correct it and set the carry-out (Cout) accordingly.
This ensures the output (Sum) is always a valid BCD digit (0-9) and Cout is generated whenever the sum exceeds 9.
We can utilize Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators for addition and comparison operators for BCD correction and carry generation.
