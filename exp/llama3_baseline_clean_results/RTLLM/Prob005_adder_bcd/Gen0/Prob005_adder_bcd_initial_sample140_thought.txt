After obtaining the sum, we check if it exceeds 9 (the maximum valid BCD digit). If the sum is greater than 9, we add 6 (binary 0110) to it to ensure the result is a valid BCD digit, and we also generate a carry-out signal. This process involves using logical operations and arithmetic additions. The key steps are: 
1. Binary addition of A, B, and Cin.
2. Check if the sum exceeds 9.
3. If the sum exceeds 9, add 6 to it and set Cout to 1; otherwise, Cout remains 0.
