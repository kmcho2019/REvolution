If it does, we'll apply a correction by adding 6 (0110 in binary) to the sum to ensure the result is a valid BCD digit. We'll also generate a carry-out signal (Cout) whenever the sum exceeds 9, indicating that the result cannot be represented as a single BCD digit.

1. Perform binary addition of A, B, and Cin.
2. Check if the sum is greater than 9 (binary 1001).
3. If the sum exceeds 9, add 6 to the sum for BCD correction and generate a carry-out.
4. Assign the corrected sum to the output Sum and generate the Cout signal accordingly.
