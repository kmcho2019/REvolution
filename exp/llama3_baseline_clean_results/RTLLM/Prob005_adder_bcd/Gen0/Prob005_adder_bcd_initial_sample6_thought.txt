To implement a 4-bit BCD adder, we will first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). The result of this binary addition is then checked to see if it exceeds 9, which is the maximum valid BCD digit. If it does, we apply a BCD correction by adding 6 (binary 0110) to the sum. This correction step effectively adjusts the sum to fall within the valid BCD range (0-9) and also generates a carry-out if necessary. The final corrected sum and the carry-out are then output as the result.
