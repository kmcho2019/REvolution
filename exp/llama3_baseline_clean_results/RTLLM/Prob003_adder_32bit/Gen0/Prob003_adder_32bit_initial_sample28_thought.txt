The first 16-bit CLA block will handle the lower 16 bits of the input operands, and the second 16-bit CLA block will handle the upper 16 bits. We need to propagate the carry output from the first 16-bit CLA block to the second 16-bit CLA block. The carry output from the second 16-bit CLA block will be the final carry-out output.
