To design a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using the Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) architecture, we first need to create a 4-bit and then a 16-bit CLA module. The 4-bit CLA module will calculate the sum and carry for 4-bit inputs, while the 16-bit CLA module will use four instances of the 4-bit CLA to compute the sum and carry for 16-bit inputs. Finally, the top module, adder_32bit, will utilize two instances of the 16-bit CLA to achieve the 32-bit addition. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of the carry-lookahead logic.
