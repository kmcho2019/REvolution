To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using the CLA architecture, we first need to design a 4-bit CLA block and then a 16-bit CLA block. The 4-bit CLA block generates the carry-out and sum for 4 bits. The 16-bit CLA block will use four instances of the 4-bit CLA block and will generate the carry-out for each group of 4 bits. The top module, adder_32bit, will use two instances of the 16-bit CLA block to compute the sum and carry-out for 32 bits.
