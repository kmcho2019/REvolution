To design a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first create a module for a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) block. This 16-bit CLA will calculate the sum and carry-out for 16-bit inputs. Then, we will use two instances of the 16-bit CLA block in the top module adder_32bit to compute the sum of two 32-bit numbers. The first 16-bit CLA block will add the lower 16 bits of the input operands A and B, and the second 16-bit CLA block will add the upper 16 bits of A and B, considering the carry-out from the first block.
