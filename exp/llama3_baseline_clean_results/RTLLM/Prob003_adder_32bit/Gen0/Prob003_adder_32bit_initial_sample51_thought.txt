To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to design a 4-bit and then a 16-bit CLA block, as these can be used to build up to the 32-bit adder. The 4-bit CLA block will calculate the sum and carry-out based on the inputs A and B, utilizing the generate and propagate signals to calculate the carry-in and carry-out for each bit position. The 16-bit CLA block will use four instances of the 4-bit CLA to compute the sum of 16-bit inputs A and B. Finally, the top module, adder_32bit, will utilize two instances of the 16-bit CLA block to compute the sum of the 32-bit inputs A and B.
