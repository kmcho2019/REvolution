To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we'll design a 4-bit and 16-bit CLA block first. The 4-bit CLA block will calculate the generate (G) and propagate (P) signals, which will then be used by the 16-bit CLA block to calculate the final sum and carry. We'll then instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks in the top module adder_32bit to form the 32-bit adder. Each 16-bit CLA block will handle 16 bits of the input operands A and B. The final carry-out will be generated by the most significant 16-bit CLA block.
