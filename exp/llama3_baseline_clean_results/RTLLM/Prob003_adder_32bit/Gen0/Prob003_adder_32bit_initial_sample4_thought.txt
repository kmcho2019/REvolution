To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to design a 4-bit and 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block. The 4-bit block will generate the sum, carry-out, and the carry-lookahead signals. The 16-bit block will use four 4-bit blocks to compute the sum and generate the carry-lookahead signals for each 4-bit block. The top-level 32-bit adder module will then use two 16-bit blocks to compute the final sum and carry-out. This approach reduces the complexity of the adder and improves its performance by minimizing the number of logic levels.
