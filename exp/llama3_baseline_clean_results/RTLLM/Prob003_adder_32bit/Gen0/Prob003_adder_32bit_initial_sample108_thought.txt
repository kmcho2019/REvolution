To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to design a basic 1-bit full adder and a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block (CLA block). The 1-bit full adder will serve as the fundamental component for calculating the sum and carry. The 16-bit CLA block will use the 1-bit full adders to generate the sum and carry bits for 16-bit inputs, utilizing the carry-lookahead architecture to calculate the carry bits in advance. Finally, the 32-bit adder will be constructed by instantiating two 16-bit CLA blocks and handling the carry propagation between them. This approach allows for efficient calculation of the sum and carry for 32-bit inputs.
