To implement the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to design a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) block and then use two instances of this block in the top module adder_32bit. The 16-bit CLA block will have two 16-bit inputs (A and B) and produce a 16-bit sum (S) along with a carry-out (C). The top module will then use two of these blocks to add the lower 16 bits and the upper 16 bits of the input operands A and B, handling the carry propagation from the lower block to the upper block to produce the final 32-bit sum and the carry-out.
