To design the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first create a 16-bit CLA block, then use two instances of this block in the top module adder_32bit. The 16-bit CLA block will take two 16-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit sum output S, and a carry-out output C. We will use the 16-bit CLA blocks to add the lower and upper 16 bits of the 32-bit inputs A and B separately. The carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA block will be used as the carry-in for the upper 16-bit CLA block.
