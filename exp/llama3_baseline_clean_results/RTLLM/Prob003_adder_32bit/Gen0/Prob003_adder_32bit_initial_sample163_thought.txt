To design the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first create a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder block, then use it to create a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block, and finally instantiate two 16-bit blocks in the top 32-bit adder module. The 4-bit CLA will compute the sum and carry-out using the CLA equations. The 16-bit CLA will be built by instantiating four 4-bit CLA blocks, handling the internal carry propagation. The 32-bit adder will then use two 16-bit CLA blocks to compute the final sum and carry-out.
