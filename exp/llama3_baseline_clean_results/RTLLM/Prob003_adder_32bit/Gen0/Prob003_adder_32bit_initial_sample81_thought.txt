To design the 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first create a 4-bit and then a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder module. We'll use these modules as building blocks for our final 32-bit adder. Each CLA block calculates the sum and carry-out based on the input bits and the incoming carry. The 32-bit adder will be constructed by instantiating two 16-bit CLA blocks and combining their outputs with an additional stage to handle the final carry propagation.
