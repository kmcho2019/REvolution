To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to design a 4-bit or 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block. Here, we choose to design a 16-bit CLA block. Each 16-bit CLA block will have input ports for two 16-bit operands (A and B), output ports for the 16-bit sum (S), and an output port for the carry-out. The 16-bit CLA block will calculate the sum and carry-out using the carry-lookahead architecture. The top module, adder_32bit, will then instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks to handle the 32-bit inputs A and B. The most significant 16 bits of A and B will go to one 16-bit CLA block, and the least significant 16 bits will go to another. The carry-out from the least significant 16-bit CLA block will be used as the carry-in for the most significant 16-bit CLA block. The final sum (S) will be the concatenation of the sums from the two 16-bit CLA blocks, and the carry-out (C32) will be the carry-out from the most significant 16-bit CLA block.
