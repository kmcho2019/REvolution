In the top module, we will connect two 16-bit CLA blocks in a hierarchical manner to achieve the 32-bit addition. The carry-out from the first 16-bit CLA block (which adds the lower 16 bits of the input operands) will be used as the carry-in for the second 16-bit CLA block (which adds the upper 16 bits of the input operands). This ensures that the carry propagation is correctly handled for the entire 32-bit addition.
