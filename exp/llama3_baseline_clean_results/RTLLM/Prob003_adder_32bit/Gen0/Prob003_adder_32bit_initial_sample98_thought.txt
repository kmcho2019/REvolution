To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we can break down the design into smaller 16-bit carry-lookahead adder blocks. Each 16-bit CLA block will generate its own carry-out, which can be used by the next block to calculate the sum of the higher 16 bits. The 32-bit adder will consist of two 16-bit CLA blocks. The first block will calculate the sum of the lower 16 bits, and the second block will calculate the sum of the higher 16 bits, taking into account the carry-out from the first block. This approach reduces the propagation delay compared to a ripple-carry adder.
