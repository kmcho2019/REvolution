To design a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we first need to create a module for a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder, then another for a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder that uses the 4-bit module, and finally, the top-level 32-bit adder module that utilizes two instances of the 16-bit adder. The key components of the carry-lookahead adder include generate and propagate logic for each bit, and the calculation of the carry signals based on these. We will use structural modeling to instantiate the smaller modules within the larger ones. 
The 4-bit CLA module will compute the sum and carry-out based on the input operands A and B. 
The 16-bit CLA module will utilize four instances of the 4-bit CLA, linking them together with the appropriate carry signals.
Finally, the 32-bit CLA will be constructed from two 16-bit CLA modules, again handling the carry between them.
