To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using the Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) architecture, we first need to design a 16-bit CLA block. This block will handle the addition of two 16-bit numbers and produce a sum and a carry-out. The 32-bit adder can then be constructed by combining two instances of the 16-bit CLA block. The first 16-bit CLA block will handle the lower 16 bits of the input operands, and the second block will handle the upper 16 bits. The carry-out from the first block will be used as the carry-in for the second block. This approach ensures that the carry propagation is handled efficiently, reducing the overall delay of the adder.
