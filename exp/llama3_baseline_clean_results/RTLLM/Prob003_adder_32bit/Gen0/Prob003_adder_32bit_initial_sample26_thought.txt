To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first design a 4-bit and then a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block, and then use two instances of the 16-bit block to create the 32-bit adder. The 4-bit CLA block will calculate the generate (G) and propagate (P) signals for each bit position. The 16-bit CLA block will use these signals to generate the carry signals for each bit position, and then use these carry signals to calculate the sum. The 32-bit adder will consist of two 16-bit CLA blocks and a final adder to handle the carry from the first 16-bit block to the second.
