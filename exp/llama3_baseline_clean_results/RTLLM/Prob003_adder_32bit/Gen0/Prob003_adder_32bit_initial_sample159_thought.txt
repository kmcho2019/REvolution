To design a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we can break down the problem into smaller parts by first creating a 4-bit and 16-bit carry-lookahead adder block. The 4-bit block will be used as a basic building block for the 16-bit block, and then two instances of the 16-bit block will be used to create the 32-bit adder. The carry-lookahead adder generates the carry for each bit position in parallel, reducing the propagation delay compared to a ripple-carry adder. We will use the standard equations for the generate (G) and propagate (P) signals in the CLA architecture to compute the sum and carry outputs.
