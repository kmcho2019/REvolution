To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first design a 4-bit and then a 16-bit CLA block. We'll then use two instances of the 16-bit CLA block in the top module to create the 32-bit adder. This hierarchical approach simplifies the design and leverages the benefits of modularity in digital design. The key components will include the basic adder cells, the generate and propagate signals for the carry-lookahead logic, and the final summation logic to produce the output and carry-out.
