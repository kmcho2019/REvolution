The Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) is a digital circuit that improves the speed of the adder by reducing the propagation delay of the carry signal. In the 32-bit CLA, we will design a 16-bit CLA block and then use two instances of it to implement the 32-bit adder. Each 16-bit CLA block generates the carry signals for each bit position and also generates the sum bits. The top 16-bit CLA block will generate the carry-out and sum bits for the lower 16 bits, and the bottom 16-bit CLA block will use this carry-out as its carry-in to generate the sum and carry-out for the upper 16 bits.
