To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we can break down the design into smaller modules, each responsible for a portion of the overall addition. The main module, `adder_32bit`, will utilize two instances of a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder module, `cla_16bit`, to handle the addition of the high and low 16 bits of the input operands. Each `cla_16bit` module will be responsible for generating the sum and carry-out for its respective portion of the input. The top-level `adder_32bit` module will then combine these results to produce the final 32-bit sum and carry-out. We'll also need a module for the 4-bit carry-lookahead adder, `cla_4bit`, as it's a basic building block for the `cla_16bit` module.
