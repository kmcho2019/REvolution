The design idea is to create a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder using the Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) architecture. The top module, adder_32bit, will be composed of two instances of a 16-bit CLA block. Each 16-bit CLA block will further be composed of two instances of an 8-bit CLA block, and each 8-bit CLA block will be composed of two instances of a 4-bit CLA block. The 4-bit CLA block will be the basic building block. We will use the generate statement to instantiate the blocks and wire them together to form the final 32-bit adder.

We will also define the propagate and generate signals for each bit position, which will be used to calculate the carry-in and sum for each bit position. The propagate signal will be used to determine if the carry from the previous bit position will propagate to the current bit position, and the generate signal will be used to determine if the current bit position will generate a carry.

The adder_32bit module will take two 32-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 32-bit output, S, and a 1-bit carry-out output, C32.
