The Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) architecture is an efficient method for adding two binary numbers. It works by computing the carry signals for each bit position in parallel, rather than waiting for the carry from the previous bit position to be computed. This approach reduces the propagation delay and makes the addition operation faster.
