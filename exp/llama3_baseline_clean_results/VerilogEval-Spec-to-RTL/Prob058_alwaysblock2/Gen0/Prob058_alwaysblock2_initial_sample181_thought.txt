To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: 
1. An assign statement for continuous assignment of the XOR operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to output 'out_assign'. 
2. A combinational always block to continuously update 'out_always_comb' with the XOR result of 'a' and 'b'. 
3. A clocked always block that uses a flip-flop to store the XOR result of 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_always_ff' on the positive edge of the clock 'clk'.
