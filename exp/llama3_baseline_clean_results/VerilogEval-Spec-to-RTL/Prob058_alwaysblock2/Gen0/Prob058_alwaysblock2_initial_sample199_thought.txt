To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: 
1. Continuous assignment: This method will directly assign the XOR of inputs a and b to the output out_assign.
2. Combinational always block: This method will use an always block with the sensitivity list including inputs a and b to generate the XOR output out_always_comb.
3. Clocked always block: This method will use an always block that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal to generate the XOR output out_always_ff. This will introduce a delay due to the sequential nature of the logic.
