To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: 
1. An assign statement, which will continuously update the output out_assign whenever inputs a or b change.
2. A combinational always block, which will also continuously update the output out_always_comb whenever inputs a or b change.
3. A clocked always block (sequential logic), which will update the output out_always_ff only at the positive edge of the clock, thus introducing a delay.

The key differences between these methods lie in how they handle timing and updates. The assign statement and combinational always block will produce the XOR result immediately when the inputs change, while the clocked always block will delay the output until the next positive clock edge.
