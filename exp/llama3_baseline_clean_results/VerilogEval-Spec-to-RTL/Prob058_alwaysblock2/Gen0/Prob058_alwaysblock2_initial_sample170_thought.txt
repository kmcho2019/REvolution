To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we need to design a module that incorporates three different methods to implement an XOR gate: using an assign statement, a combinational always block, and a clocked always block. The assign statement and the combinational always block will produce the XOR of the inputs a and b immediately, while the clocked always block will produce the XOR but with a delay, essentially acting as a flip-flop that updates its output on the positive edge of the clock.
