To solve this problem, we need to design a Verilog module named TopModule that implements an XOR gate three different ways: using an assign statement, a combinational always block, and a clocked always block. The assign statement and the combinational always block will produce the same output, which is the XOR of the inputs a and b. The clocked always block will produce a delayed output due to the flip-flop, so its output will be the XOR of the inputs a and b from the previous clock cycle.
