To implement the TopModule, we will use three different approaches to create an XOR gate. 
First, we will use an assign statement to continuously assign the XOR of inputs 'a' and 'b' to 'out_assign'. 
Next, we will use a combinational always block to calculate the XOR of 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_always_comb'. 
Finally, we will use a clocked always block to create a sequential XOR gate, where the output 'out_always_ff' is the XOR of 'a' and 'b' at the previous clock edge.
