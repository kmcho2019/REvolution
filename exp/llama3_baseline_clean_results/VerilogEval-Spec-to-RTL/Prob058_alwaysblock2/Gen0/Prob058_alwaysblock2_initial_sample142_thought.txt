To implement the TopModule with the specified interface and functionality, we need to use three different methods for creating an XOR gate: a continuous assignment, a combinational always block, and a clocked (sequential) always block. The continuous assignment and the combinational always block will produce the same result (the XOR of inputs 'a' and 'b'), while the clocked always block will introduce a delay due to the flip-flop, updating its output only on the positive edge of the clock. This means that the output of the clocked always block (out_always_ff) will be the XOR of 'a' and 'b' from the previous clock cycle.
