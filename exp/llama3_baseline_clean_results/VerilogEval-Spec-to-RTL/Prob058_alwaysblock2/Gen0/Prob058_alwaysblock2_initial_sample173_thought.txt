To implement the TopModule, we'll use three different methods to create an XOR gate. 
First, we'll use a continuous assignment statement to directly assign the XOR of inputs a and b to out_assign.
Next, we'll use an always_comb block to combine inputs a and b and assign the result to out_always_comb.
Finally, we'll use an always_ff block to create a flip-flop that XORs inputs a and b on each positive clock edge, 
assigning the result to out_always_ff. This will introduce a delay in the output compared to the other two methods.
