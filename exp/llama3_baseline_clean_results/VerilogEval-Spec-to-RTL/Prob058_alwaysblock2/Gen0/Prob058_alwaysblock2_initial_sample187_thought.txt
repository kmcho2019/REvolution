To implement the TopModule, we need to create three different XOR gates for the three output ports. 
The out_assign port will be directly assigned the XOR of inputs a and b using a continuous assignment.
The out_always_comb port will be assigned the XOR of inputs a and b inside a combinational always block.
The out_always_ff port will be assigned the XOR of inputs a and b inside a sequential always block with a positive edge clock trigger, introducing a flip-flop and thus a delay in the output.
