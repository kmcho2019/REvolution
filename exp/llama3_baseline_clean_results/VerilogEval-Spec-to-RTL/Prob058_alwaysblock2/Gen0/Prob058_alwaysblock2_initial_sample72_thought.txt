The module TopModule can be implemented by using three different methods to create an XOR gate. 
First, an assign statement is used to continuously assign the XOR of inputs a and b to out_assign. 
Second, a combinational always block is used to assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_comb whenever either a or b changes. 
Lastly, a clocked always block is used to assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_ff on every positive edge of the clock, effectively delaying the output by one clock cycle.
