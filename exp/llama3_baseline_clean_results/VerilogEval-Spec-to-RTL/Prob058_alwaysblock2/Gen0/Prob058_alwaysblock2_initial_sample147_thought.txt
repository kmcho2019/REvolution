To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to perform an XOR operation on inputs 'a' and 'b'. 
The first method uses a continuous assign statement to directly assign the XOR result to the output 'out_assign'. 
The second method uses a combinational always block to achieve the same result and assign it to 'out_always_comb'. 
The third method uses a clocked always block, which introduces a flip-flop and thus delays the output, assigning the result to 'out_always_ff'.
