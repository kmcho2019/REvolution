To implement the TopModule, we need to create three separate implementations of an XOR gate. 
First, we use an assign statement to continuously assign the XOR of inputs a and b to out_assign. 
Next, we use a combinational always block to calculate the XOR of a and b whenever either input changes and assign the result to out_always_comb. 
Finally, we use a clocked always block to create a flip-flop that captures the XOR of a and b on the positive edge of the clock and assigns the result to out_always_ff.
