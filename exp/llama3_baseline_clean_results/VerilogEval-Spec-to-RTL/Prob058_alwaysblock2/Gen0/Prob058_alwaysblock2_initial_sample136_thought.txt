To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: 
1. Continuous assignment (assign statement) for the out_assign output.
2. A combinational always block for the out_always_comb output.
3. A clocked always block for the out_always_ff output, which will introduce a delay due to the flip-flop.

The assign statement and combinational always block will produce the same circuit, with the output being the XOR of inputs a and b.
The clocked always block will produce a circuit with a flip-flop, causing the output to be delayed by one clock cycle.
