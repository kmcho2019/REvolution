Secondly, we will utilize a combinational always block to perform the XOR operation, assigning the result to 'out_always_comb'. 
Lastly, for the 'out_always_ff' output, we will implement a clocked always block that also performs an XOR operation, but due to its sequential nature, the output will be delayed by one clock cycle.
