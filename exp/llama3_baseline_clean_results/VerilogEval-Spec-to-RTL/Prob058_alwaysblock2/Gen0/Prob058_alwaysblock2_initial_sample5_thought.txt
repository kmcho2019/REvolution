To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate. The first method will utilize a continuous assignment statement, which will directly assign the XOR of inputs a and b to the output out_assign. The second method will use a combinational always block, which will assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_comb. The third method will use a clocked always block, which will assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_ff on the positive edge of the clock, effectively creating a flip-flop that delays the output.
