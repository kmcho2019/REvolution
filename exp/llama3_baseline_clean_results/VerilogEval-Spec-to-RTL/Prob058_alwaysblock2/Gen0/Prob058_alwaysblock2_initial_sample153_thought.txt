To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: 
1. An assign statement for continuous assignment (out_assign).
2. A combinational always block (out_always_comb).
3. A clocked always block (out_always_ff) to introduce a flip-flop and delay the output.

The assign statement and combinational always block will produce identical outputs without any delay, while the clocked always block will introduce a one-clock-cycle delay due to the flip-flop.
