The TopModule can be designed with three different approaches to implement an XOR gate. 
1. Continuous assignment: This will be a simple XOR operation using the assign statement.
2. Combinational always block: This will use an always block without any timing controls (sensitivity list or clock) to perform the XOR operation.
3. Sequential logic (clocked always block): This will use an always block with a positive edge clock to perform the XOR operation and introduce a flip-flop, thus delaying the output.
