The module TopModule should have three separate implementations of an XOR gate: one using an assign statement, one using a combinational always block, and one using a clocked always block. The assign statement and the combinational always block will produce the same circuit, with the output being the XOR of inputs a and b. The clocked always block, however, will introduce a delay due to the flip-flop, so the output will be the XOR of the previous values of a and b.
