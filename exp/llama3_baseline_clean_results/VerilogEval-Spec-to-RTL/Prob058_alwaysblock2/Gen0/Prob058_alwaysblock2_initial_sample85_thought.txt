To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we need to create three separate XOR gates. 
The first XOR gate will use an assign statement to continuously drive the output out_assign with the XOR result of inputs a and b.
The second XOR gate will use a combinational always block to calculate the XOR result of inputs a and b and assign it to output out_always_comb.
The third XOR gate will use a clocked always block to calculate the XOR result of inputs a and b at the positive edge of the clock and assign it to output out_always_ff. 
Since it's a sequential logic, the output out_always_ff will be delayed compared to the other two outputs.
