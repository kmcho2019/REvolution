To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate. 
Firstly, we will use an assign statement to continuously assign the XOR of inputs a and b to out_assign. 
Secondly, we will use a combinational always block to assign the XOR of a and b to out_always_comb whenever either a or b changes.
Lastly, we will use a clocked always block to create a sequential logic circuit. This circuit will create a flip-flop that updates out_always_ff with the XOR of a and b on the positive edge of the clock.
