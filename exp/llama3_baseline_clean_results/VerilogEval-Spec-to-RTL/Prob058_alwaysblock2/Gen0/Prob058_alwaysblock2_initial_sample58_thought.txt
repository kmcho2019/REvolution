To implement the TopModule as described, we will use three different methods to create an XOR gate: an assign statement, a combinational always block, and a clocked always block. The assign statement and the combinational always block will produce the same output, performing the XOR operation directly on the inputs a and b. The clocked always block, however, will introduce a delay, effectively acting as a flip-flop, where the output out_always_ff will be the result of the XOR operation from the previous clock cycle.
