To implement the TopModule, we will use three different methods to perform an XOR operation on the inputs a and b. 
1. Continuous Assignment: We will use the assign statement to directly assign the result of the XOR operation to the output out_assign.
2. Combinational Always Block: We will use an always block with a sensitivity list to update the output out_always_comb whenever either of the inputs a or b changes.
3. Clocked Always Block: We will use an always block with a clocked event to update the output out_always_ff on every positive edge of the clock signal. This will introduce a delay in the output.
