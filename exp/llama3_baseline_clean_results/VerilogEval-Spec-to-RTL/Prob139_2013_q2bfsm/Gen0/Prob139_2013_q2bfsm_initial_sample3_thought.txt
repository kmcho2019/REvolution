4. **State D (Set g=1 and Monitor y):** In this state, the FSM sets g=1 and then monitors the y input. If y becomes 1 within two clock cycles, the FSM stays in this state with g=1 permanently. If y does not become 1 within two clock cycles, the FSM moves to the next state.
5. **State E (Set g=0):** The FSM sets g=0 permanently (until reset).
