To create the submodule "TopModule" with a 2:1 multiplexer and a flip-flop, we will use the following design approach:
- The 2:1 multiplexer will select between the 'q_in' and 'r_in' based on the 'L' input.
- The output of the multiplexer will be connected to the data input of the flip-flop.
- The flip-flop will store the selected value at the positive edge of the 'clk' and output it as 'Q'.
This hierarchical design allows for easy reuse of the TopModule in the full_module, improving modularity and reducing code duplication.
