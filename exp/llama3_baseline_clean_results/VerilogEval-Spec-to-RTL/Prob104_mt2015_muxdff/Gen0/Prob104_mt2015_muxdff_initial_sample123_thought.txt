To create the submodule "TopModule", we need to identify the components involved in the operation of a single flip-flop within the full_module. This includes a 2:1 multiplexer to select between the load value (r_in) and the feedback from the flip-flop (q), and the flip-flop itself to store the value. The 2:1 multiplexer is controlled by the load signal (L). The flip-flop is clocked by the clk signal.
