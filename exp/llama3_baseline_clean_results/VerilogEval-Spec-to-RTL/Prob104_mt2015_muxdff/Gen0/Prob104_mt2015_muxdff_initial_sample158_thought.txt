To create the submodule "TopModule", we need to consider the functionality of the full_module and factor it into smaller components. The TopModule will have a 2:1 multiplexer and a flip-flop. The 2:1 multiplexer will select either the input q_in or r_in based on the input L. The selected input will then be stored in the flip-flop on the positive edge of the clock signal clk. The output of the flip-flop will be Q.
