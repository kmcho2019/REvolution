To create the submodule "TopModule", we will design it as a 2:1 multiplexer that selects between the input q_in (from the previous flip-flop or other source) and the input r_in (load value), based on the value of L (load enable). When L is high, the input r_in is passed to the flip-flop, otherwise the input q_in is passed. The flip-flop will be clocked by the input clk, and its output will be Q.
