The TopModule will contain a 4-digit BCD counter. Each digit will be represented by 4 bits. We will use a 16-bit register to store the current count. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the current count. When the ones digit reaches 9, the tens digit should be incremented and the ones digit should be reset to 0. Similarly, when the tens digit reaches 9, the hundreds digit should be incremented and the tens and ones digits should be reset to 0. When the thousands digit reaches 9, the counter should wrap around to 0. We will use a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0.
