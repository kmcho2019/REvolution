To implement the TopModule as a 4-digit BCD counter, we will create a module that counts up to 9999 in binary-coded decimal. The counter will have four 4-bit BCD counters. The lower three BCD counters will have an enable signal that is asserted when their current value is 9, so the next clock cycle will reset them and increment the next higher BCD counter. We will use a separate signal for the enable of each of the upper three digits. The counter will also have a synchronous active-high reset that resets all BCD counters and enable signals when asserted.
