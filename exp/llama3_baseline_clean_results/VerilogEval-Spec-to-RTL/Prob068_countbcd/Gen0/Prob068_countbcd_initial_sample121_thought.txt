To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four 4-bit counters to represent the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands places. Each counter will be incremented based on the carry from the previous counter. The enable signals will be generated based on the count of each digit. When a digit reaches 9, it will reset to 0 and the next higher digit will be incremented, and an enable signal will be generated for the next higher digit. We will also include a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0 when the reset signal is high.
