To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we need to design a counter that can count up to 9 in binary-coded decimal (BCD) and then reset to 0 for each digit. We will use four 4-bit counters to represent each digit. The ones digit will be incremented every clock cycle. The tens digit will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., it reaches 9 in BCD). Similarly, the hundreds and thousands digits will be incremented when the tens and hundreds digits overflow, respectively. The enable signals for each of the upper three digits will be asserted when their corresponding lower digit overflows. The entire counter will be reset to 0 when the reset signal is asserted.
