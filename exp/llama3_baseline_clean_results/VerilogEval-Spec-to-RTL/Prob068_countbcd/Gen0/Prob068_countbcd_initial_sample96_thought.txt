To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will design a module with a clocked process that updates the counter value on every positive clock edge. The counter will be reset to zero when the reset signal is asserted high. We will use four 4-bit registers to hold the BCD values of the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands digits. The enable signals will be generated based on the carry conditions of each digit. When the value of a digit reaches 9, it will be reset to 0 and the next higher digit will be enabled to increment. This process will be repeated for all digits.
