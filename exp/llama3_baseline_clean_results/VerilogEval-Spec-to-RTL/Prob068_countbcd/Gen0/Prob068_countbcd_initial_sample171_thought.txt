To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use a combination of sequential logic and arithmetic operations. We will first initialize the counter to zero when the reset signal is asserted. Then, on every positive edge of the clock, we will increment the ones digit. If the ones digit reaches 9, we will reset it to 0 and increment the tens digit. If the tens digit reaches 9, we will reset it to 0 and increment the hundreds digit, and so on. The enable signals will be asserted whenever the corresponding digit needs to be incremented. This approach ensures that each digit is incremented only when necessary, which is the essence of a BCD counter.
