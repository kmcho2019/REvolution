To implement the TopModule, we need to create a 4-digit BCD counter with enable signals for the upper three digits. We will use four 4-bit counters for each digit. The ones digit will increment on every clock cycle. The tens, hundreds, and thousands digits will increment when their respective lower digits reach 9. We will use the enable signals (ena) to indicate when each of the upper three digits should be incremented. We will also include a synchronous active-high reset to reset all counters to zero when reset is high.
