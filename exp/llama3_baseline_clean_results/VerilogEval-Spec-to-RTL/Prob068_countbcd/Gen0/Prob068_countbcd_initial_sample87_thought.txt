To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we will create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. The counter will have four 4-bit binary-coded decimal digits. The ones digit (q[3:0]) will be incremented on every clock cycle. The tens digit (q[7:4]) will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., when it reaches 9). Similarly, the hundreds digit (q[11:8]) will be incremented when the tens digit overflows, and the thousands digit (q[15:12]) will be incremented when the hundreds digit overflows. The enable signals (ena) will be asserted when the corresponding digit should be incremented. The reset signal will be used to reset the counter to zero on the next positive clock edge when it is asserted high.
