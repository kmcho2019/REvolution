To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we need to design a module that can count from 0000 to 9999 in BCD format. We'll use four 4-bit counters for each digit. The ones digit will increment on every clock cycle, the tens digit will increment when the ones digit wraps around from 9 to 0, the hundreds digit will increment when the tens digit wraps around from 9 to 0, and the thousands digit will increment when the hundreds digit wraps around from 9 to 0. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on these wrap-around conditions. We'll also include a synchronous reset that resets the counter to 0000 when asserted high.
