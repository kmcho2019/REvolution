To implement a 4-digit BCD counter with enable signals for the upper three digits, 
we will use a combination of counters and logic to generate the enable signals. 
The counter will be divided into four 4-bit BCD counters. Each counter will 
increment when its corresponding enable signal is high. The enable signal for 
each counter will be generated based on the state of the counters. For example, 
the enable signal for the tens counter will be high when the ones counter 
wraps around from 9 to 0. This logic will be repeated for the hundreds and 
thousands counters. 
We will use a case statement or if-else condition to handle the wrap-around 
for each digit (9 to 0). We will also handle the reset condition, where all 
counters will be reset to 0 and the enable signals will be set accordingly.
