To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will create a module named TopModule that includes four 4-bit counters for each digit. The ones digit (q[3:0]) will be incremented every clock cycle. The tens digit (q[7:4]) will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., it reaches 9 and needs to be reset to 0). Similarly, the hundreds digit (q[11:8]) will be incremented when the tens digit overflows, and the thousands digit (q[15:12]) will be incremented when the hundreds digit overflows. We will also generate enable signals (ena) for the upper three digits, which will be active when their respective digits need to be incremented.
