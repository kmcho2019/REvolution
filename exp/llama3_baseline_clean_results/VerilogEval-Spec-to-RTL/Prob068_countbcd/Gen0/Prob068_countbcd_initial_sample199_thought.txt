To implement the TopModule, we'll first design a 4-bit binary-coded decimal (BCD) counter that can count from 0 to 9. We will then use four instances of this BCD counter to create a 4-digit BCD counter. Each BCD counter will be assigned to one of the digits (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands). The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the current count of the lower digits. For example, the tens digit should be incremented when the ones digit reaches 9 and wraps around to 0. We will also implement a synchronous active-high reset that resets all counters to 0.
