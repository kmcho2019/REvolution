The TopModule will implement a 4-digit BCD counter. The counter will have a reset input to reset the counter to zero when it's high. The counter will be incremented on the positive edge of the clock. 
Each decimal digit (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands) will be represented by 4 bits. 
The enable signals will be used to determine when the upper digits should be incremented. 
For example, when the ones digit reaches 9, the next clock edge should increment the tens digit and reset the ones digit to zero, and so on for the hundreds and thousands digits. 
The synchronous active-high reset will be used to reset all the digits to zero.
