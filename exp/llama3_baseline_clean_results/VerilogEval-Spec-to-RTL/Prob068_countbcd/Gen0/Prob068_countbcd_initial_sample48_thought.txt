To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we can use four 4-bit counters. 
The ones digit will be incremented on every clock cycle. 
The tens digit will be incremented when the ones digit wraps around from 9 to 0.
The hundreds digit will be incremented when the tens digit wraps around from 9 to 0.
The thousands digit will be incremented when the hundreds digit wraps around from 9 to 0.
We can use the 'ena' signal to indicate when each of the upper three digits should be incremented.
We will also include a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0.
