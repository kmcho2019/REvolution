To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will create a module named TopModule. This module will have four 4-bit counters for each BCD digit. The ones digit will be incremented on every clock cycle. The tens, hundreds, and thousands digits will be incremented when their respective lower digits reach 9. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the values of their lower digits. We will also implement a synchronous active-high reset to reset all the counters to zero when the reset signal is high.
