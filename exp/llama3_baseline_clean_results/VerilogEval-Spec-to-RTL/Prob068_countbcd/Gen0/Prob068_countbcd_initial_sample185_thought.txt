To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four 4-bit counters. 
Each counter will represent a decimal digit in BCD format. We will use 
enable signals to control the increment of the upper three digits. When 
the ones digit overflows from 9 to 0, the tens digit will be incremented, 
and so on. If the thousands digit overflows, it will wrap around to 0. 
The synchronous reset will reset all counters and enable signals to 0. 
We will use if-else statements to check for overflow conditions and 
increment the corresponding digits.
