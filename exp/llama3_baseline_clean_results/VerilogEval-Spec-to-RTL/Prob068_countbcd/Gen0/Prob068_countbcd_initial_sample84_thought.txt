To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we'll use a combination of sequential logic and combinatorial logic. We'll have a 16-bit register to store the current count, divided into four 4-bit fields for the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands digits. We'll increment the count on each positive clock edge, propagating the carry from each digit to the next when it reaches 9. We'll also generate enable signals for the upper three digits, setting them high when the corresponding digit needs to be incremented. The reset will be synchronous, resetting the count to 0 on the next positive clock edge after reset goes high.
