To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we need to first create a BCD counter for a single digit. Then, we can use three instances of this counter, connecting the enable signals between them to form a 4-digit BCD counter. The enable signal for each digit will be high only when the next lower digit has reached its maximum value (9 in BCD). This way, when the ones digit reaches 9, the tens digit will be incremented, and so on. The most significant digit will not have its own enable signal but will be incremented when the third digit reaches 9. We also need to include a synchronous reset that clears the counter when high and asserted on the positive edge of the clock.
