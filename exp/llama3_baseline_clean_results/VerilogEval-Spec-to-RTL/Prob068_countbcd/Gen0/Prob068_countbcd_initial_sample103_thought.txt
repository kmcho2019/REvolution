To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we need to use a series of counters that increment their values based on certain conditions. The ones digit will increment every clock cycle, the tens digit will increment when the ones digit reaches 9, the hundreds digit will increment when the tens and ones digits reach 9, and the thousands digit will increment when the hundreds, tens, and ones digits reach 9. We will use a combination of sequential logic and combinational logic to achieve this. The enable signals for each digit will be used to indicate when the next digit should be incremented. The synchronous reset will be used to reset all the counters to 0.
