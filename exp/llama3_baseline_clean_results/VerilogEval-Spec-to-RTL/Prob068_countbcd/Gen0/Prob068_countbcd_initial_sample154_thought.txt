To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use a 4-stage counter, where each stage is responsible for counting a decimal digit. Each digit will be represented by 4 bits. We will use a simple counter design for each stage, with the next stage enabled when the current stage reaches 9 (i.e., '1001' in binary). We will use a synchronous reset to reset all stages to 0 when the reset signal is high. The enable signals will be asserted when the corresponding stage should be incremented.
