To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we will use a combination of sequential logic to update the counter values and combinational logic to determine when to increment each digit. The counter will be divided into four BCD digits (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands). Each BCD digit will be 4 bits wide, allowing for values from 0 to 9. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be set high when the lower digits wrap around (i.e., reach 10), indicating that the next higher digit should be incremented. We will use a synchronous reset to initialize the counter and enable signals.
