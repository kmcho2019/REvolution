To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we'll use four 4-bit counters to represent each digit. We'll also use a reset signal to initialize the counter to zero when it's high. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be asserted when the previous digit reaches 9. This means that when the ones digit reaches 9, it will wrap around to 0 and assert the enable signal for the tens digit, and so on. We'll use Verilog's built-in arithmetic operators to increment the digits and check for the wrap-around condition.
