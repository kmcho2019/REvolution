To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we'll use four 4-bit counters for each digit. The ones digit (q[3:0]) will be incremented every clock cycle. The tens digit (q[7:4]) will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., when it reaches 9 and is about to wrap around to 0). Similarly, the hundreds digit (q[11:8]) will be incremented when the tens digit overflows, and the thousands digit (q[15:12]) will be incremented when the hundreds digit overflows.

We'll also generate enable signals for the upper three digits based on these overflow conditions. 

The synchronous active-high reset will reset all digits and enable signals to zero when asserted.
