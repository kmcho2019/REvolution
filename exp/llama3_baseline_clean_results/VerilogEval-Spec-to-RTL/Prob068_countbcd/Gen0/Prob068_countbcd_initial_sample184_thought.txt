To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we can utilize four 4-bit counters, one for each digit. The ones digit counter will increment on every clock cycle. The tens digit counter will increment when the ones digit counter overflows (i.e., when it reaches 10). Similarly, the hundreds digit counter will increment when the tens digit counter overflows, and the thousands digit counter will increment when the hundreds digit counter overflows. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be asserted when the corresponding lower digit counter overflows. A synchronous active-high reset will be used to reset all counters to zero.
