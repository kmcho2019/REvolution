To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we'll use a modular approach. We'll create a separate module for a single BCD digit counter, which will handle the increment and reset logic for one digit. The TopModule will instantiate four of these BCD counters, connecting the enable signals to control the increment of the upper three digits. The enable signals will be generated based on the carry from the lower digits. The reset signal will be propagated to all counters to ensure a synchronous reset.
