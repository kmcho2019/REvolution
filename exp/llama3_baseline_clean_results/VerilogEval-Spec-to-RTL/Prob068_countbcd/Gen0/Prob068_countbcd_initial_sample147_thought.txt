To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we need to keep track of each digit and increment them accordingly. We will use four 4-bit counters to represent the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands digits. Each counter will be implemented using a flip-flop with a synchronous reset. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the value of the lower digits. For example, the tens digit should be incremented when the ones digit reaches 9. Similarly, the hundreds and thousands digits should be incremented when the tens and hundreds digits reach 9, respectively. We will also ensure that the counter wraps around to 0000 when it reaches 9999.
