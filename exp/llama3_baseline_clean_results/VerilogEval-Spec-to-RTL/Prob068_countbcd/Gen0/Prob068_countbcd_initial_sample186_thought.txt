To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we need to keep track of each digit and its enable signal. We will use a counter for each digit, where each counter counts from 0 to 9 (in BCD). When a counter reaches 9, it resets to 0 and the enable signal for the next higher digit is set. This will propagate the increment to the next higher digit. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be set when their corresponding counters reach 9 and are then reset. The reset signal will be used to reset all counters to 0.
