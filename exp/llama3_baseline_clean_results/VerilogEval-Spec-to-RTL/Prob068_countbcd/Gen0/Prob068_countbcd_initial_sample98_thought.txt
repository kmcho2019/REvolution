To implement the TopModule, we will first initialize the enable signals (ena) and the output (q). We'll then design the counter logic to increment the digits based on the enable signals. The counter will start from 0000 and will increment the ones digit first. When the ones digit reaches 9, the tens digit will be incremented and the ones digit will reset to 0. This logic will be applied to all four digits. We'll use if-else statements to check the values of the digits and apply the counter logic accordingly. We'll also use a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0000 when the reset signal is high.
