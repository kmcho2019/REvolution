To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we can use four 4-bit counters for each digit. The ones digit counter increments on every clock cycle. The tens, hundreds, and thousands digits counters increment only when the lower digit counter reaches 9 (1111 in binary) and a new clock cycle begins, and they reset to 0 when they reach 10 (1010 in binary). The enable signals for the upper three digits are generated based on the values of the lower digits.
