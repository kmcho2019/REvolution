To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we can create separate counters for each digit, using a 4-bit binary counter for each. We need to increment the upper digits when the lower digits reach 9 (1001 in binary). The enable signals will be generated based on this condition. The counters will reset synchronously when the reset signal is asserted high. We will use the enable signals to increment the upper digits when the lower digits roll over from 9 to 0.
