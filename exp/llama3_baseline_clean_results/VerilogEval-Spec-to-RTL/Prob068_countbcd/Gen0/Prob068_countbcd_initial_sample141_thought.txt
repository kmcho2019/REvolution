To implement a 4-digit BCD counter, we need to design a module that can count from 0000 to 9999 in BCD format. 
We will use four 4-bit counters to represent each digit of the BCD number. 
The ones digit (q[3:0]) will increment on every clock cycle, the tens digit (q[7:4]) will increment when the ones digit overflows (i.e., when it reaches 9), 
the hundreds digit (q[11:8]) will increment when the tens digit overflows, and the thousands digit (q[15:12]) will increment when the hundreds digit overflows. 
We will also generate enable signals (ena[2:0]) to indicate when each of the upper three digits should be incremented. 
A synchronous active-high reset will be used to reset the counter to 0000 when the reset signal is high.
