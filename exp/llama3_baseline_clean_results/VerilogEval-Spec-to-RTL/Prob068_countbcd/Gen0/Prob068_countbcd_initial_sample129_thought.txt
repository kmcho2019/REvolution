The TopModule can be designed using a 4-digit BCD counter. We will use four 4-bit counters to represent each digit of the BCD counter. The counters will be incremented based on the enable signals. The ones digit will always be incremented on every clock cycle when the reset is not asserted. The tens digit will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., when it reaches 9), and similarly, the hundreds and thousands digits will be incremented when the tens and hundreds digits overflow, respectively. The enable signals for the upper three digits can be generated based on these overflow conditions. We will also include a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to zero when the reset signal is asserted.
