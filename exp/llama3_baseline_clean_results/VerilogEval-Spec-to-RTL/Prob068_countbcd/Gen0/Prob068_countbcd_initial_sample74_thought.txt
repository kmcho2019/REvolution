To implement the TopModule, we need to design a 4-digit BCD counter. We can break down the counter into four 4-bit counters, each representing a decimal digit. We'll use a separate counter for each digit and enable the increment of the upper digits only when the lower digit overflows (i.e., reaches 9). The enable signals (ena) will be used to control the increment of the upper digits. On reset, all counters and enable signals should be reset to their initial values.
