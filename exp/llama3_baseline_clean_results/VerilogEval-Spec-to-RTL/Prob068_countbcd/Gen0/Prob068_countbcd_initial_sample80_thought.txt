The TopModule will implement a 4-digit BCD counter. To achieve this, we will use a combination of counters and conditional statements to control the enable signals. We will first design a 4-bit BCD counter that increments the ones digit. When the ones digit reaches 9, we will generate an enable signal for the tens digit and reset the ones digit. We will repeat this process for the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits. The enable signals will be asserted when their respective digits need to be incremented. A synchronous active-high reset will be used to reset the counter to zero.
