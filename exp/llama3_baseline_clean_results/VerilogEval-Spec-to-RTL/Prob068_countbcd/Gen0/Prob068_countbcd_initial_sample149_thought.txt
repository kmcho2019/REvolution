To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four 4-bit counters, 
each representing a decimal digit. The ones digit will be incremented on 
every clock cycle. The tens, hundreds, and thousands digits will be 
incremented when the previous digit overflows (i.e., reaches 9). The 
enable signals for the upper three digits will be asserted when their 
corresponding digits should be incremented. A synchronous reset will be 
used to clear all the digits and enable signals when the reset input is 
high.
