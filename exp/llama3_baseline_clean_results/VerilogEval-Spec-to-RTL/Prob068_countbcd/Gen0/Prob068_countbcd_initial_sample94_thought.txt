The TopModule will be implemented as a 4-digit BCD counter. 
We will use four 4-bit counters to represent the four digits. 
The enable signals for each digit will be generated based on the 
count value of the previous digits, ensuring that the next digit 
is incremented only when the previous digit reaches 9. 
The module will also have a synchronous active-high reset to 
clear the count and enable signals when asserted.
