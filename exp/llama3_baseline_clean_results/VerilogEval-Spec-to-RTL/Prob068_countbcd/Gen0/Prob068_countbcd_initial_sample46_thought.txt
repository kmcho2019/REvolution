To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four 4-bit counters for each decimal digit. We will start with the ones digit and increment it until it reaches 9 (1001 in binary), then reset it to 0 and increment the tens digit. This process continues for each digit. To implement the enable signals, we will set ena[0] to 1 whenever we increment the tens digit, ena[1] to 1 whenever we increment the hundreds digit, and ena[2] to 1 whenever we increment the thousands digit. We will use a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0.
