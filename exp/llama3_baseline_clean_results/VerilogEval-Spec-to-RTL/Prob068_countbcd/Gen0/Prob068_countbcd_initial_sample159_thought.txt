To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four separate counters, each incrementing the corresponding decimal digit. We will use a total of 16 bits to store the current count. The counter will reset to 0 when the reset signal is high. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the current value of the corresponding lower digit, to ensure proper BCD counting. Specifically, the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits will be incremented when the ones, tens, and hundreds digits, respectively, reach 9.
