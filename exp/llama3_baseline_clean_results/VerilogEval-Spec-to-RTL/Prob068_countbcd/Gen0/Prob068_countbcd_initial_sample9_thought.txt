To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we'll use a synchronous approach with a reset. We'll create four 4-bit binary counters, one for each decimal digit, and chain them together. Each counter will be enabled when the previous counter overflows (reaches 9) and the clock edge occurs. We'll use if-else statements to handle the counting and overflow conditions for each digit. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be derived from the overflow conditions of the lower digits.
