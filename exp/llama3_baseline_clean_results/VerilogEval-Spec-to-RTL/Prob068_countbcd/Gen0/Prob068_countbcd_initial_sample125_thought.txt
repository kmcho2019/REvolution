To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we can use a hierarchical approach. We will create a separate module for a single BCD digit counter that counts from 0 to 9. The TopModule will instantiate four instances of this BCD counter module. The enable signals for the upper three digits will be generated based on the value of the lower digits. When the lower digit reaches 9, the next digit will be enabled to increment. We will also include a synchronous active-high reset to reset the counter to 0.
