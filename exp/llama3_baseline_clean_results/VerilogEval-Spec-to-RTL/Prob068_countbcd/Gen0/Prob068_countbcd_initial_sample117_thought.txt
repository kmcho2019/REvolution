To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will use four 4-bit counters for each decimal digit. The ones digit will increment on every clock cycle, and the tens, hundreds, and thousands digits will increment when their respective lower digits reach 9 (i.e., 1001 in binary) and reset to 0. We will use a hierarchical approach to implement each 4-bit BCD counter. For the enable signals, we will output a '1' for each upper digit when its lower digit reaches 9. The reset signal will reset all counters to 0.
