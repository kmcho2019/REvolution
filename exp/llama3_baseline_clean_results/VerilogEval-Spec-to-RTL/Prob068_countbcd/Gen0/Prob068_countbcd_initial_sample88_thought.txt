To implement the 4-digit BCD counter, we will create a module named TopModule that includes the specified interface. We will use four 4-bit counters to represent each decimal digit. The ones digit will be incremented on every clock cycle. The tens digit will be incremented when the ones digit overflows (i.e., when it reaches 9 and then resets to 0), and similarly for the hundreds and thousands digits. We will also generate enable signals for the upper three digits based on their respective overflow conditions. A synchronous active-high reset will be implemented to reset all counters and enable signals when the reset input is high.
