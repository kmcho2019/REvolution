The Karnaugh map describes a Boolean function of three variables (a, b, and c) that outputs a single bit. Looking at the Karnaugh map, we can observe that the output is 1 whenever 'a' is 1, regardless of the values of 'b' and 'c'. When 'a' is 0, the output is 1 only when both 'b' and 'c' are 1. This can be expressed as a simple logical equation: out = a + (b & c).
