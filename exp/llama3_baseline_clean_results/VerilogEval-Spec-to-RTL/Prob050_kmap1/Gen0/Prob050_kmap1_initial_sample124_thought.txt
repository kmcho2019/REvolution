To implement the given Karnaugh map in Verilog, we first need to identify the logic pattern. The output 'out' is 1 for all combinations of 'b' and 'c' when 'a' is 1, regardless of 'b' and 'c'. When 'a' is 0, 'out' is 1 only when 'b' and 'c' are both 1. This can be simplified into the logic expression: out = a | (b & c).
