The Karnaugh map provided can be simplified into a Boolean expression. Looking at the map, we can see that whenever b is 1, the output is 1 regardless of the values of a and c. This can be expressed as b. Additionally, when b is 0 and a is 1, the output is also 1. This gives us the expression a*c when b is 0. Combining these conditions, we get the expression out = b || (a && c). This expression represents the logic described by the Karnaugh map.
