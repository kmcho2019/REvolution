The Karnaugh map describes a circuit with inputs a, b, and c, and output out. We can simplify the circuit by applying the rules of the Karnaugh map. Looking at the map, we can see that out is 1 when a is 1 and b or c is 1, or when a is 0 and both b and c are 1. Therefore, we can implement this circuit using a combination of logical operators: AND, OR. 
The equation can be derived by combining the minterms for each output 1 in the Karnaugh map.
