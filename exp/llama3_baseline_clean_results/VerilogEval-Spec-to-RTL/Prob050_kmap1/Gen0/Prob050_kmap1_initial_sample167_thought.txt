The Karnaugh map can be simplified by looking for adjacent 1s and combining them. The map shows that when b is 1 (regardless of c), out is 1. Additionally, when b is 0, c is 0, and a is 1, out is also 1. This can be expressed as out = (b) OR (a AND c') where ' denotes NOT operation. However, upon closer inspection, the expression can be further simplified to out = b OR (a AND NOT(c) AND NOT(b)) is not necessary because the map indicates out is 1 when b is 1 regardless of a or c. Thus, focusing on the condition when b is 0, the map shows that when a is 1, out is 1. Therefore, out can be directly derived from the condition that it's 1 when b is 1 or when a is 1 (considering the initial simplification was partially incorrect in including c'). The correct simplification is out = b OR a.
