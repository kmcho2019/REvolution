The Karnaugh map shows the output 'out' for different combinations of inputs 'a', 'b', and 'c'. We can observe that the output 'out' is '1' whenever 'a' is '1' or 'b' and 'c' are both '1'. This logic can be implemented using simple logic gates, specifically OR and AND gates. The Verilog module can be designed to reflect this logic.
