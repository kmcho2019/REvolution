To implement the circuit described by the Karnaugh map, we first need to understand the output pattern. 
Looking at the map, the output is 1 whenever b is 1, or when a and c are both 0. 
This can be expressed as an OR operation between the signal b and the AND operation between the inverted signals a and c. 
Therefore, the Verilog module should implement the logical expression: out = b | (~a & ~c).
