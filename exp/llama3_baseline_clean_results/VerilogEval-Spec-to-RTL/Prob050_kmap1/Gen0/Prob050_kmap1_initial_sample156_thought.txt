The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 whenever 'c' is 1, regardless of the values of 'a' and 'b'. Additionally, when 'c' is 0, the output is 1 only when 'b' is 1 and 'a' is 0. We can implement this using a combination of logical operators in Verilog, specifically using the OR and AND operators to cover all the conditions. We notice that the output is 1 whenever 'c' is 1 or when 'b' is 1 and 'a' is 0. This can be directly translated into a Verilog expression.
