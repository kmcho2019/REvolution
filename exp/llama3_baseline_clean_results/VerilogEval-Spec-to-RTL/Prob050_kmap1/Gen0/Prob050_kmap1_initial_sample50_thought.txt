To implement the circuit described by the Karnaugh map, we first need to analyze the map to find the simplest logic expression that represents the output 'out'. From the map, we can see that 'out' is 1 whenever 'b' is 1, or when 'a' is 1 and 'c' is 0. This can be represented by the Boolean expression: out = b + (a & ~c). This expression will be the basis of our Verilog implementation.
