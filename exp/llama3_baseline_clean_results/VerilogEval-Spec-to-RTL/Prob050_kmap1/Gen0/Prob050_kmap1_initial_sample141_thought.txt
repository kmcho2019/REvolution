To implement the circuit described by the Karnaugh map, we need to identify the minterms that cover all the cells with an output of 1. The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 for the following combinations of inputs: a'b'c', a'bc, ab'c, abc, ab'c', abc'. We can simplify this by noticing that the output is 1 whenever b and c are both 1, or when a is 1 and either b or c (or both) is 1. This can be expressed as the Boolean equation: out = (b & c) | (a & (b | c)).
