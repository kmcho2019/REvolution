To implement the circuit described by the Karnaugh map, we need to analyze the output for each combination of inputs a, b, and c. The Karnaugh map shows that the output is 1 for all combinations except when a is 0 and both b and c are 0. This can be achieved by implementing a logic expression that ORs the results of checking for these conditions. The expression will be out = (a) OR (b AND c).
