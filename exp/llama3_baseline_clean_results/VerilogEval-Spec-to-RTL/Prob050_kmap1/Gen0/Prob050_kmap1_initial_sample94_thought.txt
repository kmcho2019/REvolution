The Karnaugh map shows the desired output of the circuit for each combination of inputs a, b, and c. 
From the map, we can see that the output is 1 whenever a is 1, regardless of the values of b and c. 
Additionally, the output is 1 when a is 0 and b and c are both 1. 
We can express this logic using a simple Boolean equation: out = a + (b & c).
This equation can be directly translated into Verilog code.
