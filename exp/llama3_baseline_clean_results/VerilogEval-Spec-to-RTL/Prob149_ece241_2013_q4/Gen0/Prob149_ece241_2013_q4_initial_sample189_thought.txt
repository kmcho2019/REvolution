To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The FSM will be controlled by the three sensors (s[2], s[1], s[0]) and the current state. When the water level is above the highest sensor, all flow rate outputs will be zero. When the water level is between the sensors, the nominal flow rate will be determined by the current state. If the previous state was lower than the current state, the Supplemental flow valve will be opened to increase the flow rate. The FSM will be reset to the initial state (below the lowest sensor) when the reset input is high.

We will use two registers: one to store the current state (as represented by the sensor values) and one to store the previous state. The previous state will be used to determine if the water level has increased or decreased.

The output logic will be determined by the current state and the previous state. If the water level has increased, the Supplemental flow valve will be opened.
