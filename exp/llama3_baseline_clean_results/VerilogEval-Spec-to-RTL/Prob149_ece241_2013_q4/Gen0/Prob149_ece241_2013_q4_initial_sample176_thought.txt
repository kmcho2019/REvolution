To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine that determines the output flow rates based on the current and previous water levels. The module will have the following states: 
- above the highest sensor (s[2])
- between the highest and middle sensors (s[1])
- between the middle and lowest sensors (s[0])
- below the lowest sensor (no sensors asserted)

The module will also track the previous sensor state to determine if the water level has increased or decreased, and adjust the flow rate accordingly. 

The module will be reset to a state equivalent to the water level being below the lowest sensor when the reset signal is asserted.
