To implement the TopModule, we will create a state machine that monitors the water level sensors and controls the flow rate accordingly. The state machine will have four states corresponding to the water levels: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. We will also keep track of the previous sensor state to determine if the water level is increasing or decreasing. Based on the current and previous sensor states, we will determine the nominal flow rate and whether to open the Supplemental flow valve.
