To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) to track the current water level and the previous level. The FSM can have four states corresponding to the water level ranges: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The FSM can use the input 's' to determine the current state and update the output flow rates accordingly. Additionally, the FSM can use a flag to track whether the previous level was lower than the current level, and update the Supplemental flow valve output based on this flag. The FSM will also be reset to a state equivalent to a low water level when the active-high synchronous reset input 'reset' is asserted.
