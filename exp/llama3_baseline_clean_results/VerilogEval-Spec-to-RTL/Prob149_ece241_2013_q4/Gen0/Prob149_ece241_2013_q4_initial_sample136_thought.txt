To implement the TopModule, we need to analyze the water level based on the sensor inputs and determine the flow rate accordingly. We can use a state machine approach to keep track of the previous water level and adjust the flow rate based on the current and previous levels. The module will have an active-high synchronous reset to reset the state machine to its initial state. 
The logic can be divided into two main parts: 
1. Determining the nominal flow rate based on the current water level.
2. Adjusting the flow rate based on the previous and current water levels.
We will use a combination of combinational logic and sequential logic to implement this functionality.
