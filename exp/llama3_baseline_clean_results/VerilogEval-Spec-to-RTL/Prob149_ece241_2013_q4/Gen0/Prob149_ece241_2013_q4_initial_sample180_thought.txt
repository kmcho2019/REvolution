To solve this problem, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that can monitor the water level and control the flow rates accordingly. The FSM will have four states corresponding to the water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. We will also need to keep track of the previous sensor state to determine if the water level is increasing or decreasing. Based on the current and previous sensor states, the FSM will set the flow rate outputs. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM will reset to the state equivalent to the water level being low for a long time.
