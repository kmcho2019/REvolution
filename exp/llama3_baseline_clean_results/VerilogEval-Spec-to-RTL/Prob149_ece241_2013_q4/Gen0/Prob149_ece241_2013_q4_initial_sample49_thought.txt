To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) that monitors the water level sensors and adjusts the flow rate accordingly. The FSM will have four states based on the water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The flow rate will be determined by the current state and the previous state (to determine if the water level is rising or falling). We will use a synchronous reset to reset the FSM to the state equivalent to a low water level.
