The design involves creating a finite state machine (FSM) that responds to the water level sensors and controls the flow rates accordingly. The FSM will have four states, each corresponding to a specific water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The nominal flow rates and the supplemental flow valve will be controlled based on the current state and the previous state. The FSM will also have an active-high synchronous reset to reset the state machine to the state equivalent to a low water level.

To implement this, we can use a case statement to define the next state and output logic based on the current state and input sensors. We will also use a register to store the previous state of the sensors to determine if the water level is rising or falling.
