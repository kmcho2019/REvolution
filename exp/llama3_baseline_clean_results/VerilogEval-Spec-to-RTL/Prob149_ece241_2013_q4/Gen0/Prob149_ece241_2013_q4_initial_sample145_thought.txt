To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) that keeps track of the current water level and the previous level to determine the flow rate. We will have four states: above_s2, between_s2_s1, between_s1_s0, and below_s0. The FSM will transition between these states based on the sensor inputs and the previous level. We will also include an active-high synchronous reset that resets the FSM to the state equivalent to the water level being low for a long time.
