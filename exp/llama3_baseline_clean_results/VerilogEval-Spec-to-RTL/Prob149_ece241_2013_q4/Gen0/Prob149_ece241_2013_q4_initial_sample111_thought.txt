The TopModule can be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states representing the water levels: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. We will use a case statement to determine the next state based on the current state and the input sensor values. The outputs fr2, fr1, fr0, and dfr will be determined by the current state and the previous state to decide whether to open the Supplemental flow valve. The module will also have an active-high synchronous reset that resets the FSM to the state equivalent to the water level being low for a long time.
