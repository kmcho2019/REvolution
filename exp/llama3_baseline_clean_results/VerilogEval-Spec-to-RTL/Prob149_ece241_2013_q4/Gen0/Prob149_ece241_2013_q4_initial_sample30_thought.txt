The module has three input bits (s) from the sensors and a clock and reset input. Based on the sensor inputs, we need to generate the output flow rates. 

When the water level is above the highest sensor (s[2] is high), all output flow rates should be zero. When the water level is below the lowest sensor (s[0] is low and s[1] and s[2] are low), the output flow rates should be at their maximum.

For the levels between the sensors, the nominal flow rates are determined by the current water level. Additionally, if the previous water level was lower than the current level, the supplemental flow rate (dfr) should be increased.

To keep track of the previous water level, we need to use a flip-flop or a state machine. The state machine should be reset to a state equivalent to a low water level (all sensors low and all output flow rates high) when the reset input is asserted.
