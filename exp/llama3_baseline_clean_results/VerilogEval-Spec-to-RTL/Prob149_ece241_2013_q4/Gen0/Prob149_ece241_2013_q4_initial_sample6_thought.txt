To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) that takes into account the current water level and the previous level. The FSM will have four states corresponding to the four ranges of water levels: above s[2], between s[2] and s[1], between s[1] and s[0], and below s[0]. We will also use two registers to keep track of the current and previous water levels. Based on the current and previous levels, we will determine the nominal flow rate and whether to open the Supplemental flow valve. We will use an active-high synchronous reset to reset the state machine to the initial state.
