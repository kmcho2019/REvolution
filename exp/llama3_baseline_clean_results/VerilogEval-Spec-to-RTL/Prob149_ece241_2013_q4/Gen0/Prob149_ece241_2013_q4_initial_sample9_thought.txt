To implement the TopModule, we will use a simple state machine that tracks the current water level and the previous level. Based on the sensor inputs and the previous level, we will determine the nominal flow rate and whether to open the supplemental flow valve. We will also include a reset mechanism to reset the state machine to its initial state when the reset signal is high.

The state machine will have the following states:
- Idle: initial state
- Above_s2: water level above the highest sensor
- Between_s2_s1: water level between the highest and middle sensors
- Between_s1_s0: water level between the middle and lowest sensors
- Below_s0: water level below the lowest sensor

We will use the sensors to determine the current state and the previous state to determine the flow rate and supplemental flow valve.

We will also use an active-high synchronous reset to reset the state machine to its initial state.
