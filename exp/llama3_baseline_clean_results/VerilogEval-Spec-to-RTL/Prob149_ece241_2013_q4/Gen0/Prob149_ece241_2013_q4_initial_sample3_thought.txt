To implement the TopModule, I will use a finite state machine (FSM) approach to track the current water level and determine the flow rate. The FSM will have four states: ABOVE, BETWEEN_2_1, BETWEEN_1_0, and BELOW. The current state will be determined by the inputs from the three sensors (s[2], s[1], s[0]). Based on the current state and the previous state, I will determine the nominal flow rate and whether to open the Supplemental flow valve. The outputs (fr2, fr1, fr0, dfr) will be set accordingly. The FSM will also have a reset state that resets the state machine to the equivalent of the water level being low for a long time.
