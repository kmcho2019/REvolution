The problem can be solved by creating a finite state machine (FSM) that monitors the sensor inputs and determines the flow rate based on the current water level and previous level. The FSM should have four states corresponding to the water levels: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensor, between the middle and lowest sensor, and below the lowest sensor. The next state of the FSM will be determined by the current sensor inputs and the current state. The output flow rates will be determined by the current state and the previous state. The active-high synchronous reset will be used to reset the FSM to a state equivalent to a low water level.
