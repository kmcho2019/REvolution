To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the flow rates based on the sensor inputs and the previous sensor change. The FSM will have four states corresponding to the four water levels: above s[2], between s[2] and s[1], between s[1] and s[0], and below s[0]. We will use a separate register to track the previous sensor state to determine if the water level has increased or decreased. The nominal flow rates and supplemental flow valve will be determined based on the current state and previous sensor state.
