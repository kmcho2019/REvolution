To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) that takes the water level sensors as inputs and controls the flow rate outputs based on the current and previous water levels. The FSM will have four states corresponding to the water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The FSM will also track the previous water level to determine if the current level is higher or lower. The outputs will be determined by the current state and the previous water level. An active-high synchronous reset will be implemented to reset the FSM to a state equivalent to a low water level.
