To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) that tracks the current water level and the previous level. The FSM will have four states, each corresponding to a different water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The FSM will also keep track of the previous level to determine if the flow rate should be increased. The nominal flow rate will be determined by the current state, and the supplemental flow valve will be controlled based on the previous level. The active-high synchronous reset will reset the FSM to the state equivalent to the water level being low for a long time.
