The design of the TopModule involves a combination of combinational and sequential logic. The combinational part will handle the decoding of the water level sensors and determine the nominal flow rate. The sequential part, implemented as a state machine, will track the changes in the water level and determine whether to open the Supplemental flow valve. 

The state machine will have two states: 
- A "low" state, where the water level was previously below the current level, and the Supplemental flow valve should be opened.
- A "high" state, where the water level was previously above or at the same level as the current level, and the Supplemental flow valve should be closed.

The reset will force the state machine into the "low" state, with all flow rate outputs asserted.
