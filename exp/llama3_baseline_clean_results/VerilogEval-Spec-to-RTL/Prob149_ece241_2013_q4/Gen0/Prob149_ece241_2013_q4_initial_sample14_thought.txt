To implement the TopModule, we will use a simple state machine that determines the output flow rates based on the input water level sensors and the previous level. The state machine will have four possible states corresponding to the water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. We will use the current and previous water level to determine whether to open the supplemental flow valve. When the reset signal is asserted, the state machine will be reset to a state equivalent to a low water level, with all four outputs asserted.
