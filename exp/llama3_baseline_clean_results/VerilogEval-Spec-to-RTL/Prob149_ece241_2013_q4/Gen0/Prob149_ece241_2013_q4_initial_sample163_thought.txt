To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) that takes into account the current water level and the previous water level. The FSM will have four states: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The output flow rates will be determined based on the current state and the previous state. We will use the 's' input to determine the current state and a registered version of 's' to determine the previous state. The reset will be used to initialize the FSM to a state equivalent to the water level being below the lowest sensor.
