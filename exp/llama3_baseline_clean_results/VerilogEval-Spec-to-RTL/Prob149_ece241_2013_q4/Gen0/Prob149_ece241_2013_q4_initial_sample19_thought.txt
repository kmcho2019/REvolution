The design involves creating a state machine that determines the input flow rate based on the water level and the level previous to the last sensor change. The state machine will have four states corresponding to the water level: above the highest sensor, between the highest and middle sensors, between the middle and lowest sensors, and below the lowest sensor. The state machine will also track the previous state to determine if the level has increased or decreased. Based on the current state and the previous state, the flow rate will be adjusted by asserting or de-asserting the nominal flow rate inputs (fr2, fr1, fr0) and the supplemental flow valve (dfr). An active-high synchronous reset will be included to reset the state machine to a state equivalent to if the water level had been low for a long time.
