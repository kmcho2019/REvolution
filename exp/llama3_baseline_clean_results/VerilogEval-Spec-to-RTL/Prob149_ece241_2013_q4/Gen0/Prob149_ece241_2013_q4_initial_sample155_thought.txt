To solve this problem, I will design a state machine that interprets the sensor inputs and determines the flow rate accordingly. The state machine will have two main states: one to determine the flow rate based on the water level and another to determine if the supplemental flow valve should be opened. The state machine will also consider the previous sensor change to decide if the supplemental flow valve should be opened.

The state machine will be reset to a state where the water level is considered low (no sensors asserted) and all four outputs are asserted when the active-high synchronous reset signal is asserted.
