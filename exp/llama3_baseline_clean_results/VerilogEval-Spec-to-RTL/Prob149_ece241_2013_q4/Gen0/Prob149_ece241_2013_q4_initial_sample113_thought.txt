To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) that takes into account the current water level (based on the sensor inputs s[2:0]) and the previous state of the water level. The FSM will then determine the flow rates (fr2, fr1, fr0) and whether to open the Supplemental flow valve (dfr). The reset signal will be used to initialize the FSM to a state equivalent to the water level being low, with all sensors deasserted and all flow rates asserted. The FSM will have four states: above_s2, between_s2_s1, between_s1_s0, and below_s0. 
We'll also maintain a variable to track the previous water level to determine if the water level has increased or decreased.
