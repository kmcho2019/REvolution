To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) or the bitwise NOT operator (~) along with the logical AND operator (&). However, since we're dealing with a simple NOR operation between two inputs 'a' and 'b', we can directly use the logical NOT operator followed by the logical OR operator. The NOR operation can be expressed as ~(a | b). This means the output 'out' will be the opposite of the logical OR of 'a' and 'b'.
