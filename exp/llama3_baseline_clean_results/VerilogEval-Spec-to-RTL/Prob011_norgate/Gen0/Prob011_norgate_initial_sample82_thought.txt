To implement a NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator in Verilog, which is represented by the symbol ~ (bitwise NOT) followed by the & (bitwise AND) operator, or by using the ! (logical NOT) operator followed by the | (logical OR) operator. However, for a NOR gate, the most straightforward way is to use the logical NOT operator outside a logical OR operation, i.e., !(a | b). This will invert the result of the OR operation, effectively implementing a NOR gate.
