To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&) or use the logical NOT operator and the OR operator. However, the most straightforward way is to directly use the logical NOR operator. We will assign the output 'out' the result of the NOR operation between inputs 'a' and 'b'. This operation can be expressed as ~(a | b), where '|' is the OR operator and '~' is the NOT operator.
