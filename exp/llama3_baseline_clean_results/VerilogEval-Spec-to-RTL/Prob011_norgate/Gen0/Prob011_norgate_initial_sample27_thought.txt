To implement a NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator in Verilog, which is the "~" operator for bitwise NOT and the "&" operator for bitwise AND. The NOR operation can be achieved by performing a bitwise NOT operation on the result of a bitwise AND operation between the two inputs. However, Verilog also allows the use of the logical operators directly in expressions. The NOR gate's logic is: out = !(a | b), where "|" is the bitwise OR operator.
