To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&) or directly use the logical NOR operator (~) in a continuous assignment. However, since the NOR operation is essentially the inverse of the OR operation, we can simply use the logical OR operator (|) followed by a negation. The continuous assignment can directly express the NOR logic as an output being the inverse of either input being high.
