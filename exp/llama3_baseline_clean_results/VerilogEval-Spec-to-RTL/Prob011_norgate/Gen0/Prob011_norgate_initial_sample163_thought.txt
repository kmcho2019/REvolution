To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&). However, a more straightforward approach is to use the logical NOT operator (~) and the bitwise OR operator (|). The NOR operation can be expressed as ~(a | b), where 'a' and 'b' are the inputs.
