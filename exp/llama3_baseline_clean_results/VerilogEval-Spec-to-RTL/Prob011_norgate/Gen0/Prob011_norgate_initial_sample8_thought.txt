To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) which inverts the result of a logical operation, and the bitwise AND operator (&) or the logical AND operator (&&) to combine the inputs. However, for a NOR gate, we directly negate the OR operation. In Verilog, the logical NOT operator is ~, and the logical OR operator is |. The NOR operation can be represented as ~(a | b), which means the opposite of (a or b).
