To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we will use the logical NOR operator (~) to negate the OR operation of inputs a and b. This means we will first perform an OR operation between a and b, and then negate the result. In Verilog, the OR operation is represented by the "|" operator, and the negation is represented by the "~" operator. So, the NOR operation can be represented as ~(a | b).
