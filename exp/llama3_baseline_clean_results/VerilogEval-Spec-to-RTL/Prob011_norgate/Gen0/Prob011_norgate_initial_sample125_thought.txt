The output of a NOR gate is 1 only when both inputs are 0, and 0 otherwise. This logic can be expressed using the logical NOT operator (~) and the logical AND operator (&) in Verilog, where ~ (a | b) represents the NOR operation. The expression ~ (a | b) means "not (a or b)", which is equivalent to the definition of a NOR gate.
