To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) or the bitwise NOT operator (~) and the logical AND operator (&). However, the most straightforward way is to use the logical NOT operator and the OR operator (|). The NOR operation can be expressed as ~(a | b), which means "not (a or b)". This will give us the desired output for a NOR gate, where the output is 1 only if both inputs are 0.
