To implement a NOR gate in Verilog, we can directly use the logical NOR operator (~) to invert the inputs and the bitwise AND operator (&) to perform the AND operation. However, Verilog provides a more straightforward way to achieve this by using the logical NOT operator to invert the result of the OR operation between the two inputs. This can be represented as out = ~(a | b), where ~ denotes the NOT operation and | denotes the OR operation.
