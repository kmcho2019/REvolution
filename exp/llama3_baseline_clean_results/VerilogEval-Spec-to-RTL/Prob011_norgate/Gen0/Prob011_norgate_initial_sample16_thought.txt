However, a simpler approach would be to directly use the logical NOR operator (~) along with the bitwise OR operator (|) in a conditional statement or use the bitwise OR operator and then invert the result. Since we aim for a straightforward implementation, we'll use the latter method. The NOR operation between two inputs a and b can be achieved by performing an OR operation between them and then inverting the result.
