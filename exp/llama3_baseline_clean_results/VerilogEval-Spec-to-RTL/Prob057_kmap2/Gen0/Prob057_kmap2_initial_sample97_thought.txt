The TopModule will be implemented by simplifying the given Karnaugh map using Karnaugh map minimization techniques. 
After minimizing the Karnaugh map, the resulting equation will be used to implement the module. 
The equation is obtained by identifying the groups of 1s in the Karnaugh map and writing the corresponding product terms.
In this case, we identify the groups:
- A group of 4 1s in the first row (cd = 00, ab = 00, 01, 10), which simplifies to a'd'.
- A group of 2 1s in the second row (cd = 01, ab = 00), which simplifies to a'cd.
- A group of 2 1s in the second row (cd = 01, ab = 10), which simplifies to b'cd.
- A group of 2 1s in the third row (cd = 11, ab = 01, 11), which simplifies to bc.
- A group of 2 1s in the fourth row (cd = 10, ab = 00, 01), which simplifies to a'dc.
The final simplified equation is: out = a'd' + a'cd + b'cd + bc + a'dc.
This equation will be used to implement the TopModule.
