Looking at the map, we identify the 1s and the corresponding input combinations that produce these 1s, aiming to minimize the logic expression if possible. We then translate this expression into a Verilog module named TopModule with the specified interface. The module should have inputs a, b, c, and d and an output out. The logic for out will be based on the minimized expression derived from the Karnaugh map.
