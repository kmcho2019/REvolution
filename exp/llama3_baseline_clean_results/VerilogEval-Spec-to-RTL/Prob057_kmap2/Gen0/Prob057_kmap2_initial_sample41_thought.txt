To implement the circuit described by the Karnaugh map, we need to identify the minimal sum of products (SOP) form that represents the map. Looking at the map, we can see that the output 'out' is 1 for the following minterms: 
- when cd is 00 and ab is 00 or 01 or 10
- when cd is 01 and ab is 00 or 10
- when cd is 11 and ab is 01 or 11
This can be simplified to:
- when c is 0 and a is 0 or b is 0, 
- when d is 1, a is 0, and b is 0 or b is 1, 
- when c and d are 1 and a or b is 1.
We can use these minterms to write the Verilog code for the TopModule.
