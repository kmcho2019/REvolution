The Karnaugh map can be simplified by finding the largest possible groups of adjacent cells containing 1s. Looking at the map, we see that we can cover all the 1s with the following groups:
- A group of 4 cells at positions (00, 00), (00, 01), (00, 10), (10, 00) can be covered by the term "c'd" (since this covers all cases where c is low or d is low, regardless of the values of a and b).
- A group of 2 cells at positions (01, 01), (11, 01) can be covered by the term "b*c*d".
- A group of 2 cells at positions (11, 10), (11, 11) can be covered by the term "a*b*c".

The final equation is "out = c'd + b*c*d + a*b*c".
