The Karnaugh map can be simplified to obtain a minimized Boolean expression. 
By grouping adjacent 1s, we can derive the following simplified expression: 
out = (a'b' + a'c' + b'c'd' + ab'c + abc + ab'd + ac'd). 
However, this can be simplified even further to obtain a more minimal form.
Upon further simplification and grouping of adjacent 1s, 
the expression becomes out = (a'b' + b'c'd' + ac'd + a'c' + ab'c + abc + ab'd).
Using K-map minimization rules, we simplify this to out = (a'b' + a'c' + b'c'd + ab'c + ab'd + abc + ac'd).
After applying the rules of Boolean algebra, we get the minimal form: 
out = (a'b' + a'c' + b'c'd + ab'c + ab'd + abc).
