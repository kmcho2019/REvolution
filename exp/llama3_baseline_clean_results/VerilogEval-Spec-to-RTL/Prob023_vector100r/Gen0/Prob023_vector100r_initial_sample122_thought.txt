To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can assign each bit of the output to the corresponding bit of the input from the opposite end. This means the 0th bit of the output gets the 99th bit of the input, the 1st bit of the output gets the 98th bit of the input, and so on, until the 99th bit of the output gets the 0th bit of the input. This can be achieved using a simple assign statement in Verilog.
