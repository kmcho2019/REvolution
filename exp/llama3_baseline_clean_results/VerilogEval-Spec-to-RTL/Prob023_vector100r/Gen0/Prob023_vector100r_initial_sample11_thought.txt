To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can use a simple assignment statement where we assign each bit of the output to the corresponding bit of the input, but from the opposite end. This can be achieved using Verilog's bit indexing feature. For a 100-bit input, the most significant bit (MSB) will be at index 99 and the least significant bit (LSB) at index 0. By assigning the MSB of the input to the LSB of the output and vice versa, we effectively reverse the bit ordering.
