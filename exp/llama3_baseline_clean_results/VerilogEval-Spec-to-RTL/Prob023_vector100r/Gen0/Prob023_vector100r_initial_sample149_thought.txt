To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can use a simple indexing technique in Verilog. We will assign each bit of the output to the corresponding bit of the input but from the opposite end. This means the 0th bit of the input will be assigned to the 99th bit of the output, the 1st bit of the input to the 98th bit of the output, and so on, until the 99th bit of the input is assigned to the 0th bit of the output.
