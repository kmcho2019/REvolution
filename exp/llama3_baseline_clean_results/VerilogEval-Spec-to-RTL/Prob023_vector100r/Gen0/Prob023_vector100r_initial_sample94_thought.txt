To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can simply assign the bits of the output in reverse order. This means the 0th bit of the output will be the 99th bit of the input, the 1st bit of the output will be the 98th bit of the input, and so on, until the 99th bit of the output is the 0th bit of the input. This can be achieved by using a for loop to assign each bit individually.
