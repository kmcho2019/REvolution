To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we need to assign the most significant bit (MSB) of the input to the least significant bit (LSB) of the output, the second MSB of the input to the second LSB of the output, and so on, until we reach the LSB of the input, which should be assigned to the MSB of the output. This can be achieved by simply assigning the output bits to the corresponding input bits in reverse order.
