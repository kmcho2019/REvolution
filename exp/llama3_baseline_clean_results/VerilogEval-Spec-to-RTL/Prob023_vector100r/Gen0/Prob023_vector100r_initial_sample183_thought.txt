To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can assign each bit of the output to the corresponding bit of the input but in reverse order. This means that the 0th bit of the output will be assigned the 99th bit of the input, the 1st bit of the output will be assigned the 98th bit of the input, and so on, until the 99th bit of the output is assigned the 0th bit of the input. This can be achieved using a simple loop or by directly specifying the bit assignments in Verilog.
