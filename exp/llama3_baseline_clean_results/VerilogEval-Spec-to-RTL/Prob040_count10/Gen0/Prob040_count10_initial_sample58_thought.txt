To implement the decade counter in the TopModule, I will use a 4-bit register to store the current count. The counter will be incremented on every positive edge of the clock, and when it reaches 10 (1010 in binary), it will wrap around to 0. This can be achieved using a simple if-else statement in the always block. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 0 when it is high, and this will also be handled in the always block.
