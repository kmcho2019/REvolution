To implement the decade counter, I will use a sequential logic approach. The counter will be designed using a finite state machine with 10 states (0 to 9). The counter will increment by 1 on every positive edge of the clock until it reaches 9, then it will wrap around to 0. If the reset input is high during a clock cycle, the counter will reset to 0. I will use a 4-bit register to store the current count and a conditional statement to handle the increment and reset logic.
