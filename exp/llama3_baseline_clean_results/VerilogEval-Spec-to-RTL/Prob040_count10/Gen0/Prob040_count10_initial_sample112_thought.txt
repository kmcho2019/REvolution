The counter should count from 0 through 9 and then wrap around back to 0, with a counting period of 10 clock cycles. This can be achieved by using a 4-bit register to hold the current count and incrementing it on every positive clock edge until it reaches 9, at which point it resets to 0. The reset input will be used to synchronously reset the counter to 0.
