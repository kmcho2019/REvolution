To implement the decade counter, we can use a 4-bit counter (since 2^4 = 16 > 10) and utilize a comparator or conditional statement to reset the counter when it reaches 10. We will use a synchronous reset to ensure the counter resets to 0 when the reset input is high on a positive clock edge. We will also use non-blocking assignments to update the counter value on each clock cycle to avoid race conditions.
