To implement a decade counter that counts from 0 through 9 with a period of 10, we can use a 4-bit binary counter and add logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10 (which is 1010 in binary). The counter should be incremented on the positive edge of the clock, and the reset should also be synchronous, meaning it should only be applied on the positive edge of the clock when reset is high. This ensures that the counter is reset to 0 immediately when the reset signal is asserted and a clock edge occurs.
