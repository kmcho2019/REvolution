To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we can use a 4-bit counter (since 2^4 = 16, which is greater than the required 10 states) and add logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10. The counter will be driven by a clock and have a synchronous reset. We will use a combinational logic to check when the count has reached 10 and use this to determine when to reset the counter to 0.
