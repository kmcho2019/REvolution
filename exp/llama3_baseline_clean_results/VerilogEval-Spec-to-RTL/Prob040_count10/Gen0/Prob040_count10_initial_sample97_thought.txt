To implement a decade counter, we will use a 4-bit register to store the count value. On every positive edge of the clock, we will increment the count by 1. When the count reaches 10 (1010 in binary), we will reset it back to 0. The reset input is active high synchronous, meaning it will reset the counter to 0 only on the positive edge of the clock. We will use an if-else statement to check the reset condition and the count value to decide whether to increment or reset the count.
