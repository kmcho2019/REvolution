To implement the decade counter module TopModule, I will use a 4-bit binary counter to store the count. Since we want to count from 0 to 9 (inclusive) with a period of 10, we can use a simple binary counter and add logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10 (1010 in binary). The counter will be updated on the positive edge of the clock, and the reset input will be synchronous, meaning it will only reset the counter on the positive edge of the clock when reset is high.
