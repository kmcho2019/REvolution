To implement a decade counter that counts from 0 through 9 with a period of 10, we can use a 4-bit counter. We will utilize a binary counter and implement logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10 (1010 in binary). The counter will be designed as a sequential logic circuit, using the positive edge of the clock signal for state transitions. The reset signal will be synchronous, meaning it will be active only on the positive edge of the clock and will reset the counter to 0.
