To implement the decade counter, we will use a 4-bit counter and 
control its counting sequence to reset at 10 (1010 in binary). We 
can use a simple if-else statement in the always block to check 
if the counter has reached 9 (1001 in binary), and if so, reset it 
to 0 at the next clock cycle. However, to make the counter more 
straightforward, we will count from 0 to 9 and use the reset 
input to reset the counter to 0 when it's asserted. We'll also 
use a separate variable to track the current count and assign 
it to the output q.
