To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we can use a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 9 and then resets to 0. We will use a simple sequential logic approach with a reset mechanism. The counter will increment on every positive clock edge until it reaches 9, at which point it will reset to 0. The reset input is active high and synchronous, meaning it will only reset the counter on the positive edge of the clock when reset is high.
