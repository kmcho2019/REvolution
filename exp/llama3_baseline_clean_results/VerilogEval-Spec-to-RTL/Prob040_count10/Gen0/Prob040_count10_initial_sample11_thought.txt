To implement a decade counter, we will create a 4-bit counter (since 2^3 = 8 and 2^4 = 16, which is more than enough to represent numbers 0 through 9). The counter will increment at each positive clock edge. When the count reaches 10 (1010 in binary), it will reset to 0 at the next positive clock edge. The reset input is active high and synchronous, meaning it will reset the counter to 0 only at a positive clock edge when reset is high.
