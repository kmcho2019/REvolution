To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we need to design a sequential circuit that can count from 0 to 9 and then wrap around back to 0. We can achieve this by using a 4-bit register to store the count and incrementing it at every positive edge of the clock until it reaches 9, then reset it to 0. The reset input should be synchronous, meaning it only resets the counter on the positive edge of the clock when the reset signal is high.
