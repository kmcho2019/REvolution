The TopModule will contain a 4-bit shift register that can shift in data from the most significant bit when shift_ena is high. It will also act as a down counter when count_ena is high. Since both shift_ena and count_ena cannot be high at the same time, we can implement a simple if-else statement to check for these conditions. If shift_ena is high, the register will shift in the new data bit from the 'data' input. If count_ena is high, the current number in the shift register will be decremented. We will use a reg type to hold the 4-bit value and assign it to the output q.
