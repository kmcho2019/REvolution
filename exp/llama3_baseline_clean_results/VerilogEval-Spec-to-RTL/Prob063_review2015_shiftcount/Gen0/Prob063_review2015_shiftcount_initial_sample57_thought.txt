To implement the TopModule, we need to design a four-bit shift register that can shift in data most-significant-bit first and also decrement the number in the register when required. We will use a combination of if-else statements inside an always block to check the status of the control inputs shift_ena and count_ena, and then perform the corresponding operations on the shift register q. If shift_ena is 1, we will shift in the data into the most-significant-bit position of the register q. If count_ena is 1, we will decrement the number in the register q. We will also initialize the register q to 0 at the beginning.
