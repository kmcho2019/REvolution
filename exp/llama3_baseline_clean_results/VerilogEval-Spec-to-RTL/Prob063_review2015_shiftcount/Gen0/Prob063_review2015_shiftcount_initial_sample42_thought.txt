To implement the TopModule, we need to create a 4-bit register that can both shift in data and decrement its value. The module will have two main modes of operation based on the shift_ena and count_ena inputs. When shift_ena is high, the module will shift in the new data bit from the most significant bit. When count_ena is high, the module will decrement the current value in the register. We will use a combinational logic to determine whether to shift or decrement and then update the register on the positive edge of the clock. Since the problem states that shift_ena and count_ena will not be used together, we don't need to worry about prioritizing one over the other.
