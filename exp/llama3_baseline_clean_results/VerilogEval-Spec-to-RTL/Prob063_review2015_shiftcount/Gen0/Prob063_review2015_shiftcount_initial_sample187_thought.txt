To implement the TopModule, we can use a combination of sequential logic (a flip-flop for each bit of the register) and combinatorial logic to handle the shifting and counting operations. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). When shift_ena is 1, the register will shift the bits to the right and the most significant bit will be set to the input data. When count_ena is 1, the register will decrement its value. Since the two enable signals are mutually exclusive, we don't need to handle the case where both are 1.
