To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current value of the shift register/counter. We will use two always blocks: one to handle the shifting operation when shift_ena is 1, and another to handle the decrement operation when count_ena is 1. Both operations will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. Since shift_ena and count_ena are mutually exclusive, we don't need to worry about prioritizing one over the other.
