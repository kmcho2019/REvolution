The TopModule can be designed as a 4-bit register that performs two distinct operations based on the control inputs shift_ena and count_ena. When shift_ena is 1, the register shifts in the new data bit from the most significant bit (MSB) position, effectively making it a shift register. When count_ena is 1, the register decrements its current value by 1, turning it into a down counter. Since these two operations are mutually exclusive in the full system, the module can simply prioritize one operation over the other without affecting the overall functionality. For simplicity, we can use a single 4-bit register and update it based on the control signals.
