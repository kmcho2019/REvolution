To implement the TopModule, we can use a 4-bit register to store the current value. We will use two separate processes: one for shifting the data into the register when shift_ena is 1, and another for decrementing the value in the register when count_ena is 1. Both processes will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. Since the full system doesn't use shift_ena and count_ena together, we can implement the logic to prioritize either shifting or counting, without worrying about the behavior when both are 1.
