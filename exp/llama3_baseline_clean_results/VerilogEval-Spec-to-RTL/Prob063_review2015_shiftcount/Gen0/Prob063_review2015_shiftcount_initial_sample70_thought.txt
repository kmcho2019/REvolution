To implement the TopModule, we'll use a 4-bit shift register and incorporate the functionality of a down counter. We'll utilize two separate processes: one for shifting data when shift_ena is high, and another for decrementing the count when count_ena is high. Since both control inputs will not be used simultaneously, we'll prioritize one over the other in the case where both are high. For simplicity, we'll prioritize the shift operation. The 4-bit output q will be driven by the current state of the shift register.
