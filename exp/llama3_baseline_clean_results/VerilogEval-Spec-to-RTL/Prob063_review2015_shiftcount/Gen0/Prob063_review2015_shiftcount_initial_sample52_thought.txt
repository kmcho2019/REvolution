The TopModule should be implemented as a sequential logic circuit with a 4-bit register. 
The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock. 
When shift_ena is 1, the register should shift in the new data bit from the most significant bit, 
effectively implementing a shift register. 
When count_ena is 1, the register should decrement the current value, 
implementing a down counter. 
Since the system doesn't use shift_ena and count_ena together, 
we can simply use a priority encoder or an if-else statement to decide 
which operation to perform.
