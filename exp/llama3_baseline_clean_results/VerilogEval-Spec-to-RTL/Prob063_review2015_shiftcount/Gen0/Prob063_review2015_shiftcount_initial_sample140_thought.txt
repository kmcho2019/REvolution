To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current value of the shift register/counter. We will have two main operations: shifting and counting. Shifting will be done when shift_ena is 1, where the data bit will be shifted into the most significant bit of the register, and the rest of the bits will be shifted one position to the right. Counting will be done when count_ena is 1, where the value in the register will be decremented by 1. We will use a if-else statement to check the control inputs and perform the corresponding operation.
