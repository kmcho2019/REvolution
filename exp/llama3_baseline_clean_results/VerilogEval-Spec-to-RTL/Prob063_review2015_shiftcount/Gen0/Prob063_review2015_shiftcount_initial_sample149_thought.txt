The TopModule will have a 4-bit register to hold the current state of the shift register and counter. 
When shift_ena is 1, the module will shift the data into the most-significant-bit of the register on the positive edge of the clock.
When count_ena is 1, the module will decrement the value in the register on the positive edge of the clock.
The output q will always reflect the current state of the register.
To implement this functionality, we will use a combination of the following:
- A 4-bit reg to hold the state of the shift register and counter.
- A conditional statement to determine whether to shift in data or decrement the count based on the shift_ena and count_ena inputs.
- A positive edge triggered clock to update the state of the register.
