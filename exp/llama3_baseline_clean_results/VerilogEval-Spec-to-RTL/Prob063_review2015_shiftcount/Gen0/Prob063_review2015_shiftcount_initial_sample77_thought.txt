To implement the TopModule, we need to design a four-bit shift register that also acts as a down counter. The module should have two separate modes of operation based on the shift_ena and count_ena signals. When shift_ena is 1, the module should shift in the data bit into the most-significant-bit of the register. When count_ena is 1, the module should decrement the number in the shift register. We can use a combination of if-else statements and assignment operators to achieve this functionality. The shift register will be implemented using a reg array, and the data will be shifted in on the positive edge of the clock.
