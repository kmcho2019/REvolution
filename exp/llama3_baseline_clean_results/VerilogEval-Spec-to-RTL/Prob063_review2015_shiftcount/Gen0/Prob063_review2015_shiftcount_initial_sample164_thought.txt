To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current value. We will use two separate processes to handle the shift and decrement operations. When shift_ena is 1, we will shift the data into the register from the most significant bit. When count_ena is 1, we will decrement the value in the register. Since both operations are triggered by the positive edge of the clock, we will use a synchronous design with a single clock domain.
