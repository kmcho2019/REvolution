To implement the TopModule, we will create a four-bit shift register with an additional functionality of a down counter. We will use a 4-bit vector to represent the shift register. When shift_ena is high, we will shift the data into the most significant bit of the register and shift the existing bits one position to the right. When count_ena is high, we will decrement the value in the register. Since both shift_ena and count_ena cannot be high at the same time, we can use a simple if-else statement to prioritize one over the other. We will use a reg to store the current state of the register and update it at every positive edge of the clock.
