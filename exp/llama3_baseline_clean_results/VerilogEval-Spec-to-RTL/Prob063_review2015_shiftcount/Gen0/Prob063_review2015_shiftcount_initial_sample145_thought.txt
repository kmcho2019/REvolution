To implement the TopModule, we can use a single 4-bit register to store the data. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock. When shift_ena is 1, we shift the data in the register one bit to the right and fill the most significant bit with the new data. When count_ena is 1, we decrement the number in the register. Since the system doesn't use shift_ena and count_ena together, we can use a simple if-else statement to prioritize one over the other. In this case, we will prioritize shift_ena over count_ena.
