To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) in Verilog, which returns 1 only if both inputs are 0. The Verilog expression for a 2-input NOR gate would be ~(in1 | in2), where ~ denotes the NOT operation and | denotes the OR operation. This expression will produce the desired output when both in1 and in2 are 0, and will be 0 when either or both of the inputs are 1.
