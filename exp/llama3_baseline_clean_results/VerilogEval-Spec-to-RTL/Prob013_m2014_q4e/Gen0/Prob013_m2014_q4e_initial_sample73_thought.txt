To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we need to understand the logic of the NOR operation. The NOR gate produces an output of 1 only when both inputs are 0. In all other cases (i.e., at least one input is 1), the output is 0. This can be implemented using a simple logical expression where the output is the inverse of the OR operation between the two inputs. In Verilog, this can be represented using the logical NOT operator (~) and the OR operator (|).
