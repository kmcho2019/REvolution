To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we need to understand that the output of a NOR gate is 1 only when all inputs are 0. Therefore, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) in Verilog to implement this functionality. The expression for a 2-input NOR gate can be written as ~(in1 | in2), where in1 and in2 are the input ports and | is the bitwise OR operator.
