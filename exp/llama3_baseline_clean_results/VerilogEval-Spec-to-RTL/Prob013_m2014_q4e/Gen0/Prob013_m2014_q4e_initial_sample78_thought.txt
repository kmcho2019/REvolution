To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOT and OR operators. The NOR gate produces an output of 1 only if both inputs are 0. This can be achieved by inverting the OR operation of the two inputs. In Verilog, we can use the `~` operator for inversion and the `|` operator for OR. The expression for the NOR gate can be written as `~(in1 | in2)`, where `in1` and `in2` are the inputs.
