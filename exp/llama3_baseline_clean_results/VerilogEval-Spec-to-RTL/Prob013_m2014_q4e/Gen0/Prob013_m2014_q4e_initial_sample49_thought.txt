To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and bitwise AND (&) or the logical AND operator (&) and logical NOT operator (!) in Verilog. However, since Verilog does not directly support a NOR operator for single bits, we will use the latter method. The output of a NOR gate is 1 only when both inputs are 0. This can be expressed as out = !(in1 | in2), which is equivalent to out = !in1 & !in2.
