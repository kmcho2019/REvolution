To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we will use the logical NOR operator (~) which inverts the bits, and the bitwise AND operator (&) to combine the inverted inputs. The NOR gate produces an output of 1 only if both inputs are 0. So, the expression for a 2-input NOR gate would be ~(in1 | in2), where | is the bitwise OR operator.
