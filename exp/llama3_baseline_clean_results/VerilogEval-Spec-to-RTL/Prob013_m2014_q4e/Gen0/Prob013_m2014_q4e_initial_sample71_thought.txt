To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we will use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&) or the logical AND operator (&&) to invert the result of the OR operation. However, since Verilog has a direct logical OR operator (|) and logical NOT operator (!), we can directly implement the NOR function using these operators.
