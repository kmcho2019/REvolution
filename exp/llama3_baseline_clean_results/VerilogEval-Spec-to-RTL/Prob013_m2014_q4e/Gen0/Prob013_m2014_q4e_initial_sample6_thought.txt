To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) or the bitwise NOR operator. However, since we are working with single-bit inputs and outputs, the logical NOR operator (~) is more suitable. The NOR operation is equivalent to an OR operation followed by an inversion. Therefore, we can use the OR operator (|) followed by the NOT operator (~) to achieve the NOR operation.
