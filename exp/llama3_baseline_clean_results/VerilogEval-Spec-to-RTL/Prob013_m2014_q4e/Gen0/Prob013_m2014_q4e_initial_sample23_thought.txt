To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator in Verilog. The NOR operation produces an output of 1 only if all inputs are 0. We can use the ~ (bitwise NOT) operator to invert the result of the OR operation (~) between the two inputs. The Verilog module will take two inputs, in1 and in2, and produce an output, out, which will be the result of the NOR operation.
