To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&) or simply use the logical OR operator (|) and then invert the result. Since NOR is the inverse of OR, we can calculate the OR of the two inputs and then invert the result to get the NOR operation. This can be achieved using a simple module with a single line of combinational logic.
