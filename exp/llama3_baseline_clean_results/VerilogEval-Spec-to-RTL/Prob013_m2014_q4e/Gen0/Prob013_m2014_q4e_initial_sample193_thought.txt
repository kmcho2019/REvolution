To implement a 2-input NOR gate, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) in Verilog. The NOR gate produces an output of 1 only if all inputs are 0. We will assign the output 'out' to the result of the NOR operation between 'in1' and 'in2'. This can be expressed as out = ~(in1 | in2), where '~' is the bitwise NOT operator and '|' is the bitwise OR operator.
