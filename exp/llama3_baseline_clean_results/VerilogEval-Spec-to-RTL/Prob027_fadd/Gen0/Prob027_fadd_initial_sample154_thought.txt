To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to understand the logic of a full adder. A full adder takes three bits as inputs (two operands and a carry-in) and produces a sum and a carry-out. The sum is calculated by performing a bitwise XOR operation on all three inputs, while the carry-out is generated when at least two of the inputs are 1. This can be achieved using a combination of logical operators in Verilog. Specifically, we can use the XOR operator (^) to calculate the sum and a combination of AND and OR operators to calculate the carry-out.
