To implement a full adder, we can use a combination of half adders and a simple logical operation to produce the sum and carry-out. A full adder adds three bits (a, b, and cin) and generates two outputs: sum and cout. We can create the sum by XORing the inputs (a ^ b ^ cin), and the carry-out can be generated by checking if any two of the inputs are high (a & b) or if one of the inputs and the carry-in are high ((a ^ b) & cin).
