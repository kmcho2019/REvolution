To implement a full adder, we need to follow the basic logic of addition, considering the carry-in (cin) and the two input bits (a and b). The sum and carry-out can be calculated using the following logic:
- The sum (sum) is the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, and cin).
- The carry-out (cout) is generated if at least two of the inputs (a, b, or cin) are 1.
This can be achieved using basic logic gates such as XOR and AND, combined with an OR operation to handle the carry-out generation.
