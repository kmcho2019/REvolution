To implement a full adder, we need to follow the logic of addition in binary arithmetic. The sum of two bits (a and b) and a carry-in (cin) bit will produce a sum (sum) and a carry-out (cout). We can use logical operations to define the sum and carry-out based on the inputs. The sum will be the XOR of a, b, and cin, because the sum is 1 only when there's an odd number of 1s among the inputs. The carry-out will be 1 when at least two of the inputs are 1, which can be implemented using the OR of the AND operations between different pairs of inputs.
