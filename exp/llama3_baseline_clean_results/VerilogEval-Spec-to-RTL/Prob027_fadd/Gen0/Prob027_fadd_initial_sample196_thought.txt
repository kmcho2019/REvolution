To implement a full adder, we need to produce a sum and carry-out based on the inputs a, b, cin. The sum is the result of the bitwise XOR operation between a, b, and cin. The carry-out is generated when at least two of the inputs are 1. This can be achieved using the logical AND operation. 
We will use two levels of logic: 
- First, compute the carry-out (cout) by checking for all combinations where two or more inputs are 1.
- Second, compute the sum by performing a bitwise XOR operation between a, b, and cin.
