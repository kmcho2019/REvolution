To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to design a module that takes three one-bit inputs (a, b, and cin) and produces two one-bit outputs (sum and cout). The sum and carry-out can be calculated using the following logic equations: 
sum = a XOR b XOR cin 
cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin)
We will use these equations to assign values to sum and cout in our Verilog module.
