To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use the standard logical equations for sum and carry-out. The sum (S) of a full adder is given by S = a XOR b XOR cin, and the carry-out (Cout) is given by Cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin). We can directly translate these equations into Verilog code using the XOR (^), AND (&), and OR (|) operators.
