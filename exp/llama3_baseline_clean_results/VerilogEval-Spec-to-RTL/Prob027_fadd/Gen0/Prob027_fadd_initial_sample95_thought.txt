To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to define a module that takes in three one-bit inputs (a, b, and cin) and produces two one-bit outputs (cout and sum). We can use basic logical operators to calculate the sum and carry-out. The sum is calculated as the XOR of a, b, and cin, while the carry-out is calculated as the OR of the AND of a and b, the AND of a and cin, and the AND of b and cin.
