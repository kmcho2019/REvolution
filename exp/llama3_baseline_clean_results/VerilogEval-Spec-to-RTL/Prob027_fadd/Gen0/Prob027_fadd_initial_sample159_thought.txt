To implement a full adder, we need to design a module that takes three inputs (a, b, and cin) and produces two outputs (sum and cout). The sum output will be the XOR of the three inputs (a, b, and cin), while the carry output will be 1 if at least two of the inputs are 1. This can be achieved using logical operations in Verilog. We will use the XOR operator (^) to calculate the sum and a combination of AND and OR operators to calculate the carry.
