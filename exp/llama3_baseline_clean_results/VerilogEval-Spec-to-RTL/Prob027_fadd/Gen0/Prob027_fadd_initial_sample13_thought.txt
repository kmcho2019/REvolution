To implement a full adder, we need to consider the inputs a, b, and cin. The sum output is calculated by performing an XOR operation between a, b, and cin. The carry-out output is generated when at least two of the inputs (a, b, cin) are 1. This can be achieved using a combination of AND and OR operations. Specifically, we can use the following logic equations: sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin).
