To implement a full adder, we can use basic digital logic principles. The sum (sum) is determined by the XOR operation of the inputs a, b, and the carry-in (cin). The carry-out (cout) is generated if at least two of the inputs are high (1), which can be achieved using a combination of AND operations. 
The sum can be represented as: sum = a ^ b ^ cin
The carry-out can be represented as: cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin)
These equations directly translate into the necessary logic gates to form the full adder.
