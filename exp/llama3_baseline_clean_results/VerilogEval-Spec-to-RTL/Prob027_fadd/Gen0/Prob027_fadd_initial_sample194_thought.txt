To implement a full adder in Verilog, we will use the basic logic equations for the sum and carry-out of a full adder. The sum (S) is calculated as S = a XOR b XOR cin, and the carry-out (Cout) is calculated as Cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin). We will write a Verilog module that takes in the inputs a, b, and cin, and outputs the sum and carry-out.
