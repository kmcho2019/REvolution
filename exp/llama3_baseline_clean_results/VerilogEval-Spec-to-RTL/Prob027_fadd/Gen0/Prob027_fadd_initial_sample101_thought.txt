To implement a full adder, we can use the standard logic equations for sum and carry-out. 
The sum (sum) is calculated as a XOR b XOR cin, because in a full adder, the sum is 1 only when either one or all three of the inputs are 1.
The carry-out (cout) is calculated as (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin), because the carry-out is 1 when at least two of the inputs are 1.
We will use these equations to write the Verilog code for the TopModule.
