To implement a full adder, we need to follow the logic of how bits are added in binary arithmetic, taking into account the carry-in and producing a sum and carry-out. The sum (sum) can be calculated by XORing all three inputs (a, b, cin), because XOR (^) in binary adds two bits without considering the carry. The carry-out (cout) can be calculated by checking if at least two of the inputs are 1, which would cause a carry in binary addition. This can be achieved using OR and AND gates. Specifically, the carry-out will be 1 if (a and b) or (a and cin) or (b and cin) are true.
