To implement a full adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out bits based on the input bits a, b, and carry-in (cin). We can achieve this by using two half-adders. The first half-adder will add a and b to produce a partial sum and a partial carry-out. Then, we can add the partial sum and cin using another half-adder to produce the final sum and carry-out. However, a simpler and more efficient approach in Verilog is to directly implement the full adder's logic equations: sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin).
