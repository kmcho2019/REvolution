To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to define the module TopModule with the given interface. The full adder's sum (sum) can be calculated as the XOR of the inputs a, b, and cin. The carry-out (cout) can be calculated as the OR of the AND operations between (a, b), (a, cin), and (b, cin). This is because a carry is generated when at least two of the inputs are 1.
