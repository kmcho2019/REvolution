To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use basic logical operations. The sum and carry-out of a full adder can be calculated using the following logical equations:
- sum = a ^ b ^ cin (where ^ denotes the bitwise XOR operation)
- cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin) (where & denotes the bitwise AND operation and | denotes the bitwise OR operation)
