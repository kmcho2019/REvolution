To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use basic logical operations. A full adder takes three inputs: two bits to be added (a and b) and a carry-in bit (cin). It produces two outputs: the sum of the three bits and a carry-out bit (cout). We can express the sum and carry-out using the following logical equations:
- sum = a XOR b XOR cin
- cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin)
These equations directly follow from the definition of how a full adder operates.
