To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use the following logic equations:
- The sum (sum) is equal to the XOR of a, b, and cin.
- The carry-out (cout) is equal to 1 when at least two of the inputs (a, b, cin) are 1.
This can be implemented using a combination of XOR and AND gates, or using a single expression for the sum and a separate expression for the carry-out.
