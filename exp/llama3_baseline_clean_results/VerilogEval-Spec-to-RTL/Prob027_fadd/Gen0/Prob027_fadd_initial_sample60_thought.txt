The sum output is determined by the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, and cin), because in binary addition, a sum of 1 can only be produced by an odd number of 1s among the inputs. The carry-out is generated when at least two of the inputs are 1, which can be implemented using the OR of the AND operations between each pair of inputs.
