To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to follow the truth table of a full adder. The full adder has two main outputs: sum and carry-out. The sum output is calculated as the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, and cin), and the carry-out is generated when at least two of the inputs are high (1). This can be achieved by using the XOR operator (^) for the sum and a combination of AND and OR operators for the carry-out.
