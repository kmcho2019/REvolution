To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can directly use the standard logic equations for a full adder. The sum (S) and carry-out (Cout) of a full adder are given by the following equations:
- Sum = A XOR B XOR Cin
- Cout = (A AND B) OR (A AND Cin) OR (B AND Cin)
These equations capture the behavior of a full adder, which adds three bits (A, B, and Cin) and produces a sum and a carry-out. We can directly translate these equations into Verilog code.
