To implement a full adder, we can use the basic equations of digital arithmetic. The sum of the full adder is calculated as (a XOR b XOR cin), where 'a', 'b', and 'cin' are the input bits. The carry-out of the full adder can be calculated using the equation (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin). We can directly implement these equations in Verilog to create the TopModule.
