To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to create a module that takes in three one-bit inputs (a, b, and cin) and produces two outputs: a one-bit sum and a one-bit carry-out (cout). The full adder's logic can be expressed using basic logical operations: the sum is the XOR of the three inputs (a ^ b ^ cin), and the carry-out is 1 if at least two of the inputs are 1, which can be achieved by using the logical OR operation after applying AND between pairs of inputs (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin).
