To implement a full adder, we can use basic logic gates. A full adder adds three bits (a, b, and cin) and produces a sum and carry-out. The sum (sum) can be calculated as the XOR of a, b, and cin. The carry-out (cout) can be calculated as the OR of the AND of (a and b), (a and cin), and (b and cin). This is because the carry-out is produced when at least two of the inputs (a, b, cin) are 1.
