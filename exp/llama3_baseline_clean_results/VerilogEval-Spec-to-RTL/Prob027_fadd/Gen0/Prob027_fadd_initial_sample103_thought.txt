To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to design a module that takes three input bits (a, b, and cin) and produces two output bits (sum and cout). The sum and carry-out can be calculated using the following logic equations: 
sum = a XOR b XOR cin 
cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin). 
These equations ensure that the module correctly adds the three input bits and produces the sum and carry-out.
