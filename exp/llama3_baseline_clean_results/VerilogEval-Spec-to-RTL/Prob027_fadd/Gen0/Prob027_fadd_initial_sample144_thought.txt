To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use basic logic gates to calculate the sum and carry-out. The sum (S) is calculated as S = A XOR B XOR Cin, and the carry-out (Cout) is calculated as Cout = (A AND B) OR (A AND Cin) OR (B AND Cin). This logic is based on the principles of digital arithmetic and the truth table of a full adder.
