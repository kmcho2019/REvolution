To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use the basic logic of addition for the sum and carry-out. The sum (S) of the full adder can be determined by the XOR operation of the inputs (a, b, cin) where S = a XOR b XOR cin. The carry-out (cout) can be generated using the logic that a carry is produced if at least two of the inputs are 1. This can be achieved with the expression (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin). We'll write the Verilog module to encapsulate this logic.
