To implement a full adder in Verilog, we will use a combination of logic gates to achieve the desired functionality. The full adder will have three inputs (a, b, and cin) and two outputs (sum and cout). We can express the sum and carry-out using the following logical equations:
- sum = a ^ b ^ cin (where ^ denotes the XOR operation)
- cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin) (where & denotes the AND operation and | denotes the OR operation)
These equations can be directly translated into Verilog code.
