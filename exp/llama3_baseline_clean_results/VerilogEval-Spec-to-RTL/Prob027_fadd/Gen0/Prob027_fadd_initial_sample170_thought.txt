To implement a full adder, we need to follow the standard binary addition rules, taking into account the carry-in (cin) bit. The sum and carry-out can be calculated using the following logic:
- The sum bit is 1 when either one or all three of the input bits (a, b, cin) are 1, but not when exactly two of them are 1.
- The carry-out bit is 1 when at least two of the input bits (a, b, cin) are 1.
This logic can be expressed using bitwise XOR and AND operations for the sum and carry-out, respectively, considering the combinations of input bits.
