To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to design a module that takes three one-bit inputs (a, b, and cin) and produces two one-bit outputs (sum and cout). The sum output will be the bitwise XOR of the three inputs, and the cout output will be 1 if at least two of the inputs are 1. We can use the XOR operator (^) to calculate the sum and a combination of AND operators (&) to calculate the cout.
