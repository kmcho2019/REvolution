To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to understand the logic of a full adder. A full adder takes three bits as input (a, b, and carry-in) and produces two outputs (sum and carry-out). The sum is the XOR of all three inputs, and the carry-out is 1 if at least two of the inputs are 1. We can use the XOR operator (^) and logical operators to implement this functionality.
