To implement a full adder, we can use the standard logic equations for sum and carry-out. The sum is calculated as the XOR of all three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out is calculated as the OR of the AND of any two inputs. This means we need to implement two main equations: sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin). These equations can be directly translated into Verilog using the corresponding operators.
