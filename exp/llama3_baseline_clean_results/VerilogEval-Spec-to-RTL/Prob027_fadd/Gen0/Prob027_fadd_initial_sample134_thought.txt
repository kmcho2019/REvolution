To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to describe the logic that calculates the sum and carry-out from three input bits (a, b, and cin). The sum (sum) is calculated by performing a bitwise XOR operation between the results of the XOR operation of a and b and cin. The carry-out (cout) can be generated by checking the conditions where the carry is propagated (i.e., when at least two of the inputs a, b, and cin are 1). This can be achieved using logical AND and OR operations.
