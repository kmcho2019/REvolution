To implement a full adder in Verilog, we can use basic logical operations. The sum output in a full adder is calculated as the XOR of all three input bits (a, b, and cin), while the carry-out (cout) is generated when at least two of the input bits are 1. This can be achieved by using the XOR operator (^) for the sum and the majority function (i.e., a AND b OR a AND cin OR b AND cin) for the carry-out.
