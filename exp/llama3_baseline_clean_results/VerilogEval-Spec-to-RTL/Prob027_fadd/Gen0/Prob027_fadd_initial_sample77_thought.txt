To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to define the logic for calculating the sum and carry-out based on the inputs a, b, and cin. The sum (sum) can be calculated using the XOR operation of a, b, and cin. The carry-out (cout) can be calculated using the logic that it is 1 when at least two of the inputs a, b, and cin are 1. This can be achieved by using the logical OR operation of the AND operations between different pairs of the inputs.
