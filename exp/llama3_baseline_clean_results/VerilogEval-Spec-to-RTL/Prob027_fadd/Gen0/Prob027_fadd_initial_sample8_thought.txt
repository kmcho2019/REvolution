To implement a full adder, we need to add three input bits: a, b, and cin. The sum and carry-out can be calculated using bitwise operations. The sum bit is the XOR of a, b, and cin. The carry-out bit is set when at least two of the input bits are 1. We can use the following logic equations: 
sum = a ^ b ^ cin
cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin)
