To implement a full adder in Verilog, we will create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. The full adder logic can be broken down into two parts: calculating the sum and calculating the carry-out. The sum (sum) is calculated as the XOR of the three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out (cout) is set if at least two of the inputs are 1. This can be achieved using logical operators in Verilog.
