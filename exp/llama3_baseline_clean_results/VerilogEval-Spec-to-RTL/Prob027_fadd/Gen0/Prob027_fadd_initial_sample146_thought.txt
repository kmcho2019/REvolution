To implement a full adder, we can use a combination of two half adders or directly use Boolean algebra to derive the sum and carry-out equations. Here, we'll directly use Boolean algebra for simplicity. The sum (S) and carry-out (Cout) for a full adder can be represented as follows:
- Sum (S) = a XOR b XOR cin
- Carry-out (Cout) = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin)
We will implement these equations in Verilog to create the TopModule.
