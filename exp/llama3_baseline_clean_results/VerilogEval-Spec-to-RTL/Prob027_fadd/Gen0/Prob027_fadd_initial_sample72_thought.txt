To implement a full adder in Verilog, we need to create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. The module will have three input ports (a, b, cin) and two output ports (cout, sum). We will use bitwise operations to calculate the sum and carry-out. The sum can be calculated as the XOR of a, b, and cin. The carry-out can be calculated as the OR of (a AND b), (a AND cin), and (b AND cin).
