To implement the 4-bit binary counter, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current count. We will use a clock signal (clk) to trigger the counting process on the positive edge, and a reset signal (reset) to reset the counter to 0. The counter will increment by 1 on each clock cycle, wrapping around to 0 after reaching the maximum count of 15. The current count will be output on the q port.
