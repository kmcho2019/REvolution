To implement the TopModule, we will use Verilog's built-in logical operators to create a 100-input AND gate, OR gate, and XOR gate. Since Verilog does not directly support 100-input gates, we can use a simple iterative approach for the AND and OR operations, and a more complex iterative approach for the XOR operation due to its nature of requiring an odd number of 1s to produce a 1. This can be achieved by applying the corresponding logical operation sequentially across all 100 bits of the input.
