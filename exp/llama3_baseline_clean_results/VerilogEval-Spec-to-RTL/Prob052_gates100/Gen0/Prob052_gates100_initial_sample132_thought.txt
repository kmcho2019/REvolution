To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in Verilog operators for AND, OR, and XOR operations. We will use the bitwise AND (&), OR (|), and XOR (^) operators in conjunction with a reduction operator to simplify the 100-bit input operations. The reduction operators for AND (&), OR (|), and XOR (^) will be used. These operators apply the respective operation to all bits of the input.
