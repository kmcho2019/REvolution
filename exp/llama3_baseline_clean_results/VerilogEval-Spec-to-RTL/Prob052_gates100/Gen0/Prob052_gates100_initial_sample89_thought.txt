To implement the TopModule, we can use a combination of Verilog's built-in reduction operators. The reduction operators (&, |, and ^) can be used to perform bitwise operations on a vector of bits. Specifically, we will use these operators to implement the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gates. We will assign the output of each reduction operation to the corresponding output port (out_and, out_or, out_xor).
