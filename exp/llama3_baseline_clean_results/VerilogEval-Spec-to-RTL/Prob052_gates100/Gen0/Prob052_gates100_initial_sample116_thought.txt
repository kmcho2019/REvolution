To implement the TopModule, we will use built-in Verilog operators for bitwise operations: 
- The AND operator (&) for the out_and output.
- The OR operator (|) for the out_or output.
- The XOR operator (^) for the out_xor output. However, we must reduce the 100-bit input to a single bit using the reduction operator (~^ for XOR, ~& for AND, ~| for OR).
