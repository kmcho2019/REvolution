To implement the TopModule, we can directly instantiate 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gates in Verilog. However, since Verilog does not support gates with 100 inputs, we will use loops to iteratively apply the AND, OR, and XOR operations to all 100 inputs. We start with a default value for each operation: out_and starts as 1 (since AND of no inputs is 1), out_or starts as 0 (since OR of no inputs is 0), and out_xor starts as 0 (since XOR of an even number of 0s is 0). Then, we iterate over the inputs, updating out_and, out_or, and out_xor accordingly.
