To implement the TopModule, we can utilize Verilog's built-in reduction operators for AND, OR, and XOR operations. These operators can be applied to a vector (such as our 100-bit input) and will perform the respective operation on all bits of the vector, producing a single output. The reduction operators are: 
- & (AND)
- | (OR)
- ^ (XOR)

Therefore, we can directly apply these operators to the input vector `in` to obtain the required outputs `out_and`, `out_or`, and `out_xor`.
