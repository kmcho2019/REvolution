To implement the TopModule, we can use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's value. 
The areset signal will be used to reset the register to zero asynchronously. 
The load and ena signals will be used to control the operation of the shift register on the positive edge of the clock. 
If the load signal is high, the register will be loaded with the data input. 
If the ena signal is high and the load signal is low, the register will be shifted right, with q[3] becoming zero and q[0] being shifted out. 
If both load and ena are high, load will take priority.
