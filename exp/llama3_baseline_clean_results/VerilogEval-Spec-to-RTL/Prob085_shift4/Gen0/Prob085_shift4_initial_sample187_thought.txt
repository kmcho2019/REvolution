- If load is not asserted, we'll then check the enable (ena) signal. If ena is high, we'll perform the right shift operation. This involves shifting all bits one position to the right and setting the most significant bit (q[3]) to zero. - To ensure that the load operation has higher priority than the shift operation when both are asserted, we'll check the load condition before the ena condition in our `always` block.
