To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the different conditions that control the behavior of the 4-bit shift register. We will use a reset signal (areset) to reset the register to zero asynchronously. For the synchronous operations, we will use the load signal to load the data into the register and the enable signal (ena) to shift the register to the right. If both load and ena are asserted, load will have higher priority. We will use an if-else statement to prioritize the load operation over the shift operation. The sequential logic will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock (clk).
