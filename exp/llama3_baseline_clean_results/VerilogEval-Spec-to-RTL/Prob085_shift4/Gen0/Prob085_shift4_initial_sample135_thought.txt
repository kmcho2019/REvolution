To implement the TopModule, we can use a 4-bit register and modify its behavior based on the input signals. 
The module will have an asynchronous reset (areset) to clear the register to zero.
The load signal will have higher priority than the enable signal (ena), so when both are asserted, the register will load the data instead of shifting.
When the enable signal (ena) is asserted and load is not, the register will shift right, with the most significant bit (q[3]) becoming zero and the least significant bit (q[0]) being shifted out and lost.
