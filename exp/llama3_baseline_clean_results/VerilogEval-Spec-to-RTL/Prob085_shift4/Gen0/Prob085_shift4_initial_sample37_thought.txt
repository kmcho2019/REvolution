To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. The module will have three main operations: reset, load, and shift. The reset operation will be triggered by the areset signal and will reset the shift register to zero. The load operation will be triggered by the load signal and will load the shift register with the data input. The shift operation will be triggered by the ena signal and will shift the contents of the shift register to the right. The load operation will have higher priority than the shift operation. All operations will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, except for the reset operation which will be asynchronous.
