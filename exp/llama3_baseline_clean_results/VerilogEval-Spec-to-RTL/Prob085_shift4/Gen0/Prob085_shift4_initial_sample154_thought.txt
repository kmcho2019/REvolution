To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, I will use a sequential logic design that responds to the asynchronous reset, load, and enable signals. The module will be designed as a 4-bit shift register with a right shift operation. The load signal will have higher priority over the enable signal. When the load signal is asserted, the shift register will load the data from the input port instead of shifting. If the enable signal is asserted, the shift register will perform a right shift operation, shifting out the least significant bit and shifting in a zero at the most significant bit position. The asynchronous reset signal will reset the shift register to zero at any time.
