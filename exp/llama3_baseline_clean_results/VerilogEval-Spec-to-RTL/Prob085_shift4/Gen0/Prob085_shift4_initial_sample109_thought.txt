To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and enable signals. We will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's contents. On every positive clock edge, we will check the load and ena signals. If the load signal is high, we will load the register with the data input. If the load signal is low but the ena signal is high, we will perform a right shift operation on the register. If both load and ena signals are high, we will prioritize the load operation. If the areset signal is high at any time, the register will be reset to zero.
