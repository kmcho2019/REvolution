The TopModule will be implemented using a 4-bit register with asynchronous reset and synchronous load and enable signals. 
The load signal will have higher priority than the enable signal, so when both are high, the register will load the data instead of shifting.
The enable signal will shift the register to the right, moving the least significant bit out and bringing in a zero as the most significant bit.
All sequential logic will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
