The module TopModule will be implemented as a 4-bit shift register. The module will have an asynchronous reset (areset) to reset the shift register to zero. The module will also have two synchronous controls: load and ena. If load is high, the shift register will load the data[3:0] instead of shifting. If ena is high and load is low, the shift register will shift right, with q[3] becoming zero and q[0] being shifted out. If both load and ena are high, load will have higher priority. All sequential logic will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock (clk).
