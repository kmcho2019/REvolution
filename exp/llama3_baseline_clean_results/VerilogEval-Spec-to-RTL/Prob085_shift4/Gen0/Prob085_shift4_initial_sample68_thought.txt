To implement the TopModule, we can use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. The register will be reset to zero when the areset signal is asserted. On the positive edge of the clock, we will check the load and ena signals. If the load signal is asserted, we will load the register with the data input. If the load signal is not asserted and the ena signal is asserted, we will shift the register to the right. If neither load nor ena signals are asserted, the register will retain its current state.
