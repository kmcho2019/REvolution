The TopModule will have a 4-bit shift register. It will be reset to zero when areset is asserted. The load input will have higher priority than the ena input. If load is asserted, the shift register will be loaded with the data[3:0]. If load is not asserted but ena is, the shift register will shift right. If neither load nor ena is asserted, the shift register will hold its current value. All operations are synchronous to the positive edge of the clock, except for the reset which is asynchronous.
