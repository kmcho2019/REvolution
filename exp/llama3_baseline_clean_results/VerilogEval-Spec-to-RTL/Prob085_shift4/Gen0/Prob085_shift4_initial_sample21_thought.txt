To implement the TopModule as described, we will use a 4-bit register with synchronous reset, load, and enable signals. The reset will be asynchronous, meaning it will reset the register immediately when asserted, regardless of the clock. The load and enable signals will be synchronous, meaning their actions will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. The load signal will have higher priority over the enable signal, so when both are asserted, the load operation will occur instead of shifting. We'll use a 4-bit vector to represent the internal state of the shift register.
