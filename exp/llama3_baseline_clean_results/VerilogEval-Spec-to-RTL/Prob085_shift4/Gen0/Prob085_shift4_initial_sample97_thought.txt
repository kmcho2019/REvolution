To implement the TopModule with a 4-bit shift register, we will first reset the shift register to zero when the asynchronous positive edge triggered areset is asserted. Then, we will use the synchronous signals load and ena to control the shift register's behavior. When load is asserted, we will load the data into the shift register. If load and ena are both asserted, load will take priority. When ena is asserted and load is not, we will shift the contents of the shift register to the right, making q[3] zero and shifting out q[0].
