To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the asynchronous reset and the synchronous load and enable signals. The module will have a 4-bit shift register that can be reset, loaded with new data, or shifted right based on the input signals. We will use a case statement to handle the different combinations of load and ena signals. If both load and ena are asserted, load will have higher priority. We will also ensure that the shift register is properly reset on the positive edge of the areset signal.
