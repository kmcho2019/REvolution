To implement the TopModule as specified, we'll use a 4-bit register and control the load, shift, and reset operations based on the provided inputs. The areset input will reset the register asynchronously, meaning as soon as areset is asserted, the register will be reset to zero regardless of the clock. The load and ena inputs will be evaluated synchronously on the positive edge of the clock, with load taking priority over ena when both are asserted. When load is asserted, the register will be loaded with the data input. When ena is asserted and load is not, the register will perform a right shift operation, with the most significant bit (q[3]) becoming zero and the least significant bit being shifted out and lost.
