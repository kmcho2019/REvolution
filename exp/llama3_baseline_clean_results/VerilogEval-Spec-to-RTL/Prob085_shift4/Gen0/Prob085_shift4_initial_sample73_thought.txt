To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. 
The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock (clk). 
When areset is asserted, the register will be reset to zero asynchronously. 
When load is asserted, the register will be loaded with the data[3:0] on the next clock edge. 
When ena is asserted, the register will shift right by one bit, with q[3] becoming zero and q[0] being shifted out. 
If both load and ena are asserted, the load operation will take priority.
