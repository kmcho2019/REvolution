The TopModule will be implemented using a 4-bit register. The register will be reset to zero when areset is high, regardless of the clock. When the clock rises and areset is low, the register will check the load and ena signals. If load is high, the register will be loaded with the data input. If load is low and ena is high, the register will shift right, with q[3] becoming zero and q[0] being shifted out. If both load and ena are low, the register will hold its current value.
