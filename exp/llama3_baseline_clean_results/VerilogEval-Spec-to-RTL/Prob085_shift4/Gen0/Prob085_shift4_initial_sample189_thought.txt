To implement the TopModule, I will use a sequential logic circuit with an asynchronous reset. The circuit will have a 4-bit register to store the shift register contents. On every positive edge of the clock, I will check the areset signal. If it is asserted, the register will be reset to zero. If not, I will check the load and ena signals. If load is asserted, the register will be loaded with the data input. If load is not asserted but ena is, the register will be shifted to the right, with the most significant bit (q[3]) becoming zero and the least significant bit (q[0]) being shifted out. If neither load nor ena is asserted, the register will retain its current value.
