To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register contents. 
The module will have an asynchronous reset (areset) to clear the register to zero. 
The load signal will have higher priority over the ena (enable) signal, so when both are asserted, 
the register will be loaded with the input data instead of shifting. 
When the ena signal is asserted and load is not, the register will shift right, 
losing the least significant bit and setting the most significant bit to zero. 
All operations will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal.
