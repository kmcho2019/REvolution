To implement the 4-bit shift register as described, we will use a register to store the current state of the shift register. The module will have an asynchronous reset (areset) that clears the register to zero immediately when asserted. 

Synchronously, on the positive edge of the clock (clk), we will check the load and ena signals. If load is high, we will load the register with the input data. If load is low but ena is high, we will perform a right shift operation on the register, shifting the bits to the right and filling the most significant bit (MSB) with zero. 

We will ensure that the load operation has higher priority than the shift operation by checking the load signal before the ena signal in the sequential logic.
