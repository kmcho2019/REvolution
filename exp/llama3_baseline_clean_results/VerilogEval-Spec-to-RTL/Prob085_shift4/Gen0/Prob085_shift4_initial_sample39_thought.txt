To implement the TopModule, I will use a combination of sequential logic and combinational logic. The sequential logic will be used to create a 4-bit shift register with asynchronous reset. On every positive edge of the clock, I will check the load and ena signals. If load is high, I will load the data into the shift register. If load is low and ena is high, I will shift the contents of the shift register to the right. If both load and ena are low, the shift register will hold its current value. I will also handle the asynchronous reset (areset) by resetting the shift register to zero whenever areset is high.
