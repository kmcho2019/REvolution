To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will design a 4-bit shift register that shifts right based on the enable signal (ena) and loads data from the input data bus when the load signal is asserted. The module will also have an asynchronous reset (areset) to reset the shift register to zero. We will prioritize the load operation over the shift operation if both load and ena are asserted. We will use a reg to hold the state of the shift register and update it on the positive edge of the clock (clk) based on the current values of load, ena, and areset.
