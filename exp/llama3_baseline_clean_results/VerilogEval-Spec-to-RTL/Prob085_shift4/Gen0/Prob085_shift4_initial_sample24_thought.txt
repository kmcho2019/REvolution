To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and enable signals. We will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register contents. On the positive edge of the clock, we will check the load and ena signals. If the load signal is high, we will load the data into the register. If the load signal is low and the ena signal is high, we will shift the register to the right. If both load and ena signals are low, the register will retain its current value. The areset signal will reset the register to zero asynchronously.
