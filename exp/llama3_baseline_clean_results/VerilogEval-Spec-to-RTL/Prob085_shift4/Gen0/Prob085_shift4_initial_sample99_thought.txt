The TopModule will have a 4-bit shift register that can be reset asynchronously, loaded synchronously, or shifted right synchronously. To implement this, we will use a 4-bit reg to store the state of the shift register, and use always blocks to handle the sequential logic. The areset will reset the shift register asynchronously to zero. The load and ena signals will be used in an always block that triggers on the positive edge of the clock to update the shift register state accordingly.
