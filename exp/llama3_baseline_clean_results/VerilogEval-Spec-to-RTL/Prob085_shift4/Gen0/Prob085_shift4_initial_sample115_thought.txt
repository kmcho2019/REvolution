To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and synchronous enable. We will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's value. The reset will be asynchronous, setting the register to zero when asserted. The load and enable signals will be checked on the positive edge of the clock. If load is asserted, we will load the register with the data input. If ena is asserted and load is not, we will shift the register to the right, with the most significant bit (q[3]) becoming zero and the least significant bit (q[0]) being shifted out. If both load and ena are asserted, load will take priority.
