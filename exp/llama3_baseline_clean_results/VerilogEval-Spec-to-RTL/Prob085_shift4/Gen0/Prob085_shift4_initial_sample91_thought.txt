To implement the TopModule, I will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's contents. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock. The asynchronous reset (areset) will be used to reset the register to zero. The load signal will have higher priority than the enable signal, so when both are asserted, the register will be loaded with the data instead of shifting. The enable signal will shift the register to the right, with the most significant bit (q[3]) becoming zero, and the least significant bit (q[0]) being shifted out and disappearing.
