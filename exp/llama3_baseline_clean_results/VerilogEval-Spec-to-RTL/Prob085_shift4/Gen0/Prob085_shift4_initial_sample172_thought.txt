To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. The module will have an asynchronous reset (areset) to reset the register to zero when asserted. On the positive edge of the clock (clk), we will implement the load and enable (ena) logic. If the load signal is asserted, we will load the data into the register. If the load signal is not asserted but the enable signal is, we will shift the register to the right, filling the most significant bit with zero and discarding the least significant bit.
