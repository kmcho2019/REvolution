To implement the TopModule, I will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register value. 
The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will have the following behavior:
- If areset is asserted, the register will be reset to zero regardless of the clock.
- If the clock edge is detected and areset is not asserted, the register will be updated based on the load and ena signals.
- If load is asserted, the register will be loaded with the data input.
- If load is not asserted and ena is asserted, the register will be shifted right.
- If neither load nor ena is asserted, the register will maintain its current value.
