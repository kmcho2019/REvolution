To implement the TopModule, we need to design a 4-bit shift register that can load data, shift data to the right, and reset asynchronously. The load and enable signals are synchronous and are only considered on the positive edge of the clock. If both load and enable are asserted, the load operation takes priority. The asynchronous reset (areset) resets the shift register to zero regardless of the clock. We will use a register to store the current state of the shift register and update it based on the load, enable, and reset signals.
