To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current state of the shift register. The module will have the following behavior:
- On the positive edge of areset, the shift register will be reset to zero asynchronously.
- On the positive edge of the clock, if load is high, the shift register will be loaded with the data input.
- If load is low and ena is high, the shift register will shift right, with q[3] becoming zero and q[0] being shifted out.
- If both load and ena are high, the load input will have higher priority, and the shift register will be loaded with the data input.
We will use a non-blocking assignment to update the register on the positive edge of the clock.
