The TopModule will be implemented as a 4-bit shift register with asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and enable. The module will use a reg to store the shift register contents. On every positive edge of the clock, the module will check the load and ena signals. If the load signal is high, the data will be loaded into the shift register, regardless of the ena signal. If the load signal is low and the ena signal is high, the shift register will be shifted to the right, with the most significant bit being set to zero and the least significant bit being shifted out. If both the load and ena signals are low, the shift register will retain its current value. The areset signal will reset the shift register to zero, regardless of the clock.
