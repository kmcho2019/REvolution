To implement the TopModule as per the given specifications, we need to consider the asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and enable signals. The module will be designed using a combination of always blocks and conditional statements to handle the different input signals. The areset signal will be handled outside the always block to ensure it resets the shift register to zero asynchronously. The load and enable signals will be handled inside the always block, ensuring they are triggered only on the positive edge of the clock. We will use non-blocking assignments to update the shift register on each clock cycle.
