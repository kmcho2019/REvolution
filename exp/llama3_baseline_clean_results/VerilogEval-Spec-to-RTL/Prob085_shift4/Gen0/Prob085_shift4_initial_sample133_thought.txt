To design the TopModule, we need to implement a 4-bit shift register that can be reset asynchronously, loaded synchronously, and shifted right synchronously. The shift register's output will be the 4-bit vector q. The asynchronous reset will be triggered by the positive edge of areset. The synchronous load and enable signals will be triggered by the positive edge of clk. When both load and ena are high, load will have higher priority. We will use a reg declaration to define the internal state of the shift register and an always block to define the sequential behavior.
