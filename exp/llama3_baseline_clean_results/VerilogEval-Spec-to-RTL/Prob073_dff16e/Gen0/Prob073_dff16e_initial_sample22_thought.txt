To implement the TopModule, we can use 16 D flip-flops to store the data. 
We will use the byteena inputs to control whether each byte of the 16 registers 
should be written to on that cycle. We will use the resetn input as a synchronous, 
active-low reset. This means that when resetn is low, the registers will be reset 
to zero on the next positive edge of the clock. When resetn is high, the registers 
will either be updated with the new data or retain their current value, depending 
on the byteena inputs.
