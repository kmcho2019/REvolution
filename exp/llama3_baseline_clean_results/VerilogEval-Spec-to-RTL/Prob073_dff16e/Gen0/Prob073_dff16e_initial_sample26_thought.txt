To implement the TopModule, we will use 16 D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. The byteena signal will control whether each byte of the 16 registers should be written to on that cycle. We will use the byteena[1] signal to control the upper byte (d[15:8]) and the byteena[0] signal to control the lower byte (d[7:0]). The resetn signal is a synchronous, active-low reset, meaning that when it is low, all the flip-flops will reset to zero on the next positive edge of clk. We will use a conditional statement to control the loading of the data into the flip-flops based on the byteena signals and the resetn signal.
