To implement the TopModule, I will first declare the module with its interface and then define 16 D flip-flops to store the data. The byte-enable inputs (byteena) will be used to control whether each byte of the 16 registers should be written to on that cycle. I will use the byteena signals to enable the writing of the corresponding bytes of the data input (d) to the flip-flops. The resetn signal will be used to reset the flip-flops synchronously. All DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk).
