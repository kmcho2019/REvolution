The module TopModule will be designed with 16 D flip-flops, divided into two bytes (upper and lower). 
Each byte can be selectively updated based on the byteena inputs. The update operation will be synchronous, 
triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. When resetn is low, all flip-flops will be reset to 0. 
This can be achieved by using two separate conditional statements for each byte, checking the corresponding byteena bit.
