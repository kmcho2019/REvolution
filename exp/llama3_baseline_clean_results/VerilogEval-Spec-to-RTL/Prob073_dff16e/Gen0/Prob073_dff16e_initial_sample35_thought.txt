To implement the TopModule, I will use 16 D flip-flops, divided into two bytes of 8 bits each. The byteena inputs will be used to control the write enable of each byte. When byteena[1] is high, the upper byte (d[15:8]) will be written to the corresponding flip-flops, and when byteena[0] is high, the lower byte (d[7:0]) will be written. The resetn input will be used to synchronously reset all flip-flops on the positive edge of the clock when it is low.
