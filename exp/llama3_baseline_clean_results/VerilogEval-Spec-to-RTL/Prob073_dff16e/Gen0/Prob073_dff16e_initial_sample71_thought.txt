To implement the TopModule, I will use a combination of 16 D flip-flops (DFFs) and logic to control the loading of data into the DFFs based on the byte-enable inputs. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will have a synchronous reset (resetn). The byte-enable inputs (byteena) will determine which bytes of the DFFs are loaded with the input data (d) on each cycle.
