The TopModule can be implemented using 16 D flip-flops, where the data is loaded into the flip-flops based on the byte enable signals and the reset condition. Each byte of the input data `d` is controlled by a separate byte enable signal `byteena[1]` for the upper byte and `byteena[0]` for the lower byte. The reset is synchronous and active-low, meaning the flip-flops will be reset to 0 when `resetn` is 0 at the positive edge of `clk`. The flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of `clk`.
