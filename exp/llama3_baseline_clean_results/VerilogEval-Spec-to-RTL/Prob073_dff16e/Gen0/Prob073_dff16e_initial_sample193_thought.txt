To implement the TopModule, we will use 16 D flip-flops (DFFs) and utilize the byteena signals to control which bytes of the input data d are written to the flip-flops. We will use a synchronous, active-low reset to reset all DFFs. We will also use the positive edge of the clk to trigger the DFFs. The byteena[1] will control the upper byte (d[15:8]) and byteena[0] will control the lower byte (d[7:0]) of the input data.
