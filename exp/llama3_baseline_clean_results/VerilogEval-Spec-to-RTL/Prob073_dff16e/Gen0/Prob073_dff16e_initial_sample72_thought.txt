To implement the TopModule, we need to create a module that includes 16 D flip-flops and controls their behavior based on the byte-enable inputs. The byte-enable inputs will allow us to selectively write to either the upper byte or the lower byte of the 16-bit register. We will use the synchronous reset to reset all the flip-flops to 0 when the resetn signal is low. We will trigger the flip-flops on the positive edge of the clk signal.
