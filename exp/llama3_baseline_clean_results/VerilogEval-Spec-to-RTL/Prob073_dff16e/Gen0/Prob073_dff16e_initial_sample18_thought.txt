The TopModule should contain 16 D flip-flops that are triggered by the positive edge of clk. 
The module should also include logic for handling synchronous reset, byte enable, and writing to the D flip-flops. 
We can use the byteena inputs to control whether each byte of the D flip-flops should be written to on that cycle.
We will use two separate 8-bit registers to store the upper and lower bytes, each controlled by a byteena signal.
