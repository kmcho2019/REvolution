The TopModule will contain 16 D flip-flops, organized into two bytes of 8 bits each. 
The byte-enable inputs, byteena[1] and byteena[0], control whether the upper and lower bytes are written to, respectively.
Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and will have a synchronous, active-low reset.
To implement this, we can use Verilog's built-in always block to create the D flip-flops and control their behavior based on the inputs.
