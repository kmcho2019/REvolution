To implement the TopModule, I will design a module with the specified interface that includes 16 D flip-flops. The byte-enable inputs will be used to control whether each byte of the 16 registers should be written to on that cycle. I will use two separate blocks of 8 D flip-flops to represent the upper and lower bytes. The resetn signal will be used to asynchronously reset the flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal.
