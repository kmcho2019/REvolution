To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of if-else statements and bitwise operations to control the byte-enable inputs. We will also utilize the D flip-flop (DFF) to store the input data. The module will include 16 DFFs, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). The byte-enable inputs (byteena) will determine whether to update the upper or lower byte of the output (q) with the corresponding byte of the input data (d).
