To implement the TopModule, we can use a combination of conditional statements and bitwise operations to control the loading of data into the D flip-flops. The module will have 16 D flip-flops, divided into two bytes of 8 bits each. The byteena input will determine whether to load the upper byte, the lower byte, or both.
