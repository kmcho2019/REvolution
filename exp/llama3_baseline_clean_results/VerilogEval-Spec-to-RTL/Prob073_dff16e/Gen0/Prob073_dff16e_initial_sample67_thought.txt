To implement the TopModule, we will first declare the module with the given interface. Then, we will use two conditional statements to determine whether to load new data into the flip-flops based on the byteena signals. We will use two 8-bit vectors to represent the upper and lower bytes of the input data and the output register. The module will be reset synchronously when resetn is low. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal.
