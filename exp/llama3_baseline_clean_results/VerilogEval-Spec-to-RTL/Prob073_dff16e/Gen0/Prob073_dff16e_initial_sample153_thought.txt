To implement the TopModule, we will use 16 D flip-flops, divided into two bytes. We will utilize the byteena signals to control whether each byte is written to or not. On the positive edge of the clock, if the corresponding byteena signal is high, we will load the input data into the flip-flops. If resetn is low, we will reset all the flip-flops. We will use a synchronous reset to ensure that the reset is triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
