To implement the TopModule, we will create a 16-bit register with 2-byte enable controls. The register will be split into two 8-bit parts, each controlled by a separate byte-enable signal (byteena[1] for the upper byte and byteena[0] for the lower byte). We will use a synchronous reset (resetn) to clear all 16 bits when resetn is low. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk).
