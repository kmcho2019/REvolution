To implement the TopModule, we can utilize the synchronous reset and the byteena inputs to control the loading of data into the 16 D flip-flops. We can divide the 16 D flip-flops into two bytes, where byteena[1] controls the upper byte and byteena[0] controls the lower byte. On the positive edge of the clock, if the corresponding byteena bit is high and resetn is high, the data from the input d is loaded into the respective D flip-flops. If resetn is low, the D flip-flops are reset to zero.
