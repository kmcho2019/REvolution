To implement the TopModule, we will use 16 D flip-flops. The D flip-flops will be controlled by the byteena inputs, which will determine whether each byte of the 16 registers should be written to on that cycle. We will use a case statement to handle the different byteena combinations. The resetn input will be used to reset the flip-flops synchronously. All DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of clk.
