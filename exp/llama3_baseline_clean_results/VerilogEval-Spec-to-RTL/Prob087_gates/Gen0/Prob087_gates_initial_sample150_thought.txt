To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators in Verilog. The module will have two input ports, a and b, and seven output ports, each corresponding to a different logical operation between a and b. We will directly assign the results of the logical operations to the output ports. The logical operators we will use are: 
- `&` for AND
- `|` for OR
- `^` for XOR
- `~` for NOT
- The absence of an operator for NAND and NOR will be handled using the logical equivalences: `~(a & b)` for NAND and `~(a | b)` for NOR
- `~(a ^ b)` will be used for XNOR
- `a & ~b` will be used for a and-not b
