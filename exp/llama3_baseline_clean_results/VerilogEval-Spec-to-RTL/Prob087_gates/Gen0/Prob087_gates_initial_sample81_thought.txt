The TopModule can be implemented using basic logical operators in Verilog. We will directly assign the results of the logical operations to the corresponding output ports. The 'and', 'or', 'xor', 'nand', 'nor', 'xnor', and 'not' operators in Verilog will be used for implementing the respective logic gates. For 'nand', 'nor', 'xnor', we will utilize the fact that ~(a & b) is equivalent to a nand b, ~(a | b) is equivalent to a nor b, and ~(a ^ b) is equivalent to a xnor b. Also, 'a and ~b' will be used for the 'a and-not b' operation.
