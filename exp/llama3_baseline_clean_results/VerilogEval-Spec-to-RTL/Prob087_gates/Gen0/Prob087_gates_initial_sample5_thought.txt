To implement the TopModule, we can directly instantiate the required logic gates in the module. 
We can use the built-in Verilog operators to generate the outputs: 
- out_and: a && b (AND gate)
- out_or: a || b (OR gate)
- out_xor: a ^ b (XOR gate)
- out_nand: ~(a && b) (NAND gate, which is the negation of an AND gate)
- out_nor: ~(a || b) (NOR gate, which is the negation of an OR gate)
- out_xnor: ~(a ^ b) (XNOR gate, which is the negation of an XOR gate)
- out_anotb: a && ~b (AND gate with one input inverted)
