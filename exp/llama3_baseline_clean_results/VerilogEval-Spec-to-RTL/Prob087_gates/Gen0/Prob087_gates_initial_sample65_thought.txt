To implement the TopModule, we will directly instantiate the basic logic gates for each output. The AND gate will drive out_and, the OR gate will drive out_or, the XOR gate will drive out_xor, the NAND gate will drive out_nand, the NOR gate will drive out_nor, the XNOR gate will drive out_xnor, and the AND-NOT gate will drive out_anotb. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for these gates: && (AND), || (OR), ^ (XOR), ~(a && b) (NAND), ~(a || b) (NOR), ~(a ^ b) (XNOR), and a && ~b (AND-NOT).
