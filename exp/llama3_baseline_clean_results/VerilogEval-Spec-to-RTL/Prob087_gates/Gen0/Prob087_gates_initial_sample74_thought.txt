To implement the TopModule, we will use the basic logical operators provided by Verilog. We will assign the result of each logical operation directly to the corresponding output. For example, the out_and output will be assigned the result of 'a and b'. Similarly, out_or will be assigned 'a or b', out_xor will be assigned 'a ^ b', out_nand will be assigned '~(a & b)', out_nor will be assigned '~(a | b)', out_xnor will be assigned '~(a ^ b)', and out_anotb will be assigned 'a & ~b'.
