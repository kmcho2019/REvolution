The TopModule can be implemented using basic logical operators in Verilog. 
We will use the built-in operators for AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, and XNOR. 
For the NAND, NOR, and XNOR operations, we will utilize the fact that 
- NAND is equivalent to NOT (AND)
- NOR is equivalent to NOT (OR)
- XNOR is equivalent to NOT (XOR) or (NOT (a XOR b))
The 'out_anotb' will be implemented as 'a AND (NOT b)'.
