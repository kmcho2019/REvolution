To implement the TopModule, we can use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to directly assign the results of the logical operations to the output ports. We will use the following operators:
- `&` for bitwise AND
- `|` for bitwise OR
- `^` for bitwise XOR
- `~` for bitwise NOT
- We can derive NAND, NOR, and XNOR operations by combining the above operators.
- For the `out_anotb` port, we need to perform a logical AND operation between `a` and the inverse of `b`.
