To implement the TopModule, we will use the standard logical operators provided by Verilog. The module will have two input ports 'a' and 'b', and seven output ports, each driven by a different logical operation between 'a' and 'b'. The operations include AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, and AND with NOT. We will use the corresponding Verilog operators (& for AND, | for OR, ^ for XOR, ~ for NOT) to implement these operations.
