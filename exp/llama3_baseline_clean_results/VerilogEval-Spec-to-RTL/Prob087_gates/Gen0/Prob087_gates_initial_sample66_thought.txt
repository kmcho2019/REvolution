The module TopModule will be implemented with a single always block to handle the combinational logic. Within this block, we'll define each output based on the corresponding logic operation between inputs a and b. 
- out_and will be assigned the result of the logical AND operation between a and b.
- out_or will be assigned the result of the logical OR operation between a and b.
- out_xor will be assigned the result of the exclusive OR operation between a and b.
- out_nand will be assigned the result of the logical NAND operation between a and b, which is equivalent to the inverse of the AND operation.
- out_nor will be assigned the result of the logical NOR operation between a and b, which is equivalent to the inverse of the OR operation.
- out_xnor will be assigned the result of the exclusive NOR operation between a and b, which is equivalent to the inverse of the XOR operation.
- out_anotb will be assigned the result of a AND (NOT b), combining the AND operation with the inversion of b.
We'll use the logical operators in Verilog, such as && (AND), || (OR), ^ (XOR), ~ (NOT), to express these operations.
