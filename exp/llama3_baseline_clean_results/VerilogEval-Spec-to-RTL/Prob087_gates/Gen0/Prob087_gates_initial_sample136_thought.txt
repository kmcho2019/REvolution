To implement the TopModule, we can directly use the logical operators provided by Verilog. The module will have two input ports 'a' and 'b', and seven output ports corresponding to each logical operation. We can use the following operators in Verilog:
- AND: &
- OR: |
- XOR: ^
- NAND: ~(a & b)
- NOR: ~(a | b)
- XNOR: ~(a ^ b)
- AND-NOT: a & ~b
We will assign the result of each operation to the corresponding output port.
