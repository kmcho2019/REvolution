To implement the TopModule, we will define the module with the given interface and then use Verilog's logical operators to compute the output of each gate. We will use the following operators:
- `&` for AND
- `|` for OR
- `^` for XOR
- `~` for NOT
- `~(a & b)` for NAND
- `~(a | b)` for NOR
- `~(a ^ b)` for XNOR
- `a & ~b` for A and not B
