To implement the TopModule, we can directly use the corresponding logical operators provided by Verilog for each output port. This includes using the bitwise operators for AND (&), OR (|), XOR (^), and the logical NOT operator (!) for the negation of inputs. For NAND, NOR, and XNOR, we'll use their definitions in terms of AND, OR, and XOR along with negation. The "and-not" operation can be implemented using the AND operator along with the negation of one of the inputs.
