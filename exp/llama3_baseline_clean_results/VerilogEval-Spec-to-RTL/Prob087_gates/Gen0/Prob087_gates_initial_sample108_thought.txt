To implement the TopModule, we will create a Verilog module with the specified input and output ports. We will then use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to generate the output signals. The logical operators we will use are: 
- `&` for AND
- `|` for OR
- `^` for XOR
- `~` for NOT
- `~(a & b)` for NAND
- `~(a | b)` for NOR
- `~(a ^ b)` for XNOR
- `a & ~b` for A and not B
