To implement the TopModule, we will use the basic logical operators provided by Verilog. We can directly implement the required logic gates using these operators. The logical operators in Verilog are:
- AND: &
- OR: |
- XOR: ^
- NOT: ~
- NAND: ~(a & b)
- NOR: ~(a | b)
- XNOR: ~(a ^ b)
- AND-NOT: a & ~b
We will use these operators to assign the values to the output ports based on the inputs a and b.
