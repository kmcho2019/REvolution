To implement the TopModule, we can directly use the built-in logical operators in Verilog. 
We will assign the output of each logic gate to the corresponding output port. 
For instance, out_and will be assigned the result of a logical AND operation between a and b. 
Similarly, out_or, out_xor, out_nand, out_nor, out_xnor, and out_anotb will be assigned 
the results of the corresponding logical operations between a and b.
