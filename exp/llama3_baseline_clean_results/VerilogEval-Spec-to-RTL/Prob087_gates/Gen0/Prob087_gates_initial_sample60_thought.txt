To implement the TopModule, we will define a module with the given inputs and outputs. Then, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to assign the outputs based on the inputs. The logical operators in Verilog are:
- AND (& or &&)
- OR (| or ||)
- XOR (^)
- NOT (!)
