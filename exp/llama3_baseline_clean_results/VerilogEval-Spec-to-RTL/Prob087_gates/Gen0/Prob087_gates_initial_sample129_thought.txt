To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logic gates in Verilog. The module will have two input ports 'a' and 'b', and seven output ports for each logic operation. We will use the corresponding logical operators in Verilog for each operation: 
- AND (&), 
- OR (|), 
- XOR (^), 
- NAND (~(&)), 
- NOR (~(|)), 
- XNOR (~(^)), 
- and NOT (&~).
