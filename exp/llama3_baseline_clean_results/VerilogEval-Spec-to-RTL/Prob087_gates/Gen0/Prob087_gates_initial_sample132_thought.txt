To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators provided by Verilog. 
We will directly assign the result of each logical operation to its corresponding output.
The logical operators we will use are: 
- & (bitwise AND) for out_and
- | (bitwise OR) for out_or
- ^ (bitwise XOR) for out_xor
- ~(a & b) (inversion of bitwise AND) for out_nand
- ~(a | b) (inversion of bitwise OR) for out_nor
- ~(a ^ b) (inversion of bitwise XOR) for out_xnor
- a & ~b (bitwise AND with inversion of b) for out_anotb
