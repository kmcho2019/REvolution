To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators provided by Verilog. We will directly assign the result of each logical operation to its corresponding output. For 'and', 'or', 'xor', 'nand', 'nor', 'xnor', and 'and-not' operations, we will use Verilog operators '&' (or 'and'), '|', '^', '~(&)' (or '~(a&b)'), '~(|)', '~(^)', and '&' with '!' (or '~') for negation, respectively.
