To implement the TopModule, we can directly instantiate the required logic gates using Verilog's built-in logical operators. We will assign the outputs of these operators to the corresponding output ports. For instance, the `out_and` port will be assigned the result of `a && b`, which is the logical AND operation between `a` and `b`. Similarly, we will assign the results of `a || b` to `out_or`, `a ^ b` to `out_xor`, `~(a && b)` to `out_nand`, `~(a || b)` to `out_nor`, `~(a ^ b)` to `out_xnor`, and `a && ~b` to `out_anotb`.
