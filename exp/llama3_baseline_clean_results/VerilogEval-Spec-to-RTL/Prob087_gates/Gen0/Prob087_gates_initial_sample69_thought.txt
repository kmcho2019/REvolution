To implement the TopModule, we need to define a Verilog module with the specified interface. We can then use basic logical operators to drive each output based on the inputs a and b. The logical operators in Verilog are:
- `&` for bitwise AND
- `|` for bitwise OR
- `^` for bitwise XOR
- `~` for bitwise NOT
- `&&` and `||` for logical AND and OR respectively, but these are not needed here as we are working with single-bit inputs.

We can directly apply these operators to implement the required logic gates:
- `out_and` is `a & b`
- `out_or` is `a | b`
- `out_xor` is `a ^ b`
- `out_nand` is `~(a & b)`
- `out_nor` is `~(a | b)`
- `out_xnor` is `~(a ^ b)` or equivalently `(a & b) | (~a & ~b)`
- `out_anotb` is `a & ~b`
