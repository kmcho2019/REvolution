To implement the TopModule, we will use the basic logical operators provided by Verilog. The module will have two input ports, 'a' and 'b', and seven output ports corresponding to each of the specified logical operations. We will use the following Verilog operators:
- `&` for logical AND
- `|` for logical OR
- `^` for logical XOR
- `~` for logical NOT, combined with `&` for NAND, `|` for NOR, and `^` for XNOR
- `&` with `~` for AND with NOT
