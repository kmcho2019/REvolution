To implement the TopModule, we need to define a Verilog module with the given interface and implement the combinational logic using the respective logical operators for each output. 
- out_and will be the logical AND of inputs a and b.
- out_or will be the logical OR of inputs a and b.
- out_xor will be the logical XOR of inputs a and b.
- out_nand will be the logical NAND of inputs a and b, which is equivalent to the logical NOT of (a AND b).
- out_nor will be the logical NOR of inputs a and b, which is equivalent to the logical NOT of (a OR b).
- out_xnor will be the logical XNOR of inputs a and b, which is equivalent to the logical NOT of (a XOR b).
- out_anotb will be the logical AND of a and the logical NOT of b.
