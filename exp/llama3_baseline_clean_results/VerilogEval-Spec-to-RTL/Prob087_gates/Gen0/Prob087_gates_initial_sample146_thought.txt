To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to generate each output. The module will not require any internal wires, as the outputs can be directly assigned using the logical operators. 
- out_and will be assigned 'a && b' (logical AND).
- out_or will be assigned 'a || b' (logical OR).
- out_xor will be assigned 'a ^ b' (bitwise XOR).
- out_nand will be assigned '~(a && b)' (logical NAND, by inverting the AND result).
- out_nor will be assigned '~(a || b)' (logical NOR, by inverting the OR result).
- out_xnor will be assigned '~(a ^ b)' (logical XNOR, by inverting the XOR result).
- out_anotb will be assigned 'a && ~b' (logical AND with b inverted).
