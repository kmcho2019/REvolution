The TopModule can be implemented by directly instantiating the required logic gates for each output port. This can be achieved by using the built-in logical operators in Verilog. We will use the following operators:
- `&` for AND
- `|` for OR
- `^` for XOR
- `~` for NOT
- No direct NAND, NOR, XNOR operators are available in Verilog, so we'll use their equivalent expressions:
  - NAND: `~(a & b)`
  - NOR: `~(a | b)`
  - XNOR: `~(a ^ b)` or `(a & b) | (~a & ~b)`
  - AND-NOT: `a & ~b`
We will assign these logical operations to their respective output ports.
