To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators in Verilog. 
We'll define each output based on the given logic gate operations. 
For example, out_and will be assigned the result of a AND b, out_or will be 
assigned the result of a OR b, and so on. 
We will utilize the bitwise operators in Verilog, which are: 
- & (AND), 
- | (OR), 
- ^ (XOR), 
- ~ (NOT), and 
- the combination of ~ and other operators for NAND, NOR, XNOR, and AND-NOT operations.
