To implement the TopModule, we can use basic logical operators provided by Verilog. We will assign the result of each logical operation between inputs a and b to the corresponding output. The logical operators in Verilog are:
- & (and)
- | (or)
- ^ (xor)
- ~ (not)
- && (and) and || (or) for bitwise operations, but since we are dealing with single bits, & and | are sufficient.
- For nand and nor, we can use the ~ operator to invert the result of the and and or operations respectively.
- For xnor, we can use the ^ operator and then invert the result.
- For and-not, we can use the & operator with one of the inputs inverted.
