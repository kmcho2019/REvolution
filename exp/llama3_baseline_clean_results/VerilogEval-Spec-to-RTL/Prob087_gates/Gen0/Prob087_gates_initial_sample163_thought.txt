To implement the TopModule, we can use the basic logic gates provided by Verilog, such as `&` for AND, `|` for OR, `^` for XOR, `~` for NOT, and the combination of these for NAND, NOR, and XNOR. The module will be a simple combinational circuit, meaning that its output will be determined solely by the current state of its inputs, without any internal state or memory.
