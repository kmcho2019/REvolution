To implement the TopModule, we can use the basic logical operators provided in Verilog. We will assign the outputs directly using the corresponding logical operators. The module will have no internal variables or complex logic, as the problem only requires simple combinational logic. We will use the following operators:
- `&` for AND
- `|` for OR
- `^` for XOR
- `~` for NOT (used in combination with others for NAND, NOR, XNOR, and AND-NOT)
- `~^` for XNOR (equivalent to ~(a ^ b))
- `~(&)` for NAND (equivalent to ~(a & b))
- `~(|)` for NOR (equivalent to ~(a | b))
- `& ~` for AND-NOT (a & ~b)
