To implement the TopModule, we will use the basic logic gates provided in Verilog. 
We can directly assign the output of each gate to the corresponding output port.
For the logic operations that are not directly available in Verilog (like nand, nor, xnor, and and-not), 
we will use the available operations to derive them.
- out_and: We will use the '&' operator.
- out_or: We will use the '|' operator.
- out_xor: We will use the '^' operator.
- out_nand: We will use ~(a & b) to derive the nand operation.
- out_nor: We will use ~(a | b) to derive the nor operation.
- out_xnor: We will use ~(a ^ b) to derive the xnor operation.
- out_anotb: We will use a & ~b to derive the and-not operation.
