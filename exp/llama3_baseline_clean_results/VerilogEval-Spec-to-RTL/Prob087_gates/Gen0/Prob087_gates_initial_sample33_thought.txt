To implement the TopModule, we can simply instantiate the basic logic gates as described in the problem statement. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for the basic logic operations: 
- `&` for AND, 
- `|` for OR, 
- `^` for XOR, 
- `~` for NOT, 
- `~(a & b)` for NAND, 
- `~(a | b)` for NOR, 
- `~(a ^ b)` for XNOR, and 
- `a & ~b` for A and not B.
We will assign these operations to the respective output ports.
