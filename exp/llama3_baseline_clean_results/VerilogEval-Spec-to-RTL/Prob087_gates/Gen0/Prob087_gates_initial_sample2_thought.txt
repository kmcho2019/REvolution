To implement the TopModule, we can directly use the built-in logical operators in Verilog. We can assign the output of each gate to its respective output port. For the NOT operation required in the NAND, NOR, XNOR, and ANOTB operations, we can use the bitwise NOT operator (~) or the logical NOT operator (!). However, since we are working with single-bit values, we will use the bitwise NOT operator (~) for consistency.
