To implement the TopModule, we will first add the two 8-bit numbers a and b. Since they are 2's complement numbers, a simple arithmetic addition will work. We'll use the built-in Verilog addition operator (+) to perform this operation. Then, we need to check for overflow. In 2's complement representation, an overflow occurs when the sign bit of the result is different from the sign bits of both the operands. We can check this by comparing the most significant bits (MSBs) of a, b, and the result. If the MSBs of a and b are the same and different from the MSB of the result, then an overflow has occurred.
