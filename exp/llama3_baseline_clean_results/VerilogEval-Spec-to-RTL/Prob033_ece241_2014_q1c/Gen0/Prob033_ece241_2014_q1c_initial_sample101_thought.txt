To implement the TopModule, we need to add the two 8-bit 2's complement numbers a and b and store the result in s. We also need to check for a signed overflow. In 2's complement representation, an overflow occurs when the sign bit of the result is different from the sign bits of the operands. We can achieve this by checking the carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) after addition. If the carry-out is different from the sign bits of the operands, it indicates an overflow.
