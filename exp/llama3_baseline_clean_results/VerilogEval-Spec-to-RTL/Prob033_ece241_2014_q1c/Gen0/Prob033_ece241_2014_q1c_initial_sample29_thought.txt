To solve this problem, we need to design a module named TopModule that adds two 8-bit 2's complement numbers, a and b, and produces an 8-bit sum, s. We also need to detect whether a signed overflow has occurred during the addition. In 2's complement representation, an overflow occurs when the sign of the result is different from the sign of the operands. We can achieve this by checking the carry from the most significant bit (MSB) of the sum. If the carry is 1, it means the result has overflowed.
