To implement the TopModule, we need to perform a signed addition of the two 8-bit 2's complement numbers, a and b. The sum will be stored in s. To detect a signed overflow, we need to check if the most significant bit (MSB) of the sum is different from the MSB of the two operands. In 2's complement representation, the MSB represents the sign of the number. If the signs of the operands are the same and the sign of the sum is different, then an overflow has occurred. We can use the $signed system function in Verilog to handle the signed arithmetic operations.
