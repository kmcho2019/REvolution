To implement the TopModule, we need to perform a signed addition of the two 8-bit 2's complement numbers, a and b. We will use the built-in Verilog addition operator to add the two numbers. To detect an overflow, we need to check if the sum of the most significant bits (MSBs) of a and b is different from the MSB of the sum. In 2's complement representation, the MSB represents the sign of the number (0 for positive and 1 for negative). If the signs of a and b are the same, and the sign of the sum is different, then an overflow has occurred.
