To implement the TopModule, we will first add the two 8-bit numbers a and b. 
Since we are dealing with 2's complement numbers, we need to check for a signed overflow. 
A signed overflow occurs when the sum of two numbers with the same sign (both positive or both negative) 
has a different sign than the original numbers. 
We can use the fact that in 2's complement representation, the most significant bit (MSB) represents the sign. 
We will check if the MSB of the sum is different from the MSB of the original numbers. 
If they are different, it means a signed overflow has occurred.
