To implement the TopModule, we need to perform an 8-bit 2's complement addition of the input numbers a and b to produce the output s. We also need to detect whether a signed overflow has occurred. A signed overflow occurs when the result of the addition exceeds the range of 8-bit 2's complement numbers, i.e., when the result is greater than 127 or less than -128. We can achieve this by checking the carry-out of the most significant bit (MSB) after the addition and the sign bits of the inputs and the result.
