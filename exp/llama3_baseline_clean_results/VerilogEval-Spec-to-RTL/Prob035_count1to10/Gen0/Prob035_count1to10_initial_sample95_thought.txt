To implement the decade counter in the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit binary counter with some modifications. We will use a 4-bit register to hold the count value. The count value will be incremented on every positive edge of the clock. To count from 1 to 10, we will check for the value 10 (1010 in binary) and reset the count to 1 (0001 in binary) when it is reached. We will also include the reset logic to reset the count to 1 when the reset signal is high.
