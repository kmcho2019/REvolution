To implement the decade counter, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current count. We'll initialize the count to 1 and increment it at each positive clock edge until it reaches 10. When the count reaches 10, we'll reset it back to 1 at the next clock edge. The reset input will also be used to reset the count to 1 synchronously, meaning that the reset will only occur at a positive clock edge when the reset signal is high. This approach ensures that the counter remains in a valid state.
