To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10 inclusive, we will use a 4-bit binary counter and add logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10 (1010 in binary) and goes to 11 (1011 in binary). The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 1 (0001 in binary) synchronously. This means that when the reset signal is high, the counter will be reset to 1 on the next positive edge of the clock.
