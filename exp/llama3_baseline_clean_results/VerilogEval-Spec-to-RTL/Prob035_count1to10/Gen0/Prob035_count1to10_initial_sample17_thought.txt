To implement the decade counter, we need to design a sequential circuit that counts from 1 to 10 and then wraps around to 1 after reaching 10. This can be achieved using a 4-bit register to store the count, and a combinational logic to determine the next state of the counter. The counter should be initialized to 1 when the reset signal is high. We will use a simple if-else statement to check the current state of the counter and decide the next state.
