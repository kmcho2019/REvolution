To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10 in the TopModule, 
we need to use a 4-bit register to store the current count since 10 can be 
represented by 4 bits (1000 in binary is 8, so 1010 is 10, which fits within 
4 bits). We'll also use a conditional statement to reset the counter to 1 
when the reset signal is high and the clock signal is at its positive edge. 
The counter should increment at each positive clock edge unless the reset 
signal is high or the counter has reached 10, in which case it should reset 
to 1 at the next positive clock edge.
