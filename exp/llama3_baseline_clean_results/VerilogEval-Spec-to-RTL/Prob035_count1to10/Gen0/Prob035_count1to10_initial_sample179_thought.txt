To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with a 4-bit counter. The counter will be initialized to 1 when reset is high. We will use a combinational logic to check if the current count has reached 10. If it has, on the next positive clock edge, the counter will wrap around to 1. The counter will increment by 1 on every positive clock edge if it hasn't reached 10 yet.
