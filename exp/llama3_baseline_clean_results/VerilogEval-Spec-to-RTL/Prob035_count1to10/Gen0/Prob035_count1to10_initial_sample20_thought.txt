To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we first need to understand the basic functionality of such a counter. It counts from 1 to 10 and then resets back to 1. This means we are not counting from 0 but starting directly from 1. 

The module named TopModule will have inputs clk (clock signal) and reset (active high synchronous reset signal), and output q (a 4-bit signal representing the count).

Since the counter only counts from 1 to 10, we need a mechanism to detect when we reach the count of 10 and reset it back to 1 on the next clock edge.

We will use a simple 4-bit register to store the count. The register will be updated on each positive edge of the clock signal unless the reset signal is high, in which case it resets to 1.

We'll use an if-else statement inside an always block (which is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock and the reset signal) to handle the counting logic and the reset functionality.
