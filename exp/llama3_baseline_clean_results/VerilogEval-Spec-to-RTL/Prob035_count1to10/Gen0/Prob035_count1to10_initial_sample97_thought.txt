To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we will create a 4-bit counter that counts from 1 to 10. The counter will be implemented using a reg variable to store the current count. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 1 when it is high. We will use a combinational logic to determine when the counter has reached 10 and should wrap around to 1. The counter will be updated on the positive edge of the clock.
