To implement the decade counter in Verilog, we can use a 4-bit register to store the count. The counter should reset to 1 when the reset input is high and the clock edge is detected. On each positive clock edge, the counter should increment by 1, wrapping around to 1 when it reaches 10 (which is represented by the binary number 1010 in 4 bits) or when it reaches 11 (1011), it wraps around to 1. This can be achieved using a simple if-else statement inside a always block that checks for the positive edge of the clock.
