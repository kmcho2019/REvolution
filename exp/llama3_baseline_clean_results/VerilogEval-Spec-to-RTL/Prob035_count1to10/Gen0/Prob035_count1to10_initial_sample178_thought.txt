To implement the decade counter, I will use a 4-bit counter that wraps around from 10 back to 1 after reaching 10. Since all sequential logic is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, I will use a sequential logic design. I will utilize an if-else statement to check if the current count is 10, and if so, reset it to 1 on the next clock edge. The reset input will also be checked, and if it's active high, the counter will reset to 1 regardless of the current count.
