To implement the decade counter, we can use a 4-bit register to store the count. 
We will initialize the count to 1 when the reset signal is high. 
On each positive edge of the clock, we will check if the current count is 10. 
If it is, we will reset the count to 1. 
Otherwise, we will increment the count by 1. 
This way, the counter will count from 1 to 10 and then wrap around to 1.
