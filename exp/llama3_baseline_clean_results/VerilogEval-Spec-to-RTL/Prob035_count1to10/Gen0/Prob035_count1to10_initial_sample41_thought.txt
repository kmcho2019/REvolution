To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10, we need a 4-bit counter since 10 can be represented with 4 bits (1010 in binary). We'll use a synchronous reset, which means the counter will reset to 1 when the reset signal is high. On every positive clock edge, if the reset signal is low, we'll increment the counter by 1 until it reaches 10 (1010 in binary), and then reset it back to 1. This can be achieved using a finite state machine or a simple counter logic with conditional statements.
