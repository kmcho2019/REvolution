To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we can use a sequential logic circuit that increments a counter variable on each clock cycle and resets it to 1 when the count reaches 10. We will utilize a 4-bit register to hold the current count, allowing us to count from 1 to 10 (binary 0001 to 1010). The reset input should be active high synchronous, meaning it resets the counter to 1 on the positive edge of the clock when reset is high.
