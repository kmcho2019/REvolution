To implement the TopModule, we will design a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10. The counter should reset to 1 when the reset signal is high. We can achieve this by using a 4-bit register to store the count and incrementing it on every positive edge of the clock until it reaches 10. When the count reaches 10, it should wrap around to 1. We'll use a simple if-else statement in the always block to check for the reset condition and the wrap-around condition.
