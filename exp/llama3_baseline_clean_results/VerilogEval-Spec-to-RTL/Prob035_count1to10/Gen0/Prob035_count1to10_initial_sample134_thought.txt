To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we need to design a module that can count from 1 to 10 and reset to 1 when the reset signal is high. This can be achieved by using a 4-bit register to store the count, and an if-else statement to check the current count and decide whether to increment or reset it. We will use a synchronous reset to ensure that the reset operation is only performed on the positive edge of the clock.
