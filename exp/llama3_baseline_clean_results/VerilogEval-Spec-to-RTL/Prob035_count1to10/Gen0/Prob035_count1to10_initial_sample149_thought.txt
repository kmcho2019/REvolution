To implement the decade counter, we will use a 4-bit binary counter and modify it to count from 1 to 10. Since we are counting from 1, we will not use the typical binary counting sequence, but instead, we will use a conditional statement to stop the counter when it reaches 10 (1001 in binary) and reset it back to 1 (0001 in binary) on the next clock cycle.
