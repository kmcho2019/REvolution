To implement the decade counter, we can use a 4-bit register to store the count. On the positive edge of the clock, we will increment the count. When the count reaches 10 (which is 1010 in binary), we will wrap around to 1 (which is 0001 in binary) on the next clock edge. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 1 synchronously. We will use an if-else statement to handle the reset condition and the wrap-around condition.
