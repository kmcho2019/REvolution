To implement the decade counter, we can use a 4-bit binary counter and decode the values to count from 1 to 10 (binary 0001 to 1010). We'll use a case statement or a if-else statement to reset the counter when it exceeds 10. For the reset functionality, we'll use a synchronous reset, which means the reset signal will only be considered on the positive edge of the clock. This ensures the reset is always synchronized with the clock and avoids potential metastability issues.
