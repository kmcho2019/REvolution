To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we can use a 4-bit binary counter and modify its behavior to count from 1 to 10 (binary 0001 to 1010) instead of the typical 0 to 15. We will use a counter variable to keep track of the current count. When the count reaches 10 (1010 in binary), it should wrap around to 1 (0001) on the next clock cycle. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 1 when it is high. We will use a conditional statement to check the count and reset values.
