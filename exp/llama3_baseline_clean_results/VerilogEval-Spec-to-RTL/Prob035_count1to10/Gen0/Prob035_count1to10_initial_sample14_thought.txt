To implement the decade counter in the TopModule, I will use a 4-bit register to store the current count. The counter will increment on every positive clock edge unless it has reached 10, at which point it will wrap around to 1. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 1 on the next positive clock edge after reset is asserted. I will use a binary encoding to represent the numbers 1 through 10, where 1 is represented as 0001, 2 as 0010, and so on, up to 10, which is represented as 1010.
