To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we need to design a module named TopModule that takes a clock (clk), a reset signal (reset), and outputs a 4-bit value (q) representing the count. The counter should increment on the positive edge of the clock, reset to 1 when the reset signal is high, and count from 1 to 10 inclusive. We will use a 4-bit register to hold the count, and use conditional statements to implement the counting logic.
