To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we will use a finite state machine that counts from 1 to 10 and then resets back to 1. The counter should be incremented on the positive edge of the clock signal and should reset synchronously when the reset signal is high. We will use a 4-bit register to store the count, as we need to count from 1 to 10 (which requires 4 bits in binary representation).
