To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we first need to understand the requirements. We have to count from 1 to 10 inclusively. This means we need a 4-bit counter since 2^4 = 16, which is enough to represent numbers 1 through 10 (binary 0001 to 1010). We will use a simple 4-bit counter with a conditional statement to reset the counter back to 1 when it reaches 11 (which is 1011 in binary). The counter should be synchronous, meaning it increments on the positive edge of the clock signal. The reset signal is also synchronous and active high, so when reset is high, the counter should reset to 1 on the next positive clock edge.
