To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we will use a 4-bit register to store the count value. We will utilize a synchronous reset to initialize the count to 1 when the reset signal is high. On each positive clock edge, we will increment the count by 1 unless it has reached 10, in which case we will reset the count to 1. This will ensure that the counter counts from 1 to 10 inclusively.
