To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we will create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. The counter should count from 1 to 10, inclusive, and reset to 1 when the reset input is high. We will use a 4-bit register to store the count, as 4 bits can represent numbers up to 15, which is more than enough for our needs. The counter will increment on every positive clock edge, and when it reaches 10, it will wrap around to 1 on the next clock edge. The reset input will be checked on every positive clock edge, and if it's high, the counter will reset to 1.
