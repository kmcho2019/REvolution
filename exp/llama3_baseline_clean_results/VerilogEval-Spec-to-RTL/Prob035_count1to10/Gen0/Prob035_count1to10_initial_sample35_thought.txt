To implement the decade counter in the TopModule, we can utilize a 4-bit counter since we need to count from 1 to 10. We will use a synchronous reset approach to reset the counter to 1 when the reset signal is high. The counter should increment on the positive edge of the clock. We will implement the counter using a register and update its value based on the current count and the reset condition. We will also ensure that the counter wraps around from 10 back to 1 instead of going beyond 10.
