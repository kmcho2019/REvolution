To implement the decade counter in Verilog, we need to design a module that increments a 4-bit counter on every positive clock edge, resetting to 1 when the count reaches 10, and also resets the counter to 1 when the reset signal is high. Since the reset is synchronous (active high), we'll use an if statement inside the always block to check for the reset condition. We will also need a conditional statement to determine when to increment the counter and when to reset it to 1 after reaching 10.
