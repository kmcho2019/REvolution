To implement a decade counter in Verilog, we'll use a sequential logic approach. The counter should count from 1 to 10 and reset to 1 when the reset input is active high. This can be achieved by using a 4-bit register to store the count value and updating it at each positive clock edge, unless the reset is active. When the count reaches 10 (1010 in binary), it should wrap around to 1 (0001 in binary) on the next clock edge.
