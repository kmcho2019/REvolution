To implement a 2-1 multiplexer in Verilog, we can use a simple if-else statement or a ternary operator to conditionally assign the output 'out' based on the value of the select line 'sel'. In this case, we will use a ternary operator for conciseness. The idea is to directly assign 'a' to 'out' when 'sel' is 0, and 'b' to 'out' when 'sel' is 1.
