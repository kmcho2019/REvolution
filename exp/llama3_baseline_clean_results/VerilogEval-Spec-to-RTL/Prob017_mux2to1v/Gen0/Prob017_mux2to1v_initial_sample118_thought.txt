To implement a 2-1 multiplexer in Verilog, we can use a simple conditional statement to choose between the two inputs based on the select signal. We will use the ternary operator (?:) to concisely express this logic. The ternary operator works as follows: result = condition ? value_if_true : value_if_false. In this case, the condition is the value of the sel signal, and the values to choose from are the signals a and b.
