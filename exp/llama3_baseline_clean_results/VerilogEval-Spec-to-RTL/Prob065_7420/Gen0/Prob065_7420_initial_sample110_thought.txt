To implement the functionality of the 7420 chip, which contains two 4-input NAND gates, we can use the basic logic of a NAND gate. A NAND gate outputs 1 only if at least one of its inputs is 0. Therefore, for each 4-input NAND gate in our TopModule, we will use a logical operation that implements this behavior. Specifically, the output of a NAND gate can be represented as the negation of the conjunction (AND) of all its inputs. For our module, we will implement two separate NAND operations: one for p1y based on inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d, and another for p2y based on inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d.
