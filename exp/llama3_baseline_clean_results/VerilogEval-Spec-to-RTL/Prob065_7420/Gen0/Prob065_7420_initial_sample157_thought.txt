To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7420 chip, we need to design two 4-input NAND gates. A 4-input NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. We can achieve this using the Verilog logical operators. We will create two separate NAND gates for the two sets of inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d), and assign their outputs to p1y and p2y respectively.
