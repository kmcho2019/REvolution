To implement the TopModule, which mimics the functionality of the 7420 chip containing two 4-input NAND gates, we need to understand that a NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. For the 7420 chip, we have two sets of inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) corresponding to two separate NAND gates. The output of each NAND gate is then assigned to p1y and p2y respectively. This means we need to implement the logic such that p1y is 1 only if none of p1a, p1b, p1c, or p1d are 1, and similarly, p2y is 1 only if none of p2a, p2b, p2c, or p2d are 1.
