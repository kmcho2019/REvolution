The 7420 chip has two 4-input NAND gates. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. In other words, the output of a NAND gate is the logical NOT of the logical AND of its inputs. We can implement this in our module by using the logical AND operator (&) and the logical NOT operator (!) or the bitwise NOT operator (~) on the inputs to each gate. We'll assign p1y to be the output of the first 4-input NAND gate (connected to p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d) and p2y to be the output of the second 4-input NAND gate (connected to p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d).
