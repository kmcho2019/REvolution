To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7420 chip, we need to understand the behavior of a 4-input NAND gate. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. We will use this understanding to create two 4-input NAND gates within the TopModule. The first NAND gate will take p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d as inputs and produce p1y as output. The second NAND gate will take p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d as inputs and produce p2y as output. We will use the logical AND and NOT operators in Verilog to implement the NAND gates.
