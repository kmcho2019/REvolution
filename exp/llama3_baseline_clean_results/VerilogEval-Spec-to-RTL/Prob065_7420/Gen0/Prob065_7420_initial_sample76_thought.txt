The 7420 chip contains two 4-input NAND gates. We can implement the TopModule by instantiating two 4-input NAND gates and connecting the inputs and outputs accordingly. The first NAND gate will be connected to inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and output p1y. The second NAND gate will be connected to inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d and output p2y.
