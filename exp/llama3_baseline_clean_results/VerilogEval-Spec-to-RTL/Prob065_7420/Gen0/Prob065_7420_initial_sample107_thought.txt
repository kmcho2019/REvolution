To implement the functionality of the 7420 chip, which contains two 4-input NAND gates, we need to understand how a NAND gate operates. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if at least one of its inputs is 0. Otherwise, if all inputs are 1, the output is 0. Given this, we can implement the TopModule by directly applying the NAND operation to the sets of inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d to produce outputs p1y and p2y, respectively.
