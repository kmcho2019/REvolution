To implement the 7420 chip, which has two 4-input NAND gates, we will design the TopModule with two separate NAND gate implementations. The first NAND gate will take inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d, and produce output p1y. The second NAND gate will take inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d, and produce output p2y. We will use the logical NAND operation, which produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1.
