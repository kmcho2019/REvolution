To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7420 chip, we need to understand that the 7420 chip consists of two 4-input NAND gates. We will create a module that takes eight 1-bit inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d, p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) and produces two 1-bit outputs (p1y, p2y). Each output will be the result of a 4-input NAND operation on the corresponding set of inputs. For p1y, the NAND operation will be performed on p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d. For p2y, the NAND operation will be performed on p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d.
