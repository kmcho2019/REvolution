To implement the TopModule, which mimics the functionality of the 7420 chip containing two 4-input NAND gates, we need to understand the operation of a NAND gate. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if at least one of its inputs is 0. For a 4-input NAND gate, the output will be 1 if any of the four inputs are 0; otherwise, it will be 0. We will implement two separate 4-input NAND gates within the TopModule, one for p1y and one for p2y, using the inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d for the first gate and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d for the second gate.
