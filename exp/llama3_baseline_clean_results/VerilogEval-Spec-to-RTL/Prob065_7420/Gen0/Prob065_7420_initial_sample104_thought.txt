To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7420 chip, we need to understand the behavior of a 4-input NAND gate. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if at least one of its inputs is 0. We can use this understanding to create two 4-input NAND gates within the TopModule. Each gate will take four inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d for the first gate and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d for the second gate) and produce an output (p1y and p2y respectively) according to the NAND gate logic. We will use the logical AND operator (&) and the logical NOT operator (!) to achieve this.
