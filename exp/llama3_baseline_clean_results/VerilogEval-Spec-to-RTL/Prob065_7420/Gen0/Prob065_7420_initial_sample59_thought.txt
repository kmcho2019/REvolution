The 7420 chip has two 4-input NAND gates, so we can implement the TopModule by creating two separate 4-input NAND gates. The first NAND gate will take inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d, and produce output p1y. The second NAND gate will take inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d, and produce output p2y. In Verilog, we can use the logical NAND operator (~&) to implement the NAND gate.
