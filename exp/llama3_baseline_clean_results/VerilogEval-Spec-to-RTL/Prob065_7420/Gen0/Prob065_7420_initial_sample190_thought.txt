To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7420 chip, we need to create two 4-input NAND gates. Each NAND gate takes four input signals and produces an output signal that is the inverse of the AND operation of the four inputs. This means the output will be 1 only if at least one of the inputs is 0. Since we have two sets of 4-input signals (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d), we can create two separate NAND gates and assign their outputs to p1y and p2y respectively.
