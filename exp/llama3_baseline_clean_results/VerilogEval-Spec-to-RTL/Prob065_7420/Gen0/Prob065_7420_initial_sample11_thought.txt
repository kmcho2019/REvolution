To implement the 7420 chip's functionality, which includes two 4-input NAND gates, we need to create a module named TopModule that takes in 8 one-bit inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d, p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) and produces two one-bit outputs (p1y, p2y). The logic for each output will be the NAND of its respective four inputs. This means p1y will be the NAND of p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d, and p2y will be the NAND of p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d. The NAND operation produces an output of 1 only if all inputs are 0; otherwise, it produces an output of 0.
