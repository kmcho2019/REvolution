The 7420 chip has two 4-input NAND gates. To implement the functionality of the 7420 chip, we need to create a module with two 4-input NAND gates. The first NAND gate should take inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, and p1d, and produce output p1y. The second NAND gate should take inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, and p2d, and produce output p2y.
