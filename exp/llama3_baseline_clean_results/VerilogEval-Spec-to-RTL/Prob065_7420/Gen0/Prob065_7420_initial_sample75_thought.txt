To implement the 7420 chip's functionality in the TopModule, we need to create two 4-input NAND gates. A NAND gate produces an output of 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. We will use the logical AND and NOT operators to achieve this. The two NAND gates will be used to calculate p1y and p2y based on inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d, and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d, respectively.
