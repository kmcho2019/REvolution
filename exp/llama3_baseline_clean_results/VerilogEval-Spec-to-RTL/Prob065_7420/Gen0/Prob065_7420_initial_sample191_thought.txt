To implement the 7420 chip functionality, we need to design two 4-input NAND gates. Each gate should take four inputs and produce an output that is the NAND of all the inputs. We will then instantiate these gates in our TopModule and connect their inputs to p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d respectively, and their outputs to p1y and p2y.
