Each 4-input NAND gate takes four inputs and produces one output. The logic for a NAND gate is such that the output is 1 only if none of the inputs are 1. For the 7420 chip, we have two sets of inputs (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) and (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) corresponding to the two NAND gates, and their outputs are p1y and p2y, respectively. We can implement the NAND logic using the logical AND operator (for the inverted inputs) and the logical NOT operator in Verilog.
