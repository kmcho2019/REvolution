To implement the functionality of the 7420 chip, which contains two 4-input NAND gates, we need to create a module that takes eight input signals (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d for the first gate and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d for the second gate) and produces two output signals (p1y and p2y). The output of each NAND gate is the inverse of the logical AND of all its inputs. This means that for each gate, the output will be 1 only if at least one of its inputs is 0. Otherwise, the output will be 0.
