The 7420 chip contains two 4-input NAND gates. To implement this functionality, we can define two separate NAND gates within the TopModule, each taking four inputs and producing one output. We will assign the inputs p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d to the first NAND gate, and the inputs p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d to the second NAND gate. The outputs of these gates will be assigned to p1y and p2y respectively.
