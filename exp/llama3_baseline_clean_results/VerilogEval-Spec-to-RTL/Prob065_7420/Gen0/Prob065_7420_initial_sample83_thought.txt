To implement the 7420 chip, which contains two 4-input NAND gates, we need to create a module TopModule that takes in eight input ports (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d for the first gate and p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d for the second gate) and produces two output ports (p1y and p2y). The output of each NAND gate is the inverse of the AND of all its inputs. This means p1y should be 1 whenever not all of (p1a, p1b, p1c, p1d) are 1, and p2y should be 1 whenever not all of (p2a, p2b, p2c, p2d) are 1.
