To implement the TopModule, we will create a module that includes 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. Each DFF will be connected to a corresponding bit of the 8-bit input 'd' and will drive a bit of the 8-bit output 'q'. We will utilize Verilog's built-in support for DFFs by assigning the input 'd' to the output 'q' on the positive edge of the clock 'clk'.
