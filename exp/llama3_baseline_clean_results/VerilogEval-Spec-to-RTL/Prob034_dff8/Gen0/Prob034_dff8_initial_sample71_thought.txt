To implement the TopModule with 8 D flip-flops, I will use a single 8-bit register with a clock enable that is always high, and a load signal that is always high, triggered by the positive edge of the clock input. Each bit of the input d will be assigned to a D flip-flop and will be assigned to the corresponding bit of the output q on the positive edge of the clock. The module can be implemented using a non-blocking assignment in the always block to ensure that the outputs change only after the clock edge.
