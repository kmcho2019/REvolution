To implement the TopModule, I will use 8 D flip-flops connected in parallel. Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and will store the corresponding bit of the input data (d). The output of each D flip-flop will be connected to the corresponding bit of the output (q). I will use the always_ff statement in Verilog to define the flip-flop behavior.
