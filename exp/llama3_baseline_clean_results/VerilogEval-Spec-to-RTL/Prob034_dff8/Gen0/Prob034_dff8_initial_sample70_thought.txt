To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) connected in parallel. Each DFF will store one bit of the input data 'd' when the clock 'clk' goes high (positive edge). We will use the non-blocking assignment operator ('<=') to avoid race conditions, and the 'posedge' keyword to trigger the DFFs on the positive edge of the clock. The 'always' block will be used to define the clock edge sensitivity.
