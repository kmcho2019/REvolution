The TopModule can be designed by instantiating 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) inside it. Each DFF will have its input connected to the corresponding bit of the input 'd' and its output connected to the corresponding bit of the output 'q'. All DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. We will use the built-in Verilog DFF syntax to create these flip-flops.
