To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we can use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) connected in parallel. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal and will have a corresponding input from the 8-bit input d and an output to the 8-bit output q. This design ensures that on every positive edge of the clock, the current value of d is captured and stored in the DFFs, and their outputs are available on q.
