The TopModule will contain 8 D flip-flops, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. The 8-bit input 'd' will be connected to the D inputs of the flip-flops, and the 8-bit output 'q' will be driven by the Q outputs of the flip-flops. This will effectively create an 8-bit register that captures the input 'd' on the positive edge of the clock and drives it out as 'q' until the next clock edge.
