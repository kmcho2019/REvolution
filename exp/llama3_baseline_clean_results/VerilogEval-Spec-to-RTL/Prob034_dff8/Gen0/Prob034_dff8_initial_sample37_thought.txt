To implement the TopModule, we need to instantiate 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) and connect their inputs to the corresponding bits of the input d. The outputs of the DFFs will be connected to the corresponding bits of the output q. The clock input clk will be used as the clock signal for all DFFs. Since the DFFs should be triggered by the positive edge of the clock, we will use the posedge keyword in the always block to specify the sensitivity list.
