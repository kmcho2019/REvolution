To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. Each DFF will have the corresponding bit of the input d connected to its D input and the corresponding bit of the output q connected to its Q output. The clock signal will be connected to all DFFs. We can use the always_ff block to describe the behavior of the DFFs in a non-blocking manner, as recommended for sequential logic.
