To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple 8-bit D flip-flop array. Each D flip-flop will store one bit of the input data `d` and output it as part of `q`. The flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock `clk`, ensuring that the data is captured and updated only when the clock transitions from low to high. We will utilize the `always` block in Verilog to describe the behavior of the flip-flops in response to the clock edge.
