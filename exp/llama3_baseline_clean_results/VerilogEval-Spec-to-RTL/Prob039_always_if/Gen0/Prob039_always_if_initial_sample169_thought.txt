To implement the 2-to-1 mux in Verilog, we can use the `assign` statement for the continuous assignment and a procedural `if` statement within an `always` block for the procedural assignment. The `assign` statement will directly assign the value of `a` or `b` to `out_assign` based on the condition that `sel_b1` and `sel_b2` are both true. For the procedural assignment, we'll use an `always` block to continuously evaluate the condition and assign the result to `out_always`.
