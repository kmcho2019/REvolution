To implement the finite-state machine (FSM) that controls the timer, we will define several states and transition between them based on the input signals and the current state. 

The states are:
- IDLE: The initial state where the FSM waits for the pattern 1101 to be detected.
- SHIFT: After detecting the pattern 1101, the FSM asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles to shift in 4 more bits.
- COUNTING: After shifting in 4 bits, the FSM asserts counting and waits until done_counting is high.
- DONE: After done_counting is high, the FSM asserts done to notify the user the timer has timed out and waits until ack is 1.

The transitions between states are based on the input signals and the current state.
