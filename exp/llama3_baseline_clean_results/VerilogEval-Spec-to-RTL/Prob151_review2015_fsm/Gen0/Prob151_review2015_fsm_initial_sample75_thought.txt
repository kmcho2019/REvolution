To implement the finite-state machine for the timer, we'll design a Moore machine with states to detect the pattern, shift in duration bits, wait for counting, and wait for acknowledgement. The states can be: 
- IDLE: The initial state where we start searching for the pattern 1101.
- MATCH1, MATCH2, MATCH3: Intermediate states to detect the pattern 1101.
- SHIFT: Shift in 4 bits to determine the duration.
- COUNT: Wait for the counters to finish counting.
- DONE: Notify the user that the timer has timed out and wait for acknowledgement.
We'll use a counter to keep track of the number of bits shifted in during the SHIFT state.
