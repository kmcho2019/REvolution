To solve this problem, I will design a finite-state machine (FSM) with several states to manage the timer's functionality. The states will include: 
- Idle: searching for the start pattern (1101)
- Shift: shifting in 4 bits to determine the duration
- Count: waiting for the counters to finish
- Done: notifying the user and waiting for acknowledgement
The FSM will transition between these states based on the inputs (data, done_counting, ack) and will generate the necessary outputs (shift_ena, counting, done). The FSM will be reset to the Idle state when the reset signal is high.
