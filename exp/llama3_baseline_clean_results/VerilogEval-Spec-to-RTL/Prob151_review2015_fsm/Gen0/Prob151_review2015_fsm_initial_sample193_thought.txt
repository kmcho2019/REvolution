The finite-state machine (FSM) can be designed with six states: 
IDLE (initial state where the FSM looks for the pattern 1101), 
PATTERN_RECEIVED (state after detecting the pattern), 
SHIFT1, SHIFT2, SHIFT3, SHIFT4 (states where the FSM shifts in the duration), 
COUNTING (state where the FSM waits for the counters to finish), and 
DONE (state where the FSM notifies the user and waits for the acknowledgment).
The FSM will transition between these states based on the input signals and its current state.
