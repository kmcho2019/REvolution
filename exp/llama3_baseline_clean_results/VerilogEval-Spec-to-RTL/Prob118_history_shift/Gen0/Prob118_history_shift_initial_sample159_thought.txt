The TopModule will implement a 32-bit global history shift register. 
When a prediction is made (predict_valid = 1), the module will shift in 
predict_taken from the LSB side to update the branch history. When a 
misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted = 1), the module will load the 
branch history register with the history after the completion of the 
mispredicted branch. The misprediction will take precedence over a 
prediction if both occur simultaneously. The module will be reset to zero 
when areset is asserted.
