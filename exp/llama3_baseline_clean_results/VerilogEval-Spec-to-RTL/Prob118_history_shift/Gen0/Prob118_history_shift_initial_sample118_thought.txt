To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. The module will have two main operations: shifting the history register when a prediction is made and loading the history register when a misprediction occurs. We will use the predict_valid signal to determine when to shift the history register and the train_mispredicted signal to determine when to load the history register. If both signals are high at the same time, the misprediction will take precedence. The areset signal will be used to reset the history register to zero asynchronously.
