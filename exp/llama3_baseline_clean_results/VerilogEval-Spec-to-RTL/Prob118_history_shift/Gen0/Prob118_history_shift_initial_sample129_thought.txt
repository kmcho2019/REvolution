The module TopModule will contain a 32-bit register to hold the global history. 
When predict_valid is 1, the register will shift in predict_taken from the LSB side. 
When train_mispredicted is 1, the register will be loaded with the train_history concatenated with train_taken. 
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are 1 at the same time, train_mispredicted will take precedence. 
The register will be reset to zero when areset is 1. 
The predict_history output will always reflect the current value of the global history register.
