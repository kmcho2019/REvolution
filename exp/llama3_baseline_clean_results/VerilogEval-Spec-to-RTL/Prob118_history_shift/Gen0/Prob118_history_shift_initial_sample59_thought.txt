To implement the TopModule, we need to design a finite state machine that controls the update of the 32-bit global history shift register. The module will have two main states: update and rollback. When a prediction is made (predict_valid = 1), the module will shift in the predicted direction from the LSB side. When a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted = 1), the module will load the history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch. If both a prediction and a misprediction occur at the same time, the misprediction will take precedence. The module will also have an asynchronous reset that resets the history register to zero.
