To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit shift register to store the branch history. 
We will use an if-else statement to handle the prediction and misprediction cases separately. 
In case of a prediction, we will shift in the predict_taken bit from the LSB side. 
In case of a misprediction, we will load the branch history register with the train_history 
concatenated with the train_taken bit. If both prediction and misprediction occur at the same time, 
the misprediction will take precedence. We will also include an asynchronous reset to reset the 
history register to zero when areset is asserted.
