To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the global history shift register. We will use the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals to determine when to update or load the history register. If predict_valid is 1, we will shift in predict_taken from the LSB side to update the branch history. If train_mispredicted is 1, we will load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch. We will use the train_history and train_taken signals to load the correct history. We will also handle the case where both prediction and misprediction occur at the same time, giving precedence to the misprediction.
