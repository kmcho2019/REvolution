To implement the TopModule, we can use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. 
The module will have two main operations: shifting in the predicted branch result and loading the history after a misprediction.
When predict_valid is high, we shift in predict_taken from the LSB side to update the branch history.
When train_mispredicted is high, we load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch, 
which is the train_history concatenated with train_taken.
We will use an if-else statement to prioritize the misprediction over the prediction when both occur at the same time.
We will also include an asynchronous reset that resets the history counter to zero when areset is high.
