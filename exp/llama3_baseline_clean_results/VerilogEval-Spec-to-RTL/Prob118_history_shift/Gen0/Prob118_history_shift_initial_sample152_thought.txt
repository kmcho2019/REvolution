The TopModule should be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with a 32-bit shift register to store the branch history. The module will have two main operations: shifting in a new prediction and loading a new history due to a misprediction. 
The FSM will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock (clk) and asynchronous reset (areset). 
When areset is asserted, the FSM will reset the branch history to zero. 
When predict_valid is asserted, the FSM will shift in the predicted branch direction (predict_taken) from the least significant bit (LSB) side. 
When train_mispredicted is asserted, the FSM will load the branch history with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch, which is the train_history concatenated with the actual result of the branch (train_taken). 
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are asserted at the same time, the FSM will prioritize the misprediction and load the new history.
