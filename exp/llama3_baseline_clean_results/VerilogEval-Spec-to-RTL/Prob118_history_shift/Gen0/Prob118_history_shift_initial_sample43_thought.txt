To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit shift register to keep track of the branch history. When a prediction is made, we will shift in the predicted direction from the LSB side. When a misprediction occurs, we will load the register with the history before the mispredicted branch and the actual result of the branch. To handle the case where both a prediction and a misprediction occur at the same time, we will prioritize the misprediction by checking its condition first. We will also include an asynchronous reset to reset the history counter to zero when areset is high.
