To implement the TopModule, we will create a 32-bit global history shift register. 
When a branch prediction is made (predict_valid = 1), we shift in predict_taken from the LSB side to update the branch history for the predicted branch.
In case of a branch misprediction (train_mispredicted = 1), we load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch.
We will use a reset signal (areset) to asynchronously reset the history counter to zero on its positive edge.
We will utilize a combinational logic to determine whether a prediction or misprediction occurs, and then update the history accordingly on the positive edge of the clock.
To prioritize the misprediction over the prediction, we will check the train_mispredicted signal first, and only update based on predict_valid if train_mispredicted is 0.
