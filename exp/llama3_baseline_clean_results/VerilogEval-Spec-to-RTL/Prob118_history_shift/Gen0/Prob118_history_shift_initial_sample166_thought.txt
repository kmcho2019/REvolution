To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the global history shift register. We will have two main operations: shifting in the predicted branch direction when a prediction is made, and loading the history register with the corrected history when a misprediction occurs. We will use the predict_valid signal to determine when to shift in the predicted branch direction, and the train_mispredicted signal to determine when to load the corrected history. If both a prediction and a misprediction occur at the same time, we will prioritize the misprediction by checking the train_mispredicted signal first.
