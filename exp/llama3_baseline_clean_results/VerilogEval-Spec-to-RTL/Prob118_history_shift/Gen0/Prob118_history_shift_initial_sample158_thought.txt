To implement the TopModule, we'll use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. The register will be updated based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals. When predict_valid is high, we shift the register to the left by one bit and fill the least significant bit with predict_taken. When train_mispredicted is high, we load the register with the train_history concatenated with train_taken. We'll use a priority encoder-like approach to ensure that when both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are high, the misprediction takes precedence. The areset signal will be used to reset the register to zero asynchronously.
