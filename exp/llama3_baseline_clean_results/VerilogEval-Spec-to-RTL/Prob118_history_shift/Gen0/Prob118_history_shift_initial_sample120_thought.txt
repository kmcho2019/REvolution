To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. The register will be updated based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals. When predict_valid is high, the register will be shifted left by one bit and the predict_taken signal will be appended to the least significant bit. When train_mispredicted is high, the register will be loaded with the concatenated value of train_history and train_taken. To handle the case where both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are high at the same time, we will give priority to train_mispredicted and update the register accordingly. The areset signal will be used to asynchronously reset the register to zero.
