The TopModule will contain a 32-bit register to hold the branch history. 
It will use an if-else statement within a clocked always block to determine 
whether to shift in a new prediction, load a new history after a misprediction, 
or do nothing. The asynchronous reset will be used to reset the history 
register when areset is asserted. 
To ensure that the misprediction takes precedence over the prediction when 
both occur at the same time, we will check for misprediction before checking 
for prediction within the if-else statement.
