To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. 
The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted inputs. 
When predict_valid is 1, we shift in the predict_taken bit from the LSB side to update the branch history. 
When train_mispredicted is 1, we load the register with the train_history concatenated with the train_taken bit. 
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are 1 at the same time, we prioritize the train_mispredicted input. 
The areset input will asynchronously reset the register to zero.
