The TopModule will implement a 32-bit global history shift register. 
It will have a predict_history register to store the current branch history. 
When a prediction is made, the predict_history will be updated by shifting 
in the predict_taken value from the LSB side. When a misprediction occurs, 
the predict_history will be loaded with the train_history concatenated 
with the train_taken value. If both prediction and misprediction occur 
at the same time, the misprediction will take precedence. 
The areset signal will reset the predict_history to zero.
