The TopModule will use a 32-bit register to store the global history shift register. 
When predict_valid is 1, the register will shift in the predict_taken bit from the LSB side. 
When train_mispredicted is 1, the register will load the train_history concatenated with train_taken. 
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are 1, the train_mispredicted will take precedence and load the register. 
The register will be reset to 0 when areset is asserted. 
The predict_history output will always reflect the current state of the register.
