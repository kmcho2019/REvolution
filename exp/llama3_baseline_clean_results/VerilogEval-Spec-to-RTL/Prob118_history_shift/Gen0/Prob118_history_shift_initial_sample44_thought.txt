The module TopModule will be designed with a 32-bit register to hold the global history shift register. 
It will have two main operations: 
1. Update the history when a prediction is made (predict_valid = 1) by shifting in the predict_taken value.
2. Load the history when a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted = 1) with the train_history and the actual result of the branch (train_taken).
The module will prioritize the misprediction operation when both prediction and misprediction occur simultaneously.
Asynchronous reset (areset) will be used to reset the history counter to zero.
