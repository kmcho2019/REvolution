To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. 
We will also use two conditional statements to update the branch history register based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals.
When predict_valid is high, we shift in the predict_taken bit from the LSB side to update the branch history.
When train_mispredicted is high, we load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch.
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are high at the same time, we prioritize the misprediction and load the branch history register accordingly.
We also add an asynchronous reset to clear the branch history register when areset is high.
