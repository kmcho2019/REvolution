The TopModule will implement a 32-bit global history shift register. The 
register will be updated based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted 
signals. When predict_valid is high, the register will be shifted to the left 
and the predict_taken bit will be shifted in from the LSB side. When 
train_mispredicted is high, the register will be loaded with the train_history 
concatenated with the train_taken bit. If both predict_valid and 
train_mispredicted are high, the train_mispredicted signal will take precedence. 
The register will be reset to zero when areset is high. The predict_history 
output will always reflect the current state of the branch history register.
