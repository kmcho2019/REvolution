To implement the TopModule, we need to create a 32-bit global history shift register that updates based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals. 
We'll use a reg to store the history and update it on the positive edge of the clock.
When predict_valid is 1, we shift in predict_taken from the LSB side to update the branch history for the predicted branch.
When train_mispredicted is 1, we load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch.
If both predict_valid and train_mispredicted are 1, train_mispredicted takes precedence.
We'll use an if-else statement to check the conditions and update the history accordingly.
The areset signal will reset the history to zero asynchronously.
