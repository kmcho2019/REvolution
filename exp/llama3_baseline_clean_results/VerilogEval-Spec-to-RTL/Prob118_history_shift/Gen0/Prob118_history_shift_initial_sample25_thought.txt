To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit shift register to store the branch history. On each clock cycle, we will check the inputs to determine whether to shift in a new prediction, load a new history due to a misprediction, or hold the current state. We will use the predict_valid and predict_taken signals to update the history when a prediction is made, and the train_mispredicted, train_history, and train_taken signals to load a new history when a misprediction occurs. If both a prediction and a misprediction occur at the same time, we will prioritize the misprediction and load the new history. We will also include an asynchronous reset to clear the history register when areset is asserted.
