To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. 
When a prediction is made, we will shift in the predicted branch direction from the LSB side. 
When a misprediction occurs, we will load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch. 
We will use a conditional statement to check the priority of prediction and misprediction, and update the branch history accordingly. 
We will also implement an asynchronous reset to reset the branch history register to zero when areset is high.
