To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit register to store the branch history. We will have two main operations: shift and load. The shift operation will be performed when a branch prediction is made, and it will shift in the predict_taken value from the LSB side. The load operation will be performed when a branch misprediction occurs, and it will load the branch history register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch. We will use the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals to determine which operation to perform. If both signals are high, the misprediction will take precedence. We will also use the areset signal to reset the history register to zero.
