To implement the TopModule, we will use a 32-bit shift register to store the branch history. We will use the predict_valid signal to shift in the predict_taken value into the LSB side of the register. When a misprediction occurs (train_mispredicted = 1), we will load the register with the history after the completion of the mispredicted branch, which is the train_history concatenated with the train_taken value. We will use the areset signal to asynchronously reset the history register to zero. We will use a sequential logic approach, where all operations are triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
