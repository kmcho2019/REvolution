To implement the Moore state machine described, we'll use two states, OFF and ON, with output out dependent on the current state. We'll use a 1-bit variable, current_state, to keep track of the state, and a 1-bit variable, next_state, to store the next state based on the inputs j and k. The module will have two main parts: state registration (update current_state with next_state on every clock cycle) and next state logic (determine next_state based on current_state and inputs j and k).
