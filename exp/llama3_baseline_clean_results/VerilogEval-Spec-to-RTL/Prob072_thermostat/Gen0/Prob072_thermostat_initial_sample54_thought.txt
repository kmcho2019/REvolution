To implement the thermostat controller module TopModule, we will use simple logical operations based on the input signals. 
The module will have two main operating modes: heating and cooling, determined by the mode input. 
In heating mode, we will check the too_cold signal and turn the heater on if it's too cold. 
In cooling mode, we will check the too_hot signal and turn the air conditioner on if it's too hot. 
We will also turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is active.
We will use Verilog's logical operators (AND, OR) to implement these conditions and assign the correct values to the heater, aircon, and fan output signals.
