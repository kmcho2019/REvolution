To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of conditional statements to control the outputs based on the inputs. We will first check the mode to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. Then, based on the mode, we will check the too_cold or too_hot signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We will also use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan independently of the heater and air conditioner. The fan will also be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on.
