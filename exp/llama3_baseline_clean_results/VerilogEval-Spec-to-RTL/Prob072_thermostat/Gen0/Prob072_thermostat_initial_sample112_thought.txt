The TopModule will be designed as a combinational logic circuit, with the output values determined solely by the current input values. We will use conditional statements to determine when to turn on the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature, and fan_on inputs. 
We will use the following logic:
- When in heating mode (mode = 1), turn on the heater if it's too cold, and turn off the air conditioner. Turn on the fan if the heater is on or if the user requests it.
- When in cooling mode (mode = 0), turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot, and turn off the heater. Turn on the fan if the air conditioner is on or if the user requests it.
