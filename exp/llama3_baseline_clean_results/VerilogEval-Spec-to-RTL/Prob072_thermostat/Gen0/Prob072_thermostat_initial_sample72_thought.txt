To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes (heating and cooling) and control the heater, air conditioner, and fan accordingly. We can use combinational logic to determine when to turn on/off each component based on the input signals. 
In heating mode, we turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn off the heater. The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on.
We can use simple logic gates and conditional statements in Verilog to implement this functionality.
