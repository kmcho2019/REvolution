To implement the thermostat controller, we need to first determine the mode of operation (heating or cooling) based on the 'mode' input. Then, we need to check the temperature conditions ('too_cold' or 'too_hot') to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. Additionally, we need to turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it. We can use simple conditional statements to implement this logic in Verilog.
