To implement the thermostat controller, we can use a combination of conditional statements to control the outputs based on the input modes and conditions. The module will have two main modes: heating and cooling, determined by the 'mode' input. Depending on the mode, it will check the 'too_cold' or 'too_hot' inputs to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner, respectively. Additionally, the fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the 'fan_on' input is asserted.
