To implement the TopModule, we will use conditional logic to determine when to turn on/off the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode and input signals. We will use if-else statements to check the mode and then evaluate the too_cold and too_hot signals accordingly. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it. We will use a simple combinational logic to implement this controller.
