To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, we need to turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode, we need to turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn off the heater. Additionally, we need to turn on the fan when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on. We can achieve this using conditional statements to check the mode, temperature, and fan request, and then assign the corresponding values to the heater, aircon, and fan outputs.
