To implement the thermostat controller, we can use a simple combinational logic approach. We will use the mode signal to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. Based on this mode, we will check the too_cold or too_hot signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We will also use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan regardless of the heater or air conditioner status. Additionally, if the heater or air conditioner is on, we will also turn on the fan.
