The TopModule will implement a simple thermostat controller using Verilog. 
It will have two modes: heating and cooling, which are controlled by the mode input. 
Based on the mode, temperature, and fan request, it will control the heater, air conditioner, and fan. 
The logic can be broken down into the following steps: 
1. Determine if the heater or air conditioner should be turned on based on the mode and temperature. 
2. Turn on the fan if the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests the fan to be on.
