To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the two modes of operation (heating and cooling) and the conditions under which the heater, air conditioner, and fan should be turned on. We can use combinational logic to generate the output signals based on the input signals. For the heater, it should be turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it's too cold. For the air conditioner, it should be turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it's too hot. The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be turned on.
