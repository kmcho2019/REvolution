The TopModule can be implemented as a simple combinational logic circuit. 
The module will have two main conditional blocks based on the mode of operation (heating or cooling). 
In heating mode, the heater will be turned on if it's too cold and the fan will be turned on if the heater is on or if the user requests it.
In cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on if it's too hot and the fan will be turned on if the air conditioner is on or if the user requests it.
The module will also ensure that the air conditioner is not turned on in heating mode and the heater is not turned on in cooling mode.
