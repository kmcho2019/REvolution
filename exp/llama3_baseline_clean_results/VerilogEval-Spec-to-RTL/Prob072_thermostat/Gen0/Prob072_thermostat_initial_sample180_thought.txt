The TopModule can be implemented by using simple combinational logic. We will first determine when the heater or air conditioner should be turned on based on the mode and temperature conditions. Then, we will control the fan based on whether the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user has requested the fan to turn on. We will use the mode input to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode, and then use the too_cold or too_hot signals accordingly.
