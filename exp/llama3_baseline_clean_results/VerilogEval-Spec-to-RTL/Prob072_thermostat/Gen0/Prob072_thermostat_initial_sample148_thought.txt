To implement the thermostat controller, we will use a combination of conditional statements to determine when to turn on the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode and temperature inputs. We will use the mode input to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode, and then use the too_cold and too_hot inputs to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it to be turned on through the fan_on input.
