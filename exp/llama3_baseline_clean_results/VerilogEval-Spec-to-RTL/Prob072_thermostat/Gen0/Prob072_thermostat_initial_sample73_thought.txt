The TopModule should first determine the current mode (heating or cooling) based on the mode input. 
In heating mode, it should check the too_cold signal and turn on the heater if it's too cold. 
In cooling mode, it should check the too_hot signal and turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot. 
The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is high. 
The air conditioner should not be used in heating mode, and the heater should not be used in cooling mode.
