To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In each mode, we need to check the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot) to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We also need to consider the fan_on input to turn on the fan regardless of the heater and air conditioner status. The fan should also be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on. We can use simple conditional statements to implement this logic in Verilog.
