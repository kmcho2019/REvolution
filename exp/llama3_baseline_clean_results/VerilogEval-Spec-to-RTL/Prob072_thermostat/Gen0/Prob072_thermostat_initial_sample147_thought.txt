The TopModule will use conditional statements to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. The module will first check the mode to decide whether to use the heater or air conditioner. Then, it will check the too_cold or too_hot signals to determine if the heater or air conditioner should be turned on. Finally, it will check the fan_on signal to see if the fan should be turned on regardless of the heater and air conditioner states. The fan will also be turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on.
