To implement the thermostat controller, we need to use a combination of conditional statements and logical operators to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. The mode signal will be used to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. The too_cold and too_hot signals will be used to determine whether the heater or air conditioner should be turned on, respectively. The fan_on signal will be used to turn on the fan regardless of the state of the heater and air conditioner. The fan will also be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on.
