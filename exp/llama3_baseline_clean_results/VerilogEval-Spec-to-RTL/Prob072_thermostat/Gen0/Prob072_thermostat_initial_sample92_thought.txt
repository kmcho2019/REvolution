The thermostat controller will be implemented as a combinational logic circuit. 
The heater output will be dependent on the mode and too_cold inputs, and will be 
high only when the mode is 1 (heating) and it's too cold. The aircon output will 
be dependent on the mode and too_hot inputs, and will be high only when the mode 
is 0 (cooling) and it's too hot. The fan output will be high whenever the heater 
or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high.
