The module TopModule will be implemented with a combination of conditional statements to control the heater, aircon, and fan based on the mode, temperature, and fan_on signals. 

The heater will be turned on when the mode is 1 (heating mode) and it's too cold, while the aircon will be turned on when the mode is 0 (cooling mode) and it's too hot. 

The fan will be turned on when either the heater or aircon is on, or when the fan_on signal is 1.
