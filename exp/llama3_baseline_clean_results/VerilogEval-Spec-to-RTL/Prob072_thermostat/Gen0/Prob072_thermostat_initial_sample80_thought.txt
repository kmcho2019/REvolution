The TopModule will use a combination of conditional statements to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode and input signals. In heating mode (mode = 1), it will turn on the heater if it's too cold and turn on the fan if the heater is on or if the fan_on signal is high. In cooling mode (mode = 0), it will turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot and turn on the fan if the air conditioner is on or if the fan_on signal is high. The fan will be turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the fan_on signal is high.
