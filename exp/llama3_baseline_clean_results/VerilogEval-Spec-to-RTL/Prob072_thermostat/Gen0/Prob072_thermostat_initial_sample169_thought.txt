To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), we turn on the heater when it is too cold (too_cold = 1) and turn off the air conditioner.
In cooling mode (mode = 0), we turn on the air conditioner when it is too hot (too_hot = 1) and turn off the heater.
We also need to consider the fan control: turn on the fan when the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it (fan_on = 1).
We can use a combination of conditional statements and logical operators to implement this behavior in Verilog.
