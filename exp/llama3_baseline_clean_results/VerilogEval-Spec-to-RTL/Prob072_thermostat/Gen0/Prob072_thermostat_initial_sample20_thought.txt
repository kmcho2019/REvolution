To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the mode of operation and the current temperature status. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), we turn on the heater if it's too cold (too_cold = 1) and turn off the air conditioner.
In cooling mode (mode = 0), we turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot (too_hot = 1) and turn off the heater.
The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it (fan_on = 1).
We can use logical operators to implement these conditions in Verilog.
