To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, we turn on the heater when it's too cold and the fan when the heater is on or the fan is requested to be on. In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and the fan when the air conditioner is on or the fan is requested to be on. We use combinational logic to achieve this, as the outputs depend directly on the current state of the inputs.
