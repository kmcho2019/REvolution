To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the mode of operation and the status of the temperature (too_cold or too_hot). Based on these conditions, we can determine whether the heater, air conditioner, or fan should be turned on or off. The module will have three output signals: heater, aircon, and fan. The logic for these signals can be derived from the input signals mode, too_cold, too_hot, and fan_on. The heater will be on when the mode is heating (mode = 1) and it is too cold (too_cold = 1). The air conditioner will be on when the mode is cooling (mode = 0) and it is too hot (too_hot = 1). The fan will be on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is asserted.
