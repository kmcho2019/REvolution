To implement the thermostat controller, we need to first check the mode of the thermostat. If it's in heating mode, we need to turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn on the fan when the heater is on or when the user requests it. If it's in cooling mode, we need to turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn on the fan when the air conditioner is on or when the user requests it. We also need to make sure that the heater and air conditioner are not on at the same time.
