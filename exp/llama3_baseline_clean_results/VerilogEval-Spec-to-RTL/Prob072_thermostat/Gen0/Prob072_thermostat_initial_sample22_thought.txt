The TopModule should implement a state machine that checks the mode of operation and the temperature conditions to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan. 
In heating mode, the heater should be turned on when it's too cold, and the air conditioner should be off. 
In cooling mode, the air conditioner should be turned on when it's too hot, and the heater should be off. 
The fan should be turned on when the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on. 
This logic can be implemented using simple conditional statements in Verilog.
