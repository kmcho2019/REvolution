To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of conditional statements to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. We will first check the mode to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. Then, we will check the temperature signals (too_cold or too_hot) to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. Finally, we will check the fan_on signal to determine whether to turn on the fan independently of the heater and air conditioner.
