To implement the thermostat controller, we can use a combination of logical conditions to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode and temperature conditions. We will use if-else statements to check the mode and then the temperature conditions to decide the states of the heater and air conditioner. The fan's state will be determined based on whether the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user has requested the fan to be on.
