The TopModule will be implemented as a simple combinational logic circuit. 
The heater will be turned on when the mode is set to heating (mode = 1) and it's too cold (too_cold = 1). 
The air conditioner will be turned on when the mode is set to cooling (mode = 0) and it's too hot (too_hot = 1). 
The fan will be turned on when either the heater or the air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to turn on (fan_on = 1).
