To implement the thermostat controller, we will first determine the mode of operation (heating or cooling) based on the input 'mode'. Then, we will check the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot) and turn on the corresponding heating/cooling unit (heater or aircon) accordingly. We will also turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it by setting 'fan_on' to 1.
