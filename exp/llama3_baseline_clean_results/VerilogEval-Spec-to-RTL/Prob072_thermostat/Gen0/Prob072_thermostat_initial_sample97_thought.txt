To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the mode of operation and the current temperature conditions. We can use a simple if-else statement to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. In heating mode, we turn on the heater if it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot and turn off the heater. The fan is turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it to be on.
