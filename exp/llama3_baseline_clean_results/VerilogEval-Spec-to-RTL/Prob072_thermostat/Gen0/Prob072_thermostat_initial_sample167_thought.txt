To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the current mode (heating or cooling) and the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot) to determine whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We also need to turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it. This can be achieved using a combination of conditional statements and logical operations. We can use the mode signal to determine whether to check for too_cold or too_hot, and then use the corresponding signal to control the heater or air conditioner. The fan control can be implemented using a simple OR operation that includes the fan_on signal and the status of the heater and air conditioner.
