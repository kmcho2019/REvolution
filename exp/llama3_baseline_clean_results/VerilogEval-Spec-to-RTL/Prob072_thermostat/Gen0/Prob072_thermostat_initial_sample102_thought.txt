To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the thermostat's mode and the temperature conditions to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan. 
We'll use if-else statements to check the mode and temperature conditions, and then assign the corresponding values to the heater, air conditioner, and fan outputs.
In heating mode, we'll turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner.
In cooling mode, we'll turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn off the heater.
We'll also turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on.
