The TopModule will control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode and temperature inputs. In heating mode, the heater will be turned on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on. This can be achieved using a combination of conditional statements and logical operators.
