To implement the TopModule, we need to follow these steps:
1. Determine the current mode of the thermostat (heating or cooling) based on the mode input.
2. Based on the mode, check the temperature status (too_cold or too_hot) to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner.
3. Turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on.
We will use a combination of conditional statements and logical operations to achieve this in the Verilog code.
