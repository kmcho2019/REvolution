To solve this problem, I will create a TopModule that implements a heating/cooling thermostat controller. The module will use the mode input to determine whether it is in heating or cooling mode. Based on the mode, it will check the too_cold or too_hot input to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. The fan will be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high. I will use a combination of logical operations to implement this functionality.
