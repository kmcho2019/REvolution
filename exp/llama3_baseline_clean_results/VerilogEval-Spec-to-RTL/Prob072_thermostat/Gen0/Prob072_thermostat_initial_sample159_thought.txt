To implement the thermostat controller, we will use a simple combinational logic circuit. The heater will be turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it's too cold. The air conditioner will be turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it's too hot. The fan will be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on. We will use logical AND and OR operators to implement these conditions.
