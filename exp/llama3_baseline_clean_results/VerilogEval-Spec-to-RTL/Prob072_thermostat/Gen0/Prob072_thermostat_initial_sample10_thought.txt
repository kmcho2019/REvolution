To implement the TopModule, we need to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature conditions (too_cold, too_hot), and the fan_on input. 
The module will have two modes: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), the heater will be turned on when it's too cold, and the air conditioner will be off. 
In cooling mode (mode = 0), the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot, and the heater will be off. 
The fan will be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high.
We will use simple logical operations (AND, OR) to implement these conditions.
