To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the mode of the thermostat and the temperature conditions to determine when to turn on the heater, air conditioner, and fan. We will use simple conditional statements to achieve this. In heating mode, we will turn on the heater if it is too cold and turn on the fan if the heater is on or if the fan is requested to be on. In cooling mode, we will turn on the air conditioner if it is too hot and turn on the fan if the air conditioner is on or if the fan is requested to be on.
