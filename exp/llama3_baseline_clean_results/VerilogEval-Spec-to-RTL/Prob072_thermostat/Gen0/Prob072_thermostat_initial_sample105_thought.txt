To implement the TopModule, we need to follow the given rules for controlling the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature, and fan request. 
First, we check the mode to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), we turn on the heater if it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. 
In cooling mode (mode = 0), we turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot and turn off the heater. 
We also need to turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on.
