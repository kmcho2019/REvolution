The TopModule should be designed to handle two main modes: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode, the heater is turned on when it's too cold and the fan is also turned on for circulation. 
In cooling mode, the air conditioner is turned on when it's too hot and the fan is also turned on for circulation. 
Additionally, the fan can be turned on independently by setting fan_on to 1, regardless of the mode or the status of the heater and air conditioner.
This can be achieved using a combination of logical operations to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals.
