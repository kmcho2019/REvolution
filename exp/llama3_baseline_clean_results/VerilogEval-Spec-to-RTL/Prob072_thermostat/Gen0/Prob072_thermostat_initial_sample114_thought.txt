To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes (heating and cooling) and the fan_on input. 
In heating mode, we turn on the heater when it's too cold and keep the air conditioner off. 
In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and keep the heater off. 
The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high. 
This can be achieved using combinational logic, specifically using if-else statements or logical operators in Verilog.
