The TopModule should be designed to control the heating and cooling system based on the input mode and temperature conditions. It should have a conditional logic to determine when to turn on the heater, air conditioner, and fan. The heater should be turned on when the mode is 1 (heating) and it's too cold, while the air conditioner should be turned on when the mode is 0 (cooling) and it's too hot. The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on.
