The module TopModule can be implemented using a set of conditional statements to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. 
First, we check the mode signal to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), we turn on the heater when it's too cold (too_cold = 1) and turn it off otherwise. We keep the air conditioner off in this mode.
In cooling mode (mode = 0), we turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot (too_hot = 1) and turn it off otherwise. We keep the heater off in this mode.
We then check if the heater or air conditioner is on, and if so, we turn on the fan. 
Additionally, if the user requests the fan to turn on (fan_on = 1), we override the previous condition and turn on the fan.
