The TopModule will use a simple combinational logic to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. It will first determine whether the heater or air conditioner should be turned on based on the mode and temperature signals. Then, it will control the fan based on whether the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the fan_on signal is asserted. The module will use basic logical operations such as AND and OR to implement the desired behavior.
