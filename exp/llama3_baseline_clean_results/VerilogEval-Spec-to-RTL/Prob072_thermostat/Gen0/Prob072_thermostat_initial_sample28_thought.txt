To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, we turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn off the heater. Additionally, we need to turn on the fan when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be turned on. We can achieve this using simple logic gates and combinational logic.
