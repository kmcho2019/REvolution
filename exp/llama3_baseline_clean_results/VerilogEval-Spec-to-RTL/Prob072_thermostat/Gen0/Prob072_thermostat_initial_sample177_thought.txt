To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation (heating and cooling) and their respective temperature conditions. We will use if-else statements to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature conditions, and the fan_on input. In heating mode, the heater will be turned on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot. The fan will be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is asserted.
