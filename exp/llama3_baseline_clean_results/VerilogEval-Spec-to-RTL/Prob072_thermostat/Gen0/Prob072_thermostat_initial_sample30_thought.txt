To implement the thermostat controller, we need to use the input mode to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. Based on this mode, we then check the too_cold or too_hot signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We also need to ensure that the fan is turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, as well as when the fan_on signal is asserted. This can be achieved using simple logical operations.
