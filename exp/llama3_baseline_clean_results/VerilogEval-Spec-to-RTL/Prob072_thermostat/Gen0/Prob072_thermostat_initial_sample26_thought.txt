The TopModule can be implemented using a combination of conditional statements to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, too_cold, too_hot, and fan_on inputs. 
The module should first determine the mode of operation (heating or cooling) and then check the corresponding temperature condition (too_cold or too_hot). 
Based on these conditions, the module can then control the heater and air conditioner. 
The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high.
