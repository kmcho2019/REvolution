The TopModule can be designed using simple conditional logic to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input mode, temperature sensors, and the fan request. The module will use if-else statements to determine the state of each output signal. In heating mode, the heater is turned on if it's too cold, and the air conditioner is always off. In cooling mode, the air conditioner is turned on if it's too hot, and the heater is always off. The fan is turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it.
