The module TopModule should implement a simple thermostat controller with two modes: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode, the heater should be turned on when it's too cold and the fan should be turned on when the heater is on or when the user requests it.
In cooling mode, the air conditioner should be turned on when it's too hot and the fan should be turned on when the air conditioner is on or when the user requests it.
The module should use combinational logic to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals.
