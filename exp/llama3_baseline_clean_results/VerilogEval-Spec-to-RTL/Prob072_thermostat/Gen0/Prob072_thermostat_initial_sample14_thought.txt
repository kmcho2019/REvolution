To implement the thermostat controller, we will use a simple combinational logic approach. The module will have two main modes: heating and cooling. In heating mode, the heater will be turned on if it is too cold, and the air conditioner will be turned off. In cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on if it is too hot, and the heater will be turned off. The fan will be turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests the fan to be turned on.

We will use the following logic to control the outputs:
- heater: mode = 1 and too_cold = 1
- aircon: mode = 0 and too_hot = 1
- fan: (mode = 1 and too_cold = 1) or (mode = 0 and too_hot = 1) or fan_on = 1
