To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the mode and the temperature conditions. In heating mode, the heater should be turned on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner should be turned on when it's too hot. The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on. We can use simple conditional statements to implement this logic in Verilog.
