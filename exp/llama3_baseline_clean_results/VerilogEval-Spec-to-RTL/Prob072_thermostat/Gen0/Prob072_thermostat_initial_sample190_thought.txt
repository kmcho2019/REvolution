The module TopModule will be designed as a simple state machine with two main states: heating and cooling. The state will be determined by the input mode. Depending on the state and the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot), the module will control the heater, aircon, and fan outputs. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high. This can be achieved using simple combinatorial logic in Verilog.
