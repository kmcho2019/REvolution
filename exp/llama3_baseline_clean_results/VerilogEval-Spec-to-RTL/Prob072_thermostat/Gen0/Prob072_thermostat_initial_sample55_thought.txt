The TopModule can be designed using simple logical operations. 
We first determine the state of the heater and air conditioner based on the mode and temperature conditions.
The heater is turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it is too cold.
The air conditioner is turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it is too hot.
The fan is turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be turned on.
