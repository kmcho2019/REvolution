To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. 
We will use if-else statements to check the mode and the temperature conditions to determine the output of the heater, aircon, and fan. 
In heating mode, the heater will be turned on if it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on if it's too hot. 
The fan will be turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it to be on.
