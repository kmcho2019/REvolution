The thermostat controller module, TopModule, needs to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input mode, temperature conditions, and fan_on request. 
The module should have two distinct modes of operation: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), the heater should be turned on when it's too cold, and the air conditioner should remain off. 
In cooling mode (mode = 0), the air conditioner should be turned on when it's too hot, and the heater should remain off. 
The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on request is made.
We can implement the logic using if-else statements or combinational logic to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input conditions.
