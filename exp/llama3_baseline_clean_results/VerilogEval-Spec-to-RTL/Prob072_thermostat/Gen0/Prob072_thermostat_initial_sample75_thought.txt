To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the different modes of operation (heating and cooling) and the conditions that determine whether the heater, air conditioner, and fan should be turned on or off. We can use logical operators to combine the input signals and generate the output signals. Specifically, we will use the mode signal to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode, and then use the too_cold and too_hot signals to control the heater and air conditioner, respectively. We will also use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan independently of the heater and air conditioner.
