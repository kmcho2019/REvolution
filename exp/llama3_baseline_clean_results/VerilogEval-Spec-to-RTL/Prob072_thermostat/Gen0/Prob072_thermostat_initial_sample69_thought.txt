The thermostat controller can be implemented using a simple state machine or combinational logic. The module will have two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, the heater will be turned on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be turned on. This can be achieved using logical operations such as AND and OR.
