The TopModule will implement a thermostat controller that can operate in two modes: heating and cooling. 
The module will use the mode input to determine whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner based on the too_cold and too_hot inputs. 
Additionally, the module will turn on the fan whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be turned on.
The logic for controlling the heater, air conditioner, and fan can be implemented using simple conditional statements in Verilog.
