To implement the thermostat controller, we can use simple conditional statements to control the outputs based on the inputs. We will use the mode input to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode, and then use the too_cold and too_hot inputs to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner, respectively. We will also use the fan_on input to control the fan output. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high.
