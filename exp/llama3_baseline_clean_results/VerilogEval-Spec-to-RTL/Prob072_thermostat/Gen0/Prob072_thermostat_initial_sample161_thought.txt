To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of conditional statements and logical operators to control the outputs based on the inputs. We will first determine the mode of the thermostat and then use the too_cold and too_hot signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We will also use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan independently. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is high.
