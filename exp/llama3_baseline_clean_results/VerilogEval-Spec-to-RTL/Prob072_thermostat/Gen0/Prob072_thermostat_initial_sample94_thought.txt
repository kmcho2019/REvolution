The TopModule can be implemented using a combination of logical operations to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. The module will first check the mode to determine whether it is in heating or cooling mode. Then, it will check the too_cold or too_hot signals to determine if the heater or air conditioner should be turned on. Finally, it will check the fan_on signal to determine if the fan should be turned on, regardless of the state of the heater and air conditioner. The output signals can be generated using a combination of AND and OR operations.
