To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the different modes and conditions. 
We will use if-else statements to check the mode and the temperature conditions, 
and then assign the outputs accordingly. The heater will be on when it's too cold in heating mode, 
the air conditioner will be on when it's too hot in cooling mode. The fan will be on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on.
