To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, we need to turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn on the fan if the heater is on or if the fan is requested to be on. In cooling mode, we need to turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn on the fan if the air conditioner is on or if the fan is requested to be on. We can use conditional statements to implement these rules.
