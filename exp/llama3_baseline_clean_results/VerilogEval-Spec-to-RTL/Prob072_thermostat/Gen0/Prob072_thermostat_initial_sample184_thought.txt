To implement the TopModule, we need to create a control logic that checks the mode of operation and the temperature status to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan accordingly. We can use a combination of if-else statements and logical operators to achieve this. The heater should be turned on when the mode is heating and it's too cold, the air conditioner should be turned on when the mode is cooling and it's too hot. The fan should be turned on when the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on. We can use the following steps:
1. Check the mode of operation and set the heater and air conditioner accordingly.
2. Check the temperature status and turn on/off the heater or air conditioner.
3. Check the fan request and turn on the fan if requested or if the heater or air conditioner is on.
