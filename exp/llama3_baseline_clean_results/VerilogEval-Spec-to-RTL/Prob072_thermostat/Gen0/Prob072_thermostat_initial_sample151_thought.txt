To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the mode of operation (heating or cooling) and the temperature status (too_cold or too_hot) to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan. We can use combinational logic to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. The heater should be on when the mode is heating and it's too cold. The air conditioner should be on when the mode is cooling and it's too hot. The fan should be on when the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on.
