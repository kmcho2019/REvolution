The TopModule will be designed as a combinational logic circuit that takes into account the mode, temperature conditions (too_cold, too_hot), and the fan_on input. The heater and air conditioner outputs will be determined based on the mode and temperature conditions. The fan output will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on input is high. This can be achieved by using logical operators (AND, OR) to implement the required functionality.
