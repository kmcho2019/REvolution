The thermostat controller module (TopModule) should implement the logic to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature, and fan request. The module should check the mode and temperature to determine whether the heater or air conditioner should be turned on. The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is asserted. The logic for the module can be broken down into the following steps: 
1. Determine whether it is heating or cooling mode.
2. Check the temperature (too_cold or too_hot) to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner.
3. Turn on the fan when the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is asserted.
The Verilog implementation should use if-else statements and logical operators to implement the above logic.
