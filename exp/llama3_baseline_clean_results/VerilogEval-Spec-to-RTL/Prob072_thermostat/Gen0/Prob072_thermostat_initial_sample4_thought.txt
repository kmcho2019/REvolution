To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple combinational logic approach. 
We will first determine if the heater or air conditioner should be turned on based on the mode and temperature inputs. 
Then, we will determine if the fan should be turned on based on whether the heater or air conditioner are on, or if the user has requested the fan to be turned on. 
This approach ensures that the thermostat controller functions as expected in both heating and cooling modes.
