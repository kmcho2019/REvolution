The thermostat controller module TopModule will have two main modes of operation: heating and cooling. 
In heating mode (mode = 1), the heater will be turned on when it's too cold (too_cold = 1) and the air conditioner will be off. 
In cooling mode (mode = 0), the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot (too_hot = 1) and the heater will be off. 
The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it to be on (fan_on = 1).
