To implement the TopModule, we will first use the mode input to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. Based on this mode, we will then check the too_cold or too_hot signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. We will also use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan regardless of the state of the heater and air conditioner. We will then use a combination of these signals to control the heater, air conditioner, and fan outputs.
