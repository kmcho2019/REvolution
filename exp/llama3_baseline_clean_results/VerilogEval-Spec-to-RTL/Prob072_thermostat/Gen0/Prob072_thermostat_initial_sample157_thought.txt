The TopModule should implement a thermostat controller that can switch between heating and cooling modes. 
In heating mode, it should turn the heater on when it's too cold and turn the fan on when the heater is on or the fan is requested to be on. 
In cooling mode, it should turn the air conditioner on when it's too hot and also turn the fan on when the air conditioner is on or the fan is requested to be on. 
The module should always turn the fan on when the fan_on input is high, regardless of the mode or the temperatures.
