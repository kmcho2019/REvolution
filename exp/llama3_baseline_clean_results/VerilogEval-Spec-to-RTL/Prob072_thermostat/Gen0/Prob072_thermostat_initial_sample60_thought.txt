To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of logical operators to determine the states of the heater, aircon, and fan based on the input signals. We will first check the mode of the thermostat to decide whether to use the heater or air conditioner. Then, we will check the too_cold and too_hot signals to determine if the corresponding heating or cooling system should be turned on. Finally, we will use the fan_on signal to decide whether to turn on the fan, regardless of the states of the heater and air conditioner.
