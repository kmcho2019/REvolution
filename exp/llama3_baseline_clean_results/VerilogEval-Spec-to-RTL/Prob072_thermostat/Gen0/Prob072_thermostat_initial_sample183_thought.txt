The TopModule will use conditional statements to determine the state of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. The mode signal will be used to decide whether the thermostat is in heating or cooling mode. The too_cold and too_hot signals will be used to determine when to turn on the heater and air conditioner, respectively. The fan_on signal will be used to turn on the fan when requested by the user. The fan will also be turned on when the heater or air conditioner is on.
