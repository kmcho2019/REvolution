The module TopModule will be implemented as a combinational logic circuit. It will use the mode signal to determine whether the thermostat is in heating or cooling mode. Based on the mode, it will check the too_cold or too_hot signals to determine whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is asserted. The module will use logical operators to implement these conditions.
