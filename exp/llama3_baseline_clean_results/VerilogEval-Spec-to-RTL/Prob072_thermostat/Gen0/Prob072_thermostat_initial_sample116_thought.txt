To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. We will use the mode input to determine which mode the thermostat is in. Then, based on the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot), we will control the heater and air conditioner accordingly. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it (fan_on = 1). We will use simple conditional logic to achieve this.
