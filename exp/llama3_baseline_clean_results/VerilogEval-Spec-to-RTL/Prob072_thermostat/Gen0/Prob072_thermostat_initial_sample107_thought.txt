To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the mode of operation (heating or cooling) and the temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot) to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan. We can use a combination of conditional statements to achieve this. 

First, we check the mode of operation. If it's in heating mode, we check if it's too cold and turn on the heater if necessary. If it's in cooling mode, we check if it's too hot and turn on the air conditioner if necessary. 

Next, we check if either the heater or air conditioner is on, and if so, we also turn on the fan. 

Finally, we check if the user has requested the fan to be turned on, and if so, we override the previous condition and turn on the fan.

This logic can be implemented using simple conditional statements in Verilog.
