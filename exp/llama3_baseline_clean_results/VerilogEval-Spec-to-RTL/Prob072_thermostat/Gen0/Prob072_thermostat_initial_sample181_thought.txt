To implement the TopModule, we will first determine whether the system is in heating or cooling mode based on the 'mode' input. Then, we will check the 'too_cold' and 'too_hot' signals to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner, respectively. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the 'fan_on' signal is high. We will use logical operations to combine these conditions and generate the output signals for the heater, air conditioner, and fan.
