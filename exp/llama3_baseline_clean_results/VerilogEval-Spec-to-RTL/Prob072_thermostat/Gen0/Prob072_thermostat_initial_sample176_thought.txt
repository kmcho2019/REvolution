To implement the thermostat controller, we can use a simple combinational logic circuit. The logic can be broken down into three parts: 
1. Controlling the heater: The heater should be turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it is too cold. 
2. Controlling the air conditioner: The air conditioner should be turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it is too hot. 
3. Controlling the fan: The fan should be turned on when either the heater or the air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to turn on.

We can use Verilog's logical operators and ternary operators to implement this logic.
