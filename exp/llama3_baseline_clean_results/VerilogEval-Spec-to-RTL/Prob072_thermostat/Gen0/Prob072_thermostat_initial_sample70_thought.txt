To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the mode of operation (heating or cooling) and the current temperature status (too hot or too cold) to decide whether to turn on the heater or air conditioner. Additionally, we must ensure that the fan is turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it to be on. This logic can be implemented using a combination of conditional statements and bitwise operations to assign the correct values to the output ports (heater, aircon, fan).
