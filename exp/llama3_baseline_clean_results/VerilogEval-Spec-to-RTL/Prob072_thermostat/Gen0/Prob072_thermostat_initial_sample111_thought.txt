To solve this problem, we need to design a digital circuit that implements the heating/cooling thermostat controller based on the provided interface and functionality. The controller should operate in two modes: heating and cooling. In heating mode, it turns on the heater when it's too cold and leaves the air conditioner off. In cooling mode, it turns on the air conditioner when it's too hot and keeps the heater off. Additionally, the fan should be on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, and also when the user requests the fan to be on. We can implement this using Verilog, utilizing conditional statements (if-else) to control the outputs based on the inputs.
