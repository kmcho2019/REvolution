The TopModule will be designed as a combinational logic circuit, where the outputs (heater, aircon, fan) are determined by the current state of the inputs (mode, too_cold, too_hot, fan_on). We will use basic logical operations such as AND, OR, and NOT to implement the required functionality. The module will have two main modes: heating and cooling, which will be controlled by the mode input. In heating mode, the heater will be turned on when it's too cold, and in cooling mode, the air conditioner will be turned on when it's too hot. The fan will be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests it.
