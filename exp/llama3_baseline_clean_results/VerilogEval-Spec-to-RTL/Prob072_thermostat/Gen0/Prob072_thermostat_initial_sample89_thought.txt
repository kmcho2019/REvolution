To implement the thermostat controller, we'll use a simple combinational logic approach. We'll first determine the state of the heater and air conditioner based on the mode and temperature conditions. Then, we'll control the fan based on the states of the heater, air conditioner, and the fan_on input. We'll use the following logic:

- In heating mode (mode = 1), turn on the heater if it's too cold (too_cold = 1).
- In cooling mode (mode = 0), turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot (too_hot = 1).
- Turn on the fan if the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the fan_on input is 1.
