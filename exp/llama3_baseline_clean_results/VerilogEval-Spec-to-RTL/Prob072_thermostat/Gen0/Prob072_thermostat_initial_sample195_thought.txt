To implement the thermostat controller in Verilog, we can use a simple combinational logic design. The module will have two modes of operation: heating and cooling. We will use the input signals too_cold and too_hot to determine when to turn on the heater or air conditioner, respectively. Additionally, we will use the fan_on signal to turn on the fan even if the heater and air conditioner are off. The output signals heater, aircon, and fan will be controlled based on the input signals and the current mode of operation.
