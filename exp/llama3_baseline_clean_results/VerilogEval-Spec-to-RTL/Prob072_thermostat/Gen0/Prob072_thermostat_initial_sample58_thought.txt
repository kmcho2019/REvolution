The TopModule can be implemented using a combination of conditional statements to determine the states of the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the input signals. 
The heater should be turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it's too cold. 
The air conditioner should be turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it's too hot. 
The fan should be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be turned on.
