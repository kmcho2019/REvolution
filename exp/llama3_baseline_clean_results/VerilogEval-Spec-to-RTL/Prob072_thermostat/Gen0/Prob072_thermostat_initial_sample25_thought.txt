To implement the thermostat controller, we need to consider the two modes (heating and cooling) and the additional control for the fan. In heating mode, we turn on the heater if it's too cold and turn off the air conditioner. In cooling mode, we turn on the air conditioner if it's too hot and turn off the heater. The fan should be turned on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests it to be on. We can use simple logical operations to achieve this.
