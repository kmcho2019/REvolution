The TopModule should first determine the mode (heating or cooling) and then check the temperature conditions. 
In heating mode, if it's too cold, turn on the heater and fan. 
In cooling mode, if it's too hot, turn on the air conditioner and fan. 
Regardless of the mode or temperature conditions, if the user requests the fan to be on, turn it on. 
Finally, ensure that the heater and air conditioner are not on simultaneously.
