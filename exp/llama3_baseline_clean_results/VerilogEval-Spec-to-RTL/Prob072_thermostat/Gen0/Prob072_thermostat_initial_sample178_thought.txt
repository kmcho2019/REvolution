The TopModule should have two main operating modes: heating and cooling, 
determined by the mode input. In heating mode (mode = 1), the heater 
should be turned on when it's too cold, and the fan should be turned on 
to circulate the air. In cooling mode (mode = 0), the air conditioner 
should be turned on when it's too hot, and the fan should be turned on. 
Additionally, the fan should be turned on if fan_on is high, regardless 
of the heater and air conditioner status. This can be achieved using 
simple logical operations based on the input signals.
