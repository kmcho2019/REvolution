The TopModule will be implemented as a simple combinational logic circuit. 
The heater and air conditioner will be controlled based on the mode and 
their respective temperature conditions (too_cold or too_hot). The fan 
will be turned on when either the heater or air conditioner is on, or 
when the user requests the fan to be on (fan_on = 1). This can be 
achieved using basic logical operations such as AND, OR, and NOT.
