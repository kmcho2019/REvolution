The TopModule will be designed as a simple combinational logic circuit. 
It will have two main states: heating and cooling, determined by the mode input. 
In heating mode, if it's too cold, the heater will be turned on, and the fan will be turned on if the heater or the user requests it. 
In cooling mode, if it's too hot, the air conditioner will be turned on, and the fan will be turned on if the air conditioner or the user requests it. 
The module will use basic logical operations such as AND and OR to implement this behavior.
