To implement a one-bit wide, 2-to-1 multiplexer, we can use a simple conditional statement in Verilog. The module will take in three inputs: a, b, and sel. Based on the value of sel, it will output either a or b. When sel is 0, the output will be a, and when sel is 1, the output will be b. This can be achieved using a simple if-else statement or a conditional operator.
