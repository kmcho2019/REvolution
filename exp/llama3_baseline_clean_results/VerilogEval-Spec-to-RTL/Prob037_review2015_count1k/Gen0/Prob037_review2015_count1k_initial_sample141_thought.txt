To implement the TopModule, we need to design a counter that counts from 0 to 999 with a period of 1000 cycles. This means the counter needs to have 10 bits, as 2^10 = 1024, which is the smallest power of 2 that can represent the numbers 0 through 999. The counter should be incremented on every positive edge of the clock, unless the reset signal is high, in which case it should be reset to 0. We will use a synchronous reset, meaning the reset will only be applied on the positive edge of the clock.
