To implement the TopModule, we will design a synchronous counter with a reset functionality. The counter will be designed as a 10-bit register that increments on every positive edge of the clock. When the reset signal is high, the counter will be reset to 0. The counter will count from 0 to 999 and then wrap around to 0 on the next clock cycle. This can be achieved using a simple 10-bit binary counter with a reset.
