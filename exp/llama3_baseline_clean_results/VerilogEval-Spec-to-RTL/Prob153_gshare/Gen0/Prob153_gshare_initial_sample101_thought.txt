To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) that maintains a 7-bit global branch history register and a 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters. We will use a 7-bit PC and 7-bit global history, hashed (using xor) into a 7-bit index to access the table. The FSM will have two main states: predict and train. In the predict state, the FSM will use the current PC and global history to make a prediction and update the global history register. In the train state, the FSM will update the pattern history table (PHT) based on the actual branch outcome and recover the global history register if the branch was mispredicted.
