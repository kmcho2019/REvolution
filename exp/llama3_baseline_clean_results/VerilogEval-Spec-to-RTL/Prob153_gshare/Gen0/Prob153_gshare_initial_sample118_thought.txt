To implement the gshare branch predictor module, we first need to identify the key components: a 7-bit global branch history register, a 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters (Pattern History Table, PHT), and the necessary logic for prediction and training. We'll use a combinational logic to hash the PC and global history into a 7-bit index for accessing the PHT. The prediction process involves using this index to read the PHT and make a prediction based on the counter value. Training updates the PHT based on the actual branch outcome and misprediction status. The global branch history register is updated based on the predicted branch direction during prediction and recovered during training if a misprediction occurred. We must also consider the precedence of operations when training and prediction occur simultaneously.
