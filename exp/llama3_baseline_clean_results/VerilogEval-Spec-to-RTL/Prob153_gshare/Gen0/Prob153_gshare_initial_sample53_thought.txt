To implement the TopModule, I will first define the internal signals and variables required for the gshare branch predictor. This includes a 7-bit global branch history register, a 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters, and the necessary logic to hash the pc and global history into a 7-bit index. I will then implement the prediction and training logic according to the given specifications. The prediction logic will produce the predicted branch direction and state of the branch history register used to make the prediction, while the training logic will update the pattern history table (PHT) and recover the branch history register if a misprediction occurs. I will also ensure that training takes precedence over prediction when both operations occur in the same cycle.
