The module will be implemented as a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two main components: the prediction logic and the training logic. The prediction logic will use the global history register and the program counter (PC) to index into the Pattern History Table (PHT) and make predictions. The training logic will update the PHT and the global history register based on the training inputs. The FSM will handle the asynchronous reset and ensure that training takes precedence over prediction when both occur in the same cycle. The PHT will be implemented as a 128-entry table of 2-bit saturating counters.
