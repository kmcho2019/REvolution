When a prediction is made, the branch history register will be updated at the next positive clock edge to reflect the predicted branch direction.

The training logic will be triggered by the train_valid signal, and it will update the pattern history table (pht) based on the actual branch outcome and whether the branch was a misprediction. 

If a misprediction occurred, the branch history register will be recovered to the state immediately after the mispredicting branch completes execution.
