To implement the TopModule, we will create a 7-bit global branch history register, a 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters, and the necessary logic to perform predictions and training. The module will use the predict_valid signal to trigger predictions, and the train_valid signal to trigger training. We will use the clk signal to trigger the update of the global branch history register and the saturating counters. The areset signal will be used to asynchronously reset the module.
