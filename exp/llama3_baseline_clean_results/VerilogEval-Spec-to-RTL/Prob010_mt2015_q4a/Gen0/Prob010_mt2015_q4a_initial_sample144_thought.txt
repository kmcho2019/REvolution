To implement the boolean function z = (x^y) & x, we need to follow the order of operations for the given expression: first calculate the XOR of x and y, then perform a bitwise AND operation between the result and x. In Verilog, the XOR operation is represented by the '^' operator and the bitwise AND operation is represented by the '&' operator. We will use these operators directly in our module to compute the output z.
