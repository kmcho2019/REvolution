To implement the boolean function z = (x^y) & x, we first need to understand the operators involved. The '^' symbol represents the bitwise XOR operation, and '&' represents the bitwise AND operation. The function can be broken down into two steps: 
1. Perform the XOR operation between x and y.
2. Perform the AND operation between the result of step 1 and x.
