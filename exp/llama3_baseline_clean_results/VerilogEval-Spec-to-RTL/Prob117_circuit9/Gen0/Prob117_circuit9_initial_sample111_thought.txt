The sequential circuit implemented by the TopModule appears to be a 3-bit counter that increments when the input 'a' is 0 and remains constant when 'a' is 1. The counter increments on the rising edge of the clock. When 'a' is 1, the counter's output is frozen at its current value. Once 'a' goes to 0, the counter starts incrementing again. This behavior suggests that the counter is a simple modulo-8 counter (counts from 0 to 7 and then wraps around) that is controlled by the 'a' input.
