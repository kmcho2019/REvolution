To implement the TopModule, we can observe from the simulation waveforms that the output q is a 3-bit binary counter that increments every time the input a goes from 1 to 0 at the rising edge of the clock clk. The counter wraps around from 7 (binary 111) to 0. When a is high, the counter does not change, regardless of the clock. This behavior can be implemented using a synchronous counter in Verilog.
