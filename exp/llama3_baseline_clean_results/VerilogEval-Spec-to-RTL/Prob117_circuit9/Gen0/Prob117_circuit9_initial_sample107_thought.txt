The module TopModule implements a 3-bit counter that increments when the input 'a' is 0. The counter resets to 1 when 'a' is 1. This can be achieved using a finite state machine (FSM) or a simple counter circuit with a conditional increment and reset logic. We will use a counter circuit for simplicity. The circuit should increment the 3-bit output 'q' on every positive edge of the clock 'clk' when 'a' is 0. If 'a' is 1, the circuit should reset 'q' to 4 (which is 100 in binary).
