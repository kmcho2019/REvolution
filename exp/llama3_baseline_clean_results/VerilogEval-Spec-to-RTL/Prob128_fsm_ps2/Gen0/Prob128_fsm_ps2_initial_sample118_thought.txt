To solve this problem, we can implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM will transition from IDLE to BYTE1 when it encounters a byte with in[3]=1, and then transition from BYTE1 to BYTE2 and from BYTE2 to BYTE3 for each subsequent byte. Once it reaches BYTE3, it will signal done in the next cycle and then reset to IDLE. The FSM will also reset to IDLE when the reset signal is asserted.
