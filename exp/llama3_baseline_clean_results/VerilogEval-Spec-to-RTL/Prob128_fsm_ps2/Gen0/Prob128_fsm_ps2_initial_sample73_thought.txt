The approach to solve this problem involves designing a finite state machine (FSM) with states to track the receipt of bytes within a message. The FSM should have states to indicate the search for the first byte of a message (where in[3]=1) and states to count the subsequent bytes. When the third byte is received, the FSM should signal 'done' in the next cycle. The FSM will be reset when the 'reset' signal is high, returning it to the initial state of searching for the first byte of a message.
