The module TopModule will be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and transitions to BYTE1 when a byte with in[3]=1 is received. It then transitions to BYTE2 after receiving the second byte, and signals done after receiving the third byte. If the FSM is in the IDLE state and receives a byte with in[3]=0, it stays in the IDLE state. The FSM resets to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
