To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, we discard bytes until we see one with in[3]=1, at which point we transition to the BYTE1 state. We then transition to the BYTE2 and BYTE3 states as we receive the next two bytes, respectively. After receiving the third byte, we signal done in the next cycle and then reset to the IDLE state. The FSM will be reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
