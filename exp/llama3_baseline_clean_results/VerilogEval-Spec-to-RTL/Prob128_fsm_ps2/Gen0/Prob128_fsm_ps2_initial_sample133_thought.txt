To solve this problem, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. 
- In the IDLE state, the FSM will continuously check for a byte with in[3] = 1, and once found, it will transition to the BYTE1 state.
- In the BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3 states, the FSM will wait for the next byte to arrive. After three bytes have been received, it will signal done in the cycle immediately after the third byte and then transition back to the IDLE state to search for the next message.
- The FSM will also have a reset state where it will reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
