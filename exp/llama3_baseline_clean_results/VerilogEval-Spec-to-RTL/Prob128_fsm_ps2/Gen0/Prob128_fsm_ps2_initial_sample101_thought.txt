To implement the TopModule, we'll use a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: 
1) IDLE: Initial state where the module discards bytes until it finds one with in[3]=1.
2) BYTE1: State after receiving the first byte of a message (in[3]=1).
3) BYTE2: State after receiving the second byte of a message.
The module will transition from IDLE to BYTE1 when a byte with in[3]=1 is received, 
from BYTE1 to BYTE2 after receiving the second byte, and from BYTE2 back to IDLE after 
signaling done in the next cycle. Reset will be handled by going back to the IDLE state.
