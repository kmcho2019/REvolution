To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) that has four states: Idle, Byte1, Byte2, and Byte3. 
In the Idle state, the FSM waits for a byte with in[3] = 1 and discards all other bytes. 
When it encounters such a byte, it transitions to the Byte1 state, 
then to Byte2 after receiving the next byte, and finally to Byte3 after receiving the next byte after that. 
Once in the Byte3 state, it signals done and then returns to the Idle state, waiting for the next byte with in[3] = 1. 
The FSM will also have a reset state that it enters when the reset input is high, forcing it back to the Idle state.
