To solve this problem, we can implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: Idle, Byte1, Byte2, and Byte3. The FSM starts in the Idle state and transitions to Byte1 when it encounters a byte with in[3] = 1. It then transitions to Byte2 and Byte3 after receiving the next two bytes. The done signal is asserted in the cycle immediately after the third byte is received, i.e., when the FSM is in the Byte3 state and the clock rises. We will use a counter to keep track of the bytes received. The FSM will reset to the Idle state when the reset signal is high.
