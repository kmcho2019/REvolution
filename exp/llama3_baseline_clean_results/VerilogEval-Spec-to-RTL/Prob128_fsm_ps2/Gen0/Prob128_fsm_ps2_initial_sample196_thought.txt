To solve this problem, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with three main states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM will be in the IDLE state initially, where it waits for a byte with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is detected, it transitions to the BYTE1 state. It then waits for two more bytes to be received, transitioning to BYTE2 after the first additional byte and then back to IDLE after the third byte, signaling done in the cycle after the third byte is received. The FSM will also have a reset state that is triggered on the positive edge of the clock when the reset signal is high.
