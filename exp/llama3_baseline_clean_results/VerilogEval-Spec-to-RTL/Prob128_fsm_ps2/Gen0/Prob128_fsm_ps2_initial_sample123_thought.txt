To implement the TopModule, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: 
1) IDLE: The initial state where the FSM waits for a byte with in[3]=1.
2) BYTE1: The state after receiving the first byte of a message.
3) BYTE2: The state after receiving the second byte of a message.
4) BYTE3: The state after receiving the third byte of a message, where the FSM will signal done.

The FSM will transition from IDLE to BYTE1 when a byte with in[3]=1 is received. 
Then, it will transition from BYTE1 to BYTE2, and from BYTE2 to BYTE3, regardless of the input byte.
Finally, it will transition from BYTE3 back to IDLE and signal done in the same cycle.
The reset signal will asynchronously reset the FSM to the IDLE state.
