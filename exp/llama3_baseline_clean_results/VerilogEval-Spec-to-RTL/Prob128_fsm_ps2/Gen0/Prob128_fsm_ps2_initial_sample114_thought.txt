The TopModule will be implemented as a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions through states based on the input byte stream. The FSM will have four states: Idle, Byte1, Byte2, and Byte3. In the Idle state, the FSM will discard bytes until it finds one with in[3]=1, at which point it will transition to the Byte1 state. The FSM will then transition through the Byte2 and Byte3 states as it receives the next two bytes. After the third byte is received, the FSM will signal done in the next cycle. The FSM will reset to the Idle state when the reset signal is high.
