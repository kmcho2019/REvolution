To solve this problem, we can implement a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and remains there until it sees a byte with in[3]=1. At this point, it transitions to the BYTE1 state and then to the BYTE2 state after receiving the next two bytes, regardless of their values. After receiving the third byte, it signals 'done' in the next clock cycle and returns to the IDLE state, ready to find the next message boundary. The reset signal is used to return the FSM to the IDLE state.
