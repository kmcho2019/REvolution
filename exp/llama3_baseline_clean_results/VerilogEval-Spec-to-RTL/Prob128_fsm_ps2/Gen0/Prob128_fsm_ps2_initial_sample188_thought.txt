The module TopModule can be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and waits for a byte with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is received, it transitions to the BYTE1 state. It then remains in BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3 states for the next two bytes, regardless of their values. After receiving the third byte, it signals done in the next cycle and returns to the IDLE state to search for the next message boundary. The reset signal is used to asynchronously reset the FSM to the IDLE state.
