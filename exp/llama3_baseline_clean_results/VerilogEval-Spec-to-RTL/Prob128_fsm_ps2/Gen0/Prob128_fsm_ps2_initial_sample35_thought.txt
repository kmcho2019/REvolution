The module TopModule will implement a finite state machine (FSM) to detect message boundaries in a PS/2 mouse protocol byte stream. The FSM will have four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, the FSM will discard bytes until it encounters a byte with in[3]=1, at which point it will transition to the BYTE1 state. The FSM will then transition to the BYTE2 and BYTE3 states after receiving the next two bytes, respectively. After the third byte is received, the FSM will signal done in the next cycle and then transition back to the IDLE state, waiting for the next message boundary.
