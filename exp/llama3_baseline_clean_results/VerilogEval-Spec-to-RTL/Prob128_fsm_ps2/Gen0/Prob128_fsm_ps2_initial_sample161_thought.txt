To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) that searches for message boundaries in the input byte stream. The FSM will have four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, it will discard bytes until it finds one with in[3] = 1. Once it finds such a byte, it will transition to the BYTE1 state, and then to BYTE2 and BYTE3 after receiving two more bytes. The done signal will be asserted in the cycle immediately after the third byte of each message is successfully received. The FSM will also have a reset state that will be triggered by the reset input.
