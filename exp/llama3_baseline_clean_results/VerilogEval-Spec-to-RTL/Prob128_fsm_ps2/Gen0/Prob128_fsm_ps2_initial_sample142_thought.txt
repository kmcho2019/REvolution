To implement the TopModule, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) that has four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. 
The FSM starts in the IDLE state, where it waits for a byte with in[3] = 1. 
Once such a byte is received, it transitions to the BYTE1 state, then to BYTE2, and finally to BYTE3. 
In the BYTE3 state, it signals the receipt of a message by asserting the 'done' output. 
The FSM resets to the IDLE state when 'reset' is asserted. 
We'll use a counter to keep track of the current state and determine when to transition to the next state.
