To solve this problem, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM will start in the IDLE state, where it will discard incoming bytes until it sees one with in[3] = 1. Once it sees such a byte, it will transition to the BYTE1 state, and then to BYTE2 and BYTE3 states as it receives subsequent bytes. After receiving the third byte, it will signal done in the next cycle. The FSM will also have a reset mechanism to return to the IDLE state when the reset signal is asserted.
