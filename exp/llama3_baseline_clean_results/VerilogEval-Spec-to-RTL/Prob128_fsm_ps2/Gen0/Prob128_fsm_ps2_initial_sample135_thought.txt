To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: 
IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM will transition from IDLE to BYTE1 when it encounters 
a byte with in[3]=1, then from BYTE1 to BYTE2 after receiving one byte, and finally 
from BYTE2 back to IDLE after receiving the third byte, signaling done in the process. 
The reset signal will asynchronously reset the FSM to the IDLE state.
