To solve this problem, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) that keeps track of the current state and transitions between states based on the input byte stream. The FSM will have four states: IDLE (initial state), BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, the FSM will discard bytes until it finds one with in[3]=1. Once it finds such a byte, it will transition to the BYTE1 state. Then, it will transition to BYTE2 and BYTE3 states after receiving the next two bytes. After receiving the third byte, it will signal done and return to the IDLE state. The FSM will also be reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
