To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM starts in the IDLE state, where it discards bytes until it sees one with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is found, it transitions to the BYTE1 state, assuming this is the first byte of a message. The FSM then proceeds to BYTE2 and BYTE3 states, collecting the next two bytes. After collecting the third byte, it signals the receipt of a message by setting the done signal high in the next cycle. The FSM returns to the IDLE state after signaling done, ready to search for the next message. Reset is active high synchronous, and all sequential logic is triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
