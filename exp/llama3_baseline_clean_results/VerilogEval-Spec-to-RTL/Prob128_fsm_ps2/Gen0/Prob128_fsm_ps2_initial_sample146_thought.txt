- In the BYTE1 state, we wait for the next byte and store it. We then transition to the BYTE2 state.
- In the BYTE2 state, we wait for the next byte and store it. We then transition to the BYTE3 state.
- In the BYTE3 state, we signal that a message has been received (done) and transition back to the IDLE state, ready to detect the next message boundary.
