To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM starts in the IDLE state, where it discards bytes until it finds one with in[3]=1. When such a byte is found, it transitions to the BYTE1 state. Then, it transitions to BYTE2 and BYTE3 after receiving the next two bytes, respectively. The done signal is asserted in the cycle immediately after the third byte of each message was successfully received, i.e., when the FSM is in the BYTE3 state and the next clock cycle begins. The FSM resets to the IDLE state when the reset signal is active high.
