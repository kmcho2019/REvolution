To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and stays there until it sees a byte with in[3]=1. Once it sees such a byte, it transitions to the BYTE1 state, assuming this is the first byte of a message. It then transitions through the BYTE2 and BYTE3 states as it receives the next two bytes, regardless of their values. After receiving the third byte (in the BYTE3 state), it signals the receipt of a message by setting the done output to 1 in the next cycle, and then resets to the IDLE state to search for the next message. If reset is asserted, the FSM immediately resets to the IDLE state.
