To implement the required functionality, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. The FSM will start in the IDLE state and stay there until it encounters a byte with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is found, it transitions to the BYTE1 state, assuming this is the first byte of a message. It then counts the next two bytes, transitioning through BYTE2 and BYTE3 states. Once in the BYTE3 state, after receiving the third byte, it signals 'done' in the next clock cycle and resets to the IDLE state to search for the next message. The FSM will be reset to the IDLE state upon assertion of the reset signal.
