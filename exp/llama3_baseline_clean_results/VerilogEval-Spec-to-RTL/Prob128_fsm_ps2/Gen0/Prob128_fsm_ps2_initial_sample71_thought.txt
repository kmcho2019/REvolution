To solve this problem, we'll design a finite state machine (FSM) with three main states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2.

- In the BYTE1 state, we expect to receive the second byte of the message and transition to the BYTE2 state.
- In the BYTE2 state, after receiving the third byte, we signal 'done' in the next clock cycle and return to the IDLE state to search for the next message.
