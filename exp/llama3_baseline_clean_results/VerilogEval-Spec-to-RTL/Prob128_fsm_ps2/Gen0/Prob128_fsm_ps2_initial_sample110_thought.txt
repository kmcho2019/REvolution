The TopModule will be designed as a finite state machine with four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, the module will continuously check for input bytes with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is detected, it will transition to the BYTE1 state. It will then wait for the next two bytes, transitioning to BYTE2 and BYTE3 respectively, and signal done in the cycle immediately after the third byte is received. The module will reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is asserted.
