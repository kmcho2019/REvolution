To solve this problem, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and remains there until it receives a byte with in[3] = 1. Once it receives such a byte, it transitions to the BYTE1 state. In the BYTE1 state, it waits for two more bytes to arrive, transitioning to the BYTE2 state after the first byte and signaling done after the second byte. We use a counter to keep track of the number of bytes received after the first byte. We also use the reset signal to reset the FSM to the IDLE state.
