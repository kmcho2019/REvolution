To implement the TopModule with the given interface and requirements, we will use a combination of sequential logic and combinational logic. The module will have a 512-bit register to store the current state of the cells. When the load input is high, the module will load the data into the register. On every positive edge of the clock, the module will compute the next state of the cells based on the Rule 90 of the one-dimensional cellular automaton and update the register. The next state of each cell will be the XOR of its two neighbors. We will handle the boundary conditions by assuming that the cells at the left and right boundaries are always zero.
