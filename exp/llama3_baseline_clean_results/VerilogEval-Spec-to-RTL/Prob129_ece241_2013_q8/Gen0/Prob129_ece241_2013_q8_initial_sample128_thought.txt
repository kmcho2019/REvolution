The IDLE state is the initial state where the FSM waits for the first '1' in the sequence. Upon detecting a '1', the FSM transitions to the SEEN_1 state. If a '0' is detected in the SEEN_1 state, the FSM transitions to the SEEN_10 state. Finally, if a '1' is detected in the SEEN_10 state, the FSM asserts the output z to logic-1 and transitions back to the SEEN_1 state, allowing it to recognize overlapping sequences.
