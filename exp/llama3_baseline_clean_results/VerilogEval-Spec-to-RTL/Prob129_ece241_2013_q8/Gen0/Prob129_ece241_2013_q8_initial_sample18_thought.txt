To solve this problem, we will create a Mealy-type finite state machine with three states: idle, seen_1, and seen_10. We start in the idle state. When 'x' is 1 in the idle state, we move to the seen_1 state. When 'x' is 0 in the seen_1 state, we move to the seen_10 state. When 'x' is 1 in the seen_10 state, we move back to the seen_1 state and assert 'z'. We reset to the idle state on the negative edge of 'aresetn'. We also reset to the idle state on the positive edge of 'clk' when 'aresetn' is 0.
