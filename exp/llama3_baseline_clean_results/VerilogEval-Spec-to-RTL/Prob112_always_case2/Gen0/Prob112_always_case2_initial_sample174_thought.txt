The output bits will represent the position of the first 1 bit in the input vector. If the most significant bit (MSB) is 1, the output will be 10 (2 in decimal), if the second MSB is 1 and the MSB is 0, the output will be 01 (1 in decimal), if the second least significant bit (LSB) is 1 and both MSBs are 0, the output will be 10 (2 in decimal) and if the least significant bit (LSB) is 1 and all other bits are 0, the output will be 00 (0 in decimal).
