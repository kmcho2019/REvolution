The output of the OR gate is connected to 'out', and the inverted output of 'out' is connected to 'out_n'. We can implement this using Verilog by declaring two intermediate wires to connect the AND gates to the OR gate and then assigning the output of the OR gate to 'out' and its inversion to 'out_n'.
