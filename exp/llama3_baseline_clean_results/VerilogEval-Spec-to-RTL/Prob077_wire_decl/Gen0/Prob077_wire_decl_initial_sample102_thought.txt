To implement the TopModule as described, we first need to declare the module and its ports. Then, we can create two intermediate wires to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the OR gate. We will name these wires 'and_out1' and 'and_out2'. The output 'out' will be directly driven by the OR gate, and 'out_n' will be the inversion of 'out'. This can be achieved by using the NOT operator on 'out' and assigning it to 'out_n'.
