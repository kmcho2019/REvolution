To implement the TopModule, I will first declare two intermediate wires, let's call them 'and_out1' and 'and_out2', to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the input of the OR gate. The AND gates will be driven by the inputs a, b, c, and d. Then, I will use the output of the OR gate to drive the output 'out' and also connect it to a NOT gate to generate the inverted output 'out_n'.
