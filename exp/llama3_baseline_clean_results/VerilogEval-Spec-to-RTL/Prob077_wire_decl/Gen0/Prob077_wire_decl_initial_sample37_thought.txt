To implement the TopModule, we first declare the module with its inputs and outputs. Then, we declare two intermediate wires to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the inputs of the OR gate. We use the logical AND operator (&) for the AND gates and the logical OR operator (|) for the OR gate. The output 'out_n' is simply the negation of 'out', which can be achieved by using the NOT operator (~). We assign the output of the OR gate to 'out' and the negation of 'out' to 'out_n'.
