To implement the TopModule, we first need to declare the input and output ports, as well as the intermediate wires. Then, we can instantiate the AND gates, the OR gate, and the NOT gate. The AND gates will be connected to the inputs a, b and c, d respectively. The outputs of the AND gates will be connected to an intermediate wire. The OR gate will be connected to the intermediate wires from the AND gates and its output will be connected to the output 'out'. Finally, the output 'out' will be inverted using a NOT gate and connected to the output 'out_n'.
