To implement the TopModule, we first need to declare the module with its input and output ports. Then, we can create two intermediate wires to connect the AND and OR gates together. We'll name these wires 'and_out1' and 'and_out2', representing the outputs of the two AND gates. The output of the OR gate will be connected to 'out', and we can simply invert 'out' to get 'out_n'. This approach ensures that each wire is driven by exactly one source and can feed multiple inputs.
