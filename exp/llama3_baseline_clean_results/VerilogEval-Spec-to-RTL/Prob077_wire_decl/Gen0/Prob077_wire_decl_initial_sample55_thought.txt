To implement the TopModule, we will first declare two intermediate wires to connect the AND and OR gates. Let's call these wires `and_out1` and `and_out2`, which will carry the output of the AND gates. Then, we'll use these wires as inputs to the OR gate to get the final output `out`. Since `out_n` is the inverted version of `out`, we can directly use the `out` signal and apply a NOT operation to get `out_n`.
