To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the circuit structure described. It involves two layers of logic gates: the first layer consists of two AND gates, and the second layer consists of an OR gate followed by a NOT gate to generate the inverted output. The AND gates will be connected to the inputs a-b and c-d, respectively. The outputs of these AND gates will be connected to an OR gate, whose output will drive both the 'out' and 'out_n' ports, with 'out_n' being the inverted version of 'out'. We will declare two intermediate wires, one for each AND gate's output, to connect them to the OR gate. The output of the OR gate will directly drive 'out', and 'out_n' will be driven by the inverted 'out'.
