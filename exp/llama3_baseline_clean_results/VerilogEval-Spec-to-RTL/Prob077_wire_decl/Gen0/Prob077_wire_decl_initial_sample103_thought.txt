To implement the TopModule, we first declare the module with the given interface. We then declare two intermediate wires, let's call them and1 and and2, to connect the outputs of the AND gates. The AND gates are instantiated with the given inputs a, b, c, and d. We then declare an OR gate to combine the outputs of the AND gates and assign it to the output out. Finally, we use a NOT gate to invert the output out and assign it to out_n.
