To implement the TopModule, we need to follow the given circuit description. 
First, we declare two intermediate wires, let's call them `and_out1` and `and_out2`, 
to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the input of the OR gate. 
Then, we use these wires to implement the logic of the circuit. 
The output of the OR gate will be connected to `out`, and the inverted output will be connected to `out_n`.
We can achieve this inversion using a NOT gate or simply by using the `~` operator in Verilog.
