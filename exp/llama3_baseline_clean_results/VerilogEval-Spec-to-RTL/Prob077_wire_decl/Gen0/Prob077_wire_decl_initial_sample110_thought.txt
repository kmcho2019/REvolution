To implement the TopModule, we need to follow the circuit description: 
1. Create two AND gates: one for inputs 'a' and 'b', and another for inputs 'c' and 'd'. 
2. Use the outputs of these AND gates as inputs to an OR gate.
3. The output of the OR gate will be 'out'. 
4. To get 'out_n', we need to invert the output of the OR gate.
We will use two intermediate wires, let's call them 'and1_out' and 'and2_out', to connect the AND gates to the OR gate.
