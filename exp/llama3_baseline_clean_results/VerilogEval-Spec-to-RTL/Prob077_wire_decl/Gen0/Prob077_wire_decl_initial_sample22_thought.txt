To implement the TopModule, I will first define two intermediate wires, `and1_out` and `and2_out`, to connect the outputs of the two AND gates to the inputs of the OR gate. Then, I will define the `out` signal as the output of the OR gate and the `out_n` signal as the inverse of the `out` signal using a NOT gate. This way, the AND gates drive the intermediate wires, which in turn drive the OR gate, and the output of the OR gate drives both the `out` and `out_n` signals.
