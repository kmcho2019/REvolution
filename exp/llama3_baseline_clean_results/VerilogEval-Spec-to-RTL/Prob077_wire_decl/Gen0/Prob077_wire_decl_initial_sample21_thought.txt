To implement the TopModule, we first need to declare the input and output ports as specified. Then, we can create two intermediate wires, let's call them `and_out1` and `and_out2`, to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the input of the OR gate. The output of the OR gate will be assigned to the `out` port. Finally, we can use a NOT gate to invert the `out` signal and assign it to the `out_n` port.
