To implement the TopModule, we will first create two intermediate wires, 
let's call them and1 and and2, to connect the outputs of the AND gates. 
We will then use these wires as inputs to the OR gate to get the output 'out'. 
Finally, we will use the output 'out' and connect it to a NOT gate to get the 
inverted output 'out_n'. This way, we ensure that each wire is driven by 
exactly one source and can feed multiple inputs.
