To implement the TopModule, we need to create a Verilog module with the given interface. 
We'll first declare the two intermediate wires, let's call them 'and1' and 'and2', 
to connect the outputs of the two AND gates. 
Then, we'll use these wires as inputs to the OR gate to produce the 'out' signal. 
Finally, we'll use the 'out' signal as the input to the NOT gate to produce the 'out_n' signal.
