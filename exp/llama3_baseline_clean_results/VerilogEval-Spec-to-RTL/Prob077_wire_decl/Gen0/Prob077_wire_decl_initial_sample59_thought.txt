To implement the TopModule, I will first declare two intermediate wires to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the input of the OR gate. Then, I will use these wires to drive the OR gate and the output 'out'. To generate the inverted output 'out_n', I will use a NOT gate to invert the 'out' signal. The module will consist of three logical operations: two AND operations for the first layer, one OR operation for the second layer, and a NOT operation for the inverted output.
