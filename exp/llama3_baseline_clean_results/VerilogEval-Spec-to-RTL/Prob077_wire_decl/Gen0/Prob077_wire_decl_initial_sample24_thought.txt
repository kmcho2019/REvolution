To implement the TopModule, we need to create two intermediate wires to connect the AND and OR gates. Let's name these wires "and_out1" and "and_out2", which will be the outputs of the two AND gates. The OR gate will then take these two wires as inputs and produce the output "out". Additionally, we will use the output "out" as the input to the NOT gate to produce the inverted output "out_n".
