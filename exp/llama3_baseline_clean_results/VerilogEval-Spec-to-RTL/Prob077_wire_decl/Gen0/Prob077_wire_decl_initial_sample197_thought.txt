To implement the TopModule, we can use two intermediate wires to connect the AND gates to the OR gate. The first wire, let's call it 'and1', will be driven by the AND gate connected to inputs 'a' and 'b'. The second wire, let's call it 'and2', will be driven by the AND gate connected to inputs 'c' and 'd'. The OR gate will then be connected to these two wires and drive the output 'out'. Additionally, we can use a NOT gate to invert the output 'out' and drive the output 'out_n'.
