To implement the TopModule, we need to follow the circuit description. First, we will create two intermediate wires, let's name them `and_out1` and `and_out2`, to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the OR gate. The AND gates will be connected to the input ports `a`, `b`, `c`, and `d`. Then, we will use the output of the OR gate as the `out` port and its inverted value as the `out_n` port.
