To implement the TopModule, we first need to define the two intermediate wires that will connect the AND gates to the OR gate. Let's name these wires 'and1' and 'and2'. The 'and1' wire will be driven by the AND gate with inputs 'a' and 'b', while the 'and2' wire will be driven by the AND gate with inputs 'c' and 'd'. Then, the 'out' wire will be driven by the OR gate with inputs 'and1' and 'and2'. Finally, the 'out_n' wire will be driven by the NOT gate with input 'out'.
