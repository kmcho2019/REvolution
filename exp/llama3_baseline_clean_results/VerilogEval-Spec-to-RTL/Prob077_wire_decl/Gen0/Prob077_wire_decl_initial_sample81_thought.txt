To implement the TopModule, we first need to create two intermediate wires to connect the AND gates to the OR gate. Let's name these wires and_or1 and and_or2, where and_or1 will be driven by the AND gate connected to inputs a and b, and and_or2 will be driven by the AND gate connected to inputs c and d. Then, the OR gate will take these two wires as inputs and drive the out port. Finally, we will use a NOT gate to invert the out port and drive the out_n port.
