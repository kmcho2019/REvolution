To implement the TopModule, we will first declare two intermediate wires, and_gate1_out and and_gate2_out, to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the OR gate. Then, we will use these wires to drive the OR gate and the NOT gate to produce the final outputs out and out_n. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for the gates: `&` for AND, `|` for OR, and `~` for NOT.
