The module TopModule can be designed by first creating two intermediate wires, let's call them 'and_out1' and 'and_out2', to connect the outputs of the two AND gates. Then, we can use these wires as inputs to an OR gate to get the 'out' signal. Finally, we can invert the 'out' signal to get the 'out_n' signal. This design ensures that each wire is driven by exactly one source and can feed multiple inputs.
