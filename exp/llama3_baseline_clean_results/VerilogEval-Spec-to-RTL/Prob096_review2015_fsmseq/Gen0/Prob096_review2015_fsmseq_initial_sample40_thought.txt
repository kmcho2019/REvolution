To implement the required module, we will design a finite-state machine (FSM) with five states: IDLE, SEEN_1, SEEN_11, SEEN_110, and FOUND. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and transitions to subsequent states based on the input data sequence. When the sequence '1101' is detected, the FSM moves to the FOUND state, where it sets the start_shifting output to 1. The FSM remains in the FOUND state until reset is asserted. The reset signal is synchronous and active high, meaning it will reset the FSM only on the positive edge of the clock.
