To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite-state machine (FSM) with five states: IDLE, SEEN1, SEEN11, SEEN110, and SEEN1101. The FSM starts in the IDLE state and transitions to the next state based on the input data. If the sequence 1101 is detected, the FSM sets start_shifting to 1 and remains in that state until reset is asserted. The reset signal is active high and synchronous, meaning it will reset the FSM on the next positive edge of the clock.
