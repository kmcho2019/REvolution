To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite-state machine (FSM) with five states: idle, got_1, got_11, got_110, and got_1101. The FSM will transition through these states as it receives the input bit stream. When it reaches the got_1101 state, it will set start_shifting to 1 and stay in that state until reset. We will use a synchronous reset to reset the FSM to the idle state.
