To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite-state machine with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and FOUND. The machine starts in the IDLE state and transitions to subsequent states upon detecting the corresponding bits of the sequence 1101 in the input data stream. Once the sequence is found (FOUND state), it sets start_shifting to 1 and remains in this state until reset. The reset signal is active high and synchronous, meaning it affects the state transition only on the positive edge of the clock.
