The TopModule will implement a finite-state machine (FSM) with multiple states to track the sequence 1101. The FSM will start in an idle state and transition through states as it detects each bit of the sequence in the correct order. Once the sequence is fully detected, it will enter a final state where start_shifting is set to 1. The FSM will remain in this final state until reset is asserted. Each state transition will be triggered by the rising edge of the clock, and the reset will also be synchronized to the clock.
