The TopModule can be implemented as a finite-state machine with five states: IDLE, FOUND_1, FOUND_11, FOUND_110, and FOUND_SEQ. 
In the IDLE state, we wait for the first '1' to arrive. Once we get a '1', we transition to the FOUND_1 state. 
In the FOUND_1 state, we wait for the second '1' to arrive. Once we get another '1', we transition to the FOUND_11 state. 
In the FOUND_11 state, we wait for a '0' to arrive. Once we get a '0', we transition to the FOUND_110 state. 
In the FOUND_110 state, we wait for the final '1' to arrive. Once we get a '1', we transition to the FOUND_SEQ state and set start_shifting to '1'.
We remain in the FOUND_SEQ state and keep start_shifting set to '1' until reset is asserted.
