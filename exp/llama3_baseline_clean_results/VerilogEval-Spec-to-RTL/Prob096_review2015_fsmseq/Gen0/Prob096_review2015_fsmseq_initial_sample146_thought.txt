The approach is to design a finite-state machine (FSM) with a series of states that correspond to each step of recognizing the sequence "1101". The FSM will have five states: Idle (initial state), S1 (one '1' received), S2 (sequence "11" received), S3 (sequence "110" received), and Found (sequence "1101" received). When the FSM reaches the Found state, it will set the start_shifting output to 1 and remain in this state until a reset is applied.
