To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite-state machine (FSM) that moves through a series of states as it searches for the sequence 1101 in the input data stream. The FSM will have a total of 5 states: 
- S0 (initial state), 
- S1 (found 1), 
- S2 (found 11), 
- S3 (found 110), 
- S4 (found 1101). 
When the FSM reaches S4, it will set start_shifting to 1. The FSM will remain in S4 until reset is asserted, which will transition the FSM back to S0 and reset start_shifting to 0.
