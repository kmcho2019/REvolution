To solve this problem, we will design a finite-state machine (FSM) with five states: Idle, Received1, Received11, Received110, and FoundSequence. The FSM starts in the Idle state and transitions to the next state when the corresponding bit in the sequence 1101 is received. If the received bit does not match the expected bit in the sequence, the FSM returns to the Idle state. When the sequence 1101 is found, the FSM enters the FoundSequence state and sets start_shifting to 1. The FSM remains in this state until reset is asserted.
