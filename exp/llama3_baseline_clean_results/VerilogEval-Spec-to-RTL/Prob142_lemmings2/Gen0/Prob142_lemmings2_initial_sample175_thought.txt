To implement the Lemming behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to define the states and transitions based on the given conditions. We can have four states: walking_left, walking_right, falling_left, and falling_right. The transitions between these states will be determined by the input signals bump_left, bump_right, and ground.

- If the Lemming is walking left and hits an obstacle on the right, it will switch to walking right.
- If the Lemming is walking right and hits an obstacle on the left, it will switch to walking left.
- If the Lemming hits obstacles on both sides at the same time, it will still switch directions.
- If the ground disappears (ground = 0), the Lemming will fall and say "aaah!" regardless of the current walking direction.
- When the ground reappears (ground = 1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.
- Being bumped while falling or at the same cycle as the ground disappears/reappears does not affect the walking direction.

We will use two bits to represent the states: the first bit (most significant bit) will indicate whether the Lemming is falling or not, and the second bit (least significant bit) will indicate the walking direction.
