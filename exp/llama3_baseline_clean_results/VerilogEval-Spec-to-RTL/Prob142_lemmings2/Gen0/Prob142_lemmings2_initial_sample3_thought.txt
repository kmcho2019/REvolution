To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we will define the states as follows: 
- idle_left: initial state where the Lemming is walking left
- idle_right: state where the Lemming is walking right
- falling: state where the Lemming is falling due to the absence of ground

The machine will transition between these states based on the inputs bump_left, bump_right, and ground. 
When ground is 1, the machine can switch between idle_left and idle_right based on bump_left and bump_right. 
When ground is 0, the machine will transition to the falling state and remain there until ground becomes 1 again.

The output signals walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined by the current state and the input signals.
