To solve this problem, we need to design a Moore state machine with three main states: walking left, walking right, and falling. We can use two bits to represent these states. The machine will start in the walking left state when reset. When the Lemming is bumped on the left or right, it will switch to the corresponding walking state. When the ground disappears, it will transition to the falling state, and when the ground reappears, it will resume walking in the previous direction. We will use a separate register to keep track of the previous walking direction. The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined by the current state.
