To implement the Lemming's behavior, we can design a Moore state machine with three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The state transitions will be based on the bump_left, bump_right, and ground inputs. If the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will enter the falling state and say "aaah!". When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. We will use a 2-bit state register to represent the three states. The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined based on the current state and the inputs.
