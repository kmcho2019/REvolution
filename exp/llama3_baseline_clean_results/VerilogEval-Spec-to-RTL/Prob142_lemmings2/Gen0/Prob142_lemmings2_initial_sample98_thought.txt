To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to identify the states and transitions. The states are: walking left, walking right, and falling. The transitions are based on the input signals: bump_left, bump_right, and ground. When ground is 0, the Lemming falls regardless of the direction. When ground is 1, the Lemming walks in the last known direction. If the Lemming is bumped on either side while walking, it changes direction. Being bumped while falling does not affect the walking direction. 

We will use two state variables: direction (to keep track of the walking direction) and falling (to keep track of whether the Lemming is falling or not). The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah are determined based on the current state.

On the positive edge of the clock, the state is updated based on the current state and input signals. If areset is high, the machine resets to the walking left state.
