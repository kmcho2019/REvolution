To implement this behavior, we can design a Moore state machine with four states: walking left, walking right, falling, and idle (which will not be used in this case). The state machine will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. When the Lemming is walking left or right and receives a bump on the corresponding side, it will switch directions. If the ground disappears, the Lemming will start falling and say "aaah!". When the ground reappears, the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. We will use two flip-flops to store the current state and the previous direction.
