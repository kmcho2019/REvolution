To implement the Lemmings' finite state machine, we will design a Moore machine with the following states:
- LEFT: Lemming is walking left
- RIGHT: Lemming is walking right
- FALL: Lemming is falling

The machine will transition between these states based on the input signals bump_left, bump_right, and ground. When the Lemming is in the FALL state, it will ignore the bump_left and bump_right signals. When the ground reappears, the Lemming will transition back to its previous walking state.

We will use two registers: one to store the current state and another to store the previous walking state (LEFT or RIGHT). This allows us to resume walking in the same direction after a fall.
