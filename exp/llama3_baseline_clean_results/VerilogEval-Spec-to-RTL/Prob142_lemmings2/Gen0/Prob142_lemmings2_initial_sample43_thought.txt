To model the Lemming's behavior, we will use a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: the Lemming is walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: the Lemming is walking right
- FALL: the Lemming is falling

We will use a 2-bit state register (STATE) to store the current state. The STATE register will be updated at every positive edge of the clock. 
When the Lemming is walking, it will change direction if it hits an obstacle (bump_left or bump_right). 
When the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will fall and say "aaah!". 
When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.

We will use two always blocks: one for sequential logic (STATE register update) and one for combinational logic (output generation).
