To implement the Lemmings state machine, we'll use a Moore state machine with two states: walking_left and walking_right. We'll also introduce a third state, falling, to model the behavior when the ground is missing. The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals bump_left, bump_right, and ground. When the ground is missing (ground = 0), the Lemming will enter the falling state and say "aaah!". When the ground reappears (ground = 1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined based on the current state.
