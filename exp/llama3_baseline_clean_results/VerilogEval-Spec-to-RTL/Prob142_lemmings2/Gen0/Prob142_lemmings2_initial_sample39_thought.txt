To model the behavior of the Lemming using a Moore state machine, we will define four states: walking_left, walking_right, falling_left, and falling_right. 
The initial state will be walking_left. 
The Lemming will switch between walking_left and walking_right when bumped from either side. 
When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition to either falling_left or falling_right depending on its previous walking direction. 
When the ground reappears, the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.
