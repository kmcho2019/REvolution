To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we will consider three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. We will also track the last walking direction before the Lemming started falling. 

The state machine will have three states: 
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling and cannot change direction due to bumps.

The state machine will change its state based on the current state and inputs. 
- If the Lemming is walking and bumps are detected, it will change direction.
- If the ground disappears while the Lemming is walking, it will start falling.
- If the ground reappears while the Lemming is falling, it will resume walking in the last known direction.

The outputs will be determined solely by the current state.
