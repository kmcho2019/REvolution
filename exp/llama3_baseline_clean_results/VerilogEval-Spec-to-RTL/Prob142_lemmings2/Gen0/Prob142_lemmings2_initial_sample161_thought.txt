To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we first need to identify the states and the conditions for transitioning between them. The key states are walking left, walking right, and falling. We will use a binary encoding for these states where walking left is represented by `00`, walking right by `01`, and falling by `10` and `11`. However, since we are considering a Moore machine, the outputs are determined solely by the state. Hence, we will focus on creating a state transition diagram based on the given conditions and then implement it in Verilog.

We'll start by initializing the state to walking left upon reset. Then, we'll handle the transitions based on the inputs `bump_left`, `bump_right`, and `ground`. If the Lemming is walking and `ground` becomes `0`, it should transition to the falling state. While falling, the Lemming's direction doesn't change due to bumps, but it resumes walking in the previous direction when `ground` becomes `1` again.

For simplicity, we'll use two registers: one to store the current state and another to store the previous walking direction when the Lemming is falling, so it can resume correctly when the ground reappears.
