To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we will define four states: 
- left: the Lemming is walking left
- right: the Lemming is walking right
- falling_left: the Lemming was walking left when it fell
- falling_right: the Lemming was walking right when it fell

We will also define the state transition logic based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, and ground). 
When the Lemming is walking, it changes direction if it receives a bump signal from either side. 
When the Lemming is walking and the ground disappears, it transitions to the corresponding falling state. 
When the Lemming is falling and the ground reappears, it resumes walking in the same direction it was before the fall.
The output signals (walk_left, walk_right, and aaah) will be determined based on the current state.
