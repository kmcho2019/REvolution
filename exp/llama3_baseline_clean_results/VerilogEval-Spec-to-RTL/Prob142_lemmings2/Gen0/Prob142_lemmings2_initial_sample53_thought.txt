To solve this problem, we will implement a Moore state machine with four states: 
- walking left
- walking right
- falling after walking left
- falling after walking right

The state machine will start in the walking left state after areset. 
The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined by the current state.
- walk_left will be 1 in the walking left state.
- walk_right will be 1 in the walking right state.
- aaah will be 1 in both falling states.

The state machine will transition between states based on the current state and the input signals.
- If the Lemming is walking left and bump_left is 1, it will transition to the walking right state.
- If the Lemming is walking left and bump_right is 1, it will not transition (will remain in the walking left state).
- If the Lemming is walking left and bump_left and bump_right are both 1, it will transition to the walking right state.
- If the Lemming is walking right and bump_right is 1, it will transition to the walking left state.
- If the Lemming is walking right and bump_left is 1, it will not transition (will remain in the walking right state).
- If the Lemming is walking right and bump_left and bump_right are both 1, it will transition to the walking left state.
- If the Lemming is walking and ground is 0, it will transition to the corresponding falling state.
- If the Lemming is falling and ground is 1, it will transition back to the corresponding walking state.

We can use a two-bit signal (state) to represent the current state.
