To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we'll define three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The machine will start in the walking left state. When the Lemming is bumped on either side, it will transition to the opposite walking state. If the ground disappears, the machine will transition to the falling state and stay there until the ground reappears. In the falling state, the walking direction will not change, but the "aaah!" signal will be active. When the ground reappears, the machine will transition back to the previous walking state. We'll use two bits to represent the three states: 2'b00 for walking left, 2'b01 for walking right, and 2'b10 for falling. We'll also use a separate signal to keep track of the previous walking direction while the Lemming is falling.
