To implement this finite state machine, we can define four states: 
- walking_left: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- walking_right: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- falling_left: The Lemming is falling and was walking to the left before.
- falling_right: The Lemming is falling and was walking to the right before.

We start in the walking_left state. When the ground disappears, we transition to the falling_left or falling_right state, depending on the current walking direction. 
When the ground reappears, we transition back to the walking_left or walking_right state, depending on the direction before the fall. 
When the Lemming is bumped on the left or right, it will switch direction. However, if the Lemming is currently falling, the bump does not affect the walking direction.

We use a Moore state machine, meaning that the outputs are a function of the current state. 
The state register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock.
