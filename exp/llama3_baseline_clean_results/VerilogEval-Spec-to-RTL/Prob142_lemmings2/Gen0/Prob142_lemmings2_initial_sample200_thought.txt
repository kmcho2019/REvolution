To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to define the states and the transitions between them. The states are:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.

The state machine transitions are:
- From WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT when bumped on the left.
- From WALK_LEFT to FALLING when the ground disappears.
- From WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT when bumped on the right.
- From WALK_RIGHT to FALLING when the ground disappears.
- From FALLING to WALK_LEFT when the ground reappears and the Lemming was walking to the left before falling.
- From FALLING to WALK_RIGHT when the ground reappears and the Lemming was walking to the right before falling.

The output signals are:
- walk_left: 1 when the Lemming is walking to the left.
- walk_right: 1 when the Lemming is walking to the right.
- aaah: 1 when the Lemming is falling.

We will use two bits to encode the states: 2'b00 for WALK_LEFT, 2'b01 for WALK_RIGHT, and 2'b10 for FALLING.
