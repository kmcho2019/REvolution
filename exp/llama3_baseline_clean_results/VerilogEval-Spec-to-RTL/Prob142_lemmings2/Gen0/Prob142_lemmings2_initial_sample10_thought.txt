To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we first identify the states: walking left, walking right, and falling. We also need to keep track of the direction before the fall to resume walking in the same direction when the ground reappears.

The state machine starts in the walking left state upon reset. It transitions to walking right upon receiving a bump on the left and to walking left upon receiving a bump on the right. If the Lemming is bumped on both sides, it still changes direction.

When the ground disappears (ground=0), the state machine transitions to the falling state, and "aaah!" is asserted. In this state, the Lemming does not change direction due to bumps. When the ground reappears (ground=1), the state machine transitions back to walking in the same direction as before the fall.

We will use two bits to encode the three states (walking left, walking right, and falling) and an additional signal to remember the direction before falling.
