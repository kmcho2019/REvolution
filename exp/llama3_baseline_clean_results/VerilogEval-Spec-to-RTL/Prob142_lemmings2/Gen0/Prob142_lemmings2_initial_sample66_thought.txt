To solve this problem, we can use a Moore state machine with three states: 
walking left, walking right, and falling. The state machine will have two 
main components: a combinational logic part to determine the next state, 
and a sequential logic part to store the current state and update it on 
the positive edge of the clock. The outputs will be determined solely by 
the current state. 

When the Lemming is walking left or right, it will switch directions if 
it hits an obstacle. If it hits both obstacles at the same time, it will 
still switch directions. 

When the ground disappears, the Lemming will fall and say "aaah!". The 
walking direction will not be affected by bumps while falling. When the 
ground reappears, the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction 
as before the fall.

We will use two bits to encode the three states: 00 for walking left, 
01 for walking right, and 10 for falling. The state machine will reset to 
the walking left state on the positive edge of the asynchronous reset.
