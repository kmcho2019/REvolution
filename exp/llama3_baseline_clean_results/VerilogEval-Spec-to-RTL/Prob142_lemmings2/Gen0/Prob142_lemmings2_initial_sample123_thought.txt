To implement the Moore state machine modeling the Lemming's behavior, we need to define the states and transitions. The Lemming can be in one of four states: walking left, walking right, falling after walking left, and falling after walking right. 

The state transitions can be described as follows:
- When the Lemming is walking left and bump_left is 1, it transitions to walking right.
- When the Lemming is walking right and bump_right is 1, it transitions to walking left.
- When the Lemming is walking left or right and ground becomes 0, it transitions to the corresponding falling state.
- When the Lemming is in a falling state and ground becomes 1, it transitions back to the corresponding walking state.
- When the Lemming is in a falling state, bump_left or bump_right does not affect its state.

We will use two bits to encode the four states. The states can be encoded as follows: 
- 2'b00: walking left
- 2'b01: walking right
- 2'b10: falling after walking left
- 2'b11: falling after walking right

We will also use a reset to initialize the Lemming to the walking left state.
