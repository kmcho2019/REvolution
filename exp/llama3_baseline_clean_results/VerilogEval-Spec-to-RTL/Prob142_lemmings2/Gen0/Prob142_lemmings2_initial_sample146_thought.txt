To implement the Lemmings state machine, we can use a Moore machine with three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The machine will transition between walking left and right based on the bump_left and bump_right inputs, and will enter the falling state when ground is 0. When ground becomes 1 again, the machine will return to its previous walking state. The outputs walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be determined by the current state and inputs. We will use an enum to represent the states, and a case statement to determine the next state and outputs based on the current state and inputs.
