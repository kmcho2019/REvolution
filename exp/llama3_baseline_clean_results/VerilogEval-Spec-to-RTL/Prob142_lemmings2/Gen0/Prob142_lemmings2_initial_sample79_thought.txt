To implement the Lemmings' finite state machine, we need to define the states and the transitions between them. We'll use a Moore state machine, where the outputs are a function of the current state. We'll have three main states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The transitions will be based on the input signals: bump_left, bump_right, and ground. When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition to the falling state. When the ground reappears, it will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.
