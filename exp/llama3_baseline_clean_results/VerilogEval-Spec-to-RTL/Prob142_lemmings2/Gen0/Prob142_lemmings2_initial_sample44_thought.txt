The problem can be solved using a Moore finite state machine with two states: walking left and walking right. 
We'll also keep track of the current direction to determine the next direction when ground reappears.
We'll have three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The transition between walking left and 
walking right will be based on the bump_left and bump_right inputs. The transition to the falling state 
will be based on the ground input. When the ground input is 0, the state machine will transition to the 
falling state and the aaah output will be asserted. When the ground input is 1 again, the state machine 
will transition back to the previous walking direction.
