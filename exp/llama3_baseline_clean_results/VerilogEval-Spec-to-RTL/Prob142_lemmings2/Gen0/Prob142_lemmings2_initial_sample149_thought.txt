The Moore state machine will have four states: idle_left, idle_right, falling, and stopped. 
The idle states represent the Lemming walking in a particular direction. 
The falling state represents the Lemming falling due to the absence of ground. 
The stopped state is used to avoid immediate state transitions due to bumps or ground re-appearance in the same cycle.

In the idle states, the machine will switch direction when bumped from either side or when the ground disappears. 
In the falling state, the machine will resume walking in the same direction as before when the ground re-appears.

We use a two-bit state variable to represent the four states and two bits to keep track of the walking direction before falling.

We will use combinational logic to determine the next state and output based on the current state, inputs, and walking direction.
