To implement the required behavior, we can design a Moore state machine with four states: walking left (WL), walking right (WR), falling left (FL), and falling right (FR). The state machine starts in the WL state. It switches between the WL and WR states when it receives a bump on either side. When the ground disappears, it transitions to the FL or FR state, depending on the current walking direction. When the ground reappears, it transitions back to the WL or WR state, depending on the previous walking direction. The output walk_left and walk_right are determined by the current state. The output aaah is 1 when the state machine is in the FL or FR state.
