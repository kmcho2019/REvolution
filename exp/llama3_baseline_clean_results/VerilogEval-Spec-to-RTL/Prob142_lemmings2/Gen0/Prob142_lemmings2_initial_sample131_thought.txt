To model the Lemmings' behavior, we will implement a Moore state machine with four states: Walking Left, Walking Right, Falling, and Resuming Walk. The machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. We will use a two-bit state variable to encode the four states. The walk_left, walk_right, and aaah outputs will be directly derived from the current state. The bump_left and bump_right signals will be used to determine the direction of walking after a bump. The ground signal will control the transitions between the walking and falling states. The areset signal will reset the machine to the Walking Left state.
