To implement this module, we'll first define the states of the finite state machine. We'll have four states: idle_left, idle_right, fall_left, and fall_right, representing the Lemming walking left or right and falling left or right. The initial state will be idle_left. When the ground disappears, we'll transition to the corresponding fall state. When the ground reappears, we'll transition back to the corresponding idle state. We'll also handle the bumps and change the direction accordingly. We'll use a Moore state machine, so the outputs will be determined by the current state.
