To implement the Lemmings' finite state machine, we can define four states: walking left, walking right, falling left, and falling right. 
The state machine will start in the walking left state after reset. 
When the Lemming is walking left and bump_left is asserted, it will transition to the walking right state. 
When the Lemming is walking right and bump_right is asserted, it will transition to the walking left state. 
If both bump_left and bump_right are asserted, it will also switch directions.
When ground becomes 0, the Lemming will transition to the falling state corresponding to its current walking direction. 
When ground becomes 1 again, the Lemming will transition back to the walking state corresponding to its previous walking direction while falling. 
The state machine will assert walk_left or walk_right based on its current state. 
When the Lemming is in a falling state, it will assert aaah.
