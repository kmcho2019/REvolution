To implement the Moore state machine, we will first identify the states and their transitions. The states are: walking left, walking right, and falling. The state transitions are as follows:
- From walking left: if bump_right is high, switch to walking right; if ground is low, switch to falling; otherwise, stay in walking left.
- From walking right: if bump_left is high, switch to walking left; if ground is low, switch to falling; otherwise, stay in walking right.
- From falling: if ground is high, switch back to the previous walking direction (before falling).

We will use two bits to represent the states, with the first bit representing the current walking direction (0 for left, 1 for right) and the second bit representing whether the Lemming is falling (1) or not (0).

We will also use two flip-flops to keep track of the current state and the previous walking direction before falling. The outputs will be determined by the current state.
