To find the minimum sum-of-products (SOP) form, we need to identify all the minterms that correspond to the input conditions that produce a logic-1 output. These are 2, 7, and 15, which in binary are 0010, 0111, and 1111, respectively. We then need to express these minterms as a sum of products.

For the minimum product-of-sums (POS) form, we need to identify all the maxterms that correspond to the input conditions that produce a logic-0 output. These are 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, and 14, which in binary are 0000, 0001, 0100, 0101, 0110, 1001, 1010, 1101, and 1110, respectively. We then need to express these maxterms as a product of sums.

The SOP expression is derived by directly summing the minterms for the given inputs, while the POS expression is derived by finding the complement of the sum of the minterms of the unwanted inputs and then taking the complement of the entire expression to get the product of sums.
