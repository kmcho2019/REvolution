The SOP expression can be derived by using the minterms corresponding to these numbers:

- For 7 (0111), the minterm is $\overline{a} \cdot b \cdot c \cdot d$.
- For 15 (1111), the minterm is $a \cdot b \cdot c \cdot d$.

Combining these minterms with OR operators gives us the SOP expression.
