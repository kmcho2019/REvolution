For SOP, we need to find the minimal sum of products that covers these cases. The binary representations are:
- 2: 0010
- 7: 0111
- 15: 1111

- For 2 (0010): `!a & !b & c & !d`
- For 7 (0111): `!a & b & c & d`
- For 15 (1111): `a & b & c & d`

The minimum SOP expression is the OR of these minterms.

For POS, we need to find the minimum product of sums. The approach involves finding the complements of the minterms that produce a 0 output and then taking the product of these sums.
