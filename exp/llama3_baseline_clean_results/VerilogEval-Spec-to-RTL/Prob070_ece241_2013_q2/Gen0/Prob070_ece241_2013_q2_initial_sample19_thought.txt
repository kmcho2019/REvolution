To find the minimum sum-of-products (SOP) and product-of-sums (POS) forms for the given conditions, we first need to identify the minterms (for SOP) and maxterms (for POS) that satisfy the output conditions. The inputs a, b, c, and d can be combined to represent the binary numbers 0 through 15. 

For out_sop, we are looking for the sum of the minterms corresponding to the numbers 2, 7, and 15, which are 0010, 0111, and 1111 in binary, respectively.

For out_pos, we are looking for the product of the maxterms corresponding to the numbers 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, and 14, which are 0000, 0001, 0100, 0101, 0110, 1001, 1010, 1101, and 1110 in binary, respectively.

However, to simplify the process, we note that for SOP, we directly look at the given conditions to form the expression, and for POS, we consider the complement of the unwanted conditions to form the expression.

The binary representations and their corresponding minterms for out_sop are:
- 2 (0010) translates to \overline{a}b\overline{c}\overline{d}
- 7 (0111) translates to \overline{a}bc\overline{d}
- 15 (1111) translates to abc\overline{d}

For out_pos, considering the maxterms for the unwanted conditions:
- 0 (0000) translates to a + b + c + d
- 1 (0001) translates to a + b + c + \overline{d}
- 4 (0100) translates to a + \overline{b} + c + d
- 5 (0101) translates to a + \overline{b} + c + \overline{d}
- 6 (0110) translates to a + \overline{b} + \overline{c} + d
- 9 (1001) translates to \overline{a} + b + c + \overline{d}
- 10 (1010) translates to \overline{a} + b + \overline{c} + d
- 13 (1101) translates to \overline{a} + \overline{b} + c + \overline{d}
- 14 (1110) translates to \overline{a} + \overline{b} + \overline{c} + d

The out_pos expression is the product of these sums.

However, considering the restrictions and desired outputs, we simplify the expressions directly in the Verilog implementation.
