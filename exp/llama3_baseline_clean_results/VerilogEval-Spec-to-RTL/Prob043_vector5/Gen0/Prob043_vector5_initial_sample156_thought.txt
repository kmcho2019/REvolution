We can use the XOR operator (^) in Verilog to compare two bits and produce a 1 if they are equal. 
The ~ operator is the bitwise NOT operator, which flips the bits of its operand. 
To compute all pairwise comparisons, we can use a combination of ~ and ^ operators to compare each signal with every other signal, including itself.
