To solve this problem, we will implement a Moore state machine with two states (walking left and walking right) and two inputs (bump_left and bump_right). The state machine will transition between the two states based on the input signals. When the Lemming is bumped on the left, it will switch to the walking right state, and when it's bumped on the right, it will switch to the walking left state. If it's bumped on both sides, it will still switch directions. The areset signal will asynchronously reset the state machine to the walking left state.
