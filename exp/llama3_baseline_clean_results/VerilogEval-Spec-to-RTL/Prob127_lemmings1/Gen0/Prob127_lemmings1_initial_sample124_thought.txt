To implement the Lemmings game, we'll design a Moore finite state machine (FSM) with two states: walking left and walking right. The FSM will have two inputs, bump_left and bump_right, and one internal output that determines the current state. We'll use a binary encoding for the states, where 0 represents walking left and 1 represents walking right. The next state logic will be based on the current state and the input values. If the Lemming is bumped on the left, it will switch to walking right, and if it's bumped on the right, it will switch to walking left. If it's bumped on both sides, it will still switch directions. The areset signal will asynchronously reset the FSM to the walking left state.
