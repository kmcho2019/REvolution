The TopModule can be designed as a Moore state machine with two states: walking left and walking right. 
The state machine has two inputs: bump_left and bump_right. The next state is determined by the current state and the input values. 
If the Lemming is walking left and bump_left is 1, it will switch to walking right. 
If the Lemming is walking left and bump_right is 1, it will switch to walking right as well. 
If the Lemming is walking right and bump_left is 1, it will switch to walking left. 
If the Lemming is walking right and bump_right is 1, it will switch to walking left as well. 
The state machine is reset to walking left when areset is 1. 
The outputs walk_left and walk_right are determined by the current state.
