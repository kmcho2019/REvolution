To implement the Lemmings game, we need a Moore state machine with two states: walking left and walking right. We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to model this behavior. The FSM will have two inputs (bump_left and bump_right) and one output (the direction of walking). When the Lemming is in the walking left state and receives a bump on the left, it will switch to the walking right state. Similarly, when it's in the walking right state and receives a bump on the right, it will switch to the walking left state. If it's bumped on both sides at the same time, it will still switch directions. We will use an asynchronous reset (areset) to reset the Lemming machine to the walking left state.
