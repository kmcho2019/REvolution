The TopModule can be implemented as a Moore state machine with two states: walking left and walking right. The state machine will have two inputs, bump_left and bump_right, and two outputs, walk_left and walk_right. The state machine will transition from walking left to walking right when it receives a bump on the left, and from walking right to walking left when it receives a bump on the right. If it receives bumps on both sides, it will still switch directions. The state machine will be reset to walking left when the areset signal is high.
