To implement the TopModule, we will first use an assign statement to directly implement the AND gate for the out_assign output. This block will have sensitivity to inputs a and b, ensuring that whenever either of these inputs changes, the block is re-evaluated. Inside the always block, we will use a simple if-else statement or a logical AND operation to compute the output out_alwaysblock.
