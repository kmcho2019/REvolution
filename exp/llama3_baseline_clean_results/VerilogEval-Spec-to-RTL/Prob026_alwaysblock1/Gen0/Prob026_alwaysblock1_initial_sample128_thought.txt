To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: 
1. Continuous assignment using the assign statement.
2. Combinational logic using an always block. 
In the assign statement, we will directly assign the result of the AND operation between 'a' and 'b' to 'out_assign'. 
For the combinational always block, we will use a sensitivity list that includes 'a' and 'b', and inside the block, we will assign the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock'. 
Both methods will ensure that 'out_assign' and 'out_alwaysblock' always reflect the AND result of 'a' and 'b'.
