To implement the TopModule with the required interface, we will use two methods: 
1. The continuous assignment statement (`assign`) to directly assign the AND operation result of `a` and `b` to `out_assign`.
2. A combinational `always` block to perform the AND operation and assign the result to `out_alwaysblock`.
Both methods achieve the same logical operation but demonstrate different ways to express combinational logic in Verilog.
