To implement the TopModule as described, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: one using a continuous assignment statement and the other using a combinational always block. The continuous assignment statement will directly assign the AND operation of inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a procedural assignment to assign the AND operation of 'a' and 'b' to 'out_alwaysblock' whenever either 'a' or 'b' changes.
