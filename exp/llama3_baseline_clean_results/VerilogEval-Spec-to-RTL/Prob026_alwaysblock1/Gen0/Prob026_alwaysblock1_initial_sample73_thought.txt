To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods: 
1. Continuous assignment: We will use the assign statement to directly assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'.
2. Combinational always block: We will use an always block without any sensitivity list (or with '@*' for SystemVerilog compatibility) to continuously execute the AND operation and assign the result to 'out_alwaysblock' whenever 'a' or 'b' changes.
