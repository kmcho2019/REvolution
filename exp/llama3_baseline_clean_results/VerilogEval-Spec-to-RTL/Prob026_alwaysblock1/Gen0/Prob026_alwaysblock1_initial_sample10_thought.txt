To implement the TopModule with the required interface, we can use a simple AND gate logic. The assign statement will continuously assign the AND result of inputs a and b to out_assign. For the combinational always block, we can use a non-blocking assignment to calculate the AND result of inputs a and b and assign it to out_alwaysblock.
