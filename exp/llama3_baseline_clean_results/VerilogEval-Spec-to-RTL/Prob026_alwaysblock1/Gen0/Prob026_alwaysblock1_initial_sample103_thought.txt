To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods: a continuous assignment and a combinational always block. The continuous assignment will directly assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use the '@(*)' sensitivity list to ensure the block is executed whenever 'a' or 'b' changes, and within this block, we will assign the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock'.
