To implement the TopModule, we will use two different methods to create an AND gate: a continuous assignment statement and a combinational always block. The continuous assignment will directly assign the AND operation result of inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. The combinational always block will perform the same operation and assign the result to 'out_alwaysblock'. This approach allows us to demonstrate both methods of creating combinational logic in Verilog.
