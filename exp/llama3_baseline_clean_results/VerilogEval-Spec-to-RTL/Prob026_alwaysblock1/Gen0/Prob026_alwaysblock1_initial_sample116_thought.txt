To implement the AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block, we will first use the assign statement to continuously assign the AND operation result of inputs 'a' and 'b' to 'out_assign'. Then, we will use an always block with a sensitivity list that includes 'a' and 'b' to continuously update 'out_alwaysblock' with the result of 'a' AND 'b' whenever either 'a' or 'b' changes.
