To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: a continuous assign statement and a combinational always block. The continuous assign statement will directly assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a sensitivity list that includes both inputs 'a' and 'b', and inside the block, we will assign the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock'. This way, 'out_alwaysblock' will also reflect the AND operation result of 'a' and 'b'.
