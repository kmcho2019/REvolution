To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods: 
1. Continuous assignment: We will use the assign statement to directly connect the output 'out_assign' to the logical AND of inputs 'a' and 'b'.
2. Combinational always block: We will use an always block with sensitivity to 'a' and 'b' to continuously evaluate the logical AND of 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_alwaysblock'.
