To implement the TopModule, we will use two different methods for creating an AND gate: 
1. Continuous assignment using the assign statement. 
2. A combinational always block.
The assign statement will directly assign the result of the AND operation between 'a' and 'b' to 'out_assign'.
The always block will use the @(*) sensitivity list to ensure it runs whenever any of the inputs change, and then assign the result of 'a' AND 'b' to 'out_alwaysblock'.
