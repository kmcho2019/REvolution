To implement the TopModule, we need to define two separate ways to create an AND gate: one using a continuous assignment and the other using a combinational always block. The continuous assignment will directly assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a simple if-else statement or logical expression to achieve the AND operation and assign the result to 'out_alwaysblock'.
