To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use two different methods to create an AND gate: an assign statement and a combinational always block. The assign statement will continuously assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. The combinational always block will perform the same operation and assign the result to 'out_alwaysblock'. This will demonstrate two approaches to implementing combinational logic in Verilog.
