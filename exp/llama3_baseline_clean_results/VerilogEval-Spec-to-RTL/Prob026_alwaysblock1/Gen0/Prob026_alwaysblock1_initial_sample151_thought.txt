To implement the TopModule, we can use two separate methods for creating an AND gate: one using a continuous assignment statement and the other using a combinational always block. The continuous assignment statement directly assigns the logical AND of inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we use an if-else statement or a logical operator to produce the AND operation and assign it to 'out_alwaysblock'. Both methods ensure that the output reflects the AND operation of the two inputs.
