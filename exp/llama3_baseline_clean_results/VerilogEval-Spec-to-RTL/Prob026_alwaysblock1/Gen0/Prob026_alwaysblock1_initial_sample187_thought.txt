To implement the TopModule, we will use two separate methods to perform an AND operation on inputs a and b. The assign statement will be used for a continuous assignment, which will drive the output out_assign directly based on the AND operation of a and b. For the combinational always block, we will use the AND operator within the block to assign the result of the operation to out_alwaysblock. Both methods will produce the same output, but they demonstrate different ways of achieving the same result in Verilog.
