To implement the TopModule, we will use two different methods to create an AND gate: one using the assign statement and the other using a combinational always block. The assign statement will directly assign the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a procedural approach to assign the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock' whenever the inputs 'a' or 'b' change.
