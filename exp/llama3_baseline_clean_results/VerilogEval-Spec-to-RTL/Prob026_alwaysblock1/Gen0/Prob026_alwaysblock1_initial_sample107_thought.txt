To implement the AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block in the TopModule, we need to define two output signals: out_assign and out_alwaysblock. The assign statement directly connects the output out_assign to the AND operation of inputs a and b. For the combinational always block, we will use a simple if-else or logical operation to produce the AND result on out_alwaysblock. This way, both methods will achieve the same logical operation but through different syntax and design approaches in Verilog.
