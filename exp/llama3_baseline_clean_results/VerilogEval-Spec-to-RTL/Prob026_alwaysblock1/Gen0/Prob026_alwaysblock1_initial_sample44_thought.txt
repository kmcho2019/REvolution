To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: 
1. Continuous assignment: This method directly assigns the output of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'.
2. Combinational always block: This method uses an always block to continuously monitor the inputs 'a' and 'b', and whenever either of them changes, it executes the block, assigning the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock'.
