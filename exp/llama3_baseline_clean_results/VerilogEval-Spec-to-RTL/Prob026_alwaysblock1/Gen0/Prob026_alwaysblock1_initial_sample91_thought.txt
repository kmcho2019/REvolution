To implement the TopModule with the required interface, we can use a combination of an assign statement and an always block. The assign statement will directly assign the AND operation result of inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a sensitive list that includes inputs 'a' and 'b', and within the block, we will assign the AND operation result to the output 'out_alwaysblock'. This way, both methods will produce the same output, which is the logical AND of 'a' and 'b'.
