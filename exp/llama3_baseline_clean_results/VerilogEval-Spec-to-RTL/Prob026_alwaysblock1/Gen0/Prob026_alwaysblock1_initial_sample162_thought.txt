To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: a continuous assignment statement and a combinational always block. The continuous assignment statement directly assigns the result of the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign'. For the combinational always block, we will use a sensitivity list that includes both 'a' and 'b', and within the block, we will assign the result of the AND operation to 'out_alwaysblock'.
