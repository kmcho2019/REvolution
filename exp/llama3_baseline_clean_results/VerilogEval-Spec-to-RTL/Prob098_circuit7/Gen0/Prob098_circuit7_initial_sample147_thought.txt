A T-Flip Flop is a sequential circuit that toggles its output (q) on every positive edge of the clock when the input (a or t) is high, and remains unchanged when the input is low. However, in this case, it seems to be an asynchronous reset (by the level of 'a') because once 'a' becomes 1, 'q' stays low regardless of the clock edges. Therefore, when 'a' is 0, it acts as a simple inverter with its output 'q' being set to 1 on the first rising clock edge after 'a' becomes 0, and stays at that value until 'a' becomes 1 again, at which point 'q' becomes 0 and stays that way until 'a' goes back to 0.
