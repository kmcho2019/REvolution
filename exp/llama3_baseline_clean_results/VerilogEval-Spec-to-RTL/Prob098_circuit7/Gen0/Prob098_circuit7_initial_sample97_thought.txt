To implement the TopModule, first analyze the given simulation waveforms. 
From the waveforms, it can be observed that the output q remains 1 as long as 
the input a remains 0. The moment a becomes 1, the output q remains 0. 
This behavior suggests a simple sequential circuit where the output q is 
inverted when a is 1, and this change occurs on the positive edge of the clock.

Therefore, the implementation of the TopModule should involve a flip-flop 
that captures the input a and inverts the output q when a is 1, triggered 
by the rising edge of the clock clk.
