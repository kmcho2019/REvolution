The circuit is a T flip-flop. A T flip-flop is a sequential circuit that toggles its output on every positive edge of the clock when the input 'a' is high. In this case, the output 'q' is initially unknown and becomes 1 on the first positive edge of the clock. Then, whenever 'a' is high at a positive edge of the clock, 'q' toggles. When 'a' is low at a positive edge of the clock, 'q' remains the same.
