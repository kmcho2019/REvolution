The module TopModule appears to be a T-Flip Flop. A T-Flip Flop is a 
sequential circuit that toggles its output (q) whenever the input (a) is 
high and the clock (clk) transitions from low to high. If the input (a) is 
low when the clock transitions from low to high, the output (q) remains 
unchanged. To implement this, we can use a reg to hold the current state 
of the flip flop and an always block to update the state on the positive 
edge of the clock.
