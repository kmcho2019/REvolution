The module TopModule can be implemented using a simple combinational logic circuit. 
By analyzing the given simulation waveform, we can see that output z is high (1) 
when either both x and y are low (0) or when both x and y are high (1). 
Otherwise, the output z is low (0). This behavior can be described by the Boolean 
expression z = (x == y). 
This can be further simplified to z = ~(x ^ y), where ^ denotes the XOR operation.
