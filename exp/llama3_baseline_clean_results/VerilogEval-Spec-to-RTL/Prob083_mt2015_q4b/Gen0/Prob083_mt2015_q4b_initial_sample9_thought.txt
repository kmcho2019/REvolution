The module TopModule can be implemented using a simple logical operation. 
By observing the simulation waveform, it appears that the output z is 1 
when both inputs x and y are 1, or when both inputs x and y are 0. 
This behavior can be achieved using the logical AND operation, the logical 
NOT operation, and the logical OR operation. 
The output z is the logical OR of the logical AND of x and y, 
and the logical AND of the logical NOT of x and the logical NOT of y.
This can be expressed as z = (x && y) || (!x && !y).
