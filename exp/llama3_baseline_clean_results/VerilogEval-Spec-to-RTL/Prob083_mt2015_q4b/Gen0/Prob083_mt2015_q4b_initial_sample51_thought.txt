The TopModule can be implemented using a simple combinational logic. 
The output z is 1 when both x and y are 1, or when both x and y are 0. 
This can be achieved using the logical AND and OR operators, and a negation operator to invert the inputs for the OR operation.
However, examining the waveform closely, it appears that z is simply the OR of x and y, or more precisely, the NOR of the inverted x and y.
We will use the logical NOR operation to implement this functionality.
