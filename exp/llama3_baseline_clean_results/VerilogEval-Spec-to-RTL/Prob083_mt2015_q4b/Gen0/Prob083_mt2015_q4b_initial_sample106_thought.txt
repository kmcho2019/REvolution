The module TopModule can be designed using a simple combinational logic circuit. 
Based on the simulation waveform provided, we can see that the output z is 
high (1) when both inputs x and y are high (1) or when both inputs are low (0). 
However, looking closely at the waveform, we can observe that the output z 
is actually the inverse of the NOR operation between x and y. 
Therefore, we can implement the TopModule using a NOR gate and then invert 
its output to get the desired output z.
