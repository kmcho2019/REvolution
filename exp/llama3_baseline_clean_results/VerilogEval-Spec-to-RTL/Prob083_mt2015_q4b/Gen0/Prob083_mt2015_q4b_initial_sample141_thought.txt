The TopModule can be designed as a simple combinational logic circuit. The output z is high (1) whenever either x is 0 and y is 0, or x is 1 and y is 1. This can be expressed as a logical OR operation between two AND operations: one checking for x and y being 0, and the other checking for x and y being 1. The output z will be 0 only when x and y are different.
