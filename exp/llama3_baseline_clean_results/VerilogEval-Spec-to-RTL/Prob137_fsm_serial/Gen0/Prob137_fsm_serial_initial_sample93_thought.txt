1. **IDLE**: Wait for the start bit (0) to begin receiving a byte.
2. **RECEIVE**: Once the start bit is detected, wait for 8 clock cycles to receive the 8 data bits.
3. **STOP**: After receiving the 8 data bits, check for the stop bit (1). If it's not present, go back to IDLE to wait for a stop bit before attempting to receive the next byte.

The FSM will use a counter to keep track of the number of bits received during the RECEIVE state. The FSM will also use a shift register to store the incoming data bits. If the stop bit is not correctly received, the FSM will reset its internal state to wait for a stop bit before proceeding.
