To implement the TopModule as a decade counter with a pause feature, we will use a 4-bit register to store the current count. The counter will be designed to increment when the slowena input is high and a positive clock edge occurs. The reset input, being active high and synchronous, will reset the counter to 0 when it is high and a positive clock edge occurs. 
The counter will wrap around to 0 after reaching 9 (1001 in binary), thus creating a decade counter that counts from 0 to 9 with a period of 10.
