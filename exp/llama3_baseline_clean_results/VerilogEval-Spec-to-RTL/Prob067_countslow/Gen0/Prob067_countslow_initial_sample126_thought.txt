To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the counter value. We will utilize a combinational logic to determine when to increment the counter. The reset will be implemented as a synchronous reset, which means it will only reset the counter when the clock is high and the reset signal is high. The slowena signal will be used to control when the counter increments. If slowena is high, the counter will increment on the next positive clock edge. If slowena is low, the counter will hold its current value.
