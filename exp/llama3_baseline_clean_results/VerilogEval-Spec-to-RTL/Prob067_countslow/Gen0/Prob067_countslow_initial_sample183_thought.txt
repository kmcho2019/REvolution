To implement a decade counter, we need a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 9 and resets to 0 when it reaches 10. We will use a simple binary counter and compare the count with 10 to determine when to wrap around. The reset input will be used to reset the counter to 0. The slowena input will be used to pause the counter. When slowena is high, the counter will increment; when slowena is low, the counter will remain at its current value.
