The problem requires designing a module named TopModule that takes two 3-bit inputs, 'a' and 'b', and produces three outputs: 
1. 'out_or_bitwise', which is the bitwise-OR of 'a' and 'b'.
2. 'out_or_logical', which is the logical-OR of 'a' and 'b'.
3. 'out_not', which is a 6-bit output where the upper half (bits [5:3]) contains the bitwise NOT of 'b', and the lower half (bits [2:0]) contains the bitwise NOT of 'a'.
This can be achieved using basic bitwise operators in Verilog.
