To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors `a` and `b` and assign it to `out_or_bitwise`.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors `a` and `b` and assign it to `out_or_logical`. 
   This can be done by checking if either of the input vectors has at least one bit set to 1.
3. Compute the bitwise-NOT (inverse) of both input vectors `a` and `b`, and assign the inverse of `b` to the upper half of `out_not` and the inverse of `a` to the lower half.
