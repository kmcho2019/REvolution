To implement the TopModule, we need to perform the following operations:
- Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors a and b, and assign the result to out_or_bitwise.
- Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors a and b. This can be done by checking if either of the vectors has at least one bit set to 1, and assign the result to out_or_logical.
- Compute the inverse (NOT) of both input vectors a and b. The inverse of b should be placed in the upper half of out_not (bits [5:3]), and the inverse of a should be placed in the lower half (bits [2:0]).
