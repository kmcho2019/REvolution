To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. 
2. Logical-OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. 
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign them to the 'out_not' output. 
We will use Verilog's built-in bitwise and logical operators to perform these operations.
