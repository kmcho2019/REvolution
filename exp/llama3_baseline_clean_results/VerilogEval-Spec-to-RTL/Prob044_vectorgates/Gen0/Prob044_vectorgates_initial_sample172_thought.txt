To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. 
2. Logical OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. 
3. Bitwise NOT operation on both input vectors 'a' and 'b' and then concatenate them to form the output 'out_not'.

We will use Verilog's bitwise operators to achieve these operations. The bitwise OR operator is '|', the logical OR operator is '||', and the bitwise NOT operator is '~'.
