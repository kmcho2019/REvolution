To implement the TopModule, we need to perform the following operations:
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_or_bitwise'.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_or_logical'. 
   We can achieve this by checking if any of the bits in either 'a' or 'b' are high. 
   If yes, then 'out_or_logical' should be 1, otherwise 0.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign them to 'out_not'. 
   The inverse of 'b' should be placed in the upper half of 'out_not' (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half (bits [2:0]).
We will use Verilog's bitwise operators to perform these operations.
