To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR operation between input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce 'out_or_bitwise'.
2. Logical-OR operation between input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce 'out_or_logical'. Since both 'a' and 'b' are 3-bit vectors, we will perform a logical-OR operation on the non-zero conditions of these vectors.
3. NOT operation on both input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce the 'out_not' vector. We will place the inverse of 'b' in the upper half (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half (bits [2:0]) of 'out_not'.

We will use Verilog's bitwise and logical operators to achieve these operations.
