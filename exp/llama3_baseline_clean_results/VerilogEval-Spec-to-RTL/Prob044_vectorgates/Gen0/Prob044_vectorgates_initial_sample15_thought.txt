The TopModule should have two input ports, a and b, each 3 bits wide. It needs to compute three outputs: the bitwise OR of a and b (out_or_bitwise), the logical OR of a and b (out_or_logical), and the inverse (NOT) of both a and b (out_not). For out_not, the inverse of b should be placed in the upper half (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of a in the lower half (bits [2:0]). We can achieve the bitwise OR using the bitwise OR operator (|), the logical OR using the logical OR operator (||), and the NOT operation using the bitwise NOT operator (~).
