To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors a and b.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors a and b. This can be done by checking if either of the vectors has any bits set.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors a and b, and then combine the results into the out_not output, where the upper half contains the inverse of b and the lower half contains the inverse of a.

We will use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators (|) for the bitwise-OR operation, 
the logical OR operator (||) for the logical-OR operation, and the bitwise NOT operator (~) for the inverse operation.
