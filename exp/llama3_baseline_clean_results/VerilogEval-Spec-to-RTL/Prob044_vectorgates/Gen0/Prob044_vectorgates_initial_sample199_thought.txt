To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise OR operation on the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to get 'out_or_bitwise'.
2. Logical OR operation on the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to get 'out_or_logical'.
3. NOT operation on both input vectors 'a' and 'b' to get the inverse, which will be placed in 'out_not' with the inverse of 'b' in the upper half and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half.
