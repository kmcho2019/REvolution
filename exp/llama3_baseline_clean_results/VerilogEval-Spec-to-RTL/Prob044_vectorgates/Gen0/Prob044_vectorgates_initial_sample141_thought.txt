To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise OR operation between inputs a and b to generate out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical OR operation between inputs a and b to generate out_or_logical.
3. Bitwise NOT operation on both inputs a and b to generate out_not.
The bitwise NOT operation will be performed on both inputs and the results 
will be concatenated to form out_not, with the inverse of b in the upper 
half and the inverse of a in the lower half.
