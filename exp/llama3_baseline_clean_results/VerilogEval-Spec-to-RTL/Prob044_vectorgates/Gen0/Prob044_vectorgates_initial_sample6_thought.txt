To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. This can be done by comparing each bit of 'a' and 'b' and setting the corresponding bit in 'out_or_bitwise' to 1 if either of the bits is 1.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. This can be done by checking if any of the bits in 'a' or 'b' is 1 and setting 'out_or_logical' to 1 if so, and 0 otherwise.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign them to 'out_not'. The inverse of 'b' should be placed in the upper half of 'out_not' (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half.
