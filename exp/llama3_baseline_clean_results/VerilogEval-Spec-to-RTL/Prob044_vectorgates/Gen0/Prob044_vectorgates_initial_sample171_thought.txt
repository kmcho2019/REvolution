To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in bitwise operators in Verilog. The bitwise-OR operation will be performed using the | operator. The logical-OR operation will be performed by checking if either of the input vectors is non-zero. The inverse (NOT) of both vectors will be performed using the ~ operator. We will use bit slicing to place the inverse of b in the upper half of out_not and the inverse of a in the lower half.
