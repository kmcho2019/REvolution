To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three operations: 
1. Bitwise OR of two 3-bit vectors a and b to get out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical OR of two 3-bit vectors a and b to get out_or_logical.
3. Inversion (NOT) of both vectors a and b and combine them to get out_not.

The bitwise OR operation will be performed using the bitwise OR operator (|).
The logical OR operation will be performed using the logical OR operator (||) and will need to be applied after checking if any of the bits in the vectors are high.
The inversion operation will be performed using the bitwise NOT operator (~) and will be applied to both vectors a and b. 
The inverted vectors will then be concatenated to form out_not with the inverse of b in the upper half and the inverse of a in the lower half.
