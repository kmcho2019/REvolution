To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_or_bitwise'.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two 3-bit input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_or_logical'. 
   Since both inputs are 3-bit vectors, we need to apply the logical-OR operation to all bits of both vectors.
   If any of the bits in either vector is 1, the result should be 1; otherwise, the result should be 0.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors and assign them to 'out_not'. 
   The inverse of 'b' should be placed in the upper half of 'out_not' (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half (bits [2:0]).
