To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise OR operation between the two input vectors a and b to get out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical OR operation between the two input vectors a and b to get out_or_logical.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors a and b and concatenate them to get out_not.
We can use the Verilog bitwise OR operator (|) for the bitwise OR operation, 
the logical OR operator (||) for the logical OR operation, and the bitwise NOT operator (~) for the inverse operation.
