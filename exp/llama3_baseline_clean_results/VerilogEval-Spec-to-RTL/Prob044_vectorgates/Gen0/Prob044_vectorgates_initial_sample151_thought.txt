To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors (a and b) and assign it to out_or_bitwise.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors (a and b) and assign it to out_or_logical.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both vectors (a and b) and assign the inverse of b to the upper half of out_not and the inverse of a to the lower half of out_not.

The bitwise-OR operation can be performed using the | operator in Verilog.
The logical-OR operation can be performed using the || operator in Verilog, which returns 1 if either of the operands is non-zero.
The NOT operation can be performed using the ~ operator in Verilog.
