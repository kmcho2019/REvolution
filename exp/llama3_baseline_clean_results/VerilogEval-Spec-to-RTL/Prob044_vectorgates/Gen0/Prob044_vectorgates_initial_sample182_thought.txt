To implement the TopModule, we need to perform the following operations:
1. Compute the bitwise OR of the two 3-bit input vectors a and b.
2. Compute the logical OR of the two 3-bit input vectors a and b.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both 3-bit input vectors a and b.
4. Assign the inverse of b to the upper half of the 6-bit output out_not (bits [5:3]) and the inverse of a to the lower half (bits [2:0]).
We will use Verilog's bitwise and logical operators to achieve this.
