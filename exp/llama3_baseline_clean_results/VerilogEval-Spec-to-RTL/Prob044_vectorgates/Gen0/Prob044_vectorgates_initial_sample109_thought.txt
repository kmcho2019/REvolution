1. Bitwise-OR: Perform a bit-by-bit OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. This will result in a 3-bit vector where each bit is the OR of the corresponding bits in 'a' and 'b'.
2. Logical-OR: This operation treats the entire input vectors 'a' and 'b' as single entities and performs a logical OR operation on them. Since both vectors are 3 bits, they are considered true if any of their bits are 1.
3. NOT (Invert) operation: Invert both vectors 'a' and 'b'. The inverted 'b' will occupy the upper half of the output 'out_not' (bits [5:3]), and the inverted 'a' will occupy the lower half (bits [2:0]).
