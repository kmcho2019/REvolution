To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce 'out_or_bitwise'. 
2. Logical-OR operation between the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce 'out_or_logical'. 
3. Bitwise-NOT operation on both 'a' and 'b' to produce the inverse, then concatenate the inverse of 'b' and 'a' to produce 'out_not'.

We will use Verilog's built-in operators for bitwise-OR (~|), logical-OR (||), and bitwise-NOT (~) to achieve these operations.
