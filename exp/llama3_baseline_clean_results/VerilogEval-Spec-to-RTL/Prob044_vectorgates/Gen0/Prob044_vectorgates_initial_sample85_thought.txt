To implement the TopModule, we need to perform the following operations:
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors `a` and `b` and assign the result to `out_or_bitwise`.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors `a` and `b` and assign the result to `out_or_logical`. 
   This can be done by checking if any of the bits in either `a` or `b` is 1.
3. Compute the bitwise NOT of the two input vectors `a` and `b`, and assign the result to the lower and upper half of `out_not` respectively.
