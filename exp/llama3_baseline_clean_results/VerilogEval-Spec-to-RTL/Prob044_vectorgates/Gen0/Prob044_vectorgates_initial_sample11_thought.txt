To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR of two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to get 'out_or_bitwise'. 
2. Logical-OR of two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to get 'out_or_logical'. 
3. Inverse (NOT) of both vectors 'a' and 'b' to get 'out_not'. 
We will use Verilog's built-in bitwise and logical operators to achieve these operations.
