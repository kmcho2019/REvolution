The TopModule will be implemented with three main operations: 
1. Computing the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' using the bitwise OR operator.
2. Computing the logical-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b'. This can be achieved by first converting the vectors to their decimal equivalents, checking if either of them is non-zero, and then outputting the result of the logical OR operation.
3. Computing the inverse (NOT) of both vectors 'a' and 'b' and placing the results in the 'out_not' vector, with the inverse of 'b' in the upper half and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half.
