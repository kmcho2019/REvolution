To solve this problem, we can design a module named TopModule with the specified interface. 
The module will perform three main operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR of the two input vectors a and b, and assign the result to out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical-OR of the two input vectors a and b, and assign the result to out_or_logical. 
   This can be done by checking if either of the vectors has at least one bit set to 1.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both input vectors a and b, and assign the result to out_not.
   The inverse of b will be placed in the upper half of out_not (bits [5:3]), and the inverse of a in the lower half.
