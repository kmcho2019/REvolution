To implement the TopModule, we need to define the bitwise-OR operation, logical-OR operation, and NOT operation for the given inputs. 
For the bitwise-OR operation, we perform a bit-by-bit OR operation on the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' to produce the 'out_or_bitwise' output. 
The logical-OR operation checks if any bit in either 'a' or 'b' is 1. If so, it sets 'out_or_logical' to 1; otherwise, it sets it to 0. 
For the NOT operation, we invert each bit of 'a' and 'b' separately and concatenate the results to form the 'out_not' output.
