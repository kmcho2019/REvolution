To implement the TopModule, we need to perform the following operations:
1. Bitwise OR: Perform a bitwise OR operation on the two 3-bit input vectors a and b. This will result in a 3-bit output vector out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical OR: Perform a logical OR operation on the two 3-bit input vectors a and b. This will result in a 1-bit output out_or_logical.
3. NOT operation: Perform a NOT operation on both input vectors a and b. The NOT of b should be placed in the upper half of the 6-bit output vector out_not, and the NOT of a should be placed in the lower half.

We can use the bitwise OR operator (|) and logical OR operator (||) in Verilog to perform the respective operations.
