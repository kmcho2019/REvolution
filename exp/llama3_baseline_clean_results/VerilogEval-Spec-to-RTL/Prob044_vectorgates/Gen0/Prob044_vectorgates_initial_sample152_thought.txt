To implement the TopModule, we need to break down the required operations:
1. Bitwise OR operation: Perform a bit-by-bit OR operation between the input vectors 'a' and 'b'.
2. Logical OR operation: Check if either of the input vectors 'a' or 'b' is non-zero.
3. NOT operation: Invert the bits of both 'a' and 'b' and assign them to the 'out_not' output, with the inverted 'b' in the upper half and the inverted 'a' in the lower half.

We can use Verilog's built-in operators for these operations: '|' for bitwise OR, '||' for logical OR, and '~' for NOT.
