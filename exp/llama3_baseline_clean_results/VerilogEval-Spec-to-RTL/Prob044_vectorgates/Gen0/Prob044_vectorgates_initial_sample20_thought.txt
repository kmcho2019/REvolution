To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three separate operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_or_bitwise'.
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_or_logical'. 
   This can be done by checking if any of the bits in either 'a' or 'b' is 1.
3. Compute the inverse (NOT) of both input vectors 'a' and 'b', and assign the results to 'out_not'. 
   The inverse of 'b' should be placed in the upper half of 'out_not' (bits [5:3]), and the inverse of 'a' in the lower half.
