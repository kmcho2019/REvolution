The TopModule should perform three operations: 
1. Bitwise-OR operation on inputs a and b to produce out_or_bitwise.
2. Logical-OR operation on inputs a and b to produce out_or_logical.
3. Bitwise-NOT operation on inputs a and b to produce out_not, where the upper half of out_not is the inverse of b and the lower half is the inverse of a.
We can achieve these operations using Verilog's built-in bitwise operators.
