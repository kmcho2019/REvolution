To implement the TopModule, we will use a 10-bit down-counter. 
When the load signal is high, the counter will be loaded with the 10-bit data. 
When the load signal is low, the counter will decrement by 1 at each positive clock edge. 
The terminal count signal tc will be asserted when the counter reaches 0 and will remain high until the counter is loaded again. 
We will use a conditional statement to check the load signal and update the counter accordingly. 
We will also use a D flip-flop to store the current count value.
