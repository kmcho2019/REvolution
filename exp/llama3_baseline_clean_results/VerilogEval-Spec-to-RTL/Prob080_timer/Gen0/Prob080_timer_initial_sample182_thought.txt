To implement the TopModule, we will use a down-counter that is loaded with the 10-bit data when the load signal is high. When the load signal is low, the counter will decrement by 1 at each clock cycle. The tc signal will be asserted when the counter reaches 0. We will use a reg to hold the counter value and assign it to the output tc when it reaches 0. We will also use a conditional statement to check the load signal and perform the corresponding action.
