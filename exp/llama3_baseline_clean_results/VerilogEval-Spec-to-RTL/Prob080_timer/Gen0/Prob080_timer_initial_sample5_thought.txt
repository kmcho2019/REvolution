To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of sequential and combinational logic. We will use a 10-bit register to store the countdown value and a D flip-flop to update the register on the positive edge of the clock. The combinational logic will handle the loading and decrementing of the counter. When the load signal is high, the register will be loaded with the 10-bit data. When the load signal is low, the register will decrement by 1. The terminal count signal will be asserted when the counter reaches 0.
