To implement the TopModule, we will use a 10-bit down-counter with a load feature. The load signal will determine whether to load the counter with the input data or decrement the counter. We will use a D flip-flop to trigger the counter on the positive edge of the clock. When the counter reaches 0, it will assert the tc signal. We will also add a mechanism to stop the counter once it reaches 0, until the load signal is asserted again.
