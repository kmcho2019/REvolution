To implement the TopModule, we will use a down-counter with a 10-bit internal register. 
We will use a single D flip-flop to load the internal counter with the 10-bit data when load = 1.
When load = 0, we will decrement the internal counter by 1 at each positive clock edge.
We will assert the output signal tc when the internal counter reaches 0.
Once the internal counter reaches 0, it will stay 0 until the counter is loaded again.
