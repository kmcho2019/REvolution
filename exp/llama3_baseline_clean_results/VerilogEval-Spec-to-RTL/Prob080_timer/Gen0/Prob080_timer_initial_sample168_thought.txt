To implement the TopModule, I will use a single 10-bit register (D flip-flop) to store the countdown value. At each positive clock edge, I will check the load signal. If load is high, I will load the internal counter with the 10-bit data. If load is low, I will decrement the internal counter by 1. The output signal tc will be asserted when the internal counter reaches 0. To prevent the counter from wrapping around, I will keep the counter value at 0 once it reaches 0 until a new value is loaded.
