To implement the TopModule, we need to design a down-counter with a load capability. The module will have a 10-bit internal counter. On every positive clock edge, if the load signal is high, the internal counter will be loaded with the 10-bit data. If the load signal is low, the internal counter will decrement by 1. The tc (terminal count) signal will be asserted when the internal counter reaches 0. The internal counter will stop decrementing once it reaches 0 until it is loaded again.
