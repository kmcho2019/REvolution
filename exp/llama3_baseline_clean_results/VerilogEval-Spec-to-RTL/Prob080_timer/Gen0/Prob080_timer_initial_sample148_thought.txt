To implement the TopModule, we will use a single D flip-flop for each bit of the internal counter, and also for the load and tc signals. The internal counter is a 10-bit down-counter. When load is 1, the internal counter is loaded with the 10-bit data. When load is 0, the internal counter decrements by 1. The tc signal is asserted when the internal counter reaches 0. Once the internal counter reaches 0, it stays 0 until it is loaded again.
