To implement the TopModule, we will use a single 10-bit register to act as the down-counter. The register will be loaded with the input data when the load signal is high. On every positive clock edge, if the load signal is low, the register will decrement by 1. The terminal count (tc) signal will be high when the register reaches 0 and will stay high until the register is reloaded with a new value. This implementation meets the requirements by using a down-counter and asserting the tc signal when the count reaches 0.
