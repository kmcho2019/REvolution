To implement Rule 110, we first need to understand the rules governing the next state of each cell based on its current state and the states of its two neighbors. We will create a 512-cell system where each cell's next state is determined by the current states of itself and its neighbors. The boundaries of the system will be handled by assuming that the cells outside the system (q[-1] and q[512]) are always zero (off). The circuit will advance by one time step on each positive edge of the clock signal and will load the initial state from the data input when the load signal is high.
