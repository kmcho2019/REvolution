The module TopModule will be designed to handle three different output vectors: out_both, out_any, and out_different. 
To generate out_both, we'll compare each bit with its left neighbor and output '1' if both are '1'. 
For out_any, we'll compare each bit with its right neighbor and output '1' if either of them is '1'. 
For out_different, we'll compare each bit with its left neighbor, considering the vector as circular, and output '1' if they are different.
We'll use bit-by-bit operations and looping to efficiently generate the output vectors.
