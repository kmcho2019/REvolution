To implement the TopModule, we'll use a combination of logical operations and loop constructs to efficiently generate the output vectors. We'll iterate through each bit of the input vector, comparing it with its neighbor to the left or right (or wrapping around for the edge cases) to produce the desired output vectors. We'll utilize the bitwise AND operator (&) for the out_both output, the bitwise OR operator (|) for the out_any output, and the bitwise XOR operator (^) for the out_different output.
