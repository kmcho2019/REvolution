To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we need to swap the individual bytes. Since a 32-bit vector consists of 4 bytes, we can achieve this by rearranging the bytes in reverse order. We can do this by assigning the least significant byte (bits 7:0) of the output to the most significant byte (bits 31:24) of the input, the next byte (bits 15:8) of the output to the next most significant byte (bits 23:16) of the input, and so on.
