To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can simply rearrange the byte positions. A 32-bit vector can be divided into four 8-bit bytes (or nibbles of 4 bits each, but for this case, we're focusing on bytes). The original order is byte0, byte1, byte2, byte3 from most significant to least significant. To reverse the order, we simply swap these positions to byte3, byte2, byte1, byte0. This can be implemented in Verilog by using bit-range or part-select to extract and rearrange the bytes.
