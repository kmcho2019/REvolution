To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can simply rearrange the bytes. A 32-bit vector consists of 4 bytes. If we consider the input vector as in[31:0], the byte order can be reversed by assigning the bytes in reverse order. This means the first byte of the input becomes the last byte of the output, the second byte becomes the third, the third becomes the second, and the last byte becomes the first.
