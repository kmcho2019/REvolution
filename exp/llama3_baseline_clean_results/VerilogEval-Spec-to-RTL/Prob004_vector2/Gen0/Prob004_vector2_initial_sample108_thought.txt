To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we need to swap the bytes in reverse order. Since each byte is 8 bits, a 32-bit vector consists of 4 bytes. We can achieve the byte reversal by assigning the first byte of the output to the last byte of the input, the second byte of the output to the third byte of the input, the third byte of the output to the second byte of the input, and the fourth byte of the output to the first byte of the input.
