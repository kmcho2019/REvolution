To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we need to swap the bytes. Since a byte is 8 bits, we can divide the 32-bit vector into four bytes (in[31:24], in[23:16], in[15:8], in[7:0]) and then reverse their order in the output (out[7:0] = in[31:24], out[15:8] = in[23:16], out[23:16] = in[15:8], out[31:24] = in[7:0]).
