To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we need to swap the bytes. Since a byte is 8 bits, we can divide the 32-bit vector into 4 bytes and then reverse their order. The first byte (most significant 8 bits) becomes the fourth byte (least significant 8 bits), the second byte becomes the third, the third byte becomes the second, and the fourth byte becomes the first. This can be achieved using simple bit manipulation and concatenation in Verilog.
