To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can assign each byte of the input to the corresponding byte of the output, but in reverse order. Since the input and output are 32 bits wide, we can divide them into four bytes (each 8 bits wide). The least significant byte (bits 7:0) of the input becomes the most significant byte (bits 31:24) of the output, the second byte (bits 15:8) becomes the second most significant byte (bits 23:16), and so on.
