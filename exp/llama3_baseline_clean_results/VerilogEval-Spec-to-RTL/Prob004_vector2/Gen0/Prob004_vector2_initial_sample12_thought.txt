To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can use Verilog's bit-slicing and concatenation features. The idea is to break the 32-bit input into four 8-bit bytes, and then concatenate these bytes in reverse order to form the output. This can be achieved by assigning the most significant byte of the input to the least significant byte of the output, the second most significant byte to the second least significant byte, and so on.
