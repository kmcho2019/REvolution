To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can simply rearrange the bytes. A 32-bit vector consists of 4 bytes. The original byte order is B3 B2 B1 B0, where B3 is the most significant byte and B0 is the least significant byte. The reversed byte order would be B0 B1 B2 B3. We can achieve this by assigning the least significant byte of the input to the most significant byte of the output, the second least significant byte to the second most significant byte, and so on.
