To reverse the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can simply swap the bytes. Since a 32-bit vector consists of 4 bytes (assuming 8 bits per byte), we can use Verilog's bit-level manipulation capabilities to achieve this. The idea is to assign the bytes of the input vector to the output vector in reverse order. Specifically, the most significant byte (MSB) of the input will become the least significant byte (LSB) of the output, and vice versa.
