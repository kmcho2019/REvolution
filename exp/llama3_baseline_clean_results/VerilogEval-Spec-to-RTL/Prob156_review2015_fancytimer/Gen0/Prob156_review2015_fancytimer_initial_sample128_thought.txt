To implement the TopModule, we'll use a finite state machine (FSM) with several states: idle, pattern detection, delay loading, counting, and done. 
We'll use a counter to count the delay and another counter to count the remaining time. 
When the pattern 1101 is detected, we'll shift in the next 4 bits to determine the delay and then start counting. 
After counting, we'll assert the done signal and wait for the ack signal before resetting to the idle state.
