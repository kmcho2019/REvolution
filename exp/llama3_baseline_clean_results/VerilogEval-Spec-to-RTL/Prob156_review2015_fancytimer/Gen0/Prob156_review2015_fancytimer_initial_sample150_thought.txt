To solve this problem, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with the following states:
- IDLE: This is the initial state, where the FSM searches for the pattern 1101.
- DELAY_LOAD: After detecting the pattern 1101, the FSM shifts in the next 4 bits to determine the delay duration.
- COUNTING: The FSM counts for the specified duration and outputs the remaining time.
- DONE: After counting, the FSM asserts the done signal and waits for the ack signal to reset.

The FSM will use a counter to keep track of the remaining time and will decrement it every 1000 clock cycles. The current remaining time will be outputted on the count[3:0] pins.

We will use a serial shift register to shift in the delay bits. The counting output will be asserted when the FSM is in the COUNTING state.

The FSM will reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high or when the ack signal is asserted after the DONE state.
