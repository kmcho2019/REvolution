To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different states of the timer. The FSM will have several states: idle (searching for the pattern), delay_capture (capturing the delay value), counting (decrementing the counter), and done (waiting for acknowledgement). We will use a counter to keep track of the remaining time and another register to store the delay value. The count output will be driven by the remaining time counter.
