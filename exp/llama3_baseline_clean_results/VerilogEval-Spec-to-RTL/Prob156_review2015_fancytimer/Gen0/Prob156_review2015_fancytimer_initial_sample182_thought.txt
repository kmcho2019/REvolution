To implement the described functionality, we'll use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with several states: 
- `SEARCH`: the initial state, looking for the pattern 1101 on the data input.
- `DELAY`: once the pattern is detected, shifting in the 4-bit delay value.
- `COUNT`: the counting state, where the circuit counts down from the specified delay value * 1000.
- `DONE`: after the countdown is finished, waiting for the ack signal.
We will also use a counter to keep track of the remaining time and another to count the clock cycles during the COUNT state.
