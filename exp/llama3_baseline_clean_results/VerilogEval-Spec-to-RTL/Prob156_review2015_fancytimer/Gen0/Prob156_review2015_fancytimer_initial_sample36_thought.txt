To implement the TopModule, we need to break it down into several states: 
(1) searching for the pattern 1101, 
(2) shifting in the delay bits, 
(3) counting down, and 
(4) waiting for the acknowledge signal. 
We will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the transitions between these states. 
The counting down state will use a counter to keep track of the remaining time, 
and we will use another counter to keep track of the current count. 
The FSM will also control the output signals, including counting, done, and count.
