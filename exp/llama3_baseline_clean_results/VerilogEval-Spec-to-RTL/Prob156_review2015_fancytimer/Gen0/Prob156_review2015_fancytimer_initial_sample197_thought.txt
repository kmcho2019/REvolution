The problem can be solved by designing a finite state machine (FSM) that has several states to handle different stages of the timer: 
(1) IDLE state: searching for the input pattern 1101, 
(2) SHIFT state: shifting in the next 4 bits after the pattern is detected, 
(3) COUNTING state: counting down from the specified delay, and 
(4) DONE state: waiting for the ack signal after the timer has timed out. 
The FSM will use a counter to keep track of the remaining time and assert the counting output when it's counting. 
After the timer has timed out, it will assert the done output and wait for the ack signal to reset.
