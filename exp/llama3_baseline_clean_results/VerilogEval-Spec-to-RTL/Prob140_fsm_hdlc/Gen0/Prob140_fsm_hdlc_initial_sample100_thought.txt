To implement the TopModule, we will design a Moore-type finite state machine (FSM) with the following states:
- S0: Initial state, assuming the previous input was 0.
- S1: One 1 received.
- S2: Two 1s received.
- S3: Three 1s received.
- S4: Four 1s received.
- S5: Five 1s received, check for discard.
- S6: Six 1s received, check for flag.
- S7: Seven or more 1s received, error.

We will use these states to recognize the three sequences specified and generate the output signals accordingly.
