To solve this problem, we'll design a Moore-type finite state machine that keeps track of consecutive 1s in the input stream and outputs the required signals. The FSM should have states to recognize the 01111110 sequence for flagging frames, the 0111110 sequence to discard a bit, and a sequence of 7 or more consecutive 1s for error detection. Since we're dealing with a Moore-type FSM, the output will be a function of the current state only. We'll implement this in Verilog, ensuring that the state transitions occur on the positive edge of the clock and that the output signals are asserted for a complete cycle after the respective condition is met.
