To implement the required functionality, we'll design a Moore-type finite state machine with states to track the number of consecutive 1s and 0s. The states can be:
- Idle: The initial state or after seeing a 0.
- Ones1 to Ones5: After seeing 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 consecutive 1s, respectively.
- Disc: The state after seeing 6 consecutive 1s (indicating a discard).
- Flag: The state after seeing 7 consecutive 1s followed by a 0 (indicating a flag).
- Error: The state after seeing 8 or more consecutive 1s (indicating an error).
We'll use these states to generate the disc, flag, and err signals.
