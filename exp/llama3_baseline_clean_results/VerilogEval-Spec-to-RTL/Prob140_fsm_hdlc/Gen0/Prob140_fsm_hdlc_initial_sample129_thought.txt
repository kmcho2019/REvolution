To solve this problem, I will implement a Moore-type finite state machine (FSM) in Verilog. The FSM will have states to track the number of consecutive 1s and 0s. The states will be designed to detect the required sequences and output the corresponding signals. 

The states will be as follows:
- IDLE: Initial state where the previous input was 0.
- ONE: One consecutive 1.
- TWO: Two consecutive 1s.
- THREE: Three consecutive 1s.
- FOUR: Four consecutive 1s.
- FIVE: Five consecutive 1s.
- DISCARD: Six consecutive 1s, needs to discard a bit.
- FLAG: Seven consecutive 1s, flag the beginning/end of a frame.
- ERROR: Eight or more consecutive 1s, signal an error.

The FSM will transition between these states based on the input and will output the corresponding signals when necessary.
