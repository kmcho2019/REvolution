To solve this problem, we can design a Moore-type finite state machine (FSM) with states to track the number of consecutive ones and the preceding zero or one. The FSM will have states to recognize the specific sequences of interest: 0111110, 01111110, and 7 or more consecutive ones. We'll use a binary counter to track the number of consecutive ones and decode the states based on this counter and the current input. The outputs disc, flag, and err will be asserted in the respective states that correspond to the desired sequences. 
The states will be:
- IDLE: Reset state
- ONE: First '1' encountered
- ONES_2_TO_5: 2 to 5 consecutive ones
- ONES_6: 6 consecutive ones (potentially followed by a 0 for disc or another 1 for flag)
- ONES_7_OR_MORE: 7 or more consecutive ones, indicating an error
- FLAG: Received flag sequence 01111110
- DISCARD: Received sequence that needs a bit discarded (0111110)

The FSM will transition between these states based on the current state, input, and the count of consecutive ones.
