To implement the required Moore-type finite state machine, we will define states to track the sequence of bits received. The states will be:
- S0: Initial state or after receiving a 0.
- S1: After receiving a 1.
- S2: After receiving two 1s.
- S3: After receiving three 1s.
- S4: After receiving four 1s.
- S5: After receiving five 1s.
- S6: After receiving six 1s (flag condition).
- ERR: Error state, after receiving seven or more 1s.

The disc, flag, and err signals will be asserted based on the current state and the next input bit. When the FSM is reset, it will transition back to S0. We will use a parameterized case statement or if-else statements to handle state transitions and output signal generation.
