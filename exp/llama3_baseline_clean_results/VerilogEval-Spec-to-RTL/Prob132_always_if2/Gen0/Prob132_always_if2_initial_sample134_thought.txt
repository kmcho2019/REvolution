The bug in the provided module is that the `shut_off_computer` and `keep_driving` signals are not being properly reset when their respective conditions are not met. The `shut_off_computer` signal should be reset to 0 when `cpu_overheated` is 0, and the `keep_driving` signal should be reset to 0 when `arrived` is 1 or `gas_tank_empty` is 1. We can achieve this by adding an else clause to each of the always blocks to handle these cases.
