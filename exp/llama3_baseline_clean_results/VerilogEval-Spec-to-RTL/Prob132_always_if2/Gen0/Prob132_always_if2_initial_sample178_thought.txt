The bug in the original module lies in the fact that the 'shut_off_computer' and 'keep_driving' signals are not being reset to their default values when their respective conditions are not met. In the case of 'shut_off_computer', it remains 1 even after the 'cpu_overheated' condition is no longer true. Similarly, 'keep_driving' remains at its last assigned value when 'arrived' is 1. To fix this, we need to ensure that these signals are reset to their default values (0 in this case, assuming that's the desired default) when their respective conditions are not met.
