When the asynchronous reset 'ar' is high, the output 'q' should be immediately reset to 0, regardless of the current state of the clock or the input 'd'. When 'ar' is low, the flip flop operates normally, capturing the value of 'd' on the rising edge of the clock 'clk' and updating the output 'q' accordingly.
