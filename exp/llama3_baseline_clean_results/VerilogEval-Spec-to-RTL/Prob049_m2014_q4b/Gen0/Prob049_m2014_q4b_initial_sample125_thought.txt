To implement a D flip-flop with an asynchronous reset, we need to consider two main aspects: 
1) the flip-flop should be triggered on the positive edge of the clock (clk) and 
2) it should be reset asynchronously whenever the reset signal (ar) is high, regardless of the clock state.
We will use a reg to store the state of the flip-flop and an always block to describe its behavior.
