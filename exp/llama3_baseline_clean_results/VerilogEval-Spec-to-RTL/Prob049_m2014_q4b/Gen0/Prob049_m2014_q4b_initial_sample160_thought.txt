To implement a D flip-flop with asynchronous reset in Verilog, we can use a simple if-else statement within an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock (clk) and the asynchronous reset (ar) signal. When ar is high, the output q should be reset to 0 regardless of the clock. When ar is low, on the rising edge of the clock, the input d should be assigned to the output q.
