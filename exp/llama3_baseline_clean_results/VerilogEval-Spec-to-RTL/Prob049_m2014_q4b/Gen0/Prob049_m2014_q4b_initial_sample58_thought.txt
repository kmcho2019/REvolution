The D flip flop should capture the data input on the positive edge of the clock and store it in the flip flop until the next positive clock edge or an asynchronous reset occurs. We will use a reg to store the output value and an always block to define the flip flop's behavior. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock and the asynchronous reset.
