The TopModule should be designed to handle synchronous load, shift enable, and shift amount selection. 
The module should first detect the load signal to decide whether to load the data into the shift register or to shift the existing data.
If the load signal is high, the data should be loaded into the shift register.
If the load signal is low and the enable signal is high, the module should shift the data according to the amount signal.
The amount signal will decide whether to shift left or right and by how many bits (1 or 8).
Arithmetic right shift should be implemented for right shifts.
