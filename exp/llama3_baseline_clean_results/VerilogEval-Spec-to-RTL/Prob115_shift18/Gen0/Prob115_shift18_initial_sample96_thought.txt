The TopModule will be implemented as a 64-bit register with synchronous load and arithmetic shift capabilities. The module will utilize the load signal to load the data into the register, the enable signal to determine whether a shift operation should occur, and the amount signal to determine the direction and amount of shift. The shifting operation will be performed based on the amount signal. For left shifts, the vacant positions will be filled with zeros. For right shifts, the vacant positions will be filled with the sign bit (most significant bit) of the register to achieve an arithmetic right shift.
