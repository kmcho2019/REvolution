To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the synchronous load and the shift operations. The module should load the data into the shift register when the load signal is high. When the enable signal is high, it should perform the shift operation based on the amount signal. The shift operation can be a left shift by 1 or 8 bits, or a right arithmetic shift by 1 or 8 bits. We will use the concatenation operator and bit manipulation to achieve the shift operations.
