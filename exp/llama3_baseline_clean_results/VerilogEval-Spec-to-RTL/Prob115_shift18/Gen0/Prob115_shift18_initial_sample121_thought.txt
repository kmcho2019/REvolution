To implement the TopModule, we will use a 64-bit register to store the current state of the shift register. We will use the load signal to load the data into the register, and the ena signal to determine whether to shift the register or not. The amount signal will be used to determine the direction and amount of the shift. We will use the Verilog shift operators to perform the shifting. For arithmetic right shift, we will use the >>> operator in combination with the sign bit to fill the vacant positions with the correct value.
