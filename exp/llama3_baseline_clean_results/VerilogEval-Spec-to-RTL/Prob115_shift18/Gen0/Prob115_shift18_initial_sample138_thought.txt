- If the load signal is high, load the data into the register.
- If the enable signal is high, perform the shift operation based on the amount signal.
- For left shift operations, shift the data to the left by the specified amount and fill the vacated positions with zeros.
- For right shift operations, perform an arithmetic right shift by shifting the data to the right and filling the vacated positions with the sign bit (most significant bit).
- Update the output q with the contents of the register.
