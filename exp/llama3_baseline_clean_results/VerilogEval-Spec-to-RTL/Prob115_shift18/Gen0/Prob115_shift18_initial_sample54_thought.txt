The TopModule will implement a 64-bit arithmetic shift register. It will first check if the load signal is high, in which case it will load the data into the shift register. If the load signal is low and the enable signal is high, it will shift the data based on the amount signal. The shift operation will be performed using the arithmetic right shift and left shift operations. 
The amount signal will determine the direction and amount of shift: 
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit.
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits.
- 2'b10: shift right by 1 bit, using arithmetic right shift.
- 2'b11: shift right by 8 bits, using arithmetic right shift.
