To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we will use a synchronous load approach, which means the load operation will be triggered by the rising edge of the clock. We will also use the 'ena' signal to determine whether to shift or not. The 'amount' signal will be used to decide the direction and amount of the shift. For arithmetic right shift, we will fill the leftmost bits with the sign bit (most significant bit) after shifting. We can use Verilog's built-in shift operators to achieve this.
