The TopModule will be designed as a 64-bit arithmetic shift register. 
It will have a synchronous load feature, which will load the data into 
the register when the load signal is high. If the load signal is low, 
the register will shift according to the amount signal. The amount 
signal will determine the direction and the number of bits to shift. 
For a left shift, the vacant positions on the right will be filled with 
zeros. For an arithmetic right shift, the vacant positions on the left 
will be filled with the sign bit of the register. The ena signal will 
determine whether the register should shift or not. If ena is low, the 
register will hold its current value.
