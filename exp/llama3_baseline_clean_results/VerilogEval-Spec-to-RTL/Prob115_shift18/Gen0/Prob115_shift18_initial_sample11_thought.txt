The TopModule will be designed as a 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load. It will have an internal 64-bit register to store the current state of the shifter. The load signal will be used to load the data into the internal register when high. The ena signal will determine whether a shift operation should be performed. The amount signal will determine the direction and amount of shift. For left shifts, the shift operator will be used to shift the bits to the left, and for right shifts, the shift operator will be used to shift the bits to the right. For arithmetic right shifts, the sign bit (the most significant bit) will be replicated to fill the vacant positions.
