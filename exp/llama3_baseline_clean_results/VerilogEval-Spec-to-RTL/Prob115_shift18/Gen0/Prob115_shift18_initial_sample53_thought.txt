To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the synchronous load and the shifting operations. 
The load operation should take priority over the shift operation, and it should occur only when the load signal is high. 
When the load signal is low and the enable signal is high, we perform the shift operation based on the amount signal. 
For left shifts, we use the left shift operator to shift the bits to the left by the specified amount. 
For right shifts, we need to preserve the sign bit (MSB) for arithmetic right shifts. 
We can use the ternary operator to decide the new MSB based on the amount signal. 
If the amount signal is 2'b10 or 2'b11, we use the current MSB for the new MSB; otherwise, we fill the vacant bit positions with zeros.
