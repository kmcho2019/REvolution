To implement the TopModule, we will use a 64-bit register to store the current value of the shifter. The module will have a synchronous load operation based on the 'load' signal. If 'load' is high, the register will be loaded with the 'data' input. If 'load' is low and 'ena' is high, the register will shift according to the 'amount' input. We will use a case statement to handle the different shift amounts and directions. For left shifts, we will use the Verilog shift operator to shift the bits to the left and fill the vacant positions on the right with zeros. For right shifts, we will also use the shift operator but fill the vacant positions on the left with the sign bit (MSB) for arithmetic right shift.
