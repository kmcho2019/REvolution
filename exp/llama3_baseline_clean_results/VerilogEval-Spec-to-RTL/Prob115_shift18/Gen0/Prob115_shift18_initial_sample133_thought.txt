The TopModule should be implemented as a 64-bit arithmetic shift register. 
It should have a synchronous load controlled by the 'load' signal, and 
shifting controlled by the 'ena' signal. The direction and amount of 
shift should be determined by the 'amount' signal, which can select 
left shift by 1 bit, left shift by 8 bits, right shift by 1 bit (arithmetic), 
or right shift by 8 bits (arithmetic). The register should be updated 
synchronously on the rising edge of the 'clk' signal.
