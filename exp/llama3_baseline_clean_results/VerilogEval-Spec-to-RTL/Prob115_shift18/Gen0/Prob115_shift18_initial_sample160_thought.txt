The TopModule will be designed with a synchronous load feature, allowing it to either shift its current state or load new data from the input port 'data' based on the 'load' signal. The shifting operation is controlled by 'ena', and the direction and amount of shift are determined by the 'amount' input. For left shifts, new bits will be filled with zeros. For right shifts, the new bits will be filled with the sign bit (most significant bit) of the register, implementing an arithmetic right shift. This will be achieved using a combination of if-else statements and bitwise operations in the Verilog code.
