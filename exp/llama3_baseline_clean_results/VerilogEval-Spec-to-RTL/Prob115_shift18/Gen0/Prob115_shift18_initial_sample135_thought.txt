To implement the TopModule, we need to first determine whether to load the data or shift the existing data based on the load and ena signals. If load is high, we load the data into the shift register. If load is low and ena is high, we shift the data according to the amount signal. We will use a case statement to determine the amount of shift and direction. We will use the arithmetic shift operator (>>> for right shift and <<< for left shift) to shift the data.
