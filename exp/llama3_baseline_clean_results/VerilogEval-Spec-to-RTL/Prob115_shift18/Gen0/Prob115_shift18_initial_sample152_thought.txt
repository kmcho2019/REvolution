To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the behavior of the shift register based on the inputs. We have a 64-bit register that can be loaded synchronously with the data input when the load signal is high. When the enable signal is high, the register can be shifted left or right by either 1 or 8 bits, depending on the amount input. For left shifts, we fill the new bit positions with zeros, and for right shifts, we fill the new bit positions with the sign bit (most significant bit) to maintain the arithmetic shift behavior.

We will use a reg declaration to declare the internal shift register and then use always blocks to describe the synchronous behavior. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock (posedge clk) to capture the synchronous load and shift operations. Inside the always block, we will use if-else statements to handle the different conditions based on the load and ena signals, and then further conditional statements to determine the direction and amount of shift based on the amount input.
