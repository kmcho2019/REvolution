To implement the TopModule, we can use a register to hold the current state of the shifter. We will use the clk signal as the clock for our synchronous module. The load signal will be used to load the data into the register when it is high. When ena is high, the module will shift the contents of the register based on the amount signal. We will use a case statement to determine the direction and amount of shift. For arithmetic right shift, we will need to sign extend the most significant bit.
