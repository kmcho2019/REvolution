To implement the TopModule, I will use a 64-bit register to hold the current state of the shift register. The module will have a synchronous load operation based on the 'load' signal, and a conditional shift operation based on the 'ena' signal. The 'amount' signal will determine the direction and amount of shift. For left shift operations, I will use simple bit shifting to fill the vacant positions with zeros. For right shift operations, I will use arithmetic right shift to fill the vacant positions with the sign bit (most significant bit).
