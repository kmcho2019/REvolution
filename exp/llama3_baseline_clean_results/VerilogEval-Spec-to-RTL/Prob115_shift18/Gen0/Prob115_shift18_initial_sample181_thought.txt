To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the following:
1. We need to load the shift register with data when the load signal is high.
2. When the ena signal is high, we shift the register based on the amount signal.
3. The amount signal determines the direction and amount of shift (left by 1 bit, left by 8 bits, right by 1 bit, or right by 8 bits).
4. The right shift should be an arithmetic right shift, meaning the most significant bit is filled with the sign bit of the shifted value.
We can achieve this by using a reg to store the shift register's value and a case statement to determine the shift amount and direction.
