To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we need to consider the load, enable, and amount signals to determine the operation to perform on the register. We can use a reg to store the current state of the shift register, and update it on each clock cycle based on the input signals. For the shift operations, we can use Verilog's built-in shift operators (<< and >>) for left and right shifts, respectively. For arithmetic right shift, we need to sign-extend the most significant bit to maintain the sign of the number.
