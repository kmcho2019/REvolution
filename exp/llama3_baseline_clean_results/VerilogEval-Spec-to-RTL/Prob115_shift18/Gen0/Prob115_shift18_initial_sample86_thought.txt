To implement the TopModule, we first need to handle the synchronous load. If the load signal is high, we load the data into the shift register. If the enable signal is high, we then check the amount signal to determine the direction and amount of the shift. For left shifts, we simply use the shift operator to shift the bits left and fill the vacant positions with zeros. For right shifts, we use the arithmetic right shift operator to shift the bits right, filling the vacant positions with the sign bit (most significant bit) to maintain the sign of the number.
