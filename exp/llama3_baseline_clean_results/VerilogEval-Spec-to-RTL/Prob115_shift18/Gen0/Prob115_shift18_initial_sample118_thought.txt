To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we need to consider the load, enable, and amount signals to control the operation. The module will have a 64-bit register to store the current value. When the load signal is high, the register will be loaded with the data input. When the enable signal is high, the register will shift according to the amount signal. We will use case statements to determine the shift direction and amount. For left shifts, we will use the left shift operator. For right shifts, we will use arithmetic right shift operators to maintain the sign bit.
