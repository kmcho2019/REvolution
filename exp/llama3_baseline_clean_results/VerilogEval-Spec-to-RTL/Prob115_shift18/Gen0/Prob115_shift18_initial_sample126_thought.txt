To implement the TopModule, we will first check if the load signal is high. If it is, we will load the data into the shift register. Otherwise, we will check if the enable signal is high. If it is, we will shift the data based on the amount signal. If the amount is 2'b00 or 2'b01, we will shift left by 1 or 8 bits respectively. If the amount is 2'b10 or 2'b11, we will perform an arithmetic right shift by 1 or 8 bits respectively.
