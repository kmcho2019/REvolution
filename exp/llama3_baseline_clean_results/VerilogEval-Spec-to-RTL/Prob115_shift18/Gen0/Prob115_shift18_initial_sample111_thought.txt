To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load, we will use a sequential logic design approach. The module TopModule will have a clocked always block to handle the synchronous load and shifting operations. The shifting operation will be determined by the amount signal, and the direction of the shift will be handled using conditional statements. The ena signal will control whether the shift operation occurs or not. If ena is low, the register will hold its current value. The load signal will take priority over the shift operation, loading the data into the register when high.
