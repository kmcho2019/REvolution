The TopModule can be designed using a combination of conditional statements and shift operators to achieve the desired functionality. 
We will use a reg to store the shift register's value and update it on every clock cycle based on the inputs.
When load is high, the shift register should be loaded with the data.
When ena is high, the shift register should be shifted according to the amount input.
We will use the arithmetic right shift operator (>>> in Verilog) for right shifts to fill the leftmost bits with the sign bit.
For left shifts, we will use the left shift operator (<< in Verilog).
