The module TopModule will use a synchronous load to update the 64-bit shift register with the input data when the load signal is high. When the enable signal (ena) is high and the load signal is low, the shift register will perform a shift operation based on the amount input. The shift amount and direction will be determined by the value of the amount input. For a left shift, the new bits will be filled with zeros. For an arithmetic right shift, the new bits will be filled with the most significant bit of the original value.
