The TopModule will be implemented as a 64-bit register with synchronous load, 
controlled by the load signal. The shift operations will be performed based on 
the amount signal, which determines the direction and number of bits to shift. 
To perform arithmetic right shifts, the sign bit (most significant bit) of the 
register will be preserved during right shifts. 
The module will be implemented using Verilog's always block for sequential 
logic and the case statement to handle different shift operations.
