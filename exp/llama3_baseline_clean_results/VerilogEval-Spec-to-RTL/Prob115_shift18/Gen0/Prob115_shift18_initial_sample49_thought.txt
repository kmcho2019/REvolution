To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we need to consider the load and enable signals. When load is high, the register should be loaded with the input data. When enable is high, the register should be shifted according to the amount signal. We will use a case statement to determine the shift amount and direction based on the amount signal. For left shifts, we will use the built-in shift operators in Verilog. For right shifts, we will use arithmetic right shift operators, which will sign-extend the most significant bit.
