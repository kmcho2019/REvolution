To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we will use a synchronous load approach where the shift register is loaded with the input data when the load signal is high. When the enable signal is high, the shift register will perform the specified shift operation based on the amount signal. The shift operation will be performed on the current contents of the shift register.

We will use a case statement to determine the shift amount and direction based on the amount signal. For left shifts, we will use the left shift operator to shift the bits to the left and fill the vacant positions with zeros. For right shifts, we will use the right shift operator with sign extension for arithmetic right shift, which means the most significant bit will be replicated to fill the vacant positions.

We will also use a reg declaration to declare the shift register q and initialize it to zero. The shift register will be updated on every positive clock edge when the enable signal is high.
