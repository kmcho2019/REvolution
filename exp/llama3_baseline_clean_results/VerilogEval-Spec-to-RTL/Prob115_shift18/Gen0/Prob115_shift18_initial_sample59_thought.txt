The TopModule should be implemented as a 64-bit register with a synchronous load and shift capabilities. 
The load signal takes priority, allowing the data to be loaded directly into the register when it is high. 
When the load signal is low and the enable signal is high, the register should shift its contents based on the amount signal. 
The amount signal determines the direction and the amount of shift (1 bit left, 8 bits left, 1 bit right, or 8 bits right). 
For left shifts, the shifted-in bits should be zeros. 
For right shifts, the shifted-in bits should be the sign bit of the register for an arithmetic right shift.
