To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the synchronous load and shifting operations. 
When the 'load' signal is high, the module should load the 'data' into the shift register 'q'. 
When the 'ena' signal is high, the module should shift the 'q' based on the 'amount' signal. 
The shifting operation can be left or right, and by 1 or 8 bit positions. 
For right shift, it's an arithmetic right shift, which means the most significant bit (MSB) will be duplicated to fill the vacant bits.
