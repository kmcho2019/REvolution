To implement the TopModule, I will use a synchronous load to load the data into the shift register when the load signal is high. When the enable signal (ena) is high, the module will shift the data based on the amount signal. The amount signal determines the direction and amount of shift: 
- 2'b00: shift left by 1 bit
- 2'b01: shift left by 8 bits
- 2'b10: shift right by 1 bit
- 2'b11: shift right by 8 bits
I will use Verilog's built-in shift operators to implement the shifting.
