The TopModule will be designed as a 64-bit arithmetic shift register. It will have synchronous load capability, meaning it loads data from the input port when the load signal is high. When the load signal is low, it will perform shifting operations based on the enable signal (ena) and the amount signal. The amount signal will determine the direction (left or right) and the amount (1 or 8 bits) of shifting. For right shifts, the register will perform arithmetic right shifts, preserving the sign bit.
