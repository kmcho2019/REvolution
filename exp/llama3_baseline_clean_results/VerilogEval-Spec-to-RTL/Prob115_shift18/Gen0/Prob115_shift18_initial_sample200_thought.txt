The load signal will be used to load the new data into the register, and the ena signal will determine whether a shift operation should be performed. The amount signal will control the direction and amount of the shift. For left shifts, we can use bitwise shift operators, and for right shifts, we can use arithmetic shift operators to preserve the sign bit.
