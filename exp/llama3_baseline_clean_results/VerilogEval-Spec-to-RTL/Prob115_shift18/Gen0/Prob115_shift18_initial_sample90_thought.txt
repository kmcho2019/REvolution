If 'load' is high, we load the register with the 'data' input. If 'ena' is high and 'load' is low, we shift the register according to the 'amount' input. We will use case statements to handle the different shift amounts and directions. For left shifts, we will use the left shift operator. For right shifts, we will use the right shift operator with sign extension for arithmetic right shift.
