To implement the TopModule, we can use a 64-bit register to store the shift register's content. We will use the load signal to decide whether to load the data into the register or to shift the register. If the ena signal is high, we will shift the register according to the amount signal. For left shifts, we will use the concatenation operator to fill the empty bits on the right with zeros. For right shifts, we will use the concatenation operator and sign extension to fill the empty bits on the left with the sign bit. The shifting will be done based on the value of the amount signal.
