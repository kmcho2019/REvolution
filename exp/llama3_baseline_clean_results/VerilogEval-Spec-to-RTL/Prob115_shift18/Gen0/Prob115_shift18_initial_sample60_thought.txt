To implement the TopModule, we will use a synchronous design approach where the load, enable, and shifting operations are all controlled by the clock signal (clk). The load operation will be performed when the 'load' signal is high, and the enable signal (ena) is not required for this operation. When 'ena' is high and 'load' is low, the module will perform the shift operation based on the 'amount' signal. The shifting operation can be achieved using Verilog's built-in shift operators. For arithmetic right shift, we need to preserve the sign bit (the most significant bit) when shifting right.
