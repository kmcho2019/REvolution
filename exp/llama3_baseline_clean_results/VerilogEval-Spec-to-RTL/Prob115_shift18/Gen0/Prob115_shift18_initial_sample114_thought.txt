The TopModule will be designed as a synchronous 64-bit arithmetic shift register. It will have a load signal to load the register with new data, an enable signal to control shifting, and an amount signal to determine the direction and amount of the shift. The shifting will be performed based on the amount signal, which will select between left shifts by 1 or 8 bits and right shifts by 1 or 8 bits. For right shifts, an arithmetic right shift will be used, which means the most significant bits will be filled with the sign bit (bit 63) of the register. The shifting operation will only occur when the enable signal is high.
