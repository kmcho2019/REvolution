To implement the TopModule, we will first create a 64-bit register to hold the shift register's value. We will use a synchronous load, where the load signal is high, to load the data into the register. When the load signal is low and the enable signal is high, we will shift the register's value based on the amount signal. For shifting left by 1 or 8 bits, we will use the corresponding shift operators. For shifting right, we will use arithmetic right shift operators to preserve the sign bit.
