The TopModule will be designed as a 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load. The module will utilize an if-else statement to check the load signal. If the load signal is high, it will load the data into the shift register. If the load signal is low, it will then check the enable signal. If the enable signal is high, it will shift the data based on the amount signal. The amount signal will determine the direction and amount of shift. For left shifts, it will simply shift the bits to the left and fill the rightmost bits with zeros. For right shifts, it will perform an arithmetic right shift by shifting the bits to the right and filling the leftmost bits with the sign bit (the most significant bit).
