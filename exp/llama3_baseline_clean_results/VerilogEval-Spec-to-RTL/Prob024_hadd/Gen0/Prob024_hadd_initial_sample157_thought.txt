To implement a half adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out from two input bits a and b. The sum (sum) is the XOR of a and b, and the carry-out (cout) is the AND of a and b. This is based on the basic principles of binary arithmetic where the sum of two bits is 1 if one and only one of the bits is 1, and the carry-out is 1 if both bits are 1.
