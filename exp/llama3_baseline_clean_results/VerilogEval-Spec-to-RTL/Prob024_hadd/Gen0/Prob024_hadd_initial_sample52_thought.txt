To implement a half adder in Verilog, we need to consider the basic logic of a half adder, which involves summing two input bits and producing a sum and a carry-out. The sum is calculated by performing an XOR operation on the two input bits, and the carry-out is generated by performing an AND operation on the two input bits. This is based on the principle that the sum of two bits will have a carry-out only if both bits are 1.
