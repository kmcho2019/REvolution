To implement a half adder in Verilog, we can use the basic logic gates that define its behavior. The sum output of a half adder is the XOR of the two input bits (a and b), and the carry output is the AND of the two input bits. This is because the sum will be 1 only if one of the inputs is 1 (exclusive OR), and the carry will be 1 only if both inputs are 1 (AND operation).
