To implement a half adder, we need to follow the basic logic rules for addition. 
The sum (S) is calculated as the XOR of the two inputs (a and b), 
and the carry-out (Cout) is calculated as the AND of the two inputs. 
This can be represented as follows:
- sum = a ^ b (XOR operation)
- cout = a & b (AND operation)
We can directly implement these operations in Verilog using the corresponding operators.
