The sum bit will be the XOR of the two input bits, and the carry-out bit will be the AND of the two input bits. This is because in binary addition, when two bits are added, the sum bit is 1 only if one of the bits is 1 (which is the definition of XOR), and the carry-out bit is 1 only if both bits are 1 (which is the definition of AND).
