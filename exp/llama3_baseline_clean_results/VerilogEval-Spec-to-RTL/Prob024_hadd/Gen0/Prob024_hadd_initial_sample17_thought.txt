To implement a half adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out based on the two input bits. The sum bit is calculated by performing an XOR operation between the two input bits, and the carry-out bit is calculated by performing an AND operation between the two input bits. This is based on the basic principles of digital arithmetic, where the sum of two bits is 1 if one of the bits is 1 and the other is 0, and the carry-out is 1 if both bits are 1.
