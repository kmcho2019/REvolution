To implement a half adder, we need to create a module that takes two 1-bit inputs (a and b) and produces two outputs: sum and carry-out (cout). The sum output is calculated by performing a bitwise XOR operation between the two inputs, and the carry-out is calculated by performing a bitwise AND operation between the two inputs. This is based on the logic that in binary addition, a carry is generated only when both bits being added are 1.
