To implement a half adder, we need to produce two outputs: sum and carry-out. 
The sum output is the XOR of the two input bits (a and b), because in binary addition, 
1+1 equals 10 (where the 0 is the sum and the 1 is the carry-out), 1+0 equals 1, 
0+1 equals 1, and 0+0 equals 0. 
The carry-out output is the AND of the two input bits, because in binary addition, 
a carry-out is only produced when both input bits are 1.
We will use these logical operations to derive the sum and carry-out in the Verilog module.
