To implement a half adder in Verilog, we need to define the logic for calculating the sum and carry-out based on the inputs a and b. The sum (sum) can be calculated using an XOR operation (a ^ b), and the carry-out (cout) can be calculated using an AND operation (a & b). This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 is produced when the inputs are different (XOR), and a carry-out of 1 is produced when both inputs are 1 (AND).
