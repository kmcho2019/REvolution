To implement a half adder, we need to perform bitwise operations on the inputs 'a' and 'b'. The sum (sum) can be calculated using the XOR operation, as it will produce 1 only when 'a' and 'b' are different. The carry-out (cout) can be calculated using the AND operation, as it will produce 1 only when both 'a' and 'b' are 1.
