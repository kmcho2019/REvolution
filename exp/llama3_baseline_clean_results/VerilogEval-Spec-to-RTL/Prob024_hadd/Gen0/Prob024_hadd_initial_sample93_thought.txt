To implement a half adder, we need to produce a sum (sum) and a carry-out (cout) from two input bits (a and b). The sum is the XOR of the two input bits, and the carry-out is the AND of the two input bits. This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 is produced when one and only one of the input bits is 1 (XOR operation), and a carry-out of 1 is produced when both input bits are 1 (AND operation).
