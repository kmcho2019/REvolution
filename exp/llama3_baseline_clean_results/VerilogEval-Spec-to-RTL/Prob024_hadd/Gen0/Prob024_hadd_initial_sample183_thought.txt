To implement a half adder in Verilog, we need to follow the basic logic of a half adder. The sum (S) is calculated as the XOR of the two input bits (a and b), and the carry-out (Cout) is calculated as the AND of the two input bits. This is based on the truth table of a half adder where the sum is 1 if the inputs are different (XOR operation) and the carry is 1 if both inputs are 1 (AND operation).
