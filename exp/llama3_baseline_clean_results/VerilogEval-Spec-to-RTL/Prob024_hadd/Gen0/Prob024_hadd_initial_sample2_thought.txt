To implement a half adder in Verilog, we will use the basic digital logic principles. A half adder is a digital circuit that adds two bits (a and b) and produces a sum and a carry-out (cout). The sum is calculated as the XOR of a and b, and the carry-out is calculated as the AND of a and b. We will define a module named TopModule with the specified interface and use these logical operations to calculate the sum and cout.
