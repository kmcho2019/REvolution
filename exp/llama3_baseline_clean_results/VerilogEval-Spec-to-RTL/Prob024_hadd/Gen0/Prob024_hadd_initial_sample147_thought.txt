To implement a half adder, we need to follow the logic of the addition of two bits. The sum (S) is calculated as the XOR of the two input bits (a and b), and the carry-out (Cout) is calculated as the AND of the two input bits. This is because in binary addition, the sum is 1 only when one of the bits is 1 (exclusive or), and the carry is generated only when both bits are 1 (and operation).
