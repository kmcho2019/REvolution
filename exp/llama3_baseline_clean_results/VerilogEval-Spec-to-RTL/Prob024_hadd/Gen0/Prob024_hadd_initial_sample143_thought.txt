To implement a half adder, we need to design a digital circuit that can add two one-bit numbers. The half adder will have two inputs (a and b) and two outputs (sum and carry-out). The sum output will be the result of the XOR operation between a and b, because in binary addition, the sum of two bits is 1 if the bits are different. The carry-out output will be the result of the AND operation between a and b, because the carry-out is 1 only when both input bits are 1.
