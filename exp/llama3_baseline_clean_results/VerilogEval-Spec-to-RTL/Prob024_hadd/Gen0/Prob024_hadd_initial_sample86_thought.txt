To implement a half adder, we need to generate two outputs: sum and carry-out. The sum output is calculated by performing an XOR operation between the two input bits (a and b), while the carry-out output is calculated by performing an AND operation between the two input bits. This is based on the binary addition rules where a sum of 1 is produced when the two bits are different (XOR operation) and a carry of 1 is produced when both bits are 1 (AND operation).
