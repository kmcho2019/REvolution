To implement a half adder, we need to produce the sum and carry-out based on the input bits a and b. The sum can be calculated using an XOR operation (a XOR b), and the carry-out can be calculated using an AND operation (a AND b). This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 is produced when the inputs are different (which XOR captures), and a carry-out of 1 is produced when both inputs are 1 (which AND captures).
