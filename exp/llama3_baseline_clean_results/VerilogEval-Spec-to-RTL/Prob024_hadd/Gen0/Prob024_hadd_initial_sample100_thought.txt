To implement a half adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out based on the inputs a and b. The sum can be calculated using the XOR operation (a ^ b), and the carry-out can be calculated using the AND operation (a & b). This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 occurs when the inputs are different (a XOR b), and a carry-out occurs when both inputs are 1 (a AND b).
