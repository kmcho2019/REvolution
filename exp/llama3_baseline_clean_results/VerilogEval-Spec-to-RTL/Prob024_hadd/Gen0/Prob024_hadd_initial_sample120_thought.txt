To implement a half adder, we need to design a module that takes two input bits, 'a' and 'b', and produces a sum and a carry-out. The sum bit is calculated by performing an XOR operation between 'a' and 'b', and the carry-out bit is calculated by performing an AND operation between 'a' and 'b'. This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 occurs when the two input bits are different (XOR operation), and a carry-out of 1 occurs when the two input bits are both 1 (AND operation).
