To implement a half adder, we need to create a module that takes two one-bit inputs (a and b), adds them together, and produces a sum and a carry-out. The sum bit is calculated by performing an XOR operation between a and b, while the carry-out bit is calculated by performing an AND operation between a and b. This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 is produced when either a or b (but not both) is 1, which is the property of XOR. A carry-out of 1 is produced when both a and b are 1, which is the property of AND.
