To implement a half adder, we need to produce two outputs: sum and carry-out. The sum is simply the XOR of the two input bits (a and b), because in binary addition, 1+1=10 (where the 1 in the sum place is the XOR result and the 1 in the carry place is the AND result). The carry-out is the AND of the two input bits, because only when both bits are 1 will there be a carry.
