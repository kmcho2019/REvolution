To implement a half adder in Verilog, we can use basic logical operators to calculate the sum and carry-out. The sum (S) is typically calculated as the XOR of inputs a and b, because in binary addition, when you add two bits, you get a sum of 1 only if one of the bits is 1 (exclusive or). The carry-out (Cout) is calculated as the AND of inputs a and b, because a carry occurs only when both bits are 1.
