To implement a half adder, we need to design a digital circuit that takes two one-bit inputs (a and b) and produces two outputs: sum and carry-out (cout). The sum bit can be calculated using an XOR operation between a and b, as it will be 1 only when either a or b (but not both) is 1. The carry-out bit can be generated using an AND operation between a and b, as it will be 1 only when both a and b are 1.
