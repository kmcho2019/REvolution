To implement a half adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out based on the inputs a and b. The sum (sum) is the XOR of a and b, and the carry-out (cout) is the AND of a and b. This is because in binary addition, a sum of 1 occurs when one and only one of the inputs is 1 (which is the definition of XOR), and a carry-out occurs when both inputs are 1 (which is the definition of AND).
