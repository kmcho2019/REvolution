To implement a half adder in Verilog, we need to define the logic for the sum and carry-out based on the input bits a and b. The sum (sum) is the XOR of a and b, and the carry-out (cout) is the AND of a and b. This is because in binary addition, a carry is generated only when both bits are 1, and the sum is the remainder of the division of the sum of the bits by 2.
