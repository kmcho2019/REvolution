To implement a half adder, we need to create a digital circuit that takes two input bits, a and b, and produces their sum and carry-out. The sum bit can be obtained by performing an XOR operation between a and b, as the XOR operation gives 1 only if one of the inputs is 1 (which corresponds to the sum of two bits without considering the carry). The carry-out bit can be obtained by performing an AND operation between a and b, as the AND operation gives 1 only if both inputs are 1 (which means there is a carry-out when adding two bits).
