To implement a half adder, we need to calculate the sum and carry-out based on the inputs a and b. The sum is calculated using the XOR operation, and the carry-out is calculated using the AND operation. The XOR operation will give us the sum of the two bits (1 if the bits are different, 0 if they are the same), and the AND operation will give us the carry-out (1 if both bits are 1, 0 otherwise).
