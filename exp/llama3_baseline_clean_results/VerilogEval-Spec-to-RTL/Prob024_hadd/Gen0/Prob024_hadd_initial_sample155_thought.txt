To implement a half adder, we need to perform two basic operations: 
1. Calculate the sum of the two input bits 'a' and 'b' (which is essentially the XOR operation).
2. Calculate the carry-out, which occurs when both 'a' and 'b' are 1 (which is essentially the AND operation).
We will use these operations to define the 'sum' and 'cout' outputs in our Verilog module TopModule.
