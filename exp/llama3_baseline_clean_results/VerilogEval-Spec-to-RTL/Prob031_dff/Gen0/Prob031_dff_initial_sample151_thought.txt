To implement a single D flip-flop in Verilog, we can use a reg variable to store the state of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of the clock signal. The D flip-flop has a simple behavior: on the positive edge of the clock, the output of the flip-flop (q) is updated to the value of the input (d) at that time. We will use a non-blocking assignment to ensure that the update of q is synchronized with the clock edge.
