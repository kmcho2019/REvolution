To implement a D flip-flop, we need to capture the input 'd' when the clock 'clk' rises (on the positive edge) and then hold that value until the next rising clock edge. We'll use a reg to store the value and assign it to the output 'q' when the clock rises. We'll also use the '@(posedge clk)' construct to specify that the assignment should happen on the positive edge of the clock.
