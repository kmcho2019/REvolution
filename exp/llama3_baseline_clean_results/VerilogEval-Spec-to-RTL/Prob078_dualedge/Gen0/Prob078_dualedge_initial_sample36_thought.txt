To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to use one flip-flop that is triggered at the positive edge of the clock and another flip-flop that is triggered at the negative edge of the clock. Then, we use an AND gate and an OR gate to determine whether the input should be passed through to the output based on the state of the two flip-flops. This way, the output will change on both edges of the clock.
