To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. The output will be the result of an OR operation between the two flip-flops. However, this approach requires a mechanism to ensure that both flip-flops do not try to capture the input at the same time, which would cause a race condition. We can use a selector signal, generated by an internal clock phase signal, to determine which flip-flop captures the input data. The phase signal will be toggled at each clock edge, selecting one flip-flop for the rising edge and the other for the falling edge.
