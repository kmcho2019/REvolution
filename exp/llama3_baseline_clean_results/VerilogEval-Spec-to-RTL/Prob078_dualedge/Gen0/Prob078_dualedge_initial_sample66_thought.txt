To create a module that functionally behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can utilize two D flip-flops and a multiplexer. The idea is to capture the data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock. We can use one flip-flop to capture the data on the rising edge and another to capture the data on the falling edge. Then, we use a multiplexer to select the output from the appropriate flip-flop based on the current clock edge.
