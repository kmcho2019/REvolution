To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined using an OR gate. The idea is to use the rising edge flip-flop to capture the data at the rising edge of the clock and the falling edge flip-flop to capture the data at the falling edge. Since we can't directly use both edges in an always block sensitivity list due to synthesis limitations, we can achieve similar behavior by detecting the edges and using those to trigger updates.
