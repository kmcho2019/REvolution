To design a circuit that behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two D flip-flops and an inverter. The idea is to create two separate flip-flops that are triggered on the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively. Then, we can use a multiplexer to select the output from one of these flip-flops based on the current clock edge. This way, when the clock is high, the first path captures the data, and when the clock is low, the second path captures the inverted data, effectively capturing the data on both edges of the clock.
