To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop can be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other can be triggered on the negative edge. The input data 'd' is first sampled by the flip-flop triggered on the positive edge, then sampled again by the flip-flop triggered on the negative edge. The outputs of the two flip-flops are combined using an OR gate. This way, whenever the clock transitions, either from low to high or high to low, the data is captured and passed through to the output 'q'.
