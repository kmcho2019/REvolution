To create a circuit that behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other on the negative edge. The data input for both flip-flops is the same. The output of the flip-flops can be combined using an OR gate to produce the final output q. This way, whenever the clock has either a positive or negative edge, the data is captured and the output is updated.
