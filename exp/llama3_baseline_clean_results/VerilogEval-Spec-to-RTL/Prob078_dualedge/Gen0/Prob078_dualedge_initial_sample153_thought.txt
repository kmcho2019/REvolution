To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other triggered on the falling edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined using a multiplexer to generate the final output. We can use the clock itself to select the output of the flip-flops. When the clock is high, the output of the rising-edge triggered flip-flop is selected, and when the clock is low, the output of the falling-edge triggered flip-flop is selected.
