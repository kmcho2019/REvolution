To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to sample the input data at both the rising and falling edges of the clock and then use a multiplexer to select the correct output based on the current state of the clock. We will use one flip-flop to sample the data at the rising edge and another flip-flop to sample the data at the falling edge.
