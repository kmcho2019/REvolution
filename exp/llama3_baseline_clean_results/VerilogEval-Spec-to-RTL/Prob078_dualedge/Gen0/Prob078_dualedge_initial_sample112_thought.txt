To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a clock edge detector to determine whether the current clock cycle is rising or falling. The first flip-flop is triggered by the rising edge of the clock, and the second flip-flop is triggered by the falling edge of the clock. The output of the flip-flops is then combined using a multiplexer, where the selection signal is based on the current clock edge.
