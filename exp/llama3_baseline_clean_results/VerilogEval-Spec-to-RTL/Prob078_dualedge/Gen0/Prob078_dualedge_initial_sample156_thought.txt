To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a multiplexer. The first flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock and captures the input data at this edge. The second flip-flop is triggered on the negative edge of the clock and captures the input data at this edge. The multiplexer then selects the output of one of the flip-flops based on the current clock edge, effectively creating a dual-edge triggered behavior.
