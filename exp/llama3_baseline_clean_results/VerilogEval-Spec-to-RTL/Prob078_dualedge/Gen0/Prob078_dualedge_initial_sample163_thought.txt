To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a multiplexer. One flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other is triggered on the negative edge. The output of the flip-flops is selected based on the current clock edge. When the clock is rising, the output of the positive edge flip-flop is selected, and when the clock is falling, the output of the negative edge flip-flop is selected.
