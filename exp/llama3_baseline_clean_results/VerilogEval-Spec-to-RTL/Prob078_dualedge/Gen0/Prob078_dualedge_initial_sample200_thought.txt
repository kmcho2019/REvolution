To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other on the negative edge. We will use the output of the positive-edge triggered flip-flop as the input to the negative-edge triggered flip-flop. The output of the negative-edge triggered flip-flop will be the final output of our circuit. This way, the circuit will capture the input data on both the positive and negative edges of the clock.
