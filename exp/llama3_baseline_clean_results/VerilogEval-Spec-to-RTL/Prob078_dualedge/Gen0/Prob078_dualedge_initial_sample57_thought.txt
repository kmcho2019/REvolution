To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The first flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock and captures the input data. The second flip-flop is triggered on the falling edge of the clock and also captures the input data, but it is sampled from the output of the first flip-flop. This way, when the clock transitions from high to low, the second flip-flop captures the data that was present at the rising edge of the clock, effectively creating a dual-edge triggered behavior.
