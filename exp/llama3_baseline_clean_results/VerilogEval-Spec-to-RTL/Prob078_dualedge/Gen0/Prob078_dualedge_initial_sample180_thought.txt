To design a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog when the direct approach of using both posedge and negedge in the sensitivity list is not allowed, we can utilize two D-type flip-flops with one triggered on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of the clock. The output of these flip-flops can be combined using a multiplexer to produce the desired output. The select line of the multiplexer can be driven by the clock itself, ensuring that on the rising edge, the output of the rising edge flip-flop is selected, and on the falling edge, the output of the falling edge flip-flop is selected. However, a simpler approach involves recognizing that the desired behavior can be achieved by using a single D-type flip-flop that is clocked on one edge of the input clock and the input data is sampled on the opposite edge of the clock. This can be achieved by using a phase-shifted version of the clock or the input data, effectively allowing the flip-flop to capture data on both edges of the original clock signal.
