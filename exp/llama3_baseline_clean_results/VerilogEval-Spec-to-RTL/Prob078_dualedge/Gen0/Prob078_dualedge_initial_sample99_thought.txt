To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a clock enable signal to mimic the behavior. The first flip-flop will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the second on the falling edge. The output of the two flip-flops will then be multiplexed to produce the final output. This way, we can achieve a functional equivalent of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
