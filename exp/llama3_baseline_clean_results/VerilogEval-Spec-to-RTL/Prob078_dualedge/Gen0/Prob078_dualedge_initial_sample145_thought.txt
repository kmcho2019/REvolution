To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can implement a circuit that uses two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. We will use an additional logic to determine which flip-flop's output should be considered as the final output. This can be achieved by using a multiplexer that selects the output of the flip-flop that was triggered most recently.
