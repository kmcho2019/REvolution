To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, which doesn't support such a flip-flop directly, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops and some logic to capture the data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock. The idea involves using one flip-flop to capture data on the rising edge of the clock and another to capture data on the falling edge. Then, use the output of these flip-flops to generate the final output, ensuring it updates on both edges of the clock. This can be achieved by using the outputs of both flip-flops and a clock edge detector to decide when to update the output.
