To design a circuit that functionally behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other will be triggered on the negative edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops can then be combined using an OR gate. This way, whenever the clock transitions (either rising or falling), the output will be updated with the input data.
