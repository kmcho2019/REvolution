To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other is triggered on the negative edge. We can then use an OR gate to combine the outputs of the two flip-flops. However, this approach requires a method to prevent the two flip-flops from competing with each other, causing the output to become unstable. We can achieve this by using a phase-shifted clock for one of the flip-flops, ensuring that only one flip-flop is active at any given edge of the original clock.
