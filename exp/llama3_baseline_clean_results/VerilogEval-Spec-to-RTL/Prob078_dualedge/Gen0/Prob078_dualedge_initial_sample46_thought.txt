To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops with their clocks being the original clock and its inverted version. This way, when the original clock rises, the first flip-flop captures the data, and when the original clock falls, the second flip-flop captures the data. The output of the circuit will be the output of the second flip-flop when the clock is low and the output of the first flip-flop when the clock is high. We need to ensure that the data is captured correctly on both edges by using an appropriate timing control.
