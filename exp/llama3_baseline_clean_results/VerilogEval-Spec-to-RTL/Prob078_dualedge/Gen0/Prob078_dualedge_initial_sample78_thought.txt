To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a multiplexer. The first flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the second flip-flop is triggered on the negative edge of the clock. The multiplexer selects the output from either the positive edge flip-flop or the negative edge flip-flop based on the current clock edge. This way, the output of the circuit will be updated on both edges of the clock, effectively behaving like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
