The second flip-flop is triggered on the negative edge of the clock and captures the output of the first flip-flop. We can then use the output of both flip-flops and a multiplexer to generate the final output, which will be the output of the first flip-flop on the positive edge and the output of the second flip-flop on the negative edge.
