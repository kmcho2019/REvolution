To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, which does not support such a component directly, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to use one flip-flop triggered on the positive edge of the clock and another on the negative edge. However, to avoid race conditions and ensure that the output reflects the correct value based on the input at the appropriate clock edge, we need to manage the inputs to these flip-flops carefully. We can achieve this by using the first flip-flop to capture the data at the positive edge and the second at the negative edge, then combine their outputs to produce the final output. This way, the circuit will behave as if it were triggered by both edges of the clock.
