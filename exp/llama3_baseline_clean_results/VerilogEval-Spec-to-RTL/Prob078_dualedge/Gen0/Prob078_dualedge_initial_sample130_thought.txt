To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. The data input (d) and its inverted version are passed through these flip-flops. The outputs of these flip-flops are then passed through an XOR gate to produce the output (q). When the clock transitions, either the rising-edge flip-flop or the falling-edge flip-flop captures the data or its inverse, causing the output of the XOR gate to change.
