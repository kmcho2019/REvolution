To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The first flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the second one on the falling edge. The output of these flip-flops can then be combined using an OR gate to produce the desired output. This way, when either the rising or falling edge of the clock occurs, the output will be updated. We will use an additional signal to keep track of the previous clock edge to ensure the correct operation.
