The idea is to sample the input 'd' on both the rising and falling edges of 'clk' and then use this information to generate an output 'q' that reflects the value of 'd' at the last edge. This can be achieved by having one flip-flop triggered on the rising edge of 'clk' and the other on the falling edge. The output 'q' can then be determined based on the outputs of these two flip-flops.
