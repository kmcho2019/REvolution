To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two regular D flip-flops with their clocks connected to the positive and negative edges of the input clock, respectively. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined using an OR gate to produce the final output. This approach allows us to simulate the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, which would normally change its output on both rising and falling edges of the clock.
