To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, which doesn't natively support such a component, we can create a circuit that captures the data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock. This can be achieved by using two regular flip-flops and some combinational logic to control their inputs. One flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and the other on the falling edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined such that the output of the circuit changes as soon as the input data changes, regardless of whether it happens on a rising or a falling edge of the clock.
