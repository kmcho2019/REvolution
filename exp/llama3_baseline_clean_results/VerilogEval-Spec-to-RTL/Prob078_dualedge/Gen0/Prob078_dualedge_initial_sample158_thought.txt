To create a circuit that functionally behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop will capture the input data on the rising edge of the clock, and the other will capture the input data on the falling edge of the clock. A multiplexer can then be used to select the output from either of the two flip-flops based on the current clock edge. Alternatively, a simpler approach can be implemented using a single D flip-flop and a latch. The D flip-flop captures the data on the rising edge, and the latch captures the data on the falling edge. The output can then be directly taken from the latch, as its output will be updated on both edges of the clock due to the nature of a latch.
