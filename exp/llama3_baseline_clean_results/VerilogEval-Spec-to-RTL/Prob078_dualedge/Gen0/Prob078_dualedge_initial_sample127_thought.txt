To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two D-type flip-flops in a master-slave configuration. The first flip-flop (master) will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and its output will be captured by the second flip-flop (slave) on the falling edge of the clock. Conversely, the master flip-flop will also be triggered on the falling edge of the clock if the clock is inverted. We can achieve this by using two clocks: the original clock for the rising edge and the inverted clock for the falling edge.
