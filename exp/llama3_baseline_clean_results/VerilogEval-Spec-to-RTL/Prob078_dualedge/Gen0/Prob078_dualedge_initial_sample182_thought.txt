To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to use one flip-flop triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. The output of the two flip-flops will be combined using a multiplexer, selecting the output of the rising-edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is high and the output of the falling-edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is low.
