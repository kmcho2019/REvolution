To design a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, which only supports single-edge triggered flip-flops, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and some combinational logic. The idea is to sample the input data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock using two separate flip-flops. One flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and the other on the falling edge. Then, we use a multiplexer to select the output of the flip-flop that corresponds to the most recent edge of the clock, effectively creating a dual-edge triggered behavior.
