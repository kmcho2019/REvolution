To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The first flip-flop will be triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the second flip-flop will be triggered on the falling edge of the clock. However, we cannot use the always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) sensitivity list as it's not a standard syntax for FPGAs. Instead, we'll use a single always block with a standard sensitivity list (posedge or negedge clk) and manually detect the edges by checking the previous state of the clock.
