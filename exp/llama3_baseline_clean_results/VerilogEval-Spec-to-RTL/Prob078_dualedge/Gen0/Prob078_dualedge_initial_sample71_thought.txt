To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using FPGA-compatible components, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and the other is triggered on the falling edge. To synchronize the outputs of these two flip-flops and ensure correct behavior, we use an additional logic that selects the output of the most recently triggered flip-flop based on the current edge of the clock.
