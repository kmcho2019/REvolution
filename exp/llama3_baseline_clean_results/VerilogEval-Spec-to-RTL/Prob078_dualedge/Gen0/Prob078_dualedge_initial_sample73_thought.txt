To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops (DFFs) in conjunction. One DFF is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other on the negative edge. The output of the two DFFs is then combined using an OR gate, ensuring that the output changes on either edge of the clock. This approach effectively emulates the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
