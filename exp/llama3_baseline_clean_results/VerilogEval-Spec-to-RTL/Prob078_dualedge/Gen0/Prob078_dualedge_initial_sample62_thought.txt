To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, since FPGAs don't inherently support dual-edge triggered flip-flops and always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) isn't valid, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops to achieve similar functionality. The idea is to have one flip-flop triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other on the falling edge. The output of the dual-edge flip-flop is then determined by the outputs of these two flip-flops, ensuring that it captures the input data on both edges of the clock. This requires a specific setup to handle the edge detection and data storage.
