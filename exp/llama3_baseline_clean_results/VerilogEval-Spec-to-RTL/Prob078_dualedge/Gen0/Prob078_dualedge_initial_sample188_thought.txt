To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using an always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) sensitivity list, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge and the other on the negative edge. We then use an OR gate to combine the outputs of these two flip-flops to generate the final output q. However, this approach would require an additional output from each of the flip-flops to indicate when they have captured a new value. A simpler and more efficient approach would be to use a single flip-flop and a mechanism to detect both rising and falling edges of the clock signal, then use that detection to decide when to capture the input d into the flip-flop.
