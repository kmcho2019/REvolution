To create a circuit that functionally behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to use one flip-flop that is triggered on the rising edge of the clock and another that is triggered on the falling edge. The output of the dual-edge flip-flop can then be determined by the outputs of these two flip-flops. This approach effectively captures the data on both rising and falling edges of the clock signal.
