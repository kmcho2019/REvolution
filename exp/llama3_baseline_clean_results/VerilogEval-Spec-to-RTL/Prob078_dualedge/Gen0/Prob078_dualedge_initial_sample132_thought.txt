To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other on the negative edge. We can then use a multiplexer to select the output of either flip-flop based on the current edge of the clock. However, an easier approach is to create an internal clock signal that is the inverse of the original clock, and then use two single-edge triggered flip-flops clocked by the original and the inverted clocks respectively.
