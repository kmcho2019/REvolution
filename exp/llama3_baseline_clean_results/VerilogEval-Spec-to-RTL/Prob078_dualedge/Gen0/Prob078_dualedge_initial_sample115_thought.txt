To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a clock divider to effectively capture the data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock. The idea is to have one flip-flop that captures data on the rising edge and another that captures data on the falling edge. We then need a mechanism to select between these two flip-flops based on the phase of the clock, ensuring that the output reflects the most recent data capture. This can be achieved by dividing the clock into two phases, each phase triggering one of the flip-flops.
