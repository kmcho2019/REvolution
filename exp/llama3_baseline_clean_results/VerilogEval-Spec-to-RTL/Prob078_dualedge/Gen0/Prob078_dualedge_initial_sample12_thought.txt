To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using a single-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two D flip-flops with an XOR gate to create the clock signal that is high for one clock cycle at every edge of the original clock. The first flip-flop is triggered at the rising edge and samples the input, while the second flip-flop samples the output of the first flip-flop at the falling edge. The outputs of the two flip-flops are then combined to produce the final output.
