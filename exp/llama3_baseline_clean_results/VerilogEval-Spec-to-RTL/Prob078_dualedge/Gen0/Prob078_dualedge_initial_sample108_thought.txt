To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using only single-edge triggered flip-flops, we can use two regular D flip-flops with one triggered on the rising edge and the other on the falling edge of the clock. The output of these two flip-flops can then be combined to produce the final output that effectively behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop. The idea is to use one flip-flop to capture data on the rising edge of the clock and another to capture data on the falling edge, then use the outputs of these flip-flops to generate the final output.
