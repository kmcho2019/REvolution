To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog without using the always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) sensitivity list, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a multiplexer. One flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other will be triggered on the negative edge. The multiplexer will then select the output from either flip-flop based on the current state of the clock. This approach ensures that the output changes on both edges of the clock, simulating the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
