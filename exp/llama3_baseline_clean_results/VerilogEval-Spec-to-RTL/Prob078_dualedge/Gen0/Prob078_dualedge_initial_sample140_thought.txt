To design a circuit that behaves like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops with their clock inputs connected to opposite edges of the main clock. The input data is sampled on both the positive and negative edges of the clock and then resolved to produce the output. We will implement this using two D flip-flops, one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other on the negative edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined using a multiplexer, with the selection signal derived from the current state of the clock.
