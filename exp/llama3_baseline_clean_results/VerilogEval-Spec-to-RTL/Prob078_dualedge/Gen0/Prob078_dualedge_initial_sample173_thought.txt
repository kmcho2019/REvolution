To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in Verilog, we can use two standard D flip-flops and a multiplexer. One D flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and the other on the falling edge. The multiplexer then selects the output from the appropriate flip-flop based on the current clock edge. This way, the output changes on both edges of the clock, mimicking the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
