To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops, each triggered by a different edge of the clock. The data input 'd' will be sampled at both the rising and falling edges of 'clk' by the two flip-flops, respectively. The outputs of these flip-flops will be combined using an OR gate to produce the final output 'q'. This way, any change in 'd' at either edge of 'clk' will be captured and reflected in 'q'.
