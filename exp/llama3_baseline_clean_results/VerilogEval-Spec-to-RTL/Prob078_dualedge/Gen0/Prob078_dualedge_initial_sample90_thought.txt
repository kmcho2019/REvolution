The idea is to use one flip-flop that is triggered on the positive edge of the clock and another that is triggered on the negative edge. The outputs of these two flip-flops are then combined using a multiplexer, where the select line of the multiplexer is controlled by the clock signal itself. This way, when the clock is high, the output of the positive edge flip-flop is selected, and when the clock is low, the output of the negative edge flip-flop is selected. This approach allows us to mimic the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, which only supports single-edge triggered flip-flops.
