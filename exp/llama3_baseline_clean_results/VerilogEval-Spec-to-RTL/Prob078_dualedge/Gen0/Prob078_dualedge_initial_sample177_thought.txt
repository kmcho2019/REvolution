To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a combination logic to decide which edge to trigger on. The idea is to generate two internal clock signals, one for the rising edge and one for the falling edge. These internal clocks are then used to trigger two single-edge triggered flip-flops, which are combined using a multiplexer to produce the output. Alternatively, a simpler approach is to use a single flip-flop with a clock enable, where the clock enable is high only on the desired edge, and the output of this flip-flop is used directly as the output.
