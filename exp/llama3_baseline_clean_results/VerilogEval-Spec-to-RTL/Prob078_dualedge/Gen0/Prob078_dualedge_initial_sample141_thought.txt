To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using the always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) sensitivity list, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. One flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other is triggered on the negative edge. However, instead of using the negative edge directly, we can create an inverted clock signal to trigger the second flip-flop on its positive edge. The output of the flip-flops can then be combined to produce the final output. This approach allows us to mimic the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using only positive edge-triggered flip-flops.
