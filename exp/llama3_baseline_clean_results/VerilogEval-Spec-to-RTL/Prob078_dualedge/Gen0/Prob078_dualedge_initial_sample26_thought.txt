To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops in a specific configuration. One flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other is triggered on the negative edge of the clock. We can then use a 2:1 multiplexer to select the output of the flip-flops based on the current state of the clock. However, a more straightforward approach is to simply use a single flip-flop with a clock that is the XOR of the original clock and its inverted version, which effectively doubles the clock frequency. This approach captures the data on both rising and falling edges of the original clock.
