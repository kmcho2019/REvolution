To create a dual-edge triggered flip-flop in an FPGA, we can utilize two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The idea is to use one flip-flop triggered on the rising edge of the clock and another on the falling edge. By combining the outputs of these two flip-flops using a multiplexer, we can achieve the functionality of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop. The select line of the multiplexer can be driven by the clock itself. When the clock is high, the multiplexer selects the output from the rising edge triggered flip-flop, and when the clock is low, it selects the output from the falling edge triggered flip-flop.
