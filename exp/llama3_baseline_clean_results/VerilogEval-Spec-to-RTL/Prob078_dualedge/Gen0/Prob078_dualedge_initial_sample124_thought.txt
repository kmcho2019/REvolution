To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The first flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the second on the negative edge. The data input of the first flip-flop is the input signal 'd' and the data input of the second flip-flop is also 'd'. The outputs of the two flip-flops are then multiplexed based on the current clock edge to produce the final output 'q'.
