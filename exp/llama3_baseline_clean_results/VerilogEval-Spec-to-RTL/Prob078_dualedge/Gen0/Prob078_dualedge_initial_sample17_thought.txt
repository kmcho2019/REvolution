To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops. The first flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock and captures the input data. The second flip-flop is triggered on the negative edge of the clock and captures the output of the first flip-flop. However, since FPGAs don't support always @(posedge clk or negedge clk) as a legal sensitivity list, we will instead use two separate always blocks, one for the positive edge and one for the negative edge, and use a variable to hold the data captured by the first always block. This allows us to functionally mimic the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
