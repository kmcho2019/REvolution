The module TopModule implements a combinational circuit. Analyzing the given simulation waveforms, we can observe the output q is 1 whenever at least two of the input signals (a, b, c, d) are 1. This is equivalent to implementing a threshold gate with a threshold value of 2. The circuit can be implemented using logical operations. We can use the formula: q = (a & b) | (a & c) | (a & d) | (b & c) | (b & d) | (c & d).
