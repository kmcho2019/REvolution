The module TopModule implements a combinational circuit that takes four one-bit inputs (a, b, c, d) and produces a one-bit output (q). By examining the simulation waveforms, it can be observed that the output q is 1 when at least one of the inputs a, b, or c is 1 and d is 1, or when both a and d are 1. This suggests that the circuit can be implemented using logical operators. 
One way to implement this is to use a combination of OR and AND gates. 
The output q is 1 when (a and d) or (b and d) or (c and d) is 1. This can be expressed as: q = (a and d) or (b and d) or (c and d).
This can be further simplified as q = (a or b or c) and d.
