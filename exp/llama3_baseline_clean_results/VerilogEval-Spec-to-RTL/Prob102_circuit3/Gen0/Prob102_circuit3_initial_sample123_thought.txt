From the waveforms, it appears that the output q is 1 when either b or d (or both) are 1, or when a and c are both 1. This can be expressed as a logical OR operation. The output q will be 1 if (b OR d OR (a AND c)) is true.

So, the thought is to implement a combinational circuit that performs this logic function: q = b OR d OR (a AND c).
