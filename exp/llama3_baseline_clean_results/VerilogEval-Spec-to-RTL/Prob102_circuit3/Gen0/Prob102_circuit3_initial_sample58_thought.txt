The problem asks us to design a combinational circuit based on given simulation waveforms. 
Analyzing the waveforms, we observe that output q is 1 whenever at least two of the inputs a, b, c, or d are 1. 
This behavior corresponds to a 2-of-4 majority function or a 4-input OR with a condition that at least two inputs must be high for the output to be 1. 
However, upon closer inspection, it seems the logic can be simplified to "q is 1 if (a and d) or (b and d) or (b and c) or (a and c) or (a and b)". 
Therefore, we will implement a combinational circuit based on this logic.
