The module TopModule implements a combinational circuit. To understand its functionality, we can analyze the given simulation waveforms. 

The output q seems to be 1 whenever inputs b and d, or inputs a and d, or inputs a and c, or inputs b and c are both 1. This pattern can be represented using a logical expression.

We will use the logical OR operation to combine the results of the AND operations between these input pairs. This will give us the output q.

This pattern resembles the behavior of a majority voter circuit where the output is 1 if at least two of the inputs (b, c, a, d) are 1.
