The problem requires us to design a combinational circuit with inputs a, b, c, d and output q. By analyzing the given simulation waveforms, we can observe that output q is 1 whenever at least two of the inputs are 1, or when input b and d are both 1. This suggests that the circuit is implementing a majority voter or a similar logic. We can implement this using basic logic gates, specifically using the OR, AND, and OR operations.

The logic equation can be derived as:
q = (a AND b) OR (a AND c) OR (a AND d) OR (b AND c) OR (b AND d) OR (c AND d) OR (b AND d)

However, a simpler equation can be observed:
q = (b AND d) OR (a AND (b OR c OR d)) OR (b AND (c OR d))
