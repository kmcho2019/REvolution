To solve this problem, we will implement a finite state machine (FSM) in Verilog. The FSM will have four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. In the IDLE state, the FSM will discard bytes until it sees one with in[3]=1. Then, it will transition to the BYTE1 state and store the current byte as the first byte of the message. In the BYTE1 and BYTE2 states, the FSM will store the current byte as the second and third byte of the message, respectively. Once the third byte is received, the FSM will transition to the IDLE state and signal the receipt of a message (done). The FSM will also reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is asserted.
