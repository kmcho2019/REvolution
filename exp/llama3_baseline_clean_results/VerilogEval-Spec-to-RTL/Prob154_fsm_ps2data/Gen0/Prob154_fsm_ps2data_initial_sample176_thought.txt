The TopModule can be implemented as a finite state machine with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. In the IDLE state, we continuously check the input byte stream until we find a byte with in[3] = 1. Once we find such a byte, we transition to the BYTE1 state and store this byte as the first byte of the message. In the BYTE1 state, we wait for the next byte and store it as the second byte of the message. Then, we transition to the BYTE2 state. In the BYTE2 state, we wait for the next byte and store it as the third byte of the message. After receiving the third byte, we signal done and output the complete 24-bit message. We reset the state machine whenever the reset signal is asserted.
