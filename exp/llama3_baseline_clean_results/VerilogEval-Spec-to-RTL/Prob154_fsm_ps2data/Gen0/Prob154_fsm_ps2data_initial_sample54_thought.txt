- BYTE1: The state where the FSM has received the first byte of the message and is waiting for the next two bytes.
- BYTE2: The state where the FSM has received the second byte of the message and is waiting for the third byte.
- DONE: The state where the FSM has received the third byte of the message and signals the receipt of a message.
