To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. In the IDLE state, we discard bytes until we see one with in[3] = 1. Once we see such a byte, we transition to the BYTE1 state and store this byte in the most significant bits of out_bytes. Then, we transition to the BYTE2 state, where we store the next byte in the middle bits of out_bytes. After that, we transition to a state where we store the next byte in the least significant bits of out_bytes and assert the done signal. We also need to handle the reset signal, which should transition us back to the IDLE state and clear out_bytes and done.
