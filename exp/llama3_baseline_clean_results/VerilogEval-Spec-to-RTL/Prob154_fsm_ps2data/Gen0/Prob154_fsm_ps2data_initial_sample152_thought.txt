To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. In the IDLE state, we wait for the input byte to have the most significant bit (in[3]) set to 1. Once we receive such a byte, we transition to the BYTE1 state and store the byte in the out_bytes[23:16] register. In the BYTE1 state, we wait for the next byte and store it in the out_bytes[15:8] register, then transition to the BYTE2 state. In the BYTE2 state, we wait for the next byte and store it in the out_bytes[7:0] register. After receiving the third byte, we assert the done signal for one clock cycle and then transition back to the IDLE state. The FSM will also be reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is asserted.
