To solve this problem, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM will start in the IDLE state and stay there until it receives a byte with in[3] = 1. When it receives such a byte, it will move to the BYTE1 state and store the byte. In the BYTE1 state, it will wait for the next byte and store it when received. Then it will move to the BYTE2 state and wait for the third byte. Once the third byte is received, it will move back to the IDLE state, signal done, and output the 24-bit message. The FSM will also be reset to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
