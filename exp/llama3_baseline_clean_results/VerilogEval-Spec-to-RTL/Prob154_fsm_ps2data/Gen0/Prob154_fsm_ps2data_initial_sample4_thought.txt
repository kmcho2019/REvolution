To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: 
1. IDLE - The initial state, where we are waiting for the first byte of a message.
2. BYTE1 - After receiving the first byte of a message.
3. BYTE2 - After receiving the second byte of a message.
4. DONE - After receiving the third byte of a message.

We will also need to store the received bytes in a register. The FSM will determine when a message is received and output the received message.

When the reset signal is high, we reset the FSM to the IDLE state and clear the registers.
