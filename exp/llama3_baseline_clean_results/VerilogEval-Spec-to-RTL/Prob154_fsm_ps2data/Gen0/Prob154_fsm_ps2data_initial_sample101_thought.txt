The TopModule will implement a finite state machine (FSM) with three states: IDLE, BYTE1, and BYTE2. The FSM starts in the IDLE state, where it discards incoming bytes until it finds a byte with in[3]=1. When it finds such a byte, it moves to the BYTE1 state, storing the byte as the first byte of the message. It then moves to the BYTE2 state, where it stores the next incoming byte as the second byte of the message. After receiving the second byte, it moves back to the BYTE2 state, waiting for the third byte. Once it receives the third byte, it moves back to the IDLE state, but asserts the done signal and outputs the 24-bit message (out_bytes). The FSM also responds to the reset signal by returning to the IDLE state.
