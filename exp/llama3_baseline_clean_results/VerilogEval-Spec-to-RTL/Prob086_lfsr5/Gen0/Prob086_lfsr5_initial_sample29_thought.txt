To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we will create a module with a 5-bit shift register where each bit is updated based on the presence of a tap. Since the taps are at positions 5 and 3, the new value of the most significant bit (q[4]) and the bit at position 3 (q[2]) will be the result of an XOR operation between the least significant bit (q[0]) and the current value of the bit at position 4 and 2 respectively, while the other bits simply shift right. The module will also include a reset mechanism that sets the LFSR output to 1 when the reset signal is high.
