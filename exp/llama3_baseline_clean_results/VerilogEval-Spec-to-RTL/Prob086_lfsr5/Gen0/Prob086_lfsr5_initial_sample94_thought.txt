To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we will use a reg to store the state of the LFSR. The module will have a reset input that resets the LFSR state to a non-zero value, and a clk input that triggers the update of the LFSR state on its positive edge. The taps for the Galois LFSR are at bit positions 5 and 3, which corresponds to bits q[4] and q[2] in the 5-bit vector q. We will calculate the new value of the MSB (q[4]) as the XOR of the current MSB and the LSB (q[0]), and similarly for the tap at bit position 3, the new value of q[2] will be the XOR of the current q[2] and the LSB (q[0]). The other bits will simply shift right, with the LSB (q[0]) being filled with the result of the XOR operation at the MSB.
