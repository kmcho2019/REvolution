To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we first need to identify the correct tap positions. Given that the taps are at positions 5 and 3, we will use these to determine the next state of the LFSR. On each clock cycle, the LFSR shifts right, and the tap positions are XORed with the LSB (q[0]) to produce their next values. For a 5-bit LFSR, we can represent the state as q[4:0]. The next state of q[4] will be q[3] XOR q[0] (since the tap at position 5 is effectively at q[4] in a 0-indexed system), q[3] will be q[2] XOR q[0] (due to the tap at position 3), and the rest of the bits shift right unchanged. We'll use a synchronous reset to set the initial state of the LFSR to a non-zero value (all ones in this case) to avoid the all-zero state.
