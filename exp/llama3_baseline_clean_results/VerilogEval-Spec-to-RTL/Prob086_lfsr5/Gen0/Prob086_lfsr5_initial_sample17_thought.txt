To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can use a register to hold the current state of the LFSR. On each positive clock edge, we calculate the next state by shifting the current state right and XORing the bits at the tap positions with the least significant bit (LSB). The LFSR is reset to 1 when the active-high reset signal is asserted. We will use a non-blocking assignment to update the register on the positive edge of the clock.
