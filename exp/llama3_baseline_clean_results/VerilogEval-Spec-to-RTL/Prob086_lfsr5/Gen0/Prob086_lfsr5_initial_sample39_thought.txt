To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we need to define the module TopModule with the given interface. The LFSR will shift right, where the tap positions (5 and 3) are XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce the next value, while other bit positions shift right unchanged. The active-high synchronous reset should reset the LFSR output to 1. We will use the IEEE 1364-2001 Verilog standard for this implementation.
