To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we will use a shift register with XOR gates to produce the next state. The Galois LFSR will shift right, where bit positions with a "tap" are XORed with the LSB output bit to produce its next value, while bit positions without a tap will shift right unchanged. The module will have an active-high synchronous reset that resets the LFSR output to 1. All sequential logic will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
