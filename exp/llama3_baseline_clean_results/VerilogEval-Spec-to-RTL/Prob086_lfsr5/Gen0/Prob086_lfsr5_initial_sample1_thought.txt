To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we first need to understand the required arrangement of taps and the operation of the shift register. Given that the taps are at positions 5 and 3, we will XOR the bits at these positions with the LSB (q[0]) to produce their next state values. All other bits will simply shift right. The reset should set the LFSR output to a non-zero state to avoid the all-zero state. We will use a 5-bit reg to hold the current state of the LFSR and update it based on the current state and the tap positions. On reset, we set the reg to a non-zero initial value, in this case, 1.
