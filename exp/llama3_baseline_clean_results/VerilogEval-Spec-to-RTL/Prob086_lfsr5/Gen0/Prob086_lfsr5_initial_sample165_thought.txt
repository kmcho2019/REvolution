To implement a 5-bit maximum-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we need to design a shift register where each bit position shifts right and the taps at positions 5 and 3 are XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce their next values. The module should also have a synchronous reset that resets the LFSR output to 1. We will use a reg type to hold the current state of the LFSR and always blocks to define the combinational and sequential logic.
