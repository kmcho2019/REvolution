To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we need to define the module TopModule with the given interface. The LFSR will have taps at bit positions 5 and 3. On the positive edge of the clock, the LFSR will shift right and the bits at the tap positions will be XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce their next values. We will use a reg variable to store the current state of the LFSR. When the reset signal is high, the LFSR will be reset to the initial value 1.
