To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we first need to understand the Galois LFSR architecture. The Galois LFSR shifts right, and bit positions with taps are XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce their next value, while bit positions without taps shift right unchanged. We will implement this using a register to hold the current state of the LFSR and a combinatorial logic to calculate the next state. The next state of the LFSR will be calculated based on the current state and the taps. We will also include a synchronous reset to reset the LFSR output to 1.
