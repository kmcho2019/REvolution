To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we will create a module named TopModule that takes in a clock and reset signal. The LFSR will have taps at bit positions 5 and 3. On each positive edge of the clock, the LFSR will shift right and XOR the taps with the least significant bit (q[0]) to produce the next state. The LFSR will be reset to 1 on the active-high synchronous reset signal. Since we are using a 5-bit LFSR, we will use a reg declaration to hold the current state of the LFSR and assign the output q to this state.
