To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we will use a shift register with XOR gates at the tap positions. The next state of each bit position without a tap is simply the current state of the bit position to its left. The next state of a bit position with a tap is the XOR of the current state of the bit position to its left and the current state of the least significant bit (LSB). We will use the active-high synchronous reset to reset the LFSR output to 1.
