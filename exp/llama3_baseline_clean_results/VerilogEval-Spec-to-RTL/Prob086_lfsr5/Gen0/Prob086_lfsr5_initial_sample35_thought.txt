To implement a 5-bit maximum-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can design a module with a 5-bit shift register. The shift register will have an active-high synchronous reset that sets the output to 1. The LFSR will shift right, and the taps at bit positions 5 and 3 will be XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce the next value. Since Verilog arrays are 0-indexed, the taps will be at positions 4 and 2. We will use a 5-bit reg to store the state of the LFSR and a 5-bit output wire to assign the state to the output q.
