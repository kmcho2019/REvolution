To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we will design a shift register with XOR gates at the tap positions (5 and 3) to produce the next state. We will use a synchronous reset to initialize the LFSR output to 1. On each positive clock edge, the register will shift right and the bits at the tap positions will be XORed with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce their next values.
