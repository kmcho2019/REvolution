To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3, we can first define the bit positions as they are numbered from 1 to 5 (MSB to LSB). Since we need taps at positions 5 and 3, we will XOR the output of the LFSR (LSB) with the values at these positions to produce the next state. The rest of the positions will simply shift right unchanged. The module will also have a synchronous reset to reset the LFSR output to 1. The LFSR output q will be a 5-bit vector.
