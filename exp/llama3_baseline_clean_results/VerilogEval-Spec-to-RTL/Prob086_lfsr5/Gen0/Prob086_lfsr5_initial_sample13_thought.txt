To implement the 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we will first define the internal 5-bit shift register. We will use a 5-bit reg to hold the state of the LFSR. The reset will be used to initialize the LFSR to a non-zero state, in this case, a single 1 in the MSB position (q[4]). On each positive clock edge, the LFSR will shift to the right, and the bits at positions with taps (5 and 3, noting that bit positions are 1-based in the problem description, but 0-based in Verilog) will be updated based on the XOR of the current values of these bits and the LSB (q[0]). Since the problem description uses 1-based indexing, the taps will actually be at positions 4 and 2 (0-based indexing). We will use the XOR operator (^) to compute the new values for the bits at positions 4 and 2.
