To implement a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we first need to define the tap positions, which are given as 5 and 3. Since the bit positions are 0-indexed, we can consider the taps as being at positions 4 and 2 (5-1 and 3-1 respectively) in a 5-bit register. The next state of the LFSR is determined by XORing the bits at the tap positions with the least significant bit (q[0]) and shifting the register right. On reset, the LFSR should be initialized to a non-zero state, in this case, 1.
