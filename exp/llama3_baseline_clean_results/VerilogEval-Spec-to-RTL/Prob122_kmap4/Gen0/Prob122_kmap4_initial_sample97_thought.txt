The Karnaugh map provided can be simplified into a Boolean expression. We can start by looking for adjacent 1's in the map and combining them into larger groups to minimize the expression. We notice that there are no adjacent 1's in the same row or column, but there are 1's that can be combined across rows and columns. We can express the output 'out' as a sum of products, where each term corresponds to a group of adjacent 1's in the Karnaugh map.

Upon analyzing the map, the expression for 'out' can be derived. The 'out' is high when:
- a is high and b is low and c is low (irrespective of d), or
- a is low and b is low and c is high (irrespective of d), or
- a is high and b is high and c is high (irrespective of d), or
- a is low and b is high and c is low and d is high.

Thus, the simplified Boolean expression is: out = (a & ~b & ~c) | (~a & ~b & c) | (a & b & c) | (~a & b & ~c & d).
