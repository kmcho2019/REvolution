To reverse the bit ordering of the input port, we can use Verilog's bit indexing feature to assign each bit of the output port the corresponding bit from the input port, but in reverse order. This means that the least significant bit (bit 0) of the output will be assigned the most significant bit (bit 7) of the input, and so on, until the most significant bit (bit 7) of the output is assigned the least significant bit (bit 0) of the input.
