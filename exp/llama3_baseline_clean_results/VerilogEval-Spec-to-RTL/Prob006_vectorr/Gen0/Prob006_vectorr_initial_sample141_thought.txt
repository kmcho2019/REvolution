To reverse the bit ordering of the input port, we can use Verilog's bit-wise indexing feature. We will create a new 8-bit output signal and assign each bit of the output to the corresponding bit of the input, but in reverse order. This means that the 0th bit of the input will be assigned to the 7th bit of the output, the 1st bit of the input will be assigned to the 6th bit of the output, and so on.
