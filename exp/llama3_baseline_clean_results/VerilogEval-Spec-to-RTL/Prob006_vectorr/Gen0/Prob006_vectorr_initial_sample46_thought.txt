To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple assignment statement within the module, where the output port bits are directly assigned the corresponding reversed bits from the input port. We can use bit indexing to achieve this reversal. For an 8-bit input, the bit at position 0 becomes the bit at position 7 in the output, the bit at position 1 becomes the bit at position 6, and so on, until the bit at position 7 becomes the bit at position 0.
