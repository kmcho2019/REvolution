To implement the TopModule, we need to create a Verilog module that matches the given interface and functionality of the 7458 chip. We will use internal wires to connect the outputs of the AND gates to the OR gates. This approach allows for clear and organized code. We will declare four internal wires: and1, and2, and3, and and4. The and1 and and2 wires will be driven by the outputs of the two 3-input AND gates for p1y, and the and3 and and4 wires will be driven by the outputs of the two 2-input AND gates for p2y. The p1y output will be the OR of and1 and and2, and the p2y output will be the OR of and3 and and4.
