The TopModule should implement the functionality of the 7458 chip by using AND and OR operations to produce the outputs p1y and p2y. 
For p1y, two 3-input AND gates are needed, one for p1a, p1b, and p1c, and the other for p1d, p1e, and p1f. 
The output of these AND gates should be ORed together to produce p1y. 
For p2y, two 2-input AND gates are needed, one for p2a and p2b, and the other for p2c and p2d. 
The output of these AND gates should be ORed together to produce p2y. 
This can be achieved using Verilog's built-in logical operators or by declaring intermediate wires for the AND gates and then using these wires in OR operations.
