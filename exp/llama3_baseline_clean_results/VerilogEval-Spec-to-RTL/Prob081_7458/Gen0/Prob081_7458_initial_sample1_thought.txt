To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we need to create a design that includes four AND gates and two OR gates. For the p1y output, we will use two 3-input AND gates and then OR their outputs. For the p2y output, we will use two 2-input AND gates and then OR their outputs. We can implement this using either assign statements to drive the output wires directly or by declaring intermediate wires to hold the outputs of the AND gates before being ORed. Here, we choose to use assign statements for simplicity and directness.
