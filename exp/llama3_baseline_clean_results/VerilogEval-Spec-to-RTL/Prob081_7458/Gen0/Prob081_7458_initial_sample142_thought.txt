To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we will use an `assign` statement to drive each of the output wires. 
For p1y, we will use two 3-input AND gates, one for p1a, p1b, and p1c, 
and the other for p1d, p1e, and p1f, and then OR the results. 
For p2y, we will use two 2-input AND gates, one for p2a and p2b, 
and the other for p2c and p2d, and then OR the results.
