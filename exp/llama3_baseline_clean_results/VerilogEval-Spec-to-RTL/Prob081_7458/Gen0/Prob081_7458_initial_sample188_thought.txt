To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we need to create a module with 10 input ports and 2 output ports. We can use either `assign` statements or intermediate wires to drive the output wires. In this case, we'll use the intermediate wires approach for better clarity and readability. The module will have two 3-input AND gates and two 2-input AND gates, with the outputs of the AND gates being ORed together to produce the final outputs.
