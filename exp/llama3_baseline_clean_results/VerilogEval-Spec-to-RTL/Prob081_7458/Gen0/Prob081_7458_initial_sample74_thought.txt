To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can use a combination of logical operations. For the output p1y, we need 
to create two 3-input AND gates and then take the OR of their outputs. 
For the output p2y, we need to create two 2-input AND gates and then take 
the OR of their outputs. We can either use assign statements to directly 
drive the output wires or declare intermediate wires to improve readability.
