we can use a combination of AND and OR gates.

and the other for p1d, p1e, and p1f. Then, we will use an OR gate to combine 
the outputs of these two AND gates. 
For p2y, we will first create two 2-input AND gates: one for p2a and p2b, 
and the other for p2c and p2d. Then, we will use an OR gate to combine 
the outputs of these two AND gates.
We can implement this logic using assign statements to drive the output wires, 
or by declaring wires as intermediate signals for the outputs of the AND gates.
