To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we will first define the module with the given interface. Then, we can use either `assign` statements to directly drive the output wires or declare internal wires to store the outputs of the AND gates. For better readability and maintainability, we'll choose to declare internal wires for the AND gates' outputs. We will then use these internal wires to compute the final outputs p1y and p2y using OR operations.
