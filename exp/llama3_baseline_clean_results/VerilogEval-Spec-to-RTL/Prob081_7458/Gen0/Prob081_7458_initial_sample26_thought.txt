To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we will first create two 3-input AND gates for p1y and two 2-input AND gates for p2y. Then, we will use two OR gates to combine the outputs of the AND gates to produce p1y and p2y. We can use either assign statements or declare intermediate wires to drive the output wires. Here, we'll use assign statements for simplicity and readability.
