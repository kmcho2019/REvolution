To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we need to design two separate logic circuits for p1y and p2y outputs. 
For p1y, we will use two 3-input AND gates and one OR gate. For p2y, we 
will use two 2-input AND gates and one OR gate. We can either use 
`assign` statements directly with logical operators to compute the output 
signals or use intermediate wires to make the code more readable. Here, 
we'll use the intermediate wires approach for better understanding of the 
internal logic.
