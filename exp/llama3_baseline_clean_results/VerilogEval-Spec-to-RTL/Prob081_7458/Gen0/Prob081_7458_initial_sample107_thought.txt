To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can first identify the logic required for each output. For p1y, it is 
the OR of two 3-input AND gates. For p2y, it is the OR of two 2-input AND 
gates. We can implement this logic using assign statements in Verilog, 
driving each output wire directly with the required logical operations, 
without needing to declare intermediate wires for the AND gates.
