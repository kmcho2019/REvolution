To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we will first create two 3-input AND gates and two 2-input AND gates. Then we will use two OR gates to combine the outputs of the AND gates. We can use either `assign` statements to directly drive the output wires or declare intermediate wires to drive the outputs. In this case, I will use `assign` statements to directly drive the outputs for simplicity and readability.
