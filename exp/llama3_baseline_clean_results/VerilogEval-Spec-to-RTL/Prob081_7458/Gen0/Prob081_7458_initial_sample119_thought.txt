The TopModule should be designed with the same functionality as the 7458 chip. To achieve this, we can use either `assign` statements to directly compute the output values or declare intermediate wires to break down the computation into smaller steps. For the output `p1y`, we need to implement two 3-input AND gates and then OR their outputs. For `p2y`, we need to implement two 2-input AND gates and OR their outputs. In this implementation, using intermediate wires for clarity and readability is a good approach. We will declare four wires for the outputs of the AND gates and then use these wires to compute the final outputs `p1y` and `p2y`.
