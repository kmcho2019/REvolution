The module TopModule can be implemented by directly mapping the functionality of the 7458 chip. For the output p1y, two 3-input AND gates are needed, and their outputs are ORed together. For the output p2y, two 2-input AND gates are needed, and their outputs are ORed together. This can be achieved using Verilog's logical operators. We can use the `assign` statement to drive each output wire directly with the calculated logic expressions.
