The TopModule will be designed to mimic the functionality of the 7458 chip. 
To achieve this, we will first create two 3-input AND gates for p1y, 
then create two 2-input AND gates for p2y. 
Finally, we will use two OR gates to combine the outputs of the AND gates 
for each of the output signals p1y and p2y. 
This can be implemented using assign statements in Verilog, 
utilizing the logical operators for AND (&) and OR (|).
