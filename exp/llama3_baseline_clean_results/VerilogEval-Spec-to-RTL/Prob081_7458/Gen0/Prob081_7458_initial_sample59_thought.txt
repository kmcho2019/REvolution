To implement the TopModule, we can use two approaches as mentioned: 
1) Directly use assign statements to drive each output wire, or 
2) Declare internal wires to connect the output of AND gates to the input of OR gates.

Here, we will use both methods for clarity and comparison. In the first method, 
we directly use assign statements to calculate the outputs p1y and p2y. 
In the second method, we declare internal wires for better readability and understanding of the circuit.

For p1y, we calculate the OR of two 3-input AND gates: (p1a AND p1b AND p1c) and (p1d AND p1e AND p1f).
For p2y, we calculate the OR of two 2-input AND gates: (p2a AND p2b) and (p2c AND p2d).
