To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can directly use logical operations to replicate the behavior of the 
AND and OR gates. The output p1y will be the logical OR of two 3-input 
AND operations: (p1a AND p1b AND p1c) and (p1d AND p1e AND p1f). The 
output p2y will be the logical OR of two 2-input AND operations: (p2a AND 
p2b) and (p2c AND p2d). This can be achieved using the logical operators 
in Verilog.
