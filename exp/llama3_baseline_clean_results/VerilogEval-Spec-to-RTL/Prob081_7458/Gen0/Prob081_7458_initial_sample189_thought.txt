The TopModule will be implemented using a combination of AND and OR operations. For p1y, two 3-input AND gates are required, which can be achieved using the logical AND operator (&) in Verilog. The outputs of these AND gates are then combined using a 2-input OR gate, which can be represented by the logical OR operator (|) in Verilog. Similarly, for p2y, two 2-input AND gates are needed, and their outputs are combined using another 2-input OR gate. The `assign` statement can be used to drive the output wires directly.
