To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we will create a module with 10 input ports and 2 output ports. 
We can use either an `assign` statement to drive each of the output wires directly 
or declare internal wires to store the outputs of the AND gates and then 
drive the output wires using these internal wires. 
In this case, we will choose to declare internal wires to improve code readability. 
The output p1y will be the OR of two 3-input AND gates, 
and the output p2y will be the OR of two 2-input AND gates.
