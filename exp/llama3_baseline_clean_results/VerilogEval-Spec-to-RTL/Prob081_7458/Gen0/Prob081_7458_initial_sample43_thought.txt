To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we need to create two separate logic paths: one for p1y and one for p2y. For p1y, we will use two 3-input AND gates and then OR their outputs. For p2y, we will use two 2-input AND gates and then OR their outputs. We can achieve this by using either assign statements to drive the output wires directly or by declaring intermediate wires for the outputs of the AND gates.
