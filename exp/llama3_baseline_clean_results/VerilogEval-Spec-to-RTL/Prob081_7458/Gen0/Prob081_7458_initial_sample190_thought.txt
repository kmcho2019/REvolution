To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can use Verilog's built-in logical operators. We can create two internal 
signals for each pair of AND gates and then use the OR operator to combine 
them. For p1y, we will AND p1a, p1b, and p1c, and p1d, p1e, and p1f separately, 
then OR the results. For p2y, we will AND p2a and p2b, and p2c and p2d separately, 
then OR the results. We can use `assign` statements to drive each of the output wires.
