The problem requires designing a Verilog module named TopModule that mimics the functionality of the 7458 chip. This chip has four AND gates and two OR gates. To implement this, we can use either continuous assignments (`assign` statements) to directly drive the output wires or declare internal wires to hold the output of the AND gates before applying the OR operation. In this case, using `assign` statements seems more straightforward as it directly implements the required logic without the need for intermediate wires.
