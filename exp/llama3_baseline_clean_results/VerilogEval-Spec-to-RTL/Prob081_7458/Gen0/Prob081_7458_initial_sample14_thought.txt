To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we will use `assign` statements to drive each of the output wires directly. 
This will involve creating the two OR gates and four AND gates as described 
in the problem statement. The two OR gates will be implemented using the 
`|` operator, and the AND gates will be implemented using the `&` operator. 
The AND gates will be combined using the OR gates to produce the final outputs.
