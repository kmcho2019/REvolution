The TopModule will be implemented using a combination of logical AND and OR operations. For the p1y output, two 3-input AND gates will be used to combine p1a, p1b, p1c and p1d, p1e, p1f respectively, and then their results will be ORed together. For the p2y output, two 2-input AND gates will be used to combine p2a, p2b and p2c, p2d respectively, and then their results will be ORed together. We can choose to use either assign statements or intermediate wires to achieve this.
