To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we can use an assign statement to directly drive the output wires. However, for better readability and maintainability, we can declare intermediate wires to represent the outputs of the AND gates. This way, we can clearly see the internal signals and their connections. 
We will declare four wires: and1, and2, and3, and and4, where and1 and and2 are the outputs of the two 3-input AND gates for p1y, and and3 and and4 are the outputs of the two 2-input AND gates for p2y. 
Then we can use assign statements to drive the output wires p1y and p2y with the OR of the respective AND gates' outputs.
