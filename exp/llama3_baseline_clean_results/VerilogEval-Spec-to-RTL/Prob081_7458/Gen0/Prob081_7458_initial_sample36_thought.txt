To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we will use two approaches: 
1. Directly drive the output wires using `assign` statements, which involves 
   combining the AND operations within the OR operations for both outputs.
2. Declare intermediate wires for each AND gate output and then use these 
   wires to drive the final output wires. 
Here, we'll choose the first approach for its simplicity and readability.
The module TopModule will have 10 input ports and 2 output ports as specified.
