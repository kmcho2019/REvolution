To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we will create a module with 10 input ports and 2 output ports. We will use internal wires to drive the output of the AND gates and then use these wires to drive the output of the OR gates. The p1y output will be the OR of two 3-input AND gates, and the p2y output will be the OR of two 2-input AND gates.
