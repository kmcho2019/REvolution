To implement the 7458 chip functionality, we need to create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. We will use `assign` statements to drive each of the output wires, p1y and p2y, directly. For p1y, we will create an OR of two 3-input AND gates, and for p2y, we will create an OR of two 2-input AND gates. This approach eliminates the need for declaring intermediate wires.
