To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we need to use the provided input ports and create the necessary logic 
to produce the desired outputs. The module should have two outputs: p1y 
and p2y. The output p1y is the result of an OR operation between two 
3-input AND gates. The first AND gate should AND the inputs p1a, p1b, 
and p1c, while the second AND gate should AND the inputs p1d, p1e, and 
p1f. On the other hand, the output p2y is the result of an OR operation 
between two 2-input AND gates. The first AND gate should AND the inputs 
p2a and p2b, while the second AND gate should AND the inputs p2c and p2d.
