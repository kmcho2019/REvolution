To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can use a combination of AND and OR logic gates. For output p1y, we need 
two 3-input AND gates and one 2-input OR gate. For output p2y, we need two 
2-input AND gates and one 2-input OR gate. We can use `assign` statements 
to drive the output wires directly or declare intermediate wires for better 
readability. In this solution, we will use both approaches for illustration.
