To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can use a combination of logical AND and OR operations. We can first 
create intermediate signals for the outputs of the AND gates, and then 
use these signals to drive the final outputs. We have two options for 
driving the outputs: we can use assign statements directly with the 
inputs, or we can declare wires for the intermediate signals and then 
use assign statements to drive the outputs. Here, I will use both 
approaches for demonstration purposes. However, for simplicity and 
clarity, I will primarily use the approach of declaring wires for 
intermediate signals and then driving the outputs.
