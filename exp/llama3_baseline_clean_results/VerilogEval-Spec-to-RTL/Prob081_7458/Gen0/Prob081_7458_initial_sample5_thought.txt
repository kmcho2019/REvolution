To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, we need to create a module with 10 input ports and 2 output ports. The output p1y will be the OR of two 3-input AND gates, while the output p2y will be the OR of two 2-input AND gates. We can achieve this using either assign statements or by declaring internal wires to drive the output of each AND gate. For simplicity and readability, we'll use the internal wires approach.
