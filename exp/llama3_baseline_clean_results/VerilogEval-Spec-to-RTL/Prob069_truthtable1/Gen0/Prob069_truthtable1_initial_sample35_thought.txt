To implement the combinational circuit described by the truth table, 
we can use a logic equation that covers all the cases where the output f is 1. 
Looking at the truth table, f is 1 in the following cases: 
- when x2 is 1 and x1 is 0 or 1 (x3 is 0), 
- when x3 is 1 and x1 is 1 (x2 is 0 or 1). 
So, the logic equation can be derived as f = (x2 & (~x3)) | (x1 & x3).
