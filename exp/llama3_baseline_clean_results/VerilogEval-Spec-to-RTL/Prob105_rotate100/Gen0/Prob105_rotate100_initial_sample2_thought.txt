The module TopModule will be designed to implement a 100-bit left/right rotator. 
The rotator will have a synchronous load and left/right enable. 
If the load signal is high, the module will load the shift register with the input data. 
If the load signal is low, the module will rotate the bits based on the ena signal. 
The ena signal will determine the direction of rotation: right rotation for ena = 2'b01, 
left rotation for ena = 2'b10, and no rotation for ena = 2'b00 or 2'b11. 
The rotated bits will be stored in the output q.
