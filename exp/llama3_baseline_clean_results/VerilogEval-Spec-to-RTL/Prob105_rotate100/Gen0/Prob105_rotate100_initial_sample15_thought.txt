To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the behavior of the module based on the given inputs. 
The module has a synchronous load signal 'load' which loads the data into the rotator when high. 
The 'ena' input is a 2-bit signal that determines the direction of rotation: 
    - 2'b01 rotates the bits to the right by one position,
    - 2'b10 rotates the bits to the left by one position,
    - 2'b00 and 2'b11 do not rotate the bits.
We will use a 100-bit reg to hold the current state of the rotator and update it based on the 'load' and 'ena' signals.
We will use a combination of concatenation and bit manipulation to achieve the rotation.
