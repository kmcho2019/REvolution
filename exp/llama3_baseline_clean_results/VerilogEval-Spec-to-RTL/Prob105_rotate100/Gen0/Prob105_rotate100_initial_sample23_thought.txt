The design of the TopModule will involve a 100-bit register to store the current state of the rotator. The module will have two main operations: loading new data and rotating the existing data. The load operation will be triggered by the 'load' signal and will replace the current state with the input 'data'. The rotation operation will be controlled by the 'ena' signal, where 'ena[1:0]' will determine the direction and whether to rotate or not. If 'ena' is '2'b01', the rotator will rotate right by one bit, and if 'ena' is '2'b10', the rotator will rotate left by one bit. For 'ena' values '2'b00' and '2'b11', there will be no rotation.
