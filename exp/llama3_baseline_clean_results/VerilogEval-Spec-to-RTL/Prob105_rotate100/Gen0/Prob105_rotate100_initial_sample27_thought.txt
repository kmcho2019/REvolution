To implement the TopModule, we need to create a 100-bit register that can load data synchronously and rotate its contents left or right based on the ena input. We can use a case statement to handle the different values of ena and decide whether to load, rotate left, or rotate right. When ena is 2'b01, we will rotate the register right by one bit, and when ena is 2'b10, we will rotate the register left by one bit. For other values of ena, the register will not change. The load signal will override the rotation and load the data into the register.
