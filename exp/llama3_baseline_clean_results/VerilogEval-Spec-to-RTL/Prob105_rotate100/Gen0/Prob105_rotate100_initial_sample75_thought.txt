The TopModule can be implemented using a 100-bit register with synchronous load and rotation capabilities. 
When the load signal is high, the register should be loaded with the input data. 
When the load signal is low, the register should rotate its contents based on the ena signal. 
The ena signal determines the direction of rotation: right for ena = 2'b01, left for ena = 2'b10, and no rotation for ena = 2'b00 or ena = 2'b11. 
This can be achieved using a combination of conditional statements and bit manipulation operations.
