To implement the 100-bit left/right rotator module TopModule, we need to follow these steps:
1. On every rising edge of the clock signal 'clk', check the 'load' signal. If 'load' is high, load the 'data' into the rotator's register 'q'.
2. If 'load' is low, check the 'ena' signal to determine the rotation direction. If 'ena' is 2'b01, rotate the bits in 'q' to the right by one position. If 'ena' is 2'b10, rotate the bits in 'q' to the left by one position. If 'ena' is 2'b00 or 2'b11, do not rotate the bits.
3. For a right rotation, the least significant bit (LSB) of 'q' will be shifted to the most significant bit (MSB) position, and all other bits will shift to the right.
4. For a left rotation, the most significant bit (MSB) of 'q' will be shifted to the least significant bit (LSB) position, and all other bits will shift to the left.
5. If the 'ena' signal is 2'b00 or 2'b11, the value of 'q' remains unchanged.
