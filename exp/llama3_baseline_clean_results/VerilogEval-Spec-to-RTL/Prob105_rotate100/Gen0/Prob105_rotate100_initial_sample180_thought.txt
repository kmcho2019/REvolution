The TopModule will be implemented with a 100-bit register and a synchronous load feature. To implement the left and right rotation, we can use a combination of concatenation and bit manipulation. For the load operation, we can simply assign the input data to the register when the load signal is high. For the rotation operations, we can use the enable signal to choose the direction of rotation and concatenate the bits accordingly. We will use the ena[1:0] signal to determine whether to rotate left, right, or not at all.
