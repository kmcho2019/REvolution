To implement the 100-bit left/right rotator module named TopModule, we can utilize Verilog's built-in bit manipulation capabilities and modular arithmetic to perform the rotation. The load signal will be used to load the data into the register. The ena signal will determine the direction of rotation. We will use a case statement to handle the different rotation scenarios based on the ena signal. The rotation will be performed on the current state of the register (q) and the new state will be assigned to q.
