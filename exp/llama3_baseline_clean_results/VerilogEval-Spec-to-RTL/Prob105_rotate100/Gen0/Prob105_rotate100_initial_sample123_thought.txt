To implement the 100-bit left/right rotator, we'll design a TopModule that takes into account the synchronous load, rotation enable, and rotation direction. The module will have a 100-bit register to store the current state of the rotator. When the load signal is high, the register will be loaded with the input data. Otherwise, the register will rotate according to the enable signal. The rotation will be performed by shifting the bits in the register and shifting in the shifted-out bit from the other end, effectively rotating the bits around.
