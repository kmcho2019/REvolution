The TopModule will be designed with a 100-bit register to store the data. 
The module will have three main operations: load, rotate right, and rotate left.
The load operation will overwrite the current data in the register with the input data when the load signal is high.
The rotate right operation will shift the bits to the right and the shifted-out bit will be rotated to the leftmost position when ena[1:0] is 2'b01.
The rotate left operation will shift the bits to the left and the shifted-out bit will be rotated to the rightmost position when ena[1:0] is 2'b10.
When ena[1:0] is 2'b00 or 2'b11, the data in the register will remain unchanged.
The output q will always reflect the current state of the register.
