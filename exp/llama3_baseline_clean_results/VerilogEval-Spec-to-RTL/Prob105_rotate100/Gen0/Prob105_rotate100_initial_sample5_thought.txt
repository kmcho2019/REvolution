The TopModule will be designed as a 100-bit register that can be loaded synchronously with the input data. The enable signal, ena, will determine the direction of rotation. If ena is 2'b01, the register will rotate right by one bit, shifting the least significant bit to the most significant bit position. If ena is 2'b10, the register will rotate left by one bit, shifting the most significant bit to the least significant bit position. For ena values of 2'b00 or 2'b11, the register will not rotate. The load signal will override the rotation operation and load the register with the input data.
