The TopModule will be implemented as a 100-bit register that can be loaded with the input data or rotated left or right based on the ena signal. The load signal will be used to load the data into the register synchronously. The ena signal will be decoded to determine the direction of rotation. If ena is 2'b01, the register will be rotated right by one bit. If ena is 2'b10, the register will be rotated left by one bit. If ena is 2'b00 or 2'b11, the register will not be rotated.
