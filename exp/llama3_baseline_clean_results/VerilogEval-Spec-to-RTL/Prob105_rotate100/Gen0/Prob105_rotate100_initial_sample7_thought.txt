To implement the 100-bit left/right rotator, we will use a single 100-bit register to store the data. We will have a multiplexer to decide whether to load new data into the register or rotate the existing data. The rotation will be done by using concatenation and indexing of the register. We will have two cases for rotation: left rotation and right rotation. The enable signal will decide whether to rotate or not, and in which direction. 
We will also use a case statement to handle the different possibilities of the enable signal.
