The module TopModule will be implemented using a 100-bit shift register. The load signal will be used to load the data into the register synchronously. The ena signal will be used to control the direction of rotation. The rotation will be achieved by concatenating the shifted bits with the rest of the register. The left and right rotation will be implemented separately and then combined based on the ena signal. If ena is 2'b00 or 2'b11, the register will not rotate.
