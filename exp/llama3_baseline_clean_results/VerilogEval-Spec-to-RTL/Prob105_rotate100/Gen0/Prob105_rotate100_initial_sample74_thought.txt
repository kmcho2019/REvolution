To implement the TopModule with the specified interface and functionality, I will design a synchronous 100-bit register that can load new data, rotate its contents left or right, or remain unchanged based on the input signals. The load signal will override any rotation when it is high, and the ena signal will determine the direction of rotation. When ena is 2'b01, the register will rotate right; when it is 2'b10, it will rotate left. For ena values 2'b00 and 2'b11, there will be no rotation. This can be achieved using a combination of conditional logic and shift operations within a always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal.
