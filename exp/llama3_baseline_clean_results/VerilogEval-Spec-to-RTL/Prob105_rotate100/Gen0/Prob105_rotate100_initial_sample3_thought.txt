The module TopModule will be designed to handle synchronous loading and rotation of a 100-bit register. 
The design will use a case statement inside an always block to check the enable signals and perform the corresponding rotation or loading. 
If load is high, the module will load the input data into the register. 
If load is low, the module will check the ena signals to determine the direction of rotation. 
The rotation will be performed by shifting the bits in the specified direction and reassigning the shifted-out bit to the other end of the register. 
For all other cases, the register will retain its current value.
