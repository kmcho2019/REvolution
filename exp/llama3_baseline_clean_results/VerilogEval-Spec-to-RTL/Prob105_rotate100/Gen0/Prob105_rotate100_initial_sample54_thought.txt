The TopModule will be designed as a 100-bit register with synchronous load and left/right rotation capabilities. The load signal will override any rotation and directly load the data into the register. The ena signal will determine the direction of rotation, with 2'b01 rotating right, 2'b10 rotating left, and 2'b00 and 2'b11 resulting in no rotation. 
We will use the always @(posedge clk) block to handle the synchronous operations and implement the rotation logic using bit manipulation and concatenation.
