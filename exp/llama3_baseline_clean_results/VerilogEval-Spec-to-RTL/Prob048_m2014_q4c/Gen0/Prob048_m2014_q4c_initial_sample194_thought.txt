To implement a simple D flip-flop with active high synchronous reset, we need to consider the behavior of the flip-flop on the rising edge of the clock signal (clk). When the reset signal (r) is high, the output (q) should be reset to 0. Otherwise, on the rising edge of the clock, the input (d) should be captured and stored as the new output (q). This behavior can be achieved using a simple if-else statement inside an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock.
