To implement a simple D flip flop with active high synchronous reset, we will use a single D flip flop component. The flip flop will capture the input 'd' on the rising edge of the clock 'clk' and store it in the output 'q'. If the reset signal 'r' is high at the same time as the rising edge of the clock, the output 'q' will be reset to 0, regardless of the value of 'd'.
