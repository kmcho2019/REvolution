To implement a D flip flop with active high synchronous reset, we need to use a clocked always block that captures the input 'd' on the rising edge of the clock 'clk'. The reset 'r' should be checked at the beginning of the always block. If 'r' is high, the output 'q' should be reset to 0. Otherwise, 'q' should be assigned the value of 'd' on the rising edge of 'clk'.
