To implement a D flip flop with active high synchronous reset, we can use a simple always block in Verilog. The always block will be sensitive to the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will check the reset signal (r). If the reset signal is high, the output (q) will be reset to 0. If the reset signal is low, the output (q) will be updated with the input (d) on the next positive edge of the clock.
