To implement the 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator, we will design a module named TopModule that includes counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be a BCD counter. 
The seconds counter will increment every time ena is asserted (once per second), the minutes counter will increment when the seconds counter overflows, and the hours counter will increment when the minutes counter overflows. 
We will also implement logic to determine whether the clock is AM or PM based on the hour value. 
Reset will be handled as a synchronous signal, resetting all counters to their initial state when asserted.
