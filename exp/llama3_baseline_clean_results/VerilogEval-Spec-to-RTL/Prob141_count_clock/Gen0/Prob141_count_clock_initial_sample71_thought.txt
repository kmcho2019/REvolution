To implement the 12-hour clock, I will design three separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock when the enable signal is high. The hours counter will be 4 bits to represent BCD digits 01-12, the minutes and seconds counters will be 6 bits to represent BCD digits 00-59. I will also include a 1-bit pm signal to indicate AM/PM. When the reset signal is high, all counters will be reset to their initial values (12:00 AM). To handle the increment logic, I will use conditional statements to check the current values of the counters and update them accordingly.
