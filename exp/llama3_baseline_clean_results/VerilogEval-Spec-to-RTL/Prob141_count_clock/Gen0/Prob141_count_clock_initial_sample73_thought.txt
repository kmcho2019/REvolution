To implement the 12-hour clock, we'll use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will have a specific range: hours will range from 1 to 12, and minutes and seconds will range from 0 to 59. We'll use Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) representation for these counters, where each digit is represented by 4 bits. The counters will increment on every positive edge of the clock when 'ena' is high. The 'pm' signal will be generated based on the hour value. When the reset signal is high, all counters will be reset to their initial values (12 for hours, 0 for minutes and seconds, and 'pm' will be set to '0' for AM).
