To implement the 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we can use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter should be able to store Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) values. The counters will increment once per second when the enable signal 'ena' is high. We will use a modulo 60 counter for seconds and minutes, and a modulo 12 counter for hours. The PM indicator will be asserted when the hours counter is greater than or equal to 12 (in 24-hour format). On reset, the counters should reset to their initial values, which are 12 for hours (in 12-hour format), 0 for minutes, and 0 for seconds. We will also need to handle the carry from seconds to minutes and minutes to hours.
