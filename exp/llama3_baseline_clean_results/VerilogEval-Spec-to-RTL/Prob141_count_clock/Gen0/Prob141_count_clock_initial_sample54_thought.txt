To implement a 12-hour clock module, we'll create separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock and will only increment when the enable signal is asserted. We'll use a 2-bit binary code for AM/PM indicator, where 'pm' will be '1' for PM and '0' for AM. The reset signal will have higher priority, and when asserted, all counters and the AM/PM indicator will reset to their initial values. We'll also ensure that hours, minutes, and seconds are in BCD format and wrap around correctly (e.g., hours from 12 to 1, minutes and seconds from 59 to 0).
