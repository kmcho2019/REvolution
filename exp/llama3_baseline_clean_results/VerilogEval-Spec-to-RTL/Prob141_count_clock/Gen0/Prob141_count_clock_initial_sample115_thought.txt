To implement the 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator, we can use a hierarchical approach with separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to count up to its maximum value (12 for hours, 59 for minutes and seconds), then wrap around to its minimum value (1 for hours, 0 for minutes and seconds). The counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk and will increment only when the ena signal is high. The pm signal will be asserted when the hour is greater than 11. We will also include a reset mechanism that resets the clock to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is high. We will use BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) representation for the hours, minutes, and seconds, which will require 8 bits for each (two 4-bit BCD digits).
