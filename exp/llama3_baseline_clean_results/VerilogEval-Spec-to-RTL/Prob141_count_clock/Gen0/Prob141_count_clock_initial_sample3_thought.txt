To implement the 12-hour clock module, we'll design three separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be clocked by the fast-running clk and will only increment when ena is high (i.e., once per second). The counters will be reset to their initial values when the reset signal is high. We'll also use a separate signal to keep track of whether the clock is in AM or PM. The hours counter will be designed to wrap around from 12 to 1, and the minutes and seconds counters will wrap around from 59 to 0. We'll use the BCD representation for hours, minutes, and seconds.
