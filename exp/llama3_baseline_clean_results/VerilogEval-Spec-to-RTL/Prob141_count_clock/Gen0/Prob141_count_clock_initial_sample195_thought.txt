To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will create separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be implemented using a combination of a binary counter and a BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) encoder. The seconds counter will be incremented every second (when ena is high), and when it reaches 59, it will reset and increment the minutes counter. The minutes counter will work similarly, and when it reaches 59, it will reset and increment the hours counter. The hours counter will be a 12-hour counter, wrapping around from 12 to 1 when it reaches 12. The pm signal will be asserted when the hours are 12 or greater. The reset signal will reset all counters to their initial state.
