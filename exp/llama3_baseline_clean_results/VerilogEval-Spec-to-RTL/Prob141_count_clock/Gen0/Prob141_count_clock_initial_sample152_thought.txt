The design idea is to create a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, along with a PM/AM indicator. The counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk signal, and they will increment once per second when the ena signal is asserted. To achieve this, we will create separate counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds counter will increment once per second and reset to 0 when it reaches 60. The minutes counter will increment once every 60 seconds and reset to 0 when it reaches 60. The hours counter will increment once every 3600 seconds (or 60 minutes) and reset to 0 when it reaches 12, wrapping around to 1 after 12. The PM/AM indicator will be set based on the current hour value. We will use a reset signal to reset all counters to their initial values.
