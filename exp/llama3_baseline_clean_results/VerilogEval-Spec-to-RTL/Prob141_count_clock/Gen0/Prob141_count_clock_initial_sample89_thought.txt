To create a 12-hour clock with a set of counters, we will use three separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to count in BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) format. The seconds counter will increment once per second when the 'ena' signal is high. When the seconds counter reaches 59, it will reset to 0 and increment the minutes counter. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 59, it will reset to 0 and increment the hours counter. The hours counter will reset to 1 (not 0, to maintain a 12-hour clock format) and toggle the AM/PM indicator when it reaches 12. The reset signal will asynchronously reset all counters to their initial state.
