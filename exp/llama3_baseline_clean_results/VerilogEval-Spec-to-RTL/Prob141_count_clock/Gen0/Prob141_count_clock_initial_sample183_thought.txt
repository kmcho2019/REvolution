To implement a 12-hour clock, we need three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter should be capable of counting in BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) format. The seconds counter should increment once per second and wrap around to 0 after reaching 59. The minutes counter should increment once every 60 seconds and wrap around to 0 after reaching 59. The hours counter should increment once every 3600 seconds (or 60 minutes) and wrap around to 1 after reaching 12, toggling the AM/PM indicator. The reset signal should synchronously reset all counters to their initial state (12:00 AM) when high.
