To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will design a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, and control the increment of these counters using the ena signal. The counters will be reset to their initial state ("12:00 AM") when the reset signal is asserted. We will also implement logic to determine whether the clock is in AM or PM mode based on the current hour. The counters will be incremented once per second, as indicated by the ena signal, and all sequential logic will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
