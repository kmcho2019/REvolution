To implement the TopModule, we will create separate counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter will be clocked by the fast-running clk and will increment whenever ena is high. The counters will be reset to their initial values whenever reset is high. We will also use the counters to generate the pm signal. The counters will wrap around to their initial values when they reach their maximum values (59 for seconds and minutes, and 12 for hours in 12-hour format). 
We will use Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) to represent the time, where each digit of the time is represented by 4 bits.
