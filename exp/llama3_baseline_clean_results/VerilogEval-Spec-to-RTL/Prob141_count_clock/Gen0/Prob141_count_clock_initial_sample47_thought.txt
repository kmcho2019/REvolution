To implement the 12-hour clock, we'll use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to handle BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) values. The seconds counter will increment once per second (when ena is high) and will reset to 0 when it reaches 60. The minutes counter will increment when the seconds counter resets and will also reset to 0 when it reaches 60. The hours counter will increment when the minutes counter resets and will wrap around to 1 when it reaches 13.
