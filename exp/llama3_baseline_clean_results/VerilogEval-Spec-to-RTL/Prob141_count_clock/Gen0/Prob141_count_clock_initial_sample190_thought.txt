To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will create a hierarchical design with separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. We will use a state machine-like approach to handle the counting and wrapping of the counters at the end of each range (60 for minutes and seconds, 12 for hours with AM/PM toggle). The counters will be driven by the positive edge of the clk signal when ena is high and reset is low. The reset signal has higher priority and will reset the counters to the initial state (12:00 AM) whenever it is high.
