To implement a 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator, we'll need three counters: one for hours (hh), one for minutes (mm), and one for seconds (ss). The counters will be implemented using BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) to simplify the display logic. The counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk, and will increment once per second based on the ena signal. The pm signal will be asserted if the clock is PM. When the reset signal is asserted, the clock will reset to "12:00 AM". We'll use Verilog's always block to implement the sequential logic, and the if-else statement to handle the reset and enable signals.
