To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to handle BCD digits and will be incremented based on the enable signal. The pm signal will be asserted when the hour is between 12 and 23 (in 24-hour format). The clock will be reset to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is asserted. We will use if-else statements to handle the increment and reset logic for each counter.
