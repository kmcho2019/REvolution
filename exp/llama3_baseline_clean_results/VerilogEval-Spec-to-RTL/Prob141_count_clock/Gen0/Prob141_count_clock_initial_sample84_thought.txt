To implement a 12-hour clock with counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, we will use three separate counters: one for hours, one for minutes, and one for seconds. Each counter will be designed to handle Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) digits, ensuring that the hours range from 01 to 12, and minutes and seconds range from 00 to 59. The counters will be clocked by a fast-running clk signal and will increment only when the ena signal is high, indicating a one-second pulse. The pm signal will be asserted when the clock is in PM mode. Upon receiving a reset signal, all counters will reset to their initial values (12:00 AM). This will be achieved through a combination of sequential logic and conditional statements within the Verilog module.
