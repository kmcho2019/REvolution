The TopModule will utilize counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be reset to its initial state when the reset signal is asserted. On the positive edge of the clock and when the enable signal is high, the counters will increment by one. For the seconds counter, when it reaches 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes counter. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 59, it will wrap around to 0 and increment the hours counter. The hours counter will range from 1 to 12, wrapping around to 1 when it reaches 12, and also toggle the pm signal to indicate AM or PM. 
We will use 8-bit outputs for hours, minutes, and seconds to display BCD digits. 
The reset signal has higher priority and can reset the counters even when the enable signal is low.
