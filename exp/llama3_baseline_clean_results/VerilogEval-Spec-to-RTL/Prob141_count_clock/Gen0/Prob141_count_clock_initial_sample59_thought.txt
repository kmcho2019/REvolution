To implement the 12-hour clock module, we'll design three BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. We'll use a fast-running clock (clk) and enable signal (ena) to increment the counters once per second. We'll also add a PM indicator (pm) based on the hour value. The counters will be reset to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is high. We'll use sequential logic with positive edge-triggered flip-flops to implement the counters and the PM indicator.
