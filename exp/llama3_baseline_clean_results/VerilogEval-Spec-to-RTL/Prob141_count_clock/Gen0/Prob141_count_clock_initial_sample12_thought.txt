The 12-hour clock module TopModule will be implemented using three BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be reset to a specific value when the reset signal is high. When the enable signal is high, the seconds counter will increment on every clock cycle, carrying over to the minutes counter when it reaches 60, and then to the hours counter when the minutes counter reaches 60. The pm signal will be high when the hours counter is greater than 11 (12 is PM). The reset signal will have higher priority than the enable signal, allowing it to reset the clock even when the enable signal is not high.
