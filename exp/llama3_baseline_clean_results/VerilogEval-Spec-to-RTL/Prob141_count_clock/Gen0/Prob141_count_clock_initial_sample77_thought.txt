To implement a 12-hour clock with counters, we need to create a hierarchical counter structure that updates hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters should be resettable through the reset signal and can be incremented by the enable signal. 
The seconds counter should increment every time ena is high and should wrap around from 59 to 0, also triggering the minutes counter to increment. 
The minutes counter should increment every 60 seconds and wrap around from 59 to 0, also triggering the hours counter to increment. 
The hours counter should increment every 60 minutes and wrap around from 12 to 1 when pm is low and from 12 to 1 when pm is high, but with an additional condition to flip the pm signal when it wraps around from 12 to 1.
