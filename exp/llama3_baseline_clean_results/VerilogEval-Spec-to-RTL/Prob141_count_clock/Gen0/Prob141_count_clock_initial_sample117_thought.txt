To implement a 12-hour clock with a seconds, minutes, and hours counter, 
we need to create a module that increments the counters once per second. 
This can be achieved by checking the 'ena' signal which pulses once per 
second. The counters should be reset to 12:00 AM when the 'reset' signal 
is asserted. We will use a binary-coded decimal (BCD) counter to keep 
track of seconds, minutes, and hours. To ensure that the counters wrap 
around correctly (i.e., 59 seconds to 00 seconds, 59 minutes to 00 
minutes, and 12 hours to 01 hours), we will implement conditional 
statements to check for these conditions and reset the counters accordingly.
