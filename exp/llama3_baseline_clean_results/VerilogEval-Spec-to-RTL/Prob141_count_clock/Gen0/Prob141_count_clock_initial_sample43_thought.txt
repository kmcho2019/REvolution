To implement the 12-hour clock, we will use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be incremented based on the enable signal. We will also use the reset signal to reset all counters to their initial values. The pm signal will be asserted when the hours counter is greater than or equal to 12. We will use Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) to represent the hours, minutes, and seconds.

Here is the step-by-step process:

1. Initialize all counters to 0 when reset is high.
2. Increment the seconds counter when the enable signal is high.
3. When the seconds counter reaches 60 (which is 01111000 in BCD), reset it to 0 and increment the minutes counter.
4. When the minutes counter reaches 60, reset it to 0 and increment the hours counter.
5. When the hours counter reaches 12 (which is 00010010 in BCD) or more, set the pm signal to 1, and when it reaches 13 (which is 00010011 in BCD) or more, reset it to 1 (which is 00000001 in BCD).

This approach will allow us to implement a 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator.
