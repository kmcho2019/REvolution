To design the TopModule, we can utilize counters to keep track of hours, minutes, and seconds. 
We'll need to implement counters that can count up to 12 for hours and 59 for minutes and seconds, 
and wrap around when they reach their maximum values. 
The enable signal (ena) will be used to determine when to increment the counters, 
and the reset signal will be used to reset the counters to their initial values.
We'll also need to generate the pm signal based on the hour value.
