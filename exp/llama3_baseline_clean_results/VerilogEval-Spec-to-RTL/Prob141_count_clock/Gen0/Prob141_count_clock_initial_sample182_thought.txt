To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will design a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. 
Each counter will be clocked by the fast-running 'clk' and will increment only when 'ena' is high. 
We will use synchronous reset to reset the counters to their initial values when 'reset' is high. 
The 'pm' signal will be asserted if the hour is greater than or equal to 12. 
The counters for hours, minutes, and seconds will wrap around to their initial values after reaching their maximum values (12 for hours, 59 for minutes and seconds). 
We will also convert the binary values of the counters to BCD for output.
