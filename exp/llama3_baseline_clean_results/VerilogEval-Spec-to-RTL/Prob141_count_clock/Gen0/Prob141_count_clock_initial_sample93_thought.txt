To implement the TopModule, we need to create a set of counters that can handle hours, minutes, and seconds. Since the clock is a 12-hour clock, we need to keep track of AM/PM. We will use a counter for seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds counter will increment every time ena is asserted, and when it reaches 60, it will reset and increment the minutes counter. The minutes counter will also reset and increment the hours counter when it reaches 60. The hours counter will wrap around from 12 to 1 when it reaches 12. We will use the most significant bit of the hours counter to determine AM/PM. The counters should be reset to their initial state when the reset signal is asserted.
