To implement the 12-hour clock with counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, 
we will create separate counters for each time component. These counters 
will be clocked by the fast-running 'clk' and will increment based on the 
'ena' signal, which represents a one-second pulse. 

The counters will be designed as follows:
- The seconds counter will increment on every 'ena' pulse, wrapping around 
  to 0 after reaching 59.
- The minutes counter will increment every 60 seconds (i.e., when the seconds 
  counter wraps around to 0), also wrapping around to 0 after reaching 59.
- The hours counter will increment every 3600 seconds (i.e., when both minutes 
  and seconds counters wrap around to 0), wrapping around to 1 after reaching 12.

To handle AM/PM, we will use the hours counter value. If the hours value is 
greater than or equal to 12, we assert 'pm', otherwise, we assert 'am'. 

We also need to handle the reset condition. On the positive edge of 'clk', if 
'reset' is high, we reset all counters to their initial values, which corresponds 
to "12:00 AM".
