To implement the 12-hour clock, I will create a TopModule that incorporates three BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters will be incremented once per second, as indicated by the 'ena' signal, and will reset to "12:00 AM" when the 'reset' signal is high. I will use always blocks to handle the sequential logic, triggered on the positive edge of the 'clk' signal. The 'pm' signal will be determined based on the hour value. I will also ensure that the hours, minutes, and seconds are wrapped around to their respective maximum values when they overflow.
