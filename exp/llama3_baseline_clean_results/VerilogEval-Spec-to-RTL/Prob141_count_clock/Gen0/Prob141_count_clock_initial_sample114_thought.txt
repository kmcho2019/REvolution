To implement the 12-hour clock module, we'll create separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be 8 bits wide to represent BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) digits. We'll use a combination of conditional statements and counters to manage the clock's time-keeping and PM/AM indicator. The reset signal will have higher priority, resetting the clock to "12:00 AM" whenever it's asserted. The enable signal will increment the clock once per second, while the clock signal (clk) will be used to trigger the sequential logic.
