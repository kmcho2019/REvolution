To implement the 12-hour clock with am/pm indicator, we need to create counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. We will use a hierarchical approach to design the counters. Each counter will be designed as a separate module, and then these modules will be instantiated in the TopModule. We will use Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) to represent the hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters will be clocked by a fast-running clk and will increment once per second when ena is asserted. The pm signal will be asserted if the clock is PM and deasserted if it is AM. The reset signal will reset the clock to "12:00 AM" when asserted.
