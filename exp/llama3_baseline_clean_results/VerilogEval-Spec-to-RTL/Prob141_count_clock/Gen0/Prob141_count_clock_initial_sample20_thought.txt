To implement the 12-hour clock module, we'll use a combination of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, along with a flag to indicate AM/PM. The counters should be clocked by the fast-running 'clk' and only increment when 'ena' is high, indicating it's time to move to the next second. The 'reset' signal, being active high and synchronous, should reset the counters and AM/PM flag whenever it's asserted, regardless of the state of 'ena'. The hours counter needs to handle the 12-hour cycle, wrapping around from 12 back to 1 after reaching 12 PM or AM, depending on the current AM/PM state.
