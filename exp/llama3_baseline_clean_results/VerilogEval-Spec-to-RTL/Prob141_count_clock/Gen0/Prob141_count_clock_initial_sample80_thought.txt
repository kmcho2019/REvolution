To implement the counters, we will use a sequence of conditional statements triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal, clk. When reset is asserted, all counters and the pm signal are reset to their initial values. 
When ena is asserted, the counters are incremented as follows: 
1. The seconds counter ss is incremented. If it reaches 60 (3C in hexadecimal), it is reset to 00 and the minutes counter mm is incremented. 
2. If the minutes counter mm reaches 60 (3C in hexadecimal), it is reset to 00 and the hours counter hh is incremented. 
3. If the hours counter hh reaches 13 (0D in hexadecimal), it is reset to 01 and the pm signal is toggled.
