The TopModule will utilize counters to track hours, minutes, and seconds. 
These counters will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 
and will increment only when the enable signal is high. The hours counter 
will handle the 12-hour AM/PM logic, while the minutes and seconds counters 
will reset at 60. The reset signal will be used to reset the clock to 12:00 AM 
when it is high. To implement this, we will use the following steps:
- Initialize the counters for hours, minutes, and seconds.
- On every positive edge of the clock, check if the reset signal is high.
  If it is, reset the counters to 12:00 AM.
- If the reset signal is low and the enable signal is high, increment the 
  seconds counter. If the seconds counter overflows (i.e., reaches 60), 
  reset it to 0 and increment the minutes counter. If the minutes counter 
  overflows, reset it to 0 and increment the hours counter. 
  Handle the 12-hour AM/PM logic in the hours counter.
