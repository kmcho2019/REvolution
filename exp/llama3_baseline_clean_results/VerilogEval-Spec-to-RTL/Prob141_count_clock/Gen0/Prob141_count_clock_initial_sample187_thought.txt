To implement the 12-hour clock, we will use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to handle BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) values. The seconds counter will increment every time 'ena' is asserted, and when it reaches 59, it will reset to 0 and the minutes counter will increment. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 59, it will reset to 0 and the hours counter will increment. The hours counter will increment from 1 to 12 and then wrap around to 1. The 'pm' signal will be asserted when the hours value is greater than or equal to 12, and de-asserted when the hours value is less than 12. However, to maintain the 12-hour clock format, we will adjust the display to show 12 when the internal hours value is 0. The reset signal will reset all counters to their initial values when asserted. The design will be implemented using always blocks to define the combinational and sequential logic.
