We will use a 12-hour clock format with am/pm indicator. We will use BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) encoding for the hours, minutes, and seconds. The pm signal will be asserted when the hours are in the range 01-12 in the PM (01-12 represents 13-00 in 24-hour format). When the reset signal is asserted, the clock will reset to "12:00 AM".
