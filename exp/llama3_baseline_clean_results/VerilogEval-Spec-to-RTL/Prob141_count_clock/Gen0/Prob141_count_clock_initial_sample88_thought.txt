To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use a combination of counters and logic to manage the hours, minutes, and seconds. We'll design separate counters for each time component (hours, minutes, seconds) and a state machine to manage the AM/PM indicator. All counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk and will only increment when the ena signal is high. The reset signal will have higher priority and will reset the clock to 12:00 AM whenever it is asserted. Since the counters are BCD, they will need to be designed to roll over correctly at 59 for minutes and seconds, and at 12 for hours, with the added condition of handling AM/PM transition at noon and midnight.
