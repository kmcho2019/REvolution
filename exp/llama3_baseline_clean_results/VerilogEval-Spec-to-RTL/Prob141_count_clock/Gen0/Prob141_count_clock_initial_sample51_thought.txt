To create a 12-hour clock module, we'll need to implement counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. We will use Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) to represent each of these counters with two digits (8 bits). The counters will be clocked by a fast-running clk and will increment when the enable signal is asserted (once per second). The pm signal will be asserted when the clock is in PM. The clock will reset to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is asserted. We'll use conditional statements to manage the flow of the clock and to determine when to reset or increment the counters.
