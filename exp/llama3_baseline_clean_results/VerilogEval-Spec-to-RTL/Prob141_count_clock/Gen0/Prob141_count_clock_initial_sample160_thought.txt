To implement the 12-hour clock, we can use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The seconds counter will increment every time ena is high, and when it reaches 59, it will reset and the minutes counter will increment. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 59, it will reset and the hours counter will increment. The hours counter will reset when it reaches 12 or 13 (depending on the pm indicator), and the pm indicator will toggle when the hours counter reaches 12. The reset signal will reset all counters to their initial state and set the pm indicator to low (AM). We will use binary-coded decimal (BCD) to represent the hours, minutes, and seconds.
