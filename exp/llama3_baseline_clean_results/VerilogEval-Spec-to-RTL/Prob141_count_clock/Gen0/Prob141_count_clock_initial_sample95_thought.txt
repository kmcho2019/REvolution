The module TopModule will contain three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each implemented as BCD counters. A 12-hour clock requires that hours wrap around from 12 back to 1, while minutes and seconds reset from 59 back to 0. The counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk and will only increment when ena is high. The reset signal will asynchronously reset the counters to their initial values when high. The pm output will be determined based on the hour value.
