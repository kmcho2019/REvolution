To implement the 12-hour clock, we'll use three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with a resolution of 8 bits to accommodate two BCD digits. We'll utilize a synchronous reset to initialize the clock to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is asserted. The enable signal (ena) will be used to increment the seconds counter once per second, and the seconds counter will be used to increment the minutes and hours counters accordingly. The am/pm indicator will be determined based on the hour value.
