To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we will use separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be 8 bits to represent two BCD digits. The counters will be clocked by the fast-running clk, and the enable signal ena will determine when to increment the counters. When the seconds counter overflows, we will increment the minutes counter. When the minutes counter overflows, we will increment the hours counter, wrapping around to 1 if the hours counter overflows. We will also use a flag to track whether the clock is AM or PM, and toggle this flag when the hours counter overflows from 12 to 1.
