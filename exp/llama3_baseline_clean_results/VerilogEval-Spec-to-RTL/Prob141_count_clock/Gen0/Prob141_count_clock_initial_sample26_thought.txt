To implement the 12-hour clock, we need to design a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Since the clock is clocked by a fast-running clk and only increments once per second when ena is asserted, we can use a counter to keep track of the seconds. When the seconds counter reaches 59, it should wrap around to 0 and increment the minutes counter. Similarly, when the minutes counter reaches 59, it should wrap around to 0 and increment the hours counter. The hours counter should wrap around to 1 after reaching 12. We also need to keep track of the AM/PM indicator, which should toggle when the hours counter wraps around from 12 to 1. All counters should reset to their initial values when the reset signal is asserted.
