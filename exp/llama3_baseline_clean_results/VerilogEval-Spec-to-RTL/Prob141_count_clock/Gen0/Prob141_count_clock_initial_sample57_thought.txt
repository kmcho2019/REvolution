To create the TopModule, we'll use separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be designed to handle Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) values. The hours counter will range from 1 to 12, and minutes and seconds counters will range from 0 to 59. The 'pm' signal will be determined based on the hour value. We will use if-else statements to control the increment of the counters based on the enable signal and to reset the counters when the reset signal is high. We will also implement the logic to handle the roll-over of seconds to minutes, minutes to hours, and hours to 12 or 1.
