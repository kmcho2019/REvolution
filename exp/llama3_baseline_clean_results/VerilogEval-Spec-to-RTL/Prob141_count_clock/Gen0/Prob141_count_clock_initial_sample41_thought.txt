To implement the 12-hour clock, we need to create counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. 
The counters should be clocked by the fast-running 'clk' and should increment when 'ena' is high. 
Since the counters are BCD, we need to handle the carry from one digit to the next. 
The 'reset' signal should reset all counters to their initial state, which is "12:00 AM" or hh = 12, mm = 00, ss = 00, and pm = 0. 
We also need to handle the wrapping of seconds and minutes from 59 to 00 and the wrapping of hours from 12 to 01 when it reaches 13 and from 12 to 12 when it wraps around to the next day.
