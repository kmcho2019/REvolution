To implement the 12-hour clock, we will create three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with the appropriate range and BCD encoding. The counters will be incremented on the positive edge of the clk signal when the ena signal is high. We will also include a pm indicator that is asserted when the hours are greater than 11. The reset signal will be checked at every positive edge of the clk signal, and if high, the clock will reset to "12:00 AM". We will use a state machine to manage the counters and the pm indicator.
