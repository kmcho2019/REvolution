The TopModule will consist of three counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, 
each with a BCD representation. The counters will be incremented when the ena 
signal is high, indicating a one-second interval has passed. The pm signal 
will be determined based on the hour counter value. All counters will reset 
when the reset signal is high, regardless of the ena signal. The counters will 
be clocked by the fast-running clk signal, with the logic triggered on the 
positive edge of the clock.
