To implement the 12-hour clock module, we will create a set of counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter will be designed to handle Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) values. The seconds counter will increment from 0 to 59, the minutes counter will increment from 0 to 59, and the hours counter will increment from 1 to 12. The 'pm' output will be asserted when the hours counter is in the range of 12 to 11 (PM). We will use the 'ena' signal to increment the counters once per second and the 'reset' signal to reset the clock to "12:00 AM" when asserted.
