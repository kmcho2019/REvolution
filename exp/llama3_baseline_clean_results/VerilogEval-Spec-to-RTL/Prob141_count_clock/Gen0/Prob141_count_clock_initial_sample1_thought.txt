To implement the 12-hour clock, we'll design a hierarchical structure of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each counter will be responsible for its respective time unit. We'll use a combination of logic gates and conditional statements to handle the counting process, including wrapping around from 59 to 00 for minutes and seconds, and from 12 to 01 for hours when transitioning from AM to PM. The enable signal (ena) will be used to increment the counters once per second, while the reset signal will reset the entire clock to 12:00 AM when asserted. The pm output will be determined by the value of the hours counter.
