To design the TopModule, we can create a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. These counters should be BCD counters, incrementing from 00 to 59 for minutes and seconds, and from 01 to 12 for hours. We can use the enable signal (ena) to increment the counters once per second. We'll also use a pm indicator flag to toggle between AM and PM. 
When the reset signal is asserted, we should reset all counters to their initial values. 
We can use a finite state machine or a simple counter with conditional statements to manage the hour counter and the pm indicator.
