To implement the TopModule, we will first define the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, OR) and connect their inputs to the input x and the outputs of the D flip-flops. We will then connect the outputs of these gates to the inputs of the D flip-flops. The outputs of the D flip-flops will be connected to a three-input NOR gate to generate the output z. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the D flip-flop outputs on the positive edge of the clock.
