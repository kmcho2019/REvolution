To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine with three D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will have its output connected to the corresponding gate (XOR, AND, OR) along with the input x. The outputs of the gates will be used as the inputs to the D flip-flops. The outputs of the flip-flops will then be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the final output z.

The three D flip-flops will have the following logic:
- The XOR flip-flop will have its output XORed with x and the result will be its new input.
- The AND flip-flop will have its complemented output ANDed with x and the result will be its new input.
- The OR flip-flop will have its complemented output ORed with x and the result will be its new input.

All of the flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal.
