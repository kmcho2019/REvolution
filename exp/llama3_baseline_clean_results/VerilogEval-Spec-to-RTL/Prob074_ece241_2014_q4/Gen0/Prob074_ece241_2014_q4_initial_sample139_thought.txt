The TopModule will be implemented as a finite state machine with three D flip-flops. 
Each flip-flop will be connected to a different two-input gate: XOR, AND, and OR. 
The second input of each gate will be connected to the output of the corresponding flip-flop, 
with the second input of the AND and OR gates being the complement of the flip-flop output. 
The outputs of the three gates will be connected to the inputs of the corresponding flip-flops. 
Finally, the outputs of the three flip-flops will be connected to a three-input NOR gate, 
whose output will be the output of the module, z.
