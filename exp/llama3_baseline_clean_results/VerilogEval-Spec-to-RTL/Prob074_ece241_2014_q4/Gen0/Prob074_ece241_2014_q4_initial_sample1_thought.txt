To implement the TopModule, we first need to define the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, OR) and their connections to the D flip-flops. Then, we need to connect the outputs of the D flip-flops to the three-input NOR gate. Since the second input of each gate is the output or complemented output of its corresponding flip-flop, we need to feedback the flip-flop outputs to the gates. The D flip-flops should be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. We also need to ensure that the flip-flops are initially reset to zero.
