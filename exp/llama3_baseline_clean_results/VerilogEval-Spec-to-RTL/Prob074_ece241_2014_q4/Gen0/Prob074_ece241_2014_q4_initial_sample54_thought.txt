To implement the TopModule, we need to follow these steps:
1. Define the module with its input and output ports.
2. Create three internal signals for the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates.
3. Connect the inputs of the D flip-flops to the outputs of these gates and the clock input to their clock port.
4. Connect the outputs of the flip-flops to the second inputs of the corresponding gates.
5. Connect the outputs of the flip-flops to a NOR gate to generate the final output z.

We will use the initial block to reset the flip-flops to zero. We'll also use the always block to model the combinational logic of the gates and the sequential logic of the flip-flops.
