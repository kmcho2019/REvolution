To implement the TopModule, we need to define a finite state machine with three D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will be connected to an XOR, AND, and OR gate, respectively. The second input of each gate will be connected to the corresponding flip-flop's output or its complement. The outputs of the flip-flops will then be connected to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be the output of the module, z. We will use the positive edge of the clock signal to trigger the flip-flops.
