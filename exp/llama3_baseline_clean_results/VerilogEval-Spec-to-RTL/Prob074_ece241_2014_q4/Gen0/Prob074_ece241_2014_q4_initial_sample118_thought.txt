The XOR gate will take the input x and its corresponding flip-flop output. The AND gate will take the input x and the complement of its corresponding flip-flop output. The OR gate will take the input x and the complement of its corresponding flip-flop output. The outputs of the gates will be connected to the D input of the flip-flops. Finally, the outputs of the flip-flops will be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output z.
