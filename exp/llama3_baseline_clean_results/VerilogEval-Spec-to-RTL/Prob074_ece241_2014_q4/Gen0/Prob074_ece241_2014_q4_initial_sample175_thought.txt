To implement the TopModule, we will first define the D flip-flops and the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, and OR). Each gate will have one input connected to the input x and the other input connected to the output of its corresponding flip-flop or the complement of the output. The outputs of the three gates will be connected to the inputs of the D flip-flops. Finally, the outputs of the D flip-flops will be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output z. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to assign values to the outputs of the flip-flops on the positive edge of the clock.
