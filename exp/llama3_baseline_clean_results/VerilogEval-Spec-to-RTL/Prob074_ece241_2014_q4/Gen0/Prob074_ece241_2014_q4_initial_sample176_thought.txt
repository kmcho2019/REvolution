To implement the TopModule, we will create a finite state machine with three D flip-flops, each connected to a different two-input gate (XOR, AND, OR). The outputs of the flip-flops will be fed back into the gates, and the outputs of the gates will be used as inputs to the flip-flops. The outputs of the flip-flops will also be connected to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be Z. We will use Verilog's reg type to declare the outputs of the flip-flops and use the always block to define the behavior of the flip-flops and the gates.
