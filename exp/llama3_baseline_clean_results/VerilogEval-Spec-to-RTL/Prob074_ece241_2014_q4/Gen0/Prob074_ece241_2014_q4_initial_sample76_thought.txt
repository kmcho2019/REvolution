To implement the TopModule, we need to define the three gates (XOR, AND, OR) with their respective second inputs coming from the outputs of the D flip-flops. The outputs of these gates will be the inputs to the D flip-flops. The outputs of the D flip-flops will also be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output Z. We will use the `always` block to describe the sequential logic of the D flip-flops and the combinational logic of the gates. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
