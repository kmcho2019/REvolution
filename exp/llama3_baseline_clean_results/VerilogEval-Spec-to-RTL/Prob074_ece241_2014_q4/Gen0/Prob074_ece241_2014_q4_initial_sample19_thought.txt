To implement the TopModule as described, we will first define the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, OR) with their respective inputs and outputs. Then, we will define the three D flip-flops, where each flip-flop's input is connected to the output of its corresponding gate, and its clock input is connected to the clk input of the module. The outputs of the flip-flops will be connected to the inputs of the three-input NOR gate, whose output will be the z output of the module. We will use non-blocking assignments to update the flip-flops on the positive edge of the clock.
