To implement the TopModule, we will first declare the necessary wires and internal signals to connect the input of the D flip-flops to the output of the three gates (XOR, AND, and OR). Then we will use these internal signals to drive the D inputs of the flip-flops. After that, we will declare the output of each flip-flop and connect them to the inputs of the NOR gate to produce the final output Z. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) for the sequential logic of the D flip-flops to avoid race conditions.
