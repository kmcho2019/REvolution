The TopModule will have three D flip-flops, one for each gate (XOR, AND, OR). The second input of each gate will be connected to the output of its corresponding flip-flop. For the AND and OR gates, the output of the flip-flop will be inverted before being used as the second input. The outputs of the three gates will then be connected to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be Z. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal clk.
