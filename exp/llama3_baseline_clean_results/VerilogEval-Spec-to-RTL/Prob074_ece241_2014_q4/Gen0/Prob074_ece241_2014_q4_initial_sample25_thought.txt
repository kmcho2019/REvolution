To implement the TopModule, we first define the module with the given interface. Then, we declare the outputs of the three D flip-flops. We calculate the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates, considering the second input of each gate is the output of its corresponding flip-flop or the complement of the output. We then assign these outputs to the D inputs of the flip-flops. Finally, we use a NOR gate to calculate the output z from the outputs of the three flip-flops.
