To implement the TopModule, we will first define the internal signals for the outputs of the D flip-flops. We will then define the inputs to the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, and OR) based on the problem description. The outputs of these gates will be used as inputs to the D flip-flops. On the positive edge of the clock, the outputs of the gates will be stored in the D flip-flops. The outputs of the D flip-flops will be used as inputs to the three-input NOR gate to produce the output Z.
