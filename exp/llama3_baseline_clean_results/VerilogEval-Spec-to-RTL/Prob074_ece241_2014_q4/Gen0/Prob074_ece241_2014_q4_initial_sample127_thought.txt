To implement the TopModule, we will first define the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, and OR) and connect them to the input of the D flip-flops. The second input of each gate will be the output of its corresponding flip-flop, with the OR and AND gates using the complemented output. Then, we will connect the outputs of the flip-flops to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be Z. We will use the always block in Verilog to describe the sequential behavior of the D flip-flops.
