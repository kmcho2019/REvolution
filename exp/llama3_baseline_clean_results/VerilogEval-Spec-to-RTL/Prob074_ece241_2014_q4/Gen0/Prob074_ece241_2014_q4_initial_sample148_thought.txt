To implement the TopModule, we first need to define the internal signals that connect the gates and the flip-flops. Let's denote the outputs of the three D flip-flops as q_xor, q_and, and q_or, respectively. Then, we can express the inputs to the XOR, AND, and OR gates as follows:
- The XOR gate's input is x and q_xor.
- The AND gate's input is x and the complement of q_and.
- The OR gate's input is x and the complement of q_or.

We can then use these expressions to define the inputs to the D flip-flops. The outputs of the flip-flops are then connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output z. The flip-flops are triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal clk.
