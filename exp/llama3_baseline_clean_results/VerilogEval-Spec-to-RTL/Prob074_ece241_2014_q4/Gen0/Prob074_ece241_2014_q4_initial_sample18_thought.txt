To implement the TopModule, we need to follow these steps:

1. Declare the inputs and outputs of the module.
2. Define the internal signals for the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates.
3. Define the internal signals for the outputs of the D flip-flops.
4. Implement the XOR, AND, and OR gates with their respective inputs.
5. Implement the D flip-flops with their respective inputs and clock.
6. Implement the NOR gate with the outputs of the D flip-flops.
7. Assign the output of the NOR gate to the output z.

We can use the following logic to implement the module:
- The XOR gate will take input x and the output of its corresponding flip-flop (q_x) as inputs.
- The AND gate will take input x and the complement of the output of its corresponding flip-flop (q_a) as inputs.
- The OR gate will take input x and the complement of the output of its corresponding flip-flop (q_o) as inputs.
- The D flip-flops will take the outputs of the gates as their inputs and will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
- The NOR gate will take the outputs of the D flip-flops as its inputs and will produce the output z.
