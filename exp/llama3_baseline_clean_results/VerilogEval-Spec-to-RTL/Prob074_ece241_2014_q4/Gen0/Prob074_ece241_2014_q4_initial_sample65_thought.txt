To implement the TopModule, we will first declare the input and output ports as specified. Then, we will define the internal signals for the outputs of the D flip-flops and the inputs to the gates. We will use the posedge of the clock to update the D flip-flop outputs. The XOR, AND, and OR gates will be connected to the input x and their respective D flip-flop outputs or complements. Finally, the outputs of the gates will be connected to the NOR gate to produce the output z.
