The TopModule will have three D flip-flops, each connected to the output of a different two-input gate. 
The gates will be an XOR gate, an AND gate, and an OR gate. 
Each gate will have input x and the corresponding flip-flop's output or its complement. 
The outputs of the flip-flops will be connected to a NOR gate, which will produce output z. 
The flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal clk.
