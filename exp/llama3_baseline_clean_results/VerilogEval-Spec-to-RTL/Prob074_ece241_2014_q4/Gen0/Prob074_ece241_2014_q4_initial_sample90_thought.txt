To implement the TopModule, we will first define the D flip-flops and their corresponding gate outputs. The XOR gate's output will be connected to the D input of the first flip-flop, the AND gate's output will be connected to the D input of the second flip-flop, and the OR gate's output will be connected to the D input of the third flip-flop. The outputs of the flip-flops will then be connected to the second inputs of their respective gates and to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be the module's output z. We will use the posedge of the clk to trigger the flip-flops.
