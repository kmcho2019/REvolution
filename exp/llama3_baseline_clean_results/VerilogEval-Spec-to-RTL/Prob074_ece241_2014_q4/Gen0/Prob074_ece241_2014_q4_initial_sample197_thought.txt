To implement the TopModule, we will first define the D flip-flops for the XOR, AND, and OR gates. Then we will define the XOR, AND, and OR gates with their respective second inputs coming from the corresponding D flip-flop outputs. The outputs of the gates will then be connected to the D inputs of the D flip-flops. Finally, we will use a three-input NOR gate to generate the output Z from the outputs of the D flip-flops.
