To implement the TopModule, we will first define the three gates (XOR, AND, and OR) with their respective inputs. Then, we will connect the output of each gate to the input of a D flip-flop. The second input of each gate will be connected to the output of its corresponding flip-flop, with the AND and OR gates having their second inputs inverted. The output of each flip-flop will be connected to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be Z. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
