To implement the TopModule, we will first define the D flip-flops and their corresponding gate inputs. 
The XOR gate's second input will be its corresponding flip-flop's output (let's call it q_xor). 
The AND gate's second input will be its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output (q_and). 
The OR gate's second input will be its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output (q_or). 
Then we'll use the outputs of the three gates as inputs to a NOR gate to get the output z. 
We will use non-blocking assignments to update the outputs of the D flip-flops at the positive edge of the clock.
