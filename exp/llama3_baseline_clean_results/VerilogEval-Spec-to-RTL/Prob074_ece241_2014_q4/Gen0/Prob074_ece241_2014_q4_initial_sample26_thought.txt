To implement the TopModule, we can start by defining the three two-input gates: XOR, AND, and OR. We'll then connect each gate's output to the input of a D flip-flop. The D flip-flop's output will be connected to the second input of its corresponding gate and also to a three-input NOR gate. The output of the NOR gate will be the module's output, Z. We'll use the positive edge of the clock signal to trigger the D flip-flops.
