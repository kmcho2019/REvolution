To implement the TopModule, we can follow these steps:
1. Define the inputs and outputs of the module.
2. Declare the necessary internal signals, including the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates, the outputs of the D flip-flops, and the intermediate signals for the complemented outputs of the flip-flops.
3. Write the equations for the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates.
4. Define the behavior of the D flip-flops, which are triggered on the positive edge of the clock and have their inputs connected to the outputs of the gates.
5. Write the equation for the output Z, which is the output of the NOR gate.
