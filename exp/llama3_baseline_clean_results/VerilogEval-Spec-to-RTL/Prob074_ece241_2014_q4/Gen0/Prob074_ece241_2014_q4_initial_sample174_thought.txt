The outputs of the D flip-flops will also be connected to a three-input NOR gate, whose output will be the output of the module, Z.

We will use the posedge of the clock signal to trigger the sequential logic in the D flip-flops. We will also use the initial block to reset the D flip-flops to zero before the machine begins.

The logic for the gates can be implemented using the corresponding operators in Verilog.
