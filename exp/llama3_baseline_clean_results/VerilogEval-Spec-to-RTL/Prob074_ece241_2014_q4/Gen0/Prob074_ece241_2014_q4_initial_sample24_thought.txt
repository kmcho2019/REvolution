To implement the TopModule, we first need to define the internal signals for the outputs of the D flip-flops. Then we define the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates as functions of the input x and the flip-flop outputs. We use these gate outputs as the inputs to the D flip-flops. On the positive edge of the clock, the D flip-flops capture the gate outputs and update their outputs accordingly. Finally, we use the outputs of the D flip-flops as inputs to the NOR gate to produce the output z.
