To implement the TopModule, we will first define the internal signals and their connections. We'll have three D flip-flops (ff_x, ff_and, ff_or) each connected to the corresponding gate (XOR, AND, OR) with input x and the flip-flop's output or its complement. The outputs of the flip-flops will then be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output z. 
The XOR gate's output will be x XOR the output of the corresponding flip-flop (ff_x). 
The AND gate's output will be x AND the complement of the output of the corresponding flip-flop (ff_and). 
The OR gate's output will be x OR the complement of the output of the corresponding flip-flop (ff_or).
We'll use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators to implement the gates and D flip-flops.
