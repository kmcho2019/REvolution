To implement the TopModule, we will first define the inputs and outputs, then create the logic for the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, and OR) and connect them to the inputs of the D flip-flops. The outputs of the D flip-flops will be used as the second inputs for the gates, and also as inputs to the three-input NOR gate. We will use the always_ff block to model the sequential behavior of the D flip-flops, which are triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
