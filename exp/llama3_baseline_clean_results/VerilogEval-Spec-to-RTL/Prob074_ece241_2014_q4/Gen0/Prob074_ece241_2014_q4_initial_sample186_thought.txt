To implement the TopModule, we will first define the three two-input gates (XOR, AND, and OR) and connect them to the input of the D flip-flops. The second input of each gate will be connected to the corresponding flip-flop's output or its complement. The outputs of the flip-flops will then be connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output Z. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for the gates and the D flip-flop.
