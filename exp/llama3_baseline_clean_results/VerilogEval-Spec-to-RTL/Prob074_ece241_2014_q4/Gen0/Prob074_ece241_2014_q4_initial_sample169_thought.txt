To implement the TopModule, we first define the module with the given interface. We then define the outputs of the D flip-flops and connect them to the corresponding gates. The second input of each gate is connected to its corresponding flip-flop's output or complemented output. The outputs of the gates are connected to the inputs of the D flip-flops. Finally, the outputs of the D flip-flops are connected to a three-input NOR gate to produce the output z. We use non-blocking assignments to update the outputs of the D flip-flops on the positive edge of the clock.
