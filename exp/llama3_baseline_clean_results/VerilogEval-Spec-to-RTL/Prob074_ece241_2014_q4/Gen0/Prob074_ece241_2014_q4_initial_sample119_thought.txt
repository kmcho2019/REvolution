To implement the TopModule, we need to follow these steps:
1. Define the module with the specified interface.
2. Declare the internal signals for the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates, as well as the outputs of the D flip-flops.
3. Implement the logic for the XOR, AND, and OR gates, considering their second inputs are connected to their corresponding D flip-flop outputs or complements.
4. Use D flip-flops to store the outputs of the gates and update them on the positive edge of the clock.
5. Implement the NOR gate that combines the outputs of the D flip-flops to produce the output Z.

We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=") to update the D flip-flop outputs, as they should be updated simultaneously on the positive edge of the clock.
