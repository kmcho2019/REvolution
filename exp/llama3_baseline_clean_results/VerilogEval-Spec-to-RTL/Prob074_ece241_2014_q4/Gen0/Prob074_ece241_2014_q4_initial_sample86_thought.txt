To implement the described finite state machine, we first need to define the module with the given interface. Then, we will create the internal signals for the outputs of the D flip-flops, and the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will use the outputs of the gates as their input. Finally, the output of the NOR gate will be assigned to the output z. The second inputs of the gates will be the outputs of the corresponding D flip-flops, with the AND and OR gates using the complemented output of their respective D flip-flops.
