The AND gate will be connected to the input x and its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output. 
The OR gate will be connected to the input x and its corresponding flip-flop's complemented output. 
The outputs of the three gates will be connected to the inputs of the three D flip-flops, 
and the outputs of the D flip-flops will be connected to a three-input NOR gate,
