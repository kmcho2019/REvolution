To implement the TopModule, we need to define the finite state machine behavior as described. 
We will start by defining the output of each gate in terms of the inputs and flip-flop outputs. 
Let's denote the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates as xor_out, and_out, and or_out respectively. 
Let's denote the outputs of the D flip-flops as xor_ff, and_ff, and or_ff respectively. 
The output of the NOR gate, which is z, will be the nor of xor_out, and_out, and or_out.

We can express xor_out as x XOR xor_ff, and_out as x AND ~and_ff, and or_out as x OR ~or_ff. 
The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and their inputs will be xor_out, and_out, and or_out respectively. 
Initially, all flip-flops are reset to zero.

In Verilog, we can implement this using always blocks for the combinational logic, and non-blocking assignments for the sequential logic.
