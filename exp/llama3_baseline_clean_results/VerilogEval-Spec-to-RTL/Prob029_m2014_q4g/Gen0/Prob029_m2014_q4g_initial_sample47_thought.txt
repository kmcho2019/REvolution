To implement the TopModule as described, we first recognize that the XNOR operation between 'in1' and 'in2' can be represented by the logical expression ~(in1 ^ in2). This result then becomes one of the inputs to the XOR operation with 'in3'. The XOR operation between two inputs can be represented by the ^ operator in Verilog. Therefore, the overall operation for 'out' can be represented as ~(in1 ^ in2) ^ in3. We will implement this directly in Verilog.
