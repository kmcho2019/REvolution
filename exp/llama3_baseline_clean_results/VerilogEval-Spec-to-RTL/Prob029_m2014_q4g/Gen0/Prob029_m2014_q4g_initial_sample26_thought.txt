To implement the TopModule as described, we first need to create a two-input XNOR gate connected to 'in1' and 'in2'. The output of this XNOR gate will then be connected to one of the inputs of a two-input XOR gate. The other input of the XOR gate will be connected to 'in3'. The output of this XOR gate will be 'out'.
