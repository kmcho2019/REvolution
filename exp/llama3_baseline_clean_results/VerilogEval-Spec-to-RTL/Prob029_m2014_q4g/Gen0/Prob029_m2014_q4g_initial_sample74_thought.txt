The TopModule will be implemented using basic logical operators. First, we will create a two-input XNOR gate using the 'in1' and 'in2' inputs. Then, we will connect the output of the XNOR gate to one of the inputs of a two-input XOR gate, while 'in3' will be connected to the other input of the XOR gate. Finally, the output of the XOR gate will be assigned to 'out'.
