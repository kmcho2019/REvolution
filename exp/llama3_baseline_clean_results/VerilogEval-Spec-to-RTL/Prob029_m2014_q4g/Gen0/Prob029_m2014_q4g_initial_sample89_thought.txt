The TopModule should have an XNOR gate that takes in1 and in2 as inputs, 
and its output should be connected to one of the inputs of an XOR gate. 
The other input of the XOR gate should be in3. The output of the XOR gate 
will be the module's output, out. 
This can be implemented using Verilog's built-in logical operators: 
~(a ^ b) for XNOR and ^ for XOR, where ~ is the bitwise NOT operator and 
^ is the bitwise XOR operator.
