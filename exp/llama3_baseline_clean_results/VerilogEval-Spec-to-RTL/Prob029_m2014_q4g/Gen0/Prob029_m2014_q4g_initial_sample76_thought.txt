To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the required circuit. The circuit consists of a two-input XNOR gate connected to 'in1' and 'in2', and its output is connected to a two-input XOR gate. The second input of the XOR gate is 'in3', and its output is 'out'. We can implement this circuit in Verilog by using the built-in logical operators for XNOR and XOR operations, which are ~= (XNOR) and ^ (XOR) respectively.
