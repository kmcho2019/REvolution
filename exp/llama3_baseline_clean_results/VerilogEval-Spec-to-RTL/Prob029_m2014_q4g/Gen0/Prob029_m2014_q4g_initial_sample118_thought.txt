To implement the TopModule as described, we will first create a two-input XNOR gate using 'in1' and 'in2'. The output of this XNOR gate will then be connected to one of the inputs of a two-input XOR gate, with 'in3' connected to the other input. The output of the XOR gate will be 'out'.
