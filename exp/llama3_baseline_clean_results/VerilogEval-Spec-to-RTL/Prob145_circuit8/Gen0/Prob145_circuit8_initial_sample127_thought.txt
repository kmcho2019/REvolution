To implement the TopModule, we need to analyze the given simulation waveforms. 
We can see that the output p is 1 whenever the input a is 1 and the clock is high (1). 
This indicates that p is directly dependent on the input a when the clock is high.
On the other hand, the output q seems to be dependent on both the input a and the clock. 
It becomes 1 whenever the clock is low (0) and remains 1 even when the clock goes high and a becomes 0, until a becomes 1. 
This suggests that q is a latch that is set when the clock is low and a is high, and reset when the clock is low and a is low.
Based on this analysis, we can implement the TopModule using Verilog.
