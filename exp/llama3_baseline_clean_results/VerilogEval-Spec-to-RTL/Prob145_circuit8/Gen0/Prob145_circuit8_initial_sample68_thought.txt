The module TopModule implements a sequential circuit with two output ports, p and q. 
The circuit seems to be a simple finite state machine that changes state based on the input signal 'a' and the clock. 
It appears that when the clock is low, 'p' and 'q' are both 0. 
When the clock is high and 'a' is 1, 'p' is 1 and 'q' is 0. 
When the clock is high and 'a' was previously 1 while the clock was low, 'p' is 0 and 'q' is 1.
However, looking at the waveform, it seems 'p' and 'q' are actually just tracking the input 'a' when the clock is high, and when the clock goes low, 'q' stays 1 for as long as the clock is low if 'a' was ever 1 while the clock was high. 
Given the provided waveform, the circuit can be implemented using a combination of flip-flops and logical gates.
