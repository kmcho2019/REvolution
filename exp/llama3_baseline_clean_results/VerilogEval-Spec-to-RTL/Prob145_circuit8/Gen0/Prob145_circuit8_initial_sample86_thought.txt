To determine the functionality of the sequential circuit, let's analyze the simulation waveforms. The output p is high only when the input a is high and the clock is high. The output q becomes high when the clock goes low after p has been high. This indicates that the circuit is implementing a rising-edge triggered D flip-flop with an asynchronous reset (or a type of latch). When the clock is low, the output q remains at its previous state unless it was set high by the rising edge of the clock when a was high, and then the clock goes low. We'll design a D flip-flop that captures the input a on the rising edge of the clock and assigns it to p, and q will be assigned the value of p when the clock goes low.
