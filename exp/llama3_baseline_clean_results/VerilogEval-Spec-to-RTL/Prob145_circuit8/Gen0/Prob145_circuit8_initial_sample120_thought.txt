The module TopModule appears to implement a simple sequential circuit with two outputs, p and q, that depend on the input a and the current state of the circuit. The output p seems to be 1 only when a is 1 and the clock is high (1), while the output q seems to be 1 when the clock is high (1) and a has been 1 at some point after the last time the clock was low (0). This behavior can be achieved by using two flip-flops (registers) to keep track of the state of the circuit.
