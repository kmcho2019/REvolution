To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the behavior of the given simulation waveforms. The module seems to have a sequential behavior, where the output p and q depend on the current and previous values of the input clock and a. 

Upon examining the waveforms, we can see that:
- When the clock is high (1), p is high (1) whenever a is high (1).
- When the clock is high (1) and a is low (0), p is low (0).
- When the clock is low (0), p remains the same as its previous value.
- The output q seems to be following the output p but with some delay. Whenever p becomes high (1), q becomes high (1) in the next clock cycle when the clock is low (0).
- Whenever the clock is high (1) and a is low (0), q remains the same as its previous value.

Based on these observations, we can implement the TopModule using two flip-flops: one for p and one for q.
