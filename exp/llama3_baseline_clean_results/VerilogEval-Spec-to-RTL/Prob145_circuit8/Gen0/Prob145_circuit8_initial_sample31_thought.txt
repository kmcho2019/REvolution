The circuit can be implemented as a sequential circuit with a clock and an input a. 
The output p is high when a is high and the clock is high. 
The output q seems to be a flip-flop that sets when a is high and the clock is high, 
and resets when the clock goes low and a goes high after it was low. 
However, it also gets set when p is high. 
To implement this, we can use a d-flip-flop with the data input d being a when the clock is high, 
and q can be the output of the flip-flop, and p can be the AND of a and the clock.
We will use a non-blocking assignment to update the state of q in the next clock cycle.
