The module TopModule is a sequential circuit with one input (a) and two outputs (p and q). 
The outputs seem to change state only when the clock changes from low to high (i.e., at the positive edge of the clock). 
When the clock is low, the outputs seem to hold their previous state. 
The output p seems to be the same as input a when the clock is high, and output q seems to be the previous state of a.
