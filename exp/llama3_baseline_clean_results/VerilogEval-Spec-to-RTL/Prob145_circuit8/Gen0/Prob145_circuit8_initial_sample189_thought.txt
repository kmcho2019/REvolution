The module TopModule implements a sequential circuit with two outputs p and q. 
By analyzing the given simulation waveforms, we can see that output p is high 
when the input a is high and the clock is high, and output q becomes high after 
output p has been high for a certain period of time. 
Also, the state of q seems to be dependent on the state of p and a. 
Based on this analysis, we can infer that this module acts like an edge 
detector for the input a, but also it has some kind of memory to keep track of 
previous states of a and the clock.

From the waveforms, we can observe that:
- p is high when a and clock are both high.
- q becomes high when a and clock are both high and remain in that state 
  even after a goes low, until the clock goes low and then goes high again 
  (which resets q), or until a goes low and then goes high again while the 
  clock is still high (which also resets q). 
  However, q will become high when it sees the first high a after the clock 
  goes high.

Considering the above points, the module can be designed using a combination 
of logic gates and flip-flops. Since the exact behavior is dependent on the 
relative timing of the clock and a, a sequential circuit seems to be a good 
fit.
