The module TopModule seems to implement a sequential circuit that is sensitive to the clock signal. 
When the clock signal is low (0), the output p is 0 and q follows the value of a when a goes from 0 to 1. 
When the clock signal goes high (1), p becomes 1 when a is 1, and q becomes 1 when a has been 1 and the clock goes high. 
This behavior can be achieved by using a Finite State Machine (FSM) or simple sequential logic with D flip-flops or other sequential elements.
However, given the waveforms, a simpler interpretation could be that p is a simple AND gate between clock and a, 
and q is essentially a D flip-flop where the input is a and the clock is the clock signal, but with an initial condition 
that sets q to 0 when the clock first goes high after being low for some time.
A more straightforward implementation considering the waveform can be achieved using simple combinational and sequential logic.
