To implement the given Karnaugh map, we can first identify the minterms that correspond to an output of 1. The minterms are the product terms where the output is 1. From the given map, we can see that the minterms are: 
- When a=0, b=0, c=0 (irrespective of d)
- When a=0, b=1 (irrespective of c and d)
- When a=1, b=0, c=1 (irrespective of d)
- When a=1, b=1 (irrespective of c and d)
These minterms can be implemented using AND gates and then ORed together to produce the final output.
