To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. We will use four full adders in cascade to add the corresponding bits of the two input numbers. Each full adder will take the corresponding bits of the two input numbers and the carry from the previous full adder (if any), and produce the sum bit and the carry for the next full adder. We will use a wire to propagate the carry from one full adder to the next.
