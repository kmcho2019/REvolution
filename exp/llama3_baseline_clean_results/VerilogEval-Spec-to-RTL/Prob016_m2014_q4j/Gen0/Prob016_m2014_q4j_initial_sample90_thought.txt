To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a module named TopModule that uses four full adder modules. Each full adder will be connected to the corresponding bits of the input x and y, and the carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. The sum bits from each full adder will form the lower 4 bits of the output sum, and the carry output from the most significant full adder will be the overflow bit, forming the most significant bit of the output sum.
