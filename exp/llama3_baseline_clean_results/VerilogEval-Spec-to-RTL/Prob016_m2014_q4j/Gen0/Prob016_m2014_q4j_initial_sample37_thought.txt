To implement the 4-bit adder, we'll first define a full adder module that takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, then produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We'll then instantiate four of these full adders in the TopModule, connecting them in a chain to perform the 4-bit addition. The carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The final carry-out will be the overflow bit.
