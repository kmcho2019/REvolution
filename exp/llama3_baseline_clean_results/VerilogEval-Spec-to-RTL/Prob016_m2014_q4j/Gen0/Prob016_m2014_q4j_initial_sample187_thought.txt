To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can start by defining a FullAdder module that takes in three inputs (a, b, and carry_in) and produces two outputs (sum and carry_out). We can then instantiate four FullAdder modules in the TopModule to perform the 4-bit addition. The carry_out of each full adder will be connected to the carry_in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder. The final carry_out will be the overflow bit.
