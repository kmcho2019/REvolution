To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to chain four full adders together. Each full adder will take two input bits from x and y, and the carry from the previous full adder. The first full adder will have a carry-in of 0. We will then connect the sum bits from each full adder to form the 4-bit sum, and the carry-out of the last full adder will be the overflow bit. This overflow bit will be appended to the 4-bit sum to form the 5-bit output sum.
