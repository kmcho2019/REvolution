To implement a 4-bit adder, we can use four full adders in a cascaded structure. Each full adder will handle one bit position from the inputs x and y, starting from the least significant bit (LSB). The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. The sum of the most significant bit (MSB) and the carry-out from the MSB full adder will give us the 5-bit output, where the extra bit is the overflow.
