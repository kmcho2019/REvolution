To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can use a chain of full adder modules. Each full adder module will take two input bits and a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We will start by defining a full adder module, then use this module four times in our TopModule to perform the 4-bit addition. The final carry-out from the last full adder will serve as the overflow bit.
