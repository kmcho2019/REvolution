To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a hierarchical design where each bit position is handled by a full adder. The full adder will have inputs for the two bits being added and the carry from the previous bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be passed to the next full adder. For the least significant bit (LSB), the carry-in will be 0. The final carry-out from the most significant bit (MSB) will be the overflow bit.
