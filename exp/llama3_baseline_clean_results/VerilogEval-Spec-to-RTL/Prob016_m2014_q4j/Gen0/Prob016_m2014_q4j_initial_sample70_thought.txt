To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will first create a FullAdder module. This module will have three input ports (a, b, and carryIn) and two output ports (sum and carryOut). The sum output will be the result of the addition of a and b plus the carryIn, and the carryOut will be set if the sum of a, b, and carryIn exceeds 1. We will then use four instances of this FullAdder module in the TopModule to perform the 4-bit addition. The carryOut of each full adder will be connected to the carryIn of the next full adder to propagate the carry. The sum outputs of the four full adders will be the bits of the sum output, and the carryOut of the last full adder will be the overflow bit.
