To implement a 4-bit adder, we will use a chain of full adders. Each full adder will take two bits from the input x and y, and a carry bit from the previous full adder. The sum and carry out of each full adder will be used as the sum and carry in for the next full adder. The first full adder will not have a carry in, so we will initialize it to 0. The sum output of the last full adder will be the 4 least significant bits of the output sum, and the carry out of the last full adder will be the most significant bit (overflow bit) of the output sum.
