To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder. A full adder is a combinational circuit that adds three one-bit numbers, producing a two-bit sum. The two-bit sum includes a carry-out bit, which can be used as an input to another full adder for multi-bit addition. 

We will create a module for a full adder and then instantiate it four times in the TopModule to perform a 4-bit addition. Each full adder will take two bits from the input numbers x and y and the carry-in from the previous full adder (except for the first full adder, which will not have a carry-in). 

The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The sum bits from each full adder will form the lower 4 bits of the sum output, and the carry-out from the last full adder will be the overflow bit (the most significant bit of the sum output).
