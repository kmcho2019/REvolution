To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use a ripple carry adder architecture. The adder will consist of four full adder modules. Each full adder module will perform a single-bit addition of the corresponding bits from the input numbers x and y, along with a carry bit from the previous addition. The carry out from each full adder will be the carry in for the next full adder. The sum output will be the result of the addition, including the overflow bit.
