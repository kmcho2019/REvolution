To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder. A full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We can use this basic unit to build a 4-bit adder. The 4-bit adder will take two 4-bit input numbers (x and y) and produce a 5-bit output (sum) which includes the overflow bit. We can connect four full adders in a chain, where the carry-out of each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next one. The input bits of the full adders will be connected to the corresponding bits of the input numbers x and y, from least significant bit to most significant bit.
