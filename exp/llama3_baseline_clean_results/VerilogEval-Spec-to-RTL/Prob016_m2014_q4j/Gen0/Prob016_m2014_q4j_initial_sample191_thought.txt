To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can use a chain of full adders where the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. Each full adder takes two bits from the inputs x and y, and the carry-in, and produces a sum and a carry-out. The first full adder does not need an external carry-in, so we can set its carry-in to 0. The sum of each full adder is one bit of the output sum. The carry-out of the last full adder is the overflow bit.
