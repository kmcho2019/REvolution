To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a hierarchy of full adders where each full adder is responsible for adding two input bits and a carry bit. We start from the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry to the next full adder for each bit position, up to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry out of the MSB full adder will serve as the overflow bit in the output sum.
