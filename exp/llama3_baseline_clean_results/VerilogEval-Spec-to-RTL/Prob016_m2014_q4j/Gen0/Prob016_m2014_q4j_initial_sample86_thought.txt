To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to create a chain of full adder modules. Each full adder will take the corresponding bits of the inputs x and y, and the carry from the previous full adder (if any), and produce the sum bit and the carry for the next full adder. The first full adder will not have a carry input, and the last full adder's carry output will be the overflow bit of the sum.
