To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use four instances of a full adder module. The full adder will be designed to take two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The 4-bit adder will be built by connecting the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next full adder, effectively allowing the carry to propagate through the adder. The sum output will be the concatenation of the sum bits from each full adder, and the final carry-out will be included as the most significant bit (overflow) of the output sum.
