To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to connect four full adders in series. The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The inputs x and y will be connected to the corresponding inputs of the full adders. The sum output will be a 4-bit output from the full adders, and the overflow bit will be the carry-out from the most significant bit full adder.
