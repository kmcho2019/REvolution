To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the basic structure of a full adder, which is a digital circuit that adds three one-bit inputs (A, B, and carry-in) and produces two one-bit outputs (sum and carry-out). For a 4-bit adder, we will chain four full adders together, where the carry-out of each full adder serves as the carry-in for the next one. The final carry-out of the last full adder will be the overflow bit of the 4-bit sum.
