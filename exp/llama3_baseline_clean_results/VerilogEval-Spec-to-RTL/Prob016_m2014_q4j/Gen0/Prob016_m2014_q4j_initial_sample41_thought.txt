To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will create a module named TopModule with the specified interface. The module will use four full adders in a chain to add the corresponding bits of the 4-bit inputs x and y. The carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The sum output will be a 5-bit signal, where the most significant bit represents the overflow.
