To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can instantiate four full adder modules. The carry-in of the first full adder will be tied to 0 (since there's no previous addition to carry from), and the carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. We'll then connect the sum outputs of each full adder to form the 4-bit sum, and the carry-out of the last full adder will serve as the overflow bit.
