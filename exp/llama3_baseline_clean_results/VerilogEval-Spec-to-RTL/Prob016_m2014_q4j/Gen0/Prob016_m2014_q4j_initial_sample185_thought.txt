To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use four full adder modules connected in series. Each full adder module takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in bit of the next full adder. The sum bits from each full adder will form the sum output, and the carry-out bit of the last full adder will be the overflow bit.
