To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder, which is a basic building block for digital adders. A full adder takes three inputs (two data bits and a carry bit) and produces two outputs (a sum bit and a carry bit). 

The TopModule will be composed of four full adder instances, each handling one bit position of the inputs x and y, and propagating the carry from one position to the next. The first full adder (for the least significant bit) will not have a carry input, so we can tie this to 0. 

Each subsequent full adder's carry input will come from the carry output of the previous full adder. The sum outputs from each full adder will form the bits of the sum output, with the most significant bit being the carry output from the most significant full adder.
