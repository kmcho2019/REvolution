To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can chain four full adder modules together. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We can use the carry-out bit from one full adder as the carry-in bit for the next full adder. This way, we can add two 4-bit numbers bit by bit, from the least significant bit to the most significant bit, and also keep track of the carry (overflow) bit.
