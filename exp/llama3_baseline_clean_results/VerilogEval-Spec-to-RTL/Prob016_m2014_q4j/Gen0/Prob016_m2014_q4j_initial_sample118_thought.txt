To implement the 4-bit adder, we can use four full adders. Each full adder takes two input bits (x and y) and the carry from the previous addition. The first full adder takes the least significant bits of x and y, and the carry is initially set to 0. The result of each full adder is used to calculate the sum and the carry for the next full adder. The final sum and the carry from the last full adder will give us the 5-bit sum, including the overflow bit.
