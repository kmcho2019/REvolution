To implement a 4-bit adder, we will use four full adders connected in a ripple carry configuration. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry bit, producing a sum and a carry out. The carry out from each full adder is used as the carry in for the next most significant full adder. This configuration allows us to propagate the carry from the least significant bit to the most significant bit. The output sum will include the overflow bit, which is the carry out from the most significant full adder.
