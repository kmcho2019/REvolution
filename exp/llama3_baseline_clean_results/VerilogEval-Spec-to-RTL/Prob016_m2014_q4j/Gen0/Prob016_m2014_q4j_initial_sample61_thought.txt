To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to create a hierarchical design with four full adder modules. Each full adder takes two input bits (x and y) and a carry-in bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder is connected to the carry-in bit of the next full adder. The sum bits from all four full adders are collected to form the 4-bit sum output, and the final carry-out bit is used as the overflow bit, making the total output 5 bits wide.
