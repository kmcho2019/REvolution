To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will first create a full adder module. The full adder will take two input bits and a carry bit, and produce an output bit and a carry-out bit. Then, we will create the TopModule which will instantiate four full adder modules, chaining them together to perform the 4-bit addition. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, and the final carry-out will be used as the overflow bit.
