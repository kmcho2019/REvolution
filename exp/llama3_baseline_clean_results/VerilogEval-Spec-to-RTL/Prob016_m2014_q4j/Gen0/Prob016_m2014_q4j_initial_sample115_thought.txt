To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder. A full adder takes two input bits and a carry bit, and it produces a sum bit and a carry bit. We will chain four full adders together, with the carry output of one full adder serving as the carry input to the next. The inputs to the adder will be two 4-bit numbers, x and y, and the output will be a 5-bit sum that includes the overflow bit.
