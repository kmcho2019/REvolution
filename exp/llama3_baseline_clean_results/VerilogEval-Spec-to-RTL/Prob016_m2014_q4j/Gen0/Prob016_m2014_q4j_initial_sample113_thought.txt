To implement a 4-bit adder, we can use four full adders connected in a cascade fashion. Each full adder takes two input bits from x and y, and the carry-in from the previous full adder. The carry-out from each full adder is passed to the next full adder as the carry-in. The sum bits from each full adder are concatenated to form the 4-bit sum, and the carry-out from the last full adder is used as the overflow bit.
