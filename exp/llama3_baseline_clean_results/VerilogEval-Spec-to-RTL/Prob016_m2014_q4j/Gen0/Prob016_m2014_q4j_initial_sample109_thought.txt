To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use a chain of four full adders. Each full adder will take the corresponding bits from inputs x and y, and the carry from the previous full adder. The first full adder will not have a carry input, so we can set it to 0. The output of each full adder will be a sum bit and a carry bit. The sum bits will form the lower 4 bits of the output sum, and the carry output of the last full adder will be the overflow bit (the most significant bit of the output sum).
