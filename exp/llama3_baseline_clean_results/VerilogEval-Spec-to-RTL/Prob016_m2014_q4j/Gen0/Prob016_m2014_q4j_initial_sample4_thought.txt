To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to create a chain of full adders where the carry output from one full adder is connected to the carry input of the next full adder. The input ports x and y will be used as the two 4-bit numbers to be added. Each bit of the input numbers will be connected to the corresponding input of a full adder. The sum output will be a 5-bit signal, with the most significant bit representing the overflow bit.
