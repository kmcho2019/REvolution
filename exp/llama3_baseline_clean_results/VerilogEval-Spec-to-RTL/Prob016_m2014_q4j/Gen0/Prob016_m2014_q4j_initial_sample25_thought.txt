To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder, which is a digital circuit that adds three one-bit numbers (two inputs and a carry input) and produces two outputs: a sum and a carry. We will chain four full adders together to handle the 4-bit inputs x and y. The carry output of each full adder will be the carry input of the next full adder. The final carry output will serve as the overflow bit (most significant bit) of the sum output.
