To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a chain of full adder modules, each handling one bit position. The carry-out from each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next most significant full adder. The sum bits from each full adder will form the sum output, with the carry-out from the most significant full adder becoming the overflow bit. We will use a separate module for the full adder to make the design more modular and reusable.
