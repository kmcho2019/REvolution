To implement a 4-bit adder, we can use four full adders, each handling one bit of the inputs x and y, and the carry from the previous addition. The first full adder will handle the least significant bits of x and y without any carry input. Each subsequent full adder will use the carry output from the previous full adder as its carry input. The final carry output from the last full adder will serve as the most significant bit (the overflow bit) of the sum output.
