To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can use four instances of a full adder module. Each full adder will take two input bits from x and y and the carry bit from the previous full adder, and produce a sum bit and a carry bit. The carry bit from each full adder will be used as the input to the next full adder, except for the first full adder which has a carry input of 0. The sum bits from each full adder will form the 4 least significant bits of the output sum, and the carry bit from the last full adder will be the most significant bit (overflow bit) of the output sum.
