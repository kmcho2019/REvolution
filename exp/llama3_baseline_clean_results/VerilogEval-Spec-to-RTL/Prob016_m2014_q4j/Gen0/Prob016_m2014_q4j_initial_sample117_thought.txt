To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will first create a full adder module. This full adder module will take two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We will then instantiate four full adders in the TopModule, connecting them in a chain to handle the 4-bit addition. The carry-out of each full adder will be connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The final carry-out will be used as the overflow bit in the output sum.
