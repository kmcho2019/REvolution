To implement the 4-bit adder, we will use four full adders. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two 4-bit input numbers, x and y. The carry-in for the first full adder will be 0, and the carry-in for the subsequent full adders will be the carry-out from the previous full adder. The sum output will be the result of the addition, and the final carry-out will be the overflow bit.
