To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to instantiate four full adder modules. The input bits of x and y will be connected to the corresponding input bits of the full adders. The carry output of each full adder will be connected to the carry input of the next full adder. The sum output of each full adder will form the sum output of the TopModule. The carry output of the most significant full adder will be the overflow bit.
