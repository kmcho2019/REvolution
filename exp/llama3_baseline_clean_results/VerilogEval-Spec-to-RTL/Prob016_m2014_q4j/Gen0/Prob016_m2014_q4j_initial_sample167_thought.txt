To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to create a hierarchical design where each bit position (from least significant bit to most significant bit) is represented by a full adder. Each full adder takes two input bits (one from x and one from y) and a carry-in from the previous full adder, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The final carry-out of the most significant bit full adder serves as the overflow bit in the output sum. We will use a module for the full adder and instantiate it four times within the TopModule, connecting the carry-outs appropriately to achieve the cascaded effect.
