To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can chain four full adders together, where each full adder handles one bit of the input numbers x and y. We will use the carry-out of each full adder as the carry-in for the next full adder, and the final carry-out will serve as the overflow bit in the output sum. Each full adder will calculate the sum of the corresponding bits of x and y, and the carry from the previous full adder (if any).
