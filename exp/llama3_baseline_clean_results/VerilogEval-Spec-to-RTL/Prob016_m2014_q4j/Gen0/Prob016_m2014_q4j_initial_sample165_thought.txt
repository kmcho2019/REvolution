To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use four full adder modules. Each full adder will take two input bits and a carry bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry bit. The carry bit from each full adder will be passed to the next full adder. The final carry bit will be used as the overflow bit in the output sum. We will start with the least significant bit (LSB) of the inputs x and y, and propagate the carry bit through each full adder to calculate the sum.
