To implement a 4-bit adder, we can use four full adders connected in a chain. Each full adder will take the corresponding bits from inputs x and y, and the carry bit from the previous full adder. The sum bit from each full adder will form the sum output, and the carry bit from the last full adder will be the overflow bit. We'll start by defining the full adder module, then use it to build the 4-bit adder.
