To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can chain four full adders together, where each full adder's carry-out is connected to the next full adder's carry-in. The sum output will be the result of the addition, and the final carry-out will serve as the overflow bit. The module will take two 4-bit inputs, x and y, and produce a 5-bit output, sum, which includes the overflow bit.
