To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will create a hierarchical design where each full adder will be responsible for adding two input bits and a carry bit. The carry bit from each full adder will be passed to the next full adder. We start by defining a full adder module that takes three inputs (two data bits and a carry bit) and produces two outputs (a sum bit and a carry bit). Then, we use four instances of this full adder module in our TopModule to perform the 4-bit addition, connecting the carry output of each full adder to the carry input of the next one. The final carry output will serve as the overflow bit in our sum.
