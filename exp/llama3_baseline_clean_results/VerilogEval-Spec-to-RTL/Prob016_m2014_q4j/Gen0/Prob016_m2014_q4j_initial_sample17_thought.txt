To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will first create a module for a full adder. This full adder will have two input bits (a and b), an input carry bit (cin), and two output bits (sum and cout). The sum will be calculated as a XOR b XOR cin, and the carry out will be set if any two of a, b, or cin are high. Then, we will create the TopModule which will instantiate four of these full adders. The carry out of each full adder will be connected to the carry in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through all the bits.
