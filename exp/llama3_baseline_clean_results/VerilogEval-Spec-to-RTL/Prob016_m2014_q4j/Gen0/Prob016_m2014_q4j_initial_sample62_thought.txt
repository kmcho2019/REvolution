To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use a chain of four full adder modules. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from x and one from y) and a carry bit from the previous full adder (if any), and produce an output bit and a carry bit. The output bits from each full adder will form the sum, and the final carry bit will be the overflow bit. We will define a FullAdder module and then use it to build the TopModule.
