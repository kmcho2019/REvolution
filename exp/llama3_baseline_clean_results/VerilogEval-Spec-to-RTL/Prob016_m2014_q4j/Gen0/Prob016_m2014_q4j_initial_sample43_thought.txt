To implement the TopModule, we can use a series of full adders to perform the 4-bit addition. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry bit, and produces a sum bit and a carry bit. We can chain these full adders together to add the 4-bit numbers x and y. The sum of the most significant bits of x and y, along with the carry from the previous bit, will produce the final carry (overflow) bit and the most significant bit of the sum.
