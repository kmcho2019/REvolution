To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we need to chain four full adders together. Each full adder will take in two input bits (one from x and one from y) and the carry-in from the previous full adder. The carry-out of each full adder will be the carry-in of the next full adder. The final carry-out of the last full adder will be the overflow bit. We will use a structural approach to instantiate the full adders within the TopModule.
