To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we'll create a module named TopModule that includes four full adder components. Each full adder will be responsible for adding corresponding bits from inputs x and y, along with a carry bit. The carry bit will be propagated from one full adder to the next, starting with a carry of 0 for the least significant bit.
