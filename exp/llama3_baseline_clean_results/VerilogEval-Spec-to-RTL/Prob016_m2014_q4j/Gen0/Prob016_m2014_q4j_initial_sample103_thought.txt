To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can use a chain of full adders where each full adder takes in the corresponding bits of the two input numbers x and y, along with a carry from the previous full adder. The first full adder takes in a carry of 0 since there is no previous full adder. The sum bits from each full adder will form the output sum, and the carry from the last full adder will be the overflow bit.
