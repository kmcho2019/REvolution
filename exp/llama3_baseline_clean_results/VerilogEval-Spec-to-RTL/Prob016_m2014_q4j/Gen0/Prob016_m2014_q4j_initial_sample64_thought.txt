To implement the 4-bit adder, we will use four full adder modules connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits from x and y, along with a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next most significant full adder. This way, the sum of the two 4-bit numbers can be calculated, and the final carry-out bit from the most significant full adder will represent the overflow.
