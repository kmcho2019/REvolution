To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder. A full adder is a basic digital circuit that takes three single-bit inputs (two operands and a carry) and produces two single-bit outputs (sum and carry). We can then use this full adder as a building block to create a 4-bit adder. The 4-bit adder will have four full adders connected in a cascade, where the carry output of each full adder is connected to the carry input of the next full adder. This way, we can handle the carry from each bit position to the next. The sum output will be a 4-bit value, and the final carry output will serve as the overflow bit, making the total output a 5-bit value.
