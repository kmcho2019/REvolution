To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will create a hierarchical design where each bit position uses a full adder module. The full adder module will take two input bits and a carry-in bit, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We will instantiate four full adder modules, one for each bit position, and connect the carry-out of each module to the carry-in of the next module. The final carry-out will be the overflow bit.
