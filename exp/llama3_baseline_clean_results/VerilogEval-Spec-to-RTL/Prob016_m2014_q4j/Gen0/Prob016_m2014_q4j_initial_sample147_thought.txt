To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we can create a module named TopModule that instantiates four full adder modules. Each full adder will be connected in a ripple-carry fashion, where the carry output of one full adder is connected to the carry input of the next full adder. The inputs x and y will be connected to the corresponding inputs of the full adders. The output sum will be formed by the sum outputs of the full adders and the final carry output.
