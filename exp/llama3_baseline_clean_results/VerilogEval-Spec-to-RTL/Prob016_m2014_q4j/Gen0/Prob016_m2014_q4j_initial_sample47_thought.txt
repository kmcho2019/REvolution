To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder, which is a basic digital circuit that adds three one-bit numbers (two operands and a carry-in). We'll then use this understanding to create a 4-bit adder by chaining full adders. Each full adder will add corresponding bits of the input numbers x and y along with a carry from the previous addition. The final carry out from the most significant bit addition will serve as the overflow bit in the output sum.
