To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we will create a module named TopModule that instantiates four full adder modules. Each full adder will be connected in a chain, with the carry-out of one full adder connected to the carry-in of the next full adder. The inputs x and y will be connected to the corresponding inputs of the full adders, and the sum output will be a 5-bit signal, with the most significant bit being the overflow bit.
