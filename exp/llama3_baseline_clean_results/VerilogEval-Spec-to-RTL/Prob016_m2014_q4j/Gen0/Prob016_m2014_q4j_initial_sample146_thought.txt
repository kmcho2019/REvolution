To implement a 4-bit adder, we can use four full adder modules. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. We will connect the carry-out bit from each full adder to the carry-in bit of the next full adder, effectively "ripping" the carry through the adders. The sum output from the last full adder, along with the carry-out from the last full adder (which represents the overflow), will form the final 5-bit sum output.
