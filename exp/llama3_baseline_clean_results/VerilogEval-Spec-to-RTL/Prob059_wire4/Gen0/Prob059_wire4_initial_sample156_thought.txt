To implement the TopModule, we can use continuous assignments in Verilog. Continuous assignments are used to drive the output of a module directly from an expression, without the need for a procedural block. We will assign the input signals 'a', 'b', and 'c' directly to the output signals 'w', 'x' and 'y' (with 'b' driving both 'x' and 'y'), and 'z' respectively.
