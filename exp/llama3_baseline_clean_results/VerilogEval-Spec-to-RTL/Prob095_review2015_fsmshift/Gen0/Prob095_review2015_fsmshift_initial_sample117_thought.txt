To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. In the IDLE state, we check for the reset signal. If reset is asserted, we transition to the SHIFT state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state. Once the counter reaches 4, we deassert shift_ena. We will also assert shift_ena for 4 cycles immediately after reset.
