To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: an idle state and a shifting state. When reset is asserted, the FSM will transition to the shifting state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, it will transition to the idle state and deassert shift_ena. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the shifting state. The reset will be synchronous, meaning it will only be considered at the positive edge of the clock.
