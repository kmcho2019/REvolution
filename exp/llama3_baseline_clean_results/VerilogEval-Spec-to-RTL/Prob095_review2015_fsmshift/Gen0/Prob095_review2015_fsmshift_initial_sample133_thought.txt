To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. In the IDLE state, we will assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles when the module is reset. After 4 clock cycles, we will deassert shift_ena and remain in the IDLE state until the next reset. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles shift_ena has been asserted. The FSM will be reset synchronously with the positive edge of the clock.
