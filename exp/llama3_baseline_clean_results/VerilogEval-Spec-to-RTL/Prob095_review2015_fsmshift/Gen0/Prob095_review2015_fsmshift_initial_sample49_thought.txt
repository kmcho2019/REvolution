To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) that has two main states: an initial state where shift_ena is asserted for 4 clock cycles after reset, and a subsequent idle state where shift_ena remains deasserted. The FSM will be designed such that upon reset, it transitions to a state that starts the 4-cycle shift_ena assertion period. After 4 cycles, it will transition to the idle state. We'll use a counter to keep track of the cycles during the shift_ena assertion period.
