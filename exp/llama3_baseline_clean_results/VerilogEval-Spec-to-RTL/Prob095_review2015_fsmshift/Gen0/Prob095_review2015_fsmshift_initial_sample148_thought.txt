To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles for which the shift_ena signal is asserted. The FSM will have two main states: an idle state and a shifting state. When the module is reset, it will immediately enter the shifting state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, it will transition back to the idle state and keep shift_ena low until the next reset. The counter will be used to keep track of the number of clock cycles while in the shifting state.
