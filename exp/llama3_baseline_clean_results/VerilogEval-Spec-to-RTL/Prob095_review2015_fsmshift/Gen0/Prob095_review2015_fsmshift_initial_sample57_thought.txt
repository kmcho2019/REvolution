To implement the TopModule, we will use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles for which the shift_ena signal is asserted. The FSM will have two main states: an idle state and a shifting state. When reset is asserted, the FSM will transition to the shifting state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, the FSM will transition back to the idle state and keep shift_ena deasserted until the next reset. The counter will be used to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the shifting state.
