To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter. The FSM will have two main states: IDLE and SHIFT. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal will be low. When the FSM is reset, it will transition to the SHIFT state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, it will transition back to the IDLE state. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state.
