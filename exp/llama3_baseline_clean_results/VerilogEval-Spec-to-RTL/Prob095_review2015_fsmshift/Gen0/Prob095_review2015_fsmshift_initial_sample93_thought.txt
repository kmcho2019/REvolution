To implement the TopModule, we can use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. The FSM will start in the IDLE state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles when reset is asserted. After the 4 cycles, it will remain in the IDLE state with shift_ena deasserted until reset is asserted again. We can use a counter to keep track of the 4 clock cycles. The FSM will be reset synchronously on the positive edge of the clock when the reset signal is high.
