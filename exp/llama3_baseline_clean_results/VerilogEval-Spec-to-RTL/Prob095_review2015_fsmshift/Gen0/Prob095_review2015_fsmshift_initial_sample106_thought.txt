To solve this problem, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. The FSM starts in the IDLE state. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT state and asserts the shift_ena signal for exactly 4 clock cycles. After 4 clock cycles, the FSM returns to the IDLE state and deasserts the shift_ena signal. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state.
