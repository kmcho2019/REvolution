To implement the TopModule, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions between states based on the reset signal and the number of clock cycles. The FSM will have two main states: an idle state and a shifting state. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM will enter the shifting state and assert the shift_ena signal for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4 cycles, the FSM will transition to the idle state and deassert the shift_ena signal until the next reset.
