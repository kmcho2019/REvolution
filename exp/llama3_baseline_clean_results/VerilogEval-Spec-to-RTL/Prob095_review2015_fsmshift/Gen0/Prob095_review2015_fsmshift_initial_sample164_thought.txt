To implement the TopModule, we will create a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two main states: an idle state and a shifting state. When reset is asserted, the module will enter the shifting state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 cycles, it will enter the idle state and keep shift_ena low until reset is asserted again. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the shifting state.
