To solve this problem, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: Idle and ShiftEnable. In the Idle state, the shift_ena signal will be deasserted. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the ShiftEnable state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles, then return to the Idle state. We can use a counter to track the number of clock cycles in the ShiftEnable state. The FSM will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
