To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal is deasserted. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT state and asserts shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 cycles, the FSM returns to the IDLE state. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state. The reset signal should be active high and synchronous, meaning it should be sampled on the positive edge of the clock.
