To solve this problem, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: idle and shift. When the module is reset, it will enter the shift state and assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After that, it will enter the idle state and keep shift_ena low until the next reset. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles that shift_ena has been asserted. The FSM will be reset synchronously, meaning the reset signal will only be considered at the rising edge of the clock.
