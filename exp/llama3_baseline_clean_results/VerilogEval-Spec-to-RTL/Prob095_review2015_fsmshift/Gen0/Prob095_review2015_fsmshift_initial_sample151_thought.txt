To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) that has two states: idle and shift. When the module is reset, it transitions to the shift state and stays there for 4 clock cycles, asserting shift_ena during this time. After 4 cycles, it transitions back to the idle state and keeps shift_ena deasserted until the next reset. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of cycles in the shift state. The reset will be active high synchronous, meaning it will only be considered on the positive edge of the clock.
