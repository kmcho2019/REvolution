To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with two main states: IDLE and SHIFT. When the module is reset, it transitions to the SHIFT state and stays there for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4th clock cycle, it transitions back to the IDLE state and stays there until the next reset. The shift_ena signal is asserted during the SHIFT state. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state. Since reset is active high synchronous, we will check for the reset condition on the positive edge of the clock.
