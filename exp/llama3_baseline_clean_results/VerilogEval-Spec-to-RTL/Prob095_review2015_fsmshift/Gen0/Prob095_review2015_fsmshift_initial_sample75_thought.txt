The TopModule can be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) that has two main states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. In the IDLE state, shift_ena is always 0. When reset is asserted, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT_ENABLE state, where shift_ena is asserted for exactly 4 clock cycles. After the 4 cycles, the FSM remains in the IDLE state. The state machine can be implemented using a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT_ENABLE state.
