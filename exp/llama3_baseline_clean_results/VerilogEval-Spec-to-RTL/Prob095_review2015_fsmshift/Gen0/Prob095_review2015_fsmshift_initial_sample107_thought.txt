To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFT. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal will be 0. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the SHIFT state and start a 4-cycle counter. During these 4 cycles, the shift_ena signal will be 1. After the 4 cycles, the FSM will return to the IDLE state and the shift_ena signal will be 0. The reset signal will be active high synchronous, meaning it will only be recognized on the positive edge of the clock.
