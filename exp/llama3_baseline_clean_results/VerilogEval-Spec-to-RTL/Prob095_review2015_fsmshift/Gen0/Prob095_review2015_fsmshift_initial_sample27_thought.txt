The module TopModule will use a finite state machine (FSM) to control the shift register. We will implement a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles for which shift_ena should be asserted after reset. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFT. When reset is asserted, the FSM will transition to the SHIFT state and start a 4-cycle counter. After the counter reaches 4, the FSM will transition to the IDLE state and keep shift_ena deasserted until the next reset.
