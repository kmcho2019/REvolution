To solve this problem, we can implement a simple Finite State Machine (FSM) in the TopModule. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFT. When the module is reset, it will transition to the SHIFT state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, it will transition to the IDLE state and deassert shift_ena. The FSM will remain in the IDLE state until it is reset again. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT state.
