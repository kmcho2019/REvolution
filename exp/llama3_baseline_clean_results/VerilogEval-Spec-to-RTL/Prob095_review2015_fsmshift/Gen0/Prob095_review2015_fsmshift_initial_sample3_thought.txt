To implement the TopModule, we need to design a finite state machine (FSM) that can detect the reset signal and enable the shift register for exactly 4 clock cycles after reset. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles since the reset. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. When the reset signal is detected, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT_ENABLE state and starts the counter. The shift_ena signal is asserted during the SHIFT_ENABLE state. After 4 clock cycles, the FSM transitions back to the IDLE state and deasserts the shift_ena signal.
