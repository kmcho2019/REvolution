To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) that has two states: IDLE and SHIFTING. When the module is reset, it enters the SHIFTING state for exactly 4 clock cycles, then transitions to the IDLE state. While in the SHIFTING state, the shift_ena signal is asserted high. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal remains low. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFTING state.
