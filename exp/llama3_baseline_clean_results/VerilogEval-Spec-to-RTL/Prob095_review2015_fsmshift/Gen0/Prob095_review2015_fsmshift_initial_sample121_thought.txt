To implement the TopModule, we can design a simple finite state machine (FSM) with two states: Idle and ShiftEnable. When reset is asserted, the FSM transitions to the ShiftEnable state and stays there for exactly 4 clock cycles, asserting shift_ena during this time. After 4 cycles, it transitions back to the Idle state, where shift_ena remains deasserted until the next reset. We can use a counter to keep track of the number of cycles in the ShiftEnable state.
