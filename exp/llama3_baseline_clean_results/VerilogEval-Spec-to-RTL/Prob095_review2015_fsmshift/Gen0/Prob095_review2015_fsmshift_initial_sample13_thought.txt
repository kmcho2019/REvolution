The design idea is to implement a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. When the FSM is reset, it enters the SHIFT_ENABLE state and stays there for 4 clock cycles, asserting the shift_ena signal. After 4 cycles, it transitions back to the IDLE state and keeps the shift_ena signal low. A counter can be used to keep track of the number of clock cycles spent in the SHIFT_ENABLE state.
