To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter. The FSM will have two main states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal will be low. When the module is reset, it will transition to the SHIFT_ENABLE state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After 4 cycles, it will return to the IDLE state and keep shift_ena low. The counter will be used to keep track of the number of cycles shift_ena has been high. The reset signal is active high and synchronous, meaning it will only take effect on the positive edge of the clock.
