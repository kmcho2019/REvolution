To implement the TopModule, we will design a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: an idle state and a shift enable state. The FSM will be reset to the idle state when reset is high. After reset, the FSM will immediately enter the shift enable state for exactly 4 clock cycles, then return to the idle state and remain there until the next reset. The shift_ena signal will be asserted during the 4 clock cycles when the FSM is in the shift enable state. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles spent in the shift enable state.
