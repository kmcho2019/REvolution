To implement the TopModule, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and SHIFTING. In the IDLE state, the shift_ena signal will be 0. When the reset signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the SHIFTING state and a counter will be initialized to count 4 clock cycles. During these 4 cycles, the shift_ena signal will be asserted. After the counter reaches 4, the FSM will transition back to the IDLE state and the shift_ena signal will be deasserted. We will use a 2-bit counter to count the 4 clock cycles, as 2 bits can represent 4 states (00, 01, 10, 11).
