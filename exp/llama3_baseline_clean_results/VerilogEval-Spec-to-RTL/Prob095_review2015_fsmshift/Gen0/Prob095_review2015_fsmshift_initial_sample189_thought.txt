To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. When the module is reset, it will enter the SHIFT_ENABLE state and assert shift_ena for 4 clock cycles. After the 4 clock cycles, it will transition to the IDLE state and deassert shift_ena. In the IDLE state, shift_ena will remain deasserted until the module is reset again. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles in the SHIFT_ENABLE state.
