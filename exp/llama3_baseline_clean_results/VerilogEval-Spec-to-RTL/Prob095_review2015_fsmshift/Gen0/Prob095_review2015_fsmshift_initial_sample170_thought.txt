The TopModule should be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. 
In the IDLE state, shift_ena should be 0. When reset is asserted, the FSM transitions to the SHIFT state, 
where shift_ena is asserted for 4 clock cycles, and then it returns to the IDLE state. 
We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles that shift_ena has been asserted.
