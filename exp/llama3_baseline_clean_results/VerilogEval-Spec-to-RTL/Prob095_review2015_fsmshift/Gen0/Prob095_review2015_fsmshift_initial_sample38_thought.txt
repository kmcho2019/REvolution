To implement the TopModule, we will use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two main states: RESET and RUN. In the RESET state, we will assert shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles after the reset signal is asserted. Once the reset signal is de-asserted, the FSM will transition to the RUN state. However, since the proper bit pattern detection is not specified, we will assume that the shift register should be enabled for 4 cycles after reset and then remain disabled until the next reset. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles that shift_ena has been asserted.
