To implement the TopModule, we will use a Finite State Machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and SHIFT. When the module is reset, it transitions to the SHIFT state and asserts shift_ena for exactly 4 clock cycles. After 4 cycles, it transitions to the IDLE state and deasserts shift_ena. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of cycles shift_ena has been asserted. The FSM will also be reset synchronous, meaning it will only respond to the reset signal on the positive edge of the clock.
