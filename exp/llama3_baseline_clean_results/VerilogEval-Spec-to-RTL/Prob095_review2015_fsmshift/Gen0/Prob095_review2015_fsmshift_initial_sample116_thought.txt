To implement the TopModule, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) that can be in one of two states: IDLE and SHIFT_ENABLE. When reset is asserted, the FSM will transition to the SHIFT_ENABLE state and remain there for 4 clock cycles, during which shift_ena will be asserted. After 4 cycles, the FSM will transition back to the IDLE state and remain there until reset is asserted again. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles for which shift_ena has been asserted.
