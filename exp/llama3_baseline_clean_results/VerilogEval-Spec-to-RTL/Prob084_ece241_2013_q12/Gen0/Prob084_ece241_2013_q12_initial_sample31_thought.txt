To implement the TopModule, we will first create an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will be loaded with the input S on the positive edge of the clock when the enable signal is high. Then, we will use a multiplexer to select the output of the shift register based on the inputs A, B, and C. The multiplexer will have 8 inputs (the outputs of the shift register), 3 select lines (A, B, and C), and 1 output (Z). When the select lines are 000, the output of the multiplexer will be Q[0], when the select lines are 001, the output will be Q[1], and so on.
