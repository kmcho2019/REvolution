To implement the TopModule, I will first create an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will have a synchronous active high enable input to control shifting. Then, I will extend the circuit by adding 3 inputs A, B, and C, and an output Z. I will use a multiplexer to select the output of the shift register based on the values of A, B, and C.
