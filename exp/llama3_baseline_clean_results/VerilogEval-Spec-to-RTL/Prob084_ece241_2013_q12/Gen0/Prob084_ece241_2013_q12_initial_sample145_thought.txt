To implement the TopModule, we first need to create an 8-bit shift register using D-type flip-flops. The shift register will have an input S and will shift its bits to the right on the rising edge of the clock when the enable signal is high. To achieve random access to the bits in the shift register, we will use multiplexers. We will have 3 inputs A, B, and C, which will serve as the select lines for the multiplexers, and an output Z. When ABC equals a certain binary number, Z will be equal to the corresponding bit in the shift register.
