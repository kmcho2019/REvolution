To implement the TopModule, we first need to create an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will be loaded with the input S on the positive edge of the clock when the enable signal is high. Then, we will use a multiplexer to select the output of the shift register based on the inputs A, B, and C. The multiplexer will have 8 inputs (Q[0] to Q[7]) and 1 output (Z), and will be controlled by the 3-bit select signal ABC.
