To implement the TopModule, we will first design an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will be controlled by the synchronous active high enable input. Then, we will use the outputs of the shift register (Q[0] to Q[7]) and the inputs A, B, and C to create a multiplexer that selects the output of the shift register based on the value of ABC. The output of the multiplexer will be the output Z of the TopModule.
