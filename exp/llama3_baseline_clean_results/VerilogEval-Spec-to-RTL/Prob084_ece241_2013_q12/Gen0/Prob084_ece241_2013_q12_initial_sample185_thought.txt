To implement the TopModule, I will first create an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will have a synchronous active high enable input to control whether to shift the bits. Then, I will use multiplexers to select the output of the shift register based on the inputs A, B, and C. Since there are 3 inputs, I can use an 8-to-1 multiplexer to select one of the 8 outputs of the shift register. The select lines of the multiplexer will be driven by the inputs A, B, and C.
