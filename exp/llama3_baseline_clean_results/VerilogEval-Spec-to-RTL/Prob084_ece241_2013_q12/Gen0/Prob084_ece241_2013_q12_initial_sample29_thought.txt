To implement the TopModule, we will first design an 8-bit shift register with 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will be designed to shift in bits from the input S when the enable signal is high. We will then use a multiplexer to select the output from one of the flip-flops based on the inputs A, B, and C. The multiplexer will have 8 inputs (one from each flip-flop) and 1 output (Z). The select lines of the multiplexer will be driven by the inputs A, B, and C.
