The TopModule can be designed by first creating an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will shift in bits from the input S when the enable signal is high. To implement the random access functionality, a multiplexer can be used to select the output from the shift register based on the inputs A, B, and C. The multiplexer will have 8 inputs (one from each flip-flop in the shift register) and 1 output (Z). The select lines of the multiplexer will be driven by the inputs A, B, and C.
