The TopModule will contain 8 D flip-flops, each with an active high asynchronous reset. 
When the areset signal is high, the output of each flip-flop (q) will be reset to 0. 
The input to each flip-flop will be from the 8-bit input d. 
Each flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. 
This means that the data from d will be captured and stored in the flip-flops at the positive edge of clk, 
unless areset is high, in which case the output q will be reset to 0 immediately.
