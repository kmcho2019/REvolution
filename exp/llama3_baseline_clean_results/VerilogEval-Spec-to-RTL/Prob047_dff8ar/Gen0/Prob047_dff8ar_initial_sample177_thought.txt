To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset. Each D flip-flop will be connected to the 8-bit input 'd' and will output to the 8-bit output 'q'. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the 'clk' signal and will reset to 0 when 'areset' is high. We will use a for loop to instantiate the 8 DFFs.
