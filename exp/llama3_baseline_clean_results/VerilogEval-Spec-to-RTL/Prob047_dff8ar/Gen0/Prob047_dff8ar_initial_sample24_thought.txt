To implement the TopModule, we can use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) in parallel, each with an active high asynchronous reset. The inputs to the DFFs will come from the 8-bit input d, and the outputs will form the 8-bit output q. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. When the areset signal is high, the outputs of all DFFs will be reset to 0, regardless of the clk signal.
