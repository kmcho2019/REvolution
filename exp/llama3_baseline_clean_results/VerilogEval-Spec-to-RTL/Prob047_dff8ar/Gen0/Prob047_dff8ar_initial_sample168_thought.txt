To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset. We will use the posedge of clk to trigger the flip-flops. The reset of the flip-flops will be tied to areset, which is active high. When areset is asserted, the output q will be reset to 0. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) in the always block to assign the value of d to q on the positive edge of clk.
