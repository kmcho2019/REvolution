To implement the TopModule, we will create a module that contains 8 D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will have an active high asynchronous reset and will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). The 8-bit input (d) will be connected to the D inputs of the flip-flops, and the 8-bit output (q) will be connected to the Q outputs of the flip-flops. When the asynchronous reset (areset) is high, all flip-flops will reset their outputs to 0.
