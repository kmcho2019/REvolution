To implement the TopModule, we can use 8 instances of a D flip-flop (DFF) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will have a single bit of the input d connected to its D input, and the corresponding bit of the output q connected to its Q output. We will use the clk signal as the clock input for all DFFs, and the areset signal as the asynchronous reset input. The DFFs should be triggered by the positive edge of clk, so we will use a rising edge triggered DFF.
