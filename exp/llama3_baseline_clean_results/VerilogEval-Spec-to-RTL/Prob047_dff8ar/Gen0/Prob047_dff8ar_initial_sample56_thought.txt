To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 instances of D flip-flops (DFFs), each with an active high asynchronous reset. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The output of each DFF will be connected to form the 8-bit output 'q'. When the asynchronous reset 'areset' is high, all DFFs will reset their outputs to 0, regardless of the clock signal.
