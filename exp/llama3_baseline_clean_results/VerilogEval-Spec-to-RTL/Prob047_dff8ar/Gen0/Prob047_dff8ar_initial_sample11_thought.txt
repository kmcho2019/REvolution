To implement the TopModule, we need to instantiate 8 D flip-flops with asynchronous active-high reset. Each D flip-flop should be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and asynchronously reset by the 'areset' signal. The output of the D flip-flops should be connected to the output port 'q' and should be reset to 0 when 'areset' is high.
