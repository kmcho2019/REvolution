To design the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) since we need to store 8 bits of data. Each DFF will have an active high asynchronous reset, which means it will reset to 0 whenever areset is high, regardless of the clock. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk. This means that the data at the input d will be captured by the DFFs and appear at the output q on the next positive edge of the clock signal after the reset is released.
