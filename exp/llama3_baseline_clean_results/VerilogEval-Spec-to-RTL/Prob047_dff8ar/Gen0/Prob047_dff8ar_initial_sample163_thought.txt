To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with asynchronous reset. Each DFF will have its input connected to the corresponding bit of the 8-bit input 'd', and its output will be connected to the corresponding bit of the 8-bit output 'q'. The clock input of all DFFs will be connected to 'clk', and the reset input will be connected to 'areset'. The reset will be active high, so when 'areset' is high, the output 'q' will be reset to 0.
