The TopModule will be designed with 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the 8-bit input data 'd'. Each DFF will have an active high asynchronous reset, meaning they will reset their outputs to 0 whenever 'areset' is high, regardless of the clock signal 'clk'. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of 'clk', meaning they will update their outputs with the input data 'd' only when 'clk' transitions from 0 to 1.
