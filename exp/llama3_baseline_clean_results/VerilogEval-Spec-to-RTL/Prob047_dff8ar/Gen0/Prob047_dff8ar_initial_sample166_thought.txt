To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops, each with an active high asynchronous reset. This means that whenever the areset signal is high, all the flip-flops will reset their outputs to 0, regardless of the clock or the input data. The outputs of the flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and will store the input data from the d port. We will use the posedge of the clk signal to trigger the flip-flops and use the areset signal to reset the flip-flops asynchronously.
