To implement the TopModule, we will create a module with the specified interface. Inside this module, we'll instantiate 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the 8-bit data. Each DFF will have an active high asynchronous reset (areset) and will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk). We'll use a simple assignment statement to connect the inputs and outputs of the DFFs to the module's ports.
