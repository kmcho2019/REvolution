To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will have its input connected to the corresponding bit of the input d, and its output connected to the corresponding bit of the output q. The clock input of all DFFs will be connected to the clk signal, and the reset input will be connected to the areset signal. This ensures that all DFFs are triggered by the positive edge of clk and reset to 0 when areset is high.
