The design idea for the TopModule is to create a module that contains 8 D flip-flops, each triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and having an active high asynchronous reset (areset). When the areset signal is high, all D flip-flops will reset their outputs to 0. Otherwise, on the positive edge of the clk, each D flip-flop will capture the value of its corresponding input bit from the 8-bit input d and present it at its output. The outputs of all 8 D flip-flops will be combined to form the 8-bit output q.
