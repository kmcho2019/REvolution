To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we can use 8 D flip-flops in parallel, each with an asynchronous active high reset and triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). The reset signal (areset) should be connected to the reset input of all D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will capture the corresponding bit of the input data (d) at the positive edge of the clock and hold it until the next clock edge or until the reset is asserted.
