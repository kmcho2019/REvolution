To design the TopModule with the specified interface, we need to instantiate 8 D flip-flops, each with an active high asynchronous reset. We will use the built-in Verilog module for a D flip-flop with asynchronous reset. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk. When the asynchronous reset signal areset is high, the outputs of the D flip-flops should be reset to 0.
