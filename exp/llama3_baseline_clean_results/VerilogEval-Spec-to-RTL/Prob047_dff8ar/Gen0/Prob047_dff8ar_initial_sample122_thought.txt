To implement the TopModule as described, we can use a single 8-bit register with asynchronous reset. The register will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and will have an active high asynchronous reset (areset). When areset is high, the output (q) will be reset to 0, regardless of the current clock state. This can be achieved using a non-blocking assignment (=) within an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of clk or the high level of areset.
