To implement the TopModule, we can use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. When the asynchronous reset signal 'areset' is high, all DFFs will be reset to 0. The input 'd' is an 8-bit signal, and each bit will be connected to the corresponding DFF. The output 'q' is also an 8-bit signal, and each bit will be the output of the corresponding DFF.
