To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with asynchronous reset. Each DFF will have a one-bit input from the 8-bit input d and a one-bit output to the 8-bit output q. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk and will have an active high asynchronous reset signal areset. When areset is high, the output q will be reset to 0, regardless of the clock signal.
