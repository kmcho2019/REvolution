To implement the TopModule, we can use 8 instances of a D flip-flop (DFF) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and will have an asynchronous reset input (areset) that resets the output to 0. The input to the DFFs will come from the 8-bit input signal (d), and the outputs of the DFFs will be combined to form the 8-bit output signal (q).
