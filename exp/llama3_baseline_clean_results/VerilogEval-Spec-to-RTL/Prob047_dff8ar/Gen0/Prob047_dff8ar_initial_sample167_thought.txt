To implement the TopModule as described, we will use 8 instances of a D flip-flop (DFF) with asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be connected to the corresponding bit of the input 'd' and will output to the corresponding bit of the output 'q'. The asynchronous reset ('areset') will be connected to all DFFs, causing them to reset their output to 0 when 'areset' is high. The clock ('clk') will trigger each DFF on its positive edge, allowing the input 'd' to be captured and presented at the output 'q' after the reset condition is cleared and the clock ticks.
