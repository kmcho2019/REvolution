To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will have its data input connected to the corresponding bit of the input d, and its output connected to the corresponding bit of the output q. The reset input of all DFFs will be connected to areset, and the clock input will be connected to clk. This ensures that when areset is high, all DFFs will be reset to 0, and when clk rises, the value of d will be captured by the DFFs and output on q.
