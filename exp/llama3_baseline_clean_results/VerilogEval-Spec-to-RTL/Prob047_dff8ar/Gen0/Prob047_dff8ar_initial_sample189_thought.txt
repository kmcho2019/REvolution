Each D flip-flop will have an asynchronous active-high reset (areset), and will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). When the reset signal is asserted, all flip-flops will reset to 0, and when the clock signal rises, the input data (d) will be captured by the flip-flops and appear at the output (q) on the next clock cycle.
