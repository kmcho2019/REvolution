To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. The asynchronous reset (areset) will reset the output q to 0 when it is high. We can use the initial block or a continuous assignment with a reset condition to achieve this. However, since we need to model D flip-flops and not just combinational logic or simple storage elements, we'll opt for using a structural approach with individual DFF modules or by directly instantiating the necessary logic within the module.
