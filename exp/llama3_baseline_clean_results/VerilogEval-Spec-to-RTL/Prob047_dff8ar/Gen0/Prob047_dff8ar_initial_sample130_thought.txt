To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. We will use the built-in Verilog constructs to define the DFFs, utilizing the non-blocking assignment operator to capture the values at the positive edge of the clock. Since the reset is asynchronous, we will ensure that the reset logic is decoupled from the clock logic. The q output will be driven directly from the output of the DFFs, thus reflecting the stored values after each clock cycle.
