To implement the TopModule, we can utilize 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk). When the asynchronous reset signal (areset) is high, all DFFs should reset their outputs to 0, regardless of the current clock edge. 
The module will have 8 input lines (d) connected to the D inputs of the DFFs and 8 output lines (q) connected to the Q outputs of the DFFs. This way, when the clock signal rises, the data on the input lines will be captured by the DFFs and appear on the output lines, unless the reset signal is high, in which case the outputs will be reset to 0.
