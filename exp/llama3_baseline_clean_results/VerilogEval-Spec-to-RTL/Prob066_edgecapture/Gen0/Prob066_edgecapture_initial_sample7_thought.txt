The TopModule will use a combination of sequential and combinational logic. 
It will have a 32-bit register to store the output values and a 
combinational logic block to compare the current and previous input values. 
On every positive clock edge, it will check for transitions from 1 to 0 in the 
input and update the corresponding output bit to 1. The output bits will 
remain 1 until the register is reset.
