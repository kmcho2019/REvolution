To implement a JK flip-flop, we will use a sequential logic design approach. The JK flip-flop's output Q will be determined based on its current state (Qold) and the values of the inputs J and K at the positive edge of the clock. We can express the next state of Q (Qnew) as a function of J, K, and Qold. Then, we will use a D flip-flop or a basic register to store Qnew at the positive edge of the clock and output it as Q. However, since Verilog's built-in registers (reg) are suitable for this task and can simplify the code, we'll directly implement the logic using a reg variable for Q and update it at the positive edge of the clock.
