To implement the JK flip-flop, we will use a sequential logic approach. We will use the always block in Verilog to describe the behavior of the flip-flop. The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal. Inside the always block, we will use a case statement or if-else statements to implement the truth table of the JK flip-flop. The output Q will be assigned based on the values of J, K, and the current state of Q (Qold).
