To implement the JK flip-flop module named TopModule, we'll use a Verilog always block to describe the sequential behavior of the flip-flop. The always block will be triggered at the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will use the values of j, k, and the current state of Q (Qold) to determine the next state of Q. We'll use a reg variable to hold the current state of Q and assign its value to the output Q.
