To implement a JK flip-flop, we can use a combination of logic gates and a D-type flip-flop. The JK flip-flop's behavior is as follows: 
- When J=0 and K=0, the output remains the same (Qold).
- When J=0 and K=1, the output becomes 0.
- When J=1 and K=0, the output becomes 1.
- When J=1 and K=1, the output becomes the inverse of Qold.

We will use a reg to hold the current state (Qold) and use the JK inputs to determine the next state.
