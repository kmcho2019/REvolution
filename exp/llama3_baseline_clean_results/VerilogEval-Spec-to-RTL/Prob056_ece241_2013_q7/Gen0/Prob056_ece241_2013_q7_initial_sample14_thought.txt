To implement a JK flip-flop in Verilog, we need to use a reg to store the current state (Qold) and update it based on the inputs (J and K) at the positive edge of the clock (clk). The output (Q) should be assigned the updated value of Qold after the positive edge of the clock. The JK flip-flop truth table should be used to determine the next state of Qold.
