To implement the JK flip-flop, we can use a combination of logic gates and a D flip-flop. However, a more straightforward approach is to use the built-in reg type in Verilog and apply the logic directly based on the truth table. The JK flip-flop's output (Q) is dependent on its previous state (Qold) and the inputs J and K. We will use a reg to hold the current state of Q and update it based on the given conditions at each positive clock edge.
