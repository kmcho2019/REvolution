To implement a JK flip-flop in Verilog, we will use a reg to store the current state of the flip-flop. The next state will be determined based on the inputs j, k, and the current state of the flip-flop (Qold). We will use a case statement or if-else statements to determine the next state of the flip-flop according to the given truth table. The flip-flop will update its state on the positive edge of the clock.
