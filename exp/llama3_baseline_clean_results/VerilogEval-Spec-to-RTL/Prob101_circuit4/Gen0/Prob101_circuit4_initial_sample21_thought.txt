The TopModule appears to implement a combinational circuit where the output q is high whenever b is high or c is high, but if a is high, the output q is also high when c is high. This can be represented using a simple logical expression: q = (a && c) || b || c.
However, looking closer at the waveform, it can be observed that q is actually high whenever b is high or c is high, regardless of the value of a. The expression q = b || c accurately represents the circuit's behavior.
