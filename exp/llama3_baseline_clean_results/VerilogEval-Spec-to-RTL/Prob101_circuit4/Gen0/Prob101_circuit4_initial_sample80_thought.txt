The module TopModule seems to implement a majority function. It outputs 1 if the majority of the input bits (a, b, c, d) are 1. However, looking closely at the simulation waveforms, it seems that the circuit actually implements a simple OR gate between 'b', 'c' when 'a' is 0, and a simple OR gate between 'a', 'b', 'c' when 'a' is 1. This means that 'd' seems to have no effect on the output. But, analyzing the truth table closely, it seems that 'd' actually has an effect. The actual logic can be simplified to (a or b or c).
