To implement the Lemming machine, we will use a Moore state machine with three main states: walking, falling, and digging. We will also keep track of the direction of the Lemming.

If the ground disappears, the Lemming will enter the falling state and will say "aaah!". Once the ground reappears, the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.

When the dig input is high, the Lemming will start digging if it is currently walking on ground. The Lemming will continue digging until it reaches the other side, at which point it will fall and then continue walking in its original direction once it hits ground again.

We will use a always_ff block to implement the state machine, with the state and direction being registered on the positive edge of the clock. We will also use a always_comb block to determine the next state and the outputs based on the current state and inputs.
