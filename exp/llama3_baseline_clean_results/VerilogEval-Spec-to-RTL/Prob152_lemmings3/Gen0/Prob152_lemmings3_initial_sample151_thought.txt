To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to identify the states and transitions based on the given conditions. The states can be represented as follows:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling due to the absence of ground.
- DIGGING_LEFT: The Lemming is digging to the left.
- DIGGING_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging to the right.

The transitions between these states will be determined by the input conditions such as bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig.

The state machine will have the following transitions:
- From WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT when bump_left is high.
- From WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT when bump_right is high.
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALLING when ground is low.
- From FALLING to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT (depending on the previous walking direction) when ground is high.
- From WALK_LEFT to DIGGING_LEFT when dig is high and ground is high.
- From WALK_RIGHT to DIGGING_RIGHT when dig is high and ground is high.
- From DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT to FALLING when ground is low.
- From FALLING to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT (depending on the previous digging direction) when ground is high.

We will use a 3-bit state register to encode the five states, with two bits for the walking direction and one bit for the digging/falling status.
