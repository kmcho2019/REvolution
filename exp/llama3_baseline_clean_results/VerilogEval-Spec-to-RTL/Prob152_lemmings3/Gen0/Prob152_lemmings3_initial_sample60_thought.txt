The problem can be solved by implementing a Moore state machine with the following states:
- idle_left: The Lemming is walking left and waiting for an event to change its state.
- idle_right: The Lemming is walking right and waiting for an event to change its state.
- falling: The Lemming has fallen and is waiting for the ground to reappear.
- digging_left: The Lemming is digging to the left.
- digging_right: The Lemming is digging to the right.

Transitions between these states will be triggered by the input signals bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. 

The state machine will start in the idle_left state after areset is asserted.
