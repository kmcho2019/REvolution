- WALKING_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALKING_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling due to lack of ground.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.

When the ground reappears, the Lemming will transition from falling to walking. When the dig signal is high and the Lemming is walking on ground, it will transition to digging. When the ground disappears during digging, the Lemming will transition to falling. When the ground reappears, the Lemming will transition from falling to walking in its original direction.
