To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we'll identify the necessary states and transitions based on the given conditions. The states can be represented as follows:
- State 1: Walking Left (WL)
- State 2: Walking Right (WR)
- State 3: Falling (F)
- State 4: Digging (D)

Transitions between these states will be based on the input signals: bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig.

- When ground = 0, the Lemming will fall (transition from WL or WR to F).
- When ground = 1 and the Lemming is falling, it resumes walking in the same direction it was before falling.
- When dig = 1 and ground = 1, the Lemming starts digging (transition from WL or WR to D).
- While digging, the Lemming continues until it reaches the other side (ground = 0), at which point it falls (transition from D to F).
- Being bumped on the left or right side causes the Lemming to switch walking directions (transition from WL to WR or from WR to WL), unless it's falling or digging.

The outputs walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging will be determined based on the current state.

We will use areset to asynchronously reset the machine to the walking left state.
