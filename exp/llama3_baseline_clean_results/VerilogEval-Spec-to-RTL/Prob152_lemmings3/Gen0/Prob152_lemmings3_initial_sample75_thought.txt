To implement the Lemmings' behavior, we can use a Moore state machine with three states: walking left, walking right, and digging. We also need to keep track of the current direction and whether the Lemming is falling. When the ground disappears, the Lemming enters a falling state. When the ground reappears, the Lemming resumes walking in its original direction. If the Lemming is told to dig while walking, it starts digging and continues until it reaches the other side, at which point it starts falling. Being bumped while walking changes the direction, but being bumped while falling or digging has no effect. The areset signal resets the machine to the walking left state.
