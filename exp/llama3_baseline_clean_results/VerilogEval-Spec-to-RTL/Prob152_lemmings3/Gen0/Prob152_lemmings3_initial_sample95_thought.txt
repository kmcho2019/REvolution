To implement the Lemming behavior, we need to design a Moore state machine with the following states:
- Walking Left (WL)
- Walking Right (WR)
- Falling (F)
- Digging (D)

We need to consider the following transitions:
- When the Lemming is walking and bumps into an obstacle, it switches direction.
- When the Lemming is walking and the ground disappears, it starts falling.
- When the Lemming is falling and the ground reappears, it resumes walking in the same direction.
- When the Lemming is walking on ground and receives a dig signal, it starts digging.
- When the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it starts falling.
- When the Lemming is falling after digging and the ground reappears, it resumes walking in the original direction.

We also need to handle the case where multiple conditions are satisfied at the same time, following the priority rules: fall > dig > switch direction.
