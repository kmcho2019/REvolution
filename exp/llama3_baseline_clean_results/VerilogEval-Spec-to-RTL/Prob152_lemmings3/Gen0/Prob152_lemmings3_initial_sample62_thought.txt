The Moore state machine will be implemented with 5 states: walking_left, walking_right, falling, digging_left, and digging_right. The machine starts in the walking_left state upon reset. When the Lemming is walking, it will switch directions upon receiving a bump signal. If the ground disappears, the Lemming will enter the falling state. While falling, the Lemming will continue to fall until the ground reappears, at which point it will resume walking in the original direction. If the Lemming is told to dig while walking, it will enter the digging state and continue digging until it reaches the other side, at which point it will fall and then resume walking in the original direction. The outputs will be determined by the current state and the inputs.
