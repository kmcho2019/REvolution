To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we can define four states: 
- walking_left: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- walking_right: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- falling: The Lemming is falling due to the absence of ground.
- digging: The Lemming is digging into the ground.

We start in the walking_left state due to the areset signal. The machine changes states based on the bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig inputs. 
- If the Lemming hits an obstacle on either side (bump_left or bump_right), it switches direction.
- If the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming starts falling.
- If the Lemming is told to dig (dig=1) while it is walking on the ground, it starts digging.
- When the Lemming reaches the other side of the ground it is digging into (ground=0), it starts falling.
- Once the Lemming hits the ground again (ground=1), it resumes walking in its original direction.

We can use a two-bit state variable to represent these four states.
