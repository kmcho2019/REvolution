The Moore state machine will be designed with states representing the Lemming's actions: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. 
The machine starts in the walking left state after asynchronous reset.
State transitions will be based on the current state and the inputs.
The walking direction will change when the Lemming is bumped.
The Lemming will fall when the ground disappears.
The Lemming will dig when it receives the dig signal and is currently walking on ground.
The output signals will be generated based on the current state.
