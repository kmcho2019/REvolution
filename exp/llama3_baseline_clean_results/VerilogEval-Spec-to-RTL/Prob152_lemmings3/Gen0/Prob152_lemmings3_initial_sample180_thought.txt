To implement the Lemmings' behavior using a Moore state machine, we can define the states as follows:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING_LEFT: The Lemming is digging to the left.
- DIGGING_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging to the right.
The transitions between these states will be based on the input signals. We will use a combinational logic block to determine the next state based on the current state and the input signals. The output signals (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, digging) will be generated based on the current state.

When the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will switch to the FALLING state from any other state. When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will switch to the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state depending on its previous walking direction.

When the Lemming is told to dig (dig=1), it will switch to the DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT state depending on its current walking direction. It will continue digging until it reaches the other side (ground=0), at which point it will switch to the FALLING state.

Being bumped while falling or digging will not affect the walking direction, and being told to dig when falling or when there is no ground will be ignored.
