To implement the TopModule as a Moore state machine, we first need to identify the different states the Lemming can be in: 
1. Walking Left
2. Walking Right
3. Falling
4. Digging
We will use a 2-bit state variable to represent these states. 
When the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will transition from walking or digging to falling. 
When the ground reappears (ground=1) and the Lemming is falling, it will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. 
When the Lemming is told to dig (dig=1) and it's walking on the ground, it will start digging. 
The Lemming will continue digging until it reaches the other side (ground=0), at which point it will fall and then resume walking in its original direction when the ground reappears.
Being bumped while falling or digging has no effect on the walking direction, and being told to dig when falling or when there is no ground is ignored.
