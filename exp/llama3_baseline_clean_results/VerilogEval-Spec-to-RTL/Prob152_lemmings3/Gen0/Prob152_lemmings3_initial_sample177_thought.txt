The Moore state machine should have at least three states: walking, falling, and digging. 
The walking state can be further divided into walking left and walking right. 
Transitions between states should be based on the given input conditions. 
If ground=0, the Lemming should transition from walking to falling. 
If dig=1, the Lemming should transition from walking to digging. 
If both bump_left and bump_right are high, the walking direction should be switched. 
Being bumped while falling or digging should not affect the walking direction. 
When the ground reappears (ground=1) while falling, the Lemming should resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.
When the ground disappears (ground=0) while digging, the Lemming should fall and then continue walking in the original direction once it hits ground again.
