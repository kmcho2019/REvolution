The Moore state machine will have the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
The state machine will change states based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig). 
When the ground signal goes low, the Lemming will fall. When the dig signal is high, the Lemming will start digging if it's walking on the ground. 
When the Lemming hits the ground again, it will resume walking in its original direction. 
Being bumped while falling or digging will not affect the walking direction. 
The Moore state machine will have outputs for walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging.
