To model the behavior of the Lemming, we can define a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- IDLE_LEFT: walking left
- IDLE_RIGHT: walking right
- FALLING: Lemming is falling
- DIGGING: Lemming is digging

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. 
- When the Lemming is walking (IDLE_LEFT or IDLE_RIGHT), it will switch direction if it hits an obstacle (bump_left or bump_right), fall if the ground disappears (ground=0), or start digging if it's told to dig (dig=1) and there is ground (ground=1).
- When the Lemming is falling (FALLING), it will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall when the ground reappears (ground=1).
- When the Lemming is digging (DIGGING), it will continue digging until it reaches the other side (ground=0), at which point it will fall (aaah!) and then continue walking in its original direction once it hits ground again.

We will use a two-stage approach to handle the case where multiple conditions are satisfied at the same time. 
First, we will check for falling, then for digging, and finally for switching directions. 
This ensures that the higher precedence conditions are handled before the lower precedence ones.
