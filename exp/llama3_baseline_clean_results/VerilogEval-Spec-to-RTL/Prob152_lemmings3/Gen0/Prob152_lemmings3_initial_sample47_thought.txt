To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we first need to identify the states the Lemming can be in. The main states are walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. Since the Lemming remembers its last walking direction even when it falls or digs, we need to incorporate this memory into our states. 

We can model the behavior using the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALL_LEFT: The Lemming is falling, and its last walking direction was left.
- FALL_RIGHT: The Lemming is falling, and its last walking direction was right.
- DIG_LEFT: The Lemming is digging, and its last walking direction was left.
- DIG_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging, and its last walking direction was right.

We then determine the transitions between these states based on the inputs provided (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig).

Next, we consider the outputs of the state machine. The walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging outputs can be determined based on the current state of the machine.
