To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we will define several states to capture the different activities of the Lemming: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The transitions between these states will be determined by the input signals.

The states will be:
- WALK_LEFT: Lemming is walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: Lemming is walking right
- FALLING: Lemming is falling
- DIGGING: Lemming is digging

The output signals will be determined by the current state. 

Transitions will be based on the input signals. For example, if the Lemming is walking left and bump_left is high, it will transition to walking right. If the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will transition to falling. If the Lemming is walking and dig is high, it will transition to digging.

We will also consider the priority of the actions: falling has higher precedence than digging, which has higher precedence than switching directions.
