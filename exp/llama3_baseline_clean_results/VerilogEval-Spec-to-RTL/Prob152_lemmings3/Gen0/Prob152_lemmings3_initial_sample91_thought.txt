To model the behavior of the Lemming, we can design a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: the Lemming is walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: the Lemming is walking right
- FALL: the Lemming is falling
- DIG: the Lemming is digging

We will use the current state and the inputs to determine the next state and the output signals. 
When the Lemming is walking and hits an obstacle, it will switch direction. 
When the ground disappears, the Lemming will fall. 
When the Lemming is told to dig, it will start digging if it is currently walking on the ground. 
Being bumped while falling or digging has no effect. 
Being told to dig when falling or when there is no ground is ignored.
