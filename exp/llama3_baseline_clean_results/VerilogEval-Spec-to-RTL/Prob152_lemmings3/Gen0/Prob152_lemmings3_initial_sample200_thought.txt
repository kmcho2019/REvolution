To implement the TopModule, we will design a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- idle_left: The Lemming is walking left and is not digging or falling.
- idle_right: The Lemming is walking right and is not digging or falling.
- digging: The Lemming is digging and will continue until it reaches the other side.
- falling: The Lemming is falling due to lack of ground or because it has reached the other side while digging.

We will use a binary encoding for the states: 
- idle_left: 2'b00
- idle_right: 2'b01
- digging: 2'b10
- falling: 2'b11

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. For example, if the Lemming is walking left and bumps into an obstacle on the right, it will switch to walking right. If the ground disappears while the Lemming is walking, it will start falling. If the Lemming is told to dig while walking on ground, it will start digging.

The outputs will be generated based on the current state. For example, if the Lemming is walking left, walk_left will be 1 and walk_right will be 0. If the Lemming is falling, aaah will be 1.
