To implement the Lemming behavior, we will create a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right
- FALLING: falling
- DIGGING_LEFT: digging while walking left
- DIGGING_RIGHT: digging while walking right

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) and the current state.

When the Lemming is walking (WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT) and the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state. 
When the Lemming is falling and the ground reappears, it will transition back to the state it was in before falling (WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT).

When the Lemming is walking and dig is high, it will transition to the corresponding digging state (DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT). 
When the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state and then continue walking in the original direction when the ground reappears.

The output signals (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, digging) will be determined by the current state.
