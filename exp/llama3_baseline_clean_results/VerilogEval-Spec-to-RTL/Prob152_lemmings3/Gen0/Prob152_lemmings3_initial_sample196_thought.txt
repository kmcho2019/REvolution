To implement the Lemming behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to identify the states and transitions based on the given conditions. 

1. States: 
   - WALK_LEFT: Walking left
   - WALK_RIGHT: Walking right
   - FALLING: Falling (aaah!)
   - DIGGING: Digging

2. Transitions:
   - From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALLING: when ground=0
   - From FALLING to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT: when ground=1, and depending on the previous walking direction
   - From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to DIGGING: when dig=1 and ground=1
   - From DIGGING to FALLING: when ground=0
   - Direction switch: when bumped on either side

The Moore state machine will have output signals that depend only on the current state, which are walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging. 

We will use two registers to store the current state and the previous walking direction.
