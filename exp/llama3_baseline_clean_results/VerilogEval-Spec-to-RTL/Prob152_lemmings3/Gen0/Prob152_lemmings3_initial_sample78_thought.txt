The Moore state machine for this problem will have several states to keep track of the Lemming's current activity (walking, falling, digging) and direction. The states can be defined as follows:
- IDLE_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left on the ground.
- IDLE_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right on the ground.
- FALLING: The Lemming has fallen and is in free fall.
- DIGGING_LEFT: The Lemming is digging to the left.
- DIGGING_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging to the right.

The transitions between these states will be based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig).

The outputs (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, digging) will be determined by the current state of the machine.
