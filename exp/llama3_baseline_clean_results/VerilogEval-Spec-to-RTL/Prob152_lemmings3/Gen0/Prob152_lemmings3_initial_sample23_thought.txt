We'll also need to keep track of the previous direction of the Lemming to resume walking in that direction after a fall or digging. 

The state machine will have the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. For example, if the Lemming is walking left and receives a bump on the left, it will transition to the WALK_RIGHT state. If the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state. If the dig signal is high and the Lemming is walking, it will transition to the DIGGING state.

We'll use a always_ff block to implement the state machine, and assign the output signals based on the current state.
