To implement the Lemming behavior, we will design a Moore finite state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on the ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on the ground
- FALLING: falling due to lack of ground
- DIGGING: digging into the ground
- FALLING_AFTER_DIGGING: falling after digging through the ground

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) and will output the corresponding signals (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, digging).

We will use a 3-bit signal to represent the current state of the Lemming. The state machine will update its state on the positive edge of the clock signal.

In the WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states, the Lemming will switch direction if it hits an obstacle (bump_left or bump_right). If the ground disappears (ground = 0), the Lemming will transition to the FALLING state.

In the FALLING state, the Lemming will continue to fall and say "aaah!" until the ground reappears (ground = 1), at which point it will transition back to the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state, depending on the previous direction.

In the WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states, if the dig signal is high, the Lemming will transition to the DIGGING state. In the DIGGING state, the Lemming will continue digging until it reaches the other side (ground = 0), at which point it will transition to the FALLING_AFTER_DIGGING state.

In the FALLING_AFTER_DIGGING state, the Lemming will continue to fall and say "aaah!" until the ground reappears (ground = 1), at which point it will transition back to the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state, depending on the previous direction.

The asynchronous reset signal (areset) will reset the state machine to the WALK_LEFT state.
