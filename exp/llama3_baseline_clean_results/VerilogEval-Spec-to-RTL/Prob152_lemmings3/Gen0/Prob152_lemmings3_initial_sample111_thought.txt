To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we can define states for walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. We'll use a reset state to initialize the machine to walk left when the asynchronous reset signal (areset) is high. The state transitions will be based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig) and the current state. The output signals (walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging) will be determined by the current state. We'll prioritize the conditions in the following order: ground, dig, and then direction change. This means that if the Lemming is falling, it will fall regardless of other conditions. If it's walking and told to dig, it will dig, and if it's walking and not digging, it will change direction based on bump signals.
