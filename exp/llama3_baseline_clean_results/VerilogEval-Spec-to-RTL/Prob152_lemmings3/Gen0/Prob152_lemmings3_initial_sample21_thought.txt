To implement the TopModule as a Moore state machine, we need to define the states and the transitions between them based on the given conditions. The states can be defined as follows:
- IDLE_LEFT: walking left
- IDLE_RIGHT: walking right
- FALLING: falling
- DIGGING_LEFT: digging while walking left
- DIGGING_RIGHT: digging while walking right

The transitions between the states will be based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig). When the Lemming is bumped, it will switch directions. When there is no ground, the Lemming will fall. When the Lemming is told to dig and there is ground, it will start digging. When the Lemming is digging and there is no ground, it will fall and then continue walking in its original direction once it hits ground again.
