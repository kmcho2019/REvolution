The Moore state machine will have three main states: walking, falling, and digging. The walking state can be further divided into walking left and walking right. The state transitions will be based on the input signals bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. 

When the Lemming is walking and ground=0, it will transition to the falling state. When it's falling and ground=1, it will transition back to the walking state in the same direction as before. 

When the Lemming is walking and dig=1, it will transition to the digging state. While digging, it will continue to dig until ground=0, at which point it will transition to the falling state. When it hits ground again, it will transition back to the walking state in the original direction. 

Being bumped while walking will switch the direction. Being bumped while falling or digging will not affect the walking direction.

The state machine will also have an asynchronous reset signal areset, which will reset the state machine to the walking left state.
