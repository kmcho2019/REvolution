When the ground disappears (ground=0), the FSM will transition to the falling state and output "aaah!". The FSM will remain in this state until the ground reappears (ground=1), at which point it will transition back to the walking state and resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.

The digging state will be entered when the Lemming is walking and receives a dig signal (dig=1). The FSM will continue digging until it reaches the other side (ground=0), at which point it will fall and then continue walking in its original direction once it hits ground again.
