To implement the behavior of the Lemming, we will design a Moore state machine with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on the ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on the ground
- FALLING: falling without ground
- DIGGING: digging into the ground
- DIGGING_FALLING: falling after digging through the ground

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals.
When the Lemming is on the ground, it can transition between WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT based on the bump_left and bump_right signals.
When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition to the FALLING state.
When the dig signal is asserted, the Lemming will transition to the DIGGING state if it is on the ground.
When the Lemming is digging and reaches the other side, it will transition to the DIGGING_FALLING state and then to the FALLING state.
When the ground reappears, the Lemming will transition back to the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state based on its previous direction.

The output signals will be generated based on the current state of the Lemming.
