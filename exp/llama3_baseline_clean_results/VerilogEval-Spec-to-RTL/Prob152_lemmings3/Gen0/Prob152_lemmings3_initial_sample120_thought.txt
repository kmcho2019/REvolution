To implement the Moore state machine that models the Lemming's behavior, we need to identify the states and the conditions for transitioning between them. The states can be: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. We also need to consider the precedence of the conditions: falling has higher precedence than digging, which has higher precedence than switching directions. We will use a finite state machine with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right
- FALLING: falling
- DIGGING: digging

The transitions between states will be based on the input signals: bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. We will use areset to reset the machine to the WALK_LEFT state.
