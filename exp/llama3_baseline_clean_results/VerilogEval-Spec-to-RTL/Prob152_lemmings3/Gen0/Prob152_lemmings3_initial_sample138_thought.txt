To implement the Lemmings' behavior using a Moore state machine, we will define the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- FALLING: falling when there is no ground
- DIGGING: digging when told to and on ground

We will use two state variables to encode these four states (two bits per state variable is needed to encode four states).

When the Lemming is in the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state and is bumped, we transition to the opposite walking state. 
When the Lemming is in the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state and the ground disappears, we transition to the FALLING state.
When the Lemming is in the FALLING state and the ground reappears, we transition to the original walking state before falling.
When the Lemming is in the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state and is told to dig, we transition to the DIGGING state.
When the Lemming is in the DIGGING state and the ground disappears, we transition to the FALLING state.
When the Lemming is in the FALLING state after digging and the ground reappears, we transition to the original walking state before digging.

We use the bump_left and bump_right signals to switch walking directions.
We use the ground signal to determine whether to fall or not.
We use the dig signal to determine whether to dig or not.

We output walk_left and walk_right based on the current state, aaah when in the FALLING state, and digging when in the DIGGING state.
