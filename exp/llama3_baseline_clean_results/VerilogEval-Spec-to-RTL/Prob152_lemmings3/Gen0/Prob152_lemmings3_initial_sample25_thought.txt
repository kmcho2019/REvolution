The Moore state machine can be designed to have three main states: walking left, walking right, and falling. The walking states can be further divided into two sub-states: walking and digging. When ground=0, the machine will transition to the falling state. When dig=1 and the Lemming is in the walking state, it will transition to the digging state. The machine will resume walking in the same direction when the ground reappears. The walking direction will be switched when a bump is detected.
