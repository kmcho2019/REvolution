The problem can be solved by designing a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on ground
- FALLING: falling due to lack of ground
- DIG_LEFT: digging while moving left
- DIG_RIGHT: digging while moving right

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. 
For example, when the Lemming is walking left and hits an obstacle on the left, it will transition to the WALK_RIGHT state.
If the Lemming is walking and the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state.
When the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state and continue walking in the original direction when it hits the ground again.
The outputs will be generated based on the current state.
