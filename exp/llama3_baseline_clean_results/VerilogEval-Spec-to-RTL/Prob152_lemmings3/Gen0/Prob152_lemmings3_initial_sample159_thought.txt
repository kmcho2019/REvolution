To implement the Lemmings' behavior using a Moore state machine, we will have four main states: 
1) walking left (walk_left = 1), 
2) walking right (walk_right = 1), 
3) falling (aaah = 1), and 
4) digging (digging = 1).

We start in the walking left state. The state transitions will be triggered by the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig). 
- When the Lemming is bumped on the left, it switches to walking right.
- When the Lemming is bumped on the right, it switches to walking left.
- When the ground disappears (ground = 0), the Lemming starts falling (aaah = 1).
- When the ground reappears (ground = 1) and the Lemming was walking, it resumes walking in the same direction.
- When the Lemming is told to dig (dig = 1) and it is currently walking on ground, it starts digging.
- When the Lemming reaches the other side while digging (ground = 0), it starts falling.

The outputs walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging will be determined by the current state of the state machine.
