To implement the Lemmings' behavior using a Moore state machine, we can define four states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, and DIGGING. The machine starts in WALK_LEFT state and transitions to WALK_RIGHT when bumped on the left, and vice versa. When the ground disappears, the machine transitions to FALLING. While falling, it ignores bumps and only resumes walking when the ground reappears. If the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it transitions to FALLING. If the Lemming is told to dig while walking on ground, it transitions to DIGGING and continues digging until the ground disappears.
