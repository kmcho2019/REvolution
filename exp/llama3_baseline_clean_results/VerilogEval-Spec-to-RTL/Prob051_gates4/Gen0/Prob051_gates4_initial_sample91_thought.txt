To implement the TopModule, we will use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators to create a 4-input AND gate, OR gate, and XOR gate. The AND gate will output 1 only if all four inputs are 1. The OR gate will output 1 if any of the four inputs are 1. The XOR gate will output 1 if an odd number of inputs are 1. We will assign the results of these operations to the output ports out_and, out_or, and out_xor respectively.
