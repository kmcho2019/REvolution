To implement the TopModule, we need to design a combinational circuit that performs 4-input AND, OR, and XOR operations. We can use the built-in bitwise operators in Verilog to achieve this. The AND operation will produce an output of 1 only if all inputs are 1. The OR operation will produce an output of 1 if any input is 1.
