To implement the TopModule, we can use Verilog's built-in logical operators to create the required AND, OR, and XOR gates. The AND operator (&) will be used to create the 4-input AND gate, the OR operator (|) will be used for the 4-input OR gate, and the XOR operator (^) will be used for the 4-input XOR gate. We can then assign the results of these operations to the corresponding output ports.
