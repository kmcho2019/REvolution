To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to create a 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gate. The AND gate will output 1 only if all inputs are 1. The OR gate will output 1 if at least one input is 1. The XOR gate will output 1 if an odd number of inputs are 1. We will assign the results of these operations to the corresponding output ports.
