The problem requires designing a Verilog module named TopModule that implements a combinational circuit. This circuit will have four inputs (in[3:0]) and three outputs (out_and, out_or, out_xor) corresponding to the results of a 4-input AND gate, a 4-input OR gate, and a 4-input XOR gate respectively. The design will directly instantiate these logic operations using Verilog's built-in logical operators (& for AND, | for OR, ^ for XOR) and assign the results to the respective output ports.
