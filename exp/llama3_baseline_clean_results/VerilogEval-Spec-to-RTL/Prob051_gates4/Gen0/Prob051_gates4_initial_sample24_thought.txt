To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators in Verilog. 
The AND operator (&) will be used to implement the 4-input AND gate, 
the OR operator (|) will be used for the 4-input OR gate, and 
the XOR operator (^) will be used for the 4-input XOR gate. 
We will apply these operators directly on the input bits in[3:0] 
to generate the outputs out_and, out_or, and out_xor.
