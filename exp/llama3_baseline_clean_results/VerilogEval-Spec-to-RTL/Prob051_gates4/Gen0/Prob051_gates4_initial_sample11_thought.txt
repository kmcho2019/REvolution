To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog for AND, OR, and XOR operations. The AND operation will be performed on all bits of the input in, and the result will be assigned to out_and. The OR operation will be performed on all bits of the input in, and the result will be assigned to out_or. The XOR operation will be performed on all bits of the input in, and the result will be assigned to out_xor.
