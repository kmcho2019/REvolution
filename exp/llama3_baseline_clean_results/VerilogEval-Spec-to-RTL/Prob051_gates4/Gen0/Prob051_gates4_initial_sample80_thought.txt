To implement the TopModule, we need to design a combinational circuit with four inputs (in[3:0]) and three outputs (out_and, out_or, out_xor). The outputs will be driven by a 4-input AND gate, a 4-input OR gate, and a 4-input XOR gate, respectively. We can achieve this by using the bitwise AND (&), OR (|), and XOR (^) operators in Verilog.
