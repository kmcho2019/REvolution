The TopModule can be designed by instantiating the AND, OR, and XOR gates directly in the module. Since Verilog supports bitwise operations on vectors, we can directly apply these operations on the input vector 'in' to produce the desired outputs. The AND operation (&) will produce 1 only if all bits of 'in' are 1, the OR operation (|) will produce 1 if at least one bit of 'in' is 1, and the XOR operation (^) will produce 1 if an odd number of bits in 'in' are 1.
