To implement the TopModule, we can directly instantiate the logic gates required for each output. 
For out_and, all inputs must be high (1) to produce an output of 1. 
For out_or, any input being high (1) will produce an output of 1. 
For out_xor, an odd number of high (1) inputs will produce an output of 1.
We can use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators (&, |, ^) to implement the AND, OR, and XOR logic gates respectively.
