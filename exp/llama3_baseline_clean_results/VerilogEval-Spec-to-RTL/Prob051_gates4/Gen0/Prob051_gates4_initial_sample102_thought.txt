To implement the TopModule, we need to create a combinational circuit that takes four 1-bit inputs (in[3:0]) and produces three outputs: out_and, out_or, and out_xor. The out_and output will be the result of a 4-input AND gate, the out_or output will be the result of a 4-input OR gate, and the out_xor output will be the result of a 4-input XOR gate. We can directly implement these logic gates in Verilog using the corresponding operators (& for AND, | for OR, and ^ for XOR).
