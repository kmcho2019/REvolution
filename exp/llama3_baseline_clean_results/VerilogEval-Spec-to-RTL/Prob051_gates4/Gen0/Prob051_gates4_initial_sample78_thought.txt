To implement the TopModule, we will use basic logical operators in Verilog. 
The AND operator (&) will be used for the 4-input AND gate, 
the OR operator (|) will be used for the 4-input OR gate, 
and the XOR operator (^) will be used for the 4-input XOR gate.
However, since the XOR operator in Verilog works on two operands, 
we need to apply it in a cascaded manner for a 4-input XOR gate.
Alternatively, for a 4-input XOR gate, we can also use the reduction XOR operator (^).
The reduction operators in Verilog work on a vector of bits.
