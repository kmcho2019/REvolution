To implement the TopModule, we can directly utilize Verilog's built-in logical operators for bitwise operations. The AND operation (&) will be used for the out_and output, the OR operation (|) will be used for the out_or output, and the XOR operation (^) will be used for the out_xor output. However, Verilog does not have a direct 4-input XOR operation. Instead, we can use a combination of XOR operations to achieve the same result. 
We will first perform XOR operations between pairs of inputs and then perform an XOR operation on the results. 
For the 4-input AND and OR gates, we can simply extend the operators to all bits of the input.
