To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to perform the AND, OR, and XOR operations. The output of each logical operation will be assigned to the corresponding output port. 
For the AND operation, we will use the '&' operator. 
For the OR operation, we will use the '|' operator. 
For the XOR operation, we will use the '^' operator.
We will use a wire to connect the output of the logical operations to the output ports.
