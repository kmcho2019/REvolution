To implement the TopModule, we will first create a D flip-flop that is triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate, where one input is the 'in' signal and the other input is the output 'out' of the flip-flop itself. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is fed back into the XOR gate. The output of the flip-flop will be connected to the 'out' port of the module.
