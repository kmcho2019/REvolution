To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that captures its input on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the flip-flop will be the output of a 2-input XOR gate, where one input is the 'in' signal and the other input is the 'out' signal of the flip-flop itself, creating a feedback loop. This means we need to instantiate a D flip-flop and an XOR gate within the module. The output of the flip-flop will be the 'out' signal.
