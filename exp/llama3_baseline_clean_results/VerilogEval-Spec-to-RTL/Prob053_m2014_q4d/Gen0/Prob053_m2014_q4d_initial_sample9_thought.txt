To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the flow of signals. The 'in' signal and the 'out' signal of the flip-flop are fed into a two-input XOR gate. The output of the XOR gate is then used as the input to a D flip-flop, which is positive edge triggered by 'clk'. The output of the D flip-flop is connected back to one of the inputs of the XOR gate and also serves as the 'out' signal of the TopModule. This creates a feedback loop where the previous state of the flip-flop affects the next state through the XOR operation with the 'in' signal.
