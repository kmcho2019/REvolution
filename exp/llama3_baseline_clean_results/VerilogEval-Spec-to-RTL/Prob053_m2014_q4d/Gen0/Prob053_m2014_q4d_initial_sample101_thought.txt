To implement the TopModule, we will first define a D flip-flop that is positive edge triggered by the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the D flip-flop will be the output of a 2-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as its inputs. Since 'out' is the output of the D flip-flop, we need to use a reg variable to store its value and update it at the positive edge of 'clk'.
