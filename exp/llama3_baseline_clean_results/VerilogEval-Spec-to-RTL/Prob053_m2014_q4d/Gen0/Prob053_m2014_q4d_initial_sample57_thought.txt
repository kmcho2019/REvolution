To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the components involved. We have a D flip-flop that is positive edge triggered by the clock signal 'clk', and it has no reset. The input to this flip-flop is the output of a 2-input XOR gate. One input of the XOR gate is the 'in' signal, and the other input is the output 'out' of the flip-flop itself, creating a feedback loop. We will use a reg to hold the output of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of 'clk'. We will also use a wire to connect the output of the XOR gate to the input of the flip-flop.
