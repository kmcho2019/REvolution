To implement the TopModule, we can use a D flip-flop and a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs and its output will be connected to the D input of the flip-flop. The flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of 'clk' and its output will be 'out'. Since the flip-flop's output is also an input to the XOR gate, this creates a feedback loop.
