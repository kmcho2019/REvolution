To implement the TopModule, we need to design a D flip-flop that takes the output of a two-input XOR gate as its input. The XOR gate will take 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop as its inputs. We can use a reg variable to store the output of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The output of the flip-flop will be connected to one of the inputs of the XOR gate, creating a feedback loop. We can use the XOR operator (^) in Verilog to implement the XOR gate.
