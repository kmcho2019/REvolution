To implement the TopModule, we will use a D flip-flop and a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs and produce an output that will be fed into the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of 'clk' and will update its output 'out' accordingly. Since the output of the flip-flop is also an input to the XOR gate, we will use a reg variable to store the output of the flip-flop and assign it to 'out' to avoid a feedback loop in the combinational logic.
