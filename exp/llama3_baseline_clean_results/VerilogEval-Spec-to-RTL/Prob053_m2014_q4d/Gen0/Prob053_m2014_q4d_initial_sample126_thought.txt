To implement the TopModule, we need to design a D flip-flop that takes the output of a two-input XOR gate as its input. The XOR gate should have 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop as its inputs. Since the flip-flop is positive edge triggered by 'clk', we will use a reg to store the output of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of 'clk'. The output of the flip-flop should be connected to one of the inputs of the XOR gate, creating a feedback loop.
