To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that is positive edge triggered by the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the D flip-flop will be the output of a 2-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and the output of the D flip-flop 'out' as its inputs. We can use a reg to hold the state of the D flip-flop and update it at each positive edge of the clock. The output of the D flip-flop will be connected to the output 'out'.
