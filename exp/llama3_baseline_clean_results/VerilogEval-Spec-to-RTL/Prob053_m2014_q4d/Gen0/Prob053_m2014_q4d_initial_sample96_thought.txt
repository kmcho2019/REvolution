To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that captures the output of a two-input XOR gate on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The XOR gate takes 'in' and the output of the flip-flop 'out' as its inputs. We can use a reg to hold the output of the flip-flop and assign it in a always block triggered by the positive edge of 'clk'. The XOR operation can be performed using the '^' operator in Verilog.
