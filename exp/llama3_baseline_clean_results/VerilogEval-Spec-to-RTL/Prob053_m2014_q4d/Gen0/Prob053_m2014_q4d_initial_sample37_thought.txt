To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the functionality described. The module includes a D flip-flop that captures its input on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this D flip-flop is the result of an XOR operation between the 'in' signal and the 'out' signal of the flip-flop itself. This means we need to feedback the 'out' signal of the flip-flop to one of the inputs of the XOR gate. The other input of the XOR gate is the 'in' signal from the module's input port.
