To implement the TopModule, we need to instantiate a D flip-flop and a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs and its output will be connected to the input of the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of 'clk' and its output will be connected back to one of the inputs of the XOR gate and also to the output 'out' of the TopModule.
