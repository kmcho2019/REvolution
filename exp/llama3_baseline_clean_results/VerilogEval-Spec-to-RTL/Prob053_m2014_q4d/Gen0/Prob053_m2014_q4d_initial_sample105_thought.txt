To implement the TopModule, we will first define the internal signals and the XOR operation. The XOR operation will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs. The result of the XOR operation will be used as the input to the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be positive edge triggered by 'clk' and its output will be connected back to the XOR gate, creating a feedback loop. This way, the output of the flip-flop will be updated on every positive edge of 'clk' based on the current input 'in' and the previous output 'out'.
