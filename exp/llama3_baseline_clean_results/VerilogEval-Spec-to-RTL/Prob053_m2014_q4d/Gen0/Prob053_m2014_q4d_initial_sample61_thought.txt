To implement the TopModule, we will use a combination of a D flip-flop and an XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs and produce an output that will be fed into the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of 'clk' and will update its output 'out' based on the input from the XOR gate. Since there is no reset, we will use a simple D flip-flop without any reset logic.
