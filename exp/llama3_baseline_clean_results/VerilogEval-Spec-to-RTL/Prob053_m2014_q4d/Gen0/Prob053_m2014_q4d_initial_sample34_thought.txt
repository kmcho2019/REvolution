To implement the TopModule, we need to instantiate a D flip-flop and an XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and 'out' as inputs and its output will be connected to the input of the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be clocked by 'clk' and its output will be 'out'. Since the flip-flop is positive edge triggered, we can use the posedge keyword in the always block to capture the input at the rising edge of the clock.
