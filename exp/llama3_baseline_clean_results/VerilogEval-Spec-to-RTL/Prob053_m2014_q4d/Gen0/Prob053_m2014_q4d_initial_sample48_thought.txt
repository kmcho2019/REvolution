To implement the TopModule, we will first define a D flip-flop that is triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate, where one input is the 'in' signal and the other input is the 'out' signal of the flip-flop. This forms a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is used as one of the inputs to the XOR gate. The output of the flip-flop will be the 'out' signal.
