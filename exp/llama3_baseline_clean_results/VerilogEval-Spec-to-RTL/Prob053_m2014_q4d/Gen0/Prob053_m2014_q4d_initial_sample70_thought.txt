To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand its components and their connections. The module consists of a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and has no reset. The input to the D flip-flop comes from a two-input XOR gate, where one input is the 'in' signal and the other input is the 'out' signal of the flip-flop itself, creating a feedback loop. This setup implies that the output of the flip-flop is fed back into the XOR gate, which then influences the next state of the flip-flop based on the current 'in' and previous 'out'.

Given this, we will use a reg to hold the output of the flip-flop (out) and wire for the XOR output. The always block will be used to capture the positive edge of the clk and update the flip-flop's output accordingly.
