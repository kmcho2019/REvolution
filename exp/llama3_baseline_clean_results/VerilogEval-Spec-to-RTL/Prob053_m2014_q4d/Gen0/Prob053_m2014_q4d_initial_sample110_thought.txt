To implement the TopModule, we need to design a D flip-flop that captures the output of a two-input XOR gate on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The XOR gate takes 'in' and the output of the flip-flop 'out' as inputs. Since there is no reset, we will use a simple D flip-flop without a reset input. The output of the XOR gate will be the input to the D flip-flop.
