To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that captures the output of a two-input XOR gate on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The XOR gate should take 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop as its inputs. Since there is no reset, we can use a simple D flip-flop without a reset input. The output of the flip-flop will be fed back into the XOR gate to create a feedback loop.
