To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate, where one input is the 'in' signal and the other input is the 'out' signal of the flip-flop itself. This means the output of the flip-flop will be fed back into the XOR gate. We can use a reg to hold the output of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of 'clk'. The XOR operation can be implemented using the '^' operator in Verilog.
