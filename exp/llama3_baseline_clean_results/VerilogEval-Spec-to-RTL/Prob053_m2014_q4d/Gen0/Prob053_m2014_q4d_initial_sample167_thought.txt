To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that takes its input from a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate should take 'in' and the flip-flop's output 'out' as its inputs. Since the flip-flop is positive edge-triggered by 'clk', we will use the always @(posedge clk) block in Verilog to update the flip-flop's output at every positive edge of 'clk'.
