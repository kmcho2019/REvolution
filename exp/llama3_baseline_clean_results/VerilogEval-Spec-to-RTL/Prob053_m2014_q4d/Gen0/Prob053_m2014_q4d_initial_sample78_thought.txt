To implement the TopModule, we need to design a D flip-flop that is triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and has no reset. The input to the flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take 'in' and the output of the flip-flop 'out' as its inputs. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is used as one of the inputs to the XOR gate, which in turn affects the input to the flip-flop on the next clock cycle.
