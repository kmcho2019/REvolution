To implement the TopModule, we will first create a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the D flip-flop will be the result of the XOR operation between 'in' and the output of the flip-flop 'out'. This means we will have a feedback loop from the output of the flip-flop to one of the inputs of the XOR gate. The other input of the XOR gate will be 'in'. The output of the XOR gate will be the input to the D flip-flop. We'll use the Verilog keyword 'reg' to declare the output 'out' and use the non-blocking assignment operator '<=' inside a 'always' block to update 'out' at the positive edge of 'clk'.
