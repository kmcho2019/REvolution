To implement the TopModule, we will first define a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate. One input of the XOR gate will be the 'in' signal, and the other input will be the 'out' signal, which is also the output of the D flip-flop. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is used as one of the inputs to the XOR gate, whose output in turn feeds back into the flip-flop.
