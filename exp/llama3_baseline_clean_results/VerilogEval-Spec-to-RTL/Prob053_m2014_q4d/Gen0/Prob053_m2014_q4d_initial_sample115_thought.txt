To implement the TopModule, we need to follow these steps:
1. Define the D flip-flop with 'out' as its output and 'in' XOR 'out' as its input.
2. The D flip-flop should be triggered on the positive edge of 'clk'.
3. Use a reg variable to store the output of the flip-flop.
4. Use a continuous assignment to compute the input of the flip-flop.
5. Use an always block to update the output of the flip-flop at the positive edge of 'clk'.
