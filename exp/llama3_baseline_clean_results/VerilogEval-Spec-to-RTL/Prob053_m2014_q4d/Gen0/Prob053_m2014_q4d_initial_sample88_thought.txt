The design requires a D flip-flop that captures its input on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the D flip-flop is the result of a two-input XOR operation between the 'in' signal and the 'out' signal, which is also the output of the flip-flop. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is used as one of the inputs to the XOR gate, whose output then updates the flip-flop on the next clock edge. We will use the built-in Verilog operators for XOR and implement the D flip-flop using a reg and an always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock signal.
