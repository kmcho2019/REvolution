To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that captures the output of a two-input XOR gate on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The XOR gate will take 'in' and the output of the flip-flop 'out' as its inputs. Since the output of the flip-flop is also an input to the XOR gate, we will need to use a wire to connect the output of the flip-flop to the XOR gate. We can use a reg to hold the output of the flip-flop and assign it to the output 'out'. The XOR operation can be performed using the ^ operator in Verilog.
