To implement the TopModule, we can use a D flip-flop (DFF) and a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate will take the input 'in' and the output 'out' of the DFF, and its output will be connected to the input of the DFF. The DFF will be positive edge triggered by the 'clk' signal. Since there is no reset, we will not include a reset port in the DFF.
