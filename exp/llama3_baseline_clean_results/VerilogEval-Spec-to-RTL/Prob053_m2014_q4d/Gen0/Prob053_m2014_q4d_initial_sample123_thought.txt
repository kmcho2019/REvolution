To implement the TopModule, we will first create a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate. One input to the XOR gate will be the 'in' signal, and the other input will be the output 'out' of the flip-flop itself, creating a feedback loop. This setup ensures that the flip-flop's output is used in the calculation of its next state, based on the current input 'in' and its previous output.
