To implement the TopModule, we first need to understand the components involved and how they interact. The circuit consists of a D flip-flop and a two-input XOR gate. The XOR gate takes 'in' and 'out' as inputs, and its output is connected to the input of the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop is positive edge triggered by 'clk', meaning it updates its output (which is 'out') with the value at its input (the output of the XOR gate) at the positive edge of 'clk'. Since there's no reset, we don't need to consider any reset logic. The key here is to understand the timing and how 'out' affects itself through the XOR gate and the flip-flop on each clock cycle.
