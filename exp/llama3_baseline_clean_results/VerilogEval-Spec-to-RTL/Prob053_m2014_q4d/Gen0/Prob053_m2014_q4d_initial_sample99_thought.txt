We can use a reg variable to hold the state of the flip-flop and use a always block to trigger the flip-flop on the positive edge of 'clk'. 
The output of the XOR gate will be calculated using the XOR operator (^) between 'in' and 'out'. 
We can then assign the output of the XOR gate to the input of the flip-flop and update the state of the flip-flop on the positive edge of 'clk'.
