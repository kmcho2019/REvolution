To implement the TopModule, we will first define the XOR gate that takes 'in' and the output 'out' of the D flip-flop. The output of the XOR gate will then be connected to the input of the D flip-flop. The D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. Since there is no reset, the flip-flop will retain its previous state when not triggered. We will use a reg to store the output of the flip-flop and update it on the positive edge of the clock.
