To implement the TopModule, we will first describe the XOR gate that takes 'in' and 'out' as inputs. Then, we will instantiate a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of 'clk' and has the output of the XOR gate as its input. The output of the flip-flop will be connected back to the XOR gate, creating a feedback loop, and also to the 'out' port of the module.
