To implement the TopModule, we can create a D flip-flop that captures its input on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the D flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate, where one input is the external signal 'in' and the other input is the output 'out' of the D flip-flop itself. This creates a feedback loop from the output of the flip-flop back to the XOR gate. The D flip-flop's output will be the 'out' signal of the module.
