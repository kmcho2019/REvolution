To implement the TopModule, we need to design a D flip-flop that takes the output of a two-input XOR gate as its input. The XOR gate should take 'in' and 'out' as its inputs. Since the flip-flop is positive edge triggered by 'clk' and has no reset, we can use a non-resettable D flip-flop. The output of the flip-flop will be fed back to the XOR gate to complete the circuit.
