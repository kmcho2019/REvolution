To implement the TopModule, we will first create a D flip-flop that captures its input on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to the flip-flop will be the result of an XOR operation between the input signal 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop. Since the flip-flop's output is used as one of the inputs to the XOR, we need to be careful with the instantiation to avoid any potential combinational loop issues. The output of the flip-flop should be registered and then fed back into the XOR to maintain the sequential nature of the circuit.
