To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that takes the output of a two-input XOR gate as its input. The XOR gate itself takes 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop as its inputs. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the flip-flop is fed back into the XOR gate. We will use a positive edge-triggered D flip-flop without a reset, meaning the flip-flop will capture the input at the rising edge of the clock 'clk' and hold it until the next rising edge.
