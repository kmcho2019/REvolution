To implement the TopModule, we will first create a D flip-flop that is triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The input to this D flip-flop will be the output of a two-input XOR gate. One input of the XOR gate will be the 'in' signal, and the other input will be the 'out' signal, which is the output of the D flip-flop. This creates a feedback loop where the output of the D flip-flop is fed back into the XOR gate. The output of the D flip-flop will be the 'out' signal.
