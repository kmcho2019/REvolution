To implement the TopModule, we need to create a D flip-flop that captures the output of a two-input XOR gate on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. The XOR gate should take 'in' and the output 'out' of the flip-flop as its inputs. We can use a reg variable to hold the state of the flip-flop and a continuous assignment to implement the XOR gate. We will use a procedural assignment within an always block to update the state of the flip-flop on the positive edge of 'clk'.
