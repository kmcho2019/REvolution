To implement the Galois LFSR, we need to define the register and its behavior. The register `q` will be 32 bits wide. We'll use a combination of shift and XOR operations to update the state of the register based on the taps. The taps are at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. However, since we are dealing with a 32-bit register, the tap at position 32 doesn't directly apply in the conventional sense as the bit positions start from 0 to 31. Assuming the tap at position 32 is a misunderstanding and should actually refer to the most significant bit (MSB) of the register, which is at position 31, we proceed with taps at positions 31, 22, 2, and 1. On reset, we need to set the register `q` to 32'h1. This can be achieved with an initial value or through a synchronous reset logic.
