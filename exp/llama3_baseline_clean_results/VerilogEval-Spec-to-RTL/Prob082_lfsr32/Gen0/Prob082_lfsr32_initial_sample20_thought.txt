To implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1, we need to design a module that can shift the bits to the right and XOR the bits at the tap positions with the LSB. Since the taps are at positions 32, 22, 2, and 1, we need to consider that in Verilog, the bit positions are 0-indexed, so the actual positions would be 31, 21, 1, and 0 respectively. The module will also need to have a reset mechanism to reset the output to 32'h1 when the reset signal is high.
