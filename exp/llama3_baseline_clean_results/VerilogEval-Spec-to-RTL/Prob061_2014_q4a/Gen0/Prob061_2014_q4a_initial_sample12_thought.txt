The TopModule will be designed as a 1-bit shift register stage with load capability. It will have two main operations: shift and load. The shift operation will occur when the enable signal E is asserted, and the load operation will occur when the load signal L is asserted. The shift operation will transfer the input from the previous stage (w) to the output Q, and the load operation will load the value R into Q. The operations will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal clk.
