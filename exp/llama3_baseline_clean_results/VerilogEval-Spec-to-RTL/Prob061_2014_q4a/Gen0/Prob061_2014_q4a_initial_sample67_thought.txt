The TopModule will be designed as a single stage of a larger shift register circuit. It will have two main operations: shifting and loading. The shifting operation will be enabled by the 'E' signal, and it will shift the input 'w' from the previous stage to the output 'Q'. The loading operation will be triggered by the 'L' signal, and it will load the value 'R' into the stage. The module will use a D flip-flop to store the value, with the input 'w' or 'R' being the D input, and the output 'Q' being the Q output of the flip-flop. The 'E' signal will control the shifting operation, and the 'L' signal will control the loading operation.
