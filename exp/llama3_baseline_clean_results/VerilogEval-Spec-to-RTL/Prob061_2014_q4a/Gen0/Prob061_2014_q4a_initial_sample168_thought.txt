To implement the TopModule, we will use a D flip-flop to store the value. The D flip-flop will have two main operations: shifting and loading. When the load signal (L) is asserted, the flip-flop will load the value from the R input. When the enable shift signal (E) is asserted, the flip-flop will shift in the value from the previous stage (w) if the load signal is not asserted. We will use a conditional statement to determine whether to load or shift the value.
