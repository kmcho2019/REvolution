To implement the TopModule, we need to first identify the sign bit of the input 8-bit number, which is the most significant bit (bit[7]). Then, we will replicate this sign bit 24 times to extend the sign of the number to 32 bits. Finally, we will concatenate the replicated sign bits with the original 8-bit number to form the 32-bit output. This can be achieved using Verilog's replication operator and concatenation operator.
