To sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we first need to identify the sign bit, which is the most significant bit (MSB) of the 8-bit number. This bit is located at position 7 (in Verilog, indexing starts from 0). We then replicate this sign bit 24 times to fill the most significant 24 bits of the 32-bit output. The remaining 8 bits are directly copied from the input 8-bit number. This can be achieved using Verilog's concatenation operator and replication operator.
