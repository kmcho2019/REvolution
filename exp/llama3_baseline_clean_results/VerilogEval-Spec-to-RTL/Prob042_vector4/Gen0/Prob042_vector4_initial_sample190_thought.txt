To implement the TopModule, we can use Verilog's concatenation operator to combine the sign bit replicated 24 times with the original 8-bit number. The sign bit of the 8-bit number is bit[7]. We will use Verilog's replication operator ({bit[7] {24 {1'b0}}}) to replicate the sign bit 24 times. However, a simpler way is to use {24{in[7]}}. Then, we concatenate this with the original 8-bit number.
