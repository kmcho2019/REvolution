To implement the TopModule, we will use Verilog's replication operator and concatenation operator. The replication operator will be used to replicate the sign bit (the most significant bit) of the input 24 times. The concatenation operator will then be used to concatenate these replicated sign bits with the original 8-bit input. The result will be a 32-bit output where the sign bit of the input is extended to fill the most significant 24 bits.
