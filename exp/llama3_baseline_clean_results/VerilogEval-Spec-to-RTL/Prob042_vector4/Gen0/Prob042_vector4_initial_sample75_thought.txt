To implement the TopModule that sign-extends an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we need to replicate the sign bit (the most significant bit, bit[7]) of the 8-bit number 24 times and then concatenate it with the original 8-bit number. This can be achieved using the replication operator in Verilog. The replication operator is denoted by '{' and '}' and is used in conjunction with the concatenate operator '{' and '}' as well.
