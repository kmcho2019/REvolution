To implement the TopModule, we need to sign-extend the 8-bit input to 32 bits. 
This involves checking the sign bit (most significant bit) of the input and 
replicating it to the left for the remaining 24 bits. 
The module will take the 8-bit input, extract its sign bit, replicate it 24 times, 
and concatenate the result with the original 8-bit input.
