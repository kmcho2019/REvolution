To sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we need to replicate the most significant bit (MSB) of the 8-bit number 24 times and then concatenate the original 8-bit number. This operation preserves the signed value of the original number when it is interpreted as a 32-bit signed number. The MSB (bit[7]) will determine whether the number is positive (0) or negative (1), and replicating it to the left maintains the same sign when viewed as a 32-bit number.
