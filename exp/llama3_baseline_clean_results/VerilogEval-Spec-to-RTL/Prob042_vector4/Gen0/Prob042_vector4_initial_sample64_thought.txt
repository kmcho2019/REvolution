To sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we first need to identify the sign bit of the 8-bit number, which is the most significant bit (bit 7). We then replicate this sign bit 24 times to create the most significant 24 bits of the 32-bit output. The remaining 8 bits of the output are the input 8-bit number itself. This can be achieved using Verilog's concatenation and replication operators.
