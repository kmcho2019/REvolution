The module TopModule will take an 8-bit signed number as input and sign-extend it to 32 bits. This will be achieved by first identifying the sign bit (most significant bit) of the 8-bit number, then replicating this bit 24 times to create the 24 most significant bits of the 32-bit output. The 8-bit input will be concatenated to these replicated bits to form the 32-bit output.
