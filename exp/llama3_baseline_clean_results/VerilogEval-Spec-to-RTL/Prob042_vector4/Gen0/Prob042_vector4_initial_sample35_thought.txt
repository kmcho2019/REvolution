To implement the TopModule that sign-extends an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we will use the concatenation operator in Verilog to replicate the sign bit (the most significant bit of the 8-bit number) 24 times and then concatenate the 8-bit number itself. This will effectively sign-extend the 8-bit number to 32 bits, preserving its signed value. We will use the syntax { {24{in[7]}}, in } to achieve this.
