To implement the TopModule that sign-extends an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we will use the concatenation operator and the replication operator. The replication operator will create 24 copies of the sign bit (the most significant bit of the 8-bit number), and then we will concatenate these copies with the original 8-bit number. The most significant bit of the 8-bit number will determine whether the result is positive or negative.
