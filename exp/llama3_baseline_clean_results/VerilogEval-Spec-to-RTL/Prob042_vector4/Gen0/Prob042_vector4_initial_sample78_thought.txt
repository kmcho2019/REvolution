To implement the TopModule that sign-extends an 8-bit number to 32 bits, 
we will first identify the sign bit of the input number. The sign bit of 
an 8-bit number is the most significant bit (MSB), which is bit[7]. Then, 
we will replicate this sign bit 24 times and concatenate it with the 
original 8-bit number. This can be achieved using the concatenation 
operator ({}) in Verilog and the replication operator ({n{bit}}) to 
repeat the sign bit.
