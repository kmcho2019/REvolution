To implement the TopModule that sign-extends an 8-bit number to 32 bits, 
we will use the replication operator in Verilog, which involves concatenating 
24 copies of the sign bit (the most significant bit of the 8-bit number) 
with the 8-bit number itself. This will preserve the signed value when 
the smaller number is represented as a larger one. The sign bit, which 
is the most significant bit of the 8-bit input (bit 7), will be replicated 
24 times and concatenated with the original 8-bit input to form the 32-bit output.
