The module TopModule will be designed to sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits. This is achieved by replicating the sign bit (the most significant bit) of the 8-bit number 24 times and then concatenating it with the 8-bit number itself. In Verilog, this can be accomplished using the replication operator ({}) and the concatenation operator ({,}). The replication operator will replicate the sign bit 24 times, and the concatenation operator will combine the replicated sign bits with the 8-bit input number to produce the 32-bit output.
