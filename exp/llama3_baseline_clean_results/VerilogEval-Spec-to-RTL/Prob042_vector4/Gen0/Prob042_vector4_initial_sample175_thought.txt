To implement the TopModule, I will first determine the sign bit of the 8-bit input number. The sign bit is the most significant bit (bit 7) of the 8-bit number. I will then replicate this sign bit 24 times and concatenate it with the 8-bit input number. This will effectively sign-extend the 8-bit number to 32 bits. I will use the concatenation operator ({}) and the replication operator ({n{value}}) in Verilog to achieve this.
