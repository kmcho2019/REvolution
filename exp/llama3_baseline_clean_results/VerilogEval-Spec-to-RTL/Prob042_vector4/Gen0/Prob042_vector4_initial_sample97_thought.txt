To sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we need to replicate the most significant bit (MSB) of the 8-bit number 24 times and then concatenate it with the original 8-bit number. This can be achieved using the replication operator in Verilog. The replication operator is denoted by the syntax {n{expression}}, where 'n' is the number of times the expression is replicated.
