The replication operator in Verilog is denoted by a pair of curly braces {n{bit}}, where n is the number of times the bit should be repeated. The sign bit of the 8-bit number is the most significant bit, which is bit 7. We can extract this bit and then replicate it 24 times. Finally, we concatenate this replicated sign bit vector with the original 8-bit number to get the 32-bit sign-extended number.
