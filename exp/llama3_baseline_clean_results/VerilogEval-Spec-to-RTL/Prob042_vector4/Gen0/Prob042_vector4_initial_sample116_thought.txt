To implement the TopModule, we need to sign-extend the 8-bit input to 32 bits. This can be achieved by concatenating 24 copies of the sign bit (the most significant bit, bit[7]) with the original 8-bit number. In Verilog, we can use the replication operator to achieve this. The replication operator is denoted by '{n{bit}}, where 'n' is the number of times the 'bit' is replicated.
