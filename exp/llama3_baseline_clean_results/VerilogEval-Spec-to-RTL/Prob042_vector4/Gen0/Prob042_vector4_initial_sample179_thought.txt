To sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits, we need to determine the sign bit of the 8-bit number (which is the most significant bit, bit[7]) and then replicate this bit 24 times to the left. This replication is followed by the original 8-bit number itself. The result will be a 32-bit number where the sign of the original 8-bit number is preserved. In Verilog, we can achieve this using concatenation and replication operators.
