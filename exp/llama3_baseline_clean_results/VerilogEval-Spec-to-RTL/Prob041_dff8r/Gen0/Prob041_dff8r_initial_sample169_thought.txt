To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the input data d. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk and will have an active high synchronous reset. When the reset signal is high, all DFFs will be reset to zero, setting the output q to zero. When the reset signal is low, the DFFs will capture the input data d on the positive edge of clk and store it until the next positive edge of clk or a reset.
