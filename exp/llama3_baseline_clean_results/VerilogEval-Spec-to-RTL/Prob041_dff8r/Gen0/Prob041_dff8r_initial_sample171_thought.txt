To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) connected in parallel, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and having an active high synchronous reset. The input data (d) will be connected to the D inputs of the DFFs, and the output of the DFFs will be connected to the output port (q). We will use the always_ff block in Verilog to model the DFFs and ensure they are triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
