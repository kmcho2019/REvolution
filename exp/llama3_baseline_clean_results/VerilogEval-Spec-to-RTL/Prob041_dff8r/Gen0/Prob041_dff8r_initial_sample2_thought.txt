To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops, each with a synchronous active high reset. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. When reset is high, all 8 flip-flops will be reset to zero, regardless of the clk signal. Otherwise, on the positive edge of the clk signal, the flip-flops will capture the input d and update their output q accordingly.
