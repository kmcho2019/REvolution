To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the 8-bit input data. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and will have an active high synchronous reset. When the reset signal is high, the outputs of all DFFs will be set to zero. We will use the always_ff block in Verilog to describe the DFFs and the synchronous reset logic.
