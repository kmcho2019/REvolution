To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops with synchronous reset. The D flip-flops will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. When 'reset' is high, all flip-flops will be reset to zero. Otherwise, they will load the input 'd' on the positive edge of 'clk'. We will use Verilog's built-in reg type to declare the output 'q' and a vector to represent the 8-bit input 'd' and output 'q'.
