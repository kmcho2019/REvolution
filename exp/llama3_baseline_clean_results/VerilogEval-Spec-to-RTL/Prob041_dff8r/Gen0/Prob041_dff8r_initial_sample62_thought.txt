The TopModule will be designed as a module containing 8 instances of D flip-flops (DFFs). Each DFF will have its input connected to the corresponding bit of the input port d, and its output connected to the corresponding bit of the output port q. All DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and will have an active high synchronous reset. When the reset signal is high, all DFFs will be reset to zero, regardless of the clk signal.
