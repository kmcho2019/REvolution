To implement the TopModule, I will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) connected in parallel, each with an active high synchronous reset. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. On the rising edge of 'clk', each DFF will capture the corresponding bit from the input 'd' and output it at 'q' unless the 'reset' signal is high, in which case all DFFs will be reset to zero.
