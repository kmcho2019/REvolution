To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) to store the 8-bit input data. Each DFF will have its own output that will contribute to the 8-bit output q. We will use the always block to model the behavior of the DFFs. The DFFs will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk, and they will be reset to zero when the reset signal is high. This behavior will be achieved using the @(posedge clk) and if (reset) conditions within the always block.
