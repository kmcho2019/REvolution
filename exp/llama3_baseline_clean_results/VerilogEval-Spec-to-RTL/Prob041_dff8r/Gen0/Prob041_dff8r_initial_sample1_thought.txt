To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) that are triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. Each DFF will have an 8-bit data input 'd' and an active high synchronous reset 'reset'. When 'reset' is high, all DFFs will be reset to zero at the next positive edge of 'clk'. Otherwise, the DFFs will capture the input data 'd' at the positive edge of 'clk'.
