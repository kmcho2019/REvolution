To implement the TopModule, we can use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) in parallel. Each DFF will have the 8-bit input 'd' distributed to its respective bit position and the output 'q' will be the 8-bit output from these DFFs. We'll use the non-blocking assignment operator ('<=') to ensure that all DFFs are triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and reset when 'reset' is high.
