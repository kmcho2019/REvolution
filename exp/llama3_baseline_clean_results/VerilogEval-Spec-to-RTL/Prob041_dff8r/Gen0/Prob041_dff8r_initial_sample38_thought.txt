To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple 8-bit register made of 8 D flip-flops. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and will have an active high synchronous reset. This means when the reset signal is high, the output of all the D flip-flops will be reset to zero at the next positive edge of the clock. The input data (d) will be used as the input to the D flip-flops and the output (q) will be the output of the D flip-flops.
