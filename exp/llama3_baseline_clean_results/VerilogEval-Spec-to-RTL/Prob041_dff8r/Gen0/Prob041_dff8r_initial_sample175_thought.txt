To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple 8-bit register made of 8 D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop should be connected to a bit of the input d and should be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk. The reset signal should be active high and should reset all flip-flops to zero when asserted. We will use a for loop in Verilog to instantiate the D flip-flops.
