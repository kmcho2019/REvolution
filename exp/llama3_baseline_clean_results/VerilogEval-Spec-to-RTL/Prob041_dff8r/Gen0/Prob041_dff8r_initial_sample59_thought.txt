To implement the TopModule, we need to instantiate 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) that are triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) and have an active high synchronous reset. This means when the reset signal is high and the clock signal has a positive edge, the output of all DFFs will be set to zero. At any other time, when the reset signal is low and the clock signal has a positive edge, the input data (d) will be captured and reflected at the output (q). We can use the always_ff block in Verilog to describe the behavior of the DFFs, utilizing the posedge of clk to trigger the update of the output.
