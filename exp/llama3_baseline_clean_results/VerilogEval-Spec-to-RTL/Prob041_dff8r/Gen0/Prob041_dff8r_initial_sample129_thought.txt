To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple register structure with 8 D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk) and have an active high synchronous reset. The reset signal will set all the outputs to zero when it is high and the clock is positive edged. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to ensure that all flip-flops are updated in parallel, and not sequentially, which could lead to incorrect results due to race conditions.
