To implement the TopModule with the given interface, we need to use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) that are triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk) and have an active high synchronous reset. This means that when the reset signal is high and the clock transitions from low to high, the output of each DFF will be reset to zero. At other times, when the reset is low and the clock transitions from low to high, the output of each DFF will be the current value at its input (d).
