To implement the TopModule, we will use a single 8-bit D flip-flop, where all bits are connected to the input d, and all bits of the output are connected to q. Each D flip-flop will have an active high synchronous reset, and will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal. This is a simple shift register where the input d is sampled on the positive edge of clk and presented at the output q, with reset overriding this operation to zero.
