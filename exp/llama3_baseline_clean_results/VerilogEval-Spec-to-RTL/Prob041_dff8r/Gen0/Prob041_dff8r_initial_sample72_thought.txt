To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) that are all triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk'. Each DFF will have a synchronous active high reset, meaning when the 'reset' signal is high, the output of all DFFs will be set to zero. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator '<=' to assign values to the output 'q' to avoid race conditions.
