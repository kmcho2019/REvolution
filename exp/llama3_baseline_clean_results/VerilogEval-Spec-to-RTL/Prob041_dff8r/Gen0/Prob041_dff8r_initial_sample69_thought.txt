To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 instances of a D flip-flop with synchronous reset. Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and will have an active high reset. When the reset signal is high, the output of all D flip-flops will be set to zero, and when the reset signal is low, the output of each D flip-flop will be the value of the corresponding input d bit at the previous positive edge of the clk signal.
