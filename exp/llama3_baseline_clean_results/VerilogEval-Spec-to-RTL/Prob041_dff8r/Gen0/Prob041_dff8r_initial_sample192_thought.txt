To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) in parallel, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk) signal. The DFFs will have an active high synchronous reset, meaning that when the reset signal is high, the outputs of all DFFs will be reset to zero. The input data (d) will be connected to the D inputs of the DFFs, and the outputs of the DFFs will be connected to the output bus (q).
