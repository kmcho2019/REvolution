To design the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops connected in parallel. Each D flip-flop will have a data input connected to the corresponding bit of the input port 'd', and the output will be connected to the corresponding bit of the output port 'q'. The clock input of all D flip-flops will be connected to 'clk', and the reset input will be connected to 'reset'. Since it's an active high synchronous reset, when 'reset' is high and 'clk' goes high, all D flip-flops will be reset to zero.
