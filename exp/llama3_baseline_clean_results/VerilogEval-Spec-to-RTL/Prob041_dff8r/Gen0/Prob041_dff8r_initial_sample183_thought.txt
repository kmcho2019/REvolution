To implement the TopModule as described, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) in parallel, each taking one bit of the 8-bit input d and producing one bit of the 8-bit output q. The reset signal will be used to asynchronously or synchronously reset all DFFs to zero. Since the specification mentions active high synchronous reset, it means that when reset is high and there is a positive edge on the clock, all DFFs will be reset to zero. Otherwise, they will capture the input d on the positive edge of the clock.
