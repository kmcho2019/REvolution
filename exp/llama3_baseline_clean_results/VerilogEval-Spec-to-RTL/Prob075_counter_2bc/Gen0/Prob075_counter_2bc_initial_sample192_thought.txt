To implement the TopModule, we will design a two-bit saturating counter with an asynchronous reset. The counter should increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low. The counter should not change when train_valid is low. We will use an if-else statement inside an always block to achieve this functionality. The counter will be reset to 2'b01 when areset is high.
