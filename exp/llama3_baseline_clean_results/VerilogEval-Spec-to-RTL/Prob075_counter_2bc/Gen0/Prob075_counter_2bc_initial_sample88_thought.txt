To implement the TopModule with a two-bit saturating counter, we need to consider the following:
- The counter should increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, up to a maximum of 3 (2'b11).
- The counter should decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low, down to a minimum of 0 (2'b00).
- When train_valid is low, the counter should hold its current value.
- The counter should reset to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken) on a positive edge of the areset signal, regardless of the current clock state.
This requires using a synchronous reset to update the counter value based on the input conditions and an asynchronous reset to handle the areset signal.
