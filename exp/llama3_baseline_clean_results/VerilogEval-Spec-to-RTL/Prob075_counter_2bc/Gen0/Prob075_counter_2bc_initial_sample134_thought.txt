The TopModule should be designed as a finite state machine with a saturating counter. 
The counter increments when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrements 
when train_valid is high but train_taken is low. When train_valid is low, the counter 
remains unchanged. The areset signal asynchronously resets the counter to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken). 
The state variable will hold the two-bit counter value and will be updated at each positive clock edge.
