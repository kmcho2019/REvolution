To implement the TopModule, I will use a two-bit register to store the counter value. The counter will be updated on the positive edge of the clock when the module is in training mode (train_valid = 1). The update logic will check the value of train_taken to decide whether to increment or decrement the counter. If the counter is already at its maximum or minimum value, it will saturate and not change. The areset signal will be used to asynchronously reset the counter to its initial value.
