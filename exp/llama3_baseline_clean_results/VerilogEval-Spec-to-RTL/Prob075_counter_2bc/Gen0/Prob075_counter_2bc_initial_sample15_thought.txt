To implement the TopModule, we need to create a two-bit saturating counter. The counter should increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low. When train_valid is low, the counter should remain unchanged. An asynchronous reset should be implemented to reset the counter to 2'b01 when areset is high. We will use a combination of conditional statements and non-blocking assignments to achieve this functionality.
