The counter should be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. We will use an if-else statement to check the conditions for incrementing or decrementing the counter. If the counter is at its maximum value (3) and we try to increment it, or if the counter is at its minimum value (0) and we try to decrement it, the counter should saturate, meaning it should not change its value.
