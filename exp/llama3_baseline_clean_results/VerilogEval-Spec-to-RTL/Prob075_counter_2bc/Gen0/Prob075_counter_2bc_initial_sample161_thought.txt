To implement the TopModule, we will use a 2-bit register to store the counter value. On the positive edge of the clock, we will check the train_valid signal. If it is high, we will check the train_taken signal to decide whether to increment or decrement the counter. We will also implement the saturation logic to ensure the counter does not exceed 3 or go below 0. The areset signal will asynchronously reset the counter to 2'b01.
