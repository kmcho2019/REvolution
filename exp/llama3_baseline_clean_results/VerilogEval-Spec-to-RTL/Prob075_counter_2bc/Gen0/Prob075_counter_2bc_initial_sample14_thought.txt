The module TopModule will be implemented as a two-bit saturating counter. 
It will have two main states: increment and decrement. 
When train_valid is high and train_taken is high, it will increment the counter. 
When train_valid is high and train_taken is low, it will decrement the counter. 
If train_valid is low, the counter will remain unchanged. 
The counter will also have a reset condition triggered by areset. 
The reset condition will set the counter to 2'b01. 
All changes to the counter will occur on the positive edge of the clock signal.
