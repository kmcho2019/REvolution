To implement the two-bit saturating counter in the TopModule, we'll use a combination of sequential logic (a counter variable) and combinational logic to determine the next state of the counter based on the current inputs. The counter will increment when both `train_valid` and `train_taken` are high, decrement when `train_valid` is high but `train_taken` is low, and remain unchanged when `train_valid` is low. An asynchronous reset will be implemented to reset the counter to 2'b01 upon the positive edge of `areset`.
