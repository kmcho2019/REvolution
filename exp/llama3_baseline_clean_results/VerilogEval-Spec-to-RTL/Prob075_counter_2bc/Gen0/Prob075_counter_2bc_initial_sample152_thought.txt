The module TopModule will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) that implements a saturating counter. The counter will be incremented when train_valid and train_taken are both high, and decremented when train_valid is high and train_taken is low. When train_valid is low, the counter will hold its value. The asynchronous reset (areset) will reset the counter to 2'b01. 
We will use a 2-bit register to store the counter value, and a combinational logic to determine the next state of the counter based on the current state and inputs.
