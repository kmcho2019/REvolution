To solve this problem, we'll implement a two-bit saturating counter within the TopModule. The counter will be designed to increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high but train_taken is low. The counter should not change when train_valid is low. We'll also include an asynchronous reset that sets the counter to '01' (weakly not-taken) on the positive edge of the areset signal. This can be achieved using a combination of conditional statements within a sequential always block in Verilog.
