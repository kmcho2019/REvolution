The design idea is to create a two-bit saturating counter that increments or decrements based on the input signals train_valid and train_taken. We will use an if-else statement inside a clocked always block to implement the logic. The counter will increment when train_valid and train_taken are both high, and decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low. When train_valid is low, the counter will hold its current value. We will also add an asynchronous reset to initialize the counter to 2'b01 when areset is high.
