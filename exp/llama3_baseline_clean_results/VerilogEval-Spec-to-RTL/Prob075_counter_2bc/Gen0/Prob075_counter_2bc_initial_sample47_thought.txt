The TopModule should contain a two-bit saturating counter that increments or decrements based on the train_valid and train_taken signals. 
It should have an asynchronous reset to initialize the counter to 2'b01. 
We will use a 2-bit reg variable to hold the counter value and use a case statement to determine whether to increment or decrement the counter. 
We will also add checks to prevent the counter from exceeding its maximum or minimum values.
