To implement the TopModule, we will use a two-bit register to store the counter value. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock. If the asynchronous reset (areset) is high, the register will be reset to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken) regardless of the clock. Otherwise, if train_valid is high, the counter will increment if train_taken is high and decrement if train_taken is low, with saturation logic to prevent the counter from exceeding 3 or going below 0. If train_valid is low, the counter will keep its current value.
