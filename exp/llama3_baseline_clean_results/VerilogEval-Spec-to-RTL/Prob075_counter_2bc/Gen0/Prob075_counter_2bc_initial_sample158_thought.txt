The TopModule will contain a 2-bit saturating counter implemented as a sequential logic circuit. The counter will increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low, and remain unchanged when train_valid is low. The counter will also be reset to 2'b01 when areset goes high. This can be achieved using an if-else statement within a always block to handle the different conditions and update the counter accordingly.
