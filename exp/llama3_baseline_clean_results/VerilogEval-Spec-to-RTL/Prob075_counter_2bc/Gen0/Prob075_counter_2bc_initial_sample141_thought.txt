The TopModule will be designed as a two-bit saturating counter. It will have an asynchronous reset that sets the counter to 2'b01 when areset is high. The counter will increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high but train_taken is low. The counter will not change when train_valid is low. The increment and decrement operations will be saturating, meaning the counter will not go below 0 or above 3.
