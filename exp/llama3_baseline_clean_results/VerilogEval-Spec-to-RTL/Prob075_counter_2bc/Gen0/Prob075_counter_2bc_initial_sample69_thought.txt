To implement the required functionality, we can use a 2-bit saturating counter within the TopModule. The counter will be initialized to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken) when the asynchronous reset (areset) is asserted. It will increment when train_valid and train_taken are both high, up to a maximum of 3. It will decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low, down to a minimum of 0. When train_valid is low, the counter will hold its current value. 
We'll use an if-else statement within a always block to implement the above logic. The counter will be updated based on the train_valid and train_taken signals at the positive edge of the clock.
