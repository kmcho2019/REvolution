To implement the two-bit saturating counter in the TopModule, I will use a sequential logic approach. The counter will be designed using a 2-bit register that is updated based on the input conditions. The register will be reset to '01' (weakly not-taken) asynchronously when areset is high. On the positive edge of the clock, the counter will check the train_valid and train_taken signals. If train_valid is high and train_taken is high, the counter will increment. If train_valid is high and train_taken is low, the counter will decrement. If train_valid is low, the counter will hold its current value. The counter will also be designed to saturate at its minimum and maximum values (0 and 3, respectively).
