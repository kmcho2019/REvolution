To implement the TopModule, we can use a 2-bit saturating counter. The counter will have an enable input (train_valid) and an increment/decrement input (train_taken). When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 1, the counter will increment. When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 0, the counter will decrement. When train_valid is 0, the counter will hold its value. We will use an if-else statement to check the conditions and update the counter accordingly. We will also include an asynchronous reset (areset) to reset the counter to 2'b01 on a positive edge.
