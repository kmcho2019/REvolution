The design involves implementing a saturating counter within the TopModule. The counter's operation is conditional, depending on the values of `train_valid` and `train_taken`. When `train_valid` is high, the counter either increments or decrements based on `train_taken`, but remains unchanged when `train_valid` is low. The counter saturates at its maximum value of 3 (2'b11) when attempting to increment further and at its minimum value of 0 (2'b00) when attempting to decrement further. An asynchronous reset (`areset`) is also implemented to reset the counter to a weakly not-taken state (2'b01) upon its assertion. This requires a combination of conditional statements to manage the counter's state transitions and an asynchronous reset logic.
