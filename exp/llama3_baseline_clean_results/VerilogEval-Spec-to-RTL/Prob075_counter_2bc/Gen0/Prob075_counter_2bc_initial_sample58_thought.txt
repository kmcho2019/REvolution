To implement the TopModule with a two-bit saturating counter, I will use a combination of always blocks and conditional statements to manage the counter value. The counter will be incremented or decremented based on the values of train_valid and train_taken, while keeping its value unchanged when not training. I will also include an asynchronous reset to reset the counter to 2'b01 when areset is asserted. The output state will be assigned the current counter value.
