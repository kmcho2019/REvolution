To implement the TopModule, we need to design a two-bit saturating counter. The counter should be able to increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low. It should remain unchanged when train_valid is low. We will use a simple if-else statement inside a always block to achieve this functionality. The areset will be used to asynchronously reset the counter to 2'b01.
