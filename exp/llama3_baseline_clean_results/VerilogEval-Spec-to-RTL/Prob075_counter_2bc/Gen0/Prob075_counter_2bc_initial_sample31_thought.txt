To implement the TopModule, we'll create a 2-bit saturating counter with sequential logic triggered on the positive edge of the clock. The counter will increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, and decrement when train_valid is high but train_taken is low. When train_valid is low, the counter will maintain its current value. An asynchronous reset will be implemented to reset the counter to 2'b01 when areset is high. 
We will use an if-else statement inside a always block to achieve the desired functionality.
