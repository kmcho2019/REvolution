To implement the two-bit saturating counter, we need to consider the following cases:
- When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 1, increment the counter up to a maximum of 3.
- When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 0, decrement the counter down to a minimum of 0.
- When train_valid is 0, keep the counter value unchanged.
- On a positive edge triggered asynchronous reset (areset), reset the counter to 2'b01.
We will use a always block to describe the sequential behavior of the counter, and conditional statements to handle the different cases.
