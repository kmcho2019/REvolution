To implement the TopModule, I will use a synchronous reset, two-bit counter with increment and decrement logic. The counter will be implemented using a 2-bit reg (state) that is updated on the positive edge of the clock (clk). When the asynchronous reset (areset) is asserted, the counter will be reset to 2'b01 (weakly not-taken) immediately. The counter will increment when train_valid = 1 and train_taken = 1, and decrement when train_valid = 1 and train_taken = 0. The counter will saturate at its maximum value (3) when incrementing and at its minimum value (0) when decrementing.
