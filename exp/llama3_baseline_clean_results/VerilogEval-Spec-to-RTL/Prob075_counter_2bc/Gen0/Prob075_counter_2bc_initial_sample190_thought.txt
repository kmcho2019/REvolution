To implement the TopModule, we will use a synchronous two-bit counter that increments or decrements based on the input signals train_valid and train_taken. We will also incorporate an asynchronous reset that sets the counter to 2'b01 when areset is high. The counter will be designed to saturate at its maximum value of 3 (2'b11) and minimum value of 0 (2'b00). This will be achieved by using conditional statements to check for the saturation conditions during increment and decrement operations.
