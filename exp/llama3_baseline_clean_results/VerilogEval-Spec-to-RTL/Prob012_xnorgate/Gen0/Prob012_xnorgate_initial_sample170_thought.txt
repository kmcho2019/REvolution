To implement an XNOR gate in Verilog, we need to create a module that outputs 1 when both inputs are the same (either both 0 or both 1) and 0 when the inputs are different. This can be achieved using a simple logical expression. The XNOR operation can be expressed as the inverse of the XOR operation. In Verilog, the XOR operation is denoted by the ^ operator, and the inversion is denoted by the ~ operator. So, the XNOR of two inputs a and b can be implemented as ~(a ^ b).
