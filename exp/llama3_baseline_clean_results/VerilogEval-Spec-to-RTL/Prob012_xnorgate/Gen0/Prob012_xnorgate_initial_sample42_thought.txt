To implement an XNOR gate, we can use the basic logical operations. The XNOR operation between two bits a and b can be represented as the negation of the XOR operation between a and b, which is ~(a ^ b). In Verilog, the XOR operation is represented by the ^ operator and the negation is represented by the ~ operator or the ! operator for logical NOT. So, the output out will be assigned the result of ~(a ^ b), which can also be expressed as !(a ^ b) for logical operations.
