To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high synchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock signal (clk). The reset signal will be active high, and when asserted, all DFFs will reset to the value 0x34. The input signal d will be connected to the D inputs of the DFFs, and the Q outputs of the DFFs will be connected to the output signal q. We will use the always_ff statement in Verilog to describe the DFFs' behavior, and the posedge and negedge keywords to specify the clock edges.
