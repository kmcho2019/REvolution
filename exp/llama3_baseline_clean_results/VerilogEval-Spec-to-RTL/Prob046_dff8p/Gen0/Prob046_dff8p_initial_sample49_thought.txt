To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops, each with an active high synchronous reset. Since the flip-flops must be reset to a specific value (0x34) rather than zero, we need to ensure that the reset value is correctly loaded into the flip-flops when the reset signal is asserted. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock (clk), meaning we will use the `posedge` of the inverted clock to trigger the flip-flops. We can achieve this by inverting the clk signal internally within the module.
