To implement the TopModule, we will create a module that includes 8 D flip-flops. 
Each flip-flop will have an active high synchronous reset. 
When the reset signal is high, the flip-flops will be reset to the value 0x34. 
The D flip-flops will be triggered by the negative edge of the clk signal. 
We will use the Verilog non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the outputs of the flip-flops on the negative edge of clk.
