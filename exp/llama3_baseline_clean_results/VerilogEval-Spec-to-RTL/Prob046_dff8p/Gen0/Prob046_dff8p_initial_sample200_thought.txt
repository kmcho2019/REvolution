To implement the TopModule, we will create a module that instantiates 8 D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock signal (clk) and will have an active high synchronous reset. When the reset signal is asserted, all flip-flops will be reset to a predefined value of 8'h34. The input d will be connected to the D inputs of the flip-flops, and the output q will be connected to the Q outputs of the flip-flops.
