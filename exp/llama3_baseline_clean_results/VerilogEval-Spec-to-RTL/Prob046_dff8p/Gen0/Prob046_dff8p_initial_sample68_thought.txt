To implement the TopModule, we can create 8 individual D flip-flops (DFFs) that are all triggered by the negative edge of the clock (clk). Each DFF should have an active high synchronous reset, meaning that when the reset signal is high, the DFFs will be reset to a predefined value, in this case, 0x34. The DFFs will be loaded with the value from the input d when the reset signal is low and the clock transitions from high to low.
