To implement the TopModule, we will create 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high synchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock signal (clk). The reset signal will be active high, meaning that when reset is 1, all DFFs will be reset to their initial values, which are specified as 0x34. We will use a Verilog always block to model the behavior of each DFF.
