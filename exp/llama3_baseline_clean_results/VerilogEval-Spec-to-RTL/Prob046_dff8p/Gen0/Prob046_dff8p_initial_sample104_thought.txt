To implement the TopModule, we need to design a module that includes 8 D flip-flops with active high synchronous reset. Since the flip-flops must be reset to 0x34 rather than zero, we will use the initial value of the D flip-flops as 0x34. We will also ensure that all DFFs are triggered by the negative edge of the clock signal. 
In Verilog, the `always` block can be used with an `@(negedge clk)` statement to trigger the D flip-flops at the negative edge of the clock. 
The `if` statement inside the `always` block will be used to reset the flip-flops to 0x34 when the reset signal is high. 
Otherwise, the flip-flops will be loaded with the value of the input `d` when the clock goes through a negative edge.
