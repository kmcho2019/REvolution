To implement the TopModule, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high synchronous reset. The DFFs will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock (clk) to capture the input data (d) on the falling edge of the clock. The reset signal will be used to reset the DFFs to a predefined value (0x34) when it is high. We will use a for loop to instantiate the 8 DFFs.
