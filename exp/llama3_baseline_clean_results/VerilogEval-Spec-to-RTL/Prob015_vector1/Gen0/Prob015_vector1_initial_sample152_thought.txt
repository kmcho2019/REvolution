To implement the TopModule, we will use simple bit-level manipulation and assignment in Verilog. Since the task is to split a 16-bit input into two 8-bit outputs, we can achieve this by directly assigning the appropriate bit ranges of the input to the outputs. Specifically, the lower byte (bits [7:0]) of the input will be assigned to out_lo, and the upper byte (bits [15:8]) of the input will be assigned to out_hi.
