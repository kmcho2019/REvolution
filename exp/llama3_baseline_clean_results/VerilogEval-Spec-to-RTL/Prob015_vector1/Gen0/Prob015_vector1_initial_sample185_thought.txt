To implement the TopModule, we need to create a simple combinational circuit that splits the 16-bit input into two 8-bit outputs: out_hi and out_lo. The out_lo output should take the lower 8 bits [7:0] of the input, and the out_hi output should take the upper 8 bits [15:8] of the input. This can be achieved using simple bit manipulation and wire connections in Verilog.
