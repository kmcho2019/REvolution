To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use a simple shift register design consisting of four D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have an active-low synchronous reset. The input to the first flip-flop will be the 'in' signal, and the output of the last flip-flop will be the 'out' signal. The data will shift from one flip-flop to the next on each positive clock edge.
