To implement the shift register, we can use four D flip-flops. Each flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have a synchronous active-low reset. The input to each flip-flop will be the output of the previous flip-flop, with the exception of the first flip-flop which will take the input 'in'. When the reset signal is asserted, all flip-flops will be reset to 0.
