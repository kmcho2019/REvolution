To implement the TopModule with a shift register composed of four D flip-flops, I will use a hierarchical approach. Each D flip-flop will be designed to capture data on the positive edge of the clock and have a synchronous reset. The output of one flip-flop will serve as the input to the next, effectively creating a shift register. The input to the first flip-flop will come from the 'in' port, and the output of the last flip-flop will drive the 'out' port. Synchronous reset will ensure that all flip-flops reset to 0 when 'resetn' is low, regardless of the clock.
