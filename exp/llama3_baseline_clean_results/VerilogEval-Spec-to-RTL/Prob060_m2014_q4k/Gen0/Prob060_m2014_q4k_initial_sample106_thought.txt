The module TopModule will be designed with a four-stage D flip-flop shift register. Each stage will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will have a synchronous active-low reset (resetn). When the reset is asserted low, all stages of the shift register will be cleared to zero. The input (in) will be clocked into the first stage on each positive clock edge, and the data will shift one stage to the right on each subsequent clock edge. The output (out) will be taken from the last stage of the shift register.
