To implement the TopModule with a shift register of four D flops, we can use four D flip-flops connected in series. Each flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have a synchronous active-low reset. The input to the first flip-flop will be the 'in' port, and the output of the last flip-flop will be the 'out' port. We will use the resetn signal to reset the flip-flops to zero when it is low.
