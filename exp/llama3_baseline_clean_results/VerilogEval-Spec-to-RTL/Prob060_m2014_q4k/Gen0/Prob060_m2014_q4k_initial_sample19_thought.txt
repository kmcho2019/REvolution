To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use four D flip-flops connected in series to create a shift register. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will have a synchronous active-low reset (resetn). The input (in) will be connected to the first D flip-flop, and the output (out) will be taken from the last D flip-flop. This way, when the clock rises, the input will be shifted through the register, and when resetn is low, all flip-flops will reset to 0.
