The TopModule will contain four D flip-flops connected in series to form a shift register. Each D flip-flop will have a synchronous active-low reset, so when resetn is low, the output of each flip-flop will be set to zero on the positive edge of the clock. The data will shift one stage to the right on each positive clock edge when resetn is high. The input data will be loaded into the first D flip-flop, and the output will be taken from the last D flip-flop.
