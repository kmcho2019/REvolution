To implement a shift register with four D flip-flops, we need to design a module that takes in a single-bit input and shifts it through four sequential stages upon each positive clock edge. We also need to incorporate a synchronous active-low reset to clear the register when resetn is low. Each stage will be a D flip-flop that captures the input (or the output of the previous stage) on the positive edge of the clock and holds it until the next clock edge. On reset, all stages should be cleared to 0.
