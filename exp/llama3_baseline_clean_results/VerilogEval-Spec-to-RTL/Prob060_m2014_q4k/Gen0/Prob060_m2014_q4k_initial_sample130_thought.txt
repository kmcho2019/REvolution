To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, I will use four D flip-flops connected in series to create a shift register. The input 'in' will be connected to the input of the first D flip-flop, and the output 'out' will be connected to the output of the last D flip-flop. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock 'clk' and will have a synchronous active-low reset 'resetn'. When 'resetn' is low, all flip-flops will reset to zero.
