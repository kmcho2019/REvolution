To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we need to design a shift register using four D flip-flops. The shift register will shift the input 'in' one bit to the right on every positive edge of the clock 'clk'. The output 'out' will be the least significant bit of the shift register. We will also implement a synchronous active-low reset 'resetn' to clear the shift register. When 'resetn' is low, the shift register will be reset to zero.
