To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use four D flip-flops connected in series to form a shift register. Each D flip-flop will have a synchronous active-low reset. On the positive edge of the clock, the input bit will be shifted into the first D flip-flop, and the bits in the other D flip-flops will be shifted one position to the right. When the reset signal is low, all D flip-flops will be reset to zero.
