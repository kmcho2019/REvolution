Each DFF will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and they will be reset synchronously when the resetn signal is low. The input 'in' will be connected to the data input of the first DFF, and the output 'out' will be connected to the data output of the last DFF. The data output of each DFF will be connected to the data input of the next DFF, thus creating a shift register.
