To implement the shift register, we'll use a 4-stage D flip-flop chain. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have an active-low synchronous reset. The input 'in' will be connected to the first D flip-flop, and each subsequent flip-flop's D input will be connected to the previous flip-flop's Q output. The output 'out' will be taken from the Q output of the last flip-flop. We'll use non-blocking assignments for sequential logic to avoid race conditions.
