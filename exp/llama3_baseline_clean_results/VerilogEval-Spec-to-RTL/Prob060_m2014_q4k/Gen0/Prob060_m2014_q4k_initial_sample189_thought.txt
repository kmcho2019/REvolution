To implement the TopModule with a shift register using four D flops, we'll use a synchronous resettable approach. This means that when the resetn signal is low, all the flops will reset to zero on the next positive clock edge. The shift register will then shift the input bit 'in' through the four D flops on each positive clock edge. We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the flop outputs at the next clock edge.
