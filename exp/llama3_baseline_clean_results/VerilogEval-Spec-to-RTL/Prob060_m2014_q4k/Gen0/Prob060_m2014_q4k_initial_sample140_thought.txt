The TopModule will contain four D-type flip-flops connected in a series to form a shift register. Each D flip-flop will have a synchronous active-low reset. On every positive clock edge, the input will be shifted into the first flip-flop, and the data will be shifted from one flip-flop to the next. The output of the last flip-flop will be the output of the module. If the reset signal is asserted (low), all flip-flops will be reset to 0 on the next positive clock edge.
