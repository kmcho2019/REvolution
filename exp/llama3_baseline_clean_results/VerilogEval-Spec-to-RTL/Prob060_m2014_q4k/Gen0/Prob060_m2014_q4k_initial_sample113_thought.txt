The TopModule will contain four D flip-flops connected in series, forming a shift register. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and will have a synchronous active-low reset input 'resetn'. When 'resetn' is low, all flip-flops will be reset to 0. Otherwise, the input 'in' will be shifted through the register on each positive clock edge.
