To implement the TopModule, we will use four D flip-flops connected in series to form a shift register. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock. The reset signal will be synchronous and active-low, meaning when resetn is 0, the output of all D flip-flops will be reset to 0. The input to the first D flip-flop will be connected to the 'in' port, and the output of the last D flip-flop will be connected to the 'out' port.
