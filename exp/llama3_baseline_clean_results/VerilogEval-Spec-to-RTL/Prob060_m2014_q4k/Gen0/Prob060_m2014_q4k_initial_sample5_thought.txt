The module TopModule will be designed with a shift register that consists of four D flip-flops. The shift register will be implemented by connecting the output of each D flip-flop to the input of the next one, forming a chain. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock 'clk' and will have a synchronous active-low reset 'resetn'. When 'resetn' is low, all flip-flops will reset, setting the output of each flip-flop to zero. The input 'in' will be connected to the first flip-flop, and the output 'out' will be taken from the last flip-flop.
