To implement the TopModule as a shift register with four D flip-flops, we can chain four D flip-flops together, with each flip-flop's output connected to the next one's input. The input `in` will be connected to the first D flip-flop's input, and the last flip-flop's output will be connected to the output `out`. Each flip-flop will have a synchronous reset, which means they will be reset when `resetn` is low and the clock `clk` is high. On every rising edge of the clock, the value of `in` will be shifted one position to the right through the chain of flip-flops.
