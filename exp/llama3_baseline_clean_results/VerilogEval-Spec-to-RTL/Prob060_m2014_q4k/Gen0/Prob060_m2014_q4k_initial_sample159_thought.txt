To implement the TopModule, we need to design a 4-stage shift register using D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have a synchronous active-low reset. The output of each D flip-flop will be connected to the input of the next D flip-flop, creating the shift register effect. The input to the first D flip-flop will come from the 'in' port, and the output of the last D flip-flop will be connected to the 'out' port.
