To implement a shift register with four D flip-flops, we need to design a module named TopModule. The module will have one-bit input and output ports. The shift register will shift the input data from left to right (MSB to LSB) on every positive edge of the clock. The reset will be synchronous and active-low, meaning it will reset the flip-flops only when the reset signal is low and the clock is high. We will use four D flip-flops to implement the shift register, and each flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
