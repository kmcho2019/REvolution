To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, I will use a shift register composed of four D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will have a synchronous active-low reset. The output of the module will be the output of the last D flip-flop in the chain. The input to the module will be the input to the first D flip-flop in the chain, and each subsequent D flip-flop will be connected to the output of the previous one, thus implementing a shift register.
