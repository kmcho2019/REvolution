To implement the TopModule as described, we will create a module with the specified interface and use four D flip-flops (DFFs) connected in series to form a shift register. Each DFF will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal 'clk' and will have a synchronous active-low reset 'resetn'. When 'resetn' is low, all DFFs will reset their outputs to 0. The input 'in' will be connected to the first DFF, and the output 'out' will be connected to the output of the fourth DFF.
