The TopModule should be designed with a shift register that consists of four D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will store a bit of data, and when the clock signal rises, the data will be shifted from one flip-flop to the next. The module will also have a synchronous active-low reset, meaning that when the reset signal is low, the data in all flip-flops will be cleared on the next positive clock edge. The input data will be stored in the first flip-flop on every positive clock edge, and the output will be the data stored in the last flip-flop.
