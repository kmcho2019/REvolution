To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use four D flip-flops (DFFs) to create a shift register. The input to the shift register will be the 'in' signal, and the output of the shift register will be the 'out' signal. Since the reset is active-low synchronous, we will use a resetn signal to reset the shift register. On every positive edge of the clock, the data will shift from one DFF to the next, effectively creating a four-stage shift register. We will use a non-blocking assignment (<=) to ensure that all DFFs are updated in parallel.
