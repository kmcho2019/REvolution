To implement the Lemming's behavior, we will design a Moore state machine with the following states:
- IDLE_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left on the ground.
- IDLE_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right on the ground.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling due to no ground.
- DIGGING_LEFT: The Lemming is digging while walking left.
- DIGGING_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging while walking right.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and will not move.

We will use a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. If this counter exceeds 20, the Lemming will splatter when it hits the ground.

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) and the current state.
