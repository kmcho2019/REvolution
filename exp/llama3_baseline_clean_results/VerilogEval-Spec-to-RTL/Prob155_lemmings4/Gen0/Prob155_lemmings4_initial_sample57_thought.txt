To implement the Lemming's behavior, we will design a Moore finite state machine (FSM) that can be in one of several states: walking left, walking right, falling, digging, or splattered. The FSM will transition between these states based on the input signals.

We will use an enumerated type to define the states of the FSM. The states will be: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING, and SPLATTERED.

The FSM will start in the WALK_LEFT state when the asynchronous reset signal areset is asserted. In the WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states, the FSM will transition to the other walking state when a bump is detected on the corresponding side. If the ground signal is deasserted, the FSM will transition to the FALLING state. If the dig signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the DIGGING state.

In the FALLING state, the FSM will continue to fall until the ground signal is asserted again. If the ground signal is asserted after more than 20 clock cycles, the FSM will transition to the SPLATTERED state. Otherwise, it will transition back to the previous walking state.

In the DIGGING state, the FSM will continue to dig until the ground signal is deasserted. At this point, the FSM will transition to the FALLING state.

Once the FSM transitions to the SPLATTERED state, it will remain in this state forever, with all outputs deasserted.

To keep track of the number of clock cycles that the Lemming has been falling, we will use a counter that is incremented every clock cycle when the FSM is in the FALLING state. If the counter exceeds 20 when the ground signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the SPLATTERED state.
