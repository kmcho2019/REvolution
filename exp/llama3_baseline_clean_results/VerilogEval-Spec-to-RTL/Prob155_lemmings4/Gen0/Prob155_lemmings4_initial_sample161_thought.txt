The solution involves designing a Moore state machine that models the Lemming's behavior. We need to define the states and transitions based on the given conditions. The states can be: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING, and SPLATTERED. 

- In the WALK_LEFT and WALK_RIGHT states, the Lemming will switch directions if it hits an obstacle.
- When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition to the FALLING state.
- When the Lemming is in the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state and the dig signal is high, it will transition to the DIGGING state.
- In the FALLING state, the Lemming will transition back to the WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT state when the ground reappears. If the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it will transition to the SPLATTERED state.
- In the DIGGING state, the Lemming will continue digging until it reaches the other side, then it will transition to the FALLING state.

We will use a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling.
