The problem can be solved by implementing a Moore state machine with the following states:
- IDLE_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left and is on the ground.
- IDLE_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right and is on the ground.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING_LEFT: The Lemming is digging and was walking left before digging.
- DIGGING_RIGHT: The Lemming is digging and was walking right before digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and is no longer active.

The state machine will transition between these states based on the inputs and the current state.
When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition to the FALLING state.
When the Lemming hits the ground after falling, it will check the number of clock cycles it has been falling.
If it has been falling for more than 20 clock cycles, it will transition to the SPLATTERED state.
Otherwise, it will transition back to the IDLE_LEFT or IDLE_RIGHT state based on its previous direction.
When the Lemming is told to dig, it will transition to the DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT state based on its current direction.
When the ground disappears while the Lemming is digging, it will transition to the FALLING state and then back to the IDLE_LEFT or IDLE_RIGHT state when the ground reappears.
The state machine will also handle bumps and update the walking direction accordingly.
