To implement the TopModule, we can design a Moore state machine with multiple states to represent the different behaviors of the Lemming. The states can be:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and ceased all activities.

We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. If the Lemming hits the ground after falling for more than 20 clock cycles, it will transition to the SPLATTERED state.

The state transitions will be based on the inputs bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. The outputs walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging will be determined by the current state.
