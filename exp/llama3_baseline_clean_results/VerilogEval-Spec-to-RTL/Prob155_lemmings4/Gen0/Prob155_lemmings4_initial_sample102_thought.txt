To model the Lemmings' behavior, we'll use a Moore state machine with states for walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. We'll also use counters to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. 
The state machine will start in the walking left state after reset. 
When the ground is absent, the Lemming will enter the falling state and start counting the clock cycles. 
If the Lemming has been falling for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it will enter a splattered state, where all outputs are 0.
When the dig signal is high and the Lemming is walking on the ground, it will enter the digging state and continue digging until the ground disappears. 
Being bumped while walking will switch the walking direction.
