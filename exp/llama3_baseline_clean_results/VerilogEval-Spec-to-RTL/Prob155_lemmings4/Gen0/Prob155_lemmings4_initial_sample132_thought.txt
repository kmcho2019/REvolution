The problem can be solved by implementing a finite state machine (FSM) with several states:
- walking_left: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- walking_right: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- falling: The Lemming is in free fall.
- digging_left: The Lemming is digging while walking to the left.
- digging_right: The Lemming is digging while walking to the right.
- splattered: The Lemming has splattered and cannot move anymore.
We need to handle the transitions between these states based on the input signals.
We will use a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming is falling.
