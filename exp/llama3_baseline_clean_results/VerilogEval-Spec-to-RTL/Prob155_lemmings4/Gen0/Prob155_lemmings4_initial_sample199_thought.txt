To implement the Moore state machine that models the behavior of a Lemming, we can define several states and transitions based on the given conditions. The states will be: 
- idle_left: The Lemming is walking left.
- idle_right: The Lemming is walking right.
- falling: The Lemming is falling due to lack of ground.
- digging: The Lemming is digging.
- splattered: The Lemming has splattered after falling for more than 20 clock cycles.

We'll use two counters to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling and the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been digging.

Transitions between states will be based on the inputs bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig, as well as the current state and the values of the counters.
