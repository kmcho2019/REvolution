To implement the Moore state machine for the Lemmings game, we can define the following states: 
- IDLE_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left on the ground.
- IDLE_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right on the ground.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling due to lack of ground.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has fallen for too long and splattered.

The transitions between these states will be based on the inputs: bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig. 
We'll use a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling to detect splattering.
