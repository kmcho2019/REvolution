The finite state machine can be designed with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and ceased all activities.

The state transitions are as follows:
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALLING: When ground=0.
- From WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT: When bump_left=1.
- From WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT: When bump_right=1.
- From FALLING to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT: When ground=1, but only if the Lemming hasn't fallen for more than 20 cycles.
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to DIGGING: When dig=1 and ground=1.
- From DIGGING to FALLING: When ground=0.
- From FALLING to SPLATTERED: When ground=1 and the Lemming has fallen for more than 20 cycles.

We will use a counter to count the number of cycles the Lemming has fallen.
