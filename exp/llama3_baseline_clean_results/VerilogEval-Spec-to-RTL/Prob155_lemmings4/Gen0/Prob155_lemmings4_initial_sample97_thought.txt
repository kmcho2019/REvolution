To implement the Lemmings' behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to identify the states and transitions based on the given conditions. 

The states can be defined as follows:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and ceased all activities.

The transitions between these states are triggered by the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) and the current state. 

When the ground disappears, the Lemming transitions from walking to falling. 
When the ground reappears after falling, the Lemming resumes walking in the same direction. 
When the Lemming is told to dig, it transitions from walking to digging if it's on the ground.
When the Lemming hits the ground after falling for more than 20 cycles, it splatters.

We will use a counter to keep track of the number of cycles the Lemming has been falling.
