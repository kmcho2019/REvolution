To implement this behavior, we can define a finite state machine (FSM) with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: the Lemming is walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: the Lemming is walking right
- FALL: the Lemming is falling
- DIG: the Lemming is digging
- SPLATTER: the Lemming has splattered and cannot move anymore

The FSM will transition between these states based on the input signals.
For example, when the Lemming hits an obstacle on the left, it will transition from WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT.
When the ground disappears, the Lemming will transition from WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALL.
When the Lemming is told to dig and it is walking on ground, it will transition from WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to DIG.
When the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it will transition from FALL to SPLATTER.

We can use a counter to keep track of how long the Lemming has been falling.
