To implement the Lemmings' behavior, we need to create a Moore state machine with the following states: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right
- FALL: falling
- DIG: digging
- SPLAT: splattered (final state)

We will use two additional variables to keep track of the Lemming's direction and the number of cycles it has been falling.

When the ground disappears, the Lemming transitions from walking to falling. 
When the ground reappears, the Lemming transitions from falling to walking in the same direction as before the fall.
When the Lemming is told to dig, it transitions from walking to digging if it is on the ground.
When the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it transitions from digging to falling.
When the Lemming hits the ground after falling for more than 20 cycles, it transitions to the SPLAT state.

We will use a modulo 21 counter to count the number of cycles the Lemming has been falling.
