- When the ground reappears, the Lemming resumes walking in the same direction as before the fall.

- When the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it splatters and ceases walking, falling, or digging.
- The FSM is reset to the walking left state when the areset signal is asserted.
