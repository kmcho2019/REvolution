To implement the Lemmings behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to identify the different states the Lemming can be in. Based on the description, the states are: walking left, walking right, falling, digging, and splattered. 

The Moore state machine will have a current state and a next state, with the next state determined by the current state and the inputs. The outputs will be determined by the current state.

The states and transitions can be described as follows:
- The initial state is walking left.
- When the Lemming is walking and bumps into an obstacle, it switches direction.
- When the ground disappears, the Lemming falls.
- When the Lemming is walking and the dig input is high, it starts digging.
- When the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it falls.
- When the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it splatters.
- The Lemming can only splatter when hitting the ground, not in mid-air.

To keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling, a counter can be used.
