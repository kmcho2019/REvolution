To implement the Lemming's behavior as a Moore state machine, we need to define the states and transitions based on the given conditions. The states can be: Walking Left, Walking Right, Falling, Digging, and Splattered. The transitions between these states are determined by the input signals (bump_left, bump_right, ground, dig) and the current state. We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. If the Lemming hits the ground after falling for more than 20 clock cycles, it will transition to the Splattered state.
