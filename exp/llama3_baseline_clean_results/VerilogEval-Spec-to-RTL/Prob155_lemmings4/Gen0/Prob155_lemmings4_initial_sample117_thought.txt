The Lemming's behavior can be modeled as a Moore state machine with several states. 
The states can be: IDLE_LEFT (walking left), IDLE_RIGHT (walking right), FALLING, DIGGING_LEFT, DIGGING_RIGHT, and DEAD (when the Lemming splatters).
The machine will transition between these states based on the input signals. 
For example, when the Lemming is in the IDLE_LEFT state and receives a bump_left signal, it will transition to the IDLE_RIGHT state.
When the Lemming is in the IDLE_LEFT or IDLE_RIGHT state and the ground signal goes low, it will transition to the FALLING state.
When the Lemming is in the IDLE_LEFT or IDLE_RIGHT state and the dig signal is high, it will transition to the DIGGING_LEFT or DIGGING_RIGHT state, respectively.
The machine will also keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling, and if this number exceeds 20 when the Lemming hits the ground, it will transition to the DEAD state.
