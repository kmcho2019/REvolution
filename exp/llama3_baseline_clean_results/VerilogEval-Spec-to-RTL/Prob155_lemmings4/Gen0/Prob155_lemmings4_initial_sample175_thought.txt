The problem can be solved by designing a Moore state machine with states 
representing the different conditions of the Lemming: walking left, walking right, 
falling, digging, and dead. The machine will also keep track of the 
number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling to determine if it 
should splatter upon hitting the ground. Based on the current state and 
inputs, the machine will transition to the next state and update its 
outputs accordingly. The machine will also handle the case where the 
Lemming is bumped while falling or digging, and the case where the 
Lemming is told to dig while falling or when there is no ground.
