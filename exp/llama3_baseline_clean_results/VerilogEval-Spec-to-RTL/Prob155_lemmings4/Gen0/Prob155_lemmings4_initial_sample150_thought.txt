To implement the Moore state machine that models the Lemming's behavior, we will use a finite state machine with several states to keep track of the Lemming's current state. The states will be:
- IDLE_LEFT: Walking left on ground
- IDLE_RIGHT: Walking right on ground
- FALLING: Falling due to no ground
- DIGGING: Digging through the ground
- SPLATTERED: Lemming has splattered after falling for too long

We will use a counter to keep track of how long the Lemming has been falling. When the Lemming hits the ground after falling for more than 20 clock cycles, it will splatter and cease all activities.

The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals:
- bump_left and bump_right: Switch the Lemming's walking direction
- ground: Transition between walking and falling states
- dig: Start digging if the Lemming is currently walking on ground

The outputs will be generated based on the current state:
- walk_left and walk_right: Indicate the Lemming's walking direction
- aaah: Indicate that the Lemming is falling
- digging: Indicate that the Lemming is digging
