To implement this behavior, we will use a Moore state machine with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and is no longer active.
We will use a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling, and reset it whenever the Lemming hits the ground or starts walking again.
