To model the behavior of the Lemming, we can use a finite state machine (FSM) with the following states:
- Walking Left (WL)
- Walking Right (WR)
- Falling (F)
- Digging (D)
- Splattered (S)

We will use a Moore machine, where the output is a function of the current state.

The transitions between states will be based on the inputs:
- bump_left and bump_right to switch walking direction
- ground to switch between walking and falling
- dig to start digging
- time spent falling to determine if the Lemming splatters

We will use a counter to keep track of the time spent falling.

We will also use a flag to keep track of the original walking direction when the Lemming starts digging.
