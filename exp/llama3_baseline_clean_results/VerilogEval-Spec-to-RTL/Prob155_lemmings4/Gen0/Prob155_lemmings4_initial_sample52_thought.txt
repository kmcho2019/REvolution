To implement the Lemming behavior, we can use a Moore state machine with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALLING: The Lemming is falling.
- DIGGING: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTERED: The Lemming has splattered and will not move anymore.

We will use two counters, one to keep track of the direction of the Lemming (left or right) and another to count the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling.

We will also use a variable to keep track of the previous state of the ground, to determine when the Lemming hits the ground.

In each state, we will check the input signals to determine the next state and the outputs.

The outputs will be determined by the current state and the inputs.
