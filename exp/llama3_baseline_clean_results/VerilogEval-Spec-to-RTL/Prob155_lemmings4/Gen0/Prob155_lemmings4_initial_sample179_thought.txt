To implement the described behavior, we will design a Moore state machine with the following states:
- WALK_LEFT: the Lemming is walking left
- WALK_RIGHT: the Lemming is walking right
- FALL: the Lemming is falling
- DIG: the Lemming is digging
- SPLATTER: the Lemming has splattered

We will use two counters: one to count the number of cycles the Lemming has been falling and another to keep track of the previous walking direction.
The state machine will have the following transitions:
- WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT: when the Lemming is bumped on the left
- WALK_LEFT to WALK_RIGHT: when the Lemming is bumped on the right
- WALK_LEFT to FALL: when the ground disappears
- WALK_LEFT to DIG: when the Lemming is told to dig and the ground is present
- WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT: when the Lemming is bumped on the left
- WALK_RIGHT to WALK_LEFT: when the Lemming is bumped on the right
- WALK_RIGHT to FALL: when the ground disappears
- WALK_RIGHT to DIG: when the Lemming is told to dig and the ground is present
- FALL to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT: when the ground reappears and the Lemming has not splattered
- FALL to SPLATTER: when the ground reappears and the Lemming has been falling for more than 20 cycles
- DIG to FALL: when the ground disappears
- DIG to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT: when the ground reappears and the Lemming was previously walking in that direction
- SPLATTER to SPLATTER: in all cases

We will use a parameter to define the number of cycles before the Lemming splatters.
