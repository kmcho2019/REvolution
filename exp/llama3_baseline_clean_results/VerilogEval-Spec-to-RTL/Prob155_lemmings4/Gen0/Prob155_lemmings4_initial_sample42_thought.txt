To model the Lemming's behavior, we can define several states for the finite state machine: 
- WALK_LEFT: walking left on the ground
- WALK_RIGHT: walking right on the ground
- FALLING: falling due to the lack of ground
- DIGGING: digging into the ground
- SPLATTERED: Lemming has splattered and ceased all activities

We will use a Moore state machine, where the outputs are determined solely by the current state. The state transitions will be determined by the current state and the input signals.

We will also need a counter to keep track of how long the Lemming has been falling, to determine if it should splatter when hitting the ground.
