- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking right.
- FALL: The Lemming is falling.
- DIG: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTER: The Lemming has splattered and is no longer active.

- If the Lemming is walking and hits an obstacle, it will switch directions.
- If the Lemming is walking and the ground disappears, it will fall.
- If the Lemming is falling and the ground reappears, it will resume walking in the same direction.
- If the Lemming is walking and is told to dig, it will start digging.
- If the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it will fall.
- If the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground, it will splatter.
