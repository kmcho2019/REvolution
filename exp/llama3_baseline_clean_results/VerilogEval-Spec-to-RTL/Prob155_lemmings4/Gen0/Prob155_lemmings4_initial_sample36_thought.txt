To implement the Lemmings behavior as a finite state machine in Verilog, we'll consider the following states:
- WALKING_LEFT
- WALKING_RIGHT
- FALLING
- DIGGING
- SPLATTERED

We will use an enum to define these states. We will also use two counters: one to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling, and another to keep track of the direction the Lemming was walking before falling or digging.

The machine starts in the WALKING_LEFT state. If the Lemming hits an obstacle on the left or right, it switches directions. If the ground disappears, the Lemming starts falling. While falling, being bumped does not change the direction. When the ground reappears, the Lemming resumes walking in the same direction as before the fall. 

If the dig signal is high and the Lemming is walking on the ground, it starts digging. The Lemming continues digging until it reaches the other side, at which point it starts falling. 

If the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and then hits the ground, it splatters and stops moving. 

The outputs walk_left, walk_right, aaah, and digging are determined by the current state.
