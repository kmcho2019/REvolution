To implement the Lemming state machine, we will use a Moore state machine with several states:
- WALK_LEFT: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- WALK_RIGHT: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- FALL: The Lemming is falling.
- DIG: The Lemming is digging.
- SPLATTER: The Lemming has splattered and will cease all activities.

We will use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. If the counter exceeds 20 and the Lemming hits the ground, it will splatter.

The state transitions will be as follows:
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to FALL: When the ground disappears (ground=0).
- From WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT to DIG: When the Lemming is told to dig (dig=1) and is on the ground (ground=1).
- From FALL to WALK_LEFT or WALK_RIGHT: When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall.
- From DIG to FALL: When the Lemming reaches the other side (ground=0).
- From any state to SPLATTER: When the Lemming splatters (falls for more than 20 clock cycles and hits the ground).

We will use a separate variable to keep track of the Lemming's walking direction before it falls or digs, so it can resume walking in the same direction when the ground reappears or it finishes digging.
