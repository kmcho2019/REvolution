To implement this finite state machine, we will break down the problem into several states and transitions between them. We can use a Moore state machine to model the behavior of the Lemming. The states we will use are:
- walking_left: The Lemming is walking to the left.
- walking_right: The Lemming is walking to the right.
- falling: The Lemming is falling.
- digging: The Lemming is digging.
- splattered: The Lemming has splattered.

The transitions between these states will be based on the inputs to the module. We will also use a counter to keep track of the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling. If the Lemming hits the ground after more than 20 clock cycles of falling, it will splatter.
