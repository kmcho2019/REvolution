To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor, we can implement a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit will have two inputs, ring and vibrate_mode, and two outputs, ringer and motor. If the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor will be turned on whenever there's an incoming call (ring = 1). Otherwise, the ringer will be turned on. This can be achieved using a basic if-else logic or a multiplexer-like structure.
