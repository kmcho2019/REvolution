To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor, we can use a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit should take into account the phone's mode (vibrate or ring) and the incoming call signal. If the phone is in vibrate mode, it should turn on the motor when there's an incoming call. Otherwise, it should turn on the ringer. We can achieve this using basic logical operators, specifically the AND and OR operators, along with the NOT operator for handling the vibrate mode.
