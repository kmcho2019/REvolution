To implement the TopModule, we will use a simple combinational logic circuit that takes into account the 'ring' and 'vibrate_mode' inputs to control the 'ringer' and 'motor' outputs. The circuit will have the following logic: if the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor will be turned on when the phone needs to ring, otherwise the ringer will be turned on. This can be implemented using basic logical operations such as AND and OR gates, along with a multiplexer or a conditional statement in Verilog.
