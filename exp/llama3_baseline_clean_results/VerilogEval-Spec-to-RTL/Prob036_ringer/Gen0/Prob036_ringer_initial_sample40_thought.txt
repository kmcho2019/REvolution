To implement the TopModule, we need to create a simple digital circuit that controls the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the ring and vibrate_mode inputs. The circuit should use logical operations to determine whether to turn on the ringer or the motor. If vibrate_mode is 1, the circuit should turn on the motor. Otherwise, it should turn on the ringer. We can achieve this using a combination of AND and OR gates, but in Verilog, we can directly use conditional statements or logical operators to simplify the design.
