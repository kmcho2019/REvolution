To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit will have two inputs, ring and vibrate_mode, and two outputs, ringer and motor. The logic can be implemented using a basic if-else statement in Verilog. If the phone is ringing (ring = 1) and it's in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we turn on the motor. If the phone is ringing but not in vibrate mode, we turn on the ringer. If the phone is not ringing, both the ringer and motor should be off.
