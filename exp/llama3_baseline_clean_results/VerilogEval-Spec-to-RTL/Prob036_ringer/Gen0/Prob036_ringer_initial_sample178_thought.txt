To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor, we need to design a simple digital circuit that takes into account the 'ring' and 'vibrate_mode' inputs. The circuit should have two output ports: 'ringer' and 'motor'. When 'ring' is high, if 'vibrate_mode' is high, 'motor' should be high and 'ringer' should be low. If 'vibrate_mode' is low, 'ringer' should be high and 'motor' should be low. This logic can be implemented using basic digital gates.
