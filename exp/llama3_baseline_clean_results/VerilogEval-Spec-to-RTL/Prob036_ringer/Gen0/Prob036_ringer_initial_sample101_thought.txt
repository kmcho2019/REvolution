To implement the TopModule, we will use a simple combinational logic approach. The module will have two inputs, ring and vibrate_mode, and two outputs, ringer and motor. Based on the input ring and vibrate_mode, we will set the outputs ringer and motor. If the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor will be turned on when there's an incoming call (ring = 1). Otherwise, the ringer will be turned on. This can be achieved using a conditional statement in Verilog that checks the value of vibrate_mode and ring to assign the correct values to ringer and motor.
