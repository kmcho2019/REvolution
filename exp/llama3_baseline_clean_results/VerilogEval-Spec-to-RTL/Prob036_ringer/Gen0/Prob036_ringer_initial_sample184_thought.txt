To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two input signals: ring and vibrate_mode. The ring signal indicates whether the phone needs to ring or not. The vibrate_mode signal determines whether the phone should ring or vibrate. We can use a simple logic circuit to control the ringer and motor outputs. When the phone needs to ring (ring = 1) and it's not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0), we turn on the ringer. If the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we turn on the motor instead.
