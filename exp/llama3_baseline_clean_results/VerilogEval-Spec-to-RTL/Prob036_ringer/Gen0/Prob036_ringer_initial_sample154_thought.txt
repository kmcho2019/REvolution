To implement the TopModule, we need to create a simple digital circuit that controls the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode signals. The circuit should have a conditional logic to decide whether to turn on the ringer or the motor when the phone needs to ring. If the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor should be turned on; otherwise, the ringer should be turned on. This can be achieved using basic logical operations such as AND and OR gates, or more conveniently, using a single conditional statement in Verilog.
