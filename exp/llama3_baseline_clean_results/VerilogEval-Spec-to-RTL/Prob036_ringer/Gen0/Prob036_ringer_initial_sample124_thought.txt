The TopModule can be implemented using a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit will have two inputs: ring and vibrate_mode. When the ring input is high, the circuit will check the vibrate_mode input. If vibrate_mode is high, the circuit will turn on the motor output. Otherwise, it will turn on the ringer output. This can be achieved using a multiplexer-like logic where the ring input is the enable signal and the vibrate_mode input is the select signal.
