To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two possible modes of operation: ring mode and vibrate mode. When the phone is in ring mode (vibrate_mode = 0), we should turn on the ringer when an incoming call is detected (ring = 1). When the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we should turn on the motor instead. This can be achieved using simple logic gates, specifically AND and OR gates, to control the outputs based on the inputs.
