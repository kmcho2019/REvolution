To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode, we can use a simple conditional logic. When the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we should turn on the motor and keep the ringer off. When the phone is not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0), we should turn on the ringer and keep the motor off. This logic can be implemented using a combination of AND and OR gates in Verilog.
