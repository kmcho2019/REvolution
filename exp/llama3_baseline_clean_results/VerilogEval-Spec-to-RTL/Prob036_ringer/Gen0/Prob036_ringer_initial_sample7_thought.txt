To implement the TopModule, we need to use a combination of logic gates to control the ringer and motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode signals. We can use an AND gate to enable the ringer when the phone is not in vibrate mode and an AND gate with a NOT gate to enable the motor when the phone is in vibrate mode. The ring signal should be one of the inputs to both AND gates to ensure that the ringer or motor is only enabled when there is an incoming call.
