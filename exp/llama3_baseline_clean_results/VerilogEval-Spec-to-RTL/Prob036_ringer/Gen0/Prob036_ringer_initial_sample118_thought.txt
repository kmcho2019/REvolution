To implement the TopModule, we will use a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit will have two inputs, 'ring' and 'vibrate_mode', and two outputs, 'ringer' and 'motor'. When 'ring' is high, we need to turn on either 'ringer' or 'motor' depending on the value of 'vibrate_mode'. If 'vibrate_mode' is high, we turn on 'motor', otherwise we turn on 'ringer'. This can be achieved using a multiplexer-like logic.
