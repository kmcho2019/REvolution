To implement the TopModule, we need to create a simple digital circuit that controls the ringer and vibration motor of a cellphone based on the input ring and vibrate_mode signals. The module will use basic logic operations to determine whether to turn on the ringer or the motor. If the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor should be turned on when there's an incoming call. Otherwise, the ringer should be turned on. This logic can be achieved using a combination of AND and NOT gates.
