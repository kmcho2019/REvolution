To implement the TopModule, we need to design a simple combinational logic circuit that takes into account the ring and vibrate_mode inputs to control the ringer and motor outputs. When the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), the circuit should turn on the motor regardless of the ring input. When the phone is not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0), the circuit should turn on the ringer when the phone needs to ring (ring = 1). This can be achieved using basic logical operators.
