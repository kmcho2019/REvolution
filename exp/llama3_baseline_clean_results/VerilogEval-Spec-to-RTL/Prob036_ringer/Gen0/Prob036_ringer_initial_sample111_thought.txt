To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two input signals: ring and vibrate_mode. The ring signal indicates whether the phone needs to ring, and the vibrate_mode signal determines whether the phone is in vibrate mode or not. Based on these inputs, we can use simple logical operations to control the outputs ringer and motor. Specifically, if the phone is in vibrate mode, we should turn on the motor when the ring signal is high. Otherwise, we should turn on the ringer when the ring signal is high.
