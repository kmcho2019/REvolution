The TopModule should be designed to control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the ring input and the vibrate_mode. To achieve this, we can use a simple logic circuit that checks the vibrate_mode and the ring input. If the phone is in vibrate mode and needs to ring, the motor should be turned on. Otherwise, if the phone needs to ring and is not in vibrate mode, the ringer should be turned on. This can be implemented using a combination of AND and NOT gates to control the outputs based on the inputs.
