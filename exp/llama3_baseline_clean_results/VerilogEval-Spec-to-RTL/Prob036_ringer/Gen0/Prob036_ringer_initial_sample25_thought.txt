To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor, we need to design a simple digital circuit that can be implemented using basic logical operations. The circuit should take into account the phone's mode (vibrate or ring) and the incoming call signal. We will use an AND gate to enable the motor when the phone is in vibrate mode and there's an incoming call. Similarly, we will use an AND gate along with a NOT gate to enable the ringer when the phone is not in vibrate mode and there's an incoming call. This ensures that either the ringer or the motor is turned on, but not both.
