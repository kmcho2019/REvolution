The TopModule should be designed to control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring signal and the vibrate_mode. To achieve this, we can use a simple conditional logic: when the ring signal is high, check the vibrate_mode. If vibrate_mode is high, turn on the motor; otherwise, turn on the ringer. This can be implemented using an if-else statement or a multiplexer in Verilog.
