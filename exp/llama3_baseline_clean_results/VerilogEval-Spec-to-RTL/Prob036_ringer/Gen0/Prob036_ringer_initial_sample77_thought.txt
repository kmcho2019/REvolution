To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode, we can use a simple logical circuit. The module TopModule will have two inputs (ring and vibrate_mode) and two outputs (ringer and motor). When the phone needs to ring (ring = 1), we will check the vibrate_mode. If the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we will turn on the motor (motor = 1) and keep the ringer off (ringer = 0). If the phone is not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0), we will turn on the ringer (ringer = 1) and keep the motor off (motor = 0). If the phone does not need to ring (ring = 0), both the ringer and the motor should be turned off.
