To implement the TopModule, we need to use basic logical operations to control the ringer and motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode signals. When the phone needs to ring (ring = 1) and it's in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1), we should turn on the motor (motor = 1) and keep the ringer off (ringer = 0). If the phone is not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0) and needs to ring, we should turn on the ringer (ringer = 1) and keep the motor off (motor = 0). We can achieve this using AND and OR gates to combine the ring and vibrate_mode signals appropriately.
