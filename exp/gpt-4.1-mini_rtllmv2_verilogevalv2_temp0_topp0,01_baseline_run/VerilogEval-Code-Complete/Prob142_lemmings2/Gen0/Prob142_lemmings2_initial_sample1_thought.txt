We model the Lemming's behavior with a Moore FSM having three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. On reset, the Lemming starts walking left. When ground=0, the Lemming enters FALLING state and asserts aaah. While falling, bump inputs do not affect direction. When ground returns to 1, the Lemming resumes walking in the direction it had before falling. Bumps only affect direction when on the ground and not falling. If bumped on either side or both, the Lemming switches direction. The outputs are determined solely by the current state.
