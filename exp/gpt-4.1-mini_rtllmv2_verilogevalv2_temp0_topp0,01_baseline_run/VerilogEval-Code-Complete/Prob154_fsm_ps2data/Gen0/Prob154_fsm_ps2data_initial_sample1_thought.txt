Implement a simple FSM with three states: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. In IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3] = 1 is found, which is the first byte of a message. Then move to BYTE2 state and store the first byte in the highest byte of out_bytes. In BYTE2, store the second byte in the middle byte of out_bytes and move to BYTE3. In BYTE3, store the third byte in the lowest byte of out_bytes and assert done for one cycle. Then return to IDLE to look for the next message. The reset is synchronous and active high, resetting the FSM to IDLE and clearing outputs.
