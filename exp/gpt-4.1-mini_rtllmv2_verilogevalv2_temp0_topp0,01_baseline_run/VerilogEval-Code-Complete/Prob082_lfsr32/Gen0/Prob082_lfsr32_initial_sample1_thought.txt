Implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bits 32, 22, 2, and 1. The LFSR shifts right each clock cycle. The new bit shifted into the MSB (bit 31) is the XOR of the LSB (q[0]) and the tapped bits. For Galois LFSR, the feedback is applied conditionally: if q[0] is 1, then the tapped bits are XORed with q[0] to produce their next state; otherwise, bits just shift right. On synchronous reset, q is set to 32'h1.
