Implement a synchronous 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. The clock increments seconds on each ena pulse. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. When seconds reach 59 and increment, minutes increment; when minutes reach 59 and increment, hours increment. Hours roll over from 12 to 1, toggling the pm signal at the rollover from 11:59:59 to 12:00:00. Reset synchronously sets the time to 12:00 AM. The counters are implemented in BCD with proper carry handling.
