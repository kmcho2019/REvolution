Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (indexing from 1). The LFSR shifts right on each clock cycle. The feedback bit is the XOR of the output bit q[0] and the tap bits q[4] (bit 5) and q[2] (bit 3). For each bit position, if it is tapped, its next value is the XOR of q[0] and the current bit; otherwise, it just shifts the bit to the right. On synchronous reset, set q to 5'b00001.
