First, interpret the Karnaugh map with variables a,b as columns and c,d as rows. The map is:

       ab
cd   01  00  10  11
00 | d | 0 | 1 | 1 |
01 | 0 | 0 | d | d |
11 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
10 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

Mapping the inputs to the outputs:

- For cd=00 (c=0,d=0):
  ab=01 (a=0,b=1): d (don't care)
  ab=00 (a=0,b=0): 0
  ab=10 (a=1,b=0): 1
  ab=11 (a=1,b=1): 1

- For cd=01 (c=0,d=1):
  ab=01: 0
  ab=00: 0
  ab=10: d
  ab=11: d

- For cd=11 (c=1,d=1):
  ab=01: 0
  ab=00: 1
  ab=10: 1
  ab=11: 1

- For cd=10 (c=1,d=0):
  ab=01: 0
  ab=00: 1
  ab=10: 1
  ab=11: 1

We can use the don't cares to simplify the expression.

Let's write the minterms where output=1:

- cd=00, ab=10 (a=1,b=0,c=0,d=0)
- cd=00, ab=11 (a=1,b=1,c=0,d=0)
- cd=11, ab=00 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=1)
- cd=11, ab=10 (a=1,b=0,c=1,d=1)
- cd=11, ab=11 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=1)
- cd=10, ab=00 (a=0,b=0,c=1,d=0)
- cd=10, ab=10 (a=1,b=0,c=1,d=0)
- cd=10, ab=11 (a=1,b=1,c=1,d=0)

Also, ab=00, cd=11 and cd=10 are 1, so for c=1,d=x and a=0,b=0 output=1.

Similarly, for c=1,d=1 or d=0 and a=1,b=0 or a=1,b=1 output=1.

For cd=00, ab=10 and 11 output=1.

Try to find groups:

Group 1: All cells where c=1 (cd=10 or 11) and b=0 or b=1 and a=0 or 1, except ab=01 (a=0,b=1) is 0.

But ab=01 is 0 for c=1 rows.

Group 2: For cd=00, ab=10 and 11 are 1.

Group 3: For cd=11 and 10, ab=00 is 1.

Group 4: For cd=00, ab=00 is 0.

Group 5: For cd=01, ab=00 and 01 are 0.

Try to write expression:

- For c=1 (cd=10 or 11), output=1 except when ab=01 (a=0,b=1) which is 0.

So for c=1 and not (a=0,b=1) output=1.

- For cd=00 (c=0,d=0), output=1 when ab=10 or 11 (a=1,b=0 or a=1,b=1).

- For cd=01 (c=0,d=1), output=0 or don't care.

So expression:

out = (c & ~(~a & b)) | (~c & ~d & a)

Explanation:

- c & ~(~a & b): c=1 and not (a=0,b=1) means c=1 and (a=1 or b=0)

- ~c & ~d & a: c=0,d=0,a=1

This matches the map.
