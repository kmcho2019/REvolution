From the waveform, the circuit has one flip-flop with output q and a combinational logic that determines the next state (state) based on inputs a, b, and current q. The state output reflects the current state of the flip-flop (q). Observing the transitions:

- q is updated on the rising edge of clk.
- The next state logic depends on a, b, and current q.
- When a=0 and b=1, q becomes 1.
- When a=1 and b=1, q becomes 0.
- When a=1 and b=0, q remains the same or toggles depending on previous state.
- When a=0 and b=0, q remains 0.

From the pattern, the next state can be described as:
next_q = (b & ~q) | (~a & b) | (a & b & ~q) simplified to next_q = (b & ~q) | (~a & b)

But more simply, from the waveform, the next state q is:
q_next = (b & ~q) | (~a & b)

The output state is just the current q.

Implement a D flip-flop with synchronous update on rising edge of clk, and combinational logic for next state as above.
