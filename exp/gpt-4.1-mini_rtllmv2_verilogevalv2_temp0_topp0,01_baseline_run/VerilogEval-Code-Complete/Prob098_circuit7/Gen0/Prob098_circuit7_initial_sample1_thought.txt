The output q updates on the rising edge of clk. From the waveform, q becomes 1 when clk rises and a is 0, and q becomes 0 when clk rises and a is 1. If a is 0 at rising edge, q is set to 1; if a is 1 at rising edge, q is reset to 0. Thus, q follows the inverse of a sampled at the rising edge of clk.
