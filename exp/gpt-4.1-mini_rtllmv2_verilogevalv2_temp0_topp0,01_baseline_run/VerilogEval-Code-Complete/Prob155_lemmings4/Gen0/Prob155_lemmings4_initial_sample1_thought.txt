We will implement a Moore FSM with states encoding the Lemming's current action and direction. The states are:

- WALK_LEFT
- WALK_RIGHT
- DIG_LEFT
- DIG_RIGHT
- FALL_LEFT
- FALL_RIGHT
- SPLATTER (final state)

The FSM transitions are based on inputs bump_left, bump_right, ground, and dig, with priority: fall > dig > bump direction change.

- When ground=0 and not already falling or splattered, transition to FALL state preserving direction.
- When falling and ground=1, if fall duration > 20 cycles, transition to SPLATTER, else resume walking in previous direction.
- When walking on ground and dig=1, transition to DIG state in current direction.
- When digging and ground=0, transition to FALL state in current direction.
- When walking and bumped (left or right or both), switch walking direction.
- Bumps have no effect when falling or digging.
- In SPLATTER state, all outputs are 0 forever until reset.

We will keep a fall counter to track how long the Lemming has been falling.

Outputs are determined solely by the current state (Moore machine).

The asynchronous reset sets the FSM to WALK_LEFT and clears the fall counter.

We will implement the FSM with a state register and a fall counter register, updating on the positive clock edge, with asynchronous reset.

Outputs are assigned combinationally from the current state.
