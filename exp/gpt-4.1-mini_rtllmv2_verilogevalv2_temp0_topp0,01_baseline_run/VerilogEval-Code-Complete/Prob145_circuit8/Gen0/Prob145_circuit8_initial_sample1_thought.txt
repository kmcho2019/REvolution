From the waveform, the outputs p and q are sequentially updated on clock edges and depend on input a and previous states. Initially, p and q are unknown (x). When clock is high, p follows a (p = a), and q remains unchanged. When clock is low, q follows p (q = p), and p is updated based on a and previous p and q values. The behavior suggests a two-stage register where p captures a on clock high, and q captures p on clock low. Implementing this with two always blocks triggered on clock edges or using clock and its negation to update p and q accordingly.
