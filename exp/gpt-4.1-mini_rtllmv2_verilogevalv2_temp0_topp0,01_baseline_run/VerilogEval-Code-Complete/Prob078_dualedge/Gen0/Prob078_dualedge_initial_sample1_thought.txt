To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop on an FPGA that only supports single-edge triggered flip-flops, we can use two flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and one triggered on the negative edge. Both flip-flops sample the input 'd' on their respective edges. Then, we combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock to produce the final output 'q'. This way, 'q' updates on both edges of the clock, effectively emulating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
