Implement a simple FSM with three states: IDLE (waiting for a byte with in[3]=1), BYTE1 (first byte received), BYTE2 (second byte received). When in IDLE, discard bytes until in[3] is 1, then capture that byte as the first byte and move to BYTE1. In BYTE1, capture the second byte and move to BYTE2. In BYTE2, capture the third byte and assert done for one cycle, outputting the concatenated 3-byte message. After done, return to IDLE to look for the next message. Use synchronous reset to initialize state and outputs.
