Implement a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The clock increments seconds on each ena pulse. Seconds and minutes count from 00 to 59 in BCD, and hours count from 01 to 12 in BCD. The pm flag toggles when the hour rolls from 11 to 12. On reset, the clock is set to 12:00 AM (pm=0, hh=12, mm=00, ss=00). All counters are synchronous and triggered on the positive edge of clk. Reset is synchronous and has priority over enable.
