Implement a 3-state FSM: IDLE, BYTE2, BYTE3. Initially in IDLE, discard bytes until a byte with in[3]=1 is received, indicating the start of a message. Transition to BYTE2 on that byte. Then on next byte, move to BYTE3, and on the next byte after that, signal done and return to IDLE to look for the next message. The done signal is asserted for one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is received. Reset synchronously returns FSM to IDLE and clears done.
