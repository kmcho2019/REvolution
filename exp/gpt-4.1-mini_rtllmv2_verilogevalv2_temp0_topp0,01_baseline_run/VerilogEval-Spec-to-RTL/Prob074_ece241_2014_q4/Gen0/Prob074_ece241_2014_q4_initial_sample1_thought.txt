Implement three D flip-flops (ff_xor, ff_and, ff_or) each updated on the positive edge of clk. For each flip-flop, compute the D input as follows:
- D_xor = x XOR ff_xor
- D_and = x AND ~ff_and
- D_or = x OR ~ff_or

After updating the flip-flops, compute output z as the NOR of all three flip-flop outputs:
z = ~(ff_xor | ff_and | ff_or)

Initialize all flip-flops to zero at the start.
