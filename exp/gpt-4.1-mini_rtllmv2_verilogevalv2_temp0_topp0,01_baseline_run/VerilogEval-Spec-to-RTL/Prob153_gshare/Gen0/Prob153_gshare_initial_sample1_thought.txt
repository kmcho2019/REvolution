Implement a gshare branch predictor with a 7-bit global history register and a 128-entry PHT of 2-bit saturating counters indexed by XOR of PC and global history. On prediction (predict_valid), output prediction and current history, then update global history with predicted direction at next clock edge. On training (train_valid), update the PHT entry indexed by train_pc XOR train_history with the actual outcome, saturating the counter. If mispredicted, restore global history to train_history (the history after the mispredicted branch). If both training (mispredicted) and prediction occur in the same cycle, training takes precedence for updating global history. Prediction reads PHT state before training update in the same cycle. Asynchronous reset clears global history and PHT entries to weakly taken (2'b10). All sequential logic on posedge clk.
