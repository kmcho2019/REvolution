Implement a 5-bit Galois LFSR with taps at bit positions 5 and 3 (1-based indexing). The LFSR shifts right on each clock edge. The feedback bit is the XOR of the output bit q[0] and the tap bits q[4] (bit 5) and q[2] (bit 3). On reset, the output q is set to 1. The LFSR updates synchronously on the positive clock edge.
