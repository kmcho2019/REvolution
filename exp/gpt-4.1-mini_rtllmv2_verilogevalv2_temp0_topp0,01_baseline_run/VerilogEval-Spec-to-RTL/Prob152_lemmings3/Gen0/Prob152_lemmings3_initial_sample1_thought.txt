Implement a Moore FSM with states representing walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The outputs depend only on the current state. On reset, the Lemming walks left. The FSM transitions are prioritized: falling (ground=0) > digging (dig=1 and ground=1 and not falling) > switching directions (bump_left or bump_right). When falling, bumps and dig commands are ignored. When digging, bumps and dig commands are ignored, and the Lemming falls when ground disappears. When walking, bumps cause direction switch. The walking direction is stored in the state. Outputs are asserted based on the state. Asynchronous reset sets state to walk left.
