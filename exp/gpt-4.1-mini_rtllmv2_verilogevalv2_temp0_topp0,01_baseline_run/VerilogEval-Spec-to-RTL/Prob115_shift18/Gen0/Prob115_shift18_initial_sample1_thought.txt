Implement a 64-bit register that on the rising edge of clk either loads the input data if load is high, or if ena is high, shifts the register contents according to the amount input. The shift direction and amount are decoded from the 2-bit amount input. For left shifts, shift in zeros on the right. For right shifts, perform arithmetic right shift by replicating the sign bit on the left. If neither load nor ena is high, hold the current value.
