To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using only single-edge triggered flip-flops (which are supported by FPGAs), we can use two flip-flops: one triggered on the positive edge of the clock and one triggered on the negative edge. Both flip-flops sample the input 'd' on their respective clock edges. Then, we combine their outputs using a multiplexer controlled by the clock to select the correct output depending on the clock level. This way, the output 'q' updates on both edges of the clock, effectively emulating a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
