The Karnaugh map shows the output for all combinations of inputs a, b, and c. The map is organized with 'bc' as rows and 'a' as columns. The output is 0 only when a=0, b=0, c=0; otherwise, the output is 1. This means the output is the negation of (a=0, b=0, c=0), or equivalently, the output is 1 when any of a, b, or c is 1. So the output is the OR of a, b, and c.
