Implement a Moore FSM with three states: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, and FALLING. The outputs walk_left and walk_right are asserted in the respective walking states, and aaah is asserted in the FALLING state. On reset, the FSM goes to WALK_LEFT. When ground=0, the FSM transitions to FALLING, remembering the last walking direction. While falling, bump inputs do not affect direction. When ground returns to 1, the FSM returns to the remembered walking direction. When walking, bump_left or bump_right (or both) cause direction to switch. The FSM is updated on the positive clock edge, with asynchronous positive edge reset.
