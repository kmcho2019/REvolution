Use a simple finite state machine (FSM) with four states to detect the pulse pattern 0->1->0 over three cycles. The states represent waiting for the first 0, detecting the rising edge to 1, detecting the falling edge back to 0, and then outputting data_out=1 for one cycle at the end of the pulse. On reset, initialize to the waiting state. On each clock, transition states based on data_in and generate data_out=1 only at the end of the pulse.
