Implement a fixed-point subtractor module parameterized by total bits N and fractional bits Q. Inputs a and b are N-bit fixed-point numbers in two's complement format. The subtraction logic depends on the signs of a and b:

- If signs are the same, subtract b from a directly.
- If signs differ, add the absolute values and determine the sign of the result based on magnitude comparison.

Use two's complement arithmetic for addition and subtraction. After computing the result, if it is zero, explicitly clear the sign bit to 0. Store the result in an internal register res and assign it to output c.

This approach ensures correct fixed-point subtraction with sign handling and precision defined by Q and N.
