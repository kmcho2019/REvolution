Implement a fixed-point subtractor module parameterized by total bits N and fractional bits Q. Inputs a and b are N-bit fixed-point numbers with sign-magnitude representation (assuming two's complement). The subtraction logic depends on the signs of a and b:

- If signs are the same, subtract magnitudes and keep the sign.
- If signs differ, add magnitudes and assign sign based on which operand has larger magnitude.
- Handle zero result by explicitly setting sign bit to 0.
- Use a register to store the result before assigning to output.

The module will perform subtraction with correct sign and magnitude handling, preserving fixed-point precision.
