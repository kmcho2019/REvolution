Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The FSM controls the traffic light signals and timing. The internal counter counts down the duration of each state. The pedestrian button shortens the green light duration to 10 cycles if the remaining green time is greater than 10. The outputs red, yellow, green are updated synchronously. The counter is loaded with the appropriate duration at state transitions or when the pedestrian button is pressed during green. The state transitions occur when the counter reaches zero.
