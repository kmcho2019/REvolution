Implement a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, s3_green. The FSM controls the traffic light signals and timing. The internal counter counts down the duration of each state. The pedestrian button shortens the green light to 10 cycles if more than 10 cycles remain. The outputs red, yellow, green are updated synchronously. The pedestrian button only affects the green state duration. The sidewalk light is implied by the lane light state. The counter reloads according to the state transitions and pedestrian requests.
