Implement a synchronous instruction register module with two 8-bit registers ins_p1 and ins_p2. On reset (active low), clear both registers. On each rising clock edge, update ins_p1 if fetch == 2'b01, update ins_p2 if fetch == 2'b10, otherwise retain previous values. Outputs ins and ad1 are derived from ins_p1 (high 3 bits and low 5 bits respectively), and ad2 is the full 8-bit value of ins_p2.
