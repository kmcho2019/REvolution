Implement a radix-2 division algorithm for 8-bit signed or unsigned integers. On opn_valid, latch inputs and initialize internal registers: SR holds the dividend shifted left by 1, NEG_DIVISOR holds the negated divisor, cnt and start_cnt start the division process. Each clock cycle, if start_cnt is active, perform one step of the division by subtracting NEG_DIVISOR from the upper bits of SR, update SR based on the subtraction result and increment cnt. When cnt reaches 8, division is done, clear start_cnt and cnt, and prepare the final result with remainder in upper 8 bits and quotient in lower 8 bits. Manage sign correction for signed division by taking absolute values at start and restoring signs at the end. res_valid signals when the result is ready.
