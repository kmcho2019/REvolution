To implement a 64-bit ripple carry adder with pipelining, I will split the 64-bit addition into multiple pipeline stages, each handling a portion of the bits and passing the carry to the next stage. Each stage will have registers to store partial sums and carry, synchronized with clk and rst_n. The input enable (i_en) will be pipelined through registers to generate the output enable (o_en) indicating when the final result is valid. The final output will be a 65-bit sum (including carry out). This approach balances the ripple carry delay by pipelining the addition across multiple clock cycles.
