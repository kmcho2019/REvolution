Implement a Moore FSM with five states: IDLE, S1, S2, S3, and S4. The FSM transitions on each clock cycle based on the input bit data_in to detect the sequence 1001. When the sequence is detected (reaching S4), the output sequence_detected is asserted for one clock cycle. The FSM resets to IDLE on reset_n being low (active low reset). The FSM transitions are designed to allow overlapping sequences.
