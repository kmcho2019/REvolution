Implement an 8-bit unsigned multiplier using a pipelined architecture. The pipeline stages include input registering, partial product generation, partial sum calculation, and final product accumulation. The input enable signal is pipelined alongside the data to generate the output enable signal. Partial products are generated by ANDing the multiplicand with each bit of the multiplier, shifted accordingly. These partial products are then summed in a pipelined manner to improve throughput. The final product and output enable are registered and output accordingly. Reset is active low and synchronous.
