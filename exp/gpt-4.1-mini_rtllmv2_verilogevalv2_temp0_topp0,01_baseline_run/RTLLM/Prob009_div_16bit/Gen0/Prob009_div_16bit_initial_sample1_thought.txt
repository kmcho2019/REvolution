Implement a combinational 16-bit by 8-bit divider using a shift-subtract division algorithm. Use two always blocks: the first to register inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg, and the second to perform the division. The division logic extracts the top bits of the current remainder (starting with the highest bits of a_reg), compares with b_reg, sets the quotient bit accordingly, subtracts if possible, and shifts in the next bit of the dividend. Repeat this for all 16 bits to produce a 16-bit quotient and a 16-bit remainder.
