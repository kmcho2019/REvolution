Implement a 16-bit divider with an 8-bit divisor using combinational logic. Use two always blocks: the first to register inputs A and B into a_reg and b_reg, and the second to perform the division algorithm. The division algorithm extracts the top bits of the current remainder (starting with the highest 8 bits of a_reg), compares with b_reg, sets the quotient bit accordingly, and updates the remainder by concatenating the difference with the next bit of the dividend. Repeat this for all 16 bits to produce a 16-bit quotient and 16-bit remainder.
