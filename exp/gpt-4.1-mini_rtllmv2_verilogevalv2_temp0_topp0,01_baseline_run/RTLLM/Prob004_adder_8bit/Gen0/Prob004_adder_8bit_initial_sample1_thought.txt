Implement an 8-bit adder by chaining eight 1-bit full adders. Each full adder takes two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out of each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next higher bit full adder. The first full adder uses the module's cin as carry-in, and the last full adder's carry-out is the module's cout.
