Implement an 8-bit barrel shifter that rotates bits based on a 3-bit control signal. The shifting is done in three stages corresponding to shifts by 4, 2, and 1 positions. Each stage uses 8 instances of a 2-to-1 multiplexer (mux2X1) to select between the original bit and the bit shifted by the stage amount. The mux selects the shifted bit if the corresponding control bit is high, otherwise the original bit. The output of one stage feeds into the next stage. The final output is the rotated result after all enabled shifts.
