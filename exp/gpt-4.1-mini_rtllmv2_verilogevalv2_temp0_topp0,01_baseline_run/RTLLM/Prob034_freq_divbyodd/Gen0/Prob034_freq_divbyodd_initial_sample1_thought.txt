To divide the input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 that count clock edges separately for rising and falling edges. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When each counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock (clk_div1 for rising edge, clk_div2 for falling edge) toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of clk_div1 and clk_div2, effectively generating a clock with a frequency divided by the odd number. The asynchronous active low reset rst_n initializes counters and outputs.
