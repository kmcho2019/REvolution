To divide the input clock by an odd number NUM_DIV, we use two counters cnt1 and cnt2 that count clock edges on the positive and negative edges respectively. Each counter counts from 0 to NUM_DIV-1. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV (integer division), the corresponding divided clock output toggles. The final output clk_div is the OR of these two divided clocks, effectively generating a clock with a period of NUM_DIV input clock cycles. The active low reset initializes counters and outputs to zero.
