To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder (CLA) using two 16-bit CLA blocks, I will first design a 16-bit CLA module that takes two 16-bit inputs and a carry-in, and produces a 16-bit sum and a carry-out. The 16-bit CLA will internally generate propagate and generate signals for each bit, then compute carry signals using the carry-lookahead logic to achieve fast addition.

Then, the top-level 32-bit adder module will instantiate two 16-bit CLA blocks: the lower 16 bits and the upper 16 bits. The carry-out from the lower 16-bit CLA will be connected as the carry-in to the upper 16-bit CLA. The final carry-out from the upper 16-bit CLA will be the 32-bit carry-out.

The inputs and outputs are 1-based indexing as per the problem statement (A[32:1], B[32:1], S[32:1]), so I will carefully handle the bit ranges accordingly.

This hierarchical design leverages the CLA architecture for fast addition and modularity.
