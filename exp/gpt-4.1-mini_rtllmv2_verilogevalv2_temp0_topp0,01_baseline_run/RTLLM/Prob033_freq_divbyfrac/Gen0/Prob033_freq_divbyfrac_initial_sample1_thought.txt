To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we count 7 input clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK=7). We generate two intermediate clocks: one with a high period of 4 cycles and another with a high period of 3 cycles, effectively creating uneven clock periods. Then, by phase-shifting one intermediate clock by half a clock cycle (using the input clock edges), and OR-ing both intermediate clocks, we produce a final output clock with a uniform period and a frequency divided by 3.5. The design uses a counter to track the clock cycles and generates the intermediate clocks accordingly. The reset initializes the counter and outputs.
