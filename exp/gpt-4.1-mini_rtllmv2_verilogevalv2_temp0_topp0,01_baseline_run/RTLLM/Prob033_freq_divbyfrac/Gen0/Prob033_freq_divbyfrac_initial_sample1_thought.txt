To implement a fractional frequency divider by 3.5 using double-edge clocking, we count 7 input clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK=7) to create a base period. We generate two clock pulses within this period: one lasting 4 cycles and the other 3 cycles, alternating to achieve the fractional division. We create two intermediate clocks: one aligned with the input clock edges and another phase-shifted by half a clock period (achieved by toggling on the opposite clock edge). By OR-ing these two intermediate clocks, we produce a final output clock with a uniform period and a 3.5 division factor. The design uses a counter to track cycles, toggles intermediate clocks accordingly, and resets asynchronously with active low reset.
