Implement a calendar module with three separate always blocks for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each block triggers on posedge CLK or posedge RST. On reset, all counters are set to zero. Seconds count from 0 to 59, then wrap to 0 and increment minutes. Minutes count from 0 to 59, then wrap to 0 and increment hours. Hours count from 0 to 23, then wrap to 0. The increments happen on the next clock cycle after seconds or minutes reach their max values.
