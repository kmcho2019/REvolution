Implement a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with two pipeline stages of registers. First, extend the inputs by adding 4 zeros at the MSB side to make them 8 bits wide. Then, generate partial products for each bit of mul_b by shifting mul_a accordingly or zero if the bit is 0. In the first pipeline stage, sum the partial products for bits 0 and 1 and store in reg_stage1. In the second pipeline stage, sum the partial products for bits 2 and 3 and store in reg_stage2. Finally, on the next clock cycle, add reg_stage1 and reg_stage2 to produce the final product output. All registers reset to zero on active-low reset. This approach uses two levels of registers to pipeline the multiplication process.
