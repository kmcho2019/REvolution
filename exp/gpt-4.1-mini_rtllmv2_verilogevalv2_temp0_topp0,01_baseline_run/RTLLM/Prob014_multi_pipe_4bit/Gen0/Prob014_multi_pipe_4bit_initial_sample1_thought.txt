The design implements a 4-bit unsigned multiplier with a two-stage pipeline. Inputs mul_a and mul_b are extended by size zeros at the MSB side. Partial products are generated for each bit of mul_b by shifting mul_a accordingly or zero if the bit is 0. These partial products are summed in two pipeline stages using registers: the first stage sums partial products 0 and 1, the second stage sums partial products 2 and 3. Finally, the output register sums the two intermediate sums to produce the final product. All registers are synchronous with clk and reset asynchronously with active-low rst_n.
