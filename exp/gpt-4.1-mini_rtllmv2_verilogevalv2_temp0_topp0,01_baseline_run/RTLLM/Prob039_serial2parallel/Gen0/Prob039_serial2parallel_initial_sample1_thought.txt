Use a 4-bit counter to count the number of valid serial inputs received. On each rising clock edge, if din_valid is high, shift the incoming din_serial bit into an 8-bit register from MSB to LSB. When the counter reaches 8, output the 8-bit register to dout_parallel and set dout_valid high for one clock cycle. Reset the counter and dout_valid on reset or after outputting the parallel data.
