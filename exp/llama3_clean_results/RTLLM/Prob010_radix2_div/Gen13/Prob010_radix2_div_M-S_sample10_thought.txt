To simplify the radix-2 divider module, the main goal is to reduce complexity while maintaining its functionality. The current implementation involves multiple states and a significant amount of logic to handle signed and unsigned division. 

A simplified approach would involve directly managing the division process without explicitly defining states. This can be achieved by focusing on the essential steps of the division algorithm: initializing the shift register, performing the subtraction, and updating the shift register based on the carry-out. 

Additionally, simplifying the handling of signed and unsigned operations by using absolute values for the dividend and divisor during the division process, and then adjusting the quotient and remainder based on the signs after the division is complete, can reduce the complexity of the code.

The use of a counter to manage the division steps can be retained, but its handling can be simplified by removing the explicit state machine and directly controlling the division process based on the counter value and the operation's validity.
