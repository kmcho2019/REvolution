To develop a novel architectural solution for the radix-2 divider module, we will implement a pipelined approach. This design will break down the division process into stages, each handling a specific step of the division algorithm. By doing so, we can improve the throughput and latency of the division operation. 

The new architecture will consist of the following stages:

1. **Input Stage**: This stage will handle the initialization of the division process, including the storage of the dividend and divisor, and the determination of the operation type (signed or unsigned).

2. **Normalization Stage**: Here, the divisor and dividend will be normalized to ensure that the divisor's most significant bit is in the most significant position. This will simplify the subsequent division steps.

3. **Division Stage**: This stage will perform the actual division using a radix-2 algorithm, where the divisor is subtracted from the dividend in each step, and the result determines the next bit of the quotient.

4. **Result Stage**: The final stage will prepare the result by combining the quotient and remainder, and handling the sign of the result based on the operation type.

By pipelining these stages, we can process multiple divisions concurrently, improving the overall performance of the radix-2 divider module.
