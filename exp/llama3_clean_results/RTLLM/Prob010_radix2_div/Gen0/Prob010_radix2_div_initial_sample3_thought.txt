To design the radix2_div module, we will follow the specified steps:
1. Implement a 16-bit shift register (SR) to store the dividend and quotient.
2. Include a subtraction unit to compute the difference between the dividend and divisor.
3. Use a multiplexer to select the correct result based on the carry-out.
4. Implement a counter (cnt) to track the division process.
5. Manage the res_valid signal based on the reset, counter, and result consumption.
6. Handle signed and unsigned operations based on the sign input.

We will also use the assign statement for continuous assignments and always blocks for combinational and sequential logic.
