To implement a simplified radix-2 divider, we will use a sequential approach with a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the division process. The FSM will handle the initialization, division steps, and result validity based on the inputs and internal states. The main components of the design will include registers to store the dividend, divisor, and temporary results, a counter to track the division steps, and logic to perform the subtraction and update the shift register. We will also use a multiplexer to select the appropriate result based on the carry-out. The design will be synchronized with the clock signal and reset when necessary.
