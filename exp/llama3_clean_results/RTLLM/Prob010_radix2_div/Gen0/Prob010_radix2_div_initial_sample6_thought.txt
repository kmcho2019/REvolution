To implement the simplified radix-2 divider, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the division process. The FSM has two main states: idle and divide. In the idle state, the module waits for a valid operation request (opn_valid high and res_valid low). Once a valid request is received, the module transitions to the divide state, where it performs the division process. The division process involves iterating 8 times, shifting the shift register SR left, subtracting the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder based on the carry-out. After the division is complete, the module updates the result and sets res_valid high to indicate that the result is valid.
