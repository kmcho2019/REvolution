3.  **Shifting:** If the result of the subtraction is negative, the dividend will be shifted to the right by one bit. Otherwise, the dividend will be shifted to the left by one bit.
4.  **Repeat:** Steps 2 and 3 will be repeated until the dividend is less than the divisor.
5.  **Result:** The final result will be stored in SR1, with the quotient in the lower 8 bits and the remainder in the upper 8 bits.
