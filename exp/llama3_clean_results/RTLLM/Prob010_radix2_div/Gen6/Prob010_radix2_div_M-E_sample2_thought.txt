To implement a simplified radix-2 divider on 8-bit signed or unsigned integers, a novel approach can be based on a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the division process. The FSM will handle the initialization, division steps, and result preparation in a systematic and efficient manner. The main components of this approach include:

1. **Input Stage**: Where the dividend and divisor are captured and prepared for division. This includes determining the sign of the operation and normalizing the divisor.
2. **Division Stage**: Implemented using a FSM that iteratively performs subtraction and shifts to achieve the division. This stage will handle both signed and unsigned division by appropriately adjusting the subtraction and result formation.
3. **Result Stage**: Where the final quotient and remainder are prepared. The quotient will be in the lower 8 bits, and the remainder in the upper 8 bits of the 16-bit result.
4. **Control Logic**: This includes managing the FSM states, controlling the counter for the division steps, and ensuring the result validity signal is correctly set.

By structuring the divider in this manner, the design aims to be efficient, scalable, and easy to understand and test. It also ensures that both signed and unsigned division operations are correctly handled, making it a versatile component for various digital systems.
