The new approach is to design a radix-2 divider using a hierarchical structure, consisting of a controller, a data path, and a result formatter. The controller is responsible for managing the operation, including handling reset, start, and completion signals. The data path performs the actual division, utilizing a shift register to iteratively subtract the divisor from the dividend. The result formatter combines the quotient and remainder to produce the final 16-bit result.

The key novelties in this design are:
1. **Modularization**: Breaking down the design into separate modules for the controller, data path, and result formatter enhances maintainability and scalability.
2. ** Pipelining**: Implementing a pipelined architecture for the division process can improve throughput and reduce latency.
3. **Flexible Data Path**: The data path is designed to support both signed and unsigned division by incorporating a sign detector and an absolute value calculator.

The new design aims to provide a more efficient, flexible, and scalable solution for radix-2 division.
