A novel architectural idea for the barrel shifter is to use a combination of lookup tables (LUTs) and bitwise operations to achieve the desired shifting. The LUTs will store the precomputed shifted values for each possible input and control signal combination, reducing the number of multiplexers and logic gates required. This approach can lead to improved performance, power efficiency, and area reduction. The design will consist of three main stages: (1) a LUT stage that generates the precomputed shifted values, (2) a bitwise operation stage that combines the precomputed values based on the control signal, and (3) a final output stage that selects the correct output bits.
