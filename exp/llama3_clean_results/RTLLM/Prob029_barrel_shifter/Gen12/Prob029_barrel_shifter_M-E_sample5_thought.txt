To design a novel barrel shifter architecture, we will utilize a combination of multiplexers and decoders to efficiently shift the bits. The main idea is to create a modular and scalable design that can be easily extended to support larger input sizes.

We will use a 3-stage approach to achieve the shifting:

1.  **Decoder Stage**: A decoder will be used to determine which stage of shifting is required based on the control signal.
2.  **Shifting Stage**: A series of multiplexers will be used to shift the input bits by 1, 2, or 4 positions.
3.  **Selection Stage**: The final shifted output will be selected based on the control signal.

This approach allows for a modular and efficient implementation of the barrel shifter, with a reduced number of logic levels and improved performance.

To further optimize the design, we will use synthesizable constructs and aim to minimize the fan-out, reducing power consumption and improving the overall design's performance.
