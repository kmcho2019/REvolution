To simplify the barrel shifter design, we can utilize a combination of bitwise shift operations and conditional statements to directly generate the shifted output based on the control signal. This approach eliminates the need for a 2D shift array and reduces the number of multiplexers required. We will use the control signal bits to determine the shift amount and apply the necessary shifts to the input.

Here's a simplified design:
1. Check the control signal bits to determine the shift amount.
2. Apply the corresponding shift operations to the input.
3. Combine the results to generate the final output.

By using bitwise shift operations and conditional statements, we can create a compact and efficient barrel shifter design.
