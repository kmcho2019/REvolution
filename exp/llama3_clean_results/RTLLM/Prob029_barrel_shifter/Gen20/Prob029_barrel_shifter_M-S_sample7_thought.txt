To simplify the existing barrel shifter design, we will focus on reducing unnecessary complexity while maintaining its functionality. The main idea is to directly perform the shift operation based on the control signal without calculating a total shift amount. This approach eliminates the need for additional logic to compute the shift amount, thus simplifying the design.

We will use the control bits directly to determine the shift amount. Since each control bit corresponds to a shift by 1, 2, or 4 positions, we can perform the shifts sequentially based on the control bits. This sequential approach ensures that the shifts are cumulative, allowing for shifts by 1, 2, 4, or any combination thereof, up to a maximum of 7 positions.

By directly using the control bits to control the shift operations, we simplify the logic and reduce the number of operations required to perform the shift. This simplification should lead to improved PPA metrics, including reduced power consumption and area usage, without compromising the design's functionality.
