The barrel shifter will be implemented as a series of stages, each stage consisting of 8 multiplexers (`mux2X1`) that select between the input shifted by a certain number of positions and the original input, based on the control signal. The control signal `ctrl` will determine which multiplexers are selected at each stage, effectively shifting the input by the desired amount. The first stage will shift by 4 positions if `ctrl[2]` is high, the second stage will shift by 2 positions if `ctrl[1]` is high, and the third stage will shift by 1 position if `ctrl[0]` is high.
