To implement the barrel shifter, we will first design a 2-to-1 multiplexer (`mux2X1`) module that selects between two inputs based on a select signal. Then, we will use this multiplexer in the `barrel_shifter` module to perform the shifting in stages controlled by the `ctrl` signal. The `barrel_shifter` module will have multiple stages of these multiplexers to achieve shifts of 1, 2, and 4 positions as per the control signal.
