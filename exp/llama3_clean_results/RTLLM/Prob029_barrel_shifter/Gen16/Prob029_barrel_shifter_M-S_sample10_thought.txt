The existing barrel shifter design can be further simplified by directly using the control bits to shift the input without explicitly calculating the shift amount, leveraging the built-in shift operators in Verilog. The control bits directly control the amount of shift in a single operation, making the design more concise and efficient. This simplification reduces the need for additional logic and minimizes the complexity of the design. By removing unnecessary components and focusing on the essential functionality, the design becomes more streamlined and easier to understand.
