To simplify the barrel shifter design, we can directly utilize the built-in shift operators in Verilog, which can handle shifting more efficiently and accurately than manual concatenation of zeros. This approach eliminates the need for multiple stages of multiplexers and significantly reduces the complexity of the design. The control signal bits (`ctrl[0]`, `ctrl[1]`, and `ctrl[2]`) will determine the amount of shift in a single operation, making the code more concise and easier to understand. This simplification maintains the functionality of the original design while improving readability and potentially reducing synthesis area due to the more straightforward implementation.
