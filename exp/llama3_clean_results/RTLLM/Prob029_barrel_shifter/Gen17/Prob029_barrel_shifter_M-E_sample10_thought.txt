A novel approach to designing the barrel shifter is to use a combination of bitwise operations and lookup tables. The idea is to split the 8-bit input into smaller segments (e.g., 4-bit segments) and use lookup tables to perform the shifts within each segment. This approach can be more efficient in terms of area and power consumption compared to the traditional multiplexer-based approach, especially for larger input sizes. Additionally, using bitwise operations can simplify the design and reduce the number of multiplexers required.
