To simplify the existing barrel shifter design, we can directly use the control bits to shift the input without explicitly calculating the shift amount. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for additional logic to calculate the shift amount. We will leverage the built-in shift operators in Verilog to perform the shift in a single operation, making it more concise and efficient. The control bits will directly influence the amount of shift performed on the input, with each bit corresponding to a shift of 1, 2, or 4 positions, and their combination determining the final shift amount.
