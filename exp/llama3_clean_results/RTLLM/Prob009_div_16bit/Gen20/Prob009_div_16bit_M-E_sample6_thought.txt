To improve the PPA metrics of the divider module, a novel architectural idea is to utilize a hybrid approach combining the benefits of both sequential and combinational logic. The main concept involves breaking down the 16-bit division into smaller, manageable parts, and then processing these parts in a sequential manner while minimizing the combinational logic depth. This can be achieved by employing a simple, iterative division algorithm that leverages the properties of binary numbers. Specifically, the design will utilize a series of shift and subtract operations to iteratively divide the dividend by the divisor, using a small state machine to control the process. By doing so, the design aims to reduce the area and power consumption while maintaining a competitive performance.
