To improve the performance, power, and area of the 16-bit divider module, we can adopt a novel architectural approach. Instead of using a single always block with a for loop, we can design a pipelined divider that processes the dividend and divisor in stages. This approach can help reduce the critical path, minimize switching activity, and optimize the use of logic resources. The pipelined divider will consist of multiple stages, each responsible for processing a subset of the dividend bits. By distributing the division operation across multiple stages, we can improve the overall efficiency and scalability of the design.
