Instead of directly comparing the dividend with the divisor, we will use a look-up table (LUT) to speed up the comparison process. This LUT will store pre-computed values of the divisor shifted by different amounts, allowing us to quickly determine the number of times the divisor can fit into the dividend.

The module will consist of two main components: a control unit and a data path. The control unit will manage the division process, controlling the shifting and subtraction operations, as well as updating the quotient and remainder. The data path will perform the actual arithmetic operations, utilizing the LUT to accelerate the division.

The division process will involve the following steps:

1. Initialize the quotient and remainder to zero.
2. Shift the divisor to the left by the maximum possible amount (i.e., until it is greater than the dividend).
3. Compare the shifted divisor with the dividend using the LUT. If the shifted divisor is less than or equal to the dividend, update the quotient and remainder accordingly.
4. Repeat steps 2-3 until the divisor can no longer be shifted to the left.
5. The final quotient and remainder values will be the result of the division operation.

This approach will allow for a more efficient and scalable division operation, as it reduces the number of comparisons and subtractions required.
