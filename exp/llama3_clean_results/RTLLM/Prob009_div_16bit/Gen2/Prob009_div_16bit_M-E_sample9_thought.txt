To create a novel architectural idea for a 16-bit divider module, we can utilize a pipeline architecture. This approach will divide the division process into several stages, each handling a part of the division algorithm. By doing so, we can improve the throughput of the divider and reduce the latency. The pipeline will consist of multiple stages, each responsible for a specific operation, such as shifting the dividend, comparing the divisor, and updating the quotient and remainder. This approach allows for a more efficient and scalable design, especially when dealing with larger divisors and dividends.
