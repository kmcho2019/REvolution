To implement a 16-bit divider module with a 16-bit dividend and an 8-bit divisor in combinational logic, a novel approach can be taken by utilizing a series of subtractors and multiplexers. The main idea is to iteratively subtract the divisor from the dividend, shifting the dividend to the right after each subtraction, until the dividend is less than the divisor. This process can be efficiently implemented using a for loop in Verilog, ensuring that the division logic is synthesizable and suitable for combinational logic. The quotient and remainder are then obtained from the final state of the dividend and the number of subtractions performed.
