- The first always block will be used to assign the values of the input ports A and B to two internal registers, a_reg and b_reg, respectively.
- In the second always block, we will implement the division logic. This logic involves iteratively comparing the higher bits of the dividend with the divisor, updating the quotient and remainder accordingly, and shifting the dividend to the left to process the remaining bits.
- We will use a temporary variable, temp, to hold the remainder and quotient during the division process.
- The division process will be repeated until all 16 bits of the dividend have been processed.
- Finally, the quotient and remainder will be assigned to the output ports result and odd, respectively.
