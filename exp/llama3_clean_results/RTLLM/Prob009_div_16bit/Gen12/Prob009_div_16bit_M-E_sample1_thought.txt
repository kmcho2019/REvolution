1.  Initialize the quotient and remainder to zero.
2.  Divide the 16-bit dividend into four 4-bit segments (D3, D2, D1, D0).
3.  For each 4-bit segment, perform the following operations:
    *   Compare the 4-bit segment with the 8-bit divisor.
    *   If the 4-bit segment is greater than or equal to the divisor, subtract the divisor from the 4-bit segment and set the corresponding quotient bit to one. Otherwise, set the quotient bit to zero.
    *   Shift the result of the subtraction (or the 4-bit segment itself if no subtraction occurred) to the right by 4 bits.
    *   Update the remainder by concatenating the result of the shift operation with the next 4-bit segment of the dividend.
4.  Repeat step 3 for each of the four 4-bit segments.
5.  After all 4-bit segments have been processed, the final quotient and remainder are obtained.

This allows us to reduce the number of operations required to compute the quotient and remainder, and can lead to significant improvements in performance and area efficiency.
