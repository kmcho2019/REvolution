The provided solution can be simplified by maintaining the same functionality while reducing the complexity of the combinational logic. The existing implementation already correctly handles the reset condition and the normal operation within a single always block. To simplify, we focus on the expression that updates the `Q` register. The key idea is to directly manipulate the `Q` register based on the least significant bit (`Q[0]`) without introducing additional complexity, thus maintaining the correct functionality of the 64-bit Johnson counter while potentially improving the design's area and power consumption.
