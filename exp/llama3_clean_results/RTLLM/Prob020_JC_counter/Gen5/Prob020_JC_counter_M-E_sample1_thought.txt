A novel approach to implementing the 64-bit Johnson counter is to utilize a modular design that separates the counter's state into smaller, more manageable parts. This could involve dividing the 64-bit register into multiple segments, each with its own simple state machine that updates based on the least significant bit of its preceding segment. 
By doing so, the design can potentially reduce the complexity of the combinational logic required for updating the entire 64-bit state in a single clock cycle. 
Furthermore, this modular approach could facilitate easier debugging, modification, and extension of the counter's functionality, as each segment can be analyzed and optimized independently.
