The new approach involves designing the 64-bit Johnson counter using a combination of shift registers and a control unit. Instead of directly shifting the entire 64-bit register based on the least significant bit, we can break down the operation into smaller, more manageable parts. We can use a 64-bit shift register and a control unit that monitors the least significant bit of the shift register. Based on this bit, the control unit decides whether to shift in a 1 or a 0 at the most significant bit position of the shift register. This approach allows for a more modular design, potentially improving maintainability and scalability. Additionally, by separating the control logic from the data path, we might achieve better performance and area utilization.
