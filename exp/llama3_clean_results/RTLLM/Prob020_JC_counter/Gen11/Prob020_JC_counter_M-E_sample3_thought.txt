To implement a novel 64-bit Johnson counter, we can utilize a combination of shift registers and a control logic block. The control logic block can be designed to analyze the least significant bit of the current count value and decide whether to append a 1 or a 0 at the most significant bit position during the next clock cycle. This approach involves breaking down the counter into smaller, manageable components and using a state machine-like control logic to dictate the operation of the counter. By doing so, we can potentially simplify the overall design, improve readability, and reduce the complexity of the combinational logic.
