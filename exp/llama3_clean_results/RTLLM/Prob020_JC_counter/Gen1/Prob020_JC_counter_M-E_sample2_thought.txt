To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter with a novel architectural approach, we can utilize a circular shift register combined with a control logic that decides whether to append a 1 or a 0 based on the least significant bit. This approach simplifies the counter update logic and potentially improves performance by minimizing the number of operations within the always block. We will name this module 'JC_counter_novel'. The key components include a 64-bit shift register, a control unit to determine the next bit to append, and a reset logic to initialize the counter when the reset signal is low. By structuring the design in this manner, we aim to enhance the overall efficiency of the Johnson counter in terms of Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
