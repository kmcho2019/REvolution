To implement a 64-bit Johnson counter with a novel architectural idea, we will utilize a combination of logic gates and shift registers to create a more efficient and scalable design. The counter will be divided into two main parts: a control unit and a data unit. The control unit will be responsible for determining the direction of the counter (increment or decrement) based on the least significant bit, while the data unit will perform the actual shifting and appending of bits. This separation of concerns will allow for a more modular and maintainable design. Additionally, we will use a Gray code-based approach to minimize the number of transitions between states, reducing power consumption.
