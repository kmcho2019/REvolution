To implement a novel solution, consider using a FIFO (First-In-First-Out) buffer to store the incoming 8-bit data. The FIFO can be designed to hold two elements, corresponding to the two 8-bit data inputs needed to form a 16-bit output. Once the FIFO is full (i.e., two valid 8-bit data are stored), the circuit can concatenate the two stored data elements to form the 16-bit output and assert the valid_out signal. This approach allows for a simpler and more modular design compared to a finite state machine (FSM) implementation. It also inherently handles the buffering of data, making the design more robust and easier to understand.
