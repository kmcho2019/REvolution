To address the 8-bit to 16-bit data width conversion problem, I propose a novel architecture that utilizes a flag-based approach with two registers to temporarily store the input data. The first valid data is stored in the first register, and the second valid data is stored in the second register. Once both registers have valid data, the contents of the two registers are concatenated to form the 16-bit output, and the valid output signal is generated. This approach ensures that the output is generated only after two consecutive valid input data are received, meeting the problem's requirements. The key innovation here is the use of a flag to track the state of the data conversion process, allowing for efficient and synchronous operation.
