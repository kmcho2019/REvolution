To simplify the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer further, we can leverage the fact that data_in remains constant during the period when data_en is high and the data change frequency of data_in is very low. We can achieve the same functionality with fewer registers by using a single register for data synchronization and simplifying the control logic. The main idea is to capture the data_in when data_en is high and then synchronize it to the clk_b domain. Since the data change interval is at least 10 clk_b clock cycles, we can minimize the control logic to just ensure that data_out is updated when data_en is active and then held until the next update.
