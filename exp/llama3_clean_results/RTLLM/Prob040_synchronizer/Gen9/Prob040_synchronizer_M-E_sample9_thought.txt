To create a novel solution, we will design a multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer that leverages the concept of clock domain crossing (CDC) to synchronize the input data signal with the output clock signal. Our approach will utilize a combination of handshake signals and data registers to ensure reliable data transfer between the two clock domains.

The new solution will feature a master-slave architecture, where the master side is responsible for capturing the input data signal and generating a handshake signal to indicate the availability of new data. The slave side will then use this handshake signal to latch the data and generate an output signal that is synchronized with the output clock.

The key novelty of our approach lies in the use of a CDC protocol that ensures data integrity and prevents metastability issues. We will implement a 2-stage synchronizer to delay the handshake signal, allowing the slave side to safely latch the data without worrying about setup and hold time violations.

By using a combination of handshake signals and data registers, our solution will provide a robust and reliable way to synchronize the input data signal with the output clock signal, while minimizing the risk of metastability and ensuring optimal performance.
