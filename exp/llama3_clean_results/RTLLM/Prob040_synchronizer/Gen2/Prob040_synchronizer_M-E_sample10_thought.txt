The new approach involves designing a synchronizer that leverages a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the data transfer between two clock domains. This FSM will monitor the enable signal `data_en` and the clock edges to ensure that data is captured and transferred correctly. The novelty of this design lies in its ability to dynamically adjust to variations in the input data rate and the clock frequencies, providing a more robust synchronization mechanism. The FSM will have states for idle, capture, and transfer, allowing it to handle the data synchronization process in a structured and efficient manner.
