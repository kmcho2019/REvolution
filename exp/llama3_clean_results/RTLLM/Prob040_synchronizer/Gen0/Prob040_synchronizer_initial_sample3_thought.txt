To implement the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we will first create two registers to store the input data signal and the input enable signal. We will then create two control registers to control the selection of the input data. Finally, we will assign the output data value based on the control signal. The key is to ensure that the data change frequency of data_in is very low and the change interval between two adjacent data is at least 10 clk_b clock cycles, and data_in remains constant during the period when data_en is high, and data_en is high for at least 3 clk_b clock cycles.
