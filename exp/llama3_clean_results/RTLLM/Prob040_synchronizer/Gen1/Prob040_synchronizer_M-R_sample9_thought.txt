To refactor the provided Verilog code for the multi-bit MUX-based synchronizer, we can leverage the use of `assign` statements for combinational logic and maintain the sequential logic within `always` blocks. This approach can enhance the readability and potentially the synthesis efficiency of the design. The core functionality of the synchronizer, including the data register, enable data register, and enable control registers, will remain intact. However, we will explore an alternative implementation method that utilizes a more concise and possibly more efficient structure for the combinational parts of the design.
