2. **Overflow Detection Mechanism:** Implement an overflow detection mechanism that not only checks for overflow conditions but also provides a means to handle or mitigate overflows in a fixed-point context, ensuring the result stays within the specified precision.

3. **Sign Handling Unit:** Design a dedicated sign handling unit that determines the sign of the result based on the inputs and their signs, simplifying the logic and reducing potential errors.

4. **Precision Maintenance:** Ensure that all arithmetic operations maintain the specified precision (`Q` fractional bits) by using bit-manipulation techniques and aligning the fractional parts correctly during addition and subtraction.

5. **Synthesizability Enhancements:** Use synthesizable constructs and avoid complex conditional statements that could hinder the synthesis process. Instead, opt for straightforward, combinational logic where possible.
