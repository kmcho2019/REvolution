To create a novel architectural solution for the parameterized fixed-point adder, we will leverage the concept of modular arithmetic and two's complement representation. The idea is to first separate the integer and fractional parts of the input operands based on the parameter Q. Then, perform the addition or subtraction on the integer parts while considering the signs and overflow conditions. For the fractional parts, a similar approach will be applied, but with a focus on maintaining the precision defined by Q. The results of the integer and fractional parts will then be combined, taking into account any carry from the fractional part to the integer part. This approach ensures accurate fixed-point arithmetic while handling potential overflows.
