The novel architectural idea for the fixed-point adder module involves utilizing a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of arithmetic logic units (ALUs) with the efficiency of shift-and-add algorithms. This approach will be referred to as the "Precision-Preserving Hybrid Adder" (PPHA).

The PPHA architecture will consist of the following key components:
1. **Sign Extraction Unit (SEU):** This module extracts the sign bits from the input operands `a` and `b` and generates control signals based on the sign combinations.
2. **Absolute Value Generator (AVG):** This unit computes the absolute values of `a` and `b` by inverting the bits and adding 1 for negative numbers.
3. **Shift-and-Add Unit (SAU):** This module performs the fixed-point addition or subtraction based on the control signals from the SEU and the absolute values from the AVG. The SAU utilizes a shift-and-add algorithm to handle the fractional parts of the operands.
4. **Overflow Detection and Correction Unit (ODCU):** This module checks for overflow conditions and applies corrections to ensure the result is within the valid range.

The PPHA architecture will provide improved performance, reduced area, and enhanced precision preservation for fixed-point arithmetic operations.
