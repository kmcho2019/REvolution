The new architecture, named `hybrid_fixed_point_adder`, utilizes a hybrid digital-analog circuit to perform the addition and subtraction operations.

The key components of this design include:
1. **Analog Front-End (AFE):** The AFE is responsible for converting the digital input signals into analog signals. This is done to leverage the advantages of analog circuits, such as reduced power consumption and increased speed.
2. **Analog Addition/Subtraction Circuit:** This circuit performs the addition and subtraction operations in the analog domain. The circuit uses a combination of op-amps and resistors to implement the necessary arithmetic operations.
3. **Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC):** The ADC converts the analog output from the addition/subtraction circuit back into a digital signal.
4. **Digital Signal Processing (DSP) Block:** The DSP block is used to perform any necessary digital signal processing operations, such as overflow handling and precision management.

By utilizing a hybrid digital-analog approach, the `hybrid_fixed_point_adder` module can achieve improved performance, power efficiency, and area utilization compared to traditional digital-only designs.

The design also incorporates a novel overflow handling mechanism, which uses a combination of digital and analog techniques to detect and correct overflow conditions.
