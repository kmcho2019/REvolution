To implement a parameterized fixed-point adder module in Verilog, we first consider the structure of the inputs and the required operations. The inputs are fixed-point numbers represented as N-bit binary numbers, with Q bits dedicated to the fractional part. The operations involve adding or subtracting these numbers based on their sign bits and then handling overflows.

The approach involves:
1. Determining the signs of the input operands.
2. Performing addition or subtraction based on the signs.
3. Handling overflow by observing the most significant bit (MSB) of the result.
4. Maintaining the precision by ensuring the result has the same number of fractional bits (Q) as the inputs.

We use a combination of logical operations to determine the signs, arithmetic operations for addition/subtraction, and conditional statements to manage overflow and precision.
