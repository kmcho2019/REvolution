The novel architectural idea for the simplified fixed-point adder involves reducing the complexity of the existing solution while maintaining its functionality. This is achieved by directly handling the sign bits and simplifying the overflow and underflow detection. 

The simplified idea includes:
1. Direct Sign Handling: Instead of using separate logic for sign detection, the signs are directly incorporated into the arithmetic operations.
2. Simplified Arithmetic: The absolute value addition and subtraction are simplified by using standard arithmetic operations, and the result's sign is directly determined based on the input signs.
3. Reduced Conditional Logic: The final result construction is simplified by reducing the number of conditional operations, thus decreasing the overall complexity of the design.

This simplified architecture reduces the complexity of the original design while maintaining its functionality, potentially leading to improved synthesizability and area efficiency.
