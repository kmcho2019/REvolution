To address the feedback and improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics of the fixed-point adder, we will apply several optimizations:
1. **Simplify Conditional Operations**: Reduce the complexity of conditional operations in the normalization and arithmetic logic stages by using more efficient constructs, such as bitwise operators and pre-computed values.
2. **Pipelining**: Introduce pipelining to break down the critical paths and reduce the maximum operating frequency limitations. Specifically, we will pipeline the normalization, sign and magnitude separation, and arithmetic logic stages.
3. **Clock Gating and Operand Isolation**: Implement clock gating and operand isolation techniques to minimize power consumption during idle periods or when specific stages are not required.
4. **Resource Sharing and Reuse**: Merge or share similar operations in the normalization and sign separation stages to reduce area utilization.
5. **Synthesis-Friendly Constructs**: Use more synthesis-friendly constructs, such as lookup tables or simple arithmetic logic, to improve synthesizability.

Additionally, we will focus on optimizing the arithmetic logic stage by using a more efficient adder architecture, such as a carry-save adder or a ripple-carry adder with optimized carry chain.

By applying these optimizations, we aim to improve the PPA metrics of the fixed-point adder, making it more suitable for a wide range of applications requiring fixed-point arithmetic.
