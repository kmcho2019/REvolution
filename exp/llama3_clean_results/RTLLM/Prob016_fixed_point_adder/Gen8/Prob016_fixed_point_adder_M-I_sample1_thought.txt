The goal is to improve the existing fixed-point adder design by addressing the identified issues and optimizing it for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. 

The improvement strategy involves:
1. **Precision Handling:** Modify the design to explicitly handle the precision (Q) of the fixed-point numbers by separating the integer and fractional parts of the input operands `a` and `b`.
2. **Overflow Handling:** Implement a proper overflow handling mechanism to ensure the result fits within the N-bit range.
3. **Absolute Value Comparison:** Correctly determine the larger absolute value when the MSBs of `a` and `b` are different by comparing the absolute values of `a` and `b`.
4. **Logic Simplification:** Simplify the logic for determining the result based on the signs of `a` and `b` to reduce the complexity of the design.
5. **Arithmetic Optimization:** Optimize the arithmetic operations to reduce the number of operations and minimize switching activity.
6. **Adder Structure:** Explore the use of more efficient adder structures, such as a carry-save adder or a redundant binary adder, to reduce the area consumption of the design.

By addressing these areas, the improved design should provide accurate fixed-point arithmetic, efficient overflow handling, and optimized PPA metrics.
