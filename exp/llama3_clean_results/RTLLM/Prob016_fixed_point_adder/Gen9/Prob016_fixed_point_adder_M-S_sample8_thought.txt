To simplify the existing fixed-point adder design, we focus on reducing the complexity of the logic for calculating the result based on the signs of `a` and `b`. We can eliminate the explicit calculation of absolute values for `a` and `b` and instead directly compare their values considering their signs. This approach simplifies the arithmetic operations and potentially reduces the switching activity and the number of logic gates required, thus improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.

We will maintain the same parameterization and port structure, ensuring that the design remains flexible and easy to integrate into larger systems.
