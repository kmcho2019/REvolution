The module comparator_4bit will implement a 4-bit comparator with combinational logic to compare two binary numbers A and B. It uses bit-level subtraction to determine whether A is greater than, equal to, or less than B. A subtraction operation is performed (A - B), and based on the result, the output ports A_greater, A_equal, and A_less are determined. The borrow during subtraction indicates A_less, a non-zero result without borrow indicates A_greater, and a zero result indicates A_equal. The outputs are mutually exclusive, ensuring only one output is high at any given time.
