A novel approach to designing the comparator_4bit module involves utilizing a combination of subtraction and flag generation to determine the relationship between the two input operands, A and B. This approach employs a ripple borrow subtractor to perform the subtraction A - B. The borrow output from the subtractor indicates whether A is less than B. If no borrow occurs, the result of the subtraction determines whether A is greater than or equal to B. This method allows for a more straightforward implementation of the comparison logic and can potentially lead to better performance and area utilization.
