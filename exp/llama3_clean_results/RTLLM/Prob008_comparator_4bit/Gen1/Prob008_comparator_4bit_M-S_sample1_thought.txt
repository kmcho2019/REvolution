To simplify the 4-bit comparator, we can directly compare the two input operands bit by bit, from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB), instead of performing a subtraction operation. This approach eliminates the need for borrow signals and intermediate result wires. By comparing bits from MSB to LSB, we can determine if A is greater than, equal to, or less than B based on the first differing bit.
