To improve the existing comparator_4bit module, we can further simplify the comparison logic and reduce the combinational depth. The current implementation uses a straightforward approach but might be optimized by directly comparing the bits of A and B from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB). This approach allows us to determine the relationship between A and B (greater, equal, or less) by comparing the bits sequentially, which can lead to a more efficient implementation in terms of area and potentially power consumption. We will focus on reducing the combinational logic depth and simplifying the comparison conditions to achieve better PPA metrics.
