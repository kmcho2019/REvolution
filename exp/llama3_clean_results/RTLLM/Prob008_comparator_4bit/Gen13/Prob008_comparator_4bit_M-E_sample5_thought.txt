The new idea involves creating a 4-bit comparator module using a combination of bitwise XOR operations and priority encoders. The XOR operation will be used to identify the bits where A and B differ, and the priority encoder will determine the most significant bit where the difference occurs. Based on the output of the priority encoder and the values of A and B at the differing bits, we can determine whether A is greater than, less than, or equal to B. This approach avoids the need for explicit subtraction or comparison operations, potentially leading to a more efficient implementation in terms of area and power consumption.
