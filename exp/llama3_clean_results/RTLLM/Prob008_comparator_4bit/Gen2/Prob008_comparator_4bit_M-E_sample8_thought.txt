To implement a novel 4-bit comparator module, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of digital arithmetic and logical operations. This approach involves using a combination of half-adders and half-subtractors to perform the comparison between the two input operands, A and B. Each bit position will have a corresponding half-adder or half-subtractor that will generate a borrow signal if A is less than B at that position. The final comparison result will be determined by the cumulative effect of these borrow signals and the result of the most significant bit (MSB) comparison. This method allows for a modular and scalable design that can be easily extended to larger bit widths.
