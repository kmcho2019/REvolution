The novel architectural idea involves using a combination of bitwise XOR and subtraction operations to determine the comparison result. This approach takes advantage of the properties of bitwise XOR to identify equality and uses subtraction to determine the greater or lesser condition. By performing these operations in a hierarchical manner, the design can efficiently compare the two 4-bit numbers and produce the required output signals. Specifically, the design will first perform a bitwise XOR operation between the two input numbers to check for equality. If the result is zero, it means the numbers are equal. Otherwise, the design will perform a subtraction operation to determine whether the first number is greater than or less than the second number.
