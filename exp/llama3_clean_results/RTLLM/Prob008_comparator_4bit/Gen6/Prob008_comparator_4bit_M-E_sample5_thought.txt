To implement a novel 4-bit comparator architecture, we can utilize a bit-level comparator array combined with a priority encoder. The bit-level comparators will compare each bit of the input operands A and B, generating signals for "A greater than B" and "A less than B" at each bit position. The priority encoder will then evaluate these signals, taking into account the most significant bit position where a difference is detected, to determine the overall relationship between A and B. This approach allows for efficient and scalable comparison logic that can be easily extended to larger bit widths.
