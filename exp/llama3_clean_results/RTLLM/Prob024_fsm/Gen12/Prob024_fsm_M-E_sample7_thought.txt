*   A 5-bit shift register (SR) to store the last 5 input bits.
*   A comparator to compare the contents of the shift register with the target sequence "10011".
*   A control unit to manage the shift operation and the comparison.

This approach allows for a simple and efficient implementation of the Mealy FSM detection circuit, with a reduced number of states and transitions compared to traditional state machine-based designs.
