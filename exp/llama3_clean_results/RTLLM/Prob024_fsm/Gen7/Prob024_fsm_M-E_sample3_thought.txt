To make the design more efficient, we can also use a reset signal to initialize the shift register when the system is reset. This way, we can ensure that the detection circuit is properly reset and ready for the next input sequence.

The advantages of this approach include:

*   Reduced complexity: The design uses a simple 5-stage shift register and a combinational logic block, making it easier to understand and implement.
*   Improved performance: The detection circuit can operate at high speeds, as the shift register and combinational logic block can be pipelined to minimize latency.
