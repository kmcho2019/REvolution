Then, we compare the contents of the shift register with the target sequence "10011". If they match, we set the output signal MATCH to 1; otherwise, it remains 0.

This approach simplifies the state machine and reduces the number of states required, making it more efficient in terms of area and power consumption. Additionally, it allows for easy modification to detect different sequences by simply changing the target sequence.
