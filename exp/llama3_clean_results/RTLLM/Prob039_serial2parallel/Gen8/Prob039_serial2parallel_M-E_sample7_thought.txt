A novel approach to the series-parallel conversion circuit is to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the conversion process. The FSM will have states for idle, receiving data, and outputting data. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design. The FSM will control a shift register to store the received serial data. Once the shift register is full, the FSM will output the parallel data and reset the shift register. This design decouples the counter and shift register operations, potentially improving timing performance and reducing area consumption.
