To simplify the serial2parallel module, we will directly implement a counter to keep track of the received bits and utilize it to shift the input bits into their correct positions in the output register. This approach eliminates the need for explicit state machine states, as the counter itself dictates the operation of the module. We will also minimize the use of intermediate signals and directly assign values to the output where possible. By doing so, we aim to reduce the overall complexity, potentially leading to better performance, power efficiency, and area utilization.
