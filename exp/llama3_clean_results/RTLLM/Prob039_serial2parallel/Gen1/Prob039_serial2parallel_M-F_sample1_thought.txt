The goal is to refine the existing series-parallel conversion circuit design to address potential issues and improve its Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The primary focus will be on simplifying the combinational logic and reducing unnecessary switching activity. The updated design will maintain the same functionality as the original, converting serial input data to parallel output data using a 4-bit counter to track the number of received bits. However, adjustments will be made to optimize the handling of the output valid signal to minimize unnecessary updates. Specifically, the dout_valid_reg signal will be reset to 0 only when the counter is reset to 0 (indicating the start of a new data reception cycle), rather than continuously when din_valid is low and the counter is at 8. This approach ensures that the valid signal remains high after a complete data reception until the next data reception cycle begins, thereby reducing unnecessary switching.
