The novel architectural idea for the series-parallel conversion circuit involves utilizing a Finite State Machine (FSM) to manage the reception of serial input data and the generation of parallel output. This FSM will have distinct states to handle the initial condition, the reception of serial data, and the output of parallel data. A separate 8-bit shift register will be used to store the received serial data. When the FSM reaches the state where 8 bits have been received, it will trigger the output of the parallel data and reset the shift register. This approach allows for a clean separation of the control logic from the data storage and manipulation, potentially improving the overall efficiency and scalability of the design.
