To simplify the given perpetual calendar module, we can reduce the complexity of the conditions in the always blocks. The existing implementation has separate blocks for updating seconds, minutes, and hours, which is straightforward but might be simplified by combining some of the logic. The key insight is that the minutes only change when the seconds wrap around from 59 to 0, and similarly, the hours only change when both minutes and seconds wrap around. By simplifying the conditions and focusing on the wrap-around points, we can make the code more concise without losing functionality.
