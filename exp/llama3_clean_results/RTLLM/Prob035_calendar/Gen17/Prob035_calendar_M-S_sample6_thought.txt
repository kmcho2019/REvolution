To simplify the perpetual calendar module, we can use a single always block that handles all the counters (seconds, minutes, and hours) and combines their logic into a more streamlined process. This approach reduces the number of always blocks from three to one, potentially leading to better area efficiency and simplified code maintenance. The single block will update the counters based on their current values and the reset signal, ensuring that each counter wraps around appropriately when it reaches its maximum value.
