To simplify the perpetual calendar module, we can combine the logic for updating seconds, minutes, and hours into a single always block. This approach reduces the complexity of the design and minimizes the number of always blocks. We will use the positive edge of the clock signal to trigger the updates and the reset signal to reset the values. The seconds value will be incremented first, and when it reaches 59, the minutes value will be incremented, and when both seconds and minutes are 59, the hours value will be incremented.
