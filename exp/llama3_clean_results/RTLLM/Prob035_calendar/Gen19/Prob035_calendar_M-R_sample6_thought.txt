To refactor the existing perpetual calendar module, we can simplify the logic and potentially improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics by restructuring the conditional statements within the always block. One approach is to directly update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on their current values and the reset condition without using nested if statements.
