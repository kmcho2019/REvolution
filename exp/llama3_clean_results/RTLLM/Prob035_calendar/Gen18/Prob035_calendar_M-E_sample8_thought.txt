A novel architectural idea for implementing the perpetual calendar is to utilize a single counter that increments every clock cycle and then use combinational logic to decode this counter value into seconds, minutes, and hours. This approach can simplify the design and potentially reduce area and power consumption. The counter can be designed to wrap around at the appropriate value to handle the 60-second and 60-minute cycles, and additional logic can be added to handle the 24-hour cycle. This design also allows for easy reset and synchronization of the calendar. The use of a single counter reduces the need for multiple always blocks, making the design more concise and potentially more efficient.
