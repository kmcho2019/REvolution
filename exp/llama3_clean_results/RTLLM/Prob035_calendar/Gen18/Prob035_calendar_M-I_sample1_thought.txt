The existing solution for the perpetual calendar module is functionally correct but can be optimized for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. To improve the performance, we will simplify the logic within the always block to reduce combinational depth. For power optimization, we will minimize switching activity by ensuring that only necessary bits are updated. For area optimization, we will look into reducing the number of gates and flip-flops by simplifying the logic and potentially sharing resources. The main strategy involves restructuring the conditional statements to minimize nesting and directly update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on their current values and the reset condition.
