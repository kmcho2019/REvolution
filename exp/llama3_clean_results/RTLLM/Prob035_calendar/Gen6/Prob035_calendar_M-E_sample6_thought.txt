To develop a novel architectural idea for the perpetual calendar module, we can consider a more modular and scalable approach. Instead of using a single always block with conditional statements to update the seconds, minutes, and hours, we can design separate modules for each time unit. Each module will handle the wrapping around and incrementing of its respective value. This modular design can improve readability, maintainability, and potentially reduce the complexity of the logic, leading to better PPA metrics. Furthermore, we can utilize parameterized modules to make the design more flexible and adaptable to different requirements.
