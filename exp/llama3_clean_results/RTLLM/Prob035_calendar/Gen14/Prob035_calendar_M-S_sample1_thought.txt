To simplify the solution, we can maintain a single counter for seconds and then calculate the minutes and hours based on this counter. This approach eliminates the need for separate counters and conditional logic for minutes and hours, thus reducing the complexity of the design.

The seconds counter will be a 32-bit register that increments every clock cycle. We can then use this counter to calculate the current seconds, minutes, and hours. Since seconds, minutes, and hours are cyclic, we can use the modulo operator to wrap around to 0 when the maximum value is reached.

This approach simplifies the logic, reduces the area usage, and minimizes the switching activity, resulting in improved PPA metrics. We will use non-blocking assignments to avoid race conditions and minimize the combinational logic inside the always block.
