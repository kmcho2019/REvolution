To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we can utilize a single always block that handles the updates of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of always blocks and combining the logic into a single sequential block. We can use a single counter to keep track of the total seconds and then calculate the hours, minutes, and seconds from this counter. This method simplifies the design and reduces the area required for implementation.
