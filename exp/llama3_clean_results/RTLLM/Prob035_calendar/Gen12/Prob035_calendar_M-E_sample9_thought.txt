To create a novel solution for the perpetual calendar module, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have states representing the current time (hours, minutes, seconds) and will transition between these states based on the clock signal and reset signal. This approach can potentially reduce the complexity of the conditional logic and make the design more modular and scalable. 

Our novel strategy involves:
1. Defining an FSM with states for hours, minutes, and seconds.
2. Implementing state transition logic based on the clock signal and reset signal.
3. Using a separate state for reset to ensure proper initialization of the counters.
4. Ensuring that the FSM correctly wraps around the hours, minutes, and seconds counters.

By utilizing an FSM, we can create a design that is not only efficient in terms of performance and area but also easy to understand and maintain.
