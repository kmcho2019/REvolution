To design the calendar module, we will use three always blocks in Verilog to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. 
The first always block will handle the seconds, incrementing it by 1 on each positive clock edge unless it has reached 59, in which case it wraps around to 0. 
The second always block will handle the minutes, checking if the seconds have wrapped around (i.e., Secs is 59) and if so, incrementing the minutes by 1 unless they too have reached 59, in which case they wrap around to 0. 
The third always block will handle the hours, checking if both minutes and seconds have wrapped around (i.e., both are 59) and if so, incrementing the hours by 1 unless they have reached 23, in which case they wrap around to 0. 
All three blocks will also handle the reset signal by setting their respective time component to 0 when the reset is active.
