To design the perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we will create a module named 'calendar' with input ports for clock (CLK) and reset (RST), and output ports for hours, minutes, and seconds. We will use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal. Each always block will trigger on the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, allowing for both synchronous reset and clock-driven updates.
