To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design a digital circuit that can keep track of seconds, minutes, and hours. The circuit should have three counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds counter should increment every clock cycle and reset to 0 when it reaches 59. The minutes counter should increment when the seconds counter resets and reset to 0 when it reaches 59. The hours counter should increment when both the minutes and seconds counters reset and reset to 0 when it reaches 23. We will use three always blocks to implement the counters, each triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal.
