To implement a perpetual calendar module in Verilog, we need to design a digital circuit that can keep track of seconds, minutes, and hours. The seconds and minutes will loop continuously from 0 to 59, and when they reach 59, the next unit of time (minutes or hours) will be incremented. The hours will loop from 0 to 23. The module will have a clock input (CLK) and an active high reset signal (RST). When the reset signal is active, all time units will be reset to 0. We can use three always blocks to update the values of seconds, minutes, and hours based on the clock signal and reset signal.
