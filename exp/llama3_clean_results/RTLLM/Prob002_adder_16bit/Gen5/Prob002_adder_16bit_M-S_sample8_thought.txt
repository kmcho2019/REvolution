To simplify the 16-bit full adder implementation, we can utilize a single 1-bit full adder module and instantiate it 16 times in a ripple-carry structure. This approach eliminates the need for separate 8-bit adder modules, reducing the overall complexity of the design. By using a single 1-bit full adder module, the design becomes more modular and easier to maintain. The ripple-carry structure is straightforward to implement and understand, making it an ideal choice for this application.
