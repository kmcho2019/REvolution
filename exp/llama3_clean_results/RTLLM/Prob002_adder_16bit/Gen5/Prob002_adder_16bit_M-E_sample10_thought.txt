The novel architectural idea is to implement a 16-bit full adder using a combination of bitwise operations and a carry-select adder approach. The carry-select adder is a fast adder structure that precomputes the sum for both possible values of the carry-in signal (0 and 1) and then selects the correct sum based on the actual carry-in value. This approach can reduce the critical path delay compared to traditional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead adders. The bitwise operations will be used to generate the precomputed sums, and the carry-select logic will choose the correct sum. This hybrid approach aims to balance performance, power, and area efficiency.
