To implement a 16-bit full adder with improved performance and area efficiency, we can design a hierarchical adder architecture that combines the benefits of both ripple-carry and carry-lookahead adders. We can divide the 16-bit operands into four 4-bit segments and implement a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder for each segment. The carry-out from each 4-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the next segment. This approach reduces the critical path delay compared to a simple ripple-carry adder while minimizing the area overhead associated with a full carry-lookahead adder.
