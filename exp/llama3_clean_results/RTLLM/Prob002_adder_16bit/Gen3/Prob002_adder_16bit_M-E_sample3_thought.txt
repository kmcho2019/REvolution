To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic with a focus on optimizing Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, we can adopt a hierarchical approach by designing an 8-bit full adder module and then instantiating two of these modules to create the 16-bit full adder. This approach allows for a more balanced tree structure for carry propagation, potentially reducing the critical path delay and improving area efficiency compared to a flat, 16-stage ripple-carry adder. Furthermore, leveraging the properties of binary addition, we can explore using a carry-lookahead mechanism within each 8-bit adder to further reduce the carry propagation delay.
