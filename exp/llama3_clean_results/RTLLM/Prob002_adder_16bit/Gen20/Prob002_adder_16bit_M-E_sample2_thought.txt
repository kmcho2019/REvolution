We will also incorporate a carry-save adder (CSA) concept, which is beneficial for reducing the number of carry propagations and thus improving performance. The CSA will add three input bits (two from the operands and one carry bit) to produce two output bits (sum and carry), which are then used in subsequent stages.

Our design will be divided into stages: 
1. A first stage that breaks down the 16-bit inputs into smaller segments and applies the initial addition and carry generation.
2. Intermediate stages that propagate and combine the carry and sum bits using the CSA principle.
3. A final stage that resolves any remaining carry propagations and produces the final 16-bit sum.

By structuring the adder in this hierarchical, tree-like fashion and utilizing the CSA principle, we aim to achieve a better balance between area, power, and performance compared to traditional linear adder structures.
