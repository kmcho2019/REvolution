To simplify the 16-bit full adder design, we can directly instantiate 16 full adder modules and handle the carry propagation between them without creating an intermediate 8-bit adder module. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for an additional module and the associated instantiations. The carry output from each full adder will be used as the carry input for the next full adder, ensuring that the carry propagates through the entire 16-bit range.
