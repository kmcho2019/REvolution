To implement the 16-bit full adder, we will first design a smaller 8-bit full adder module (adder_8bit) using the same principle of a full adder, where each bit is added with a carry-in from the previous bit addition. The 16-bit adder (adder_16bit) will then instantiate two of these 8-bit adders, one for the lower 8 bits and one for the upper 8 bits, with the carry-out from the first 8-bit adder serving as the carry-in for the second 8-bit adder. This approach simplifies the design and reduces complexity by breaking it down into smaller, manageable components.
