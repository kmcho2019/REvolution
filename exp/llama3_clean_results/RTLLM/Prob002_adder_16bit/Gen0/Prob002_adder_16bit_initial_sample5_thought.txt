To implement a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we will first create a module for an 8-bit full adder. This smaller adder will be used as a building block for the larger 16-bit adder. We will instantiate two 8-bit adders in the 16-bit adder module: one for the least significant 8 bits and one for the most significant 8 bits. The carry-out from the least significant 8-bit adder will be used as the carry-in for the most significant 8-bit adder. This way, we can chain the adders to handle the carry propagation across the entire 16-bit width.
