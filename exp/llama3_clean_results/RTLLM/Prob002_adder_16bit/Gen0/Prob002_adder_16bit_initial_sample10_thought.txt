To design a 16-bit full adder in combinational logic, we first need to create a smaller bit-width adder, such as an 8-bit adder, which will be instantiated multiple times. The 8-bit adder itself can be constructed from full adder (1-bit adder) modules. Each full adder will handle the addition of two bits (from the two input operands) and a carry input, producing a sum bit and a carry output. For the 16-bit adder, we will instantiate two 8-bit adders and handle the carry propagation between them to cover the entire 16-bit range.
