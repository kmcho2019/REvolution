The new solution leverages a hierarchical, bit-sliced design approach for the 16-bit full adder. Instead of using two 8-bit adders, it utilizes a combination of a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) for the lower 8 bits and a ripple-carry adder (RCA) for the upper 8 bits. The CLA is chosen for the lower bits to minimize the critical path delay, while the RCA is used for the upper bits to reduce the overall area. To further optimize the design, a novel "carry-precomputation" technique is employed, where the carry signals for each bit slice are precomputed using a separate set of logic, allowing for a reduction in the overall switching activity and power consumption. This approach enables a better balance between performance, power, and area (PPA) metrics.
