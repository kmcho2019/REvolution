To implement a 16-bit full adder, we can leverage a novel architectural approach by utilizing a combination of look-ahead carry generation and bit-slicing. This design, called "adder_16bit", will split the 16-bit operands into four 4-bit slices and use a look-ahead carry generator for each slice. This approach aims to reduce the critical path delay associated with traditional ripple-carry adders while maintaining a moderate area usage. The look-ahead carry generator will compute the carry-out for each 4-bit slice in parallel, allowing for faster propagation of carries through the adder.
