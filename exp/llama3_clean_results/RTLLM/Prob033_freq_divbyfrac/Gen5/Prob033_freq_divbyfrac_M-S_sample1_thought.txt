To simplify the frequency divider design, we can utilize a single counter to manage the uneven clock periods of 4 and 3 clock cycles. The counter will cycle through 7 clock cycles, generating `clk_div_4` for 4 cycles and `clk_div_3` for 3 cycles. We will then introduce a half-cycle delayed version of `clk_div_4` (`clk_div_4_dly`) and a half-cycle advanced version of `clk_div_3` (`clk_div_3_adv`). These phase-shifted signals will be logically OR-ed to produce the final divided clock output (`clk_div`).

By simplifying the design, we eliminate the need for a state machine with multiple states and reduce the complexity of the code, improving its readability while maintaining the desired functionality of the frequency divider.
