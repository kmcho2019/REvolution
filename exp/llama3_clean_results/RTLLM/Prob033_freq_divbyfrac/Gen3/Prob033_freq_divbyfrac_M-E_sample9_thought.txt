To address the challenge of fractional frequency division, we will implement a novel architecture that utilizes a combination of digital signal processing and phase-locked loop (PLL) principles. The key components of this design include:

1. **Fractional Frequency Divider (FFD)**: A digital circuit that generates a fractional frequency division ratio by counting clock cycles and adjusting the phase of the output clock signal.
2. **Phase-Shifting Oscillator (PSO)**: A module that generates a clock signal with a variable phase shift, allowing for fine-tuned control over the output clock frequency.
3. **Clock Signal Combiner (CSC)**: A circuit that combines the output clock signals from the FFD and PSO to produce the final fractional divided clock output.

The FFD will be implemented using a digital counter that cycles through a predetermined number of clock cycles, generating a control signal that adjusts the phase of the output clock signal. The PSO will utilize a digitally controlled oscillator (DCO) to generate a clock signal with a variable frequency, which will be phase-shifted to achieve the desired fractional frequency division ratio. The CSC will use a logical OR operation to combine the output clock signals from the FFD and PSO, producing the final fractional divided clock output.

This novel architecture will enable accurate and efficient fractional frequency division, with improved performance and reduced area consumption compared to traditional designs.
