To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by fractional values, I propose a novel architectural approach using a hybrid of digital and phase-locked loop (PLL) techniques. The primary goal is to generate a smooth fractional clock output with minimal jitter and high frequency accuracy.

The new architecture, called "Hybrid Fractional Frequency Divider" (HFFD), combines the advantages of digital frequency division and PLL-based frequency synthesis. The HFFD consists of three main components:

1.  **Digital Frequency Divider (DFD)**: This module performs integer frequency division using a counter-based approach. It generates two clock signals, `clk_div_int` and `clk_div_frac`, which represent the integer and fractional parts of the desired frequency division ratio.
2.  **Phase-Locked Loop (PLL)**: This module is used to generate a clock signal with a frequency that is a fraction of the input clock frequency. The PLL consists of a phase detector, a loop filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO).
3.  **Phase-Shifting and Clock Generation**: This module generates the final fractional clock output by phase-shifting the `clk_div_int` and `clk_div_frac` signals and logically OR-ing them.

The HFFD operates as follows:

*   The DFD generates `clk_div_int` and `clk_div_frac` based on the input clock frequency and the desired frequency division ratio.
*   The PLL generates a clock signal, `clk_pll`, with a frequency that is a fraction of the input clock frequency.
*   The phase-shifting and clock generation module phase-shifts `clk_div_int` and `clk_div_frac` using the `clk_pll` signal and generates the final fractional clock output, `clk_div`, by logically OR-ing the phase-shifted signals.

This novel architecture offers several advantages, including:

*   **High frequency accuracy**: The PLL ensures that the generated clock signal has a high frequency accuracy, which is essential for many applications.
*   **Low jitter**: The HFFD architecture minimizes jitter by using a combination of digital and analog techniques.
*   **Flexibility**: The HFFD can be easily configured to support different frequency division ratios and input clock frequencies.
