To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by a fractional value (3.5x), we will utilize the double-edge clocking technique. This approach enables the achievement of half-integer division while adjusting for duty cycle imbalance. The core idea is to divide uneven clock cycles and phase-shift them to produce a smooth fractional clock output. The design involves counting clock cycles, generating intermediate divided clock signals with uneven periods, phase-shifting these signals, and finally logically OR-ing them to produce the final fractionally divided clock output. Key components include a counter to cycle through a specified number of clock cycles (MUL2_DIV_CLK = 7 for 3.5x division), logic to generate the uneven clock periods (4 cycles and 3 cycles), a phase-shifting mechanism to delay and advance the divided clock phases, and a logical OR gate to combine these phases into the final output.
