To introduce a novel architectural solution for the triangle wave generator, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of state machines and arithmetic logic. By utilizing a single state variable to control the direction of the waveform and a separate counter to generate the waveform values, we can create a design that efficiently produces the desired triangle wave. This approach will incorporate an up/down counter with a saturation mechanism to prevent overflow and underflow, ensuring that the waveform stays within the specified bounds (0 to 31). The counter will be incremented or decremented based on the current state, and state transitions will occur when the counter reaches the maximum or minimum value. This design aims to minimize the number of logic elements required while maintaining the desired functionality and potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
