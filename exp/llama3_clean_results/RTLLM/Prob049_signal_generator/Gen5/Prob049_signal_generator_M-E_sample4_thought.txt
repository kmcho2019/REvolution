The novel architectural solution for the triangle wave generator involves utilizing a phase accumulator and a comparator to generate the waveform. The phase accumulator is incremented by a constant value on each clock cycle, and the comparator checks if the accumulated phase exceeds a certain threshold, determining whether to increment or decrement the output waveform. This approach allows for a flexible and scalable design that can be easily modified to generate different waveforms by adjusting the phase increment and threshold values.
