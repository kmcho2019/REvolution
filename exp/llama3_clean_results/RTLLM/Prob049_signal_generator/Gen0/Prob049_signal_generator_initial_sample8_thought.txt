To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module, we will use a state machine with two states (0 and 1) to control the direction of the waveform (incrementing or decrementing). The waveform will be represented by a 5-bit signal named "wave". We will use an if-else statement inside an always block to check the state of the reset signal and update the state and wave registers accordingly. The case statement will be used to determine the direction of the waveform based on the current state.
