To implement the Triangle Wave signal generator module in Verilog, we will use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: incrementing and decrementing. The module will have a 5-bit output signal "wave" and will utilize a state register to control the direction of the waveform. We will use an always block triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal and the negative edge of the reset signal to update the state and waveform registers. The waveform generation will be controlled using a case statement based on the current state.
