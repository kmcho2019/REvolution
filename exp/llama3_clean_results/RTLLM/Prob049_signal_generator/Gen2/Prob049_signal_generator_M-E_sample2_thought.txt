To implement a novel Triangle Wave signal generator module, we can utilize a lookup table (LUT) to store the waveform values and a counter to index into the LUT. This approach allows for easy modification of the waveform shape and amplitude. We will use a parameterized LUT to make the design more configurable and reusable. The LUT will store the waveform values in ascending and descending order, and the counter will be used to index into the LUT. When the counter reaches the maximum value, the direction will be changed, and the counter will start decrementing.
