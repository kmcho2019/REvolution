The key idea here is to simplify the existing Verilog module for generating a triangle wave signal while maintaining its functionality. To achieve this, we can focus on reducing the complexity of the combinational logic within the always block. One approach is to use a single direction flag to control the increment or decrement of the waveform, eliminating the need for a multi-bit state register and associated case statement. This simplification can help reduce area and potentially power consumption, given that fewer logic elements are required.
