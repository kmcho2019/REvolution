To create a novel architectural solution for the triangle wave generator, we can utilize a counter-based approach that eliminates the need for a separate state machine. By employing a single counter that increments and decrements based on a direction signal, we can simplify the design and potentially improve performance and area efficiency. The counter will wrap around when it reaches the maximum value (31) or the minimum value (0), creating the desired triangle waveform. This approach also allows for easy modification to generate different waveforms by adjusting the counter's bounds and direction control logic.
