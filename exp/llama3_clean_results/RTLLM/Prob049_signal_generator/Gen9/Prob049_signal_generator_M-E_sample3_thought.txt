To create a novel architectural solution for the triangle wave generator, we will introduce a new concept that combines the benefits of simplicity, efficiency, and scalability. The idea revolves around using a single up-down counter with a variable step size, controlled by a simple state machine. This approach minimizes the number of registers and conditional statements, potentially reducing area and power consumption.

The new architecture will include the following components:
1. An up-down counter: This will be the core element for generating the triangle waveform. The counter will increment or decrement based on the current state.
2. A state machine: A simple, two-state machine will control the direction of the counter. The states will be 'increment' and 'decrement'.
3. A comparator: To detect when the counter reaches the maximum (31) or minimum (0) value, a comparator will be used to trigger state transitions.

The benefits of this approach include:
- Reduced area consumption due to the minimal number of registers and logic gates.
- Improved power efficiency by minimizing unnecessary signal toggling and using a simple state machine.
- Scalability, as the design can be easily modified to generate different waveforms by adjusting the counter step size and state machine logic.

Overall, this novel architectural idea aims to provide a balance between simplicity, performance, and area efficiency, making it suitable for a wide range of applications requiring triangle wave generation.
