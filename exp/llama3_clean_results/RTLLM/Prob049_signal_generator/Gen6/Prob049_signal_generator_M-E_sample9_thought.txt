To devise an innovative solution for the triangle wave generator, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the principles of a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) with a modulo counter. The LFSR will generate a pseudorandom sequence, and by applying a modulo operation, we can create a cyclic pattern that mimics the triangle waveform. This method eliminates the need for explicit state machines or direction signals, thereby reducing the complexity and potentially improving the overall performance. This novel architecture not only streamlines the design but also offers a unique perspective on generating periodic waveforms using pseudorandom sequences.
