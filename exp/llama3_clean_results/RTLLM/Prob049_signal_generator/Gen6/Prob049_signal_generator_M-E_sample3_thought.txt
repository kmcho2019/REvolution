The new architectural idea involves using a counter to generate the triangle wave signal. The counter will be incremented or decremented based on a control signal. When the counter reaches the maximum value (31), the control signal will switch to decrement mode, and when the counter reaches the minimum value (0), the control signal will switch to increment mode. This approach eliminates the need for a separate state register and associated case statement, simplifying the design. The counter will be implemented using a 5-bit register, and the control signal will be generated using a simple combinational logic.
