A novel approach to generating a triangle wave signal involves utilizing a counter-based architecture. This method employs two counters: one for incrementing the waveform and another for decrementing it. The counters are controlled by a single enable signal that toggles based on the waveform reaching its maximum or minimum value. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the number of conditional statements, potentially leading to improved performance and area efficiency. Additionally, it allows for easy modification of the waveform's frequency and amplitude by adjusting the counter's width and the enable signal's generation.
