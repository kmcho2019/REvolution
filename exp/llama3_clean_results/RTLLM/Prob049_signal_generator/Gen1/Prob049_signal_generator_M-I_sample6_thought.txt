1. Simplifying the combinational logic to improve Performance (Timing) by reducing the complexity of the case statement and conditional checks.
2. Minimizing the switching activity of the 'wave' register to reduce Power consumption.
3. Optimizing the logic structure to reduce Area usage.

- Replacing the case statement with a single conditional statement to control the increment or decrement of the waveform, which can help reduce the complexity of the combinational logic and improve timing.
- Introducing a more power-efficient method for generating the triangle wave, such as using a counter with a modified increment/decrement scheme that reduces the number of transitions, to minimize the switching activity of the 'wave' register.
- Using more area-efficient logic structures, such as a single multiplexer or demultiplexer, to control the state transitions, rather than separate conditional statements.
