To implement a triangle wave generator, we can utilize a counter-based approach. The counter will serve as the core of the waveform generation, incrementing and decrementing to produce the desired output. The state machine can be simplified by using a single variable to track the direction of the waveform, eliminating the need for a separate state register. By using a counter with a specific width (5 bits in this case), we can easily generate a waveform that cycles between 0 and 31. The direction of the counter will be controlled based on whether the current value is at its maximum (31) or minimum (0), flipping the direction accordingly. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the number of operations required per clock cycle.
