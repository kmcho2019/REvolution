The novel architectural idea for the Triangle Wave signal generator module involves using a counter to generate the waveform and a comparator to control the state transitions. This approach eliminates the need for explicit state registers and conditional statements, making the design more concise and efficient. The counter will increment or decrement based on the current state, and the comparator will detect when the counter reaches the maximum or minimum value to toggle the state. This design leverages the simplicity of arithmetic operations and comparisons to generate the triangle wave, potentially reducing the area and power consumption.
