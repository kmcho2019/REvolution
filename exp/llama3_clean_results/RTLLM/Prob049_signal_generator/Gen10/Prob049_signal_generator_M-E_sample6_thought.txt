The novel idea for the signal generator module involves utilizing a counter-based approach with a modular arithmetic to generate the triangle wave signal. This design eliminates the need for explicit state machines and conditional statements, reducing the complexity and area consumption.

The main concept is to use two counters: one for the rising edge of the triangle wave and another for the falling edge. Both counters will be 5-bit wide to cover the range from 0 to 31. The direction of the counter (incrementing or decrementing) will be determined by a toggle signal that changes state when the counter reaches the maximum or minimum value.

To further optimize the design, we will use a single always block to update both counters and the toggle signal. This approach minimizes the area consumption and reduces the power consumption.

The implementation involves:
1. Initializing the counters and toggle signal to their reset values.
2. On the rising edge of the clock signal, updating the counters based on the toggle signal.
3. When the counter reaches the maximum or minimum value, toggling the direction signal to change the direction of the counter.
4. Assigning the current value of the counter to the output wave signal.

This design is expected to improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics by reducing the complexity, area consumption, and power consumption compared to the existing solution.
