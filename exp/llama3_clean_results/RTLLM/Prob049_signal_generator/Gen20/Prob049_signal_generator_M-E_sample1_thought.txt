This process will continue, generating a triangle wave pattern.

The novel architectural idea here is to use a single counter and a simple comparator to achieve the desired waveform, eliminating the need for a separate direction signal and case statements. This approach simplifies the design, reduces switching activity, and potentially improves Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.

Additionally, we will use a more efficient way to update the waveform value by using a single assignment statement that takes into account the current value and the maximum or minimum value. This will reduce the number of operations and minimize the critical path delay.
