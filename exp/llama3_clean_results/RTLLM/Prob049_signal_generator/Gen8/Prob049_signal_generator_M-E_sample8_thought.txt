The novel architectural idea is to implement a triangle wave signal generator using a counter-based approach with a single register to store the waveform value. The counter will be incremented or decremented based on the current direction of the waveform. To simplify the logic and reduce the number of conditional checks, we will use a single conditional statement to update the counter and direction. This approach will minimize the combinatorial logic depth and reduce the switching activity, leading to improved PPA metrics.

The key components of this design include:

1. A 5-bit counter register to store the waveform value.
2. A direction signal to determine whether the counter should be incremented or decremented.
3. A single conditional statement to update the counter and direction based on the current waveform value and direction.

This approach eliminates the need for a separate state register and reduces the number of conditional checks, resulting in a more efficient and optimized design.
