The key idea is to simplify the existing design by eliminating unnecessary conditional checks and directly updating the counter and direction based on the current state. We can achieve this by using a single variable to track the direction of the waveform and updating it based on the counter value. This approach reduces the complexity of the always block and minimizes the number of conditional checks, leading to improved PPA metrics.

The simplified design includes:

1. A 5-bit counter register to store the waveform value.
2. A direction signal to determine whether the counter should be incremented or decremented.
3. A single conditional statement to update the counter and direction based on the current waveform value.

By simplifying the logic, we can reduce the combinatorial depth and switching activity, resulting in improved performance and power efficiency.
