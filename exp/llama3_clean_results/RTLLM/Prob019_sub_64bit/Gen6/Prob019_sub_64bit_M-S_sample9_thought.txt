To simplify the existing solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can further reduce the complexity of the overflow detection logic. The key insight is that an overflow occurs when the signs of A and the result are different, and A and B have different signs. This can be expressed using a single conditional statement that checks the sign bits of A, B, and the result. By directly comparing these sign bits, we can eliminate the need for separate if-else conditions and simplify the logic.
