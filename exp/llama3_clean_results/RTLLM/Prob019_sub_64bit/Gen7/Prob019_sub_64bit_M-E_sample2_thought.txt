To implement a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the properties of two's complement representation and employ a modular approach. Our novel idea involves dividing the subtraction operation into smaller, manageable parts, utilizing separate modules for the subtraction and overflow detection. This modular design allows for easier verification, testing, and potential reusability of the individual components. We will create a `sub_64bit_core` module responsible for the actual subtraction and a `overflow_detector` module to check for overflow conditions. This separation of concerns can lead to a cleaner, more maintainable design.
