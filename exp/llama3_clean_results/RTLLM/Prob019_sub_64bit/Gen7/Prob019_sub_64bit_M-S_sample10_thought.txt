To simplify the existing solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the properties of two's complement representation and the behavior of signed arithmetic operations. The key insight is that in two's complement, subtraction can be performed by inverting the bits of the subtrahend (B), adding 1, and then performing a standard addition. The overflow condition can be detected by examining the carry-out of the most significant bit position. This approach allows us to merge the subtraction and overflow detection into a single step, reducing complexity.
