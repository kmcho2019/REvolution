A novel architectural idea for implementing a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking involves utilizing a hierarchical approach. This design breaks down the 64-bit subtraction into smaller, more manageable parts by dividing the input operands into four 16-bit segments. Each segment is then processed separately using a combination of subtractors and overflow detection logic. The results from each segment are then combined to form the final 64-bit result. This hierarchical approach can potentially improve the design's performance by reducing the critical path length and allowing for more efficient use of resources. Additionally, it may offer better scalability for even larger arithmetic operations.
