To design a novel 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the concept of a carry-lookahead adder to improve performance. However, instead of using the traditional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead approach for subtraction, we will utilize a parallel prefix tree structure to compute the borrow signals in parallel. This approach reduces the critical path delay and improves overall performance.

The new architecture, named `sub_64bit_cla`, will be composed of several key components:
1. **Parallel Prefix Tree (PPT) Borrow Generation**: A tree-like structure that computes borrow signals in parallel, reducing the delay associated with sequential borrow propagation.
2. **64-bit Subtractor Core**: Uses the generated borrow signals to perform the subtraction operation.
3. **Overflow Detection**: Examines the sign bits of the operands and the result to detect overflow conditions.

By using a parallel prefix tree for borrow generation, we can significantly reduce the critical path delay compared to traditional subtractor designs, leading to improved performance. Additionally, optimizing the overflow detection logic to minimize switching activity and using low-power design techniques can help reduce power consumption.
