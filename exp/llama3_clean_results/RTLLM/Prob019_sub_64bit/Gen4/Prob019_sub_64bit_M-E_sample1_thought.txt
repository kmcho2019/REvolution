To design a novel 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the efficiency of binary subtraction with the flexibility of modular arithmetic. The new idea involves representing the 64-bit inputs as two's complement numbers and performing the subtraction operation in a modular fashion. This approach enables the detection of overflows based on the modular properties of the numbers. We will use a hierarchical design, where the 64-bit subtractor is composed of multiple smaller subtractors, each handling a segment of the input bits. This segmentation allows for a more efficient handling of carry propagation and overflow detection. The key innovation lies in the use of a carry-save adder (CSA) tree to efficiently handle the subtraction and carry propagation. The CSA tree structure reduces the number of carry propagation stages, leading to improved performance and reduced area usage.
