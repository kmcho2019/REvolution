To develop a novel architectural solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the concept of utilizing a parallel prefix adder to perform the subtraction operation. This approach allows for a potentially faster and more efficient computation by breaking down the subtraction into smaller, more manageable parts that can be executed concurrently. Additionally, we can implement a separate overflow detection module that utilizes a tree-like structure to compare the sign bits of the input operands and the result, enabling a more efficient and scalable way to detect overflow conditions.
