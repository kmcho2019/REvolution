A novel approach to designing a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking involves utilizing a combination of adders and subtractors to handle the sign bits separately from the rest of the bits. This approach recognizes that overflow conditions are primarily dependent on the sign bits of the operands and the result. By processing the sign bits separately and using the properties of two's complement arithmetic, we can simplify the overflow detection logic and potentially improve the overall efficiency of the module. The idea involves calculating the result of the subtraction operation in two parts: one for the 63 least significant bits and another for the most significant bit (sign bit). The overflow condition is then determined based on the signs of the operands and the result. This approach allows for a more modular and potentially area-efficient design.
