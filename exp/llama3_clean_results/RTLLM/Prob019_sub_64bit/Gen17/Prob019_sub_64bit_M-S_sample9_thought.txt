To simplify the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can directly use the built-in subtraction operation in Verilog and then implement the overflow detection logic. This approach eliminates the need for the `sub_16bit` module and the associated complexity of handling borrow and overflow through multiple instances. The overflow detection can be simplified by checking the sign bits of the operands and the result, which directly indicates whether an overflow has occurred.

The key idea is to perform the subtraction and then check for overflow conditions based on the signs of the operands and the result. If the signs of the operands are the same but different from the sign of the result, an overflow has occurred.

This simplified approach reduces the complexity of the circuit, potentially leading to improvements in power consumption, performance, and area utilization, as there are fewer gates and less complex logic involved.
