To simplify the existing solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the properties of two's complement arithmetic. In two's complement, the most significant bit (MSB) represents the sign of the number (0 for positive, 1 for negative). An overflow occurs when the subtraction of two numbers with the same sign results in a number with a different sign. Thus, we can directly compare the MSBs of the operands and the result to determine if an overflow has occurred. This approach maintains the functionality of the original design while reducing the complexity of the overflow detection logic.
