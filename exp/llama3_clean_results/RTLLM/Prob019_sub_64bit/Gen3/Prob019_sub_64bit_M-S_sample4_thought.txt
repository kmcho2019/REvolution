To further simplify the existing 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the properties of two's complement representation. In two's complement, the most significant bit (MSB) represents the sign of the number. By directly comparing the MSB of the operands and the result, we can determine if an overflow has occurred. This approach simplifies the overflow detection logic and reduces the overall complexity of the design. Additionally, we can utilize the built-in subtraction operation in Verilog, which inherently handles the borrow and overflow conditions for two's complement numbers.
