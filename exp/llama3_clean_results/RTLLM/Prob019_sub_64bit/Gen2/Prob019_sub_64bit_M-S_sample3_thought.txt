To further simplify the existing solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can maintain the basic structure but reduce the complexity of the overflow detection logic. The key insight is that an overflow occurs when the signs of A and the result are different, and the sign of B is opposite to that of A. We can express this condition using a single logical statement, eliminating the need for an if-else statement and directly assigning the result to the overflow flag. This simplification aims to reduce the overall complexity of the module while preserving its functionality.
