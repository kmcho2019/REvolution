To develop a novel architectural idea for a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we can leverage the concept of modular arithmetic and overflow prediction. The key insight is to predict the overflow condition before performing the actual subtraction, allowing for a more efficient and potentially power-reducing implementation.
