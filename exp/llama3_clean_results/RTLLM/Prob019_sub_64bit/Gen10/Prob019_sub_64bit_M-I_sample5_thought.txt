To improve the existing solution for the 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking, we will focus on optimizing the overflow detection logic for better area efficiency while maintaining or improving the performance and power consumption. The current implementation uses a straightforward comparison of the most significant bits (sign bits) of A, B, and the result to detect overflows. However, we can simplify this logic further by leveraging the properties of two's complement arithmetic used for signed numbers. Specifically, we can detect overflows by examining the carry-out from the most significant bit position during the subtraction operation. This approach eliminates the need for explicit comparison of the sign bits, potentially reducing area utilization while maintaining or improving performance and power efficiency.
