A novel approach to designing a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking involves utilizing a combination of arithmetic and logic circuits. The new architecture, called "sub_64bit_arch2," employs a hierarchical structure, where the 64-bit subtraction is broken down into smaller sub-operations. This is achieved by dividing the input operands into four 16-bit segments and performing the subtraction on each segment separately. The results from each segment are then combined to form the final 64-bit result. Additionally, the overflow detection logic is distributed across the segments, allowing for early detection of overflow conditions. This approach enables a more scalable and efficient design, as it can be easily extended to support larger operand sizes by adding more segments.
