A novel approach to designing a 64-bit subtractor with overflow checking is to utilize a hierarchical structure, where the subtraction operation is broken down into smaller, more manageable parts. This can be achieved by dividing the 64-bit operands into four 16-bit segments and performing the subtraction on each segment separately. The overflow detection logic can then be applied to each segment, allowing for a more modular and scalable design. This approach enables the use of smaller, more efficient arithmetic circuits, which can lead to improvements in power consumption, area, and performance.
