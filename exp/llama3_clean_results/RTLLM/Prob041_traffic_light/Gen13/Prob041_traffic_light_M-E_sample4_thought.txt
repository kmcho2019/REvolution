To create a novel solution, we will design a traffic light controller using a modular, hierarchical approach. The controller will consist of a top-level module that orchestrates the overall state machine, and separate sub-modules for each state (red, yellow, green). Each sub-module will handle its own timing and logic, allowing for a more distributed and scalable design.

The top-level module will manage the transitions between states, while the sub-modules will focus on their specific tasks. This separation of concerns will simplify the design and make it easier to maintain or modify individual components without affecting the overall system.

Additionally, we will implement a clock gating mechanism to reduce dynamic power consumption. The clock will be gated for each sub-module when it is not in use, minimizing unnecessary switching activity.

This hierarchical, modular approach will not only improve the design's maintainability but also provide a framework for potential future expansions, such as adding more complex traffic scenarios or integrating with other traffic management systems.
