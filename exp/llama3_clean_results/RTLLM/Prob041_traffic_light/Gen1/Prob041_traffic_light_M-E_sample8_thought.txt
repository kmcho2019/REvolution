To create a novel architectural solution for the traffic light controller, I'll employ a modular and scalable approach using a Finite State Machine (FSM) with a more intuitive and maintainable structure. The key idea is to separate the concerns of state management, timing, and output generation into distinct modules, each with a clear and focused responsibility.

The architecture will consist of three main modules:
1. **State Controller**: This module manages the current state of the traffic light (red, yellow, green) based on the internal counter and external requests (pedestrian button press).
2. **Timer**: This module handles the countdown logic for each state, ensuring that the traffic light phases are correctly timed.
3. **Output Generator**: This module generates the output signals for the traffic lights based on the current state.

This modular approach allows for easier maintenance, scalability, and potential reuse of individual modules in other designs. The use of a FSM ensures that the traffic light controller follows a well-defined sequence of states, making the design more predictable and reliable.

Additionally, to address the syntax errors and improve the overall design, I will ensure that the output signals (`red`, `yellow`, `green`) are properly declared and assigned values, either through continuous assignments or by being declared as reg-type variables with correct procedural assignments.
