The traffic light controller will be designed using a finite state machine (FSM) with four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be implemented in the first always block, while the counting logic will be handled in the second always block. The output signals will be assigned in the third always block. The counter values will be adjusted based on the pass_request signal and the current state of the traffic light controller.
