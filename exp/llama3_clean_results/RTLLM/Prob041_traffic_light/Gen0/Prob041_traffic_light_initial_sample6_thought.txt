To implement the traffic light controller, we will follow the design track provided. The traffic light controller will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in the first always block, the counting logic in the second always block, and the output signals in the third always block. The pedestrian button press will be handled in the second always block where the counter value will be adjusted based on the pass_request signal and the current state of the green signal. The lane light and the sidewalk light will be paired as per the problem statement.

The internal counter (cnt) will be used to keep track of the time spent in each state. The counter will be decremented on every positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. The counter values will be adjusted based on various conditions to implement the traffic light sequence. The output clock will be assigned the value of the internal counter (cnt).

The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be assigned the previous values (p_red, p_yellow, p_green) on the positive edge of the clock or negative edge of the reset signal. This will ensure that the output signals are updated correctly based on the current state of the traffic light controller.
