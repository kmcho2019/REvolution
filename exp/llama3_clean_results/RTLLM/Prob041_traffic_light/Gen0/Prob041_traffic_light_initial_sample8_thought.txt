To implement the traffic light system, we will follow the recommended design track. 
The system will have an initial state (idle) where it immediately transitions to the red state. 
It will then cycle through the red, green, and yellow states based on the internal counter values. 
The pass_request signal will be used to adjust the green state duration. 
We will use three always blocks to handle the state transition logic, the counting logic, and the output signals.
