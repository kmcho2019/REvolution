To implement the traffic light controller in Verilog, we need to follow the given design track and incorporate all the required features, including the state transition logic, counting logic, and output signal assignment. The traffic light controller should have three states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green, representing the different colors of the traffic light. The internal counter (cnt) will be used to control the timing of the traffic light. We also need to consider the pass_request signal to adjust the counter values accordingly. The assign statement will be used to output the current count value of the internal counter.
