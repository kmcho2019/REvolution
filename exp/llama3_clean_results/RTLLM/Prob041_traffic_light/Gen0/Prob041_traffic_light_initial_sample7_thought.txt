The problem can be solved by designing a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the traffic light based on the input signals and internal counter. The FSM will have four states: idle, s1_red, s2_yellow, and s3_green. The state transition logic will be handled in one always block, the counting logic will be handled in another always block, and the output signals will be handled in a third always block. The internal counter will be used to keep track of the time spent in each state. The pass_request signal will be used to adjust the counter values based on the current state and previous state.
