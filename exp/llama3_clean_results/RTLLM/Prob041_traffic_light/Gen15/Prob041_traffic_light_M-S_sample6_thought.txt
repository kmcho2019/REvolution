To simplify the traffic light controller, we will:
1.  **Simplify State Machine:** Reduce the number of states to only the necessary ones: red, yellow, and green.
2.  **Remove Unnecessary Logic:** Eliminate redundant logic in the next state and state transition blocks.
3.  **Minimize Conditional Checks:** Simplify the combinational logic for counting and state transitions.
4.  **Optimize Counter:** Use a smaller counter or optimize its usage to reduce area and power consumption.
5.  **Clock Gating:** Apply clock gating efficiently to reduce switching activity and power consumption.
6.  **Remove Unused Signals:** Eliminate any unused signals to reduce area and power consumption.

The simplified solution aims to achieve:
- Reduced critical path delay by minimizing combinational logic complexity.
- Lower power consumption through efficient clock gating and reduced switching activity.
- Improved area utilization by removing redundant logic and optimizing the counter.
- Simplified design for easier maintenance and modification.

The expected outcome is a simplified traffic light controller with reduced PPA metrics, making it more suitable for implementation in resource-constrained environments.
