To design a novel traffic light controller, we can adopt a Finite State Machine (FSM) approach with a focus on modularity and simplicity. The system will have three primary states: red, yellow, and green, each with its own timer. The pedestrian button press will be handled by interrupting the green state timer when necessary. 

We will utilize a counter to manage the state transitions based on the specified time intervals (60 clock cycles for green, 5 clock cycles for yellow, and 10 clock cycles for red). When the pedestrian button is pressed during the green phase, if the remaining time is greater than 10 clock cycles, it will be shortened to 10 clock cycles.

The design will incorporate two always blocks: one for the combinational logic to handle state transitions and another for the sequential logic to update the state and counter registers. The output signals (red, yellow, green) will be directly assigned based on the current state.

This approach aims to provide a clean, efficient, and scalable solution for the traffic light controller, focusing on ease of implementation and modification.
