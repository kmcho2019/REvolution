The counter's value is then used to determine when to toggle each of the output clock signals. By utilizing modulo operations, we can generate the required frequencies without the need for separate counters for each output clock.

For `CLK_10`, we toggle the signal every 5 counts of the counter, dividing the frequency by 10.

For `CLK_1`, we toggle the signal every 50 counts of the counter, dividing the frequency by 100.
