To design a novel frequency divider architecture, we can leverage the concept of using a single, high-speed counter to generate all output clock frequencies. This approach simplifies the design by minimizing the number of counters and logic gates required.

The proposed architecture consists of a single, high-speed counter that increments at the input clock frequency (100MHz). The counter is designed to have a sufficient number of bits to accommodate the maximum divisor (100) for generating the 1MHz clock.

To generate the output clocks, we use a combination of bitwise operations and conditional statements to extract the desired clock frequencies from the high-speed counter. Specifically, we use bitwise AND operations to detect when the counter reaches the desired divisors for each output clock frequency (2, 10, and 100).

This approach allows for a more efficient and scalable design, as it eliminates the need for multiple counters and reduces the overall logic complexity. Additionally, the use of bitwise operations enables the design to operate at high speeds, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.
