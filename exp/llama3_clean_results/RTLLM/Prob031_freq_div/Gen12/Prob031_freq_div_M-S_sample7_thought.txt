To simplify the frequency divider solution, we focus on maintaining the essential functionality while reducing complexity. The key insight is recognizing that the frequency division is achieved through counters that toggle the output clocks at specific intervals. 

We maintain separate counters for each output clock (50MHz, 10MHz, and 1MHz) to ensure accurate frequency division. The counters are reset upon the active reset signal, and the output clocks are toggled based on the counter values reaching specific thresholds.

For the 50MHz clock, we simply toggle the output clock on every positive edge of the input clock, effectively dividing the frequency by 2.

For the 10MHz and 1MHz clocks, we increment the respective counters and toggle the output clocks when the counters reach the values of 9 and 99, respectively. This approach ensures the output clocks are generated at the desired frequencies.

The simplification involves ensuring that the logic is straightforward, easy to understand, and minimizes unnecessary complexity while maintaining the required functionality.
