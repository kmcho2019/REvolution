To implement the frequency divider, we will design a module named 'freq_div' with input ports 'CLK_in' and 'RST', and output ports 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1'. We will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency: one for each output clock signal. The counters will be incremented on every positive edge of the input clock signal. The output clock signals will be toggled when their respective counters reach the desired values.
