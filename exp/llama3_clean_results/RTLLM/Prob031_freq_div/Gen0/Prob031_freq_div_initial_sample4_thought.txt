To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. The input clock signal 'CLK_in' will be divided by 2, 10, and 100 to generate the output clock signals 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', and 'CLK_1' respectively. 
We will use a reset signal 'RST' to reset the output clock signals and counters. 
On every positive edge of 'CLK_in' or 'RST', we will check if 'RST' is active. If it is, we will reset 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', 'CLK_1' and the counters 'cnt_10', 'cnt_100' to their initial values. 
Otherwise, we will update the values of 'CLK_50', 'CLK_10', 'CLK_1' and the counters based on their respective division factors. 
We will use a simple toggle logic for 'CLK_50', and counters with specific thresholds for 'CLK_10' and 'CLK_1'.
