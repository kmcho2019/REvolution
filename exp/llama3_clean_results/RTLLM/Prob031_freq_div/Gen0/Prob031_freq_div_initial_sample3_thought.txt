To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we need to consider the three output clocks: CLK_50, CLK_10, and CLK_1. 
- For CLK_50, we toggle its value on every positive edge of the input clock CLK_in. 
- For CLK_10, we use a counter cnt_10 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_10 reaches 9 (since 10/2 - 1 = 4 and we toggle on 5th cycle, and then reset), we toggle CLK_10.
- For CLK_1, we use a counter cnt_100 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_100 reaches 99 (since 100/2 - 1 = 49 and we toggle on 50th cycle, and then reset), we toggle CLK_1.
All these counters and clocks should be reset when the RST signal is active.
