To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use three counters to divide the input clock frequency. 
For CLK_50, we simply toggle its value on every positive edge of CLK_in.
For CLK_10, we use a counter cnt_10 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_10 reaches 4, we toggle CLK_10 and reset cnt_10 to 0.
For CLK_1, we use a counter cnt_100 that increments on every positive edge of CLK_in. When cnt_100 reaches 49, we toggle CLK_1 and reset cnt_100 to 0.
The reset signal RST is used to reset all the counters and output clocks to their initial state.
