To implement the frequency divider module 'freq_div', we will use a novel architectural approach that utilizes a single counter and then derives the various clock frequencies from it using combinational logic. This approach reduces the area usage and improves the design's efficiency. The counter will be used to generate a clock enable signal for each of the output clocks, which will then be used to toggle the output clocks. This approach eliminates the need for multiple counters and reduces the switching activity, resulting in improved power consumption.
