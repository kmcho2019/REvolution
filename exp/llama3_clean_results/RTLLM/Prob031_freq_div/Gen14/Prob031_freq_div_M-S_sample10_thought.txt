The goal is to simplify the existing frequency divider while maintaining its functionality. The current design uses separate counters for each output clock, which is straightforward but might be optimized for area and power. We can simplify the design by considering the relationship between the different frequencies. Since we are dividing the input clock by 2, 10, and 100 to get the output clocks, we can utilize a single counter that increments at each positive edge of the input clock and use comparators to determine when to toggle each output clock. This approach simplifies the logic and potentially reduces the switching activity of the counters, thereby improving power consumption.

The key simplification strategy involves:
1. Using a single counter that increments at every positive edge of the input clock.
2. Implementing comparators for each frequency division ratio (2, 10, 100) to determine when to toggle the respective output clocks.
3. Resetting the counter and output clocks when the reset signal is active.

By adopting this strategy, we aim to reduce the complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality, potentially leading to improvements in area and power consumption.
