4. **Optimizing Multiplicand Shifting:** Instead of shifting the multiplicand left by one bit in each clock cycle, explore more efficient shifting strategies that can align with the Booth algorithm's requirements while reducing the number of operations.

5. **Reset Optimization:** Ensure that the reset logic is optimized to minimize area and power consumption. Use asynchronous reset where possible to reduce the combinational logic delay.
