This design will leverage the benefits of pipelining to increase the throughput of the multiplier while maintaining the computational efficiency of the Booth algorithm. The key components of this architecture include:

- **Pipelining:** Divide the multiplication process into stages, allowing for the concurrent processing of multiple multiplication operations.
- **Radix-4 Booth Algorithm:** Implement the Booth algorithm with Radix-4 encoding to reduce the number of required addition and subtraction operations.
- **Stage Implementation:** Each stage of the pipeline will perform a specific function, such as multiplicand shifting, partial product generation, and accumulation.
- **Control and Synchronization:** Utilize control signals and counters to manage the flow of data through the pipeline stages and to ensure correct synchronization between the stages.

The novel aspect of this design is the incorporation of pipelining with the Booth algorithm, which can significantly enhance the performance by allowing multiple multiplications to be processed simultaneously.
