The 8-bit Radix-4 Booth multiplier module will be designed using a finite state machine (FSM) with a counter to track the multiplication process. The multiplier will have two main states: reset and multiplication. 
In the reset state, the module will initialize two 16-bit registers with the input values (a and b) and their sign extensions. 
In the multiplication state, the module will perform the Booth algorithm by shifting the multiplicand register, accumulating the product register based on the current bit of the multiplier register, and incrementing the counter. 
Once the counter reaches 16, the module will set the ready signal (rdy) to 1 to indicate the completion of the multiplication process.
