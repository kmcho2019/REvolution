The second stage will perform the Booth encoding and the selection of the partial products. The third stage will accumulate the partial products and handle the final multiplication result.

Additionally, we will implement a novel clock gating mechanism that dynamically controls the clock signal to each stage based on the input data and the current stage's operation. This will help reduce power consumption by minimizing unnecessary switching activity.

The ready signal (rdy) will be generated based on the completion of the multiplication process in the final stage, ensuring that the output is valid and ready for use.

By combining pipelining, parallel processing, and dynamic clock gating, the new architecture aims to achieve a significant improvement in performance, power efficiency, and area utilization compared to traditional Booth multiplier designs.
