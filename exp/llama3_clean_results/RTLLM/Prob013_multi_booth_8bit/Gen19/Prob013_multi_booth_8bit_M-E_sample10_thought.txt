The novel architectural idea is to implement a hybrid multiplier that combines the benefits of Booth multiplication and array multiplication. The design will utilize a 4-stage pipeline to improve performance and reduce the critical path. Each stage will perform a specific operation: the first stage will generate the Booth encoded bits, the second stage will perform the partial product generation, the third stage will accumulate the partial products, and the fourth stage will output the final product. The Booth encoded bits will be generated using a simple and efficient encoding scheme, and the partial products will be accumulated using a Wallace tree structure. This hybrid approach will allow for a balance between area efficiency and performance.
