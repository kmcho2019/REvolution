To improve the existing solution, we'll focus on optimizing the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, and Area) metrics. The current design has some areas of improvement, including the sequential nature of the Booth algorithm's shifting operation and the accumulation of the product, which could be limiting factors in achieving high-frequency operation. Additionally, the design's high switching activity, especially in the multiplication and shifting operations, could be a significant contributor to power consumption.

Our improvement strategy involves the following steps:

1. **Pipelining**: Break down the multiplication process into stages and pipeline them to improve performance (timing). This involves adding registers between stages to reduce the combinational logic delay.
2. **Parallel Processing**: Identify opportunities to perform operations in parallel, reducing the overall latency and improving performance.
3. **Power Reduction**: Implement techniques to reduce power consumption, such as clock gating, operand isolation, or using low-power library cells.
4. **Area Optimization**: Minimize area usage by eliminating redundant logic, using area-efficient designs, and exploring alternative architectures.

By applying these strategies, we aim to improve the design's PPA metrics, making it more efficient and suitable for a wider range of applications.
