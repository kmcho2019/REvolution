To implement a pulse detection module that accurately identifies sequences of 0 to 1 to 0 within 3 cycles, a finite state machine (FSM) can be utilized. The FSM will transition through states based on the input signal `data_in`, ensuring that the specific sequence is detected correctly.

The proposed architecture consists of an FSM with four states: IDLE, RISING, HIGH, and FALLING. The FSM starts in the IDLE state, waiting for the rising edge of the pulse (transition from 0 to 1). Upon detecting the rising edge, it transitions to the RISING state. If `data_in` remains high for one cycle, the FSM moves to the HIGH state. Finally, if `data_in` goes low after being high, the FSM transitions to the FALLING state, where it sets `data_out` to 1, indicating the detection of a complete pulse sequence. After setting `data_out` to 1, the FSM returns to the IDLE state, ready to detect the next pulse.

This approach ensures that the pulse detection logic is accurately implemented, meeting the requirements specified in the problem statement.
