To improve the pulse detection module, we will focus on two main aspects: functional correctness and optimization for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. For functional correctness, we need to accurately identify the start and end of a pulse, considering the possibility of the pulse occurring over three consecutive clock cycles. This involves adjusting the logic to properly detect the sequence 0-1-0 in the input signal `data_in`. For PPA optimization, we will aim to simplify the logic, reduce switching activity, and minimize area consumption without compromising the module's functionality.

Our strategy involves using a finite state machine (FSM) approach to track the different states of the pulse detection process. The FSM will have states to represent the detection of the rising edge (0 to 1), the high state (1), and the falling edge (1 to 0) of the pulse. By using an FSM, we can explicitly define the conditions for each state transition, ensuring accurate pulse detection.

To optimize for PPA, we will ensure that the logic is as simple as possible while maintaining functional correctness. We will also minimize the use of unnecessary logic gates and optimize the design for the target technology, reducing area consumption and power dissipation.
