To improve the existing pulse detection module, we can enhance the functional correctness by accurately detecting the pulse pattern (0-1-0) within three cycles as described in the problem statement. We will introduce a finite state machine (FSM) approach to track the states of the pulse detection process more clearly and efficiently. The FSM will have specific states for detecting the start, middle, and end of a pulse, ensuring that the module correctly identifies pulses even in scenarios where the pulse might be preceded or followed by other pulses. For PPA optimization, we aim to minimize area consumption and reduce switching activity by using a simple yet effective FSM structure.
