The idea is to count the number of consecutive clock cycles where data_in is high and then check if data_in falls after this count. If it does, we indicate the presence of a pulse by setting data_out to 1.

The counter will be used to track the duration of the high pulse in data_in. When data_in rises, we start counting, and when data_in falls, we check if the count is greater than 1 (indicating at least two consecutive high cycles, followed by a low cycle). If this condition is met, we set data_out to 1, indicating a pulse.

This approach simplifies the state machine logic and reduces the number of states required, potentially improving area efficiency and reducing the complexity of the combinational logic.
