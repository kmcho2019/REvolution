To improve the existing pulse detection module, we can focus on two main aspects: functional correctness and optimization for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. For functional correctness, we need to ensure that the module accurately detects the pulse pattern (0-1-0) within three cycles as described in the problem statement. This involves adjusting the logic to properly identify the start and end of a pulse, considering the possibility of the pulse occurring over non-consecutive clock cycles. For PPA optimization, we will aim to simplify the logic, reduce switching activity, and minimize area consumption without compromising the module's functionality.
