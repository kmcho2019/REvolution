The FSM will transition from IDLE to COUNTING when the `valid_count` signal is asserted, and it will transition back to IDLE when `valid_count` is deasserted. This approach provides a clean and modular design, making it easier to understand and modify the counter's behavior. The use of an FSM also allows for easy extension of the counter's functionality, such as adding more states or transitions.
