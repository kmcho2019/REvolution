To simplify the existing solution, we focus on minimizing the logic while preserving the functionality. The main components of the design are the gated clock generation and the sequential logic for the counter. We aim to simplify these components without compromising the performance or functionality of the counter.

The key simplification steps involve:
1. **Removing Redundant Logic**: Eliminate any redundant or unnecessary logic that does not contribute to the core functionality of the counter.
2. **Direct Clock Gating**: Instead of generating a separate gated clock signal, we can directly use the `valid_count` signal within the sequential logic to control the counting.
3. **Natural Wrap-Around**: Continue leveraging the natural wrap-around behavior of the 4-bit counter to avoid explicit conditional logic for handling the maximum count value.

By streamlining the design in this manner, we can reduce complexity, potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics of the implementation.
