To introduce a novel architectural idea for the counter_12 module, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the efficiency of a simple counter with the flexibility of a finite state machine (FSM). This design will utilize a 4-bit counter as the core counting element and integrate an FSM to manage the counting process, including the reset, pause, and wrap-around logic.

The FSM will have three main states: Idle, Counting, and Reset. The Idle state represents the counter's initial state or when it's paused due to the valid_count signal being inactive. The Counting state is where the counter increments on each clock cycle. The Reset state is entered when the reset signal (rst_n) is active low, forcing the counter to reset to 0.

This hybrid approach allows for a modular and scalable design. The counter and FSM can be optimized separately, and the FSM can be easily extended to manage more complex counting sequences or additional features like multiple reset levels or count thresholds.

Moreover, to further enhance the design, we can incorporate a Clock Enable (CE) signal within the FSM to control the counter's clock input. This ensures that the counter only increments when both the valid_count signal and the CE signal from the FSM are active, providing an additional layer of control over the counting process.
