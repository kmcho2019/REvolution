The novel approach to designing the counter_12 module involves utilizing a Finite State Machine (FSM) to manage the counting process. This FSM will have two main states: an idle state where the counter does not increment, and a counting state where the counter increments until it reaches the maximum value (4'd11). The transition between these states will be controlled by the valid_count signal. When the reset signal (rst_n) is active, the FSM will transition to the idle state, resetting the counter. 

To optimize for power, the clock to the counter will be gated based on the current state of the FSM, ensuring that the counter only consumes power when it is in the counting state. The FSM's state will be encoded using a 2-bit binary encoding, allowing for easy extension to additional states if needed in the future.

The counter itself will be designed using a simple up-counter logic, with a wrap-around condition checked at the maximum count value. The use of a dedicated FSM for managing the counter state provides a modular and scalable design approach, making it easier to integrate with other modules or extend the functionality of the counter in the future.
