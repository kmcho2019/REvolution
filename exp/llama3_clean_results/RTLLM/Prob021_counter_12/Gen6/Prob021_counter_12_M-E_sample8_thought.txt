To create a novel solution for the counter design, we will focus on a modular, scalable approach that incorporates a separate control unit to manage the counting process. This control unit will handle the enable signal (`valid_count`), the reset signal (`rst_n`), and the wrap-around logic, making the design more modular and easier to extend for different counting ranges.

The counter itself will be a simple, parameterizable module that can be easily adapted for different bit widths. This modular design will improve maintainability, scalability, and readability. The control unit will generate the necessary control signals for the counter, including an enable signal for the clock gating and a reset signal for the counter.

This approach will also allow for easier integration of additional features, such as programmable counting ranges or multiple counting modes, by simply modifying or extending the control unit without affecting the counter module. The use of a separate control unit will also facilitate the optimization of the counting logic and the control logic independently, potentially leading to better PPA metrics.

Moreover, to further optimize the design for power consumption, we will incorporate a clock gating technique at the counter module level, ensuring that the clock is only enabled when the counter needs to increment, thus reducing dynamic power consumption.
