To simplify the counter_12 module, we will focus on reducing the complexity of the existing design while maintaining its functionality. 

The key simplification steps include:
- Removing the gated clock signal (clk_gated) and instead, using the always_ff block with the posedge clk condition directly. This is because the counting logic is already conditional on the valid_count signal, which achieves the same effect as clock gating in terms of preventing unnecessary state changes.
- Removing the separate combinational logic block for calculating next_out and instead, perform the next state calculation directly within the sequential logic block. This reduces the need for an additional signal (next_out) and simplifies the overall design structure.
- Maintaining the reset logic as it is, to ensure the counter resets correctly when the rst_n signal is active low.

By implementing these simplifications, the design becomes more straightforward and easier to understand while maintaining its original functionality.
