This approach enables the counter to operate in a manner that is both power-efficient and highly responsive to changes in the `valid_count` signal.

Furthermore, to enhance the design's robustness and testability, we'll incorporate a built-in self-test (BIST) mechanism. The overall goal is to create a counter design that not only meets the original requirements but also offers improved performance, power efficiency, and testability, making it suitable for a wide range of applications.
