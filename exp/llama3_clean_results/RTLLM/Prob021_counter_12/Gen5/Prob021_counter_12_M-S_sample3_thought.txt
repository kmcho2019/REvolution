To simplify the counter_12 module, we can eliminate the need for a temporary variable (next_out) and directly update the output register (out) within the always block. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of signals and the combinational logic required. The reset logic can still be handled at the beginning of the always block to ensure the counter resets properly when the reset signal is active. The counting logic can then be implemented using a simple if-else statement to check the valid_count signal and update the counter accordingly.
