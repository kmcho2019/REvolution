To implement the counter_12 module with a novel architectural idea, we will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and COUNTING. In the IDLE state, the counter will remain unchanged. When the valid_count signal is asserted, the FSM will transition to the COUNTING state, where the counter will increment on each clock cycle until it reaches the maximum count value (4'd11) and then wrap around to 0. The reset signal (rst_n) will be used to transition the FSM back to the IDLE state and reset the counter to 0. This approach allows for a more structured and scalable design.
