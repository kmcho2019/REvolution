To design a novel counter architecture, we'll explore a state machine-based approach. Instead of a traditional counter that increments or wraps around based on conditions, we can define a finite state machine (FSM) that progresses through states representing the count values. This approach allows for a modular and scalable design, especially when considering more complex counting sequences or larger counter sizes. The FSM will have 12 states (from 0 to 11), and it will transition between these states based on the valid_count signal. When the reset signal is active, the FSM will return to its initial state (0). This design decouples the counting logic from the conditional checks, potentially simplifying the synthesis and optimization process.
