To simplify the existing design of an unsigned 8-bit multiplier based on pipelining processing, we can focus on optimizing the partial product generation and accumulation stages. The main idea is to use a more straightforward approach for generating partial products and accumulating them.

The simplified architecture will still consist of four main stages: input buffering, partial product generation, partial product accumulation, and final product calculation.

In the input buffering stage, we will register the input multiplicand and multiplier.

In the partial product generation stage, we will generate partial products by shifting the multiplicand according to the multiplier bits and conditionally adding it to the accumulator based on the multiplier bit.

In the partial product accumulation stage, we will accumulate the partial products to produce the final product.

In the final product calculation stage, we will register the final product and generate the output enable signal.

We will also simplify the output assignment logic and ensure that the output is only valid when the multiplication operation is complete.
