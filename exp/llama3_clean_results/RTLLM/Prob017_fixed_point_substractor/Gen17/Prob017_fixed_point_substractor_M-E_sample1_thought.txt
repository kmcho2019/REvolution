The novel idea here is to create a fixed-point subtractor module that utilizes a combination of arithmetic logic units (ALUs) and shift registers to handle the subtraction operation. This approach allows for more flexibility in terms of handling different precision points and also enables easier extension to more complex arithmetic operations. The module will take two fixed-point numbers as input, perform the subtraction, and then output the result. The precision of the subtraction will be controlled by the parameters Q (number of fractional bits) and N (total number of bits). The subtraction operation will be broken down into three main stages: sign bit detection, magnitude subtraction, and result sign determination. This modular approach will improve readability, maintainability, and scalability of the code.
