The novel approach involves designing a fixed-point subtractor module that utilizes a combination of bitwise operations and arithmetic logic to handle the subtraction while maintaining precision. The architecture will consist of three main stages: 
1. A sign detection stage where the signs of the inputs are determined.
2. A magnitude comparison stage where the absolute values of the inputs are compared to determine the result's sign.
3. A core computation stage where the actual subtraction is performed based on the signs and magnitudes of the inputs.
To improve the handling of edge cases, such as zero results, and to ensure correct sign propagation, additional logic will be integrated into the computation stage.
This approach aims to provide a more efficient and scalable solution for fixed-point subtraction, capable of handling a wide range of input values and precision requirements.
