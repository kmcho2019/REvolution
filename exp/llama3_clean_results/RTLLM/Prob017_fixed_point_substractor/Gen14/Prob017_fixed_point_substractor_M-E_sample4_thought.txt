The novel architectural idea for the fixed-point subtractor module involves utilizing a combination of bitwise operations and conditional statements to handle the subtraction of two fixed-point numbers. This approach aims to improve the performance and synthesizability of the design by avoiding the use of complex arithmetic operations.

The new architecture will employ the following key concepts:

1. **Bitwise Operations**: Utilize bitwise operations such as bit shifting and masking to extract the integer and fractional parts of the input numbers.
2. **Conditional Statements**: Use conditional statements to determine the sign of the result and handle the cases where the signs of the input numbers are the same or different.
3. **Lookup Table (LUT)**: Implement a small LUT to store the results of common subtraction operations, reducing the complexity of the design and improving performance.
4. **Pipelining**: Pipeline the subtraction operation to improve timing performance and reduce the critical path.

By combining these concepts, the new architecture will provide a efficient and synthesizable solution for the fixed-point subtractor module.

The design will consist of the following stages:

1. **Input Stage**: Extract the integer and fractional parts of the input numbers using bitwise operations.
2. **Subtraction Stage**: Perform the subtraction operation using conditional statements and the LUT.
3. **Output Stage**: Determine the sign of the result and output the final result.

This novel architecture will provide a unique and efficient solution for the fixed-point subtractor module, improving performance, synthesizability, and area efficiency.
