To design the fixed_point_subtractor module, we first need to determine the signs of the input operands and then perform the subtraction based on these signs. For same-sign subtraction, we simply subtract the absolute values of the inputs, and for different-sign subtraction, we add the absolute values of the inputs and determine the sign of the result based on the relative sizes of the inputs. We will use the parameters Q and N to ensure accurate fixed-point arithmetic with the specified precision.
