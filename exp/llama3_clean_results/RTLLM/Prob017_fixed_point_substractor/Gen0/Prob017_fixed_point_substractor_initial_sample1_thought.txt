To implement the fixed_point_subtractor module, we will first determine the signs of the input operands a and b. Based on the signs, we will perform the subtraction operation. If the signs are the same, we will directly subtract the two numbers. If the signs are different, we will compare the absolute values of a and b and determine the sign of the result accordingly. We will also handle the edge case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0. The subtraction operation will be performed while maintaining the fixed-point precision defined by the parameters Q and N.
