The fixed_point_subtractor module will be implemented with the following logic:
- The Q parameter will determine the fixed-point precision by dividing the N-bit input into integer and fractional parts.
- Same sign subtraction will be performed when the signs of inputs a and b are the same, preserving the sign of the result.
- Different sign subtraction will involve comparing the absolute values of a and b to determine the sign of the result.
- Zero handling will be implemented by explicitly setting the sign bit to 0 when the result is zero.
- The subtraction operation will be performed while maintaining the precision defined by the parameters Q and N.
