To implement the fixed-point subtractor module, we first need to understand the conditions for same sign and different sign subtraction. We can utilize the most significant bit (MSB) of the input operands to determine their signs. For same sign subtraction, we directly perform the subtraction operation. For different sign subtraction, we add the absolute values of the input operands and determine the sign of the result based on their relative sizes. We also need to handle the edge case where the result is zero and ensure that the subtraction operation maintains the specified fixed-point precision defined by the parameters Q and N.
