The new approach for the fixed-point subtractor module involves leveraging a combination of fixed-point arithmetic libraries and a novel architecture that utilizes a two-stage pipeline for improved performance. This design focuses on accurately handling the subtraction of fixed-point numbers with a specified number of fractional bits, while also simplifying the logic for better synthesizability and optimizing for power, performance, and area (PPA) metrics.

The two-stage pipeline consists of:
1. **Sign Handling Stage**: This stage determines the signs of the input operands and decides whether to perform a direct subtraction or an addition based on the sign bits. This stage also handles the case where the result is zero by explicitly setting the sign bit to zero.
2. **Arithmetic Stage**: This stage performs the actual subtraction or addition operation based on the decision made in the sign handling stage. It utilizes a fixed-point arithmetic module to ensure accurate handling of the fractional bits.

To improve synthesizability and optimize for PPA, the design incorporates the following strategies:
- **Parameterized Module**: The `fixed_point_subtractor` module is parameterized to allow for flexible configuration of the number of fractional bits (`Q`) and the total number of bits (`N`).
- **Synthesis-Friendly Constructs**: The design uses synthesis-friendly constructs, such as non-blocking assignments and clocked registers, to enhance the synthesizability of the module.
- **Minimized Signal Usage**: The design minimizes the number of signals and wires used, reducing the area requirements and improving performance.

This novel architecture and design approach aim to provide a high-performance, low-power, and area-efficient fixed-point subtractor module that accurately handles the subtraction of fixed-point numbers.
