To develop a novel architectural idea for the fixed-point subtractor module, we will introduce a hierarchical design approach that leverages modular arithmetic components. The idea is to break down the subtraction operation into its constituent parts: sign bit handling, integer part subtraction, and fractional part subtraction. Each of these parts will be handled by separate, reusable modules. This approach enhances modularity, maintainability, and scalability.

The top-level module, `fixed_point_subtractor`, will serve as an aggregator of these components. It will manage the flow of data between the modules, ensuring that the final result is correctly assembled from the outputs of the constituent parts. The use of separate modules for sign handling, integer subtraction, and fractional subtraction allows for easier optimization and customization of each component based on the specific requirements of the target application.

For the integer part, we will use an `integer_subtractor` module that performs a standard two's complement subtraction. This module will be responsible for handling overflows and underflows according to the defined bit width.

The fractional part will be handled by a `fractional_subtractor` module. This module will perform the subtraction while ensuring that the fractional precision is maintained and that any necessary rounding or truncation is applied according to the defined precision.

To manage the flow and ensure that the signs of the inputs are correctly handled, a `sign_handler` module will be introduced. This module will determine whether the subtraction involves numbers of the same sign or different signs and direct the outputs accordingly.

By modularizing the design in this way, the `fixed_point_subtractor` module becomes more flexible and easier to optimize for specific use cases. Each component can be refined independently, and new features or optimizations can be added without affecting the overall structure of the top-level module.

This hierarchical, modular approach not only improves the maintainability and scalability of the design but also enhances its readability and understandability, making it easier for designers to comprehend and modify the architecture as needed.
