A novel approach to implementing the fixed-point subtractor involves utilizing a two-stage pipeline architecture, where the first stage handles the sign analysis and potential inversion of the operands, and the second stage performs the actual subtraction while maintaining the precision defined by the parameters `Q` and `N`. This approach enhances the module's performance by breaking down the complex operation into simpler, more manageable stages, and it ensures that the subtraction operation respects the fixed-point representation's precision requirements.

In this architecture, the first stage compares the signs of the input operands `a` and `b`. Depending on the signs, it either passes the operands through unchanged or inverts the sign of one or both operands to prepare them for the subtraction operation in the second stage. This stage also generates control signals that dictate how the second stage should handle the operands to maintain the correct sign and precision in the result.

The second stage, which is responsible for the actual subtraction, receives the processed operands and control signals from the first stage. It performs the subtraction while considering the precision defined by `Q` and `N`, ensuring that the integer and fractional parts of the result are correctly computed. This stage also applies the necessary sign adjustments based on the control signals from the first stage to produce the final result `c`.

By using a two-stage pipeline, this design achieves a balance between complexity and performance. It simplifies the logic required in each stage, making it more synthesizable and potentially reducing the area and improving the timing of the resulting digital circuit.

Moreover, to improve testability and verification, the design includes a testbench that exercises the `fixed_point_subtractor` module with a variety of input scenarios, including different sign combinations and values that test the precision and edge cases of the subtraction operation. This ensures that the module behaves correctly under various conditions and adheres to the specified requirements.
