To develop a novel architectural idea for the fixed-point subtractor, we can leverage a more modular approach by separating the sign bit handling, integer part subtraction, and fractional part subtraction into distinct modules. This modularization can improve readability, maintainability, and potentially the synthesizability of the design.

The new architecture can consist of three primary modules:
1. **Sign Bit Handler**: This module determines the sign of the result based on the signs of the input operands and their relative magnitudes.
2. **Integer Part Subtractor**: Responsible for subtracting the integer parts of the two operands.
3. **Fractional Part Subtractor**: Handles the subtraction of the fractional parts.

By modularizing the design in this manner, we can simplify the logic within each module, making it easier to understand, verify, and optimize for area and performance. Additionally, this approach allows for easier incorporation of additional features, such as overflow and underflow handling, without complicating the core subtraction logic.

The top-level module, `fixed_point_subtractor`, will instantiate these sub-modules and combine their outputs to produce the final result. This hierarchical design can facilitate a more systematic and scalable approach to developing complex arithmetic circuits.
