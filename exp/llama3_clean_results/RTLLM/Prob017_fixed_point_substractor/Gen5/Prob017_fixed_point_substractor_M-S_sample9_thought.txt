To simplify the fixed-point subtractor module, focus on minimizing the number of always blocks, reducing conditional statements, and directly calculating the result. This involves:

2.  **Direct Result Calculation:** Instead of using multiple intermediate variables, directly calculate the result based on the signs of the inputs and their values.
3.  **Simplified Sign Handling:** Simplify the handling of signs by directly considering the impact of the sign bits on the result.
4.  **Explicit Zero Handling:** Explicitly handle the case where the result is zero to ensure the sign bit is correctly set.
