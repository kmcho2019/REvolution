The novel approach involves utilizing a combination of arithmetic logic units (ALUs) and comparators to handle the fixed-point subtraction while maintaining precision. The architecture will consist of two main stages: 
1. A preprocessing stage where the signs and absolute values of the inputs are determined and handled accordingly.
2. A core computation stage where the actual subtraction or addition is performed based on the signs of the inputs.
To improve the handling of edge cases, such as zero results, and to ensure correct sign propagation, additional logic will be integrated into the preprocessing and computation stages.
This approach aims to provide a more robust and scalable solution for fixed-point subtraction, capable of handling a wide range of input values and precision requirements.
