To implement a novel fixed-point subtractor module, we can utilize a combination of arithmetic logic units (ALUs) and multiplexers to efficiently handle same sign and different sign subtractions. This approach takes advantage of the properties of two's complement representation to simplify the subtraction operation. By using ALUs to perform the subtraction and addition operations, and multiplexers to select the appropriate result based on the signs of the input operands, we can create a more streamlined and efficient fixed-point subtractor.
