To design a novel fixed-point subtractor module, we can leverage a pipeline architecture that incorporates a combination of bitwise operations and arithmetic logic units (ALUs) to efficiently handle same sign and different sign subtractions. The proposed architecture, dubbed "Bitwise Subtraction Pipeline" (BSP), involves a series of stages that progressively handle the subtraction operation.

The BSP consists of three primary stages:

1.  **Sign Detection Stage (SDS):** This stage determines the signs of the input operands and generates control signals based on whether the signs are the same or different.

2.  **Bitwise Subtraction Stage (BSS):** This stage performs the bitwise subtraction operation based on the control signals generated by the SDS. If the signs are the same, it performs a straightforward subtraction. If the signs are different, it adds the absolute values of the operands.

3.  **Result Adjustment Stage (RAS):** This stage adjusts the result of the subtraction operation based on the signs of the input operands and the result itself. It handles cases where the result is zero, negative, or positive, ensuring that the final result is accurately represented.

To further optimize the design, we can incorporate bitwise operations and ALUs that are optimized for the specific bit widths and arithmetic operations required by the fixed-point subtractor. This approach allows for efficient handling of same sign and different sign subtractions while minimizing the number of logic gates and improving the overall performance.

Additionally, we can utilize a "borrow propagation" mechanism to efficiently handle borrow signals during the subtraction operation. This mechanism involves propagating borrow signals from the least significant bits (LSBs) to the most significant bits (MSBs) in a way that minimizes the number of logic gates required.

By leveraging the BSP architecture and incorporating optimized bitwise operations, ALUs, and borrow propagation, we can create a highly efficient and scalable fixed-point subtractor module that is optimized for synthesizability and performance.
