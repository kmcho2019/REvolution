To refactor the clkgenerator module, we will shift from the counter-based approach to a simpler method that leverages the delay and assign statements in Verilog. The initial state of the clock will still be set to 0, and the clock will toggle at regular intervals defined by the PERIOD parameter. The use of #delay will ensure the clock toggles at the desired frequency, creating a square wave signal. This approach simplifies the module and avoids the use of $global_clock, making it more compatible with standard Verilog synthesis and simulation tools.
