To simplify the clock generator module, we can use a single always block with a sensitivity to a virtual clock signal, and utilize a counter to track the current time. When the counter reaches half of the specified PERIOD, we toggle the clock signal. This approach ensures a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency. We will remove the initial block and instead use a default initialization for the clock signal and the counter. The counter will increment at every time unit of the virtual clock signal, and the clock signal will toggle when the counter reaches half of the PERIOD. This design guarantees a reliable and efficient clock signal generation.
