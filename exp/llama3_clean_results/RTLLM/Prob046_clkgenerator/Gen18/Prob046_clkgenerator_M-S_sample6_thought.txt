A simplified approach to designing the clock generator module is to utilize a single always block with a non-blocking assignment to the clock signal. This block would toggle the clock signal at regular intervals defined by the PERIOD parameter, creating a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency. We can achieve this by using a counter to track the time and toggle the clock when the counter reaches half of the PERIOD. This approach eliminates the need for an internal ring oscillator and provides a straightforward, synthesizable solution.
