The goal is to simplify the existing clock generator module while maintaining its functionality. The main concept involves directly using a parameterized delay within an always block to toggle the output clock signal at regular intervals, eliminating the need for a counter variable. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the potential for synthesizability issues. The initial block will still be used to set the initial state of the clock signal. The key simplification involves:
1. **Removing the counter variable**: Directly utilize the delay within the always block to control the clock toggling.
2. **Simplifying the always block**: Eliminate the conditional statement for toggling the clock, instead using the delay to create the desired periodicity.
3. **Maintaining the initial condition**: Ensure the clock signal starts in the correct state.

By simplifying the logic and focusing on the essential functionality, the module becomes more straightforward and potentially more efficient in terms of PPA metrics.
