To create a novel clock generator module, we can utilize a counter-based approach. The idea is to use a counter that increments at every time step, and when the counter reaches half of the specified period, the clock signal is toggled. This approach ensures a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency. We will use a parameterizable module to make the clock generator flexible and reusable. The counter will be reset to zero when it reaches the specified period, effectively creating a continuous square wave.
