To create a reliable clock generator module, we will use a conventional approach that involves a counter to track the current time and toggle the clock signal when the counter reaches half of the specified PERIOD. This approach ensures a continuous and synchronized clock signal. We will also use a single always block with a conditional statement to optimize the code for better performance. The initial block will be used to initialize the clock signal and the counter. The counter will increment at every time unit, and the clock signal will toggle when the counter reaches half of the PERIOD. This design guarantees a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency.
