A novel approach to designing the clock generator module is to utilize a combination of a counter and a modulo operation to generate the clock signal. This approach allows for a more flexible and scalable design, where the clock period can be easily adjusted by modifying the parameter. We can use a single always block with a sensitivity to the counter to update the clock signal. The counter will be incremented at every positive edge of a default clock (e.g., a 1Hz clock), and the clock signal will be toggled when the counter reaches half of the specified PERIOD. This approach ensures a clean and synthesizable implementation.
