To address the requirement of generating a periodic clock signal with a specified period, a novel approach can be taken by utilizing a counter-based mechanism. The idea revolves around creating a digital circuit that increments a counter at every clock cycle (using a default clock, e.g., a system clock) and toggles the output clock signal when the counter reaches a specific threshold, which is derived from the desired period of the output clock. This approach ensures a straightforward and efficient generation of a clock signal with a precise period defined by the PERIOD parameter. The key is to accurately calculate the threshold value based on the relationship between the system clock frequency and the desired output clock frequency.
