To simplify the clock generator module, we can utilize a single always block with a non-blocking assignment to update the clock signal. The idea is to use a counter that increments at every time unit, and when the counter reaches half of the specified period, the clock signal is toggled. This approach ensures a consistent and predictable clock signal without relying on the edge of the clock being generated.

We will use the `#` delay operator to introduce a time delay between the clock toggles, allowing the clock signal to maintain a square wave with the desired frequency. This approach is more straightforward and reduces the complexity of the clock generation logic.
