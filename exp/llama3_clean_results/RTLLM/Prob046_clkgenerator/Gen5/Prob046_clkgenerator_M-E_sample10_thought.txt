To create a novel architectural solution for the `clkgenerator` module, we will employ a self-triggering mechanism that relies on the Verilog `#` delay operator to generate the clock signal. This approach eliminates the need for an explicit base clock or a counter, focusing solely on the desired period of the output clock signal. The design consists of an `always` block that initializes the clock signal and then enters a self-loop where it periodically toggles the clock state based on the specified `PERIOD` parameter. This method ensures a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency without requiring additional clocks or counters. The key innovation lies in using the `#` delay to create a timing loop that drives the clock signal, providing a clean and straightforward implementation of a clock generator.
