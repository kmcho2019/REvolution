To simplify the `clkgenerator` module, we should focus on directly generating a periodic clock signal without unnecessary complexity. This can be achieved by using a single counter that increments at every time step (in this case, every simulation time unit since we're not tied to a specific clock for this generation) and toggles the clock output when it reaches a threshold defined by the `PERIOD` parameter. This approach eliminates the need for a ring oscillator or feedback mechanisms, making the design more straightforward and reliable.

The key components of this simplified design are:

1. **Counter**: A counter that increments at every time step. This counter will determine when to toggle the clock signal.
2. **Threshold Check**: A conditional statement that checks when the counter reaches the threshold (half of the `PERIOD` for toggling the clock). Once this threshold is reached, the counter resets, and the clock signal toggles.
3. **Clock Signal Generation**: The clock signal is generated by toggling its state whenever the counter resets.

This approach simplifies the design, reduces complexity, and maintains the functionality of generating a periodic clock signal as specified by the `PERIOD` parameter.
