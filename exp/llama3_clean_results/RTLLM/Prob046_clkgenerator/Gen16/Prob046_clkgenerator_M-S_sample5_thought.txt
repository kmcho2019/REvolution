To simplify the clock generator module, we can use a traditional approach with a single always block and a delay control to generate the clock signal. This approach eliminates the need for an accumulator and non-standard always block trigger, making the design more suitable for digital synthesis. We maintain the parameter PERIOD to define the clock period and toggle the clock signal every half of the specified PERIOD to ensure a square wave output. This simplified design reduces complexity, improves synthesizability, and maintains the desired functionality.
