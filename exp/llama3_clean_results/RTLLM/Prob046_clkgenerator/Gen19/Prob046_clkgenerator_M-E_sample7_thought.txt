To create a novel architectural solution for the clock generator module, we can leverage the concept of a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) to produce a pseudorandom sequence that can be used to generate a clock signal. However, since the requirement is for a periodic clock signal, we'll use a different approach that involves a counter to ensure the clock signal toggles at precise intervals defined by the `PERIOD` parameter. The idea is to use an `always` block that increments a counter at every time unit, and when the counter reaches half of the `PERIOD`, the clock signal is toggled. This approach ensures a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency without relying on an `initial` block for the clock signal's initial state or a `forever` loop for toggling. Instead, the `always` block with a conditional statement controls the clock signal's behavior, making it synthesizable and suitable for digital design implementation.
