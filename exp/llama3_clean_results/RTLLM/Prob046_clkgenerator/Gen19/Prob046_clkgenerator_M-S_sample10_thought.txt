To simplify the clock generator module, we can utilize a traditional approach that directly generates a periodic clock signal without relying on an external clock or complex state machines. This can be achieved by using an `always` block with a delay to toggle the clock signal at regular intervals, directly creating a square wave clock signal with the desired frequency defined by the `PERIOD` parameter. By directly using the `always` block with a delay, we avoid the potential issues related to division operations within delay specifications. We also declare the output port `clk` as a `reg` to ensure it can be assigned a value within the `always` block.
