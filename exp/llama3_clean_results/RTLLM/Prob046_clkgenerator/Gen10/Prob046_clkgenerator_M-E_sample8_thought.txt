To design a novel clock generator module, we will utilize a ring oscillator approach, which employs a chain of inverters connected in a loop to generate a clock signal. This approach allows for a high degree of flexibility and can be easily parameterized to achieve the desired clock frequency. We will use a generic parameterizable module that takes the period as an input and generates a clock signal with the specified period.

Our novel architectural idea involves the following steps:
1. Create a ring oscillator with a specified number of stages to achieve the desired delay and, consequently, the desired clock frequency.
2. Use a counter-based approach to control the number of stages in the ring oscillator, allowing the module to be parameterized for different clock periods.
3. Ensure that the ring oscillator is initialized correctly to prevent any potential issues with metastability or startup behavior.

By adopting this ring oscillator approach, we aim to achieve a synthesizable and efficient clock generator module that meets the requirements while providing a high degree of flexibility and customizability.
