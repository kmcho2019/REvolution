To refactor the existing `adder_pipe_64bit` module, we will introduce a pipeline structure with multiple stages, each handling a portion of the 64-bit addition. The main objectives are:
1. **Stage-based Addition**: Break down the 64-bit addition into smaller segments (e.g., 16-bit or 8-bit segments), with each segment processed in a separate pipeline stage. This approach helps in reducing the critical path delay and improving the overall throughput.
2. **Carry Management**: Properly manage the carry signals between stages to ensure accurate results. This involves capturing the carry from each segment's addition and propagating it to the next segment.
3. **Clock and Reset Control**: Use the clock signal to synchronize the pipeline stages and ensure that the reset signal properly initializes all registers.
4. **Output Synchronization**: Ensure that the final output and its enable signal are synchronized with the clock, reflecting the completion of the addition operation.

By structuring the addition process into stages and managing carry signals effectively, we aim to create a more efficient and scalable pipelined adder.
