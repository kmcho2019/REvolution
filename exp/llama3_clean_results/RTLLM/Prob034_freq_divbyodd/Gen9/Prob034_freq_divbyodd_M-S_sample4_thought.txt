To simplify the frequency divider module, we will use a single counter that counts up to the divisor value minus one. The counter will be incremented on every clock edge. We will calculate the midpoint of the divisor and use it to toggle the clock divider output directly, eliminating the need for separate clock dividers for positive and negative edges.

This approach reduces the complexity of the module by removing unnecessary logic for tracking rising and falling edges separately and simplifies the calculation for toggling the clock divider output. The result is a more straightforward and efficient implementation that maintains the functionality of dividing the input clock frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter.
