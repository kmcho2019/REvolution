To simplify the frequency divider, we can directly implement the counters and clock dividers as described in the problem statement. This approach eliminates the need for a phase accumulator and complex calculations. We will use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, and two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, to generate the divided clock output for positive and negative edges, respectively. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. This design ensures that the input clock frequency is divided by an odd number, as specified by the parameter NUM_DIV.
