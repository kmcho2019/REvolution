To simplify the existing frequency divider solution, I will utilize a single counter and a more straightforward logic for toggling the clock dividers. 

The simplification strategy involves the following steps:

1.  Initialize a counter that increments on every clock edge.
2.  Update the clock divider output when the counter reaches half of the desired division period for the positive edge and the desired division period for the negative edge.
3.  Derive the final divided clock output based on the counter value and the desired division period.
4.  Initialize the counter and clock divider output using the active low reset signal rst_n.

The goal is to reduce the complexity of the design while maintaining its accuracy in dividing the input clock frequency by an odd number.
