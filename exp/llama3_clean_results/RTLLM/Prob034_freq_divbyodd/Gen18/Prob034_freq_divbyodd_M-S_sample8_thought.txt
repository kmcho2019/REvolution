To simplify the frequency divider code, we will:
1. Remove unnecessary variables and logic.
2. Utilize a single counter to track both rising and falling edges of the clock.
3. Implement a straightforward logic to toggle the divided clock output at the correct times.
4. Ensure the correct handling of the reset signal.

The main idea is to use a single counter that increments on every clock cycle, regardless of the edge. When the counter reaches half of the odd divisor `NUM_DIV`, we toggle the divided clock output. This approach accurately divides the input clock frequency by an odd number defined by the parameter `NUM_DIV`.
