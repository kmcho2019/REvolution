To address the issues with the existing frequency divider, we propose a novel architectural idea that leverages a single counter and a phase accumulator to generate the divided clock output. This approach simplifies the design, reduces area consumption, and minimizes unnecessary switching activity.

The main strategy involves:
1. Utilizing a single counter to track the clock cycles and generate a phase accumulator.
2. Implementing a phase accumulator that increments by a fixed step size on every clock cycle.
3. Comparing the phase accumulator with a threshold value derived from the odd divisor `NUM_DIV` to generate the divided clock output.
4. Employing a reset mechanism to initialize the counter and phase accumulator.

This approach ensures that the divided clock output is accurately generated by the odd divisor value, and the design is optimized for better PPA metrics.
