A novel approach to designing the frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, involves utilizing a single counter that counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. The divided clock output, clk_div, is generated by toggling it every time the counter reaches (NUM_DIV - 1) / 2 and then again when the counter wraps around to (NUM_DIV - 1). This ensures that the divided clock output is correctly derived, taking into account the odd divisor value. The module will also be resettable using the active low reset signal rst_n. To achieve this, a state machine can be used to control the counter and the divided clock output.
