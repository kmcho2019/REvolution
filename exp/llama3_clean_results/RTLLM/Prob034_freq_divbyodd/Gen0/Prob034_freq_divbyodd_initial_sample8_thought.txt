To implement the frequency divider module freq_divbyodd, we first define the module with the specified input and output ports. We then use two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. These counters increment on every clock cycle and reset when they reach NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, are used for the positive and negative edges of the clock. These dividers are toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
