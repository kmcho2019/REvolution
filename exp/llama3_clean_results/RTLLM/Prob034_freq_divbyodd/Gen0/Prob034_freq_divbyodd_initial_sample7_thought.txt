The frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, can be designed by using two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the input clock. The counters will count up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, will be used for positive and negative edges of the clock. These will be toggled when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, will be derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The module will also be resettable using the active low reset signal rst_n.
