To design the frequency divider module, we'll follow these steps:
1. Define the module with the required input and output ports.
2. Implement the counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
3. Develop the logic to generate the divided clock outputs for positive and negative edges based on the counters and the parameter NUM_DIV.
4. Combine the divided clock outputs for both edges to produce the final divided clock output.
5. Add the reset logic to initialize the counters and the divided clock outputs when the active low reset signal is asserted.
