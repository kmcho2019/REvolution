1. Define the module with the specified input and output ports.
2. Declare the parameter NUM_DIV with a default value of 5, representing the odd divisor value.
3. Initialize two counters (cnt1 and cnt2) to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
4. Create two separate clock dividers (clk_div1 and clk_div2) for positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively.
5. Toggle clk_div1 and clk_div2 when their respective counters reach half of NUM_DIV.
6. Derive the final divided clock output (clk_div) by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2.
7. Implement the active low reset signal (rst_n) to initialize the counters and clock divider outputs.
