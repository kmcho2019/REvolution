To design the freq_divbyodd module, we first define the module with the required input and output ports. We use a parameter NUM_DIV to specify the odd divisor value. Two counters, cnt1 and cnt2, are implemented to track the rising and falling edges of the clock, respectively. Each counter counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. Two separate clock dividers, clk_div1 and clk_div2, are used for the positive and negative edges of the clock, toggling when the counters reach half of NUM_DIV. The final divided clock output, clk_div, is derived by logically OR-ing clk_div1 and clk_div2. The active low reset signal rst_n is used to initialize the counters and the clock divider outputs.
