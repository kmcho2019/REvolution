To improve the frequency divider solution, I propose a novel architecture that leverages a single counter and a clever use of bitwise operations to simplify the division logic. This approach will utilize a counter that increments on every clock edge and employs bitwise operations to determine when to toggle the clock divider output. The key insight is to use the properties of binary numbers to efficiently divide the clock frequency by an odd number.

The new strategy involves the following steps:

1. Initialize a counter that increments on every clock edge.
2. Use bitwise operations to determine when the counter has reached half of the desired division period for the positive edge and the desired division period for the negative edge.
3. Derive the final divided clock output based on the counter value and the desired division period, using bitwise operations to simplify the logic.
4. Initialize the counter and clock divider output using the active low reset signal rst_n.

This approach aims to reduce the complexity of the design while maintaining its accuracy in dividing the input clock frequency by an odd number. By leveraging bitwise operations, we can simplify the division logic and improve the overall efficiency of the frequency divider.
