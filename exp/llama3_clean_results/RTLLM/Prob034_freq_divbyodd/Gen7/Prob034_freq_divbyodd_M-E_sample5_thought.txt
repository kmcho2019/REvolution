To create a novel solution for the frequency divider that accurately divides the input clock frequency by an odd number, I propose a design that utilizes a single counter and a clever use of modular arithmetic to generate the divided clock output. The idea is to count the clock cycles and toggle the divided clock output when the counter reaches a specific threshold that corresponds to half of the desired division ratio. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the number of sequential elements, leading to improved PPA.

The new architecture will use a single counter that counts up to NUM_DIV - 1. When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV, the divided clock output will be toggled. To achieve this, we can use the following logic:

- Count the clock cycles using a single counter.
- When the counter reaches half of NUM_DIV, toggle the divided clock output.
- Use modular arithmetic to ensure the counter wraps around to 0 after reaching NUM_DIV - 1.

This approach eliminates the need for separate counters for positive and negative edges, reducing the overall complexity of the design.
