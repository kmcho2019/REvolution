2. A PWM generator that produces a pulse-width modulated signal based on the counter value. The PWM signal has a pulse width that is proportional to the counter value.
3. A DCO that generates the divided clock output based on the PWM signal. The DCO's frequency is controlled by the PWM signal, allowing for precise frequency division.
