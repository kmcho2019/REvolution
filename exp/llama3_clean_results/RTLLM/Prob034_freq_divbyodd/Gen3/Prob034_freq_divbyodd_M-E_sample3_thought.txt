To create a novel solution for the frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, we can leverage a different architectural approach that involves using a single counter and taking advantage of the properties of odd numbers to simplify the division process. The key idea is to use a single counter that counts up to the desired odd divisor (NUM_DIV) and then resets. The divided clock output can be generated by toggling it at every other count, effectively dividing the frequency by the odd number. This approach eliminates the need for separate counters for rising and falling edges and simplifies the logic for generating the divided clock.

The strategy involves:
1. Initializing a counter to zero.
2. Counting up to NUM_DIV on every clock cycle.
3. When the counter reaches NUM_DIV, resetting it to zero and toggling the divided clock output.
4. Using the active low reset signal (rst_n) to reset the counter and the divided clock output when asserted low.

This approach ensures that the input clock frequency is divided by an odd number, as specified by the parameter NUM_DIV, and provides a straightforward implementation that can be easily optimized for area and power efficiency.
