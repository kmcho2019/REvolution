To simplify the frequency divider module, we can eliminate the state machine and directly use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock. Each counter will count up to half of the desired divisor. We can then use these counters to generate two separate clock dividers, one for positive and one for negative edges of the clock. The final divided clock output can be derived by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers. This approach simplifies the code and reduces the complexity of the design.

Improvement Strategy:
1. Use two counters to track the rising and falling edges of the clock.
2. Generate two separate clock dividers, one for positive and one for negative edges of the clock.
3. Derive the final divided clock output by logically OR-ing the two clock dividers.
4. Implement a simple reset logic to initialize the counters and clock dividers.
