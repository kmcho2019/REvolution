To simplify the frequency divider module, freq_divbyodd, we can utilize a single counter that counts up to the odd divisor value, NUM_DIV. The divided clock output, clk_div, can be generated by toggling it at the middle point of the counter. This approach eliminates the need for separate counters and clock dividers for positive and negative edges, reducing complexity while maintaining functionality. The active low reset signal, rst_n, can be used to initialize the counter and the clock divider output. 
We will use a single counter to track the number of clock cycles, and when the counter reaches half of the odd divisor value, the divided clock output is toggled. This ensures the divided clock frequency is accurately one-third, one-fifth, etc., of the input clock frequency.
