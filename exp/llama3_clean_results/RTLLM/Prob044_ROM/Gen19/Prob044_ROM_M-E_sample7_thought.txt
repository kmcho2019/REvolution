To create a novel architectural solution for the ROM module, we can leverage the concept of distributed memory and hierarchical addressing. This approach involves dividing the ROM into smaller, more manageable blocks, each with its own addressing mechanism. By using a hierarchical addressing scheme, we can reduce the complexity of the addressing logic and potentially improve the overall performance and area efficiency of the ROM. Additionally, we can explore the use of parameterization to make the ROM more flexible and reusable across different designs. This new architecture will not only provide a fresh perspective on the traditional ROM design but also offer a scalable and efficient solution for storing and accessing large amounts of data.
