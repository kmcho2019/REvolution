To create a novel architectural solution for the ROM module, we can leverage the concept of a hierarchical memory structure, where the ROM is divided into smaller sub-modules, each responsible for a portion of the total address space. This approach can lead to area and power savings by reducing the number of memory locations that need to be accessed simultaneously. We will implement a two-level hierarchy, where the first level decodes the higher bits of the address to select one of several smaller ROMs, and the second level uses the lower bits of the address to access the specific data within the selected ROM. This hierarchical approach can also facilitate easier modification and expansion of the ROM contents in the future.
