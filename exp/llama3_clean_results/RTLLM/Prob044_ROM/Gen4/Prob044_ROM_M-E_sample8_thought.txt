To develop a novel architectural idea for the ROM module, let's explore the concept of a distributed ROM, where the memory is divided into smaller segments, each with its own access logic. This approach could potentially offer benefits in terms of area efficiency and performance by allowing for parallel access to different segments of the memory. We can implement this by dividing the 8-bit address into two parts: the upper 4 bits to select one of 16 segments, and the lower 4 bits to address a location within the selected segment. Each segment would then contain 16 locations, making the overall memory organization more hierarchical. This distributed architecture can be particularly useful if the data stored in the ROM has certain patterns or is divided into categories that can be efficiently stored and accessed in this manner.
