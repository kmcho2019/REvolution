To implement a novel architectural idea for the sequence detector, we can utilize a combination of counters and comparators to detect the specific sequence. The detector will be designed as a finite state machine (FSM) with states representing the progress through the sequence. We will employ a counter to keep track of the number of consecutive matches of the desired sequence. The FSM will transition through states based on the input data and the counter value. This approach allows for a straightforward and efficient detection of the sequence, with the added benefit of being easily scalable for detecting longer sequences.
