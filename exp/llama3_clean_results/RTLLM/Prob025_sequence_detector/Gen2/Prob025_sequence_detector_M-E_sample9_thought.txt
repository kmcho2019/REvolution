To implement a novel sequence detector, we can use a shift register approach combined with a comparator. The idea is to shift the incoming data into a 4-bit shift register. After each shift operation, we compare the contents of the shift register with the target sequence (1001). If the contents match the target sequence, we assert the sequence_detected output for one clock cycle. This approach simplifies the state machine and makes the design more straightforward. We will also include a reset mechanism to clear the shift register and reset the sequence_detected output when the reset signal is high.
