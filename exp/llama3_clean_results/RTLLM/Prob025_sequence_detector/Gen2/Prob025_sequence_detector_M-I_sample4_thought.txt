The existing sequence detector design can be improved for better performance, power, and area (PPA) metrics. The current design uses a finite state machine (FSM) with four states (IDLE, S1, S2, S3) to detect the sequence 1001. However, the transition logic can be simplified, and the code can be optimized for better synthesis results. The main improvements will focus on reducing the number of states and transitions, simplifying the combinational logic, and ensuring proper reset behavior. Additionally, the design will be optimized to reduce power consumption and area usage.
