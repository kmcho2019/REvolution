To implement an innovative sequence detector, we can utilize a shift register-based approach combined with a comparator to detect the specific 4-bit sequence 1001. The shift register will store the last 4 bits of the input data stream, and the comparator will check if the stored bits match the desired sequence. This approach allows for efficient detection of the sequence without the need for a complex finite state machine. We will also incorporate a reset mechanism to return the detector to its initial state when the reset_n signal is low.
