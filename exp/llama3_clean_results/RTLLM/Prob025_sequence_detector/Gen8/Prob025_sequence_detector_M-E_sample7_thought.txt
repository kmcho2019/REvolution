To implement a sequence detector with a novel architectural solution, we can utilize a counter-based approach combined with a specific sequence checking mechanism. This approach involves counting the number of bits that match the desired sequence in a specific order. The detector will have a counter that increments when a bit matches the expected sequence and resets when a mismatch occurs. The sequence is detected when the counter reaches the length of the desired sequence. This approach allows for a simple and efficient detection of the sequence without the need for complex finite state machines or shift registers. The module will have a counter to keep track of the correct sequence bits and logic to compare the input data with the desired sequence.
