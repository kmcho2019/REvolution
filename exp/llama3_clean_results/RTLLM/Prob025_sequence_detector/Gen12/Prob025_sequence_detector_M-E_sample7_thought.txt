To implement a novel solution for the sequence detector, we can utilize a different architectural approach that incorporates a combination of counters and comparators to identify the desired sequence. This approach focuses on detecting the specific bits in the sequence rather than storing the entire sequence in a register.

Novel Idea:
1. **Bit Counter**: Implement a counter to keep track of the number of bits that match the desired sequence in sequence.
2. **Bit Comparator**: Compare each incoming bit with the corresponding bit in the desired sequence.
3. **State Machine**: Use a simple state machine to track the progress through the sequence, resetting when a mismatch is found.
4. **Output Generation**: When the counter reaches the length of the desired sequence, assert the `sequence_detected` output.

This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the complexity of the design by focusing on individual bit comparisons rather than storing and comparing entire sequences. The state machine ensures that the detection process resets upon finding a mismatch, allowing for continuous detection of the sequence in a stream of data.
