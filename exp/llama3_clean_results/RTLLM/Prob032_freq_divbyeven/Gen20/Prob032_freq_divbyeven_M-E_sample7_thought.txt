A novel approach to designing the frequency divider module (`freq_diveven`) is to utilize a more modular and scalable architecture. This can be achieved by incorporating a separate module for the counter logic and another for the clock division logic. The counter module (`cnt_mod`) will handle the incrementing and resetting of the counter based on the `NUM_DIV` parameter, while the clock division module (`clk_div_mod`) will generate the divided clock signal (`clk_div`) based on the counter output. This separation of concerns allows for easier maintenance, modification, and reuse of the individual components. Additionally, the `NUM_DIV` parameter validation can be performed within the counter module to ensure that it is an even number greater than 1. The divided clock signal will be generated by toggling the output every time the counter reaches half of the `NUM_DIV` value, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the specified even number.
