To implement the frequency divider module (freq_diveven), we need to design a counter that increments on each positive edge of the input clock (clk) until it reaches half of the division factor minus one. Once this limit is reached, the counter resets, and the divided clock output (clk_div) is toggled. This approach ensures the output clock frequency is divided by the specified even number. The counter will be a 4-bit register, and the module will have an active-low reset (rst_n) to initialize the counter and the divided clock output when needed.
