To design the freq_diveven module, we will use a counter-based approach. The counter will track the number of clock cycles and reset when it reaches a specified limit, which is half of the division factor minus one. The divided clock signal will toggle every time the counter resets, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by the NUM_DIV parameter. We will implement the counter as a 4-bit register and use a parameterized approach to make the design flexible and reusable.
