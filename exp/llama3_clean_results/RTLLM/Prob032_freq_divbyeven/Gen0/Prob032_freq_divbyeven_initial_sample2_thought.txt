The module freq_diveven will be designed with a counter that increments on each positive edge of the input clock and toggles the divided clock output when it reaches half of the specified division factor minus one. This will effectively divide the input clock frequency by the even number specified by NUM_DIV. The counter will be reset to zero and the divided clock output will be initialized to zero when the active-low reset signal is asserted. 
The 4-bit counter will allow the module to divide the input clock frequency by even numbers up to 16. 
The use of a parameter NUM_DIV will make the module reusable for different division factors.
