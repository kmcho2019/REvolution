To simplify the existing frequency divider module, we focus on the essential logic required to divide the input clock frequency by an even number specified by `NUM_DIV`. The key simplification involves directly checking the counter value against the required limit to decide when to toggle the output and reset the counter.

1. **Maintain Correct Division**: The counter should reach a value that corresponds to half of `NUM_DIV - 1` before toggling the `clk_div` signal and resetting.
2. **Simplify Counter Logic**: The counter (`cnt`) increments on every positive edge of the input clock (`clk`) until it reaches the specified limit. At this point, the counter resets, and the `clk_div` signal toggles.
3. **Optimize Always Block**: Ensure the `always` block only includes necessary logic for the counter increment, reset, and `clk_div` signal toggle based on the counter value and reset condition.

This approach aims to reduce complexity by focusing on the critical conditions for the counter and `clk_div` signal updates.
