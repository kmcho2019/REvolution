To create a novel architectural idea for the frequency divider module, we can utilize a dual-stage counter approach. The first stage (coarse counter) will be responsible for counting up to half of the specified division factor, while the second stage (fine counter) will handle the remaining count. This design takes advantage of the fact that the division factor is an even number, allowing us to simplify the counter logic and improve overall efficiency. By dividing the counting process into two stages, we can reduce the complexity of the counter and minimize the number of operations required to achieve the desired frequency division.
