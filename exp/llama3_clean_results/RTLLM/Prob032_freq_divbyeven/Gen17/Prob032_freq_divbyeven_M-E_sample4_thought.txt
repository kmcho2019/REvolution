The new architectural idea involves using a combination of two counters to achieve the frequency division by an even number. The first counter (`cnt_odd`) counts up to half of the division factor minus one, and when it reaches this limit, it resets and sets a flag (`toggle_en`). The second counter (`cnt_even`) counts up to half of the division factor, and when it reaches this limit, it resets and toggles the divided clock output (`clk_div`) if the `toggle_en` flag is set. This approach allows for a more straightforward implementation of the frequency division logic and eliminates the need for the generate block used in the previous solution. Additionally, it ensures that the divided clock output is always toggled at the correct interval, regardless of the division factor.
