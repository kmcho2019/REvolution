To create a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by an even number, we can employ a novel approach using a phase-locked loop (PLL)-inspired architecture, but without the complexity of a full PLL. The idea is to use two counters: one to count the input clock cycles and another to generate the divided clock signal at the desired frequency. The division factor is determined by the `NUM_DIV` parameter, which must be an even number. When the counter reaches the specified limit, it resets to zero and toggles the divided clock output, effectively dividing the frequency of the input clock. This approach ensures a simple, efficient, and accurate frequency division.
