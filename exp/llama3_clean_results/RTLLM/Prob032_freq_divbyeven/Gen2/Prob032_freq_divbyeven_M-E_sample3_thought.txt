To create a novel architectural solution for the frequency divider module `freq_diveven`, we can utilize a dual-counter approach. The first counter (`cnt_high`) will count up to half of the division factor (`NUM_DIV / 2`), and the second counter (`cnt_low`) will count from half of the division factor to the full division factor. This approach allows for a more distributed and pipelined handling of the division process. The `rst_n` signal will reset both counters and the divided clock signal (`clk_div`). The divided clock output will toggle when the high counter wraps around to zero, indicating a full cycle of the input clock division. This architecture simplifies the logic and makes it more scalable for larger division factors.
