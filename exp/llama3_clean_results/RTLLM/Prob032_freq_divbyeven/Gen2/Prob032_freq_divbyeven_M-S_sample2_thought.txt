To simplify the freq_diveven module, we can eliminate the FSM and directly use a counter to achieve the frequency division. The counter will increment on each positive edge of the input clock until it reaches half of the division factor minus one. Then, it resets to zero and toggles the divided clock output signal. This approach reduces the complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
