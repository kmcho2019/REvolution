The simplified architectural idea for the frequency divider module (`freq_diveven`) is to directly utilize a counter to track the number of clock cycles and toggle the divided clock output (`clk_div`) when the counter reaches the specified limit. This approach eliminates the need for a separate toggle signal, simplifying the logic and reducing the number of registers required. The counter will count up to half of `NUM_DIV - 1` and then reset, effectively dividing the input clock frequency by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`.
