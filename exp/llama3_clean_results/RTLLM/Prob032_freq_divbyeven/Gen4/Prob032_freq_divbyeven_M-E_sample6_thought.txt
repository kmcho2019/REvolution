The novel approach to implementing the frequency divider involves utilizing a two-stage counter mechanism. The first stage (`cnt_half`) counts up to half of `NUM_DIV - 1`, and upon reaching this threshold, it triggers the second stage (`toggle_stage`). The `toggle_stage` is a single-bit register that toggles the `clk_div` output when the `cnt_half` reaches its limit, ensuring the output signal changes at the correct interval. This two-stage mechanism simplifies the logic and potentially reduces the area and power consumption of the design. Furthermore, incorporating a synchronization mechanism for the `rst_n` signal with the `clk` ensures that the reset operation is properly synchronized with the clock domain, preventing potential metastability issues.
