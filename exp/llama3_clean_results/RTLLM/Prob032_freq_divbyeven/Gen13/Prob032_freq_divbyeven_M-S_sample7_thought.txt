To simplify the frequency divider module, we can eliminate the unnecessary complexity introduced by the two-stage counter architecture and the gradual reset mechanism. Instead, we can use a single counter to track the number of clock cycles and toggle the divided clock output when the counter reaches the specified limit. The counter limit will be set to half of the division factor minus one, which will effectively divide the frequency of the input clock by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`. This simplified approach will reduce the overall complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
