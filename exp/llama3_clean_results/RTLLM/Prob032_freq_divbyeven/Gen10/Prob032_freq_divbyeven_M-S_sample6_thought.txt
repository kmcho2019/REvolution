To simplify the frequency divider module, we focus on reducing unnecessary logic while maintaining the core functionality. The primary idea is to utilize a single counter and directly toggle the output when this counter reaches half of the division factor minus one, specified by the `NUM_DIV` parameter. Since the division factor must be an even number, we can simplify the logic by removing unnecessary comparisons and directly resetting the counter and toggling the output when the specified limit is reached. This approach reduces complexity and makes the module easier to understand and maintain, while still adhering to the requirement of dividing the input clock frequency by an even number.
