To implement a frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by even numbers, a novel approach is to utilize a combination of a counter and a toggle flip-flop. The counter is used to track the number of clock cycles, and the toggle flip-flop is used to generate the divided clock output signal. The key idea is to use a parameterizable counter that can be easily adjusted to achieve the desired division factor. Additionally, the module will include a reset mechanism to initialize the counter and the divided clock output signal. The division factor will be specified by the `NUM_DIV` parameter, which must be an even number. The module will validate this constraint at compile-time to ensure correctness.
