The novel approach to designing the frequency divider module (`freq_diveven`) involves using a dual-counter mechanism. The first counter (`cnt_lo`) counts the number of clock cycles until it reaches half of the specified division factor (`NUM_DIV`), at which point the second counter (`cnt_hi`) starts counting. Once `cnt_hi` reaches the midpoint, it resets to zero, and the divided clock output (`clk_div`) is toggled. This approach ensures that the frequency of the input clock is divided by the even number specified by `NUM_DIV`. The use of two counters allows for a more straightforward and efficient implementation of the frequency division logic. Additionally, the module includes input validation to ensure that `NUM_DIV` is an even number greater than 1.
