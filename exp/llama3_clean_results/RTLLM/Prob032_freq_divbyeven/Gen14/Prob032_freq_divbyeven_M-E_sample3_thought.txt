To design a novel frequency divider that divides the input clock frequency by even numbers, we can leverage a modular and scalable architecture based on a ring counter. The concept involves using a ring counter with a length equal to the desired division factor (`NUM_DIV`), where each stage of the ring counter represents a state in the division process. 

The ring counter is initialized with a single '1' and the rest as '0's. On each positive edge of the input clock (`clk`), the ring counter shifts its state, effectively circulating the '1' through the counter. When the '1' reaches the middle of the ring counter (for even `NUM_DIV`), it toggles the `clk_div` output signal, achieving the desired frequency division.

To optimize the design, we can incorporate a control unit that monitors the ring counter's state and adjusts the division factor based on the `NUM_DIV` parameter. Additionally, we can utilize a pipeline architecture to improve the timing performance of the module.

This novel architectural idea enables flexibility, scalability, and high-performance operation, making it suitable for a wide range of applications requiring frequency division by even numbers.
