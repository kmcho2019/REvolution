To simplify the existing frequency divider module (`freq_diveven`), we focus on the essential logic required for dividing the input clock frequency by an even number (`NUM_DIV`). The core functionality involves a counter (`cnt`) that increments on each positive edge of the input clock (`clk`) until it reaches half of `NUM_DIV`, at which point it resets to zero and toggles the divided clock output (`clk_div`). We eliminate unnecessary checks and variables, ensuring that the `NUM_DIV` parameter is validated as an even number greater than 1. This simplification aims to maintain the module's functionality while reducing complexity and improving synthesis efficiency.
