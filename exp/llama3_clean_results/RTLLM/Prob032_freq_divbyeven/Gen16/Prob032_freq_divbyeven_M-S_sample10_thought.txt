The frequency divider module (`freq_diveven`) can be simplified by using a single counter and improving the validation of the `NUM_DIV` parameter. The counter will track the number of clock cycles, and when it reaches half of `NUM_DIV`, it will toggle the divided clock output (`clk_div`). To reduce complexity, I will eliminate the unnecessary conditional checks within the `always` block and use a more straightforward approach to update the counter and toggle the output. Additionally, I will improve the compile-time validation of `NUM_DIV` to ensure it is an even number greater than 1.
