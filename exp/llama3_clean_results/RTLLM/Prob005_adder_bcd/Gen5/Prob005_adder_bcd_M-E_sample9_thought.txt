This approach simplifies the correction logic and reduces the complexity of the overall design.

The novelty in this approach lies in using a combinational logic circuit to detect the condition for BCD correction and then applying the correction directly to the binary sum, rather than attempting to perform the addition in a way that inherently produces a BCD result. This method allows for a straightforward implementation that is easy to understand and analyze for power, performance, and area (PPA) optimizations.
