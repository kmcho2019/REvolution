The existing 4-bit BCD adder design can be refactored to improve its structure and potentially its performance, power, and area (PPA) metrics. One approach to refactor the design is to use a more combinational logic-oriented structure, minimizing the use of temporary wires and focusing on direct assignments to output signals. This can involve rethinking the way the correction logic is implemented, potentially using a single step to determine both the corrected sum and the carry-out, rather than separating these operations. Additionally, simplifying the conditional logic for applying corrections and generating carry-outs can lead to a more streamlined and efficient design. By focusing on reducing the number of intermediate signals and optimizing the logic for direct computation of outputs, the refactored design aims to achieve the same functionality with improved PPA metrics.
