1. **Binary Adder:** A 4-bit binary adder that sums the two BCD inputs (`A` and `B`) and the carry-in (`Cin`).
2. **BCD Correction LUT:** A lookup table that maps the binary sum to its corrected BCD value, ensuring the result is within the valid BCD range (0-9).
