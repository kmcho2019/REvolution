The novel approach involves designing a 4-bit BCD adder using a combination of binary addition and a correction module. 
The main idea is to first perform a binary addition of the two 4-bit BCD inputs and the carry-in input. 
Then, instead of directly checking if the sum exceeds 9 and applying the correction, we use a separate correction module that takes the binary sum and produces the corrected BCD sum and carry-out. 
This correction module uses a lookup table (LUT) to map the binary sums to their corresponding corrected BCD values, ensuring that the output is always a valid BCD digit (0-9). 
Additionally, the correction module generates a carry-out signal based on whether the binary sum exceeds 9 or if there's a carry from the binary addition. 
This approach simplifies the logic by separating the binary addition and BCD correction into distinct modules, making it easier to optimize and verify the design.
