To develop a novel architectural idea for a 4-bit BCD adder, we will leverage the concept of binary to BCD conversion and utilize a look-up table (LUT) to directly map the binary sum of A, B, and Cin to the correct BCD output. This approach minimizes the need for complex arithmetic operations and conditional statements, potentially leading to improved performance and reduced power consumption.

The new architecture will consist of two primary components: a binary adder to compute the sum of A, B, and Cin, and a LUT to map this binary sum to the corresponding BCD output. The LUT will be designed to account for the BCD correction and carry generation, ensuring that the output is always a valid BCD digit.

To further optimize the design, we will use a single LUT to compute both the Sum and Cout outputs, reducing the overall complexity and potential area usage. This approach takes advantage of Verilog's ability to efficiently implement LUTs using case statements or arrays, allowing for a concise and readable implementation.
