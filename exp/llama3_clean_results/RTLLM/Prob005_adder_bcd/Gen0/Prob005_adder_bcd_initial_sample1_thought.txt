To implement the 4-bit BCD adder, we first perform a binary addition of the two input BCD numbers (A and B) along with the carry-in (Cin). This step is crucial as it lays the foundation for the subsequent steps. 
After obtaining the binary sum, we check if the sum exceeds 9, which is the maximum value a BCD digit can represent. 
If the sum is greater than 9, we apply a correction by adding 6 (0110 in binary) to the sum. This correction ensures that the final result is within the valid BCD range (0 to 9). 
Additionally, we generate a carry-out (Cout) whenever the sum exceeds 9, signaling that the BCD sum cannot be represented by a single decimal digit and requires further processing or propagation of the carry to the next higher digit position.
