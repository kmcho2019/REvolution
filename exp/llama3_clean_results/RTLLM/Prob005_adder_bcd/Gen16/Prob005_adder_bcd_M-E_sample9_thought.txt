To develop a novel architectural solution for the 4-bit BCD adder, we can leverage the concept of look-up tables (LUTs) to pre-compute and store the results of all possible BCD additions, including the carry-out. This approach allows for a straightforward and potentially fast implementation, as it eliminates the need for complex arithmetic operations and conditional statements within the adder itself. The idea involves creating a ROM (Read-Only Memory) or a set of LUTs that store the pre-computed sums and carry-outs for all combinations of inputs (A, B, and Cin). During operation, the adder simply looks up the result based on the current input values. This method can be particularly efficient in FPGAs, which often have embedded memory blocks that can be used as ROMs or LUTs.
