To create a novel 4-bit BCD adder architecture, let's explore a design that leverages a combination of arithmetic and lookup table (LUT) based approaches. The idea is to perform the binary addition of the inputs and the carry-in, and then use a LUT to determine the corrected BCD sum and the carry-out. This approach can potentially offer advantages in terms of power, performance, and area, as it simplifies the logic required for BCD correction and carry generation. By utilizing LUTs, which are typically optimized for performance and power in FPGAs, we can reduce the complexity of the arithmetic operations and the associated switching activity. Furthermore, this design can be easily pipelined or parallelized to improve performance in high-speed applications.
