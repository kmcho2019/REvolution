To design a novel 4-bit BCD adder architecture, we'll explore a hybrid approach combining both arithmetic and lookup table (LUT) based methods. The idea revolves around using a small LUT to store the results of BCD additions for all possible input combinations, thus avoiding the need for complex arithmetic and correction logic. This LUT will store both the corrected sum and the carry-out for each possible combination of A, B, and Cin. By indexing into this LUT with the inputs A, B, and Cin, we can directly obtain the Sum and Cout without needing to perform binary addition and subsequent correction. This method potentially offers advantages in terms of area, power, and performance, as it simplifies the logic and reduces the number of operations required.
