A novel approach to implementing a 4-bit BCD adder is to utilize a hybrid architecture that combines the efficiency of binary arithmetic with the accuracy of BCD correction. This approach involves first performing a binary addition of the inputs A, B, and Cin to obtain a preliminary sum. Then, instead of directly applying a correction if the sum exceeds 9, the design checks the decimal value of the sum and applies the necessary correction based on the BCD representation. This is achieved by using a combination of logic gates and arithmetic operations to determine the correct BCD output and generate the carry-out signal. The key innovation here is to break down the BCD correction into smaller, more manageable parts, reducing the complexity of the logic required for the correction and making the overall design more efficient and scalable.
