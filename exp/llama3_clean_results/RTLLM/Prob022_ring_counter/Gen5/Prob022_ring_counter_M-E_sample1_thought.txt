To implement the 8-bit ring counter with a novel architectural idea, we can leverage a combination of a counter and a decoder. The counter will keep track of the current state (0 to 7), and the decoder will set the corresponding bit high in the output based on the counter value. This approach separates the state tracking from the output generation, making the design more modular and easier to understand. The decoder will set the corresponding output bit high based on the counter value, effectively creating the cyclic shift pattern without directly manipulating the output bits.
