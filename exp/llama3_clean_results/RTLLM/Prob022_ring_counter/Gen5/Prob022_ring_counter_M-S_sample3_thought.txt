To simplify the ring counter implementation, we can directly manipulate the `out` register without needing a temporary variable or an additional register like `counter`. The `out` register itself can be shifted to achieve the cyclic pattern. On each clock pulse, we shift the bits of `out` one position to the right, and to maintain the cyclic pattern, we set the most significant bit (MSB) of `out` to the value of the least significant bit (LSB) before the shift. This approach ensures that when the 1 reaches the MSB and is about to shift out, it wraps around to the LSB, creating the desired cyclic sequence. 
When the reset signal is high, we simply initialize `out` to have its LSB set to 1 and all other bits to 0.
