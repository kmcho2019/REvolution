A simplified approach to designing an 8-bit ring counter involves using a shift register and taking advantage of Verilog's ability to handle bit-level operations directly. The key idea is to use the syntax correctly to achieve the cyclic shift of bits in the `out` register. By properly concatenating the bits, we can shift the register to the right and wrap the MSB around to the LSB, implementing the desired cyclic behavior without the need for complex logic. The reset logic remains straightforward, setting the shift register to its initial state when the reset signal is high.
