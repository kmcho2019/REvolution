This approach not only adheres to the supported syntax and semantics of most tools but also is efficient in terms of PPA.

Additionally, we will consider simplifying the logic for resetting the counter and generating the output to minimize area consumption and improve performance. The use of a counter with the appropriate size (3 bits for an 8-state ring counter) is already efficient, but we will ensure that the logic for wrapping around to the initial state when the counter reaches its maximum value is straightforward and does not introduce additional complexity.
