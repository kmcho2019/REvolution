The pipelined architecture is achieved by inserting registers between each row of the multiplier array. This allows the intermediate results to be stored and propagated through the pipeline, reducing the critical path and improving the overall performance.

The key innovation of this approach is the use of a compact multiplier array that minimizes the number of multiplexers and reduce the complexity of the design.
