The new architectural idea is to design a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier using a combination of shift registers and adders. The design will consist of multiple stages, each responsible for shifting and adding the partial products. This approach allows for a high degree of parallelism and pipelining, resulting in improved performance and area efficiency.

The design will have the following stages:

1.  **Input Stage**: This stage will receive the multiplicand and multiplier inputs and extend them with zeros to create a 8-bit input.
2.  **Shift and Add Stage**: This stage will consist of four identical sub-stages, each responsible for shifting the multiplicand by a certain number of positions and adding it to the partial product if the corresponding bit in the multiplier is 1.
3.  **Pipelining Stage**: This stage will consist of two registers to store the intermediate results.
4.  **Output Stage**: This stage will output the final product.

By using a combination of shift registers and adders, the design can efficiently handle the multiplication operation while minimizing the number of logic gates and improving performance.
