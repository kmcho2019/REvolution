The input multiplicand and multiplier will be fed into the array, and the partial products will be generated and accumulated as the data flows through the array. The final product will be output at the end of the array.

To implement this architecture, we will use a combination of `always` blocks and `assign` statements to describe the behavior of the PEs and the connections between them. We will also use a `generate` block to instantiate the PEs and create the systolic array.
