To implement a 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, I propose a novel architectural idea that utilizes a hierarchical approach to perform the multiplication operation. The design consists of two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process.

The first level of registers will store the multiplicand and multiplier, while the second level of registers will store the intermediate results of the partial products. The multiplication operation will be performed using a shift-and-add approach, where the multiplicand is shifted left by one position for each bit in the multiplier.

The design will use a generate block to create the partial products, and the addition of partial products will be performed using a hierarchical adder tree. This approach will reduce the latency and improve the throughput of the design.

The control logic will be implemented using a finite state machine (FSM) that will manage the flow of data between the registers and the adder tree. The FSM will ensure that the correct partial products are added together to produce the final product.

The novel aspect of this design is the use of a hierarchical adder tree to add the partial products, which reduces the latency and improves the throughput of the design. Additionally, the use of a FSM to control the flow of data between the registers and the adder tree ensures that the correct partial products are added together to produce the final product.
