To implement the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, I propose a novel architectural idea that leverages a systolic array of processing elements (PEs) to perform the multiplication. Each PE will be responsible for calculating a partial product based on the corresponding bit of the multiplier. The partial products will be accumulated in a pipeline fashion, allowing for efficient and scalable multiplication.

The key components of this architecture will include:

1. Input Registers: To store the multiplicand and multiplier.
2. Processing Elements (PEs): A systolic array of PEs, each responsible for calculating a partial product based on the corresponding bit of the multiplier.
3. Pipeline Registers: To store the intermediate results and propagate them through the pipeline.
4. Output Register: To store the final product.

The operation of the pipeline multiplier will be as follows:

1. The multiplicand and multiplier will be loaded into the input registers.
2. The PEs will calculate the partial products based on the corresponding bits of the multiplier.
3. The partial products will be accumulated in the pipeline registers.
4. The final product will be stored in the output register.

This architecture will provide a scalable and efficient solution for the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier.
