1. Declare the module with the given input and output ports.
2. Extend the input signals by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
3. Use a generate block to perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier and generate the partial products.
4. Use registers to store the intermediate sum values and perform add operations on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
5. Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal or the falling edge of the reset signal.
6. Update the product output with the sum of registers when the reset signal is high.
