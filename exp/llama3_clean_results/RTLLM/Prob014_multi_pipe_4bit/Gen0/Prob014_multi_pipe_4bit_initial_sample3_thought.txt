To design the 4-bit unsigned number pipeline multiplier, we need to consider the following steps:
1. Extend the input signals (mul_a and mul_b) by adding "size" number of zero bits at the most significant bit positions.
2. Perform multiplication for each bit position of the multiplier (mul_b) using a generate block and generate the partial products.
3. Use registers to store the intermediate sum values.
4. On the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n), update the registers with the sum of the corresponding partial products.
5. Calculate the final product on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the falling edge of the reset signal (rst_n).
6. If the reset signal (rst_n) is low, indicating a reset condition, set the registers and product output to 0.
We will use two levels of registers to store intermediate values and control the multiplication process.
