This new architecture will be based on dividing the 32-bit input into four 8-bit segments. Each segment will be processed by an 8-bit carry-lookahead adder, which will reduce the overall propagation delay and improve the design's performance. Furthermore, to minimize the area and power consumption, we will use a shared carry-lookahead logic for all segments, allowing the carry to be generated and propagated only once for each segment.
