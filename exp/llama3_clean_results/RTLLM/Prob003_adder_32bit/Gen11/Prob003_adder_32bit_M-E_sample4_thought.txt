A novel approach to designing a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder involves utilizing a combination of 4-bit and 8-bit CLA blocks. This hierarchical structure allows for further optimization of the PPA metrics by reducing the overall complexity and propagation delay. The idea is to first design efficient 4-bit and 8-bit CLA blocks, and then use these blocks to construct the 32-bit adder. This modular design enables easier optimization and scalability. Additionally, by using a mixture of block sizes, we can potentially reduce the number of gates required and minimize the critical path, leading to improvements in power consumption and area utilization.
