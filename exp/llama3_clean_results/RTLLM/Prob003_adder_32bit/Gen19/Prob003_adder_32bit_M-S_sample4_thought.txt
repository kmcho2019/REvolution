To simplify the provided 32-bit Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) Verilog code, we focus on reducing complexity while maintaining functionality. The main idea is to:

1.  **Remove Redundancy:** Eliminate redundant code and computations.
2.  **Parametrization:** Use parameters to make the design flexible and reusable.
3.  **Modular Design:** Keep the hierarchical structure but simplify the modules.
4.  **Direct Computation:** Compute the generate and propagate signals directly.

We aim to simplify the CLA module and the hierarchical adder_32bit module by reducing the number of wires, eliminating redundant computations, and maintaining a modular structure.
