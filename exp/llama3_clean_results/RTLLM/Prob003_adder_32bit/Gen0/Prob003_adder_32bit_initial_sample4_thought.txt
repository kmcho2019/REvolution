To implement a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder, we will first design a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder block, then use it to build a 16-bit carry-lookahead adder, and finally use two instances of the 16-bit adder to create the 32-bit adder. The 4-bit CLA block will compute the sum and carry-out based on the input bits and generate propagate and generate signals for the carry-lookahead logic. The 16-bit CLA will use four instances of the 4-bit CLA, and the 32-bit adder will use two instances of the 16-bit CLA.
