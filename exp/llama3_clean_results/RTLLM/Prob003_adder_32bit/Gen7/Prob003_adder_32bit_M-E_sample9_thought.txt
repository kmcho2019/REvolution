A novel approach to designing a 32-bit carry-lookahead adder involves utilizing a hybrid architecture that combines the benefits of both carry-lookahead and ripple-carry adders. This hybrid design, termed the "hierarchical carry-lookahead adder," divides the 32-bit addition into four 8-bit blocks, each of which uses a carry-lookahead mechanism. The outputs from these blocks are then combined using a higher-level carry-lookahead logic to generate the final 32-bit sum and carry-out. This approach minimizes the number of levels of logic required for the carry-lookahead, reducing the overall delay and potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics of the design. Additionally, this hierarchical structure allows for easier scalability and modification for different bit-width requirements.
