To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combination of shifting and adding operations. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted left by one bit after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each set bit, we will add the shifted multiplicand to the product. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in a 16-bit product stored in the output port.
