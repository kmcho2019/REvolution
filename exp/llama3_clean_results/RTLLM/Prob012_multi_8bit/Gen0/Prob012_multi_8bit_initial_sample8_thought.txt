The design involves creating an 8-bit multiplier module named 'multi_8bit' that takes two 8-bit inputs (A and B) and produces a 16-bit output (product) representing their product. The module will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication, where each bit of the multiplier (B) is checked, and if set, the multiplicand (A) is added to the product at the corresponding shifted position. The multiplicand (A) is logically shifted left by one bit after each addition to simulate the traditional multiplication process.
