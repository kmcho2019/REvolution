To design the 8-bit multiplier module, we will utilize the shift-and-add method for multiplication. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit set (1), adding the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. We will use a loop to iterate through the bits of the multiplier, shifting the multiplicand left by one bit after each addition to prepare for the next operation. The final product will be stored in a 16-bit output port to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers.
