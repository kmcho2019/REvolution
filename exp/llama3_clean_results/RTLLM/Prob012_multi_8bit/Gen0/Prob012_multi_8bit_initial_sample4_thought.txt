To implement the 8-bit multiplier module named "multi_8bit", we will follow the shift-and-add method for multiplication. The module will take two 8-bit inputs, A and B, and produce a 16-bit output representing the product of A and B. We will iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B), and for each bit that is set (1), we will add the multiplicand (A) to the product at the corresponding shifted position. After each addition, we will logically shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next addition. This process will continue until all bits of the multiplier have been processed, resulting in the final product stored in the 16-bit output port.
