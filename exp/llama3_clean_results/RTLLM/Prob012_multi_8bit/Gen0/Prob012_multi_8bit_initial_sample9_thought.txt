To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we will use a combinational logic approach with the shift-and-add method. The multiplicand (A) will be shifted and added to a running total (product) based on the bits of the multiplier (B). We'll use a generate loop to iterate through each bit of the multiplier. For each '1' bit in the multiplier, we'll add the shifted multiplicand to the product. We will utilize the signed arithmetic operators and shift operators provided by Verilog to achieve this. The product will be accumulated in a 16-bit register to accommodate the maximum possible product of two 8-bit numbers.
