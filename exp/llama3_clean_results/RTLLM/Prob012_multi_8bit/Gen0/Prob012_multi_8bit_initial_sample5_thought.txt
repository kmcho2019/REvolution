To implement the 8-bit multiplier module, we'll follow the shift-and-add method. This involves iterating through each bit of the multiplier (B). If a bit is set (1), we add the multiplicand (A) shifted by the bit position to the product. We continue this process for all bits of the multiplier, shifting the multiplicand left by one bit after each operation to simulate the traditional multiplication process. The result is accumulated in the product variable, which is 16 bits wide to handle the maximum product of two 8-bit numbers.
