1. **Initialization:** Initialize the product to zero before the multiplication process starts.
2. **Iteration:** Iterate through each bit of the multiplier (B). For each bit set to 1, add the multiplicand (A) shifted left by the current bit position to the product.
3. **Shifting and Adding:** After each addition, shift the multiplicand left by one bit to prepare for the next iteration, simulating the multiplication process.
4. **Output:** After all bits of the multiplier have been processed, the final product is stored in the 16-bit output port.
