1. **Partial Product Generation**: For each bit in the multiplier, generate a partial product by shifting the multiplicand to the left by the position of the current bit in the multiplier. This simulates the process of multiplying the multiplicand by each bit of the multiplier.

2. **Conditional Addition**: If the current bit in the multiplier is 1, add the corresponding partial product to the running total. This step effectively accumulates the contributions of each partial product to the final product.

3. **Accumulation and Shifting**: After adding each partial product, shift the multiplicand one bit to the left to prepare for the next partial product generation. This shifting process aligns the multiplicand with the next bit position in the multiplier.

4. **Final Product**: After processing all bits of the multiplier, the accumulated sum represents the final product of the multiplication operation.

This novel approach allows for a structured and systematic method of performing multiplication, leveraging the properties of binary numbers and the efficiency of shift registers and adders.
