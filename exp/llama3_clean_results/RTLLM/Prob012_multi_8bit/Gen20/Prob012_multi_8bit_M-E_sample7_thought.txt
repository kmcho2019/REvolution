This method will utilize a series of multiplexers and adders to achieve the multiplication result. The key idea is to conditionally add the multiplicand (A) to the partial product based on the bits of the multiplier (B), effectively implementing the shift-and-add algorithm without using loops or the built-in multiplication operator.

Our strategy involves:

1. **Bit-Level Processing:** Process each bit of the multiplier (B) individually, starting from the least significant bit (LSB) to the most significant bit (MSB).

2. **Conditional Addition:** For each bit of B, if the bit is '1', add the multiplicand (A) shifted by the corresponding position to the partial product. This is achieved using multiplexers to conditionally select between adding A (shifted appropriately) or zero to the partial product.

3. **Partial Product Accumulation:** Use a series of adders to accumulate the partial products. Each adder's output becomes the input to the next stage, effectively adding the multiplicand (appropriately shifted) to the accumulating partial product when the corresponding bit in the multiplier is '1'.

4. **Final Product:** The final output of the last adder stage represents the product of A and B.

This approach allows for a more manual and detailed implementation of the shift-and-add multiplication algorithm, providing insight into the underlying process and potentially offering optimization opportunities for specific use cases.
