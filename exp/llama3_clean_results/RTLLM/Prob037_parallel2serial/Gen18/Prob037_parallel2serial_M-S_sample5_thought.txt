To simplify the solution, I will focus on minimizing the complexity of the counter logic and the assignment of the valid signal and serial output. The main idea is to use a single variable to keep track of the current bit position and directly derive the valid signal and serial output from this variable. This approach eliminates the need for explicit conditional statements and complex arithmetic operations.

The design will be optimized by reducing the number of internal registers and simplifying the combinational logic. This will not only decrease the area usage but also reduce the power consumption by minimizing the switching activity.

The new code will also consider best practices for coding style, naming conventions, and comments to improve readability and maintainability.
