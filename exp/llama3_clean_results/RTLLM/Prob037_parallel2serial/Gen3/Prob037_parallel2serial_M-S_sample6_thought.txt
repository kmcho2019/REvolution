To simplify the existing solution, we focus on maintaining the essential components for parallel-to-serial conversion while streamlining the logic. The key components include a counter to track the bit position and a data register to hold the parallel input. By directly updating the data register based on the counter value and simplifying the valid signal generation, we can reduce the complexity of the combinational logic. Additionally, ensuring a straightforward reset logic and proper initialization of all registers will contribute to simplifying the design. We aim to minimize unnecessary operations and conditional statements to achieve a more efficient implementation.
