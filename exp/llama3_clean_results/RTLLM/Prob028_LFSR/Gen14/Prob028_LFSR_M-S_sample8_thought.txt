To simplify the Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) design, we can maintain the core functionality while focusing on clarity and minimalism. The existing design already captures the essential logic of an LFSR, including the feedback mechanism and the reset condition. However, we can refine the code for better readability and efficiency.
