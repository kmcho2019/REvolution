To simplify the existing Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) solution, we can focus on minimizing the combinational logic and reducing the use of intermediate signals. The key to simplification lies in directly updating the register's state within the always block, eliminating the need for additional wires and logic. By using the non-blocking assignment operator (`<=`) and simplifying the feedback calculation, we can maintain the required functionality while reducing the overall complexity of the code.
