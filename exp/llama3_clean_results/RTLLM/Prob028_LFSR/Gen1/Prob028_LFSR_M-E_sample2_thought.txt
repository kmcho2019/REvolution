The new architectural idea is to use a combination of shift registers and XOR gates to implement the Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR). Instead of using a single 4-bit register and shifting its bits, we can use four 1-bit registers (D flip-flops) connected in a chain. Each D flip-flop will have its input connected to the output of the previous flip-flop, and the last flip-flop will have its input connected to the XOR of the outputs of the first and second flip-flops, inverted to produce the feedback signal. This architecture allows for a more modular and scalable implementation of the LFSR. The use of individual D flip-flops also makes it easier to visualize and understand the shifting operation.
