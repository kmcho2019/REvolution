To design a new Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) module, we can adopt a novel architectural approach that uses a ring structure to efficiently manage the feedback mechanism and shifting operation. This ring structure can be implemented using a circular buffer, allowing for easier management of the shift operation and feedback calculation. The new approach will involve a parameterized design to make the LFSR more versatile and reusable across different applications. We will focus on a generic LFSR module that can be easily customized for various bit widths and feedback polynomials. This approach will enhance the module's flexibility and scalability.
