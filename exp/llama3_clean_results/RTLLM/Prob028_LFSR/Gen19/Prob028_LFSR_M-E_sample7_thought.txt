To create a novel Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) solution, I propose a modular and scalable architecture that allows for easy configuration of the LFSR's size and feedback polynomial. This approach involves breaking down the LFSR into smaller, manageable components, each responsible for a specific aspect of the operation, such as bit shifting, feedback calculation, and reset handling. The core idea is to use a combination of sequential logic for shifting and combinatorial logic for feedback calculation, ensuring that the design is both efficient and flexible.

The key components of this new architecture include:
1. **Bit Shifting Module:** Responsible for shifting the bits of the LFSR one position to the left, making room for the new feedback value.
2. **Feedback Calculation Module:** Calculates the feedback value based on the XOR of specific bits in the LFSR, according to a predefined polynomial.
3. **Reset and Control Module:** Handles the reset operation, initializing the LFSR to a known state, and controls the shifting operation based on the clock and reset signals.

By modularizing the LFSR's functionality, this design can be easily adapted for different sizes of LFSRs and various feedback polynomials, making it highly versatile and reusable.
