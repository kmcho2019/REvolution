To design the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a combination of sequential logic to shift the bits and combinational logic to generate the feedback signal. The feedback signal will be calculated by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. The LFSR will be reset to zero when the active high reset signal is asserted. Otherwise, on each rising edge of the clock, the bits will be shifted left and the new feedback value will be inserted at the least significant bit (LSB).
