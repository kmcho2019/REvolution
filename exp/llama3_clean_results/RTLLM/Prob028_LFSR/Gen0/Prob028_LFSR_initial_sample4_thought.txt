To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will use a combination of sequential logic (for the shift register) and combinational logic (for the feedback calculation). The process involves calculating the feedback by XORing the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, then inverting the result. On each clock cycle, the register shifts left and the new feedback value is inserted at the least significant bit (LSB). We also include an asynchronous reset to initialize the register to zero when the reset signal is high.
