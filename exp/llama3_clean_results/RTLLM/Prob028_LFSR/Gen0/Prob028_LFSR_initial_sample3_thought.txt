This signal will be updated on the rising edge of the clock signal based on the reset condition and the feedback mechanism. The feedback signal is calculated by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit of the current state and then inverting the result. If the reset signal is high, the internal register is reset to zero. Otherwise, the register is updated by shifting its bits to the left and inserting the feedback value at the least significant bit position. The output of the module is the current state of the internal register.
