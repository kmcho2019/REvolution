To implement the 4-bit Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), I will use a register to store the current state of the LFSR. The feedback mechanism will be implemented using the XOR operation on the most significant bit (MSB) and the second most significant bit, followed by an inversion. The LFSR will be synchronized with the clock signal, and the reset signal will be used to initialize the register. On each rising edge of the clock, the register will be updated by shifting the bits left and inserting the new feedback value at the least significant bit position.
