To design the LFSR module, we will first define the module with its input and output ports. We will then declare a 4-bit reg variable to hold the current state of the LFSR. In the always block, we will handle the reset condition to initialize the register to zero when the reset signal is high. If the reset signal is low, we will shift the bits in the register to the left and calculate the feedback value by XORing the most significant bit and the second most significant bit, inverting the result, and inserting it at the least significant bit. 
We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to assign the new value to the register on the rising edge of the clock. The output port will be continuously assigned the current state of the register.
