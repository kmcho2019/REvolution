To simplify the Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) design, we can eliminate the modular structure and directly implement the shift register and feedback mechanism within a single module. The feedback will be calculated as the XOR of the most significant bit (out[3]) and the second most significant bit (out[2]), and then inverted. The LFSR will operate by shifting its bits left on the rising edge of the clock, inserting the new feedback value at the least significant bit (LSB), and resetting to zero when the reset signal is high. This approach reduces the complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
