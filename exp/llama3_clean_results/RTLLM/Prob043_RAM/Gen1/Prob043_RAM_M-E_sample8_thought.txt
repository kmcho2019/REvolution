The novel approach involves implementing a dual-port RAM using a combination of synchronous and asynchronous operations. The write operation will be performed synchronously with the clock signal, while the read operation will be performed asynchronously. This approach will reduce the power consumption associated with the clock signal's propagation during read operations. Additionally, the RAM array will be divided into two smaller arrays, one for each port, to reduce the area usage and improve the overall performance. The design will also incorporate a simple arbitration mechanism to handle simultaneous read and write operations.
