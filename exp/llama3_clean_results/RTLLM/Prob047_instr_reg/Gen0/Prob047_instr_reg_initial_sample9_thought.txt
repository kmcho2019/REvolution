To implement the instruction register module, we will use two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions from different sources. We'll use the fetch signal to determine which register to update on the rising edge of the clock. If the reset signal is low, both registers will be initialized to zero. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions by bit-slicing and concatenation. The ins output will be the high 3 bits of ins_p1, ad1 will be the low 5 bits of ins_p1, and ad2 will be the full 8-bit data from ins_p2.
