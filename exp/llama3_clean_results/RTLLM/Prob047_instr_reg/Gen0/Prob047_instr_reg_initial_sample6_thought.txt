The instruction register module (instr_reg) is designed to capture and process CPU instructions from various sources. It will have two 8-bit registers (ins_p1 and ins_p2) to store instructions. The module will synchronize with the clock signal (clk) and reset when the active low reset signal (rst) is asserted. The fetch signal will determine which register to update with the incoming instruction from the data input. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with the high 3 bits representing the opcode (ins), the low 5 bits representing the register address (ad1) for the first instruction, and the full 8-bit data (ad2) from the second source.
