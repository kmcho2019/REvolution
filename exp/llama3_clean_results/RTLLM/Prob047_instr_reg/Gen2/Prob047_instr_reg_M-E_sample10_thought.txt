To implement the instruction register module with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a single 16-bit register to store instructions from both sources. This approach simplifies the design and reduces the area consumption. We will use a clock signal (clk) and a reset signal (rst) to control the register. Based on the fetch signal, we will either load the data input into the lower 8 bits or the upper 8 bits of the 16-bit register. Finally, we will derive the outputs (ins, ad1, and ad2) from the stored instructions. This design takes advantage of the fact that the instructions from different sources are stored in separate parts of the register, allowing for efficient retrieval and processing.
