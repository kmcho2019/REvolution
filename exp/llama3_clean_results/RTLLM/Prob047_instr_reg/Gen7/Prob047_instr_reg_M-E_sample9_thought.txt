A novel approach to designing the instruction register module involves utilizing a Time-Division Multiplexing (TDM) technique. This method allocates specific time slots for each instruction source, allowing for the efficient transmission and storage of instructions from different sources. The TDM-based design incorporates a single 8-bit register and a counter to keep track of the current time slot. The instruction is fetched from the data input and stored in the register during the allocated time slot. The outputs ins, ad1, and ad2 are derived from the stored instruction based on the current time slot. This design also includes a reset mechanism to initialize the register when the reset signal is low. By utilizing TDM, the design aims to improve the instruction fetch efficiency and reduce the complexity of the instruction register module.
