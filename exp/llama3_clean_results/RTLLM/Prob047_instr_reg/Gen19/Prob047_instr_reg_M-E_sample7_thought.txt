To address the instruction register module requirements, we will implement a novel architectural idea that utilizes two separate 8-bit registers to store instructions from different sources. This approach will not only meet the specified functionality but also provide a clear and modular design. 

The design includes two 8-bit registers, `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`, which are updated based on the `fetch` signal. When `fetch` is `2'b01`, the instruction is stored in `ins_p1`, and when `fetch` is `2'b10`, the instruction is stored in `ins_p2`. The outputs `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` are derived from the stored instructions in `ins_p1` and `ins_p2`. 

This design approach ensures that instructions from different sources are handled separately, allowing for more flexibility and easier modification if the instruction format or the number of sources changes in the future.
