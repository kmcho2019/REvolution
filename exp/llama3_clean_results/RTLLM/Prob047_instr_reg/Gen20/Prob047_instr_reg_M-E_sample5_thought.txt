The novel approach to designing the instruction register module involves utilizing a single, dual-port register to store instructions from different sources. This design aims to reduce area usage by minimizing the number of registers while maintaining the required functionality. The dual-port register allows for simultaneous read and write operations, enabling efficient handling of instructions from various sources. Additionally, the design incorporates a control unit that manages the data flow and ensures that the correct instruction is stored and processed based on the fetch signal. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the critical path delay, leading to improved performance and reduced power consumption.
