The FSM will have three states: `IDLE`, `FETCH_INSTR`, and `PROCESS_INSTR`. In the `IDLE` state, the module waits for a valid `fetch` signal. Once a valid `fetch` signal is received, the FSM transitions to the `FETCH_INSTR` state, where it captures the instruction from the input `data`. The captured instruction is then stored in a register.

In the `PROCESS_INSTR` state, the FSM extracts the opcode, register address, and full 8-bit data from the stored instruction. The extracted information is then assigned to the output signals `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2`.

The novel architectural solution will provide improved performance, reduced power consumption, and minimized area usage.
