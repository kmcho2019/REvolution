A novel architectural idea for the instruction register module involves utilizing a multiplexed, dual-register design. This approach employs two separate 8-bit registers (`ins_p1` and `ins_p2`) to store instructions from different sources, along with a multiplexer-based control mechanism to manage the updating of these registers based on the `fetch` signal. The `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` outputs are derived directly from the stored instructions in the respective registers, eliminating the need for additional bitwise operations. This design allows for a more modular and scalable architecture, potentially simplifying future expansions or modifications. Additionally, it enables a more straightforward implementation of clock gating and reset logic, which could contribute to improved power efficiency.
