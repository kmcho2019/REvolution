The new architectural idea is to implement the instruction register module using a single 16-bit register, `ins_reg`, to store the instructions from both sources. This approach reduces the number of registers and simplifies the logic for storing and retrieving instructions. The `fetch` signal is used to determine which part of the `ins_reg` to update with the incoming `data`. The outputs `ins`, `ad1`, and `ad2` are derived from the `ins_reg` based on the instruction format. This design aims to minimize area usage and power consumption while maintaining the required functionality.
