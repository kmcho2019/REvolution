This approach can be achieved by implementing a small FIFO (First-In-First-Out) buffer. The FIFO will allow us to store instructions from different sources and provide a more efficient way to manage the instruction flow. We will also introduce a simple control unit to manage the FIFO operations, such as enqueue, dequeue, and reset. This design will not only improve the functionality of the instruction register module but also provide a more scalable and maintainable architecture.
