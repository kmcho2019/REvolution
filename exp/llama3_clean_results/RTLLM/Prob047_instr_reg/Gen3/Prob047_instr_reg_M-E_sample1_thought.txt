The novel architectural idea for the instruction register module (instr_reg) involves utilizing a single 16-bit register to store both instructions from different sources. This approach reduces the area usage by eliminating the need for two separate 8-bit registers. The fetch signal will determine which half of the 16-bit register to update with the incoming instruction from the data input. The outputs will be derived from the stored instructions, with the high 3 bits representing the opcode (ins), the low 5 bits representing the register address (ad1) for the first instruction, and the full 8-bit data (ad2) from the second source. This design also incorporates a decoding mechanism to extract the necessary fields from the stored instructions, ensuring correct operation and minimizing unnecessary logic.
