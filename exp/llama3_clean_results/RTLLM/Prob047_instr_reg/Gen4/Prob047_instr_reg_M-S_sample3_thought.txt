To simplify the instruction register module, we can eliminate unnecessary complexity while maintaining its functionality. The module's primary purpose is to capture and store instructions from different sources, based on the fetch signal, and derive specific outputs from these stored instructions. 

The clock gating logic, while intended for power reduction, adds unnecessary complexity in this context. Simplifying the sequential logic to directly update the registers based on the fetch signal can reduce area and potentially improve performance by minimizing the critical path delay.

Moreover, the derivation of outputs (ins, ad1, ad2) from the stored instructions can remain straightforward, directly assigning bits from the registers to the outputs without additional combinational logic. This approach ensures that the module remains functionally correct while simplifying its implementation.

By focusing on the essential functionality and removing unnecessary features, the simplified module can achieve improved PPA metrics, especially in terms of area and power consumption, without compromising its performance.
