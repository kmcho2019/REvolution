The existing solution can be simplified by leveraging the fact that the registers ins_p1 and ins_p2 are only updated on the rising edge of the clock when the fetch signal is either 2'b01 or 2'b10, respectively. Instead of using assign statements with conditional expressions for both the reset and the clocked update logic, we can utilize always blocks to handle both the reset and the fetch conditions explicitly. This approach simplifies the logic and potentially reduces the combinational path delay. Additionally, we keep the output logic straightforward, deriving ins, ad1, and ad2 directly from ins_p1 and ins_p2.
