The new design will utilize a hierarchical approach, dividing the 8-bit addition into smaller segments and employing a combination of half adders and full adders.

The proposed architecture, called the "Segmented Carry-Lookahead Adder" (SCLA), will divide the 8-bit input into four 2-bit segments. Each segment will have a dedicated carry-lookahead circuit to generate the carry signal for that segment. The carry signals from each segment will then be combined using a secondary carry-lookahead circuit to produce the final carry-out signal.

This hierarchical approach will reduce the critical path delay by minimizing the number of levels required to propagate the carry signal. Additionally, the use of half adders and full adders in each segment will reduce the overall area consumption.

The SCLA architecture will provide a balanced trade-off between performance, power consumption, and area utilization, making it an attractive solution for a wide range of applications.
