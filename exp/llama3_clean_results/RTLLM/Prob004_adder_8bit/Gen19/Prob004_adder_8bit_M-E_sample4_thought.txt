The idea is to first calculate the sum of the two input numbers without considering the carry, and then use the result to determine the carry. This approach allows us to minimize the number of full adder cells required, reducing the overall area and power consumption.

We will design the adder using a series of bitwise AND, OR, and XOR operations, along with some basic arithmetic logic. The speculative addition technique will be applied in a hierarchical manner, starting from the least significant bits and propagating upwards.
