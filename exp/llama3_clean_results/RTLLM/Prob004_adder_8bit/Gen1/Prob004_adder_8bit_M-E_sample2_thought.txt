To design a novel 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a combination of bitwise operations and arithmetic logic units (ALUs) to perform the addition operation in a more efficient and scalable manner. This approach involves breaking down the 8-bit addition into smaller, more manageable parts, and then combining the results using a hierarchical structure. This hierarchical approach enables the 8-bit adder to be implemented in a more modular and flexible manner, allowing for easier optimization and modification of the design.
