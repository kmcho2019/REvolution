The 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit' will be designed using a series of full adders. Each full adder will take in two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) along with a carry-in bit, producing a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out bit from each full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder, thus propagating the carry across all bits. The full adders will be instantiated for each bit position from 0 to 7, with the carry-in for the first full adder coming from the external 'cin' input and the carry-out from the last full adder becoming the 'cout' output.
