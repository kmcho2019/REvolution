To implement the 8-bit adder module 'adder_8bit', we'll use a series of full adders connected in a chain, where the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next. Each full adder will perform the addition of corresponding bits from the two input operands 'a' and 'b', along with the carry from the previous bit position. We'll start with the least significant bit (LSB) and propagate the carry to the most significant bit (MSB). The final carry-out will be the carry from the MSB full adder.
