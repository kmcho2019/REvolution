To implement an 8-bit adder module, we will use a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder takes two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and the carry-in from the previous bit position, producing a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the bits. This approach ensures that the addition operation is performed correctly, taking into account any carry generated during the addition of lower bits.
