To implement an 8-bit adder, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B) and a carry-in input, producing a sum output and a carry-out output. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This approach allows us to perform the addition operation bit by bit, starting from the least significant bit (LSB) and propagating the carry to the most significant bit (MSB).
