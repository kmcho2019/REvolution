To design an 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of bit-level full adders connected in a ripple-carry fashion. Each full adder will take a pair of input bits from operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out bit. The carry-out from one full adder will serve as the carry-in for the next full adder. This way, we can perform the addition of the two 8-bit numbers bit by bit, propagating the carry through the adders as needed.
