The adder_8bit module can be implemented by utilizing a series of full adders in a ripple carry configuration. Each bit-level full adder takes two input bits (one from operand A and one from operand B), a carry input, and produces a sum output and a carry output. The carry output from each full adder is passed to the next full adder for the next bit position, allowing the carry to ripple through the adder. This design will be implemented using a single module, with a series of full adders instantiated within it.
