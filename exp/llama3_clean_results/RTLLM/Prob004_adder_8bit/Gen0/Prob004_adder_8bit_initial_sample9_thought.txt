To design the 8-bit adder module, we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will take two bits from the input operands A and B, along with a carry-in input, and produce a sum bit and a carry-out. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, allowing the carry to propagate through the adder chain. This approach ensures that the 8-bit adder can correctly handle the addition of two 8-bit numbers with a carry-in.
