To design the 8-bit adder module named 'adder_8bit', we can utilize a series of full adders connected in a ripple-carry configuration. Each full adder will perform the addition of two corresponding bits from the input operands 'a' and 'b', along with the carry-in from the previous bit. The carry-out from each full adder will be propagated to the next full adder to calculate the sum of the current bit and the overall carry-out. The final carry-out will be the carry-out of the most significant bit (MSB) full adder. The sum of each bit will be the output of the corresponding full adder. We will use a hierarchical approach, first designing a full adder module, and then using it within the 'adder_8bit' module to perform the 8-bit addition.
