To create a novel and efficient 8-bit adder, we'll employ a hierarchical approach combining the benefits of both ripple-carry adders and carry-lookahead adders. The design, termed "Hybrid Adder," will divide the 8-bit input into two 4-bit segments. Each 4-bit segment will utilize a ripple-carry adder for its simplicity and area efficiency. To improve performance, we'll implement a carry-lookahead circuit to predict the carry-out of each 4-bit segment. This hybrid approach balances the trade-offs between area, power, and performance.

The architecture will consist of two main components: 
1. Two 4-bit ripple-carry adders for the lower and upper bits of the inputs.
2. A carry-lookahead circuit to generate the carry-out of each 4-bit adder, enabling fast carry propagation between the two segments.

This design aims to reduce the critical path delay by leveraging the fast carry-lookahead mechanism while maintaining area efficiency through the use of ripple-carry adders for the 4-bit segments.
