To simplify the 8-bit adder design while maintaining its functionality, we can utilize a single full adder module and instantiate it eight times to perform the addition operation bit by bit. This approach eliminates the need for complex carry lookahead logic and allows for a more straightforward implementation. Each full adder will take two input bits and a carry-in, producing a sum and a carry-out. The carry-out from each full adder will be used as the carry-in for the next full adder, enabling the ripple-carry addition process. This design simplifies the overall architecture and reduces the complexity of the Verilog code.
