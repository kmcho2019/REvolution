To simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality, we can leverage the inherent properties of bitwise operations and modular design principles. 

The main idea is to directly instantiate a series of full adders to perform the 8-bit addition in a straightforward manner, eliminating the need for the hierarchical approach with separate modules for 2-bit and 4-bit adders. 

Each full adder will compute the sum and carry-out for each bit position, utilizing the carry-in from the previous bit position to calculate the sum and carry-out for the current bit position.

This simplified approach reduces the complexity of the design, minimizes the number of modules, and potentially reduces the latency by avoiding the overhead of module instantiation and signal propagation between modules.
