To implement an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic, I propose a novel architectural approach that utilizes a tree-like structure of 2-bit adders, allowing for a more parallelized and efficient calculation of the sum and carry signals.

The idea is to divide the 8-bit input operands into four 2-bit blocks and perform the addition operation for each block in parallel using 2-bit adders. The carry signals from each 2-bit adder are then propagated to the next level of adders, forming a tree-like structure. This approach reduces the critical path delay and improves the overall performance of the adder.

To further optimize the design, I will utilize a carry-lookahead approach to calculate the carry signals in advance, reducing the dependency on the carry chain and improving the overall performance. The carry-lookahead signals will be calculated using a separate module, which will take the input operands and the previous carry signals as inputs and produce the carry-lookahead signals as outputs.

The proposed architecture will have the same functionality as the original design but with improved code organization, structure, and PPA metrics.
