The goal is to design an 8-bit adder with multiple bit-level adders in combinational logic. To achieve this, we can utilize a novel architectural approach that combines the principles of hierarchical design and bit-parallel addition. 

Our new design idea involves the following steps:

1. Divide the 8-bit input operands into four 2-bit segments.
2. Design a 2-bit adder module that can perform addition on two 2-bit numbers and generate a carry signal.
3. Use four instances of the 2-bit adder module to perform addition on the corresponding segments of the input operands.
4. Implement a carry propagation mechanism to forward the carry signal from one 2-bit adder to the next.
5. Combine the sum bits from each 2-bit adder to form the final 8-bit sum.

The key innovation in this design is the use of a "carry-merge" unit that simplifies the carry propagation mechanism. This unit takes the carry signals from each 2-bit adder and merges them to generate the final carry-out signal.

The advantages of this design include:

* Reduced critical path delay due to the parallel processing of 2-bit segments.
* Improved area efficiency by using a modular 2-bit adder design.
* Simplified carry propagation mechanism using the carry-merge unit.
