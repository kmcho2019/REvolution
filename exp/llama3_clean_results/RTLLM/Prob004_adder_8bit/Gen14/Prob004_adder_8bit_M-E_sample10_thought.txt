To create a novel solution, I will explore an alternative architecture that utilizes a hierarchical approach to construct the 8-bit adder. This design will employ a combination of 2-bit and 4-bit adders to form the final 8-bit adder. Each 2-bit adder will be implemented using two full adders, and two 4-bit adders will be combined to form the 8-bit adder.

The key idea is to break down the 8-bit addition into smaller, more manageable parts, and then combine these parts to achieve the final result. This approach can potentially offer advantages in terms of area and power consumption, as it allows for a more modular and structured design.

The 2-bit adders will be designed using full adders, which will provide the sum and carry outputs for each 2-bit segment. The 4-bit adders will then be constructed by combining two 2-bit adders and propagating the carry signals appropriately.

By using this hierarchical approach, the design can take advantage of the regularity and modularity of the smaller adder blocks, potentially leading to a more efficient and scalable implementation.
