To simplify the solution, we can utilize a single type of adder module, a full adder, and chain them together to create the 8-bit adder. This approach eliminates the need for separate 2-bit and 4-bit adder modules, reducing the overall complexity of the design.

Each full adder will have three inputs: two bits from the operands A and B, and a carry-in input. The outputs of each full adder will be the sum of the two input bits and the carry-in, as well as a carry-out to be used in the next stage.

By chaining these full adders together, we can perform the 8-bit addition operation. This approach is straightforward and efficient, making it suitable for this task.
