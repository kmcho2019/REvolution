The novel architectural idea is to implement a hybrid adder that combines the benefits of ripple-carry adders and carry-lookahead adders. This hybrid approach, called a "segmented carry-lookahead adder," divides the 8-bit input into two segments: a 4-bit segment and a 4-bit segment. Each segment uses a ripple-carry adder, and then a carry-lookahead adder is used to compute the final carry-out. This approach reduces the critical path delay compared to a traditional ripple-carry adder while minimizing the area consumption compared to a full carry-lookahead adder. Additionally, this design can be optimized for power consumption by using low-power logic cells and minimizing switching activity.
