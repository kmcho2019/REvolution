To simplify the 8-bit adder implementation, we will utilize a ripple-carry adder structure, which is the most straightforward and area-efficient approach for implementing multi-bit adders. This design will consist of a series of full adders, where each full adder adds two input bits and a carry bit from the previous addition. The carry output from each full adder is used as the carry input for the next full adder, hence the name "ripple-carry". This approach eliminates the need for complex carry lookahead logic, reducing the overall complexity and area consumption of the design.
