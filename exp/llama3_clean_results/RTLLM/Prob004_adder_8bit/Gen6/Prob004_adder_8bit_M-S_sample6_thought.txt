To simplify the 8-bit adder, we can utilize a straightforward approach by cascading full adders for each bit position. This method involves creating a full adder module that performs the addition of two bits along with a carry-in, and then instantiating this module eight times for an 8-bit operation. The carry-out from one full adder is used as the carry-in for the next full adder. This design is intuitive, easy to implement, and effectively demonstrates the concept of bit-level adders in combinational logic.
