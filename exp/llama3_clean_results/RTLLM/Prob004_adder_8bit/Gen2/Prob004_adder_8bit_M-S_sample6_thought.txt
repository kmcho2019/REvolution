To simplify the 8-bit adder design, we can utilize a ripple-carry adder architecture, where each bit of the sum is generated by a full adder that takes the corresponding bits of the input operands and the carry from the previous bit. This approach eliminates the need for multiple levels of bitwise adders and complex carry propagation logic. By directly chaining the full adders together, we can create a straightforward and efficient 8-bit adder.
