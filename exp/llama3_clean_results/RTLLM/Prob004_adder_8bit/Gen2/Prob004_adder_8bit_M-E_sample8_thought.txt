To implement an 8-bit adder with improved performance, power efficiency, and area utilization, we can utilize a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) architecture. This approach allows for faster calculation of the sum and carry by generating the carry signals in parallel, rather than the sequential manner used in ripple-carry adders. The CLA architecture reduces the critical path and can improve the overall speed of the adder. Additionally, by using a hierarchical approach to generate the carry signals, we can minimize the area and power consumption of the adder.

The proposed architecture will consist of multiple stages, each responsible for generating the carry signals for a subset of the bits. The final stage will use these carry signals to compute the sum. This hierarchical approach enables the adder to operate more efficiently, especially for larger bit widths.

We will use a combination of logical operations and bit manipulation to generate the carry signals and compute the sum. The use of built-in Verilog operators and functions will simplify the implementation and improve the readability of the code.
