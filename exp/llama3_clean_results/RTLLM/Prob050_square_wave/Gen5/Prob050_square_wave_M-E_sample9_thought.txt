The novel architectural idea is to implement the square wave generator using a phase accumulator and a comparator. This approach allows for flexible and efficient generation of square waves with variable frequencies. This design is advantageous as it eliminates the need for explicit counters and reset logic, reducing the complexity and potential area consumption of the module.

The threshold value will be calculated based on the input frequency and the maximum value of the phase accumulator. This approach also allows for easier modification and expansion of the design, such as adding more features or improving the resolution of the frequency control.

To further improve the design, the phase accumulator and comparator can be optimized for area and power efficiency.
