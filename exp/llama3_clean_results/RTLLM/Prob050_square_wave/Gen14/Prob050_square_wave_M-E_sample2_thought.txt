The novel architectural idea for the square_wave module is to utilize a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of both counter-based and phase-locked loop (PLL) designs. This approach involves using a counter to generate a square wave signal at a fixed frequency and then utilizing a PLL to dynamically adjust the phase of the output signal to achieve the desired frequency. The frequency control input (freq) will be used to adjust the PLL's phase, allowing for a wide range of frequency control. This hybrid approach provides a high degree of flexibility and accuracy in generating square wave signals with variable frequencies.
