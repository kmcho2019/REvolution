The new approach focuses on utilizing a digital phase-locked loop (DPLL) to generate the square wave signal. The DPLL will be used to lock onto a reference frequency, which is derived from the input clock signal and the frequency control input (freq). Once locked, the DPLL will generate a square wave signal at the desired frequency. This method provides a more robust and flexible solution, as it can handle a wide range of frequency control values and can also be used to generate other types of waveforms. The DPLL will consist of a phase detector, a loop filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The phase detector will compare the phase of the input clock signal with the phase of the VCO output, and the loop filter will adjust the VCO control signal to minimize the phase error. The VCO will generate a square wave signal at a frequency determined by the VCO control signal.
