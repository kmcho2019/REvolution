The novel approach for the square_wave module is to utilize a Phase-Locked Loop (PLL) to generate the square wave signal. A PLL is a control system that generates a signal that is locked to the frequency of a reference signal. By using a PLL, we can generate a square wave signal that is synchronized with the input clock signal and has a frequency determined by the frequency control input (freq). This approach provides a more robust and reliable way to generate the square wave signal, especially in the presence of clock jitter or other noise sources. The PLL will be designed to have a low jitter and high frequency stability, ensuring a high-quality square wave output.
