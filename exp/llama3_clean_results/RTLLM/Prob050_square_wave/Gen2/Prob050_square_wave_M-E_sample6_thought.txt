To design a more efficient square_wave module, we can utilize a combination of a phase accumulator and a comparator to generate the square wave signal. The phase accumulator will increment at a rate proportional to the input frequency, and the comparator will toggle the output signal when the phase accumulator overflows. This approach eliminates the need for a separate counter register and reduces the critical path delay, leading to improved performance and power efficiency. We can also use a glitch-free toggle logic to minimize power consumption.
