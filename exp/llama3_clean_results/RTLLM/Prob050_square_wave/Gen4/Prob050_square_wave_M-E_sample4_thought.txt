A novel approach to implement the square_wave module could involve using a combination of a phase accumulator and a comparator to generate the square wave signal. The phase accumulator would increment at a rate determined by the input clock frequency and the desired output frequency. When the phase accumulator reaches a certain threshold, the wave_out signal would be toggled. This approach allows for a more flexible and efficient generation of square waves with variable frequencies. Additionally, this design could potentially reduce the area and power consumption compared to the original counter-based design.
