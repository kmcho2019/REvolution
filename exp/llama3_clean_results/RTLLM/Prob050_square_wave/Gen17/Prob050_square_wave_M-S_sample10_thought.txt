To simplify the solution, we can maintain the counter-based approach but aim to reduce the complexity of the conditional logic within the always block. The current implementation uses an if-else statement to check if the counter has reached the target frequency and then either toggles the wave_out signal and resets the counter or increments the counter. This logic can be simplified by removing the else clause and letting the increment operation be the default action when the counter hasn't reached the target frequency. The reset and toggle operation will only occur when the counter equals the frequency, reducing the conditional complexity.
