The new approach involves utilizing a phase-locked loop (PLL) to generate the square wave signal. The PLL will be used to lock onto the input clock signal and then divide it down to the desired frequency. This method allows for a high degree of flexibility and accuracy in generating the output square wave signal. The frequency control input (freq) will be used to control the divider ratio, thereby adjusting the output frequency. This novel architectural idea provides a unique and innovative solution to the problem. The PLL will be implemented using a simple digital phase detector, a loop filter, and a voltage-controlled oscillator (VCO). The VCO will be replaced with a digital clock divider to generate the desired output frequency.
