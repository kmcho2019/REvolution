To implement the square_wave module, we will first declare the module and its input/output ports. We will then use a counter register (count) to keep track of the number of cycles since the last toggle. On every rising edge of the clk, we will increment the counter. Once the counter reaches the specified frequency (freq - 1), we will reset the counter and toggle the wave_out signal. If the counter has not reached freq, it will simply increment by one on the next clock cycle.
