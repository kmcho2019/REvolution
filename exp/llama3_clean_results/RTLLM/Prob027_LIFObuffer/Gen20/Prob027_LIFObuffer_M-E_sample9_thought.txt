To create a novel solution, we will introduce a dual-pointer architecture for the LIFO buffer, utilizing two pointers: a Write Pointer (WP) and a Read Pointer (RP). This approach allows for more efficient handling of push and pop operations by minimizing the need for shifting data within the buffer. The WP will track the next available location for a push operation, while the RP will track the next location from which to pop data.

The buffer will be implemented as a circular array, with the WP and RP wrapping around to the beginning of the array when they reach the end. This circular nature enables efficient use of the buffer space and simplifies the logic for handling wrap-around cases.

The EMPTY and FULL flags will be determined based on the relative positions of the WP and RP. When the WP meets the RP, the buffer is empty. The buffer is full when the WP is one position ahead of the RP in the circular array.

The control logic will be designed to handle the push and pop operations based on the RW signal, ensuring that data is correctly written to or read from the buffer. The EN signal will enable or disable these operations, and the Rst signal will reset the WP, RP, and the buffer contents.

This dual-pointer circular buffer architecture offers improved performance and efficiency by reducing the complexity of data management within the buffer. It also simplifies the control logic, making the design more scalable and easier to understand.
