To implement the LIFO buffer, we will utilize a stack-based approach with a memory array to store the data. The stack pointer (SP) will keep track of the current position in the stack, and flags will be used to indicate whether the buffer is empty or full. The design will be synchronous, relying on the clock signal for all operations. On the rising edge of the clock, the enable signal will determine whether to perform the operation or not. Based on the read/write control signal, the module will either push data onto the stack (if it's not full) or pop data from the stack (if it's not empty). The reset signal will clear the stack and reset the stack pointer to indicate an empty buffer.
