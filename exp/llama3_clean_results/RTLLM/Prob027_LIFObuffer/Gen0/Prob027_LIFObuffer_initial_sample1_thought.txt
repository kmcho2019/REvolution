To implement the LIFObuffer module, we'll use a stack memory array (stack_mem) to store the data. A stack pointer (SP) will track the current position in the stack. The buffer operations will be controlled by the RW signal, and the enable (EN) signal will activate buffer operations. On the rising edge of the clock (Clk), the module will check for the reset (Rst) signal and either reset the buffer or perform the desired operation based on the RW signal and the state of the buffer (EMPTY or FULL). The flags for EMPTY and FULL will be updated based on the stack pointer status.
