To simplify the existing LIFObuffer design, focus on reducing the complexity of the combinational logic and sequential logic while maintaining the same functionality. The key improvements involve:

2.  **Optimizing Sequential Logic:** Restructure the `always` block to minimize the number of operations performed on each clock cycle, improving performance and reducing power consumption.
3.  **Streamlining Flag Updates:** Update the EMPTY and FULL flags in a more straightforward manner, eliminating unnecessary logic.
