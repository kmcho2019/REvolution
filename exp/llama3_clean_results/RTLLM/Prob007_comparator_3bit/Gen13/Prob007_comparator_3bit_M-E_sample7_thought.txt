The comparison logic can be broken down into several steps:
1. Compare the MSBs of A and B. If they are different, the number with the higher MSB is greater.
2. If the MSBs are the same, compare the next significant bits. Repeat this process until a difference is found or all bits are compared.
3. If all bits are the same, the numbers are equal.

This bit-by-bit comparison approach can be efficiently implemented using digital logic gates and does not require complex arithmetic operations, making it suitable for a simple yet effective comparator design.
