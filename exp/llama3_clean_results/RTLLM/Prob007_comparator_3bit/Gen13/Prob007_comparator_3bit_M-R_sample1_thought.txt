To refactor the existing code while maintaining its functionality, we can utilize continuous assignment (`assign`) statements instead of the `always` block. This approach is suitable for combinational logic, as it directly assigns the result of an expression to a wire or reg without the need for a procedural block. Additionally, considering the comparison logic, we can ensure that the outputs are mutually exclusive by using a simple if-else structure within the assign statements, although in this case, the mutual exclusivity is inherently guaranteed by the nature of the comparison operators. The focus will be on simplifying the code structure while preserving its efficiency and readability.
