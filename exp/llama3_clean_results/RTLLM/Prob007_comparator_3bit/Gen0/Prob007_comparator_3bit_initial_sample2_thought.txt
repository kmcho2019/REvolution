The comparator_3bit module can be designed using combinational logic to compare the two 3-bit binary numbers A and B. 
We will use simple comparison logic to determine the values of A_greater, A_equal, and A_less.
For A_greater, we check if A is greater than B by comparing the bits of A and B from most significant bit (MSB) to least significant bit (LSB).
For A_equal, we check if A is equal to B by comparing all bits of A and B.
For A_less, we can use the inverse of A_greater and A_equal to determine if A is less than B, ensuring the outputs are mutually exclusive.
