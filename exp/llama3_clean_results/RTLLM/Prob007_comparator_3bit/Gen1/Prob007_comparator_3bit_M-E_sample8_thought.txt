To design a novel 3-bit comparator, we can leverage a priority-encoded comparison approach. This involves generating a priority-encoded signal based on the most significant bit (MSB) down to the least significant bit (LSB) of the input operands A and B. By comparing these bits sequentially, we can determine the relationship between A and B and assert the corresponding output signal (A_greater, A_equal, A_less). This approach ensures mutual exclusivity of the output signals, as only one condition can be true at any given time.
