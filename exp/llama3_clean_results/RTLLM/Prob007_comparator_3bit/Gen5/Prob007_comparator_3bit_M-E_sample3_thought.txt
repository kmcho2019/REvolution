To create a novel architectural idea for the 3-bit comparator module, we can utilize a cascaded comparison approach. This involves comparing the most significant bits (MSBs) of the two input numbers first, and then cascading the comparison to the less significant bits only if the MSBs are equal. This approach can potentially reduce the number of comparisons required and might lead to a more efficient implementation in terms of area and power.

The idea is to break down the comparison into stages, where each stage compares a specific bit position from the two input numbers. If the bits at the current stage are not equal, the comparison result can be determined immediately. If the bits are equal, the comparison proceeds to the next stage.

This cascaded comparison can be implemented using a combination of logical operators and conditional statements in Verilog, allowing for a potentially more efficient and scalable comparator design.
