To implement a novel 3-bit comparator module, we can leverage a cascade of 1-bit comparators. Each 1-bit comparator will compare corresponding bits from the two input operands A and B, starting from the most significant bit (MSB) down to the least significant bit (LSB). The outputs from each 1-bit comparator will be used to determine the overall comparison result. This approach allows for a modular and scalable design that can be easily extended to compare larger binary numbers. By using a systematic and hierarchical structure, the comparator can be made more efficient and easier to understand.
