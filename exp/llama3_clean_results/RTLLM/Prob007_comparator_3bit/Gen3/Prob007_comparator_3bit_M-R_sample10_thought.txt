To refactor the comparator_3bit module, we will utilize a different approach for implementing the comparison logic. Instead of using ternary operators directly in the assign statements, we can use a single always block to evaluate the conditions and assign the outputs. This approach allows for a more structured and potentially more readable implementation, especially for more complex combinational logic. The core idea remains the same: to compare two 3-bit binary numbers and set the outputs based on their relationship.
