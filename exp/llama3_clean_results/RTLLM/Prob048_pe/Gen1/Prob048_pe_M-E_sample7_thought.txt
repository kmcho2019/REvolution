To create a novel architectural solution for the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC), we can leverage a combination of pipelining and a more efficient multiplication strategy. One approach is to break down the multiplication into smaller, more manageable parts and pipeline these operations to improve the overall throughput and reduce the critical path. Additionally, utilizing a Booth multiplier or a similar multiplication algorithm optimized for the specific word length (32-bit in this case) can offer improvements in terms of area and power efficiency compared to a straightforward multiplication approach. The accumulator can be designed to accept the partial products as they become available, allowing for a continuous flow of data through the pipeline. This design can potentially improve the performance by allowing the system to process new inputs every cycle, after the initial latency of filling the pipeline.
