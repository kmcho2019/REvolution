To refactor the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, I will maintain the core functionality while exploring alternative structural approaches. One method is to use a combination of combinational logic for the multiplication and an `always` block solely for the register update, explicitly separating the logic for better readability and potential optimization. Additionally, considering the explicit use of a reset condition within the clocked `always` block, I will evaluate the impact of using an `initial` block or a separate `always` block for reset handling. However, the use of `assign` for the multiplication result and then feeding it into the register update logic seems less straightforward due to the need for a clocked update of the accumulator register. Therefore, the focus will be on clarifying the logic flow and potentially simplifying the reset handling.
