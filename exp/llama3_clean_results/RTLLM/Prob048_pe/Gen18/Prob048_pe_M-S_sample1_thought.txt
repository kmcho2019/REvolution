To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) design, we can eliminate the hierarchical segmentation and focus on a straightforward multiplier and accumulator architecture. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing unnecessary segmentation and focusing on the core multiplication and accumulation operations.

The simplified design will include:

1.  A register to store the accumulated result.
2.  A multiplier to perform the multiplication of input operands.
3.  An adder to accumulate the result of the multiplication into the register.
4.  A reset mechanism to clear the accumulated result when required.

By simplifying the design, we can reduce the area usage and potentially improve the power consumption and performance metrics.
