To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, we will focus on reducing complexity while maintaining functionality. The previous solution utilizes a segmented multiplication approach, which, although efficient in terms of area and power, introduces unnecessary complexity for this specific task. 

A simpler approach involves using a single multiplication operation followed by accumulation in a sequential logic block. This method reduces the complexity of the combinational logic and is potentially faster and more straightforward to implement.

The simplified MAC module will:

1.  **Direct Multiplication:** Perform a direct multiplication of the 32-bit input operands `a` and `b`.
2.  **Sequential Accumulation:** Use a sequential logic block to accumulate the result of the multiplication in the output register `c`.
3.  **Robust Reset Handling:** Ensure proper reset handling by resetting the accumulator register `c` to 0 when the reset signal `rst` is high.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce the overall complexity, potentially leading to improved performance and reduced area utilization, while maintaining the required functionality of the MAC module.
