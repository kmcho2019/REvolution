To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, we can adopt a more straightforward approach by eliminating the segmentation of the input operands and directly multiplying the two 32-bit integers. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design by removing the need for multiple parallel multipliers and the associated combinational logic for combining their results.

The simplified design will focus on the essential components:

1.  **Multiplication:** Perform a single 32-bit multiplication operation between the input operands `a` and `b`.
2.  **Accumulator Register:** Use a single register `c_reg` to store the accumulated result.
3.  **Reset Handling:** Incorporate a reset signal `rst` to initialize the accumulator register to 0 when asserted.
4.  **Accumulation Logic:** Implement a simple accumulation logic that adds the product of `a` and `b` to the current value of `c_reg` on each clock cycle, unless the reset signal is asserted.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce the area utilization and power consumption while maintaining the functionality of the MAC module. The critical path of the design will primarily depend on the multiplication operation and the accumulation logic, which can be optimized for better performance.
