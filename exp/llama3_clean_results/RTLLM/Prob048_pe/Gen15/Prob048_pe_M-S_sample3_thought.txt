To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, we will focus on reducing complexity while maintaining the required functionality. The main simplification strategy involves:

1.  **Removing Segmentation:** Instead of segmenting the 32-bit operands into smaller parts and performing parallel multiplications, we will use a single 32-bit multiplication operation. This approach reduces the number of multipliers and associated logic, potentially decreasing area usage and power consumption.
2.  **Direct Accumulation:** We will directly accumulate the result of the multiplication into the output register without using temporary variables or complex logic, which can simplify the design and reduce latency.
3.  **Simplified Reset Handling:** The reset handling will be maintained to ensure the accumulator register is properly reset when the reset signal is high.
4.  **Optimized Combinational Logic:** The multiplication operation will be performed using a single, efficient 32-bit multiplier, reducing complexity and potentially improving performance.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce the area and power consumption while maintaining the necessary functionality of the Multiplying Accumulator module.
