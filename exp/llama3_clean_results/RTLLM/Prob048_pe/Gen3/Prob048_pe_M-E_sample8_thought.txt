To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a pipelined approach. The pipelined MAC_PE will have three stages: (1) a multiplier stage to calculate the product of the input operands 'a' and 'b', (2) an accumulator stage to add the product to the current accumulated result, and (3) a register stage to store the updated accumulated result. This pipelined architecture can improve the performance by reducing the critical path delay and allowing for higher clock frequencies. Additionally, we will use a separate register to store the intermediate product, which can help in reducing the power consumption by avoiding unnecessary calculations.
