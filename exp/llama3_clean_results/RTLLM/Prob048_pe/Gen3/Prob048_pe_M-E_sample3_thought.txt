The novel architectural idea involves utilizing a combination of a Booth multiplier and a pipelined accumulator to improve the performance and power efficiency of the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module. The Booth multiplier will be used to reduce the number of partial products and thus decrease the complexity of the multiplication operation. Additionally, the pipelined accumulator will help to break down the accumulation operation into stages, reducing the critical path length and increasing the clock speed. The reset functionality will be retained and integrated into the pipelined architecture. This approach aims to optimize the performance, power consumption, and area usage of the MAC module.
