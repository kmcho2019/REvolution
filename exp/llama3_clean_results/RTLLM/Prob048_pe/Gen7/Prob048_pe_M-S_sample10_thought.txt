To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) design, let's consider a more straightforward approach that eliminates unnecessary complexity while maintaining functionality. The main idea is to use a single register to store the accumulated result and update it by adding the product of the input operands in each clock cycle.

The key components of this simplified design include:

1.  **Register for Accumulation:** A 32-bit register, `c_reg`, to store the accumulated result.
2.  **Multiplication and Accumulation:** An always block that performs the multiplication of the input operands `a` and `b`, and accumulates the result in the register `c_reg`.
3.  **Reset Mechanism:** A reset signal `rst` that, when high, resets the register `c_reg` to zero.

By using a simple register and always block, we can significantly reduce the complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
