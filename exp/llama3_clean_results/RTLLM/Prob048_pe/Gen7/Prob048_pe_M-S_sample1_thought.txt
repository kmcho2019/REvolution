To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, we can adopt a more straightforward approach that still meets the requirements but reduces complexity. 

1.  **Direct Multiplication and Accumulation:** Instead of segmenting the 32-bit operands into smaller parts and performing parallel multiplications, we can directly multiply the two 32-bit operands and then accumulate the result. This simplifies the combinational logic and reduces the number of operations.

2.  **Sequential Logic for Accumulation:** We will use a simple sequential logic block to handle the accumulation. This involves updating the accumulator register with the sum of its current value and the product of the input operands on each clock cycle, unless the reset signal is high, in which case the register is reset to zero.

3.  **Reset Handling:** We ensure that the accumulator register is properly reset when the reset signal is high, guaranteeing correct operation under all scenarios.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce the area and power consumption while maintaining the required functionality. This approach focuses on minimizing unnecessary complexity and optimizing the design for better PPA metrics.
