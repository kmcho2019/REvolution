To simplify the given Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) design, we focus on reducing complexity while maintaining its functionality. The current implementation uses segmented multipliers and accumulates the result in a register. We can simplify this by directly multiplying the 32-bit input operands and accumulating the result without segmentation. This approach eliminates the need for multiple multipliers and the associated combinational logic, potentially reducing area consumption and improving performance.

On the positive edge of the clock signal or the positive edge of the reset signal, the design will update the accumulator register. If the reset signal is high, the register will be reset to 0; otherwise, it will accumulate the product of the input operands.

By simplifying the multiplier logic and using a more straightforward accumulation approach, we aim to reduce the design's complexity, potentially leading to improvements in its Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
