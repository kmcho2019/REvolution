To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) design, we can directly utilize the built-in multiplication operator in Verilog to compute the product of the input operands `a` and `b`, and then accumulate this product into a register `c`. This approach eliminates the need for hierarchical segmentation, parallel multipliers, and hierarchical accumulation, resulting in a more straightforward and efficient implementation.

The key components of the simplified design include:

1.  **Multiplication and Accumulation:** Multiply the input operands `a` and `b` using the built-in multiplication operator and accumulate the result into the register `c`.
2.  **Reset and Clocking:** Utilize the `always` block to update the register `c` on the positive edge of the clock signal `clk` or when the reset signal `rst` is high.

By leveraging this simplified design, we can achieve a more efficient MAC while maintaining the required functionality.
