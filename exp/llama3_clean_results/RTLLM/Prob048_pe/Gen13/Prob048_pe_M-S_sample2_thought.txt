To simplify the given Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, we will focus on minimizing the complexity while maintaining its functionality. The original design involves segmentation of 32-bit operands into 8-bit segments, parallel multiplication, and accumulation. However, this approach introduces unnecessary complexity for a straightforward MAC operation.

A simplified approach involves directly multiplying the 32-bit input operands and accumulating the result in a register. This method reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for segmentation and multiple parallel multiplications.

Key simplifications include:

1.  **Direct Multiplication:** Multiply the 32-bit input operands directly, eliminating the segmentation step.
2.  **Single Accumulator:** Use a single register to accumulate the result, simplifying the accumulation logic.
3.  **Reset Handling:** Maintain robust reset handling to ensure the accumulator register is properly reset when the reset signal is high.

By adopting this simplified approach, we can reduce the complexity of the MAC module while maintaining its core functionality, thereby improving the overall design efficiency.
