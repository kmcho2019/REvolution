The goal is to simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality. The current implementation uses segmentation and parallel multiplication, which can be complex and area-intensive. A simpler approach would be to use a single multiplication operation followed by accumulation. This approach reduces the complexity of the design and potentially improves area efficiency.

The key steps in simplifying the design are:

1.  Remove segmentation: Instead of dividing the 32-bit operands into smaller segments, perform a single 32-bit multiplication.
2.  Use a single multiplier: Replace the parallel multipliers with a single 32-bit multiplier.
3.  Simplify the accumulator: Update the accumulator logic to handle the result of the single multiplication operation.
4.  Maintain reset handling: Ensure that the reset signal correctly resets the accumulator register.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce area usage and power consumption while maintaining the correct functionality of the Multiplying Accumulator module.
