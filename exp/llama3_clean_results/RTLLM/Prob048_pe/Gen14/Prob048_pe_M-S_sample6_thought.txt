The goal is to simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) design while maintaining its functionality. We can achieve this by reducing the complexity of the multiplier and accumulator logic. Instead of using segmented multipliers, we can utilize a single 32-bit multiplier to directly multiply the input operands. Additionally, we can simplify the accumulation logic by removing the temporary register and directly updating the accumulator register.

The key steps in simplifying the design are:

1.  **Multiplier Simplification:** Replace the segmented multipliers with a single 32-bit multiplier.
2.  **Accumulator Simplification:** Remove the temporary register and directly update the accumulator register.
3.  **Reset Handling:** Maintain the existing reset handling mechanism to ensure the accumulator register is properly reset.

By simplifying the design in this manner, we aim to reduce the complexity and potentially improve the performance, power consumption, and area efficiency of the MAC.
