To design the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, I will utilize a register to store the partial sum and update it in each cycle. The module will take two 32-bit input operands, a and b, and produce a 32-bit output representing the accumulated result, c. The design will incorporate a clock signal, clk, and a reset signal, rst. When rst is high, the accumulator register will be reset to 0. Otherwise, on each positive edge of the clock signal, the module will multiply a and b, and add the result to the accumulator register, c.
