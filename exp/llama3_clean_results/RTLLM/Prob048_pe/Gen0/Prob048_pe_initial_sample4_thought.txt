To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers in the MAC_PE, I will use a combination of Verilog's always block and registers. The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) or the positive edge of the reset signal (rst). Inside the always block, I will first check the reset condition. If the reset signal (rst) is high, I will set the accumulator register (c) to 0. If the reset signal (rst) is low, I will multiply the input operands (a and b) and add the result to the accumulator register (c). This way, the accumulator register (c) will store the partial sum (the intermediate accumulation result).
