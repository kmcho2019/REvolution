To implement the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE), we will use a Verilog module named 'pe' that takes in a clock signal 'clk', a reset signal 'rst', and two 32-bit input operands 'a' and 'b'. The module will have a register 'c' to store the accumulated result. On every positive edge of the clock signal, the module will update the register 'c' by adding the product of 'a' and 'b' to its current value. If the reset signal 'rst' is high, the register 'c' will be reset to 0.
