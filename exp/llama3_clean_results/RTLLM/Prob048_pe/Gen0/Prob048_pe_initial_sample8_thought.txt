To implement a Multiplying Accumulator for 32-bit integers, we will design a module named "pe" that takes in two 32-bit operands "a" and "b", and a clock signal "clk" along with a reset signal "rst". The module will output a 32-bit accumulated result "c". We will use an always block to handle the synchronous operations based on the clock and reset signals. Inside this block, we'll check for the reset condition to either reset the accumulator register "c" to 0 or accumulate the product of "a" and "b" into "c".
