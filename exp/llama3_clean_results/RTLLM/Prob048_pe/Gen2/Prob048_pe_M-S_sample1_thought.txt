To simplify the Multiplying Accumulator (MAC_PE) implementation while maintaining its functionality, we focus on minimizing unnecessary complexity in the logic. The essential operations include resetting the accumulator when the reset signal 'rst' is high and accumulating the product of 'a' and 'b' when 'rst' is low. By directly using the 'always' block with the clock and reset as sensitivities, and performing the multiplication and accumulation within this block, we can ensure the design remains straightforward and efficient. This approach ensures the core functionality of the MAC_PE is preserved while simplifying the overall design.
