To develop a novel architectural idea for the Multiplying Accumulator, we can leverage the concept of a pipelined architecture with a focus on reducing the critical path delay and improving the overall performance. The design will incorporate a multi-stage pipeline to break down the multiplication and accumulation operations into smaller, more manageable parts. This approach allows for a significant increase in the clock frequency, as the critical path is shortened. Furthermore, we can utilize a combinational multiplier in each stage to reduce the dependency on sequential operations, thereby improving the overall throughput of the system. By distributing the multiplication and accumulation across multiple stages, we can also explore opportunities for power reduction through clock gating and voltage scaling.
