To design a novel Multiplying Accumulator (MAC) module, I will propose a pipelined architecture that separates the multiplication and accumulation operations into different stages. This approach aims to improve the performance and reduce the power consumption by breaking down the critical path into shorter segments. The new architecture will utilize two registers, one for storing the product of the input operands and another for accumulating the result. By pipelining the operations, the design can potentially achieve higher clock frequencies and lower power consumption.
