To create a more efficient and scalable ALU design, we can leverage the concept of modular arithmetic and bit-level operations. Our novel architectural idea involves using a combination of bitwise operations, arithmetic circuits, and flag generation units to perform the required operations.

The proposed design consists of three main modules: 

1. Bitwise Operations Module: This module performs bitwise AND, OR, XOR, and NOR operations. It uses a combination of bitwise operators and multiplexers to select the correct operation based on the control signal (aluc).

2. Arithmetic Circuits Module: This module performs arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction, and comparison. It uses a combination of adders, subtractors, and comparators to perform the required operations.

3. Flag Generation Unit: This unit generates the zero, carry, negative, overflow, and flag outputs based on the result of the operation. It uses a combination of logical operators and conditional statements to determine the correct flag values.

By using a modular design approach, we can easily add or remove modules to support additional operations or optimize the design for specific use cases. This approach also allows for easier testing and verification of individual modules, reducing the overall design complexity.

To further optimize the design, we can use techniques such as pipelining, clock gating, and resource sharing to reduce the power consumption and area requirements.
