The existing ALU implementation uses a large case statement to perform various operations based on the control signal (aluc). To create a novel architectural idea, we can design a pipelined ALU that breaks down the operation into multiple stages, allowing for improved performance and reduced combinational logic depth.

Our new ALU architecture will consist of four stages:

1.  **Instruction Decode Stage:** This stage will decode the operation to be performed and generate the necessary control signals for the subsequent stages.
2.  **Operand Fetch Stage:** This stage will fetch the operands (a and b) and perform any necessary sign extensions.
3.  **Execution Stage:** This stage will perform the actual operation based on the control signals generated in the instruction decode stage.
4.  **Result Stage:** This stage will compute the final result, including any necessary flag updates.

To implement this pipelined ALU, we will use a combination of always blocks and assign statements to ensure correct functionality and efficient resource utilization. The result will be a more modular and maintainable design that can be easily extended to support additional operations or modified to meet specific performance requirements.
