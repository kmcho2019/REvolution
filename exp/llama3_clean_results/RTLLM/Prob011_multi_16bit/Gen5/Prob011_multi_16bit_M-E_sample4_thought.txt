The new idea is to implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier using a novel architecture based on a combination of a counter, a barrel shifter, and an accumulator. This architecture takes advantage of the properties of binary multiplication to reduce the complexity of the design. The counter will be used to keep track of the current bit position being processed, the barrel shifter will be used to shift the multiplier to the correct position, and the accumulator will be used to accumulate the partial products. The design will also include a control unit to manage the flow of the multiplication operation. This approach will simplify the logic and reduce the number of conditional statements, potentially leading to improvements in Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
