The novel idea is to use a pipeline architecture to improve the performance of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier. The pipeline will have four stages: input, shift, multiply, and accumulate. The input stage will load the multiplicand and multiplier into registers. The shift stage will shift the multiplicand to the left by the current bit position. The multiply stage will perform the multiplication of the shifted multiplicand and the multiplier. The accumulate stage will accumulate the partial products. This architecture will reduce the critical path and improve the overall performance of the multiplier. Additionally, the pipeline will be controlled by a finite state machine (FSM) that will manage the flow of data through the pipeline.
