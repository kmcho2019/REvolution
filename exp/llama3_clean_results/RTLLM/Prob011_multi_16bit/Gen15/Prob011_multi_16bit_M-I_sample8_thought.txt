To improve the existing solution, we will focus on optimizing the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The current implementation uses a sequential approach for the multiplication, which may limit the achievable clock frequency and contribute to higher power consumption.

Our improvement strategy involves the following steps:

1. **Pipelining**: We will introduce pipelining to break down the multiplication operation into smaller stages. This will reduce the critical path and improve the performance (timing) of the design.
2. **Clock Gating**: We will incorporate clock gating to reduce unnecessary switching activity and thus power consumption. This involves introducing a `clock_enable` signal that is only active when necessary.
3. **Area Optimization**: We will minimize the use of redundant logic and utilize resources more efficiently. This may involve sharing resources or using more compact implementations where possible.
4. **Reset Handling**: We will use a synchronous reset approach to simplify the design and avoid potential issues with asynchronous resets.
5. **Non-Blocking Assignments**: We will use non-blocking assignments (=) instead of blocking assignments (<=) to avoid race conditions and improve timing performance.

By implementing these strategies, we aim to create a solution that balances simplicity, efficiency, and performance, addressing both power and area considerations.
