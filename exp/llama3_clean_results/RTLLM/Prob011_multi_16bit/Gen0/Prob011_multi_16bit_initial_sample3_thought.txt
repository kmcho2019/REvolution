The design of the unsigned 16-bit multiplier utilizes shift and accumulate operations to generate the product output. It incorporates control signals such as clock, reset, and start, along with a completion flag indicating the completion of the multiplication operation. The design involves three primary components: the shift count register (i), the multiplication completion flag (done_r), and the shift and accumulate operation. The shift count register is updated based on the start signal and reset condition. The multiplication completion flag is set based on the shift count register value. The shift and accumulate operation involves storing the multiplicand and multiplier in respective registers, shifting and accumulating the multiplier based on the multiplicand bits, and generating the product output.
