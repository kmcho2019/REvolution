To implement the unsigned 16-bit multiplier, we will use a novel architectural approach based on a bit-serial multiplier with a pipeline architecture. The multiplier will be divided into four stages, each stage handling 4 bits of the multiplicand. Each stage will perform a partial multiplication and accumulate the results. The pipeline architecture will allow for a high throughput and a relatively low latency. The multiplication completion flag (done_r) will be generated based on the pipeline's status.
