A novel approach to designing the up_down_counter module is to utilize a single always block that handles both the reset and the counting logic. This approach eliminates the need for a separate counter_logic module and directly updates the count output based on the up_down control signal. To improve performance and reduce area, the design can leverage the fact that the counter wraps around at 65535, using bitwise operations to simplify the increment and decrement logic. This method reduces the complexity of the design and potentially improves the PPA metrics by minimizing the combinational logic depth and the number of operations required for each clock cycle.
