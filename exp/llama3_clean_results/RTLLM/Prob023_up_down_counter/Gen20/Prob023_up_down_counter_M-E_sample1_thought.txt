Our novel solution involves implementing the up-down counter using a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of both synchronous and asynchronous design methodologies. The core idea is to use a toggle-based mechanism to control the counting direction, eliminating the need for complex arithmetic operations. 

We introduce a new internal signal, `toggle`, which alternates its value based on the `up_down` control signal. This toggle signal then determines whether the counter increments or decrements on each clock cycle. 

To minimize the complexity of the arithmetic operations and optimize for power consumption, we utilize a simple adder/subtractor module that can be efficiently implemented using dedicated hardware resources in the target technology. 

The design consists of three main components: 
1. A toggle controller that generates the `toggle` signal based on the `up_down` control signal.
2. A counter module that increments or decrements the count value based on the `toggle` signal.
3. A reset controller that resets the counter to zero when the `reset` signal is active.

By decoupling the counting direction logic from the arithmetic operations, our design achieves a better balance between performance, power consumption, and area utilization.
