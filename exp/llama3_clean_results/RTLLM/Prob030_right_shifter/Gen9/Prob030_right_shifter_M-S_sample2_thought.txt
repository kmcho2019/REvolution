To simplify the right shifter module, we can eliminate unnecessary complexity by maintaining the same logic but improving readability and synthesis efficiency. The main concept remains shifting the register `q` to the right by one bit and inserting the new input `d` into the most significant position on every rising edge of the clock. This can be achieved with a minimalistic approach using a single always block and a non-blocking assignment to update `q`. The initialization of `q` to 0 is implicit in Verilog, and the shifting operation can be directly implemented within the always block.
