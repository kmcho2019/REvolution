A novel architectural idea for the right shifter module is to utilize a circular buffer to mimic the right shift operation. This approach involves using a register file with 8 bits, where each bit is shifted in a circular manner. On each clock cycle, the most significant bit is replaced with the new input bit, and the other bits are shifted one position to the right. This circular buffer approach allows for a simple and efficient implementation of the right shift operation without requiring complex combinational logic.
