To implement a novel right shifter module, we can utilize a shift register architecture that leverages a serial-in parallel-out (SIPO) approach. The design will consist of eight D-flip flops, each representing one bit of the output. On each clock cycle, the input signal 'd' will be captured by the most significant bit (MSB) D-flip flop, and the output of each D-flip flop will be connected to the input of the next less significant bit, effectively shifting the bits to the right. This approach avoids the need for explicit right shift operators or concatenation, potentially offering improved performance and area efficiency.
