To simplify the right shifter module, we can eliminate the conditional initialization of the register `q` and directly perform the right shift operation. This can be achieved by utilizing the non-blocking assignment (`<=`) to update `q` with the new value on every rising edge of the clock, which inherently shifts the current value of `q` to the right and inserts the new input `d` into the most significant position. The initialization of `q` to 0 is implicitly handled by the default initialization of registers in Verilog to 0 when not explicitly initialized otherwise.
