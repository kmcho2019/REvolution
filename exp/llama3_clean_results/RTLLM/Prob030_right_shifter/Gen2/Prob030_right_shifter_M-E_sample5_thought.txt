To implement a novel right shifter module, we can utilize a serial-in parallel-out shift register architecture. This approach involves using a series of D flip-flops to store the bits of the input signal d as they are shifted into the register. Each D flip-flop will represent one bit position in the 8-bit output q. On each rising edge of the clock, the input bit d is shifted into the most significant bit position, and the bits are propagated through the flip-flops, effectively shifting the contents of the register to the right. This design will allow for a more modular and scalable implementation.
