A novel architectural idea for the right shifter module involves utilizing a tree-like structure of multiplexers to achieve the right shift operation. This approach can be more efficient in terms of area and power consumption compared to traditional register-based implementations, especially for larger shift widths. The basic idea is to create a hierarchical structure where each level of the tree represents a shift by a certain number of bits, and the final output is constructed by selecting the appropriate bits from the input signal and the shifted values. This approach can be particularly beneficial when the target technology has abundant multiplexer resources or when the design requires a high degree of flexibility and scalability.
