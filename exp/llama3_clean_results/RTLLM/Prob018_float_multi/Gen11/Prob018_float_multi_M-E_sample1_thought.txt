3. **Exponent Calculation:** The exponents of the input numbers will be added together to calculate the exponent of the result.

5. **Rounding and Normalization:** The result will be rounded and normalized to ensure accurate representation in the IEEE-754 format.

6. **Special Cases Handling:** Special cases such as NaN (Not a Number), infinity, and overflow/underflow scenarios will be handled according to the IEEE-754 standard.
