The novel approach to designing a 32-bit floating-point multiplier for IEEE-754 standard single-precision arithmetic involves a hybrid architecture that combines the benefits of both sequential and parallel processing. This architecture, termed "Hybrid Floating-Point Multiplier" (HFPM), integrates a finite state machine (FSM) with a set of dedicated hardware accelerators for critical operations such as mantissa multiplication, exponent calculation, and rounding. The HFPM features a dual-path processing mechanism where the FSM oversees the overall operation sequence, while the hardware accelerators execute specific tasks in parallel, thereby enhancing throughput and reducing latency. Additionally, the design incorporates a dynamic rounding mode selector and an advanced underflow/overflow handling mechanism to ensure compliance with the IEEE 754 standard and to optimize performance.
