To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we can leverage a lookup table (LUT) approach. The LUT will encode all possible combinations of inputs and their corresponding outputs for the required logic gates. This approach allows for a more centralized management of logic and can potentially offer advantages in terms of design simplicity and optimization for specific technologies. The idea involves creating a single, compact structure that encapsulates the logic for all gates, utilizing a case statement or a similar mechanism to handle the different input combinations and produce the outputs accordingly. This method can be particularly effective in technologies where LUTs are native or in situations where the design needs to be highly optimized for a specific set of inputs and outputs.
