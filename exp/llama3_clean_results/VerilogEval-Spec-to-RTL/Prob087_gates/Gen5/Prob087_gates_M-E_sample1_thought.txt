The idea involves using bitwise operators to compute the outputs. For instance, the XOR operation can be used as a building block for other operations. By carefully combining bitwise XOR, AND, and OR operations, we can compute all the required outputs.

Additionally, this approach allows for a more efficient use of resources, as it eliminates the need for multiple separate modules, reducing the overall area consumption and potentially leading to improved power efficiency.
