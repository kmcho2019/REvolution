To simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality, we can eliminate the use of separate gate modules and directly implement all the logic operations within the TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of module instantiations, which can lead to improvements in area efficiency and potentially reduce power consumption due to lower switching activity. The logic for each gate function will be directly assigned to the corresponding output within the TopModule, leveraging Verilog's logical operators for AND, OR, XOR, and their complements.
