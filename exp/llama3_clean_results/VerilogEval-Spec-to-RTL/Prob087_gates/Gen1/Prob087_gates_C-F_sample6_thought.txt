To implement the TopModule, we will directly use Verilog's built-in bitwise operators for each output port. We will use '&' for AND, '|' for OR, '^' for XOR, and '~' for NOT operations. The '~' operator will also be utilized to invert signals for implementing NAND, NOR, and XNOR gates, as well as the 'a and-not b' operation. This approach combines the conventional use of bitwise operators in Verilog with clear and readable code structure.
