To create a novel solution, we can utilize Verilog's ability to define and instantiate modules. Instead of directly implementing the logic gates in the TopModule, we can define separate modules for each type of gate (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, and AND-NOT) and then instantiate these modules within the TopModule. This approach promotes modularity, reusability, and maintainability. It also allows for easier modification or extension of the circuit in the future, as changes can be made at the individual gate level without affecting the rest of the circuit.
