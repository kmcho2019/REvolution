To develop a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, let's consider using a lookup table (LUT) approach. In digital circuit design, lookup tables are often used to implement complex logic functions in a compact and efficient manner. The idea here is to use a single LUT that encapsulates the truth table for all the required logic gates (AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, AND-NOT). This approach can potentially simplify the design and improve its area efficiency, as it eliminates the need for multiple individual logic gates.

The LUT will be based on a 2-input, 7-output truth table, where each output corresponds to one of the required logic gates. The inputs 'a' and 'b' will select the appropriate output from the LUT, effectively implementing all the required logic gates in a single, compact structure.

This novel approach can offer several benefits, including reduced area usage, improved performance due to the elimination of intermediate logic levels, and simplified design and verification processes.
