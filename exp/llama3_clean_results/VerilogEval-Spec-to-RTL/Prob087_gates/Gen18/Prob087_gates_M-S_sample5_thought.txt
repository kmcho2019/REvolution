To simplify the solution, we will directly implement the required logic gates within the TopModule without instantiating separate gate modules. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for individual gate modules, thus minimizing the overall area and potentially improving PPA metrics. The simplified design will use Verilog's logical operators to implement the AND, OR, XOR, NAND, NOR, XNOR, and ANDNOT gates directly within the TopModule.
