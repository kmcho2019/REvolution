To simplify the solution while maintaining functionality, we can eliminate the modular approach of defining a separate module for the XOR gate. Instead, we can directly implement the XOR gate logic for `out_assign` and `out_always_comb` within the TopModule. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design by removing the need for instantiating separate modules, thus potentially reducing area usage and improving power consumption. For `out_always_ff`, we keep the direct implementation of the flip-flop logic as it is already optimized for area and performance.
