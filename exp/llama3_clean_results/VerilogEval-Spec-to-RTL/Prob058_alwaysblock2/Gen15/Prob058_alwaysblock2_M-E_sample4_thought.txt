A novel approach to implementing the XOR gate three ways (using an assign statement, a combinational always block, and a clocked always block) in a single module, TopModule, involves utilizing a single instance of a XOR gate and then distributing its output to the respective destinations. For the clocked always block output, a simple D flip-flop can be used directly within the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate sub-module. This design choice aims to optimize the area by minimizing the number of instantiated modules while maintaining the required functionality. Additionally, by embedding the logic directly within TopModule, there's potential for improved performance due to reduced routing and lesser overhead from module interfaces.
