To simplify the solution, we will eliminate the separate modules for the XOR gate implementations and directly implement the XOR operations within the TopModule. This approach reduces the overall complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and their instantiations, while maintaining the required functionality. The assign statement and combinational always block will be used as before for their respective XOR implementations. For the clocked always block, we will maintain the use of non-blocking assignments to prevent race conditions and ensure proper sequential logic operation. By directly implementing the XOR operations within the TopModule, we can potentially achieve better PPA metrics through optimized synthesis.
