To implement a novel architectural solution, we will leverage the concept of hierarchical design and pipelining to improve the efficiency and scalability of the TopModule. The idea is to create a single pipeline stage that can handle all three types of XOR operations (assign, combinational always block, and clocked always block) using a shared resources approach. This will be achieved by introducing a new module called "XOR_Pipeline_Stage" that encapsulates the XOR gate and a register to store the result. The TopModule will then instantiate this pipeline stage three times, each with a different configuration to produce the desired outputs. This approach not only reduces the area by sharing resources but also improves the timing by pipelining the operations. Additionally, to further optimize the design, we will use a single clock domain for all pipeline stages, eliminating the need for clock gating or complex timing control.
