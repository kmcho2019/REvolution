The novel architectural idea is to implement the three XOR operations in a single always block. This approach aims to minimize the area consumption by reusing the logic within a single block. To achieve this, we will use a single always block with a conditional statement to differentiate between the combinational and sequential logic. For the combinational XOR operations, we will use the always_comb keyword. However, to incorporate the sequential logic, we will utilize the always_ff keyword and implement the clocked XOR operation within it. To optimize the area, we will share the XOR logic between the combinational and sequential operations. This novel approach will simplify the design and reduce the overall area consumption while maintaining the required functionality.
