To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a shared combinational logic block for both continuous assignment and combinational always block. This shared block will be instantiated twice, once for continuous assignment and once for combinational always block, with the output of the combinational always block being the input to the clocked always block. This approach reduces the redundancy of implementing the XOR operation multiple times and allows for easy reusability and maintenance of the code. Additionally, we can explore the use of parameters to make the design more flexible and scalable.
