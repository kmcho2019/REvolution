To introduce a novel architectural idea, we will implement the TopModule using a hierarchical design that leverages a combination of logic gates and finite state machines (FSMs) to produce the XOR outputs. The out_assign and out_always_comb outputs will be generated using a reconfigurable logic block that can be dynamically switched between an XOR gate and other basic logic functions, enhancing flexibility. The out_always_ff output will be produced by a simple Mealy FSM that outputs the XOR of its current state and input, effectively creating a sequential logic implementation.

This approach combines the benefits of modular design, flexibility through reconfigurability, and the unique properties of FSMs to create a more complex and interesting architectural solution. The reconfigurable logic block allows for potential extensions where the module could be easily adapted to perform other logical operations beyond XOR, simply by reconfiguring the logic block. The FSM-based implementation of the out_always_ff adds a layer of complexity and demonstrates how sequential logic can be used to implement combinational functions with a delay.

By incorporating these elements, the design showcases a blend of combinational and sequential logic principles, along with the concept of reconfigurability, making it a unique and educational example of digital circuit design.
