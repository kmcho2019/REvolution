To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a hierarchical approach. Instead of directly implementing the XOR operations within the TopModule, we will create separate sub-modules for each type of XOR operation: continuous assignment, combinational always block, and clocked always block. This modular approach allows for better code organization, reusability, and easier maintenance. The TopModule will then instantiate these sub-modules and connect their outputs to the respective output ports.
