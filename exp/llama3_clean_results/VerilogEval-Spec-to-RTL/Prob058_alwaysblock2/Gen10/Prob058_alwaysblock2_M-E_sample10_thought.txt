To create a novel solution, we will introduce a new architectural idea that utilizes a single, unified module to implement the XOR gate three ways, using an assign statement, a combinational always block, and a clocked always block. This approach will incorporate clock gating and input activity reduction techniques to minimize power consumption. We will also leverage SystemVerilog's conditional operator to simplify the combinational logic and reduce switching activity. The unified module will have a reconfigurable architecture, allowing it to adapt to different optimization priorities, such as power, performance, or area. This adaptability will be achieved through the use of parameters and generics, enabling the module to be easily customized for various design constraints.
