To implement the TopModule as described, we need to consider the different ways to express an XOR gate in Verilog. The assign statement is used for continuous assignment, which means the output is immediately updated whenever the inputs change. A combinational always block (always @*) is used for more complex logic but essentially behaves the same as an assign statement in terms of continuous update, but it's more flexible for complex expressions. For the clocked always block, we'll use an always @(posedge clk) block to introduce a flip-flop, causing the output to be delayed by one clock cycle compared to the other implementations.
