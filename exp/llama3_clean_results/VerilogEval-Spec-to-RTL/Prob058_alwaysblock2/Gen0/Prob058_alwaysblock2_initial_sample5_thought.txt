To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use three different methods to achieve the XOR operation: 
1. Continuous assignment: This method will directly assign the XOR result of inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out_assign' using the assign statement.
2. Combinational always block: This method will use an always_comb block to continuously evaluate the XOR of 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_always_comb'.
3. Clocked always block: This method will utilize a clocked always_ff block, with the output 'out_always_ff' being a flip-flop that is updated on the positive edge of the clock 'clk' with the XOR result of 'a' and 'b'.
