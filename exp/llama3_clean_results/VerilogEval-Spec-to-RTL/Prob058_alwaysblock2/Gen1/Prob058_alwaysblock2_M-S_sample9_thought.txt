To simplify the implementation of the TopModule, we will maintain the three methods for achieving the XOR operation: 
1. Continuous assignment: Directly assign the XOR result of inputs 'a' and 'b' to 'out_assign'.
2. Combinational always block: Use an always_comb block to evaluate the XOR of 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_always_comb'.
3. Clocked always block: Utilize an always_ff block, updating 'out_always_ff' on the positive edge of 'clk' with the XOR result of 'a' and 'b'.
We will ensure that output ports are correctly declared as wires and reg types are used within the always blocks for 'out_always_comb' and 'out_always_ff'.
