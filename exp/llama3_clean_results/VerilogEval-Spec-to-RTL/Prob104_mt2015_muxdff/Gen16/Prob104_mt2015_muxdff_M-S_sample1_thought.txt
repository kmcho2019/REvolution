To simplify the solution, we focus on the essential functionality of the TopModule, which is to act as a flip-flop with a 2:1 multiplexer at its input. The multiplexer selects between the external input 'r_in' and the internal feedback 'q_in' based on the load signal 'L'. When 'L' is asserted, the flip-flop loads 'r_in'; otherwise, it retains its previous state 'q_in' (for the context of a single flip-flop in the hierarchical design). This simplification directly addresses the requirement without introducing unnecessary complexity.
