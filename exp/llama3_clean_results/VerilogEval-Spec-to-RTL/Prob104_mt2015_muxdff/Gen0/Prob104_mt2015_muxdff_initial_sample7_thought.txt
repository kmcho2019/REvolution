To create the submodule "TopModule", we need to understand that it will be instantiated three times in the full_module. Each instance will represent one of the three flip-flops (or bits) in the original full_module's q[2:0] output. The TopModule should include a 2:1 multiplexer to select between loading a new value (r_in) or the feedback from the flip-flop (q_in), based on the load signal (L). The selected value is then clocked into the flip-flop on the rising edge of the clock (clk). The output of the flip-flop is Q.
