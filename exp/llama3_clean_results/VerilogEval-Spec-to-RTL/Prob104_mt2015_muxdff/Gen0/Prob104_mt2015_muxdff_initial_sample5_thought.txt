To implement the TopModule, we need to break down the functionality of the full_module into smaller submodules. The TopModule should contain a flip-flop and a 2:1 multiplexer. The flip-flop will store the value of q_in, and the multiplexer will select between the value of r_in and the output of the flip-flop based on the value of L. The flip-flop should be clocked by the clk signal.
