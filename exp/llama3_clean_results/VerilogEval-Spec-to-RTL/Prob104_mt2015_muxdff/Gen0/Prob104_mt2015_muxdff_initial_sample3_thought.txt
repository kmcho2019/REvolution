The TopModule should be designed as a 2:1 multiplexer and a flip-flop combined. The multiplexer will select between the q_in and r_in based on the L signal. If L is asserted, the multiplexer will pass r_in, otherwise, it will pass q_in. The output of the multiplexer will be fed into the flip-flop. The flip-flop will store the value at the positive edge of the clock signal clk and output it as Q.
