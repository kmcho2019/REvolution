A novel architectural idea for the TopModule is to utilize a different approach to combine the 2:1 multiplexer and the flip-flop. Instead of directly feeding the output of the multiplexer into the flip-flop, consider using a separate register to hold the output of the multiplexer. This register can then be used to store the value when the clock signal clk is asserted, and its output can be fed back into the multiplexer as the q_in input. This design allows for a more modular and flexible implementation, as it separates the multiplexing and storage functions. The TopModule will now have two internal components: a multiplexer that selects between r_in and q_in based on L, and a flip-flop that captures the output of the multiplexer at the positive edge of clk.
