The novel architectural idea is to utilize a 2:1 multiplexer in conjunction with a flip-flop to create the TopModule. The multiplexer will select between the external input `r_in` and the internal feedback from the flip-flop `q_in`, based on the control signal `L`. The selected value will then be clocked into the flip-flop on the rising edge of the clock `clk`, thus updating the output `Q`. This approach decouples the logic selection from the flip-flop update, providing a clean and modular design.

By using a multiplexer, we explicitly control the data path, which can improve readability and maintainability of the design. Moreover, this structure emphasizes the separate functions of data selection and storage, potentially making it easier to optimize or modify the module in the future.
