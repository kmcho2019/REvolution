The idea is to directly implement the required functionality within the TopModule without creating separate modules for XNOR and XOR operations. This can be achieved by utilizing the properties of bitwise XOR and XNOR operations. The XNOR operation between 'in1' and 'in2' can be expressed as ~(in1 ^ in2), and the XOR operation with 'in3' can be directly applied to this result. By combining these operations into a single statement, we can simplify the code and reduce the overall complexity.
