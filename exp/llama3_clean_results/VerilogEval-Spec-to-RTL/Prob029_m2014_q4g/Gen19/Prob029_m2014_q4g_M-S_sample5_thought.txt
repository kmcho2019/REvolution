The idea is to directly implement the desired functionality within the TopModule without creating separate modules for XNOR and XOR operations. This can be achieved by using the bitwise XOR operator (^) and the fact that the XNOR operation can be represented as ~(a ^ b), where ~ denotes bitwise negation (NOT). By combining these operations into a single statement, we can simplify the code, reduce the number of modules, and potentially improve the overall efficiency of the design.
