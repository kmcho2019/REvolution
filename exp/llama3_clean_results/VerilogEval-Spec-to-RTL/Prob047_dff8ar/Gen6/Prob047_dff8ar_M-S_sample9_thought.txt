To simplify the solution, we will directly implement the 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset within the TopModule. This approach eliminates the need for a separate module for a single D flip-flop and the for-generate loop, reducing the overall complexity of the design. We will use a single always block to describe the behavior of all 8 D flip-flops, which will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal and will reset to 0 when the asynchronous reset signal is high.
