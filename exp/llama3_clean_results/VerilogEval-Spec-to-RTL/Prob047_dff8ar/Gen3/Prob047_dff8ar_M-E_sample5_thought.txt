A novel approach to implementing the TopModule with 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset is to use a single module that utilizes a register to store the 8-bit data. This module, named SyncReg, will have a clock input, an asynchronous reset input, an 8-bit data input, and an 8-bit output. The SyncReg module will be designed to latch the input data on the positive edge of the clock, and reset the output to zero when the asynchronous reset is asserted. This approach eliminates the need for individual DFF modules, potentially reducing the area and power consumption. Additionally, this design can be optimized for better performance and power efficiency by minimizing the combinational logic and using clock gating techniques.
