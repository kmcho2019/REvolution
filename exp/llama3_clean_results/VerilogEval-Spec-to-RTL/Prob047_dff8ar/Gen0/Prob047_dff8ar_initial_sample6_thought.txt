To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will use 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with asynchronous active high reset. Each DFF will have an input (d) and an output (q), and all DFFs will share the clock (clk) and asynchronous reset (areset) signals. The output of each DFF will be combined to form the 8-bit output q. We will use the posedge of the clk to trigger the DFFs and the areset signal to reset the outputs to 0.
