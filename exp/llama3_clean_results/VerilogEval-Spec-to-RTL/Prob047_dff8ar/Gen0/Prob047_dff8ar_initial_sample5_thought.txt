To implement the TopModule, we can use a simple sequential logic design. The module will have an 8-bit input d and an 8-bit output q. It will include 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high asynchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal clk. When the asynchronous reset signal areset is high, all DFFs will reset their outputs to 0. The output q will be updated with the value of d at the positive edge of clk when areset is low.
