The TopModule will contain 8 D flip-flops. Each D flip-flop will have an asynchronous active high reset and will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. When the asynchronous reset signal (areset) is high, the output of all D flip-flops will be reset to 0. The input d is an 8-bit vector, and the output q will also be an 8-bit vector, where each bit is connected to the output of a corresponding D flip-flop.
