To simplify the solution, we can directly use 8 individual D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset without implementing a separate shift register module. This approach eliminates the need for an additional module and reduces the complexity of the design.

Each D flip-flop will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal and will have an active high asynchronous reset. When the reset is asserted, the output of each D flip-flop will be reset to 0.

This simplified approach achieves the same functionality as the original design but with reduced complexity and potentially improved performance.
