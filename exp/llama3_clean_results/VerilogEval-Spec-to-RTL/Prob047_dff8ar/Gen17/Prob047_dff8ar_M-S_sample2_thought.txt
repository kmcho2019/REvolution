To simplify the solution, we will directly implement the 8 D flip-flops with active high asynchronous reset within the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate DFF_8bit module. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing the hierarchical module structure, which can lead to a more straightforward and possibly more efficient implementation.

The core functionality will remain the same: 8 D flip-flops will be triggered by the positive edge of the clock (clk) and will reset to 0 when the asynchronous reset (areset) is high. This simplification maintains the required functionality while reducing the overall complexity of the design.
