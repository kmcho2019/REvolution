To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we will utilize a shift register approach. Instead of instantiating 8 separate D flip-flops, we can design a single module that shifts the input data into a register on each clock cycle. This approach allows us to take advantage of the sequential nature of the input data and reduce the overall area consumption. We will also incorporate an asynchronous reset to clear the register when the reset signal is asserted. By using a shift register, we can minimize the number of flip-flops required and potentially improve the area and power metrics.
