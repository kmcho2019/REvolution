To create a novel solution, we can implement the TopModule using a shift register approach. Instead of instantiating 8 separate DFF modules, we can use a single 8-bit shift register with a load signal controlled by the asynchronous reset. When the reset is high, the shift register loads all zeros. Otherwise, on the positive edge of the clock, the shift register shifts in the new data from the input port `d`. This approach simplifies the design and potentially reduces area consumption. Additionally, to minimize switching activity and reduce power consumption, we can ensure that the clock and reset signals are properly gated and that unnecessary transitions are avoided.
