To create a novel solution, we will employ a shift register approach to reverse the bit ordering of the input. The idea is to use a series of D flip-flops to store the input bits, and then shift the bits in reverse order to the output. This approach will not only achieve the desired bit reversal but also provide a unique architectural perspective. We will use a parameterized module to make the design scalable and adaptable to different input widths. The shift register will be implemented using a generate block to create the required number of D flip-flops, and the output will be assigned by shifting the bits in reverse order.
