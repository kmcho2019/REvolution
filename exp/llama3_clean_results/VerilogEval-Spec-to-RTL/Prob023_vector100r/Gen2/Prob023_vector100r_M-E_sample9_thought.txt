To reverse the bit ordering of the input, we can utilize a barrel shifter architecture, which is commonly used for bit manipulation in digital circuits. The barrel shifter will shift the bits of the input signal in a circular manner, effectively reversing their order. This approach can be implemented using a combination of multiplexers and wires in Verilog. By using a barrel shifter, we can create a more modular and scalable design that can be easily extended to support larger input widths.
