A novel approach to reversing the bit ordering of the input is to use a technique similar to a barrel shifter, but instead of rotating the bits, we simply rearrange them. However, to introduce a completely new architectural idea, we can utilize a parallel-in, parallel-out (PIPO) register and use the for loop in Verilog to shift the bits in a loop, effectively reversing the input without needing to explicitly assign each bit individually. This can provide an elegant and scalable solution, especially for larger input sizes.
