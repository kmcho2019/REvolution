A novel approach to reversing the bit ordering of the input is to utilize a combination of shift registers and multiplexers. The idea is to shift the input bits into a series of registers, and then use multiplexers to select the bits in reverse order. This approach can be implemented using a hierarchical structure, where each level of the hierarchy shifts and multiplexes a subset of the input bits. By recursively applying this structure, we can efficiently reverse the bit ordering of the input. This approach can potentially reduce the area and power consumption of the design, while maintaining a reasonable performance.
