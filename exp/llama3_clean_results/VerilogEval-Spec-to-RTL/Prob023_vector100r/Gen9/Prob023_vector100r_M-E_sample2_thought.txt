To create a novel solution, we will implement a barrel shifter architecture. A barrel shifter is a digital circuit that can shift a binary number by a specified number of bits in a single clock cycle. In this case, we can use a barrel shifter to reverse the bit ordering of the input. The idea is to shift the input bits to the right by half the width of the input, and then shift the upper half of the bits to the left by half the width, while shifting the lower half of the bits to the right by half the width. This process effectively reverses the bit ordering of the input.
