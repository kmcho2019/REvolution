A novel approach to reversing the bit ordering of the input is to utilize a barrel shifter architecture. This architecture typically involves a series of multiplexers that can shift the bits of the input word by a specified number of positions. In the context of bit reversal, we can configure the barrel shifter to shift the input by 99 positions, effectively reversing the bit order. The barrel shifter can be implemented using a combination of multiplexers and wires, allowing for a highly efficient and scalable design.
