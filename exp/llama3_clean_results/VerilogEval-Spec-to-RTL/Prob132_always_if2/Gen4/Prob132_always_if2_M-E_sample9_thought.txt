A novel approach to designing the TopModule would be to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) architecture. The FSM can have two states: "driving" and "shutdown". The "driving" state would correspond to the condition where the computer is not overheated and the vehicle has not arrived at its destination, while the "shutdown" state would be entered when the computer is overheated or the vehicle has arrived. This approach allows for a more structured and scalable design, as additional states or transitions can be easily added if needed. The FSM can be implemented using a single always block, which can improve the performance and reduce the area consumption.
