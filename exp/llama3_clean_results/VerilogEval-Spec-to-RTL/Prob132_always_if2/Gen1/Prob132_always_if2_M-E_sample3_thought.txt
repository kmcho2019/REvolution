A novel approach to designing the TopModule would be to utilize a state machine that encapsulates the conditions and actions for both `shut_off_computer` and `keep_driving`. This state machine would have states representing the status of the computer (on or off due to overheating) and the car's driving status (driving or stopped due to arrival or gas tank empty). By using a state machine, the logic becomes more organized and scalable if more conditions or states need to be added in the future.
