A novel approach to the problem is to use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the states of the computer and the driving condition. The FSM can have two states: "running" and "shut_off". In the "running" state, the computer is on and the driving condition is checked. If the CPU is overheated, the FSM transitions to the "shut_off" state and the computer is shut off. For the driving condition, we can have another FSM with two states: "driving" and "stopped". The "driving" state is active when the driver has not arrived and the gas tank is not empty. If the driver arrives or the gas tank becomes empty, the FSM transitions to the "stopped" state. This approach allows for a more structured and scalable design, making it easier to add more conditions or states in the future.
