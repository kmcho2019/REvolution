A novel approach to improve the existing module is to incorporate a finite state machine (FSM) that manages the states of the computer and the driving conditions. The FSM can have four states: IDLE, DRIVE, SHUTDOWN, and STOP. The IDLE state represents the initial condition, DRIVE is when the car is moving, SHUTDOWN is when the computer needs to be shut down due to overheating, and STOP is when the car needs to stop due to arrival or an empty gas tank.

The FSM will transition between these states based on the input signals. This approach allows for more flexibility and scalability if additional conditions or states need to be added in the future. It also provides a clear and structured way to manage the different conditions and their interactions.

The FSM will use a combination of combinatorial logic and sequential logic to manage the state transitions and output signals. The `shut_off_computer` and `keep_driving` signals will be generated based on the current state of the FSM.
