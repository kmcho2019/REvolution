A refactored approach to implementing the decade counter involves utilizing a binary counter instead of a finite state machine (FSM). This approach simplifies the design and reduces the complexity of the combinational logic generated by the case statement in the original code. To ensure the counter counts from 0 to 9 and then wraps around, a conditional statement will be used to reset the counter when it exceeds 9.
