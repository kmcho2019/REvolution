A novel approach to designing the decade counter could involve using a Johnson counter, which is a type of counter that uses a ring of flip-flops to store the count. The Johnson counter can be designed to count up to 10 and then wrap around to 0. This approach can simplify the logic and potentially reduce the area and power consumption of the counter. The enable signal (`slowena`) can be used to gate the clock to the counter, effectively pausing the count when `slowena` is low. The reset signal can be used to reset the counter to 0. This approach can be more efficient and scalable than traditional counter designs, especially for larger counts.
