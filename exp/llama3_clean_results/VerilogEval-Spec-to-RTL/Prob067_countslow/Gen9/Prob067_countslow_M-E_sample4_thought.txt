An alternative approach to implementing the decade counter is to use a 4-bit binary counter and a comparator to control the wrapping around from 9 to 0. This design takes advantage of the regularity of binary counting and avoids the need for a large case statement or complex state machine. By using a binary counter, we can leverage the natural incrementing behavior of binary numbers and simply reset the counter when it exceeds 9. This approach should result in a more efficient and scalable design.
