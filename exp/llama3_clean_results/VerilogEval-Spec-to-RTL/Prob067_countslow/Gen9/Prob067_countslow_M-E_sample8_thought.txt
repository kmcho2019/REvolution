A novel approach to implementing the decade counter could involve utilizing a binary counter and then decoding the binary output to select the correct decade counter output. This method would simplify the logic required for the counter itself, as binary counters are straightforward to implement. The decoding logic can then be used to convert the binary count into the desired decade counter output. This approach may provide opportunities for optimization by using a smaller binary counter and then applying a simple decoding logic to achieve the desired output, potentially reducing the area and power requirements.
