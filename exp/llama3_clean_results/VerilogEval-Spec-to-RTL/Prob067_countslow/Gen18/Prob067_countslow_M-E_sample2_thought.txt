To implement a novel decade counter architecture, we can utilize a hybrid approach combining a traditional counter with a saturation mechanism. The counter will increment normally until it reaches the saturation point (9 in this case), at which point it will wrap around to 0. This approach can be more area-efficient than the FSM approach as it eliminates the need for a large case statement or complex next-state logic. The `slowena` input will be used to gate the clock signal for the counter, effectively pausing it when `slowena` is low. This method reduces the power consumption by minimizing switching activity when the counter is paused. Additionally, the use of a simple counter structure allows for easy optimization of the sequential logic to reduce area usage.
