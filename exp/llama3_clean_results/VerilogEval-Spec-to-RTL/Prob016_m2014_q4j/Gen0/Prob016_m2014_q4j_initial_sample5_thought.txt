To implement a 4-bit adder, we'll use four full adder modules. Each full adder will take a bit from the x input, a bit from the y input, and the carry from the previous full adder. The first full adder won't have a carry input, so we'll set it to 0. The sum output will be a 4-bit value, and the carry output from the last full adder will serve as the overflow bit, making the total sum a 5-bit value.
