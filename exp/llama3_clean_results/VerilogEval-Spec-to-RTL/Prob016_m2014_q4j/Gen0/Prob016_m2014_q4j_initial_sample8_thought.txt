To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, we first need to understand the structure of a full adder. A full adder is a digital circuit that adds three one-bit inputs and produces a two-bit output: sum and carry. The sum output is 1 if either of the inputs is 1, but not both. The carry output is 1 if at least two of the inputs are 1. We can chain four full adders together to create a 4-bit adder. The carry output from each full adder will serve as one of the inputs to the next full adder. The sum output from each full adder will contribute to the final sum. The final carry output will represent the overflow bit.
