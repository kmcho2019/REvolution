To implement the 4-bit adder with full adders, we will use a chain of four full adders. Each full adder will take two input bits from x and y and the carry from the previous full adder. The output of each full adder will be the sum bit and the carry for the next full adder. The first full adder will not have a carry input, so we will tie it to 0. The final sum will be the concatenation of the sum bits from each full adder and the final carry.
