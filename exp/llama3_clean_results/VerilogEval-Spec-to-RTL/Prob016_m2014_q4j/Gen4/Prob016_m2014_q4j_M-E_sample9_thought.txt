To create a novel solution, we can leverage the concept of a carry-lookahead adder. This type of adder calculates the carry for each bit position in advance, allowing for a more parallel and efficient calculation of the sum. By calculating the carry in advance, we can reduce the critical path delay and potentially improve the power consumption. We will design a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder, which will take two 4-bit inputs and produce a 5-bit output that includes the overflow bit. The carry-lookahead adder will be composed of two main components: the generate and propagate blocks for calculating the carry, and the sum blocks for calculating the sum.
