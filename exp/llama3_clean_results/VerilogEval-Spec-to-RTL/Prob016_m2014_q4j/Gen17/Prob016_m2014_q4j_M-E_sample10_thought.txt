To create a novel solution, we will explore a hierarchical approach, breaking down the 4-bit adder into smaller, more manageable components. We will utilize a combination of half-adders and multiplexers to minimize the number of logic gates and optimize the design for both area and performance.

The design will consist of two main stages: the sum generation stage and the carry generation stage. The sum generation stage will use half-adders to calculate the sum of corresponding bits from the two input numbers. The carry generation stage will use multiplexers to propagate the carry from one bit position to the next, depending on the values of the previous bits.

This hierarchical approach allows for a more structured and scalable design, making it easier to extend to larger bit widths if needed. Additionally, the use of multiplexers can help reduce the overall logic depth, potentially improving the performance of the design.
