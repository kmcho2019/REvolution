To implement a novel 4-bit adder architecture, we can utilize a combination of half-adders and a multiplexer-based approach to reduce the gate count and improve the overall performance. The idea involves using half-adders to generate the sum and carry for each bit position, and then utilizing a multiplexer to select the correct sum bit based on the carry from the previous bit position. This approach can potentially reduce the area and power consumption while maintaining the performance of the adder.
