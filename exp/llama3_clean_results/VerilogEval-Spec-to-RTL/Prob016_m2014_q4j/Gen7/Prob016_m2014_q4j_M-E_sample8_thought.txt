To implement a 4-bit adder with full adders, a novel architectural idea is to utilize a tree-like structure with each node representing a half adder. This approach allows for a more efficient handling of carry signals and can potentially reduce the critical path delay. Each half adder will take two input bits, one from the x and y inputs, and produce a sum and carry output. The carry output of each half adder will be passed to the next level of the tree, where it will be combined with the corresponding input bits from the next two half adders. This approach enables the simultaneous calculation of the sum and carry signals for each bit position, resulting in a more efficient and scalable design.
