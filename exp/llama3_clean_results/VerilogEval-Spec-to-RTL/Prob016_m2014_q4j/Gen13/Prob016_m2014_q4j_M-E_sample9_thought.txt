To develop a novel architectural idea for the 4-bit adder, I will explore the concept of using a parallel prefix adder (PPA) architecture, which can efficiently compute the sum and carry bits simultaneously. This approach involves designing a network of prefix computation modules that calculate the propagate and generate signals for each bit position in parallel. By structuring the adder in this way, the critical path delay can be significantly reduced compared to traditional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead adders. Additionally, I will incorporate a hierarchical design methodology, breaking down the adder into smaller, more manageable modules, to enhance maintainability and scalability. The PPA architecture will be optimized for both performance and area efficiency, leveraging the benefits of parallel computation to minimize delay and reduce the number of required gates.
