To simplify the solution, we can use the FullAdder module directly in the TopModule without manually defining the carry signals and sum calculations. This approach reduces code complexity and improves readability. We will instantiate the FullAdder module four times, connecting the carry-out of each full adder to the carry-in of the next one, thus creating a ripple-carry adder. This simplification maintains the functionality of the original design while making it easier to understand and modify.
