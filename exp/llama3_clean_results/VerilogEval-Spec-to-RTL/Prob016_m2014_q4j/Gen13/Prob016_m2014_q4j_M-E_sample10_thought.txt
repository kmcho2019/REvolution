The novel architectural idea for a 4-bit adder with full adders involves using a modular approach with two levels of adders. The first level consists of two 2-bit adders that add the lower and upper bits of the input numbers separately, producing two 3-bit sums and two carry bits. The second level then adds these two sums along with the carry bits using another 3-bit adder. This approach simplifies the design by breaking down the addition into smaller, more manageable parts and reduces the overall complexity of the circuit. Additionally, it can potentially improve performance by reducing the critical path delay compared to a traditional ripple-carry adder or even the carry-lookahead adder used in the previous design.
