To simplify the existing 4-bit adder solution, we can leverage the fact that a 4-bit adder with full adders is essentially a series of connected full adder modules. Each full adder calculates the sum and carry for a bit position based on the inputs from the previous stage's carry-out. We can simplify the logic by directly connecting the outputs of one full adder to the inputs of the next without needing intermediate wires for the carry signals. This reduces the complexity of the code while maintaining the same functionality.
