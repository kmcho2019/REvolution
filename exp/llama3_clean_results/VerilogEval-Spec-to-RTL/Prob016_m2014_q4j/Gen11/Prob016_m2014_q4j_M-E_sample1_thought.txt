To implement a 4-bit adder, I will design a novel architecture that leverages the properties of binary numbers and the concept of bit-level parallelism. The idea is to use a combination of bitwise operations and conditional statements to calculate the sum and overflow in a single step, rather than using a traditional ripple-carry or carry-lookahead approach. This architecture will utilize a hierarchical approach, with the top-level module instantiating a series of bitwise operation modules to perform the addition. Each bitwise operation module will take two input bits and a carry input, and produce a sum output and a carry output. The final carry output will be used as the overflow bit. This approach will reduce the critical path delay and improve the overall performance of the adder.
