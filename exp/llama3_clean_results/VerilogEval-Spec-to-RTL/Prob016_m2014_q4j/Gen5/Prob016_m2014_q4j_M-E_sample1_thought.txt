To create a novel architectural solution for the 4-bit adder, we can utilize a parallel prefix adder architecture. This design takes advantage of the fact that the carry bits can be computed in parallel, reducing the critical path and potentially improving performance. The key idea is to use a tree-like structure to calculate the carry signals, allowing for a more efficient computation of the sum bits. We will use a combination of XOR and AND gates to implement the carry-save adder (CSA) and the final ripple-carry adder (RCA) to compute the sum bits.
