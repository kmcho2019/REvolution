The existing solution is already quite efficient in terms of area and power. However, to further optimize it, I will focus on reducing the number of levels of logic in the adder chain. The current implementation uses a ripple-carry adder, which is straightforward but may not be the most efficient in terms of timing. 

To improve the performance, I will use a carry-lookahead adder architecture. This approach will reduce the critical path delay and improve the overall performance of the adder. 

Additionally, I will ensure that the inputs to the adders are properly synchronized to minimize dynamic power consumption.
