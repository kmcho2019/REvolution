The existing 4-bit adder implementation using full adders can be simplified by removing unnecessary optimizations and focusing on a straightforward implementation. The main goal is to ensure the adder correctly calculates the sum of two 4-bit numbers and includes the overflow bit in the output. This can be achieved by using a simple ripple-carry adder structure, which chains full adders together to propagate the carry signal from one bit position to the next. Each full adder will take two input bits from the numbers being added and the carry from the previous bit position, producing the sum for the current bit position and the carry for the next position.
