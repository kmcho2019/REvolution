The new approach involves utilizing a combination of lookup tables (LUTs) and a carry-save adder (CSA) architecture to implement the 4-bit adder. This method deviates from the traditional ripple-carry adder or full adder-based implementations. By employing LUTs, we can map the input combinations to the corresponding sum and carry values, reducing the need for complex logical operations. The CSA architecture will enable the parallel processing of the input bits, enhancing the overall performance. To further optimize the design, the LUTs will be used in conjunction with the CSA to minimize the critical path delay and reduce the area utilization. This novel approach is expected to provide a more efficient and compact solution for the 4-bit adder, addressing the PPA metrics.
