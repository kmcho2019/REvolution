To simplify the solution, I will utilize a ripple-carry approach instead of the carry-lookahead adder. This approach involves connecting full adders in series, where the carry-out of one full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next. This method is simpler and more intuitive, reducing the complexity of the design. I will define a FullAdder module and then instantiate it within the TopModule, connecting the inputs and outputs in a series to implement the 4-bit adder.
