The existing code implements a 4-bit adder using a ripple-carry approach with full adders, providing a clear and maintainable solution. To improve the PPA metrics, I will focus on reducing the critical path delay, power consumption, and area usage. 
Firstly, I will utilize a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) architecture, which can significantly reduce the critical path delay compared to the ripple-carry approach. 
Secondly, I will minimize the use of XOR/XNOR gates and instead use a combination of AND/OR gates to reduce power consumption. 
Lastly, I will optimize the implementation of the CLA to minimize the area usage. 
By adopting a CLA architecture and optimizing the gate usage, I aim to achieve better PPA metrics while maintaining the same functionality as the original code.
