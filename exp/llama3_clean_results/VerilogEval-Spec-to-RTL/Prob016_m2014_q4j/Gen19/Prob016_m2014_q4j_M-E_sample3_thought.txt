This approach allows us to generate the sum and carry bits in parallel, reducing the critical path delay. 1. Perform bitwise XOR between the input vectors `x` and `y` to get the sum bits without considering the carry.
2. Perform bitwise AND between the input vectors `x` and `y` to get the generate bits for the carry.
3. Perform bitwise OR between the input vectors `x` and `y` to get the propagate bits for the carry.
4. Use the generate and propagate bits to calculate the carry bits.
5. Combine the sum bits and the carry bits to get the final sum.
