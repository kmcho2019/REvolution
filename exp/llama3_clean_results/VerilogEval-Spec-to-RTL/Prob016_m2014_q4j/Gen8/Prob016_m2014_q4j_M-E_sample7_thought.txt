The existing implementation uses a ripple-carry approach, which may lead to a longer critical path. To address this, I propose a novel architectural idea that utilizes a tree-like structure for the carry chain, similar to a carry-lookahead adder, but with a focus on utilizing a combination of half adders and OR gates to generate the carry signals. This approach allows for a more balanced distribution of logic depth across the carry chain, potentially reducing the critical path delay and improving overall performance. By leveraging half adders to generate the sum and carry signals, and then combining these carry signals using OR gates, the new design aims to minimize the number of logic gates required, thus reducing area consumption. Furthermore, this approach enables a more efficient handling of the carry chain, potentially leading to lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity.
