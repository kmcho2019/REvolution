To improve the existing 4-bit adder solution, we should focus on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given the current design's metrics, the main area of improvement lies in reducing the power consumption and potentially minimizing the area usage without compromising the performance. One potential strategy is to apply a more efficient adder architecture, such as a carry-lookahead adder, which can reduce the number of logic gates and minimize switching activity. However, considering the small size of the adder (4 bits), the ripple-carry approach is already relatively efficient. Therefore, the improvement strategy will focus on simplifying the logic within the FullAdder module and ensuring that the adder is only active when necessary. Additionally, we can explore using a more compact representation of the adder to minimize area usage. The goal is to find a balance between power, performance, and area optimization.
