To create a novel architectural idea for the 4-bit adder, I will utilize a prefix tree structure to implement a carry-lookahead adder. This approach will enable the adder to generate the carry signals in parallel, reducing the critical path and improving the overall performance. The prefix tree will be used to compute the carry signals for each bit position, allowing the adder to produce the sum and overflow bits concurrently. By leveraging the parallel nature of the prefix tree, this design will provide a significant improvement in timing performance compared to the traditional ripple-carry adder architecture. Additionally, this approach will also lead to a reduction in power consumption due to the reduced switching activity in the carry chain.
