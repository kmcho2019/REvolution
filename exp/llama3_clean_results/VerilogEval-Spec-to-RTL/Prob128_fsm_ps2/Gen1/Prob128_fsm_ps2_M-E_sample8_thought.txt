To solve this problem, we will implement a novel finite state machine (FSM) architecture in the TopModule that utilizes a counter to keep track of the bytes received since the last byte with in[3]=1. 

The FSM will have three states: IDLE, RECV, and DONE. 

In the IDLE state, the FSM will wait for a byte with in[3]=1 and then transition to the RECV state, resetting the counter.

In the RECV state, the FSM will increment the counter for each byte received and store the byte. 

Once the counter reaches 3, the FSM will transition to the DONE state, signaling the receipt of a message by setting the done signal high. The FSM will then transition back to the IDLE state, ready to search for the next message.

The FSM will also have a reset mechanism to reset the FSM to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
