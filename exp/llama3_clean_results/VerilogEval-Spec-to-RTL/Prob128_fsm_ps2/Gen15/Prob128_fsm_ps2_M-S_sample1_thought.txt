To simplify the provided solution, we can observe that the 'msg_received' signal is only used to delay the assertion of the 'done' signal by one cycle. This functionality can be directly incorporated into the state machine by adding an additional state. The new state, 'DONE', will be entered immediately after receiving the third byte of a message and will assert the 'done' signal. This approach eliminates the need for the 'msg_received' signal and simplifies the combinational logic. By reducing the number of signals and states, we aim to minimize the area and power consumption of the design while maintaining its functionality.
