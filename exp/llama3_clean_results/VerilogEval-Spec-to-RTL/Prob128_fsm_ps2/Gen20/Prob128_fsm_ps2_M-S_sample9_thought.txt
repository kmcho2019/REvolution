To simplify the existing solution, we will focus on reducing the complexity of the finite state machine (FSM) while maintaining its functionality. Our strategy involves:
1. Simplifying the state machine to have only the necessary states, in this case, searching for the start byte and counting the bytes of a message.
2. Removing unnecessary signals and variables that do not contribute to the functionality of the FSM.
3. Ensuring that the sequential logic is optimized for the target technology, reducing unnecessary transitions.

By addressing these areas, we aim to reduce the complexity of the design, specifically targeting reductions in area and power consumption, while maintaining the required functionality.
