To solve this problem, we will implement a finite state machine (FSM) in the TopModule. The FSM will have four states: IDLE, BYTE1, BYTE2, and BYTE3. 

In the IDLE state, the FSM will discard incoming bytes until it sees a byte with in[3]=1. Once such a byte is seen, the FSM will transition to the BYTE1 state and store the byte. 

In the BYTE1 and BYTE2 states, the FSM will store the incoming bytes and transition to the next state. 

In the BYTE3 state, the FSM will store the incoming byte and then signal the receipt of a message by setting the done signal high. The FSM will then transition back to the IDLE state, ready to search for the next message.

The FSM will also have a reset mechanism to reset the FSM to the IDLE state when the reset signal is high.
