To solve this problem, we will implement a finite state machine (FSM) with the following states:
- IDLE: The initial state where the FSM waits for the first byte of a message (in[3]=1) to arrive.
- BYTE1: The state after receiving the first byte of a message.
- BYTE2: The state after receiving the second byte of a message.
- DONE: The state where the FSM signals done after receiving the third byte of a message.
The FSM will transition from IDLE to BYTE1 when in[3]=1, then from BYTE1 to BYTE2 after one clock cycle, from BYTE2 to DONE after one clock cycle, and finally reset to IDLE after signaling done.
