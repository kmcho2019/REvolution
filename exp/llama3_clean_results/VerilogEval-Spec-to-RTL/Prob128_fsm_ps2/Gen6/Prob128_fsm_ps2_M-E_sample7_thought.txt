1.  **Byte Buffer:** A register to store the incoming bytes as they are received.
2.  **FSM:** A simple FSM with states to represent the different stages of message reception (SEARCH, BYTE1, BYTE2, BYTE3).
3.  **Counter:** A counter to keep track of the number of bytes received since the start of a message (indicated by a byte with in[3]=1).

This approach allows for efficient detection of message boundaries and ensures that the 'done' signal is correctly asserted after the third byte of each message.
