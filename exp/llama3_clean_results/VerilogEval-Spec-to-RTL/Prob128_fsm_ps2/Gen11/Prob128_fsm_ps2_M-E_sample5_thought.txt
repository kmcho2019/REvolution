Once such a byte is received, the module transitions to the 'COUNTING' state and starts counting the number of bytes received. After three bytes have been received, the 'done' signal is asserted, and the module transitions back to the 'IDLE' state. The counter is reset when the module is in the 'IDLE' state or when the 'done' signal is asserted.
