To implement the Lemmings state machine, we will define the states based on the walking direction and whether the Lemming is walking, falling, or digging. 
The states can be: WALK_LEFT, WALK_RIGHT, FALLING, DIGGING, SPLATTERED.
Since the Lemming can change direction based on bumps, we need to keep track of the current direction.
When the ground disappears or the Lemming starts digging, it will transition to the FALLING state.
If the ground reappears while the Lemming is falling, it will transition back to its previous walking state.
If the Lemming is digging and the ground disappears, it will transition to the FALLING state.
We also need a counter to track the number of clock cycles the Lemming has been falling.
If this counter exceeds 20 and the ground reappears, the Lemming will splatter.
The outputs will be determined based on the current state.
