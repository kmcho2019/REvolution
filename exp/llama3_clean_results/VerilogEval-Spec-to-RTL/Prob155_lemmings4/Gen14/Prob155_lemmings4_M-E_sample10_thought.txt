To create a novel solution, we can utilize a hybrid approach combining the benefits of both Mealy and Moore state machines. We'll design a state machine that tracks the Lemming's overall state (walking, falling, digging, or splattered) and use a separate controller to manage the walking direction and digging behavior. This approach will allow for a cleaner separation of concerns, easier management of state transitions, and improved scalability.

The state machine will have four main states: WALKING, FALLING, DIGGING, and SPLATTERED. The WALKING state will have two sub-states: WALKING_LEFT and WALKING_RIGHT, which will be controlled by a separate direction controller. The FALLING state will have a counter to track the fall duration, and the DIGGING state will be controlled by a separate digging controller.

This hybrid approach will enable us to efficiently manage the Lemming's behavior, ensuring that it correctly responds to inputs such as bumps, ground, and dig commands, while also accurately modeling the fall duration and splattering behavior.
