To create a novel solution, we will design a module named TopModule that utilizes a structural approach, instantiating two separate modules: one for the assign statement implementation and another for the combinational always block implementation. Each of these modules will be designed to perform the AND gate operation. This approach allows for a clear, modular design where each module has a single responsibility, making the overall design more understandable and maintainable. Additionally, this strategy enables the possibility of reusing these modules in other parts of the design or in completely different projects, enhancing design modularity and reusability.
