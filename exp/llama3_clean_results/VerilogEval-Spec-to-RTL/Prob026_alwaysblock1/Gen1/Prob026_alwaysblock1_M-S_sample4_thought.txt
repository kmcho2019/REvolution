The TopModule can be simplified by maintaining the essential AND gate functionality using both a continuous assignment statement and a combinational always block, but with a focus on clarity and minimalism. The assign statement directly implements the AND operation between inputs 'a' and 'b' and assigns the result to 'out_assign'. The combinational always block, triggered by any change in 'a' or 'b', also computes the AND operation and assigns it to 'out_alwaysblock'. This approach ensures both outputs 'out_assign' and 'out_alwaysblock' reflect the AND operation result without unnecessary complexity.
