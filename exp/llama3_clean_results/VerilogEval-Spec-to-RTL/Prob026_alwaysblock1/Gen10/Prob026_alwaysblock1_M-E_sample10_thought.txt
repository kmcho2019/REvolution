To create a novel solution, we can leverage the concept of a parameterized module that allows for flexible implementation of logical operations. The idea is to design a TopModule that can be easily configured to perform different logical operations, including the AND operation, by utilizing a parameter to select the operation. This approach enables a more versatile and reusable design. We will utilize an always block with a case statement to implement the different logical operations based on the parameter. This design will not only meet the requirements but also provide a framework for easily adding more logical operations in the future.
