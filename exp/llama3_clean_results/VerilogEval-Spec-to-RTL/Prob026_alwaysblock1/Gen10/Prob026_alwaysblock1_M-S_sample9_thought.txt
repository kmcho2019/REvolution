To simplify the given solution, we will maintain the required functionality of implementing an AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block within the TopModule. The previous solution included a separate module for the AND gate, which, while beneficial for larger designs due to modularity, can be simplified in this case by directly implementing the AND operations within the TopModule. This approach reduces complexity by eliminating the need for a separate module and its instantiation, thus minimizing the overall design hierarchy.
