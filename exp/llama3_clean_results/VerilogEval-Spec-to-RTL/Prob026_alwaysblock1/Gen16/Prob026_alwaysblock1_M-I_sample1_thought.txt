To improve the existing solution, we will focus on optimizing the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics. The current design is functionally correct but has potential for improvement, especially in terms of area and power consumption. Our strategy involves simplifying the design by reducing the number of instantiated modules and directly implementing the logic within the TopModule. This approach aims to minimize the area consumption by reducing the overhead associated with module instantiation and potentially lowering power consumption by reducing switching activity. Furthermore, we will maintain the use of both an assign statement and a combinational always block to implement the AND gate operation as required.
