To introduce a novel architectural idea, we will depart from the traditional hierarchical approach and instead focus on exploiting the strengths of SystemVerilog, which is a superset of Verilog. The new idea involves utilizing SystemVerilog's interface and modport features to create a more structured and reusable design. 

The concept revolves around defining an interface for the AND gate, which encapsulates both the assign statement and the combinational always block within a single entity. This interface will be used to connect the AND gate module directly to the TopModule, ensuring a clean and modular design.

Furthermore, to optimize the design for PPA metrics, we will utilize the `always_comb` keyword instead of `always @(*)`, which is more explicit for combinational logic and might offer better optimization opportunities for modern synthesis tools.

The novel aspect of this solution lies in its use of interfaces and the explicit declaration of combinational logic, making the design more readable, maintainable, and potentially more efficient in terms of PPA metrics.
