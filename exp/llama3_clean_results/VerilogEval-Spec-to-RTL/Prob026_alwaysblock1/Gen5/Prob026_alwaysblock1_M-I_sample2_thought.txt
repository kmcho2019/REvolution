The existing solution already implements the AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block as required. To optimize this design for better PPA (Power, Performance, and Area) metrics, the redundancy in implementing the same operation (AND gate) twice can be removed. Since both the assign statement and the always block achieve the same result, one of them can be eliminated. The always block provides more flexibility for complex operations and is generally used when the logic involves more than just simple continuous assignments. In contrast, the assign statement is straightforward and is typically used for simple combinational logic. For a simple AND gate operation, the assign statement is sufficient and more concise, potentially leading to better PPA metrics due to its simplicity and directness. Therefore, the always block can be removed to simplify the design, reduce redundancy, and potentially improve PPA metrics.
