The given solution implements an AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block. However, to improve the PPA metrics, specifically to reduce power consumption, we can eliminate the redundancy in the logic by choosing one method to implement the AND operation. Since the assign statement is more straightforward and directly connects the output to the AND operation of the inputs without the need for a reg declaration, we can remove the always block. This approach not only simplifies the design but also reduces the area required and potentially the power consumption by eliminating unnecessary logic and switching activity. For the always block implementation, it's beneficial for more complex combinational logic where multiple statements are required, but for a simple AND operation, the assign statement is sufficient and more efficient.
