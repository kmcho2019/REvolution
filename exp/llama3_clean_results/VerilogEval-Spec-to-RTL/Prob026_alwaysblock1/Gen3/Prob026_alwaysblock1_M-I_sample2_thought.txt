To improve the given solution, the focus will be on optimizing its Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics while maintaining its functional correctness. The current design uses both an assign statement and a combinational always block to implement an AND gate, which introduces redundancy that could be optimized for better power consumption. The strategy for improvement involves simplifying the design by removing one of the redundant methods for implementing the AND gate, thereby reducing switching activity and potentially improving power consumption. Additionally, ensuring the combinational always block is properly sensitive to all inputs and that the output of the always block is declared as a reg will be maintained to comply with Verilog syntax and semantics. Since the current design is already quite simple, area optimization may be limited, but reducing redundancy will contribute to both power and area efficiency.
