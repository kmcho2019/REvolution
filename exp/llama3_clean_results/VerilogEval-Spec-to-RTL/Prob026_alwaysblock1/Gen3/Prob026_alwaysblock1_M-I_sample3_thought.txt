The provided solution already implements the required functionality, but there are opportunities for improvement in terms of PPA metrics. To optimize the design, we can combine the two separate modules (AndAssign and AndAlways) into a single module (TopModule), thus reducing the overhead of module instantiations. This should lead to improvements in Performance (Timing) and Area efficiency. For Power consumption, we will maintain the simplicity of the current design, as the combinational always block is already minimal. By using a single module, we can also avoid any potential redundancy in logic that might arise from having separate modules for assign and always block implementations.
