To simplify the solution, we can directly implement the AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block within the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate AndGate module. This approach reduces the overall complexity and hierarchy of the design while maintaining its functionality. We retain the use of `@(*)` for the always block's sensitivity list to ensure it's combinational and sensitive to all inputs, and we keep the output of the always block as a reg to comply with Verilog syntax.
