To introduce a novel architectural idea, we will redesign the TopModule by incorporating a multiplexer to dynamically select between the output of an assign statement and a combinational always block. This approach not only meets the original requirements of implementing an AND gate using both an assign statement and a combinational always block but also adds an extra layer of flexibility and reconfigurability to the design.

The key components of this new design are:
1. A multiplexer (MUX) that can select between the output of the assign statement and the combinational always block.
2. A control signal (select) that determines which output the MUX should select.
3. The assign statement and the combinational always block, which are similar to the previous design, implementing the AND gate operation.

By incorporating a multiplexer, this design offers the ability to switch between different implementation methods of the AND gate operation dynamically, which could be useful in scenarios where different conditions or modes of operation require different implementation methods. This could be due to various reasons such as power consumption, performance optimization, or fault tolerance.

Furthermore, this design can be easily extended to include additional implementation methods or even different logical operations, making it a versatile and scalable solution.
