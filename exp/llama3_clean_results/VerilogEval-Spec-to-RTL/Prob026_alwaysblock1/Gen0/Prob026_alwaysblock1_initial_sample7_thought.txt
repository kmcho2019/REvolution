To implement the TopModule, we will use two methods to create an AND gate: 
1. Continuous assignment using the assign statement, where we directly assign the output out_assign to be the AND of inputs a and b.
2. Combinational always block, where we use an if statement or a logical AND operator inside an always block to assign the output out_alwaysblock.
This approach will allow us to compare and understand the differences between these two methods of implementing combinational logic in Verilog.
