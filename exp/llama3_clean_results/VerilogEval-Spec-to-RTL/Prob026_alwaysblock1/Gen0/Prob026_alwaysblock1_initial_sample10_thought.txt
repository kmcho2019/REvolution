To implement the TopModule, we will create two outputs: out_assign and out_alwaysblock. 
The out_assign output will be directly assigned the result of the AND operation between inputs a and b. 
For the out_alwaysblock output, we will use a combinational always block to calculate the AND operation result and assign it to out_alwaysblock. 
This will demonstrate two different methods of achieving the same logical operation within a Verilog module.
