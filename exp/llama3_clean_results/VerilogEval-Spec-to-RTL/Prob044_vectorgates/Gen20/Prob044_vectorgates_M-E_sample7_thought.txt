The new solution involves a shift from the traditional modular approach to a more integrated and pipelined architecture. The idea is to leverage the bitwise-OR operation as a building block and cascade it with the logical-OR and inversion operations. This approach not only reduces the latency by minimizing the module boundaries but also enables a more efficient utilization of resources.

The proposed architecture consists of a single module, `TopModule`, which includes three stages: 
1. Bitwise-OR stage: This stage performs the bitwise-OR operation between the two input vectors `a` and `b`.
2. Logical-OR stage: This stage computes the logical-OR of the two input vectors `a` and `b` using the simplified condition (`(|a) || (|b)`).
3. Inversion stage: This stage generates the inverse of both input vectors `a` and `b` and concatenates them to form the `out_not` signal.

By integrating these stages within a single module, the design aims to achieve a better balance between performance, power consumption, and area utilization. The pipelined architecture allows for a more efficient use of resources, reducing the overall area and power consumption while maintaining a competitive performance.
