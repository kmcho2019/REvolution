To simplify the existing solution, we can eliminate the need for separate Inverter modules by directly computing the inverse of the input vectors within the TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and instances, which can potentially improve area efficiency and reduce power consumption due to lower switching activity.

We will maintain the same functionality as the original solution, computing the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors, the logical-OR of the two vectors, and the inverse of both vectors, but we will perform these operations directly within the TopModule for simplicity and efficiency.
