Our novel approach involves utilizing a single, unified module that performs all required operations within a single clock cycle. This is achieved by leveraging the ability of Verilog to perform multiple operations in parallel within a single always block. To optimize for area, we will leverage the capabilities of the synthesis tool to minimize logic area by ensuring that each operation is implemented in the most efficient manner possible. This involves using direct assignments for all operations, including the bitwise-OR, logical-OR, and NOT operations.

For power optimization, we will focus on reducing switching activity by minimizing the number of signals that change state between clock cycles. This is achieved by performing all operations in a single clock cycle, reducing the need for intermediate storage or buffering.

In terms of timing optimization, we will ensure that the critical path of the design is minimized by performing all operations in parallel. This eliminates the need for sequential logic, reducing the overall delay of the design.
