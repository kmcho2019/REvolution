The new approach involves a single, unified module that combines the bitwise-OR, logical-OR, and inversion operations within a single always block, utilizing a more sequential and algorithmic approach. This can potentially reduce the area and power consumption by minimizing the number of operators and simplifying the combinatorial logic. The bitwise-OR operation will be performed bit-by-bit, and the logical-OR condition will be evaluated based on the presence of any '1' bits in the input vectors. For the inversion operation, instead of directly inverting the entire vectors, a loop will be used to invert each bit individually, potentially reducing switching activity.
