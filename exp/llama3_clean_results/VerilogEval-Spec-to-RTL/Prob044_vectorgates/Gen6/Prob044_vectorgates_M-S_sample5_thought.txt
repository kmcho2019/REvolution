The current implementation of the TopModule can be simplified by removing the separate BitwiseOR module and performing the bitwise OR operation directly within the TopModule. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for an additional module instantiation, which can help in reducing area and power consumption. The logical OR operation can be performed directly on the result of the bitwise OR operation without needing a separate comparison with a zero vector, as suggested in the provided feedback. Additionally, the inversion of vectors a and b can be kept as direct assignments within the TopModule for simplicity and efficiency.
