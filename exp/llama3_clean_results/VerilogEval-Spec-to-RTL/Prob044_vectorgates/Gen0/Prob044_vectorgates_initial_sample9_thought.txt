To implement the TopModule, we need to perform three main operations: 
1. Compute the bitwise-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_or_bitwise'. 
2. Compute the logical-OR of the two input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign the result to 'out_or_logical'. 
3. Compute the bitwise-NOT of both input vectors 'a' and 'b' and assign the results to the upper and lower halves of 'out_not' respectively.
