The module TopModule will have two 3-bit input ports 'a' and 'b'. It will compute the bitwise-OR of 'a' and 'b' and assign it to 'out_or_bitwise'. The logical-OR of 'a' and 'b' will be assigned to 'out_or_logical'. The inverse of 'b' will be assigned to the upper half of 'out_not' (bits [5:3]), and the inverse of 'a' will be assigned to the lower half of 'out_not' (bits [2:0]).
