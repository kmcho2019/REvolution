To simplify the existing solution, we can eliminate the separate Inverter module and directly compute the inverse of the input vectors within the TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing the need for module instantiation and the associated wiring. The bitwise-OR and logical-OR operations can remain as they are, since they are straightforward and do not benefit significantly from being in separate modules. By integrating the inversion operation directly into the TopModule, we can minimize the overall design complexity while maintaining its functionality.
