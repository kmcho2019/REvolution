A novel architectural idea for the TopModule is to utilize a single, unified module that performs all the required operations (bitwise-OR, logical-OR, and NOT) within a single block of combinational logic. This approach can potentially reduce the overall area and power consumption by minimizing the number of instantiated modules and their associated interconnects. By leveraging Verilog's ability to perform complex operations within a single statement, we can express the entire functionality of the TopModule in a concise and efficient manner, potentially leading to improved Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
