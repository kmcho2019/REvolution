A novel architectural approach to implementing the TopModule could involve utilizing a combination of bitwise operations and direct assignments to improve readability and maintainability. This approach would focus on eliminating the need for an always block and instead use continuous assignments to directly drive the outputs. By doing so, the design aims to reduce complexity and potentially improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The idea is to assign the input vector directly to the output vector and then use bitwise operations to extract individual bits from the input vector and assign them to the corresponding output bits. This strategy ensures simplicity, readability, and efficiency in the implementation.
