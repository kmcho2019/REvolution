To develop a novel architectural solution for the given Moore state machine, we will introduce a two-stage pipeline approach. The first stage will handle the state transition logic, and the second stage will generate the output based on the current state. This pipelined architecture can potentially improve the performance by reducing the combinational logic delay and enhancing the clock frequency. Additionally, we will explore using a different encoding for the states to minimize the area usage and power consumption. The reset mechanism will remain synchronous and active-high as per the requirements.
