The novel architectural idea for implementing the 2-to-1 multiplexer is to utilize a single always block that incorporates both the combinational logic for the direct assignment and the procedural assignment. This approach aims to minimize area usage by avoiding the instantiation of separate modules for each type of assignment. Instead, it leverages the versatility of the always block to handle both assignments within a unified structure.

The key concept is to use a single always block with a sensitivity list that includes all the inputs (a, b, sel_b1, sel_b2). Within this block, both the direct assignment logic and the procedural assignment logic are implemented. The direct assignment is achieved through a continuous assignment statement that is conditionally updated based on the selection signals. The procedural assignment, on the other hand, is implemented using a procedural if statement that updates the output based on the same selection criteria.

This unified approach not only reduces the area by minimizing the number of modules and their instantiation overhead but also potentially simplifies the synthesis process by providing a clearer, more straightforward logic structure for the synthesis tool to optimize. Furthermore, by maintaining both assignment types within a single always block, the design becomes more compact and easier to manage, especially for small to medium-sized digital circuits.

In terms of PPA optimization, this approach could lead to improvements in area usage due to the reduced module count and potentially in power consumption as a result of the simplified logic structure. The performance (timing) should remain unaffected or even see minor improvements due to the reduced complexity and the potential for better optimization by the synthesis tool.
