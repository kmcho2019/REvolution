To improve the existing solution, we will focus on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The current design consists of two separate modules for direct and procedural assignment, which might introduce additional delay and increase power consumption due to unnecessary switching activity.

Our improvement strategy involves the following steps:

1.  **Merge Modules:** We will merge the direct and procedural assignment modules into a single module, reducing the overall area consumption and minimizing the delay introduced by module instantiation.
2.  **Optimize Combinational Logic:** We will ensure that the combinational logic is optimized for performance, power, and area. Since the design primarily consists of simple combinational logic (a 2-to-1 mux), we can focus on minimizing the number of gates required and reducing unnecessary switching activity.
3.  **Reduce Redundancy:** We will remove any redundant logic or components to minimize area and power consumption.
4.  **Leverage Synthesis Tools:** We will utilize synthesis tools' capabilities to optimize the design for area, power, and performance.

By implementing these strategies, we aim to create an improved solution that meets the requirements while achieving better PPA metrics.
