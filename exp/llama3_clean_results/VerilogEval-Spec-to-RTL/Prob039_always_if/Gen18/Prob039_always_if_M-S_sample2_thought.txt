To simplify the solution, we will remove the unnecessary modularity introduced by the separate DirectAssignModule and ProceduralAssignModule. Instead, we can directly implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer logic within the TopModule using both an assign statement and an always block. This simplification reduces the overall complexity of the design by eliminating the need for module instantiations and the associated wiring. The logic for choosing between inputs 'a' and 'b' based on the select signals sel_b1 and sel_b2 remains unchanged.
