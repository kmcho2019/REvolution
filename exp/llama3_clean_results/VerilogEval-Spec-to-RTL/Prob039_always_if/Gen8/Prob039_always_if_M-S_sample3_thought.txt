To simplify the existing solution, we will combine the direct and procedural assignment logic into a single module, TopModule, eliminating the need for separate modules. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and the associated instantiations. Within TopModule, we will utilize both an assign statement and an always block to implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer logic, maintaining the original functionality. By simplifying the hierarchy and minimizing the number of modules, we aim to reduce power consumption and maintain area efficiency.
