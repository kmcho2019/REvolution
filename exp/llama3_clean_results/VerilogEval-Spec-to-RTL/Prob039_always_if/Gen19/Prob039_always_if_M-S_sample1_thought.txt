The existing solution can be simplified by eliminating the need for separate modules for direct assignment and procedural assignment. This simplification reduces the overall complexity of the design by removing unnecessary module instantiations and interfaces.

By directly implementing the required logic within a single module, the design becomes more streamlined and easier to understand. The assign statement can be used for the combinational logic implementation of the 2-to-1 multiplexer, and an always block can be used for the procedural implementation. This approach simplifies the design while maintaining the functionality of both direct and procedural assignments.

Furthermore, this simplified design should also improve Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. With fewer modules and less complexity, the potential for reduced power consumption, improved performance, and smaller area usage exists.
