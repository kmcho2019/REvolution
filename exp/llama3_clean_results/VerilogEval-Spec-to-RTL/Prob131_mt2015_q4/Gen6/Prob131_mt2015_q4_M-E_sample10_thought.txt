The novel architectural idea involves reorganizing the TopModule to utilize a more hierarchical and reusable structure. Instead of directly instantiating ModuleA and ModuleB within the TopModule, we create intermediate modules that encapsulate the OR and AND operations. This allows for better modularity and scalability. Furthermore, we introduce a new ModuleC that simplifies the boolean function by factoring out common terms, reducing the overall complexity of the design. This approach enables easier maintenance, modification, and optimization of the individual components without affecting the overall system.
