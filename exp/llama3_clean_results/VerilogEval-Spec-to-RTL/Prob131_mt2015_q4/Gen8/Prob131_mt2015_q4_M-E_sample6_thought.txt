The new architectural idea involves utilizing a single multiplexer (MUX) to select between the outputs of ModuleA and ModuleB based on the input values of 'x' and 'y'. This approach eliminates the need for separate OR and AND gates, as well as the XOR gate, simplifying the combinatorial logic and potentially reducing area usage and power consumption. By using a MUX, we can directly select the desired output based on the input conditions, streamlining the logic and improving performance.
