A novel architectural idea for the TopModule is to utilize a single flip-flop with an asynchronous reset and a conditional assignment based on the input signals L, E, R, and w. This approach allows for a more concise design and can potentially lead to better area efficiency. The flip-flop will be used to store the current state of Q, and the conditional assignment will be used to update Q based on the control signals. This design takes advantage of the Verilog's ability to perform conditional assignments within the sequential logic block, reducing the need for explicit combinational logic.
