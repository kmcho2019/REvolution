The existing solution can be simplified by removing the intermediate signal `D` and directly assigning the value to `Q` inside the `always` block. This approach eliminates the need for an extra signal and reduces the combinational logic. The `always` block will still capture the sequential behavior, ensuring that `Q` updates only on the positive edge of the clock.

By directly assigning the value to `Q` based on the conditions, we maintain the same functionality as the original design but with fewer signals and potentially less area usage.
