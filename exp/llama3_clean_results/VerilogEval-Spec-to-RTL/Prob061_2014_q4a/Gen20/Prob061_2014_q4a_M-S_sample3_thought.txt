The current implementation uses a multiplexer-like approach with a case statement to determine the next value of Q. However, this can be simplified by directly using the control signals (L and E) in the always block that updates Q. This approach eliminates the need for the select signal and its associated combinational logic, potentially simplifying the design and improving area efficiency. By leveraging the conditional statements within the clocked always block, we can directly express the desired behavior of the shift register stage without the need for a multiplexer or an additional signal to encode the selection.
