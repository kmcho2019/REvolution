To introduce a novel architectural idea, we can design the module using a mux-based approach instead of conditional assignments. This can potentially simplify the logic and make it more efficient in terms of area and power consumption. The idea is to use a multiplexer to select the next state of `q_reg` based on the input conditions. When `L` is asserted, the multiplexer selects `R` as the next state. When `L` is not asserted but `E` is, the multiplexer selects `w` as the next state. Otherwise, the multiplexer selects the current state of `q_reg`, effectively retaining its value. This approach can lead to a more streamlined and possibly more efficient implementation.
