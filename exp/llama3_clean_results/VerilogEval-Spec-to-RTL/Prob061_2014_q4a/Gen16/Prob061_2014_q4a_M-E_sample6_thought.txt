A novel approach to designing the TopModule could involve utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) to select between the input from the previous stage (w) and the load value (R), based on the control signals. This design leverages the enable signal (E) and the load signal (L) to decide whether to shift or load the value. By using a MUX, the conditional logic within the always block can be simplified, potentially improving the overall timing and area efficiency of the design. The idea revolves around exploiting the properties of digital multiplexers to streamline the data selection process, thereby achieving the same functionality with a possibly more optimized structure.
