To simplify the existing solution, we can eliminate the need for the internal "hold" signal and directly incorporate the conditional logic into the clocked process. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design by removing the combinational logic block and the associated register, thereby potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The main idea is to directly update the output Q based on the inputs E, L, R, and w within a single always block, triggered on the positive edge of the clock. This approach minimizes the number of conditional statements and reduces the overall area by eliminating unnecessary logic and registers.
