The new solution introduces a novel architectural idea by utilizing a 2:1 multiplexer to handle the selection between the load value and the previous stage's input, and a D flip-flop to store the output. The multiplexer is controlled by the enable signal E, and the load signal L is used to decide whether to load the value R or the selected input from the multiplexer. This approach allows for a clean and modular design, making it easier to understand and analyze. Additionally, it provides a clear separation of concerns between the multiplexing and the storage of the output, which can lead to better optimization opportunities during synthesis.
