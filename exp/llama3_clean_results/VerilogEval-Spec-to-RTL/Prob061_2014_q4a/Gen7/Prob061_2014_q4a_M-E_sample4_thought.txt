The new solution introduces a novel architectural idea by utilizing a single D flip-flop and a combinational logic block to handle the selection and loading of the output Q. The combinational logic block will generate the next state of Q based on the inputs w, R, E, and L. This approach eliminates the need for a multiplexer and instead uses a simple logical expression to determine the next state of Q. This design is more compact and could potentially lead to area and power savings.
