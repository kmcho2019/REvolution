The new solution introduces a novel architectural idea by utilizing a single D flip-flop with an asynchronous reset and a 3:1 multiplexer to handle the selection between the load value, the previous stage's input, and the current output. This approach eliminates the need for internal signal sel and allows for a more direct control over the next state of Q. The 3:1 multiplexer is controlled by the enable signal E and the load signal L, providing a flexible and efficient way to manage the different operational modes of the module. By using an asynchronous reset, the module can immediately respond to the load signal, ensuring that the load operation is prioritized.
