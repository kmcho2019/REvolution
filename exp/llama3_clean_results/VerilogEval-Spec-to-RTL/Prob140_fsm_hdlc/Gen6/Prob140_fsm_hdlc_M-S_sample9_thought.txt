To simplify the existing solution, we can take advantage of the fact that the state transitions and output signals are highly dependent on the number of consecutive ones encountered. Instead of using a case statement with multiple states, we can utilize counters to keep track of the number of consecutive ones and directly derive the output signals based on this count. This approach simplifies the logic, reduces the number of states, and potentially leads to better performance and area efficiency. The idea is to maintain a counter that increments when a '1' is encountered and resets when a '0' is encountered, triggering the appropriate output signals when the counter reaches specific thresholds.
