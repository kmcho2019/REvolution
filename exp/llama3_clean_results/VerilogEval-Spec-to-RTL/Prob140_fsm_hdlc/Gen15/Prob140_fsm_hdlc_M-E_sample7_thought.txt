The novel architectural idea involves using a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of a finite state machine (FSM) with the efficiency of a counter-based mechanism. The design will utilize a counter to track the consecutive ones and a separate FSM to manage the overall state and output signals. This approach aims to reduce the number of states in the FSM, thereby simplifying the combinational logic and potentially improving timing and area efficiency.

The counter will be used to track the number of consecutive ones, and the FSM will be responsible for detecting the specific sequences (0111110, 01111110, and 01111111...) and generating the output signals accordingly. By separating the counter and FSM logic, the design can take advantage of the strengths of each approach, leading to a more efficient implementation.

To further optimize the design, we will use a one-hot encoding scheme for the FSM states, which typically results in simpler and faster combinational logic. Additionally, the output signals (disc, flag, and err) will be generated based on the current state and counter value, minimizing the need for complex logic and reducing power consumption.
