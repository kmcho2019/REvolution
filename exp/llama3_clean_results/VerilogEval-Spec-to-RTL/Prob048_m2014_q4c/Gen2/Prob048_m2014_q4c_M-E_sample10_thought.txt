To implement a D flip flop with active high synchronous reset, a novel approach could involve using a latch-based design, where two back-to-back latches are used to create the flip-flop behavior. This design would use two separate clock phases: one for the master latch and a complementary phase for the slave latch. The reset signal would be applied to both latches. This design can offer a different perspective on implementing a basic flip-flop, although it might not necessarily be more efficient in terms of area or power compared to the traditional edge-triggered flip-flop implementation.
