To simplify the given solution, we focus on the essential functionality of a D flip-flop with an active high synchronous reset. The key elements are the clock sensitivity, the conditional assignment based on the reset signal, and the assignment of the input data when reset is not asserted. We maintain the use of non-blocking assignments to ensure synchronous operation and prevent potential race conditions. The reset condition directly sets the output to 0, and otherwise, it assigns the input data to the output at the positive edge of the clock. Given the simplicity of the design, we also consider implicit initialization of the output to minimize code, relying on the synthesis tool to handle the initial state appropriately.
