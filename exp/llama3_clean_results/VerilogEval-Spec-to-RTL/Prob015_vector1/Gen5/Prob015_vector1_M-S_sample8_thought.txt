To simplify the existing solution, we recognize that the essence of the task is to split a 16-bit input into two 8-bit outputs: the upper byte (`out_hi`) and the lower byte (`out_lo`). This can be achieved directly without the need for a parameterized module like `BitSplitter`. We can directly assign the bits of the input to the output ports within the `TopModule` itself, eliminating the need for an additional module instantiation. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
