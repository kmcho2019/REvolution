To simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality, we can eliminate the need for a parameterized module and directly implement the required logic within the TopModule. This simplification reduces the overall complexity by removing the instantiation of a separate module and directly assigning the input bits to the output ports. The basic idea is to use direct assignments to divide the input into two parts: the upper byte (`out_hi`) and the lower byte (`out_lo`), thus maintaining the required functionality with reduced complexity.
