The existing solution already utilizes continuous assignment statements, which is the most straightforward and efficient way to implement this functionality in Verilog. However, the port names can be considered for a slight adjustment to better reflect their purpose. Instead of `out_hi` and `out_lo`, we could use `out_upper` and `out_lower` to explicitly indicate that they represent the upper and lower bytes of the input, respectively. This change does not affect the functionality but enhances readability and understandability.
