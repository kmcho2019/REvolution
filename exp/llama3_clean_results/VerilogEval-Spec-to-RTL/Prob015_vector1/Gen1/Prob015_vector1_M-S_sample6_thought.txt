The existing solution is already quite simple and effectively utilizes Verilog's bit slicing feature to split the 16-bit input into two 8-bit outputs. However, to simplify it further, we can consider the naming convention of the output ports. The names `out_hi` and `out_lo` seem to be counterintuitive given their assignments. Typically, `out_lo` would represent the lower bits, and `out_hi` would represent the higher bits. Thus, a simplification could involve renaming these ports for clarity, but in terms of logic, the current implementation is already minimal and efficient.
