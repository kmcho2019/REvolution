The novel approach is to implement a barrel shifter-like mechanism but instead of shifting, we directly extract the bits required for the upper and lower bytes using a combination of bit manipulation and concatenation. This approach avoids the need for explicit bit slicing and instead uses the Verilog concatenation operator to combine the bits into the desired output formats. The idea leverages the flexibility of Verilog's bit manipulation capabilities to achieve the byte splitting in a unique and potentially more area-efficient manner.

By utilizing the concatenation operator and bit manipulation, the design avoids the use of traditional slicing methods, potentially reducing the area required for the combinatorial logic.
