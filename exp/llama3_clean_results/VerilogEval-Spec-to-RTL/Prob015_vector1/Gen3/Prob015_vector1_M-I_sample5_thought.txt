To improve the existing solution, the focus will be on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given that the current design is functionally correct and purely combinational, the primary strategy for improvement will be to reduce the overhead associated with the parameterized module and potentially simplify the design to minimize area and power consumption. Since the input width and split point are fixed, a non-parameterized version of the module can be considered to reduce complexity. Additionally, instead of using a submodule, the logic can be directly incorporated into the TopModule to minimize interfaces and switching activity, which could help in reducing power consumption and area.
