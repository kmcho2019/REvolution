To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we need to design a shift register using four D flip-flops. The shift register will be clocked on the positive edge of the clk signal and will have a synchronous active-low reset. When resetn is low, the shift register will be reset to zero. When resetn is high, the shift register will shift the input in to the right on every positive clock edge, effectively creating a four-bit delay line.
