The novel architectural idea is to implement the shift register using a single 4-bit register and a counter to keep track of the shift position. This approach allows for more flexibility and can potentially reduce the area usage by minimizing the number of flip-flops required. The counter will be used to select the bit position to shift in the new input, and the register will be updated accordingly. This design can also be easily extended to support larger shift registers by simply increasing the size of the counter and the register.
