The new solution involves a modular approach that utilizes a chain of four individual D flip-flops to implement the shift register. This approach allows for a clear and straightforward implementation of the shift register functionality, making it easier to understand and debug. Each D flip-flop is designed with an active-low synchronous reset, ensuring that the entire shift register can be reset to a known state when required. The outputs of each D flip-flop are connected in series, creating a shift register that can store and shift the input data one bit at a time. This approach provides a simple and efficient way to implement a shift register with four stages.
