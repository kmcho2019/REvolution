A simplified architectural idea for an adder-subtractor with a zero flag is to directly perform the addition or subtraction based on the operation mode and then check the result for zero. This approach eliminates the need for a complex carry-lookahead adder structure and dedicated zero detection circuit, instead relying on straightforward combinational logic. The operation mode (addition or subtraction) can be controlled by a simple conditional statement that inverts the second operand and sets the carry input accordingly. The result can then be directly checked for zero using a logical operation.
