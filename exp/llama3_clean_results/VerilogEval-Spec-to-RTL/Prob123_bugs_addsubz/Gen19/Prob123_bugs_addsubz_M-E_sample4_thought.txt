To create a novel solution for the adder-subtractor with a zero flag, I propose a design that utilizes a hybrid approach, combining the efficiency of a ripple-carry adder with the speed of a parallel prefix adder. This design, dubbed "Hybrid Adder-Subtractor" (HAS), will employ a two-stage architecture.

In the first stage, a ripple-carry adder will be used to perform the addition and subtraction operations. This will allow for efficient handling of the carry propagation. The second stage will utilize a parallel prefix adder to accelerate the computation of the sum and the zero flag.

The HAS design will also incorporate a novel technique for calculating the zero flag, which will be computed in parallel with the sum. This technique, called "Zero Flag Prediction" (ZFP), will use a separate circuit to predict whether the result of the addition or subtraction will be zero, based on the input operands.

The ZFP circuit will be designed to minimize area and power consumption, while maintaining high accuracy. The predicted zero flag will be used to gate the output of the adder, reducing power consumption and improving overall efficiency.

The HAS design will also include a control unit to manage the operation selection and the flow of data between the different stages. This control unit will be responsible for selecting the correct operation (addition or subtraction) and for generating the necessary control signals for the adder and the ZFP circuit.
