The new approach involves designing a configurable arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that can perform addition and subtraction operations and includes a zero flag. The key idea is to utilize a single ALU module that can be instantiated with different operand widths, making it scalable and reusable. 

The ALU will use a single assignment for the output and another for the zero flag, simplifying the code and potentially improving synthesis results. For calculating the output, it will use a conditional statement to decide between addition and subtraction based on the `do_sub` signal.

To generate the `result_is_zero` flag, it will use a bitwise NOR operation on the output, setting the flag high if all bits of the output are zero. This approach will ensure that the design is efficient, scalable, and easy to understand.

Additionally, the ALU will be designed with a focus on power, performance, and area (PPA) optimization. It will utilize combinational logic with immediate assignments to minimize the critical path delay, reduce switching activity to lower power consumption, and maintain a compact design to minimize area.
