One novel architectural idea for an adder-subtractor with a zero flag is to utilize a carry-lookahead adder (CLA) structure for faster arithmetic operations and a dedicated zero detection circuit. The CLA can be designed to perform both addition and subtraction efficiently by conditionally inverting the second operand and the carry input based on the operation mode. The zero detection can be achieved using a NOR tree or a priority encoder to check if all output bits are zero. This approach can potentially offer improved performance and area efficiency compared to traditional ripple-carry adders or simple combinational logic.

Furthermore, to enhance the design, the CLA can be pipelined to reduce the critical path delay, allowing for higher clock frequencies. The zero detection circuit can also be optimized to minimize the number of gates and reduce the propagation delay. By combining these strategies, a high-performance adder-subtractor with a zero flag can be developed.
