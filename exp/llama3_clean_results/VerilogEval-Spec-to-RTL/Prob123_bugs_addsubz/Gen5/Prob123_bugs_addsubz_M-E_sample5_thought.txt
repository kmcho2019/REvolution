A novel approach to implementing the adder-subtractor with a zero flag is to utilize a single arithmetic logic unit (ALU) that can perform both addition and subtraction. This ALU can be designed using a combination of full adders and a multiplexer to select between the two operations. Additionally, the zero flag can be generated by checking the output of the ALU and setting the flag accordingly. This approach simplifies the overall design and reduces the number of logic gates required, making it more efficient in terms of area and power consumption.
