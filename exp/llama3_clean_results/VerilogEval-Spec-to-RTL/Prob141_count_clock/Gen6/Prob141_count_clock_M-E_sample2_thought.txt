A novel approach to designing the TopModule is to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) that manages the seconds, minutes, and hours counters. The FSM will be responsible for updating the counters based on the enable signal and handling the reset functionality.

Each state in the FSM will correspond to a specific time interval, allowing the module to accurately keep track of time. The seconds counter will be incremented every time the enable signal is asserted, and the minutes and hours counters will be updated accordingly.

To ensure a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, the FSM will also keep track of the current hour and adjust the AM/PM signal accordingly. The BCD format for the hours, minutes, and seconds will be maintained by using separate registers for each time component.

This approach will simplify the design, reduce the number of lines of code, and improve readability. It will also allow for easy modification and extension of the module's functionality in the future.
