To implement the 12-hour clock with AM/PM indication, I propose a modular approach that separates the time tracking into seconds, minutes, and hours counters. The counters will be designed to handle the BCD conversion and wrap-around logic internally. 

The `ena` signal will be used to increment the seconds counter, which will then trigger the minutes and hours counters to update accordingly. The `reset` signal will be used to reset all counters to their initial values.

To simplify the BCD conversion, I will use a separate module for each counter, allowing for easier management and modification of the code. The `pm` signal will be generated based on the hour value, and the output signals (`hh`, `mm`, `ss`) will be updated accordingly.

The use of separate modules for each counter will also facilitate PPA optimization by allowing for more efficient implementation and reduction of unnecessary logic.
