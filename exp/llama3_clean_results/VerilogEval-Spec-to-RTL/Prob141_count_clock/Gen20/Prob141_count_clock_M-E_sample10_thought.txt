The novel architectural idea involves designing a hierarchical counter system with separate modules for hours, minutes, and seconds. Each module will be responsible for its own counting and BCD conversion, and will communicate with the top-level module through well-defined interfaces. The top-level module will manage the enable signal, reset, and AM/PM indicator, and will provide the necessary inputs to the hour, minute, and second modules.

The hour module will count from 1 to 12, with a special case for wrapping around from 12 to 1 (AM to PM or PM to AM). The minute and second modules will count from 0 to 59. The BCD conversion will be performed within each module, allowing for a more modular and maintainable design.

To implement this idea, we will create separate modules for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with its own internal counter and BCD conversion logic. The top-level module will instantiate these modules and manage the overall clock logic.
