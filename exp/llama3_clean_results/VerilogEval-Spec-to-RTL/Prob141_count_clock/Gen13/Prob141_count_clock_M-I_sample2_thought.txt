To improve the existing solution, we need to address the issues mentioned in the feedback and analysis. The main problems are:

1. Incorrect initialization of hours.
2. Incorrect determination of the PM indicator.
3. Incorrect handling of the wrap-around from 12 to 1 for the hours counter.

Our improvement strategy will be to:

1. Correctly initialize the hours to 12 (12:00:00 AM) during reset.
2. Correctly determine the PM indicator based on the hours.
3. Correctly handle the wrap-around from 12 to 1 for the hours counter.

Additionally, we can optimize the design for better power, performance, and area (PPA) by using a more efficient clock gating strategy and considering the use of Gray code counters.

We will also ensure that the minutes and seconds counters are correctly implemented and that the entire design is syntactically correct and functional.
