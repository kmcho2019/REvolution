To design a 12-hour clock with BCD counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, along with an AM/PM indicator, we can employ a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will manage the clock's state transitions based on the seconds, minutes, and hours counters. This approach allows for a modular and scalable design, making it easier to implement and maintain the clock's functionality.

The FSM will have two main states: "AM" and "PM". The clock will start in the "AM" state and transition to the "PM" state when the hour reaches 12. The FSM will also handle the hour, minute, and second increments, ensuring that the clock resets correctly when the seconds, minutes, or hours reach their maximum values.

We can use a hierarchical design approach, breaking down the clock into smaller modules, each responsible for a specific function. The top-level module will contain the FSM and the counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters will be designed using separate modules, each with its own reset and increment logic.

To optimize the design for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, we can use clock gating to reduce power consumption and minimize the switching activity of the output signals. We can also use efficient coding techniques, such as using operators and built-in functions, to reduce the design's area and improve its performance.
