To implement a 12-hour clock with counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, along with an AM/PM indicator, I will design a modular architecture that separates the clock's state machine from the output conversion logic. The state machine will handle the incrementation of seconds, minutes, and hours, as well as the toggling of the AM/PM indicator. The output conversion logic will then translate the internal state into the corresponding BCD representations for hours, minutes, and seconds.

The state machine will utilize a hierarchical approach, with separate modules for managing seconds, minutes, and hours. Each module will handle the incrementation and wrap-around logic for its respective counter. The AM/PM indicator will be toggled based on the hours counter.

The output conversion logic will utilize a combination of bit manipulation and concatenation to generate the BCD representations for hours, minutes, and seconds. The 'hh' output will require special handling to ensure correct BCD representation for hours (01-12).

This modular architecture will allow for easier maintenance, modification, and optimization of the design. By separating the state machine from the output conversion logic, the design becomes more scalable and adaptable to future changes or requirements.
