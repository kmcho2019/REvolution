To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we can utilize a modular approach, breaking down the clock into its constituent parts: hours, minutes, and seconds. We'll use separate counters for each of these components, with each counter being responsible for its own increment and wrap-around logic. The hours counter will be responsible for toggling the AM/PM flag when it reaches 12. The minutes and seconds counters will reset to 0 when they reach 60. We'll also implement a BCD encoder to ensure the output is in Binary-Coded Decimal format. The clock will be reset to 12:00 AM when the reset signal is high. The enable signal will be used to increment the clock once per second.
