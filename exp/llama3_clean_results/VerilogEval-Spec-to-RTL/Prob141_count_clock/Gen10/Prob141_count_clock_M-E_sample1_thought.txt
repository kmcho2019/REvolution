A novel approach to designing the 12-hour clock module involves using a single counter to keep track of the total number of seconds elapsed, and then deriving the hours, minutes, and seconds from this total. This approach simplifies the carry logic and makes the design more modular.

The key components of this design are:

1. **Total Seconds Counter**: A 32-bit counter that increments every second (when the `ena` signal is high).
2. **Time Calculation**: A combinational logic block that calculates the hours, minutes, and seconds from the total seconds count.
3. **AM/PM Indicator**: A simple logic block that determines whether the current time is AM or PM based on the hour value.
4. **Reset Logic**: A synchronous reset that resets the total seconds counter to 0 when the `reset` signal is high.

This design is more efficient and easier to implement than the original solution, and it reduces the complexity of the carry logic between counters.
