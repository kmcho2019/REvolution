To implement a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator, we can utilize a modular approach that focuses on breaking down the clock's functionality into smaller, manageable units. Our novel architectural idea involves designing a clock controller module that handles the incrementation of hours, minutes, and seconds, while also managing the AM/PM flag. This controller will be responsible for generating the correct BCD (Binary-Coded Decimal) outputs for the hours, minutes, and seconds.

Our design will consist of a central clock controller that receives the enable signal and reset, and outputs the updated time values. The time values will be stored in internal registers, which will be updated based on the enable signal and the current time. We will use a separate module to convert the binary time values into BCD format for the output.

This modular design approach simplifies the overall architecture, making it easier to manage and debug. It also allows for easier modification and expansion of the clock's functionality in the future.
