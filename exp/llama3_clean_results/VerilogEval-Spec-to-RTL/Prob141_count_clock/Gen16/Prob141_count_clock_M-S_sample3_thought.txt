To simplify the existing 12-hour clock design in Verilog, we will maintain the core functionality while reducing unnecessary complexity. This can be achieved by streamlining the hour, minute, and second counter logic, as well as simplifying the BCD conversion process.

We will utilize a single counter for each time unit (seconds, minutes, hours), and manage the roll-over logic within the same counter module, eliminating the need for separate conditional checks for each unit. The AM/PM indicator will be toggled at noon and midnight, based on the hour value.

Additionally, the BCD conversion will be simplified by utilizing arithmetic operations to directly calculate the BCD representations of hours, minutes, and seconds. This approach will minimize the complexity of the conversion logic, making it more efficient and easier to understand.

The reset functionality will remain unchanged, ensuring that the clock resets to "12:00 AM" upon assertion of the reset signal. By simplifying the design, we aim to improve code readability, reduce the risk of errors, and enhance the overall maintainability of the Verilog code.
