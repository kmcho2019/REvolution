The novel architectural idea is to utilize a single counter for the entire clock system, which increments every second. This counter will be divided into three sections: seconds, minutes, and hours. Each section will have its own logic to handle the wrapping around and the AM/PM toggle. 

The seconds section will be a 6-bit counter (0-59), the minutes section will also be a 6-bit counter (0-59), and the hours section will be a 5-bit counter (0-11) with an additional bit to handle the AM/PM indicator. 

The benefits of this approach include reduced complexity, as there is only one counter, and improved scalability, as additional features like alarm functionality can be easily added. 

Moreover, to minimize switching activity and optimize PPA, the design will be structured to only update the output values when necessary, i.e., when the counter increments. 

The reset logic will be simplified, setting the entire counter to zero and the AM/PM indicator to AM. 

To achieve better PPA, the design will utilize bitwise operations for the BCD conversion and will minimize the number of combinational logic gates.
