A novel approach to designing the 12-hour clock module involves utilizing a hybrid architecture that combines the benefits of both counters and a modular arithmetic approach to handle the time increments and AM/PM indication. This design leverages a central time register that stores the total number of seconds since the last reset, which is then converted into hours, minutes, and seconds in the 12-hour format with AM/PM indication.

The key components of this design are:

1. **Time Register**: A counter that accumulates the total number of seconds since the last reset, which serves as the central reference for calculating the hours, minutes, and seconds.
2. **Modular Arithmetic**: Utilizing modular arithmetic to calculate the hours, minutes, and seconds from the total seconds, ensuring accurate wrapping around 24 hours and correct AM/PM indication.
3. **AM/PM Logic**: A dedicated logic block that determines the AM/PM state based on the calculated hours, ensuring correct indication.
4. **BCD Conversion**: A module that converts the decimal hours, minutes, and seconds into Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) format for output.
5. **Reset and Enable Logic**: A control block that handles the reset and enable signals, ensuring the time register and output signals are correctly updated.

This design provides a robust, scalable, and modular implementation of the 12-hour clock, allowing for easy maintenance and modification.
