A novel approach to designing the 12-hour clock module involves using separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with its own logic for wrapping and AM/PM indication. This design focuses on simplicity, readability, and accurate timekeeping.

The key components of this design are:

1. **Hours Counter**: A 4-bit counter that increments hours from 1 to 12, with logic to handle AM/PM transitions.
2. **Minutes and Seconds Counters**: 6-bit counters that increment minutes and seconds from 0 to 59, wrapping around to 0 when reaching 60.
3. **AM/PM Logic**: A simple logic block that determines whether the current time is AM or PM based on the hour value.
4. **Reset Logic**: A synchronous reset that resets the counters and AM/PM logic to their initial states when the `reset` signal is high.
5. **Enable Logic**: A logic block that generates enable signals for the counters based on the `ena` signal.

This design provides a straightforward and efficient implementation of the 12-hour clock, making it easy to understand and maintain.
