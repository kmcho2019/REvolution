The novel idea is to use three separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with its own logic for incrementing and wrapping around. The hours counter will have a special condition to handle the 12-hour format, where it wraps around from 12 to 1 at the end of the day. The minutes and seconds counters will wrap around from 59 to 0. The AM/PM indicator will be updated based on the hour. This approach eliminates the need for division and modulus operations, reducing the computational complexity and improving the PPA metrics.

The architecture will consist of three main blocks: the seconds counter, the minutes counter, and the hours counter. Each counter will have its own enable signal, which will be derived from the `ena` input and the wrap-around conditions of the previous counter. The outputs of the counters will be converted to BCD format using a simple encoding scheme.

The reset condition will be handled by resetting all counters to their initial values, which will be "12:00:00 AM". The AM/PM indicator will be updated based on the hour, with a special condition to handle the case where the hour is 12.
