The novel architectural idea for the 12-hour clock design involves utilizing separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds. The counters will be clocked by a fast-running `clk`, with a pulse on `ena` whenever the clock should increment (i.e., once per second). 

The hours, minutes, and seconds will be represented as separate counters, each with its own wrapping logic. The hours counter will have 12 possible values (01-12), while the minutes and seconds counters will have 60 possible values (00-59). 

To handle the AM/PM logic, a separate variable `am_pm` will be used to keep track of the current AM/PM state. When the hours counter reaches 12, the `am_pm` state will be toggled. 

The reset functionality will be handled by a simple if statement, which will reset all counters and the AM/PM indicator to their initial values when the `reset` signal is high.
