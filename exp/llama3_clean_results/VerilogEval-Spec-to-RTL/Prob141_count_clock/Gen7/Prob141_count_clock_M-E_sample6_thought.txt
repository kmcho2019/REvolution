To create a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, I will focus on designing a modular and scalable architecture that can efficiently handle the requirements of a 12-hour clock with AM/PM indicator. 

Firstly, I will separate the clock into three distinct modules: Hours, Minutes, and Seconds. Each module will be responsible for incrementing and resetting its respective counter. 

Secondly, I will introduce a centralized controller module that will manage the interactions between the three counter modules. The controller will handle the wrap-around conditions, reset logic, and the AM/PM indicator.

Lastly, I will implement a decoder module that will convert the internal counter values to the required BCD format for the output signals.

This modular architecture will allow for easier maintenance, debugging, and scalability, making it a more efficient and reliable solution.
