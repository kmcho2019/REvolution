To implement the TopModule, we need to design a set of counters for hours, minutes, and seconds that are triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and are enabled by the ena signal. We also need to include a reset mechanism that resets the clock to "12:00 AM" when the reset signal is asserted. We will use a binary-coded decimal (BCD) representation for the hours, minutes, and seconds. We will also include a pm indicator that is asserted when the clock is in PM mode. The logic will be designed to handle the 12-hour clock format with AM/PM indicator.
