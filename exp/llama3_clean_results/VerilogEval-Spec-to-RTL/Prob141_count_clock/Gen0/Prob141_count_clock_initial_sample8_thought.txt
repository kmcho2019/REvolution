To implement the TopModule, we need to design a set of counters that can handle the 12-hour clock functionality. We will use separate counters for hours, minutes, and seconds, each with the necessary logic to handle carry-over and wrapping. The enable signal will be used to increment the counters once per second, while the reset signal will reset the clock to 12:00 AM. We will also use a pm flag to indicate whether the clock is in AM or PM mode. The hours counter will wrap around from 12 to 1 after reaching 12, and the minutes and seconds counters will reset to 0 after reaching 59.
