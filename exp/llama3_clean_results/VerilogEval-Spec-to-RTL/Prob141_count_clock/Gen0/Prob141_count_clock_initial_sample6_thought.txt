To design the TopModule as a 12-hour clock, we'll implement separate counters for seconds, minutes, and hours. Each counter will be driven by the enable signal (ena), which increments the counters once per second. The counters will reset to their initial values when the reset signal is asserted. The counters will be implemented using Binary-Coded Decimal (BCD) representation. For the hours counter, we'll ensure that it wraps around to 1 after reaching 12. The pm output will be driven by the hours value, asserting pm when the hours value is greater than or equal to 12 and deasserting it otherwise.
