A novel approach to implementing the finite state machine is to utilize a shift register to track the values of 'w' in the three consecutive clock cycles after transitioning to state B. By shifting in the current value of 'w' and shifting out the oldest value, we can efficiently track the history of 'w' values without the need for explicit counters. This approach simplifies the logic and reduces the number of variables required. The FSM can then examine the contents of the shift register to determine if 'w' was 1 exactly twice in the three clock cycles and set 'z' accordingly.
