The new solution introduces a pipelined approach to handling the updates of the history register. This involves breaking down the update process into stages, allowing for a more efficient use of clock cycles and potentially improving the overall performance of the module. 

The idea is to use a two-stage pipeline: the first stage handles the prediction and misprediction detection, determining whether to update the history register based on a prediction or a misprediction. The second stage then performs the actual update, either shifting in the predicted taken or loading the trained history and actual result.

This pipelined approach can help reduce the combinational path delay within each stage, as the logic for detection and update is separated. Additionally, it allows for a more modular design, where each stage can be optimized independently.

To further optimize the design, the use of registered outputs for intermediate results can help reduce the area required for the combinational logic. By registering the output of each stage, the design can take advantage of the clock cycle to perform complex operations without increasing the critical path delay.
