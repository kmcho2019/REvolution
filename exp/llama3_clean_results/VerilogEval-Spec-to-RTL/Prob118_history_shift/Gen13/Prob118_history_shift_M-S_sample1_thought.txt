The existing solution can be simplified further by maintaining a clean and straightforward approach to updating the history register. Since the history is either updated based on a prediction, a misprediction, or remains unchanged, a direct conditional assignment within the always block can encapsulate this logic efficiently. The predict_history can directly reflect the state of the history register, eliminating the need for additional temporary or intermediate signals. This approach ensures that the history is accurately updated according to the input conditions while minimizing unnecessary complexity and signals.
