The design idea for simplifying the TopModule involves maintaining the same functionality while reducing complexity. This can be achieved by leveraging the properties of the input signals and the required operations on the history register. Specifically, we can directly update the history register based on the predict_valid and train_mispredicted signals, eliminating the need for explicit conditional checks for no change in history. The use of the non-blocking assignment operator (<=')) ensures that the updates are scheduled for the next clock edge, allowing for a more straightforward and efficient implementation.
