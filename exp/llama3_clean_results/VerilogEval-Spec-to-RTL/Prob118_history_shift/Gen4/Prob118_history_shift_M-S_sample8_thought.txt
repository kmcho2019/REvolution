The key steps involve:

1. Implementing the asynchronous reset to set the history register to zero.
2. Updating the history register based on the misprediction signal, which takes precedence over the prediction signal.
3. Shifting the history register when a prediction is made and there is no misprediction.

This simplified approach maintains the functionality of the original design while reducing the number of signals and variables, thus decreasing the overall complexity.
