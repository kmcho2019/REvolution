To simplify the existing solution, we can eliminate the need for the `predicted` and `predicted_taken` registers. The `history` register can be directly updated based on the `train_mispredicted` and `predict_valid` signals. When `train_mispredicted` is asserted, the `history` register is loaded with the corrected history. When `predict_valid` is asserted and `train_mispredicted` is not, the `history` register is shifted and the predicted branch outcome is added to the least significant bit. This approach reduces the number of registers and simplifies the sequential logic, potentially leading to improvements in area consumption and timing.
