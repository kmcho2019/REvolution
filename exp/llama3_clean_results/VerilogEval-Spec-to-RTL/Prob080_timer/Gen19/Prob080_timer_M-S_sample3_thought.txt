To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for a separate DownCounter module and integrate its functionality directly into the TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing the module interface overhead and simplifies the overall structure. The internal counter's behavior remains unchanged: it loads the data when the load signal is high and decrements when the load signal is low and the counter is not zero. The terminal count (tc) signal is directly generated based on the counter's value, asserting high when the counter reaches zero. This simplified design maintains the required functionality while reducing the overall complexity and potentially improving PPA metrics.
