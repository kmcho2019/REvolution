A novel approach to implement the TopModule is to use a single always block and a single reg variable to store the count. The idea is to remove the unnecessary modules and directly implement the required functionality in the TopModule. This approach reduces the area and power consumption by minimizing the number of flip-flops and combinational logic.

The always block will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and will check the load signal to decide whether to load the new count or decrement the existing count. If the load signal is high, the count will be loaded with the new data. If the load signal is low, the count will be decremented by 1. The tc signal will be asserted when the count reaches 0.

This approach is more efficient and scalable as it eliminates the need for separate modules and reduces the number of wires and flip-flops required. It also simplifies the design and makes it easier to understand and maintain.
