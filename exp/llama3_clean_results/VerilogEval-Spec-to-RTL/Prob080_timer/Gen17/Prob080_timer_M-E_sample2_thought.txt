However, to align with the problem's requirements, we must ensure the LFSR-based timer counts down in a predictable, linear fashion, similar to a traditional counter. This might require additional logic to manage the LFSR's state transitions and to generate the terminal count signal accurately.

Our novel architectural idea involves designing an LFSR that, when seeded with an appropriate initial value, will cycle through a sequence of states such that, when combined with simple control logic, it emulates a countdown timer. This approach combines the efficiency of LFSRs with the simplicity of a countdown timer, potentially offering a more compact and power-efficient implementation compared to traditional counter-based designs.
