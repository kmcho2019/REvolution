A simplified approach to implementing the timer could involve combining the down-counter and control logic into a single module, eliminating the need for separate enable and control logic modules. This single module would handle both the loading of the counter and the decrementing logic based on the load signal. The terminal count signal would be asserted when the counter reaches zero. By reducing the number of modules and signals, the design becomes more straightforward and easier to understand, potentially leading to improvements in power, performance, and area metrics.
