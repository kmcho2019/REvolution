A novel approach to implementing the timer module could involve utilizing a shift register instead of a counter. This method would load the data into a shift register and then shift the bits out one by one on each clock cycle when the load signal is low. The terminal count signal could be asserted when the most significant bit (MSB) of the shift register reaches the output, indicating that all bits have been shifted out. This approach would eliminate the need for arithmetic operations like subtraction, potentially simplifying the design and reducing area and power consumption. However, it would require careful handling of the load and shift operations to ensure correct functionality.
