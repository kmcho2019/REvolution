A simplified approach is to use a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: idle and active. The FSM transitions from idle to active when 'a' is high and the clock is high, and remains active until 'a' is low. When the clock goes low, 'q' is set to 1 if the FSM is in the active state. 'p' is set to 1 when 'a' is high and the clock is high. This approach eliminates the need for counters and flags, reducing complexity while maintaining functionality.
