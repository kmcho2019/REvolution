To implement a novel solution for the 6-to-1 multiplexer, we can utilize a combination of logical operations and bitwise selection. The idea is to create a mask that selects the appropriate data input based on the sel signal. This approach avoids the use of case statements or if-else chains, potentially leading to a more compact and efficient implementation. This method can be more scalable and might offer better synthesis results in terms of area and power consumption.
