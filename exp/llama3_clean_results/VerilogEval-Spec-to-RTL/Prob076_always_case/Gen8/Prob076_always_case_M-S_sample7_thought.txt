To simplify the existing 6-to-1 multiplexer solution, we can utilize Verilog's built-in case statement within a combinational always block. This approach eliminates the need for concatenation and shifting operations, directly selecting the desired data input based on the `sel` value. When `sel` is out of range (i.e., greater than 5), we can default the output to 0, ensuring the multiplexer behavior is maintained. This method is more straightforward and likely to be more area and power-efficient than the original implementation.
