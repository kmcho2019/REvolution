To implement a decade counter with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) that transitions through states representing the count from 1 to 10. This approach allows for a more explicit control over the counting sequence and can potentially lead to optimizations in terms of area and power. By encoding the states in a way that minimizes the number of state bits required, we can reduce the area usage. Additionally, using a one-hot encoding scheme for the states can simplify the logic and reduce power consumption due to fewer transitions.
