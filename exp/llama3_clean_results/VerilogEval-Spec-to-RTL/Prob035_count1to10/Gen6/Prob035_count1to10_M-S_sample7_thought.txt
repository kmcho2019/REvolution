To simplify the decade counter, we can maintain the same basic structure but simplify the condition for wrapping around from 10 to 1 and resetting to 1. Since the counter is a 4-bit register, we can directly check the condition without needing to explicitly compare the count to 10. The reset condition remains the same, resetting the counter to 1 when the reset input is high. By reducing conditional complexity, we aim to minimize the combinational logic delay within the clock cycle, thus improving the effective clock speed.
