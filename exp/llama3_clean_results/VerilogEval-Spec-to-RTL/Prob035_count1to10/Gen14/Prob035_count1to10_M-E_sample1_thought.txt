A novel approach to designing the decade counter could involve using a finite state machine (FSM) with a unique encoding scheme that directly maps the state to the output, potentially simplifying the logic and improving performance. The FSM would have 10 states (one for each count from 1 to 10) and would transition between these states based on the clock and reset signals. This approach eliminates the need for explicit counters and arithmetic operations, potentially reducing area and improving timing.
