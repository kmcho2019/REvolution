To implement the decade counter, we will use a 4-bit register to store the count. The register will be reset to 1 when the reset input is high and the clock is rising. On every rising clock edge when reset is low, the register will increment its count by 1. However, when the count reaches 10, it should wrap around back to 1 instead of 0, thus implementing a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10 inclusive. This can be achieved by checking the current count and deciding whether to increment or reset it based on the condition.
