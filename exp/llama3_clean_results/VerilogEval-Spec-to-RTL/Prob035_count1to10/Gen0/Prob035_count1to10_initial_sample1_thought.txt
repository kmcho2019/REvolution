To implement a decade counter that counts from 1 through 10, we need to design a sequential logic circuit that can keep track of the current count. We will use a 4-bit register to store the count, as 4 bits can represent numbers up to 15, which is sufficient for our needs. The counter will be reset to 1 when the reset input is high. On each positive edge of the clock, the counter will increment by 1 unless it has reached 10, in which case it will wrap around to 1.
