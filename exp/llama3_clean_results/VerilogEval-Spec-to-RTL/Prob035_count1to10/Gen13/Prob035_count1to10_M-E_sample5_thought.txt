The novel architectural idea is to use a binary counter and then decode the binary count to a 4-bit BCD (Binary Coded Decimal) output. This approach simplifies the counter logic and allows for easier implementation of the wrap-around condition. By counting in binary and then converting to BCD, we can leverage the efficient binary counting mechanism of digital circuits and then convert the result to the desired decimal representation. This method can potentially reduce the complexity of the state transition logic and minimize the area consumption.
