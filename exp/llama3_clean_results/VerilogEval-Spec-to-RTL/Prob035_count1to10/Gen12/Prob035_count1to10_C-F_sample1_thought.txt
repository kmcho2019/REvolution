To implement an optimized decade counter, we will use a straightforward counter architecture with synchronous reset. The counter will be designed to count from 1 to 10 and then wrap around to 1. The reset signal will be active high and synchronous, ensuring that the counter resets to 1 on the positive edge of the clock when reset is asserted. By keeping the design simple and ensuring that the counter only changes when necessary, we aim to minimize power consumption and maintain a small area footprint.
