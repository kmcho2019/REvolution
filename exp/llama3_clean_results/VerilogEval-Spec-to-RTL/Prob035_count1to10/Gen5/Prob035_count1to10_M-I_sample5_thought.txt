The provided decade counter module can be optimized for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. To improve performance, we can utilize a more efficient encoding scheme for the counter, such as binary-coded decimal (BCD), which could reduce the propagation delay and increase the maximum achievable clock frequency. Additionally, we will ensure that the reset logic is implemented using synchronous reset, which is generally considered better practice for FPGAs. For power optimization, clock gating can be implemented to reduce dynamic power consumption by only enabling the clock when the counter needs to change. To optimize area, the use of a more area-efficient counter architecture, such as a carry-save or carry-lookahead adder, could potentially reduce the overall area of the design. However, for simplicity and given the specific constraints of counting 1 through 10, we will focus on the reset logic and clock gating for power efficiency. The use of blocking versus non-blocking assignments will be reviewed to ensure that the design is not inadvertently introducing unnecessary delays or race conditions.
