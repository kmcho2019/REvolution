To implement a decade counter with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a combination of a binary counter and a decoder. The binary counter will count from 0 to 9, and the decoder will be used to detect when the count reaches 10 (in binary, 1010) and reset the counter to 1. This approach simplifies the conditional logic and potentially reduces the area and power consumption. Additionally, we can use a separate variable to store the decoded output, ensuring that the output is always a valid decimal value between 1 and 10.
