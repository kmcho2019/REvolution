A novel approach to implementing a decade counter could involve using a Johnson counter, which is a type of cyclic counter that can be used to generate a sequence of binary numbers. By designing a Johnson counter that generates a 4-bit sequence, we can then use decoding logic to drive the output 'q' based on the counter's state. The idea is to use the Johnson counter's property of generating a cyclic sequence and then use a simple decoding logic to ensure the output 'q' counts from 1 to 10. This approach might offer advantages in terms of simplicity and potentially better PPA metrics compared to traditional counter designs.
