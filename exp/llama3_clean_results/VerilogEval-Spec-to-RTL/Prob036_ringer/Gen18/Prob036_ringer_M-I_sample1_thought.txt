The existing solution is already optimized for simplicity and readability. However, to further improve the design for better PPA metrics, we can focus on minimizing the switching activity of the outputs. Since the power consumption is already low (0.000102), we can aim to reduce the area usage. One potential approach is to use a single logical expression to drive both outputs, instead of separate assignments. This can be achieved by using a single always_comb block with a conditional statement that assigns the values of 'ringer' and 'motor' based on the 'ring' and 'vibrate_mode' inputs.
