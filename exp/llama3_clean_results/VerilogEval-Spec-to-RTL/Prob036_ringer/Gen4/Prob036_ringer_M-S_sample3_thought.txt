The existing solution is already quite straightforward, utilizing direct conditional logic to control the ringer and motor outputs based on the ring and vibrate_mode inputs. To simplify this further while maintaining the functionality, we can consider the logic from a different perspective. Since the only two conditions that result in an output being turned on are when the phone is ringing and either in vibrate mode or not, we can directly assign the outputs based on these conditions without altering the logical operations. This means our thought process is to keep the logic concise and easy to understand, focusing on the minimal operations required to meet the conditions.
