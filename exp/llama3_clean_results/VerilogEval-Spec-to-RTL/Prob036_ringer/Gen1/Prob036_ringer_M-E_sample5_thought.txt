To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and RINGING. In the IDLE state, both the ringer and motor are off. When the ring input is asserted, the FSM transitions to the RINGING state. Depending on the vibrate_mode input, the FSM will either turn on the ringer or the motor in the RINGING state. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design, which can be easily extended to include additional features or states in the future.
