To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode, we can use a simple combinational logic circuit. The circuit will have two inputs: ring and vibrate_mode, and two outputs: ringer and motor. The output ringer will be high when the phone needs to ring and it is not in vibrate mode. The output motor will be high when the phone needs to ring and it is in vibrate mode. This can be achieved by using AND and NOT gates to implement the required logic.
