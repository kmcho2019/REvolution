To control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor based on the input ring and vibrate_mode, we can use a simple conditional logic. When the phone is in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 1) and it needs to ring (ring = 1), we turn on the motor. Otherwise, when it's not in vibrate mode (vibrate_mode = 0) and it needs to ring, we turn on the ringer. This logic can be implemented using AND and OR gates in Verilog.
