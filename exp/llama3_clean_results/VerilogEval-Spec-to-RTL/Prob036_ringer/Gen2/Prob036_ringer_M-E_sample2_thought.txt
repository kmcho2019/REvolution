To create a novel architectural solution for the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor control, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach. This FSM will have two states: IDLE and RINGING. When the phone is in the IDLE state and receives an incoming call (ring = 1), it transitions to the RINGING state. Depending on the vibrate_mode, it will either turn on the ringer or the motor. This approach allows for a more structured and scalable design, which can be easily extended if additional features are required in the future.
