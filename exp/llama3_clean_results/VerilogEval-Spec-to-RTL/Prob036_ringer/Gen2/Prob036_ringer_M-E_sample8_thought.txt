A novel approach to implement the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor control circuit would involve using a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the states of the ringer and motor. The FSM can have two states: an idle state and a ringing state. When the phone is in the idle state and receives an incoming call (ring input is high), it transitions to the ringing state. In the ringing state, the FSM checks the vibrate_mode input to decide whether to turn on the ringer or the motor. This approach provides a clean and scalable way to manage the different states of the cellphone's notification system, allowing for easy extension to additional features or states in the future.
