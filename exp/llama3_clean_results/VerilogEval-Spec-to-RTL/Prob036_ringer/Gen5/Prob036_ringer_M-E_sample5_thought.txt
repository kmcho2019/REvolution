To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can use a multiplexer (MUX) to select between the ringer and motor outputs based on the vibrate_mode signal. When the ring signal is high, the MUX can be controlled by the vibrate_mode signal to route the ring signal either to the ringer output (when vibrate_mode is 0) or to the motor output (when vibrate_mode is 1). This approach utilizes the properties of a MUX to efficiently manage the output selection, providing a distinct and alternative solution to the original implementation.
