The existing solution can be simplified by recognizing that the logic for determining whether to turn on the ringer or the motor can be directly expressed using a single conditional expression for each output. This approach eliminates the need for explicit logical operations and directly assigns the result of the conditional expression to the outputs. The simplified logic remains: if the phone is ringing and not in vibrate mode, turn on the ringer; if the phone is ringing and in vibrate mode, turn on the motor.
