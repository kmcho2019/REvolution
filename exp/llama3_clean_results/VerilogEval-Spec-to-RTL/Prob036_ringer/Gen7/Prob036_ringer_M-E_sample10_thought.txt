The new architectural idea is to use a single multiplexer to control both the ringer and the motor. The multiplexer will have the ring signal as the select input, and the vibrate_mode signal will be used to determine which output to drive. When the ring signal is low, both outputs will be driven low. When the ring signal is high, the vibrate_mode signal will determine whether the ringer or the motor is driven high.

This approach reduces the number of logical operations required and minimizes the number of transistors needed to implement the circuit, resulting in a more area-efficient design. Additionally, this approach can potentially reduce power consumption by minimizing the number of switching transistors.
