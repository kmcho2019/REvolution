A novel approach to implement the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor control circuit is to utilize a state machine. The state machine can have two states: IDLE and RINGING. When the phone is not ringing (input ring = 0), it stays in the IDLE state, and both the ringer and motor are turned off. When the phone needs to ring (input ring = 1), it transitions to the RINGING state. In the RINGING state, the state machine checks the vibrate_mode input. If vibrate_mode is 1, it turns on the motor; otherwise, it turns on the ringer. This approach allows for easy extension of the state machine to include additional states or functionality, such as different ring modes or silent mode, making it more scalable and maintainable.
