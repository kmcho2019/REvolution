To implement a novel architectural idea, we can design a finite state machine (FSM) that controls the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and RINGING. In the IDLE state, both the ringer and motor are off. When the phone receives an incoming call (input ring = 1), the FSM transitions to the RINGING state. In the RINGING state, the FSM checks the vibrate_mode input and turns on either the ringer or the motor accordingly. If vibrate_mode is 1, the motor is turned on; otherwise, the ringer is turned on. The FSM remains in the RINGING state until the ring input goes low, at which point it transitions back to the IDLE state.
