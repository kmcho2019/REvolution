A novel approach to implement the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor control circuit involves using a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: IDLE and RINGING. The FSM transitions from IDLE to RINGING when the input 'ring' is high. Once in the RINGING state, the FSM checks the 'vibrate_mode' input to decide whether to turn on the ringer or the motor. This approach allows for a more scalable and flexible design, as additional states or transitions can be easily added if more features are required in the future.
