To simplify the existing Verilog code for the TopModule, we can maintain the core logic but express it in a more straightforward and concise manner. The idea is to directly assign the outputs based on the conditions given, without any additional logic or complexity. The 'ring' signal acts as an enable, and 'vibrate_mode' decides whether to turn on the ringer or the motor. This simplification does not change the functionality but reduces the design to its most basic and efficient form.
