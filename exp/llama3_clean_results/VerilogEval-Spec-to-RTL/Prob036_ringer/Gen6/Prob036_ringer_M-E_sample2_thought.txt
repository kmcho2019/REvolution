To implement a novel architectural solution for the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor control, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) approach. The FSM will have two states: IDLE and RINGING. When the phone is not ringing (input ring = 0), it will remain in the IDLE state. Upon receiving a ring signal (input ring = 1), it will transition to the RINGING state. In the RINGING state, the FSM will check the vibrate mode (input vibrate_mode) and accordingly set the ringer or motor output. This approach allows for a structured and scalable design that can be easily extended to accommodate additional features or states in the future.
