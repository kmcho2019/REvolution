The novel approach involves using a multiplexer (MUX) to select between the ringer and the motor based on the 'vibrate_mode' input. This design treats the 'ring' signal as the enable for both the ringer and the motor, and the 'vibrate_mode' as the select line for the MUX. When 'vibrate_mode' is 0, the MUX selects the ringer, and when 'vibrate_mode' is 1, it selects the motor. This architecture inherently ensures that only one of the outputs (ringer or motor) is active at any given time, aligning with the requirements of the problem.
