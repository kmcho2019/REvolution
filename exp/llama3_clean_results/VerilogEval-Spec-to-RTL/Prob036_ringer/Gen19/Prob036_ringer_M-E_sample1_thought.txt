To introduce a novel architectural idea, let's consider using a finite state machine (FSM) to control the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor. The FSM can have two states: IDLE and RINGING. When the phone is in the IDLE state, both the ringer and motor are off. Upon receiving an incoming call (ring = 1), the FSM transitions to the RINGING state. Depending on the vibrate_mode, the FSM will either turn on the ringer or the motor in the RINGING state. The FSM will stay in the RINGING state until the ring signal is deasserted. This approach provides a clean and modular way to manage the different states of the cellphone's ringer and vibration motor.
