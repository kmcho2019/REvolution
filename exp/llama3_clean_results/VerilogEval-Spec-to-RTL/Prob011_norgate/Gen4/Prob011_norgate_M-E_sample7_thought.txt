To implement a NOR gate with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a combination of two NOT gates and an AND gate. The idea is to invert both input signals 'a' and 'b' separately and then perform an AND operation on the inverted signals. This approach ensures that the output will be 1 only if both 'a' and 'b' are 0, which is the definition of a NOR gate. This method, although less straightforward than using a direct NOR operator, demonstrates an alternative way to achieve the same logical operation by breaking it down into simpler components.
