The existing implementation of the NOR gate is already quite straightforward and efficient, using the logical NOT and OR operators directly. However, simplification can be approached by considering the basic definition of a NOR gate: it outputs 1 only when all inputs are 0. Thus, a simplified thought process involves recognizing that the NOR operation can be directly expressed in terms of the inputs without needing to explicitly consider the PPA implications in the code, as the simplicity of the operation inherently supports good PPA metrics.
