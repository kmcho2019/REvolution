Given the PPA metrics, the focus should be on optimizing the existing design for better power, performance, and area efficiency without overcomplicating the logic.

Instead, we can directly implement the NOR gate logic within the TopModule, either using the direct NOR operator or the modular approach.

For power optimization, the current implementation is already minimalistic. However, ensuring that the inputs are properly gated when not in use can help reduce power consumption. This can be particularly relevant in larger designs where input signals might not always be actively used.

Given the simplicity of the NOR gate, performance (timing) optimization might not yield significant benefits. The design is already quite straightforward and does not contain long combinational logic paths.

Area optimization seems less relevant here as well, given the minimalistic nature of the design. However, avoiding unnecessary logic or complex arithmetic can help keep the area usage low.

Therefore, the strategy involves simplifying the design, ensuring proper input handling for power efficiency, and maintaining the simplicity of the logic for area and performance efficiency.
