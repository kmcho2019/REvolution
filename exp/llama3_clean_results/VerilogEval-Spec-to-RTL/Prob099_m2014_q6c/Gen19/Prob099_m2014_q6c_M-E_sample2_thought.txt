The novel architectural idea here is to implement the state machine as a hierarchical finite state machine with two levels. The top level will handle the major state transitions, and the lower level will handle the finer details of the transitions based on the input 'w'. This approach simplifies the design by breaking it down into manageable parts. Each part can be designed and tested separately, making the overall design and debugging process more efficient. Additionally, this hierarchical approach allows for easier modification of the state machine if needed, as changes can be localized to specific parts of the hierarchy without affecting the entire design.
