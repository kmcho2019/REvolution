To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we will utilize a conditional operator to check the sign bit of the input 8-bit number. If the sign bit is 1 (indicating a negative number), we will fill the upper 24 bits of the output with 1s. Otherwise, we will fill the upper 24 bits with 0s. This approach eliminates the need for explicit replication of the sign bit and achieves the same sign extension functionality. The conditional operator allows for a concise and efficient implementation.
