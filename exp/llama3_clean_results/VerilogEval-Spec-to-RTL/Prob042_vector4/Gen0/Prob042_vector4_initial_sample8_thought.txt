To implement the TopModule, we will first identify the sign bit of the input 8-bit number, which is the most significant bit (bit[7]). Then, we will replicate this sign bit 24 times to create the sign extension. Finally, we will concatenate the sign extension with the original 8-bit number to form the 32-bit output. This can be achieved using Verilog's replication operator ({}) and concatenation operator ({...}).
