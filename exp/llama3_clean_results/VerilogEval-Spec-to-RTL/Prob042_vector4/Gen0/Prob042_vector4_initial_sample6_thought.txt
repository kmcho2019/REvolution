To implement the TopModule, we need to sign-extend an 8-bit number to 32 bits. This can be achieved by concatenating 24 copies of the sign bit (the most significant bit of the 8-bit number) with the 8-bit number itself. We can use the replication operator in Verilog to create 24 copies of the sign bit. The replication operator is denoted by '{n{bit}}, where 'n' is the number of times the bit is to be replicated.
