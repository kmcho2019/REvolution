The current solution uses a parameterized `SignExtend` module, which is a good practice for flexibility and reusability. However, for this specific task, we can simplify the design by removing the parameterization and directly implementing the sign extension logic in the `TopModule`.

The main idea is to concatenate 24 copies of the sign bit (the most significant bit of the 8-bit input) with the 8-bit input itself to produce the 32-bit output. This can be achieved using a simple concatenation operation in Verilog.

By simplifying the design, we can reduce the complexity and potentially improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
