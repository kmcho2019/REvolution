To simplify the D latch implementation, we can directly use a conditional assignment inside the always block. This approach removes the need for a specific sensitivity list and utilizes a blocking assignment (`=`) which is more suitable for combinatorial logic. This method directly reflects the combinatorial behavior of a D latch, where the output 'q' is directly dependent on the input 'd' when 'ena' is high, and retains its value when 'ena' is low.
