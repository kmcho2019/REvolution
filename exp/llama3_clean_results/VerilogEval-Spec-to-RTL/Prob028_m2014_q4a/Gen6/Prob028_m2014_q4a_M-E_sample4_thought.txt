A novel approach to designing a D latch involves utilizing a different architectural structure that still adheres to the fundamental behavior of a latch but is optimized for synthesizability. This approach involves using a clocked always block, which, although not a traditional choice for a latch (since latches are level-sensitive and do not have a clock), can provide a more synthesizable and predictable implementation. By introducing an 'internal' clock signal that is derived from the enable signal, we can create a structure that mimics the latch behavior but in a clocked context. This might not be directly applicable in all scenarios due to the introduction of a clock, but it serves as an alternative method to implement the desired functionality in a potentially more synthesizable way.
