To implement a D latch using an always block, we can utilize a clock enable approach. In this design, the enable signal 'ena' acts as a virtual clock, and when it is high, the input data 'd' is captured and stored in the latch. When 'ena' is low, the latch retains its previous value. This approach ensures that the latch is level-sensitive, meaning it captures the data as long as the enable signal is high, rather than edge-sensitive like a traditional flip-flop.
