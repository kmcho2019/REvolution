An alternative approach to implementing the TopModule could involve leveraging the concept of bus-based signal routing. Instead of treating each input-output connection as a separate entity, we can group the input signals into a single bus and similarly group the output signals into another bus. By using a simple decoder or a multiplexer-like structure, we can then route the signals from the input bus to the output bus based on their positions. This method can simplify the design and potentially reduce the overall area and power consumption. In this specific case, since the connections are straightforward (a->w, b->x, b->y, c->z), we can directly assign the signals without needing a complex decoder or multiplexer.
