The existing solution, although functionally correct, can be optimized for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The current implementation uses a hierarchical design with a separate module for each connection, which might introduce unnecessary overhead. A more efficient approach would be to directly assign the input signals to their corresponding output signals within the TopModule, eliminating the need for separate WireModule instances. This strategy should reduce the area and potentially lower the power consumption by minimizing the number of instantiated modules and the associated switching activity. For performance, the simplified design should maintain or even improve the effective clock period due to the reduced complexity. Thus, the improvement strategy involves simplifying the design by using direct assign statements within the TopModule, removing the need for the WireModule.
