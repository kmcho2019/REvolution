To simplify the given solution, we can directly use continuous assignments instead of an always block. Continuous assignments in Verilog are used to assign a value to a net (a wire or a reg that is driven by a single source). Since the module's behavior is purely wire-like and doesn't involve any combinational logic that could be simplified further, using continuous assignments directly expresses the designer's intention in a concise manner without the overhead of an always block. This approach also eliminates the need to explicitly declare the outputs as reg, as they can be directly connected to the inputs.
