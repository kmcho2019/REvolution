The existing design can be simplified by eliminating the need for the WireModule submodule. Since the functionality of TopModule is merely to connect input signals directly to output signals, we can directly use assign statements within TopModule to achieve this. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing the overhead of instantiating multiple submodules, which can potentially improve the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. By using assign statements, the design becomes more straightforward and easier to maintain.
