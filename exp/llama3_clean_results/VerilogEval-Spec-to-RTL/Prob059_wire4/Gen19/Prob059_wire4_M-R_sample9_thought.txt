The existing design uses a hierarchical approach with a separate module (WireModule) to act as a buffer or wire between input and output signals. While this approach can improve readability and maintainability, it may introduce unnecessary overhead due to module instantiations. A more straightforward and potentially more efficient approach would be to directly use assign statements within the TopModule to connect the input signals to their corresponding output signals. This method eliminates the need for separate module instantiations, potentially reducing the overhead and improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
