Given that the original solution is functionally correct but could be optimized for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, the improvement strategy should focus on simplifying the design. The current design uses multiple instances of a WireModule, which, although conceptually clear, introduces unnecessary overhead. By directly assigning the input signals to their corresponding output signals within the TopModule, we can eliminate the need for these module instantiations. This approach is expected to reduce the area by minimizing the number of logic elements required and potentially decrease power consumption by reducing switching activity. Additionally, since the design is simple and primarily consists of direct connections, the performance, in terms of critical path delay, should not be negatively affected and might even be slightly improved due to the reduced complexity.
