A novel approach to implementing the TopModule could involve using a single, continuous assign statement to directly connect all input signals to their corresponding output signals. This method eliminates the need for sub-modules or instantiation overhead, potentially reducing the area and power consumption of the design. By using a single statement, we can also simplify the design and improve readability. Furthermore, this approach can take advantage of the fact that Verilog allows for concurrent assignments, where all assign statements are evaluated simultaneously, which can lead to more efficient synthesis and implementation.
