To improve the existing solution, the focus should be on optimizing the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given that the current design uses a hierarchical approach with separate modules for each wire connection, a more straightforward approach would be to directly assign input signals to their corresponding output signals within the TopModule. This eliminates the overhead of module instantiations and reduces the complexity of the design. Since the task is essentially just wiring signals through, this direct assignment method is the most efficient way to achieve the desired functionality. By reducing the number of instantiated modules, we can potentially decrease switching activity, thereby improving power consumption. Additionally, this approach is likely to be area-efficient as it eliminates unnecessary module overhead.
