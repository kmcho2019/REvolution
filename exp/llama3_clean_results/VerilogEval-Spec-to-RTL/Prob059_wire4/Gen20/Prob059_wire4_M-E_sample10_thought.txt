A novel approach to implementing the TopModule involves leveraging the concept of a multiplexer (mux) to efficiently route input signals to their corresponding output signals. The idea is to create a single module that can dynamically connect any input to any output based on the requirements. However, since the connections are fixed (a -> w, b -> x and y, c -> z), we can simplify this concept by directly assigning the inputs to their respective outputs within a single module, thereby eliminating the need for sub-modules or complex multiplexing logic. This approach focuses on simplicity, reduced module instantiation overhead, and direct signal assignment for optimal performance, power, and area efficiency.
