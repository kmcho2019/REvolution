Given the existing solution and the feedback provided, the focus should be on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The current design uses a hierarchical approach with a sub-module for each connection, which, while modular and maintainable, may introduce unnecessary overhead. A direct approach, where the connections are made within the TopModule using assign statements, could potentially reduce the area and power consumption. This strategy takes advantage of the simplicity of the task, which only involves passing input signals to output signals without any complex logic. By eliminating the need for sub-module instantiations, the design can be optimized for better PPA metrics.
