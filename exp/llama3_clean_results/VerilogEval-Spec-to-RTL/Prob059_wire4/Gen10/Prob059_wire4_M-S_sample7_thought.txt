A more straightforward approach to implementing the TopModule would be to directly assign the input signals to their corresponding output signals within the module itself. This method eliminates the need for separate sub-modules or the overhead associated with instantiating them. By using simple assign statements, the design can be made more efficient in terms of area and potentially reduce power consumption by minimizing switching activity. This direct assignment approach aligns well with the simplicity of the task, which is to essentially act as wires connecting input signals to output signals.
