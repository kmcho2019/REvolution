The existing design uses a hierarchical approach with a sub-module for each wire connection. However, this might introduce unnecessary overhead in terms of module instantiation. A more straightforward and efficient approach would be to directly use assign statements within the TopModule to connect the input signals to their corresponding output signals. This method reduces the complexity of the design, eliminates the need for sub-module instantiations, and can potentially lead to better PPA metrics by minimizing the area and power consumption associated with the instantiation of unnecessary modules. The assign statements directly map inputs to outputs without introducing any additional logic or delay, making it a simpler and more efficient solution.
