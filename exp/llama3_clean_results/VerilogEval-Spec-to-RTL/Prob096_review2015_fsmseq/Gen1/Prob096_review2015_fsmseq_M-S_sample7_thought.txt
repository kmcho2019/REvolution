To simplify the existing solution, we can design a finite-state machine with five states: IDLE, SEEN_1, SEEN_11, SEEN_110, and SEEN_1101. The machine starts in the IDLE state and transitions through the states based on the input data. When the sequence 1101 is detected, the machine will set start_shifting to 1 and stay in that state until reset.
