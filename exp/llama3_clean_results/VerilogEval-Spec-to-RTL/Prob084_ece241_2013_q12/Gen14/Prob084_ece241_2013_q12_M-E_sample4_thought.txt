To create a novel architectural idea for the given problem, I propose a design that utilizes a combination of sequential and combinational logic to achieve the desired functionality. The new idea involves using a 3-to-8 decoder to generate eight selection signals, each corresponding to a specific input combination of A, B, and C. These selection signals are then used to enable the corresponding D-type flip-flops in the shift register, allowing the data to be shifted and stored in the register. The output Z is then generated by using a priority encoder to select the output of the flip-flop that corresponds to the current input combination of A, B, and C. This design approach eliminates the need for a multiplexer and instead uses the decoder and priority encoder to achieve the desired functionality.
