To implement the TopModule, we will first design an 8-bit shift register using D-type flip-flops. This shift register will have the ability to shift-in bits synchronously based on the enable input. Then, we will add a 3-to-8 multiplexer to select which bit of the shift register to output as Z based on the inputs A, B, and C. The shift register's outputs will be labeled Q[0] to Q[7], with Q[0] being the most significant bit (MSB) that is shifted in first.
