To implement the TopModule, we will first create an 8-bit shift register using 8 D-type flip-flops. The shift register will be designed to shift the bits to the right when the enable signal is high. We will then use a multiplexer to select the output of the shift register based on the inputs A, B, and C. The multiplexer will have 8 inputs (Q[0] to Q[7]) and 1 output (Z). The select lines of the multiplexer will be driven by the inputs A, B, and C. When ABC is 000, the multiplexer will select Q[0], when ABC is 001, it will select Q[1], and so on.
