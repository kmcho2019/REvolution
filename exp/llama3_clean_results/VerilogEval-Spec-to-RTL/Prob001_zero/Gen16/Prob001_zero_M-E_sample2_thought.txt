A novel approach to implementing the TopModule with an always-LOW output could involve using a combinational logic gate that inherently produces a constant output. Since the output needs to be a constant 0, we could use a NOT gate with its input tied to a constant 1. This way, regardless of the input (which is always 1 in this case), the output will always be the opposite, which is 0. This method, although slightly more complex than a direct assignment, demonstrates an alternative way of achieving the same result through logical operations.
