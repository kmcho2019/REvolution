To create a novel solution, we will utilize a single, centralized control module that manages the data flow to all 16 D flip-flops. This approach will leverage a more direct and centralized control mechanism, differing from the modular byte-wise approach. We will implement a control logic that, based on the byteena signals, selectively enables or disables the update of each byte of the 16-bit data register. This centralized control unit will handle the synchronous reset and the conditional update of the registers, aiming to provide a unique, efficient, and scalable design. Specifically, we will use a single `always` block to manage the update logic for all 16 bits, incorporating conditional statements to implement the byte-wise enable functionality and the synchronous reset.
