The novel architectural idea is to implement the 16-bit register bank using a single module, TopModule, which will utilize a for loop within an always block to generate the 16 D flip-flops. This approach aims to reduce area by minimizing the overhead of multiple module instantiations. To control the byte-write functionality, we will use a ternary operator within the loop to conditionally update the registers based on the byteena signal. This design should be more compact and efficient in terms of area, while maintaining the required functionality and performance.
