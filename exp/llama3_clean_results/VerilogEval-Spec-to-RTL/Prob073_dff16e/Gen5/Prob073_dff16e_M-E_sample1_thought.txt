A novel approach to implementing the TopModule could involve using a more modular and hierarchical design. Instead of implementing the byte-enable logic directly within the always block, we could create separate modules for the byte registers and instantiate them within the TopModule. This approach allows for better modularity, reusability, and potentially easier maintenance or modification of the design. Each byte register module could handle the enable logic for its respective byte, simplifying the top-level logic and making the design more scalable if additional bytes need to be added in the future.
