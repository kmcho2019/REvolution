To provide a novel architectural solution, we can design the TopModule using a more modular approach. Instead of directly implementing 16 D flip-flops within the TopModule, we can create a separate module named ByteModule that represents a byte of data (8 bits) and includes the necessary logic for byte-enable control and synchronous reset. The TopModule can then instantiate two instances of the ByteModule, each handling a byte of the 16-bit data. This approach enhances modularity and reusability, making it easier to manage and modify the design.
