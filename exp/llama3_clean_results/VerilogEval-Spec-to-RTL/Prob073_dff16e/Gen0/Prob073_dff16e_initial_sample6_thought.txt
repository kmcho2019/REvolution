To implement the TopModule, we'll create a module that includes 16 D flip-flops, with each byte (8 bits) controlled by the byteena inputs. When byteena[1] or byteena[0] is high, the corresponding byte of the input d is written to the D flip-flops on the positive edge of the clock. The module will also have a synchronous reset, where all D flip-flops are reset to zero when resetn is low.
