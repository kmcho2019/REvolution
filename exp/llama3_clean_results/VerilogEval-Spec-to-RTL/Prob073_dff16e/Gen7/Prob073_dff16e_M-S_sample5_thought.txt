To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for the ByteRegister module by directly implementing the 16 D flip-flops within the TopModule. This approach reduces the overall complexity of the design by removing the unnecessary module hierarchy. We can still maintain the byte-enable functionality by using conditional statements within a single always block to control the update of the registers based on the byteena signals.
