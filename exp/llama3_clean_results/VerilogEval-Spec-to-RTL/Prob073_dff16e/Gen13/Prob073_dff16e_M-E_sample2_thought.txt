To provide a novel architectural solution, we can design the TopModule using a serial-in-parallel-out (SIPO) register approach. Instead of directly implementing 16 D flip-flops within the TopModule, we can use a single 16-bit shift register to store the data. The byte-enable inputs can be used to control the loading of new data into the shift register, and the synchronous reset can be used to clear the register. This approach reduces the number of flip-flops required and simplifies the logic, making it easier to manage and modify the design.
