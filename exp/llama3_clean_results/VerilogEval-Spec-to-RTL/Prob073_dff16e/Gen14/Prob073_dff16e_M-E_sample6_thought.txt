A novel approach to implementing the TopModule could involve utilizing a single, large register with bit-level enable controls, rather than byte-level controls. This can be achieved by creating a large array to hold the enable signals for each bit, where each bit in the array corresponds to the respective bit in the register. The enable array can be populated based on the byteena signals, and then used to conditionally update the register bits. This approach allows for a more flexible and scalable design, as it can easily be adapted to support different word lengths or enable granularities. Additionally, it avoids the need for explicit byte register modules, which can simplify the design and reduce the overall area.
