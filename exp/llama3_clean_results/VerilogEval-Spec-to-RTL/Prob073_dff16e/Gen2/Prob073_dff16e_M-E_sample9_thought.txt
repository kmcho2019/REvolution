To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a single 16-bit register and control the updates to this register based on the byte enable signals. This approach simplifies the design by eliminating the need for separate registers for the upper and lower bytes. Instead, we will use bitwise operations to mask the input data and the current state of the register, allowing only the specified bytes to be updated. This design could offer advantages in terms of reducing the overall area and potentially improving the performance by minimizing the number of registers and the associated control logic.
