The TopModule can be designed as a synchronous sequential circuit with a 100-bit shift register. 
On every rising edge of the clock, the circuit checks the load signal. If load is high, the circuit loads the 100-bit data into the shift register. 
If load is low, the circuit checks the ena signal. If ena is 2'b01, the circuit rotates the shift register to the right by one bit. 
If ena is 2'b10, the circuit rotates the shift register to the left by one bit. 
If ena is 2'b00 or 2'b11, the circuit does not change the shift register.
The output q always reflects the current state of the shift register.
