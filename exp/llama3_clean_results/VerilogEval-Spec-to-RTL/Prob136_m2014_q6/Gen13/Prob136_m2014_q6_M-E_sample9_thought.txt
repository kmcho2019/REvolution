A novel approach to implement the state machine is to use a hybrid encoding scheme that combines the benefits of one-hot encoding with a more compact representation. This can be achieved by grouping the states into clusters based on their transition patterns and using a combination of one-hot and binary encoding within each cluster. Additionally, the next-state logic can be simplified by using a combination of multiplexers and logic gates instead of a large case statement. This approach can potentially reduce the area requirements while maintaining the benefits of one-hot encoding for the next-state logic.
