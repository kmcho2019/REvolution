The existing solution already utilizes a direct XOR operation to calculate the even parity bit, which is efficient in terms of area and power consumption. However, to further optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, and Area) metrics, we can explore reducing the number of XOR gates by implementing a tree structure for the XOR operations. This approach can potentially reduce the critical path and hence improve the timing performance. Additionally, minimizing unnecessary switching activities and ensuring proper buffering of inputs can contribute to power optimization. Given the small size of the module and the efficient use of XOR gates, significant improvements might be challenging without altering the fundamental approach or considering the context of the larger system.
