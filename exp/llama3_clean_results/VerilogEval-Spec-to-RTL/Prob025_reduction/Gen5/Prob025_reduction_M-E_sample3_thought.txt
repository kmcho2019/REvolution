The FSM will have two states: an idle state where it waits for input and an active state where it accumulates the XOR of the input bits.

This approach can potentially offer benefits in terms of area efficiency, as it might allow for the reuse of logic or more efficient use of resources in certain technologies. However, it may come at the cost of increased latency, as the calculation of the parity bit would be spread across multiple clock cycles.
