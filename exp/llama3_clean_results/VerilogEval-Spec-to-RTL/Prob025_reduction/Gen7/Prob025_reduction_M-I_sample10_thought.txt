This method can potentially distribute the delay more evenly and reduce the critical path, thus improving performance. Additionally, it might lead to a reduction in power consumption by minimizing the number of gates and their switching activity. For area, the impact could be neutral or slightly positive if the tree structure results in a more efficient use of the cell library's logic gates.
