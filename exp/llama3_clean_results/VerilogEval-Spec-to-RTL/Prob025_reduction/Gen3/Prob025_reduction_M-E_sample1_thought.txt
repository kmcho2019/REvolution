A novel approach to calculating the parity bit for an 8-bit byte involves using a tree structure of XOR gates. Instead of sequentially XORing each bit, we can divide the byte into smaller groups and calculate the XOR of each group in parallel. Then, we XOR the results of these groups to obtain the final parity bit. This approach can reduce the critical path delay and potentially improve performance, as it leverages the parallelism of digital circuits.
