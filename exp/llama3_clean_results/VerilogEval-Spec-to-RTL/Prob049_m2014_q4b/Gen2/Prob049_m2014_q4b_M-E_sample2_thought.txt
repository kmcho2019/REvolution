To create a novel architectural idea for the D flip-flop with an asynchronous reset, let's consider using a combination of a simple latch and a clocked feedback loop. The asynchronous reset can be directly applied to the latch, and on the positive edge of the clock, the latch's output is used to update the flip-flop's state. This approach allows for a clear separation of the reset logic from the clocked logic, potentially simplifying the design and improving its timing characteristics.
