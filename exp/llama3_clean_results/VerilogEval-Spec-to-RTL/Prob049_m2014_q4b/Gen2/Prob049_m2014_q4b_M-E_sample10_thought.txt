This can be achieved using a single always block with a sensitivity list that includes both 'clk' and 'ar', ensuring the block is executed on the positive edge of either signal.

We will use the non-blocking assignment operator (<=) to update the value of 'q' to ensure that the update is scheduled to occur at the end of the current time step, which is suitable for sequential logic and helps in avoiding race conditions.

This approach aims to maintain the required functionality while potentially reducing the area usage and power consumption by simplifying the logic and minimizing the number of operations performed within the always block.
