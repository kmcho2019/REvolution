A novel approach to implementing a D flip flop with an asynchronous reset is to use a two-stage latch-based design. The first stage is a transparent latch that captures the input data when the clock is low, and the second stage is an edge-triggered latch that captures the output of the first stage on the rising edge of the clock. The asynchronous reset is applied to both latches. This approach can provide improved performance and area efficiency by reducing the number of transistors required and minimizing the clock-to-output delay.
