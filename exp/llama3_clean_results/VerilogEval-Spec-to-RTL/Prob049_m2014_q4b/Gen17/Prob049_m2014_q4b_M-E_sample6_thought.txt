The concept involves using a latch to store the current state of the flip-flop and a combinational logic block to determine the next state based on the inputs. This approach separates the sequential logic from the combinational logic, providing a different perspective on implementing the D flip-flop. The key component here is the use of a transparent latch that is controlled by the clock and reset signals, allowing for asynchronous reset while maintaining the synchronous capture of the input 'd' on the positive edge of the clock.
