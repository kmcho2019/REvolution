To simplify the existing solution, we focus on maintaining the core functionality of a D flip-flop with an asynchronous reset while reducing unnecessary complexity. The current design already implements the required behavior with an `always` block that is sensitive to both the positive edge of the clock (`posedge clk`) and the positive edge of the asynchronous reset (`posedge ar`). The reset condition is prioritized, and the flip-flop captures the input `d` on the rising edge of `clk` when `ar` is not asserted. We aim to preserve this functionality while simplifying the logic to minimize area and improve power efficiency.
