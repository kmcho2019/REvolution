To introduce a novel architectural idea, we can implement the D flip-flop using a combination of a multiplexer and a latch. The multiplexer will select between the input data and the current state of the flip-flop based on the clock signal. The latch will then capture the selected value on the positive edge of the clock. Additionally, we will incorporate the asynchronous reset by using it to override the multiplexer selection, ensuring the flip-flop resets immediately when the reset signal is asserted. This approach can provide a unique perspective on designing the D flip-flop, focusing on the selection and capture of the input data.
