The existing solution is already quite straightforward and minimal, using a single assign statement to implement the NOT gate. However, to further simplify it while maintaining its functionality, we can consider the basic logical operation of a NOT gate, which simply inverts its input. Thus, the most straightforward implementation in Verilog for a NOT gate remains the use of the logical NOT operator (~) directly in an assign statement. This means our simplified solution does not deviate from the original in terms of logic, as the original is already about as simple as it can be for a NOT gate implementation.
