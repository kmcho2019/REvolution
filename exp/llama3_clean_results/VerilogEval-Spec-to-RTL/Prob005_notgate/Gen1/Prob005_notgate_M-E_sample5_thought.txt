To implement a NOT gate with a different architectural approach, we can utilize a basic digital circuit principle that involves a multiplexer (MUX). The idea is to use a 2-to-1 MUX where one input is the original signal, and the other input is the inverse of the signal. The select line of the MUX can be tied to a constant value (either 0 or 1) in such a way that the MUX always selects the inverse of the input signal, effectively behaving like a NOT gate. This approach might seem more complex than the straightforward NOT operator, but it demonstrates a novel way of achieving the same functionality using different components.
