A novel architectural idea for implementing a NOT gate involves utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) with two inputs, where one input is tied to a logic '0' and the other to a logic '1'. The select line of the MUX is driven by the input signal to be inverted. When the input is '0', the MUX selects the '1' input, and when the input is '1', it selects the '0' input, effectively inverting the input signal. This approach leverages the conditional selection capability of the MUX to achieve inversion, providing an alternative to traditional gate-level implementations.
