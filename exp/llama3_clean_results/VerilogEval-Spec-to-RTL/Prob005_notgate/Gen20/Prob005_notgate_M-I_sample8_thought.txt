The existing solution implements a NOT gate using a multiplexer, which is not the most efficient approach in terms of Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. A more straightforward and efficient way to implement a NOT gate is by directly assigning the inverted input to the output. This approach eliminates the need for a multiplexer and its associated control logic, reducing area and potentially power consumption while maintaining or improving performance.
