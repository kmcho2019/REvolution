A novel approach to implementing a NOT gate could involve using a multiplexer (MUX) to select between the input and its inverted version based on a control signal. However, since the control signal would always need to select the inverted version to achieve the NOT gate functionality, this could be simplified by fixing the select line to always choose the inverted input, effectively turning the MUX into a simple inverter. This idea, while more complex than the straightforward `~in` approach, demonstrates an alternative architectural perspective that could be useful in more complex designs where such a MUX is already present or required for other functionalities.
