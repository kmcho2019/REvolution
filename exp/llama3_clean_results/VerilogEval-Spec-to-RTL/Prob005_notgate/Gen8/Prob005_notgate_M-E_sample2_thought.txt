To introduce a novel architectural idea for implementing a NOT gate, we can utilize a different paradigm that still maintains simplicity and efficiency. Instead of relying solely on continuous assignment, we can explore the use of a simple always block. This approach allows for more flexibility, especially when dealing with more complex combinational logic or when the design needs to be easily scalable.
