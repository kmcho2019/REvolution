A different approach to implementing a NOT gate in Verilog is to utilize a XOR gate with one of its inputs tied to a constant '1'. This configuration exploits the property of the XOR operation, where a XOR 1 results in the inversion of the other input. By using this property, we can create a NOT gate without directly using the bitwise NOT operator (~) or explicitly defining an inverter module. This method showcases an alternative way to achieve inversion using a different basic logical operation, potentially offering flexibility in designs where XOR gates are already prevalent or preferred.
