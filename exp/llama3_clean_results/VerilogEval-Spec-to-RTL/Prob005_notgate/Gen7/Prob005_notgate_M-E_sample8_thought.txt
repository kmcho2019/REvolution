To implement a novel architectural idea for a NOT gate, we can consider using a multiplexer (MUX) to achieve the same functionality. The MUX can be used to select between the original input and its inverse, based on a control signal. In this case, the control signal can be a constant, ensuring that the MUX always selects the inverse of the input, thus acting as a NOT gate. This approach is more complex than the traditional implementation but demonstrates an alternative way to achieve the same result, leveraging the properties of multiplexers in digital design.
