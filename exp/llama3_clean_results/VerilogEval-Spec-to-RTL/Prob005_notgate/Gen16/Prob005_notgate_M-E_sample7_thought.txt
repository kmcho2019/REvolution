The novel architectural idea for implementing a NOT gate is to use a multiplexer (MUX) as a logic element. The MUX can be configured such that it selects between the input signal and its inverse, based on a control signal. In this case, the control signal can be a constant, ensuring the MUX always selects the inverse of the input. This approach, while not as straightforward as a direct assignment, demonstrates an alternative method of achieving the NOT gate functionality using a different digital component. The idea is to utilize the MUX's ability to select between two inputs based on a control signal, thereby leveraging its inherent logic capabilities to perform the inversion operation.
