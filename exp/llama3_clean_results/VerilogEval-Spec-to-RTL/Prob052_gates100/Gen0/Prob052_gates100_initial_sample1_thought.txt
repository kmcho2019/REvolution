To implement the TopModule, we can utilize Verilog's built-in reduction operators to simplify the process. The reduction operators (&, |, and ^) can be used to apply the respective operations (AND, OR, and XOR) to all bits in the input vector 'in'. 
The output of the AND reduction operator will be 1 only if all bits in 'in' are 1. 
The output of the OR reduction operator will be 1 if at least one bit in 'in' is 1. 
The output of the XOR reduction operator will be 1 if an odd number of bits in 'in' are 1.
We will assign the result of each reduction operation to the corresponding output (out_and, out_or, out_xor).
