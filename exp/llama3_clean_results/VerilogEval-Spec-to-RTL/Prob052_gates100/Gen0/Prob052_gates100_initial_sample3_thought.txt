To implement the TopModule, we can use the built-in Verilog reduction operators to simplify the implementation of the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gates. 
The reduction AND operator (&) performs a bitwise AND operation on all the bits of the input, 
the reduction OR operator (|) performs a bitwise OR operation on all the bits of the input, 
and the reduction XOR operator (^) performs a bitwise XOR operation on all the bits of the input.
