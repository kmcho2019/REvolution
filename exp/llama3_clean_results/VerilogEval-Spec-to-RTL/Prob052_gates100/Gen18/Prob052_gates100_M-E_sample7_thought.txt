The novel architectural idea for implementing the TopModule is to utilize a pipeline-based approach with a focus on minimizing the combinational logic depth and optimizing the data flow. This approach involves dividing the 100-bit input into smaller chunks and processing each chunk in a separate pipeline stage. Each stage will perform the AND, OR, and XOR operations on its corresponding chunk of the input data.

To further optimize the design, a reduction tree structure can be employed for the AND, OR, and XOR operations within each pipeline stage. This reduction tree will minimize the number of gates required and reduce the overall combinational logic depth.

The pipeline stages will be designed to operate in a sequential manner, with each stage processing its chunk of data and passing the results to the next stage. This sequential processing will help in reducing the overall power consumption and area utilization.

Additionally, to optimize the data flow and minimize the latency, the pipeline stages will be designed to operate in a way that the output of each stage is available at the end of each clock cycle. This will enable the pipeline to process a new set of input data every clock cycle, thus maximizing the throughput.

The final stage of the pipeline will collect the results from all the previous stages and perform the necessary operations to produce the final output.
