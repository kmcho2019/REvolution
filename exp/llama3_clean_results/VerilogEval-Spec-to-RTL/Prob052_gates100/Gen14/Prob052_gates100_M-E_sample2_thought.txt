To implement a novel solution for the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gates, I will utilize a pipelined architecture with a cascaded approach. This design will divide the input into smaller segments and process each segment in a pipeline stage. The outputs from each stage will be cascaded to produce the final result. The pipelined architecture will allow for improved performance and reduced area compared to the original design. Additionally, the cascaded approach will simplify the logic and reduce the number of gates required.

The design will consist of 10 pipeline stages, each processing 10 inputs. The AND, OR, and XOR operations will be performed in parallel within each stage, and the results will be cascaded to the next stage. This approach will reduce the overall area and power consumption while maintaining the performance.

To further optimize the design, I will use a hierarchical approach to reduce the fan-in of the gates. Within each pipeline stage, the inputs will be divided into smaller groups, and the operations will be performed in a tree-like structure. This will reduce the number of levels in the logic and minimize the critical path delay.

Overall, the pipelined architecture with a cascaded approach will provide a novel and efficient solution for the 100-input AND, OR, and XOR gates.
