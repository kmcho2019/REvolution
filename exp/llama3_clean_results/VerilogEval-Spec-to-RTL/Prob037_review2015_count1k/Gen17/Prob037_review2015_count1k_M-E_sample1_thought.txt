To implement a counter that counts from 0 to 999 with a period of 1000 cycles, we can utilize a novel architectural approach. Instead of using a traditional counter, we can use a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) with a custom polynomial to generate the count sequence. The LFSR will produce a pseudo-random sequence, and by selecting the appropriate polynomial and initial conditions, we can ensure that the sequence repeats every 1000 cycles. This approach will not only provide a unique solution but also potentially reduce the area consumption and improve the power efficiency.
