To implement the counter, we can utilize a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) to generate a sequence of numbers from 0 to 999. The LFSR can be designed to have a period of 1000, ensuring that the counter resets to 0 after reaching 999. This approach eliminates the need for a traditional counter and enables the use of a more efficient and compact design. We will use a 10-bit LFSR and modify it to produce the desired counting sequence.
