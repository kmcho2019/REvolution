A novel architectural idea for the counter module is to utilize a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) to generate a pseudo-random sequence that wraps around after 1000 cycles. This approach eliminates the need for a traditional counter and its associated adder logic, potentially reducing area and power consumption. The LFSR can be designed to produce a sequence that covers all possible 10-bit values from 0 to 999, inclusive, and then wraps around to 0. This method leverages the properties of LFSRs to create a cyclical counter with a period of 1000 cycles. To achieve this, we need to find an appropriate LFSR polynomial that satisfies the required cycle length and sequence properties.
