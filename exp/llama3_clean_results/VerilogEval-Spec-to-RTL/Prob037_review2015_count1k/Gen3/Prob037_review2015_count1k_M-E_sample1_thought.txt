A novel architectural idea for the counter module is to utilize a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) with a specific polynomial to generate the counting sequence. This approach eliminates the need for a traditional counter and the associated increment logic. By carefully selecting the LFSR's polynomial and initial condition, we can create a sequence that wraps around after reaching the desired maximum value (999 in this case). This method can potentially offer advantages in terms of area and power consumption, as it relies on simple shift and XOR operations.
