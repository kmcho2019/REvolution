To implement the counter with improved PPA metrics, we can leverage the concept of Gray code, which is a binary numeral system where two successive values differ in only one bit. This property can reduce the number of bits that need to change during each increment operation, potentially leading to power and area savings. However, since the output needs to be in binary, a Gray to binary converter will be necessary. This approach introduces additional combinational logic but might offer advantages in terms of power consumption due to the reduced switching activity in the Gray-coded counter.
