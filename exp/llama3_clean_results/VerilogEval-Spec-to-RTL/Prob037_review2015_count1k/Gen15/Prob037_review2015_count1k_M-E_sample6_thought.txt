To create a novel architectural solution, we can utilize a modular approach with a focus on reducing area and power consumption while maintaining the required functionality. Instead of using a traditional counter, we can design a solution based on a ring counter or a Johnson counter. However, these counters would require a complex reset mechanism to reset to a specific value. Another approach is to use a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) or a combination of counters to achieve the desired counting sequence. But, considering the specific requirement of counting from 0 to 999, a more straightforward approach would be to use a decade counter (or a BCD counter) for the hundreds and tens place, and a binary counter for the ones place, ensuring synchronization and correct counting. This approach simplifies the design and reduces the overall area and power consumption by minimizing the number of flip-flops required and the complexity of the logic.
