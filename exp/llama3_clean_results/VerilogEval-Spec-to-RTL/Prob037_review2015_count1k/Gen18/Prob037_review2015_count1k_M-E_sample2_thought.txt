To create a novel solution, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) to control the counter. This approach allows us to break down the counter's behavior into distinct states, making the design more modular and easier to understand. We can define two states: an idle state and a counting state. In the idle state, the counter is reset to 0. In the counting state, the counter increments until it reaches 999, at which point it wraps around to 0. This FSM-based approach enables us to implement the counter's logic in a more structured and scalable way. Additionally, we can optimize the FSM's transitions and states to minimize area and power consumption.
