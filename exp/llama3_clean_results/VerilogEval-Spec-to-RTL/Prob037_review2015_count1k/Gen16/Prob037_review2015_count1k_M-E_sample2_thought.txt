To create a novel solution, we can implement a counter using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) with a period of 1000 cycles. The LFSR will generate a sequence of bits that can be used to drive the counter. The counter will be designed as a simple accumulator, incrementing its value on each clock cycle and resetting to 0 when the LFSR sequence repeats. This approach eliminates the need for a traditional counter and reduces the area consumption.
