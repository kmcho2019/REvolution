To create a novel architectural solution, we will utilize a Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) approach to generate the counting sequence. LFSRs are commonly used for generating pseudo-random numbers or implementing counters with specific properties. By designing an LFSR that cycles through 1000 distinct states (0 to 999), we can create a counter that resets to 0 after reaching 999. This approach eliminates the need for explicit conditional statements to check for the reset condition, potentially reducing area and improving performance.
