A novel approach to implementing the 10-bit counter would be to utilize a hierarchical design, where the counter is divided into two smaller counters: one for the lower 4 bits and another for the upper 6 bits. This approach can potentially offer advantages in terms of area optimization, as the logic for handling the lower and upper parts of the counter can be simplified and optimized separately. The lower 4-bit counter can be designed to count from 0 to 9 and reset when it reaches 10, while the upper 6-bit counter can count from 0 to 999 (in terms of its contribution to the overall 10-bit count) and reset when the lower counter wraps around. This modular design might also facilitate easier verification and debugging, as each component can be tested and validated independently.
