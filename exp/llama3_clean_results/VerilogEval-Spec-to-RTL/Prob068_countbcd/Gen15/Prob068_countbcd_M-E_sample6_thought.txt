A novel approach to designing a 4-digit BCD counter involves utilizing a hierarchical structure with separate modules for each digit. Each digit module would manage its own counting and carry logic, and would receive an enable signal from the previous digit module to handle the carry propagation. This design could simplify the overall structure and improve performance by minimizing the number of operations and dependencies between bits. Additionally, the use of separate modules for each digit could allow for easier optimization and customization of the design for different requirements. The `TopModule` would then instantiate these digit modules, ensuring that they are properly connected and that the reset signal is correctly propagated to reset the counter. This hierarchical design could also facilitate the use of clock gating and other power optimization techniques to reduce power consumption.
