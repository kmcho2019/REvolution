A novel architectural approach for designing a 4-digit BCD counter involves utilizing a modular, pipeline-based structure. This design incorporates four separate BCD counters, each responsible for a single digit of the 4-digit counter. Each counter module is connected in a pipeline fashion, allowing the carry signal from one counter to propagate to the next. The enable signals for the upper three digits are generated based on the carry-out signals from the previous counters. This design allows for a more distributed and scalable architecture, potentially improving performance and reducing area consumption. Additionally, the use of separate counters for each digit can simplify the design and reduce the complexity of the BCD increment logic.
