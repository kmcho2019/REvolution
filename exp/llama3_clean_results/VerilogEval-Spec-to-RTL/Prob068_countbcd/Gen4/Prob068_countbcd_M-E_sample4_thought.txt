To implement a 4-digit BCD counter with an enable signal for each digit, we can utilize a modular approach. Instead of directly manipulating the bits of the count, we can separate the counter into individual 4-bit BCD counters for each digit (ones, tens, hundreds, thousands). Each counter will have its own enable signal, which will be generated based on whether the next lower digit has reached its maximum value (9). This approach simplifies the logic and makes it easier to manage the carries between digits. We will also use a synchronous reset to ensure that the counter resets correctly on the positive edge of the clock.
