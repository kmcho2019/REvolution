A novel approach to designing a 4-digit BCD counter involves utilizing a single, parametrizable counter module that can handle the counting and carry logic internally. This module, `ParametricBCDCounter`, would manage the counting for all four digits and would incorporate an enable signal that propagates through the digits based on the carry logic. The `TopModule` would then instantiate this parametric counter, ensuring that it's properly connected and that the reset signal is correctly propagated to reset the counter. This design could simplify the overall structure and potentially improve PPA metrics by minimizing the number of modules and the complexity of their interconnections. The parametric nature of the counter module allows for flexibility and scalability, making it easier to adjust the design for different requirements, such as changing the number of digits or the encoding scheme.
