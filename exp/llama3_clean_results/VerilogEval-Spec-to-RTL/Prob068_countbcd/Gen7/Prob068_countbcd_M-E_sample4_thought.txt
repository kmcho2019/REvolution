The novel architectural idea involves utilizing a single, centralized counter that increments based on a clock signal, and then uses combinational logic to determine the values of the BCD digits and the enable signals. This approach eliminates the need for multiple counters and their associated enable logic, potentially reducing area and power consumption. The centralized counter will be designed to count up to the maximum value of 9999, and then reset to 0. The BCD digits and enable signals will be derived from this centralized counter value.

To further optimize the design, the enable signals will be generated based on the centralized counter value, ensuring that each BCD digit is incremented only when necessary. This will reduce unnecessary state changes and minimize power consumption.

The design will also incorporate a synchronous reset mechanism to ensure that the counter and all derived signals are reset to their initial values when the reset signal is asserted.
