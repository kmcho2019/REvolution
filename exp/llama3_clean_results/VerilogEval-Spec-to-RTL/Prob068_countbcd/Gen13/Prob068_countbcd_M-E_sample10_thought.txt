A novel approach to designing a 4-digit BCD counter involves using a combination of sequential logic and combinational logic to handle the counting and carry logic. This approach utilizes a single, shared clock signal and a reset signal to control the counting process. The counter is divided into four separate modules, each handling one digit of the BCD counter. Each module consists of a 4-bit binary counter and a combinational logic block that generates the enable signal for the next digit based on the current count. The enable signals are propagated through the digits, allowing the counter to handle the carry logic automatically. This design simplifies the overall structure and reduces the complexity of the interconnections between the modules. Additionally, it allows for easy modification and expansion of the counter to handle different numbers of digits or encoding schemes.
