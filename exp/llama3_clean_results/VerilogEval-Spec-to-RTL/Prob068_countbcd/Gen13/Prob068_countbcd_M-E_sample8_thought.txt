A novel approach to designing a 4-digit BCD counter involves breaking down the counter into separate modules for each digit. Each module would manage its own counting and carry logic, allowing for a more modular and scalable design. This design, referred to as the "ModularBCDCounter", could utilize a single control module to manage the enable signals for each digit, ensuring that the digits are incremented correctly based on the BCD scheme. The ModularBCDCounter would simplify the overall structure, improve readability, and potentially improve PPA metrics by minimizing the number of complex operations and dependencies between bits. Additionally, this modular approach would make it easier to modify or extend the design, such as changing the number of digits or the encoding scheme.
