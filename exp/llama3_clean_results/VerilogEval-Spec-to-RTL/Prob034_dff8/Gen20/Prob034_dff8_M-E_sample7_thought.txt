A novel approach to implement the TopModule with 8 D flip-flops is to utilize a single 8-bit shift register. This shift register can be designed such that it shifts the input data into the register on each positive clock edge, effectively creating the behavior of 8 D flip-flops. The idea is to use a single always block to update the state of the shift register, ensuring that all bits are synchronized and updated correctly on each clock cycle. By doing so, we avoid the need for individual flip-flops and reduce the overall complexity of the design.
