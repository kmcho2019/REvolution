A novel architectural idea for the TopModule is to utilize a single 8-bit register, implemented using a 'reg' type, to store the value of 'd' at each positive edge of the clock. This register can be directly assigned to 'q', eliminating the need for an internal signal. By doing so, the design becomes more straightforward and synthesizable, addressing the potential issues mentioned in the feedback. Additionally, this approach can improve performance and area efficiency by reducing the number of signals and assignments. To further enhance the design, a reset mechanism can be introduced to initialize 'q' to a default value (e.g., 0) when the system is powered on or reset.
