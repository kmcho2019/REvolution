The core idea is to replace the shift register with 8 individual D flip-flops, where each flip-flop is triggered by the positive edge of the clock. Instead of using a load signal, we can directly assign the input `d` to the output `q` through the D flip-flops. We will utilize a straightforward and clock-edge-sensitive approach to implement the D flip-flops. This refactoring aims to simplify the design, improve timing, and meet the required behavior.
