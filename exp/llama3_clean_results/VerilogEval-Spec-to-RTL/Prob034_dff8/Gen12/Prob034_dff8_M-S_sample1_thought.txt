A simplified approach to implementing the TopModule with 8 D flip-flops involves directly assigning the input `d` to the output `q` within a single statement inside the always block, triggered by the positive edge of the clock. This method eliminates the need for a for-loop, making the code more concise while maintaining the required functionality. By using a non-blocking assignment (`<=`) for `q`, we ensure that the assignment is scheduled to occur after the current time step, aligning with the behavior of a D flip-flop.
