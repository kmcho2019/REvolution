To introduce a novel architectural idea, we can implement the 8 D flip-flops using a shift register approach. The module will utilize a single always block and an 8-bit shift register. When the positive edge of the clock is detected, the input d is shifted into the shift register, effectively updating the output q. This approach differs from the traditional D flip-flop implementation, offering an alternative perspective on how to capture input data at the clock edge. The key concept here is to leverage the shifting mechanism to update the output q in a sequential manner, maintaining simplicity and efficiency.
