To create a novel architectural solution for the TopModule, we can leverage the concept of a shift register. Instead of implementing 8 individual D flip-flops, we can design a single 8-bit shift register that shifts its contents one bit to the left on every positive clock edge and loads the new input data into the least significant bit. This approach not only achieves the desired functionality but also simplifies the design and reduces the number of components required. The shift register can be implemented using a single always block that is sensitive to the positive edge of the clock, making the design more efficient and easier to understand.
