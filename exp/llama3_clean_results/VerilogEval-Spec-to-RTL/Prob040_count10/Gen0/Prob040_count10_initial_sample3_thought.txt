To implement a decade counter, we need to design a 4-bit counter that counts from 0 to 9 and then wraps around to 0. We can use a 4-bit binary counter and add logic to reset the counter when it reaches 10 (1010 in binary). We will use a synchronous reset, which means the reset will only occur on the positive edge of the clock when the reset signal is high. 
We will use a case statement or an if-else condition to check when the counter reaches 10 and reset it to 0.
