To implement the decade counter, we can utilize an arithmetic approach by employing a 4-bit binary counter and then use a comparator to determine when the count reaches 10 (1010 in binary). However, instead of directly comparing, we can exploit the binary representation to simplify the logic. The counter will increment on every clock cycle, and when it reaches 10 (1010), we reset it back to 0. This approach simplifies the combinational logic and potentially reduces the area and power consumption compared to a finite state machine implementation.
