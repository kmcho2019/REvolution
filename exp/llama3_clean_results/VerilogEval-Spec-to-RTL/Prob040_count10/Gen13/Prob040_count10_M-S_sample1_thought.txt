A simpler approach to implementing a decade counter is to use a 4-bit register and increment it on every clock cycle. When the counter reaches 10 (4'd10), reset it to 0. This approach eliminates the need for a complex case statement and reduces the overall logic complexity.

The counter can be implemented using a simple always block that checks the current state of the counter and decides whether to increment it or reset it to 0 based on the reset signal and the current count.

This simplified approach reduces the complexity of the original solution while maintaining the same functionality, making it more efficient in terms of area, power, and performance.
