The new solution involves implementing a decade counter using a binary-encoded finite state machine (FSM). Instead of using a traditional counter, the FSM will have 10 states, each representing a count from 0 to 9. The next state logic will be simplified to directly transition between these states based on the current state and the reset condition. This approach avoids the need for explicit increment logic and may offer a different trade-off in terms of area, power, and performance compared to the traditional counter implementation.
