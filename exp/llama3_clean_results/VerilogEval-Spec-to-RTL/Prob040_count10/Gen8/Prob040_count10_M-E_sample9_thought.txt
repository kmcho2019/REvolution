The novel architectural idea for the decade counter is to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) with a one-hot encoding scheme. This approach involves representing each state of the counter as a unique binary code, where only one bit is high at any given time. The FSM will transition between these states based on the clock and reset inputs. The one-hot encoding scheme can simplify the logic for determining the next state, as it eliminates the need for complex comparisons or arithmetic operations. Instead, the next state can be determined by simply setting the appropriate bit high based on the current state and the reset condition. This design can potentially reduce the area and power consumption compared to traditional counter designs.
