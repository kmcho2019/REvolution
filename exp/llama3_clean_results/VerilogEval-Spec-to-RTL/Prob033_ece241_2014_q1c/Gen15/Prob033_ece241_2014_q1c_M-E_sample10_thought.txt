To create a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we can leverage the concept of a hybrid adder that combines the benefits of both ripple-carry and carry-lookahead adders. This approach can provide a balance between performance, area, and power consumption.

Our idea involves dividing the 8-bit addition into two segments: a 4-bit ripple-carry adder for the lower bits and a 4-bit carry-lookahead adder for the higher bits. This division allows us to take advantage of the simplicity and area efficiency of the ripple-carry adder for the lower bits, where the carry chain is shorter, and utilize the performance benefits of the carry-lookahead adder for the higher bits, where the carry chain is longer and more critical for overall performance.

Additionally, we will integrate the overflow detection logic within the adder itself, eliminating the need for a separate module. By doing so, we can simplify the design, reduce area, and potentially lower power consumption by minimizing the number of gates and signals required.

The new architecture will involve the following components:
1. A 4-bit ripple-carry adder for the lower bits (bits 0-3) of the input numbers.
2. A 4-bit carry-lookahead adder for the higher bits (bits 4-7) of the input numbers.
3. Integrated overflow detection logic within the adder, focusing on the most significant bits.

This approach aims to optimize the PPA metrics by balancing performance, area, and power consumption, offering a novel and efficient solution for the TopModule.
