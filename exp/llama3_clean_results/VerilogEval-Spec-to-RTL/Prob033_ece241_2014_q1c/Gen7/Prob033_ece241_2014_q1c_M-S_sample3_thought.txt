A simplified approach to implement the TopModule would be to utilize the built-in arithmetic operations in Verilog. Instead of designing a ripple-carry adder from scratch, we can leverage the language's support for addition and then manually check for signed overflow conditions. This method reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the amount of code and the number of components required. It also improves readability and maintainability, as it directly expresses the intent of the module in a straightforward manner.
