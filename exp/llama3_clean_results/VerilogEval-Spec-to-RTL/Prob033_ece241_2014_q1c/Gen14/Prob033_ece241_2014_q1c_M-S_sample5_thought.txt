To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for separate modules for the adder and overflow detection. Instead, we can directly calculate the sum and check for overflow within a single module, TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and their interactions. The sum of two 8-bit numbers can be computed directly using Verilog's built-in addition operator, and the overflow can be detected by comparing the most significant bits of the inputs and the result.
