To improve the solution, we will focus on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. 

First, we notice that the current implementation uses a carry-lookahead adder, which has a relatively low delay due to its parallel nature. However, the pipelining of the adder does not provide significant benefits in this case, as the output of the adder is not being used immediately in a subsequent operation that can take advantage of pipelining. Therefore, we will remove the pipelining to reduce area usage.

Second, we observe that the OverflowDetector module uses a simple and correct logic. However, we can simplify it further to minimize area and power consumption by using a single logical operation to detect the overflow.

Lastly, we can apply clock gating and other dynamic power management techniques to parts of the design that are not always active to reduce power consumption. We will also consider using low-power adder cells to reduce power consumption.

Our improvement strategy involves the following steps:
- Remove the pipelining of the adder to reduce area usage.
- Simplify the OverflowDetector module to minimize area and power consumption.
- Apply clock gating and other dynamic power management techniques to reduce power consumption.
