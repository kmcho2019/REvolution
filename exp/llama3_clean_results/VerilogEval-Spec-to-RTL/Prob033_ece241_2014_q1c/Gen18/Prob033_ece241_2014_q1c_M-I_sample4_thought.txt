To improve the given Verilog solution, the strategy will be to optimize the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, and Area) metrics. Given that the current solution has a power consumption of 2.24e-05 and an area of 38.0, the focus will be on reducing these values while maintaining or improving performance.

Firstly, to reduce area, the design can be simplified by integrating the overflow detection logic directly into the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate OverflowDetector module. This will also reduce the number of module instantiations, potentially improving performance by reducing the number of ports and wires.

Secondly, for power consumption optimization, the adder logic can be improved. The current implementation uses a carry-lookahead adder, which, while fast, can be power-hungry due to its complex logic. A simpler adder, such as a ripple-carry adder, could be more power-efficient, but at the cost of potentially increased propagation delay. However, given the small size of the adder (8 bits), a ripple-carry adder's performance impact might be acceptable.

Lastly, to further improve power consumption, the use of combinational logic without unnecessary modules can help reduce switching activity and thus power consumption.

The approach will be to implement these optimizations step by step, starting with area reduction, followed by power optimization, and then assessing any necessary adjustments for performance.
