To implement the TopModule, we can use a different approach by utilizing two separate registers: one for storing the data to be loaded and another for the shift register itself. This approach can simplify the logic and potentially improve performance.

The idea is to have two always blocks: one for handling the asynchronous reset and loading the data into a temporary register (`temp_data`), and another for handling the enable signal and shifting the contents of the shift register (`q`).

When the load signal is high, the data is loaded into `temp_data`. On the next clock edge, if the enable signal is not high, the contents of `temp_data` are transferred to `q`. If the enable signal is high, the contents of `q` are shifted right instead.

This architecture allows for a cleaner separation of the load and enable logic, potentially making it easier to optimize and improve performance.
