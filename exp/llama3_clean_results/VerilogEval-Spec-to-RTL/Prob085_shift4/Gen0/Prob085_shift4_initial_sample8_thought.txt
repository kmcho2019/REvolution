The TopModule should be designed with an internal 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. The module will have asynchronous reset to clear the register on areset's positive edge. On every positive edge of the clock, the module will check the load and ena signals. If load is high, it will load the data into the register. If load is low and ena is high, it will shift the register to the right. If both load and ena are low, the register will remain unchanged.
