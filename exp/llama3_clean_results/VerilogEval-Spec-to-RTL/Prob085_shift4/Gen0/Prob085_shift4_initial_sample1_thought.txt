To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit register to store the shift register's state. The register will be updated on the positive edge of the clock. The areset signal will be used to asynchronously reset the register to zero. The load signal will have higher priority than the ena signal, so when both are asserted, the register will be loaded with the data. When ena is asserted and load is not, the register will be shifted right. If neither load nor ena is asserted, the register will retain its current state.
