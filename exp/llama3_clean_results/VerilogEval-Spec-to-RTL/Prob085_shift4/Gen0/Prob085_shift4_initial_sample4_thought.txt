To implement the TopModule, we will use a 4-bit reg to store the shift register's state. We will handle the asynchronous reset first, setting the shift register to zero when areset is high. Then, at the positive edge of the clock, we will check the load and ena signals. If load is high, we will load the shift register with the data input. If load is not high but ena is high, we will perform a right shift operation on the shift register, shifting out the least significant bit and shifting in a zero from the most significant bit position. If neither load nor ena is high, the shift register's state will remain unchanged.
