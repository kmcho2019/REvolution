To improve the existing design, the focus will be on reducing power consumption and area while maintaining the required functionality. One key improvement strategy is to eliminate the need for separate load and shift modules by directly implementing the load and shift logic within the control module. This simplification reduces the complexity of the design, potentially leading to lower power consumption and smaller area usage. Additionally, by directly controlling the loading and shifting operations within a single always block, the design can take advantage of the synthesis tool's optimization capabilities to minimize combinational logic levels and reduce the number of flip-flops required. Furthermore, since the design is already clock-gated implicitly through the enable and load signals, we can focus on simplifying the logic to reduce power consumption further. The improved design should maintain the same functionality, including asynchronous reset, synchronous load, and shift operations, while optimizing for better PPA metrics.
