When the load signal is low, we can then consider the enable signal to decide whether to shift the register or keep its current value. This simplification maintains the required functionality while reducing the number of conditional checks.

The main idea is to use a single always block for the sequential logic and minimize the complexity of the logic inside this block. The asynchronous reset is maintained to ensure the register resets to zero when the areset signal is asserted. The clock enable and data load are handled synchronously, ensuring that the design operates as expected under all conditions.

By simplifying the logic, we aim to achieve a balance between power consumption, timing performance, and area utilization, although the provided solution is already quite efficient.
