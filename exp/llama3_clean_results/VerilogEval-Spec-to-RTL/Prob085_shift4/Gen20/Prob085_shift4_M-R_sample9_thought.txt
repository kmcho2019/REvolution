To refactor the existing code, we can simplify the design by removing the separate load and shift modules and directly implementing the load and shift operations within the ControlModule. This simplification reduces the overall complexity and can potentially improve PPA metrics by minimizing the number of components and the associated wiring. Additionally, we can maintain the use of clock gating to optimize power consumption. By directly assigning the output of the load and shift operations to the internal register q_reg, we can eliminate the need for the intermediate wires load_data and shift_data. This refactored design aims to reduce the area consumption, minimize the critical path delay, and improve the overall efficiency of the shift register implementation.
