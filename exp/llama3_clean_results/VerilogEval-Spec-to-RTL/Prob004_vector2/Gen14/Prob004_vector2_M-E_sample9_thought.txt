To implement a novel architectural solution for reversing the byte order of a 32-bit vector, we can utilize a shift-based approach. The idea is to use a combination of bitwise shift operators to manipulate the input vector and reverse its byte order. This approach eliminates the need for intermediate modules or complex concatenations, making it potentially more efficient in terms of power and area. By leveraging the properties of bitwise shift operations, we can achieve the desired reversal in a single, compact module.
