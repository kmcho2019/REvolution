To refactor the existing Verilog code, we aim to maintain the same functionality while simplifying the design and potentially improving performance, power, and area (PPA) metrics. The core idea of reversing the byte order of a 32-bit vector can be achieved more straightforwardly without the need for instantiating separate modules for each byte. We can directly use Verilog's concatenation and bit slicing capabilities to reorder the bytes within the `TopModule`. This approach eliminates the need for the `ByteReversalModule`, making the design more concise and potentially more efficient in terms of synthesis and area usage.
