To improve the existing solution, we focus on optimizing the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given that the current design is functionally correct but has room for improvement, particularly in power consumption, our strategy involves simplifying the design to reduce unnecessary overhead. We can eliminate the need for the `ByteReversalModule` altogether since it doesn't actually reverse the byte order but merely passes through the input. Instead, we can directly assign the bytes of the input vector to the output vector in the reversed order. This approach reduces the number of operations and the overall complexity of the design, potentially leading to improvements in power consumption, performance, and area utilization.
