A simplified approach to reversing the byte order of a 32-bit vector could involve directly assigning the bytes of the input vector to the corresponding bytes of the output vector in reverse order. This method eliminates the need for a parameterized module and a for-loop, resulting in a more straightforward and compact design. The idea is to use simple bit manipulation and assignment operations to rearrange the bytes, taking advantage of Verilog's bit-slicing and concatenation capabilities.
