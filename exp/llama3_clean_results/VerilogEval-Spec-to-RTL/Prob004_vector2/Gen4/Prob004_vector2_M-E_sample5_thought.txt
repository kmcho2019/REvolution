The idea is to shift the input bits into a register from the most significant bit (MSB) to the least significant bit (LSB), but instead of shifting them to the left, we will shift them to the right and at the same time, we will build the output by shifting the bits to the left. This approach requires a temporary register to hold the input value and then use a loop or a series of assignments to shift and build the output.
