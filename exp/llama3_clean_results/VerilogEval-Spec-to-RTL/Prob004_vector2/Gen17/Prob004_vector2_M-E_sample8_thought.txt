Instead of directly assigning the bytes to their reversed positions, we can leverage the properties of bitwise shifts to achieve the same result in a potentially more efficient manner. This approach will involve a series of left and right shifts to "cycle" the bytes through their positions, effectively reversing their order. The key insight is recognizing that a byte reversal can be achieved by a sequence of operations that manipulate the bits in a way that aligns with the shift and mask operations available in digital logic.
