To simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality, we can directly implement the 8-bit register within the TopModule using a single always block, without the need for a separate DFF module. This approach eliminates the overhead of instantiating and connecting multiple DFF modules, potentially reducing area consumption and complexity. The reset value can be directly assigned within the always block, ensuring that the register resets to 0x34 when the reset signal is active. Since the design requires triggering on the negative edge of the clock, we will maintain the use of the negedge clk sensitivity in the always block. By simplifying the design in this manner, we aim to reduce complexity while maintaining the required functionality.
