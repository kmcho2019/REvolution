To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for a parameterized D flip-flop module and instead directly implement the required functionality within the TopModule. We will use a single always block to handle the reset and data update for all 8 bits. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by removing the need for instantiation of multiple DFF modules.

We will maintain the active high synchronous reset functionality and ensure that the DFFs are triggered by the negative edge of the clock. The reset value will be set to 0x34 as required.

By simplifying the design, we aim to reduce the area consumption while maintaining the current power and performance metrics.
