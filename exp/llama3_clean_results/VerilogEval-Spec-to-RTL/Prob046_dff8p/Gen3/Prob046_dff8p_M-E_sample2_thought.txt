We can implement a novel architectural idea for the TopModule by utilizing a shift register to load the initial reset value and then use it to reset the D flip-flops. This approach involves designing a separate module for the shift register and another for the D flip-flop, promoting modular design and reusability. The shift register will be loaded with the value 0x34, and on reset, it will shift out this value to reset the D flip-flops. The D flip-flops will be triggered by the negative edge of the clock.
