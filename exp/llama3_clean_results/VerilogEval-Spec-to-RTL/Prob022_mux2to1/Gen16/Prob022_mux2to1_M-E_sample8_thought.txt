A novel approach to designing a 2-to-1 multiplexer could involve utilizing a single logic gate (like an AND-OR gate combination) rather than relying on conditional statements or ternary operators. This method leverages the properties of digital logic to select between two inputs based on a control signal. The idea is to use the select signal to enable or disable the paths to the output, effectively acting as a switch between the two inputs. This can be achieved by using AND gates to conditionally pass through inputs `a` and `b` based on the select signal `sel` and its inverse, then using an OR gate to combine these conditional outputs into a single output `out`.
