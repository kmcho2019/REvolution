To refactor the existing 2-to-1 multiplexer module, we can leverage the strengths of continuous assignment using the `assign` keyword. This approach eliminates the need for an `always` block and provides a direct implementation of the multiplexer logic. By using `assign`, we can express the logic in a more concise manner, which can potentially lead to better synthesis results in terms of area and power consumption.
