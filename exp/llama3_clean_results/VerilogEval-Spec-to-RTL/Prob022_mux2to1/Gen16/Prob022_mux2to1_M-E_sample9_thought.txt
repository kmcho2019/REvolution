A novel approach to implementing a 2-to-1 multiplexer could involve using a combination of logic gates rather than a conditional statement or ternary operator. This method would utilize basic digital logic principles to achieve the same functionality. The idea is to use AND gates to select either input 'a' or 'b' based on the select signal 'sel' and its inverted version, then use an OR gate to combine the outputs of these AND gates, effectively selecting one of the inputs based on 'sel'. This method not only demonstrates an alternative way to implement a multiplexer but also serves as an educational tool to illustrate how basic logic gates can be combined to achieve more complex digital functions.
