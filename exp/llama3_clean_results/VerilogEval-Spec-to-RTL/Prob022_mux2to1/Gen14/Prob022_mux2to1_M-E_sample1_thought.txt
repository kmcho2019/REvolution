A novel approach to implementing a 2-to-1 multiplexer is to use a combination of logical operations instead of a direct conditional assignment. This can be achieved by utilizing the properties of bitwise AND and OR operations. The idea is to mask the input that is not selected by the `sel` signal, effectively allowing the other input to pass through. This method, while less straightforward than the ternary operator, demonstrates an alternative way of achieving the same functionality using basic logical operations.
