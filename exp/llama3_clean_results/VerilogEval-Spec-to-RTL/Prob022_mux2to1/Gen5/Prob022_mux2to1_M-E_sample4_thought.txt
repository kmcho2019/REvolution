A novel architectural approach to implementing a 2-to-1 multiplexer could involve using a combination of logic gates instead of the ternary operator. This method would explicitly demonstrate the underlying digital logic that enables the multiplexer's operation. By using AND, OR, and NOT gates, we can construct a multiplexer that selects between two inputs based on a control signal. This approach not only provides an alternative implementation but also offers a deeper understanding of the fundamental logic operations involved in multiplexing.
