The novel architectural idea is to implement the 2-to-1 multiplexer using a combination of logical AND and OR operations. This approach involves generating two signals: one that is high when the select signal is low and the input 'a' is high, and another that is high when the select signal is high and the input 'b' is high. Then, the output of the multiplexer is the logical OR of these two signals. This method provides an alternative way to achieve the multiplexer's functionality without directly using a ternary operator or an if-else statement.
