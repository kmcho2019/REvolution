To introduce a novel architectural idea for the 2-to-1 multiplexer, let's consider using a case statement within a combinational always block. The case statement allows for a clear and readable implementation where the output is determined based on the value of the select input. This approach provides an alternative to the ternary operator and if-else statements, offering a structured way to handle the selection logic. By using a case statement, the design becomes more modular and easier to understand, especially when dealing with more complex selection criteria or multiple inputs. This method also maintains the efficiency and conciseness required for digital circuit design, making it suitable for a 2-to-1 multiplexer implementation.
