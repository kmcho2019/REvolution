To implement a 2-to-1 multiplexer with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize the bitwise AND and OR operators in Verilog, along with bitwise NOT operator, to create a multiplexer. The idea is to use the select line to enable or disable the inputs. When sel is 0, we want to choose input 'a', so we use the bitwise AND operator with 'a' and the bitwise NOT of 'sel' (which will be 1 when sel is 0) to enable 'a', and bitwise AND operator with 'b' and 'sel' (which will be 0 when sel is 0) to disable 'b'. When sel is 1, the opposite happens. Then, we use the bitwise OR operator to combine the two results, effectively choosing either 'a' or 'b' based on the value of 'sel'. This approach allows for a concise and efficient representation of the multiplexer's functionality without using a case statement or conditional assignment.
