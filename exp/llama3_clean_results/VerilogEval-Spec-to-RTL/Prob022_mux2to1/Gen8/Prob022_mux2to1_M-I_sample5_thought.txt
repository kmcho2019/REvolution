The existing solution for the 2-to-1 multiplexer is functionally correct and simple. However, considering the PPA metrics, the focus should be on optimizing the area and power consumption while maintaining the same functionality. One strategy is to utilize the ternary operator directly within a continuous assignment, which is a more concise way to express the multiplexing logic and may result in more efficient synthesis by the tool. This approach eliminates the need for an `always` block and the associated overhead, potentially leading to better area and power metrics. The ternary operator (`?:`) is a compact and clear way to express conditional assignments, which can be beneficial for both readability and synthesis efficiency.
