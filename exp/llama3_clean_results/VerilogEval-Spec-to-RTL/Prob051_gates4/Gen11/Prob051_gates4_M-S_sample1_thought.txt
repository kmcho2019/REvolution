To simplify the solution, we can directly implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates within the TopModule without using separate modules for each gate type. This approach reduces the complexity by minimizing the number of modules and instantiation required, thus improving readability and potentially reducing synthesis area and power consumption. By leveraging Verilog's built-in bitwise operators (&, |, ^) for AND, OR, and XOR operations respectively, we can express the functionality of the 4-input gates in a concise manner within the TopModule.
