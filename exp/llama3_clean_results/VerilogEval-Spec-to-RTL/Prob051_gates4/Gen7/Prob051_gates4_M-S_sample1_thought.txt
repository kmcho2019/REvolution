To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for separate modules for each gate type. Instead, we can directly implement the AND, OR, and XOR logic within the TopModule itself, utilizing Verilog's built-in bitwise operators (&, |, ^) to perform the operations on the input bits. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for additional module instantiations and hierarchical design, thus simplifying the overall implementation.
