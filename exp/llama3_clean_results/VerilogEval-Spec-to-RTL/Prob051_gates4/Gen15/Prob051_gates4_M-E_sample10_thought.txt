To create a novel solution, we can leverage the concept of bitwise operations and bitwise reduction. Instead of designing separate modules for AND, OR, and XOR gates, we can utilize SystemVerilog's built-in reduction operators to simplify the implementation. This approach eliminates the need for instantiating multiple gates and reduces the complexity of the design.

The `TopModule` will directly apply the reduction operators on the input vector to produce the desired outputs. This strategy not only minimizes the area and power consumption but also enhances the readability and maintainability of the code.

Furthermore, we can utilize the `assign` statement with the reduction operators to directly drive the output signals, making the code more concise and efficient.
