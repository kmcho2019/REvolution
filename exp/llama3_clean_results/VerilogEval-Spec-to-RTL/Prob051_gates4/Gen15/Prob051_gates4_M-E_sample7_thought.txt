To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we can leverage the concept of a 'universal' logic gate that can be configured to behave as an AND, OR, or XOR gate based on its input configuration. This can be achieved by utilizing a lookup table (LUT) or a programmable logic array (PLA) within the module. The basic idea is to implement a single, reconfigurable logic block that can perform any of the required operations, thus reducing the overall gate count and potentially improving area efficiency and power consumption.

The approach involves:
1. Designing a universal logic gate (ULG) that takes an additional 'control' input to determine its operation (AND, OR, or XOR).
2. Implementing the ULG using a LUT or a small PLA, which can be more area-efficient than using separate gates for each operation.
3. Instantiating the ULG within the TopModule and configuring it to perform the AND, OR, and XOR operations based on the control inputs.
4. Utilizing multiplexers or demultiplexers to manage the inputs and outputs of the ULG, ensuring that the correct data is processed for each operation.

This novel architectural idea aims to minimize the number of gates, reduce switching activity, and optimize the overall design for better PPA metrics.
