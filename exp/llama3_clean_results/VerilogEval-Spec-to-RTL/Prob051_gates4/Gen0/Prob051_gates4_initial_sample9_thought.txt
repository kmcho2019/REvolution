To implement the TopModule, we will use the built-in logical operators in Verilog to create the AND, OR, and XOR gates. We will assign the result of each gate operation directly to the corresponding output port. The AND operation (&) will be applied to all bits of the input to produce out_and. The OR operation (|) with a reduction operator will be applied to produce out_or. The XOR operation (^) with a reduction operator will be applied to produce out_xor.
