To implement the TopModule, we need to design a combinational circuit that performs 4-input AND, OR, and XOR operations. 
The AND operation will produce an output of 1 only if all the inputs are 1. 
The OR operation will produce an output of 1 if any of the inputs are 1. 
The XOR operation will produce an output of 1 if an odd number of inputs are 1. 
We can use Verilog's built-in logical operators (&, |, ^) to implement these operations.
