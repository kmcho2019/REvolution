A simplified architectural idea for implementing the combinational circuit with 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates involves directly utilizing the built-in logical operators in Verilog for the 4-input operations. This approach eliminates the need for breaking down the gates into smaller components and instantiating them, thereby reducing the complexity of the design. By using the logical operators (&, |, ^) directly on the input vector, we can implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates in a more straightforward and concise manner. This simplification can lead to improved design efficiency, reduced area consumption, and potentially lower power consumption due to the minimized switching activity.
