To simplify the existing solution, we can directly implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates within the TopModule without creating separate modules for each gate type. This approach eliminates the need for the intermediate TwoInputAND, TwoInputOR, TwoInputXOR, FourInputAND, FourInputOR, and FourInputXOR modules, reducing complexity and potential overhead from module instantiations. By using Verilog's built-in logical operators (& for AND, | for OR, and ^ for XOR), we can express the functionality of these gates in a concise manner within the TopModule.
