To simplify the solution, we can reduce the complexity by directly implementing the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates within the TopModule without instantiating separate modules for each gate type. This approach eliminates the need for the intermediate modules (TwoInputAND, TwoInputOR, TwoInputXOR, FourInputAND, FourInputOR, FourInputXOR) and wires, thus simplifying the design hierarchy and reducing the area usage.

For the 4-input AND and OR gates, we can use a tree-like structure to minimize the critical path delay. The 4-input XOR gate can also be implemented using a tree-like structure of 2-input XOR gates, similar to the original design.

This simplified approach maintains the functionality of the original design while reducing the complexity and potentially improving the PPA metrics.
