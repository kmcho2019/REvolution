The new solution involves leveraging the bitwise operations available in Verilog to directly implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates within the `TopModule`. Instead of creating separate modules for 2-input and 4-input gates, we can directly apply the bitwise operators to the input vector `in`. This approach simplifies the design and potentially reduces area and power consumption by minimizing the number of gates and levels of logic.

For the 4-input AND gate, we can use a loop to iteratively apply the bitwise AND operation to all bits of the input vector. Similarly, for the 4-input OR and XOR gates, we can apply the bitwise OR and XOR operations, respectively. This approach not only simplifies the design but also makes it more scalable, as adding more inputs would only require adjusting the loop or the operation sequence.

This design strategy focuses on leveraging the native capabilities of Verilog to efficiently implement the required functionality, potentially leading to a more compact and efficient implementation compared to the hierarchical approach.
