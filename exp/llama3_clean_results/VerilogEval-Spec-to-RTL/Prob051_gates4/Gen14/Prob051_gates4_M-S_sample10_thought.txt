A simplified approach to implementing the combinational circuit involves directly using the built-in bitwise operators in Verilog to implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates. This approach eliminates the need for hierarchical design and instantiation of separate gate modules, reducing complexity and potentially improving performance. By using the bitwise AND (&), OR (|), and XOR (^) operators, we can directly compute the outputs of the 4-input gates from the input vector in a single step, streamlining the design and minimizing the number of components required.
