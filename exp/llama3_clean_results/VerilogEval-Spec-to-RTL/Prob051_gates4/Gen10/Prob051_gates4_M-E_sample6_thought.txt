To create a novel architectural solution, we will employ a parallel and pipelined approach to implement the 4-input AND, OR, and XOR gates. This design will utilize a single clock cycle to produce all three outputs, reducing the overall latency and improving throughput.

The core idea is to use a combination of look-up tables (LUTs) and flip-flops to implement the gates in a pipelined fashion. Each stage of the pipeline will compute a portion of the output, and the final output will be produced after all stages have completed.

For the AND gate, we will use a 4-input LUT to compute the output directly. For the OR gate, we will use a similar approach. For the XOR gate, we will employ a tree-like structure of 2-input XOR gates, but with a twist: each XOR gate will be implemented using a 2-input LUT, and the outputs will be fed into the next stage of the pipeline.

To further improve the design, we will use a technique called "gate duplication" to reduce the critical path delay. This involves duplicating certain gates in the pipeline to reduce the fan-out and fan-in of each gate, resulting in a faster overall design.

The `TopModule` will instantiate the pipelined gates and provide the necessary clock and reset signals to control the pipeline.
