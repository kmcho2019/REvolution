The `assign` statement in Verilog is used to assign a value to a wire or a variable continuously. It is a concurrent statement, meaning it is executed whenever the expression on the right-hand side of the assignment operator changes. This is different from the `always` block, which is used for sequential logic and requires a sensitivity list or an edge specification to determine when it should be executed.
