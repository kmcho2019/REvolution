A novel architectural idea for the TopModule is to use a logic gate that always outputs a logic high (1'b1) regardless of its input. This can be achieved by using an OR gate with one of its inputs tied to a constant logic high (1'b1). Since one of the inputs to the OR gate is always high, the output of the OR gate will also always be high. This approach introduces a minimal amount of logic while still fulfilling the requirement of always driving the output to a logic high.
