To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we could utilize a state machine, albeit a very simple one, to drive the output 'one' high. This approach might seem overkill for such a simple task but could be part of a larger system where the state machine manages various outputs or conditions. The state machine will have a single state where it drives the output 'one' to 1. Upon initialization or reset, it transitions to this state and remains there, continuously driving the output high. This concept, while not necessary for the current requirement, demonstrates how the module could be integrated into more complex designs.
