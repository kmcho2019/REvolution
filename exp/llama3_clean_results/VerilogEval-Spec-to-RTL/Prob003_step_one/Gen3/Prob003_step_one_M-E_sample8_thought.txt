A novel approach to implementing the TopModule could involve using a parameterized module that allows for flexibility in the output signal's value, even though the current requirement is to output a constant high signal. This could be achieved by defining a parameter within the module that defaults to 1 (logic high) but can be overridden if needed in the future. This method promotes reusability and maintainability of the code, as the same module could be instantiated with different output values in other parts of the design without requiring modifications to the module itself.
