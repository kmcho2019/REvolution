A novel architectural idea to implement the TopModule is to utilize a clocked always block that continuously drives the output to 1, regardless of any input conditions. This approach introduces a clock signal into the design, which may seem counterintuitive at first since the output is constant. However, this method allows for potential integration with other clocked designs or for situations where the output might need to be conditionally driven in the future, providing flexibility. The always block will run at every positive edge of the clock, ensuring the output remains at 1. To maintain simplicity and adhere to the given constraints (since there are no inputs), the clock can be assumed to be a global signal or generated internally if needed.
