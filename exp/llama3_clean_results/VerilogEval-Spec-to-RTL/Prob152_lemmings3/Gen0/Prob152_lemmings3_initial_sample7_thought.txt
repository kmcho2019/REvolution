The Lemmings' behavior can be modeled as a finite state machine with four states: walking left, walking right, falling, and digging. The state machine will change its state based on the input signals. When the Lemming is walking and encounters an obstacle, it will switch directions. If the ground disappears, the Lemming will start falling and say "aaah!". When the ground reappears, the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. If the Lemming is told to dig and is currently walking on ground, it will start digging and continue until it reaches the other side. At that point, it will fall and then continue walking in its original direction once it hits ground again.
