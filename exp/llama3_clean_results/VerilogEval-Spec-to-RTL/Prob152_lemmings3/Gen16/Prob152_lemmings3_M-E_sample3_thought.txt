The falling state can have a sub-state that detects when the ground reappears and transitions the Lemming back to walking. The digging state can have sub-states for the actual digging action and for transitioning to falling when the ground disappears.

This hierarchical approach allows for a cleaner and more modular design, making it easier to manage complexity and ensure that each state and transition is correctly handled. It also enables a more efficient use of combinational logic, as each sub-state can be optimized independently.
