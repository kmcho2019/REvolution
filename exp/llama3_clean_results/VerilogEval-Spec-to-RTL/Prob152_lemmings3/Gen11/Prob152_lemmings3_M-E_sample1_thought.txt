The microcode-based state machine will be controlled by a program counter (PC) that sequences through the micro-instructions. The PC will be updated based on the current state and input conditions. This architecture allows for a high degree of flexibility and modularity, making it easier to add or modify behaviors.

The new solution will utilize a separate control unit to manage the microcode execution, which will improve the overall scalability and maintainability of the design.
