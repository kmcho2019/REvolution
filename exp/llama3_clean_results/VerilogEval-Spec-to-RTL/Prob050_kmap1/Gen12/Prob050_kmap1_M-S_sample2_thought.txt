The existing solution already provides a straightforward and optimized implementation of the Karnaugh map logic using a single `assign` statement with logical operators. However, the expression `a || (b | c)` can be further simplified by removing the unnecessary parentheses, as the OR operator (`|`) has a lower precedence than the logical OR operator (`||`) in Verilog. This simplification does not change the functionality but reduces the visual complexity of the code.
