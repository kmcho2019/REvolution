To implement a 4-bit wide, 256-to-1 multiplexer, we can use a tree-based architecture. The idea is to divide the 256 4-bit inputs into 16 groups of 16 inputs each. We can then use 16 16-to-1 multiplexers to select one input from each group based on the 4 least significant bits of the select signal. The outputs of these multiplexers are then fed into another 16-to-1 multiplexer, which selects the final output based on the 4 most significant bits of the select signal. This approach reduces the complexity of the multiplexer and can lead to better PPA metrics.
