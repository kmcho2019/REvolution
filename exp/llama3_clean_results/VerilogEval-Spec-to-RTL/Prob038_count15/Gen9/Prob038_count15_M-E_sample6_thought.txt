However, another interesting approach could be to use a counter with a preload capability. In this design, instead of directly incrementing the counter, we load a new value into the counter based on its current state and the reset condition. This could potentially allow for more flexibility in the future if we wanted to introduce additional functionality, such as programmable counting sequences.
