To implement the 4-bit binary counter, we will use a simple sequential logic design that leverages the synchronous reset and positive edge-triggered clock. The counter will be implemented using a 4-bit register (to hold the current count value) and combinational logic to compute the next count value. On the positive edge of the clock, the counter will increment its value, wrapping around to 0 after reaching 15. If the reset input is high during a clock edge, the counter will reset to 0.
