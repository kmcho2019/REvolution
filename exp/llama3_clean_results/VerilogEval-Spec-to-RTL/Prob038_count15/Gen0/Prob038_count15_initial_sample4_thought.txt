To design the TopModule, we'll use a 4-bit binary counter. This counter will increment by 1 on every positive edge of the clock and reset to 0 when the reset input is high. The counter's output will be the current count, represented by the 4-bit signal q. We will utilize a always block to capture the sequential behavior of the counter. Inside the always block, we will check for the reset condition and the clock edge to decide whether to increment the counter or reset it.
