To generate a completely new and different solution, we can implement a 4-bit binary counter using a linear feedback shift register (LFSR) approach. An LFSR is a type of digital circuit that can generate a pseudorandom binary sequence. By carefully choosing the LFSR's polynomial, we can create a 4-bit counter that cycles through all 16 possible values (0 to 15) in a specific order. This approach can provide a novel architectural idea for implementing the binary counter. The LFSR will be designed to have a period of 16, ensuring it counts from 0 to 15 before resetting to 0.
