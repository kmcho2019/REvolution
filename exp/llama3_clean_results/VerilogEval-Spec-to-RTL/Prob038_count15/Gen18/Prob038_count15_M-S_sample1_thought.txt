To simplify the 4-bit binary counter design, we can abandon the finite state machine (FSM) approach with its complex case statement and instead use a straightforward arithmetic operation to increment the counter. This involves using a register to hold the current count and incrementing it by 1 at each clock cycle, unless the reset input is active, in which case the counter resets to 0. This approach reduces the complexity of the logic, potentially improving performance, power efficiency, and area utilization.
