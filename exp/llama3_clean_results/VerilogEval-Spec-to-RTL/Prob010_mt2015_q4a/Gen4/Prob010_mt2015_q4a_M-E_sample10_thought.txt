Instead of directly applying bitwise operators, we can utilize a Karnaugh map or truth table analysis to identify a more efficient logic realization. This hierarchical approach can offer benefits in terms of readability, maintainability, and potentially in PPA metrics if the separation allows for more efficient technology mapping during synthesis. This modular approach can also facilitate easier reuse of the XOR module in other parts of a larger design.
