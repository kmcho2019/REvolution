To improve the existing solution for better PPA metrics, we should focus on minimizing the design's complexity while maintaining its functionality. The current implementation utilizes a separate module for the XOR operation, which, although beneficial for readability and maintainability, might introduce additional delay and power consumption due to the module interface and the intermediate signal used. Therefore, our strategy is to integrate the XOR operation directly within the TopModule, removing the need for an additional module and intermediate signals. This approach is expected to improve timing performance by reducing the number of levels of logic and potentially decrease power consumption by minimizing switching activity. The area efficiency might also improve slightly due to the removal of the module interface overhead.
