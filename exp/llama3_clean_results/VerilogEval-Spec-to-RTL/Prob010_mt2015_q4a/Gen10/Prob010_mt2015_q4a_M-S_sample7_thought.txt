To simplify the existing solution, we can directly implement the required boolean function z = (x^y) & x within a single module, TopModule, without the need for a separate XorModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the overhead associated with instantiating another module. The simplified TopModule can directly calculate the XOR of x and y, and then apply the AND operation with x to obtain the final result. This simplification should lead to improvements in area and potentially in power consumption, as there are fewer components and less switching activity involved.
