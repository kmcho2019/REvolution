We can simplify the given boolean function z = (x^y) & x by analyzing its components. The XOR operation (x^y) will be 1 only when x and y are different. The result of this XOR operation is then ANDed with x. This means z will be 1 only when x is 1 and x is different from y, which implies y must be 0. Therefore, z is 1 only when x is 1 and y is 0. This simplifies the boolean function to z = x & (~y), where ~y represents the negation of y (i.e., the logical NOT of y).
