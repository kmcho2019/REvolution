To simplify the given boolean function z = (x^y) & x, observe that the function can be simplified by considering the properties of XOR and AND operations. The XOR operation (x^y) results in 1 only when x and y are different. When this result is ANDed with x, z will be 1 only if x is 1 and x^y is 1, meaning y must be 0 for the whole expression to be true. This simplifies the function to z = x & ~y, where ~y denotes the negation of y.
