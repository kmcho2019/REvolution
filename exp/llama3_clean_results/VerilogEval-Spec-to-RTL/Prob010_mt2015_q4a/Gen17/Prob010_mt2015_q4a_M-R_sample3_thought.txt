The goal of refactoring is to improve the design's performance, power efficiency, and area utilization while maintaining the same functionality. Given the current implementation, we can simplify the design by directly integrating the XOR and AND operations within the TopModule, eliminating the need for an intermediate signal and a separate module for the XOR operation. This approach reduces the complexity of the design, potentially leading to better timing performance and lower power consumption due to reduced switching activity and fewer signals. Additionally, the area efficiency could be improved by minimizing the number of logic operations and signals.
