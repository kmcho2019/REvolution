To simplify the implementation of the 32-bit Galois Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR), we will utilize a more systematic approach to shift the bits and apply the XOR operations at the tap positions. We recognize that the previous implementation had flaws in shifting and applying the taps. A more straightforward method is to shift all bits to the right and then apply the XOR operations only where necessary, specifically at the tap positions (32, 22, 2, and 1). This approach ensures that the next state of each bit is correctly determined based on its current state and the state of the least significant bit (LSB) for tap positions. The synchronous active high reset will still be used to reset the output q to 32'h1.
