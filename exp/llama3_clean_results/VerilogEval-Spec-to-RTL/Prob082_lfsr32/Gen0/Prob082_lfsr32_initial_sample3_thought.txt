To implement a 32-bit Galois Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) in Verilog, we need to consider the taps and the shift operation. The taps are at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. Since we are implementing a Galois LFSR, the bits at the tap positions are XORed with the least significant bit (LSB) to generate the next state. We will use a 32-bit register to hold the current state of the LFSR. On every positive edge of the clock, if the reset is not active, we will shift the bits to the right and apply the XOR operation at the tap positions. If the reset is active, we will reset the LFSR to 32'h1.
