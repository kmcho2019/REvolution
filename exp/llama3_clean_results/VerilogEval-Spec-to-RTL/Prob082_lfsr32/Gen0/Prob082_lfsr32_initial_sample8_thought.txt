To implement the TopModule with the specified interface, we will create a 32-bit Galois Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) with taps at bit positions 32, 22, 2, and 1. The LFSR will shift right, and the bit positions with taps will be XORed with the Least Significant Bit (LSB) output bit (q[0]) to produce their next state. We will use a synchronous reset that is active high, which means when the reset signal is high, the LFSR will reset to 32'h1 on the positive edge of the clock. The module will be implemented using Verilog's always block for sequential logic and bitwise operations for the LFSR's XOR and shift operations.
