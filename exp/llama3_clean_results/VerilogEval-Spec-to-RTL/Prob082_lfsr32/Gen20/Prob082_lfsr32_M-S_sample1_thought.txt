To simplify the implementation of the 32-bit Galois LFSR, we should focus on directly applying the Galois LFSR rule. This involves shifting bits to the right and applying the feedback to the tapped positions. The tapped positions are at bit positions 31, 21, 2, and 1 (considering 0-indexing). We will use the LSB (q[0]) to compute the next state of these tapped positions, while other positions will simply shift their values to the right. The reset mechanism remains the same, setting the output `q` to `32'h1` when `reset` is high. This approach minimizes unnecessary logic and directly implements the required functionality.
