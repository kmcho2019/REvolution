To simplify the implementation of the 32-bit Galois LFSR, we focus on accurately applying the XOR operation at the specified tap positions (32, 22, 2, and 1) with the least significant bit (LSB), while shifting other bits right unchanged. Considering 0-indexing, these taps correspond to `q[31]`, `q[21]`, `q[1]`, and `q[0]`. The process involves first shifting all bits one position to the right to capture the new state of the shift register. Then, the tapped positions are updated based on the XOR of the current LSB (`q[0]`) with the bits at their respective positions after the shift. This approach ensures the generation of a pseudorandom sequence as per the Galois LFSR's operational principle. The reset operation should set the output `q` to `32'h1`, initializing the LFSR with the LSB set to 1 and all other bits to 0.
