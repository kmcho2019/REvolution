To implement a 32-bit Galois LFSR, we can utilize a novel approach that leverages a lookup table (LUT) to generate the next state of the LFSR. This approach involves storing the tap positions in a LUT and using the current state of the LFSR to index into the LUT. The LUT would contain the XOR of the taps for each possible input, allowing for efficient calculation of the feedback term. This approach would simplify the design and improve its scalability.
