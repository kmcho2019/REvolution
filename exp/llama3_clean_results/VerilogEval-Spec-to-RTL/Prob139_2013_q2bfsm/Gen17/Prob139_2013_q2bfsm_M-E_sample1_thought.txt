The Idle state is the initial state, and the FSM transitions to Seq1 when 'x' is 1. The FSM then transitions to Seq2 when 'x' is 0, and finally to Active when 'x' is 1 again. In the Active state, the FSM checks the input 'y' and sets 'g' to 1 if 'y' is 1 within two clock cycles. If 'y' is not 1 within two clock cycles, the FSM sets 'g' to 0.

This approach eliminates the need for separate modules for sequence detection and output control, reducing the overall complexity of the design. Additionally, the use of a single FSM simplifies the state transitions and output control, making the design more efficient and easier to understand.
