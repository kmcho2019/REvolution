To simplify the existing solution, I propose a revised architectural approach that focuses on reducing complexity while maintaining functionality. The main idea is to eliminate unnecessary sub-modules and directly implement the finite state machine (FSM) logic within the TopModule.

The FSM will have a simple structure, with states for resetting, pulsing 'f', and controlling 'g'. The sequence detection for 'x' and 'y' will be handled within the TopModule, eliminating the need for separate sub-modules.

To reduce complexity, the sequence detection for 'x' will be implemented using a simple state machine with two states: idle and sequence detected. The 'y' detection will be handled using a counter-based approach, but with a simplified logic to reduce area consumption and improve timing performance.

By eliminating unnecessary sub-modules and simplifying the FSM logic, the design becomes more compact, efficient, and easier to understand.
