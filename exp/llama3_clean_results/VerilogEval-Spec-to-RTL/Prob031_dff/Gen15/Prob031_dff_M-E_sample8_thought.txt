This design would use two latches in series to create a master-slave flip-flop, ensuring that the output is updated only on the positive edge of the clock. The first latch (master) would be transparent when the clock is high, allowing the input to propagate through. The second latch (slave) would be transparent when the clock is low, capturing the state of the master latch and holding it until the next clock edge. This architecture ensures that the output is stable during the entire clock cycle, adhering to the principles of sequential logic.
