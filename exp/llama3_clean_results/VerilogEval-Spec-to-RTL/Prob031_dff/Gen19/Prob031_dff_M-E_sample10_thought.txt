A transparent latch is essentially a level-sensitive latch that captures its input when the enable signal is high and holds the value when the enable signal is low. By cascading two such latches with complementary enable signals derived from the clock, we can create an edge-triggered D flip-flop. This approach inherently utilizes the positive edge of the clock to capture the input value and can be an alternative method to achieve the functionality of a D flip-flop.

The second latch is enabled during the high phase of the clock.
