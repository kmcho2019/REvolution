A novel architectural idea for implementing a D flip-flop is to utilize a latch-based approach. Instead of directly using a reg and assigning it on the positive edge of the clock, we can implement two latches in a master-slave configuration. The master latch captures the input data on the positive edge of the clock, and the slave latch then passes this captured data to the output on the negative edge of the clock, but since we are only interested in the positive edge for our output, we can simplify this by directly using the master latch's output as our final output. This approach, while more complex, illustrates an alternative method of achieving the same functionality.
