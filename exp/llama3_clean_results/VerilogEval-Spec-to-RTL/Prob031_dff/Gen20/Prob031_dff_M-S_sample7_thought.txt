The goal is to simplify the existing D flip-flop implementation while maintaining its functionality. Since the current design is already minimal and directly implements a D flip-flop, simplification efforts should focus on removing any unnecessary elements or comments that do not contribute to the design's functionality or optimization. The existing code is concise and effectively implements a D flip-flop. The addition of a reset signal could enhance the module's versatility but would also introduce additional complexity. Given the focus on simplification, we will aim to preserve the essential elements that make the design functional and optimized for PPA metrics.
