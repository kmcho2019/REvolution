The existing implementation of the D flip-flop uses an always block to update the output 'q' on the positive edge of the clock 'clk'. However, another approach is to utilize the built-in Verilog primitive for a D flip-flop, which directly models the behavior of a D flip-flop without the need for an always block. This can be achieved by using the 'FD' (D Flip-Flop) primitive provided by Verilog. The 'FD' primitive is a basic digital circuit element that captures the value of its input 'd' at the positive edge of its clock 'clk' and stores this value at its output 'q' until the next clock edge. By directly instantiating this primitive within our TopModule, we can maintain the same functionality while potentially improving the readability and efficiency of the design.
