To simplify the existing D flip-flop solution, we can maintain the core functionality while ensuring the design remains triggered on the positive edge of the clock. The essential operation of a D flip-flop is to capture the input `d` at the rising edge of `clk` and assign it to the output `q`. This can be achieved with a basic `always` block sensitive to the positive edge of the clock, directly assigning `d` to `q` within this block. Since the `q` is already defined as a `reg` type, which is suitable for sequential logic, no further modifications are necessary to maintain functionality.
