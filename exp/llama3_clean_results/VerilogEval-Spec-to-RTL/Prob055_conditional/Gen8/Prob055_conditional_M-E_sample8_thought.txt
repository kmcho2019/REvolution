However, instead of directly sorting all four values, we can adopt a hybrid approach that first identifies the smallest two values and then compares them to determine the overall minimum. This method involves selecting pairs of inputs and determining the smaller value within each pair, then further comparing these smaller values to arrive at the minimum. The key here is to use a combination of multiplexers (MUX) that can be controlled based on comparison results to steer the smallest value towards the output.
