A novel approach to finding the minimum of four 8-bit unsigned numbers involves using a sorting network. A sorting network is a circuit that rearranges its inputs to produce a sorted output. By leveraging this concept, we can create a module that identifies the smallest input value. The idea is to use a comparator-based sorting network to compare and rearrange the inputs until the smallest value emerges at the first position. This approach will not only provide an alternative method for finding the minimum but also demonstrate how a sorting network can be utilized for such a task.
