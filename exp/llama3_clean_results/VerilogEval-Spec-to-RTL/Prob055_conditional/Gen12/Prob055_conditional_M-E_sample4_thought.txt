To implement a novel architectural idea for finding the minimum of four input values, we can utilize a priority encoder-like structure where all four inputs are compared simultaneously, and the smallest value is selected based on a priority-based selection mechanism. This approach can be realized using a combination of comparison operations and a multiplexer. The multiplexer can be controlled by the comparison results, allowing it to select the smallest value among the four inputs. This approach eliminates the need for a hierarchical structure, reducing the area utilization and potentially improving performance.
