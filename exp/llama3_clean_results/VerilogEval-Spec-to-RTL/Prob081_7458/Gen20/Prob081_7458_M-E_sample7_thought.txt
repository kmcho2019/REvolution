To create a novel solution, we will rethink the approach to implementing the 7458 chip's functionality. Instead of using separate modules for AND and OR gates, we will utilize a more integrated approach that leverages Verilog's built-in logical operators directly within the TopModule. This method aims to reduce the overall complexity and potentially improve PPA metrics by minimizing the number of instantiated modules and focusing on a streamlined, combinational logic implementation.

Our new architectural idea involves using intermediate wires to compute the outputs of the AND gates directly within the TopModule, followed by the computation of the final outputs (p1y and p2y) using these intermediate results. This approach maintains readability and modularity while potentially offering better optimization opportunities for the synthesis tool.

Furthermore, we will explore the use of a single, hierarchical module that encapsulates all the necessary logic, thereby reducing the number of separate module instantiations and potentially leading to a more area-efficient design. This design choice is motivated by the goal of achieving a better balance between the design's complexity and its PPA metrics, especially considering the already favorable power consumption and area metrics of the original design.
