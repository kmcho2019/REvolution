The original code implements the 7458 chip functionality using separate modules for AND gates and continuous assignments for OR gates. To refactor this code, we will maintain the same core functionality but simplify the design by directly implementing the AND and OR logic within the TopModule using only `assign` statements. This approach reduces the number of modules and potentially simplifies the synthesis process.

The main changes include:
1. Removing the separate AND gate modules (`and2` and `and3`) and directly implementing the AND operations within the `TopModule`.
2. Utilizing `assign` statements for both AND and OR operations to simplify the design and potentially reduce synthesis overhead.

By doing so, we aim to achieve a more streamlined design that is easier to understand and maintain, while potentially offering minor improvements in terms of power consumption and area utilization due to reduced module instantiation overhead.
