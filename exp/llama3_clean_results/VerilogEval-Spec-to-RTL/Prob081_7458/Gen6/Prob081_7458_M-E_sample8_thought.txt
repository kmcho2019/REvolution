The novel architectural idea here is to utilize a single, parameterized module for both AND and OR operations, leveraging Verilog's ability to handle generic modules with parameters. This approach reduces the number of module definitions and potentially simplifies the instantiation within the TopModule. Additionally, we'll use a more direct method to calculate the outputs p1y and p2y by embedding the logic for AND and OR gates directly within the TopModule, eliminating the need for intermediate wires for the AND operations. This strategy aims to improve performance by reducing module overhead and simplifying the design hierarchy.
