To implement the TopModule with the same functionality as the 7458 chip, 
we can use a different approach by utilizing internal wires to store the 
outputs of the AND gates. For p1y, we will create two internal wires, 
and_gate1 and and_gate2, to store the results of the 3-input AND gates 
using p1a, p1b, p1c and p1d, p1e, p1f respectively. Then, we will use 
an OR gate to combine the values stored in and_gate1 and and_gate2. 
Similarly, for p2y, we will create two internal wires, and_gate3 and 
and_gate4, to store the results of the 2-input AND gates using p2a, p2b 
and p2c, p2d respectively, and then use an OR gate to combine their 
values. This approach allows for a more modular design and easier 
debugging.
