The novel architectural idea is to implement the 7458 chip functionality using a single module that directly combines the required AND and OR operations without instantiating separate gate modules. This approach simplifies the design hierarchy and potentially reduces overhead. By utilizing Verilog's built-in logical operators, we can express the desired functionality in a concise manner. The p1y output will be the result of an OR operation between two AND operations, one involving p1a, p1b, and p1c, and the other involving p1d, p1e, and p1f. Similarly, p2y will be the result of an OR operation between two AND operations, one involving p2a and p2b, and the other involving p2c and p2d. This approach minimizes the use of intermediate wires and modules, potentially leading to a more efficient design.
