The TopModule can be designed using basic logic gates. Looking at the simulation waveform, we can observe that the output z is 1 when both x and y are 1, or when both x and y are 0. This behavior can be implemented using a combination of AND and OR gates, along with inverters to handle the cases where x or y is 0.
