The novel architectural idea here is to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) to implement the logic of the TopModule. The FSM will have two states: S0 and S1. In state S0, the output z will be 1 if both x and y are 0, and in state S1, the output z will be 0 if either x or y (but not both) is 1. The FSM will transition from S0 to S1 when either x or y becomes 1, and it will transition from S1 to S0 when both x and y are 0.

This approach allows for a more sequential and state-based implementation of the logic, which can be useful in certain design contexts where a more traditional combinational logic approach may not be feasible or desirable.
