A simplified approach to implementing the TopModule is to recognize the logic pattern from the given simulation waveform. The output `z` can be observed to be high when either both `x` and `y` are high or when both `x` and `y` are low. This can be directly implemented using a simple logic expression without needing to explicitly apply K-map simplification or complex Boolean algebra manipulations. The expression for `z` can thus be derived by inspecting the waveform and understanding the conditions under which `z` is high or low.
