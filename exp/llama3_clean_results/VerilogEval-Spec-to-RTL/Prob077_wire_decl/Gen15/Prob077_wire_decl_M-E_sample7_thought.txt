To create a novel solution, I will introduce a new architectural idea that involves a multi-stage logic implementation. Instead of directly implementing the AND and OR gates, I will break down the logic into smaller, more manageable stages. The first stage will consist of two XOR gates that compute the parity of the inputs (a, b) and (c, d). The second stage will use the outputs of the XOR gates to compute the final output. This approach will not only simplify the logic but also reduce the overall area and power consumption. Additionally, I will use a single continuous assignment to compute the inverted output, out_n, to minimize area and power overhead.
