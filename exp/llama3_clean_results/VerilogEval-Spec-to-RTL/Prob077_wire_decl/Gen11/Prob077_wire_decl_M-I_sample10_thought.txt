To optimize the existing solution for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, the approach involves simplifying the design by integrating the AND, OR, and NOT gates directly into the TopModule. This simplification can help reduce the overhead associated with module instantiation, potentially leading to improved synthesis results. Specifically, we will eliminate the need for separate AND, OR, and NOT gate modules and instead use continuous assignments within the TopModule to achieve the desired functionality. This method can enhance power efficiency and reduce area consumption. Additionally, directly inverting the output using a simple assignment within the TopModule can further reduce the need for separate module instantiations, contributing to area and power savings.
