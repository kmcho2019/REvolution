To simplify the solution, I will eliminate the need for separate modules for the AND and OR gates by directly implementing their logic within the TopModule. This approach reduces the overall complexity by minimizing the number of module instances and their associated overheads. The NOT gate can still be efficiently implemented using a simple assign statement within the TopModule, as seen in the previous solution. By integrating the gate logic directly into the TopModule, the design becomes more concise and potentially more efficient in terms of power consumption and area usage.
