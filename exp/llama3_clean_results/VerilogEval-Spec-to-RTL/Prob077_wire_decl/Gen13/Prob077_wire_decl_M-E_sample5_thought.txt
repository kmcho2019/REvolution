To generate a new and different solution, I will employ a structural approach to implement the TopModule. Instead of using continuous assignments or separate sub-modules for each gate, I will create a single module that instantiates basic logic gates as primitive components. This approach allows for a more direct representation of the digital circuit, making it easier to visualize and understand the flow of signals. The TopModule will consist of two AND gates, one OR gate, and one NOT gate, all connected according to the given circuit diagram. By using a structural approach, I can focus on the interconnectivity of the gates, ensuring that the design accurately reflects the desired functionality.
