To refactor the existing TopModule code, we will utilize continuous assignments to directly express the logic of the AND gates, the OR gate, and the NOT gate without explicitly instantiating these gates. This approach streamlines the code by eliminating the need to declare intermediate wires for the AND gates' outputs, making the design more concise while maintaining the same functionality. The core idea remains the same: the module takes inputs a, b, c, and d, applies them to two AND gates, then an OR gate, and finally inverts the OR gate's output for the out_n signal.
