To simplify the solution while maintaining its functionality, we can eliminate the need for separate modules for the AND and OR gates. Instead, we can directly use the bitwise operators `&` and `|` within the `TopModule` to implement the AND and OR logic. This approach reduces the overall complexity by minimizing the number of modules and directly assigns the result of the AND operations to intermediate wires. The output of the OR operation can then be directly assigned to the `out` port, and the inverted output can be assigned to `out_n` using the `~` operator. This simplification maintains the design's readability while potentially reducing area usage and power consumption due to fewer module instantiations.
