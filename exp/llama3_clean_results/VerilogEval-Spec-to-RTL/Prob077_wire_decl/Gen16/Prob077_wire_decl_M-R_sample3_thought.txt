To refactor this code, I will simplify the design by removing the separate modules for the AND and OR gates. Instead, I will use continuous assignments to directly implement the logic within the TopModule. This approach reduces the number of module instances and potentially lowers power consumption due to reduced inter-module overhead. The NOT gate for the out_n signal will be implemented using a simple assign statement. The core idea remains the same: two AND gates are used to compute the intermediate results, and then an OR gate is used to compute the final output. The refactored code will maintain the same functionality while improving conciseness and efficiency.
