The novel approach involves implementing the 2-1 multiplexer using a combination of bitwise AND and OR operations, along with bitwise NOT operations on the 'sel' signal. This method provides an alternative perspective on implementing multiplexers and can be useful in scenarios where the synthesizer prefers or optimizes bitwise operations over conditional statements.
