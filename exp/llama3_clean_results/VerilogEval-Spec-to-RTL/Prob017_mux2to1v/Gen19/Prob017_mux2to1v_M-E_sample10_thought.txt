A novel approach to implementing the 2-1 multiplexer in Verilog involves utilizing the ternary operator within a continuous assignment statement for each output bit. This method, although similar in functionality to the original for-loop approach, emphasizes the use of a single, concise statement to perform the selection between inputs 'a' and 'b' based on 'sel'. However, to introduce a different architectural idea, we can consider using a case statement within a always block to select the output. This method, while less common for such a straightforward multiplexing operation, can provide an alternative perspective on how to achieve the desired functionality, especially in scenarios where more complex decision logic might be needed in the future.
