A novel architectural approach to implement the 2-1 multiplexer is to use a combination of bitwise AND and OR operations along with bit-wise inversion. The idea involves creating two mask vectors based on the 'sel' input: one for selecting bits from 'a' and the other for selecting bits from 'b'. When 'sel' is 0, the mask for 'a' will have all bits set to 1, and the mask for 'b' will have all bits set to 0. Conversely, when 'sel' is 1, the mask for 'a' will be all 0s, and the mask for 'b' will be all 1s. These masks are then used in bitwise AND operations with 'a' and 'b', respectively. The results of these AND operations are then combined using a bitwise OR operation to produce the final output. This method leverages the properties of bitwise operations to effectively implement the multiplexer logic without directly using the ternary operator or explicit if-else statements.
