To create a novel solution, we will utilize a unique architectural approach by employing a decoder and a set of tri-state buffers to implement the 2-1 multiplexer. This approach will allow us to select between the two inputs, 'a' and 'b', based on the 'sel' input. When 'sel' is 0, the tri-state buffers associated with input 'a' will be enabled, and when 'sel' is 1, the tri-state buffers associated with input 'b' will be enabled. This design will not only implement the required multiplexer functionality but also provide an alternative perspective on how to approach digital design problems.
