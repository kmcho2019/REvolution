To implement a novel architectural solution for the 2-1 multiplexer, we can utilize a combination of clocked registers and a simple control logic. The idea involves using two registers to store the input values 'a' and 'b', and then using the 'sel' input to control a multiplexer that selects between the two registered values. This approach introduces a slight delay due to the registration of inputs but can be beneficial in scenarios where the inputs 'a' and 'b' are not stable at the same time or when the system requires a synchronized output. The novelty lies in the registration of inputs and the use of a clock signal to drive the multiplexer, which could be particularly useful in synchronous designs where the timing of signals is critical.
