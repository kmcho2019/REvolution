The novel idea is to implement the 2-1 multiplexer using a combination of bitwise AND and OR operations, with the select line 'sel' and its inverse controlling the selection of inputs 'a' and 'b'. This approach leverages the properties of bitwise operations to minimize the number of logic gates required, potentially reducing area usage and power consumption. By using the bitwise AND operation with 'sel' and 'b', and the bitwise AND operation with the inverse of 'sel' and 'a', we can then use the bitwise OR operation to combine these results, effectively implementing the multiplexer functionality in a more compact form.

The conceptual steps are:
1. Compute the bitwise AND of 'sel' and 'b'.
2. Compute the bitwise AND of the inverse of 'sel' and 'a'.
3. Compute the bitwise OR of the results from steps 1 and 2 to produce the final output 'out'.

This approach can potentially optimize the PPA metrics by reducing the number of logic operations and the switching activity on the output, as the output 'out' will only change when either 'sel', 'a', or 'b' changes, and the bitwise operations minimize the combinatorial logic depth.
