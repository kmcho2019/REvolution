This approach involves dividing the input vectors into smaller segments and then using a hierarchical multiplexer tree to make the selection. The idea is to reduce the number of multiplexers required and minimize the propagation delay by selecting between smaller segments of 'a' and 'b' at each level of the tree. By doing so, we aim to improve the overall performance and reduce the area and power consumption of the design.

The proposed architecture will involve the following steps:
1. Divide the 100-bit input vectors into smaller segments, for example, 4-bit or 8-bit segments.
2. Implement a level of multiplexers to select between corresponding segments of 'a' and 'b' based on the 'sel' signal.
3. The outputs of these multiplexers will then be combined to form the final 100-bit output.
