A novel architectural solution for the 2-1 multiplexer problem is to utilize a bitwise conditional assignment approach. Instead of using smaller multiplexers or hierarchical structures, the idea is to directly assign each bit of the output based on the select line. This approach leverages the conciseness of Verilog's conditional operator to minimize the amount of code and potential area usage, while maintaining the simplicity and performance of the original requirement. By assigning each output bit conditionally, the design can achieve the same functionality as the original problem statement but with a focus on minimalism and potentially improved area efficiency.
