A novel architectural idea for implementing a 2-1 multiplexer in Verilog involves utilizing a case statement within a always block. This approach, although not as concise as the conditional operator, demonstrates an alternative way of achieving the multiplexing functionality. The always block will be sensitive to changes in the select signal 'sel', as well as the input vectors 'a' and 'b'. Based on the value of 'sel', the case statement will assign either 'a' or 'b' to the output 'out'.
