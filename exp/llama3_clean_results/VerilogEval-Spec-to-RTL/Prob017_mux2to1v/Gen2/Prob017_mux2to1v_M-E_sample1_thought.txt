To implement a 2-1 multiplexer in a novel way, we can utilize a combination of bitwise AND and OR operations with the select line 'sel' to control the flow of data from inputs 'a' and 'b' to the output 'out'. This approach exploits the fact that in digital logic, a bitwise AND operation with 1 (or a high signal) passes the signal through unchanged, while a bitwise AND operation with 0 (or a low signal) results in 0. Similarly, a bitwise OR operation with 0 passes the signal through unchanged, and with 1 results in 1. By using the select line 'sel' and its inverted version to control these operations, we can effectively multiplex between 'a' and 'b' without directly using conditional statements or ternary operators.
