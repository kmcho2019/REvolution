To implement a 2-1 multiplexer with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a combination of bitwise operations and a masking approach. The idea is to create a mask based on the select signal 'sel' that, when applied to the inputs 'a' and 'b', effectively selects the desired input. This can be achieved by using bitwise AND and OR operations. When 'sel' is 0, the mask will allow 'a' to pass through, and when 'sel' is 1, the mask will allow 'b' to pass through.
