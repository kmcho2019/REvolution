To create a novel architectural solution for the 2-1 multiplexer problem, we will leverage the concept of bit-level parallelism and modular design. The idea is to split the 100-bit inputs into smaller chunks, process each chunk independently using smaller multiplexers, and then combine the outputs. This approach can potentially offer better scalability, improved area efficiency, and reduced power consumption due to the smaller switching activities in each chunk. The novelty lies in the hierarchical structure, where each level of hierarchy deals with a smaller subset of the input bits, thereby reducing the overall complexity and potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
