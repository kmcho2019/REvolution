A novel approach to implementing the 2-1 multiplexer in Verilog is to utilize a case statement within a combinational always block. This method differs from the traditional ternary operator or for loop approaches by leveraging the case statement's ability to evaluate multiple conditions and select a specific action based on the input. In this context, the case statement will evaluate the 'sel' input and assign either 'a' or 'b' to the output 'out' accordingly. This architectural idea allows for clear and explicit control over the multiplexing logic, potentially offering better readability and maintainability of the code. Furthermore, to optimize area and power consumption, we will explore using a single, wide assignment for the output within the always block, similar to the vectorized assignment in the previous examples. This approach may allow the synthesis tool to more effectively optimize the logic, leading to improved PPA metrics.
