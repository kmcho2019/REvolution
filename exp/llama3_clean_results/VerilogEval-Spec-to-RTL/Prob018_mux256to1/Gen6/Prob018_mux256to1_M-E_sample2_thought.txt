The novel architectural idea for the 256-to-1 multiplexer involves utilizing a binary decoding approach to directly address the 256-bit input vector. Instead of dividing the selection process into multiple stages, we can use the 8-bit select signal to generate a one-hot encoded signal that corresponds to the desired input bit. This approach can potentially reduce the overall area and power consumption while maintaining performance.
