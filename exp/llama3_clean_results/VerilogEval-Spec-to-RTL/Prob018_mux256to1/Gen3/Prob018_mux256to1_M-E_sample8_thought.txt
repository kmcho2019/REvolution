A novel approach to implement a 256-to-1 multiplexer is to divide the 256-bit input vector into smaller chunks, specifically 16 chunks of 16 bits each. Then, use a two-stage multiplexer structure: the first stage selects one of the 16 chunks based on the 4 most significant bits of the sel input, and the second stage selects one bit from the chosen chunk based on the 4 least significant bits of the sel input. This hierarchical approach can potentially reduce the area and power consumption compared to a flat 256-to-1 multiplexer.
