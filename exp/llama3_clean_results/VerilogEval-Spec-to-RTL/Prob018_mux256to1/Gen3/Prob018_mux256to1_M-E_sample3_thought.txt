The novel approach is to implement the 256-to-1 multiplexer using a tree-like structure of smaller multiplexers. This design leverages the fact that a large multiplexer can be broken down into smaller, more manageable pieces, which can then be combined to achieve the desired functionality. Specifically, this solution will utilize 16-to-1 multiplexers as the building blocks, as 16 is a power of 2 (2^4) and fits neatly into the 256-bit input vector, allowing for efficient division and combination. By structuring the multiplexers in a hierarchical manner, we can create a more scalable and potentially more efficient design in terms of area and power consumption.
