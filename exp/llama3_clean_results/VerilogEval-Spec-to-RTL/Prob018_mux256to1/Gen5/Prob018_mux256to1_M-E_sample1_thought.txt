A novel architectural idea for the 256-to-1 multiplexer involves using a tree-like structure composed of smaller, 16-to-1 multiplexers. The 256-bit input vector is divided into 16 groups of 16 bits, and each group is fed into a 16-to-1 multiplexer. The select signal is divided into two parts: the most significant 4 bits select one of the 16 multiplexers, and the least significant 4 bits select one bit from the output of the chosen multiplexer. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design, and the use of smaller multiplexers can potentially reduce the overall complexity and improve performance.
