The novel architectural idea is to utilize a shift register to efficiently compare each bit with its neighbors. By shifting the input vector through a series of flip-flops, we can directly access each bit and its neighbor, simplifying the logic for calculating `out_both`, `out_any`, and `out_different`. This approach avoids the need for complex conditional statements and generate loops, potentially improving the design's performance, power efficiency, and area usage. The shift register can be implemented using a simple D Flip-Flop chain, with the input vector being shifted in one bit at a time.
