To simplify the TopModule implementation, we can maintain the same logic but use more straightforward conditional statements and indexing. We can directly compare the current bit with its neighbors without using complex conditional expressions. For out_both, we compare the current bit with its left neighbor. For out_any, we compare the current bit with its right neighbor. For out_different, we compare the current bit with its left neighbor, wrapping around to the start of the vector for the last bit.
