To improve the existing population count circuit, we will focus on optimizing the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given the current design's PPA metrics, the main concerns are the area consumption and power usage. To address these issues, we will adopt a pipelined approach to reduce the combinational logic depth, thereby improving performance. We will also utilize a more efficient counter architecture, such as a tree-based counter, to minimize the number of addition operations and reduce the area consumption. Additionally, we will optimize the bit width of the intermediate signals to further reduce area requirements.
