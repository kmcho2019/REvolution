To implement the TopModule with a 5-bit maximal-length Galois LFSR, we first need to understand the arrangement of the LFSR. Since the taps are at bit positions 5 and 3, we will XOR these positions with the LSB output bit (q[0]) to produce the next value. The rest of the bits will shift right unchanged. We will use a 5-bit register to store the current state of the LFSR and update it at each positive edge of the clock. The initial state of the LFSR will be set to 1 when the reset signal is high.
