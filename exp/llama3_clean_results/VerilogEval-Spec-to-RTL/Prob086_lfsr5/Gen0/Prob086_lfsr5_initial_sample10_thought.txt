To implement the TopModule, we'll create a 5-bit Galois Linear Feedback Shift Register (LFSR) with taps at positions 5 and 3. Since the taps are at positions 5 and 3, and the bit positions are 0-indexed, we will tap bits at positions 4 and 2 (since the positions are usually 0-indexed). The next state of each bit position will be determined by the XOR of the current bit position (if tapped) with the least significant bit (LSB), and the current bit position shifted right by one position (if not tapped). On reset, we will initialize the LFSR output to 1. The implementation will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock signal.
