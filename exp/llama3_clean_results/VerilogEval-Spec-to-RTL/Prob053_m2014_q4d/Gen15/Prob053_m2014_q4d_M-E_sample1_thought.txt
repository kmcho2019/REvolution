To address the given problem, a novel architectural approach is to design the circuit using a separate module for the XOR operation and another for the D flip-flop. This modular approach enhances maintainability, readability, and scalability. The XOR module will be designed using a continuous assignment to reflect the combinational nature of the XOR operation, while the D flip-flop module will be implemented using a procedural assignment within an always block to capture its sequential behavior. This separation allows for clear and efficient implementation of each component's functionality, making the overall design more understandable and easier to debug.
