To implement the TopModule with a novel architectural idea, we can leverage the concept of a "sample and hold" circuit to capture the input 'in' and the current state of the flip-flop 'out' at the positive edge of the clock 'clk', and then use these captured values to drive the XOR gate. This approach decouples the XOR gate's inputs from the flip-flop's output 'out', avoiding the combinational loop issue. The captured input 'captured_in' and captured output 'captured_out' are then used to compute the XOR result, which is stored in a separate register 'xor_result'. On the next positive edge of 'clk', the 'xor_result' is used to update the flip-flop's output 'out'. This architecture ensures a clean separation of the combinational logic from the sequential logic, resolving the timing issues associated with the original design.
