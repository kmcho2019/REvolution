To simplify the solution, we should maintain the same functionality but reduce the complexity. The current implementation directly uses 'out' in the XOR operation within the always block, which might cause issues due to the simultaneous read and write of 'out'. A simpler approach is to separate the XOR operation from the flip-flop's assignment. We can introduce a temporary variable to hold the result of the XOR operation, and then assign this result to 'out' on the positive edge of 'clk'. This separation can help in avoiding potential issues related to the timing and behavior of 'out' being used in its own calculation.
