To implement the required circuit with a novel approach, we can leverage the concept of a pipelined architecture, although in this simple case, it might not directly apply due to the feedback loop. Instead, we focus on breaking down the problem into more manageable, synthesizable components. The key insight is to explicitly separate the XOR operation from the flip-flop's operation, even though we're still using a single always block for simplicity. This is conceptually different from the original attempt because we're now focusing on the explicit handling of the feedback path, recognizing that the output of the flip-flop is indeed the previous state before the current clock edge. We will introduce an internal signal to hold the output of the XOR gate, computed based on the current input and the previous state of the flip-flop (which is the output before the clock edge), thus avoiding the race condition issue.
