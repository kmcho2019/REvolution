The current solution can be simplified by directly evaluating the conditions for incrementing and decrementing the counter within the `always` block, eliminating the need for separate `increment` and `decrement` wires. This approach reduces the combinational logic gates required, potentially improving area and power consumption. Additionally, the use of non-blocking assignments (`<=`) ensures that the updates to `state_reg` are synchronized with the clock edge, which is crucial for sequential logic. By simplifying the conditional logic and reducing the number of signals, the design can become more efficient and easier to understand.
