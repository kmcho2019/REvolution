A novel architectural idea for implementing the two-bit saturating counter is to use a state machine with an enumeration of all possible states and transitions. This approach allows for a clear and explicit definition of the counter's behavior, making it easier to understand and maintain. The state machine will have four states: S0 (2'b00), S1 (2'b01), S2 (2'b10), and S3 (2'b11), representing the possible values of the counter. The transitions between these states will be determined by the input signals train_valid and train_taken. This approach also allows for easy extension or modification of the counter's behavior by simply adding or modifying states and transitions.
