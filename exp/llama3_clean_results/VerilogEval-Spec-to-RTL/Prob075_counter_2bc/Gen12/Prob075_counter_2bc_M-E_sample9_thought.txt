The novel architectural idea is to implement a simple, two-bit saturating counter using a combination of sequential logic and conditional statements. This approach involves using a single always block to handle both the reset and the clock edge, and then using if-else statements to determine the next state of the counter based on the input conditions. The counter will be designed to increment when both train_valid and train_taken are high, decrement when train_valid is high and train_taken is low, and remain unchanged when train_valid is low. The counter will saturate at a maximum value of 3 (2'b11) and a minimum value of 0 (2'b00).
