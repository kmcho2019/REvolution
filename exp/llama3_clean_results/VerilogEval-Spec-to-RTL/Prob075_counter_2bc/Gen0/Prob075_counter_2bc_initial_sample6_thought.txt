The TopModule will contain a two-bit saturating counter that increments or decrements based on the values of train_valid and train_taken. The counter will be reset to 2'b01 when areset is high. On every positive edge of the clock, the counter will be updated if train_valid is high, otherwise it will retain its current value. We will use a if-else statement inside a always block to check the conditions and update the counter accordingly.
