The module TopModule will be implemented as a finite state machine with a two-bit saturating counter. The counter will be updated based on the values of train_valid and train_taken. When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 1, the counter will increment. When train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 0, the counter will decrement. The counter will not change when train_valid is 0. The areset input will asynchronously reset the counter to 2'b01. The state of the counter will be output as state[1:0].
