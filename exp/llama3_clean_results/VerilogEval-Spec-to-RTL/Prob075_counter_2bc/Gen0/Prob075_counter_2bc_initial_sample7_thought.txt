To implement the TopModule, I will use a two-bit register to store the counter value. The register will be reset to 2'b01 on the positive edge of areset. On the positive edge of clk, I will check the values of train_valid and train_taken. If train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 1, I will increment the counter unless it is already at its maximum value (3). If train_valid is 1 and train_taken is 0, I will decrement the counter unless it is already at its minimum value (0). If train_valid is 0, I will keep the counter value unchanged.
