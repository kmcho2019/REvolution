To create a novel solution, we can leverage a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of finite state machines (FSMs) with arithmetic logic units (ALUs) while introducing a novel aspect: utilizing a single, parameterizable increment/decrement module that can be easily configured based on the input conditions. This approach will not only simplify the control logic but also make the design more modular and scalable. By using a single module for both increment and decrement operations, we reduce code duplication and make the design easier to maintain and extend. The FSM will determine the direction of the counter update (increment or decrement) based on the `train_valid` and `train_taken` signals, and then the parameterizable module will perform the actual update while ensuring saturation at the minimum and maximum values.
