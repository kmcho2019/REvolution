To implement a novel solution, I will utilize a finite state machine (FSM) with two states: idle and training. The FSM will be responsible for managing the two-bit counter based on the input signals. When in the idle state, the counter remains unchanged. When in the training state, the counter increments or decrements according to the train_taken signal. This approach allows for a clear separation of concerns between the counter logic and the state management, potentially improving the overall design's maintainability and scalability.
