To implement the TopModule, we will design a Moore state machine with four states: idle_left, idle_right, falling_left, and falling_right. 
The idle_left and idle_right states represent the Lemming walking left and right, respectively. 
The falling_left and falling_right states represent the Lemming falling while previously walking left and right, respectively. 
The state machine will transition between these states based on the input signals bump_left, bump_right, and ground. 
When the ground signal is high, the state machine will be in either idle_left or idle_right. 
When the ground signal is low, the state machine will transition to either falling_left or falling_right based on the previous walking direction. 
The walking direction will be updated based on the bump_left and bump_right signals. 
The output signals walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be generated based on the current state of the state machine.
