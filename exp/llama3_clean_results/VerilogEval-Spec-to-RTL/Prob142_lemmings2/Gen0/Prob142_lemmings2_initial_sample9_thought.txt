To implement the Lemmings behavior as a Moore state machine, we will use three states: walking_left, walking_right, and falling. When the Lemming is in the walking_left or walking_right state, it will output the corresponding walk direction and check for bumps and ground conditions. If the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will transition to the falling state and output "aaah!". While in the falling state, it will ignore bumps and wait for the ground to reappear. When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will transition back to the previous walking direction state. If a bump is detected while walking, the Lemming will switch direction. The asynchronous reset (areset) will reset the Lemming to the walking_left state.
