To solve this problem, we'll design a Moore state machine that keeps track of the Lemming's direction and whether it's currently falling. The state machine will have three states: walking left, walking right, and falling. When the Lemming is bumped, it will change its direction. If the ground disappears (ground=0), the Lemming will start falling and say "aaah!". When the ground reappears (ground=1), the Lemming will resume walking in the same direction as before the fall. Being bumped while falling or in the same cycle as ground disappears or reappears will not affect the walking direction.
