To simplify the solution, we can merge the WalkingDirection and FallingLogic modules into a single module, TopModule, and use a single finite state machine to handle both the walking direction and the falling logic. We will use two bits to represent the state: the first bit will indicate whether the Lemming is walking (0) or falling (1), and the second bit will indicate the direction (0 for left, 1 for right).

When the Lemming is walking and hits an obstacle, we switch the direction. If the ground disappears, the Lemming starts falling. When the ground reappears, the Lemming resumes walking in the same direction as before the fall. Being bumped while falling does not affect the walking direction.

By using a single state machine, we can reduce the complexity of the design and make it easier to understand and maintain.
