However, the falling states could be combined into a single state, as the direction of falling doesn't affect the next state (when the ground reappears, the Lemming resumes walking in the same direction as before the fall). This simplification reduces the complexity of the state machine.

Another strategy is to use `assign` statements for output logic, as seen in the original solution. This approach can reduce area usage and improve readability.

Additionally, we can simplify the combinational logic for determining the next state. By analyzing the conditions for each state transition, we can express the next state logic in a more concise manner.

The refactored design will aim to balance simplicity, performance, and area efficiency while maintaining the required functionality.
