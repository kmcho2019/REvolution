To implement the TopModule, we will design a novel state machine architecture that utilizes a separate direction controller and a state controller, both implemented as independent finite state machines. The direction controller will manage the walking direction (left or right) based on the bump_left and bump_right signals. The state controller will handle the overall state of the Lemming (walking or falling) based on the ground signal. We will use a 1-hot encoding scheme for both controllers to simplify the logic and improve performance. The output signals walk_left, walk_right, and aaah will be generated by combining the outputs of the direction and state controllers.
