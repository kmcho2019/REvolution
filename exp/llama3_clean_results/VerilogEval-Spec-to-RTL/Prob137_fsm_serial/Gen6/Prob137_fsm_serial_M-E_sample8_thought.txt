To minimize switching activity, the design will use a gated clock for the shift register. The clock will only be enabled when a new bit is being received, reducing unnecessary switching.

The state machine will be simplified to have only two states: IDLE and RECEIVE. The IDLE state will wait for the start bit, and the RECEIVE state will collect the incoming bits and verify the stop bit.

To further reduce power consumption, the design will use a dynamic clock gating technique. The clock will be gated off when the FSM is in the IDLE state or when the stop bit is being verified.
