To generate a new and different solution, I propose an architectural approach that leverages the concept of bit manipulation and vector shifting. The idea involves creating a single, unified module that combines the functions of concatenation, appending, and splitting, utilizing Verilog's built-in bit-manipulation capabilities to streamline the process. This novel approach, dubbed "BitWeaver," aims to minimize the number of intermediate signals and modules, potentially reducing the overall area and power consumption of the design.

The core of the BitWeaver module will be a series of bit-manipulation operations that concatenate the input vectors, append the two 1 bits, and then split the resulting vector into the output vectors using a combination of shifting and masking operations. This approach allows for a more compact and efficient implementation compared to the traditional module-based design.

Furthermore, to optimize the design for performance, power, and area, the BitWeaver module will be designed with a focus on minimizing the number of logic gates required, reducing the switching activity on internal signals, and leveraging the synthesis tool's ability to optimize the design for the target technology.
