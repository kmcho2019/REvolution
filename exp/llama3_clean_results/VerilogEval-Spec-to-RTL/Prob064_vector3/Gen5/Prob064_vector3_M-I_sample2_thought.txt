To improve the existing solution, we will focus on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The current design has a hierarchical approach with three sub-modules: ConcatModule, AppendModule, and SplitModule. While this approach is beneficial for modularity and readability, it may introduce some overhead due to the module boundaries.

Our improvement strategy will involve the following steps:
1. Flatten the hierarchical structure into a single TopModule to reduce overhead and potentially improve timing.
2. Simplify the logic within the TopModule to minimize switching activity and reduce power consumption.
3. Optimize the physical placement of the logic to improve area efficiency.

By implementing these improvements, we aim to reduce the power consumption, improve the timing, and optimize the area of the design.
