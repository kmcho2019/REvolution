This approach can offer several benefits, including improved timing performance due to reduced combinational logic depth and potentially lower power consumption by reducing the number of elements switching at any given time.

2. **Appending Stage:** Following the concatenation, this stage appends two '1' bits to the end of the concatenated vector.
3. **Splitting Stage:** The final stage splits the resulting vector from the appending stage into the output vectors w, x, y, and z.

Each stage will be designed to minimize area and power consumption while maintaining the required functionality. The pipeline architecture will allow for a more efficient processing of the input vectors, potentially leading to improved performance metrics.
