The refactoring strategy involves simplifying the design by removing unnecessary modules and directly implementing the required functionality within the TopModule. This approach aims to minimize the overhead associated with module boundaries and interfaces, potentially leading to better PPA metrics. The main idea is to concatenate the input vectors, append two '1' bits to the concatenated vector, and then split the resulting vector into the output vectors w, x, y, and z. This refactored design will utilize direct bit assignment and concatenation for efficiency.
