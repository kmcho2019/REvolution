The new architectural idea involves using a single module, TopModule, that performs the concatenation and splitting of vectors in a pipeline fashion. Instead of concatenating the input vectors into pairs and then into a 30-bit vector, we will use a shift register to shift the input vectors into a 32-bit register. This approach allows us to append the two '1' bits at the end of the 30-bit vector as we shift the bits into the register. We will then use a simple assignments to split the 32-bit register into the four output vectors.

This approach reduces the number of modules and the complexity of the design, making it more efficient in terms of area and power consumption. The pipeline fashion also improves the timing performance of the design.
