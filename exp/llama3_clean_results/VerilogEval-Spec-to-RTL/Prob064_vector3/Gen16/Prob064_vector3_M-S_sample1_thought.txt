To simplify the existing solution, we can eliminate the need for separate modules for concatenation, appending, and splitting. Instead, we can directly perform these operations within the TopModule. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and intermediate signals.

The simplified design will involve the following steps:

1. Concatenate the input vectors a, b, c, d, e, and f to form a 30-bit vector.
2. Append two 1 bits to the concatenated vector to form a 32-bit vector.
3. Split the 32-bit vector into four 8-bit output vectors w, x, y, and z.

By combining these operations within the TopModule, we can reduce the overall complexity of the design while maintaining its functionality.
