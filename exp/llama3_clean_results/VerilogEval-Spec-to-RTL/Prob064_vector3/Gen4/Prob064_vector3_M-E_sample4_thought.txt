The new architectural idea for the TopModule is to use a single, compact, and efficient module that directly concatenates the input vectors, appends the two '1' bits, and then splits the resulting vector into the output vectors. This approach eliminates the need for multiple sub-modules and minimizes the overhead associated with module boundaries. The new design uses bitwise operations to concatenate and split the vectors, which can lead to improved timing and area efficiency. Additionally, the design takes advantage of Verilog's built-in bit-manipulation capabilities to simplify the implementation and reduce the amount of code required.
