To improve the existing solution, we focus on optimizing for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics. Since the current design does not exhibit complex combinational logic, we look into reducing the power consumption and area usage while maintaining or improving the performance. The strategy involves minimizing the switching activity on the wide buses and reducing the overall width of the concatenated vector if possible. Additionally, we consider resource sharing techniques and more compact representations of the input data. However, given the direct nature of the task (concatenation and splitting), the primary optimization will focus on the efficient handling of the concatenated vector and the splitting process.
