To improve the existing solution, we will focus on optimizing the design for better PPA (Power, Performance, Area) metrics. Based on the provided feedback and PPA metrics, the main areas for improvement are reducing latency (performance), minimizing power consumption, and optimizing area utilization.

The current design involves a hierarchical approach with separate modules for concatenation, appending, and splitting. While this approach is modular and easy to understand, it might introduce additional latency and overhead due to the inter-module connections.

Our improvement strategy will involve simplifying the design by directly implementing the required functionality within the TopModule, thus reducing the number of levels of logic and potentially improving timing. We will also aim to minimize internal signals and optimize net fan-out to reduce power consumption and area utilization.

Specifically, we will eliminate the need for separate ConcatModule, AppendModule, and SplitModule by performing the concatenation, appending, and splitting operations directly within the TopModule. This approach should reduce latency, power consumption, and area utilization, resulting in improved PPA metrics.
