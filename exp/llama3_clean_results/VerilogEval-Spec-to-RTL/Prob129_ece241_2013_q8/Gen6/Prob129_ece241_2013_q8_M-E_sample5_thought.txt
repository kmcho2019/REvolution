To create a novel architectural idea for the Mealy-type finite state machine, we can utilize a counter-based approach. We can use two counters: one to track the consecutive occurrences of "1" after a "0" (to detect the "01" sequence) and another to track the consecutive occurrences of "1" after a "10" sequence (to detect the "101" sequence). When the second counter reaches a count of 1, we assert the output z to indicate the detection of the "101" sequence. This approach simplifies the state machine logic and reduces the number of required states.
