A novel approach to recognizing the sequence "101" on an input signal named x involves utilizing a counter-based state machine. This design consists of two counters: one for tracking the number of consecutive "1"s seen (Counter1) and another for tracking the number of consecutive "0"s seen after a "1" has been encountered (Counter0). When a "1" is detected after a "0" has been seen (indicating the sequence "10" has occurred), and then immediately followed by another "1", the output z is asserted. This approach leverages counters to track the sequence, providing an alternative perspective on recognizing the "101" pattern without explicitly using shift registers or complex state machines.
