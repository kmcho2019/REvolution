To create a novel architectural idea for reversing the bit ordering of an input vector, we can utilize a barrel shifter approach. A barrel shifter is a digital circuit that can shift the bits of a binary number to the left or right by a specified number of positions. By cleverly using a barrel shifter, we can effectively reverse the bits of the input vector. This can be achieved by shifting the bits to the right by 7 positions for an 8-bit vector, effectively rotating the bits and reversing their order. This approach is different from the original generate block method and offers a unique perspective on solving the problem.
