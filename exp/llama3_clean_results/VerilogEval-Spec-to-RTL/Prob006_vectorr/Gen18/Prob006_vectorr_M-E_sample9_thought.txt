A novel approach to reverse the bit ordering of the input port is to use a shift register. By shifting the input bits through the register from one end to the other, we can effectively reverse their order. This approach is distinct from the concatenation method and can be implemented using a for loop to create the shift register. The idea is to iterate through each bit of the input, shifting it to its corresponding position in the output. This method maintains the same functionality as the original but offers a different architectural perspective on achieving bit reversal.
