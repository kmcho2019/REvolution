A novel approach to reversing the bit ordering of the input port involves utilizing a barrel shifter architecture. This concept typically involves using a series of multiplexers to shift the bits of the input vector to the desired positions. However, for a simple bit reversal operation, we can design a custom barrel shifter that directly maps the input bits to their reversed positions without the need for actual shifting operations. This approach simplifies the design and reduces the area required. By decomposing the 8-bit input into two 4-bit halves and then swapping them, followed by swapping individual bits within each half, we can achieve the bit reversal efficiently.
