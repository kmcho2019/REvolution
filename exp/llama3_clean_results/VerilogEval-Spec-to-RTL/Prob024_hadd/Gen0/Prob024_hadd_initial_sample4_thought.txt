To implement a half adder in Verilog, we need to create a module named TopModule that takes two one-bit inputs (a and b), and produces two one-bit outputs (sum and cout). The sum output will be the XOR of the two inputs (since in binary addition, the sum of two bits is their XOR), and the cout output will be the AND of the two inputs (since a carry is produced only when both input bits are 1).
