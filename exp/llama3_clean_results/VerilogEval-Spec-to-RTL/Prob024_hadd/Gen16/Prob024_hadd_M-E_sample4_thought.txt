A novel architectural approach for the half adder module involves utilizing a lookup table (LUT) to directly map the input combinations to their respective sum and carry-out values. This approach eliminates the need for explicit logic gates like XOR and AND, potentially leading to a more area-efficient and power-friendly design. The LUT can be implemented using a case statement within the TopModule, directly assigning the outputs based on the input combinations. This method leverages the ability of modern synthesis tools to optimize the LUT for the target technology, potentially resulting in improved PPA metrics.
