To simplify the solution, we will directly implement the XOR and AND operations within the TopModule without instantiating separate modules for these gates. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and the associated wiring. The simplified design maintains the functionality of a half adder, which is to produce a sum and a carry-out from two input bits using XOR and AND operations, respectively. This simplification is expected to have a negligible impact on the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, given the basic nature of the half adder circuit. The focus remains on ensuring the design is straightforward, efficient, and easy to understand.
