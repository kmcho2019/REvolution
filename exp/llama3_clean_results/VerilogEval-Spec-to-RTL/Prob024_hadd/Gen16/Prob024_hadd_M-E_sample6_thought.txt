To create a novel architectural solution for the half adder module, we will leverage a lookup table (LUT) based approach. The idea is to implement the half adder using a single 4-LUT (4-input Look-Up Table), which can be programmed to produce the sum and carry-out outputs directly based on the input combinations of 'a' and 'b'. This approach can potentially offer better performance and area efficiency compared to traditional gate-level implementations, especially in modern FPGAs where LUTs are abundant and optimized for performance. The LUT will be programmed with the truth table of the half adder, where the inputs 'a' and 'b' are used to index into the LUT to retrieve the corresponding sum and carry-out values. This method eliminates the need for separate XOR and AND gates, potentially reducing area and improving timing due to the optimized nature of LUTs in FPGAs.
