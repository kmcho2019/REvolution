A novel approach to implementing a half adder could involve utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) to select between different combinations of inputs to produce the sum and carry-out. This method diverges from the traditional XOR and AND gate implementation, offering an alternative perspective on digital circuit design. By employing a MUX, we can encode the truth table of a half adder directly into the selection and input lines of the multiplexer. This design strategy can lead to an interesting comparison with traditional methods, potentially offering insights into the flexibility of digital logic implementation.
