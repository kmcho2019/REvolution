The novel architectural idea for the half adder module involves leveraging a multiplexer-based approach. This approach exploits the properties of a 2-to-1 multiplexer to perform the addition. By cleverly selecting the inputs to the multiplexer based on the carry-out condition, we can achieve the sum and carry-out operations in a unified manner. The key insight is to use the carry-out signal (which is essentially the AND of the two inputs) to control the multiplexer. This allows us to compute both the sum and carry-out using the same basic operation, thereby simplifying the design. Additionally, this approach could offer advantages in terms of area efficiency and power consumption, as it minimizes the number of gates required.
