The existing implementation of the half adder using separate XOR and AND gates can be refactored by directly using the logical operators in a single module. This approach eliminates the need for instantiating separate modules for XOR and AND operations, simplifying the design and potentially improving performance and area metrics by reducing the number of modules and levels of logic. The `assign` statement can be used directly within the TopModule to compute the sum and carry-out, providing a straightforward and efficient implementation.
