The existing solution can be simplified by directly implementing the half adder logic within the TopModule, eliminating the need for separate XOR and AND gate modules. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by minimizing the number of modules and instantiations, which can lead to improvements in Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The sum and carry-out of a half adder can be calculated using the XOR and AND operations, respectively, which can be directly implemented in a single module.
