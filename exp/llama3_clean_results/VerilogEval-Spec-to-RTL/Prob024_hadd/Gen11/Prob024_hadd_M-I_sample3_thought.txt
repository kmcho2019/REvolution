To improve the existing solution, the main goal is to optimize the design for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics without compromising its functionality. The current design uses separate modules for XOR and AND gates, which contributes to its modularity but might introduce some overhead in terms of area and power due to the instantiation and wiring of these modules. A potential optimization strategy is to integrate the XOR and AND operations into a single module, reducing the number of internal signals and gate inputs. This approach could decrease the area and potentially lower the power consumption by minimizing the switching activity. Additionally, simplifying the design by directly implementing the half adder logic within a single module could further reduce the overhead associated with module instantiation and improve performance by minimizing the critical path.
