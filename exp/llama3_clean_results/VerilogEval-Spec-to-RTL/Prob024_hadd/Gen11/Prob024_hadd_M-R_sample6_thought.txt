To refactor the existing half adder implementation, we can utilize a behavioral approach instead of the current structural one. This involves directly describing the desired behavior using continuous assignments (`assign` statements) within the `TopModule`. The idea is to directly express the sum and carry-out in terms of the inputs `a` and `b`, using the XOR and AND operators, thereby reducing the need for separate gate modules. This approach simplifies the design by eliminating the need for explicit gate instantiations and can improve readability by directly implementing the logic within the module.
