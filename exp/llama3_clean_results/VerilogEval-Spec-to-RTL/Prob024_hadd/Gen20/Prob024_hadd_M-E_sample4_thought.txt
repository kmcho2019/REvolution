To devise a novel architectural solution for the half adder module, let's explore the concept of utilizing a lookup table (LUT) to perform the addition. The LUT can be implemented using a case statement in Verilog, which allows for a straightforward and efficient definition of the truth table for the half adder. This approach enables the combination of the XOR and AND operations into a single, compact module, potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics by reducing the number of internal signals and gate inputs.
