The novel architectural idea for the half adder module involves utilizing a single, more complex combinatorial logic circuit that directly generates both the sum and carry-out signals from the input bits a and b. This approach aims to reduce the area and potentially improve performance by minimizing the number of logic levels and gates required. The idea is to use a single expression that computes both the sum and carry-out, leveraging the properties of bitwise operations and boolean algebra to achieve this in a single step. This method could also simplify the design process and potentially reduce power consumption by minimizing the number of transistors required for the implementation.
