The existing design for the half adder module is functionally correct but can be optimized for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given the current design's simplicity and the marginal room for improvement, the focus will be on fine-tuning the logic and potentially reducing the complexity of the module instantiations. One strategy to optimize this design is to directly integrate the XOR and AND operations within the TopModule, eliminating the need for separate module instantiations for XOR and AND gates. This approach aims to reduce the area consumption and potentially lower the power consumption by minimizing unnecessary switching and module overhead. Since the design is already minimal, the impact on performance is expected to be negligible, but any reduction in area and power consumption without compromising functionality would be beneficial.
