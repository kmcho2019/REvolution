To implement a novel half adder module, we can leverage the concept of lookup tables (LUTs) to map the input combinations to their corresponding sum and carry-out values. This approach allows for a straightforward and efficient implementation, especially in FPGAs where LUTs are abundant. By defining a 2-input LUT for both the sum and the carry-out, we can directly map the four possible input combinations (00, 01, 10, 11) to their respective outputs. This method provides a unique and simple way to implement the half adder functionality without needing to explicitly define XOR and AND gates.
