To create a novel architectural solution for the half adder module, we will explore the concept of using a Karnaugh map (K-map) to simplify the logic expressions for the sum and carry-out. A K-map is a method of simplifying Boolean algebraic expressions and is useful for minimizing the number of logic gates required. By analyzing the truth table of the half adder, we can derive the simplified expressions for the sum and carry-out using a K-map. This approach allows us to potentially reduce the number of gates and improve the overall efficiency of the design. Furthermore, we can leverage the properties of Boolean algebra to minimize the expressions, leading to a more area-efficient and power-friendly implementation. The new solution will be designed with a focus on minimizing the critical path, reducing switching activity, and utilizing area-efficient logic structures to optimize the PPA metrics.
