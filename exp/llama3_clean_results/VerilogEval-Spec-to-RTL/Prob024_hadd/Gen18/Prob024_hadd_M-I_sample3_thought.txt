2. **Performance Optimization**: Since the design is purely combinational, ensuring that the propagation delay through the XOR and AND gates is minimized is crucial. However, given the direct implementation, the critical path is already optimized.

3. **Power Consumption Reduction**: To reduce power consumption, minimizing switching activity and ensuring proper input buffering are key. However, for a basic combinational circuit like a half-adder, there's limited scope without altering the functionality.
