To refactor the existing half adder implementation, we can utilize a behavioral approach instead of the current structural approach. This involves using a single module with combinational logic (assign statements) to directly calculate the sum and carry-out. The idea is to simplify the design by removing the need for separate XOR and AND gate modules, thereby reducing the overall complexity and potentially improving readability. This approach can also help in reducing the number of lines of code and making the design more compact.
