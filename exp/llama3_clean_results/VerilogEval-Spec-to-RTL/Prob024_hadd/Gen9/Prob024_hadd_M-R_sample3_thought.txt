The existing half adder implementation uses separate modules for XOR and AND gates, which is a straightforward and modular approach. However, this can be further refactored for improved performance and reduced area by directly using the built-in bitwise operators in Verilog. By eliminating the need for separate gate modules, we can simplify the design and potentially reduce the area and power consumption. The new approach involves directly assigning the result of the XOR and AND operations to the output ports using the bitwise XOR (`^`) and AND (`&`) operators, respectively. This simplification maintains the original functionality while optimizing the design for potential PPA improvements.
