To create a novel solution, I will introduce a new architectural idea that utilizes a toggle flip-flop to simplify the state machine. Instead of using a traditional state machine with multiple states, I will use a single toggle flip-flop to switch between the two states (walking left and walking right). The toggle flip-flop will be triggered by the bump_left and bump_right signals, allowing the Lemming to change direction when it hits an obstacle. This approach eliminates the need for a complex state machine and reduces the area usage.

My novel idea involves the following steps:

1. Use a single toggle flip-flop to represent the current state of the Lemming (walking left or walking right).
2. Use the bump_left and bump_right signals to trigger the toggle flip-flop, allowing the Lemming to change direction when it hits an obstacle.
3. Use the output of the toggle flip-flop to generate the walk_left and walk_right signals.
