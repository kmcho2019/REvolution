To simplify the solution, we can directly use a single flip-flop to represent the state of the Lemming (walking left or right). This approach eliminates the need for an enumeration and reduces the overall complexity of the code. The next state logic can be simplified by considering the current state and the bump signals. If the Lemming is walking left and bumped on the left, or walking right and bumped on the right, it should switch direction. The output logic can be directly derived from the state flip-flop.
