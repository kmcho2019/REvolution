To implement a novel architectural idea for the Lemmings game, I will use a different approach by utilizing a binary counter to keep track of the Lemming's direction. The counter will have two bits, where the first bit represents the direction (0 for left, 1 for right) and the second bit acts as a toggle flag. When a bump is detected on either side, the counter will increment, causing the direction to change. This approach allows for a more concise implementation and eliminates the need for explicit state machines or enumerations.
