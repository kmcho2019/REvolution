To implement the Lemmings game using a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a counter-based approach. Instead of using a traditional state machine, we can employ a 1-bit counter that increments or decrements based on the bump inputs. The counter's value determines the direction of the Lemmings. When the counter is 0, the Lemmings walk left, and when it's 1, they walk right. The bump inputs will toggle the counter, effectively switching the direction. This approach simplifies the logic and eliminates the need for a traditional state machine.
