To create a completely new and different solution, we can introduce a novel architectural idea that utilizes a toggle-based state machine. Instead of explicitly defining states for walking left or right, we can use a single bit to represent the direction. When a bump signal is received, we simply toggle this direction bit. This approach simplifies the sequential logic and reduces the number of conditional statements, potentially improving the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. We will also utilize a separate output logic to generate the walk_left and walk_right signals based on the direction bit. This separation of concerns allows for a more modular design and easier optimization of individual components.
