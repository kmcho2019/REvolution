To implement the TopModule, we will first define a Moore state machine with two states: walking left and walking right. We will use two inputs, bump_left and bump_right, to determine when to switch directions. When the Lemming is walking left and bumps into something on the left, it will switch to walking right, and vice versa. If the Lemming is bumped on both sides at the same time, it will still switch directions. The state machine will be reset to walking left when the areset signal is high.

We will use two always blocks: one for combinational logic to determine the next state, and another for sequential logic to update the current state. We will also use a case statement to define the next state based on the current state and inputs.
