A novel approach to implementing a 2-input NOR gate can be achieved by utilizing a different combinational logic structure, such as a multiplexer-based design. This can involve using a 2:1 multiplexer to select between two possible output values based on the inputs. By setting the select lines and input values appropriately, the multiplexer can be configured to produce the NOR gate functionality. This approach may provide an alternative perspective on the implementation, potentially offering insights into the versatility of digital design.
