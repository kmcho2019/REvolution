A novel approach to implement a 2-input NOR gate is to use a combination of NOT gates and an AND gate. This approach involves inverting the inputs to the NOR gate using NOT gates, and then using an AND gate to combine the inverted inputs. The output of the AND gate will be the inverse of the NOR operation, which can then be inverted again to obtain the final NOR result. This approach allows for a different perspective on the NOR operation and can be useful in certain digital design contexts.
