A novel approach to implementing the 2-input NOR gate involves utilizing the properties of bitwise operations in Verilog. Specifically, this method leverages the fact that the NOR operation can be represented as the inversion of the OR operation. By using the bitwise OR operator and then inverting the result, we can achieve the same functionality as the original NOR gate but with a potentially more concise and efficient implementation. This approach avoids the need for explicit case statements or conditional logic, instead relying on the inherent properties of digital logic to produce the desired output.
