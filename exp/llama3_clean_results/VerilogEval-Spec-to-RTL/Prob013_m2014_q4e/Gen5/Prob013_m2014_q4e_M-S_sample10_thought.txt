The existing implementation of the 2-input NOR gate using the logical NOT and OR operators is already quite straightforward and efficient. To simplify it further while maintaining functionality, we can consider using the Verilog operator that directly represents the NOR operation, which is the "~" and "&" operators combined, but since Verilog doesn't have a direct NOR operator, we'll stick with the current approach but acknowledge it as the most simplified form given the language's constructs.
