A novel approach to implementing a 2-input NOR gate involves utilizing the bitwise NOT operator (~) in combination with the bitwise OR operator (|) to achieve the desired logic. This method leverages Verilog's built-in operators to create a concise and efficient implementation. The idea is to first perform a bitwise OR operation on the inputs and then apply a bitwise NOT operation to the result, effectively inverting the output of the OR gate, which mimics the behavior of a NOR gate. This approach is not only elegant but also easy to understand and maintain, making it suitable for both simple and complex digital designs.
