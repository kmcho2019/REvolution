To create a novel architectural idea for implementing a 2-input NOR gate, we can consider using a lookup table (LUT) approach. This method involves using a case statement to explicitly define the output for every possible combination of inputs. Although this approach might seem less efficient than the straightforward logical expression, it demonstrates an alternative way of thinking about implementing simple digital logic functions.
