To simplify the existing implementation of the 2-input NOR gate, we can utilize the logical NOT and OR operators directly as done in the previous solution. The NOR operation can be expressed as the negation of the OR operation between the two inputs. This straightforward approach maintains the combinational nature of the design and is already quite minimal in terms of area due to its simplicity. Thus, the focus should be on ensuring that the design remains efficient and easy to understand while maintaining the desired functionality.
