A novel approach to implementing a 2-input NOR gate is to utilize a multiplexer (MUX) based design. This involves selecting between two possible output values based on the inputs. By using a MUX, the NOR operation can be performed by selecting the output based on the state of the inputs. This method, although not the most straightforward, offers an alternative perspective on implementing logical operations using multiplexers, which are fundamental components in digital design. The idea is to treat the inputs as the selection lines of the MUX and the outputs as the data inputs, thereby leveraging the MUX's capability to select one of its data inputs based on the selection lines.
