A novel approach to implementing a 2-input NOR gate is to use a case statement to explicitly define the output for each possible combination of input values. This approach can provide a clear and straightforward way to implement the NOR gate, especially for designers who prefer a more explicit and visual representation of the logic. The case statement will check the values of in1 and in2 and assign the corresponding output value to out. This method can be useful for educational purposes or for designers who prefer a more verbose implementation.
