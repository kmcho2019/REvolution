The inputs to the LUT will be the two input signals (in1 and in2), and the output of the LUT will be the result of the NOR operation.

This approach can be beneficial in certain situations, such as in FPGA designs where LUTs are readily available and can be efficiently utilized. By using a LUT, we can avoid the need for explicit logic gates and instead rely on the LUT to produce the correct output based on the input combinations.
