To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a lookup table (LUT) approach. The LUT will be a 2x1 table that maps all possible input combinations of in1 and in2 to their corresponding output values. This approach can provide flexibility and ease of modification if the gate's functionality needs to be altered in the future. We will use a case statement within a always block to implement the LUT.
