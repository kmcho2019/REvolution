A novel approach to implementing a 2-input NOR gate involves using a combination of NOT gates and an AND gate. This method is based on De Morgan's laws and the properties of Boolean algebra. By first inverting both inputs and then applying an AND operation, we can achieve the equivalent of a NOR gate. This approach can provide an alternative perspective on digital logic implementation and might be useful in specific design scenarios where certain gate types are favored or optimized in the target technology.
