To implement a 2-input NOR gate in Verilog, we can use the logical NOR operator (~) and the bitwise AND operator (&) along with the bitwise NOT operator (!) or directly use the logical NOR operator. The NOR gate produces an output of 1 only if both inputs are 0. This can be achieved by using the expression ~(in1 | in2), where ~ is the bitwise NOT operator and | is the bitwise OR operator.
