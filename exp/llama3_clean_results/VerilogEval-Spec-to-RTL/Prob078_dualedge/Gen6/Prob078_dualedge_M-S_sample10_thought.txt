The idea is to simplify the existing solution by reducing unnecessary logic and leveraging the properties of FPGA primitives to improve performance. Since the goal is to functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can exploit the characteristics of positive and negative edge triggered flip-flops to directly capture the input data on both edges of the clock without needing a multiplexer to select between them. This approach minimizes the combinational logic required, thus simplifying the circuit.

The simplified circuit can be achieved by using two separate flip-flops triggered on the positive and negative edges of the clock, respectively, and directly assigning their outputs to a single variable. This approach eliminates the need for a multiplexer and reduces the overall complexity of the design.

By simplifying the logic, we can reduce the area usage and potentially minimize power consumption by reducing unnecessary switching activity. Furthermore, this design should be more efficient in terms of performance, as it directly captures the input data without any additional combinational logic delays.
