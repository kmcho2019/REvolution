To simplify the existing design for a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can directly use a single register and update its value based on both edges of the clock. This can be achieved by using a single always block that is sensitive to both the positive and negative edges of the clock, and updating the register accordingly. This approach eliminates the need for two separate flip-flops and the associated logic to combine their outputs, thus reducing complexity and potentially improving Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
