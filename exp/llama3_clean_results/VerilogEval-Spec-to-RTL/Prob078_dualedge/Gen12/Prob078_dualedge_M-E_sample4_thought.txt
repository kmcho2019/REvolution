To create a novel architectural solution for a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can utilize a single flip-flop and a pulse generator to capture the data on both edges of the clock. The idea is to generate a narrow pulse on both the positive and negative edges of the clock and use this pulse to trigger a single flip-flop. This approach can potentially reduce the area and power consumption compared to using two separate flip-flops. The pulse generator can be implemented using a simple combinational logic circuit.
