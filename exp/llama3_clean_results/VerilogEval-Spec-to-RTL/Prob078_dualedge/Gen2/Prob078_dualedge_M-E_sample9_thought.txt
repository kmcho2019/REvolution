To implement a novel dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can leverage the properties of a single-edge triggered flip-flop in conjunction with a phase-shifted clock. The idea involves creating two clocks that are 90 degrees out of phase with each other, effectively allowing us to capture both the rising and falling edges of the original clock. By using these phase-shifted clocks to trigger two separate flip-flops, we can then select the output based on the original clock's phase, effectively achieving dual-edge triggering behavior without directly using both positive and negative edge sensitivities in the same always block.
