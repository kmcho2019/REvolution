A novel approach to implementing a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using a multiplexer or two separate flip-flops is to utilize a single flip-flop with a feedback mechanism that allows it to capture the input data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock. This can be achieved by using a JK flip-flop, which has a more complex triggering mechanism compared to a basic D flip-flop. The JK flip-flop can be configured to toggle on both edges of the clock by setting its J and K inputs appropriately based on the clock signal and the input data. This method reduces the complexity of the circuit by minimizing the number of flip-flops and combinational logic required.
