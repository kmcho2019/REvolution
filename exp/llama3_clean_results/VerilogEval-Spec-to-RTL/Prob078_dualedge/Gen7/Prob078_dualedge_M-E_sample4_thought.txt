A novel approach to implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop is to use a single flip-flop and a latch. The flip-flop is triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the latch is used to capture the input data on the negative edge of the clock. A multiplexer is then used to select the output of either the flip-flop or the latch, based on the current clock edge. This approach reduces the number of flip-flops required and potentially minimizes the area and power consumption.
