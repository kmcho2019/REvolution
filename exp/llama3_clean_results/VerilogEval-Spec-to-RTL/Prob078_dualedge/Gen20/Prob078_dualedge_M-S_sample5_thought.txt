The existing implementation effectively achieves the functionality of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop by utilizing two separate flip-flops, one for the positive edge and one for the negative edge of the clock, and then selecting between these two based on the current clock level. To simplify this solution, we can maintain the core concept but focus on minimizing the logic required for the output selection. Since the flip-flops already capture the input `d` on their respective edges, we can directly use their outputs in a simple logic to determine the final output `q`, without needing to explicitly consider the clock edges for the selection. This can potentially reduce the complexity of the combinational logic involved.
