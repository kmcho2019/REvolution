To functionally behave like a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and some additional logic. The idea is to sample the input data on both the rising and falling edges of the clock and then use the sampled values to update the output of the flip-flop. This can be achieved by using two D-type flip-flops: one triggered on the rising edge of the clock and the other triggered on the falling edge. The output of these flip-flops is then combined using an OR gate and a multiplexer to produce the final output.
