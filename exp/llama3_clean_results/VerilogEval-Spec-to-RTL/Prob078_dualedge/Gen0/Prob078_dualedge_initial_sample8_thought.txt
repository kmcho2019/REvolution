To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop using standard single-edge triggered flip-flops available in FPGAs, we can use a combination of two flip-flops. One will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock, and the other on the negative edge. We will then use the output of both flip-flops and the clock signal to determine the final output. Essentially, we can use a multiplexer to select the output of the positive-edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is high and the output of the negative-edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is low. This way, the circuit behaves as if it were triggered on both edges of the clock.
