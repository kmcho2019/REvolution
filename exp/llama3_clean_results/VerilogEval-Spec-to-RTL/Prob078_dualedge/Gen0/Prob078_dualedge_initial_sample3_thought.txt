To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two D-type flip-flops. One will be triggered on the positive edge of the clock and the other on the negative edge. The input data 'd' will be sampled by both flip-flops, and the output 'q' will be a combination of the outputs from both flip-flops. This ensures that the output 'q' is updated on both edges of the clock. We can use a multiplexer to combine the outputs from both flip-flops.
