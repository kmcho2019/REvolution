To simplify the implementation of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can still utilize the concept of two single-edge triggered flip-flops but directly select the output based on the clock edge without the need for additional combinational logic blocks. This simplification can be achieved by directly assigning the output of the positive edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is high and the output of the negative edge triggered flip-flop when the clock is low, within the flip-flop description itself. However, this isn't directly feasible due to the constraints of typical HDL synthesis tools and FPGA architectures. An alternative simplification is to recognize that the output 'q' can be directly updated based on the input 'd' at both positive and negative edges of the clock without needing a separate always block for the output selection, thus reducing the number of logic elements required.
