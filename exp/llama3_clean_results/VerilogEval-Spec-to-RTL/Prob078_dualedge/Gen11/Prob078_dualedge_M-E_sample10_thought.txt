A novel approach to implementing a dual-edge triggered flip-flop could involve using a single flip-flop and a clock divider to generate two phases of the clock signal. By doing so, we can create a pseudo-dual edge triggered behavior using a single flip-flop. The idea revolves around dividing the clock into two phases (e.g., 0 and 180 degrees) and using these phases to sample the input data. This method requires careful consideration of the timing and synchronization of the clock phases to ensure correct operation. The goal is to create a design that is both functionally correct and efficient in terms of area and power consumption.
