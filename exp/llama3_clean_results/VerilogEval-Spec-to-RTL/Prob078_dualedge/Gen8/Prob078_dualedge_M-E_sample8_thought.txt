A novel approach to implementing a dual-edge triggered flip-flop could involve using a single flip-flop and a phase-locked loop (PLL) or a clock divider to generate two non-overlapping clock signals. However, a more straightforward and efficient method would be to use a single flip-flop and a toggle flip-flop to detect the clock edges and trigger the flip-flop accordingly. This approach eliminates the need for a PLL or clock divider and reduces the area and power consumption. We can use the toggle flip-flop to generate a pulse on every clock edge, which can then be used to trigger the main flip-flop. This method ensures that the flip-flop is triggered on both rising and falling edges of the clock signal.
