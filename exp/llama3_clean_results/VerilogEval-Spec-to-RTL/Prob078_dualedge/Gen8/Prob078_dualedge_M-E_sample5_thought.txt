A novel approach to designing a dual-edge triggered flip-flop without using the `always @(posedge clk or negedge clk)` block is to utilize a single flip-flop and a clock edge detector to generate a "clock enable" signal. This clock enable signal is then used to control the loading of data into the flip-flop. The clock edge detector generates a pulse on every edge of the clock, and this pulse is used as the clock enable. This way, the flip-flop is effectively triggered on both the rising and falling edges of the clock, mimicking the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop.
