To simplify the implementation of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use a single D flip-flop with its input driven by the input 'd' and its clock driven by the XOR of the original clock and its inverted version. This ensures that on both edges of the clock, the flip-flop will capture the input 'd', effectively mimicking the behavior of a dual-edge triggered flip-flop. This approach eliminates the need for two separate flip-flops and an OR gate, reducing the overall complexity of the design.
