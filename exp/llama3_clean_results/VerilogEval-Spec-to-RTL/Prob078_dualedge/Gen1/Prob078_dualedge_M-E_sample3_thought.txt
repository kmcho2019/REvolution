To implement a dual-edge triggered flip-flop, we can use two single-edge triggered flip-flops and a simple logic circuit to detect the edge transitions of the clock signal. The key idea here is to use the current and previous values of the clock signal to generate edge detection signals for both the rising and falling edges. We can then use these edge detection signals to control the updating of the output of the dual-edge triggered flip-flop. The first flip-flop is triggered on the rising edge of the clock, and the second is triggered on the falling edge. We use a simple logic to select the output of the flip-flop that corresponds to the most recent edge.
