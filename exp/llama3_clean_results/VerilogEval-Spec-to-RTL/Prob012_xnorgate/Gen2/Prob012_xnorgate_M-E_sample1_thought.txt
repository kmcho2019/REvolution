To implement an XNOR gate with a novel architectural idea, we can use a combination of multiplexers and basic logic gates. The idea is to use two instances of an AND gate and an OR gate, along with a multiplexer to select between the outputs of these gates based on the inputs. This approach provides an alternative perspective on achieving the XNOR functionality, focusing on selection and combination rather than direct logical operations. However, realizing that this might not be the most efficient in terms of area and power due to the additional logic required, it's an interesting exercise in exploring different design possibilities.
