To simplify the XNOR gate implementation, we can leverage the fact that the XNOR operation is equivalent to the negation of the XOR operation. In Verilog, this can be expressed using the `^` operator for XOR and the `~` operator for inversion. Thus, the XNOR operation between two inputs `a` and `b` can be directly implemented as `~(a ^ b)`, which is simpler and more straightforward than the original logical expression.
