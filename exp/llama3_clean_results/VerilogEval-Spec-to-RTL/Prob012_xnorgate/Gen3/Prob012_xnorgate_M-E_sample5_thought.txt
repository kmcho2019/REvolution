A novel architectural idea for implementing an XNOR gate is to use a combination of AND and OR gates, along with inverters, to create a logic circuit that produces the XNOR output. This approach involves creating two paths: one where both inputs are the same (either both 0 or both 1), and another where the inputs are different. By using AND gates for the same-input paths and an OR gate to combine these paths, we can create a circuit that effectively implements the XNOR operation without directly using the XOR operator. This method is more complex than the direct XOR and inversion method but offers an alternative perspective on implementing the XNOR logic, which can be useful in specific design contexts or for educational purposes.
