The novel architectural idea is to implement the XNOR gate using a combination of AND and OR gates, along with inverters. The XNOR operation can be represented as ((a AND b) OR (NOT a AND NOT b)), where 'AND' represents the logical AND operation and 'NOT' represents the logical NOT operation. This approach allows for a different perspective on the XNOR gate implementation, utilizing basic logical operations to achieve the desired output. The idea here is to break down the XNOR functionality into smaller, manageable parts that can be easily implemented using standard logical gates.
