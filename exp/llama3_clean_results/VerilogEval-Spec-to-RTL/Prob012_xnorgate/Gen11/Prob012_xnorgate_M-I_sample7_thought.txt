To improve the XNOR gate implementation for better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics, we can explore an alternative approach that directly implements the XNOR logic without using the XOR operator and inversion. This can potentially reduce the number of operations and thus improve the area and power consumption. Since the current implementation already has a very short effective clock period (close to 0.0), our main focus should be on optimizing the area and power metrics. By utilizing basic logical operations (AND, OR, NOT) directly, we might achieve a more efficient implementation in terms of area and power, depending on the target technology's cell library and optimization capabilities.
