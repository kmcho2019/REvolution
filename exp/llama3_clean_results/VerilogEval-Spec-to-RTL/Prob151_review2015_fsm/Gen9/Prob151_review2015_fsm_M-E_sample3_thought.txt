3. **SHIFT**: Once the pattern is detected, the FSM shifts in the next 4 bits of data.
4. **COUNT**: After shifting, the FSM asserts the 'counting' signal and waits for the 'done_counting' signal.
5. **DONE**: When 'done_counting' is asserted, the FSM asserts the 'done' signal and waits for the 'ack' signal.
