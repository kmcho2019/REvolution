A novel approach to implement the equality comparison between two 2-bit inputs A and B is to use a lookup table (LUT) or a ROM (Read-Only Memory) that stores the expected output for every possible combination of A and B. This approach involves designing a truth table that lists all possible input combinations and their corresponding outputs. The LUT can then be implemented using case statements or a combination of logic gates. However, for simplicity and efficiency, we can also utilize the built-in case statement in Verilog to achieve this. This method, while less intuitive for simple equality checks, demonstrates how lookup tables or similar concepts can be applied to more complex digital design problems.
