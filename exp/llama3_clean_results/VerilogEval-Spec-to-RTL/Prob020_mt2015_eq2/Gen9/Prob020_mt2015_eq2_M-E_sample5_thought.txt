A novel approach to implement the equality comparison between two 2-bit inputs A and B is to use bitwise XOR operations followed by a reduction OR operation. The XOR operation between corresponding bits of A and B will result in 0 if the bits are equal and 1 if they are not. Then, a reduction OR operation on the XOR results will give 0 only if all bits are equal (i.e., A equals B), and 1 otherwise. To obtain the desired output z (1 if A equals B, and 0 otherwise), we can invert the result of the reduction OR operation.
