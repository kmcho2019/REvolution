My novel approach involves using bitwise XOR operations to compare the two 2-bit inputs A and B. The idea is to perform a bitwise XOR between corresponding bits of A and B, and then check if the result is zero. If the result is zero, it means that A and B are equal. This approach can be implemented using Verilog's bitwise XOR operator (^) and a reduction operator to check if all bits of the result are zero.
