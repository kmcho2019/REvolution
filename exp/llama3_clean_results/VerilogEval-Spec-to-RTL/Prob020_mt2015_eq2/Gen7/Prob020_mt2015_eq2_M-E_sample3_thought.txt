A novel approach to implement the comparison of two 2-bit inputs A and B could involve using a lookup table (LUT) or a small ROM (Read-Only Memory) that stores the results of all possible comparisons between A and B. This method would essentially map every possible input combination of A and B to its corresponding output, which indicates whether A equals B or not. By using a LUT or ROM, the comparison operation can be reduced to a simple memory lookup, which could potentially be more efficient in terms of area and power consumption, especially in FPGAs or other programmable logic devices that have dedicated resources for implementing LUTs or small memories.
