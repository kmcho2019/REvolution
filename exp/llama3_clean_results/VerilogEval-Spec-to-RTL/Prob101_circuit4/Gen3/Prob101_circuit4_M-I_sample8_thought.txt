The existing solution attempts to implement the combinational circuit by breaking down the logic into smaller parts. However, upon closer inspection, the logic expression for `q` can be simplified and optimized. The given simulation waveforms indicate that `q` is high whenever `b` or `c` is high, regardless of `a` and `d`. When both `b` and `c` are low, `q` is low if `a` is high and `d` is low, and high otherwise. This can be simplified by observing that `q` is essentially high whenever `b` or `c` is high, or when `a` is low. Therefore, we can directly assign `q` based on these conditions without the need for an internal wire like `a_low_bc`. This approach reduces the complexity of the logic and should improve the PPA metrics.
