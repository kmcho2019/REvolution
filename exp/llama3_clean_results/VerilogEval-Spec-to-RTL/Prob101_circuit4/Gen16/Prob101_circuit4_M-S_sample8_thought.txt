The current solution involves a modular approach with a separate OR_Gate module. However, this can be simplified by directly implementing the OR operation within the TopModule. This simplification reduces complexity while maintaining the same functionality.

The output `q` is determined by the logical OR of inputs `b` and `c`, regardless of the values of `a` and `d`. Therefore, we can directly assign `q` as the result of the OR operation between `b` and `c` using the `|` operator in Verilog.

By eliminating the need for a separate module, we reduce the overall complexity of the design and improve readability. This simplified approach does not compromise the functionality of the circuit and aligns with the provided simulation waveforms.
