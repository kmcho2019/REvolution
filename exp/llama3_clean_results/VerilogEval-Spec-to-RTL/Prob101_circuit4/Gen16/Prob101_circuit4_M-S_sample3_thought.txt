A closer examination of the given simulation waveforms reveals that the output q is actually the logical OR of inputs b, c, and a when a is high, and the logical OR of b and c when a is low. This means we can simplify the logic to q = a || b || c, since when a is high, the output q will always be high, and when a is low, the output q will depend on the values of b and c. This simplification can be implemented in a single assign statement within the TopModule, eliminating the need for a separate OR_Gate module.
