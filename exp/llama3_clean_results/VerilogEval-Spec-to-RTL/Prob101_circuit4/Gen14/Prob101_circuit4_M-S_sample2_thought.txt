By analyzing the simulation waveforms, we can see that the output q is the logical OR of a, b, and c. When a is high, q is high regardless of b and c, and when a is low, q is the logical OR of b and c. This can be represented by the SOP expression: q = a || b || c.

We can implement this expression in Verilog using a single assign statement with a logical OR operation. This approach eliminates the need for a separate module, reducing complexity and improving readability.
