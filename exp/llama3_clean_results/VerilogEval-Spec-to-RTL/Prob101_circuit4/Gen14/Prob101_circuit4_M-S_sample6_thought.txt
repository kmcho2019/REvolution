To simplify the existing solution while maintaining its functionality, we can eliminate unnecessary modules and directly implement the required logic within the TopModule. Given the observed functionality, where q is the logical OR of b, c, and a (as when a is high, q is high regardless of b and c, and when a is low, q is the logical OR of b and c), we can express this relationship as q = a || b || c. This expression accurately captures the behavior described by the simulation waveforms.

By directly implementing this logic within the TopModule using an assign statement with a logical OR operation, we can eliminate the need for the separate OR_Gate module. This simplification reduces the overall complexity of the design, making it more efficient in terms of area and potentially power consumption, as it minimizes the number of gates required to implement the functionality.
