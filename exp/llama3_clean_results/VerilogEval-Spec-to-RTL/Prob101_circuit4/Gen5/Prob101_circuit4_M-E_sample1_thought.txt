A novel approach to implementing the combinational circuit is to utilize a Karnaugh map (K-map) to derive a simplified Boolean expression. The K-map is a method of simplifying Boolean algebraic expressions and is a powerful tool for digital circuit design. By analyzing the given simulation waveforms, we can construct a truth table and then apply K-map reduction to obtain a minimized expression for the output q. This approach ensures that the resulting circuit is optimized in terms of the number of logic gates required, thereby improving the area and potentially the performance of the circuit.
