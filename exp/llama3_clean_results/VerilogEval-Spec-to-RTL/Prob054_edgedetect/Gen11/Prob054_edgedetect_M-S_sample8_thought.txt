To simplify the existing Verilog solution for the TopModule, we can eliminate unnecessary registers and directly compute the positive edge detection within the `always` block. By removing the `prev_in` register and calculating its value on the fly based on the previous state of `in`, we reduce the number of registers required, thus simplifying the design. We also merge the computation of the positive edge condition directly into the update of the `pedge_reg`, further streamlining the logic. This approach maintains the core functionality of detecting positive edges in the input signal while reducing complexity.
