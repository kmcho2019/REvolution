A novel approach to implement the given circuit is to utilize a multiplexer (MUX) to select between 'in1' and '0' based on the value of 'in2'. When 'in2' is '0', the MUX selects 'in1', and when 'in2' is '1', the MUX selects '0'. This effectively achieves the same result as the AND gate with an inverted 'in2' input, but through a different architectural component. This approach can provide an alternative perspective on optimizing the circuit for PPA, especially in scenarios where MUXes are more efficient than basic logic gates in the target technology.
