To create a novel architectural solution for the given circuit, we can implement a multiplexer-based approach. The idea is to use a 2-to-1 multiplexer where the select line is driven by the in2 input, and the two inputs to the multiplexer are in1 and the inverted in1 (since in2 has a bubble, we effectively want to select between in1 and the opposite of in1 based on in2). This way, when in2 is 0, the multiplexer selects in1, and when in2 is 1, it selects the inverted in1, which is equivalent to the AND operation with in2 inverted.
