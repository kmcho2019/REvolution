To implement the TopModule as described, we first recognize that the bubble on the in2 input to the AND gate signifies an inversion. This means that instead of directly connecting in2 to the AND gate, we should connect the inverse of in2. In Verilog, inversion can be achieved using the logical NOT operator (!). Therefore, our TopModule will contain an AND gate where one input is in1 and the other input is the inverse of in2, with the result of this operation assigned to out.
