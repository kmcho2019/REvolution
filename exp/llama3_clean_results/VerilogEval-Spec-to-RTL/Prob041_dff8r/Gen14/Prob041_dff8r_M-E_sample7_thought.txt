The idea is to use a single shift register that shifts the input data into the register on each clock cycle and also incorporates a reset mechanism to clear the register when needed.

This approach is novel because it leverages the properties of shift registers to implement the D flip-flop functionality in a more compact and efficient manner. The shift register will be designed to shift the input data into the register on each clock cycle, effectively implementing the D flip-flop behavior. The reset mechanism will be integrated into the shift register to clear the register when the reset signal is asserted.

This design has the potential to reduce area and power consumption compared to traditional D flip-flop implementations, as it uses a single shift register to implement the functionality of 8 D flip-flops. Additionally, this design may also improve performance by reducing the number of clock domains and improving clock skew.
