To implement the TopModule, we need to create a module that includes 8 D flip-flops (DFFs) with active high synchronous reset. Each DFF will be triggered by the positive edge of the clk signal and will have its output set to zero when the reset signal is high. The inputs to the DFFs will come from the 8-bit input port d, and the outputs of the DFFs will be connected to the 8-bit output port q.
