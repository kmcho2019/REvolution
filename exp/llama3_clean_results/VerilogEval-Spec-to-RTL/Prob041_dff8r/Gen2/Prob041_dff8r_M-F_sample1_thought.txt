The issue with the current implementation is that it includes `posedge reset` in the sensitivity list of the always block, making the reset asynchronous. To fix this, we should remove `posedge reset` from the sensitivity list and only consider the reset condition when the clock is high. This ensures that the reset is synchronous, only taking effect on the positive edge of the clock. We keep the rest of the logic unchanged, as it correctly implements the 8 D flip-flops with active high synchronous reset.
