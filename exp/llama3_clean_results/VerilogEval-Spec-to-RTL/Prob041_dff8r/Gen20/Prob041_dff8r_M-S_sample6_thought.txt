To simplify the solution, we can directly implement the required functionality within the TopModule without instantiating a separate DFF module. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for an additional module and its associated instantiation and port connections.

The simplified TopModule will still contain 8 D flip-flops, each with an active high synchronous reset and triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal. The reset and clock signals will be applied directly to the always_ff block, which will update the output q based on the input d when the reset signal is not active.

By reducing the hierarchy and eliminating the separate module, the design becomes more straightforward and easier to understand, which can also potentially simplify the synthesis and optimization process.
