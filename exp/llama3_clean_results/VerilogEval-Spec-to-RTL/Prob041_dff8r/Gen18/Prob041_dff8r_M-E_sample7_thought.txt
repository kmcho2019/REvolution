This approach not only reduces the number of flip-flops required but also introduces a degree of parallelism, as each bit of the input data is processed simultaneously. 

The shift register will be initialized with zeros when the reset signal is asserted. On each positive edge of the clock signal, the shift register will shift its contents one bit to the right, and the new input bit will be loaded into the most significant bit position. 

This architectural idea simplifies the design, reduces the number of components, and potentially improves the overall performance by minimizing the number of flip-flops and the associated routing delays.
