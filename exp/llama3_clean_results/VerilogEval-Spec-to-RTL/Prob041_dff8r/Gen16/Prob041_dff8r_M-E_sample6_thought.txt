To create a novel solution, we will explore the concept of a serial-in, parallel-out (SIPO) shift register combined with a parallel-in, parallel-out (PIPO) register to achieve the functionality of 8 D flip-flops with synchronous reset. The SIPO shift register will be used to load the 8-bit data in a serial manner, and then the PIPO register will be used to hold the data and provide it to the output. The synchronous reset will be applied to the PIPO register, ensuring that the output is reset to zero when the reset signal is high. This approach will provide a unique and different solution from the previous ones.
