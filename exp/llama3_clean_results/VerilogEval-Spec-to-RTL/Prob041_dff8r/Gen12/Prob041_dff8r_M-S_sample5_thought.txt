The existing design can be simplified by removing unnecessary logic and signals. Since the clock enable signal (clk_en) is always set to 1, it can be eliminated, and the D flip-flop can be directly triggered by the positive edge of the clock signal (clk) with a synchronous reset. This approach reduces the complexity of the design while maintaining the required functionality. The simplified design will directly assign the input value (d) to the output (q) when the reset is not active and the clock signal is high, thus eliminating the need for the clock enable signal.
