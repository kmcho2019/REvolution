To simplify the existing solution, we can remove the separate D flip-flop module and directly implement the 8 D flip-flops within the TopModule using a single `always` block. This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for a separate module and its instantiation. We can still maintain the active high synchronous reset and the positive edge triggering of the D flip-flops. This simplification should lead to reduced area and power consumption.
