The novel architectural idea is to use a combination of bit-wise operations and conditional assignments to achieve the desired functionality. Instead of using a decoder or a multiplexer, we can directly manipulate the bits of the output signal q based on the value of input c. This approach eliminates the need for an intermediate select signal and reduces the overall complexity of the design. By using bit-wise operations, we can also reduce the number of logic gates required, potentially leading to area and power savings.
