A novel approach to implementing the TopModule as a wire can involve utilizing a buffer gate, which is essentially a non-inverting amplifier that isolates the input from the output, preventing any loading effects. This design choice emphasizes simplicity, reliability, and adherence to the fundamental principles of digital circuit design. By using a buffer, we not only ensure that the signal is passed through without inversion but also provide a clear, straightforward solution that can be easily understood and analyzed for its performance, power consumption, and area requirements. This method differs significantly from the previous MUX-based approach, focusing on direct signal propagation rather than multiplexing, which can be more intuitive for designs that do not require the selection between multiple inputs.
