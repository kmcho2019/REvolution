A novel approach to implementing the TopModule with wire-like behavior could involve utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) with a fixed selection signal. By setting the selection signal to always choose the input port, we effectively create a pass-through circuit that mimics the behavior of a wire. This approach allows for flexibility and modularity, as the MUX can be easily reconfigured or replaced with a different component if needed. Furthermore, this design can be useful in scenarios where the input needs to be routed through a specific path or when the wire-like behavior needs to be conditionally enabled or disabled.
