A novel architectural solution for the TopModule can be achieved by utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) with a fixed selection input that always selects the input signal to be passed through to the output. This approach introduces a different paradigm where the module's behavior is not just a simple wire but is instead controlled by a selection mechanism, albeit a static one. This methodology can offer flexibility for future enhancements, such as dynamic signal selection, while maintaining the current wire-like behavior.
