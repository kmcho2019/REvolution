To create a novel architectural idea for the TopModule that behaves like a wire, we can consider using a transparent latch. A transparent latch is a type of sequential logic circuit that can store a value when the clock signal is low and propagate the input to the output when the clock signal is high. By always keeping the clock signal high, the latch will continuously propagate the input to the output, effectively acting like a wire. This approach introduces a new level of complexity and can be optimized further based on the specific technology and requirements. However, it's essential to note that this method may not be the most efficient in terms of power consumption and area compared to a simple direct assignment. Nevertheless, it represents a different architectural approach to achieving the desired functionality.
