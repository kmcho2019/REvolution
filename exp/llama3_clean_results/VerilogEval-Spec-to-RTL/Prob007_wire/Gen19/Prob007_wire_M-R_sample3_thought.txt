To refactor the existing code, the idea is to maintain the same wire-like behavior for the TopModule but simplify the implementation. The current design uses a separate BufferModule instantiation within TopModule. A more straightforward approach would be to directly use a continuous assignment within the TopModule itself, eliminating the need for a separate module. This simplification aligns with the feedback to reduce unnecessary overhead in terms of area and power consumption. By directly assigning the input to the output within the TopModule, we can potentially decrease the area and power metrics without affecting the functionality.
