To improve the existing solution for the TopModule, we can adopt a direct assignment approach, eliminating the need for instantiating another module like BufferGate. This method not only simplifies the design but also reduces the overhead associated with module instantiation, potentially leading to better Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. By directly assigning the input to the output within the TopModule, we maintain the wire-like behavior while minimizing the complexity and the number of components involved. This approach aligns with the principles of digital design, aiming for simplicity, reliability, and optimal resource utilization. Given the metrics provided, with a focus on improving the effective clock period and considering the minimal area usage, the direct assignment method seems promising for achieving better PPA metrics without altering the fundamental functionality.
