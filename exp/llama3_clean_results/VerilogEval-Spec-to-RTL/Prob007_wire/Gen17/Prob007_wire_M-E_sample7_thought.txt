A novel approach to implementing the TopModule with wire-like behavior could involve utilizing a multiplexer (MUX) with a single input and output, where the select line is tied to a constant value that always selects the input. This approach may seem counterintuitive for a simple wire-like behavior, but it introduces an interesting architectural element - the ability to easily expand the module to handle multiple inputs in the future by simply changing the select line's control. This could be beneficial in designs where the requirement might evolve to handle multiple inputs, and the existing module can be adapted without a significant redesign.
