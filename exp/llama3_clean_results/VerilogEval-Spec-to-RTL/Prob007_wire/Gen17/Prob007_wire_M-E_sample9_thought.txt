To create a novel solution, we can leverage the concept of a multiplexer (MUX) to act as a wire. By using a 2:1 MUX and tying one of the input ports to the input signal and the other to a constant (either 0 or 1), and then controlling the select line to always choose the input signal, we effectively create a buffer that passes the input to the output. This approach, although less direct than a simple assignment, demonstrates an alternative method to achieve the desired functionality and showcases the versatility of digital design components.
