To improve the existing solution, the focus should be on optimizing the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. Given that the current implementation already achieves the desired functionality of behaving like a wire, the strategy for improvement involves simplifying the design to reduce unnecessary overhead. The use of a separate buffer module, although demonstrating modularity, may introduce additional area and power consumption due to the module instantiation. Therefore, the approach should be to eliminate unnecessary components or complexity while maintaining the wire-like behavior. This can be achieved by directly assigning the input to the output within the TopModule, effectively bypassing the need for a separate buffer module. This method reduces the design's complexity, potentially leading to better area and power efficiency without affecting the timing or functionality.
