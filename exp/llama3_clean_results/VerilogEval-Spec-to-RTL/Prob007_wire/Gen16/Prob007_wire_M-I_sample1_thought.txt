To improve the existing solution, we should focus on optimizing the Area and Power consumption, as suggested by the PPA metrics. The current implementation uses a separate Buffer module which, although providing modularity, may not be necessary for such a simple functionality. By removing the Buffer module and directly assigning the input to the output within the TopModule, we can reduce the area consumption. This simplification should also lead to a slight reduction in power consumption due to the removal of unnecessary components. The improved design will maintain the same functionality as a wire while enhancing its performance in terms of Area and Power.
