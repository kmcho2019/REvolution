The current implementation of the TopModule, which utilizes a BufferModule to achieve wire-like behavior, is functionally correct but may not be optimal in terms of Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. To improve the PPA, the focus should be on simplifying the design to reduce unnecessary overhead. Since the functionality required is essentially that of a wire, the most straightforward and efficient implementation would be to directly assign the input to the output within the TopModule, eliminating the need for an additional module instantiation. This approach ensures that the design behaves like a wire while minimizing area and power consumption. By removing the BufferModule and using a continuous assignment directly within the TopModule, we can achieve the desired functionality with the least possible overhead, thereby optimizing the PPA metrics.
