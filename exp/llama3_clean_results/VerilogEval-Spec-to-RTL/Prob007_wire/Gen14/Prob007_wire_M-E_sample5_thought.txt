To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we can utilize a latch-based design. This approach involves using a level-sensitive latch to capture the input value when it is valid (i.e., when a specific condition is met) and then hold that value until the next update. In this context, we can simplify the condition to always capture the input, effectively mirroring its behavior. This method diverges from the traditional wire-like assignment by incorporating sequential logic elements, albeit in a manner that still achieves the desired wire-like functionality. The use of a latch implies that the output will reflect the input as long as the latch is transparent, which, in our simplified case, is always. This design choice allows for an exploration of different architectural possibilities, even though the functionality remains similar to a straightforward wire.
