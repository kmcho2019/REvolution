To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule while maintaining its wire-like behavior, we can consider utilizing a buffer element. A buffer in digital design is essentially a device that isolates the input from the output, preventing backflow of signals, but for the purpose of this module, it can be used to introduce a delay or simply to demonstrate a different way of achieving the same wire-like functionality. This approach involves using a combinational logic block that simply passes the input to the output without any alteration, thereby acting as a buffer.
