A novel approach to implementing the TopModule with a constant output of 0 is to utilize a simple clocked sequential logic. This involves using a register to hold the output value, which is initialized to 0. The output of the register is then directly connected to the module's output port. This method ensures the output remains at 0 and introduces a basic form of sequential logic, which could be beneficial in larger designs where this module might be integrated as a component. The idea here is not to improve the existing purely combinational solution in terms of functionality but to explore an alternative architectural approach that still meets the requirements.
