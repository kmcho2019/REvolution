A novel approach to driving the output to a constant 0 is to utilize a combinational logic block that always evaluates to 0, regardless of the inputs. Since there are no inputs to the module, we can create a simple always block that assigns the output to 0. This method maintains the simplicity of the original design while introducing a different architectural concept. By using an always block, we can potentially leverage the benefits of procedural assignment, even though the outcome in this case remains constant.
