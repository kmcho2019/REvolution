The `assign` statement in Verilog is used for continuous assignment, which means the right-hand side expression is evaluated whenever any of the inputs change, and the result is assigned to the left-hand side. In this case, since we are assigning a constant value, the expression is trivial and does not depend on any inputs, making it a simple and efficient way to implement the required functionality.
