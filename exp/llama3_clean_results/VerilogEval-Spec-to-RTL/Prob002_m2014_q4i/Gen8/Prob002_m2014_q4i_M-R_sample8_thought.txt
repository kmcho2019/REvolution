The refactored approach to implement the TopModule involves utilizing a continuous assignment within an always block, albeit indirectly, by leveraging the fact that a non-blocking assignment within an always block can effectively act as a continuous assignment for combinational logic when the always block is not dependent on any clock signal. This approach allows for the same functionality as the original code but with a different structure, showcasing the versatility of Verilog in implementing digital circuits. The idea revolves around the understanding that in digital design, the same functionality can often be achieved through different architectural choices, each with its own trade-offs in terms of area, power, and performance.
