To create a novel solution for implementing a 2-input AND gate within the TopModule, we'll explore a behavioral modeling approach. This method involves describing the desired functionality using behavioral Verilog constructs without explicitly defining the gate-level implementation. The key idea is to leverage the synthesis tool's capability to infer the optimal hardware implementation from the behavioral description, potentially leading to more efficient designs in terms of area and power consumption. We will utilize a combinational always block to describe the AND operation directly, without instantiating a separate module for the AND gate. This approach simplifies the design hierarchy and relies on the synthesis tool to generate an efficient implementation.
