The idea is to implement the 2-input AND gate using a multiplexer (MUX) with a novel architectural approach. This approach takes advantage of the properties of a 2-to-1 MUX, where the select line controls which input is passed to the output. By assigning the inputs 'a' and 'b' to the MUX inputs and using their logical AND operation as the select line, we can effectively implement the AND gate functionality. This approach not only provides an alternative to the traditional AND gate implementation but also offers a unique perspective on digital logic design.
