To introduce a novel architectural idea, let's implement the 2-input AND gate using a combinational logic approach with a focus on bitwise operations. This involves directly utilizing the bitwise AND operator (&) to compute the output. The novelty in this approach lies in its straightforwardness and the fact that it doesn't require the instantiation of a separate module for the AND operation. Instead, the TopModule directly incorporates the logic, making it a flat, efficient design. This method promotes simplicity and can be beneficial for designs where hierarchy is not a priority, or when aiming for minimal overhead in terms of area and power consumption.
