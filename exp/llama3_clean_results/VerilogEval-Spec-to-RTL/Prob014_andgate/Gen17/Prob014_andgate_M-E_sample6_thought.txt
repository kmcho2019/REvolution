To implement a 2-input AND gate with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a lookup table (LUT) based approach. This method involves creating a module that essentially acts as a small memory, where the inputs to the AND gate serve as addresses to this memory, and the output is determined by the value stored at the addressed location. This approach allows for a flexible and scalable way to implement logic gates, as it can be easily extended to more complex gates or even small combinational logic circuits by simply adding more entries to the lookup table.
