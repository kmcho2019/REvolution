To implement a 2-input AND gate in Verilog with a novel architectural idea, we can utilize a multiplexer (MUX) to select between two inputs based on a control signal. The MUX can be configured to pass the output of the 'a' input when 'b' is high, and pass a constant '0' when 'b' is low. This approach leverages the properties of the AND operation to achieve the desired result without directly using the logical AND operator.
