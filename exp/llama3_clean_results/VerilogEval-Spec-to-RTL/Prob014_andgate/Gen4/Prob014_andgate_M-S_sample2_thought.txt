The existing solution is already quite simple and efficient, directly implementing a 2-input AND gate with a single line of code. To further simplify, we can maintain the same approach, as any additional simplification might compromise readability without offering significant improvements in terms of Power, Performance, or Area (PPA). The current implementation using an `assign` statement for a continuous assignment is the most straightforward way to express a simple combinational logic circuit like an AND gate in Verilog.
