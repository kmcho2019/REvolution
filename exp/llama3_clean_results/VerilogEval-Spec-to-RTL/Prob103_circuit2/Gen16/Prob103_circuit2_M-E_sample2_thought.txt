The novel architectural idea is to implement a Karnaugh map (K-map) reduction for the given truth table. This method involves creating a K-map, then applying the rules of K-map simplification to minimize the logic expression. The resulting simplified expression can then be implemented in Verilog to create the desired combinational circuit. The K-map will help identify the essential prime implicants, and by doing so, we might be able to find an alternative, potentially more efficient implementation compared to the straightforward inverse XOR logic.
