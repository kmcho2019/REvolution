A novel architectural idea for implementing the TopModule is to utilize a Karnaugh map (K-map) based approach. This involves analyzing the simulation log to identify patterns and deriving a simplified logic expression that represents the required functionality. By applying the principles of Boolean algebra and K-map reduction, we can obtain a minimal sum-of-products (SOP) or product-of-sums (POS) form that directly implements the inverse XOR logic.

The key insight here is to recognize that the desired output q is 1 whenever there is an even number of 1s among the inputs a, b, c, and d. This can be achieved by counting the number of 1s and then using a simple logic to determine the output based on this count. The K-map approach allows us to systematically reduce the logic expression and identify the minimal terms required to implement the desired functionality.

Our new solution will involve the following steps:
1. Derive the truth table from the simulation log.
2. Apply K-map reduction to obtain a simplified SOP or POS form.
3. Implement the resulting logic expression in Verilog.
