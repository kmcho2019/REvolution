The existing solution already implements a full adder using basic Boolean algebra. However, we can further simplify the implementation by breaking down the carry-out equation into more straightforward logic. Since the carry-out of a full adder is generated when at least two of the inputs (a, b, or cin) are high, we can use this property to simplify the equation. The sum remains the same, calculated as the XOR of the three inputs. The carry-out can be generated by checking for any combination of two or more high inputs. This simplification maintains the same functionality but may provide a slight improvement in readability and maintainability.
