To implement a full adder, we need to consider the truth table for the addition of three bits. The sum (S) and carry-out (Cout) can be calculated using the following logic:
- Sum (S) = a XOR b XOR cin
- Carry-out (Cout) = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin)
This logic can be directly implemented in Verilog to create the full adder module.
