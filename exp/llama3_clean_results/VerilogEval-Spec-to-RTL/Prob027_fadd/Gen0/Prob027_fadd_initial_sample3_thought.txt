To implement a full adder, we need to follow the standard full adder logic, which takes into account the inputs a, b, and cin (carry-in), and produces sum and cout (carry-out). The sum is calculated by adding a, b, and cin, considering only the least significant bit of the result, and the carry-out is set if the sum of a, b, and cin exceeds 1. This can be achieved by using the following Boolean expressions: sum = a ^ b ^ cin and cout = (a & b) | (a & cin) | (b & cin).
