To implement a full adder, we can use basic digital logic gates. The sum (S) of a full adder can be calculated using the XOR operation among the three inputs (a, b, and cin), which can be expressed as S = a XOR b XOR cin. The carry-out (Cout) can be generated by checking all combinations where the sum of any two inputs is 1 (since in binary, 1 + 1 = 10, which means a carry is generated). This can be expressed as Cout = (a AND b) OR (a AND cin) OR (b AND cin).
