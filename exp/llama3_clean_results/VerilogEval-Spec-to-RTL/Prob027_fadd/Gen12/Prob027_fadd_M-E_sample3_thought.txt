A novel approach to implement a full adder is to use a Karnaugh map (K-map) reduction technique to derive the minimal sum of products (SOP) expressions for both the sum and carry-out. This method involves creating a truth table for all possible input combinations and then using the K-map to simplify the logic expressions. The resulting SOP expressions can then be implemented directly in Verilog, potentially offering a different perspective on optimizing the design for area and power consumption.
