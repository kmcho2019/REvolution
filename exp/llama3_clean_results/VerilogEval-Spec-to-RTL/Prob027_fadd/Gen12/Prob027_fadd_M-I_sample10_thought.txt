To improve the existing full adder implementation, we can simplify the logic and reduce the number of components by directly calculating the sum and carry-out in a single always block. This approach eliminates the need for two half adders and the associated wiring, which should improve performance by reducing the number of logic levels. Additionally, this simplified design may lead to area and power reductions due to the minimized use of logic gates and reduced complexity. The new design will directly compute the sum and carry-out based on the input bits and the carry-in signal, leveraging the bitwise XOR and AND operations for sum calculation and the bitwise OR operation for carry-out calculation.
