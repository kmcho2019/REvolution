To simplify the full adder implementation, we can directly express the sum and carry-out logic using basic Boolean operations without needing to instantiate two half-adders. The sum can be calculated by XORing all three inputs (a, b, cin), and the carry-out can be generated by checking all conditions where a carry is produced (a and b, a and cin, or b and cin). This approach reduces the complexity of the design by eliminating the need for intermediate wires and half-adder modules.
