To implement a full adder, we can use a novel approach based on a lookup table (LUT) and combinational logic. The idea is to first generate all possible combinations of the three input bits, then use a LUT to determine the sum and carry-out for each combination. This approach allows for a flexible and efficient design, as it eliminates the need for multiple half-adders and OR gates. Additionally, this design can be easily extended to support larger adders by increasing the size of the LUT.
