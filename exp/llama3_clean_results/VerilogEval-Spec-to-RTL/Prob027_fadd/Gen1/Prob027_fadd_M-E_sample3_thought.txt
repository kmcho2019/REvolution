To implement a full adder, we can use a novel approach based on a half-adder and an OR gate. The idea is to first add two of the input bits using a half-adder, and then add the result with the third input bit using another half-adder. The carry-out of the full adder can be obtained by ORing the carry-out of the two half-adders. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design.
