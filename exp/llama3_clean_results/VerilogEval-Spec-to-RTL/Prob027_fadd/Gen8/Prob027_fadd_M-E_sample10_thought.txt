To implement a full adder, we can leverage the properties of binary numbers and utilize a novel approach based on a Karnaugh map (K-map) simplification. The idea is to directly implement the sum and carry-out logic using a single, optimized logic equation derived from the truth table of a full adder. This approach eliminates the need for separate half-adder modules and the OR gate, potentially reducing area and power consumption. The K-map simplification provides a systematic method to minimize the logic, leading to an efficient and compact implementation.
