The TopModule will be designed with the following components: 
1. A 7-bit global branch history register (ghr) to keep track of the branch history.
2. A 128-entry table of two-bit saturating counters (pht) to store the prediction outcomes.
3. Combinational logic to generate the index for accessing the pht table by hashing (using xor) the 7-bit pc and 7-bit global history.
4. Sequential logic to update the ghr and pht based on the prediction and training requests.

When predict_valid is high, the current state of the ghr and the predicted branch direction (based on the current pht state) will be output. The ghr will then be updated at the next positive clock edge for the predicted branch.

When train_valid is high, the pht will be updated at the next positive clock edge to train the branch predictor. If the branch was mispredicted, the ghr will also be recovered to the state immediately after the mispredicting branch completes execution.

If both prediction and training occur in the same cycle, training takes precedence over prediction.
