To simplify the existing solution, I propose to maintain the same architectural approach but with a reduced complexity in the logic. The prediction and training logic will be separated, and the history update logic will be simplified. The branch predictor will still consist of a prediction block, a training block, and a history update block. However, the logic within these blocks will be reduced to the minimum necessary to maintain the functionality.

The prediction block will generate the predicted branch direction based on the current PC and branch history. The training block will update the PHT based on the actual branch outcome and the branch history. The history update block will update the branch history register based on the predicted branch outcome and the training information.

I will introduce an asynchronous reset (active-high) and modify the prediction logic to correctly predict the branch direction based on the PHT value. The training logic will be updated to correctly update the PHT and history register. The history update logic will be simplified to correctly update the history register based on the predicted branch outcome and training information.

To reduce complexity, I will remove unnecessary conditions and combine the logic where possible. The result will be a more efficient and simpler design.
