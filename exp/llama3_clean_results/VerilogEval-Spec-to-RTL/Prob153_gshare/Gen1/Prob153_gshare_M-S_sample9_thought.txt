The TopModule will be simplified by removing unnecessary variables and reducing the complexity of the combinational logic. 
1. The global branch history register (ghr) will be updated directly based on the prediction and training requests.
2. The pattern history table (pht) will be updated based on the training request.
3. The index for accessing the pht table will be generated using a single XOR operation between the 7-bit pc and the 7-bit global history.
4. The prediction and training logic will be simplified to reduce the number of conditional statements and operations.
