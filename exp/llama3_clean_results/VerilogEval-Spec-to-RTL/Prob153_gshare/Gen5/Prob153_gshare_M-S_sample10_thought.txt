We will maintain a 7-bit GBHR and a 128-entry PHT table of 2-bit saturating counters. The index into the PHT table will be a 7-bit hash of the 7-bit PC and 7-bit GBHR.

When a prediction is made, we update the GBHR based on the predicted branch direction. When training is requested, we update the PHT table based on the actual branch outcome. If the branch being trained is a misprediction, we recover the GBHR to its previous state.
