The novel architectural idea for the TopModule involves utilizing a single, unified module that integrates all three operations (out_both, out_any, out_different) into a single generate loop. This approach aims to minimize the overall area consumption by reducing the number of modules and potentially sharing intermediate results between operations.

To further optimize the design, the unified module will utilize a shift register-like structure to efficiently handle the wrap-around condition for the out_different operation. This structure will enable the design to maintain a consistent and efficient architecture across all operations, reducing the complexity and area consumption.

By combining the operations into a single module and utilizing a shift register-like structure, the design aims to achieve a better balance between area efficiency and performance, ultimately leading to improved PPA metrics.
