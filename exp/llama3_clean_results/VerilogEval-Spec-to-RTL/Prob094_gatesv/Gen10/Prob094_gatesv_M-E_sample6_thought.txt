This modular approach also improves maintainability, as any changes to the logic can be made in a single place, rather than having to update multiple locations.

For out_any, we use a similar approach but shift the input vector to the left. The out_different logic remains the same, utilizing the XOR operator to find the difference between each bit and its neighbor to the left, wrapping around for the last bit.
