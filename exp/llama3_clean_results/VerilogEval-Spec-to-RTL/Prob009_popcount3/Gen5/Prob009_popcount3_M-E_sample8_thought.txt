To introduce a novel architectural idea for the population count circuit, we can leverage a lookup table (LUT) approach. The concept involves pre-computing all possible combinations of the 3-bit input vector and storing their respective population counts in a LUT. This method eliminates the need for arithmetic operations at runtime, potentially reducing power consumption and improving performance by minimizing the critical path. Given the small input size (3 bits), the LUT will be relatively small, containing 2^3 = 8 entries, making this approach feasible in terms of area. Each entry in the LUT corresponds to a unique input combination and its associated count of '1's, which directly feeds into the output without needing any additional computation.
