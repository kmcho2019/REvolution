A novel approach to implementing the combinational circuit could involve using a lookup table (LUT) to directly map the input combinations to their corresponding output values. This method takes advantage of the fact that FPGAs and many ASICs have LUTs as a basic building block, which can be configured to implement small combinational logic functions. By using a LUT, we can directly implement the truth table without needing to derive and optimize a Boolean expression. This approach could potentially offer advantages in terms of area and power consumption, as it leverages the existing, highly optimized LUT structures within the target technology.
