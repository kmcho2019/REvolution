We can design a module named TopModule with a simple combinational logic architecture that uses a priority encoder to determine the flow rate based on the water level sensors. The priority encoder will assign a higher priority to the higher sensor signals, and the output signals will be asserted or deasserted based on the encoded value. This approach eliminates the need for a state machine and reduces the complexity of the design. Additionally, we can use a separate module to handle the logic for asserting the 'dfr' signal, which will be triggered by the rising edge of the sensor signals.
