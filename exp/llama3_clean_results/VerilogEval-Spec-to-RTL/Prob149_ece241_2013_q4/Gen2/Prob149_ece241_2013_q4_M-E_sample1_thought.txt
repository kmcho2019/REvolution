To create a novel solution, we can utilize a finite state machine (FSM) that takes into account the current and previous states of the water level sensors. This FSM will be able to track the current water level and make decisions based on the previous level to control the flow rate. The states of the FSM will be defined based on the sensors' inputs, and the transitions between these states will be determined by the current and previous sensor states. We will use a more modular approach, breaking down the logic into smaller, more manageable sections. The FSM will be implemented using a combination of `always` blocks and `case` statements to handle the different states and transitions.
