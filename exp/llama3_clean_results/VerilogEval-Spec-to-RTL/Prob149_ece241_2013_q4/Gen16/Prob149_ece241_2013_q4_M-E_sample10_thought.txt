My novel approach involves designing a module that uses a priority encoder to determine the current water level and then uses a combination of logic gates and counters to control the flow rate. The priority encoder will assign a higher priority to the higher water level sensors, ensuring that the correct flow rate is applied based on the current water level. The module will also include a counter to track the previous water level and assert the supplemental flow valve when the level is increasing. This approach simplifies the state machine and output logic, making the code more efficient and easier to understand.
