To simplify the solution, we can eliminate the need for a complex state machine by directly calculating the flow rates based on the sensor inputs. We can use combinatorial logic to determine the flow rates and the Supplemental flow valve (`dfr`) status. The `fr2`, `fr1`, and `fr0` outputs can be set based on the water level sensors, and the `dfr` output can be set based on the change in sensor state. This approach reduces the complexity of the design and makes it easier to understand and maintain.
