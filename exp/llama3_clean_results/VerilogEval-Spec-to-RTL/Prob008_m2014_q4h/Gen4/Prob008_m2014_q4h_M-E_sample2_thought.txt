A novel architectural idea for implementing the TopModule is to utilize a basic digital component such as a buffer. However, instead of directly using a continuous assignment, we could encapsulate the input-output relationship within a procedural block, specifically inside an always combinatorial block. This approach, although more complex than necessary for such a simple module, demonstrates how to achieve the same functionality through different means, showcasing Verilog's flexibility in implementing digital circuits. The idea is to use an always block to continuously update the output based on the input, highlighting an alternative method to continuous assignments for combinational logic.
