To propose a novel solution, we can leverage the concept of a buffer, which is a digital circuit that isolates the input from the output and does not alter the signal. In this context, using a buffer to assign the output port to the same value as the input port combinationally presents a straightforward yet distinct architectural approach. This method directly addresses the requirement without introducing unnecessary complexity, such as the MUX-based implementation. By utilizing a buffer, we can ensure a clean and simple design that optimizes for Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
