A simplified approach to implementing the TopModule involves directly assigning the input signal to the output port without any conditional logic or additional components like multiplexers. This straightforward method eliminates unnecessary switching activity, reduces the area by removing redundant logic, and maintains the combinational nature of the design, thereby potentially improving Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics. The key insight here is recognizing that the direct assignment satisfies the requirement of the output being the same as the input, achieving the desired functionality in the most straightforward and efficient manner.
