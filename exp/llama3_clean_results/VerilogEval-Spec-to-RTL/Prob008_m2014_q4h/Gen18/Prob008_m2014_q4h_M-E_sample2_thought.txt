To introduce a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we can leverage the concept of a buffer, which essentially acts as a digital repeater. The buffer can be used to drive the output port directly with the input port's value, ensuring a strong signal without any additional combinational logic. This approach maintains the simplicity and efficiency of the original direct assignment method but frames it within the context of signal buffering, which can be beneficial in certain digital design scenarios where signal integrity and strength are crucial. This buffer-based approach can be particularly useful in situations where the input signal needs to be amplified or repeated for driving longer wires or more complex logic downstream.
