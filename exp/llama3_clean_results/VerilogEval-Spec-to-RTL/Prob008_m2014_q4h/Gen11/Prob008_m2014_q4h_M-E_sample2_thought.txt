A novel approach to implementing the TopModule could involve using a simple always block to continuously monitor the input and update the output accordingly. This method deviates from the traditional continuous assignment and instead utilizes procedural assignment within an always block. Although this might introduce additional overhead due to the implicit sensitivity list and potential for sequential behavior, it provides an alternative perspective on achieving the same combinational functionality. The key idea here is to ensure the always block does not introduce any sequential logic, thus maintaining the combinational nature of the module.
