A simplified approach to implementing the finite state machine is to directly calculate the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates and feed them into the D flip-flops. This eliminates the need for intermediate stages and simplifies the combinational logic. The second inputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates can be directly connected to the outputs of the corresponding D flip-flops, with the AND and OR gates using the complemented output of the flip-flops. The outputs of the D flip-flops can then be directly used to calculate the output 'z' using a NOR gate.
