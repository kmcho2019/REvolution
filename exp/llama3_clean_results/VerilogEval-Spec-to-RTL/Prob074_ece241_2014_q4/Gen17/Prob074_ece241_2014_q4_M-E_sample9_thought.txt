A novel architectural idea for implementing the TopModule involves using a more hierarchical approach. Instead of directly integrating all the gate logic and D flip-flops within the TopModule, we can create separate sub-modules for each type of gate connected to a D flip-flop. This approach allows for better modularity, reusability, and potentially easier debugging.

Each sub-module will handle the logic for its respective gate type, including the D flip-flop and the calculation of the gate output. The TopModule will then instantiate these sub-modules and compute the final output 'z' as the NOR of the outputs from each sub-module.

This modular design could potentially improve maintainability and allow for easier modifications or replacements of individual gate logics without affecting the overall architecture. Additionally, it may facilitate better optimization opportunities at the sub-module level, depending on the specific requirements and constraints of each gate type.
