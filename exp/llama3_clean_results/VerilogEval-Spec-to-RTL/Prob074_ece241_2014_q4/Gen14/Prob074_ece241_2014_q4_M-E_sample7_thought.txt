A novel approach to implement the finite state machine is to utilize a hierarchical design, where each gate and its corresponding D flip-flop are encapsulated within a separate module. This approach allows for a clear and organized structure, making it easier to understand and maintain the code.

Each module, named GateFF, will take the input 'x', the clock 'clk', and the gate type as inputs, and produce the output and the state of the flip-flop. The gate type will determine the operation performed by the gate.

The TopModule will instantiate three GateFF modules, one for each gate type (XOR, AND, and OR), and connect their outputs to a NOR gate to compute the final output 'z'.

This hierarchical design enables a more modular and scalable implementation, allowing for easy modification or extension of the finite state machine's behavior.
