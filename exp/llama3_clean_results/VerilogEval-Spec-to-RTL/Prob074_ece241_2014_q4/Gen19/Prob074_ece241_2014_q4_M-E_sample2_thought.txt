A novel approach to solving this problem is to create a hierarchical module structure, where each gate and its corresponding D flip-flop are encapsulated within a separate submodule. This modular design allows for easier maintenance, debugging, and reuse of the individual components. Additionally, it enables a clearer separation of concerns between the combinational logic of the gates and the sequential logic of the D flip-flops.

The top-level module, TopModule, will instantiate three instances of the submodule, GateFF, each representing a different gate type (XOR, AND, OR) connected to a D flip-flop. The outputs of these submodules will be connected to a NOR gate to produce the final output 'z'.

This hierarchical design promotes a more organized and scalable architecture, making it easier to extend or modify the system in the future.
