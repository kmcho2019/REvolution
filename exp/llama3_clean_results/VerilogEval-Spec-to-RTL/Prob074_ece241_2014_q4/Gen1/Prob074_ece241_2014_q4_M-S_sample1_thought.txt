To simplify the TopModule, we can directly assign the output of the NOR gate using the built-in Verilog operators and eliminate the need for explicit assignments of the gate inputs. The XOR, AND, and OR gates will be directly connected to the D flip-flops, and the outputs of the flip-flops will be directly connected to the NOR gate. This approach reduces the complexity of the code and makes it more straightforward to understand and maintain.
