A novel approach to implementing the finite state machine involves using a two-stage process within the always block. In the first stage, the outputs of the XOR, AND, and OR gates are computed based on the current state of the flip-flops and the input 'x'. These gate outputs are then used to update the state of the flip-flops. In the second stage, the outputs of the updated flip-flops are used to compute the final output 'z' as the NOR of the flip-flop outputs. This approach separates the concerns of gate output computation, state update, and final output computation, leading to a clearer and more maintainable design. Additionally, this approach ensures that the outputs of the flip-flops are correctly used as inputs to the gates and that the final output 'z' is computed based on the updated state of the flip-flops.
