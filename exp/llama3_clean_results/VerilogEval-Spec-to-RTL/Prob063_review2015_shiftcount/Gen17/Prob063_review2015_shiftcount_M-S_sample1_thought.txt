The problem can be solved by using a simpler approach that directly manipulates the register `q_reg` based on the control inputs `shift_ena` and `count_ena`. When `shift_ena` is asserted, the new data bit is shifted into the most significant bit position of `q_reg`. When `count_ena` is asserted, `q_reg` is decremented. This approach eliminates the need for a finite state machine and reduces the overall complexity of the design.
