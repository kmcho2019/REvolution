A novel approach to designing the TopModule is to utilize a finite state machine (FSM) that manages the shift and decrement operations based on the enable signals. The FSM can be designed to handle the two operations separately, ensuring that the module behaves correctly under all conditions. By using an FSM, we can explicitly define the states and transitions, making it easier to understand and verify the module's behavior. This approach allows for a clear separation of concerns, making the design more modular and maintainable.
