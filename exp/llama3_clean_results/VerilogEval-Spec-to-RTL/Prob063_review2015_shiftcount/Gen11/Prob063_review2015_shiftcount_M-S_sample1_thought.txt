A simplified approach is to directly update the 'q_reg' based on the 'shift_ena' and 'count_ena' signals. When 'shift_ena' is high, the new data bit is shifted into the most significant bit of 'q_reg', and the existing bits are shifted to the right. When 'count_ena' is high and 'q_reg' is not zero, 'q_reg' is decremented by 1. If 'q_reg' is zero and 'count_ena' is high, 'q_reg' remains at zero. We can achieve this by using a single always block with conditional statements to handle the different cases.
