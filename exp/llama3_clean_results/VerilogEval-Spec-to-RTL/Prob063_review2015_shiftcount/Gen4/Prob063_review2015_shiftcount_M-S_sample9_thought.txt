A simplified approach to designing the TopModule is to combine the shift and decrement operations directly into the sequential logic, eliminating the need for a separate next state register. The module can be designed to check for the shift enable signal first, and if it's active, perform the shift operation. If the shift enable signal is not active, the module can then check for the count enable signal and perform the decrement operation if it's active. This approach reduces the complexity of the combinational logic block and minimizes the number of conditional statements, resulting in a more efficient and area-friendly design. By directly updating the register within the sequential logic block, the design also reduces the number of unnecessary assignments and improves the overall timing.
