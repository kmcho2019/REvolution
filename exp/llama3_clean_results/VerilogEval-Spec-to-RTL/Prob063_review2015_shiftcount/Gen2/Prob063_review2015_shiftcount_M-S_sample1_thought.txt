To simplify the solution, we can combine the two always blocks into a single always block. This block will handle both the shift and decrement operations based on the values of shift_ena and count_ena. If shift_ena is 1, the shift operation will be performed. If shift_ena is 0 and count_ena is 1, the decrement operation will be performed. This approach eliminates the need for separate always blocks and reduces the overall complexity of the design.
