The novel idea is to implement the TopModule using a finite state machine (FSM) with two distinct states: one for shifting and one for counting. This approach allows for a clear separation of the two functionalities and simplifies the logic. The FSM will have two states: 'shift' and 'count'. The 'shift' state will be responsible for shifting in the new data bit when shift_ena is high, and the 'count' state will handle the decrement operation when count_ena is high. The FSM will transition between these states based on the control inputs. This approach also allows for easy extension to more complex functionalities if needed.
