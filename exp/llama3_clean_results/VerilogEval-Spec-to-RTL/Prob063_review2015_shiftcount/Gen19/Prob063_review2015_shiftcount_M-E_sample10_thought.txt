To create a novel architectural idea for the TopModule, we will consider a hybrid approach that combines the benefits of both shift registers and counters. Instead of using a traditional counter for the decrement operation, we can utilize the shift register itself to implement the counter functionality. This approach eliminates the need for a separate counter circuit, thereby reducing area complexity. Additionally, we will add a mechanism to detect when the shift register is about to overflow during the shift operation and prevent it, ensuring that the register remains within its 4-bit boundary. This novel approach aims to optimize the design for both area and performance.
