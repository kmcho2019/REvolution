A novel approach to implementing the TopModule is to use two separate 4-bit registers: one for the shift operation and another for the count operation. This way, the logic for each operation can be kept separate, making it easier to manage and avoid any potential conflicts between the two operations. When `shift_ena` is high, we update the shift register by shifting in the new data bit from the most significant bit position. When `count_ena` is high, we decrement the count register. The final output `q` is taken from the shift register when `shift_ena` is high, and from the count register when `count_ena` is high. This approach simplifies the logic and makes it easier to ensure that both operations are performed correctly.
