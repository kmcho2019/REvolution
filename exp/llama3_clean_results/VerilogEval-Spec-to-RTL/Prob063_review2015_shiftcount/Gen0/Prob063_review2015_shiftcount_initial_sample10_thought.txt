The TopModule will be implemented as a 4-bit shift register with a down counter. 
When shift_ena is 1, the module will shift in the new data from the most significant bit. 
When count_ena is 1, the module will decrement the current value in the shift register. 
Since the shift_ena and count_ena will not be used together, we can implement a simple priority logic where one of the operations gets priority over the other. 
In this case, we will prioritize the shift operation over the decrement operation when both shift_ena and count_ena are 1.
