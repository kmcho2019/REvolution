To implement the TopModule, we will create a 4-bit register that can perform two functions: shifting and counting. When shift_ena is 1, we will shift the data into the register from the most-significant-bit. When count_ena is 1, we will decrement the value in the register. We will use a 4-bit variable to store the current state of the register. On the positive edge of the clock, we will check the values of shift_ena and count_ena and update the register accordingly.
