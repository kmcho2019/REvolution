A novel approach to implement the JK flip-flop is to use a combination of two D flip-flops with additional logic to handle the JK inputs. The first D flip-flop (DFF1) is used to store the current state (Q), and the second D flip-flop (DFF2) is used to compute the next state based on the JK inputs and the current state. The D input of DFF2 is driven by a combinational logic that implements the JK truth table. This approach allows for a more modular design and easier debugging.
