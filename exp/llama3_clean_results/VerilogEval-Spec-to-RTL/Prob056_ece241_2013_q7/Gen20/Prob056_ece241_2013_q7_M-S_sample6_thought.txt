To simplify the existing JK flip-flop implementation, we can eliminate the unnecessary `Qold` signal and directly express the next state of `Q` based on the current state `Q` and inputs `j` and `k`. The key idea is to use a single `always` block with non-blocking assignments to update the state of `Q` at the positive clock edge, incorporating the logic from the truth table directly into the assignment. This approach reduces the complexity of the design, minimizes unnecessary switching activity, and potentially improves the Power, Performance, and Area (PPA) metrics.
