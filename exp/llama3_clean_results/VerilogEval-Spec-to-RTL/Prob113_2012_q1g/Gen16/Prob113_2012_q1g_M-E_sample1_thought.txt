The novel architectural idea is to use a combination of conditional statements and bitwise operations to simplify the logic. By analyzing the Karnaugh map, we can identify patterns where 'f' is 1, such as when x[2] and x[3] are both 1, or when x[2] is 1 and x[3] is 0 with specific conditions on x[0] and x[1]. We can use if-else statements in a sequential block to evaluate these conditions and assign the value of 'f' accordingly. This approach reduces the complexity of the combinational logic and makes the code easier to understand and maintain.
