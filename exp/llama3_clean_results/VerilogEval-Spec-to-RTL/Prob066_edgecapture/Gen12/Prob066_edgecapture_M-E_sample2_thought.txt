When a falling edge is detected, the corresponding bit in the output register will be set to 1. The output register will be designed to persist the output value until the reset signal is asserted.

This architecture eliminates the need for multiple EdgeDetector modules and reduces the complexity of the design. It also ensures that the output persists once an edge has been detected, until the reset signal is asserted.
