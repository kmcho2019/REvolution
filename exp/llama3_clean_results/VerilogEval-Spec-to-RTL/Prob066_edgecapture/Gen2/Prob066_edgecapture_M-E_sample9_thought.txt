To address the issue of maintaining the output bits at 1 after capturing a transition from 1 to 0, we can utilize a different approach. Instead of relying on a separate `prev_in` register and checking the transition within the sequential logic block, we can maintain a separate register `out_reg` that accumulates the detected transitions. Once a transition is detected, the corresponding bit in `out_reg` is set to 1 and remains so until a reset occurs. This way, each output bit will correctly reflect whether a transition from 1 to 0 has been detected on the corresponding input bit since the last reset.
