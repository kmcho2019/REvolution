To design a novel solution for the TopModule, we can use a shift register to keep track of the previous state of the input vector. This approach allows us to compare the current and previous states directly, enabling us to detect transitions from 1 to 0 accurately. We can then use this information to update the output register, setting the corresponding bit to 1 when a transition is detected and keeping it 1 until the register is reset. This design is modular, efficient, and accurately implements the desired functionality.
