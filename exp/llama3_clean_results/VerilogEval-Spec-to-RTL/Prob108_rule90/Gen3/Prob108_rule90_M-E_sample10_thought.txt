A novel architectural idea for implementing Rule 90 cellular automaton is to leverage the inherent properties of bitwise operations, specifically XOR, to simplify the computation of the next state for each cell. By recognizing that the next state of a cell is determined by the XOR of its left and right neighbors, we can utilize a shift register-like structure to efficiently compute the next state of all cells in parallel. This approach eliminates the need for explicit looping and conditional statements, potentially leading to improved performance and reduced area utilization. Furthermore, we can exploit the regularity of the cellular automaton to create a modular design, where the logic for computing the next state of each cell is identical, allowing for easy replication and scalability.
