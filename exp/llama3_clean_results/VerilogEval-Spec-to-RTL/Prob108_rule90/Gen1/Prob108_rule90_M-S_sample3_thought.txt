To simplify the implementation of the TopModule, we can apply Rule 90 directly by iterating over each cell and computing its next state as the XOR of its left and right neighbors. This approach eliminates the need for complex bitwise operations and shifting. We can use a for loop to iterate over each cell, and the XOR operator (^) to compute the next state. We will assume the boundaries (q[-1] and q[512]) are zero (off) by using 0 in the XOR operation for these cases. The design will still be triggered on the positive edge of the clock.
