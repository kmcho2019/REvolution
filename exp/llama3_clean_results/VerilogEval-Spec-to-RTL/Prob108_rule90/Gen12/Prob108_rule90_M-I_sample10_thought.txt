The main strategy for improvement will be to optimize the combinational logic for better timing and area efficiency. Since the rules of the cellular automaton are straightforward and based on the XOR of neighboring cells, ensuring that the synthesis tool optimizes these operations will be crucial.

However, the conditions can be further simplified by recognizing that the next state is essentially the XOR of the left, center, and right cells.

Secondly, considering the boundary conditions and how they are handled, the current implementation already simplifies these conditions by assigning the left and right boundaries to 0 when necessary. This approach is efficient and does not require further simplification.

Lastly, the sequential logic will be reviewed to ensure it is optimized for minimal switching activity, which directly impacts power consumption. Given the current implementation already updates the register directly without unnecessary checks, this aspect seems to be well-handled. However, any further optimizations that can reduce switching activity without compromising timing will be explored.

Overall, the goal is to achieve a balanced improvement across all PPA metrics, ensuring that the design is efficient in terms of area, power, and timing.
