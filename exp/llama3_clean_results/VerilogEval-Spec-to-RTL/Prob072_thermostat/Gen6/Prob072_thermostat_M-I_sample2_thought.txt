The provided solution is already quite efficient in terms of PPA metrics, but there is a potential optimization that can be made to the fan logic. Currently, the fan logic is implemented as `(mode & too_cold) | (~mode & too_hot) | fan_on`. However, this can be simplified to `heater | aircon | fan_on`, where `heater` and `aircon` are the outputs of the heater and air conditioner logic, respectively. This simplification reduces the number of gates required, which can lead to improvements in area and potentially power consumption.

Additionally, the current solution uses a separate assign statement for the fan logic, which can introduce additional overhead. We can combine the fan logic with the existing assign statements to reduce the overall complexity of the design.
