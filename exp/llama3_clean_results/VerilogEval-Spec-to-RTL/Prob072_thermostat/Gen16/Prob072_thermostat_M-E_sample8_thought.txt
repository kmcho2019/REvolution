To develop a novel solution for the thermostat controller, we can implement a finite state machine (FSM) that manages the different states of the system based on the input conditions. This approach allows for a more structured and scalable design. The FSM will have states for heating, cooling, and idle modes, and it will transition between these states based on the input signals.

The FSM will have the following states:
- `HEAT`: The heater is on, and the fan is on.
- `COOL`: The air conditioner is on, and the fan is on.
- `FAN_ONLY`: The fan is on, but the heater and air conditioner are off.
- `IDLE`: All devices are off.

The transitions between these states will be determined by the input signals `mode`, `too_cold`, `too_hot`, and `fan_on`. This approach provides a clear and organized way to manage the different operating modes of the thermostat controller.
