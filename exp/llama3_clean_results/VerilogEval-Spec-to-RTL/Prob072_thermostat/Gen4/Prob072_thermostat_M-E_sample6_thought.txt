A novel approach to designing the TopModule is to use a priority-based encoding scheme. The idea is to assign a priority to each condition (too_cold, too_hot, fan_on) and then use a priority encoder to generate the outputs (heater, aircon, fan). The priority encoder will ensure that the highest priority condition is met first, and then the next highest priority condition, and so on. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design, making it easier to add or remove conditions in the future. The mode input can be used to select between the heating and cooling modes, and the priority encoder can be designed to take this into account.
