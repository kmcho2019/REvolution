To implement a novel architectural idea for the thermostat controller, we can introduce a finite state machine (FSM) approach. This involves defining states for the system (e.g., idle, heating, cooling, fan_on) and transitioning between these states based on the input conditions. The use of an FSM can provide a structured way to manage the system's behavior, potentially making it easier to understand and modify the logic. Additionally, an FSM can be more scalable if the system's requirements become more complex in the future.
