A novel approach to designing the thermostat controller could involve using a finite state machine (FSM) architecture. The FSM would have two primary states: HEATING and COOLING, determined by the mode input. Within each state, the FSM would then use the too_cold and too_hot inputs to decide whether to activate the heater or air conditioner, respectively. Additionally, the FSM would always turn on the fan if either the heater or air conditioner is active or if the fan_on input is high. This approach allows for a clear, structured implementation of the thermostat logic and provides an easy framework for adding more states or functionality if needed in the future.
