A novel approach to implementing the thermostat controller is to use a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different modes and conditions of the thermostat. This can provide a clear and organized way to handle the various states and transitions of the system. The FSM can have separate states for heating and cooling modes, as well as states for when the heater or air conditioner is on or off. The transitions between these states can be triggered by the input signals (mode, too_cold, too_hot, fan_on) and the current state of the system. This approach can make it easier to add or modify functionality in the future, as the state machine can be easily extended or modified to accommodate new requirements.
