The existing solution is already quite optimized in terms of logic and area. However, to further improve the PPA metrics, we should focus on minimizing the switching activity and reducing the number of gates. One potential improvement is to directly derive the fan control signal from the conditions that turn on the heater and air conditioner, along with the fan_on signal. Since the heater and air conditioner are mutually exclusive based on the mode signal, we can use this property to simplify the logic. Additionally, we can use the fact that the fan is on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan_on signal is high. This can help in reducing the number of gates and operations required to compute the outputs.
