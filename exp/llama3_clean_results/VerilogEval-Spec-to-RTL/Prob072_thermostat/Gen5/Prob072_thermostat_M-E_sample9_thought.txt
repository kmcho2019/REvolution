A novel approach to implementing the thermostat controller could involve using a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different modes and conditions. The FSM can have states for heating, cooling, and idle, and transition between these states based on the mode, temperature conditions, and fan request. This approach allows for a more modular and scalable design, where each state can be managed independently, and new states or conditions can be added easily.

The FSM will have four states: IDLE, HEATING, COOLING, and FAN_ONLY. The transitions between these states will be determined by the inputs mode, too_cold, too_hot, and fan_on. The outputs heater, aircon, and fan will be determined by the current state of the FSM.

This approach provides a clear and structured way to manage the different conditions and modes, making the design more readable, maintainable, and scalable.
