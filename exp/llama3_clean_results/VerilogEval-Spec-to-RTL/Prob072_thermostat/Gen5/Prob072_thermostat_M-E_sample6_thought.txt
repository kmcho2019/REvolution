A novel approach to designing the thermostat controller involves using a finite state machine (FSM) to manage the different operating modes and transitions between them. This FSM can have four states: idle, heating, cooling, and fan_only. The idle state represents the default condition where no heating or cooling is required, and the fan is off. The heating and cooling states are self-explanatory, with the corresponding heater or air conditioner turned on along with the fan. The fan_only state allows the fan to run independently of the heater or air conditioner when the fan_on signal is active. By using an FSM, the design becomes more modular and easier to extend if additional features or operating modes are required in the future.
