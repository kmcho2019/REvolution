To simplify the solution, we can first identify the conditions under which each output (heater, aircon, fan) should be active. The heater should be on when it's in heating mode and too cold. The air conditioner should be on when it's in cooling mode and too hot. The fan should be on whenever the heater or air conditioner is on, or when the fan is requested. By directly implementing these conditions with logical operators, we can simplify the design without needing intermediate variables or more complex logic structures.
