The TopModule can be designed using a simple state machine or combinational logic. We can start by implementing the logic for the heating mode and cooling mode separately. In heating mode, we need to turn on the heater when it's too cold and turn on the fan when the heater is on or the fan is requested. In cooling mode, we need to turn on the air conditioner when it's too hot and turn on the fan when the air conditioner is on or the fan is requested.
