To implement the TopModule, we need to consider the two modes of operation: heating and cooling. In heating mode, the heater should be turned on when it is too cold, and the fan should also be turned on to circulate the air. In cooling mode, the air conditioner should be turned on when it is too hot, and the fan should also be turned on. Additionally, the fan can be turned on independently of the heater and air conditioner. We can use simple logical operations to implement this behavior.
