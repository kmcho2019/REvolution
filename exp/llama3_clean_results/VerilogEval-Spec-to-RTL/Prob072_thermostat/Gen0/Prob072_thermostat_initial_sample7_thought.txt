To implement the thermostat controller, we will use a combination of logical operations to determine when to turn on/off the heater, air conditioner, and fan based on the mode, temperature conditions, and fan request. The heater will be turned on when the thermostat is in heating mode and it's too cold. The air conditioner will be turned on when the thermostat is in cooling mode and it's too hot. The fan will be turned on if either the heater or air conditioner is on, or if the user requests the fan to turn on.
