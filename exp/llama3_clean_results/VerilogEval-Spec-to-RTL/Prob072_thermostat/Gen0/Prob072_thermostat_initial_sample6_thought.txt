To implement the thermostat controller, we need to use the mode signal to determine whether we are in heating or cooling mode. 
In heating mode, we check if it's too cold and turn on the heater and fan accordingly. 
In cooling mode, we check if it's too hot and turn on the air conditioner and fan accordingly. 
We also need to check the fan_on signal and turn on the fan if it's requested, regardless of the heating or cooling mode.
