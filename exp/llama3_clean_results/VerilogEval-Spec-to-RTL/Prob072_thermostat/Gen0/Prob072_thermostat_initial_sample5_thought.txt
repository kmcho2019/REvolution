To implement the thermostat controller, we need to create a digital circuit that can switch between heating and cooling modes based on the input mode signal. We'll use simple logic to turn the heater, air conditioner, and fan on or off according to the mode, temperature status (too_cold or too_hot), and fan request (fan_on). 
The logic can be broken down into the following rules:
1. In heating mode (mode = 1), the heater is on when it's too cold (too_cold = 1), and the air conditioner is always off.
2. In cooling mode (mode = 0), the air conditioner is on when it's too hot (too_hot = 1), and the heater is always off.
3. The fan is on when either the heater or the air conditioner is on, or when the user requests the fan to be on (fan_on = 1).
