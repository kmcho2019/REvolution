This can be directly implemented using XOR operations. The existing solution `(a ^ b) ^ (c ^ d)` already does this but in two steps. This means `a ^ b ^ c ^ d` will produce the same result as `(a ^ b) ^ (c ^ d)`, simplifying the expression.
