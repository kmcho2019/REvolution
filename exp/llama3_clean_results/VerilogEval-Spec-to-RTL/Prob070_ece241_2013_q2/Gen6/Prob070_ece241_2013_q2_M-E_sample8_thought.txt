The novel architectural idea here is to approach the problem by first accurately identifying the binary patterns that lead to a logic-1 output for `out_sop` and then use these patterns to derive both the sum-of-products and product-of-sums forms. To implement `out_sop`, we need to express these patterns as a sum-of-products, focusing on directly translating the conditions into a Verilog expression. For `out_pos`, instead of taking the complement of `out_sop`, we will derive the product-of-sums form by considering the conditions that lead to a logic-0 output, which are the decimal numbers 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, and 14.
