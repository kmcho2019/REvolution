A novel architectural idea for implementing the 64-bit arithmetic shift register is to use a combination of a barrel shifter and a multiplexer-based load controller. The barrel shifter can be designed to perform the actual shifting operation, while the multiplexer-based load controller can handle the loading of new data into the shift register. This approach can potentially reduce the area usage and improve the performance of the design. Additionally, the use of a barrel shifter can simplify the implementation of the shift operations, making the code more readable and maintainable. To further optimize the design, we can use a clock-gated approach to reduce power consumption when the shift register is not being used.
