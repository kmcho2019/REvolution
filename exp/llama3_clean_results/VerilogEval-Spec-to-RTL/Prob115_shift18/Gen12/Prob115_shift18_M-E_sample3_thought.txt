To create a new and different solution, I will consider a pipelined architecture that breaks down the shift operation into smaller stages, allowing for a more balanced and efficient design. This approach can help in improving the overall performance by reducing the critical path and optimizing the utilization of hardware resources.

The new design will utilize a 3-stage pipeline to implement the shift register. The first stage will handle the input data and load signal, the second stage will perform the shift operation based on the amount signal, and the third stage will handle the output and enable signal.

By using a pipelined architecture, we can improve the throughput of the design and reduce the area usage by reusing the same hardware for different operations. Additionally, the pipelined design allows for better clock gating, which can help in reducing power consumption.

The use of a pipeline also enables the implementation of more complex shift operations, such as variable-length shifts, without significantly increasing the critical path. This can make the design more versatile and reusable in different applications.
