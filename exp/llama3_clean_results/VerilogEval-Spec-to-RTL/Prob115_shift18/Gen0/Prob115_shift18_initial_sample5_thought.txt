To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register, we can use a register to store the current value of the shifter. The module will have two main operations: loading the register with the input data and shifting the register based on the amount and direction specified. We will use a case statement to determine the direction and amount of the shift. For arithmetic right shifts, we will fill the most significant bits with the sign bit (the most significant bit of the register). For left shifts, we will fill the least significant bits with zeros.
