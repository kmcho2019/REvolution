To implement the 64-bit arithmetic shift register with synchronous load, we will use a reg to store the current state of the shift register. We will then use always @(posedge clk) to capture the clock signal and perform the shifting operation based on the control signals. The load signal will be used to determine whether to load the register with the input data or shift the current state. The ena signal will determine whether to perform a shift operation or not. The amount signal will determine the direction and amount of the shift.
